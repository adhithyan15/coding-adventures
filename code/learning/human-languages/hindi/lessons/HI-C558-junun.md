## You'll want to know: जुनून
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C558-KHVAHISH558-05]; assesses=[] -->

**जुनून** — *junūn* — "a passion; an obsession".

> उसे क्रिकेट का जुनून है।
>
> *Use krikeṭ kā junūn hai.*
>
> He is crazy about cricket.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**जुनून** (*junūn*) is usually traced to Arabic *junūn*, "madness, possession", through Persian. Hindi uses it for a consuming passion.
