# کیسے جاتے ہیں (kaise jāte haiṅ) — asking the way

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[UR-LEX-C270-KITNA-01, UR-LEX-C270-ZARA-01, UR-LEX-KAHAN] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "where?" (*kahāṅ?*) Then "how far is the station?" (*isṭeshan kitnī dūr hai?*) Now ask the way there.

## You'll want to know: how do you get there?
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-GRAMMAR-C270-DIRECTION-01]; assesses=[UR-LEX-C80-TOWN80-01, UR-LEX-KAISE-KAISI, UR-LEX-KAHAN, UR-LEX-C78-TIME78-02, UR-LEX-C98-LOC98-03] -->

> **اسٹیشن کہاں ہے؟**
>
> *isṭeshan kahāṅ hai?* — "Where is the station?"

> **اسٹیشن کیسے جاتے ہیں؟**
>
> *isṭeshan kaise jāte haiṅ?* — "How do you get to the station?"

Word for word, "how do people go to the station?": the plural **جاتے ہیں** (*jāte haiṅ*) asks about anyone.

> **کیا راستہ لمبا ہے؟**
>
> *kyā rāstā lambā hai?* — "Is it a long way?"

## You'll want to know: the polite request
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-GRAMMAR-C270-DIRECTION-02]; assesses=[UR-LEX-C97-LOC97-01, UR-LEX-C96-TIME96-05, UR-LEX-C96-TIME96-04, UR-LEX-C240-ACT240-05, UR-LEX-C80-TOWN80-04, UR-LEX-C52-VERBS52-04, UR-LEX-C46-FAMILY46-05] -->

> **سیدھے جائیے۔**
>
> *sīdhe jāiye.* — "Go straight on."

**جائیے** (*jāiye*) is **جانا** asked of someone you call **آپ**. The stem takes **-یے** (*-iye*); after a vowel, **-ئیے**:

> **بائیں مڑیے۔**
>
> *bāeṅ muṛiye.* — "Turn left."

> **پل کے بعد دائیں مڑیے۔**
>
> *pul ke bād dāeṅ muṛiye.* — "After the bridge, turn right."

> **ذرا بتائیے، بازار کہاں ہے؟**
>
> *zarā batāiye, bāzār kahāṅ hai?* — "Could you tell me where the market is?"

**سنیے** (*suniye*), "listen", opens a question to a stranger: "excuse me". Three verbs bend the stem: **دیجیے** (*dījiye*) from **دینا**, **لیجیے** (*lījiye*) from **لینا**, **کیجیے** (*kījiye*) from **کرنا**.
