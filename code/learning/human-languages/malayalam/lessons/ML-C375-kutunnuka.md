# കുടുങ്ങുക (kuṭuṅṅuka) — to get stuck; to be trapped

## You'll want to know: കുടുങ്ങുക
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C375-RAKSHA375-05]; assesses=[] -->

**കുടുങ്ങുക** — *kuṭuṅṅuka* — "to get stuck; to be trapped".

> മൂന്ന് പേർ കെട്ടിടത്തിനുള്ളിൽ കുടുങ്ങി.
>
> *mūnnŭ pēr keṭṭiṭattinuḷḷil kuṭuṅṅi.*
>
> Three people were trapped inside the building.

Past: കുടുങ്ങി, *kuṭuṅṅi*.

Also of traffic: ട്രാഫിക്കിൽ കുടുങ്ങി, stuck in traffic.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**കുടുങ്ങുക** (*kuṭuṅṅuka*) is a native Malayalam verb whose origin is not settled. It is said of people, vehicles and things caught where they cannot move.

## What you've built

One more word for telling what happened.
