## You'll want to know: おちつく
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C268-KYUUJO268-01]; assesses=[] -->

**おちつく** — *ochitsuku* — "to calm down; to keep calm".

> **じこが あったら、おちついて ください。** — *jiko ga attara, ochitsuite kudasai* — "If there is an accident, please keep calm."

Polite **おちつきます**, *ochitsukimasu*; a godan verb. Past (polite): **ははが おちつきました。**, *haha ga ochitsukimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**おちつく** (*ochitsuku*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, built from *ochi*, "falling" (from *ochiru*), and *tsuku*, "to settle" (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
