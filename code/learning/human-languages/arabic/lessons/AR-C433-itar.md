## You'll want to know: إطار
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C433-AJALA433-01]; assesses=[] -->

**إطار** — *iṭār* — "a tyre; a frame".

> كان إطار الدراجة قديما.
>
> *kāna iṭār ad-darrāja qadīman.*
>
> The bicycle's tyre was old.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**إطار** (*iṭār*) is usually traced to the native Arabic root *ʾ-ṭ-r*, "to bend round". An *iṭār* is a ring or rim that goes round a thing.
