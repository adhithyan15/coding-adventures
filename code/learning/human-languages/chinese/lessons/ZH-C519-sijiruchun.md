## You'll want to know: 四季如春
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C519-OUTDOORS519-03]; assesses=[] -->

**四季如春** — *sìjì rú chūn* — "mild like spring all year round (of a place)".

> **这儿四季如春我最喜欢这儿的阳光** *zhèr sìjì rú chūn wǒ zuì xǐhuan zhèr de yángguāng* — It's like spring here all year round, and the sunshine is what I love most.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**四季如春** (*sìjì rú chūn*) is built from 四季 'the four seasons', 如 'like' and 春 'spring', all inherited from Middle Chinese, *sijH*, *kjwijH*, *nyo* and *tsyhwin* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses 如 'to resemble' and the last, after an older book, as the head of the four seasons: every season like the spring.
