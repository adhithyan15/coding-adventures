# কুলোনো (kulono) — to be enough; to suffice

## You'll want to know: কুলোনো
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C528-UPAY528-04]; assesses=[] -->

**কুলোনো** — *kulono* — "to be enough; to suffice".

> এই টাকায় মাস না কুলোলে আমি ধার নেব।
>
> *Ei ṭākāy mās nā kulole āmi dhār nebô.*
>
> If this money doesn't last the month, I'll borrow.

Past (he/she …): সেই টাকায় তার কুলোল না, *sei ṭākāy tār kulolô nā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**কুলোনো** (*kulono*) is said of money, time or food that will or will not stretch far enough; where the word comes from is not settled.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
