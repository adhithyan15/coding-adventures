## You'll want to know: o recorde
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C511-TORNEIO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**o recorde** — "record (in sport)".

> Bateu o recorde nacional dos cem metros.
>
> *He broke the national hundred-metre record.*

*Bater um recorde* — to break a record.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*recorde* was borrowed from English *record* (EtymDB), which came from Old French.
