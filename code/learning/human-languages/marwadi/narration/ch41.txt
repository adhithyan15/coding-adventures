Lesson 1 of 1.
My own line — the first writing with no model.

Before we start: this one needs your hands, so it is not a driving lesson. You will want a pen and something to write on and your eyes or your hands for the steps that ask you to read, point, write or gesture. You can listen to everything else now. I will say so again when we reach it, but leave this section until you have stopped: Warm-up.

Warm-up.
[once you have stopped driving — this part needs your eyes or your hands; if you are driving, skip ahead to the next part]
[pause 2 seconds]
You have read signs and notices. Now write one.
[pause 1 second]
Everything you have written so far had something to reach for. This does not.

Writing — controlled composition.
[pause 2 seconds]
Write one line about yourself, choosing the words yourself from a supply that is closed: only what this book has taught you.
[pause 2 seconds]
One thing to decide deliberately before you start. The letters hang from a line drawn across the top, and that line belongs to the word, not to each letter. Draw the bodies first and the top line last, running it across the whole word.
[pause 3 seconds]
Write the line. Then read it back and check that top line before you check anything else — one long stroke, not a row of short ones.

Wrap-up recall.
[pause 2 seconds]
Does the top line belong to a letter or to a word? (To the word.)
[pause 2 seconds]
And what did you have to choose here that the earlier lessons chose for you? (The words.)
[question — say your answer, then pause 12 seconds]
Does the line across the top belong to a letter or to a word?
