## You'll want to know: el refugiado
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C552-EXILIO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**el refugiado** — "the refugee".

> El país acogió a miles de refugiados.
>
> *The country took in thousands of refugees.*

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*refugiado* is built on *refugiarse* 'to take shelter', from *refugio*, which comes from Latin *refugium* 'a taking refuge' (Lewis & Short), built on *fugere* 'to flee'.
