## You'll want to know: die Suchmaschine
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C394-INTERNET-03]; assesses=[] -->

**die Suchmaschine** — "the search engine".

> Mit einer Suchmaschine habe ich das Rezept schnell gefunden.
>
> *I quickly found the recipe with a search engine.*

Plural: *die Suchmaschinen*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Suchmaschine* joins *suchen* "to search", Proto-Germanic *sōkijaną* (EtymDB), to *Maschine* "machine", from Latin *māchina* and Greek *mēkhanḗ* (EtymDB; Lewis & Short).
