## You'll want to know: le conte
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C522-GENRES-05]; assesses=[] -->

**le conte** — "tale, story".

> Le soir, la mère lit un conte à ses enfants.
>
> *In the evening the mother reads her children a story.*

*Un conte de fées* — a fairy tale.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*le conte* is built on *conter* "to tell", inherited from Latin *computāre* "to count, to reckon", built on *putāre* (Lewis & Short): to recount.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.
