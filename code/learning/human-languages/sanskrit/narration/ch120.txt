Lesson 1 of 5.
मुद्रा (mudrā) — a coin, a seal.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a bottle, a flask, then the Sanskrit for a small umbrella.

You'll want to know: मुद्रा.
मुद्रा — mudrā — "a coin, a seal".

The word, taken apart.
mudrā "a seal, a signet-ring", and later a coin, is probably borrowed from Iranian (CDIAL 10203, after EWA). It lives on as Punjabi mundī "ring" (CDIAL 10203).

You'll want to know: पत्रालयः.
पत्रालयः — patrālayaḥ — "a post office".

The word, taken apart.
patrālaya is built from patra "a leaf, a letter" (MW) and ālaya "dwelling, house": "the house of letters". The pairing for a post office is modern.

Lesson 3 of 5.
लेखः (lekhaḥ) — a letter, an article.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a coin, then the Sanskrit for a post office.

You'll want to know: लेखः.
लेखः — lekhaḥ — "a letter, an article".

The word, taken apart.
lekha "a line, a stroke", then "a writing, a letter", comes from the root √likh "scratch, write" (CDIAL 11101): writing began as scratching. MW compares √likh with Greek ereikō "tear".

Lesson 4 of 5.
ग्रन्थः (granthaḥ) — a book, a text.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a post office, then the Sanskrit for a letter.

You'll want to know: ग्रन्थः.
ग्रन्थः — granthaḥ — "a book, a text".

The word, taken apart.
grantha comes from the root √granth "tie, string together" (CDIAL 4350): it first meant "a knot", and later "a book", a composition strung together. Sinhala kept it as gata "book" (CDIAL 4350).

Lesson 5 of 5.
चित्रम् (citram) — a picture.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a letter, then the Sanskrit for a book.

You'll want to know: चित्रम्.
चित्रम् — citram — "a picture".

The word, taken apart.
citra comes from the root √cit "perceive, appear" (CDIAL 4803): it first meant "conspicuous, bright, many-coloured", and as a noun "a picture" (MW). Pali kept it as citta "picture" (CDIAL 4803).
