## You'll want to know: बल
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C483-TAQAT483-04]; assesses=[] -->

**बल** — *bal* — "force; might".

> अरुण ने पूरे बल से दरवाज़ा धकेला।
>
> *Aruṇ ne pūre bal se darvāzā dhakelā.*
>
> Arun pushed the door with all his strength.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**बल** (*bal*) is inherited from Sanskrit *bála*, "strength, power": CDIAL lists Hindi *bal*, "strength, ability", under it (CDIAL 9161). *dublā* is built on its opposite, *durbala*.
