## You'll want to know: fermentum, fermentī
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C352-SUBIGO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**fermentum, fermentī** — "leaven, yeast".

> Sine fermentō pānis dūrus fit.
>
> *Without leaven bread turns out hard.*

Neuter, from a verb meaning 'to boil, to bubble'. English *ferment*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**fermentum** is built on *ferveō*, "to boil, to bubble" (EtymDB): what makes dough bubble. English *ferment* comes from it.
