## You'll want to know: bisher
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C265-INZWISCHEN-04]; assesses=[] -->

**bisher** — "so far, until now".

> Bisher ist alles gut gegangen.
>
> *So far everything has gone well.*

With the present or the *Perfekt*: *Bisher habe ich nichts gehört*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*bisher* joins *bis* "until" and *her* "hither, to here", so "up to here". Both are inherited Germanic words; EtymDB lists no parent.
