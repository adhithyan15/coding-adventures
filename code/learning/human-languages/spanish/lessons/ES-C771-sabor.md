## You'll want to know: el sabor
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C771-SABOR-01]; assesses=[] -->

**el sabor** — "the taste, the flavour".

> Me gusta por el sabor, no por el precio.
>
> *I like it for the taste, not the price.*

*¿De qué sabor?* — which flavour? *Saber a* is to taste of: *Sabe a limón.*

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*sabor* is inherited from Latin *sapōrem*, from *sapor* 'a taste, a flavour' (EtymDB), built on *sapere* 'to taste', the verb behind *saber*.
