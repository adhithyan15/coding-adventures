## You'll want to know: fondare
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C328-IMPERO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**fondare** — "to found, to establish".

> Secondo la leggenda, due fratelli hanno fondato la città.
>
> *According to the legend, two brothers founded the city.*

*Fondato nel 1900* — founded in 1900.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*fondare* is inherited from Latin *fundāre* "to lay the bottom, to found" (EtymDB), from *fundus* "bottom".
