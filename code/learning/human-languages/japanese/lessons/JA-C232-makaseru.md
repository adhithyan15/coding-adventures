## You'll want to know: まかせる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C232-TALK232-03]; assesses=[] -->

**まかせる** — *makaseru* — "to entrust; to leave to".

> **しゃちょうは どうりょうに しごとを まかせます。** — *shachō wa dōryō ni shigoto o makasemasu* — "The company president leaves the work to my colleague."

Polite **まかせます**, *makasemasu*; an ichidan verb.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**まかせる** (*makaseru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
