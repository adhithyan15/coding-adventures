Hindi, chapter 412: Abroad and Away.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
विदेश (videś) — abroad; a foreign country.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for cancelled, then the Hindi for departure.

You'll want to know: विदेश.
विदेश — videś — "abroad; a foreign country".
मेरा भाई विदेश में काम करता है। merā bhāī videś meṁ kām kartā hai. My brother works abroad.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
विदेश (videś) is a tatsama, Sanskrit videśa, "another country, abroad" (Monier-Williams), from vi-, "apart", and deśa, "country"; विदेशी, foreign, is its adjective.

Lesson 2 of 5.
परदेस (pardes) — a land far from home.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for departure, then the Hindi for abroad.

You'll want to know: परदेस.
परदेस — pardes — "a land far from home".
परदेस में अपने घर की बहुत याद आती है। pardes meṁ apne ghar kī bahut yād ātī hai. Far from home, you miss your own house a lot.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
परदेस (pardes) is a tadbhava of Sanskrit paradeśa, "another or a foreign country" (Monier-Williams). It may be another state of India: anywhere that is not home.

सरहद — sarhad — "a frontier; a border".

The word, taken apart.
सरहद (sarhad) is usually traced to Persian sarhad, "a frontier", literally the head of the boundary.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a land far from home, then the Hindi for a frontier.

You'll want to know: उतारना.
उतारना — utārnā — "to unload; to take down".
कुली ने ट्रेन से हमारा सामान उतारा। kulī ne ṭren se hamārā sāmān utārā. The porter unloaded our luggage from the train.
Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने छत से कपड़े उतारे, usne chat se kapṛe utāre.

The word, taken apart.
उतारना (utārnā) is the causative partner of उतरना, from Sanskrit uttārayati, "makes descend" (CDIAL 1770), which lists Hindi utārnā, "to make descend".

You'll want to know: चढ़ाना.
चढ़ाना — caṛhānā — "to put on board; to put up".
हमने अटैची बस की छत पर चढ़ाई। hamne aṭaicī bas kī chat par caṛhāī. We put the suitcase up on the roof of the bus.
Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने सामान ट्रेन में चढ़ाया, usne sāmān ṭren meṁ caṛhāyā.

The word, taken apart.
चढ़ाना (caṛhānā) is the causative partner of चढ़ना, from cāḍhayati, "raises", a form CDIAL reconstructs (CDIAL 4578), which lists Hindi caṛhānā.
