## You'll want to know: спасатель
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C296-SPASATEL-01]; assesses=[] -->

**спасатель** (*spasátel*) — "a rescuer, a lifeguard".

> Спасатели приехали через десять минут.
>
> *Spasáteli priyékhali chérez désyat minút.*
>
> The rescuers arrived ten minutes later.

Genitive *спасателя*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*спасатель* is built on *спасать* "to save", from *спасти*, Old East Slavic *sŭpasti* (EtymDB), the verb also behind *спасибо*, with the ending *-тель* for a person.
