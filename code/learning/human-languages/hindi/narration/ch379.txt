Hindi, chapter 379: Formal Conditions.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
यदि (yadi) — if (formal).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for except, then the Hindi for to forbid.

You'll want to know: यदि.
यदि — yadi — "if (formal)".
यदि आप सहमत हैं तो कृपया यहाँ हस्ताक्षर कीजिए। yadi āp sahmat haiṁ to kṛpayā yahā̃ hastākṣar kījiye. If you agree, please sign here.

The word, taken apart.
यदि (yadi) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit yadi, "if" (Monier-Williams).

What you've built.
One more word for saying what happens if.

Lesson 4 of 5.
ख़बरदार (khabardār) — beware!, watch out.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for otherwise (formal), then the Hindi for mere.

You'll want to know: ख़बरदार.
ख़बरदार — khabardār — "beware!, watch out".
ख़बरदार, अगर तुम फिर देर से आए तो दरवाज़ा बंद मिलेगा। khabardār, agar tum phir der se āe to darvāzā band milegā. Watch out: if you come late again, you'll find the door shut.

The word, taken apart.
ख़बरदार (khabardār) is usually traced to Persian khabardār, "aware, informed". It joins Arabic khabar, "news", to Persian -dār, "holding": one who has been told.

Lesson 5 of 5.
पटकना (paṭaknā) — to slam, to throw down hard.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for mere, then the Hindi for beware.

You'll want to know: पटकना.
पटकना — paṭaknā — "to slam, to throw down hard".
अगर तुम दरवाज़ा पटकोगे तो बच्चा जाग जाएगा। agar tum darvāzā paṭkoge to baccā jāg jāegā. If you slam the door, the baby will wake up.
Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने दरवाज़ा पटका, usne darvāzā paṭkā.

The word, taken apart.
पटकना (paṭaknā) is a tadbhava, inherited from Sanskrit paṭ, "a sudden noise" (CDIAL 7691). CDIAL glosses the Hindi verb "to dash against".
