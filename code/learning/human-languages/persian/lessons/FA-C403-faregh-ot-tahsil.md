## You'll want to know: فارغ التحصیل
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C403-TAHSILAT-01]; assesses=[] -->

**فارغ التحصیل** (*fâregh ot-tahsil*) — "a graduate".

> او سال گذشته فارغ التحصیل شد.
>
> *u sâl-e gozashte fâregh ot-tahsil shod.*
>
> He graduated last year.

An Arabic phrase, literally "free of study". *Fâregh ot-tahsil shodan* — to graduate.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**فارغ التحصیل** is usually traced to the Arabic phrase *fārigh al-taḥṣīl*, "free of study": *fārigh* "free, done" + *al-* "the" + *taḥṣīl* "study". A graduate has finished studying.
