## You'll want to know: حزن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C488-MAZAJ488-04]; assesses=[] -->

**حزن** — *ḥazina* — "to grieve, to be sad".

> حزنت البنت عندما سافرت صديقتها.
>
> *ḥazinat al-bint ʿindamā sāfarat ṣadīqatuhā.*
>
> The girl was sad when her friend went away.

Present: يحزن, *yaḥzan*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**حزن** (*ḥazina*) is built on the native Arabic root *ḥ-z-n*, "grief, sadness" (Lane). The adjective ḥazīn, sad, which you already know, comes from it.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
