# आप चाय पीवो हो? — a yes-or-no question

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MW-GRAMMAR-C154-PRES-POLITE-01, MW-GRAMMAR-C155-VSTEM-MORE-01, MW-SCRIPT-CHAAY-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say *you drink tea*, to **आप**: **आप चाय पीवो हो।**

## Grammar Lens: the voice does it
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-GRAMMAR-C158-POLAR-01]; assesses=[MW-GRAMMAR-C154-PRES-POLITE-01, MW-LEX-C47-HEALTH47-02, MW-GRAMMAR-C152-VO-HE-01] -->

Say the same sentence again and let your voice rise at the end. That is the whole
question:

> **आप चाय पीवो हो?**
>
> *āp chāy pīvo ho?* — Do you drink tea?

> **वो बीमार है?**
>
> *vo bīmār hai?* — Is he ill?

Nothing moves and nothing is added. In print, only the end mark changes: **?** for
**।**.
