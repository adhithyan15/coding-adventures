Russian, chapter 471: Hurt Feelings.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
обида (obída) — hurt feelings, offence.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to blush, then the Russian for shy.

You'll want to know: обида.
обида (obída) — "hurt feelings, offence".
Он до сих пор помнит эту обиду. On do sikh pór pómnit étu obídu. He still remembers that hurt.
Feminine. Не в обиду — no offence meant.

The word, taken apart.
обида (obída) is inherited from Proto-Slavic obvida (EtymDB), derived from obviděti, which is built on ob- and viděti 'to see'.

Lesson 2 of 5.
обижаться (obizhátsya) — to take offence.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for shy, then the Russian for hurt feelings.

You'll want to know: обижаться.
обижаться (obizhátsya) — "to take offence".
Она часто обижается на мои шутки. Oná chásto obizháyetsya na moí shútki. She often takes offence at my jokes.
Imperfective; the perfective is обидеться (обижусь, обидишься). Обижаться на кого — to be offended with someone.

The word, taken apart.
обижаться (obizhátsya) is the reflexive of обижать (obizhát), the imperfective partner of обидеть; all three share the root of обида (obída) 'offence', from Proto-Slavic obvida (EtymDB).

Lesson 3 of 5.
обижать (obizhát) — to hurt (someone's feelings).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for hurt feelings, then the Russian for to take offence.

You'll want to know: обижать.
обижать (obizhát) — "to hurt (someone's feelings)".
Старший брат никогда не обижал младшего. Stárshiy brat nikogdá ne obizhál mládshego. The older brother never picked on the younger one.
Imperfective; perfective обидеть. Takes the accusative: обижать (obizhát) младшего.

The word, taken apart.
обижать (obizhát) is the imperfective partner of обидеть 'to offend' (EtymDB lists both without a source); they share the root of обида (obída), from Proto-Slavic obvida (EtymDB).

Lesson 4 of 5.
обидчивый (obídchivyy) — touchy.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to take offence, then the Russian for to hurt.

You'll want to know: обидчивый.
обидчивый (obídchivyy) — "touchy".
С обидчивым человеком трудно шутить. S obídchivym chelovékom trúdno shutít. It's hard to joke with a touchy person.
Easily offended. Short forms: обидчив, обидчива.

The word, taken apart.
обидчивый (obídchivyy) is built on обида (obída) 'offence' with the ending -чивый, which marks a habit; обида (obída) is inherited from Proto-Slavic obvida (EtymDB).

Lesson 5 of 5.
прощать (proshchát) — to forgive.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to hurt, then the Russian for touchy.

You'll want to know: прощать.
прощать (proshchát) — "to forgive".
Мама всегда прощает нас. Máma vsegdá proshcháyet nas. Mum always forgives us.
Imperfective; perfective простить (прощу, простишь), the verb behind простите. Прощать кому что: прощать (proshchát) другу ошибку.

The word, taken apart.
прощать (proshchát) is the imperfective partner of простить 'to forgive'; EtymDB links it to Old East Slavic prostiti, and both verbs are usually traced to простой 'simple, free', so to forgive is to set someone free.
