Spanish, chapter 687: Caminos de montaña.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
el refugio — the mountain hut; the shelter.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for the rock, then the Spanish for to climb.

You'll want to know: el refugio.
el refugio — "the mountain hut; the shelter".
Dormimos en un refugio de montaña. We slept in a mountain hut.
A refugio is a simple hut for walkers.

The word, taken apart.
refugio is a learned borrowing from Latin refugium "a taking refuge" (Lewis & Short), from fugere "to flee".

What you've built.
One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.

Guided Practice.

[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: a sentence of your own with el refugio]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Spanish for the rock, then the Spanish for to climb, then say el refugio again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does el refugio mean? ("The mountain hut; the shelter".) Say it once more.

You'll want to know: trepar.
trepar — "to clamber, to climb (using your hands)".
Los niños trepan a los árboles. The children climb trees.
Escalar is the sport; trepar is any scrambling climb.

The word, taken apart.
The origin of trepar is not settled; it may imitate the sound of feet stamping.
