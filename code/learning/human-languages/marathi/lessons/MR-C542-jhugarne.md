# झुगारणे (jhugārṇe) — to defy; to throw off

## You'll want to know: झुगारणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C542-NIYAM542-05]; assesses=[] -->

**झुगारणे** — *jhugārṇe* — "to defy; to throw off".

> काही लोकांनी नियम झुगारले आणि दंड भरला.
>
> *kāhī lokānnī niyam jhugārle āṇi daṇḍ bharlā.*
>
> Some people defied the rules and paid a fine.

Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने नियम झुगारले, *tyāne niyam jhugārle*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**झुगारणे** (*jhugārṇe*) is a Marathi verb for throwing off a rule or a burden; its origin is not settled.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
