## You'll want to know: てぶくろ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C150-GLOVES150-02]; assesses=[] -->

**てぶくろ** — *tebukuro* — "gloves".

> **ふゆは てぶくろを つかいます。** — *fuyu wa tebukuro o tsukaimasu* — "In winter I use gloves."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**てぶくろ** (*tebukuro*) is native Japanese, built from *te*, "hand", and *fukuro*, "bag", voiced to *bukuro*; both parts are inherited rather than borrowed (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
