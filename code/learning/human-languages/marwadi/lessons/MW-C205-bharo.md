## You'll want to know: भाड़ो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C205-BUS205-03]; assesses=[] -->

**भाड़ो** — *bhāṛo* — "a fare; hire".

> भाड़ो कितणो है?
>
> *bhāṛo kitṇo hai?*
>
> How much is the fare?

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**भाड़ो** (*bhāṛo*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*bhārta*, "pertaining to servants", by way of Middle Indo-Aryan *bhāṭa*, "wages". CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry, but lists Sindhi *bhāṛo*, "hire", and Hindi *bhāṛā*, "hire, fare, rent" (CDIAL 9468).
