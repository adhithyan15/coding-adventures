## You'll want to know: तलाश
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C310-BICHHUD310-03]; assesses=[] -->

**तलाश** — *talāś* — "a search".

> दो दिन तक बच्चे की तलाश चली।
>
> *do din tak bacce kī talāś calī.*
>
> The search for the child went on for two days.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**तलाश** (*talāś*) is usually traced to Persian *talāš*, "a search, an effort", which may itself come from Turkic. **की तलाश में** is in search of.
