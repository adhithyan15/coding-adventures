## You'll want to know: राजमार्ग
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C407-NAKA407-01]; assesses=[] -->

**राजमार्ग** — *rājmārg* — "a highway".

> यह राजमार्ग पहाड़ों से होकर जाता है।
>
> *yah rājmārg pahāṛoṁ se hokar jātā hai.*
>
> This highway goes through the mountains.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**राजमार्ग** (*rājmārg*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *rājamārga*, "the king's road, a main road" (Monier-Williams), from *rāja*, "king", and *mārga*, "road"; the national highways carry it on their signs.
