## You'll want to know: zuòpǐn
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C503-ART503-03]; assesses=[] -->

*zuòpǐn* — "a work of art; a piece of writing".

> *zhè shì tā zuì hǎo de zuòpǐn* — This is his best work.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*zuòpǐn* is built from 作 'to make' and *pǐn* 'a kind', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *tsak* and *phimX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'a rank' and 'a kind'.
