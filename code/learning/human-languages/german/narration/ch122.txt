German, chapter 122: Garlic, Flour, Chocolate, Biscuit, Bread roll.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
der Knoblauch — garlic.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for a cherry, then the German for a melon.

You'll want to know: der Knoblauch.
der Knoblauch — "garlic".

The word, taken apart.
Knoblauch from Middle High German knobelouch (EtymDB 2.1), older klobelouch, "split leek" for its cloves: the "leek" word from Proto-Germanic laukaz, English leek.

Lesson 2 of 5.
das Mehl — flour.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for a melon, then the German for garlic.

You'll want to know: das Mehl.
das Mehl — "flour".

The word, taken apart.
Mehl from Old High German melo from Proto-Germanic melwą, "meal, flour" — inherited, the same word as English meal in oatmeal. (EtymDB 2.1).

The word, taken apart.
Brötchen is Brot + -chen, "little": a little bread. Brot from Proto-Germanic braudą, English bread. (EtymDB 2.1).
