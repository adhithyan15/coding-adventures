A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
goes back to Sanskrit chattvará, "a house, a bower", and CDIAL lists the Gujarati noun, "a thatched roof, a cottage" (CDIAL 4976). છત is a ceiling or a flat roof; a છાપરું (chāprũ) is a sloping roof of thatch or tiles.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: ઉજાડવું.
ઉજાડવું — ujāḍvũ — "to lay waste".
વાવાઝોડાએ આખું ગામ ઉજાડ્યું. vāvājhoḍāe ākhũ gām ujāḍyũ. The cyclone laid waste to the whole village.
Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે ખેતર ઉજાડ્યું, teṇe khetar ujāḍyũ.

The word, taken apart.
ઉજાડવું (ujāḍvũ) comes from ujjaṭati, a form CDIAL reconstructs, and CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb, "to lay waste" (CDIAL 1661). A storm, a war or a flood ઉજાડે છે: it leaves a place empty.
