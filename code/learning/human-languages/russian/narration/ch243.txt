Russian, chapter 243: Young, Elderly, Tall, Low, Ugly.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
молодой (molodóy) — young.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a zoo, then the Russian for a circus.

You'll want to know: молодой.
молодой (molodóy) — "young".
