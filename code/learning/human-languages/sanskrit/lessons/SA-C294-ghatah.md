## You'll want to know: घातः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C294-SANGHATTA294-02]; assesses=[] -->

**घातः** — *ghātaḥ* — "a blow; a bruise".

> चालकस्य हस्ते घातः अस्ति।
>
> *cālakasya haste ghātaḥ asti.*
>
> The driver has a bruise on his hand.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**घातः** (*ghātaḥ*) comes from the root √han, "to strike, kill"; MW gives *ghāta* as a blow or bruise (MW).
