## You'll want to know: o fósforo
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C448-TABACARIA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**o fósforo** — "the match; phosphorus".

> Acendi a vela com um fósforo.
>
> *I lit the candle with a match.*

*Uma caixa de fósforos* — a box of matches.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*fósforo* is a learned borrowing, through Latin, from Greek *phōsphóros* 'light-bearing', from *phôs* 'light' and *phérein* 'to carry' (Lewis & Short).
