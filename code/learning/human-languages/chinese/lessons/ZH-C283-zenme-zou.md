## Grammar Lens: 去 … 怎么走, asking the way
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-GRAMMAR-ZENME-ZOU-01]; assesses=[ZH-LEX-C43-GO43-03, ZH-LEX-C73-STATION73-03, ZH-LEX-C190-TOWN190-01, ZH-LEX-C65-SORRY65-02, ZH-LEX-C197-WAY197-04, ZH-LEX-C111-FRONT111-04, ZH-LEX-C111-FRONT111-05, ZH-LEX-C82-SIDES82-02, ZH-LEX-C82-SIDES82-03] -->

> **去, the place, then 怎么走 asks the way there.**

> **去火车站怎么走** *qù huǒchēzhàn zěnme zǒu* — How do I get to the station?
> **请问 去书店怎么走** *qǐngwèn, qù shūdiàn zěnme zǒu* — Excuse me, how do I get to the bookshop?

走 is the *zǒu* you know, to walk; the next lesson puts it in your hand. 请问 in front makes the question polite.

The answer shows the way: *yìzhí zǒu*, straight on; *dào lùkǒu zuǒ guǎi*, turn left at the crossing. zuǒ is the zuǒ of *zuǒbian*; yòu, the yòu of *yòubian*, turns you right.
