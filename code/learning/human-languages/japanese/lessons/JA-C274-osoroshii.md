## You'll want to know: おそろしい
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C274-JISHIN274-04]; assesses=[] -->

**おそろしい** — *osoroshii* — "frightening; dreadful".

> **それは おそろしい じしんでした。** — *sore wa osoroshii jishin deshita* — "It was a frightening earthquake."

An i-adjective.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**おそろしい** (*osoroshii*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict).
