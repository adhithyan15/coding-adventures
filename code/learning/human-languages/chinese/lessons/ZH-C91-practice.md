## The exchange
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-PERFORMANCE-WANT-01]; assesses=[ZH-LEX-C91-XIANGYAO-01, ZH-LEX-C91-YIDIANR-01, ZH-LEX-C91-BUYAO-01, ZH-LEX-C91-XUYAO-01, ZH-LEX-C55-LIKE55-01, ZH-LEX-C41-MONEY41-05] -->

> **A:** 你想喝什么
>
> *nǐ xiǎng hē shénme* — What would you like to drink?
>
> **B:** 我想要一点儿水
>
> *wǒ xiǎngyào yìdiǎnr shuǐ* — I'd like a little water.
>
> **A:** *nǐ yào chá ma* — Would you like tea?
>
> **B:** 不要 谢谢
>
> *bú yào, xièxie* — No, thank you.
>
> **A:** *nǐ xūyào xiūxi ma* — Do you need a rest?
>
> **B:** *bù, wǒ bú lèi* — No, I'm not tired.

Four ways of wanting: 想 + a verb, 想要 + a thing, *yào* + a thing, *xūyào* for a need. And one way of refusing that is still polite: 不要, 谢谢.
