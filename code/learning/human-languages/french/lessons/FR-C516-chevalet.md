## You'll want to know: le chevalet
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C516-PEINTURE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**le chevalet** — "easel".

> Le peintre installe son chevalet au bord de la rivière.
>
> *The painter sets up his easel by the river.*

Literally a little horse.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*le chevalet* is built on *cheval* "horse" with the small ending *-et* (EtymDB); *cheval* is inherited from Latin *caballus* "a horse" (Lewis & Short).
