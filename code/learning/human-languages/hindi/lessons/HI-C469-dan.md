## You'll want to know: दान
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C469-UDAR469-03]; assesses=[] -->

**दान** — *dān* — "a donation; charity".

> दादी हर साल मंदिर में दान देती हैं।
>
> *dādī har sāl mandir meṁ dān detī haiṁ.*
>
> Grandmother gives to the temple every year.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**दान** (*dān*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *dāna*, "the act of giving", from *dā*, "to give" (Monier-Williams), the same root as *denā*.
