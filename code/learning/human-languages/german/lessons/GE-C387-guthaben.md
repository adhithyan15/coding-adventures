## You'll want to know: das Guthaben
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C387-KONTO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**das Guthaben** — "the credit (balance)".

> Auf meinem Handy habe ich noch zehn Euro Guthaben.
>
> *I still have ten euros of credit on my mobile.*

Plural: *die Guthaben*. Literally what you have to your good.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Guthaben* joins *gut* "good", Old High German *guot* (EtymDB), to *haben* "to have": the sum that stands to your good.
