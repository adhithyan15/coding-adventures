## You'll want to know: der Esslöffel
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C322-ZUTAT-03]; assesses=[] -->

**der Esslöffel** — "the tablespoon".

> Man nimmt zwei Esslöffel Zucker.
>
> *You take two tablespoons of sugar.*

Plural: *die Esslöffel*. Short: *EL*. A teaspoon is *ein Teelöffel*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Esslöffel* joins *essen* "to eat" and *Löffel* "spoon", from Proto-Germanic *lapilaz* "spoon" (EtymDB).
