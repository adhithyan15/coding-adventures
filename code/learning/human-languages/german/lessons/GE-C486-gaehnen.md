## You'll want to know: gähnen
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C486-MUEDE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**gähnen** — "to yawn".

> In der langen Besprechung gähnten alle.
>
> *Everyone was yawning in the long meeting.*

Regular: *hat gegähnt*. *Gähnende Leere* — total emptiness.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*gähnen* is inherited from Old High German *ginēn* "to be wide open, to gape" (EtymDB).
