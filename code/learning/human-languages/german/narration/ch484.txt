German, chapter 484: Stirn, Wange und Kinn.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
die Stirn — forehead.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for wheelchair, then the German for to heal.

You'll want to know: die Stirn.
die Stirn — "forehead".
Das Kind hat Fieber, die Stirn ist ganz heiß. The child has a fever; its forehead is very hot.
Plural: die Stirnen. Die Stirn runzeln — to frown.

The word, taken apart.
Stirn is inherited from Middle High German stirne and Old High German stirna, from Proto-Germanic stirnijō "forehead" (EtymDB).

Lesson 5 of 5.
die Wimper — eyelash.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for chin, then the German for eyebrow.

You'll want to know: die Wimper.
die Wimper — "eyelash".
Das Mädchen hat lange, dunkle Wimpern. The girl has long, dark eyelashes.
Plural: die Wimpern. Ohne mit der Wimper zu zucken — without batting an eyelid.

The word, taken apart.
Wimper is usually traced to Old High German wintbrāwa, a compound with brāwa "brow"; the first part is not settled, and EtymDB lists the word without a source.
