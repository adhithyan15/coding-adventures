## You'll want to know: زعم
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C366-IDDIA366-02]; assesses=[] -->

**زعم** — *zaʿama* — "to allege".

> زعم التاجر أن الساعة من ذهب.
>
> *zaʿama at-tājir anna as-sāʿa min dhahab.*
>
> The merchant alleged that the watch was made of gold.

Present: يزعم, *yazʿum*.

زعم suggests the speaker doubts the claim.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**زعم** (*zaʿama*) is built on the native Arabic root *z-ʿ-m*, "to assert" (Lane). *Zaʿama* is a Form I verb; Lane notes that it often marks a claim that may be untrue.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
