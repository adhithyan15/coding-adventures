Sanskrit, chapter 395: Caravans and Companions.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
सार्थः (sārthaḥ) — a caravan.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for travels, then the Sanskrit for an ascetic's water-pot.

You'll want to know: सार्थः.
सार्थः — sārthaḥ — "a caravan".
सार्थः ग्रामात् ग्रामं गच्छति। sārthaḥ grāmāt grāmaṃ gacchati. The caravan goes from village to village.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
सार्थः (sārthaḥ) is built from sa, "with", and artha, "an aim, business": MW gives sārtha first as having a purpose, then as a travelling company of traders or pilgrims, a caravan (MW).

Lesson 3 of 5.
अनुयाति (anuyāti) — goes along with; follows.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a caravan, then the Sanskrit for a caravan leader.

You'll want to know: अनुयाति.
अनुयाति — anuyāti — "goes along with; follows".
मम मित्रं माम् अनुयाति। mama mitraṃ mām anuyāti. My friend goes along with me.
Past (he …): सः माम् अनुयातवान्, saḥ mām anuyātavān.

The word, taken apart.
अनुयाति (anuyāti) is built from anu, "after, along", and the root √yā, "to go"; MW gives anu-yā as to go after or follow, with the participle anuyāta (MW).

Lesson 4 of 5.
आपृच्छते (āpṛcchate) — takes leave; says goodbye.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a caravan leader, then the Sanskrit for goes along with.

You'll want to know: आपृच्छते.
आपृच्छते — āpṛcchate — "takes leave; says goodbye".
यात्री मातरम् आपृच्छते। yātrī mātaram āpṛcchate. The traveller takes leave of his mother.
Past (he …): सः मातरम् आपृष्टवान्, saḥ mātaram āpṛṣṭavān.

The word, taken apart.
आपृच्छते (āpṛcchate) is built from ā, "towards", and the root √prach, "to ask", which also gives pṛcchati, "asks"; in the middle voice MW gives ā-prach as to take leave, to bid farewell (MW).
