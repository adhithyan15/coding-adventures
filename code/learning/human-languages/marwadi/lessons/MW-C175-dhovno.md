## You'll want to know: ढोवणो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C175-PANI175-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ढोवणो** — *ḍhovṇo* — "to carry (a load)".

> मां घड़ो ढोवै है।
>
> *mā̃ ghaṛo ḍhovai hai.*
>
> Mother carries the water pot.

Past (I …): म्हैं पाणी ढोयो, *mhaĩ pāṇī ḍhoyo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ढोवणो** (*ḍhovṇo*) is inherited from Sanskrit *ḍhaukayati*, "brings near"; CDIAL lists Hindi *ḍhonā*, "to convey" (CDIAL 5610). CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry.
