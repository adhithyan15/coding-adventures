Hindi, chapter 169: To join, To reduce, To increase, To save, To escape.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
जोड़ना (joṛnā) — to join, to add.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for to drive, then the Hindi for to leave.

You'll want to know: जोड़ना.
जोड़ना — joṛnā — "to join, to add".

The word, taken apart.
जोड़ना (joṛnā) is a tadbhava, a word inherited and reshaped by sound change. It comes from yoṭayati, "joins", one of a family of forms CDIAL reconstructs from their descendants under yuṭati, "is joined" (CDIAL 10496). The plain yuṭati gave Hindi the intransitive juṛnā, "to be joined". CDIAL calls the whole family's link with Sanskrit yuj-, "to join", obscure.

Lesson 4 of 5.

Before the new one: say the Hindi for to reduce, then the Hindi for to increase.

You'll want to know: बचाना.
बचाना — bacānā — "to save, to rescue".

The word, taken apart.
बचाना (bacānā) is the causing form of bacnā, "to escape": to save is to make someone escape. CDIAL lists both under Sanskrit vañcati, "moves", whose causing form meant "escapes from" (CDIAL 11208).
