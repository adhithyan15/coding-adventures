## You'll want to know: la musculation
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C504-GYM-01]; assesses=[] -->

**la musculation** — "weight training, bodybuilding".

> Je fais de la musculation deux fois par semaine.
>
> *I do weight training twice a week.*

Informally *la muscu*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*la musculation* is built on *muscle*, a learned borrowing from Latin *musculus* "a little mouse, a muscle", from *mūs* "mouse" (Lewis & Short): a moving muscle looked like a mouse under the skin.
