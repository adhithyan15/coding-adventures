Sanskrit, chapter 429: Opening Hours and Rent.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
उद्घाटयति (udghāṭayati) — opens; unlocks.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for goes out to meet, then the Sanskrit for sees a guest off.

You'll want to know: उद्घाटयति.
उद्घाटयति — udghāṭayati — "opens; unlocks".
आपणिकः प्रातः आपणम् उद्घाटयति। āpaṇikaḥ prātaḥ āpaṇam udghāṭayati. The shopkeeper opens the shop in the morning.
Past (he …): सः आपणम् उद्घाटितवान्, saḥ āpaṇam udghāṭitavān.

The word, taken apart.
उद्घाटयति (udghāṭayati) is built from ud, "up, out", and the causative of the root √ghaṭ, which also gives ghaṭate, "happens"; MW gives ud-ghāṭayati as to open, unlock, unfasten, with the participle udghāṭita (MW).

Lesson 2 of 5.
घटिका (ghaṭikā) — a clock; a ghatika of twenty-four minutes.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for sees a guest off, then the Sanskrit for unlocks.

You'll want to know: घटिका.
घटिका — ghaṭikā — "a clock; a ghatika of twenty-four minutes".
आपणस्य भित्तौ घटिका अस्ति। āpaṇasya bhittau ghaṭikā asti. There is a clock on the wall of the shop.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
घटिका (ghaṭikā) is built on ghaṭī, "a small water-jar": MW gives ghaṭikā as a water-jar, as a period of twenty-four minutes once measured by such a jar, and as the Indian gong clock; a clock or a watch on the wall is that sense in modern use (MW).

Lesson 3 of 5.
आवसथः (āvasathaḥ) — a travellers' lodge.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for unlocks, then the Sanskrit for a clock.

You'll want to know: आवसथः.
आवसथः — āvasathaḥ — "a travellers' lodge".
आवसथे पथिकाः रात्रिं वसन्ति। āvasathe pathikāḥ rātriṃ vasanti. Wayfarers spend the night at the lodge.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
आवसथः (āvasathaḥ) is built from ā and the root √vas, "to dwell", which also gives vasati, "dwells"; MW gives āvasatha as a dwelling-place, an abode, a night's lodging (MW).

Lesson 4 of 5.
अर्गलः (argalaḥ) — a door bolt.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a clock, then the Sanskrit for a travellers' lodge.

You'll want to know: अर्गलः.
अर्गलः — argalaḥ — "a door bolt".
रात्रौ अहं द्वारे अर्गलं स्थापयामि। rātrau ahaṃ dvāre argalaṃ sthāpayāmi. At night I put the bolt on the door.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
अर्गलः (argalaḥ) is the word MW gives for a wooden bolt or pin that fastens a door, and for a bar or check; MW gives no derivation, so its origin is uncertain (MW).

Lesson 5 of 5.
अवक्रयः (avakrayaḥ) — rent.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a travellers' lodge, then the Sanskrit for a door bolt.

You'll want to know: अवक्रयः.
अवक्रयः — avakrayaḥ — "rent".
प्रकोष्ठस्य अवक्रयः कियत् अस्ति? prakoṣṭhasya avakrayaḥ kiyat asti? How much is the rent for the room?
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
अवक्रयः (avakrayaḥ) is built from ava and the root √krī, "to buy", which also gives krīṇāti, "buys"; MW gives avakraya as letting out to hire and as rent (MW).
