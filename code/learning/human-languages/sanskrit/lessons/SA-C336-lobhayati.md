## You'll want to know: लोभयति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C336-NITI336-05]; assesses=[] -->

**लोभयति** — *lobhayati* — "tempts; lures".

> शृगालः फलेन काकं लोभयति।
>
> *śṛgālaḥ phalena kākaṃ lobhayati.*
>
> The jackal tempts the crow with a fruit.

Past (he …): सः काकं लोभितवान्, *saḥ kākaṃ lobhitavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**लोभयति** (*lobhayati*) comes from the root √lubh, "to desire greatly", as in *lobha*, in its causative form: to make desire, tempt, lure (MW).
