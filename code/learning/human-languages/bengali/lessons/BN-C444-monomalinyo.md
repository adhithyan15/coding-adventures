## You'll want to know: মনোমালিন্য
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C444-OBHIMAN444-05]; assesses=[] -->

**মনোমালিন্য** — *mônomālinyô* — "ill feeling; bad blood (between people)".

> জমি নিয়ে দুই ভাইয়ের মনোমালিন্য হল।
>
> *jômi niye dui bhāiyer mônomālinyô hôlô.*
>
> The two brothers fell out over land.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**মনোমালিন্য** (*mônomālinyô*) joins Sanskrit *manas*, "mind" (CDIAL 9822), in its compound form *mano-*, and *mālinya*, "dirtiness, darkness" (Monier-Williams): a mind clouded against someone.
