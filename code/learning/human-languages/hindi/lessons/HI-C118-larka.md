# लड़का (laṛkā) — a boy; a son

## You'll want to know: लड़का
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C118-SPOUSE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**लड़का** (*laṛkā*) — "a boy", and in a family, "a son".

**बेटा** is "son" as a family word; **लड़का** is any boy.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

A boy, beside the word for son you know.
