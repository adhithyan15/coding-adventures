German, chapter 380: Anmelden und zugelassen werden.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
die Zulassung — the admission; the registration (of a car).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to make up, then the German for to correct.

You'll want to know: die Zulassung.
die Zulassung — "the admission; the registration (of a car)".
Für die Zulassung zum Studium braucht man gute Noten. For admission to university you need good marks.
Plural: die Zulassungen. For a car, die Zulassung is its registration.

The word, taken apart.
Zulassung is the noun of zulassen, built on lassen "to let", inherited from Old High German lāzan and Proto-Germanic lētaną (EtymDB): being let in.

Lesson 2 of 5.
zulassen — to let in, to admit; to allow.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to correct, then the German for the admission.

You'll want to know: zulassen.
zulassen — "to let in, to admit; to allow".
Die Universität lässt jedes Jahr nur hundert Studenten zu. The university admits only a hundred students each year.
Separable and strong: sie lässt zu, sie hat zugelassen. Das Auto ist zugelassen — the car is registered.

The word, taken apart.
zulassen is built from zu and lassen "to let, to let go", inherited from Old High German lāzan and Proto-Germanic lētaną (EtymDB).

Lesson 3 of 5.
das Stipendium — the scholarship, the grant.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: das Stipendium.
das Stipendium — "the scholarship, the grant".
Mit einem Stipendium kann sie im Ausland studieren. With a scholarship she can study abroad.
Plural: die Stipendien.

The word, taken apart.
Stipendium was taken from Latin stīpendium "a tax, a soldier's pay" (Lewis & Short), from stips "a small coin" and pendere "to weigh out, to pay".

Lesson 4 of 5.
die Anmeldung — the registration; the reception desk.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to let in, then the German for the scholarship.

You'll want to know: die Anmeldung.
die Anmeldung — "the registration; the reception desk".
Die Anmeldung für die Sprachschule ist bis Freitag möglich. Registration for the language school is possible until Friday.
Plural: die Anmeldungen. At a doctor's surgery, die Anmeldung is the reception desk.

The word, taken apart.
Anmeldung is the noun of anmelden "to register", built on melden "to report, to announce", inherited from Old High German meldōn and Proto-Germanic melþōną "to divulge" (EtymDB).

Lesson 5 of 5.
abmelden — to withdraw, to deregister (often sich abmelden); to log off.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for the scholarship, then the German for the registration.

You'll want to know: abmelden.
abmelden — "to withdraw, to deregister (often sich abmelden); to log off".
Ich habe mich vom Tanzkurs abgemeldet, weil ich keine Zeit habe. I withdrew from the dance class because I have no time.
Separable, often reflexive: ich melde mich ab, ich habe mich abgemeldet. Also to log off a computer.

The word, taken apart.
abmelden is built from ab "off" and melden "to report", inherited from Old High German meldōn and Proto-Germanic melþōną (EtymDB): you report yourself off a list.
