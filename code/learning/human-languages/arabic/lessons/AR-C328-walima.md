## You'll want to know: وليمة
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C328-HAFL328-04]; assesses=[] -->

**وليمة** — *walīma* — "a banquet, a feast".

> طبخت النساء الطعام للوليمة.
>
> *ṭabakhat an-nisāʾ aṭ-ṭaʿām li-l-walīma.*
>
> The women cooked the food for the banquet.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**وليمة** (*walīma*) is built on the native Arabic root *w-l-m* (Lane), where *walīma* is first the feast made for a wedding, and then for any occasion.
