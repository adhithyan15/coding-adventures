## You'll want to know: ಸ್ಪಂದಿಸು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C502-SABHYA502-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸ್ಪಂದಿಸು** — *spandisu* — "to react; to respond".

> ನನ್ನ ಪತ್ರಕ್ಕೆ ಕಚೇರಿ ಬೇಗ ಸ್ಪಂದಿಸಿತು.
>
> *nanna patrakke kacēri bēga spandisitu.*
>
> The office responded quickly to my letter.

Past (I …): ಸ್ಪಂದಿಸಿದೆ, *spandiside*.

To what: -ಗೆ. Literally to throb in answer.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸ್ಪಂದಿಸು** (*spandisu*) is built on Sanskrit *spanda*, "a tremor, throbbing" (CDIAL 13805), with the verb ending -*isu*.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
