# (reading) — the first passage

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[RU-SKILL-READ-NOTICES] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Six words, then six lines. Now the whole thing at once.

[PAUSE 1s] Not one word in it is new.

## Reading
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-SKILL-CONNECTED-READING]; assesses=[RU-LEX-MENYA-ZOVUT, RU-LEX-I-01] -->

> Привет!
> Меня зовут Анна.
> Сестра и мама.
> Папа тоже.
> Я читаю.
> Я хочу читать.
> Вода, хлеб и чай.
> Кофе или чай?
> Я не знаю.
> Спасибо.
> До свидания.

[PAUSE 3s] Read it through once without stopping. Do not translate as you go.

[PAUSE 3s] Again — and this time notice that two lines differ by one word, and
that the one word changes when the reading happens.

## You'll want to know
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[RU-LEX-ILI-01, RU-LEX-TOZHE-01, RU-LEX-CHITAT] -->

[PAUSE 2s] **Я читаю** is now. **Я хочу читать** is not yet. Russian marks that
difference by changing the ending of the verb in the first and leaving it
whole in the second, which is the opposite of what an English speaker expects.

[PAUSE 2s] Look at **и** and **или** as well. One letter apart on the page, and
the difference between a list and a choice. Read quickly, that is the easiest
mistake on this page to make.

[PAUSE 2s] This is the first whole passage the book has asked you to hold at
once, and it works because every line was paid for.
