Punjabi, chapter 232: Putting Things Away.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ਸਜਾਉਣਾ (sajāuṇā) — to arrange neatly; to decorate.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for a hair tassel, then the Punjabi for a lock of hair.

You'll want to know: ਸਜਾਉਣਾ.
ਸਜਾਉਣਾ — sajāuṇā — "to arrange neatly; to decorate".
ਮੈਂ ਅਲਮਾਰੀ ਵਿੱਚ ਕਿਤਾਬਾਂ ਸਜਾਉਂਦੀ ਹਾਂ। maiṁ almārī vich kitābāṁ sajāundī hāṁ. I arrange the books neatly in the cupboard.
Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਕਿਤਾਬਾਂ ਸਜਾਈਆਂ, maiṁ kitābāṁ sajāīāṁ.

The word, taken apart.
ਸਜਾਉਣਾ (sajāuṇā) is inherited from Sanskrit sajjayati, "equips, prepares"; CDIAL lists Punjabi sajāuṇā, "to prepare" and Lahnda sajāvaṇ, "to adorn" (CDIAL 13091), but does not give the sense "arrange neatly".

Lesson 4 of 5.

Before the new one: say the Punjabi for to gather up, then the Punjabi for memory.

You'll want to know: ਉੱਧੜਨਾ.
ਉੱਧੜਨਾ — uddhaṛnā — "to come unstitched".
ਜੇ ਧਾਗਾ ਕਮਜ਼ੋਰ ਹੈ ਤਾਂ ਜੇਬ ਉੱਧੜੇਗੀ। je dhāgā kamzor hai tāṁ jeb uddhṛegī. If the thread is weak, the pocket will come unstitched.
Past (it …): ਉਹ ਜੇਬ ਉੱਧੜੀ, oh jeb uddhṛī.

The word, taken apart.
ਉੱਧੜਨਾ (uddhaṛnā) is inherited from Sanskrit uddharati, "takes out"; CDIAL lists Punjabi uddhaṛṇā, "to be ripped" (CDIAL 2009).
