## You'll want to know: jìniànpǐn
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C247-TRIP247-04]; assesses=[] -->

*jìniànpǐn* — "souvenir".

> *wǒ mǎile liǎng ge jìniànpǐn* — I bought two souvenirs.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*jìniànpǐn* is built from *jì* 'to record', *niàn* 'to think of' and *pǐn* 'an article', all inherited from Middle Chinese, *kiX*, *nemH* and *phimX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'to think' and the third 'a kind' and 'a rank'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for talking about the past.
