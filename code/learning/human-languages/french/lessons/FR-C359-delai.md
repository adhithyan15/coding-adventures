## You'll want to know: le délai
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C359-TACHE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**le délai** — "the deadline, the time allowed".

> Nous avons un délai de deux semaines pour finir.
>
> *We have two weeks to finish.*

A false friend: *un délai* is the time you have, not a delay.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*délai* comes from Old French *delai* (EtymDB), a noun formed on *delaier* "to put off". The further origin is not settled.
