Japanese, chapter 187: At the Bank.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
あずける (azukeru) — to deposit; to leave in someone's care.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for fee, then the Japanese for tax.

You'll want to know: あずける.
あずける — azukeru — "to deposit; to leave in someone's care".
ぎんこうに おかねを あずけます。 — ginkō ni okane o azukemasu — "I deposit money in the bank."
Polite あずけます, azukemasu; an ichidan verb.

The word, taken apart.
あずける (azukeru) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).

Lesson 3 of 5.
つうちょう (tsūchō) — bankbook; passbook.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for to deposit, then the Japanese for to withdraw.

You'll want to know: つうちょう.
つうちょう — tsūchō — "bankbook; passbook".
ぎんこうで つうちょうを だします。 — ginkō de tsūchō o dashimasu — "I hand in my bankbook at the bank."

The word, taken apart.
つうちょう (tsūchō) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are tsū, "pass through" (Middle Chinese thuwng) and chō, "notebook, account book" (trjangH) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).

Lesson 5 of 5.
りょうがえ (ryōgae) — money exchange; changing money.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for bankbook, then the Japanese for to borrow.

You'll want to know: りょうがえ.
りょうがえ — ryōgae — "money exchange; changing money".
ぎんこうで りょうがえを します。 — ginkō de ryōgae o shimasu — "I change money at the bank."

The word, taken apart.
りょうがえ (ryōgae) is built from Sino-Japanese ryō, an old Japanese coin, a reading borrowed from Chinese (Middle Chinese ljangX), and native kae, "exchange", from kaeru, "to exchange" (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
