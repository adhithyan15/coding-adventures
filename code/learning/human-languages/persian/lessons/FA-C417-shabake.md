## You'll want to know: شبکه
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C417-SHABAKE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**شبکه** (*shabake*) — "a network; a TV channel".

> این شبکه فیلمهای خوبی پخش میکند.
>
> *in shabake filmhâ-ye khubi pakhsh mikonad.*
>
> This channel shows good films.

Plural *shabakehâ*. A computer network too.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**شبکه** was borrowed from Arabic *shabaka* "a net" (EtymDB): a network is a net of links.
