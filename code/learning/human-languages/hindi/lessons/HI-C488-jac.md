## You'll want to know: जाँच
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C488-JANCH488-01]; assesses=[] -->

**जाँच** — *jā̃c* — "a check-up; a test".

> डाक्टर ने मीरा की आँखों की जाँच की।
>
> *ḍākṭar ne Mīrā kī ā̃khoṁ kī jā̃c kī.*
>
> The doctor examined Mira's eyes.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**जाँच** (*jā̃c*) is inherited: CDIAL lists Hindi *jā̃c*, "examination", under Sanskrit *yācñā*, "request, asking" (CDIAL 10448): asking questions of a thing. *jā̃c karnā* is to examine.
