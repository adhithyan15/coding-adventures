# കളിമണ്ണ് (kaḷimaṇṇŭ) — clay

## You'll want to know: കളിമണ്ണ്
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C566-VINODAM566-01]; assesses=[] -->

**കളിമണ്ണ്** — *kaḷimaṇṇŭ* — "clay".

> കുട്ടികൾ കളിമണ്ണ് കൊണ്ട് ചെറിയ പാത്രങ്ങൾ ഉണ്ടാക്കി.
>
> *kuṭṭikaḷ kaḷimaṇṇŭ koṇṭŭ ceṟiya pātraṅṅaḷ uṇṭākki.*
>
> The children made little pots out of clay.

The potter's earth; its second part is മണ്ണ്, earth.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**കളിമണ്ണ്** (*kaḷimaṇṇŭ*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 1381 gives the Malayalam compound itself as potter's clay, built on മണ്ണ്, "earth" (DEDR 4666).

## What you've built

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
