## You'll want to know: तीजो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C206-LANE206-01]; assesses=[] -->

**तीजो** — *tījo* — "third".

> तीजो घर म्हारो है।
>
> *tījo ghar mhāro hai.*
>
> The third house is mine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**तीजो** (*tījo*) is inherited from Sanskrit *tṛtīya*, "third". CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry, but lists Hindi *tījā*, "third" (CDIAL 5912).
