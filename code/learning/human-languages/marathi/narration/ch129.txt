Marathi, chapter 129: Library, Restaurant, Post, Building, Bathroom.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ग्रंथालय (granthālay) — a library.

Warm-up.

Before the new one: say the Marathi for an airport, then the Marathi for a hospital.
