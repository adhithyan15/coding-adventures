## You'll want to know: ののしる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C430-ANGER430-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ののしる** — *nonoshiru* — "to curse at; to shout abuse at".

> **ともだちを ののしらないで ください。** — *tomodachi o nonoshiranaide kudasai* — "Please do not swear at your friends."

Polite **ののしります**, *nonoshirimasu*; a godan verb. Past (polite): **かれが てんいんを ののしりました。**, *kare ga ten'in o nonoshirimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ののしる** (*nonoshiru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji spelling was attached to it later (JMdict).
