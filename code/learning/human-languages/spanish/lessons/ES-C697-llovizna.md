## You'll want to know: la llovizna
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C697-LLUVIA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**la llovizna** — "the drizzle".

> Con esta llovizna no hace falta paraguas.
>
> *You do not need an umbrella in this drizzle.*

Light, fine rain.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*llovizna* is built on *lloviznar* "to drizzle", which comes from *llover* "to rain", Latin *pluere* (EtymDB).
