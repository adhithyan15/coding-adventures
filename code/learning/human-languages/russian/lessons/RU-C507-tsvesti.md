## You'll want to know: цвести
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C507-BLOSSOM-02]; assesses=[] -->

**цвести** (*tsvestí*) — "to bloom".

> В мае в нашем саду цветёт вишня.
>
> *V máye v náshem sadú tsvetyót víshnya.*
>
> In May the cherry tree blossoms in our garden.

Imperfective; present цветёт, цветут (ст becomes т). Perfective расцвести.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*цвести* is inherited from Proto-Slavic *kvisti* (EtymDB), the root also behind *цветок* 'flower'.
