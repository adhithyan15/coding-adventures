# পৌরসভা (pourôsôbhā) — a municipality; a town council

## You'll want to know: পৌরসভা
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C347-DOPTOR347-02]; assesses=[] -->

**পৌরসভা** — *pourôsôbhā* — "a municipality; a town council".

> পৌরসভা পাড়ার রাস্তায় নতুন বাতি লাগাল।
>
> *pourôsôbhā pāṛār rāstāy nôtun bāti lāgālô.*
>
> The town council put up new lamps in the neighbourhood's streets.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**পৌরসভা** (*pourôsôbhā*) is Sanskrit *paura*, a townsman (Monier-Williams), with সভা, an assembly: the town's council.
