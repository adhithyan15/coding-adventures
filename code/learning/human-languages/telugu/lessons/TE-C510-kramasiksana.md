## You'll want to know: క్రమశిక్షణ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C510-GUNAM510-03]; assesses=[] -->

**క్రమశిక్షణ** — *kramaśikṣaṇa* — "discipline".

> ఆ బడిలో క్రమశిక్షణ చాలా ముఖ్యం.
>
> *ā baḍilō kramaśikṣaṇa cālā mukhyaṁ.*
>
> Discipline matters a great deal at that school.

క్రమం, order or regularity, + a word for training.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**క్రమశిక్షణ** (*kramaśikṣaṇa*) is a learned compound built from Sanskrit *krama*, "step, series" (CDIAL 3577), and *śikṣaṇa*, "teaching" (CDIAL 12429): training in order.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
