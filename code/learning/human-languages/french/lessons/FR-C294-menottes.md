## You'll want to know: les menottes
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C294-ARRESTATION-03]; assesses=[] -->

**les menottes** — "the handcuffs".

> Le policier lui a mis les menottes.
>
> *The police officer put the handcuffs on him.*

Literally 'little hands', from *main*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*menottes* is a diminutive of *main* "hand", which is inherited from Latin *manus* "hand" (Lewis & Short).
