## You'll want to know: grosseiro
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C473-MANEIRAS-03]; assesses=[] -->

**grosseiro** — "rude, coarse".

> O empregado foi grosseiro com os clientes.
>
> *The waiter was rude to the customers.*

Of cloth or work: rough, badly made.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*grosseiro* is built on *grosso* "thick, coarse", which is inherited from Late Latin *grossus* "thick" (EtymDB).
