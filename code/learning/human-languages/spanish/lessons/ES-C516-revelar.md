## You'll want to know: revelar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C516-VERSION-03]; assesses=[] -->

**revelar** — "to reveal; to develop (a photo)".

> El periódico reveló el nombre del ladrón.
>
> *The newspaper revealed the thief's name.*

With old film, *revelar las fotos* is to develop the photos.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*revelar* is a learned borrowing from Latin *revēlāre* 'to draw back the veil' (EtymDB), built on *re-* and *vēlum* 'a cloth, a veil'. To reveal is to pull the cloth away.
