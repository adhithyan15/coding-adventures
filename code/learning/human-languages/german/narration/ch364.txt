German, chapter 364: Unter einer Bedingung.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
die Bedingung — the condition.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to recycle, then the German for to consume.

You'll want to know: die Bedingung.
die Bedingung — "the condition".
Ich komme mit, unter einer Bedingung: Du bezahlst das Taxi. I will come along on one condition: you pay for the taxi.
Plural: die Bedingungen. Unter der Bedingung, dass … — on condition that …

The word, taken apart.
Bedingung is the noun of bedingen "to stipulate", inherited from Middle High German bedingen "to hire, to engage" (EtymDB). The verb is usually explained as built on Ding in its old sense of a court or assembly, Old High German ding, Proto-Germanic þingą (EtymDB): terms agreed before the assembly.

Lesson 4 of 5.
sofern — provided that, as long as.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for the precondition, then the German for to require.

You'll want to know: sofern.
sofern — "provided that, as long as".
Wir grillen am Samstag, sofern das Wetter gut ist. We are having a barbecue on Saturday, provided the weather is good.
Sofern sends the verb to the end, like wenn: …, sofern das Wetter gut ist. It sounds a little more formal.

The word, taken apart.
sofern joins so and fern "far", from Old High German ferrana (EtymDB): "so far as", to the extent that something holds.

Lesson 5 of 5.
soweit — as far as.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to require, then the German for provided that.

You'll want to know: soweit.
soweit — "as far as".
Soweit ich weiß, ist das Geschäft am Montag geschlossen. As far as I know, the shop is closed on Monday.
Soweit sends the verb to the end: soweit ich weiß, soweit ich mich erinnere.

The word, taken apart.
soweit joins so and weit "wide, far", inherited from Middle High German wīt, Old High German wīt and Proto-Germanic wīdaz (EtymDB), a cousin of English wide.
