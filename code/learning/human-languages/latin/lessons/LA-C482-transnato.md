## You'll want to know: trānsnatō, trānsnatāre
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C482-AMNIS-05]; assesses=[] -->

**trānsnatō, trānsnatāre** — "to swim across".

> Mīlitēs flūmen trānsnatant, quod pōns nūllus est.
>
> *The soldiers swim across the river, because there is no bridge.*

*trānsnatō, trānsnatāre, trānsnatāvī*, also *trānatō*, with an accusative.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*trānsnatō* is built from *trāns* 'across' and *natō* 'to swim' (Lewis & Short).
