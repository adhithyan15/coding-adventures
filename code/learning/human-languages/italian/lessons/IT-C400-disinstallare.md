## You'll want to know: disinstallare
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C400-INSTALLARE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**disinstallare** — "to uninstall".

> Se un'app non ti serve, disinstallala.
>
> *If you do not need an app, uninstall it.*

*Dis-* reverses the verb.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*disinstallare* is built from *dis-* "un-" and *installare*, which was borrowed from French *installer*, from Medieval Latin *installāre* "to put in a stall", on a Germanic word for a place; compare Old High German *stal* (EtymDB).
