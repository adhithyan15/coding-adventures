## You'll want to know: صاف کردن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C359-ZAFARAN-03]; assesses=[] -->

**صاف کردن** (*sâf kardan*) — "to strain, to filter; to smooth".

> چای را صاف کرد و در لیوان ریخت.
>
> *Chây râ sâf kard va dar livân rikht.*
>
> She strained the tea and poured it into a glass.

Also "to smooth, to straighten". Present *sâf mikonad*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*sâf* "clear, smooth" is borrowed from Arabic *ṣāfī*, "pure" (EtymDB). *kardan* "to do" is inherited Persian: to make clear.
