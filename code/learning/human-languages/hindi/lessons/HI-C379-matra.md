## You'll want to know: मात्र
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C379-YADI379-03]; assesses=[] -->

**मात्र** — *mātra* — "mere, only (formal)".

> यह मात्र एक सवाल है, अगर जवाब नहीं पता तो कोई बात नहीं।
>
> *yah mātra ek savāl hai, agar javāb nahīṃ patā to koī bāt nahīṃ.*
>
> It's only a question; if you don't know the answer, never mind.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**मात्र** (*mātra*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *mātra*, "a measure; nothing but" (Monier-Williams).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
