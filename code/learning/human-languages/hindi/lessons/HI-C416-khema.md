## You'll want to know: ख़ेमा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C416-KHEMA416-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ख़ेमा** — *khemā* — "a tent (for camping)".

> नदी के किनारे हमारा ख़ेमा था।
>
> *nadī ke kināre hamārā khemā thā.*
>
> Our tent was on the river bank.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ख़ेमा** (*khemā*) is usually traced to Arabic *khayma*, "a tent", which came through Persian; the diphthong has become a plain e.
