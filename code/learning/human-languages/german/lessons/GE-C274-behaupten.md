## You'll want to know: behaupten
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C274-GERUECHT-03]; assesses=[] -->

**behaupten** — "to claim, to assert".

> Er behauptete, dass er nichts gesehen hatte.
>
> *He claimed that he had seen nothing.*

Often with *dass* and the verb at the end. Reflexive, *sich behaupten* means to hold one's own.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*behaupten* is usually traced to Middle High German *behoubeten* "to hold one's ground", built on *Haupt* "head", a cousin of English *head* (EtymDB).
