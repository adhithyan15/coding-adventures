French, chapter 185: Muscle, Thumb, Wound, Scar, Cold.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
le muscle — a muscle.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for to disappear, then the French for to borrow.

You'll want to know: le muscle.
le muscle — "a muscle".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: le muscle]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: le muscle, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say le muscle]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the French for to disappear, then the French for to borrow, then say le muscle again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does le muscle mean? ("A muscle".) Say it once more.
