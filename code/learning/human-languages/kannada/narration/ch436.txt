Kannada, chapter 436: Porters and Trolleys.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಹಮಾಲಿ (hamāli) — a porter.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to peep, then the Kannada for a fare.

You'll want to know: ಹಮಾಲಿ.
ಹಮಾಲಿ — hamāli — "a porter".
ಹಮಾಲಿ ನನ್ನ ಎರಡು ಸೂಟ್ಕೇಸ್ಗಳನ್ನು ಹೊತ್ತನು. hamāli nanna eraḍu sūṭkēsgaḷannu hottanu. The porter carried my two suitcases.
Plural: ಹಮಾಲಿಗಳು, hamāligaḷu.
At stations porters wear a red shirt and a numbered badge; ಕೂಲಿ also names them.

The word, taken apart.
ಹಮಾಲಿ (hamāli) is usually traced to Arabic ḥammāl, "a carrier, a porter", which reached Kannada through Hindustani or Marathi.

Lesson 3 of 5.
ಹೇರು (hēru) — to load; to impose.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a porter, then the Kannada for a trolley.

You'll want to know: ಹೇರು.
ಹೇರು — hēru — "to load; to impose".
ಅವರು ವಾಹನದ ಮೇಲೆ ಸಾಮಾನು ಹೇರಿದರು. avaru vāhanada mēle sāmānu hēridaru. They loaded the luggage on top of the vehicle.
Past (I …): ಹೇರಿದೆ, hēride.
What is loaded takes -ಅನ್ನು or no ending; figuratively, ತೆರಿಗೆ ಹೇರು (hēru), to impose a tax.

The word, taken apart.
ಹೇರು (hēru) is native Dravidian: DEDR 4446 lists Kannada pēṟu, "to lift up and put on, to load; a load", the older form with the p- that modern Kannada turns into h-. The same article holds Telugu pērcu, to pile up.

Lesson 4 of 5.
ಕೈಸಾಮಾನು (kaisāmānu) — hand luggage.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a trolley, then the Kannada for to load.

You'll want to know: ಕೈಸಾಮಾನು.
ಕೈಸಾಮಾನು — kaisāmānu — "hand luggage".
ಕೈಸಾಮಾನು ಏಳು ಕಿಲೋಗಿಂತ ಹೆಚ್ಚು ಇರಬಾರದು. kaisāmānu ēḷu kilōginta heccu irabāradu. Hand luggage must not weigh more than seven kilos.
ಕೈ, hand, plus ಸಾಮಾನು, luggage: the bag you keep with you on a plane or a bus.

The word, taken apart.
ಕೈಸಾಮಾನು (kaisāmānu) is a Kannada compound of native kai, "hand", and ಸಾಮಾನು, "luggage, things", which is usually traced to Persian sāmān, "goods, equipment".

Lesson 5 of 5.
ಭರ್ತಿಯಾಗು (bhartiyāgu) — to fill up; to be full.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to load, then the Kannada for hand luggage.

You'll want to know: ಭರ್ತಿಯಾಗು.
ಭರ್ತಿಯಾಗು — bhartiyāgu — "to fill up; to be full".
ಹಬ್ಬದ ಕಾರಣ ಎಲ್ಲಾ ಬಸ್ಸುಗಳು ಭರ್ತಿಯಾದವು. habbada kāraṇa ellā bassugaḷu bhartiyādavu. Because of the festival all the buses filled up.
Past (it …): ಭರ್ತಿಯಾಯಿತು, bhartiyāyitu.
ಭರ್ತಿ alone means full or filled; ಭರ್ತಿ ಮಾಡು is to fill in, as a form.

The word, taken apart.
ಭರ್ತಿಯಾಗು (bhartiyāgu) joins bharti, "full, filled", usually traced to Hindustani bhartī, "filling, recruitment", and the native verb ಆಗು, to become.
