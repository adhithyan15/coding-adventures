Mandarin Chinese, chapter 184: Holiday Plans.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
dùjià — to go on holiday; to spend a holiday.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for to lock up, then the Mandarin for to lie down.

You'll want to know: dùjià.
dùjià — "to go on holiday; to spend a holiday".
xià ge yuè wǒmen qù Běijīng dùjià — Next month we're going on holiday to Beijing.
Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
dùjià is built from dù and jià 'leave', both inherited from Middle Chinese, duH and kaeH (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription). The dictionary glosses the second 'leave from duty' and the first 'a rule'; 'to spend time' is not given there.

What you've built.
One more word for talking about plans.

Lesson 2 of 5.
juédìng — to decide; a decision.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for to lie down, then the Mandarin for to go on holiday.

You'll want to know: juédìng.
juédìng — "to decide; a decision".
wǒmen hái méi juédìng qù nǎr — We haven't decided where to go yet.
Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
juédìng is built from jué and dìng, both inherited from Middle Chinese, kwet and dengH (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription). The dictionary glosses the first 'to cut off' and 'to break', and the second 'settled': to cut off and settle.

lǎojiā — "home town; family home".

Lesson 5 of 5.
fàngsōng — to relax; to loosen.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: fàngsōng.
fàngsōng — "to relax; to loosen".

The word, taken apart.
fàngsōng is built from fàng 'to let go' and sōng 'loose', both inherited from Middle Chinese, pjangH and sowng (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second as hair hanging loose.
