Spanish, chapter 563: Contar una historia.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
la fábula — the fable.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for to burst out, then the Spanish for to move away.

You'll want to know: la fábula.
la fábula — "the fable".
En la fábula, la tortuga le gana a la liebre. In the fable, the tortoise beats the hare.
La moraleja is the moral at the end.

The word, taken apart.
fábula is a learned borrowing from Latin fābula 'a story, a tale' (EtymDB), built on fārī 'to speak' (Lewis & Short). The inherited form of the same word is habla.

What you've built.
One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.

[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
