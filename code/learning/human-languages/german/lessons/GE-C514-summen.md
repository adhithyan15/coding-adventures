## You'll want to know: summen
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C514-MELODIE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**summen** — "to hum; to buzz".

> Beim Kochen summt meine Mutter ein Lied.
>
> *My mother hums a song while she cooks.*

Also of bees: *Die Bienen summen.*

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*summen* is inherited from Middle High German *summen* (EtymDB), an imitative word for a humming sound.
