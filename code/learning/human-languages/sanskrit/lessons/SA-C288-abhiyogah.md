## You'll want to know: अभियोगः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C288-APARADHA288-03]; assesses=[] -->

**अभियोगः** — *abhiyogaḥ* — "a charge; an accusation".

> अभियोगः केवलं सन्देहाधीनः।
>
> *abhiyogaḥ kevalaṃ sandehādhīnaḥ.*
>
> The charge rests only on suspicion.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अभियोगः** (*abhiyogaḥ*) is built from *abhi*, "towards, against", and the root √yuj, "to join"; MW gives *abhiyoga* as an effort, an attack and, in law, a charge or accusation (MW).
