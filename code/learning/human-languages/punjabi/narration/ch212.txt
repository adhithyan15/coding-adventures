Punjabi, chapter 212: I Don't Drink Tea.
5 lessons. You can do the first one in the car; after that you will want to have stopped.


Lesson 1 of 5.
Return to a familiar no — retrieve no and the familiar I did not understand by ear.

Warm-up.
[your turn — hear: nahīṁ]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
Say ਨਹੀਂ. Then the whole sentence you have used since the early lessons: ਮੈਨੂੰ ਸਮਝ ਨਹੀਂ ਆਈ, "I did not understand".

Grammar Lens.
You learned that ਨਹੀਂ goes directly before the verb and that nothing else in the sentence moves. Hold that idea next to a sentence you can say now: ਮੈਂ ਚਾਹ ਪੀਂਦਾ ਹਾਂ.

Guided Practice.
[your turn — hear: no]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
Say ਨਹੀਂ.
[your turn — hear: I did not understand]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
Say ਮੈਨੂੰ ਸਮਝ ਨਹੀਂ ਆਈ.
[your turn — hear: I drink tea, as a man]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
Say ਮੈਂ ਚਾਹ ਪੀਂਦਾ ਹਾਂ.

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
Say no, then I drink tea. The next lesson joins them.


Lesson 2 of 5.
ਮੈਂ ਚਾਹ ਨਹੀਂ ਪੀਂਦਾ ਹਾਂ (nahīṁ pīndā hāṁ) — I don't drink tea.

Before we start: this one needs your eyes, so it is not fully a driving lesson. You will want your eyes or your hands for the steps that ask you to read, point, write or gesture. You can listen to everything else now. I will say so again when we reach it, but leave this section until you have stopped: Guided Practice.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say I drink tea every day, with the ending that fits you.

Grammar Lens: ਨਹੀਂ before the verb.
Put ਨਹੀਂ directly before ਪੀਂਦਾ. Everything else stays where it was, the helper ਹਾਂ included:
ਮੈਂ ਚਾਹ ਨਹੀਂ ਪੀਂਦਾ ਹਾਂ। — maiṁ chāh nahīṁ pīndā hāṁ. — I don't drink tea (a man speaking).
ਮੈਂ ਚਾਹ ਨਹੀਂ ਪੀਂਦੀ ਹਾਂ। — maiṁ chāh nahīṁ pīndī hāṁ. — I don't drink tea (a woman speaking).
ਉਹ ਰੋਟੀ ਨਹੀਂ ਖਾਂਦਾ ਹੈ। — uh roṭī nahīṁ khāndā hai. — He doesn't eat roti.
This book keeps the helper after ਨਹੀਂ in every sentence it prints.

[once you have stopped driving — this part needs your eyes or your hands; if you are driving, skip ahead to the next part]
Guided Practice.
[your turn — hear: I don't drink milk, as a woman]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
Say ਮੈਂ ਦੁੱਧ ਨਹੀਂ ਪੀਂਦੀ ਹਾਂ।.
[your turn — hear: he doesn't go to school]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
Say ਉਹ ਸਕੂਲ ਨਹੀਂ ਜਾਂਦਾ ਹੈ।.
[once you have stopped driving — read: ਮੈਂ ਚਾਹ ਨਹੀਂ ਪੀਂਦਾ ਹਾਂ। (maiṁ chāh nahīṁ pīndā hāṁ.)]
Point to ਨਹੀਂ, then to the verb after it.
[once you have stopped driving — write: copy ਨਹੀਂ ਪੀਂਦਾ ਹਾਂ (nahīṁ pīndā hāṁ) once]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
Where does ਨਹੀਂ go? (Directly before the verb.) Does ਹਾਂ stay? (Yes.)

Lesson 4 of 5.
ਬਾਗ਼ ਖੁੱਲ੍ਹਾ ਨਹੀਂ ਹੈ — the garden is not open.

Before we start: this one needs your eyes, so it is not fully a driving lesson. You will want your eyes or your hands for the steps that ask you to read, point, write or gesture. You can listen to everything else now. I will say so again when we reach it, but leave this section until you have stopped: Guided Practice.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say the garden is open in the morning, then the garden is closed. Then I don't drink tea.

Grammar Lens: when ਹੈ is the verb.
In ਬਾਗ਼ ਖੁੱਲ੍ਹਾ ਹੈ the verb is ਹੈ itself. So ਨਹੀਂ goes directly before ਹੈ:
ਬਾਗ਼ ਖੁੱਲ੍ਹਾ ਨਹੀਂ ਹੈ। — bāgh khullhā nahīṁ hai. — The garden is not open.
ਬਾਗ਼ ਬੰਦ ਨਹੀਂ ਹੈ। — bāgh band nahīṁ hai. — The garden is not closed.
ਉਹ ਘਰ ਵਿੱਚ ਨਹੀਂ ਹੈ। — uh ghar vichch nahīṁ hai. — He is not at home.

[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

[once you have stopped driving — read: ਬਾਗ਼ ਖੁੱਲ੍ਹਾ ਨਹੀਂ ਹੈ। (bāgh khullhā nahīṁ hai.)]
