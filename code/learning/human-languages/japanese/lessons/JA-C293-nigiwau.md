## You'll want to know: にぎわう
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C293-HANABI293-05]; assesses=[] -->

**にぎわう** — *nigiwau* — "to bustle; to be lively with people".

> **まつりの とき、まちは にぎわいます。** — *matsuri no toki, machi wa nigiwaimasu* — "At festival time the town is bustling."

Polite **にぎわいます**, *nigiwaimasu*; a godan verb. Past (polite): **まちが にぎわいました。**, *machi ga nigiwaimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**にぎわう** (*nigiwau*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict).
