## You'll want to know: あたらしい
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C290-UTSURU290-05]; assesses=[] -->

**あたらしい** — *atarashii* — "new".

> **あたらしい うちは えきに ちかいです。** — *atarashii uchi wa eki ni chikai desu* — "The new house is close to the station."

An i-adjective.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**あたらしい** (*atarashii*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict).
