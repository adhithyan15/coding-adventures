## You'll want to know: மெச்சு
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C473-AZHAKU473-05]; assesses=[] -->

**மெச்சு** — *meccu* — "to admire; to commend".

> அவனுடைய நேர்மையை எல்லோரும் மெச்சினார்கள்.
>
> *avaṉuṭaiya nērmaiyai ellōrum mecciṉārkaḷ.*
>
> Everyone admired his honesty.

Past (I …): மெச்சினேன், *mecciṉēṉ*.

The quality admired takes -ஐ.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**மெச்சு** (*meccu*) is native Dravidian (DEDR 4722), where it means to praise and to admire greatly.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
