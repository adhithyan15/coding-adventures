# iūcunditās, iūcunditātis — pleasantness, delight

## You'll want to know: iūcunditās, iūcunditātis
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C489-GAUD-05]; assesses=[] -->

**iūcunditās, iūcunditātis** — "pleasantness, delight".

> Iūcunditātem hortī nostrī omnēs amīcī laudant.
>
> *All our friends praise the pleasantness of our garden.*

Feminine, third declension, built on *iūcundus* — pleasant.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**iūcunditās** is built on *iūcundus* "pleasant, agreeable" with the noun ending *-itās* (Lewis & Short, under *jucunditas*); Lewis & Short links *iūcundus* with *iocus* "a joke", but its origin is not settled.
