## You'll want to know: ひので
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C416-YOAKE416-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ひので** — *hinode* — "sunrise".

> **やまの うえで ひのでを まちました。** — *yama no ue de hinode o machimashita* — "We waited for the sunrise on the mountain."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ひので** (*hinode*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, built from *hi*, "sun", the particle *no* and *de*, "coming out" (from *deru*); its kanji were attached later as kun'yomi (JMdict).
