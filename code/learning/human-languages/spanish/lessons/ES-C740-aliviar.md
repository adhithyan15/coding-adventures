## You'll want to know: aliviar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C740-MAREO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**aliviar** — "to relieve, to ease".

> Esta pastilla alivia el dolor.
>
> *This tablet relieves the pain.*

*El alivio* is the relief.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*aliviar* is inherited from Latin *alleviāre* "to lighten" (EtymDB), built on *levis* "light".
