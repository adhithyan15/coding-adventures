## You'll want to know: هاجم
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C339-GHAZW339-02]; assesses=[] -->

**هاجم** — *hājama* — "to attack".

> هاجم الكلب الرجل في الحديقة.
>
> *hājama al-kalb ar-rajul fī al-ḥadīqa.*
>
> The dog attacked the man in the garden.

Present: يهاجم, *yuhājim*.

In sport an attacker is مهاجم.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**هاجم** (*hājama*) is built on the native Arabic root *h-j-m* (Lane), "to come upon someone suddenly". It is Form III, "to rush upon an enemy".
