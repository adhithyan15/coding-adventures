## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**മത്സരം** (*matsaraṁ*) ← Sanskrit *matsara*, "jealous, rivalrous", taken over whole as a tatsama, with Malayalam's noun ending *-ṁ* (CDIAL 9756). Rivalry became a contest.
