## You'll want to know

> *kitṇo ākhrī bhāv doge?* — **what final price will you give?**

Four words, four of them yours in some form. *kitṇo* asks, *ākhrī* closes,
*bhāv* is the price you have been able to name since Chapter 18, and *doge* is
the *do* of the last lesson with an ending that puts it in the future: not
*give*, but *will you give*.

Learn the line as one heard shape. The ending on *doge* is real Marwadi
machinery and it gets its own lesson later; taking it whole now costs nothing,
because *do* has already been taught on its own.
