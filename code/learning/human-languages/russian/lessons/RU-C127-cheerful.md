**весёлый** (*vesyólyy*) — "cheerful, fun".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**весёлый** ← Proto-Slavic *\*veselŭ* — inherited. Vasmer relates it to Latvian *vesels*, "healthy, whole", and Sanskrit *vásu-*, "good".
