## You'll want to know: хоронить
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C309-POKHORONY-02]; assesses=[] -->

**хоронить** (*khoronít*) — "to bury".

> Его похоронили рядом с женой.
>
> *Yevó pokhoroníli ryádom s zhenóy.*
>
> He was buried next to his wife.

Imperfective; perfective *похоронить* (*pokhoronít*).

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*хоронить* is an inherited Slavic verb whose older sense was "to keep safe, to hide away"; EtymDB lists no parent, and it is usually traced to Proto-Slavic *xorniti*.
