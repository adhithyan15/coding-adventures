Punjabi, chapter 218: I Got Up Early.
5 lessons. You can do the first one in the car; after that you will want to have stopped.


Lesson 1 of 5.
Return to getting up and sitting — retrieve to get up, to sit and came by ear.

Warm-up.
[your turn — hear: uṭṭhṇā; baiṭhṇā]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
Say ਉੱਠਣਾ, ਬੈਠਣਾ. Then Aman came yesterday.

Grammar Lens.
You already say ਮੈਂ ਅਮਨ ਨੂੰ ਮਿਲਿਆ, "I met Aman". Listen to its end: -iā, the same sound that ends ਆਇਆ. The next lesson gives that ending to getting up and sitting down.

Guided Practice.
[your turn — hear: to get up]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
Say ਉੱਠਣਾ.
[your turn — hear: to sit]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
Say ਬੈਠਣਾ.
[your turn — hear: I met Aman]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
Say ਮੈਂ ਅਮਨ ਨੂੰ ਮਿਲਿਆ.

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
Say please get up, then please sit.


Lesson 2 of 5.
ਉੱਠਿਆ, ਬੈਠਿਆ — got up, sat down.

Before we start: this one needs your eyes, so it is not fully a driving lesson. You will want your eyes or your hands for the steps that ask you to read, point, write or gesture. You can listen to everything else now. I will say so again when we reach it, but leave this section until you have stopped: Guided Practice.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say to get up, to sit, then I met Aman.

Grammar Lens: stem + ਿਆ, stem + ੀ.
Most stems make their past by adding ਿਆ for a man and ੀ for a woman:
ਉੱਠ + ਿਆ becomes ਉੱਠਿਆ, ਉੱਠ + ੀ becomes ਉੱਠੀ.
ਮੈਂ ਸਵੇਰੇ ਜਲਦੀ ਉੱਠਿਆ। — maiṁ savere jaldī uṭṭhiā. — I got up early in the morning (a man speaking).
ਮੈਂ ਸਵੇਰੇ ਜਲਦੀ ਉੱਠੀ। — maiṁ savere jaldī uṭṭhī. — I got up early in the morning (a woman speaking).
ਉਹ ਇੱਥੇ ਬੈਠਿਆ। — uh itthe baiṭhiā. — He sat here.
Getting up and sitting are done by you, not to anything, so the ending agrees with you.

[once you have stopped driving — this part needs your eyes or your hands; if you are driving, skip ahead to the next part]
Guided Practice.
[your turn — hear: she sat here]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
Say ਉਹ ਇੱਥੇ ਬੈਠੀ।.
[your turn — hear: Aman got up early]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
Say ਅਮਨ ਜਲਦੀ ਉੱਠਿਆ।.
[once you have stopped driving — read: ਉੱਠਿਆ]
Point to ਿਆ after the stem.
[once you have stopped driving — write: copy ਉੱਠਿਆ and ਉੱਠੀ once]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
Got up, for a man? (ਉੱਠਿਆ.) For a woman? (ਉੱਠੀ.)

Lesson 4 of 5.
ਮੈਂ ਨਹੀਂ ਗਿਆ — I didn't go.

Before we start: this one needs your eyes, so it is not fully a driving lesson. You will want your eyes or your hands for the steps that ask you to read, point, write or gesture. You can listen to everything else now. I will say so again when we reach it, but leave this section until you have stopped: Guided Practice.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say I don't drink tea. Then Aman went to school.

Grammar Lens: ਨਹੀਂ before the past.
ਨਹੀਂ goes before the past form, as before any verb. A past like this has no helper to keep:
ਮੈਂ ਕੱਲ੍ਹ ਸਕੂਲ ਨਹੀਂ ਗਿਆ। — maiṁ kallh sakūl nahīṁ giā. — I didn't go to school yesterday (a man speaking).
ਮੈਂ ਕੱਲ੍ਹ ਸਕੂਲ ਨਹੀਂ ਗਈ। — maiṁ kallh sakūl nahīṁ gaī. — I didn't go to school yesterday (a woman speaking).
ਉਹ ਜਲਦੀ ਨਹੀਂ ਉੱਠਿਆ। — uh jaldī nahīṁ uṭṭhiā. — He didn't get up early.

[once you have stopped driving — this part needs your eyes or your hands; if you are driving, skip ahead to the next part]
Guided Practice.
[your turn — hear: Aman didn't come yesterday]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
Say ਅਮਨ ਕੱਲ੍ਹ ਨਹੀਂ ਆਇਆ।.
[your turn — hear: she didn't sit]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
Say ਉਹ ਨਹੀਂ ਬੈਠੀ।.
[once you have stopped driving — read: ਮੈਂ ਕੱਲ੍ਹ ਸਕੂਲ ਨਹੀਂ ਗਿਆ।]
Point to ਨਹੀਂ, then to the past form.
[once you have stopped driving — write: copy ਨਹੀਂ ਗਿਆ once]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
Say one place you went yesterday, and one place you didn't.
