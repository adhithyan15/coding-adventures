Arabic, chapter 230: To bark, To feed, To add, To divide, To kick.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
نبح (nabaḥa) — to bark.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to knead, then the Arabic for to wipe.
