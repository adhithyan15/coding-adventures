Lesson 1 of 5.
la salade — a salad; lettuce.

Warm-up.

You'll want to know: la salade.
la salade — "a salad; lettuce".

The word, taken apart.
salade from Italian salata, "salted", that is, salted greens (EtymDB 2.1) — borrowed; behind it is Latin sal, "salt".

Lesson 2 of 5.
les pâtes — pasta.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for ham, then the French for a salad; lettuce.

You'll want to know: les pâtes.
les pâtes — "pasta".

The word, taken apart.
pâtes is the plural of the French word for "dough, paste", which from Old French paste from Late Latin pasta, "dough", from Greek pasta, "barley porridge" — inherited. (EtymDB 2.1) The circumflex marks the lost s.

Lesson 3 of 5.
la crème — cream.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for a salad; lettuce, then the French for pasta.

You'll want to know: la crème.
la crème — "cream".

The word, taken apart.
crème from Old French cresme from Late Latin crama, "cream", probably a Gaulish word (EtymDB 2.1); its spelling was later pulled toward Church Latin chrisma, "anointing oil".

Lesson 4 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for pasta, then the French for cream.

You'll want to know: le poivre.
le poivre — "pepper".

The word, taken apart.
poivre from Old French peivre from Latin piper, from Greek peperi — inherited. (EtymDB 2.1) The word travelled with the spice from India: Sanskrit has pippali, "long pepper".
