# पाठपुरावा करणे (pāṭhpurāvā karṇe) — to follow up; to pursue

## You'll want to know: पाठपुरावा करणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C393-SABAB393-05]; assesses=[] -->

**पाठपुरावा करणे** — *pāṭhpurāvā karṇe* — "to follow up; to pursue".

> तक्रारीचा पाठपुरावा केल्यावर काम झालं.
>
> *takrārīcā pāṭhpurāvā kelyāvar kām jhālaṁ.*
>
> After the complaint was followed up, the work got done.

Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने पाठपुरावा केला, *tyāne pāṭhpurāvā kelā*.

What is pursued takes -चा: तक्रारीचा पाठपुरावा.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पाठपुरावा करणे** (*pāṭhpurāvā karṇe*) joins पाठपुरावा, "pursuit, follow-up", a Marathi noun whose origin is not settled, to करणे, "to do".
