## You'll want to know: چھپر
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C413-KOTHI413-04]; assesses=[] -->

**چھپر** — *chhappar* — "a thatched roof".

> جھونپڑی کا چھپر بارش میں ٹپکتا ہے۔
>
> *jhoṅpṛī kā chhappar bārish meṅ ṭapaktā hai.*
>
> The hut's thatch leaks in the rain.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**چھپر** (*chhappar*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *chappar*, "a sloping thatch, a hut", under Sanskrit *chattvara* (CDIAL 4976).
