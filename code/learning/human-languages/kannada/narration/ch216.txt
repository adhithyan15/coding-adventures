Kannada, chapter 216: Success, Freedom, Justice, Responsibility, Duty.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಯಶಸ್ಸು (yaśassu) — success.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a tradition, then the Kannada for culture.

You'll want to know: ಯಶಸ್ಸು.
ಯಶಸ್ಸು — yaśassu — "success".

The word, taken apart.
ಯಶಸ್ಸು (yaśassu) is Sanskrit yaśas, "splendour, renown", taken over whole as a learned loan, a tatsama, with Kannada's added -u (CDIAL 10443). The final -s is doubled before Kannada's -u.
