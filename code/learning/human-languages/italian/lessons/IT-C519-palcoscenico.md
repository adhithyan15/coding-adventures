## You'll want to know: il palcoscenico
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C519-TEATRO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**il palcoscenico** — "stage".

> Gli attori salgono sul palcoscenico.
>
> *The actors go up on stage.*

Often shortened to *il palco*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*palcoscenico* is built from *palco* "platform", usually traced to a Germanic word for a beam (EtymDB lists it beside German *Balken*), and *scenico* "of the stage", from Latin *scaena*, from Greek *skēnḗ* "a tent, a stage" (EtymDB).
