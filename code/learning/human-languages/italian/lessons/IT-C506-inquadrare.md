## You'll want to know: inquadrare
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C506-FOTO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**inquadrare** — "to frame (a shot)".

> Inquadra bene il paesaggio prima di fare la foto.
>
> *Frame the landscape well before you take the photo.*

Built on *quadro* "square, picture"; also to put in context.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*inquadrare* is built on *quadro* "a square, a picture", from Latin *quadrum* "a square" (Lewis & Short).
