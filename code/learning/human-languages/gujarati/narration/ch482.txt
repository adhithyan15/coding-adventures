Gujarati, chapter 482: A Bad Fall.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
કાખઘોડી (kākhghoḍī) — a crutch.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to ooze, then the Gujarati for to get grazed.

You'll want to know: કાખઘોડી.
કાખઘોડી — kākhghoḍī — "a crutch".
પડ્યા પછી દાદા કાખઘોડીથી ચાલે છે. paḍyā pachī dādā kākhghoḍīthī cāle che. Since his fall my grandfather walks with a crutch.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
કાખઘોડી (kākhghoḍī) is built from kākh, "armpit", which CDIAL lists under Sanskrit kakṣa (CDIAL 2588), and ghoḍī, "a little horse", from the family of ઘોડો (ghoḍo) (CDIAL 4516): a little horse under the arm.

You'll want to know: ઢીમડું.
ઢીમડું — ḍhīmḍũ — "a bump (from a knock)".
મારા માથા પર મોટું ઢીમડું થયું. mārā māthā par moṭũ ḍhīmḍũ thayũ. A big bump came up on my head.
A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
ઢીમડું (ḍhīmḍũ) goes back to a reconstructed ḍhīmma-, "lump"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati word, "big swelling or bump" (CDIAL 5591).

પડ્યા પછી પગની ઘૂંટી ફૂલી ગઈ. paḍyā pachī pagnī ghū̃ṭī phūlī gaī. After the fall my ankle swelled up.
Past (it …): તે ઘૂંટી ફૂલી ગઈ, te ghū̃ṭī phūlī gaī.

ફૂલવું (phūlvũ) is inherited from Sanskrit phullati, "expands, opens like a flower"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb, "to swell, blossom" (CDIAL 9093).

Lesson 5 of 5.

Warm-up.

You'll want to know: અફાળવું.
અફાળવું — aphāḷvũ — "to bang; to dash against".
અંધારામાં મેં માથું દીવાલ સાથે અફાળ્યું. andhārāmā̃ mẽ māthũ dīvāl sāthe aphāḷyũ. In the dark I banged my head on the wall.
Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે માથું અફાળ્યું, teṇe māthũ aphāḷyũ.

The word, taken apart.
અફાળવું (aphāḷvũ) is inherited from Sanskrit āsphālayati, "causes to flap, shakes, throws"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb, "to dash against" (CDIAL 1531).
