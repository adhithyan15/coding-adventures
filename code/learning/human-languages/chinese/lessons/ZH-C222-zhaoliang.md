## You'll want to know: zháoliáng
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C222-CHILL222-01]; assesses=[] -->

*zháoliáng* — "to catch a chill".

> *zuótiān wǒ zháoliáng le* — I caught a chill yesterday.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*zháoliáng* is built from *zháo* 'to be touched by' and *liáng* 'cool', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *drjak* and *ljang* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to attach to' and the second 'thin' and 'cold'.
