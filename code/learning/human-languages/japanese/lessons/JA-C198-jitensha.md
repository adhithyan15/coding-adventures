## You'll want to know: じてんしゃ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C198-RIDE198-03]; assesses=[] -->

**じてんしゃ** — *jitensha* — "a bicycle".

> **じてんしゃで えきまで いきます。** — *jitensha de eki made ikimasu* — "I go to the station by bicycle."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**じてんしゃ** (*jitensha*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): three Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *ji*, "self" (Middle Chinese *dzijH*), *ten*, "to turn" (*trjwenX*) and *sha*, "cart" (*tsyhae*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
