## You'll want to know: concinō, concinere
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C543-CANT-04]; assesses=[] -->

**concinō, concinere** — "to sing together, sound in harmony".

> Puellae in templō concinunt.
>
> *The girls sing together in the temple.*

*concinō, concinere, concinuī*: *con-* + *canō*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**concinō** is built on *con-* "together" and *canō* "to sing" (Lewis & Short; EtymDB).
