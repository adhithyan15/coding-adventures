## You'll want to know: la vergüenza
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C705-ORGULLO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**la vergüenza** — "shame, embarrassment".

> ¡Qué vergüenza! Me caí en la calle.
>
> *How embarrassing! I fell over in the street.*

*Me da vergüenza* — I am embarrassed (to). The two dots on *ü* make the *u* sound.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*vergüenza* is inherited from Latin *verēcundia* "modesty, shame", built on *verērī* "to respect, to fear" (EtymDB).
