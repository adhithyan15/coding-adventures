# (more than one) — from cold

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ML-GRAMMAR-C93-PLURAL-01, ML-GRAMMAR-C93-ANGAL-01] -->

[PAUSE 3s] Close the lessons before this one. Make *a child* into *children*,
and *a book* into *books*. The two answers do not end the same way.

## Grammar Lens: one ending, wearing two faces
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ML-GRAMMAR-C93-PLURAL-01, ML-GRAMMAR-C93-ANGAL-01, ML-CONCEPT-C36-KUTTI-01, ML-LEX-C54-TREE-01, ML-LEX-C91-SHOP-01] -->

| one | more than one |
|---|---|
| **കുട്ടി** *kuṭṭi* | **കുട്ടികൾ** *kuṭṭikaḷ* |
| **കട** *kaṭa* | **കടകൾ** *kaṭakaḷ* |
| **പുസ്തകം** *pustakaṁ* | **പുസ്തകങ്ങൾ** *pustakaṅṅaḷ* |
| **മരം** *maraṁ* | **മരങ്ങൾ** *maraṅṅaḷ* |

**The last letter of the noun decides which face you see.** A noun ending in
**ം** trades that letter for **ങ്ങൾ**; anything else takes **കൾ** on the end
and keeps everything it had.

Look down the right-hand column and read only the ends: **ൾ** closes all four.
The two shapes are one ending, and the **ം** is what bends it.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ML-CONCEPT-C93-COUNTED-01, ML-LEX-C91-MONEY-03, ML-LEX-NII-NINGAL-01] -->

Two things worth holding onto, and neither is a new word.

**The ending was already in your mouth.** **നിങ്ങൾ** — the respectful *you* —
has carried it since your earliest pages, and it was plural in shape from the
day you met it.

**The ending comes off again when a number is there.**

> **രണ്ട് പുസ്തകം** — two books. **അമ്പത് രൂപ** — fifty rupees.

The number in front has said how many, so the noun goes back to the form you
first learned. Every price you have given and every hour you have told was
already built this way.

So this chapter added **no vocabulary at all**. What it added was a reading of
material you already had: an ending you had been pronouncing, and a habit you
had been performing correctly without being told why.

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ML-GRAMMAR-C93-PLURAL-01, ML-GRAMMAR-C93-ANGAL-01, ML-CONCEPT-C93-COUNTED-01] -->

[PAUSE 3s] Which ending does a noun ending in **ം** take? (**ങ്ങൾ**, in place of
the **ം**.) Which does every other noun take? (**കൾ**, on the end.) And when a
number stands in front? (**Neither** — the noun stays as it was.)
