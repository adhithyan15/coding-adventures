Bengali, chapter 146: Next, After This, a Plan, To Decide, To Hope.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
আগামী (āgāmī) — next, coming.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for once, then the Bengali for to spend.

You'll want to know: আগামী.
আগামী — āgāmī — "next, coming". Bengali's future adds -ব for "I": আমি আগামী সপ্তাহে যাব — āmi āgāmī sôptāhe jābô — "I will go next week."

The word, taken apart.
আগামী (āgāmī) is Sanskrit āgāmin, "coming, future", taken over whole as a learned loan, a tatsama.

Lesson 2 of 5.
এরপর (erpôr) — after this, from now on.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for to spend, then the Bengali for next.

You'll want to know: এরপর.
এরপর — erpôr — "after this, from now on". It is এর, "of this", with পর, "after"; add থেকে, "from", for "from now on": এরপর থেকে আমি রোজ হাঁটব — erpôr theke āmi roj hā̃ṭbô — "From now on I will walk every day."

The word, taken apart.
এরপর (erpôr) is er, "of this", the near head e- with the possessive -r, + pôr, "after", from Sanskrit para, "later" (CDIAL 7793).

Lesson 4 of 5.
স্থির করা (sthir kôrā) — to decide.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for after this, then the Bengali for a plan.

You'll want to know: স্থির করা.
স্থির করা — sthir kôrā — "to decide". আমি স্থির করলাম যে রোজ হাঁটব — āmi sthir kôrlām je roj hā̃ṭbô — "I decided that I would walk every day." Its past for "I" is করলাম.

The phrase, taken apart.
স্থির করা (sthir kôrā) is sthir, "firm, fixed", Sanskrit sthira taken over whole, whose inherited form is thir (CDIAL 13771), + kôrā, "to do", from Sanskrit karoti, "does" (CDIAL 2814).
