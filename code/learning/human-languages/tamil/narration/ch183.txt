Tamil, chapter 183: Pongal, Upma, Sambar, Chutney, Dry stir-fried vegetable dish.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
பொங்கல் (poṅkal) — pongal, a rice and lentil dish.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for a pan, then the Tamil for a lid.

You'll want to know: பொங்கல்.
பொங்கல் — poṅkal — "pongal, a rice and lentil dish".

The word, taken apart.
பொங்கல் (poṅkal) is native Dravidian. DEDR 4469 lists Kannada poṅgu, "to boil over" and Telugu poṅgu. It is named for poṅku, "to boil over": the pot is let boil over at the festival.

Lesson 3 of 5.
சாம்பார் (cāmpār) — sambar, a lentil and vegetable stew.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for pongal, then the Tamil for upma.

You'll want to know: சாம்பார்.
சாம்பார் — cāmpār — "sambar, a lentil and vegetable stew".

The word, taken apart.
The origin of சாம்பார் (cāmpār) is not settled. It is usually traced to Sanskrit saṁbhāra, "bringing together, materials" (CDIAL 12966), through Marathi sāmbār, but that route is not proven.

பொரியல் (poriyal) is native Dravidian. DEDR 4537 lists Malayalam pori, "what is parched". It comes from pori, "to be parched, roasted, fried".
