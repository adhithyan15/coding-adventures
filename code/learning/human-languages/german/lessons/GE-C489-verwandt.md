## You'll want to know: verwandt
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C489-VERWANDTE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**verwandt** — "related (by family)".

> Wir haben den gleichen Nachnamen, aber wir sind nicht verwandt.
>
> *We have the same surname, but we are not related.*

*Verwandt mit* + dative. *Der Verwandte* or *die Verwandte* — a relative.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*verwandt* is the old past participle of *verwenden* "to turn, to turn towards" (EtymDB), built on *wenden* "to turn".
