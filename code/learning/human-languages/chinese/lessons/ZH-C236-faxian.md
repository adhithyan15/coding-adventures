## You'll want to know: fāxiàn
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C236-ODD236-02]; assesses=[] -->

*fāxiàn* — "to discover; to find out".

> *huí jiā yǐhòu wǒ fāxiàn qiánbāo diū le* — When I got home, I found my wallet was gone.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*fāxiàn* is built from *fā* 'to send out' and 现 'to show', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *pjot* and *henH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to rise' and 'to make clear'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for talking about the past.
