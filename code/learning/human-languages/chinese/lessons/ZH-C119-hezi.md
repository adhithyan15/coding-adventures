## You'll want to know: hézi
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C119-BAGS119-03]; assesses=[] -->

*hézi* — "box; small case".

> *hézi lǐ yǒu shénme* — What's in the box?

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*hézi* is built from hé and the noun ending zi, both inherited from Middle Chinese, *hop* and *tsiX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription); the dictionary glosses the first as a dish with a lid.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for everyday things.
