## You'll want to know: इरादा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C546-NISHCHAY546-03]; assesses=[] -->

**इरादा** — *irādā* — "an intention".

> पुढच्या वर्षी परदेशी जाण्याचा माझा इरादा आहे.
>
> *puḍhcyā varṣī pardeśī jāṇyācā mājhā irādā āhe.*
>
> I intend to go abroad next year.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**इरादा** (*irādā*) is a loan through Persian usually traced to Arabic *irāda*, "will, wish".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
