## You'll want to know: la acuarela
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C751-AFICION-05]; assesses=[] -->

**la acuarela** — "watercolour".

> Pinto acuarelas los fines de semana.
>
> *I paint watercolours at weekends.*

Both the paint and the picture.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*acuarela* was borrowed from Italian *acquerello* (EtymDB), built on *acqua* "water", from Latin *aqua*.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.
