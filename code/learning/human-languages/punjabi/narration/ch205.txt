Lesson 2 of 5.
One whole word for all — all; everyone; everything in one bounded frame.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Recall the English idea “all,” then listen for one new Punjabi word.

You'll want to know.
ਸਭ — sabh — all; everyone; everything here.
Hear and say the whole word before reading it. Keep ਸਭ (sabh) whole. In this chapter it carries one beginner idea: all, understood as everyone or everything in the small frame that follows. The lesson does not open every grammatical use.

The word, taken apart — where the trail runs out.
Punjabi University Patiala's lexical statistics lists ਸਭ (sabh) as an indefinite pronoun with the meanings “all / every / whole.” Its pronoun grammar also lists the word among plural indefinite pronouns and glosses it “all.” Its number introduction independently shows that the same form can serve an indefinite numeral role. Sources: Punjabi University lexical statistics, Punjabi University pronoun grammar, and Punjabi University number introduction.
Those sources support the present meaning, but they do not establish a deeper history. The Punjabi origin remains unknown in the sources used here. Make no historical derivation claim; remember the whole form.

ਸਭ ਠੀਕ ਹੈ — sabh thik hai — everything is fine.
For this one frame, put the whole word ਸਭ (sabh) before familiar ਠੀਕ ਹੈ (thik hai). Here “all” is naturally understood as “everything.” Nothing else is added.
