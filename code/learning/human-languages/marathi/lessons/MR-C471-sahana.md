## You'll want to know: शहाणा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C471-SAHANA471-01]; assesses=[] -->

**शहाणा** — *śahāṇā* — "sensible; wise".

> शहाणी मुलगी आईला कामात मदत करते.
>
> *śahāṇī mulgī āīlā kāmāt madat karte.*
>
> The sensible girl helps her mother with the work.

Agrees: शहाणा, शहाणी, शहाणं. शहाणं बाळ, a good baby.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**शहाणा** (*śahāṇā*) is a Marathi adjective whose origin is not settled.
