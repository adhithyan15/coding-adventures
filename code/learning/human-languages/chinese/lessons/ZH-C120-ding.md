## You'll want to know: dìng
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C120-TAKEAWAY120-02]; assesses=[] -->

*dìng* — "to make a booking; to order".

> *wǒ xiǎng dìng yí ge fángjiān* — I'd like to book a room.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*dìng* is inherited from Middle Chinese *dengX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'to discuss and settle', which regularly gives dìng. Booking is the sense today, probably by way of settling an arrangement, but the step is not given there.
