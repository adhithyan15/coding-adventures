## You'll want to know: مختبر
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C391-BAHTH391-03]; assesses=[] -->

**مختبر** — *mukhtabar* — "a laboratory".

> يعمل أخي في مختبر كبير.
>
> *yaʿmalu akhī fī mukhtabar kabīr.*
>
> My brother works in a big laboratory.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**مختبر** (*mukhtabar*) is built on the native Arabic root *kh-b-r*, "to know thoroughly, to test" (Lane). *Mukhtabar* is a noun of place from Form VIII *ikhtabara*, to test: a place for testing, a laboratory.
