## You'll want to know: 家长
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C248-TERM248-05]; assesses=[] -->

**家长** — *jiāzhǎng* — "pupil's parent; parent of a schoolchild".

> **今天家长来学校** *jīntiān jiāzhǎng lái xuéxiào* — The parents are coming to school today.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**家长** (*jiāzhǎng*) is built from 家 'a home' and 长 'the head', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *kae* and *trjangX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription): the head of a household, and at school a pupil's parent.
