## You'll want to know: भावः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C448-BHAVA448-01]; assesses=[] -->

**भावः** — *bhāvaḥ* — "a feeling; a state of mind".

> भावः हृदये जायते।
>
> *bhāvaḥ hṛdaye jāyate.*
>
> A feeling arises in the heart.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**भावः** (*bhāvaḥ*) is built on the root √bhū, "to be, to become"; MW gives *bhāva* the senses "a state of mind or body, a way of feeling" and, in poetics, "emotion" (MW).
