## You'll want to know: ಸಮಂಜಸ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C377-TARKA377-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸಮಂಜಸ** — *samañjasa* — "reasonable; fitting".

> ಅವರು ಕೇಳಿದ ಬೆಲೆ ಸಮಂಜಸವಾಗಿತ್ತು.
>
> *avaru kēḷida bele samañjasavāgittu.*
>
> The price they asked was reasonable.

Before a noun: ಸಮಂಜಸವಾದ ಉತ್ತರ, a reasonable answer.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸಮಂಜಸ** (*samañjasa*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *samañjasa*, "proper, right, sound" (Monier-Williams).
