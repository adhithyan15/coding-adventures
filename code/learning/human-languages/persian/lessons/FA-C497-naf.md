## You'll want to know: ناف
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C497-BONES-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ناف** (*nâf*) — "the navel".

> پرستار بند ناف بچه را بست.
>
> *parastâr band-e nâf-e bachche râ bast.*
>
> The nurse tied the baby's umbilical cord.

*band-e nâf* — the umbilical cord; *nâf-e shahr* — the very heart of a town.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*nâf* is inherited: EtymDB traces it to a Proto-Indo-European word for 'navel', and lists Sanskrit *nābhi* and Avestan as cousins (EtymDB).
