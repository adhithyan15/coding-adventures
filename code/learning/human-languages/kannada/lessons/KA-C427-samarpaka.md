## You'll want to know: ಸಮರ್ಪಕ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C427-ANUPATA427-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸಮರ್ಪಕ** — *samarpaka* — "adequate; proper".

> ಹಳ್ಳಿಯಲ್ಲಿ ಸಮರ್ಪಕ ಕುಡಿಯುವ ನೀರು ಇಲ್ಲ.
>
> *haḷḷiyalli samarpaka kuḍiyuva nīru illa.*
>
> The village has no proper drinking water.

ಸಮರ್ಪಕವಾಗಿ, properly.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸಮರ್ಪಕ** (*samarpaka*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *samarpaka*, "handing over completely, imparting" (Monier-Williams); Kannada uses it for "adequate".
