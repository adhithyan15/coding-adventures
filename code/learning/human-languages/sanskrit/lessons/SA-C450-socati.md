## You'll want to know: शोचति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C450-VISADA450-02]; assesses=[] -->

**शोचति** — *śocati* — "grieves".

> बालिका नष्टं शुनकं शोचति।
>
> *bālikā naṣṭaṃ śunakaṃ śocati.*
>
> The girl grieves for her lost dog.

Past (he …): सः बहु शोचितवान्, *saḥ bahu śocitavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**शोचति** (*śocati*) comes from the root √śuc, "to glow, to burn" and "to grieve", the root of **शोकः** (MW).
