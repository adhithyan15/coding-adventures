## You'll want to know: ਪਰਛੱਤੀ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C246-GHR246-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ਪਰਛੱਤੀ** — *parchhattī* — "a high shelf; a shelf under the roof".

> ਪੁਰਾਣੇ ਭਾਂਡੇ ਪਰਛੱਤੀ ਉੱਤੇ ਹਨ।
>
> *purāṇe bhāṇḍe parchhattī utte han.*
>
> The old pots are on the high shelf.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਪਰਛੱਤੀ** (*parchhattī*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*praticchattika*, "near the roof"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *paṛchattī*, "shelf under roof" (CDIAL 8556).
