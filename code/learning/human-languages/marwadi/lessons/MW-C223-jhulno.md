## You'll want to know: झूलणो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C223-FAIR223-01]; assesses=[] -->

**झूलणो** — *jhūlṇo* — "to swing".

> मेळो काल है अर हूं झूलसूं।
>
> *meḷo kāl hai ar hū̃ jhūlsū̃.*
>
> The fair is tomorrow, and I'll ride the swings.

Past (he …): वो झूल्यो, *vo jhūlyo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**झूलणो** (*jhūlṇo*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*jhulyati*, "swings"; CDIAL lists Marwari *jhūlṇo*, "to swing" (CDIAL 5406).
