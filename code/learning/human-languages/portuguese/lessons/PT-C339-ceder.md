## You'll want to know: ceder
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C339-ACORDO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ceder** — "to give in, to give way; to hand over".

> No fim, o meu pai cedeu e deixou-me ir à festa.
>
> *In the end my father gave in and let me go to the party.*

*Ceder o lugar* — to give up your seat.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ceder* is a learned borrowing from Latin *cēdere* "to go, to withdraw, to yield" (EtymDB, Lewis & Short).
