## You'll want to know: মর্জি
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C529-DOTANA529-05]; assesses=[] -->

**মর্জি** — *môrji* — "a whim; a mood".

> আমার মর্জি হলে আমি সারাদিন বই পড়ি।
>
> *Āmār môrji hôle āmi sārādin bôi pôṛi.*
>
> When the mood takes me, I read books all day.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**মর্জি** (*môrji*) is usually traced to Arabic *marḍī*, "what is pleasing", through Persian.
