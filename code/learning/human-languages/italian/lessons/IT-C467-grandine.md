## You'll want to know: la grandine
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C467-TEMPORALE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**la grandine** — "the hail".

> La grandine ha rovinato le macchine.
>
> *The hail damaged the cars.*

*Un chicco di grandine* — a hailstone.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*grandine* is inherited from Latin *grandō, grandinem* 'hail' (EtymDB).
