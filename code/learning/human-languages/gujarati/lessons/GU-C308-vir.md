## You'll want to know: વીર
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C308-SAHAS308-03]; assesses=[] -->

**વીર** — *vīr* — "a hero; a brave man".

> ગામના લોકો તેને વીર કહે છે.
>
> *gāmnā loko tene vīr kahe che.*
>
> The villagers call him a hero.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**વીર** (*vīr*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *vīra*, "a man, a hero" (CDIAL 12056). It is the brave one in war stories and in everyday courage alike.
