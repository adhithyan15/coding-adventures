## You'll want to know: إيقاع
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C543-MUSIQA543-02]; assesses=[] -->

**إيقاع** — *īqāʿ* — "a rhythm, a beat".

> رقص الأطفال على إيقاع الموسيقى.
>
> *raqaṣa al-aṭfāl ʿalā īqāʿ al-mūsīqā.*
>
> The children danced to the rhythm of the music.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**إيقاع** (*īqāʿ*) is built on the native Arabic root *w-q-ʿ*, "to fall, to happen" (Lane). Īqāʿ is the verbal noun of Form IV: making the beats fall.
