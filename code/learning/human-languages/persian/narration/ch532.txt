Persian, chapter 532: Watching and Listening.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
اکران (ekrân) — a screening, a (film) release.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for dubbed, then the Persian for a TV channel.

You'll want to know: اکران.
اکران (ekrân) — "a screening, a (film) release".
فیلم تازه او از هفته بعد اکران میشود. film-e tâze-ye u az hafte-ye ba'd ekrân mishavad. His new film opens next week.
ekrân shodan — to be screened.

The word, taken apart.
ekrân is a loan, usually traced to French écran, 'screen'; Persian took the screen and made it the screening. EtymDB has no entry for it.

Lesson 3 of 5.
صفحه نمایش (safhe-ye namâyesh) — a screen (display).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a screening, then the Persian for a remote control.

You'll want to know: صفحه نمایش.
صفحه نمایش (safhe-ye namâyesh) — "a screen (display)".
صفحه نمایش موبایلم شکست. safhe-ye namâyesh-e mobâylam shekast. My phone screen broke.
safhe 'page, surface' + namâyesh 'showing'.

The word, taken apart.
safhe-ye namâyesh is built in Persian on safhe, 'page, surface', from Arabic ṣafḥa (EtymDB), and native namâyesh, 'showing', from namudan.
