# మళ్ళింపు (maḷḷimpu) — a diversion; a detour

## You'll want to know: మళ్ళింపు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C449-ADDANKI449-02]; assesses=[] -->

**మళ్ళింపు** — *maḷḷimpu* — "a diversion; a detour".

> వంతెన పనుల వల్ల రోడ్డు మీద మళ్ళింపు ఉంది.
>
> *vantena panula valla rōḍḍu mīda maḷḷimpu undi.*
>
> There is a diversion on the road because of work on the bridge.

The noun of మళ్ళించు, to turn something aside. మళ్ళీ, again, comes from the same root: to turn back.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**మళ్ళింపు** (*maḷḷimpu*) is native Dravidian, the noun of మళ్ళించు, "to turn aside", from మళ్ళు, "to turn back, to return" (DEDR 4761), the root of మళ్ళీ, "again".
