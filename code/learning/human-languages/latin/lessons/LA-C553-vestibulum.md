## You'll want to know: vestibulum, vestibulī
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C553-LIMN-01]; assesses=[] -->

**vestibulum, vestibulī** — "a forecourt, entrance court".

> Hospitēs in vestibulō exspectant.
>
> *The guests wait in the forecourt.*

Neuter, second declension. The space between the street and the front door. English *vestibule*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**vestibulum** is of uncertain origin; Lewis & Short connects it with a root meaning "to dwell", but it is not settled. English *vestibule* comes from it.
