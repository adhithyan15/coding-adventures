# उछळणो (uchhaḷṇo) — to leap up; to bounce

## You'll want to know: उछळणो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C224-FUN224-04]; assesses=[] -->

**उछळणो** — *uchhaḷṇo* — "to leap up; to bounce".

> छोरो ढोल सुणै है अर उछळै है।
>
> *chhoro ḍhol suṇai hai ar uchhaḷai hai.*
>
> The boy hears the drum and leaps up.

Past (he …): वो छोरो उछळ्यो, *vo chhoro uchhaḷyo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**उछळणो** (*uchhaḷṇo*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*ut-śalati*, "springs up"; CDIAL lists the Old Marwari verb *ūchaḷaï*, "flies up, flutters" (CDIAL 1843).
