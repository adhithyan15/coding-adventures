## You'll want to know: सिंहासन
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C326-SAMRAT326-02]; assesses=[] -->

**सिंहासन** — *siṁhāsan* — "a throne".

> नवा राजा सिंहासनावर बसला.
>
> *navā rājā siṁhāsanāvar baslā.*
>
> The new king sat on the throne.

Neuter.

सिंहासनावर बसणे, to take the throne.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सिंहासन** (*siṁhāsan*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *siṁhāsana*, 'a lion-seat, a throne' (Monier-Williams): *siṁha*, 'a lion', and *āsana*, 'a seat'.
