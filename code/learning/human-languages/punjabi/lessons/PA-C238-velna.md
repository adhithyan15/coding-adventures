## You'll want to know: ਵੇਲਣਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C238-KAM238-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ਵੇਲਣਾ** — *velṇā* — "to roll out (dough)".

> ਮੈਂ ਪੇੜਾ ਪਤਲਾ ਵੇਲਦੀ ਹਾਂ।
>
> *maiṁ peṛā patlā veldī hāṁ.*
>
> I roll the ball of dough out thin.

Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਰੋਟੀ ਵੇਲੀ, *maiṁ roṭī velī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਵੇਲਣਾ** (*velṇā*) is inherited from Sanskrit *vēllayati*, "causes to shake, kneads"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *velṇā*, "to roll out" (CDIAL 12121).
