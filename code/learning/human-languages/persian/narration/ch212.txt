Persian, chapter 212: Lid, Cardboard box, Bag, Clay jar, Small tea glass.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
درپوش (darpush) — a lid, a cap.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a brick, then the Persian for metal.

You'll want to know: درپوش.
درپوش — darpush — "a lid, a cap".

The word, taken apart.
darpush is dar, "opening", with push, "cover" (from pushidan, "to cover", EtymDB): the opening's cover. Both are native Persian.

Lesson 3 of 5.
کیسه (kise) — a bag, a sack.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a lid, then the Persian for a cardboard box.

You'll want to know: کیسه.
کیسه — kise — "a bag, a sack".

The word, taken apart.
kise is a native Persian word, inherited rather than borrowed: it continues Middle Persian kīsag, "pouch, bag" (EtymDB). EtymDB traces it further back to an Akkadian word for a leather money-bag.
