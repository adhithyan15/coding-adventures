## You'll want to know: جولة
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C438-SIYAHA438-02]; assesses=[] -->

**جولة** — *jawla* — "a tour; a round".

> قمنا بجولة في المدينة القديمة.
>
> *qumnā bi-jawla fī al-madīna al-qadīma.*
>
> We went on a tour of the old city.

*qāma bi-jawla*: to go on a tour.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**جولة** (*jawla*) is built on the native Arabic root *j-w-l*, "to go round, to roam" (Lane).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for getting around.
