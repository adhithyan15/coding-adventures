## You'll want to know: il battello
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C422-BARCA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**il battello** — "the boat (on a lake or river), the small steamer".

> Sul lago prendiamo il battello.
>
> *On the lake we take the boat.*

For lakes and rivers; on the sea you take *il traghetto*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*battello* was borrowed from Old French *batel*, a little form of a word taken from Old English *bāt* 'boat' (EtymDB).

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.
