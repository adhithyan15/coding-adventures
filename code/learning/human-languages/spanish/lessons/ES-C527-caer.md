## You'll want to know: caer
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C527-CHOQUE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**caer** — "to fall".

> Un árbol cayó en medio de la calle.
>
> *A tree fell in the middle of the street.*

*Caerse* is to fall over: *Me caí en la calle.* Forms: *caigo*, *cayó*, *cayeron*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*caer* is inherited from Latin *cadere* 'to fall, to happen', through spoken Latin *\*cadēre* (EtymDB). The *d* between vowels was lost.
