## You'll want to know: die Abstimmung
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C358-GESELLSCHAFT-04]; assesses=[] -->

**die Abstimmung** — "the vote, the ballot".

> Das Ergebnis der Abstimmung war sehr knapp.
>
> *The result of the vote was very close.*

Plural: *die Abstimmungen*. In Switzerland *die Volksabstimmung*, a vote of the whole people, takes place several times a year.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Abstimmung* is the noun of *abstimmen*, formed with *-ung*; the verb is made from *Stimme* "voice, vote" (EtymDB).
