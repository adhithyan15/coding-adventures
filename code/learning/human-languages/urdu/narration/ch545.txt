Urdu, chapter 545: For Now or for Good.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
عارضی (ārzī) — temporary.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a series, then the Urdu for order (a set sequence).

You'll want to know: عارضی.
عارضی — ārzī — "temporary".
اگر یہ نوکری عارضی ہے تو میں نہیں لوں گا۔ agar yih naukrī ārzī hai to maiṅ nahīṅ lūṅ gā. If this job is temporary, I won't take it.

The word, taken apart.
عارضی (ārzī) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic ʿāriḍī, "passing, accidental".
