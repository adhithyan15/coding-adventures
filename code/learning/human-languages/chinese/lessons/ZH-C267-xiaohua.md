## You'll want to know: 笑话
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C267-FILM267-04]; assesses=[] -->

**笑话** — *xiàohua* — "joke".

> **他说了一个笑话** *tā shuōle yí ge xiàohua* — He told a joke.

The new character, in pieces: 笑 — ten strokes: two small pieces on top, each a short stroke falling to the left, a short bar and a dot; under them a short stroke falling to the left, then 大. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**笑话** (*xiàohua*) is built from 笑 'to laugh' and 话 'talk', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *sjewH* and *hwaejH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'glad' and the second 'speech'. In the word the second loses its tone.
