Sanskrit, chapter 489: Habits and Self-Control.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
व्यसनम् (vyasanam) — a vice; an addiction.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for sleepless, then the Sanskrit for a three-hour watch.

You'll want to know: व्यसनम्.
व्यसनम् — vyasanam — "a vice; an addiction".
द्यूतम् एकं व्यसनम् अस्ति। dyūtam ekaṃ vyasanam asti. Gambling is a vice.
A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
व्यसनम् (vyasanam) is built from vi and the root √as, "to throw": throwing oneself into a thing; MW gives "attachment or addiction to" and "evil passion, vice" (MW).

परित्यजति — parityajati — "gives up entirely".
पिता धूमपानं परित्यजति। pitā dhūmapānaṃ parityajati. Father gives up smoking.
Past (he …): सः धूमपानं परित्यक्तवान्, saḥ dhūmapānaṃ parityaktavān.

The word, taken apart.
परित्यजति (parityajati) is built from pari, "around, wholly", and the root √tyaj, "to leave", the root of त्यजति; MW gives "to leave, quit, abandon, give up" (MW).
