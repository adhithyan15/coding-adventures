# પ્રસૂતિ (prasūti) — childbirth; a delivery

## You'll want to know: પ્રસૂતિ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C495-SAGARBHA495-02]; assesses=[] -->

**પ્રસૂતિ** — *prasūti* — "childbirth; a delivery".

> ભાભીની પ્રસૂતિ હોસ્પિટલમાં થઈ.
>
> *bhābhīnī prasūti hospiṭalmā̃ thaī.*
>
> My sister-in-law gave birth in hospital.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**પ્રસૂતિ** (*prasūti*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *prasūti*, "procreation, birth", as Monier-Williams records it.
