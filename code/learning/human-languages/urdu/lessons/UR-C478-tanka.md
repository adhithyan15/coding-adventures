## You'll want to know: ٹانکا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C478-CUT478-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ٹانکا** — *ṭāṅkā* — "a stitch".

> زخم پر تین ٹانکے لگے۔
>
> *zakhm par tīn ṭāṅke lage.*
>
> The wound needed three stitches.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ٹانکا** (*ṭāṅkā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *ṭā̃kā*, "a stitch, a join", under Sanskrit *ṭáṅkati*, "ties" (CDIAL 5432).
