## You'll want to know: yùfáng
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C484-WELL484-01]; assesses=[] -->

*yùfáng* — "to prevent; to guard against".

> *chángcháng xǐ shǒu kěyǐ yùfáng gǎnmào* — Washing your hands often helps prevent colds.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*yùfáng* is built from *yù* 'beforehand' and *fáng* 'to ward off', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *yoH* and *bjang* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'in advance' and the second 'to guard against'.
