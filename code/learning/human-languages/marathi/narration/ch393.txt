Marathi, chapter 393: Excuses and Requests.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
सबब (sabab) — an excuse.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to follow, then the Marathi for to embrace.

You'll want to know: सबब.
सबब — sabab — "an excuse".
उशिरा येण्याची त्याची सबब कोणालाच पटली नाही. uśirā yeṇyācī tyācī sabab koṇālāc paṭlī nāhī. Nobody believed his excuse for coming late.
Feminine.
Feminine: सबब (sabab) सांगणे, to make an excuse.

The word, taken apart.
सबब (sabab) is a loan from Persian, usually traced to Arabic sabab, "cause, reason".

What you've built.
One more word for saying what happens if.

You'll want to know: धुडकावणे.
धुडकावणे — dhuḍkāvṇe — "to brush aside; to spurn".
त्याने माझा सल्ला धुडकावला. tyāne mājhā sallā dhuḍkāvlā. He brushed aside my advice.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने सल्ला धुडकावला, tyāne sallā dhuḍkāvlā.
Stronger than नाकारणे.

The word, taken apart.

Lesson 4 of 5.
विनंती करणे (vinantī karṇe) — to request.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a pretext, then the Marathi for to brush aside.

You'll want to know: विनंती करणे.
विनंती करणे — vinantī karṇe — "to request".
आम्ही त्यांना थोडं थांबण्याची विनंती केली. āmhī tyānnā thoḍaṁ thāmbaṇyācī vinantī kelī. We asked them to wait a little.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने विनंती केली, tyāne vinantī kelī.
The person asked takes -ला, the thing asked for -ची.

The word, taken apart.
विनंती करणे (vinantī karṇe) joins विनंती, "a request", a Marathi noun whose origin is not settled, to करणे, "to do".

What you've built.
One more word for saying what happens if.

Lesson 5 of 5.
पाठपुरावा करणे (pāṭhpurāvā karṇe) — to follow up; to pursue.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to brush aside, then the Marathi for to request.

You'll want to know: पाठपुरावा करणे.
पाठपुरावा करणे — pāṭhpurāvā karṇe — "to follow up; to pursue".
तक्रारीचा पाठपुरावा केल्यावर काम झालं. takrārīcā pāṭhpurāvā kelyāvar kām jhālaṁ. After the complaint was followed up, the work got done.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने पाठपुरावा केला, tyāne pāṭhpurāvā kelā.
What is pursued takes -चा: तक्रारीचा पाठपुरावा.

The word, taken apart.
पाठपुरावा करणे (pāṭhpurāvā karṇe) joins पाठपुरावा, "pursuit, follow-up", a Marathi noun whose origin is not settled, to करणे, "to do".
