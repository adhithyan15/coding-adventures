## You'll want to know: বার্থ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C384-REL384-03]; assesses=[] -->

**বার্থ** — *bārth* — "a berth (on a train)".

> রাতের ট্রেনে আমার বার্থটা ওপরে ছিল।
>
> *rāter ṭrene āmār bārthṭā opôre chhilô.*
>
> On the night train my berth was the upper one.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**বার্থ** (*bārth*) is borrowed from English *berth*, a sleeping place on a ship or train; the English th is said with থ.
