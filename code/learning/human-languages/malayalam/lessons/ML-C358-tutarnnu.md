# തുടർന്ന് (tuṭarnnŭ) — following that; subsequently

## You'll want to know: തുടർന്ന്
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C358-PANDUPANDU358-04]; assesses=[] -->

**തുടർന്ന്** — *tuṭarnnŭ* — "following that; subsequently".

> ശക്തമായ മഴ പെയ്തു, തുടർന്ന് വെള്ളപ്പൊക്കം ഉണ്ടായി.
>
> *śaktamāya maḻa peytu, tuṭarnnŭ veḷḷappokkaṁ uṇṭāyi.*
>
> Heavy rain fell, and following that there was a flood.

A form of തുടരുക, to continue: in what followed. Common in the news.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**തുടർന്ന്** (*tuṭarnnŭ*) is native Dravidian, the past participle of തുടരുക (*tuṭaruka*), "to continue, to follow on" (DEDR 3480). In the news it links one event to the next.

One more word for telling what happened.
