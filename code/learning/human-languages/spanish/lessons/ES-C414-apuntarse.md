# apuntarse — put your name on the list

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-C342-CLOCK-03] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Recall **el punto**, a point or item. Spanish builds **apuntar** from
that family, and **apuntarse** can mean to put your own name down: to sign up.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-APUNTARSE]; assesses=[] -->

The base **punto** goes back to Latin **punctum**, a mark made by a prick. To
**apuntar** is to note or point something; with **se**, **apuntarse** often
means to add oneself to a list. The root is already anchored in **punto**, so
this lesson extends that family.

## Grammar Lens: who signs up?
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-APUNTARSE] -->

The pronoun changes with the person: **me apunto** for “I sign up” and
**apuntarse** after another verb, as in **hay que apuntarse**, “one must sign
up.” Use **a** for the activity: **apuntarse a una clase**.
