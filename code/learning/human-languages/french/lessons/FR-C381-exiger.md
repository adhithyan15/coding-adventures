## You'll want to know: exiger
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C381-AUTORISER-05]; assesses=[] -->

**exiger** — "to demand, to require".

> Le patron exige beaucoup de ses salariés.
>
> *The boss demands a lot of his employees.*

*Exiger que* takes the subjunctive. *Nous exigeons*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*exiger* is a learned borrowing from Latin *exigere* "to drive out, to demand" (Lewis & Short), built from *ex* "out" and *agere* "to drive".
