Gujarati, chapter 222: Earth air and sea.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
પૃથ્વી (pṛthvī) — planet earth, the world.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to catch fire, then the Gujarati for earthquake.

You'll want to know: પૃથ્વી.
પૃથ્વી — pṛthvī — "planet earth, the world".
પૃથ્વી હંમેશા સુંદર રહેશે. — pṛthvī hammeśā sundar raheśe — "The earth will always stay beautiful."
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
પૃથ્વી (pṛthvī) is Sanskrit pṛthvī, "the earth", taken over whole as a learned loan, a tatsama. CDIAL lists the inherited Old Gujarati puhavī, "the earth", under the same entry (CDIAL 8357).

Lesson 2 of 5.
વાતાવરણ (vātāvaraṇ) — atmosphere, surroundings.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for earthquake, then the Gujarati for planet earth.

You'll want to know: વાતાવરણ.
વાતાવરણ — vātāvaraṇ — "atmosphere, surroundings".
ગામનું વાતાવરણ શાંત હશે. — gāmnũ vātāvaraṇ śānt haśe — "The atmosphere in the village will be calm."

The word, taken apart.
વાતાવરણ (vātāvaraṇ) is a learned word built from Sanskrit vāta, "wind, air", and āvaraṇa, "covering" (CDIAL 1413), so the atmosphere is the air that wraps the earth; EtymDB lists it as borrowed from Sanskrit.

Lesson 3 of 5.
કુદરત (kudrat) — nature.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for planet earth, then the Gujarati for atmosphere.

You'll want to know: કુદરત.
કુદરત — kudrat — "nature".
કુદરત અમને પાણી આપશે. — kudrat amne pāṇī āpśe — "Nature will give us water."
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
કુદરત (kudrat) is a loan from Arabic qudra, "power", which reached Gujarati through Persian, so nature is the power that makes things. Its Arabic root is q-d-r (Lane).

Lesson 4 of 5.
ભરતી (bhartī) — high tide.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for atmosphere, then the Gujarati for nature.

You'll want to know: ભરતી.
ભરતી — bhartī — "high tide".
સાંજે દરિયામાં ભરતી આવશે. — sā̃je dariyāmā̃ bhartī āvśe — "High tide will come in the evening."
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ભરતી (bhartī) is built on the inherited verb bharvũ, "to fill", so high tide is the sea filling up. CDIAL derives bharvũ from Sanskrit bharati, "bears, brings" (CDIAL 9397).

Lesson 5 of 5.
ઓટ (oṭ) — low tide, ebb.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for nature, then the Gujarati for high tide.

You'll want to know: ઓટ.
ઓટ — oṭ — "low tide, ebb".
સવારે ઓટ હશે. — savāre oṭ haśe — "It will be low tide in the morning."
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ઓટ (oṭ) is a tadbhava. CDIAL lists it, glossed "ebb tide", under apavṛtti, the noun beside Sanskrit apavartate, "turns back", so the ebb is the sea turning back (CDIAL 452).
