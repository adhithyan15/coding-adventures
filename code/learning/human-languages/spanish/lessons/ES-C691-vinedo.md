## You'll want to know: el viñedo
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C691-CAMPO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**el viñedo** — "the vineyard".

> En otoño trabajan en el viñedo.
>
> *In autumn they work in the vineyard.*

Same family as *la viña* and *el vino*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*viñedo* is inherited from Latin *vīnētum* "a vineyard" (EtymDB), from *vīnum* "wine".
