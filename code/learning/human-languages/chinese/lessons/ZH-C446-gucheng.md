## You'll want to know: 古城
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C446-OLDTOWN446-03]; assesses=[] -->

**古城** — *gǔchéng* — "ancient walled town; old town".

> **我们在古城里走了一个下午** *wǒmen zài gǔchéng lǐ zǒu le yí ge xiàwǔ* — We spent the afternoon walking round the old town.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**古城** (*gǔchéng*) is built from 古 'ancient' and 城 'a walled town', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *kuX* and *dzyeng* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription).
