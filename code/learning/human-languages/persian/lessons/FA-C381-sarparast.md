## You'll want to know: سرپرست
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C381-SARPARAST-01]; assesses=[] -->

**سرپرست** (*sarparast*) — "a supervisor; a guardian".

> سرپرست ما هر صبح کارها را تقسیم میکند.
>
> *sarparast-e mâ har sobh kârhâ râ taqsim mikonad.*
>
> Our supervisor shares out the jobs every morning.

*Sar* "head" + *parast*, from *parastidan* "to serve, to tend". Also the guardian of a child.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**سرپرست** is a native Persian compound built from *sar* "head" + *parast*, which EtymDB traces to a Middle Persian verb "to serve, to minister". A supervisor is the one who looks after the head of the work.
