## You'll want to know: முதலாவது
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C82-ORDINAL-05]; assesses=[TA-LEX-C82-ORDINAL-01, TA-GRAMMAR-C82-ORDINAL-04] -->

**முதலாவது** (*mutalāvadu*) — "**first**", said the long way.

Look at what happened:

> **முதல்** + **ஆவது** → **முதலாவது**

The ending is the *same* ending. **முதல்** takes **-ஆவது** exactly as **இரண்டு**
did. So the exception is narrower than it looked a lesson ago: *first* does not
refuse **-ஆவது** at all. It simply takes it on **முதல்** rather than on
**ஒன்று**.

The **stem** is the exception. The **ending** is not.

Both forms are current: **முதல் நாள்** and **முதலாவது நாள்** are both the first
day, and **முதல்** is the shorter and commoner of the two in front of a noun.
