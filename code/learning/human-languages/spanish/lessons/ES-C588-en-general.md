## You'll want to know: en general
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C588-ORDENA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**en general** — "in general, generally".

> En general, la gente está contenta.
>
> *In general, people are happy.*

*Por lo general* means the same.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*en general* is built on *general*, a learned borrowing from Latin *generalis* 'of a whole class', built on *genus* 'kind' (EtymDB).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.
