## You'll want to know: منظره
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C439-MALAFE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**منظره** (*manzare*) — "a view, a scene".

> از پنجره اتاق منظره دریا را میدیدیم.
>
> *Az panjere-ye otâq manzare-ye daryâ râ mididim.*
>
> From the room's window we had a view of the sea.

Arabic plural *manâzer*. *Manzare-ye zibâ* — a lovely view.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*manzare* was borrowed from Arabic *manẓara* (EtymDB), a word built on the same root as *nazar* 'look, view'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.
