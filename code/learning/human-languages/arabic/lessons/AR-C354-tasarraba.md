## You'll want to know: تسرب
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C354-HUTAM354-05]; assesses=[] -->

**تسرب** — *tasarraba* — "to leak, to seep".

> تسرب الماء من السقف.
>
> *tasarraba al-māʾ min as-saqf.*
>
> Water leaked through the ceiling.

Present: يتسرب, *yatasarrab*.

Of news: تسرب الخبر, the news leaked out.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**تسرب** (*tasarraba*) is built on the native Arabic root *s-r-b* (Lane), where *sarab* is a burrow or tunnel under the ground. It is Form V, "to pass in, little by little".
