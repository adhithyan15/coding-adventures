Hindi, chapter 475: Character and Conduct.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
चरित्र (caritra) — character; moral nature.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for mad, then the Hindi for to go astray.

You'll want to know: चरित्र.
चरित्र — caritra — "character; moral nature".
मीरा के दादा का चरित्र बहुत साफ़ था। Mīrā ke dādā kā caritra bahut sāf thā. Mira's grandfather was a man of spotless character.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
चरित्र (caritra) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit caritra, "acting, behaving, behaviour, deeds" (Monier-Williams), from car, "to move, to act".

Lesson 3 of 6.
मिज़ाज (mizāj) — mood; temperament.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for character, then the Hindi for moral conduct.

You'll want to know: मिज़ाज.
मिज़ाज — mizāj — "mood; temperament".
आज दादा का मिज़ाज अच्छा नहीं है। āj dādā kā mizāj acchā nahīṃ hai. Grandfather isn't in a good mood today.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
मिज़ाज (mizāj) is usually traced to Arabic mizāj, "mixture", and so "temperament", the mix of humours that old medicine said made a person's nature; it reached Hindi through Persian.

धैर्य अरुण का सबसे बड़ा गुण है। dhairya Aruṇ kā sabse baṛā guṇ hai. Patience is Arun's greatest virtue.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
गुण (guṇ) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit guṇa, first "a single strand of a cord" and then "a quality" and "good quality, virtue, merit" (Monier-Williams).
