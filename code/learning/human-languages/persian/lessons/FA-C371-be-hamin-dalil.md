## You'll want to know: به همین دلیل
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C371-DALIL-02]; assesses=[] -->

**به همین دلیل** (*be hamin dalil*) — "for this reason, that is why".

> کارش را دوست نداشت، به همین دلیل دنبال یک کار تازه است.
>
> *kârash râ dust nadâsht, be hamin dalil donbâl-e yek kâr-e tâze ast.*
>
> He did not like his job; that is why he is looking for a new one.

*Be* + *hamin* "this very" + *dalil* "reason". It opens a clause that states a result.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**به همین دلیل** joins the native words *be* "to" and *hamin* "this very" to *dalil*, from Arabic *dalīl* (EtymDB): for this very reason.
