# comeré — the same endings keep -er

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-COMER, ES-GRAMMAR-FUTURE-PREDICTION-INTENTION, ES-GRAMMAR-AR-FUTURE-SINGULAR] -->

[PAUSE 2s] You built **hablaré** without removing **-ar**. Keep **comer** just
as intact: **comeré** — “I will eat.”

## Grammar Lens: the singular -er row
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-PATTERN-ER-FUTURE-SINGULAR]; assesses=[ES-LEX-COMER, ES-GRAMMAR-FUTURE-PREDICTION-INTENTION, ES-GRAMMAR-AR-FUTURE-SINGULAR] -->

| person | simple future |
|---|---|
| yo | comer**é** |
| tú | comer**ás** |
| él / ella / usted | comer**á** |

Say **comeré · comerás · comerá**. Compare **hablaré / comeré**: the
infinitives differ, but the future endings do not. Plural persons wait.

## The ending, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-ETYMON-ROMANCE-FUTURE-CONDITIONAL, ES-PATTERN-ER-FUTURE-SINGULAR] -->

The historical infinitive-plus-**habēre** construction helps explain the shared
endings: the verb-family vowel remains inside **hablar** or **comer**, rather
than selecting a different ending set.
