## You'll want to know: zhǒng
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C240-SICK240-03]; assesses=[] -->

*zhǒng* — "swollen".

> *tā de jiǎo zhǒng le* — His foot has swollen up.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*zhǒng* is inherited from Middle Chinese *tsyowngX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'an illness' and, after an older book, 'a boil'.
