Sanskrit, chapter 400: Round the Shrine.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
परिक्रामति (parikrāmati) — walks around; goes round.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for an arched gateway, then the Sanskrit for a pennant.

You'll want to know: परिक्रामति.
परिक्रामति — parikrāmati — "walks around; goes round".
यात्रिकाः मन्दिरं परिक्रामन्ति। yātrikāḥ mandiraṃ parikrāmanti. The pilgrims walk around the temple.
Past (he …): सः मन्दिरं परिक्रान्तवान्, saḥ mandiraṃ parikrāntavān.

The word, taken apart.
परिक्रामति (parikrāmati) is built from pari, "around", and the root √kram, "to step, to stride"; MW gives pari-kram as to walk or step round, with the participle parikrānta (MW).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a pennant, then the Sanskrit for walks around.

You'll want to know: जपमाला.
जपमाला — japamālā — "a string of prayer beads".
वृद्धः हस्ते जपमालां धारयति। vṛddhaḥ haste japamālāṃ dhārayati. The old man holds prayer beads in his hand.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
जपमाला (japamālā) is built from japa, "the quiet repeating of prayers", from the root √jap, "to mutter", and mālā, "a garland"; MW gives the compound as a rosary for counting prayers (MW).

Lesson 5 of 5.
आहरति (āharati) — fetches; brings.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a rudraksha bead, then the Sanskrit for a chariot procession.

You'll want to know: आहरति.
आहरति — āharati — "fetches; brings".
बालिका उद्यानात् पुष्पाणि आहरति। bālikā udyānāt puṣpāṇi āharati. The girl fetches flowers from the garden.
Past (she …): सा पुष्पाणि आहृतवती, sā puṣpāṇi āhṛtavatī.

The word, taken apart.
आहरति (āharati) is built from ā, "towards", and the root √hṛ, "to carry", which also gives harati, "takes away"; MW gives ā-hṛ as to fetch or bring, with the participle āhṛta (MW).
