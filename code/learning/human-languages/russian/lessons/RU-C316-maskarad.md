## You'll want to know: маскарад
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C316-KARNAVAL-02]; assesses=[] -->

**маскарад** (*maskarád*) — "a masquerade, a fancy-dress ball".

> На маскараде все были в масках.
>
> *Na maskaráde vse býli v máskakh.*
>
> At the masquerade everyone wore masks.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*маскарад* is usually traced through French *mascarade* to Italian, from the same source as *маска* "a mask"; EtymDB lists no parent for either.
