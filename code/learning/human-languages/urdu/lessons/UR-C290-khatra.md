## You'll want to know: خطرہ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C290-AILAN290-03]; assesses=[] -->

**خطرہ** — *khatrā* — "a danger".

> دریا میں خطرہ ہے۔
>
> *daryā meṅ khatrā hai.*
>
> There is danger in the river.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**خطرہ** (*khatrā*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *khaṭar*, "danger, risk". **خطرناک**, dangerous, is built on it.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
