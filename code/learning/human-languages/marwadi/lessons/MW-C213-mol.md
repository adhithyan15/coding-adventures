## You'll want to know: मोल
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C213-PRICE213-01]; assesses=[] -->

**मोल** — *mol* — "the cost; worth".

> मोल पूछो, पछै खरीदो।
>
> *mol pūchho, pachhai kharīdo.*
>
> Ask the cost, then buy.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**मोल** (*mol*) is inherited from Sanskrit *maulya*, "price"; CDIAL lists Old Marwari *mola*, "price" (CDIAL 10373).
