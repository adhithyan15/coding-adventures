# ಮೂಡು (mūḍu) — to rise; to appear

## Warm-up

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Kannada for a statue, then the Kannada for dusk.

## You'll want to know: ಮೂಡು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C363-SANJE363-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ಮೂಡು** — *mūḍu* — "to rise; to appear".

> ಬೆಟ್ಟದ ಹಿಂದೆ ಚಂದ್ರ ಮೂಡಿತು.
>
> *beṭṭada hinde candra mūḍitu.*
>
> The moon rose behind the hill.

Past (it …): ಮೂಡಿತು, *mūḍitu*.

The sun, the moon, a doubt or a smile ಮೂಡು: ಮನಸ್ಸಿನಲ್ಲಿ ಪ್ರಶ್ನೆ ಮೂಡಿತು.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಮೂಡು** (*mūḍu*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 5035 lists Kannada *mūḍu*, "to arise, to originate, to become visible", and *mūḍa*, "the east".
