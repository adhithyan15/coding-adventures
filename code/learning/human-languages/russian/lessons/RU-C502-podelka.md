## You'll want to know: поделка
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C502-CRAFTS-05]; assesses=[] -->

**поделка** (*podélka*) — "a handmade craft item".

> Дети показали нам свои поделки из бумаги.
>
> *Déti pokazáli nam svoí podélki iz bumági.*
>
> The children showed us the things they had made out of paper.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*поделка* is built on *поделать*, from *делать* 'to do, to make'; EtymDB has no entry.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.
