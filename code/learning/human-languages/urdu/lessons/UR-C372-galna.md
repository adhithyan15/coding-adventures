## You'll want to know: گلنا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C372-JAMNA372-03]; assesses=[] -->

**گلنا** — *galnā* — "to soften in cooking; to melt away".

> اگر دال نہیں گلی تو اور پکائیے۔
>
> *agar dāl nahīṅ galī to aur pakāiye.*
>
> If the lentils have not softened, cook them longer.

Past (it …): وہ دال گل گئی, *voh dāl gal gaī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**گلنا** (*galnā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *galnā*, "to melt, to trickle", under Sanskrit *galati*, "drips, oozes" (CDIAL 4074).
