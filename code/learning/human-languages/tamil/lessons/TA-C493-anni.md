## You'll want to know: அண்ணி
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C493-URAVU493-01]; assesses=[] -->

**அண்ணி** — *aṇṇi* — "an elder brother’s wife".

> என் அண்ணி நன்றாகச் சமைப்பாள்.
>
> *eṉ aṇṇi naṉṟākac camaippāḷ.*
>
> My elder brother’s wife cooks well.

Made from அண்ணன் "elder brother" with the -இ of a woman.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**அண்ணி** (*aṇṇi*) is native Dravidian, built on அண்ணன் *aṇṇaṉ*, "elder brother" (DEDR 131).
