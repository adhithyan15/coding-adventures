## You'll want to know: दक्षता
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C541-SAVADH541-03]; assesses=[] -->

**दक्षता** — *dakṣatā* — "vigilance".

> सणांच्या दिवसांत पोलीस विशेष दक्षता घेतात.
>
> *saṇāñcyā divsānt polīs viśeṣ dakṣatā ghetāt.*
>
> On festival days the police are especially vigilant.

Feminine.

दक्षता घेणे, to stay vigilant.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**दक्षता** (*dakṣatā*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *dakṣatā*, "dexterity, ability" (Monier-Williams).
