## You'll want to know: قیدی
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C316-SURANG316-01]; assesses=[] -->

**قیدی** — *qaidī* — "a prisoner".

> قلعے میں سو قیدی تھے۔
>
> *qile meṅ sau qaidī the.*
>
> There were a hundred prisoners in the fort.

Masculine or feminine, by the person meant.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**قیدی** (*qaidī*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *qayd*, "a fetter", with the ending *-ī*: one held in chains.
