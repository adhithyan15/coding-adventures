Gujarati, chapter 506: At the Loom.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
વણકર (vaṇkar) — a weaver.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to spin (thread), then the Gujarati for khadi.

You'll want to know: વણકર.
વણકર — vaṇkar — "a weaver".
આ ગામના વણકર સુંદર સાડીઓ બનાવે છે. ā gāmnā vaṇkar sundar sāḍīo banāve che. The weavers of this village make beautiful saris.
A masculine or feminine noun, by the person meant.

The word, taken apart.
વણકર (vaṇkar) is built on vaṇvũ, "to weave", which CDIAL lists under vināti (CDIAL 11773), with the ending -kar, "one who does".

Lesson 2 of 5.
તાંતણો (tā̃tṇo) — a strand (of fibre).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for khadi, then the Gujarati for a weaver.

You'll want to know: તાંતણો.
તાંતણો — tā̃tṇo — "a strand (of fibre)".
રૂના તાંતણામાંથી દોરો બને છે. rūnā tā̃tṇāmā̃thī doro bane che. Thread is made from strands of cotton.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
તાંતણો (tā̃tṇo) is inherited from Sanskrit tántu, "a thread, the warp", and CDIAL lists the Gujarati word with the sense "thread" (CDIAL 5661). Before a postposition it becomes તાંતણા-.

Lesson 4 of 5.
તાણવું (tāṇvũ) — to stretch tight.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a strand, then the Gujarati for a weave.

You'll want to know: તાણવું.
તાણવું — tāṇvũ — "to stretch tight".
વણકરે બે લાકડાં વચ્ચે દોરા તાણ્યા. vaṇakre be lākḍā̃ vacce dorā tāṇyā. The weaver stretched the threads tight between two poles.
Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે દોરો તાણ્યો, teṇe doro tāṇyo.

The word, taken apart.
તાણવું (tāṇvũ) is inherited from Sanskrit tānáyati, "makes stretch", and CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb, "to stretch, pull" (CDIAL 5762).

Lesson 5 of 5.
વીંટવું (vī̃ṭvũ) — to wind round (a thread).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a weave, then the Gujarati for to stretch tight.

You'll want to know: વીંટવું.
વીંટવું — vī̃ṭvũ — "to wind round (a thread)".
મીરાએ દોરો આંગળી પર વીંટ્યો. Mīrāe doro ā̃gaḷī par vī̃ṭyo. Mira wound the thread round her finger.
Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે દોરો વીંટ્યો, teṇe doro vī̃ṭyo.

The word, taken apart.
વીંટવું (vī̃ṭvũ) is filed by CDIAL under Sanskrit vīṭā, a small rolled piece, and CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb, "to wrap, roll, wind" (CDIAL 12045). The ring વીંટી belongs to the same family.
