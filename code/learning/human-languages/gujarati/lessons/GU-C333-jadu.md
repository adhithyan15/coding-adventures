## You'll want to know: જાદુ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C333-PARI333-02]; assesses=[] -->

**જાદુ** — *jādu* — "magic".

> પરીના જાદુથી ઝૂંપડું મહેલ બની ગયું.
>
> *parīnā jāduthī jhū̃pḍũ mahel banī gayũ.*
>
> By the fairy's magic the hut turned into a palace.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**જાદુ** (*jādu*) is usually traced to Persian *jādū*, "magic". It is masculine: **પરીનો જાદુ**, the fairy's magic; **જાદુથી**, by magic.
