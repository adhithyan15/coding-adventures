## Script — four parts, none of them new
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-SCRIPT-KYAAN-01]; assesses=[GU-SCRIPT-KA-01, GU-SCRIPT-VIRAMA-01, GU-SCRIPT-YA-01, GU-SCRIPT-AA-MATRA-01, GU-SCRIPT-ANUSVARA-01] -->

Uncover **ક્યાં** and take it apart in order:

- **ક** · the **્** that binds it to · **ય** · then the **ા** stroke · and the
  **ં** dot above

Five signs, every one taught before chapter 30. This is the last word this run of
chapters puts into the hand, and like every other one it spends nothing new.

Now cover every Gujarati model above and keep earlier lessons closed. From the
meaning alone, write the Gujarati question **Where is the home?**

There is no word bank, romanization, or copyable sentence. Choose the three
known pieces, put them in Gujarati order, and add the question mark. The task is
untimed: finish the line before opening the answer key.
