# Chapter 13 checkpoint — head, hand, ಚ and ೫

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

letter and the digit this chapter put on the page.

## Guided Practice — head and hand
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[KA-LEX-C13-DEHADA-BHAGAGALU-01] -->

[PAUSE 1s]
- [YOU SAY: "head", then "hand", in Kannada]

(*Tale*; *kai*.)

- How close are they to Tamil's and Malayalam's words? (Very close: Tamil
  *talai* and *kai*, Malayalam *tala* and *kai*; all three kept the native
  Dravidian pair.)

## Script — ಚ and ೫
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[KA-SCRIPT-RECOG-122, KA-SCRIPT-RECOG-144, KA-LEX-C07-AIDU-01] -->

- **ಚೆನ್ನಾಗಿ** is "well". Which letter opens it, and what sound does that
  letter carry on its own? (**ಚ**, *ca*: a consonant with its *a* built in.)
- Which number is **೫**, and which word does it stand for? (**5**, *aidu*.)

[YOU READ: **ಚೆನ್ನಾಗಿ** aloud, and point to the ಚ in it]

[YOU READ: **೫** aloud as *aidu*]
