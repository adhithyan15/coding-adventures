## You'll want to know: महार्घः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C431-ARGHA431-02]; assesses=[] -->

**महार्घः** — *mahārghaḥ* — "costly; expensive".

> सुवर्णं महार्घम् अस्ति।
>
> *suvarṇaṃ mahārgham asti.*
>
> Gold is costly.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**महार्घः** (*mahārghaḥ*) is built from *mahā*, "great", and *argha*, "value, price"; MW gives the compound as high-priced, costly, very precious (MW).
