## You'll want to know: শোধরানো
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C325-UPODESH325-04]; assesses=[] -->

**শোধরানো** — *shodhrāno* — "to mend one's ways; to put right".

> শাস্তির পরে লোভী রাজা শোধরালেন।
>
> *shāstir pôre lobhī rājā shodhrālen.*
>
> After his punishment the greedy king mended his ways.

Past (he/she …): সেই ছেলে শোধরাল, *sei chhele shodhrālô*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**শোধরানো** (*shodhrāno*) is usually explained as built on শোধ, 'a clearing, a setting right' — Sanskrit *śodha*, 'purification, correction' (Monier-Williams) — as a verb: to put oneself right.
