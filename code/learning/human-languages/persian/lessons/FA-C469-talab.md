## You'll want to know: تالاب
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C469-TALAB-02]; assesses=[] -->

**تالاب** (*tâlâb*) — "a wetland, a lagoon".

> پرنده ها زمستان را در تالاب میگذرانند.
>
> *Parande-hâ zemestân râ dar tâlâb migozarânand.*
>
> The birds spend the winter in the wetland.

Iran's wetlands are winter homes for many birds from the north.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*tâlâb* is a native word, which EtymDB derives from *âb* 'water', itself from Middle Persian *āb*.
