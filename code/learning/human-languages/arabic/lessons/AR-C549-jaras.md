## You'll want to know: جرس
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C549-BAB549-02]; assesses=[] -->

**جرس** — *jaras* — "a bell".

> رن جرس الباب في الصباح.
>
> *ranna jaras al-bāb fī aṣ-ṣabāḥ.*
>
> The doorbell rang in the morning.

Plural: أجراس, *ajrās*.

جرس الباب is a doorbell.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**جرس** (*jaras*) is built on the native Arabic root *j-r-s*, "a sound, a low voice" (Lane). Lane gives jaras as a sound, and a bell that makes it.
