## You'll want to know: তর সওয়া
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C455-DHOIRJO455-05]; assesses=[] -->

**তর সওয়া** — *tôr sôoyā* — "to be able to wait (mostly with not: can't wait)".

> ছুটি আসছে, মীরার আর তর সইছে না।
>
> *chhuṭi āschhe, mīrār ār tôr sôichhe nā.*
>
> The holidays are coming and Mira can't wait any longer.

Past (he/she …): সেই ছেলের তর সইল না, *sei chheler tôr sôilô nā*.

The one who waits is in the genitive: আমার তর সইছে না, I can't wait.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**তর সওয়া** (*tôr sôoyā*) is *tôr*, "waiting", whose origin is not settled, with *sôoyā*, "to bear, to put up with" (CDIAL 13304).
