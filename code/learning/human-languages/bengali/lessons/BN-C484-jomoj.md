## You'll want to know: যমজ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C484-SONTAN484-02]; assesses=[] -->

**যমজ** — *jômôj* — "twins; a twin".

> আমার কাকিমার যমজ ছেলে হল।
>
> *āmār kākimār jômôj chhele hôlô.*
>
> My aunt had twin boys.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**যমজ** (*jômôj*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *yamaja*, "twin-born" (Monier-Williams), from *yama*, a twin, and *ja*, born.
