## Script — an eye, then a right side of six
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-SCRIPT-YAN-EYE-01]; assesses=[] -->

> 眼

**Two pieces, left before right.** Count the pieces, not the strokes.

**The left piece, the eye, five strokes.** Like 日, made tall and narrow, with one bar more: a vertical down the left, a stroke that runs right and turns down, two short bars inside, then the bar across the foot.

**The right piece, six strokes.** A stroke that runs right and turns down. A bar inside, and a second one under it. Then a vertical down the left that turns up to the right at the foot, a short stroke falling to the left, and last a long stroke falling to the right.

5 + 6 = eleven.
