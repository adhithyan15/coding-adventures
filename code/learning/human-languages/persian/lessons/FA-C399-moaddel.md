## You'll want to know: معدل
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C399-KONKUR-05]; assesses=[] -->

**معدل** (*mo'addel*) — "a grade average".

> معدل او امسال بیست بود.
>
> *mo'addel-e u emsâl bist bud.*
>
> Her grade average this year was twenty.

Iranian marks go up to twenty.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**معدل** is usually traced to Arabic *mu'addal* "evened out, balanced", from the same Arabic family as *'adl* "justice": an average evens out the marks.
