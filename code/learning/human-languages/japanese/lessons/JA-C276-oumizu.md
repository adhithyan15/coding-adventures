## You'll want to know: おおみず
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C276-OOAME276-02]; assesses=[] -->

**おおみず** — *ōmizu* — "a flood".

> **おおみずの とき、日本人の ともだちが たすけに きました。** — *ōmizu no toki, nihonjin no tomodachi ga tasuke ni kimashita* — "In the flood, a Japanese friend came to help."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**おおみず** (*ōmizu*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, built from *ō*, "big", and *mizu*, "water" (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
