## You'll want to know: عقد
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C334-ASHEQ-05]; assesses=[] -->

**عقد** (*'aqd*) — "the marriage contract, the wedding ceremony".

> عقد آنها در یک محضر کوچک بود.
>
> *'Aqd-e ânhâ dar yek mahzar-e kuchak bud.*
>
> Their marriage ceremony was at a small registry office.

The legal and religious signing; *sofre-ye 'aqd* is the spread laid out for it.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*'aqd* is borrowed from Arabic *'aqd*, "a contract" (EtymDB lists the Arabic word), from a root for tying a knot.
