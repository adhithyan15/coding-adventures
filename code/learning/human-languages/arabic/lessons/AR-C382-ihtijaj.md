# احتجاج (iḥtijāj) — a protest

## You'll want to know: احتجاج
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C382-IHTIJAJ382-01]; assesses=[] -->

**احتجاج** — *iḥtijāj* — "a protest".

> خرج العمال في احتجاج على الرواتب.
>
> *kharaja al-ʿummāl fī iḥtijāj ʿalā ar-rawātib.*
>
> The workers came out in protest over wages.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**احتجاج** (*iḥtijāj*) is built on the native Arabic root *ḥ-j-j*, "to make for; to overcome in argument" (Lane). *Iḥtijāj* is the Form VIII verbal noun, bringing an argument against: a protest.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
