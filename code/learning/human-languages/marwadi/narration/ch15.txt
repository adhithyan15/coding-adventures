Marwadi (Marwari), chapter 15: Hand and Money: No New Signs.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
Hear hāth — hand.

Warm-up.
[pause 16 seconds]
Recall the three-place and water four-skill payoffs, then say home.

You'll want to know.
hāth — hand.
The source uses this word in right-hand and left-hand direction cues. Say the one long beat. Writing waits even though every sign is familiar.

Wrap-up Recall.
Source: Marwari Pathshala, Lesson 5.
[question — say your answer, then pause 10 seconds]
What body word does hāth name?


Lesson 2 of 7.
हाथ (hāth) — hand on the page.

Warm-up.
[pause 18 seconds]
Say hand and my, write ह, ा, थ, घर, and पति.

You'll want to know.
हा + थ becomes हाथ — hāth — hand.

Guided Practice.
Look, cover, wait five seconds, and write the word.

Wrap-up Recall.
Source: Marwari Pathshala, Lesson 5.
[question — say your answer, then pause 10 seconds]
From hāth, write the body word.


Lesson 3 of 7.
Hear paisā — money.

Warm-up.
[pause 16 seconds]
Say hand and market, write virama ्, then recall the ten-label four-skill checkpoint.

You'll want to know.
paisā — money.
The source uses this word when asking travel cost. Hear the opening pai and say the whole word. Writing waits until the next step.

Wrap-up Recall.
Source: Marwari Pathshala, Lesson 5.
[question — say your answer, then pause 10 seconds]
What does paisā mean here?


Lesson 4 of 7.
पैसा (paisā) — money on the page.

Warm-up.
[pause 18 seconds]
Say money, hand, and wife. Write प, ै, स, ा, ो, then हाथ (hāth).

You'll want to know.
पै + सा becomes पैसा — paisā — money.

Guided Practice.
Look, cover, wait five seconds, and write the word. Check ै before ा.

Wrap-up Recall.
Source: Marwari Pathshala, Lesson 5.
[question — say your answer, then pause 10 seconds]
From paisā, write the travel-cost word.


Lesson 5 of 7.
Retrieve five direction and travel words — five direction and travel words.

Warm-up.
[pause 18 seconds]
Write ज, घ, म्हारो, and पत्नी, then recall the three-place payoff.

Guided Practice.
Hear all five in mixed order, say each from meaning, read the five cards, then write the two named aloud.

Wrap-up Recall.
Source: Marwari Pathshala, Lesson 5.
[question — say your answer, then pause 40 seconds]
Write market, home, temple, hand, and money in order.


Lesson 6 of 7.
Payoff — five travel words in four skills.

Warm-up.
[pause 18 seconds]
Recall the three-place checkpoint, then say name and child before adding hand and money.

Guided Practice.
Identify five heard words.
Produce five words from meaning cues.
Match five printed cards to meanings.
Write three heard words without a model.
Pass each skill separately.

Wrap-up Recall.
Source: Marwari Pathshala, Lesson 5.
[question — say your answer, then pause 45 seconds]
Complete all four scored checks, then write the five words in order.


Lesson 7 of 7.
