Tamil, chapter 127: Star, Dew, Thunder, Lightning, Storm.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
நட்சத்திரம் (naṭcattiram) — a star.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for a leaf, then the Tamil for soil.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say the day words, நாள் and the formal தினம், then the night words, இரவு, ராத்திரி, and இருள், darkness.

You'll want to know: நட்சத்திரம்.
நட்சத்திரம் — naṭcattiram — "a star".

The word, taken apart.
நட்சத்திரம் (naṭcattiram) is a loan from Sanskrit nakṣatra, "star" (CDIAL 6913).

Lesson 5 of 5.
புயல் (puyal) — a storm.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for thunder, then the Tamil for lightning.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say வானிலை, then மழை பெய்கிறது. Name the rainy season, மழைக் காலம், the one that shapes the year here.

You'll want to know: புயல்.
புயல் — puyal — "a storm".

The word, taken apart.
புயல் (puyal) is native Dravidian (DEDR 4407). The entry lists puyal, "cloud, rain, gale, storm", under pey, "to rain".
