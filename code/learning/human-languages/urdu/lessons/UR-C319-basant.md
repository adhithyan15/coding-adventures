## You'll want to know: بسنت
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C319-BASANT319-01]; assesses=[] -->

**بسنت** — *basant* — "the spring kite festival".

> لاہور میں بسنت کا میلہ تھا۔
>
> *Lāhaur meṅ basant kā melā thā.*
>
> There was a spring kite fair in Lahore.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**بسنت** (*basant*) is inherited from Sanskrit *vasanta*, "spring" (Monier-Williams). In Lahore it names the day of kites at the end of winter.
