## You'll want to know: ঢাকনা
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C512-UNUN512-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ঢাকনা** — *ḍhāknā* — "a lid".

> হাঁড়ির ঢাকনাটা কোথায়?
>
> *Hā̃ṛir ḍhāknāṭā kothāy?*
>
> Where is the lid of the pot?

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ঢাকনা** (*ḍhāknā*) comes from a reconstructed root *ḍhakk*, "cover"; CDIAL lists Bengali *ḍhākan*, "lid" (CDIAL 5574).
