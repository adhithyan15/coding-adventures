## You'll want to know: ಸರಕು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C452-SARIGE452-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸರಕು** — *saraku* — "goods; cargo".

> ಲಾರಿಯಲ್ಲಿ ಸರಕು ತುಂಬಿದೆ.
>
> *lāriyalli saraku tumbide.*
>
> The lorry is full of goods.

ಸರಕು ರೈಲು, a goods train.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸರಕು** (*saraku*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 2353 lists Kannada *saraku*, *sarku*, "goods, things, merchandise, cargo".
