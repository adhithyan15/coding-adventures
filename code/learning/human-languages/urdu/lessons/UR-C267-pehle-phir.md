# پہلے، پھر (pehle, phir) — first, then, after that, in the end

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[UR-GRAMMAR-C267-KAR-01, UR-LEX-C77-TIME77-05, UR-LEX-C76-TIME76-05] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "having eaten". (*khā kar*.) Then "before" and "then". (*pehle*, *phir*.) A story puts its steps in a row; these words mark the row.

## Grammar Lens: first and then
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-GRAMMAR-C267-THEN-01]; assesses=[UR-LEX-C53-VERBS53-02, UR-LEX-C46-FAMILY46-02, UR-GRAMMAR-C265-ERGATIVE-01] -->

> **پہلے میں نہایا۔**
>
> *pehle maiṅ nahāyā.* — "First I had a bath."

> **پھر میں نے کھانا کھایا۔**
>
> *phir maiṅ ne khānā khāyā.* — "Then I ate."

**پہلے** (*pehle*) opens the first step and **پھر** (*phir*) the next. Each goes at the front of its line, so the order is clear before the verb arrives.

## You'll want to know: after that, in the end
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-GRAMMAR-C267-THEN-02]; assesses=[UR-LEX-C267-BAD-01, UR-LEX-C245-VAQT245-04, UR-LEX-C102-LOC102-04] -->

> **اس کے بعد میں دفتر گیا۔**
>
> *us ke bād maiṅ daftar gayā.* — "After that I went to the office."

> **آخر میں سارا گھر آئی۔**
>
> *ākhir meṅ Sārā ghar āī.* — "In the end Sara came home."

**اس کے بعد** (*us ke bād*) is "after that": **بعد** with **اس** (*us*). **آخر میں** (*ākhir meṅ*) closes the story.
