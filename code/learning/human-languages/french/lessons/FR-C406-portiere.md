## You'll want to know: la portière
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C406-CARROSSERIE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**la portière** — "the car door".

> Il a fermé la portière trop fort.
>
> *He shut the car door too hard.*

*La porte* for a house, *la portière* for a car or a train.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*portière* is built on *porte* 'door', which is inherited from Latin *porta* 'gate' (EtymDB).
