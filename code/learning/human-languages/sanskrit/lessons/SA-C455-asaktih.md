## You'll want to know: आसक्तिः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C455-ANURAGA455-04]; assesses=[] -->

**आसक्तिः** — *āsaktiḥ* — "attachment".

> वृद्धस्य गृहे आसक्तिः अस्ति।
>
> *vṛddhasya gṛhe āsaktiḥ asti.*
>
> The old man is attached to his house.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**आसक्तिः** (*āsaktiḥ*) is built from *ā* and the root √sañj, "to cling, to stick"; MW gives "devotedness, attachment" (MW).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
