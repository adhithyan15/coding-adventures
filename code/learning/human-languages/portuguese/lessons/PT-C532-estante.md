## You'll want to know: a estante
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C532-ACOLHEDORA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**a estante** — "bookcase".

> A estante da sala está cheia de livros.
>
> *The bookcase in the living room is full of books.*

A single shelf is *uma prateleira*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*estante* comes from Latin *stāns* "standing" (EtymDB), from *stāre* "to stand": a piece of furniture that stands up.
