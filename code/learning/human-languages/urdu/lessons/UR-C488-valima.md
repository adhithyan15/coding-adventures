## You'll want to know: ولیمہ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C488-INLAWS488-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ولیمہ** — *valīmā* — "a wedding feast (given by the groom)".

> ولیمے میں بریانی تھی۔
>
> *valīme meṅ biryānī thī.*
>
> There was biryani at the wedding feast.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ولیمہ** (*valīmā*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *walīma*, "a wedding feast".
