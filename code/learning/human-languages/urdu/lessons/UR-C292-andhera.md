## You'll want to know: اندھیرا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C292-TUFAN292-01]; assesses=[] -->

**اندھیرا** — *andherā* — "darkness".

> شہر میں اندھیرا تھا۔
>
> *shahr meṅ andherā thā.*
>
> The city was dark.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**اندھیرا** (*andherā*) is inherited from Sanskrit *andhakāra*, "darkness" (CDIAL 386), where CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *ãdherā*.
