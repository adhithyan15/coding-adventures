Bengali, chapter 277: Who Saw It.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
সাক্ষী (sākkhī) — a witness.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a human being, then the Bengali for an accident.

You'll want to know: সাক্ষী.
সাক্ষী — sākkhī — "a witness".
আগে পাড়ার বুড়ো দারোয়ান সব ঘটনার সাক্ষী থাকতেন। āge pāṛār buṛo dāroyān sôb ghôṭônār sākkhī thākten. In the old days the old gatekeeper of the neighbourhood used to witness everything that happened.

The word, taken apart.
সাক্ষী (sākkhī) is Sanskrit sākṣin, one who sees with his own eyes, and so a witness at law (Monier-Williams). The ক্ষ is read kkh.

Lesson 2 of 5.
কেউ (keu) — someone; anyone.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for an accident, then the Bengali for a witness.

You'll want to know: কেউ.
কেউ — keu — "someone; anyone".
দরজায় কেউ ছিল, কিন্তু আমি তার মুখ দেখলাম না। dôrjāy keu chhilô, kintu āmi tār mukh dekhlām nā. Someone was at the door, but I didn't see their face.
With না it means no one: কেউ (keu) এল না, nobody came.

The word, taken apart.
কেউ (keu) comes down from Sanskrit ko 'pi, 'someone' (CDIAL 3518, which lists Bengali keu): kaḥ 'who' with api 'even', whoever it may be.

Lesson 3 of 5.
সবাই (sôbāi) — everyone; everybody.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a witness, then the Bengali for someone.

You'll want to know: সবাই.
সবাই — sôbāi — "everyone; everybody".
আওয়াজ শুনে সবাই বাইরে এল। āoyāj shune sôbāi bāire elô. When they heard the noise, everyone came outside.

The word, taken apart.
সবাই (sôbāi) is সব, 'all' — Sanskrit sarva (CDIAL 13276, which lists Bengali sab) — with the emphatic ending that makes it 'every single one'. সবাই এল, everyone came.

Lesson 5 of 5.
হাজির হওয়া (hājir hôoyā) — to turn up; to be present.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for everyone, then the Bengali for a crowd.

You'll want to know: হাজির হওয়া.
হাজির হওয়া — hājir hôoyā — "to turn up; to be present".
পরের দিন সাক্ষী থানায় হাজির হল। pôrer din sākkhī thānāy hājir hôlô. The next day the witness turned up at the police station.
Past (he/she …): সেই সাক্ষী থানায় হাজির হল, sei sākkhī thānāy hājir hôlô.
With থাকা it means to be there: সবাই (sôbāi) হাজির ছিল, everyone was there.

The word, taken apart.
হাজির হওয়া (hājir hôoyā) puts হাজির, usually traced to Arabic ḥāḍir, 'present, in attendance', which reached Bengali through Persian, before হওয়া, to become: to become present, to turn up.
