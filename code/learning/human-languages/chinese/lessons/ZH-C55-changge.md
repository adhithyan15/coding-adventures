## You'll want to know: chànggē
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C55-LIKE55-04]; assesses=[] -->

*chànggē* — "to sing".

> *tā xǐhuan chànggē* — She loves singing.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*chànggē* is built from chàng 'to strike up a song' and gē 'song', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *tsyhangH* and *ka* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for everyday answers.
