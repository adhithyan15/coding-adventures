## You'll want to know: स्मयते
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C457-HASA457-01]; assesses=[] -->

**स्मयते** — *smayate* — "smiles".

> शिशुः मातरं पश्यति, स्मयते च।
>
> *śiśuḥ mātaraṃ paśyati, smayate ca.*
>
> The baby sees its mother and smiles.

Past (she …): सा स्मितवती, *sā smitavatī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**स्मयते** (*smayate*) comes from the root √smi, "to smile", the root of **विस्मयः** (MW).
