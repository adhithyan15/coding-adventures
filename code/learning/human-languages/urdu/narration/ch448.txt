Urdu, chapter 448: Bored or Curious.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
بیزار (bezār) — fed up; weary (of something).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for consolation, then the Urdu for pleasant.

You'll want to know: بیزار.
بیزار — bezār — "fed up; weary (of something)".
وہ روز ایک ہی کام سے بیزار ہے۔ voh roz ek hī kām se bezār hai. He is fed up with doing the same work every day.

The word, taken apart.
بیزار (bezār) is usually traced to Persian bēzār, "weary of, sick of".

What you've built.
One more word for describing what you lived and felt.

Lesson 3 of 5.
تجسس (tajassus) — curiosity.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for fed up, then the Urdu for interesting.

You'll want to know: تجسس.
تجسس — tajassus — "curiosity".
بچے کی آنکھوں میں تجسس تھا۔ bachche kī āṅkhoṅ meṅ tajassus thā. There was curiosity in the child's eyes.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
تجسس (tajassus) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic tajassus, "prying"; Urdu uses it for plain curiosity.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ٹوہنا (ṭohnā) — to feel about for; to probe.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for curiosity, then the Urdu for eager.

You'll want to know: ٹوہنا.
ٹوہنا — ṭohnā — "to feel about for; to probe".
اس نے جیب میں چابی ٹوہی۔ us ne jeb meṅ chābī ṭohī. He felt about in his pocket for the key.
Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے دیوار ٹوہی, us ne dīvār ṭohī.

The word, taken apart.
ٹوہنا (ṭohnā) is inherited from ṭōh, "grope", a form CDIAL reconstructs (CDIAL 5486).
