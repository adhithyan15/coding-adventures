Telugu, chapter 96: Names, Ages, Birthdays.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ఇంటిపేరు (iṇṭipēru) — a surname, the family name.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for please show me, then the Telugu for I could not hear it.

You'll want to know: ఇంటిపేరు.
ఇంటిపేరు (iṇṭipēru) — "a surname", literally "the house name".
It is ఇంటి, "of the house", and పేరు, "name". In Telugu the house name comes FIRST, before the given name, so a form that asks for a first name is asking the wrong question.

What you've built.
The family name, and where it goes.

[your turn — say: iṇṭipēru]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: iṇṭipēru, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say nā iṇṭipēru..., then your given name]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Telugu for please show me, then the Telugu for I could not hear it, then say iṇṭipēru again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does ఇంటిపేరు (iṇṭipēru) mean? ("A surname, the family name".) Say it once more.

You'll want to know: ముద్దుపేరు.
ముద్దుపేరు (muddupēru) — "a nickname, a pet name".
ముద్దు is "a kiss, affection", so a nickname is an affection name: the one family uses.

Lesson 3 of 5.
వయసు (vayasu) — age.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a surname, then the Telugu for a nickname.

You'll want to know: వయసు.
వయసు (vayasu) — "age".
You have asked it already, inside నీ వయసెంత?. Now the word stands alone: మీ వయసు (vayasu) ఎంత? — "what is your age?", the respectful form.

What you've built.
The word you have been using inside a question, taken out.

Lesson 4 of 5.
పుట్టినరోజు (puṭṭinarōju) — a birthday.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a nickname, then the Telugu for age.

You'll want to know: పుట్టినరోజు.
పుట్టినరోజు (puṭṭinarōju) — "a birthday", literally "the day one was born".
రోజు is the day you know. On the day, say పుట్టినరోజు (puṭṭinarōju) శుభాకాంక్షలు — "happy birthday", with the good wishes you already have.

What you've built.
A day to name, and a wish to make on it.

Lesson 5 of 5.
సంతకం (santakaṁ) — a signature.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for age, then the Telugu for a birthday.

You'll want to know: సంతకం.
సంతకం (santakaṁ) — "a signature".
ఇక్కడ సంతకం (santakaṁ) చేయండి — "please sign here". A signature is something you do, with the చేయు of పని చేయు.

What you've built.
Surname, nickname, age, birthday, signature: every box on a form.
