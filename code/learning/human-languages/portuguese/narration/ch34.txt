Portuguese, chapter 34: Pain, Fever, Cough, Medicine.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
a dor — pain.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for the throat, then the Portuguese for the stomach.

You'll want to know: a dor.
a dor — "pain". Put it with cabeça, "head", and you have a headache:
dor + de + cabeça becomes dor de cabeça.

The word, taken apart.
dor from Latin dolor, "pain" (EtymDB 2.1) — inherited. The Latin -l- between vowels fell out: dolor became Old Portuguese door, then dor.
