## You'll want to know: fās
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C414-INIURIA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**fās** — "divine law; what is right".

> Fās nōn est hoc dīcere.
>
> *It is not right to say this.*

*fās* n., indeclinable. *Fās est*, it is right, it is allowed by the gods; it covers what human law leaves out.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*fās* is inherited from a Proto-Indo-European noun meaning "a saying, an utterance" (EtymDB), and Lewis & Short link it with *fārī* "to speak": what the gods have declared.
