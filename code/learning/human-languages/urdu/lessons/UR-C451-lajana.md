## You'll want to know: لجانا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C451-REGRET451-02]; assesses=[] -->

**لجانا** — *lajānā* — "to blush; to be bashful".

> دولہا کو دیکھ کر دلہن لجا گئی۔
>
> *dūlhā ko dekh kar dulhan lajā gaī.*
>
> The bride blushed when she saw the bridegroom.

Past (she …): وہ لجا گئی, *voh lajā gaī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**لجانا** (*lajānā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *lajānā*, "to be ashamed", under Sanskrit *lajjátē*, "is ashamed" (CDIAL 10909).
