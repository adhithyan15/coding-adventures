# చదివేవాడిని (cadivēvāḍini) — what used to happen

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[TE-GRAMMAR-C316-SCENE-EVENT-01, TE-GRAMMAR-C316-PAST-SIMPLE-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "While I was having a meal, a call came." (*nēnu bhōjanaṁ cēstunnappuḍu phōnu vaccindi*.)

[PAUSE 1s] That happened once. Some things happened many times: what you did every day as a child.

## You'll want to know: -ఏవాడిని, 'used to'
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-GRAMMAR-C316-PAST-HABITUAL-01]; assesses=[TE-LEX-C290-SAMAYAM290-05, TE-LEX-C180-GUNAM180-03, TE-LEX-C312-PANI312-01, TE-LEX-C103-LOC103-02] -->

> **బాల్యంలో నేను ప్రతి రోజు ఒక కథ చదివేవాడిని.**
>
> *bālyaṁlō nēnu prati rōju oka katha cadivēvāḍini.* — "In my childhood I used to read a story every day."

**చదివేవాడిని** is "I used to read", said by a man. A woman says **చదివేదాన్ని** (*cadivēdānni*). Taken apart, it is the
**-ఏ** form of the verb (**చదివే**, "who reads") with **వాడు**, "that man", or **దాని**, "that one",
and the **-ని** of "I": roughly "I was one who reads".

- **నేను చదివేవాడిని** (*nēnu cadivēvāḍini*) / **చదివేదాన్ని** (*cadivēdānni*) — I used to read
- **అతను చదివేవాడు** (*atanu cadivēvāḍu*) — he used to read
- **ఆమె చదివేది** (*āme cadivēdi*) — she used to read
- **మేము చదివేవాళ్ళం** (*mēmu cadivēvāḷḷaṁ*) — we used to read
- **వాళ్ళు చదివేవాళ్ళు** (*vāḷḷu cadivēvāḷḷu*) — they used to read

The same **-ఏ** sits on any verb: **వెళ్ళేవాడిని**, "I used to go"; **తినేవాడిని**, "I used to eat".

## You'll want to know: ఉండేది, 'there used to be'
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-GRAMMAR-C316-UNDEDI-01]; assesses=[TE-LEX-C55-ROAD-04, TE-LEX-C72-MARKET-01, TE-LEX-C173-STHALAM173-03, TE-LEX-C42-ADJ-01, TE-LEX-C42-ADJ-02] -->

> **మా గ్రామంలో ఒక చిన్న అంగడి ఉండేది.**
>
> *mā grāmaṁlō oka cinna aṅgaḍi uṇḍēdi.* — "There used to be a small shop in our village."

**ఉండేది** is the same form of **ఉండు**, "to be": "it used to be there". Set it against the present:

> **ఇప్పుడు అక్కడ ఒక పెద్ద హోటలు ఉంది.**
>
> *ippuḍu akkaḍa oka pedda hōṭalu undi.* — "Now there is a big restaurant there."

A story about the old days leans on **ఉండేది** for the background.
