## You'll want to know: आकस्मिक
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C551-YADI551-05]; assesses=[] -->

**आकस्मिक** — *ākasmik* — "unforeseen; sudden".

> आकस्मिक खर्चासाठी मी थोडे पैसे बाजूला ठेवले.
>
> *ākasmik kharcāsāṭhī mī thoḍe paise bājūlā ṭhevle.*
>
> I put a little money aside for unforeseen expenses.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**आकस्मिक** (*ākasmik*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *ākasmika*, "causeless, unforeseen" (Monier-Williams).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
