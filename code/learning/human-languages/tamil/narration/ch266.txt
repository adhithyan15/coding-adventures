Tamil, chapter 266: Easily, On purpose, By chance, Luckily, Reason.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
எளிதாக (eḷitāka) — easily.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for a prison, then the Tamil for the world.

You'll want to know: எளிதாக.
எளிதாக — eḷitāka — "easily".

The word, taken apart.
எளிதாக (eḷitāka) is native: eḷitu, "what is easy", from eḷi, "easy" (DEDR 855), and āka, "as" (DEDR 333).
