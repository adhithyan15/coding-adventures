Lesson 1 of 6.
అట్టపెట్టె (aṭṭapeṭṭe) — a cardboard box.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a refund, then the Telugu for to double-check.

You'll want to know: అట్టపెట్టె.
అట్టపెట్టె — aṭṭapeṭṭe — "a cardboard box".
పుస్తకాలను ఒక అట్టపెట్టెలో పెట్టాం. pustakālanu oka aṭṭapeṭṭelō peṭṭāṁ. We put the books in a cardboard box.
అట్ట, cardboard, + పెట్టె, a box.

The word, taken apart.
అట్టపెట్టె (aṭṭapeṭṭe) is native Dravidian: అట్ట, "leather, cardboard" (DEDR 98), and పెట్టె, "a box" (DEDR 4388).

Lesson 2 of 6.
వదిలివెళ్ళు (vadiliveḷḷu) — to leave behind.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to double-check, then the Telugu for a cardboard box.

You'll want to know: వదిలివెళ్ళు.
వదిలివెళ్ళు — vadiliveḷḷu — "to leave behind".
నా గొడుగు బస్సులో వదిలివెళ్ళాను. nā goḍugu bassulō vadiliveḷḷānu. I left my umbrella behind on the bus.
Past (I …): వదిలివెళ్ళాను, vadiliveḷḷānu.
వదిలి, having left, + వెళ్ళు, to go: to go and leave something.

The word, taken apart.
వదిలివెళ్ళు (vadiliveḷḷu) is native Dravidian: వదిలి, from వదులు, "to let go, to leave" (DEDR 5243), and వెళ్ళు, "to go".

Lesson 3 of 6.
పారేసుకో (pārēsukō) — to mislay; to lose by carelessness.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a cardboard box, then the Telugu for to leave behind.

You'll want to know: పారేసుకో.
పారేసుకో — pārēsukō — "to mislay; to lose by carelessness".
అతను స్టేషన్లో పర్సు పారేసుకున్నాడు. atanu sṭēṣanlō parsu pārēsukunnāḍu. He lost his purse at the station.
Past (I …): పారేసుకున్నాను, pārēsukunnānu.
పారేయు, to throw away, + -కో: to lose something of one's own by carelessness.

The word, taken apart.
పారేసుకో (pārēsukō) is a Telugu verb built on పారేయు, "to throw away", a compound whose first part has an origin that is not settled and whose second part is వేయు, "to put" (DEDR 5549), with -కో, "for oneself" (DEDR 2151).

Lesson 4 of 6.
తరలించు (taraliñcu) — to shift elsewhere; to move.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to leave behind, then the Telugu for to mislay.

You'll want to know: తరలించు.
తరలించు — taraliñcu — "to shift elsewhere; to move".
వరద ముందు ప్రజలను సురక్షిత ప్రాంతాలకు తరలించారు. varada mundu prajalanu surakṣita prāntālaku taraliñcāru. Before the flood people were moved to safe places.
Past (I …): తరలించాను, taraliñcānu.
Said of moving people, goods or an office to another place.

The word, taken apart.
తరలించు (taraliñcu) is native Dravidian: DEDR 3128 lists Telugu taralu, "to set out, to start", here made causative with -ఇంచు: to make something move off.

Lesson 5 of 6.
చేరవేయు (cēravēyu) — to deliver; to carry to.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to mislay, then the Telugu for to shift elsewhere.

You'll want to know: చేరవేయు.
చేరవేయు — cēravēyu — "to deliver; to carry to".
కూలీలు సామాను రైలు దగ్గరికి చేరవేశారు. kūlīlu sāmānu railu daggariki cēravēśāru. The porters carried the luggage to the train.
Past (I …): చేరవేశాను, cēravēśānu.
చేర, to the place, + వేయు, to put: to get something to where it must go.

The word, taken apart.
చేరవేయు (cēravēyu) is native Dravidian: చేర, from చేరు, "to reach" (DEDR 2814), and వేయు, "to put" (DEDR 5549).
