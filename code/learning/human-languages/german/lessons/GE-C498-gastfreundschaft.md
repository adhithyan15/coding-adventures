## You'll want to know: die Gastfreundschaft
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C498-FREUNDE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**die Gastfreundschaft** — "hospitality".

> Vielen Dank für eure Gastfreundschaft!
>
> *Thank you so much for your hospitality!*

No plural.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Gastfreundschaft* is built on *Gast* "guest", inherited from Old High German *gast*, and *Freundschaft* "friendship" (EtymDB).
