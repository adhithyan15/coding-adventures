## You'll want to know: اعداد
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C350-AUSAT350-02]; assesses=[] -->

**اعداد** — *adād* — "figures (statistics)".

> یہ اعداد کیا بتاتے ہیں؟
>
> *yih adād kyā batāte haiṅ?*
>
> What do these figures tell us?

Masculine, used in the plural.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**اعداد** (*adād*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *aʿdād*, "numbers", the plural of *ʿadad*, "a number".
