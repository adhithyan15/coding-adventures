## You'll want to know: أوصى
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C446-KHIDMA446-05]; assesses=[] -->

**أوصى** — *awṣā* — "to recommend".

> أوصانا المرشد بمطعم صغير.
>
> *awṣānā al-murshid bi-maṭʿam ṣaghīr.*
>
> The guide recommended a small restaurant to us.

Present: يوصي, *yūṣī*.

It takes *bi-* before the thing recommended.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**أوصى** (*awṣā*) is built on the native Arabic root *w-ṣ-y*, "to join; to enjoin" (Lane). It is Form IV.
