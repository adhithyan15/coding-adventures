## You'll want to know: невиновный
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C392-OGRANICHENIYA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**невиновный** (*nevinóvnyy*) — "innocent, not guilty".

> Судья решил, что он невиновен.
>
> *Sudyá reshíl, chto on nevinóven.*
>
> The judge decided that he was not guilty.

Short form *невиновен*. A guilty person is *виновный*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**невиновный** is built from *не-* "not" and *виновный* "guilty", on *вина* "guilt", which is usually traced to Proto-Slavic *\*vina*.
