## You'll want to know: ઉદ્યોગ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C513-KARKHANU513-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ઉદ્યોગ** — *udyog* — "an industry".

> અમારા શહેરમાં ઘણા મોટા ઉદ્યોગ છે.
>
> *amārā śahermā̃ ghaṇā moṭā udyog che.*
>
> There are many big industries in our city.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ઉદ્યોગ** (*udyog*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *udyoga*, "exertion, an undertaking" (Monier-Williams); modern Gujarati uses it for an industry.
