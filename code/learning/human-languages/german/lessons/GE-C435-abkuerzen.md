## You'll want to know: abkürzen
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C435-UMWEG-02]; assesses=[] -->

**abkürzen** — "to take a shortcut; to shorten".

> Wir kürzen über den Parkplatz ab.
>
> *We take a shortcut across the car park.*

Separable: *wir kürzen ab*, *wir haben abgekürzt*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*abkürzen* is built from *ab* and *kürzen* 'to shorten', from *kurz* 'short', which goes back to Latin *curtus* 'shortened' (EtymDB; Lewis & Short).
