## You'll want to know: caballus, caballī
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C433-IUMENTUM-02]; assesses=[] -->

**caballus, caballī** — "a nag, a pack horse".

> Caballus vetus et lentus est, sed sarcinās portat.
>
> *The nag is old and slow, but it carries the bags.*

Second declension, masculine: *caballus, caballī m.* An everyday, low word; the fine word is *equus*. French *cheval* and English *cavalry* come from it.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*caballus* is usually traced to Gaulish *caballos* 'pack horse' (EtymDB); Lewis & Short also call it perhaps Celtic and compare Greek *kaballēs*. French *cheval* and, through Italian, English *cavalry* go back to it.
