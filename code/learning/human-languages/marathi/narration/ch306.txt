Marathi, chapter 306: Bit by Bit.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
क्रमशः (kramaśaḥ) — step by step; gradually.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for over time, then the Marathi for to elapse.

You'll want to know: क्रमशः.
क्रमशः — kramaśaḥ — "step by step; gradually".
नदीचं पाणी क्रमशः कमी झालं. nadīcaṁ pāṇī kramaśaḥ kamī jhālaṁ. The water in the river went down step by step.
Formal; at the end of a serial story it means to be continued.

The word, taken apart.
क्रमशः (kramaśaḥ) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit kramaśas, 'gradually, by degrees' (Monier-Williams).

You'll want to know: अनेकदा.
अनेकदा — anekdā — "many times".
मी हा किल्ला अनेकदा पाहिला आहे. mī hā killā anekdā pāhilā āhe. I have seen this fort many times.
Built from अनेक, many, and -दा, times.

The word, taken apart.
अनेकदा (anekdā) is built from Sanskrit aneka, 'not one, many' (Monier-Williams), and the ending -dā, 'times'.

Lesson 3 of 5.
अधूनमधून (adhūnmadhūn) — now and then.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for step by step, then the Marathi for many times.

You'll want to know: अधूनमधून.
अधूनमधून — adhūnmadhūn — "now and then".
आजोबा अधूनमधून जुन्या गोष्टी सांगतात. ājobā adhūnmadhūn junyā goṣṭī sāṅgtāt. Grandpa tells old stories now and then.
A rhyming pair; कधीकधी means much the same.

The word, taken apart.
अधूनमधून (adhūnmadhūn) is a rhyming pair built on madhūn, 'from the middle'; the origin of the first half, adhūn, is not settled.

Lesson 4 of 5.
आपोआप (āpoāp) — by itself; automatically.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for many times, then the Marathi for now and then.

You'll want to know: आपोआप.
आपोआप — āpoāp — "by itself; automatically".
वारा आला आणि दार आपोआप बंद झालं. vārā ālā āṇi dār āpoāp band jhālaṁ. The wind blew and the door shut by itself.
A doubling of आप, self.

The word, taken apart.
आपोआप (āpoāp) is built from āp, 'self', said twice; CDIAL lists Marathi āplā, 'own', under Sanskrit ātman, 'self' (CDIAL 1135).

Lesson 5 of 5.
नुकताच (nuktāc) — lately; a moment ago.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for now and then, then the Marathi for by itself.

You'll want to know: नुकताच.
नुकताच — nuktāc — "lately; a moment ago".
तो नुकताच गावाहून परत आला. to nuktāc gāvāhūn parat ālā. He has recently come back from the village.
It agrees like an adjective: तो नुकताच (nuktāc) आला, ती नुकतीच आली, ते नुकतंच आलं.

The word, taken apart.
नुकताच (nuktāc) is a Marathi word for lately, made with the emphatic -c; its origin is not settled.
