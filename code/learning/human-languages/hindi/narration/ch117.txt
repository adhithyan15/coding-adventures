Hindi, chapter 117: Aunts, Uncles, In-Laws.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
मौसी (mausī) — aunt, the mother's sister.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for the mother's mother, then the Hindi for the father's younger brother.

You'll want to know: मौसी.
मौसी (mausī) — "aunt", your mother's sister.
It is often explained as माँ जैसी, "like a mother": the aunt closest to a mother's place.

What you've built.
An aunt, on your mother's side.

Lesson 2 of 5.
बुआ (buā) — aunt, the father's sister.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for the father's younger brother, then the Hindi for the mother's sister.

You'll want to know: बुआ.
बुआ (buā) — "aunt", your father's sister.
मौसी (mausī) and बुआ (buā) are both "aunt" in English; in Hindi the side of the family decides.

What you've built.
Both aunts, and the side each belongs to.

Lesson 4 of 5.
भाभी (bhābhī) — sister-in-law, a brother's wife.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for the father's sister, then the Hindi for the mother's brother.

You'll want to know: भाभी.
भाभी (bhābhī) — "sister-in-law", specifically your brother's wife.
It shares its start with भाई, "brother": she is named through him.

What you've built.
A relative by marriage, named through your brother.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ससुराल (sasurāl) — the in-laws' home.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for the mother's brother, then the Hindi for a brother's wife.

You'll want to know: ससुराल.
ससुराल (sasurāl) — "the in-laws' home", the family a person marries into.
वह ससुराल (sasurāl) गई है — "she has gone to her in-laws' home".

What you've built.
Aunts, uncles, a sister-in-law, and the home of the in-laws.
