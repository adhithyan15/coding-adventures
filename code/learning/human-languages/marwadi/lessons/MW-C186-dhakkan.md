## You'll want to know: ढक्कण
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C186-DHAKKAN186-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ढक्कण** — *ḍhakkaṇ* — "a lid".

> हांडी माथै ढक्कण है।
>
> *hā̃ḍī māthai ḍhakkaṇ hai.*
>
> There's a lid on the pot.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ढक्कण** (*ḍhakkaṇ*) is built on a reconstructed *\*ḍhakk*, "cover", the word behind **ढांकणो**; CDIAL lists Sindhi and Gujarati words for a lid under it, beside Marwari *ḍhā̃kṇo* (CDIAL 5574).
