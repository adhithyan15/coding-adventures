# ਟ · ਠ · ਡ — three pieces met inside a word you already say

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-SCRIPT-RECOG-MA-01, PA-SCRIPT-RECOG-RA-01, PA-SCRIPT-RECOG-LAVA-01, PA-SCRIPT-RECOG-SA-01, PA-SCRIPT-RECOG-TA-01, PA-SCRIPT-RECOG-KA-01, PA-SCRIPT-RECOG-NANNA-01, PA-SCRIPT-RECOG-BIHARI-01, PA-SCRIPT-RECOG-DULAVA-01, PA-PHRASE-HOW-ARE-YOU-03, PA-GRAMMAR-TUSI-HO-03, PA-LEX-MAIN-03, PA-GRAMMAR-MAIN-HAAN-03, PA-ETYMON-FIRST-PERSON-M-03] -->

[PAUSE 2s] English has no letter for any of these. The tongue curls back to the roof of the mouth for all three.

memory:

- [YOU SAY: *tusī̃ kivēṁ ho?* · *tusī̃ kivēṁ ho — k-* · *maiṁ*]

## Script you'll notice: ਟ · ਠ · ਡ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-SCRIPT-RECOG-TAINKA-01, PA-SCRIPT-RECOG-TTHA-01, PA-SCRIPT-RECOG-DDA-01]; assesses=[] -->

> ਟ

**ṭaiṅkā** — *ṭa*. A **retroflex** *t*, with no breath: the tongue curls back and taps the roof of the mouth.

> ਠ

**ṭhaṭṭhā** — *ṭha*. A **retroflex** *t* with a puff of breath: tongue curled back to the roof of the mouth.

> ਡ

**ḍaḍḍā** — *ḍa*. A **retroflex** *d*, the voiced partner of ṭaiṅkā.

Now put them where you have already heard them:

> **ਟ**  **ਠ**  **ਡ** — one row, one tongue position, three degrees of breath and voice
>
> **ਠ** + **ੀ** + **ਕ** → **ਠੀਕ** (*ṭhīk*)
>
> **ਠੀਕ** — *ṭhīk* — **fine, all right**

You are not being asked to write any of this. You are being asked to
**recognise** it the next time it goes past.

## Writing: copy what you can see
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-SCRIPT-RECOG-TAINKA-01, PA-SCRIPT-RECOG-TTHA-01, PA-SCRIPT-RECOG-DDA-01] -->

Keep ਟ · ਠ · ਡ in front of you and do not cover any of them. Follow each shape once with a
finger, then copy it once with a pencil, larger than it is printed. Say its
sound as your hand stops.

> This is **observe-and-trace with the model visible**. Nothing in these
> chapters asks you to write a Punjabi word from memory; that ladder starts
> later, and it starts from these same shapes.
