# నుంచి (nunci) — from

## You'll want to know: నుంచి
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C77-FROM-01]; assesses=[] -->

**నుంచి** (*nunci*) — "from".

You already have **-కు**, "to", and **-లో**, "in". నుంచి is the third of the set and the one an introduction needs.

Like them it follows its noun: **గ్రామం నుంచి** — "from the village".

## Grammar Lens: the third relation word
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-GRAMMAR-C77-FROM-01]; assesses=[] -->

Three now sit behind a noun and do three different jobs: **-లో** puts you inside, **-కు** sends you toward, **నుంచి** takes you out of. Telugu has no words in front of nouns at all; everything a preposition would do in English is done by a piece glued to the back.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

The direction you came out of.
