## You'll want to know: ひなん
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C274-JISHIN274-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ひなん** — *hinan* — "evacuation; taking refuge".

> **じしんの あと、ちちが ひなんを 言い出しました。** — *jishin no ato, chichi ga hinan o iidashimashita* — "After the earthquake, my father said we should leave."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ひなん** (*hinan*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *hi*, "avoid" (Middle Chinese *bjieH*) and *nan*, "trouble" (*nanH*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
