## You'll want to know: le cil
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C478-VISAGE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**le cil** — "eyelash".

> Le bébé a de longs cils noirs.
>
> *The baby has long black eyelashes.*

Mostly plural: *les cils*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*le cil* is inherited from Latin *cilium* "eyelid" (Lewis & Short); in French it came to name the hairs on the eyelid.
