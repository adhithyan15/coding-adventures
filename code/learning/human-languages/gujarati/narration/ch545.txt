Gujarati, chapter 545: Easy or Complicated.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
આસાન (āsān) — effortless.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a copy, then the Gujarati for evenly matched.

You'll want to know: આસાન.
આસાન — āsān — "effortless".
જો તમે આ રીત વાપરશો તો કામ આસાન બનશે. jo tame ā rīt vāparśo to kām āsān banśe. If you use this method, the work will become easy.

The word, taken apart.
આસાન (āsān) is usually traced to Persian āsān, "easy"; the everyday Gujarati word beside it is સહેલું.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for complicated, then the Gujarati for importance.

You'll want to know: અજમાયશ.
અજમાયશ — ajmāyaś — "a try-out".
જો અજમાયશમાં મશીન બરાબર ચાલશે તો અમે તે ખરીદીશું. jo ajmāyaśmā̃ maśīn barābar cālśe to ame te kharīdīśũ. If the machine runs properly in the trial, we'll buy it.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
અજમાયશ (ajmāyaś) is usually traced to Persian āzmāyish, "a trial, a test"; the verb અજમાવવું, to try out, belongs with it.
