## You'll want to know: esplorare
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C502-CAMPEGGIO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**esplorare** — "to explore".

> I bambini vogliono esplorare il bosco.
>
> *The children want to explore the woods.*

The person is *l'esploratore*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*esplorare* is a learned borrowing from Latin *explōrāre* "to search out, to examine" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short).
