## You'll want to know: कृन्तति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C512-CHURIKA512-02]; assesses=[] -->

**कृन्तति** — *kṛntati* — "cuts up".

> सः आलुकानि कृन्तति।
>
> *saḥ ālukāni kṛntati.*
>
> He cuts up the potatoes.

Past (he …): सः पलाण्डुं कृत्तवान्, *saḥ palāṇḍuṃ kṛttavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**कृन्तति** (*kṛntati*) comes from the root √kṛt, "to cut, to cut in pieces, to divide", whose present is *kṛntati*; its past participle is *kṛtta* (MW).
