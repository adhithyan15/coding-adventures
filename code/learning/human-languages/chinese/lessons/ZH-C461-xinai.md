## You'll want to know: 心爱
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C461-KIND461-04]; assesses=[] -->

**心爱** — *xīn'ài* — "beloved; treasured".

> **这是我最心爱的书** *zhè shì wǒ zuì xīn'ài de shū* — This is my most treasured book.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**心爱** (*xīn'ài*) is built from 心 'heart' and 爱 'to love', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *sim* and *'ojH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'to cherish'.
