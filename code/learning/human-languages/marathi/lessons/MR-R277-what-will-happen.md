# Review — what will happen

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MR-GRAMMAR-C277-FUTURE-PERSON-01, MR-GRAMMAR-C276-PAST-PERSON-01, MR-GRAMMAR-C276-ERGATIVE-NE-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "Yesterday I went to Pune" and "Tomorrow I will go to Pune". (*kāl mī puṇyālā gelo*, *udyā mī puṇyālā jāīn*.) Then "he did the work". (*tyāne kām kelaṁ*.)

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MR-GRAMMAR-C277-FUTURE-PERSON-01, MR-GRAMMAR-C277-FUTURE-STEMS-01, MR-GRAMMAR-C277-NAR-PLAN-01, MR-GRAMMAR-C277-NAR-NAHI-01, MR-GRAMMAR-C276-WAS-01, MR-GRAMMAR-C276-WAS-NOT-01, MR-GRAMMAR-C276-OBJECT-AGREEMENT-01, MR-LEX-C99-NATURE99-01, MR-LEX-C109-ADJ109-02] -->

[PAUSE 2s each]
- [YOU SAY: Yesterday it rained. Tomorrow it will rain too.]
- [YOU SAY: Yesterday I was not at home. Tomorrow I will be at home.]
- [YOU SAY: Next week she is going to go to the village.]
- [YOU SAY: I won't come tomorrow, because I am going to read a book.]
- [YOU SAY: What will happen? What happened?]

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MR-GRAMMAR-C277-FUTURE-PERSON-01, MR-GRAMMAR-C277-FUTURE-STEMS-01, MR-GRAMMAR-C277-NAR-PLAN-01, MR-GRAMMAR-C277-NAR-NAHI-01] -->

[PAUSE 3s] "I will"? (**-ईन**: **जाईन**, **येईन**.) "It will"? (**-एल** or **-ईल**: **पडेल**, **जाईल**.) A plan? (**-णार आहे**.) "Won't"? (**-णार नाही**.)
