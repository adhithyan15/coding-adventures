## You'll want to know: أوقف
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C317-TAWQIF317-03]; assesses=[] -->

**أوقف** — *awqafa* — "to stop someone; to arrest".

> أوقف الشرطي السيارة عند الجسر.
>
> *awqafa ash-shurṭī as-sayyāra ʿinda al-jisr.*
>
> The policeman stopped the car at the bridge.

Present: يوقف, *yūqif*.

توقف is to stop oneself; أوقف is to stop someone or something.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**أوقف** (*awqafa*) is built on the native Arabic root *w-q-f* (Lane), "to stand still", the root of *tawaqqafa*, to stop. It is Form IV, "to make stand still".
