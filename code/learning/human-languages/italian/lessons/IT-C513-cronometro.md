## You'll want to know: il cronometro
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C513-GARA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**il cronometro** — "stopwatch".

> L'allenatore ferma il cronometro all'arrivo.
>
> *The coach stops the stopwatch at the finish.*

*Una gara a cronometro* — a time trial.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*cronometro* is a modern learned word built from Greek *khrónos* "time" and *métron* "measure" (EtymDB).
