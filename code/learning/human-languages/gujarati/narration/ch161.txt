Gujarati, chapter 161: Moving things about.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ખસેડવું (khaseḍvũ) — to move, to shift.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for electric light, then the Gujarati for to put out.

You'll want to know: ખસેડવું.
ખસેડવું — khaseḍvũ — "to move, to shift".
અમે પલંગ ખસેડીએ છીએ. — ame palaṅg khaseḍīe chīe — "We move the bed."

The word, taken apart.
ખસેડવું (khaseḍvũ) is built on the inherited khasvũ, "to slide, move", with a causative ending. CDIAL traces khasvũ to khasati, "falls, slips", a reconstructed form (CDIAL 3856).

Lesson 2 of 5.

You'll want to know: ખંખેરવું.
ખંખેરવું — khankhervũ — "to shake out, to dust off".
તે ચાદર ખંખેરે છે. — te cādar khaṅkhere che — "She shakes the dust out of the sheet."

The word, taken apart.
ખંખેરવું (khankhervũ) is a tadbhava. CDIAL lists it, glossed "to shake off dust", under khaṅkhālayati, "rinses", a reconstructed form it takes to imitate a sound (CDIAL 3762).

Lesson 3 of 5.

Warm-up.

You'll want to know: ઢાંકવું.
ઢાંકવું — ḍhā̃kvũ — "to cover".
માતા તપેલી ઢાંકે છે. — mātā tapelī ḍhā̃ke che — "Mother covers the pot."

The word, taken apart.
ઢાંકવું (ḍhā̃kvũ) is a tadbhava. It comes from ḍhakk, ḍhaṅk, "cover", a reconstructed form beside Sanskrit ḍhakkana, "shutting" (CDIAL 5574).

You'll want to know: તૂટવું.
તૂટવું — tūṭvũ — "to break, to get broken".
પાતળી સોય જલદી તૂટે છે. — pātḷī soy jaldī tūṭe che — "A thin needle breaks quickly."

The word, taken apart.
તૂટવું (tūṭvũ) is a tadbhava. It comes from Sanskrit truṭyati, "is broken, falls apart", and CDIAL lists the Gujarati tuṭvũ, "is broken" (CDIAL 6065).

Lesson 5 of 5.
ભેગું કરવું (bhegũ karvũ) — to gather, to collect.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to cover, then the Gujarati for to break.

You'll want to know: ભેગું કરવું.
ભેગું કરવું — bhegũ karvũ — "to gather, to collect".
તે કચરો ભેગો કરે છે. — te kacro bhego kare che — "He gathers up the rubbish."
Here ભેગો agrees with કચરો.

The word, taken apart.
ભેગું કરવું (bhegũ karvũ) is built from bhegũ, "together, collected", and the inherited karvũ, "to do". CDIAL lists bhegũ, "collected, mixed together", under bhēkkha, bhēgga, "lump", a reconstructed form (CDIAL 9602).
