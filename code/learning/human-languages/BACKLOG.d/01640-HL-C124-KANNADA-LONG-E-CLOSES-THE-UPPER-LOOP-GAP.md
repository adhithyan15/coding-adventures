## HL-C124 — Kannada long e closes the upper-loop gap

After Japanese hiragana **な** landed, Kannada independent vowel **ಏ** led the
measured queue at **3 affected realizations**.

Gopala Krishna A's CC BY-SA 4.0, 31-frame Wikimedia Commons animation supplies
the two-run order: carry the compact left loop through the joined lower curves
and tall outer arch, then lift once and draw the small upper loop from left to
right. The learner filmstrip fits that order to the bundled Noto Sans Kannada
outline without presenting print geometry as an independent handwriting
source.

This closes all 3 affected realizations for **ಏ** and reranks Kannada
independent vowel **ಒ** first at **3**.
