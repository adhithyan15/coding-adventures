## You'll want to know: わりびき
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C185-SALE185-01]; assesses=[] -->

**わりびき** — *waribiki* — "discount; reduction".

> **この くつは わりびきです。** — *kono kutsu wa waribiki desu* — "These shoes are discounted."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**わりびき** (*waribiki*) is native Japanese, built from *wari*, the stem of *waru*, "to divide", and *biki*, from *hiku*, "to pull, to take off" (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
