## You'll want to know: नेक
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C466-DAYA466-03]; assesses=[] -->

**नेक** — *nek* — "good-hearted; upright".

> अरुण के दादा बहुत नेक इंसान थे।
>
> *Aruṇ ke dādā bahut nek insān the.*
>
> Arun's grandfather was a very good-hearted man.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**नेक** (*nek*) is usually traced to Persian *nēk*, "good".
