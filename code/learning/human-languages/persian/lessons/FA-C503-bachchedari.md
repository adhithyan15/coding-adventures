## You'll want to know: بچه داری
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C503-PLAY-03]; assesses=[] -->

**بچه داری** (*bachchedâri*) — "childcare, bringing up children".

> بچه داری کار سختی است.
>
> *bachchedâri kâr-e sakhti ast.*
>
> Bringing up children is hard work.

*bachche* 'child' + *dâr* 'keeping' (stem of *dâshtan*) + *-i*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*bachchedâri* is built on native Persian *bachche*, 'child', which EtymDB traces to a Middle Persian word for 'child, baby, cub' (EtymDB), and *dâr*, the stem of *dâshtan*, 'to have, to keep'.
