## You'll want to know: પટારો
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C394-PORTER394-02]; assesses=[] -->

**પટારો** — *paṭāro* — "a trunk (a large box)".

> દાદીનો જૂનો પટારો ઘણો ભારે છે.
>
> *dādīno jūno paṭāro ghaṇo bhāre che.*
>
> Grandmother's old trunk is very heavy.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**પટારો** (*paṭāro*) goes back to Sanskrit *piṭaka*, "a basket, a box"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati word for a large box (CDIAL 8164).
