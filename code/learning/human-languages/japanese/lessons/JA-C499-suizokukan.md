## You'll want to know: すいぞくかん
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C499-PARK499-03]; assesses=[] -->

**すいぞくかん** — *suizokukan* — "an aquarium".

> **すいぞくかんには さめが います。** — *suizokukan ni wa same ga imasu* — "There are sharks at the aquarium."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**すいぞくかん** (*suizokukan*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): three Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *sui*, "water" (Middle Chinese *sywijX*), *zoku*, "family" (*dzuwk*) and *kan*, "building" (*kwanH*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
