## You'll want to know: টস
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C534-BAJI534-03]; assesses=[] -->

**টস** — *ṭôs* — "a coin toss".

> টস জিতলে অধিনায়ক আগে ব্যাট করবে।
>
> *Ṭôs jitle ôdhināyôk āge bæṭ kôrbe.*
>
> If the captain wins the toss, she will bat first.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**টস** (*ṭôs*) is borrowed from English *toss*.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
