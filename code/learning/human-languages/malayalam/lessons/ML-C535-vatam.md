## You'll want to know: വാതം
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C535-VATAM535-04]; assesses=[] -->

**വാതം** — *vātaṁ* — "rheumatism; joint pain".

> മഴക്കാലത്ത് മുത്തച്ഛന് വാതം കൂടും.
>
> *maḻakkālattŭ muttacchanŭ vātaṁ kūṭuṁ.*
>
> In the rainy season grandfather's rheumatism gets worse.

In traditional medicine വാതം is also one of the three humours of the body.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**വാതം** (*vātaṁ*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *vāta*, "wind", and in medicine "wind as a humour of the body" (CDIAL 11491).
