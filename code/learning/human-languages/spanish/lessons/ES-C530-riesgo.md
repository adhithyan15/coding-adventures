## You'll want to know: el riesgo
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C530-PELIGRO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**el riesgo** — "the risk".

> Hay riesgo de incendio.
>
> *There is a risk of fire.*

*Correr un riesgo* — to take a risk.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

The origin of *riesgo* is not settled. EtymDB links it to old Spanish *riesco* and Italian *rischio*, but the older source of that word is still disputed.
