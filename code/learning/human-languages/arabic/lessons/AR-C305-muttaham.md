## You'll want to know: متهم
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C305-MAHKAMA305-04]; assesses=[] -->

**متهم** — *muttaham* — "a defendant, an accused".

> قال متهم إنه كان في بيته.
>
> *qāla muttaham innahu kāna fī baytihi.*
>
> One defendant said he had been at home.

Plural متهمون.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**متهم** (*muttaham*) is built on the native Arabic root *w-h-m* (Lane), "to suspect". *Muttaham* is the passive participle of *ittahama*: "the one accused".
