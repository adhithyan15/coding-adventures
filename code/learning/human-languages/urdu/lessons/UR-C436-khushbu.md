## You'll want to know: خوشبو
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C436-QUDRAT436-02]; assesses=[] -->

**خوشبو** — *khushbū* — "a fragrance; a scent".

> پھولوں کی خوشبو کمرے تک آتی ہے۔
>
> *phūloṅ kī khushbū kamre tak ātī hai.*
>
> The scent of the flowers reaches the room.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**خوشبو** (*khushbū*) is built from Persian *khush*, "pleasant", and *bū*, "a smell": it is usually traced to Persian *khushbū*; **بدبو** is its opposite.
