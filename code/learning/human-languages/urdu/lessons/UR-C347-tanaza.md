## You'll want to know: تنازعہ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C347-MUAMALA347-03]; assesses=[] -->

**تنازعہ** — *tanāzā* — "a dispute".

> زمین پر دو بھائیوں میں تنازعہ تھا۔
>
> *zamīn par do bhāiyoṅ meṅ tanāzā thā.*
>
> Two brothers had a dispute over land.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**تنازعہ** (*tanāzā*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *tanāzuʿ*, "contending with one another".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
