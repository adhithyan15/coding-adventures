# Review — bigger, better, and how long

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[SA-GRAMMAR-C282-FEELING-01, SA-GRAMMAR-C282-AMBITION-02] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "I am pleased", then "my dream is that I will become a doctor". (*ahaṃ prasannaḥ asmi*; *mama svapnaḥ asti, yat ahaṃ vaidyaḥ bhaviṣyāmi*.)

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[SA-GRAMMAR-C283-COMPARISON-01, SA-GRAMMAR-C283-COMPARISON-02, SA-GRAMMAR-C283-DURATION-01, SA-GRAMMAR-C283-DURATION-02, SA-LEX-C283-IDRSAH-01, SA-SCRIPT-RECOG-229, SA-LEX-C16-PLACE-05, SA-LEX-C183-TIME183-03] -->

[PAUSE 2s each]
- [YOU SAY: The city is bigger than the village.]
- [YOU SAY: The train goes faster than the bus.]
- [YOU SAY: Of all fruits, the mango is the best.]
- [YOU SAY: I worked from morning until evening.]
- [YOU SAY: She stayed in the village for two months. I like such a life.]
- [YOU READ: **ईदृशः** aloud, and say the sign that is its sitting form]

## Wrap-up Recall

[PAUSE 3s] **ग्रामापेक्षया** or **ग्रामात्**, with **महत्तरम्**? (**ग्रामात्**.) **एकां होराम्**: one hour, or the hour itself? (For one hour.) Now the other way round: "until"? (**पर्यन्तम्**.)
