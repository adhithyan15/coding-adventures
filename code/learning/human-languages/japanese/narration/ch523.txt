Japanese, chapter 523: Anyone, Any One.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
だれでも (daredemo) — anyone; everyone.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for at any time, then the Japanese for anything.

You'll want to know: だれでも.
だれでも — daredemo — "anyone; everyone".
もし じかんが あったら、だれでも きて ください。 — moshi jikan ga attara, daredemo kite kudasai — "If you have time, anyone is welcome to come."

The word, taken apart.
だれでも (daredemo) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese: built from dare, "who", and demo, "even" (JMdict).

Warm-up.

You'll want to know: さぞ.
さぞ — sazo — "no doubt; I imagine".
もし かぞくで りょこうしたら、さぞ たのしいでしょう。 — moshi kazoku de ryokō shitara, sazo tanoshii deshō — "If the family went on a trip together, it would surely be fun."

The word, taken apart.
さぞ (sazo) is usually counted a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese: JMdict marks it as usually written in kana, with one rare kanji spelling, and no dictionary we use records an older form, so its history is not settled (JMdict).
