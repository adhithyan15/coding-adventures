## You'll want to know: उत्सारयति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C510-AVAKARA510-02]; assesses=[] -->

**उत्सारयति** — *utsārayati* — "clears away".

> माता अवकरं सम्मार्जन्या उत्सारयति।
>
> *mātā avakaraṃ sammārjanyā utsārayati.*
>
> Mother clears away the rubbish with a broom.

Past (she …): सा अवकरम् उत्सारितवती, *sā avakaram utsāritavatī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**उत्सारयति** (*utsārayati*) is built on *ud*, "out", and the causative of the root √sṛ, "to run, to flow"; MW gives *utsārayati* as to drive away, to put or throw away (MW).
