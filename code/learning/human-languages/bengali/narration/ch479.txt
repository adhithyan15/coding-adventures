Bengali, chapter 479: Sleep.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ঘুম (ghum) — sleep.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a cold compress, then the Bengali for to nurse.

You'll want to know: ঘুম.
ঘুম — ghum — "sleep".
কাল রাতে আমার ভালো ঘুম হল। kāl rāte āmār bhālo ghum hôlô. I slept well last night.
ঘুম (ghum) পাওয়া, to feel sleepy: আমার ঘুম (ghum) পাচ্ছে.

The word, taken apart.
ঘুম (ghum) is listed by CDIAL for Bengali, as dozing, under a reconstructed verb of turning round (CDIAL 4485); it shows the new letter ঘ.

দুপুরে খাওয়ার পরে আমার তন্দ্রা আসে। dupure khāoyār pôre āmār tôndrā āse. After lunch I get drowsy.

The word, taken apart.
তন্দ্রা (tôndrā) is a tatsama, Sanskrit tandrā, "lassitude, exhaustion" (Monier-Williams); Bengali uses it for the drowsiness before sleep.

Lesson 5 of 5.
আড়মোড়া ভাঙা (āṛmoṛā bhāngā) — to stretch on waking; to stretch one's limbs.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for drowsiness, then the Bengali for to nod off.

You'll want to know: আড়মোড়া ভাঙা.
আড়মোড়া ভাঙা — āṛmoṛā bhāngā — "to stretch on waking; to stretch one's limbs".
সকালে জেগে বিড়ালটা আড়মোড়া ভাঙল। sôkāle jege biṛālṭā āṛmoṛā bhānglô. Waking in the morning, the cat had a long stretch.
Past (he/she …): তিনি জেগে আড়মোড়া ভাঙলেন, tini jege āṛmoṛā bhānglen.

The word, taken apart.
আড়মোড়া ভাঙা (āṛmoṛā bhāngā) is āṛmoṛā, "a stretch of the limbs", whose origin is not settled, with bhāngā, "to break" (CDIAL 9353): to break out of the stiffness of sleep.
