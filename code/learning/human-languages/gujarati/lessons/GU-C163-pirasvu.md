## You'll want to know: પીરસવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C163-MEAL163-04]; assesses=[] -->

**પીરસવું** — *pīrasvũ* — "to serve (food)".

> **માતા થાળીમાં ભાત પીરસે છે.** — *mātā thāḷīmā̃ bhāt pīrase che* — "Mother serves rice on the plate."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**પીરસવું** (*pīrasvũ*) is a tadbhava. It comes from Sanskrit *pariveṣati*, "attends, serves food", and CDIAL glosses the Gujarati verb "to serve a meal" (CDIAL 7888).
