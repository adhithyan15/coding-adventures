# అవమానం (avamānaṁ) — humiliation; an insult

## You'll want to know: అవమానం
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C498-SIGGU498-02]; assesses=[] -->

**అవమానం** — *avamānaṁ* — "humiliation; an insult".

> అందరి ముందు తిట్టడం అతనికి అవమానంగా అనిపించింది.
>
> *andari mundu tiṭṭaḍaṁ ataniki avamānaṅgā anipiñcindi.*
>
> Being scolded in front of everyone felt humiliating to him.

Its opposite is గౌరవం, respect.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**అవమానం** (*avamānaṁ*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *avamāna*, "disrespect, dishonour" (Monier-Williams), from *ava-*, "down", and *man*, "to think": to think someone low.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
