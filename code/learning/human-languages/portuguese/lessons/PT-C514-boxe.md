## You'll want to know: o boxe
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C514-RINGUE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**o boxe** — "boxing".

> Ele treina boxe no ginásio.
>
> *He trains in boxing at the gym.*

A boxer is *um pugilista*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*boxe* is usually traced, through French *boxe*, to English *box* "to hit with the fist".
