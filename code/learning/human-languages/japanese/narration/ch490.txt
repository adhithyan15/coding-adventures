Japanese, chapter 490: Fishing Boats.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
りょうし (ryōshi) — a fisherman.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for to ripen, then the Japanese for wheat.

You'll want to know: りょうし.
りょうし — ryōshi — "a fisherman".
りょうしは あさ はやく うみに でます。 — ryōshi wa asa hayaku umi ni demasu — "Fishermen go out to sea early in the morning."

The word, taken apart.
りょうし (ryōshi) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are ryō, "fishing" and shi, "expert" (Middle Chinese srij) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).

Lesson 4 of 5.
えさ (esa) — animal feed; bait.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for the fishing industry, then the Japanese for a fishing boat.

You'll want to know: えさ.
えさ — esa — "animal feed; bait".
ねこの えさを かいます。 — neko no esa o kaimasu — "I buy food for the cat."

The word, taken apart.
えさ (esa) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi, and its older origin is not settled (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).

Lesson 5 of 5.
はねる (haneru) — to jump; to leap.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for a fishing boat, then the Japanese for animal feed.

You'll want to know: はねる.
はねる — haneru — "to jump; to leap".
さかなが みずの なかで はねました。 — sakana ga mizu no naka de hanemashita — "A fish jumped in the water."
Polite はねます, hanemasu; an ichidan verb. Past (polite): さかなが はねました。, sakana ga hanemashita.

The word, taken apart.
はねる (haneru) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, and usually written in kana; its older origin is not settled (JMdict).
