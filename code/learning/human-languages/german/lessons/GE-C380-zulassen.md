## You'll want to know: zulassen
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C380-ZULASSUNG-02]; assesses=[] -->

**zulassen** — "to let in, to admit; to allow".

> Die Universität lässt jedes Jahr nur hundert Studenten zu.
>
> *The university admits only a hundred students each year.*

Separable and strong: *sie lässt zu*, *sie hat zugelassen*. *Das Auto ist zugelassen* — the car is registered.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*zulassen* is built from *zu* and *lassen* "to let, to let go", inherited from Old High German *lāzan* and Proto-Germanic *lētaną* (EtymDB).
