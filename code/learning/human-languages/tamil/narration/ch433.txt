Tamil, chapter 433: Peaks and Plains.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
சிகரம் (cikaram) — a peak; a summit.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for a water well, then the Tamil for to slosh.

You'll want to know: சிகரம்.
சிகரம் — cikaram — "a peak; a summit".
சிகரத்தில் எப்போதும் பனி இருக்கிறது. cikarattil eppōtum paṉi irukkiṟatu. There is always snow on the peak.
A -அம் noun: சிகரத்தில், on the peak.

The word, taken apart.
சிகரம் (cikaram) is borrowed from Sanskrit śikhara, "a point, a peak" (CDIAL 12435).

Lesson 2 of 5.
மலையேறு (malaiyēṟu) — to trek; to climb mountains.

[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for to slosh, then the Tamil for a peak.

You'll want to know: மலையேறு.
மலையேறு — malaiyēṟu — "to trek; to climb mountains".
விடுமுறையில் நான் நண்பர்களுடன் மலையேறினேன். viṭumuṟaiyil nāṉ naṇparkaḷuṭaṉ malaiyēṟiṉēṉ. In the holidays I went trekking with friends.
Past (I …): மலையேறினேன், malaiyēṟiṉēṉ.
மலை, a hill, + ஏறு, to climb, written as one word.

The word, taken apart.
மலையேறு (malaiyēṟu) is built from மலை, "a hill, a mountain" (DEDR 4742), and ஏறு, "to climb" (DEDR 916).

Lesson 5 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for a volcano, then the Tamil for a glacier.

You'll want to know: சமவெளி.
சமவெளி — camaveḷi — "a plain; flat land".
மலையிலிருந்து கீழே பரந்த சமவெளி தெரிந்தது. malaiyiliruntu kīḻē paranta camaveḷi terintatu. From the hill a wide plain could be seen below.
சம, level, + வெளி, an open space.

The word, taken apart.
சமவெளி (camaveḷi) is built from cama, "level", borrowed from Sanskrit sama, "equal, level" (CDIAL 13173), and வெளி, "an open space" (DEDR 5498).
