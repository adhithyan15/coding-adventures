## You'll want to know: ఇంకా
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C64-JOIN-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ఇంకా** (*iṅkā*) — "still, yet".

ఇంకా says the thing is not finished. ఇంకా ఉంది is "there is still some"; ఇంకా రాలేదు is "she hasn't come yet".

It is the natural answer to అంతే. Where అంతే closes a list off, ఇంకా says there is more of it coming, and the two make a pair the way కూడా and మాత్రమే do.

Five small words, and not one of them names a thing. కూడా, మాత్రమే, అయితే, తరువాత, ఇంకా are what turn a set of separate answers into a conversation.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

Five: కూడా, మాత్రమే, అయితే, తరువాత, ఇంకా. Enough to add to an answer, limit it, order it, or keep it open.
