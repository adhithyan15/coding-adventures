## You'll want to know: xīngfèn
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C458-MOOD458-03]; assesses=[] -->

*xīngfèn* — "thrilled; keyed up".

> *míngtiān qù Zhōngguó wǒ gǎnjué hěn xīngfèn* — I'm going to China tomorrow, and I feel really excited.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*xīngfèn* is built from *xīng* 'to rise' and *fèn* 'to spread the wings', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *xing* and *pjunH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to rise' and 'to flourish' and the second as a bird spreading its feathers. The sense 'excited' is not given there.
