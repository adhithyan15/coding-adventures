Persian, chapter 393: Sure and unsure.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
قطعا (qat'an) — definitely, certainly.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for unlikely, then the Persian for definite.

You'll want to know: قطعا.
قطعا (qat'an) — "definitely, certainly".
او قطعا فردا میآید. u qat'an fardâ miâyad. He is definitely coming tomorrow.
Close to hatman in meaning, and a little more formal.

The word, taken apart.
قطعا (qat'an) is usually traced to Arabic qaṭ'an "decisively", an adverb built on qaṭ' "cutting", like qat'i "definite".

Lesson 2 of 5.
تردید (tardid) — doubt, hesitation.

Warm-up.

Before the new one: say the Persian for definite, then the Persian for definitely.

You'll want to know: تردید.
تردید (tardid) — "doubt, hesitation".
بدون تردید این بهترین راه است. bedun-e tardid in behtarin râh ast. Without doubt this is the best way.
Tardid dâshtan — to be in doubt, to hesitate.

The word, taken apart.
تردید (tardid) is usually traced to Arabic tardīd, a verbal noun usually explained as going back and forth: a doubting mind wavers.

Lesson 3 of 5.
اطمینان (etminân) — certainty, confidence.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for definitely, then the Persian for doubt.

You'll want to know: اطمینان.
اطمینان (etminân) — "certainty, confidence".
با اطمینان میگویم که حق با توست. bâ etminân miguyam ke haq bâ tost. I can say for certain that you are right.
Etminân dâshtan — to be sure; etminân dâdan — to reassure.

The word, taken apart.
اطمینان (etminân) was borrowed from Arabic iṭmi'nān "calm, assurance" (EtymDB), from the same Arabic family as muṭma'inn "sure", the source of Persian motma'en.

Lesson 4 of 5.
مطمئن شدن (motma'en shodan) — to make sure, to become sure.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for doubt, then the Persian for certainty.

You'll want to know: مطمئن شدن.
مطمئن شدن (motma'en shodan) — "to make sure, to become sure".
قبل از رفتن مطمئن شدم که در قفل است. qabl az raftan motma'en shodam ke dar qofl ast. Before leaving I made sure that the door was locked.
Motma'en "sure" + shodan. Motma'en budan — to be sure. It is spelled with ئ.

The word, taken apart.
مطمئن comes from Arabic muṭma'inn "calm, sure" (EtymDB); with the inherited Persian verb shodan "to become", it means to become sure.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ظاهرا (zâheran) — apparently, seemingly.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for certainty, then the Persian for to make sure.

You'll want to know: ظاهرا.
ظاهرا (zâheran) — "apparently, seemingly".
ظاهرا قطار امروز دیر میآید. zâheran qatâr emruz dir miâyad. Apparently the train is late today.
From zâher "outward appearance". For what seems true from the outside.

The word, taken apart.
ظاهرا (zâheran) is usually traced to Arabic ẓāhiran "outwardly", an adverb built on ẓāhir "visible, apparent".
