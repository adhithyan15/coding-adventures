## You'll want to know: मुरगळणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C486-IJA486-04]; assesses=[] -->

**मुरगळणे** — *murgaḷṇe* — "to sprain; to twist".

> खेळताना माझा घोटा मुरगळला.
>
> *kheḷtānā mājhā ghoṭā murgaḷlā.*
>
> I sprained my ankle while playing.

Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने कान मुरगळला, *tyāne kān murgaḷlā*.

Of a sprain the joint is the subject: माझा पाय मुरगळला. With -ने it is to twist: कान मुरगळणे.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**मुरगळणे** (*murgaḷṇe*) is a Marathi verb whose origin is not settled.
