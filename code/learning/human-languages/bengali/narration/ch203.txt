Lesson 1 of 5.
স্বভাব (svôbhāb) — a nature, a temperament.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a lawsuit, then the Bengali for a vote.

You'll want to know: স্বভাব.
স্বভাব — svôbhāb — "a nature, a temperament".

The word, taken apart.
স্বভাব (svôbhāb) is Sanskrit svabhāva, "own nature", taken over whole as a learned loan, a tatsama.

The word, taken apart.
মেজাজ (mejāj) is a loan from Arabic mizāj, "mixture; temperament", which reached Bengali through Persian. Its Arabic root is m-z-j (Lane).

The word, taken apart.
হিংসা (hingsā) is Sanskrit hiṁsā, "injury, harm", taken over whole as a learned loan, a tatsama. In Bengali it has come to mean "envy".
