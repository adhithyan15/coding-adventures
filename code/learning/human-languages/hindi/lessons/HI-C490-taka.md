## You'll want to know: टाँका
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C490-CHHALA490-02]; assesses=[] -->

**टाँका** — *ṭā̃kā* — "a stitch".

> घाव पर चार टाँके लगे।
>
> *ghāv par cār ṭā̃ke lage.*
>
> The wound needed four stitches.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**टाँका** (*ṭā̃kā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists Hindi *ṭā̃kā*, "stitch, join", under Sanskrit *ṭáṅkati*, "binds" (CDIAL 5432).
