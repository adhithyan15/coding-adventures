Gujarati, chapter 71: White, Brown, Pink, and What Looks Good.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
સફેદ (saphed) — white.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for blue, then the Gujarati for black.

You'll want to know: સફેદ.
સફેદ (saphed) — "white".

What you've built.
One more word for white, brown, pink, and what looks good.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: saphed]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: saphed, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say saphed]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Gujarati for blue, then the Gujarati for black, then say saphed again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does સફેદ (saphed) mean? ("White".) Say it once more.
