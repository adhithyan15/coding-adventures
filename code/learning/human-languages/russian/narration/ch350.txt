Russian, chapter 350: Consequences.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
последствие (poslédstviye) — a consequence.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for influence, then the Russian for an impact.

You'll want to know: последствие.
последствие (poslédstviye) — "a consequence".
Это решение будет иметь серьёзные последствия. Éto reshéniye búdet imét seryóznyye poslédstviya. This decision will have serious consequences.
Neuter; mostly plural, последствия, and usually unpleasant.

The word, taken apart.
последствие (poslédstviye) is built from после "after" and след "a track, a trail, a mark" (EtymDB), with the suffix -ствие: what comes after, in the wake of an event.

Lesson 2 of 5.

You'll want to know: следствие.
следствие (slédstviye) — "a result, an outcome; an investigation".
Его болезнь — следствие стресса. Yegó bolézn — slédstviye stréssa. His illness is the result of stress.
Neuter, stressed on the first syllable. In law, следствие (slédstviye) is a criminal investigation.

The word, taken apart.
следствие (slédstviye) is built on след "a track, a trail, a mark" (EtymDB) with the suffix -ствие, a Church Slavonic pattern: a result follows its cause like a track.

Lesson 3 of 5.
отражаться (otrazhátsya) — to affect, to tell on; to be reflected.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a consequence, then the Russian for a result.

You'll want to know: отражаться.
отражаться (otrazhátsya) — "to affect, to tell on; to be reflected".
Усталость отражается на работе. Ustálost otrazháyetsya na rabóte. Tiredness tells on your work.
Imperfective; perfective отразиться. Отражаться на + prepositional — to affect. Literally to be reflected: в воде отражается луна.

The word, taken apart.
отражаться (otrazhátsya) is built from от- "back" and the root of разить "to strike" (EtymDB): light that strikes a surface and comes back is reflected, and so is a cause that comes back in its effects.

Lesson 4 of 5.
эффект (effékt) — an effect.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a result, then the Russian for to affect.

You'll want to know: эффект.
эффект (effékt) — "an effect".
Новое лекарство дало хороший эффект. Nóvoye lekárstvo dálo khoróshiy effékt. The new medicine had a good effect.
Masculine. Произвести эффект (effékt) — to make an impression.

The word, taken apart.
эффект (effékt) comes from Latin effectus "an effect" (EtymDB), from efficere "to bring about", built from ex "out" and facere "to do, to make" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short).

Lesson 5 of 5.
в результате (v rezultáte) — as a result.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to affect, then the Russian for an effect.

You'll want to know: в результате.
в результате (v rezultáte) — "as a result".
В результате мы опоздали на поезд. V rezultáte my opozdáli na póyezd. As a result, we missed the train.
With a noun it takes the genitive: в результате (v rezultáte) аварии. On its own it opens a sentence.

The word, taken apart.
в результате (v rezultáte) is в "in" with the prepositional of результат "a result", which is usually traced, through western languages, to Medieval Latin resultatum, built on resultāre "to spring back" (Lewis & Short).
