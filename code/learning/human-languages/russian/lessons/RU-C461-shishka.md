## You'll want to know: шишка
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C461-POLYANA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**шишка** (*shíshka*) — "a cone (of a pine or a fir); a bump".

> Под елью лежат шишки.
>
> *Pod yélyu lezhát shíshki.*
>
> There are cones lying under the fir tree.

Feminine. Also a bump on the head; colloquial *большая шишка* — a big shot.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*шишка* is inherited through Old East Slavic *šišĭka* from Proto-Slavic *šišĭka* (EtymDB).
