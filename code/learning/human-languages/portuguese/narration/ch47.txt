Portuguese, chapter 47: Cake, Honey, Lunch, Dinner, Lemon.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
o bolo — a cake.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for a carrot, then the Portuguese for beans.

You'll want to know: o bolo.
o bolo — "a cake".

The word, taken apart.
bolo is a masculine of the word for a ball (EtymDB 2.1). That word came through Old Provençal bola from Latin bulla, "bubble, knob" (EtymDB 2.1).

Lesson 4 of 5.
o jantar — dinner.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for honey, then the Portuguese for lunch.

You'll want to know: o jantar.
o jantar — "dinner". Also the verb: jantar, to have dinner.

The word, taken apart.
jantar from Latin ientare, "to have breakfast" (EtymDB 2.1) — inherited. The verb became a noun, and the meal moved to the evening.
