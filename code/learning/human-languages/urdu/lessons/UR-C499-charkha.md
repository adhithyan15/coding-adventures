## You'll want to know: چرخہ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C499-CHARKHA499-03]; assesses=[] -->

**چرخہ** — *charkhā* — "a spinning wheel".

> نانی شام کو چرخہ چلاتی تھیں۔
>
> *nānī shām ko charkhā chalātī thīṅ.*
>
> Grandmother used to work the spinning wheel in the evening.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**چرخہ** (*charkhā*) is usually traced to Persian *charkha*, "a wheel", built on *charkh*, "a wheel; the turning sky".
