## You'll want to know: duōkuī
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C541-FINAL541-02]; assesses=[] -->

*duōkuī* — "it is thanks to; luckily for".

> *duōkuī nǐ bāng wǒ bùrán wǒ jiù zuò bu wán* — Thanks to your help I finished; otherwise I'd never have managed.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*duōkuī* is built from 多 'much' and *kuī*, both inherited from Middle Chinese, *ta* and *khjwe* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'many' and the second 'to be lacking'. How the pair came to mean 'thanks to' is not settled.
