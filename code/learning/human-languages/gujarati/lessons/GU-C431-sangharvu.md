# સંઘરવું (sangharvũ) — to store up; to hoard

## You'll want to know: સંઘરવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C431-STOCK431-05]; assesses=[] -->

**સંઘરવું** — *sangharvũ* — "to store up; to hoard".

> વેપારીએ ગોદામમાં ખાંડ સંઘરી.
>
> *vepārīe godāmmā̃ khā̃ḍ sanghrī.*
>
> The trader stored up sugar in his warehouse.

Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે ઘઉં સંઘર્યા, *teṇe ghaũ sangharyā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**સંઘરવું** (*sangharvũ*) is built on Sanskrit *saṁgraha*, "holding together, gathering in" (Monier-Williams): to keep gathering things and not let them go.
