## You'll want to know: nummulārius, nummulāriī
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C465-MODIUS-05]; assesses=[] -->

**nummulārius, nummulāriī** — "a money-changer, a banker at a table".

> Nummulārius nummōs peregrīnōs in forō mūtat.
>
> *The money-changer changes foreign coins in the forum.*

Second declension, masculine: *nummulārius, nummulāriī m.* He tested and changed coins at a small table.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*nummulārius* is built on *nummulus* 'a little coin, small change', a diminutive of *nummus* 'coin' (EtymDB; Lewis & Short).
