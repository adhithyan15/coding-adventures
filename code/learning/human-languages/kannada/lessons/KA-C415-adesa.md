## You'll want to know: ಆದೇಶ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C415-AMANATU415-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ಆದೇಶ** — *ādēśa* — "an official order".

> ಸರ್ಕಾರ ಶಾಲೆಗಳಿಗೆ ಹೊಸ ಆದೇಶ ಕಳುಹಿಸಿತು.
>
> *sarkāra śālegaḷige hosa ādēśa kaḷuhisitu.*
>
> The government sent a new order to the schools.

ಆದೇಶ ಹೊರಡಿಸು, to issue an order.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಆದೇಶ** (*ādēśa*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *ādeśa*, "advice, instruction, command" (Monier-Williams).
