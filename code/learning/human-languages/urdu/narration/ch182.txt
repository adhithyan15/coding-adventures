Lesson 1 of 5.
تھکنا (thaknā) — to get tired.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for to weigh, then the Urdu for to check.

You'll want to know: تھکنا.
تھکنا — thaknā — "to get tired".

The word, taken apart.
تھکنا (thaknā) is a tadbhava, a word inherited and reshaped by sound change. It comes from sthakk, "stop, halt", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 13737). CDIAL calls a link with sthā-, "to stand", probable but not clear.

Lesson 3 of 5.
لیٹنا (leṭnā) — to lie down.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for to get tired, then the Urdu for to rest.

You'll want to know: لیٹنا.
لیٹنا — leṭnā — "to lie down".

The word, taken apart.
لیٹنا (leṭnā) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from leṭyati, "sleeps" (CDIAL 11109). The Sanskrit verb is known only from a grammarians' list, and CDIAL thinks it probably of non-Aryan origin.

Lesson 4 of 5.
کھڑا ہونا (khaṛā honā) — to stand up.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for to rest, then the Urdu for to lie down.

You'll want to know: کھڑا ہونا.
کھڑا ہونا — khaṛā honā — "to stand up".

The phrase, taken apart.
کھڑا ہونا (khaṛā honā) is "to become standing". CDIAL lists khaṛā, "standing erect", under Sanskrit khaḍaka, "a bolt, a post", for which it supposes an older sense "erect" (CDIAL 3784). honā, "to be", comes from Sanskrit bhavati, "becomes, is" (CDIAL 9416).

Lesson 5 of 5.
انتظار کرنا (intizār karnā) — to wait.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for to lie down, then the Urdu for to stand up.

You'll want to know: انتظار کرنا.
انتظار کرنا — intizār karnā — "to wait".

The phrase, taken apart.
انتظار کرنا (intizār karnā) joins intizār, "waiting", and karnā, "to do". intizār is Arabic intiẓār, "waiting, looking out for", which reached Urdu through Persian (Lane, root n-ẓ-r); karnā comes from Sanskrit karoti, "does" (CDIAL 2814).
