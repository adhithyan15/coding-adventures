## You'll want to know: विघ्नः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C372-VIGHNA372-01]; assesses=[] -->

**विघ्नः** — *vighnaḥ* — "an obstacle".

> यदि विघ्नः आगच्छति, तर्हि धैर्यं न त्यजतु।
>
> *yadi vighnaḥ āgacchati, tarhi dhairyaṃ na tyajatu.*
>
> If an obstacle comes, do not lose heart.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**विघ्नः** (*vighnaḥ*) is built on *vi* and the root √han, "to strike"; MW gives *vi-ghna* "an obstacle, an impediment, a hindrance" (MW).
