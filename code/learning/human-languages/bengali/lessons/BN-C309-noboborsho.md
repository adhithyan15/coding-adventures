## You'll want to know: নববর্ষ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C309-GHURI309-03]; assesses=[] -->

**নববর্ষ** — *nôbôbôrshô* — "the new year".

> নববর্ষের দিন সবাই নতুন জামা পরে বেড়াতে যায়।
>
> *nôbôbôrsher din sôbāi nôtun jāmā pôre beṛāte jāy.*
>
> On the first day of the new year everyone puts on new clothes and goes out visiting.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**নববর্ষ** (*nôbôbôrshô*) joins Sanskrit *nava*, 'new', to *varṣa*, 'rain; a year' (Monier-Williams), the year counted in rainy seasons.
