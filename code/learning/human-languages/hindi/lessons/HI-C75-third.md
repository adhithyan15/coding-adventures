## You'll want to know: तीसरा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C75-ORDINAL-04]; assesses=[] -->

**तीसरा** (*tīsrā*) — "**third**".

**तीसरा दिन** (*tīsrā din*), **तीसरी किताब** (*tīsrī kitāb*).

Say **तीन** and then **तीसरा** and you can hear the number this time — the first
ordinal where you can. And say *dūsrā, tīsrā* together: they rhyme, because they
share an ending, **-सरा**, that they share with nothing else in the language.

Two words with a private ending is not a pattern; it is a pair. The next lesson
goes a third way, and the one after that finally states a rule.
