## You'll want to know: അലയുക
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C479-ALACCIL479-01]; assesses=[] -->

**അലയുക** — *alayuka* — "to wander; to roam".

> വഴി അറിയാതെ ഞങ്ങൾ ഒരു മണിക്കൂർ അലഞ്ഞു.
>
> *vaḻi aṟiyāte ñaṅṅaḷ oru maṇikkūr alaññu.*
>
> Not knowing the way, we wandered for an hour.

Past: അലഞ്ഞു, *alaññu*.

It means wandering without a plan, often tired or lost.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**അലയുക** (*alayuka*) is native Dravidian; DEDR gives the Malayalam verb as to be tossed about and to roam (DEDR 240).
