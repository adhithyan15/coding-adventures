Marathi, chapter 210: Dentist, Butcher, Craftsman, Worker, Employee.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
दंतवैद्य (dantavaidya) — a dentist.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for an army, then the Marathi for a soldier.

You'll want to know: दंतवैद्य.
दंतवैद्य — dantavaidya — "a dentist".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: dantavaidya]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: dantavaidya, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say dantavaidya]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Marathi for an army, then the Marathi for a soldier, then say dantavaidya again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does दंतवैद्य (dantavaidya) mean? ("A dentist".) Say it once more.

Lesson 6 of 7.
Words from chapters 202-205 — a first pass.

Warm-up.
[pause 3 seconds]
Say the words for a worker and an employee.

Guided Practice.
[pause 2 seconds after each]
[your turn — say: a border, a region, a capital city, a suburb, up]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: certainly, really, almost, about, only]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: serious, shy, valiant, fearful, curious]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: free of charge, modern, ancient, cooked, ripe]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
A border? (सीमा, sīmā.) A region? (प्रदेश, pradeś.) A capital city? (राजधानी, rājdhānī.) A suburb? (उपनगर, upnagar.) Up? (वरती, varatī.) Certainly? (नक्की, nakkī.) Really? (खरोखर, kharokhar.) Almost? (जवळजवळ, javaḷjavaḷ.) About? (सुमारे, sumāre.) Only? (फक्त, phakta.) Serious? (गंभीर, gambhīr.) Shy? (लाजाळू, lājāḷū.) Valiant? (शूर, śūr.) Fearful? (भित्रा / भित्री, bhitrā / bhitrī.) Curious? (उत्सुक, utsuk.) Free of charge? (मोफत, mophat.) Modern? (आधुनिक, ādhunik.) Ancient? (प्राचीन, prācīn.) Cooked? (शिजलेला, śijlelā.) Ripe? (पिकलेला, piklelā.)
