Bengali, chapter 518: Games Indoors.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
লুডো (luḍo) — ludo.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for hide-and-seek, then the Bengali for a marble.

You'll want to know: লুডো.
লুডো — luḍo — "ludo".
বৃষ্টির দিনে আমরা লুডো খেলি। Brishṭir dine āmrā luḍo kheli. On rainy days we play ludo.

The word, taken apart.
লুডো (luḍo) is borrowed from English ludo, the name of the game.

Lesson 4 of 5.
শব্দজব্দ (shôbdôjôbdô) — a crossword.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for dice, then the Bengali for snakes and ladders.

You'll want to know: শব্দজব্দ.
শব্দজব্দ — shôbdôjôbdô — "a crossword".
বাবা রোজ সকালে পত্রিকার শব্দজব্দ করেন। Bābā roj sôkāle pôtrikār shôbdôjôbdô kôren. My father does the magazine's crossword every morning.

The word, taken apart.
শব্দজব্দ (shôbdôjôbdô) is a playful modern compound: শব্দ, "a word", and জব্দ, "caught out, beaten", which is usually traced to Arabic ḍabṭ, "seizing": the words that catch you out.
