## You'll want to know: शिखर
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C442-SHIKHAR442-01]; assesses=[] -->

**शिखर** — *śikhar* — "a peak, a summit".

> हम सुबह तक पहाड़ के शिखर पर पहुँचे।
>
> *ham subah tak pahāṛ ke śikhar par pahũce.*
>
> We reached the top of the mountain by morning.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**शिखर** (*śikhar*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *śikhara*, "pointed, crested" and so a peak (Monier-Williams); the spire of a temple is its शिखर too.
