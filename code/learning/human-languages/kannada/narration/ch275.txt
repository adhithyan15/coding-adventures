Kannada, chapter 275: To charge, To check, To translate, To apply, To compare.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಚಾರ್ಜ್ ಮಾಡು (cārj māḍu) — to charge (a phone).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to withdraw, then the Kannada for to sign.

You'll want to know: ಚಾರ್ಜ್ ಮಾಡು.
ಚಾರ್ಜ್ ಮಾಡು — cārj māḍu — "to charge (a phone)".

The word, taken apart.
ಚಾರ್ಜ್ ಮಾಡು (cārj māḍu) is English charge with native māḍu, "to do" (DEDR 4797).

Lesson 4 of 5.
ಅರ್ಜಿ ಹಾಕು (arji hāku) — to apply (for a job, a loan).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to check, then the Kannada for to translate.

You'll want to know: ಅರ್ಜಿ ಹಾಕು.
ಅರ್ಜಿ ಹಾಕು — arji hāku — "to apply (for a job, a loan)".

The word, taken apart.
ಅರ್ಜಿ ಹಾಕು (arji hāku) is Hindustani arzī, "a petition", from Arabic ʿarḍ, "presenting", through Persian, with hāku, "to put", whose history neither DEDR nor CDIAL gives.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ಹೋಲಿಸು (hōlisu) — to compare.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to translate, then the Kannada for to apply.

You'll want to know: ಹೋಲಿಸು.
ಹೋಲಿಸು — hōlisu — "to compare".

The word, taken apart.
ಹೋಲಿಸು (hōlisu) is native Dravidian, from hōl, "to resemble", and DEDR 4597 lists pōlisu, "to cause to resemble, compare", with the older p-; the entry also has Tamil pōl, Telugu pōlu and Malayalam pōluka.
