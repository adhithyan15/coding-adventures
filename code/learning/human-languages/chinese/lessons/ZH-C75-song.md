## You'll want to know: sòng
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C75-PARTY75-05]; assesses=[] -->

*sòng* — "to give as a present; to see off".

> *wǒmen sòng shénme lǐwù* — What present shall we give?

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*sòng* is inherited from Middle Chinese *suwngH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'to send off'; giving a present is sending something off with somebody.

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for meeting people.
