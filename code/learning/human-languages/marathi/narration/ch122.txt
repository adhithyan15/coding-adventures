Marathi, chapter 122: Means, Owner, Strength, Wanted, Wish.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
साधन (sādhan) — a means, a tool.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for ability, then the Marathi for a right.

You'll want to know: साधन.
साधन — sādhan — "a means, a tool".

Guided Practice.

[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does साधन (sādhan) mean? ("A means, a tool".) Say it once more.
