## You'll want to know: युक्तिः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C342-ADHARA342-02]; assesses=[] -->

**युक्तिः** — *yuktiḥ* — "a line of reasoning; an argument".

> अरुणस्य युक्तिः दृढा अस्ति।
>
> *aruṇasya yuktiḥ dṛḍhā asti.*
>
> Arun's argument is strong.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**युक्तिः** (*yuktiḥ*) comes from the root √yuj, "to join"; MW gives *yukti* the sense "union" and then "reasoning, argument, proof" (MW).
