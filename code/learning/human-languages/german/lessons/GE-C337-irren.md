## You'll want to know: irren
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C337-IRRTUM-01]; assesses=[] -->

**irren** — "to be mistaken (mostly reflexive, sich irren); to wander".

> Ich habe mich geirrt: Das Konzert ist am Sonntag, nicht am Samstag.
>
> *I was mistaken: the concert is on Sunday, not Saturday.*

Mostly reflexive: *sich irren*, *ich habe mich geirrt*. *Irren ist menschlich* — to err is human.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*irren* is inherited from Old High German *irren* and Proto-Germanic *irzijaną* "to delude, to mislead" (EtymDB), a cousin of Latin *errāre* "to wander": to be mistaken is to wander off the path.
