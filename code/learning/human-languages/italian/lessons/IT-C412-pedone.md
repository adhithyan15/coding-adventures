## You'll want to know: il pedone
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C412-VIGILE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**il pedone** — "the pedestrian".

> Attenzione ai pedoni!
>
> *Watch out for pedestrians!*

*La zona pedonale* is a pedestrian zone. In chess, *il pedone* is a pawn.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*pedone* is inherited from Late Latin *pedō* 'foot soldier', built on *pēs, pedis* 'foot' (EtymDB).
