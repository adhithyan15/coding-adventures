## You'll want to know: el monarca
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C568-REY-03]; assesses=[] -->

**el monarca** — "the monarch".

> El monarca visitó la ciudad en 1990.
>
> *The monarch visited the city in 1990.*

Masculine although it ends in *-a*. *La monarquía* is the monarchy.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*monarca* is a learned borrowing from Late Latin *monarcha*, which comes from Greek *monárchēs* 'one who rules alone' (EtymDB), built on *mónos* 'alone' and *árchein* 'to rule'.
