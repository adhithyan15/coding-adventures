## You'll want to know: 今天
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C45-TODAY45-02]; assesses=[] -->

**今天** — *jīntiān* — "today".

> **你们今天去吗** *nǐmen jīntiān qù ma* — Are you going today?

The new character, in pieces: 今 — 人 as a roof, a dot under it, and a stroke that runs across and turns down. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**今天** (*jīntiān*) is built from 今 'now' and 天 'day', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *kim* and *then* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription); the dictionary glosses *kim* as the opposite of olden times.

One more word for times and partings.
