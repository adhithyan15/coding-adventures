# привет — one supported copy

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[RU-LEX-PRIVET, RU-SCRIPT-P-01, RU-SCRIPT-R-01, RU-SCRIPT-I-01, RU-SCRIPT-V-01, RU-SCRIPT-E-01, RU-SCRIPT-T-01] -->

Keep **привет** visible. Point to its two traps: **р** says *r* and **в** says
*v*. You may look back after every letter.

## Writing — guided copy
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[RU-LEX-PRIVET, RU-SCRIPT-P-01, RU-SCRIPT-R-01, RU-SCRIPT-I-01, RU-SCRIPT-V-01, RU-SCRIPT-E-01, RU-SCRIPT-T-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: guided-copy -->

Copy **привет** once. Use three small chunks: **при · ве · т**. After each
chunk, look back and compare only those letters. Then read the finished word.
