## You'll want to know: कारीगर
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C262-JANO262-05]; assesses=[] -->

**कारीगर** — *kārīgar* — "a craftsman; a skilled worker".

> गांव मांय दो कारीगर है।
>
> *gā̃v mā̃y do kārīgar hai.*
>
> There are two craftsmen in the village.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**कारीगर** (*kārīgar*) is usually traced to Persian *kārīgar*, "craftsman".
