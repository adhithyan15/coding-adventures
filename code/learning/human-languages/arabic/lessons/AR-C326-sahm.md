## You'll want to know: سهم
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C326-SILAH326-04]; assesses=[] -->

**سهم** — *sahm* — "an arrow; a share".

> رمى الصياد سهما نحو الشجرة.
>
> *ramā aṣ-ṣayyād sahman naḥwa ash-shajara.*
>
> The hunter shot an arrow at the tree.

Plural: سهام, *sihām*.

In business a share is سهم, plural أسهم. A road sign arrow is سهم too.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**سهم** (*sahm*) is built on the native Arabic root *s-h-m* (Lane), where *sahm* is an arrow, and an arrow drawn as a lot: so also a share, *sāhama*, to take part.
