## You'll want to know: そら
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C208-FORECAST208-04]; assesses=[] -->

**そら** — *sora* — "the sky".

> **あしたは そらが あおいでしょう。** — *ashita wa sora ga aoi deshō* — "The sky will probably be blue tomorrow."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**そら** (*sora*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi, and its older origin is not settled (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
