German, chapter 477: Tapfer oder feige.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
tapfer — brave.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for irritable, then the German for sensitive.

You'll want to know: tapfer.
tapfer — "brave".
Das Kind war beim Arzt sehr tapfer. The child was very brave at the doctor's.
The noun: die Tapferkeit — bravery.

The word, taken apart.
tapfer is inherited from Middle High German tapfer and Old High German tapfar, from Proto-Germanic dapraz "stout, solid, heavy" (EtymDB); the sense moved from firm to brave.

Lesson 5 of 5.
trauen — to dare (sich trauen); to trust.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for coward, then the German for reckless.

You'll want to know: trauen.
trauen — "to dare (sich trauen); to trust".
Er traut sich nicht, vom Sprungturm zu springen. He doesn't dare to jump off the diving tower.
Reflexive for to dare: sich trauen. With a dative it means to trust: Ich traue ihm nicht.

The word, taken apart.
trauen is inherited from Old High German trūwēn "to trust, to believe" (EtymDB), a Germanic verb related to treu "faithful".
