## You'll want to know: el yerno
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C747-POLITICA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**el yerno** — "the son-in-law".

> Su yerno es médico.
>
> *Her son-in-law is a doctor.*

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*yerno* is inherited from Latin *gener*, *generum* "son-in-law" (EtymDB); Latin *ge-* became *ye-*.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
