## You'll want to know: નિદાન
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C484-ILAJ484-03]; assesses=[] -->

**નિદાન** — *nidān* — "a diagnosis".

> દાક્તરે રોગનું સાચું નિદાન કર્યું.
>
> *dāktare rognũ sācũ nidān karyũ.*
>
> The doctor made the right diagnosis.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**નિદાન** (*nidān*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *nidāna*, which Monier-Williams gives as "a band" and as a first cause; the old medicine used it for finding the cause of an illness.
