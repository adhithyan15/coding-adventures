## You'll want to know: اوکھلی
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C510-CHHALNI510-03]; assesses=[] -->

**اوکھلی** — *okhlī* — "a mortar (for pounding)".

> دادی اوکھلی میں مسالہ کوٹتی تھیں۔
>
> *dādī okhlī meṅ masālā kūṭtī thīṅ.*
>
> Grandmother used to pound spices in a mortar.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**اوکھلی** (*okhlī*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *okhlī*, "a small wooden mortar", under Sanskrit *ulūkhala*, "a mortar" (CDIAL 2360).
