Bengali, chapter 126: Barber, Tailor, Washerman, Grocer, Nurse.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
নাপিত (nāpit) — a barber.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a potter, then the Bengali for a weaver.

You'll want to know: নাপিত.
নাপিত — nāpit — "a barber".

The word, taken apart.
নাপিত (nāpit) is Sanskrit nāpita, "barber", taken over whole as a learned loan, a tatsama (CDIAL 13792). CDIAL lists the inherited Bengali nāi, "barber", under it, and nāu, "barber", under the related snāpaka, "bath attendant", in the same entry.

Lesson 3 of 5.
ধোপা (dhopā) — a washerman.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a barber, then the Bengali for a tailor.

You'll want to know: ধোপা.
ধোপা — dhopā — "a washerman".

The word, taken apart.
ধোপা (dhopā) is a tadbhava, a word inherited and reshaped by sound change. It comes from dhauvati, "washes", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 6886). CDIAL lists dhopā, "washerman", among the later forms built on it.
