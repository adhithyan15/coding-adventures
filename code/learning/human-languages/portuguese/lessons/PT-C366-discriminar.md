## You'll want to know: discriminar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C366-SOCIEDADE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**discriminar** — "to discriminate (against)".

> Ninguém deve ser discriminado pela cor da pele.
>
> *Nobody should be discriminated against for the colour of their skin.*

*Discriminar alguém* — to discriminate against someone.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*discriminar* is a learned borrowing from Latin *discrīmināre* "to divide, to separate", built on *discrīmen* "that which divides two things" (Lewis & Short).
