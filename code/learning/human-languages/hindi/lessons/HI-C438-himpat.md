## You'll want to know: हिमपात
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C438-JADA438-04]; assesses=[] -->

**हिमपात** — *himpāt* — "snowfall".

> पहाड़ों में कल रात भारी हिमपात हुआ।
>
> *pahāṛoṁ meṁ kal rāt bhārī himpāt huā.*
>
> There was heavy snowfall in the hills last night.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**हिमपात** (*himpāt*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *himapāta*, "a fall of snow" (Monier-Williams), from *hima*, "snow", as in हिमालय, and *pāta*, "a fall".
