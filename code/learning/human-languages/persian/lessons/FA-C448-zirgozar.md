## You'll want to know: زیرگذر
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C448-MIYANBOR-04]; assesses=[] -->

**زیرگذر** (*zirgozar*) — "an underpass; a pedestrian subway".

> برای رد شدن از اتوبان از زیرگذر رفتیم.
>
> *Barâ-ye rad shodan az otobân az zirgozar raftim.*
>
> To cross the motorway we went through the underpass.

*zir* 'under' + *gozar* 'passage'.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*zirgozar* is a native compound of *zir* 'under', which EtymDB lists, and *gozar* 'passage', the present stem of *gozashtan* 'to pass'.
