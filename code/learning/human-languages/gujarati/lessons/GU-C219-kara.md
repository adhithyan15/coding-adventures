## You'll want to know: કરા
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C219-THUNDER219-04]; assesses=[] -->

**કરા** — *karā* — "hailstones, hail".

> **કાલે કરા પડશે.** — *kāle karā paḍśe* — "Hail will fall tomorrow."

Masculine, and usually plural.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**કરા** (*karā*) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from *karaka*, "hail" (CDIAL 2782).
