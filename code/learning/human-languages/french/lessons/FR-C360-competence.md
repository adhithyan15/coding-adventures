## You'll want to know: la compétence
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C360-CARRIERE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**la compétence** — "the skill, the competence".

> Il a de bonnes compétences en langues.
>
> *He has good language skills.*

Mostly in the plural for skills: *des compétences*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*compétence* comes from Late Latin *competentia* (EtymDB), from *competere* "to come together, to be suitable", built on *petere* "to seek" (Lewis & Short).
