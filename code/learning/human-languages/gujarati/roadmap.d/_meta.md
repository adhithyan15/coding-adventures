# Gujarati Roadmap

This roadmap distinguishes what the book actually teaches from the path to a
book-only pre-A1-through-C2 course. The framework is [HL00](../../../specs/HL00-human-language-curriculum-framework.md),
the exact authored order is the [session map](session-map.md), and the exam
destination is defined by [assessment-spec.md](assessment-spec.md) and
[assessment.json](assessment.json).

Every lesson remains five minutes or less. Writing, listening, speaking,
reading, grammar, vocabulary, retrieval, and assessment grow together in small
steps; later-level labels are not readiness evidence by themselves.
