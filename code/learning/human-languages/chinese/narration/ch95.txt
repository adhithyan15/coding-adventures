You'll want to know: 为什么.
为什么 — wèishénme — "why?".
你为什么不去 nǐ wèishénme bú qù — Why aren't you going?
The next lesson puts the new character in your hand.

The word, taken apart.
为什么 (wèishénme) is built from 为 (wèi) 'for' and 什么 'what': for what? 为 (wèi) is inherited from Middle Chinese hjweH (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'to help'; 'for' is the sense of acting on somebody's behalf, which the gloss does not spell out. Where 什么 comes from is not settled.

What you've built.
为什么 (wèishénme) sits after the person and before the verb: 你, 为什么 (wèishénme), 不去. 不 before the fall of 去 rises: bú qù.

Script — a dot, a long fall, the hook of 男, a dot.
为 (wèi).
Four strokes, and the long one in the middle is a stroke your hand knows from the foot of 男.
A dot at the upper left.
A long left-falling stroke from the top, down to the lower left.
The hooked turn: across, down the right side, hooking to the left, as at the foot of 男.
A dot inside, last.
Four strokes.

Lesson 3 of 6.
yǒushì — to have something on.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: read the last lesson's character, then two words for a full day.
[once you have stopped driving — read: 为什么, aloud — wèishénme]
[your turn — recall: say máng, busy — R4, recognition at distance]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say 今天 — jīntiān, today]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

You'll want to know: yǒushì.
yǒushì — "to have something on; to be busy".
wǒ jīntiān yǒushì — I've got something on today.
Its second character comes later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
yǒushì is built from yǒu 'to have', the 有 you write, and shì 'a matter'. Both are inherited from Middle Chinese, hjuwX and dzriH (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription). The Guangyun glosses dzriH 'to employ; to set up', and 'a matter' is the everyday sense, not the gloss.

What you've built.
yǒushì is the polite reason that gives nothing away: something has come up. máng says you are busy; yǒushì says there is a particular thing.

Lesson 4 of 6.
dǔchē — a traffic jam.

You'll want to know: dǔchē.
dǔchē — "traffic jam; to be stuck in traffic".
zǎoshang dǔchē — There's a jam in the mornings.
Its first character comes later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
dǔchē is built from dǔ 'to block' and chē 'a vehicle', the 车 you write. Both are inherited from Middle Chinese, tuX and tsyhae (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription); the Guangyun glosses tuX as 'a wall', and 'to block' is assumed from that, not quoted.

What you've built.
dǔchē is a verb as well as a noun: zǎoshang dǔchē, the traffic jams in the morning. No 有 needed.

You'll want to know: wàng.
wàng — "to forget".
wǒ wàng le — I forgot.
wǒ wàng le wǒ de yàoshi — I forgot my key.
Its character comes later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
wàng is inherited from Middle Chinese mjangH (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'to leave behind; to forget'.

What you've built.
wàng le: with 了 after it, the forgetting has already happened, which is how it is almost always said.

Lesson 6 of 6.
你为什么不去 (nǐ wèishénme bú qù) — late, not going, no homework: why, three times.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
The chapter closes here, with one person asking why three times.
[your turn — recall: say wàng, to forget — R1, one lesson back]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say láibují, to run out of time — R2]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say zuòyè, homework — R1]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

The exchange.
A: nǐ wèishénme chídào — Why are you late? B: zǎoshang dǔchē — The traffic this morning. A: 你明天去吗 nǐ míngtiān qù ma — Are you going tomorrow? B: 我不去 wǒ bú qù — I'm not going. A: 为什么 wèishénme — Why? B: wǒ yǒushì — I've got something on. A: nǐ de zuòyè ne — And your homework? B: wǒ wàng le — I forgot.
为什么 (wèishénme) can stand alone as a whole question, as A's fifth line does. Each answer here is a reason with nothing in front of it; the next chapter gives the word that introduces one.

Reading — the exchange at once.
[once you have stopped driving — read: the exchange through once without stopping, then answer the question below]
[question — say your answer, then pause 12 seconds]
Why isn't B going tomorrow?

What you've built — asking why.
你为什么不去 nǐ wèishénme bú qù — why aren't you going?
为什么 wèishénme — why?
wǒ yǒushì — something has come up.
zǎoshang dǔchē — the traffic this morning.
Listen. Nǐ wèishénme bù chī fàn?
Speak. Answer it with any reason you have, then ask somebody why.
