# هاجر (hājara) — to emigrate

## You'll want to know: هاجر
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C332-HIJRA332-01]; assesses=[] -->

**هاجر** — *hājara* — "to emigrate".

> هاجر عمي إلى بلد بعيد قبل عشرين سنة.
>
> *hājara ʿammī ilā balad baʿīd qabla ʿishrīn sana.*
>
> My uncle emigrated to a distant country twenty years ago.

Present: يهاجر, *yuhājir*.

An emigrant is مهاجر.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**هاجر** (*hājara*) is built on the native Arabic root *h-j-r* (Lane), "to leave, to cut oneself off from". It is Form III, "to leave one's land for another".
