## You'll want to know: نوازنده
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C520-MUSICIANS-01]; assesses=[] -->

**نوازنده** (*navâzande*) — "a musician (who plays)".

> نوازنده جوان در کنسرت ویولن زد.
>
> *navâzande-ye javân dar konsert viyolon zad.*
>
> The young musician played the violin at the concert.

*navâz-* (stem of *navâkhtan*) + *-ande* 'one who'. A singer is *khânande*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*navâzande* is built on native Persian *navâz-*, the present stem of *navâkhtan*, 'to play music', with the ending *-ande*, 'one who'. EtymDB has no entry for it.
