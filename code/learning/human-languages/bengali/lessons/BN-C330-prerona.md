## You'll want to know: প্রেরণা
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C330-UTSO330-05]; assesses=[] -->

**প্রেরণা** — *preronā* — "an inspiration; a motivation".

> আমার বাবাই আমার প্রেরণা।
>
> *āmār bābāi āmār preronā.*
>
> My father is my inspiration.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**প্রেরণা** (*preronā*) is Sanskrit *preraṇa*, setting in motion or urging on (Monier-Williams), in the feminine form. In Bengali it is the person or idea that pushes you forward.
