Portuguese, chapter 51: Glass, Plate, Spoon, Knife, Fork.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
o copo — a glass (to drink from).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for stairs; a ladder, then the Portuguese for a mirror.

You'll want to know: o copo.
o copo — "a glass (to drink from)". um copo de água: a glass of water.

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: o copo]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: o copo, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say o copo]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Portuguese for stairs; a ladder, then the Portuguese for a mirror, then say o copo again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does o copo mean? ("A glass (to drink from)".) Say it once more.
