## You'll want to know: 出色
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C451-SPECIAL451-03]; assesses=[] -->

**出色** — *chūsè* — "outstanding; excellent".

> **他的中文很出色** *tā de Zhōngwén hěn chūsè* — His spoken Chinese is outstanding.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**出色** (*chūsè*) is built from 出 'to stand out' and 色 'a look', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *tsyhwit* and *srik* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription); how the pair came to mean 'outstanding' is not settled.
