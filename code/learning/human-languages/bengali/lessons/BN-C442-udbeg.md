# উদ্বেগ (udbeg) — anxiety; unease

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[BN-LEX-C441-ATONKO441-04, BN-LEX-C441-ATONKO441-05] -->

## You'll want to know: উদ্বেগ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C442-UDBEG442-01]; assesses=[] -->

**উদ্বেগ** — *udbeg* — "anxiety; unease".

> ছেলের জ্বর নিয়ে বাবার উদ্বেগ বাড়ল।
>
> *chheler jbôr niye bābār udbeg bāṛlô.*
>
> Father's anxiety over his son's fever grew.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**উদ্বেগ** (*udbeg*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *udvega*, "trembling, agitation, distress" (Monier-Williams).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
