## You'll want to know: ظاهرا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C393-ETMINAN-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ظاهرا** (*zâheran*) — "apparently, seemingly".

> ظاهرا قطار امروز دیر میآید.
>
> *zâheran qatâr emruz dir miâyad.*
>
> Apparently the train is late today.

From *zâher* "outward appearance". For what seems true from the outside.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ظاهرا** is usually traced to Arabic *ẓāhiran* "outwardly", an adverb built on *ẓāhir* "visible, apparent".
