# برداشت کرنا (bardāsht karnā) — to put up with; to endure

## You'll want to know: برداشت کرنا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C458-PATIENCE458-03]; assesses=[] -->

**برداشت کرنا** — *bardāsht karnā* — "to put up with; to endure".

> وہ درد برداشت نہیں کر سکتی۔
>
> *voh dard bardāsht nahīṅ kar saktī.*
>
> She cannot bear the pain.

Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے درد برداشت کیا, *us ne dard bardāsht kiyā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**برداشت کرنا** (*bardāsht karnā*) is built from **برداشت**, usually traced to Persian *bardāsht*, "a taking up, a bearing", and **کرنا**, "to do".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
