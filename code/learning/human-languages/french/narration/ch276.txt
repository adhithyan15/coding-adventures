French, chapter 276: Les époques.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
l'époque — the time, the era, the period.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for the hitch, then the French for the deadline.

You'll want to know: l'époque.
l'époque — "the time, the era, the period".
À l'époque, on n'avait pas de téléphone portable. Back then, people did not have mobile phones.
À l'époque — back then; à cette époque-là — at that time.

The word, taken apart.
époque is a learned borrowing from Greek epokhḗ "a halt, a fixed point in time", which reached French through Medieval Latin (EtymDB lists the French word without a parent).

Lesson 2 of 5.
la décennie — the decade.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for the deadline, then the French for the time.

You'll want to know: la décennie.
la décennie — "the decade".
La ville a beaucoup changé en une décennie. The town changed a lot in a decade.
Ten years. La décade is also heard in this sense, though strictly it means ten days.

The word, taken apart.
décennie is a learned borrowing from Latin decennium "a period of ten years" (EtymDB), from decem "ten" and annus "year".
