## You'll want to know: das Referat
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C378-HAUSARBEIT-02]; assesses=[] -->

**das Referat** — "the class talk, the oral report".

> Morgen halte ich ein Referat über Wale.
>
> *Tomorrow I am giving a talk in class about whales.*

Plural: *die Referate*. *Ein Referat halten* — to give a talk. In an office, *das Referat* is also a section.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Referat* comes from the Latin verb form *referat* "let him report", from *referre* "to carry back, to bring back word" (Lewis & Short). It is usually said to have been first a note on a document, then the report itself.
