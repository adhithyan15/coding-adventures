## You'll want to know: സൗകര്യം
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C344-SAUKARYAM-01]; assesses=[ML-LEX-C121-TRAVEL-04, ML-LEX-C42-ADJ-03, ML-LEX-C139-TIME-01, ML-CONCEPT-C06-DATIVE-SUBJECT-01] -->

**സൗകര്യം** — *saukaryaṁ* — "convenience", and in the plural "facilities".

> **സ്റ്റേഷനിൽ നല്ല സൗകര്യങ്ങൾ ഉണ്ട്.**
>
> *sṟṟēṣanil nalla saukaryaṅṅaḷ uṇṭŭ.* — "The station has good facilities."

With the dative it says what suits you:

> **നാളെ എനിക്ക് സൗകര്യമാണ്.**
>
> *nāḷe enikkŭ saukaryamāṇŭ.* — "Tomorrow suits me."

**നിങ്ങളുടെ സൗകര്യം പോലെ**, "as suits you", is how a polite Malayali leaves the choice to you.

Its first syllable carries a vowel sign you have not written alone. The next lesson takes it out.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**സൗകര്യം** (*saukaryaṁ*) ← Sanskrit *saukarya*, "ease, facility", a tatsama with Malayalam's noun
ending. Monier-Williams derives *saukarya* from *su-kara*, "easy to do": *su-*, "well, easily",
and *kara*, "doing".
