Russian, chapter 366: Elections.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
выборы (výbory) — an election.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for politics, then the Russian for a politician.

You'll want to know: выборы.
выборы (výbory) — "an election".
Выборы будут в сентябре. Výbory búdut v sentyabré. The election will be in September.
Plural only. Выборы президента — a presidential election.

The word, taken apart.
выборы (výbory) is built from вы- "out" and the root of брать "to take", as in выбирать "to choose": an election picks people out.

Lesson 2 of 5.
голосовать (golosovát) — to vote.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a politician, then the Russian for an election.

You'll want to know: голосовать.
голосовать (golosovát) — "to vote".
Мы голосовали за нового мэра. My golosováli za nóvogo méra. We voted for a new mayor.
Imperfective; the present is голосую, голосуешь; the perfective is проголосовать. Голосовать за + accusative, против + genitive.

The word, taken apart.
голосовать (golosovát) is built on голос "voice", inherited through Old East Slavic golosŭ from Proto-Slavic golsŭ "voice" (EtymDB). The -оло- is the native Russian shape: to vote is to give your voice.

Lesson 3 of 5.
партия (pártiya) — a party (in politics); a game.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for an election, then the Russian for to vote.

You'll want to know: партия.
партия (pártiya) — "a party (in politics); a game".
Он член этой партии. On chlen étoy pártii. He is a member of this party.
Feminine. Also one game of chess: партия (pártiya) в шахматы.

The word, taken apart.
партия (pártiya) was borrowed from Polish partia, from French partie "a part", from partir "to divide", from Latin partīre, built on pars "a part, a piece" (EtymDB).

Lesson 4 of 5.
протест (protést) — a protest.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to vote, then the Russian for a party.

You'll want to know: протест.
протест (protést) — "a protest".
Студенты вышли на протест. Studénty výshli na protést. The students came out to protest.
Masculine. В знак протеста — as a sign of protest.

The word, taken apart.
протест (protést) is usually traced, through western languages, to Latin prōtestārī "to declare in public", built on testis "a witness" (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 5 of 5.
протестовать (protestovát) — to protest.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a party, then the Russian for a protest.

You'll want to know: протестовать.
протестовать (protestovát) — "to protest".
Жители протестуют против стройки. Zhíteli protestúyut prótiv stróyki. The residents are protesting against the building work.
Imperfective; the present is протестую, протестуешь. Протестовать против + genitive.

The word, taken apart.
протестовать (protestovát) is built on протест (protést) with the verb suffix -овать; the noun is usually traced to Latin prōtestārī "to declare in public", on testis "a witness" (Lewis & Short).
