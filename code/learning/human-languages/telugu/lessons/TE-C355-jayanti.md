# జయంతి (jayanti) — a birth anniversary

## You'll want to know: జయంతి
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C355-VARSHIKAM355-04]; assesses=[] -->

**జయంతి** — *jayanti* — "a birth anniversary".

> ఆ కవి జయంతి సందర్భంగా సభ జరిగింది.
>
> *ā kavi jayanti sandarbhaṅgā sabha jarigindi.*
>
> A meeting was held for the poet's birth anniversary.

For the famous: a poet's or a leader's జయంతి.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**జయంతి** (*jayanti*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *jayantī* (Monier-Williams), a word built on *jaya*, "victory" (CDIAL 5141).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
