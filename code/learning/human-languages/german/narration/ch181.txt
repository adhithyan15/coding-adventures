Lesson 1 of 5.
fotografieren — to photograph.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to hold on to, then the German for to whisper.

You'll want to know: fotografieren.
fotografieren — "to photograph".

The word, taken apart.
fotografieren is the verb of the word for "photography", a modern coinage from Greek phōs, "light", and gráphein, "to write", + the ending -ieren. See Foto.

Lesson 3 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to photograph, then the German for to have breakfast.

You'll want to know: gießen.
gießen — "to pour, to water".

The word, taken apart.
gießen from Middle High German giezen from Old High German giozan, "to pour" (EtymDB 2.1), from Proto-Germanic geutaną — inherited. English ingot, poured metal, shares the root.
