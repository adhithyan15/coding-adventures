## You'll want to know: chūzūchē
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C44-PLACES44-05]; assesses=[] -->

*chūzūchē* — "taxi".

> *chūzūchē hěn guì* — Taxis are expensive.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*chūzūchē* is built from chūzū 'to rent out' and 车 'car'; its parts are inherited from Middle Chinese *tsyhwit*, *tsu* 'tax' and *tsyhae* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription).
