## You'll want to know: ਜਵਾਈ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C329-INLAW329-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ਜਵਾਈ** — *javāī* — "a son-in-law".

> ਸਾਡਾ ਜਵਾਈ ਬੈਂਕ ਵਿੱਚ ਕੰਮ ਕਰਦਾ ਹੈ।
>
> *sāḍā javāī baiṅk vich kamm kardā hai.*
>
> Our son-in-law works in a bank.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਜਵਾਈ** (*javāī*) is inherited from Sanskrit *jāmātṛ*, "a daughter's husband"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *javāī*, "daughter's husband", under it (CDIAL 5198).
