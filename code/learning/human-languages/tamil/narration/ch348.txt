Tamil, chapter 348: For and Against.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
எதிர் (etir) — to oppose; to resist.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for a basis, then the Tamil for an explanation.

You'll want to know: எதிர்.
எதிர் — etir — "to oppose; to resist".
பல மாணவர்கள் இந்தத் திட்டத்தை எதிர்த்தார்கள். pala māṇavarkaḷ intat tiṭṭattai etirttārkaḷ. Many students opposed this plan.
Past (I …): எதிர்த்தேன், etirttēṉ.
What is opposed takes -ஐ: திட்டத்தை எதிர் (etir). The same word means opposite, as in எதிரே.

The word, taken apart.
எதிர் (etir) is native Dravidian (DEDR 795): the noun means "that which is in front, opposite, adverse", and the verb "to oppose, to face". Tamil already uses it in எதிரே, "opposite".

Lesson 2 of 5.
ஆட்சேபி (āṭcēpi) — to object.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for an explanation, then the Tamil for to oppose.

You'll want to know: ஆட்சேபி.
ஆட்சேபி — āṭcēpi — "to object".
அந்த முடிவை யாரும் ஆட்சேபிக்கவில்லை. anta muṭivai yārum āṭcēpikkavillai. Nobody objected to that decision.
Past (I …): ஆட்சேபித்தேன், āṭcēpittēṉ.
What is objected to takes -ஐ: முடிவை ஆட்சேபி (āṭcēpi). The noun is ஆட்சேபனை, an objection.

The word, taken apart.
ஆட்சேபி (āṭcēpi) is a verb built on Sanskrit ākṣepa, "objection" (CDIAL 1032). Tamil writes the Sanskrit kṣ as ட்ச, as in சாட்சி from sākṣin.

Lesson 3 of 5.
வாதிடு (vātiṭu) — to argue.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for to oppose, then the Tamil for to object.

You'll want to know: வாதிடு.
வாதிடு — vātiṭu — "to argue".
அவள் தன் உரிமைக்காக வாதிட்டாள். avaḷ taṉ urimaikkāka vātiṭṭāḷ. She argued for her right.
Past (I …): வாதிட்டேன், vātiṭṭēṉ.
With -உடன் for the other side: அவருடன் வாதிட்டேன், I argued with him.

The word, taken apart.
வாதிடு (vātiṭu) is a compound: vātam, "argument", is borrowed from Sanskrit vāda, "speech" (CDIAL 11507), and native iṭu, "to put" (DEDR 442), makes it a verb, as in முத்தமிடு.

Lesson 4 of 5.
உடன்பாடு (uṭaṉpāṭu) — a consensus; agreement.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for to object, then the Tamil for to argue.

You'll want to know: உடன்பாடு.
உடன்பாடு — uṭaṉpāṭu — "a consensus; agreement".
இந்த விஷயத்தில் எங்களுக்குள் உடன்பாடு இருக்கிறது. inta viṣayattil eṅkaḷukkuḷ uṭaṉpāṭu irukkiṟatu. We are in agreement on this matter.
எனக்கு உடன்பாடு (uṭaṉpāṭu) இல்லை, I do not agree: the dative experiencer again.

The word, taken apart.
உடன்பாடு (uṭaṉpāṭu) is native: DEDR 945 lists Tamil uṭaṉ, "together with", and uṭaṉ-paṭu, "to agree"; pāṭu is the noun of paṭu, so the word means "a being-together".

Lesson 5 of 5.
சிலர் (cilar) — some people.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for to argue, then the Tamil for a consensus.

You'll want to know: சிலர்.
சிலர் — cilar — "some people".
சிலர் இந்த யோசனையை ஒத்துக்கொள்ளவில்லை. cilar inta yōcaṉaiyai ottukkoḷḷavillai. Some people did not accept this idea.
Take -ஐ for an object: சிலரைக் கேட்டேன், I asked some people.

The word, taken apart.
சிலர் (cilar) is native Dravidian: DEDR 1571 lists Tamil cil, cila, "some, few", and cilar, "a few persons", with the personal plural -அர்.
