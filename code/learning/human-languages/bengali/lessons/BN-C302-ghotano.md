## You'll want to know: ঘটানো
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C302-KAHINI302-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ঘটানো** — *ghôṭāno* — "to bring about; to cause".

> একটা ছোট ভুল বড় দুর্ঘটনা ঘটাল।
>
> *ekṭā chhoṭo bhul bôṛo durghôṭônā ghôṭālô.*
>
> One small mistake caused a big accident.

Past (he/she …): সেই ভুল দুর্ঘটনা ঘটাল, *sei bhul durghôṭônā ghôṭālô*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ঘটানো** (*ghôṭāno*) is ঘটা, to happen — Sanskrit *ghaṭatē*, 'is busy with; happens' (CDIAL 4407) — with the -ānō ending: to make something happen.
