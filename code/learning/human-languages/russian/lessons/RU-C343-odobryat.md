## You'll want to know: одобрять
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C343-KRITIKA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**одобрять** (*odobryát*) — "to approve of".

> Отец не одобряет мой выбор.
>
> *Otéts ne odobryáyet moy výbor.*
>
> My father does not approve of my choice.

Imperfective; perfective *одобрить*. It takes the accusative.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**одобрять** is built from *о-* and *добрый* "good", so to approve is to call a thing good. *Добрый* is inherited from Proto-Slavic *\*dobrŭ* (EtymDB).
