Urdu, chapter 502: Rattles and Rust.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
کھڑکھڑانا (khaṛkhaṛānā) — to rattle.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for to melt (something), then the Urdu for to plate (with metal).

You'll want to know: کھڑکھڑانا.
کھڑکھڑانا — khaṛkhaṛānā — "to rattle".
ہوا میں کھڑکی کھڑکھڑاتی ہے۔ hawā meṅ khiṛkī khaṛkhaṛātī hai. The window rattles in the wind.
Past (it …): وہ دروازہ کھڑکھڑایا, voh darvāzā khaṛkhaṛāyā.

The word, taken apart.
کھڑکھڑانا (khaṛkhaṛānā) is inherited from Sanskrit khaṭakhaṭāyatē, "crackles", a word imitating the sound (CDIAL 3771).

Lesson 2 of 5.
قبضہ (qabzā) — a hinge; possession (a hold on something).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for to plate (with metal), then the Urdu for to rattle.

You'll want to know: قبضہ.
قبضہ — qabzā — "a hinge; possession (a hold on something)".
دروازے کا ایک قبضہ ٹوٹ گیا۔ darvāze kā ek qabzā ṭūṭ gayā. One of the door's hinges has broken.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
قبضہ (qabzā) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic qabḍa, "a grip, a handle"; Urdu also uses it for "possession", a hold on something, while the sense "a hinge" came later, and the sources we use do not show how it arose.
