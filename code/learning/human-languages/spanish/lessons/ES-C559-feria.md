## You'll want to know: la feria
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C559-DESFILE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**la feria** — "the fair".

> En abril hay feria en Sevilla.
>
> *In April there is a fair in Seville.*

*Una feria del libro* — a book fair.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*feria* is inherited from Latin *fēriae* 'holidays, feast days' (EtymDB). Markets were held on feast days.
