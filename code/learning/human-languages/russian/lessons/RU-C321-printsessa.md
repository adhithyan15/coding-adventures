## You'll want to know: принцесса
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C321-KOROL-03]; assesses=[] -->

**принцесса** (*printséssa*) — "a princess".

> Принцесса не хотела выходить замуж.
>
> *Printséssa ne khotéla vykhodít zámuzh.*
>
> The princess did not want to marry.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*принцесса* was borrowed from French *princesse* (EtymDB), built on *prince*, from Latin *prīnceps* "first, chief".
