## You'll want to know: 说
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C53-SPEAK53-01]; assesses=[] -->

**说** — *shuō* — "to say; to speak".

> **他说你的手很好看** *tā shuō nǐ de shǒu hěn hǎokàn* — He says you have lovely hands.

The new character, in pieces: 说 — 讠, the speech piece, on the left; on the right a dot and a short fall, then 口, then two legs. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**说** (*shuō*) is inherited from Middle Chinese *sywet* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'to tell'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for asking politely.
