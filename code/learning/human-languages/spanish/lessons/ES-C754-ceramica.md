## You'll want to know: la cerámica
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C754-PESCA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**la cerámica** — "pottery, ceramics".

> Hago un curso de cerámica.
>
> *I am doing a pottery course.*

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*cerámica* comes from Greek *keramikḗ* "the potter's art", built on *kéramos* "potter's clay"; it probably came in through French *céramique*.
