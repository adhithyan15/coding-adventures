Telugu, chapter 159: To squeeze, To soak, To repair, To throw away, To lose.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
పిండు (piṇḍu) — to squeeze.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to take off, then the Telugu for to measure.

You'll want to know: పిండు.
పిండు — piṇḍu — "to squeeze".

The word, taken apart.
పిండు (piṇḍu) is native Dravidian. DEDR 4183 lists piṇḍu, "to squeeze, press", with Tamil piḻi and Kannada piḻi, "to squeeze out". DEDR compares Sanskrit piṇyāka, "oilcake" (CDIAL 8174).

Lesson 4 of 5.
పారేయు (pārēyu) — to throw away.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to soak, then the Telugu for to repair.

You'll want to know: పారేయు.
పారేయు — pārēyu — "to throw away".

The word, taken apart.
The second part of పారేయు (pārēyu) is native vēyu, "to put, place" (DEDR 5549). The first part matches pāṟu, "to run, flow" (DEDR 4020), though DEDR does not list the compound.

Lesson 5 of 5.
పోగొట్టుకో (pōgoṭṭukō) — to lose (something).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to repair, then the Telugu for to throw away.

You'll want to know: పోగొట్టుకో.
పోగొట్టుకో — pōgoṭṭukō — "to lose (something)".

The word, taken apart.
పోగొట్టుకో (pōgoṭṭukō) is built on native pō, "to go" (DEDR 4572), with the reflexive -kō, "for oneself" (DEDR 2151): "to let something go from oneself". DEDR does not list the middle -goṭṭu in this use.
