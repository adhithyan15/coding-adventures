## You'll want to know: स्नेहसंमेलन
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C321-PANGAT321-03]; assesses=[] -->

**स्नेहसंमेलन** — *snehsammelan* — "a get-together; an annual social".

> शाळेचं वार्षिक स्नेहसंमेलन पुढच्या आठवड्यात आहे.
>
> *śāḷecaṁ vārṣik snehsammelan puḍhacyā āṭhvaḍyāt āhe.*
>
> The school's annual get-together is next week.

Neuter.

स्नेह, affection, + संमेलन, a gathering.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**स्नेहसंमेलन** (*snehsammelan*) is a modern compound built from Sanskrit *sneha*, 'affection', and *sammelana*, 'a meeting, a gathering' (Monier-Williams).
