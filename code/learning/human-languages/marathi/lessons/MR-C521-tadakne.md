## You'll want to know: तडकणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C521-DURUSTI521-02]; assesses=[] -->

**तडकणे** — *taḍakṇe* — "to crack".

> खाली पडल्यावर आरसा तडकला.
>
> *khālī paḍlyāvar ārsā taḍaklā.*
>
> The mirror cracked when it fell.

Past (he …): तो आरसा तडकला, *to ārsā taḍaklā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**तडकणे** (*taḍakṇe*) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi *taḍakṇẽ*, "to crack", with the sound word *traṭ*, "crackle" (CDIAL 5988).
