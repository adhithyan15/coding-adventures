## You'll want to know: ઓળવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C155-READY155-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ઓળવું** — *oḷvũ* — "to comb one's hair, to comb".

> **તે રોજ વાળ ઓળે છે.** — *te roj vāḷ oḷe che* — "She combs her hair every day."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

The origin of **ઓળવું** (*oḷvũ*) is not settled. CDIAL lists no Gujarati verb of this form with the sense of combing, and EtymDB has no entry for it.
