## You'll want to know: さばく

**さばく** — *sabaku* — "a desert".

> **さばくは ひるは あついです。** — *sabaku wa hiru wa atsui desu* — "The desert is hot during the day."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**さばく** (*sabaku*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *sa*, "sand" (Middle Chinese *srae*) and *baku*, "desert" (*mak*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
