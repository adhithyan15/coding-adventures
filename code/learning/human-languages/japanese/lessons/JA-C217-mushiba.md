## You'll want to know: むしば
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C217-DOCTOR217-05]; assesses=[] -->

**むしば** — *mushiba* — "a cavity; a decayed tooth".

> **むしばは ありません。** — *mushiba wa arimasen* — "I have no cavities."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**むしば** (*mushiba*) is native Japanese (wago), built from *mushi*, "insect, worm", and *ha*, "tooth", voiced to *ba*, both inherited rather than borrowed (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
