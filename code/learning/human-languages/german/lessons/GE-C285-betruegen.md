## You'll want to know: betrügen
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C285-BETRUG-01]; assesses=[] -->

**betrügen** — "to cheat, to deceive".

> Der Verkäufer betrog viele alte Leute.
>
> *The salesman cheated many old people.*

Strong verb: *betrog*, *hat betrogen*. Also to be unfaithful: *Er betrügt seine Frau*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*betrügen* is inherited from Old High German *bitriogan*, which EtymDB traces to Proto-Germanic *bidreuganą* "to betray, to deceive".
