## Pre-A1: two timed four-skill mocks

Added two distinct Bengali-script pre-A1 papers, answer keys, and a shared
rubric matching the existing task-shape inventory: 14 reading and 14 listening
selections, eight writing tasks, and six speaking prompts per form. Examiner-only
listening scripts and ten-second writing model cards are kept out of the learner
copy. Each skill is independently scored out of 100 with a 60-point threshold;
Bengali matra-line, vowel-sign-side, and hasanta evidence is assessed rather
than inferred from a copied model.

The level-owned test checks the two forms and their four-skill envelopes. This
does not establish book-only learner readiness: the gentle writing runway and
human validation remain separate work.
