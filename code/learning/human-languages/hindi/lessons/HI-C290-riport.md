## You'll want to know: रिपोर्ट
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C290-THANA290-02]; assesses=[] -->

**रिपोर्ट** — *riporṭ* — "a report (to the police)".

> मीरा ने थाने जाकर चोरी की रिपोर्ट की।
>
> *Mīrā ne thāne jākar corī kī riporṭ kī.*
>
> Mira went to the police station and reported the theft.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**रिपोर्ट** (*riporṭ*) is borrowed from English *report*, taken into Hindi as a feminine noun: **चोरी की रिपोर्ट**, the report of a theft, and **रिपोर्ट करना**, to report it.
