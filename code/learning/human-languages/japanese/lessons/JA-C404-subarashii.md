## You'll want to know: すばらしい
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C404-FUUKEI404-04]; assesses=[] -->

**すばらしい** — *subarashii* — "wonderful; splendid".

> **やまの うえの ふうけいは すばらしかったです。** — *yama no ue no fūkei wa subarashikatta desu* — "The scenery from the mountaintop was wonderful."

An i-adjective.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**すばらしい** (*subarashii*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict).
