## You'll want to know: o skate
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C507-RODAS-01]; assesses=[] -->

**o skate** — "skateboard; skateboarding".

> O rapaz vai para a escola de skate.
>
> *The boy goes to school on his skateboard.*

*Andar de skate* — to skateboard.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*skate* was borrowed from English *skate* (EtymDB), which came from Dutch *schaats*.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
