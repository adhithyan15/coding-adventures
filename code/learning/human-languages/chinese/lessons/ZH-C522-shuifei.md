## You'll want to know: shuǐfèi
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C522-FIXIT522-03]; assesses=[] -->

*shuǐfèi* — "water bill".

> *zhège yuè de shuǐfèi bǐ shàng ge yuè guì* — This month's water bill is higher than last month's.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*shuǐfèi* is built from 水 'water' and *fèi* 'a cost', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *sywijX* and *phj+jH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'to use up, to spend'.
