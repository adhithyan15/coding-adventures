## You'll want to know: باربر
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C435-DARBAST-04]; assesses=[] -->

**باربر** (*bârbar*) — "a porter".

> باربر چمدانهای ما را تا تاکسی برد.
>
> *Bârbar chamedân-hâ-ye mâ râ tâ tâksi bord.*
>
> The porter took our suitcases to the taxi.

*bâr* 'load' + *bar* 'carrier'.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*bârbar* is a native compound of *bâr* 'load', which EtymDB lists, and *bar*, the present stem of *bordan* 'to carry': a load-carrier.
