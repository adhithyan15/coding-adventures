## You'll want to know: сообщать
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C280-SOOBSHCHAT-01]; assesses=[] -->

**сообщать** (*soobshchát*) — "to report, to inform".

> Радио сообщает о сильном ветре.
>
> *Rádio soobshcháyet o sílnom vétre.*
>
> The radio is reporting a strong wind.

Imperfective; perfective *сообщить* (*soobshchít*). The person is in the dative, the news in the accusative or with *о*: *сообщить маме новость*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*сообщать* is built on *со-* and *общий* "common", so "to make shared"; *общий* is itself a Church Slavonic form (EtymDB: from Church Slavonic *obĭščĭ*, Proto-Slavic *obĭťĭ* "common"), and EtymDB lists no parent for the verb.
