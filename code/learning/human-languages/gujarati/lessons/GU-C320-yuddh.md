## You'll want to know: યુદ્ધ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C320-YUDDH320-01]; assesses=[] -->

**યુદ્ધ** — *yuddh* — "a war".

> બે રાજાઓ વચ્ચે યુદ્ધ થયું.
>
> *be rājāo vacce yuddh thayũ.*
>
> War broke out between two kings.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**યુદ્ધ** (*yuddh*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *yuddha*, "fought", and as a noun "a battle" (Monier-Williams). It is neuter: **યુદ્ધ થયું**, there was a war.
