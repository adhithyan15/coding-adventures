## You'll want to know: कळशी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C525-BHANDI525-01]; assesses=[] -->

**कळशी** — *kaḷśī* — "a brass water pot".

> आजीने नळावरून कळशी भरून आणली.
>
> *ājīne naḷāvarūn kaḷśī bharūn āṇlī.*
>
> Grandmother filled a water pot at the tap and brought it.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**कळशी** (*kaḷśī*) is a tadbhava, inherited from Sanskrit *kalaśa*, "waterpot" (CDIAL 2920).
