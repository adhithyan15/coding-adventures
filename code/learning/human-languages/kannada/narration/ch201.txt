Kannada, chapter 201: To postpone, To explain, To pass, To remember, To print.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಮುಂದೂಡು (mundūḍu) — to postpone.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to finish, then the Kannada for to start.

You'll want to know: ಮುಂದೂಡು.
ಮುಂದೂಡು — mundūḍu — "to postpone".

The word, taken apart.
ಮುಂದೂಡು (mundūḍu) is native Dravidian: mundu, "ahead, in front" (DEDR 5020), and dūḍu, "to push, shove" (DEDR 3380): "to push ahead".

Lesson 2 of 5.
ವಿವರಿಸು (vivarisu) — to explain.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to start, then the Kannada for to postpone.

You'll want to know: ವಿವರಿಸು.
ವಿವರಿಸು — vivarisu — "to explain".

The word, taken apart.
ವಿವರಿಸು (vivarisu) is Sanskrit vivaraṇa, "an opening out, an explanation", made into a Kannada verb with -isu. CDIAL 11915 lists the root noun vivara, "hole, opening".
