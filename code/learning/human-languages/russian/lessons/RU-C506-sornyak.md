## You'll want to know: сорняк
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C506-VEGETABLES-04]; assesses=[] -->

**сорняк** (*sornyák*) — "a weed".

> После дождя сорняки растут очень быстро.
>
> *Pósle dozhdyá sornyakí rastút óchen býstro.*
>
> After rain the weeds grow very fast.

Masculine; genitive сорняка.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*сорняк* is built on *сор* 'litter, rubbish' with *-няк*; EtymDB has no entry.
