## You'll want to know: ತೀರ್ಮಾನಿಸು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C431-TIRMANISU431-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ತೀರ್ಮಾನಿಸು** — *tīrmānisu* — "to conclude; to resolve".

> ಕೊನೆಗೆ ನಾನು ಊರಿಗೆ ಹಿಂತಿರುಗಲು ತೀರ್ಮಾನಿಸಿದೆ.
>
> *konege nānu ūrige hintirugalu tīrmāniside.*
>
> In the end I resolved to go back to my town.

Past (I …): ತೀರ್ಮಾನಿಸಿದೆ, *tīrmāniside*.

ತೀರ್ಮಾನ, a decision or a conclusion.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ತೀರ್ಮಾನಿಸು** (*tīrmānisu*) is built on *tīrmāna*, "a settlement, a conclusion", from native *tīr*, "to be finished, to be settled" (DEDR 3278).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
