Gujarati, chapter 261: Kites and fairs.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
પતંગ (patang) — kite.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to guess, then the Gujarati for to choose.

You'll want to know: પતંગ.
પતંગ — patang — "kite".
મારો પતંગ દૂર ગયો. — māro patang dūr gayo — "My kite went far."

The word, taken apart.
પતંગ (patang) comes from Sanskrit pataṅga, "bird; flying insect", taken over in a learned shape and used for the paper kite (CDIAL 7721). CDIAL lists no Gujarati form.

Lesson 2 of 5.
ઉડાડવું (uḍāḍvũ) — to fly a kite, to make fly.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to choose, then the Gujarati for kite.

You'll want to know: ઉડાડવું.
ઉડાડવું — uḍāḍvũ — "to fly a kite, to make fly".
અમે બપોરે પતંગ ઉડાડ્યા. — ame bapore patang uḍāḍyā — "We flew kites in the afternoon."
The causative of ઊડવું, ūḍvũ, to fly up: to make something fly.

The word, taken apart.
ઉડાડવું (uḍāḍvũ), to make something fly, is a causative of the inherited ūḍvũ, "to fly up". CDIAL lists that verb, as uḍvũ, under Sanskrit uḍḍīyatē, "flies up", and uḍāḍvũ under its causative uḍḍāpayati (CDIAL 1697).

Lesson 3 of 5.
મેળો (meḷo) — fair.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for kite, then the Gujarati for to fly a kite.

You'll want to know: મેળો.
મેળો — meḷo — "fair".
અમે ગામના મેળામાં ગયા. — ame gāmnā meḷāmā̃ gayā — "We went to the village fair."

The word, taken apart.
મેળો (meḷo) is a tadbhava. It comes from Sanskrit mēla, "meeting"; CDIAL glosses the Gujarati word "assembly, fair" (CDIAL 10331).

Lesson 4 of 5.
ગરબો (garbo) — garba, a circle dance.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to fly a kite, then the Gujarati for fair.

You'll want to know: ગરબો.
ગરબો — garbo — "garba, a circle dance".
અમે રાત્રે ગરબો ગાયો. — ame rātre garbo gāyo — "We sang a garba at night."

The word, taken apart.
The origin of ગરબો (garbo) is not settled. A link with Sanskrit gárbha, "womb, inside", is often proposed, but CDIAL lists no Gujarati garbo under that word (CDIAL 4055).

Lesson 5 of 5.
ફટાકડો (phaṭākḍo) — firecracker.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for fair, then the Gujarati for garba.

You'll want to know: ફટાકડો.
ફટાકડો — phaṭākḍo — "firecracker".
દિવાળીમાં મેં ફટાકડા ખરીદ્યા. — divāḷīmā̃ mẽ phaṭākḍā kharīdyā — "I bought firecrackers at Diwali."

The word, taken apart.
The origin of ફટાકડો (phaṭākḍo) is not settled. It looks built on a sound-word for a bang, but CDIAL has no entry for it and our sources do not trace it.
