## You'll want to know: ವರಾಂಡ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C543-GRHA543-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ವರಾಂಡ** — *varāṇḍa* — "a veranda".

> ಸಂಜೆ ಅಜ್ಜ ವರಾಂಡದಲ್ಲಿ ಕುಳಿತು ಪತ್ರಿಕೆ ಓದುತ್ತಾರೆ.
>
> *sañje ajja varāṇḍadalli kuḷitu patrike ōduttāre.*
>
> In the evening grandfather sits on the veranda and reads the paper.

A roofed space along the front of the house; ಜಗಲಿ is the raised one.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ವರಾಂಡ** (*varāṇḍa*) is a loan from English *veranda*.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
