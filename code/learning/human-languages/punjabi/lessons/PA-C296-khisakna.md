# ਖਿਸਕਣਾ (khisakṇā) — to slide; to slip away

## You'll want to know: ਖਿਸਕਣਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C296-SLIP296-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ਖਿਸਕਣਾ** — *khisakṇā* — "to slide; to slip away".

> ਮੀਂਹ ਵਿੱਚ ਪਹਾੜ ਤੋਂ ਪੱਥਰ ਖਿਸਕਦੇ ਹਨ।
>
> *mīṁh vich pahāṛ ton patthar khisakde han.*
>
> In the rain, stones slide down from the mountain.

Past (it …): ਉਹ ਪੱਥਰ ਖਿਸਕਿਆ, *oh patthar khiskiā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਖਿਸਕਣਾ** (*khisakṇā*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*khis*, "slip"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *khiskaṇā*, "to slip away", under it (CDIAL 3888).
