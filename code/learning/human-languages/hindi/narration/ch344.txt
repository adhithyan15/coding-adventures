Hindi, chapter 344: What Follows From It.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
परिणाम (pariṇām) — a consequence, an outcome.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a basis, grounds, then the Hindi for responsible.

You'll want to know: परिणाम.
परिणाम — pariṇām — "a consequence, an outcome".
हर काम का कोई परिणाम होता है। har kām kā koī pariṇām hotā hai. Every action has some consequence.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
परिणाम (pariṇām) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit pariṇāma, "a change; a result" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 5 of 5.
स्रोत (srot) — a source.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for to rise, then the Hindi for to make worse.

You'll want to know: स्रोत.
स्रोत — srot — "a source".
ख़बर का स्रोत क्या है? khabar kā srot kyā hai? What is the source of the news?
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
स्रोत (srot) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit srotas, "a stream, a current" (Monier-Williams). Hindi uses it for the source of anything, news included.
