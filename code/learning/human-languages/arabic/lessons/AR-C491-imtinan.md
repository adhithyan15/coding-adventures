## You'll want to know: امتنان
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C491-FAKHR491-03]; assesses=[] -->

**امتنان** — *imtinān* — "gratitude".

> كتبت رسالة امتنان إلى الممرضة.
>
> *katabtu risālat imtinān ilā al-mumarriḍa.*
>
> I wrote a letter of thanks to the nurse.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**امتنان** (*imtinān*) is built on the native Arabic root *m-n-n*, "to grant a favour" (Lane). Imtinān is the feeling of one who has been granted a favour.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
