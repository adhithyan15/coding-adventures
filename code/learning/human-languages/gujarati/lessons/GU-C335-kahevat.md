## You'll want to know: કહેવત
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C335-SADHU335-04]; assesses=[] -->

**કહેવત** — *kahevat* — "a proverb".

> મારા દાદાએ એક કહેવત કહી.
>
> *mārā dādāe ek kahevat kahī.*
>
> My grandfather quoted a proverb.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**કહેવત** (*kahevat*) is built on **કહેવું**, to say, from Sanskrit *kathayati*, "converses, describes" (CDIAL 2703): a saying everybody knows. It is feminine: **એક કહેવત કહી**.
