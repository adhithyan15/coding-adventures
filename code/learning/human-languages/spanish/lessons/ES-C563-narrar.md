## You'll want to know: narrar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C563-RELATO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**narrar** — "to narrate, to tell".

> El libro narra la vida de un pescador.
>
> *The book tells the life of a fisherman.*

A written word; in speech, *contar*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*narrar* is a learned borrowing from Latin *nārrāre* 'to tell, to relate' (EtymDB).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.
