# पधारिए (padhārie) — please come (formal)

## You'll want to know: पधारिए
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C115-COMEIN-02]; assesses=[] -->

**पधारिए** (*padhārie*) — "please come", in its most formal, honouring form.

The everyday *āie* comes first in this chapter; **पधारिए** is what you say to an honoured guest.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

A second welcome, for an honoured guest.
