## You'll want to know: serpentear
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C410-CURVAS-05]; assesses=[] -->

**serpentear** — "to wind, to snake (of a road or river)".

> A estrada serpenteia pela montanha.
>
> *The road winds up the mountain.*

Built on *a serpente*, the snake.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*serpentear* is built on *serpente* 'snake', from Latin *serpēns* 'creeping thing', from *serpere* 'to creep' (Lewis & Short).
