## You'll want to know: годовщина
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C310-YUBILEY-02]; assesses=[] -->

**годовщина** (*godovshchína*) — "an anniversary".

> Сегодня годовщина их свадьбы.
>
> *Sevódnya godovshchína ikh svádby.*
>
> Today is their wedding anniversary.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*годовщина* is built on *год* "year", inherited through Old East Slavic *godŭ* from Proto-Slavic *godŭ* "the right time" (EtymDB).
