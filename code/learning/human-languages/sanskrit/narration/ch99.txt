[your turn — say: a moment, before, after, now, always]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
A day? (अहः, ahaḥ.) Early evening? (प्रदोषः, pradoṣaḥ.) Afternoon? (अपराह्णः, aparāhṇaḥ.) Midnight? (मध्यरात्रिः, madhyarātriḥ.) Dawn? (उषः, uṣaḥ.) Morning? (प्रभातम्, prabhātam.) Twilight? (सन्ध्या, sandhyā.) Early winter? (हेमन्तः, hemantaḥ.) Late winter? (शिशिरः, śiśiraḥ.) Autumn? (शरद्, śarad.) A moment? (निमेषः, nimeṣaḥ.) Before? (पूर्वम्, pūrvam.) After? (पश्चात्, paścāt.) Now? (अधुना, adhunā.) Always? (सर्वदा, sarvadā.) Ever? (कदापि, kadāpi.) A delay? (विलम्बः, vilambaḥ.) Left? (वामः, vāmaḥ.) Right; south? (दक्षिणः, dakṣiṇaḥ.) Inside? (अन्तः, antaḥ.) Outside? (बहिः, bahiḥ.) Above? (उपरि, upari.) Below? (अधः, adhaḥ.) In front? (अग्रे, agre.) Behind? (पृष्ठतः, pṛṣṭhataḥ.)
