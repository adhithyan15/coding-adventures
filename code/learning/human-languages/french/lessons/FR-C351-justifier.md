## You'll want to know: justifier
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C351-REACTION-04]; assesses=[] -->

**justifier** — "to justify".

> Elle a justifié sa décision devant l'équipe.
>
> *She justified her decision in front of the team.*

*Se justifier* — to explain yourself.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*justifier* comes from Late Latin *iūstificāre* "to justify" (EtymDB), built on *iūstus* "right, fair" (Lewis & Short) and *-ficāre* "to make".
