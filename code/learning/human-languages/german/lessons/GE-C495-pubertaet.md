## You'll want to know: die Pubertät
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C495-ERZIEHUNG-05]; assesses=[] -->

**die Pubertät** — "puberty".

> In der Pubertät hatte mein Sohn oft schlechte Laune.
>
> *During puberty my son was often in a bad mood.*

No plural.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Pubertät* is a learned borrowing from Latin *pūbertās* "the age of maturity", built on *pūbēs* "grown up" (Lewis & Short).
