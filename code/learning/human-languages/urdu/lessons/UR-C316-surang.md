## You'll want to know: سرنگ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C316-SURANG316-03]; assesses=[] -->

**سرنگ** — *surang* — "a tunnel".

> قیدیوں نے ایک سرنگ کھودی۔
>
> *qaidiyoṅ ne ek surang khodī.*
>
> The prisoners dug a tunnel.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**سرنگ** (*surang*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *suraṅg*, "a burglar's hole, a mine", under Sanskrit *suruṅgā* (CDIAL 13506). It is feminine: **سرنگ کھودی**.
