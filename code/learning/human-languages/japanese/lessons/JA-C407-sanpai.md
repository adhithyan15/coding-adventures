## You'll want to know: さんぱい
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C407-SANPAI407-01]; assesses=[] -->

**さんぱい** — *sanpai* — "a visit to worship (at a shrine or temple)".

> **かぞくで てらに さんぱいに いきました。** — *kazoku de tera ni sanpai ni ikimashita* — "My family went to the temple to worship."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**さんぱい** (*sanpai*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *san*, "visit" (Middle Chinese *tshom*) and *hai*, "worship" (*peajH*), joined as *sanpai* (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
