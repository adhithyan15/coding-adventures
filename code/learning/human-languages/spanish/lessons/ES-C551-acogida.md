## You'll want to know: la acogida
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C551-TRASLADO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**la acogida** — "the welcome, the reception".

> La acogida en el pueblo fue muy buena.
>
> *The welcome in the village was very good.*

*Un centro de acogida* is a shelter for refugees or homeless people.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*acogida* is built on *acoger* 'to take in, to welcome' (EtymDB), which is inherited from spoken Latin *\*accolligere*, built on *colligere* 'to gather together'.
