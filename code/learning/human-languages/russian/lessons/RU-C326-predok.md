## You'll want to know: предок
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C326-PREDOK-01]; assesses=[] -->

**предок** (*prédok*) — "an ancestor".

> Наши предки жили в этой деревне.
>
> *Náshi prédki zhíli v étoy derévne.*
>
> Our ancestors lived in this village.

Genitive *предка*. In slang *предки* are one's parents.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*предок* is a Church Slavonic word, *prědŭkŭ* (EtymDB), built on *пред-* "before", the church shape of native *перед*: the one who went before.
