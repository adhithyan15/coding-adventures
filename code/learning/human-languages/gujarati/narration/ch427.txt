Gujarati, chapter 427: Savings and Deposits.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
પેન્શન (penśan) — a pension.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to multiply, then the Gujarati for to increase.

You'll want to know: પેન્શન.
પેન્શન — penśan — "a pension".
નિવૃત્ત થયા પછી દાદાને પેન્શન મળે છે. nivṛtt thayā pachī dādāne penśan maḷe che. Since he retired, grandfather has received a pension.
A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
પેન્શન (penśan) is borrowed from English pension.

Lesson 2 of 5.
રોકાણ (rokāṇ) — an investment.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to increase, then the Gujarati for a pension.

You'll want to know: રોકાણ.
રોકાણ — rokāṇ — "an investment".
તેણે બચતનું રોકાણ બેંકમાં કર્યું. teṇe bacatnũ rokāṇ beṅkmā̃ karyũ. She invested her savings in the bank.
A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
રોકાણ (rokāṇ) is built on રોકવું, to stop, to hold back, which goes back to rōkk, a form CDIAL reconstructs (CDIAL 10827): money held back and put to work.

Lesson 3 of 5.
થાપણ (thāpaṇ) — a deposit (of money); something held in trust.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a pension, then the Gujarati for an investment.

You'll want to know: થાપણ.
થાપણ — thāpaṇ — "a deposit (of money); something held in trust".
મારી થાપણ પર બેંક વ્યાજ આપે છે. mārī thāpaṇ par beṅk vyāj āpe che. The bank pays interest on my deposit.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
થાપણ (thāpaṇ) seems to go back to Sanskrit sthāpana, "setting down, placing" (Monier-Williams): money set down with someone to keep.

આ થાપણની મુદત પાંચ વરસની છે. ā thāpaṇnī mudat pā̃c varasnī che. The term of this deposit is five years.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
મુદત (mudat) is usually traced to Arabic mudda, "a space of time", which came through Persian.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ફાળવવું (phāḷavvũ) — to set aside (money); to allot.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a deposit, then the Gujarati for a fixed term.

You'll want to know: ફાળવવું.
ફાળવવું — phāḷavvũ — "to set aside (money); to allot".
મેં પગારમાંથી બચત માટે પૈસા ફાળવ્યા. mẽ pagārmā̃thī bacat māṭe paisā phāḷavyā. I set aside money from my salary for savings.
Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે પૈસા ફાળવ્યા, teṇe paisā phāḷavyā.

The word, taken apart.
ફાળવવું (phāḷavvũ) is built on ફાળો, a share, which goes back to phāla, a form CDIAL reconstructs and lists for the Gujarati word (CDIAL 9073): to give each thing its share.
