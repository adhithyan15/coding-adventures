## You'll want to know: чаща
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C461-POLYANA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**чаща** (*cháshcha*) — "a thicket, the depths of a forest".

> Медведь ушёл в чащу.
>
> *Medvéd ushyól v cháshchu.*
>
> The bear went off into the thicket.

Feminine. *В лесной чаще* — deep in the forest.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*чаща* is inherited from Proto-Slavic *čęsťa* (EtymDB), built on the root of *частый* 'dense, frequent': where the trees stand thick.
