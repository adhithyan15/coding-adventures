# દ — one continuous shape, da

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-SCRIPT-RA-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Without looking back, write **ર** once. Check it, then set it aside.

## Script — meet the shape
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-SCRIPT-DA-01]; assesses=[] -->

Keep **દ** visible. It is **da**. Begin at the upper right, circle the rounded
upper body into the narrow middle turn, then continue without lifting around
the broad lower body and rise into the compact right terminal.

## Writing — notice and trace
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-SCRIPT-DA-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: observe-trace -->

Finger-trace **દ** once, then air-trace it once. One continuous run; no lift.

## Writing — copy with the model
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-SCRIPT-DA-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: guided-copy -->

Copy visible **દ** once. Check upper round, narrow turn, broad lower round. Repair
only the landmark that differs.

## Writing — recall after hiding
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-SCRIPT-DA-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: delayed-copy -->
<!-- hl-activity: {"id":"GU-W02-da-recall","kind":"text","assesses":["GU-SCRIPT-DA-01"],"prompt":"Hide the model. Write the Gujarati consonant da from memory.","answer":"દ","accepted":[],"feedback":{"correct":"દ — one continuous upper-turn-lower path.","incorrect":"Look once at દ, hide it, and repair only the differing turn."},"response_seconds":20} -->

Hide the model and write *da* once. Uncover **દ**, compare, and stop after one repair.

## Wrap-up Recall
