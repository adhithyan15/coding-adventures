## Script — a three-stroke frame, then 口
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-SCRIPT-HOU-AFTER-01]; assesses=[] -->

> 后

**Two pieces, and the second you already write.**

**The frame, three strokes**: a short left-falling stroke along the top, from right to left; a long left-falling stroke down the left side, curving out to the lower left; then a horizontal across the middle, from the long stroke to the right.

**口, three strokes**, under that horizontal, on the right: the left side, the top that turns down the right side, the bottom bar.

3 + 3 = six.
