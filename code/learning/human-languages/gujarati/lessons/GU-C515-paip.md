## You'll want to know: પાઈપ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C515-POPDO515-04]; assesses=[] -->

**પાઈપ** — *pāīp* — "a pipe".

> રસોડાની પાઈપમાંથી પાણી ટપકે છે.
>
> *rasoḍānī pāīpmā̃thī pāṇī ṭapke che.*
>
> Water is dripping from the kitchen pipe.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**પાઈપ** (*pāīp*) is borrowed from English *pipe*.

## What you've built
