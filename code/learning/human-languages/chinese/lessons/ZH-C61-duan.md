## You'll want to know: duǎn
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C61-LONG61-02]; assesses=[] -->

*duǎn* — "short in length; brief".

> *wǒ de kùzi hěn duǎn* — My trousers are short.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*duǎn* is inherited from Middle Chinese *twanX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'hurried' and 'not long'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for everyday answers.
