Lesson 3 of 5.
آگے (āge) — ahead, forward.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a garage, then the Urdu for everywhere.

You'll want to know: آگے.
آگے — āge — "ahead, forward".

The word, taken apart.
آگے (āge) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from agra, "top, front" (CDIAL 68). āge continues the Sanskrit forms agre, agreṇa, "in front".

Lesson 4 of 5.
سرحد (sarhad) — a border.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for everywhere, then the Urdu for ahead.

You'll want to know: سرحد.
سرحد — sarhad — "a border".

The word, taken apart.
سرحد (sarhad) is "the head of the limit". It joins Persian sar, "head, end", and Arabic ḥadd, "a limit, a boundary" (Lane, root ḥ-d-d).

The word, taken apart.
حکومت (hukūmat) is a loan from Arabic ḥukūma, "rule, government", which reached Urdu through Persian. Its Arabic root is ḥ-k-m (Lane).
