## You'll want to know: तथापि
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C351-JODNE351-04]; assesses=[] -->

**तथापि** — *tathāpi* — "nevertheless".

> पाऊस पडत होता, तथापि कार्यक्रम वेळेवर सुरू झाला.
>
> *pāūs paḍat hotā, tathāpi kāryakram veḷevar surū jhālā.*
>
> It was raining; nevertheless, the programme began on time.

Formal and written; in speech पण or तरीही.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**तथापि** (*tathāpi*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *tathāpi*, "even so, nevertheless" (CDIAL 5647), from *tathā*, "so", and *api*, "even".
