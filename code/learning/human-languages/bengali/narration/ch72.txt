Bengali, chapter 72: Big, Small, New, Old, Bad.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
বড় (bôṛo) — big.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for an ant, then the Bengali for a hen.

You'll want to know: বড়.
বড় — bôṛo — "big".

The word, taken apart.
বড় (bôṛo) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from vaḍra, "big" (CDIAL 11225). CDIAL rejects, as unlikely, a derivation from vṛddha, "grown".

Lesson 5 of 5.
খারাপ (khārāp) — bad.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for new, then the Bengali for old.

You'll want to know: খারাপ.
খারাপ — khārāp — "bad".

The word, taken apart.
খারাপ (khārāp) is a loan from Arabic kharāb, "ruin, desolation", which reached Bengali through Persian. Its Arabic root is kh-r-b (Lane). In Bengali it means "bad".
