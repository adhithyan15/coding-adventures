# antipático — the opposite made by fastening four letters on the front

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-C380-COUNT-36] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say *simpático*. Now say what you would expect its opposite to look
like, before reading on. This is one of the times the obvious guess is right.

## You'll want to know first
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

- [simpático](./ES-C380-simpatico.md) — likeable, and a false friend: it does
  not mean *sympathetic*.

## Sounds you'll need
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

- `accent-acute` — *an-ti-PÁ-ti-co*: the written accent sits where the stress
  does, and adding *anti-* does not move it. →
  [reference](../pronunciation-reference.md)
- `t-dental` — both **t** sounds are made with the tongue on the teeth, with no
  puff of air after them.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C423-CHAR-01]; assesses=[] -->

**antipático** — "unpleasant, disagreeable." Take *simpático* and put Greek
**anti-**, "against, opposite," on the front.

The two Greek prefixes underneath are a pair, and seeing them together makes
both words transparent:

| prefix | Greek meaning | with *-pático* |
|---|---|---|
| sym- | together with | feeling **with** you |
| anti- | against | feeling **against** you |

The *-patí-* in the middle is *páthos*, "feeling, suffering" — the same piece
inside English **sympathy**, **empathy** and **pathetic**.

So *simpático* and *antipático* are literally *feeling-with* and
*feeling-against*. Spanish uses them for people who are pleasant and unpleasant
to be around, which is a fair everyday translation of the same idea.

*Anti-* is doing exactly the job you already know it does in English:
**anti**biotic, **anti**septic, **Anti**arctic — against life, against rot,
opposite the Bear.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-C423-CHAR-01, ES-LEX-C380-COUNT-36] -->

Both words end in **-o** and both change ending to match the person:

| | masculine | feminine |
|---|---|---|
| pleasant | simpátic**o** | simpátic**a** |
| unpleasant | antipátic**o** | antipátic**a** |

The prefix never moves and never agrees. Only the last letter does the work.
