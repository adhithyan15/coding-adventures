Arabic, chapter 483: Cold.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
برودة (burūda) — coldness; a chill.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to grow stronger, then the Arabic for danger.

You'll want to know: برودة.
برودة — burūda — "coldness; a chill".
شعرت ببرودة في الغرفة. shaʿartu bi-burūda fī al-ghurfa. I felt a chill in the room.

The word, taken apart.
برودة (burūda) is built on the native Arabic root b-r-d, "to be cold" (Lane). It is a verbal noun.

Lesson 3 of 5.
قارس (qāris) — biting (of cold); bitter.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for coldness, then the Arabic for chilly.

You'll want to know: قارس.
قارس — qāris — "biting (of cold); bitter".
كان الشتاء قارسا في الجبل. kāna ash-shitāʾ qārisan fī al-jabal. The winter was bitterly cold in the mountains.
It goes with cold: bard qāris, a biting cold.

The word, taken apart.
قارس (qāris) is built on the native Arabic root q-r-s, "to be intense (of cold); to freeze" (Lane). It is the active participle.

Lesson 5 of 5.
برد (barada) — to get cold; to cool.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for biting, then the Arabic for frost.

You'll want to know: برد.
برد — barada — "to get cold; to cool".
برد الشاي قبل أن أشربه. barada ash-shāy qabla an ashrabahu. The tea got cold before I drank it.
Present: يبرد, yabrud.
The noun bard is cold, and barad is hail.

The word, taken apart.
برد (barada) is built on the native Arabic root b-r-d, "to be cold" (Lane). Lane gives barada and baruda, with the present yabrudu.
