## You'll want to know: гол
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C509-FOOTBALL-04]; assesses=[] -->

**гол** (*gol*) — "a goal".

> В первом тайме не было ни одного гола.
>
> *V pérvom táyme né bylo ni odnogó góla.*
>
> There wasn't a single goal in the first half.

Masculine. *Забить гол* — to score a goal.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*гол* was borrowed from English *goal* (EtymDB).
