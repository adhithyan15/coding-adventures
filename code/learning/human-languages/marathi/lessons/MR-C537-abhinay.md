## You'll want to know: अभिनय
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C537-NATYA537-01]; assesses=[] -->

**अभिनय** — *abhinay* — "acting".

> त्या अभिनेत्रीचा अभिनय सगळ्यांना आवडला.
>
> *tyā abhinetrīcā abhinay sagḷyānnā āvaḍlā.*
>
> Everyone liked that actress's acting.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अभिनय** (*abhinay*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *abhinaya*, "expressing a feeling on stage, acting" (Monier-Williams).
