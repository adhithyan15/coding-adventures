## You'll want to know: padecer
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C738-CONSULTA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**padecer** — "to suffer from".

> Padece una enfermedad del corazón.
>
> *She suffers from a heart condition.*

*Padecer de* + illness. More formal than *sufrir*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*padecer* is inherited from a spoken Latin *patīscere* (EtymDB), from *patī* "to suffer".
