## You'll want to know: لگ بھگ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C376-MIQDAR376-04]; assesses=[] -->

**لگ بھگ** — *lag-bhag* — "about; approximately".

> اگر ابھی چلے تو لگ بھگ دو گھنٹے لگیں گے۔
>
> *agar abhī chale to lag-bhag do ghanṭe lageṅ ge.*
>
> If we leave now, it will take about two hours.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**لگ بھگ** (*lag-bhag*) is built on *lag*, "near, up to", which is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *lag* under Sanskrit *lagna*, "attached" (CDIAL 10893); *bhag* is a rhyming partner with no meaning of its own, as far as our sources show.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
