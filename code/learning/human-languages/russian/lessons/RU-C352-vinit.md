## You'll want to know: винить
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C352-VINA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**винить** (*vinít*) — "to blame".

> Она винит себя в этой аварии.
>
> *Oná vinít sebyá v étoy avárii.*
>
> She blames herself for this accident.

Imperfective. *Винить кого в чём*: the fault goes after *в* + prepositional.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**винить** is the verb made from *вина* "guilt", which is usually traced to Proto-Slavic *\*vina*: to put the guilt on someone.
