## You'll want to know: ਯਾਦ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C232-DIN232-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ਯਾਦ** — *yād* — "memory; remembering".

> ਮੈਨੂੰ ਤੁਹਾਡਾ ਨਾਂ ਯਾਦ ਹੈ।
>
> *mainū̃ tuhāḍā nāṁ yād hai.*
>
> I remember your name.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਯਾਦ** (*yād*) is borrowed: it is usually traced to Persian *yād*, "memory, remembrance".
