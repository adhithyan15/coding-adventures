## You'll want to know: પરચૂરણ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C186-TILL186-03]; assesses=[] -->

**પરચૂરણ** — *parcūraṇ* — "small change; odds and ends".

> **દુકાનદાર પાસે પરચૂરણ નથી.** — *dukāndār pāse parcūraṇ nathī* — "The shopkeeper has no small change."

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

The origin of **પરચૂરણ** (*parcūraṇ*) is not settled. CDIAL has no entry for it, and EtymDB lists no source.
