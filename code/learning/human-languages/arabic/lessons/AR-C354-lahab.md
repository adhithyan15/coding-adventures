## You'll want to know: لهب
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C354-HUTAM354-04]; assesses=[] -->

**لهب** — *lahab* — "a flame, flames".

> رأينا لهب النار من بعيد.
>
> *raʾaynā lahab an-nār min baʿīd.*
>
> We saw the flames of the fire from far away.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**لهب** (*lahab*) is built on the native Arabic root *l-h-b* (Lane), "to blaze up"; *lahab* is the tongue of the fire.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
