## You'll want to know: اقرار
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C303-INKAR303-02]; assesses=[] -->

**اقرار** — *iqrār* — "an admission; a confession".

> آخر اس نے چوری کا اقرار کیا۔
>
> *ākhir us ne chorī kā iqrār kiyā.*
>
> In the end he admitted the theft.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**اقرار** (*iqrār*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *iqrār*, "an acknowledging". It is the opposite of an **انکار**.
