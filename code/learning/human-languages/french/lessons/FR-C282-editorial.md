## You'll want to know: l'éditorial
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C282-TITRES-04]; assesses=[] -->

**l'éditorial** — "the editorial, the leading article".

> Le directeur du journal écrit l'éditorial.
>
> *The editor of the paper writes the editorial.*

Plural *les éditoriaux*. Its writer is *l'éditorialiste*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*éditorial* is usually traced to English *editorial* (EtymDB lists no parent), built on *editor*, which comes from Latin *ēditor* "one who brings out", from *ēdere* "to give out" (Lewis & Short).
