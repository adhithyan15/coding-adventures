## You'll want to know: സന്ദർഭം
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C361-YUGAM361-03]; assesses=[] -->

**സന്ദർഭം** — *sandarbhaṁ* — "an occasion; a context".

> ആ സന്ദർഭത്തിൽ ഞാൻ ഒന്നും പറഞ്ഞില്ല.
>
> *ā sandarbhattil ñān onnuṁ paṟaññilla.*
>
> On that occasion I said nothing.

Also the context of a word or a sentence.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**സന്ദർഭം** (*sandarbhaṁ*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *saṁdarbha*, "a stringing or weaving together; a composition" (Monier-Williams). An occasion is how things are strung together at one time.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
