## You'll want to know: শব্দজব্দ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C518-GHOROA518-04]; assesses=[] -->

**শব্দজব্দ** — *shôbdôjôbdô* — "a crossword".

> বাবা রোজ সকালে পত্রিকার শব্দজব্দ করেন।
>
> *Bābā roj sôkāle pôtrikār shôbdôjôbdô kôren.*
>
> My father does the magazine's crossword every morning.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**শব্দজব্দ** (*shôbdôjôbdô*) is a playful modern compound: শব্দ, "a word", and জব্দ, "caught out, beaten", which is usually traced to Arabic *ḍabṭ*, "seizing": the words that catch you out.
