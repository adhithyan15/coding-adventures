# అభిమానం (abhimānaṁ) — fondness; regard

## You'll want to know: అభిమానం
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C503-PREMA503-05]; assesses=[] -->

**అభిమానం** — *abhimānaṁ* — "fondness; regard".

> ఆ నటుడంటే నాకు చాలా అభిమానం.
>
> *ā naṭuḍaṇṭē nāku cālā abhimānaṁ.*
>
> I am very fond of that actor.

Also self-respect or pride, depending on the sentence.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**అభిమానం** (*abhimānaṁ*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *abhimāna* (CDIAL 524), which meant pride and self-regard; in Telugu it is also the warm regard you feel for a person.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
