## You'll want to know: دھیان
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C370-JALDBAZI370-02]; assesses=[] -->

**دھیان** — *dhyān* — "attention; care".

> دھیان سے چلیے، ورنہ آپ گر جائیں گے۔
>
> *dhyān se chaliye, varnā āp gir jāeṅ ge.*
>
> Walk carefully, or you will fall.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**دھیان** (*dhyān*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *dhyāna*, "meditation, thought" (CDIAL 6889); in Urdu, the mind kept on what you are doing.
