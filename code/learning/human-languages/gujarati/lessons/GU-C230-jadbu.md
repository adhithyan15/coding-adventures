# જડબું (jaḍbũ) — jaw

## You'll want to know: જડબું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C230-TOOTH230-02]; assesses=[] -->

**જડબું** — *jaḍbũ* — "jaw".

> **શું તમારું જડબું દુખે છે?** — *śũ tamārũ jaḍbũ dukhe che?* — "Does your jaw hurt?"

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

The origin of **જડબું** (*jaḍbũ*) is not settled: CDIAL lists no Gujarati form for it, and none of its entries for the jaw holds the word.
