## You'll want to know: مستنقع
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C473-WAHA473-04]; assesses=[] -->

**مستنقع** — *mustanqaʿ* — "a swamp; a marsh".

> يعيش الضفدع في المستنقع.
>
> *yaʿīshu aḍ-ḍifdaʿ fī al-mustanqaʿ.*
>
> The frog lives in the swamp.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**مستنقع** (*mustanqaʿ*) is built on the native Arabic root *n-q-ʿ*, "standing water" (Lane). It is the place-noun of Form X: where water stands.
