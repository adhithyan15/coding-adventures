## You'll want to know: wéifǎn
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C359-RULES359-03]; assesses=[] -->

*wéifǎn* — "to break a rule; to go against the law".

> *tā wéifǎn le xuéxiào de guīdìng* — He broke the school rules.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*wéifǎn* is built from *wéi* and *fǎn*, both inherited from Middle Chinese, *hjw+j* and *pjonX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to turn one's back on' and the second 'to turn over'.
