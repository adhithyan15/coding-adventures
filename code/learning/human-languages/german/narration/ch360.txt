German, chapter 360: Vorurteile.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
das Vorurteil — the prejudice.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for wealth, then the German for to put at a disadvantage.

You'll want to know: das Vorurteil.
das Vorurteil — "the prejudice".
Er hat viele Vorurteile gegen Menschen aus der Stadt. He has a lot of prejudices against people from the city.
Plural: die Vorurteile. Literally a judgement made beforehand.

The word, taken apart.
Vorurteil joins vor "before" to Urteil "judgement", from Old High German urteil (EtymDB). It is usually explained as a rendering of Latin praeiūdicium "a judgement beforehand" (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 2 of 5.
die Toleranz — tolerance.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to put at a disadvantage, then the German for the prejudice.

You'll want to know: die Toleranz.
die Toleranz — "tolerance".
In einer guten Schule lernen die Kinder Toleranz. In a good school the children learn tolerance.
No plural in this sense. The adjective is tolerant.

The word, taken apart.
Toleranz is a learned borrowing from Latin tolerantia "a bearing, endurance" (Lewis & Short), from tolerāre "to bear, to put up with".

Lesson 3 of 5.
die Minderheit — the minority.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for the prejudice, then the German for tolerance.

You'll want to know: die Minderheit.
die Minderheit — "the minority".
In diesem Land gibt es mehrere sprachliche Minderheiten. There are several language minorities in this country.
Plural: die Minderheiten. The opposite is die Mehrheit.

The word, taken apart.
Minderheit is built from minder "less", inherited from Middle High German minder, Old High German minniro and Proto-Germanic minnizô (EtymDB), a cousin of Latin minor, with -heit.

Lesson 4 of 5.
diskriminieren — to discriminate against.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for tolerance, then the German for the minority.

You'll want to know: diskriminieren.
diskriminieren — "to discriminate against".
Die Firma darf niemanden wegen seiner Herkunft diskriminieren. The firm may not discriminate against anyone because of their origin.
Perfekt: hat diskriminiert. It takes a direct object: jemanden diskriminieren.

The word, taken apart.
diskriminieren is a learned borrowing from Latin discrīmināre "to divide, to separate", from discrīmen "that which divides" (Lewis & Short): you set people apart.

Lesson 5 of 5.
die Gleichberechtigung — equal rights, equality.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for the minority, then the German for to discriminate against.

You'll want to know: die Gleichberechtigung.
die Gleichberechtigung — "equal rights, equality".
Die Gleichberechtigung von Männern und Frauen ist noch nicht überall Realität. Equal rights for men and women are not yet a reality everywhere.
No plural. The adjective is gleichberechtigt — with equal rights.

The word, taken apart.
Gleichberechtigung joins gleich "equal", Proto-Germanic galīkaz (EtymDB), to Berechtigung "entitlement", from Recht "right", Proto-Germanic rehtaz (EtymDB).
