# సంతోషపెట్టు (santōṣapeṭṭu) — to make happy; to please

## Warm-up

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Telugu for to thrill, then the Telugu for cheerfulness.

## You'll want to know: సంతోషపెట్టు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C501-NAVVU501-01]; assesses=[] -->

**సంతోషపెట్టు** — *santōṣapeṭṭu* — "to make happy; to please".

> పుట్టినరోజున అన్నయ్య అమ్మను సంతోషపెట్టాడు.
>
> *puṭṭinarōjuna annayya ammanu santōṣapeṭṭāḍu.*
>
> On her birthday my brother made mum happy.

Past (I …): సంతోషపెట్టాను, *santōṣapeṭṭānu*.

సంతోషం, happiness, + పెట్టు, to put: to make someone happy.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**సంతోషపెట్టు** (*santōṣapeṭṭu*) joins సంతోషం, from Sanskrit *saṁtōṣa*, "satisfaction" (CDIAL 12895), to the native verb *peṭṭu*, "to put".
