## You'll want to know: درہ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C397-FASLA397-03]; assesses=[] -->

**درہ** — *darrā* — "a mountain pass".

> سردیوں میں درہ برف سے بند ہوتا ہے۔
>
> *sardiyoṅ meṅ darrā barf se band hotā hai.*
>
> In winter the pass is closed by snow.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**درہ** (*darrā*) is usually traced to Persian *dara*, "a valley, a mountain pass".
