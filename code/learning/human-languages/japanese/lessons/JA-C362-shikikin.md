# しききん (shikikin) — a rental deposit

## You'll want to know: しききん
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C362-HIYOU362-05]; assesses=[] -->

**しききん** — *shikikin* — "a rental deposit".

> **へやを でたら、しききんが もどるでしょう。** — *heya o detara, shikikin ga modoru deshō* — "When you move out, you will probably get your deposit back."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**しききん** (*shikikin*) is built from the native *shiki*, "spreading" or laying down, a kun'yomi, and the Sino-Japanese *kin*, "gold" or money, an on'yomi borrowed from Chinese (Middle Chinese *kim*) (KANJIDIC2; JMdict; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
