## You'll want to know: die Erzählung
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C317-ERZAEHLUNG-01]; assesses=[] -->

**die Erzählung** — "the narrative, the short story".

> Die Erzählung spielt in einem kleinen Dorf.
>
> *The narrative is set in a small village.*

Plural: *die Erzählungen*. As a book, a short story.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Erzählung* is built on *erzählen* "to tell", from *zählen* "to count", Old High German *zellen* (EtymDB), with the ending *-ung*.
