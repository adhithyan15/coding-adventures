Marathi, chapter 404: Traffic and Detours.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
वळसा (vaḷsā) — a detour; a roundabout way.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to tip over, then the Marathi for to bang against.

You'll want to know: वळसा.
वळसा — vaḷsā — "a detour; a roundabout way".
पूल बंद होता म्हणून आम्ही लांबचा वळसा घेतला. pūl band hotā mhaṇūn āmhī lāmbcā vaḷsā ghetlā. The bridge was closed, so we took a long detour.
Masculine.
वळसा (vaḷsā) घेणे or वळसा (vaḷsā) घालणे, to go the long way round.

The word, taken apart.
वळसा (vaḷsā) is built on the stem of वळणे, "to turn"; how the noun was formed is not settled.

Lesson 2 of 5.
रहदारी (rahdārī) — road traffic; movement.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to bang against, then the Marathi for a detour.

You'll want to know: रहदारी.
रहदारी — rahdārī — "road traffic; movement".
सकाळी या चौकात खूप रहदारी असते. sakāḷī yā caukāt khūp rahdārī aste. In the morning there is a lot of traffic at this square.
Feminine.
A feminine noun; वाहतूक is the same idea in a more official word.

The word, taken apart.
रहदारी (rahdārī) is a loan usually traced to Persian rāhdārī, built on rāh, "road": the coming and going on a road.

Lesson 4 of 5.
खेटणे (kheṭṇe) — to brush against.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for road traffic, then the Marathi for a traffic signal.

You'll want to know: खेटणे.
खेटणे — kheṭṇe — "to brush against".
गर्दीत एक माणूस मला खेटून गेला. gardīt ek māṇūs malā kheṭūn gelā. In the crowd a man brushed past me.
Past (he …): तो खेटला, to kheṭlā.
Often in the -ऊन form: खेटून उभं राहणे, to stand pressed close.

The word, taken apart.
खेटणे (kheṭṇe) is a Marathi verb for touching someone or something as you pass very close; its origin is not settled.

Lesson 5 of 5.
दाटणे (dāṭṇe) — to crowd thickly.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a traffic signal, then the Marathi for to brush against.

You'll want to know: दाटणे.
दाटणे — dāṭṇe — "to crowd thickly".
संध्याकाळी रस्त्यावर गर्दी दाटली. sandhyākāḷī rastyāvar gardī dāṭlī. In the evening the crowd thickened on the road.
Past (he …): तो दाटला, to dāṭlā.
Clouds, crowds and feelings all दाटतात: ढग दाटले, the clouds gathered.

The word, taken apart.
दाटणे (dāṭṇe) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi dāṭṇẽ, "to be pressed in, become thick", under draṭ (CDIAL 6618).
