Punjabi, chapter 250: Pots and Pans.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ਹਾਂਡੀ (hāṇḍī) — a clay cooking pot.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for charcoal, then the Punjabi for an ember.

You'll want to know: ਹਾਂਡੀ.
ਹਾਂਡੀ — hāṇḍī — "a clay cooking pot".
ਹਾਂਡੀ ਚੁੱਲ੍ਹੇ ਉੱਤੇ ਹੈ। hāṇḍī chullhe utte hai. The clay pot is on the stove.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ਹਾਂਡੀ (hāṇḍī) is inherited from a reconstructed hāṇḍa, "pot"; CDIAL lists Punjabi hā̃ḍī, "cooking pot" (CDIAL 14050).

Before the new one: say the Punjabi for an ember, then the Punjabi for a clay cooking pot.

ਕੜਾਹਾ — kaṛāhā — "a large pan".
ਕੜਾਹੇ ਵਿੱਚ ਬਹੁਤ ਹਲਵਾ ਹੈ। kaṛāhe vich bahut halvā hai. There is a lot of halwa in the big pan.

The word, taken apart.
ਕੜਾਹਾ (kaṛāhā) is inherited from Sanskrit kaṭāha, "boiler, saucepan with handles"; CDIAL lists Punjabi kaṛāhā, "shallow pan for boiling sugarcane juice" (CDIAL 2638).

Lesson 3 of 5.
ਗੜਵਾ (gaṛvā) — a metal jug.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for a clay cooking pot, then the Punjabi for a large pan.

You'll want to know: ਗੜਵਾ.
ਗੜਵਾ — gaṛvā — "a metal jug".
ਗੜਵੇ ਵਿੱਚ ਲੱਸੀ ਹੈ। gaṛve vich lassī hai. There is lassi in the jug.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ਗੜਵਾ (gaṛvā) is inherited from Sanskrit gaḍḍuka, "waterpot"; CDIAL lists Punjabi gaṛvā, "brass jug" (CDIAL 3984). CDIAL records the view that the Sanskrit word may be from a Munda language.
