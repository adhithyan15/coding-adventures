## You'll want to know: rimborsare
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C420-RITARDO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**rimborsare** — "to refund, to pay back".

> Ci hanno rimborsato metà del prezzo.
>
> *They refunded us half the price.*

*Farsi rimborsare* — to get a refund.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*rimborsare* is built from *ri-* 'back' and *borsa* 'purse', which is inherited from Late Latin *bursa*, from Greek *býrsa* 'hide, leather'.
