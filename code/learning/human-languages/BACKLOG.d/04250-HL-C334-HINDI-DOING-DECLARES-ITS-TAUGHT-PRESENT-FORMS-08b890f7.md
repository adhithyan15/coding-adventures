## HL-C334 — Hindi doing declares its taught present forms

After the thinking repair, **करता** is the highest-ranked independent modern
Hindi form in the glossed-but-never-taught queue. Chapter 5 already derives
**करता** and **करती** from **करना**, models the masculine form in a complete
sentence, and asks learners to produce both forms in guided practice.

The old metadata declared only **करना** and **काम करना**, leaving the
explicitly taught present forms invisible to reports and downstream cards.

Declare the full taught family as the lesson headword, print the Devanagari
sentence and practice forms, and regenerate the derived Hindi artifacts. This
is a metadata repair for existing teaching, not a new vocabulary claim.
