## You'll want to know: પ્રથા
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C357-MULYA357-04]; assesses=[] -->

**પ્રથા** — *prathā* — "a social practice; a custom".

> ગામમાં આ જૂની પ્રથા હજી ચાલે છે.
>
> *gāmmā̃ ā jūnī prathā hajī cāle che.*
>
> This old practice still goes on in the village.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**પ્રથા** (*prathā*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *prathā*, "spreading out, extending" (Monier-Williams); Gujarati uses it for a custom that has spread and stayed.
