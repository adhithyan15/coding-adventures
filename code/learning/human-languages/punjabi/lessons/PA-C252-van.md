## You'll want to know: ਵਾਣ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C252-GHR252-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ਵਾਣ** — *vāṇ* — "cot rope; coarse rope for weaving a cot".

> ਮੰਜੇ ਦਾ ਵਾਣ ਪੁਰਾਣਾ ਹੈ।
>
> *manje dā vāṇ purāṇā hai.*
>
> The rope of the cot is old.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਵਾਣ** (*vāṇ*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*vāṇa*, "a sort of rush, twisted grass rope"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *vāṇ*, "rush for rope-making, coarse twine" (CDIAL 11483).
