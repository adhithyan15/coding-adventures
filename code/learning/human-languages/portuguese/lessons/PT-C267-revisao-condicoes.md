# Review — plans with a branch in them

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PT-LEX-DEPENDE-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Someone asks *Vais à festa amanhã?* Give the answer that decides
nothing. (*Depende.*)

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PT-GRAMMAR-SE-01, PT-GRAMMAR-SE-PRESENTE-01, PT-GRAMMAR-DEPENDE-DE-02, PT-GRAMMAR-DUAS-SETAS-01, PT-LEX-IDA-E-VOLTA-01, PT-GRAMMAR-QUERIA-UM-BILHETE-02] -->

Three ways now to talk about a cause, and they differ in how sure you are.

> *Chove, **por isso** não saio.* — It is raining: decided.
> ***Se** chove, não saio.* — It might: the branch is ready.
> ***Depende.*** — Not even the branch is named yet.

Put them together at a ticket window and you have a whole conversation:

> — *Queria um bilhete para Lisboa.* — *Ida e volta?*
> — *Depende. Se volto hoje, ida e volta. Se fico, só de ida.*

*Fico* is "I stay", from the *ficar* that tells where places are: a place
stays where it is.

## Wrap-up Recall
