## You'll want to know: familiāritās, familiāritātis
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C533-FAMI-02]; assesses=[] -->

**familiāritās, familiāritātis** — "close friendship, intimacy".

> Inter eōs magna familiāritās est.
>
> *There is a close friendship between them.*

Feminine, third declension. English *familiarity*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**familiāritās** is built on *familiāris* "intimate, of the household" (Lewis & Short). English *familiarity* comes from it.
