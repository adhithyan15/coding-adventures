## You'll want to know: sháozi
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C170-POT170-03]; assesses=[] -->

*sháozi* — "spoon; ladle".

> *wǒ yào yí ge sháozi* — Could I have a spoon?

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*sháozi* is built from *sháo* 'ladle' and the noun ending 子, both inherited from Middle Chinese, *dzyak* and *tsiX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first as a drinking vessel. In the word the second loses its tone.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for everyday things.
