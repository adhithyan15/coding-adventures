Spanish, chapter 716: Malas noticias.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
la lástima — pity, shame.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for to kiss, then the Spanish for passion.

You'll want to know: la lástima.
la lástima — "pity, shame".
¡Qué lástima que no vengas! What a shame you are not coming!
¡Qué lástima! — What a pity! Qué lástima que takes the subjunctive.

The word, taken apart.
lástima is built on lastimar "to hurt", which comes from a spoken Latin blastemāre, Latin blasphēmāre "to insult", from Greek blasphēmía (EtymDB). The sense moved from insulting to hurting to pity.

Lesson 4 of 5.
decepcionado — disappointed.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for to console, then the Spanish for to regret.

You'll want to know: decepcionado.
decepcionado — "disappointed".
Estoy decepcionado con el hotel. I am disappointed with the hotel.
A false friend: it means disappointed, not deceived.

The word, taken apart.
decepcionado is built on decepción, a learned borrowing from Latin dēceptiō "deceit", from dēcipere "to trick"; the modern sense of disappointment probably came through French déception.
