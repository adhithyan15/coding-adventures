## You'll want to know: ركام
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C316-KARITHA316-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ركام** — *rukām* — "rubble, a heap".

> بحث الناس تحت الركام عن الأطفال.
>
> *baḥatha an-nās taḥta ar-rukām ʿan al-aṭfāl.*
>
> People searched under the rubble for the children.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ركام** (*rukām*) is built on the native Arabic root *r-k-m* (Lane), "to pile one thing on another". *Rukām* is a heap, of clouds or of stones.
