# ਦੱਖਣ (dakkhaṇ) — the south

## You'll want to know: ਦੱਖਣ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C271-COMPASS271-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ਦੱਖਣ** — *dakkhaṇ* — "the south".

> ਸਮੁੰਦਰ ਦੱਖਣ ਵਿੱਚ ਹੈ।
>
> *samundar dakkhaṇ vich hai.*
>
> The sea is in the south.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਦੱਖਣ** (*dakkhaṇ*) is inherited from Sanskrit *dakṣiṇa*, "right (hand), southern"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *dakkhaṇ*, "the south", under it (CDIAL 6119).
