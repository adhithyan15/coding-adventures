## You'll want to know: खीजणो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C270-MAPHI270-05]; assesses=[] -->

**खीजणो** — *khījṇo* — "to get cross".

> मत खीजो सा।
>
> *mat khījo sā.*
>
> Please don't be cross.

Past (he …): वो खीज्यो, *vo khījyo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**खीजणो** (*khījṇo*) is inherited from Sanskrit *kṣīyate*, "is worn away, is injured"; CDIAL lists Old Marwari *khijaï*, "to be irritated", and Gujarati *khījvũ*, "to take offence" (CDIAL 3695).
