## Chapter 155 joins six taught Punjabi A1 form fields

Six sessions of at most four minutes join name, language, residence, work,
age, and phone without adding a new value. Learners first rehearse the six
labels and old cue maps, then make supported and delayed entries before
repairing selection, spelling, spacing, digit order, and placement in
separate passes. The final six-line form is untimed and has no value bank,
romanization, or copyable answer. Date remains an independently practised
seventh field; this slice does not claim a timed paper or A1 attainment.
