## You'll want to know: यदृच्छया
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C536-ADRSTA536-04]; assesses=[] -->

**यदृच्छया** — *yadṛcchayā* — "by chance".

> यदि यदृच्छया मार्गे मित्रं पश्यामि, तर्हि मोदे।
>
> *yadi yadṛcchayā mārge mitraṃ paśyāmi, tarhi mode.*
>
> If I happen to meet a friend on the road, I am glad.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**यदृच्छया** (*yadṛcchayā*) is built from *yad*, "what, whatever", and *ṛcchā*, as MW gives it; MW records *yadṛcchā* as self-will, accident, chance, and *yadṛcchayā* as by accident (MW).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
