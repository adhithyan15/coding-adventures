## You'll want to know: افتخار کردن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C480-PRIDE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**افتخار کردن** (*eftekhâr kardan*) — "to be proud (of)".

> مادرم به موفقیت من افتخار میکند.
>
> *mâdaram be movaffaqiyat-e man eftekhâr mikonad.*
>
> My mother is proud of my success.

*eftekhâr* 'pride, honour' + *kardan* 'to do'; present stem *kon-*. What you are proud of takes *be*: *be chizi eftekhâr kardan*. Unlike *ghorur*, a good kind of pride.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*eftekhâr* was borrowed from Arabic *iftiḫār*; EtymDB links it with Arabic *faḫr*, 'honour, pride' (EtymDB). *kardan*, 'to do', is inherited Persian.
