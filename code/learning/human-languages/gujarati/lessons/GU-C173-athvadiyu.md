## You'll want to know: અઠવાડિયું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C173-WEEK173-01]; assesses=[] -->

**અઠવાડિયું** — *aṭhvāḍiyũ* — "week".

> **હું એક અઠવાડિયું ગામમાં રહીશ.** — *hũ ek aṭhvāḍiyũ gāmmā̃ rahīś* — "I will stay in the village for a week."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**અઠવાડિયું** (*aṭhvāḍiyũ*) is a tadbhava. It comes from *\*aṣṭavāra*, "period of eight days", a reconstructed form built from Sanskrit *aṣṭā*, "eight", and *vāra*, "day"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati *aṭhvāḍiyũ* and takes it to be crossed with compounds of *vāṭa*, "enclosure" (CDIAL 940).
