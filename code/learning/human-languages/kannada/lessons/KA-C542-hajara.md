## You'll want to know: ಹಜಾರ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C542-MANE542-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ಹಜಾರ** — *hajāra* — "a hall; a central room".

> ಮನೆಯ ಹಜಾರದಲ್ಲಿ ಎಲ್ಲರೂ ಕುಳಿತು ಮಾತಾಡಿದರು.
>
> *maneya hajāradalli ellarū kuḷitu mātāḍidaru.*
>
> Everyone sat and talked in the hall of the house.

The open room inside the front door.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಹಜಾರ** (*hajāra*) is a Kannada word for the hall of a house; its origin is not settled.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
