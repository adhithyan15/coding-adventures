Urdu, chapter 343: Offices and Officials.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
محکمہ (mahkamā) — a government department.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a candidate, then the Urdu for to impose.

You'll want to know: محکمہ.
محکمہ — mahkamā — "a government department".
وہ پولیس کے محکمے میں کام کرتا ہے۔ voh polīs ke mahkame meṅ kām kartā hai. He works in the police department.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
محکمہ (mahkamā) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic maḥkama, "a court"; Urdu uses it for a department of government, a sense our sources do not trace.

Lesson 5 of 5.
سجھانا (sujhānā) — to suggest; to put (an idea) to someone.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a post in an office, then the Urdu for an instruction.

You'll want to know: سجھانا.
سجھانا — sujhānā — "to suggest; to put (an idea) to someone".
اس نے مجھے ایک اچھا راستہ سجھایا۔ us ne mujhe ek achchhā rāstā sujhāyā. She suggested a good way to me.
Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے راستہ سجھایا, us ne rāstā sujhāyā.

The word, taken apart.
سجھانا (sujhānā) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin sujhānā, "to explain", under Sanskrit śúdhyati, "becomes clear (of doubts)" (CDIAL 12527). To make an idea clear to someone.
