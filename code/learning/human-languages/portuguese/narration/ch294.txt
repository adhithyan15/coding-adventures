Portuguese, chapter 294: A investigação.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
a investigação — the investigation; research.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for to keep watch over, then the Portuguese for surveillance.

You'll want to know: a investigação.
a investigação — "the investigation; research".
A investigação durou dois anos. The investigation lasted two years.
At a university it means research.

The word, taken apart.
investigação is built on investigar, from Latin investīgāre "to track down" (EtymDB), from vestīgium "a footprint".

A woman is a inspetora. Before the 1990 spelling reform, inspector.

inspetor is a learned borrowing from Latin īnspector "one who looks into", from īnspicere "to look into" (Lewis & Short).
