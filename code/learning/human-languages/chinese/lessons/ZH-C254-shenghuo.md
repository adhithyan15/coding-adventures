## You'll want to know: shēnghuó
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C254-BORN254-05]; assesses=[] -->

*shēnghuó* — "life; daily life".

> *zhèr de shēnghuó hěn hǎo* — Life here is good.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*shēnghuó* is built from 生 'to grow' and *huó* 'alive', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *srjaeng* and *hwat* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'not dead'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for talking about the past.
