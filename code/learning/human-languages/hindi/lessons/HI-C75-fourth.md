## You'll want to know: चौथा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C75-ORDINAL-05]; assesses=[] -->

**चौथा** (*cauthā*) — "**fourth**".

**चौथा दिन** (*cauthā din*), **चौथी किताब** (*cauthī kitāb*).

**चार** is four, and *cauthā* comes from Sanskrit **चतुर्थ** (*caturtha*) — you can
hear the four, but the ending is **-था**, not the **-सरा** of the last two.

Count what has happened. Four ordinals, and four different shapes: *pahlā* from a
word that never meant one, *dūsrā* and *tīsrā* sharing a private ending, and now
*cauthā* with an ending of its own. Nothing so far has generalised.

That is the honest state of it, and it is also the good news: these four are the
whole of the memorising. The next lesson states the rule, and it holds for
everything after — with one hole, which gets a lesson of its own.
