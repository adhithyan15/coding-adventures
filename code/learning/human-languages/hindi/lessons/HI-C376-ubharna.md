## You'll want to know: उभरना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C376-ZAHIR376-05]; assesses=[] -->

**उभरना** — *ubharnā* — "to emerge, to come to the surface".

> अगर पानी कम होगा तो नदी के पत्थर उभरेंगे।
>
> *agar pānī kam hogā to nadī ke patthar ubhreṅge.*
>
> If the water goes down, the stones in the river will show.

Past (it …): वह सवाल फिर उभरा, *vah savāl phir ubhrā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**उभरना** (*ubharnā*) is a tadbhava, inherited from Sanskrit *udbharati*, "rises up" (CDIAL 2038).
