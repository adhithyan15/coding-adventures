## You'll want to know: اسلحه
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C318-JENAYAT-05]; assesses=[] -->

**اسلحه** (*aslahe*) — "a weapon, a gun".

> مرد اسلحه نداشت.
>
> *Mard aslahe nadâsht.*
>
> The man did not have a weapon.

An Arabic plural in origin, used as a singular in Persian. *Aslahe-ye sard* is a knife or blade.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*aslahe* is borrowed from Arabic *asliḥa* (EtymDB), the plural of *silāḥ* "weapon"; Persian treats it as a singular.
