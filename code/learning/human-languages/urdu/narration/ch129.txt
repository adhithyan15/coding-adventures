Urdu, chapter 129: Back, Forehead, Cheek, Chin, Wrist.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
پیٹھ (pīṭh) — the back.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a bear, then the Urdu for the waist.
