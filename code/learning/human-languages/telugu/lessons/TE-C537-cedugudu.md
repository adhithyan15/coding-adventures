# చెడుగుడు (ceḍuguḍu) — kabaddi

## You'll want to know: చెడుగుడు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C537-ATALU537-04]; assesses=[] -->

**చెడుగుడు** — *ceḍuguḍu* — "kabaddi".

> పల్లెల్లో యువకులు చెడుగుడు ఆడతారు.
>
> *pallellō yuvakulu ceḍuguḍu āḍatāru.*
>
> In the villages young men play kabaddi.

A team game of raids, played while holding one breath; also called కబడ్డీ.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**చెడుగుడు** (*ceḍuguḍu*) is the Telugu village name of the team game kabaddi; its origin is not settled.
