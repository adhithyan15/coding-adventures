# નાડી (nāḍī) — pulse

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-LEX-C224-DOCTOR224-01, GU-LEX-C224-DOCTOR224-02] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Gujarati for doctor, then the Gujarati for patient.

## You'll want to know: નાડી
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C224-DOCTOR224-03]; assesses=[] -->

**નાડી** — *nāḍī* — "pulse".

> **શું દાક્તર નાડી જુએ છે?** — *śũ dāktar nāḍī jue che?* — "Is the doctor taking the pulse?"

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**નાડી** (*nāḍī*) comes from Sanskrit *nāḍī*, "tubular stalk, tubular organ, tube". CDIAL lists Gujarati *nāṛī*, "pulse", and *nāṛ*, "vein, pulse" (CDIAL 7047).
