Urdu, chapter 132: Dirty, Rich, True, Strong, Weak.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
گندا (gandā) — dirty.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for fast, then the Urdu for slow.

You'll want to know: گندا.
گندا — gandā — "dirty".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.

[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does گندا (gandā) mean? ("Dirty".) Say it once more.
