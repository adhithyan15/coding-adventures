Arabic, chapter 315: Victory and defeat.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
نصر (naṣr) — a victory in battle.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to escort a coffin, then the Arabic for a grave.

You'll want to know: نصر.
نصر — naṣr — "a victory in battle".
احتفل الناس بالنصر في الشوارع. iḥtafala an-nās bi-n-naṣr fī ash-shawāriʿ. People celebrated the victory in the streets.

The word, taken apart.
نصر (naṣr) is built on the native Arabic root n-ṣ-r (Lane), "to aid someone against an enemy". Help that wins the day is victory.

You'll want to know: هزيمة.
هزيمة — hazīma — "a defeat".
كانت الهزيمة صعبة على الفريق. kānat al-hazīma ṣaʿba ʿalā al-farīq. The defeat was hard on the team.

The word, taken apart.
هزيمة (hazīma) is built on the native Arabic root h-z-m (Lane), "to rout, to put to flight". Hazīma is the rout of the side that loses.

انتصر (intaṣara) is built on the native Arabic root n-ṣ-r (Lane), "to help against an enemy". It is Form VIII, "to get the victory".

Lesson 4 of 5.
هزم (hazama) — to defeat.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a defeat, then the Arabic for to win a victory.

You'll want to know: هزم.
هزم — hazama — "to defeat".
هزم الفريق الصغير الفريق الكبير. hazama al-farīq aṣ-ṣaghīr al-farīq al-kabīr. The small team beat the big team.
Present: يهزم, yahzim.
The passive هزم (hazama), huzima, is to be defeated.

The word, taken apart.
هزم (hazama) is built on the native Arabic root h-z-m (Lane), "to rout; to put an army to flight": Lane gives inhazama, of an army that was routed.

Lesson 5 of 5.
استسلم (istaslama) — to surrender, to give in.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to win a victory, then the Arabic for to defeat.

You'll want to know: استسلم.
استسلم — istaslama — "to surrender, to give in".
استسلم الجنود بعد يومين. istaslama al-junūd baʿda yawmayn. The soldiers surrendered after two days.
Present: يستسلم, yastaslim.
لا تستسلم! Do not give up!

The word, taken apart.
استسلم (istaslama) is built on the native Arabic root s-l-m (Lane), "to be safe, sound", the root of salām, peace. It is Form X, "to yield oneself up".
