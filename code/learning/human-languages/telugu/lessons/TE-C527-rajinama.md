# రాజీనామా (rājīnāmā) — a resignation

## You'll want to know: రాజీనామా
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C527-VIJAYAM527-04]; assesses=[] -->

**రాజీనామా** — *rājīnāmā* — "a resignation".

> జీతం సరిపోక అతను రాజీనామా చేశాడు.
>
> *jītaṁ saripōka atanu rājīnāmā cēśāḍu.*
>
> His pay was not enough, so he resigned.

రాజీనామా చేయు, to resign.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**రాజీనామా** (*rājīnāmā*) is a loan from Persian *rāzīnāma*, "a written agreement, a letter of consent", which reached Telugu through Hindustani.

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
