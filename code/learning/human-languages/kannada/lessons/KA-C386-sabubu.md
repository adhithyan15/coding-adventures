## You'll want to know: ಸಬೂಬು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C386-SAMAJAYISHI386-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸಬೂಬು** — *sabūbu* — "a lame excuse".

> ಕೆಲಸ ಮಾಡದಿರಲು ಅವನು ಸಬೂಬು ಹೇಳಿದನು.
>
> *kelasa māḍadiralu avanu sabūbu hēḷidanu.*
>
> He made an excuse for not working.

ಸಬೂಬು ಹೇಳು, to make excuses.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸಬೂಬು** (*sabūbu*) is usually traced to Arabic *sabab*, "a cause, a reason", which reached Kannada through Hindustani.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
