## You'll want to know: ਢੇਲਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C294-POOR294-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ਢੇਲਾ** — *ḍhelā* — "a clod of earth".

> ਸਾਡੇ ਖੇਤ ਵਿੱਚ ਵੱਡੇ ਢੇਲੇ ਹਨ।
>
> *sāḍe khet vich vaḍḍe ḍhele han.*
>
> There are big clods in our field.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਢੇਲਾ** (*ḍhelā*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*ḍala*, "lump", in its form *\*ḍhēlla*; CDIAL lists Punjabi *ḍhelā*, "clod", under it (CDIAL 5536).
