## You'll want to know: die Wut
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C468-WUT-01]; assesses=[] -->

**die Wut** — "rage, fury".

> Vor Wut warf er sein Handy auf das Sofa.
>
> *In a rage, he threw his phone onto the sofa.*

No plural. *Eine Wut haben auf* + accusative — to be furious with.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Wut* is inherited from Middle High German *wuot* and Old High German *wuot* "mad, madness", from Proto-Germanic *wōdaz* (EtymDB).
