## You'll want to know: shānyáng
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C242-FARMANIMAL242-02]; assesses=[] -->

*shānyáng* — "goat".

> *zhè zhī shānyáng hěn xǐhuan chī cǎo* — This goat loves eating grass.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*shānyáng* is built from 山 'hill' and *yáng* 'sheep'. The first is inherited from Middle Chinese *srean* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription); the dictionary glosses the second, *yang* there, with the pair 'oxen and sheep'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
