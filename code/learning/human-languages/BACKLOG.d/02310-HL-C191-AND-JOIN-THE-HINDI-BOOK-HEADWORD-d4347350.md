## HL-C191 — पुस्तक and पोथा join the Hindi book headword

After **पाँव**, the ranked Hindi queue reaches **पुस्तक**, the first remaining
independent learner-facing word after the already classified script fragments,
historical ancestors, and verb pieces. Its owning lesson reveals a complete
three-register set rather than a single hidden synonym.

Chapter 37 defines ordinary **किताब**, formal **पुस्तक**, and old or homely
**पोथा** as three Hindi words for “book.” The register comparison introduces a
dedicated concept, guided practice assesses that concept, and wrap-up recall
asks learners to choose between the ordinary and formal forms. The old headword
named only **किताब**, so both alternatives remained invisible to reports and
downstream cards.

The headword is now **किताब / पुस्तक / पोथा**, and its gloss names the register
of each form. This repairs metadata for an existing taught contrast without
adding vocabulary or moving its teaching point. Rerun the ranked report next
and continue the evidence-first audit after the explicitly classified script,
morphology, and historical-source rows.
