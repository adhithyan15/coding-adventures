## You'll want to know: શંકા
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C286-GUNO286-03]; assesses=[] -->

**શંકા** — *śankā* — "a suspicion".

> મીરાને ચોકીદાર પર શંકા હતી.
>
> *Mīrāne cokīdār par śankā hatī.*
>
> Mira suspected the watchman.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**શંકા** (*śankā*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *śaṅkā*, "fear, distrust" (CDIAL 12258). The person suspected takes **પર**: **ચોકીદાર પર શંકા**, a suspicion about the watchman.
