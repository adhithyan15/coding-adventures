## You'll want to know: köcheln
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C327-KOECHELN-01]; assesses=[] -->

**köcheln** — "to simmer".

> Die Suppe köchelte eine Stunde.
>
> *The soup simmered for an hour.*

A small form of *kochen*: gentle cooking.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*köcheln* is built on *kochen* "to cook", Old High German *kochōn*, which EtymDB traces to Latin *coquus* "a cook", from *coquere* "to cook"; the ending *-eln* makes it small and gentle.
