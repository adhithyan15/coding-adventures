## You'll want to know: ごうかくする
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C515-EXAM515-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ごうかくする** — *gōkaku suru* — "to pass (a test); to be accepted".

> **もし ごうかくしたら、りょうしんは よろこぶでしょう。** — *moshi gōkakushitara, ryōshin wa yorokobu deshō* — "If I pass, my parents will surely be glad."

Polite **ごうかくします**, *gōkakushimasu*; a する verb. Past (polite): **むすめが ごうかくしました。**, *musume ga gōkakushimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ごうかくする** (*gōkaku suru*) is the Sino-Japanese (kango) noun *gōkaku* with the native verb *suru*, "to do": two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *gō*, "fit" (Middle Chinese *hop*) and *kaku*, "status" (*kaek*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
