## You'll want to know: भोळा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C470-LABAD470-03]; assesses=[] -->

**भोळा** — *bhoḷā* — "naive; simple-hearted".

> भोळ्या आजोबांना कोणीही फसवतं.
>
> *bhoḷyā ājobānnā koṇīhī phasavtaṁ.*
>
> Anyone can fool our simple-hearted grandfather.

Agrees: भोळा, भोळी, भोळं. भोळाभाबडा, guileless.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**भोळा** (*bhoḷā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi *bhoḷā*, "foolish", under the reconstructed *\*bhulla*, "defective" (CDIAL 9539).
