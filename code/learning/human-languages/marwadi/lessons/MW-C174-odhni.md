## You'll want to know: ओढणी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C174-ODHNI174-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ओढणी** — *oḍhṇī* — "a head-cloth worn by women".

> म्हारी ओढणी लाल है।
>
> *mhārī oḍhṇī lāl hai.*
>
> My head-cloth is red.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ओढणी** (*oḍhṇī*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*ōḍḍh*, "put on, wear, cover", which CDIAL takes from Dravidian; it lists the Old Marwari verb *auḍhaï*, "covers", under it (CDIAL 2547). It is the cloth you cover yourself with: **ओढणो**.
