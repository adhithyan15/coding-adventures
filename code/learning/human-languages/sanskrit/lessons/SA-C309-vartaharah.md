## You'll want to know: वार्ताहरः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C309-VRITTANTA309-05]; assesses=[] -->

**वार्ताहरः** — *vārtāharaḥ* — "a news reporter".

> वार्ताहरः यामिकं पृच्छति।
>
> *vārtāharaḥ yāmikaṃ pṛcchati.*
>
> The reporter questions the watchman.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**वार्ताहरः** (*vārtāharaḥ*) is built from *vārtā*, "news" (MW writes *vārttā*), and *hara*, "carrying", from the root √hṛ; MW gives it as a carrier of tidings, a messenger, and a news reporter is its modern use (MW).
