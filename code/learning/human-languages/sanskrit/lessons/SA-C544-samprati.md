## You'll want to know: सम्प्रति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C544-PARASPARA544-05]; assesses=[] -->

**सम्प्रति** — *samprati* — "at present".

> यदि सम्प्रति वर्षा भवति, तर्हि वयं पश्चात् गमिष्यामः।
>
> *yadi samprati varṣā bhavati, tarhi vayaṃ paścāt gamiṣyāmaḥ.*
>
> If it is raining now, we will go later.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सम्प्रति** (*samprati*) is built on *sam* and *prati*, "towards, against"; MW gives *samprati* as directly opposite, at the right time, and now, at present (MW).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
