## You'll want to know: पड़ोस
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C421-BASTI421-04]; assesses=[] -->

**पड़ोस** — *paṛos* — "the neighbourhood; next door".

> हमारे पड़ोस में एक नई दुकान खुली है।
>
> *hamāre paṛos meṁ ek naī dukān khulī hai.*
>
> A new shop has opened in our neighbourhood.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पड़ोस** (*paṛos*) is a tadbhava of Sanskrit *prativeśa*, "neighbouring; a neighbour" (CDIAL 8598), which lists Hindi *paṛos*, "neighbourhood". पड़ोसी, a neighbour, is built on it.
