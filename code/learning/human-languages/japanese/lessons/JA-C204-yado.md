## You'll want to know: やど
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C204-TRIP204-05]; assesses=[] -->

**やど** — *yado* — "lodging; a place to stay".

> **やどは うみの ちかくです。** — *yado wa umi no chikaku desu* — "Our lodging is near the sea."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**やど** (*yado*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi, and its older origin is not settled (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
