## You'll want to know: les applaudissements
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C515-SCENE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**les applaudissements** — "applause".

> À la fin, il y a eu de longs applaudissements.
>
> *At the end there was long applause.*

Usually plural. The verb is *applaudir* "to clap".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*les applaudissements* is built on *applaudir* "to clap", a learned borrowing from Latin *applaudere* "to clap at", built on *plaudere* "to clap" (Lewis & Short).
