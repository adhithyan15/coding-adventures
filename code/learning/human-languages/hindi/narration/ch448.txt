Hindi, chapter 448: Beaks, Wings and Thorns.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
चोंच (coñc) — a beak.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for an insect, then the Hindi for to wind round.

You'll want to know: चोंच.
चोंच — coñc — "a beak".
तोते की चोंच लाल होती है। tote kī coñc lāl hotī hai. A parrot's beak is red.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
चोंच (coñc) is a tadbhava of Sanskrit cañcu, "a beak" (CDIAL 4569), which lists Hindi cõc; CDIAL thinks the Sanskrit word itself came from Dravidian.

Lesson 2 of 5.
पंख (paṅkh) — a wing; a feather.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for to wind round, then the Hindi for a beak.

You'll want to know: पंख.
पंख — paṅkh — "a wing; a feather".
चिड़िया ने अपने पंख फैलाए। ciṛiyā ne apne paṅkh phailāe. The bird spread its wings.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
पंख (paṅkh) comes from Sanskrit pakṣa, "a wing, a feather, a side" (CDIAL 7627), which lists Hindi pā̃kh, "wing", and पंखा, a fan: the fan is a wing that moves the air.

Lesson 4 of 5.
चुगना (cugnā) — to peck at; to pick up (grain).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a wing, then the Hindi for hunting.

You'll want to know: चुगना.
चुगना — cugnā — "to peck at; to pick up (grain)".
आँगन में कबूतर चावल चुग रहे थे। āṁgan meṁ kabūtar cāval cug rahe the. Pigeons were pecking at rice in the courtyard.
Past (it …): वह चिड़िया पूरा अनाज चुग गई, vah ciṛiyā pūrā anāj cug gaī.

The word, taken apart.
चुगना (cugnā) goes back to cugyati, "pecks", a form CDIAL reconstructs (CDIAL 4852), which lists Hindi cugnā, "to peck, pick up, feed".

Lesson 5 of 5.
चुभना (cubhnā) — to prick; to stick into.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for hunting, then the Hindi for to peck.

You'll want to know: चुभना.
चुभना — cubhnā — "to prick; to stick into".
एक काँटा मेरे पैर में चुभ गया। ek kā̃ṭā mere pair meṁ cubh gayā. A thorn pricked my foot.
Past (it …): वह काँटा उँगली में चुभा, vah kā̃ṭā uṅglī meṁ cubhā.

The word, taken apart.
चुभना (cubhnā) goes back to cubhyate, "is pierced", a form CDIAL reconstructs (CDIAL 4867), which lists Hindi cubhnā. A harsh word चुभता too: it stings.
