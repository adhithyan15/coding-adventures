# A small marker after the agent

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-FORM-NAME-AMAN-01, PA-LEX-KAMM-05, PA-FORM-KITA-190] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Recall the practice name **ਅਮਨ**, “work” **ਕੰਮ**, and the
completed form **ਕੀਤਾ**.

## Grammar Lens: direct owner
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-GRAMMAR-AGENT-NE-210]; assesses=[PA-FORM-NAME-AMAN-01, PA-LEX-KAMM-05, PA-FORM-KITA-190] -->

> **ਨੇ** — *ne* — marks the person who did the action in this frame
>
> **ਅਮਨ ਨੇ ਕੰਮ ਕੀਤਾ** — *Aman ne kamm kiitaa* — Aman did the work

Put **ਨੇ** immediately after **ਅਮਨ** in this one third-person completed
transitive action. The short marker points to the doer. Familiar **ਕੀਤਾ**
still agrees with the unmarked masculine-singular object **ਕੰਮ**. Say the
whole sentence once, then point to its doer, work, and action.

## Grammar Lens: source boundary
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-GRAMMAR-AGENT-NE-210] -->

Punjabi University Patiala's [lesson 14](https://pt.learnpunjabi.org/lesson14.aspx?l=14)
shows a comparable completed-action pattern and warns that usage varies by
sentence. This is a safe small example; not every past sentence takes
**ਨੇ**. The same written sound can also be a present-tense auxiliary at
the *end* of a different sentence; that is not today's marker. We do not
generalise beyond this named-agent, third-person frame.
