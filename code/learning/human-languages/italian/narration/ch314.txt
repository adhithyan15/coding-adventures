Italian, chapter 314: Il testamento.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
il testamento — the will, the testament.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for mourning, then the Italian for to bury.

You'll want to know: il testamento.
il testamento — "the will, the testament".
Il nonno ha lasciato un testamento. Grandfather left a will.
Fare testamento — to make a will.

The word, taken apart.
testamento is inherited from Latin testāmentum "a will" (EtymDB), built on testārī "to bear witness".
