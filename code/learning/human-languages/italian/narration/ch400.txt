Italian, chapter 400: Scaricare un'app.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
l'applicazione — the app, the application; the effort.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for to log in, then the Italian for to sign up.

You'll want to know: l'applicazione.
l'applicazione — "the app, the application; the effort".
Con questa applicazione puoi pagare il parcheggio. With this app you can pay for parking.
In speech, often l'app. It also means diligence: studiare con applicazione.

The word, taken apart.
applicazione is a learned borrowing from Latin applicātiō "a joining to, an attaching" (EtymDB), from applicāre "to attach", built on plicāre "to fold"; the software sense came from English.

Lesson 2 of 5.
installare — to install.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for to sign up, then the Italian for the app.

You'll want to know: installare.
installare — "to install".
Se installi l'aggiornamento, il telefono va più veloce. If you install the update, the phone runs faster.
Also to fit something in a house: installare una lampada.

The word, taken apart.
installare was borrowed from French installer, from Medieval Latin installāre "to put in a stall, to seat in office", built on stallum, a word of Germanic origin; compare Old High German stal "place, stall" (EtymDB).

Lesson 3 of 5.
disinstallare — to uninstall.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for the app, then the Italian for to install.

You'll want to know: disinstallare.
disinstallare — "to uninstall".
Se un'app non ti serve, disinstallala. If you do not need an app, uninstall it.
Dis- reverses the verb.

The word, taken apart.
disinstallare is built from dis- "un-" and installare, which was borrowed from French installer, from Medieval Latin installāre "to put in a stall", on a Germanic word for a place; compare Old High German stal (EtymDB).

Lesson 4 of 5.
il dispositivo — the device.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for to install, then the Italian for to uninstall.

You'll want to know: il dispositivo.
il dispositivo — "the device".
Puoi usare l'app su tre dispositivi. You can use the app on three devices.
Phones, tablets and computers. Also a safety device: un dispositivo di sicurezza.

The word, taken apart.
dispositivo is modelled on French dispositif, built on Latin dispositus "arranged", past participle of dispōnere "to set in order" (Lewis & Short), on pōnere "to put".

Lesson 5 of 5.
il software — the software.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for to uninstall, then the Italian for the device.

You'll want to know: il software.
il software — "the software".
Il software è nuovo e ancora non lo conosco. The software is new and I do not know it yet.
Masculine, invariable. L'hardware is the machine itself.

The word, taken apart.
software was borrowed from English software (EtymDB), built from soft and ware "goods", on the model of hardware.
