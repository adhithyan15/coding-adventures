## You'll want to know: cídiǎn
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C104-DESK104-05]; assesses=[] -->

*cídiǎn* — "dictionary".

> *wǒ yǒu yì běn hànyǔ cídiǎn* — I've got a Chinese dictionary.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*cídiǎn* is built from cí 'words' and diǎn 'a standard work', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *zi* and *tenX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription); the dictionary glosses the second as a law and a classic.

## What you've built

One more word for everyday things.
