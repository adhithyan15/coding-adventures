## You'll want to know: traicionar

**traicionar** — "to betray".

> Me traicionó mi mejor amigo.
>
> *My best friend betrayed me.*

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*traicionar* is built on *traición*, usually traced to Latin *trāditiō* "a handing over", from *trādere* "to hand over".
