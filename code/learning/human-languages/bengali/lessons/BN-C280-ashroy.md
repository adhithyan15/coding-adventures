## You'll want to know: আশ্রয়
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C280-UDDHAR280-05]; assesses=[] -->

**আশ্রয়** — *āshrôy* — "shelter; refuge".

> আগুনের পরে অনেক পরিবার স্কুলে আশ্রয় নিল।
>
> *āguner pôre ônek pôribār skule āshrôy nilô.*
>
> After the fire, many families took shelter in the school.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**আশ্রয়** (*āshrôy*) is Sanskrit *āśraya*, that on which something leans or rests (Monier-Williams). আশ্রয় নেওয়া, to take shelter.
