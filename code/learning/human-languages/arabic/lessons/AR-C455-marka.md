## You'll want to know: ماركة
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C455-WAJIHA455-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ماركة** — *mārka* — "a brand".

> اشترى أخي حذاء من ماركة مشهورة.
>
> *ishtarā akhī ḥidhāʾ min mārka mashhūra.*
>
> My brother bought shoes from a famous brand.

Formal writing also says *ʿalāma tijāriyya*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ماركة** (*mārka*) is usually traced to Italian *marca*, "a mark".
