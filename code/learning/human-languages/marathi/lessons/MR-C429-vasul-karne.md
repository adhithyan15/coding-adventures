## You'll want to know: वसूल करणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C429-LILAV429-04]; assesses=[] -->

**वसूल करणे** — *vasūl karṇe* — "to recover money".

> कंपनीने थकबाकी वसूल केली.
>
> *kampanīne thakbākī vasūl kelī.*
>
> The company recovered the arrears.

Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने वसूल केलं, *tyāne vasūl kelaṁ*.

The money recovered is the object; वसूल होणे, to be recovered.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**वसूल करणे** (*vasūl karṇe*) joins **वसूल**, "recovered, collected", a loan through Persian, usually traced to Arabic *wuṣūl*, "arrival, receipt", to **करणे**.
