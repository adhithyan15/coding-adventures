## You'll want to know: das Brettspiel
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C501-BRETTSPIEL-01]; assesses=[] -->

**das Brettspiel** — "board game".

> Am Wochenende spielen wir gern Brettspiele.
>
> *At the weekend we like playing board games.*

Plural: *die Brettspiele*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Brettspiel* is built on *Brett* "board, plank", inherited from Middle High German *bret*, and *Spiel* "game", from Old High German *spil* (EtymDB).
