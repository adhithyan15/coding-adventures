## You'll want to know: absurdo
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C579-CRITICA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**absurdo** — "absurd, ridiculous".

> Es absurdo pagar tanto.
>
> *It is absurd to pay so much.*

*Es absurdo que* + subjunctive.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*absurdo* is a learned borrowing from Latin *absurdus* 'out of tune, senseless', built from *ab* and *surdus* 'deaf, dull-sounding' (EtymDB).
