Gujarati, chapter 317: Pages of History.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ઇતિહાસ (itihās) — history.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for secret, then the Gujarati for to last.

You'll want to know: ઇતિહાસ.
ઇતિહાસ — itihās — "history".
મીરાને ઇતિહાસ ખૂબ ગમે છે. Mīrāne itihās khūb game che. Mira loves history.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ઇતિહાસ (itihās) is a tatsama, Sanskrit itihāsa, from iti ha āsa, "so indeed it was" (Monier-Williams): the telling of what happened long ago. It is masculine: ગુજરાતનો ઇતિહાસ, the history of Gujarat.

Lesson 3 of 5.
પ્રાચીન (prācīn) — ancient.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for history, then the Gujarati for a century.

You'll want to know: પ્રાચીન.
પ્રાચીન — prācīn — "ancient".
આ ગામમાં એક પ્રાચીન કૂવો છે. ā gāmmā̃ ek prācīn kūvo che. There is an ancient well in this village.

The word, taken apart.
પ્રાચીન (prācīn) is a tatsama, Sanskrit prācīna, "turned to the east", and so "former, ancient" (Monier-Williams). It is for things of long ago; જૂનું is old in the everyday sense.

ભૂતકાળમાં આ શહેર ખૂબ નાનું હતું. bhūtkāḷmā̃ ā śaher khūb nānũ hatũ. In the past this city was very small.

The word, taken apart.
ભૂતકાળ (bhūtkāḷ) is built from Sanskrit bhūta, "been, gone, past", and kāla, "time" (Monier-Williams): the time that has been. It is masculine: ભૂતકાળમાં, in the past.

Lesson 5 of 5.
જન્મવું (janmavũ) — to be born.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for ancient, then the Gujarati for the past.

You'll want to know: જન્મવું.
જન્મવું — janmavũ — "to be born".
મારા દાદા આ ગામમાં જન્મ્યા હતા. mārā dādā ā gāmmā̃ janmyā hatā. My grandfather was born in this village.
Past (he …): તે અહીં જન્મ્યો, te ahī̃ janmyo.

The word, taken apart.
જન્મવું (janmavũ) is built on janma, birth, from Sanskrit jánman, "birth" (CDIAL 5113), the word in જન્મદિવસ, a birthday. The past is plural for an elder, out of respect: દાદા જન્મ્યા હતા.
