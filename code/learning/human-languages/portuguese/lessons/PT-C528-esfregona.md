## You'll want to know: a esfregona
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C528-LIMPEZA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**a esfregona** — "mop".

> Passei a esfregona no chão da cozinha.
>
> *I mopped the kitchen floor.*

Built on *esfregar*. *Passar a esfregona* — to mop.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*esfregona* is built on *esfregar* "to scrub", inherited from Vulgar Latin *exfricāre*, from Latin *fricāre* "to rub" (EtymDB).
