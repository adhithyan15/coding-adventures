## You'll want to know: अलाव
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C332-PARAMPARA332-04]; assesses=[] -->

**अलाव** — *alāv* — "a bonfire".

> ठंड की रात में लोग अलाव के पास बैठे।
>
> *ṭhaṇḍ kī rāt meṁ log alāv ke pās baiṭhe.*
>
> On the cold night people sat around the bonfire.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अलाव** (*alāv*) goes back to Sanskrit *alāta*, "a firebrand, a burning stick" (CDIAL 710): the open fire people sit around in winter.
