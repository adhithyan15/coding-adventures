## You'll want to know: die Fabel
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C313-MAERCHEN-03]; assesses=[] -->

**die Fabel** — "the fable".

> In der Fabel spricht der Fuchs mit der Maus.
>
> *In the fable the fox talks to the mouse.*

Plural: *die Fabeln*. Animals that talk, and a lesson at the end.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Fabel* comes from Middle High German *fabel* (EtymDB), from Latin *fābula* "a story, a tale" (Lewis & Short).
