## You'll want to know: بازیافت
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C388-ALUDEGI-02]; assesses=[] -->

**بازیافت** (*bâzyâft*) — "recycling".

> بازیافت کاغذ درختها را نجات میدهد.
>
> *bâzyâft-e kâghaz derakhthâ râ nejât midahad.*
>
> Recycling paper saves trees.

*Bâz* "again, back" + *yâft* "found", from *yâftan* "to find".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**بازیافت** is a modern native coinage built from *bâz* "back, again" + *yâft*, the past stem of *yâftan* "to find". Recycling finds a material again.
