## You'll want to know: ਸੁੱਥਣ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C230-DIN230-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ਸੁੱਥਣ** — *sutthaṇ* — "loose trousers (worn by women)".

> ਦਾਦੀ ਸੁੱਥਣ ਅਤੇ ਕੁੜਤਾ ਪਹਿਨਦੀ ਹੈ।
>
> *dādī sutthaṇ ate kuṛtā pahindī hai.*
>
> Grandmother wears loose trousers and a kurta.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਸੁੱਥਣ** (*sutthaṇ*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*sutthanā*, "trousers"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *sutthaṇ*, "women's loose trousers" (CDIAL 13468).
