## You'll want to know: مخلص
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C499-AMANA499-04]; assesses=[] -->

**مخلص** — *mukhliṣ* — "loyal, devoted".

> الكلب صديق مخلص.
>
> *al-kalb ṣadīq mukhliṣ.*
>
> The dog is a loyal friend.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**مخلص** (*mukhliṣ*) is built on the native Arabic root *kh-l-ṣ*, "to be pure, to be free of anything mixed" (Lane). Mukhliṣ is the participle of Form IV: one whose feeling is pure.

## What you've built

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
