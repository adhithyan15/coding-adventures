Lesson 1 of 5.
déménager — to move house.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for to weigh, then the French for to check.

You'll want to know: déménager.
déménager — "to move house".

The word, taken apart.
déménager — built from dé-, "out", and the word for "a household", from Old French maisnie, "a household", from Vulgar Latin mansionata, from mansio, "a dwelling": to take the household out.
