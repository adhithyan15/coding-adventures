## You'll want to know: ভবিষ্যৎ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C367-ANUMAN367-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ভবিষ্যৎ** — *bhôbishyôt* — "the future".

> এখন টাকা জমালে ভবিষ্যৎ নিরাপদ হবে।
>
> *ekhôn ṭākā jômāle bhôbishyôt nirāpôd hôbe.*
>
> If you save money now, your future will be secure.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ভবিষ্যৎ** (*bhôbishyôt*) is Sanskrit *bhaviṣyat*, 'what is going to be' (Monier-Williams). The last letter is ৎ, a t with no vowel after it.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
