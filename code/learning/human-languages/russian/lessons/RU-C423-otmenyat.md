## You'll want to know: отменять
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C423-ZADERZHKA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**отменять** (*otmenyát*) — "to cancel".

> В плохую погоду часто отменяют рейсы.
>
> *V plokhúyu pogódu chásto otmenyáyut réysy.*
>
> In bad weather flights are often cancelled.

Imperfective; perfective *отменить* (*отменю, отменишь*).

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*отменять* is built from *от-* 'away' and *менять* 'to change', on *мена* 'an exchange', which is inherited from Proto-Slavic *měna* (EtymDB).
