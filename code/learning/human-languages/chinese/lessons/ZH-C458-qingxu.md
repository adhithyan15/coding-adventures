## You'll want to know: qíngxù
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C458-MOOD458-01]; assesses=[] -->

*qíngxù* — "emotions; spirits".

> *zuìjìn wǒ de qíngxù bú tài hǎo* — My mood hasn't been great lately.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*qíngxù* is built from *qíng* 'feeling' and *xù* 'the end of a thread', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *dzjeng* and *zjoX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription). The dictionary explains the first, after an older book, as what a person desires, and the second as the loose end of silk; the sense 'mood' is not given there.
