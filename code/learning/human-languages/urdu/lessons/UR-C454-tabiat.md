## You'll want to know: طبیعت
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C454-CHARACTER454-02]; assesses=[] -->

**طبیعت** — *tabīat* — "disposition; how one feels (in health)".

> آج میری طبیعت ٹھیک نہیں ہے۔
>
> *āj merī tabīat ṭhīk nahīṅ hai.*
>
> I'm not feeling well today.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**طبیعت** (*tabīat*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *ṭabīʿa*, "nature, temperament"; Urdu also uses it to ask how someone feels.
