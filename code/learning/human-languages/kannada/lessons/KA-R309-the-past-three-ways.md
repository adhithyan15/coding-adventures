# Review — the past three ways

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[KA-GRAMMAR-C309-PAST-PERSON-01, KA-GRAMMAR-C309-PAST-STEMS-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "Yesterday I went to my home town." (*ninne nānu ūrige hōde*.) Then "she gave" and "it happened". (*koṭṭaḷu*, *āyitu*.)

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[KA-GRAMMAR-C309-PAST-PERSON-01, KA-GRAMMAR-C309-PAST-STEMS-01, KA-GRAMMAR-C309-ONGOING-PAST-01, KA-GRAMMAR-C309-HABITUAL-PAST-01, KA-GRAMMAR-C309-WHILE-01, KA-GRAMMAR-C309-THERE-WAS-01, KA-LEX-C298-VASTU298-02, KA-ETYMON-C12-KUTUMBA-01, KA-LEX-C106-SOON-04, KA-LEX-C55-ROAD-04] -->

[PAUSE 2s each]
- [YOU SAY: We drank coffee, and afterwards we went to the cinema.]
- [YOU SAY: While I was reading a book, my younger sister phoned.]
- [YOU SAY: At nine last night it was raining.]
- [YOU SAY: My grandmother used to tell a story every evening.]
- [YOU SAY: There was a small shop in our village. Now there is a big hotel.]
- [YOU SAY: one thing you were doing yesterday evening, with -ಉವಾಗ, and one thing that cut into it]

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[KA-GRAMMAR-C309-PAST-PERSON-01, KA-GRAMMAR-C309-ONGOING-PAST-01, KA-GRAMMAR-C309-HABITUAL-PAST-01, KA-GRAMMAR-C309-WHILE-01, KA-GRAMMAR-C309-THERE-WAS-01] -->

[PAUSE 3s] Which past is the event? (**The plain past**: **ಹೋದೆ**, **ಬಂತು**.) Which is the scene? (**-ಉವಾಗ**, or **-ಉತ್ತಿದ್ದೆ** with one point in time.) Which is the habit? (**-ಉತ್ತಿದ್ದೆ** with **ಪ್ರತಿದಿನ**.) "There was"? (**ಇತ್ತು**.)
