# सायद (sāyad) — perhaps

## You'll want to know: सायद
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C163-PLAN163-01]; assesses=[MW-GRAMMAR-C162-FUT-1SG-01, MW-LEX-C79-TIME79-04] -->

**सायद** — *sāyad* — "perhaps".

> **सायद हूं काल आसूं।**
>
> *sāyad hū̃ kāl āsū̃.* — Perhaps I'll come tomorrow.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MW-LEX-C80-TIME80-01] -->

**सायद** (*sāyad*) is a loan, usually traced to Persian *shāyad*, "it may be".
Marwari says *s* where the Persian has *sh*, as it does in **हमेसा**.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MW-LEX-C163-PLAN163-01, MW-GRAMMAR-C163-FUT-PLAN-01] -->

**सायद** opens the sentence and leaves the rest as it was: **सायद परसूं हूं बाजार
जासूं।** (*sāyad parsū̃ hū̃ bājār jāsū̃.*)
