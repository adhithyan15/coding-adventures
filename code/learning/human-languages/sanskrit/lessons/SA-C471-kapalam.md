## You'll want to know: कपालम्
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C471-MASTISKA471-02]; assesses=[] -->

**कपालम्** — *kapālam* — "the skull".

> कठिनं कपालं मस्तिष्कं रक्षति।
>
> *kaṭhinaṃ kapālaṃ mastiṣkaṃ rakṣati.*
>
> The hard skull protects the brain.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**कपालम्** (*kapālam*) is traced to the root √kamp in the Uṇādi list that MW follows; its first senses are "a cup, a dish", and "the skull, the cranium" comes after them (MW).
