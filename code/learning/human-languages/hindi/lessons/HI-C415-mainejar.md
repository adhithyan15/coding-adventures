## You'll want to know: मैनेजर
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C415-RAJISTAR415-01]; assesses=[] -->

**मैनेजर** — *mainejar* — "a manager".

> होटल का मैनेजर बहुत अच्छा आदमी था।
>
> *hoṭal kā mainejar bahut acchā ādmī thā.*
>
> The hotel manager was a very nice man.

Masculine or feminine, by the person meant.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**मैनेजर** (*mainejar*) is borrowed from English *manager*.
