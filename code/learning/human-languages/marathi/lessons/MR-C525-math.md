## You'll want to know: माठ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C525-BHANDI525-02]; assesses=[] -->

**माठ** — *māṭh* — "a clay water pot".

> उन्हाळ्यात माठातलं पाणी थंड राहतं.
>
> *unhāḷyāt māṭhātlaṁ pāṇī thaṇḍ rāhtaṁ.*
>
> In summer the water in the clay pot stays cool.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**माठ** (*māṭh*) is the word for the big clay pot that keeps water cool; its origin is not settled.
