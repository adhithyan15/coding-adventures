# সম্পর্ক (sômpôrkô) — a relationship; a connection

## You'll want to know: সম্পর্ক
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C487-SOMPORKO487-01]; assesses=[] -->

**সম্পর্ক** — *sômpôrkô* — "a relationship; a connection".

> দুই ভাইয়ের সম্পর্ক ভালো।
>
> *dui bhāiyer sômpôrkô bhālo.*
>
> The two brothers get on well.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**সম্পর্ক** (*sômpôrkô*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *samparka*, "mixing together, union, contact" (Monier-Williams).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
