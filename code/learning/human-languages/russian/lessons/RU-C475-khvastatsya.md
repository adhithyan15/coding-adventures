## You'll want to know: хвастаться
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C475-PRIDE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**хвастаться** (*khvástatsya*) — "to boast, to show off".

> Он любит хвастаться своей машиной.
>
> *On lyúbit khvástatsya svoyéy mashínoy.*
>
> He likes to show off his car.

Imperfective; the perfective is похвастаться. Takes the instrumental: *хвастаться машиной*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

The origin of *хвастаться* is not settled: EtymDB lists the word without a source, and it is often compared with *хвалить* 'to praise'.
