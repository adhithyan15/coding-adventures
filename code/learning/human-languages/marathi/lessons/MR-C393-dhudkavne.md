## You'll want to know: धुडकावणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C393-SABAB393-03]; assesses=[] -->

**धुडकावणे** — *dhuḍkāvṇe* — "to brush aside; to spurn".

> त्याने माझा सल्ला धुडकावला.
>
> *tyāne mājhā sallā dhuḍkāvlā.*
>
> He brushed aside my advice.

Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने सल्ला धुडकावला, *tyāne sallā dhuḍkāvlā*.

Stronger than नाकारणे.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**धुडकावणे** (*dhuḍkāvṇe*) is a Marathi verb whose origin is not settled.

## What you've built
