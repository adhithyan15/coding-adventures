## You'll want to know: تسمية
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C311-WILADA311-05]; assesses=[] -->

**تسمية** — *tasmiya* — "a naming; a name given".

> اختار الجد تسمية المولود.
>
> *ikhtāra al-jadd tasmiyat al-mawlūd.*
>
> The grandfather chose the baby’s name.

The verb is سمى, to name.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**تسمية** (*tasmiya*) is built on the native Arabic root *s-m-w* (Lane), "to be high", the root of the taught *ism*, a name. *Tasmiya* is the verbal noun of Form II *sammā*, "to give a name".
