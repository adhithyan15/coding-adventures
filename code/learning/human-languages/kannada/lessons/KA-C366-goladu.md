# ಗೋಳಾಡು (gōḷāḍu) — to wail; to lament

## You'll want to know: ಗೋಳಾಡು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C366-GHANATE366-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ಗೋಳಾಡು** — *gōḷāḍu* — "to wail; to lament".

> ಮನೆ ಕಳೆದುಕೊಂಡ ಹೆಂಗಸು ಗೋಳಾಡಿದಳು.
>
> *mane kaḷedukoṇḍa heṅgasu gōḷāḍidaḷu.*
>
> The woman who had lost her house wailed.

Past (I …): ಗೋಳಾಡಿದೆ, *gōḷāḍide*.

ಗೋಳು, wailing, and ಆಡು: to keep on wailing. ಗೋಳು ಹೊಯ್ದುಕೊ is the same.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಗೋಳಾಡು** (*gōḷāḍu*) is a native Dravidian compound: *gōḷ*, "the sound of howling, lamenting, wailing" (DEDR 2252), and *āḍu*, "to move, to act" (DEDR 347).
