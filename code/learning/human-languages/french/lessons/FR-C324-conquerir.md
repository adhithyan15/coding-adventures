## You'll want to know: conquérir
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C324-CONQUETE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**conquérir** — "to conquer; to win over".

> Ils ont conquis toute la région en un an.
>
> *They conquered the whole region in a year.*

Past participle *conquis*. Also *conquérir le public* — to win the audience over.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*conquérir* comes from Old French *conquerre*, from Vulgar Latin *conquaerere* "to seek to gain" (EtymDB), built on *quaerere* "to seek".
