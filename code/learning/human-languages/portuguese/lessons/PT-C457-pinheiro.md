## You'll want to know: o pinheiro
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C457-ARVORES-02]; assesses=[] -->

**o pinheiro** — "the pine (tree)".

> Os pinheiros cheiram bem depois da chuva.
>
> *The pine trees smell good after the rain.*

*A pinha* — the pine cone; *o pinhão* — the pine nut.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*pinheiro* is built on *pinho* 'pine wood' (EtymDB) with *-eiro*; *pinho* is inherited from Latin *pīnus* 'pine'.
