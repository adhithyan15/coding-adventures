## You'll want to know: ચાકરી
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C487-CAKRI487-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ચાકરી** — *cākrī* — "nursing care; looking after".

> વહુએ સાસુની ઘણી ચાકરી કરી.
>
> *vahue sāsunī ghaṇī cākrī karī.*
>
> The daughter-in-law looked after her mother-in-law devotedly.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ચાકરી** (*cākrī*) is usually traced to Persian *čākarī*, "service", built on *čākar*, "a servant".
