## You'll want to know: उरकणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C514-KAMKAJ514-04]; assesses=[] -->

**उरकणे** — *urakṇe* — "to get done; to finish off".

> दुपारच्या आधी त्याने सगळी कामं उरकली.
>
> *dupārcyā ādhī tyāne sagḷī kāmaṁ uraklī.*
>
> He got all the jobs done before noon.

Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने काम उरकलं, *tyāne kām uraklaṁ*.

With a plural neuter object the past ends -ली: कामं उरकली.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**उरकणे** (*urakṇe*) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi *urakṇẽ*, "to be over, be finished", under *\*urvara*, "left over" (CDIAL 2356).
