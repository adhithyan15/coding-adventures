## You'll want to know: हंसः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C48-BEAST-05]; assesses=[] -->

**हंसः** (*haṁsaḥ*) — "a goose".

**हंसः** is a goose, and in poetry a swan.

Another secure cousin, as far-travelled as वृकः: Latin *ānser*, Greek *khēn*, German *Gans*, English *goose* and *gander*. The bird held on to its name as faithfully as the wolf did.

In Sanskrit poetry it is the bird that can take milk out of water, so the word came to carry a sense of a mind that sorts things — and दुग्धम् is already yours to put beside it. Ends in **-अः**.
