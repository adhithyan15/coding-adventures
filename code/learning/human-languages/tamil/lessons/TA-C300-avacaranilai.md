## You'll want to know: அவசரநிலை
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C300-AVASARAM300-01]; assesses=[] -->

**அவசரநிலை** — *avacaranilai* — "an emergency".

> மழை நின்றால், அவசரநிலை முடிவுக்கு வரும்.
>
> *maḻai niṉṟāl, avacaranilai muṭivukku varum.*
>
> If the rain stops, the emergency will come to an end.

From அவசரம், urgency, and நிலை, a state.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**அவசரநிலை** (*avacaranilai*) joins அவசரம், "urgency", borrowed from Sanskrit *avasara*, "occasion" (CDIAL 858), with native நிலை, "state, condition" (DEDR 3675).

## What you've built

One more word for telling what happened.
