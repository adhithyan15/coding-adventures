## You'll want to know: sculpter
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C517-SCULPTURE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**sculpter** — "to sculpt, to carve".

> Il sculpte des animaux dans le bois.
>
> *He carves animals out of wood.*

The *p* is silent.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*sculpter* was made in French from *sculpture* and *sculpteur*, which go back to Latin *sculpere* "to carve" (Lewis & Short).
