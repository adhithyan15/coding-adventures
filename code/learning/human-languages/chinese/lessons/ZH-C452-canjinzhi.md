## You'll want to know: cānjīnzhǐ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C452-AFTERMEAL452-05]; assesses=[] -->

*cānjīnzhǐ* — "paper napkin".

> *fúwùyuán qǐng gěi wǒ jǐ zhāng cānjīnzhǐ* — Excuse me, could I have a few napkins, please?

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*cānjīnzhǐ* is built from *cān* 'a meal', *jīn* 'a cloth' and *zhǐ* 'paper', all inherited from Middle Chinese, *tshan*, *kin* and *tsyeX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to swallow'.
