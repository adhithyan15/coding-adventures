# Session Map — Telugu Chapters 1–2

Same spaced-repetition schedule as the other tracks (a word from session *N*
resurfaces at *N+1, N+3, N+7, N+15*). Lessons named by slug; this map is the
authoritative order.

**There is no reading course.** Telugu is learned *through* the words: each
lesson's *"The letters in this word"* section introduces exactly the letters
that word needs. By the end of Chapter 1 you have met the inherent vowel, the
common vowel signs, the virama and below-stacking conjuncts, the anusvāra, and
the independent vowels — all in the service of real greetings.

## Chapter 1 — Greetings

| Session | Lesson | Word | New letters / reading idea | Origin / family thread |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | namaskaram | నమస్కారం | inherent *a*, ā-sign, స్క stacked conjunct, anusvāra ం | Sanskrit loan (vs. Tamil's native *vaṇakkam*) |
| 2 | dhanyavadamulu | ధన్యవాదములు | aspirated ధ, న్య conjunct, plural *-mulu* | Sanskrit + Telugu plural; agglutination |
| 3 | avunu | అవును | independent అ, u-sign | native; yes/no as being |
| 4 | ledu | లేదు | ē-sign, u-sign | native, **different root** *lē-* (not *il-*); existence vs. identity |
| 5 | sare | సరే | స, రే (ē-sign) | native; the family word *sari* in Telugu dress |
| 6 | practice | (recap) | read all five | the *veḷḷi vastānu* farewell |

## Chapter 2 — Introducing Yourself

| Session | Lesson | Word | Root / hook |
|---|---|---|---|
| 7 | peru | పేరు | "name" ← Dravidian *\*pēr* (twin of Tamil *peyar*, not *name*) |
| 8 | naa | నా | "my" ← *nēnu* ("I") |
| 9 | naa-peru | నా పేరు … | **"my name is…"** — the **zero copula** (no "is") |
| 10 | nuvvu-miiru | నువ్వు / మీరు | **"you"** familiar/respectful; respect by plural |
| 11 | emiti | ఏమిటి | "what" ← Dravidian question-stem *\*yā-/\*e-* |
| 12 | mii-peru-emiti | మీ పేరు ఏమిటి? | **"what's your name?"** (still no "is") |
| 13 | santosham | సంతోషం | "pleased to meet you" — **Sanskrit** (vs. Tamil's native *magiḻcci*) |
| 14 | practice | (dialogue) | the whole exchange |
