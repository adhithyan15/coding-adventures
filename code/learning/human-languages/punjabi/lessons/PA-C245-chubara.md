## You'll want to know: ਚੁਬਾਰਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C245-GHR245-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ਚੁਬਾਰਾ** — *chubārā* — "an upper room".

> ਚੁਬਾਰੇ ਵਿੱਚ ਚਾਰ ਖਿੜਕੀਆਂ ਹਨ।
>
> *chubāre vich chār khiṛkīāṁ han.*
>
> The upper room has four windows.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਚੁਬਾਰਾ** (*chubārā*) is inherited from Sanskrit *caturdvāra*, "having four doors"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *cubārā*, "upper room with four windows" (CDIAL 4611).
