## You'll want to know: die Zulassung
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C380-ZULASSUNG-01]; assesses=[] -->

**die Zulassung** — "the admission; the registration (of a car)".

> Für die Zulassung zum Studium braucht man gute Noten.
>
> *For admission to university you need good marks.*

Plural: *die Zulassungen*. For a car, *die Zulassung* is its registration.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Zulassung* is the noun of *zulassen*, built on *lassen* "to let", inherited from Old High German *lāzan* and Proto-Germanic *lētaną* (EtymDB): being let in.
