## You'll want to know: لوس
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C503-PLAY-04]; assesses=[] -->

**لوس** (*lus*) — "spoiled (of a child)".

> پدربزرگ نوه اش را لوس کرده است.
>
> *pedarbozorg naveash râ lus karde ast.*
>
> Grandfather has spoiled his grandchild.

*lus kardan* — to spoil; *lus shodan* — to act spoilt. Colloquial.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*lus* is a colloquial Persian word, but no source used here gives its history, and its origin is not settled.
