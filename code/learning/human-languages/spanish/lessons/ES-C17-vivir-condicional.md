# viviría — the regular conditional closes its small frame

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-VIVIR, ES-GRAMMAR-IR-FUTURE-SINGULAR, ES-GRAMMAR-CONDITIONAL-HYPOTHETICAL, ES-GRAMMAR-ER-CONDITIONAL-SINGULAR] -->

[PAUSE 2s] You can move from **comeré** to **comería**. Apply the same move to
**viviré**: **viviría** — “I would live.”

## Grammar Lens: the singular -ir row
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-GRAMMAR-IR-CONDITIONAL-SINGULAR]; assesses=[ES-LEX-VIVIR, ES-GRAMMAR-CONDITIONAL-HYPOTHETICAL, ES-GRAMMAR-AR-CONDITIONAL-SINGULAR, ES-GRAMMAR-ER-CONDITIONAL-SINGULAR] -->

| person | conditional |
|---|---|
| yo | vivir**ía** |
| tú | vivir**ías** |
| él / ella / usted | vivir**ía** |

Say **viviría · vivirías · viviría**. The complete regular frame is now
**hablaría, comería, viviría**. Plural persons wait.

## Grammar Lens: meaning with known words
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-GRAMMAR-IR-CONDITIONAL-SINGULAR, ES-LEX-EN] -->

**Viviría en Madrid** says “I would live in Madrid.” The lesson adds no new
place or preposition.
