Grammar Lens: direct owner.
ਨੇ — ne — marks the person who did the action in this frame ਅਮਨ ਨੇ ਕੰਮ ਕੀਤਾ — Aman ne kamm kiitaa — Aman did the work.
Put ਨੇ (ne) immediately after ਅਮਨ in this one third-person completed transitive action. The short marker points to the doer. Familiar ਕੀਤਾ still agrees with the unmarked masculine-singular object ਕੰਮ. Say the whole sentence once, then point to its doer, work, and action.

Grammar Lens: source boundary.
Punjabi University Patiala's lesson 14 shows a comparable completed-action pattern and warns that usage varies by sentence. This is a safe small example; not every past sentence takes ਨੇ (ne). The same written sound can also be a present-tense auxiliary at the end of a different sentence; that is not today's marker. We do not generalise beyond this named-agent, third-person frame.
