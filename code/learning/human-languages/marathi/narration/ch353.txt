Lesson 1 of 5.
पुष्टी करणे (puṣṭī karṇe) — to confirm.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for especially, then the Marathi for leading.

You'll want to know: पुष्टी करणे.
पुष्टी करणे — puṣṭī karṇe — "to confirm".
पोलिसांनी अपघाताची पुष्टी केली. polisānnī apghātācī puṣṭī kelī. The police confirmed the accident.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने पुष्टी केली, tyāne puṣṭī kelī.
पुष्टी is feminine, so the past is केली.

The word, taken apart.
पुष्टी करणे (puṣṭī karṇe) joins the tatsama पुष्टी, Sanskrit puṣṭi, "growth, thriving" (Monier-Williams), to करणे, "to make": to strengthen a report until it is sure.

Lesson 2 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for leading, then the Marathi for to confirm.

You'll want to know: दुजोरा देणे.
दुजोरा देणे — dujorā deṇe — "to corroborate".
मीराच्या बोलण्याला तिच्या भावाने दुजोरा दिला. mīrācyā bolaṇyālā ticyā bhāvāne dujorā dilā. Her brother backed up what Meera said.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने दुजोरा दिला, tyāne dujorā dilā.
The statement backed takes -ला.

The word, taken apart.
दुजोरा देणे (dujorā deṇe) joins दुजोरा, "support, backing", a Marathi noun whose origin is not settled, to देणे, "to give".

Lesson 3 of 5.
खंडन करणे (khaṇḍan karṇe) — to refute.

Before we start: this one needs your eyes, so it is not fully a driving lesson. You will want your eyes or your hands for the steps that ask you to read, point, write or gesture. You can listen to everything else now. I will say so again when we reach it, but leave this section until you have stopped: Guided Practice.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to confirm, then the Marathi for to corroborate.

You'll want to know: खंडन करणे.
खंडन करणे — khaṇḍan karṇe — "to refute".
त्याने एकेक मुद्दा घेऊन आरोपांचं खंडन केलं. tyāne ekek muddā gheūn āropāñcaṁ khaṇḍan kelaṁ. He refuted the accusations point by point.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने खंडन केलं, tyāne khaṇḍan kelaṁ.
Formal; the claim refuted takes -चं: आरोपांचं खंडन.

The word, taken apart.
खंडन करणे (khaṇḍan karṇe) joins the tatsama खंडन, Sanskrit khaṇḍana, "breaking" (CDIAL 3796), to करणे, "to do": to break an argument.

You'll want to know: वास्तविक.
वास्तविक — vāstavik — "actual; real".
त्याने सांगितलेलं कारण वास्तविक नव्हतं. tyāne sāṅgitlelaṁ kāraṇ vāstavik navhataṁ. The reason he gave was not the real one.

The word, taken apart.
वास्तविक (vāstavik) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit vāstavika, "substantial, real" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 5 of 5.
प्रतिक्रिया देणे (pratikriyā deṇe) — to react; to respond.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to refute, then the Marathi for actual.

You'll want to know: प्रतिक्रिया देणे.
प्रतिक्रिया देणे — pratikriyā deṇe — "to react; to respond".
या बातमीवर तिने लगेच प्रतिक्रिया दिली. yā bātmīvar tine lagec pratikriyā dilī. She reacted to the news at once.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने प्रतिक्रिया दिली, tyāne pratikriyā dilī.
What is reacted to takes -वर.

The word, taken apart.
प्रतिक्रिया देणे (pratikriyā deṇe) joins the tatsama प्रतिक्रिया, Sanskrit pratikriyā, "requital, counteraction" (Monier-Williams), to देणे, "to give".
