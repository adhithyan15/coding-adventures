## You'll want to know: 中医
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C231-DOCTOR231-01]; assesses=[] -->

**中医** — *zhōngyī* — "traditional Chinese medicine; a doctor of it".

> **我想看中医** *wǒ xiǎng kàn zhōngyī* — I'd like to see a Chinese-medicine doctor.

The new character, in pieces: 医 — seven strokes: a bar on top, five strokes inside it (a short fall, a short bar, a long bar, a long fall and a dot), then a stroke down the left that turns right along the foot. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**中医** (*zhōngyī*) is built from 中, as in 中国, and 医 'medicine', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *trjuwng* and *'i* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'to heal'.
