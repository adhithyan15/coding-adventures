## You'll want to know: どうりょう
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C229-OFFICE229-05]; assesses=[] -->

**どうりょう** — *dōryō* — "a colleague; a co-worker".

> **ちちは どうりょうと よく はなします。** — *chichi wa dōryō to yoku hanashimasu* — "My father often talks with his colleagues."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**どうりょう** (*dōryō*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *dō*, "same" (Middle Chinese *duwng*) and *ryō*, "companion, official" (*lew*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
