## You'll want to know: बहानो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C269-BAHANO269-01]; assesses=[] -->

**बहानो** — *bahāno* — "an excuse".

> यो बहानो साचो कोनी।
>
> *yo bahāno sācho konī.*
>
> This excuse isn't true.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**बहानो** (*bahāno*) is usually traced to Persian *bahāna*, "a pretext"; Marwadi gives it the -ो ending of a masculine noun.
