Lesson 1 of 5.
വേണ്ടി (vēṇṭi) — for the sake of, on behalf of.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for on this side, then the Malayalam for at the foot of.

You'll want to know: വേണ്ടി.
വേണ്ടി — vēṇṭi — "for the sake of, on behalf of".

The word, taken apart.
വേണ്ടി (vēṇṭi) is native Dravidian, a form of the wanting verb: "having been wanted"; DEDR 5528 lists the root with Tamil vēṇṭu.

Lesson 3 of 5.
പ്രശ്നം (praśnaṁ) — a problem.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for for the sake of, then the Malayalam for instead.

You'll want to know: പ്രശ്നം.
പ്രശ്നം — praśnaṁ — "a problem".

The word, taken apart.
പ്രശ്നം (praśnaṁ) from Sanskrit praśna, "question", taken over whole as a tatsama, with Malayalam's noun ending -ṁ (CDIAL 8818). A question became a problem.

അനുസരിച്ച് (anusariccŭ) is a Malayalam verb form, "having followed", built on Sanskrit anusaraṇa, "following, going after".
