Persian, chapter 442: Putting it right.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
عذرخواهی کردن ('ozrkhâhi kardan) — to apologize.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to back down, then the Persian for dissatisfied.

You'll want to know: عذرخواهی کردن.
عذرخواهی کردن ('ozrkhâhi kardan) — "to apologize".
مدیر هتل از ما عذرخواهی کرد. Modir-e hotel az mâ 'ozrkhâhi kard. The hotel manager apologized to us.
'ozr 'excuse' + khâh 'asking' + -i + kardan. Note the letter ذ, said z.

The word, taken apart.
'ozrkhâhi kardan is built from 'ozr 'an excuse', a loan usually traced to Arabic ʿuḏr, native khâh, the present stem of khâstan 'to ask, to want', and kardan 'to do'.

Lesson 2 of 5.
جبران کردن (jobrân kardan) — to make up for, to make amends for.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for dissatisfied, then the Persian for to apologize.

You'll want to know: جبران کردن.
جبران کردن (jobrân kardan) — "to make up for, to make amends for".
هتل اشتباهش را با یک شب مجانی جبران کرد. Hotel eshtebâhash râ bâ yek shab-e majâni jobrân kard. The hotel made up for its mistake with a free night.
Also to repay a kindness: lotf-e shomâ râ jobrân mikonam.

The word, taken apart.
jobrân kardan is built from jobrân 'compensation', a loan usually traced to Arabic jubrān 'mending, setting right', and kardan 'to do'. EtymDB does not list jobrân.

Lesson 4 of 5.
پس گرفتن (pas gereftan) — to take back; to get back (money).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to make up for, then the Persian for to exchange.

You'll want to know: پس گرفتن.
پس گرفتن (pas gereftan) — "to take back; to get back (money)".
پول بلیت را پس گرفتیم. Pul-e bilit râ pas gereftim. We got the ticket money back.
pas 'back' + gereftan. The opposite of pas dâdan 'to give back'.

The word, taken apart.
pas gereftan is built from pas 'back, behind', inherited from Middle Persian pas (EtymDB), and the native verb gereftan 'to take', usually traced to Old Persian grab-.

Lesson 5 of 5.
برگرداندن (bargardândan) — to give back, to return (something).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to exchange, then the Persian for to take back.

You'll want to know: برگرداندن.
برگرداندن (bargardândan) — "to give back, to return (something)".
فروشنده پول ما را برگرداند. Forushande pul-e mâ râ bargardând. The shopkeeper gave our money back.
The causative of bargashtan 'to go back'. Present stem bargardân-: barmigardânam.

The word, taken apart.
bargardândan is built from bar 'up, back' and gardândan 'to turn something', the causative built on gard-, the present stem of gashtan 'to turn', usually traced to Middle Persian gaštan.
