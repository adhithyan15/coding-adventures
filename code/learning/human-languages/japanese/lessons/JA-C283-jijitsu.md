## You'll want to know: じじつ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C283-HAPPYOU283-05]; assesses=[] -->

**じじつ** — *jijitsu* — "a fact; the truth of the matter".

> **けいさつは じじつを しらべています。** — *keisatsu wa jijitsu o shirabete imasu* — "The police are looking into the facts."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**じじつ** (*jijitsu*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *ji*, "matter" (Middle Chinese *dzriH*) and *jitsu*, "reality" (*zyit*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
