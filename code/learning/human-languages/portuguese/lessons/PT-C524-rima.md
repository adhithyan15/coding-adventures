## You'll want to know: a rima
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C524-POESIA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**a rima** — "rhyme".

> As canções infantis têm muitas rimas.
>
> *Children's songs have lots of rhymes.*

The verb is *rimar*: *amor rima com dor*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*rima* goes back to Medieval Latin *rithmus* (EtymDB), which is usually traced to Greek *rhythmós* "measured flow, rhythm".
