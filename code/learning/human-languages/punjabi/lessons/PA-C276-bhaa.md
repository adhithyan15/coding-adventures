## You'll want to know: ਭਾਅ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C276-PRICE276-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ਭਾਅ** — *bhāa* — "a rate; the market rate".

> ਅੱਜ ਸੇਬਾਂ ਦਾ ਭਾਅ ਕੀ ਹੈ?
>
> *ajj sebāṁ dā bhāa kī hai?*
>
> What is the rate for apples today?

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਭਾਅ** (*bhāa*) is filed by CDIAL under Sanskrit *bhāva*, "being, state"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *bhāu*, "market rate", there (CDIAL 9475).
