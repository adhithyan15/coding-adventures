## HL-C332 — Hindi speak declares its taught stem

After the wanting repair, **बोल** is the highest-ranked independent modern
Hindi form in the glossed-but-never-taught queue. Chapter 5 already explains
how to remove **-ना** from **बोलना**, identifies *bol-* as the stem, and asks
learners to recall it. Chapter 70 then prints **बोल** repeatedly in complete
ability constructions and contrasts the bare stem with the full infinitive.

The old metadata declared only infinitive **बोलना**, leaving the explicitly
taught and repeatedly used stem invisible to reports and downstream cards.

Declare **बोलना / बोल** as the lesson headword, print the Devanagari stem in
the grammar explanation and guided practice, and regenerate the derived Hindi
artifacts. This is a metadata repair for existing teaching, not a new
vocabulary claim.
