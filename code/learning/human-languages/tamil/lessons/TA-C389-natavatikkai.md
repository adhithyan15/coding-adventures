# நடவடிக்கை (naṭavaṭikkai) — a course of action; a measure

## You'll want to know: நடவடிக்கை
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C389-CEYALPATUTTU389-04]; assesses=[] -->

**நடவடிக்கை** — *naṭavaṭikkai* — "a course of action; a measure".

> இந்தப் பிரச்சினைக்கு அரசு உடனே நடவடிக்கை எடுக்க வேண்டும்.
>
> *intap piracciṉaikku aracu uṭaṉē naṭavaṭikkai eṭukka vēṇṭum.*
>
> The government must take action on this problem at once.

நடவடிக்கை எடு, to take action, to take steps.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**நடவடிக்கை** (*naṭavaṭikkai*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 3582 lists it in the family of *naṭa*, "to walk, proceed, happen", meaning "conduct, proceeding".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
