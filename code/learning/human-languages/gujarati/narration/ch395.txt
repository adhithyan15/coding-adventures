Gujarati, chapter 395: Berths and Reservations.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
બર્થ (barth) — a berth (on a train).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to slide (something), then the Gujarati for to catch hold of.

You'll want to know: બર્થ.
બર્થ — barth — "a berth (on a train)".
મારી બર્થ બારી પાસે છે. mārī barth bārī pāse che. My berth is by the window.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
બર્થ (barth) is borrowed from English berth, the bed on a night train.

What you've built.
One more word for getting around.

Lesson 3 of 5.
ભાથું (bhāthũ) — food packed for a journey.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a berth (on a train), then the Gujarati for a reservation (of a seat).

You'll want to know: ભાથું.
ભાથું — bhāthũ — "food packed for a journey".
માતાએ મુસાફરી માટે થેપલાંનું ભાથું બાંધ્યું. mātāe musāpharī māṭe theplā̃nũ bhāthũ bā̃dhyũ. Mother packed thepla as food for the journey.
A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
ભાથું (bhāthũ) seems to belong with ભાત, cooked rice, which CDIAL lists under Sanskrit bhaktá, "a meal, food" (CDIAL 9331), beside Punjabi bhattā, "a travelling allowance"; CDIAL gives no form for ભાથું itself, so the link is hedged.

Lesson 4 of 5.
ઠાંસવું (ṭhā̃svũ) — to cram; to stuff (into).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a reservation (of a seat), then the Gujarati for food packed for a journey.

You'll want to know: ઠાંસવું.
ઠાંસવું — ṭhā̃svũ — "to cram; to stuff (into)".
તેણે બધાં કપડાં એક થેલીમાં ઠાંસ્યાં. teṇe badhā̃ kapḍā̃ ek thelīmā̃ ṭhā̃syā̃. He crammed all the clothes into one bag.
Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે કપડાં ઠાંસ્યાં, teṇe kapḍā̃ ṭhā̃syā̃.

The word, taken apart.
ઠાંસવું (ṭhā̃svũ) goes back to ṭhass, "to press down", a form CDIAL reconstructs; CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb, "to cram" (CDIAL 5499).

Lesson 5 of 5.
બેસાડવું (besāḍvũ) — to seat (someone); to make (someone) sit.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for food packed for a journey, then the Gujarati for to cram.

You'll want to know: બેસાડવું.
બેસાડવું — besāḍvũ — "to seat (someone); to make (someone) sit".
માતા બાળકને બારી પાસે બેસાડે છે. mātā bāḷakne bārī pāse besāḍe che. The mother seats the child by the window.
Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે બાળકને બેસાડ્યું, teṇe bāḷakne besāḍyũ.

The word, taken apart.
બેસાડવું (besāḍvũ) is the causative partner of બેસવું, "to sit", which goes back to Sanskrit upaviśati, "sits down" (CDIAL 2245): to make someone sit.
