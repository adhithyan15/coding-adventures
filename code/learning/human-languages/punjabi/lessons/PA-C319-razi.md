## You'll want to know: ਰਾਜ਼ੀ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C319-HEALTH319-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ਰਾਜ਼ੀ** — *rāzī* — "well again after an illness".

> ਮੈਂ ਬੀਮਾਰ ਸੀ ਪਰ ਹੁਣ ਮੈਂ ਰਾਜ਼ੀ ਹਾਂ।
>
> *maiṁ bīmār sī par huṇ maiṁ rāzī hāṁ.*
>
> I was ill, but now I am well again.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਰਾਜ਼ੀ** (*rāzī*) is a loan, usually traced to Persian *rāzī*, "content, satisfied"; how it came to mean "well again" is not traced in the sources used here.
