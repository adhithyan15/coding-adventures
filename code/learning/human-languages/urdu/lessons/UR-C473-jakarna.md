# جکڑنا (jakaṛnā) — to grip tight; to stiffen

## You'll want to know: جکڑنا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C473-ACHE473-03]; assesses=[] -->

**جکڑنا** — *jakaṛnā* — "to grip tight; to stiffen".

> سردی میں میری کمر جکڑ گئی۔
>
> *sardī meṅ merī kamar jakaṛ gaī.*
>
> My back stiffened up in the cold.

Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے رسی جکڑی, *us ne rassī jakṛī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**جکڑنا** (*jakaṛnā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *jakaṛnā*, "to tighten, to bind", under Sanskrit *yatá*, "restrained" (CDIAL 10400).
