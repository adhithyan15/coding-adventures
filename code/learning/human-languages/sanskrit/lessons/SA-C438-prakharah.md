# प्रखरः (prakharaḥ) — scorching; very hot

## You'll want to know: प्रखरः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C438-NIDAGHA438-03]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रखरः** — *prakharaḥ* — "scorching; very hot".

> निदाघे आतपः प्रखरः भवति।
>
> *nidāghe ātapaḥ prakharaḥ bhavati.*
>
> In the hot season the sunshine is scorching.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रखरः** (*prakharaḥ*) is built from *pra*, "very", and *khara*, "hard, sharp"; MW gives *prakhara* as very hard or rough and very hot (MW).
