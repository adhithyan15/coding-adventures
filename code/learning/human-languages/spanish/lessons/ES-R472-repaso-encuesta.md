## Grammar Lens: three of the five were already inside words you hold
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-C472-ENCUESTA-01, ES-LEX-C472-ENCUESTA-04] -->

This chapter minted very little. Most of it was recombination.

| word | what it is made of |
|---|---|
| usuario | **usar** plus *-ario* |
| lectura | **leer** plus *-ura* |
| encuesta | the same Latin verb as **querer** |

*La factura* had even printed *lectura* in a table already, as its own example
of the *-ura* ending, and moved on. So the work here was less learning words
than being handed ones the book had been using in front of you.

## The words, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-C472-ENCUESTA-03, ES-LEX-C472-ENCUESTA-05] -->

The two that were genuinely new both hide a picture.

| word | underneath |
|---|---|
| surgir | *surgere*, to **rise** — so it wants *de* |
| copiar | *copia*, **abundance** — a copy made there be more |

And the one worth keeping: **encuesta** is *inquirere*, **in-** plus
*quaerere*, to seek — and *quaerere* is **querer**. Wanting and asking are the
same Latin verb, which is a better joke than either language intended.
