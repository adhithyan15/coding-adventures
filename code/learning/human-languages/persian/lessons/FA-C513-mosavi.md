## You'll want to know: مساوی
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C513-SCORES-02]; assesses=[] -->

**مساوی** (*mosâvi*) — "equal; level (a draw)".

> بازی دیشب یک یک مساوی شد.
>
> *bâzi-ye dishab yek yek mosâvi shod.*
>
> Last night's match was a one-all draw.

*mosâvi shodan* — to end level. In sums, *mosâvi* is 'equals'.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*mosâvi* is a loan, usually traced to Arabic *musāwī*, 'equal'. EtymDB has no entry for it.
