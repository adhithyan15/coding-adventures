## You'll want to know: terribilis, terribile
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C496-PAV-05]; assesses=[] -->

**terribilis, terribile** — "frightful, terrible".

> Leō vōcem terribilem habet.
>
> *The lion has a terrible voice.*

Two endings. From *terreō* — to frighten. English *terrible*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**terribilis** is built on *terreō* "to frighten" with *-ibilis* (Lewis & Short); EtymDB traces *terreō* to a Proto-Indo-European root meaning "to tremble, to frighten". English *terrible* comes from it.
