> ब

**ba** — the *b* of English *bin*, with **no** puff of air.

Now put it beside the aspirated letter from a moment ago. Same family, same basic
skeleton, one extra stroke on the aspirated one. That is the pattern this script
uses across the whole consonant chart, and seeing it once here means you can guess
at pairs you have not met.

Two letters, one distinction, and English speakers hear it as no distinction at
all — which is why it is worth the whole lesson.
