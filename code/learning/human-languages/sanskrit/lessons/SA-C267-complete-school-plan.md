## Guided Practice

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[SA-LEX-C83-SCHOOL-01, SA-LEX-C83-SCHOOL-02, SA-LEX-C83-SCHOOL-03, SA-LEX-C83-SCHOOL-04, SA-LEX-C83-SCHOOL-05, SA-LEX-C140-READ140-02, SA-LEX-C168-SCH168-01, SA-LEX-C169-SCH169-02, SA-LEX-C255-SCH255-04, SA-LEX-C255-SCH255-05, SA-LEX-C18-ASK-01, SA-LEX-C139-FUT139-02, SA-LEX-C139-FUT139-04, SA-VERB-C264-KARISYAMI-01, SA-VERB-C264-PATHISYAMI-02, SA-PATTERN-C267-SCHOOL-PLAN-01] -->

First name the setting and its useful records: **विद्यालयः**, school;
**कक्षा**, classroom; **समयसारणी**, timetable; **अंकः**, mark; **उत्तरम्**,
answer. Name the people: **छात्रः**, student; **अध्यापकः**, teacher.

Then say the working plan:

1. **परीक्षा कदा भविष्यति?** — When will the exam be?
2. **श्वः परीक्षा भविष्यति।** — The exam will be tomorrow.
3. **गृहकार्यं करिष्यामि।** — I will do the homework.
4. **पाठं पठिष्यामि।** — I will study the lesson.
