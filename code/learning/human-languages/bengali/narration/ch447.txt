Bengali, chapter 447: Regret.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
আফসোস (āphsos) — regret; a pity.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for to breathe a sigh of relief, then the Bengali for to cope with.

You'll want to know: আফসোস.
আফসোস — āphsos — "regret; a pity".
ছুটিতে গ্রামে যেতে পারলাম না, এটাই আমার আফসোস। chhuṭite grāme jete pārlām nā, eṭāi āmār āphsos. I couldn't go to the village for the holidays, and that is my one regret.
আফসোস (āphsos) করা, to say you regret something; আফসোস (āphsos) হওয়া, to feel regret.

The word, taken apart.
আফসোস (āphsos) is usually traced to Persian afsōs, a cry of "alas" that also means regret.

Lesson 2 of 5.
অনুশোচনা (ônushochônā) — remorse; repentance.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for to cope with, then the Bengali for regret.

You'll want to know: অনুশোচনা.
অনুশোচনা — ônushochônā — "remorse; repentance".
মিথ্যে বলার পরে অরুণের অনুশোচনা হল। mitthe bôlār pôre ôruner ônushochônā hôlô. After telling the lie, Arun felt remorse.

The word, taken apart.
অনুশোচনা (ônushochônā) is a tatsama, Sanskrit anuśocana, "sorrow, repentance" (Monier-Williams), grief that comes after the deed.

অনুতপ্ত (ônutôptô) is a tatsama, Sanskrit anutapta, "heated" and so "filled with regret" (Monier-Williams): burning afterwards.

You'll want to know: পস্তানো.
পস্তানো — pôstāno — "to rue; to regret (something done)".
সস্তা জুতো কিনে পরে পস্তালাম। sôstā juto kine pôre pôstālām. I bought cheap shoes and regretted it later.
Past (he/she …): তিনি পরে পস্তালেন, tini pôre pôstālen.

The word, taken apart.
পস্তানো (pôstāno) is listed by CDIAL for Bengali under a reconstructed Sanskrit paścōttāpa, "burning afterwards", that is, remorse (CDIAL 8010).

স্বীকার করা (svīkār kôrā) is Sanskrit svīkāra, "making one's own, accepting" (Monier-Williams), with kôrā, "to do" (CDIAL 2814).
