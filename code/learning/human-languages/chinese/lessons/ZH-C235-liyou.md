## You'll want to know: lǐyóu
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C235-REASON235-03]; assesses=[] -->

*lǐyóu* — "reason; grounds".

> *nǐ yǒu shénme lǐyóu* — What are your grounds?

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*lǐyóu* is built from *lǐ* 'reason' and *yóu* 'cause', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *liX* and *yuw* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to put in order' and explains it, after an older book, as working jade.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons.
