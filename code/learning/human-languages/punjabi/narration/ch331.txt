Punjabi, chapter 331: Uncles and Aunts.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ਚਾਚਾ (chāchā) — a father's younger brother.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for a husband's younger brother's wife, then the Punjabi for a husband's elder brother's wife.

You'll want to know: ਚਾਚਾ.
ਚਾਚਾ — chāchā — "a father's younger brother".
ਮੇਰਾ ਚਾਚਾ ਖੇਤ ਵਿੱਚ ਕੰਮ ਕਰਦਾ ਹੈ। merā chāchā khet vich kamm kardā hai. My father's younger brother works in the fields.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ਚਾਚਾ (chāchā) is inherited from a reconstructed cācca, "uncle"; CDIAL lists Punjabi cācā, "father's younger brother", under it (CDIAL 4734).

Lesson 3 of 5.

Warm-up.

ਗਰਮੀ ਦੀ ਛੁੱਟੀ ਵਿੱਚ ਅਸੀਂ ਮਾਮੇ ਦੇ ਘਰ ਗਏ। garmī dī chhuṭṭī vich asīṁ māme de ghar gae. In the summer holidays we went to my mother's brother's house.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ਮਾਮਾ (māmā) is inherited from Sanskrit māma, "uncle", a nursery word; CDIAL lists Punjabi māmmā under it (CDIAL 10055).
