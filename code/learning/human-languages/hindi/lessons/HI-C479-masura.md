## You'll want to know: मसूड़ा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C479-MUNH479-02]; assesses=[] -->

**मसूड़ा** — *masūṛā* — "a gum (of the teeth)".

> मीरा के मसूड़ों में सूजन है।
>
> *Mīrā ke masūṛoṁ meṁ sūjan hai.*
>
> Mira's gums are swollen.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**मसूड़ा** (*masūṛā*) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Hindi *masūṛā*, "the gums", under *\*māṁsapuṭaka*, a form it reconstructs from Sanskrit *māṁsa*, "flesh" (CDIAL 9984).
