## You'll want to know: してつ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C376-SHINKANSEN376-03]; assesses=[] -->

**してつ** — *shitetsu* — "a private railway".

> **この まちには してつの えきが ふたつ あります。** — *kono machi ni wa shitetsu no eki ga futatsu arimasu* — "This town has two stations on the private railway."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**してつ** (*shitetsu*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *shi*, "private" (Middle Chinese *sij*) and *tetsu*, "iron" (*thet*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
