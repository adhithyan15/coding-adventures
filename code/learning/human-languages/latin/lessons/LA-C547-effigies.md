## You'll want to know: effigiēs, effigiēī
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C547-IMAG-02]; assesses=[] -->

**effigiēs, effigiēī** — "a likeness, effigy".

> Effigiēs avī in ātriō stat.
>
> *A likeness of grandfather stands in the hall.*

Feminine, fifth declension, from *effingō* — to shape, portray. English *effigy*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**effigiēs** is built on *effingō* "to form, portray", from *ex-* and *fingō* "to shape" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short). English *effigy* comes from it.
