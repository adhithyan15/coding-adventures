## You'll want to know: অতীত
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C303-SEDIN303-02]; assesses=[] -->

**অতীত** — *ôtīt* — "the past".

> দাদু প্রায়ই অতীতের গল্প বলেন।
>
> *dādu prāyi ôtīter gôlpo bôlen.*
>
> Grandfather often talks about the past.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**অতীত** (*ôtīt*) is Sanskrit *atīta*, 'gone by, past' (Monier-Williams), from *ati-*, 'beyond', and *√i*, 'to go'.
