Gujarati, chapter 76: A Lot, All, Slowly, Quickly, Again.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ઘણું (ghaṇũ) — a lot, much.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for outside, then the Gujarati for between.

You'll want to know: ઘણું.
ઘણું (ghaṇũ) — "a lot, much".

The word, taken apart.
ઘણું (ghaṇũ) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from ghana, "compact, firm, dense" (CDIAL 4424).

What you've built.
One more word for a lot, all, slowly, quickly, again.

You'll want to know: ધીમે.
ધીમે (dhīme) — "slowly". Ask for it when someone speaks fast.

The word, taken apart.
ધીમે (dhīme) is the adverb of dhīmũ, "slow", which CDIAL lists under a reconstructed dhīmma, "slow" (CDIAL 6814).

Lesson 5 of 5.

You'll want to know: ફરી.
ફરી (pharī) — "again".

The word, taken apart.
ફરી (pharī) comes from phirati, "moves, turns", a form CDIAL reconstructs (CDIAL 9078). It is the old absolutive, "having turned", used as an adverb.
