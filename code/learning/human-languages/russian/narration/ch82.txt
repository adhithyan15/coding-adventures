Russian, chapter 82: This, This, That, That, Such.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
этот (étot) — this (masculine).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a park, then the Russian for a taxi.

You'll want to know: этот.
этот (étot) — "this (masculine)".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: étot]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: étot, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say étot]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Russian for a park, then the Russian for a taxi, then say étot again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does этот (étot) mean? ("This (masculine)".) Say it once more.
