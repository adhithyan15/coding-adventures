Telugu, chapter 344: Saying Goodbye.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
సంతాపం (santāpaṁ) — condolence; sympathy.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for narrowly, then the Telugu for to get out.

You'll want to know: సంతాపం.
సంతాపం — santāpaṁ — "condolence; sympathy".
అందరూ ఆ కుటుంబానికి సంతాపం తెలిపారు. andarū ā kuṭumbāniki santāpaṁ telipāru. Everyone offered condolences to that family.
సంతాపం (santāpaṁ) తెలుపు, to express condolence; the one who receives it takes -కి. It is offered to a family in దుఃఖం, grief.

The word, taken apart.
సంతాపం (santāpaṁ) is borrowed from Sanskrit saṁtāpa, "burning heat", and then "affliction, remorse" (CDIAL 12885).

Lesson 2 of 5.
మరణం (maraṇaṁ) — death.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to get out, then the Telugu for condolence.

You'll want to know: మరణం.
మరణం — maraṇaṁ — "death".
ఆయన మరణం గురించి పత్రికలో వార్త వచ్చింది. āyana maraṇaṁ guriñci patrikalō vārta vaccindi. The news of his death appeared in the paper.
A formal word: in speech people say చనిపోవు, to die.

The word, taken apart.
మరణం (maraṇaṁ) is borrowed from Sanskrit maraṇa, "dying, death" (CDIAL 9869), from the root mṛ, "to die".

Lesson 3 of 5.
తుదిశ్వాస (tudiśvāsa) — one's last breath.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for condolence, then the Telugu for death.

You'll want to know: తుదిశ్వాస.
తుదిశ్వాస — tudiśvāsa — "one's last breath".
తాతగారు ఆసుపత్రిలో తుదిశ్వాస విడిచారు. tātagāru āsupatrilō tudiśvāsa viḍicāru. Grandfather breathed his last in hospital.
తుదిశ్వాస (tudiśvāsa) విడుచు, to let go the last breath: a gentle way to say died.

The word, taken apart.
తుదిశ్వాస (tudiśvāsa) is a mixed compound: native tudi, "the end" (DEDR 3314), and Sanskrit śvāsa, "breath" (CDIAL 12769).

Before the new one: say the Telugu for death, then the Telugu for one's last breath.

You'll want to know: అంతిమయాత్ర.
అంతిమయాత్ర — antimayātra — "a funeral procession".
అంతిమయాత్రలో వందల మంది నడిచారు. antimayātralō vandala mandi naḍicāru. Hundreds walked in the funeral procession.
అంతిమ, last, and యాత్ర, a journey: the last journey.

The word, taken apart.
అంతిమయాత్ర (antimayātra) is borrowed from Sanskrit: antima, "last" (Monier-Williams), and yātrā, "a journey" (CDIAL 10456).
