## You'll want to know: नीतिः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C336-NITI336-01]; assesses=[] -->

**नीतिः** — *nītiḥ* — "wise conduct; a moral".

> पितामहः बालाय नीतिं कथयति।
>
> *pitāmahaḥ bālāya nītiṃ kathayati.*
>
> Grandfather tells the boy about wise conduct.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**नीतिः** (*nītiḥ*) comes from the root √nī, "to lead", as in *nayati*; MW gives *nīti* as guidance, management, and right or wise conduct, prudence (MW).
