# జన్మించు (janmiñcu) — to take birth; to be born

## You'll want to know: జన్మించు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C334-JANANAM334-04]; assesses=[] -->

**జన్మించు** — *janmiñcu* — "to take birth; to be born".

> నేను ఒక చిన్న పల్లెలో జన్మించాను.
>
> *nēnu oka cinna pallelō janmiñcānu.*
>
> I was born in a small village.

Past (I …): జన్మించాను, *janmiñcānu*.

A formal partner of పుట్టు: life stories and the news use జన్మించు.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**జన్మించు** (*janmiñcu*) is built on Sanskrit *janman*, "birth" (CDIAL 5113), with the Telugu verb ending *-iñcu*.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
