## You'll want to know: la cicatriz
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C734-HERIDA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**la cicatriz** — "the scar".

> Tengo una cicatriz en la mano.
>
> *I have a scar on my hand.*

Plural *las cicatrices*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*cicatriz* comes from Latin *cicātrīx*, *cicātrīcem* "scar" (EtymDB).
