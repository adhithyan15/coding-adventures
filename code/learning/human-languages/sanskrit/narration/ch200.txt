Lesson 1 of 5.
पठनम् (paṭhanam) — reading.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for mathematics, then the Sanskrit for science.

You'll want to know: पठनम्.
पठनम् — paṭhanam — "reading".

The word, taken apart.
paṭhana is built on the root √paṭh, "to read aloud, recite" (MW; CDIAL 7713), the root of paṭhati, "reads", which you have already met. The verb lives on as Hindi paṛhnā, "to read" (CDIAL 7712).

Lesson 2 of 5.
श्रवणम् (śravaṇam) — listening.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for science, then the Sanskrit for reading.

You'll want to know: श्रवणम्.
श्रवणम् — śravaṇam — "listening".

The word, taken apart.
śravaṇa is built on the root √śru, "to hear" (MW), with the action suffix -ana. The root is inherited from Indo-European: MW compares Greek klyō, "I hear", and English loud. The same root gives śruti, "what is heard", the name for the Veda.

Lesson 3 of 5.
भाषणम् (bhāṣaṇam) — a speech.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for reading, then the Sanskrit for listening.

You'll want to know: भाषणम्.
भाषणम् — bhāṣaṇam — "a speech".

The word, taken apart.
bhāṣaṇa is built on the root √bhāṣ, "to speak", with the action suffix -ana (MW): "the act of speaking", and so a speech. It shares its root with bhāṣā, "language", and Hindi took the word over as bhāṣaṇ.

Lesson 4 of 5.
चर्चा (carcā) — a discussion.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for listening, then the Sanskrit for a speech.

You'll want to know: चर्चा.
चर्चा — carcā — "a discussion".

The word, taken apart.
carcā first meant the repeating of a word in Vedic recitation (CDIAL 4695). It goes with the verb carcayati, which MW glosses "to repeat a word" and "to talk over, discuss". MW gives no older source for the root, and Telugu and Malayalam borrowed the noun.

Lesson 5 of 5.
विवादः (vivādaḥ) — a dispute.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a speech, then the Sanskrit for a discussion.

You'll want to know: विवादः.
विवादः — vivādaḥ — "a dispute".

The word, taken apart.
vivāda is built on vi-, "apart", and the root √vad, "to speak" (MW), which you met early on in vadāmi, "I speak". People who speak apart from each other are disputing.
