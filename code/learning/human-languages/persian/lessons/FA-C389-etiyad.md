## You'll want to know: اعتیاد
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C389-DAVTALAB-05]; assesses=[] -->

**اعتیاد** (*e'tiyâd*) — "addiction".

> اعتیاد به موبایل بین نوجوانها زیاد است.
>
> *e'tiyâd be mobâyl beyn-e nojavânhâ ziyâd ast.*
>
> Addiction to mobile phones is common among teenagers.

*E'tiyâd be* — addiction to. *Mo'tâd* — addicted.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**اعتیاد** comes from Arabic *i'tiyād* "habit, familiarity" (EtymDB); in Persian a habit you cannot leave became an addiction.
