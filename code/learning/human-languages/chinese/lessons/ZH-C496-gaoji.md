## You'll want to know: 高级
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C496-LEVELS496-02]; assesses=[] -->

**高级** — *gāojí* — "advanced; high-level".

> **我学了三年汉语现在学高级汉语** *wǒ xué le sān nián Hànyǔ xiànzài xué gāojí Hànyǔ* — I've studied Chinese for three years, and now I'm doing advanced Chinese.

The new character, in pieces: 级 — six strokes in two pieces: on the left the silk piece of 红 *hóng*, three strokes; on the right three strokes, a long stroke falling left, a stroke that runs right and turns twice, and a long stroke falling right. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**高级** (*gāojí*) is built from 高 'high' and 级 'a grade', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *kaw* and *kip* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'a grade' and, after an older dictionary, 'the order of silk threads'.
