# కూల్చు (kūlcu) — to demolish; to bring down

## You'll want to know: కూల్చు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C369-RAJABHAVANAM369-04]; assesses=[] -->

**కూల్చు** — *kūlcu* — "to demolish; to bring down".

> వాళ్ళు పాత భవనాన్ని కూల్చారు.
>
> *vāḷḷu pāta bhavanānni kūlcāru.*
>
> They demolished the old building.

Past (I …): కూల్చాను, *kūlcānu*.

The causative of కూలు, to fall: to make something fall.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**కూల్చు** (*kūlcu*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 1907 lists Telugu *kūlcu*, "to cause to fall, knock down", beside *kūlu*, "to fall down".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
