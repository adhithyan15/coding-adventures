Malayalam, chapter 196: So far, Still, Finally, On time, Free time.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ഇതുവരെ (ituvare) — so far, until now.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for quickly, then the Malayalam for in advance.

You'll want to know: ഇതുവരെ.
ഇതുവരെ — ituvare — "so far, until now".

The word, taken apart.
ഇതുവരെ (ituvare) is native Dravidian, "up to this": itu, "this" (DEDR 410), and vare, "until", a form of the verb "to come" (DEDR 5270).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for still, then the Malayalam for finally.

കൃത്യസമയത്ത് (kṛtyasamayattŭ) is built from Sanskrit kṛtya, "proper to be done" (CDIAL 3428), and samaya, "time" (CDIAL 13185), with the Malayalam place ending: "at the proper time".
