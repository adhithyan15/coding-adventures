# Sanskrit pre-A1 mock rubric

This rubric scores either pre-A1 mock against the exact task envelope in
[`task-shapes/pre-a1.json`](../../task-shapes/pre-a1.json). Reading, listening,
writing, and speaking are separate 100-point papers. A learner passes the rung
only by earning at least 60/100 on **every** paper. A high score in one skill
never compensates for a failed skill.

## Reading — 100 points

<!-- mock-part:prea1-reading-signs-and-words -->
- signs and words: 40 points across 6 keyed items;
<!-- /mock-part:prea1-reading-signs-and-words -->
<!-- mock-part:prea1-reading-phrases-and-notices -->
- phrases and notices: 30 points across 4 keyed items;
<!-- /mock-part:prea1-reading-phrases-and-notices -->
<!-- mock-part:prea1-reading-personal-details -->
- personal details: 30 points across 4 keyed items.
<!-- /mock-part:prea1-reading-personal-details -->

Within each part, divide its points equally among keyed items and retain one
decimal place. Accept only the keyed meaning or situation. Reading aloud does
not earn reading credit. Pre-A1 text is deliberately unjoined; correct external
sandhi is accepted but never required.

## Listening — 100 points

<!-- mock-part:prea1-listening-sounds-and-words -->
- sounds and words: 40 points across 6 keyed items;
<!-- /mock-part:prea1-listening-sounds-and-words -->
<!-- mock-part:prea1-listening-greeting-responses -->
- greeting responses: 30 points across 4 keyed items;
<!-- /mock-part:prea1-listening-greeting-responses -->
<!-- mock-part:prea1-listening-personal-details -->
- personal details: 30 points across 4 keyed items.
<!-- /mock-part:prea1-listening-personal-details -->

Play every item twice at 70–90 words per minute with an eight-second answer
pause. Score only the learner's selected answer. Do not show a transcript,
romanization, or answer-bearing picture label.

## Writing — 100 points

<!-- mock-part:prea1-writing-delayed-recall -->
- delayed recall: 20 points, 10 per word;
<!-- /mock-part:prea1-writing-delayed-recall -->
<!-- mock-part:prea1-writing-dictation -->
- dictation: 40 points, 10 per item;
<!-- /mock-part:prea1-writing-dictation -->
<!-- mock-part:prea1-writing-bounded-production -->
- bounded independent production: 40 points, 20 per response.
<!-- /mock-part:prea1-writing-bounded-production -->

For each delayed-recall or dictation item award 4 points for a recognizable
ordered Devanagari sequence and continuous śirorekhā, 3 for required vowel
marks, 2 for the correct word ending (including the correct absence of visarga
or anusvāra), and 1 for a clean word boundary. For each independent response
award 8 for answering the stated
purpose, 6 for recognizable Devanagari word formation, 4 for required marks,
and 2 for boundary control. A romanized, traced, or visibly copied answer earns
zero because it is not independent writing evidence. Do not require external
sandhi at pre-A1; preserving unjoined word boundaries is correct.

## Speaking — 100 points

<!-- mock-part:prea1-speaking-greeting-and-identity -->
- greeting and identity: 30 points;
<!-- /mock-part:prea1-speaking-greeting-and-identity -->
<!-- mock-part:prea1-speaking-familiar-questions -->
- three familiar questions: 40 points;
<!-- /mock-part:prea1-speaking-familiar-questions -->
<!-- mock-part:prea1-speaking-short-request -->
- short request: 30 points.
<!-- /mock-part:prea1-speaking-short-request -->

For each part award 40% for completing the communicative purpose, 25% for
intelligibility to a trained speaker of the living spoken register, 20% for an
expected known word or chunk, and 15% for responding without an answer script.
The interlocutor may repeat once slowly without penalty. Accept a documented
pronunciation tradition when it is internally consistent. A memorized chunk is
valid at pre-A1; a support-language-only response is not Sanskrit speaking
evidence.

## Readiness boundary

These rubrics and keys make the project-defined mock executable. They do not by
themselves establish exam readiness. The repository must still record a clean
timed administration, qualified Sanskrit review, standard setting, and
book-only human validation before making a pass or learner-readiness claim.
`assessment.json` is active as a source-artifact contract; it records these
existing files without supplying any of that outstanding evidence.
