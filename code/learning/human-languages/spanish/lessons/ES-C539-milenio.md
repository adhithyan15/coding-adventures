## You'll want to know: el milenio
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C539-ANYO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**el milenio** — "the millennium".

> La ciudad tiene más de un milenio.
>
> *The city is more than a thousand years old.*

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*milenio* is a learned borrowing from New Latin *millennium*, built on *mīlle* 'a thousand' and *annus* 'year' (Lewis & Short).
