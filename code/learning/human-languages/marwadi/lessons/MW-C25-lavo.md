# लावो — bring it, on the page

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-SCRIPT-LAVO-01]; assesses=[MW-LEX-LAVO-01, MW-LEX-HAWA-01, MW-SCRIPT-HAWA-01, MW-SCRIPT-DIKHAVO-01] -->

[PAUSE 22s] Say *bring it*, then write wind and *show it*.

## You'll want to know
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MW-SCRIPT-LA-01, MW-SCRIPT-AA-MATRA-01, MW-SCRIPT-VA-01, MW-SCRIPT-O-MATRA-01, MW-SCRIPT-LO-01, MW-LEX-LO-01] -->

> **ला** + **वो** → **लावो** — *lāvo* — bring it

Three signs, none new. Its ending **वो** is the same one that closes
**दिखावो**. Write **लो** and **लावो** one under the other: same first letter,
and everything after it is what separates taking from bringing.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MW-SCRIPT-LAVO-01, MW-SCRIPT-LO-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: delayed-copy -->

Look at the pair, cover it, wait five seconds, and write both from their
meanings: first *take it*, then *bring it*.
