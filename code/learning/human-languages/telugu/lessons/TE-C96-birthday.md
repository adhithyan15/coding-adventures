# పుట్టినరోజు (puṭṭinarōju) — a birthday

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[TE-LEX-C96-NAMES-02, TE-LEX-C96-NAMES-03] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Telugu for a nickname, then the Telugu for age.

## You'll want to know: పుట్టినరోజు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C96-NAMES-04]; assesses=[] -->

**పుట్టినరోజు** (*puṭṭinarōju*) — "a birthday", literally "the day one was born".

**రోజు** is the day you know. On the day, say **పుట్టినరోజు శుభాకాంక్షలు** — "happy birthday",
with the good wishes you already have.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

A day to name, and a wish to make on it.
