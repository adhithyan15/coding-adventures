## You'll want to know: rédiger
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C279-REDIGER-01]; assesses=[] -->

**rédiger** — "to write, to draft (a text)".

> Le journaliste a rédigé son article en une heure.
>
> *The journalist wrote his article in an hour.*

For a text with a purpose: *rédiger une lettre, un rapport*. In daily speech, *écrire*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*rédiger* is a learned borrowing from Latin *redigere* "to bring back, to reduce to order" (EtymDB), from *red-* "back" and *agere* "to drive" (Lewis & Short).
