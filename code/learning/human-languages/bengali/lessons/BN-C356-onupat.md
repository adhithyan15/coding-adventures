## You'll want to know: অনুপাত
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C356-SHOTANGSHO356-02]; assesses=[] -->

**অনুপাত** — *ônupāt* — "a ratio; a proportion".

> ছেলে আর মেয়ের অনুপাত সমান।
>
> *chhele ār meyer ônupāt sômān.*
>
> The ratio of boys to girls is even.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**অনুপাত** (*ônupāt*) is Sanskrit *anupāta*, proportion in arithmetic (Monier-Williams).
