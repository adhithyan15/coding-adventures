## You'll want to know: le panneau
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C435-PANNEAUX-01]; assesses=[] -->

**le panneau** — "the road sign; the panel".

> Le panneau indique le centre-ville à deux kilomètres.
>
> *The sign shows the town centre two kilometres away.*

Plural *les panneaux*. *Tomber dans le panneau* — to fall for it.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*panneau* comes from Old French *panel* (EtymDB), from Vulgar Latin *pannellus*, a small *pannus* 'piece of cloth'.
