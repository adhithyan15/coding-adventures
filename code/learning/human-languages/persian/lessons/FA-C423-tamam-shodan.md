## You'll want to know: تمام شدن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C423-BENZIN-04]; assesses=[] -->

**تمام شدن** (*tamâm shodan*) — "to run out; to be finished, to end".

> وسط جاده بنزین ما تمام شد.
>
> *Vasat-e jâdde benzin-e mâ tamâm shod.*
>
> Our petrol ran out in the middle of the road.

*tamâm* 'complete, finished' + *shodan*. *Tamâm kardan* is to finish something.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*tamâm shodan* is built from *tamâm* 'complete, finished', borrowed from Arabic *tamām* 'complete' (EtymDB), and the native verb *shodan* 'to become'.
