Lesson 2 of 5.
One noun for a message — message or information communicated from one party to another.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Retrieve the familiar first and last words of the identity frame.

Grammar Lens: direct owner.
ਸੁਨੇਹਾ (sunehaa) — sunehā — message; information communicated from one party to another.
Hear and say the whole word before reading it. Keep it as one whole noun rather than inventing smaller Punjabi pieces. This lesson owns the word alone; the complete identity frame comes only after a separate retrieval.

The word, taken apart — where the trail runs out.
Punjabi University's Punjabipedia defines this exact word as information given from one party to another. It supports only the present-day message sense taught here. Source: Punjabi University Punjabipedia.
The exact history of modern written ਸੁਨੇਹਾ (sunehaa) remains unresolved in the sources used here. For a historical comparison only, Turner's CDIAL entry 12904 groups Punjabi sanehā̃ with Sanskrit saṁdēśa, “message, information.” The source does not document the intermediate changes into this modern spelling. This historical comparison is not a letter-by-letter derivation and does not create a productive Punjabi rule: keep the present-day Punjabi noun whole. Source: Turner, CDIAL 12904.

Lesson 4 of 5.
Put the new noun into one identity frame — this is a message.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Retrieve ਇਹ, then the new noun, then ਹੈ.

Grammar Lens.
ਇਹ ਸੁਨੇਹਾ ਹੈ (eh sunehaa hai) — eh sunehā hai — this is a message.
Keep the new whole noun between familiar ਇਹ and ਹੈ. Familiar ਹੈ stays last in the already-owned identity frame. This chapter teaches only this one label; it does not add an inflection rule or a send-message construction.
