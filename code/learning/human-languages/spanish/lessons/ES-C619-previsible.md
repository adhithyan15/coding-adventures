## You'll want to know: previsible
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C619-QUIZA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**previsible** — "predictable, foreseeable".

> Era algo previsible.
>
> *It was something you could see coming.*

The opposite is *imprevisible*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*previsible* is built on *prever*, a learned borrowing from Latin *praevidere* 'to foresee', from *videre* 'to see' (EtymDB).
