## You'll want to know: వ్యాధి
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C513-VYADHI513-01]; assesses=[] -->

**వ్యాధి** — *vyādhi* — "a disorder; a disease".

> ఆ వ్యాధికి ఇంకా మందు లేదు.
>
> *ā vyādhiki iṅkā mandu lēdu.*
>
> There is still no cure for that disease.

More formal than రోగం or జబ్బు; doctors and the news say వ్యాధి.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**వ్యాధి** (*vyādhi*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *vyādhi*, "disease" (CDIAL 12202).
