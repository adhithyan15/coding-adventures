## You'll want to know: مشتريات
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C459-MIZANIYYA459-04]; assesses=[] -->

**مشتريات** — *mushtarayāt* — "purchases; the shopping".

> وضعت المشتريات في السيارة.
>
> *waḍaʿtu al-mushtarayāt fī as-sayyāra.*
>
> I put the shopping in the car.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**مشتريات** (*mushtarayāt*) is built on the native Arabic root *sh-r-y*, "to buy, to sell" (Lane). It is a plural of the Form VIII passive participle: things bought.
