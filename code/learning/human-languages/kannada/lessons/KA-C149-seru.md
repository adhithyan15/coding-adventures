# ಸೇರು (sēru) — to join, to reach

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[KA-LEX-C148-ACT148-05, KA-LEX-C149-ACT149-01, KA-GRAMMAR-C108-DESTINATION-GE-06] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Kannada for to jump and to get down. Recall the destination in **ನಾನು ಶಾಲೆಗೆ ಹೋಗುತ್ತೇನೆ** — “I go to school.”

## You'll want to know: ಸೇರು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C149-ACT149-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸೇರು** — *sēru* — "to join, to reach".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸೇರು** (*sēru*) is native Dravidian, one word with Tamil *cēr* and Telugu *cēru* (DEDR 2814). DEDR lists Kannada *sēr*, "to approach, reach, join".
