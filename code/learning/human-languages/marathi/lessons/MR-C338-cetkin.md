# चेटकीण (ceṭkīṇ) — a witch

## You'll want to know: चेटकीण
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C338-RAKSHAS338-04]; assesses=[] -->

**चेटकीण** — *ceṭkīṇ* — "a witch".

> जंगलातल्या चेटकिणीने राजकन्येला झोपवलं.
>
> *jaṅglātalyā ceṭkiṇīne rājkanyelā jhopavlaṁ.*
>
> The witch of the forest put the princess to sleep.

Feminine.

The oblique is चेटकिणी-: चेटकिणीने, by the witch.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**चेटकीण** (*ceṭkīṇ*) is the Marathi word for a witch, with the feminine ending *-īṇ*; its origin is not settled.
