## You'll want to know: роль
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C520-SCREENACTING-03]; assesses=[] -->

**роль** (*rol*) — "a role, a part".

> Он играет главную роль в новом фильме.
>
> *On igráyet glávnuyu rol v nóvom fílme.*
>
> He plays the lead role in the new film.

Feminine; genitive роли.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*роль* was borrowed from French *rôle* and German *Rolle*, which go back to Latin *rotulus* 'a small roll, a scroll' (EtymDB), the actor's roll of lines.
