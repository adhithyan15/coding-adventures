## You'll want to know: જમાઈ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C491-JAMAI491-01]; assesses=[] -->

**જમાઈ** — *jamāī* — "a son-in-law".

> અમારા જમાઈ ઘણા નમ્ર છે.
>
> *amārā jamāī ghaṇā namra che.*
>
> Our son-in-law is very polite.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**જમાઈ** (*jamāī*) is inherited from Sanskrit *jāmātṛ*, "daughter's husband"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati word (CDIAL 5198).
