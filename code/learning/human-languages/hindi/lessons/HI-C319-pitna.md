# पीटना (pīṭnā) — to beat

## You'll want to know: पीटना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C319-MARPEET319-01]; assesses=[] -->

**पीटना** — *pīṭnā* — "to beat".

> दो आदमियों ने एक चोर को पीटा।
>
> *do ādmiyoṁ ne ek cor ko pīṭā.*
>
> Two men beat up a thief.

Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने ढोल पीटा, *usne ḍhol pīṭā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

goes back to Sanskrit *piṭṭayati*, to pound into a flat mass, beside which CDIAL reconstructs *\*piṭṭyatē*, "is struck" (CDIAL 8165). It is hitting again and again; you also **पीटना** a drum: **ढोल पीटना**.

One more word for telling what happened.
