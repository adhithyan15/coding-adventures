Kannada, chapter 348: The Pirate Treasure.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ನಿಧಿ (nidhi) — a treasure.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to mention, then the Kannada for a summary.

You'll want to know: ನಿಧಿ.
ನಿಧಿ — nidhi — "a treasure".
ಕಥೆಯಲ್ಲಿ ಹುಡುಗರಿಗೆ ಗುಹೆಯಲ್ಲಿ ನಿಧಿ ಸಿಕ್ಕಿತು. katheyalli huḍugarige guheyalli nidhi sikkitu. In the story the boys found a treasure in a cave.
Plural: ನಿಧಿಗಳು, nidhigaḷu.
Also a fund: ಕಲ್ಯಾಣ ನಿಧಿ (nidhi), a welfare fund.

The word, taken apart.
ನಿಧಿ (nidhi) is borrowed from Sanskrit nidhi, "setting down, a hoard" (CDIAL 7207), from ni-dhā, "to put down".

Lesson 2 of 5.

You'll want to know: ಕಡಲ್ಗಳ್ಳ.
ಕಡಲ್ಗಳ್ಳ — kaḍalgaḷḷa — "a pirate".
ಕಡಲ್ಗಳ್ಳರು ಹಡಗಿನ ಚಿನ್ನವನ್ನು ದೋಚಿದರು. kaḍalgaḷḷaru haḍagina cinnavannu dōcidaru. The pirates looted the ship's gold.
Plural: ಕಡಲ್ಗಳ್ಳರು, kaḍalgaḷḷaru.
ಕಡಲ್, sea, and ಕಳ್ಳ, thief: a thief of the sea.

The word, taken apart.
ಕಡಲ್ಗಳ್ಳ (kaḍalgaḷḷa) is a native Dravidian compound: kaḍal, "the sea" (DEDR 1118), and kaḷḷa, "a thief" (DEDR 1372).

Lesson 4 of 5.
ರಹಸ್ಯ (rahasya) — a secret; a mystery.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a pirate, then the Kannada for an adventure.

You'll want to know: ರಹಸ್ಯ.
ರಹಸ್ಯ — rahasya — "a secret; a mystery".
ನಿಧಿಯ ರಹಸ್ಯ ಒಂದು ಹಳೆಯ ನಕ್ಷೆಯಲ್ಲಿ ಇತ್ತು. nidhiya rahasya ondu haḷeya nakṣeyalli ittu. The treasure's secret was in an old map.
Plural: ರಹಸ್ಯಗಳು, rahasyagaḷu.
As an adjective: ರಹಸ್ಯ (rahasya) ದಾರಿ, a secret path. ರಹಸ್ಯವಾಗಿ, secretly.

The word, taken apart.
ರಹಸ್ಯ (rahasya) is borrowed from Sanskrit rahasya, "private, secret" (CDIAL 10669), from rahas, "a lonely spot".

Lesson 5 of 5.
ಮಾಂತ್ರಿಕ (māntrika) — a sorcerer; a magician.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for an adventure, then the Kannada for a secret.

You'll want to know: ಮಾಂತ್ರಿಕ.
ಮಾಂತ್ರಿಕ — māntrika — "a sorcerer; a magician".
ಮಾಂತ್ರಿಕ ಆ ನಿಧಿಯನ್ನು ಕಾಯುತ್ತಿದ್ದನು. māntrika ā nidhiyannu kāyuttiddanu. A sorcerer was guarding that treasure.
Plural: ಮಾಂತ್ರಿಕರು, māntrikaru.
One who works spells, ಮಂತ್ರ, in a story.

The word, taken apart.
ಮಾಂತ್ರಿಕ (māntrika) is borrowed from Sanskrit māntrika, "a reciter of spells, a sorcerer" (CDIAL 10050), from mantra, "a spell".
