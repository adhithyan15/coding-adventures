## You'll want to know: ناشر
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C528-BOOKS-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ناشر** (*nâsher*) — "a publisher".

> ناشر کتاب تازه او را چاپ کرد.
>
> *nâsher ketâb-e tâze-ye u râ châp kard.*
>
> The publisher printed her new book.

Plural *nâsherân*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*nâsher* is a loan, usually traced to Arabic *nāšir*, 'one who spreads abroad, a publisher'. EtymDB has no entry for it.
