Russian, chapter 519: Curtain Up.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
занавес (zánaves) — a stage curtain.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to rehearse, then the Russian for a tour.

You'll want to know: занавес.
занавес (zánaves) — "a stage curtain".
Занавес поднялся, и спектакль начался. Zánaves podnyálsya, i spektákl nachalsyá. The curtain went up and the play began.
Masculine. A window curtain is штора or занавеска.

The word, taken apart.
занавес (zánaves) is derived from навес 'an awning' (EtymDB), built on the root of вешать 'to hang'.

Lesson 3 of 5.
аплодировать (aplodírovat) — to applaud.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a stage curtain, then the Russian for an interval.

You'll want to know: аплодировать.
аплодировать (aplodírovat) — "to applaud".
Публика долго аплодировала актёрам. Públika dólgo aplodírovala aktyóram. The audience applauded the actors for a long time.
Imperfective; present аплодирую. Takes the dative. The noun is аплодисменты — applause.

The word, taken apart.
аплодировать (aplodírovat) is a learned borrowing, usually traced through French applaudir to Latin applaudere 'to applaud' (Lewis & Short); EtymDB lists the verb without a source.

Lesson 5 of 5.
кланяться (klányatsya) — to bow.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to applaud, then the Russian for a premiere.

You'll want to know: кланяться.
кланяться (klányatsya) — "to bow".
В конце спектакля актёры кланяются. V kontsé spektáklya aktyóry klányayutsya. At the end of the play the actors take a bow.
Imperfective; perfective поклониться.

The word, taken apart.
EtymDB lists кланяться (klányatsya) without a source; it is usually linked to the root of клонить 'to bend, to tilt'.
