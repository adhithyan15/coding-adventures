## You'll want to know: दर्शक
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C529-NATAK529-05]; assesses=[] -->

**दर्शक** — *darśak* — "a spectator; a member of the audience".

> नाटक देखकर दर्शक बहुत हँसे।
>
> *Nāṭak dekhkar darśak bahut hãse.*
>
> The audience laughed a lot at the play.

Masculine or feminine, by the person meant.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**दर्शक** (*darśak*) is a tatsama, taken whole from Sanskrit *darśaka*, "seeing, looking at" (Monier-Williams): the one who watches.
