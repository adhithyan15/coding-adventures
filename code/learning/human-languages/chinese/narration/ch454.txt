Mandarin Chinese, chapter 454: The Information Desk.
6 lessons. You can do the first 3 of them in the car; after that you will want to have stopped.


Lesson 1 of 6.
fúwùtái — service desk; information desk.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for to carry in the hand, then the Mandarin for a luggage tag.

You'll want to know: fúwùtái.
fúwùtái — "service desk; information desk".
qù fúwùtái zěnme zǒu — How do I get to the information desk?
Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
fúwùtái is built from fúwù 'to serve' and tái 'a platform', all inherited from Middle Chinese, bjuwk, mjuH and doj (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to serve', the second 'affairs' and the last as a high square terrace.

Lesson 3 of 6.
图书 (túshū) — books on the shelves (of a library or bookshop).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for an information desk, then the Mandarin for to make an inquiry.

You'll want to know: 图书.
图书 — túshū — "books on the shelves (of a library or bookshop)".
这家书店的中文图书很多 zhè jiā shūdiàn de Zhōngwén túshū hěn duō — This bookshop has lots of Chinese books.
The new character, in pieces: 图 (tú) — eight strokes: the frame of 国, and inside it a short stroke falling to the left, a bar that turns down to the left, a long stroke falling to the right, and two dots. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

The word, taken apart.
图书 (túshū) is built from 图 (tú) 'a picture' and 书 'a book', both inherited from Middle Chinese, du and syo (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to plan'; the sense 'a picture, a map' is not given there.

What you've built.

Lesson 4 of 6.
图 (tú) — a frame, and five strokes inside it.

Before we start: this one needs your hands, so it is not a driving lesson. You will want a pen and something to write on and your eyes, for letter shapes on the page. You can listen to everything else now. Script — a frame, and five strokes inside it.
图 (tú).
A frame and what is inside it, as in 国: the frame opens first, takes its inside, and closes last.
The frame, three strokes. A vertical down the left. A bar that turns down the right side. The bar across the foot comes last, after the inside.
The inside, five strokes. A short stroke falling to the left. A bar that turns down to the left. A long stroke falling to the right, across it. Then two dots, one under the other.
2 + 5 + 1 = eight.

[once you have stopped driving — this part needs your eyes or your hands; if you are driving, skip ahead to the next part]
Guided Practice.
[once you have stopped driving — write: 图 (tú), all eight, with the model in front of you]
[once you have stopped driving — cover: write it again from memory, and count the pieces, not the strokes]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
How many strokes in 图 (tú)? (Eight.) Which stroke comes last? (The bar that closes the frame at the foot.) How many dots inside? (Two.)
