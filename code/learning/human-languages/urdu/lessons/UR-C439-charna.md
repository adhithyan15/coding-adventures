## You'll want to know: چرنا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C439-GIDAR439-04]; assesses=[] -->

**چرنا** — *charnā* — "to graze".

> مویشی دن بھر میدان میں چرتے ہیں۔
>
> *maveshī din bhar maidān meṅ charte haiṅ.*
>
> The cattle graze in the meadow all day.

Past (it …): وہ گائے دیر تک چری, *voh gāe der tak charī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**چرنا** (*charnā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *carnā*, "to move about, to graze", under Sanskrit *cárati*, "moves" (CDIAL 4686).
