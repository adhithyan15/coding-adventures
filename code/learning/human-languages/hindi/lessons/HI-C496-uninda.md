## You'll want to know: उनींदा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C496-NEEND496-02]; assesses=[] -->

**उनींदा** — *unīndā* — "drowsy; heavy with sleep".

> सुबह छह बजे बच्चे उनींदे थे।
>
> *subah chah baje bacce unīnde the.*
>
> At six in the morning the children were still drowsy.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**उनींदा** (*unīndā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists Hindi *unī̃dā*, with the sense "sleepless, with eyes open", under Sanskrit *unnidra*, "sleepless" (CDIAL 2108). Today it means eyes heavy from too little sleep, a turn CDIAL does not give.
