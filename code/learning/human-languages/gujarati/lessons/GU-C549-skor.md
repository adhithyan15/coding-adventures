## You'll want to know: સ્કોર
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C549-GOAL549-03]; assesses=[] -->

**સ્કોર** — *skor* — "a score".

> જો સ્કોર બરાબર રહેશે તો મેચ વધારે સમય ચાલશે.
>
> *jo skor barābar raheśe to mec vadhāre samay cālśe.*
>
> If the score stays level, the match will go on longer.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**સ્કોર** (*skor*) is borrowed from English *score*.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
