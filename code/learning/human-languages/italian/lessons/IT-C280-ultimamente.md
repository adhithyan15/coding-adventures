## You'll want to know: ultimamente
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C280-FINORA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ultimamente** — "lately".

> Ultimamente dormo poco.
>
> *Lately I have not been sleeping much.*

With the present or the *passato prossimo*: *ultimamente ho lavorato molto*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ultimamente* is built on *ultimo* "last", from Latin *ultimus* "farthest, last" (Lewis & Short).
