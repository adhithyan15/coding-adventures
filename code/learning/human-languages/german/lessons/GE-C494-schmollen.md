## You'll want to know: schmollen
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C494-STREIT-02]; assesses=[] -->

**schmollen** — "to sulk".

> Nach dem Streit schmollte sie den ganzen Abend.
>
> *After the argument she sulked all evening.*

Regular: *hat geschmollt*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*schmollen* goes back to Middle High German *smollen*; its older origin is not settled, and EtymDB has no entry for it.
