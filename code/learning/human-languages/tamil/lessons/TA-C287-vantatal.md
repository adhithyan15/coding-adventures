# வந்ததால் (vantatāl) — because it happened

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[TA-GRAMMAR-C287-OBLIGATIVE-01, TA-GRAMMAR-C286-SO-RESULT-01, TA-GRAMMAR-C284-WHEN-PAST-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "I have to see a doctor today." (*nāṉ iṉṟu maruttuvaraip pārkka vēṇṭum*.) Then "So I stayed at home." (*ataṉāl nāṉ vīṭṭil iruntēṉ*.)

## You'll want to know: -ததால், 'because (it) did'
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-GRAMMAR-C287-BECAUSE-DID-01]; assesses=[TA-LEX-C105-WORK-04, TA-LEX-C61-FEEL-04, TA-LEX-C78-WHY-03, TA-GRAMMAR-C284-WHEN-PAST-01, TA-GRAMMAR-C284-PAST-PERSON-01] -->

> **மழை பெய்ததால் நான் வரவில்லை.**
>
> *maḻai peytatāl nāṉ varavillai.* — "Because it rained, I did not come."

**பெய்ததால்** is three pieces. **பெய்த** is the past with **-அ**, the piece you met in **வந்த போது**; **அது**, "it", turns the event into a thing; **-ஆல்**, "because of", is the **-ஆல்** of **அதனால்**. "Because of its having rained."

> **எனக்குக் காய்ச்சல் இருந்ததால் நான் பள்ளிக்குப் போகவில்லை.**
>
> *eṉakkuk kāyccal iruntatāl nāṉ paḷḷikkup pōkavillai.* — "Because I had a fever, I did not go to school."

The reason comes first and the result after it, in one sentence: **வந்ததால்**, **போனதால்**, **இருந்ததால்**.

## You'll want to know: the reason after: ஏனென்றால், and காரணம்
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-GRAMMAR-C287-REASON-ORDER-01]; assesses=[TA-LEX-C78-WHY-02, TA-LEX-C266-KARANAM266-05, TA-PRAGMATICS-C81-REG-01] -->

> **நான் வரவில்லை. ஏனென்றால் மழை பெய்தது.**
>
> *nāṉ varavillai. ēṉeṉṟāl maḻai peytatu.* — "I did not come. Because it rained."

The same reason in the other order: the result first, then **ஏனென்றால்** opens a second sentence with the cause. In speech you hear **ஏன்னா**.

> **அதற்குக் காரணம் மழை.**
>
> *ataṟkuk kāraṇam maḻai.* — "The reason for that is the rain."

**அதற்கு** (*ataṟku*, "for that") is **அது** with **-க்கு**; **காரணம்**, "the reason", stands in the middle, and the reason itself comes last.
