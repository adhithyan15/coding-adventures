Gujarati, chapter 178: Starting and finishing.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
શરૂ કરવું (śarū karvũ) — to start, to begin.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for regular, then the Gujarati for everyday.

You'll want to know: શરૂ કરવું.
શરૂ કરવું — śarū karvũ — "to start, to begin".
હું કાલે નવું કામ શરૂ કરીશ. — hũ kāle navũ kām śarū karīś — "I will start a new job tomorrow."

The word, taken apart.
શરૂ કરવું (śarū karvũ) is built from śarū, "begun", and the inherited karvũ, "to do". Śarū is a loan from Arabic shurūʿ, "a beginning", through Persian, the word behind śarūāt (Lane).

Lesson 2 of 5.
પતાવવું (patāvvũ) — to finish, to settle.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for everyday, then the Gujarati for to start.

You'll want to know: પતાવવું.
પતાવવું — patāvvũ — "to finish, to settle".
તે આજે કામ પતાવશે. — te āje kām patāvśe — "He will finish the work today."

The word, taken apart.
પતાવવું (patāvvũ) is a tadbhava. CDIAL lists it, glossed "to have done with, settle", under Sanskrit prāpta, "reached, obtained" (CDIAL 8945).

Lesson 3 of 5.
સમયસર (samaysar) — on time.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to start, then the Gujarati for to finish.

સમયસર — samaysar — "on time".
હું સમયસર આવીશ. — hũ samaysar āvīś — "I will come on time."

The word, taken apart.
સમયસર (samaysar) is built from the taught samay, "time", a tatsama from Sanskrit samaya, and the ending -sar, "in keeping with".

Lesson 4 of 5.
બાકી (bākī) — remaining, left over.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to finish, then the Gujarati for on time.

You'll want to know: બાકી.
બાકી — bākī — "remaining, left over".
હજી કામ બાકી છે. — hajī kām bākī che — "There is still work left."

The word, taken apart.
બાકી (bākī) is a loan from Arabic bāqī, "remaining, lasting", which reached Gujarati through Persian. Its Arabic root is b-q-y, "to remain" (Lane).

Lesson 5 of 5.
વીતવું (vītvũ) — to go by, to pass (of time).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for on time, then the Gujarati for remaining.

You'll want to know: વીતવું.
વીતવું — vītvũ — "to go by, to pass (of time)".
સમય જલદી વીતશે. — samay jaldī vītśe — "Time will pass quickly."

The word, taken apart.
વીતવું (vītvũ) is a tadbhava. It comes from Sanskrit vṛtta, "turned; passed, elapsed (of time)", and CDIAL glosses the Gujarati verb "to pass (of time)" (CDIAL 12069).
