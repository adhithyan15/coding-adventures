## You'll want to know: रजा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C267-RAJA267-02]; assesses=[] -->

**रजा** — *rajā* — "leave to go".

> हूं रजा मांगूं हूं।
>
> *hū̃ rajā māṅgū̃ hū̃.*
>
> I ask leave to go.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**रजा** (*rajā*) is usually traced to Persian *riżā*, "consent"; asking for it is asking leave to go.
