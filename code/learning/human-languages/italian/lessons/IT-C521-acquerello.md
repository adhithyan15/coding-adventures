## You'll want to know: l'acquerello
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C521-PITTURA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**l'acquerello** — "watercolour".

> Dipingo all'acquerello paesaggi di mare.
>
> *I paint seascapes in watercolour.*

*All'acquerello* — in watercolour.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*acquerello* is built on *acqua* "water" (EtymDB), inherited from Latin *aqua*.
