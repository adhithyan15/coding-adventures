## You'll want to know: der Pfeil
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C434-WEGWEISER-03]; assesses=[] -->

**der Pfeil** — "the arrow".

> Der Pfeil zeigt nach links.
>
> *The arrow points to the left.*

Plural: *die Pfeile*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Pfeil* is an early borrowing from Latin *pīlum* 'javelin' (Lewis & Short), already in Old High German as *pfīl* (EtymDB).
