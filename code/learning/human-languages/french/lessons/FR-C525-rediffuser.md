## You'll want to know: rediffuser
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C525-ECRAN-05]; assesses=[] -->

**rediffuser** — "to repeat, to rebroadcast".

> La chaîne rediffuse ce vieux film chaque été.
>
> *The channel repeats this old film every summer.*

*Une rediffusion* — a repeat.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*rediffuser* is built from *re-* and *diffuser* "to broadcast", from Latin *diffundere* "to pour out, to spread", built on *fundere* "to pour" (Lewis & Short).
