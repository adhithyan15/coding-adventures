## You'll want to know: il vassoio
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C533-TAVOLA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**il vassoio** — "tray".

> Porto il caffè sul vassoio.
>
> *I bring the coffee on the tray.*

*Un vassoio di pasticcini* — a tray of pastries.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*vassoio* is usually traced to Vulgar Latin *missōrium* "a serving dish" (EtymDB), from *mittere* "to send"; the details are not settled.
