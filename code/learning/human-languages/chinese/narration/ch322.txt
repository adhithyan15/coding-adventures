Mandarin Chinese, chapter 322: Back Then.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
当年 (dāngnián) — back then; in those years.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for age-old, then the Mandarin for a script for a language.

You'll want to know: 当年.
当年 — dāngnián — "back then; in those years".
当年他常常给我写信 dāngnián tā chángcháng gěi wǒ xiě xìn — Back then he often sent me letters.

The word, taken apart.
当年 (dāngnián) is built from 当 'to face, that very' and 年 'a year', both inherited from Middle Chinese, tang and nen (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to face' and 'to match' and says that when the grain ripens it is called a year.

Lesson 4 of 5.
一生 (yìshēng) — all one's life; a lifetime.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for in one's early years, then the Mandarin for in one's later years.

You'll want to know: 一生.
一生 — yìshēng — "all one's life; a lifetime".
他一生都在这个学校工作 tā yìshēng dōu zài zhège xuéxiào gōngzuò — He worked at this school all his life.
A tone change: 一 is said with a falling tone, yì, before the level tone of shēng.

The word, taken apart.
一生 (yìshēng) is built from 一 'one' and 生 'to live', both inherited from Middle Chinese, 'jit and srjaeng (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which calls the first the start of the numbers and glosses the second 'to grow'.

Lesson 5 of 5.
huáiniàn — to cherish the memory of; to look back fondly on.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for in one's later years, then the Mandarin for all one's life.

You'll want to know: huáiniàn.
huáiniàn — "to cherish the memory of; to look back fondly on".
wǒ hěn huáiniàn xiǎoshíhou de shēnghuó — I really miss my childhood days.
Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
huáiniàn is built from huái and niàn, both inherited from Middle Chinese, hweaj and nemH (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to hold to the breast' and 'to think of', and the second 'to think of'.
