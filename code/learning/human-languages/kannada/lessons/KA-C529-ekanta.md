## You'll want to know: ಏಕಾಂತ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C529-JIVANA529-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ಏಕಾಂತ** — *ēkānta* — "solitude".

> ವಯಸ್ಸಾದ ಮೇಲೆ ಅಜ್ಜನಿಗೆ ಏಕಾಂತ ಇಷ್ಟ.
>
> *vayassāda mēle ajjanige ēkānta iṣṭa.*
>
> In old age grandfather likes solitude.

Chosen quiet; ಒಂಟಿತನ is loneliness you did not choose.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಏಕಾಂತ** (*ēkānta*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *ekānta*, "a lonely or retired place" (Monier-Williams).
