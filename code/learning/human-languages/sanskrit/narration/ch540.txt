Sanskrit, chapter 540: Time Running Out.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
आसन्नः (āsannaḥ) — close at hand.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for suspects, then the Sanskrit for keeps safe.

You'll want to know: आसन्नः.
आसन्नः — āsannaḥ — "close at hand".
यदि परीक्षा आसन्ना अस्ति, तर्हि अद्य एव पठतु। yadi parīkṣā āsannā asti, tarhi adya eva paṭhatu. If the exam is close, study today.

The word, taken apart.
आसन्नः (āsannaḥ) is built on ā and the root √sad, "to sit", as its past participle: sat down near; MW gives āsanna as seated down, and near, close at hand (MW).

Lesson 3 of 5.
समाप्तिः (samāptiḥ) — completion.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for close at hand, then the Sanskrit for left over.

You'll want to know: समाप्तिः.
समाप्तिः — samāptiḥ — "completion".
यदि कार्यस्य समाप्तिः भवति, तर्हि वयं विश्रामं करिष्यामः। yadi kāryasya samāptiḥ bhavati, tarhi vayaṃ viśrāmaṃ kariṣyāmaḥ. When the work is finished, we will rest.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
समाप्तिः (samāptiḥ) is built on sam, "wholly", and the root √āp, "to reach", which also gives samāpayati, "finishes"; MW gives samāpti as accomplishment, completion, conclusion (MW).

Lesson 4 of 5.
कालक्षेपः (kālakṣepaḥ) — a waste of time; delay.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for left over, then the Sanskrit for completion.

You'll want to know: कालक्षेपः.
कालक्षेपः — kālakṣepaḥ — "a waste of time; delay".
यदि कालक्षेपः भवति, तर्हि वयं समये न आगमिष्यामः। yadi kālakṣepaḥ bhavati, tarhi vayaṃ samaye na āgamiṣyāmaḥ. If there is a delay, we will not arrive on time.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
कालक्षेपः (kālakṣepaḥ) is built from kāla, "time", and kṣepa, "a throwing, a letting go", from the root √kṣip, "to throw"; MW gives kālakṣepa as letting time pass, delay, loss of time (MW).

Lesson 5 of 5.
अतिवाहयति (ativāhayati) — spends time.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for completion, then the Sanskrit for a waste of time.

You'll want to know: अतिवाहयति.
अतिवाहयति — ativāhayati — "spends time".
यदि वर्षा भवति, तर्हि वयं दिनं गृहे अतिवाहयामः। yadi varṣā bhavati, tarhi vayaṃ dinaṃ gṛhe ativāhayāmaḥ. If it rains, we spend the day at home.
Past (he …): सः दिनं गृहे अतिवाहितवान्, saḥ dinaṃ gṛhe ativāhitavān.

The word, taken apart.
अतिवाहयति (ativāhayati) is built on ati, "beyond", and the causative of the root √vah, "to carry", which also gives vahati; MW gives ativāhayati as to let pass, to let time pass, to spend it (MW).
