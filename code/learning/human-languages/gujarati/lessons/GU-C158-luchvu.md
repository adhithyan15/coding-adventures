# લૂછવું (lūchvũ) — to wipe, to dry off

## You'll want to know: લૂછવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C158-SWEEP158-03]; assesses=[] -->

**લૂછવું** — *lūchvũ* — "to wipe, to dry off".

> **હું ટુવાલથી હાથ લૂછું છું.** — *hũ ṭuvālthī hāth lūchũ chũ* — "I wipe my hands with a towel."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

The origin of **લૂછવું** (*lūchvũ*) is not settled. CDIAL lists no Gujarati verb of this form; it lists the similar *pũchvũ*, "to wipe", under Sanskrit *proñchati*, "wipes off", without linking the two (CDIAL 9011).
