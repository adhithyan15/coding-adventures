# हूं जाऊं हूं — a stem that ends in a vowel

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MW-LEX-C51-VERBS51-02, MW-GRAMMAR-C154-PRES-1SG-01, MW-GRAMMAR-C154-PRES-3SG-01, MW-SCRIPT-BAJAR-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say *to go*, **जाणो**. Then *I listen to songs* and *he listens to
songs*. And *the market*, **बाजार**.

## Grammar Lens: जा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-GRAMMAR-C155-VSTEM-01]; assesses=[MW-LEX-C51-VERBS51-02, MW-SCRIPT-C153-UU-01, MW-GRAMMAR-C154-PRES-1SG-01, MW-GRAMMAR-C154-PRES-3SG-01] -->

**जाणो** leaves **जा**, a stem that ends in a vowel. For *I*, its ending is the
full letter **ऊं**:

> **हूं रोज बाजार जाऊं हूं।**
>
> *hū̃ roj bājār jāū̃ hū̃.* — I go to the market every day.

For **वो**, a **व** comes in before **-ै**:

> **वो रोज बाजार जावै है।**
>
> *vo roj bājār jāvai hai.* — He goes to the market every day.

After **आप**, the **व** stays: **आप जावो हो**, the ending of the request **लावो**.
Old Marwari had *jāvaï*, "goes" (CDIAL 10452).
