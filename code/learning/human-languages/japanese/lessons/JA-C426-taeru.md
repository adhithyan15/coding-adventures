## You'll want to know: たえる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C426-FEEL426-05]; assesses=[] -->

**たえる** — *taeru* — "to endure; to bear".

> **あには あつい なつに たえています。** — *ani wa atsui natsu ni taete imasu* — "My elder brother is putting up with the hot summer."

Polite **たえます**, *taemasu*; an ichidan verb. Past (polite): **ははは いたみに たえました。**, *haha wa itami ni taemashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**たえる** (*taeru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji spelling was attached to it later (JMdict).
