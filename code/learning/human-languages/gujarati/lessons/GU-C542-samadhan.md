## You'll want to know: સમાધાન
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C542-SAMADHAN542-01]; assesses=[] -->

**સમાધાન** — *samādhān* — "a compromise".

> જો તમે બંને બેસીને વાત કરશો તો સમાધાન થશે.
>
> *jo tame banne besīne vāt karśo to samādhān thaśe.*
>
> If you two sit down and talk, you'll reach a compromise.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**સમાધાન** (*samādhān*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *samādhāna*, "putting together" (Monier-Williams); in Gujarati it is the settling of a quarrel.
