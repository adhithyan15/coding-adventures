## You'll want to know: la fauna
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C695-FAUNA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**la fauna** — "the fauna, the wildlife".

> La fauna de la isla es única.
>
> *The wildlife on the island is unique.*

The animals of a place: *la fauna marina* — sea life.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*fauna* is a learned borrowing from Latin *Fauna*, a Roman goddess, the sister of the woodland god *Faunus* (Lewis & Short).
