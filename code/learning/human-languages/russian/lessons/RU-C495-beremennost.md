## You'll want to know: беременность
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C495-EXPECTING-01]; assesses=[] -->

**беременность** (*berémennost*) — "pregnancy".

> Во время беременности она много гуляла.
>
> *Vo vrémya berémennosti oná mnógo gulyála.*
>
> During her pregnancy she walked a lot.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*беременность* is built on *беременная* 'pregnant', from the older *беремя* 'a load, an armful', usually linked to *брать* 'to take'; EtymDB has no entry.
