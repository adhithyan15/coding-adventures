## You'll want to know: 怪
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C539-BLAME539-02]; assesses=[] -->

**怪** — *guài* — "to put the blame on someone".

> **这不能怪你原因不在你** *zhè bù néng guài nǐ yuányīn bú zài nǐ* — You can't be blamed for this: the cause doesn't lie with you.

The new character, in pieces: 怪 — eight strokes in three pieces: the upright heart on the left, three strokes, as in 快; on the right 又, two strokes; and under it the earth piece, three strokes. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**怪** (*guài*) is inherited from Middle Chinese *kweajH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'strange'. The sense 'to blame' is not given there; it is usually explained as finding fault, but how it came is not settled.
