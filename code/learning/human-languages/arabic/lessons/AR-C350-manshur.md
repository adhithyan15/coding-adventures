## You'll want to know: منشور
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C350-NASHIR350-04]; assesses=[] -->

**منشور** — *manshūr* — "a leaflet; a post online".

> وزع الطلاب منشورا عن الحفلة.
>
> *wazzaʿa aṭ-ṭullāb manshūran ʿan al-ḥafla.*
>
> The students handed out a leaflet about the party.

On social media a منشور is a post.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**منشور** (*manshūr*) is built on the native Arabic root *n-sh-r* (Lane), "to spread out". *Manshūr* is the passive participle, "a thing spread about".
