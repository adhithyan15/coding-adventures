# تحول (taḥawwala) — to turn into, to change

## You'll want to know: تحول
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C344-HIKAYA344-03]; assesses=[] -->

**تحول** — *taḥawwala* — "to turn into, to change".

> تحول الضفدع إلى أمير.
>
> *taḥawwala aḍ-ḍifdaʿ ilā amīr.*
>
> The frog turned into a prince.

Present: يتحول, *yataḥawwal*.

It takes إلى: تحول إلى.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**تحول** (*taḥawwala*) is built on the native Arabic root *ḥ-w-l* (Lane), "to change, to shift", the root of *ḥāl*, a state. It is Form V, "to change oneself".

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
