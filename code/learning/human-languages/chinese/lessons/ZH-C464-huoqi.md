## You'll want to know: 火气
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C464-ANGRY464-02]; assesses=[] -->

**火气** — *huǒqì* — "temper; anger".

> **他的火气比我大** *tā de huǒqì bǐ wǒ dà* — He has a worse temper than I do.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**火气** (*huǒqì*) is built from 火 'fire' and 气 'breath', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *xwaX* and *khj+jH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'breath'. The sense 'anger' is not given there.
