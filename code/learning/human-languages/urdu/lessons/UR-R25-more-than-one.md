# More than one

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[UR-GRAMMAR-PLURAL, UR-GRAMMAR-OBLIQUE-PLURAL, UR-GRAMMAR-OBLIQUE] -->

[PAUSE 4s] Say all four shapes of **کمرہ**. Two of them are spelled the same —
which two, and what tells them apart?

## Guided Practice: the distant band — three chapters of endings, run cold
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[UR-GRAMMAR-OBLIQUE, UR-GRAMMAR-PLURAL, UR-GRAMMAR-OBLIQUE-PLURAL, UR-GRAMMAR-KO, UR-GRAMMAR-MEIN-POST, UR-GRAMMAR-PAR, UR-GRAMMAR-IS-OBLIQUE, UR-GRAMMAR-UN-OBLIQUE, UR-GRAMMAR-HAMARA, UR-GRAMMAR-AAP-KA, UR-GRAMMAR-ADJECTIVE-AGREEMENT, UR-LEX-YAHAN, UR-LEX-VAHAN, UR-LEX-KAMRA, UR-LEX-KAAN, UR-LEX-MAAN, UR-LEX-KALA, UR-LEX-NILA, UR-LEX-TOPI, UR-LEX-DOODH, UR-LEX-BARISH, UR-LEX-HUM, UR-LEX-MAIN, UR-LEX-MERA-NAAM-HAI] -->

[PAUSE 4s] Three chapters ago no Urdu noun in this book had ever changed shape,
been more than one, or been anywhere. Run all of it, cold.

- [YOU SAY: the shape ladder — *kamrā*, *kamre*, *kamroṅ*]
- [YOU SAY: the wardrobe, placed — *ṭopī kamre meṅ*, *ṭopī un kamroṅ meṅ*]
- [YOU SAY: the pantry — *dūdh yahāṅ hai*, *dūdh vahāṅ nahīṅ hai*]
- [YOU SAY: the weather — *bārish hai*, and *yahāṅ bārish hai*]
- [YOU SAY: the ear that finally moves — *kān*, then *kānoṅ ko*]
- [YOU SAY: colours following the noun over — *kāle kamre meṅ*, *nīle kamroṅ meṅ*]
- [YOU SAY: all the possessives — *merā*, *hamārā*, *āp kā*]
- [YOU SAY: the demonstratives bending — *is kamre meṅ*, *un kamroṅ meṅ*]
- [YOU SAY: I, and we — *maiṅ yahāṅ hūṅ*, *ham yahāṅ haiṅ*]

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[UR-GRAMMAR-PLURAL, UR-GRAMMAR-OBLIQUE-PLURAL, UR-GRAMMAR-HAMARA, UR-LEX-HUM, UR-GRAMMAR-OBLIQUE] -->

[PAUSE 4s] Which ending means *plural, and a postposition is coming*? (**-وں**.)
Which two of the four cells collide, and what settles them? (**Plural direct and
oblique singular**; **whether a postposition follows**.) What is **ہمارا** made
of? (**ہم** and **کا**.) And which three words in this book use a plural form
for one person? (**آپ**, **ان**, **ہم**.)
