## You'll want to know: いがい
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C532-LIMIT532-05]; assesses=[] -->

**いがい** — *igai* — "surprising; unexpected".

> **もし かれが かったら、いがいでしょう。** — *moshi kare ga kattara, igai deshō* — "If he wins, it will be a surprise."

A na-adjective: な before a noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**いがい** (*igai*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *i*, "idea" (Middle Chinese *'iH*) and *gai*, "outside" (*ngwajH*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
