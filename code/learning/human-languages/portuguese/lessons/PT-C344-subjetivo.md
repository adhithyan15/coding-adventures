## You'll want to know: subjetivo
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C344-JUIZO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**subjetivo** — "subjective".

> O gosto é muito subjetivo.
>
> *Taste is very subjective.*

AO90 spelling; the older form was *subjectivo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*subjetivo* is a learned borrowing from Latin *subiectīvus* (EtymDB), built on *subiectum* "what lies underneath", from *subicere*, on *iacere* "to throw" (Lewis & Short).
