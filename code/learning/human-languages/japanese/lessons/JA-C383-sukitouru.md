## You'll want to know: すきとおる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C383-SHIMAGUNI383-05]; assesses=[] -->

**すきとおる** — *sukitōru* — "to be clear; to be see-through".

> **この うみは みずが すきとおっています。** — *kono umi wa mizu ga sukitōtte imasu* — "The water of this sea is crystal clear."

Polite **すきとおります**, *sukitōrimasu*; a godan verb. Past (polite): **いけの みずが すきとおりました。**, *ike no mizu ga sukitōrimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**すきとおる** (*sukitōru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict).
