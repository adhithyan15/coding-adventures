French, chapter 363: Dans la rue.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
la manifestation — the demonstration, the protest march; the event.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for the freedom, then the French for the equality.

You'll want to know: la manifestation.
la manifestation — "the demonstration, the protest march; the event".
Il y a une manifestation dans le centre-ville samedi. There is a demonstration in the town centre on Saturday.
Often shortened to la manif. Also any public event: une manifestation sportive.

The word, taken apart.
manifestation comes from Late Latin manifēstātiō "a making plain" (EtymDB), from manifestus "clear, plain" (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 3 of 5.
le manifestant — the demonstrator, the marcher.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for the demonstration, then the French for to demonstrate.

You'll want to know: le manifestant.
le manifestant — "the demonstrator, the marcher".
Les manifestants marchent vers la mairie. The demonstrators are marching towards the town hall.
Feminine manifestante.

The word, taken apart.
manifestant is the present participle of manifester used as a noun. The verb comes from Latin manifēstāre "to make plain", from manifestus "clear" (Lewis & Short).
