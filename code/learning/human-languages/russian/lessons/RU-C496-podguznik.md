## You'll want to know: подгузник
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C496-BABY-01]; assesses=[] -->

**подгузник** (*podgúznik*) — "a nappy, a diaper".

> Папа научился быстро менять подгузники.
>
> *Pápa naúchilsya býstro menyát podgúzniki.*
>
> Dad has learnt to change nappies quickly.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*подгузник* is built on *под* 'under' and the colloquial *гузно* 'rump'; EtymDB lists the word without a source.
