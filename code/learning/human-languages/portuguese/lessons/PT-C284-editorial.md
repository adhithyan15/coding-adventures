## You'll want to know: o editorial
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C284-ESCANDALO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**o editorial** — "the editorial, the leading article".

> O editorial de hoje critica o governo.
>
> *Today’s editorial criticises the government.*

As an adjective: *uma decisão editorial*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*editorial* is built on *editor*, a learned borrowing from Latin *ēditor* "one who brings forth, a producer" (Lewis & Short).
