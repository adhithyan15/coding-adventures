## You'll want to know: प्रदूषित
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C448-DURGANDHI448-03]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रदूषित** — *pradūṣit* — "polluted".

> शहरातली हवा प्रदूषित झाली आहे.
>
> *śahrātlī havā pradūṣit jhālī āhe.*
>
> The air in the city has become polluted.

It does not change: प्रदूषित हवा, प्रदूषित पाणी.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रदूषित** (*pradūṣit*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *pradūṣita*, "corrupted, spoilt" (Monier-Williams).
