Bengali, chapter 54: To make, To lift, To pull, To hide, To win.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
বানানো (bānāno) — to make.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for to fly, then the Bengali for to teach.

You'll want to know: বানানো.
বানানো — bānāno — "to make".

The word, taken apart.
বানানো (bānāno) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from vanati, "desires, makes ready" (CDIAL 11260). Bānāno is the causing form of banā, a verb CDIAL glosses "to agree, do".

The word, taken apart.
টানা (ṭānā) is a tadbhava, a word inherited and reshaped by sound change. It comes from ṭān, "tighten", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 5443). CDIAL calls its relation to Sanskrit tanoti, "stretches", obscure.
