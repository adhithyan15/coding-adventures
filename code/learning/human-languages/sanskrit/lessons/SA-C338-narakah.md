## You'll want to know: नरकः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C338-SVARGA338-04]; assesses=[] -->

**नरकः** — *narakaḥ* — "hell".

> पापेन जनः नरकं गच्छति इति वदन्ति।
>
> *pāpena janaḥ narakaṃ gacchati iti vadanti.*
>
> People say that sin leads a person to hell.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**नरकः** (*narakaḥ*) MW gives *naraka* as hell, a place of torment, without a derivation, so its origin is uncertain (MW).
