Gujarati, chapter 404: Pavements and Paths.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ફૂટપાથ (phūṭpāth) — a pavement; a footpath (beside a road).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to branch off, then the Gujarati for to crash (into).

You'll want to know: ફૂટપાથ.
ફૂટપાથ — phūṭpāth — "a pavement; a footpath (beside a road)".
બાળકોએ ફૂટપાથ પર ચાલવું જોઈએ. bāḷkoe phūṭpāth par cālvũ joīe. Children should walk on the pavement.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ફૂટપાથ (phūṭpāth) is borrowed from English footpath, the raised walk beside a city road.

Lesson 3 of 5.
સાંકડું (sā̃kḍũ) — narrow.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a pavement, then the Gujarati for a trail.

You'll want to know: સાંકડું.
સાંકડું — sā̃kḍũ — "narrow".
આ ગલી એટલી સાંકડી છે કે ગાડી અંદર નથી જતી. ā galī eṭlī sā̃kḍī che ke gāḍī andar nathī jatī. This lane is so narrow that a car does not go in.

The word, taken apart.
સાંકડું (sā̃kḍũ) is a tadbhava of Sanskrit saṁkaṭa, "narrow, pressed together"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati word (CDIAL 12817).

Lesson 4 of 5.
ચઢાણ (caḍhāṇ) — an uphill climb; an ascent.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a trail, then the Gujarati for narrow.

You'll want to know: ચઢાણ.
ચઢાણ — caḍhāṇ — "an uphill climb; an ascent".
મંદિર સુધીનું ચઢાણ ઘણું અઘરું છે. mandir sudhīnũ caḍhāṇ ghaṇũ agharũ che. The climb up to the temple is very hard.
A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
ચઢાણ (caḍhāṇ) is built on ચઢવું, "to climb", which goes back to caḍhati, "rises", a form CDIAL reconstructs (CDIAL 4578): the stretch of road you have to climb.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ટપવું (ṭapvũ) — to jump over; to leap across.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for narrow, then the Gujarati for an uphill climb.

You'll want to know: ટપવું.
ટપવું — ṭapvũ — "to jump over; to leap across".
છોકરો ખાડો ટપી ગયો. chokro khāḍo ṭapī gayo. The boy jumped over the pothole.
Past (he …): તે ખાડો ટપી ગયો, te khāḍo ṭapī gayo.

The word, taken apart.
ટપવું (ṭapvũ) goes back to tarpati, "jumps", a form CDIAL reconstructs; CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb, "to leap, to jump over" (CDIAL 5727).
