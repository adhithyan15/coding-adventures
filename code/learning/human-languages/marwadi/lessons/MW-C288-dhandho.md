## You'll want to know: धंधो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C288-KAM288-04]; assesses=[] -->

**धंधो** — *dhandho* — "a trade".

> म्हारो धंधो सोरो कोनी।
>
> *mhāro dhandho soro konī.*
>
> My trade isn't easy.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**धंधो** (*dhandho*) is inherited from Sanskrit *dhandha*, "awkwardness"; CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry, but lists Hindi *dhandhā*, "craft, way of life", and Sindhi *dhandho*, "business" (CDIAL 6727). How the sense moved to work is not settled.
