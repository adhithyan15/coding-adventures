## You'll want to know: a reforma
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C362-ORDENADO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**a reforma** — "the retirement, the pension; the reform".

> O meu avô está na reforma há dez anos.
>
> *My grandfather has been retired for ten years.*

European Portuguese for retirement; in Brazil *a aposentadoria*. Also a reform: *a reforma da escola*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*reforma* is built on *reformar* "to reshape" (EtymDB), a learned borrowing from Latin *refōrmāre* "to shape again, to transform" (Lewis & Short), on *fōrma* "shape".
