Persian, chapter 424: Speed and safety.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
سرعت (sor'at) — speed.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to run out, then the Persian for a car wash.

You'll want to know: سرعت.
سرعت (sor'at) — "speed".
سرعت (sor'at) این قطار خیلی زیاد است. Sor'at-e in qatâr kheyli ziyâd ast. This train's speed is very high.
Bâ sor'at — quickly, at speed.

The word, taken apart.
sor'at was borrowed from Arabic surʿa 'speed, haste' (EtymDB).

Lesson 2 of 5.
سرعت گرفتن (sor'at gereftan) — to speed up, to gather speed.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a car wash, then the Persian for speed.

You'll want to know: سرعت گرفتن.
سرعت گرفتن (sor'at gereftan) — "to speed up, to gather speed".
بعد از شهر اتوبوس سرعت گرفت. Ba'd az shahr otobus sor'at gereft. After the town the bus sped up.
sor'at + gereftan (to take). Present stem gir-: sor'at migirad.

The word, taken apart.
sor'at gereftan is built from sor'at 'speed', borrowed from Arabic surʿa (EtymDB), and the native verb gereftan 'to take', which is usually traced to Old Persian grab- 'to seize'.

Lesson 3 of 5.
سبقت گرفتن (sebqat gereftan) — to overtake (US: to pass).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for speed, then the Persian for to speed up.

You'll want to know: سبقت گرفتن.
سبقت گرفتن (sebqat gereftan) — "to overtake (US: to pass)".
در پیچ هیچ وقت از اتوبوسها سبقت نمیگیرم. Dar pich hich vaqt az otobus-hâ sebqat nemigiram. I never overtake buses on a bend.
sebqat 'going ahead' + gereftan; the one overtaken takes az: az u sebqat gereft.

The word, taken apart.
sebqat gereftan is built from sebqat 'getting ahead', a loan usually traced to Arabic sabq 'going ahead, a race', and the native verb gereftan 'to take'. EtymDB lists sebqat without a source.

Lesson 4 of 5.
کنار زدن (kenâr zadan) — to pull over (to the side of the road).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to speed up, then the Persian for to overtake.

You'll want to know: کنار زدن.
کنار زدن (kenâr zadan) — "to pull over (to the side of the road)".
راننده کنار زد و ما پیاده شدیم. Rânande kenâr zad va mâ piyâde shodim. The driver pulled over and we got out.
kenâr (side, edge) + zadan. Kenâr zadan can also mean to push something aside.

The word, taken apart.
kenâr zadan is built from native kenâr 'side, edge', usually traced to Middle Persian kanārag, and the native verb zadan 'to strike'.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ایمنی (imeni) — safety.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to overtake, then the Persian for to pull over.

You'll want to know: ایمنی.
ایمنی (imeni) — "safety".
کمربند برای ایمنی مسافران مهم است. Kamarband barâ-ye imeni-ye mosâferân mohem ast. The belt matters for the safety of the passengers.
Kamarband-e imeni — a seat belt. Built on imen 'safe' + -i.

The word, taken apart.
imeni is built from imen 'safe, secure' and the noun ending -i. Imen is usually traced to Arabic āmin 'safe, secure'; EtymDB does not list imen or imeni.
