## You'll want to know: वसतिगृह
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C369-VIDYAPITH369-05]; assesses=[] -->

**वसतिगृह** — *vasatigṛh* — "a hostel".

> मी पहिल्या वर्षी वसतिगृहात राहिलो.
>
> *mī pahilyā varṣī vasatigṛhāt rāhilo.*
>
> I lived in the hostel in my first year.

Neuter.

वसती + गृह, dwelling + house.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**वसतिगृह** (*vasatigṛh*) is a learned compound built from Sanskrit *vasati*, "dwelling, staying", and *gṛha*, "house" (Monier-Williams).
