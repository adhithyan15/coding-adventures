## You'll want to know: मुद्दा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C359-MUDDA359-01]; assesses=[] -->

**मुद्दा** — *muddā* — "an issue, a point at issue".

> आज की बैठक में पानी सबसे बड़ा मुद्दा था।
>
> *āj kī baiṭhak meṁ pānī sabse baṛā muddā thā.*
>
> Water was the biggest issue at today's meeting.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**मुद्दा** (*muddā*) is usually traced to Arabic *muddaʿā*, "that which is claimed", which reached Hindi through Persian.
