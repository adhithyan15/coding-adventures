## You'll want to know: સસરો
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C490-SASRA490-01]; assesses=[] -->

**સસરો** — *sasro* — "a father-in-law".

> મારા સસરા રોજ સવારે મંદિરે જાય છે.
>
> *mārā sasrā roj savāre mandire jāy che.*
>
> My father-in-law goes to the temple every morning.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**સસરો** (*sasro*) is inherited from Sanskrit *śvaśura*, "husband's father"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati word (CDIAL 12753). One's own father-in-law is spoken of in the respectful plural, **મારા સસરા** (*mārā sasrā*).
