## You'll want to know: gānxǐ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C215-DELIVER215-05]; assesses=[] -->

*gānxǐ* — "to dry-clean".

> *zhège kěyǐ gānxǐ ma* — Can this be dry-cleaned?

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*gānxǐ* is built from *gān* 'dry' and 洗 'to wash', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *kan* and *sejX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for everyday actions.
