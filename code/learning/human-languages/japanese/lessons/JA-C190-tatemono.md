## You'll want to know: たてもの
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C190-TOWN190-01]; assesses=[] -->

**たてもの** — *tatemono* — "building".

> **あの たかい たてものは ぎんこうです。** — *ano takai tatemono wa ginkō desu* — "That tall building is a bank."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**たてもの** (*tatemono*) is native Japanese, built from *tate*, the stem of *tateru*, "to build", and *mono*, "thing" (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
