## You'll want to know: ぴったり
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C370-MATOMARU370-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ぴったり** — *pittari* — "perfectly; exactly".

> **けいさんが ぴったり あったら、おわりです。** — *keisan ga pittari attara, owari desu* — "If the sums come out exactly, we are done."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ぴったり** (*pittari*) is a native mimetic word, one of the Japanese words that picture a manner or a feeling in sound; JMdict marks it as mimetic. It is inherited, not borrowed, and its older origin is not settled (JMdict).

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
