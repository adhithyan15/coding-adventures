## You'll want to know: एवाळियो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C234-HERD234-01]; assesses=[] -->

**एवाळियो** — *evāḷiyo* — "a shepherd".

> एवाळियो जंगळ मांय जावै है।
>
> *evāḷiyo jaṅgaḷ mā̃y jāvai hai.*
>
> The shepherd goes out into the scrubland.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**एवाळियो** (*evāḷiyo*) is inherited from Sanskrit *avipāla*, "a shepherd", built from *avi*, "sheep", and *pāla*, "a keeper"; CDIAL lists Marwari *evāḷiyo* (CDIAL 893).
