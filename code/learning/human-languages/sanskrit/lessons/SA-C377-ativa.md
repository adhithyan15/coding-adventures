## You'll want to know: अतीव
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C377-ATIVA377-01]; assesses=[] -->

**अतीव** — *atīva* — "exceedingly; very".

> यदि भवान् आगच्छति, तर्हि अहम् अतीव प्रसन्नः भविष्यामि।
>
> *yadi bhavān āgacchati, tarhi aham atīva prasannaḥ bhaviṣyāmi.*
>
> If you come, I will be very pleased.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अतीव** (*atīva*) MW gives *atīva* "exceedingly, very" and gives no derivation, so its origin is uncertain (MW).
