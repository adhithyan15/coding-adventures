5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
o docente — the lecturer, the teacher.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for the semester, then the Portuguese for the tuition fee.

You'll want to know: o docente.
o docente — "the lecturer, the teacher".
Os docentes desta faculdade são muito exigentes. The lecturers at this faculty are very demanding.
Formal; o corpo docente — the teaching staff.

The word, taken apart.
docente is a learned borrowing from Latin docēns "teaching", the present participle of docēre "to teach, to instruct" (Lewis & Short).

o orientador — "the thesis supervisor".
O meu orientador lê tudo o que escrevo. My supervisor reads everything I write.

The word, taken apart.
orientador is built on orientar "to guide", on oriente "east", from Latin oriēns "rising (sun)", on orīrī "to rise" (Lewis & Short): to find the east is to find your way.

Lesson 3 of 5.
a bolsa de estudo — the scholarship, the grant.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for the lecturer, then the Portuguese for the thesis supervisor.

You'll want to know: a bolsa de estudo.
a bolsa de estudo — "the scholarship, the grant".
Ela recebeu uma bolsa de estudo para estudar fora. She got a scholarship to study abroad.
O bolseiro — the grant holder (in Brazil o bolsista).

The word, taken apart.
bolsa "purse" goes back through Old Portuguese bolssa to Late Latin bursa "oxhide, purse" (EtymDB), usually traced to Greek býrsa "hide"; estudo is inherited from Latin studium "zeal, eagerness, study" (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 5 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: o doutoramento.
o doutoramento — "the doctorate, the PhD".
O doutoramento demora pelo menos três anos. The doctorate takes at least three years.
European Portuguese; in Brazil o doutorado.

The word, taken apart.
doutoramento is built on doutorar "to award a doctorate", from doutor, inherited from Latin doctor "a teacher, an instructor" (Lewis & Short), on docēre "to teach".
