Arabic, chapter 52: Face, Blood, Knee, Back, Heart.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
وجه (wajh) — a face.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a shoulder, then the Arabic for the neck.

You'll want to know: وجه.
وجه — wajh — "a face".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: wajh]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: wajh, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say wajh]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Arabic for a shoulder, then the Arabic for the neck, then say wajh again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does وجه (wajh) mean? ("A face".) Say it once more.
