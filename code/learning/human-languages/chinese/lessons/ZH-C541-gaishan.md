## You'll want to know: gǎishàn
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C541-FINAL541-03]; assesses=[] -->

*gǎishàn* — "to make conditions better".

> *zhǐyào dàjiā yìqǐ nǔlì huánjìng jiù huì gǎishàn* — As long as we all work together, the environment will get better.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*gǎishàn* is built from 改 'to change' and *shàn* 'good', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *kojX* and *dzyenX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to alter' and the second 'good'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what would happen.
