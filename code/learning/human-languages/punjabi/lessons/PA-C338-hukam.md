## You'll want to know: ਹੁਕਮ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C338-PERM338-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ਹੁਕਮ** — *hukam* — "an order; a command".

> ਮੁੰਡਾ ਪਿਤਾ ਦਾ ਹੁਕਮ ਨਹੀਂ ਮੰਨਦਾ ਹੈ।
>
> *muṇḍā pitā dā hukam nahīṁ manndā hai.*
>
> The boy does not obey his father's order.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਹੁਕਮ** (*hukam*) is a loan, usually traced through Persian to Arabic *ḥukm*, "judgement".
