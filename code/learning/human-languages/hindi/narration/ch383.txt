Hindi, chapter 383: Danger and Damage.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
संकट (saṅkaṭ) — a crisis.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for to rub hard, then the Hindi for fragile.

You'll want to know: संकट.
संकट — saṅkaṭ — "a crisis".
अगर बारिश नहीं होगी तो किसानों पर संकट आएगा। agar bāriś nahīṃ hogī to kisānoṁ par saṅkaṭ āegā. If it doesn't rain, the farmers will face a crisis.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
संकट (saṅkaṭ) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit saṃkaṭa, "narrow; dangerous, critical" (Monier-Williams).

Before the new one: say the Hindi for damage, then the Hindi for deadly.

You'll want to know: उधेड़ना.
उधेड़ना — udheṛnā — "to unpick, to unravel".
अगर तुम यह धागा खींचोगे तो पूरा स्वेटर उधेड़ दोगे। agar tum yah dhāgā khīñcoge to pūrā sveṭar udheṛ doge. If you pull this thread, you'll unravel the whole jumper.
Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने पुराना स्वेटर उधेड़ा, usne purānā sveṭar udheṛā.

The word, taken apart.
उधेड़ना (udheṛnā) is a tadbhava, inherited from Sanskrit uddharati, "takes out" (CDIAL 2009).

Lesson 5 of 5.
भीगना (bhīgnā) — to get wet, to get soaked.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for deadly, then the Hindi for to unravel.

You'll want to know: भीगना.
भीगना — bhīgnā — "to get wet, to get soaked".
छाता ले लो, वरना भीग जाओगे। chātā le lo, varnā bhīg jāoge. Take an umbrella, or you'll get soaked.
Past (he …): वह बारिश में भीग गया, vah bāriś meṁ bhīg gayā.

The word, taken apart.
भीगना (bhīgnā) is inherited from bhiyagna, "smeared, wet", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 9500).
