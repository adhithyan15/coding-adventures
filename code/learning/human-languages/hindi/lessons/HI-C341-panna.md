## You'll want to know: पन्ना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C341-PANNA341-01]; assesses=[] -->

**पन्ना** — *pannā* — "a page; a leaf (of a book)".

> बारिश में इस किताब के कुछ पन्ने फट गए।
>
> *bāriś meṁ is kitāb ke kuch panne phaṭ gae.*
>
> Some pages of this book got torn in the rain.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पन्ना** (*pannā*) goes back to Sanskrit *parṇa*, "a leaf" (CDIAL 7918), where CDIAL gives Hindi *pannā*, a leaf or a page. A page of a book is a leaf, as in English.
