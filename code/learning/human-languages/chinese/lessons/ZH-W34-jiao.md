## Script — a small mouth, then two strokes
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-SCRIPT-JIAO-01]; assesses=[] -->

> 叫

**Two pieces, left before right.**

**The left piece, 口, three strokes**, as in 吗, small and set a little high: the left side, the top that turns down the right, the bottom that closes it.

**The right piece, two strokes.** A vertical that flicks up to the right at the foot. Then a long vertical, the tallest stroke in the character.

3 + 2 = five.
