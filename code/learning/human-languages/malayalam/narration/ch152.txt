Malayalam, chapter 152: Rat, Frog, Tortoise, Peacock, Parrot.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
എലി (eli) — a rat, a mouse.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for a pig, then the Malayalam for a lion.
[pause 1 second]
Say "good morning," and name the neighbour that uses the same word. (Suprabhātaṁ; Telugu.)

You'll want to know: എലി.
എലി — eli — "a rat, a mouse".

The word, taken apart.
എലി (eli) is native Dravidian, one word with Tamil eli, Kannada eli and Telugu eluka (DEDR 833).

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: eli]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: eli, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say eli]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Malayalam for a pig, then the Malayalam for a lion, then say eli again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does എലി (eli) mean? ("A rat, a mouse".) Say it once more.
