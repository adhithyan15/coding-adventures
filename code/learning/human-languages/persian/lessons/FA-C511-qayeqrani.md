## You'll want to know: قایقرانی
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C511-OUTDOORS-05]; assesses=[] -->

**قایقرانی** (*qâyeqrâni*) — "rowing, boating".

> تابستان در دریاچه قایقرانی کردیم.
>
> *tâbestân dar daryâche qâyeqrâni kardim.*
>
> In summer we went boating on the lake.

*qâyeq* 'boat' + *rân* 'driving' (stem of *rândan*) + *-i*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*qâyeqrâni* is built in Persian on *qâyeq*, 'boat', a loan usually traced to Turkic *qayıq*, and *rân*, the stem of inherited *rândan*, 'to drive'.
