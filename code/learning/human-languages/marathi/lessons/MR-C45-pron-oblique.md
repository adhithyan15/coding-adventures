# माझ्याबरोबर (mājhyābarobar) — a pronoun bends too

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MR-POSTP-BAROBAR, MR-LEX-MI] -->

[PAUSE 2s] *With my friend* works. Try *with me* and you will find you cannot
say it: **मी बरोबर** is not Marathi.

## Grammar Lens: the pronoun goes through its possessive first
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-GRAMMAR-PRON-OBLIQUE]; assesses=[MR-LEX-MAJHE, MR-LEX-TU-TUMHI, MR-GRAMMAR-OBLIQUE] -->

A noun takes an oblique stem before a postposition. A pronoun does too, and its
oblique stem is built on the **possessive** you already know rather than on the
subject form:

> **मी** → **माझ्या-** → **माझ्याबरोबर** — *with me*
>
> **तू** → **तुझ्या-** → **तुझ्याबरोबर** — *with you*
>
> **तुम्ही** → **तुमच्या-** → **तुमच्याबरोबर** — *with you* (respectful)

Read the middle column. Each one is **माझं / तुझं / तुमचं** with its ending
changed to **-या**, which is exactly what a noun in **-ए** did back in
chapter forty-one. The machinery is the same; only the starting form is
different, and the starting form is the possessive.

That is the rule worth holding, because it saves you from guessing every time:
**to put a pronoun in front of a postposition, take its possessive and bend
that.**

## Wrap-up Recall

[PAUSE 3s] Which form of a pronoun does the oblique start from? (**The
possessive**.) So what is *with you*, said to a stranger? (**तुमच्याबरोबर**.)
