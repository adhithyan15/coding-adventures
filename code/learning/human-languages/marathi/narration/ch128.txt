Marathi, chapter 128: Square, Garden, Park, Airport, Hospital.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
चौक (cauk) — a square, a crossroads.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a turn, then the Marathi for a corner.
[pause 2 seconds]
Point near and far: हा for the one here, तो for the one there, and तो again for he.
