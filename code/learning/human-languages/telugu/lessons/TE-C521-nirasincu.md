# నీరసించు (nīrasiñcu) — to grow weak

## You'll want to know: నీరసించు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C521-BALAM521-02]; assesses=[] -->

**నీరసించు** — *nīrasiñcu* — "to grow weak".

> జ్వరంతో పాప బాగా నీరసించింది.
>
> *jvarantō pāpa bāgā nīrasiñcindi.*
>
> With the fever the little girl grew very weak.

Past (I …): నీరసించాను, *nīrasiñcānu*.

The verb of నీరసం, weakness.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**నీరసించు** (*nīrasiñcu*) is built on Sanskrit *nīrasa*, "sapless, tasteless" (CDIAL 7556), with the Telugu verb ending -ఇంచు: to lose one's sap.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
