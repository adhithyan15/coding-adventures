Kannada, chapter 404: Helping Out.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
ಸದಸ್ಯ (sadasya) — a member.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for diversity, then the Kannada for to devise.

You'll want to know: ಸದಸ್ಯ.
ಸದಸ್ಯ — sadasya — "a member".
ನನ್ನ ಅಣ್ಣ ಈ ಸಂಘದ ಸದಸ್ಯ. nanna aṇṇa ī saṅghada sadasya. My elder brother is a member of this association.
Plural: ಸದಸ್ಯರು, sadasyaru.
ಸದಸ್ಯತ್ವ, membership.

The word, taken apart.
ಸದಸ್ಯ (sadasya) is borrowed from Sanskrit sadasya, "one who belongs to an assembly" (Monier-Williams), from sadas, "a seat, an assembly".

What you've built.
One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
