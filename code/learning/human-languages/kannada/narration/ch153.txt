Kannada, chapter 153: To open, To fill, To push, To pull, To cry.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
ತೆರೆ (tere) — to open.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to sell, then the Kannada for to walk.

You'll want to know: ತೆರೆ.
ತೆರೆ — tere — "to open".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: tere]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: tere, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say tere]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Kannada for to sell, then the Kannada for to walk, then say tere again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does ತೆರೆ (tere) mean? ("To open".) Say it once more.

Lesson 6 of 7.
Describing words and verbs — a first pass.

[your turn — say: bitter, sour, sweet, lazy, clever]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: bad, terrible, brave, golden, silver]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: to drink, to run, to sleep, to cut, to break]

[your turn — say: to send, to look for, to learn, to teach, to jump]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
Bitter? (ಕಹಿ, kahi.) Sour? (ಹುಳಿ, huḷi.) Sweet? (ಸಿಹಿ, sihi.) Lazy? (ಸೋಮಾರಿ, sōmāri.) Clever? (ಜಾಣ, jāṇa.) Bad? (ಕೆಟ್ಟ, keṭṭa.) Terrible? (ಭಯಂಕರ, bhayaṅkara.) Brave? (ಧೈರ್ಯವಂತ, dhairyavanta.) Golden? (ಚಿನ್ನದ, cinnada.) Silver? (ಬೆಳ್ಳಿಯ, beḷḷiya.) To drink? (ಕುಡಿ, kuḍi.) To run? (ಓಡು, ōḍu.) To sleep? (ಮಲಗು, malagu.) To cut? (ಕತ್ತರಿಸು, kattarisu.) To break? (ಒಡೆ, oḍe.) To send? (ಕಳಿಸು, kaḷisu.) To look for? (ಹುಡುಕು, huḍuku.) To learn? (ಕಲಿ, kali.) To teach? (ಕಲಿಸು, kalisu.) To jump? (ಜಿಗಿ, jigi.)
