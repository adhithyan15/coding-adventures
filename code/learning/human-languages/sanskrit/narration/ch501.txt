Lesson 1 of 7.
गृहस्थः (gṛhasthaḥ) — a householder.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a great-grandfather, then the Sanskrit for offspring.

You'll want to know: गृहस्थः.
गृहस्थः — gṛhasthaḥ — "a householder".
गृहस्थः कुटुम्बं पालयति। gṛhasthaḥ kuṭumbaṃ pālayati. The householder takes care of the family.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
गृहस्थः (gṛhasthaḥ) is built from गृहम्, "a house", and stha, "staying", from the root √sthā of तिष्ठति; MW gives "a householder", a Brāhman in the second stage of his life (MW).

What you've built.
One more word for describing what you lived and felt.

अरुणः भ्रात्रा सह सम्यक् संगच्छते। aruṇaḥ bhrātrā saha samyak saṅgacchate. Arun gets along well with his brother.
Past (he …): सः भ्रात्रा सह संगतवान्, saḥ bhrātrā saha saṅgatavān.

The word, taken apart.
संगच्छते (saṅgacchate) is built from sam, "together", and the root √gam, "to go", the root of गच्छति: to go together; MW's ppp saṃgata is "met, united, allied with" (MW).
