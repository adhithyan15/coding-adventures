Bengali, chapter 108: Broom, Pillow, Sheet, Quilt, Mat.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ঝাঁটা (jhā̃ṭā) — a broom.

Warm-up.

Before the new one: say the Bengali for a water pot, then the Bengali for a bucket.
