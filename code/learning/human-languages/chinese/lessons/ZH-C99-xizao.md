## You'll want to know: xǐzǎo
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C99-WASH99-04]; assesses=[] -->

*xǐzǎo* — "to have a shower; to have a bath".

> *wǒ wǎnshang xǐzǎo* — I have a shower in the evening.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

Two third tones again: xǐ rises before zǎo, and is still written xǐ.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*xǐzǎo* is built from 洗 and zǎo, both inherited from Middle Chinese, *sejX* and *tsawX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription); the dictionary glosses the first 'to wash and bathe' and the second with the first: to wash.
