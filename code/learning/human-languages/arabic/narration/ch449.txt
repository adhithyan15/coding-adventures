Arabic, chapter 449: Making a complaint.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
شكوى (shakwā) — a complaint.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to calm down, then the Arabic for to complain.

You'll want to know: شكوى.
شكوى — shakwā — "a complaint".
كتب أبي شكوى إلى المدير. kataba abī shakwā ilā al-mudīr. My father wrote a complaint to the manager.

The word, taken apart.
شكوى (shakwā) is built on the native Arabic root sh-k-w, "to complain" (Lane).

What you've built.

Lesson 2 of 5.
تعويض (taʿwīḍ) — compensation.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to complain, then the Arabic for a complaint.

You'll want to know: تعويض.
تعويض — taʿwīḍ — "compensation".
طلب المسافرون تعويضا بعد التأخير. ṭalaba al-musāfirūn taʿwīḍan baʿda at-taʾkhīr. The travellers asked for compensation after the delay.

The word, taken apart.
تعويض (taʿwīḍ) is built on the native Arabic root ʿ-w-ḍ, "to give in exchange" (Lane). It is the verbal noun of Form II; an ʿiwaḍ is a thing given in place of another.

Lesson 3 of 5.
عوض (ʿawwaḍa) — to compensate.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a complaint, then the Arabic for compensation.

You'll want to know: عوض.
عوض — ʿawwaḍa — "to compensate".
عوضت الشركة الركاب عن التأخير. ʿawwaḍat ash-sharika ar-rukkāb ʿan at-taʾkhīr. The company compensated the passengers for the delay.
Present: يعوض, yuʿawwiḍ.
Someone is compensated ʿan a loss.

عوض (ʿawwaḍa) is built on the native Arabic root ʿ-w-ḍ, "to give in exchange" (Lane). It is Form II.

تذمر (tadhammara) — to grumble.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for compensation, then the Arabic for to compensate.

You'll want to know: تذمر.
تذمر — tadhammara — "to grumble".
تذمر أخي من الطعام في الفندق. tadhammara akhī min aṭ-ṭaʿām fī al-funduq. My brother grumbled about the food in the hotel.
Present: يتذمر, yatadhammar.

The word, taken apart.
تذمر (tadhammara) is usually traced to the native Arabic root dh-m-r, "to be angry, to growl". It is Form V.

Lesson 5 of 5.
استبدل (istabdala) — to swap; to exchange (for another).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to compensate, then the Arabic for to grumble.

You'll want to know: استبدل.
استبدل — istabdala — "to swap; to exchange (for another)".
استبدلت القميص بقميص أكبر. istabdaltu al-qamīṣ bi-qamīṣ akbar. I swapped the shirt for a bigger one.
Present: يستبدل, yastabdil.
In modern use the new thing takes bi-; classical usage put bi- on the thing given up.

The word, taken apart.
استبدل (istabdala) is built on the native Arabic root b-d-l, "to change, to replace" (Lane). It is Form X.
