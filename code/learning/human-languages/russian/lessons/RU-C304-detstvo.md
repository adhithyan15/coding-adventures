## You'll want to know: детство
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C304-DETSTVO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**детство** (*détstvo*) — "childhood".

> Детство я провёл в деревне.
>
> *Détstvo ya provyól v derévne.*
>
> I spent my childhood in the countryside.

Neuter. *С детства* — since childhood.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*детство* is built on *дети* "children", from Proto-Slavic *dětę* "a child", which EtymDB traces to PIE *dʰeh₁(y)-* "to suckle".
