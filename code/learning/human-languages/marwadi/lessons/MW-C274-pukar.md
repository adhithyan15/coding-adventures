## You'll want to know: पुकार
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C274-SAHARO274-02]; assesses=[] -->

**पुकार** — *pukār* — "a cry for help".

> हूं पुकार सुणूं हूं।
>
> *hū̃ pukār suṇū̃ hū̃.*
>
> I hear a cry for help.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पुकार** (*pukār*) is inherited from an imitative *\*pukkār*, "shout"; CDIAL lists Old Marwari *pukāra*, "complaint" (CDIAL 8246).
