## You'll want to know: કોયડો
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C524-SANGRAH524-05]; assesses=[] -->

**કોયડો** — *koyḍo* — "a puzzle".

> અરુણે ગણિતનો એક અઘરો કોયડો ઉકેલ્યો.
>
> *Aruṇe gaṇitno ek aghro koyḍo ukelyo.*
>
> Arun solved a hard maths puzzle.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**કોયડો** (*koyḍo*) is something to be solved, **ઉકેલવું**. CDIAL files the Gujarati word, "a puzzle, a riddle", under Sanskrit *kuha*, "a cheat" (CDIAL 3385); how a trick became a puzzle is not explained there.
