You'll want to know: o século.
o século — "a century".

The word, taken apart.
século from Latin saeculum, "an age, a generation" (EtymDB 2.1) — a learned borrowing from Latin.

Lesson 2 of 5.
muitas vezes — often.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for a date, then the Portuguese for a century.

You'll want to know: muitas vezes.
muitas vezes — "often".

The word, taken apart.
muitas vezes is "many times": muito from Latin multus, "much" (EtymDB 2.1), and vez, "time, turn" from Latin vicis, "turn, change" (Lewis & Short).
