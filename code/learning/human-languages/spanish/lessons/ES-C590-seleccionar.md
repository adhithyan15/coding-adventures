## You'll want to know: seleccionar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C590-VACANTE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**seleccionar** — "to select, to choose".

> Seleccionamos a tres personas para la entrevista.
>
> *We selected three people for the interview.*

More formal than *elegir*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*seleccionar* is built on *selección*, a learned borrowing from Latin *selectio* 'a picking out' (EtymDB), from *seligere*, built on *legere* 'to gather'.

One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.
