## You'll want to know: ارتجف
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C516-HARAKA516-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ارتجف** — *irtajafa* — "to tremble".

> ارتجفت يد الطالب من التوتر.
>
> *irtajafat yad aṭ-ṭālib min at-tawattur.*
>
> The student's hand trembled from nerves.

Present: يرتجف, *yartajif*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ارتجف** (*irtajafa*) is built on the native Arabic root *r-j-f*, "to quake, to shake" (Lane). Form VIII irtajafa is to shake all over.

## What you've built
