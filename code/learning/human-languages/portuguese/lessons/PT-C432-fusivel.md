## You'll want to know: o fusível
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C432-LUZ-01]; assesses=[] -->

**o fusível** — "the fuse".

> Saltou um fusível e a cozinha ficou sem luz.
>
> *A fuse blew and the kitchen lost its lights.*

*Saltar* — to blow, of a fuse or a breaker.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*fusível* came from French *fusible* 'that can melt', built on Latin *fūsus*, from *fundere* 'to melt, to pour' (Lewis & Short).
