# കരകവിയുക (karakaviyuka) — to overflow its banks

## You'll want to know: കരകവിയുക
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C512-PRALAYAM512-01]; assesses=[] -->

**കരകവിയുക** — *karakaviyuka* — "to overflow its banks".

> കനത്ത മഴയിൽ പുഴ കരകവിഞ്ഞു.
>
> *kanatta maḻayil puḻa karakaviññu.*
>
> In the heavy rain the river overflowed its banks.

Past: കരകവിഞ്ഞു, *karakaviññu*.

കര, the bank, plus കവിയുക, to overflow.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**കരകവിയുക** (*karakaviyuka*) is a native Dravidian compound of കര, a bank or shore (DEDR 1293), and കവിയുക, to overflow (DEDR 1221).
