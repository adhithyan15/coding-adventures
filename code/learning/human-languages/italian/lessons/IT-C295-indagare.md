## You'll want to know: indagare
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C295-INDAGINE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**indagare** — "to investigate".

> La polizia indaga sulla rapina.
>
> *The police are investigating the robbery.*

*Indagare su* qualcosa.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*indagare* is a learned borrowing from Latin *indāgāre* "to track down" (Lewis & Short), first said of hunting dogs; EtymDB links it with *indāgō* "a ring of hunters".
