## You'll want to know: مجلس
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C385-MUNAZZAMA385-04]; assesses=[] -->

**مجلس** — *majlis* — "a council".

> اجتمع مجلس المدينة أمس.
>
> *ijtamaʿa majlis al-madīna ams.*
>
> The city council met yesterday.

Plural: مجالس, *majālis*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**مجلس** (*majlis*) is built on the native Arabic root *j-l-s*, "to sit" (Lane). *Majlis* is a noun of place, a place of sitting: a council.
