## You'll want to know: юридический
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C391-ZAKONODATELSTVO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**юридический** (*yuridícheskiy*) — "of the law, legal".

> Мне нужна юридическая помощь.
>
> *Mne nuzhná yuridícheskaya pómoshch.*
>
> I need legal help.

*Юридический факультет* — a law faculty.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**юридический** is usually traced to Latin *iūridicus* "of the administering of justice", from *iūs* "right, law" and *dīcere* "to say" (Lewis & Short).
