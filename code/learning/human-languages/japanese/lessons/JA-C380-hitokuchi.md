## You'll want to know: ひとくち
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C380-EKIBEN380-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ひとくち** — *hitokuchi* — "a mouthful; a bite".

> **えきべんを ひとくち たべました。** — *ekiben o hitokuchi tabemashita* — "I had a bite of the station lunch box."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ひとくち** (*hitokuchi*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, built from *hito*, "one" and *kuchi*, "mouth"; its kanji were attached later as kun'yomi (JMdict).
