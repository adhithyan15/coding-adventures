## You'll want to know: la poppa
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C423-TRAGHETTO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**la poppa** — "the stern (of a ship)".

> Ci sediamo a poppa, c'è meno vento.
>
> *We sit at the stern, there is less wind.*

*Avere il vento in poppa* — to have the wind behind you; to do well.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*poppa* is inherited from Latin *puppis* 'stern' (EtymDB).
