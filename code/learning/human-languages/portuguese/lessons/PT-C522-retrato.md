## You'll want to know: o retrato
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C522-GALERIA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**o retrato** — "portrait".

> Na sala há um retrato da bisavó.
>
> *In the living room there is a portrait of my great-grandmother.*

Also a photo of a person.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*retrato* comes from Italian *ritratto* (EtymDB), from Latin *retractus*, built on *retrahere* "to draw back", from *trahere* "to draw".
