Telugu, chapter 348: Robbers on the Road.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
దోపిడీ (dōpiḍī) — a robbery; plunder.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to greet warmly, then the Telugu for to meet.

You'll want to know: దోపిడీ.
దోపిడీ — dōpiḍī — "a robbery; plunder".
రాత్రి బ్యాంకులో దోపిడీ జరిగింది. rātri byāṅkulō dōpiḍī jarigindi. There was a robbery at the bank at night.
You know దోచుకో, to rob; దోపిడీ (dōpiḍī) is the deed.

The word, taken apart.
దోపిడీ (dōpiḍī) is native Dravidian: DEDR 3541 lists Telugu dōpiḍi, "plunder, pillage", with dōcu, "to plunder, rob".

దాడి చేయు (dāḍi cēyu) is a Telugu phrase: dāḍi, "an attack, a raid", whose origin is not settled, with the native verb cēyu, "to do".

Lesson 3 of 5.
లాక్కో (lākkō) — to snatch; to grab.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a robbery, then the Telugu for to attack.

You'll want to know: లాక్కో.
లాక్కో — lākkō — "to snatch; to grab".
దొంగ ఆమె సంచి లాక్కుని పారిపోయాడు. doṅga āme sañci lākkuni pāripōyāḍu. The thief snatched her bag and ran away.
Past (I …): లాక్కున్నాను, lākkunnānu.
లాగు, to pull, with -కో, for oneself: to pull something away to yourself.

The word, taken apart.
లాక్కో (lākkō) is lāgu, "to pull", with the self-ending -kō; the origin of lāgu is not settled.

Lesson 4 of 5.

[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: కాపుకాచు.
కాపుకాచు — kāpukācu — "to lie in wait".
దొంగలు చీకట్లో దారి పక్కన కాపుకాశారు. doṅgalu cīkaṭlō dāri pakkana kāpukāśāru. The robbers lay in wait by the road in the dark.
Past (I …): కాపుకాశాను, kāpukāśānu.
కాపు, watch, with a verb meaning to guard: to keep watch for someone.

The word, taken apart.
కాపుకాచు (kāpukācu) is native Dravidian: DEDR 1416 lists Telugu kācu, "to guard, watch; to wait for", and kāpu, "protection, a guard".

Lesson 5 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to snatch, then the Telugu for to lie in wait.

You'll want to know: మారువేషం.
మారువేషం — māruvēṣaṁ — "a disguise".
దొంగ పాలవాడి మారువేషంలో వచ్చాడు. doṅga pālavāḍi māruvēṣaṁlō vaccāḍu. The thief came disguised as a milkman.
మారు, other or changed, with వేషం, dress: మారువేషంలో, in disguise.

The word, taken apart.
మారువేషం (māruvēṣaṁ) is built from māru, "other, changed", and Sanskrit veṣa, "dress, an assumed appearance" (Monier-Williams).
