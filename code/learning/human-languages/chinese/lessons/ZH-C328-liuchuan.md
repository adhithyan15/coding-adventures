## You'll want to know: liúchuán
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C328-LEGEND328-03]; assesses=[] -->

*liúchuán* — "to be handed down; to spread".

> *zhège chuánshuō liúchuán le yìqiān nián* — This legend has been handed down for a thousand years.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*liúchuán* is built from *liú* 'to flow' and 传 'to pass on', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *ljuw* and *drjwen* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first, quoting an older dictionary, 'water moving'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
