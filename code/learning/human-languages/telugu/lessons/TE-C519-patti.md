## You'll want to know: పట్టీ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C519-CIKITSA519-04]; assesses=[] -->

**పట్టీ** — *paṭṭī* — "a bandage; a strip".

> నర్సు గాయానికి పట్టీ కట్టింది.
>
> *narsu gāyāniki paṭṭī kaṭṭindi.*
>
> The nurse bandaged the wound.

పట్టీ కట్టు, to bandage. Also a strip of cloth or a band.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**పట్టీ** (*paṭṭī*) is usually traced to Sanskrit *paṭṭa*, "cloth; a bandage, a fillet" (CDIAL 7700).
