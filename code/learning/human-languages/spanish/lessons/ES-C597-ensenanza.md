## You'll want to know: la enseñanza
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C597-APRENDE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**la enseñanza** — "the teaching, the education".

> La enseñanza pública es gratuita.
>
> *State education is free.*

*La enseñanza secundaria* — secondary education.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*enseñanza* is built on *enseñar*, inherited from Medieval Latin *insignare* 'to mark', from *signum* 'sign' (EtymDB).
