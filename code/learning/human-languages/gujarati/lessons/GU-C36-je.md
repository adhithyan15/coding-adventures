## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-PRON-JE-01]; assesses=[GU-JOIN-JYAARE-01, GU-PRON-TE-01] -->

> **જે** — *je* — **the one who**, **the thing that**

Now the stem from **જ્યારે** stands alone. **જે** opens a clause that describes
somebody or something, instead of naming them:

> **જે** *(what they do)* … — "the one who …"

Notice the pairing: **જ્યારે** was *when*, **જે** is *who* or *which*, and both
begin the same way. Gujarati marks a describing clause at its front, the way it
marked a condition with **જો**.
