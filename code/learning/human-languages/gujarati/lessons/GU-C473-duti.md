## You'll want to know: ડૂંટી
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C473-JANGH473-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ડૂંટી** — *ḍū̃ṭī* — "the navel".

> બાળકની ડૂંટી પર દાક્તરે મલમ લગાડ્યો.
>
> *bāḷaknī ḍū̃ṭī par dāktare malam lagāḍyo.*
>
> The doctor put ointment on the baby's navel.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ડૂંટી** (*ḍū̃ṭī*) is inherited from Sanskrit *tundi*, "navel"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati word (CDIAL 5860).
