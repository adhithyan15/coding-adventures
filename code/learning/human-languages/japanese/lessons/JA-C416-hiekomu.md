## You'll want to know: ひえこむ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C416-YOAKE416-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ひえこむ** — *hiekomu* — "to turn very cold".

> **ふゆの あさは ひえこみます。** — *fuyu no asa wa hiekomimasu* — "Winter mornings turn very cold."

Polite **ひえこみます**, *hiekomimasu*; a godan verb. Past (polite): **けさは ひえこみました。**, *kesa wa hiekomimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ひえこむ** (*hiekomu*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, built from *hie*, "growing cold" (from *hieru*) and *komu*, "going deep"; its kanji were attached later as kun'yomi (JMdict).
