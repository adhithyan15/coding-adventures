# చెడగొట్టు (ceḍagoṭṭu) — to spoil; to ruin

## You'll want to know: చెడగొట్టు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C490-CIRUGU490-01]; assesses=[] -->

**చెడగొట్టు** — *ceḍagoṭṭu* — "to spoil; to ruin".

> వర్షం మా విహారయాత్రను చెడగొట్టింది.
>
> *varṣaṁ mā vihārayātranu ceḍagoṭṭindi.*
>
> The rain spoiled our outing.

Past (I …): చెడగొట్టాను, *ceḍagoṭṭānu*.

చెడ, bad, + కొట్టు, to strike: to make something go bad. Said of plans, food and children who are spoilt.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**చెడగొట్టు** (*ceḍagoṭṭu*) is native Dravidian: చెడు, "to go bad" (DEDR 2760), and కొట్టు, "to strike" (DEDR 2063).
