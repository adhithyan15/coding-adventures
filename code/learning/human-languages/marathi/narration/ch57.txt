Marathi, chapter 57: Thirteen to Sixteen.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
तेरा (terā) — thirteen.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Count from एक to बारा and stop. The book has taken you no further than that.

You'll want to know: तेरा.
तेरा — terā — 13.
Sanskrit त्रयोदश (trayodaśa), three-ten. The त्र- of three is still at the front — the same त्र you can hear in English three — and the -रा is the ten again.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: terā — thirteen]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: naū dahā akrā bārā — the run up to it, without looking]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say sudhā — too]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say sudhā nāhī — not even]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[question — say your answer, then pause 20 seconds]
Say thirteen in Marathi, then run the last few numbers up to it.

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What is thirteen in Marathi? (तेरा (terā).) Say the number before it, and the one before that.
Sources: Wiktionary: तेरा (terā).


Lesson 2 of 5.
चौदा (chaudā) — fourteen.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Count from एक to तेरा (terā) and stop. The book has taken you no further than that.

You'll want to know: चौदा.
चौदा — chaudā — 14.
Sanskrit चतुर्दश (caturdaśa), four-ten. The चतुर् collapsed into a single चौ, which is why this is the number that needed a new vowel sign before it could be written at all.
The -दा here rather than -रा is not a break in the pattern: it is the same दश, stopped one step earlier on the way down.

Guided Practice.

[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: dahā akrā bārā terā — the run up to it, without looking]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say khūp — very]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[question — say your answer, then pause 20 seconds]
Say fourteen in Marathi, then run the last few numbers up to it.

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What is fourteen in Marathi? (चौदा (chaudā).) Say the number before it, and the one before that.

Lesson 3 of 5.
पंधरा (pandhrā) — fifteen.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Count from एक to चौदा (chaudā) and stop. The book has taken you no further than that.

You'll want to know: पंधरा.
पंधरा — pandhrā — 15.
Sanskrit pañcadaśa, five-ten, written with a nasal this book has not taught. The पं- is the पाच of chapter thirteen with its vowel squeezed out, and the -रा is the ten. You have now seen the same construction five times.

[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[question — say your answer, then pause 20 seconds]
Say fifteen in Marathi, then run the last few numbers up to it.

Lesson 4 of 5.
सोळा (soḷā) — sixteen.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Count from एक to पंधरा (pandhrā) and stop. The book has taken you no further than that.

You'll want to know: सोळा.
सोळा — soḷā — 16.
Sanskrit षोडश (ṣoḍaśa), six-ten. The सो- is the सहा you learned first in this run, and the -ळा is the ten again — but written with ळ, the retroflex l that Marathi has and Hindi does not.

[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say kitī? — how many?]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[question — say your answer, then pause 20 seconds]
Say sixteen in Marathi, then run the last few numbers up to it.

Wrap-up Recall.

Lesson 5 of 5.
Four more teens — four more teens — and the unit in front of each one is a number you already had.

Warm-up.
[pause 4 seconds]
Count from दहा to सोळा (soḷā). Then take each teen apart and name the number hiding at its front.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: dahā … soḷā — seven numbers without stopping]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: pandhrā and then pāch — and hear the five inside the fifteen]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: soḷā and then sahā — and hear the six inside the sixteen]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[question — say your answer, then pause 30 seconds]
Count ten to sixteen, then name the unit inside each teen.

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
Which number is hiding inside सोळा (soḷā)? (सहा.) Which sign did चौदा (chaudā) need before it could be written? (ौ.)
