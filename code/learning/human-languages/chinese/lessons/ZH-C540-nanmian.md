## You'll want to know: nánmiǎn
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C540-NOWONDER540-03]; assesses=[] -->

*nánmiǎn* — "bound to happen; hard to avoid".

> *rúguǒ shì dì yī cì zuò nánmiǎn huì chūcuò* — If it's your first time, mistakes are bound to happen.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*nánmiǎn* is built from 难 'hard' and *miǎn* 'to escape', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *nan* and *mjenX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'difficult' and the second 'to stop' and 'to get free of': hard to get free of.

## What you've built
