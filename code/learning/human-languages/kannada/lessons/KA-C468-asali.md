## You'll want to know: ಅಸಲಿ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C468-GATRA468-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ಅಸಲಿ** — *asali* — "genuine; real".

> ಇದು ಅಸಲಿ ರೇಷ್ಮೆ ಅಂತ ಅಂಗಡಿಯವನು ಹೇಳಿದನು.
>
> *idu asali rēṣme anta aṅgaḍiyavanu hēḷidanu.*
>
> The shopkeeper said this is genuine silk.

Its opposite is ನಕಲಿ, a fake, from ನಕಲು, a copy.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಅಸಲಿ** (*asali*) is usually traced to Arabic *aṣl*, "root, origin", through Hindustani *aslī*, "original, genuine".
