## You'll want to know: मकड़ी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C519-DHOOL519-02]; assesses=[] -->

**मकड़ी** — *makṛī* — "a spider".

> कोने में एक बड़ी मकड़ी बैठी है।
>
> *Kone meṁ ek baṛī makṛī baiṭhī hai.*
>
> A big spider is sitting in the corner.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**मकड़ी** (*makṛī*) is a tadbhava, inherited from Sanskrit *markaṭa*, "a spider" (CDIAL 9883).
