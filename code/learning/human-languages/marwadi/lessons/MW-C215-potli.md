## You'll want to know: पोटली
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C215-WRAP215-03]; assesses=[] -->

**पोटली** — *poṭlī* — "a small bundle".

> पोटली मांय रोटी है।
>
> *poṭlī mā̃y roṭī hai.*
>
> There is flatbread in the bundle.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पोटली** (*poṭlī*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*pōṭṭa*, "bundle". CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry, but lists Hindi *poṭlī*, "small bundle" (CDIAL 8396).
