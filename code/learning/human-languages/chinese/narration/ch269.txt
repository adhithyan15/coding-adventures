Mandarin Chinese, chapter 269: When I Was Little.
6 lessons. You can do the first 2 of them in the car; after that you will want to have stopped.


Lesson 1 of 6.
从小 (cóngxiǎo) — since childhood; since I was little.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for the answer to a question, then the Mandarin for to explain.

You'll want to know: 从小.
从小 — cóngxiǎo — "since childhood; since I was little".
我从小喜欢看书 wǒ cóngxiǎo xǐhuan kànshū — I've loved books since I was little.

The word, taken apart.
从小 (cóngxiǎo) is built from 从 'from' and 小 'small', both inherited from Middle Chinese, dzjowng and sjewX (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription).

What you've built.
One more word for talking about the past.

Lesson 2 of 6.
先 (xiān) — first; before anything else.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for to explain, then the Mandarin for since childhood.

You'll want to know: 先.
先 — xiān — "first; before anything else".
我们先吃饭 wǒmen xiān chīfàn — Let's eat first.
The new character, in pieces: 先 (xiān) — six strokes: a short stroke falling to the left, a short bar, a vertical and a long bar; then 儿 at the foot. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

The word, taken apart.
先 (xiān) is inherited from Middle Chinese sen (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'before and after'.

Lesson 3 of 6.
先 (xiān) — a cross and a long bar, over 儿.

Before we start: this one needs your hands, so it is not a driving lesson. You will want a pen and something to write on and your eyes, for letter shapes on the page. [once you have stopped driving — this part needs your eyes or your hands; if you are driving, skip ahead to the next part]
Script — a cross and a long bar, over 儿.
先 (xiān).
Two pieces, top before bottom.
The top piece, four strokes. A short stroke falling to the left. A short bar to the right of it. A vertical down through the bar. Then a long bar across, wider than everything above it.
The bottom piece, 儿, two strokes, as on its own: a stroke falling to the left, then a vertical that bends to the right along the foot and hooks up.
4 + 2 = six.

Guided Practice.

[once you have stopped driving — cover: write it again from memory, and count the pieces, not the strokes]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
How many strokes in 先 (xiān)? (Six.) What sits at the foot? (儿.) Which bar is longer? (The second, across the middle.)

Lesson 4 of 6.
tóngnián — childhood.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for since childhood, then the Mandarin for before anything else.

You'll want to know: tóngnián.
tóngnián — "childhood".
wǒ de tóngnián hěn kuàilè — I had a very happy childhood.
Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
tóngnián is built from tóng 'a child' and 年 'a year', both inherited from Middle Chinese, duwng and nen (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first as a young person who does not yet have a household.

Lesson 6 of 6.
wánjù — toy.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for childhood, then the Mandarin for originally.

You'll want to know: wánjù.
wánjù — "toy".
zhège wánjù hěn yǒu yìsi — This toy is a lot of fun.
Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
wánjù is built from wán 'to play' and jù, both inherited from Middle Chinese, ngwanH and gjuH (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to toy with' and the second 'to provide'. Middle Chinese ngwanH would regularly give wàn; the rising tone of wán is irregular.
