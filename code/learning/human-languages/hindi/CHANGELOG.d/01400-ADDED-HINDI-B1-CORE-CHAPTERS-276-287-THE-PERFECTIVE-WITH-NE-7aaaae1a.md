## Added — Hindi B1 core, chapters 276–287: the perfective with ने, the full future and all five B1 spine nodes

- Added twelve core chapters, 50 lessons (sequence 15300–15790). Chapters
  276–277 bridge the past and the future the A2 track had only touched: the
  perfective by person (वह गया, वह गई, वे गए; आया, हुआ, and गया after a bare
  stem), the ने construction with the verb agreeing with the object (उसने रोटी
  खाई, उसने खाना खाया, मैंने किताबें पढ़ीं), the masculine default after को
  (उसने मीरा को देखा), the irregular किया, दिया, लिया, पिया, and कहना (कहा,
  कही) for reporting speech, taught inside the ने lesson; then the future for
  every person (करूँगा … करेंगे, जाऊँगा, लूँगा, दूँगा) and होगा / होगी / होंगे.
- Chapters 278–287 realize the five B1 nodes, two chapters each, with one
  grammar lesson per B1 concept under its exact concept tag: narrating (कर रहा
  था, a scene with जब … तब, -कर in a story with the last verb deciding ने,
  -ने के बाद, पहले / फिर / उसके बाद / आख़िर में), giving reasons (मुझे लगता है
  कि, मेरी राय में, की वजह से, के कारण, वजह यह है कि, चाहिए with a verb,
  पड़ना of obligation, इसलिए between cause and result, तो), handling travel
  (स्टेशन कैसे जाते हैं?, कितनी दूर है?, सीधे जाइए, बाएँ मुड़िए, buying a
  ticket, कितने बजे चलेगी?, कितना समय लगेगा?, a lost bag and a missed train),
  describing experience (feelings with मुझे … लगता है and with होना, बनना
  चाहना, मुझे उम्मीद है कि with the future, से ज़्यादा / से कम, सबसे in a
  group, से for a stretch up to now, दिन भर) and expressing conditions (अगर …
  तो with the future or the plain past, नहीं तो, … पर निर्भर करता है, the
  counterfactual -ता). Spine mirrors 0250–0290 no longer omit any concept.
  No subjunctive and no passive anywhere in the core.
- Thirteen new headwords, each a word lesson with a cited origin (CDIAL,
  Monier-Williams, or a named and hedged Persian or Arabic donor): पिछला,
  आख़िर, लगना, ऐसा, अतः, बताना, दूर, अगला, खोना, ख़ुश, सबसे, वरना and निर्भर
  करना. Four of them are verbs.
- Two letter lessons, each right after its anchor word: ऐ (HI-W280-letter-ai,
  after ऐसा) and the visarga ः (HI-W281-sign-visarga, after अतः, labelled
  formal). Each quotes its inventory row's numbered stroke order, pen lifts,
  note and source; script-ductus already draws both, and ः is drawn alone with
  its piece of headline left grey. No inventory row changes.
- An eighty-nine-word reading in chapter 279 (Mira's summer holiday in the
  village) and a six-line connected writing task, HI-W287-six-lines, in
  chapter 287. Each chapter ends on its review, which is the chapter's payoff.
