Persian, chapter 471: Fishing and blossom.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
شکار (shekâr) — hunting; prey, game.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to go for an outing, then the Persian for to stop for the night.

You'll want to know: شکار.
شکار (shekâr) — "hunting; prey, game".
شکار (shekâr) در این منطقه ممنوع است. Shekâr dar in mantaqe mamnu' ast. Hunting is forbidden in this area.
A hunter is a shekârchi.

The word, taken apart.
shekâr is a native word, which EtymDB lists with the senses 'hunting, game'; it is usually traced to Middle Persian škār.

Lesson 2 of 5.
ماهیگیری (mâhigiri) — fishing.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to stop for the night, then the Persian for hunting.

You'll want to know: ماهیگیری.
ماهیگیری (mâhigiri) — "fishing".
پدرم جمعه ها به ماهیگیری میرود. Pedaram jom'e-hâ be mâhigiri miravad. My father goes fishing on Fridays.
mâhi 'fish' + gir 'catching' + -i.

The word, taken apart.
mâhigiri is a native compound of mâhi 'fish', from Middle Persian māhīg (EtymDB), and gir, the present stem of gereftan 'to take', with the ending -i.

Warm-up.

Before the new one: say the Persian for hunting, then the Persian for fishing.

You'll want to know: ماهی گرفتن.
ماهی گرفتن (mâhi gereftan) — "to fish, to catch fish".
کنار دریاچه ماهی گرفتیم. Kenâr-e daryâche mâhi gereftim. We fished by the lake.
Present stem gir-: mâhi migirim.

The word, taken apart.
mâhi gereftan is built from mâhi 'fish', inherited from Middle Persian māhīg (EtymDB), and the native verb gereftan 'to take'.

Lesson 5 of 5.
شکفتن (shekoftan) — to bloom, to open (of a flower).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to fish, then the Persian for a blossom.

You'll want to know: شکفتن.
شکفتن (shekoftan) — "to bloom, to open (of a flower)".
گلهای حیاط امروز صبح شکفتند. Gol-hâ-ye hayât emruz sobh shekoftand. The flowers in the courtyard opened this morning.
Present stem shekof-: mishekofad. A smiling face is also said to shekoftan.

The word, taken apart.
shekoftan is a native verb, usually traced to Middle Persian škuftan 'to bloom, to burst open'. EtymDB does not list it.
