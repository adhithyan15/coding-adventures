## You'll want to know: 怪事
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C539-BLAME539-03]; assesses=[] -->

**怪事** — *guàishì* — "a strange business; an odd thing".

> **这真是怪事** *zhè zhēn shì guàishì* — That really is a strange business.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**怪事** (*guàishì*) is built from 怪 'strange' and 事 'a matter', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *kweajH* and *dzriH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'strange'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what would happen.
