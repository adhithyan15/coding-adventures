## You'll want to know: чокаться
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C315-UGOSHCHENIYE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**чокаться** (*chókatsya*) — "to clink glasses".

> Гости чокались и смеялись.
>
> *Gósti chókalis i smeyális.*
>
> The guests clinked glasses and laughed.

Imperfective; perfective *чокнуться* (*chóknutsya*). Careful: in slang *чокнутый* means "crazy".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*чокаться* is built on an imitative syllable *чок* for the ring of glasses; EtymDB lists no parent.
