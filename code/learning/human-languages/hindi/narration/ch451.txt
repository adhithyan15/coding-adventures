Hindi, chapter 451: Outings and Views.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
सैर (sair) — an outing; a stroll.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for to grow (of crops), then the Hindi for to milk.

You'll want to know: सैर.
सैर — sair — "an outing; a stroll".
छुट्टी में हम पहाड़ों की सैर पर गए। chuṭṭī meṁ ham pahāṛoṁ kī sair par gae. On holiday we went on a trip to the hills.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
सैर (sair) is usually traced to Arabic sayr, "travel, going about", through Persian: a trip for pleasure.

Lesson 2 of 7.
पर्यटक (paryaṭak) — a tourist.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for to milk, then the Hindi for an outing.

You'll want to know: पर्यटक.
पर्यटक — paryaṭak — "a tourist".
गर्मियों में यहाँ बहुत पर्यटक आते हैं। garmiyoṁ meṁ yahā̃ bahut paryaṭak āte haiṁ. A lot of tourists come here in the summer.
Masculine or feminine, by the person meant.

The word, taken apart.
पर्यटक (paryaṭak) is a tatsama, Sanskrit paryaṭaka, one who wanders about (Monier-Williams gives a tramp, a vagabond); the modern sense of a tourist is the newer one.

[pause 2 seconds]

नज़ारा — nazārā — "a view, a sight".
पहाड़ से शहर का नज़ारा बहुत सुंदर था। pahāṛ se śahar kā nazārā bahut sundar thā. The view of the city from the mountain was beautiful.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
नज़ारा (nazārā) is usually traced to Arabic naẓāra, "a looking, a sight", through Persian; the root is the same as in नज़र, a glance.

Lesson 4 of 7.
स्मारक (smārak) — a monument, a memorial.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a tourist, then the Hindi for a view.

You'll want to know: स्मारक.
स्मारक — smārak — "a monument, a memorial".
शहर के बीच में एक पुराना स्मारक है। śahar ke bīc meṁ ek purānā smārak hai. There is an old monument in the middle of the town.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
स्मारक (smārak) is a tatsama, Sanskrit smāraka, "recalling, reminding of" (Monier-Williams), from the root smṛ, "to remember": a monument is what reminds.

Lesson 5 of 7.
निहारना (nihārnā) — to gaze at (admiringly).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a view, then the Hindi for a monument.

You'll want to know: निहारना.
निहारना — nihārnā — "to gaze at (admiringly)".
मीरा ने देर तक पहाड़ों को निहारा। Mīrā ne der tak pahāṛoṁ ko nihārā. Mira gazed at the mountains for a long time.
Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने सूर्योदय को निहारा, usne sūryoday ko nihārā.

The word, taken apart.
निहारना (nihārnā) is a tadbhava of Sanskrit nibhālayati, "perceives" (CDIAL 7228), which lists Hindi nihārnā, "to look at": to look long, and with pleasure.
