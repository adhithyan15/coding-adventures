# सांझ (sā̃jh) — dusk

## You'll want to know: सांझ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C166-JAG166-05]; assesses=[] -->

**सांझ** — *sā̃jh* — "dusk".

> सांझ घणी सांत है।
>
> *sā̃jh ghaṇī sānt hai.*
>
> Dusk is very calm.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सांझ** (*sā̃jh*) is inherited from Sanskrit *sandhyā*, "twilight", the joining of day and night; CDIAL lists Hindi *sā̃jh* (CDIAL 12918). CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry.
