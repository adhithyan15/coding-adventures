Japanese, chapter 488: Building Work.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
けんちく (kenchiku) — architecture; building.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for sales, then the Japanese for an employee.

You'll want to know: けんちく.
けんちく — kenchiku — "architecture; building".
あにの しごとは けんちくです。 — ani no shigoto wa kenchiku desu — "My elder brother works in building."

The word, taken apart.
けんちく (kenchiku) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are ken, "build" (Middle Chinese kjonH) and chiku, "fabricate" (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
