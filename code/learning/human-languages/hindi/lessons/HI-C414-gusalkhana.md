## You'll want to know: गुसलख़ाना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C414-GUSALKHANA414-01]; assesses=[] -->

**गुसलख़ाना** — *gusalkhānā* — "a bathroom".

> हमारे कमरे का गुसलख़ाना छोटा है।
>
> *hamāre kamre kā gusalkhānā choṭā hai.*
>
> The bathroom in our room is small.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**गुसलख़ाना** (*gusalkhānā*) is usually traced to Persian *ġusl-khāna*, a room (*khāna*) for washing (*ġusl*); Hindi speech smooths ġusl to gusal.
