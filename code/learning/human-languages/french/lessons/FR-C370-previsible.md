## You'll want to know: prévisible
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C370-IMPREVU-02]; assesses=[] -->

**prévisible** — "foreseeable, predictable".

> Ce retard était prévisible avec cette neige.
>
> *This delay was predictable with this snow.*

One form for both genders.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*prévisible* is built on *prévoir* "to foresee" and the suffix *-ible*. *prévoir* comes from Latin *praevidēre* "to foresee", built on *vidēre* "to see" (Lewis & Short).
