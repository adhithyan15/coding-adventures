## You'll want to know: nieseln
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C461-NIESELREGEN-02]; assesses=[] -->

**nieseln** — "to drizzle".

> Es nieselt schon den ganzen Vormittag.
>
> *It has been drizzling all morning.*

Mostly with *es*: *es nieselt*, *es hat genieselt*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

The origin of *nieseln* is not settled; EtymDB lists it without a source. It is sometimes linked to *niesen* 'to sneeze', for the fine spray.
