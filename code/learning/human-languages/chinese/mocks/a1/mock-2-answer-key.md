## Writing — 100 points

Score independent Chinese production with the form-specific rubric. These
examples are assessor calibration only, never a copyable model.

<!-- mock-part:a1-writing-practical-form -->
41. Five fields, 8 points each. Valid examples: actual name; 我今天在家 or
    我今天不在家; 我明天在家看书 or 我明天在大学看书; 我要水 or 我要点心;
    再见. Accept other truthful, comprehensible A1 equivalents.
<!-- /mock-part:a1-writing-practical-form -->

<!-- mock-part:a1-writing-short-message -->
42. Require 2–4 independently produced clauses: greeting, tomorrow's reading
    place, water/snack choice, and a simple question back. A calibration response
    could be “你好！我明天在家看书。我要水。你呢？” Do not require these exact
    words; score communication and character legibility.
<!-- /mock-part:a1-writing-short-message -->

## Speaking — 100 points

Say these prompts aloud in Mandarin without showing this key. One slow repeat
is allowed. Time and record responses. Do not coach or supply content words.

<!-- mock-part:a1-speaking-personal-exchange -->
43. “早上好！” — a reciprocal greeting is appropriate.
44. “你叫什么名字？” — the learner gives a name and asks yours.
45. “你今天在家看书吗？” — a truthful answer is appropriate.
46. “你要水吗？” — the learner answers and asks you back.
47. “你今天吃早饭吗？” — a truthful answer is appropriate.
<!-- /mock-part:a1-speaking-personal-exchange -->

<!-- mock-part:a1-speaking-rehearsed-role-play -->
48. Tell the learner in English that you are their reading partner at the
    station. Say “早上好！” and answer their school-today question with
    “我今天不去大学。” If asked about the exit, say “出口在前边。”
    The learner must greet, ask about school, respond, and ask about the exit
    in a 20–45-second exchange. A memorized monologue is insufficient.
<!-- /mock-part:a1-speaking-rehearsed-role-play -->

Score each skill out of 100. Pass requires 60/100 in **each** skill, not a
compensating average. Keep scripts and calibration answers secure.
