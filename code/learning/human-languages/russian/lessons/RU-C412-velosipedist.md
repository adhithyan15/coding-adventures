## You'll want to know: велосипедист
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C412-PESHEKHOD-02]; assesses=[] -->

**велосипедист** (*velosipedíst*) — "a cyclist".

> В парке много велосипедистов.
>
> *V párke mnógo velosipedístov.*
>
> There are a lot of cyclists in the park.

Feminine *велосипедистка*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*велосипедист* is built on *велосипед* 'a bicycle' with the ending *-ист*. *Велосипед* was borrowed from French *vélocipède*, from Latin *vēlōx* 'swift' and *pēs* 'foot' (EtymDB).
