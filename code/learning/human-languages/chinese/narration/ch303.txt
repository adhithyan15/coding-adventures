Mandarin Chinese, chapter 303: A Name and a School.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
起名 (qǐmíng) — to name a baby; to give a name.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for to bring back to life, then the Mandarin for a pair of twins.

You'll want to know: 起名.
起名 — qǐmíng — "to name a baby; to give a name".
gěi háizi qǐmíng bù róngyì — Choosing a name for a child isn't easy.

The word, taken apart.
起名 (qǐmíng) is built from 起 'to raise' and 名 'a name', both inherited from Middle Chinese, khiX and mjieng (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to rise' and 'to start'.

Lesson 3 of 5.
yòu'éryuán — kindergarten; nursery school.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for to name a baby, then the Mandarin for a childhood pet name.

You'll want to know: yòu'éryuán.
yòu'éryuán — "kindergarten; nursery school".
háizi sān suì de shíhou kāishǐ qù yòu'éryuán — The child started kindergarten at three.
Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
yòu'éryuán is built from yòu 'young', 儿 'a child' and yuán 'a garden', all inherited from Middle Chinese, 'jiwH, nye and hjwon (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'young' and the third 'a garden': a garden of young children.

开学 — kāixué — "to start the school term".

Lesson 5 of 5.
新生 (xīnshēng) — new student.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for a kindergarten, then the Mandarin for to start the school term.

You'll want to know: 新生.
新生 — xīnshēng — "new student".
新生下星期开学 xīnshēng xià xīngqī kāixué — The new students start next week.

The word, taken apart.
新生 (xīnshēng) is built from 新 'new' and 生, both inherited from Middle Chinese, sin and srjaeng (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'to grow'; its sense 'a student' is not given there.
