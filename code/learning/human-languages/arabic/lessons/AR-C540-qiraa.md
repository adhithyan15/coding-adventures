## You'll want to know: قراءة
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C540-NADI540-01]; assesses=[] -->

**قراءة** — *qirāʾa* — "reading".

> القراءة هوايتي المفضلة.
>
> *al-qirāʾa hiwāyatī al-mufaḍḍala.*
>
> Reading is my favourite hobby.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**قراءة** (*qirāʾa*) is built on the native Arabic root *q-r-a*, "to read, to recite" (Lane). Qirāʾa is the verbal noun of qaraʾa, to read, which you know.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
