Arabic, chapter 85: Light, Heavy, Correct, Mistake, Important.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
خفيف (khafīf) — light (in weight).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for clean, then the Arabic for dirty.
