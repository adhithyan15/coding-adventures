## You'll want to know: ਖੜ੍ਹਨਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C270-SEARCH270-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ਖੜ੍ਹਨਾ** — *khaṛhnā* — "to stand; to stand waiting".

> ਲੋਕ ਕਤਾਰ ਵਿੱਚ ਖੜ੍ਹਦੇ ਹਨ।
>
> *lok katār vich khaṛhde han.*
>
> People stand in the queue.

Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਕਤਾਰ ਵਿੱਚ ਖੜ੍ਹਿਆ, *maiṁ katār vich khaṛhiā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਖੜ੍ਹਨਾ** (*khaṛhnā*) goes back to Sanskrit *khaḍaka*, "erect; a post", beside a reconstructed *\*khaḍati*, "stands"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *khaṛā*, "standing erect", under it (CDIAL 3784).
