## Chapters 49-98: 250 headwords, thirty-six verbs, and Punjabi attains pre-A1

Punjabi had two pre-A1 gaps: 247 headwords and 4 verbs.

**The vocabulary.** Fifty chapters of five words each, and four reviews: two
after chapter 73 and two after chapter 98. Every word is chained into the next
two lessons.

- **49-73**:
  - the body and health
  - the family on both sides
  - thirty-six verbs
  - food, the home and school
- **74-98**:
  - nature and animals
  - describing words and colours
  - time and place
  - the town
  - clothes
  - six to ten, and the tens to a thousand

Every headword is spelled only with letters the reader has already written.
That is why the words with ਢ, ਔ or ਊ wait: **ਢਿੱਡ**, **ਮੋਢਾ**, **ਔਰਤ**,
**ਠੰਢਾ**, **ਔਖਾ** and **ਊਠ**. Some words were held back for other reasons:

- **ਤਾਰਾ** and **ਘੋੜਾ** are already used in earlier lesson text.
- **ਰਾਤ** is already taught inside **ਸ਼ੁਭ ਰਾਤ**.
- **ਮਿਲਣਾ** is already the concept behind **ਮਿਲਾਂਗੇ**.

The session map gains rows 279-532, appended after chapter 48, so every existing
session keeps its number.
