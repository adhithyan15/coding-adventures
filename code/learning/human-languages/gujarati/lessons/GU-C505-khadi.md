## You'll want to know: ખાદી
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C505-RENTIYO505-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ખાદી** — *khādī* — "khadi (handspun cloth)".

> મીરાએ ખાદીનો કુરતો ખરીદ્યો.
>
> *Mīrāe khādīno kurto kharīdyo.*
>
> Mira bought a khadi kurta.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ખાદી** (*khādī*) comes from *\*khadda*, "coarse cloth", a form CDIAL reconstructs, and CDIAL lists the Gujarati word as thick coarse cotton cloth (CDIAL 3808).
