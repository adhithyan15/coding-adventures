## You'll want to know: disfarçar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C321-CARNAVAL-05]; assesses=[] -->

**disfarçar** — "to disguise; to hide (a feeling)".

> Ele disfarçou o medo com um sorriso.
>
> *He hid his fear behind a smile.*

*Disfarçar-se de* — to dress up as: *disfarçou-se de pirata*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*disfarçar* is usually linked to *des-* and an old word for a face or a farce, but the origin is not settled; EtymDB has no entry.
