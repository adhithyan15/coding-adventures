## You'll want to know: كنز
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C312-KASHF312-05]; assesses=[] -->

**كنز** — *kanz* — "a treasure".

> وجد الصياد كنزا تحت الرمل.
>
> *wajada aṣ-ṣayyād kanzan taḥta ar-raml.*
>
> The fisherman found a treasure under the sand.

Plural: كنوز, *kunūz*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**كنز** (*kanz*) is built on the root *k-n-z*, "to store up, to bury wealth" (Lane). A link with Persian *ganj*, treasure, is often proposed, but the origin is not settled.
