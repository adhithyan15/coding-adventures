## You'll want to know: валюта
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C456-OBMENNIK-02]; assesses=[] -->

**валюта** (*valyúta*) — "a foreign currency, a currency".

> Мы поменяли валюту в банке.
>
> *My pomenyáli valyútu v bánke.*
>
> We changed currency at the bank.

Feminine. In Russia *валюта* usually means foreign money.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*валюта* was borrowed from Italian *valuta* 'currency' (EtymDB), which goes back to Latin *valēre* 'to be worth'.
