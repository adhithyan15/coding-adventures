## You'll want to know: gǎnjī
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C457-HELP457-04]; assesses=[] -->

*gǎnjī* — "to feel deeply grateful".

> *nǐ bāng le wǒ hěn duō wǒ hěn gǎnjī* — You've helped me so much; I'm very grateful.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*gǎnjī* is built from *gǎn* 'to be moved' and *jī* 'to surge', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *komX* and *kek* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to move' and the second as a rushing wave.
