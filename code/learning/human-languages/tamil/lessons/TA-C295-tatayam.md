## You'll want to know: தடயம்
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C295-THUPPU295-02]; assesses=[] -->

**தடயம்** — *taṭayam* — "a trace; a mark left behind".

> திருடன் எந்தத் தடயமும் விட்டுச் செல்லவில்லை.
>
> *tiruṭaṉ entat taṭayamum viṭṭuc cellavillai.*
>
> The thief left no trace behind.

With -உம் and a negative verb it means not a single one: எந்தத் தடயமும் இல்லை.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**தடயம்** (*taṭayam*), "a trace, a mark left behind", is a Tamil word whose origin is not settled in the sources at hand.
