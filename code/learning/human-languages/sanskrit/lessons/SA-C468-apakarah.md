## You'll want to know: अपकारः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C468-KRTAVEDIN468-03]; assesses=[] -->

**अपकारः** — *apakāraḥ* — "a wrong; an injury".

> सज्जनः अपकारं क्षमते।
>
> *sajjanaḥ apakāraṃ kṣamate.*
>
> A good person forgives a wrong.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अपकारः** (*apakāraḥ*) is built from *apa*, "away, off", and *kāra*, "doing", from the root √kṛ of **करोति**, the opposite of **उपकारः**; MW gives "wrong, offence, injury, hurt" (MW).
