## You'll want to know: عاقب
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C348-TUHMA348-04]; assesses=[] -->

**عاقب** — *ʿāqaba* — "to punish".

> عاقب المعلم الطالب على كذبه.
>
> *ʿāqaba al-muʿallim aṭ-ṭālib ʿalā kadhibihi.*
>
> The teacher punished the pupil for lying.

Present: يعاقب, *yuʿāqib*.

It takes على for the deed.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**عاقب** (*ʿāqaba*) is built on the native Arabic root *ʿ-q-b* (Lane), "to follow after". It is Form III, "to requite someone for what he did".
