# در صورت (dar surat-e) — in case of, in the event of

## You'll want to know: در صورت
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C390-SHART-03]; assesses=[] -->

**در صورت** (*dar surat-e*) — "in case of, in the event of".

> در صورت نیاز، دکتر به خانه میآید.
>
> *dar surat-e niyâz, doktor be khâne miâyad.*
>
> If needed, the doctor comes to the house.

It takes a noun with ezafe: *dar surat-e niyâz* "if needed", *dar surat-e bârân* "if it rains".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**در صورت** joins the Persian preposition *dar* "in" to *surat*, from Arabic *ṣūra* "form, image" (EtymDB): in the shape of, in the case of.
