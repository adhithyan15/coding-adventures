## You'll want to know: उत्खनन
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C335-UTKHANAN335-01]; assesses=[] -->

**उत्खनन** — *utkhanan* — "an excavation".

> उत्खननात जुनी भांडी सापडली.
>
> *utkhananāt junī bhāṇḍī sāpaḍlī.*
>
> Old pots were found in the excavation.

Neuter.

उत्खनन करणे, to excavate.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**उत्खनन** (*utkhanan*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *utkhanana*, 'a digging up' (Monier-Williams).
