## You'll want to know: विनीतः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C458-DARPA458-05]; assesses=[] -->

**विनीतः** — *vinītaḥ* — "modest".

> विद्वान् अपि सः विनीतः अस्ति।
>
> *vidvān api saḥ vinītaḥ asti.*
>
> Though learned, he is modest.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**विनीतः** (*vinītaḥ*) is built from *vi* and the root √nī, "to lead", the root of **नयति**: led and trained; MW gives "trained, well-behaved, humble, modest" (MW).

## What you've built
