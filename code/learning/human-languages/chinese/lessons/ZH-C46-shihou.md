## You'll want to know: shíhou
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C46-WEEK46-03]; assesses=[] -->

*shíhou* — "time; moment".

> *nǐ shénme shíhou qù* — When are you going?

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*shíhou* is built from shí 'time' and hou 'to wait for', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *dzyi* and *huwH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for times and partings.
