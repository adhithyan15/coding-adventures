## You'll want to know: प्रारंभ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C307-ANT307-01]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रारंभ** — *prārambh* — "a commencement".

> कार्यक्रमाचा प्रारंभ गाण्याने झाला.
>
> *kāryakramācā prārambh gāṇyāne jhālā.*
>
> The programme began with a song.

Masculine.

The formal word for सुरुवात.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रारंभ** (*prārambh*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *prārambha*, 'a commencement, a beginning' (Monier-Williams).
