## You'll want to know: すぐ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C263-KYUUKYUU263-05]; assesses=[] -->

**すぐ** — *sugu* — "at once; right away".

> **けいさつが すぐ きました。** — *keisatsu ga sugu kimashita* — "The police came straight away."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**すぐ** (*sugu*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict).
