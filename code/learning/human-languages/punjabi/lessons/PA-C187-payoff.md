# A gentle past-copula payoff

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-FORM-SI-187, PA-FORM-SAN-187] -->

[PAUSE 3s] Without looking, say the singular past form and then the plural or
polite past form.

## Guided Practice: four skills, bounded to the two forms
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-FORM-SI-187, PA-FORM-SAN-187, PA-FORM-NAME-AMAN-01, PA-LEX-C67-FOOD67-03, PA-POSTPOSITION-VICH-01, PA-LEX-C73-SCHOOL73-04, PA-LEX-C84-ADJ84-02, PA-PRON-OH-01, PA-LEX-KAMM-05, PA-FORM-KARDE-183] -->

- [YOU HEAR: *Aman ghar vich sii; phull laal san; oh kamm karde san*; YOU SAY: **ਅਮਨ ਘਰ ਵਿੱਚ ਸੀ; ਫੁੱਲ ਲਾਲ ਸਨ; ਉਹ ਕੰਮ ਕਰਦੇ ਸਨ**]
- [YOU READ: **ਅਮਨ ਘਰ ਵਿੱਚ ਸੀ। ਫੁੱਲ ਲਾਲ ਸਨ। ਉਹ ਕੰਮ ਕਰਦੇ ਸਨ।**]
- [YOU WRITE: cover the model and write **ਸੀ, ਸਨ**]

Underline the singular form once and the plural form twice.
