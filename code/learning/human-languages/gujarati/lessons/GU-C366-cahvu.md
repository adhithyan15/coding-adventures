## You'll want to know: ચાહવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C366-SHARAT366-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ચાહવું** — *cāhvũ* — "to want".

> જો તમે ચાહો છો તો આપણે આજે નીકળીશું.
>
> *jo tame cāho cho to āpaṇe āje nīkḷīśũ.*
>
> If you want, we will set off today.

Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે મદદ ચાહી, *teṇe madad cāhī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ચાહવું** (*cāhvũ*) CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb, "to long for, to love", under a reconstructed *cāh*, "see, look for, desire" (CDIAL 4775); "to want" is its everyday sense.
