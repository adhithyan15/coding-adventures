## You'll want to know: ستم
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C456-KIND456-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ستم** — *sitam* — "cruelty; injustice".

> اس پر بڑا ستم ہوا۔
>
> *us par baṛā sitam huā.*
>
> A great injustice was done to him.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ستم** (*sitam*) is usually traced to Persian *sitam*, "oppression".
