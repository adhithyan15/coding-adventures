## You'll want to know: a queixa
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C387-TRIBUNAL-01]; assesses=[] -->

**a queixa** — "the complaint (to the police); the grievance".

> Ela apresentou queixa na polícia.
>
> *She reported it to the police.*

*Apresentar queixa* — to report something to the police, to file a complaint.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*queixa* is built on *queixar* "to complain", usually traced to Vulgar Latin *quassiāre*, from Latin *quassāre* "to shake", on *quatere* (Lewis & Short); Spanish *queja* is a cousin (EtymDB).
