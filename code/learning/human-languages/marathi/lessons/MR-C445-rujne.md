## You'll want to know: रुजणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C445-ANKUR445-02]; assesses=[] -->

**रुजणे** — *rujṇe* — "to take root".

> पावसानंतर रोपं लवकर रुजली.
>
> *pāvsānantar ropaṁ lavkar rujlī.*
>
> After the rain the saplings took root quickly.

Past (he …): तो रुजला, *to rujlā*.

Also for ideas and habits: सवय रुजली.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**रुजणे** (*rujṇe*) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi *ruj̈ṇẽ*, "to sprout, grow", under *\*ruhyati*, "grows" (CDIAL 10797).
