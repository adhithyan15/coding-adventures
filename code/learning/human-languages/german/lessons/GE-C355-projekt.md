## You'll want to know: das Projekt
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C355-PROJEKT-01]; assesses=[] -->

**das Projekt** — "the project".

> Unser Projekt muss bis Ende Mai fertig sein.
>
> *Our project has to be finished by the end of May.*

Plural: *die Projekte*. The stress is on the second syllable: *pro-JEKT*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Projekt* is a learned borrowing from Latin *prōiectum* (EtymDB), from *prōicere* "to throw forth" (Lewis & Short), built on *iacere* "to throw": a plan thrown forward.
