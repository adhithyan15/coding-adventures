## You'll want to know: طعنہ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C491-MENDING491-03]; assesses=[] -->

**طعنہ** — *tānā* — "a taunt".

> بھائی نے دیر سے آنے پر طعنہ دیا۔
>
> *bhāī ne der se āne par tānā diyā.*
>
> My brother made a dig about my coming late.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**طعنہ** (*tānā*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *ṭaʿna*, "a thrust", and so a cutting remark.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
