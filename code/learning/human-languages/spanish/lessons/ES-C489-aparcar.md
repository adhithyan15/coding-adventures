## You'll want to know: aparcar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C489-CIUDAD-01]; assesses=[] -->

**aparcar** — "to park".

> No se puede aparcar aquí.
>
> *You cannot park here.*

The verb behind *el aparcamiento*, the car park. In Latin America people say *estacionar*.
