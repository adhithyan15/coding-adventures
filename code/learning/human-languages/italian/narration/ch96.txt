Italian, chapter 96: Blanket, Towel, Soap, Toothbrush, Toothpaste.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
la coperta — a blanket.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for a curtain; a tent, then the Italian for a cushion, a pillow.

You'll want to know: la coperta.
la coperta — "a blanket".

The word, taken apart.
coperta — built from the verb "to cover", which from Latin cooperire, "to cover over". (EtymDB 2.1).
