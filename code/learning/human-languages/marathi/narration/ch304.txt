Marathi, chapter 304: Telling It Well.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
वर्णन करणे (varṇan karṇe) — to describe.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for an anecdote, then the Marathi for to narrate.

You'll want to know: वर्णन करणे.
वर्णन करणे — varṇan karṇe — "to describe".
तिने सहलीचं सुंदर वर्णन केलं. tine sahlīcaṁ sundar varṇan kelaṁ. She described the trip beautifully.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने वर्णन केलं, tyāne varṇan kelaṁ.
What is described takes -चं: गावाचं वर्णन करणे (varṇan karṇe).

The word, taken apart.
वर्णन करणे (varṇan karṇe) joins varṇan, 'a description', a tatsama taken from Sanskrit varṇana, 'depicting, describing' (Monier-Williams), to karṇe, 'to do'.

Lesson 3 of 5.
उलगडणे (ulgaḍṇe) — to unfold; to come clear.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to describe, then the Marathi for an occasion.

You'll want to know: उलगडणे.
उलगडणे — ulgaḍṇe — "to unfold; to come clear".
हळूहळू त्या घटनेचा अर्थ उलगडला. haḷūhaḷū tyā ghaṭnecā artha ulgaḍlā. Little by little the meaning of that event came clear.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने उलगडलं, tyāne ulgaḍlaṁ.
Intransitive (अर्थ उलगडतो, the meaning unfolds) and transitive (कागद उलगडणे (ulgaḍṇe), to unfold a paper).

The word, taken apart.
उलगडणे (ulgaḍṇe) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi algaḍṇẽ, 'to get unravelled; to unravel', most likely a variant of this verb, under ullagyati, 'is unfastened' (CDIAL 2363).

Lesson 4 of 5.
गाजणे (gājṇe) — to make a stir.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for an occasion, then the Marathi for to unfold.

You'll want to know: गाजणे.
गाजणे — gājṇe — "to make a stir".
तो चित्रपट त्या वर्षी खूप गाजला. to citrapaṭ tyā varṣī khūp gājlā. That film made a big stir that year.
Past (he …): तो गाजला, to gājlā.
Intransitive; गाजलेला, much talked about.

The word, taken apart.
गाजणे (gājṇe) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Marathi gājṇe, 'thunders', under Sanskrit garjati, 'thunders, roars' (CDIAL 4046). What thunders is heard everywhere.

Lesson 5 of 5.
पसरणे (pasarṇe) — to spread.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to unfold, then the Marathi for to make a stir.

You'll want to know: पसरणे.
पसरणे — pasarṇe — "to spread".
ही बातमी संपूर्ण गावात पसरली. hī bātmī sampūrṇa gāvāt pasarlī. This news spread through the whole village.
Past (he …): तो पसरला, to pasarlā.
Intransitive; its transitive partner is पसरवणे, to spread something.

The word, taken apart.
पसरणे (pasarṇe) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Marathi pasarṇe, 'to spread out', under Sanskrit prasarati, 'advances, extends' (CDIAL 8825).
