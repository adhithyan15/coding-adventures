# മുറിഞ്ഞുപോകുക (muṟiññupōkuka) — to be disconnected; to break off

## Warm-up

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Malayalam for to crack open, then the Malayalam for to get scorched.

## You'll want to know: മുറിഞ്ഞുപോകുക
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C514-VARALCA514-04]; assesses=[] -->

**മുറിഞ്ഞുപോകുക** — *muṟiññupōkuka* — "to be disconnected; to break off".

> സംസാരിക്കുന്നതിനിടെ ഫോൺ മുറിഞ്ഞുപോയി.
>
> *saṁsārikkunnatiniṭe phōṇ muṟiññupōyi.*
>
> In the middle of talking, the phone call was cut off.

Past: മുറിഞ്ഞുപോയി, *muṟiññupōyi*.

മുറിഞ്ഞ്, being cut, plus പോകുക: for calls, power and roads.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**മുറിഞ്ഞുപോകുക** (*muṟiññupōkuka*) is a native Dravidian compound of മുറിയുക, to be cut or broken (DEDR 5008), and പോകുക, to go.
