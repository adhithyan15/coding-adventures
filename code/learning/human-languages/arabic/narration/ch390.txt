Arabic, chapter 390: University life.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
كلية (kulliyya) — a faculty.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to force, then the Arabic for stability.

You'll want to know: كلية.
كلية — kulliyya — "a faculty".
درس أبي في كلية (kulliyya) الطب. darasa abī fī kulliyyat aṭ-ṭibb. My father studied at the faculty of medicine.

The word, taken apart.
كلية (kulliyya) is built on the native Arabic root k-l-l, "to be all, the whole" (Lane). Kulliyya is a modern abstract noun from kull, all; its use for a college of a university is usually explained as a whole branch of learning.

Warm-up.

You'll want to know: عميد.
عميد — ʿamīd — "a dean".
تكلم العميد مع الطلاب الجدد. takallama al-ʿamīd maʿa aṭ-ṭullāb al-judud. The dean spoke with the new students.

The word, taken apart.
عميد (ʿamīd) is built on the native Arabic root ʿ-m-d, "to prop up, to support" (Lane). An ʿamīd is a support, the one others lean on: a chief, and now a dean.

Lesson 5 of 5.
التحق (iltaḥaqa) — to enrol.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a dean, then the Arabic for a scholarship.

You'll want to know: التحق.
التحق — iltaḥaqa — "to enrol".
التحق ابني بالجامعة هذه السنة. iltaḥaqa ibnī bi-l-jāmiʿa hādhihi as-sana. My son enrolled at the university this year.
Present: يلتحق, yaltaḥiq.
It takes بـ: التحق (iltaḥaqa) بـ ….

The word, taken apart.
التحق (iltaḥaqa) is built on the native Arabic root l-ḥ-q, "to overtake, to catch up" (Lane). It is Form VIII, to attach oneself to: to join, to enrol.
