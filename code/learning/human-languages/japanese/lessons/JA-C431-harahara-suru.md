# はらはらする (harahara suru) — to be on edge; to be in suspense

## You'll want to know: はらはらする
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C431-SHOCK431-05]; assesses=[] -->

**はらはらする** — *harahara suru* — "to be on edge; to be in suspense".

> **おとうとの しあいで はらはらしました。** — *otōto no shiai de harahara shimashita* — "I was on edge at my younger brother's match."

Polite **はらはらします**, *haraharashimasu*; a する verb. Past (polite): **ははが はらはらしました。**, *haha ga harahara shimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**はらはらする** (*harahara suru*) is a native Japanese mimetic word, *harahara*, inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, made a verb with *suru*, "to do"; its older origin is not settled (JMdict).
