## You'll want to know: منسوخ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C390-RAVANA390-04]; assesses=[] -->

**منسوخ** — *mansūkh* — "cancelled".

> آج رات کی گاڑی منسوخ ہو گئی۔
>
> *āj rāt kī gāṛī mansūkh ho gaī.*
>
> Tonight's train has been cancelled.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**منسوخ** (*mansūkh*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *mansūkh*, "annulled, done away with".
