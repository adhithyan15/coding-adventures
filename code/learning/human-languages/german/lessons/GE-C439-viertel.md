## You'll want to know: das Viertel
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C439-VIERTEL-02]; assesses=[] -->

**das Viertel** — "the neighbourhood, the quarter (of a town); a quarter".

> Sie wohnt in einem ruhigen Viertel am Rand der Stadt.
>
> *She lives in a quiet neighbourhood on the edge of town.*

Same form in the plural. Also a quarter: *ein Viertel nach drei*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Viertel* is built on *vier* 'four' and first meant a fourth part, then a part of a town. Both are inherited Germanic words.
