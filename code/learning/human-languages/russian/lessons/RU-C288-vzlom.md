## You'll want to know: взлом
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C288-KRAZHA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**взлом** (*vzlom*) — "a break-in, forced entry".

> Соседи не заметили взлома.
>
> *Sosédi ne zamétili vzlóma.*
>
> The neighbours did not notice the break-in.

*Со взломом* — by force.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*взлом* is built on *вз-* and the root of *ломать* "to break", an inherited Slavic verb: a breaking open. EtymDB lists no parent.
