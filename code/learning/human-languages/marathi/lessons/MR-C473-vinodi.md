## You'll want to know: विनोदी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C473-KHODKAR473-03]; assesses=[] -->

**विनोदी** — *vinodī* — "funny; witty".

> आमच्या मावशी खूप विनोदी आहेत.
>
> *āmcyā māvśī khūp vinodī āhet.*
>
> Our aunt is very funny.

From विनोद, a joke. Unchanging.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**विनोदी** (*vinodī*) is a Marathi adjective built on विनोद, "a joke", a tatsama from Sanskrit *vinoda*, "driving away; diversion" (Monier-Williams).
