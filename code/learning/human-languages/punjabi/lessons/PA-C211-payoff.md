# Every day: a four-skill payoff

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-GRAMMAR-VOWEL-STEM-211, PA-GRAMMAR-KHANDA-PINDA-211] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say *goes*, *eats* and *drinks*, for a man and then for a woman.

## Guided Practice: four skills
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-GRAMMAR-VOWEL-STEM-211, PA-GRAMMAR-KHANDA-PINDA-211, PA-GRAMMAR-VOWEL-STEM-PLURAL-211, PA-LEX-C100-TIME100-04, PA-LEX-CHA, PA-LEX-C71-HOME71-05] -->

- [YOU HEAR: *I drink tea every day*] Say it as yourself: **ਮੈਂ ਰੋਜ਼ ਚਾਹ ਪੀਂਦਾ ਹਾਂ।** or **ਮੈਂ ਰੋਜ਼ ਚਾਹ ਪੀਂਦੀ ਹਾਂ।**
- [YOU HEAR: *we go to school every day*] Say **ਅਸੀਂ ਰੋਜ਼ ਸਕੂਲ ਜਾਂਦੇ ਹਾਂ।**
- [YOU READ: **ਉਹ ਰੋਜ਼ ਰੋਟੀ ਖਾਂਦੀ ਹੈ।**] Give its meaning: she eats roti every day.
- [YOU WRITE: cover the line and write **ਮੈਂ ਰੋਜ਼ ਪਾਣੀ ਪੀਂਦਾ ਹਾਂ।** or its woman's form]

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-GRAMMAR-VOWEL-STEM-211, PA-GRAMMAR-KHANDA-PINDA-211, PA-GRAMMAR-VOWEL-STEM-PLURAL-211] -->

[PAUSE 3s] Tell someone three things you do every day: where you go, what you
eat, what you drink.
