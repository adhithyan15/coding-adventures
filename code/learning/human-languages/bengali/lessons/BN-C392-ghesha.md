## You'll want to know: ঘেঁষা
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C392-TRAFFIC392-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ঘেঁষা** — *ghẽshā* — "to keep close to; to brush against".

> সাইকেলটা ফুটপাত ঘেঁষে চলল।
>
> *sāikelṭā phuṭpāt ghẽshe chôllô.*
>
> The bicycle kept close to the pavement.

Past (he/she …): সেই গাড়ি দেয়াল ঘেঁষল, *sei gāṛi deyāl ghẽshlô*.

Most often in the -এ form before a verb of motion: দেয়াল ঘেঁষে দাঁড়ানো, to stand close against the wall.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ঘেঁষা** (*ghẽshā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists Bengali *ghẽs*, "a touch, a rub", under Sanskrit *gharṣa*, "friction" (CDIAL 4448); to brush past is to touch in passing.
