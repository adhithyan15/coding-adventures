## You'll want to know: el exilio
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C552-EXILIO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**el exilio** — "the exile".

> Vivió veinte años en el exilio.
>
> *He lived in exile for twenty years.*

*Exiliarse* is to go into exile.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*exilio* is a learned borrowing from Latin *exilium* 'banishment' (EtymDB).
