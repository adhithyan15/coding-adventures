## You'll want to know: ਜੋੜਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C274-SIZE274-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ਜੋੜਾ** — *joṛā* — "a pair".

> ਮੈਂ ਜੁੱਤੀਆਂ ਦਾ ਇੱਕ ਨਵਾਂ ਜੋੜਾ ਖ਼ਰੀਦਾਂਗਾ।
>
> *maiṁ juttīāṁ dā ikk navāṁ joṛā kharīdāṅgā.*
>
> I will buy a new pair of shoes.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਜੋੜਾ** (*joṛā*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*yuṭati*, "is joined"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *joṛā*, "couple", under it (CDIAL 10496).
