## You'll want to know: वीर
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C470-SAHAS470-02]; assesses=[] -->

**वीर** — *vīr* — "valiant; a hero".

> दादा एक वीर सिपाही थे।
>
> *dādā ek vīr sipāhī the.*
>
> Grandfather was a valiant soldier.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**वीर** (*vīr*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *vīra*, "a man, esp. a brave one; a hero" (Monier-Williams).
