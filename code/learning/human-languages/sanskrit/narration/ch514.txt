Sanskrit, chapter 514: Serving the Meal.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
परिवेषयति (pariveṣayati) — serves food.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for strains, then the Sanskrit for oily.

You'll want to know: परिवेषयति.
परिवेषयति — pariveṣayati — "serves food".
माता अतिथये भोजनं परिवेषयति। mātā atithaye bhojanaṃ pariveṣayati. Mother serves food to the guest.
Past (she …): सा ओदनं परिवेषितवती, sā odanaṃ pariveṣitavatī.

The word, taken apart.
परिवेषयति (pariveṣayati) is built on pari, "around", and the root √viṣ, "to be active, to work"; MW gives pariviṣ as to serve, to wait on, to offer or dress food (MW).

सा शिशुं भोजयति। sā śiśuṃ bhojayati. She feeds the baby.
Past (she …): सा बालं भोजितवती, sā bālaṃ bhojitavatī.

The word, taken apart.
भोजयति (bhojayati) comes from the causative of the root √bhuj, "to enjoy, to eat": to make someone eat, to feed (MW).

Lesson 3 of 5.
अश्नाति (aśnāti) — dines.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for serves food, then the Sanskrit for feeds.

You'll want to know: अश्नाति.
अश्नाति — aśnāti — "dines".
पिता सायं भोजनम् अश्नाति। pitā sāyaṃ bhojanam aśnāti. Father has his meal in the evening.
Past (he …): सः भोजनम् अशितवान्, saḥ bhojanam aśitavān.

The word, taken apart.
अश्नाति (aśnāti) comes from the root √aś, "to eat, to consume", whose present is aśnāti; its past participle is aśita (MW).

Lesson 4 of 5.
उच्छिष्टम् (ucchiṣṭam) — leftovers.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for feeds, then the Sanskrit for dines.

You'll want to know: उच्छिष्टम्.
उच्छिष्टम् — ucchiṣṭam — "leftovers".
उच्छिष्टं धेनवे ददातु। ucchiṣṭaṃ dhenave dadātu. Please give the leftovers to the cow.
A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
उच्छिष्टम् (ucchiṣṭam) is built on ud, "out, over", and the root √śiṣ, "to leave", as its past participle; MW gives ucchiṣṭa as left over, and the remnants of food (MW).

Lesson 5 of 5.
पिधानम् (pidhānam) — a lid.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for dines, then the Sanskrit for leftovers.

You'll want to know: पिधानम्.
पिधानम् — pidhānam — "a lid".
पात्रस्य पिधानं कुत्र अस्ति? pātrasya pidhānaṃ kutra asti? Where is the lid of the pot?
A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
पिधानम् (pidhānam) is built on pi, a short form of api, "on", and the root √dhā, "to put"; MW gives pidhāna as a covering, and a cover or lid (MW).
