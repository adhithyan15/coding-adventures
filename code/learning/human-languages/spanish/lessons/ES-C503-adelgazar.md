## You'll want to know: adelgazar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C503-FORMA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**adelgazar** — "to lose weight".

> Quiere adelgazar antes del verano.
>
> *He wants to lose weight before the summer.*

The opposite is *engordar*. It takes *c* before *e*: *adelgacé*.
