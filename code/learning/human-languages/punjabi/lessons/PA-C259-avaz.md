## You'll want to know: ਆਵਾਜ਼
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C259-PHONE259-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ਆਵਾਜ਼** — *āvāz* — "a voice; a sound".

> ਫ਼ੋਨ ਉੱਤੇ ਅਮਨ ਦੀ ਆਵਾਜ਼ ਸਾਫ਼ ਹੈ।
>
> *fon utte Aman dī āvāz sāf hai.*
>
> Aman's voice is clear on the phone.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਆਵਾਜ਼** (*āvāz*) is a loan, usually traced to Persian *āvāz*, "a voice".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for reading notices and messages.
