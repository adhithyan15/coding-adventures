## You'll want to know: tǐyù
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C498-PITCH498-01]; assesses=[] -->

*tǐyù* — "physical education (a school subject)".

> *wǒ zuì xǐhuan de kè shì tǐyù* — My favourite lesson is PE.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*tǐyù* is built from 体 'the body' and *yù* 'to rear', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *thejX* and *yuwk* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'the body' and the second 'to nourish' and 'to grow'.
