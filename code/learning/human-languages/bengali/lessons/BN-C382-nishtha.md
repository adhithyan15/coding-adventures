# নিষ্ঠা (nishṭhā) — dedication; devotion (to work)

## You'll want to know: নিষ্ঠা
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C382-NISHTHA382-01]; assesses=[] -->

**নিষ্ঠা** — *nishṭhā* — "dedication; devotion (to work)".

> নিষ্ঠা থাকলে তুমি কঠিন কাজও শিখতে পারবে।
>
> *nishṭhā thākle tumi kôṭhin kājo shikhte pārbe.*
>
> With dedication, you can learn even a hard skill.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**নিষ্ঠা** (*nishṭhā*) is Sanskrit *niṣṭhā*, firmness and steady devotion (Monier-Williams): staying with a task out of care for it.
