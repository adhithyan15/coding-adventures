## You'll want to know: विषम्
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C480-DAMSA480-02]; assesses=[] -->

**विषम्** — *viṣam* — "poison".

> वृश्चिकस्य विषं वेदनां ददाति।
>
> *vṛścikasya viṣaṃ vedanāṃ dadāti.*
>
> A scorpion's poison causes agony.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**विषम्** (*viṣam*) MW glosses it "anything active", then "poison, venom, bane", and gives no root for it, so its origin is uncertain (MW).
