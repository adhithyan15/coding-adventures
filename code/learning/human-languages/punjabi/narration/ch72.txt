You'll want to know: ਬਸਤਾ.
ਬਸਤਾ — bastā — "a school bag".

The word, taken apart.
ਬਸਤਾ (bastā) is a loan, usually traced to Persian basta, "tied up; a bundle".

Lesson 5 of 5.
ਸ਼ਬਦ (shabad) — a word.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for a question, then the Punjabi for an answer.

You'll want to know: ਸ਼ਬਦ.
ਸ਼ਬਦ — shabad — "a word".

The word, taken apart.
ਸ਼ਬਦ (shabad) is a semi-tatsama: Sanskrit śabda, "sound, word", borrowed after the old sound changes had run their course and then lightly reshaped (CDIAL 12298). Punjabi puts a vowel between the b and the d. The inherited Punjabi form is sadd, "call, shout".
