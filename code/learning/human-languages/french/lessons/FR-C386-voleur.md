## You'll want to know: le voleur
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C386-ACCUSER-03]; assesses=[] -->

**le voleur** — "the thief".

> Le voleur est entré par la fenêtre.
>
> *The thief came in through the window.*

Feminine *voleuse*. *Au voleur !* — stop thief!

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*voleur* is built from *voler* "to steal" and *-eur*. *voler* "to steal" is the same word as *voler* "to fly", from Latin *volāre* "to fly" (Lewis & Short): the falcon that flies off with its prey.
