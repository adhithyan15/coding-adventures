Punjabi, chapter 83: Thin, Light, Heavy, Important, Green.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ਪਤਲਾ (patlā) — thin.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for sweet, then the Punjabi for fat.

You'll want to know: ਪਤਲਾ.
ਪਤਲਾ — patlā — "thin".

The word, taken apart.
ਪਤਲਾ (patlā) came into Punjabi from Hindi. CDIAL lists Hindi patlā, "thin, weak", under Sanskrit pattrala, "leafy", and so "leaf-like, thin", and marks the Punjabi word as taken from it (CDIAL 7736).

Lesson 2 of 5.
ਹਲਕਾ (halkā) — light (in weight).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for fat, then the Punjabi for thin.

You'll want to know: ਹਲਕਾ.
ਹਲਕਾ — halkā — "light (in weight)".

The word, taken apart.
ਹਲਕਾ (halkā) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from laghu, "light" (CDIAL 10896). Its sounds have been swapped, and a -k- added.

ਜ਼ਰੂਰੀ (zarūrī) — important, necessary.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for light, then the Punjabi for heavy.

You'll want to know: ਜ਼ਰੂਰੀ.
ਜ਼ਰੂਰੀ — zarūrī — "important, necessary".

The word, taken apart.
ਜ਼ਰੂਰੀ (zarūrī) is a loan from Arabic ḍarūrī, "necessary", which reached Punjabi through Persian. Its Arabic root is ḍ-r-r (Lane).
