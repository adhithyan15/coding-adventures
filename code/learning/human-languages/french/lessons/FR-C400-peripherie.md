## You'll want to know: la périphérie
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C400-PERIPH-02]; assesses=[] -->

**la périphérie** — "the outskirts, the edge (of a town)".

> Mes cousins habitent à la périphérie de la ville.
>
> *My cousins live on the outskirts of the town.*

*En périphérie* — on the edge of town, away from the centre.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*périphérie* is usually traced to Late Latin *peripheria*, a learned borrowing from Greek *periphéreia* 'circumference', built from *peri* 'around' and *phérein* 'to carry'. EtymDB does not list the word, so the line is hedged.
