## You'll want to know: die Reinigung
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C446-REINIGUNG-01]; assesses=[] -->

**die Reinigung** — "the dry cleaner; the cleaning".

> Den Anzug bringt er in die Reinigung.
>
> *He takes the suit to the dry cleaner.*

Plural: *die Reinigungen*. Also the cleaning of a room.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Reinigung* is built on *reinigen* 'to clean', from *rein* 'clean, pure', inherited from Proto-Germanic *hrainiz* (EtymDB lists *Reinigung*).
