# ಮೋಹ (mōha) — infatuation; enchantment

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[KA-LEX-C529-JIVANA529-02, KA-LEX-C529-JIVANA529-03] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Kannada for the teenage years, then the Kannada for solitude.

## You'll want to know: ಮೋಹ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C529-JIVANA529-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ಮೋಹ** — *mōha* — "infatuation; enchantment".

> ಹದಿಹರೆಯದಲ್ಲಿ ಅವನಿಗೆ ಸಿನಿಮಾದ ಮೇಲೆ ಮೋಹ ಇತ್ತು.
>
> *hadihareyadalli avanige sinimāda mēle mōha ittu.*
>
> In his teens he was infatuated with the cinema.

For what: … ಮೇಲೆ ಮೋಹ.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಮೋಹ** (*mōha*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *moha*, from *muh*, "to be bewildered": "loss of consciousness, bewilderment, infatuation" (Monier-Williams).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
