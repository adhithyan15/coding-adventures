# ఏమీ లేదు (ēmī lēdu) — nothing, it's nothing

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[TE-LEX-C97-MOOD-04, TE-LEX-C97-MOOD-05] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Telugu for worry, then the Telugu for alone.

## You'll want to know: ఏమీ లేదు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C98-REPLY-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ఏమీ లేదు** (*ēmī lēdu*) — "nothing", "it's nothing".

The answer to **ఏమైంది?**, "what happened?", when you would rather not say. It ends on the
**లేదు** you have had since your first chapter.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

An answer that closes a question kindly.
