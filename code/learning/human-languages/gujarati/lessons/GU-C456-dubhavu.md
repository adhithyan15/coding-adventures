# દુભાવું (dubhāvũ) — to be hurt (in one's feelings)

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-LEX-C455-GAMGIN455-04, GU-LEX-C455-GAMGIN455-05] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to make someone cry, then the Gujarati for weeping.

## You'll want to know: દુભાવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C456-DUBHA456-01]; assesses=[] -->

**દુભાવું** — *dubhāvũ* — "to be hurt (in one's feelings)".

> મારા જવાબથી મીરા દુભાઈ ગઈ.
>
> *mārā javābthī Mīrā dubhāī gaī.*
>
> Mira was hurt by my answer.

Past (she …): તે દુભાઈ ગઈ, *te dubhāī gaī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**દુભાવું** (*dubhāvũ*) is inherited from Sanskrit *dabhnōti*, "hurts, deceives"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati verbs of this family, "to tease, vex" (CDIAL 6175).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
