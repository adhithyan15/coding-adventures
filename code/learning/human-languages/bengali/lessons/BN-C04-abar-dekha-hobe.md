# আবার দেখা হবে (ābār dækhā hôbe) — "we'll meet again"

## Warm-up

[PAUSE 2s] The warm, everyday goodbye — the fuller cousin of Chapter 1's *āshi*.

## The phrase, taken apart

**আবার দেখা হবে** = **আবার** (*ābār*, "again") + **দেখা হবে** (*dækhā hôbe*, "[a]
seeing will happen") — "**we'll meet again**" (literally "again a seeing will
happen"). Chapter 1 gave you the tender **আসি** (*āshi*, "I'll come [again]"),
the "promise of return" goodbye; *ābār dækhā hôbe* is its warm, explicit form. It
is the sibling of the whole subcontinent's parting habit (Hindi *phir milenge*,
Tamil *pōy varugiṟēṉ*): never a blunt "goodbye," always a return promised.
