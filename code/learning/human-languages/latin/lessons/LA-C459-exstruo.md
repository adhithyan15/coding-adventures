## You'll want to know: exstruō, exstruere
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C459-PORTICUS-05]; assesses=[] -->

**exstruō, exstruere** — "to build up, to raise, to pile up".

> Cīvēs novam porticum in forō exstruunt.
>
> *The citizens put up a new colonnade in the forum.*

*exstruō, exstruere, exstrūxī, exstrūctum*. Also to pile up: *mēnsās epulīs exstruere*, to load tables with food.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*exstruō* is built from *ex-* 'up, out' and *struō* 'to pile up, to build' (EtymDB; Lewis & Short). English *construct* and *structure* come from *struō*.
