Bengali, chapter 380: The Right Conditions.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
অনুকূল (ônukūl) — favourable.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for to break a promise, then the Bengali for to vow.

You'll want to know: অনুকূল.
অনুকূল — ônukūl — "favourable".
আবহাওয়া অনুকূল হলে আমরা কাল নৌকায় যাব। ābôhāoyā ônukūl hôle āmrā kāl noukāy jābô. If the weather is favourable, we'll go by boat tomorrow.

The word, taken apart.
অনুকূল (ônukūl) is Sanskrit anukūla, 'along the bank', and so going with you, favourable (Monier-Williams).

One more word for saying what happens if.

Lesson 4 of 5.
যোগ্য (jogyô) — qualified; deserving.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for adverse, then the Bengali for suitable.

You'll want to know: যোগ্য.
যোগ্য — jogyô — "qualified; deserving".
তুমি যদি যোগ্য হও, তাহলে চাকরিটা পাবে। tumi jôdi jogyô hôo, tāhôle chākriṭā pābe. If you're qualified, you'll get the job.

The word, taken apart.
যোগ্য (jogyô) is Sanskrit yogya, fit for the yoke and so fit for a task (Monier-Williams). A person is যোগ্য for a post or a prize.

Lesson 5 of 5.
অভ্যস্ত হওয়া (ôbhyôstô hôoyā) — to get used to.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for suitable, then the Bengali for qualified.

You'll want to know: অভ্যস্ত হওয়া.
অভ্যস্ত হওয়া — ôbhyôstô hôoyā — "to get used to".
এখানে এক মাস থাকলে তুমি গরমে অভ্যস্ত হয়ে যাবে। ekhāne ek mās thākle tumi gôrôme ôbhyôstô hôye jābe. If you stay here a month, you'll get used to the heat.
Past (he/she …): তিনি নতুন শহরে অভ্যস্ত হলেন, tini nôtun shôhôre ôbhyôstô hôlen.

The word, taken apart.
অভ্যস্ত হওয়া (ôbhyôstô hôoyā) is Sanskrit abhyasta, practised (Monier-Williams), with হওয়া: to become practised at living with something, to get used to it. What you get used to takes -এ.
