## You'll want to know: способствовать
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C349-VLIYANIYE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**способствовать** (*sposóbstvovat*) — "to contribute to, to promote".

> Спорт способствует здоровому сну.
>
> *Sport sposóbstvuyet zdoróvomu snu.*
>
> Sport promotes healthy sleep.

Imperfective; present *способствую, способствуешь*. It takes the dative: *способствовать успеху*. Bookish.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**способствовать** is built on *способ* "a way, a means" with the verb suffix *-ствовать*: to be a means to something. The further origin of *способ* is not settled.
