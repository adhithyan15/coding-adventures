## You'll want to know: lūcānica, lūcānicae
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C354-ASSUS-02]; assesses=[] -->

**lūcānica, lūcānicae** — "a smoked sausage".

> Senex lūcānicam et pānem edēbat.
>
> *The old man was eating a sausage and bread.*

Feminine. A smoked pork sausage from southern Italy; Martial sends one as a little present.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**lūcānica** is the sausage of Lucania, a region of southern Italy, named after the people who made it (EtymDB).
