## You'll want to know: el comunicado
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C515-RUMOR-02]; assesses=[] -->

**el comunicado** — "the official statement, the press release".

> La empresa envió un comunicado a la prensa.
>
> *The company sent a statement to the press.*

*Según el comunicado* — according to the statement.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*comunicado* is built on the participle of *comunicar* 'to communicate', which comes from Latin *commūnicāre* 'to share' (Lewis & Short), from *commūnis* 'common'.
