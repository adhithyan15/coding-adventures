## You'll want to know: مذاکره
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C366-BAHS-03]; assesses=[] -->

**مذاکره** (*mozâkere*) — "a negotiation, talks".

> مذاکره دو کشور سه روز طول کشید.
>
> *mozâkere-ye do keshvar se ruz tul keshid.*
>
> The talks between the two countries lasted three days.

Formal talks are often plural: *mozâkerât*. *Mozâkere kardan* — to negotiate.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**مذاکره** comes from Arabic *mudhākara* (EtymDB), a mutual verbal noun usually explained as reminding one another; in Persian ذ sounds like z.
