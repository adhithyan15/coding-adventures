## You'll want to know: ずきずき
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C445-ACHE445-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ずきずき** — *zukizuki* — "throbbing (of pain)".

> **むしばが ずきずき いたみます。** — *mushiba ga zukizuki itamimasu* — "My bad tooth is throbbing."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ずきずき** (*zukizuki*) is a native Japanese mimetic word, inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, and its older origin is not settled (JMdict).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
