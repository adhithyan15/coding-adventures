## You'll want to know: cruel
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C723-GENEROSO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**cruel** — "cruel".

> Fue muy cruel con su hermano.
>
> *He was very cruel to his brother.*

Same form for men and women; plural *crueles*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*cruel* is usually traced to Latin *crūdēlis* "hard-hearted, cruel" (Lewis & Short), a word built on *crūdus* "raw, bloody".
