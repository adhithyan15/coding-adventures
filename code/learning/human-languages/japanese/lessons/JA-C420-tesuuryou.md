## You'll want to know: てすうりょう
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C420-RYOUSHUUSHO420-01]; assesses=[] -->

**てすうりょう** — *tesūryō* — "a handling fee".

> **ぎんこうで てすうりょうを はらいました。** — *ginkō de tesūryō o haraimashita* — "I paid a handling fee at the bank."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**てすうりょう** (*tesūryō*) is built from both layers of the vocabulary: the native Japanese (wago) *te*, "hand", inherited rather than borrowed; the Sino-Japanese *sū*, "number" (Middle Chinese *srjuH*), an on'yomi borrowed from Chinese; and the Sino-Japanese *ryō*, "fee" (*lew*), an on'yomi borrowed from Chinese (JMdict; KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
