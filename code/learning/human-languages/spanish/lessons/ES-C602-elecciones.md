## You'll want to know: las elecciones
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C602-VOTO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**las elecciones** — "the elections".

> Las elecciones son en mayo.
>
> *The elections are in May.*

Plural for a political vote; in the singular *la elección* is a choice.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*elecciones* is the plural of *elección*, a learned borrowing from Latin *electio* 'choice', built on *eligere* 'to pick out', from *legere* 'to gather' (EtymDB).
