## You'll want to know: خشکسالی
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C478-RIZGARD-03]; assesses=[] -->

**خشکسالی** (*khoshksâli*) — "a drought".

> خشکسالی به کشاورزها ضرر زد.
>
> *Khoshksâli be keshâvarz-hâ zarar zad.*
>
> The drought hurt the farmers.

*khoshk* 'dry' + *sâl* 'year' + *-i*: a dry year.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*khoshksâli* is a native compound of *khoshk* 'dry', which EtymDB lists, *sâl* 'year', from Middle Persian *sāl* (EtymDB), and the ending *-i*.
