## You'll want to know: довод
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C338-DOVOD-02]; assesses=[] -->

**довод** (*dóvod*) — "a point made, a reason given".

> Его доводы меня не убедили.
>
> *Yegó dóvody menyá ne ubedíli.*
>
> His points did not convince me.

Masculine. More bookish than *аргумент*: *привести довод* — to put forward a point.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**довод** is built from *до-* "up to" and *вод-*, the root of *водить* "to lead", the iterative partner of *вести*, from Proto-Slavic *\*voditi* "to lead, to conduct" (EtymDB). A point made leads the listener up to a conclusion.
