## You'll want to know: उपगच्छति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C412-VIRAMA412-03]; assesses=[] -->

**उपगच्छति** — *upagacchati* — "approaches; comes near".

> धूमयानं स्थानकम् उपगच्छति।
>
> *dhūmayānaṃ sthānakam upagacchati.*
>
> The train is coming up to the station.

Past (it …): धूमयानं स्थानकम् उपगतवत्, *dhūmayānaṃ sthānakam upagatavat*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**उपगच्छति** (*upagacchati*) is built from *upa*, "towards, near", and the root √gam, "to go", which also gives *gacchati*, "goes"; MW gives *upa-gam* as to go near, approach, arrive at (MW).
