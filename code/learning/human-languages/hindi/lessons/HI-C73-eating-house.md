## You'll want to know: होटल
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C73-EATING-01]; assesses=[] -->

**होटल** (*hoṭal*) — "an eating house".

English *hotel*, and behind it Latin *hospitāle*, a house for guests. The same
Latin word gave English *hospital* and *hospitality*: one root for the place that
takes a stranger in, whether to feed, to lodge or to heal.

Here is the part a dictionary will not tell you loudly enough. In everyday Hindi
a होटल is most often somewhere you **eat**, not somewhere you sleep. A small
roadside place with six tables and no rooms at all calls itself a होटल, and
**चाय होटल** is a tea shop.

So take the word as "eating house" first and "hotel" second, and you will read
far more signboards correctly.

The ो is the *o* matra, a single stroke above the headline with a tail.
