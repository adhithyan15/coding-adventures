## Script — the shape
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-SCRIPT-SO-01]; assesses=[JA-SCRIPT-KO-01] -->

> **そ** — *so*

One stroke, and the pen never lifts:

1. a short **bar** at the top, left to right
2. turn sharply and run **down and to the left** until you reach the long middle bar
3. turn back and run **right** along the whole of that bar
4. double back a little way, then drop into an **open curve** that rounds the
   lower left and finishes along the base to the right

The pen zigzags twice before it reaches the curve. Some typefaces print the top
apart from the rest; written by hand here, it is one unbroken stroke.

With **こ** after it, you can now write the word from the last lesson: **そこ**,
*soko*, there.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[JA-SCRIPT-SO-01, JA-LEX-ANCHOR-SOKO, JA-SCRIPT-KO-01] -->

1. Trace **そ** saying "bar, down, across, curve".
2. Write **そこ** from memory and say *soko*.
3. Write **ここ** and **そこ** side by side, and say which one is the place by the listener.
