Spanish, chapter 538: Antes y después.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
anteriormente — previously, earlier.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for a short time ago, then the Spanish for the day before yesterday.

You'll want to know: anteriormente.
anteriormente — "previously, earlier".
Anteriormente vivía en Madrid. Previously she lived in Madrid.
A formal word; in speech people say antes.

The word, taken apart.
anteriormente is built on anterior and the ending -mente (EtymDB). Anterior is a learned word from Latin anterior 'former', built on ante 'before'.
