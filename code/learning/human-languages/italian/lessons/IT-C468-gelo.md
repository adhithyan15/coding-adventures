## You'll want to know: il gelo
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C468-GELO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**il gelo** — "the frost, the icy cold".

> Con questo gelo resto a casa.
>
> *In this icy cold I am staying at home.*

Also a chill between people: *è sceso il gelo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*gelo* is inherited from Latin *gelū* 'frost, chill' (EtymDB).
