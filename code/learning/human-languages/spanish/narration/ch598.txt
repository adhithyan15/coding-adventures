Spanish, chapter 598: Saber hacer.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
el conocimiento — the knowledge.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for to assess, then the Spanish for the assessment.

You'll want to know: el conocimiento.
el conocimiento — "the knowledge".
Tiene buenos conocimientos de inglés. She has a good knowledge of English.
In the plural it means what you know about a subject.

The word, taken apart.
conocimiento is built on conocer, inherited from Latin cognoscere 'to get to know' (EtymDB).

What you've built.
One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.

You'll want to know: esforzarse.
esforzarse — "to make an effort, to try hard".
Me esfuerzo mucho en clase. I try very hard in class.
Stem change o becomes ue: me esfuerzo. Spelling: me esforcé.

The word, taken apart.
esforzarse is built on fuerza 'strength', inherited from Late Latin fortia, from fortis 'strong' (EtymDB).
