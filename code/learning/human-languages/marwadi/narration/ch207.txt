Marwadi (Marwari), chapter 207: A Garden and a Tank.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
बगीचो (bagīcho) — a garden.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for on the other side, then the Marwadi for separate.

You'll want to know: बगीचो.
बगीचो — bagīcho — "a garden".
बगीचो कोट कनै है। bagīcho koṭ kanai hai. The garden is near the fort.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
बगीचो (bagīcho) is usually traced to Persian bāghcha, "a small garden".

Lesson 2 of 5.
कुंड (kuṇḍ) — a water tank.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for separate, then the Marwadi for a garden.

You'll want to know: कुंड.
कुंड — kuṇḍ — "a water tank".
कुंड मांय पाणी कोनी। kuṇḍ mā̃y pāṇī konī. There's no water in the tank.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
कुंड (kuṇḍ) is inherited from Sanskrit kuṇḍa, "bowl, basin of water". CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry, but lists Hindi kū̃ḍ, "tub", and Gujarati kũḍ, "basin" (CDIAL 3264).

Lesson 3 of 5.
नाको (nāko) — a checkpoint; the mouth of a road.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for a garden, then the Marwadi for a water tank.

You'll want to know: नाको.
नाको — nāko — "a checkpoint; the mouth of a road".
नाको अठै सूं नजीक है। nāko aṭhai sū̃ najīk hai. The checkpoint is close to here.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
नाको (nāko) is inherited from a reconstructed nākka, "like a nose, projecting". CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry, but lists Hindi nākā, "end of road, passage", and Sindhi nāko, "entrance, pass" (CDIAL 7037).

Lesson 4 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for a water tank, then the Marwadi for a checkpoint.

You'll want to know: फाटक.
फाटक — phāṭak — "a big gate; a railway crossing".
फाटक बंद है, धीरे चालो। phāṭak band hai, dhīre chālo. The gate is shut, go slowly.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
फाटक (phāṭak) is inherited from a reconstructed phāṭṭakka, "gate, door". CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry, but lists Hindi phāṭak, "gate" (CDIAL 9069).
