# లావెక్కు (lāvekku) — to put on weight

## Warm-up

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Telugu for to grow weak, then the Telugu for to grow thin.

## You'll want to know: లావెక్కు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C521-BALAM521-04]; assesses=[] -->

**లావెక్కు** — *lāvekku* — "to put on weight".

> పెళ్ళి తరువాత అన్నయ్య లావెక్కాడు.
>
> *peḷḷi taruvāta annayya lāvekkāḍu.*
>
> After his wedding my brother put on weight.

Past (I …): లావెక్కాను, *lāvekkānu*.

లావు, stout, + ఎక్కు, to rise.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**లావెక్కు** (*lāvekku*) is a native compound: *lāvu*, "bigness, fatness" (DEDR 291), and *ekku*, "to increase, to rise" (DEDR 768).
