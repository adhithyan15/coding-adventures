## You'll want to know: le cintre
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C526-LINGE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**le cintre** — "coat hanger".

> Je mets ma chemise sur un cintre.
>
> *I put my shirt on a hanger.*

In Québec *le support*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*le cintre* is built on *cintrer* "to curve" (EtymDB), from Vulgar Latin *cinctūrāre*, from Latin *cinctūra* "a girdle", from *cingere* "to gird" (Lewis & Short).
