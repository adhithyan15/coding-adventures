Punjabi, chapter 162: Return to Three Familiar A1 Writing Moves.
3 lessons. The first lesson already needs your eyes or your hands, so save this one for when you have stopped.


Lesson 1 of 3.
Return to three old card choices — select three previously taught form values from two old card banks.

Before we start: this one needs your hands, so it is not a driving lesson. You will want a pen and something to write on. You can listen to all of it now and come back to the parts that need looking at once you have stopped.

Warm-up.
The old A, ਕ and B, ਖ cards choose values you have already learned. For this supported return, reopen the old word banks: A has ਅਮਨ, ਪੰਜਾਬੀ, ਪਿੰਡ; B has ਮਨਨ, ਹਿੰਦੀ, ਸ਼ਹਿਰ. No new word or person is being introduced.

Writing — supported mixed selection.
With the banks visible, write the old value chosen by each mark. Go one line at a time. There is no clock and no independent-writing credit here.
ਨਾਂ (B, ਖ): ______ ਭਾਸ਼ਾ (A, ਕ): ______ ਰਿਹਾਇਸ਼ (B, ਖ): ______.

Guided Practice — check after writing.
First check the three card choices: ਮਨਨ, ਪੰਜਾਬੀ, ਸ਼ਹਿਰ. On a separate pass, check spelling and the space after each colon. Close the banks again. This small supported return is not a complete A1 mock.

Wrap-up recall.
Say which card mark belonged to each of your three lines. The next return uses all six familiar fields without a visible bank.


Lesson 2 of 3.
Return to the six familiar fields — retrieve six taught mixed-card values in Gurmukhi under a practice timer.

Before we start: this one needs your hands, so it is not a driving lesson. You will want a pen and something to write on and your eyes, because the lesson points at something written down. You can listen to all of it now and come back to the parts that need looking at once you have stopped.

Warm-up.
Close the value banks and earlier forms. Have a blank page and a three-minute timer ready. The marks below select only old values. Do not look at a model while the timer runs.

Writing — independent timed form.
Start the timer. Write all six marked fields in Gurmukhi; use Gurmukhi digits for age and phone. Stop at three minutes, leaving unfinished lines as they are. Keep this first attempt unchanged.
ਨਾਂ (B, ਖ): ______ ਭਾਸ਼ਾ (A, ਕ): ______ ਰਿਹਾਇਸ਼ (B, ਖ): ______ ਕੰਮ (A, ਕ): ______ ਉਮਰ (B, ਖ): ______ ਫ਼ੋਨ (A, ਕ): ______.

Guided Practice — repair separately.
After stopping, reopen the old banks and check values, spelling, spacing, digit order, and phone grouping one at a time. Repair on another page; it does not replace the clocked evidence. This return is not a complete A1 mock.

Wrap-up recall.
Record how many lines were complete at the stop. The full form-plus-message condition belongs to the later two complete mocks.


Lesson 3 of 3.
Return to the reader and the clock — pace a Gurmukhi greeting and wellbeing question for one known reader.

Before we start: this one needs your hands, so it is not a driving lesson. You will want a pen and something to write on and your eyes or your hands for the steps that ask you to read, point, write or gesture. You can listen to everything else now. I will say so again when we reach it, but leave this section until you have stopped: Warm-up.

Warm-up.
[once you have stopped driving — this part needs your eyes or your hands; if you are driving, skip ahead to the next part]
Keep the previous lesson's six-field first attempt. In one minute, point to a line whose card mark you selected correctly and a line you would repair. Do not rewrite the timed page; its first-attempt evidence stays as it was.

Writing — one small timed opening.
Close the earlier message pages. As Aman, write to Manan in Gurmukhi. In ninety seconds, greet your named reader and ask one familiar wellbeing question. Choose your own taught words; no sample sentence is visible. Stop on time and keep the first attempt.

Guided Practice — inspect one boundary.
After the timer, check whether Manan is clearly the reader. Then check the question mark and spacing. A repair belongs on another page and is not timed evidence. This opening is not a complete A1 mock.

Wrap-up recall.
The complete paper still needs a fresh purpose, a 30–40-word message, and a twenty-minute form-plus-message clock. Those conditions belong to the two separate mocks, not this short return.
