## You'll want to know: けんこう
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C220-WEIGHT220-01]; assesses=[] -->

**けんこう** — *kenkō* — "health".

> **けんこうは たいせつです。** — *kenkō wa taisetsu desu* — "Health is important."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**けんこう** (*kenkō*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *ken*, "strong" (Middle Chinese *gjonH*) and *kō*, "at ease" (*khang*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
