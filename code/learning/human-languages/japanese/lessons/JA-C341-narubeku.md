## You'll want to know: なるべく
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C341-SETTOKU341-04]; assesses=[] -->

**なるべく** — *narubeku* — "as much as possible".

> **あぶないから、なるべく はやく かえった ほうが いいです。** — *abunai kara, narubeku hayaku kaetta hō ga ii desu* — "It's dangerous, so you had better go home as early as you can."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**なるべく** (*narubeku*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, and its older origin is not settled. JMdict notes that it is usually written in kana (JMdict).
