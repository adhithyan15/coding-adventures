## You'll want to know: гипс
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C484-PLASTER-01]; assesses=[] -->

**гипс** (*gips*) — "a plaster cast; plaster".

> Шесть недель он ходил с рукой в гипсе.
>
> *Shest nedél on khodíl s rukóy v gípse.*
>
> He went about with his arm in plaster for six weeks.

Masculine. *В гипсе* — in plaster.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

EtymDB lists *гипс* without a source; it is usually traced through German *Gips* to Greek *gýpsos* 'chalk, gypsum'.
