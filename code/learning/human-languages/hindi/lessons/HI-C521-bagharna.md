## You'll want to know: बघारना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C521-TADKA521-04]; assesses=[] -->

**बघारना** — *baghārnā* — "to temper (with hot spiced oil)".

> माँ ने दाल को घी और जीरे से बघारा।
>
> *Māṁ ne dāl ko ghī aur jīre se baghārā.*
>
> Mother tempered the dal with ghee and cumin.

Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने सब्ज़ी बघारी, *usne sabzī baghārī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**बघारना** (*baghārnā*) is a tadbhava, inherited from Sanskrit *vyāghārayati*, "sprinkles (with hot fat)" (CDIAL 12191). CDIAL gives the Hindi verb as "to brown onions and spices in heated oil".
