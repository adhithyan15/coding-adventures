## You'll want to know: گناه
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C376-TAQSIR-02]; assesses=[] -->

**گناه** (*gonâh*) — "a sin; guilt".

> دزدی گناه است.
>
> *dozdi gonâh ast.*
>
> Stealing is a sin.

*Gonâh-e u nist* — it is not his fault. *Gonâhkâr* — guilty.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**گناه** is an inherited Persian word, usually traced to Middle Persian *wināh* "sin, offence"; EtymDB lists it without a parent.
