## You'll want to know: سنورنا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C547-TASAVVUR547-05]; assesses=[] -->

**سنورنا** — *sanvarnā* — "to take shape nicely; to be put right".

> اگر بچوں نے اچھا سیکھا تو کل سنور جائے گا۔
>
> *agar bachchoṅ ne achchhā sīkhā to kal sanvar jāe gā.*
>
> If the children learn well, tomorrow will turn out well.

Past (it …): وہ کمرہ سنور گیا, *voh kamrā sanvar gayā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**سنورنا** (*sanvarnā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousins *sãwārṇā*, "to arrange, to deck", and its intransitive *sãwarnā*, "to be arranged", under Sanskrit *saṃvārayati*, "puts in order" (CDIAL 13021).
