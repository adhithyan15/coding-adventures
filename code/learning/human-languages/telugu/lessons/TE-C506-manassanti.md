# మనశ్శాంతి (manaśśānti) — peace of mind

## You'll want to know: మనశ్శాంతి
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C506-SAHANAM506-04]; assesses=[] -->

**మనశ్శాంతి** — *manaśśānti* — "peace of mind".

> పల్లెలో ఉంటే నాకు మనశ్శాంతిగా ఉంటుంది.
>
> *pallelō uṇṭē nāku manaśśāntigā uṇṭundi.*
>
> In the village I have peace of mind.

మనసు, mind, + శాంతి, peace.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**మనశ్శాంతి** (*manaśśānti*) is borrowed from Sanskrit: *manas*, "mind", and *śānti*, "rest, peace" (CDIAL 12391); the *s* of *manas* becomes *ś* before *ś*.
