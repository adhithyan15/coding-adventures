Marathi, chapter 434: Mix-ups and Fiascos.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
गैरसोय (gairsoy) — inconvenience.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to get dented, then the Marathi for to stop working.

You'll want to know: गैरसोय.
गैरसोय — gairsoy — "inconvenience".
पाणी नसल्यामुळे सगळ्यांची गैरसोय झाली. pāṇī nasalyāmuḷe sagaḷyāñcī gairsoy jhālī. Everyone was put out because there was no water.
Feminine.
गैरसोय (gairsoy) होणे, to be inconvenienced: the person takes -ची.

The word, taken apart.
गैरसोय (gairsoy) is built from gair-, "un-, other than", a loan usually traced to Arabic ġair, and सोय, "convenience".

Lesson 4 of 5.
धावपळ करणे (dhāvpaḷ karṇe) — to rush about.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for missing each other, then the Marathi for a fiasco.

You'll want to know: धावपळ करणे.
धावपळ करणे — dhāvpaḷ karṇe — "to rush about".
गाडी पकडण्यासाठी आम्ही खूप धावपळ केली. gāḍī pakḍaṇyāsāṭhī āmhī khūp dhāvpaḷ kelī. We rushed about a lot to catch the train.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने धावपळ केली, tyāne dhāvpaḷ kelī.
धावपळ is feminine, so the past is धावपळ केली.

The word, taken apart.
धावपळ करणे (dhāvpaḷ karṇe) is built from धाव, "a run" (CDIAL 6800), and paḷ, the stem of पळणे, "to run off", with करणे.

Lesson 5 of 5.
मार्ग काढणे (mārga kāḍhṇe) — to find a way; to get round a problem.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a fiasco, then the Marathi for to rush about.

You'll want to know: मार्ग काढणे.
मार्ग काढणे — mārga kāḍhṇe — "to find a way; to get round a problem".
पुलावर कोंडी होती, पण चालकाने मार्ग काढला. pulāvar koṇḍī hotī, paṇ cālakāne mārga kāḍhlā. The bridge was jammed, but the driver found a way through.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने मार्ग काढला, tyāne mārga kāḍhlā.
Also to solve a problem: अडचणीतून मार्ग काढणे (mārga kāḍhṇe).

The word, taken apart.
मार्ग काढणे (mārga kāḍhṇe) joins मार्ग, "a way", a tatsama from Sanskrit mārga (Monier-Williams), to काढणे, "to take out".
