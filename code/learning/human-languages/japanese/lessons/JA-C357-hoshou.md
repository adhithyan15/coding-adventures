## You'll want to know: ほしょう
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C357-KEIYAKU357-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ほしょう** — *hoshō* — "a guarantee; a warranty".

> **こわれたら、ほしょうが ありますか。** — *kowaretara, hoshō ga arimasu ka* — "If it breaks, is there a warranty?"

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ほしょう** (*hoshō*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *ho*, "preserve" (Middle Chinese *pawX*) and *shō*, "evidence" (*tsyingH*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
