## You'll want to know: पदोन्नती
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C509-BADHATI509-01]; assesses=[] -->

**पदोन्नती** — *padonnatī* — "a promotion".

> मीराला या वर्षी पदोन्नती मिळाली.
>
> *mīrālā yā varṣī padonnatī miḷālī.*
>
> Meera got a promotion this year.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पदोन्नती** (*padonnatī*) is a tatsama compound of Sanskrit *pada*, "step, position", and *unnati*, "rising" (Monier-Williams).
