# HL69 — Reconcile Marwadi A1 evidence after chapters 44–149

## Outcome

The Marwadi A1 inventory must measure the curriculum that exists now. Its last
substantive reconciliation predates the additive vocabulary course through
chapter 149, so the dependency planner currently schedules several already
taught points as if they were missing.

This change is an evidence repair, not a proficiency claim. It must attach
existing introduced knowledge atoms to fully satisfied inventory points, leave
partial demands unmapped, and retain every external- and human-validation
boundary.

## Exact reconciliation

The following still-null points have complete direct owners and may become
covered:

- common periods of time;
- the reflexive possessive `आपणो`;
- here/there, today/yesterday/tomorrow, how/where, and why/because;
- the dictionary-form infinitive;
- relative position, distance, and direction;
- calendar/season location, colour, good/bad, and speaking/writing;
- body parts, physical characteristics and character, body actions, and social
  relationships beyond the family;
- the sky/universe, country/town/city, and flora.

Every probe must name atoms already introduced by a direct Marwadi lesson. A
review occurrence, a filename, incidental prose, or an atom invented for this
inventory is not evidence.

## Partial points stay partial

The audit must also correct stale notes without turning partial vocabulary into
complete functional or thematic coverage. In particular:

- one taught ordinal does not constitute an ordinal series;
- isolated infinitives for thinking, knowing, ability, liking, and wanting do
  not teach conjugated opinion, knowledge, ability, preference, or desire
  frames;
- parts of the day do not teach clock time or meal names;
- personal objects do not teach identity documents;
- some school, work, media, health, travel, service, leisure, and arts words do
  not satisfy all proxy subdomains grouped into their inventory points;
- five independent vowel letters are still not the full Devanagari independent
  vowel inventory.

Those points retain `probe: null` and their notes must name the exact remaining
joint rather than repeat obsolete zero-occurrence claims.

## Measurement and boundaries

The inventory prose and its owning test must use the current direct-owner
snapshot: 897 lessons across 150 chapters, introducing 831 distinct atoms. The
test must pin every newly mapped point to its exact atom set and verify that:

- the atoms exist in the current Marwadi lesson corpus;
- every deliberately partial point named above remains null;
- all four inventory scope dimensions remain `partial`;
- the inventory remains incomplete and makes no readiness, attainment,
  external-syllabus, mock-exam, or human-validation claim.

After the new count is pinned, the dependency planner is rerun. Its result, not
the pre-reconciliation 118-gap estimate, selects the next authoring tranche.
