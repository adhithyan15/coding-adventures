## You'll want to know: فدية
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C347-KHATF347-04]; assesses=[] -->

**فدية** — *fidya* — "a ransom".

> طلبت العصابة فدية كبيرة.
>
> *ṭalabat al-ʿiṣāba fidya kabīra.*
>
> The gang demanded a large ransom.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**فدية** (*fidya*) is built on the native Arabic root *f-d-y* (Lane), "to ransom, to give a thing for another"; *fidāʾ* is the same.
