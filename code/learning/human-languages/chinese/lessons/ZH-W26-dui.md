## Script — a hand, and a thumb's width
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-SCRIPT-DUI-01]; assesses=[] -->

> 对

**Five strokes.** Two on the left, three on the right.

The left half is 又, an old picture of a hand:

1. the **héngpiě** — a short flat start that turns and falls to the lower left,
   one stroke, no lift
2. the **nà**, crossing it and falling to the lower right

The right half is 寸:

3. the **héng** across the right half
4. the long **shùgōu** down through it, hooking left at the foot
5. the **diǎn** dot, placed last, to the left of the hook

**The dot is the final stroke** — the same rule 的 followed. And the left half is
finished before the right begins, as always.

Hold on to strokes 1 and 2. That 又 opens **欢** two chapters from now, and you
will not have to learn it twice.
