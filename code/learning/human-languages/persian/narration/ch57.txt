Persian, chapter 57: Bear, Camel, Donkey, Butterfly, Ant.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
خرس (khers) — a bear.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a monkey, then the Persian for a snake.

You'll want to know: خرس.
خرس — khers — "a bear".

The word, taken apart.
khers is a native Persian word, inherited rather than borrowed; it is usually traced to Middle Persian xirs, "bear". EtymDB takes it back to the Indo-European word for "bear" behind Latin ursus and Greek arktos.

You'll want to know: پروانه.
پروانه — parvâne — "a butterfly".

The word, taken apart.
parvâne continues Middle Persian parwānag (EtymDB), which meant "guide, leader". How the word came to name the butterfly and the moth is not settled.
