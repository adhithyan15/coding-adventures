## You'll want to know: سرد
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C295-RIWAYA295-04]; assesses=[] -->

**سرد** — *sarada* — "to recount, to narrate in order".

> سرد المسافر تفاصيل رحلته.
>
> *sarada al-musāfir tafāṣīl riḥlatihi.*
>
> The traveller recounted the details of his journey.

Present: يسرد, *yasrud*.

The noun سرد is narration, as in a novel.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**سرد** (*sarada*) is built on the native Arabic root *s-r-d* (Lane), first "to stitch" and "to link rings of mail one into the next", then "to carry on a story without a break".
