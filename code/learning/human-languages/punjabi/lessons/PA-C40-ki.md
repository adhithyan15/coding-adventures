## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-JOIN-KI-01]; assesses=[PA-LEX-KI-02, PA-JOIN-ATE-CLAUSE-01] -->

Look at these two very carefully, because they are not the same word:

- **ਕੀ** — *kī* — **what**, with the long bihari
- **ਕਿ** — *ki* — **that**, with the short sihari

One stroke apart, and completely different jobs. You have had **ਕੀ** since
you first asked someone's name. **ਕਿ** is new.

**ਅਤੇ** and **ਪਰ** put two sentences **side by side**. **ਕਿ** does something
different: it lets one sentence be **swallowed** by another, as the thing a verb
is about.

> *(verb of thinking, knowing or saying)* **ਕਿ** *(whole sentence)*

English does the same with *that*, and like English, Punjabi often drops it in
speech. Learn it in place first.
