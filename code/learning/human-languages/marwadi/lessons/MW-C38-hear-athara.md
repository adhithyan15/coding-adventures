## You'll want to know
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-ATHARA-01]; assesses=[MW-LEX-AATH-01] -->

> *aṭhārā* — **eighteen**

Eight is *āṭh*. Eighteen is *aṭhārā*: the same two sounds, with the length moved
from the front of the word to the middle of it.

Say them back to back — *āṭh*, *aṭhārā* — and hear the long vowel change places.
Eighteen is the second of the two teens that begin on a vowel, which is why the
letter came before it.
