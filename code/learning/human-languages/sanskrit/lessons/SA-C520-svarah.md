## You'll want to know: स्वरः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C520-SVARA520-01]; assesses=[] -->

**स्वरः** — *svaraḥ* — "a musical note".

> गुरुः सप्त स्वरान् पाठयति।
>
> *guruḥ sapta svarān pāṭhayati.*
>
> The teacher teaches the seven notes.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**स्वरः** (*svaraḥ*) comes from the root √svṛ, "to sound, to sing"; MW gives *svara* as sound, voice, tone, and a note of the musical scale (MW).
