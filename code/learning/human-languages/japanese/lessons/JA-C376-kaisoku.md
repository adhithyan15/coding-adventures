## You'll want to know: かいそく
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C376-SHINKANSEN376-02]; assesses=[] -->

**かいそく** — *kaisoku* — "a rapid train".

> **かいそくは ちいさい えきに とまりません。** — *kaisoku wa chiisai eki ni tomarimasen* — "The rapid train does not stop at small stations."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**かいそく** (*kaisoku*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *kai*, "pleasant" (Middle Chinese *khwaejH*) and *soku*, "fast" (*suwk*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
