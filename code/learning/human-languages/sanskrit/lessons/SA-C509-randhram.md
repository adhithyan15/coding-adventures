## You'll want to know: रन्ध्रम्
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C509-JARJARA509-03]; assesses=[] -->

**रन्ध्रम्** — *randhram* — "an opening; a hole".

> मम स्यूते एकं रन्ध्रम् अस्ति।
>
> *mama syūte ekaṃ randhram asti.*
>
> There is a hole in my bag.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**रन्ध्रम्** (*randhram*) probably comes from the root √rad, "to split", as MW suggests; MW gives *randhra* as a slit, an opening, a hole (MW).
