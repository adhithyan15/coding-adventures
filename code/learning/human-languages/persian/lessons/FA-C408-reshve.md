## You'll want to know: رشوه
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C408-KOLAHBARDARI-03]; assesses=[] -->

**رشوه** (*reshve*) — "a bribe".

> او رشوه نگرفت.
>
> *u reshve nagereft.*
>
> He did not take a bribe.

*Reshve dâdan / gereftan* — to give / take a bribe.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**رشوه** was borrowed from Arabic *rashwa* "a bribe" (EtymDB).
