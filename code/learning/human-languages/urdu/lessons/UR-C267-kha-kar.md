# کھا کر (khā kar) — one thing, then the next

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[UR-GRAMMAR-C265-ERGATIVE-01, UR-GRAMMAR-C264-PAST-02, UR-LEX-NICE-TO-MEET] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "I ate" and "I slept". (*maiṅ ne khāyā*, *maiṅ soyā*.) Two events. This lesson joins them into one sentence.

## Grammar Lens: the stem and کر
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-GRAMMAR-C267-KAR-01]; assesses=[UR-LEX-C46-FAMILY46-02, UR-LEX-C47-VERBS47-02, UR-LEX-ANA, UR-LEX-C47-VERBS47-01] -->

> **کھا کر سویا۔**
>
> *khā kar soyā.* — "Having eaten, he slept."

The first action keeps only its stem, **کھا** (*khā*), and takes **کر** (*kar*), written apart. The last verb carries the person and the time:

> **میں کھانا کھا کر سویا۔**
>
> *maiṅ khānā khā kar soyā.* — "I ate, then slept."

> **سارا گھر آ کر بیٹھی۔**
>
> *Sārā ghar ā kar baiṭhī.* — "Sara came home and sat down."

> **ہم کھا کر جائیں گے۔**
>
> *ham khā kar jāeṅ ge.* — "We'll eat, then go."

You have said one for a long time: **مل کر** (*mil kar*), "having met", in the greeting **خوشی ہوئی** closes.

## You'll want to know: کر کے, and the last verb
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-GRAMMAR-C267-KAR-02]; assesses=[UR-LEX-KAAM, UR-LEX-CHAI, UR-LEX-C46-FAMILY46-03] -->

**کرنا** (*karnā*) makes **کر کے** (*kar ke*), never a doubled **کر**:

> **کام کر کے میں گھر آیا۔**
>
> *kām kar ke maiṅ ghar āyā.* — "I finished the work and came home."

The last verb decides **نے** (*ne*). With **پی** (*pī*), "drank", the doer takes it:

> **اس نے کھا کر چائے پی۔**
>
> *us ne khā kar chāy pī.* — "He ate, then drank tea."
