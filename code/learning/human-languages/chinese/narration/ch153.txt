You'll want to know: 比.
比 — bǐ — "than; compared with".
我比他大 wǒ bǐ tā dà — I'm older than him.
The next lesson puts the character in your hand. It sounds like bǐ, a pen, and it is a different word.

The word, taken apart.
比 (bǐ) is inherited from Middle Chinese pjijX (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'to compare; side by side'. It is the bǐ of bǐsài, a match. The bǐ of qiānbǐ, a pencil, is another word with the same sound.

What you've built.
比 (bǐ) sits between the two things compared, and the adjective comes last: 我, 比 (bǐ), 他, 大.
大 for people is old: 我比他大 is I'm older than him. 比 itself is the bǐ of bǐsài, a match: two sides set against each other.

Script — two halves side by side, the left one first.
比 (bǐ).
Two halves, side by side, the left one first. The right half you write already, twice over, on the right of 能.
The left half, two strokes: a short bar, then a vertical that runs down and turns up to the right at its foot.
The right half, two strokes, as on the right of 能: a short left-falling stroke, then a vertical that bends right along the foot and hooks up, the same movement that ends 七.
2 + 2 = four.

Lesson 3 of 7.
他比我大两岁 (tā bǐ wǒ dà liǎng suì) — comparing with 比.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Two people, one comparison, and then by how much.
[once you have stopped driving — read: 我比他大, aloud — wǒ bǐ tā dà]
[your turn — recall: say 二十岁 — èrshí suì, twenty years old]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say zuì, most]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Grammar Lens: 比, then the adjective, then by how much.
A, 比 (bǐ), B, then the adjective: A is more so than B. How much more goes after the adjective, at the very end.
我比他大 wǒ bǐ tā dà — I'm older than him. 他比我大两岁 tā bǐ wǒ dà liǎng suì — He's two years older than me. 这个比那个大一点儿 zhège bǐ nàge dà yìdiǎnr — This one is a bit bigger than that one.
There is no 很 in these. 很 describes one thing on its own; a comparison sets two against each other. 他很大 describes him; 他比我大 measures him against me.
The other way round, not as much as, comes with 没有:
我没有他那么大 wǒ méiyǒu tā nàme dà — I'm not as old as he is.
And zuì, most, needs no second thing at all: tā zuì dà.

You'll want to know: gèng.
gèng — "even more; still more".
tā gèng gāo — He's even taller.
tā bǐ wǒ gèng gāo — He's even taller than me.
Its character comes later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
gèng is inherited from Middle Chinese kaengH (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'to change; to alter'. 'Even more' is a later sense, assumed here, not quoted.

What you've built.
gèng goes straight before the adjective: tā gèng gāo.
With 比 (bǐ) it sharpens the comparison: both are tall, and he is taller still.

gēn — with; the same as.

You'll want to know: gēn.
gēn — "with; and"; with yíyàng after it, "the same as".
wǒ gēn tā yíyàng gāo — I'm as tall as he is.
wǒ gēn māma qù — I'm going with Mum.
Its character comes later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
gēn is inherited from Middle Chinese kon (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'the heel'. How the heel became 'with', following at someone's heels, is a guess the gloss does not make, so the history is not settled.

What you've built.
gēn … yíyàng, then an adjective, says two things are the same: wǒ gēn tā yíyàng gāo.
比 (bǐ) says one is more; gēn … yíyàng says neither is.

Warm-up.

You'll want to know: 那么.
那么 — nàme — "so; that (much)".
我没有他那么大 wǒ méiyǒu tā nàme dà — I'm not as old as he is.
那么大 nàme dà — that big.

The word, taken apart.
那么 (nàme) is built from 那 'that' and 么, the 么 of 什么. The Guangyun records 那 as na (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'how; what'; how it came to mean 'that', and where the ending 么 comes from, are not settled.

What you've built.
没有 … 那么 (nàme), with an adjective after it, says not as much as: 我没有他那么大.
It is the other side of 比 (bǐ): 他比我大, so 我没有他那么大.

The exchange.
A: 你多大 nǐ duō dà — How old are you? B: 我二十岁 wǒ èrshí suì — I'm twenty. A: 他呢 tā ne — And him? B: 他比我大两岁 tā bǐ wǒ dà liǎng suì — He's two years older than me. A: nǐ bǐ tā gāo ma — Are you taller than him? B: wǒ méiyǒu tā nàme gāo — I'm not as tall as he is. A: 你妈妈呢 nǐ māma ne — And your mum? B: tā gēn wǒ yíyàng gāo — She's as tall as me. A: wǒ bǐ tā dà, tā gèng gāo — I'm older than him, and he's taller still.
比 (bǐ) puts the difference last: 大两岁. 没有 … 那么 (nàme) turns it round. gēn … yíyàng says neither is more.

Reading — the exchange at once.
[once you have stopped driving — read: the exchange through once without stopping, then answer the question below]
[question — say your answer, then pause 12 seconds]
How many years older than B is he?

What you've built — more, less and the same.
Three ways to set two things side by side:
他比我大两岁 tā bǐ wǒ dà liǎng suì — he's two years older than me.
我没有他那么大 wǒ méiyǒu tā nàme dà — I'm not as old as he is.
wǒ gēn tā yíyàng gāo — I'm as tall as he is.
Listen. Nǐ bǐ nǐ māma gāo ma?
Speak. Compare yourself with somebody you know: older or younger, taller or not as tall.
