Telugu, chapter 517: Symptoms.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
లక్షణం (lakṣaṇaṁ) — a symptom; a trait.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for an allergy, then the Telugu for sleeplessness.

You'll want to know: లక్షణం.
లక్షణం — lakṣaṇaṁ — "a symptom; a trait".
జ్వరం, దగ్గు ఆ జబ్బు లక్షణాలు. jvaraṁ, daggu ā jabbu lakṣaṇālu. Fever and cough are the symptoms of that illness.
Plural: లక్షణాలు, lakṣaṇālu.
Also a person's traits: మంచి లక్షణాలు, good qualities.

The word, taken apart.
లక్షణం (lakṣaṇaṁ) is borrowed from Sanskrit lakṣaṇa, "line, mark" (CDIAL 10882): the marks by which an illness or a person is known.

Lesson 2 of 6.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for sleeplessness, then the Telugu for a symptom.

You'll want to know: మైకం.
మైకం — maikaṁ — "dizziness; a daze".
ఎండలో నడిచాక నాకు మైకంగా అనిపించింది. eṇḍalō naḍicāka nāku maikaṅgā anipiñcindi. After walking in the sun, I felt giddy.
మైకంగా ఉంది, I feel giddy; also the daze of sleep.

The word, taken apart.
మైకం (maikaṁ) is native Dravidian: DEDR 4706 lists Telugu maikamu, "intoxication, unconsciousness, drowsiness", in the article of Tamil mayakku, "to bewilder, to confuse".

కొండ ఎక్కి తాతయ్య ఆయాసపడ్డారు. koṇḍa ekki tātayya āyāsapaḍḍāru. Climbing the hill, grandpa got breathless.
Past (I …): ఆయాసపడ్డాను, āyāsapaḍḍānu.
ఆయాసం, breathlessness, + పడు.

The word, taken apart.
ఆయాసపడు (āyāsapaḍu) is built from ఆయాసం, from Sanskrit āyāsa, "effort, exertion; fatigue" (Monier-Williams) and the native verb paḍu, "to feel, to suffer" (DEDR 3853).

కక్కు — kakku — "to throw up; to spit out".
బస్సులో ప్రయాణం చేస్తూ పాప కక్కింది. bassulō prayāṇaṁ cēstū pāpa kakkindi. The little girl threw up on the bus journey.
Past (I …): కక్కాను, kakkānu.

The word, taken apart.
కక్కు (kakku) is native Dravidian: DEDR 1079 lists Telugu k(r)akku, "to vomit, to disgorge".

వర్షాకాలంలో అమ్మమ్మకు కీళ్ళనొప్పులు ఎక్కువ. varṣākālaṁlō ammammaku kīḷḷanoppulu ekkuva. In the rainy season grandma's joints ache more.

కీళ్ళనొప్పులు (kīḷḷanoppulu) is built from కీళ్ళు, "joints", and native noppulu, "pains" (DEDR 3793).
