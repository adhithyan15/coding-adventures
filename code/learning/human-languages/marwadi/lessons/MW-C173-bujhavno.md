# बुझावणो (bujhāvṇo) — to put out (a fire or a lamp)

## You'll want to know: बुझावणो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C173-CHULHO173-04]; assesses=[] -->

**बुझावणो** — *bujhāvṇo* — "to put out (a fire or a lamp)".

> रात मांय हूं दीयो बुझाऊं हूं।
>
> *rāt mā̃y hū̃ dīyo bujhāū̃ hū̃.*
>
> At night I put out the lamp.

Past (I …): म्हैं दीयो बुझायो, *mhaĩ dīyo bujhāyo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**बुझावणो** (*bujhāvṇo*) is built on a reconstructed *\*vijjhāyati*, "is burnt out, goes out"; CDIAL lists Old Marwari *bujhāvaï*, "to put out" (CDIAL 11703).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
