## You'll want to know: shāngliang
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C348-TALK348-02]; assesses=[] -->

*shāngliang* — "to talk it over together; to consult".

> *wǒmen yīnggāi gēn lǎoshī shāngliang* — We should talk it over with the teacher.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*shāngliang* is built from *shāng* and *liáng*, both inherited from Middle Chinese, *syang* and *ljang* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first, among other senses, 'to deliberate', and the second 'to measure'. The second is said light here.
