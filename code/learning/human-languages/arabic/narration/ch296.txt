Arabic, chapter 296: The press.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
صحافة (ṣaḥāfa) — journalism; the press.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to recount, then the Arabic for one day.

You'll want to know: صحافة.
صحافة — ṣaḥāfa — "journalism; the press".
درس أخي الصحافة في الجامعة. darasa akhī aṣ-ṣaḥāfa fī al-jāmiʿa. My brother studied journalism at university.
A journalist is صحفي; the newspaper is صحيفة: one root.

The word, taken apart.
صحافة (ṣaḥāfa) is built on the native Arabic root ṣ-ḥ-f (Lane), a written leaf or page, the root of ṣaḥīfa. The modern sense, the press, comes from ṣaḥīfa, a newspaper.

Lesson 2 of 5.
محرر (muḥarrir) — an editor.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for one day, then the Arabic for journalism.

You'll want to know: محرر.
محرر — muḥarrir — "an editor".
يعمل خالي محررا في مجلة. yaʿmalu khālī muḥarriran fī majalla. My uncle works as an editor at a magazine.
The verbal noun تحرير is editing: the editorial office is قسم التحرير.

The word, taken apart.
محرر (muḥarrir) is built on the native Arabic root ḥ-r-r (Lane), "to be free", the root of ḥurr, free. Form II ḥarrara means "to set free" and "to write a text out clean"; muḥarrir is its participle.

Lesson 3 of 5.
مقال (maqāl) — an article, an essay.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for journalism, then the Arabic for an editor.

You'll want to know: مقال.
مقال — maqāl — "an article, an essay".
نشرت الصحيفة مقالا عن البحر. nasharat aṣ-ṣaḥīfa maqālan ʿan al-baḥr. The newspaper published an article about the sea.
Plural مقالات. مقالة is the same word.

The word, taken apart.
مقال (maqāl) is built on the native Arabic root q-w-l (Lane), "to say", the root of qāla. Maqāl is first "a saying"; in the modern press it is a written piece.

Lesson 4 of 5.
حرر (ḥarrara) — to edit; to set free.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for an editor, then the Arabic for an article.

You'll want to know: حرر.
حرر — ḥarrara — "to edit; to set free".
حررت أختي كتابا عن الطبخ. ḥarrarat ukhtī kitāban ʿan aṭ-ṭabkh. My sister edited a book about cooking.
Present: يحرر, yuḥarrir.
Of people and lands it means to free: حرر (ḥarrara) الجيش المدينة.

The word, taken apart.
حرر (ḥarrara) is built on the native Arabic root ḥ-r-r (Lane), "to be free". It is Form II, "to make free", and of a text "to write it out clean and correct".

Lesson 5 of 5.
نشرة (nashra) — a bulletin, a news broadcast.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for an article, then the Arabic for to edit.

You'll want to know: نشرة.
نشرة — nashra — "a bulletin, a news broadcast".
شاهد أبي نشرة (nashra) الأخبار في المساء. shāhada abī nashrat al-akhbār fī al-masāʾ. My father watched the news bulletin in the evening.
نشرة (nashra) الأخبار is the news on radio or television.

The word, taken apart.
نشرة (nashra) is built on the native Arabic root n-sh-r (Lane), "to spread out, to unfold", the root of the taught nashara, to publish. A nashra is what is spread among people.
