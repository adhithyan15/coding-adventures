# ಸೋಲಿಸು (sōlisu) — to defeat; to beat

## You'll want to know: ಸೋಲಿಸು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C538-PADAKA538-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸೋಲಿಸು** — *sōlisu* — "to defeat; to beat".

> ನಮ್ಮ ಶಾಲೆಯ ತಂಡ ನಗರದ ತಂಡವನ್ನು ಸೋಲಿಸಿತು.
>
> *namma śāleya taṇḍa nagarada taṇḍavannu sōlisitu.*
>
> Our school's team beat the city team.

Past (I …): ಸೋಲಿಸಿದೆ, *sōliside*.

ಸೋಲು is to lose; ಸೋಲಿಸು is to make someone lose.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸೋಲಿಸು** (*sōlisu*) is native Dravidian: the causative of *sōlu*, "to be defeated, to lose" (DEDR 3558), so "to make someone lose".
