## You'll want to know: দোষ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C343-DOSH343-01]; assesses=[] -->

**দোষ** — *dosh* — "a fault; blame".

> এটা তোমার দোষ নয়।
>
> *eṭā tomār dosh nôy.*
>
> It's not your fault.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**দোষ** (*dosh*) is Sanskrit *doṣa*, a fault or defect (Monier-Williams). It belongs to the one at fault: তোমার দোষ.
