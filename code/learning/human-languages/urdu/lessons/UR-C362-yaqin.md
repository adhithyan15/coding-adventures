## You'll want to know: یقین
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C362-QIYAS362-02]; assesses=[] -->

**یقین** — *yaqīn* — "certainty; belief (that something is so)".

> اگر آپ کو یقین نہیں تو خود دیکھ لیجیے۔
>
> *agar āp ko yaqīn nahīṅ to khud dekh lījiye.*
>
> If you don't believe it, see for yourself.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**یقین** (*yaqīn*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *yaqīn*, "certainty".
