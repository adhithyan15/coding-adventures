## You'll want to know: बहकाना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C366-VAJIB366-04]; assesses=[] -->

**बहकाना** — *bahkānā* — "to lead astray, to mislead".

> कुछ लोगों ने बच्चों को बहकाया।
>
> *kuch logoṁ ne baccoṁ ko bahkāyā.*
>
> Some people led the children astray.

Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने मुझे बहकाया, *usne mujhe bahkāyā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**बहकाना** (*bahkānā*) is the causative of बहकना, "to be misled", which CDIAL files under Sanskrit *vyathate*, "trembles, goes astray" (CDIAL 12164).
