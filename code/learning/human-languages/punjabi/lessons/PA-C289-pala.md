## You'll want to know: ਪਾਲਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C289-COLD289-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ਪਾਲਾ** — *pālā* — "bitter cold; a hard chill".

> ਮਾਘ ਵਿੱਚ ਬਹੁਤ ਪਾਲਾ ਪੈਂਦਾ ਹੈ।
>
> *māgh vich bahut pālā paindā hai.*
>
> In Magh it gets bitterly cold.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਪਾਲਾ** (*pālā*) is inherited from Sanskrit *prālēya*, "hail, frost, snow"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *pālā*, "frost, winter, cold, a chill", under it (CDIAL 8959).
