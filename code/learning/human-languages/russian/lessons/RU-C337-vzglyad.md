## You'll want to know: взгляд
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C337-TOCHKAZRENIYA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**взгляд** (*vzglyad*) — "a view, an opinion; a look".

> У нас разные взгляды на жизнь.
>
> *U nas ráznyye vzglyády na zhizn.*
>
> We have different views on life.

Masculine. *На мой взгляд* — in my view. It is also a glance: *быстрый взгляд*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**взгляд** is inherited from Proto-Slavic *\*vŭzględŭ* (EtymDB), built from the prefix *\*vŭz-* "up" and *\*ględěti* "to look", the verb that survives in *глядеть*. A view began as a look upwards.
