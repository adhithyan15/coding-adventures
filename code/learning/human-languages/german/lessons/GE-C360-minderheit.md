## You'll want to know: die Minderheit
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C360-VORURTEIL-03]; assesses=[] -->

**die Minderheit** — "the minority".

> In diesem Land gibt es mehrere sprachliche Minderheiten.
>
> *There are several language minorities in this country.*

Plural: *die Minderheiten*. The opposite is *die Mehrheit*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Minderheit* is built from *minder* "less", inherited from Middle High German *minder*, Old High German *minniro* and Proto-Germanic *minnizô* (EtymDB), a cousin of Latin *minor*, with *-heit*.
