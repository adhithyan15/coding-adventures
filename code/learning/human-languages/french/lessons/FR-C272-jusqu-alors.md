## You'll want to know: jusqu'alors
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C272-JADIS-04]; assesses=[] -->

**jusqu'alors** — "until then, up to then".

> Jusqu'alors, personne ne connaissait son secret.
>
> *Until then, nobody knew his secret.*

Looks back to a moment in the past; from today, say *jusqu'à maintenant*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*jusque* is built on Latin *ūsque* "all the way" (Lewis & Short) with *inde* (EtymDB) or, in another view, *dē*; *alors* joins *à* and *lors*, which EtymDB traces to Latin *illā hōrā* "at that hour".
