## You'll want to know: jiǎngjiu
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C340-CUSTOM340-02]; assesses=[] -->

*jiǎngjiu* — "to be particular about".

> *wǒ nǎinai hěn jiǎngjiu chī* — My grandma is very particular about food.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*jiǎngjiu* is built from *jiǎng* and *jiū*, both inherited from Middle Chinese, *kaewngX* and *kjuwH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to discuss' and the second 'to go to the end' and 'deep'.
