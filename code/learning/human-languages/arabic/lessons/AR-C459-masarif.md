## You'll want to know: مصاريف
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C459-MIZANIYYA459-03]; assesses=[] -->

**مصاريف** — *maṣārīf* — "expenses".

> كانت مصاريف الفندق كبيرة.
>
> *kānat maṣārīf al-funduq kabīra.*
>
> The hotel expenses were large.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**مصاريف** (*maṣārīf*) is built on the native Arabic root *ṣ-r-f*, "to turn away, to change" (Lane). Expenses are money turned out of the purse.
