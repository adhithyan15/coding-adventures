## You'll want to know: دستیاب
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C527-MASRUF527-03]; assesses=[] -->

**دستیاب** — *dastyāb* — "available".

> اگر ٹکٹ دستیاب ہوئے تو ہم کل جائیں گے۔
>
> *agar ṭikaṭ dastyāb hue to ham kal jāeṅ ge.*
>
> If tickets are available, we will go tomorrow.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**دستیاب** (*dastyāb*) is usually traced to Persian *dastyāb*, "come to hand", built from *dast*, "a hand", and *yāb-*, "to find".
