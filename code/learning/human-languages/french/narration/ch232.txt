7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
le magazine — a magazine.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for a destination, then the French for a landscape.

What you've built.

Guided Practice.
