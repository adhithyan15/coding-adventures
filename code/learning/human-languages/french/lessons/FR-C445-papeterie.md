## You'll want to know: la papeterie
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C445-BOUTIQUES-03]; assesses=[] -->

**la papeterie** — "the stationer’s".

> Avant la rentrée, la papeterie est pleine d'enfants.
>
> *Before school starts again, the stationer's is full of children.*

Pronounced *pa-pè-tri*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*papeterie* is built on *papier* 'paper' (EtymDB), which goes back to Latin *papyrus*, from Greek *pápyros*.
