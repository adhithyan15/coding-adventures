## You'll want to know: મોતી
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C507-BHARAT507-02]; assesses=[] -->

**મોતી** — *motī* — "a pearl".

> તેણે દોરામાં મોતી પરોવ્યાં.
>
> *teṇe dorāmā̃ motī parovyā̃.*
>
> She strung pearls on a thread.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**મોતી** (*motī*) is a tadbhava, inherited from Sanskrit *mauktika*, "a pearl", and CDIAL lists the Gujarati word (CDIAL 10365). It is neuter, and the plural looks the same: **મોતી પરોવ્યાં**.
