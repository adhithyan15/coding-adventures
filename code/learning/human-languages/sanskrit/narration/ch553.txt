Sanskrit, chapter 553: If Only.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
स्खलनम् (skhalanam) — a slip.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for makes use of, then the Sanskrit for helpful.

You'll want to know: स्खलनम्.
स्खलनम् — skhalanam — "a slip".
यदि स्खलनं न अभविष्यत्, तर्हि सः विजेता अभविष्यत्। yadi skhalanaṃ na abhaviṣyat, tarhi saḥ vijetā abhaviṣyat. If there had been no slip, he would have been the victor.
A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
स्खलनम् (skhalanam) comes from the root √skhal, "to stumble, to trip", with the ending -ana; MW gives skhalana as stumbling, tripping, faltering (MW).

Lesson 2 of 5.
हन्त (hanta) — come now; alas.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for helpful, then the Sanskrit for a slip.

You'll want to know: हन्त.
हन्त — hanta — "come now; alas".
हन्त, यदि अहं ह्यः अगमिष्यम्, तर्हि तम् अद्रक्ष्यम्। hanta, yadi ahaṃ hyaḥ agamiṣyam, tarhi tam adrakṣyam. Alas, if I had gone yesterday, I would have seen him.

The word, taken apart.
हन्त (hanta) is the particle MW gives for urging and for asking attention, "come, see", and in later use for grief, joy or pity; MW gives no derivation, so its origin is uncertain (MW).

Lesson 3 of 5.
परिरक्षति (parirakṣati) — guards well; rescues.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a slip, then the Sanskrit for come now.

You'll want to know: परिरक्षति.
परिरक्षति — parirakṣati — "guards well; rescues".
यदि रक्षकः न आगमिष्यत्, तर्हि कः बालं पर्यरक्षिष्यत्? yadi rakṣakaḥ na āgamiṣyat, tarhi kaḥ bālaṃ paryarakṣiṣyat? If the guard had not come, who would have saved the child?
Past (he …): सः बालं परिरक्षितवान्, saḥ bālaṃ parirakṣitavān.
Future: परिरक्षिष्यति, parirakṣiṣyati.

The word, taken apart.
परिरक्षति (parirakṣati) is built on pari, "all round", and the root √rakṣ, "to protect", which also gives rakṣati; MW gives parirakṣ as to guard well, to rescue, to save (MW).

Lesson 4 of 5.
विपर्ययः (viparyayaḥ) — a reversal; a mix-up.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for come now, then the Sanskrit for guards well.

You'll want to know: विपर्ययः.
विपर्ययः — viparyayaḥ — "a reversal; a mix-up".
यदि दिशायाः विपर्ययः न अभविष्यत्, तर्हि अहं समये आगमिष्यम्। yadi diśāyāḥ viparyayaḥ na abhaviṣyat, tarhi ahaṃ samaye āgamiṣyam. If I had not mixed up the direction, I would have come on time.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
विपर्ययः (viparyayaḥ) is built on vi, pari and the root √i, "to go": a going round the wrong way; MW gives viparyaya as reversed, inverted, contrary (MW).

Lesson 5 of 5.
उपालम्भः (upālambhaḥ) — a reproach.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for guards well, then the Sanskrit for a reversal.

You'll want to know: उपालम्भः.
उपालम्भः — upālambhaḥ — "a reproach".
यदि अहं सत्यम् अवदिष्यम्, तर्हि उपालम्भः न अभविष्यत्। yadi ahaṃ satyam avadiṣyam, tarhi upālambhaḥ na abhaviṣyat. If I had told the truth, there would have been no reproach.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
उपालम्भः (upālambhaḥ) is built on upa, ā and the root √labh, "to take hold of", which also gives labhate; MW lists upālambha under upālabh, "to censure, to reproach", and gives it as reproach, censure, finding fault (MW).
