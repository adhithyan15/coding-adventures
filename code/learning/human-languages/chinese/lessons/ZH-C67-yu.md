## You'll want to know: yú
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C67-ANIMALS67-05]; assesses=[] -->

*yú* — "fish".

> *wǒ xǐhuan chī yú* — I love eating fish.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*yú* is inherited from Middle Chinese *ngjo* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed, through an older book, as a water creature.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for everyday answers.
