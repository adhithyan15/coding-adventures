## You'll want to know: il nastro
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C426-BAGAGLI-03]; assesses=[] -->

**il nastro** — "the belt (for baggage); the ribbon".

> Aspettiamo le valigie al nastro sei.
>
> *We wait for the suitcases at belt six.*

*Il nastro trasportatore* — conveyor belt; also a ribbon for hair or gifts.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*nastro* was borrowed from Gothic *nastilō* 'strap, band' (EtymDB), a Germanic word.
