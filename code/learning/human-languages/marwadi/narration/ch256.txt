Marwadi (Marwari), chapter 256: Fear and Joy.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
डरणो (ḍarṇo) — to be afraid.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for a tear (from the eye), then the Marwadi for to tremble.

You'll want to know: डरणो.
डरणो — ḍarṇo — "to be afraid".
छोरी सांप सूं डरै है। chhorī sā̃p sū̃ ḍarai hai. The girl is afraid of snakes.
Past (he …): वो छोरो डर्यो, vo chhoro ḍaryo.

The word, taken apart.
डरणो (ḍarṇo) is inherited from Sanskrit darati, "is frightened"; CDIAL lists Marwari ḍarṇo under it (CDIAL 6190).

Lesson 2 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for to tremble, then the Marwadi for to be afraid.

You'll want to know: दुख.
दुख — dukh — "sorrow; grief".
गाय मरी, घर मांय दुख है। gāy marī, ghar mā̃y dukh hai. The cow died; there is sorrow in the house.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
दुख (dukh) is inherited from Sanskrit duḥkha, "difficulty, pain"; CDIAL lists Marwari dukh, "sorrow", under it (CDIAL 6375).

Lesson 3 of 5.
सुख (sukh) — ease and contentment; happiness.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for to be afraid, then the Marwadi for sorrow.

You'll want to know: सुख.
सुख — sukh — "ease and contentment; happiness".
गांव मांय सुख है। gā̃v mā̃y sukh hai. Life in the village is content.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
सुख (sukh) is a tatsama, the Sanskrit sukha, "ease, comfort, happiness", taken over with its final vowel dropped in speech (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 4 of 5.
आणंद (āṇand) — joy; delight.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for sorrow, then the Marwadi for contentment.

You'll want to know: आणंद.
आणंद — āṇand — "joy; delight".
ब्याव मांय घणो आणंद है। byāv mā̃y ghaṇo āṇand hai. There is great joy at the wedding.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
आणंद (āṇand) is inherited from Sanskrit ānanda, "happiness". CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry; it lists Gujarati āṇãd, "joy", with the same ण (CDIAL 1172).

Lesson 5 of 5.
उमंग (umaṅg) — high spirits; eagerness.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for contentment, then the Marwadi for joy.

You'll want to know: उमंग.
उमंग — umaṅg — "high spirits; eagerness".
आज मन मांय घणी उमंग है। āj man mā̃y ghaṇī umaṅg hai. Today my spirits are high.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
उमंग (umaṅg) is inherited from Sanskrit unmajjati, "emerges": a feeling that wells up. CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry; it lists Hindi umãg, "elation" (CDIAL 2110).
