## You'll want to know: durar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C272-DURAR-01]; assesses=[] -->

**durar** — "to last".

> A viagem durou cinco horas.
>
> *The journey lasted five hours.*

*Durar muito* — to last a long time; *durar pouco* — not to last long.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*durar* is inherited from Latin *dūrāre* "to harden, to hold out, to last" (Lewis & Short), built on *dūrus* "hard".
