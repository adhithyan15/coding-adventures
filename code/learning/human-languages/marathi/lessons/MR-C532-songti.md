## You'll want to know: सोंगटी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C532-BAITHE532-02]; assesses=[] -->

**सोंगटी** — *soṅgṭī* — "a game piece; a counter".

> बुद्धिबळाची एक सोंगटी हरवली.
>
> *buddhibaḷācī ek soṅgṭī haravlī.*
>
> One of the chess pieces got lost.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सोंगटी** (*soṅgṭī*) is the word for a piece in a board game; its origin is not settled.

## What you've built
