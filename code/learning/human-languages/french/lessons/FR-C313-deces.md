## You'll want to know: le décès
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C313-RETRAITE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**le décès** — "the death (formal)".

> Le décès a eu lieu à l'hôpital.
>
> *The death took place at the hospital.*

The formal and official word; in daily speech, *la mort*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*décès* is inherited from Latin *dēcessus* "a going away, a death" (EtymDB), from *dēcēdere* "to go away".
