## You'll want to know: bǎochí
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C209-RULE209-03]; assesses=[] -->

*bǎochí* — "to keep up; to keep as it is".

> *qǐng bǎochí ānjìng* — Please keep quiet.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*bǎochí* is built from *bǎo* 'to guard' and *chí* 'to hold', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *pawX* and *dri* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to keep safe' and the second 'to grasp'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for reading notices and messages.
