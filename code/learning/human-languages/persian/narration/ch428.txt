Persian, chapter 428: Other road users.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
عابر پیاده ('âber-e piyâde) — a pedestrian.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a tow truck, then the Persian for to tow.

You'll want to know: عابر پیاده.
عابر پیاده ('âber-e piyâde) — "a pedestrian".
راننده برای عابر پیاده ایستاد. Rânande barâ-ye 'âber-e piyâde istâd. The driver stopped for the pedestrian.
'âber 'passer-by' + piyâde 'on foot'. Plural 'âberân-e piyâde.

The word, taken apart.
'âber-e piyâde is built from 'âber 'one who passes', a loan usually traced to Arabic ʿābir 'crossing, passing', and native piyâde 'on foot', which EtymDB traces to Middle Persian payādag.

Lesson 2 of 5.
موتورسیکلت (motorsiklet) — a motorcycle, a motorbike.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to tow, then the Persian for a pedestrian.

You'll want to know: موتورسیکلت.
موتورسیکلت (motorsiklet) — "a motorcycle, a motorbike".
پسر همسایه با موتورسیکلت به مدرسه میرود. Pesar-e hamsâye bâ motorsiklet be madrese miravad. The neighbour's son goes to school on a motorbike.
In speech usually shortened to motor.

The word, taken apart.
motorsiklet was borrowed from a European language and is usually traced to French motocyclette or English motorcycle. EtymDB does not list the Persian word.

Lesson 4 of 5.
وانت (vânet) — a pickup (truck), a small van.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a motorcycle, then the Persian for a lorry.

You'll want to know: وانت.
وانت (vânet) — "a pickup (truck), a small van".
میوه فروش میوه را با وانت آورد. Mive-forush mive râ bâ vânet âvard. The fruit seller brought the fruit in a pickup.
Vans like this carry goods, furniture and fruit all over Iranian towns.

The word, taken apart.
vânet is a loan word whose exact source is not settled: it is usually linked with English van, a small goods vehicle. EtymDB does not list it.

Lesson 5 of 5.
زیر گرفتن (zir gereftan) — to run over, to knock down (with a vehicle).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a lorry, then the Persian for a pickup.

You'll want to know: زیر گرفتن.
زیر گرفتن (zir gereftan) — "to run over, to knock down (with a vehicle)".
راننده نزدیک بود یک گربه را زیر بگیرد. Rânande nazdik bud yek gorbe râ zir begirad. The driver nearly ran over a cat.
zir 'under' + gereftan 'to take'. Present stem gir-; nazdik bud + subjunctive — nearly.

The word, taken apart.
zir gereftan is built from native zir 'under', which EtymDB lists, and gereftan 'to take', usually traced to Old Persian grab- 'to seize'.
