## You'll want to know: вызывать
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C299-SIRENA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**вызывать** (*vyzyvát*) — "to call (for), to send for".

> Сосед вызвал скорую помощь.
>
> *Soséd výzval skóruyu pómoshch.*
>
> The neighbour called an ambulance.

Imperfective; perfective *вызвать* (*výzvat*), *вызову*. *Вызывать врача*, *такси*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*вызывать* is built on *вы-* "out" and *звать* "to call", an inherited Slavic verb: to call someone out. EtymDB lists no parent for the compound.
