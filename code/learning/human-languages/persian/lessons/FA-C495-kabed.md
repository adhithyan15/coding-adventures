## You'll want to know: کبد
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C495-ORGANS-03]; assesses=[] -->

**کبد** (*kabed*) — "the liver".

> دکتر گفت کبد او سالم است.
>
> *doktor goft kabed-e u sâlem ast.*
>
> The doctor said his liver is healthy.

Medical. Everyday Persian also says *jegar*, which is also the liver in kebab.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*kabed* is a loan, usually traced to Arabic *kabid*, 'liver'. EtymDB has no entry for it.
