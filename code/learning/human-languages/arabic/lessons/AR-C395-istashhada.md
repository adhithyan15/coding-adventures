## You'll want to know: استشهد
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C395-MARJI395-02]; assesses=[] -->

**استشهد** — *istashhada* — "to cite".

> استشهد الكاتب بكلام جده.
>
> *istashhada al-kātib bi-kalām jaddih.*
>
> The writer cited his grandfather's words.

Present: يستشهد, *yastashhid*.

It takes بـ: استشهد بـ …. In another sense it means to die as a martyr.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**استشهد** (*istashhada*) is built on the native Arabic root *sh-h-d*, "to witness, to be present" (Lane). It is Form X, to call for a witness: to cite as evidence.
