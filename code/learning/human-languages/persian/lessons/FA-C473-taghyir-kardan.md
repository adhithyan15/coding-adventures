## You'll want to know: تغییر کردن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C473-HAVASHENASI-04]; assesses=[] -->

**تغییر کردن** (*taghyir kardan*) — "to change".

> هوا ناگهان تغییر کرد.
>
> *Havâ nâgahân taghyir kard.*
>
> The weather suddenly changed.

Intransitive: the thing changes. *Taghyir dâdan* is to change something.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*taghyir kardan* is built from *taghyir* 'change', borrowed from Arabic *taġyīr* (EtymDB), and *kardan* 'to do', inherited from Middle Persian *kardan* (EtymDB).
