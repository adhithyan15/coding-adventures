Lesson 1 of 6.
el terapeuta — the therapist.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for exercise, then the Spanish for to chew.

You'll want to know: el terapeuta.
el terapeuta — "the therapist".
Mi terapeuta me escucha mucho. My therapist really listens to me.
Same form for men and women.

The word, taken apart.
terapeuta comes from Greek therapeutḗs "attendant" (EtymDB), from therapeúein "to serve, to treat".

Mi hermana es psicóloga. My sister is a psychologist.
The p is often silent in speech; sicólogo is also accepted.

The word, taken apart.
psicólogo is built on Greek psykhḗ "breath, soul, mind" and lógos "word, study": one who studies the mind.
