## You'll want to know: guān
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C65-SORRY65-05]; assesses=[] -->

*guān* — "to shut; to close; to switch off".

> *qǐng guān mén* — Please shut the door.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*guān* is inherited from Middle Chinese *kwaen* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which the dictionary explains, through an older book, as a wooden bar held across a door. Shutting is probably what the bar does, but the step is not given there.
