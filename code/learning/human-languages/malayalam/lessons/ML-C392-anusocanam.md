# അനുശോചനം (anuśōcanaṁ) — condolence

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ML-LEX-C391-SAVAM391-04, ML-LEX-C391-SAVAM391-05] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Malayalam for to cremate, then the Malayalam for to bury.

## You'll want to know: അനുശോചനം
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C392-ANUSOCHANAM392-01]; assesses=[] -->

**അനുശോചനം** — *anuśōcanaṁ* — "condolence".

> മാനേജർ കുടുംബത്തിന് അനുശോചനം അറിയിച്ചു.
>
> *mānējar kuṭumbattinŭ anuśōcanaṁ aṟiyiccu.*
>
> The manager conveyed his condolences to the family.

Condolences are conveyed: അനുശോചനം അറിയിക്കുക.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**അനുശോചനം** (*anuśōcanaṁ*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *anuśocana*, "sorrow, repentance" (Monier-Williams), from *anu-śuc*, "to grieve over".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
