# ADJ-STDLIB-COVERAGE: Kindergarten Through Medical Licensure

**Status:** Gap ledger and delivery contract.

**Structural inventory:**
[`data/adj-stdlib-coverage/COVERAGE.md`](data/adj-stdlib-coverage/COVERAGE.md),
generated by [`../scripts/adj_stdlib_report.py`](../scripts/adj_stdlib_report.py).

## 1. Goal and claim boundary

The goal is not to accumulate a large directory of plausible facts. The goal is
to let a model translate an input into typed ADJ, retrieve the required grounded
knowledge and operations, execute them deterministically, and return an answer
whose complete dependency graph can be re-checked from source bytes.

"Anything from kindergarten through a medical licensing exam" must be a
versioned, finite coverage claim. For a given release, it means:

1. every target learning or exam objective has a stable ID;
2. every objective is mapped to the facts, rules, formulas, programs, and
   prerequisites needed to exercise it;
3. every non-structural clause is pinned to source bytes in the CAS;
4. every objective has positive, negative, composition, and abstention tests;
5. held-out evaluation measures blind-model and framework-assisted behavior;
6. unsupported inputs abstain and identify the missing objective or clause.

A file count is not a coverage measure. A source URL is not byte provenance. A
green worked example is not competence. This document keeps those claims apart.

## 3. Measured baseline

The 2026-08-02 `origin/main` inventory contains:

This is a strong executable base. Every content library is named by a validation
test. Of 359 libraries, 356 have at least one complete
`source`/`locator`/`trust` envelope per grounded clause. Three flagged medical
libraries are incomplete: `anemia-edges.adj` has two relations without locators,
while `endocrine-edges.adj` and `iem-edges.adj` each have one. The corpus is not
yet fully byte-verifiable: none of the 359 content libraries has a
`quote ... at ... snapshot <sha256>` pin, so `adj-verify` cannot confirm the
source bytes from a content-addressed snapshot.

The content distribution is also skewed:

- 84 of 155 formula libraries are clinical calculators.
- Foundational mathematics has 10 arithmetic libraries and one geometry file.
- The fact library is broad but mostly shallow lookup tables and taxonomies.
- The language subject has five lookup libraries; it does not cover literacy.
- Medical recall has 634 direct relations, not an exam-blueprint knowledge graph.
- Synthetic ADJ ladder rungs prove engine capabilities but are not a substitute
  for stdlib curriculum coverage.

## 4. P0 gaps: provenance and control plane

These gaps block every defensible coverage claim, independent of subject.

### 4.1 Source bytes are not pinned

All shipped libraries have source labels, but zero have committed content hashes,
byte offsets, and resolvable CAS snapshots. The migration must:

1. fetch the source as bytes and store it by SHA-256;
2. record media type, retrieval time, canonical locator, and fetch receipt;
3. decompose the source into IR with byte spans and discarded-byte reasons;
4. recursively resolve cited dependencies until reaching an accepted root;
5. attach each ADJ clause to one or more exact byte spans;
6. make `adj-verify --snapshots` report `fully_verified: true`;
7. fail closed on unavailable, drifted, or mismatched bytes.

The CAS must deduplicate by content hash. A corrected fact produces a new
content-addressed artifact and replays all dependent proofs without changing a
model weight.

### 4.2 Machine-readable curriculum manifest: seed shipped, backfill open

`data/adj-stdlib-coverage/manifest.json` now provides the version-1 control
plane, with a JSON schema and `code/scripts/adj_stdlib_manifest.py` validator.
The seed declares all six coverage roots and maps the ten arithmetic libraries
to internal objectives. It records:

- objective ID and standards version;
- grade band, domain, and competency type;
- prerequisite objective IDs and imported libraries;
- supported question and answer shapes;
- modality: text, table, chart, image, audio, or time series;
- jurisdiction, population, context, and validity dates;
- evidence tier and source CAS hashes;
- implementation, provenance, test, and benchmark status.

The validator rejects duplicate IDs, missing or cyclic prerequisites, unsafe or
unknown library paths, unknown coverage roots, and status claims stronger than
the structural evidence. It deliberately reports the seed as `source_labeled`,
`unmapped`, and without held-out benchmarks. The external roots are `declared`,
not falsely presented as fetched or byte-pinned.

The remaining gap is large and explicit: 349 of 359 content libraries still
need reviewed objective mappings. The standards indexes must enter the CAS
before those entries can become a defensible standards crosswalk.

### 4.3 No semantic quality gates

The existing tests show that named examples execute. The standard library still
needs corpus-wide gates for:

- duplicate and contradictory canonical terms;
- incompatible units, reference ranges, populations, or guideline versions;
- orphaned prerequisites and import cycles;
- source retraction, expiry, and supersession;
- paraphrases that exceed the cited bytes;
- retrieval aliases and surface-form collisions;
- adversarial negatives and out-of-scope abstention.

## 5. Curriculum gaps

Status in this section means coverage in the importable stdlib, not capability
demonstrated by a synthetic ladder item.

### 5.1 Kindergarten and elementary

| Area | Present | Major gaps |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mathematics | Basic arithmetic formulas, number words, place value, shapes, money, time units | Cardinality and sets, comparison, number lines, operation properties, word-problem schemas, base-ten procedures, fractions/decimals, measurement, data displays, spatial composition |
| Literacy | Alphabet, vowels, opposites, Morse and Greek alphabets | Phonemic awareness, phonics, decoding, morphology, vocabulary in context, grammar, sentence meaning, comprehension, evidence use, writing, speaking/listening |
| Science | Many names, parts, classes, cycles, and simple physical categories | Observation and measurement, causal explanations, experiments, models, matter/energy systems, heredity, ecosystems, Earth processes, engineering design |
| Social knowledge | Coins, bills, calendar, traffic lights, continents | Civics, community roles, maps, history, economics, culture, safety, health, and source-aware comparison of perspectives |

The key defect is not just missing nouns. Most existing entries answer a direct
lookup. Grade-level competence requires procedures, relations, explanations,
comparison, composition, and evidence-backed claims.

### 5.2 Middle school

The stdlib does not yet provide complete objective families for rational-number
operations, expressions and equations, proportional reasoning, functions,
coordinate geometry, transformations, statistics, probability, sampling, or
data interpretation. Science has useful labels and a few laws but lacks coherent
mechanistic models, experimental design, uncertainty, graph interpretation, and
cross-domain cause-and-effect chains. Research literacy and argument from
multiple sources are absent as curriculum families.

### 5.3 High school and pre-medical foundations

The shipped stdlib does not demonstrate full coverage of:

- algebra, inequalities, functions, polynomials, exponentials, logarithms,
  trigonometry, analytic geometry, vectors, matrices, combinatorics, probability
  distributions, statistical inference, limits, derivatives, and integrals;
- chemical equations, stoichiometry, bonding models, thermodynamics, kinetics,
  equilibrium, acids/bases, electrochemistry, and introductory organic chemistry;
- mechanics, fluids, thermodynamics, waves, optics, electricity and magnetism,
  modern physics, experimental error, and model selection;
- molecular/cell biology, genetics, evolution, ecology, homeostasis, physiology,
  and interpretation of experimental data;
- history, civics, economics, computer science, information literacy, and arts as
  reasoned domains rather than isolated lookup tables.

The existing CAS packages are not yet wired through the ADJ language surface.
Until that lands, symbolic rearrangement, simplification, and calculus cannot be
ordinary importable stdlib operations with the same proof contract as formulas.

## 6. Medical licensing gaps

The current medical corpus is a valuable first slice: 84 calculators, 634 recall
relations, a 155-item structured board bank, and a fail-closed decomposition
path. It does not yet represent licensing-exam competence.

### 6.1 Content breadth

The recall graph is not crosswalked to all current organ systems, foundational
disciplines, and physician tasks. Thin or absent families include normal and
abnormal physiology, pathology mechanisms, clinical microbiology, pharmacologic
mechanisms and management, contraindications, interactions, adverse effects,
screening/prevention, nutrition, pregnancy, pediatrics, geriatrics, ethics,
communication, patient safety, systems practice, prognosis, and longitudinal
monitoring.

### 6.2 Reasoning and exam shape

The committed harness is dominated by typed recall. The remaining gaps are:

- raw vignette to complete, byte-accounted IR;
- four-or-more-option single-best-answer mapping and distractor comparison;
- derived facts feeding probabilistic differentials in the same query;
- multi-hop diagnosis, test selection, treatment, and next-best-step reasoning;
- conditional rules for age, pregnancy, renal/hepatic function, comorbidity,
  timing, severity, and prior treatment;
- guideline conflicts, version dates, jurisdiction, and patient preference;
- chart, table, image, pathology, radiology, ECG, audio, and video evidence;
- scientific abstracts, biostatistics, epidemiology, and literature appraisal;
- Step 3-style temporal simulation, orders, response to therapy, and monitoring.

### 6.3 Evaluation

A defensible medical result needs a source-disjoint held-out bank, an official
blueprint crosswalk, contamination checks, blind and framework arms, multiple
model families, calibration and abstention metrics, and a judge that receives
answers without arm-identifying artifacts. Every error must classify as input
decomposition, retrieval, missing knowledge, bad rule, engine execution,
option mapping, or judge/evaluation failure.

2. **Complete:** define the curriculum-manifest schema, arithmetic seed, and validator.
3. Import versioned Common Core, NGSS, and USMLE objective indexes into the CAS.
4. Backfill reviewed objective mappings for the remaining 349 libraries.
5. **Partial:** CI now gates manifest integrity, JSON Schema validity, validator
   tests, and test references. Complete the source-envelope and
   `--require-byte-pins` gates as those migrations reach zero debt.
6. [complete] Close the ten current worked-query gaps with executable consumers
   and a CLI integration test over their answers and provenance.
7. **Complete:** parse executable `import` statements when measuring worked
   queries; filename mentions in comments are not coverage evidence. The stricter
   audit exposed and closed two additional gaps (`arithmetic.adj`, `ratio.adj`).

### Wave 1: prove the full provenance path

0. **Complete:** computed answers are independently verifiable. `observe` inputs
   can carry byte pins, and `adj-verify` re-evaluates a separately stored compiler
   plan in its original fact/binding scope plus every formula/input quote before
   a formula-only query can become `fully_verified`.
1. **In progress:** migrate the ten arithmetic libraries first because every
   later layer imports them. The four-clause `arithmetic.adj` primitive root,
   the composed `ratio.adj` root, and both worked queries are now fully
   byte-verified end to end.
2. Store source bytes and fetch receipts in the CAS.
3. Add byte offsets and snapshot hashes to every grounded clause.
4. Recursively decompose source dependencies with discarded-byte accounting.
5. Require independent `adj-verify` success in tests.
6. **Complete:** preserve imported and nested formula pins as first-class
   transitive evidence. Display corroborations remain available, but
   `adj-verify` now checks every lossless formula-source envelope independently.
7. **Complete:** add first-class fetch-receipt objects and a deterministic
   fanout-CAS-to-flat snapshot projection instead of relying on receipt-shaped
   metadata. CI rehashes every object and checks bundle reachability, exact claim
   slices and quote hashes, deterministic text transforms, and complete
   represented/discarded byte partitions.
7a. **Complete:** add first-class repository-input receipts and require every
    provenance bundle clause to bind its decomposed ADJ source bytes as well as
    its external grounding bytes. Code is evidence and must not bypass IR.
7b. **Complete:** make per-root generators register owned bundle IDs in a
    validating transaction under an OS-released CAS-root lock shared by
    authoritative readers and every CLI writer. Registration is additive and
    idempotent, preserves unrelated roots, and rolls back newly materialized
    objects and index changes on failure.
7c. Add an explicit transactional root-replacement migration that validates the
    replacement graph and prunes only objects unreachable from every new root.
7d. Add a write-ahead recovery journal for process termination between the
    atomic CAS-index and manifest publications.
7e. **Complete:** scope native snapshot projection to a named bundle and its
    recursive dependency closure so adding an unrelated manifest root cannot
    perturb an already verified consumer.
8. Replace the dead MathWorld `PercentageChange` locator before migrating
   `percent.adj`; a source returning 404 cannot ground that clause.

### Wave 2: complete K-8 foundations

Build objective-complete math first, then literacy and science. Each tranche
must include composition and word-problem evaluation, not only direct recall.
Add curriculum routing so a decomposed question selects objectives and imports
their transitive prerequisites without domain-specific prompts.

### Wave 3: high school and pre-medical foundations

Wire the symbolic CAS, then fill algebra through statistics/calculus and the
mechanistic science graph. Add data, chart, experiment, and uncertainty IRs.
Use held-out mixed-domain tasks to ensure composition across math and science.

### Wave 4: medical blueprint completion

Crosswalk every USMLE system, discipline, and physician task. Fill Step 1
mechanisms before Step 2 diagnosis/management, then Step 3 longitudinal cases.
Prioritize multi-hop composition, contraindications, interactions, prevention,
ethics/safety, evidence interpretation, and temporal management over adding more
isolated calculators.

## 8. Definition of done for one objective

An objective is complete only when all of the following are committed:

- manifest entry with version, grade/exam band, prerequisites, and scope;
- source graph whose leaf bytes are available by content hash;
- IR decomposition with complete byte accounting and discard reasons;
- typed fact, rule, formula, table, constraint, or program library;
- worked query plus positive, negative, composition, and abstention tests;
- independent re-execution with `fully_verified: true`;
- held-out benchmark items that are source-disjoint from the library;
- failure localization that names the smallest editable CAS artifact;
- measured blind-versus-framework result with no arm leakage.

That contract makes mistakes repairable. A wrong clause can be traced to source
bytes, corrected in the CAS, and every dependent answer replayed. The system does
not need to pretend models stop making mistakes; it needs to make each mistake
locatable, correctable, and measurable.
