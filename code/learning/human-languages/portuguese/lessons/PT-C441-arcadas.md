## You'll want to know: as arcadas
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C441-PRACA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**as arcadas** — "the arcades".

> Debaixo das arcadas há cafés e lojas.
>
> *Under the arcades there are cafés and shops.*

Singular *a arcada* — a single arch or arcade.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*arcada* is built on *arco* 'arch', which is inherited from Latin *arcus* 'bow, arch' (EtymDB).
