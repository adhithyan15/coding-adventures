## You'll want to know: बैलेंस
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C426-RAQAM426-04]; assesses=[] -->

**बैलेंस** — *bailens* — "a balance (in an account)".

> बैंक में मेरा बैलेंस कम है।
>
> *baiṅk meṁ merā bailens kam hai.*
>
> My bank balance is low.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**बैलेंस** (*bailens*) is borrowed from English *balance*, the money left in an account; बैलेंस देखना, to check your balance.
