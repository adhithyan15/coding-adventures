## You'll want to know: il gesso
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C484-GESSO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**il gesso** — "plaster cast; chalk".

> Porto il gesso al braccio per un mese.
>
> *I am wearing a cast on my arm for a month.*

At school, *il gesso* is the chalk for the blackboard.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*gesso* is inherited from Latin *gypsum* "chalk, plaster" (EtymDB), from Greek *gýpsos*.
