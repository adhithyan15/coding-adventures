# おきて / いって (okite / itte) — one action, then the next

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[JA-GRAMMAR-C250-PAST-01, JA-GRAMMAR-C250-TA-SOUND-02, JA-LEX-C147-WASH147-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say, to a friend, "I got up" and "I went". (*okita*, *itta*.) Then say "I washed my face", politely. (*kao o araimashita*.)

## You'll want to know: た becomes て
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-GRAMMAR-C252-TE-01]; assesses=[JA-LEX-KAO, JA-LEX-C138-OKIMASU, JA-LEX-C172-MEALS172-04, JA-LEX-C138-NEMASU] -->

> **おきて、かおを あらいました。**
>
> *okite, kao o araimashita.* — "I got up and washed my face."

**おきて** (*okite*) is **おきた** (*okita*) with **え** in place of **あ**: **た** becomes **て**. A sentence can hold several actions this way, in the order they happened, and the last verb says when: **ました** puts all of them in the past.

> **あさ おきて、かおを あらいました。**
>
> *asa okite, kao o araimashita.* — "In the morning I got up and washed my face."

The other verbs that drop **る** do the same, and so do **する** and **くる**: **たべた** gives **たべて** (*tabete*), **した** gives **して** (*shite*), **きた** gives **きて** (*kite*).

> **ばんごはんを たべて、ねました。**
>
> *bangohan o tabete, nemashita.* — "I ate dinner and went to bed."

You know this form already from a request: **いって ください** (*itte kudasai*), "please say it". That **いって** comes from **言う**, "to say"; **いく** makes the same sound, as the next section shows.

## You'll want to know: って, んで, いて, して
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-GRAMMAR-C252-TE-02]; assesses=[JA-LEX-C152-READY152-03, JA-LEX-ANCHOR-KIPPU, JA-LEX-ANCHOR-EKI, JA-LEX-C26-FAMILY26-05] -->

The past by the last kana gives the **-て** form by the last kana, with the same swap: **だ** becomes **で**.

- **いった** → **いって** (*itte*), **かった** → **かって** (*katte*), **あらった** → **あらって** (*aratte*)
- **のんだ** → **のんで** (*nonde*), **あそんだ** → **あそんで** (*asonde*)
- **かいた** → **かいて** (*kaite*), **およいだ** → **およいで** (*oyoide*), **はなした** → **はなして** (*hanashite*)

> **かおを あらって、でかけました。**
>
> *kao o aratte, dekakemashita.* — "I washed my face and went out."

> **えきに いって、きっぷを かいました。**
>
> *eki ni itte, kippu o kaimashita.* — "I went to the station and bought a ticket."
