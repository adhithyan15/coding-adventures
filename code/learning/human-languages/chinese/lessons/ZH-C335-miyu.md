## You'll want to know: míyǔ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C335-LANTERN335-04]; assesses=[] -->

*míyǔ* — "riddle".

> *Yuánxiāo de shíhou wǒmen cāi míyǔ* — At the lantern festival we guess riddles.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*míyǔ* is built from *mí* and 语 'speech', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *mejH* and *ngjoX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'veiled words'.

## What you've built
