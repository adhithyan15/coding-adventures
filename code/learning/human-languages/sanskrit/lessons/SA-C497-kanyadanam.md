## You'll want to know: कन्यादानम्
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C497-KANYADANA497-01]; assesses=[] -->

**कन्यादानम्** — *kanyādānam* — "giving away the bride".

> पिता कन्यादानं करोति।
>
> *pitā kanyādānaṃ karoti.*
>
> The father gives away the bride.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**कन्यादानम्** (*kanyādānam*) is built from *kanyā*, "a girl, a daughter", and *dāna*, "a gift", from the root √dā of **ददाति**; MW gives "giving a girl in marriage" (MW).
