## You'll want to know: رانندگی کردن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C420-FARMAN-02]; assesses=[] -->

**رانندگی کردن** (*rânandegi kardan*) — "to drive (a car)".

> خواهرم هر روز تا دفتر رانندگی میکند.
>
> *Khâharam har ruz tâ daftar rânandegi mikonad.*
>
> My sister drives to the office every day.

*rânandegi* — driving, from *rânande* (a driver), + *kardan*. Present stem *kon-*: *rânandegi mikonam* — I drive.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*rânandegi kardan* is built from *rânandegi* 'driving', formed on the native verb *rândan* 'to drive', and *kardan* 'to do', which is inherited from Middle Persian *kardan* (EtymDB).
