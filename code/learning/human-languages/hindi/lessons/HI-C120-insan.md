## You'll want to know: इंसान
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C120-FORM-04]; assesses=[] -->

**इंसान** (*insān*) — "a human being, a person".

**अच्छा इंसान** — "a good person", with **अच्छा**. **आदमी** is a man; **इंसान** is anyone.

## What you've built
