## You'll want to know: نگرش
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C360-AQIDE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**نگرش** (*negaresh*) — "an attitude, an outlook".

> نگرش او به کار عوض شده است.
>
> *negaresh-e u be kâr 'avaz shode ast.*
>
> His attitude to work has changed.

*Negaresh be* — an attitude towards. Formal and written; in speech people often say *tarz-e fekr*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**نگرش** is built on *negar-*, the present stem of the native verb *negaristan* "to look" (EtymDB lists the verb), with the noun suffix *-esh*: an attitude is a way of looking.
