Lesson 1 of 5.
বেরোনো (berono) — to go out.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for to inform, then the Bengali for to chat.

You'll want to know: বেরোনো.
বেরোনো — berono — "to go out".

The word, taken apart.
The origin of বেরোনো (berono) is not settled: CDIAL has no entry for it, and a search by form and by the glosses "go out" and "come out" finds only words of other shapes, such as Bengali nikalā, "to come out" (CDIAL 7478).

Lesson 2 of 5.
ছড়ানো (chhôṛāno) — to scatter, to spread.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for to chat, then the Bengali for to go out.

You'll want to know: ছড়ানো.
ছড়ানো — chhôṛāno — "to scatter, to spread".

The word, taken apart.
ছড়ানো (chhôṛāno) is a tadbhava, a word inherited and reshaped by sound change. It comes from chaṭ, "scatter, sift", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 4965). CDIAL lists Bengali chaṛāna, "to scatter".

The word, taken apart.
গোটানো (goṭāno) appears to be built on Bengali goṭā, "whole, a seed", which CDIAL lists under gōṭṭa, "something round" (CDIAL 4271): "to make round".

The word, taken apart.
খোঁড়া (khõṛā) is a tadbhava, a word inherited and reshaped by sound change. It comes from khodd, "dig", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 3934). CDIAL lists Bengali khõṛā, "to dig", here and also under khuṭati, "breaks" (CDIAL 3892).
