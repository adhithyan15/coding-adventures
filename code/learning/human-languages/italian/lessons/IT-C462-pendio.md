## You'll want to know: il pendio
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C462-MONTAGNA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**il pendio** — "the slope, the hillside".

> Le case sono sul pendio della collina.
>
> *The houses are on the slope of the hill.*

*In pendio* — sloping.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*pendio* is built on *pendere* 'to lean, to slope', which is inherited from Latin *pendēre* 'to hang' (Lewis & Short).

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
