## You'll want to know: みず
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-ANCHOR-MIZU]; assesses=[JA-SCRIPT-MI-01] -->

**みず** — *mizu* — "water". It means cold or cool water: the water you
drink, or ask for in a restaurant. Hot water has a word of its own.

Two beats: *mi–zu*. **み** you already write. The second sign is **す** with
the voicing mark, so *su* becomes *zu*. The next lesson writes it.
