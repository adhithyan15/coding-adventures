## You'll want to know: انتقال دادن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C414-ARZ-05]; assesses=[] -->

**انتقال دادن** (*enteqâl dâdan*) — "to transfer, to move".

> پول را به حساب برادرم انتقال دادم.
>
> *pul râ be hesâb-e barâdaram enteqâl dâdam.*
>
> I transferred the money to my brother's account.

*Enteqâl* "transfer" + *dâdan*. Also to move a patient or an office.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**انتقال** comes from Arabic *intiqāl* "moving, passing" (EtymDB); with *dâdan* "to give" it means to move something on.
