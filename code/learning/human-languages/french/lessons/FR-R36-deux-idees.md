## Guided Practice: the distant band — every verb, given a reason
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[FR-LEX-PARCEQUE-01, FR-LEX-MAIS-03, FR-GRAM-SVO-01, FR-LEX-PARLER-01, FR-GRAM-ER-PRESENT-02, FR-LEX-TRAVAILLER-06, FR-ETYMON-TRAVAILLER-07, FR-LEX-HABITER-04, FR-ETYMON-HABITER-05, FR-LEX-AIMER-09, FR-ETYMON-AIMER-10, FR-LEX-DEMANDER-04, FR-ETYMON-DEMANDER-05, FR-LEX-MARCHER-07, FR-ETYMON-MARCHER-08, FR-LEX-COURIR-10, FR-ETYMON-COURIR-11, FR-LEX-LIRE-06, FR-ETYMON-LIRE-07, FR-LEX-ECRIRE-08, FR-ETYMON-ECRIRE-09, FR-LEX-ENTENDRE-01, FR-ETYMON-ENTENDRE-02, FR-LEX-DORMIR-04, FR-ETYMON-DORMIR-05, FR-LEX-PRENDRE-01, FR-ETYMON-PRENDRE-02, FR-LEX-COMPRENDRE-01, FR-ETYMON-COMPRENDRE-02, FR-LEX-VOULOIR-01, FR-LEX-POUVOIR-02, FR-LEX-DEVOIR-03, FR-GRAMMAR-MODAL-CHAIN-04, FR-LEX-FRANCAIS-09] -->

[PAUSE 4s] Every verb this book owns can now carry a reason. Say the sentence,
then say why.

- [YOU SAY: "Je parle français, parce que j'habite à Paris."]
- [YOU SAY: "Je travaille, mais je suis fatigué."]
- [YOU SAY: the verbs of the page — "Je lis et j'écris."]
- [YOU SAY: the verbs of the body — "Je marche ou je cours."]
- [YOU SAY: "J'entends, mais je ne comprends pas."]
- [YOU SAY: with the three modals — "Je veux dormir, parce que je suis fatigué."
  "Je peux prendre le café." "Je dois travailler."]
- [YOU SAY: "J'aime le café, mais je demande du thé."]

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[FR-GRAM-SVO-01, FR-LEX-ET-01, FR-LEX-OU-CONJ-02, FR-LEX-MAIS-03, FR-LEX-PARCEQUE-01] -->

[PAUSE 4s] Which joiner was Latin for *more*? (***Mais***, from *magis*.) Which
one has three words inside it? (***Parce que***.) Which one's *t* never makes a
liaison? (***Et***.) And which two are kept apart only by an accent? (***Ou***
and ***où***.)
