## You'll want to know: fù
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C114-PAY114-02]; assesses=[] -->

*fù* — "to pay".

> *wǒ fù qián* — I'll pay.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*fù* is inherited from Middle Chinese *pjuH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'to give'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for asking for what I want.
