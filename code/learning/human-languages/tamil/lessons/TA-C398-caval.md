## You'll want to know: சவால்
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C398-POKKU398-02]; assesses=[] -->

**சவால்** — *cavāl* — "a challenge".

> தண்ணீர் இல்லாவிட்டால் விவசாயம் ஒரு பெரிய சவால்.
>
> *taṇṇīr illāviṭṭāl vivacāyam oru periya cavāl.*
>
> Without water, farming is a big challenge.

சவால் விடு, to throw down a challenge.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**சவால்** (*cavāl*) is usually traced to Arabic *suʾāl*, "a question, a demand", which reached Tamil through Hindustani, where it also means a challenge.
