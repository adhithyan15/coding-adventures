# HL68 — Punjabi A1 pointing and place

## Status

Proposed for issue #17679 under the Punjabi bridge #13384 and the
Indian-language C2 programme #13295.

## Problem

Punjabi's partial A1 inventory reports five related pointing and location gaps.
Two are stale wiring: chapter 89 already introduces **ਇੱਥੇ** and **ਉੱਥੇ** as
exact knowledge atoms, but neither inventory point probes those atoms. Three are
genuine curriculum gaps: the learner has **ਉਹ** for distant reference but not
**ਇਹ** for near reference, has no taught **ਵਿੱਚ**, and has no explicitly taught
location-existential frame.

## Required learner experience

Add one chapter-addressable three-lesson sequence after chapter 175. It must:

- contrast **ਇਹ** with the already-taught **ਉਹ** without introducing a new
  interpretation of **ਉਹ**;
- teach **ਵਿੱਚ** as a postposition after a familiar place noun;
- combine a familiar noun, a location, and **ਹੈ** in a verb-final existence or
  presence statement;
- reuse **ਕਿੱਥੇ**, **ਇੱਥੇ**, **ਉੱਥੇ**, **ਘਰ**, **ਸਕੂਲ**, **ਕਿਤਾਬ**, and
  **ਦੁਕਾਨ** only through their existing atoms;
- keep every lesson at or below five minutes and move from recognition through
  substitution to a short practical location exchange; and
- introduce no idiom, sense, or culture claim.

## Inventory evidence

Wire the five named points only to exact introduced atoms:

- `PA-A1-DEM-01` requires the new proximal demonstrative plus the existing
  distant demonstrative;
- `PA-A1-DEM-02` and `PA-A1-ADV-05` reuse the chapter-89 here/there atoms;
- `PA-A1-CASE-04` requires the new `ਵਿੱਚ` atom; and
- `PA-A1-S-06` requires the new location-existential frame.

Coverage must move from 137/227 to 142/227, with unmapped points moving from 90
to 85. No unrelated point may be claimed by implication.

## Assessment boundary

This is a gentle curriculum tranche against a partial project-defined inventory.
It is not an A1 mock, readiness decision, attainment claim, calibration result,
independent review, or human validation.

## Ownership and verification

The specification, lessons, chapter, curriculum path, extension, memberships,
book target, generated book and narration owners, modality owners, session-map
rows, changelog fragment, and corpus test use Punjabi or direct generated shards.
The focused test must pin the five exact points, the three genuinely new atoms,
the chapter payoff, and the 142/227 boundary without adding a shared aggregate.
