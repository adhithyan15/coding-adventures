## You'll want to know: گرانی
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C413-EQTESAD-03]; assesses=[] -->

**گرانی** (*gerâni*) — "high prices, the cost of living".

> مردم از گرانی شکایت میکنند.
>
> *mardom az gerâni shekâyat mikonand.*
>
> People complain about high prices.

*Gerân* "expensive" + *-i*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**گرانی** is native Persian, built from *gerân* "heavy, expensive", which EtymDB traces to Middle Persian *grān*, with the noun suffix *-i*.
