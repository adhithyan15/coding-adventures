# ಪರಿಸ್ಥಿತಿ (paristhiti) — a state of affairs; a situation

## You'll want to know: ಪರಿಸ್ಥಿತಿ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C430-SANDARBHA430-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ಪರಿಸ್ಥಿತಿ** — *paristhiti* — "a state of affairs; a situation".

> ಪ್ರವಾಹದ ನಂತರ ಊರಿನ ಪರಿಸ್ಥಿತಿ ಕೆಟ್ಟದಾಗಿತ್ತು.
>
> *pravāhada nantara ūrina paristhiti keṭṭadāgittu.*
>
> After the flood the situation in town was bad.

ಪರಿ- (around) + ಸ್ಥಿತಿ (state).

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಪರಿಸ್ಥಿತಿ** (*paristhiti*) is built from Sanskrit *pari-*, "around", and *sthiti*, "standing, state" (Monier-Williams): the state of things around.
