## You'll want to know: लोभी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C469-UDAR469-04]; assesses=[] -->

**लोभी** — *lobhī* — "greedy; grasping".

> लोभी आदमी को कभी संतोष नहीं होता।
>
> *lobhī ādmī ko kabhī santoṣ nahīṃ hotā.*
>
> A greedy man is never content.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**लोभी** (*lobhī*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *lobhin*, "covetous, avaricious" (Monier-Williams), from *lobha*, "greed".
