## You'll want to know: उजेड
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C519-UJED519-03]; assesses=[] -->

**उजेड** — *ujeḍ* — "daylight; light".

> सकाळी खोलीत छान उजेड येतो.
>
> *sakāḷī kholīt chān ujeḍ yeto.*
>
> In the morning lovely light comes into the room.

Masculine.

उजेड येणे, light comes in; उजेड पडणे, light falls.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**उजेड** (*ujeḍ*) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Marathi *ujeḍ*, "light", under Sanskrit *uddyōta*, "flashing, light" (CDIAL 2000).
