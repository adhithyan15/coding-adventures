## You'll want to know: ひっぱる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C266-OBORE266-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ひっぱる** — *hipparu* — "to tug; to pull hard".

> **ちちが こどもの てを ひっぱりました。** — *chichi ga kodomo no te o hipparimashita* — "My father pulled the child by the hand."

Polite **ひっぱります**, *hipparimasu*; a godan verb. Past (polite): **あにが なわを ひっぱりました。**, *ani ga nawa o hipparimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ひっぱる** (*hipparu*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, built from *hik-*, "pulling" (from *hiku*), and *haru*, "to stretch" (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
