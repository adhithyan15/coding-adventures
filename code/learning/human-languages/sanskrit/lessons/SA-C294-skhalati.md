## You'll want to know: स्खलति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C294-SANGHATTA294-03]; assesses=[] -->

**स्खलति** — *skhalati* — "stumbles; slips".

> वृद्धः आर्द्रे मार्गे स्खलति।
>
> *vṛddhaḥ ārdre mārge skhalati.*
>
> The old man stumbles on the wet road.

Past (he …): सः मार्गे स्खलितवान्, *saḥ mārge skhalitavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**स्खलति** (*skhalati*) comes from the root √skhal, "to stumble, trip, totter" (MW).

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
