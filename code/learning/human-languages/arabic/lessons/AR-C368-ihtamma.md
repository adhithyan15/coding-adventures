## You'll want to know: اهتم
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C368-AHAMMIYYA368-05]; assesses=[] -->

**اهتم** — *ihtamma* — "to care about".

> تهتم أختي بالموسيقى.
>
> *tahtammu ukhtī bi-l-mūsīqā.*
>
> My sister cares about music.

Present: يهتم, *yahtamm*.

It takes بـ: اهتم بـ …. The noun اهتمام is interest.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**اهتم** (*ihtamma*) is built on the native Arabic root *h-m-m*, "to concern, to trouble" (Lane). It is Form VIII, to be concerned for: to care about.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
