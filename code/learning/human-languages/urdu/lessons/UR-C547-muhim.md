## You'll want to know: مہم
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C547-TASAVVUR547-03]; assesses=[] -->

**مہم** — *muhim* — "a campaign; a venture".

> اگر مہم ختم ہوئی تو سب گھر لوٹیں گے۔
>
> *agar muhim khatm huī to sab ghar lauṭeṅ ge.*
>
> If the campaign ends, everyone will go home.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**مہم** (*muhim*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *muhimm*, "an important matter"; the sense "a campaign" came later, and the sources we use do not show how it arose.
