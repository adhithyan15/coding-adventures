## You'll want to know: إرهاق
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C514-TAAB514-02]; assesses=[] -->

**إرهاق** — *irhāq* — "exhaustion".

> نام العامل من الإرهاق.
>
> *nāma al-ʿāmil min al-irhāq.*
>
> The worker fell asleep from exhaustion.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**إرهاق** (*irhāq*) is built on the native Arabic root *r-h-q*, "to come upon, to overburden" (Lane). Irhāq is the verbal noun of Form IV, to overburden.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
