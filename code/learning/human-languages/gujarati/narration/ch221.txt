Gujarati, chapter 221: Drought fire and earthquake.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
દુકાળ (dukāḷ) — drought, famine.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to recede, then the Gujarati for to clear up.

You'll want to know: દુકાળ.
દુકાળ — dukāḷ — "drought, famine".
આ વરસે દુકાળ પડશે. — ā varase dukāḷ paḍśe — "There will be a drought this year."
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
દુકાળ (dukāḷ) is a tadbhava. It comes from Sanskrit duṣkāla, "evil time", built from duṣ-, "bad", and kāla, "time"; CDIAL glosses the Gujarati word "famine" (CDIAL 6470).

You'll want to know: ખરવું.
ખરવું — kharvũ — "to fall off, to shed".
પાનખરમાં પાન ખરશે. — pānkharmā̃ pān kharśe — "Leaves will fall in autumn."

The word, taken apart.
ખરવું (kharvũ) is a tadbhava. It comes from Sanskrit kṣarati, "trickles; melts away", and CDIAL glosses the Gujarati verb "to fall, fade" (CDIAL 3663).

Lesson 3 of 5.
આગ (āg) — fire.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for drought, then the Gujarati for to fall off.

You'll want to know: આગ.
આગ — āg — "fire".
જંગલમાં આગ ફેલાશે. — jangalmā̃ āg phelāśe — "The fire will spread in the forest."
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
આગ (āg) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from agni, "fire" (CDIAL 55).

સળગવું — saḷagvũ — "to catch fire, to burn".
સૂકું ઘાસ તરત સળગશે. — sūkũ ghās tarat saḷagśe — "Dry grass will catch fire at once."

The word, taken apart.
સળગવું (saḷagvũ) is a tadbhava. CDIAL lists it, glossed "to take fire", under Sanskrit saṁlagna, "closely attached", so the fire is what takes hold (CDIAL 12999).

Lesson 5 of 5.
ભૂકંપ (bhūkamp) — earthquake.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for fire, then the Gujarati for to catch fire.

You'll want to know: ભૂકંપ.
ભૂકંપ — bhūkamp — "earthquake".
કદાચ અહીં ભૂકંપ આવશે. — kadāc ahī̃ bhūkamp āvśe — "Perhaps an earthquake will come here."
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ભૂકંપ (bhūkamp) is a learned loan, a tatsama, built from Sanskrit bhū, "earth" (CDIAL 9546), and kampa, "tremor" (CDIAL 2765), so the earth itself shakes.
