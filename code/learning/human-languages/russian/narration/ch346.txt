Russian, chapter 346: Pros and cons.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
преимущество (preimúshchestvo) — an advantage.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for reasonable, then the Russian for the truth.

You'll want to know: преимущество.
преимущество (preimúshchestvo) — "an advantage".
У этой квартиры есть одно преимущество: она рядом с метро. U étoy kvartíry yest odnó preimúshchestvo: oná ryádom s metró. This flat has one advantage: it is next to the metro.
Neuter. Преимущество перед + instrumental — an advantage over.

The word, taken apart.
преимущество (preimúshchestvo) is built from пре- "over, beyond" and имущий, the Church Slavonic participle of иметь "to have", from Proto-Slavic jĭměti (EtymDB): what you have over the others. The щ is the Church Slavonic shape where Russian has ч.

Lesson 2 of 5.
недостаток (nedostátok) — a drawback, a shortcoming; a lack.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for the truth, then the Russian for an advantage.

You'll want to know: недостаток.
недостаток (nedostátok) — "a drawback, a shortcoming; a lack".
Его главный недостаток — лень. Yegó glávnyy nedostátok — len. His main shortcoming is laziness.
Masculine; genitive недостатка, with the о dropped. Недостаток денег — a lack of money.

The word, taken apart.
недостаток (nedostátok) is не- "not" in front of достаток "sufficiency", from достать "to reach, to be enough", built on стать, the partner of стоять, Proto-Slavic stojati "to stand" (EtymDB).

Lesson 3 of 5.
польза (pólza) — benefit, use.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for an advantage, then the Russian for a drawback.

You'll want to know: польза.
польза (pólza) — "benefit, use".
От этого лекарства нет никакой пользы. Ot étogo lekárstva net nikakóy pólzy. This medicine does no good at all.
Feminine. В пользу + genitive — in favour of. Полезный (useful) is built on it.

The word, taken apart.
польза (pólza) was borrowed from Church Slavonic польза (pólza), from Proto-Slavic polĭdza, built from po "by, after" and lĭga "possibility", the root that also gives нельзя (EtymDB).

Lesson 4 of 5.
вред (vred) — harm.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a drawback, then the Russian for benefit.

You'll want to know: вред.
вред (vred) — "harm".
Курение приносит вред здоровью. Kuréniye prinósit vred zdoróvyu. Smoking does harm to your health.
Masculine. Без вреда для + genitive. Вредный (harmful) is built on it.

The word, taken apart.
вред (vred) was borrowed from Church Slavonic vrědŭ, from Proto-Slavic verdŭ (EtymDB). The -ре- is the Church Slavonic shape; the native Russian development of the same word survives in dialect веред "a boil".

Lesson 5 of 5.
оценивать (otsénivat) — to assess, to evaluate.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: оценивать.
оценивать (otsénivat) — "to assess, to evaluate".
Учитель оценивает работу каждого ученика. Uchítel otsénivayet rabótu kázhdogo ucheniká. The teacher assesses each pupil’s work.
Imperfective; perfective оценить. Built on цена, price: to put a price on.

The word, taken apart.
оценивать (otsénivat) is built from о- and цена "price", inherited from Proto-Slavic cěna; to assess a thing is to put a price on it.
