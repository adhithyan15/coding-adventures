## You'll want to know: pǎobù
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C56-PLAY56-04]; assesses=[] -->

*pǎobù* — "to run; to jog".

> *wǒ zǎoshang pǎobù* — I go for a run in the mornings.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*pǎobù* is built from pǎo 'to run' and bù 'step', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *baew* and *buH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription); the dictionary glosses *baew* as a foot pawing the ground.
