## You'll want to know: योधः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C318-SENA318-02]; assesses=[] -->

**योधः** — *yodhaḥ* — "a warrior".

> योधः हस्ते खड्गं धारयति।
>
> *yodhaḥ haste khaḍgaṃ dhārayati.*
>
> The warrior holds a sword in his hand.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**योधः** (*yodhaḥ*) comes from the root √yudh, "to fight"; MW gives *yodha* as a fighter, warrior, soldier (MW).
