## You'll want to know: yùsuàn
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C387-MONEY387-05]; assesses=[] -->

*yùsuàn* — "budget".

> *wǒmen de yùsuàn bú gòu suǒyǐ zuìhǎo mǎi èrshǒu de* — Our budget isn't enough, so we'd better buy second-hand.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*yùsuàn* is built from *yù* 'beforehand' and *suàn* 'to count', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *yoH* and *swanX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first, among other senses, 'before'.
