## You'll want to know: congregarse
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C560-ENCUENTRO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**congregarse** — "to gather, to assemble".

> Cientos de personas se congregaron en la plaza.
>
> *Hundreds of people gathered in the square.*

It takes *gu* before *e*: *se congreguen*. Common in the news.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*congregarse* is a learned borrowing from Latin *congregāre* 'to gather into a flock, to assemble' (Lewis & Short), built on *grex*, *gregis* 'a flock'.
