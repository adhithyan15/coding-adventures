## You'll want to know: शिखरम्
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C446-SHIKHARA446-02]; assesses=[] -->

**शिखरम्** — *śikharam* — "a summit; a peak".

> पर्वतस्य शिखरे हिमम् अस्ति।
>
> *parvatasya śikhare himam asti.*
>
> There is snow on the summit of the mountain.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**शिखरम्** (*śikharam*) is the word MW gives for pointed, crested and, as a noun, a point, a peak, a summit; MW gives no derivation, so its origin is uncertain (MW).
