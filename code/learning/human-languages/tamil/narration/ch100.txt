Tamil, chapter 100: Children, Husbands, Wives.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
குழந்தைகள் (kuḻantaikaḷ) — children.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for please forgive me, then the Tamil for don't worry.

You'll want to know: குழந்தைகள்.
குழந்தைகள் (kuḻantaikaḷ) — "children".
-கள் is the plural. உங்களுக்குக் குழந்தைகள் (kuḻantaikaḷ) இருக்கிறார்களா? — "do you have children?".

What you've built.
The question that follows a name.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: kuḻantaikaḷ]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: kuḻantaikaḷ, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: ask about children]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Tamil for please forgive me, then the Tamil for don't worry, then say kuḻantaikaḷ again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does குழந்தைகள் (kuḻantaikaḷ) mean? ("Children".) Say it once more.

Lesson 4 of 5.
மனைவி (maṉaivi) — a wife.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for a boy, then the Tamil for a husband.

You'll want to know: மனைவி.
மனைவி (maṉaivi) — "a wife".
It shares its start with மனை, "a house", from the hospital word: the wife is named from the home.

What you've built.
Husband and wife.

Lesson 5 of 5.
உறவினர் (uṟaviṉar) — a relative.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for a husband, then the Tamil for a wife.

You'll want to know: உறவினர்.
உறவினர் (uṟaviṉar) — "a relative".
உறவு is "a tie, a relationship"; a relative is one who has one with you.

What you've built.
Children, boy, husband, wife, relative.
