## You'll want to know: てんいん
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C183-STAFF183-02]; assesses=[] -->

**てんいん** — *ten'in* — "shop assistant; clerk".

> **あの てんいんは しんせつです。** — *ano ten'in wa shinsetsu desu* — "That shop assistant is kind."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**てんいん** (*ten'in*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *ten*, "shop" (Middle Chinese *temH*) and *in*, "member, staff" (*hjwen*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
