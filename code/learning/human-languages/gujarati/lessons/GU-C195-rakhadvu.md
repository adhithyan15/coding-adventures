## You'll want to know: રખડવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C195-LOST195-02]; assesses=[] -->

**રખડવું** — *rakhaḍvũ* — "to roam, to wander about".

> **તે બજારમાં રખડે છે.** — *te bajārmā̃ rakhaḍe che* — "He wanders about the market."

The origin of **રખડવું** (*rakhaḍvũ*) is not settled. CDIAL has no entry for it, and EtymDB lists no source.
