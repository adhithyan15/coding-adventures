## You'll want to know: bèn
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C470-FAULTS470-05]; assesses=[] -->

*bèn* — "slow-witted; clumsy".

> *wǒ gǎnjué zìjǐ hěn bèn* — I feel really stupid.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*bèn* is inherited from Middle Chinese *bwonX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed as the inside of a bamboo stem; the dictionary also records a fat man nicknamed with this word. The sense 'stupid' is not given there, so how it came is not settled.
