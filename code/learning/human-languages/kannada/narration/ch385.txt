Kannada, chapter 385: Causes and Effects.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಪ್ರಭಾವ (prabhāva) — influence.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for an aspect, then the Kannada for a feature.

You'll want to know: ಪ್ರಭಾವ.
ಪ್ರಭಾವ — prabhāva — "influence".
ಅವನ ಮೇಲೆ ಅಜ್ಜನ ಪ್ರಭಾವ ಹೆಚ್ಚು. avana mēle ajjana prabhāva heccu. His grandfather's influence on him is strong.
ಮೇಲೆ marks who is influenced.

The word, taken apart.
ಪ್ರಭಾವ (prabhāva) is borrowed from Sanskrit prabhāva, "might, power, majesty" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 2 of 5.
ಬಾಧಿಸು (bādhisu) — to affect badly.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a feature, then the Kannada for influence.

You'll want to know: ಬಾಧಿಸು.
ಬಾಧಿಸು — bādhisu — "to affect badly".
ಬೆಲೆ ಏರಿಕೆ ಬಡವರನ್ನು ಹೆಚ್ಚು ಬಾಧಿಸಿತು. bele ērike baḍavarannu heccu bādhisitu. The rise in prices hit the poor hardest.
Past (it …): ಬಾಧಿಸಿತು, bādhisitu.
Who is affected takes -ಅನ್ನು. ಬಾಧೆ, distress.

The word, taken apart.
ಬಾಧಿಸು (bādhisu) is built on Sanskrit bādhana, "oppressing, harassing" (Monier-Williams), with the Kannada verb ending -isu.

Lesson 3 of 5.
ಉಂಟಾಗು (uṇṭāgu) — to come about; to arise.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for influence, then the Kannada for to affect badly.

You'll want to know: ಉಂಟಾಗು.
ಉಂಟಾಗು — uṇṭāgu — "to come about; to arise".
ಮಳೆಯಿಂದ ರಸ್ತೆಯಲ್ಲಿ ತೊಂದರೆ ಉಂಟಾಯಿತು. maḷeyinda rasteyalli tondare uṇṭāyitu. The rain caused trouble on the road.
Past (it …): ಉಂಟಾಯಿತು, uṇṭāyitu.
ಉಂಟು, there is, + ಆಗು. ಉಂಟುಮಾಡು, to cause.

The word, taken apart.
ಉಂಟಾಗು (uṇṭāgu) is a native Dravidian compound: uṇṭu, "there is" (DEDR 697), and āgu, "to become" (DEDR 333).

ಯುವಕರಲ್ಲಿ ನಗರಕ್ಕೆ ಹೋಗುವ ಪ್ರವೃತ್ತಿ ಹೆಚ್ಚಾಗಿದೆ. yuvakaralli nagarakke hōguva pravṛtti heccāgide. Among young people the trend of moving to the city has grown.

ಪ್ರವೃತ್ತಿ (pravṛtti) is borrowed from Sanskrit pravṛtti, "coming forth, activity, a course of action" (Monier-Williams).
