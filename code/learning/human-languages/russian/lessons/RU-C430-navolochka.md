## You'll want to know: наволочка
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C430-POSTEL-03]; assesses=[] -->

**наволочка** (*návolochka*) — "a pillowcase".

> На подушке не было наволочки.
>
> *Na podúshke né bylo návolochki.*
>
> There was no pillowcase on the pillow.

Feminine; the stress is on the first syllable.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*наволочка* is built from *на-* 'onto' and the root of *волочь* 'to drag, to pull', which EtymDB takes from Proto-Slavic *velťi* 'to draw': a cover pulled on.
