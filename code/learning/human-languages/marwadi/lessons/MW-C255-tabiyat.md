## You'll want to know: तबियत
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C255-TABIYAT255-01]; assesses=[] -->

**तबियत** — *tabiyat* — "one's health; how one feels".

> आज म्हारी तबियत ठीक है।
>
> *āj mhārī tabiyat ṭhīk hai.*
>
> I am feeling well today.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**तबियत** (*tabiyat*) is usually traced to Persian *tabīat*, "nature, temperament"; in Marwari it is how one feels in body.
