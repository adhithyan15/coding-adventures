## You'll want to know: 要紧
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C481-SERIOUS481-01]; assesses=[] -->

**要紧** — *yàojǐn* — "urgent; a cause for concern".

> **有一点儿血不要紧** *yǒu yìdiǎnr xiě bú yàojǐn* — There's a little blood, but it's nothing serious.

The new character, in pieces: 紧 — ten strokes in three pieces: at the top two short verticals and 又 *yòu*; under them two short zigzag strokes and a dot; and at the foot 小 *xiǎo*, three strokes. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**要紧** (*yàojǐn*) is built from 要 'to need' and 紧 'tight', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *'jiewH* and *kjinX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to bind' and the second 'tightly bound'.
