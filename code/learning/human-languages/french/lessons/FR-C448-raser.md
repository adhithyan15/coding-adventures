## You'll want to know: raser
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C448-OPTICIEN-02]; assesses=[] -->

**raser** — "to shave (often se raser); to raze".

> Le coiffeur lui a rasé la barbe.
>
> *The barber shaved off his beard.*

*Se raser* — to shave; informal *ça me rase* — that bores me.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*raser* is inherited from Vulgar Latin *rāsāre*, from Latin *rāsus* 'scraped', the past participle of *rādere* 'to scrape' (EtymDB).
