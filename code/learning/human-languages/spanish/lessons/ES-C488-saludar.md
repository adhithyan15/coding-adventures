## You'll want to know: saludar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C488-GENTE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**saludar** — "to greet, to say hello".

> Saluda a tu madre de mi parte.
>
> *Say hello to your mother from me.*

The verb behind *el saludo*, the greeting.
