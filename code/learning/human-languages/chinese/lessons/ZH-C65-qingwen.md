## You'll want to know: 请问
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C65-SORRY65-02]; assesses=[] -->

**请问** — *qǐngwèn* — "excuse me, may I ask".

> **请问这是你的手机吗** *qǐngwèn zhè shì nǐ de shǒujī ma* — Excuse me, is this your phone?

The new character, in pieces: 问 — 门 with 口 inside it. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**请问** (*qǐngwèn*) is built from 请 'please' and 问 'to ask', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *tshjengX* and *mjunH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription); the dictionary already glosses the first as 'to beg, to ask'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for asking politely.
