## You'll want to know: विचारयति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C366-NISHKARSHA366-04]; assesses=[] -->

**विचारयति** — *vicārayati* — "considers; deliberates".

> न्यायाधीशः सर्वं विचारयति।
>
> *nyāyādhīśaḥ sarvaṃ vicārayati.*
>
> The judge considers everything.

Past (he …): सः प्रश्नं विचारितवान्, *saḥ praśnaṃ vicāritavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**विचारयति** (*vicārayati*) is built on *vi* and the root √car, "to move", in its causative form; MW gives *vi-cārita* "deliberated, considered, discussed, judged" (MW).
