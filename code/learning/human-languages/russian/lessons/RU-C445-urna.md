## You'll want to know: урна
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C445-FONAR-03]; assesses=[] -->

**урна** (*úrna*) — "a litter bin; an urn".

> Он бросил старую газету в урну.
>
> *On brósil stáruyu gazétu v úrnu.*
>
> He threw the old newspaper into the bin.

Feminine. *Урна для голосования* — a ballot box.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*урна* was borrowed from Latin *urna* 'a vessel, a jar' (EtymDB).
