Arabic, chapter 329: Revolts and independence.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ثورة (thawra) — a revolution, a revolt.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a banquet, then the Arabic for an invited guest.

You'll want to know: ثورة.
ثورة — thawra — "a revolution, a revolt".
غيرت الثورة حياة الناس. ghayyarat ath-thawra ḥayāt an-nās. The revolution changed people’s lives.

The word, taken apart.
ثورة (thawra) is built on the native Arabic root th-w-r (Lane), "to rise up, to be stirred", said of dust; the taught thawr, a bull, is the same root.

What you've built.
One more word for telling what happened.

Lesson 3 of 5.
استقل (istaqalla) — to become independent; to board.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a revolution, then the Arabic for to revolt.

You'll want to know: استقل.
استقل — istaqalla — "to become independent; to board".
استقل البلد بعد حرب طويلة. istaqalla al-balad baʿda ḥarb ṭawīla. The country became independent after a long war.
Present: يستقل, yastaqill.
It also takes a vehicle: استقل (istaqalla) القطار, he took the train.

The word, taken apart.
استقل (istaqalla) is built on the native Arabic root q-l-l (Lane), "to be little; to lift", the root of qalīl, little, and of the taught aqall, less. Form X istaqalla is "to bear oneself up", alone.

يحتفل البلد بعيد الاستقلال كل سنة. yaḥtafilu al-balad bi-ʿīd al-istiqlāl kull sana. The country celebrates Independence Day every year.

استقلال (istiqlāl) is built on the native Arabic root q-l-l (Lane), "to lift". Istiqlāl is the verbal noun of Form X istaqalla, "to stand on one's own".

Lesson 5 of 5.
شعب (shaʿb) — a country’s people; a nation.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to become independent, then the Arabic for independence.

You'll want to know: شعب.
شعب — shaʿb — "a country’s people; a nation".
شعب هذا البلد كريم جدا. shaʿb hādhā al-balad karīm jiddan. The people of this country are very generous.
Plural: شعوب, shuʿūb.
The adjective شعبي is popular, folk: أكلة شعبية.

The word, taken apart.
شعب (shaʿb) is built on the native Arabic root sh-ʿ-b (Lane), which means both "to branch apart" and "to gather"; Lane gives shaʿb as a great tribe from which smaller ones branch.
