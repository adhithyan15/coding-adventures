## You'll want to know: die Aussage
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C283-ZEUGE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**die Aussage** — "the statement, the testimony".

> Seine Aussage war sehr genau.
>
> *His statement was very precise.*

Plural: *die Aussagen*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Aussage* is built on *aussagen* "to testify", from *sagen* "to say", an inherited Germanic verb (EtymDB).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.
