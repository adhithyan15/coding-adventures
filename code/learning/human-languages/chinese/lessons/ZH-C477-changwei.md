## You'll want to know: chángwèi
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C477-INSIDE477-04]; assesses=[] -->

*chángwèi* — "stomach and gut; digestion".

> *wǒ chángwèi bù shūfu le liǎng tiān* — My stomach has been upset for two days.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*chángwèi* is built from *cháng* 'the gut' and *wèi* 'the stomach', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *drjang* and *hjw+jH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first in this very pair and the second, after an older book, as the storehouse of grain.
