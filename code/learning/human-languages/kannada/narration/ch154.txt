Kannada, chapter 154: Nobody, Never, Nowhere, Anyone, Anything.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಯಾರೂ (yārū) — nobody (with a negative verb).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to pull, then the Kannada for to cry.

You'll want to know: ಯಾರೂ.
ಯಾರೂ — yārū — "nobody (with a negative verb)". Kannada negates a verb by joining ಇಲ್ಲ (illa) onto it: ಯಾರೂ ಬರಲಿಲ್ಲ — yārū baralilla — "Nobody came." ಇಲ್ಲ also means "is not there"; to say something is not a thing, it uses ಅಲ್ಲ (alla): ಇದು ನನ್ನ ಮನೆ ಅಲ್ಲ — idu nanna mane alla — "This is not my house."

Lesson 4 of 5.
ಯಾರಾದರೂ (yārādarū) — anyone, someone.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for never, then the Kannada for nowhere.

You'll want to know: ಯಾರಾದರೂ.
ಯಾರಾದರೂ — yārādarū — "anyone, someone". A yes-or-no question changes the last vowel of the sentence's final word to -ಆ (-ā): ಇದ್ದಾರೆ becomes ಯಾರಾದರೂ ಇದ್ದಾರಾ? — yārādarū iddārā? — "Is anyone there?"
