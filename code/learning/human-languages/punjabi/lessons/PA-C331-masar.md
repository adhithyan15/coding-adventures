## You'll want to know: ਮਾਸੜ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C331-UNCLE331-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ਮਾਸੜ** — *māsaṛ* — "a mother's sister's husband".

> ਮੇਰਾ ਮਾਸੜ ਇੱਕ ਦਰਜ਼ੀ ਹੈ।
>
> *merā māsaṛ ikk darzī hai.*
>
> My mother's sister's husband is a tailor.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਮਾਸੜ** (*māsaṛ*) is inherited from Sanskrit *mātuḥṣvasṛ*, "a mother's sister"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *māsaṛ*, "mother's sister's husband", under it (CDIAL 10001).
