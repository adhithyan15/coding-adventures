## You'll want to know: rě
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C539-BLAME539-01]; assesses=[] -->

*rě* — "to stir up trouble; to provoke".

> *nǐ búyào rě tā bùrán tā huì shēngqì* — Don't provoke him, or he'll get angry.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*rě* is inherited from Middle Chinese *nyaeX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'a troubled mind'; the dictionary also lists *nyak*. The sense 'to provoke' is not given there.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what would happen.
