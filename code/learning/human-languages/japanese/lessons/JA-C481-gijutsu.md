## You'll want to know: ぎじゅつ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C481-SKILL481-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ぎじゅつ** — *gijutsu* — "technology; technique".

> **にほんの ぎじゅつは すごいです。** — *nihon no gijutsu wa sugoi desu* — "Japanese technology is amazing."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ぎじゅつ** (*gijutsu*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *gi*, "skill" (Middle Chinese *gjeX*) and *jutsu*, "art" (*zywit*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
