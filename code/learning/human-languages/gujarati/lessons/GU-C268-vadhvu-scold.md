## You'll want to know: વઢવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C268-QUARREL268-02]; assesses=[] -->

**વઢવું** — *vaḍhvũ* — "to scold".

> **મારી માતા મને નથી વઢતી.** — *mārī mātā mane nathī vaḍhtī* — "My mother does not scold me."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

The origin of **વઢવું** (*vaḍhvũ*) is not settled. CDIAL lists no Gujarati verb of this form with the meaning "to scold", and our other sources do not trace it.
