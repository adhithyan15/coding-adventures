## You'll want to know: दैवम्
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C369-BHAGYA369-02]; assesses=[] -->

**दैवम्** — *daivam* — "fate".

> यदि दैवम् इच्छति, तर्हि सर्वं भविष्यति।
>
> *yadi daivam icchati, tarhi sarvaṃ bhaviṣyati.*
>
> If fate wills it, everything will happen.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**दैवम्** (*daivam*) is built on *deva*, "a god"; MW gives *daiva* "divine power or will, destiny, fate, chance" (MW).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
