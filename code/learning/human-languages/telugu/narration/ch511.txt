Telugu, chapter 511: Memory and Muddle.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
తెలివితేటలు (telivitēṭalu) — wits; intelligence.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for self-confidence, then the Telugu for curiosity.

You'll want to know: తెలివితేటలు.
తెలివితేటలు — telivitēṭalu — "wits; intelligence".
ఆమె తెలివితేటలతో సమస్యను పరిష్కరించింది. āme telivitēṭalatō samasyanu pariṣkariñcindi. She solved the problem with her wits.
Plural in form. తెలివి is understanding.

The word, taken apart.
తెలివితేటలు (telivitēṭalu) is native Dravidian: DEDR 3433 lists Telugu telivi, "understanding", from teliyu, "to be known, to be clear"; tēṭalu goes with it as a pair word.

Lesson 2 of 5.
తికమకపడు (tikamakapaḍu) — to be confused; to be muddled.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for curiosity, then the Telugu for wits.

You'll want to know: తికమకపడు.
తికమకపడు — tikamakapaḍu — "to be confused; to be muddled".
కొత్త నగరంలో దారి తెలియక నేను తికమకపడ్డాను. kotta nagaraṁlō dāri teliyaka nēnu tikamakapaḍḍānu. Not knowing the way in the new city, I got muddled.
Past (I …): తికమకపడ్డాను, tikamakapaḍḍānu.
తికమక, a muddle: తికమకగా ఉంది, it is confusing.

The word, taken apart.
తికమకపడు (tikamakapaḍu) is native Dravidian: DEDR 3207 lists Telugu tikamaka, "intricacy, confusion", and tikamakalāḍu, "to be puzzled", with Tamil tikai, "to be perplexed"; the native verb paḍu, "to feel, to suffer" (DEDR 3853) completes the verb.

Lesson 3 of 5.
తలచుకో (talacukō) — to think back on; to recall fondly.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for wits, then the Telugu for to be confused.

You'll want to know: తలచుకో.
తలచుకో — talacukō — "to think back on; to recall fondly".
చిన్నప్పటి రోజులను తలచుకుని అమ్మమ్మ నవ్వింది. cinnappaṭi rōjulanu talacukuni ammamma navvindi. Thinking back on her childhood, grandma smiled.
Past (I …): తలచుకున్నాను, talacukunnānu.
Often -ని తలచుకుని, thinking of: fondly, or with regret.

The word, taken apart.
తలచుకో (talacukō) is built on తలచు, "to think of", with the reflexive -కో; the origin of తలచు is not settled.

Lesson 4 of 5.
నెమరువేయు (nemaruvēyu) — to go over again; to chew the cud.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to be confused, then the Telugu for to think back on.

You'll want to know: నెమరువేయు.
నెమరువేయు — nemaruvēyu — "to go over again; to chew the cud".
ఆ సంతోషకరమైన రోజును నేను మళ్ళీ మళ్ళీ నెమరువేశాను. ā santōṣakaramaina rōjunu nēnu maḷḷī maḷḷī nemaruvēśānu. I went over that happy day again and again.
Past (I …): నెమరువేశాను, nemaruvēśānu.
First said of a cow chewing the cud, then of turning a memory over and over.

The word, taken apart.
నెమరువేయు (nemaruvēyu) is native Dravidian: DEDR 3595 lists Telugu nemaru, "rumination, chewing the cud", beside namalu, "to chew"; the verb vēyu, "to put", makes it an action.

Lesson 5 of 5.
గుర్తుచేయు (gurtucēyu) — to remind.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to think back on, then the Telugu for to go over again.

You'll want to know: గుర్తుచేయు.
గుర్తుచేయు — gurtucēyu — "to remind".
పుట్టినరోజు సంగతి చెల్లెలు నాకు గుర్తుచేసింది. puṭṭinarōju saṅgati cellelu nāku gurtucēsindi. My younger sister reminded me about the birthday.
Past (I …): గుర్తుచేశాను, gurtucēśānu.
Who is reminded takes -కి: నాకు గుర్తుచేసింది. గుర్తు is a mark or a memory.

The word, taken apart.
గుర్తుచేయు (gurtucēyu) is native Dravidian: DEDR 1847 lists Telugu guṟutu, "a mark, a sign", and gurt(u) uṇḍu, "to be remembered", with Tamil kuṟi; cēyu, "to do", makes the verb.
