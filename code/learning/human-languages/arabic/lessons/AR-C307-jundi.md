## You'll want to know: جندي
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C307-JAYSH307-02]; assesses=[] -->

**جندي** — *jundī* — "a soldier".

> كان جدي جنديا في شبابه.
>
> *kāna jaddī jundiyyan fī shabābihi.*
>
> My grandfather was a soldier in his youth.

Plural: جنود, *junūd*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**جندي** (*jundī*) is built on *jund*, "troops, a host", filed under the root *j-n-d* (Lane), with the nisba ending *-ī*: "one of the troops".
