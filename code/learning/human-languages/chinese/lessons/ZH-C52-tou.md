## You'll want to know: tóu
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C52-BODY52-02]; assesses=[] -->

*tóu* — "head".

> *tā de tóu hěn dà* — He's got a big head.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*tóu* is inherited from Middle Chinese *duw* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'head'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying how you are.
