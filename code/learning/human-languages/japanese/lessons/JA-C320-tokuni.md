## You'll want to know: とくに
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C320-TACHIBA320-05]; assesses=[] -->

**とくに** — *tokuni* — "especially; in particular".

> **なつは とくに あついから、みずを のんで ください。** — *natsu wa tokuni atsui kara, mizu o nonde kudasai* — "Summer is especially hot, so please drink water."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**とくに** (*tokuni*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): one Chinese character read with its on'yomi, reading borrowed from Chinese. It is *toku*, "special" (Middle Chinese *dok*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription). The native particle *ni* after it makes the adverb (JMdict).
