Tamil, chapter 431: Drying Up.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
வற்று (vaṟṟu) — to dry up; to ebb.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for to glitter, then the Tamil for to sound.

You'll want to know: வற்று.
வற்று — vaṟṟu — "to dry up; to ebb".
கோடையில் ஏரி வற்றியது. kōṭaiyil ēri vaṟṟiyatu. In summer the lake dried up.
Past (it …): வற்றியது, vaṟṟiyatu.
Of a river or a well; வறட்சி, a drought, is a close cousin.

The word, taken apart.
வற்று (vaṟṟu) is native Dravidian (DEDR 5320), "to grow dry, to evaporate, to ebb".

Lesson 4 of 6.
கருகு (karuku) — to get scorched; to be charred.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for to become dry, then the Tamil for to evaporate.

You'll want to know: கருகு.
கருகு — karuku — "to get scorched; to be charred".
கடும் வெயிலில் செடிகள் கருகின. kaṭum veyilil ceṭikaḷ karukiṉa. The plants were scorched in the fierce sun.
Past (it …): கருகியது, karukiyatu.
Of food: சாதம் கருகியது, the rice got burnt.

The word, taken apart.
கருகு (karuku) is native Dravidian (DEDR 1278), "to be scorched, blackened by fire or sun", the family of கரு, "black".
