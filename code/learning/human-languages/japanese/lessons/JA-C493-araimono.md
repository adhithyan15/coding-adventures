## You'll want to know: あらいもの
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C493-FREEZE493-04]; assesses=[] -->

**あらいもの** — *araimono* — "the washing-up; things to be washed".

> **ばんごはんの あとで あらいものを します。** — *bangohan no ato de araimono o shimasu* — "After dinner I do the washing-up."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**あらいもの** (*araimono*) is a native Japanese compound (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese: built from *arai*, "washing", from *arau*, "to wash", and *mono*, "thing" (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
