Tamil, chapter 331: Decrees and Patrons.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
அரசாணை (aracāṇai) — a royal decree.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for a royal tribute, then the Tamil for to flourish.

You'll want to know: அரசாணை.
அரசாணை — aracāṇai — "a royal decree".
மன்னர் ஒரு புதிய அரசாணையை வெளியிட்டார். maṉṉar oru putiya aracāṇaiyai veḷiyiṭṭār. The king issued a new royal decree.
Today also a government order.

The word, taken apart.
அரசாணை (aracāṇai) joins அரசு, "king, government", which DEDR 201 traces to Sanskrit rājan, with ஆணை, "order, command", whose origin is not settled in the sources at hand.

Lesson 2 of 5.
செப்பேடு (ceppēṭu) — a copper-plate grant.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for to flourish, then the Tamil for a royal decree.

You'll want to know: செப்பேடு.
செப்பேடு — ceppēṭu — "a copper-plate grant".
அருங்காட்சியகத்தில் பழைய செப்பேடுகள் இருக்கின்றன. aruṅkāṭciyakattil paḻaiya ceppēṭukaḷ irukkiṉṟaṉa. There are old copper-plate grants in the museum.
Plural: செப்பேடுகள், ceppēṭukaḷ.
செம்பு, copper + ஏடு, leaf: records cut on copper sheets.

The word, taken apart.
செப்பேடு (ceppēṭu) is a Tamil compound built from செம்பு, "copper" (DEDR 2775), and ஏடு, "a strip of palmyra leaf for writing, a book" (DEDR 884).

Lesson 3 of 5.
வள்ளல் (vaḷḷal) — a patron; a generous giver.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for a royal decree, then the Tamil for a copper-plate grant.

You'll want to know: வள்ளல்.
வள்ளல் — vaḷḷal — "a patron; a generous giver".
அந்த வள்ளல் புலவர்களுக்கு நிறையப் பரிசுகள் கொடுத்தார். anta vaḷḷal pulavarkaḷukku niṟaiyap paricukaḷ koṭuttār. That patron gave many gifts to poets.
A famous figure in old Tamil poems.

The word, taken apart.
வள்ளல் (vaḷḷal) is native Dravidian (DEDR 5304): a person of boundless liberality, from வள், "abundance".

Lesson 4 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for a copper-plate grant, then the Tamil for a patron.

You'll want to know: கொடை.
கொடை — koṭai — "a donation; a gift".
இந்தப் பள்ளி ஒரு வணிகரின் கொடை. intap paḷḷi oru vaṇikariṉ koṭai. This school is a merchant's donation.
From கொடு, to give.

The word, taken apart.
கொடை (koṭai) is native Dravidian: DEDR 2053 gives it under கொடு, "to give", as "giving away as a gift".

Lesson 5 of 5.
செழி (ceḻi) — to prosper; to thrive.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for a patron, then the Tamil for a donation.

You'll want to know: செழி.
செழி — ceḻi — "to prosper; to thrive".
ஆற்றின் கரையில் நகரங்கள் செழித்தன. āṟṟiṉ karaiyil nakaraṅkaḷ ceḻittaṉa. Towns prospered on the banks of the river.
Past (it …): செழித்தது, ceḻittatu.
Of plants too: to grow well.

The word, taken apart.
செழி (ceḻi) is native Dravidian (DEDR 2789): to thrive, flourish, prosper, as a crop or a kingdom.
