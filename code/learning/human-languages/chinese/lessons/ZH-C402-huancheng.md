## You'll want to know: huànchéng
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C402-STATION402-04]; assesses=[] -->

*huànchéng* — "to change trains or lines".

> *wǒmen zài zhèr huànchéng* — We change trains here.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*huànchéng* is built from *huàn* 'to exchange' and *chéng* 'to ride', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *hwanH* and *zying* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to exchange'.
