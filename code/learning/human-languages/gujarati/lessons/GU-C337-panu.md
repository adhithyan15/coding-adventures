## You'll want to know: પાનું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C337-PANU337-01]; assesses=[] -->

**પાનું** — *pānũ* — "a page".

> મેં પુસ્તકનાં સો પાનાં વાંચ્યાં.
>
> *mẽ pustaknā̃ so pānā̃ vā̃cyā̃.*
>
> I read a hundred pages of the book.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**પાનું** (*pānũ*) goes back to Sanskrit *parṇá*, "a leaf", where CDIAL lists Gujarati *pān*, a leaf, and *pānũ* (CDIAL 7918): a book's leaf is its page. **પાન** is still a leaf on a tree.
