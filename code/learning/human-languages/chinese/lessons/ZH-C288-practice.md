## The exchange
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-PERFORMANCE-IF-01]; assesses=[ZH-GRAMMAR-YAOSHI-JIU-01, ZH-LEX-C288-YAOSHI-01, ZH-LEX-C288-DEHUA-01, ZH-LEX-C288-JIU-01, ZH-GRAMMAR-KAN-DEPENDS-01, ZH-GRAMMAR-HUI-FUTURE-01, ZH-LEX-C66-WEATHER66-03, ZH-LEX-NE-01, ZH-LEX-C83-WAIT83-03] -->

> **A:** 明天你去山上吗
>
> *míngtiān nǐ qù shān shàng ma* — Are you going up the mountain tomorrow?
>
> **B:** 要看天气
>
> *yào kàn tiānqì* — It depends on the weather.
>
> **A:** *tiānqì yùbào shuō kěnéng huì xiàyǔ* — The forecast says it might rain.
>
> **B:** 要是下雨 我就不去了
>
> *yàoshi xiàyǔ, wǒ jiù bú qù le* — If it rains, I won't go.
>
> **A:** 不下雨的话呢
>
> *bú xiàyǔ dehuà ne* — And if it doesn't?
>
> **B:** 不下雨的话 我就去
>
> *bú xiàyǔ dehuà, wǒ jiù qù* — If it doesn't, I'll go.
>
> **A:** 要是你有时间的话 就来我家
>
> *yàoshi nǐ yǒu shíjiān dehuà, jiù lái wǒ jiā* — If you've got time, come round.
>
> **B:** 好 我马上就来
>
> *hǎo, wǒ mǎshàng jiù lái* — All right, I'll be right over.

要是 opens one condition, 的话 closes the next, and 就 opens each result. 要看天气 answers the question before it is settled.

## What you've built — if, and what it depends on
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ZH-PERFORMANCE-IF-01, ZH-SCRIPT-JIU-01, ZH-LEX-C288-JIU-01, ZH-LEX-C288-YAOSHI-01, ZH-GRAMMAR-YAOSHI-JIU-01, ZH-LEX-C288-DEHUA-01, ZH-GRAMMAR-KAN-DEPENDS-01] -->

Three ways to put a condition, and one to say it is not settled:

- **要是下雨 我就不去了** *yàoshi xiàyǔ, wǒ jiù bú qù le* — 要是 at the front.
- **下雨的话 我们就不去了** *xiàyǔ dehuà, wǒmen jiù bú qù le* — 的话 at the back.
- **要是你有时间的话** *yàoshi nǐ yǒu shíjiān dehuà* — both.
- **要看天气** *yào kàn tiānqì* — it depends.

**Listen.** *Yàoshi míngtiān hěn lěng, nǐ qù ma?*

**Speak.** Say two things you will do this weekend, each depending on something.

[YOU WRITE: 就 — the opening of 文, 口 and 小, then the right side]
