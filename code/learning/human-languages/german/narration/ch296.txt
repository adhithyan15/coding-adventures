German, chapter 296: Die Geburt.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
die Geburt — the birth.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to damage, then the German for to destroy.

You'll want to know: die Geburt.
die Geburt — "the birth".
Bei der Geburt wog das Baby drei Kilo. At birth the baby weighed three kilos.
Plural: die Geburten. Geburtstag — birthday — is built on it.

The word, taken apart.
Geburt is inherited from Old High German giburt, from Proto-Germanic gaburþiz, built on beraną "to carry, to bear" (EtymDB).

Lesson 4 of 5.
taufen — to baptise, to christen.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for pregnant, then the German for the pregnancy.

You'll want to know: taufen.
taufen — "to baptise, to christen".
Das Kind wurde in der alten Kirche getauft. The child was baptised in the old church.
Also to name a ship: ein Schiff taufen.

The word, taken apart.
taufen is inherited from Old High German toufen, from Proto-Germanic daupijaną "to dip", from a root meaning "deep" (EtymDB): baptism by dipping.
