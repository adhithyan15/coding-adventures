# ഭരണം (bharaṇaṁ) — administration; rule

## You'll want to know: ഭരണം
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C421-BHARANAM421-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ഭരണം** — *bharaṇaṁ* — "administration; rule".

> പുതിയ ഭരണം ജനങ്ങൾക്ക് ഇഷ്ടമായി.
>
> *putiya bharaṇaṁ janaṅṅaḷkkŭ iṣṭamāyi.*
>
> The people liked the new administration.

Under a rule: -ന്റെ ഭരണത്തിൽ.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ഭരണം** (*bharaṇaṁ*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *bharaṇa*, "bearing, maintaining" (CDIAL 9395; Monier-Williams), from the root *bhṛ*, "to bear": to rule is to bear the load of a land.
