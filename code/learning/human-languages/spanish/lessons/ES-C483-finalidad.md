## You'll want to know: la finalidad
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C483-VIDA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**la finalidad** — "the purpose, the aim".

> La finalidad del curso es hablar más.
>
> *The aim of the class is to speak more.*

A formal word, common on forms and notices.
