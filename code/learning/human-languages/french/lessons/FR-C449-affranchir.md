## You'll want to know: affranchir
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C449-BURALISTE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**affranchir** — "to put a stamp on, to frank; to free".

> Elle a affranchi la lettre au tarif normal.
>
> *She put a normal-rate stamp on the letter.*

*L'affranchissement* — the postage. First 'to set free'.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*affranchir* is built from *a-* and *franc* 'free', the word behind *franchir* (EtymDB links the two); *franc* comes from the name of the Franks: a letter with its stamp travels free.
