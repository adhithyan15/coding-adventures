## You'll want to know: smacchiare
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C456-CURA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**smacchiare** — "to remove stains from".

> In lavanderia possono smacchiare la giacca.
>
> *At the dry cleaner's they can get the stains out of the jacket.*

*La macchia* — a stain.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*smacchiare* is built from *s-* and *macchia* 'stain', which is inherited from Latin *macula* 'spot' (Lewis & Short).
