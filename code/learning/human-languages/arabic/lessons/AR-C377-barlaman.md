## You'll want to know: برلمان
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C377-HUKUMA377-04]; assesses=[] -->

**برلمان** — *barlamān* — "a parliament".

> ناقش البرلمان خطة الحكومة.
>
> *nāqasha al-barlamān khiṭṭat al-ḥukūma.*
>
> Parliament discussed the government's plan.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**برلمان** (*barlamān*) was borrowed from French *parlement* (EtymDB), a place of speaking. Arabic has no p, so the word begins with b.
