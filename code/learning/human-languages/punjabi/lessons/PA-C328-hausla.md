## You'll want to know: ਹੌਸਲਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C328-CARE328-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ਹੌਸਲਾ** — *hauslā* — "courage".

> ਮਾਤਾ ਜੀ ਮਰੀਜ਼ ਨੂੰ ਹੌਸਲਾ ਦਿੰਦੇ ਹਨ।
>
> *mātā jī marīz nū̃ hauslā dinde han.*
>
> Mother keeps the patient's spirits up.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਹੌਸਲਾ** (*hauslā*) is a loan, usually traced through Persian to Arabic *ḥawṣala*, "capacity".
