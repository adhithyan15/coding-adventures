## You'll want to know: మజిలీ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C456-SHIKARU456-05]; assesses=[] -->

**మజిలీ** — *majilī* — "a stopover; a halt".

> రెండు రోజుల ప్రయాణంలో మొదటి మజిలీ ఒక చిన్న పట్టణం.
>
> *reṇḍu rōjula prayāṇaṁlō modaṭi majilī oka cinna paṭṭaṇaṁ.*
>
> On the two-day journey the first stopover was a small town.

A stop on a long journey. మజిలీ కథలు are stories told at each halt of a journey.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**మజిలీ** (*majilī*) is usually traced to Arabic *manzil*, "a place where travellers halt", which came into Telugu through Persian and Hindustani.
