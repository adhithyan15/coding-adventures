## You'll want to know: o estendal
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C529-ROUPA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**o estendal** — "clothes horse; washing line".

> O estendal está na varanda.
>
> *The clothes horse is on the balcony.*

European Portuguese; in Brazil, *o varal*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*estendal* is built on *estender* "to hang out, to stretch", inherited from Latin *extendere* (EtymDB).

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
