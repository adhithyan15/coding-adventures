## You'll want to know: सावधान
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C191-NOTICE191-04]; assesses=[] -->

**सावधान** — *sāvdhān* — "careful".

> सड़क माथै सावधान रैवो।
>
> *saṛak māthai sāvdhān raivo.*
>
> Take care on the road.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सावधान** (*sāvdhān*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *sāvadhāna*, "attentive, heedful" (Monier-Williams).
