## You'll want to know: der Feigling
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C477-MUT-03]; assesses=[] -->

**der Feigling** — "coward".

> Im Film ist der Held am Anfang ein Feigling.
>
> *In the film the hero is a coward at first.*

Plural: *die Feiglinge*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Feigling* is built on *feige* "cowardly" with *-ling*; *feige* is inherited from Old High German *feigi*, from Proto-Germanic *faigijaz* (EtymDB).
