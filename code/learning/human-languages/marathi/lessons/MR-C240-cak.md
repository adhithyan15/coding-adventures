## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**चाक** (*cāk*) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from *cakra*, "wheel" (CDIAL 4538).
