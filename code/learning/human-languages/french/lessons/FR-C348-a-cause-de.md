## You'll want to know: à cause de
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C348-GRACE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**à cause de** — "because of".

> Nous sommes restés à la maison à cause de la neige.
>
> *We stayed at home because of the snow.*

Followed by a noun; with a verb, use *parce que*. *À cause du*, *à cause des*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*à cause de* is built on *cause*, which comes from Latin *causa* "cause, reason, lawsuit" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short).
