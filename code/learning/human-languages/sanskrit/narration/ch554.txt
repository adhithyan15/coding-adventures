Sanskrit, chapter 554: Looking Ahead.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
सम्भवति (sambhavati) — is possible; comes about.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a reversal, then the Sanskrit for a reproach.

You'll want to know: सम्भवति.
सम्भवति — sambhavati — "is possible; comes about".
यदि श्वः वर्षा सम्भवति, तर्हि छत्रं नयतु। yadi śvaḥ varṣā sambhavati, tarhi chatraṃ nayatu. If rain is possible tomorrow, take an umbrella.
Past (it …): उत्सवः सम्भूतवान्, utsavaḥ sambhūtavān.

The word, taken apart.
सम्भवति (sambhavati) is built on sam, "together", and the root √bhū, "to be, to become", which also gives bhavati; MW gives sambhū as to come together, to arise, to happen, and to be possible; its past participle is sambhūta (MW).

What you've built.

[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: दीर्घदर्शी.
दीर्घदर्शी — dīrghadarśī — "far-sighted".
यदि नायकः दीर्घदर्शी अस्ति, तर्हि देशः सुरक्षितः भवति। yadi nāyakaḥ dīrghadarśī asti, tarhi deśaḥ surakṣitaḥ bhavati. If the leader is far-sighted, the country is safe.

The word, taken apart.
दीर्घदर्शी (dīrghadarśī) is built from dīrgha, "long, far", and darśin, "seeing", from the root √dṛś, "to see"; MW gives dīrghadarśin as the same as dīrghadarśa, far-sighted (MW).

Lesson 3 of 5.
अभिप्रैति (abhipraiti) — intends.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for is possible, then the Sanskrit for far-sighted.

You'll want to know: अभिप्रैति.
अभिप्रैति — abhipraiti — "intends".
यदि भवान् गन्तुम् अभिप्रैति, तर्हि अहम् अपि आगच्छामि। yadi bhavān gantum abhipraiti, tarhi aham api āgacchāmi. If you intend to go, I will come too.
Past (he …): सः गन्तुम् अभिप्रेतवान्, saḥ gantum abhipretavān.

The word, taken apart.
अभिप्रैति (abhipraiti) is built on abhi, "towards", pra, "forth", and the root √i, "to go"; MW gives abhipre as to approach in mind, to aim at, to intend; its past participle is abhipreta (MW).

Lesson 4 of 5.
आयतिः (āyatiḥ) — the time ahead.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for far-sighted, then the Sanskrit for intends.

You'll want to know: आयतिः.
आयतिः — āyatiḥ — "the time ahead".
यदि आयतिः शुभा अस्ति, तर्हि वयं प्रसन्नाः स्मः। yadi āyatiḥ śubhā asti, tarhi vayaṃ prasannāḥ smaḥ. If the future looks bright, we are glad.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
आयतिः (āyatiḥ) is built on ā and the root √yam, "to hold, to stretch": a stretching out; MW gives āyati as extension, and following or future time (MW).
