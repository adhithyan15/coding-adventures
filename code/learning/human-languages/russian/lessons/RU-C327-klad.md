## You'll want to know: клад
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C327-KLAD-05]; assesses=[] -->

**клад** (*klad*) — "a buried treasure, a hoard".

> В саду старого дома нашли клад.
>
> *V sadú stárovo dóma nashlí klad.*
>
> A hoard was found in the garden of an old house.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*клад* is built on the root of *класть* "to put, to lay", which EtymDB traces to PIE *kleh₂-* "to put, to lay out": what was laid away.
