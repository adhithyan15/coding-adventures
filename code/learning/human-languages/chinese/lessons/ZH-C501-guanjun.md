## You'll want to know: guànjūn
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C501-WINNERS501-02]; assesses=[] -->

*guànjūn* — "champion".

> *tā dāng guo liǎng cì guànjūn* — He's been champion twice.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*guànjūn* is built from *guàn* 'to put on a cap' and *jūn* 'an army', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *kwanH* and *kjun* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to tie on the cap' and the second 'a body of troops'. How the pair came to mean 'champion' is not settled.
