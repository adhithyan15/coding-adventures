## You'll want to know: चोरी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C243-CHOR243-01]; assesses=[] -->

**चोरी** — *chorī* — "a theft".

> चोरी माड़ी बात है।
>
> *chorī māṛī bāt hai.*
>
> Stealing is a bad thing.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**चोरी** (*chorī*) is inherited from Sanskrit *caurikā*, "theft"; CDIAL lists Marwari *corī* under it (CDIAL 4937).
