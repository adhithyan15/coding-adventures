## You'll want to know: دقت
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C368-HAL368-01]; assesses=[] -->

**دقت** — *diqqat* — "a difficulty; a hassle".

> اگر کوئی دقت ہے تو مجھے بتائیے۔
>
> *agar koī diqqat hai to mujhe batāiye.*
>
> If there is any difficulty, tell me.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**دقت** (*diqqat*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *diqqa*, "fineness, exactness"; Urdu uses it for a difficulty or a hassle, a sense our sources do not trace.
