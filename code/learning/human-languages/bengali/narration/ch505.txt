Bengali, chapter 505: Light and Dark.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
অন্ধকার (ôndhôkār) — darkness; dark.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for to rust, then the Bengali for a motor.

You'll want to know: অন্ধকার.
অন্ধকার — ôndhôkār — "darkness; dark".
রাতে গলিটা অন্ধকার। Rāte gôliṭā ôndhôkār. The lane is dark at night.

The word, taken apart.
অন্ধকার (ôndhôkār) is a tatsama, Sanskrit andhakāra, "darkness" (Monier-Williams), built on andha, "blind".

Lesson 4 of 5.
টিমটিম করা (ṭimṭim kôrā) — to flicker; to glimmer.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for bright, then the Bengali for a chandelier.

You'll want to know: টিমটিম করা.
টিমটিম করা — ṭimṭim kôrā — "to flicker; to glimmer".
ঘরে একটা মোমবাতি টিমটিম করছিল। Ghôre ekṭā mombāti ṭimṭim kôrchhilô. A candle was flickering in the room.
Past (he/she …): সেই বাতি টিমটিম করল, sei bāti ṭimṭim kôrlô.

The word, taken apart.
টিমটিম করা (ṭimṭim kôrā) joins টিমটিম, an imitative word for a weak, wavering light, to করা, "to do". No dictionary we use records an older form, so its history is not settled.

Lesson 5 of 5.
নেভা (nebhā) — to go out (of a fire); to go out (of a light).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a chandelier, then the Bengali for to flicker.

You'll want to know: নেভা.
নেভা — nebhā — "to go out (of a fire); to go out (of a light)".
হঠাৎ বাতাসে মোমবাতিটা নিভল। Hôṭhāt bātāse mombātiṭā nibhlô. Suddenly the candle went out in the wind.
Past (he/she …): সেই আগুন নিভল, sei āgun nibhlô.
The stem vowel rises in the past: নিভল, it went out.

The word, taken apart.
নেভা (nebhā) is the plain partner of নেভানো, "to put out", from Sanskrit nirvāyati, "is extinguished" (CDIAL 7399).
