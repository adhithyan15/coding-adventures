Hindi, chapter 212: Ugly, Strong, Weak, Cheap, Hard.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
भद्दा (bhaddā) — ugly, clumsy.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for coriander, then the Hindi for a lemon.

You'll want to know: भद्दा.
भद्दा — bhaddā — "ugly, clumsy".

The word, taken apart.
भद्दा (bhaddā) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from bhadra, "fortunate, delightful" (CDIAL 9377). Its sense turned round: CDIAL glosses the Hindi word "stupid, clumsy".

Lesson 2 of 5.
मज़बूत (mazbūt) — strong, sturdy.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a lemon, then the Hindi for ugly.

You'll want to know: मज़बूत.
मज़बूत — mazbūt — "strong, sturdy".

The word, taken apart.
मज़बूत (mazbūt) is a loan from Arabic maḍbūṭ, "held firm, secured", which reached Hindi through Persian. Its Arabic root is ḍ-b-ṭ (Lane).

Lesson 3 of 5.
कमज़ोर (kamzor) — weak.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for ugly, then the Hindi for strong.

You'll want to know: कमज़ोर.
कमज़ोर — kamzor — "weak".

The word, taken apart.
कमज़ोर (kamzor) is a loan, usually traced to Persian kamzōr, "weak". It is built from kam, "little", and zōr, "strength".

Lesson 5 of 5.
कठिन (kaṭhin) — hard, difficult.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for weak, then the Hindi for cheap.

You'll want to know: कठिन.
कठिन — kaṭhin — "hard, difficult".

The word, taken apart.
कठिन (kaṭhin) is Sanskrit kaṭhina, "hard, stiff", taken over whole as a learned loan, a tatsama (CDIAL 2650). CDIAL reports that this group of words has been traced to Dravidian.
