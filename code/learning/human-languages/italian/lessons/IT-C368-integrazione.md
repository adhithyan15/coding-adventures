## You'll want to know: l'integrazione
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C368-CONVIVENZA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**l'integrazione** — "the integration".

> La scuola aiuta l'integrazione dei bambini stranieri.
>
> *School helps the integration of foreign children.*

*Integrarsi* — to fit in.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*integrazione* is a learned borrowing from Latin *integrātiō* "a renewal" (Lewis & Short), from *integrāre* "to make whole", built on *integer* "whole, untouched".
