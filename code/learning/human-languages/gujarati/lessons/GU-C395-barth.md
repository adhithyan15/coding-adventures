## You'll want to know: બર્થ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C395-BERTH395-01]; assesses=[] -->

**બર્થ** — *barth* — "a berth (on a train)".

> મારી બર્થ બારી પાસે છે.
>
> *mārī barth bārī pāse che.*
>
> My berth is by the window.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**બર્થ** (*barth*) is borrowed from English *berth*, the bed on a night train.
