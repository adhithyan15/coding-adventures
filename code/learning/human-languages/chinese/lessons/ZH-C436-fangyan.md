## You'll want to know: fāngyán
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C436-SOUTH436-04]; assesses=[] -->

*fāngyán* — "dialect".

> *zhèr de rén shuō fāngyán wǒ tīng bu dǒng* — People here speak a dialect I can't understand.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*fāngyán* is built from 方 'a region' and *yán* 'speech', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *pjang* and *ngjon* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'speech'. The Guangyun itself quotes an old book called the Fangyan, a record of the speech of the regions.
