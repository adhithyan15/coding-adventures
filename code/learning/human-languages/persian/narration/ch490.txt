Persian, chapter 490: Colds and Flu.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
سرماخوردگی (sarmâkhordegi) — a cold (illness).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for shy, then the Persian for to make fun of.

You'll want to know: سرماخوردگی.
سرماخوردگی (sarmâkhordegi) — "a cold (illness)".
او سرماخوردگی دارد و سر کار نمیرود. u sarmâkhordegi dârad va sar-e kâr nemiravad. He has a cold and isn't going to work.
The noun from sarmâ khordan 'to catch a cold', with the ending -egi.

The word, taken apart.
sarmâkhordegi is built on native Persian pieces: sarmâ, 'cold', khord, the past stem of khordan, 'to eat', and the noun ending -egi: the state of having eaten the cold.

Lesson 2 of 5.
آنفولانزا (ânfolânzâ) — flu, influenza.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to make fun of, then the Persian for a cold.

You'll want to know: آنفولانزا.
آنفولانزا (ânfolânzâ) — "flu, influenza".
این زمستان خیلی از بچه ها آنفولانزا گرفتند. in zemestân kheyli az bachchehâ ânfolânzâ gereftand. This winter a lot of children caught flu.
ânfolânzâ gereftan — to catch flu.

The word, taken apart.
ânfolânzâ is a modern loan, usually traced to French influenza, which French took from Italian. EtymDB has no entry for the Persian word.

Lesson 3 of 5.
عطسه کردن ('atse kardan) — to sneeze.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a cold, then the Persian for flu.

You'll want to know: عطسه کردن.
عطسه کردن ('atse kardan) — "to sneeze".
او در بهار زیاد عطسه میکند. u dar bahâr ziyâd 'atse mikonad. She sneezes a lot in spring.
'atse 'a sneeze' + kardan; present stem kon-.

The word, taken apart.
'atse is a loan, usually traced to Arabic ʿaṭsa, 'a sneeze'. EtymDB has no entry for it. kardan, 'to do', is inherited Persian.

Lesson 4 of 5.
گلودرد (galudard) — a sore throat.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for flu, then the Persian for to sneeze.

You'll want to know: گلودرد.
گلودرد (galudard) — "a sore throat".
از دیروز گلودرد دارم. az diruz galudard dâram. I've had a sore throat since yesterday.
galu 'throat' + dard 'pain', on the pattern of sardard 'headache'.

The word, taken apart.
galudard is built on two inherited words: galu, 'throat', usually traced to Middle Persian galōg, and dard, 'pain', as in sardard.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: تب کردن.
تب کردن (tab kardan) — "to run a fever".
بچه دیشب تب کرد. bachche dishab tab kard. The child ran a fever last night.
tab 'fever' + kardan. tab dâshtan — to have a temperature.

The word, taken apart.
tab is a native Persian word for fever; EtymDB lists Armenian vap, 'heat', beside it (EtymDB). kardan, 'to do', is inherited Persian.
