## You'll want to know: ਤਾਂਬਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C251-GHR251-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ਤਾਂਬਾ** — *tāmbā* — "copper".

> ਪੁਰਾਣਾ ਲੋਟਾ ਤਾਂਬੇ ਦਾ ਹੈ।
>
> *purāṇā loṭā tāmbe dā hai.*
>
> The old water pot is made of copper.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਤਾਂਬਾ** (*tāmbā*) is inherited from Sanskrit *tāmra*, "copper"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *tāmbā*, "copper" (CDIAL 5779).
