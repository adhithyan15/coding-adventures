## You'll want to know: compensar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C665-QUEJA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**compensar** — "to compensate, to make up for".

> El hotel nos compensó con una noche gratis.
>
> *The hotel made up for it with a free night.*

*Compensar a alguien por algo* — to compensate someone for something.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*compensar* is a learned borrowing from Latin *compēnsāre* "to weigh things against one another, to balance" (Lewis & Short; EtymDB), built on *pēnsāre* "to weigh".
