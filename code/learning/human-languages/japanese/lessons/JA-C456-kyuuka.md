## You'll want to know: きゅうか
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C456-PAY456-03]; assesses=[] -->

**きゅうか** — *kyūka* — "time off work; leave".

> **なつに いっしゅうかん きゅうかが あります。** — *natsu ni isshūkan kyūka ga arimasu* — "I have a week off work in summer."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**きゅうか** (*kyūka*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *kyū*, "rest" (Middle Chinese *xjuw*) and *ka*, "leave of absence" (*haeH*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
