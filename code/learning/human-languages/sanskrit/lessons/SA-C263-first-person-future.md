# The speaker inside the future

## Warm-up

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[SA-LEX-C139-FUT139-05, SA-VERB-C263-GAMISYAMI-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Contrast **गमिष्यति**, “will go,” with **गमिष्यामि**, “I will go.”

## Grammar Lens: first-person singular

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-GRAMMAR-C263-FUTURE-FIRST-SINGULAR-02]; assesses=[] -->

In the complete form **गमिष्यामि**, the ending **-आमि** (*-āmi*) marks the
speaker: “I.” The future portion comes before it. This is a **first-person
singular** ramp. Learn this whole form before extending the pattern.

## Guided Practice

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[SA-LEX-C139-FUT139-05, SA-VERB-C263-GAMISYAMI-01, SA-GRAMMAR-C263-FUTURE-FIRST-SINGULAR-02] -->

[PAUSE 2s each]
- [YOU SAY: another person “will go” — *gamiṣyati*]
- [YOU SAY: the speaker says “I will go” — *gamiṣyāmi*]
- [YOU SAY: name the ending that carries “I” — *-āmi*]
