# Begin the purpose: Marathi

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-A1-MESSAGE-LINE-KNOW-MARATHI]; assesses=[MR-LEX-YENE, MR-GRAMMAR-DATIVE-KNOWLEDGE] -->

Say the known sentence **मला मराठी येते.** Marathi puts the knower in the
dative and lets **मराठी** govern **येते**. Nothing grammatical is being added.

## Writing — delayed copy
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MR-LEX-YENE, MR-GRAMMAR-DATIVE-KNOWLEDGE, MR-A1-MESSAGE-LINE-KNOW-MARATHI] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: delayed-copy -->

Study **मला मराठी येते.** for five seconds. Cover it, wait five more seconds,
then write it. Repair the ending **-ते** before any other spelling.
