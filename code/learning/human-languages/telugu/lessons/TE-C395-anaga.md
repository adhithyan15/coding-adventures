## You'll want to know: అనగా
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C395-KANUKA395-04]; assesses=[] -->

**అనగా** — *anagā* — "that is to say; namely".

> రేపు అనగా శుక్రవారం మాకు సెలవు.
>
> *rēpu anagā śukravāraṁ māku selavu.*
>
> Tomorrow, that is Friday, is a holiday for us.

A bookish cousin of అంటే; it explains the word before it.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**అనగా** (*anagā*) is native Dravidian, a form of *anu*, "to say" (DEDR 868), the verb behind అని and అంటే.
