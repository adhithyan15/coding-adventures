## You'll want to know: objecter
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C336-DESACCORD-02]; assesses=[] -->

**objecter** — "to object, to say in reply".

> Elle a objecté que c'était trop cher.
>
> *She objected that it was too expensive.*

*Objecter que…* — to object that… Formal; in speech many people say *je ne suis pas d'accord*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*objecter* is a learned borrowing from Latin *obiectāre* "to throw against, to set before" (Lewis & Short), formed on *obicere* "to throw before", from *ob* and *iacere* "to throw".
