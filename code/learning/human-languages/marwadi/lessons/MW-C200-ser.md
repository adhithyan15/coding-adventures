## You'll want to know: सेर
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C200-WEIGH200-02]; assesses=[] -->

**सेर** — *ser* — "a seer (a measure of weight)".

> एक सेर आटो दो।
>
> *ek ser āṭo do.*
>
> Give me a seer of flour.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सेर** (*ser*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*satēra*, "a measure of weight"; CDIAL lists Old Marwari *sera* (CDIAL 13106).
