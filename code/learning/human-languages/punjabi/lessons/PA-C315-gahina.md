## You'll want to know: ਗਹਿਣਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C315-BANDIT315-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ਗਹਿਣਾ** — *gahiṇā* — "a piece of jewellery".

> ਮਾਤਾ ਜੀ ਦੇ ਗਹਿਣੇ ਅਲਮਾਰੀ ਵਿੱਚ ਸਨ।
>
> *mātā jī de gahiṇe almārī vich san.*
>
> Mother's jewellery was in the cupboard.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਗਹਿਣਾ** (*gahiṇā*) is inherited from Sanskrit *grahaṇa*, "seizing, holding"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *gahiṇā*, "a pledge, a pawn", under it; the sense of a jewel is given there only for Hindi *gahnā* (CDIAL 4364).
