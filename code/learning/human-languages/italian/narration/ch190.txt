Italian, chapter 190: Useful, Useless, Strange, Normal, Different.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
utile — useful.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for fearful, then the Italian for curious.

You'll want to know: utile.
utile — "useful".

The word, taken apart.
utile from Latin utilis, "useful", from uti, "to use" — a learned borrowing. (EtymDB 2.1 records the Latin word.)
