## You'll want to know: منحة
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C390-KULLIYYA390-04]; assesses=[] -->

**منحة** — *minḥa* — "a scholarship".

> حصلت أختي على منحة من الجامعة.
>
> *ḥaṣalat ukhtī ʿalā minḥa min al-jāmiʿa.*
>
> My sister got a scholarship from the university.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**منحة** (*minḥa*) is built on the native Arabic root *m-n-ḥ*, "to give, to bestow" (Lane). A *minḥa* is a gift: a grant, a scholarship.
