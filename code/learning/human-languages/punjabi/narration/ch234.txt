Punjabi, chapter 234: Lights Out.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ਨੀਂਦ (nīnd) — sleep.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for a thick cotton sheet, then the Punjabi for a fan.

You'll want to know: ਨੀਂਦ.
ਨੀਂਦ — nīnd — "sleep".
ਰਾਤ ਨੂੰ ਮੈਨੂੰ ਚੰਗੀ ਨੀਂਦ ਆਉਂਦੀ ਹੈ। rāt nū̃ mainū̃ chaṅgī nīnd āundī hai. I sleep well at night.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ਨੀਂਦ (nīnd) is inherited from Sanskrit nidrā, "sleep"; CDIAL lists Punjabi nī̃d, "sleep" (CDIAL 7200).

Lesson 3 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: ਬਲਣਾ.
ਬਲਣਾ — balṇā — "to burn (of a flame)".
ਦੀਵਾ ਰਾਤ ਨੂੰ ਬਲਦਾ ਹੈ। dīvā rāt nū̃ baldā hai. The lamp burns at night.
Past (it …): ਉਹ ਦੀਵਾ ਬਲਿਆ, oh dīvā baliā.

The word, taken apart.
ਬਲਣਾ (balṇā) is inherited from a reconstructed dvalati, "burns"; CDIAL lists Punjabi balṇā, "to burn, blaze" (CDIAL 6654).

Lesson 4 of 5.
ਬੁਝਾਉਣਾ (bujhāuṇā) — to put out (a light or a fire).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for an oil lamp, then the Punjabi for to burn.

You'll want to know: ਬੁਝਾਉਣਾ.
ਬੁਝਾਉਣਾ — bujhāuṇā — "to put out (a light or a fire)".
ਮੈਂ ਰਾਤ ਨੂੰ ਬੱਤੀ ਬੁਝਾਉਂਦੀ ਹਾਂ। maiṁ rāt nū̃ battī bujhāundī hāṁ. At night I put out the light.
Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਦੀਵਾ ਬੁਝਾਇਆ, maiṁ dīvā bujhāiā.

The word, taken apart.
ਬੁਝਾਉਣਾ (bujhāuṇā) is inherited from a reconstructed vijjhāyati, "is burnt out, is extinguished"; CDIAL lists Punjabi bujhāuṇā, "to extinguish" (CDIAL 11703).
