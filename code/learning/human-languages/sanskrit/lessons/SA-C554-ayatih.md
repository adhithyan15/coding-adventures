## You'll want to know: आयतिः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C554-BHAVI554-04]; assesses=[] -->

**आयतिः** — *āyatiḥ* — "the time ahead".

> यदि आयतिः शुभा अस्ति, तर्हि वयं प्रसन्नाः स्मः।
>
> *yadi āyatiḥ śubhā asti, tarhi vayaṃ prasannāḥ smaḥ.*
>
> If the future looks bright, we are glad.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**आयतिः** (*āyatiḥ*) is built on *ā* and the root √yam, "to hold, to stretch": a stretching out; MW gives *āyati* as extension, and following or future time (MW).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
