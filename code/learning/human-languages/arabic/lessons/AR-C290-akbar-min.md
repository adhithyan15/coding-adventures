# أكبر من (akbar min) — bigger than

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[AR-LEX-C289-TAJRIBA289-01, AR-GRAMMAR-C289-WISH-AN-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "it was a new experience", then "I hope to travel". (*Kānat
tajribatan jadīda*, *atamannā an usāfira*.)

[PAUSE 1s] To describe an experience well, you set it beside another: the
city or the village, the train or the bus.

## Grammar Lens: the shape aCCaC, and min
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-GRAMMAR-C290-ELATIVE-01]; assesses=[AR-LEX-C81-ANIMALS81-04, AR-LEX-C81-ANIMALS81-05, AR-LEX-C83-ADJ83-02, AR-LEX-C93-TOWN93-04, AR-LEX-C93-TOWN93-03] -->

Take the three root letters of a describing word and pour them into the
shape *a-C-C-a-C*: *kabīr*, big, becomes **أكبر** *akbar*, bigger;
*ṣaghīr* becomes *aṣghar*; *sarīʿ*, fast, becomes *asraʿ*. **من** — *min*,
"from" — is the "than":

> **المدينة أكبر من القرية.** — *al-madīna akbar min al-qarya* — The city is
> bigger than the village.

The shape does not change for a feminine noun: *al-madīna akbar*, *al-qiṭār
asraʿ min ad-darrāja*. You have owned two of these since chapter one hundred
and seventy-six: *akthar*, more, is the shape of *kathīr*, many, and
*aqall*, less, of *qalīl*, few.

## Grammar Lens: the biggest, and better
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-GRAMMAR-C290-SUPERLATIVE-01]; assesses=[AR-LEX-C115-WANT115-03, AR-LEX-C92-TOWN92-01, AR-LEX-C91-TOWN91-05, AR-CONCEPT-C06-MIN-FADLIK-01] -->

Put *al-* on it for "the biggest": *al-akbar*. Or set it before a noun with
no article: **أكبر مدينة** *akbar madīna*, the biggest city.

"Better" has a shape of its own: **أفضل** *afḍal*, from the root of
*faḍḍala*, to prefer, and of *min faḍlik*.

> **القطار أفضل من الحافلة.** — *al-qiṭār afḍal min al-ḥāfila* — The train is
> better than the bus.

*Al-afḍal* is "the best".

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[AR-GRAMMAR-C290-ELATIVE-01, AR-GRAMMAR-C290-SUPERLATIVE-01, AR-LEX-C176-SABAB176-03, AR-LEX-C176-SABAB176-04, AR-LEX-C142-THINGS142-01, AR-GRAMMAR-C285-OPINION-ANNA-01] -->

[PAUSE 1s]
- [YOU SAY: *kabīr* … *akbar*; *ṣaghīr* … *aṣghar*; *sarīʿ* … *asraʿ*]
- [YOU SAY: "The city is bigger than the village" — *al-madīna akbar min al-qarya*]
- [YOU SAY: "The train is faster than the bicycle" — *al-qiṭār asraʿ min ad-darrāja*]
- [YOU SAY: "the biggest city" — *akbar madīna*]
- [YOU SAY: "I think the train is faster" — *aʿtaqidu anna al-qiṭāra asraʿ*]
- [YOU SAY: "The train is better than the bus" — *al-qiṭār afḍal min al-ḥāfila*]
