# Review — the harbour, the light, and see you soon

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-C302-AWAY-04, ES-LEX-C302-AWAY-05] -->

[PAUSE 2s] The last two nouns of the run. Say "the harbour." (***El puerto***.)
Say "the lighthouse." (***El faro***.)

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-C302-AWAY-04, ES-LEX-C302-AWAY-05, ES-LEX-HASTA-PRONTO, ES-LEX-HASTA, ES-LEX-PRONTO] -->

A leaving needs a **place** and a **time**, and you have both.

[PAUSE 2s] The place: **el puerto**, the gate off the sea, and **el faro**, the
light standing at its mouth. The time: **hasta pronto** — *hasta*, "until", and
*pronto*, "soon".

[PAUSE 2s] Say the whole thing once, slowly, as one leaving:

> **El puerto. El faro. Hasta pronto.**

[PAUSE 2s] Three words out of three different centuries and two different
languages — a Roman harbour, a Greek island, and an Arabic preposition holding a
Latin adjective. You can hear all of that now, which you could not at the start.
