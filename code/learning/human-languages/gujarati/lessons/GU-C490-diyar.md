## You'll want to know: દિયર
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C490-SASRA490-02]; assesses=[] -->

**દિયર** — *diyar* — "a husband's younger brother".

> મીરાનો દિયર શાળામાં ભણે છે.
>
> *Mīrāno diyar śāḷāmā̃ bhaṇe che.*
>
> Mira's husband's younger brother is at school.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**દિયર** (*diyar*) is inherited from Sanskrit *dēvṛ*, "husband's younger brother", by way of *dēvara-*; CDIAL lists the Gujarati word (CDIAL 6546).
