## You'll want to know: recorrer
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C298-RECURSO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**recorrer** — "to appeal (in court); to turn to (for help)".

> O advogado vai recorrer da decisão.
>
> *The lawyer is going to appeal against the decision.*

*Recorrer a alguém* — to turn to someone for help.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*recorrer* comes from Latin *recurrere* "to run back, to have recourse" (EtymDB), built on *currere* "to run".
