## You'll want to know: وصية
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C337-IRTH337-01]; assesses=[] -->

**وصية** — *waṣiyya* — "a will, a testament; advice".

> كتب الرجل وصيته قبل موته.
>
> *kataba ar-rajul waṣiyyatahu qabla mawtihi.*
>
> The man wrote his will before he died.

Plural: وصايا, *waṣāyā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**وصية** (*waṣiyya*) is built on the native Arabic root *w-ṣ-y* (Lane), "to enjoin, to give a charge". A *waṣiyya* is the charge a person leaves behind.
