Gujarati, chapter 350: Contracts and Papers.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
કરાર (karār) — a contract.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for neutral, then the Gujarati for to behave.

You'll want to know: કરાર.
કરાર — karār — "a contract".
બંને વેપારીઓએ એક કરાર પર સહી કરી. banne vepārīoe ek karār par sahī karī. Both merchants signed a contract.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
કરાર (karār) is usually traced to Arabic qarār, "firmness, a settlement", through Persian.

Lesson 3 of 5.
મુક્ત (mukt) — free of; released (from a duty, a debt or a worry).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a contract, then the Gujarati for a document.

You'll want to know: મુક્ત.
મુક્ત — mukt — "free of; released (from a duty, a debt or a worry)".
બધું બિલ ચૂકવ્યા પછી તે ચિંતાથી મુક્ત થયો. badhũ bil cūkavyā pachī te cintāthī mukt thayo. Once he had paid the whole bill he was free of worry.

The word, taken apart.
મુક્ત (mukt) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit mukta, "loosened, set free" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 5 of 5.
વસૂલવું (vasūlvũ) — to recover a debt; to collect money owed.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for free of, then the Gujarati for seized.

You'll want to know: વસૂલવું.
વસૂલવું — vasūlvũ — "to recover a debt; to collect money owed".
બેંક બાકી રકમ વ્યાજ સાથે વસૂલશે. beṅk bākī rakam vyāj sāthe vasūlśe. The bank will recover the outstanding amount with interest.
Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે બધી રકમ વસૂલી, teṇe badhī rakam vasūlī.

The word, taken apart.
વસૂલવું (vasūlvũ) is built on vasūl, "recovered, received", usually traced to Arabic wuṣūl, "arrival, receipt", through Persian.
