## You'll want to know: la marmite
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C330-MIJOTER-03]; assesses=[] -->

**la marmite** — "the cooking pot, the stockpot".

> La soupe cuit dans une grande marmite.
>
> *The soup is cooking in a big pot.*

A large pot with a lid and two handles.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

The origin of *marmite* is not settled; an Old French adjective *marmite* "hypocritical" is listed beside it (EtymDB), perhaps a pot that hides what it holds.
