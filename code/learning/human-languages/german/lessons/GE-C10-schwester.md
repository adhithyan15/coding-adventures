## You'll want to know: die Schwester
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-SCHWESTER-01]; assesses=[GE-SOUND-SCHW-01, GE-LEX-DER-DIE-DAS-02] -->

> **die Schwester** — "sister."

*SHVES-ter*. A *die*-word, and the counterpart of *der Bruder*.

On the page it looks unrelated to **sister**. In the mouth it is not: take the
*shv* back to a plain *s* and what is left — *s-e-s-t-e-r* — is the English word
with one vowel moved.

## Sounds you'll need
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GE-LEX-SCHWESTER-01, GE-SOUND-SCHW-01] -->

- `schw-cluster` — *shv-*, straight into the vowel.
- `herz-short-e` — a short open *e*, the vowel of English *bed*.
- `r-final` — and the same soft ending as *Vater*, *Mutter* and *Bruder*.

Four family words now, and all four end the same way.
