## You'll want to know: lōrīca, lōrīcae
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C340-ARMA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**lōrīca, lōrīcae** — "a breastplate, a cuirass".

> Lōrīca mīlitem ā sagittā servāvit.
>
> *His breastplate saved the soldier from the arrow.*

Feminine. The first ones were made of leather straps.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**lōrīca** is built on *lōrum*, "a leather strap" (Lewis & Short): a corselet of leather thongs.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
