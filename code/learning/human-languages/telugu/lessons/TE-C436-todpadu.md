# తోడ్పడు (tōḍpaḍu) — to contribute; to help along

## You'll want to know: తోడ్పడు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C436-PALGONU436-02]; assesses=[] -->

**తోడ్పడు** — *tōḍpaḍu* — "to contribute; to help along".

> ఆ పథకం గ్రామ అభివృద్ధికి తోడ్పడింది.
>
> *ā pathakaṁ grāma abhivṛddhiki tōḍpaḍindi.*
>
> That scheme helped the village develop.

Past (I …): తోడ్పడ్డాను, *tōḍpaḍḍānu*.

What it helps takes -కి. The noun is తోడ్పాటు, a contribution.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**తోడ్పడు** (*tōḍpaḍu*) is native Telugu, a compound of *tōḍu*, "a companion, help", and the verb *paḍu* (DEDR 3853).
