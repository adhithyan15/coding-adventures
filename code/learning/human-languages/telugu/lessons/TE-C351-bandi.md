# బందీ (bandī) — a captive; a hostage

## You'll want to know: బందీ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C351-BANDI351-01]; assesses=[] -->

**బందీ** — *bandī* — "a captive; a hostage".

> దొంగలు ఇద్దరిని బందీలుగా పట్టుకున్నారు.
>
> *doṅgalu iddarini bandīlugā paṭṭukunnāru.*
>
> The robbers took two people captive.

Plural: బందీలు, *bandīlu*.

బందీగా పట్టుకో, to take captive.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**బందీ** (*bandī*) is a loan from Persian *bandī*, "a prisoner, a captive", built on *band*, "a bond".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
