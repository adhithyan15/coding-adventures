## You'll want to know: അരുവി
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C496-NADI496-02]; assesses=[] -->

**അരുവി** — *aruvi* — "a brook; a stream".

> കാട്ടിലൂടെ ഒരു ചെറിയ അരുവി ഒഴുകുന്നു.
>
> *kāṭṭilūṭe oru ceṟiya aruvi oḻukunnu.*
>
> A little stream flows through the forest.

Smaller than a പുഴ: water that runs down from the hills.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**അരുവി** (*aruvi*) is native Dravidian, from a root that means to spring out and flow down gently (DEDR 226).
