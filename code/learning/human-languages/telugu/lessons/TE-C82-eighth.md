## You'll want to know: ఎనిమిదవ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C82-ORDINAL-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ఎనిమిదవ** (*enimidava*) — "**eighth**".

**ఎనిమిది** → **ఎనిమిదవ**, and here the vowel that goes is an **i**, not a **u**.
Every ordinal so far has traded a final *u*; this one and the next trade a final
*i*, because that is what their cardinals happen to end in.

Which is the useful way to hold the rule: it is not "drop the *u*", it is **drop
whatever vowel is at the end**. The ending does not know which vowel it replaced.

Note the short **ఎ** at the front, against the long **ఏ** of the last lesson.
