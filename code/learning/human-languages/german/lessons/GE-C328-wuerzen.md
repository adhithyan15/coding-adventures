## You'll want to know: würzen
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C328-SIEB-05]; assesses=[] -->

**würzen** — "to season, to spice".

> Er würzte das Fleisch mit Pfeffer.
>
> *He seasoned the meat with pepper.*

*Das Gewürz* — the spice.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*würzen* is built on *Würze* "seasoning", from Middle High German *wurz* "herb, root" (EtymDB).
