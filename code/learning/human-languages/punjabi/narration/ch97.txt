Punjabi, chapter 97: Thousand, World, Colour, Song, Journey.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ਹਜ਼ਾਰ (hazār) — a thousand.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for fifty, then the Punjabi for a hundred.

You'll want to know: ਹਜ਼ਾਰ.
ਹਜ਼ਾਰ — hazār — "a thousand".

The word, taken apart.
ਹਜ਼ਾਰ (hazār) is a loan, usually traced to Persian hazār, "thousand".

Lesson 2 of 5.
ਦੁਨੀਆ (dunīā) — the world.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for a hundred, then the Punjabi for a thousand.

You'll want to know: ਦੁਨੀਆ.
ਦੁਨੀਆ — dunīā — "the world".

The word, taken apart.
ਦੁਨੀਆ (dunīā) is a loan from Arabic dunyā, "the world, this nearer life", which reached Punjabi through Persian. Its Arabic root is d-n-w (Lane).

[pause 2 seconds]

The word, taken apart.
ਰੰਗ (raṅg) is listed by CDIAL under Sanskrit raṅga, "dye, colour" (CDIAL 10560). Persian has rang, "colour", too, and the two words may have merged.
