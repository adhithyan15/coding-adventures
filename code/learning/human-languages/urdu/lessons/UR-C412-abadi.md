## You'll want to know: آبادی
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C412-QASBA412-02]; assesses=[] -->

**آبادی** — *ābādī* — "a population; a settlement".

> اس قصبے کی آبادی کم ہے۔
>
> *is qasbe kī ābādī kam hai.*
>
> Not many people live in this little town.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**آبادی** (*ābādī*) is built on *ābād*, "inhabited, flourishing", usually traced to Persian *ābād*, with the ending *-ī*.
