## You'll want to know: процент
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C394-DOLG-05]; assesses=[] -->

**процент** (*protsént*) — "a per cent; interest (on money)".

> Банк даёт пять процентов в год.
>
> *Bank dayót pyat protséntov v god.*
>
> The bank pays five per cent a year.

Masculine. *Сто процентов* — a hundred per cent. *Проценты* is also interest on money.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**процент** is usually traced, through German *Prozent*, to Latin *prō centum* "for a hundred" (Lewis & Short).
