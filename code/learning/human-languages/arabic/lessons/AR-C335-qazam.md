## You'll want to know: قزم
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C335-SIHR335-05]; assesses=[] -->

**قزم** — *qazam* — "a dwarf".

> ساعد الأقزام البنت في الغابة.
>
> *sāʿada al-aqzām al-bint fī al-ghāba.*
>
> The dwarfs helped the girl in the forest.

Plural: أقزام, *aqzām*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**قزم** (*qazam*) is usually traced to a native Arabic root *q-z-m*, where *qazam* is something small and mean; Lane's article on it is empty. It now names a dwarf.
