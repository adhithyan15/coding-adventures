## You'll want to know: le documentaire
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C525-ECRAN-04]; assesses=[] -->

**le documentaire** — "documentary".

> Hier, nous avons vu un documentaire sur les baleines.
>
> *Yesterday we watched a documentary about whales.*

Also an adjective: *un film documentaire*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*le documentaire* is built on *document* (EtymDB), a learned borrowing from Latin *documentum* "a lesson, a proof", from *docēre* "to teach" (Lewis & Short).
