## You'll want to know: કાર્યક્રમ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C179-PLAN179-03]; assesses=[] -->

**કાર્યક્રમ** — *kāryakram* — "programme, event".

> **શાળામાં સાંજે કાર્યક્રમ હશે.** — *śāḷāmā̃ sā̃je kāryakram haśe* — "There will be a programme at the school in the evening."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**કાર્યક્રમ** (*kāryakram*) is a learned compound built from Sanskrit *kārya*, "work, task", and *krama*, "step, order", a tatsama.
