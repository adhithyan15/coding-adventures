## Reading — labels, a message, and a first paragraph

Chapter 62 is Marathi's reading rung, and the first connected Marathi the track
has asked anyone to read straight through.

Three lessons, ramping 6 -> 16 -> 83 tokens: six form labels and things
(**नाव**, **शहर**, **भाषा**, **घर**, **खोली**, **पाणी**), the six-line message
the track already writes, then an 83-word paragraph.

- **No new word in any of the three.** Every Devanagari token was checked to
  occur in a lesson with a lower sequence number, so the claim that nothing is
  new is verified rather than asserted. Four drafted words -- सकाळी, त्याचं,
  आम्ही, एकत्र -- and later दिवस, होता, आज were all cut for failing that check,
  rather than being waved through as obvious.
- The message lesson is deliberately not a review. The track already WRITES
  those six lines; reading them means meeting them in somebody else's order and
  at their pace, which is a different skill.
- The paragraph clears Marathi's own A1 reading part: 83 words against the
  70-90 word `a1-reading-signs-and-forms` stimulus. pre-A1 moves 0/3 -> 3/3 and
  A1 0/3 -> 1/3. The two longer parts (90-120 and 90-140 words) remain.
- Each lesson introduces its own skill -- labels, message, connected reading --
  because a reading lesson that declares nothing is unmeasurable, and because
  these are three real abilities rather than one split three ways.
