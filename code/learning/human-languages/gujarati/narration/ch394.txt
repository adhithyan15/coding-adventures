Gujarati, chapter 394: Porters and Trunks.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
હમાલ (hamāl) — a porter.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a whistle, then the Gujarati for to couple.

You'll want to know: હમાલ.
હમાલ — hamāl — "a porter".
હમાલે મારો સામાન ટ્રેનમાં મૂક્યો. hamāle māro sāmān ṭrenmā̃ mūkyo. The porter put my luggage on the train.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
હમાલ (hamāl) is usually traced to Arabic ḥammāl, "a carrier of loads", which reached Gujarati through Persian; at a big station the porters carry bags on their heads.

Lesson 2 of 5.
પટારો (paṭāro) — a trunk (a large box).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to couple, then the Gujarati for a porter.

You'll want to know: પટારો.
પટારો — paṭāro — "a trunk (a large box)".
દાદીનો જૂનો પટારો ઘણો ભારે છે. dādīno jūno paṭāro ghaṇo bhāre che. Grandmother's old trunk is very heavy.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
પટારો (paṭāro) goes back to Sanskrit piṭaka, "a basket, a box"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati word for a large box (CDIAL 8164).

Lesson 3 of 5.
પોટલું (poṭlũ) — a bundle (tied in cloth).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a porter, then the Gujarati for a trunk (a large box).

You'll want to know: પોટલું.
પોટલું — poṭlũ — "a bundle (tied in cloth)".
તેણે કપડાંનું પોટલું માથા પર મૂક્યું. teṇe kapḍā̃nũ poṭlũ māthā par mūkyũ. He put the bundle of clothes on his head.
A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
પોટલું (poṭlũ) goes back to pōṭṭa, "a bundle", a form CDIAL reconstructs beside Sanskrit pōṭṭala; CDIAL lists the Gujarati word (CDIAL 8396).

Lesson 4 of 5.
સરકાવવું (sarkāvvũ) — to slide (something); to push along.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a trunk (a large box), then the Gujarati for a bundle (tied in cloth).

You'll want to know: સરકાવવું.
સરકાવવું — sarkāvvũ — "to slide (something); to push along".
મેં ભારે થેલી બારણા પાસે સરકાવી. mẽ bhāre thelī bārṇā pāse sarkāvī. I slid the heavy bag over to the door.
Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે પટારો સરકાવ્યો, teṇe paṭāro sarkāvyo.

The word, taken apart.
સરકાવવું (sarkāvvũ) is the causative partner of સરકવું, "to slide", which CDIAL lists under Sanskrit sárati, "runs, flows, glides" (CDIAL 13250): to make a thing slide.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ઝાલવું (jhālvũ) — to catch hold of; to grip.

Warm-up.

ઝાલવું — jhālvũ — "to catch hold of; to grip".
ભીડમાં મીરાએ બાળકનો હાથ ઝાલ્યો. bhīḍmā̃ Mīrāe bāḷakno hāth jhālyo. In the crowd Mira caught hold of the child's hand.
Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે હાથ ઝાલ્યો, teṇe hāth jhālyo.

The word, taken apart.
ઝાલવું (jhālvũ) goes back to jhal, "a sudden movement", a form CDIAL reconstructs; CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb, "to catch hold of" (CDIAL 5351): a quick grab.
