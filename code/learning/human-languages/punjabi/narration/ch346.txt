Punjabi, chapter 346: Owning Up.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ਕਸੂਰ (kasūr) — a fault; blame.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for to settle a matter, then the Punjabi for a proposal.

You'll want to know: ਕਸੂਰ.
ਕਸੂਰ — kasūr — "a fault; blame".
ਇਹ ਮੇਰਾ ਕਸੂਰ ਨਹੀਂ ਹੈ। eh merā kasūr nahīṁ hai. This is not my fault.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ਕਸੂਰ (kasūr) is a loan, usually traced through Persian to Arabic quṣūr, "shortcoming".

What you've built.
One more word for asking and answering.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: kasūr]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: kasūr, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: eh merā kasūr nahīṁ hai. — This is not my fault.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Punjabi for to settle a matter, then the Punjabi for a proposal, then say kasūr again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does ਕਸੂਰ (kasūr) mean? ("A fault; blame".) Say it once more.


Lesson 2 of 5.
ਲੁਕਾਉਣਾ (lukāuṇā) — to hide something; to keep something secret.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for a proposal, then the Punjabi for a fault.

You'll want to know: ਲੁਕਾਉਣਾ.
ਲੁਕਾਉਣਾ — lukāuṇā — "to hide something; to keep something secret".
ਮੈਂ ਕੁਝ ਨਹੀਂ ਲੁਕਾਉਂਦਾ ਹਾਂ। maiṁ kujh nahīṁ lukāundā hāṁ. I am not hiding anything.
Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਚਾਬੀ ਲੁਕਾਈ, maiṁ chābī lukāī.

The word, taken apart.
ਲੁਕਾਉਣਾ (lukāuṇā) is the causative partner of lukṇā, "to hide oneself", which goes back to Sanskrit lupyate, "is lost, is removed"; CDIAL lists Punjabi lukṇā under it (CDIAL 11083).
