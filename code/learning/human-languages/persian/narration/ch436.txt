Persian, chapter 436: By boat.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
اسکله (eskele) — a pier, a jetty, a quay.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a porter, then the Persian for to give someone a lift.

You'll want to know: اسکله.
اسکله (eskele) — "a pier, a jetty, a quay".
صبح قایق از اسکله حرکت کرد. Sobh qâyeq az eskele harekat kard. In the morning the boat left the pier.
Ports on the Persian Gulf have long eskele for boats to the islands.

The word, taken apart.
eskele came into Persian from Turkish iskele 'a landing place'; EtymDB lists the Turkish word without a gloss. The Turkish word is usually traced to Italian scala 'ladder, landing'.

Lesson 2 of 5.
ناخدا (nâkhodâ) — a captain (of a ship), a skipper.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to give someone a lift, then the Persian for a pier.

You'll want to know: ناخدا.
ناخدا (nâkhodâ) — "a captain (of a ship), a skipper".
ناخدا (nâkhodâ) سی سال روی دریا کار کرده است. Nâkhodâ si sâl ru-ye daryâ kâr karde ast. The captain has worked at sea for thirty years.
Literally 'ship-master'. The navy rank is also nâkhodâ.

The word, taken apart.
nâkhodâ is a native compound, which EtymDB analyses as nâv 'ship' and khodâ 'chief, master': the master of the ship. Khodâ is usually traced to Middle Persian xwadāy 'lord'.

Lesson 3 of 5.
عرشه ('arshe) — a deck (of a ship).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a pier, then the Persian for a captain.

You'll want to know: عرشه.
عرشه ('arshe) — "a deck (of a ship)".
مسافران روی عرشه ایستاده بودند. Mosâferân ru-ye 'arshe istâde budand. The passengers were standing on the deck.
Ru-ye 'arshe — on deck.

The word, taken apart.
'arshe was borrowed from Arabic and is usually traced to Arabic ʿarš 'a roof, a raised platform, a throne'. EtymDB does not list it.

Lesson 4 of 5.
لنج (lenj) — a dhow, a wooden cargo boat (of the Gulf).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a captain, then the Persian for a deck.

You'll want to know: لنج.
لنج (lenj) — "a dhow, a wooden cargo boat (of the Gulf)".
این لنج از بندر به جزیره میرود. In lenj az bandar be jazire miravad. This dhow goes from the port to the island.
Built of wood in the ports of the south, and still in use for trade.

The word, taken apart.
The origin of lenj is not settled, and EtymDB does not list it. It names the large wooden boats of the Persian Gulf.

Lesson 5 of 5.
لنگر انداختن (langar andâkhtan) — to drop anchor, to anchor.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a deck, then the Persian for a dhow.

You'll want to know: لنگر انداختن.
لنگر انداختن (langar andâkhtan) — "to drop anchor, to anchor".
کشتی نزدیک ساحل لنگر انداخت. Kashti nazdik-e sâhel langar andâkht. The ship dropped anchor near the shore.
langar 'anchor' + andâkhtan 'to throw'. Present stem andâz-: langar miandâzad.

The word, taken apart.
langar andâkhtan is built from langar 'anchor', which EtymDB links with Greek ánkyra 'anchor', and andâkhtan 'to throw', usually traced to Middle Persian handāxtan.
