## You'll want to know: dàodá
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C196-TICKET196-05]; assesses=[] -->

*dàodá* — "to reach a destination; to arrive".

> *huǒchē xiàwǔ sān diǎn dàodá* — The train arrives at three in the afternoon.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*dàodá* is built from 到 'to arrive' and *dá* 'to get through', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *tawH* and *dat* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for reading notices and messages.
