Persian, chapter 259: To check, To haggle, To steal, To escape, To sweep.

Lesson 1 of 5.
بررسی کردن (barrasi kardan) — to check, to examine.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to agree, then the Persian for to imagine.

You'll want to know: بررسی کردن.
بررسی کردن — barrasi kardan — "to check, to examine".

The word, taken apart.
barrasi kardan pairs barrasi, "examination", with kardan. barrasi is bar, "on", with rasi, "reaching" (from residan): going over a thing. All are native Persian. kardan, "to do", is an inherited verb, from Middle Persian kardan (EtymDB).

Lesson 4 of 5.
فرار کردن (farâr kardan) — to escape, to run away.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to haggle, then the Persian for to steal.

You'll want to know: فرار کردن.
فرار کردن — farâr kardan — "to escape, to run away".

The word, taken apart.
farâr kardan pairs farâr, borrowed from Arabic firār, "flight, running away" (root f-r-r, Lane), with the Persian verb kardan, "to do, to make". kardan, "to do", is an inherited verb, from Middle Persian kardan (EtymDB).

Lesson 5 of 5.
جارو کردن (jâru kardan) — to sweep.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to steal, then the Persian for to escape.

You'll want to know: جارو کردن.
جارو کردن — jâru kardan — "to sweep".

The word, taken apart.
jâru kardan pairs jâru, "broom", with kardan. jâru is short for jârub, usually explained as built on rub-, "sweep" (from ruftan). Both are native. kardan, "to do", is an inherited verb, from Middle Persian kardan (EtymDB).
