## You'll want to know: चाकू
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C523-CHAKU523-01]; assesses=[] -->

**चाकू** — *cākū* — "a knife, a pocket knife".

> सब्ज़ी काटने के लिए एक तेज़ चाकू दीजिए।
>
> *Sabzī kāṭne ke liye ek tez cākū dījiye.*
>
> Please give me a sharp knife to cut the vegetables.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**चाकू** (*cākū*) is usually traced to Persian *chāqū*, "a knife".
