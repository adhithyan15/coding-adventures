Marwadi (Marwari), chapter 175: Water for the House.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
पणिहारी (paṇihārī) — a water-carrier; a woman who fetches water.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for to take off, then the Marwadi for a plait.

You'll want to know: पणिहारी.
पणिहारी — paṇihārī — "a water-carrier; a woman who fetches water".
पणिहारी बावड़ी सूं पाणी लावै है। paṇihārī bāvṛī sū̃ pāṇī lāvai hai. The water-carrier brings water from the stepwell.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
पणिहारी (paṇihārī) is built on a reconstructed pānīyahāra, "a water-carrier", from the word behind पाणी and a word for carrying; CDIAL lists Old Marwari pāṇiyārī (CDIAL 8088).

Lesson 2 of 5.
ईंडाणी (ī̃ḍāṇī) — a head-pad (for carrying pots).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for a plait, then the Marwadi for a water-carrier.

You'll want to know: ईंडाणी.
ईंडाणी — ī̃ḍāṇī — "a head-pad (for carrying pots)".
ईंडाणी नरम है। ī̃ḍāṇī naram hai. The head-pad is soft.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ईंडाणी (ī̃ḍāṇī) is inherited from Sanskrit iṇḍva, two grass pads that kept the hands from the fire; CDIAL lists Hindi ī̃ḍwā, a ring of rolled grass or cloth that cushions a load on the head (CDIAL 1559). CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry.

Lesson 3 of 5.

[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for a water-carrier, then the Marwadi for a head-pad.

You'll want to know: ढोवणो.
ढोवणो — ḍhovṇo — "to carry (a load)".
मां घड़ो ढोवै है। mā̃ ghaṛo ḍhovai hai. Mother carries the water pot.
Past (I …): म्हैं पाणी ढोयो, mhaĩ pāṇī ḍhoyo.

The word, taken apart.
ढोवणो (ḍhovṇo) is inherited from Sanskrit ḍhaukayati, "brings near"; CDIAL lists Hindi ḍhonā, "to convey" (CDIAL 5610). CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry.

Lesson 4 of 5.
कळसियो (kaḷsiyo) — a small water pot.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for a head-pad, then the Marwadi for to carry.

You'll want to know: कळसियो.
कळसियो — kaḷsiyo — "a small water pot".
कळसियो मेज माथै है। kaḷsiyo mej māthai hai. The small water pot is on the table.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
कळसियो (kaḷsiyo) is inherited from Sanskrit kalaśa, "a waterpot"; CDIAL lists Old Marwari kalasa, "a pitcher", and a Gujarati word for a jug that is close to this one (CDIAL 2920).

Lesson 5 of 5.
झारी (jhārī) — a spouted water pot.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for to carry, then the Marwadi for a small water pot.

You'll want to know: झारी.
झारी — jhārī — "a spouted water pot".
झारी मांय पाणी है। jhārī mā̃y pāṇī hai. There's water in the spouted pot.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
झारी (jhārī) comes from a reconstructed jhārikā, "a water-pot"; CDIAL lists Hindi and Gujarati jhārī, a spouted pot to drink from (CDIAL 5377). CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry.
