## You'll want to know: ಡಿಪೋ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C452-SARIGE452-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ಡಿಪೋ** — *ḍipō* — "a depot; a bus depot".

> ರಾತ್ರಿ ಎಲ್ಲಾ ಬಸ್ಸುಗಳು ಡಿಪೋಗೆ ಹಿಂತಿರುಗುತ್ತವೆ.
>
> *rātri ellā bassugaḷu ḍipōge hintiruguttave.*
>
> At night all the buses go back to the depot.

Where buses are kept and mended.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಡಿಪೋ** (*ḍipō*) is a loan from English *depot*.
