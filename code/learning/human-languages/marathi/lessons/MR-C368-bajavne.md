## You'll want to know: बजावणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C368-PALAN368-03]; assesses=[] -->

**बजावणे** — *bajāvṇe* — "to discharge (a duty)".

> पोलिसाने आपलं कर्तव्य प्रामाणिकपणे बजावलं.
>
> *polisāne āplaṁ kartavya prāmāṇikpaṇe bajāvlaṁ.*
>
> The policeman did his duty honestly.

Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने कर्तव्य बजावलं, *tyāne kartavya bajāvlaṁ*.

कर्तव्य बजावणे, to do one's duty; also to warn sternly: त्याला बजावलं.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**बजावणे** (*bajāvṇe*) is a Marathi verb whose origin is not settled.
