Russian, chapter 487: Rashes and Jabs.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
сыпь (syp) — a rash.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to go hoarse, then the Russian for to tickle.

You'll want to know: сыпь.
сыпь (syp) — "a rash".
После клубники у ребёнка появилась сыпь. Pósle klubníki u rebyónka poyavílas syp. After the strawberries the child came out in a rash.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
сыпь (syp) is built on сыпать 'to pour, to strew', which is inherited from Proto-Slavic sypati (EtymDB): a rash is strewn over the skin.

Lesson 3 of 5.
чесаться (chesátsya) — to itch; to scratch oneself.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a rash, then the Russian for an itch.

You'll want to know: чесаться.
чесаться (chesátsya) — "to itch; to scratch oneself".
После укуса комара рука очень чешется. Pósle ukúsa komará ruká óchen chéshetsya. After the mosquito bite my hand itches a lot.
Imperfective; present чешусь, чешешься (с becomes ш). Perfective почесаться.

The word, taken apart.
чесаться (chesátsya) is the reflexive of чесать 'to scratch, to comb', which is inherited from Proto-Slavic česati (EtymDB).

Lesson 4 of 5.
прививка (privívka) — a vaccination, a jab.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for an itch, then the Russian for to itch.

You'll want to know: прививка.
прививка (privívka) — "a vaccination, a jab".
Перед зимой мы сделали прививку от гриппа. Péred zimóy my sdélali privívku ot gríppa. Before the winter we had a flu jab.
Feminine. Сделать прививку от + genitive — to be vaccinated against.

The word, taken apart.
прививка (privívka) is built on прививать 'to graft, to inoculate', from вить 'to twist, to weave', which is inherited from Proto-Slavic viti (EtymDB).

Lesson 5 of 5.
заражаться (zarazhátsya) — to get infected.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to itch, then the Russian for a vaccination.

You'll want to know: заражаться.
заражаться (zarazhátsya) — "to get infected".
Дети быстро заражаются друг от друга. Déti býstro zarazháyutsya drug ot drúga. Children catch things from each other very quickly.
Imperfective; perfective заразиться (заражусь). Заражаться от кого — to catch it from someone.

The word, taken apart.
заражаться (zarazhátsya) is built on зараза 'infection'; EtymDB lists зараза and заразить without a source, and they are usually traced to разить 'to strike'.
