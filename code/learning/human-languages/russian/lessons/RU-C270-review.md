# Review — what will be, and what would be

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[RU-GRAMMAR-BY-POLITE-01, RU-GRAMMAR-BY-PAST-FORM-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Ask politely for a tea, as a woman. (*Ya by khotéla chay.*)
Then ask someone to speak slowly. (*Vy ne moglí by govorít médlenno?*)

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[RU-GRAMMAR-YESLI-BY-01, RU-GRAMMAR-U-MENYA-BYLO-01, RU-GRAMMAR-YESLI-FUTURE-01, RU-GRAMMAR-PERFECTIVE-FUTURE-01, RU-LEX-C53-THINGS-04] -->

Two lessons, and Russian can now say what is not so. **Бы** with the
*-л* form, whatever the time. **Я бы хотел** to ask for a thing, **вы не
могли бы** to ask for a favour. And two kinds of *если*: the future for a
plan, **бы** on both sides for a wish.

[PAUSE 2s] The same dream, two ways:

> **Если у меня будут деньги, я буду путешествовать.** — a plan.
> **Если бы у меня были деньги, я бы путешествовал.** — a wish.

*Деньги*, "money", is plural, so *будут* and *были*. *Путешествовать* is
imperfective, so its future is *буду* and the infinitive.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[RU-GRAMMAR-YESLI-BY-01, RU-GRAMMAR-YESLI-FUTURE-01, RU-LEX-ZAVISIT-OT-01, RU-GRAMMAR-OT-GENITIVE-01, RU-GRAMMAR-LI-NOT-YESLI-01, RU-LEX-MECHTAT-01, RU-LEX-C56-NATURE2-05, RU-LEX-C135-ACT135-05, RU-LEX-C71-SEASONS-04] -->

[PAUSE 1s]
- [YOU SAY: *Yésli by u menyá býli déngi, ya by puteshéstvoval.*]
- [YOU SAY: *Yésli by ya znála, ya by pomoglá.*]
- [YOU ANSWER: *Ty búdesh puteshéstvovat létom?* *Éto zavísit ot déneg*, with *déneg*, the "of" form of *déngi*; then one line with *yésli*]
- [YOU SAY: *Ya mechtáyu o móre. Yésli by u menyá býlo vrémya, ya by puteshéstvoval.*]
