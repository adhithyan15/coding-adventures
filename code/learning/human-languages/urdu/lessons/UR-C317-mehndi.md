## You'll want to know: مہندی
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C317-EID317-03]; assesses=[] -->

**مہندی** — *mehndī* — "henna".

> اس کے ہاتھوں پر مہندی تھی۔
>
> *us ke hāthoṅ par mehndī thī.*
>
> She had henna on her hands.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**مہندی** (*mehndī*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *mẽhdī*, the henna plant, under Sanskrit *mēndhī* (CDIAL 10328). It is feminine.
