Spanish, chapter 618: Supongamos que....
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
imaginar — to imagine.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for the priority, then the Spanish for the criterion.

You'll want to know: imaginar.
imaginar — "to imagine".
Imagina que ganas la lotería. Imagine that you win the lottery.
Me imagino que — I suppose that.

The word, taken apart.
imaginar is a learned borrowing from Latin imaginari 'to picture to oneself', built on imago 'image' (EtymDB).

Lesson 2 of 5.
la hipótesis — the hypothesis.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for the criterion, then the Spanish for to imagine.

You'll want to know: la hipótesis.
la hipótesis — "the hypothesis".
Es solo una hipótesis. It is only a hypothesis.
The same form in the plural: las hipótesis.

The word, taken apart.
hipótesis is a learned borrowing from Late Latin hypothesis, taken from Greek hypothesis 'a base, a supposition', from hypo 'under' and thesis 'a placing' (EtymDB).

You'll want to know: en teoría.
en teoría — "in theory".
En teoría, el tren llega a las ocho. In theory, the train arrives at eight.
It often goes with en la práctica.

The word, taken apart.
en teoría is built on teoría, from Late Latin theoria, taken from Greek theōria 'a looking at, contemplation' (EtymDB).

You'll want to know: posiblemente.
posiblemente — "possibly".

posiblemente is built on posible, a learned borrowing from Latin possibilis, built on posse 'to be able', with the ending -mente (EtymDB).

Lesson 5 of 5.
seguramente — most likely, probably.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for in theory, then the Spanish for possibly.

You'll want to know: seguramente.
seguramente — "most likely, probably".
Seguramente está en casa. She is probably at home.
It means probably, not surely: seguramente leaves room for doubt.

The word, taken apart.
seguramente is built on seguro and -mente; seguro is inherited from Latin securus 'free from care' (EtymDB).
