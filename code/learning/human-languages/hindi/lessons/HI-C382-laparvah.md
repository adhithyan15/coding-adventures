## You'll want to know: लापरवाह
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C382-JALDBAZI382-02]; assesses=[] -->

**लापरवाह** — *lāparvāh* — "careless".

> अगर ड्राइवर लापरवाह होगा तो दुर्घटना हो सकती है।
>
> *agar ḍrāivar lāparvāh hogā to durghaṭnā ho saktī hai.*
>
> If the driver is careless, there can be an accident.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**लापरवाह** (*lāparvāh*) is usually traced to Persian *lāparvā*, "heedless".
