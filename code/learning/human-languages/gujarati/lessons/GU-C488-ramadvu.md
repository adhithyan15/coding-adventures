## You'll want to know: રમાડવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C488-NAVDAV488-04]; assesses=[] -->

**રમાડવું** — *ramāḍvũ* — "to play with a child".

> સાંજે દાદા બાળકને બગીચામાં રમાડે છે.
>
> *sā̃je dādā bāḷakne bagīcāmā̃ ramāḍe che.*
>
> In the evening my grandfather plays with the baby in the garden.

Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે બાળકને રમાડ્યું, *teṇe bāḷakne ramāḍyũ*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**રમાડવું** (*ramāḍvũ*) is the causative of **રમવું** (*ramvũ*), "to play", which is inherited from Sanskrit *ramatē*, "takes pleasure in" (CDIAL 10628).
