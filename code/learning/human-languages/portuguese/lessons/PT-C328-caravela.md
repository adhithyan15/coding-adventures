## You'll want to know: a caravela
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C328-NAVEGAR-02]; assesses=[] -->

**a caravela** — "the caravel".

> A caravela era um barco leve e rápido.
>
> *The caravel was a light, fast ship.*

The small sailing ship of the age of discovery.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*caravela* comes down from Old Portuguese (EtymDB); it is usually traced through Late Latin *carabus* to Greek *kárabos* "a light boat".
