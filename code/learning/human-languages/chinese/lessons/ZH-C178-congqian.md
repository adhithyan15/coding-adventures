## You'll want to know: 从前
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C178-DAY178-03]; assesses=[] -->

**从前** — *cóngqián* — "once upon a time; in the past".

> **我从前常常喝茶** *wǒ cóngqián chángcháng hē chá* — I used to drink a lot of tea.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**从前** (*cóngqián*) is built from 从 'from' and 前 'before', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *dzjowng* and *dzen* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription): from before.
