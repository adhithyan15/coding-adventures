## You'll want to know: അഭയം
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C378-ABHAYAM378-01]; assesses=[] -->

**അഭയം** — *abhayaṁ* — "shelter; refuge".

> വെള്ളപ്പൊക്കത്തിൽ ആളുകൾ പള്ളിക്കൂടത്തിൽ അഭയം തേടി.
>
> *veḷḷappokkattil āḷukaḷ paḷḷikkūṭattil abhayaṁ tēṭi.*
>
> In the flood people took shelter in the school.

Literally freedom from fear: a safe place in a disaster.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**അഭയം** (*abhayaṁ*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *abhaya*, "fearless", and as a noun "safety" (CDIAL 507): *a-*, "without", and *bhaya*, "fear".
