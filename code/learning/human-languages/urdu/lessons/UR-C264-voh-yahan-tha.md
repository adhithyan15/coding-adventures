# وہ یہاں تھا (voh yahāṅ thā) — was, were, used to

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[UR-GRAMMAR-C264-PAST-01, UR-GRAMMAR-COPULA-FINAL, UR-GRAMMAR-AAP-HAIN] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "he went" and "she came". (*voh gayā*, *voh āī*.) Then "he is here". (*voh yahāṅ hai*.) Now put **ہے** (*hai*) into the past.

## You'll want to know: تھا, تھی, تھے
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-GRAMMAR-C264-WAS-01]; assesses=[UR-LEX-YAHAN, UR-LEX-C41-HEALTH41-02, UR-LEX-C61-HOME61-05, UR-LEX-KAHAN, UR-GRAMMAR-PAR] -->

> **وہ یہاں تھا۔**
>
> *voh yahāṅ thā.* — "He was here."

> **وہ یہاں تھی۔**
>
> *voh yahāṅ thī.* — "She was here."

**تھا** (*thā*) is **ہے** in the past. Like **گیا**, it follows the person:

> **میں گھر پر تھا۔**
>
> *maiṅ ghar par thā.* — "I was at home."

> **سارا بیمار تھی۔**
>
> *Sārā bīmār thī.* — "Sara was ill."

> **ہم اسکول میں تھے۔**
>
> *ham iskūl meṅ the.* — "We were at school."

| who | was |
|---|---|
| a man | **تھا** (*thā*) |
| a woman | **تھی** (*thī*) |
| men; **ہم**; **آپ** | **تھے** (*the*) |
| women | **تھیں** (*thīṅ*) |

> **کل آپ کہاں تھے؟**
>
> *kal āp kahāṅ the?* — "Where were you yesterday?"

## You'll want to know: what I used to do
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-GRAMMAR-C264-WAS-02]; assesses=[UR-LEX-JANA, UR-LEX-C89-DEF89-02, UR-LEX-C94-TIME94-01] -->

Keep the present **جاتا** (*jātā*) and put **تھا** where **ہے** stood. That is a habit in the past, "used to":

> **میں اسکول جاتا تھا۔**
>
> *maiṅ iskūl jātā thā.* — "I used to go to school."

> **وہ ہر روز آتی تھی۔**
>
> *voh har roz ātī thī.* — "She used to come every day."

Both halves follow the person: **جاتی تھی** (*jātī thī*) for a woman, **جاتے تھے** (*jāte the*) for men.
