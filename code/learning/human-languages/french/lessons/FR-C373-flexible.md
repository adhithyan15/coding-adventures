## You'll want to know: flexible
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C373-DISPONIBLE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**flexible** — "flexible".

> Mon patron est assez flexible sur les horaires.
>
> *My boss is quite flexible about hours.*

One form for both genders. Close to *souple*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*flexible* is a learned borrowing from Latin *flexibilis* "easily bent" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short), from *flectere* "to bend".
