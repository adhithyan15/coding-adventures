## You'll want to know: el taxista
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C641-TAXI-02]; assesses=[] -->

**el taxista** — "the taxi driver".

> El taxista conoce todas las calles.
>
> *The taxi driver knows every street.*

One form for a man or a woman: *el taxista*, *la taxista*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*taxista* is built on *taxi* with *-ista*, the ending for someone who does a job, which comes from Greek *-istḗs* through Latin.
