# 天气 (tiānqì) — weather

## You'll want to know: 天气
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C66-WEATHER66-01]; assesses=[] -->

**天气** — *tiānqì* — "weather".

> **今天天气很好** *jīntiān tiānqì hěn hǎo* — The weather is lovely today.

The new character, in pieces: 气 — a short fall, two short bars, and a long stroke that turns, slants out and hooks. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**天气** (*tiānqì*) is built from 天 'sky' and 气 'breath', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *then* and *khj+jH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription): the breath of the sky.
