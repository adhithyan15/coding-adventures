# भीती (bhītī) — fear

## You'll want to know: भीती
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C284-BHITI-01]; assesses=[MR-LEX-C102-ANIMALS102-01, MR-LEX-VATNE] -->

**भीती** — *bhītī* — "fear".

> **मला सापाची भीती वाटते.**
>
> *malā sāpācī bhītī vāṭte.* — "I am afraid of snakes."

What you fear takes **-ची**: **सापाची भीती**, "fear of snakes". The feeling **वाटते**, "is felt", and it is feminine, so the verb ends in **-ते**.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**भीती** (*bhītī*) is a tatsama: Sanskrit *bhīti*, "fear" (CDIAL 9512), from the root *bhī*, "to be afraid". Marathi writes it with a long final *ī*.
