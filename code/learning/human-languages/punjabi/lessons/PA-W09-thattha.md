# ਠ — the tongue curled back

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-SCRIPT-TA-01, PA-SCRIPT-DDA-01, PA-LEX-THIK-THAK] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Write **ਤ** once — the *t* made with the tongue against the teeth.
Then write **ਡ**, whose sound is made with the tongue curled back against the
roof. Say *ṭhīk-ṭhāk* aloud and notice that its *ṭh* belongs to the curled-back
family, not the teeth family.

## Script — one letter
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-SCRIPT-TTHA-01]; assesses=[PA-SCRIPT-TA-01] -->

Trace **ਠ** twice and say *ṭha*: tongue curled back, then a puff of breath.
Two small circles hanging from the top line, one above the other.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-SCRIPT-TTHA-01, PA-SCRIPT-TA-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: observe-trace -->

Cover the models. Write **ਤ**, then **ਠ**. Compare only where your tongue goes
for each one.
