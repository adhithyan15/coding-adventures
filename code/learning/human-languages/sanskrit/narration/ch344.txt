Sanskrit, chapter 344: Taking Sides.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
प्रतिपक्षः (pratipakṣaḥ) — the opposing side.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a listener, then the Sanskrit for sets out (a case).

You'll want to know: प्रतिपक्षः.
प्रतिपक्षः — pratipakṣaḥ — "the opposing side".
प्रतिपक्षस्य मतम् अन्यत् अस्ति। pratipakṣasya matam anyat asti. The other side's view is different.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
प्रतिपक्षः (pratipakṣaḥ) is built from prati, "against", and pakṣa, "a wing, a side"; MW gives prati-pakṣa "the opposite side, a hostile party" (MW).

Lesson 2 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for sets out (a case), then the Sanskrit for the opposing side.

You'll want to know: विरोधः.
विरोधः — virodhaḥ — "opposition".
नूतनस्य नियमस्य विरोधः भवति। nūtanasya niyamasya virodhaḥ bhavati. There is opposition to the new rule.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
विरोधः (virodhaḥ) is built on vi, "apart", and the root √rudh, "to obstruct"; MW gives vi-rodha "opposition, hostility, quarrel" (MW).

Lesson 3 of 5.
समर्थयति (samarthayati) — supports (a view).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for the opposing side, then the Sanskrit for opposition.

You'll want to know: समर्थयति.
समर्थयति — samarthayati — "supports (a view)".
अहं तव मतं समर्थयामि। ahaṃ tava mataṃ samarthayāmi. I support your view.
Past (he …): सः मम मतं समर्थितवान्, saḥ mama mataṃ samarthitavān.

The word, taken apart.
समर्थयति (samarthayati) is built on sam, "together", and artha, "aim"; MW gives sam-√arth the senses "to make fit" and "to judge, to consider", and "to support (a view)" is the sense modern Sanskrit gives it (MW).

अनुमन्यते — anumanyate — "consents; approves".
पिता अनुमन्यते, अतः अहं गमिष्यामि। pitā anumanyate, ataḥ ahaṃ gamiṣyāmi. Father consents, so I will go.

The word, taken apart.
अनुमन्यते (anumanyate) is built on anu, "after, along", and the root √man, "to think"; MW gives anu-mata "approved, assented to, permitted" (MW).

Lesson 5 of 5.
ननु (nanu) — is it not so; surely.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for supports (a view), then the Sanskrit for consents.

You'll want to know: ननु.
ननु — nanu — "is it not so; surely".
ननु एतत् उचितम् अस्ति? nanu etat ucitam asti? Surely this is right, is it not?

The word, taken apart.
ननु (nanu) is built from na, "not", and nu, "now"; MW gives nanu "not?, is it not?" and so "certainly" (MW).
