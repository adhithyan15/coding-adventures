## You'll want to know: enamorado
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C715-AMOR-03]; assesses=[] -->

**enamorado** — "in love".

> Están muy enamorados.
>
> *They are very much in love.*

*Estar enamorado de alguien* — to be in love with someone.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*enamorado* is built on *enamorar* "to make fall in love", from *en-* and *amor* "love" (EtymDB); *amor* comes from Latin *amor*.
