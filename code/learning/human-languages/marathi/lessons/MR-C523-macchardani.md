## You'll want to know: मच्छरदाणी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C523-GALICHA523-03]; assesses=[] -->

**मच्छरदाणी** — *macchardāṇī* — "a mosquito net".

> डास खूप होते, म्हणून आम्ही मच्छरदाणी लावली.
>
> *ḍās khūp hote, mhaṇūn āmhī macchardāṇī lāvlī.*
>
> There were lots of mosquitoes, so we put up a mosquito net.

Feminine.

मच्छरदाणी लावणे, to put up a net.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**मच्छरदाणी** (*macchardāṇī*) is a compound of मच्छर, "mosquito", and -दाणी, "a holder"; the origin of मच्छर is not settled.
