## You'll want to know: आतुरः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C482-CIKITSA482-02]; assesses=[] -->

**आतुरः** — *āturaḥ* — "a patient; sick".

> वैद्यः आतुरस्य नाडीं स्पृशति।
>
> *vaidyaḥ āturasya nāḍīṃ spṛśati.*
>
> The physician feels the patient's pulse.

A masculine or feminine noun, by the person meant.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**आतुरः** (*āturaḥ*) MW divides it *ā-tura* and gives "suffering, sick (in body or mind)"; it names no root, so its origin is uncertain (MW).
