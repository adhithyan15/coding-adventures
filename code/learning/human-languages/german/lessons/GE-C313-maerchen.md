## You'll want to know: das Märchen
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C313-MAERCHEN-01]; assesses=[] -->

**das Märchen** — "the fairy tale".

> Meine Oma erzählte uns jeden Abend ein Märchen.
>
> *My grandma told us a fairy tale every evening.*

Plural: *die Märchen*. They begin *Es war einmal* — once upon a time.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Märchen* is the diminutive of the old word *Mär* "news, tale", from Middle High German *mære* (EtymDB), with the ending *-chen*: a little tale.
