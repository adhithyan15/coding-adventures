## You'll want to know: तर्जयति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C288-APARADHA288-04]; assesses=[] -->

**तर्जयति** — *tarjayati* — "threatens".

> चौरः बालं तर्जयति।
>
> *cauraḥ bālaṃ tarjayati.*
>
> The thief threatens the boy.

Past (he …): सः बालं तर्जितवान्, *saḥ bālaṃ tarjitavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**तर्जयति** (*tarjayati*) comes from the root √tarj, "to threaten, scold"; *tarjayati* is its causative, which MW gives with the same sense (MW).
