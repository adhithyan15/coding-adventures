Kannada, chapter 386: Excuses and Explanations.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಸಮಜಾಯಿಷಿ (samajāyiṣi) — an explanation.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a tendency, then the Kannada for a source.

You'll want to know: ಸಮಜಾಯಿಷಿ.
ಸಮಜಾಯಿಷಿ — samajāyiṣi — "an explanation".
ತಡವಾಗಿ ಬಂದಿದ್ದಕ್ಕೆ ಅವನು ಸಮಜಾಯಿಷಿ ಕೊಟ್ಟನು. taḍavāgi bandiddakke avanu samajāyiṣi koṭṭanu. He gave an explanation for coming late.
ಸಮಜಾಯಿಷಿ (samajāyiṣi) ಕೊಡು, to explain oneself.

The word, taken apart.
ಸಮಜಾಯಿಷಿ (samajāyiṣi) is usually traced to Hindustani samjhāish, "an explanation", from samajhnā, "to understand"; DEDR and CDIAL do not list the Kannada form.

Lesson 3 of 5.
ಆಕಸ್ಮಿಕ (ākasmika) — accidental; sudden.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for an explanation, then the Kannada for a lame excuse.

You'll want to know: ಆಕಸ್ಮಿಕ.
ಆಕಸ್ಮಿಕ — ākasmika — "accidental; sudden".
ನಮ್ಮ ಭೇಟಿ ಆಕಸ್ಮಿಕವಾಗಿತ್ತು. namma bhēṭi ākasmikavāgittu. Our meeting was accidental.
ಆಕಸ್ಮಿಕವಾಗಿ, by chance.

The word, taken apart.
ಆಕಸ್ಮಿಕ (ākasmika) is borrowed from Sanskrit ākasmika, "causeless, unforeseen" (Monier-Williams), from akasmāt, "without a why".

Lesson 4 of 5.
ಬಿಂಬಿಸು (bimbisu) — to portray; to present as.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a lame excuse, then the Kannada for accidental.

You'll want to know: ಬಿಂಬಿಸು.
ಬಿಂಬಿಸು — bimbisu — "to portray; to present as".
ಅವನು ತನ್ನನ್ನು ಒಳ್ಳೆಯವನಂತೆ ಬಿಂಬಿಸಿದನು. avanu tannannu oḷḷeyavanante bimbisidanu. He made himself out to be a good man.
Past (I …): ಬಿಂಬಿಸಿದೆ, bimbiside.
As what: -ಅಂತೆ, like. ಬಿಂಬ, an image.

The word, taken apart.
ಬಿಂಬಿಸು (bimbisu) is built on Sanskrit bimba, "a round form; a form and its reflection, an image" (CDIAL 9242), with the Kannada verb ending -isu.
