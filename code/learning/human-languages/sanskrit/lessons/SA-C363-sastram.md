## You'll want to know: शास्त्रम्
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C363-ADHYAYANA363-03]; assesses=[] -->

**शास्त्रम्** — *śāstram* — "a branch of learning; a treatise".

> पण्डितः शास्त्रं पाठयति।
>
> *paṇḍitaḥ śāstraṃ pāṭhayati.*
>
> The scholar teaches a branch of learning.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**शास्त्रम्** (*śāstram*) comes from the root √śās, "to instruct, to order"; MW gives *śāstra* "teaching, instruction" and "any book or treatise" (MW).
