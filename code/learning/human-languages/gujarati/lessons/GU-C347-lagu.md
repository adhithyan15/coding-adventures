## You'll want to know: લાગુ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C347-KAYDO347-04]; assesses=[] -->

**લાગુ** — *lāgu* — "in force; applicable".

> નવો કાયદો આવતા મહિનાથી લાગુ થશે.
>
> *navo kāydo āvtā mahināthī lāgu thaśe.*
>
> The new law comes into force next month.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**લાગુ** (*lāgu*) CDIAL lists Gujarati *lāgu*, "near", under Sanskrit *lagna*, "sticking to, fixed on" (CDIAL 10893); "in force, applicable" is its sense in law and in offices.
