# Review — where I went, and where I was

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[UR-GRAMMAR-C264-PAST-01, UR-GRAMMAR-C264-WAS-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "he went" and "she was here". (*voh gayā*, *voh yahāṅ thī*.)

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[UR-GRAMMAR-C264-PAST-01, UR-GRAMMAR-C264-PAST-02, UR-GRAMMAR-C264-WAS-01, UR-GRAMMAR-C264-WAS-02, UR-LEX-C100-LOC100-04, UR-LEX-C57-FOOD57-05, UR-LEX-C41-HEALTH41-02, UR-LEX-C61-HOME61-05] -->

[PAUSE 2s each]
- [YOU SAY: Yesterday I went to the market. Sara went home.]
- [YOU SAY: He came, and then he sat down.]
- [YOU SAY: I didn't go. I was ill.]
- [YOU SAY: Where were you yesterday? We were at home.]
- [YOU SAY: I used to go to school every day.]

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[UR-GRAMMAR-C264-PAST-01, UR-GRAMMAR-C264-PAST-02, UR-GRAMMAR-C264-WAS-01, UR-GRAMMAR-C264-WAS-02] -->

[PAUSE 3s] **وہ گئی** or **وہ گیا**, for Sara? (**گئی**.) Now the other way round: what does **تھے** tell you about the people? (Men, or **ہم**, or **آپ**.) "I used to come"? (**میں آتا تھا**.) In every one, who decides the ending? (The one who went, or who was.)
