Arabic, chapter 121: Cabbage, Mint, Parsley, Thyme, Cumin.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ملفوف (malfūf) — a cabbage.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for lettuce, then the Arabic for an aubergine.
[pause 1 second]
Say "dog" and "cat." (Kalb, like Hebrew kelev; qiṭṭ, compared with Latin cattus.)

You'll want to know: ملفوف.
ملفوف — malfūf — "a cabbage".

What you've built.

Guided Practice.

[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does ملفوف (malfūf) mean? ("A cabbage".) Say it once more.
