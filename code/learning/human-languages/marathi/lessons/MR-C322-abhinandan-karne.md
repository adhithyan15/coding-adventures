# अभिनंदन करणे (abhinandan karṇe) — to congratulate

## You'll want to know: अभिनंदन करणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C322-PURASKAR322-04]; assesses=[] -->

**अभिनंदन करणे** — *abhinandan karṇe* — "to congratulate".

> सगळ्यांनी पुरस्कार मिळाल्याबद्दल तिचं अभिनंदन केलं.
>
> *sagaḷyānnī puraskār miḷālyābaddal ticaṁ abhinandan kelaṁ.*
>
> Everyone congratulated her on receiving the award.

Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने अभिनंदन केलं, *tyāne abhinandan kelaṁ*.

The person takes -चं: तिचं अभिनंदन केलं; the reason takes -बद्दल.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अभिनंदन करणे** (*abhinandan karṇe*) joins *abhinandan*, a tatsama taken from Sanskrit *abhinandana*, 'rejoicing at, praising' (Monier-Williams), to *karṇe*, 'to do'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
