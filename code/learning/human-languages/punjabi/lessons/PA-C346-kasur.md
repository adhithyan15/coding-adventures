## You'll want to know: ਕਸੂਰ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C346-SORRY346-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ਕਸੂਰ** — *kasūr* — "a fault; blame".

> ਇਹ ਮੇਰਾ ਕਸੂਰ ਨਹੀਂ ਹੈ।
>
> *eh merā kasūr nahīṁ hai.*
>
> This is not my fault.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਕਸੂਰ** (*kasūr*) is a loan, usually traced through Persian to Arabic *quṣūr*, "shortcoming".
