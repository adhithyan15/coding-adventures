## You'll want to know: l'officina
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C410-GUASTO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**l'officina** — "the garage (for repairs), the workshop".

> La macchina resta in officina tre giorni.
>
> *The car stays at the garage for three days.*

A workshop for repairs; *il garage* is where you keep the car.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*officina* is a learned borrowing from Latin *officīna* 'workshop' (EtymDB), a shortened *opificīna*, from *opifex* 'worker', built on *opus* 'work'.
