## You'll want to know: la fase
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C541-DURAR-01]; assesses=[] -->

**la fase** — "the phase, the stage".

> El proyecto está en la primera fase.
>
> *The project is in its first phase.*

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*fase* comes from Greek *phásis* 'an appearance' (EtymDB), the word for each new look of the moon.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.
