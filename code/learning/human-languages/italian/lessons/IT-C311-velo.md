## You'll want to know: il velo
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C311-SPOSARSI-03]; assesses=[] -->

**il velo** — "the veil".

> La sposa ha un velo lunghissimo.
>
> *The bride has a very long veil.*

*Il velo da sposa* — the bridal veil.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*velo* is inherited from Latin *vēlum* "a sail, a curtain" (EtymDB).
