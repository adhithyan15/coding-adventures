## You'll want to know: dificultar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C621-IMPIDE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**dificultar** — "to make difficult, to hinder".

> La nieve dificulta el viaje.
>
> *The snow makes the journey difficult.*

The opposite of *facilitar*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*dificultar* is a learned borrowing from Latin *difficultare* 'to make hard', built on *difficultas*, from *facilis* 'easy' (EtymDB).
