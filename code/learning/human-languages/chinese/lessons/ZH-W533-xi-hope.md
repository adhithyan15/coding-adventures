## Script — a cross, a bar and a long stroke, then three at the foot
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-SCRIPT-XI-HOPE-01]; assesses=[] -->

> 希

**Three pieces, top to bottom.** Count the pieces, not the strokes.

**The top piece, two strokes**: a short stroke falling to the left, then a short stroke falling to the right across it, a small cross.

**The middle piece, two strokes**: a long bar, then a long stroke falling to the left from above the bar down through it.

**The bottom piece, three strokes**: a short vertical on the left, a bar that turns down the right side and hooks to the left, then a long vertical down through the middle, past the foot.

2 + 2 + 3 = seven.
