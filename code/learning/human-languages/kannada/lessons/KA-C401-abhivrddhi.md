# ಅಭಿವೃದ್ಧಿ (abhivṛddhi) — development

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[KA-LEX-C400-BAJET400-04, KA-LEX-C400-BAJET400-05] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Kannada for to utilise, then the Kannada for to waste.

## You'll want to know: ಅಭಿವೃದ್ಧಿ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C401-ABHIVRIDDHI401-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ಅಭಿವೃದ್ಧಿ** — *abhivṛddhi* — "development".

> ಹೊಸ ರಸ್ತೆಯಿಂದ ಊರಿನ ಅಭಿವೃದ್ಧಿ ಆಯಿತು.
>
> *hosa rasteyinda ūrina abhivṛddhi āyitu.*
>
> The new road brought development to the town.

ಅಭಿವೃದ್ಧಿ ಹೊಂದು, to develop.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಅಭಿವೃದ್ಧಿ** (*abhivṛddhi*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *abhivṛddhi*, "growth, increase, prosperity" (Monier-Williams).
