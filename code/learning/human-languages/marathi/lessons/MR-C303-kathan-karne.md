# कथन करणे (kathan karṇe) — to narrate; to recount

## You'll want to know: कथन करणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C303-KATHA303-05]; assesses=[] -->

**कथन करणे** — *kathan karṇe* — "to narrate; to recount".

> साक्षीदाराने घडलेली घटना न्यायालयात कथन केली.
>
> *sākṣīdārāne ghaḍlelī ghaṭnā nyāyālayāt kathan kelī.*
>
> The witness recounted the event in court.

Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने कथन केलं, *tyāne kathan kelaṁ*.

Formal; in speech सांगणे. What is told agrees: घटना कथन केली.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**कथन करणे** (*kathan karṇe*) joins *kathan*, 'a telling', a tatsama taken from Sanskrit *kathana*, 'narration' (Monier-Williams), to *karṇe*, 'to do'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
