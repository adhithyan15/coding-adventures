## You'll want to know: mobilar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C530-SENHORIO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**mobilar** — "to furnish".

> Mobilámos a casa com móveis usados.
>
> *We furnished the house with second-hand furniture.*

In Brazil: *mobiliar*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*mobilar* is built on *mobília* "furniture", from Latin *mōbilis* "movable" (Lewis & Short), from *movēre* "to move".
