## You'll want to know: ડ્રાઈવર
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C207-TAXI207-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ડ્રાઈવર** — *ḍraivar* — "driver".

> **ડ્રાઈવરે ગાડી ધીમે ચલાવી.** — *ḍraivare gāḍī dhīme calāvī* — "The driver drove slowly."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ડ્રાઈવર** (*ḍraivar*) is a loan from English *driver*.
