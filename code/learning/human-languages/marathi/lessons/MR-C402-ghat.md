## You'll want to know: घाट
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C402-GHAT402-01]; assesses=[] -->

**घाट** — *ghāṭ* — "a ghat road; a mountain pass".

> बस हळूहळू घाट चढत होती.
>
> *bas haḷūhaḷū ghāṭ caḍhat hotī.*
>
> The bus was slowly climbing the ghat.

Masculine.

In the western hills a घाट is the steep road up the slope; on a river it is a flight of steps to the water.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**घाट** (*ghāṭ*) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi *ghāṭ*, "mountain pass, landing place", under Sanskrit *ghaṭṭa*, "landing place, quay" (CDIAL 4414), and notes a possible Dravidian source.
