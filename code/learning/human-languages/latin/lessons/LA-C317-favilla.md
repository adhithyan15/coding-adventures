## You'll want to know: favilla, favillae
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C317-INCENDIUM-03]; assesses=[] -->

**favilla, favillae** — "glowing ash, embers".

> Ventus favillam in tēcta vīcīna ferēbat.
>
> *The wind was carrying the glowing ash onto the roofs nearby.*

Feminine, mostly poetic: the hot ash that drifts and starts new fires.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**favilla** is linked by Lewis & Short with a root meaning "to shine", seen in Greek *phōs*, "light"; the link is not certain.
