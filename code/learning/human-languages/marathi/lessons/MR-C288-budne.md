## You'll want to know: बुडणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C288-GHATANA288-04]; assesses=[] -->

**बुडणे** — *buḍṇe* — "to sink; to drown".

> वादळात एक जुनी होडी नदीत बुडाली.
>
> *vādḷāt ek junī hoḍī nadīt buḍālī.*
>
> An old boat sank in the river during the storm.

Past (he …): तो बुडाला, *to buḍālā*.

The water takes -त: पाण्यात बुडणे, to sink in the water.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**बुडणे** (*buḍṇe*) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi *buḍṇe*, 'to be drowned', under *\*buḍyati*, 'sinks' (CDIAL 9272).
