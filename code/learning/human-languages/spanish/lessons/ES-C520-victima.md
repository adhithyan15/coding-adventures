## You'll want to know: la víctima
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C520-DELITO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**la víctima** — "the victim".

> La víctima era un hombre de cuarenta años.
>
> *The victim was a man of forty.*

Always feminine, even for a man: *la víctima*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*víctima* is a learned borrowing from Latin *victima* 'an animal offered in sacrifice' (EtymDB). The word moved from the altar to anyone who suffers harm.
