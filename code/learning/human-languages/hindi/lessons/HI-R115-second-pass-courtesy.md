# A visit, start to finish — a second pass

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[HI-LEX-C115-COMEIN-01, HI-LEX-C115-COMEIN-03] -->

[PAUSE 3s] A guest is at your door. Welcome them in, and hand them a cup of tea.

## Grammar Lens: the respectful ending
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[HI-LEX-C115-COMEIN-01, HI-LEX-C115-COMEIN-02, HI-LEX-C115-COMEIN-03] -->

| word | from |
|---|---|
| *āie* | come |
| **लीजिए** | take |
| **पधारिए** | arrive, formally |

One ending turns a verb into a polite request, as it did for **बोलिए**.

- [YOU SAY: you bumped into someone: beg their pardon; they answer "no problem"]
- [YOU SAY: congratulate a friend; then send them good wishes]
- [YOU SAY: thank someone for an invitation]
- [YOU SAY: a guest asks to sit: say "by all means"; offered more food, say a polite no]

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[HI-LEX-C114-SORRY-01, HI-LEX-C114-SORRY-02, HI-LEX-C114-SORRY-03, HI-LEX-C114-SORRY-04, HI-LEX-C114-SORRY-05, HI-LEX-C115-COMEIN-01, HI-LEX-C115-COMEIN-02, HI-LEX-C115-COMEIN-03, HI-LEX-C115-COMEIN-04, HI-LEX-C115-COMEIN-05] -->

[PAUSE 3s] An apology? (**माफ़ी**.) No problem? (**कोई बात नहीं**.) Congratulations?
(**बधाई**.) Good wishes? (**शुभकामनाएँ**.) An invitation? (**निमंत्रण**.) Come in? (*āie*.)
Please come, formally? (**पधारिए**.) Here you are? (**लीजिए**.) By all means? (**शौक़ से**.)
A polite no? (**जी नहीं**.)
