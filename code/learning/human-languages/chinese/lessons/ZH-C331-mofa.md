## You'll want to know: mófǎ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C331-MAGIC331-03]; assesses=[] -->

*mófǎ* — "magic".

> *nàge shénxiān huì mófǎ* — That immortal can do magic.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*mófǎ* is built from *mó* 'a demon' and *fǎ* 'a method', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *ma* and *pjop* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'a demon' and the second 'a rule' and 'a norm'.
