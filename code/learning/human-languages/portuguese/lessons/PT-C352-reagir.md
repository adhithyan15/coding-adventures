## You'll want to know: reagir
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C352-REACAO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**reagir** — "to react".

> Ele reagiu mal à notícia.
>
> *He reacted badly to the news.*

*Reagir a* — to react to. Spelling: *reajo, reages, reage*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*reagir* is a learned formation on Latin *re-* "back" and *agere* "to do, to act" (EtymDB, Lewis & Short).
