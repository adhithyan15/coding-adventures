# വരൾച്ച (varaḷcca) — a drought

## You'll want to know: വരൾച്ച
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C514-VARALCA514-01]; assesses=[] -->

**വരൾച്ച** — *varaḷcca* — "a drought".

> വരൾച്ച കാരണം കർഷകർ ബുദ്ധിമുട്ടി.
>
> *varaḷcca kāraṇaṁ karṣakar buddhimuṭṭi.*
>
> The farmers suffered because of the drought.

The noun of വരളുക, to dry up.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**വരൾച്ച** (*varaḷcca*) is native Dravidian, the noun of വരളുക, to grow dry (DEDR 5320).
