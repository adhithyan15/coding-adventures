## You'll want to know: ふせぐ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C279-KAZAN279-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ふせぐ** — *fusegu* — "to prevent; to keep off".

> **みんなで じこを ふせいで ください。** — *minna de jiko o fuseide kudasai* — "Please all help to prevent accidents."

Polite **ふせぎます**, *fusegimasu*; a godan verb. Past (polite): **かさが あめを ふせぎました。**, *kasa ga ame o fusegimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ふせぐ** (*fusegu*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict).
