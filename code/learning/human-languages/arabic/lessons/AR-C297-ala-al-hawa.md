# على الهواء (ʿalā al-hawāʾ) — on air, live

## You'll want to know: على الهواء
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C297-IDHAA297-05]; assesses=[] -->

**على الهواء** — *ʿalā al-hawāʾ* — "on air, live".

> تكلم الوزير على الهواء مساء أمس.
>
> *takallama al-wazīr ʿalā al-hawāʾ masāʾa ams.*
>
> The minister spoke on air yesterday evening.

على الهواء مباشرة: live on air.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**على الهواء** (*ʿalā al-hawāʾ*) is *ʿalā*, "on", with *hawāʾ*, "air", which is built on the native Arabic root *h-w-y* (Lane). The phrase is usually traced to the English broadcasting phrase on the air.
