Sanskrit, chapter 114: Sheep, Pigeon, Owl, Heron, Bee.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
मेषः (meṣaḥ) — a sheep.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a bull, then the Sanskrit for a buffalo.

You'll want to know: मेषः.
मेषः — meṣaḥ — "a sheep".
