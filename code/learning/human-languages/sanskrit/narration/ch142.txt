Lesson 1 of 5.
तुष्यति (tuṣyati) — is pleased, is satisfied.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for touches, then the Sanskrit for leaves.

You'll want to know: तुष्यति.
तुष्यति — tuṣyati — "is pleased, is satisfied".

The word, taken apart.
tuṣyati comes from the root √tuṣ "become calm, be satisfied, be pleased" (CDIAL 5897), inherited from Indo-European (EtymDB). santoṣa "contentment" comes from the same root.

The word, taken apart.
śrāmyati comes from the root √śram "toil, exert oneself, grow tired" (CDIAL 12693), already in the Rigveda. śrama "labour, fatigue" comes from the same root, and so does śramika "a labourer".

Lesson 3 of 5.
रचयति (racayati) — composes, makes.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for is pleased, then the Sanskrit for gets tired.

You'll want to know: रचयति.
रचयति — racayati — "composes, makes".

The word, taken apart.
racayati comes from the root √rac "produce, fashion, arrange" (MW), a class-10 root known from the grammarians' Dhātupāṭha. MW gives no outside cousins for it, and racanā "a composition" comes from the same root.

Lesson 4 of 5.
दर्शयति (darśayati) — shows.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for gets tired, then the Sanskrit for composes.

You'll want to know: दर्शयति.
दर्शयति — darśayati — "shows".

The word, taken apart.
darśayati is the causative of the root √dṛś "see": "makes see", so "shows" (CDIAL 6210). MW compares √dṛś with Greek derkomai "look", and darśana "sight" comes from the same root.

You'll want to know: पूजयति.
पूजयति — pūjayati — "worships".

The word, taken apart.
pūjayati comes from the root √pūj "honour, worship" (MW; CDIAL 8316), and pūjā "worship" is the noun beside it. Its deeper origin is not settled.
