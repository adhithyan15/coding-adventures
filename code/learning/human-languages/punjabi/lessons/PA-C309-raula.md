## You'll want to know: ਰੌਲਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C309-NEWS309-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ਰੌਲਾ** — *raulā* — "a din; a lot of noise".

> ਹਾਦਸੇ ਤੋਂ ਬਾਅਦ ਸੜਕ ਉੱਤੇ ਬਹੁਤ ਰੌਲਾ ਸੀ।
>
> *hādse ton bāad saṛak utte bahut raulā sī.*
>
> After the accident there was a lot of noise on the road.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਰੌਲਾ** (*raulā*) is inherited from Sanskrit *rava*, "a roar, a cry"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *raulā*, "noise", under it (CDIAL 10641).
