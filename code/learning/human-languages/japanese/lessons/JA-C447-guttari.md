## You'll want to know: ぐったり
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C447-TIRED447-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ぐったり** — *guttari* — "worn out; limp".

> **なつに ながく あるいて、ぐったりしました。** — *natsu ni nagaku aruite, guttari shimashita* — "I walked a long way in summer and was worn out."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ぐったり** (*guttari*) is a native Japanese mimetic word, inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, and its older origin is not settled (JMdict).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
