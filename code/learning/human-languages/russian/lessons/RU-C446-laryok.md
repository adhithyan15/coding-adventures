## You'll want to know: ларёк
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C446-VITRINA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ларёк** (*laryók*) — "a stall, a booth".

> В ларьке у остановки продают пирожки.
>
> *V larké u ostanóvki prodayút pirozhkí.*
>
> The stall by the bus stop sells pies.

Masculine; the ё drops out: *ларька*, *ларьки*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ларёк* is a diminutive of *ларь* 'a chest, a bin', which came through Old East Slavic *larĭ* from Old Norse *lárr* 'a chest, a drawer' (EtymDB).
