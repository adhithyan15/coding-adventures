## You'll want to know: 国画
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C502-PAINT502-02]; assesses=[] -->

**国画** — *guóhuà* — "traditional Chinese painting".

> *wǒ juéde guóhuà bǐ yóuhuà yǒuyìsi* — I find Chinese painting more interesting than oil painting.

The new character, in pieces: 画 — eight strokes: a bar on top, then five strokes as on top of 男 *nán*, then an open box of two strokes around them. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**国画** (*guóhuà*) is built from 国 'a country' and 画 'a painting', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *kwok* and *hweaH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'a state'.
