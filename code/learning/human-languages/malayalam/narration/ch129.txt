Malayalam, chapter 129: Things You Carry.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
കിടക്ക (kiṭakka) — a bed.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for the bathroom, then the Malayalam for the front yard.

You'll want to know: കിടക്ക.
കിടക്ക (kiṭakka) — "a bed", the first thing in a മുറി.

What you've built.
Where the sleeping happens.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: kiṭakka]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: kiṭakka, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say kiṭakka]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Malayalam for the bathroom, then the Malayalam for the front yard, then say kiṭakka again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does കിടക്ക (kiṭakka) mean? ("A bed".) Say it once more.
