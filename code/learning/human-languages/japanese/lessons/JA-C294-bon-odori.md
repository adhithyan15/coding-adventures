## You'll want to know: ぼんおどり
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C294-MIKOSHI294-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ぼんおどり** — *bon'odori* — "a Bon festival dance".

> **なつには 日本中で ぼんおどりが あります。** — *natsu ni wa nihonjū de bon'odori ga arimasu* — "In summer there is festival dancing all over Japan."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ぼんおどり** (*bon'odori*) is a mixed word, native and Sino-Japanese: *bon*, "lantern festival", is a Chinese character read with its on'yomi, a reading borrowed from Chinese (Middle Chinese *bwon*), and *odori*, "dance", is native Japanese (JMdict, KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
