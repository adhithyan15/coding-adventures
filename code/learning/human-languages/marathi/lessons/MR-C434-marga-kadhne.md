# मार्ग काढणे (mārga kāḍhṇe) — to find a way; to get round a problem

## You'll want to know: मार्ग काढणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C434-GAIRSOY434-05]; assesses=[] -->

**मार्ग काढणे** — *mārga kāḍhṇe* — "to find a way; to get round a problem".

> पुलावर कोंडी होती, पण चालकाने मार्ग काढला.
>
> *pulāvar koṇḍī hotī, paṇ cālakāne mārga kāḍhlā.*
>
> The bridge was jammed, but the driver found a way through.

Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने मार्ग काढला, *tyāne mārga kāḍhlā*.

Also to solve a problem: अडचणीतून मार्ग काढणे.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**मार्ग काढणे** (*mārga kāḍhṇe*) joins **मार्ग**, "a way", a tatsama from Sanskrit *mārga* (Monier-Williams), to **काढणे**, "to take out".
