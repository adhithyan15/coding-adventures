# كبد (kabid) — the liver

## You'll want to know: كبد
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C504-BATN504-01]; assesses=[] -->

**كبد** — *kabid* — "the liver".

> أكل جدي كبد الخروف.
>
> *akala jaddī kabid al-kharūf.*
>
> My grandfather ate the sheep's liver.

Plural: أكباد, *akbād*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**كبد** (*kabid*) is built on the native Arabic root *k-b-d*, "the liver" (Lane). The word is old; its plural is akbād.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
