## You'll want to know: পথচারী
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C392-TRAFFIC392-02]; assesses=[] -->

**পথচারী** — *pôthchārī* — "a pedestrian".

> পথচারীরা মোড়ে রাস্তা পার হল।
>
> *pôthchārīrā moṛe rāstā pār hôlô.*
>
> The pedestrians crossed the road at the corner.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**পথচারী** (*pôthchārī*) is built from **পথ**, "a way", and *-cārin*, "moving, going about" (Monier-Williams for both): one who goes on foot along the way.
