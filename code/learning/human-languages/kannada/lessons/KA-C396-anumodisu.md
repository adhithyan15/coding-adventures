# ಅನುಮೋದಿಸು (anumōdisu) — to endorse

## You'll want to know: ಅನುಮೋದಿಸು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C396-MASUDE396-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ಅನುಮೋದಿಸು** — *anumōdisu* — "to endorse".

> ಸಭೆಯಲ್ಲಿ ಎಲ್ಲರೂ ಅವಳ ಸಲಹೆಯನ್ನು ಅನುಮೋದಿಸಿದರು.
>
> *sabheyalli ellarū avaḷa salaheyannu anumōdisidaru.*
>
> At the meeting everyone endorsed her suggestion.

Past (I …): ಅನುಮೋದಿಸಿದೆ, *anumōdiside*.

ಅನುಮೋದನೆ, approval.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಅನುಮೋದಿಸು** (*anumōdisu*) is built on Sanskrit *anumodana*, "applauding, assent" (Monier-Williams), with the Kannada verb ending -*isu*.

## What you've built

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
