## You'll want to know: عاقبة
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C414-NATIJA414-04]; assesses=[] -->

**عاقبة** — *ʿāqiba* — "a consequence".

> لم يفكر الرجل في العاقبة.
>
> *lam yufakkir ar-rajul fī al-ʿāqiba.*
>
> The man did not think about the consequence.

Plural: عواقب, *ʿawāqib*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**عاقبة** (*ʿāqiba*) is built on the native Arabic root *ʿ-q-b*, "to come after, to follow" (Lane). An *ʿāqiba* is what comes after a thing: its end, its consequence.
