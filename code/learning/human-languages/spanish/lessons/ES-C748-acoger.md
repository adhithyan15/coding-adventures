## You'll want to know: acoger
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C748-NIETOS-02]; assesses=[] -->

**acoger** — "to take in, to foster; to welcome".

> Acogieron a dos niños durante un año.
>
> *They fostered two children for a year.*

Spelling: *acojo*. *Una familia de acogida* — a foster family.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*acoger* is inherited from a spoken Latin *accolligere* (EtymDB), built on *colligere* "to gather": to gather someone in.
