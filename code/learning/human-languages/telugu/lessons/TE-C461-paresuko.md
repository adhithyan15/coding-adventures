# పారేసుకో (pārēsukō) — to mislay; to lose by carelessness

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[TE-LEX-C461-SAMANU461-01, TE-LEX-C461-SAMANU461-02] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Telugu for a cardboard box, then the Telugu for to leave behind.

## You'll want to know: పారేసుకో
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C461-SAMANU461-03]; assesses=[] -->

**పారేసుకో** — *pārēsukō* — "to mislay; to lose by carelessness".

> అతను స్టేషన్లో పర్సు పారేసుకున్నాడు.
>
> *atanu sṭēṣanlō parsu pārēsukunnāḍu.*
>
> He lost his purse at the station.

Past (I …): పారేసుకున్నాను, *pārēsukunnānu*.

పారేయు, to throw away, + -కో: to lose something of one's own by carelessness.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**పారేసుకో** (*pārēsukō*) is a Telugu verb built on పారేయు, "to throw away", a compound whose first part has an origin that is not settled and whose second part is వేయు, "to put" (DEDR 5549), with -కో, "for oneself" (DEDR 2151).
