Lesson 1 of 5.
il pedaggio — a toll.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for a parcel, then the Italian for a postcard.

You'll want to know: il pedaggio.
il pedaggio — "a toll".

The word, taken apart.
pedaggio from Medieval Latin pedaticum, a toll for passing, built on pes, "foot" — taken from Medieval Latin. (EtymDB 2.1).
