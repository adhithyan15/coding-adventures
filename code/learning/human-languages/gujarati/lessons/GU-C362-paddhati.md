## You'll want to know: પદ્ધતિ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C362-SANSHODHAN362-03]; assesses=[] -->

**પદ્ધતિ** — *paddhati* — "a method".

> આ પદ્ધતિથી કામ ઝડપી બને છે.
>
> *ā paddhatithī kām jhaḍpī bane che.*
>
> This method makes the work go faster.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**પદ્ધતિ** (*paddhati*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *paddhati*, "a path, a way, a course" (Monier-Williams).
