## You'll want to know: افزایش یافتن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C373-AFZAYESH-02]; assesses=[] -->

**افزایش یافتن** (*afzâyesh yâftan*) — "to increase, to go up".

> تعداد دانشجوها هر سال افزایش مییابد.
>
> *te'dâd-e dâneshjuhâ har sâl afzâyesh miyâbad.*
>
> The number of students goes up every year.

Formal; *yâftan* "to find" has the present stem *yâb-*. In speech, *ziyâd shodan*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**افزایش یافتن** is native Persian, built from *afzâyesh* "increase" with *yâftan* "to find", which in formal Persian turns a noun of change into a verb.
