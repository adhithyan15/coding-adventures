## You'll want to know: पर्याय
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C349-UPAY349-04]; assesses=[] -->

**पर्याय** — *paryāy* — "an alternative; an option".

> आमच्याकडे दुसरा पर्याय नव्हता.
>
> *āmacyākaḍe dusrā paryāy navhatā.*
>
> We had no other option.

Masculine.

X ला पर्याय नाही, there is no alternative to X.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पर्याय** (*paryāy*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *paryāya*, "turning round; succession, turn" (Monier-Williams). A turn taken in place of another became an alternative.
