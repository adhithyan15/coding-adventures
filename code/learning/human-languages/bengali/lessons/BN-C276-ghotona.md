## You'll want to know: ঘটনা
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C276-GHOTONA276-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ঘটনা** — *ghôṭônā* — "an event; an incident".

> আমরা কী করব, সেটা ঘটনার ওপর নির্ভর করে।
>
> *āmrā kī kôrbô, seṭā ghôṭônār opôr nirbhôr kôre.*
>
> What we do will depend on the incident.

ঘটনা ঘটে, an event takes place: একটা ঘটনা ঘটল, something happened.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ঘটনা** (*ghôṭônā*) is Sanskrit *ghaṭanā*, taken over whole as a tatsama; Monier-Williams gives it senses from effort and motion to a taking effect. It sits beside the verb ঘটা, to happen.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
