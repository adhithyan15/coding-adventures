## You'll want to know: ഹർജി
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C426-NIVEDANAM426-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ഹർജി** — *harji* — "a petition (to a court)".

> അവർ കോടതിയിൽ ഒരു ഹർജി നൽകി.
>
> *avar kōṭatiyil oru harji nalki.*
>
> They filed a petition in court.

Plural: ഹർജികൾ.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ഹർജി** (*harji*) is usually traced to Hindustani *arzī*, "a petition", which came from Arabic through Persian; where the Malayalam initial ഹ- comes from is not settled.
