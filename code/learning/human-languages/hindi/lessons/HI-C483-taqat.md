## You'll want to know: ताक़त
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C483-TAQAT483-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ताक़त** — *tāqat* — "strength; power".

> दूध पीने से बच्चों को ताक़त मिलती है।
>
> *dūdh pīne se baccoṁ ko tāqat miltī hai.*
>
> Drinking milk gives children strength.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ताक़त** (*tāqat*) is usually traced to Arabic *ṭāqa*, "ability, strength", which reached Hindi through Persian.
