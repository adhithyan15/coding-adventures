## You'll want to know: creativo
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C766-RETO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**creativo** — "creative".

> Es una persona muy creativa.
>
> *She is a very creative person.*

*Un creativo* also means someone who works in advertising.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*creativo* is a learned borrowing from Late Latin *creātīvus* (EtymDB), built on *creāre* "to create".
