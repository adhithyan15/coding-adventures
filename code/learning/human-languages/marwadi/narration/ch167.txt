Marwadi (Marwari), chapter 167: The Day's Work.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
काम (kām) — work.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for to lie down, then the Marwadi for dusk.

You'll want to know: काम.
काम — kām — "work".
हूं रोज घणो काम करूं हूं। hū̃ roj ghaṇo kām karū̃ hū̃. I do a lot of work every day.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
काम (kām) is inherited from Sanskrit karman, "act, work"; CDIAL lists Marwari kām (CDIAL 2892). With करणो it is to work: काम करो.

Lesson 2 of 5.
पैलो (pailo) — first.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for dusk, then the Marwadi for work.

You'll want to know: पैलो.
पैलो — pailo — "first".
पैलो काम घणो दोरो है। pailo kām ghaṇo doro hai. The first job is very hard.

The word, taken apart.
पैलो (pailo) is inherited from a reconstructed prathila, "first"; CDIAL lists Old Marwari pahalo (CDIAL 8652). It agrees like an adjective: पैली रोटी, the first roti.

Lesson 3 of 5.
छांटणो (chhā̃ṭṇo) — to sprinkle.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for work, then the Marwadi for first.

You'll want to know: छांटणो.
छांटणो — chhā̃ṭṇo — "to sprinkle".
दिनूगै मां पाणी छांटै है। dinūgai mā̃ pāṇī chhā̃ṭai hai. Early in the morning my mother sprinkles water.
Past (I …): म्हैं पाणी छांट्यो, mhaĩ pāṇī chhā̃ṭyo.

The word, taken apart.
छांटणो (chhā̃ṭṇo) comes from a reconstructed chaṇṭ, "scatter"; CDIAL lists Gujarati chā̃ṭvũ, "to sprinkle" (CDIAL 4970). CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry.

Lesson 4 of 5.
छेलो (chhelo) — last.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for first, then the Marwadi for to sprinkle.

You'll want to know: छेलो.
छेलो — chhelo — "last".
छेलो काम सोरो है। chhelo kām soro hai. The last job is easy.

The word, taken apart.
छेलो (chhelo) is inherited from Sanskrit cheda, "a piece cut off; a stopping"; CDIAL lists Old Marwari chehalaü, "last, final" (CDIAL 5064). It agrees like पैलो (pailo): छेली बात, the last thing.

Lesson 5 of 5.
झट (jhaṭ) — at once.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for to sprinkle, then the Marwadi for last.

You'll want to know: झट.
झट — jhaṭ — "at once".
मां झट रोटी बणावै है। mā̃ jhaṭ roṭī baṇāvai hai. Mother makes the rotis in no time.

The word, taken apart.
झट (jhaṭ) comes from a reconstructed jhaṭ, "a sudden movement"; CDIAL lists Hindi and Gujarati jhaṭ, "quickly" (CDIAL 5327). CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry.
