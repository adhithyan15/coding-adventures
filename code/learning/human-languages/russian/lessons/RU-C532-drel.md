## You'll want to know: дрель
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C532-DIY-04]; assesses=[] -->

**дрель** (*drel*) — "a power drill".

> У нас есть дрель, но нет свёрл.
>
> *U nas yest drel, no net svyorl.*
>
> We've got a drill but no drill bits.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*дрель* is usually traced to Dutch or German, where *drillen* means 'to bore'; EtymDB has no entry.
