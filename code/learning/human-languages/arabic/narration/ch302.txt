Arabic, chapter 302: An interview.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
حوار (ḥiwār) — a dialogue, a conversation.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a wounded person, then the Arabic for to give first aid.

You'll want to know: حوار.
حوار — ḥiwār — "a dialogue, a conversation".
نشرت المجلة حوارا مع الكاتب. nasharat al-majalla ḥiwāran maʿa al-kātib. The magazine published a conversation with the writer.
In the press a حوار (ḥiwār) is an interview printed as questions and answers.

The word, taken apart.
حوار (ḥiwār) is built on the native Arabic root ḥ-w-r (Lane), "to go back, to return". Speech that goes back and forth is a ḥiwār, the verbal noun of Form III.

Lesson 3 of 5.
أجرى (ajrā) — to conduct, to carry out.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a dialogue, then the Arabic for to hold a dialogue.

You'll want to know: أجرى.
أجرى — ajrā — "to conduct, to carry out".
أجرت الصحفية مقابلة مع الطبيب. ajrat aṣ-ṣaḥafiyya muqābala maʿa aṭ-ṭabīb. The journalist did an interview with the doctor.
Present: يجري, yujrī.
أجرى (ajrā) مقابلة, to conduct an interview; أجرى (ajrā) عملية, to perform an operation.

The word, taken apart.
أجرى (ajrā) is built on the native Arabic root j-r-y (Lane), "to run, to flow", the root of jarā, to run. It is Form IV, "to make a thing run": to carry it through.

استضاف — istaḍāfa — "to host, to have as a guest".
استضافت المدرسة كاتبا معروفا. istaḍāfat al-madrasa kātiban maʿrūfan. The school hosted a well-known writer.
Present: يستضيف, yastaḍīf.

The word, taken apart.
استضاف (istaḍāfa) is built on the native Arabic root ḍ-y-f (Lane), the root of the taught ḍayf, a guest. It is Form X, first "to ask someone for protection or refuge"; today a programme or a city that hosts.

Warm-up.

You'll want to know: متحدث.
متحدث — mutaḥaddith — "a spokesperson; a speaker".
قال متحدث إن المصنع مغلق اليوم. qāla mutaḥaddith inna al-maṣnaʿ mughlaq al-yawm. A spokesman said that the factory is closed today.
متحدث (mutaḥaddith) باسم الشركة: a spokesman for the company.

The word, taken apart.
متحدث (mutaḥaddith) is built on the native Arabic root ḥ-d-th (Lane), the root of the taught ḥadīth, talk. Mutaḥaddith is the Form V participle, "one who speaks".
