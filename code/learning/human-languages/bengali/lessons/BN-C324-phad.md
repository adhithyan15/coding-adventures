## You'll want to know: ফাঁদ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C324-KOUSHOL324-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ফাঁদ** — *phā̃d* — "a trap".

> লোকেরা ইঁদুর ধরতে একটা ফাঁদ বানাল।
>
> *lokerā ĩdur dhôrte ekṭā phā̃d bānālô.*
>
> The people made a trap to catch the mice.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ফাঁদ** (*phā̃d*) comes down from Sanskrit *spāśa*, 'a fetter, a noose' (CDIAL 13813, which lists Bengali *phā̃d*, 'a net'); **ফাঁসানো**, to frame, is its neighbour.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
