## You'll want to know: strepō, strepere
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C448-STERTO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**strepō, strepere** — "to make a noise, to clatter, to roar".

> In viā plaustra strepunt, et nēmō dormīre potest.
>
> *In the street the wagons clatter, and nobody can sleep.*

*strepō, strepere, strepuī, strepitum*. Any loud noise: wheels, voices, water.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

The origin of *strepō* is not settled: Lewis & Short give its senses, to rattle, rumble, roar, but no etymology, and EtymDB gives no parent.
