## You'll want to know: いきのこる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C268-KYUUJO268-03]; assesses=[] -->

**いきのこる** — *ikinokoru* — "to survive; to come through".

> **じこで ふたりが いきのこりました。** — *jiko de futari ga ikinokorimashita* — "Two people came through the accident alive."

Polite **いきのこります**, *ikinokorimasu*; a godan verb. Past (polite): **いぬが いきのこりました。**, *inu ga ikinokorimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**いきのこる** (*ikinokoru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, built from *iki*, "living" (from *ikiru*), and *nokoru*, "to remain" (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
