Persian, chapter 122: Whale, Dolphin, Shark, Squirrel, Bat.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
نهنگ (nahang) — a whale.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for an insect, then the Persian for a giraffe.

You'll want to know: نهنگ.
نهنگ — nahang — "a whale".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: nahang]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: nahang, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say nahang]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Persian for an insect, then the Persian for a giraffe, then say nahang again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does نهنگ (nahang) mean? ("A whale".) Say it once more.
