## You'll want to know: cruciale
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C371-RILEVANTE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**cruciale** — "crucial".

> Il primo anno di lavoro è cruciale.
>
> *The first year of work is crucial.*

*Un momento cruciale* — a turning point.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*cruciale* is usually traced to French *crucial*, built on Latin *crux, crucis* "a cross" (Lewis & Short): the point where two ways cross and you must choose one.
