## You'll want to know: காமாலை
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C477-NOYKAL477-05]; assesses=[] -->

**காமாலை** — *kāmālai* — "jaundice".

> காமாலை வந்ததால் அவனுடைய கண்கள் மஞ்சளாக இருந்தன.
>
> *kāmālai vantatāl avaṉuṭaiya kaṇkaḷ mañcaḷāka iruntaṉa.*
>
> Because he had jaundice, his eyes were yellow.

Often மஞ்சள் காமாலை, yellow jaundice.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**காமாலை** (*kāmālai*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *kāmalā*, "jaundice" (CDIAL 3044).
