## You'll want to know: la rafale
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C462-ORAGE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**la rafale** — "the gust (of wind); the burst".

> Une rafale de vent a emporté son chapeau.
>
> *A gust of wind blew his hat away.*

*Des rafales à cent kilomètres-heure*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

The origin of *rafale* is not settled. It is usually linked to Italian *raffica* 'gust'; EtymDB lists it without a source.
