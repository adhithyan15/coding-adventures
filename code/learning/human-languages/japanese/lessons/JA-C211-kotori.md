## You'll want to know: ことり
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C211-ANIMAL211-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ことり** — *kotori* — "a small bird".

> **あさ ことりが なきます。** — *asa kotori ga nakimasu* — "Small birds sing in the morning."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ことり** (*kotori*) is native Japanese (wago), built from *ko*, "small", and *tori*, "bird", both inherited rather than borrowed (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
