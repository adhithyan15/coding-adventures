## You'll want to know: विरुद्ध
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C347-VIRODH347-05]; assesses=[] -->

**विरुद्ध** — *viruddha* — "against; opposite".

> आमचं मत तुमच्या मताच्या अगदी विरुद्ध आहे.
>
> *āmcaṁ mat tumacyā matācyā agdī viruddha āhe.*
>
> Our view is the exact opposite of yours.

After -च्या or attached: तुमच्या विरुद्ध, संघाविरुद्ध, against the team.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**विरुद्ध** (*viruddha*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *viruddha*, "opposed, hindered" (CDIAL 11864).
