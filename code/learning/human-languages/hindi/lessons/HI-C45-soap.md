## You'll want to know: साबुन
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C45-ASK-05]; assesses=[] -->

**साबुन** (*sābun*) — "soap".

Arabic *ṣābūn*, reaching Hindi through Persian — and behind the Arabic sits Latin *sāpō*, which the Romans had themselves borrowed from their northern neighbours, who used the stuff on their hair before anyone used it for washing.

So the word went north to south, south to east, and arrived here. English "soap" and साबुन are the same ancient word arriving by two different roads, one short and one very long.
