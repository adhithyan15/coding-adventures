## You'll want to know: そそぐ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C179-DRINK179-03]; assesses=[] -->

**そそぐ** — *sosogu* — "to pour; to sprinkle".

> **ゆのみに おちゃを そそぎます。** — *yunomi ni ocha o sosogimasu* — "I pour tea into a teacup."

Polite **そそぎます**, *sosogimasu*; a godan verb.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**そそぐ** (*sosogu*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
