Russian, chapter 68: Day, Week, Month, Year, Evening.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
день (den) — a day.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for cheap, then the Russian for badly.

You'll want to know: день.
день (den) — "a day".

What you've built.
One more word for day, week, month, year, evening.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: den]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: den, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say den]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Russian for cheap, then the Russian for badly, then say den again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does день (den) mean? ("A day".) Say it once more.
