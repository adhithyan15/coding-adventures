## You'll want to know: سور
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C322-QALA322-03]; assesses=[] -->

**سور** — *sūr* — "a city wall".

> حول المدينة القديمة سور طويل.
>
> *ḥawla al-madīna al-qadīma sūr ṭawīl.*
>
> There is a long wall round the old city.

Plural: أسوار, *aswār*.

A wall round a garden or a school is also سور; the wall of a room is جدار.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**سور** (*sūr*) is built on the native Arabic root *s-w-r* (Lane), where Lane gives *sūr* as the wall of a town, and *sāra*, to climb over it.
