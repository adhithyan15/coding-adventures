## You'll want to know: persuader
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C335-DEBAT-05]; assesses=[] -->

**persuader** — "to persuade".

> J'ai essayé de persuader mon père d'acheter une nouvelle voiture.
>
> *I tried to persuade my father to buy a new car.*

*Persuader quelqu'un de faire quelque chose*. Close to *convaincre*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*persuader* comes from Latin *persuādēre* "to bring over by talking, to convince" (Lewis & Short), through Middle French (EtymDB). It is built from *per-* and *suādēre* "to advise".
