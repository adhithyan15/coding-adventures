# ಕೋಲಾಹಲ (kōlāhala) — an uproar; a commotion

## You'll want to know: ಕೋಲಾಹಲ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C339-GALABHE339-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ಕೋಲಾಹಲ** — *kōlāhala* — "an uproar; a commotion".

> ಗೆದ್ದ ಸುದ್ದಿ ಕೇಳಿ ಮೈದಾನದಲ್ಲಿ ಕೋಲಾಹಲ ಎದ್ದಿತು.
>
> *gedda suddi kēḷi maidānadalli kōlāhala edditu.*
>
> Hearing of the win, an uproar rose in the ground.

An uproar "rises": ಕೋಲಾಹಲ ಏಳು. It can be joyful or angry.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಕೋಲಾಹಲ** (*kōlāhala*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *kolāhala*, "a loud and confused sound, uproar" (Monier-Williams), a word that imitates its own noise.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
