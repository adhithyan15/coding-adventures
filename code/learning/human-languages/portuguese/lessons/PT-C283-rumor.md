## You'll want to know: o rumor
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C283-BOATO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**o rumor** — "the murmur, a low noise; a rumour".

> Ouvia-se o rumor do mar.
>
> *You could hear the murmur of the sea.*

In news it can also mean a rumour, like *o boato*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*rumor* comes from Latin *rūmor* "a noise, common talk, a rumour" (EtymDB).
