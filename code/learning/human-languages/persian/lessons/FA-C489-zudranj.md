## You'll want to know: زودرنج
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C489-MANNERS-03]; assesses=[] -->

**زودرنج** (*zudranj*) — "touchy, easily hurt".

> او زودرنج است و با کوچکترین حرفی ناراحت میشود.
>
> *u zudranj ast va bâ kuchektarin harfi nârâhat mishavad.*
>
> She's touchy and gets upset at the smallest remark.

*zud* 'quickly' + *ranj* 'hurt', the noun behind *ranjidan*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*zudranj* is built on two native Persian words: *zud*, 'quickly', and *ranj*, 'suffering', the noun behind *ranjidan*: quick to be hurt. EtymDB has no entry for it.
