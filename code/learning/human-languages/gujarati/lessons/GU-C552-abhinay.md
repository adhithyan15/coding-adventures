## You'll want to know: અભિનય
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C552-MANCH552-02]; assesses=[] -->

**અભિનય** — *abhinay* — "acting".

> જો તારો અભિનય સારો હશે તો તું ફિલ્મોમાં કામ કરી શકીશ.
>
> *jo tāro abhinay sāro haśe to tũ philmomā̃ kām karī śakīś.*
>
> If your acting is good, you'll be able to work in films.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**અભિનય** (*abhinay*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *abhinaya*, the showing of a feeling by look and gesture: acting (Monier-Williams).
