## You'll want to know: la troupe
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C514-THEATRE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**la troupe** — "(theatre) company, troupe; troop".

> La troupe joue la même pièce depuis deux ans.
>
> *The company has been playing the same play for two years.*

Also a group of soldiers or animals.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*la troupe* is usually traced to Frankish *thorp* "a gathering, a village" (EtymDB), a Germanic word.
