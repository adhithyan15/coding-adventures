## You'll want to know: माल
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C432-MAAL432-01]; assesses=[] -->

**माल** — *māl* — "goods; stock".

> दुकान में नया माल आया है।
>
> *dukān meṁ nayā māl āyā hai.*
>
> New stock has come into the shop.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**माल** (*māl*) is usually traced to Arabic *māl*, "property, wealth", through Persian; in Hindi it is what a shop has to sell. मालगाड़ी is a goods train.
