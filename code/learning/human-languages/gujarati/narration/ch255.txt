Gujarati, chapter 255: Stories, poems and films.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
વાર્તા (vārtā) — story.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for flute, then the Gujarati for tabla drum.

You'll want to know: વાર્તા.
વાર્તા — vārtā — "story".
દાદીએ અમને વાર્તા કહી. — dādīe amne vārtā kahī — "Grandmother told us a story."

The word, taken apart.
વાર્તા (vārtā) comes from Sanskrit vārttā, "livelihood; tidings", in a learned shape, a semi-tatsama (CDIAL 11564). CDIAL lists the inherited vāt, "news", from the same word.

Lesson 2 of 5.
કવિતા (kavitā) — poem, poetry.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for tabla drum, then the Gujarati for story.

You'll want to know: કવિતા.
કવિતા — kavitā — "poem, poetry".
મેં શાળામાં એક કવિતા વાંચી. — mẽ śāḷāmā̃ ek kavitā vā̃cī — "I read a poem at school."

The word, taken apart.
કવિતા (kavitā) is Sanskrit kavitā, "poetry", taken over whole as a tatsama. It is built on kaví, "wise man, poet" (CDIAL 2964).

Lesson 3 of 5.
નવલકથા (navalkathā) — novel.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for story, then the Gujarati for poem.

You'll want to know: નવલકથા.
નવલકથા — navalkathā — "novel".
તેણે મોટી નવલકથા વાંચી. — teṇe moṭī navalkathā vā̃cī — "He read a long novel."

The word, taken apart.
નવલકથા (navalkathā) is a modern learned compound built from naval, "new, wonderful", and Sanskrit kathā, "conversation; story" (CDIAL 2704).
