## You'll want to know: बाल्यम्
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C494-LALANA494-05]; assesses=[] -->

**बाल्यम्** — *bālyam* — "childhood".

> बाल्ये अहं ग्रामे अवसम्।
>
> *bālye ahaṃ grāme avasam.*
>
> In childhood I lived in the village.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**बाल्यम्** (*bālyam*) is built on **बालः**, "a child", by lengthening its vowel and adding *-ya*; MW gives "boyhood, childhood, infancy" (MW).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
