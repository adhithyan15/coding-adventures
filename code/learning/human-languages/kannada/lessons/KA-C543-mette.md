# ಮೆತ್ತೆ (mette) — a cushion; a soft mattress

## You'll want to know: ಮೆತ್ತೆ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C543-GRHA543-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ಮೆತ್ತೆ** — *mette* — "a cushion; a soft mattress".

> ಅಜ್ಜ ಸೋಫಾದ ಮೇಲೆ ಮೆತ್ತೆ ಇಟ್ಟು ಕುಳಿತರು.
>
> *ajja sōphāda mēle mette iṭṭu kuḷitaru.*
>
> Grandfather put a cushion on the sofa and sat down.

Soft like the word ಮೆತ್ತಗೆ, softly.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಮೆತ್ತೆ** (*mette*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 5068 lists Kannada *mette*, "bedding, mattress".

## What you've built

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
