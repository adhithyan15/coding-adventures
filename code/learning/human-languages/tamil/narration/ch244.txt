Tamil, chapter 244: Postage stamp, Parcel, Form, Document, Passport.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
அஞ்சல் தலை (añcal talai) — a postage stamp.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for a fragrance, then the Tamil for a cucumber.

You'll want to know: அஞ்சல் தலை.
அஞ்சல் தலை — añcal talai — "a postage stamp".

The word, taken apart.
அஞ்சல் தலை (añcal talai) is native: añcal, "letter post" (DEDR 54), and talai, "head" (DEDR 3103). Early stamps carried the ruler's head, so a stamp became "the post head".

Lesson 5 of 5.
கடவுச்சீட்டு (kaṭavuccīṭṭu) — a passport.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for a form, then the Tamil for a document.

You'll want to know: கடவுச்சீட்டு.
கடவுச்சீட்டு — kaṭavuccīṭṭu — "a passport".

The word, taken apart.
கடவுச்சீட்டு (kaṭavuccīṭṭu) is a modern coinage: native kaṭavu, "passing, way through", from kaṭa, "to cross" (DEDR 1109), and cīṭṭu, "ticket, slip": "the crossing ticket". The origin of cīṭṭu is uncertain.
