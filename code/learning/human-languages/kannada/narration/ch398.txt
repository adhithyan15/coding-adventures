Kannada, chapter 398: Protest and Struggle.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಪ್ರತಿಭಟಿಸು (pratibhaṭisu) — to protest.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a community, then the Kannada for to unite.

You'll want to know: ಪ್ರತಿಭಟಿಸು.
ಪ್ರತಿಭಟಿಸು — pratibhaṭisu — "to protest".
ವಿದ್ಯಾರ್ಥಿಗಳು ಶುಲ್ಕ ಏರಿಕೆಯನ್ನು ಪ್ರತಿಭಟಿಸಿದರು. vidyārthigaḷu śulka ērikeyannu pratibhaṭisidaru. The students protested the rise in fees.
Past (I …): ಪ್ರತಿಭಟಿಸಿದೆ, pratibhaṭiside.
ಪ್ರತಿಭಟನೆ, a protest.

The word, taken apart.
ಪ್ರತಿಭಟಿಸು (pratibhaṭisu) is built on Sanskrit pratibhaṭa, "a match for, rivalling" (Monier-Williams), with the Kannada verb ending -isu: to stand up against.

Lesson 2 of 5.
ಹೋರಾಟ (hōrāṭa) — a struggle; a fight.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to unite, then the Kannada for to protest.

You'll want to know: ಹೋರಾಟ.
ಹೋರಾಟ — hōrāṭa — "a struggle; a fight".
ನೀರಿಗಾಗಿ ಹಳ್ಳಿಯವರ ಹೋರಾಟ ಹತ್ತು ವರ್ಷ ನಡೆಯಿತು. nīrigāgi haḷḷiyavara hōrāṭa hattu varṣa naḍeyitu. The villagers' struggle for water went on for ten years.
From ಹೋರಾಡು, to fight.

The word, taken apart.
ಹೋರಾಟ (hōrāṭa) is a native Dravidian compound, the noun beside ಹೋರಾಡು: pōr, "to fight, to strive" (DEDR 4540), whose p- became h-, and āṭa, "moving, playing", from āḍu (DEDR 347).

Lesson 5 of 5.
ಕಾರ್ಯಕರ್ತ (kāryakarta) — an activist.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a petition, then the Kannada for to mobilise.

You'll want to know: ಕಾರ್ಯಕರ್ತ.
ಕಾರ್ಯಕರ್ತ — kāryakarta — "an activist".
ಕಾರ್ಯಕರ್ತರು ಬೀದಿಯಲ್ಲಿ ಮರ ನೆಟ್ಟರು. kāryakartaru bīdiyalli mara neṭṭaru. The activists planted trees in the street.
Plural: ಕಾರ್ಯಕರ್ತರು, kāryakartaru.
Built from the Sanskrit words for work and for a doer.

The word, taken apart.
ಕಾರ್ಯಕರ್ತ (kāryakarta) is borrowed from Sanskrit kāryakartṛ, "one who works in the interest of another" (Monier-Williams).
