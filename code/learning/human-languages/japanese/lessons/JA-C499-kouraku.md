## You'll want to know: こうらく
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C499-PARK499-05]; assesses=[] -->

**こうらく** — *kōraku* — "a pleasure trip; an outing".

> **あきは こうらくの きせつです。** — *aki wa kōraku no kisetsu desu* — "Autumn is the season for outings."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**こうらく** (*kōraku*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *kō*, "going" (Middle Chinese *haeng*) and *raku*, "comfort" (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
