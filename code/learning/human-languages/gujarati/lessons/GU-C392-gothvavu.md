## You'll want to know: ગોઠવાવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C392-SAJJ392-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ગોઠવાવું** — *goṭhvāvũ* — "to fall into place".

> જો બધા મદદ કરશે તો બધું જલદી ગોઠવાઈ જશે.
>
> *jo badhā madad karśe to badhũ jaldī goṭhvāī jaśe.*
>
> If everyone helps, everything will soon fall into place.

Past (he …): તે નવા ઘરમાં ગોઠવાઈ ગયો, *te navā gharmā̃ goṭhvāī gayo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ગોઠવાવું** (*goṭhvāvũ*) is the *-āvũ* partner of **ગોઠવવું** (*goṭhavvũ*), "to arrange", whose own history is not settled.
