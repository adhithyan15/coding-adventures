## You'll want to know: apearse
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C651-TREN-05]; assesses=[] -->

**apearse** — "to get off (a train or bus), to step down".

> Nos apeamos en la próxima estación.
>
> *We are getting off at the next station.*

A formal word heard in announcements; in speech people say *bajarse*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*apearse* is usually traced to a Vulgar Latin verb *appedāre* "to set foot down", built on Latin *pēs, pedis* "a foot" (Lewis & Short).
