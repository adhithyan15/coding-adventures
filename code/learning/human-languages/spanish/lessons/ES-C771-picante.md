## You'll want to know: picante
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C771-SABOR-02]; assesses=[] -->

**picante** — "spicy, hot".

> No lo como porque es demasiado picante.
>
> *I don't eat it because it is too spicy.*

*Picante* is spicy-hot; hot in temperature is *caliente*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*picante* is built on *picar* 'to sting, to prick'. *Picar* is usually traced to spoken Latin *\*pīccāre* 'to sting, to strike', the form EtymDB gives for its French cousin *piquer*. Spicy food stings the tongue.
