## You'll want to know: नादान
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C474-AQL474-03]; assesses=[] -->

**नादान** — *nādān* — "naive; innocent".

> वह बच्चा अभी नादान है, ख़तरा नहीं समझता।
>
> *vah baccā abhī nādān hai, khatrā nahīṃ samajhtā.*
>
> That child is still naive; he doesn't understand danger.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**नादान** (*nādān*) is usually traced to Persian *nādān*, "not knowing", built on *nā-*, "not", and *dān*, "knowing". Hindi uses it kindly, for someone too young to know.
