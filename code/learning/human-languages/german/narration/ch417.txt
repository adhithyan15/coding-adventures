German, chapter 417: Kontrolle und Zoll.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
die Sicherheitskontrolle — the security check.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for the hand luggage, then the German for to give up.

You'll want to know: die Sicherheitskontrolle.
die Sicherheitskontrolle — "the security check".
An der Sicherheitskontrolle muss er seinen Gürtel ausziehen. At the security check he has to take off his belt.
Plural: die Sicherheitskontrollen.

The word, taken apart.
Sicherheitskontrolle is built from Sicherheit 'safety, security', from sicher, which goes back to Latin sēcūrus (Lewis & Short), and Kontrolle 'check', borrowed from French contrôle (EtymDB).

Lesson 2 of 5.
durchsuchen — to search (a bag, a place).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to give up, then the German for the security check.

You'll want to know: durchsuchen.
durchsuchen — "to search (a bag, a place)".
Eine Beamtin durchsucht ihren Rucksack. An official searches her rucksack.
Inseparable: sie durchsucht, sie hat durchsucht.

The word, taken apart.
durchsuchen is built from durch 'through' and suchen 'to look for', inherited from Old High German suohhen and Proto-Germanic sōkijaną (EtymDB). English seek is a cousin.

Lesson 3 of 5.
der Zoll — the customs; the duty (tax).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for the security check, then the German for to search.

You'll want to know: der Zoll.
der Zoll — "the customs; the duty (tax)".
Beim Zoll fragt ein Beamter nach Zigaretten. At customs an officer asks about cigarettes.
Plural: die Zölle, for the taxes. Der Zoll is also an inch, a different word.

The word, taken apart.
Zoll comes from Old High German zol (EtymDB), and it is usually traced to Late Latin tolōneum 'toll house', from Greek telṓnion. EtymDB instead links it with a Germanic word for what is counted, so the line is hedged. English toll is a cousin.

Wir haben nichts zu verzollen. We have nothing to declare.
Nichts zu verzollen — nothing to declare, the green exit.

The word, taken apart.
verzollen is built from ver- and Zoll 'customs duty', from Old High German zol (EtymDB), a word usually traced to Late Latin tolōneum.

Lesson 5 of 5.
der Reisepass — the passport.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for the customs, then the German for to declare.

You'll want to know: der Reisepass.
der Reisepass — "the passport".
Für diese Reise braucht sie einen Reisepass. For this trip she needs a passport.
Plural: die Reisepässe. Often shortened to der Pass.

The word, taken apart.
Reisepass is built from Reise 'journey', inherited from Middle High German reise (EtymDB), and Pass 'pass, passport', which came through Italian passaporto from Latin passus 'step' (EtymDB).
