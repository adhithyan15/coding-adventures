## You'll want to know: सिद्धान्तः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C365-SIDDHANTA365-01]; assesses=[] -->

**सिद्धान्तः** — *siddhāntaḥ* — "a principle; a theory".

> सिद्धान्तः सरलः, किन्तु प्रयोगः कठिनः।
>
> *siddhāntaḥ saralaḥ, kintu prayogaḥ kaṭhinaḥ.*
>
> The principle is simple, but putting it into practice is hard.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सिद्धान्तः** (*siddhāntaḥ*) is built from *siddha*, "established", and *anta*, "an end"; MW gives *siddhānta* "an established end" and "a demonstrated conclusion" (MW).
