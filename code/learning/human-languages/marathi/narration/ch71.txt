Marathi, chapter 71: Stomach, Shoulder, Finger, Neck, Face.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
पोट (poṭ) — the stomach.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a tooth, then the Marathi for the throat.

You'll want to know: पोट.
पोट — poṭ — "the stomach".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: poṭ]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: poṭ, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say poṭ]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Marathi for a tooth, then the Marathi for the throat, then say poṭ again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does पोट (poṭ) mean? ("The stomach".) Say it once more.
