## You'll want to know: ಮೃತ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C330-DIKKI330-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ಮೃತ** — *mṛta* — "the deceased; dead".

> ಅಪಘಾತದಲ್ಲಿ ಮೃತರಾದವರ ಹೆಸರುಗಳು ಪತ್ರಿಕೆಯಲ್ಲಿ ಬಂದವು.
>
> *apaghātadalli mṛtarādavara hesarugaḷu patrikeyalli bandavu.*
>
> The names of those who died in the accident came out in the paper.

A news word: ಮೃತರಾದರು, they died; ಮೃತರ ಕುಟುಂಬ, the family of the dead.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಮೃತ** (*mṛta*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *mṛta*, "dead, deceased" (Monier-Williams), from the root *mṛ*, "to die".
