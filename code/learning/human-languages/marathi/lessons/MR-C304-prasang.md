## You'll want to know: प्रसंग
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C304-VARNAN304-02]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रसंग** — *prasaṅg* — "an occasion; an episode".

> शाळेतला तो प्रसंग मी कधीच विसरणार नाही.
>
> *śāḷetlā to prasaṅg mī kadhīc visarṇār nāhī.*
>
> I will never forget that episode at school.

Masculine.

प्रसंगी, on occasion.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रसंग** (*prasaṅg*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *prasaṅga*, 'attachment; an occasion, a juncture' (Monier-Williams).
