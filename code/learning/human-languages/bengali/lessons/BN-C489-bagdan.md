# বাগদান (bāgdān) — an engagement (to marry)

## You'll want to know: বাগদান
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C489-SONGSAR489-01]; assesses=[] -->

**বাগদান** — *bāgdān* — "an engagement (to marry)".

> গত মাসে মীরার বাগদান হল।
>
> *gôtô māse mīrār bāgdān hôlô.*
>
> Mira got engaged last month.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**বাগদান** (*bāgdān*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *vāgdāna*, "a gift by word of mouth", the promise of a bride in marriage (Monier-Williams).
