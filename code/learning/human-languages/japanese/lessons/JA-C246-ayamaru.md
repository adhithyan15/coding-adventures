## You'll want to know: あやまる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C246-ANGER246-03]; assesses=[] -->

**あやまる** — *ayamaru* — "to apologize".

> **おとうとは いもうとに あやまりますか。** — *otōto wa imōto ni ayamarimasu ka* — "Does my younger brother apologize to my younger sister?"

Polite **あやまります**, *ayamarimasu*; a godan verb.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**あやまる** (*ayamaru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
