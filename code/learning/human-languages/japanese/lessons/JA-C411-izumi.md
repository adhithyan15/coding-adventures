## You'll want to know: いずみ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C411-OGAWA411-02]; assesses=[] -->

**いずみ** — *izumi* — "a spring (of water)".

> **やまの いずみの みずは つめたいです。** — *yama no izumi no mizu wa tsumetai desu* — "The water from the mountain spring is cold."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**いずみ** (*izumi*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict).
