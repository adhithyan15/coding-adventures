## You'll want to know: parier
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C500-CARTES-03]; assesses=[] -->

**parier** — "to bet".

> Il a parié dix euros sur son équipe.
>
> *He bet ten euros on his team.*

*Je parie que…* — I bet that…

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*parier* comes from Late Latin *pariāre* "to make equal, to match", built on *pār* "equal" (Lewis & Short): two sides put up equal stakes.
