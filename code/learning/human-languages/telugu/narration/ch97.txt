Telugu, chapter 97: Laughing, Crying, Worrying.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
నవ్వు (navvu) — a laugh, a smile.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a birthday, then the Telugu for a signature.

You'll want to know: నవ్వు.
నవ్వు (navvu) — "a laugh", and "a smile": Telugu uses one word for both.
నవ్వండి — "smile!", with the polite ending, what a photographer says.

The word, taken apart.
నవ్వు (navvu) is native Dravidian, one word with Tamil naku and Kannada nagu (DEDR 3569). DEDR lists Telugu navvu, "to laugh, smile; laughter".

What you've built.
A laugh and a smile, in one word.

Lesson 2 of 5.
ఏడుపు (ēḍupu) — crying, tears.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a signature, then the Telugu for a laugh.

You'll want to know: ఏడుపు.
ఏడుపు (ēḍupu) — "crying".
నాకు ఏడుపు (ēḍupu) వచ్చింది — "I felt like crying", literally "crying came to me", on the pattern of కోపం వచ్చింది.

The word, taken apart.
DEDR 282 lists ఏడుపు (ēḍupu), "weeping, crying", with the verb ēḍucu, "to cry", beside Tamil aḻu and Kannada aḻu, "to weep", but puts a query against the Telugu forms.

What you've built.
Crying, which comes to you like anger does.

Lesson 3 of 5.
విచారం (vicāraṁ) — regret, sorrow.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a laugh, then the Telugu for crying.

You'll want to know: విచారం.
విచారం (vicāraṁ) — "regret, sorrow".
బాధ is pain; విచారం (vicāraṁ) is the sorrow of looking back. విచారంగా ఉంది — "I am sorry about it", "I regret it".

The word, taken apart.
విచారం (vicāraṁ) is Sanskrit vicāra, "pondering", taken over whole as a learned loan, a tatsama, with the Telugu noun ending -ṁ (CDIAL 11683). The sense "regret" is the one Telugu gives it.

What you've built.
A sorrow about something that has happened.

Lesson 4 of 5.
ఆందోళన (āndōḷana) — anxiety, worry.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for crying, then the Telugu for regret.

You'll want to know: ఆందోళన.
ఆందోళన (āndōḷana) — "anxiety, worry".
ఆందోళన (āndōḷana) పడకండి — "don't worry". Telugu falls into worry: పడు is "to fall".

The word, taken apart.
ఆందోళన (āndōḷana) is Sanskrit āndolana, "swinging, agitation", from andolayati, "swings" (CDIAL 384), taken over as a learned loan.

What you've built.
A way to tell someone not to worry.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ఒంటరిగా (oṇṭarigā) — alone.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for regret, then the Telugu for worry.

You'll want to know: ఒంటరిగా.
ఒంటరిగా (oṇṭarigā) — "alone".
నేను ఒంటరిగా (oṇṭarigā) ఉన్నాను — "I am alone". The -గా ending again, on a word for "single, on one's own".

The word, taken apart.
ఒంటరిగా (oṇṭarigā) is native Dravidian: oṇṭari, "one who is alone", which DEDR 990 lists under the root for "one", followed by the adverb ending -gā.

What you've built.
Laughing, crying, regret, worry, alone: a fuller answer to how are you.
