## You'll want to know: подвозить
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C411-POPUTCHIK-03]; assesses=[] -->

**подвозить** (*podvozít*) — "to give (someone) a lift".

> Каждое утро сосед подвозит меня до работы.
>
> *Kázhdoye útro soséd podvózit menyá do rabóty.*
>
> Every morning the neighbour gives me a lift to work.

Imperfective; perfective *подвезти* (*подвезу, подвезёшь*). Present *подвожу, подвозишь*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*подвозить* is built from *под-* 'up to' and *возить* 'to carry by vehicle'. *Возить* is inherited from Proto-Slavic *voziti* 'to cart, to convey' (EtymDB).
