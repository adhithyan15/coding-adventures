## You'll want to know: अक़्ल
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C474-AQL474-01]; assesses=[] -->

**अक़्ल** — *aql* — "sense; wits".

> अरुण छोटा है, लेकिन उसकी अक़्ल बहुत तेज़ है।
>
> *Aruṇ choṭā hai, lekin uskī aql bahut tez hai.*
>
> Arun is young, but he's very sharp-witted.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अक़्ल** (*aql*) is usually traced to Arabic *ʿaql*, "reason, intelligence", which reached Hindi through Persian.
