## You'll want to know: o direito
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C363-CIDADAO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**o direito** — "the right; law (as a subject)".

> Todas as crianças têm direito à educação.
>
> *All children have a right to education.*

*Ter direito a* — to have a right to. *Estudar Direito* — to study law. Also the adjective right: *a mão direita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*direito* is inherited from Latin *dīrēctus* "straight", the participle of *dīrigere* "to set straight" (Lewis & Short), built on *regere* "to guide, to rule"; *-ct-* became *-it-* as in *feito*.
