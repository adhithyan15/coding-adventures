## You'll want to know: سمت
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C408-SIMT408-01]; assesses=[] -->

**سمت** — *simt* — "a direction".

> اسٹیشن کس سمت میں ہے؟
>
> *isṭeshan kis simt meṅ hai?*
>
> Which direction is the station in?

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**سمت** (*simt*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *samt*, "a way, a direction".
