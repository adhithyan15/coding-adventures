## You'll want to know: fēixíng
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C416-STORM416-05]; assesses=[] -->

*fēixíng* — "flight; flying (of an aircraft)".

> *cóng zhèr zuò fēijī qù Běijīng fēixíng shíjiān shì liǎng ge xiǎoshí* — Flying from here to Beijing takes two hours.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*fēixíng* is built from 飞 'to fly' and *xíng* 'to go', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *pj+j* and *haeng* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'to walk, to go'.
