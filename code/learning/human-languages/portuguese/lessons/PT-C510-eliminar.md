## You'll want to know: eliminar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C510-RESULTADO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**eliminar** — "to knock out, to eliminate".

> Fomos eliminados nos quartos de final.
>
> *We were knocked out in the quarter-finals.*

*Ser eliminado* — to be knocked out.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*eliminar* is a learned borrowing from Latin *ēlīmināre* "to put out of doors" (EtymDB), built on *līmen* "a threshold".
