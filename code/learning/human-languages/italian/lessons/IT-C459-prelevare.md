## You'll want to know: prelevare
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C459-BANCA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**prelevare** — "to withdraw (money)".

> Devo prelevare al bancomat.
>
> *I need to withdraw cash at the cash machine.*

*Prelevare il sangue* — to take a blood sample.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*prelevare* is a learned borrowing from Late Latin *praelevāre* 'to lift first', from *levāre* 'to lift' (Lewis & Short).
