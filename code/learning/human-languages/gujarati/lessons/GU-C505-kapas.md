## You'll want to know: કપાસ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C505-RENTIYO505-01]; assesses=[] -->

**કપાસ** — *kapās* — "the cotton plant".

> અમારા ગામનાં ખેતરોમાં ઘણો કપાસ ઊગે છે.
>
> *amārā gāmnā̃ khetromā̃ ghaṇo kapās ūge che.*
>
> A lot of cotton grows in our village's fields.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**કપાસ** (*kapās*) is a tadbhava, inherited from Sanskrit *karpāsa*, "the cotton plant", and CDIAL lists the Gujarati word (CDIAL 2877).
