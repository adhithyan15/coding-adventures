## You'll want to know: ગાદી
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C318-RAJA318-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ગાદી** — *gādī* — "a throne".

> રાજાના મૃત્યુ પછી તેનો દીકરો ગાદી પર બેઠો.
>
> *rājānā mṛtyu pachī teno dīkro gādī par beṭho.*
>
> After the king's death his son took the throne.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ગાદી** (*gādī*) comes from *\*garda*, a form CDIAL reconstructs, which lists Gujarati *gādī*, "a pad, a raised seat" (CDIAL 4053): the cushioned seat of a ruler. **ગાદી પર બેઠો**, he came to the throne.
