## You'll want to know: dēverticulum, dēverticulī
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C445-DEVERTICULUM-01]; assesses=[] -->

**dēverticulum, dēverticulī** — "a roadside inn, a lodging; a side road".

> Nox venit, itaque viātōrēs in dēverticulum intrant.
>
> *Night is coming, so the travellers go into a roadside inn.*

Second declension, neuter: *dēverticulum, dēverticulī n.* A place you turn off the road for: first a side road, then the inn at its end.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*dēverticulum* is built on *dēvertō* 'to turn aside' with the suffix *-culum* 'place for' (EtymDB; Lewis & Short): a place you turn aside to. *dēvertō* comes from *vertō* 'to turn'.
