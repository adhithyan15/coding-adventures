## You'll want to know: ओखळी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C184-OKHLI184-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ओखळी** — *okhḷī* — "a mortar".

> ओखळी मांय मिरच है।
>
> *okhḷī mā̃y mirach hai.*
>
> There are chillies in the mortar.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ओखळी** (*okhḷī*) is inherited from Sanskrit *ulūkhala*, "a wooden mortar"; CDIAL lists Hindi *okhlī*, "a small wooden mortar" (CDIAL 2360). CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry. Its pestle is the **मूसळ** you know.
