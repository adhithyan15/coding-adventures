# ēvādō, ēvādere — to get away, to come out

## You'll want to know: ēvādō, ēvādere
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C322-FUGA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ēvādō, ēvādere** — "to get away, to come out".

> Nauta ex undīs ad lītus ēvāsit.
>
> *The sailor got out of the waves to the shore.*

*ēvādō, ēvādere, ēvāsī, ēvāsum*: *ē* + *vādō*, 'to go'. English *evade*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ēvādō** is built on *vādō*, "to go, to rush", with *ex-*, "out" (EtymDB). English *evade* comes from it.
