# અનિદ્રા (anidrā) — sleeplessness

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-LEX-C476-NINDAR476-03, GU-LEX-C476-NINDAR476-04] -->

## You'll want to know: અનિદ્રા
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C476-NINDAR476-05]; assesses=[] -->

**અનિદ્રા** — *anidrā* — "sleeplessness".

> ચિંતાને લીધે મામાને અનિદ્રાનો રોગ છે.
>
> *cintāne līdhe māmāne anidrāno rog che.*
>
> Because of worry my uncle suffers from sleeplessness.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**અનિદ્રા** (*anidrā*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *anidrā*, "sleeplessness", as Monier-Williams records it: *a-*, "not", and *nidrā*, "sleep".
