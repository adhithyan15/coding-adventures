Marathi, chapter 486: Small Injuries.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
इजा (ijā) — an injury; harm.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for jaundice, then the Marathi for chickenpox.

You'll want to know: इजा.
इजा — ijā — "an injury; harm".
अपघातात त्याला किरकोळ इजा झाली. apghātāt tyālā kirkoḷ ijā jhālī. He was slightly injured in the accident.
Feminine.
इजा (ijā) होणे, to be hurt, with the dative; इजा (ijā) करणे, to harm.

The word, taken apart.
इजा (ijā) is a loan from Persian īzā, "harm, injury", usually traced to Arabic; Marathi writes the z as ज.

Lesson 3 of 5.
खरचटणे (kharcaṭṇe) — to get grazed.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: खरचटणे.
खरचटणे — kharcaṭṇe — "to get grazed".
सायकलवरून पडल्यावर त्याच्या गुडघ्याला खरचटलं. sāykalvarūn paḍlyāvar tyācyā guḍghyālā kharcaṭlaṁ. When he fell off his bicycle he grazed his knee.
Past (to him …): त्याला खरचटलं, tyālā kharcaṭlaṁ.
Impersonal: the grazed part takes -ला: हाताला खरचटलं.

Lesson 4 of 5.
मुरगळणे (murgaḷṇe) — to sprain; to twist.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a scab, then the Marathi for to get grazed.

You'll want to know: मुरगळणे.
मुरगळणे — murgaḷṇe — "to sprain; to twist".
खेळताना माझा घोटा मुरगळला. kheḷtānā mājhā ghoṭā murgaḷlā. I sprained my ankle while playing.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने कान मुरगळला, tyāne kān murgaḷlā.
Of a sprain the joint is the subject: माझा पाय मुरगळला. With -ने it is to twist: कान मुरगळणे (murgaḷṇe).

The word, taken apart.
मुरगळणे (murgaḷṇe) is a Marathi verb whose origin is not settled.

Lesson 5 of 5.
सुजणे (sujṇe) — to swell.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to get grazed, then the Marathi for to sprain.

You'll want to know: सुजणे.
सुजणे — sujṇe — "to swell".
मधमाशी चावल्यामुळे त्याचा हात सुजला. madhmāśī cāvlyāmuḷe tyācā hāt sujlā. His hand swelled up from a bee sting.
Past (he …): तो सुजला, to sujlā.
Usually the swollen part is the subject: त्याचा पाय सुजला. सूज, swelling: सूज येणे.

The word, taken apart.
सुजणे (sujṇe) is a tadbhava, inherited from Sanskrit śūyate, "is swollen"; CDIAL lists Marathi sujṇẽ, "to swell" (CDIAL 12568).
