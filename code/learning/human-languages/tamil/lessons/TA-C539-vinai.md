## You'll want to know: வீணை
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C539-ISAI539-02]; assesses=[] -->

**வீணை** — *vīṇai* — "a veena".

> அவள் பத்து வயது முதல் வீணை கற்றுக்கொள்கிறாள்.
>
> *avaḷ pattu vayatu mutal vīṇai kaṟṟukkoḷkiṟāḷ.*
>
> She has been learning the veena since she was ten.

A large stringed instrument of South Indian classical music.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**வீணை** (*vīṇai*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *vīṇā*, "a lute" (CDIAL 12048); Tamil writes the long final vowel as -ஐ.
