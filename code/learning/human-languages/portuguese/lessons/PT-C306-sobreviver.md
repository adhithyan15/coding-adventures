## You'll want to know: sobreviver
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C306-RESGATE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**sobreviver** — "to survive".

> Todos sobreviveram ao acidente.
>
> *Everyone survived the accident.*

*Sobreviver a* + what you lived through.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*sobreviver* comes from Latin *supervīvere* "to outlive, to survive" (EtymDB), from *super* "over" and *vīvere* "to live".
