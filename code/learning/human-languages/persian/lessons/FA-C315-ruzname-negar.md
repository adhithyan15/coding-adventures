## You'll want to know: روزنامه نگار
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C315-KHABARFOWRI-02]; assesses=[] -->

**روزنامه نگار** (*ruznâme negâr*) — "a journalist".

> او بیست سال روزنامه نگار بود.
>
> *U bist sâl ruznâme negâr bud.*
>
> She was a journalist for twenty years.

*ruznâme* "newspaper" + *negâr* "one who writes or paints", from *negâshtan*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ruznâme negâr* is built from Persian *ruznâme* "newspaper" (*ruz* "day" + *nâme* "letter") and *negâr-*, the stem of *negâshtan* "to write, to paint". All of it is native.
