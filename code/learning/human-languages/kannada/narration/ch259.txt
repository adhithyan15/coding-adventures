Kannada, chapter 259: Button, Sweater, Shawl, Handkerchief, Necklace.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಗುಂಡಿ (guṇḍi) — a button.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a biscuit, then the Kannada for bread.

You'll want to know: ಗುಂಡಿ.
ಗುಂಡಿ — guṇḍi — "a button".

The word, taken apart.
Where ಗುಂಡಿ (guṇḍi) comes from is uncertain. It looks like the northern word for "button", Hindi ghuṇḍī, which CDIAL 4483 traces to a reconstructed ghuṇṭa, "knot, button", but no source records the Kannada word.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ಸರ (sara) — a necklace, a chain.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a shawl, then the Kannada for a handkerchief.

You'll want to know: ಸರ.
ಸರ — sara — "a necklace, a chain".

The word, taken apart.
Where ಸರ (sara) comes from is uncertain. It looks like the first part of sarapaṇi, "a neck chain", which DEDR 2358 lists for Kannada, Tamil and Telugu, but DEDR does not split that word.
