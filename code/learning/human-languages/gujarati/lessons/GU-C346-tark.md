## You'll want to know: તર્ક
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C346-DAKHLO346-03]; assesses=[] -->

**તર્ક** — *tark* — "reasoning".

> મીરાનો તર્ક સીધો અને સાચો હતો.
>
> *Mīrāno tark sīdho ane sāco hato.*
>
> Mira's reasoning was straightforward and sound.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**તર્ક** (*tark*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *tarka*, "conjecture, reasoning" (Monier-Williams).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
