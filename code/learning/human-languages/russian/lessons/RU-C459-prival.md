## You'll want to know: привал
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C459-POKHOD-03]; assesses=[] -->

**привал** (*privál*) — "a rest stop (on a hike), a halt".

> Через два часа мы сделали привал.
>
> *Chérez dva chasá my sdélali privál.*
>
> After two hours we stopped for a rest.

Masculine. *Сделать привал* — to stop for a rest.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*привал* is built on *привалить* 'to lean against; to pull in to shore', from *при-* and *валить* 'to tumble'. EtymDB does not list it, so the line is hedged.
