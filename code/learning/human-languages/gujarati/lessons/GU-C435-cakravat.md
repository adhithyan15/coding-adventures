## You'll want to know: ચક્રવાત
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C435-STORM435-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ચક્રવાત** — *cakravāt* — "a whirlwind; a cyclone (in weather reports)".

> દરિયામાં ચક્રવાત આવ્યો, એટલે હોડીઓ કિનારે રહી.
>
> *dariyāmā̃ cakravāt āvyo, eṭle hoḍīo kināre rahī.*
>
> A cyclone came over the sea, so the boats stayed on the shore.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ચક્રવાત** (*cakravāt*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *cakravāta*, "a whirlwind" (Monier-Williams), made of *cakra*, "a wheel", and *vāta*, "wind": the wind that turns like a wheel.
