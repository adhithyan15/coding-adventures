## You'll want to know: confirmar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C515-RUMOR-05]; assesses=[] -->

**confirmar** — "to confirm".

> La policía confirmó la noticia.
>
> *The police confirmed the news.*

*Confirmar una reserva* — to confirm a booking.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*confirmar* is a learned borrowing from Latin *cōnfirmāre* 'to make firm, to strengthen' (EtymDB), built on *con-* and *firmāre*, from *firmus* 'firm'.
