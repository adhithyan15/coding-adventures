## Chapters 121-158: 190 headwords, can and want, and Marathi attains A1

Marathi had four A1 gaps:

- It was 174 headwords short.
- Two A1 spine nodes had no segment: SAY-WHAT-I-HAVE-AND-CAN-DO and SAY-WHAT-I-WANT.
- One lesson went over the new-atom budget.
- Eleven atoms were revisited fewer than twice.

**The words.** Thirty-eight chapters of five:

- **can:** **शकणे**, **जमणे**, **कौशल्य**, **क्षमता**, **अधिकार**, **साधन**, **मालक**, **ताकद** (SAY-WHAT-I-HAVE-AND-CAN-DO)
- **want:** **हवा**, **इच्छा**, **गरज**, **आशा**, **स्वप्न**, **मागणे**, **ठरवणे**, **योजना** (SAY-WHAT-I-WANT)
- **time:** **पहाट**, **मध्यरात्र**, **परवा**, the seven days, **तारीख**, **रोज** and the three seasons
- **places:** **सरळ**, **वळण**, **कोपरा**, **चौक**, **उद्यान**, **रुग्णालय**, **विमानतळ**, **ग्रंथालय**, and more
- **things:** the house, tools, school, clothes, food, nature, animals and the body
- **qualities:** thirty describing words
- **verbs:** thirty-two in all

**Checks.** Every candidate was checked against every earlier headword,
romanization and lesson text. Every word is spelled only with letters the book
has already taught the reader to write, so no word brings in a letter it cannot
write, such as ॅ, ॉ, ञ or ङ. Forward references stay at 10. Letter anchoring is
unchanged.

**The atom budget.** Chapter 13's numbers lesson introduced four atoms, one
over the budget. Two of them were the visible spelling tells the lesson itself
groups together: **दोन**'s final *n* and **पाच**'s missing nasal mark. They are
now one atom, `MR-SCRIPT-DON-FINAL-N`. The lesson still teaches both. The
lessons and the exam point that used the old atom now use the merged one.

**The eleven revisits.** Each is a new warm-up line:

- **मी मराठी बोलतो** with **शकणे**
- **काम करणे** with **कौशल्य**
- **दिवस** and **शुभ संध्याकाळ** with **मध्यरात्र**
- **पाच वाजता** with **तारीख**
- **हा** and **तो** at the **चौक**
- the chapter-62 reading skills: three signs read as one line, and a
  two-line message

      marathi headwords at or below A1   426  ->  600+ (A1 target met)
      marathi lessons                    652  ->  850
      level gate                         pre-A1 -> A1
