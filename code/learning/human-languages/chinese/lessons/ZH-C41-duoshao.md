## You'll want to know: 多少
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C41-MONEY41-01]; assesses=[] -->

**多少** — *duōshao* — "how much; how many".

> **你买了多少** *nǐ mǎi le duōshao* — How many did you buy?

The new character, in pieces: 多 — one three-stroke piece twice, one above the other; 少 — 小, the small piece, without its hook, and one long sweep below it. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**多少** (*duōshao*) is built from 多 'many' and 少 'few', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *ta* and *syewX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription): many-few, asked as a question.
