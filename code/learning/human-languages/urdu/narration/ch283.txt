6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
گرفتار کرنا (giraftār karnā) — to arrest.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for proof, then the Urdu for to look for.

You'll want to know: گرفتار کرنا.
گرفتار کرنا — giraftār karnā — "to arrest".
پولیس نے چور کو گرفتار کیا۔ polīs ne chor ko giraftār kiyā. The police arrested the thief.
Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے مجرم کو گرفتار کیا, us ne mujrim ko giraftār kiyā.

The word, taken apart.
گرفتار کرنا (giraftār karnā) is built from Persian giriftār, "seized, taken captive", usually traced to the Persian verb for taking hold, and کرنا. The person arrested takes کو: چور کو گرفتار کیا.

Lesson 2 of 6.
عدالت (adālat) — a court of law.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for to look for, then the Urdu for to arrest.

You'll want to know: عدالت.
عدالت — adālat — "a court of law".
کل عدالت بند تھی۔ kal adālat band thī. The court was closed yesterday.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
عدالت (adālat) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic ʿadāla, "justice, fairness": the place where justice is done. It is feminine: عدالت بند تھی.

Lesson 3 of 6.
مقدمہ (muqaddamā) — a court case; a lawsuit.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for to arrest, then the Urdu for a court of law.

You'll want to know: مقدمہ.
مقدمہ — muqaddamā — "a court case; a lawsuit".
مقدمہ ایک سال چلا۔ muqaddamā ek sāl chalā. The case went on for a year.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
مقدمہ (muqaddamā) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic muqaddama, "what is put first, a preface"; how it came to mean a court case is not certain. A case چلتا ہے: it goes on.

Lesson 5 of 6.
ہتھکڑی (hathkaṛī) — handcuffs.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a court case, then the Urdu for a punishment.

You'll want to know: ہتھکڑی.
ہتھکڑی — hathkaṛī — "handcuffs".
چور کے ہاتھوں میں ہتھکڑیاں تھیں۔ chor ke hāthoṅ meṅ hathkaṛiyāṅ thīṅ. The thief was in handcuffs.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
ہتھکڑی (hathkaṛī) is built from ہاتھ, a hand (CDIAL 14024), and kaṛī, a metal ring, which CDIAL lists for Hindi under Sanskrit káṭa (CDIAL 2629): rings for the hands. The first part shortens, as ہاتھ does in compounds.
