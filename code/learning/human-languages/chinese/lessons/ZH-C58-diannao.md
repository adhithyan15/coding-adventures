## You'll want to know: diànnǎo
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C58-THINGS58-03]; assesses=[] -->

*diànnǎo* — "computer".

> *wǒ xǐhuan wán diànnǎo* — I like playing on the computer.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*diànnǎo* is built from diàn 'lightning', now 'electricity', and nǎo 'brain', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *denH* and *nawX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription): an electric brain.
