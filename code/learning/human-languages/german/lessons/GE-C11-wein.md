# der Wein — "wine"

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GE-LEX-WASSER-01, GE-SOUND-W-AS-V-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Two *das*-words so far. The third drink takes a different article,
and that is the smaller of the two things different about it.

## You'll want to know: der Wein
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-WEIN-01]; assesses=[GE-LEX-DER-DIE-DAS-02, GE-LEX-WASSER-01] -->

> **der Wein** — "wine."

A ***der***-word, where *Brot* and *Wasser* are both *das*. So the three now run
one of each: *das Brot*, *das Wasser*, *der Wein*.

## Sounds you'll need
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GE-SOUND-W-AS-V-01, GE-SOUND-EI-AS-EYE-01, GE-LEX-WEIN-01] -->

- `w-as-v` — the *W* is a **v** again, as in *Wasser*.
- `diphthong-ei` — and *ei* is the vowel of English **eye**, which you were
  given on *eins*.

Put the two together and *Wein* comes out sounding like English **vine**. Hold
on to that, because the next lesson is about why.
