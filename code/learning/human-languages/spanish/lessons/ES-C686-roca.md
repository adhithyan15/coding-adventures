## You'll want to know: la roca
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C686-CIMA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**la roca** — "the rock".

> Nos sentamos en una roca a descansar.
>
> *We sat on a rock to rest.*

*Firme como una roca* — solid as a rock.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*roca* comes from Vulgar Latin *rocca* (EtymDB); where that word comes from is not settled.
