## You'll want to know: la ciotola
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C331-RICETTA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**la ciotola** — "the bowl".

> Metti la farina in una ciotola.
>
> *Put the flour in a bowl.*

Also for pets: *la ciotola del gatto*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ciotola* is usually traced to a little form of Latin *cyathus* "a ladle, a cup" (Lewis & Short); the path is not settled.
