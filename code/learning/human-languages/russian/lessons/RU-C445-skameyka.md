## You'll want to know: скамейка
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C445-FONAR-02]; assesses=[] -->

**скамейка** (*skaméyka*) — "a bench".

> Бабушки сидят на скамейке у подъезда.
>
> *Bábushki sidyát na skaméyke u podyézda.*
>
> The grandmothers are sitting on the bench by the entrance.

Feminine; a diminutive of *скамья*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*скамейка* is a diminutive of *скамья* 'a bench', which EtymDB takes from Byzantine Greek *skamnía*. EtymDB lists *скамейка* itself without a source.
