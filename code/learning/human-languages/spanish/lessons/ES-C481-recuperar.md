## You'll want to know: recuperar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C481-ARREGLO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**recuperar** — "to get back, to recover".

> Quiero recuperar mi dinero.
>
> *I want to get my money back.*

*Recuperarse* is to get better after an illness.
