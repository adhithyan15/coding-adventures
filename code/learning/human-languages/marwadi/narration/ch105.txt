Marwadi (Marwari), chapter 105: To stay, To read, To know, To fill, To pull.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
रैवणो (raivṇo) — to stay, to live.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for to ask for, then the Marwadi for to look.

You'll want to know: रैवणो.
रैवणो — raivṇo — "to stay, to live".

The word, taken apart.
रैवणो (raivṇo) is a tadbhava, a word inherited and reshaped by sound change. It comes from rahati, "remains", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants. CDIAL lists Old Marwari rahaï under it (CDIAL 10666).

Lesson 2 of 5.
बांचणो (bā̃chṇo) — to read.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for to look, then the Marwadi for to stay.

You'll want to know: बांचणो.
बांचणो — bā̃chṇo — "to read".

The word, taken apart.
बांचणो (bā̃chṇo) is usually linked with Sanskrit vācayati, "causes to say, reads", which CDIAL follows into Prakrit vāyēi, "reads" (CDIAL 11471), but not into Marwari or Hindi. That entry does not explain the nasal and the ch of बांचणो (bā̃chṇo), so the link is not settled.
