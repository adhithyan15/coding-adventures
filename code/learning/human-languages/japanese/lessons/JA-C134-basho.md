## You'll want to know: ばしょ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C134-BASHO]; assesses=[JA-SCRIPT-SHI-01, JA-SCRIPT-SMALL-YO-01] -->

**ばしょ** — *basho* — "a place, a spot": where something is, or where
something happens.

The new sign is spent at once. **ば**, then **し** with a small **ょ**: two
beats, *ba–sho*. The small **ょ** joins **し** into one beat, the way the small
**ゃ** did in **でんしゃ**. You write every sign in it now.
