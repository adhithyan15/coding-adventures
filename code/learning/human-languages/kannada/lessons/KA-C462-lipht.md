# ಲಿಫ್ಟ್ (liphṭ) — an elevator; a lift

## You'll want to know: ಲಿಫ್ಟ್
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C462-KOTHADI462-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ಲಿಫ್ಟ್** — *liphṭ* — "an elevator; a lift".

> ಲಿಫ್ಟ್ ಕೆಲಸ ಮಾಡದ ಕಾರಣ ನಾವು ಮೆಟ್ಟಿಲು ಹತ್ತಿದೆವು.
>
> *liphṭ kelasa māḍada kāraṇa nāvu meṭṭilu hattidevu.*
>
> Because the lift was not working we climbed the stairs.

ಲಿಫ್ಟ್ ಕೊಡು is also to give someone a ride.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಲಿಫ್ಟ್** (*lipṭ*) is a loan from English *lift*.
