Marathi, chapter 352: Mentioning and Stressing.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
उल्लेख करणे (ullekh karṇe) — to mention.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for nevertheless, then the Marathi for for example.

You'll want to know: उल्लेख करणे.
उल्लेख करणे — ullekh karṇe — "to mention".
अरुणने पत्रात तुमचा उल्लेख केला. aruṇne patrāt tumcā ullekh kelā. Arun mentioned you in his letter.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने उल्लेख केला, tyāne ullekh kelā.
What is mentioned takes -चा: तुमचा उल्लेख.

The word, taken apart.
उल्लेख करणे (ullekh karṇe) joins the tatsama उल्लेख, Sanskrit ullekha, "causing to appear clearly" (Monier-Williams), to करणे, "to do".

Lesson 2 of 5.
नमूद करणे (namūd karṇe) — to state (formally).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for for example, then the Marathi for to mention.

You'll want to know: नमूद करणे.
नमूद करणे — namūd karṇe — "to state (formally)".
अहवालात सगळे खर्च नमूद केले आहेत. ahvālāt sagḷe kharca namūd kele āhet. All the expenses are stated in the report.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने नमूद केलं, tyāne namūd kelaṁ.
Formal, of writing: अर्जात नमूद करणे (namūd karṇe), to state in an application.

The word, taken apart.
नमूद करणे (namūd karṇe) joins नमूद, a loan usually traced to Persian namūd, "appearance, showing", to करणे, "to make".

Lesson 3 of 5.
भर देणे (bhar deṇe) — to emphasize; to stress.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to mention, then the Marathi for to state.

You'll want to know: भर देणे.
भर देणे — bhar deṇe — "to emphasize; to stress".
शिक्षकांनी वाचनावर भर दिला. śikṣakānnī vācnāvar bhar dilā. The teacher stressed reading.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने भर दिला, tyāne bhar dilā.
The point stressed takes -वर: वाचनावर भर देणे (bhar deṇe).

The word, taken apart.
भर देणे (bhar deṇe) joins भर, "weight, load", inherited from Sanskrit bhara, "carrying, weight" (CDIAL 9393), to देणे, "to give": to put weight on a point.

Lesson 4 of 5.
विशेषतः (viśeṣataḥ) — especially.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to state, then the Marathi for to emphasize.

You'll want to know: विशेषतः.
विशेषतः — viśeṣataḥ — "especially".
हिवाळ्यात, विशेषतः सकाळी, खूप थंडी असते. hivāḷyāt, viśeṣataḥ sakāḷī, khūp thaṇḍī aste. In winter, especially in the mornings, it is very cold.
Formal; in speech खास करून.

The word, taken apart.
विशेषतः (viśeṣataḥ) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit viśeṣatas, "especially, particularly" (Monier-Williams).
