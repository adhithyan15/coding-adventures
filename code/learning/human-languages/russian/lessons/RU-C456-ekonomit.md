## You'll want to know: экономить
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C456-OBMENNIK-05]; assesses=[] -->

**экономить** (*ekonómit*) — "to save (money or time), to economise".

> Студенты экономят на всём.
>
> *Studénty ekonómyat na vsyom.*
>
> Students save money on everything.

Imperfective; the perfective is *сэкономить*. Present *экономлю, экономишь*. Takes *на* + prepositional.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*экономить* is built on *экономия* 'thrift', which is usually traced to Greek *oikonomía* 'running a household'. EtymDB lists *экономить* and *экономия* without a source.
