## You'll want to know: भूसो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C235-YARD235-05]; assesses=[] -->

**भूसो** — *bhūso* — "chaff; straw fodder".

> ऊंट भूसो खावै है।
>
> *ū̃ṭ bhūso khāvai hai.*
>
> The camel eats chaff.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**भूसो** (*bhūso*) is inherited from Sanskrit *busa*, "chaff"; CDIAL lists Hindi *bhūsā*, "chaff, straw", and a Gujarati cousin. CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry (CDIAL 9293).
