## You'll want to know: rispettare
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C368-CONVIVENZA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**rispettare** — "to respect".

> Dobbiamo rispettare le idee degli altri.
>
> *We have to respect other people's ideas.*

Also to keep to: *rispettare un orario*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*rispettare* is built on *rispetto* "respect", from Latin *respectus* "regard", from *respicere* "to look back at" (Lewis & Short), built on *specere* "to look".
