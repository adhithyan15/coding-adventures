Urdu, chapter 61: Bottle, Book, Pen, Newspaper, School.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
بوتل (botal) — a bottle.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a knife, then the Urdu for a fork; a thorn.

You'll want to know: بوتل.
بوتل — botal — "a bottle".

The word, taken apart.
بوتل (botal) is usually traced to English bottle; some trace it to Portuguese botelha, "bottle", which reached India first. CDIAL has no entry for it.

Lesson 2 of 5.
کتاب (kitāb) — a book.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a fork; a thorn, then the Urdu for a bottle.

You'll want to know: کتاب.
کتاب — kitāb — "a book".

The word, taken apart.
کتاب (kitāb) is a loan from Arabic kitāb, "book, writing", which reached Urdu through Persian. Its Arabic root is k-t-b (Lane). The same root gives kātib, "scribe", and maktab, "school, office".

Lesson 4 of 5.
اخبار (akhbār) — a newspaper.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a book, then the Urdu for a pen.

You'll want to know: اخبار.
اخبار — akhbār — "a newspaper".

The word, taken apart.
اخبار (akhbār) is a loan from Arabic akhbār, "reports, news", which reached Urdu through Persian. Its Arabic root is kh-b-r (Lane). It is the plural of khabar, "news"; Urdu uses it for the paper that carries them.

اسکول — iskūl — "a school".

اسکول (iskūl) is a loan from English school. English had it from Latin schola, and Latin from Greek skholē, "leisure".
