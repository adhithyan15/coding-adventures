## You'll want to know: 先
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C269-YOUNG269-02]; assesses=[] -->

**先** — *xiān* — "first; before anything else".

> **我们先吃饭** *wǒmen xiān chīfàn* — Let's eat first.

The new character, in pieces: 先 — six strokes: a short stroke falling to the left, a short bar, a vertical and a long bar; then 儿 at the foot. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**先** (*xiān*) is inherited from Middle Chinese *sen* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'before and after'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for talking about the past.
