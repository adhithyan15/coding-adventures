Lesson 1 of 5.
die Universität — a university.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for an email, then the German for a message.

You'll want to know: die Universität.
die Universität — "a university".

The word, taken apart.
Universität from Latin ūniversitās, "the whole, a community", in the Middle Ages a guild of teachers and students (EtymDB 2.1) — borrowed.

The word, taken apart.
Aufgabe from Middle High German ūfgābe (EtymDB 2.1): auf + the word for "gift", from the verb "to give". A task is something given to you.
