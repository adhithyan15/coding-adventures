# ડુંગળી (ḍungḷī) — onion

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-LEX-C166-CHOP166-01, GU-LEX-C166-CHOP166-02] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to chop, then the Gujarati for to peel.

## You'll want to know: ડુંગળી
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C166-CHOP166-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ડુંગળી** — *ḍungḷī* — "onion".

> **તે ડુંગળી સમારે છે.** — *te ḍungḷī samāre che* — "He chops an onion."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

The origin of **ડુંગળી** (*ḍungḷī*) is not settled. CDIAL lists no Gujarati form of it, and EtymDB has no entry for it.
