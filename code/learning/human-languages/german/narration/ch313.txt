German, chapter 313: Es war einmal.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
das Märchen — the fairy tale.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for the traditional dress, then the German for the band.

You'll want to know: das Märchen.
das Märchen — "the fairy tale".
Meine Oma erzählte uns jeden Abend ein Märchen. My grandma told us a fairy tale every evening.
Plural: die Märchen. They begin Es war einmal — once upon a time.

The word, taken apart.
Märchen is the diminutive of the old word Mär "news, tale", from Middle High German mære (EtymDB), with the ending -chen: a little tale.

What you've built.
