Portuguese, chapter 64: Big, Small, New, Old, Bad.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
grande — big, large.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for an ant, then the Portuguese for an elephant.

You'll want to know: grande.
grande — "big, large".
