# اس لیے (is liye) — so

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[UR-LEX-C159-VAJAH159-01, UR-GRAMMAR-C268-REASON-01, UR-LEX-C269-CHUNANCHE-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "because I was ill". (*kyūṅki maiṅ bīmār thā*.) That gives the reason. This lesson gives what follows from it.

## You'll want to know: اس لیے
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-GRAMMAR-C269-SO-01]; assesses=[UR-LEX-C159-VAJAH159-01, UR-LEX-C77-TIME77-04, UR-LEX-C77-TIME77-03, UR-LEX-C202-CHIZ202-04] -->

> **اس لیے میں نہیں آیا۔**
>
> *is liye maiṅ nahīṅ āyā.* — "So I didn't come."

**اس لیے** (*is liye*), "for this", opens the result. The reason comes first, then the result:

> **دیر ہو رہی تھی،**
>
> *der ho rahī thī,*
>
> **اس لیے ہم نے ٹیکسی لی۔**
>
> *is liye ham ne ṭaiksī lī.* — "It was getting late, so we took a taxi."

**ٹیکسی** (*ṭaiksī*) is feminine: **لی** (*lī*).

## You'll want to know: تو, "then"
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-GRAMMAR-C269-SO-02]; assesses=[UR-LEX-C78-TIME78-05, UR-LEX-BARISH, UR-GRAMMAR-C265-FUTURE-01, UR-LEX-C269-CHUNANCHE-01] -->

> **باہر بارش ہے۔**
>
> *bāhar bārish hai.* — "It's raining outside."

> **تو میں گھر پر رہوں گا۔**
>
> *to maiṅ ghar par rahūṅ gā.* — "Then I'll stay at home."

**تو** (*to*) opens an answer that follows from what someone said: "so, then". In writing, **چنانچہ** does the work of **اس لیے**.
