## Chapters 115-141: 135 headwords, and Malayalam attains pre-A1

Twenty-seven vocabulary chapters, five words each, closed by four review
lessons (two after chapter 127, two after chapter 141). Every word is chained:
each new lesson warms up on the two before it, so every atom is revisited twice.

- **115-127**: feeling unwell, feelings, the body, the weather, asking someone
  to slow down or repeat, travel, short replies, "maybe" and "not like that",
  welcome and congratulations, kin, questions about you, and taking leave.
- **128-141**: the house, things you carry, meals, the kitchen, Kerala dishes,
  fruit and sugar, the table, the town, the water, getting around, animals,
  spans of time, the classroom, and festivals.

Each headword was checked before it was written. It had to be new to the track,
use only glyphs the script lessons already teach, and not appear as a whole word
in any earlier lesson (that would create a forward reference). Two places in the
new text were reworded for the same reason: "it's getting late" uses **നേരം**, not
**സമയം**, because സമയം is taught later, and a cross-reference names the welcome
chapter rather than its number.
