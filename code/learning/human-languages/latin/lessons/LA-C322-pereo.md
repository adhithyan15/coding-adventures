## You'll want to know: pereō, perīre
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C322-FUGA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**pereō, perīre** — "to perish, to die; to be lost".

> In illō incendiō multī periērunt.
>
> *Many died in that fire.*

*pereō, perīre, periī, peritum*, like *eō* (taught). In comedy a slave cries *periī!*, 'I am done for!'

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**pereō** is built on *eō*, "to go", with *per-*, here "to destruction" (Lewis & Short): to go to ruin. English *perish* comes from it through French.
