Arabic, chapter 455: Shop windows.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
منتج (muntaj) — a product.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a swimming pool, then the Arabic for a shopping centre.

You'll want to know: منتج.
منتج — muntaj — "a product".
هذا منتج محلي جيد. hādhā muntaj maḥallī jayyid. This is a good local product.

The word, taken apart.
منتج (muntaj) is built on the native Arabic root n-t-j, "to bring forth young" (Lane). It is the Form IV passive participle, "what is brought forth"; natīja, "a result", shares the root.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

واجهة — wājiha — "a shop window; a front".
رأيت فستانا جميلا في واجهة (wājiha) المتجر. raʾaytu fustānan jamīlan fī wājihat al-matjar. I saw a beautiful dress in the shop window.

The word, taken apart.
واجهة (wājiha) is built on the native Arabic root w-j-h, "the face; to face" (Lane). It is the part of a building that faces the street.

Lesson 4 of 5.
عرض (ʿaraḍa) — to display; to offer.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a brand, then the Arabic for a shop window.

You'll want to know: عرض.
عرض — ʿaraḍa — "to display; to offer".
عرض المتجر ملابس الشتاء في الواجهة. ʿaraḍa al-matjar malābis ash-shitāʾ fī al-wājiha. The shop displayed winter clothes in the window.
Present: يعرض, yaʿriḍ.
The noun ʿarḍ is an offer or a show.

The word, taken apart.
عرض (ʿaraḍa) is built on the native Arabic root ʿ-r-ḍ, "to show, to present" (Lane). It is the plain verb; ʿarīḍ, "wide", shares the root.

Lesson 5 of 5.
بسطة (basṭa) — a market stall.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a shop window, then the Arabic for to display.

You'll want to know: بسطة.
بسطة — basṭa — "a market stall".
يبيع جاري الفاكهة على بسطة في السوق. yabīʿu jārī al-fākiha ʿalā basṭa fī as-sūq. My neighbour sells fruit on a stall in the market.
The word is common in the Levant.

The word, taken apart.
بسطة (basṭa) is built on the native Arabic root b-s-ṭ, "to spread out" (Lane). A basṭa is goods spread out for sale.
