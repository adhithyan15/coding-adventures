## You'll want to know: ایراد گرفتن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C441-DARKHAST-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ایراد گرفتن** (*irâd gereftan*) — "to find fault, to pick holes".

> او از همه چیز هتل ایراد میگرفت.
>
> *U az hame chiz-e hotel irâd migereft.*
>
> He found fault with everything about the hotel.

*irâd* 'objection, fault' + *gereftan*. The thing criticized takes *az*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*irâd gereftan* is built from *irâd* 'an objection, a flaw', a loan usually traced to Arabic *īrād* 'bringing forward', and the native verb *gereftan* 'to take'. EtymDB does not list *irâd*.
