Malayalam, chapter 118: Hot, Cold and Sleepy.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
തണുപ്പ് (taṇuppŭ) — cold (weather).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for vomiting, then the Malayalam for a wound.

You'll want to know: തണുപ്പ്.
തണുപ്പ് (taṇuppŭ) — "cold", the cold of the weather rather than the illness ജലദോഷം. നല്ല തണുപ്പ് — "it's really cold".

What you've built.
The cold you feel outside.

Guided Practice.
