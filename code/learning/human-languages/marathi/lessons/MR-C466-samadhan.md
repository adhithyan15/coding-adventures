## You'll want to know: समाधान
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C466-SAMADHAN466-01]; assesses=[] -->

**समाधान** — *samādhān* — "satisfaction; contentment".

> काम पूर्ण झाल्यावर त्याला समाधान वाटलं.
>
> *kām pūrṇa jhālyāvar tyālā samādhān vāṭlaṁ.*
>
> He felt satisfied when the work was done.

Neuter.

समाधान वाटणे, with the dative; समाधानी, content.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**समाधान** (*samādhān*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *samādhāna*, "putting together; reconciliation" (CDIAL 13210); in Marathi it is contentment.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
