## You'll want to know: रोजगार
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C507-ROJGAR507-02]; assesses=[] -->

**रोजगार** — *rojgār* — "employment; a livelihood".

> शहरात रोजगार शोधायला अनेक तरुण येतात.
>
> *śahrāt rojgār śodhāylā anek taruṇ yetāt.*
>
> Many young people come to the city to look for work.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**रोजगार** (*rojgār*) is a loan from Persian *rōzgār*, "time, fortune, livelihood", built on *rōz*, "day".
