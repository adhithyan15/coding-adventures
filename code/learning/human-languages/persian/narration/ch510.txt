Persian, chapter 510: Free Time.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
تفریح (tafrih) — recreation, fun; an outing.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a die, then the Persian for to play cards.

You'll want to know: تفریح.
تفریح (tafrih) — "recreation, fun; an outing".
آخر هفته برای تفریح به کوه میرویم. âkhar-e hafte barâ-ye tafrih be kuh miravim. At the weekend we go to the mountains for fun.
tafrih kardan — to have fun, to go on an outing. Plural tafrihât.

The word, taken apart.
tafrih comes from Arabic tafrīḥ (EtymDB), a word usually glossed 'cheering up, gladdening'.

Lesson 2 of 5.
وقت گذراندن (vaqt gozarândan) — to spend time.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to play cards, then the Persian for recreation.

You'll want to know: وقت گذراندن.
وقت گذراندن (vaqt gozarândan) — "to spend time".
او با نوه هایش وقت میگذراند. u bâ navehâyash vaqt migozarânad. She spends time with her grandchildren.
vaqt 'time' + gozarândan 'to pass (something)'; present stem gozarân-.

The word, taken apart.
vaqt gozarândan is built in Persian on vaqt, 'time', which was borrowed from Arabic waqt (EtymDB), and native gozarândan, 'to make pass', the causative of gozashtan.

Lesson 3 of 5.
سرگرم شدن (sargarm shodan) — to be absorbed (in); to be entertained.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for recreation, then the Persian for to spend time.

You'll want to know: سرگرم شدن.
سرگرم شدن (sargarm shodan) — "to be absorbed (in); to be entertained".
بچه ها با بازی سرگرم شدند. bachchehâ bâ bâzi sargarm shodand. The children got absorbed in the game.
sargarm 'busy, absorbed' (sar 'head' + garm 'warm') + shodan. sargarmi is a hobby.

The word, taken apart.
sargarm shodan is built on inherited words: sar, 'head', and garm, 'warm', which EtymDB traces back to a Proto-Indo-European root meaning 'to heat' (EtymDB), and shodan, 'to become': a warm head, busy with something.

Lesson 4 of 5.
خوش گذشتن (khosh gozashtan) — to have a good time.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to spend time, then the Persian for to be absorbed.

You'll want to know: خوش گذشتن.
خوش گذشتن (khosh gozashtan) — "to have a good time".
در مهمانی به ما خیلی خوش گذشت. dar mehmâni be mâ kheyli khosh gozasht. We had a great time at the party.
Impersonal: be man khosh gozasht — 'it passed pleasantly for me'. Present stem gozar-.

The word, taken apart.
khosh gozashtan is built on two native Persian words: khosh, 'pleasant', and gozashtan, 'to pass': time passed pleasantly. EtymDB has no entry for the phrase.

Lesson 5 of 5.
عضو شدن ('ozv shodan) — to become a member, to join.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to be absorbed, then the Persian for to have a good time.

You'll want to know: عضو شدن.
عضو شدن ('ozv shodan) — "to become a member, to join".
او عضو باشگاه شطرنج شد. u 'ozv-e bâshgâh-e shatranj shod. He joined the chess club.
'ozv 'member; limb' + shodan. Plural a'zâ (Arabic).

The word, taken apart.
'ozv comes from Arabic ʿuḍw (EtymDB), a word for a limb and so a member of a body. shodan, 'to become', is inherited Persian.
