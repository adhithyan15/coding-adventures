## You'll want to know: ಪರ್ವತ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C459-CARANA459-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ಪರ್ವತ** — *parvata* — "a mountain".

> ಆ ಪರ್ವತದ ಮೇಲೆ ಯಾವಾಗಲೂ ಹಿಮ ಇರುತ್ತದೆ.
>
> *ā parvatada mēle yāvāgalū hima iruttade.*
>
> There is always snow on that mountain.

ಬೆಟ್ಟ is a hill; ಪರ್ವತ is a high mountain.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಪರ್ವತ** (*parvata*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *parvata*, "a rock, a mountain" (CDIAL 7945).
