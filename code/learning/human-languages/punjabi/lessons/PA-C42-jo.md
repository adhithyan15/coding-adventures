## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-PRON-JO-01]; assesses=[PA-JOIN-JE-01, PA-JOIN-JADON-01, PA-PRON-OH-01] -->

> **ਜੋ** — *jo* — **the one who**, **the thing that**

The third member of the family. **ਜੇ** supposes, **ਜਦੋਂ** times, and **ਜੋ**
describes — and all three open their clause with the same letter.

> **ਜੋ** *(what they do)* … — "the one who …"

Punjabi marks a describing clause at its front, the way it marked a condition
with **ਜੇ**. Once you hear the **ਜ**, you know a clause is starting and that
something will come back to be pointed at.
