## You'll want to know: tandis que
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C269-QUAND-05]; assesses=[] -->

**tandis que** — "while, whereas".

> Il lisait tandis que sa sœur jouait du piano.
>
> *He was reading while his sister was playing the piano.*

Two uses: time, 'while', and contrast, 'whereas': *il aime la mer, tandis que je préfère la montagne*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*tandis* is inherited from Latin *tam diū* "for so long" (EtymDB), from *tam* "so" and *diū* "for a long time" (Lewis & Short).
