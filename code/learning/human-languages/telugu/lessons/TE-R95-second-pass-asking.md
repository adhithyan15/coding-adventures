# Asking about the language — a second pass

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[TE-LEX-C95-ASK-01, TE-LEX-C95-ASK-02] -->

[PAUSE 3s] Point at something and ask how to say it in Telugu.

## Grammar Lens: two kinds of polite
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[TE-LEX-C94-POLITE-01, TE-LEX-C94-POLITE-02, TE-LEX-C94-POLITE-03, TE-LEX-C94-POLITE-04] -->

| you ask | you answer |
|---|---|
| **మన్నించండి** | **పరవాలేదు** |
| **టీ కావాలా?** | **అవసరం లేదు** |

An apology is met with "no bother"; an offer is refused with "no need". Both answers end in
**లేదు**.

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[TE-LEX-C94-POLITE-01, TE-LEX-C94-POLITE-02, TE-LEX-C94-POLITE-03, TE-LEX-C94-POLITE-04, TE-LEX-C94-POLITE-05, TE-LEX-C95-ASK-01, TE-LEX-C95-ASK-02, TE-LEX-C95-ASK-03, TE-LEX-C95-ASK-04, TE-LEX-C95-ASK-05] -->

[PAUSE 3s] Pardon me? (**మన్నించండి**.) An apology? (**క్షమాపణ**.) Do you want it?
(**కావాలా?**) No need? (**అవసరం లేదు**.) An introduction? (**పరిచయం**.) In Telugu?
(**తెలుగులో**.) How do you say it? (**ఎలా అంటారు?**) Gently? (**మెల్లగా**.) Show me?
(**చూపించండి**.) I could not hear? (**వినిపించలేదు**.)
