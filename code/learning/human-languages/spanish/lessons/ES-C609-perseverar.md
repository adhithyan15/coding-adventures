## You'll want to know: perseverar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C609-PROGRESO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**perseverar** — "to persevere, to keep at it".

> Si perseveras, lo vas a conseguir.
>
> *If you keep at it, you will manage.*

*La perseverancia* — perseverance.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*perseverar* is a learned borrowing from Latin *perseverare* 'to persist' (EtymDB), usually traced to *severus* 'strict, serious'.
