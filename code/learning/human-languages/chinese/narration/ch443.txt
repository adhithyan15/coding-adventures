Mandarin Chinese, chapter 443: Keeping It Clean.
6 lessons. You can do the first 2 of them in the car; after that you will want to have stopped.


Lesson 1 of 6.
qīngchè — crystal-clear (of water).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for to rise after rain, then the Mandarin for a great river.

You'll want to know: qīngchè.
qīngchè — "crystal-clear (of water)".
zhè tiáo hé de shuǐ hěn qīngchè — The water in this river is crystal clear.
Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
qīngchè is built from qīng 'clear' and chè 'clear water', both inherited from Middle Chinese, tshjeng and drjet (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second as water that has settled clear.

What you've built.

Lesson 2 of 6.
保护 (bǎohù) — to protect; to keep from harm.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for a great river, then the Mandarin for crystal-clear.

You'll want to know: 保护.
保护 — bǎohù — "to protect; to keep from harm".
我们应该保护河水 wǒmen yīnggāi bǎohù héshuǐ — We should keep the river water clean.
The new character, in pieces: 护 (hù) — seven strokes in two pieces: on the left the hand piece of 打, three strokes; on the right a dot over a frame and a long stroke falling to the left, four strokes, as on top of 房. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

The word, taken apart.
保护 (bǎohù) is built from 保 'to keep' and 护 (hù) 'to guard', both inherited from Middle Chinese, pawX and huH (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to keep safe' and the second 'to rescue' and 'to help'.

Script — the hand piece, then the top of 房.
护 (hù).
Two pieces, left before right. Count the pieces, not the strokes.
The left piece, the hand piece, three strokes, as in 打: a bar, a vertical with a hook, and a stroke that rises to the right through the vertical.
The right piece, four strokes, as on top of 房: a dot; a stroke that runs right and turns down; a bar that closes the frame at the foot; then a long stroke falling to the left from the top corner of the frame.
3 + 4 = seven.

[once you have stopped driving — this part needs your eyes or your hands; if you are driving, skip ahead to the next part]
Guided Practice.
[once you have stopped driving — write: 护 (hù), all seven, with the model in front of you]
[once you have stopped driving — cover: write it again from memory, and count the pieces, not the strokes]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
How many strokes in 护 (hù)? (Seven.) What is the left piece? (The hand piece, as in 打.) Where have you made the right piece before? (On top of 房.)

保护区 — bǎohùqū — "nature reserve; protected area".
我们坐车去山里的保护区 wǒmen zuò chē qù shān lǐ de bǎohùqū — We're driving to the nature reserve in the mountains.

The word, taken apart.
保护区 (bǎohùqū) is built from 保护 (bǎohù) 'to protect' and 区 'an area', all inherited from Middle Chinese, pawX, huH and khju (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription).
