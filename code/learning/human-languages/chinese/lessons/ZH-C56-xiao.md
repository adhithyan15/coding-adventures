## You'll want to know: xiào
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C56-PLAY56-05]; assesses=[] -->

*xiào* — "to laugh; to smile".

> *tā xiào le* — She laughed.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*xiào* is inherited from Middle Chinese *sjewH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'glad, joyful'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for everyday answers.
