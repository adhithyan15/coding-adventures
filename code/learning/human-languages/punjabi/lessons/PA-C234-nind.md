## You'll want to know: ਨੀਂਦ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C234-DIN234-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ਨੀਂਦ** — *nīnd* — "sleep".

> ਰਾਤ ਨੂੰ ਮੈਨੂੰ ਚੰਗੀ ਨੀਂਦ ਆਉਂਦੀ ਹੈ।
>
> *rāt nū̃ mainū̃ chaṅgī nīnd āundī hai.*
>
> I sleep well at night.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਨੀਂਦ** (*nīnd*) is inherited from Sanskrit *nidrā*, "sleep"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *nī̃d*, "sleep" (CDIAL 7200).
