Arabic, chapter 452: At the bank.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
فرع (farʿ) — a branch office; a branch.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to check over, then the Arabic for to ban.

You'll want to know: فرع.
فرع — farʿ — "a branch office; a branch".
فتح البنك فرعا جديدا في حينا. fataḥa al-bank farʿan jadīdan fī ḥayyinā. The bank opened a new branch in our neighbourhood.
Plural: فروع, furūʿ.

The word, taken apart.
فرع (farʿ) is built on the native Arabic root f-r-ʿ, "a branch of a tree" (Lane).

Lesson 3 of 5.

[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a branch office, then the Arabic for a balance.

You'll want to know: حوالة.
حوالة — ḥawāla — "a money transfer".
أرسلت حوالة إلى أختي في الخارج. arsaltu ḥawāla ilā ukhtī fī al-khārij. I sent a money transfer to my sister abroad.

The word, taken apart.
حوالة (ḥawāla) is built on the native Arabic root ḥ-w-l, "to change, to shift" (Lane). A ḥawāla shifts a sum from one hand to another.

صرف — ṣarafa — "to change money; to spend".
صرفت مالا في المطار. ṣaraftu mālan fī al-maṭār. I changed money at the airport.
Present: يصرف, yaṣrif.
siʿr aṣ-ṣarf is the exchange rate.

The word, taken apart.
صرف (ṣarafa) is built on the native Arabic root ṣ-r-f, "to turn away, to change" (Lane). It is the root of ṣarrāf, "a money changer".

Lesson 5 of 5.
أودع (awdaʿa) — to deposit.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a money transfer, then the Arabic for to change money.

You'll want to know: أودع.
أودع — awdaʿa — "to deposit".
أودع أبي المال في البنك. awdaʿa abī al-māl fī al-bank. My father deposited the money in the bank.
Present: يودع, yūdiʿ.

The word, taken apart.
أودع (awdaʿa) is built on the native Arabic root w-d-ʿ, "to leave, to let rest" (Lane). It is Form IV, "to leave a thing in someone's keeping".
