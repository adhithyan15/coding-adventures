## You'll want to know: सहप्रवासी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C399-VAHAK399-04]; assesses=[] -->

**सहप्रवासी** — *sahapravāsī* — "a fellow traveller".

> माझ्या सहप्रवाशाने मला पाणी दिलं.
>
> *mājhyā sahapravāśāne malā pāṇī dilaṁ.*
>
> My fellow traveller gave me some water.

Masculine.

सह- means together with; before -ने and -ला it becomes सहप्रवाशा-.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सहप्रवासी** (*sahapravāsī*) is a compound of Sanskrit *saha*, "together with" (Monier-Williams), and **प्रवासी**, "a traveller", built on Sanskrit *pravāsa*, "a journey abroad".
