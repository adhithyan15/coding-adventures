## You'll want to know: lo spoiler
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C528-SERIE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**lo spoiler** — "spoiler".

> Non dirmi niente, niente spoiler!
>
> *Do not tell me anything, no spoilers!*

From English; the plural is *gli spoiler*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*spoiler* was borrowed from English *spoiler*, built on *spoil*, from Latin *spoliāre* "to strip" (Lewis & Short).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.
