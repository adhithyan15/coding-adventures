## You'll want to know: निष्ठुर
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C466-DAYA466-01]; assesses=[] -->

**निष्ठुर** — *niṣṭhur* — "heartless; unfeeling".

> बच्चा रो रहा था, लेकिन वह निष्ठुर आदमी नहीं रुका।
>
> *baccā ro rahā thā, lekin vah niṣṭhur ādmī nahīṃ rukā.*
>
> The child was crying, but that heartless man didn't stop.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**निष्ठुर** (*niṣṭhur*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *niṣṭhura*, "hard, rough, harsh, cruel", said especially of words (Monier-Williams).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
