# मिटना (miṭnā) — to be wiped out, to fade away

## You'll want to know: मिटना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C385-BIKHAR385-02]; assesses=[] -->

**मिटना** — *miṭnā* — "to be wiped out, to fade away".

> अगर बारिश होगी तो दीवार का रंग मिट जाएगा।
>
> *agar bāriś hogī to dīvār kā raṅg miṭ jāegā.*
>
> If it rains, the colour on the wall will fade.

Past (it …): वह नाम मिट गया, *vah nām miṭ gayā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**मिटना** (*miṭnā*) is a tadbhava, inherited from Sanskrit *mṛṣṭa*, "rubbed, wiped" (CDIAL 10299).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
