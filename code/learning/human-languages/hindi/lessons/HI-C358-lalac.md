## You'll want to know: लालच
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C358-SEVA358-04]; assesses=[] -->

**लालच** — *lālac* — "greed".

> लालच बुरी चीज़ है।
>
> *lālac burī cīz hai.*
>
> Greed is a bad thing.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**लालच** (*lālac*) is a tadbhava, inherited from Sanskrit *lālitya*, "amorousness" (CDIAL 11029). CDIAL lists the Hindi *lālac*, "cupidity", under this entry; how a word for amorousness came to mean greed is not spelled out there.
