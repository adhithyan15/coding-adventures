## You'll want to know: 做客
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C180-VISIT180-01]; assesses=[] -->

**做客** — *zuòkè* — "to visit as a guest; to be a guest".

> **我去他家做客喝了两杯茶** *wǒ qù tā jiā zuòkè hē le liǎng bēi chá* — I went round to his place and had two cups of tea.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**做客** (*zuòkè*) is built from 做 'to do, to be' and 客 'guest'. The second is inherited from Middle Chinese *khaek* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'guest'; 做 has no form in that dictionary, so the history of the first part is not settled.
