## You'll want to know: mātūrē
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C295-NOCTU-04]; assesses=[] -->

**mātūrē** — "early; in good time".

> Agricola mātūrē surrēxit et in agrum īvit.
>
> *The farmer got up early and went to the field.*

From *mātūrus*, 'ripe, timely'. Its opposite is *sērō*, 'late', the adverb of *sērus* (taught). English *mature*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**mātūrē** is the adverb of *mātūrus*, "ripe, timely" (Lewis & Short): at the right time, and so early. English *mature* comes from *mātūrus*.
