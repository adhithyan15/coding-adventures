## You'll want to know: にゅうこく
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C386-ZEIKAN386-03]; assesses=[] -->

**にゅうこく** — *nyūkoku* — "entry into a country".

> **にゅうこくの とき、りょけんが ひつようです。** — *nyūkoku no toki, ryoken ga hitsuyō desu* — "You need your passport when you enter the country."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**にゅうこく** (*nyūkoku*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *nyū*, "enter" (Middle Chinese *nyip*) and *koku*, "country" (*kwok*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
