## Chapters 90-149: 300 headwords, and Marwadi attains A1

Marwadi had three A1 gaps:

- ten of the twelve A1 spine nodes had no lesson;
- it taught 324 headwords against 600;
- the connected-reading skill of chapter 40 was never revisited.

**The words.** Sixty chapters of five, closed by twelve review lessons:

- **time:** **दोपार** (*dopār*), **आथण** (*āthaṇ*), **परसूं** (*parsū̃*), the
  days from **सोमवार** to **रविवार** (all but **बुधवार**, which needs **ध**),
  the months from **चैत** to **माघ** (all but **असाढ** and **फागण**, which
  need **ढ** and **फ**), and the three seasons of the year, **उनाळो**, **सियाळो** and **चौमासो**
  (TIME-OF-DAY)
- **where:** **कठै** (*kaṭhai*), **लारै** (*lārai*), **माथै** (*māthai*),
  **हेठै** (*heṭhai*), **कनै** (*kanai*), **डावो** and **जीमणो**, and the
  places of a Rajasthani town, from the **बावड़ी** to the **हवेली**
  (ASK-LOCATION)
- **this and that:** **यो**, **इसो**, **उसो**, **किसो**, **कुण**, **सगळा**
  and more (DEFINITE-REFERENCE)
- **actions:** forty verbs in **-णो**, from **न्हावणो** to **तोलणो**
  (NAME-EVERYDAY-ACTIONS)
- **want, like, have and why:** **चावणो**, **मरजी**, **लागणो**, **हेत**,
  **सकणो**, **हुनर**, **क्यूं**, **क्यूंकै** and more (SAY-WHAT-I-WANT,
  SAY-WHAT-I-LIKE, SAY-WHAT-I-HAVE-AND-CAN-DO, SAY-WHY)
- **things:** 115 of them, covering the kitchen and the house, tools, clothes
  and jewellery, desert food (**केर**, **सांगरी**, **बाजरो**, **राबड़ी**),
  vegetables and spices, and animals (NAME-EVERYDAY-THINGS)
- **qualities:** forty-five describing words, from **चोखो** to **गैलो**
  (DESCRIBE-QUALITIES)

**The spelling rule.** Every headword still uses only letters the reader has
already written. So **ऊंट**, **धोरो**, **फूल**, **साफो** and the other words
that need **ऊ**, **ध**, **फ**, **झ**, **ढ** or **ओ** keep waiting. **वो** was
held back because earlier lesson text already uses it. Each new lesson carries
one typed recall, as Marwadi pins.

**The revisit.** Two warm-up lines, in chapters 90 and 120, ask the reader to
read lines of the chapter-40 bargain without stopping.

      marwadi lessons     584  ->  896
      marwadi headwords   324  ->  624
      level gate        pre-A1  ->  A1
