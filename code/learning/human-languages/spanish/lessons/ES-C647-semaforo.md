## You'll want to know: el semáforo
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C647-CRUZAR-01]; assesses=[] -->

**el semáforo** — "the traffic lights".

> Espera, el semáforo está en rojo.
>
> *Wait, the lights are red.*

One word for the whole set of lights: *el semáforo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*semáforo* was borrowed from French *sémaphore* (EtymDB), a word made from Greek *sêma* "sign" and *-phóros* "carrying": a thing that carries signals.
