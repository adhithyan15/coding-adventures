## You'll want to know: ನಗದು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C441-SHULKA441-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ನಗದು** — *nagadu* — "cash".

> ಈ ಅಂಗಡಿಯಲ್ಲಿ ನಗದು ಮಾತ್ರ ತೆಗೆದುಕೊಳ್ಳುತ್ತಾರೆ.
>
> *ī aṅgaḍiyalli nagadu mātra tegedukoḷḷuttāre.*
>
> This shop takes cash only.

ನಗದು ಕೊಡು, to pay in cash.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ನಗದು** (*nagadu*) is usually traced to Arabic *naqd*, "ready money", which reached Kannada through Hindustani.
