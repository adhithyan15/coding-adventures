# Marwadi five-minute session map

Every row is one session and every canonical lesson is at most five minutes.
Writing is not postponed until an alphabet unit: it begins in S1 and grows one
memory demand at a time.

| Session | New work | Retrieval before moving on | Writing demand |
|---|---|---|---|
| S1 | `MW-W01-ra`: **र** | point to **र** and say *ra* | observe, finger-trace, copy |
| S2 | `MW-W01-aa-matra`: **ा** | distinguish **र** and **रा** | guided copy |
| S3 | `MW-W01-raam`: **म**, then **राम** | read **राम** without romanization | delayed copy of one word |
| S4 | `MW-W01-sa`: **स** | distinguish **र** and **स** | observe, trace, copy |
| S5 | `MW-W01-saa`: **सा** | read **राम सा** | delayed copy of **सा** |
| S6 | `MW-C01-raam-raam-saa` | say the respectful greeting from a situation cue | copy the full greeting |
| S7 | `MW-C01-practice` | hear/read one side and supply the other | write the greeting from memory |
| S8 | `MW-W02-aa-independent`: **आ** | distinguish independent **आ** from attached **ा** | observe, trace, copy one sign |
| S9 | `MW-W02-bha`: **भ** | read **भ** as *bha* with a breath | observe, trace, copy one sign |
| S10 | `MW-C02-aabhaar`: **आभार** | build it from **आ + भ + ा + र** | delayed copy of one word |
| S11 | `MW-C02-practice` | hear/read a formal-gratitude cue and supply **आभार** | dictation and independent recall |
| S12 | `MW-W03-ha`: **ह** | distinguish **ह** from **भ** | observe, trace, copy one sign |
| S13 | `MW-W03-anusvara`: **ं** | add the nasal mark to known **हा** | guided copy of one small mark |
| S14 | `MW-C03-haan-saa`: **हां सा** | build it from **ह + ा + ं**, then known **सा** | delayed copy of one response |
| S15 | `MW-C03-listen-say` | distinguish *hā(n) sā* from *ābhār* by ear | hidden-model recall of **हां** |
| S16 | `MW-C03-practice` | hear/read a yes cue and answer respectfully | dictation and independent phrase recall |
| S17 | `MW-C04-hear-paani`: *pāṇī* | identify water from sound alone | none; meaning precedes spelling |
| S18 | `MW-W04-pa`: **प** | write **पा** with familiar **ा** | observe, trace, copy one sign |
| S19 | `MW-W04-nna`: **ण** | distinguish curled-back **ण** from Hindi **न** | observe, trace, copy one sign |
| S20 | `MW-W04-ii-matra`: **ी** | attach **ी** to **ण** and read **णी** | guided copy of one vowel mark |
| S21 | `MW-C04-paani`: **पाणी** | join **पा + णी** and give the meaning | delayed copy of one word |
| S22 | `MW-C04-practice` | retrieve water by ear, voice, eye, and meaning | dictation and independent word recall |
| S23 | `MW-C05-hear-mharo`: *mhāro* | distinguish my from water by ear | none; meaning precedes spelling |
| S24 | `MW-W05-virama`: **्** | switch off the built-in vowel in **म्** | observe, trace, copy one mark |
| S25 | `MW-W05-o-matra`: **ो** | turn **र** into **रो** | guided copy of one vowel sign |
| S26 | `MW-C05-mharo`: **म्हारो** | give its meaning and inspect three chunks | delayed copy of one word |
| S27 | `MW-C05-hear-naam`: *nām* | contrast *mhāro* and *nām* by meaning | none; sound and meaning only |
| S28 | `MW-W05-na`: **न** | distinguish dental **न** from retroflex **ण** | observe, trace, copy one sign |
| S29 | `MW-C05-naam`: **नाम** | assemble **न + ा + म** | delayed copy of one word |
| S30 | `MW-C05-hear-hai`: *hai* | hear the four-beat name statement | none; meaning precedes spelling |
| S31 | `MW-W05-ai-matra`: **ै** | turn **ह** into **है** | guided copy of one vowel sign |
| S32 | `MW-C05-answer` | retrieve each group in **म्हारो नाम राम है** | sentence dictation from sound |
| S33 | `MW-C05-hear-tharo`: *thāro* | contrast my and your by pointing | none; meaning precedes spelling |
| S34 | `MW-W05-tha`: **थ** | read *tha* with a small breath | observe, trace, copy one sign |
| S35 | `MW-C05-tharo`: **थारो** | contrast written **म्हारो** and **थारो** | delayed copy of one word |
| S36 | `MW-C05-hear-kain`: *kāĩ* | hear the complete question and identify its purpose | none; meaning precedes spelling |
| S37 | `MW-W05-ka`: **क** | read the first sign in *kāĩ* | observe, trace, copy one sign |
| S38 | `MW-W05-ii-independent`: **ई** | distinguish independent **ई** from attached **ी** | guided copy of one vowel sign |
| S39 | `MW-C05-kain`: **कांई** | assemble only known signs and give the meaning | delayed copy of one word |
| S40 | `MW-C05-practice` | ask and answer one name in four separate skills | question dictation plus spoken answer |

Reviews use expanding spacing: repeat S7 the next day, after three days, after
one week, and after two weeks. A failed written recall sends the learner back to
the one sign that stalled, not through the whole chapter again.
