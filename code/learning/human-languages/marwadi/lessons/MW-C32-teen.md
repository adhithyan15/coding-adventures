# तीन — three on the page

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MW-SCRIPT-ROTI-01, MW-SCRIPT-TTA-01, MW-SCRIPT-EK-01, MW-SCRIPT-DO-TWO-01, MW-LEX-FINAL-PRICE-01] -->

[PAUSE 20s] Say the final-price question. Write flatbread and put a finger on
its retroflex **ट**. Then write one and two.

## You'll want to know
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-SCRIPT-TEEN-01]; assesses=[MW-SCRIPT-TA-01, MW-SCRIPT-II-MATRA-01, MW-SCRIPT-NA-01, MW-LEX-TEEN-01] -->

> **त** + **ी** + **न** → **तीन** — *tīn* — three

The **त** here is the dental one, made with the tongue against the teeth — not
the **ट** of **रोटी**, which is made further back. The two look different on the
page and sound different in the mouth, and three uses the front one.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MW-SCRIPT-TEEN-01, MW-SCRIPT-TTA-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: delayed-copy -->

Look at **तीन**, cover it, wait five seconds, write it. Then write **ट** beside
**त** and say which one three needs.
