## You'll want to know: aulaeum, aulaeī
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C546-CAVE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**aulaeum, aulaeī** — "a theatre curtain".

> Aulaeum premitur, et fābula incipit.
>
> *The curtain is lowered, and the play begins.*

Neuter, second declension. Roman curtains worked upside down: they sank into a slot at the start of a play and rose at the end.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**aulaeum** was borrowed from Greek *aulaia* "curtain, hanging" (Lewis & Short; EtymDB).
