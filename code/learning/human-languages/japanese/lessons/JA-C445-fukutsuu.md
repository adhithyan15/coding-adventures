## You'll want to know: ふくつう
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C445-ACHE445-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ふくつう** — *fukutsū* — "a stomach ache".

> **ふくつうで がっこうを やすみました。** — *fukutsū de gakkō o yasumimashita* — "I stayed home from school with a stomach ache."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ふくつう** (*fukutsū*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *fuku*, "abdomen" (Middle Chinese *pjuwk*) and *tsū*, "pain" (*thuwngH*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
