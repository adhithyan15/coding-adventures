## You'll want to know: こざら
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C475-TABLE475-04]; assesses=[] -->

**こざら** — *kozara* — "a small plate".

> **こざらに しょうゆを いれました。** — *kozara ni shōyu o iremashita* — "I put soy sauce on a small plate."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**こざら** (*kozara*) is a native Japanese compound (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese: *ko*, "small" and *zara*, "plate (sara)" (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
