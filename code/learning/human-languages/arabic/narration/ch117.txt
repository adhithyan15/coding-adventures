Arabic, chapter 117: Television, Newspaper, Magazine, Letter, Envelope.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
تلفاز (tilfāz) — a television.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a fan, then the Arabic for a computer.

تلفاز — tilfāz — "a television".
