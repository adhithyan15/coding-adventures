## You'll want to know: गोद
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C494-DEKHBHAL494-02]; assesses=[] -->

**गोद** — *god* — "a lap".

> बच्चा माँ की गोद में सो गया।
>
> *baccā māṁ kī god meṁ so gayā.*
>
> The baby fell asleep in its mother's lap.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**गोद** (*god*) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Hindi *god*, "lap", under *\*gōddī*, a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 4284). *god lenā*, "to take into one's lap", is to adopt a child.
