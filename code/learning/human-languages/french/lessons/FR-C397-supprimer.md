## You'll want to know: supprimer
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C397-FICHIER-02]; assesses=[] -->

**supprimer** — "to delete; to remove".

> J'ai supprimé toutes les vieilles photos.
>
> *I deleted all the old photos.*

Also to abolish: *supprimer une loi*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*supprimer* is a learned borrowing from Latin *supprimere* "to press under, to hold back" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short), built on *premere* "to press".
