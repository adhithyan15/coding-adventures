# توفي (tuwuffiya) — to pass away

## You'll want to know: توفي
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C314-JANAZA314-02]; assesses=[] -->

**توفي** — *tuwuffiya* — "to pass away".

> توفي جدي قبل سنتين.
>
> *tuwuffiya jaddī qabla sanatayn.*
>
> My grandfather passed away two years ago.

Present: يتوفى, *yutawaffā*.

A polite word for to die, passive in form.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**توفي** (*tuwuffiya*) is built on the native Arabic root *w-f-y* (Lane), "to be complete, to fulfil". *Tawaffā* is "to take a thing in full"; its passive, *tuwuffiya*, is said of one whose life is complete.
