## You'll want to know: రాజీ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C534-GODAVA534-04]; assesses=[] -->

**రాజీ** — *rājī* — "a compromise; a reconciliation".

> చివరికి ఇద్దరూ రాజీ పడ్డారు.
>
> *civariki iddarū rājī paḍḍāru.*
>
> In the end the two of them came to terms.

రాజీ పడు, to come to terms.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**రాజీ** (*rājī*) came into Telugu through Hindustani *rāzī*, "agreed, content", which is usually traced to Arabic.
