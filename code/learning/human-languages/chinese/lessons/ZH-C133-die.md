## You'll want to know: dié
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C133-HOUSE133-05]; assesses=[] -->

*dié* — "to fold".

> *wǒ dié yīfu nǐ shōushi fángjiān* — I'll fold the clothes; you tidy the room.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*dié* is inherited from Middle Chinese *dep* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'layered' and 'to pile up'; folding, a cloth laid in layers, is the likely step, though it is not given there.
