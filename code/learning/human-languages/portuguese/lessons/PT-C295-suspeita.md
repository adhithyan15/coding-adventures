## You'll want to know: a suspeita
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C295-SUSPEITO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**a suspeita** — "the suspicion".

> Tenho uma suspeita: foi o vizinho.
>
> *I have a suspicion: it was the neighbour.*

*Sob suspeita* — under suspicion.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*suspeita* goes back to Latin *suspecta*, from *suspicere* "to look up at, to suspect" (Lewis & Short).
