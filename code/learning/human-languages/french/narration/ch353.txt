French, chapter 353: Trouver un remède.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
le remède — the remedy, the cure.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for the asset, then the French for the brake.

You'll want to know: le remède.
le remède — "the remedy, the cure".
Le meilleur remède contre la fatigue, c'est le sommeil. The best remedy for tiredness is sleep.
Un remède de grand-mère — a home remedy.

The word, taken apart.
remède comes from Latin remedium "a cure, a remedy" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short), built from re- and medērī "to heal".

What you've built.
One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.

Guided Practice.

[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
