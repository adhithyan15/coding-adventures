Lesson 1 of 7.
विश्वासघात (viśvāsghāt) — a betrayal.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to seethe, then the Marathi for to stop speaking to.

You'll want to know: विश्वासघात.
विश्वासघात — viśvāsghāt — "a betrayal".
जवळच्या मित्रानेच विश्वासघात केला. javaḷcyā mitrānec viśvāsghāt kelā. It was a close friend who betrayed him.
Masculine.
विश्वास, trust, + घात, a blow: विश्वासघात (viśvāsghāt) करणे, to betray.

The word, taken apart.
विश्वासघात (viśvāsghāt) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit viśvāsaghāta, "destruction of confidence, treachery" (Monier-Williams).

त्याचे कडू शब्द मला अजून बोचतात. tyāce kaḍū śabda malā ajūn boctāt. His bitter words still rankle with me.
Past (to him …): त्याला ते बोचलं, tyālā te boclaṁ.

डिवचणे — ḍivacṇe — "to goad; to needle".
भावाने मला सारखं डिवचलं. bhāvāne malā sārkhaṁ ḍivaclaṁ. My brother kept needling me.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने मला डिवचलं, tyāne malā ḍivaclaṁ.
To poke at someone till they react; the one provoked takes -ला.

उशिरा आल्याबद्दल शिक्षकांनी त्याला खडसावलं. uśirā ālyābaddal śikṣakānnī tyālā khaḍsāvlaṁ. The teacher rebuked him for coming late.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने मुलाला खडसावलं, tyāne mulālā khaḍsāvlaṁ.

Lesson 5 of 7.
माफ करणे (māph karṇe) — to forgive.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to goad, then the Marathi for to rebuke.

You'll want to know: माफ करणे.
माफ करणे — māph karṇe — "to forgive".
मी चूक केली, तरी आईने मला माफ केलं. mī cūk kelī, tarī āīne malā māph kelaṁ. I made a mistake, but Mother forgave me.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने मला माफ केलं, tyāne malā māph kelaṁ.
माफी मागणे, to apologise; the one forgiven takes -ला.

The word, taken apart.
माफ करणे (māph karṇe) is a Marathi phrase built on माफ, "excused", a loan from Persian muaf, usually traced to Arabic, with करणे.
