## You'll want to know: தொடை
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C481-KAL481-01]; assesses=[] -->

**தொடை** — *toṭai* — "a thigh".

> ஓடும்போது என் தொடையில் வலி வந்தது.
>
> *ōṭumpōtu eṉ toṭaiyil vali vantatu.*
>
> While running I got a pain in my thigh.

தொடை and தொடு "to touch" are different words.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**தொடை** (*toṭai*) is native Dravidian (DEDR 3302); the entry also gives the form துடை *tuṭai*.
