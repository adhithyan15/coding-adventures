Arabic, chapter 111: To long for, Expectation, Enthusiasm, Wish, Preference.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
اشتاق (ishtāqa) — to long for.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to keep, then the Arabic for a talent.

You'll want to know: اشتاق.
اشتاق — ishtāqa — "to long for".

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does اشتاق (ishtāqa) mean? ("To long for".) Say it once more.
