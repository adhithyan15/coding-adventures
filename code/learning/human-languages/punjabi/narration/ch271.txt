Punjabi, chapter 271: North, South, East, West.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ਉੱਤਰ (uttar) — the north.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for a mile, then the Punjabi for open ground.

You'll want to know: ਉੱਤਰ.
ਉੱਤਰ — uttar — "the north".
ਪਹਾੜ ਪਿੰਡ ਦੇ ਉੱਤਰ ਵਿੱਚ ਹਨ। pahāṛ piṇḍ de uttar vich han. The mountains are to the north of the village.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ਉੱਤਰ (uttar) is inherited from Sanskrit uttara, "upper, northern"; CDIAL lists Punjabi uttar, "the north", under it (CDIAL 1767).

Lesson 3 of 5.
ਪੂਰਬ (pūrab) — the east.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for north, then the Punjabi for south.

You'll want to know: ਪੂਰਬ.
ਪੂਰਬ — pūrab — "the east".
ਸੂਰਜ ਪੂਰਬ ਵਿੱਚ ਚੜ੍ਹਦਾ ਹੈ। sūraj pūrab vich chaṛhdā hai. The sun rises in the east.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ਪੂਰਬ (pūrab) goes back to Sanskrit pūrva, "in front; eastern" (Monier-Williams), in a half-learned form reshaped to Punjabi sounds; CDIAL gives no Punjabi form under it (CDIAL 8343).

Lesson 4 of 5.
ਪੱਛਮ (pachchham) — the west.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for south, then the Punjabi for east.

You'll want to know: ਪੱਛਮ.
ਪੱਛਮ — pachchham — "the west".
ਬੱਸ ਅੱਡਾ ਸ਼ਹਿਰ ਦੇ ਪੱਛਮ ਵਿੱਚ ਹੈ। bass aḍḍā shahir de pachchham vich hai. The bus stand is in the west of the town.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ਪੱਛਮ (pachchham) goes back to Sanskrit paścima, "behind; western" (Monier-Williams), in a half-learned form reshaped to Punjabi sounds; CDIAL lists only an older Punjabi pachvā, "western", under it (CDIAL 8007).

Lesson 5 of 5.
ਘੁਮਾਉਣਾ (ghumāuṇā) — to show someone around; to turn round.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for east, then the Punjabi for west.

You'll want to know: ਘੁਮਾਉਣਾ.
ਘੁਮਾਉਣਾ — ghumāuṇā — "to show someone around; to turn round".
ਮੈਂ ਮਹਿਮਾਨਾਂ ਨੂੰ ਸ਼ਹਿਰ ਘੁਮਾਵਾਂਗਾ। maiṁ mahimānāṁ nū̃ shahir ghumāvāṅgā. I will show the guests around the town.
Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਮਨਨ ਨੂੰ ਸ਼ਹਿਰ ਘੁਮਾਇਆ, maiṁ Manan nū̃ shahir ghumāiā.

The word, taken apart.
ਘੁਮਾਉਣਾ (ghumāuṇā) is the causative partner of ghummaṇā, "to turn, wander"; CDIAL lists Punjabi ghummaṇā under a reconstructed ghummati, "revolves" (CDIAL 4485).
