## You'll want to know: नक़द
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C426-RAQAM426-02]; assesses=[] -->

**नक़द** — *naqad* — "in cash; cash".

> मैंने दुकानदार को नक़द पैसे दिए।
>
> *maiṁne dukāndār ko naqad paise diye.*
>
> I paid the shopkeeper in cash.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**नक़द** (*naqad*) is usually traced to Arabic *naqd*, "ready money", through Persian; Hindi breaks the final qd with a vowel.
