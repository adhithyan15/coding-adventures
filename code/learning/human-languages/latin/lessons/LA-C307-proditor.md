## You'll want to know: prōditor, prōditōris
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C307-VENENUM-05]; assesses=[] -->

**prōditor, prōditōris** — "a traitor".

> Prōditor portās hostibus aperuit.
>
> *The traitor opened the gates to the enemy.*

Masculine, 3rd declension, from *prōdō*, 'to betray'. *Prōditor patriae*: a traitor to his country.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**prōditor** is built on *prōdō*, "to give up, to betray" (Lewis & Short), with the ending *-tor*.
