## You'll want to know: せかい
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C282-SEKAI282-01]; assesses=[] -->

**せかい** — *sekai* — "the world".

> **せかいで いちばん たかい やまは どこですか。** — *sekai de ichiban takai yama wa doko desu ka* — "Which is the highest mountain in the world?"

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**せかい** (*sekai*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *se*, "world" (Middle Chinese *syejH*) and *kai*, "boundary" (*keajH*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
