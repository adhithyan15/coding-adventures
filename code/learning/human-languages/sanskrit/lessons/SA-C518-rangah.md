## You'll want to know: रंगः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C518-TULIKA518-02]; assesses=[] -->

**रंगः** — *raṅgaḥ* — "paint; a dye".

> चित्रकारः रंगं जलेन संयोजयति।
>
> *citrakāraḥ raṅgaṃ jalena saṃyojayati.*
>
> The painter mixes the paint with water.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**रंगः** (*raṅgaḥ*) comes from the root √rañj, "to be coloured"; MW gives *raṅga* as colour, paint, dye, and also as a theatre, a stage (MW).
