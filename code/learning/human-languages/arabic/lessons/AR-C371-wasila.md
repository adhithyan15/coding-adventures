## You'll want to know: وسيلة
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C371-WASILA371-01]; assesses=[] -->

**وسيلة** — *wasīla* — "a means".

> الحافلة وسيلة رخيصة للسفر.
>
> *al-ḥāfila wasīla rakhīṣa li-s-safar.*
>
> The bus is a cheap means of travel.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**وسيلة** (*wasīla*) is built on the native Arabic root *w-s-l*, "to seek to draw near" (Lane). A *wasīla* is what brings you near to a goal: a means.
