Lesson 1 of 5.
vergleichen — to compare.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to forbid, then the German for to connect.

You'll want to know: vergleichen.
vergleichen — "to compare".

The word, taken apart.
vergleichen is ver- + gleich, "alike", + -en (EtymDB 2.1): to set things side by side to see how alike they are.
