Kannada, chapter 536: On Stage.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ನಾಟಕ (nāṭaka) — a stage play; a drama.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a violin, then the Kannada for to beat a drum.

You'll want to know: ನಾಟಕ.
ನಾಟಕ — nāṭaka — "a stage play; a drama".
ಶಾಲೆಯ ನಾಟಕದಲ್ಲಿ ನಾನು ರಾಜನ ಪಾತ್ರ ಮಾಡಿದೆ. śāleya nāṭakadalli nānu rājana pātra māḍide. I played the king's part in the school play.
ನಾಟಕ (nāṭaka) ಆಡು, to stage a play.

The word, taken apart.
ನಾಟಕ (nāṭaka) is borrowed from Sanskrit nāṭaka, "a dancer, an actor", and later a drama (CDIAL 7045), built on naṭa, "an actor".

Lesson 2 of 5.
ರಂಗಭೂಮಿ (raṅgabhūmi) — theatre; the stage.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to beat a drum, then the Kannada for a stage play.

You'll want to know: ರಂಗಭೂಮಿ.
ರಂಗಭೂಮಿ — raṅgabhūmi — "theatre; the stage".
ನನ್ನ ಮಾವ ರಂಗಭೂಮಿಯ ಹಳೆಯ ನಟ. nanna māva raṅgabhūmiya haḷeya naṭa. My uncle is a veteran stage actor.
ರಂಗ, stage + ಭೂಮಿ, ground.

The word, taken apart.
ರಂಗಭೂಮಿ (raṅgabhūmi) is a learned compound built from Sanskrit raṅga, "a place of public amusement, a stage" (CDIAL 10561), and bhūmi, "earth, ground" (CDIAL 9557).

Lesson 3 of 5.

You'll want to know: ಮುಖವಾಡ.
ಮುಖವಾಡ — mukhavāḍa — "a mask".
ನಾಟಕದಲ್ಲಿ ನಟರು ಮುಖವಾಡ ಹಾಕಿದ್ದರು. nāṭakadalli naṭaru mukhavāḍa hākiddaru. The actors wore masks in the play.
Also a false face: ಅವನ ಮುಖವಾಡ (mukhavāḍa) ಕಳಚಿತು, his mask slipped.

The word, taken apart.
ಮುಖವಾಡ (mukhavāḍa) is built on Sanskrit mukha, "mouth, face" (CDIAL 10158); the origin of the ending -vāḍa is not settled.

Lesson 4 of 5.
ಪ್ರದರ್ಶಿಸು (pradarśisu) — to perform; to put on show.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for theatre, then the Kannada for a mask.

You'll want to know: ಪ್ರದರ್ಶಿಸು.
ಪ್ರದರ್ಶಿಸು — pradarśisu — "to perform; to put on show".
ಮಕ್ಕಳು ವೇದಿಕೆಯ ಮೇಲೆ ಒಂದು ಸಣ್ಣ ನಾಟಕ ಪ್ರದರ್ಶಿಸಿದರು. makkaḷu vēdikeya mēle ondu saṇṇa nāṭaka pradarśisidaru. The children performed a short play on the stage.
Past (I …): ಪ್ರದರ್ಶಿಸಿದೆ, pradarśiside.
Also to exhibit: ಚಿತ್ರಗಳನ್ನು ಪ್ರದರ್ಶಿಸು (pradarśisu). ಪ್ರದರ್ಶನ is a show.

The word, taken apart.
ಪ್ರದರ್ಶಿಸು (pradarśisu) is built on Sanskrit pradarśana, "a showing, look, appearance" (Monier-Williams), with the verb ending -isu.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ಅಭಿನಯಿಸು (abhinayisu) — to act.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a mask, then the Kannada for to perform.

You'll want to know: ಅಭಿನಯಿಸು.
ಅಭಿನಯಿಸು — abhinayisu — "to act".
ಆ ಸಿನಿಮಾದಲ್ಲಿ ನನ್ನ ಸ್ನೇಹಿತ ಅಭಿನಯಿಸಿದನು. ā sinimādalli nanna snēhita abhinayisidanu. My friend acted in that film.
Past (I …): ಅಭಿನಯಿಸಿದೆ, abhinayiside.
ಅಭಿನಯ is acting.

The word, taken apart.
ಅಭಿನಯಿಸು (abhinayisu) is built on Sanskrit abhinaya, from abhi-nī, "to bring near, to act out" (Monier-Williams), with the verb ending -isu.
