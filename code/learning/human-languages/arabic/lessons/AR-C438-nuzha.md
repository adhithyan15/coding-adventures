## You'll want to know: نزهة
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C438-SIYAHA438-03]; assesses=[] -->

**نزهة** — *nuzha* — "an outing; a pleasure trip".

> خرجنا في نزهة إلى البحيرة.
>
> *kharajnā fī nuzha ilā al-buḥayra.*
>
> We went out on an outing to the lake.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**نزهة** (*nuzha*) is usually traced to the native Arabic root *n-z-h*, "to keep far from what is unclean".
