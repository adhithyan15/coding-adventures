## You'll want to know: la amenaza
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C530-PELIGRO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**la amenaza** — "the threat".

> Recibió una amenaza por teléfono.
>
> *He received a threat over the phone.*

*Amenazar* is to threaten.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*amenaza* comes from spoken Latin *\*mināciā* 'a threat' (EtymDB), built on *mināx* 'threatening', from *minārī* 'to threaten'.
