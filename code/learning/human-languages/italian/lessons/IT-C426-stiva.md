## You'll want to know: la stiva
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C426-BAGAGLI-02]; assesses=[] -->

**la stiva** — "the hold (of a ship or a plane)".

> Le valigie grandi vanno in stiva.
>
> *The big suitcases go in the hold.*

*Il bagaglio da stiva* — checked luggage.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*stiva* is built on *stivare* 'to stow, to load', which is inherited from Latin *stīpāre* 'to press together' (EtymDB).
