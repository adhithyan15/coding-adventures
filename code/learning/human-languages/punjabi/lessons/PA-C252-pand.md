## You'll want to know: ਪੰਡ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C252-GHR252-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ਪੰਡ** — *paṇḍ* — "a large bundle (carried on the head)".

> ਉਸ ਦੇ ਸਿਰ ਉੱਤੇ ਕੱਪੜਿਆਂ ਦੀ ਪੰਡ ਹੈ।
>
> *us de sir utte kappṛiāṁ dī paṇḍ hai.*
>
> There is a bundle of clothes on her head.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਪੰਡ** (*paṇḍ*) is inherited from Sanskrit *piṇḍa*, "lump, clod, piece"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *paṇḍ*, "bundle" (CDIAL 8168).
