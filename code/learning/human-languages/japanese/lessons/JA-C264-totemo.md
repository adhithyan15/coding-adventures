## You'll want to know: とても
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C264-KOROBU264-04]; assesses=[] -->

**とても** — *totemo* — "very; really".

> **あしが とても いたいです。** — *ashi ga totemo itai desu* — "My leg really hurts."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**とても** (*totemo*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, and it is usually written in kana (JMdict).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
