## You'll want to know: झिरपणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C444-DABKE444-04]; assesses=[] -->

**झिरपणे** — *jhirapṇe* — "to seep".

> पावसाचं पाणी छपरातून झिरपलं.
>
> *pāvsācaṁ pāṇī chaprātūn jhiraplaṁ.*
>
> The rainwater seeped through the roof.

Past (he …): तो झिरपला, *to jhiraplā*.

Slower than पाझरणे: drop by drop.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**झिरपणे** (*jhirapṇe*) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi *jhirapṇẽ*, "to trickle", under *\*jhirati* (CDIAL 5390).
