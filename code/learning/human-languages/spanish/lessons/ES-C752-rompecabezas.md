## You'll want to know: el rompecabezas
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C752-JUEGOS-02]; assesses=[] -->

**el rompecabezas** — "the jigsaw puzzle; the puzzle".

> Hicimos un rompecabezas de mil piezas.
>
> *We did a thousand-piece jigsaw.*

Same form in the plural. In Spain also *el puzle*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*rompecabezas* is built on *romper* "to break" and *cabeza* "head", from Latin *rumpere* and *caput* (EtymDB).
