## You'll want to know: సంస్కరణ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C407-NIRASANA407-05]; assesses=[] -->

**సంస్కరణ** — *saṁskaraṇa* — "a reform".

> విద్యా రంగంలో కొత్త సంస్కరణలు వచ్చాయి.
>
> *vidyā raṅgaṁlō kotta saṁskaraṇalu vaccāyi.*
>
> New reforms have come in education.

Plural: సంస్కరణలు, *saṁskaraṇalu*.

Mostly in the plural, సంస్కరణలు.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**సంస్కరణ** (*saṁskaraṇa*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *saṁskaraṇa*, "the act of putting together, preparing" (Monier-Williams); Telugu uses it for putting a system right.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
