Persian, chapter 235: Sharp, Available, Enough, Free of charge, Unemployed.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
تیز (tiz) — sharp.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for life, then the Persian for a part.

You'll want to know: تیز.
تیز — tiz — "sharp".

The word, taken apart.
tiz is a native Persian word, inherited rather than borrowed; it is usually traced to Middle Persian tēz, "sharp". EtymDB takes it back to the Indo-European root (s)teyg-, "to be sharp, to prick", the root usually also given for English stick.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for enough, then the Persian for free of charge.

You'll want to know: بیکار.
بیکار — bikâr — "unemployed".

The word, taken apart.
bikâr is bi-, "without", with kâr, "work": without work. Both are native Persian; kâr continues Middle Persian kār (EtymDB). (EtymDB also lists Arabic bikr, "virgin", a different word.)
