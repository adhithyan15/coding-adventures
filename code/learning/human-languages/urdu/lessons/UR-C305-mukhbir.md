## You'll want to know: مخبر
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C305-MUAMMA305-02]; assesses=[] -->

**مخبر** — *mukhbir* — "an informer".

> مخبر نے پولیس کو سب بتایا۔
>
> *mukhbir ne polīs ko sab batāyā.*
>
> The informer told the police everything.

Masculine or feminine, by the person meant.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**مخبر** (*mukhbir*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *mukhbir*, "one who informs", from the family of **خبر**.
