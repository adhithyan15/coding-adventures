German, chapter 222: Thrifty, Sporty, Strict, Likeable, Dead.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
sparsam — thrifty.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for independent, then the German for sunny.

You'll want to know: sparsam.
sparsam — "thrifty".

The word, taken apart.
sparsam is sparen, "to save", + -sam, the ending of English -some: given to saving.

The word, taken apart.
streng from Middle High German strenge from Old High German strengi, "strong" — inherited, the same word as English strong. (EtymDB 2.1) Strict is strong in rule.
