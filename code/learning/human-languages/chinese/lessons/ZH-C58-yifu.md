## You'll want to know: yīfu
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C58-THINGS58-05]; assesses=[] -->

*yīfu* — "clothes".

> *wǒ xǐhuan mǎi yīfu* — I love buying clothes.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*yīfu* is built from yī and fu, both inherited from Middle Chinese, *'j+j* and *bjuwk* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription). The dictionary glosses the first as the garment worn on the upper body, and lists 'clothes' among the senses of the second.
