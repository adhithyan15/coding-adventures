Russian, chapter 437: Getting lost.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
заблудиться (zabludítsya) — to get lost.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to fix, then the Russian for a receipt.

You'll want to know: заблудиться.
заблудиться (zabludítsya) — "to get lost".
В старом городе мы быстро заблудились. V stárom górode my býstro zabludílis. We quickly got lost in the old town.
Perfective. Future заблужусь, заблудишься.

The word, taken apart.
заблудиться (zabludítsya) is built from за-, блудить 'to wander, to stray' and -ся; EtymDB lists it with the sense 'to get lost'. The root is Proto-Slavic blǫdŭ (EtymDB), the same root as in блуждать (bluzhdát) 'to wander'.

Lesson 2 of 5.
ориентироваться (oriyentírovatsya) — to find one's way, to get one's bearings.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a receipt, then the Russian for to get lost.

You'll want to know: ориентироваться.
ориентироваться (oriyentírovatsya) — "to find one's way, to get one's bearings".
В новом городе я плохо ориентируюсь. V nóvom górode ya plókho oriyentíruyus. I am bad at finding my way in a new town.
Imperfective; perfective сориентироваться. Takes в + prepositional.

The word, taken apart.
ориентироваться (oriyentírovatsya) is usually traced to French orienter 'to orient, to set facing north' (EtymDB), on Latin oriēns 'rising; the east' (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 3 of 5.
блуждать (bluzhdát) — to roam, to wander.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to get lost, then the Russian for to find one's way.

You'll want to know: блуждать.
блуждать (bluzhdát) — "to roam, to wander".
Мы два часа блуждали по лесу. My dva chasá bluzhdáli po lésu. We wandered around the forest for two hours.
Imperfective; takes по + dative. Present блуждаю.

The word, taken apart.
блуждать (bluzhdát) is built on блуд (EtymDB), from Proto-Slavic blǫdŭ; the same root gives заблудиться (zabludítsya) 'to get lost'.

Lesson 4 of 5.
компас (kómpas) — a compass.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to find one's way, then the Russian for to roam.

You'll want to know: компас.
компас (kómpas) — "a compass".
Без компаса в лесу легко заблудиться. Bez kómpasa v lesú legkó zabludítsya. Without a compass it is easy to get lost in the forest.
Masculine. Sailors stress the second syllable: kompás.

The word, taken apart.
компас (kómpas) was borrowed from German Kompass (EtymDB).

Lesson 5 of 5.
навигатор (navigátor) — a satnav, a navigator.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to roam, then the Russian for a compass.

You'll want to know: навигатор.
навигатор (navigátor) — "a satnav, a navigator".
Навигатор показал нам дорогу. Navigátor pokazál nam dorógu. The satnav showed us the way.
Masculine. Also a ship's or a plane's navigator.

The word, taken apart.
навигатор (navigátor) is usually traced to Latin nāvigātor 'a sailor' (EtymDB lists the Latin word but not the Russian), from nāvigāre 'to sail', on nāvis 'a ship' (Lewis & Short).
