## You'll want to know: धर्मशाला
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C402-DHARMASHALA402-01]; assesses=[] -->

**धर्मशाला** — *dharmaśālā* — "a pilgrims' rest house".

> यात्रिकाः धर्मशालायां रात्रिं वसन्ति।
>
> *yātrikāḥ dharmaśālāyāṃ rātriṃ vasanti.*
>
> The pilgrims stay the night in the rest house.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**धर्मशाला** (*dharmaśālā*) is built from *dharma*, "right conduct, charity", and *śālā*, "a hall, a house"; MW gives the compound as a charitable asylum, especially a religious one (MW).
