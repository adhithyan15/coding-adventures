# మేనత్త (mēnatta) — a paternal aunt

## You'll want to know: మేనత్త
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C529-MENAMAMA529-02]; assesses=[] -->

**మేనత్త** — *mēnatta* — "a paternal aunt".

> మా మేనత్త పిల్లలతో నేను ఆడుకునేవాడిని.
>
> *mā mēnatta pillalatō nēnu āḍukunēvāḍini.*
>
> I used to play with my father's sister's children.

The father's sister; అత్త alone also means mother-in-law.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**మేనత్త** (*mēnatta*) joins native *atta*, "mother-in-law, father's sister" (DEDR 142), to *mēna-*, from DEDR's appendix of Indo-Aryan loans (DEDR App. 53).
