## You'll want to know: bestreiten
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C333-WIDERSPRUCH-05]; assesses=[] -->

**bestreiten** — "to deny, to dispute".

> Der Junge bestreitet, dass er das Fenster kaputt gemacht hat.
>
> *The boy denies that he broke the window.*

Inseparable and strong: *er bestreitet, er hat bestritten*. *Das kann man nicht bestreiten* — that cannot be denied.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*bestreiten* is inherited from Middle High German *bestrīten* "to fight against" (EtymDB), built on *strīten*, today *streiten* "to quarrel": you fight a claim.
