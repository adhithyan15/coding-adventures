Gujarati, chapter 425: Loans and Instalments.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
લોન (lon) — a bank loan.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a token, then the Gujarati for a bank card.

You'll want to know: લોન.
લોન — lon — "a bank loan".
તેણે ઘર માટે બેંકમાંથી લોન લીધી. teṇe ghar māṭe beṅkmā̃thī lon līdhī. He took a loan from the bank for a house.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
લોન (lon) is borrowed from English loan.

Lesson 2 of 5.
હપતો (hapto) — an instalment.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a bank card, then the Gujarati for a bank loan.

You'll want to know: હપતો.
હપતો — hapto — "an instalment".
મારે લોનનો હપતો દસમી તારીખે ભરવો પડે છે. māre lonno hapto dasmī tārīkhe bharvo paḍe che. I have to pay the loan instalment on the tenth.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
હપતો (hapto) is usually traced to Persian hafta, "a week": a sum paid at regular times, once by the week, now often by the month.

Lesson 4 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for an instalment, then the Gujarati for a broker.

You'll want to know: ગીરો.
ગીરો — gīro — "a pledge; a pawn (left as security)".
તેણે ઘરેણાં ગીરો મૂક્યાં. teṇe ghareṇā̃ gīro mūkyā̃. She pawned her jewellery.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ગીરો (gīro) is usually traced to Persian giraw, "a pledge, a pawn": what you leave with a lender until you pay.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ચૂકતે (cūkte) — paid in full; settled.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a broker, then the Gujarati for a pledge.

You'll want to know: ચૂકતે.
ચૂકતે — cūkte — "paid in full; settled".
આજે મેં લોન ચૂકતે કરી. āje mẽ lon cūkte karī. Today I paid off the loan in full.

The word, taken apart.
ચૂકતે (cūkte) is built on the same stem as ચૂકવવું, to pay off, which goes back to cukk, "to fall short, to stop", a form CDIAL reconstructs (CDIAL 4848).
