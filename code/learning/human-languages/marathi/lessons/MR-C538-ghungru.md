## You'll want to know: घुंगरू
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C538-NRUTYA538-03]; assesses=[] -->

**घुंगरू** — *ghuṅgrū* — "ankle bells".

> नाचताना तिच्या घुंगरांचा आवाज येत होता.
>
> *nāctānā ticyā ghuṅgrāñcā āvāj yet hotā.*
>
> As she danced the sound of her ankle bells could be heard.

Neuter.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**घुंगरू** (*ghuṅgrū*) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi *ghũghrū*, "bell", under *\*ghuṅghura* (CDIAL 4477), a word that copies the ringing.
