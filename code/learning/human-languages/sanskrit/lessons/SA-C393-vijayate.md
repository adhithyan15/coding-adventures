## You'll want to know: विजयते
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C393-VIJAYA393-01]; assesses=[] -->

**विजयते** — *vijayate* — "triumphs; wins through".

> यदि धैर्यम् अस्ति, तर्हि सः अवश्यं विजयते।
>
> *yadi dhairyam asti, tarhi saḥ avaśyaṃ vijayate.*
>
> If he has courage, he will surely win through.

Past (he …): सः शत्रुं विजितवान्, *saḥ śatruṃ vijitavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**विजयते** (*vijayate*) is built on *vi* and the root √ji, "to win"; MW gives *vi-√ji* "to conquer, to be victorious" (MW).
