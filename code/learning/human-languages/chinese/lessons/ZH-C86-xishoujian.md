## You'll want to know: xǐshǒujiān
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C86-XISHOUJIAN-01]; assesses=[] -->

*xǐshǒujiān* — "toilet; washroom".

> *qǐngwèn, xǐshǒujiān zài nǎr* — Excuse me, where is the toilet?

Its first and last characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*xǐshǒujiān* is built from xǐ 'to wash', shǒu 'hand', the 手 you write, and jiān 'room', the jiān of *fángjiān*. All three are inherited from Middle Chinese, *sejX*, *syuwX* and *kean* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription). The Guangyun glosses *sejX* 'to wash' and *kean* 'a gap; between'; 'a room' is a later sense of jiān.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ZH-LEX-C65-SORRY65-02, ZH-LEX-C86-NAR-01] -->

*qǐngwèn, xǐshǒujiān zài nǎr* may be the most useful sentence in the chapter: a polite opener, the thing, 在, and the question word in the place slot.
