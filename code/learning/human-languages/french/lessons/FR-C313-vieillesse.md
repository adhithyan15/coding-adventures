## You'll want to know: la vieillesse
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C313-RETRAITE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**la vieillesse** — "old age".

> Il a eu une vieillesse heureuse.
>
> *He had a happy old age.*

The opposite of *la jeunesse*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*vieillesse* is built on *vieil*, *vieux* "old", inherited from Late Latin *veclus*, from Latin *vetulus* "little old" (Lewis & Short).
