## You'll want to know: نزيل
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C441-ISTIQBAL441-02]; assesses=[] -->

**نزيل** — *nazīl* — "a hotel guest; a lodger".

> طلب النزيل غرفة بعيدة عن الشارع.
>
> *ṭalaba an-nazīl ghurfa baʿīda ʿan ash-shāriʿ.*
>
> The guest asked for a room far from the street.

Plural: نزلاء, *nuzalāʾ*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**نزيل** (*nazīl*) is built on the native Arabic root *n-z-l*, "to alight, to stop at a place" (Lane). A *nazīl* is one who has stopped somewhere for the night.
