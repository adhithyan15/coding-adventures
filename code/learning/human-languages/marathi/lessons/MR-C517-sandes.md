## You'll want to know: संदेश
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C517-JAL517-03]; assesses=[] -->

**संदेश** — *sandeś* — "a message".

> अरुणने मित्राला एक छोटा संदेश पाठवला.
>
> *aruṇne mitrālā ek choṭā sandeś pāṭhavlā.*
>
> Arun sent his friend a short message.

Masculine.

संदेश पाठवणे, to send a message.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**संदेश** (*sandeś*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *saṁdeśa*, "message, information" (Monier-Williams).
