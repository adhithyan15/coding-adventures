## You'll want to know: угощение
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C315-UGOSHCHENIYE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**угощение** (*ugoshchéniye*) — "treats, refreshments".

> Угощение было очень вкусным.
>
> *Ugoshchéniye býlo óchen vkúsnym.*
>
> The treats were very tasty.

Neuter.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*угощение* is built on *угощать* "to treat", from *гость* "a guest", inherited from Proto-Slavic *gostĭ*, PIE *gʰóstis* (EtymDB).
