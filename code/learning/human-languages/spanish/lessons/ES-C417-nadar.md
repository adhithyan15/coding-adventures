# nadar — move through the water

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-CURSO, ES-LEX-PISCINA] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Recall **la piscina** and **un curso**. What might a course at a pool
teach? **Nadar**, to swim.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-NADAR]; assesses=[] -->

Spanish **nadar** continues Latin **natāre**, to swim. English keeps the root in
**natatorium**, a building with a swimming pool. The infinitive ends in
**-ar**, like many familiar action words.

## Grammar Lens: learn to swim
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-NADAR, ES-LEX-CURSO] -->

After **aprender**, Spanish uses **a** before another verb: **aprender a nadar**,
to learn to swim. The related noun is **la natación**, swimming, as in **curso
de natación**.
