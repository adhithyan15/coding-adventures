## You'll want to know: きんにく
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C442-INSIDE442-02]; assesses=[] -->

**きんにく** — *kinniku* — "a muscle".

> **まいにち はしって、あしの きんにくが つよく なりました。** — *mainichi hashitte, ashi no kinniku ga tsuyoku narimashita* — "I ran every day, and my leg muscles got stronger."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**きんにく** (*kinniku*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *kin*, "muscle" (Middle Chinese *kj+n*) and *niku*, "meat" (*nyuwk*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
