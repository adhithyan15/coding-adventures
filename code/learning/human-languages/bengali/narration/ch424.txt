Bengali, chapter 424: Prices Up and Down.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
সেল (sel) — a sale (at reduced prices).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a size (of clothes or shoes), then the Bengali for to palm off.

You'll want to know: সেল.
সেল — sel — "a sale (at reduced prices)".
পুজোর আগে দোকানে সেল চলে। pujor āge dokāne sel chôle. Before the Pujas the shops have sales on.

The word, taken apart.
সেল (sel) is borrowed from English sale, in the shop-window sense of prices cut for a time.

গত বছর ব্যবসায় মন্দা ছিল। gôtô bôchhôr bæbsāy môndā chhilô. Last year business was slack.

The word, taken apart.
মন্দা (môndā) goes back to Sanskrit manda, "slow, weak, slack" (Monier-Williams): trade that has gone slow.

চাহিদা — chāhidā — "demand (for goods)".
শীতে কম্বলের চাহিদা বেশি। shīte kômbôler chāhidā beshi. In winter there is more demand for blankets.

The word, taken apart.
চাহিদা (chāhidā) is built on চাহ-, the old stem of চাওয়া, "to want", from the reconstructed cāh, "see, desire" (CDIAL 4775): what people want to buy.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ওঠানামা করা (oṭhānāmā kôrā) — to go up and down; to fluctuate.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a slump, then the Bengali for demand (for goods).

You'll want to know: ওঠানামা করা.
ওঠানামা করা — oṭhānāmā kôrā — "to go up and down; to fluctuate".
বাজারে মাছের দাম রোজ ওঠানামা করে। bājāre māchher dām roj oṭhānāmā kôre. Fish prices in the market go up and down every day.
Past (he/she …): সেই দাম ওঠানামা করল, sei dām oṭhānāmā kôrlô.
ওঠানামা alone is the rise and fall: দামের ওঠানামা, the ups and downs of prices; সিঁড়িতে ওঠানামা, going up and down stairs.

The word, taken apart.
ওঠানামা করা (oṭhānāmā kôrā) pairs ওঠা, "to rise" (CDIAL 1900), with নামা, "to come down" (CDIAL 6964), and makes a verb of the pair with করা.
