# తప్పించు (tappiñcu) — to avoid; to dodge

## You'll want to know: తప్పించు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C446-KUDUPU446-03]; assesses=[] -->

**తప్పించు** — *tappiñcu* — "to avoid; to dodge".

> డ్రైవరు గుంటను తప్పించి వెళ్ళాడు.
>
> *ḍraivaru guṇṭanu tappiñci veḷḷāḍu.*
>
> The driver steered round the pothole.

Past (I …): తప్పించాను, *tappiñcānu*.

తప్పించుకో, to escape, is the same verb with -కో: you get yourself out of something. తప్పించు is to steer clear of a thing.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**తప్పించు** (*tappiñcu*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 3071 lists Telugu *tappincu* beside *tappu*, "to slip, to miss, to escape".
