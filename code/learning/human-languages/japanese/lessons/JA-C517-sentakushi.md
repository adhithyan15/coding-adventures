## You'll want to know: せんたくし
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C517-DECIDE517-05]; assesses=[] -->

**せんたくし** — *sentakushi* — "an option; a choice".

> **もし せんたくしが ふたつ あったら、どちらを えらびますか。** — *moshi sentakushi ga futatsu attara, dochira o erabimasu ka* — "If there were two options, which would you choose?"

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**せんたくし** (*sentakushi*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): three Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *sen*, "elect" (Middle Chinese *sjwenX*), *taku*, "choose" and *shi*, "limb" (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
