Russian, chapter 416: On board the train.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
проводник (provodník) — a sleeping-car attendant; a guide.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to announce, then the Russian for to make it.

You'll want to know: проводник.
проводник (provodník) — "a sleeping-car attendant; a guide".
Проводник принёс нам чай. Provodník prinyós nam chay. The attendant brought us tea.
Feminine проводница. The stress moves: проводника (provodniká). Also a conductor of electricity.

The word, taken apart.
проводник (provodník) is built on проводить 'to lead through' with -ник 'one who'. Водить is inherited from Proto-Slavic voditi 'to lead' (EtymDB).

Lesson 2 of 5.
тамбур (támbur) — a vestibule (between train carriages).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to make it, then the Russian for a sleeping-car attendant.

You'll want to know: тамбур.
тамбур (támbur) — "a vestibule (between train carriages)".
Пассажир разговаривал по телефону в тамбуре. Passazhír razgovárival po telefónu v támbure. The passenger was talking on the phone in the vestibule.
Masculine. Also the small space inside the door of a building.

The word, taken apart.
тамбур (támbur) is usually traced to French tambour 'a drum', which EtymDB traces to Arabic ṭunbūr; the sense of a small closed space came later. EtymDB does not list the Russian word.

Lesson 4 of 5.
тележка (telézhka) — a trolley, a cart.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a vestibule, then the Russian for a porter.

You'll want to know: тележка.
тележка (telézhka) — "a trolley, a cart".
На вокзале мы взяли тележку для багажа. Na vokzále my vzyáli telézhku dlya bagázha. At the station we took a trolley for the luggage.
Feminine; a diminutive of телега — a cart. In shops too: тележка (telézhka) для покупок.

The word, taken apart.
тележка (telézhka) is a diminutive of телега 'a cart' (EtymDB lists тележка (telézhka)). The origin of телега itself is not settled.

Lesson 5 of 5.
предъявлять (predyavlyát) — to present (a ticket or a document).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a porter, then the Russian for a trolley.

You'll want to know: предъявлять.
предъявлять (predyavlyát) — "to present (a ticket or a document)".
В вагоне пассажиры предъявляют билеты и паспорта. V vagóne passazhíry predyavlyáyut biléty i pasportá. In the carriage the passengers present their tickets and passports.
Imperfective; perfective предъявить (предъявлю, предъявишь). Formal.

The word, taken apart.
предъявлять (predyavlyát) is built from пред- 'before' and являть 'to show': to put something before someone's eyes. EtymDB traces явить to Proto-Slavic aviti 'to make visible'.
