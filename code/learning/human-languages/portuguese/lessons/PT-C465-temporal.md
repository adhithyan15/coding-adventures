## You'll want to know: o temporal
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C465-TEMPORAL-03]; assesses=[] -->

**o temporal** — "the storm (wind and rain)".

> O temporal deixou muitas árvores no chão.
>
> *The storm left many trees on the ground.*

A long spell of bad weather, often in winter.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*temporal* 'storm' is built on *tempo* 'weather' (EtymDB), which is inherited from Latin *tempus* 'time, season'.
