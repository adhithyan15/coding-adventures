Sanskrit, chapter 459: Respect and Contempt.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
अवज्ञा (avajñā) — contempt.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for arrogant, then the Sanskrit for modest.

You'll want to know: अवज्ञा.
अवज्ञा — avajñā — "contempt".
गुरोः अवज्ञा न उचिता। guroḥ avajñā na ucitā. Contempt for a teacher is not proper.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
अवज्ञा (avajñā) is built from ava, "down", and the root √jñā, "to know", the root of जानाति: to think little of; MW gives "contempt, disesteem, disrespect" (MW).

तिरस्करोति — tiraskaroti — "insults".
सः अतिथिं तिरस्करोति, तत् अनुचितम्। saḥ atithiṃ tiraskaroti, tat anucitam. He insults the guest; that is improper.
Past (he …): सः अतिथिं तिरस्कृतवान्, saḥ atithiṃ tiraskṛtavān.

The word, taken apart.
तिरस्करोति (tiraskaroti) is built from tiras, "across, aside", and the root √kṛ, "to do", the root of करोति: to set aside; MW gives "to set aside" and later senses of slighting (MW).
