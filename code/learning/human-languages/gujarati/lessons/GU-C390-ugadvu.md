# ઉગાડવું (ugāḍvũ) — to grow plants

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Gujarati for mature, then the Gujarati for barren.

## You'll want to know: ઉગાડવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C390-PAK390-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ઉગાડવું** — *ugāḍvũ* — "to grow plants".

> જો તમે આંગણામાં ટામેટાં ઉગાડશો તો બજારમાંથી ખરીદવાં નહીં પડે.
>
> *jo tame ā̃gṇāmā̃ ṭāmeṭā̃ ugāḍśo to bajārmā̃thī kharīdvā̃ nahī̃ paḍe.*
>
> If you grow tomatoes in the courtyard, you will not have to buy them at the market.

Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે બગીચામાં ફૂલ ઉગાડ્યાં, *teṇe bagīcāmā̃ phūl ugāḍyā̃*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ઉગાડવું** (*ugāḍvũ*) is the causative of **ઊગવું** (*ūgvũ*), "to rise, to sprout", from a reconstructed *udgāti*, "rises, comes forth" (CDIAL 1954).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
