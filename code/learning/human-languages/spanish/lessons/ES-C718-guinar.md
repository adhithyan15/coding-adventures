## You'll want to know: guiñar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C718-SONRISA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**guiñar** — "to wink".

> Me guiñó un ojo y sonreí.
>
> *He winked at me and I smiled.*

*Guiñar un ojo* — to wink. *Un guiño* — a wink.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

For *guiñar* the origin is not settled; a Germanic source has been suggested, and some see an imitative word, but none is proved.
