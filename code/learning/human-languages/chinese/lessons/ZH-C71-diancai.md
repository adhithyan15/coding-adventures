## You'll want to know: diǎncài
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C71-TASTE71-05]; assesses=[] -->

*diǎncài* — "to order food".

> *wǒ xiǎng diǎncài* — I'd like to order.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*diǎncài* is built from 点 'a dot, to mark' and cài 'dish', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *temX* and *tshojH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription); the dictionary glosses the second as any plant fit to eat. Marking the dishes you want is the likely picture, but the step is not settled.
