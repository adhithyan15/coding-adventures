## You'll want to know: だれか
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C522-SOME522-02]; assesses=[] -->

**だれか** — *dareka* — "someone; anyone".

> **もし だれか きたら、おしえて ください。** — *moshi dareka kitara, oshiete kudasai* — "If someone comes, please tell me."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**だれか** (*dareka*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese: built from *dare*, "who", and the particle *ka* (JMdict).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
