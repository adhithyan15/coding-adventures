## You'll want to know: उन्मादः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C466-MEDHA466-03]; assesses=[] -->

**उन्मादः** — *unmādaḥ* — "madness; frenzy".

> उन्मादेन सः मार्गे धावति।
>
> *unmādena saḥ mārge dhāvati.*
>
> In a frenzy he runs along the road.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**उन्मादः** (*unmādaḥ*) is built from *ud*, "up", and the root √mad, "to be drunk, to rejoice"; MW gives "insanity, madness", "mania (as illness)" and "intoxication" (MW).
