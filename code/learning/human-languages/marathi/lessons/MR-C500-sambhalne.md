## You'll want to know: सांभाळणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C500-SANGOPAN500-02]; assesses=[] -->

**सांभाळणे** — *sāmbhāḷṇe* — "to take care of; to look after".

> आई कामावर गेली की आजी बाळाला सांभाळते.
>
> *āī kāmāvar gelī kī ājī bāḷālā sāmbhāḷte.*
>
> When Mother goes to work, Grandmother looks after the baby.

Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने बाळ सांभाळलं, *tyāne bāḷ sāmbhāḷlaṁ*.

Also to handle with care: सांभाळून, carefully.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
