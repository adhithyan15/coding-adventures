## You'll want to know: सम्भावयति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C367-SAMBHAVA367-02]; assesses=[] -->

**सम्भावयति** — *sambhāvayati* — "thinks likely; supposes".

> यदि आकाशः कृष्णः, तर्हि अहं वर्षां सम्भावयामि।
>
> *yadi ākāśaḥ kṛṣṇaḥ, tarhi ahaṃ varṣāṃ sambhāvayāmi.*
>
> If the sky is dark, I think rain likely.

Past (he …): सः वर्षां सम्भावितवान्, *saḥ varṣāṃ sambhāvitavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सम्भावयति** (*sambhāvayati*) is built on *sam* and the root √bhū, "to be", in its causative form; MW gives the senses "to suggest a possibility" and "to suppose anything possible" (MW).
