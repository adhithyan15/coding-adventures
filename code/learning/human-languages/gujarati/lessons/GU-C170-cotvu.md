## You'll want to know: ચોંટવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C170-SWEET170-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ચોંટવું** — *cõṭvũ* — "to stick".

> **ભાત તપેલીમાં ચોંટે છે.** — *bhāt tapelīmā̃ cõṭe che* — "The rice sticks to the pot."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

The origin of **ચોંટવું** (*cõṭvũ*) is not settled. CDIAL lists no Gujarati form of it with the sense of sticking, and EtymDB has no entry for it.
