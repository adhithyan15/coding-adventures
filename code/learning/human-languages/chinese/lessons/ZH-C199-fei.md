## You'll want to know: féi
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C199-SIZE199-03]; assesses=[] -->

*féi* — "loose; baggy (of clothes)".

> *zhè tiáo kùzi yǒudiǎnr féi* — These trousers are a bit baggy.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*féi* is inherited from Middle Chinese *bj+j* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'fat' and 'fleshy'. Its use for loose clothes is not given there, so how it came is not settled.
