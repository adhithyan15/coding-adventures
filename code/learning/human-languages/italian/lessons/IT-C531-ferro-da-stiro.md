## You'll want to know: il ferro da stiro
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C531-BUCATO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**il ferro da stiro** — "iron (for clothes)".

> Attenta, il ferro da stiro è ancora caldo.
>
> *Careful, the iron is still hot.*

*Stirare* — to iron.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ferro da stiro* is built from *ferro* "iron", inherited from Latin *ferrum* (EtymDB), and *stiro*, from *stirare* "to stretch, to iron".
