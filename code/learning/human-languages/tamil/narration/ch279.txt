Tamil, chapter 279: Worker, Meeting, Manager, Engineer, Lawyer.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
தொழிலாளி (toḻilāḷi) — a worker, a labourer.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for a watermelon, then the Tamil for murukku.

You'll want to know: தொழிலாளி.
தொழிலாளி — toḻilāḷi — "a worker, a labourer".

The word, taken apart.
தொழிலாளி (toḻilāḷi) is native: toḻil, "work, occupation" (DEDR 3524), and āḷi, "one who does", from āḷ, "person" (DEDR 399).

Lesson 2 of 5.
கூட்டம் (kūṭṭam) — a meeting; a crowd.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for murukku, then the Tamil for a worker.

You'll want to know: கூட்டம்.
கூட்டம் — kūṭṭam — "a meeting; a crowd".

The word, taken apart.
கூட்டம் (kūṭṭam) is native Dravidian. DEDR 1882 lists Malayalam kūṭuka, "to come together" and Kannada kūḍu. It comes from kūṭu, "to come together, to assemble".

Lesson 4 of 5.
பொறியாளர் (poṟiyāḷar) — an engineer.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for a meeting; a crowd, then the Tamil for a manager.

You'll want to know: பொறியாளர்.
பொறியாளர் — poṟiyāḷar — "an engineer".

The word, taken apart.
பொறியாளர் (poṟiyāḷar) is poṟi, "machine, contrivance", and native āḷar, "persons", from āḷ (DEDR 399): "machine person". DEDR has poṟi only as "mark, impression", so the origin of the "machine" sense is uncertain.
