## You'll want to know: die Webseite
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C394-INTERNET-02]; assesses=[] -->

**die Webseite** — "the web page, the website".

> Die Öffnungszeiten stehen auf der Webseite.
>
> *The opening hours are on the website.*

Plural: *die Webseiten*. Also written *die Website*, said the English way.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Webseite* joins English *web* to *Seite* "page, side", inherited from Old High German *sīta* and Proto-Germanic *sīdōn* (EtymDB), after English *web page*.
