## You'll want to know: रसायनम्
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C485-SUSRUSA485-05]; assesses=[] -->

**रसायनम्** — *rasāyanam* — "a rejuvenating tonic".

> वैद्यः वृद्धाय रसायनं ददाति।
>
> *vaidyaḥ vṛddhāya rasāyanaṃ dadāti.*
>
> The physician gives the old man a tonic.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**रसायनम्** (*rasāyanam*) is built from **रसः**, "sap, essence", and *ayana*, "a way"; MW gives "a medicine supposed to prevent old age and prolong life, an elixir" (MW).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
