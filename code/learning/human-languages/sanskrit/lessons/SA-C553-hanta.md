## You'll want to know: हन्त
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C553-SKHALANA553-02]; assesses=[] -->

**हन्त** — *hanta* — "come now; alas".

> हन्त, यदि अहं ह्यः अगमिष्यम्, तर्हि तम् अद्रक्ष्यम्।
>
> *hanta, yadi ahaṃ hyaḥ agamiṣyam, tarhi tam adrakṣyam.*
>
> Alas, if I had gone yesterday, I would have seen him.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**हन्त** (*hanta*) is the particle MW gives for urging and for asking attention, "come, see", and in later use for grief, joy or pity; MW gives no derivation, so its origin is uncertain (MW).
