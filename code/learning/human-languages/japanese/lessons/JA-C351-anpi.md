## You'll want to know: あんぴ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C351-MANICHI351-04]; assesses=[] -->

**あんぴ** — *anpi* — "whether someone is safe".

> **たいふうが きたら、かぞくの あんぴを かくにんします。** — *taifū ga kitara, kazoku no anpi o kakunin shimasu* — "If a typhoon comes, I check that my family are safe."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**あんぴ** (*anpi*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *an*, "peaceful" (Middle Chinese *'an*) and *hi*, "negate" (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription). After *an* the second is said *pi*.
