## You'll want to know: जिताना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C534-MAICH534-03]; assesses=[] -->

**जिताना** — *jitānā* — "to bring victory to; to win (a match) for".

> अरुण ने आख़िर में दौड़कर टीम को जिताया।
>
> *Aruṇ ne ākhir meṁ dauṛkar ṭīm ko jitāyā.*
>
> In the end Arun ran it in and won the match for the team.

Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने अपनी टीम को जिताया, *usne apnī ṭīm ko jitāyā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**जिताना** (*jitānā*) is the causative of *jītnā*, "to win", which comes from Sanskrit *jita*, "won, conquered" (CDIAL 5224): to make someone win.
