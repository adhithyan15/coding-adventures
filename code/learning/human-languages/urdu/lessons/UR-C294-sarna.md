## You'll want to know: سڑنا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C294-QAHAT294-04]; assesses=[] -->

**سڑنا** — *saṛnā* — "to rot".

> گرمی سے پھل سڑ گئے۔
>
> *garmī se phal saṛ gae.*
>
> The fruit rotted in the heat.

Past (it …): وہ پھل سڑ گیا, *voh phal saṛ gayā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**سڑنا** (*saṛnā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *saṛnā*, "to rot", under Sanskrit *śáṭati* (CDIAL 12268).
