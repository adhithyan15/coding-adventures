# Bengali B1 mock rubric

Score either B1 mock against [task-shapes/b1.json](../../task-shapes/b1.json).
Reading, listening, writing, and speaking are separate 100-point papers; every
paper must independently reach 60/100. A 240/400 total cannot compensate for a
failed paper, and every section must be attempted.

## Reading and listening

Each of the four reading parts is worth 25 points. Each of the four listening
parts is also worth 25 points. Divide each part's points equally among its keyed
items and retain one decimal place. Play the transaction and interview twice;
play the longer narrative and short talk once after their unscored orienting
previews. Use 130–150 words per minute throughout. No transcript, romanization,
dictionary, or translator is allowed.

## Writing

The 50–70 word functional text is worth 35 points: task fulfilment and relevant
content 12, reader and purpose awareness 7, register and organisation 6,
Bengali vocabulary and grammar 6, and Bengali-script control 4. The 130–170
word connected text is worth 65 points: task fulfilment and relevant content
20, organisation and cohesion 15, audience-aware register 10, Bengali
vocabulary and grammar 14, and Bengali-script control 6. Script control includes
the matra line, hasanta, and correct side and legibility of vowel signs.
Romanized, translated, non-Bengali-script, or visibly copied answers earn
zero independent-writing credit.

## Speaking

The interview is worth 20 points, the prepared account 30, collaborative
planning 30, and follow-up discussion 20. Within each part award 30% for task
fulfilment and relevant content, 25% for interaction or organisation, 30% for
Bengali vocabulary and grammar, and 15% for pronunciation and fluency.
Successful clarification and repair count positively. No written answer script
is allowed; ten-minute preparation notes may contain keywords, not sentences.

## Readiness boundary

The mock is executable but not self-validating. A readiness claim additionally
requires clean timed administration and qualified human review of the paper,
recordings, productive responses, and scoring decisions.
