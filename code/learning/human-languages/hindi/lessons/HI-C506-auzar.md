## You'll want to know: औज़ार
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C506-KARIGAR506-02]; assesses=[] -->

**औज़ार** — *auzār* — "a tool".

> बढ़ई अपने औज़ार लकड़ी के एक डिब्बे में रखता है।
>
> *Baṛhaī apne auzār lakṛī ke ek ḍibbe meṁ rakhtā hai.*
>
> The carpenter keeps his tools in a wooden box.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**औज़ार** (*auzār*) is usually traced to Arabic *awzār*, a plural meaning "loads" or "gear", which reached Hindi through Persian; the sense "tools" is the one Persian and Hindi use.
