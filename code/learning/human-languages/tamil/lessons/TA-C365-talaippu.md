# தலைப்பு (talaippu) — a title; a heading

## You'll want to know: தலைப்பு
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C365-KATTURAI365-02]; assesses=[] -->

**தலைப்பு** — *talaippu* — "a title; a heading".

> இந்தக் கட்டுரையின் தலைப்பு என்ன?
>
> *intak kaṭṭuraiyiṉ talaippu eṉṉa?*
>
> What is the title of this essay?

From தலை, head: the head of a piece of writing.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**தலைப்பு** (*talaippu*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 3103 lists Tamil *talai*, "head, top, end", and *talaippu*, "beginning; edge of a cloth", the noun that now names a heading.
