## You'll want to know: xíguàn
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C158-WAKE158-05]; assesses=[] -->

*xíguàn* — "to be used to; a habit".

> *wǒ hái méi xíguàn liù diǎn qǐchuáng* — I'm still not used to getting up at six.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*xíguàn* is built from *xí* and *guàn*, both inherited from Middle Chinese, *zip* and *kwaenH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription). The dictionary glosses the first 'to learn' and explains the second with the first: practised.
