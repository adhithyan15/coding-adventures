## You'll want to know: تپش
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C288-CHIRAGH288-05]; assesses=[] -->

**تپش** — *tapish* — "scorching heat".

> دوپہر کی تپش سے سب تھک گئے۔
>
> *dopahar kī tapish se sab thak gae.*
>
> Everyone was worn out by the scorching midday heat.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**تپش** (*tapish*) is usually traced to Persian *tapish*, "heat, a burning". It is the heat you feel on the skin, of fire or of the sun.
