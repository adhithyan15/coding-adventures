## You'll want to know: provável
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C373-RISCO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**provável** — "likely, probable".

> É provável que chova amanhã.
>
> *It is likely to rain tomorrow.*

*É provável que* takes the subjunctive.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*provável* comes from Latin *probābilis* "that may be believed or proved, likely" (EtymDB, Lewis & Short), built on *probāre* "to test, to prove".
