# ಕೈಸಾಮಾನು (kaisāmānu) — hand luggage

## You'll want to know: ಕೈಸಾಮಾನು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C436-HAMALI436-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ಕೈಸಾಮಾನು** — *kaisāmānu* — "hand luggage".

> ಕೈಸಾಮಾನು ಏಳು ಕಿಲೋಗಿಂತ ಹೆಚ್ಚು ಇರಬಾರದು.
>
> *kaisāmānu ēḷu kilōginta heccu irabāradu.*
>
> Hand luggage must not weigh more than seven kilos.

ಕೈ, hand, plus ಸಾಮಾನು, luggage: the bag you keep with you on a plane or a bus.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಕೈಸಾಮಾನು** (*kaisāmānu*) is a Kannada compound of native *kai*, "hand", and ಸಾಮಾನು, "luggage, things", which is usually traced to Persian *sāmān*, "goods, equipment".
