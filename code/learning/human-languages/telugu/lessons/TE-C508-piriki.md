# పిరికి (piriki) — timid; cowardly

## You'll want to know: పిరికి
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C508-DHAIRYAM508-05]; assesses=[] -->

**పిరికి** — *piriki* — "timid; cowardly".

> మా కుక్క చాలా పిరికిది.
>
> *mā kukka cālā pirikidi.*
>
> Our dog is very timid.

పిరికివాడు, a coward.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**పిరికి** (*piriki*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 4200 lists Telugu *piṟiki*, "timid, cowardly", with Kannada *piṟiki*, "coward".
