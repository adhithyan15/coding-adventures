## You'll want to know: el piropo
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C744-NOVIOS-02]; assesses=[] -->

**el piropo** — "the compliment (flirtatious)".

> Mi marido siempre me echa piropos.
>
> *My husband is always paying me compliments.*

*Echar un piropo* — to pay someone a flirtatious compliment. From a stranger in the street, a *piropo* is now widely seen as harassment.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*piropo* comes from Latin *pyrōpus* "gold-bronze", from Greek *pyrōpós* "fire-coloured" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short); the name of a red gem became the word for a sparkling compliment.
