## You'll want to know: ಸಮುದಾಯ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C397-NAGARIKA397-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸಮುದಾಯ** — *samudāya* — "a community".

> ನಮ್ಮ ಸಮುದಾಯ ಒಟ್ಟಿಗೆ ಹಬ್ಬ ಆಚರಿಸುತ್ತದೆ.
>
> *namma samudāya oṭṭige habba ācarisuttade.*
>
> Our community celebrates the festival together.

ಸಮುದಾಯ ಭವನ, a community hall.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸಮುದಾಯ** (*samudāya*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *samudāya*, "a combination, a multitude, a whole" (Monier-Williams).
