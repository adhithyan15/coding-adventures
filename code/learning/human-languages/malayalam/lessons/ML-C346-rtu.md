# ഋതു (ṛtu) — a season

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ML-CONCEPT-C14-KAALANGAL-01, ML-GRAMMAR-C346-ETTAVUM-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say "the rainy season", then "summer". (*maḻakkālaṁ*,
*vēnalkkālaṁ*.) Then "the most". (*ēṟṟavuṁ*.)

## You'll want to know: ഋതു
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C346-RTU-01]; assesses=[ML-CONCEPT-C14-KAALANGAL-01, ML-GRAMMAR-C346-ETTAVUM-01, ML-LEX-C139-TIME-05] -->

**ഋതു** — *ṛtu* — "a season".

> **മഴക്കാലം എനിക്ക് ഏറ്റവും ഇഷ്ടമുള്ള ഋതുവാണ്.**
>
> *maḻakkālaṁ enikkŭ ēṟṟavuṁ iṣṭamuḷḷa ṛtuvāṇŭ.* — "The rainy season is my favourite season."

> **പഴയ കണക്കിൽ ഒരു വർഷത്തിൽ ആറ് ഋതുക്കൾ ഉണ്ട്.**
>
> *paḻaya kaṇakkil oru varṣattil āṟŭ ṛtukkaḷ uṇṭŭ.* — "In the old reckoning, a year has six seasons."

In everyday talk, **-കാലം** names each season, as in **മഴക്കാലം**. **ഋതു** is the word for the
season itself, in writing, songs and the calendar. Most speakers say it close to *rutu*.

Its first letter is new; the next lesson puts it in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ഋതു** (*ṛtu*) ← Sanskrit *ṛtu*, "a fixed time; a season", taken over whole (Monier-Williams). The
same dictionary records the later Sanskrit count of six seasons in a year, the count the old
reckoning keeps.
