# രണ്ട് ടിക്കറ്റ് വേണം (raṇṭŭ ṭikkaṟṟŭ vēṇaṁ) — two tickets, please

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ML-LEX-C121-TRAVEL-02, ML-LEX-C210-VASTU210-02, ML-PHRASE-C102-WHAT-TIME-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "a ticket", "a seat", and "at what time?" (*ṭikkaṟṟŭ*, *sīṟṟŭ*, *etra maṇikkŭ*.)

## Grammar Lens: at the counter
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-GRAMMAR-C344-BUY-TICKET-01]; assesses=[ML-LEX-C121-TRAVEL-02, ML-LEX-C58-WANT-01, ML-LEX-C225-KRIYA225-04, ML-GRAMMAR-C74-AAN-01] -->

Name the place with **-ലേക്ക്**, "to", then how many, then **വേണം**:

> **കൊച്ചിയിലേക്ക് രണ്ട് ടിക്കറ്റ് വേണം.**
>
> *kocciyilēkkŭ raṇṭŭ ṭikkaṟṟŭ vēṇaṁ.* — "Two tickets to Kochi, please."

For the way back, use **മടങ്ങുക**, "to return", in its **-ആൻ** form, with **-ഉം**, "too". At a
counter you will also hear the English *return ticket*.

> **മടങ്ങാനും ടിക്കറ്റ് വേണം.**
>
> *maṭaṅṅānuṁ ṭikkaṟṟŭ vēṇaṁ.* — "I need a ticket to come back too."

Then the price: **എത്രയാണ്?** (*etrayāṇŭ?*), "how much is it?"

## Grammar Lens: times and seats
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-GRAMMAR-C344-TRAIN-TIME-01]; assesses=[ML-LEX-C158-THINGS158-02, ML-LEX-C50-LEAVE-04, ML-PHRASE-C102-WHAT-TIME-01, ML-LEX-C225-KRIYA225-03, ML-LEX-C210-VASTU210-02] -->

> **ട്രെയിൻ എത്ര മണിക്ക് പുറപ്പെടും?**
>
> *ṭreyin etra maṇikkŭ puṟappeṭuṁ?* — "What time does the train leave?"

**പുറപ്പെടുക** is "to set out"; the future **പുറപ്പെടും** asks about the timetable. So
**എപ്പോൾ എത്തും?** (*eppōḷ ettuṁ?*), "when will it arrive?", and **സീറ്റ് ഉണ്ടോ?** (*sīṟṟŭ uṇṭō?*), "is there a seat?"
