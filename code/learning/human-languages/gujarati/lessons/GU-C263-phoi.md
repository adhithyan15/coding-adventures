## You'll want to know: ફોઈ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C263-INLAWS263-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ફોઈ** — *phoī* — "paternal aunt".

> **શું ફોઈ આજે આવશે?** — *śũ phoī āje āvśe?* — "Will Aunt come today?"

Your father's sister.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ફોઈ** (*phoī*) is a tadbhava. It comes from *\*phupphu-*, *\*phupphī-*, "father's sister", reconstructed forms (CDIAL 9089).
