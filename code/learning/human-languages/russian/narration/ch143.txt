Russian, chapter 143: To fly, To ride, To walk, To come, To leave.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
летать (letát) — to fly.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to catch, then the Russian for to push.

You'll want to know: летать.
летать (letát) — "to fly".

The word, taken apart.
летать (letát) is the repeated-action partner of лететь, "to fly", Proto-Slavic letěti — inherited (Vasmer). The same root is in самолёт, "aeroplane".

Lesson 2 of 5.
ездить (yézdit) — to ride, to go (by transport).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to push, then the Russian for to fly.

You'll want to know: ездить.
ездить (yézdit) — "to ride, to go (by transport)".

The word, taken apart.
ездить (yézdit) goes with езда, "a ride" (Vasmer), Old Russian ezdŭ, "a journey". Vasmer thinks езда probably comes from the root of ехать, "to go (by vehicle)", and Sanskrit yāti, "travels".

Lesson 3 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to fly, then the Russian for to ride.

You'll want to know: ходить.
ходить (khodít) — "to walk, to go (on foot)".

The word, taken apart.
ходить (khodít) from Proto-Slavic xoditi, built on ход, "a going" (Vasmer) — inherited. The same root is in вход, "entrance", and выход, "exit".

Lesson 4 of 5.
приходить (prikhodít) — to come, to arrive.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to ride, then the Russian for to walk.

You'll want to know: приходить.
приходить (prikhodít) — "to come, to arrive".

The word, taken apart.
приходить (prikhodít) is при-, "to, toward", with ходить (khodít), "to walk": to come to a place. Vasmer ties ход to the Indo-European root sed-, "to sit", its х grown from an s after prefixes.
