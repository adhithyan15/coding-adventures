Telugu, chapter 378: A Brawl.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
కొట్లాట (koṭlāṭa) — a brawl; a scuffle.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a patrol, then the Telugu for an action.

You'll want to know: కొట్లాట.
కొట్లాట — koṭlāṭa — "a brawl; a scuffle".
బజారులో ఇద్దరి మధ్య కొట్లాట జరిగింది. bajārulō iddari madhya koṭlāṭa jarigindi. There was a brawl between two men in the market.
Plural: కొట్లాటలు, koṭlāṭalu.
From కొట్టు, to hit: a fight with blows.

The word, taken apart.
కొట్లాట (koṭlāṭa) is native Dravidian, built on koṭṭu, "to beat, strike" (DEDR 2063).

Lesson 2 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for an action, then the Telugu for a brawl.

You'll want to know: కాల్పులు.
కాల్పులు — kālpulu — "gunfire; shooting".
రాత్రి అడవిలో కాల్పులు వినిపించాయి. rātri aḍavilō kālpulu vinipiñcāyi. Gunfire was heard in the forest at night.
A plural noun from కాల్చు, to burn or to fire a gun.

The word, taken apart.
కాల్పులు (kālpulu) is native Dravidian, the plural noun of kālcu, "to burn, set fire to" (DEDR 1500), the verb also used of firing a gun.

Lesson 3 of 5.
గాయపరచు (gāyaparacu) — to injure; to wound.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a brawl, then the Telugu for gunfire.

You'll want to know: గాయపరచు.
గాయపరచు — gāyaparacu — "to injure; to wound".
దొంగ కాపలాదారును కత్తితో గాయపరిచాడు. doṅga kāpalādārunu kattitō gāyaparicāḍu. The thief injured the watchman with a knife.
Past (I …): గాయపరిచాను, gāyaparicānu.
You know గాయం, a wound; the ending -పరచు turns it into something done to another.

The word, taken apart.
గాయపరచు (gāyaparacu) is built on గాయం, "a wound", which goes back to Sanskrit ghāta, "a blow, a bruise" (CDIAL 4460), with paracu, which makes a verb of a noun.

Lesson 5 of 5.
గుమిగూడు (gumigūḍu) — to gather round.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to injure, then the Telugu for to grapple.

You'll want to know: గుమిగూడు.
గుమిగూడు — gumigūḍu — "to gather round".
ప్రమాదం జరిగిన చోట జనం గుమిగూడారు. pramādaṁ jarigina cōṭa janaṁ gumigūḍāru. People gathered where the accident happened.
Past (I …): గుమిగూడాను, gumigūḍānu.
గుమి, a crowd, and కూడు, to come together.

The word, taken apart.
గుమిగూడు (gumigūḍu) is a native compound: gumi, "a crowd" (DEDR 1741), and kūḍu, "to meet, gather" (DEDR 1882).
