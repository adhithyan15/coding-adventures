## You'll want to know: गुस्सा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C108-FEEL-02]; assesses=[] -->

**गुस्सा** (*gussā*) — "anger".

**मुझे गुस्सा आया** — "I got angry", literally "anger came to me", on the **आना** you know.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

A feeling that comes to you.
