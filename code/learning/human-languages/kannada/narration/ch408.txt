5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಕೇಂದ್ರೀಕರಿಸು (kēndrīkarisu) — to focus.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to analyse, then the Kannada for a survey.

You'll want to know: ಕೇಂದ್ರೀಕರಿಸು.
ಕೇಂದ್ರೀಕರಿಸು — kēndrīkarisu — "to focus".
ಪರೀಕ್ಷೆಗೆ ಮೊದಲು ನಾನು ಗಣಿತದ ಮೇಲೆ ಗಮನ ಕೇಂದ್ರೀಕರಿಸಿದೆ. parīkṣege modalu nānu gaṇitada mēle gamana kēndrīkariside. Before the exam I focused on mathematics.
Past (I …): ಕೇಂದ್ರೀಕರಿಸಿದೆ, kēndrīkariside.
What you focus on takes ಮೇಲೆ.

The word, taken apart.
ಕೇಂದ್ರೀಕರಿಸು (kēndrīkarisu) is built on Sanskrit kendra, "the centre of a circle" (Monier-Williams), itself taken from Greek kentron, with -īkarisu, "to make into".

Lesson 3 of 5.
ಐಚ್ಛಿಕ (aicchika) — optional; elective.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to focus, then the Kannada for a study.

You'll want to know: ಐಚ್ಛಿಕ.
ಐಚ್ಛಿಕ — aicchika — "optional; elective".
ಶಾಲೆಯಲ್ಲಿ ಕನ್ನಡ ಕಡ್ಡಾಯ, ಸಂಗೀತ ಐಚ್ಛಿಕ. śāleyalli kannaḍa kaḍḍāya, saṅgīta aicchika. At school Kannada is compulsory and music is optional.
Its opposite is ಕಡ್ಡಾಯ, compulsory. ಐಚ್ಛಿಕ (aicchika) ವಿಷಯ, an elective subject.

The word, taken apart.
ಐಚ್ಛಿಕ (aicchika) is borrowed from Sanskrit aicchika, "optional, arbitrary, at will" (Monier-Williams), an adjective formed from icchā, "a wish".

Lesson 4 of 5.
ಪಠ್ಯಕ್ರಮ (paṭhyakrama) — a syllabus.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a study, then the Kannada for optional.

You'll want to know: ಪಠ್ಯಕ್ರಮ.
ಪಠ್ಯಕ್ರಮ — paṭhyakrama — "a syllabus".
ಈ ವರ್ಷ ಪಠ್ಯಕ್ರಮ ಬದಲಾಯಿತು. ī varṣa paṭhyakrama badalāyitu. The syllabus changed this year.
ಪಠ್ಯ, a text to be read; ಪಠ್ಯಪುಸ್ತಕ, a textbook.

The word, taken apart.
ಪಠ್ಯಕ್ರಮ (paṭhyakrama) is a modern word built from Sanskrit paṭhya, "to be read", from paṭh, "to read", and krama, "a step, an order" (CDIAL 3577).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for optional, then the Kannada for a syllabus.

You'll want to know: ಪುನರಾವರ್ತನೆ.
ಪುನರಾವರ್ತನೆ — punarāvartane — "revision; repetition".
ಪರೀಕ್ಷೆಗೆ ಮೊದಲು ಪುನರಾವರ್ತನೆ ಮುಖ್ಯ. parīkṣege modalu punarāvartane mukhya. Revision is important before an exam.

The word, taken apart.
ಪುನರಾವರ್ತನೆ (punarāvartane) is built from Sanskrit punar, "again", and āvartana, "turning round, revolving" (Monier-Williams).
