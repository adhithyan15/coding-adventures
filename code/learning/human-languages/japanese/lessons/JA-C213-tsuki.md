## You'll want to know: つき
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C213-SKY213-02]; assesses=[] -->

**つき** — *tsuki* — "the moon; a month".

> **あしたは よなかに つきが でます。** — *ashita wa yonaka ni tsuki ga demasu* — "Tomorrow the moon will come out at midnight."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**つき** (*tsuki*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi, and its older origin is not settled (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
