## You'll want to know: a rajada
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C465-TEMPORAL-01]; assesses=[] -->

**a rajada** — "the gust (of wind)".

> Uma rajada de vento levou o meu chapéu.
>
> *A gust of wind took my hat.*

Forecasts give *rajadas* in kilometres per hour.

## The word, taken apart
