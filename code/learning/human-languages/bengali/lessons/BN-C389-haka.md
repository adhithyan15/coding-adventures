## You'll want to know: হাঁকা
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C389-CHALOK389-05]; assesses=[] -->

**হাঁকা** — *hā̃kā* — "to call out; to cry (stops or wares)".

> কন্ডাক্টর পাড়ার নাম হাঁকলেন।
>
> *kônḍākṭôr pāṛār nām hā̃klen.*
>
> The conductor called out the name of the neighbourhood.

Past (he/she …): তিনি নাম হাঁকলেন, *tini nām hā̃klen*.

Conductors হাঁকে the stops from the door; hawkers হাঁকে their goods in the lane.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**হাঁকা** (*hā̃kā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists Bengali *hā̃kā*, "to call, to drive off", under Sanskrit *hakkayati*, "calls to" (CDIAL 13939).
