# সংকোচ (sôngkoch) — reluctance; bashful hesitation

## You'll want to know: সংকোচ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C454-SONGKOCH454-01]; assesses=[] -->

**সংকোচ** — *sôngkoch* — "reluctance; bashful hesitation".

> সংকোচ না করে তোমার প্রশ্নটা জিজ্ঞাসা করো।
>
> *sôngkoch nā kôre tomār prôshnôṭā jigyāsā kôro.*
>
> Don't hold back: ask your question.

সংকোচ is the shrinking-back of a guest or a newcomer who does not want to impose: সংকোচ করবেন না, please don't hold back.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**সংকোচ** (*sôngkoch*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *saṃkoca*, "contraction, shrinking together" (Monier-Williams): shrinking back.
