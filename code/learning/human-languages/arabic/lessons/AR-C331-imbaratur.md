## You'll want to know: إمبراطور
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C331-IMBARATUR331-05]; assesses=[] -->

**إمبراطور** — *imbarāṭūr* — "an emperor".

> حكم الإمبراطور بلادا واسعة.
>
> *ḥakama al-imbarāṭūr bilādan wāsiʿa.*
>
> The emperor ruled wide lands.

An empire is إمبراطورية.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**إمبراطور** (*imbarāṭūr*) is usually traced to Latin *imperator*, "a commander", which reached Arabic through a European language.
