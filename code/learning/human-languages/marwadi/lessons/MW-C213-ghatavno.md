# घटावणो (ghaṭāvṇo) — to lower (a price)

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MW-LEX-C213-PRICE213-01, MW-LEX-C213-PRICE213-02] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Marwadi for the cost, then the Marwadi for to decrease.

## You'll want to know: घटावणो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C213-PRICE213-03]; assesses=[] -->

**घटावणो** — *ghaṭāvṇo* — "to lower (a price)".

> आप भाव थोड़ु घटावो।
>
> *āp bhāv thoṛu ghaṭāvo.*
>
> Please lower the price a little.

Past (I …): म्हैं भाव घटायो, *mhaĩ bhāv ghaṭāyo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**घटावणो** (*ghaṭāvṇo*) is the causative of **घटणो**, "to decrease", inherited from the same reconstructed *\*ghaṭṭati*; CDIAL lists Old Marwari *ghaṭāvai*, which it glosses "belittles" (CDIAL 4415).
