# Hear *garmī* — heat

## You'll want to know
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-GARMI-01]; assesses=[] -->

> *garmī* — **heat; hot weather**

The source also groups this word with summer. Keep the narrow heat meaning for
now; the season contrast comes later.
