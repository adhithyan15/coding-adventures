## You'll want to know: ਖੁੰਢਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C297-TOUCH297-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ਖੁੰਢਾ** — *khuṇḍhā* — "blunt".

> ਇਹ ਚਾਕੂ ਖੁੰਢਾ ਹੈ।
>
> *eh chākū khuṇḍhā hai.*
>
> This knife is blunt.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਖੁੰਢਾ** (*khuṇḍhā*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*khuṇṭha*, "defective"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *khuṇḍhā*, "blunt", under it (CDIAL 3899).
