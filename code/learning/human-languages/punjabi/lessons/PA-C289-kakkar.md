## You'll want to know: ਕੱਕਰ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C289-COLD289-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ਕੱਕਰ** — *kakkar* — "frost".

> ਕੱਲ੍ਹ ਸਵੇਰੇ ਘਾਹ ਉੱਤੇ ਕੱਕਰ ਹੋਵੇਗਾ।
>
> *kall savere ghāh utte kakkar hovegā.*
>
> Tomorrow morning there will be frost on the grass.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਕੱਕਰ** (*kakkar*) is inherited from Sanskrit *karkara*, "hard, firm"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *kakkar*, "frost", under it (CDIAL 2819).
