## You'll want to know: ખરાશ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C478-KAPH478-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ખરાશ** — *kharāś* — "a scratchy throat".

> ઠંડું પાણી પીધા પછી ગળામાં ખરાશ થઈ.
>
> *ṭhanḍũ pāṇī pīdhā pachī gaḷāmā̃ kharāś thaī.*
>
> After drinking cold water I got a scratchy throat.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ખરાશ** (*kharāś*) is usually traced to Persian *xarāš*, "a scratch, a scraping".
