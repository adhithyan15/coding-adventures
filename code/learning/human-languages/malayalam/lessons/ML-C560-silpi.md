## You'll want to know: ശില്പി
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C560-SILPAM560-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ശില്പി** — *śilpi* — "a sculptor".

> ആ ശില്പി തടിയിൽ ഒരു ആനയെ കൊത്തി.
>
> *ā śilpi taṭiyil oru ānaye kotti.*
>
> That sculptor carved an elephant in wood.

One who makes ശില്പങ്ങൾ; വാസ്തുശില്പി, an architect, holds it.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ശില്പി** (*śilpi*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *śilpin*, "skilled in art, an artisan" (CDIAL 12471).
