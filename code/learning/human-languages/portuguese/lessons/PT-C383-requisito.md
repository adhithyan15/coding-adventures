## You'll want to know: o requisito
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C383-EXCECAO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**o requisito** — "the requirement".

> Falar inglês é um requisito para este trabalho.
>
> *Speaking English is a requirement for this job.*

*Cumprir os requisitos* — to meet the requirements.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*requisito* is a learned borrowing from Latin *requīsītus* "required, sought" (EtymDB), the participle of *requīrere*, built on *quaerere* "to seek" (Lewis & Short).
