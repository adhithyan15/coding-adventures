## You'll want to know: переносить
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C377-PLANY-01]; assesses=[] -->

**переносить** (*perenosít*) — "to postpone, to move (to another time); to carry over".

> Мы переносим встречу на понедельник.
>
> *My perenósim vstréchu na ponedélnik.*
>
> We are moving the meeting to Monday.

Imperfective; present *переношу, переносишь*; perfective *перенести*. *Переносить на* + accusative — to move to. It also means to bear: *плохо переносить холод*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**переносить** is built from *пере-* "across, over" and *носить* "to carry", inherited from Proto-Slavic: to carry a meeting over to another day.
