## You'll want to know: むかい
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C195-CORNER195-03]; assesses=[] -->

**むかい** — *mukai* — "across the street; the place opposite".

> **ぎんこうの むかいに やっきょくが あります。** — *ginkō no mukai ni yakkyoku ga arimasu* — "There is a pharmacy across from the bank."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**むかい** (*mukai*) is native Japanese, inherited rather than borrowed, derived from *mukau*, "to face" (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
