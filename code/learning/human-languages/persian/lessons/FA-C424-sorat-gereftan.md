## You'll want to know: سرعت گرفتن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C424-SORAT-02]; assesses=[] -->

**سرعت گرفتن** (*sor'at gereftan*) — "to speed up, to gather speed".

> بعد از شهر اتوبوس سرعت گرفت.
>
> *Ba'd az shahr otobus sor'at gereft.*
>
> After the town the bus sped up.

*sor'at* + *gereftan* (to take). Present stem *gir-*: *sor'at migirad*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*sor'at gereftan* is built from *sor'at* 'speed', borrowed from Arabic *surʿa* (EtymDB), and the native verb *gereftan* 'to take', which is usually traced to Old Persian *grab-* 'to seize'.
