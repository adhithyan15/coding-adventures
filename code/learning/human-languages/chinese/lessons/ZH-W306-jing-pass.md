## Script — the silk piece, then a fork over 工
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-SCRIPT-JING-PASS-01]; assesses=[] -->

> 经

**Three pieces: the silk piece on the left; on the right a fork over 工.** Count the pieces, not the strokes.

**The silk piece, three strokes.** As on the left of 给: two strokes that each drop to the left and turn to the right, then a stroke rising to the right.

**The fork, two strokes.** A bar that turns into a sweep down to the lower left, then a short stroke falling to the right.

**工, three strokes**: a short bar, a vertical, and a long bar across the foot.

3 + 2 + 3 = eight.
