## You'll want to know: బుర్రకథ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C373-ATMAKATHA373-04]; assesses=[] -->

**బుర్రకథ** — *burrakatha* — "a burrakatha ballad".

> జాతరలో మేము బుర్రకథ విన్నాము.
>
> *jātaralō mēmu burrakatha vinnāmu.*
>
> We heard a burrakatha at the fair.

A Telugu storytelling show: a lead singer and two companions sing and talk through a tale.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**బుర్రకథ** (*burrakatha*) is a Telugu compound: native *burra*, "an empty gourd shell; the head" (DEDR 4332), and *katha*, "a story", from Sanskrit.
