Gujarati, chapter 169: Khichdi, bhakhri and papad.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ભાખરી (bhākhrī) — bhakhri, thick flatbread.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for griddle, then the Gujarati for to roast.

You'll want to know: ભાખરી.
ભાખરી — bhākhrī — "bhakhri, thick flatbread".
દાદા સવારે ભાખરી ખાય છે. — dādā savāre bhākhrī khāy che — "Grandfather eats bhakhri in the morning."

The word, taken apart.
ભાખરી (bhākhrī) is inherited. CDIAL lists the kindred masculine bhākhro, "lump of bread", under bhakkha, "lump", a reconstructed form, though not bhākhrī itself (CDIAL 9330).

Lesson 2 of 5.
ખીચડી (khīcḍī) — khichdi, rice and lentils.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to roast, then the Gujarati for bhakhri.

You'll want to know: ખીચડી.
ખીચડી — khīcḍī — "khichdi, rice and lentils".
રાત્રે અમે ખીચડી જમીએ છીએ. — rātre ame khīcḍī jamīe chīe — "At night we eat khichdi."

The word, taken apart.
ખીચડી (khīcḍī) is a tadbhava. It comes from Sanskrit khiccā, "a dish of rice and peas", extended with -ḍ-, and CDIAL glosses the Gujarati word "rice and lentils" (CDIAL 3880).

પાપડ પાતળો છે. — pāpaḍ pātḷo che — "The papad is thin."

પાપડ (pāpaḍ) is a tadbhava. It comes from Sanskrit parpaṭa, "a thin cake of rice or pease-meal", and CDIAL lists the Gujarati pāpaḍ, "a thin cake" (CDIAL 7934).

Lesson 4 of 5.
કડાઈ (kaḍāī) — wok, deep pan.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for khichdi, then the Gujarati for papad.

You'll want to know: કડાઈ.
કડાઈ — kaḍāī — "wok, deep pan".
કડાઈમાં તેલ ગરમ છે. — kaḍāīmā̃ tel garam che — "The oil in the wok is hot."

The word, taken apart.
કડાઈ (kaḍāī) is a tadbhava. It comes from Sanskrit kaṭāha, "boiler, saucepan with handles", and CDIAL glosses the Gujarati word "frying-pan" (CDIAL 2638).

Lesson 5 of 5.
ઢાંકણું (ḍhā̃kṇũ) — lid.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for papad, then the Gujarati for wok.

You'll want to know: ઢાંકણું.
ઢાંકણું — ḍhā̃kṇũ — "lid".
તપેલીનું ઢાંકણું ક્યાં છે? — tapelīnũ ḍhā̃kṇũ kyā̃ che? — "Where is the lid of the pot?"

The word, taken apart.
ઢાંકણું (ḍhā̃kṇũ) is a tadbhava, kin to ḍhā̃kvũ, "to cover". CDIAL lists it, glossed "cover, lid", under ḍhakk, ḍhaṅk, "cover" (CDIAL 5574).
