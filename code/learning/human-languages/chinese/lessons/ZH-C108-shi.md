## You'll want to know: 试
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C108-SHOP108-01]; assesses=[] -->

**试** — *shì* — "to try; to try on".

> **这个能试吗** *zhège néng shì ma* — Can I try this one on?

The new character, in pieces: 试 — the speech piece of 说 on the left; on the right six strokes: a horizontal across the top, a small three-stroke piece low under it, and a long stroke that slants down and hooks, with a dot at its top right. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**试** (*shì*) is inherited from Middle Chinese *syiH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'to use'. Trying a thing by putting it to use is the usual explanation, but the step is not given there.
