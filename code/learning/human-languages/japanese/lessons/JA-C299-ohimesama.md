# おひめさま (ohimesama) — a princess

## You'll want to know: おひめさま
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C299-OUSAMA299-02]; assesses=[] -->

**おひめさま** — *ohimesama* — "a princess".

> **おうさまには うつくしい おひめさまが いました。** — *ōsama ni wa utsukushii ohimesama ga imashita* — "The king had a beautiful daughter."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**おひめさま** (*ohimesama*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, built from *o-*, "the polite prefix", *hime*, "princess", and *sama*, "the honorific" (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
