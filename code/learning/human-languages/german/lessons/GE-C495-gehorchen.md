## You'll want to know: gehorchen
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C495-ERZIEHUNG-04]; assesses=[] -->

**gehorchen** — "to obey".

> Der Hund gehorcht nur meiner Mutter.
>
> *The dog only obeys my mother.*

With a dative: *dem Lehrer gehorchen*. Inseparable: *hat gehorcht*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*gehorchen* is built on *horchen* "to listen, to hearken" (EtymDB) with *ge-*: to listen and do as you are told.
