Lesson 1 of 5.
der Staubsauger — a vacuum cleaner.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for an oven, then the German for a dishwasher.

You'll want to know: der Staubsauger.
der Staubsauger — "a vacuum cleaner".

The word, taken apart.
Staubsauger is the word for "dust", Old High German stoup, + a noun from the verb "to suck", Old High German sūgan from Proto-Germanic sūganą, English suck (EtymDB 2.1).
