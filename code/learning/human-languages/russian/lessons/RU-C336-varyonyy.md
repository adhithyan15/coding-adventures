## You'll want to know: варёный
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C336-GOTOVO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**варёный** (*varyónyy*) — "boiled".

> На завтрак он съел варёное яйцо.
>
> *Na závtrak on syel varyónoye yaytsó.*
>
> He had a boiled egg for breakfast.

*Варёная колбаса* (*Varyónaya kolbasá*) — a soft boiled sausage.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*варёный* is the old participle of *варить* "to boil", inherited from Proto-Slavic *variti* (EtymDB).
