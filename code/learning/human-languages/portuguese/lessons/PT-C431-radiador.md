## You'll want to know: o radiador
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C431-AQUECIMENTO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**o radiador** — "the radiator".

> O radiador do quarto está frio.
>
> *The radiator in the room is cold.*

A car has *um radiador* too.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*radiador* is built on *radiar* 'to give off rays', a learned borrowing from Latin *radiāre*, on *radius* 'ray, spoke' (Lewis & Short).
