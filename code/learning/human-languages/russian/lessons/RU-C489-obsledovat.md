## You'll want to know: обследовать
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C489-CHECKUP-01]; assesses=[] -->

**обследовать** (*obslédovat*) — "to examine (medically)".

> Врач внимательно обследовал пациента.
>
> *Vrach vnimátelno obslédoval patsiyénta.*
>
> The doctor examined the patient carefully.

Imperfective and perfective (one form for both). Present обследую.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*обследовать* is built on *об-* and *следовать* 'to follow', from *след* 'track, trail' (EtymDB lists all three without a source).
