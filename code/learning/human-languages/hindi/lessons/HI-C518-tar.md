## You'll want to know: तार
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C518-TAAR518-01]; assesses=[] -->

**तार** — *tār* — "a wire; a string".

> बिजली का तार बहुत पुराना है।
>
> *Bijlī kā tār bahut purānā hai.*
>
> The electric wire is very old.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**तार** (*tār*) is usually traced to Persian *tār*, "a thread, a string".
