Malayalam, chapter 135: Around Town.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ചന്ത (canta) — a market.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for a spoon, then the Malayalam for a plate.

You'll want to know: ചന്ത.
ചന്ത (canta) — "a market", where മീൻ and പച്ചക്കറി are sold.

What you've built.
Where the shopping happens.

Guided Practice.

Lesson 3 of 5.
പള്ളി (paḷḷi) — a church, a mosque.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for a market, then the Malayalam for a temple.

You'll want to know: പള്ളി.
പള്ളി (paḷḷi) — both "a church" and "a mosque"; the word is older than the difference. It is also in പള്ളിക്കൂടം, "school".

What you've built.
One word, two buildings.
