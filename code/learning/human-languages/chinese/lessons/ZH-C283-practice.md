## The exchange
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-PERFORMANCE-DIRECTIONS-01]; assesses=[ZH-GRAMMAR-ZENME-ZOU-01, ZH-LEX-C283-WANG-01, ZH-LEX-C283-ZAI-01, ZH-LEX-C283-LUGUO-01, ZH-LEX-C65-SORRY65-02, ZH-LEX-C111-FRONT111-04, ZH-LEX-C111-FRONT111-05, ZH-LEX-C82-SIDES82-03, ZH-LEX-C190-TOWN190-01] -->

> **A:** 请问 去书店怎么走
>
> *qǐngwèn, qù shūdiàn zěnme zǒu* — Excuse me, how do I get to the bookshop?
>
> **B:** *wǎng qián zǒu* — Go straight ahead.
>
> **B:** *dào lùkǒu wǎng yòu guǎi* — Turn right at the crossing.
>
> **A:** *duìbuqǐ, qǐng zài shuō yí cì* — Sorry, could you say that again?
>
> **B:** *wǎng qián zǒu, dào lùkǒu wǎng yòu guǎi* — Straight ahead, then right at the crossing.
>
> **A:** *yuǎn ma* — Is it far?
>
> **B:** *bù yuǎn, wǒ měitiān lùguò* — Not far. I go past it every day.
>
> **A:** 谢谢 再见
>
> *xièxie, zàijiàn* — Thank you. Goodbye.

Directions run in the order you walk them, and the person is left out: *wǎng qián zǒu*, then *dào lùkǒu wǎng yòu guǎi*. Asking again is *qǐng zài shuō yí cì*.

## What you've built — asking the way, and following it
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ZH-PERFORMANCE-DIRECTIONS-01, ZH-SCRIPT-ZEN-01, ZH-SCRIPT-ZOU-01, ZH-GRAMMAR-ZENME-ZOU-01, ZH-LEX-C283-WANG-01, ZH-LEX-C283-LUGUO-01, ZH-LEX-C283-ZAI-01] -->

A question, three answers, and a way to hear them twice:

- **去火车站怎么走** *qù huǒchēzhàn zěnme zǒu* — the question.
- *wǎng qián zǒu* — straight ahead.
- *dào lùkǒu wǎng zuǒ guǎi* — left at the crossing.
- **我每天路过** *wǒ měitiān lùguò* — I pass it every day.
- *qǐng zài shuō yí cì* — once more, please.

**Listen.** *Qù xuéxiào zěnme zǒu?*

**Speak.** Give the way from your door to the nearest shop, in three steps.
