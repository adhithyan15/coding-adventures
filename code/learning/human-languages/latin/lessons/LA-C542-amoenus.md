## You'll want to know: amoenus, amoena, amoenum
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C542-STRI-05]; assesses=[] -->

**amoenus, amoena, amoenum** — "lovely, pleasant (of places)".

> Prope rīvum locus amoenus est.
>
> *There is a lovely spot near the stream.*

Of gardens, groves and views. *Locus amoenus* — a pleasant place.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**amoenus** is a native Latin adjective; Lewis & Short reports links with *amō* "to love" and with a Greek word, but the origin is not settled.
