Kannada, chapter 532: Song, Dance and Laughter.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
ಗೀತೆ (gīte) — a song.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a swing, then the Kannada for a toy.

You'll want to know: ಗೀತೆ.
ಗೀತೆ — gīte — "a song".
ಶಾಲೆಯ ಸಮಾರಂಭದಲ್ಲಿ ಮಕ್ಕಳು ಒಂದು ಗೀತೆ ಹಾಡಿದರು. śāleya samārambhadalli makkaḷu ondu gīte hāḍidaru. The children sang a song at the school function.
Formal; in talk people say ಹಾಡು. ನಾಡಗೀತೆ, the state song.

The word, taken apart.
ಗೀತೆ (gīte) goes back to Sanskrit gīta, "singing" (CDIAL 4167), from gai, "to sing".

[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a dance form, then the Kannada for humour.

You'll want to know: ಜೋಗುಳ.
ಜೋಗುಳ — jōguḷa — "a lullaby".
ಅಮ್ಮ ಜೋಗುಳ ಹಾಡಿದಾಗ ಮಗು ಮಲಗಿತು. amma jōguḷa hāḍidāga magu malagitu. When the mother sang a lullaby, the baby fell asleep.
Built on ಜೋ ಜೋ, the soothing sound sung over a cradle.

The word, taken apart.
ಜೋಗುಳ (jōguḷa) is native Dravidian, built on Kannada jō, "hush!", the word sung in lullabies (DEDR 2869).

Lesson 5 of 6.
ಮೆಲುಕು ಹಾಕು (meluku hāku) — to reminisce; to chew over.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for humour, then the Kannada for a lullaby.

You'll want to know: ಮೆಲುಕು ಹಾಕು.
ಮೆಲುಕು ಹಾಕು — meluku hāku — "to reminisce; to chew over".
ಅಜ್ಜ ತಮ್ಮ ಬಾಲ್ಯದ ದಿನಗಳನ್ನು ಮೆಲುಕು ಹಾಕಿದರು. ajja tamma bālyada dinagaḷannu meluku hākidaru. Grandfather went back over his childhood days.
Past (I …): ಮೆಲುಕು ಹಾಕಿದೆ, meluku hākide.
ಮೆಲುಕು is a cow chewing the cud: going back over memories again and again.

The word, taken apart.
ಮೆಲುಕು ಹಾಕು (meluku hāku) is a native Kannada phrase: meluku, "bringing food up again to chew the cud" (DEDR 5077), with ಹಾಕು, "to put".

What you've built.
