Lesson 1 of 5.
пыль (pyl) — dust.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a subscriber, then the Russian for a gamer.

You'll want to know: пыль.
пыль (pyl) — "dust".
На полках много пыли. Na pólkakh mnógo pýli. There's a lot of dust on the shelves.
Feminine; no plural.

The word, taken apart.
пыль (pyl) is inherited from Proto-Slavic pylъ (EtymDB).

Lesson 2 of 5.
уборка (ubórka) — cleaning, tidying up.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a gamer, then the Russian for dust.

You'll want to know: уборка.
уборка (ubórka) — "cleaning, tidying up".
В субботу у нас генеральная уборка. V subbótu u nas generálnaya ubórka. On Saturday we're having a big clean.
Feminine. Генеральная уборка (ubórka) — a thorough clean.

The word, taken apart.
уборка (ubórka) is built on убрать 'to tidy away', from у- and брать 'to take'; EtymDB lists уборка (ubórka) without a source.

Lesson 3 of 5.
пылесосить (pylesósit) — to vacuum.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for dust, then the Russian for cleaning.

You'll want to know: пылесосить.
пылесосить (pylesósit) — "to vacuum".
Сын пылесосит ковёр в гостиной. Syn pylesósit kovyór v gostínoy. My son is vacuuming the carpet in the living room.
Imperfective; present пылесошу, пылесосишь (с becomes ш). Perfective пропылесосить.

The word, taken apart.
пылесосить (pylesósit) is built on пылесос 'a vacuum cleaner', a compound of пыль (pyl) 'dust' and сосать 'to suck'; EtymDB has no entry.

Lesson 5 of 5.
вытирать (vytirát) — to wipe; to dry.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to vacuum, then the Russian for a cloth.

You'll want to know: вытирать.
вытирать (vytirát) — "to wipe; to dry".
После ужина брат моет тарелки, а я их вытираю. Pósle úzhina brat móyet tarélki, a ya ikh vytiráyu. After dinner my brother washes the plates and I dry them.
Imperfective; perfective вытереть (вытру; past вытер). Вытирать пыль (pyl) — to dust.

The word, taken apart.
вытирать (vytirát) is built on вы- and the root of тереть 'to rub'; EtymDB has no entry.
