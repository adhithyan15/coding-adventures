# బయటపడు (bayaṭapaḍu) — to get out; to come to light

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Telugu for a stampede, then the Telugu for narrowly.

## You'll want to know: బయటపడు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C343-TRUTILO343-05]; assesses=[] -->

**బయటపడు** — *bayaṭapaḍu* — "to get out; to come to light".

> మంటల నుంచి అందరూ క్షేమంగా బయటపడ్డారు.
>
> *maṇṭala nuñci andarū kṣēmaṅgā bayaṭapaḍḍāru.*
>
> Everyone got out of the fire safely.

Past (I …): బయటపడ్డాను, *bayaṭapaḍḍānu*.

బయట, outside, with పడు: to come out, or for a secret to come out.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**బయటపడు** (*bayaṭapaḍu*) is a native compound: *bayaṭa*, "outside", which sits beside *bayalu*, "the outside, open space" (DEDR 3940), with the helping verb *paḍu*.
