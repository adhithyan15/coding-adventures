## You'll want to know: la muerte
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C555-VEJEZ-03]; assesses=[] -->

**la muerte** — "death".

> La muerte de su madre le cambió la vida.
>
> *His mother's death changed his life.*

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*muerte* is inherited from Latin *mortem*, the object form of *mors* 'death' (EtymDB). The stressed *o* became *ue*, as in *muerto*.
