Lesson 1 of 5.
പ്രദേശം (pradēśaṁ) — a region, an area.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for a valley, then the Malayalam for a desert.

You'll want to know: പ്രദേശം.
പ്രദേശം — pradēśaṁ — "a region, an area".

The word, taken apart.
പ്രദേശം (pradēśaṁ) from Sanskrit pradēśa, "region, country", taken over whole as a tatsama, with Malayalam's noun ending -ṁ (CDIAL 8668).

Lesson 2 of 5.

[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: പറമ്പ്.
പറമ്പ് — paṟampŭ — "the land around a house, a plot".

The word, taken apart.
പറമ്പ് (paṟampŭ) is native Dravidian, one word with Tamil paṟampu and Kannada padavu (DEDR 4026). DEDR's sense is higher, dry ground, as against the wet rice fields.

The word, taken apart.
ചരിവ് (carivŭ) is native Dravidian, the noun of the verb "to slide, lean"; DEDR 2360 lists the root with Tamil cari, Kannada sari and Telugu jaragu.
