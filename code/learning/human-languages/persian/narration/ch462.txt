Persian, chapter 462: Sales.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
حراج (harâj) — a sale (with reduced prices); an auction.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for small change, then the Persian for a bureau de change.

You'll want to know: حراج.
حراج (harâj) — "a sale (with reduced prices); an auction".
در حراج زمستانی یک کاپشن ارزان خریدم. Dar harâj-e zemestâni yek kâpshan-e arzân kharidam. I bought a cheap jacket in the winter sale.
Harâj! written in a shop window means a sale is on.

The word, taken apart.
harâj was borrowed from Arabic and is usually traced to Arabic ḥarāj 'an auction'. EtymDB does not list it.

Lesson 2 of 5.
ارزیدن (arzidan) — to be worth.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a bureau de change, then the Persian for a sale.

You'll want to know: ارزیدن.
ارزیدن (arzidan) — "to be worth".
این کفش به این قیمت نمیارزد. In kafsh be in qeymat nemiarzad. These shoes aren't worth this price.
Present stem arz-: miarzad. Built on the same root as arzân 'cheap'.

The word, taken apart.
arzidan is inherited: EtymDB traces it through Proto-Iranian Hárǰati to the PIE root h₂elgʷʰ- 'to be worth, value'.

Lesson 3 of 5.
خرید کردن (kharid kardan) — to do the shopping, to go shopping.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a sale, then the Persian for to be worth.

You'll want to know: خرید کردن.
خرید کردن (kharid kardan) — "to do the shopping, to go shopping".
شنبه ها مادرم در بازار خرید میکند. Shanbe-hâ mâdaram dar bâzâr kharid mikonad. On Saturdays my mother does the shopping in the bazaar.
kharid 'purchase' + kardan. Kharidan is to buy a thing; kharid kardan is shopping in general.

The word, taken apart.
kharid kardan is built from kharid 'purchase', the past stem of kharidan 'to buy', usually traced to Middle Persian xrīdan, and kardan 'to do'.

Lesson 4 of 5.
فروش رفتن (forush raftan) — to sell (well), to be sold.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to be worth, then the Persian for to do the shopping.

You'll want to know: فروش رفتن.
فروش رفتن (forush raftan) — "to sell (well), to be sold".
همه بلیتها در یک روز فروش رفت. Hame-ye bilit-hâ dar yek ruz forush raft. All the tickets sold in one day.
forush 'sale' + raftan 'to go'. Khub forush miravad — it sells well.

The word, taken apart.
forush raftan is built from forush 'sale', the present stem of forukhtan 'to sell', usually traced to Middle Persian frōxtan, and the native verb raftan 'to go'.

Lesson 5 of 5.
جعبه (ja'be) — a box, a case.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to do the shopping, then the Persian for to sell.

You'll want to know: جعبه.
جعبه (ja'be) — "a box, a case".
فروشنده کفشها را در جعبه گذاشت. Forushande kafsh-hâ râ dar ja'be gozâsht. The shopkeeper put the shoes in a box.
Ja'be-ye shirini — a box of pastries, a common gift.

The word, taken apart.
ja'be was borrowed from Arabic jaʿba (EtymDB), which in Arabic is a quiver for arrows; in Persian it came to mean any box.
