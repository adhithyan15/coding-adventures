## You'll want to know: увольняться
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C307-RAZVOD-04]; assesses=[] -->

**увольняться** (*uvolnyátsya*) — "to quit (a job), to resign".

> Она уволилась и открыла своё кафе.
>
> *Oná uvólilas i otkrýla svoyó kafé.*
>
> She quit her job and opened her own café.

Imperfective; perfective *уволиться* (*uvólitsya*). It takes *с* + genitive: *уволиться с работы*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*увольняться* is built on *у-* and *воля* "will, freedom", inherited from Proto-Slavic *voľa*, from PIE *welh₁-* "to choose, to wish" (EtymDB): to set oneself free.
