# 〜に なりたいです (ni naritai desu) — what I want to become

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[JA-LEX-C251-NARU-01, JA-LEX-C258-SHOURAI-01, JA-LEX-C131-ISHA] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "my elder brother became a teacher". (*ani wa sensei ni narimashita*.) Then "a doctor". (*isha*.)

## You'll want to know: 〜に なりたいです
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-GRAMMAR-C258-AMBITION-01]; assesses=[JA-LEX-C25-FAMILY25-05, JA-LEX-C240-LIKES240-01] -->

> **いしゃに なりたいです。**
>
> *isha ni naritai desu.* — "I want to become a doctor."

**なる** takes **-たい**, as **いく** does in **いきたい**: **なりたい** (*naritai*), "want to become". **です** makes it polite.

> **しょうらい せんせいに なりたいです。**
>
> *shōrai sensei ni naritai desu.* — "In the future I want to become a teacher."

A na-adjective takes **に** too:

> **日本語が じょうずに なりたいです。**
>
> *nihongo ga jōzu ni naritai desu.* — "I want to get good at Japanese."

## You'll want to know: ゆめは 〜ことです
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-GRAMMAR-C258-AMBITION-02]; assesses=[JA-LEX-C63-WORLD63-01, JA-LEX-C29-VERBS29-03, JA-LEX-NIHONGO] -->

**こと** (*koto*) is "a thing, a matter". After the dictionary form of a verb it turns the action into a thing, and **ゆめは** (*yume wa*), "my dream is", can name it:

> **ゆめは 日本に すむ ことです。**
>
> *yume wa nihon ni sumu koto desu.* — "My dream is to live in Japan."

> **ゆめは うみの ちかくに すむ ことです。**
>
> *yume wa umi no chikaku ni sumu koto desu.* — "My dream is to live near the sea."
