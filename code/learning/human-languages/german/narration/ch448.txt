German, chapter 448: Briefe und Päckchen.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
frankieren — to put postage on, to stamp (a letter).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for the watchmaker, then the German for the jeweller.

You'll want to know: frankieren.
frankieren — "to put postage on, to stamp (a letter)".
Sie frankiert den Brief und bringt ihn zur Post. She puts a stamp on the letter and takes it to the post office.
Past participle without ge-: ein frankierter Umschlag — a stamped envelope.

The word, taken apart.
frankieren was borrowed from Italian francare 'to free from charges', built on franco 'free': a stamped letter travels free. EtymDB does not list the German verb, so the line is hedged.

Lesson 2 of 5.
verschicken — to send (out).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for the jeweller, then the German for to put postage on.

You'll want to know: verschicken.
verschicken — "to send (out)".
Zu Weihnachten verschickt sie viele Karten. At Christmas she sends lots of cards.
Verschicken often means sending many things, or sending something away.

The word, taken apart.
verschicken is built from ver- and schicken 'to send', inherited from Middle High German schicken 'to arrange, to put in order' (EtymDB).

Lesson 3 of 5.
einwerfen — to post (a letter); to throw in.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to put postage on, then the German for to send.

You'll want to know: einwerfen.
einwerfen — "to post (a letter); to throw in".
Auf dem Weg zur Arbeit wirft er den Brief ein. On the way to work he posts the letter.
Separable, strong: wirft ein, warf ein, hat eingeworfen. Der Briefkasten — the postbox.

The word, taken apart.
einwerfen is built from ein 'in' and werfen 'to throw', inherited from Old High German werfan and Proto-Germanic werpaną (EtymDB).

Lesson 4 of 5.
das Päckchen — the small parcel, the packet.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to send, then the German for to post.

You'll want to know: das Päckchen.
das Päckchen — "the small parcel, the packet".
Das Päckchen für meine Tante ist nicht schwer. The small parcel for my aunt is not heavy.
Same form in the plural. A small Paket, cheaper to send.

The word, taken apart.
Päckchen is built from Pack 'pack, bundle', usually traced to Low German or Dutch pak, and the ending -chen 'little'.

Lesson 5 of 5.
das Einschreiben — the registered letter, registered post.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to post, then the German for the small parcel.

You'll want to know: das Einschreiben.
das Einschreiben — "the registered letter, registered post".
Den Vertrag schickt er per Einschreiben. He sends the contract by registered post.
Same form in the plural. The verb sich einschreiben means to enrol.

The word, taken apart.
Einschreiben is the verb einschreiben 'to write in, to register' used as a noun; schreiben is an early borrowing from Latin scrībere 'to write' (Lewis & Short).
