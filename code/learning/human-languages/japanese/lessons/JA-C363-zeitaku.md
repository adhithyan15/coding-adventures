## You'll want to know: ぜいたく
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C363-KAIKEI363-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ぜいたく** — *zeitaku* — "luxury; extravagant".

> **おかねが あったら、ぜいたくを したいです。** — *okane ga attara, zeitaku o shitai desu* — "If I had the money, I would like to live it up."

A na-adjective: な before a noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ぜいたく** (*zeitaku*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *zei*, "luxury" (Middle Chinese *tsywejH*) and *taku*, "swamp" (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
