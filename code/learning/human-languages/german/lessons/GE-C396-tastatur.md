## You'll want to know: die Tastatur
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C396-COMPUTER-01]; assesses=[] -->

**die Tastatur** — "the keyboard".

> Auf der deutschen Tastatur sind Y und Z vertauscht.
>
> *On the German keyboard Y and Z have swapped places.*

Plural: *die Tastaturen*. *Die Taste* — the key.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Tastatur* is built on *Taste* "key", borrowed from Italian *tasto* "key", from *tastare* "to touch, to feel", which goes back to Latin *taxitāre* (EtymDB), with the Latin-style ending *-atur*.
