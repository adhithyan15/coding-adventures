## You'll want to know: biérén
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C138-EVERY138-03]; assesses=[] -->

*biérén* — "other people".

> *wǒ de shū wǒ bù xiǎng gěi biérén* — I don't want to give my books to anyone else.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*biérén* is built from *bié* 'different', as in *biéde*, and 人 'person', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *bjet* and *nyin* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription).
