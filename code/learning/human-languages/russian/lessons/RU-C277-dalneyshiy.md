## You'll want to know: дальнейший
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C277-OCHEREDNOY-05]; assesses=[] -->

**дальнейший** (*dalnéyshiy*) — "further, subsequent".

> О дальнейших планах он не говорил.
>
> *O dalnéyshikh plánakh on ne govoríl.*
>
> He did not talk about further plans.

*В дальнейшем* — in future, later on.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*дальнейший* is built on *дальний* "distant" with the superlative suffix *-ейш-*; *дальний* comes from *даль* "distance", which is inherited from Proto-Slavic *dalĭ* (EtymDB).
