## You'll want to know: किळस
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C462-HEVA462-03]; assesses=[] -->

**किळस** — *kiḷas* — "disgust".

> घाणेरडं पाणी पाहून मला किळस आली.
>
> *ghāṇerḍaṁ pāṇī pāhūn malā kiḷas ālī.*
>
> Seeing the filthy water, I felt disgust.

Feminine.

किळस येणे, to feel disgust, with the dative; किळसवाणा, disgusting.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**किळस** (*kiḷas*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Marathi verb *kiḷasṇẽ*, "to loathe", under Sanskrit *kliśyate*, "is troubled" (CDIAL 3623).
