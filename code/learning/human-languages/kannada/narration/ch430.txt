Kannada, chapter 430: Situations and Requirements.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಪರಿಸ್ಥಿತಿ (paristhiti) — a state of affairs; a situation.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to suggest, then the Kannada for a solution.

You'll want to know: ಪರಿಸ್ಥಿತಿ.
ಪರಿಸ್ಥಿತಿ — paristhiti — "a state of affairs; a situation".
ಪ್ರವಾಹದ ನಂತರ ಊರಿನ ಪರಿಸ್ಥಿತಿ ಕೆಟ್ಟದಾಗಿತ್ತು. pravāhada nantara ūrina paristhiti keṭṭadāgittu. After the flood the situation in town was bad.
ಪರಿ- (around) + ಸ್ಥಿತಿ (state).

The word, taken apart.
ಪರಿಸ್ಥಿತಿ (paristhiti) is built from Sanskrit pari-, "around", and sthiti, "standing, state" (Monier-Williams): the state of things around.

What you've built.

ಇಂಥ ಸಂದರ್ಭದಲ್ಲಿ ಏನು ಮಾಡಬೇಕು ಅಂತ ನನಗೆ ಗೊತ್ತಿಲ್ಲ. intha sandarbhadalli ēnu māḍabēku anta nanage gottilla. I do not know what to do in such a situation.

ಸಂದರ್ಭ (sandarbha) is borrowed from Sanskrit saṃdarbha, "stringing together, a composition" (Monier-Williams).

ಉದ್ಭವಿಸು — udbhavisu — "to arise; to spring up".
ಹೊಸ ಮನೆಯಲ್ಲಿ ನೀರಿನ ಸಮಸ್ಯೆ ಉದ್ಭವಿಸಿತು. hosa maneyalli nīrina samasye udbhavisitu. A water problem arose in the new house.
Past (it …): ಉದ್ಭವಿಸಿತು, udbhavisitu.
Of problems and questions.

The word, taken apart.
ಉದ್ಭವಿಸು (udbhavisu) is built on Sanskrit udbhava, "existence, origin, production" (CDIAL 2039), with the Kannada verb ending -isu.
