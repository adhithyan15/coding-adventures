## You'll want to know: నష్టపోవు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C393-PRAYOJANAM393-03]; assesses=[] -->

**నష్టపోవు** — *naṣṭapōvu* — "to suffer a loss".

> వర్షాల వల్ల రైతులు చాలా నష్టపోయారు.
>
> *varṣāla valla raitulu cālā naṣṭapōyāru.*
>
> The farmers lost a great deal because of the rains.

Past (I …): నష్టపోయాను, *naṣṭapōyānu*.

నష్టం, a loss, which you know, + పోవు, to go.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**నష్టపోవు** (*naṣṭapōvu*) is built from నష్టం, from Sanskrit *naṣṭa*, "lost, destroyed" (CDIAL 7027), and the native verb *pōvu*, "to go, to be lost" (DEDR 4572).

## What you've built
