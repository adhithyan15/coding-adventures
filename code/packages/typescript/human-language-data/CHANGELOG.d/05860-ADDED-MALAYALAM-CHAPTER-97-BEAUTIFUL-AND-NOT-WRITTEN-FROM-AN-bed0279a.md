### Added — Malayalam chapter 97, beautiful and not, written from an enumeration rather than a guess

- `ML-A1-LEX-52` closes. Malayalam A1 coverage 203/243 -> **204/243 (84%)**, 39
  points unmapped.
- **The method changed, and it changed the chapter.** Chapter 96's explanatory
  paragraph was wrong four times across five review rounds because the tidy
  version got written first and checked afterwards. Here the corpus was swept
  **before** drafting.
- The sweep found **three** attested welds with **ആണ്**, not one: **-ം → മാണ്**
  (സുഖമാണ്, മോശമാണ്, എളുപ്പമാണ്), **-ി → യാണ്** (കുട്ടിയാണ്), and **-അത് →
  താണ്** (നല്ലതാണ്, from `ML-C89-nallathu`).
- So the lesson claims only what the sweep supports: **a letter arrives to carry
  ആണ്'s vowel, and which one depends on how the word ended.** It says nothing
  about the third case, which no lesson here demonstrates. Under the old habit
  this would have said "the ം becomes മ" again, and കുട്ടിയാണ് would have turned
  up in review three rounds later.
- `ML-C97-sundaram` is deliberately cheap: **സുന്ദരം** drops into the frame
  chapter 96 built, and the lesson says so — *"the chapter before this one cost
  you a rule; this one costs you a word."*
- `ML-C97-bhangi` earns its place by ending differently. **ഭംഗി → ഭംഗിയാണ്**
  against **സുന്ദരം → സുന്ദരമാണ്**, with **കുട്ടിയാണ്** as the precedent the
  learner already owns.
- **The sweep also decided the chapter's shape.** `ML-C77-alla` teaches **അല്ല**
  *standing apart* — ഞാൻ അധ്യാപകൻ അല്ല — never welded. So the far end of the
  axis is **ഇത് സുന്ദരം അല്ല**, and `ML-C97-alla-sundaram` makes the
  **not**-welding its point: സുന്ദരം keeps its **ം** precisely because nothing
  attached to it. A real contrast with ആണ്, drawn from what the corpus shows.
- **No dedicated word for "ugly" is taught, deliberately.** വൃത്തികെട്ട would
  need an attributive form and a register judgement that could not be defended
  under review — the same call made about ബുദ്ധിമുട്ട് in chapter 96. One new
  quality gives both ends of the line, which is the honest A1 shape.
- All four candidates grepped as tokens first (zero occurrences each); glyph
  pre-check run with its control validated before the clean result was trusted.
