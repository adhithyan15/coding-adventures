## You'll want to know: orientarsi
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C439-ORIENTARSI-01]; assesses=[] -->

**orientarsi** — "to find one's way, to get one's bearings".

> In questa città è facile orientarsi.
>
> *In this city it is easy to find your way.*

Built on *oriente*, the east: once you knew where the sun rose, you knew the rest.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*orientarsi* is built on *oriente* 'east', which is inherited from Latin *oriēns* 'rising (sun)' (EtymDB), from *orīrī* 'to rise'.
