# Three clauses underneath, three jobs

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MR-JOIN-KAARAN, MR-JOIN-KII, MR-JOIN-SAATHI, MR-JOIN-EK-DUSRA, MR-LEX-DUSRA] -->

[PAUSE 5s] Three things can now go underneath a Marathi main clause: a reason, a
content, and an aim. Name the word or ending that carries each.

Then reach further back and say the frame that shares a pair out — **एक** in
front of one, **दुसरा** in front of the other — and notice that it puts its two
halves side by side as equals, which is exactly what these three do not do.
