## You'll want to know: بھوننا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C508-DHAKKAN508-02]; assesses=[] -->

**بھوننا** — *bhūnnā* — "to roast; to brown (in a pan)".

> اس نے کڑاہی میں مونگ پھلی بھونی۔
>
> *us ne kaṛāhī meṅ mūṅg phalī bhūnī.*
>
> She roasted peanuts in the wok.

Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے گوشت بھونا, *us ne gosht bhūnā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**بھوننا** (*bhūnnā*) is probably inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousins *bhujnā* and *bhūjnā*, "fries, parches", under Sanskrit *bhṛjjati*, "roasts" (CDIAL 9583); the form *bhūnnā*, with its *-n-*, is not among its Hindi rows, so the link is likely rather than certain.
