Arabic, chapter 463: The climate.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
مناخ (munākh) — climate.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to have room for, then the Arabic for to raise.

You'll want to know: مناخ.
مناخ — munākh — "climate".
مناخ الصحراء جاف جدا. munākh aṣ-ṣaḥrāʾ jāff jiddan. The climate of the desert is very dry.

The word, taken apart.
مناخ (munākh) is built on the native Arabic root n-w-kh, "to make a camel kneel" (Lane). A munākh was a halting place where camels knelt; modern Arabic gave the old word the sense of climate.

Lesson 2 of 5.
احتباس حراري (iḥtibās ḥarārī) — global warming.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to raise, then the Arabic for climate.

You'll want to know: احتباس حراري.
احتباس حراري — iḥtibās ḥarārī — "global warming".
قرأت عن الاحتباس الحراري في الجريدة. qaraʾtu ʿan al-iḥtibās al-ḥarārī fī al-jarīda. I read about global warming in the newspaper.

The word, taken apart.
احتباس حراري (iḥtibās ḥarārī) is a modern coinage built from two native Arabic words (Lane gives both roots): iḥtibās, "being held in", on ḥ-b-s, "to confine", and ḥarārī, "of heat", on ḥ-r-r: heat held in.

يحمي الغلاف الجوي الأرض. yaḥmī al-ghilāf al-jawwī al-arḍ. The atmosphere protects the earth.

غلاف جوي (ghilāf jawwī) is a modern coinage built from two native Arabic words (Lane gives both roots): ghilāf, "a cover, a sheath", on gh-l-f, and jawwī, "of the air", on j-w-w.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for global warming, then the Arabic for the atmosphere.

انبعاث — inbiʿāth — "an emission; a release".
كان الانبعاث من المصنع كبيرا. kāna al-inbiʿāth min al-maṣnaʿ kabīran. The emission from the factory was large.

The word, taken apart.
انبعاث (inbiʿāth) is built on the native Arabic root b-ʿ-th, "to send forth, to rouse" (Lane). It is the verbal noun of Form VII, "being sent out".
