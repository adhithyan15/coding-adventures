## You'll want to know: प्रवेश
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C74-SIGNS-03]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रवेश** (*pravesh*) — "entrance".

Two Sanskrit pieces. **प्र-** is "forward", and **विश्** (*viś-*) is "to enter".
Going forward into a place: प्रवेश.

That प्र- is worth keeping. It is the same ancient piece as Latin *pro-* and
English *fore-*, and once you can see it you will spot it at the front of a great
many formal Hindi words.

This is a **written** register, not a spoken one. Nobody says प्रवेश to a friend
at a doorway; it is painted over the door, printed on a form, and read out in a
station announcement. That is the same shelf **कृपया** sits on — the please of
signs rather than the please of counters.

The opening प्र stacks प on र with the vowel-killer, as क्र did in क्रिकेट.
