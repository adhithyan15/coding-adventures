## You'll want to know: ਟੱਕਰ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C318-MISHAP318-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ਟੱਕਰ** — *ṭakkar* — "a collision".

> ਪੁਲ ਉੱਤੇ ਦੋ ਗੱਡੀਆਂ ਦੀ ਟੱਕਰ ਹੋਈ।
>
> *pul utte do gaḍḍīāṁ dī ṭakkar hoī.*
>
> Two cars crashed into each other on the bridge.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਟੱਕਰ** (*ṭakkar*) is inherited from Sanskrit *ṭakkarā*, "a blow on the head"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *ṭakkar*, "pushing, knocking", under it (CDIAL 5424).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
