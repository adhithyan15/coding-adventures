## You'll want to know: dīlātiō, dīlātiōnis
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C395-DIFFERO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**dīlātiō, dīlātiōnis** — "a putting off, a delay".

> Rēs dīlātiōnem nōn patitur.
>
> *The matter allows no delay.*

*dīlātiō, dīlātiōnis* f., the noun of *differō*, from its supine *dīlātum*. English *dilatory*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*dīlātiō* is the noun made on *dīlātum*, the supine of *differō* "to put off" (Lewis & Short), whose *-lātum* comes from a different old verb than *ferō*.
