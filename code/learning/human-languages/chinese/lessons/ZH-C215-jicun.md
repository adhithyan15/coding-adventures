## You'll want to know: jìcún
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C215-DELIVER215-03]; assesses=[] -->

*jìcún* — "to leave in safekeeping".

> *xíngli kěyǐ jìcún ma* — Can I leave my luggage here?

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*jìcún* is built from *jì* 'to entrust' and *cún* 'to keep', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *kjeH* and *dzwon* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to entrust'.
