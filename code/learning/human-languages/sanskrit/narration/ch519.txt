Sanskrit, chapter 519: Craft and Carving.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
मूर्तिः (mūrtiḥ) — a statue.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for paints, then the Sanskrit for a portrait.

You'll want to know: मूर्तिः.
मूर्तिः — mūrtiḥ — "a statue".
मन्दिरे एका प्राचीना मूर्तिः अस्ति। mandire ekā prācīnā mūrtiḥ asti. There is an ancient statue in the temple.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
मूर्तिः (mūrtiḥ) comes from the root √mūrch, whose causative MW gives as "to make settle into a solid form"; MW sets mūrti, any solid body or material form, an embodiment, an image, among that root's words (MW).

Lesson 2 of 5.
उत्किरति (utkirati) — carves.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a portrait, then the Sanskrit for a statue.

You'll want to know: उत्किरति.
उत्किरति — utkirati — "carves".
शिल्पी शिलायां पुष्पम् उत्किरति। śilpī śilāyāṃ puṣpam utkirati. The sculptor carves a flower on the stone.
Past (he …): सः शिलायां नाम उत्कीर्णवान्, saḥ śilāyāṃ nāma utkīrṇavān.

The word, taken apart.
उत्किरति (utkirati) is built on ud, "up, out", and the root √kṝ, "to scatter"; MW gives utkṝ as to dig out, and to engrave; its past participle is utkīrṇa (MW).

Warm-up.

शिल्पं कठिनं परन्तु सुन्दरम् अस्ति। śilpaṃ kaṭhinaṃ parantu sundaram asti. A craft is hard but beautiful.
A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
शिल्पम् (śilpam) is the word MW gives for decoration, ornament, artistic work, and MW itself calls it of doubtful derivation, so its origin is uncertain (MW).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for carves, then the Sanskrit for a craft.

You'll want to know: कृतिः.
कृतिः — kṛtiḥ — "a creation; a work".
एषा मूर्तिः मम पितुः कृतिः अस्ति। eṣā mūrtiḥ mama pituḥ kṛtiḥ asti. This statue is my father's work.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
कृतिः (kṛtiḥ) comes from the root √kṛ, "to make, to do", with the ending -ti; MW gives kṛti as the act of doing, a creation, a work (MW).

Lesson 5 of 5.
निर्माति (nirmāti) — fashions; builds.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a craft, then the Sanskrit for a creation.

You'll want to know: निर्माति.
निर्माति — nirmāti — "fashions; builds".
शिल्पी शिलायाः गजं निर्माति। śilpī śilāyāḥ gajaṃ nirmāti. The craftsman fashions an elephant out of stone.
Past (he …): सः शिलायाः गजं निर्मितवान्, saḥ śilāyāḥ gajaṃ nirmitavān.

The word, taken apart.
निर्माति (nirmāti) is built on nis, "out", and the root √mā, "to measure, to form"; MW gives nirmā as to build, to make out of a material, to create; its past participle is nirmita (MW).
