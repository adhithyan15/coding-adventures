Thirty-one lessons, eight chapters, and the target was picked by measurement
rather than by topic: `core/exam-inventory-tamil-a1.json` reported
`Iṇaittoḍar (joining clauses)` at **0 of 7** while the verb column stood at
20 of 26 and the script column at 52 of 52 taught glyphs. Nineteen A1 points
close here, and Tamil A1 coverage moves **155/262 (59%) -> 174/262 (66%)**.

**Three concepts stop being omissions.** `SPINE-NEGATE-AND-ASK` had an empty
segment list and omitted `QUESTION-POLAR`; `SPINE-SAY-WHY` omitted
`CONNECTIVE-BECAUSE`; `SPINE-SAY-WHAT-I-DO` omitted `VERB-SAY`. All three are
realized here, and the omission ledgers were re-derived rather than hand-edited.

**Retrieval at three distances.** The two-back rollback from chapters 67-73
holds R1 and nothing else, so a plain append would have taken the reinforcement
finding from 1200 to 1300 — partly on the new atoms, and partly on old ones
whose R3 and R4 windows only became judgeable because the track grew past 358
lessons. Every lesson here ends with a second retrieval line naming one item
from a few lessons back, one from around chapter 70 and one from around chapter
62, and the task is always this chapter's own pattern. The finding came back to
**1200 exactly**.

**The numerals cost no content.** Eight chapter-7 lessons taught ௧–௰ and declared
nothing. Four atoms make them measurable, which closes two exam points and pushes
chapter 7 from 12 introduced atoms to 16 — one over its budget, filed as HL-C305
rather than fixed by a renumbering that would touch every chapter after it.

**Register.** Every joiner carries both forms, standard as the headword and the
spoken clip named beside it — `āṉāl`/`āṉā`, `ēṉeṉṟāl`/`ēṉṉā`, `eṉṟu`/`-ṉṉu` —
because the standard form predicts the clip and the clip does not predict it
back. Chapter 81 states the diglossia outright, which is what closes `TA-A1-REG-01`.
