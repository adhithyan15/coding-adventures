## You'll want to know: おしえる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C224-SCHOOL224-04]; assesses=[] -->

**おしえる** — *oshieru* — "to teach; to tell".

> **せんせいは にほんごを おしえます。** — *sensei wa nihongo o oshiemasu* — "The teacher teaches Japanese."

Polite **おしえます**, *oshiemasu*; an ichidan verb.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**おしえる** (*oshieru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
