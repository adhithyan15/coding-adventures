# Review — comparing and time

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[TA-GRAMMAR-C291-THAN-01, TA-GRAMMAR-C291-SPAN-01, TA-LEX-C285-IRAM-01, TA-SCRIPT-RECOG-141] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "The train is better than the bus." Then "for three months". Then read **ஈரம்** aloud. (*īram*.)

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[TA-GRAMMAR-C291-THAN-01, TA-GRAMMAR-C291-MOST-01, TA-GRAMMAR-C291-SPAN-01, TA-GRAMMAR-C291-FROM-UNTIL-01, TA-GRAMMAR-C290-FEELING-STATE-01, TA-GRAMMAR-C290-WISH-01, TA-LEX-C285-IRAM-01, TA-SCRIPT-RECOG-141] -->

[PAUSE 2s each]
- [YOU SAY: My town is smaller than Madurai.]
- [YOU SAY: I like tea most of all.]
- [YOU SAY: I have been learning Tamil for two years.]
- [YOU SAY: It rained from morning until evening. My shirt was wet.]
- [YOU SAY: two things you have done for a long time, and for how long]
