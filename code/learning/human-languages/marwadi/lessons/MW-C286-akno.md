## You'll want to know: आंकणो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C286-IMTIHAN286-05]; assesses=[] -->

**आंकणो** — *ā̃kṇo* — "to mark schoolwork; to assess".

> मास्टर हिसाब आंकै है।
>
> *māsṭar hisāb ā̃kai hai.*
>
> The teacher marks the sums.

Past (I …): म्हैं हिसाब आंक्यो, *mhaĩ hisāb ā̃kyo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**आंकणो** (*ā̃kṇo*) is inherited from Sanskrit *aṅkayati*, "marks, brands"; CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry, but lists Gujarati *ā̃kvũ* and Hindi *ā̃knā*, both "to mark, appraise" (CDIAL 104).
