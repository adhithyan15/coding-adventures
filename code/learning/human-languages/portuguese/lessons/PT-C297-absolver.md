## You'll want to know: absolver
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C297-TRIBUNAL-05]; assesses=[] -->

**absolver** — "to acquit; to absolve".

> O tribunal absolveu a mulher.
>
> *The court acquitted the woman.*

The opposite of *condenar*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*absolver* is a learned borrowing from Latin *absolvere* "to loosen, to set free", built on *solvere* "to loosen" (Lewis & Short).
