Russian, chapter 120: Lion, Monkey, Elephant, Bee, Ant.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
лев (lev) — a lion.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for lips, then the Russian for the chest.

You'll want to know: лев.
