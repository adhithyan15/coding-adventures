Marathi, chapter 345: Reaching Conclusions.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
निष्कर्ष (niṣkarṣa) — a conclusion.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for an inconsistency, then the Marathi for a standpoint.

You'll want to know: निष्कर्ष.
निष्कर्ष — niṣkarṣa — "a conclusion".
शेवटी आम्ही एका निष्कर्षावर आलो. śevṭī āmhī ekā niṣkarṣāvar ālo. In the end we came to a conclusion.
Masculine.
निष्कर्ष (niṣkarṣa) काढणे, to draw a conclusion.

The word, taken apart.
निष्कर्ष (niṣkarṣa) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit niṣkarṣa, "drawing out, extracting" (Monier-Williams). A conclusion is what is drawn out of the facts.

Lesson 2 of 5.
अनुमान काढणे (anumān kāḍhṇe) — to infer.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a standpoint, then the Marathi for a conclusion.

You'll want to know: अनुमान काढणे.
अनुमान काढणे — anumān kāḍhṇe — "to infer".
काळं आभाळ पाहून त्याने पावसाचं अनुमान काढलं. kāḷaṁ ābhāḷ pāhūn tyāne pāvsācaṁ anumān kāḍhlaṁ. Seeing the dark sky, he inferred that it would rain.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने अनुमान काढलं, tyāne anumān kāḍhlaṁ.
अनुमान, an inference; काढणे, to draw out.

The word, taken apart.
अनुमान काढणे (anumān kāḍhṇe) joins the tatsama अनुमान, Sanskrit anumāna, "inference, thought" (CDIAL 328), to काढणे, "to take out".

Lesson 3 of 5.
आधार (ādhār) — a basis; support.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a conclusion, then the Marathi for to infer.

You'll want to know: आधार.
आधार — ādhār — "a basis; support".
तुमच्या मताला काय आधार आहे? tumacyā matālā kāy ādhār āhe? What basis does your view have?
Masculine.
X ला आधार (ādhār) देणे, to support X; च्या आधारावर, on the basis of.

The word, taken apart.
आधार (ādhār) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit ādhāra, "support, base" (CDIAL 1165); the same entry lists an inherited Marathi āhārā, a ring of grass that holds a pot steady.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: सारांश.
सारांश — sārāṁś — "a summary".
मीराने धड्याचा सारांश सांगितला. mīrāne dhaḍyācā sārāṁś sāṅgitlā. Meera gave a summary of the lesson.
Masculine.
X चा सारांश (sārāṁś), a summary of X.

The word, taken apart.
सारांश (sārāṁś) is a learned compound built from Sanskrit sāra, "essence, best part", and aṁśa, "part" (Monier-Williams): the essential part.

Lesson 5 of 5.
थोडक्यात (thoḍkyāt) — in short; briefly.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a basis, then the Marathi for a summary.

You'll want to know: थोडक्यात.
थोडक्यात — thoḍkyāt — "in short; briefly".
थोडक्यात सांगायचं तर, आम्हाला उशीर झाला. thoḍkyāt sāṅgāycaṁ tar, āmhālā uśīr jhālā. To put it briefly, we were late.
थोडक्यात (thoḍkyāt) सांगायचं तर, to put it briefly; also narrowly: थोडक्यात (thoḍkyāt) वाचला, had a narrow escape.

The word, taken apart.
थोडक्यात (thoḍkyāt) is built from थोडका, a form of थोडा, "a little", with the ending -त, "in": in a little space. थोडा is inherited from Sanskrit stoka, "little" (CDIAL 13720).
