Lesson 1 of 5.
ಪ್ರವಾಸಿ (pravāsi) — a tourist.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a room, then the Kannada for a courtyard.

You'll want to know: ಪ್ರವಾಸಿ.
ಪ್ರವಾಸಿ — pravāsi — "a tourist".

The word, taken apart.
ಪ್ರವಾಸಿ (pravāsi) is Sanskrit pravāsin, "one who is away from home, a traveller", from pravāsa, "a stay abroad". Kannada drops the final -n.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ಅರಮನೆ (aramane) — a palace.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a cave, then the Kannada for a zoo.

You'll want to know: ಅರಮನೆ.
ಅರಮನೆ — aramane — "a palace".

The word, taken apart.
ಅರಮನೆ (aramane) is "the king's house". DEDR 201 lists ara-mane, "royal dwelling, palace", itself. Its first part is arasu, "king", which DEDR derives from Sanskrit rājan, "king", through an early southern form; the second is native mane, "house" (DEDR 4776).
