Malayalam, chapter 487: Local Government.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
നഗരസഭ (nagarasabha) — a municipality.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for office hours, then the Malayalam for a working day.

You'll want to know: നഗരസഭ.
നഗരസഭ — nagarasabha — "a municipality".
നഗരസഭ തെരുവുകളിൽ പുതിയ വിളക്കുകൾ വച്ചു. nagarasabha teruvukaḷil putiya viḷakkukaḷ vaccu. The municipality put up new lamps in the streets.
The council of a town; a village has a പഞ്ചായത്ത്.

The word, taken apart.
നഗരസഭ (nagarasabha) is a Malayalam compound built from നഗരം, a town, from Sanskrit nagara (CDIAL 6924), and സഭ, an assembly, from Sanskrit sabhā (Monier-Williams).

വില്ലേജ് ഓഫീസ് — villējŭ ōphīsŭ — "a village office".
സർട്ടിഫിക്കറ്റിനായി ഞാൻ വില്ലേജ് ഓഫീസിൽ പോയി. sarṭṭiphikkaṟṟināyi ñān villējŭ ōphīsil pōyi. I went to the village office for a certificate.

Lesson 3 of 5.
താലൂക്ക് (tālūkkŭ) — a taluk.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for a municipality, then the Malayalam for a village office.

You'll want to know: താലൂക്ക്.
താലൂക്ക് — tālūkkŭ — "a taluk".
ഞങ്ങളുടെ ജില്ലയിൽ ആറ് താലൂക്കുകൾ ഉണ്ട്. ñaṅṅaḷuṭe jillayil āṟŭ tālūkkukaḷ uṇṭŭ. Our district has six taluks.
Plural: താലൂക്കുകൾ, tālūkkukaḷ.
A district is split into taluks, and a taluk into villages.

The word, taken apart.
താലൂക്ക് (tālūkkŭ) is usually traced to Arabic taʿalluq, "a dependency", which reached the south through Hindustani as a word for a revenue division.

കളക്ടർ — kaḷakṭar — "a district collector".
വെള്ളപ്പൊക്കം കാരണം കളക്ടർ പള്ളിക്കൂടങ്ങൾക്ക് അവധി നൽകി. veḷḷappokkaṁ kāraṇaṁ kaḷakṭar paḷḷikkūṭaṅṅaḷkkŭ avadhi nalki. Because of the flood, the collector gave the schools a holiday.
The officer who runs a district; the title goes back to collecting the land revenue.

The word, taken apart.
കളക്ടർ (kaḷakṭar) was borrowed from English collector, the officer in charge of a district.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ശല്യം (śalyaṁ) — a nuisance; a bother.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for a taluk, then the Malayalam for a district collector.

You'll want to know: ശല്യം.
ശല്യം — śalyaṁ — "a nuisance; a bother".
രാത്രി നായകളുടെ ശല്യം കാരണം ഞങ്ങൾക്ക് ഉറങ്ങാൻ കഴിഞ്ഞില്ല. rātri nāyakaḷuṭe śalyaṁ kāraṇaṁ ñaṅṅaḷkkŭ uṟaṅṅān kaḻiññilla. Because of the dogs' nuisance at night, we could not sleep.
What bothers you comes first: കൊതുകുശല്യം, the trouble from mosquitoes.

The word, taken apart.
ശല്യം (śalyaṁ) is borrowed from Sanskrit śalya, "a dart, a thorn" (Monier-Williams): something that sticks in and will not leave you alone.
