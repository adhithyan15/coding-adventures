## You'll want to know: imprevedibile
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C374-IMPREVISTO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**imprevedibile** — "unpredictable".

> Il tempo in montagna è imprevedibile.
>
> *The weather in the mountains is unpredictable.*

Of a person too: *un carattere imprevedibile*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*imprevedibile* is built from *in-* "not" and *prevedibile*, on *prevedere*, from Latin *praevidēre* "to foresee" (Lewis & Short), on *vidēre* "to see".
