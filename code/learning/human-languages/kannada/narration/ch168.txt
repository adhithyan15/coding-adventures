Kannada, chapter 168: Forehead, Throat, Heel, Thigh, Palm.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಹಣೆ (haṇe) — the forehead.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to burst, then the Kannada for to snore.

You'll want to know: ಹಣೆ.
ಹಣೆ — haṇe — "the forehead".

The word, taken apart.
ಹಣೆ (haṇe) is native Dravidian (DEDR 3896). DEDR lists Kannada paṇe, haṇe, "forehead": the p- has become h-.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ಅಂಗೈ (aṅgai) — the palm (of the hand).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for the heel, then the Kannada for the thigh.

You'll want to know: ಅಂಗೈ.
ಅಂಗೈ — aṅgai — "the palm (of the hand)".

The word, taken apart.
ಅಂಗೈ (aṅgai) is native Dravidian, "the inside of the hand": DEDR 7 lists Kannada aṅ-gey, "palm of the hand", with Tamil aṅkai, under the root for "inside", and the second part is kai, "hand" (DEDR 2023).
