# Hear *hǎokàn* — good-looking

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ZH-LEX-HAO, ZH-LEX-KAN-01, ZH-LEX-KANJIAN-01] -->

[PAUSE 12s] Say **good**, **look**, and **see** as three separate Mandarin
answers.

## You'll want to know
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-HAOKAN-01]; assesses=[] -->

> *hǎokàn* — **good-looking; nice to look at**

Let *hǎo* dip, then let *kàn* fall. This is a description, not an instruction
to look. The characters wait until the next tiny step.
