## You'll want to know: transgredir
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C383-EXCECAO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**transgredir** — "to transgress, to break (a rule)".

> Quem transgride as regras paga uma multa.
>
> *Whoever breaks the rules pays a fine.*

Conjugated like *agredir*: *transgrido, transgrides, transgride*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*transgredir* is a learned borrowing from Latin *trānsgredī* "to step across, to pass over", built from *trāns* "across" and *gradī* "to step" (Lewis & Short).
