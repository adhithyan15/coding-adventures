## You'll want to know: اکثر
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C362-QIYAS362-03]; assesses=[] -->

**اکثر** — *aksar* — "often; usually".

> اگر وہ اکثر دیر سے آتا ہے تو اسے بتائیے۔
>
> *agar voh aksar der se ātā hai to use batāiye.*
>
> If he is often late, tell him so.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**اکثر** (*aksar*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *akthar*, "more, most": most of the time.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
