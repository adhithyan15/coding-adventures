French, chapter 395: Mon compte en ligne.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
l'utilisateur — the user.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for to pay back, then the French for the loan.

You'll want to know: l'utilisateur.
l'utilisateur — "the user".
Chaque utilisateur a son propre profil. Each user has their own profile.
Feminine utilisatrice.

The word, taken apart.
utilisateur is built on utiliser "to use", from utile "useful", which comes from Latin ūtilis "useful", from ūtī "to use" (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 2 of 5.
le mot de passe — the password.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for the loan, then the French for the user.

You'll want to know: le mot de passe.
le mot de passe — "the password".
J'ai encore oublié mon mot de passe. I forgot my password again.
The plural is les mots de passe.

The word, taken apart.
mot de passe is a word that lets you pass. mot comes from Late Latin muttum "a mutter, a grunt" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short), and passer from Vulgar Latin passāre, built on passus "a step" (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 3 of 5.
l'identifiant — the username, the login.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for the user, then the French for the password.

You'll want to know: l'identifiant.
l'identifiant — "the username, the login".
Chaque employé a un identifiant et un mot de passe. Each employee has a username and a password.
Masculine. From identifier, to identify.

The word, taken apart.
identifiant is the present participle of identifier used as a noun. The verb comes from Medieval Latin identificāre, built on Latin īdem "the same" (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 4 of 5.
connecter — to connect; to log in (reflexive, se connecter).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for the password, then the French for the username.

You'll want to know: connecter.
connecter — "to connect; to log in (reflexive, se connecter)".
Je me connecte à mon compte tous les matins. I log in to my account every morning.
Se connecter — to log in; se déconnecter — to log out.

The word, taken apart.
connecter is built on Latin cōnectere "to bind together" (Lewis & Short), from nectere "to tie", partly through English connect.

Lesson 5 of 5.
accéder — to access, to reach.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for the username, then the French for to connect.

You'll want to know: accéder.
accéder — "to access, to reach".
Sans mot de passe, on ne peut pas accéder au site. Without a password, you cannot get into the website.
Accéder à, with à. J'accède.

The word, taken apart.
accéder is a learned borrowing from Latin accēdere "to go toward, to approach" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short), built on cēdere "to go".
