## You'll want to know: رخ دادن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C376-TAQSIR-04]; assesses=[] -->

**رخ دادن** (*rokh dâdan*) — "to happen, to occur".

> این حادثه دیشب رخ داد.
>
> *in hâdese dishab rokh dâd.*
>
> This accident happened last night.

Formal; in speech *ettefâq oftâdan*. Stem *rokh deh-*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**رخ دادن** "to occur" is in EtymDB; it is native Persian, built from *rokh* "face, appearance" and *dâdan* "to give", which EtymDB traces to PIE *deh₃-*: an event shows its face.
