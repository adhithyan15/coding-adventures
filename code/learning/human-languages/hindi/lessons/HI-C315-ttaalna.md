## You'll want to know: टालना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C315-DAVA315-05]; assesses=[] -->

**टालना** — *ṭālnā* — "to put off; to dodge".

> मंत्री ने पत्रकारों का सवाल टाल दिया।
>
> *mantrī ne patrakāroṁ kā savāl ṭāl diyā.*
>
> The minister dodged the journalists' question.

Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने बात टाल दी, *usne bāt ṭāl dī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**टालना** (*ṭālnā*) is the transitive partner of **टलना**, from the same Sanskrit *ṭalati* (CDIAL 5450): to make something move away from you, a question, a visit or a danger.
