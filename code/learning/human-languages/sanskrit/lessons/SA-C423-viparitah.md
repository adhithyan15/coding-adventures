## You'll want to know: विपरीतः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C423-PRAVESHA423-03]; assesses=[] -->

**विपरीतः** — *viparītaḥ* — "opposite; reversed".

> मम गृहं विपरीतायां दिशायाम् अस्ति।
>
> *mama gṛhaṃ viparītāyāṃ diśāyām asti.*
>
> My house is in the opposite direction.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**विपरीतः** (*viparītaḥ*) is built from *vi*, *pari* and the root √i, "to go": gone round the other way; MW gives *viparīta* as turned round, reversed, opposite (MW).
