## You'll want to know: le sculpteur
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C517-SCULPTURE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**le sculpteur** — "sculptor".

> Le sculpteur travaille dans un vieil atelier.
>
> *The sculptor works in an old workshop.*

A woman is *la sculptrice* or *la sculpteure*. The *p* is silent.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*le sculpteur* is a learned borrowing from Latin *sculptor* "a carver" (EtymDB), from *sculpere* "to carve" (Lewis & Short).
