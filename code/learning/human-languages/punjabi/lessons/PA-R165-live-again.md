# An old word for living

Hear one familiar meaning, pause, and retrieve its Punjabi word before the
model. This is a gentle return, not a new word or a scored mock.

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-LEX-C151-ACT151-02] -->

Hear **to live**. [PAUSE 4s] Recall **ਜੀਉਣਾ** (*jīuṇā*).

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-LEX-C151-ACT151-02] -->

Hear **ਜੀਉਣਾ**. [PAUSE 4s] Recall **to live**.
Then hear **to live** again. [PAUSE 4s] Say **ਜੀਉਣਾ** before the model.
Only this old word is needed.

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-LEX-C151-ACT151-02] -->

One last cue: **to live**. [PAUSE 4s] Recall **ਜੀਉਣਾ**.
Listen and retry once if it does not come back.
