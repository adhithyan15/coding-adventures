## You'll want to know: ahnen
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C338-VERMUTUNG-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ahnen** — "to suspect, to have a feeling".

> Ich habe es schon geahnt: Der Zug hat Verspätung.
>
> *I already had a feeling: the train is late.*

*Keine Ahnung* — no idea — belongs to the same family.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ahnen* is inherited from Middle High German *anen* (EtymDB). It is usually explained as a verb made from *an* "on": a feeling comes upon you.
