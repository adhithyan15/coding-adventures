## You'll want to know: гостеприимство
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C499-VISITS-03]; assesses=[] -->

**гостеприимство** (*gostepriímstvo*) — "hospitality".

> Спасибо за ваше гостеприимство!
>
> *Spasíbo za váshe gostepriímstvo!*
>
> Thank you for your hospitality!

Neuter.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*гостеприимство* is built on *гостеприимный* 'hospitable', a compound of *гость* 'guest' and *приимать* 'to receive'; EtymDB has no entry.
