6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
સફર (saphar) — a voyage; a long journey.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for on foot, then the Gujarati for to pine for home.

You'll want to know: સફર.
સફર — saphar — "a voyage; a long journey".
દરિયાની સફર મને ઘણી ગમી. dariyānī saphar mane ghaṇī gamī. I really enjoyed the sea voyage.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
સફર (saphar) is usually traced to Arabic safar, "a journey", which came through Persian; મુસાફર, a traveller, is built on the same word.

Lesson 2 of 6.
ફેરો (phero) — a round trip; a round.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to pine for home, then the Gujarati for a voyage.

You'll want to know: ફેરો.
ફેરો — phero — "a round trip; a round".
આ બસ દિવસમાં ચાર ફેરા કરે છે. ā bas divasmā̃ cār pherā kare che. This bus does four round trips a day.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ફેરો (phero) goes back to phēra, "a turn, a turning", a form CDIAL reconstructs beside phirati, "moves, turns"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati word (CDIAL 9078). ફરવું, to go around, is its relative.

Lesson 3 of 6.
આંટો (ā̃ṭo) — a turn; a short walk.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a voyage, then the Gujarati for a round trip.

You'll want to know: આંટો.
આંટો — ā̃ṭo — "a turn; a short walk".
સાંજે અમે બગીચામાં આંટો માર્યો. sā̃je ame bagīcāmā̃ ā̃ṭo māryo. In the evening we took a turn in the garden.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
આંટો (ā̃ṭo) is a tadbhava of Sanskrit āvarta, "a whirlpool, a turning"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati word, "a turn, a twist" (CDIAL 1418). આંટો મારવો is to go for a short walk.

Lesson 5 of 6.
ટહેલવું (ṭahelvũ) — to walk up and down; to stroll.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a short walk, then the Gujarati for a rest on the way.

You'll want to know: ટહેલવું.
ટહેલવું — ṭahelvũ — "to walk up and down; to stroll".
જમ્યા પછી દાદા આંગણામાં ટહેલે છે. jamyā pachī dādā ā̃gṇāmā̃ ṭahele che. After eating, grandfather walks up and down the courtyard.
Past (he …): તે આંગણામાં ટહેલ્યો, te ā̃gṇāmā̃ ṭahelyo.

The word, taken apart.
ટહેલવું (ṭahelvũ) goes back to ṭahall, "to walk up and down", a form CDIAL reconstructs; CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb, in the sense of going round from house to house, and Hindi ṭahalnā, "to take a walk" (CDIAL 5453).
