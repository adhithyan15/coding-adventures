Lesson 1 of 5.
मालिश (māliś) — a massage; a rub.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for to have someone sent for, then the Hindi for a life.

You'll want to know: मालिश.
मालिश — māliś — "a massage; a rub".
रोज़ की मालिश से दादी के घुटनों का दर्द कम हो गया। roz kī māliś se dādī ke ghuṭnoṁ kā dard kam ho gayā. With a daily massage, the pain in my grandmother's knees eased.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
मालिश (māliś) is usually traced to Persian mālish, "rubbing", the noun of the Persian verb for "to rub". māliś karnā is to massage; malnā, "to rub in", is a separate, inherited word.

Lesson 2 of 5.
स्वस्थ (svasth) — healthy; well.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a life, then the Hindi for a massage.

You'll want to know: स्वस्थ.
स्वस्थ — svasth — "healthy; well".
रोज़ दौड़कर अरुण स्वस्थ रहता है। roz dauṛkar Aruṇ svasth rahtā hai. Arun keeps healthy by running every day.

The word, taken apart.
स्वस्थ (svasth) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit svastha, "self-abiding, being in one's natural state", and so "well, healthy" (Monier-Williams), built on sva, "self", and stha, "standing".

Lesson 4 of 5.
आयुर्वेद (āyurved) — ayurveda; traditional Indian medicine.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for healthy, then the Hindi for well again.

You'll want to know: आयुर्वेद.
आयुर्वेद — āyurved — "ayurveda; traditional Indian medicine".
आयुर्वेद की दवा से दादा जल्दी ठीक हो गए। āyurved kī davā se dādā jaldī ṭhīk ho gae. With ayurvedic medicine, my grandfather soon got well.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
आयुर्वेद (āyurved) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit āyurveda, which Monier-Williams explains as the learning of health and healing (Monier-Williams), built on āyus, "life", and veda, "knowledge". It names India's traditional medicine and its herbal remedies.

Lesson 5 of 5.
स्फूर्ति (sphūrti) — vigour; fresh energy.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for well again, then the Hindi for ayurveda.

You'll want to know: स्फूर्ति.
स्फूर्ति — sphūrti — "vigour; fresh energy".
सुबह की सैर के बाद दादी में नई स्फूर्ति थी। subah kī sair ke bād dādī meṁ naī sphūrti thī. After her morning walk my grandmother felt full of fresh energy.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
स्फूर्ति (sphūrti) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit sphūrti, "quivering, throbbing" (Monier-Williams); Hindi uses it for the lively energy one feels after rest, a sense the dictionary does not give. It is the learned cousin of phurtī.
