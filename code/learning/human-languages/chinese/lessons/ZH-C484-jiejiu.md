## You'll want to know: jièjiǔ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C484-WELL484-02]; assesses=[] -->

*jièjiǔ* — "to give up drinking".

> *yīshēng shuō tā yīnggāi jièjiǔ* — The doctor says he ought to give up drinking.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*jièjiǔ* is built from *jiè* 'to guard against' and 酒 'alcohol', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *keajH* and *tsjuwX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to be careful' and 'to be on guard' and the second as wine.
