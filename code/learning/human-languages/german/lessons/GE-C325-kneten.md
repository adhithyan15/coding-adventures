## You'll want to know: kneten
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C325-TEIG-03]; assesses=[] -->

**kneten** — "to knead".

> Er knetete den Teig zehn Minuten lang.
>
> *He kneaded the dough for ten minutes.*

Regular: *knetete*, *hat geknetet*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*kneten* is inherited from Old High German *knetan*, from Proto-Germanic *knedaną* "to knead" (EtymDB), the same source as English *knead*.
