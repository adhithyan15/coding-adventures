Arabic, chapter 384: Funding and development.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
مول (mawwala) — to fund.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for inflation, then the Arabic for to invest.

You'll want to know: مول.
مول — mawwala — "to fund".
مولت الدولة المشروع. mawwalat ad-dawla al-mashrūʿ. The state funded the project.
Present: يمول, yumawwil.
The noun تمويل, tamwīl, funding.

The word, taken apart.
مول (mawwala) is built on the native Arabic root m-w-l, "wealth, property" (Lane). It is Form II of the root of māl, money: to provide with money, to fund.

Lesson 5 of 5.
تنمية (tanmiya) — development.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for industry, then the Arabic for agriculture.

You'll want to know: تنمية.
تنمية — tanmiya — "development".
تحتاج القرية إلى تنمية. taḥtāju al-qarya ilā tanmiya. The village needs development.

The word, taken apart.
تنمية (tanmiya) is built on the native Arabic root n-m-w, usually traced to the sense "to grow, to increase". Tanmiya is the Form II verbal noun, making a thing grow: development.
