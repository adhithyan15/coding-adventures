Punjabi, chapter 292: Damp and Dry.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ਸਿੱਲ੍ਹਾ (sillhā) — damp.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for lukewarm, then the Punjabi for to cool something down.

You'll want to know: ਸਿੱਲ੍ਹਾ.
ਸਿੱਲ੍ਹਾ — sillhā — "damp".
ਬਰਸਾਤ ਵਿੱਚ ਕੰਧਾਂ ਸਿੱਲ੍ਹੀਆਂ ਹੁੰਦੀਆਂ ਹਨ। barsāt vich kandhāṁ sillhīāṁ hundīāṁ han. In the rainy season the walls are damp.

The word, taken apart.
ਸਿੱਲ੍ਹਾ (sillhā) is inherited from Sanskrit śītala, "cold"; CDIAL lists Punjabi sillhā, "damp", under it (CDIAL 12487).

Lesson 3 of 5.
ਨਮੀ (namī) — moisture; humidity.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for damp, then the Punjabi for arid.

You'll want to know: ਨਮੀ.
ਨਮੀ — namī — "moisture; humidity".
ਸਮੁੰਦਰ ਦੇ ਨੇੜੇ ਹਵਾ ਵਿੱਚ ਬਹੁਤ ਨਮੀ ਹੁੰਦੀ ਹੈ। samundar de neṛe havā vich bahut namī hundī hai. Near the sea there is a lot of moisture in the air.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ਨਮੀ (namī) is a loan, usually traced to Persian namī, "dampness", built on nam, "damp".

Lesson 4 of 5.
ਸੁੱਕਣਾ (sukkṇā) — to dry up; to become dry.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for arid, then the Punjabi for moisture.

You'll want to know: ਸੁੱਕਣਾ.
ਸੁੱਕਣਾ — sukkṇā — "to dry up; to become dry".
ਧੁੱਪ ਵਿੱਚ ਕੱਪੜੇ ਜਲਦੀ ਸੁੱਕਦੇ ਹਨ। dhupp vich kappṛe jaldī sukkde han. Clothes dry quickly in the sunshine.
Past (it …): ਉਹ ਕੱਪੜਾ ਸੁੱਕਿਆ, oh kappṛā sukkiā.

The word, taken apart.
ਸੁੱਕਣਾ (sukkṇā) is inherited from a reconstructed śuṣkati, "becomes dry"; CDIAL lists Punjabi sukkṇā, "dries up", under it (CDIAL 12552).
