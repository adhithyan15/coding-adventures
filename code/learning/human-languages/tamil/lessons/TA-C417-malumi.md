## You'll want to know: மாலுமி
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C417-MALUMI417-01]; assesses=[] -->

**மாலுமி** — *mālumi* — "a sailor".

> மாலுமிகள் கப்பலைச் சுத்தம் செய்தார்கள்.
>
> *mālumikaḷ kappalaic cuttam ceytārkaḷ.*
>
> The sailors cleaned the ship.

The plural is மாலுமிகள்.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**மாலுமி** (*mālumi*) is usually traced to Arabic *muʿallim*, "a master, a ship's captain"; the path into Tamil is not settled.
