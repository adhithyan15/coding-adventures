## You'll want to know: xīnláng
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C305-ENGAGE305-05]; assesses=[] -->

*xīnláng* — "bridegroom".

> *xīnláng jīntiān hěn shuài* — The groom is very handsome today.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*xīnláng* is built from 新 'new' and *láng*, both inherited from Middle Chinese, *sin* and *lang* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second as the title of an official; its use for a young man is not given there.
