## You'll want to know: प्रसाधनम्
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C487-PRASADHANA487-02]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रसाधनम्** — *prasādhanam* — "decoration; dressing up".

> उत्सवात् पूर्वं मीरा प्रसाधनं करोति।
>
> *utsavāt pūrvaṃ mīrā prasādhanaṃ karoti.*
>
> Before the festival Mira dresses up.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रसाधनम्** (*prasādhanam*) is built from *pra* and the root √sādh, "to accomplish": a making ready; MW gives "embellishment, decoration, toilet" (MW).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
