## You'll want to know: گنگ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C463-TALK463-05]; assesses=[] -->

**گنگ** — *gung* — "speechless; struck dumb".

> خبر سن کر وہ گنگ رہ گیا۔
>
> *khabar sun kar voh gung rah gayā.*
>
> He was struck dumb when he heard the news.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**گنگ** (*gung*) is usually traced to Persian *gung*, "mute".
