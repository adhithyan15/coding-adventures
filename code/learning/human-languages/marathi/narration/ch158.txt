Marathi, chapter 158: To wipe, To sweep, To quarrel, To obtain, To save.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
पुसणे (pusṇe) — to wipe.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to get tired, then the Marathi for to stay awake.

You'll want to know: पुसणे.
पुसणे — pusṇe — "to wipe".

The word, taken apart.
पुसणे (pusṇe) is a tadbhava of Sanskrit proñchati, "wipes off" (CDIAL 9011). CDIAL suggests the Sanskrit verb renders a Middle Indo-Aryan poṁch-, perhaps from Dravidian, comparing Tamil pūcu, "to smear, rub".

भांडणे — bhāṇḍṇe — "to quarrel".

The word, taken apart.
भांडणे (bhāṇḍṇe) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from bhaṇḍate, "reproves, mocks, jests" (CDIAL 9372). CDIAL glosses the Marathi verb "to reproach, abuse".

Lesson 5 of 7.
वाचवणे (vācavṇe) — to save, to rescue.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to quarrel, then the Marathi for to obtain.

You'll want to know: वाचवणे.
वाचवणे — vācavṇe — "to save, to rescue".

The word, taken apart.
वाचवणे (vācavṇe) is the "make" form of vācṇẽ, "to escape, survive", which CDIAL lists as vāṁcṇẽ under Sanskrit vañcati, a verb whose forms meant "moves, escapes from" (CDIAL 11208). The b- form, bacāviṇẽ, "to save", it marks as taken from Hindi.
