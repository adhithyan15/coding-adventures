You'll want to know: 想要.
想要 — xiǎngyào — "to want; would like".
我想要一个手机 wǒ xiǎngyào yí ge shǒujī — I'd like a phone.
The next lesson puts the second character in your hand: it is the yào you have said since the chapter on money.

The word, taken apart.
想要 (xiǎngyào) is built from 想 'to think of; to want to' and yào 'to want', both inherited from Middle Chinese, sjangX and 'jiewH (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'to think' and 'to bind by agreement'. 'To want' is not given there for yào, so how it got that sense is not settled.

What you've built.
想 + a verb: you would like to do it, 我想去. yào + a thing: you want it. 想要 (xiǎngyào) + a thing says the same as yào, more gently.

Script — the top of 西, then 女.
要 (yào).
Two pieces, top before bottom, and the bottom one you have written since 好.
覀, six strokes: the top bar; then a box, its left side and its top turning down the right side; two short verticals inside it; then the bar that closes the box.
女, three strokes, wide under it: the stroke that falls left and turns down to the right, the left-falling stroke, then the long bar.
6 + 3 = nine.

[pause 3 seconds]

Lesson 3 of 6.
不要 (bú yào) — don't; do not want.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: read the last lesson's character, then two words with the same rising 不.
[once you have stopped driving — read: 我想要, aloud — wǒ xiǎngyào]

[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say 不客气 — bú kèqi, you're welcome]

You'll want to know: 不要.
不要 — bú yào — "don't; do not want".
我不要 wǒ bú yào — I don't want it.
不要说 bú yào shuō — Don't say it.
不 before the fall of 要 rises: bú yào, as in bú shì.

The word, taken apart.
不要 (bú yào) is built from 不 'not' and 要 'to want', both inherited from Middle Chinese, pjut and 'jiewH (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription).

What you've built.
Two jobs. 我不要: I don't want it. 不要 (bú yào) + a verb: don't do it. The second is how Mandarin tells somebody not to.

You'll want to know: xūyào.
xūyào — "to need".
wǒ xūyào xiūxi — I need a rest.
nǐ xūyào bāngmáng ma — Do you need a hand?
Its first character comes later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
xūyào is built from xū and yào 'to want', both inherited from Middle Chinese, sju and 'jiewH (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription). The Guangyun glosses sju only as the name of a hexagram, so 'to need' is not given there.

What you've built.
想要 (xiǎngyào) is what you would like; xūyào is what you cannot do without.

You'll want to know: 一点儿.
一点儿 — yìdiǎnr — "a little; a bit".
我要一点儿水 wǒ yào yìdiǎnr shuǐ — I'd like a little water.
一 before the dip of 点 falls: yì.

The word, taken apart.
一点儿 (yìdiǎnr) is built from 一 'one', 点 'a dot' and the r ending 儿: a dot's worth. 一 and 点 are inherited from Middle Chinese, 'jit and temX (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), temX glossed 'a dot; a stroke of the brush'.

What you've built.
一点儿 (yìdiǎnr) goes after the verb and before the thing: 要 (yào), 一点儿 (yìdiǎnr), 水.

Lesson 6 of 6.
我想要一点儿水 (wǒ xiǎngyào yìdiǎnr shuǐ) — a host and a guest: what you would like, what you need, and a polite no.

Warm-up.

[your turn — recall: say yìdiǎnr, a little — R1, one lesson back]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say chá, tea — R4, recognition at distance]

The exchange.
A: 你想喝什么 nǐ xiǎng hē shénme — What would you like to drink? B: 我想要一点儿水 wǒ xiǎngyào yìdiǎnr shuǐ — I'd like a little water. A: nǐ yào chá ma — Would you like tea? B: 不要 谢谢 bú yào, xièxie — No, thank you. A: nǐ xūyào xiūxi ma — Do you need a rest? B: bù, wǒ bú lèi — No, I'm not tired.
Four ways of wanting: 想 + a verb, 想要 (xiǎngyào) + a thing, yào + a thing, xūyào for a need. And one way of refusing that is still polite: 不要 (bú yào), 谢谢.

Reading — the exchange at once.
[once you have stopped driving — read: the exchange through once without stopping, then answer the question below]
[question — say your answer, then pause 12 seconds]
Does B want tea?

What you've built — wanting, needing, refusing.
我想要一点儿水 wǒ xiǎngyào yìdiǎnr shuǐ — I'd like a little water.
我要这个 wǒ yào zhège — I want this one.
wǒ xūyào xiūxi — I need a rest.
不要 谢谢 bú yào, xièxie — no, thank you.
Listen. Nǐ yào shénme?
Speak. Ask for a little of something, then turn something down politely.
