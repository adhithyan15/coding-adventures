## You'll want to know: ગારો
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C504-KUMBHAR504-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ગારો** — *gāro* — "mud plaster".

> અમારા ગામનાં જૂનાં ઘર ગારાનાં છે.
>
> *amārā gāmnā̃ jūnā̃ ghar gārānā̃ che.*
>
> The old houses in our village are made of mud.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ગારો** (*gāro*) comes from *\*gāra*, a form CDIAL reconstructs, and CDIAL lists the Gujarati word as earth and water mixed for building a wall (CDIAL 4137).
