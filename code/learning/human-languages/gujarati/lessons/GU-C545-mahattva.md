## You'll want to know: મહત્ત્વ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C545-AASAN545-03]; assesses=[] -->

**મહત્ત્વ** — *mahattva* — "importance".

> જો તમે સમયનું મહત્ત્વ સમજશો તો તમે ક્યારેય મોડા નહીં પડો.
>
> *jo tame samaynũ mahattva samajśo to tame kyārey moḍā nahī̃ paḍo.*
>
> If you understand the value of time, you will never be late.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**મહત્ત્વ** (*mahattva*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *mahattva*, "greatness, magnitude" (Monier-Williams).
