## You'll want to know: おくれる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C152-READY152-05]; assesses=[] -->

**おくれる** — *okureru* — "to be late".

> **いもうとは よく がっこうに おくれます。** — *imōto wa yoku gakkō ni okuremasu* — "My younger sister is often late for school."

Polite **おくれます**, *okuremasu*; an ichidan verb.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**おくれる** (*okureru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi, and its older origin is not settled (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
