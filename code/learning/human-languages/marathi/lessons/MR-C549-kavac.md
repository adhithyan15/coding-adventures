## You'll want to know: कवच
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C549-SANRAKSHAN549-03]; assesses=[] -->

**कवच** — *kavac* — "a shield; armour; a shell".

> कासवाचं कवच खूप कठीण असतं.
>
> *kāsvācaṁ kavac khūp kaṭhīṇ astaṁ.*
>
> A tortoise's shell is very hard.

Neuter.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**कवच** (*kavac*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *kavaca*, "a coat of mail" (Monier-Williams).
