## The exchange
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-PERFORMANCE-MEASURE-01]; assesses=[ZH-LEX-C88-LIANG-01, ZH-LEX-C88-BEI-01, ZH-LEX-C88-ZHANG-01, ZH-LEX-C88-ZHI-01, ZH-LEX-C88-BEN-01, ZH-GRAMMAR-MEASURE-WORD-01, ZH-LEX-GE-01] -->

> **A:** *nǐmen jǐ ge rén* — How many of you are there?
>
> **B:** 两个人
>
> *liǎng ge rén* — Two.
>
> **A:** 你们喝什么
>
> *nǐmen hē shénme* — What will you drink?
>
> **B:** *liǎng bēi chá* — Two cups of tea.
>
> **A:** *hǎo, nà zhāng zhuōzi* — Fine. That table, then.
>
> **B:** *nàr yǒu liǎng zhī māo* — There are two cats there.
>
> **A:** *zhuōzi shàngbian yǒu yì běn shū* — And there's a book on the table.

Every count has its measure word: 个 for people, *bēi* for tea, *zhāng* for the table, *zhī* for the cats, *běn* for the book. And every two is 两.

## What you've built — a number, a measure word, a thing
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ZH-PERFORMANCE-MEASURE-01, ZH-SCRIPT-LIANG-01, ZH-RULE-TONE-SANDHI-YI-01, ZH-LEX-C88-LIANG-01] -->

Every count has three parts, in this order: the number, the measure word, the thing.

- **两个人** *liǎng ge rén* — two people
- *yì bēi shuǐ* — a glass of water
- *yì běn shū* — a book
- *liǎng zhāng piào* — two tickets
- *liǎng zhī māo* — two cats

**Listen.** *Nǐ yǒu jǐ běn shū?*

**Speak.** Count what is on your table, with the right measure word for each.

[YOU WRITE: 两 — the bar, the frame, then the two small 人]
