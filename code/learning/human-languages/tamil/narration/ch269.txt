Tamil, chapter 269: Best, Different, Boring, Simple, Comfortable.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
சிறந்த (ciṟanta) — best, finest.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for gentle, then the Tamil for honest.

You'll want to know: சிறந்த.
சிறந்த — ciṟanta — "best, finest".

The word, taken apart.
சிறந்த (ciṟanta) is native Dravidian. DEDR 2589 lists Malayalam ciṟakka, "to be glorious". It is a participle of ciṟa, "to be eminent, to excel".
