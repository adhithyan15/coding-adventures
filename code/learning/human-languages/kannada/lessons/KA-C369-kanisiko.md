# ಕಾಣಿಸಿಕೊ (kāṇisiko) — to appear; to turn up

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[KA-LEX-C369-ANTHU369-03, KA-LEX-C369-ANTHU369-04] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Kannada for unexpected, then the Kannada for a jail.

## You'll want to know: ಕಾಣಿಸಿಕೊ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C369-ANTHU369-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ಕಾಣಿಸಿಕೊ** — *kāṇisiko* — "to appear; to turn up".

> ತುಂಬಾ ವರ್ಷದ ನಂತರ ಆ ನಟ ಟೀವಿಯಲ್ಲಿ ಕಾಣಿಸಿಕೊಂಡನು.
>
> *tumbā varṣada nantara ā naṭa ṭīviyalli kāṇisikoṇḍanu.*
>
> After many years that actor appeared on television.

Past (I …): ಕಾಣಿಸಿಕೊಂಡೆ, *kāṇisikoṇḍe*.

Of people and things showing up: ಜ್ವರ ಕಾಣಿಸಿಕೊಂಡಿತು, a fever appeared.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಕಾಣಿಸಿಕೊ** (*kāṇisiko*) is a native Dravidian compound: ಕಾಣಿಸು, "to be seen", from *kāṇ* "to see" (DEDR 1443), with -*ko*, from *koḷ*, "to take" (DEDR 2151).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
