## You'll want to know: tái
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C174-JOBS174-03]; assesses=[] -->

*tái* — "to lift; to carry (between two people)".

> *zhège zhuōzi hěn zhòng wǒmen yìqǐ tái* — This table is heavy: let's lift it together.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*tái* is inherited from Middle Chinese *doj* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), written there another way and glossed 'to lift up'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what I do.
