## You'll want to know: مستمع
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C356-JUMHUR356-03]; assesses=[] -->

**مستمع** — *mustamiʿ* — "a listener".

> اتصل مستمع بالبرنامج صباحا.
>
> *ittaṣala mustamiʿ bi-l-barnāmaj ṣabāḥan.*
>
> A listener phoned the programme in the morning.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**مستمع** (*mustamiʿ*) is built on the native Arabic root *s-m-ʿ* (Lane), "to hear". *Mustamiʿ* is the participle of the taught *istamaʿa*, to listen.
