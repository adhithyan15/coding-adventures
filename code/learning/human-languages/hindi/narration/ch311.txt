Lesson 1 of 6.
सहायता (sahāytā) — aid; assistance.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for safe and sound, then the Hindi for comfort.

You'll want to know: सहायता.
सहायता — sahāytā — "aid; assistance".
कई देशों ने सहायता भेजी। kaī deśoṁ ne sahāytā bhejī. Many countries sent aid.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
सहायता (sahāytā) is Sanskrit sahāyatā (Monier-Williams), from sahāya, a companion who goes along with you. It is the formal word beside मदद.

Lesson 2 of 6.
स्वयंसेवक (svayaṁsevak) — a volunteer.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for comfort, then the Hindi for aid.

You'll want to know: स्वयंसेवक.
स्वयंसेवक — svayaṁsevak — "a volunteer".
मुझे उम्मीद है कि कल और स्वयंसेवक आएँगे। mujhe ummīd hai ki kal aur svayaṁsevak āeṅge. I hope more volunteers will come tomorrow.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
स्वयंसेवक (svayaṁsevak) is a modern compound of Sanskrit svayam, "oneself, of one's own accord", and sevaka, "a servant" (Monier-Williams): someone who serves because they chose to.

Lesson 3 of 6.
जुटना (juṭnā) — to gather; to come together.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for aid, then the Hindi for a volunteer.

You'll want to know: जुटना.
जुटना — juṭnā — "to gather; to come together".
मदद के लिए स्कूल में सौ लोग जुटे। madad ke liye skūl meṁ sau log juṭe. A hundred people gathered at the school to help.
Past (it …): वह भीड़ जुटी, vah bhīṛ juṭī.

The word, taken apart.
जुटना (juṭnā) is inherited from yuṭati, "is joined", a form CDIAL reconstructs (CDIAL 10496), the family of जोड़ना, to join. People जुटते हैं when they come together for one purpose.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: जुटाना.
जुटाना — juṭānā — "to collect; to raise (money)".
स्वयंसेवकों ने एक लाख रुपये जुटाए। svayaṁsevakoṁ ne ek lākh rupaye juṭāe. The volunteers raised a hundred thousand rupees.
Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने पैसा जुटाया, usne paisā juṭāyā.

The word, taken apart.
जुटाना (juṭānā) is the causative of जुटना (juṭnā), to gather, from the same yuṭati (CDIAL 10496): to make things come together. You जुटाते हो money, help or courage.

Lesson 5 of 6.
हौसला (hauslā) — spirit; morale.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for to gather, then the Hindi for to collect.

You'll want to know: हौसला.
हौसला — hauslā — "spirit; morale".
स्वयंसेवकों ने पीड़ितों का हौसला बढ़ाया। svayaṁsevakoṁ ne pīṛitoṁ kā hauslā baṛhāyā. The volunteers raised the victims' spirits.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
हौसला (hauslā) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic ḥawṣala, first the crop of a bird and then capacity; in Hindi it is the courage to keep going. हौसला बढ़ाना is to encourage.
