## You'll want to know: ચાવવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C474-CAVVU474-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ચાવવું** — *cāvvũ* — "to chew".

> દાદા ધીમે ધીમે રોટલી ચાવે છે.
>
> *dādā dhīme dhīme roṭlī cāve che.*
>
> Grandfather chews his flatbread slowly.

Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે ભાત ચાવ્યો, *teṇe bhāt cāvyo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ચાવવું** (*cāvvũ*) is inherited from Sanskrit *carvati*, "chews"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb (CDIAL 4711).
