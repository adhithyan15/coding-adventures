# ভাবা (bhābā) — "to think," and the verb it was built out of

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

[PAUSE 2s] You already have the verb "to be." Sanskrit built "to think" out of
it, by a trick Bengali still uses every day.

## You'll want to know: ভাবা
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C08-BHABA-01]; assesses=[] -->

> **ভাবা** — *bhābā* — **to think**

আমি ভাবি (*āmi bhābi*), "I think." আপনি ভাবেন (*āpni bhāben*), respectfully.

## The letters in this word
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ভাবা** is **ভা** (*bhā*) + **বা** (*bā*). **ভ** is *bh*, a *b* with breath
behind it; **ব** is the plain *b* underneath. Two letters, one puff of air
between them.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ভাবা** is Sanskrit **ভাবয়তি** (*bhāvayati*), and *bhāvayati* is not its own
verb: it is **হওয়া**'s verb with a gear changed. *Bhavati* is "it becomes";
*bhāvayati* is "he **makes** it become" — and from making-something-be to
imagining it is one short step. So Bengali *bhābi* and Bengali *hôi* are one
root, √ভূ (*√bhū*), which is also English **be** and **been**, Latin *futūrus*
(English **future**), and Greek *phúsis* (English **physics**). The noun
*bhāva*, "a state of mind," sits between them: thinking is holding a state.

## Grammar Lens: the gear that makes someone else do it
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-GRAMMAR-C08-BHABA-02]; assesses=[] -->

Sanskrit changed that gear with a lengthened vowel. Bengali changes it with the
ending **-আনো** (*-āno*), and it is completely alive:

| plain verb | the made-to-happen verb |
|---|---|
| দেখা (*dækhā*), to see | দেখানো (*dækhāno*), to **show** — make see |
| খাওয়া (*khāwā*), to eat | খাওয়ানো (*khāwāno*), to **feed** — make eat |
| করা (*kôrā*), to do | করানো (*kôrāno*), to have it done |

One suffix, and a verb you already own becomes a verb about making someone else
do it. There is no separate word for "show" or "feed" to learn; there is a gear.
That is the same machinery that built ভাবা itself, two thousand years earlier
and one language back.
