## You'll want to know: નારાજ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C267-UPSET267-01]; assesses=[] -->

**નારાજ** — *nārāj* — "displeased, upset".

> **શું તમે મારાથી નારાજ છો?** — *śũ tame mārāthī nārāj cho?* — "Are you upset with me?"

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**નારાજ** (*nārāj*) is a loan, usually traced to Persian *nārāz*, "displeased". CDIAL has no entry for it.
