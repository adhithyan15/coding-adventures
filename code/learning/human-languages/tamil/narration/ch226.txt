Tamil, chapter 226: To begin, To touch, To sneeze, To swallow, To chew.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ஆரம்பி (ārampi) — to begin.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for to take off, then the Tamil for to change.

You'll want to know: ஆரம்பி.
ஆரம்பி — ārampi — "to begin".

The word, taken apart.
ஆரம்பி (ārampi) is a verb made from Sanskrit ārambha, "beginning" (CDIAL 1307).
