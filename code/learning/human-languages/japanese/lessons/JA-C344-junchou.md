## You'll want to know: じゅんちょう
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C344-KEIKAKU344-05]; assesses=[] -->

**じゅんちょう** — *junchō* — "going smoothly; favourable".

> **はれたら、けいかくは じゅんちょうでしょう。** — *haretara, keikaku wa junchō deshō* — "If the weather clears, the plan will probably go smoothly."

A na-adjective: な before a noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**じゅんちょう** (*junchō*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *jun*, "order" (Middle Chinese *zywinH*) and *chō*, "tune" (*dew*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
