Arabic, chapter 326: Old weapons.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
سلاح (silāḥ) — a weapon.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to confess, then the Arabic for to deny.

You'll want to know: سلاح.
سلاح — silāḥ — "a weapon".
كان مع الحارس سلاح صغير. kāna maʿa al-ḥāris silāḥ ṣaghīr. The guard had a small weapon.
Plural: أسلحة, asliḥa.

The word, taken apart.
سلاح (silāḥ) is built on the native Arabic root s-l-ḥ (Lane), where silāḥ names the arms of war and musallaḥ, armed, is its participle.

Lesson 2 of 5.
سيف (sayf) — a sword.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to deny, then the Arabic for a weapon.

You'll want to know: سيف.
سيف — sayf — "a sword".
علق الفارس سيفه على الجدار. ʿallaqa al-fāris sayfahu ʿalā al-jidār. The knight hung his sword on the wall.
Plural: سيوف, suyūf.

The word, taken apart.
سيف (sayf) is filed by Lane under the root s-y-f. A link with Greek xiphos, a sword, is often proposed, but the direction of the borrowing, if any, is not settled.

Lesson 4 of 5.
سهم (sahm) — an arrow; a share.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a sword, then the Arabic for a spear.

You'll want to know: سهم.
سهم — sahm — "an arrow; a share".
رمى الصياد سهما نحو الشجرة. ramā aṣ-ṣayyād sahman naḥwa ash-shajara. The hunter shot an arrow at the tree.
Plural: سهام, sihām.
In business a share is سهم (sahm), plural أسهم. A road sign arrow is سهم (sahm) too.

The word, taken apart.
سهم (sahm) is built on the native Arabic root s-h-m (Lane), where sahm is an arrow, and an arrow drawn as a lot: so also a share, sāhama, to take part.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: درع.
درع — dirʿ — "armour; a shield".
لبس الجندي درعا من الحديد. labisa al-jundī dirʿan min al-ḥadīd. The soldier wore armour of iron.
Plural: دروع, durūʿ.
Today a درع (dirʿ) is also a shield-shaped trophy.

The word, taken apart.
درع (dirʿ) is built on the native Arabic root d-r-ʿ (Lane), where dirʿ is a coat of mail, rings linked over the body.
