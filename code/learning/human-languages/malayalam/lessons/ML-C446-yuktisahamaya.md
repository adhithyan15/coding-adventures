## You'll want to know: യുക്തിസഹമായ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C446-VYAKTAM446-04]; assesses=[] -->

**യുക്തിസഹമായ** — *yuktisahamāya* — "logical; reasonable".

> അവളുടെ വാദം യുക്തിസഹമായിരുന്നു.
>
> *avaḷuṭe vādaṁ yuktisahamāyirunnu.*
>
> Her argument was logical.

യുക്തി, logic, with -സഹ, with: in keeping with logic.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**യുക്തിസഹമായ** (*yuktisahamāya*) is built from Sanskrit *yukti*, "reasoning" (CDIAL 10480), and *saha*, "together with" (CDIAL 13297), with the adjective ending -ആയ: going with reason.
