# গোঁ ধরা (gõ dhôrā) — to dig one's heels in

## You'll want to know: গোঁ ধরা
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C461-JEDI461-05]; assesses=[] -->

**গোঁ ধরা** — *gõ dhôrā* — "to dig one's heels in".

> নতুন সাইকেলের জন্য ছেলেটা গোঁ ধরল।
>
> *nôtun sāikeler jônyô chheleṭā gõ dhôrlô.*
>
> The boy dug his heels in about getting a new bicycle.

Past (he/she …): তিনি গোঁ ধরলেন, *tini gõ dhôrlen*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**গোঁ ধরা** (*gõ dhôrā*) is *gõ*, a stubborn will, which CDIAL lists for Bengali as a whim (CDIAL 4025), with ধরা (taught), to hold (CDIAL 6747).

## What you've built

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
