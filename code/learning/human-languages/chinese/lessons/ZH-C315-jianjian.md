## You'll want to know: jiànjiàn
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C315-AGAIN315-01]; assesses=[] -->

*jiànjiàn* — "gradually; little by little".

> *wǒ jiànjiàn xíguàn le zhèr de shēnghuó* — Little by little I got used to life here.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*jiànjiàn* is the word *jiàn* said twice, inherited from Middle Chinese *dzjemX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses it 'step by step' and 'to advance'.
