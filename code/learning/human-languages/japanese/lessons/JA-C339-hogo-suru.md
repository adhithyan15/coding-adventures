## You'll want to know: ほごする
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C339-BUNBETSU339-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ほごする** — *hogo suru* — "to safeguard; to protect (wildlife)".

> **どうぶつが すくなく なったので、ほごしています。** — *dōbutsu ga sukunaku natta node, hogo shite imasu* — "The animals have become few, so they are being protected."

Polite **ほごします**, *hogoshimasu*; a する verb. Past (polite): **まちが もりを ほごしました。**, *machi ga mori o hogo shimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ほごする** (*hogo suru*) is built from a Sino-Japanese noun and the native verb *suru*, "to do". The noun, *hogo*, is two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese: *ho*, "preserve" (Middle Chinese *pawX*) and *go*, "protect" (*huH*) (KANJIDIC2; JMdict; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
