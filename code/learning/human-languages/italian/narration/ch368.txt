Italian, chapter 368: Vivere insieme.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
la convivenza — the living together, the coexistence.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for the charity, then the Italian for the community.

You'll want to know: la convivenza.
la convivenza — "the living together, the coexistence".
La convivenza con i vicini non è sempre facile. Living alongside the neighbours is not always easy.
It also means a couple living together without being married.

The word, taken apart.
convivenza is built on convivere "to live together", from Latin convīvere "to live with" (Lewis & Short), built on vīvere "to live".

Lesson 3 of 5.
il rispetto — the respect.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for the living together, then the Italian for the tolerance.

You'll want to know: il rispetto.
il rispetto — "the respect".
Bisogna parlare agli anziani con rispetto. You should speak to old people with respect.
Rispetto a — compared with: rispetto a ieri fa più caldo.

The word, taken apart.
rispetto comes from Latin respectus "a looking back, regard" (Lewis & Short), from respicere "to look back at", built on specere "to look": you look again at someone.

integrazione is a learned borrowing from Latin integrātiō "a renewal" (Lewis & Short), from integrāre "to make whole", built on integer "whole, untouched".
