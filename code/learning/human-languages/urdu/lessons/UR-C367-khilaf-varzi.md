## You'll want to know: خلاف ورزی
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C367-QAIDA367-03]; assesses=[] -->

**خلاف ورزی** — *khilāf-varzī* — "a breach; a violation".

> اگر قانون کی خلاف ورزی ہوئی تو جرمانہ ہو گا۔
>
> *agar qānūn kī khilāf-varzī huī to jurmānā ho gā.*
>
> If the law is broken, there will be a fine.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**خلاف ورزی** (*khilāf-varzī*) is built from **خلاف**, "against", usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *khilāf*, and Persian *varzī*, "practising", from *varzīdan*. Acting against a rule.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
