## You'll want to know: défiler
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C317-CARNAVAL-02]; assesses=[] -->

**défiler** — "to march past, to parade; to scroll".

> Les soldats ont défilé dans la rue.
>
> *The soldiers marched down the street.*

On a screen: *faire défiler une page* — to scroll.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*défiler* is built on *file* "a row, a line" (EtymDB), which comes from *fil* "thread", from Latin *fīlum* "a thread" (Lewis & Short).
