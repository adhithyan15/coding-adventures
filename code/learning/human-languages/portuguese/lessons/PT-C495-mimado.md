## You'll want to know: mimado
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C495-EDUCAR-05]; assesses=[] -->

**mimado** — "spoiled".

> É um menino mimado que quer tudo.
>
> *He is a spoiled little boy who wants everything.*

Of a child who gets too much: *uma criança mimada*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*mimado* is the participle of *mimar* "to pamper", built on *mimo* "a treat, a caress"; the origin of *mimo* is not settled.
