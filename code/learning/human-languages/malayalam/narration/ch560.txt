Malayalam, chapter 560: Sculptures and Exhibitions.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
വിനോദയാത്ര (vinōdayātra) — an excursion; a pleasure trip.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for to cherish, then the Malayalam for to ask forgiveness.

You'll want to know: വിനോദയാത്ര.
വിനോദയാത്ര — vinōdayātra — "an excursion; a pleasure trip".
സ്കൂളിൽ നിന്ന് കുട്ടികൾ വിനോദയാത്രയ്ക്ക് പോയി. skūḷil ninnŭ kuṭṭikaḷ vinōdayātraykkŭ pōyi. The children went on a school excursion.
വിനോദം a pastime + യാത്ര a journey.

The word, taken apart.
വിനോദയാത്ര (vinōdayātra) is a learned compound built from Sanskrit vinoda, "diversion, pastime" (Monier-Williams), and yātrā, "a journey" (CDIAL 10456).
