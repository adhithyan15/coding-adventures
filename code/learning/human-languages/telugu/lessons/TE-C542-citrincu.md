# చిత్రించు (citriñcu) — to portray; to paint

## You'll want to know: చిత్రించు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C542-SILPAM542-02]; assesses=[] -->

**చిత్రించు** — *citriñcu* — "to portray; to paint".

> ఆ కళాకారుడు పల్లె జీవితాన్ని చిత్రించాడు.
>
> *ā kaḷākāruḍu palle jīvitānni citriñcāḍu.*
>
> That artist portrayed village life.

Past (I …): చిత్రించాను, *citriñcānu*.

In paint or in words.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**చిత్రించు** (*citriñcu*) is built on Sanskrit *citra*, "bright, variegated", the source of చిత్రం, "a picture" (CDIAL 4803), with the Telugu verb ending -ఇంచు.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
