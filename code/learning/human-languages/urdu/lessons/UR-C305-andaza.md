## You'll want to know: اندازہ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C305-MUAMMA305-03]; assesses=[] -->

**اندازہ** — *andāzā* — "a guess; an estimate".

> میرا اندازہ غلط تھا۔
>
> *merā andāzā ghalat thā.*
>
> My guess was wrong.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**اندازہ** (*andāzā*) is usually traced to Persian *andāza*, "a measure". **اندازہ لگانا** is to make a guess.
