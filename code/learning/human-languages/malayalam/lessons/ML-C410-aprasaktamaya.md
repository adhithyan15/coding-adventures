## You'll want to know: അപ്രസക്തമായ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C410-SUCANA410-05]; assesses=[] -->

**അപ്രസക്തമായ** — *aprasaktamāya* — "irrelevant".

> ആ ചോദ്യം ഇപ്പോൾ അപ്രസക്തമാണ്.
>
> *ā cōdyaṁ ippōḷ aprasaktamāṇŭ.*
>
> That question is irrelevant now.

The Sanskrit prefix അ- makes an opposite: പ്രസക്തമായ, അപ്രസക്തമായ.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**അപ്രസക്തമായ** (*aprasaktamāya*) is built from Sanskrit *a-*, "not", and *prasakta*, "attached to, connected with" (Monier-Williams), with the adjective ending -ആയ.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
