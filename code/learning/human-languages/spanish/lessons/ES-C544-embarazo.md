## You'll want to know: el embarazo
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C544-PARTO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**el embarazo** — "the pregnancy".

> Tuvo un embarazo tranquilo.
>
> *She had an easy pregnancy.*

It once meant an obstacle, and in formal Spanish it still can.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*embarazo* is built on *embarazar*, first 'to get in the way, to hinder'. The origin of *embarazar* is not settled; it is often linked to Portuguese *embaraçar* and an old word *baraça* 'a cord'.
