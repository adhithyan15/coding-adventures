## You'll want to know: il supplemento
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C418-BIGLIETTERIA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**il supplemento** — "the supplement, the extra charge".

> Per il treno veloce c'è un supplemento.
>
> *For the fast train there is a supplement.*

Also a newspaper supplement.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*supplemento* is a learned borrowing from Latin *supplēmentum* 'a filling up' (EtymDB), from *supplēre* 'to fill up', built on *plēnus* 'full'.
