## You'll want to know: तापयति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C511-KASTHA511-02]; assesses=[] -->

**तापयति** — *tāpayati* — "makes hot; heats up".

> सा स्थाल्यां तैलं तापयति।
>
> *sā sthālyāṃ tailaṃ tāpayati.*
>
> She heats oil in the pan.

Past (she …): सा जलं तापितवती, *sā jalaṃ tāpitavatī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**तापयति** (*tāpayati*) comes from the causative of the root √tap, "to be hot, to give out heat", which also gives *tapati*: to make a thing hot (MW).
