Lesson 1 of 5.
मंत्री (mantrī) — a minister.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a player, then the Hindi for a leader.

You'll want to know: मंत्री.
मंत्री — mantrī — "a minister".

The word, taken apart.
मंत्री (mantrī) is Sanskrit mantrin, "wise; counsellor", taken over whole as a learned loan, a tatsama (CDIAL 9838). Hindi took its nominative, mantrī.

नौकर — naukar — "a servant".

The word, taken apart.
नौकर (naukar) is a loan, usually traced to Persian naukar, "servant", which Persian took from Mongolic nökör, "companion".
