Portuguese, chapter 394: Garantias e reembolsos.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
a garantia — the guarantee, the warranty.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for to transfer, then the Portuguese for the transfer.

You'll want to know: a garantia.
a garantia — "the guarantee, the warranty".
A máquina de lavar tem dois anos de garantia. The washing machine has a two-year warranty.
Estar na garantia — to be under warranty.

The word, taken apart.
garantia is usually traced to French garantie, which EtymDB takes back to Old French garantie "protection, defence", on garantir and garant "guarantor", a word with Germanic cousins such as Old High German werento "guarantor" (EtymDB).

Lesson 3 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for the guarantee, then the Portuguese for to guarantee.

You'll want to know: o reembolso.
o reembolso — "the refund".
Pedi o reembolso do bilhete. I asked for a refund on the ticket.
Pedir o reembolso — to ask for a refund.

The word, taken apart.
reembolso is built from re- and embolsar "to put in your purse", on bolsa "purse", from Late Latin bursa "oxhide, purse" (EtymDB): the money goes back into your purse.

Lesson 5 of 5.
o crédito — the credit; the loan.

Warm-up.

You'll want to know: o crédito.
o crédito — "the credit; the loan".
Pedimos um crédito ao banco para comprar casa. We asked the bank for a loan to buy a house.
Crédito à habitação — mortgage. A crédito — on credit.

The word, taken apart.
crédito came through Italian credito (EtymDB), from Latin crēditum "a loan", on crēdere "to trust, to lend" (Lewis & Short).
