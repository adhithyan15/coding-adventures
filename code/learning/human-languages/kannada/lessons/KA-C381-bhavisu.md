# ಭಾವಿಸು (bhāvisu) — to suppose; to assume

## You'll want to know: ಭಾವಿಸು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C381-PARIGANISU381-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ಭಾವಿಸು** — *bhāvisu* — "to suppose; to assume".

> ನೀನು ಬರುವುದಿಲ್ಲ ಅಂತ ನಾನು ಭಾವಿಸಿದೆ.
>
> *nīnu baruvudilla anta nānu bhāviside.*
>
> I assumed you would not come.

Past (I …): ಭಾವಿಸಿದೆ, *bhāviside*.

What is supposed comes before ಅಂತ or ಎಂದು.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಭಾವಿಸು** (*bhāvisu*) is built on Sanskrit *bhāva*, "being, state, disposition" (CDIAL 9475), from *bhū*, "to be", with the Kannada verb ending -*isu*.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
