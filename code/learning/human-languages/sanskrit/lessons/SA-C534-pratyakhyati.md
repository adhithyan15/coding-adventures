## You'll want to know: प्रत्याख्याति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C534-SAMVID534-04]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रत्याख्याति** — *pratyākhyāti* — "turns down".

> यदि मूल्यम् उचितं न अस्ति, तर्हि अहं तत् प्रत्याख्यामि।
>
> *yadi mūlyam ucitaṃ na asti, tarhi ahaṃ tat pratyākhyāmi.*
>
> If the price is not fair, I turn it down.

Past (he …): सः निमन्त्रणं प्रत्याख्यातवान्, *saḥ nimantraṇaṃ pratyākhyātavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रत्याख्याति** (*pratyākhyāti*) is built on *prati*, "back", *ā* and the root √khyā, "to tell"; MW gives *pratyākhyā* as to refuse, to reject, to deny (MW).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
