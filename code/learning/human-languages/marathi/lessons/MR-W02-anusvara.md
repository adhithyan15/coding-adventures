# ं — one nasal dot

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

Prepare to place one mark above a headline.

## Script
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-SCRIPT-ANUSVARA-01]; assesses=[] -->

> ं

The **anusvāra** is one dot above the headline. It marks a nasal sound whose
exact place follows the sound after it. Place one dot; do not turn it into the
two-dot visarga.

## Writing — guided copy
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MR-SCRIPT-ANUSVARA-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: guided-copy -->

Keep **ं** visible and copy it once. Point above the imaginary headline where
the dot belongs.
