## You'll want to know: ವಿಧೇಯ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C502-SABHYA502-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ವಿಧೇಯ** — *vidhēya* — "obedient; dutiful".

> ಆ ವಿಧೇಯ ಮಗ ಅಪ್ಪನ ಮಾತು ಕೇಳುತ್ತಾನೆ.
>
> *ā vidhēya maga appana mātu kēḷuttāne.*
>
> That dutiful son listens to his father.

Formal letters end with ತಮ್ಮ ವಿಧೇಯ, yours faithfully.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ವಿಧೇಯ** (*vidhēya*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *vidheya*, "to be done, to be governed", and so "docile, obedient" (Monier-Williams).
