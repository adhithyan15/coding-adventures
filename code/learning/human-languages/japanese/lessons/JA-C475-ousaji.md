## You'll want to know: おおさじ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C475-TABLE475-03]; assesses=[] -->

**おおさじ** — *ōsaji* — "a tablespoon".

> **おおさじで さとうを いれて ください。** — *ōsaji de satō o irete kudasai* — "Please put the sugar in with a tablespoon."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**おおさじ** (*ōsaji*) is a native Japanese compound (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese: *ō*, "big" and *saji*, "spoon" (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
