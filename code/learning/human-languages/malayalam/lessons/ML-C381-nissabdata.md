# നിശ്ശബ്ദത (niśśabdata) — silence

## You'll want to know: നിശ്ശബ്ദത
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C381-IRUTTU381-03]; assesses=[] -->

**നിശ്ശബ്ദത** — *niśśabdata* — "silence".

> അറിയിപ്പ് കേട്ടപ്പോൾ ക്ലാസിൽ നിശ്ശബ്ദത പരന്നു.
>
> *aṟiyippŭ kēṭṭappōḷ klāsil niśśabdata parannu.*
>
> When they heard the notice, silence spread through the class.

The adjective is നിശ്ശബ്ദമായ, silent; the -ത ending makes the noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**നിശ്ശബ്ദത** (*niśśabdata*) is built from Sanskrit *niḥśabda*, "noiseless, silent" (Monier-Williams), with -ത, the ending that makes a noun of a state. The visarga of *niḥ-* becomes a second ശ before ശബ്ദം, so the word is written with ശ്ശ.
