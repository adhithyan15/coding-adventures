## You'll want to know: o suplente
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C508-ARBITRO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**o suplente** — "substitute (player)".

> O suplente entrou e marcou um golo.
>
> *The substitute came on and scored a goal.*

*O banco de suplentes* — the bench.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*suplente* is a learned borrowing from Latin *supplēns*, from *supplēre* "to fill up, to make good" (Lewis & Short).

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
