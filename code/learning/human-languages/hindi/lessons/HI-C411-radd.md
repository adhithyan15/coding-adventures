## You'll want to know: रद्द
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C411-VAZAN411-04]; assesses=[] -->

**रद्द** — *radd* — "cancelled".

> आँधी की वजह से उड़ान रद्द हो गई।
>
> *ā̃dhī kī vajah se uṛān radd ho gaī.*
>
> The flight was cancelled because of the storm.

रद्द होना, to be cancelled; रद्द करना, to cancel.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**रद्द** (*radd*) is usually traced to Arabic *radd*, "a turning back, a rejection", through Persian. It does not change: उड़ान रद्द हुई, ट्रेन रद्द हुई.
