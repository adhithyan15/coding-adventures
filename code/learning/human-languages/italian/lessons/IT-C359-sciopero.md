## You'll want to know: lo sciopero
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C359-SCIOPERO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**lo sciopero** — "the strike".

> Domani c'è lo sciopero dei treni.
>
> *Tomorrow there is a train strike.*

*Essere in sciopero* — to be on strike. Strikes are announced in advance, so check before you travel.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*sciopero* is built on *scioperare*, usually traced to a Vulgar Latin *exoperāre* "to be out of work", from *ex-* "out of" and *operāre* "to work", built on *opus, operis* "work" (Lewis & Short).
