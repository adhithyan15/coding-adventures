## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-COMMENT-05]; assesses=[FR-GRAMMAR-INTONATION-01, FR-GRAMMAR-ESTCEQUE-02, FR-LEX-OU-03, FR-LEX-QUAND-04] -->

> **comment** — how

You already say it. **Comment allez-vous ?** is *how are you*, and you learned it
before you knew it contained a question word.

> **Comment tu dis ça ?** — How do you say that?
> **Comment ?** — on its own: *Sorry?* / *Pardon?*

That last one is worth having. Used alone it is how a French speaker asks you to
repeat — politer than *quoi ?*, which is the blunt *what?*

The origin is Latin **quomodo**, "in what manner", and English took the same idea
by a different road: **mode**, **modal** and **manner** are all from *modus*, the
"how" half of it.
