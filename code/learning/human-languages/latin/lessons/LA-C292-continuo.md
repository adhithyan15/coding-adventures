## You'll want to know: continuō
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C292-REPENTE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**continuō** — "straightaway, without a pause".

> Puella epistulam lēgit et continuō flēvit.
>
> *The girl read the letter and straightaway burst into tears.*

The adverb of *continuus*, 'unbroken': nothing comes between. Do not confuse the verb *continuō, continuāre*, 'to join in a row'.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**continuō** is the ablative of *continuus*, "unbroken", which is built on *contineō*, "to hold together" (EtymDB). English *continue* comes from the same family.
