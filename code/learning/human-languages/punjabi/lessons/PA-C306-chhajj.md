## You'll want to know: ਛੱਜ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C306-WINNOW306-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ਛੱਜ** — *chhajj* — "a winnowing basket".

> ਮਾਤਾ ਜੀ ਛੱਜ ਨਾਲ ਦਾਣੇ ਸਾਫ਼ ਕਰਦੇ ਹਨ।
>
> *mātā jī chhajj nāl dāṇe sāf karde han.*
>
> Mother cleans the grain with a winnowing basket.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਛੱਜ** (*chhajj*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*chajja*, "basket"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *chajj*, "winnowing basket", under it (CDIAL 4964).
