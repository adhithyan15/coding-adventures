Punjabi, chapter 82: Full, Empty, Clean, Sweet, Fat.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ਭਰਿਆ (bhariā) — full.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for high, then the Punjabi for low.

You'll want to know: ਭਰਿਆ.
ਭਰਿਆ — bhariā — "full".

The word, taken apart.
ਭਰਿਆ (bhariā) is the past participle of bharnā, "to fill", inherited from Sanskrit bharati, "bears, brings; fills". CDIAL gives no Punjabi form, but lists the matching Lahnda bharaṇ, "to fill" (CDIAL 9397).

The word, taken apart.
ਖ਼ਾਲੀ (khālī) is a loan from Arabic khālī, "empty", which reached Punjabi through Persian. Its Arabic root is kh-l-w (Lane).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: ਮਿੱਠਾ.
ਮਿੱਠਾ — miṭṭhā — "sweet".

The word, taken apart.
ਮਿੱਠਾ (miṭṭhā) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from miṣṭa, "dainty, sweet" (CDIAL 10299).
