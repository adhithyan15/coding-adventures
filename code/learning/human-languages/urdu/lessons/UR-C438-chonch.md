## You'll want to know: چونچ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C438-CHONCH438-01]; assesses=[] -->

**چونچ** — *choṅch* — "a beak".

> پرندے کی چونچ میں ایک تنکا ہے۔
>
> *parinde kī choṅch meṅ ek tinkā hai.*
>
> The bird has a straw in its beak.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**چونچ** (*choṅch*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *cõc*, "a beak", under Sanskrit *cañcu* (CDIAL 4569).
