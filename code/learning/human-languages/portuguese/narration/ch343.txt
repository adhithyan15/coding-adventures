Portuguese, chapter 343: Explico-me melhor.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
o equívoco — the misunderstanding, the mistake.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for credible, then the Portuguese for to claim.

You'll want to know: o equívoco.
o equívoco — "the misunderstanding, the mistake".
Houve um equívoco: eu disse terça, não quinta. There was a misunderstanding: I said Tuesday, not Thursday.
Por equívoco — by mistake. As an adjective, ambiguous: uma frase equívoca.

The word, taken apart.
equívoco is a learned borrowing from Late Latin aequivocus "of like significations, ambiguous" (Lewis & Short), built on aequus "equal" and vocāre "to call": one name that calls two things.

Lesson 2 of 5.
esclarecer — to clarify, to clear up.

You'll want to know: esclarecer.
esclarecer — "to clarify, to clear up".
Quero esclarecer uma coisa antes de começar. I want to clarify one thing before I start.
Esclarecer uma dúvida — to clear up a doubt.

The word, taken apart.
esclarecer is built in Portuguese from claro "clear", inherited from Latin clārus "bright, clear" (Lewis & Short), with es- and the verb ending -ecer.

Lesson 4 of 5.
sublinhar — to stress, to emphasise; to underline.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for to clarify, then the Portuguese for the clarification.

You'll want to know: sublinhar.
sublinhar — "to stress, to emphasise; to underline".
A médica sublinhou que é preciso descansar. The doctor stressed that rest is needed.
Literally to draw a line under a word; figuratively to stress a point.

The word, taken apart.
sublinhar is built from sub- "under" and linha "line", which is inherited from Latin līnea "a linen thread, a string, a line" (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 5 of 5.
salientar — to point out, to highlight.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for the clarification, then the Portuguese for to stress.

You'll want to know: salientar.
salientar — "to point out, to highlight".
Ela salientou que o hotel fica perto da praia. She pointed out that the hotel is near the beach.
Close to sublinhar: é de salientar que… — it is worth pointing out that…

The word, taken apart.
salientar is built on saliente "prominent" (EtymDB), from Latin saliēns, the present participle of salīre "to leap, to jump" (Lewis & Short): what jumps out at you.
