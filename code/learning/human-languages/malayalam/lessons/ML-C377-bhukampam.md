## You'll want to know: ഭൂകമ്പം
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C377-BHUKAMPAM377-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ഭൂകമ്പം** — *bhūkampaṁ* — "an earthquake".

> ഭൂകമ്പത്തിൽ നഗരത്തിലെ ഒരുപാട് കെട്ടിടങ്ങൾ തകർന്നു.
>
> *bhūkampattil nagarattile orupāṭŭ keṭṭiṭaṅṅaḷ takarnnu.*
>
> Many buildings in the city collapsed in the earthquake.

ഭൂ, the earth, + കമ്പം, trembling.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ഭൂകമ്പം** (*bhūkampaṁ*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *bhūkampa*, "an earthquake" (CDIAL 9547), made of *bhū*, "the earth", and *kampa*, "trembling".
