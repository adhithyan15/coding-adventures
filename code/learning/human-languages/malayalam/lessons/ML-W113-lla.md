# ള (ḷa) — the letter inside നാളെ

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ML-SCRIPT-C113-SHA-01, ML-SCRIPT-C113-NGA-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: write ശ and ങ once each, and name them.

[PAUSE 2s] Now say **നാളെ** (*nāḷe*), "tomorrow".
You have read it for a long time. This lesson takes one letter out of it and puts
it in your hand.

## Script you'll notice: ള
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-SCRIPT-C113-LLA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ള** — *ḷa*, an *l* made with the tongue curled back.

It is inside **നാളെ** (*nāḷe*), "tomorrow", from **നാളെ കാണാം**, "see you
tomorrow". The dot under the romanization (*ḷ*) is what marks the curled tongue.

## Writing: ള
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ML-SCRIPT-C113-LLA-01] -->

Put your pen on ള and follow its line. Copy the shape you can see — slowly,
and larger than it is printed.

> This book does not yet tell you **where to start this letter or which way to
> travel**. It is not written down here until it can be written down with a
> source. Copying what is in front of you needs no such source, and it is how the
> shape gets into your hand in the meantime.
