## You'll want to know: cavea, caveae
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C546-CAVE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**cavea, caveae** — "the seats of a theatre; a cage".

> Tōta cavea plaudit.
>
> *The whole audience applauds.*

Feminine, first declension, from *cavus* — hollow. The hollow bowl of seats, and so the audience; also a cage or den. English *cage*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**cavea** is built on *cavus* "hollow" (Lewis & Short). English *cage* comes from it through Old French.
