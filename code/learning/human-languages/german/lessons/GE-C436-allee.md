## You'll want to know: die Allee
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C436-GASSE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**die Allee** — "the avenue (lined with trees)".

> Vom Schloss führt eine lange Allee zum Dorf.
>
> *A long avenue leads from the palace to the village.*

Plural: *die Alleen*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Allee* was borrowed from French *allée* 'path, walk' (EtymDB), from *aller* 'to go'.
