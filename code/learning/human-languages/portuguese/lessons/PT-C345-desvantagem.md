## You'll want to know: a desvantagem
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C345-VANTAGEM-02]; assesses=[] -->

**a desvantagem** — "the disadvantage".

> A desvantagem é que fica longe do centro.
>
> *The disadvantage is that it is far from the centre.*

*Estar em desvantagem* — to be at a disadvantage.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*desvantagem* is built from *des-* and *vantagem* "advantage", which is usually traced to Old French *avantage*, on *avant*, from Late Latin *abante* "before", built on *ante* (EtymDB).
