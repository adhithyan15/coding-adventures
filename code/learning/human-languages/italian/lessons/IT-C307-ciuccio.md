## You'll want to know: il ciuccio
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C307-NASCITA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**il ciuccio** — "the dummy, the pacifier".

> Il bambino non dorme senza ciuccio.
>
> *The baby will not sleep without his dummy.*

Informal and very common; in shops also *il succhiotto*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ciuccio* is built on *ciucciare* "to suck", which is usually explained as a word that imitates the sound of sucking (EtymDB has no entry).
