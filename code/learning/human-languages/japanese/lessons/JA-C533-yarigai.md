## You'll want to know: やりがい
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C533-WORTH533-01]; assesses=[] -->

**やりがい** — *yarigai* — "a sense of reward; satisfaction (in a task)".

> **やりがいは しごとに よります。** — *yarigai wa shigoto ni yorimasu* — "How rewarding it is depends on the job."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**やりがい** (*yarigai*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, and usually written in kana; its older origin is not settled (JMdict).
