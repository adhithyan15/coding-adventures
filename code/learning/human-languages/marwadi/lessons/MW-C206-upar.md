## You'll want to know: ऊपर
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C206-LANE206-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ऊपर** — *ūpar* — "up; upstairs".

> दफ्तर ऊपर है।
>
> *daphtar ūpar hai.*
>
> The office is upstairs.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ऊपर** (*ūpar*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*uppari*, "above"; CDIAL lists Old Marwari *ūpara*, "on" (CDIAL 2333).
