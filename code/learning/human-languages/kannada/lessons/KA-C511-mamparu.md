# ಮಂಪರು (mamparu) — drowsiness; grogginess

## You'll want to know: ಮಂಪರು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C511-SONKU511-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ಮಂಪರು** — *mamparu* — "drowsiness; grogginess".

> ಮಾತ್ರೆ ತೆಗೆದುಕೊಂಡ ಮೇಲೆ ನನಗೆ ಮಂಪರು ಬಂದಿತು.
>
> *mātre tegedukoṇḍa mēle nanage mamparu banditu.*
>
> After taking the tablet I felt groggy.

The haze of half-sleep, after medicine or a long night.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಮಂಪರು** (*mamparu*) is an everyday Kannada word for a drowsy haze; its origin is not settled.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
