# এখানে (ekhāne) — here

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[BN-SCRIPT-THA-01, BN-LEX-C21-EI-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Write **থ**. Then say **this shirt**.

## You'll want to know: এখানে
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C26-EKHANE-01]; assesses=[BN-LEX-C21-EI-01, BN-LEX-C10-CHA-01] -->

> **চা এখানে** — *chā ekhāne* — **the tea is here.**

> **সে এখানে** — *she ekhāne* — **he is here.**

Thirty-six chapters, and nothing in this book has had a **place**. Things could
be named, pointed at, counted, owned and acted upon, and not one of them could
be said to be anywhere.

**এখানে** takes the near head **এ-** you have owned since chapter thirty-two and
puts it on a frame that means *place*. And as with **এটা জল**, there is no verb
in the middle: the thing, then where it is, and stop.

## Grammar Lens: the grid, now in three dimensions
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[BN-LEX-C26-EKHANE-01, BN-LEX-C21-EI-01, BN-LEX-C21-OI-01, BN-GRAMMAR-C16-NEI-01] -->

| head | frame | word |
|---|---|---|
| **এ-** near | thing | **এটা** |
| **এ-** near | person | **এ** |
| **এ-** near | place | **এখানে** |
| **ও-** far | thing | **ওটা** |
| **ও-** far | person | **ও** |
| **ও-** far | place | *next lesson* |
| **ক-** asking | thing | **কী** |
| **ক-** asking | person | **কে** |
| **ক-** asking | place | *the lesson after* |

Nine cells, three heads, three frames. You have met seven of the nine, and the
last two arrive in the next two lessons — which is the whole reason the pointing
chapter was worth its five lessons.

And the denial is already yours: **চা এখানে নেই** — *the tea is not here* —
using the **নেই** of chapter twenty-seven, which denies existence rather than
identity.
