## You'll want to know: mūnus, mūneris
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C336-GLADIATOR-03]; assesses=[] -->

**mūnus, mūneris** — "a public show; a gift; a duty".

> Ad mūnus tōta urbs vēnit.
>
> *The whole city came to the show.*

Neuter, 3rd declension. First a duty, then a gift, and so the gladiator show given to the people. English *munificent*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**mūnus** is inherited from Proto-Italic *\*moinos*, "service" (EtymDB): a duty, then a gift, then a show. English *munificent* comes from its family.
