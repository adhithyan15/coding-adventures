## You'll want to know: साबास
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C275-SABAS275-02]; assesses=[] -->

**साबास** — *sābās* — "well done".

> साबास, छोरी!
>
> *sābās, chhorī!*
>
> Well done, girl!

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**साबास** (*sābās*) is usually traced to Persian *shābāsh*, "bravo"; it is said on its own, as praise.
