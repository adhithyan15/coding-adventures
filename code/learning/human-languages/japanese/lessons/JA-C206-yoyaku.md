## You'll want to know: よやく

**よやく** — *yoyaku* — "a booking; a reservation".

> **よやくは ひつようですか。** — *yoyaku wa hitsuyō desu ka* — "Is a booking necessary?"

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**よやく** (*yoyaku*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *yo*, "beforehand" (Middle Chinese *yoH*) and *yaku*, "to bind, promise" (*'jak*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
