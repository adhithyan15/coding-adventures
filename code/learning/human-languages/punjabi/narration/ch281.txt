Punjabi, chapter 281: Plans for the Holidays.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ਪ੍ਰੋਗਰਾਮ (progrām) — a plan; a programme.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for to take out, then the Punjabi for perhaps.

You'll want to know: ਪ੍ਰੋਗਰਾਮ.
ਪ੍ਰੋਗਰਾਮ — progrām — "a plan; a programme".
ਛੁੱਟੀ ਦਾ ਪ੍ਰੋਗਰਾਮ ਕੀ ਹੈ? chhuṭṭī dā progrām kī hai? What is the plan for the holiday?
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ਪ੍ਰੋਗਰਾਮ (progrām) is borrowed from English programme.

Lesson 2 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: ਇਰਾਦਾ.
ਇਰਾਦਾ — irādā — "an intention".
ਮੇਰਾ ਇਰਾਦਾ ਪੱਕਾ ਹੈ। merā irādā pakkā hai. My intention is firm.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ਇਰਾਦਾ (irādā) is a loan, usually traced through Persian to Arabic irāda, "will, wish".

Lesson 4 of 5.
ਟਾਲਣਾ (ṭālṇā) — to put off; to postpone.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for an intention, then the Punjabi for advice.

You'll want to know: ਟਾਲਣਾ.
ਟਾਲਣਾ — ṭālṇā — "to put off; to postpone".
ਅਮਨ ਹਰ ਕੰਮ ਕੱਲ੍ਹ ਉੱਤੇ ਟਾਲਦਾ ਹੈ। Aman har kamm kall utte ṭāldā hai. Aman puts every job off until tomorrow.
Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਯਾਤਰਾ ਟਾਲੀ, maiṁ yātrā ṭālī.

The word, taken apart.
ਟਾਲਣਾ (ṭālṇā) is inherited from Sanskrit ṭalati, "moves aside", through its causative ṭālayati; CDIAL lists Punjabi ṭālṇā, "to evade", under it (CDIAL 5450).

Lesson 5 of 5.
ਤੁਰੰਤ (turant) — at once; immediately.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for advice, then the Punjabi for to put off.

You'll want to know: ਤੁਰੰਤ.
ਤੁਰੰਤ — turant — "at once; immediately".
ਮੈਂ ਤੁਰੰਤ ਘਰ ਜਾਵਾਂਗਾ। maiṁ turant ghar jāvāṅgā. I will go home at once.

The word, taken apart.
ਤੁਰੰਤ (turant) goes back to Sanskrit turant, "hastening", as a word taken from Hindi: CDIAL lists Punjabi turant, "quickly", under it and marks it as borrowed from Hindi (CDIAL 5879).
