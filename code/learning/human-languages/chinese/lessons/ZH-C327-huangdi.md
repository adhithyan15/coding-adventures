## You'll want to know: huángdì
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C327-HERO327-03]; assesses=[] -->

*huángdì* — "emperor".

> *tā shí suì de shíhou dāng le huángdì* — He became emperor when he was ten.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*huángdì* is built from *huáng* and *dì*, both inherited from Middle Chinese, *hwang* and *tejH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'a sovereign' and 'great', and calls the second the title of one who rules all under heaven.
