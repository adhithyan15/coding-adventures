## You'll want to know: der Nachwuchs
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C491-PATEN-05]; assesses=[] -->

**der Nachwuchs** — "offspring, new arrival; young talent".

> Unsere Nachbarn haben Nachwuchs bekommen.
>
> *Our neighbours have had a baby.*

No plural. Also young talent: *der Nachwuchs im Fußball*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Nachwuchs* is built on *nach* "after" and *Wuchs* "growth", from *wachsen* "to grow", inherited from Old High German *wahsan*, from Proto-Germanic *wahsijaną* (EtymDB).
