## You'll want to know: संगच्छते
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C501-SAMBANDHA501-03]; assesses=[] -->

**संगच्छते** — *saṅgacchate* — "gets along".

> अरुणः भ्रात्रा सह सम्यक् संगच्छते।
>
> *aruṇaḥ bhrātrā saha samyak saṅgacchate.*
>
> Arun gets along well with his brother.

Past (he …): सः भ्रात्रा सह संगतवान्, *saḥ bhrātrā saha saṅgatavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**संगच्छते** (*saṅgacchate*) is built from *sam*, "together", and the root √gam, "to go", the root of **गच्छति**: to go together; MW's ppp *saṃgata* is "met, united, allied with" (MW).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
