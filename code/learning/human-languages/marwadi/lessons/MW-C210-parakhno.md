# परखणो (parakhṇo) — to test; to check

## You'll want to know: परखणो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C210-CHOOSE210-01]; assesses=[] -->

**परखणो** — *parakhṇo* — "to test; to check".

> मां कपड़ा परखै है।
>
> *mā̃ kapṛā parkhai hai.*
>
> Mother checks the cloth.

Past (I …): म्हैं कपड़ा परख्या, *mhaĩ kapṛā parakhyā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**परखणो** (*parakhṇo*) is inherited from Sanskrit *parīkṣate*, "observes, examines"; CDIAL lists an Old Marwari form beside Hindi *parakhnā*, "to test" (CDIAL 7904).
