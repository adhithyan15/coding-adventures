## You'll want to know: उत्तेजयति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C311-KIMVADANTI311-03]; assesses=[] -->

**उत्तेजयति** — *uttejayati* — "stirs up; excites".

> धूर्तः जनान् उत्तेजयति।
>
> *dhūrtaḥ janān uttejayati.*
>
> The rogue stirs up the people.

Past (he …): सः जनान् उत्तेजितवान्, *saḥ janān uttejitavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**उत्तेजयति** (*uttejayati*) is built from *ud*, "up", and the causative of the root √tij, "to be sharp"; MW gives *ut-tejayati* as to excite, stimulate, incite (MW).
