Spanish, chapter 739: Curarse.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
curar — to cure, to heal.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for the treatment, then the Spanish for to suffer from.

You'll want to know: curar.
curar — "to cure, to heal".
El tiempo lo cura todo. Time heals everything.
Curarse — to get better.

The word, taken apart.
curar is inherited from Latin cūrāre "to take care of" (EtymDB), built on cūra "care".

Lesson 5 of 5.
la cirugía — surgery.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for recovery, then the Spanish for to operate.

You'll want to know: la cirugía.
la cirugía — "surgery".
Necesita cirugía urgente. He needs urgent surgery.
Cirugía estética — cosmetic surgery. The surgeon is el cirujano.

The word, taken apart.
cirugía is usually traced to Latin chīrurgia, borrowed from Greek kheirourgía "handwork", built on kheír "hand" and érgon "work".
