## You'll want to know: alegrarse
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C487-ANIMO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**alegrarse** — "to be glad".

> Me alegro de verte.
>
> *I am glad to see you.*

It goes with *de*: *alegrarse de algo*.
