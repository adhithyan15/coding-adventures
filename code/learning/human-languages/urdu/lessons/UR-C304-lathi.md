## You'll want to know: لاٹھی
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C304-FASAD304-04]; assesses=[] -->

**لاٹھی** — *lāṭhī* — "a stick; a police baton".

> داروغے کے ہاتھ میں لاٹھی تھی۔
>
> *dāroghe ke hāth meṅ lāṭhī thī.*
>
> The inspector had a baton in his hand.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**لاٹھی** (*lāṭhī*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *lāṭhī*, "a staff, a club", under a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 10991). It is the long bamboo stick of the police.
