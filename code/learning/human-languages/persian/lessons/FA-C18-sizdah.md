# سیزده — thirteen, and how far a unit can bend

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[FA-LEX-YAZDAH, FA-GRAMMAR-TEENS-DAH] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say **yâzdah**, split it in two, and build fourteen.

## You'll want to know first — one word
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-SIZDAH]; assesses=[FA-LEX-SE, FA-LEX-DAH, FA-GRAMMAR-TEENS-DAH] -->

> **سیزده** — *sizdah* — **thirteen**

*si* + **dah**. The unit is *se*, with its vowel lengthened and
darkened — and English does the same thing without anyone noticing: *three*
becomes *thir*- in **thirteen**, and *five* becomes *fif*- in **fifteen**.

Listen past the bending, then, and hear what does not bend: **the ten stays the
same**, on the end, every time.
