French, chapter 54: Hospital, Illness, Thirst, Tiredness, Ill.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
l'hôpital — a hospital.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for a medicine, then the French for a doctor.

You'll want to know: l'hôpital.
l'hôpital — "a hospital".

The word, taken apart.
hôpital from Old French hospital from Medieval Latin hospitale, "a guest house", from Latin hospes, "guest" — a learned borrowing. (EtymDB 2.1) The circumflex marks the s that French later stopped writing. The same Latin word, worn down in everyday speech, also gave French its word for "hotel".

[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for a doctor, then the French for a hospital.

You'll want to know: la maladie.
la maladie — "an illness".

The word, taken apart.
maladie — built in Old French from the adjective for "sick", with the ending -ie. That adjective goes back to Latin male habitus, "in poor condition". (EtymDB 2.1).
