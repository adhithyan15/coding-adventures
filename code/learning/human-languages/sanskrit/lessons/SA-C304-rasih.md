## You'll want to know: राशिः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C304-RASHI304-01]; assesses=[] -->

**राशिः** — *rāśiḥ* — "a heap; a pile".

> भग्नायाः भित्तेः राशिः मार्गे अस्ति।
>
> *bhagnāyāḥ bhitteḥ rāśiḥ mārge asti.*
>
> A heap from the broken wall lies in the road.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**राशिः** (*rāśiḥ*) MW gives *rāśi* as a heap, mass or pile and calls its derivation doubtful, so its origin is uncertain (MW).
