## You'll want to know: विभागः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C356-VIJNAPANA356-04]; assesses=[] -->

**विभागः** — *vibhāgaḥ* — "a department; a division".

> कार्यालये पञ्च विभागाः सन्ति।
>
> *kāryālaye pañca vibhāgāḥ santi.*
>
> The office has five departments.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**विभागः** (*vibhāgaḥ*) is built on *vi*, "apart", and the root √bhaj, "to divide, to share"; MW gives *vi-bhāga* "a share, a section, a division", and "a department" is its modern sense (MW).
