# ಜಮಖಾನ (jamakhāna) — a rug; a woven floor carpet

## You'll want to know: ಜಮಖಾನ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C545-BATTE545-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ಜಮಖಾನ** — *jamakhāna* — "a rug; a woven floor carpet".

> ಹಬ್ಬಕ್ಕೆ ನೆಲದ ಮೇಲೆ ಜಮಖಾನ ಹಾಸಿದರು.
>
> *habbakke nelada mēle jamakhāna hāsidaru.*
>
> They spread a rug on the floor for the festival.

Thick and woven, spread for guests to sit on.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಜಮಖಾನ** (*jamakhāna*) is usually traced to a Persian word that reached Kannada through Hindustani; the exact source is not settled.
