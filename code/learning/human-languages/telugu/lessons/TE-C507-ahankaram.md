## You'll want to know: అహంకారం
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C507-SVABHAVAM507-05]; assesses=[] -->

**అహంకారం** — *ahaṅkāraṁ* — "arrogance; ego".

> డబ్బు వచ్చాక అతనికి అహంకారం పెరిగింది.
>
> *ḍabbu vaccāka ataniki ahaṅkāraṁ perigindi.*
>
> Once he had money, his arrogance grew.

The opposite of వినయం. అహం, I, + కారం, making.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**అహంకారం** (*ahaṅkāraṁ*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *ahaṁkāra*, "self-consciousness, egotism, pride" (CDIAL 991), from *aham*, "I", and *kāra*, "making".
