## You'll want to know: a cidadania
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C363-CIDADAO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**a cidadania** — "the citizenship".

> Ela pediu a cidadania portuguesa.
>
> *She applied for Portuguese citizenship.*

*Cidadania* is also a school subject about civic life.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*cidadania* is built on *cidadão* "citizen", on *cidade* "city", which comes from Latin *cīvitās* "citizenship, city", on *cīvis* "citizen" (Lewis & Short).
