Punjabi, chapter 98: Holiday, Love, Music, Way, Country.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
ਛੁੱਟੀ (chhuṭṭī) — a holiday, time off.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for a song, then the Punjabi for a journey.

You'll want to know: ਛੁੱਟੀ.
ਛੁੱਟੀ — chhuṭṭī — "a holiday, time off".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: chhuṭṭī]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: chhuṭṭī, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say chhuṭṭī]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Punjabi for a song, then the Punjabi for a journey, then say chhuṭṭī again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does ਛੁੱਟੀ (chhuṭṭī) mean? ("A holiday, time off".) Say it once more.
