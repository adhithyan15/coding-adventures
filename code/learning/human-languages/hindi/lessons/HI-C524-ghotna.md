## You'll want to know: घोंटना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C524-PAROSNA524-03]; assesses=[] -->

**घोंटना** — *ghõṭnā* — "to grind into a paste; to rub smooth".

> माँ पत्थर पर चटनी घोंट रही है।
>
> *Māṁ patthar par caṭnī ghõṭ rahī hai.*
>
> Mother is grinding chutney on a stone.

Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने मसाला घोंटा, *usne masālā ghõṭā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**घोंटना** (*ghõṭnā*) is a tadbhava, inherited from Sanskrit *ghaṭṭayati*, "rubs" (CDIAL 4417). CDIAL gives the Hindi verb as "to rub".
