German, chapter 177: To apply, To bend, To fry, To spell, To print.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
bewerben — to apply (sich bewerben).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to describe, then the German for to enter.

You'll want to know: bewerben.
bewerben — "to apply (sich bewerben)".

The word, taken apart.
bewerben is be- + the verb "to recruit, to woo", Old High German werban from Proto-Germanic hwerbaną, "to turn" (EtymDB 2.1). To apply is to court a post.

Lesson 4 of 5.
buchstabieren — to spell.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to bend, then the German for to fry.

You'll want to know: buchstabieren.
buchstabieren — "to spell".

The word, taken apart.
buchstabieren is built on the word for "a letter of the alphabet", literally "beech staff", + the ending -ieren. The beech staff is traditionally explained by the beech staves runes were carved on, though this is disputed.
