## You'll want to know: घटनास्थल
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C313-PATRAKAR313-02]; assesses=[] -->

**घटनास्थल** — *ghaṭnāsthal* — "the scene (of an incident)".

> घटनास्थल के लिए पुल के बाद बाएँ मुड़िए।
>
> *ghaṭnāsthal ke liye pul ke bād bāẽ muṛiye.*
>
> For the scene of the accident, turn left after the bridge.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**घटनास्थल** (*ghaṭnāsthal*) joins **घटना**, an event, with Sanskrit *sthala*, "a place" (Monier-Williams): the place where it happened. It is the word of news reports.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
