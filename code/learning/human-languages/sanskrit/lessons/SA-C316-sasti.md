## You'll want to know: शास्ति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C316-SAMRAT316-05]; assesses=[] -->

**शास्ति** — *śāsti* — "rules; governs".

> सम्राट् धर्मेण राज्यं शास्ति।
>
> *samrāṭ dharmeṇa rājyaṃ śāsti.*
>
> The emperor rules the kingdom justly.

Past (he …): सः राज्यं शासितवान्, *saḥ rājyaṃ śāsitavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**शास्ति** (*śāsti*) comes from the root √śās, "to chastise, correct", which MW also gives as to restrain, rule, govern; MW gives *śāsita* as governed, ruled (MW).
