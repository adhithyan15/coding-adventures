Urdu, chapter 510: Peel, Pound, Squeeze.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
چھیلنا (chhīlnā) — to peel.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for an earthen cooking pot, then the Urdu for to spill.

You'll want to know: چھیلنا.
چھیلنا — chhīlnā — "to peel".
میں نے چھری سے آلو چھیلے۔ maiṅ ne chhurī se ālū chhīle. I peeled the potatoes with a knife.
Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے سیب چھیلا, us ne seb chhīlā.

The word, taken apart.
چھیلنا (chhīlnā) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin chīlnā, "to peel", under chilla, a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 5051).

Lesson 4 of 5.
پسوانا (pisvānā) — to have (something) ground.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for to pound, then the Urdu for a mortar (for pounding).

You'll want to know: پسوانا.
پسوانا — pisvānā — "to have (something) ground".
امی نے گندم کا آٹا پسوایا۔ ammī ne gandum kā āṭā pisvāyā. Mum had the wheat ground into flour.
Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے مسالہ پسوایا, us ne masālā pisvāyā.

The word, taken apart.
پسوانا (pisvānā) is the second causative of pīsnā, "to grind", which CDIAL lists under Sanskrit piṃṣati, "grinds" (CDIAL 8142): to have someone grind.

Lesson 5 of 5.
نچوڑنا (nichoṛnā) — to squeeze out (juice or water).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a mortar (for pounding), then the Urdu for to have (something) ground.

You'll want to know: نچوڑنا.
نچوڑنا — nichoṛnā — "to squeeze out (juice or water)".
اس نے سلاد پر لیموں نچوڑا۔ us ne salād par lemū̃ nichoṛā. She squeezed a lemon over the salad.
Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے تولیہ نچوڑا, us ne tauliyā nichoṛā.

نچوڑنا (nichoṛnā) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin nicoṛnā, "to squeeze out", under niscōṭayati, a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 7449).
