Russian, chapter 470: Shame and Embarrassment.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
стыд (styd) — shame.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for envy, then the Russian for to envy.

You'll want to know: стыд.
стыд (styd) — "shame".
От стыда он не мог сказать ни слова. Ot stydá on ne mog skazát ni slóva. He couldn't say a word for shame.
Masculine; genitive стыда. Мне стыдно — I feel ashamed.

The word, taken apart.
стыд (styd) is inherited from Proto-Slavic stydъ (EtymDB); it is usually linked to the root of стыть 'to grow cold'.

Lesson 2 of 5.
стыдиться (stydítsya) — to be ashamed.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to envy, then the Russian for shame.

You'll want to know: стыдиться.
стыдиться (stydítsya) — "to be ashamed".
Он стыдится своей ошибки. On stydítsya svoyéy oshíbki. He is ashamed of his mistake.
Imperfective; present стыжусь, стыдишься (д becomes ж). Takes the genitive: стыдиться (stydítsya) ошибки. Perfective постыдиться.

The word, taken apart.
стыдиться (stydítsya) is built on стыд (styd) 'shame' with the reflexive -ся; стыд (styd) is inherited from Proto-Slavic stydъ (EtymDB).

Lesson 3 of 5.
смущаться (smushchátsya) — to get embarrassed.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for shame, then the Russian for to be ashamed.

You'll want to know: смущаться.
смущаться (smushchátsya) — "to get embarrassed".
Мальчик смущается, когда его хвалят. Málchik smushcháyetsya, kogdá yegó khvályat. The boy gets embarrassed when people praise him.
Imperfective; perfective смутиться (смущусь, смутишься). The noun is смущение — embarrassment.

The word, taken apart.
смущаться (smushchátsya) is the reflexive of смущать, the imperfective of смутить 'to trouble, to confuse'; EtymDB lists these verbs without a source, and they are usually traced to the root of мутный 'cloudy'.

Lesson 4 of 5.
краснеть (krasnét) — to blush.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to be ashamed, then the Russian for to get embarrassed.

You'll want to know: краснеть.
краснеть (krasnét) — "to blush".
Она краснеет, когда говорит с ним. Oná krasnéyet, kogdá govorít s nim. She blushes when she talks to him.
Imperfective; present краснею, краснеешь. Perfective покраснеть. Also to turn red.

The word, taken apart.
краснеть (krasnét) is built on красный 'red', whose older sense 'beautiful' lives on in красивый; EtymDB lists the verb without a source, and the root is usually traced to Proto-Slavic krasa 'beauty'.

Lesson 5 of 5.
застенчивый (zasténchivyy) — shy.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to get embarrassed, then the Russian for to blush.

You'll want to know: застенчивый.
застенчивый (zasténchivyy) — "shy".
Застенчивый студент редко задаёт вопросы. Zasténchivyy studént rédko zadayót voprósy. The shy student rarely asks questions.
Shy by nature. Short forms: застенчив, застенчива.

The word, taken apart.
застенчивый (zasténchivyy) is usually traced to за стеной 'behind a wall', a person who keeps out of sight; EtymDB lists the word without a source, so the link is not settled.
