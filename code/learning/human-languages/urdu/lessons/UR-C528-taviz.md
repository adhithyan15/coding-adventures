## You'll want to know: تعویذ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C528-BARKAT528-03]; assesses=[] -->

**تعویذ** — *tāvīz* — "an amulet".

> اگر دادی ہوتیں تو بچے کو تعویذ دیتیں۔
>
> *agar dādī hotīṅ to bachche ko tāvīz detīṅ.*
>
> If grandmother were here, she would give the child an amulet.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**تعویذ** (*tāvīz*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *taʿwīdh*, "seeking protection".
