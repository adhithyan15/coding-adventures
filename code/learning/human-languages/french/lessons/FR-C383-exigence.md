## You'll want to know: l'exigence
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C383-CRITERE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**l'exigence** — "the requirement, the demand".

> Ce poste a beaucoup d'exigences.
>
> *This job has a lot of requirements.*

Feminine. *Être exigeant* — to be demanding.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*exigence* is usually traced to Late Latin *exigentia* "urgency, need", from *exigere* "to drive out, to demand" (Lewis & Short), built on *agere* "to drive".
