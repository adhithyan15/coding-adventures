## You'll want to know: اعتراض
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C366-BAHS-04]; assesses=[] -->

**اعتراض** (*e'terâz*) — "an objection, a protest".

> اعتراض او فایده نداشت.
>
> *e'terâz-e u fâyede nadâsht.*
>
> His objection was no use.

Plural *e'terâzât*. *Be chizi e'terâz kardan* — to object to something.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**اعتراض** was borrowed from Arabic *i'tirāḍ* "objection" (EtymDB), usually explained as standing across someone's way.
