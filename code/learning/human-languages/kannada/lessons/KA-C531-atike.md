## You'll want to know: ಆಟಿಕೆ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C531-ATIKE531-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ಆಟಿಕೆ** — *āṭike* — "a toy".

> ಅಜ್ಜ ಮೊಮ್ಮಗನಿಗೆ ಮರದ ಆಟಿಕೆ ತಂದರು.
>
> *ajja mommaganige marada āṭike tandaru.*
>
> Grandfather brought his grandson a wooden toy.

Plural: ಆಟಿಕೆಗಳು, *āṭikegaḷu*.

Built on ಆಟ, play.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಆಟಿಕೆ** (*āṭike*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 347 lists Kannada ಆಡು, "to move, to play", with the nouns *āṭa* and *āṭike*, "motion, play"; a toy is the thing you play with.
