## You'll want to know: तालु
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C472-TALU472-01]; assesses=[] -->

**तालु** — *tālu* — "the palate".

> जिह्वा तालु स्पृशति।
>
> *jihvā tālu spṛśati.*
>
> The tongue touches the palate.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**तालु** (*tālu*) MW gives "the palate", from the Vājasaneyi Saṃhitā on; MW gives no derivation, so its origin is uncertain (MW).
