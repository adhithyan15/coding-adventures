German, chapter 121: Beans, Cucumber, Strawberry, Cherry, Melon.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
die Bohnen — beans.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for vegetables, then the German for a mushroom.
[pause 2 seconds]
Beans come in the plural: say die Bohnen with its plural die, and then die Jahre.

You'll want to know: die Bohnen.
die Bohnen — "beans".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: die Bohnen]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: die Bohnen, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say die Bohnen]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the German for vegetables, then the German for a mushroom, then say die Bohnen again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does die Bohnen mean? ("Beans".) Say it once more.
