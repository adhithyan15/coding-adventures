# (reading) — the doorway words, on the page

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[JA-SKILL-READ-WORDS] -->

[PAUSE 2s] The last lesson gave you six words. These are the six the book
opened with.

[PAUSE 1s] Every one of them reached your ear before it reached a page. Today
that order is reversed.

## Reading
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-SKILL-READ-GREETINGS]; assesses=[JA-LEX-KONNICHIWA, JA-LEX-ARIGATOU] -->

> はい
> いいえ
> こんにちは
> ありがとう
> さようなら
> すみません

[PAUSE 3s] Read down once, without stopping.

[PAUSE 3s] Again — and notice that the two longest are the two you say most
often. Length is not difficulty here; familiarity is.

## You'll want to know
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[JA-LEX-HAI, JA-LEX-IIE, JA-LEX-SUMIMASEN] -->

[PAUSE 2s] **こんにちは** ends in **は**, and you know that sign says *ha*. In
this word it is read *wa*, because the greeting keeps a spelling from an older
sentence that nobody finishes any more.

[PAUSE 2s] You do not need the history to read it. You need to know that this
one word is spelled the way it is for a reason, and that reading a language
means meeting a handful of such words rather than a rule you can apply.

[PAUSE 2s] Six lines, and one of them is a small piece of history.
