# મુદ્દો (muddo) — a point at issue

## You'll want to know: મુદ્દો
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C341-DALIL341-03]; assesses=[] -->

**મુદ્દો** — *muddo* — "a point at issue".

> તમારો મુદ્દો સાચો છે.
>
> *tamāro muddo sāco che.*
>
> Your point is right.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**મુદ્દો** (*muddo*) is usually traced to Arabic *muddaʿā*, "a thing claimed", through Persian; in Gujarati it is the point a discussion turns on.
