## You'll want to know: আসন্ন
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C538-DURJOG538-04]; assesses=[] -->

**আসন্ন** — *āsônnô* — "imminent; close at hand".

> আসন্ন ঝড়ের জন্য নৌকাগুলো ঘাটে ফিরল।
>
> *Āsônnô jhôṛer jônyô noukāgulo ghāṭe phirlô.*
>
> Because of the coming storm the boats returned to the landing.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**আসন্ন** (*āsônnô*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *āsanna*, "set down near, close at hand" (Monier-Williams).
