# Review — what was going on

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[JA-GRAMMAR-C253-ONGOING-01, JA-LEX-C253-TOKI-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "It is raining now". (*ima ame ga futte imasu*.) Then "when I got up". (*okita toki*.)

- [YOU SAY: I am waiting for a friend.]
- [YOU SAY: Yesterday it was raining, and the train stopped.]
- [YOU SAY: When I was a child, I went to the river.]
- [YOU SAY: It is cold here all year round.]
- [YOU SAY: My father was reading the newspaper.]
- [YOU SAY: I brush my teeth before I go to bed.]
