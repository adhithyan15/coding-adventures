## You'll want to know: la connexion
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C398-RESEAU-02]; assesses=[] -->

**la connexion** — "the connection".

> La connexion est très lente ce soir.
>
> *The connection is very slow tonight.*

Spelled with *x*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*connexion* is a learned borrowing from Latin *cōnexiō* "a joining, a connection" (EtymDB), from *cōnectere* "to bind together", built on *nectere* "to tie" (Lewis & Short).
