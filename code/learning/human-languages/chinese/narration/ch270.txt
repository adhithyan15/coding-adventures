Mandarin Chinese, chapter 270: Do You Agree?.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
tóngyì — to agree.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for originally, then the Mandarin for a toy.

You'll want to know: tóngyì.
tóngyì — "to agree".
nǐ tóngyì ma — Do you agree?
Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
tóngyì is built from 同 'the same' and yì 'a mind', both inherited from Middle Chinese, duwng and 'iH (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'will': to be of one mind.

What you've built.
One more word for saying no and asking.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: tóngyì]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: tóngyì, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: nǐ tóngyì ma — Do you agree?]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Mandarin for originally, then the Mandarin for a toy, then say tóngyì again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does tóngyì mean? ("To agree".) Say it once more.
