## You'll want to know: el municipio
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C683-REGION-03]; assesses=[] -->

**el municipio** — "the municipality, the town council".

> El municipio tiene tres pueblos.
>
> *The municipality has three villages.*

Its people and its council: *el ayuntamiento* runs *el municipio*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*municipio* is a learned borrowing from Latin *mūnicipium* "a free town with its own rights" (EtymDB).
