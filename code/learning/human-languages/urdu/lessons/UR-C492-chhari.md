## You'll want to know: چھڑی
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C492-OLDAGE492-03]; assesses=[] -->

**چھڑی** — *chhaṛī* — "a walking stick".

> دادا چھڑی لے کر سیر کو جاتے ہیں۔
>
> *dādā chhaṛī le kar sair ko jāte haiṅ.*
>
> My grandfather takes his stick when he goes for a walk.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**چھڑی** (*chhaṛī*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *chaṛī*, "a stick, a rod", under *chaṭa*, a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 4966).
