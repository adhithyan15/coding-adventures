## You'll want to know: अपरः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C541-DVIDHA541-04]; assesses=[] -->

**अपरः** — *aparaḥ* — "the other one".

> यदि एषः मार्गः कठिनः अस्ति, तर्हि अपरं मार्गं गच्छामः।
>
> *yadi eṣaḥ mārgaḥ kaṭhinaḥ asti, tarhi aparaṃ mārgaṃ gacchāmaḥ.*
>
> If this road is hard, we take the other one.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अपरः** (*aparaḥ*) comes from *apa*, "away, off", as MW gives it; MW records *apara* as later, latter, the other (MW).

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
