## You'll want to know: তুচ্ছ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C450-OPOMAN450-01]; assesses=[] -->

**তুচ্ছ** — *tuchchhô* — "trifling; petty".

> একটা তুচ্ছ ব্যাপার নিয়ে দুই ভাই ঝগড়া করল।
>
> *ekṭā tuchchhô bæpār niye dui bhāi jhôgṛā kôrlô.*
>
> The two brothers quarrelled over a trifling matter.

তুচ্ছ করা, to brush aside, to treat as nothing.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**তুচ্ছ** (*tuchchhô*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *tuccha*, "empty, vain, small, trifling" (Monier-Williams).
