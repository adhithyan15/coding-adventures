# sera / buonasera — "evening" and "good evening"

## Warm-up

[PAUSE 2s] The evening swap for *buongiorno*.

## The word, taken apart

**la sera** (fem.) ← Latin **sera** ("late [hour]"), from **serus**, "late."
English cousin: the rare *serotine* (a bat of the *late* evening).
**buonasera** = *buona* + *sera*, "good evening" — *buona* in its **feminine**
form, because *sera* is feminine (contrast the masculine *buon giorno*).

## Grammar Lens: feminine agreement

Notice *buon**a** sera* vs. *buon giorno*: the "good" word matches its noun's
gender — feminine *sera* takes *buona*, masculine *giorno* takes *buon*. Same
adjective, two coats.
