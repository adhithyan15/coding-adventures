## You'll want to know: il campanile
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C445-PIAZZA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**il campanile** — "the bell tower".

> Dal campanile si vede tutta la città.
>
> *From the bell tower the whole city is in view.*

*Il campanilismo* — loyalty to your own town and its bell tower.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*campanile* is built on *campana* 'bell', which is inherited from Late Latin *campāna* (EtymDB).
