## You'll want to know: la ferramenta
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C453-NEGOZI-01]; assesses=[] -->

**la ferramenta** — "the hardware shop; hardware".

> In ferramenta ho trovato le viti giuste.
>
> *At the hardware shop I found the right screws.*

*Il negozio di ferramenta* in full; *la ferramenta* is also the goods.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ferramenta* is inherited from Latin *ferrāmentum* 'iron tool' (EtymDB), from *ferrum* 'iron'.
