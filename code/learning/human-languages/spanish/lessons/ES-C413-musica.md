# la música — another art of the Muses

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-C353-WHERE-25] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Recall **el museo**, a place whose name once meant a seat of the
Muses. Their art also gives Spanish **la música**.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-MUSICA]; assesses=[] -->

Greek **mousikḗ**, the art of the Muses, travelled into Latin **mūsica** and
then Spanish **música**. English **music** followed the same road through
French. The root is already anchored in **museo**, so this lesson extends that
family rather than assigning it a second owner.

## Grammar Lens: the written accent
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-MUSICA] -->

The stress falls on the first beat: **MÚ-si-ca**. Spanish writes the accent
because that antepenultimate stress must always be marked. The noun is
feminine: **la música**, **música clásica**.
