## You'll want to know: でんせつ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C302-YUUREI302-04]; assesses=[] -->

**でんせつ** — *densetsu* — "a legend".

> **この やまには ふるい でんせつが あります。** — *kono yama ni wa furui densetsu ga arimasu* — "There is an old legend about this mountain."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**でんせつ** (*densetsu*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *den*, "transmit" (Middle Chinese *drjwen*) and *setsu*, "explanation" (*sywet*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
