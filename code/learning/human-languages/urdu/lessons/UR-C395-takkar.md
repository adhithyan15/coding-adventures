## You'll want to know: ٹکر
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C395-TAYAR395-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ٹکر** — *ṭakkar* — "a collision; a knock".

> پل پر دو گاڑیوں کی ٹکر ہوئی۔
>
> *pul par do gāṛiyoṅ kī ṭakkar huī.*
>
> Two cars collided on the bridge.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ٹکر** (*ṭakkar*) is inherited: CDIAL lists Hindi *ṭakkar*, "a blow", under *ṭakkarā*, "a blow on the head" (CDIAL 5424).
