## You'll want to know: der Knöchel
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C485-GELENKE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**der Knöchel** — "ankle; knuckle".

> Beim Wandern habe ich mir den Knöchel verstaucht.
>
> *I sprained my ankle while hiking.*

Plural: *die Knöchel*. Also a knuckle of the finger.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Knöchel* is usually traced to a small form of *Knochen* "bone", from Middle High German *knöchel*; EtymDB lists the German word without a source.
