## Script — the metal piece, then a cross-bar over the sun
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-SCRIPT-CUO-WRONG-01]; assesses=[] -->

> 错

**Two pieces, left before right.** Count the pieces, not the strokes.

**The left piece, the metal piece, five strokes**, as in 钱: a long left-falling stroke, a short horizontal, a second horizontal, a third that rises to the right, then a vertical that ends rising to the right.

**The right piece, eight strokes**: a short horizontal, two short verticals down through it, a long horizontal under them, then 日 *rì*, four strokes, at the foot.

5 + 8 = thirteen.
