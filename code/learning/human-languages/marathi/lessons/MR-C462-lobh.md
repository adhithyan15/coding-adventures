## You'll want to know: लोभ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C462-HEVA462-05]; assesses=[] -->

**लोभ** — *lobh* — "greed".

> पैशाच्या लोभाने त्याने मित्राला फसवलं.
>
> *paiśācyā lobhāne tyāne mitrālā phasavlaṁ.*
>
> Out of greed for money he cheated his friend.

Masculine.

लोभी, greedy.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**लोभ** (*lobh*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *lobha*, "eager desire" (CDIAL 11147).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
