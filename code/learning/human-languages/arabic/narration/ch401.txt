Arabic, chapter 401: Likely or not.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
احتمال (iḥtimāl) — a probability.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for whether, then the Arabic for wherever.

You'll want to know: احتمال.
احتمال — iḥtimāl — "a probability".
هناك احتمال كبير للمطر غدا. hunāka iḥtimāl kabīr li-l-maṭar ghadan. There is a high probability of rain tomorrow.

The word, taken apart.
احتمال (iḥtimāl) is built on the native Arabic root ḥ-m-l, "to carry, to bear" (Lane). Iḥtimāl is the Form VIII verbal noun, bearing a thing, allowing for it: a probability.

Lesson 3 of 5.
احتمل (iḥtamala) — to tolerate; to allow for.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a probability, then the Arabic for probable.

You'll want to know: احتمل.
احتمل — iḥtamala — "to tolerate; to allow for".
لا يحتمل جدي الحر. lā yaḥtamilu jaddī al-ḥarr. My grandfather cannot tolerate the heat.
Present: يحتمل, yaḥtamil.
يحتمل أن …, it is possible that ….

The word, taken apart.
احتمل (iḥtamala) is built on the native Arabic root ḥ-m-l, "to carry, to bear" (Lane). It is Form VIII, to carry a load oneself: to bear, to tolerate, and so to allow for a thing.

Lesson 4 of 5.
رجح (rajjaḥa) — to consider likely.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for probable, then the Arabic for to tolerate.

You'll want to know: رجح.
رجح — rajjaḥa — "to consider likely".
يرجح الأطباء أن السبب هو التوتر. yurajjiḥu al-aṭibbāʾ anna as-sabab huwa at-tawattur. Doctors consider it likely that the cause is stress.
Present: يرجح, yurajjiḥ.

The word, taken apart.
رجح (rajjaḥa) is built on the native Arabic root r-j-ḥ, "to outweigh (of a scale)" (Lane). It is Form II, to make one pan of the scale outweigh the other: to judge a thing more likely.

Lesson 5 of 5.
استبعد (istabʿada) — to rule out.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to tolerate, then the Arabic for to consider likely.

You'll want to know: استبعد.
استبعد — istabʿada — "to rule out".
استبعد المدير هذا الاحتمال. istabʿada al-mudīr hādhā al-iḥtimāl. The manager ruled out this possibility.
Present: يستبعد, yastabʿid.

The word, taken apart.
استبعد (istabʿada) is built on the native Arabic root b-ʿ-d, "to be far" (Lane). It is Form X, to hold a thing to be far off: to rule it out. بعيد, far, is from the same root.
