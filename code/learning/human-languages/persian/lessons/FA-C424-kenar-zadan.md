## You'll want to know: کنار زدن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C424-SORAT-04]; assesses=[] -->

**کنار زدن** (*kenâr zadan*) — "to pull over (to the side of the road)".

> راننده کنار زد و ما پیاده شدیم.
>
> *Rânande kenâr zad va mâ piyâde shodim.*
>
> The driver pulled over and we got out.

*kenâr* (side, edge) + *zadan*. *Kenâr zadan* can also mean to push something aside.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*kenâr zadan* is built from native *kenâr* 'side, edge', usually traced to Middle Persian *kanārag*, and the native verb *zadan* 'to strike'.
