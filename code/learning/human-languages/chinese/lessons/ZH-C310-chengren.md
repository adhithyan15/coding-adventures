## You'll want to know: chéngrèn
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C310-SUSPECT310-05]; assesses=[] -->

*chéngrèn* — "to admit; to own up".

> *zuìhòu tā chéngrèn le* — In the end he owned up.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*chéngrèn* is built from *chéng* 'to receive' and *rèn* 'to recognize', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *dzying* and *nyinH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to receive' and the second 'to know'.
