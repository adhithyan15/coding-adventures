# ಪ್ಲಾಟ್ಫಾರ್ಮ್ (plāṭphārm) — a railway platform

## You'll want to know: ಪ್ಲಾಟ್ಫಾರ್ಮ್
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C433-RAIL433-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ಪ್ಲಾಟ್ಫಾರ್ಮ್** — *plāṭphārm* — "a railway platform".

> ನಮ್ಮ ರೈಲು ಮೂರನೇ ಪ್ಲಾಟ್ಫಾರ್ಮ್ಗೆ ಬರುತ್ತದೆ.
>
> *namma railu mūranē plāṭphārmge baruttade.*
>
> Our train comes in at the third platform.

ವೇದಿಕೆ is a stage or any raised platform; at a station people say ಪ್ಲಾಟ್ಫಾರ್ಮ್.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಪ್ಲಾಟ್ಫಾರ್ಮ್** (*plāṭphārm*) is a loan from English *platform*, which came in with the railways. Its last consonant keeps the virama, as recent English loans do.
