## You'll want to know: चर्चा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C314-AFVAH314-02]; assesses=[] -->

**चर्चा** — *carcā* — "talk; a discussion".

> पूरे शहर में उस अफ़वाह की चर्चा थी।
>
> *pūre śahar meṁ us afvāh kī carcā thī.*
>
> The whole town was talking about that rumour.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**चर्चा** (*carcā*) is Sanskrit *carcā* (Monier-Williams), a repeating of words and a thinking over; in Hindi it is what everyone is talking about.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
