# प्रथमम् · ततः · अन्ते (prathamam · tataḥ · ante) — first, then, in the end

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[SA-GRAMMAR-C277-SEQUENCE-01, SA-GRAMMAR-C277-SEQUENCE-02] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "she went home and read". (*sā gṛhaṃ gatvā paṭhitavatī*.) Two actions in one sentence. A story needs more sentences, and words to order them.

## You'll want to know: प्रथमम्, ततः, अनन्तरम्
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-GRAMMAR-C277-THEN-01]; assesses=[SA-LEX-C63-ORDINAL-07, SA-LEX-C153-LOC153-02, SA-LEX-C213-TIME213-04, SA-LEX-KHADATI-EAT] -->

**प्रथमम्** (*prathamam*), "first", is **प्रथमः** (*prathamaḥ*), "first", said as an adverb. **ततः** (*tataḥ*), "then", and **अनन्तरम्** (*anantaram*), "afterwards", you know.

> **प्रथमं सः खादितवान्।**
>
> *prathamaṃ saḥ khāditavān.* — "First he ate."

> **ततः सः पठितवान्।**
>
> *tataḥ saḥ paṭhitavān.* — "Then he read."

> **अनन्तरं सः गतवान्।**
>
> *anantaraṃ saḥ gatavān.* — "Afterwards he left."

## You'll want to know: अन्ते
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-GRAMMAR-C277-THEN-02]; assesses=[SA-LEX-C74-MARKET-04, SA-LEX-C24-ASK-03, SA-LEX-C74-MARKET-01, SA-LEX-C16-PLACE-01] -->

**अन्ते** (*ante*), "in the end", closes the story.

> **अन्ते सः गृहम् आगतवान्।**
>
> *ante saḥ gṛham āgatavān.* — "In the end he came home."

Four lines, in order. **क्रीतवती** (*krītavatī*) is the past of **क्रीणाति** (*krīṇāti*), "buys":

> **प्रथमं मीरा आपणं गतवती।**
>
> *prathamaṃ mīrā āpaṇaṃ gatavatī.* — "First Mira went to the shop."

> **ततः सा फलं क्रीतवती।**
>
> *tataḥ sā phalaṃ krītavatī.* — "Then she bought a fruit."

> **अनन्तरं सा गृहम् आगतवती।**
>
> *anantaraṃ sā gṛham āgatavatī.* — "Afterwards she came home."

> **अन्ते सा फलं खादितवती।**
>
> *ante sā phalaṃ khāditavatī.* — "In the end she ate the fruit."
