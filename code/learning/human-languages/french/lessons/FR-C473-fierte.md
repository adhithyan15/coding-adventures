## You'll want to know: la fierté
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C473-ORGUEIL-01]; assesses=[] -->

**la fierté** — "pride".

> Mon père parle de mon travail avec fierté.
>
> *My father speaks about my work with pride.*

Built on *fier* "proud"; a good kind of pride.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*la fierté* is built on *fier* "proud" with *-té* (EtymDB). *Fier* is inherited from Latin *ferus* "wild, untamed" (Lewis & Short).
