## You'll want to know: praevideō, praevidēre
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C394-APPARO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**praevideō, praevidēre** — "to foresee".

> Perīculum praevīdit et fūgit.
>
> *He foresaw the danger and fled.*

*praevideō, praevidēre, praevīdī, praevīsum*: *prae-* beforehand + *videō*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*praevideō* is built from *prae-* "before" and *videō* "to see" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short).
