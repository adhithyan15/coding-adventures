## You'll want to know: प्रक्षिपति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C524-SPARDHA524-02]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रक्षिपति** — *prakṣipati* — "hurls".

> क्रीडकः कन्दुकं दूरं प्रक्षिपति।
>
> *krīḍakaḥ kandukaṃ dūraṃ prakṣipati.*
>
> The player hurls the ball a long way.

Past (he …): सः कन्दुकं प्रक्षिप्तवान्, *saḥ kandukaṃ prakṣiptavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रक्षिपति** (*prakṣipati*) is built on *pra*, "forth", and the root √kṣip, "to throw", which also gives *kṣipati*; MW gives *prakṣip* as to cast, to hurl, to fling (MW).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
