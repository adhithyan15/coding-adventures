## You'll want to know: தேதி
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C83-ORDINAL-09]; assesses=[] -->

**தேதி** (*tēdi*) — "**a date**", the day-number on a calendar.

## Grammar Lens: a second ending, for one job
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-GRAMMAR-C83-ORDINAL-08]; assesses=[TA-LEX-C83-ORDINAL-09, TA-GRAMMAR-C82-ORDINAL-04, TA-LEX-C82-ORDINAL-03] -->

Tamil has a **second** ordinal ending, and it is not a free alternative to the
first: **-ஆம்** (*-ām*) is what a **date** takes.

| said of a place in a line | said of a date |
|---|---|
| **இரண்டாவது** *iraṇḍāvadu* | **இரண்டாம் தேதி** *iraṇḍām tēdi* |
| **மூன்றாவது** *mūṉṟāvadu* | **மூன்றாம் தேதி** *mūṉṟām tēdi* |

Same number, same job in the world, two endings — and the calendar keeps the
older, shorter one.

You already have the months. **சித்திரை இரண்டாம் தேதி** (*Chithirai iraṇḍām
tēdi*) is the second of Chithirai, and that is a date you can now write down.
