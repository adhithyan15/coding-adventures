## You'll want to know: ਵੜਨਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C285-HOME285-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ਵੜਨਾ** — *vaṛnā* — "to go in; to enter".

> ਬਿੱਲੀ ਰਸੋਈ ਵਿੱਚ ਵੜਦੀ ਹੈ।
>
> *billī rasoī vich vaṛdī hai.*
>
> The cat goes into the kitchen.

Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਕਮਰੇ ਵਿੱਚ ਵੜਿਆ, *maiṁ kamre vich vaṛiā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਵੜਨਾ** (*vaṛnā*) is inherited from Sanskrit *upapatati*, "hastens towards"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *vaṛnā*, "to enter", under it (CDIAL 2189).
