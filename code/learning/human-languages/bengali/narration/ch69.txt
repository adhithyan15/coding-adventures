Bengali, chapter 69: Cow, Goat, Sheep, Bird, Mouse.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
গরু (gôru) — a cow.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a dog, then the Bengali for a cat.

You'll want to know: গরু.
গরু — gôru — "a cow".

The word, taken apart.
গরু (gôru) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from gorūpa, "cow-shaped" (CDIAL 4313). It is go, "cow", + rūpa, "shape".

The word, taken apart.
ছাগল (chhāgôl) is Sanskrit chāgala, "of a goat; a goat", taken over whole as a learned loan, a tatsama (CDIAL 4963). CDIAL lists the inherited form cheli, "goat", under the related chagala.
