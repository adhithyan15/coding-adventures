## You'll want to know: huán
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C64-HELP64-05]; assesses=[] -->

*huán* — "to give back".

> *wǒ míngtiān huán nǐ* — I'll give it back tomorrow.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The same character is read hái in hái xíng; here, giving something back, it is huán.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*huán* is inherited from Middle Chinese *hwaen* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'to return' and 'to go back'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for asking politely.
