## You'll want to know: પામવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C385-PURASKAR385-04]; assesses=[] -->

**પામવું** — *pāmvũ* — "to attain".

> જો તે સતત મહેનત કરશે તો બધાનો આદર પામશે.
>
> *jo te satat mahenat karśe to badhāno ādar pāmśe.*
>
> If he keeps working hard, he will win everyone's respect.

Past (he …): તે બધાનો આદર પામ્યો, *te badhāno ādar pāmyo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**પામવું** (*pāmvũ*) is inherited from Sanskrit *prāpnōti*, "attains to, obtains"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb (CDIAL 8947).
