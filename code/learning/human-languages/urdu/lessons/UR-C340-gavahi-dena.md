# گواہی دینا (gavāhī denā) — to testify; to bear witness

## You'll want to know: گواہی دینا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C340-FAISLA340-05]; assesses=[] -->

**گواہی دینا** — *gavāhī denā* — "to testify; to bear witness".

> سارا نے اس کے حق میں گواہی دی۔
>
> *sārā ne us ke haq meṅ gavāhī dī.*
>
> Sara testified in his favour.

Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے گواہی دی, *us ne gavāhī dī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**گواہی دینا** (*gavāhī denā*) is built from *gavāhī*, "testimony", usually traced to Persian *gavāhī*, from *gavāh*, "a witness", and **دینا**, "to give".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
