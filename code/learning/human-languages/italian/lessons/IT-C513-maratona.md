## You'll want to know: la maratona
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C513-GARA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**la maratona** — "marathon".

> Ho corso la mia prima maratona a quarant'anni.
>
> *I ran my first marathon at forty.*

Also a long session: *una maratona di film*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*maratona* comes from Greek, from the name of a town, which is built on *márathon* "fennel" (EtymDB): the story goes that a runner carried news of a victory from there.
