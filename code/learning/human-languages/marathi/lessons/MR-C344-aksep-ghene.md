## You'll want to know: आक्षेप घेणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C344-MATBHED344-03]; assesses=[] -->

**आक्षेप घेणे** — *ākṣep gheṇe* — "to object".

> अरुणने या योजनेवर आक्षेप घेतला.
>
> *aruṇne yā yojnevar ākṣep ghetlā.*
>
> Arun objected to this plan.

Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने आक्षेप घेतला, *tyāne ākṣep ghetlā*.

What is objected to takes -वर: योजनेवर आक्षेप घेणे.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**आक्षेप घेणे** (*ākṣep gheṇe*) joins the tatsama आक्षेप, Sanskrit *ākṣepa*, "throwing at; objection" (Monier-Williams), to घेणे, "to take".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
