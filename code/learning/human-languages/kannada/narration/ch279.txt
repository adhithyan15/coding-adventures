Lesson 1 of 5.
ಚುಚ್ಚು (cuccu) — to prick, to pierce.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to prevent, then the Kannada for to prepare.

You'll want to know: ಚುಚ್ಚು.
ಚುಚ್ಚು — cuccu — "to prick, to pierce".

The word, taken apart.
ಚುಚ್ಚು (cuccu) is native Dravidian (DEDR 2778). DEDR lists cuccu, "to make enter, put on a spit".

Lesson 4 of 5.
ಬಗೆಹರಿಸು (bageharisu) — to solve, to sort out.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to spit, then the Kannada for to yawn.

You'll want to know: ಬಗೆಹರಿಸು.
ಬಗೆಹರಿಸು — bageharisu — "to solve, to sort out".

The word, taken apart.
ಬಗೆಹರಿಸು (bageharisu) is native Dravidian: bage, "thought, concern" (DEDR 5205), and harisu, "to make go away", from hari, "to run, flow, go away" (DEDR 3963). Reading the whole as "to make a worry go away" is likely, but no source spells it out.
