## You'll want to know: ちょうじょう
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C412-TOUGE412-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ちょうじょう** — *chōjō* — "a summit; the top".

> **やまの ちょうじょうは さむかったです。** — *yama no chōjō wa samukatta desu* — "It was cold at the top of the mountain."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ちょうじょう** (*chōjō*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *chō*, "summit" (Middle Chinese *tengX*) and *jō*, "above" (*dzyangH*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
