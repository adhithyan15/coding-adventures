Marwadi (Marwari), chapter 210: Choosing the Right One.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
परखणो (parakhṇo) — to test; to check.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for a deal, then the Marwadi for to order.

You'll want to know: परखणो.
परखणो — parakhṇo — "to test; to check".
मां कपड़ा परखै है। mā̃ kapṛā parkhai hai. Mother checks the cloth.
Past (I …): म्हैं कपड़ा परख्या, mhaĩ kapṛā parakhyā.

The word, taken apart.
परखणो (parakhṇo) is inherited from Sanskrit parīkṣate, "observes, examines"; CDIAL lists an Old Marwari form beside Hindi parakhnā, "to test" (CDIAL 7904).

Lesson 2 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for to order, then the Marwadi for to test.

You'll want to know: भांत.
भांत — bhā̃t — "a sort; a kind".
दूजी भांत दिखावो। dūjī bhā̃t dikhāvo. Show me another kind.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
भांत (bhā̃t) is inherited from Sanskrit bhakti, "a share; an arrangement, a manner"; CDIAL lists Old Marwari bhā̃ti, "manner, kind" (CDIAL 9338).

Lesson 5 of 5.
चुणणो (chuṇṇo) — to pick out; to choose.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for a sample, then the Marwadi for fake.

You'll want to know: चुणणो.
चुणणो — chuṇṇo — "to pick out; to choose".
हूं चोखी जूती चुणूं हूं। hū̃ chokhī jūtī chuṇū̃ hū̃. I pick out a good pair of shoes.
Past (I …): म्हैं लाल टोपी चुणी, mhaĩ lāl ṭopī chuṇī.

The word, taken apart.
चुणणो (chuṇṇo) is inherited from Sanskrit cinoti, "heaps up, collects". CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry, but lists Hindi cunnā, "to pick up, gather" (CDIAL 4814); picking out the best is probably a later step from gathering.
