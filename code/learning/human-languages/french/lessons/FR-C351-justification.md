## You'll want to know: la justification
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C351-REACTION-05]; assesses=[] -->

**la justification** — "the justification".

> Il n'y a aucune justification pour ce prix.
>
> *There is no justification for this price.*

Feminine: *une justification*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*justification* is a learned borrowing from Late Latin *iūstificātiō* (EtymDB), from *iūstificāre* "to justify", built on *iūstus* "right, fair" (Lewis & Short).
