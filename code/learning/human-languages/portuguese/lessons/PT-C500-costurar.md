## You'll want to know: costurar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C500-COSTURA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**costurar** — "to sew".

> Costurei um botão na camisa.
>
> *I sewed a button on the shirt.*

Built on *a costura*. In Portugal *coser* is also very common, especially for small mending jobs.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*costurar* is built on *costura* "sewing", usually traced to Latin *cōnsuere* "to sew together" (Lewis & Short).
