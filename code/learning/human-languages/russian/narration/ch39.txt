Russian, chapter 39: Wash, Cook, Buy, Sell, Swim.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
мыть (myt) — to wash.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to laugh, then the Russian for to cry.

You'll want to know: мыть.
мыть (myt) — "to wash".

The word, taken apart.
мыть (myt) from Proto-Slavic myti, present myjǫ — inherited, a cousin of Lithuanian máudyti, "to bathe, to wash", and Latvian maût, "to swim" (Vasmer).

Lesson 2 of 5.
готовить (gotóvit) — to cook, to prepare.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to cry, then the Russian for to wash.

You'll want to know: готовить.
готовить (gotóvit) — "to cook, to prepare".

The word, taken apart.
готовить (gotóvit) is готовый, "ready", made into a verb: "to make ready", and so "to cook". готовый goes back to Proto-Slavic gotovŭ; Vasmer relates it to Albanian gatuanj, "I cook", and rejects a loan from Gothic.

Lesson 3 of 5.
покупать (pokupát) — to buy.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to wash, then the Russian for to cook.

You'll want to know: покупать.
покупать (pokupát) — "to buy".

The word, taken apart.
покупать (pokupát) is the ongoing partner of купить, "to buy", with по-. купить was borrowed from Gothic kaupōn, "to trade", and the Germanic word comes from Latin caupō, "innkeeper, petty trader" (Vasmer). German kaufen, "to buy", has the same source.

Lesson 4 of 5.
продавать (prodavát) — to sell.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to cook, then the Russian for to buy.

You'll want to know: продавать.
продавать (prodavát) — "to sell".

The word, taken apart.
продавать (prodavát) is про-, "through, forth", with давать, "to give", on the root of дать, "to give", which Vasmer relates to Lithuanian dúoti and Greek didōmi, "to give"; Latin dare is a cousin too.

Lesson 5 of 5.
плавать (plávat) — to swim.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to buy, then the Russian for to sell.

You'll want to know: плавать.
плавать (plávat) — "to swim".

The word, taken apart.
плавать (plávat) from Proto-Slavic plavati — inherited, a repeated-action form on the root of плыть, "to swim, to sail" (Vasmer). Vasmer relates that root to Sanskrit plávate, "floats", Greek pléō, "I sail", and Latin pluit, "it rains".
