## You'll want to know: la decisión
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C617-DECIDE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**la decisión** — "the decision".

> Es una decisión difícil.
>
> *It is a difficult decision.*

*Tomar una decisión* — to make a decision.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*decisión* is a learned borrowing from Latin *decisio* 'a settlement', built on *decidere* 'to cut off, to settle', from *caedere* 'to cut' (EtymDB).
