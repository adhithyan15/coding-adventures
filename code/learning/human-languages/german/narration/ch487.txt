German, chapter 487: Beim Hausarzt.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
der Hausarzt — family doctor, GP.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to snore, then the German for nightmare.

You'll want to know: der Hausarzt.
der Hausarzt — "family doctor, GP".
Mit Fieber gehe ich zuerst zum Hausarzt. With a fever I go to my GP first.
Plural: die Hausärzte. A woman is die Hausärztin.

The word, taken apart.
Hausarzt is built on Haus "house" and Arzt "doctor", which comes through Old High German arzāt from Late Latin archiāter "physician" (EtymDB), a word from Greek.

What you've built.
One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.

Lesson 3 of 5.
untersuchen — to examine.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for family doctor, then the German for specialist.

You'll want to know: untersuchen.
untersuchen — "to examine".
Die Ärztin untersucht mein Knie. The doctor examines my knee.
Inseparable: hat untersucht. The noun: die Untersuchung.

The word, taken apart.
untersuchen is built on unter "under" and suchen "to seek", inherited from Old High German suohhen, from Proto-Germanic sōkijaną (EtymDB).

Lesson 4 of 5.
behandeln — to treat.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for specialist, then the German for to examine.

You'll want to know: behandeln.
behandeln — "to treat".
Ein junger Arzt hat meinen Vater gut behandelt. A young doctor treated my father well.
Also of people in general: Er behandelt seine Kollegen fair.

The word, taken apart.
behandeln is built on be- and handeln "to act, to handle", from Hand; EtymDB sets it beside Dutch behandelen "to handle, to treat".

Lesson 5 of 5.
verschreiben — to prescribe.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to examine, then the German for to treat.

You'll want to know: verschreiben.
verschreiben — "to prescribe".
Der Arzt hat mir Tabletten gegen den Husten verschrieben. The doctor prescribed me tablets for my cough.
Irregular: verschrieb, hat verschrieben. Sich verschreiben — to make a slip of the pen.

The word, taken apart.
verschreiben is built on ver- and schreiben, which is inherited from Old High German scrīban (EtymDB), an early borrowing from Latin scrībere "to write".
