## You'll want to know: laudātiō, laudātiōnis
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C331-LUCTUS-03]; assesses=[] -->

**laudātiō, laudātiōnis** — "a speech of praise, a eulogy".

> Fīlius in forō laudātiōnem patris habuit.
>
> *The son gave a speech in praise of his father in the forum.*

Feminine, from *laudō*, 'to praise' (taught). *Laudātiō fūnebris*: the funeral speech.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**laudātiō** is built on *laudō*, "to praise" (Lewis & Short), with the ending *-tiō*.
