## You'll want to know: der Sicherheitsgurt
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C401-GURT-03]; assesses=[] -->

**der Sicherheitsgurt** — "the seat belt".

> Im Taxi trägt sie immer den Sicherheitsgurt.
>
> *In a taxi she always wears her seat belt.*

Plural: *die Sicherheitsgurte*. Often shortened to *der Gurt*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Sicherheitsgurt* is built from *Sicherheit* 'safety', from *sicher*, an early borrowing from Latin *sēcūrus* 'free from care' (Lewis & Short), and *Gurt* 'belt', inherited from Middle High German *gurt* (EtymDB).
