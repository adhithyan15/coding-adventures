## You'll want to know: ಪಕ್ಷಪಾತ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C378-NILUVU378-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ಪಕ್ಷಪಾತ** — *pakṣapāta* — "bias; partiality".

> ಶಿಕ್ಷಕರು ಯಾರಿಗೂ ಪಕ್ಷಪಾತ ಮಾಡುವುದಿಲ್ಲ.
>
> *śikṣakaru yārigū pakṣapāta māḍuvudilla.*
>
> The teacher shows no bias to anyone.

ಪಕ್ಷಪಾತ ಮಾಡು, to be partial.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಪಕ್ಷಪಾತ** (*pakṣapāta*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *pakṣapāta*, "siding with, partiality" (Monier-Williams), from *pakṣa*, "a side, a wing", and *pāta*, "falling".
