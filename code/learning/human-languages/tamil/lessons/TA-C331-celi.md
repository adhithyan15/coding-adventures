## You'll want to know: செழி
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C331-KODAI331-05]; assesses=[] -->

**செழி** — *ceḻi* — "to prosper; to thrive".

> ஆற்றின் கரையில் நகரங்கள் செழித்தன.
>
> *āṟṟiṉ karaiyil nakaraṅkaḷ ceḻittaṉa.*
>
> Towns prospered on the banks of the river.

Past (it …): செழித்தது, *ceḻittatu*.

Of plants too: to grow well.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**செழி** (*ceḻi*) is native Dravidian (DEDR 2789): to thrive, flourish, prosper, as a crop or a kingdom.
