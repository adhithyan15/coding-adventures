## You'll want to know: silex, silicis
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C425-AGGER-02]; assesses=[] -->

**silex, silicis** — "a flint; a hard paving stone".

> Haec via ē silice facta est, itaque in pluviā quoque bona est.
>
> *This road is made of hard stone, so it is good in the rain too.*

Third declension, usually masculine: *silex, silicis m.* Roman roads were paved with blocks of it. English *silica* and *silicon* come from it.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

The origin of *silex* is not settled: Lewis & Short tentatively link it with a root meaning 'firm', as in *solidus*, and EtymDB gives no parent. EtymDB lists English *silica* and *silicon* among the words made from it.
