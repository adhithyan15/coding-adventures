## You'll want to know: હાસ્ય
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C448-HASYA448-01]; assesses=[] -->

**હાસ્ય** — *hāsya* — "laughter".

> મીરાના હાસ્યથી આખું ઘર ખુશ થઈ ગયું.
>
> *Mīrānā hāsyathī ākhũ ghar khuś thaī gayũ.*
>
> Mira's laughter made the whole house happy.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**હાસ્ય** (*hāsya*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *hāsya*, "laughter, mirth", as Monier-Williams records it; it shares its root with **હસવું** (*hasvũ*), "to laugh".
