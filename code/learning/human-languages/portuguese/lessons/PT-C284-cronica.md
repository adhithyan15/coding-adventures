## You'll want to know: a crónica
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C284-ESCANDALO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**a crónica** — "the newspaper piece, a short personal essay; the chronicle".

> Leio a crónica dela todos os domingos.
>
> *I read her piece in the paper every Sunday.*

In Brazil: *crônica*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*crónica* is a learned borrowing from Latin *chronica* "a chronicle" (EtymDB), from Greek *khronikós* "of time", built on *khrónos* "time".
