## You'll want to know: गुलाल
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C249-HOLI249-02]; assesses=[] -->

**गुलाल** — *gulāl* — "coloured powder for Holi".

> गुलाल लाल अर गुलाबी है।
>
> *gulāl lāl ar gulābī hai.*
>
> The Holi powder is red and pink.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**गुलाल** (*gulāl*) is usually traced to Persian *gulāl*, a red powder; no dictionary we can open gives an older Indian form.
