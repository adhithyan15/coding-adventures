## You'll want to know: জলরং
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C524-TULI524-02]; assesses=[] -->

**জলরং** — *jôlrông* — "watercolour".

> শিল্পী জলরঙে একটা নদীর ছবি আঁকলেন।
>
> *Shilpī jôlrônge ekṭā nôdīr chhôbi ā̃klen.*
>
> The artist painted a river in watercolour.

Before a vowel ending ং is written ঙ: জলরঙে, in watercolour.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**জলরং** (*jôlrông*) joins জল, "water", to রং, "colour", a word that matches both Sanskrit *raṅga* (CDIAL 10560) and Persian *rang*: colour mixed with water.
