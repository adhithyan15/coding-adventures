## You'll want to know: زمن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C304-ZAMAN304-01]; assesses=[] -->

**زمن** — *zaman* — "an age, a span of time".

> كان ذلك في زمن جدي.
>
> *kāna dhālika fī zaman jaddī.*
>
> That was in my grandfather’s time.

Plural: أزمنة, *azmina*.

وقت is a time for something; زمن is time as a stretch, an age.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**زمن** (*zaman*) is built on the native Arabic root *z-m-n* (Lane), where *zaman* and *zamān* name time, a short span or a long one.
