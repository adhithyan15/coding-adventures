# ನೇರವಾಗಿ ಹೋಗಿ (nēravāgi hōgi) — giving the way

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[KA-LEX-C107-LOC107-02, KA-LEX-C107-LOC107-01, KA-LEX-C107-LOC107-03, KA-GRAMMAR-C313-HOW-FAR-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "left", "right" and "straight". (*eḍa*, *bala*, *nēra*.) Then "How far is it from here?" (*illinda eṣṭu dūra?*)

## You'll want to know: straight on, left, right
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-GRAMMAR-C313-GIVE-WAY-01]; assesses=[KA-LEX-C110-LOC110-05, KA-LEX-C106-SOON-04, KA-GRAMMAR-C310-CONJUNCTIVE-01, KA-LEX-C310-SVACCHA-01] -->

> **ನೇರವಾಗಿ ಹೋಗಿ. ಮೊದಲ ತಿರುವಿನಲ್ಲಿ ಎಡಕ್ಕೆ ಹೋಗಿ.**
>
> *nēravāgi hōgi. modala tiruvinalli eḍakke hōgi.* — "Go straight on. At the first turn, go left."

**ಹೋಗಿ** here is a polite "go", said to a listener: the stem with **-ಇ**, as in **ಫೋನ್ ಮಾಡಿ**, "please phone". It sounds like the **ಹೋಗಿ** of "having gone", but it is a different form, and other verbs keep the two apart: "please give" is **ಕೊಡಿ**, "having given" is **ಕೊಟ್ಟು**. **ನೇರವಾಗಿ** is **ನೇರ** with **-ವಾಗಿ**, the piece you met in **ಸ್ವಚ್ಛವಾಗಿದೆ**: "straight on". **ಎಡಕ್ಕೆ** is **ಎಡ** with **-ಕ್ಕೆ**, "to": "to the left"; and **ಬಲಕ್ಕೆ** (*balakke*), "to the right".

**ಮೊದಲ** (*modala*, "first") is the **ಮೊದಲು** of a story, standing in front of a noun. **ತಿರುವಿನಲ್ಲಿ** (*tiruvinalli*, "at the turn") is **ತಿರುವು** with **-ಇನ-** and **-ಅಲ್ಲಿ**, "at".

## You'll want to know: what it is next to
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-GRAMMAR-C313-LANDMARK-01]; assesses=[KA-LEX-C84-BODY-04, KA-LEX-C104-JOBS-05, KA-LEX-C175-STHALA175-03, KA-LEX-C100-FIND-03, KA-LEX-C100-FIND-04] -->

> **ಆಸ್ಪತ್ರೆ ಬಲಕ್ಕೆ ಇದೆ, ಅಂಗಡಿಯ ಪಕ್ಕದಲ್ಲಿ.**
>
> *āspatre balakke ide, aṅgaḍiya pakkadalli.* — "The hospital is on the right, next to the shop."

**ಅಂಗಡಿಯ** (*aṅgaḍiya*, "of the shop"); **ಪಕ್ಕದಲ್ಲಿ** (*pakkadalli*, "at the side, next to"), from **ಪಕ್ಕ**. Most directions are these few pieces in a row: straight on, a turn, left or right, and one thing it is next to.

> **ಬಸ್ ನಿಲ್ದಾಣ ಎಡಕ್ಕೆ ಇದೆ, ಹೋಟೆಲ್ ಪಕ್ಕದಲ್ಲಿ.**
>
> *bas nildāṇa eḍakke ide, hōṭel pakkadalli.* — "The bus station is on the left, next to the hotel."
