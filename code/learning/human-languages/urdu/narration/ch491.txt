Urdu, chapter 491: Falling Out and Making Up.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
رنجش (ranjish) — ill feeling; a grudge.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for an orphan, then the Urdu for to adopt.

You'll want to know: رنجش.
رنجش — ranjish — "ill feeling; a grudge".
دونوں بھائیوں میں رنجش ہے۔ donoṅ bhāiyoṅ meṅ ranjish hai. There is ill feeling between the two brothers.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
رنجش (ranjish) is usually traced to Persian ranjish, "offence, vexation".

What you've built.
One more word for describing what you lived and felt.

[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

[pause 3 seconds]

Lesson 5 of 5.
معاف کرنا (māf karnā) — to forgive.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a taunt, then the Urdu for a divorce.

You'll want to know: معاف کرنا.
معاف کرنا — māf karnā — "to forgive".
بہن نے بھائی کو معاف کیا۔ bahan ne bhāī ko māf kiyā. The sister forgave her brother.
Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے دوست کو معاف کیا, us ne dost ko māf kiyā.

The word, taken apart.
معاف کرنا (māf karnā) is built from معاف, usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic muʿāf, "excused", and کرنا, "to do".
