## You'll want to know: جبڑا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C465-BONE465-05]; assesses=[] -->

**جبڑا** — *jabṛā* — "a jaw".

> شیر کا جبڑا بڑا مضبوط ہوتا ہے۔
>
> *sher kā jabṛā baṛā mazbūt hotā hai.*
>
> A lion's jaw is very strong.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**جبڑا** (*jabṛā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *jābṛā*, "jaw-bone", under Sanskrit *jámbha*, "tooth, jaw" (CDIAL 5137).
