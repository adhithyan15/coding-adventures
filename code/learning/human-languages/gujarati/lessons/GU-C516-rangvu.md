# રંગવું (rangvũ) — to colour (with paint or dye)

## You'll want to know: રંગવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C516-CHUNO516-04]; assesses=[] -->

**રંગવું** — *rangvũ* — "to colour (with paint or dye)".

> અરુણે બારણું લીલા રંગથી રંગ્યું.
>
> *Aruṇe bārṇũ līlā rangthī rangyũ.*
>
> Arun painted the door green.

Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે બારણું રંગ્યું, *teṇe bārṇũ rangyũ*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**રંગવું** (*rangvũ*) is built on **રંગ**, "colour", from Sanskrit *raṅga*, "colour, dye" (Monier-Williams); it is also used for dyeing cloth.
