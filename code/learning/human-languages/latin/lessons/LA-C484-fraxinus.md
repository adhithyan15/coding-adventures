## You'll want to know: fraxinus, fraxinī
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C484-FAGUS-04]; assesses=[] -->

**fraxinus, fraxinī** — "an ash tree; an ash-wood spear".

> Ex fraxinō hastae fīunt.
>
> *Spears are made from ash wood.*

Second declension but feminine: *fraxinus, fraxinī f.* Its hard, springy wood made spear shafts.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*fraxinus* is inherited from Proto-Italic *fraksinos*, from PIE *bʰrHǵ-s-inos* 'ash tree' (EtymDB).
