## You'll want to know: കരുതൽ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C556-VANCANA556-04]; assesses=[] -->

**കരുതൽ** — *karutal* — "care; concern".

> രോഗിയായ അച്ഛനോട് മകൾ വലിയ കരുതൽ കാണിച്ചു.
>
> *rōgiyāya acchanōṭŭ makaḷ valiya karutal kāṇiccu.*
>
> The daughter showed great care for her sick father.

From കരുതുക, to keep in mind; കരുതലോടെ means carefully.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**കരുതൽ** (*karutal*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 1283 gives it as care and regard, beside കരുതുക, "to think, to attend to" (DEDR 1283).
