## You'll want to know: montare
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C338-FRUSTA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**montare** — "to whip (cream, eggs); to climb; to assemble".

> Monta le uova con la frusta per dieci minuti.
>
> *Whip the eggs with the whisk for ten minutes.*

*Montare a neve* — to beat until stiff. Also *montare un mobile* — to put furniture together.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*montare* is built on *monte* "mountain", from Latin *mōns, montis* (Lewis & Short): to go up, and in the kitchen, to make it rise.
