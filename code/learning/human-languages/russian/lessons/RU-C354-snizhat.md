## You'll want to know: снижать
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C354-BOLSHE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**снижать** (*snizhát*) — "to lower, to reduce".

> Магазин снижает цены перед праздниками.
>
> *Magazín snizháyet tsény péred prázdnikami.*
>
> The shop lowers its prices before the holidays.

Imperfective; perfective *снизить* (*снижу, снизишь*). *Снижаться* — to go down.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**снижать** is built from *с-* and *низ* "the bottom", from Proto-Slavic *\*nizŭ*, the root of *низкий* "low" (EtymDB).
