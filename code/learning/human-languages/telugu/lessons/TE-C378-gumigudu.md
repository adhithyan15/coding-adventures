## You'll want to know: గుమిగూడు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C378-KOTLATA378-05]; assesses=[] -->

**గుమిగూడు** — *gumigūḍu* — "to gather round".

> ప్రమాదం జరిగిన చోట జనం గుమిగూడారు.
>
> *pramādaṁ jarigina cōṭa janaṁ gumigūḍāru.*
>
> People gathered where the accident happened.

Past (I …): గుమిగూడాను, *gumigūḍānu*.

గుమి, a crowd, and కూడు, to come together.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**గుమిగూడు** (*gumigūḍu*) is a native compound: *gumi*, "a crowd" (DEDR 1741), and *kūḍu*, "to meet, gather" (DEDR 1882).
