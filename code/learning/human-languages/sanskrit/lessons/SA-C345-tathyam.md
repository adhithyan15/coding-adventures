# तथ्यम् (tathyam) — a fact; the truth of a matter

## You'll want to know: तथ्यम्
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C345-PRATYAYA345-03]; assesses=[] -->

**तथ्यम्** — *tathyam* — "a fact; the truth of a matter".

> एतत् तथ्यम् अस्ति, न मिथ्या।
>
> *etat tathyam asti, na mithyā.*
>
> This is a fact, not a falsehood.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**तथ्यम्** (*tathyam*) MW gives *tathya* "being really so, true" and the neuter "truth"; "a fact" is the sense modern Sanskrit gives it, and MW gives no derivation, so its origin is uncertain (MW).
