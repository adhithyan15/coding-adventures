Lesson 1 of 6.
ચંચળ (cancaḷ) — never still; fickle.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for charity, then the Gujarati for a donation.

You'll want to know: ચંચળ.
ચંચળ — cancaḷ — "never still; fickle".
નાનો ભત્રીજો ઘણો ચંચળ છે. nāno bhatrījo ghaṇo cancaḷ che. My little nephew is never still.

The word, taken apart.
ચંચળ (cancaḷ) is a tatsama, Sanskrit cañcala, "moving to and fro, unsteady", as Monier-Williams records it.

You'll want to know: તોફાની.
તોફાની — tophānī — "naughty".
તોફાની છોકરાએ મારું રમકડું તોડ્યું. tophānī chokrāe mārũ ramakḍũ toḍyũ. The naughty boy broke my toy.

The word, taken apart.
તોફાની (tophānī) is built on તોફાન (tophān), "a storm", traced to Arabic ṭūfān, "a flood", through Persian; with -ī it names a child who stirs up a storm.

મારો મોજીલો ભાઈ હંમેશા ગીતો ગાય છે. māro mojīlo bhāī hammeśā gīto gāy che. My fun-loving brother is always singing.

મોજીલું (mojīlũ) is built on moj, "fun, high spirits", which is usually traced to Arabic mawj, "a wave", with the adjective ending -īlũ.

વંઠવું — vanṭhvũ — "to go astray; to be spoilt".
ખરાબ દોસ્તોને લીધે દીકરો વંઠી ગયો. kharāb dostone līdhe dīkro vanṭhī gayo. Because of bad friends the son went astray.
Past (he …): તે વંઠી ગયો, te vanṭhī gayo.

The word, taken apart.
વંઠવું (vanṭhvũ) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb, "to be dissipated", under Sanskrit vaṇṭa, "tailless, unmarried" (CDIAL 11236).
