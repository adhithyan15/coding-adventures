## You'll want to know: पूँजी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C430-DIVALA430-03]; assesses=[] -->

**पूँजी** — *pū̃jī* — "capital (money); savings".

> दुकान खोलने के लिए पूँजी चाहिए।
>
> *dukān kholne ke liye pū̃jī cāhiye.*
>
> You need capital to open a shop.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पूँजी** (*pū̃jī*) is a tadbhava of Sanskrit *puñja*, "a heap, a quantity" (CDIAL 8251), which lists Hindi *pū̃jī*, "heap, store": the store of money a business is built on.
