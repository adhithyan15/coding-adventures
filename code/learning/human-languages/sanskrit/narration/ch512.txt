Sanskrit, chapter 512: Chop and Grind.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
छुरिका (churikā) — a knife.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for cooking, then the Sanskrit for roasts.

You'll want to know: छुरिका.
छुरिका — churikā — "a knife".
माता छुरिकया शाकं छिनत्ति। mātā churikayā śākaṃ chinatti. Mother cuts the vegetables with a knife.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
छुरिका (churikā) comes from kṣura, "a razor", as MW derives it; MW records churikā as a knife (MW).

Lesson 2 of 5.
कृन्तति (kṛntati) — cuts up.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for roasts, then the Sanskrit for a knife.

You'll want to know: कृन्तति.
कृन्तति — kṛntati — "cuts up".
सः आलुकानि कृन्तति। saḥ ālukāni kṛntati. He cuts up the potatoes.
Past (he …): सः पलाण्डुं कृत्तवान्, saḥ palāṇḍuṃ kṛttavān.

The word, taken apart.
कृन्तति (kṛntati) comes from the root √kṛt, "to cut, to cut in pieces, to divide", whose present is kṛntati; its past participle is kṛtta (MW).

Lesson 3 of 5.

चूर्णयति — cūrṇayati — "grinds to powder".
सा मरीचं चूर्णयति। sā marīcaṃ cūrṇayati. She grinds the pepper.
Past (she …): सा मरीचं चूर्णितवती, sā marīcaṃ cūrṇitavatī.

The word, taken apart.
चूर्णयति (cūrṇayati) is built on cūrṇa, "powder, flour": MW gives cūrṇayati as to reduce to powder, to grind, to pound (MW).

Lesson 4 of 5.
शूर्पम् (śūrpam) — a winnowing basket.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for cuts up, then the Sanskrit for grinds to powder.

You'll want to know: शूर्पम्.
शूर्पम् — śūrpam — "a winnowing basket".
माता शूर्पेण तण्डुलान् शोधयति। mātā śūrpeṇa taṇḍulān śodhayati. Mother cleans the rice with a winnowing basket.
A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
शूर्पम् (śūrpam) is the word MW records for a winnowing basket, the wicker tray that is shaken to fan the husk away; MW gives no derivation, so its origin is uncertain (MW).

Lesson 5 of 5.
संयोजयति (saṃyojayati) — combines.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for grinds to powder, then the Sanskrit for a winnowing basket.

You'll want to know: संयोजयति.
संयोजयति — saṃyojayati — "combines".
सा दधि शर्करया संयोजयति। sā dadhi śarkarayā saṃyojayati. She combines the curd with sugar.
Past (he …): सः शर्करां दुग्धेन संयोजितवान्, saḥ śarkarāṃ dugdhena saṃyojitavān.

The word, taken apart.
संयोजयति (saṃyojayati) is built on sam, "together", and the causative of the root √yuj, "to join", which also gives yojayati; MW gives saṃyuj as to join together, to combine (MW).
