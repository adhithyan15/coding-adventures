# el lado — the side

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-PUERTA] -->

[PAUSE 2s] A door has a left edge and a right edge. Each edge belongs to **un
lado**, a side.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-LADO]; assesses=[] -->

**Lado** continues Latin **latus**, “side.” English **lateral** is the learned
cousin: a lateral movement goes toward one side.

## Grammar Lens: al lado de
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-LADO, ES-LEX-PUERTA] -->

The useful location frame is **al lado de**, beside or next to: **al lado de la
puerta**. The **al** is **a + el**, literally “at the side of.”
