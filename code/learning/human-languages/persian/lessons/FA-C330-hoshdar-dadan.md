## You'll want to know: هشدار دادن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C330-TUFAN-05]; assesses=[] -->

**هشدار دادن** (*hoshdâr dâdan*) — "to warn".

> پلیس به مردم هشدار داد که از خانه بیرون نروند.
>
> *Polis be mardom hoshdâr dâd ke az khâne birun naravand.*
>
> The police warned people not to go out of their homes.

The person warned takes *be*; the warning follows in a *ke* clause with the subjunctive.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*hoshdâr dâdan* joins the native noun *hoshdâr* "a warning" with *dâdan* "to give", inherited Persian.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.
