## You'll want to know: la nuitée
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C424-HEBERGEMENT-03]; assesses=[] -->

**la nuitée** — "the overnight stay (paid for at a hotel)".

> La nuitée coûte quatre-vingts euros avec le petit-déjeuner.
>
> *A night costs eighty euros with breakfast.*

A word from hotel bills and prices.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*nuitée* is built on *nuit* 'night' (EtymDB), which is inherited from Latin *nox, noctis* 'night'.
