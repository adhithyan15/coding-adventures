Telugu, chapter 436: Taking Part.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
పాల్గొను (pālgonu) — to take part; to participate.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a reformer, then the Telugu for to inspire.

You'll want to know: పాల్గొను.
పాల్గొను — pālgonu — "to take part; to participate".
చాలా మంది విద్యార్థులు ఆ పోటీలో పాల్గొన్నారు. cālā mandi vidyārthulu ā pōṭīlō pālgonnāru. Many students took part in that competition.
Past (I …): పాల్గొన్నాను, pālgonnānu.
What you take part in takes -లో.

The word, taken apart.
పాల్గొను (pālgonu) is native Dravidian, a compound of pālu, "a part, a share, a lot" (DEDR 4097), and konu, "to take" (DEDR 2151): to take one’s share.

Lesson 2 of 5.
తోడ్పడు (tōḍpaḍu) — to contribute; to help along.

[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to inspire, then the Telugu for to take part.

You'll want to know: తోడ్పడు.
తోడ్పడు — tōḍpaḍu — "to contribute; to help along".
ఆ పథకం గ్రామ అభివృద్ధికి తోడ్పడింది. ā pathakaṁ grāma abhivṛddhiki tōḍpaḍindi. That scheme helped the village develop.
Past (I …): తోడ్పడ్డాను, tōḍpaḍḍānu.
What it helps takes -కి. The noun is తోడ్పాటు, a contribution.

The word, taken apart.
తోడ్పడు (tōḍpaḍu) is native Telugu, a compound of tōḍu, "a companion, help", and the verb paḍu (DEDR 3853).

Lesson 3 of 5.
కూడగట్టు (kūḍagaṭṭu) — to rally; to gather (support).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to take part, then the Telugu for to contribute.

You'll want to know: కూడగట్టు.
కూడగట్టు — kūḍagaṭṭu — "to rally; to gather (support)".
ఆమె గ్రామస్తుల మద్దతు కూడగట్టింది. āme grāmastula maddatu kūḍagaṭṭindi. She rallied the villagers’ support.
Past (I …): కూడగట్టాను, kūḍagaṭṭānu.
కూడ, together, + కట్టు, to tie, sounded గట్టు after it: to tie people together.

The word, taken apart.
కూడగట్టు (kūḍagaṭṭu) is native Dravidian, a compound of kūḍu, "to join, to gather, to collect" (DEDR 1882), and kaṭṭu, "to tie, to bind" (DEDR 1147), whose k sounds as g after the first word.

Lesson 4 of 5.
ఆకర్షించు (ākarṣiñcu) — to attract.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to contribute, then the Telugu for to rally.

You'll want to know: ఆకర్షించు.
ఆకర్షించు — ākarṣiñcu — "to attract".
ఆ కొత్త పథకం చాలా మందిని ఆకర్షించింది. ā kotta pathakaṁ cālā mandini ākarṣiñcindi. That new scheme attracted many people.
Past (I …): ఆకర్షించాను, ākarṣiñcānu.
The noun is ఆకర్షణ, attraction.

The word, taken apart.
ఆకర్షించు (ākarṣiñcu) is built on Sanskrit ākarṣaṇa, "pulling, drawing near, attracting" (Monier-Williams), with the Telugu verb ending -ఇంచు.
