Portuguese, chapter 253: Fitting room, Size, Change you get back, Receipt, Queue.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
o provador — a fitting room.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for a coach, then the Portuguese for a fan.

You'll want to know: o provador.
o provador — "a fitting room".

The word, taken apart.
provador is built from provar, "to try on", and -dor: the place where you try.
