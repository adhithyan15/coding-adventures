Portuguese, chapter 504: Coleções e artesanato.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
a coleção — collection.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for jigsaw puzzle, then the Portuguese for crossword.

You'll want to know: a coleção.
a coleção — "collection".
Tem uma coleção enorme de discos. He has a huge record collection.
Before AO90 spelled colecção in Portugal.

The word, taken apart.
coleção is a learned borrowing from Latin collēctiō "a gathering together" (EtymDB), from colligere "to gather".

Lesson 3 of 5.
o artesanato — handicrafts, crafts.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for collection, then the Portuguese for to collect.

You'll want to know: o artesanato.
o artesanato — "handicrafts, crafts".
Na feira comprámos artesanato da região. At the fair we bought local crafts.
The craftsman is o artesão.

The word, taken apart.
artesanato is built on artesão "craftsman", which is usually traced to Italian artigiano; that word goes back to Latin ars "skill, craft" (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 4 of 5.
o barro — clay.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for to collect, then the Portuguese for handicrafts.

You'll want to know: o barro.
o barro — "clay".
As crianças fizeram pratos de barro. The children made plates out of clay.
The famous painted roosters are made of barro.

The word, taken apart.
barro comes from Vulgar Latin barrum "clay, mud" (EtymDB), probably a word from a language spoken in Iberia before Latin.
