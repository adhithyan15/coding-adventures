Sanskrit, chapter 315: A Blast.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
विस्फोटः (visphoṭaḥ) — an explosion.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for honour, then the Sanskrit for congratulates.

You'll want to know: विस्फोटः.
विस्फोटः — visphoṭaḥ — "an explosion".
प्रातः नगरे विस्फोटः अभवत्। prātaḥ nagare visphoṭaḥ abhavat. There was an explosion in the city in the morning.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
विस्फोटः (visphoṭaḥ) is built from vi and the root √sphuṭ, "to burst", as in sphuṭati; MW gives visphoṭa as cracking, crashing, and an explosion is its modern use (MW).

[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: आकस्मिकः.
आकस्मिकः — ākasmikaḥ — "sudden; unexpected".
आकस्मिकात् शब्दात् बालाः भीताः। ākasmikāt śabdāt bālāḥ bhītāḥ. The children are frightened by the sudden noise.

The word, taken apart.
आकस्मिकः (ākasmikaḥ) is built on akasmāt, "without a cause, suddenly" (from a, "not", and kasmāt, "from what?"); MW gives ākasmika as causeless, unforeseen, sudden (MW).

Lesson 4 of 5.
उद्विजते (udvijate) — is alarmed; starts in fear.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for an echo, then the Sanskrit for unexpected.

You'll want to know: उद्विजते.
उद्विजते — udvijate — "is alarmed; starts in fear".
शब्दात् सर्वे उद्विजन्ते। śabdāt sarve udvijante. Everyone is alarmed by the noise.
Past (he …): सः शब्दात् उद्विग्नवान्, saḥ śabdāt udvignavān.

The word, taken apart.
उद्विजते (udvijate) is built from ud and the root √vij, "to start, move with a jerk", as in vega; MW gives ud-vij as to shudder, tremble, start and fear (MW).
