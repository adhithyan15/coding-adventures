## You'll want to know: ડાકુ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C290-DAKU290-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ડાકુ** — *ḍāku* — "a bandit".

> જૂના વખતમાં આ જંગલમાં ડાકુ રહેતા હતા.
>
> *jūnā vakhatmā̃ ā jangalmā̃ ḍāku rahetā hatā.*
>
> In the old days bandits lived in this forest.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

comes from *\*ḍākka*, "robber, robbery", a form CDIAL reconstructs; CDIAL lists Gujarati *ḍāko*, an attack by robbers, in the same entry (CDIAL 5543). A **ડાકુ** belongs to a gang that robs on the roads.
