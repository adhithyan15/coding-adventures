## You'll want to know: shūshu
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C272-KIN272-04]; assesses=[] -->

*shūshu* — "uncle; father's younger brother".

> *wǒ shūshu shì diàngōng* — My uncle is an electrician.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*shūshu* is built from *shū*, said twice, the second time with no tone. It is inherited from Middle Chinese *syuwk* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'a father's youngest brother'.
