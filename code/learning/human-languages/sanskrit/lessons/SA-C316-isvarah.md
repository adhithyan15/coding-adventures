## You'll want to know: ईश्वरः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C316-SAMRAT316-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ईश्वरः** — *īśvaraḥ* — "a lord; a ruler".

> सम्राट् सर्वस्य देशस्य ईश्वरः अस्ति।
>
> *samrāṭ sarvasya deśasya īśvaraḥ asti.*
>
> The emperor is lord of the whole country.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ईश्वरः** (*īśvaraḥ*) comes from the root √īś, "to own, be master of"; MW gives *īśvara* as a master, lord, prince, king, and also God (MW).
