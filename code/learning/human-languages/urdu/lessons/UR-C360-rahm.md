## You'll want to know: رحم
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C360-RAHM360-01]; assesses=[] -->

**رحم** — *rahm* — "mercy; pity".

> جج نے ملزم پر رحم کیا۔
>
> *jaj ne mulzim par rahm kiyā.*
>
> The judge showed the accused mercy.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**رحم** (*rahm*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *raḥm*, "tenderness, compassion".
