## You'll want to know: たてかえる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C359-SHAKKIN359-03]; assesses=[] -->

**たてかえる** — *tatekaeru* — "to pay for someone (to be paid back)".

> **さいふを わすれたら、ともだちが たてかえるでしょう。** — *saifu o wasuretara, tomodachi ga tatekaeru deshō* — "If you forget your purse, a friend will probably pay for you."

Polite **たてかえます**, *tatekaemasu*; an ichidan verb. Past (polite): **あねが りょうきんを たてかえました。**, *ane ga ryōkin o tatekaemashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**たてかえる** (*tatekaeru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese: it is built from *tate*, "setting up (tateru)" and *kaeru*, "to exchange", and its kanji were attached later as kun'yomi (JMdict).

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
