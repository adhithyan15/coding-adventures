## You'll want to know: बेलना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C520-AATA520-04]; assesses=[] -->

**बेलना** — *belnā* — "to roll out (dough)".

> मीरा ने चकले पर गोल रोटी बेली।
>
> *Mīrā ne cakle par gol roṭī belī.*
>
> Mira rolled out a round roti on the board.

Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने पराठा बेला, *usne parāṭhā belā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**बेलना** (*belnā*) is a tadbhava, inherited from Sanskrit *vēllayati*, "causes to shake, kneads" (CDIAL 12121). CDIAL gives the Hindi verb as "to roll out".
