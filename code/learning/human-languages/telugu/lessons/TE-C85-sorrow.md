## You'll want to know: బాధ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C85-FEEL-05]; assesses=[] -->

**బాధ** (*bādha*) — "sorrow, distress".

**నాకు బాధగా ఉంది** — "I feel sad". It also covers the ache behind worry, so it answers
*మీరు ఎలా ఉన్నారు?* honestly on a bad day.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

An honest answer to how are you, when the answer is not బాగా.
