# ಪ್ರದರ್ಶಿಸು (pradarśisu) — to perform; to put on show

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[KA-LEX-C536-NATAKA536-02, KA-LEX-C536-NATAKA536-03] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Kannada for theatre, then the Kannada for a mask.

## You'll want to know: ಪ್ರದರ್ಶಿಸು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C536-NATAKA536-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ಪ್ರದರ್ಶಿಸು** — *pradarśisu* — "to perform; to put on show".

> ಮಕ್ಕಳು ವೇದಿಕೆಯ ಮೇಲೆ ಒಂದು ಸಣ್ಣ ನಾಟಕ ಪ್ರದರ್ಶಿಸಿದರು.
>
> *makkaḷu vēdikeya mēle ondu saṇṇa nāṭaka pradarśisidaru.*
>
> The children performed a short play on the stage.

Past (I …): ಪ್ರದರ್ಶಿಸಿದೆ, *pradarśiside*.

Also to exhibit: ಚಿತ್ರಗಳನ್ನು ಪ್ರದರ್ಶಿಸು. ಪ್ರದರ್ಶನ is a show.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಪ್ರದರ್ಶಿಸು** (*pradarśisu*) is built on Sanskrit *pradarśana*, "a showing, look, appearance" (Monier-Williams), with the verb ending -*isu*.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
