## You'll want to know: おえる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C167-DONE167-03]; assesses=[] -->

**おえる** — *oeru* — "to finish (something)".

> **あした しごとを おえます。** — *ashita shigoto o oemasu* — "I will finish the work tomorrow."

Polite **おえます**, *oemasu*; an ichidan verb.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**おえる** (*oeru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, and built on the same root as *owaru*, "to end"; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
