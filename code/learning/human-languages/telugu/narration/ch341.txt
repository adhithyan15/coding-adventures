6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
ముఖ్యాంశాలు (mukhyāṁśālu) — the main points; highlights.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for in the end, then the Telugu for to wake up.

You'll want to know: ముఖ్యాంశాలు.
ముఖ్యాంశాలు — mukhyāṁśālu — "the main points; highlights".
రేడియోలో వార్తల ముఖ్యాంశాలు చెప్పారు. rēḍiyōlō vārtala mukhyāṁśālu ceppāru. The radio gave the main points of the news.
ముఖ్య, chief, and అంశాలు, points: also the headlines read at the start of a bulletin. On election night the ముఖ్యాంశాలు (mukhyāṁśālu) are all about the ఓటు.

The word, taken apart.
ముఖ్యాంశాలు (mukhyāṁśālu) is a compound borrowed from Sanskrit: mukhya, "chief" (CDIAL 10174), and aṁśa, "a share, a part" (CDIAL 2).

Lesson 2 of 6.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to wake up, then the Telugu for the main points.

You'll want to know: సంపాదకుడు.
సంపాదకుడు — sampādakuḍu — "an editor".
సంపాదకుడు ఆ శీర్షిక మార్చాడు. sampādakuḍu ā śīrṣika mārcāḍu. The editor changed that headline.
Plural: సంపాదకులు, sampādakulu.
The person who puts a paper together each day.

The word, taken apart.
సంపాదకుడు (sampādakuḍu) is built on Sanskrit sampādaka, "producing, bringing about" (Monier-Williams), with the Telugu ending -uḍu: the one who brings the paper about.

చాలా మంది పాఠకులు ఉత్తరాలు రాశారు. cālā mandi pāṭhakulu uttarālu rāśāru. Many readers wrote letters.
Plural: పాఠకులు, pāṭhakulu.
Built on the root of పాఠం, a lesson: పాఠకుడు (pāṭhakuḍu), one who reads.

The word, taken apart.
పాఠకుడు (pāṭhakuḍu) is borrowed from Sanskrit pāṭhaka, "a reader, a teacher" (CDIAL 8036), from paṭh, "to read aloud", which also gives పాఠం, a lesson.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for an editorial, then the Telugu for a reader.

You'll want to know: ధ్రువీకరించు.
ధ్రువీకరించు — dhruvīkariñcu — "to certify; to confirm".
పోలీసులు ఆ వార్తను ధ్రువీకరించారు. pōlīsulu ā vārtanu dhruvīkariñcāru. The police confirmed the news.
Past (I …): ధ్రువీకరించాను, dhruvīkariñcānu.
Built on ధ్రువ, fixed or firm: to make a report firm. Print often spells it ధృవీకరించు.

The word, taken apart.
ధ్రువీకరించు (dhruvīkariñcu) is built on Sanskrit dhruva, "fixed, firm" (CDIAL 6892), with -īkariñcu, "to make into".
