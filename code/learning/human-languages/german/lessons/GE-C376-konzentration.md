## You'll want to know: die Konzentration
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C376-LERNEN-04]; assesses=[] -->

**die Konzentration** — "concentration".

> Nach zwei Stunden lässt meine Konzentration nach.
>
> *After two hours my concentration fades.*

No plural in this sense. *Nachlassen* — to ease off, to fade.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Konzentration* goes with *konzentrieren*, from French *concentrer* (EtymDB), built on *centre*, from Latin *centrum* "the middle point" (Lewis & Short).
