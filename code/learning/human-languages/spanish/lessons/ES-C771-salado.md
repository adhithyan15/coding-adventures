## You'll want to know: salado
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C771-SABOR-03]; assesses=[] -->

**salado** — "salty; savoury".

> No me gusta la sopa porque está muy salada.
>
> *I don't like the soup because it is very salty.*

In Spain *salado* can also mean witty or charming; in parts of Latin America, unlucky.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*salado* is built on the participle of *salar* 'to salt', from *sal*, which is inherited from Latin *sāl* 'salt' (Lewis & Short).
