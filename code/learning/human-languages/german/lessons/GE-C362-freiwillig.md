## You'll want to know: freiwillig
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C362-EHRENAMT-03]; assesses=[] -->

**freiwillig** — "voluntary, of your own free will".

> Niemand hat ihn gezwungen, er hat es freiwillig gemacht.
>
> *Nobody forced him; he did it of his own free will.*

*Der Freiwillige, die Freiwillige* — the volunteer.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*freiwillig* joins *frei* "free", Proto-Germanic *frijaz* (EtymDB), to *Wille* "will", with *-ig*: with a free will.
