Urdu, chapter 400: Far from Home.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
وطن (vatan) — a homeland.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for forbidden, then the Urdu for to put on board.

You'll want to know: وطن.
وطن — vatan — "a homeland".
میرا وطن یہاں سے دور ہے۔ merā vatan yahāṅ se dūr hai. My homeland is a long way from here.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
وطن (vatan) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic waṭan, "a dwelling place, a homeland".

Lesson 2 of 5.
پردیس (pardes) — a foreign land.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for to put on board, then the Urdu for a homeland.

You'll want to know: پردیس.
پردیس — pardes — "a foreign land".
میری بہن پردیس میں کام کرتی ہے۔ merī bahan pardes meṅ kām kartī hai. My sister works abroad.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
پردیس (pardes) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin pares, "a foreign country", under Sanskrit paradeśa (CDIAL 7799a), built from para, "other", and deśa, "a country".

Lesson 4 of 5.
گزارنا (guzārnā) — to spend (time).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a foreign land, then the Urdu for a stranger.

You'll want to know: گزارنا.
گزارنا — guzārnā — "to spend (time)".
سارا نے پردیس میں دو سال گزارے۔ sārā ne pardes meṅ do sāl guzāre. Sara spent two years abroad.
Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے وہاں ایک ہفتہ گزارا, us ne vahāṅ ek haftā guzārā.

The word, taken apart.
گزارنا (guzārnā) is usually traced to Persian guzār, "a passing"; گزشتہ, "last, previous", is a time that has passed.

Lesson 5 of 5.
واپسی (vāpasī) — a return; the way back.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

ہماری واپسی اگلے ہفتے ہے۔ hamārī vāpasī agle hafte hai. We come back next week.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
واپسی (vāpasī) is built on vāpas, "back", usually traced to Persian vāpas, with the ending -ī.
