## You'll want to know: વારસો
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C500-VARSO500-01]; assesses=[] -->

**વારસો** — *vārso* — "an inheritance; a heritage".

> દાદાએ અમને પુસ્તકોનો વારસો આપ્યો.
>
> *dādāe amne pustakono vārso āpyo.*
>
> Grandfather left us a heritage of books.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**વારસો** (*vārso*) is usually traced to Arabic *wāriṯ*, "heir", which reached Gujarati through Persian *wāris*.
