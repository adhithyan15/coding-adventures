## You'll want to know: સચેત
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C372-VIMO372-04]; assesses=[] -->

**સચેત** — *sacet* — "alert".

> ભીડમાં સચેત રહો, નહીંતર પાકીટ ખોવાઈ જશે.
>
> *bhīḍmā̃ sacet raho, nahī̃tar pākīṭ khovāī jaśe.*
>
> Stay alert in the crowd, or you will lose your wallet.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**સચેત** (*sacet*) is usually traced to Sanskrit *sacetas*, "conscious, intelligent" (Monier-Williams), built from *sa-*, "with", and *cetas*, "mind": with one's mind awake to what goes on.

One more word for saying what happens if.
