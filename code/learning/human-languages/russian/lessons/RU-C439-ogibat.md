## You'll want to know: огибать
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C439-RAZVILKA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**огибать** (*ogibát*) — "to go round, to skirt".

> Дорога огибает озеро.
>
> *Doróga ogibáyet ózero.*
>
> The road goes round the lake.

Imperfective; perfective *обогнуть* (*обогну, обогнёшь*).

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*огибать* is built from *о-* 'round' and *-гибать*, on the root of *гнуть* 'to bend', which is inherited from Proto-Slavic *gŭnǫti* (EtymDB).
