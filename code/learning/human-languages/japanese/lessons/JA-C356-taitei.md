## You'll want to know: たいてい
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C356-JOUKEN356-04]; assesses=[] -->

**たいてい** — *taitei* — "in most cases; usually".

> **あめが ふったら、たいてい うちに います。** — *ame ga futtara, taitei uchi ni imasu* — "When it rains, I usually stay at home."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**たいてい** (*taitei*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *tai*, "large" (Middle Chinese *dajH*) and *tei*, "reach" (*tejX*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription). JMdict notes that it is usually written in kana.
