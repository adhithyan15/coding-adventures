Kannada, chapter 359: The Town Festival.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಉತ್ಸವ (utsava) — a public festival; a celebration.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for bail, then the Kannada for to confess.

You'll want to know: ಉತ್ಸವ.
ಉತ್ಸವ — utsava — "a public festival; a celebration".
ಊರಿನ ಉತ್ಸವಕ್ಕೆ ಹಳ್ಳಿಗಳಿಂದ ಜನ ಬಂದರು. ūrina utsavakke haḷḷigaḷinda jana bandaru. People came from the villages to the town festival.
Plural: ಉತ್ಸವಗಳು, utsavagaḷu.
A public festival, often of a temple or a town; ಹಬ್ಬ is the family festival.

The word, taken apart.
ಉತ್ಸವ (utsava) is borrowed from Sanskrit utsava, "an enterprise", and later "a festival" (CDIAL 1876).

Lesson 2 of 5.
ಮೆರವಣಿಗೆ (meravaṇige) — a procession.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to confess, then the Kannada for a public festival.

You'll want to know: ಮೆರವಣಿಗೆ.
ಮೆರವಣಿಗೆ — meravaṇige — "a procession".
ಮೆರವಣಿಗೆ ಮುಖ್ಯ ರಸ್ತೆಯಲ್ಲಿ ನಿಧಾನವಾಗಿ ಸಾಗಿತು. meravaṇige mukhya rasteyalli nidhānavāgi sāgitu. The procession moved slowly along the main road.
Plural: ಮೆರವಣಿಗೆಗಳು, meravaṇigegaḷu.
A procession "moves on": ಮೆರವಣಿಗೆ (meravaṇige) ಸಾಗಿತು.

The word, taken apart.
ಮೆರವಣಿಗೆ (meravaṇige) is native Dravidian: DEDR 5074 lists Tamil meravaṇai, "a procession", with Kannada merugu, "to shine", and mere, "to parade".

Lesson 3 of 5.
ಘೋಷವಾಕ್ಯ (ghōṣavākya) — a slogan.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a public festival, then the Kannada for a procession.

You'll want to know: ಘೋಷವಾಕ್ಯ.
ಘೋಷವಾಕ್ಯ — ghōṣavākya — "a slogan".
ಮೆರವಣಿಗೆಯಲ್ಲಿ ಜನ ಘೋಷವಾಕ್ಯ ಕೂಗಿದರು. meravaṇigeyalli jana ghōṣavākya kūgidaru. People shouted slogans in the procession.
Plural: ಘೋಷವಾಕ್ಯಗಳು, ghōṣavākyagaḷu.
ಘೋಷ, a cry, and ವಾಕ್ಯ, a sentence.

The word, taken apart.
ಘೋಷವಾಕ್ಯ (ghōṣavākya) is borrowed from Sanskrit, a modern compound of ghoṣa, "a noise, a cry" (Monier-Williams), and vākya, "speech, words" (CDIAL 11468).

ಮೆರೆಸು — meresu — "to parade; to carry in procession".
ಜನರು ಗೆದ್ದ ಆಟಗಾರನನ್ನು ಬೀದಿಯಲ್ಲಿ ಮೆರೆಸಿದರು. janaru gedda āṭagāranannu bīdiyalli meresidaru. The people paraded the winning player through the streets.
Past (I …): ಮೆರೆಸಿದೆ, mereside.
Someone honoured is carried or led through the streets with music.

The word, taken apart.
ಮೆರೆಸು (meresu) is native Dravidian: the causative of mere, "to shine, to show off", which DEDR 5074 lists with Kannada merugu, "to shine".

Lesson 5 of 5.
ಧ್ವಜ (dhvaja) — a flag; a banner.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a slogan, then the Kannada for to parade.

You'll want to know: ಧ್ವಜ.
ಧ್ವಜ — dhvaja — "a flag; a banner".
ಉತ್ಸವದ ದಿನ ಎಲ್ಲ ಮನೆಗಳ ಮೇಲೆ ಧ್ವಜ ಹಾರುತ್ತಿತ್ತು. utsavada dina ella manegaḷa mēle dhvaja hāruttittu. On the festival day a flag flew on every house.
Plural: ಧ್ವಜಗಳು, dhvajagaḷu.
A flag is "hoisted": ಧ್ವಜ (dhvaja) ಹಾರಿಸು. ರಾಷ್ಟ್ರಧ್ವಜ is the national flag.

The word, taken apart.
ಧ್ವಜ (dhvaja) is borrowed from Sanskrit dhvaja, "a banner, a flag, a standard" (Monier-Williams).
