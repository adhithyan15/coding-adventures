## You'll want to know: ঘা
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C475-KHOTO475-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ঘা** — *ghā* — "a sore; an ulcer".

> অরুণের হাতে একটা ঘা হল।
>
> *ôruner hāte ekṭā ghā hôlô.*
>
> Arun got a sore on his hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ঘা** (*ghā*) is listed by CDIAL for Bengali, as a wound or sore, under Sanskrit *ghāta*, "a blow" (CDIAL 4460).
