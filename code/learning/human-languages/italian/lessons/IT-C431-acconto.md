## You'll want to know: l'acconto
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C431-CAPARRA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**l'acconto** — "the advance, the down payment".

> Ho pagato un acconto e il resto all'arrivo.
>
> *I paid an advance and the rest on arrival.*

*In acconto* — on account.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*acconto* is built from *a* 'towards' and *conto* 'account', which is inherited from Late Latin *computus*, from *computāre* 'to reckon', built on *putāre* (Lewis & Short).
