## You'll want to know: કુસ્તી
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C259-MATCH259-05]; assesses=[] -->

**કુસ્તી** — *kustī* — "wrestling".

> **મારા ભાઈએ ગામમાં કુસ્તી જોઈ.** — *mārā bhāīe gāmmā̃ kustī joī* — "My brother watched wrestling in the village."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**કુસ્તી** (*kustī*) is a loan, usually traced to Persian *kushtī*, "wrestling". CDIAL has no entry for it.
