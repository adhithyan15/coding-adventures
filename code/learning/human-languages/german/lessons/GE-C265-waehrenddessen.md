## You'll want to know: währenddessen
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C265-INZWISCHEN-03]; assesses=[] -->

**währenddessen** — "in the meantime, while that was going on".

> Sie telefonierte. Währenddessen kochte das Wasser.
>
> *She was on the phone. In the meantime the water boiled.*

Built on *während*. As an adverb, the verb comes second after it.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*währenddessen* joins *während* "during", the present participle of *währen* "to last" (EtymDB), and *dessen* "of that": while that lasted.
