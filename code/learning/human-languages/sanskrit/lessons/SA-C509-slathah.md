## You'll want to know: श्लथः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C509-JARJARA509-02]; assesses=[] -->

**श्लथः** — *ślathaḥ* — "slack; loose".

> द्वारस्य कीलः श्लथः अस्ति।
>
> *dvārasya kīlaḥ ślathaḥ asti.*
>
> The nail in the door is loose.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**श्लथः** (*ślathaḥ*) comes from the root √ślath, "to slacken, to become loose"; MW gives *ślatha* as loose, relaxed, untied (MW).
