## You'll want to know: باری
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C533-JUA533-03]; assesses=[] -->

**باری** — *bārī* — "a turn (in a game); one's turn".

> اگر آپ کی باری ہے تو پانسہ پھینکیے۔
>
> *agar āp kī bārī hai to pāṅsā pheṅkiye.*
>
> If it's your turn, throw the die.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**باری** (*bārī*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *bārī*, "a turn, a time", under Sanskrit *vāra* (CDIAL 11547).
