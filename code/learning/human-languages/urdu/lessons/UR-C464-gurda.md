## You'll want to know: گردہ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C464-ORGAN464-02]; assesses=[] -->

**گردہ** — *gurdā* — "a kidney".

> انسان کے دو گردے ہوتے ہیں۔
>
> *insān ke do gurde hote haiṅ.*
>
> A person has two kidneys.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**گردہ** (*gurdā*) is usually traced to Persian *gurda*, "kidney".
