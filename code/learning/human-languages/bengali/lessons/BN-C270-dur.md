# দূর (dūr) — far; a distance

## You'll want to know: দূর
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C270-DUR-01]; assesses=[BN-LEX-C84-TOWN84-03, BN-LEX-C81-PLACE81-01, BN-LEX-C66-NATURE66-03, BN-GRAMMAR-C264-PAST-PERSON-01] -->

**দূর** — *dūr* — "far; a distance".

> **বাজার আমাদের বাড়ি থেকে দূরে।**
>
> *bājār āmāder bāṛi theke dūre.* — "The market is far from our house."

**দূরে**, with **-এ**, is "far away, at a distance", the opposite of **কাছে**. **থেকে** gives the point it is measured from. **দূর থেকে** is "from far away": **নদীটা দূর থেকে দেখলাম** (*nôdīṭā dūr theke dekhlām*), "I saw the river from far away".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**দূর** (*dūr*) is a tatsama: Sanskrit *dūra*, "distant", and as a noun "distance" (CDIAL 6495). CDIAL lists the Bengali word under it as *dur*: Bengali does not lengthen the vowel, and the long **ূ** is the Sanskrit spelling kept.
