## You'll want to know: しっけ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C354-TENKI354-03]; assesses=[] -->

**しっけ** — *shikke* — "dampness; humidity".

> **つゆに なったら、しっけが おおく なるでしょう。** — *tsuyu ni nattara, shikke ga ōku naru deshō* — "When the rainy season comes, it will probably get damp."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**しっけ** (*shikke*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *shitsu*, "damp" (Middle Chinese *syip*) and *ke*, "spirit" (*khj+jH*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription). The two join as *shik-ke*.
