## You'll want to know: partus, partūs
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C323-PARTUS-02]; assesses=[] -->

**partus, partūs** — "a birth; offspring".

> Partus facilis erat, et māter laeta.
>
> *The birth was easy, and the mother happy.*

Masculine, 4th declension, from *pariō*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**partus** is built on *pariō*, "to give birth" (EtymDB): a bearing, and what is born.
