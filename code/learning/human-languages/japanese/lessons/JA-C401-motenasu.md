## You'll want to know: もてなす
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C401-CHOUSHOKU401-05]; assesses=[] -->

**もてなす** — *motenasu* — "to entertain guests; to look after visitors".

> **りょかんは きゃくを ていねいに もてなしました。** — *ryokan wa kyaku o teinei ni motenashimashita* — "The inn looked after its guests with great care."

Polite **もてなします**, *motenashimasu*; a godan verb. Past (polite): **そぼが きゃくを もてなしました。**, *sobo ga kyaku o motenashimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**もてなす** (*motenasu*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese (JMdict).
