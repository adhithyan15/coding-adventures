Kannada, chapter 331: Long Ago.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಹಿಂದೊಮ್ಮೆ (hindomme) — once before.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for the deceased, then the Kannada for a mishap.

You'll want to know: ಹಿಂದೊಮ್ಮೆ.
ಹಿಂದೊಮ್ಮೆ — hindomme — "once before".
ನಾನು ಹಿಂದೊಮ್ಮೆ ಈ ಕೋಟೆಯನ್ನು ನೋಡಿದ್ದೆ. nānu hindomme ī kōṭeyannu nōḍidde. I had seen this fort once before.
ಹಿಂದೆ, before, and ಒಮ್ಮೆ, once.

The word, taken apart.
ಹಿಂದೊಮ್ಮೆ (hindomme) is a native Dravidian compound: hinde, "behind, in the past", from pin (DEDR 4205), and ಒಮ್ಮೆ, "once".

Lesson 2 of 5.
ಅಂದು (andu) — on that day; then.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a mishap, then the Kannada for once before.

You'll want to know: ಅಂದು.
ಅಂದು — andu — "on that day; then".
ಅಂದು ಊರಿನಲ್ಲಿ ದೊಡ್ಡ ಹಬ್ಬ ಇತ್ತು. andu ūrinalli doḍḍa habba ittu. On that day there was a big festival in the village.
ಇಂದು is today, ಅಂದು (andu) is that day. Stories often begin ಅಂದು (andu) …

The word, taken apart.
ಅಂದು (andu) is native Dravidian: it is built on the pointing base a-, "that, yonder" (DEDR 1), as ಇಂದು, "today", is built on i-, "this".

Lesson 3 of 5.
ಬಹುಕಾಲ (bahukāla) — a long time.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for once before, then the Kannada for on that day.

You'll want to know: ಬಹುಕಾಲ.
ಬಹುಕಾಲ — bahukāla — "a long time".
ಅಜ್ಜ ಬಹುಕಾಲ ಆ ಹಳ್ಳಿಯಲ್ಲಿ ಇದ್ದರು. ajja bahukāla ā haḷḷiyalli iddaru. Grandfather lived in that village for a long time.
A formal phrase; in talk people say ತುಂಬಾ ಸಮಯ.

The word, taken apart.
ಬಹುಕಾಲ (bahukāla) is borrowed from Sanskrit, a compound of bahu, "much, many" (CDIAL 9187), and kāla, "time" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 4 of 5.
ಪ್ರಾಚೀನ (prācīna) — ancient.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for on that day, then the Kannada for a long time.

You'll want to know: ಪ್ರಾಚೀನ.
ಪ್ರಾಚೀನ — prācīna — "ancient".
ಇದು ಒಂದು ಪ್ರಾಚೀನ ದೇವಸ್ಥಾನ. idu ondu prācīna dēvasthāna. This is an ancient temple.
It stands before a noun as it is: ಪ್ರಾಚೀನ (prācīna) ಕಾಲ, ancient times.

The word, taken apart.
ಪ್ರಾಚೀನ (prācīna) is borrowed from Sanskrit prācīna, "turned to the front, eastern" (CDIAL 8920), which also came to mean "former, ancient".

Lesson 5 of 5.
ಯುಗ (yuga) — an era; an age.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a long time, then the Kannada for ancient.

You'll want to know: ಯುಗ.
ಯುಗ — yuga — "an era; an age".
ಇದು ಕಂಪ್ಯೂಟರ್ ಯುಗ. idu kampyūṭar yuga. This is the computer age.
Plural: ಯುಗಗಳು, yugagaḷu.
ಹೊಸ ಯುಗ (yuga) is a new era. In talk ಯುಗ (yuga) can also mean a very long time.

The word, taken apart.
ಯುಗ (yuga) is borrowed from Sanskrit yuga, "a yoke, a pair" (CDIAL 10482), which also meant "an age of the world" (Monier-Williams).
