## You'll want to know: la frizione
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C411-COFANO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**la frizione** — "the clutch; the rub, the rubdown".

> Premi la frizione e cambia marcia.
>
> *Press the clutch and change gear.*

*Frizione* also means friction, and a rubdown with lotion.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*frizione* is a learned borrowing from Latin *frictiō* 'rubbing' (EtymDB), from *fricāre* 'to rub': the clutch plates rub together.
