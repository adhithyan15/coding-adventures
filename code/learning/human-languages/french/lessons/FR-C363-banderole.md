## You'll want to know: la banderole
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C363-MANIFESTATION-04]; assesses=[] -->

**la banderole** — "the banner".

> Les manifestants portent une grande banderole.
>
> *The demonstrators are carrying a big banner.*

A long strip of cloth with words on it.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*banderole* was borrowed from Italian *banderuola* "a little flag" (EtymDB), a small form of *bandiera* "flag".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.
