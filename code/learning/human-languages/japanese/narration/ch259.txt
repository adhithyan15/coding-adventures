Japanese, chapter 259: Comparing, and How Long.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
より (yori) — than.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say "fast" and "a plane". (hayai, hikōki.) Then "you had better rest". (yasunda hō ga ii desu.) Its ほう is back today.

You'll want to know: A は B より.
ひこうきは でんしゃより はやいです。 hikōki wa densha yori hayai desu. — "A plane is faster than a train."
より (yori), "than", follows the thing you measure against. The adjective does not change: はやい is "faster" here.
なつは ふゆより あついです。 natsu wa fuyu yori atsui desu. — "Summer is hotter than winter."

You'll want to know: B より A の ほうが.
To put the winner last, の ほうが (no hō ga), "the side of", marks it:
ふゆより なつの ほうが すきです。 fuyu yori natsu no hō ga suki desu. — "I like summer more than winter."
やまより うみの ほうが すきです。 yama yori umi no hō ga suki desu. — "I like the sea more than the mountains."

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: "I like the sea more than the mountains" — yama yori umi no hō ga suki desu]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: "I like the river more than the mountains" — yama yori kawa no hō ga suki desu]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: "I like the town more than the mountains" — yama yori machi no hō ga suki desu]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[question — say your answer, then pause 20 seconds]
Say that a plane is faster than a train.

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
"Than"? (より (yori).) Does はやい change for "faster"? (No.) "I like summer more than winter"? (ふゆより なつの ほうが すきです.)


Lesson 2 of 5.
どちら (dochira) — which (of two).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new word: say "I like the sea more than the mountains". (yama yori umi no hō ga suki desu.) Then "where" and "which". (doko, dono.)

You'll want to know: どちら.
どちら — dochira — "which" of two. Name the two with と, then ask:
なつと ふゆと どちらが すきですか。 natsu to fuyu to dochira ga suki desu ka. — "Which do you like better, summer or winter?"
なつの ほうが すきです。 natsu no hō ga suki desu. — "I like summer better."

The word, taken apart.
どちら (dochira) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese: the do- of どこ and どの, "which", with -chira, a direction ending. JMdict marks it as usually written in kana and glosses it "which one (esp. of two alternatives)" and "which way" (JMdict).

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: dochira]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: "Which do you like better, the sea or the mountains?" — umi to yama to dochira ga suki desu ka]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: "Which do you like better, tea or coffee?" — ocha to kōhī to dochira ga suki desu ka]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[question — say your answer, then pause 14 seconds]
Ask which someone likes better, summer or winter.

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
"Which", of two? (どちら, dochira.) And the answer starts? (With the winner and の ほうが.)


Lesson 3 of 5.
いちばん (ichiban) — the most; number one.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new word: ask "which do you like better, summer or winter?" (natsu to fuyu to dochira ga suki desu ka.) Then say "summer is hotter than winter". (natsu wa fuyu yori atsui desu.) And "one". (ichi.)

You'll want to know: いちばん.
いちばん — ichiban — "the most", "number one". It goes before the word it raises to the top, with more than two things to choose from:
なつが いちばん すきです。 natsu ga ichiban suki desu. — "I like summer best."
なかで (naka de), "inside" with で, names the group the choice is made in: "among the seasons", "in my family".
きせつの なかで なつが いちばん すきです。 kisetsu no naka de natsu ga ichiban suki desu. — "Of all the seasons, I like summer best."
かぞくの なかで ちちが いちばん はやく おきます。 kazoku no naka de chichi ga ichiban hayaku okimasu. — "In my family my father gets up earliest."

The word, taken apart.
いちばん (ichiban) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are ichi, "one" (Middle Chinese 'jit) and ban, "number in a series" (phjon), so "number one" (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription). JMdict gives "number one; first" and "best; most" (JMdict).

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: ichiban]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: "I like spring best" — haru ga ichiban suki desu]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: "I like autumn best" — aki ga ichiban suki desu]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[question — say your answer, then pause 12 seconds]
Say that you like summer best.

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
"The most"? (いちばん, ichiban.) Two things to compare: どちら (dochira) or いちばん? (どちら (dochira); いちばん is for more than two.)


Lesson 4 of 5.
にじかんぐらい / くじから ごじまで (nijikan gurai / kuji kara goji made) — how long.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say "time, an hour" and "a minute". (jikan, fun.) Then "to take", of time. (kakaru.)

You'll want to know: 〜じかんぐらい.
くうこうまで にじかん かかります。 kūkō made nijikan kakarimasu. — "It takes two hours to the airport."
A number before じかん counts hours: にじかん (nijikan), two hours. Before ふん it counts minutes: ごふん (gofun), five minutes. ぐらい (gurai), "about", follows the time:
くうこうまで にじかんぐらい かかります。 kūkō made nijikan gurai kakarimasu. — "It takes about two hours to the airport."
えきまで ごふんぐらい あるきます。 eki made gofun gurai arukimasu. — "I walk about five minutes to the station."

You'll want to know: 〜から 〜まで.
から (kara) after a time or a place is "from", and まで is "until", "as far as":
くじから ごじまで はたらきます。 kuji kara goji made hatarakimasu. — "I work from nine to five."
げつようびから きんようびまで はたらきます。 getsuyōbi kara kin'yōbi made hatarakimasu. — "I work from Monday to Friday."

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: "I work from nine to five" — kuji kara goji made hatarakimasu]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: "I work from eight to five" — hachiji kara goji made hatarakimasu]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: "I work from ten to five" — jūji kara goji made hatarakimasu]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[question — say your answer, then pause 20 seconds]
Say that it takes about two hours to the airport.

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
"About two hours"? (にじかんぐらい.) "Five minutes"? (ごふん.) "From Monday to Friday"? (げつようびから きんようびまで.)
