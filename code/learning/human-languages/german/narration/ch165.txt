German, chapter 165: People, Luck, Fun, Desire, An opinion.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
die Leute — people.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for a baby, then the German for a human being.

You'll want to know: die Leute.
die Leute — "people".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: die Leute]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: die Leute, once more]

[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does die Leute mean? ("People".) Say it once more.
