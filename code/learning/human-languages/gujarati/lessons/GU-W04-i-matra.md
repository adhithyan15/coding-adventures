# િ — short i sits before, sounds after

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-SCRIPT-INDEPENDENT-A-01, GU-SCRIPT-SHA-01] -->

Point to **અ** and **શ**. Say independent short *a*, then *sha*. Do not combine
them; today one small sign changes only the vowel on **શ**.

## Script — meet one form
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-SCRIPT-I-MATRA-01]; assesses=[] -->

Keep **િ** visible. It is the attached short-*i* sign. It is written to the left
of its consonant even though *i* is pronounced after that consonant. With **શ**,
it makes **શિ**, *shi*. Start with the upright stem and finish through the curl
that reaches toward the consonant.

## Writing — notice and trace
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-SCRIPT-I-MATRA-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: observe-trace -->

Finger-trace **િ** once, then air-trace it once. Notice the sign's left-side
place in **શિ**. Say *shi* only after the whole form is visible.

## Writing — copy with the model
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-SCRIPT-I-MATRA-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: guided-copy -->

Copy visible **શિ** once. Circle only the short-*i* sign and check that it sits
to the left of **શ**; repair its placement once if needed.

## Writing — recall after hiding
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-SCRIPT-I-MATRA-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: delayed-copy -->
<!-- hl-activity: {"id":"GU-W04-i-matra-recall","kind":"text","assesses":["GU-SCRIPT-I-MATRA-01"],"prompt":"Hide the model. Add short i to શ and write shi.","answer":"શિ","accepted":[],"feedback":{"correct":"શિ — the sign is seen before શ and pronounced after it.","incorrect":"Look once at શિ, hide it, and repair only the short-i sign's placement."},"response_seconds":20} -->

Hide the model. Add short *i* to **શ** and write *shi*. Uncover **શિ**, compare,
and stop after one repair.

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-SCRIPT-I-MATRA-01] -->

[PAUSE 3s] Is **િ** seen before or after its consonant? (**Before.**) Is it
pronounced before or after? (**After.**)
