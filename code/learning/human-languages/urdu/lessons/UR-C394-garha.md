## You'll want to know: گڑھا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C394-GARHA394-01]; assesses=[] -->

**گڑھا** — *gaṛhā* — "a pothole; a pit".

> اس سڑک میں بڑا گڑھا ہے۔
>
> *is saṛak meṅ baṛā gaṛhā hai.*
>
> There is a big pothole in this road.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**گڑھا** (*gaṛhā*) probably belongs with the family CDIAL files under *gaḍḍa*, "a hole, a pit", a form it reconstructs: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *gāṛā*, "a hole", there (CDIAL 3981), but not this form with *ṛh*.
