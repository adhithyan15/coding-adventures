## You'll want to know: běibian
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C117-COMPASS117-04]; assesses=[] -->

*běibian* — "the north; on the north side".

> *Běijīng zài Zhōngguó de běibian* — Beijing is in the north of China.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*běibian* is built from běi 'north' and 边 'side', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *pok* and *pen* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for asking where things are.
