## You'll want to know: rēng
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C103-USE103-03]; assesses=[] -->

*rēng* — "to throw; to throw away".

> *bú yào rēng wǒ de shū* — Don't throw my books away.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*rēng* is inherited from Middle Chinese *nying* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'to pull'. The regular change from *nying* gives réng, with a rising tone, not the level rēng of today, and throwing is not given there; how the word came to its sound and sense is not settled.
