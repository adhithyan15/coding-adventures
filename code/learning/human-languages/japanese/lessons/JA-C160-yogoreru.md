## You'll want to know: よごれる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C160-DIRT160-03]; assesses=[] -->

**よごれる** — *yogoreru* — "to get dirty".

> **こどもの ふくは よく よごれます。** — *kodomo no fuku wa yoku yogoremasu* — "Children's clothes often get dirty."

Polite **よごれます**, *yogoremasu*; an ichidan verb.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**よごれる** (*yogoreru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi, and its older origin is not settled (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
