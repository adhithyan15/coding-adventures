## You'll want to know: dīmicō, dīmicāre
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C339-PROELIUM-05]; assesses=[] -->

**dīmicō, dīmicāre** — "to fight it out, to struggle".

> Ambō exercitūs ācriter dīmicāvērunt.
>
> *Both armies fought it out fiercely.*

*dīmicō, dīmicāre, dīmicāvī, dīmicātum*. A hard, deciding fight.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**dīmicō** is built on *micō*, "to flash, to quiver", with *dis-* (EtymDB): perhaps first the flashing of swords to and fro.
