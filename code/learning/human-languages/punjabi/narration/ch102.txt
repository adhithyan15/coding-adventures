Punjabi, chapter 102: May, June, July, August, September.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ਮਈ (maī) — May.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for March, then the Punjabi for April.

You'll want to know: ਮਈ.
ਮਈ — maī — "May".

The word, taken apart.
ਮਈ (maī) is a loan from English May.

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: maī]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: maī, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say maī]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Punjabi for March, then the Punjabi for April, then say maī again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does ਮਈ (maī) mean? ("May".) Say it once more.
