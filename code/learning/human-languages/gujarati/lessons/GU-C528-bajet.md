## You'll want to know: બજેટ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C528-YOJVU528-02]; assesses=[] -->

**બજેટ** — *bajeṭ* — "a budget".

> જો બજેટ ઓછું હશે તો આપણે નાનો કાર્યક્રમ કરીશું.
>
> *jo bajeṭ ochũ haśe to āpaṇe nāno kāryakram karīśũ.*
>
> If the budget is small, we will hold a small event.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**બજેટ** (*bajeṭ*) is borrowed from English *budget*.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
