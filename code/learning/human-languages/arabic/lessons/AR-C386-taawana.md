## You'll want to know: تعاون
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C386-TAAWUN386-01]; assesses=[] -->

**تعاون** — *taʿāwana* — "to cooperate".

> تعاون الجيران لتنظيف الحديقة.
>
> *taʿāwana al-jīrān li-tanẓīf al-ḥadīqa.*
>
> The neighbours cooperated to clean the park.

Present: يتعاون, *yataʿāwan*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**تعاون** (*taʿāwana*) is built on the native Arabic root *ʿ-w-n*, "to help, to aid" (Lane). It is Form VI, to help one another: to cooperate.
