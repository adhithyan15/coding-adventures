## You'll want to know: o seguidor
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C400-PUBLICAR-03]; assesses=[] -->

**o seguidor** — "the follower".

> Ela tem dez mil seguidores.
>
> *She has ten thousand followers.*

Feminine *a seguidora*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*seguidor* is built on *seguir* "to follow", inherited from Latin *sequī* "to follow" (Lewis & Short), with *-dor*.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.
