## You'll want to know: protestar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C604-PROTESTA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**protestar** — "to protest; to complain".

> Los vecinos protestan por el ruido.
>
> *The neighbours are protesting about the noise.*

*Protestar por* or *contra* something.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*protestar* is a learned borrowing from Latin *protestari* 'to declare publicly', built on *testis* 'witness' (Lewis & Short).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.
