## You'll want to know: પસ્તાવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C381-PASTAVO381-01]; assesses=[] -->

**પસ્તાવું** — *pastāvũ* — "to regret".

> જો તમે આ તક ખોશો તો પછી પસ્તાશો.
>
> *jo tame ā tak khośo to pachī pastāśo.*
>
> If you lose this chance, you will regret it later.

Past (he …): તે પોતાની ભૂલ પર પસ્તાયો, *te potānī bhūl par pastāyo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**પસ્તાવું** (*pastāvũ*) CDIAL lists the Gujarati word under a reconstructed *paścōttāpa*, "repentance" (CDIAL 8010), which is built from *paścāt*, "after", and *tāpa*, "burning": the heat that comes afterwards.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
