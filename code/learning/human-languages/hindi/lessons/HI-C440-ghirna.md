## You'll want to know: घिरना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C440-GHIRNA440-02]; assesses=[] -->

**घिरना** — *ghirnā* — "to be surrounded; to gather round".

> बाढ़ में हमारा गाँव पानी से घिर गया।
>
> *bāṛh meṁ hamārā gā̃v pānī se ghir gayā.*
>
> In the flood our village was cut off by water.

Past (it …): वह मैदान पेड़ों से घिरा, *vah maidān peṛoṁ se ghirā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**घिरना** (*ghirnā*) goes back to *\*ghir*, "go round", a form CDIAL reconstructs and thinks may come from Dravidian (CDIAL 4474); it lists Hindi *ghirnā*, "to be surrounded".
