Arabic, chapter 524: Talks, turns and headquarters.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
فاوض (fāwaḍa) — to hold talks (with); to negotiate.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for an evaluation, then the Arabic for production.

You'll want to know: فاوض.
فاوض — fāwaḍa — "to hold talks (with); to negotiate".
فاوض العمال المدير على الأجر. fāwaḍa al-ʿummāl al-mudīr ʿalā al-ajr. The workers negotiated their pay with the manager.
Present: يفاوض, yufāwiḍ.
It takes على for the matter.

The word, taken apart.
فاوض (fāwaḍa) is built on the native Arabic root f-w-ḍ, "to entrust, to share in" (Lane). Form III fāwaḍa is to talk a matter through with someone.
