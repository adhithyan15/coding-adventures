## You'll want to know: યાદ કરવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C251-MEANING251-05]; assesses=[] -->

**યાદ કરવું** — *yād karvũ* — "to memorize, to recall".

> **હું રોજ દસ નવા શબ્દ યાદ કરું છું.** — *hũ roj das navā śabda yād karũ chũ* — "I memorize ten new words every day."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**યાદ કરવું** (*yād karvũ*) is built from *yād*, "memory", a loan usually traced to Persian *yād*, and the verb *karvũ*, "to do".
