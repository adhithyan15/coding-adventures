## You'll want to know: dévorer
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C520-LIBRAIRE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**dévorer** — "to devour".

> Ma fille dévore tous les romans de cette série.
>
> *My daughter devours every novel in this series.*

Of food or books: *dévorer un livre* — to read it eagerly.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*dévorer* is a learned borrowing from Latin *dēvorāre* "to swallow down", built on *vorāre* "to swallow" (Lewis & Short).
