## You'll want to know: こげる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C177-TASTE177-04]; assesses=[] -->

**こげる** — *kogeru* — "to get burnt; to scorch".

> **さかなが よく こげます。** — *sakana ga yoku kogemasu* — "The fish often gets burnt."

Polite **こげます**, *kogemasu*; an ichidan verb.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**こげる** (*kogeru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
