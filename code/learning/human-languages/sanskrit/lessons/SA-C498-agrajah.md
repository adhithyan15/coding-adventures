## You'll want to know: अग्रजः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C498-AGRAJA498-01]; assesses=[] -->

**अग्रजः** — *agrajaḥ* — "an elder brother".

> मम अग्रजः नगरे कार्यं करोति।
>
> *mama agrajaḥ nagare kāryaṃ karoti.*
>
> My elder brother works in the city.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अग्रजः** (*agrajaḥ*) is built from *agra*, "first, front", and *ja*, "born", from the root √jan; MW gives "the first-born, an elder brother" (MW).
