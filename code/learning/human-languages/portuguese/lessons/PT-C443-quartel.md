## You'll want to know: o quartel
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C443-SERVICOS-02]; assesses=[] -->

**o quartel** — "the barracks; the station (of firefighters)".

> O quartel dos bombeiros fica ao lado da escola.
>
> *The fire station is next to the school.*

*O quartel dos bombeiros* — the fire station.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*quartel* came from French *quartier* 'quarter, district' (EtymDB), from Latin *quartārius* 'a fourth part', on *quārtus* 'fourth'.
