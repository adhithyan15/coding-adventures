## You'll want to know: тормозить
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C403-RUL-03]; assesses=[] -->

**тормозить** (*tormozít*) — "to brake; to slow down".

> Перед школой водитель всегда тормозит.
>
> *Péred shkóloy vodítel vsegdá tormozít.*
>
> In front of the school the driver always brakes.

Imperfective; the perfective is *затормозить*. Present *торможу, тормозишь*: з becomes ж in the first person.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*тормозить* is built on *тормоз* 'a brake' with the verb ending *-ить*. *Тормоз* goes back to Greek *tórmos* 'a socket for a peg' (EtymDB).
