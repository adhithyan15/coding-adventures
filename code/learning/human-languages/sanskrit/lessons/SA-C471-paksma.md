## You'll want to know: पक्ष्म
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C471-MASTISKA471-04]; assesses=[] -->

**पक्ष्म** — *pakṣma* — "an eyelash".

> मम नेत्रे पक्ष्म अस्ति।
>
> *mama netre pakṣma asti.*
>
> There is an eyelash in my eye.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पक्ष्म** (*pakṣma*) MW records *pakṣman*, "the eyelashes"; MW gives no derivation, so its origin is uncertain (MW).
