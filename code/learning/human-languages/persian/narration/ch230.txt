Persian, chapter 230: North, South, East, Straight ahead, Opposite.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
شمال (shomâl) — north.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for any more, then the Persian for dawn.

You'll want to know: شمال.
شمال — shomâl — "north".

The word, taken apart.
shomâl is borrowed from Arabic šamāl, "north; the north wind". Its Arabic root is š-m-l (Lane).

Lesson 5 of 5.
روبرو (rubaru) — opposite.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for east, then the Persian for straight ahead.

You'll want to know: روبرو.
روبرو — rubaru — "opposite".

The word, taken apart.
rubaru is ru, "face", with be, "to", and ru again: face to face. All are native Persian; ru is usually traced to Middle Persian rōy.
