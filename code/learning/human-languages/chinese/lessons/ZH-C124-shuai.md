## You'll want to know: shuài
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C124-BUILD124-03]; assesses=[] -->

*shuài* — "handsome".

> *nǐ gēge hěn shuài* — Your brother is handsome.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*shuài* is inherited from Middle Chinese *srwijH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'a commander of troops'. The sense handsome is not given there, and how it came is not settled.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing things.
