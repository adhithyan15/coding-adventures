## You'll want to know: تحدى
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C345-MUWAJAHA345-01]; assesses=[] -->

**تحدى** — *taḥaddā* — "to challenge, to defy".

> تحدى الولد صديقه في السباق.
>
> *taḥaddā al-walad ṣadīqahu fī as-sibāq.*
>
> The boy challenged his friend to a race.

Present: يتحدى, *yataḥaddā*.

A challenge is تحد; plural تحديات.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**تحدى** (*taḥaddā*) is built on the native Arabic root *ḥ-d-w* (Lane), where *ḥadā* is to drive camels on with song, and Form V *taḥaddā* is "to vie with someone, to dare him".

## What you've built
