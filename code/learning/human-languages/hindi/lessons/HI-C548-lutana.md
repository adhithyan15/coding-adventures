## You'll want to know: लुटाना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C548-AVADHI548-05]; assesses=[] -->

**लुटाना** — *luṭānā* — "to squander; to give away freely".

> उसने अपने पैसे जुए में लुटा दिए।
>
> *Usne apne paise jue meṁ luṭā diye.*
>
> He squandered his money on gambling.

Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने पैसे लुटाए, *usne paise luṭāe*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**लुटाना** (*luṭānā*) is the causative of *lūṭnā*, "to plunder", which CDIAL derives from *\*luṭṭati*, a reconstructed form (CDIAL 11078): to let one's goods be carried off.
