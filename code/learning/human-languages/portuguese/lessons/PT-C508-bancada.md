## You'll want to know: a bancada
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C508-ARBITRO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**a bancada** — "stand (in a stadium)".

> Os adeptos cantavam na bancada.
>
> *The fans were singing in the stand.*

Also a kitchen worktop: *a bancada da cozinha*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*bancada* is built on *banco* "bench", which goes back to a Germanic word for a bench (EtymDB).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
