## You'll want to know: みらい
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C527-TIME527-02]; assesses=[] -->

**みらい** — *mirai* — "the distant future".

> **みらいは いまの まいにちに よります。** — *mirai wa ima no mainichi ni yorimasu* — "The future depends on how we live each day now."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**みらい** (*mirai*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *mi*, "not yet" and *rai*, "come" (Middle Chinese *loj*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
