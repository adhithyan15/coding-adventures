Hindi, chapter 514: Metal and Gold.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ठप्पा (ṭhappā) — a stamp, a die.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for to thread (a needle), then the Hindi for to come unstitched.

You'll want to know: ठप्पा.
ठप्पा — ṭhappā — "a stamp, a die".
अंगूठी पर सुनार का ठप्पा है। Aṅgūṭhī par sunār kā ṭhappā hai. The ring bears the goldsmith's stamp.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
ठप्पा (ṭhappā) is a tadbhava, inherited from ṭhapp, "strike, press", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 5495). CDIAL gives the Hindi word as "stamp, mould".

Lesson 2 of 5.
रेतना (retnā) — to file (metal or wood).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for to come unstitched, then the Hindi for a stamp, a die.

You'll want to know: रेतना.
रेतना — retnā — "to file (metal or wood)".
लोहार ने चाबी का किनारा रेता। Lohār ne cābī kā kinārā retā. The blacksmith filed the edge of the key.
Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने कील रेती, usne kīl retī.

The word, taken apart.
रेतना (retnā) is a tadbhava, inherited from Sanskrit rētra, "perfumed powder" (CDIAL 10816), the entry under which CDIAL lists Hindi ret, "sand, a file", and retnā, "to file".

Lesson 4 of 5.
चमकाना (camkānā) — to polish, to make shine.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for to file (metal or wood), then the Hindi for to set (a stone).

You'll want to know: चमकाना.
चमकाना — camkānā — "to polish, to make shine".
मीरा ने पुरानी चाँदी की थाली चमकाई। Mīrā ne purānī cā̃dī kī thālī camkāī. Mira polished the old silver platter.
Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने बर्तन चमकाए, usne bartan camkāe.

The word, taken apart.
चमकाना (camkānā) is the causative of camaknā, "to glitter", which CDIAL derives from camakka, a reconstructed form (CDIAL 4676): it means "to make glitter".

Lesson 5 of 5.
पिघलाना (pighlānā) — to melt (something).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for to set (a stone), then the Hindi for to polish, to make shine.

You'll want to know: पिघलाना.
पिघलाना — pighlānā — "to melt (something)".
सुनार ने आग पर चाँदी पिघलाई। Sunār ne āg par cā̃dī pighlāī. The goldsmith melted the silver over the fire.
Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने बर्फ़ पिघलाई, usne barf pighlāī.

The word, taken apart.
पिघलाना (pighlānā) is the causative of pighalnā, "to melt", which CDIAL files under praghirati, a reconstructed form, noting the older causative paghilānā (CDIAL 8486).
