## You'll want to know: శాపం
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C371-MAYA371-05]; assesses=[] -->

**శాపం** — *śāpaṁ* — "a curse".

> ఆ శాపం వల్ల రాకుమారుడు రాయిగా మారాడు.
>
> *ā śāpaṁ valla rākumāruḍu rāyigā mārāḍu.*
>
> Because of that curse, the prince turned to stone.

Plural: శాపాలు, *śāpālu*.

శాపం పెట్టు, to lay a curse.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**శాపం** (*śāpaṁ*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *śāpa*, "a curse, abuse" (CDIAL 12393).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
