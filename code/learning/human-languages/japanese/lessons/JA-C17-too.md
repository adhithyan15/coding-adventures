## You'll Want to Know — hear and say

> **とお** — *tō* — **ten things**

Two signs, both old, and **no つ at all**.

Nine words in this count carry the **つ** that does the counting. The tenth drops
it, and that is not a spelling quirk — it is the edge of the count. **とお** is
where the native series ends. There is no *eleven things* in it.

Above ten, Japanese has only the borrowed count and a counter word, which is
exactly the machinery the next chapter is for. **じゅう** goes on to
ninety-nine; **とお** goes nowhere.
