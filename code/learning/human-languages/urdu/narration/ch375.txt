Urdu, chapter 375: Very, Extremely.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
بہت (bahut) — very; a lot.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for reliance, then the Urdu for to insist.

You'll want to know: بہت.
بہت — bahut — "very; a lot".
اگر بہت سردی ہوئی تو ہم گھر پر رہیں گے۔ agar bahut sardī huī to ham ghar par raheṅ ge. If it is very cold, we will stay at home.

The word, taken apart.
بہت (bahut) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin bahut, "much, many", under Sanskrit bahutva, "abundance" (CDIAL 9190).

Lesson 2 of 5.
انتہائی (intihāī) — extreme; extremely.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for to insist, then the Urdu for very.

You'll want to know: انتہائی.
انتہائی — intihāī — "extreme; extremely".
اگر مریض کی حالت انتہائی نازک ہے تو ہسپتال جائیے۔ agar marīz kī hālat intihāī nāzuk hai to haspatāl jāiye. If the patient is in a very bad way, go to the hospital.

The word, taken apart.
انتہائی (intihāī) is built on intihā, usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic intihāʾ, "the end, the furthest point", with the ending -ī: as far as a thing can go.

اگر تم خوب پڑھو گے تو امتحان پاس کرو گے۔ agar tum khūb paṛho ge to imtihān pās karo ge. If you study hard, you will pass the exam.

The word, taken apart.
خوب (khūb) is usually traced to Persian khūb, "good, fine"; Urdu uses it for doing a thing well and fully.
