## You'll want to know: उपकल्पयति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C528-AYOJAYATI528-04]; assesses=[] -->

**उपकल्पयति** — *upakalpayati* — "makes ready; prepares".

> यदि श्वः यात्रा अस्ति, तर्हि अद्य स्यूतम् उपकल्पयतु।
>
> *yadi śvaḥ yātrā asti, tarhi adya syūtam upakalpayatu.*
>
> If the trip is tomorrow, get your bag ready today.

Past (he …): सः स्यूतम् उपकल्पितवान्, *saḥ syūtam upakalpitavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**उपकल्पयति** (*upakalpayati*) is built on *upa*, "near", and the causative of the root √kḷp, "to be fit, to be in order"; MW gives *upakalpayati* as to prepare, to make ready, to equip (MW).
