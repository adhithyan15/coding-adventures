Lesson 2 of 8.
ड़ — one dot under familiar ड.

Before we start: this one needs your hands, so it is not a driving lesson. You will want a pen and something to write on and your eyes, for letter shapes on the page. You can listen to everything else now — leave the section called Script — one mark until you have stopped, and I will say so again when we reach it.

Warm-up.
[pause 20 seconds]
Say clothes, then write familiar ड, paternal grandmother, and goods.

Script — one mark.
ड + ़ (nukta) becomes ड़.
The dot below is called a nukta. Here it marks the quick curled sound in kapṛā.

Guided Practice.
Trace ड़ once. Copy it once. Cover it, wait five seconds, and write ड़ from the sound cue.

Wrap-up Recall.
Source: Unicode Devanagari chart.
[question — say your answer, then pause 10 seconds]
Add the dot below ड and write the curled sound.
