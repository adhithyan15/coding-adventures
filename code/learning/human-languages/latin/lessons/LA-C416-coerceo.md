## You'll want to know: coerceō, coercēre
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C416-IUSTITIA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**coerceō, coercēre** — "to restrain, to keep in check".

> Cōnsul sēditiōsōs coercuit.
>
> *The consul restrained the rebels.*

*coerceō, coercēre, coercuī, coercitum*, *co-* + *arceō*, to shut in. English *coerce*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*coerceō* is built from *co-* "together" and *arceō* "to shut in, to keep off" (Lewis & Short): to hem in on all sides. English *coerce* comes from it.
