## You'll want to know: le garagiste
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C405-DEPANNEUSE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**le garagiste** — "the garage owner, the car mechanic".

> Le garagiste a trouvé le problème tout de suite.
>
> *The mechanic found the problem straight away.*

*Le garagiste* runs the garage; *le mécanicien* does the repairs.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*garagiste* is built on *garage* with *-iste*. *Garage* comes from *garer* 'to put in a safe place', which is usually traced to a Germanic verb meaning 'to watch over'.
