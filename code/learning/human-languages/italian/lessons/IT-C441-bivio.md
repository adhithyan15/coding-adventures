## You'll want to know: il bivio
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C441-VICOLI-01]; assesses=[] -->

**il bivio** — "the fork (in a road)".

> Al bivio prendi la strada per il mare.
>
> *At the fork take the road to the sea.*

Figuratively, a crossroads in life: *essere a un bivio*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*bivio* is a learned borrowing from Latin *bivium* 'a place where two roads meet', from *bi-* 'two' and *via* 'way' (Lewis & Short).
