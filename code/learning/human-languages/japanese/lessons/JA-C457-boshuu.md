## You'll want to know: ぼしゅう
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C457-JOBHUNT457-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ぼしゅう** — *boshū* — "recruitment; a call for applicants".

> **みせで てんいんを ぼしゅうしています。** — *mise de ten'in o boshū shite imasu* — "The shop is looking for staff."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ぼしゅう** (*boshū*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *bo*, "recruit" (Middle Chinese *muH*) and *shū*, "gather" (*dzip*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
