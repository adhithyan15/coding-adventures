# excusez-moi — the question that finds the way

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[FR-LEX-ALLER-06, FR-LEX-C100-VILLE52-05] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "to go". (*Aller.*) Say "the museum". (*Le musée.*)

[PAUSE 1s] You are in a town you do not know, and the museum is somewhere.

## You'll want to know: excusez-moi
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-EXCUSEZ-MOI-01]; assesses=[FR-LEX-MOI-01, FR-GRAM-IMPERATIF-01] -->

**excusez-moi** — "excuse me", to someone you do not know.

It is the *vous* command of **excuser**, "to excuse", with *moi* after it:
word for word, "excuse me". A stranger in the street is owed *vous*; to a
friend it is **excuse-moi**. The same words apologise: bump into someone and
you say *excusez-moi !* The short **pardon** does both jobs too, and is
as polite.

## Grammar Lens: pour aller à…
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-GRAM-POUR-ALLER-A-02]; assesses=[FR-GRAM-AU-AUX-01, FR-LEX-PLEASE-02, FR-LEX-C96-TEMPS48-04, FR-LEX-C96-TEMPS48-05] -->

> **Excusez-moi, pour aller au musée, s'il vous plaît ?** — Excuse me, how do I get to the museum?

Word for word, "to go to the museum, please". The rest of the question is
left unsaid, and every French speaker hears it anyway. The place takes
**à**, which welds onto *le*:

> **au** musée · **à la** plage · **à l'**hôtel

A second way asks for the **place** rather than the **way**, with *où est*,
"where is". Either can be followed by a short question that saves a walk,
*c'est loin*, "is it far", or *c'est près d'ici*, "is it near here":

> *Où est le musée ? C'est loin ? C'est près d'ici ?*

## The phrase, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**excuser** is inherited from Latin *excūsāre* (EtymDB 2.1), built on *ex-*,
"out of", and *causa*, "a cause, a charge in court" (Lewis & Short, under
*excuso*): to release someone from a charge, to free them from blame. So
*excusez-moi* asks, at root, to be let off. English **excuse** is the same
word, and **cause** is its second half.

**stranger**; a **friend**.) Ask the way to the hotel. (***Excusez-moi, pour aller à l'hôtel ?***)
What does *à* plus *le* become? (***Au.***) And which Latin word is inside *excuser*? (***Causa***, a charge.)
