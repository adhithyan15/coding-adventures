## You'll want to know: લણવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C242-HARVEST242-02]; assesses=[] -->

**લણવું** — *laṇvũ* — "to harvest, to reap".

> **અમે ઘઉં લણ્યા.** — *ame ghaũ laṇyā* — "We harvested the wheat."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**લણવું** (*laṇvũ*) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from *lunāti*, "cuts, reaps" (CDIAL 11082).
