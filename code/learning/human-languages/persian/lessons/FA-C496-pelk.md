## You'll want to know: پلک
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C496-NERVES-04]; assesses=[] -->

**پلک** (*pelk*) — "an eyelid".

> او بدون پلک زدن به من نگاه کرد.
>
> *u bedun-e pelk zadan be man negâh kard.*
>
> She looked at me without blinking.

*pelk zadan* — to blink.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*pelk* is usually taken as a native Persian word, but EtymDB lists it without any source, and its origin is not settled.
