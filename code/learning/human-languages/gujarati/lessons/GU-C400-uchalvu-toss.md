## You'll want to know: ઉછાળવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C400-SCOOTER400-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ઉછાળવું** — *uchāḷvũ* — "to toss (up); to throw up in the air".

> બાળક દડો ઉપર ઉછાળે છે.
>
> *bāḷak daḍo upar uchāḷe che.*
>
> The child tosses the ball up.

Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે દડો ઉછાળ્યો, *teṇe daḍo uchāḷyo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ઉછાળવું** (*uchāḷvũ*) goes back to *\*ut-śālayati*, "throws up", a form CDIAL reconstructs; CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb (CDIAL 1848). ઊછળવું, to bounce, is its intransitive partner.
