# ज़िला (zilā) — a district

## You'll want to know: ज़िला
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C370-SABHA370-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ज़िला** — *zilā* — "a district".

> हमारा ज़िला पहाड़ों में है।
>
> *hamārā zilā pahāṛoṁ meṁ hai.*
>
> Our district is in the mountains.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ज़िला** (*zilā*) is usually traced to Arabic *ḍilʿ*, "a side", which reached Hindi through Persian. Persian and Hindi use it for a district.
