## You'll want to know: ગલ્લો
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C422-STALL422-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ગલ્લો** — *gallo* — "a paan stall; a kiosk".

> નાકા પરના ગલ્લા પરથી તેણે પાન લીધું.
>
> *nākā parnā gallā parthī teṇe pān līdhũ.*
>
> He bought a paan from the stall at the corner.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ગલ્લો** (*gallo*) No dictionary we use records an older form, so its history is not settled.
