## You'll want to know: la habilidad
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C598-SABER-02]; assesses=[] -->

**la habilidad** — "the skill, the ability".

> Tiene mucha habilidad con las manos.
>
> *He is very skilful with his hands.*

*Habilidades sociales* — social skills.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*habilidad* is a learned borrowing from Latin *habilitas* 'aptness', built on *habilis* 'handy, fit', from *habere* 'to hold' (EtymDB).
