# શ — the consonant sha

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-SCRIPT-NNA-01] -->

Write retroflex nna once from memory. Check the left hook, middle bowl, and right spine.

## Script — meet one form
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-SCRIPT-SHA-01]; assesses=[] -->

Keep **શ** visible. It is the consonant sha, pronounced *sha*. Begin at the upper right, circle the small upper loop, and continue through the broad lower body into its tail. Lift once; descend the tall right spine and turn through its lower foot.

## Writing — notice and trace
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-SCRIPT-SHA-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: observe-trace -->

Finger-trace **શ** once, then air-trace it once. Notice small upper loop, broad body, separate right spine; one pen lift.

## Writing — copy with the model
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-SCRIPT-SHA-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: guided-copy -->

Copy visible **શ** once. Compare only the landmark named above and repair it once if needed.
