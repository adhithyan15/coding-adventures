## You'll want to know: l'allegria
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C479-ALLEGRIA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**l'allegria** — "cheerfulness, joy".

> La musica porta allegria alla festa.
>
> *Music brings cheer to the party.*

Built on *allegro* "cheerful".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*allegria* is built on *allegro* "cheerful", usually traced to Vulgar Latin *alecrus*, from Latin *alacer* "lively, quick" (Lewis & Short).
