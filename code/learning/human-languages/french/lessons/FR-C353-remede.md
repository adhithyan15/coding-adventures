## You'll want to know: le remède
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C353-REMEDE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**le remède** — "the remedy, the cure".

> Le meilleur remède contre la fatigue, c'est le sommeil.
>
> *The best remedy for tiredness is sleep.*

*Un remède de grand-mère* — a home remedy.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*remède* comes from Latin *remedium* "a cure, a remedy" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short), built from *re-* and *medērī* "to heal".
