## You'll want to know: ગાયક
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C526-SUR526-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ગાયક** — *gāyak* — "a singer".

> આ ગાયકનાં ગીતો મને ઘણાં ગમે છે.
>
> *ā gāyaknā̃ gīto mane ghaṇā̃ game che.*
>
> I like this singer's songs very much.

A masculine or feminine noun, by the person meant.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ગાયક** (*gāyak*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *gāyaka*, "a singer" (Monier-Williams), from the root of **ગાવું**, to sing.
