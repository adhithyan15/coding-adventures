## You'll want to know: احتمى
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C487-MALJA487-03]; assesses=[] -->

**احتمى** — *iḥtamā* — "to take shelter".

> احتمينا من المطر تحت الجسر.
>
> *iḥtamaynā min al-maṭar taḥta al-jisr.*
>
> We took shelter from the rain under the bridge.

Present: يحتمي, *yaḥtamī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**احتمى** (*iḥtamā*) is built on the native Arabic root *ḥ-m-y*, "to protect, to defend" (Lane). It is Form VIII, "to protect oneself".
