## You'll want to know: しんさつ

**しんさつ** — *shinsatsu* — "a consultation; a medical examination".

> **しんさつは ごごですか。** — *shinsatsu wa gogo desu ka* — "Is the consultation in the afternoon?"

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**しんさつ** (*shinsatsu*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *shin*, "to examine" (Middle Chinese *tsyinX*) and *satsu*, "to judge, understand" (*tsrheat*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
