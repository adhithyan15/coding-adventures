## You'll want to know: 市场
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C448-MARKET448-02]; assesses=[] -->

**市场** — *shìchǎng* — "market; marketplace".

> **去市场怎么走** *qù shìchǎng zěnme zǒu* — How do I get to the market?

The new character, in pieces: 市 — five strokes: a dot, a long bar, then a short vertical, a stroke that turns down and hooks, and a long vertical through the middle. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**市场** (*shìchǎng*) is built from 市 'a market' and 场 'an open ground', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *dzyiX* and *drjang* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first, after an older dictionary, as where people go to buy and sell, and the second as a threshing floor.
