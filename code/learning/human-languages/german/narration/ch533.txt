Lesson 1 of 7.
der Kamin — fireplace; chimney.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for caretaker, then the German for stairwell.

You'll want to know: der Kamin.
der Kamin — "fireplace; chimney".
Im Winter sitzen wir abends vor dem Kamin. In winter we sit in front of the fire in the evenings.
Plural: die Kamine. In southern Germany and Austria also the chimney.

The word, taken apart.
Kamin is inherited from Middle High German kamīn and Old High German kemīn, an early borrowing from Latin camīnus "furnace", from Greek káminos (EtymDB).

die Schublade — "drawer".
Die Schere ist in der obersten Schublade. The scissors are in the top drawer.
Plural: die Schubladen. In Austria also die Lade.

The word, taken apart.
Schublade is built on schieben "to push", from Proto-Germanic skeubaną, and Lade "box, drawer", inherited from Middle High German lade (EtymDB).

Lesson 5 of 7.
die Gemütlichkeit — cosiness.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for drawer, then the German for duvet.

You'll want to know: die Gemütlichkeit.
die Gemütlichkeit — "cosiness".
Kerzen und leise Musik sorgen für Gemütlichkeit. Candles and soft music make it cosy.
No plural. Built on gemütlich — cosy, relaxed.

The word, taken apart.
Gemütlichkeit is built on gemütlich "cosy", inherited from Middle High German gemüetlich, built on gemüete "mind, mood" (EtymDB), with -keit.
