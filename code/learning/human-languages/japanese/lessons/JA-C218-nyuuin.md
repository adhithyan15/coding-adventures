## You'll want to know: にゅういん
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C218-HOSPITAL218-01]; assesses=[] -->

**にゅういん** — *nyūin* — "going into hospital; a hospital stay".

> **にゅういんは ながいですか。** — *nyūin wa nagai desu ka* — "Is the hospital stay long?"

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**にゅういん** (*nyūin*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *nyū*, "to enter" (Middle Chinese *nyip*) and *in*, "courtyard, institution" (*hjwenH*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
