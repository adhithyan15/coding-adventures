Arabic, chapter 346: Spies and suspects.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
تجسس (tajassasa) — to spy.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to approach, then the Arabic for to move away.

You'll want to know: تجسس.
تجسس — tajassasa — "to spy".
تجسس الرجل على جيرانه. tajassasa ar-rajul ʿalā jīrānihi. The man spied on his neighbours.
Present: يتجسس, yatajassas.
A spy is جاسوس.

The word, taken apart.
تجسس (tajassasa) is built on the native Arabic root j-s-s (Lane), "to feel with the hand, to search out news". It is Form V; a spy is jāsūs.

Lesson 3 of 5.
اشتبه (ishtabaha) — to suspect.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to spy, then the Arabic for to disguise oneself.

You'll want to know: اشتبه.
اشتبه — ishtabaha — "to suspect".
اشتبهت الشرطة في رجل طويل. ishtabahat ash-shurṭa fī rajul ṭawīl. The police suspected a tall man.
Present: يشتبه, yashtabih.
It takes في or بـ. A suspect is مشتبه به.

The word, taken apart.
اشتبه (ishtabaha) is built on the native Arabic root sh-b-h (Lane), "to resemble", the root of the taught mushābih, similar. Form VIII is "to be doubtful", when things look alike: to suspect.
