French, chapter 393: Garanties et devis.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
la garantie — the guarantee, the warranty.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for the bank transfer, then the French for to take out.

You'll want to know: la garantie.
la garantie — "the guarantee, the warranty".
Le frigo est encore sous garantie. The fridge is still under warranty.
Sous garantie — under warranty.

The word, taken apart.
garantie is formed on garantir (EtymDB), from Old French garant "protector, warrant", usually traced to a Frankish word.

Lesson 3 of 5.
le devis — the quote, the estimate.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for the guarantee, then the French for to guarantee.

You'll want to know: le devis.
le devis — "the quote, the estimate".
Le plombier m'a envoyé un devis. The plumber sent me a quote.
The s is silent. Demander un devis — to ask for a quote.

The word, taken apart.
devis is formed on deviser "to divide, to arrange" (EtymDB), from a Vulgar Latin form of dīvidere "to divide" (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 4 of 5.
rentable — profitable, worth it.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for to guarantee, then the French for the quote.

You'll want to know: rentable.
rentable — "profitable, worth it".
Ce magasin n'est plus rentable. This shop is no longer profitable.
From rente, income.

The word, taken apart.
rentable is built from rente "income" and -able. rente comes from a Vulgar Latin rendita, from the verb behind rendre "to give back", Latin reddere (Lewis & Short).
