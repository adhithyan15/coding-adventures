(**ਧੁਨ**, *dhun*.) Music practice? (**ਰਿਆਜ਼**, *riāz*.) To echo? (**ਗੂੰਜਣਾ**, *gūnjṇā*.) A singer? (**ਗਾਇਕ**, *gāik*.) A flute? (**ਵੰਝਲੀ**, *vanjhlī*.) A harmonium? (**ਵਾਜਾ**, *vājā*.) A poem?
