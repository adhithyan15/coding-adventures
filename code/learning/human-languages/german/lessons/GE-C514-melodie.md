## You'll want to know: die Melodie
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C514-MELODIE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**die Melodie** — "melody, tune".

> Die Melodie von diesem Lied ist sehr schön.
>
> *The tune of this song is very beautiful.*

Plural: *die Melodien*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Melodie* was borrowed, through Late Latin *melōdia*, from Greek *melōidía* "singing, a song"; EtymDB lists the German word without a source.
