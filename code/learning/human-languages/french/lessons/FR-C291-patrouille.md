## You'll want to know: la patrouille
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C291-GENDARMES-04]; assesses=[] -->

**la patrouille** — "the patrol".

> Une patrouille passe dans la rue toutes les heures.
>
> *A patrol goes down the street every hour.*

*En patrouille* — on patrol.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*patrouille* comes from Old French *patrouille* (EtymDB), built on *patrouiller* "to tramp about".
