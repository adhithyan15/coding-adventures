# بعد (bād) — after

## You'll want to know: بعد
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C267-BAD-01]; assesses=[UR-LEX-C46-FAMILY46-02, UR-LEX-C47-VERBS47-02, UR-GRAMMAR-C265-FUTURE-01] -->

**بعد** — *bād* — "after".

> **کھانے کے بعد میں سویا۔**
>
> *khāne ke bād maiṅ soyā.* — "After the meal I slept."

Like **دوران** (*daurān*), it stands after **کے** (*ke*). With **میں** (*meṅ*) and no noun, it is "later":

> **میں بعد میں آؤں گا۔**
>
> *maiṅ bād meṅ āūṅ gā.* — "I'll come later."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**بعد** (*bād*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *baʿd*, "after". The letter in its middle is new to your hand; the next lesson takes it out.
