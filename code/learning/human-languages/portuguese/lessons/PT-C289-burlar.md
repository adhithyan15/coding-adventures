## You'll want to know: burlar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C289-BURLA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**burlar** — "to swindle, to con".

> Um homem burlou várias pessoas idosas.
>
> *A man swindled several elderly people.*

In Brazil it means rather ‘to get round’ a rule: *burlar a lei*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*burlar* is built on *burla* "a trick, a scam" (EtymDB), from Spanish *burla* "mockery".
