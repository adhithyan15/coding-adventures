## You'll want to know: تظاهر
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C382-IHTIJAJ382-03]; assesses=[] -->

**تظاهر** — *taẓāhara* — "to demonstrate".

> تظاهر الطلاب أمام الجامعة.
>
> *taẓāhara aṭ-ṭullāb amāma al-jāmiʿa.*
>
> The students demonstrated in front of the university.

Present: يتظاهر, *yataẓāhar*.

Also to pretend: تظاهر بالنوم, pretended to sleep.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**تظاهر** (*taẓāhara*) is built on the native Arabic root *ẓ-h-r*, "to appear, to come out" (Lane). Lane gives Form VI for appearing and for people backing one another; modern Arabic uses it for a public show, to demonstrate, and with بـ for a false show, to pretend.
