## You'll want to know: పనితనం
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C524-PRATIBHA524-03]; assesses=[] -->

**పనితనం** — *panitanaṁ* — "workmanship; craft".

> ఆ కుర్చీలో వడ్రంగి పనితనం కనిపిస్తుంది.
>
> *ā kurcīlō vaḍraṅgi panitanaṁ kanipistundi.*
>
> The carpenter's craft shows in that chair.

పని, work, + -తనం.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**పనితనం** (*panitanaṁ*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 3884 lists Telugu *pani*, "work, labour, workmanship, art", with Tamil *paṇi*; -తనం makes the abstract noun.
