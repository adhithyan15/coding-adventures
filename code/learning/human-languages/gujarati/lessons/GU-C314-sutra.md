# સૂત્ર (sūtra) — a slogan

## You'll want to know: સૂત્ર
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C314-HADTAL314-03]; assesses=[] -->

**સૂત્ર** — *sūtra* — "a slogan".

> લોકો સૂત્રો બોલતા હતા.
>
> *loko sūtro boltā hatā.*
>
> People were shouting slogans.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**સૂત્ર** (*sūtra*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *sūtra*, "a thread", and then a short rule held in a line (CDIAL 13561). In a march it is the short line everyone shouts together.

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
