## You'll want to know: गोंद
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C521-DURUSTI521-03]; assesses=[] -->

**गोंद** — *gond* — "glue; gum".

> मी तुटलेलं खेळणं गोंदाने चिकटवलं.
>
> *mī tuṭlelaṁ kheḷṇaṁ gondāne cikṭavlaṁ.*
>
> I stuck the broken toy together with glue.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**गोंद** (*gond*) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi *gõd*, "gum", beside Hindi *gõd*, under Sanskrit *gundra*, the name of a reed (CDIAL 4199). How a reed's name came to mean gum is not explained there.
