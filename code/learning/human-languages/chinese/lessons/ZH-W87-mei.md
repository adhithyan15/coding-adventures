## Script — water, a small roof, and 又
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-SCRIPT-MEI-01]; assesses=[] -->

> 没

**Three pieces, left before right, top before bottom.**

**The water piece 氵, three strokes**, as 汉 opens: two dots, then a stroke rising to the right.

**A small roof, two strokes**: a short left-falling stroke, then a horizontal that turns down and runs out to the right.

**又, two strokes**, under the roof, as 对 opens: across and falling to the left, then the long stroke falling to the right.

3 + 2 + 2 = seven.
