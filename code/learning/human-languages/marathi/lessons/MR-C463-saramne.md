## You'll want to know: शरमणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C463-GARVA463-04]; assesses=[] -->

**शरमणे** — *śaramṇe* — "to feel ashamed".

> आपली चूक लक्षात आल्यावर तो शरमला.
>
> *āplī cūk lakṣāt ālyāvar to śaramlā.*
>
> He felt ashamed when he realised his mistake.

Past (he …): तो शरमला, *to śaramlā*.

From शरम, shame, a Persian word beside the Marathi लाज.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**शरमणे** (*śaramṇe*) is a Marathi verb built on शरम, "shame", a loan from Persian *sharm*.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
