Portuguese, chapter 303: Quando a água sobe.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
a inundação — the flood.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for to collapse, then the Portuguese for the rubble.

You'll want to know: a inundação.
a inundação — "the flood".
A inundação cobriu as ruas do centro. The flood covered the streets of the centre.
When a river overflows people also say a cheia.

The word, taken apart.
inundação is a learned borrowing from Latin inundātiō "a flooding" (EtymDB), built on unda "a wave".

What you've built.

Lesson 3 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for the flood, then the Portuguese for to flood.

You'll want to know: o afogamento.
o afogamento — "the drowning".
Houve um afogamento na praia ontem. There was a drowning at the beach yesterday.
Built on afogar, ‘to drown’.

The word, taken apart.
afogamento is built on afogar "to drown", which comes from Latin offōcāre "to choke", built on faucēs "the throat" (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 4 of 5.

[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: o alerta.
o alerta — "the alert, the warning".
O governo deu o alerta vermelho. The government issued a red alert.
Estar alerta — to be on guard.

The word, taken apart.
alerta was borrowed from Italian all’erta "on the lookout" (EtymDB), from erta "a height, a watchtower".
