# Session Map — Tamil Chapter 1

Same spaced-repetition schedule as the other tracks (a word from session *N*
resurfaces at *N+1, N+3, N+7, N+15*). Lessons named by slug; this map is the
authoritative order.

**There is no reading course.** Tamil is learned *through* the words: each
lesson's *"The letters in this word"* section introduces exactly the letters
that word needs. By the end of Chapter 1 you have met the inherent vowel, the
puḷḷi, the common vowel signs, the independent vowels, and the three-way
*n*/*l*/*r* distinctions — all in the service of real greetings.

## Chapter 1 — Greetings

| Session | Lesson | Word | New letters / reading idea | Grammar / family thread |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | vanakkam | வணக்கம் | inherent *a*, puḷḷi (vowel-killer), retroflex ண | native Dravidian vs. Sanskrit "hello" |
| 2 | nandri | நன்றி | dental ந / alveolar ன, ற, *i*-sign | three-way *n*/*l*/*r*; Malayalam shares *nandi* |
| 3 | aam | ஆம் | independent vowel ஆ | verb-echo "yes" |
| 4 | illai | இல்லை | independent இ, ல், *ai*-sign | negation by a negative verb (*uṇḍu* / *illai*) |
| 5 | sari | சரி | ச (one letter, many sounds) | voicing read from position |
| 6 | practice | (recap) | read all five | the *pōy varugiṟēṉ* farewell |
