## You'll want to know: fingir
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C471-MENTIRA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**fingir** — "to pretend".

> O gato fingiu que estava a dormir.
>
> *The cat pretended it was asleep.*

*Fingir que* + verb — to pretend that. It has nothing to do with fingers.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*fingir* is a learned borrowing from Latin *fingere* "to form, to shape, to devise, to feign" (EtymDB).
