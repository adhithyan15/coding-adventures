## You'll want to know: el albergue
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C660-ALOJAMIENTO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**el albergue** — "the hostel; the shelter".

> Dormimos en un albergue juvenil.
>
> *We slept in a youth hostel.*

*Albergue juvenil* — youth hostel.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*albergue* is built on *albergar* "to shelter" (EtymDB), which comes from a Germanic compound *heriberga* "a shelter for an army", from *harjaz* "army" and *bergô* "shelter".
