Mandarin Chinese, chapter 400: Moving the Dates.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
tiáozhěng — to adjust.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for to meet (a requirement), then the Mandarin for to marry and settle down.

You'll want to know: tiáozhěng.
tiáozhěng — "to adjust".
rúguǒ shíjiān bù héshì wǒmen kěyǐ tiáozhěng — If the time doesn't suit, we can adjust it.
Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
tiáozhěng is built from tiáo 'to put in tune' and zhěng 'to put in order', both inherited from Middle Chinese, dew and tsyengX (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to harmonise' and the second 'straight' and 'neat'.

What you've built.

Lesson 4 of 5.
yuándìng — originally planned.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for to extend (in time), then the Mandarin for to shorten.

You'll want to know: yuándìng.
yuándìng — "originally planned".
yuándìng de shíjiān gǎi le — The time we first planned has changed.
Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
yuándìng is built from yuán 'original' and 定 'settled', both inherited from Middle Chinese, ngjwon and dengH (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'a broad plain'. The sense 'original' is not given there.

Lesson 5 of 5.
安定 (āndìng) — settled; stable.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for to shorten, then the Mandarin for originally planned.

You'll want to know: 安定.
安定 — āndìng — "settled; stable".
只有工作安定了我们才能成家 zhǐyǒu gōngzuò āndìng le wǒmen cái néng chéngjiā — Only once our jobs are settled can we get married.

The word, taken apart.
安定 (āndìng) is built from 安 'calm' and 定 'settled', both inherited from Middle Chinese, 'an and dengH (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second with the first: 'calm'.
