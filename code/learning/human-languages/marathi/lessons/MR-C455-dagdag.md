## You'll want to know: दगदग
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C455-DHASTI455-03]; assesses=[] -->

**दगदग** — *dagdag* — "a wearing rush; exhausting bustle".

> रोजच्या दगदगीमुळे आई थकते.
>
> *rojcyā dagdagīmuḷe āī thakte.*
>
> Mother gets tired from the daily rush.

Feminine.

Feminine; oblique दगदगी-. दगदग करणे, to wear oneself out rushing about.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**दगदग** (*dagdag*) is a Marathi noun built by doubling a syllable; its origin is not settled.
