## You'll want to know: 母语
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C509-PARENTS509-04]; assesses=[] -->

**母语** — *mǔyǔ* — "mother tongue".

> *wǒ de mǔyǔ shì Yīngyǔ* — My mother tongue is English.

Two third tones meet in this word: mǔ is said with a rising tone before yǔ, and the spelling keeps both marks.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**母语** (*mǔyǔ*) is built from 母 'a mother' and 语 'speech', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *muwX* and *ngjoX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription).
