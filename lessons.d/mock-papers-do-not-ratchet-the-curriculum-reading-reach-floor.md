# Mock papers do not ratchet the curriculum reading-reach floor

The Sanskrit A2 mock plan initially proposed raising the reading-reach floor
from the length of the new mock sources. That floor is computed only from
`comprehension` blocks in curriculum lessons; mock papers are deliberately not
inputs. Keep the floor unchanged unless the taught lesson corpus itself reaches
another task-shape minimum, and enforce mock-source length with the mock's owned
tests instead.
