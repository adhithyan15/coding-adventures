## You'll want to know: improbabile
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C376-RISCHIO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**improbabile** — "unlikely".

> È improbabile che nevichi a maggio.
>
> *It is unlikely to snow in May.*

Also takes the subjunctive after *è improbabile che*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*improbabile* is a learned borrowing from Latin *improbābilis* "not deserving approval" (Lewis & Short), from *in-* "not" and *probābilis*; the sense "unlikely" follows *probabile*.
