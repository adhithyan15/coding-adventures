## You'll want to know: انگشتانہ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C514-KARHAI514-04]; assesses=[] -->

**انگشتانہ** — *angushtānā* — "a thimble".

> نانی کی انگلی میں ایک پرانا انگشتانہ ہے۔
>
> *nānī kī unglī meṅ ek purānā angushtānā hai.*
>
> Grandmother has an old thimble on her finger.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**انگشتانہ** (*angushtānā*) is usually traced to Persian *angushtāna*, "a thimble", built on *angusht*, "a finger".
