## You'll want to know: ضمانت
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C340-FAISLA340-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ضمانت** — *zamānat* — "bail; a guarantee".

> اسے ضمانت مل گئی۔
>
> *use zamānat mil gaī.*
>
> He got bail.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ضمانت** (*zamānat*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *ḍamāna*, "surety": a pledge that someone will come back.
