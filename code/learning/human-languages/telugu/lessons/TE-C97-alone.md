## You'll want to know: ఒంటరిగా
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C97-MOOD-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ఒంటరిగా** (*oṇṭarigā*) — "alone".

**నేను ఒంటరిగా ఉన్నాను** — "I am alone". The **-గా** ending again, on a word for "single, on
one's own".
