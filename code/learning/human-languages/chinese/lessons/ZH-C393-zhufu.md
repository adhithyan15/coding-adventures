## You'll want to know: zhǔfu
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C393-BESURE393-01]; assesses=[] -->

*zhǔfu* — "to exhort; to tell (someone) to be sure to do something".

> *māma zhǔfu wǒ wǎnshang zǎo huí jiā* — Mum told me to be sure to come home early tonight.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*zhǔfu* is built from *zhǔ* 'to entrust' and *fu*. The first is inherited from Middle Chinese *tsyowk* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'to entrust to'; the dictionary has no entry for the second, so its history is not settled.

## What you've built

One more word for saying what would happen.
