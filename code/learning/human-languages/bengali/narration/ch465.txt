Bengali, chapter 465: Good Sense.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
বিবেক (bibek) — conscience.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for indifferent, then the Bengali for to adapt.

You'll want to know: বিবেক.
বিবেক — bibek — "conscience".
আমার বিবেক বলছে, টাকাটা ফিরিয়ে দেওয়া উচিত। āmār bibek bôlchhe, ṭākāṭā phiriye deoyā uchit. My conscience tells me the money ought to be given back.

The word, taken apart.
বিবেক (bibek) is a tatsama, Sanskrit viveka, "discrimination, distinction" (Monier-Williams); Bengali uses it for the inner judge that tells right from wrong.

Past (he/she …): তিনি সিদ্ধান্তটা আবার বিবেচনা করলেন, tini siddhāntôṭā ābār bibechônā kôrlen.

The word, taken apart.
বিবেচনা করা (bibechônā kôrā) is Sanskrit vivecana, "discriminating, examining" (Monier-Williams), with kôrā, "to do" (CDIAL 2814).
