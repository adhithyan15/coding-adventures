# ढ — one character, met first in दृढः

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[SA-LEX-C278-DRDHAH-01, SA-SCRIPT-RECOG-221, SA-SCRIPT-RECOG-226] -->

[PAUSE 1s] Before the new one: say **दृढः** (*dṛḍhaḥ*), "firm". Then say the sounds of **ड** and **घ**. (*ḍa*, *gha*.)

[PAUSE 2s] One character this time: the one in the middle of **दृढः**.

## Script you'll notice: ढ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-SCRIPT-RECOG-227]; assesses=[SA-SCRIPT-RECOG-221] -->

**ढ** — *ḍha*.

It is a **consonant**: **ड** (*ḍa*) with a breath after it, as **घ** is **ग** with a breath.

What it is made of:

- a descending stem joined to a broad outer bowl and closed inner loop
- the top shirorekhā

You have met it here:

- **दृढः** *dṛḍhaḥ* — firm

## Writing: ढ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[SA-SCRIPT-RECOG-227] -->

- **1.** start at the stem's headline junction, descend, turn left through the upper shoulder, sweep counterclockwise around the broad outer bowl, and continue counterclockwise around the closed inner loop without lifting
- **2.** lift and draw the top shirorekhā left-to-right

**Pen lifts: 1.** The pen comes up 1 time and no more.

> Verified two-stroke teaching form fitted to the bundled printed outline.

> This is one attested teaching order and not a national standard — handwriting
> here is taught with school-to-school variation. Source: Opiaterein, ‘Deva-ढ-order.gif’, strokes 1–2, Wikimedia Commons, 10 May 2009.
