Punjabi, chapter 125: Halwa, Spice, Turmeric, Cumin, Fritter.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
ਹਲਵਾ (halvā) — halwa.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for lassi, then the Punjabi for rice pudding.

You'll want to know: ਹਲਵਾ.
ਹਲਵਾ — halvā — "halwa".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: halvā]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: halvā, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say halvā]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Punjabi for lassi, then the Punjabi for rice pudding, then say halvā again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does ਹਲਵਾ (halvā) mean? ("Halwa".) Say it once more.

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]

(ਸਿਰਹਾਣਾ, sirhāṇā.) A doll? (ਗੁੱਡੀ, guḍḍī.) A toy? (ਖਿਡੌਣਾ, khiḍauṇā.) A cot? (ਮੰਜਾ, mañjā.) Pyjamas? (ਪਜਾਮਾ, pajāmā.) A salwar? (ਸਲਵਾਰ, salvār.) A scarf? (ਚੁੰਨੀ, cunnī.) A belt? (ਪੇਟੀ, peṭī.) A handkerchief? (ਰੁਮਾਲ, rumāl.) A sweater? (ਸਵੈਟਰ, savaiṭar.) A coat? (ਕੋਟ, koṭ.) A ring? (ਅੰਗੂਠੀ, angūṭhī.) A bangle? (ਕੰਗਣ, kangaṇ.)
