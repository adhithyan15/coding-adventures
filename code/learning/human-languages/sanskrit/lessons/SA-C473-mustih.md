## You'll want to know: मुष्टिः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C473-ANGA473-04]; assesses=[] -->

**मुष्टिः** — *muṣṭiḥ* — "a fist".

> कुपितः बालः मुष्टिं बध्नाति।
>
> *kupitaḥ bālaḥ muṣṭiṃ badhnāti.*
>
> The angry boy clenches his fist.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**मुष्टिः** (*muṣṭiḥ*) MW suggests, with a "perhaps", that it first meant the hand closed on something stolen (√muṣ, "to steal"); the history is not settled (MW).
