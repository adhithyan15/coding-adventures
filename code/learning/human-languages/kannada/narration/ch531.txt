Kannada, chapter 531: Games at Home.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಪಗಡೆ (pagaḍe) — a dice game.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to savour, then the Kannada for to be absorbed.

You'll want to know: ಪಗಡೆ.
ಪಗಡೆ — pagaḍe — "a dice game".
ಹಬ್ಬದ ದಿನ ಅಜ್ಜಿ ಮತ್ತು ಅತ್ತೆ ಪಗಡೆ ಆಡಿದರು. habbada dina ajji mattu atte pagaḍe āḍidaru. On the festival day grandmother and aunt played pagade.
A board game played with cowries or long dice.

The word, taken apart.
ಪಗಡೆ (pagaḍe) is the name of an old board game played with dice; DEDR 1461 records the game, but the origin of the name is not settled.

Lesson 3 of 5.
ಕಣ್ಣಾಮುಚ್ಚಾಲೆ (kaṇṇāmuccāle) — hide-and-seek.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a dice game, then the Kannada for carrom.

You'll want to know: ಕಣ್ಣಾಮುಚ್ಚಾಲೆ.
ಕಣ್ಣಾಮುಚ್ಚಾಲೆ — kaṇṇāmuccāle — "hide-and-seek".
ಮಕ್ಕಳು ತೋಟದಲ್ಲಿ ಕಣ್ಣಾಮುಚ್ಚಾಲೆ ಆಡುತ್ತಿದ್ದರು. makkaḷu tōṭadalli kaṇṇāmuccāle āḍuttiddaru. The children were playing hide-and-seek in the garden.
From ಕಣ್ಣು, eye, and ಮುಚ್ಚು, to close: the seeker closes his eyes.

The word, taken apart.
ಕಣ್ಣಾಮುಚ್ಚಾಲೆ (kaṇṇāmuccāle) is a native Kannada game name built on ಕಣ್ಣು, "eye", and ಮುಚ್ಚು, "to close"; the origin of the ending is not settled.

ಉದ್ಯಾನದಲ್ಲಿ ಮಕ್ಕಳು ಉಯ್ಯಾಲೆ ಆಡಿದರು. udyānadalli makkaḷu uyyāle āḍidaru. The children played on the swings in the park.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ಆಟಿಕೆ (āṭike) — a toy.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for hide-and-seek, then the Kannada for a swing.

You'll want to know: ಆಟಿಕೆ.
ಆಟಿಕೆ — āṭike — "a toy".
ಅಜ್ಜ ಮೊಮ್ಮಗನಿಗೆ ಮರದ ಆಟಿಕೆ ತಂದರು. ajja mommaganige marada āṭike tandaru. Grandfather brought his grandson a wooden toy.
Plural: ಆಟಿಕೆಗಳು, āṭikegaḷu.
Built on ಆಟ, play.

The word, taken apart.
ಆಟಿಕೆ (āṭike) is native Dravidian: DEDR 347 lists Kannada ಆಡು, "to move, to play", with the nouns āṭa and āṭike, "motion, play"; a toy is the thing you play with.
