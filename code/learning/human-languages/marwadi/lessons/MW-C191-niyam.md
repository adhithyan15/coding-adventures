## You'll want to know: नियम
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C191-NOTICE191-02]; assesses=[] -->

**नियम** — *niyam* — "a rule".

> ये नियम घणो जरूरी है।
>
> *ye niyam ghaṇo jarūrī hai.*
>
> This rule matters a great deal.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**नियम** (*niyam*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *niyama*, "restraining, holding back" (Monier-Williams): a rule holds back what it does not allow.
