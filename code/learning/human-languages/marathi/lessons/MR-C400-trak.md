## You'll want to know: ट्रक
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C400-VAHAN400-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ट्रक** — *ṭrak* — "a truck; a lorry".

> रात्री महामार्गावर खूप ट्रक असतात.
>
> *rātrī mahāmārgāvar khūp ṭrak astāt.*
>
> There are lots of trucks on the highway at night.

Masculine.

The plural is the same word: एक ट्रक, दहा ट्रक.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ट्रक** (*ṭrak*) is a loan from English *truck*.
