Marathi, chapter 80: To forget, To send, To count, To catch, To cut.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
विसरणे (visarṇe) — to forget.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to sing; a song, then the Marathi for to dance.

You'll want to know: विसरणे.
विसरणे — visarṇe — "to forget".

The word, taken apart.
विसरणे (visarṇe) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from vismarati, "forgets" (CDIAL 12021). It is vi-, "away", and smṛ, "to remember".

The word, taken apart.
मोजणे (mojṇe) is listed by CDIAL under Sanskrit meya, "to be measured", from mā, "to measure", beside Marathi mejṇẽ, "to measure". CDIAL leaves the -o- of मोजणे unexplained (CDIAL 10329).
