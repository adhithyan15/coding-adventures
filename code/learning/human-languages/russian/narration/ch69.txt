Russian, chapter 69: Today, Yesterday, Hour, Minute, Time.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
сегодня (sevódnya) — today.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a year, then the Russian for the evening.

You'll want to know: сегодня.
сегодня (sevódnya) — "today". The г is said as v.

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.

[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
