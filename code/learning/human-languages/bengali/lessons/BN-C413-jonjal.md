## You'll want to know: জঞ্জাল
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C413-NORDOMA413-05]; assesses=[] -->

**জঞ্জাল** — *jônjāl* — "junk; clutter; a heap of rubbish".

> নর্দমাটা জঞ্জালে ভর্তি।
>
> *nôrdômāṭā jônjāle bhôrti.*
>
> The drain is choked with junk.

Old things piled up and in the way: ঘরের জঞ্জাল, the clutter in a room.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**জঞ্জাল** (*jônjāl*) is inherited: CDIAL lists Bengali *jañjāl*, "disorder, rubbish", under the reconstructed *jañjāla* (CDIAL 5085).
