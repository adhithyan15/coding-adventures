## You'll want to know: उकळणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C527-SHIJAVNE527-02]; assesses=[] -->

**उकळणे** — *ukaḷṇe* — "to boil".

> चहासाठी आईने पाणी उकळलं.
>
> *cahāsāṭhī āīne pāṇī ukaḷlaṁ.*
>
> Mother boiled water for tea.

Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने पाणी उकळलं, *tyāne pāṇī ukaḷlaṁ*.

Both ways: पाणी उकळतं, the water boils; मी पाणी उकळतो, I boil the water.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**उकळणे** (*ukaḷṇe*) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi *ukaḷṇẽ*, "to boil", under *\*utkalati*, "goes out or up" (CDIAL 1716).
