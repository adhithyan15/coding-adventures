Malayalam, chapter 264: To get ready, To brush, To wipe, To sweep, To fold.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ഒരുങ്ങുക (oruṅṅuka) — to get ready.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for a bakery, then the Malayalam for a supermarket.

You'll want to know: ഒരുങ്ങുക.
ഒരുങ്ങുക — oruṅṅuka — "to get ready".

The word, taken apart.
ഒരുങ്ങുക (oruṅṅuka) is native Dravidian, one word with Telugu oggu (DEDR 992).
