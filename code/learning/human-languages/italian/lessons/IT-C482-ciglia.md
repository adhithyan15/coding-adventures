## You'll want to know: le ciglia
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C482-VISO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**le ciglia** — "eyelashes".

> La bambina ha le ciglia lunghe.
>
> *The little girl has long eyelashes.*

The singular *il ciglio* also means an edge: *il ciglio della strada* — the roadside.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ciglia* is inherited from Latin *cilia*, the plural of *cilium* "eyelid" (Lewis & Short).
