# ਤੱਤਾ (tattā) — hot to the touch

## You'll want to know: ਤੱਤਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C291-WARM291-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ਤੱਤਾ** — *tattā* — "hot to the touch".

> ਚਾਹ ਬਹੁਤ ਤੱਤੀ ਹੈ।
>
> *chāh bahut tattī hai.*
>
> The tea is very hot.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਤੱਤਾ** (*tattā*) is inherited from Sanskrit *tapta*, "heated, hot"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *tattā*, "hot, burning", under it (CDIAL 5679).
