## You'll want to know: 电话
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C63-PHONE63-01]; assesses=[] -->

**电话** — *diànhuà* — "telephone; phone call".

> **你有电话吗** *nǐ yǒu diànhuà ma* — Have you got a phone?

The new character, in pieces: 电 — 日 with one long stroke running down through its middle, bending right along the bottom and hooking up. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**电话** (*diànhuà*) is built from 电 'lightning', now 'electricity', and 话 'talk', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *denH* and *hwaejH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription): electric talk.
