Portuguese, chapter 512: No pavilhão.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
o pavilhão — sports hall; pavilion.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for podium, then the Portuguese for record.

You'll want to know: o pavilhão.
o pavilhão — "sports hall; pavilion".
Os treinos são no pavilhão da escola. Training is in the school's sports hall.
Pavilhão desportivo — sports hall.

The word, taken apart.
pavilhão was borrowed from French pavillon (EtymDB), from Latin pāpiliō "a butterfly", and so "a tent", whose spread cloth looked like wings.

Lesson 5 of 5.
a ginástica — gymnastics; exercise.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for handball, then the Portuguese for volleyball.

You'll want to know: a ginástica.
a ginástica — "gymnastics; exercise".
Faço ginástica três vezes por semana. I exercise three times a week.
Fazer ginástica — to do exercise.

The word, taken apart.
ginástica is a learned borrowing through Latin gymnasticus "of bodily exercise" (Lewis & Short) from Greek gymnastikós, built on gymnós "naked": Greek athletes trained unclothed.
