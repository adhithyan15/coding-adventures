## You'll want to know: ਕਸਬਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C266-TOWN266-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ਕਸਬਾ** — *kasbā* — "a small town".

> ਮੇਰਾ ਦੋਸਤ ਇੱਕ ਛੋਟੇ ਕਸਬੇ ਵਿੱਚ ਰਹਿੰਦਾ ਹੈ।
>
> *merā dost ikk chhoṭe kasbe vich rahindā hai.*
>
> My friend lives in a small town.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਕਸਬਾ** (*kasbā*) is a loan, usually traced through Persian to Arabic *qasaba*, "a town".
