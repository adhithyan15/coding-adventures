Tamil, chapter 264: Opposite, Between, Way, Land, Valley.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
எதிரே (etirē) — opposite.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for an airport, then the Tamil for a ship.

You'll want to know: எதிரே.
எதிரே — etirē — "opposite".

The word, taken apart.
எதிரே (etirē) is native Dravidian. DEDR 795 lists Malayalam etir, "opposite" and Telugu eduru. -ē adds emphasis: "right opposite".
