## You'll want to know: ਖੁਰਪਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C304-GROW304-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ਖੁਰਪਾ** — *khurpā* — "a weeding hoe".

> ਮੈਂ ਖੁਰਪੇ ਨਾਲ ਘਾਹ ਕੱਟਦਾ ਹਾਂ।
>
> *maiṁ khurpe nāl ghāh kaṭṭdā hāṁ.*
>
> I cut the grass with a weeding hoe.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਖੁਰਪਾ** (*khurpā*) is inherited from Sanskrit *kṣurapra*, "a sort of hoe"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *khurpā*, "grubber for grass", under it (CDIAL 3730).
