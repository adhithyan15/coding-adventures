Mandarin Chinese, chapter 399: Only If.
6 lessons. You can do the first 2 of them in the car; after that you will want to have stopped.


Lesson 1 of 6.
yāoqiú — to require; to demand (of someone).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for ready-made, then the Mandarin for to hinge on.

You'll want to know: yāoqiú.
yāoqiú — "to require; to demand (of someone)".
lǎoshī yāoqiú wǒmen měitiān shuō hànyǔ — The teacher asks us to speak Chinese every day.
Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
yāoqiú is built from yāo 'to press' and qiú 'to seek', both inherited from Middle Chinese, 'jiew and gjuw (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to force, in common speech' and the second 'to look for'.

Lesson 2 of 6.
只有 (zhǐyǒu) — only if (with cái after it).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for to hinge on, then the Mandarin for to demand (of someone).

You'll want to know: 只有.
只有 — zhǐyǒu — "only if (with cái after it)".
只有你去他才会去 zhǐyǒu nǐ qù tā cái huì qù — He'll only go if you go.
The new character, in pieces: 只 (zhǐ) — five strokes: 口 on top, and 八 under it. The next lesson puts it in your hand.
Two third tones meet in this word: zhǐ is said with a rising tone before yǒu, and the spelling keeps both marks.

The word, taken apart.
只有 (zhǐyǒu) is built from zhǐ 'only' and 有 'to have'. The second is inherited from Middle Chinese hjuwX (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription); the dictionary lists the first, tsyeX there, as a particle.

Script — 口 over 八.
只 (zhǐ).
Two pieces, top before bottom.
口 on top, three strokes, as you wrote it: a short vertical, a stroke that runs right and turns down, then the bar that closes the foot.
八 under it, two strokes: a stroke falling to the left, then a dot falling to the right.
3 + 2 = five.

[once you have stopped driving — this part needs your eyes or your hands; if you are driving, skip ahead to the next part]
Guided Practice.
[once you have stopped driving — write: 只 (zhǐ), all five, with the model in front of you]
[once you have stopped driving — cover: write it again from memory, and count the pieces, not the strokes]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
How many strokes in 只 (zhǐ)? (Five.) What is on top? (口.) What is under it? (八.)

Lesson 4 of 6.
只要 (zhǐyào) — as long as; provided (that).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for to demand (of someone), then the Mandarin for only if.

You'll want to know: 只要.
只要 — zhǐyào — "as long as; provided (that)".
只要不下雨我们就去 zhǐyào bú xiàyǔ wǒmen jiù qù — As long as it doesn't rain, we'll go.

The word, taken apart.
只要 (zhǐyào) is built from 只 (zhǐ) 'only' and 要 'to need'. The second is inherited from Middle Chinese 'jiewH (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'to bind by agreement'; the dictionary lists the first, tsyeX there, as a particle.

mǎnzú — "to meet (a requirement); to satisfy".
zhǐyào mǎnzú zhèxiē yāoqiú nǐ jiù néng qù — As long as you meet these requirements, you can go.

The word, taken apart.
mǎnzú is built from mǎn 'full' and zú 'enough', both inherited from Middle Chinese, manX and tsjowk (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'brimming' and the second 'the foot' and 'full'.
