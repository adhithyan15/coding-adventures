## You'll want to know: कृतवेदी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C468-KRTAVEDIN468-05]; assesses=[] -->

**कृतवेदी** — *kṛtavedī* — "grateful".

> कृतवेदी शिष्यः गुरुं सदा स्मरति।
>
> *kṛtavedī śiṣyaḥ guruṃ sadā smarati.*
>
> A grateful disciple always remembers his teacher.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**कृतवेदी** (*kṛtavedī*) is built from *kṛta*, "what was done", and *vedin*, "knowing", from the root √vid: one who knows what was done for him; MW gives "grateful" (MW).
