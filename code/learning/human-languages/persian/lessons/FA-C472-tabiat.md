## You'll want to know: طبیعت
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C472-TABIAT-01]; assesses=[] -->

**طبیعت** (*tabi'at*) — "nature".

> ما جمعه ها به دل طبیعت میرویم.
>
> *Mâ jom'e-hâ be del-e tabi'at miravim.*
>
> On Fridays we go out into the countryside.

*Del-e tabi'at* — the heart of nature, out in the wild.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*tabi'at* was borrowed from Arabic *ṭabīʿa* 'nature, natural disposition' (EtymDB).
