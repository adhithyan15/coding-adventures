## You'll want to know: o furto
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C287-ROUBO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**o furto** — "the theft (without violence)".

> O furto da bicicleta aconteceu de noite.
>
> *The bike was stolen at night.*

*Roubo* involves force or threat; *furto* is taking something quietly.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*furto* is inherited from Latin *fūrtum* "theft" (EtymDB), from *fūr* "a thief".
