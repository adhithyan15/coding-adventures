## You'll want to know: あらかじめ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C354-TENKI354-05]; assesses=[] -->

**あらかじめ** — *arakajime* — "in advance; beforehand".

> **よていが かわったら、あらかじめ おしえて ください。** — *yotei ga kawattara, arakajime oshiete kudasai* — "If the plans change, please let me know in advance."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**あらかじめ** (*arakajime*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, and its older origin is not settled. JMdict notes that it is usually written in kana (JMdict).
