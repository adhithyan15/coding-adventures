## You'll want to know: दृष्टिः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C469-INDRIYA469-02]; assesses=[] -->

**दृष्टिः** — *dṛṣṭiḥ* — "eyesight".

> वृद्धस्य दृष्टिः दुर्बला अस्ति।
>
> *vṛddhasya dṛṣṭiḥ durbalā asti.*
>
> The old man's eyesight is weak.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**दृष्टिः** (*dṛṣṭiḥ*) comes from the root √dṛś, "to see", the root of **दर्शयति** and **दृष्टवान्**; MW gives "seeing" and "sight, the faculty of seeing" (MW).
