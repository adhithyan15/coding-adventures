Spanish, chapter 477: El dinero de cada mes.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
el ahorro — the saving; savings.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for the mistake, then the Spanish for bored.

You'll want to know: el ahorro.
el ahorro — "the saving; savings".
Tengo unos ahorros para el verano. I have some savings for the summer.
In the plural, los ahorros is the money you have put aside.

Before the new one: say the Spanish for the saving, then the Spanish for the rent.

You'll want to know: la multa.
la multa — "the fine".
Me pusieron una multa por aparcar mal. They gave me a fine for parking badly.
Poner una multa — to give a fine. Aparcar is the word in Spain; in Latin America people say estacionar.
