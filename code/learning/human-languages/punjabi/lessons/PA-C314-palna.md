## You'll want to know: ਪਾਲਣਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C314-BABY314-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ਪਾਲਣਾ** — *pālṇā* — "to bring up".

> ਅਸੀਂ ਬੱਚਿਆਂ ਨੂੰ ਪਿਆਰ ਨਾਲ ਪਾਲਿਆ।
>
> *asīṁ bachchiāṁ nū̃ piār nāl pāliā.*
>
> We brought up the children with love.

Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਤਿੰਨ ਬੱਚੇ ਪਾਲੇ, *maiṁ tinn bachche pāle*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਪਾਲਣਾ** (*pālṇā*) is inherited from Sanskrit *pālayati*, "guards, protects"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *pālaṇā*, "to nourish, feed up", under it (CDIAL 8129).
