## You'll want to know: शोर
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C423-GARD423-03]; assesses=[] -->

**शोर** — *śor* — "noise".

> सड़क पर बहुत शोर है।
>
> *saṛak par bahut śor hai.*
>
> It is very noisy on the road.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**शोर** (*śor*) is usually traced to Persian *šor*, "noise, clamour".
