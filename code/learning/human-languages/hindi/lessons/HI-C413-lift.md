## You'll want to know: लिफ़्ट
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C413-SARAY413-03]; assesses=[] -->

**लिफ़्ट** — *lifṭ* — "a lift".

> लिफ़्ट बंद है, सीढ़ी से जाइए।
>
> *lifṭ band hai, sīṛhī se jāiye.*
>
> The lift is out of order; please take the stairs.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**लिफ़्ट** (*lifṭ*) is borrowed from English *lift*; it is feminine in Hindi.
