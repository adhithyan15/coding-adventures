Malayalam, chapter 182: To stir, To fry, To boil, To roast, To pour.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ഇളക്കുക (iḷakkuka) — to stir.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for to pull, then the Malayalam for to push.

You'll want to know: ഇളക്കുക.
ഇളക്കുക — iḷakkuka — "to stir".

The word, taken apart.
ഇളക്കുക (iḷakkuka) is native Dravidian, one word with Tamil iḷaku and Kannada eḷadu (DEDR 509). Its first sense is "to move, to shake".
