## You'll want to know: అగ్నిపర్వతం
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C474-SIKHARAM474-04]; assesses=[] -->

**అగ్నిపర్వతం** — *agniparvataṁ* — "a volcano".

> ఆ ద్వీపంలో ఒక అగ్నిపర్వతం ఉంది.
>
> *ā dvīpaṁlō oka agniparvataṁ undi.*
>
> There is a volcano on that island.

అగ్ని, fire, + పర్వతం, a mountain: a fire mountain.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**అగ్నిపర్వతం** (*agniparvataṁ*) is a compound of two words borrowed from Sanskrit: *agni*, "fire", and *parvata*, "a rock, a mountain" (CDIAL 7945).
