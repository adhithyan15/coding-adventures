## You'll want to know: कृतिः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C519-MURTI519-04]; assesses=[] -->

**कृतिः** — *kṛtiḥ* — "a creation; a work".

> एषा मूर्तिः मम पितुः कृतिः अस्ति।
>
> *eṣā mūrtiḥ mama pituḥ kṛtiḥ asti.*
>
> This statue is my father's work.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**कृतिः** (*kṛtiḥ*) comes from the root √kṛ, "to make, to do", with the ending *-ti*; MW gives *kṛti* as the act of doing, a creation, a work (MW).
