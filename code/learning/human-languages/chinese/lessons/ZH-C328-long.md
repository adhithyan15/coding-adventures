## You'll want to know: lóng
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C328-LEGEND328-04]; assesses=[] -->

*lóng* — "dragon".

> *chuánshuō lǐ lóng zhù zài shuǐ lǐ* — In legends, dragons live in water.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*lóng* is inherited from Middle Chinese *ljowng* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which calls it the chief of the scaly creatures and quotes an older book: clouds follow the dragon.

## What you've built
