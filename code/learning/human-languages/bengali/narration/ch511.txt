Bengali, chapter 511: Pots and Pouring.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
বাসন (bāsôn) — dishes; pots and pans.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for to wring out, then the Bengali for to scour.

You'll want to know: বাসন.
বাসন — bāsôn — "dishes; pots and pans".
খাওয়ার পরে আমি বাসন ধুই। Khāoyār pôre āmi bāsôn dhui. After eating I wash the dishes.

The word, taken apart.
বাসন (bāsôn) probably goes back to Sanskrit vāsana, "a box, a casket"; CDIAL gives no Bengali form there, but lists Prakrit vāsaṇa, "a vessel, a plate", and Hindi bāsan, "a pot, a dish" (CDIAL 11599).

Lesson 4 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a wide cooking pot, then the Bengali for a container.

You'll want to know: ঢালা.
ঢালা — ḍhālā — "to pour".
আমি জগ থেকে গ্লাসে জল ঢাললাম। Āmi jôg theke glāse jôl ḍhāllām. I poured water from the jug into the glass.
Past (he/she …): তিনি কাপে চা ঢাললেন, tini kāpe chā ḍhāllen.

The word, taken apart.
ঢালা (ḍhālā) comes from a reconstructed ḍhalati, "tilts"; CDIAL lists Bengali ḍhālā, "to pour" (CDIAL 5581).

Lesson 5 of 5.
ডোবানো (ḍobāno) — to dip; to sink (something).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a container, then the Bengali for to pour.

You'll want to know: ডোবানো.
ডোবানো — ḍobāno — "to dip; to sink (something)".
ছেলেটা বিস্কুটটা চায়ে ডোবাল। Chheleṭā biskuṭṭā chāye ḍobālô. The boy dipped the biscuit in his tea.
Past (he/she …): তিনি রুটিটা ডালে ডোবালেন, tini ruṭiṭā ḍāle ḍobālen.

The word, taken apart.
ডোবানো (ḍobāno) is the causative partner of ডোবা, "to sink", from a reconstructed ḍubb (CDIAL 5561): to make something go under.
