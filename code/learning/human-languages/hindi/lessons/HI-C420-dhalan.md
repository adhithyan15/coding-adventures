## You'll want to know: ढलान
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C420-DHALAN420-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ढलान** — *ḍhalān* — "a slope".

> ढलान पर गाड़ी धीरे चलाइए।
>
> *ḍhalān par gāṛī dhīre calāiye.*
>
> Drive slowly on the slope.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ढलान** (*ḍhalān*) is built on the stem of ढलना, "to slope, to go down", which goes back to *\*ḍhalati*, "bends over, falls", a form CDIAL reconstructs (CDIAL 5581); CDIAL does not list the noun itself.
