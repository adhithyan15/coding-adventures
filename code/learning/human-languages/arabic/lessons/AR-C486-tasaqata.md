## You'll want to know: تساقط
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C486-TAQALLUB486-03]; assesses=[] -->

**تساقط** — *tasāqaṭa* — "to fall steadily (of snow or leaves)".

> تساقط الثلج في الليل.
>
> *tasāqaṭa ath-thalj fī al-layl.*
>
> Snow fell steadily in the night.

Present: يتساقط, *yatasāqaṭ*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**تساقط** (*tasāqaṭa*) is built on the native Arabic root *s-q-ṭ*, "to fall" (Lane). It is Form VI, "to fall one after another".
