## You'll want to know: смерть
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C309-POKHORONY-03]; assesses=[] -->

**смерть** (*smert*) — "death".

> После смерти отца он стал хозяином фермы.
>
> *Pósle smérti ottsá on stal khozyáinom férmy.*
>
> After his father's death he became the owner of the farm.

Feminine; genitive *смерти*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*смерть* is inherited through Old East Slavic *sŭmĭrtĭ* from Proto-Slavic *sŭmĭrtĭ* (EtymDB), usually explained as *sŭ-* and the root of *умереть* "to die", PIE *mer-*.
