Persian, chapter 493: At the Doctor.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
معاینه کردن (mo'âyene kardan) — to examine (a patient).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to feel faint, then the Persian for to sweat.

You'll want to know: معاینه کردن.
معاینه کردن (mo'âyene kardan) — "to examine (a patient)".
دکتر گلوی او را معاینه کرد. doktor galu-ye u râ mo'âyene kard. The doctor examined her throat.
mo'âyene 'medical examination' + kardan; present stem kon-.

The word, taken apart.
mo'âyene is a loan, usually traced to Arabic muʿāyana, 'inspection, seeing with one's eyes'. EtymDB has no entry for it. kardan is inherited Persian.

Lesson 2 of 5.
نبض (nabz) — pulse.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to sweat, then the Persian for to examine.

You'll want to know: نبض.
نبض (nabz) — "pulse".
پرستار نبض بیمار را گرفت. parastâr nabz-e bimâr râ gereft. The nurse took the patient's pulse.
nabz gereftan — to take a pulse.

The word, taken apart.
nabz was borrowed from Arabic nabḍ, 'pulse' (EtymDB). EtymDB takes Urdu and Hindi nabz from the same Arabic word (EtymDB).

Lesson 3 of 5.
درمان کردن (darmân kardan) — to treat (an illness).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to examine, then the Persian for pulse.

You'll want to know: درمان کردن.
درمان کردن (darmân kardan) — "to treat (an illness)".
این دکتر بیماری پوست را درمان میکند. in doktor bimâri-ye pust râ darmân mikonad. This doctor treats skin disease.
darmân 'cure, treatment' + kardan. darmângâh 'clinic' is built on the same noun.

The word, taken apart.
darmân is inherited: EtymDB traces it to Middle Persian, where it meant 'remedy, medicine' (EtymDB). kardan, 'to do', is inherited too.

Lesson 4 of 5.
واکسن زدن (vâksan zadan) — to get vaccinated; to vaccinate.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for pulse, then the Persian for to treat.

You'll want to know: واکسن زدن.
واکسن زدن (vâksan zadan) — "to get vaccinated; to vaccinate".
بچه ها در مدرسه واکسن زدند. bachchehâ dar madrese vâksan zadand. The children had their jabs at school.
vâksan 'vaccine' + zadan 'to strike'. It covers both giving and getting a jab.

The word, taken apart.
vâksan is a modern loan, usually traced to French vaccin, 'vaccine'. EtymDB has no entry for it. zadan, 'to strike', is inherited Persian.
