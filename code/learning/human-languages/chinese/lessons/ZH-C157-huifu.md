## You'll want to know: huífù
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C157-HUIFU-01]; assesses=[] -->

*huífù* — "to reply (to a message)".

> *qǐng huífù* — Please reply.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*huífù* is built from *huí* 'to go back' and fù 'to return', the fù of *fùxí*. Both are inherited from Middle Chinese, *hwoj* and *bjuwk* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'to turn round' and 'to return'. The regular change from *bjuwk* gives fú; fù continues the Guangyun's other reading, *bjuwH*, glossed 'again'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ZH-LEX-C121-LEARN121-03, ZH-LEX-QING] -->

*huídá* answers a question; *huífù* answers a message.

请 in front makes it the polite request at the foot of a notice: *qǐng huífù*.
