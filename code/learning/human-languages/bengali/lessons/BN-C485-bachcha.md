## You'll want to know: বাচ্চা
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C485-BACHCHA485-04]; assesses=[] -->

**বাচ্চা** — *bāchchā* — "a baby; a small child".

> বাচ্চাটা সারারাত কাঁদল।
>
> *bāchchāṭā sārārāt kā̃dlô.*
>
> The baby cried all night.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**বাচ্চা** (*bāchchā*) is usually traced to Persian *bachcha*, "a child, the young of an animal".
