## You'll want to know: sostare
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C416-MOTORINO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**sostare** — "to stop briefly, to park (for a short time)".

> Qui è vietato sostare.
>
> *No stopping here.*

*Divieto di sosta* — no parking; *una sosta* is a short stop.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*sostare* is inherited from Latin *substāre* 'to stand firm, to hold out', built from *sub* and *stāre* 'to stand' (Lewis & Short).
