## You'll want to know: discrepar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C578-DESACUER-02]; assesses=[] -->

**discrepar** — "to disagree, to differ".

> Discrepo un poco de tu opinión.
>
> *I disagree a little with your opinion.*

A polite, formal verb: *discrepar de* someone or something.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*discrepar* is a learned borrowing from Latin *discrepare* 'to be out of tune, to differ', built from *dis-* 'apart' and *crepare* 'to rattle, to creak' (EtymDB).
