French, chapter 120: Strawberry, Cherry, Melon, Garlic, Flour.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
la fraise — a strawberry.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for beans, then the French for a cucumber.
