# el gorro — the cap with no invented origin

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-CAMISETA, ES-LEX-TALLA, ES-LEX-COSTAR] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Ask the size of the T-shirt, then ask its cost. (*¿Qué talla tiene
la camiseta? ¿Cuánto cuesta?*)

## Sounds you'll need
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

- *GO-rro*: hold the rolled **rr** clear. Contrast **gorro**, with *rr*, and
  **gordo**, with *rd*. The new word does not contain a *d*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-GORRO]; assesses=[] -->

**el gorro** — "the brimless cap," often round or close-fitting.

There is no secure etymology to turn into a story here. The Academy marks the
origin of **gorra** as uncertain and derives **gorro** from it. Learn the shape
instead: a **gorra** normally has a peak; a **gorro** is round or brimless.
They are not masculine and feminine forms of one object.
