## You'll want to know: निकम्मा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C472-PHURTI472-03]; assesses=[] -->

**निकम्मा** — *nikammā* — "good-for-nothing; idle".

> वह लड़का निकम्मा है, दिन भर कुछ नहीं करता।
>
> *vah laṛkā nikammā hai, din bhar kuch nahīṃ kartā.*
>
> That boy is good for nothing; he does nothing all day.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**निकम्मा** (*nikammā*) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Hindi *nikammā*, "useless, unemployed", under Sanskrit *niṣkarman* (CDIAL 7475), from *niḥ*, "without", and *karman*, "work".
