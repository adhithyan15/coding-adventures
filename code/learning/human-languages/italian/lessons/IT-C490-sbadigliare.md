## You'll want to know: sbadigliare
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C490-SONNO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**sbadigliare** — "to yawn".

> Lo spettacolo era così noioso che tutti sbadigliavano.
>
> *The show was so boring that everyone was yawning.*

The noun is *lo sbadiglio*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*sbadigliare* is usually traced to a Vulgar Latin verb *bataclāre* "to gape", with *s-* in front, a form usually linked to *batāre* "to gape"; EtymDB lists it beside French *bâiller* and Occitan *badalhar*, which come from *bataclāre*.
