## You'll want to know: ज़िंदगी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C559-KALPANA559-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ज़िंदगी** — *zindagī* — "life (as one lives it)".

> गाँव की ज़िंदगी शहर से अलग है।
>
> *Gā̃v kī zindagī śahar se alag hai.*
>
> Village life is different from city life.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ज़िंदगी** (*zindagī*) is usually traced to Persian *zindagī*, "life".
