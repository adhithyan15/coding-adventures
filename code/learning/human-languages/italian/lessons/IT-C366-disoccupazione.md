## You'll want to know: la disoccupazione
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C366-SOCIETA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**la disoccupazione** — "the unemployment".

> La disoccupazione tra i giovani è alta.
>
> *Unemployment among young people is high.*

Built on *disoccupato*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*disoccupazione* is built on *disoccupato* "unemployed", from *dis-* and *occupato*, from Latin *occupāre* "to seize, to fill" (Lewis & Short), built on *capere* "to take".
