## You'll want to know: すず
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C469-INSTR469-02]; assesses=[] -->

**すず** — *suzu* — "a small bell".

> **ねこの くびに すずが あります。** — *neko no kubi ni suzu ga arimasu* — "The cat has a little bell on its neck."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**すず** (*suzu*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji spelling was attached to it later (JMdict).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
