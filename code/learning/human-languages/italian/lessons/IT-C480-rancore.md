## You'll want to know: il rancore
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C480-RANCORE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**il rancore** — "grudge, resentment".

> Dopo tanti anni non ho più rancore verso di lui.
>
> *After so many years I no longer bear him a grudge.*

*Serbare rancore* — to hold a grudge.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*rancore* comes from Late Latin *rancōrem* "rancidness, a grudge" (EtymDB), from *rancēre* "to be rancid" (Lewis & Short).
