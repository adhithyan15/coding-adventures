## You'll want to know: 小时
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C97-XIAOSHI-01]; assesses=[] -->

**小时** — *xiǎoshí* — "hour".

> **一个小时** *yí ge xiǎoshí* — an hour

The next lesson puts the second character in your hand. It is the shí of *shíhou* and *xiǎoshíhou*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**小时** (*xiǎoshí*) is built from 小 'small' and 时 'time'. Both are inherited from Middle Chinese, *sjewX* and *dzyi* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), *dzyi* glossed 'a season; a time'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ZH-TONE-SANDHI-YI, ZH-RULE-TONE-SANDHI-YI-01] -->

一 before 个 rises: *yí ge xiǎoshí*, an hour. Before the other tones it falls: *yì tiān*, a day; *yì nián*, a year. Time words count with 一 as often as anything does.
