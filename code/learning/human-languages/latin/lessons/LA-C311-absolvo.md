## You'll want to know: absolvō, absolvere
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C311-DAMNO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**absolvō, absolvere** — "to acquit; to set free; to finish".

> Reus absolūtus est et laetus domum rediit.
>
> *The defendant was acquitted and went home happy.*

*absolvō, absolvere, absolvī, absolūtum*: *ab* + *solvō* (taught), to loosen from. English *absolve*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**absolvō** joins *ab*, "from", and *solvō*, "to loosen" (EtymDB): to set loose from a charge. English *absolve* comes from it.
