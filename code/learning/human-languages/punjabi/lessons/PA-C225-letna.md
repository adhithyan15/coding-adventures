# ਲੇਟਣਾ (leṭṇā) — to lie down

## You'll want to know: ਲੇਟਣਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C225-DIN225-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ਲੇਟਣਾ** — *leṭṇā* — "to lie down".

> ਦੁਪਹਿਰ ਨੂੰ ਮੈਂ ਮੰਜੇ ਉੱਤੇ ਲੇਟਦਾ ਹਾਂ।
>
> *dupahir nū̃ maiṁ manje utte leṭdā hāṁ.*
>
> In the afternoon I lie down on the cot.

Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਮੰਜੇ ਉੱਤੇ ਲੇਟਿਆ, *maiṁ manje utte leṭiā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਲੇਟਣਾ** (*leṭṇā*) is inherited from Sanskrit *lēṭyati*, "sleeps"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *leṭṇā*, "to lie down, roll" (CDIAL 11109).
