Persian, chapter 95: Towel, Cup, Pot, Frying pan, Stove.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
حوله (hule) — a towel.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for thread, then the Persian for soap.

You'll want to know: حوله.
