Lesson 1 of 5.
der Laden — a shop.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for far, then the German for on the right.

You'll want to know: der Laden.
der Laden — "a shop".

The word, taken apart.
Laden from Middle High German lade, "board, plank, shutter" from Old High German lado from Proto-Germanic laþô (EtymDB 2.1) — inherited: a shop was first the shutter-board goods were laid out on.

Bahnhof is built from the word for "way, track" (Old High German bana, "path") and the word for "yard, court" (Old High German hof) — both inherited (EtymDB 2.1): the railway's yard.

Lesson 5 of 5.
das Auto — a car.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for a railway station, then the German for an aeroplane.

You'll want to know: das Auto.
das Auto — "a car".

The word, taken apart.
Auto is short for the longer word for "automobile", taken from French and built from Greek autós, "self", and Latin mōbilis, "movable": a carriage that moves itself. EtymDB 2.1 has the French auto as its source.
