## You'll want to know: betonen
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C335-DISKUSSION-05]; assesses=[] -->

**betonen** — "to stress, to emphasize".

> Die Lehrerin betont, dass die Prüfung sehr wichtig ist.
>
> *The teacher stresses that the exam is very important.*

Inseparable: *sie betont, sie hat betont*. First of sounds: *die erste Silbe betonen* — to stress the first syllable.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*betonen* is built on *Ton* "tone, accent", which came through Latin *tonus* "a sound, a tone" (Lewis & Short) from Greek *tónos* "a stretching, a tone": you give a word more tone.
