## You'll want to know: शयनगृह
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C522-KHOLI522-02]; assesses=[] -->

**शयनगृह** — *śayangṛh* — "a bedroom".

> आमच्या घरात दोन शयनगृहं आहेत.
>
> *āmcyā gharāt don śayangṛhaṁ āhet.*
>
> There are two bedrooms in our house.

Neuter. Plural: शयनगृहं, *śayangṛhaṁ*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**शयनगृह** (*śayangṛh*) is a tatsama compound of Sanskrit *śayana*, "lying down, sleeping", and *gṛha*, "house" (Monier-Williams).
