# به تعویق انداختن (be ta'viq andâkhtan) — to postpone

## You'll want to know: به تعویق انداختن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C396-MONSAREF-03]; assesses=[] -->

**به تعویق انداختن** (*be ta'viq andâkhtan*) — "to postpone".

> دانشگاه امتحانها را به تعویق انداخت.
>
> *dâneshgâh emtehânhâ râ be ta'viq andâkht.*
>
> The university postponed the exams.

Formal. Without an object: *be ta'viq oftâdan* — to be postponed.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**به تعویق انداختن** is literally "to throw into delay": *ta'viq* is usually traced to Arabic *ta'wīq* "delaying", and *andâkhtan* "to throw" is native Persian.
