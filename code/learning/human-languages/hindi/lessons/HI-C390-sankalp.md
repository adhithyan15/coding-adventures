## You'll want to know: संकल्प
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C390-LAKSHYA390-03]; assesses=[] -->

**संकल्प** — *saṅkalp* — "a resolve, a firm decision".

> अगर संकल्प पक्का होगा तो कोई तुम्हें रोक नहीं सकेगा।
>
> *agar saṅkalp pakkā hogā to koī tumhẽ rok nahīṃ sakegā.*
>
> If your resolve is firm, nobody will be able to stop you.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**संकल्प** (*saṅkalp*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *saṃkalpa*, "a notion; a purpose, a will" (Monier-Williams).
