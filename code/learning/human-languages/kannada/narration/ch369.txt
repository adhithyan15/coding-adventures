Kannada, chapter 369: At Last, with My Own Eyes.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಅಂತೂ (antū) — at last; anyhow.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a turning point, then the Kannada for to take place.

You'll want to know: ಅಂತೂ.
ಅಂತೂ — antū — "at last; anyhow".
ಅಂತೂ ಮೂರು ವರ್ಷದ ನಂತರ ಅಣ್ಣ ಮನೆಗೆ ಬಂದನು. antū mūru varṣada nantara aṇṇa manege bandanu. At last, after three years, my elder brother came home.
Often with ಇಂತೂ: ಅಂತೂ (antū) ಇಂತೂ, somehow or other, at long last.

The word, taken apart.
ಅಂತೂ (antū) is native Dravidian: it is built on the pointing base a-, "that, yonder" (DEDR 1), with the ending -ū, "even".

Lesson 2 of 5.
ಕಣ್ಣಾರೆ (kaṇṇāre) — with one's own eyes.

[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to take place, then the Kannada for at last.

You'll want to know: ಕಣ್ಣಾರೆ.
ಕಣ್ಣಾರೆ — kaṇṇāre — "with one's own eyes".
ನಾನು ಆ ಅಪಘಾತವನ್ನು ಕಣ್ಣಾರೆ ನೋಡಿದೆ. nānu ā apaghātavannu kaṇṇāre nōḍide. I saw that accident with my own eyes.
ಕಣ್ಣು, eye, and ಆರೆ, to the full. ಮನಸಾರೆ, with all one's heart, is built the same way.

The word, taken apart.
ಕಣ್ಣಾರೆ (kaṇṇāre) is a native Dravidian compound: kaṇ, "eye" (DEDR 1159), and āra, "to the full of, fully" (DEDR 368).

Lesson 3 of 5.
ಅನಿರೀಕ್ಷಿತ (anirīkṣita) — unexpected.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for at last, then the Kannada for with one's own eyes.

You'll want to know: ಅನಿರೀಕ್ಷಿತ.
ಅನಿರೀಕ್ಷಿತ — anirīkṣita — "unexpected".
ಅದು ಒಂದು ಅನಿರೀಕ್ಷಿತ ಭೇಟಿ. adu ondu anirīkṣita bhēṭi. It was an unexpected visit.
Before a noun as it is; ಅನಿರೀಕ್ಷಿತವಾಗಿ, unexpectedly.

The word, taken apart.
ಅನಿರೀಕ್ಷಿತ (anirīkṣita) is borrowed from Sanskrit: the negative an- with nirīkṣita, "looked for", from nir-īkṣ, "to observe, to regard" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 4 of 5.
ಜೈಲು (jailu) — a jail.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for with one's own eyes, then the Kannada for unexpected.

You'll want to know: ಜೈಲು.
ಜೈಲು — jailu — "a jail".
ಅಂತೂ ಅವನು ಐದು ವರ್ಷದ ನಂತರ ಜೈಲಿನಿಂದ ಹೊರಗೆ ಬಂದನು. antū avanu aidu varṣada nantara jailininda horage bandanu. At last, after five years, he came out of jail.
Plural: ಜೈಲುಗಳು, jailugaḷu.
The formal word is ಕಾರಾಗೃಹ; ಜೈಲು (jailu) ಶಿಕ್ಷೆ is a prison sentence.

The word, taken apart.
ಜೈಲು (jailu) is a loan from English jail, with the final -u Kannada gives many loans.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ಕಾಣಿಸಿಕೊ (kāṇisiko) — to appear; to turn up.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for unexpected, then the Kannada for a jail.

You'll want to know: ಕಾಣಿಸಿಕೊ.
ಕಾಣಿಸಿಕೊ — kāṇisiko — "to appear; to turn up".
ತುಂಬಾ ವರ್ಷದ ನಂತರ ಆ ನಟ ಟೀವಿಯಲ್ಲಿ ಕಾಣಿಸಿಕೊಂಡನು. tumbā varṣada nantara ā naṭa ṭīviyalli kāṇisikoṇḍanu. After many years that actor appeared on television.
Past (I …): ಕಾಣಿಸಿಕೊಂಡೆ, kāṇisikoṇḍe.
Of people and things showing up: ಜ್ವರ ಕಾಣಿಸಿಕೊಂಡಿತು, a fever appeared.

The word, taken apart.
ಕಾಣಿಸಿಕೊ (kāṇisiko) is a native Dravidian compound: ಕಾಣಿಸು, "to be seen", from kāṇ "to see" (DEDR 1443), with -ko, from koḷ, "to take" (DEDR 2151).
