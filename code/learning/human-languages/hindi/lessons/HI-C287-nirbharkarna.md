# निर्भर करना (nirbhar karnā) — to depend

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[HI-CONCEPT-C20-MAUSAM-01, HI-GRAMMAR-C286-AGAR-TO-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: "Will we go tomorrow? If it rains, we will stay at home." (**अगर बारिश होगी, तो हम घर पर रहेंगे**.) There is a shorter answer: "it depends on the weather".

## You'll want to know: निर्भर करना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C287-NIRBHARKARNA-01]; assesses=[HI-CONCEPT-C20-MAUSAM-01, HI-LEX-C87-POST-01, HI-LEX-C245-THG245-02, HI-LEX-C245-THG245-03] -->

**निर्भर करना** — *nirbhar karnā* — "to depend (on)".

> **यह मौसम पर निर्भर करता है।**
>
> *yah mausam par nirbhar kartā hai.* — "It depends on the weather."

What it depends on takes **पर**, "on". **निर्भर** stays as it is, and **करना** carries the ending.

- **सब कुछ तुम पर निर्भर करता है।** (*sab kuch tum par nirbhar kartā hai.*) — Everything depends on you.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**निर्भर** (*nirbhar*) is a tatsama. Monier-Williams gives Sanskrit *nirbhara* as "full of; excessive"; how it came to mean "depending on" in Hindi is not recorded there. **करना** is the everyday "to do", from Sanskrit *karoti*, "does" (CDIAL 2814).
