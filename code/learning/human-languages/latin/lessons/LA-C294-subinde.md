## You'll want to know: subinde
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C294-IDENTIDEM-02]; assesses=[] -->

**subinde** — "immediately after; now and then".

> Prīmō rīsit, subinde flēvit.
>
> *At first she laughed, and immediately after she wept.*

Horace and later prose. In Seneca and Pliny it also means 'from time to time'.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**subinde** joins *sub*, "close after", and *inde*, "from there, then" (Lewis & Short): following closely on.
