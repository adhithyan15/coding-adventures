## You'll want to know: chāozhòng
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C418-CHECKIN418-05]; assesses=[] -->

*chāozhòng* — "overweight; over the weight limit".

> *nǐ de xiāngzi chāozhòng le* — Your suitcase is overweight.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*chāozhòng* is built from *chāo* 'to go beyond' and 重 'heavy', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *trhjew* and *drjowngX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to leap'.
