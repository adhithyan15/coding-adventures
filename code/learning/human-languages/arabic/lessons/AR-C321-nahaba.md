# نهب (nahaba) — to loot, to plunder

## You'll want to know: نهب
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C321-SARIQ321-05]; assesses=[] -->

**نهب** — *nahaba* — "to loot, to plunder".

> نهب اللصوص المحلات في الليل.
>
> *nahaba al-luṣūṣ al-maḥallāt fī al-layl.*
>
> Thieves looted the shops in the night.

Present: ينهب, *yanhab*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**نهب** (*nahaba*) is built on the native Arabic root *n-h-b* (Lane), "to take as plunder"; *nahb* is spoil.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
