## You'll want to know: 春晚
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C333-EVE333-01]; assesses=[] -->

**春晚** — *Chūnwǎn* — "the Spring Festival Gala".

> **每年春节我们都在家看春晚** *měinián Chūnjié wǒmen dōu zài jiā kàn Chūnwǎn* — Every lunar new year we have the gala on at home.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**春晚** (*Chūnwǎn*) is built from 春 'spring' and 晚 'evening', a short form of the longer name of the Spring Festival evening show. Both are inherited from Middle Chinese, *tsyhwin* and *mjonX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which calls the first the first of the four seasons and glosses the second 'dusk'.
