Hindi, chapter 333: Down to the River.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
घाट (ghāṭ) — a ghat, steps down to a river.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a bonfire, then the Hindi for an effigy.

You'll want to know: घाट.
घाट — ghāṭ — "a ghat, steps down to a river".
माफ़ कीजिए, घाट कैसे जाते हैं? māf kījiye, ghāṭ kaise jāte haiṁ? Excuse me, how do I get to the ghat?
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
घाट (ghāṭ) goes back to Sanskrit ghaṭṭa, "a landing place" (CDIAL 4414): the steps where people come down to bathe.

Lesson 2 of 5.
विसर्जन (visarjan) — immersion (of an image).

Warm-up.

Before the new one: say the Hindi for an effigy, then the Hindi for a ghat, steps down to a river.

You'll want to know: विसर्जन.
विसर्जन — visarjan — "immersion (of an image)".
शाम को नदी में मूर्ति का विसर्जन हुआ। śām ko nadī meṁ mūrti kā visarjan huā. In the evening the image was immersed in the river.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
विसर्जन (visarjan) is Sanskrit visarjana, "a letting go, a sending away" (Monier-Williams): at the end of a festival the image is let go into the water.

Lesson 3 of 5.
डुबोना (ḍubonā) — to dip; to immerse.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a ghat, steps down to a river, then the Hindi for immersion (of an image).

You'll want to know: डुबोना.
डुबोना — ḍubonā — "to dip; to immerse".
भक्तों ने फूल पानी में डुबोए। bhaktoṁ ne phūl pānī meṁ ḍuboe. The devotees dipped flowers in the water.
Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने कपड़ा पानी में डुबोया, usne kapṛā pānī meṁ ḍuboyā.

The word, taken apart.
डुबोना (ḍubonā) is the causative of डूबना, to sink, which CDIAL 5561 sets under ḍubb: to make something go under the water.

Lesson 4 of 5.
बिखरना (bikharnā) — to be scattered.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for immersion (of an image), then the Hindi for to dip.

You'll want to know: बिखरना.
बिखरना — bikharnā — "to be scattered".
हवा से फूल पूरे घाट पर बिखर गए। havā se phūl pūre ghāṭ par bikhar gae. The wind scattered the flowers all over the ghat.
Past (it …): वह माला बिखर गई, vah mālā bikhar gaī.

The word, taken apart.
बिखरना (bikharnā) is inherited from viṣkirati, "scatters", a form CDIAL reconstructs (CDIAL 11985). Flowers, beads or a crowd बिखरते हैं.

Lesson 5 of 5.
दीपदान (dīpdān) — the floating of lamps.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for to dip, then the Hindi for to be scattered.

You'll want to know: दीपदान.
दीपदान — dīpdān — "the floating of lamps".
शाम को घाट पर दीपदान हुआ। śām ko ghāṭ par dīpdān huā. In the evening lamps were floated on the river at the ghat.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
दीपदान (dīpdān) joins Sanskrit dīpa, "a lamp", and dāna, "a giving" (Monier-Williams): lamps given to the river, set afloat at dusk.
