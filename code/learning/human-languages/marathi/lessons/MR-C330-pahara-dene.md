## You'll want to know: पहारा देणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C330-SENAPATI330-04]; assesses=[] -->

**पहारा देणे** — *pahārā deṇe* — "to stand guard".

> दोन सैनिकांनी रात्रभर दारावर पहारा दिला.
>
> *don sainikānnī rātrabhar dārāvar pahārā dilā.*
>
> Two soldiers stood guard at the gate all night.

Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने पहारा दिला, *tyāne pahārā dilā*.

The place guarded takes -वर; पहारा is masculine, so the past is पहारा दिला.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पहारा देणे** (*pahārā deṇe*) is built from *pahārā*, 'a watch, a guard', of the family of Sanskrit *prahara*, 'a watch of about three hours' (CDIAL 8900), and *deṇe*, 'to give'.
