## You'll want to know: el difunto
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C555-VEJEZ-05]; assesses=[] -->

**el difunto** — "the deceased, the late".

> La familia del difunto llegó a la iglesia.
>
> *The family of the deceased arrived at the church.*

Formal. As an adjective: *mi difunto marido* — my late husband.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*difunto* comes from Latin *dēfūnctus*, the participle of *dēfungī* 'to finish, to discharge' (Lewis & Short). The dead are those who have finished their life's work.
