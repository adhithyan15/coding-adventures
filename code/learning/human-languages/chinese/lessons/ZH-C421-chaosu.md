## You'll want to know: chāosù
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C421-EXPRESS421-05]; assesses=[] -->

*chāosù* — "to break the speed limit".

> *nǐ kāi de tài kuài le chāosù le* — You're driving too fast: you're over the limit.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*chāosù* is built from *chāo* 'to go beyond' and 速 'speed', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *trhjew* and *suwk* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to leap'.
