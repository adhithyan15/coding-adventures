## You'll want to know: چوکنا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C536-MOHTAT536-02]; assesses=[] -->

**چوکنا** — *chaukannā* — "alert; on guard".

> اگر کھلاڑی چوکنا ہے تو گیند نہیں چھوٹے گی۔
>
> *agar khilāṛī chaukannā hai to gend nahīṅ chhūṭe gī.*
>
> If the player is alert, the ball won't slip past him.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**چوکنا** (*chaukannā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *caukannā*, "vigilant", under Sanskrit *catuṣkarṇa*, "heard by four ears" (CDIAL 4630): with both ears pricked up.
