## You'll want to know: mēta, mētae
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C335-AURIGA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**mēta, mētae** — "a turning post; a goal, a limit".

> Aurīga prope mētam cecidit.
>
> *The charioteer fell near the turning post.*

Feminine. The cone-shaped posts at each end of the track, where crashes happened. Also any limit or goal.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**mēta** is inherited from Proto-Italic *\*mētā*, "boundary" (EtymDB): a marker at the limit.
