## You'll want to know: der Lebkuchen
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C311-ADVENT-05]; assesses=[] -->

**der Lebkuchen** — "the gingerbread".

> Meine Oma backte jedes Jahr Lebkuchen.
>
> *My grandma baked gingerbread every year.*

Plural: *die Lebkuchen*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Lebkuchen* comes from Middle High German *lebekuoche* (EtymDB), with *Kuchen* "cake"; the origin of the first part is not settled.
