## You'll want to know: ખેલાડી
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C257-TEAM257-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ખેલાડી** — *khelāḍī* — "player".

> **તે સારો ખેલાડી હતો.** — *te sāro khelāḍī hato* — "He was a good player."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ખેલાડી** (*khelāḍī*) is built on *khel*, "play", with *-āḍī*. CDIAL lists Gujarati *khel* and *khelvũ* under *\*khēḍ*, *\*khēl-*, "play", reconstructed forms (CDIAL 3918).
