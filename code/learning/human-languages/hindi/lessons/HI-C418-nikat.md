## You'll want to know: निकट
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C418-NAZDIK418-02]; assesses=[] -->

**निकट** — *nikaṭ* — "near (formal)".

> होटल स्टेशन के निकट है।
>
> *hoṭal sṭeśan ke nikaṭ hai.*
>
> The hotel is near the station.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**निकट** (*nikaṭ*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *nikaṭa*, "being at the side, near" (Monier-Williams); it is the formal partner of नज़दीक and पास.
