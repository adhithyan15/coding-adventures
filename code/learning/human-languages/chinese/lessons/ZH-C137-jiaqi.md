## You'll want to know: jiàqī
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C137-HOLIDAY137-05]; assesses=[] -->

*jiàqī* — "holidays; time off".

> *fàngjià le jiàqī nǐ xiǎng qù nǎr* — We're on holiday now; where would you like to go?

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*jiàqī* is built from *jià* 'leave' and 期 'a set time', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *kaeH* and *gi* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription).
