## You'll want to know: कलाकार
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C535-CHITRA535-02]; assesses=[] -->

**कलाकार** — *kalākār* — "an artist".

> हा कलाकार मातीपासून सुंदर वस्तू बनवतो.
>
> *hā kalākār mātīpāsūn sundar vastū banavto.*
>
> This artist makes beautiful things out of clay.

Masculine.

One word for a man or a woman.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**कलाकार** (*kalākār*) is a compound built from Sanskrit *kalā*, "art", and *kāra*, "maker" (Monier-Williams).
