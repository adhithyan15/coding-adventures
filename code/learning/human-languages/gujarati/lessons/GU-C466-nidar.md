## You'll want to know: નીડર
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C466-SHAURYA466-02]; assesses=[] -->

**નીડર** — *nīḍar* — "fearless".

> નીડર મીરા અંધારામાં એકલી ગઈ.
>
> *nīḍar Mīrā andhārāmā̃ eklī gaī.*
>
> Fearless Mira went out alone in the dark.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**નીડર** (*nīḍar*) is inherited from Sanskrit *nirdara*, "fearless", built from *nir-*, "without", and *dara*, "fear"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati word (CDIAL 7339).
