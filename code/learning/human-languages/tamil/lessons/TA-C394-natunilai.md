## You'll want to know: நடுநிலை
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C394-ORUTALAIPPAT394-03]; assesses=[] -->

**நடுநிலை** — *naṭunilai* — "neutrality; neutral".

> நீதிபதி எப்போதும் நடுநிலையாக இருக்க வேண்டும்.
>
> *nītipati eppōtum naṭunilaiyāka irukka vēṇṭum.*
>
> A judge must always be neutral.

நடுநிலையாக, impartially.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**நடுநிலை** (*naṭunilai*) is native: *naṭu*, "middle, centre, equity" (DEDR 3584), and *nilai*, "a standing" (DEDR 3675): standing in the middle.
