## You'll want to know: इजाज़त
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C122-LATE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**इजाज़त** (*ijāzat*) — "permission".

**अब इजाज़त दीजिए** — "now please give me leave to go", the most courteous way to excuse yourself
from a host.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

A formal way out of a room.
