## You'll want to know: चर्वयति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C472-TALU472-03]; assesses=[] -->

**चर्वयति** — *carvayati* — "chews".

> बालः शनैः भोजनं चर्वयति।
>
> *bālaḥ śanaiḥ bhojanaṃ carvayati.*
>
> The child chews his food slowly.

Past (he …): सः भोजनं चर्वितवान्, *saḥ bhojanaṃ carvitavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**चर्वयति** (*carvayati*) comes from the root √carv, "to grind with the teeth, to chew" (MW).
