## You'll want to know: बंदरगाह
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C444-BANDARGAH444-01]; assesses=[] -->

**बंदरगाह** — *bandargāh* — "a harbour, a port".

> बंदरगाह पर बड़े जहाज़ खड़े थे।
>
> *bandargāh par baṛe jahāz khaṛe the.*
>
> Big ships were standing in the harbour.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**बंदरगाह** (*bandargāh*) is usually traced to Persian *bandargāh*, from *bandar*, "a port", and *-gāh*, "a place".
