## You'll want to know: компромисс
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C341-KOMPROMISS-03]; assesses=[] -->

**компромисс** (*kompromíss*) — "a compromise".

> После долгого спора мы нашли компромисс.
>
> *Pósle dólgogo spóra my nashlí kompromíss.*
>
> After a long argument we found a compromise.

Masculine. *Пойти на компромисс* — to make a compromise.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**компромисс** is usually traced to French *compromis*, from Latin *compromissum* "a mutual promise to accept what an arbiter decides" (Lewis & Short), built on *mittere* "to send".
