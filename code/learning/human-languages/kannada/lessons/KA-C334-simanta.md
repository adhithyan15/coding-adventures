# ಸೀಮಂತ (sīmanta) — a baby shower

## You'll want to know: ಸೀಮಂತ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C334-JANANA334-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸೀಮಂತ** — *sīmanta* — "a baby shower".

> ಅಕ್ಕನ ಸೀಮಂತಕ್ಕೆ ತುಂಬಾ ಹೆಂಗಸರು ಬಂದರು.
>
> *akkana sīmantakke tumbā heṅgasaru bandaru.*
>
> Many women came to my elder sister's baby shower.

A ceremony for a woman expecting her first child, with bangles, flowers and sweets.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸೀಮಂತ** (*sīmanta*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *sīmanta*, "the parting of the hair" (CDIAL 13436): the old rite for a pregnant woman began with parting her hair.

One more word for telling what happened.
