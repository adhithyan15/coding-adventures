Arabic, chapter 537: Hobbies indoors and out.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
تخييم (takhyīm) — camping.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to unwind, then the Arabic for to enjoy.

You'll want to know: تخييم.
تخييم — takhyīm — "camping".
نحب التخييم في الصحراء. nuḥibbu at-takhyīm fī aṣ-ṣaḥrāʾ. We love camping in the desert.

The word, taken apart.
تخييم (takhyīm) is built on the native Arabic root kh-y-m, "a tent" (Lane). Khayma, a tent, which you know, is from the same root.

What you've built.

فخار (fakhkhār) is built on the native Arabic root f-kh-r, "to boast, to glory in" (Lane). Lane gives fakhkhār, under the same root as fakhūr, proud, as baked pottery and baked clay.

Lesson 5 of 5.
كلمات متقاطعة (kalimāt mutaqāṭiʿa) — a crossword.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for photography, then the Arabic for pottery.

You'll want to know: كلمات متقاطعة.
كلمات متقاطعة — kalimāt mutaqāṭiʿa — "a crossword".
تحل أمي الكلمات المتقاطعة كل صباح. taḥullu ummī al-kalimāt al-mutaqāṭiʿa kull ṣabāḥ. My mother does the crossword every morning.

The word, taken apart.
كلمات متقاطعة (kalimāt mutaqāṭiʿa), "crossing words", is a modern calque built from kalima, a word, and mutaqāṭiʿa, "cutting across one another", from the native root q-ṭ-ʿ, "to cut" (Lane).
