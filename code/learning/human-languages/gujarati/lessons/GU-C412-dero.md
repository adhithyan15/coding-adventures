## You'll want to know: ડેરો
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C412-CAMP412-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ડેરો** — *ḍero* — "a tent; a camp (for a stay)".

> નદીના કિનારે અમે ડેરો નાખ્યો.
>
> *nadīnā kināre ame ḍero nākhyo.*
>
> We pitched our tent on the river bank.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ડેરો** (*ḍero*) goes back to *\*ḍēra*, "a resting place", a form CDIAL reconstructs; CDIAL lists the Gujarati word, "a tent, a shelter" (CDIAL 5564).
