Sanskrit, chapter 343: A Debate.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
वादः (vādaḥ) — a debate; a thesis.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for as a result, then the Sanskrit for argues for.

You'll want to know: वादः.
वादः — vādaḥ — "a debate; a thesis".
श्वः सभायां वादः भविष्यति। śvaḥ sabhāyāṃ vādaḥ bhaviṣyati. There will be a debate in the assembly tomorrow.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
वादः (vādaḥ) comes from the root √vad, "to speak"; MW gives vāda the senses "a thesis, an argument" and "discussion, controversy, dispute" (MW).

Lesson 5 of 5.
प्रतिपादयति (pratipādayati) — sets out (a case); explains.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a speaker, then the Sanskrit for a listener.

You'll want to know: प्रतिपादयति.
प्रतिपादयति — pratipādayati — "sets out (a case); explains".
अरुणः तर्केण मतं प्रतिपादयति। aruṇaḥ tarkeṇa mataṃ pratipādayati. Arun sets out his view with reasoning.
Past (he …): सः मतं प्रतिपादितवान्, saḥ mataṃ pratipāditavān.

The word, taken apart.
प्रतिपादयति (pratipādayati) is built on prati, "towards", and the root √pad, "to go", in its causative form; MW gives the senses "to establish, to prove, to set forth, to explain" (MW).
