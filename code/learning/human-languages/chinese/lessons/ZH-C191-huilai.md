## You'll want to know: 回来
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C191-BACK191-01]; assesses=[] -->

**回来** — *huílái* — "to come back".

> **我下个月五号回来** *wǒ xià ge yuè wǔ hào huílái* — I'll be back on the fifth of next month.

The new character, in pieces: 回 — six strokes: a large frame, a small 口 inside it, and the bar that closes the large frame last. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**回来** (*huílái*) is built from 回 'to turn back' and 来 'to come', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *hwoj* and *loj* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to turn round'.
