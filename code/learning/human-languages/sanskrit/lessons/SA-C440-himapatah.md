## You'll want to know: हिमपातः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C440-HIMA440-01]; assesses=[] -->

**हिमपातः** — *himapātaḥ* — "a snowfall".

> पर्वते रात्रौ हिमपातः भवति।
>
> *parvate rātrau himapātaḥ bhavati.*
>
> There is a snowfall on the mountain at night.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**हिमपातः** (*himapātaḥ*) is built from *hima*, "snow", and *pāta*, "a fall", from the root √pat, "to fall", which also gives *patati*, "falls"; MW gives the compound as a fall of snow (MW).
