Telugu, chapter 124: Deer, Fox, Snake, Tortoise, Parrot.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
జింక (jiṅka) — a deer.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a monkey, then the Telugu for a rabbit.

You'll want to know: జింక.
జింక — jiṅka — "a deer".

The word, taken apart.
జింక (jiṅka) is native Dravidian, one word with Kannada jiṅke (DEDR 2504). DEDR lists Telugu jiṅka, "deer".
