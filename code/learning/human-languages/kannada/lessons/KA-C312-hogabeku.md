# ಹೋಗಬೇಕು (hōgabēku) — must, should, must not

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[KA-LEX-C72-WANT-03, KA-GRAMMAR-C72-WANT-04, KA-GRAMMAR-C311-HAGAGI-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "I want tea". (*nanage cahā bēku*.) Then "Yesterday it rained. So I stayed at home." (*ninne maḷe bantu. hāgāgi nānu maneyallē idde.*)

[PAUSE 1s] **ಬೇಕು** says a thing is needed. Put it on a verb, and the action is needed.

## You'll want to know: -ಬೇಕು on a verb
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-GRAMMAR-C312-OBLIGATIVE-01]; assesses=[KA-LEX-C04-NAALE-SIGONA-01, KA-LEX-C64-JOIN-04, KA-LEX-C48-ROLE-03, KA-LEX-C32-NOODU-01, KA-LEX-C05-MAADU-01, KA-LEX-C33-OODU-01, KA-LEX-C147-ACT147-01] -->

> **ನಾನು ನಾಳೆ ಬೇಗ ಹೋಗಬೇಕು.**
>
> *nānu nāḷe bēga hōgabēku.* — "I must go early tomorrow."

The verb drops its **-ಉ** and takes **-ಅ**: **ಹೋಗು** → **ಹೋಗ-**, then **ಬೇಕು**: **ಹೋಗಬೇಕು**. In the same way **ಮಾಡಬೇಕು**, **ಓದಬೇಕು**, **ಬರಬೇಕು**. A verb in **-ಇ** or **-ಎ** adds **-ಯ** first: **ಕುಡಿ** → **ಕುಡಿಯಬೇಕು**, **ತೊಳೆ** → **ತೊಳೆಯಬೇಕು**.

**ಬೇಕು** stays the same for every person: **ನಾನು ಹೋಗಬೇಕು**, **ಅವಳು ಹೋಗಬೇಕು**, **ನಾವು ಹೋಗಬೇಕು**. Said about someone else, it is advice:

> **ನೀವು ವೈದ್ಯರನ್ನು ನೋಡಬೇಕು.**
>
> *nīvu vaidyarannu nōḍabēku.* — "You should see a doctor."

**ವೈದ್ಯರನ್ನು** (*vaidyarannu*, "the doctor"): **ವೈದ್ಯ** with the respectful **-ರು**, as the one you see.

## You'll want to know: -ಬಾರದು, must not
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-GRAMMAR-C312-PROHIBITIVE-01]; assesses=[KA-ETYMON-C24-RATRI-01, KA-LEX-C73-ABLE-01, KA-LEX-C39-KAAPI-01, KA-LEX-C220-VASTU220-05] -->

> **ರಾತ್ರಿ ತುಂಬಾ ಕಾಫಿ ಕುಡಿಯಬಾರದು.**
>
> *rātri tumbā kāphi kuḍiyabāradu.* — "You shouldn't drink a lot of coffee at night."

**ಬಾರದು** sits where **ಬೇಕು** sat: **ಕುಡಿಯಬಾರದು**, "must not drink"; **ಹೋಗಬಾರದು**, "must not go". With no person named, it is a rule for anyone, and the right shape for a notice: **ಇಲ್ಲಿ ಕಸ ಹಾಕಬಾರದು** (*illi kasa hākabāradu*, "do not throw rubbish here").
