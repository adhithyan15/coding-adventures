## You'll want to know: ほとんど
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C316-MONOGATARI316-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ほとんど** — *hotondo* — "almost; nearly all".

> **ともだちの なまえは ほとんど わすれました。** — *tomodachi no namae wa hotondo wasuremashita* — "I have forgotten nearly all my friends' names."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ほとんど** (*hotondo*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, and it is usually written in kana (JMdict).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
