## You'll want to know: かんこう
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C402-KANKOU402-01]; assesses=[] -->

**かんこう** — *kankō* — "tourism; sightseeing".

> **この しまに かんこうに いきました。** — *kono shima ni kankō ni ikimashita* — "We went sightseeing on this island."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**かんこう** (*kankō*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *kan*, "view" (Middle Chinese *kwan*) and *kō*, "light" (*kwang*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
