## You'll want to know: मसलन
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C350-MISAAL350-02]; assesses=[] -->

**मसलन** — *maslan* — "for instance".

> मुझे फल पसंद हैं, मसलन आम और केला।
>
> *mujhe phal pasand haiṁ, maslan ām aur kelā.*
>
> I like fruit, for instance mangoes and bananas.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**मसलन** (*maslan*) is usually traced to Arabic *mathalan*, "for example", which reached Hindi through Persian.
