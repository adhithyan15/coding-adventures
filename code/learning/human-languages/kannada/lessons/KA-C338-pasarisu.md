# ಪಸರಿಸು (pasarisu) — to spread far

## You'll want to know: ಪಸರಿಸು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C338-VADANTI338-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ಪಸರಿಸು** — *pasarisu* — "to spread far".

> ಆ ಸುದ್ದಿ ನಗರದ ತುಂಬಾ ಪಸರಿಸಿತು.
>
> *ā suddi nagarada tumbā pasarisitu.*
>
> That news spread all over the city.

Past (it …): ಪಸರಿಸಿತು, *pasarisitu*.

News, fame and fragrance spread far: ಸುಗಂಧ ಪಸರಿಸಿತು, a fragrance spread.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಪಸರಿಸು** (*pasarisu*) is built on Sanskrit *prasara*, "advance, extension", in its Prakrit shape *pasara* (CDIAL 8824), with the Kannada verb ending -*isu*.
