## You'll want to know: la météo
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C460-METEO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**la météo** — "the weather forecast; the weather".

> Selon la météo, il va faire beau demain.
>
> *According to the forecast it is going to be nice tomorrow.*

Short for *la météorologie*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*météo* is short for *météorologie*, a learned borrowing from Greek *meteōrología*, from *metéōros* 'high in the air' (EtymDB lists the short form).
