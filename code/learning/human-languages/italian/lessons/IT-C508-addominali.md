## You'll want to know: gli addominali
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C508-PALESTRA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**gli addominali** — "abs; sit-ups".

> L'allenatore ci fa fare cento addominali.
>
> *The coach makes us do a hundred sit-ups.*

The muscles, and also the exercise: *fare gli addominali*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*addominali* is built on *addome* "belly", a learned borrowing from Latin *abdōmen* (Lewis & Short).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.
