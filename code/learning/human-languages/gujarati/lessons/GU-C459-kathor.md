## You'll want to know: કઠોર
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C459-KOMAL459-02]; assesses=[] -->

**કઠોર** — *kaṭhor* — "harsh; stern".

> પિતાએ કઠોર અવાજે જવાબ આપ્યો.
>
> *pitāe kaṭhor avāje javāb āpyo.*
>
> Father answered in a harsh voice.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**કઠોર** (*kaṭhor*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *kaṭhora*, "hard, solid, stiff", as Monier-Williams records it.
