# A short clock for six known fields

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-A1-FORM-MIXED-CUES-01] -->

Close the old cards. Keep a blank page and a three-minute timer. The six marks
below mix familiar values; no model is shown.

## Writing — independent timed form
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-A1-FORM-TIMED-01]; assesses=[PA-FORM-SIX-CUE-01, PA-FORM-SIX-NO-MODEL-01, PA-A1-FORM-MIXED-CUES-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: timed-assessment-production -->

Start the three-minute timer. Retrieve each marked value and write all six
fields in Gurmukhi, with Gurmukhi digits for age and phone. Stop on time and
leave unfinished lines as evidence. Keep models and aids closed.

> ਨਾਂ (A · ਕ): ______
>
> ਭਾਸ਼ਾ (B · ਖ): ______
>
> ਰਿਹਾਇਸ਼ (A · ਕ): ______
>
> ਕੰਮ (B · ਖ): ______
>
> ਉਮਰ (A · ਕ): ______
>
> ਫ਼ੋਨ (B · ਖ): ______

## Guided Practice — record, then check
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-A1-FORM-TIMED-01, PA-FORM-SIX-REPAIR-01] -->

Record completed lines and elapsed seconds. After the timer, check values,
spelling, spacing, digit order, and phone grouping separately. Preserve the
first attempt; repair is not timed evidence.
