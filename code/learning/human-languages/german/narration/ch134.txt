Lesson 1 of 5.
langweilig — boring.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for famous, then the German for interesting.

You'll want to know: langweilig.
langweilig — "boring".

The word, taken apart.
langweilig is built from the word for "long" + the word for "a while", Old High German wīla, English while (EtymDB 2.1), + -ig: when time feels long.

Warm-up.

Before the new one: say the German for interesting, then the German for boring.

You'll want to know: schwierig.
schwierig — "difficult".

The word, taken apart.
schwierig from Middle High German swiric, "full of sores": swër, "ulcer" (compare Geschwür, "abscess"), + -ic. What was full of sores became troublesome, then difficult.

The word, taken apart.
fertig from Old High German fartīg, "ready for the journey" (EtymDB 2.1), built on the word for "a journey", from fahren. Ready to go became finished.

You'll want to know: sicher.
sicher — "safe; sure".

The word, taken apart.
sicher is an early West Germanic loan (Old High German sihhur, Old English sicor) from Latin sēcūrus, "free from care" (EtymDB 2.1). English secure is a later loan of the same Latin word.
