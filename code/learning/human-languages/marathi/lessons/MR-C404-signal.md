## You'll want to know: सिग्नल
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C404-VALSA404-03]; assesses=[] -->

**सिग्नल** — *signal* — "a traffic signal".

> सिग्नल लाल असला की गाड्या थांबतात.
>
> *signal lāl aslā kī gāḍyā thāmbtāt.*
>
> When the signal is red, the cars stop.

Masculine.

सिग्नल तोडणे, to jump a signal.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सिग्नल** (*signal*) is a loan from English *signal*.
