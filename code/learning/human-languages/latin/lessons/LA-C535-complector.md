## You'll want to know: complector, complectī
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C535-AMAN-02]; assesses=[] -->

**complector, complectī** — "to clasp, embrace; to include".

> Amīcī post longum tempus sē complectuntur.
>
> *The friends embrace after a long time.*

Deponent: *complector, complectī, complexus sum*. Also to take in, to include. English *complex* comes from its participle.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**complector** is built on *con-* and *plectō* "to twine" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short). English *complex* comes from its participle *complexus*.
