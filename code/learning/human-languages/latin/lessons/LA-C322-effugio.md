## You'll want to know: effugiō, effugere
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C322-FUGA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**effugiō, effugere** — "to escape".

> Paucī ex incendiō effūgērunt.
>
> *Few escaped from the fire.*

*effugiō, effugere, effūgī*: *ex* + *fugiō*, also with the accusative: *perīculum effugere*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**effugiō** is built on *fugiō*, "to flee", with *ex-*, "out" (Lewis & Short): to flee out of danger.
