Hindi, chapter 155: Soft, Hard, Clean, Dirty, Fast.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
नरम (naram) — soft.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for heavy, then the Hindi for light.
[pause 2 seconds]
Describe with words that never bend: आसान, मुश्किल, सुंदर, बदसूरत.

You'll want to know: नरम.
नरम — naram — "soft".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: naram]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: naram, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say naram]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Hindi for heavy, then the Hindi for light, then say naram again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does नरम (naram) mean? ("Soft".) Say it once more.
