Italian, chapter 307: Benvenuto al mondo.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
la nascita — the birth.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for missing, then the Italian for to survive.

You'll want to know: la nascita.
la nascita — "the birth".
La nascita di mia figlia è stata il giorno più bello della mia vita. My daughter's birth was the best day of my life.
La data di nascita — the date of birth.

The word, taken apart.
nascita is built on nascere "to be born", which is inherited from Latin nāscī (Lewis & Short).

What you've built.

You'll want to know: il primogenito.
il primogenito — "the first-born child".
Il primogenito si chiama come il nonno. The first-born is named after his grandfather.
A daughter is la primogenita.

The word, taken apart.
primogenito is a learned borrowing from Latin prīmōgenitus "first-born" (EtymDB), from prīmus "first" and genitus "born".

Lesson 4 of 5.
svezzare — to wean.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for childbirth, then the Italian for the first-born child.

You'll want to know: svezzare.
svezzare — "to wean".
Abbiamo svezzato il bambino a sei mesi. We weaned the baby at six months.
Literally to take out of a habit: vezzo is an old word for habit.

The word, taken apart.
svezzare is built on vezzo "habit", from Latin vitium "a fault" (Lewis & Short), with s- "out of".

Lesson 5 of 5.
il ciuccio — the dummy, the pacifier.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for the first-born child, then the Italian for to wean.

You'll want to know: il ciuccio.
il ciuccio — "the dummy, the pacifier".
Il bambino non dorme senza ciuccio. The baby will not sleep without his dummy.
Informal and very common; in shops also il succhiotto.

The word, taken apart.
ciuccio is built on ciucciare "to suck", which is usually explained as a word that imitates the sound of sucking (EtymDB has no entry).
