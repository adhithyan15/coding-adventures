## You'll want to know: सन्दंशः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C507-BHASTRA507-02]; assesses=[] -->

**सन्दंशः** — *sandaṃśaḥ* — "tongs".

> लोहकारः सन्दंशेन अंगारं गृह्णाति।
>
> *lohakāraḥ sandaṃśena aṅgāraṃ gṛhṇāti.*
>
> The blacksmith picks up a live coal with the tongs.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सन्दंशः** (*sandaṃśaḥ*) is built on *sam*, "together", and the root √daṃś, "to bite": a biting together; MW gives *saṃdaṃśa* as a pair of tongs or pincers (MW).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
