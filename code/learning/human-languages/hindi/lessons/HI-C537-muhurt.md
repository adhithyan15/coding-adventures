## You'll want to know: मुहूर्त
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C537-KARYAKRAM537-04]; assesses=[] -->

**मुहूर्त** — *muhūrt* — "an auspicious moment (to begin something)".

> पुजारी ने शादी का मुहूर्त निकाला।
>
> *Pujārī ne śādī kā muhūrt nikālā.*
>
> The priest worked out the auspicious time for the wedding.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**मुहूर्त** (*muhūrt*) is a tatsama, taken whole from Sanskrit *muhūrta*, "a moment, a short space of time" (Monier-Williams). Hindi uses it for the moment the stars favour.
