## You'll want to know: বিবেক
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C465-BIBEK465-01]; assesses=[] -->

**বিবেক** — *bibek* — "conscience".

> আমার বিবেক বলছে, টাকাটা ফিরিয়ে দেওয়া উচিত।
>
> *āmār bibek bôlchhe, ṭākāṭā phiriye deoyā uchit.*
>
> My conscience tells me the money ought to be given back.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**বিবেক** (*bibek*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *viveka*, "discrimination, distinction" (Monier-Williams); Bengali uses it for the inner judge that tells right from wrong.
