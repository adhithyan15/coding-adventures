## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**భూకంపం** (*bhūkampaṁ*) is Sanskrit: *bhū*, "world; ground" (CDIAL 9546), and *kampa*, "tremor" (CDIAL 2765), with the Telugu ending *-ṁ*.
