## You'll want to know: پان
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C415-DHABA415-03]; assesses=[] -->

**پان** — *pān* — "a betel leaf; paan".

> کھانے کے بعد کچھ لوگ پان کھاتے ہیں۔
>
> *khāne ke bād kuchh log pān khāte haiṅ.*
>
> After a meal some people chew paan.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**پان** (*pān*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *pān*, "a betel leaf", under Sanskrit *parṇá*, "a leaf" (CDIAL 7918).
