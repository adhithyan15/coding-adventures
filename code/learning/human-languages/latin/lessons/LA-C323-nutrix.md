## You'll want to know: nūtrīx, nūtrīcis
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C323-PARTUS-05]; assesses=[] -->

**nūtrīx, nūtrīcis** — "a nurse, a wet nurse".

> Nūtrīx īnfantem in bracchiīs tenēbat.
>
> *The nurse was holding the baby in her arms.*

Feminine, 3rd declension, from *nūtriō*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**nūtrīx** is built on *nūtriō*, "to suckle, to feed" (EtymDB), with the feminine ending *-trīx*.
