## You'll want to know: допрашивать
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C292-DOPROS-03]; assesses=[] -->

**допрашивать** (*dopráshivat*) — "to question, to interrogate".

> Следователь допрашивал свидетелей весь день.
>
> *Slédovatel dopráshival svidételey ves den.*
>
> The investigator questioned witnesses all day.

Imperfective; perfective *допросить* (*doprosít*), *допрошу*. It takes the accusative.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*допрашивать* is built on *до-* and the root of *просить* "to ask", inherited from Proto-Slavic *prositi*, from PIE *preḱ-* "to ask" (EtymDB): to ask to the end.
