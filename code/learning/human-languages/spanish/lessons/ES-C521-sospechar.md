## You'll want to know: sospechar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C521-PISTA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**sospechar** — "to suspect".

> Nadie sospechaba de él.
>
> *Nobody suspected him.*

*Sospechar de alguien* — to suspect someone.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*sospechar* is inherited from Latin *suspectāre* 'to look up at, to suspect' (EtymDB), built on *suspicere* 'to look up at'.
