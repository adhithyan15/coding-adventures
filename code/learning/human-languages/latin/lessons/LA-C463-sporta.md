## You'll want to know: sporta, sportae
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C463-HOLITOR-03]; assesses=[] -->

**sporta, sportae** — "a plaited basket, a hamper".

> Ancilla pānem et cāseum in sportā portat.
>
> *The maid carries bread and cheese in a hamper.*

First declension, feminine: *sporta, sportae f.* Its diminutive *sportula* was the food basket, later the cash, a patron gave his clients.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*sporta* is usually traced to Greek *spyris* 'plaited basket' (Lewis & Short compare it); EtymDB links it with PIE *sper-* 'to twist, to turn'. The path is not settled.
