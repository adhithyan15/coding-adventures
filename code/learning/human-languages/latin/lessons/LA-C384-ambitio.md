## You'll want to know: ambitiō, ambitiōnis
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C384-SUFFRAGIUM-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ambitiō, ambitiōnis** — "canvassing; ambition".

> Ambitiō eius omnibus nōta erat.
>
> *His canvassing was known to all.*

*ambitiō, ambitiōnis* f., built on *ambiō*, to go round: the candidate went round asking for votes. English *ambition*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ambitiō* is the noun built on *ambiō* "to go round" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short), from *amb-* "around" and *eō* "to go". English *ambition* comes from it.
