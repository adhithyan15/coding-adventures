## You'll want to know: ঝিল
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C433-JHIL433-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ঝিল** — *jhil* — "a lake; a marshy pool".

> শীতে ঝিলে অনেক পাখি আসে।
>
> *shīte jhile ônek pākhi āse.*
>
> In winter many birds come to the lake.

হ্রদ is a lake in geography; ঝিল is the pool or marsh lake near a village.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ঝিল** (*jhil*) is inherited: CDIAL lists Bengali *jhil*, "a lake, a pool", under the reconstructed *jhilla* (CDIAL 5392).
