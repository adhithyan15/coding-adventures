## You'll want to know: सम्भारः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C528-AYOJAYATI528-03]; assesses=[] -->

**सम्भारः** — *sambhāraḥ* — "supplies; provisions".

> यदि अतिथयः आगच्छन्ति, तर्हि सम्भारः आवश्यकः अस्ति।
>
> *yadi atithayaḥ āgacchanti, tarhi sambhāraḥ āvaśyakaḥ asti.*
>
> If guests are coming, we need supplies.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सम्भारः** (*sambhāraḥ*) is built on *sam*, "together", and the root √bhṛ, "to bear, to bring", which also gives *bharati*; MW gives *sambhāra* as preparation, provision, the materials needed (MW).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
