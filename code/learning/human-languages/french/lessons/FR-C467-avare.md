## You'll want to know: avare
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C467-DEFAUTS-03]; assesses=[] -->

**avare** — "stingy, mean with money; miser".

> Il n'est pas avare, mais il compte chaque euro.
>
> *He is not stingy, but he counts every euro.*

Also a noun: *un avare* — a miser.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*avare* is a learned borrowing from Latin *avārus* "greedy", related to *avēre* "to long for, to crave" (Lewis & Short).
