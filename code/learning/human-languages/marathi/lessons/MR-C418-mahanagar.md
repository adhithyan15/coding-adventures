## You'll want to know: महानगर
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C418-NAGAR418-02]; assesses=[] -->

**महानगर** — *mahānagar* — "a metropolis".

> पुणे आता एक मोठं महानगर आहे.
>
> *puṇe ātā ek moṭhaṁ mahānagar āhe.*
>
> Pune is now a big metropolis.

Neuter.

महा-, great: a great city.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**महानगर** (*mahānagar*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *mahānagara*, "a great city" (Monier-Williams).
