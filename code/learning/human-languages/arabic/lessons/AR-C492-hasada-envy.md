## You'll want to know: حسد
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C492-GHIRA492-04]; assesses=[] -->

**حسد** — *ḥasada* — "to envy".

> حسد الموظف زميله على راتبه.
>
> *ḥasada al-muwaẓẓaf zamīlahu ʿalā rātibihi.*
>
> The employee envied his colleague his salary.

Present: يحسد, *yaḥsud*.

It takes على for the thing envied.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**حسد** (*ḥasada*) is built on the native Arabic root *ḥ-s-d*, "to envy" (Lane). Lane gives yaḥsudu as the usual present.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
