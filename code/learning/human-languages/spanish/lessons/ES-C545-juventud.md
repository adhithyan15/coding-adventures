## You'll want to know: la juventud
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C545-NINEZ-03]; assesses=[] -->

**la juventud** — "youth; young people".

> En mi juventud viajé mucho.
>
> *In my youth I travelled a lot.*

*La juventud* is also young people as a group.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*juventud* comes from Latin *iuventūs*, *iuventūtis* 'youth', built on *iuvenis* 'young' (Lewis & Short). English *juvenile* is from the same root.
