## You'll want to know: die Schlacht
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C318-KRIEG-03]; assesses=[] -->

**die Schlacht** — "the battle".

> Die Schlacht fand vor der Stadt statt.
>
> *The battle took place outside the town.*

Plural: *die Schlachten*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Schlacht* is inherited from Old High German *slahta*, Proto-Germanic *slahtō* "a striking" (EtymDB), from the root of *schlagen* "to strike".
