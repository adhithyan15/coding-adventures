# ಕೆಮ್ಮು (kemmu) — a cough

## You'll want to know: ಕೆಮ್ಮು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C82-UNWELL-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ಕೆಮ್ಮು** (*kemmu*) — "a cough", and also the verb "to cough".

**ಕೆಮ್ಮು ಇದೆ** (*kemmu ide*) — "there is a cough", the plain way to tell a doctor.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

A second complaint for the doctor.
