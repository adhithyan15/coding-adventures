## You'll want to know: ભાવિ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C536-MUHURT536-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ભાવિ** — *bhāvi* — "the future".

> જો તમે મહેનત કરશો તો તમારું ભાવિ સારું બનશે.
>
> *jo tame mahenat karśo to tamārũ bhāvi sārũ banśe.*
>
> If you work hard, your future will be good.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ભાવિ** (*bhāvi*) is a tatsama, from Sanskrit *bhāvin*, "about to be, future" (Monier-Williams).
