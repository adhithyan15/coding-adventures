## You'll want to know: el jardinero
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C475-OFICIO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**el jardinero** — "the gardener".

> El jardinero viene los martes.
>
> *The gardener comes on Tuesdays.*

A woman who does the job is *la jardinera*; *el jardín* is the garden.
