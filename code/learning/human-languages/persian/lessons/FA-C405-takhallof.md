## You'll want to know: تخلف
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C405-QANUNI-03]; assesses=[] -->

**تخلف** (*takhallof*) — "an offence, a violation".

> این کار یک تخلف جدی است.
>
> *in kâr yek takhallof-e jeddi ast.*
>
> This is a serious offence.

Plural *takhallofât*. Lighter than a crime, such as traffic offences.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**تخلف** is usually traced to Arabic *takhalluf*, a verbal noun usually explained as staying behind or failing to comply, from the same Arabic family as *mokhâlef*.
