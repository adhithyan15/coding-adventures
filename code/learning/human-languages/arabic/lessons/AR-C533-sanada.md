## You'll want to know: ساند
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C533-RIAYA533-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ساند** — *sānada* — "to stand by".

> ساندني أخي في وقت صعب.
>
> *sānadanī akhī fī waqt ṣaʿb.*
>
> My brother stood by me at a difficult time.

Present: يساند, *yusānid*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ساند** (*sānada*) is built on the native Arabic root *s-n-d*, "to lean on, to prop" (Lane). Form III sānada is to be a prop for someone.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
