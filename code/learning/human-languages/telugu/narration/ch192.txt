Telugu, chapter 192: Wick, Latch, Paintbrush, Necklace, Braid.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
వత్తి (vatti) — a wick.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a net, then the Telugu for a weighing scale.

You'll want to know: వత్తి.
వత్తి — vatti — "a wick".

[pause 3 seconds]
