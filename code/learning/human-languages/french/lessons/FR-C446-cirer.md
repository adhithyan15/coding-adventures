## You'll want to know: cirer
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C446-CORDONNIER-03]; assesses=[] -->

**cirer** — "to polish, to wax".

> Le dimanche, mon père cire ses chaussures.
>
> *On Sundays my father polishes his shoes.*

*La cire* — wax; *le cirage* — shoe polish.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*cirer* is built on *cire* 'wax', which is inherited from Latin *cēra* (Lewis & Short).
