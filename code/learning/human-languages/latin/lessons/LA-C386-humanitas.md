## You'll want to know: hūmānitās, hūmānitātis
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C386-SOCIETAS-02]; assesses=[] -->

**hūmānitās, hūmānitātis** — "human nature; kindness; culture".

> Hospitem summā hūmānitāte accēpit.
>
> *He received the guest with the greatest kindness.*

*hūmānitās, hūmānitātis* f., built on *hūmānus*, human. The kindness and learning that make a person truly human. English *humanity*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*hūmānitās* is built on *hūmānus* "human" (Lewis & Short), an adjective kin to *homō* "a human being". English *humanity* comes from it.
