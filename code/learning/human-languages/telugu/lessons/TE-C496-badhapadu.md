# బాధపడు (bādhapaḍu) — to feel sad; to grieve

## You'll want to know: బాధపడు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C496-BADHA496-01]; assesses=[] -->

**బాధపడు** — *bādhapaḍu* — "to feel sad; to grieve".

> స్నేహితుడు వెళ్ళిపోయాక నేను చాలా బాధపడ్డాను.
>
> *snēhituḍu veḷḷipōyāka nēnu cālā bādhapaḍḍānu.*
>
> After my friend left, I was very sad.

Past (I …): బాధపడ్డాను, *bādhapaḍḍānu*.

The cause takes -కి: ఆ మాటకి బాధపడ్డాను, that remark upset me.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**బాధపడు** (*bādhapaḍu*) joins బాధ, "pain, sorrow", a loan from Sanskrit *bādhā*, to the native verb *paḍu*, "to feel, to suffer" (DEDR 3853).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
