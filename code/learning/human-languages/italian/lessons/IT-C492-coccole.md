## You'll want to know: le coccole
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C492-ABBRACCI-03]; assesses=[] -->

**le coccole** — "cuddles".

> Il gatto vuole le coccole.
>
> *The cat wants cuddles.*

Usually plural: *fare le coccole a qualcuno*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*coccole* is the noun that goes with *coccolare* "to cuddle"; the origin of the word is not settled.
