## You'll want to know: निराश
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C455-GAM455-02]; assesses=[] -->

**निराश** — *nirāś* — "disappointed; without hope".

> परीक्षा का नतीजा देखकर अरुण निराश हुआ।
>
> *parīkṣā kā natījā dekhkar Aruṇ nirāś huā.*
>
> Arun was disappointed when he saw his exam result.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**निराश** (*nirāś*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *nirāśa*, "without any hope or wish" (Monier-Williams).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
