## You'll want to know: इजा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C486-IJA486-01]; assesses=[] -->

**इजा** — *ijā* — "an injury; harm".

> अपघातात त्याला किरकोळ इजा झाली.
>
> *apghātāt tyālā kirkoḷ ijā jhālī.*
>
> He was slightly injured in the accident.

Feminine.

इजा होणे, to be hurt, with the dative; इजा करणे, to harm.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**इजा** (*ijā*) is a loan from Persian *īzā*, "harm, injury", usually traced to Arabic; Marathi writes the *z* as ज.
