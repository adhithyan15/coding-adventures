# Kannada A1 writing paper 1 — examiner rubric

Keep this file separate from the
[learner sheet](writing-paper-1.md). This is a writing-only practice paper
against [the A1 task-shape inventory](../../task-shapes/a1.json), not one of
the two complete timed four-skill mocks required by the assessment contract.
It cannot alone establish A1 readiness. The 20-minute clock is continuous:
Task 1 receives the first 7 minutes and Task 2 the remaining 13. Never place
this paper inside a five-minute lesson or count it as a completed full mock.

## Scoring — 100 points

Score each task in four dimensions, **0–5 per dimension**, then multiply the
20-point sum by **2** for Task 1 (40 points) or **3** for Task 2 (60 points).
An omitted task earns zero. Use these dimensions consistently:

1. Task fulfilment and requested content: seven independent walk-sign-up
   responses, or a message to Anu with an introduction, proposed day, time,
   meeting place, possible companion, attendance question, and polite close.
2. Comprehensibility and organisation: usable form details, or a message whose
   reader can follow the plan and respond.
3. Kannada vocabulary and grammar: independently chosen language appropriate
   to the A1 task, not a memorised or copied answer.
4. Kannada orthographic control: recognisable rounded letters, vowel signs,
   subscript consonants (ಒತ್ತಕ್ಷರ) where used, spacing, and legibility. A
   declared accessibility accommodation may change the response mode without
   changing the construct; document it.

For each dimension, use **0** for no assessable evidence, **1** for isolated
fragments that rarely communicate, **2** for partial communication with major
losses, **3** for understandable basic performance with some errors, **4** for
clear performance with minor errors, and **5** for complete clear performance
within the A1 task. Do not demand advanced vocabulary or perfect calligraphy.
The 30–40-word band is the intended scope, not a reason to discard an otherwise
intelligible 29- or 41-word message; reflect meaningful under- or over-response
in task fulfilment. No copyable model response is provided here.

Accept safe invented names, places, a phone-like number, day, and time. Do not
require personal data or one preferred cultural choice. Documented regional
forms used consistently may receive credit; a qualified Kannada reviewer
adjudicates unfamiliar forms. Kannada written in Latin romanization, a traced
model, or another language is not independent Kannada writing evidence. A
support-language instruction is not an answer model.

Record the four dimension marks for each task, both scaled task totals, and
the 100-point writing score. The provisional project threshold is **60/100
for writing independently**; a four-skill pass additionally requires at least
60/100 in each of reading, listening, and speaking in the same administration.
Two complete timed mock passes and human validation remain separate gaps.
