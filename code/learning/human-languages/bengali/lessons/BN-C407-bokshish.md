## You'll want to know: বকশিশ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C407-BOKSHISH407-04]; assesses=[] -->

**বকশিশ** — *bôkshish* — "a tip".

> আমরা ওয়েটারকে কুড়ি টাকা বকশিশ দিলাম।
>
> *āmrā oyeṭārke kuṛi ṭākā bôkshish dilām.*
>
> We gave the waiter a twenty-taka tip.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**বকশিশ** (*bôkshish*) is usually traced to Persian *bakhshish*, "a gift, a present", from *bakhshīdan*, to give; English *baksheesh* is the same word.
