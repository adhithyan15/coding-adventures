## You'll want to know: عرق کردن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C492-UNWELL-05]; assesses=[] -->

**عرق کردن** (*'araq kardan*) — "to sweat".

> در باشگاه خیلی عرق کردم.
>
> *dar bâshgâh kheyli 'araq kardam.*
>
> I sweated a lot at the gym.

*'araq* 'sweat' + *kardan*. *'araq* also names a distilled herbal water, such as *'araq-e na'nâ* 'mint water'.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*'araq* comes from Arabic *ʿaraq*, 'sweat' (EtymDB). *kardan*, 'to do', is inherited Persian.
