## You'll want to know: defender
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C569-LUCHA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**defender** — "to defend".

> Defendió a su hermano en el colegio.
>
> *He stood up for his brother at school.*

Stem change: *defiendo*. *Defenderse* — to defend yourself; also to get by.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*defender* is inherited from Latin *dēfendere* 'to ward off, to defend' (EtymDB).
