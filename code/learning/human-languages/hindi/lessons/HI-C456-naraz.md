## You'll want to know: नाराज़
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C456-KRODH456-01]; assesses=[] -->

**नाराज़** — *nārāz* — "cross; displeased".

> पिता अरुण से नाराज़ थे क्योंकि वह देर से आया।
>
> *pitā Aruṇ se nārāz the kyoṁki vah der se āyā.*
>
> Arun's father was cross with him because he came late.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**नाराज़** (*nārāz*) is usually traced to Persian *nārāz*, "displeased", built on *nā-*, "not", and a word for "content".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
