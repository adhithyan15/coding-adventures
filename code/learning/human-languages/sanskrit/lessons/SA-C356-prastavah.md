## You'll want to know: प्रस्तावः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C356-VIJNAPANA356-05]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रस्तावः** — *prastāvaḥ* — "a proposal".

> अध्यक्षः प्रस्तावं स्वीकरोति।
>
> *adhyakṣaḥ prastāvaṃ svīkaroti.*
>
> The president accepts the proposal.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रस्तावः** (*prastāvaḥ*) is built on *pra* and the root √stu, "to praise"; MW gives *pra-stāva* "an introduction" and "introducing a topic", and "a proposal" is its modern sense (MW).
