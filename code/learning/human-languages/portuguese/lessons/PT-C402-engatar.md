## You'll want to know: engatar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C402-VOLANTE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**engatar** — "to put into gear; to hook up".

> Ele engata a primeira e o carro começa a andar.
>
> *He puts it into first gear and the car starts to move.*

*Engatar a marcha atrás* — to put it into reverse. You also *engatar* a trailer to a car.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

The origin of *engatar* is not settled. It is usually linked with *gato*, which also meant 'hook, clamp'; *gato* is inherited from Late Latin *cattus* (EtymDB).
