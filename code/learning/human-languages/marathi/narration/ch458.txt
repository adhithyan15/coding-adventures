Marathi, chapter 458: Comfort and Relief.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
दिलासा (dilāsā) — comfort; reassurance.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to feel sorry, then the Marathi for to sob quietly.

You'll want to know: दिलासा.
दिलासा — dilāsā — "comfort; reassurance".
मित्राच्या फोनने मला खूप दिलासा मिळाला. mitrācyā phonne malā khūp dilāsā miḷālā. My friend's phone call was a great comfort to me.
Masculine.
दिलासा (dilāsā) देणे, to reassure; दिलासा (dilāsā) मिळणे, to be reassured, with the dative.

The word, taken apart.
दिलासा (dilāsā) is a loan from Persian dilāsā, "consolation", built on dil, "heart".

Lesson 2 of 5.
धीर (dhīr) — fortitude; heart.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to sob quietly, then the Marathi for comfort.

You'll want to know: धीर.
धीर — dhīr — "fortitude; heart".
आईने धीर दिला म्हणून मी पुन्हा प्रयत्न केला. āīne dhīr dilā mhaṇūn mī punhā prayatna kelā. Mother gave me heart, so I tried again.
Masculine.
धीर (dhīr) देणे, to give someone heart; धीर (dhīr) धरणे, to be patient.

The word, taken apart.
धीर (dhīr) is a tatsama, Sanskrit dhīra, "wise; steady, resolute"; CDIAL lists Marathi dhīr, "patience" (CDIAL 6817).

Lesson 3 of 5.
सांत्वन करणे (sāntvan karṇe) — to console.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for comfort, then the Marathi for fortitude.

You'll want to know: सांत्वन करणे.
सांत्वन करणे — sāntvan karṇe — "to console".
मित्रांनी आजीचं सांत्वन केलं. mitrānnī ājīcaṁ sāntvan kelaṁ. Friends consoled Grandmother.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने तिचं सांत्वन केलं, tyāne ticaṁ sāntvan kelaṁ.
The one consoled takes the genitive: मित्राचं सांत्वन करणे (sāntvan karṇe).

The word, taken apart.
सांत्वन करणे (sāntvan karṇe) is the tatsama सांत्वन, Sanskrit sāntvana, "soothing with kind words" (Monier-Williams), with the light verb करणे.

Lesson 4 of 5.
हायसे वाटणे (hāyse vāṭṇe) — to feel relieved.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for fortitude, then the Marathi for to console.

You'll want to know: हायसे वाटणे.
हायसे वाटणे — hāyse vāṭṇe — "to feel relieved".
मुलगा सुखरूप घरी आला तेव्हा आईला हायसं वाटलं. mulgā sukhrūp gharī ālā tevhā āīlā hāysaṁ vāṭlaṁ. Mother felt relieved when her son came home safe.
Past (to him …): त्याला हायसं वाटलं, tyālā hāysaṁ vāṭlaṁ.
Dative, like वाटणे: मला हायसं वाटलं. Sentences write the neuter as हायसं.

The word, taken apart.
हायसे वाटणे (hāyse vāṭṇe) is a Marathi phrase built on हायसे, "eased", whose origin is not settled, with the verb वाटणे, "to feel".

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: गहिवरणे.
गहिवरणे — gahivarṇe — "to be choked up; to be overcome with emotion".
मुलीचं भाषण ऐकून वडील गहिवरले. mulīcaṁ bhāṣaṇ aikūn vaḍīl gahivarle. Father was choked with emotion listening to his daughter's speech.
Past (he …): तो गहिवरला, to gahivarlā.
Tears near, voice thick: गहिवरून येणे, to be overcome.

The word, taken apart.
गहिवरणे (gahivarṇe) is a Marathi verb whose origin is not settled.
