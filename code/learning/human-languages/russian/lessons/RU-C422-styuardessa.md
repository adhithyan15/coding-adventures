## You'll want to know: стюардесса
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C422-VYLET-05]; assesses=[] -->

**стюардесса** (*styuardéssa*) — "a flight attendant (woman), a stewardess".

> Стюардесса принесла нам воду.
>
> *Styuardéssa prineslá nam vódu.*
>
> The flight attendant brought us water.

The official word is *бортпроводница*; a man is a *стюард*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*стюардесса* was borrowed from English *stewardess* (EtymDB).
