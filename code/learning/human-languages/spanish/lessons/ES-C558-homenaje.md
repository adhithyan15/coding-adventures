## You'll want to know: el homenaje
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C558-HOMENAJE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**el homenaje** — "the tribute, the homage".

> Le hicieron un homenaje al viejo profesor.
>
> *They held a tribute to the old teacher.*

*En homenaje a* — in honour of.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*homenaje* is usually traced to Occitan or Catalan *homenatge*, built on the word for 'man', from Latin *homō* (Lewis & Short). Homage was the vow of a lord's man.
