## You'll want to know: こうばん

**こうばん** — *kōban* — "police box".

> **えきの まえに こうばんが あります。** — *eki no mae ni kōban ga arimasu* — "There is a police box in front of the station."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**こうばん** (*kōban*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *kō*, "mingle, coming and going" (Middle Chinese *kaew*), and *ban*, "turn, number in a series" (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
