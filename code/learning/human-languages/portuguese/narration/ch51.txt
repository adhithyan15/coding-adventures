Portuguese, chapter 51: Glass, Plate, Spoon, Knife, Fork.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
o copo — a glass (to drink from).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for stairs; a ladder, then the Portuguese for a mirror.

You'll want to know: o copo.
o copo — "a glass (to drink from)". um copo de água: a glass of water.

The word, taken apart.
copo from Late Latin cuppa, "cup", from Latin cupa, "tub" — inherited. EtymDB 2.1 has no ancestor row for copo; it derives the older feminine word for a cup from cuppa. The Portuguese word took a masculine ending.
