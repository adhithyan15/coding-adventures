# إجازة (ijāza) — time off, leave

## You'll want to know: إجازة
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C520-DAWAM520-03]; assesses=[] -->

**إجازة** — *ijāza* — "time off, leave".

> أخذت أمي إجازة من العمل.
>
> *akhadhat ummī ijāza min al-ʿamal.*
>
> My mother took time off work.

It also means a permit or a licence.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**إجازة** (*ijāza*) is built on the native Arabic root *j-w-z*, "to pass, to go through" (Lane). Jawāz, a passport, which you know, is from the same root: both let you pass.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
