# সেরে ওঠা (sere oṭhā) — to recover; to get well

## You'll want to know: সেরে ওঠা
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C472-OSHUDH472-05]; assesses=[] -->

**সেরে ওঠা** — *sere oṭhā* — "to recover; to get well".

> দুই সপ্তাহ পরে অরুণ জ্বর থেকে সেরে উঠল।
>
> *dui sôptāhô pôre ôrun jbôr theke sere uṭhlô.*
>
> Two weeks later Arun recovered from the fever.

Past (he/she …): তিনি শিগগির সেরে উঠলেন, *tini shiggir sere uṭhlen*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**সেরে ওঠা** (*sere oṭhā*) is *sere*, the -এ form of *sārā*, "to get well", the plain partner of সারানো (taught), to repair, with *oṭhā*, "to rise" (CDIAL 1793).
