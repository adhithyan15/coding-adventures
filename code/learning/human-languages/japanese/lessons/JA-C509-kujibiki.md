## You'll want to know: くじびき
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C509-LOTTERY509-02]; assesses=[] -->

**くじびき** — *kujibiki* — "drawing lots".

> **ばしょは くじびきに よります。** — *basho wa kujibiki ni yorimasu* — "The places depend on drawing lots."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**くじびき** (*kujibiki*) is a native Japanese compound (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese: built from *kuji*, "lot" and *biki*, "pulling" (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
