## You'll want to know: चमकाना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C514-SUNAR514-04]; assesses=[] -->

**चमकाना** — *camkānā* — "to polish, to make shine".

> मीरा ने पुरानी चाँदी की थाली चमकाई।
>
> *Mīrā ne purānī cā̃dī kī thālī camkāī.*
>
> Mira polished the old silver platter.

Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने बर्तन चमकाए, *usne bartan camkāe*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**चमकाना** (*camkānā*) is the causative of *camaknā*, "to glitter", which CDIAL derives from *\*camakka*, a reconstructed form (CDIAL 4676): it means "to make glitter".
