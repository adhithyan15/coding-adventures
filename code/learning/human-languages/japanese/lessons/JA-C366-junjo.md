## You'll want to know: じゅんじょ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C366-SAIAKU366-03]; assesses=[] -->

**じゅんじょ** — *junjo* — "order; sequence".

> **じゅんじょを まちがえたら、うごきません。** — *junjo o machigaetara, ugokimasen* — "If you do it in the wrong order, it won't work."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**じゅんじょ** (*junjo*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *jun*, "order" (Middle Chinese *zywinH*) and *jo*, "order" (*zjoX*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
