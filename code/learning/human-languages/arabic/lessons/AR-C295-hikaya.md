## You'll want to know: حكاية
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C295-RIWAYA295-01]; assesses=[] -->

**حكاية** — *ḥikāya* — "a tale, a story told aloud".

> سمع الأطفال حكاية قبل النوم.
>
> *samiʿa al-aṭfāl ḥikāya qabla an-nawm.*
>
> The children heard a story before bed.

Plural حكايات. A حكاية is told; a قصة can be told or read.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**حكاية** (*ḥikāya*) is built on the native Arabic root *ḥ-k-y* (Lane), where *ḥakā* means both "to relate" and "to imitate": a tale repeats what someone said or did.
