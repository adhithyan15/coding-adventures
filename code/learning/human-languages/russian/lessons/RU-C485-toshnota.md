## You'll want to know: тошнота
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C485-NAUSEA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**тошнота** (*toshnotá*) — "nausea".

> Это лекарство помогает от тошноты.
>
> *Éto lekárstvo pomogáyet ot toshnotý.*
>
> This medicine helps with nausea.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*тошнота* is built on *тошно* 'sickening' with *-ота*; EtymDB has no entry, and the root is usually linked to *тоска*.
