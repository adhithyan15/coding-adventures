## You'll want to know: prōtinus
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C292-REPENTE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**prōtinus** — "immediately; straight on".

> Ē raedā dēscendit et prōtinus domum festīnāvit.
>
> *He got down from the carriage and hurried straight home.*

First in space, 'onward, straight ahead'; then in time, 'at once'. Both senses are classical.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**prōtinus** is built on *prō*, "forward", and *tenus*, "as far as" (Lewis & Short): onward, and then without a break.
