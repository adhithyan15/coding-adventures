## You'll want to know: अधिकः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C379-ADHIKA379-01]; assesses=[] -->

**अधिकः** — *adhikaḥ* — "more; additional".

> यदि अधिकं धनम् अस्ति, तर्हि दरिद्राय ददातु।
>
> *yadi adhikaṃ dhanam asti, tarhi daridrāya dadātu.*
>
> If there is extra money, give it to a poor man.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अधिकः** (*adhikaḥ*) MW gives *adhika* "additional" and "surpassing in number or quantity, more numerous"; MW gives no derivation here, so its origin is uncertain (MW).
