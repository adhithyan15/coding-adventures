Gujarati, chapter 521: Milk, Curd and Butter.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
મેળવણ (meḷvaṇ) — a curd starter.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to sprinkle, then the Gujarati for to parch.

You'll want to know: મેળવણ.
મેળવણ — meḷvaṇ — "a curd starter".
દહીં માટે દૂધમાં એક ચમચી મેળવણ નાખો. dahī̃ māṭe dūdhmā̃ ek camcī meḷvaṇ nākho. For curd, put a spoonful of starter in the milk.
A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
મેળવણ (meḷvaṇ) is built on મેળવવું, "to get; to mix in", with the ending -aṇ: the spoonful of old curd mixed into milk to set it. CDIAL files the family under Sanskrit miláti, "meets" (CDIAL 10133).

Lesson 2 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to parch, then the Gujarati for a curd starter.

You'll want to know: જમાવવું.
જમાવવું — jamāvvũ — "to set curd".
દાદીએ રાત્રે દહીં જમાવ્યું. dādīe rātre dahī̃ jamāvyũ. Grandmother set the curd at night.
Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે દહીં જમાવ્યું, teṇe dahī̃ jamāvyũ.

The word, taken apart.
જમાવવું (jamāvvũ) is filed by CDIAL under Sanskrit yamyátē, "is fixed", and CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb as "to collect" (CDIAL 10428); in the kitchen it is what you do to milk to make curd.
