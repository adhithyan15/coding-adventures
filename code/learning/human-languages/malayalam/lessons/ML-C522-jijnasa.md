## You'll want to know: ജിജ്ഞാസ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C522-JIJNASA522-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ജിജ്ഞാസ** — *jijñāsa* — "curiosity".

> കുട്ടികൾക്ക് എല്ലാ കാര്യത്തിലും ജിജ്ഞാസയുണ്ട്.
>
> *kuṭṭikaḷkkŭ ellā kāryattiluṁ jijñāsayuṇṭŭ.*
>
> Children are curious about everything.

What one is curious about takes -ഇൽ. ജിജ്ഞാസയുള്ള, curious, is the adjective.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ജിജ്ഞാസ** (*jijñāsa*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *jijñāsā*, "the wish to know" (Monier-Williams), the wish-form of the root *jñā*, "to know".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
