## You'll want to know: ચઢાણ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C404-PATH404-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ચઢાણ** — *caḍhāṇ* — "an uphill climb; an ascent".

> મંદિર સુધીનું ચઢાણ ઘણું અઘરું છે.
>
> *mandir sudhīnũ caḍhāṇ ghaṇũ agharũ che.*
>
> The climb up to the temple is very hard.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ચઢાણ** (*caḍhāṇ*) is built on ચઢવું, "to climb", which goes back to *\*caḍhati*, "rises", a form CDIAL reconstructs (CDIAL 4578): the stretch of road you have to climb.
