## You'll want to know: कुश्ती
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C533-KUSHTI533-01]; assesses=[] -->

**कुश्ती** — *kuśtī* — "wrestling; a bout".

> गाँव के मेले में कुश्ती होती है।
>
> *Gā̃v ke mele meṁ kuśtī hotī hai.*
>
> There is wrestling at the village fair.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**कुश्ती** (*kuśtī*) is usually traced to Persian *kushtī*, "wrestling".
