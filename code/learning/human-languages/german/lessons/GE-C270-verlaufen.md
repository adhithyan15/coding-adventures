## You'll want to know: verlaufen
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C270-ABWARTEN-04]; assesses=[] -->

**verlaufen** — "to go, to proceed; reflexive: to get lost".

> Die Reise verlief ohne Probleme.
>
> *The journey went without problems.*

Perfekt with *sein*: *Alles ist gut verlaufen*. Reflexive, it means to get lost: *Ich habe mich verlaufen*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*verlaufen* is inherited from Middle High German *verloufen*, from Old High German *farhloufan* "to pass, to overtake" (EtymDB), built on *laufen* "to run".
