## You'll want to know: arrepender
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C480-REMORSO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**arrepender** — "to regret (arrepender-se de)".

> Arrependi-me de não ter estudado mais.
>
> *I regretted not having studied more.*

Reflexive in practice: *arrepender-se de* + noun or infinitive.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*arrepender* is inherited, through Old Portuguese *arrepentir* (EtymDB), from Vulgar Latin *repoenitēre*, built on Latin *paenitēre* "to cause regret".
