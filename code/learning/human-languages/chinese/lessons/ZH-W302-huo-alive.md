## Script — the water piece, 千, then 口
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-SCRIPT-HUO-ALIVE-01]; assesses=[] -->

> 活

**Three pieces: the water piece on the left, then 千 over 口 on the right.**

**The water piece, three strokes.** As on the left of 洗: two dots, then a stroke rising to the right.

**千, three strokes.** A short sweep down to the left, a bar across, and a vertical down through the bar.

**口, three strokes**, under 千: a short vertical, a stroke that runs right and turns down, and the bar that closes the foot.

3 + 3 + 3 = nine.
