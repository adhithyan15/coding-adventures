## You'll want to know: عمم
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C370-NAQD370-05]; assesses=[] -->

**عمم** — *ʿammama* — "to generalize".

> لا يجوز أن نعمم من مثال واحد.
>
> *lā yajūzu an nuʿammima min mithāl wāḥid.*
>
> We should not generalize from one example.

Present: يعمم, *yuʿammim*.

The noun تعميم, *taʿmīm*, a generalization.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**عمم** (*ʿammama*) is built on the native Arabic root *ʿ-m-m*, "to be general, to include all" (Lane). It is Form II, to make general: to generalize.
