## You'll want to know: calmar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C710-NERVIOS-05]; assesses=[] -->

**calmar** — "to calm, to soothe".

> Esta música me calma.
>
> *This music calms me.*

*Calmarse* — to calm down. *¡Cálmate!* — Calm down!

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*calmar* is built on *calma*, which comes from Late Latin *cauma*, from Greek *kauma* "burning heat" (EtymDB); at sea the hottest hours were the windless ones.
