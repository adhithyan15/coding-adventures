## You'll want to know: ముచ్చటించు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C533-SAMSARAM533-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ముచ్చటించు** — *muccaṭiñcu* — "to chat; to have a heart-to-heart".

> సాయంత్రం అమ్మమ్మ పక్కింటివాళ్ళతో ముచ్చటించింది.
>
> *sāyantraṁ ammamma pakkiṇṭivāḷḷatō muccaṭiñcindi.*
>
> In the evening grandma chatted with the neighbours.

Past (I …): ముచ్చటించాను, *muccaṭiñcānu*.

-తో ముచ్చటించు; ముచ్చట, a pleasant chat or a charming thing.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ముచ్చటించు** (*muccaṭiñcu*) is built on ముచ్చట, "a pleasant talk; a charming thing", with the verb ending -ఇంచు; the origin of ముచ్చట is not settled.
