# ಹುದ್ದೆ (hudde) — a post; a position

## You'll want to know: ಹುದ್ದೆ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C516-VRITTI516-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ಹುದ್ದೆ** — *hudde* — "a post; a position".

> ಅವಳಿಗೆ ಬ್ಯಾಂಕಿನಲ್ಲಿ ಒಳ್ಳೆಯ ಹುದ್ದೆ ಸಿಕ್ಕಿತು.
>
> *avaḷige byāṅkinalli oḷḷeya hudde sikkitu.*
>
> She got a good post at the bank.

A rank or seat in an office.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಹುದ್ದೆ** (*hudde*) is usually traced to Hindustani *ohdā*, "an office, a rank", itself from Arabic; it came in with the language of offices.
