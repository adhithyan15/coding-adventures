## You'll want to know: der Waldbrand
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C293-BRENNEN-05]; assesses=[] -->

**der Waldbrand** — "the forest fire".

> Im Sommer gab es viele Waldbrände.
>
> *In summer there were many forest fires.*

Plural: *die Waldbrände*. *Der Brand* alone is a large fire.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Waldbrand* joins *Wald* "forest", Old High German *wald* (EtymDB), and *Brand* "fire", built on *brennen* "to burn".
