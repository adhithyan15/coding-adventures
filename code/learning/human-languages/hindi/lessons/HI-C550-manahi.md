## You'll want to know: मनाही
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C550-CHAYAN550-03]; assesses=[] -->

**मनाही** — *manāhī* — "a ban, a prohibition".

> मंदिर में जूते पहनकर जाने की मनाही है।
>
> *Mandir meṁ jūte pahankar jāne kī manāhī hai.*
>
> Wearing shoes inside the temple is not allowed.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**मनाही** (*manāhī*) is usually traced to Arabic *manāhī*, "forbidden things", through Persian; Hindi uses it for a ban.
