Malayalam, chapter 414: Ideas and Sources.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
തത്വം (tatvaṁ) — a principle.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for to urge, then the Malayalam for to express.

You'll want to know: തത്വം.
തത്വം — tatvaṁ — "a principle".
സത്യം പറയുക എന്നതാണ് അവന്റെ തത്വം. satyaṁ paṟayuka ennatāṇŭ avanṟe tatvaṁ. Telling the truth is his principle.
Also spelled തത്ത്വം in older books.

The word, taken apart.
തത്വം (tatvaṁ) is borrowed from Sanskrit tattva, "the real state of a thing, truth" (CDIAL 5642), from tad, "that": thatness, what a thing is.

Lesson 2 of 5.
ധാരണ (dhāraṇa) — an understanding; a notion.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for to express, then the Malayalam for a principle.

You'll want to know: ധാരണ.
ധാരണ — dhāraṇa — "an understanding; a notion".
ആ വിഷയത്തെക്കുറിച്ച് എനിക്ക് നല്ല ധാരണയുണ്ട്. ā viṣayattekkuṟiccŭ enikkŭ nalla dhāraṇayuṇṭŭ. I have a good understanding of that subject.
It is also an agreement between two sides: അവർ തമ്മിൽ ധാരണയായി, they reached an understanding.

The word, taken apart.
ധാരണ (dhāraṇa) is borrowed from Sanskrit dhāraṇā, "holding, bearing; keeping in the mind" (Monier-Williams). An understanding is what the mind holds.

Lesson 4 of 5.
ഉറവിടം (uṟaviṭaṁ) — a source.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for an understanding, then the Malayalam for a theory.

You'll want to know: ഉറവിടം.
ഉറവിടം — uṟaviṭaṁ — "a source".
വാർത്തയുടെ ഉറവിടം ആരും പറഞ്ഞില്ല. vārttayuṭe uṟaviṭaṁ āruṁ paṟaññilla. Nobody said what the source of the news was.
It is first the place a spring rises, then any source of news or money.

The word, taken apart.
ഉറവിടം (uṟaviṭaṁ) is a native Malayalam compound: ഉറവ, "a spring of water", from ഊറുക, "to spring, to ooze" (DEDR 761), and ഇടം, "a place" (DEDR 434). A source is where things spring from.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ശതമാനം (śatamānaṁ) — per cent; a percentage.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for a theory, then the Malayalam for a source.

You'll want to know: ശതമാനം.
ശതമാനം — śatamānaṁ — "per cent; a percentage".
എഴുപത് ശതമാനം കുട്ടികളും പരീക്ഷ ജയിച്ചു. eḻupatŭ śatamānaṁ kuṭṭikaḷuṁ parīkṣa jayiccu. Seventy per cent of the children passed the exam.
The number comes first: പത്ത് ശതമാനം (śatamānaṁ), ten per cent.

The word, taken apart.
ശതമാനം (śatamānaṁ) is built from Sanskrit śata, "a hundred" (CDIAL 12278), and māna, "a measure" (CDIAL 10041): a measure out of a hundred.
