Marathi, chapter 155: To join, To tie, To sew, To paint, To take out.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
जोडणे (joḍṇe) — to join.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to break off, then the Marathi for to break.

You'll want to know: जोडणे.
जोडणे — joḍṇe — "to join".

The word, taken apart.
जोडणे (joḍṇe) comes from yōṭayati, "joins", a form CDIAL reconstructs under yuṭati, "is joined" (CDIAL 10496).

शिवणे (śivṇe) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from sīvyati, "sews" (CDIAL 13444). The same ancient root as English sew.

Lesson 4 of 5.
रंगवणे (raṅgavṇe) — to paint.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to tie, then the Marathi for to sew.

You'll want to know: रंगवणे.
रंगवणे — raṅgavṇe — "to paint".

The word, taken apart.
रंगवणे (raṅgavṇe) is the "make" form built on रंग, "colour", which may come from Sanskrit raṅga, "dye, colour" (CDIAL 10560), or from Persian rang, "colour"; the two may have merged.

काढणे (kāḍhṇe) is a tadbhava, a word inherited and reshaped by sound change. It comes from kaḍḍhati, "pulls, draws", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 2660).
