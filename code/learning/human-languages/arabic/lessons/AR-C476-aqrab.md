## You'll want to know: عقرب
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C476-AQRAB476-01]; assesses=[] -->

**عقرب** — *ʿaqrab* — "a scorpion".

> وجدنا عقربا تحت حجر.
>
> *wajadnā ʿaqraban taḥta ḥajar.*
>
> We found a scorpion under a stone.

Plural: عقارب, *ʿaqārib*.

The hands of a clock are also *ʿaqārib*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**عقرب** (*ʿaqrab*) goes back to an old Semitic word, with relatives in Hebrew, Syriac and Ugaritic (EtymDB).
