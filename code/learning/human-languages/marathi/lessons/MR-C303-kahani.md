# कहाणी (kahāṇī) — a tale

## You'll want to know: कहाणी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C303-KATHA303-03]; assesses=[] -->

**कहाणी** — *kahāṇī* — "a tale".

> त्या जुन्या घराची कहाणी फार दुःखाची आहे.
>
> *tyā junyā gharācī kahāṇī phār duḥkhācī āhe.*
>
> The tale of that old house is very sad.

Feminine. Plural: कहाण्या, *kahāṇyā*.

Plural कहाण्या.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**कहाणी** (*kahāṇī*) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Marathi *kahāṇī*, 'a tale', under Sanskrit *kathānaka*, 'a little tale' (CDIAL 2705).
