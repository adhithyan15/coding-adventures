## You'll want to know: o período
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C272-DURAR-04]; assesses=[] -->

**o período** — "the period, the stretch of time".

> Vivi numa aldeia durante um período curto.
>
> *I lived in a village for a short period.*

At school, a term can also be *um período*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*período* is a learned borrowing from Latin *periodus*, from Greek *períodos* "a going round, a cycle" (Lewis & Short).
