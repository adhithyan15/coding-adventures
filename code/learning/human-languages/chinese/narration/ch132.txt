Mandarin Chinese, chapter 132: Thousands.
6 lessons. You can do the first one in the car; after that you will want to have stopped.


Lesson 1 of 6.
千 (qiān) — thousand.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for winter, then the Mandarin for season.

You'll want to know: 千.
千 — qiān — "thousand".
我们大学有三千个学生 wǒmen dàxué yǒu sānqiān ge xuésheng — Our university has three thousand students.
The new character, in pieces: 千 (qiān) — a short fall over 十. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

The word, taken apart.
千 (qiān) is inherited from Middle Chinese tshen (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'ten hundreds'.

What you've built.

Lesson 2 of 6.
千 (qiān) — a short fall over 十.

Before we start: this one needs your hands, so it is not a driving lesson. You will want a pen and something to write on and your eyes, for letter shapes on the page. You can listen to everything else now. I will say so again when we reach them, but leave these sections until you have stopped: Warm-up, Script — a short fall over 十 and Guided Practice.

Script — a short fall over 十.
千 (qiān).
Two pieces, top to bottom.
A short left-falling stroke at the top, from the right down to the left.
十, two strokes: the horizontal across, then the vertical down through it.
1 + 2 = three.

[once you have stopped driving — this part needs your eyes or your hands; if you are driving, skip ahead to the next part]
Guided Practice.
[once you have stopped driving — write: 千 (qiān), all three, with the model in front of you]
[once you have stopped driving — cover: write it again from memory, and count the pieces, not the strokes]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
How many strokes in 千 (qiān)? (Three.) Which number you know sits under the fall? (十.) Which comes first, the bar or the vertical? (The bar.)

Lesson 3 of 6.
wàn — ten thousand.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for season, then the Mandarin for thousand.

You'll want to know: wàn.
wàn — "ten thousand".
wǒ xiǎng yǒu yíwàn kuài qián — I wish I had ten thousand yuan.
Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.
The tone of 一 changes: yī is said yí before the fourth tone of wàn.

The word, taken apart.
wàn is inherited from Middle Chinese mjonH (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription). The dictionary glosses this short way of writing the character 'ten thousands'; under its longer way of writing it gives the name of a dance and of an insect.

Lesson 4 of 6.
líng — zero.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for thousand, then the Mandarin for ten thousand.

You'll want to know: líng.
líng — "zero".
tā yìbǎi líng yī suì le — He is a hundred and one.
Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
líng is inherited from Middle Chinese leng (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'to fall' and, after an older book, 'a gentle rain'. The sense zero is not given there; how it came is not settled.

Lesson 5 of 6.
yígòng — altogether; in all.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for ten thousand, then the Mandarin for zero.

You'll want to know: yígòng.
yígòng — "altogether; in all".
wǒmen yígòng yǒu wǔ ge rén — There are five of us in all.
Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.
The tone of 一 changes: yī is said yí before the fourth tone of gòng.

The word, taken apart.
yígòng is built from 一 'one' and gòng, both inherited from Middle Chinese, 'jit and gjowngH (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'together' and 'all'.
