## You'll want to know: జలాశయం
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C475-ANAKATTA475-03]; assesses=[] -->

**జలాశయం** — *jalāśayaṁ* — "a reservoir".

> వర్షాలకు జలాశయం పూర్తిగా నిండింది.
>
> *varṣālaku jalāśayaṁ pūrtigā niṇḍindi.*
>
> With the rains the reservoir filled up completely.

జల-, water, + -ఆశయం, a resting place: a place where water rests.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**జలాశయం** (*jalāśayaṁ*) is a compound of two words borrowed from Sanskrit: *jala*, "water", and *āśaya*, "a resting place, a receptacle" (Monier-Williams).
