Malayalam, chapter 161: Soil, Coconut palm, Banana plant, Jasmine, Lotus.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
മണ്ണ് (maṇṇŭ) — soil, earth.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for a waterfall, then the Malayalam for a stone.
[pause 1 second]
Say "midnight." (Pātirā — pāti, "half," plus night.)

You'll want to know: മണ്ണ്.
മണ്ണ് — maṇṇŭ — "soil, earth".

The word, taken apart.
മണ്ണ് (maṇṇŭ) is native Dravidian, one word with Tamil maṇ and Kannada maṇ (DEDR 4666).

Lesson 3 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for soil, then the Malayalam for a coconut palm.
[pause 1 second]
Say "noon." (Ucca.) Is it a madhya, "middle," compound like the Telugu word? (No: a different root.)

You'll want to know: വാഴ.
വാഴ — vāḻa — "a banana plant".

The word, taken apart.
വാഴ (vāḻa) is native Dravidian, one word with Tamil vāḻai and Kannada bāḻe (DEDR 5373). DEDR 5373 compares Sanskrit vārabuṣā.

താമര (tāmara) is native Dravidian, one word with Tamil tāmarai, Kannada tāmare and Telugu tāmara (DEDR 3163). DEDR 3163 compares Sanskrit tāmarasa.
