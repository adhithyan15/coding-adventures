## You'll want to know: ふゆやすみ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C498-REST498-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ふゆやすみ** — *fuyuyasumi* — "the winter holidays".

> **ふゆやすみに そふの うちへ いきます。** — *fuyuyasumi ni sofu no uchi e ikimasu* — "In the winter holidays I go to my grandfather's house."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ふゆやすみ** (*fuyuyasumi*) is a native Japanese compound (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese: built from *fuyu*, "winter" and *yasumi*, "rest" (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
