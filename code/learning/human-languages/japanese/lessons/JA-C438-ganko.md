## You'll want to know: がんこ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C438-STUBBORN438-01]; assesses=[] -->

**がんこ** — *ganko* — "stubborn; obstinate".

> **そふは すこし がんこです。** — *sofu wa sukoshi ganko desu* — "My grandfather is a little stubborn."

A na-adjective: な before a noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**がんこ** (*ganko*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *gan*, "stubborn" (Middle Chinese *ngwaen*) and *ko*, "harden" (*kuH*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
