## You'll want to know: camīnus, camīnī
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C469-INCUS-03]; assesses=[] -->

**camīnus, camīnī** — "a forge, a furnace; a fireplace".

> In camīnō ignis magnus ardet.
>
> *A big fire burns in the forge.*

Second declension, masculine: *camīnus, camīnī m.* A smith's forge, or a stove for heating a room. English *chimney* comes from a word built on it.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*camīnus* was borrowed from Greek *kamīnos* 'furnace' (EtymDB; Lewis & Short). English *chimney* comes, through French, from Late Latin *camīnāta*, built on it.
