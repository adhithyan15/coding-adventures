## You'll want to know: оформлять
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C450-SPRAVKA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**оформлять** (*oformlyát*) — "to get (documents) done, to process; to design".

> Мы три дня оформляли визу.
>
> *My tri dnya oformlyáli vízu.*
>
> It took us three days to get the visa done.

Imperfective; perfective *оформить* (*оформлю, оформишь*).

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*оформлять* is built from *о-* and *форма* 'a form, a shape', which is usually traced to Latin *forma* 'a shape' (Lewis & Short; EtymDB lists *форма* without a source): to give a matter its proper form.
