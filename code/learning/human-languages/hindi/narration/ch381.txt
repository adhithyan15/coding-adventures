Hindi, chapter 381: If Only.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
काश (kāś) — if only, I wish.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for to get stuck, then the Hindi for to pull out of trouble.

You'll want to know: काश.
काश — kāś — "if only, I wish".
काश मैं वहाँ होता, तो तुम्हारी मदद करता! kāś maiṁ vahā̃ hotā, to tumhārī madad kartā! If only I had been there, I would have helped you!

The word, taken apart.
काश (kāś) is usually traced to Persian kāsh, "would that".

What you've built.
One more word for saying what happens if.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for if only, then the Hindi for a pity.

You'll want to know: सताना.
सताना — satānā — "to torment, to harass".
अगर तुम उसे नहीं सताते तो वह नहीं रोता। agar tum use nahīṃ satāte to vah nahīṃ rotā. If you hadn't tormented him, he wouldn't have cried.
Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने छोटे भाई को सताया, usne choṭe bhāī ko satāyā.

The word, taken apart.
सताना (satānā) is a tadbhava, inherited from Sanskrit saṃtāpayati, "inflames; torments" (CDIAL 12886).
