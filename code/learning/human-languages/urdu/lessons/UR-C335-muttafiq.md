# متفق (muttafiq) — in agreement

## You'll want to know: متفق
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C335-HIMAYAT335-04]; assesses=[] -->

**متفق** — *muttafiq* — "in agreement".

> میں آپ سے متفق ہوں۔
>
> *maiṅ āp se muttafiq hūṅ.*
>
> I agree with you.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**متفق** (*muttafiq*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *muttafiq*, "agreeing, of one mind".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
