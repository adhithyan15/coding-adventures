## You'll want to know: मेहंदी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C500-BARAT500-02]; assesses=[] -->

**मेहंदी** — *mehndī* — "henna".

> शादी के दिन मीरा के हाथों में मेहंदी लगी थी।
>
> *śādī ke din Mīrā ke hāthoṁ meṁ mehndī lagī thī.*
>
> On her wedding day Mira's hands were patterned with henna.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**मेहंदी** (*mehndī*) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Hindi *mẽhdī*, the henna plant, under Sanskrit *mēndhī* (CDIAL 10328). Its leaves are ground to a paste that leaves red-brown patterns on the hands, above all before a wedding.
