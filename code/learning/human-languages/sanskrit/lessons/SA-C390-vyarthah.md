## You'll want to know: व्यर्थः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C390-LAKSHANA390-03]; assesses=[] -->

**व्यर्थः** — *vyarthaḥ* — "useless; to no purpose".

> यदि यन्त्रं न चलति, तर्हि तत् व्यर्थम्।
>
> *yadi yantraṃ na calati, tarhi tat vyartham.*
>
> If the machine does not run, it is useless.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**व्यर्थः** (*vyarthaḥ*) is built from *vi*, "without", and *artha*, "purpose, use"; MW gives *vy-artha* "useless, unavailing, unprofitable, vain" (MW).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
