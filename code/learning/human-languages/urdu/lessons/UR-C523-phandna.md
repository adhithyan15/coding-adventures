## You'll want to know: پھاندنا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C523-STADIUM523-05]; assesses=[] -->

**پھاندنا** — *phāṅdnā* — "to leap over".

> گھوڑے نے دیوار پھاندی۔
>
> *ghoṛe ne dīvār phāṅdī.*
>
> The horse leapt over the wall.

Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے دیوار پھاندی, *us ne dīvār phāṅdī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**پھاندنا** (*phāṅdnā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *phā̃dnā*, "to leap", under Sanskrit *spandatē*, "trembles, kicks" (CDIAL 13806).
