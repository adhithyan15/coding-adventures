# तत् समयाधीनम् (tat samayādhīnam) — it depends

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[SA-LEX-C285-ADHINAM-01, SA-GRAMMAR-C284-IF-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "that depends on time". (*tat samayādhīnam*.) Then say "if it rains, I stay at home". (*yadi varṣā bhavati, tarhi ahaṃ gṛhe tiṣṭhāmi*.)

## You'll want to know: what depends sets the ending
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-GRAMMAR-C285-DEPENDS-01]; assesses=[SA-LEX-C256-WRK256-03, SA-LEX-C28-LEAVE-03, SA-LEX-C74-MARKET-02, SA-LEX-C170-WRK170-01] -->

**-अधीन** describes the thing that depends, so it takes that thing's gender: *-aḥ* for a masculine noun, *-ā* for a feminine one, *-am* for a neuter one.

> **मम निर्णयः धनाधीनः।**
>
> *mama nirṇayaḥ dhanādhīnaḥ.* — "My decision depends on money."

> **यात्रा वर्षाधीना।**
>
> *yātrā varṣādhīnā.* — "The trip depends on the rain."

> **कार्यं समयाधीनम्।**
>
> *kāryaṃ samayādhīnam.* — "The work depends on time."

**निर्णयः** is masculine, so **-अधीनः**; **यात्रा** is feminine, so **-अधीना**.

## You'll want to know: it depends, and then the condition
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-GRAMMAR-C285-DEPENDS-02]; assesses=[SA-GRAMMAR-C284-IF-01, SA-GRAMMAR-C284-CONDITIONAL-REAL-01, SA-LEX-C76-WEATHER-01, SA-LEX-GACCHATI-GO] -->

Say what it depends on, then lay out the condition with **यदि** … **तर्हि**:

> **मम यात्रा वर्षाधीना।**
>
> *mama yātrā varṣādhīnā.* — "My trip depends on the rain."

> **यदि वर्षा न भविष्यति,**
>
> *yadi varṣā na bhaviṣyati,*
>
> **तर्हि वयं गमिष्यामः।**
>
> *tarhi vayaṃ gamiṣyāmaḥ.* — "If it does not rain, we will go."

> **तत् भवदधीनम्।**
>
> *tat bhavadadhīnam.* — "That is up to you."

**भवदधीनम्** (*bhavadadhīnam*) is *bhavat*, the stem of **भवान्**, with *adhīnam*: "depending on you", that is, "up to you".
