## You'll want to know: હથોડી
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C502-SUTHAR502-03]; assesses=[] -->

**હથોડી** — *hathoḍī* — "a hammer".

> સુથારની હથોડી ટેબલ પર છે.
>
> *suthārnī hathoḍī ṭebal par che.*
>
> The carpenter's hammer is on the table.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**હથોડી** (*hathoḍī*) comes from *\*hastakūṭa*, "a hand hammer", a form CDIAL builds from *hásta*, "hand", and *kūṭa*, "a hammer"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati word (CDIAL 14028).
