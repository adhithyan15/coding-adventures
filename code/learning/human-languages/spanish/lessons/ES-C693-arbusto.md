## You'll want to know: el arbusto
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C693-ARBOLES-03]; assesses=[] -->

**el arbusto** — "the bush, the shrub".

> Hay arbustos a los dos lados del camino.
>
> *There are bushes on both sides of the path.*

Smaller than *un árbol*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*arbusto* is a learned borrowing from Latin *arbustum* "a place planted with trees" (Lewis & Short; EtymDB), from *arbor* "tree".
