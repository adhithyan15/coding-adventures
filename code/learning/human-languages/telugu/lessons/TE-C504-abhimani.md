# అభిమాని (abhimāni) — an admirer; a fan

## You'll want to know: అభిమాని
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C504-ABHIMANAM504-03]; assesses=[] -->

**అభిమాని** — *abhimāni* — "an admirer; a fan".

> నా తమ్ముడు ఆ ఆటగాడికి పెద్ద అభిమాని.
>
> *nā tammuḍu ā āṭagāḍiki pedda abhimāni.*
>
> My younger brother is a big fan of that player.

Plural: అభిమానులు, *abhimānulu*.

-కి అభిమాని, a fan of.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**అభిమాని** (*abhimāni*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *abhimānin*, built on *abhimāna* (CDIAL 524): one who holds someone in high regard.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
