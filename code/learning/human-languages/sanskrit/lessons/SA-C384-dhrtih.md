## You'll want to know: धृतिः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C384-UDYAMA384-02]; assesses=[] -->

**धृतिः** — *dhṛtiḥ* — "constancy; resolve".

> यदि धृतिः अस्ति, तर्हि भयं न भवति।
>
> *yadi dhṛtiḥ asti, tarhi bhayaṃ na bhavati.*
>
> If there is constancy, there is no fear.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**धृतिः** (*dhṛtiḥ*) comes from the root √dhṛ, "to hold"; MW gives *dhṛti* "firmness, constancy, resolution, will" (MW).
