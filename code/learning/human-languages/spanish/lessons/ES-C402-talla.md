# la talla — the cut that became a size

[PAUSE 2s] Name a shirt and a T-shirt. (*Una camisa, una camiseta*.)

## Sounds you'll need
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

- *TA-ya* for most speakers: the written **ll** commonly has the same sound as
  **y**. → [pronunciation reference](../pronunciation-reference.md)

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-TALLA]; assesses=[] -->

**la talla** — "the size," especially a clothing size.

The noun belongs to **tallar**, "to cut or shape." A garment is cut to a
measure, and that cut becomes its **talla**. In a shop, **¿Qué talla?** asks
"What size?"
