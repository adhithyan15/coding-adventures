## You'll want to know: хихикать
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C480-LAUGHTER-02]; assesses=[] -->

**хихикать** (*khikhíkat*) — "to giggle".

> Девочки хихикают на последней парте.
>
> *Dévochki khikhíkayut na poslédney párte.*
>
> The girls are giggling at the back desk.

Imperfective; perfective хихикнуть (once).

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*хихикать* is built on the imitative sound *хи-хи*; EtymDB lists the word without a source.
