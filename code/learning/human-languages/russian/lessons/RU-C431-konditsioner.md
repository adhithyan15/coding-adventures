## You'll want to know: кондиционер
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C431-SKVOZNYAK-01]; assesses=[] -->

**кондиционер** (*konditsionér*) — "an air conditioner; a hair conditioner".

> В жаркие дни кондиционер работает весь день.
>
> *V zhárkiye dni konditsionér rabótayet ves den.*
>
> On hot days the air conditioner runs all day.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*кондиционер* was borrowed from English *air conditioner* (EtymDB), keeping the second word.
