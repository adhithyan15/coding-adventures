## You'll want to know: jīchǎng
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C73-STATION73-04]; assesses=[] -->

*jīchǎng* — "airport".

> *wǒ qù jīchǎng* — I'm going to the airport.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*jīchǎng* is built from 机 'machine' and chǎng 'open ground', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *kj+j* and *drjang* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription); the dictionary glosses the second as a threshing floor. The machine here is the plane.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for times and partings.
