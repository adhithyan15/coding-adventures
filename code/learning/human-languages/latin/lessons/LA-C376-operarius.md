## You'll want to know: operārius, operāriī
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C376-OPUS-04]; assesses=[] -->

**operārius, operāriī** — "a labourer, a workman".

> Operāriī tōtum diem in agrō labōrant.
>
> *The labourers work all day in the field.*

*operārius, operāriī* m., a man who works with his hands for a wage, built on *opera*, work, effort.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*operārius* is built on *opera* "work, effort" (Lewis & Short), a word of the same family as *opus, operis* "work", with the ending *-ārius* of a person busy with something.
