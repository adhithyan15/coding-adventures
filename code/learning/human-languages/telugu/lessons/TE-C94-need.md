## You'll want to know: అవసరం
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C94-POLITE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**అవసరం** (*avasaraṁ*) — "need".

**అవసరం లేదు** — "there is no need", the polite way to refuse help, with the **లేదు** of
your first chapter.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

A way to refuse kindly.
