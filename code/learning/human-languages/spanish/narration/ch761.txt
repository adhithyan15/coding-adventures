Lesson 1 of 5.
la serie — the series (TV).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for the premiere, then the Spanish for to play.

You'll want to know: la serie.
la serie — "the series (TV)".
Estoy viendo una serie muy buena. I am watching a really good series.
Same form in the plural: las series.

The word, taken apart.
serie comes from Latin seriēs "a row, a chain" (EtymDB), from serere "to join together".

Lesson 3 of 5.
el cojín — the cushion.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for the series, then the Spanish for the video game.

You'll want to know: el cojín.
el cojín — "the cushion".
El sofá tiene muchos cojines. The sofa has lots of cushions.
Plural cojines.

The word, taken apart.
cojín comes from Old Spanish coxín, from a spoken Latin coxīnum (EtymDB), built on coxa "hip": something to rest the hip on. Many dictionaries say it came in through French coussin.

Lesson 4 of 5.
bostezar — to yawn.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for the video game, then the Spanish for the cushion.

You'll want to know: bostezar.
bostezar — "to yawn".
Bostezo mucho cuando tengo sueño. I yawn a lot when I am sleepy.
El bostezo — the yawn. Spelling: bostecé.

The word, taken apart.
bostezar is usually traced to Latin ōscitāre "to gape, to yawn" (EtymDB); how it gained its b- is not clear.

Lesson 5 of 5.
grabar — to record; to engrave.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for the cushion, then the Spanish for to yawn.

You'll want to know: grabar.
grabar — "to record; to engrave".
Grabamos el concierto con el móvil. We recorded the concert on our phones.
For sound and video; also to engrave.

The word, taken apart.
grabar was borrowed from French graver "to engrave" (EtymDB), which comes from Frankish graban "to dig"; a recording is sound cut into a surface.
