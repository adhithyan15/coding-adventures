## You'll want to know: सौभाग्यम्
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C497-KANYADANA497-05]; assesses=[] -->

**सौभाग्यम्** — *saubhāgyam* — "good fortune".

> सौभाग्यं भवतु इति वृद्धाः वदन्ति।
>
> *saubhāgyaṃ bhavatu iti vṛddhāḥ vadanti.*
>
> "May good fortune be yours," say the elders.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सौभाग्यम्** (*saubhāgyam*) is built on *su-bhaga*, "lucky", with the lengthening of its first vowel; MW gives "good luck, good fortune, happiness (esp. conjugal felicity)" (MW).
