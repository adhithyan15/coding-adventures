Russian, chapter 333: Seasoning.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
солить (solít) — to salt.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to get burnt, then the Russian for to brown.

You'll want to know: солить.
солить (solít) — "to salt".
Ты уже солил суп? Ty uzhé solíl sup? Have you salted the soup yet?
Imperfective; perfective посолить (posolít). Also to pickle in salt: солить (solít) огурцы.

The word, taken apart.
солить (solít) is built on соль "salt", an inherited Slavic word from the PIE root of Latin sal and English salt; EtymDB lists no parent for the verb.

Lesson 2 of 5.

перчить (perchít) — "to pepper".
Он всегда много перчит мясо. On vsegdá mnógo perchít myáso. He always puts a lot of pepper on meat.
Imperfective; perfective поперчить (poperchít).

The word, taken apart.
перчить (perchít) is built on перец "pepper", inherited through Old East Slavic pĭpĭrĭcĭ from Proto-Slavic pĭpĭrĭ, which EtymDB traces to Latin piper "pepper".

Lesson 3 of 5.
посыпать (posýpat) — to sprinkle.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to salt, then the Russian for to pepper.

You'll want to know: посыпать.
посыпать (posýpat) — "to sprinkle".
Она посыпала салат укропом. Oná posýpala salát ukrópom. She sprinkled the salad with dill.
Perfective, stressed on -сы-: посыплю; the imperfective is посыпать (posýpat) (posypát). The topping is in the instrumental.

The word, taken apart.
посыпать (posýpat) is built on по- and сыпать "to pour grains or powder", an inherited Slavic verb; EtymDB lists no parent.

приправа (pripráva) — "a seasoning".
Эта приправа очень острая. Éta pripráva óchen óstraya. This seasoning is very hot.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
приправа (pripráva) is built on приправить "to season", from при- and править "to set right", from правый "right": what puts a dish right. EtymDB lists no parent.

Lesson 5 of 5.
мариновать (marinovát) — to marinate; to pickle.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to sprinkle, then the Russian for a seasoning.

You'll want to know: мариновать.
мариновать (marinovát) — "to marinate; to pickle".
Мясо для шашлыка маринуют всю ночь. Myáso dlya shashlyká marinúyut vsyu noch. The meat for kebabs is marinated all night.
Imperfective; the perfective is замариновать (zamarinovát). Present мариную.

The word, taken apart.
мариновать (marinovát) is usually traced through French mariner "to pickle in brine", built on marin "of the sea", from Latin mare "sea"; EtymDB lists no parent.
