## You'll want to know: भागिनेयः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C499-SVASR499-04]; assesses=[] -->

**भागिनेयः** — *bhāgineyaḥ* — "a sister's son".

> मातुलः भागिनेयाय उपहारं ददाति।
>
> *mātulaḥ bhāgineyāya upahāraṃ dadāti.*
>
> The uncle gives his sister's son a present.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**भागिनेयः** (*bhāgineyaḥ*) is built on **भगिनी**, "a sister", by lengthening its first vowel; MW gives "a sister's son" (MW).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
