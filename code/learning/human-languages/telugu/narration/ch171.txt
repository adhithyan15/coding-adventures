Telugu, chapter 171: Sunset, Every day, Usually, Rarely, Constantly.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
సూర్యాస్తమయం (sūryāstamayaṁ) — sunset.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a date, then the Telugu for a weekend.

You'll want to know: సూర్యాస్తమయం.
సూర్యాస్తమయం — sūryāstamayaṁ — "sunset".

The word, taken apart.
సూర్యాస్తమయం (sūryāstamayaṁ) is Sanskrit: sūrya, "sun" (CDIAL 13574), and astamaya, "setting of the sun" (CDIAL 974), with the Telugu ending -ṁ.

Lesson 2 of 5.
ప్రతిరోజు (pratirōju) — every day.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a weekend, then the Telugu for sunset.

You'll want to know: ప్రతిరోజు.
ప్రతిరోజు — pratirōju — "every day".

The word, taken apart.
ప్రతిరోజు (pratirōju) joins Sanskrit prati, "towards, against" (CDIAL 8540), used in Telugu as "each", to rōju, "day", from Persian rōz.

[once you have stopped driving — read: two words again — ఛాయ (chāya, shade) is written with ఛ, and ఝరి (jhari, a stream) with ఝ]

The word, taken apart.
నిరంతరం (nirantaraṁ) is Sanskrit nirantara, "without interval, perpetual", taken over whole as a learned loan, a tatsama, with the Telugu noun ending -ṁ (CDIAL 7265).
