## You'll want to know: കവാടം
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C485-KAVATAM485-03]; assesses=[] -->

**കവാടം** — *kavāṭaṁ* — "a gateway; an entrance".

> സ്റ്റേഷന്റെ പ്രധാന കവാടത്തിൽ നമുക്ക് കാണാം.
>
> *sṟṟēṣanṟe pradhāna kavāṭattil namukkŭ kāṇāṁ.*
>
> Let's meet at the station's main entrance.

A formal word, used for big gates: a temple, a station, a park.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**കവാടം** (*kavāṭaṁ*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *kavāṭa*, "the leaf of a door, a door" (Monier-Williams).
