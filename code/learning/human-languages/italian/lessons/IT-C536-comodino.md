## You'll want to know: il comodino
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C536-CASA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**il comodino** — "bedside table".

> Lascio sempre il telefono sul comodino.
>
> *I always leave my phone on the bedside table.*

A little *comò* "chest of drawers".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*comodino* is built on French *commode* "a chest of drawers" (EtymDB), from Latin *commodus* "convenient".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
