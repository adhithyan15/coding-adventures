Gujarati, chapter 293: An Accident near the Bridge.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
અકસ્માત (akasmāt) — an accident.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a detective, then the Gujarati for to nab.

You'll want to know: અકસ્માત.
અકસ્માત — akasmāt — "an accident".
ગઈકાલે સાંજે પુલ પાસે મોટો અકસ્માત થયો. gaīkāle sā̃je pul pāse moṭo akasmāt thayo. Yesterday evening there was a big accident near the bridge.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
અકસ્માત (akasmāt) is Sanskrit akasmāt, "without a why or a wherefore, suddenly" (Monier-Williams), used in Gujarati as a noun: what happens without warning. It is masculine: અકસ્માત થયો, there was an accident.

Lesson 3 of 5.
ઘાયલ (ghāyal) — injured.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for an accident, then the Gujarati for a collision.

You'll want to know: ઘાયલ.
ઘાયલ — ghāyal — "injured".
આ અકસ્માતમાં ત્રણ લોકો ઘાયલ થયા. ā akasmātmā̃ traṇ loko ghāyal thayā. Three people were hurt in this accident.

The word, taken apart.
ઘાયલ (ghāyal) is built on ghāy, a wound, from Sanskrit ghāta, "a blow"; CDIAL lists Gujarati ghāyo, a wounded person, under that word (CDIAL 4460). A person ઘાયલ થાય છે, gets hurt.

Lesson 4 of 5.
ગંભીર (gambhīr) — serious.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a collision, then the Gujarati for injured.

You'll want to know: ગંભીર.
ગંભીર — gambhīr — "serious".
એક છોકરાને ગંભીર ઈજા થઈ. ek chokrāne gambhīr ījā thaī. One boy was seriously injured.

The word, taken apart.
ગંભીર (gambhīr) is a tatsama, Sanskrit gambhīra, "deep" (Monier-Williams). In Gujarati it is a deep, grave matter: ગંભીર ઈજા, a serious injury; ગંભીર માણસ, a serious man.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ગભરાવું (gabhrāvũ) — to panic.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for injured, then the Gujarati for serious.

You'll want to know: ગભરાવું.
ગભરાવું — gabhrāvũ — "to panic".
અકસ્માત જોઈને ડ્રાઈવર ગભરાઈ ગયો. akasmāt joīne ḍrāīvar gabhrāī gayo. When he saw the accident, the driver panicked.
Past (he …): તે ડ્રાઈવર ગભરાઈ ગયો, te ḍrāīvar gabhrāī gayo.

The word, taken apart.
ગભરાવું (gabhrāvũ) probably comes from ghabbhara, "confused", a form CDIAL reconstructs, under which it lists Gujarati ghābhrũ, "confused" (CDIAL 4427). It is to lose your head with fright: ગભરાઈ ગયો.
