## You'll want to know: કદ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C471-VAN471-02]; assesses=[] -->

**કદ** — *kad* — "height; size (of a person)".

> અરુણનું કદ પિતા કરતાં મોટું છે.
>
> *Aruṇnũ kad pitā kartā̃ moṭũ che.*
>
> Arun is taller than his father.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**કદ** (*kad*) is usually traced to Arabic *qadd*, "stature", which reached Gujarati through Persian.
