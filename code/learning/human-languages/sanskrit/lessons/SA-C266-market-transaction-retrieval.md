# Retrieve the whole market transaction

## Warm-up

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[SA-LEX-C249-THG249-04, SA-VERB-C263-GAMISYAMI-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say “a film,” then “I will go.” (*Citrapaṭaḥ; gamiṣyāmi.*)

## Guided Practice: independent round two

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[SA-LEX-C74-MARKET-01, SA-LEX-C74-MARKET-02, SA-LEX-C74-MARKET-03, SA-LEX-C87-NUMBERS-03, SA-LEX-C138-ACT138-01, SA-LEX-C185-WHY185-01, SA-LEX-C215-QUAL215-05, SA-LEX-C248-THG248-03, SA-VERB-C266-KRINAMI-01, SA-PATTERN-C266-MARKET-EXCHANGE-02, SA-LEX-C74-MARKET-04, SA-LEX-C74-MARKET-05, SA-LEX-C24-ASK-03, SA-LEX-C222-THG222-03, SA-LEX-C222-THG222-04, SA-LEX-DHANYAVADA, SA-GRAMMAR-C265-DADATU-01, SA-PATTERN-C265-POLITE-ORDER-02] -->

No Sanskrit model is shown before you answer.

[PAUSE 12s] Choose a shop or market and a different familiar item. Say that
you buy it. Ask its price. The shopkeeper says that the price is twenty
rupees. Request the item politely and close with thanks.

## What you've built: check after speaking

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[SA-LEX-C74-MARKET-01, SA-LEX-C74-MARKET-03, SA-LEX-C185-WHY185-01, SA-LEX-C248-THG248-03, SA-LEX-DHANYAVADA, SA-GRAMMAR-C265-DADATU-01, SA-PATTERN-C265-POLITE-ORDER-02, SA-VERB-C266-KRINAMI-01, SA-PATTERN-C266-MARKET-EXCHANGE-02] -->

A complete answer still has five jobs: say what you buy; ask the price; hear
the rupee amount; request the item; thank the shopkeeper. Your chosen item may
differ from the first round.

## Wrap-up Recall: boundary

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[SA-LEX-C74-MARKET-01, SA-LEX-C74-MARKET-03, SA-LEX-C248-THG248-03, SA-VERB-C266-KRINAMI-01, SA-PATTERN-C266-MARKET-EXCHANGE-02] -->

You can now complete one small A1 market exchange with familiar language. This
is **not** complete commerce, currency, bargaining, consumer-rights, or present
paradigm coverage, and it is not evidence of exam readiness or learner success.
