## You'll want to know: 王国
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C330-KING330-05]; assesses=[] -->

**王国** — *wángguó* — "kingdom".

> **王子离开了他的王国去看外边的世界** *wángzǐ líkāi le tā de wángguó qù kàn wàibian de shìjiè* — The prince left his kingdom to go and see the world outside.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**王国** (*wángguó*) is built from 王 'a king' and 国 'a state', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *hjwang* and *kwok* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'a realm'.
