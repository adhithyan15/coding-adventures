## You'll want to know: கண்
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C45-BODY-01]; assesses=[] -->

**கண்** (*kaṇ*) — "an eye".

Proto-Dravidian **\*kaṇ**, held almost still everywhere it went: Malayalam കണ്ണ്, Telugu కన్ను, Kannada ಕಣ್ಣು. Tamil's is the shortest of the four, one syllable and done.

It reaches well past the face. A *kaṇ* is the joint on a stalk of sugarcane and the mesh of a net — wherever a thing has an opening or a knot, Tamil is willing to call it an eye.

*talai* and *kai*, the head and the hand, came earlier. The face gets its own turn now, one piece at a time.
