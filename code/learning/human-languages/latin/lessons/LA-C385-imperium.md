## You'll want to know: imperium, imperiī
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C385-REGO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**imperium, imperiī** — "command, supreme power; an empire".

> Cōnsulēs summum imperium habent.
>
> *The consuls hold supreme power.*

*imperium, imperiī* n., built on *imperō*, to command: the power to command an army and govern, then the land ruled. English *empire* and *imperial*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*imperium* is built on *imperō* "to command" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short). English *empire* comes from it through French, and *imperial* from its adjective.
