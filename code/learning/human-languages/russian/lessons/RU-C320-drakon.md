## You'll want to know: дракон
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C320-VOLSHEBNIK-04]; assesses=[] -->

**дракон** (*drakón*) — "a dragon".

> В сказке дракон охранял золото.
>
> *V skázke drakón okhranyál zóloto.*
>
> In the tale a dragon guarded gold.

Masculine. A Russian folk dragon is *змей*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*дракон* goes back to Greek *drákōn* "a serpent, a dragon" (EtymDB).
