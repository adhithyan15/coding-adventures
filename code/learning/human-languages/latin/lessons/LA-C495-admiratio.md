## You'll want to know: admīrātiō, admīrātiōnis
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C495-MIRA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**admīrātiō, admīrātiōnis** — "admiration, wonder".

> Hospitēs templum novum cum admīrātiōne spectant.
>
> *The guests gaze at the new temple in wonder.*

Feminine, third declension, from *admīror*, already known. English *admiration*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**admīrātiō** is the action noun of *admīror* "to wonder at", built on *ad-* and *mīror* "to wonder" (Lewis & Short; EtymDB). English *admiration* comes from it.
