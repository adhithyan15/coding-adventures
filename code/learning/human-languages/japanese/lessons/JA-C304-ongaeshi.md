## You'll want to know: おんがえし
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C304-ONGAESHI304-02]; assesses=[] -->

**おんがえし** — *ongaeshi* — "repaying a kindness".

> **その とりは おじいさんに おんがえしを しました。** — *sono tori wa ojiisan ni ongaeshi o shimashita* — "The bird repaid the old man's kindness."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**おんがえし** (*ongaeshi*) is a mixed word, native and Sino-Japanese: *on*, "kindness", is a Chinese character read with its on'yomi, a reading borrowed from Chinese (Middle Chinese *'on*), and *gaeshi*, "return", is native Japanese (JMdict, KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
