## You'll want to know: رکورد
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C513-SCORES-03]; assesses=[] -->

**رکورد** (*rekord*) — "a best result, a record".

> او رکورد مدرسه را دارد.
>
> *u rekord-e madrese râ dârad.*
>
> She holds the school record.

Plural *rekordhâ*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*rekord* is a modern loan, usually traced to English *record*. EtymDB has no entry for the Persian word.
