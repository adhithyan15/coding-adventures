Gujarati, chapter 509: Bellows, Hammer and Rust.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ધમણ (dhamaṇ) — bellows.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to heat (something), then the Gujarati for an anvil.

You'll want to know: ધમણ.
ધમણ — dhamaṇ — "bellows".
લુહારનો દીકરો ધમણ ચલાવે છે. luhārno dīkro dhamaṇ calāve che. The blacksmith's son works the bellows.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ધમણ (dhamaṇ) is inherited from Sanskrit dhamanī, "bellows", from a root meaning "to blow", and CDIAL lists the Gujarati word (CDIAL 6734).

Lesson 2 of 5.
ફૂંકવું (phū̃kvũ) — to blow out (a flame).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for an anvil, then the Gujarati for bellows.

You'll want to know: ફૂંકવું.
ફૂંકવું — phū̃kvũ — "to blow out (a flame)".
જન્મદિવસે મીરાએ બધી મીણબત્તીઓ ફૂંકી. janmadivse Mīrāe badhī mīṇbattīo phū̃kī. On her birthday Mira blew out all the candles.
Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે મીણબત્તી ફૂંકી, teṇe mīṇbattī phū̃kī.

The word, taken apart.
ફૂંકવું (phū̃kvũ) comes from phūtka, "blowing", a form CDIAL reconstructs, and CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb (CDIAL 9102). The wind's own verb is ફૂંકાવું.

Lesson 3 of 5.
ટીપવું (ṭīpvũ) — to beat (metal) flat.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for bellows, then the Gujarati for to blow out.

You'll want to know: ટીપવું.
ટીપવું — ṭīpvũ — "to beat (metal) flat".
લુહારે ગરમ લોખંડ હથોડીથી ટીપ્યું. luhāre garam lokhanḍ hathoḍīthī ṭīpyũ. The blacksmith beat the hot iron flat with the hammer.
Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે લોખંડ ટીપ્યું, teṇe lokhanḍ ṭīpyũ.

The word, taken apart.
ટીપવું (ṭīpvũ) is said of metal, and of dough patted flat by hand. CDIAL lists a Gujarati ṭīpvũ under a reconstructed ṭipp, "squeeze, nip", with another sense, and Hindi ṭīpnā, "to press", beside it (CDIAL 5464); beating flat is not recorded there, so its history is not settled.

Lesson 4 of 5.
કાટ (kāṭ) — rust.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to blow out, then the Gujarati for to beat (metal) flat.

You'll want to know: કાટ.
કાટ — kāṭ — "rust".
વરસાદ પછી લોખંડના દરવાજાને કાટ લાગ્યો. varsād pachī lokhanḍnā darvājāne kāṭ lāgyo. After the rain the iron gate went rusty.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
કાટ (kāṭ) is filed by CDIAL under Sanskrit kiṭṭa, "secretion, dross", and CDIAL lists the Gujarati word as "rust" (CDIAL 3156). Rust strikes the metal: દરવાજાને કાટ લાગ્યો.

You'll want to know: કટાવું.
કટાવું — kaṭāvũ — "to rust".
વરસાદમાં બધાં ઓજારો કટાઈ ગયાં. varsādmā̃ badhā̃ ojāro kaṭāī gayā̃. All the tools rusted in the rain.

The word, taken apart.
કટાવું (kaṭāvũ) is built on કાટ (kāṭ), "rust", which CDIAL files under Sanskrit kiṭṭa, "dross" (CDIAL 3156). It is intransitive: છરી કટાઈ ગઈ.
