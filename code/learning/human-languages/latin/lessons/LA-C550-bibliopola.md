## You'll want to know: bibliopōla, bibliopōlae
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C550-LIBR-02]; assesses=[] -->

**bibliopōla, bibliopōlae** — "a bookseller".

> Bibliopōla novōs librōs in tabernā vendit.
>
> *The bookseller sells new books in his shop.*

Masculine, first declension, from Greek *biblion* 'book' + *pōlein* 'to sell'.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**bibliopōla** was borrowed from Greek *bibliopoles*, built on *biblion* "book" and *poles* "seller" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short).
