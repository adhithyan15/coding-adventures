Marathi, chapter 328: War Comes.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
शत्रू (śatrū) — an enemy.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a chieftain, then the Marathi for to found a settlement.

You'll want to know: शत्रू.
शत्रू — śatrū — "an enemy".
शत्रू रात्रीच्या अंधारात गावात शिरला. śatrū rātrīcyā andhārāt gāvāt śirlā. The enemy crept into the village in the dark of night.
Masculine.
Plural शत्रू (śatrū).

The word, taken apart.
शत्रू (śatrū) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit śatru, 'an enemy, a rival' (Monier-Williams).

Past (he …): तो लढला, to laḍhlā.
The opponent takes -शी or -विरुद्ध: शत्रूशी लढणे (laḍhṇe).

Lesson 5 of 5.
रणांगण (raṇāṅgaṇ) — a battlefield.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for an invasion, then the Marathi for to fight.

You'll want to know: रणांगण.
रणांगण — raṇāṅgaṇ — "a battlefield".
रणांगणावर दोन्ही सैन्यं समोरासमोर आली. raṇāṅgaṇāvar donhī sainyaṁ samorāsamor ālī. On the battlefield the two armies came face to face.
Neuter.
रण, battle, + अंगण, a yard.

The word, taken apart.
रणांगण (raṇāṅgaṇ) is built from Sanskrit raṇa, 'battle', and aṅgaṇa, 'a courtyard' (Monier-Williams): the yard of battle.
