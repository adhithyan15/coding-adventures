Gujarati, chapter 479: Taken Ill.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
માંદું (mā̃dũ) — unwell; sick.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to cough, then the Gujarati for constipation.

You'll want to know: માંદું.
માંદું — mā̃dũ — "unwell; sick".
મીરા ત્રણ દિવસથી માંદી છે. Mīrā traṇ divasthī mā̃dī che. Mira has been unwell for three days.

The word, taken apart.
માંદું (mā̃dũ) goes back to a reconstructed matthara-, "defective", beside Sanskrit manthara, "slow, dull"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati word, "sick" (CDIAL 9754).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a convulsion, then the Gujarati for a lump.

You'll want to know: પથારીવશ.
પથારીવશ — pathārīvaś — "bedridden".
તાવને લીધે દાદી એક અઠવાડિયું પથારીવશ રહ્યાં. tāvne līdhe dādī ek aṭhvāḍiyũ pathārīvaś rahyā̃. Because of the fever my grandmother was bedridden for a week.

The word, taken apart.
પથારીવશ (pathārīvaś) is built from પથારી (pathārī), "bedding", and vaś, from Sanskrit vaśa, "will, power" (Monier-Williams): in the power of the bed.
