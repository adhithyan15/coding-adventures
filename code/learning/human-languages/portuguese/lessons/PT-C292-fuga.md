## You'll want to know: a fuga
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C292-FUGA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**a fuga** — "the escape, the flight".

> A fuga do assaltante durou pouco.
>
> *The robber’s escape did not last long.*

*Pôr-se em fuga* — to take flight. Also a leak: *uma fuga de gás*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*fuga* comes from Latin *fuga* "flight, escape" (EtymDB), from *fugere* "to flee".
