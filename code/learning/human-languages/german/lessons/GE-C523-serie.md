## You'll want to know: die Serie
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C523-FERNSEHEN-03]; assesses=[] -->

**die Serie** — "series, TV series".

> Meine Schwester schaut gerade eine neue Serie über Ärzte.
>
> *My sister is watching a new series about doctors.*

Plural: *die Serien*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Serie* comes from Latin *seriēs* "a row, a chain" (EtymDB), built on *serere* "to join, to bind".
