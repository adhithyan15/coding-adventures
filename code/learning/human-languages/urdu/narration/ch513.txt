Urdu, chapter 513: Free Time.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
فرصت (fursat) — free time; leisure.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for to water (plants), then the Urdu for to uproot.

You'll want to know: فرصت.
فرصت — fursat — "free time; leisure".
آج مجھے بالکل فرصت نہیں ہے۔ āj mujhe bilkul fursat nahīṅ hai. I have no free time at all today.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
فرصت (fursat) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic furṣa, "an occasion, a chance".

What you've built.
One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
