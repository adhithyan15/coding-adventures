# ਵਾਪਸ — back or returning

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-TRAVEL-JOURNEY-01, PA-SCRIPT-RECOG-YA-01, PA-LEX-C67-FOOD67-03] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say *journey*, then *home*. (**ਯਾਤਰਾ**, **ਘਰ**.)

## Grammar Lens: one direction word
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-TRAVEL-BACK-01]; assesses=[PA-LEX-C67-FOOD67-03] -->

> **ਵਾਪਸ** — *vāpas* — back; returning

> **ਵਾਪਸ ਘਰ** — *vāpas ghar* — back home

Keep this as one direction word and one short chunk.
