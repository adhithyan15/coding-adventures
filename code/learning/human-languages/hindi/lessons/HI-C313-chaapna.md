## You'll want to know: छापना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C313-PATRAKAR313-03]; assesses=[] -->

**छापना** — *chāpnā* — "to print".

> अख़बार ने हादसे की तस्वीर छापी।
>
> *akhbār ne hādse kī tasvīr chāpī.*
>
> The newspaper printed a picture of the accident.

Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने ख़बर छापी, *usne khabar chāpī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**छापना** (*chāpnā*) is inherited from *\*chapp*, a pressing, a form CDIAL reconstructs (CDIAL 4994): printing first meant pressing a mark on to cloth or paper.
