## You'll want to know: ofender
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C473-MANEIRAS-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ofender** — "to offend".

> As palavras dele ofenderam a minha mãe.
>
> *His words offended my mother.*

*Ofender-se* — to take offence.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ofender* comes from Latin *offendere* "to strike against, to offend" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short).
