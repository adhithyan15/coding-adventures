# ನಿರ್ಗಮಿಸು (nirgamisu) — to depart; to leave

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[KA-LEX-C434-VILAMBA434-01, KA-LEX-C434-VILAMBA434-02] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Kannada for a delay, then the Kannada for to arrive.

## You'll want to know: ನಿರ್ಗಮಿಸು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C434-VILAMBA434-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ನಿರ್ಗಮಿಸು** — *nirgamisu* — "to depart; to leave".

> ರೈಲು ಬೆಳಗ್ಗೆ ಆರು ಗಂಟೆಗೆ ನಿರ್ಗಮಿಸುತ್ತದೆ.
>
> *railu beḷagge āru gaṇṭege nirgamisuttade.*
>
> The train departs at six in the morning.

Past (I …): ನಿರ್ಗಮಿಸಿದೆ, *nirgamiside*.

Its partner is ಆಗಮಿಸು, to arrive; station boards pair the two.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ನಿರ್ಗಮಿಸು** (*nirgamisu*) is built from Sanskrit *nirgama*, "departure, a way out" (CDIAL 7294), from *nis-* "out" and *gam* "to go", with the verb ending -*isu*.
