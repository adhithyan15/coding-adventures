## You'll want to know: تایید کردن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C310-TAKZIB-02]; assesses=[] -->

**تایید کردن** (*ta'yid kardan*) — "to confirm, to approve".

> پلیس خبر را تایید کرد.
>
> *Polis khabar râ ta'yid kard.*
>
> The police confirmed the report.

Present *ta'yid mikonad*. Careful print adds a hamza sign over the alef.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ta'yid* is usually traced to Arabic *ta'yīd*, "support, backing"; EtymDB lists no entry. *kardan* "to do" is inherited Persian.
