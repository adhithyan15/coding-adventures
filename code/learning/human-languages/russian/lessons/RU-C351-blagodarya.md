## You'll want to know: благодаря
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C351-POSKOLKU-03]; assesses=[] -->

**благодаря** (*blagodaryá*) — "thanks to".

> Благодаря тебе я сдал экзамен.
>
> *Blagodaryá tebé ya sdal ekzámen.*
>
> Thanks to you I passed the exam.

It takes the dative and names a good cause: *благодаря помощи*. For a bad cause Russian says *из-за* + genitive.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**благодаря** is the gerund of *благодарить* "to thank", taken from Church Slavonic *blagodariti* (EtymDB), a compound of *благо* "good" and *дар* "gift". The *-ла-* is the Church Slavonic shape.
