## You'll want to know: la industria
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C592-EMPRESA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**la industria** — "the industry".

> La industria del coche es grande aquí.
>
> *The car industry is big here.*

*Industrial* is the adjective.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*industria* is a learned borrowing from Latin *industria* 'diligence, activity' (EtymDB).
