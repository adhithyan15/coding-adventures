## You'll want to know: ಅಗ್ನಿಶಾಮಕ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C324-BENKI324-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ಅಗ್ನಿಶಾಮಕ** — *agniśāmaka* — "a firefighter".

> ಒಂದು ವೇಳೆ ಬೆಂಕಿ ಹತ್ತಿದರೆ ಅಗ್ನಿಶಾಮಕರು ತಕ್ಷಣ ಬರುತ್ತಾರೆ.
>
> *ondu vēḷe beṅki hattidare agniśāmakaru takṣaṇa baruttāre.*
>
> If a fire breaks out, the firefighters come at once.

Plural: ಅಗ್ನಿಶಾಮಕರು, *agniśāmakaru*.

The fire brigade is ಅಗ್ನಿಶಾಮಕ ದಳ.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಅಗ್ನಿಶಾಮಕ** (*agniśāmaka*) is built from Sanskrit: a modern compound of *agni*, "fire" (CDIAL 55), and a noun built on the root *śam*, "to calm, to put out" (Monier-Williams): one who puts out fire.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
