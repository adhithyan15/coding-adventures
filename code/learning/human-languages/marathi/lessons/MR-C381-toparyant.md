## You'll want to know: तोपर्यंत
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C381-JOPARYANT381-02]; assesses=[] -->

**तोपर्यंत** — *toparyant* — "until then; by then".

> बस सात वाजता येईल, तोपर्यंत आपण चहा घेऊ.
>
> *bas sāt vājtā yeīl, toparyant āpaṇ cahā gheū.*
>
> The bus will come at seven; until then, let us have tea.

It answers जोपर्यंत: जोपर्यंत … तोपर्यंत ….

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**तोपर्यंत** (*toparyant*) is built from तो, "that", and -पर्यंत, "up to", from Sanskrit *paryanta*, "limit, edge" (Monier-Williams): up to that point.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
