## You'll want to know: ढोलक
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C531-DHOLAK531-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ढोलक** — *ḍholak* — "a small two-headed drum".

> शादी में औरतें ढोलक बजाकर गाती हैं।
>
> *Śādī meṁ aurteṁ ḍholak bajākar gātī haiṁ.*
>
> At weddings the women play the dholak and sing.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ढोलक** (*ḍholak*) is a tadbhava, inherited from Sanskrit *ḍhōla*, "a drum" (CDIAL 5608).
