German, chapter 123: Butter, Sauce, Vinegar, Dessert, Meal.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
die Butter — butter.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for a biscuit, then the German for a bread roll.

You'll want to know: die Butter.
die Butter — "butter".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: die Butter]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: die Butter, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say die Butter]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the German for a biscuit, then the German for a bread roll, then say die Butter again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does die Butter mean? ("Butter".) Say it once more.
