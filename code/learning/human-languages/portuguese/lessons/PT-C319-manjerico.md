## You'll want to know: o manjerico
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C319-SANTOS-03]; assesses=[] -->

**o manjerico** — "the bush basil (in a pot)".

> Ofereci um manjerico à minha namorada.
>
> *I gave my girlfriend a pot of basil.*

In June, a small pot of basil with a paper flower and a short poem is a typical gift.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*manjerico* is related to *manjericão* "basil"; the origin of both is not settled, and EtymDB has no entry.
