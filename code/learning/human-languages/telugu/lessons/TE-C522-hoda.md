## You'll want to know: హోదా
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C522-VRTTI522-02]; assesses=[] -->

**హోదా** — *hōdā* — "status; a rank".

> కొత్త ఉద్యోగంలో అతని హోదా పెరిగింది.
>
> *kotta udyōgaṁlō atani hōdā perigindi.*
>
> In his new job his status rose.

Rank in an office or in society.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**హోదా** (*hōdā*) came into Telugu through Hindustani *ohdā*, "an office, a rank", which is usually traced to Arabic.
