Russian, chapter 347: However and moreover.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
однако (odnáko) — however.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for harm, then the Russian for to assess.

You'll want to know: однако.
однако (odnáko) — "however".
Было холодно, однако мы пошли гулять. Býlo khólodno, odnáko my poshlí gulyát. It was cold; however, we went for a walk.
Like но, but more formal. It can open the clause or sit inside it: Мы, однако (odnáko), пошли гулять.

The word, taken apart.
однако (odnáko) is usually traced to один "one", with an old suffix; by that account it first meant "in one and the same way, all the same"; один is inherited from Proto-Slavic.

Lesson 2 of 5.
зато (zató) — but then, on the other hand.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to assess, then the Russian for however.

You'll want to know: зато.
зато (zató) — "but then, on the other hand".
Квартира маленькая, зато в центре. Kvartíra málenkaya, zató v tséntre. The flat is small, but then it is in the centre.
It brings in a plus that makes up for a minus: дорого, зато (zató) удобно. Often но зато (zató). Stress on the second syllable.

The word, taken apart.
зато (zató) is fused from за "for, in return for" and то "that": the good side you get in return for the bad.

Lesson 3 of 5.
причём (prichyóm) — and what is more, moreover.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for however, then the Russian for but then.

You'll want to know: причём.
причём (prichyóm) — "and what is more, moreover".
Он опоздал, причём уже в третий раз. On opozdál, prichyóm uzhé v trétiy raz. He was late, and what is more, for the third time.
It adds a detail that sharpens what came before. Stress on the second syllable.

The word, taken apart.
причём (prichyóm) is fused from при "at, alongside" and чём, the prepositional case of что: "alongside which".

Lesson 4 of 5.
итак (iták) — so, and so (summing up).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for but then, then the Russian for and what is more.

You'll want to know: итак.
итак (iták) — "so, and so (summing up)".
Итак, все готовы? Тогда начинаем. Iták, vse gotóvy? Togdá nachináyem. So, is everyone ready? Then we begin.
It opens a summary or a conclusion. Stress on the second syllable.

The word, taken apart.
итак (iták) is fused from и "and" and так "so", both inherited Slavic words.

Lesson 5 of 5.
иными словами (inými slovámi) — in other words.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for and what is more, then the Russian for so.

You'll want to know: иными словами.
иными словами (inými slovámi) — "in other words".
Он не ответил, иными словами, он против. On ne otvétil, inými slovámi, on prótiv. He did not answer; in other words, he is against it.
The instrumental plural of иной "other" and слово "word". Другими словами means the same.

The word, taken apart.
иными словами (inými slovámi) is the instrumental plural of иной "other", the adjective behind иначе, and слово "word", inherited from Proto-Slavic slovo (EtymDB).
