6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
la angustia — anguish, distress.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for to joke, then the Spanish for to make fun of.

You'll want to know: la angustia.
la angustia — "anguish, distress".
Sentí angustia al no tener noticias. I felt anguish at having no news.
Me da angustia — it makes me anxious.

The word, taken apart.
angustia is a learned borrowing from Latin angustia "narrowness, difficulty, distress" (EtymDB), built on angustus "narrow": distress is a tightness in the chest.

No soporto el ruido. I cannot stand the noise.
No soporto a mi vecino — I cannot stand my neighbour. A false friend: to support is apoyar.

soportar comes from Latin supportāre "to carry, to convey" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short), built on sub "under" and portāre "to carry".

Lesson 4 of 6.
la preocupación — worry, concern.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for to bear, then the Spanish for to doubt.

You'll want to know: la preocupación.
la preocupación — "worry, concern".
Mi mayor preocupación es la salud. My biggest worry is health.
The verb preocuparse is to worry.

The word, taken apart.
preocupación is a learned borrowing from Latin praeoccupātiō "a seizing beforehand" (EtymDB); a worry takes hold of the mind before anything else can.

Lesson 5 of 6.
preocupado — worried.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for to doubt, then the Spanish for worry.

You'll want to know: preocupado.
preocupado — "worried".
Estoy preocupado por mi padre. I am worried about my father.
Used with estar: estar preocupado por.

The word, taken apart.
preocupado is the participle of preocupar, a learned borrowing from Latin praeoccupāre "to seize beforehand" (EtymDB), built on occupāre "to seize".
