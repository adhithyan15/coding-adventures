## Script — a mouth, then three strokes
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-SCRIPT-CHI-01]; assesses=[] -->

> 吃

**Two pieces, left before right.**

**The left piece, 口, three strokes**, small, as in 叫.

**The right piece, three strokes.** A short left-falling stroke. A short horizontal under it. Then one long stroke: across, down, bending right along the bottom and hooking up.

3 + 3 = six.
