## You'll want to know: धोरण
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C358-SANVIDHAN358-02]; assesses=[] -->

**धोरण** — *dhoraṇ* — "a policy".

> सरकारचं नवं शिक्षण धोरण जाहीर झालं.
>
> *sarkārcaṁ navaṁ śikṣaṇ dhoraṇ jāhīr jhālaṁ.*
>
> The government's new education policy was announced.

Neuter.

Neuter: नवं धोरण.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**धोरण** (*dhoraṇ*) is a Marathi noun whose origin is not settled.
