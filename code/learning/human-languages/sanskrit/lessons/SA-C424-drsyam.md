## You'll want to know: दृश्यम्
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C424-PARYATANA424-04]; assesses=[] -->

**दृश्यम्** — *dṛśyam* — "a view; a sight".

> पर्वतात् नगरस्य दृश्यं सुन्दरम् अस्ति।
>
> *parvatāt nagarasya dṛśyaṃ sundaram asti.*
>
> The view of the city from the mountain is beautiful.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**दृश्यम्** (*dṛśyam*) is built on the root √dṛś, "to see", with the ending *-ya*: MW gives *dṛśya* as visible, worth seeing and, as a noun, any visible object; a view is that sense (MW).
