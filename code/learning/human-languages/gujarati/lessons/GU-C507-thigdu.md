## You'll want to know: થીંગડું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C507-BHARAT507-04]; assesses=[] -->

**થીંગડું** — *thī̃gḍũ* — "a patch (on cloth)".

> મારા જૂના ખમીસ પર એક થીંગડું છે.
>
> *mārā jūnā khamīs par ek thī̃gḍũ che.*
>
> There is a patch on my old shirt.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**થીંગડું** (*thī̃gḍũ*) comes from *\*thigga*, a form CDIAL reconstructs, and CDIAL lists the Gujarati word as "a patch, a rag" (CDIAL 6096).
