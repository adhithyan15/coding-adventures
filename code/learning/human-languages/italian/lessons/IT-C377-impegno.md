## You'll want to know: l'impegno
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C377-IMPEGNO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**l'impegno** — "the commitment, the engagement; the effort".

> Ho un impegno, ma se finisco presto ti chiamo.
>
> *I have something on, but if I finish early I will call you.*

*Con impegno* — with effort, seriously.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*impegno* is built on *impegnare* "to pledge", from *pegno* "a pledge", inherited from Latin *pignus* "a pledge, a security" (Lewis & Short).
