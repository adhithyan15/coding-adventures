Marathi, chapter 307: Beginnings and Endings.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
प्रारंभ (prārambh) — a commencement.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for by itself, then the Marathi for lately.

You'll want to know: प्रारंभ.
प्रारंभ — prārambh — "a commencement".
कार्यक्रमाचा प्रारंभ गाण्याने झाला. kāryakramācā prārambh gāṇyāne jhālā. The programme began with a song.
Masculine.
The formal word for सुरुवात.

The word, taken apart.
प्रारंभ (prārambh) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit prārambha, 'a commencement, a beginning' (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 2 of 5.
अंत (ant) — an ending; an end.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for lately, then the Marathi for a commencement.

You'll want to know: अंत.
अंत — ant — "an ending; an end".
या कथेचा अंत फार अनपेक्षित होता. yā kathecā ant phār anpekṣit hotā. The ending of this story was very unexpected.
Masculine.
शेवट is the everyday word; अंत (ant) is also the end of a life.

The word, taken apart.
अंत (ant) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit anta, 'an end, a limit' (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 3 of 5.
अखेर (akher) — at last; finally.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a commencement, then the Marathi for an ending.

You'll want to know: अखेर.
अखेर — akher — "at last; finally".
तीन दिवसांनी अखेर पाऊस थांबला. tīn divsānnī akher pāūs thāmblā. After three days the rain finally stopped.
Also a noun: महिन्याच्या अखेरीस, at the end of the month.

The word, taken apart.
अखेर (akher) is a loan from Persian āxir, 'the end', usually traced to Arabic ākhir, 'last'.

Lesson 4 of 5.
कालखंड (kālkhaṇḍ) — a period; an era.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for an ending, then the Marathi for at last.

You'll want to know: कालखंड.
कालखंड — kālkhaṇḍ — "a period; an era".
तो कालखंड देशासाठी फार कठीण होता. to kālkhaṇḍ deśāsāṭhī phār kaṭhīṇ hotā. That period was very hard for the country.
Masculine.
Built from काल, time, and खंड, a piece.

The word, taken apart.
कालखंड (kālkhaṇḍ) is a learned compound built from Sanskrit kāla, 'time', and khaṇḍa, 'a piece, a section' (Monier-Williams).
