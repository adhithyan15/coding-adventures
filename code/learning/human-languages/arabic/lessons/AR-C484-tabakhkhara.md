## You'll want to know: تبخر
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C484-HARR484-05]; assesses=[] -->

**تبخر** — *tabakhkhara* — "to evaporate".

> تبخر الماء في الشمس.
>
> *tabakhkhara al-māʾ fī ash-shams.*
>
> The water evaporated in the sun.

Present: يتبخر, *yatabakhkhar*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**تبخر** (*tabakhkhara*) is built on the native Arabic root *b-kh-r*, "vapour, steam" (Lane). It is Form V, "to turn to vapour".
