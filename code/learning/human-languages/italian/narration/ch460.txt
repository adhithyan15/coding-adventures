Lesson 1 of 6.
la radura — the clearing (in a wood).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for the bank transfer, then the Italian for the instalment.

You'll want to know: la radura.
la radura — "the clearing (in a wood)".
Abbiamo mangiato in una radura. We ate in a clearing.
Where the trees are radi, far apart.

The word, taken apart.
radura is built on rado 'sparse', which is inherited from Latin rārus 'thin, far apart' (EtymDB).

What you've built.
One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.

A small, quiet stream; il fiume is a river.

Lesson 3 of 6.
il torrente — the mountain stream, the torrent.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for the clearing, then the Italian for the brook.

You'll want to know: il torrente.
il torrente — "the mountain stream, the torrent".
Dopo la pioggia il torrente è pieno. After the rain the mountain stream is full.
Fast, and often dry in summer.

The word, taken apart.
torrente is inherited from Latin torrēns, torrentem 'rushing stream' (EtymDB), a form of torrēre 'to scorch'.

Lesson 5 of 6.
guadare — to wade across, to ford.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for the mountain stream, then the Italian for the bush.

You'll want to know: guadare.
guadare — "to wade across, to ford".
Abbiamo guadato il ruscello a piedi. We waded across the brook on foot.
Il guado — the ford.

The word, taken apart.
guadare is built on guado 'ford', which is inherited from Latin vadum 'shallow place' (Lewis & Short), its gu- shaped by a Germanic word.
