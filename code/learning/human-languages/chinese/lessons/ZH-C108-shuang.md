## You'll want to know: shuāng
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C108-SHOP108-03]; assesses=[] -->

*shuāng* — "a pair of (a measure word)".

> *wǒ xiǎng mǎi yì shuāng xié* — I'd like to buy a pair of shoes.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*shuāng* is inherited from Middle Chinese *sraewng* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'a pair, two of a kind'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for asking for what I want.
