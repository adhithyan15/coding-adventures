Bengali, chapter 189: Court of law, Prison, Cave, Ravine, Waterfall.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
আদালত (ādālôt) — a court of law.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a flat, then the Bengali for a storey.

You'll want to know: আদালত.
আদালত — ādālôt — "a court of law".

The word, taken apart.
আদালত (ādālôt) is a loan from Arabic ʿadāla, "justice", which reached Bengali through Persian. Its Arabic root is ʿ-d-l (Lane). Persian keeps the -t of the Arabic ending.

Lesson 3 of 5.
গুহা (guhā) — a cave.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a court of law, then the Bengali for a prison.

You'll want to know: গুহা.
গুহা — guhā — "a cave".

The word, taken apart.
গুহা (guhā) is Sanskrit guhā, "hiding place, cave", taken over whole as a learned loan, a tatsama. CDIAL lists Bengali gophā, "cave", under gupphā (CDIAL 4204), which it links to guhā only doubtfully.

Lesson 4 of 5.
খাদ (khād) — a ravine.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a prison, then the Bengali for a cave.

You'll want to know: খাদ.
খাদ — khād — "a ravine".

The word, taken apart.
খাদ (khād), "a ravine", appears to go back to Sanskrit khāta, "dug up; a ditch", under which CDIAL lists Bengali khāī, "ditch", and a khād, "refuse" (CDIAL 3862); CDIAL does not list khād in the sense "ravine".

Lesson 5 of 5.
ঝরনা (jhôrnā) — a waterfall.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a cave, then the Bengali for a ravine.

You'll want to know: ঝরনা.
ঝরনা — jhôrnā — "a waterfall".

The word, taken apart.
ঝরনা (jhôrnā) is a tadbhava, a word inherited and reshaped by sound change. It comes from jharati, "flowing down", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 5346). CDIAL lists the verb jharā, "to ooze out, drip"; jhôrnā is a noun built on it.
