## You'll want to know: a lebre
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C460-SELVAGENS-03]; assesses=[] -->

**a lebre** — "the hare".

> A lebre corre mais depressa do que o coelho.
>
> *The hare runs faster than the rabbit.*

*Gato por lebre* — a cat for a hare: being cheated.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*lebre* is inherited from Latin *leporem*, the accusative of *lepus* 'hare' (EtymDB).
