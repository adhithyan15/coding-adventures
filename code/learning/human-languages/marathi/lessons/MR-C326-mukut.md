## You'll want to know: मुकुट
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C326-SAMRAT326-03]; assesses=[] -->

**मुकुट** — *mukuṭ* — "a crown".

> राणीच्या मुकुटात मोती होते.
>
> *rāṇīcyā mukuṭāt motī hote.*
>
> There were pearls in the queen's crown.

Masculine.

मुकुट घालणे, to wear a crown.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**मुकुट** (*mukuṭ*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *mukuṭa*, 'a crown, a diadem' (Monier-Williams).
