# ઇન્ટરવ્યૂ (inṭaravyū) — a job interview

## You'll want to know: ઇન્ટરવ્યૂ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C512-NOKRI512-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ઇન્ટરવ્યૂ** — *inṭaravyū* — "a job interview".

> મારો ઇન્ટરવ્યૂ કાલે સવારે છે.
>
> *māro inṭaravyū kāle savāre che.*
>
> My interview is tomorrow morning.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ઇન્ટરવ્યૂ** (*inṭaravyū*) is borrowed from English *interview*.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
