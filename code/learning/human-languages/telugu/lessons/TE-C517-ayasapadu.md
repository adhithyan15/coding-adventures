## You'll want to know: ఆయాసపడు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C517-LAKSANAM517-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ఆయాసపడు** — *āyāsapaḍu* — "to pant; to get breathless".

> కొండ ఎక్కి తాతయ్య ఆయాసపడ్డారు.
>
> *koṇḍa ekki tātayya āyāsapaḍḍāru.*
>
> Climbing the hill, grandpa got breathless.

Past (I …): ఆయాసపడ్డాను, *āyāsapaḍḍānu*.

ఆయాసం, breathlessness, + పడు.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ఆయాసపడు** (*āyāsapaḍu*) is built from ఆయాసం, from Sanskrit *āyāsa*, "effort, exertion; fatigue" (Monier-Williams) and the native verb *paḍu*, "to feel, to suffer" (DEDR 3853).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
