## You'll want to know: palleō, pallēre
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C522-PALL-01]; assesses=[] -->

**palleō, pallēre** — "to be pale".

> Ancilla timōre pallet.
>
> *The maid is pale with fear.*

*palleō, pallēre, palluī*. English *pallor* comes from its noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**palleō** is inherited from a Proto-Indo-European root meaning "grey, pale" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short). English *pallor* comes from the noun built on it.
