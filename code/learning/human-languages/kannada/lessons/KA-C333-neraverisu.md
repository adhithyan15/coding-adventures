## You'll want to know: ನೆರವೇರಿಸು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C333-NERAVERU333-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ನೆರವೇರಿಸು** — *neravērisu* — "to carry out (a rite); to perform".

> ಅರ್ಚಕರು ಮದುವೆಯ ಪೂಜೆಯನ್ನು ನೆರವೇರಿಸಿದರು.
>
> *arcakaru maduveya pūjeyannu neravērisidaru.*
>
> The priest performed the wedding puja.

Past (I …): ನೆರವೇರಿಸಿದೆ, *neravēriside*.

Rites and ceremonies are carried out with it. A wish or a plan that comes true ನೆರವೇರುತ್ತದೆ. Before a rite is carried out, the house is made ಸ್ವಚ್ಛ, spotless.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ನೆರವೇರಿಸು** (*neravērisu*) is usually traced to the native Dravidian *neṟe*, "to become complete, to be realized, to occur" (DEDR 3682), with the causative ending -*isu*.
