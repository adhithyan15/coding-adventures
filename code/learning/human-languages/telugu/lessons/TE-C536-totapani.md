## You'll want to know: తోటపని
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C536-VIRAMAM536-03]; assesses=[] -->

**తోటపని** — *tōṭapani* — "gardening".

> ఆదివారాల్లో నాన్న తోటపని చేస్తారు.
>
> *ādivārāllō nānna tōṭapani cēstāru.*
>
> On Sundays dad does the gardening.

తోట, garden, + పని, work.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**తోటపని** (*tōṭapani*) is a native compound: *tōṭa*, "garden, grove" (DEDR 3549), and *pani*, "work" (DEDR 3884).
