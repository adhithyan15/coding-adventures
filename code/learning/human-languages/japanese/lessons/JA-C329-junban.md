## You'll want to know: じゅんばん
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C329-JOUSHIKI329-04]; assesses=[] -->

**じゅんばん** — *junban* — "one's turn; order".

> **じゅんばんが きたら、はいって ください。** — *junban ga kitara, haitte kudasai* — "When your turn comes, please go in."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**じゅんばん** (*junban*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *jun*, "order" (Middle Chinese *zywinH*) and *ban*, "turn" (*phjon*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
