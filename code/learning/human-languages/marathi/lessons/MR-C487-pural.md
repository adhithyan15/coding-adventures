## You'll want to know: पुरळ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C487-CATKA487-03]; assesses=[] -->

**पुरळ** — *puraḷ* — "a rash; spots".

> उन्हामुळे मुलाच्या पाठीवर पुरळ आलं.
>
> *unhāmuḷe mulācyā pāṭhīvar puraḷ ālaṁ.*
>
> The sun brought out a rash on the boy's back.

Neuter.

Neuter; पुरळ येणे, to break out in a rash.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पुरळ** (*puraḷ*) is a Marathi noun whose origin is not settled.

## What you've built
