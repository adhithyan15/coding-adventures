German, chapter 439: Stadtteile.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
der Stadtteil — the district, the part of town.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for the town wall, then the German for the town gate.

You'll want to know: der Stadtteil.
der Stadtteil — "the district, the part of town".
In unserem Stadtteil gibt es zwei Schulen. There are two schools in our part of town.
Plural: die Stadtteile.

The word, taken apart.
Stadtteil is built from Stadt 'town' and Teil 'part', inherited from Proto-Germanic dailą (EtymDB).

Lesson 2 of 5.
das Viertel — the neighbourhood, the quarter (of a town); a quarter.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for the town gate, then the German for the district.

You'll want to know: das Viertel.
das Viertel — "the neighbourhood, the quarter (of a town); a quarter".
Sie wohnt in einem ruhigen Viertel am Rand der Stadt. She lives in a quiet neighbourhood on the edge of town.
Same form in the plural. Also a quarter: ein Viertel nach drei.

The word, taken apart.
Viertel is built on vier 'four' and first meant a fourth part, then a part of a town. Both are inherited Germanic words.

Lesson 4 of 5.
das Polizeirevier — the police station.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for the neighbourhood, then the German for the suburb.

You'll want to know: das Polizeirevier.
das Polizeirevier — "the police station".
Nach dem Diebstahl geht er zum Polizeirevier. After the theft he goes to the police station.
Plural: die Polizeireviere. Also die Polizeiwache or die Wache.

The word, taken apart.
Polizeirevier is built from Polizei 'police', from Latin polītīa 'the state, government' (EtymDB), and Revier 'district', which goes back to Middle High German riviere 'hunting ground' (EtymDB), usually traced to French.

Lesson 5 of 5.
die Kaserne — the barracks.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for the suburb, then the German for the police station.

You'll want to know: die Kaserne.
die Kaserne — "the barracks".
Die alte Kaserne ist heute ein Museum. The old barracks is a museum today.
Plural: die Kasernen.

The word, taken apart.
Kaserne was borrowed from French caserne (EtymDB), from Provençal cazerna, a small room for four soldiers, which goes back to Latin quaternī 'four at a time' (Lewis & Short).
