## You'll want to know: दरजी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C265-MOCHI265-04]; assesses=[] -->

**दरजी** — *darjī* — "a tailor".

> दरजी कुरतो सीवै है।
>
> *darjī kurto sīvai hai.*
>
> The tailor sews a kurta.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**दरजी** (*darjī*) is usually traced to Persian *darzī*, "tailor".
