## You'll want to know: le doctorat
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C379-DIPLOME-04]; assesses=[] -->

**le doctorat** — "the doctorate, the PhD".

> Mon cousin fait un doctorat en chimie.
>
> *My cousin is doing a PhD in chemistry.*

The holder is *un docteur*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*doctorat* comes from Medieval Latin *doctōrātus*, built on *doctor* "teacher", from *docēre* "to teach" (Lewis & Short).
