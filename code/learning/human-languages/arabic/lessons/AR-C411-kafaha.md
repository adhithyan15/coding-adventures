## You'll want to know: كافح
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C411-IJAZA411-04]; assesses=[] -->

**كافح** — *kāfaḥa* — "to struggle against".

> تكافح الدولة الفقر.
>
> *tukāfiḥu ad-dawla al-faqr.*
>
> The state is fighting poverty.

Present: يكافح, *yukāfiḥ*.

What is fought is the direct object: كافح الفساد, fought corruption.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**كافح** (*kāfaḥa*) is built on the native Arabic root *k-f-ḥ*, "to meet face to face" (Lane). It is Form III, to face an enemy head on: to struggle against.
