## You'll want to know: じょうだん
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C371-JOUDAN371-01]; assesses=[] -->

**じょうだん** — *jōdan* — "a joke".

> **じょうだんを 言ったら、みんな わらうでしょう。** — *jōdan o ittara, minna warau deshō* — "If you tell a joke, everyone will probably laugh."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**じょうだん** (*jōdan*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *jō*, "superfluous" and *dan*, "discuss" (Middle Chinese *dam*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
