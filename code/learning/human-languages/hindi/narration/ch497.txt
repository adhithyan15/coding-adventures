Hindi, chapter 497: A New Baby.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
जन्म (janm) — birth.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for to mumble, then the Hindi for a bedroll.

You'll want to know: जन्म.
जन्म — janm — "birth".
मीरा का जन्म एक छोटे गाँव में हुआ था। Mīrā kā janm ek choṭe gā̃v meṁ huā thā. Mira was born in a small village.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
जन्म (janm) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit janman, "birth, origin" (Monier-Williams). janmdin, "birthday", is built on it.

Lesson 5 of 5.
पैदा होना (paidā honā) — to be born.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for childbirth, then the Hindi for newborn.

You'll want to know: पैदा होना.
पैदा होना — paidā honā — "to be born".
अरुण की बहन के घर एक बेटी पैदा हुई। Aruṇ kī bahan ke ghar ek beṭī paidā huī. A daughter was born to Arun's sister.
Past (he …): वह गाँव में पैदा हुआ, vah gā̃v meṁ paidā huā.

The word, taken apart.
पैदा होना (paidā honā) joins paidā, usually traced to Persian paidā, "visible, born", to honā, "to be": to come into view. janm honā says the same thing more formally.
