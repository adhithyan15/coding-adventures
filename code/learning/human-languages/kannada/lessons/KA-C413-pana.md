## You'll want to know: ಪಣ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C413-PANA413-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ಪಣ** — *paṇa* — "a bet; a stake".

> ನಾಳೆ ಮಳೆ ಬರುತ್ತದೆ ಅಂತ ನಾವು ಪಣ ಕಟ್ಟಿದೆವು.
>
> *nāḷe maḷe baruttade anta nāvu paṇa kaṭṭidevu.*
>
> We bet that it would rain tomorrow.

ಪಣ ಕಟ್ಟು, to place a bet; ಪಣಕ್ಕೆ ಇಡು, to put at stake.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಪಣ** (*paṇa*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *paṇa*, "a wager, a stake" (CDIAL 7714).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
