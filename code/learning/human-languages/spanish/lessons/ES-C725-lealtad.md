## You'll want to know: la lealtad
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C725-FIEL-03]; assesses=[] -->

**la lealtad** — "loyalty".

> Valoro la lealtad por encima de todo.
>
> *I value loyalty above everything.*

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*lealtad* is built on *leal* "loyal" with the ending *-tad*; EtymDB traces its Portuguese twin *lealdade* to Medieval Latin *lēgālitās*, from *lēgālis* "of the law".
