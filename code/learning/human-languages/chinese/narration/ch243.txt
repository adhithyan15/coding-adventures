Mandarin Chinese, chapter 243: Changing Seasons.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
气候 (qìhòu) — climate.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for a bug, then the Mandarin for a mosquito.

You'll want to know: 气候.
气候 — qìhòu — "climate".
西边的气候不好风很大 xībiān de qìhòu bù hǎo fēng hěn dà — The climate in the west isn't good: it's very windy.

The word, taken apart.
气候 (qìhòu) is built from 气 'air' and 候 'a season', both inherited from Middle Chinese, khj+jH and huwH (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'to wait on'; the sense of a season is not given there.

What you've built.
One more word for describing things.

Lesson 3 of 5.
biànhuà — to change over time; a change.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for climate, then the Mandarin for a snowflake.

You'll want to know: biànhuà.
biànhuà — "to change over time; a change".
zhèr de tiānqì biànhuà hěn dà — The weather here changes a lot.
Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
biànhuà is built from biàn 'to alter' and huà 'to transform', both inherited from Middle Chinese, pjenH and xwaeH (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses each with the other.

Lesson 4 of 5.
jiébīng — to freeze over.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for a snowflake, then the Mandarin for to change over time.

You'll want to know: jiébīng.
jiébīng — "to freeze over".
dōngtiān hú lǐ de shuǐ jiébīng le — In winter the water in the lake freezes over.
Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
jiébīng is built from jié 'to knot' and bīng 'ice', both inherited from Middle Chinese, ket and ping (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to tie' and the second 'water frozen'.
