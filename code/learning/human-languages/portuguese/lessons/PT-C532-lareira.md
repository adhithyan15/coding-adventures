## You'll want to know: a lareira
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C532-ACOLHEDORA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**a lareira** — "fireplace".

> No inverno, jantamos ao pé da lareira.
>
> *In winter, we have dinner by the fireplace.*

*Acender a lareira* — to light the fire.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*lareira* is built on *lar* "hearth, home", which comes from Latin *lar*, the god of the household (Lewis & Short).
