## You'll want to know: ભંગાર
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C513-KARKHANU513-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ભંગાર** — *bhangār* — "scrap".

> આ જૂનું મશીન હવે ભંગારમાં જશે.
>
> *ā jūnũ maśīn have bhangārmā̃ jaśe.*
>
> This old machine will now go for scrap.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ભંગાર** (*bhangār*) comes from *\*bhaṅgākara*, a form CDIAL reconstructs, and CDIAL lists the Gujarati word as "broken pots" (CDIAL 9356); today it is any broken metal or junk sold for scrap.
