# أن أذهب (an adhhaba) — to go: the verb after an

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[AR-GRAMMAR-C285-OPINION-ANNA-01, AR-GRAMMAR-C285-FA-SO-01, AR-LEX-C285-RAY285-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "I think that the exam is hard". (*Aʿtaqidu anna al-imtiḥāna
ṣaʿb*.) Then "in my opinion". (*Fī raʾyī*.)

[PAUSE 1s] You have said *anwī an azūra jaddatī*, "I intend to visit my
grandmother". Listen to the end of *azūra*. It is not the *azūru* of "I
visit", and this lesson says why.

## Grammar Lens: -u becomes -a
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-GRAMMAR-C286-SUBJUNCTIVE-01]; assesses=[AR-LEX-C157-MUSTAQBAL157-05, AR-LEX-C61-VERBS61-03, AR-LEX-C93-TOWN93-02] -->

After **أن** — *an*, "to", "that" before a verb — the present changes its
last vowel. *Adhhabu*, I go, becomes *an adhhaba*, to go:

> **أريد أن أذهب إلى السوق.** — *urīdu an adhhaba ilā as-sūq* — I want to go
> to the market.

This shape of the present is the **subjunctive**: the verb for a thing
wanted, planned or needed, not yet a fact. *Urīdu*, I want, is the present
of *arāda*.

Where the present ends in *-ūna* or *-īna*, the *-na* falls away:
*yadhhabūna*, they go, becomes *an yadhhabū*; *tadhhabīna*, you go (to a
woman), becomes *an tadhhabī*.

## Grammar Lens: an and anna
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[AR-GRAMMAR-C40-ANNA-01, AR-LEX-C157-MUSTAQBAL157-04, AR-LEX-C159-ACT159-04, AR-LEX-C92-TOWN92-01] -->

> **قررنا أن نسافر بالقطار.** — *qarrarnā an nusāfira bi-l-qiṭār* — We
> decided to travel by train.

*An* takes a verb: what someone wants or decides to do. *Anna*, with the
doubled *n*, takes a sentence: what someone thinks is so. *Aʿtaqidu anna
al-qiṭāra sarīʿ*; *urīdu an arkaba al-qiṭār*.

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[AR-GRAMMAR-C286-SUBJUNCTIVE-01, AR-GRAMMAR-C40-ANNA-01] -->

[PAUSE 3s] After *an*, what happens to *-u*? (**It becomes *-a***: *an
adhhaba*.) And to *-ūna*? (**The *-na* falls away**: *an yadhhabū*.) Which
particle takes a verb, and which a sentence? (***An*** a verb, ***anna*** a
sentence.)
