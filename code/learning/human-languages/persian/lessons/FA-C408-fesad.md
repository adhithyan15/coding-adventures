## You'll want to know: فساد
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C408-KOLAHBARDARI-04]; assesses=[] -->

**فساد** (*fesâd*) — "corruption".

> مردم از فساد خسته شده اند.
>
> *mardom az fesâd khaste shode and.*
>
> People are tired of corruption.

Also decay: *fesâd-e mive*, fruit going bad.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**فساد** was borrowed from Arabic *fasād* "corruption, decay" (EtymDB).
