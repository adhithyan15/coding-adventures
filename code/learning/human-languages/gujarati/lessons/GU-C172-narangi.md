## You'll want to know: નારંગી
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C172-STALE172-04]; assesses=[] -->

**નારંગી** — *nārangī* — "orange".

> **નારંગી ખાટી અને મીઠી છે.** — *nārangī khāṭī ane mīṭhī che* — "The orange is sour and sweet."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**નારંગી** (*nārangī*) is a tadbhava. It comes from Sanskrit *nāraṅga*, *nāraṅgī*, "orange tree", and CDIAL glosses the Gujarati word "the tree and fruit" (CDIAL 7073).
