## You'll want to know: impedir
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C353-OBSTACULO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**impedir** — "to prevent, to stop (from)".

> A chuva impediu-nos de sair.
>
> *The rain stopped us from going out.*

*Impedir alguém de fazer algo* — to prevent someone from doing something.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*impedir* is a learned borrowing from Latin *impedīre* "to entangle, to hinder" (EtymDB), built from *in* and *pēs, pedis* "foot" (Lewis & Short): to catch someone by the feet.
