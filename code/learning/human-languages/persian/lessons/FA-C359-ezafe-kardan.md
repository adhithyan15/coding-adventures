## You'll want to know: اضافه کردن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C359-ZAFARAN-02]; assesses=[] -->

**اضافه کردن** (*ezâfe kardan*) — "to add".

> در آخر کمی نمک اضافه کرد.
>
> *Dar âkhar kami namak ezâfe kard.*
>
> At the end she added a little salt.

With *be*: *be khoresh ezâfe kard*. *Ezâfe* is also the name of the Persian linking *-e*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ezâfe* is borrowed from Arabic *iḍāfa*, "an adding, an adjunct" (EtymDB). *kardan* "to do" is inherited Persian.
