## You'll want to know: واژگون شدن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C324-HADESE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**واژگون شدن** (*vâzhgun shodan*) — "to overturn, to flip over".

> اتوبوس در برف واژگون شد.
>
> *Otobus dar barf vâzhgun shod.*
>
> The bus overturned in the snow.

Written Persian; in speech *chap kardan*. Present *vâzhgun mishavad*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*vâzhgun* "upside down" is a native Persian word written with ژ; EtymDB lists no entry, and its deeper origin is not settled. *shodan* "to become" is inherited Persian.
