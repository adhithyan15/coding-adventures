## You'll want to know: दिलासा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C310-BICHHUD310-05]; assesses=[] -->

**दिलासा** — *dilāsā* — "comfort; consolation".

> पड़ोसियों ने माँ को दिलासा दिया।
>
> *paṛosiyoṁ ne māṁ ko dilāsā diyā.*
>
> The neighbours comforted the mother.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**दिलासा** (*dilāsā*) is usually traced to Persian *dilāsā*, "a consoling", whose first part is *dil*, the heart, as in **दिल**. **दिलासा देना** is to comfort someone.
