## You'll want to know: ભોળું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C460-BHOLU460-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ભોળું** — *bhoḷũ* — "innocent; trusting".

> નાનું બાળક ભોળું હોય છે.
>
> *nānũ bāḷak bhoḷũ hoy che.*
>
> A small child is trusting.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ભોળું** (*bhoḷũ*) goes back to a reconstructed *\*bhulla-*, "defective"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati word, "foolish, credulous" (CDIAL 9539), and today it is mostly the innocence.
