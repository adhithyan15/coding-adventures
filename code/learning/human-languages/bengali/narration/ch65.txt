Bengali, chapter 65: Word, Picture, Tree, Flower, Leaf.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
শব্দ (shôbdo) — a word.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for money; taka, then the Bengali for a question.

You'll want to know: শব্দ.
শব্দ — shôbdo — "a word".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]

[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: shôbdo, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say shôbdo]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Bengali for money; taka, then the Bengali for a question, then say shôbdo again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does শব্দ (shôbdo) mean? ("A word".) Say it once more.
