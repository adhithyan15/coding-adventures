Malayalam, chapter 117: The Body Asks.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ദാഹം (dāhaṁ) — thirst.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for laughter, then the Malayalam for worry.

You'll want to know: ദാഹം.
ദാഹം (dāhaṁ) — "thirst". എനിക്ക് ദാഹമുണ്ട് — "I am thirsty", built like എനിക്ക് വിശപ്പുണ്ട്, "I am hungry".

What you've built.
Hunger's partner.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: dāhaṁ]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: dāhaṁ, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say enikkŭ dāhamuṇṭŭ]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Malayalam for laughter, then the Malayalam for worry, then say dāhaṁ again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does ദാഹം (dāhaṁ) mean? ("Thirst".) Say it once more.
