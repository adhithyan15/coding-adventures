Italian, chapter 453: Piccoli negozi.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
la ferramenta — the hardware shop; hardware.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for the newsagent, then the Italian for the stationer's.

You'll want to know: la ferramenta.
la ferramenta — "the hardware shop; hardware".
In ferramenta ho trovato le viti giuste. At the hardware shop I found the right screws.
Il negozio di ferramenta in full; la ferramenta is also the goods.

The word, taken apart.
ferramenta is inherited from Latin ferrāmentum 'iron tool' (EtymDB), from ferrum 'iron'.

What you've built.

Lesson 3 of 5.

la profumeria — "the perfume shop, the perfumery".

Make-up and soaps too.

The word, taken apart.
profumeria is built on profumo 'scent', from per 'through' and fumo 'smoke', which is inherited from Latin fūmus (Lewis & Short): scent carried through smoke.

gioielleria is built on gioiello 'jewel', which was borrowed from Old French joel, usually traced to Latin iocus 'game, joke' (Lewis & Short).
