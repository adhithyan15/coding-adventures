## You'll want to know: प्रशिक्षण
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C507-ROJGAR507-03]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रशिक्षण** — *praśikṣaṇ* — "training".

> नोकरीच्या आधी मला एक महिना प्रशिक्षण मिळालं.
>
> *nokrīcyā ādhī malā ek mahinā praśikṣaṇ miḷālaṁ.*
>
> Before the job I got a month of training.

Neuter.

The trainer is प्रशिक्षक.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रशिक्षण** (*praśikṣaṇ*) is a tatsama coinage from Sanskrit *pra-*, "forth", and *śikṣaṇa*, "teaching, instruction" (Monier-Williams).
