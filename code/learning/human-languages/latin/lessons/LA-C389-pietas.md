## You'll want to know: pietās, pietātis
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C389-SERVITUS-03]; assesses=[] -->

**pietās, pietātis** — "dutifulness, devotion (to gods, family, country)".

> Fīlius magnā pietāte patrem cūrābat.
>
> *The son looked after his father with great devotion.*

*pietās, pietātis* f., built on *pius*, dutiful: the loyalty owed to gods, parents and country. English *piety* and *pity*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*pietās* is built on *pius* "dutiful" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short), with the ending *-tās* that makes a quality. English *piety* and *pity* both come from it.
