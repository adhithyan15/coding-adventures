Gujarati, chapter 533: A Roll of the Dice.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
પાસો (pāso) — a die.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for convenient, then the Gujarati for a resignation.

You'll want to know: પાસો.
પાસો — pāso — "a die".
જો તારો પાસો છ પર પડશે તો તું ફરી રમીશ. jo tāro pāso cha par paḍśe to tũ pharī ramīś. If your die lands on six, you'll play again.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
પાસો (pāso) goes back to Sanskrit pāśa, "a die", and CDIAL lists the Gujarati word (CDIAL 8132).

Lesson 2 of 5.
ચોપાટ (copāṭ) — chaupat (a dice board game).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a resignation, then the Gujarati for a die.

You'll want to know: ચોપાટ.
ચોપાટ — copāṭ — "chaupat (a dice board game)".
જો બહાર વરસાદ પડશે તો આપણે ઘરમાં ચોપાટ રમીશું. jo bahār varsād paḍśe to āpaṇe gharmā̃ copāṭ ramīśũ. If it rains outside, we'll play chaupat indoors.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ચોપાટ (copāṭ) comes from catuṣpaṭṭa, "a four-sided tablet", a form CDIAL reconstructs, and CDIAL lists the Gujarati word as a dice board (CDIAL 4639).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a die, then the Gujarati for chaupat.

દાવ — dāv — "a turn (in a game)".
જો હવે મારો દાવ હોત તો હું જીતી જાત. jo have māro dāv hot to hũ jītī jāt. If it were my turn now, I would win.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
દાવ (dāv) is filed by CDIAL under Sanskrit dātu, "a share", with the Gujarati word glossed "a turn, a throw" (CDIAL 6258).

Lesson 5 of 5.
ખેલવું (khelvũ) — to play a game of chance.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a turn (in a game), then the Gujarati for a round (of a game).

You'll want to know: ખેલવું.
ખેલવું — khelvũ — "to play a game of chance".
જો તું આ દાવ ખેલત તો તું જીતી જાત. jo tũ ā dāv khelat to tũ jītī jāt. If you had played that turn, you would have won.
Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે દાવ ખેલ્યો, teṇe dāv khelyo.

The word, taken apart.
ખેલવું (khelvũ) comes from khēḍ, "play", a form CDIAL reconstructs, and CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb (CDIAL 3918). Beside રમવું it is the bolder word, for a game played for a stake.
