## You'll want to know: そで
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C151-KIMONO151-04]; assesses=[] -->

**そで** — *sode* — "a sleeve".

> **この ふくは そでが ながいです。** — *kono fuku wa sode ga nagai desu* — "The sleeves of these clothes are long."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**そで** (*sode*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi, and its older origin is not settled (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
