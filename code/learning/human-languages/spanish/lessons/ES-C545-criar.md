## You'll want to know: criar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C545-NINEZ-01]; assesses=[] -->

**criar** — "to bring up, to raise".

> Mi abuela me crió en el pueblo.
>
> *My grandmother brought me up in the village.*

*Criarse* is to grow up: *Me crié en Valencia.* It takes an accent: *crío*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*criar* is inherited from Latin *creāre* 'to make, to bring forth' (EtymDB). Spanish kept the everyday sense of rearing children; *crear* 'to create' is the same verb taken again from books.
