French, chapter 279: Écrire pour le journal.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
rédiger — to write, to draft (a text).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for the weekly, then the French for the subscriber.

You'll want to know: rédiger.
rédiger — "to write, to draft (a text)".
Le journaliste a rédigé son article en une heure. The journalist wrote his article in an hour.
For a text with a purpose: rédiger une lettre, un rapport. In daily speech, écrire.

The word, taken apart.
rédiger is a learned borrowing from Latin redigere "to bring back, to reduce to order" (EtymDB), from red- "back" and agere "to drive" (Lewis & Short).

What you've built.
One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.

la rédaction — "the newsroom, the editorial staff; a school essay".
Toute la rédaction a travaillé tard ce soir-là. The whole newsroom worked late that evening.
At school, une rédaction is an essay.

The word, taken apart.
rédaction is a learned borrowing from Latin redāctiō (EtymDB), from redigere "to put in order": first the writing of a text, then the people who write it.

Lesson 4 of 5.
paraître — to come out, to be published; to seem.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for the newsroom, then the French for to print.

You'll want to know: paraître.
paraître — "to come out, to be published; to seem".
Le magazine paraît tous les jeudis. The magazine comes out every Thursday.
Two senses: to be published, and il paraît que — it seems, people say. The current spelling also allows paraitre.

The word, taken apart.
paraître is inherited from Late Latin pārēscere, built on Latin pārēre "to appear, to come in sight" (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 5 of 5.
la parution — the publication, the release (of a book or paper).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for to print, then the French for to come out.

You'll want to know: la parution.
la parution — "the publication, the release (of a book or paper)".
Le livre a eu du succès dès sa parution. The book was a success as soon as it came out.
La date de parution — the publication date.

The word, taken apart.
parution is built on paru, the past participle of paraître, which comes from Latin pārēre "to appear" (Lewis & Short).
