## You'll want to know: そくど
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C390-KOUSOKU390-03]; assesses=[] -->

**そくど** — *sokudo* — "speed".

> **あめの ときは、そくどを おとして ください。** — *ame no toki wa, sokudo o otoshite kudasai* — "Please slow down when it rains."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**そくど** (*sokudo*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *soku*, "fast" (Middle Chinese *suwk*) and *do*, "degrees" (*duH*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
