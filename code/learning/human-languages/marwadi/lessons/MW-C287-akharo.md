## You'll want to know: अखाड़ो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C287-GURU287-05]; assesses=[] -->

**अखाड़ो** — *akhāṛo* — "a wrestling ground; a training ground".

> गांव मांय एक अखाड़ो है।
>
> *gā̃v mā̃y ek akhāṛo hai.*
>
> There's a wrestling ground in the village.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अखाड़ो** (*akhāṛo*) is inherited from Sanskrit *akṣavāṭa*, "a wrestling ground"; CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry, but lists Hindi *akhāṛā*, "wrestling ground", and Gujarati *akhāṛo*, "wrestling place" (CDIAL 39).
