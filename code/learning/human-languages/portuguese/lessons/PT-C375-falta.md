## You'll want to know: a falta
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C375-ESCOLA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**a falta** — "the absence (from class); the lack; the foul".

> Tive uma falta porque cheguei atrasado.
>
> *I was marked absent because I arrived late.*

*Fazer falta* — to be missed or needed; *sem falta* — without fail. In football, a foul.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*falta* is built on *faltar* "to be missing", usually traced to Vulgar Latin *fallita*, from Latin *fallere* "to deceive, to disappoint" (Lewis & Short).
