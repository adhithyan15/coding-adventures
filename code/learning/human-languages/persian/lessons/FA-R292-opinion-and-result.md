# Review — what I think, and what follows

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[FA-LEX-BE-NAZAR-E-MAN-01, FA-GRAMMAR-FEKR-MIKONAM-KE-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "in my opinion", then "I think that". (*Be nazar-e man*,
*fekr mikonam ke*.)

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[FA-LEX-BE-NAZAR-E-MAN-01, FA-GRAMMAR-FEKR-MIKONAM-KE-01, FA-GRAMMAR-BARAYE-HAMIN-01, FA-GRAMMAR-PAS-SO-01, FA-LEX-C92-WHY92-03, FA-LEX-C146-KONESH146-04] -->

Two lessons, and an opinion now has both ends. It opens with *be nazar-e
man* or *fekr mikonam ke*, or carefully with *be nazar miresad ke*. Its
cause comes with *chon*. Its result comes with *barâ-ye hamin*, or with *pas*
when you are drawing a conclusion.

[PAUSE 2s] Think back to the winter trip in chapter two hundred and
ninety-one, and give your view of it:

> **به نظر من سفر خوب بود، چون هتل گرم بود.** — *be nazar-e man safar khub
> bud, chon hotel garm bud* — In my opinion the trip was good, because the
> hotel was warm.
