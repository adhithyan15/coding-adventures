Sanskrit, chapter 185: How much?, Of what kind?, For, Surely, Certainly.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
कियत् (kiyat) — how much?

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for loudly, then the Sanskrit for softly.

You'll want to know: कियत्.
कियत् — kiyat — "how much?".

The word, taken apart.
kiyat is built on ki-, the stem that asks a question (MW), the way iyat means "so much". That interrogative stem is inherited from Indo-European, and Latin quis, "who", is usually traced to the same source.

Lesson 2 of 5.
कीदृशः (kīdṛśaḥ) — of what kind?

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for softly, then the Sanskrit for how much?.

You'll want to know: कीदृशः.
कीदृशः — kīdṛśaḥ — "of what kind?".

The word, taken apart.
kīdṛśa is built on the question stem kī- and -dṛśa, "looking like", from the root √dṛś, "to see" (MW): "of what appearance". It lives on as Hindi kaisā, with ka- in place of kī-, and Gujarati śũ, "what" (CDIAL 3197).

Lesson 3 of 5.
हि (hi) — for, indeed.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for how much?, then the Sanskrit for of what kind?.

You'll want to know: हि.
हि — hi — "for, indeed".

The word, taken apart.
hi is an old particle, already in the Vedas, that never stands first in its sentence (MW). MW compares it with the particles ha and gha; where it came from before that is not settled.

Lesson 4 of 5.
नूनम् (nūnam) — surely.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for of what kind?, then the Sanskrit for for.

You'll want to know: नूनम्.
नूनम् — nūnam — "surely".

The word, taken apart.
nūnam is built on nu, "now" (CDIAL 7576): in the Rigveda it means "now", and later "surely" (MW). The base is inherited from Indo-European; MW compares Greek nyn, Latin nunc and English now.

Lesson 5 of 5.
अवश्यम् (avaśyam) — certainly, necessarily.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for for, then the Sanskrit for surely.

You'll want to know: अवश्यम्.
अवश्यम् — avaśyam — "certainly, necessarily".

The word, taken apart.
avaśyam is built on a-, "not", and vaśa, "will, control" (MW): what is not under anyone's control is inevitable, so "necessarily, certainly". MW sets it beside āvaśyaka, "necessary".
