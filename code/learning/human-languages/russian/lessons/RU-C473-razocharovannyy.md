## You'll want to know: разочарованный
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C473-LETDOWN-02]; assesses=[] -->

**разочарованный** (*razocharóvannyy*) — "disappointed".

> Он вышел из кабинета разочарованный.
>
> *On výshel iz kabinéta razocharóvannyy.*
>
> He came out of the office disappointed.

Past participle of *разочаровать*, used as an adjective. Short form: *разочарован в ком* — disappointed in someone.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*разочарованный* is the past participle of *разочаровать*, built on *очаровать* 'to enchant' and *чары* 'spells', which EtymDB traces to Proto-Slavic *čarъ* 'magic'.
