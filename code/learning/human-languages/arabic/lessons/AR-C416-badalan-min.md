# بدلا من (badalan min) — instead of

## You'll want to know: بدلا من
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C416-BADAL416-02]; assesses=[] -->

**بدلا من** — *badalan min* — "instead of".

> شربت الشاي بدلا من القهوة.
>
> *sharibtu ash-shāy badalan min al-qahwa.*
>
> I drank tea instead of coffee.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**بدلا من** (*badalan min*) joins *badalan*, "as a substitute", built on the native Arabic root *b-d-l*, "to change, to put in place of" (Lane), with *min*, "from": instead of.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
