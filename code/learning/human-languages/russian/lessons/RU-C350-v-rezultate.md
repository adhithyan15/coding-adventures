## You'll want to know: в результате
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C350-POSLEDSTVIYA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**в результате** (*v rezultáte*) — "as a result".

> В результате мы опоздали на поезд.
>
> *V rezultáte my opozdáli na póyezd.*
>
> As a result, we missed the train.

With a noun it takes the genitive: *в результате аварии*. On its own it opens a sentence.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**в результате** is *в* "in" with the prepositional of *результат* "a result", which is usually traced, through western languages, to Medieval Latin *resultatum*, built on *resultāre* "to spring back" (Lewis & Short).
