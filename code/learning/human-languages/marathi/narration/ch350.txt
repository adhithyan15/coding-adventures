Marathi, chapter 350: Accepting and Blaming.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
स्वीकारणे (svīkārṇe) — to accept.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for an alternative, then the Marathi for to unravel.

You'll want to know: स्वीकारणे.
स्वीकारणे — svīkārṇe — "to accept".
त्याने नवीन नोकरी आनंदाने स्वीकारली. tyāne navīn nokrī ānandāne svīkārlī. He gladly accepted the new job.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने स्वीकारलं, tyāne svīkārlaṁ.
Transitive: the past agrees with what is accepted, नोकरी स्वीकारली.

The word, taken apart.
स्वीकारणे (svīkārṇe) is built on the tatsama स्वीकार, Sanskrit svīkāra, "making one's own, accepting" (Monier-Williams), with the verb ending -णे.

Lesson 2 of 5.
मान्य करणे (mānya karṇe) — to acknowledge; to agree to.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to unravel, then the Marathi for to accept.

You'll want to know: मान्य करणे.
मान्य करणे — mānya karṇe — "to acknowledge; to agree to".
शेवटी त्याने आपली चूक मान्य केली. śevṭī tyāne āplī cūk mānya kelī. In the end he acknowledged his mistake.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने मान्य केलं, tyāne mānya kelaṁ.
मान्य असणे, to be acceptable: मला हे मान्य आहे.

The word, taken apart.
मान्य करणे (mānya karṇe) joins the tatsama मान्य, Sanskrit mānya, "to be respected or honoured" (Monier-Williams), to करणे, "to make".

Lesson 4 of 5.
दोष देणे (doṣ deṇe) — to blame.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to acknowledge, then the Marathi for to treat as valid.

You'll want to know: दोष देणे.
दोष देणे — doṣ deṇe — "to blame".
तो आपल्या अपयशासाठी नशिबाला दोष देतो. to āpalyā apayśāsāṭhī naśibālā doṣ deto. He blames fate for his failure.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने दोष दिला, tyāne doṣ dilā.
The one blamed takes -ला; the fault takes -साठी or -बद्दल.

The word, taken apart.
दोष देणे (doṣ deṇe) joins the tatsama दोष, Sanskrit doṣa, "fault, defect" (Monier-Williams), to देणे, "to give".

Lesson 5 of 5.
आरोप करणे (ārop karṇe) — to accuse; to allege.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to treat as valid, then the Marathi for to blame.

You'll want to know: आरोप करणे.
आरोप करणे — ārop karṇe — "to accuse; to allege".
शेजारी त्याच्यावर चोरीचा आरोप करत होता. śejārī tyācyāvar corīcā ārop karat hotā. The neighbour was accusing him of theft.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने आरोप केला, tyāne ārop kelā.
The accused takes -वर and the charge -चा: त्याच्यावर चोरीचा आरोप.

The word, taken apart.
आरोप करणे (ārop karṇe) joins the tatsama आरोप, Sanskrit āropa, "placing upon, imputing" (Monier-Williams), to करणे, "to do".
