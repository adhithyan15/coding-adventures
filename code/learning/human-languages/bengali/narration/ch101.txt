You'll want to know: রান্না.
রান্না — rānnā — "cooking".

The word, taken apart.
রান্না (rānnā) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from randhana, "cooking" (CDIAL 10615).

The word, taken apart.
ভরানো (bhôrāno) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from bharati, "bears, fills" (CDIAL 9397). Bhôrāno is the causing form of bharā, the verb CDIAL lists.

সেলাই (selāi) appears to be a noun built on the verb "to sew", which CDIAL lists as Bengali siyāna under Sanskrit sīvyati, "sews" (CDIAL 13444). CDIAL has no entry for সেলাই (selāi) itself, and its e is not explained there.
