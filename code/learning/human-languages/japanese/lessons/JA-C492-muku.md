## You'll want to know: むく
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C492-BOIL492-05]; assesses=[] -->

**むく** — *muku* — "to peel".

> **りんごの かわを むきます。** — *ringo no kawa o mukimasu* — "I peel the apple."

Polite **むきます**, *mukimasu*; a godan verb. Past (polite): **あねが みかんを むきました。**, *ane ga mikan o mukimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**むく** (*muku*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, and usually written in kana; its older origin is not settled (JMdict).
