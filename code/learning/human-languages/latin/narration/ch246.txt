Because of? (propter.) Rough? (asper, aspera, asperum.) Deep? (profundus, profunda, profundum.) Unknown? (ignōtus, ignōta, ignōtum.) Necessary? (necessārius, necessāria, necessārium.) Useless? (inūtilis, inūtile.) Industrious? (industrius, industria, industrium.) Talkative? (loquāx, loquācis.) Worried? (sollicitus, sollicita, sollicitum.) Unmarried? (caelebs, caelibis.) Rainy? (pluvius, pluvia, pluvium.)
