## You'll want to know: محصول
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C383-TOWLID-02]; assesses=[] -->

**محصول** (*mahsul*) — "a product; a crop".

> محصولات این شرکت خیلی معروف است.
>
> *mahsulât-e in sherkat kheyli ma'ruf ast.*
>
> This company's products are very well known.

Plural *mahsulât*. Also a harvest from the land.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**محصول** is usually traced to Arabic *maḥṣūl* "yield, what is obtained": a product or a crop is what work brings in.
