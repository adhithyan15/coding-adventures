# rellenar — fill the blanks with what they need

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-IMPRESO, ES-LEX-DATO, ES-LEX-C365-COUNT-25] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say *the form*, *the data* and *full*. (*El impreso, los datos,
lleno*.) The action that connects them is **rellenar**.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-RELLENAR]; assesses=[] -->

**Rellenar** shows its construction on the surface: **re-** + **llenar**, “to
fill.” Its broad sense is to fill something again or completely. In an office,
it has the practical meaning “to fill in” or “to fill out.”

The DLE gives the form sense precisely: putting the necessary data into blank
spaces in forms or documents. That is why the noun chain from this chapter ends
in an action:

> **rellenar el impreso con los datos**

## Grammar Lens: a regular -ar action
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-RELLENAR, ES-LEX-IMPRESO, ES-LEX-DATO] -->

You already know the regular **-ar** machine:

- **relleno el impreso** — I fill in the form
- **rellena el impreso** — he/she fills in the form; fill in the form

The noun **relleno** can also mean a filling, but the sentence and article tell
you what job the form is doing.
