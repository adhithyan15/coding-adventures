## You'll want to know: करज
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C195-BANK195-03]; assesses=[] -->

**करज** — *karaj* — "a debt".

> बाप माथै घणो करज है।
>
> *bāp māthai ghaṇo karaj hai.*
>
> Father has a large debt.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**करज** (*karaj*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *qarḍ*, "a loan".
