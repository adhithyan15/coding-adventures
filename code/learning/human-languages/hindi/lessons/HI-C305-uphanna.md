# उफनना (uphannā) — to swell up; to boil over

## You'll want to know: उफनना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C305-BAADH305-01]; assesses=[] -->

**उफनना** — *uphannā* — "to swell up; to boil over".

> भारी बारिश के बाद नदी उफन गई।
>
> *bhārī bāriś ke bād nadī uphan gaī.*
>
> After the heavy rain the river swelled over its banks.

Past (it …): वह नदी उफनी, *vah nadī uphnī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**उफनना** (*uphannā*) is inherited from *\*utphaṇati*, a form CDIAL reconstructs (CDIAL 1836). Milk on the fire **उफनता है**, and so does a river in flood.
