## You'll want to know: a bifurcação
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C437-RUAS-05]; assesses=[] -->

**a bifurcação** — "the fork (in the road), the split".

> Na bifurcação, a estrada da direita vai para a praia.
>
> *At the fork, the right-hand road goes to the beach.*

The verb is *bifurcar-se*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*bifurcação* is a learned formation on Latin *bifurcus* 'two-pronged', from *bi-* 'two' and *furca* 'fork' (Lewis & Short).
