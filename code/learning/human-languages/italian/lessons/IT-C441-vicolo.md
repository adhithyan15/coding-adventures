## You'll want to know: il vicolo
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C441-VICOLI-02]; assesses=[] -->

**il vicolo** — "the alley, the lane".

> Il ristorante è in un vicolo dietro la piazza.
>
> *The restaurant is in an alley behind the square.*

*Un vicolo cieco* — a dead end.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*vicolo* is inherited from Latin *vīculus* (EtymDB), a little *vīcus* 'village, row of houses, street'.
