# ప్రశంస (praśaṁsa) — acclaim; praise

## You'll want to know: ప్రశంస
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C504-ABHIMANAM504-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ప్రశంస** — *praśaṁsa* — "acclaim; praise".

> ఆ సినిమాకు చాలా ప్రశంసలు వచ్చాయి.
>
> *ā sinimāku cālā praśaṁsalu vaccāyi.*
>
> That film won a lot of acclaim.

Plural: ప్రశంసలు, *praśaṁsalu*.

Often plural: ప్రశంసలు అందుకో, to receive praise.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ప్రశంస** (*praśaṁsa*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *praśaṁsā*, "praise, commendation, fame" (Monier-Williams).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
