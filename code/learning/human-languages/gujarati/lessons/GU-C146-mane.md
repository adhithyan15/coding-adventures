# મને, તને, તેને — the pronouns things happen to

## Warm-up

[PAUSE 2s] Ask *are you coming or not?*, then *you live here, right?* And from
the verb chapters long ago: **મને ગુજરાતી ગમે છે**, *I like Gujarati*.

## Grammar Lens: the person something happens to
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-GRAMMAR-C146-DATIVE-SG-01]; assesses=[GU-GRAMMAR-DATIVE-LIKING, GU-LEX-C48-BODY3-04] -->

In **મને ગુજરાતી ગમે છે** the thing liked is the subject, and *I* stand to one
side as **મને**, *to me*. Gujarati speaks of a fever the same way: it arrives
*to* someone.

> **મને તાવ છે.** — *mane tāv che.* — I have a fever.

The *to* forms for one person:

| who | pronoun | *to* form |
|---|---|---|
| I | હું | મને |
| you (familiar) | તું | તને |
| he, she | તે | તેને |

## Grammar Lens: more than one
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-GRAMMAR-C146-DATIVE-PL-01]; assesses=[GU-CONCEPT-C02-ANAND-01, GU-PRON-AME-01, GU-LEX-C145-NEG145-01] -->

**તમને** has been in the book since the second day, inside **તમને મળીને આનંદ
થયો**: *to you, meeting, joy happened*. Its partners:

| who | pronoun | *to* form |
|---|---|---|
| we | અમે | અમને |
| you (respectful) | તમે | તમને |
| they | તેઓ | તેમને |

**તેઓ** turns into **તેમ-** before the ending: **તેમને**, *to them*.
