Italian, chapter 329: La rivolta.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
la rivolta — the revolt, the uprising.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for to govern, then the Italian for to found.

You'll want to know: la rivolta.
la rivolta — "the revolt, the uprising".
La rivolta è cominciata in piazza. The uprising began in the square.
Built on rivoltare, ‘to turn over’.

The word, taken apart.
rivolta is built on rivoltare "to turn over" (EtymDB), from Latin volvere "to roll" (Lewis & Short).

ribellarsi is built on ribelle "rebel", from Latin rebellis, from rebellāre "to make war again", built on bellum "war" (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 4 of 5.
arrendersi — to surrender, to give up.

Warm-up.

Before the new one: say the Italian for to rebel, then the Italian for to invade.

You'll want to know: arrendersi.
arrendersi — "to surrender, to give up".
Dopo un mese la città si è arresa. After a month the city surrendered.
Past participle arreso. Non arrenderti! — do not give up!

The word, taken apart.
arrendersi is a- with rendere "to give back", from Vulgar Latin rendere, a form of Latin reddere "to give back" (Lewis & Short).
