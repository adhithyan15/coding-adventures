## You'll want to know: la rapina
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C292-RAPINA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**la rapina** — "the robbery, the hold-up".

> C'è stata una rapina in banca stamattina.
>
> *There was a bank robbery this morning.*

With force or threats; *a mano armata* means armed.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*rapina* is inherited from Latin *rapīna* "robbery, plunder" (EtymDB), from *rapere* "to seize".
