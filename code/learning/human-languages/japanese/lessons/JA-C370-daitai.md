## You'll want to know: だいたい
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C370-MATOMARU370-04]; assesses=[] -->

**だいたい** — *daitai* — "roughly; mostly".

> **だいたい わかったら、はじめて ください。** — *daitai wakattara, hajimete kudasai* — "Once you more or less understand, please begin."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**だいたい** (*daitai*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *dai*, "large" (Middle Chinese *dajH*) and *tai*, "body" (*thejX*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
