## You'll want to know: લાળ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C474-CAVVU474-01]; assesses=[] -->

**લાળ** — *lāḷ* — "saliva; drool".

> ઊંઘમાં બાળકના મોઢામાંથી લાળ પડે છે.
>
> *ū̃ghmā̃ bāḷaknā moḍhāmā̃thī lāḷ paḍe che.*
>
> The baby drools in its sleep.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**લાળ** (*lāḷ*) is inherited from Sanskrit *lālā*, "spittle, saliva"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati word (CDIAL 11027).
