## You'll want to know: l'aumento
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C361-CARRIERA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**l'aumento** — "the rise, the increase; the pay rise".

> Ho chiesto un aumento al mio capo.
>
> *I asked my boss for a pay rise.*

*In aumento* — rising: *i prezzi sono in aumento*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*aumento* is a learned borrowing from Latin *augmentum* "an increase, growth" (EtymDB), built on *augēre* "to increase".
