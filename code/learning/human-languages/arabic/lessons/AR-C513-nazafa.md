## You'll want to know: نزف
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C513-MUZMIN513-05]; assesses=[] -->

**نزف** — *nazafa* — "to bleed".

> نزف أنف اللاعب بعد الضربة.
>
> *nazafa anf al-lāʿib baʿda aḍ-ḍarba.*
>
> The player's nose bled after the blow.

Present: ينزف, *yanzif*.

The noun نزيف, bleeding, shares the root.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**نزف** (*nazafa*) is built on the native Arabic root *n-z-f*, "to drain, to exhaust" (Lane). Lane gives nazafa as to drain a well dry; bleeding drains the blood.
