## You'll want to know: પડીકું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C430-SCALES430-03]; assesses=[] -->

**પડીકું** — *paḍīkũ* — "a small packet (wrapped in paper)".

> દુકાનદારે ચાનું પડીકું વાળીને આપ્યું.
>
> *dukāndāre cānũ paḍīkũ vāḷīne āpyũ.*
>
> The shopkeeper folded up a packet of tea and handed it over.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**પડીકું** (*paḍīkũ*) seems to belong with Sanskrit *puṭa*, "a fold, a pocket", under which CDIAL lists a Gujarati word for a packet (CDIAL 8253).
