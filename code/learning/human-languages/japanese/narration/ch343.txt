Japanese, chapter 343: Trust and Doubt.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
しんようする (shin'yō suru) — to trust; to believe in.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for the reason is, then the Japanese for an attitude.

You'll want to know: しんようする.
しんようする — shin'yō suru — "to trust; to believe in".
うそを 言ったので、かれを しんようしません。 — uso o itta node, kare o shin'yō shimasen — "He lied, so I don't trust him."
Polite しんようします, shin'yōshimasu; a する verb. Past (polite): みんなが かれを しんようしました。, minna ga kare o shin'yō shimashita.

The word, taken apart.
しんようする (shin'yō suru) is built from a Sino-Japanese noun and the native verb suru, "to do". The noun, shin'yō, is two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese: shin, "trust" (Middle Chinese sinH) and yō, "utilize" (yowngH) (KANJIDIC2; JMdict; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).

Lesson 3 of 5.
かばう (kabau) — to stand up for (someone).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for to trust, then the Japanese for confidence.

You'll want to know: かばう.
かばう — kabau — "to stand up for (someone)".
ちちが おこったので、あには おとうとを かばいました。 — chichi ga okotta node, ani wa otōto o kabaimashita — "My father got angry, so my older brother stood up for my younger brother."
Polite かばいます, kabaimasu; a godan verb. Past (polite): あねが いもうとを かばいました。, ane ga imōto o kabaimashita.

The word, taken apart.
かばう (kabau) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, and its older origin is not settled. JMdict notes that it is usually written in kana (JMdict).

Lesson 5 of 5.
きめつける (kimetsukeru) — to jump to conclusions; to decide one-sidedly.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for to stand up for, then the Japanese for to be found out.

You'll want to know: きめつける.
きめつける — kimetsukeru — "to jump to conclusions; to decide one-sidedly".
まだ わからないから、きめつけないで ください。 — mada wakaranai kara, kimetsukenaide kudasai — "We don't know yet, so please do not jump to conclusions."
Polite きめつけます, kimetsukemasu; an ichidan verb. Past (polite): ちちが きめつけました。, chichi ga kimetsukemashita.

The word, taken apart.
きめつける (kimetsukeru) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese: it is built from kime, "deciding (kimeru)" and tsukeru, "to attach", and its kanji were attached later as kun'yomi (JMdict).
