## You'll want to know: o tricô
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C500-COSTURA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**o tricô** — "knitting".

> O tricô acalma-me depois do trabalho.
>
> *Knitting calms me down after work.*

*Fazer tricô* — to knit. In Portugal *fazer malha* is also common.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*tricô* was borrowed from French *tricot* "knitting" (EtymDB), from *tricoter*.
