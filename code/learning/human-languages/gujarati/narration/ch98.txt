Lesson 2 of 5.
જેઠ (jeṭh) — Jeth, a Gujarati month.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for Chaitra, then the Gujarati for Vaishakh.

You'll want to know: જેઠ.
જેઠ (jeṭh) — "Jeth, a Gujarati month".

The word, taken apart.
જેઠ (jeṭh) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from jyaiṣṭha, "the month May-June" (CDIAL 5293). It is named after jyeṣṭhā, a lunar mansion.

You'll want to know: આસો.
આસો (āso) — "Aso, the month of Diwali".

The word, taken apart.
આસો (āso) is a tadbhava of Sanskrit aśvayuja, "the month Āśvina, September-October" (CDIAL 925). CDIAL compares aśvayuj, "harnessing horses", also the name of a constellation.
