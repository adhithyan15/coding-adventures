## You'll want to know: تعادل
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C546-MUBARA546-03]; assesses=[] -->

**تعادل** — *taʿādala* — "to finish level; to end in a draw".

> تعادل الفريقان في المباراة.
>
> *taʿādala al-farīqān fī al-mubārā.*
>
> The two teams drew the match.

Present: يتعادل, *yataʿādal*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**تعادل** (*taʿādala*) is built on the native Arabic root *ʿ-d-l*, "to be equal, to be fair" (Lane). Form VI taʿādala is to be equal to one another.
