Gujarati, chapter 289: In Court.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
અદાલત (adālat) — a court (of law).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a criminal, then the Gujarati for a jail.

You'll want to know: અદાલત.
અદાલત — adālat — "a court (of law)".
આજે સવારે પોલીસ ગુનેગારને અદાલતમાં લઈ ગઈ. āje savāre polīs gunegārne adālatmā̃ laī gaī. This morning the police took the criminal to court.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
અદાલત (adālat) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic ʿadāla, "justice, fairness": the place where justice is done. It is feminine: અદાલતમાં, in court.

Lesson 3 of 5.
ન્યાય (nyāy) — justice.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a court, then the Gujarati for a lawyer.

You'll want to know: ન્યાય.
ન્યાય — nyāy — "justice".
અદાલતે ગરીબ માણસને ન્યાય આપ્યો. adālte garīb māṇasne nyāy āpyo. The court gave the poor man justice.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ન્યાય (nyāy) is a tatsama, Sanskrit nyāya, "rule, manner, method" (CDIAL 7614); in Gujarati it is above all what is fair and right, the thing a court gives.

Lesson 4 of 5.
સજા (sajā) — a punishment.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a lawyer, then the Gujarati for justice.

You'll want to know: સજા.
સજા — sajā — "a punishment".
જો અદાલત તેને ગુનેગાર માનશે તો તેને સજા થશે. jo adālat tene gunegār mānśe to tene sajā thaśe. If the court finds him guilty, he will be punished.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
સજા (sajā) is usually traced to Persian sazā, "what is fitting, what one deserves". It is feminine; a person સજા થવી, gets punished, and a court સજા આપે છે, passes sentence.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ભોગવવું (bhogavvũ) — to undergo; to serve (a sentence).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for justice, then the Gujarati for a punishment.

You'll want to know: ભોગવવું.
ભોગવવું — bhogavvũ — "to undergo; to serve (a sentence)".
ગુનેગારે બે વરસની જેલ ભોગવી. gunegāre be varasnī jel bhogvī. The criminal served two years in jail.
Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે સજા ભોગવી, teṇe sajā bhogvī.

The word, taken apart.
ભોગવવું (bhogavvũ) is built on Sanskrit bhoga, "enjoyment, use" (Monier-Williams): to have something to the full. In Gujarati it is also used of what one has to go through, good or bad: a prisoner સજા (sajā) ભોગવે છે, serves his sentence.
