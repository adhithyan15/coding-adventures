## You'll want to know: die Taufe
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C296-GEBURT-05]; assesses=[] -->

**die Taufe** — "the christening, the baptism".

> Nach der Taufe gab es Kuchen für alle.
>
> *After the christening there was cake for everyone.*

Plural: *die Taufen*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Taufe* is built on *taufen* "to baptise", from Proto-Germanic *daupijaną* "to dip" (EtymDB).
