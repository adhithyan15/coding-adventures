## You'll want to know: gemellus, gemella, gemellum
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C528-GENS-05]; assesses=[] -->

**gemellus, gemella, gemellum** — "twin".

> Frātrēs gemellī simillimī sunt.
>
> *The twin brothers are very alike.*

A diminutive of *geminus*; as a noun, *gemellus* — a twin.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**gemellus** is a diminutive of *geminus* "twin" (Lewis & Short; EtymDB).
