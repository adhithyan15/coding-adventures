## You'll want to know: しじする
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C455-TEAM455-02]; assesses=[] -->

**しじする** — *shiji suru* — "to give instructions".

> **かちょうは ぶかに しじしました。** — *kachō wa buka ni shiji shimashita* — "The section chief gave instructions to his staff."

Polite **しじします**, *shijishimasu*; a する verb. Past (polite): **ぶちょうが しゃいんに しじしました。**, *buchō ga shain ni shiji shimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**しじする** (*shiji suru*) is Sino-Japanese (kango) *shiji* with the native verb *suru*, "to do": two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *shi*, "point to" (Middle Chinese *tsyijX*) and *ji*, "show" (*zyijH*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
