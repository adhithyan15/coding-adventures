## You'll want to know: पटणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C387-GANANA387-05]; assesses=[] -->

**पटणे** — *paṭṇe* — "to be convinced; to make sense (to)".

> तुमचं म्हणणं मला पटलं.
>
> *tumcaṁ mhaṇṇaṁ malā paṭlaṁ.*
>
> What you said convinced me.

Past (to him …): त्याला पटलं, *tyālā paṭlaṁ*.

Dative: मला पटलं, I am convinced; पटवणे, to convince, is its causative.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पटणे** (*paṭṇe*) is a Marathi verb whose origin is not settled; पटवणे, to convince, is built on it.
