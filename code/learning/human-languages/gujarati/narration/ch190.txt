Gujarati, chapter 190: At the bank counter.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ખાતું (khātũ) — bank account, account.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to be left over, then the Gujarati for to accept.

You'll want to know: ખાતું.
ખાતું — khātũ — "bank account, account".
મારું બેંકમાં ખાતું છે. — mārũ bẽkmā̃ khātũ che — "I have an account at the bank."
A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
ખાતું (khātũ) is a tadbhava. CDIAL lists it under Sanskrit kṣatrá, "might, rule", with the Gujarati senses "administrative department, account, account book" (CDIAL 3648).

Lesson 2 of 5.
જમા કરવું (jamā karvũ) — to deposit.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to accept, then the Gujarati for bank account.

You'll want to know: જમા કરવું.
જમા કરવું — jamā karvũ — "to deposit".
હું બેંકમાં પૈસા જમા કરું છું. — hũ bẽkmā̃ paisā jamā karũ chũ — "I deposit money in the bank."

The word, taken apart.
જમા કરવું (jamā karvũ) is built from jamā, "deposited, credited", a loan from Arabic jamʿ, "gathering, sum", that reached Gujarati through Persian, and the light verb karvũ, "to do". Its Arabic root is j-m-ʿ, "to gather" (Lane).

Lesson 4 of 5.
વટાવવું (vaṭāvvũ) — to cash a cheque, to exchange.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to deposit, then the Gujarati for cheque.

You'll want to know: વટાવવું.
વટાવવું — vaṭāvvũ — "to cash a cheque, to exchange".
તે બેંકમાં ચેક વટાવે છે. — te bẽkmā̃ cek vaṭāve che — "She cashes a cheque at the bank."

The word, taken apart.
વટાવવું (vaṭāvvũ) is a tadbhava. It comes from Sanskrit vartáyati, "causes to turn", and CDIAL glosses the Gujarati verb "to exchange, cash" (CDIAL 11356).

Lesson 5 of 5.
ઉધાર લેવું (udhār levũ) — to borrow.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for cheque, then the Gujarati for to cash a cheque.

You'll want to know: ઉધાર લેવું.
ઉધાર લેવું — udhār levũ — "to borrow".
તે બેંકમાંથી પૈસા ઉધાર લે છે. — te bẽkmā̃thī paisā udhār le che — "He borrows money from the bank."
Past તેણે ઉધાર લીધું, teṇe udhār līdhũ.

The word, taken apart.
ઉધાર લેવું (udhār levũ) is built from udhār, "credit, loan", and levũ, "to take". udhār is a tadbhava from Sanskrit uddhārá, "raising; a loan", and CDIAL lists Gujarati udhāro, "credit account" (CDIAL 2018).
