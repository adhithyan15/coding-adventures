## You'll want to know: luchar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C569-LUCHA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**luchar** — "to fight, to struggle".

> Lucharon por sus derechos.
>
> *They fought for their rights.*

*Luchar por* — to fight for. *Luchar contra* — to fight against.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*luchar* is inherited from Latin *luctārī* 'to wrestle', through spoken Latin *\*luctāre* (EtymDB).
