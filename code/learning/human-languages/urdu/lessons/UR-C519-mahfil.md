## You'll want to know: محفل
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C519-DHOL519-04]; assesses=[] -->

**محفل** — *mahfil* — "a gathering (for music or poetry)".

> کل رات دادا کے گھر محفل تھی۔
>
> *kal rāt dādā ke ghar mahfil thī.*
>
> Last night there was a musical evening at grandfather's house.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**محفل** (*mahfil*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *maḥfil*, "a place where people gather".
