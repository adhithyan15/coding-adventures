## You'll want to know: बधिरः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C481-ANDHA481-02]; assesses=[] -->

**बधिरः** — *badhiraḥ* — "deaf".

> मम बधिरः पितामहः उच्चैः वदति।
>
> *mama badhiraḥ pitāmahaḥ uccaiḥ vadati.*
>
> My deaf grandfather talks loudly.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**बधिरः** (*badhiraḥ*) MW gives "deaf" (sometimes written *vadhira*); MW gives no derivation, so its origin is uncertain (MW).
