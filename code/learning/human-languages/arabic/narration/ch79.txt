Arabic, chapter 79: Cow, Goat, Sheep, Mouse, Lion.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
بقرة (baqara) — a cow.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for the sky, then the Arabic for a horse.

You'll want to know: بقرة.
بقرة — baqara — "a cow".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: baqara]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: baqara, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say baqara]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Arabic for the sky, then the Arabic for a horse, then say baqara again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does بقرة (baqara) mean? ("A cow".) Say it once more.
