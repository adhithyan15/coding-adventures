Hindi, chapter 473: Talkers and Quiet Ones.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
बातूनी (bātūnī) — talkative.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for dedicated, then the Hindi for alert.

You'll want to know: बातूनी.
बातूनी — bātūnī — "talkative".
मेरी मौसी बहुत बातूनी हैं, घंटों बात करती हैं। merī mausī bahut bātūnī haiṁ, ghaṇṭoṁ bāt kartī haiṁ. My aunt is very talkative; she talks for hours.

The word, taken apart.
बातूनी (bātūnī) is bāt, "talk", with the ending -ūnī; CDIAL lists Hindi bāt, "talk, matter, thing", under Sanskrit vārtta (CDIAL 11564).

Lesson 5 of 5.
मौन (maun) — silence.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for idle chat, then the Hindi for to grumble.

You'll want to know: मौन.
मौन — maun — "silence".
स्कूल में बच्चों ने दो मिनट का मौन रखा। skūl meṁ baccoṁ ne do minaṭ kā maun rakhā. At school the children kept two minutes' silence.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
मौन (maun) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit mauna, "silence, taciturnity", from muni, "a silent sage" (Monier-Williams). It is the formal, chosen silence; cup is the everyday kind.
