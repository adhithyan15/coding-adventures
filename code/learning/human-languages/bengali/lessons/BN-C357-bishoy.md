## You'll want to know: বিষয়
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C357-PROBONDHO357-02]; assesses=[] -->

**বিষয়** — *bishôy* — "a subject; a topic".

> তোমার প্রবন্ধের বিষয় কী?
>
> *tomār prôbôndher bishôy kī?*
>
> What is your essay about?

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**বিষয়** (*bishôy*) is Sanskrit *viṣaya*, a sphere or an object of attention (Monier-Williams). In Bengali it is the subject of a talk, a book or a school course.
