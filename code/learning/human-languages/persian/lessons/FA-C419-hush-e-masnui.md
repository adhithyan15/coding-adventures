## You'll want to know: هوش مصنوعی
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C419-HARIM-05]; assesses=[] -->

**هوش مصنوعی** (*hush-e masnu'i*) — "artificial intelligence".

> هوش مصنوعی کار خیلی از مردم را عوض میکند.
>
> *hush-e masnu'i kâr-e kheyli az mardom râ 'avaz mikonad.*
>
> Artificial intelligence is changing many people's work.

*Hush* "intelligence" + ezafe + *masnu'i* "artificial".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**هوش مصنوعی** joins *hush* "intelligence", which EtymDB traces to a Middle Persian word for consciousness, to *masnu'i* "artificial", built on Arabic *maṣnū'* "made".
