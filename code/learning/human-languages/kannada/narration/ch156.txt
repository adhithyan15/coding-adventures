Kannada, chapter 156: Next, From Now On, the Future, To Decide, To Expect.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಮುಂದಿನ (mundina) — next, coming.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for once, then the Kannada for to spend.

You'll want to know: ಮುಂದಿನ.
ಮುಂದಿನ — mundina — "next, coming". The future for "I" ends in -ಉತ್ತೇನೆ (-uttēne): ಮುಂದಿನ ವಾರ ನಾನು ಬೆಂಗಳೂರಿಗೆ ಹೋಗುತ್ತೇನೆ — mundina vāra nānu beṅgaḷūrige hōguttēne — "Next week I will go to Bengaluru."
