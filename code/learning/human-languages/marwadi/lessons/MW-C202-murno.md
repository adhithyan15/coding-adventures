## You'll want to know: मुड़णो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C202-WAY202-01]; assesses=[] -->

**मुड़णो** — *muṛṇo* — "to turn".

> आप मोड़ माथै मुड़ो।
>
> *āp moṛ māthai muṛo.*
>
> Turn at the bend.

Past (he …): वो मुड़्यो, *vo muṛyo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**मुड़णो** (*muṛṇo*) is inherited from Sanskrit *muṭati*, "twists". CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry, but lists Hindi *muṛnā*, "to be turned" (CDIAL 10186). **मोड़**, "a bend", belongs to the same family.
