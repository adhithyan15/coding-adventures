## You'll want to know: हजेरी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C321-PANGAT321-05]; assesses=[] -->

**हजेरी** — *hajerī* — "attendance; presence".

> गुरुजींनी वर्गात हजेरी घेतली.
>
> *gurujīnnī vargāt hajerī ghetlī.*
>
> The teacher took attendance in class.

Feminine.

हजेरी घेणे, to take attendance; हजेरी लावणे, to put in an appearance.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**हजेरी** (*hajerī*) is a loan from Persian *hāzirī*, 'attendance', usually traced to Arabic *ḥāḍir*, 'present'.
