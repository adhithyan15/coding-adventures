## You'll want to know: रुसणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C454-RUSVA454-03]; assesses=[] -->

**रुसणे** — *rusṇe* — "to sulk".

> खेळणं मिळालं नाही म्हणून बाळ रुसलं.
>
> *kheḷṇaṁ miḷālaṁ nāhī mhaṇūn bāḷ ruslaṁ.*
>
> The baby sulked because it did not get the toy.

Past (he …): तो रुसला, *to ruslā*.

The noun रुसवा, a sulk; रुसवा काढणे, to coax someone out of a sulk.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**रुसणे** (*rusṇe*) is a tadbhava, inherited from Sanskrit *ruṣyati*, "is angry"; CDIAL lists Marathi *rusṇẽ*, "to be offended" (CDIAL 10794).
