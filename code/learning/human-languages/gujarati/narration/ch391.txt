5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ગડબડ (gaḍbaḍ) — a mix-up.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to grow plants, then the Gujarati for to be produced.

You'll want to know: ગડબડ.
ગડબડ — gaḍbaḍ — "a mix-up".
જો ટિકિટની તારીખમાં ગડબડ હશે તો મુસાફરી રદ થશે. jo ṭikiṭnī tārīkhmā̃ gaḍbaḍ haśe to musāpharī rad thaśe. If there is a mix-up over the date on the ticket, the trip will be cancelled.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ગડબડ (gaḍbaḍ) CDIAL lists the Gujarati word, "confused; confusion, disorder", under a reconstructed gaḍa-baḍa (CDIAL 3974), a jingle of sounds for a muddle.

Warm-up.

Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to be produced, then the Gujarati for a mix-up.

You'll want to know: અવ્યવસ્થા.
અવ્યવસ્થા — avyavasthā — "disorder".
જો સ્ટેશન પર ભીડ વધશે તો અવ્યવસ્થા ફેલાશે. jo sṭeśan par bhīḍ vadhśe to avyavasthā phelāśe. If the crowd at the station grows, disorder will spread.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
અવ્યવસ્થા (avyavasthā) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit avyavasthā, "irregularity, want of order" (Monier-Williams).

You'll want to know: વેરાવું.
વેરાવું — verāvũ — "to get scattered".
જો તમે ડબ્બો ઉતાવળમાં ખોલશો તો બધી ખાંડ વેરાઈ જશે. jo tame ḍabbo utāvaḷmā̃ kholśo to badhī khā̃ḍ verāī jaśe. If you open the tin in a hurry, the sugar will spill everywhere.
Past (it …): તે ચોખા વેરાઈ ગયા, te cokhā verāī gayā.

The word, taken apart.
વેરાવું (verāvũ) is the -āvũ partner of vervũ, "to scatter"; CDIAL lists that verb under a reconstructed vikērayati, "scatters" (CDIAL 11636).

The word, taken apart.
ભૂંસાવું (bhū̃sāvũ) is the -āvũ partner of ભૂંસવું (bhū̃svũ), "to rub out", whose own history is not settled.
