Lesson 1 of 5.
దురద (durada) — an itch.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to be born, then the Telugu for to confront.

You'll want to know: దురద.
దురద — durada — "an itch".

The word, taken apart.
దురద (durada) is native Dravidian. DEDR 2865 lists durada, "itching", with Kannada tuṟi and Tamil coṟi, "itching".

Lesson 5 of 5.
నుదురు (nuduru) — the forehead.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a test, then the Telugu for medical treatment.

You'll want to know: నుదురు.
నుదురు — nuduru — "the forehead".

The word, taken apart.
నుదురు (nuduru) is native Dravidian, one word with Tamil nutal and Kannada nosalu, "forehead" (DEDR 3705). DEDR compares Sanskrit niṭāla, "forehead", which CDIAL 10970 takes as a later borrowing from Dravidian.
