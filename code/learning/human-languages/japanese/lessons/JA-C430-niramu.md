## You'll want to know: にらむ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C430-ANGER430-03]; assesses=[] -->

**にらむ** — *niramu* — "to glare at".

> **ねこが いぬを にらんでいます。** — *neko ga inu o nirande imasu* — "The cat is glaring at the dog."

Polite **にらみます**, *niramimasu*; a godan verb. Past (polite): **あにが おとうとを にらみました。**, *ani ga otōto o niramimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**にらむ** (*niramu*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji spelling was attached to it later (JMdict).
