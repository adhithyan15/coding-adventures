Marwadi (Marwari), chapter 191: A Notice on the Door.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
सूचना (sūchnā) — a notice.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for brave, then the Marwadi for crazy.

You'll want to know: सूचना.
सूचना — sūchnā — "a notice".
दुकान माथै नुवी सूचना है। dukān māthai nuvī sūchnā hai. There is a new notice on the shop.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
सूचना (sūchnā) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit sūcanā, "indication, communication" (Monier-Williams).

What you've built.
One more word for reading notices and messages.

Lesson 2 of 5.
नियम (niyam) — a rule.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for crazy, then the Marwadi for a notice.

You'll want to know: नियम.
नियम — niyam — "a rule".
ये नियम घणो जरूरी है। ye niyam ghaṇo jarūrī hai. This rule matters a great deal.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
नियम (niyam) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit niyama, "restraining, holding back" (Monier-Williams): a rule holds back what it does not allow.

Lesson 3 of 5.

[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: मना.
मना — manā — "forbidden".
अठै खाणो मना है। aṭhai khāṇo manā hai. No eating here.

The word, taken apart.
मना (manā) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic manʿ, "prevention, holding back".

Lesson 5 of 5.
मानणो (mānṇo) — to obey; to follow (a rule).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for forbidden, then the Marwadi for careful.

You'll want to know: मानणो.
मानणो — mānṇo — "to obey; to follow (a rule)".
हूं हरेक नियम मानूं हूं। hū̃ harek niyam mānū̃ hū̃. I follow every rule.
Past (I …): म्हैं नियम मान्यो, mhaĩ niyam mānyo.

The word, taken apart.
मानणो (mānṇo) is inherited from Sanskrit manyate, "thinks, honours"; CDIAL lists the Old Marwari verb mānaï, "to respect, obey" (CDIAL 9857).
