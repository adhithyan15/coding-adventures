## You'll want to know: गटर
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C425-HALLA425-03]; assesses=[] -->

**गटर** — *gaṭar* — "a sewer; a covered drain".

> बारिश में गटर भर गया।
>
> *bāriś meṁ gaṭar bhar gayā.*
>
> The sewer filled up in the rain.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**गटर** (*gaṭar*) is borrowed from English *gutter*; in Indian cities it names the covered sewer under the street.
