Hindi, chapter 303: When the Ground Shook.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
हिलना (hilnā) — to shake (by itself); to move.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for careful, then the Hindi for to be averted.

You'll want to know: हिलना.
हिलना — hilnā — "to shake (by itself); to move".
रात को अचानक पूरा घर हिला। rāt ko acānak pūrā ghar hilā. Suddenly, in the night, the whole house shook.
Past (it …): वह पेड़ हिला, vah peṛ hilā.

The word, taken apart.
हिलना (hilnā) is inherited from hillati, "shakes", a form CDIAL reconstructs (CDIAL 14120). It is the intransitive partner of हिलाना: you हिलाते हैं a branch, and the branch हिलती है.

भूकंप में सड़क नीचे धँस गई। bhūkamp meṁ saṛak nīce dhãs gaī. In the earthquake the road caved in.
Past (it …): वह सड़क धँस गई, vah saṛak dhãs gaī.

The word, taken apart.
धँसना (dhãsnā) goes back to Sanskrit dhvaṁsati, "falls to pieces" (CDIAL 6896). Ground, a road or a foot in mud धँसता है: it goes down into what is under it.
