## You'll want to know: la serie
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C528-SERIE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**la serie** — "series".

> Stiamo guardando una serie poliziesca.
>
> *We are watching a detective series.*

The same form in the plural: *le serie*. *Una serie TV*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*serie* is a learned borrowing from Latin *seriēs* "a row, a chain" (Lewis & Short), from *serere* "to join".
