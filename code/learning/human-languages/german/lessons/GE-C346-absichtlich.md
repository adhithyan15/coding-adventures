## You'll want to know: absichtlich
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C346-ZWECK-04]; assesses=[] -->

**absichtlich** — "on purpose, deliberate".

> Er hat das Glas nicht absichtlich fallen lassen.
>
> *He did not drop the glass on purpose.*

The opposite is *aus Versehen* — by mistake.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*absichtlich* is built with *-lich* on *Absicht* "intention", from *ab* and *Sicht* "sight", of the family of *sehen* "to see" (EtymDB).
