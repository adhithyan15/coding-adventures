Telugu, chapter 343: A Narrow Escape.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
క్షతగాత్రులు (kṣatagātrulu) — the injured.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a speech, then the Telugu for to give a speech.

You'll want to know: క్షతగాత్రులు.
క్షతగాత్రులు — kṣatagātrulu — "the injured".
క్షతగాత్రులను ఆసుపత్రికి తీసుకెళ్ళారు. kṣatagātrulanu āsupatriki tīsukeḷḷāru. The injured were taken to hospital.
A news word, nearly only in the plural: క్షత, wounded, and గాత్ర, the body. At the hospital the క్షతగాత్రులు (kṣatagātrulu) are given ఔషధం.

The word, taken apart.
క్షతగాత్రులు (kṣatagātrulu) is borrowed from Sanskrit: kṣata, "wounded" (CDIAL 3645), and gātra, "a limb, the body" (CDIAL 4124): those wounded in body.

Lesson 2 of 5.
దుర్ఘటన (durghaṭana) — a tragic accident; a disaster.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to give a speech, then the Telugu for the injured.

You'll want to know: దుర్ఘటన.
దుర్ఘటన — durghaṭana — "a tragic accident; a disaster".
ఆ దుర్ఘటన నిన్న సాయంత్రం జరిగింది. ā durghaṭana ninna sāyantraṁ jarigindi. That tragic accident happened yesterday evening.
దుర్-, ill, with ఘటన, a happening: compare సంఘటన.

The word, taken apart.
దుర్ఘటన (durghaṭana) is built from Sanskrit dur-, "ill, bad", and ghaṭana, "a joining, a coming together" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 3 of 5.
తొక్కిసలాట (tokkisalāṭa) — a stampede.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for the injured, then the Telugu for a tragic accident.

You'll want to know: తొక్కిసలాట.
తొక్కిసలాట — tokkisalāṭa — "a stampede".
జాతరలో తొక్కిసలాట జరిగింది. jātaralō tokkisalāṭa jarigindi. There was a stampede at the fair.
From తొక్కు, to tread on: people treading on one another.

The word, taken apart.
తొక్కిసలాట (tokkisalāṭa) is native Dravidian, built on tokku, trokku, "to tread, trample" (DEDR 3522).

Lesson 4 of 5.
త్రుటిలో (truṭilō) — narrowly; by a whisker.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a tragic accident, then the Telugu for a stampede.

You'll want to know: త్రుటిలో.
త్రుటిలో — truṭilō — "narrowly; by a whisker".
అతను త్రుటిలో ప్రమాదం నుంచి తప్పించుకున్నాడు. atanu truṭilō pramādaṁ nuñci tappiñcukunnāḍu. He narrowly escaped the accident.
త్రుటి, a tiny instant, with -లో, in: in a split second. Headlines often spell it తృటిలో.

The word, taken apart.
త్రుటిలో (truṭilō) is built on Sanskrit truṭi, "a small space of time" (CDIAL 6064), with the Telugu locative -lō, "in".

Lesson 5 of 5.
బయటపడు (bayaṭapaḍu) — to get out; to come to light.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a stampede, then the Telugu for narrowly.

You'll want to know: బయటపడు.
బయటపడు — bayaṭapaḍu — "to get out; to come to light".
మంటల నుంచి అందరూ క్షేమంగా బయటపడ్డారు. maṇṭala nuñci andarū kṣēmaṅgā bayaṭapaḍḍāru. Everyone got out of the fire safely.
Past (I …): బయటపడ్డాను, bayaṭapaḍḍānu.
బయట, outside, with పడు: to come out, or for a secret to come out.

The word, taken apart.
బయటపడు (bayaṭapaḍu) is a native compound: bayaṭa, "outside", which sits beside bayalu, "the outside, open space" (DEDR 3940), with the helping verb paḍu.
