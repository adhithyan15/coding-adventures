## You'll want to know: あじわう
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C173-FOOD173-05]; assesses=[] -->

**あじわう** — *ajiwau* — "to savour; to taste, to relish".

> **ゆっくり おちゃを あじわいます。** — *yukkuri ocha o ajiwaimasu* — "I slowly savour my tea."

Polite **あじわいます**, *ajiwaimasu*; a godan verb.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**あじわう** (*ajiwau*) is a native Japanese verb, inherited, built on *aji*, "taste"; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
