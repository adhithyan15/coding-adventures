## You'll want to know: ふなたび
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C381-FUNATABI381-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ふなたび** — *funatabi* — "a journey by boat".

> **しまへの ふなたびは たのしかったです。** — *shima e no funatabi wa tanoshikatta desu* — "The boat trip to the island was fun."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ふなたび** (*funatabi*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, built from *funa*, "boat" (from *fune*) and *tabi*, "journey"; its kanji were attached later as kun'yomi (JMdict).
