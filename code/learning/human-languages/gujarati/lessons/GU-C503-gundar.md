## You'll want to know: ગુંદર
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C503-RANDO503-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ગુંદર** — *gundar* — "glue".

> આ ગુંદર લાકડા પર સારું ચોંટે છે.
>
> *ā gundar lākḍā par sārũ cõṭe che.*
>
> This glue sticks well to wood.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ગુંદર** (*gundar*) is filed by CDIAL under Sanskrit *gundra*, the name of a reed, and CDIAL lists the Gujarati word with the sense "gum" (CDIAL 4199); how the reed's name came to mean gum is not explained there.
