## You'll want to know: expugnō, expugnāre
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C341-OBSIDIO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**expugnō, expugnāre** — "to take by storm".

> Tandem oppidum expugnāvērunt.
>
> *At last they took the town by storm.*

*expugnō, expugnāre, expugnāvī, expugnātum*. After the attack comes, if things go well, the storming.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**expugnō** is built on *pugnō*, "to fight", with *ex-*, "out, to the end" (Lewis & Short): to fight a place out of the enemy's hands.
