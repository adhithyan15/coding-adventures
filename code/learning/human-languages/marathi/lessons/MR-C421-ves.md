## You'll want to know: वेस
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C421-BANDHKAM421-04]; assesses=[] -->

**वेस** — *ves* — "a village gate".

> गावाच्या वेशीवर एक जुनं झाड आहे.
>
> *gāvācyā veśīvar ek junaṁ jhāḍ āhe.*
>
> There is an old tree at the village gate.

Feminine.

Before an ending वेस becomes वेशी-: वेशीवर, at the gate; also the edge of the village.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**वेस** (*ves*) is a Marathi word for the gateway of an old village, and for the village edge; its origin is not settled.
