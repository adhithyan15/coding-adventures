# கடவை (kaṭavai) — a level crossing; a crossing place

## You'll want to know: கடவை
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C409-KUZHI409-03]; assesses=[] -->

**கடவை** — *kaṭavai* — "a level crossing; a crossing place".

> ரயில் போகும்வரை நாங்கள் கடவையில் காத்திருந்தோம்.
>
> *rayil pōkumvarai nāṅkaḷ kaṭavaiyil kāttiruntōm.*
>
> We waited at the level crossing until the train went by.

From கட, to cross, + -வை: a place for crossing.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**கடவை** (*kaṭavai*) is built from கட, "to cross, to pass through" (DEDR 1109), with a noun ending: a place to cross.
