Bengali, chapter 168: Mother-in-law, Bridegroom, Bride, Wedding, Relative.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
শাশুড়ি (shāshuṛi) — a mother-in-law.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a motorbike, then the Bengali for a lorry.

You'll want to know: শাশুড়ি.
শাশুড়ি — shāshuṛi — "a mother-in-law".

The word, taken apart.
শাশুড়ি (shāshuṛi) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from śvaśrū, "husband's or wife's mother" (CDIAL 12759). CDIAL lists Bengali sāsuṛi and the shorter sās.
