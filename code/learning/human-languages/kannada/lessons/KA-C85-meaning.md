## You'll want to know: ಅರ್ಥ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C85-SLOWLY-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ಅರ್ಥ** (*artha*) — "meaning". You have seen it inside **ಅರ್ಥಮಾಡಿಕೊ**,
"to understand", which is "to make the meaning one's own".

**ಇದರ ಅರ್ಥ ಏನು?** (*idara artha ēnu?*) — "what is the meaning of this?"
