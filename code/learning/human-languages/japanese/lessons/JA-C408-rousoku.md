## You'll want to know: ろうそく

**ろうそく** — *rōsoku* — "a candle".

> **ろうそくの ひが きえました。** — *rōsoku no hi ga kiemashita* — "The candle went out."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ろうそく** (*rōsoku*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *rō*, "wax" (Middle Chinese *lap*) and *soku*, "light" (*tsyowk*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
