## You'll want to know: કેન્દ્ર
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C416-AREA416-01]; assesses=[] -->

**કેન્દ્ર** — *kendra* — "a centre".

> શહેરના કેન્દ્રમાં મોટું બજાર છે.
>
> *śahernā kendramā̃ moṭũ bajār che.*
>
> There is a big market in the centre of the city.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**કેન્દ્ર** (*kendra*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *kendra*, "the centre of a circle", a word Sanskrit itself took from Greek (Monier-Williams).
