## You'll want to know: あびる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C147-WASH147-03]; assesses=[] -->

**あびる** — *abiru* — "to pour over oneself; to take (a shower)".

> **まいあさ みずを あびます。** — *maiasa mizu o abimasu* — "Every morning I pour cold water over myself."

Polite **あびます**, *abimasu*; an ichidan verb.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**あびる** (*abiru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi, and its older origin is not settled (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
