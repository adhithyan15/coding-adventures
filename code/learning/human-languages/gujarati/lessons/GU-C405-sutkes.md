## You'll want to know: સૂટકેસ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C405-FLIGHT405-04]; assesses=[] -->

**સૂટકેસ** — *sūṭkes* — "a suitcase".

> મારી સૂટકેસ ઘણી ભારે છે.
>
> *mārī sūṭkes ghaṇī bhāre che.*
>
> My suitcase is very heavy.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**સૂટકેસ** (*sūṭkes*) is borrowed from English *suitcase*.
