## You'll want to know: die Toleranz
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C360-VORURTEIL-02]; assesses=[] -->

**die Toleranz** — "tolerance".

> In einer guten Schule lernen die Kinder Toleranz.
>
> *In a good school the children learn tolerance.*

No plural in this sense. The adjective is *tolerant*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Toleranz* is a learned borrowing from Latin *tolerantia* "a bearing, endurance" (Lewis & Short), from *tolerāre* "to bear, to put up with".
