## You'll want to know: மாதிரி
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C545-PANKU545-05]; assesses=[] -->

**மாதிரி** — *mātiri* — "a model; a sample".

> கடைக்காரர் துணியின் மாதிரியைக் காட்டினார்.
>
> *kaṭaikkārar tuṇiyiṉ mātiriyaik kāṭṭiṉār.*
>
> The shopkeeper showed a sample of the cloth.

In speech it also means "like": அவனை மாதிரி, like him.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

The origin of **மாதிரி** (*mātiri*) is not settled: no dictionary we can check gives its source.
