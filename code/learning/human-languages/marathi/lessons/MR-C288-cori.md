## You'll want to know: चोरी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C288-GHATANA288-03]; assesses=[] -->

**चोरी** — *corī* — "a theft".

> आमच्या गल्लीत दोन घरांमध्ये चोरी झाली.
>
> *āmacyā gallīt don gharāmmadhye corī jhālī.*
>
> There was a theft in two houses on our lane.

Feminine.

चोरी होणे, a theft happens; the verb चोरणे is to steal.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**चोरी** (*corī*) is a tadbhava, inherited from Sanskrit *caurikā*, 'theft' (CDIAL 4937), which is built on *cora*, 'a thief'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
