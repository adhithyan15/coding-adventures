## You'll want to know: lēniō, lēnīre
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C492-SOLA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**lēniō, lēnīre** — "to soothe, soften".

> Medicus dolōrem aegrī medicāmentō lēnit.
>
> *The doctor soothes the patient's pain with a medicine.*

*lēniō, lēnīre, lēnīvī, lēnītum*, from *lēnis*. English *lenient* comes from its present participle.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**lēniō** is built on *lēnis* "gentle, soft" (Lewis & Short). English *lenient* comes from its present participle *lēniēns*.
