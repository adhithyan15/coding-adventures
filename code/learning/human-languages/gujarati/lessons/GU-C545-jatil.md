## You'll want to know: જટિલ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C545-AASAN545-02]; assesses=[] -->

**જટિલ** — *jaṭil* — "complicated".

> જો નિયમો જટિલ હશે તો કોઈ તે નહીં સમજે.
>
> *jo niymo jaṭil haśe to koī te nahī̃ samje.*
>
> If the rules are complicated, nobody will understand them.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**જટિલ** (*jaṭil*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *jaṭila*, "wearing matted hair" (Monier-Williams): tangled like matted locks.
