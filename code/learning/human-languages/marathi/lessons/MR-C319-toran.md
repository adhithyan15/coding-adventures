## You'll want to know: तोरण
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C319-ROSHNAI319-03]; assesses=[] -->

**तोरण** — *toraṇ* — "a door garland".

> सणाच्या दिवशी दाराला आंब्याच्या पानांचं तोरण लावलं.
>
> *saṇācyā divśī dārālā āmbyācyā pānāñcaṁ toraṇ lāvlaṁ.*
>
> On the festival day a garland of mango leaves was hung on the door.

Neuter.

दाराला तोरण बांधणे, to hang a festoon over the door.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**तोरण** (*toraṇ*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *toraṇa*, 'an arched doorway, a festive gateway' (Monier-Williams).
