Punjabi, chapter 134: Cheek, Forehead, Eyebrow, Beard, Moustache.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
ਗੱਲ੍ਹ (gallh) — a cheek.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for an owl, then the Punjabi for a crow.

You'll want to know: ਗੱਲ੍ਹ.
ਗੱਲ੍ਹ — gallh — "a cheek".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: gallh]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: gallh, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say gallh]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Punjabi for an owl, then the Punjabi for a crow, then say gallh again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does ਗੱਲ੍ਹ (gallh) mean? ("A cheek".) Say it once more.

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
Grass? (ਘਾਹ, ghāh.) A seed? (ਬੀਜ, bīj.) A root? (ਜੜ੍ਹ, jaṛh.) A bud? (ਕਲੀ, kalī.) An island? (ਟਾਪੂ, ṭāpū.) A shore? (ਕਿਨਾਰਾ, kinārā.) A waterfall? (ਝਰਨਾ, jharnā.) A cave? (ਗੁਫ਼ਾ, gufā.) A valley? (ਵਾਦੀ, vādī.) A sand hill? (ਟਿੱਬਾ, ṭibbā.) A desert? (ਮਾਰੂਥਲ, mārūthal.) Lightning? (ਬਿਜਲੀ, bijlī.) A storm? (ਤੂਫ਼ਾਨ, tūfān.) The earth? (ਧਰਤੀ, dhartī.) Moonlight? (ਚਾਨਣੀ, cānṇī.) Darkness? (ਹਨੇਰਾ, hanerā.) Light? (ਚਾਨਣ, cānaṇ.) Shade? (ਛਾਂ, chā̃.) A rainbow? (ਸਤਰੰਗੀ ਪੀਂਘ, satrangī pīṅgh.) A rabbit? (ਖ਼ਰਗੋਸ਼, khargosh.)
