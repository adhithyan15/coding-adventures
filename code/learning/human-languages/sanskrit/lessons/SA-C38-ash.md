## You'll want to know: भस्म
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C38-EARTH-02]; assesses=[] -->

**भस्म** (*bhasma*) — "ash".

**भस्म** is what अग्निः leaves behind — ash, and the grey line of it drawn across a forehead.

It stands on *bhas*, "to chew, to grind small", so ash is the ground-down thing: what is left when fire has finished eating.

Hindi and Marathi keep *bhasm* with the meaning untouched.
