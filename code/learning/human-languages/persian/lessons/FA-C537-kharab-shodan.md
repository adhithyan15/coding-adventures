## You'll want to know: خراب شدن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C537-CHANGES-04]; assesses=[] -->

**خراب شدن** (*kharâb shodan*) — "to break down; to go wrong".

> یخچال دیروز خراب شد.
>
> *yakhchâl diruz kharâb shod.*
>
> The fridge broke down yesterday.

*kharâb* 'broken, ruined' + *shodan* 'to become'.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*kharâb* comes from Arabic *ḫarāb*, 'ruin' (EtymDB). *shodan*, 'to become', is inherited Persian.
