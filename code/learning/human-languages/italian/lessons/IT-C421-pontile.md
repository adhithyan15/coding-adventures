## You'll want to know: il pontile
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C421-PORTO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**il pontile** — "the landing stage, the wooden jetty".

> Dal pontile i bambini guardano i pesci.
>
> *From the landing stage the children watch the fish.*

Built on *ponte*: a bridge that reaches out over the water.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*pontile* is built on *ponte* 'bridge', which is inherited from Latin *pōns, pontis* 'bridge' (EtymDB).
