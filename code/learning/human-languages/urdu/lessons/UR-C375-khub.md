## You'll want to know: خوب
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C375-BAHUT375-03]; assesses=[] -->

**خوب** — *khūb* — "thoroughly; plenty".

> اگر تم خوب پڑھو گے تو امتحان پاس کرو گے۔
>
> *agar tum khūb paṛho ge to imtihān pās karo ge.*
>
> If you study hard, you will pass the exam.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**خوب** (*khūb*) is usually traced to Persian *khūb*, "good, fine"; Urdu uses it for doing a thing well and fully.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
