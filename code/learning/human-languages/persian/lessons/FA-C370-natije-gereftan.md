## You'll want to know: نتیجه گرفتن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C370-PAYAMAD-05]; assesses=[] -->

**نتیجه گرفتن** (*natije gereftan*) — "to conclude, to infer".

> از حرفهای او نتیجه گرفتم که خسته است.
>
> *az harfhâ-ye u natije gereftam ke khaste ast.*
>
> From what he said I concluded that he was tired.

*Natije* "result" + *gereftan* "to take" (present stem *gir-*). Also to get results.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**نتیجه گرفتن** is literally "to take a result": *natije* comes from Arabic *natīja* "result" (EtymDB), and *gereftan* "to take" is the inherited Persian verb.
