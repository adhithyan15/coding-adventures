## You'll want to know: erstaunt
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C474-STAUNEN-02]; assesses=[] -->

**erstaunt** — "astonished".

> Sie war erstaunt, wie gut ihr Sohn kochen kann.
>
> *She was astonished at how well her son can cook.*

The participle of *erstaunen* — to astonish.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*erstaunt* is built on *er-* and *staunen* "to be amazed", which came from Swiss German *stūnen* "to stiffen" (EtymDB).
