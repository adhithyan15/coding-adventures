## You'll want to know: विचरति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C403-PANTHA403-05]; assesses=[] -->

**विचरति** — *vicarati* — "roams; rambles about".

> पथिकः वने विचरति।
>
> *pathikaḥ vane vicarati.*
>
> The wayfarer roams in the forest.

Past (he …): सः वने विचरितवान्, *saḥ vane vicaritavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**विचरति** (*vicarati*) is built from *vi*, "apart, here and there", and the root √car, "to move", which also gives *carati*, "moves about"; MW gives *vi-car* as to rove or ramble about (MW).
