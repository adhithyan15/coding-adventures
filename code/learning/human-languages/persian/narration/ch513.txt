Persian, chapter 513: Scores and Records.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
بازنده (bâzande) — a loser (of a game).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a league, then the Persian for a point.

You'll want to know: بازنده.
بازنده (bâzande) — "a loser (of a game)".
بازنده بازی با لبخند دست داد. bâzande-ye bâzi bâ labkhand dast dâd. The loser of the match shook hands with a smile.
From bâz-, the present stem of bâkhtan 'to lose (a game)', + -ande 'one who'.

The word, taken apart.
bâzande is built on native Persian bâz-, the present stem of bâkhtan, 'to lose a game', with the ending -ande, 'one who'. EtymDB has no entry for it.

Lesson 2 of 5.
مساوی (mosâvi) — equal; level (a draw).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a point, then the Persian for a loser.

You'll want to know: مساوی.
مساوی (mosâvi) — "equal; level (a draw)".
بازی دیشب یک یک مساوی شد. bâzi-ye dishab yek yek mosâvi shod. Last night's match was a one-all draw.
mosâvi shodan — to end level. In sums, mosâvi is 'equals'.

The word, taken apart.
mosâvi is a loan, usually traced to Arabic musāwī, 'equal'. EtymDB has no entry for it.

Lesson 3 of 5.
رکورد (rekord) — a best result, a record.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a loser, then the Persian for equal.

You'll want to know: رکورد.
رکورد (rekord) — "a best result, a record".
او رکورد مدرسه را دارد. u rekord-e madrese râ dârad. She holds the school record.
Plural rekordhâ.

The word, taken apart.
rekord is a modern loan, usually traced to English record. EtymDB has no entry for the Persian word.

Lesson 4 of 5.
رکورد زدن (rekord zadan) — to set a new best, to break a record.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for equal, then the Persian for a best result.

You'll want to know: رکورد زدن.
رکورد زدن (rekord zadan) — "to set a new best, to break a record".
او در مسابقه شنا رکورد زد. u dar mosâbeqe-ye shenâ rekord zad. He set a record in the swimming race.
rekord + zadan 'to strike'; present stem zan-.

The word, taken apart.
rekord zadan joins rekord, a loan usually traced to English record, and inherited zadan, 'to strike'.

Lesson 5 of 5.
گل زدن (gol zadan) — to score a goal.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a best result, then the Persian for to set a new best.

You'll want to know: گل زدن.
گل زدن (gol zadan) — "to score a goal".
او در دقیقه آخر گل زد. u dar daqiqe-ye âkhar gol zad. He scored in the last minute.
This gol 'goal' is spelled like gol 'flower'; + zadan 'to strike'.

The word, taken apart.
This gol was borrowed from English goal (EtymDB); it only looks like native gol, 'flower'. zadan, 'to strike', is inherited Persian.
