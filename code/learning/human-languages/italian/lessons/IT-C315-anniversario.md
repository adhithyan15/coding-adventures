## You'll want to know: l'anniversario
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C315-AUGURI-03]; assesses=[] -->

**l'anniversario** — "the anniversary".

> Oggi è il nostro anniversario di matrimonio.
>
> *Today is our wedding anniversary.*

*Buon anniversario!*

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*anniversario* is a learned borrowing from Latin *anniversārius* "returning yearly" (EtymDB), from *annus* "year" and *vertere* "to turn".
