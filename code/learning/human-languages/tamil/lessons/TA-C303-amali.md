## You'll want to know: அமளி
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C303-KOOCHAL303-02]; assesses=[] -->

**அமளி** — *amaḷi* — "a commotion".

> கூட்டத்தில் திடீரென்று அமளி தொடங்கியது.
>
> *kūṭṭattil tiṭīreṉṟu amaḷi toṭaṅkiyatu.*
>
> A commotion suddenly broke out at the meeting.

அமளி செய், to make a commotion.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**அமளி** (*amaḷi*) is native Dravidian (DEDR 166): tumult, uproar, bustle.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
