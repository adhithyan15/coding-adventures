## You'll want to know: مثال
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C338-MISAL338-01]; assesses=[] -->

**مثال** — *misāl* — "an example".

> ایک مثال دیجیے۔
>
> *ek misāl dījiye.*
>
> Give an example, please.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**مثال** (*misāl*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *mithāl*, "a likeness, a model".
