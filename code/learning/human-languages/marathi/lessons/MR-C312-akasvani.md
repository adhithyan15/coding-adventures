## You'll want to know: आकाशवाणी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C312-VAHINI312-01]; assesses=[] -->

**आकाशवाणी** — *ākāśvāṇī* — "radio; the radio service".

> आजी रोज सकाळी आकाशवाणीवर गाणी ऐकते.
>
> *ājī roj sakāḷī ākāśvāṇīvar gāṇī aikte.*
>
> Grandma listens to songs on the radio every morning.

Feminine.

आकाशवाणीवर, on the radio. रेडिओ is also said.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**आकाशवाणी** (*ākāśvāṇī*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *ākāśavāṇī*, 'a voice from the air or from heaven' (Monier-Williams), a fitting name for the radio.
