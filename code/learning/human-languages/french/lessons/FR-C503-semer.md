## You'll want to know: semer
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C503-JARDIN-03]; assesses=[] -->

**semer** — "to sow".

> En avril, nous semons des carottes.
>
> *In April we sow carrots.*

*Je sème*, *nous semons*. Informally, to lose someone: *il nous a semés*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*semer* is inherited from Latin *sēmināre* "to sow" (EtymDB), from *sēmen* "seed" (Lewis & Short).
