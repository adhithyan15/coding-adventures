## You'll want to know: यशः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C360-YASHAS360-01]; assesses=[] -->

**यशः** — *yaśaḥ* — "fame; glory".

> लेखकस्य यशः सर्वत्र अस्ति।
>
> *lekhakasya yaśaḥ sarvatra asti.*
>
> The writer's fame is everywhere.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**यशः** (*yaśaḥ*) MW gives *yaśas* "honour, glory, fame, renown" and gives no derivation, so its origin is uncertain (MW).
