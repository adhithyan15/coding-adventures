## You'll want to know: ಶಿಬಿರ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C375-SHIBIRA375-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ಶಿಬಿರ** — *śibira* — "a camp".

> ಪ್ರವಾಹದ ನಂತರ ಶಾಲೆಯಲ್ಲಿ ಒಂದು ಶಿಬಿರ ತೆರೆದರು.
>
> *pravāhada nantara śāleyalli ondu śibira teredaru.*
>
> After the flood they opened a camp in the school.

Plural: ಶಿಬಿರಗಳು, *śibiragaḷu*.

Also a training or health camp: ಆರೋಗ್ಯ ಶಿಬಿರ.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಶಿಬಿರ** (*śibira*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *śibira*, "a royal camp, a tent, an entrenchment" (Monier-Williams).
