Russian, chapter 420: Security and customs.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
досмотр (dosmótr) — a security check, an inspection.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for luggage, then the Russian for boarding.

You'll want to know: досмотр.
досмотр (dosmótr) — "a security check, an inspection".
Перед посадкой все проходят досмотр. Péred posádkoy vse prokhódyat dosmótr. Before boarding everyone goes through the security check.
Masculine. Таможенный досмотр (dosmótr) — a customs inspection.

The word, taken apart.
досмотр (dosmótr) is built from до- 'thoroughly, to the end' and смотр 'a review', on смотреть 'to look', which EtymDB relates to Proto-Slavic motriti 'to watch'.

Lesson 3 of 5.
пограничник (pograníchnik) — a border guard.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a security check, then the Russian for to search.

You'll want to know: пограничник.
пограничник (pograníchnik) — "a border guard".
Пограничник долго проверял мой паспорт. Pograníchnik dólgo proveryál moy pásport. The border guard checked my passport for a long time.
Masculine. Built on граница — a border.

The word, taken apart.
пограничник (pograníchnik) is built on пограничный 'of the border', from граница 'a border', which is inherited from Proto-Slavic granica (EtymDB).

Lesson 4 of 5.
декларация (deklarátsiya) — a customs declaration; a declaration.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to search, then the Russian for a border guard.

You'll want to know: декларация.
декларация (deklarátsiya) — "a customs declaration; a declaration".
В самолёте нам дали декларацию. V samolyóte nam dáli deklarátsiyu. On the plane they gave us a declaration form.
Feminine. Заполнить декларацию — to fill in a declaration.

The word, taken apart.
декларация (deklarátsiya) is usually traced through French déclaration to Latin dēclārātiō 'a making clear' (EtymDB lists the French and the Latin words), on clārus 'clear'.

Lesson 5 of 5.
декларировать (deklarírovat) — to declare (at customs).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a border guard, then the Russian for a customs declaration.

You'll want to know: декларировать.
декларировать (deklarírovat) — "to declare (at customs)".
Деньги выше лимита надо декларировать. Déngi výshe límita nádo deklarírovat. Money above the limit has to be declared.
The same form serves both aspects. Present декларирую.

The word, taken apart.
декларировать (deklarírovat) is usually traced to Latin dēclārāre 'to make clear', on clārus 'clear' (Lewis & Short). EtymDB lists the Russian verb without a source.
