## You'll want to know: भाडेकरू
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C417-ANAMAT417-03]; assesses=[] -->

**भाडेकरू** — *bhāḍekarū* — "a tenant".

> शेजारच्या घरात नवे भाडेकरू आले.
>
> *śejārcyā gharāt nave bhāḍekarū āle.*
>
> New tenants have moved into the house next door.

Masculine.

The owner is the मालक; the plural is the same: दोन भाडेकरू.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**भाडेकरू** (*bhāḍekarū*) is built from **भाडे**, "rent", and the ending -करू, "one who does": the one who pays the rent; the origin of भाडे is not settled.
