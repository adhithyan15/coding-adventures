# બનવું (banvũ) — to happen; to become

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-GRAMMAR-C275-ONGOING-PAST-01, GU-LEX-C155-READY155-02, GU-GRAMMAR-C147-THAYO-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: ask "what happened?" the A2 way. (**શું થયું?**, *śũ thayũ?*) Then say "it was raining". (*varsād paḍto hato*.)

## You'll want to know: બનવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C275-BANVU-01]; assesses=[GU-GRAMMAR-C147-E-TIME-01, GU-LEX-RAAT-01, GU-LEX-C224-DOCTOR224-01] -->

**બનવું** — *banvũ* — "to happen; to become".

> **ગઈકાલે રાતે શું બન્યું?**
>
> *gaīkāle rāte śũ banyũ?* — "What happened last night?"

**શું થયું?** is the everyday question. **બનવું** is for an event, a thing that takes place, and it suits a story. It takes no object, so its past follows its subject, as **ગયો** does: **બન્યું**, **બન્યો**, **બની**.

Its second sense is "to become":

> **અરુણ દાક્તર બન્યો.**
>
> *Aruṇ dāktar banyo.* — "Arun became a doctor."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**બનવું** (*banvũ*) sits beside **બનાવવું**, "to make", which you know: one makes a thing, the other is what a thing does when it comes about. CDIAL lists it under Sanskrit *vanati*, "desires, gains, makes ready", and marks the Gujarati verb as taken from Hindi *bannā* (CDIAL 11260).
