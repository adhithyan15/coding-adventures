# (reading) — six words off a board

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[RU-LEX-VODA] -->

[PAUSE 2s] You have written every letter in every one of these. Not one word is
new.

[PAUSE 1s] What is new is that nobody says them before you read them.

## Reading
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-SKILL-READ-WORDS]; assesses=[RU-LEX-KHLEB, RU-LEX-CHAI] -->

> вода
> хлеб
> чай
> кофе
> суп
> сыр

[PAUSE 3s] Read down the list once. Do not spell each one out — look at the
whole word and let it arrive.

[PAUSE 3s] Again, and notice which came at once and which you had to spell.
Both are fine. The difference is what practice removes.

## You'll want to know
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[RU-LEX-KOFE, RU-LEX-SUP, RU-LEX-SYR] -->

[PAUSE 2s] These six would be on a board over a counter, and they are the six
you would want first.

[PAUSE 2s] Two of them are traps of exactly the kind this book warned you
about. **суп** and **сыр** look like *cyn* and *cbip* to an eye trained on
English, and they are *sup* and *syr*.

[PAUSE 2s] That is the whole reason to read a word cold rather than after
hearing it. Heard first, you never test whether the letters actually reached
you.
