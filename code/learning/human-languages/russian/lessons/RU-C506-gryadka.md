## You'll want to know: грядка
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C506-VEGETABLES-01]; assesses=[] -->

**грядка** (*gryádka*) — "a garden bed".

> На этой грядке растёт морковь.
>
> *Na étoy gryádke rastyót morkóv.*
>
> Carrots are growing in this bed.

Feminine. A diminutive of *гряда* — a ridge.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*грядка* is a diminutive of *гряда* 'a ridge, a row'; EtymDB has no entry.
