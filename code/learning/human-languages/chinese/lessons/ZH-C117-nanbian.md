## You'll want to know: nánbian
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C117-COMPASS117-02]; assesses=[] -->

*nánbian* — "the south; on the south side".

> *gōngyuán zài nánbian* — The park is to the south.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*nánbian* is built from nán 'south' and 边 'side', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *nom* and *pen* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription); the dictionary calls the south the direction of fire.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for asking where things are.
