Kannada, chapter 477: Creatures of Forest and Field.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ತೋಳ (tōḷa) — a wolf.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for biodiversity, then the Kannada for to migrate.

You'll want to know: ತೋಳ.
ತೋಳ — tōḷa — "a wolf".
ತೋಳಗಳು ರಾತ್ರಿ ಗುಂಪಿನಲ್ಲಿ ತಿರುಗುತ್ತವೆ. tōḷagaḷu rātri gumpinalli tiruguttave. Wolves roam about in packs at night.
Plural: ತೋಳಗಳು, tōḷagaḷu.
The plural takes -ಗಳು: ತೋಳಗಳು.

The word, taken apart.
ತೋಳ (tōḷa) is native Dravidian: DEDR 3548 lists Kannada tōḷa, "a wolf".

Lesson 2 of 5.

ಕಾಡುಹಂದಿ — kāḍuhandi — "a wild boar".
ಕಾಡುಹಂದಿಗಳು ರೈತನ ಹೊಲವನ್ನು ಹಾಳು ಮಾಡಿದವು. kāḍuhandigaḷu raitana holavannu hāḷu māḍidavu. Wild boars ruined the farmer's field.
ಕಾಡು, forest, plus ಹಂದಿ, a pig.

The word, taken apart.
ಕಾಡುಹಂದಿ (kāḍuhandi) is a native Kannada compound of ಕಾಡು, "a forest" (DEDR 1438), and ಹಂದಿ, "a pig" (DEDR 4039).

Lesson 3 of 5.
ಘರ್ಜಿಸು (gharjisu) — to roar.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a wolf, then the Kannada for a wild boar.

You'll want to know: ಘರ್ಜಿಸು.
ಘರ್ಜಿಸು — gharjisu — "to roar".
ರಾತ್ರಿ ಕಾಡಿನಲ್ಲಿ ಸಿಂಹ ಘರ್ಜಿಸಿತು. rātri kāḍinalli siṃha gharjisitu. At night a lion roared in the forest.
Past (it …): ಘರ್ಜಿಸಿತು, gharjisitu.
Of lions and tigers, and of thunder.

The word, taken apart.
ಘರ್ಜಿಸು (gharjisu) is built from Sanskrit garj, "to thunder, to roar" (CDIAL 4046, garjati), with the verb ending -isu; Kannada writes it with the aspirate ಘ.

Lesson 4 of 5.
ಮೇಯು (mēyu) — to graze.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a wild boar, then the Kannada for to roar.

You'll want to know: ಮೇಯು.
ಮೇಯು — mēyu — "to graze".
ಹಸುಗಳು ಹೊಲದಲ್ಲಿ ಹುಲ್ಲು ಮೇಯುತ್ತಿದ್ದವು. hasugaḷu holadalli hullu mēyuttiddavu. The cows were grazing on grass in the field.
Past (it …): ಮೇಯಿತು, mēyitu.
Of cattle and deer; ಮೇಯಿಸು is to take animals to graze.

The word, taken apart.
ಮೇಯು (mēyu) is native Dravidian: DEDR 5093 lists Kannada mē, mēyu, "to graze, to eat grass".
