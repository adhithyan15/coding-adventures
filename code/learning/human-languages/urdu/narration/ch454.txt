Urdu, chapter 454: Character.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
فطرت (fitrat) — a person's nature; disposition.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for to sob, then the Urdu for teasing.

You'll want to know: فطرت.
فطرت — fitrat — "a person's nature; disposition".
اس کی فطرت بڑی نرم ہے۔ us kī fitrat baṛī narm hai. She has a very gentle nature.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
فطرت (fitrat) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic fiṭra, "the nature a person is born with".
