# One reason — a line of your own with 因为 and 所以

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ZH-LEX-C281-LIZI-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Someone asks you a question you have surely been asked before: 你为什么学汉语?

[PAUSE 1s] This lesson answers it, in one line of your own.

- [YOU RECALL: say *lìzi*, an example]

## Writing — controlled composition
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ZH-GRAMMAR-YINWEI-SUOYI-01, ZH-SCRIPT-SUO-01, ZH-LEX-C96-YINWEI-01, ZH-LEX-C96-SUOYI-01, ZH-LEX-C95-WEISHENME-01, ZH-ORTHO-HANYU-01, ZH-LEX-C281-BIRU-01, ZH-LEX-C281-LIZI-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: controlled-composition -->

[PAUSE 2s] Write **one line** that answers 你为什么学汉语. Use the frame: 因为 and your reason, then 所以 and 我学汉语. If you want to add an example, 比如 brings it in at the end.

[PAUSE 2s] A word whose characters you have not written goes in pinyin, inside the line, with its tone marks: 中国菜 is all characters you write, but *kěyǐ* has one you do not write yet, so it goes in pinyin. Every character comes from the ones you have learned, each in the order you learned it, each in the same size of square.

[PAUSE 2s] No Chinese commas or full stops: leave a space between the two halves, as this book prints them.

[PAUSE 3s] Write the line. Then read it back: is 因为 in front of the reason, and 所以 in front of what follows?
