## You'll want to know: ધારણા
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C367-SAMBHAV367-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ધારણા** — *dhārṇā* — "an assumption".

> જો મારી ધારણા ખોટી હશે તો હું માફી માગીશ.
>
> *jo mārī dhārṇā khoṭī haśe to hũ māphī māgīś.*
>
> If my assumption is wrong, I will apologise.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ધારણા** (*dhārṇā*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *dhāraṇā*, "holding, bearing, keeping in mind" (Monier-Williams).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
