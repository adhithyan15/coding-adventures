## You'll want to know: der Irrtum
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C337-IRRTUM-02]; assesses=[] -->

**der Irrtum** — "the mistake, the error".

> Das war ein Irrtum: Die Rechnung ist schon bezahlt.
>
> *That was an error: the bill has already been paid.*

Plural: *die Irrtümer*. *Im Irrtum sein* — to be mistaken.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Irrtum* is inherited through Middle High German *irretuom* from Old High German *irrituom* (EtymDB), built on *irren* "to err" with the noun ending *-tum*.
