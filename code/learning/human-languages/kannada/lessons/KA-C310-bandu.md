# ಬಂದು (bandu) — having come, and then

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[KA-GRAMMAR-C310-FIRST-THEN-01, KA-GRAMMAR-C309-PAST-PERSON-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "First I drank coffee. After that I went to school." (*modalu nānu kāphi kuḍide. adara nantara śālege hōde.*)

[PAUSE 1s] Two sentences, two full verbs. Kannada can fold them into one.

## You'll want to know: the -ಇ and -ಉ form
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-GRAMMAR-C310-CONJUNCTIVE-01]; assesses=[KA-LEX-C103-HOME-01, KA-LEX-C40-OOTA-01, KA-LEX-C104-JOBS-05, KA-LEX-C46-ASK-05, KA-LEX-C158-KRIYE158-03] -->

> **ನಾನು ಮನೆಗೆ ಬಂದು ಊಟ ಮಾಡಿದೆ.**
>
> *nānu manege bandu ūṭa māḍide.* — "I came home and had a meal."

The first verb carries no person and no time: **ಬಂದು**, "having come". Only the last verb says who and when. So a chain of these runs on to one full verb at the end:

> **ಅವನು ಅಂಗಡಿಗೆ ಹೋಗಿ ಒಂದು ಪುಸ್ತಕ ಖರೀದಿಸಿ ಮನೆಗೆ ಬಂದನು.**
>
> *avanu aṅgaḍige hōgi ondu pustaka kharīdisi manege bandanu.* — "He went to the shop, bought a book and came home."

Build it from the past without its ending. Where the **-ಇ-** came in front of the bead (**ಮಾಡಿದ-**, **ಖರೀದಿಸಿದ-**), keep the **-ಇ** and drop the bead: **ಮಾಡಿ**, **ಖರೀದಿಸಿ**, **ನೋಡಿ**. Everywhere else, the last **-ಅ** becomes **-ಉ**: **ಬಂದ-** → **ಬಂದು**, **ಕುಡಿದ-** → **ಕುಡಿದು**, **ಕೊಟ್ಟ-** → **ಕೊಟ್ಟು**, **ಇದ್ದ-** → **ಇದ್ದು**. Two common verbs go their own way: **ಹೋಗು** → **ಹೋಗಿ**, **ಆಗು** → **ಆಗಿ**.

You have met this form inside words already: **ಕಳೆದುಹೋಗು**, "to get lost", starts with **ಕಳೆದು**, the **-ಉ** form of **ಕಳೆ**, "to lose, to leave behind", and **ದಯವಿಟ್ಟು**, "please", is "having placed compassion".

## You'll want to know: -ಇದ ನಂತರ, after doing
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-GRAMMAR-C310-AFTER-DOING-01]; assesses=[KA-LEX-C106-SOON-03, KA-LEX-C147-ACT147-03, KA-LEX-C72-WANT-01] -->

> **ಊಟ ಮಾಡಿದ ನಂತರ ನಾನು ಮಲಗಿದೆ.**
>
> *ūṭa māḍida nantara nānu malagide.* — "After having the meal, I lay down."

**ಮಾಡಿದ** is the past without its ending, "that was done", the same piece as the **ಬಂದ** inside **ಬಂದಾಗ**. Before **ನಂತರ** it gives "after doing": **ಬಂದ ನಂತರ**, "after coming"; **ನೋಡಿದ ನಂತರ**, "after seeing". Where **-ಇ** and **-ಉ** tie two actions tightly, **-ಇದ ನಂತರ** marks a clear step in time.
