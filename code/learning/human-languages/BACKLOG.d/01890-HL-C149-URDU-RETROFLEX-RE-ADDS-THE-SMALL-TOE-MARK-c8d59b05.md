## HL-C149 — Urdu retroflex re adds the small to'e mark

After Kannada independent long-i **ಈ** landed, Urdu retroflex re **ڑ** leads
the reranked source-gated queue at **1 affected realization** (tied with nine
other glyphs and selected first by the stable glyph ordering).

Northwestern University Libraries' open *Zer o Zabar* chapter on fe, qāf,
ṭe, ḍāl, and ṛe identifies **ڑ** as the nonconnecting re body with a small
to'e above and provides separate independent-form calligraphic and handwriting
animations.

This item closes the **ڑ** realization with its canonical Urdu letter record,
exact source ownership, a font-fitted independent learner path, and
cross-consumer evidence. The animations establish the two pen-down runs and
one lift; the printed outline is used only to fit those movements.
