You'll want to know: 就.
就 — jiù — "then; right away".
我马上就来 wǒ mǎshàng jiù lái — I'll be right there.
吃了饭 我就去 chī le fàn, wǒ jiù qù — Once I've eaten, I'll go.
It sounds like jiù, old, which you know: the same sound, a different word. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

The word, taken apart.
就 (jiù) is inherited from Middle Chinese dzjuwH (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'to accomplish', 'to go to meet' and 'to approach'. 'Then; right away' is not given there, so how the word came by it is not settled.

What you've built.
就 (jiù) sits after the person, right before the verb: 我马上就来. It ties what comes next to what came first: 吃了饭, 我就去.

Script — a dot, a bar, 口 and 小, then a four-stroke right side.
就 (jiù).
Four pieces: three you write, and one new one on the right.
A dot and a horizontal, as 文 opens.
口, three strokes, under them.
小, three strokes, under 口: the hooked vertical, then the left dot, then the right dot.
The right side, four strokes: a horizontal; a long left-falling stroke; a vertical that bends right along the foot and hooks up; and last the dot at the upper right.
2 + 3 + 3 + 4 = twelve.

Lesson 3 of 7.
要是 (yàoshi) — if.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: the character you wrote last, and a word that sounds the same as this one.
[once you have stopped driving — read: 我马上就来, aloud — wǒ mǎshàng jiù lái]
[your turn — recall: say yàoshi, a key — R3, durable]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

You'll want to know: 要是.
要是 — yàoshi — "if".
要是明天下雨 我就不去了 yàoshi míngtiān xiàyǔ, wǒ jiù bú qù le — If it rains tomorrow, I won't go.
It sounds like yàoshi, a key: the same sound, a different word. Every character in it is one you write.

The word, taken apart.
要是 (yàoshi) is built from 要 and 是, both inherited from Middle Chinese, 'jiewH and dzyeX (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'to bind by agreement' and 'right, as against wrong'. 'If' for the pair is the modern word's own sense.

What you've built.
要是 (yàoshi) opens the condition; 就 opens what follows, after its subject: 我就不去了 wǒ jiù bú qù le.

Grammar Lens: 要是 … 就 …, if and then.
要是 (yàoshi) opens what might happen; 就 (jiù) opens what follows if it does.
要是明天下雨 我就不去了 yàoshi míngtiān xiàyǔ, wǒ jiù bú qù le — If it rains tomorrow, I won't go. 要是你病了 就回家 yàoshi nǐ bìng le, jiù huí jiā — If you're ill, go home.
就 (jiù) goes after the subject of the second clause, right before its verb: 我就不去了. With no subject there, 就 (jiù) opens the clause: 就回家.
了 at the end of 我就不去了 says the plan has changed.

Lesson 5 of 7.
的话 (dehuà) — if, at the end.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: last lesson's frame, and a word you have had since the early chapters.
[once you have stopped driving — read: 要是你病了 就回家, aloud — yàoshi nǐ bìng le, jiù huí jiā]
[your turn — recall: say 话 — huà, words — R3, durable]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

You'll want to know: 的话.
的话 — dehuà — "if", closing a condition from behind.
下雨的话 我们就不去了 xiàyǔ dehuà, wǒmen jiù bú qù le — If it rains, we won't go.
要是你有时间的话 就来我家 yàoshi nǐ yǒu shíjiān dehuà, jiù lái wǒ jiā — If you've got time, come round.
Both characters are ones you write.

The word, taken apart.
的话 (dehuà) is built from 的 and 话 'words'. 话 is inherited from Middle Chinese hwaejH (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'speech'; the particle 的 is the one whose history is not settled. 'If' for the pair is the modern word's own sense, and its first syllable is light.

What you've built.
要是 (yàoshi) opens the condition from the front; 的话 (dehuà) closes it from behind. Use either, or both: 要是你有时间的话.

Grammar Lens: 看 and a noun, it depends on.
看 and a noun can say 'it depends on': what happens turns on that.
去不去 要看天气 qù bu qù, yào kàn tiānqì — Whether we go depends on the weather. 那要看你 nà yào kàn nǐ — That's up to you.
去不去, go or not, is the open question; 要看 and the noun says what settles it.

The exchange.
A: 明天你去山上吗 míngtiān nǐ qù shān shàng ma — Are you going up the mountain tomorrow? B: 要看天气 yào kàn tiānqì — It depends on the weather. A: tiānqì yùbào shuō kěnéng huì xiàyǔ — The forecast says it might rain. B: 要是下雨 我就不去了 yàoshi xiàyǔ, wǒ jiù bú qù le — If it rains, I won't go. A: 不下雨的话呢 bú xiàyǔ dehuà ne — And if it doesn't? B: 不下雨的话 我就去 bú xiàyǔ dehuà, wǒ jiù qù — If it doesn't, I'll go. A: 要是你有时间的话 就来我家 yàoshi nǐ yǒu shíjiān dehuà, jiù lái wǒ jiā — If you've got time, come round. B: 好 我马上就来 hǎo, wǒ mǎshàng jiù lái — All right, I'll be right over.
要是 (yàoshi) opens one condition, 的话 (dehuà) closes the next, and 就 (jiù) opens each result. 要看天气 answers the question before it is settled.

Reading — the exchange at once.

What you've built — if, and what it depends on.

要是你有时间的话 yàoshi nǐ yǒu shíjiān dehuà — both.
要看天气 yào kàn tiānqì — it depends.
