## You'll want to know: おこなう
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C285-MOYOOSHI285-01]; assesses=[] -->

**おこなう** — *okonau* — "to carry out; to hold (an event)".

> **らいしゅう まちで まつりを おこないます。** — *raishū machi de matsuri o okonaimasu* — "The town holds a festival next week."

Polite **おこないます**, *okonaimasu*; a godan verb. Past (polite): **がっこうが しけんを おこないました。**, *gakkō ga shiken o okonaimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**おこなう** (*okonau*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict).
