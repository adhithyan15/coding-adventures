## You'll want to know: el hallazgo
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C531-BUSQUEDA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**el hallazgo** — "the find, the discovery".

> Fue un hallazgo muy importante.
>
> *It was a very important find.*

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*hallazgo* is built on *hallar* 'to find', which is inherited from Latin *afflāre* 'to breathe on' (Lewis & Short).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.
