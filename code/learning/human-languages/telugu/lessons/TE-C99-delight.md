# ఆనందం (ānandaṁ) — delight, joy

## You'll want to know: ఆనందం
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C99-WELCOME-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ఆనందం** (*ānandaṁ*) — "delight, joy".

**మిమ్మల్ని చూసి ఆనందంగా ఉంది** — "I am delighted to see you". Stronger than **సంతోషం**, and
in the frame you know.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

A warmer word for glad.
