## You'll want to know: obligatorio
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C479-TRAMITE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**obligatorio** — "compulsory".

> El uso de casco es obligatorio.
>
> *Wearing a helmet is compulsory.*

The opposite on a notice is *opcional* or *voluntario*.
