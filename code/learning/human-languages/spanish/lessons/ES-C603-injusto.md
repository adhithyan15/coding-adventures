## You'll want to know: injusto
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C603-DERECHO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**injusto** — "unfair".

> Es injusto pagar más.
>
> *It is unfair to pay more.*

*Es injusto que* + subjunctive.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*injusto* is a learned borrowing from Latin *iniustus* 'unfair', built from *in-* 'not' and *iustus* 'right' (Lewis & Short).
