## You'll want to know: মঞ্চ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C527-MONCHO527-01]; assesses=[] -->

**মঞ্চ** — *mônchô* — "a stage".

> নাটকের আগে মঞ্চে আলো জ্বলল।
>
> *Nāṭôker āge mônche ālo jvôllô.*
>
> Before the play, the lights came on on the stage.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**মঞ্চ** (*mônchô*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *mañca*, "a raised platform, a stage" (Monier-Williams).
