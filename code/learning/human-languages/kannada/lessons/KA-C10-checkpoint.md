# Chapter 10 checkpoint — the week, ◌ೋ and ೨

chapter put on the page.

## Guided Practice — the week
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[KA-LEX-C10-VAARA-01] -->

[PAUSE 1s]
- [YOU SAY: the seven days, Monday to Sunday]

(*Sōmavāra, maṅgaḷavāra, budhavāra, guruvāra, śukravāra, śanivāra,
bhānuvāra*.)

- What ending does every day share, and what does it mean? (*-vāra*, "day.")
- Which day takes a different Sanskrit sun-name from Hindi's, and what are the
  two names? (Sunday: Kannada *bhānuvāra*, "light"; Hindi *ravivār*.)

## Script — ◌ೋ and ೨
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[KA-SCRIPT-RECOG-119, KA-SCRIPT-RECOG-141, KA-LEX-C07-ERADU-01] -->

- **ಸೋಮವಾರ** is Monday. Which mark in it gives its first consonant the *ō*,
  and what does that mark take away? (**◌ೋ**: it puts *ō* on and takes the
  built-in *a* off.)
- Which number is **೨**, and which word does it stand for? (**2**, *eraḍu*.)

[YOU READ: **ಸೋಮವಾರ** aloud, and point to the ◌ೋ in it]

[YOU READ: **೨** aloud as *eraḍu*]
