## You'll want to know: o alpinismo
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C507-RODAS-05]; assesses=[] -->

**o alpinismo** — "mountaineering".

> O alpinismo é perigoso sem experiência.
>
> *Mountaineering is dangerous without experience.*

A climber is *um alpinista*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*alpinismo* is built on the name of the Alps, the mountains where the sport grew up, with *-ismo*.
