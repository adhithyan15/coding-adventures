## You'll want to know: حرفه ای
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C382-PROZHE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**حرفه ای** (*herfe'i*) — "professional".

> او یک راننده حرفه ای است.
>
> *u yek rânande-ye herfe'i ast.*
>
> He is a professional driver.

*Herfe* "trade, profession" + *-i*, written apart. The opposite is *âmâtor* or *gheyr-e herfe'i*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**حرفه ای** is *herfe*, from Arabic *ḥirfa* "occupation" (EtymDB), with the Persian adjective suffix *-i*.
