## You'll want to know: いみ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C318-SETSUMEI318-04]; assesses=[] -->

**いみ** — *imi* — "a meaning".

> **いみが わからないから、せんせいに ききます。** — *imi ga wakaranai kara, sensei ni kikimasu* — "I don't know what it means, so I will ask the teacher."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**いみ** (*imi*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *i*, "mind" (Middle Chinese *'iH*) and *mi*, "taste" (*mj+jH*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
