# अपेक्षया (apekṣayā) — compared with

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[SA-GRAMMAR-C282-FEELING-01, SA-LEX-C15-ADJ-01, SA-LEX-C16-PLACE-05] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say "big". (*mahat*.) Then say "the city is big". (*nagaraṃ mahat asti*.) Now set it beside the village.

## You'll want to know: Y-अपेक्षया
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-GRAMMAR-C283-COMPARISON-01]; assesses=[SA-LEX-C16-PLACE-02, SA-LEX-C15-ADJ-01, SA-LEX-C89-DESCRIBE-01, SA-LEX-C81-PLANTS-04, SA-LEX-C16-PLACE-04, SA-LEX-C257-TRV257-02] -->

> **नगरं ग्रामापेक्षया महत्।**
>
> *nagaraṃ grāmāpekṣayā mahat.* — "The city is bigger than the village."

**अपेक्षया** (*apekṣayā*), "compared with", joins the end of the thing compared, as one word: *grāma* and *apekṣayā* meet as **ग्रामापेक्षया** (*grāmāpekṣayā*), two short *a*s making one long *ā*. Monier-Williams gives *apekṣayā* as "with regard to", at the end of a compound: with regard to the village, the city is big. The describing word stays as it is: **महत्**, "big", says "bigger" here.

> **उद्यानं वनापेक्षया सुन्दरम्।**
>
> *udyānaṃ vanāpekṣayā sundaram.* — "The garden is more beautiful than the forest."

> **धूमयानं लोकयानापेक्षया शीघ्रं गच्छति।**
>
> *dhūmayānaṃ lokayānāpekṣayā śīghraṃ gacchati.* — "The train goes faster than the bus."

## You'll want to know: -आत् with -तर, and the best
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-GRAMMAR-C283-COMPARISON-02]; assesses=[SA-LEX-C36-REPLY-02, SA-LEX-C78-FOOD-01, SA-LEX-C24-ASK-03, SA-GRAMMAR-ABLATIVE-ORIGIN-01] -->

The other way puts the thing compared in the *-āt* form you know from **भारतात्** (*bhāratāt*), "from India", and adds **-तर** (*-tara*), "more", to the describing word (Pāṇini 2.3.42):

> **नगरं ग्रामात् महत्तरम्।**
>
> *nagaraṃ grāmāt mahattaram.* — "The city is bigger than the village."

> **उद्यानं वनात् सुन्दरतरम्।**
>
> *udyānaṃ vanāt sundarataram.* — "The garden is more beautiful than the forest."

For the best of all, **सर्वेषु** (*sarveṣu*), "among all", with **श्रेष्ठः** (*śreṣṭhaḥ*), "best":

> **सर्वेषु फलेषु आम्रं श्रेष्ठम्।**
>
> *sarveṣu phaleṣu āmraṃ śreṣṭham.* — "Of all fruits, the mango is the best."

> **सर्वेषु मीरा श्रेष्ठा।**
>
> *sarveṣu mīrā śreṣṭhā.* — "Mira is the best of all."
