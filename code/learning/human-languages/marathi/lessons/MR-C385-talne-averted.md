## You'll want to know: टळणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C385-CHIGHALNE385-04]; assesses=[] -->

**टळणे** — *ṭaḷṇe* — "to be averted".

> थोडक्यात अपघात टळला.
>
> *thoḍkyāt apghāt ṭaḷlā.*
>
> An accident was narrowly averted.

Past (he …): तो टळला, *to ṭaḷlā*.

Intransitive: the danger is the subject; टाळणे, to avoid, is its causative partner.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**टळणे** (*ṭaḷṇe*) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi *ṭaḷṇẽ*, "to pass off", under *ṭalati*, "moves aside" (CDIAL 5450), beside *ṭāḷṇẽ*, "to avoid".
