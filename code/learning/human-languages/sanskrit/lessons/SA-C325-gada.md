## You'll want to know: गदा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C325-GADA325-01]; assesses=[] -->

**गदा** — *gadā* — "a mace; a club".

> वीरः हस्ते गदां धारयति।
>
> *vīraḥ haste gadāṃ dhārayati.*
>
> The hero holds a mace in his hand.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**गदा** (*gadā*) MW gives *gadā* as a mace, club or bludgeon, without a derivation, so its origin is uncertain (MW).
