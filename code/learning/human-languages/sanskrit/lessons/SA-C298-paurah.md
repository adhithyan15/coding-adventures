## You'll want to know: पौरः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C298-KSHATI298-04]; assesses=[] -->

**पौरः** — *pauraḥ* — "a citizen; a townsman".

> आहताः पौराः औषधं पिबन्ति।
>
> *āhatāḥ paurāḥ auṣadhaṃ pibanti.*
>
> The injured townspeople take medicine.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पौरः** (*pauraḥ*) is built on *pura*, "a town", with the lengthened first vowel that makes "belonging to"; MW gives *paura* as a townsman, a citizen (MW).
