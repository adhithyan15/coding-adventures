Japanese, chapter 25: Friend, Adult, Man, Woman, Teacher.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ともだち (tomodachi) — a friend.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for a son, then the Japanese for a daughter.

You'll want to know: ともだち.
ともだち — tomodachi — "a friend".

The word, taken apart.
ともだち (tomodachi) is a native compound, inherited rather than borrowed: tomo, "companion", with -dachi, the plural ending -tachi voiced, so it was once a plural, "companions" (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).

Lesson 5 of 5.
せんせい (sensei) — a teacher.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for a man, then the Japanese for a woman.

You'll want to know: せんせい.
せんせい — sensei — "a teacher".

The word, taken apart.
せんせい (sensei) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are sen, "first, before" (Middle Chinese sen) and sei, "life, birth" (srjaeng) (Unihan, KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese after Baxter–Sagart). Read literally, it is "born before".
