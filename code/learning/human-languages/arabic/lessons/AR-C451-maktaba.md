## You'll want to know: مكتبة
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C451-MAKHFAR451-02]; assesses=[] -->

**مكتبة** — *maktaba* — "a library; a bookshop".

> قرأت في المكتبة حتى المساء.
>
> *qaraʾtu fī al-maktaba ḥattā al-masāʾ.*
>
> I read in the library until the evening.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**مكتبة** (*maktaba*) is built on the native Arabic root *k-t-b*, "to write" (Lane). The pattern *mafʿala* makes it the place of books.
