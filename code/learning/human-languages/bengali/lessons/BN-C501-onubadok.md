## You'll want to know: অনুবাদক
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C501-BISHESHOGGO501-03]; assesses=[] -->

**অনুবাদক** — *ônubādôk* — "a translator".

> এই বইয়ের অনুবাদক একজন বিখ্যাত কবি।
>
> *Ei bôiyer ônubādôk ekjôn bikhyātô kôbi.*
>
> The translator of this book is a famous poet.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**অনুবাদক** (*ônubādôk*) is Sanskrit *anuvādaka*, "repeating with explanation" (Monier-Williams); "translator" is the modern sense.
