## You'll want to know: فلکه
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C445-TAGHATO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**فلکه** (*falake*) — "a roundabout (US: a traffic circle)".

> هتل ما کنار فلکه اول است.
>
> *Hotel-e mâ kenâr-e falake-ye avval ast.*
>
> Our hotel is next to the first roundabout.

Many big roundabouts are also called *meydân*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*falake* is usually traced to Arabic *falak* 'the turning sphere of the sky, a circle', with the Persian ending *-e*. EtymDB does not list it.
