## You'll want to know: আদর
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C449-SNEHO449-03]; assesses=[] -->

**আদর** — *ādôr* — "affectionate care; cuddling and petting".

> বাবা মেয়েকে আদর করলেন।
>
> *bābā meyeke ādôr kôrlen.*
>
> Father gave his daughter a cuddle.

আদর করা, to pet, to cuddle; আদরের, beloved, the pet of the family.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**আদর** (*ādôr*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *ādara*, "respect, regard, care" (Monier-Williams); Bengali uses it for warm, petting affection, a sense the dictionary does not give.
