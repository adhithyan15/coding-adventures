## You'll want to know: xiàng
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C124-BUILD124-05]; assesses=[] -->

*xiàng* — "to resemble; to take after".

> *wǒ xiàng wǒ bàba* — I take after my dad.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*xiàng* is inherited from Middle Chinese *zjangX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'to be like'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing things.
