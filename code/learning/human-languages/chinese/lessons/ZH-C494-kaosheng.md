## You'll want to know: 考生
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C494-GAOKAO494-02]; assesses=[] -->

**考生** — *kǎoshēng* — "exam candidate".

> *kǎoshēng dōu hěn jǐnzhāng* — The candidates are all nervous.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**考生** (*kǎoshēng*) is built from 考 'to examine' and 生 'a student', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *khawX* and *srjaeng* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'to grow'.
