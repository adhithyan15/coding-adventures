Gujarati, chapter 194: Crossing at the corner.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
નાકું (nākũ) — street corner, end of a road.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for straight, then the Gujarati for to turn.

You'll want to know: નાકું.
નાકું — nākũ — "street corner, end of a road".
દુકાન ગલીના નાકે છે. — dukān galīnā nāke che — "The shop is at the end of the lane."
A neuter noun; નાકે, nāke, 'at the corner'.

The word, taken apart.
નાકું (nākũ) is a tadbhava. CDIAL lists it, glossed "eye of a needle, end of a road, passage", under nākka, "like a nose, projecting", a reconstructed form (CDIAL 7037).

Lesson 3 of 6.
સામે (sāme) — opposite, facing.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for street corner, then the Gujarati for traffic signal.

You'll want to know: સામે.
સામે — sāme — "opposite, facing".
બેંક સ્ટેશનની સામે છે. — bẽk sṭeśannī sāme che — "The bank is opposite the station."

The word, taken apart.
સામે (sāme) is a tadbhava. It comes from Sanskrit saṁmukhá, "facing, present", and CDIAL lists Gujarati sāmũ, "facing" (CDIAL 12982).

Lesson 4 of 6.
બાજુ (bāju) — side.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for traffic signal, then the Gujarati for opposite.

You'll want to know: બાજુ.
બાજુ — bāju — "side".
હોટેલ મંદિરની બાજુમાં છે. — hoṭel mandirnī bājumā̃ che — "The hotel is next to the temple."
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
બાજુ (bāju) is a loan, usually traced to Persian bāzū, "arm", and so "side". CDIAL has no entry for it.

ઓળંગવું — oḷangvũ — "to cross".
બાળકો સિગ્નલ પાસે રસ્તો ઓળંગે છે. — bāḷko signal pāse rasto oḷange che — "Children cross the road at the signal."

ઓળંગવું (oḷangvũ) is a tadbhava. CDIAL lists it as oḷãghvũ, "to cross, violate", under avalaṅghati, a reconstructed form built on laṅgh, "to leap" (CDIAL 824).
