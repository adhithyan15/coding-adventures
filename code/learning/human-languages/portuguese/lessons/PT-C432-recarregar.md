## You'll want to know: recarregar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C432-LUZ-04]; assesses=[] -->

**recarregar** — "to recharge; to reload".

> Recarrego o telemóvel durante a noite.
>
> *I recharge my phone overnight.*

Also to top up a travel card: *recarregar o passe*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*recarregar* is built from *re-* and *carregar* 'to load' (EtymDB), which goes back to Latin *carrus* 'cart'.
