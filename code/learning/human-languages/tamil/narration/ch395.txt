Tamil, chapter 395: Taking Action.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
நடத்து (naṭattu) — to conduct; to hold; to run.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for on behalf of, then the Tamil for about.

You'll want to know: நடத்து.
நடத்து — naṭattu — "to conduct; to hold; to run".
நல்ல வானிலை இருந்தால் பள்ளி விளையாட்டுப் போட்டி நடத்தும். nalla vāṉilai iruntāl paḷḷi viḷaiyāṭṭup pōṭṭi naṭattum. If the weather is good, the school will hold a sports meet.
Past (I …): நடத்தினேன், naṭattiṉēṉ.
கூட்டம் நடத்து (naṭattu), to hold a meeting. It is the causative of நட, to walk: to make things walk.

The word, taken apart.
நடத்து (naṭattu) is native Dravidian: DEDR 3582 lists Tamil naṭa, "to walk, proceed, happen", and its causative naṭattu, "to make walk, conduct, manage".

Lesson 2 of 5.
உருவாக்கு (uruvākku) — to create; to form.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for about, then the Tamil for to conduct.

You'll want to know: உருவாக்கு.
உருவாக்கு — uruvākku — "to create; to form".
நாம் ஒன்றாக வேலை செய்தால் ஒரு நல்ல திட்டத்தை உருவாக்கலாம். nām oṉṟāka vēlai ceytāl oru nalla tiṭṭattai uruvākkalām. If we work together, we can create a good plan.
Past (I …): உருவாக்கினேன், uruvākkiṉēṉ.
உரு, form + ஆக்கு, to make. Its intransitive partner is உருவாகு, to take shape.

The word, taken apart.
உருவாக்கு (uruvākku) is built from uru, "form", borrowed from Sanskrit rūpa (CDIAL 10803), and native ākku, "to make", the causative of āku (DEDR 333).

Lesson 3 of 5.
நீக்கு (nīkku) — to remove; to dismiss.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for to conduct, then the Tamil for to create.

You'll want to know: நீக்கு.
நீக்கு — nīkku — "to remove; to dismiss".
தவறுகளை நீக்கினால் இந்தக் கட்டுரை நன்றாக இருக்கும். tavaṟukaḷai nīkkiṉāl intak kaṭṭurai naṉṟāka irukkum. If the mistakes are removed, this essay will be good.
Past (I …): நீக்கினேன், nīkkiṉēṉ.
What is removed takes -ஐ; வேலையிலிருந்து நீக்கு (nīkku), to dismiss from a job.

The word, taken apart.
நீக்கு (nīkku) is native Dravidian: DEDR 3685 lists Tamil nī, "to separate from, put away, abandon", and nīkku, "to remove".

Lesson 4 of 5.
ஒழி (oḻi) — to abolish; to root out.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for to create, then the Tamil for to remove.

You'll want to know: ஒழி.
ஒழி — oḻi — "to abolish; to root out".
எல்லோரும் சேர்ந்தால் ஊழலை ஒழிக்கலாம். ellōrum cērntāl ūḻalai oḻikkalām. If everyone joins together, we can root out corruption.
Past (I …): ஒழித்தேன், oḻittēṉ.
Transitive with -ஐ and the past ஒழித்தேன்; the intransitive ஒழி (oḻi), to cease, has ஒழிந்தது.

The word, taken apart.
ஒழி (oḻi) is native Dravidian: DEDR 1009 lists Tamil oḻi, "to cease, be finished" with the past in -ந்த-, and "to finish, bring to an end" with the past in -த்த-.

Lesson 5 of 5.
கைவிடு (kaiviṭu) — to abandon; to give up.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for to remove, then the Tamil for to abolish.

You'll want to know: கைவிடு.
கைவிடு — kaiviṭu — "to abandon; to give up".
மழை பெய்ததால் நாங்கள் பயணத்தைக் கைவிட்டோம். maḻai peytatāl nāṅkaḷ payaṇattaik kaiviṭṭōm. Because it rained, we gave up the trip.
Past (I …): கைவிட்டேன், kaiviṭṭēṉ.
What is given up takes -ஐ: பழக்கத்தைக் கைவிடு (kaiviṭu), to give up a habit.

The word, taken apart.
கைவிடு (kaiviṭu) is native: kai, "hand" (DEDR 2023), and viṭu, "to let go, leave" (DEDR 5393): to let something go from the hand.
