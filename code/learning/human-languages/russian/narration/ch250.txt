Russian, chapter 250: Watermelon, Plum, Pasta, Cake, Sweet.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
арбуз (arbúz) — a watermelon.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for lamb (meat), then the Russian for ham.

You'll want to know: арбуз.
арбуз (arbúz) — "a watermelon".

The word, taken apart.
арбуз (arbúz) — recorded in the Domostroy; it came through Kipchak xarbuz and Turkish karpuz from Persian xarbuza, "a melon", literally "donkey melon" (Vasmer).

Lesson 2 of 5.
слива (slíva) — a plum.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for ham, then the Russian for a watermelon.

You'll want to know: слива.
слива (slíva) — "a plum".

The word, taken apart.
слива (slíva) from Proto-Slavic sliva — inherited; it first named a colour (Vasmer). Vasmer relates it to Latin līvidus, "bluish", and Old High German slêha, "sloe": English sloe is a cousin.

Lesson 3 of 5.
макароны (makaróny) — pasta.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a watermelon, then the Russian for a plum.

You'll want to know: макароны.
макароны (makaróny) — "pasta".

The word, taken apart.
макароны (makaróny) comes, like German Makkaroni and French macaroni, from Italian maccaroni, which is traced to Greek makaría, "a dish of barley" (Vasmer).

Lesson 4 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a plum, then the Russian for pasta.

You'll want to know: торт.
торт (tort) — "a cake".

The word, taken apart.
торт (tort) apparently came through German Torte from Italian torta, from Latin tortus, "twisted", that is, a round piece of pastry (Vasmer).

You'll want to know: конфета.
конфета (konféta) — "a sweet".

The word, taken apart.
конфета (konféta) — first конфекты, under Peter the Great, through German Konfekt; the form without -к- came through Italian confetto, from Latin cōnfectum, "something prepared" (Vasmer).
