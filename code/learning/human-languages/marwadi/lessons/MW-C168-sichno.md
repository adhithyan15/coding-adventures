## You'll want to know: सींचणो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C168-SURAJ168-05]; assesses=[] -->

**सींचणो** — *sī̃chṇo* — "to water plants".

> हूं रोज तुळसी सींचूं हूं।
>
> *hū̃ roj tuḷsī sī̃chū̃ hū̃.*
>
> I water the holy basil every day.

Past (I …): म्हैं तुळसी सींची, *mhaĩ tuḷsī sī̃chī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सींचणो** (*sī̃chṇo*) is inherited from Sanskrit *siñcati*, "pours out, sprinkles"; CDIAL lists Hindi *sī̃cnā* and Gujarati *sĩcvũ* (CDIAL 13394). CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry.
