# ಖುಷಿ (khuṣi) — gladness, happiness

## You'll want to know: ಖುಷಿ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C315-KHUSI-01]; assesses=[KA-LEX-C32-NOODU-01, KA-LEX-C73-ABLE-01, KA-GRAMMAR-C310-CONJUNCTIVE-01, KA-GRAMMAR-C06-DATIVE-SUBJECT-02] -->

**ಖುಷಿ** — *khuṣi* — "gladness, happiness".

> **ನಿಮ್ಮನ್ನು ನೋಡಿ ನನಗೆ ತುಂಬಾ ಖುಷಿ ಆಯಿತು.**
>
> *nimmannu nōḍi nanage tumbā khuṣi āyitu.* — "I was very glad to see you."

**ನಿಮ್ಮನ್ನು** (*nimmannu*, "you"), as the one seen; **ನೋಡಿ**, "having seen". The gladness "happened" to me: **ನನಗೆ … ಖುಷಿ ಆಯಿತು**, with the **ನನಗೆ** of **ನನಗೆ ಕನ್ನಡ ಗೊತ್ತು**.

**ಖುಷಿ** and **ಸಂತೋಷ** both mean happiness. **ಸಂತೋಷ** is also the word you say on meeting someone; in everyday talk about how you feel you will hear **ಖುಷಿ** a great deal.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಖುಷಿ** (*khuṣi*) is a loan, usually traced to Persian *khushī*, "happiness", from *khush*, "glad, pleasant". The same word is everyday Hindi and Urdu. It is not the only Persian piece in words you know: the *havā* of **ಹವಾಮಾನ**, "weather", is Persian for "air".
