## You'll want to know: संपणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C401-INDHAN401-05]; assesses=[] -->

**संपणे** — *sampṇe* — "to run out; to come to an end".

> रस्त्यात पेट्रोल संपलं.
>
> *rastyāt peṭrol samplaṁ.*
>
> The petrol ran out on the way.

Past (he …): तो संपला, *to samplā*.

What runs out is the subject: पैसे संपले, the money ran out; सुट्टी संपली, the holiday ended.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**संपणे** (*sampṇe*) is a common Marathi verb, "to be finished"; its origin is not settled.
