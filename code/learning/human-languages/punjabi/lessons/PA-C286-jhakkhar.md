# ਝੱਖੜ (jhakkhaṛ) — a squall; a gale

## You'll want to know: ਝੱਖੜ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C286-STORM286-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ਝੱਖੜ** — *jhakkhaṛ* — "a squall; a gale".

> ਝੱਖੜ ਨਾਲ ਪੁਰਾਣਾ ਰੁੱਖ ਡਿੱਗੇਗਾ।
>
> *jhakkhaṛ nāl purāṇā rukkh ḍiggegā.*
>
> The old tree will fall in the squall.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਝੱਖੜ** (*jhakkhaṛ*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*jhakk*, "sudden movement or blast"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *jhakkhaṛ*, "squall", under it (CDIAL 5316).
