## You'll want to know: a lenda
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C326-LENDA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**a lenda** — "the legend".

> Segundo a lenda, ninguém pode entrar na gruta à noite.
>
> *According to the legend, nobody may go into the cave at night.*

*Conta a lenda que…* — legend has it that…

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*lenda* comes down from Old Portuguese *leenda* (EtymDB), from Medieval Latin *legenda* "things to be read", from *legere* "to read".
