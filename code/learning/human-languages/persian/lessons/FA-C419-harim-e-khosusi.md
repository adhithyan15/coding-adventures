## You'll want to know: حریم خصوصی
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C419-HARIM-01]; assesses=[] -->

**حریم خصوصی** (*harim-e khosusi*) — "privacy".

> این برنامه به حریم خصوصی ما احترام نمیگذارد.
>
> *in barnâme be harim-e khosusi-ye mâ ehterâm nemigozârad.*
>
> This app does not respect our privacy.

*Harim* "protected space" + ezafe + *khosusi* "private".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**حریم خصوصی** is literally "the private protected space": *harim* is usually traced to Arabic *ḥarīm* "a protected precinct", and *khosusi* to Arabic *khuṣūṣī* "particular, private".
