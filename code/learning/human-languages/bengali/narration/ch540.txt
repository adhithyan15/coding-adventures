Bengali, chapter 540: Paying the Price.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
মাশুল (māshul) — a fee; the price paid (for something).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for to give up hope, then the Bengali for to get tangled up.

You'll want to know: মাশুল.
মাশুল — māshul — "a fee; the price paid (for something)".
পার্সেলটা ভারী হলে বেশি মাশুল লাগবে। Pārselṭā bhārī hôle beshi māshul lāgbe. If the parcel is heavy, the fee will be higher.

The word, taken apart.
মাশুল (māshul) is usually traced to Arabic maḥṣūl, "revenue, produce", through Persian.

You'll want to know: চুকানো.
চুকানো — chukāno — "to settle; to finish off (a matter)".
আমরা ঝামেলাটা আজই চুকালাম। Āmrā jhāmelāṭā āji chukālām. We settled the trouble that very day.
Past (he/she …): তিনি দোকানের ধার চুকালেন, tini dokāner dhār chukālen.

The word, taken apart.
চুকানো (chukāno) comes from a reconstructed cukk, "stop"; CDIAL lists Bengali cukāna, "to get rid of, to finish, to settle" (CDIAL 4848).
