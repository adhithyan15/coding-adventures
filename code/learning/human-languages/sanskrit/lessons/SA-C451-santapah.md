## You'll want to know: सन्तापः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C451-ANUNAYA451-05]; assesses=[] -->

**सन्तापः** — *santāpaḥ* — "anguish".

> मम हृदये महान् सन्तापः अस्ति।
>
> *mama hṛdaye mahān santāpaḥ asti.*
>
> There is great anguish in my heart.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सन्तापः** (*santāpaḥ*) is built from *sam* and the root √tap, "to burn", the root of **तापः**: a burning through and through; MW gives "affliction, sorrow, anguish" (MW).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
