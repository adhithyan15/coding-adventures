## You'll want to know: शिंकणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C481-SARDI481-03]; assesses=[] -->

**शिंकणे** — *śiṅkṇe* — "to sneeze".

> धुळीमुळे आजोबा सारखे शिंकत होते.
>
> *dhuḷīmuḷe ājobā sārkhe śiṅkat hote.*
>
> Grandfather kept sneezing because of the dust.

Past (he …): तो शिंकला, *to śiṅklā*.

शिंक, a sneeze; शिंक येणे, with the dative.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**शिंकणे** (*śiṅkṇe*) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Marathi *śikṇẽ*, "sneeze", under Sanskrit *chikkā* (CDIAL 5032).
