## You'll want to know: el talento
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C766-RETO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**el talento** — "talent".

> Tiene mucho talento para la música.
>
> *She has a great talent for music.*

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*talento* was borrowed from Latin *talentum*, from Greek *tálanton* "a balance, a weight of silver" (EtymDB); the parable of the talents turned money into gifts.
