## You'll want to know: शिस्त
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C474-SHISTA474-05]; assesses=[] -->

**शिस्त** — *śista* — "discipline".

> सैन्यात कडक शिस्त असते.
>
> *sainyāt kaḍak śista aste.*
>
> There is strict discipline in the army.

Feminine.

शिस्त लावणे, to instil discipline; शिस्तीने, in an orderly way.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**शिस्त** (*śista*) is a Marathi noun whose origin is not settled.
