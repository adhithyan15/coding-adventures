## You'll want to know: चोळणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C490-PATHYA490-05]; assesses=[] -->

**चोळणे** — *coḷṇe* — "to rub".

> थंडीत त्याने हात चोळले.
>
> *thaṇḍīt tyāne hāt coḷle.*
>
> He rubbed his hands in the cold.

Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने हात चोळले, *tyāne hāt coḷle*.

Of oil or a sore spot: तेल चोळणे, to rub in oil.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**चोळणे** (*coḷṇe*) is a Marathi verb whose origin is not settled.
