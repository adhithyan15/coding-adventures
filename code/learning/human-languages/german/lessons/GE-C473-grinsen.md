## You'll want to know: grinsen
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C473-KICHERN-02]; assesses=[] -->

**grinsen** — "to grin".

> Er grinste, als er seine gute Note sah.
>
> *He grinned when he saw his good mark.*

Often a little cheeky: *frech grinsen*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*grinsen* is usually traced to Middle High German *grinsen*, beside *grīnen* "to bare the teeth"; its older origin is not settled, and EtymDB has no entry.
