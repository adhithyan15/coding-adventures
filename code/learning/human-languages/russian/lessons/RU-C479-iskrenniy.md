## You'll want to know: искренний
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C479-TRUST-05]; assesses=[] -->

**искренний** (*ískrenniy*) — "sincere".

> Она дала мне искренний совет.
>
> *Oná dalá mne ískrenniy sovét.*
>
> She gave me sincere advice.

Soft adjective: искренняя, искреннее. The adverb is *искренне* — sincerely.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*искренний* is inherited from Proto-Slavic *iskrьnjь* (EtymDB), usually glossed 'near, close'.
