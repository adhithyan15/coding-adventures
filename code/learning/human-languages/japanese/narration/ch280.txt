Japanese, chapter 280: What the News Said.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
きじ (kiji) — a newspaper article; a report.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for to prevent, then the Japanese for big.

You'll want to know: きじ.
きじ — kiji — "a newspaper article; a report".
今 しんぶんの きじを よんでいます。 — ima shinbun no kiji o yonde imasu — "I am reading a newspaper article now."

The word, taken apart.
きじ (kiji) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are ki, "account" (Middle Chinese kiH) and ji, "matter" (dzriH) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).

Lesson 2 of 5.
のせる (noseru) — to print (in a paper); to put on.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for big, then the Japanese for a newspaper article.

You'll want to know: のせる.
のせる — noseru — "to print (in a paper); to put on".
しんぶんが じけんの しゃしんを のせました。 — shinbun ga jiken no shashin o nosemashita — "The paper ran a photo of the incident."
Polite のせます, nosemasu; an ichidan verb. Past (polite): しんぶんが しゃしんを のせました。, shinbun ga shashin o nosemashita.

The word, taken apart.
のせる (noseru) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict).

Lesson 3 of 5.
できごと (dekigoto) — an event; a happening.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for a newspaper article, then the Japanese for to print (in a paper).

You'll want to know: できごと.
できごと — dekigoto — "an event; a happening".
きのうの できごとを はなして ください。 — kinō no dekigoto o hanashite kudasai — "Please tell me what happened yesterday."

The word, taken apart.
できごと (dekigoto) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, built from deki, "coming about" (from dekiru), and goto, "matter" (from koto) (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
