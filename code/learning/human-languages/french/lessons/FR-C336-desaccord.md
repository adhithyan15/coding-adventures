## You'll want to know: le désaccord
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C336-DESACCORD-01]; assesses=[] -->

**le désaccord** — "the disagreement".

> Nous sommes en désaccord sur presque tout.
>
> *We disagree about almost everything.*

*Être en désaccord* — to disagree. The opposite is *être d'accord*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*désaccord* is built from *dés-* "not" and *accord* "agreement", which comes from the Old French verb *acorder* (EtymDB). That verb is usually traced to a Latin formation on *cor, cordis* "heart".
