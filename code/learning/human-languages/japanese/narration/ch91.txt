[pause 3 seconds]

(あの, ano.) Which? (どの, dono.) This kind of? (こんな, konna.) That kind of? (あんな, anna.) What kind of? (どんな, donna.) Other? (ほか, hoka.) Everybody? (みんな, minna.) This sort of? (こういう, kouiu.) That sort of? (ああいう, aaiu.) To hit? (うつ, utsu.) To breathe in? (すう, suu.) To sow; to wind? (まく, maku.) To cook? (たく, taku.) To put on? (はく, haku.) To bloom? (さく, saku.) To solve? (とく, toku.) To hold in one's arms? (だく, daku.) To step on? (ふむ, fumu.) To bite? (かむ, kamu.)
[question — say your answer, then pause 10 seconds]
