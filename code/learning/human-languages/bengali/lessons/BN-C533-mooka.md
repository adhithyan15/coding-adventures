## You'll want to know: মওকা
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C533-DOIBAT533-03]; assesses=[] -->

**মওকা** — *môokā* — "a lucky break; an opening".

> দোকানে ছাড় ছিল, আমি মওকা বুঝে একটা জ্যাকেট কিনলাম।
>
> *Dokāne chhāṛ chhilô, āmi môokā bujhe ekṭā jækeṭ kinlām.*
>
> There was a discount at the shop; I saw my chance and bought a jacket.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**মওকা** (*môokā*) is usually traced to Arabic *mawqiʿ*, "a place, an occasion", through Persian.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
