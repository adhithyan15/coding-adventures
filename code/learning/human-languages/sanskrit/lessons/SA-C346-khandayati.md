## You'll want to know: खण्डयति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C346-AKSHEPA346-02]; assesses=[] -->

**खण्डयति** — *khaṇḍayati* — "refutes".

> पण्डितः अरुणस्य युक्तिं खण्डयति।
>
> *paṇḍitaḥ aruṇasya yuktiṃ khaṇḍayati.*
>
> The scholar refutes Arun's argument.

Past (he …): सः युक्तिं खण्डितवान्, *saḥ yuktiṃ khaṇḍitavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**खण्डयति** (*khaṇḍayati*) is built on *khaṇḍa*, "a piece, a part"; MW gives the verb the senses "to break, to tear" and "to refute" (MW).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
