## You'll want to know: پائیدار
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C545-MUSTAQIL545-05]; assesses=[] -->

**پائیدار** — *pāedār* — "durable; hard-wearing".

> اگر جوتا پائیدار ہوتا تو ابھی تک چلتا۔
>
> *agar jūtā pāedār hotā to abhī tak chaltā.*
>
> If the shoe were well made, it would still be going.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**پائیدار** (*pāedār*) is usually traced to Persian *pāydār*, "lasting, firm", built from *pāy*, "a foot", and *-dār*, "holding": keeping its footing.
