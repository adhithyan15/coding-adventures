## You'll want to know: ਟੀਮ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C351-BALL351-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ਟੀਮ** — *ṭīm* — "a team".

> ਸਾਡੀ ਟੀਮ ਬਹੁਤ ਤਕੜੀ ਹੈ।
>
> *sāḍī ṭīm bahut takṛī hai.*
>
> Our team is very strong.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਟੀਮ** (*ṭīm*) is borrowed from English *team*.
