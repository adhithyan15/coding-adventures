# كنت سعيدا (kuntu saʿīdan) — I was happy

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[AR-GRAMMAR-C288-PROBLEM-PAST-01, AR-LEX-C288-SAFAR288-03] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "I missed the train", then "who is in charge here?".
(*Fātanī al-qiṭār*, *man al-masʾūl hunā?*)

[PAUSE 1s] A story of a trip is not finished until you say how it felt.

## Grammar Lens: kāna and the -an
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-GRAMMAR-C289-KANA-ACCUSATIVE-01]; assesses=[AR-LEX-C54-HEALTH54-02, AR-LEX-C54-HEALTH54-03, AR-LEX-C147-QUAL147-02, AR-LEX-C147-QUAL147-05] -->

Today needs no verb: *anā saʿīd*, I'm happy. In the past *kāna* comes in,
and the word that describes takes *-an*, the accusative:

> **كنت سعيدا.** — *kuntu saʿīdan* — I was happy.

> **كانت حزينة.** — *kānat ḥazīna* — She was sad.

On most words the *-an* is written with a final **ا**: **سعيدا**, **متعبا**,
**غاضبا**. On a word ending in **ة** it is not written, and at the end of a
sentence it is not said: *kānat ḥazīna*. You met it already in *kuntu
mutʿaban* and *kāna aṭ-ṭaqs jamīlan*.

## Grammar Lens: I felt
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-GRAMMAR-C289-SHAARTU-BI-01]; assesses=[AR-LEX-C206-SABAB206-05, AR-LEX-C161-ACT161-05, AR-GRAMMAR-C284-INDAMA-01, AR-LEX-C288-SAFAR288-02] -->

To name the feeling as a noun, say *shaʿartu bi-*, "I felt", with the
feeling after *bi-*:

> **شعرت بالخوف عندما طارت الطائرة.** — *shaʿartu bi-l-khawf ʿindamā ṭārat
> aṭ-ṭāʾira* — I felt afraid when the plane took off.

Word for word, "I felt fear". *Aḥsastu bi-*, from *aḥassa*, to feel, says the
same: *aḥsastu bi-l-khawf*.
