## You'll want to know: مزیت
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C375-MAZIYYAT-01]; assesses=[] -->

**مزیت** (*maziyat*) — "an advantage, a merit".

> مزیت این کار این است که نزدیک خانه است.
>
> *maziyat-e in kâr in ast ke nazdik-e khâne ast.*
>
> The advantage of this job is that it is near home.

Its Arabic broken plural *mazâyâ* also means benefits at work.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**مزیت** is usually traced to Arabic *maziyya* "merit, distinction", the quality that sets one thing above another.
