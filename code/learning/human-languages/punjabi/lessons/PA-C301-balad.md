## You'll want to know: ਬਲਦ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C301-PLOUGH301-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ਬਲਦ** — *balad* — "an ox; a bullock".

> ਸਾਡੇ ਕੋਲ ਦੋ ਬਲਦ ਹਨ।
>
> *sāḍe kol do balad han.*
>
> We have two oxen.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਬਲਦ** (*balad*) is inherited from Sanskrit *balivarda*, "ox, bull"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *bald*, "ox", under it (CDIAL 9176).
