Telugu, chapter 459: Meals and Comforts.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ఫలహారం (phalahāraṁ) — a snack; a light meal.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a toilet, then the Telugu for drinking water.

You'll want to know: ఫలహారం.
ఫలహారం — phalahāraṁ — "a snack; a light meal".
ప్రయాణంలో అమ్మ ఫలహారం తెచ్చింది. prayāṇaṁlō amma phalahāraṁ teccindi. Mother brought a snack for the journey.
In Sanskrit it meant living on fruit; in Telugu it is any light meal, such as tiffin.

The word, taken apart.
ఫలహారం (phalahāraṁ) is borrowed from Sanskrit phalāhāra, "feeding on fruit" (Monier-Williams), from phala, "fruit", and āhāra, "food".

Lesson 2 of 5.
భోజనశాల (bhōjanaśāla) — a dining hall.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for drinking water, then the Telugu for a snack.

You'll want to know: భోజనశాల.
భోజనశాల — bhōjanaśāla — "a dining hall".
హోటలు భోజనశాల కింది అంతస్తులో ఉంది. hōṭalu bhōjanaśāla kindi antastulō undi. The hotel dining hall is on the ground floor.
భోజనం, a meal, + శాల, a hall.

The word, taken apart.
భోజనశాల (bhōjanaśāla) is a compound of two words borrowed from Sanskrit: bhojana, "eating, a meal", and śālā, "a shed, a hall" (CDIAL 12414).

Lesson 3 of 5.
సదుపాయం (sadupāyaṁ) — a facility; an amenity.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a snack, then the Telugu for a dining hall.

You'll want to know: సదుపాయం.
సదుపాయం — sadupāyaṁ — "a facility; an amenity".
ఆ హోటలులో అన్ని సదుపాయాలు ఉన్నాయి. ā hōṭalulō anni sadupāyālu unnāyi. That hotel has every amenity.
Plural: సదుపాయాలు, sadupāyālu.
Plural సదుపాయాలు, the -ం noun plural. సౌకర్యం is a near twin.

The word, taken apart.
సదుపాయం (sadupāyaṁ) is built from two Sanskrit words: sat, "good", and upāya, "a means, an approach" (CDIAL 2308): a good means to an end.

Lesson 4 of 5.
అంతర్జాలం (antarjālaṁ) — the web; the internet.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a dining hall, then the Telugu for a facility.

You'll want to know: అంతర్జాలం.
అంతర్జాలం — antarjālaṁ — "the web; the internet".
ఆ సత్రంలో అంతర్జాలం లేదు. ā satraṁlō antarjālaṁ lēdu. There is no internet at that inn.
A formal word made for the internet; in speech people mostly say ఇంటర్నెట్.

The word, taken apart.
అంతర్జాలం (antarjālaṁ) is a modern Telugu coinage built from two Sanskrit words: antar, "between, inside", and jāla, "a net" (CDIAL 5213).
