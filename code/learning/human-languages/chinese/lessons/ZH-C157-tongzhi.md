## You'll want to know: tōngzhī
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C157-TONGZHI-01]; assesses=[] -->

*tōngzhī* — "to notify; a notice".

> *lǎoshī tōngzhī wǒmen míngtiān kāihuì* — The teacher let us know there's a meeting tomorrow.

> *zhè shì yí ge tōngzhī* — This is a notice.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*tōngzhī* is built from tōng 'to get through' and zhī 'to know', the zhī of *zhīdào*. Both are inherited from Middle Chinese, *thuwng* and *trje* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'to reach' and 'to perceive': making something known.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ZH-LEX-C157-KAIHUI-01] -->

As a verb, *tōngzhī* takes the people told, then the news: *tōngzhī wǒmen míngtiān kāihuì*.

As a noun it is the notice itself, pinned up or sent round.
