Arabic, chapter 196: To plant, To pick, To rain, To congratulate, To trust.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
زرع (zaraʿa) — to plant.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to get lost, then the Arabic for to show the way.

You'll want to know: زرع.
زرع — zaraʿa — "to plant".

The word, taken apart.
زرع (zaraʿa) is built on the native Arabic root z-r-ʿ, "to sow" (Lane). Zaraʿa is the plain verb, "he sowed".
