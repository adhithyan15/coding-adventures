## You'll want to know: einweihen
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C305-FEIER-05]; assesses=[] -->

**einweihen** — "to open officially, to inaugurate; to let in on".

> Am Sonntag weihte die Stadt die neue Brücke ein.
>
> *On Sunday the town officially opened the new bridge.*

Separable: *sie weihen ein*. Also to let someone in on a secret: *jemanden einweihen*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*einweihen* is built on *weihen* "to make holy", Middle High German *wīhen*, from Proto-Germanic *wīhijaną* "to hallow" (EtymDB).
