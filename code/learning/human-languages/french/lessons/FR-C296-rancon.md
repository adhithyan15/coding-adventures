## You'll want to know: la rançon
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C296-BRAQUAGE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**la rançon** — "the ransom".

> La famille a payé une rançon.
>
> *The family paid a ransom.*

*La rançon du succès* — the price of success.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*rançon* comes from Old French *rençon* (EtymDB), which is inherited from Latin *redēmptiō* "a buying back", from *redimere* (Lewis & Short).
