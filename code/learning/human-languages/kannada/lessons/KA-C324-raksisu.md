# ರಕ್ಷಿಸು (rakṣisu) — to rescue; to protect

## Warm-up

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Kannada for presence of mind, then the Kannada for to extinguish.

## You'll want to know: ರಕ್ಷಿಸು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C324-BENKI324-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ರಕ್ಷಿಸು** — *rakṣisu* — "to rescue; to protect".

> ಅಗ್ನಿಶಾಮಕರು ಆ ಮನೆಯಿಂದ ಮೂರು ಮಕ್ಕಳನ್ನು ರಕ್ಷಿಸಿದರು.
>
> *agniśāmakaru ā maneyinda mūru makkaḷannu rakṣisidaru.*
>
> The firefighters rescued three children from that house.

Past (I …): ರಕ್ಷಿಸಿದೆ, *rakṣiside*.

Who is rescued takes -ಅನ್ನು; what from takes -ಇಂದ.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ರಕ್ಷಿಸು** (*rakṣisu*) is built on Sanskrit *rakṣaṇa*, "guarding, tending, protection" (CDIAL 10546), with the Kannada verb ending -*isu*.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
