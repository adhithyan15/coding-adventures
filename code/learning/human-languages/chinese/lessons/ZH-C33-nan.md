## You'll want to know: 男
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C33-FAMILY33-01]; assesses=[] -->

**男** — *nán* — "male".

> **我有一个男同学** *wǒ yǒu yí ge nán tóngxué* — One of my classmates is a boy.

The new character, in pieces: 男 — a field on top, built like 日 with a cross inside, and below it a two-stroke piece meaning strength. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**男** (*nán*) is inherited from Middle Chinese *nom* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'a man'.
