## You'll want to know: पाटी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C284-BASTO284-02]; assesses=[] -->

**पाटी** — *pāṭī* — "a slate".

> म्हारी पाटी काळी है।
>
> *mhārī pāṭī kāḷī hai.*
>
> My slate is black.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पाटी** (*pāṭī*) is inherited from Sanskrit *paṭṭikā*, "a slab, a tablet"; CDIAL lists Old Marwari *pāṭī*, "plank", and Kumaoni *pāṭī*, "board, writing board" (CDIAL 7699).
