## You'll want to know: o aumento
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C360-PROMOCAO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**o aumento** — "the rise, the increase; the pay rise".

> Pedi um aumento ao meu chefe.
>
> *I asked my boss for a pay rise.*

*Um aumento de salário* — a pay rise.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*aumento* comes from Latin *augmentum* "an increase, growth" (EtymDB, Lewis & Short), built on *augēre* "to increase".
