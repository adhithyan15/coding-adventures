## You'll want to know: прищепка
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C530-LAUNDRY-02]; assesses=[] -->

**прищепка** (*prishchépka*) — "a clothes peg".

> На верёвке висит бельё на прищепках.
>
> *Na veryóvke visít belyó na prishchépkakh.*
>
> The washing is hanging on the line with pegs.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*прищепка* is built on *прищепить* 'to pin on, to clip', usually linked to *щепа* 'a chip of wood'; EtymDB has no entry.
