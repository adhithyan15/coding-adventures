Mandarin Chinese, chapter 211: Local or From Out of Town.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
本地 (běndì) — local.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for fitting room, then the Mandarin for to choose.

You'll want to know: 本地.
本地 — běndì — "local".
我们找一个本地人问路 wǒmen zhǎo yí ge běndì rén wènlù — Let's find someone local and ask the way.

The word, taken apart.
本地 (běndì) is built from 本 'root, one's own' and 地 'place', both inherited from Middle Chinese, pwonX and dijH (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first as the root of a tree and 'the beginning'.

What you've built.
One more word for asking where things are.

Guided Practice.

[your turn — say: běndì]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: běndì, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: wǒmen zhǎo yí ge běndì rén wènlù — Let's find someone local and ask the way.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Mandarin for fitting room, then the Mandarin for to choose, then say běndì again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does 本地 (běndì) mean? ("Local".) Say it once more.

Lesson 3 of 6.
shìzhōngxīn — city centre.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for local, then the Mandarin for from out of town.

You'll want to know: shìzhōngxīn.
shìzhōngxīn — "city centre".
wǒ jiā zài shìzhōngxīn — My home is in the city centre.
Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
shìzhōngxīn is built from shì 'a market, a city', 中 'middle' and 心 'heart', all inherited from Middle Chinese, dzyiX, trjuwng and sim (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first as the place where buying and selling is done.
