## You'll want to know: ਸਹੁਰਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C329-INLAW329-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ਸਹੁਰਾ** — *sahurā* — "a father-in-law".

> ਮੇਰਾ ਸਹੁਰਾ ਇੱਕ ਅਧਿਆਪਕ ਹੈ।
>
> *merā sahurā ikk adhiāpak hai.*
>
> My father-in-law is a teacher.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਸਹੁਰਾ** (*sahurā*) is inherited from Sanskrit *śvaśura*, "a husband's or wife's father"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *sahurā*, "father-in-law", under it (CDIAL 12753).
