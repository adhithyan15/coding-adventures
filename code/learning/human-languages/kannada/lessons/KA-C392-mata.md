# ಮತ (mata) — a vote

## You'll want to know: ಮತ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C392-CHUNAVANE392-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ಮತ** — *mata* — "a vote".

> ಹದಿನೆಂಟು ವರ್ಷ ತುಂಬಿದವರಿಗೆ ಮತ ಹಾಕುವ ಹಕ್ಕು ಇದೆ.
>
> *hadineṇṭu varṣa tumbidavarige mata hākuva hakku ide.*
>
> Those who have turned eighteen have the right to vote.

ಮತ ಹಾಕು, to cast a vote. It also means an opinion or a creed.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಮತ** (*mata*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *mata*, "thought, opinion" (Monier-Williams), from *man*, "to think".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
