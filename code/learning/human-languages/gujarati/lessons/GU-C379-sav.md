## You'll want to know: સાવ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C379-ATYANT379-02]; assesses=[] -->

**સાવ** — *sāv* — "utterly".

> જો તમે આ દવા લેશો તો દુખાવો સાવ મટી જશે.
>
> *jo tame ā davā leśo to dukhāvo sāv maṭī jaśe.*
>
> If you take this medicine, the pain will go away completely.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**સાવ** (*sāv*) is inherited from Sanskrit *sárva*, "all, whole"; CDIAL lists Gujarati *sāv*, "wholly" (CDIAL 13276).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
