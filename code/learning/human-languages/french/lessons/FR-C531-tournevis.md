## You'll want to know: le tournevis
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C531-BRICOLAGE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**le tournevis** — "screwdriver".

> Pour ouvrir la radio, il a pris un petit tournevis.
>
> *To open the radio, he took a small screwdriver.*

Literally turn-screw. The *s* is sounded.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*le tournevis* is built from *tourner* "to turn", from Latin *tornāre*, and *vis* "screw", inherited from Latin *vītis* "a vine" (EtymDB).
