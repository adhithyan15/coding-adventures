## You'll want to know: धूप
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C168-SURAJ168-02]; assesses=[] -->

**धूप** — *dhūp* — "sunshine".

> आज धूप घणी तेज है।
>
> *āj dhūp ghaṇī tej hai.*
>
> The sun is very strong today.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**धूप** (*dhūp*) comes from a reconstructed *\*dhuppā*, "sunshine"; CDIAL lists Hindi and Gujarati *dhūp* (CDIAL 6825). CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry. It is feminine: **धूप घणी तेज है**.
