# ورث (waritha) — to inherit

## You'll want to know: ورث
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C337-IRTH337-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ورث** — *waritha* — "to inherit".

> ورثت البنت بيتا من جدتها.
>
> *warithat al-bint baytan min jaddatihā.*
>
> The girl inherited a house from her grandmother.

Present: يرث, *yarith*.

The present drops the و: يرث.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ورث** (*waritha*) is built on the native Arabic root *w-r-th* (Lane), "to inherit"; *mīrāth* and *turāth* are both what is inherited.
