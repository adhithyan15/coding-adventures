## You'll want to know: わすれる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C152-READY152-04]; assesses=[] -->

**わすれる** — *wasureru* — "to forget; to leave behind".

> **ちちは よく かさを わすれます。** — *chichi wa yoku kasa o wasuremasu* — "My father often forgets his umbrella."

Polite **わすれます**, *wasuremasu*; an ichidan verb.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**わすれる** (*wasureru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi, and its older origin is not settled (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
