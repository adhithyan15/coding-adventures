# క్షీణించు (kṣīṇiñcu) — to deteriorate; to decline

## You'll want to know: క్షీణించు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C491-TUPPU491-05]; assesses=[] -->

**క్షీణించు** — *kṣīṇiñcu* — "to deteriorate; to decline".

> వృద్ధుడి ఆరోగ్యం క్షీణించింది.
>
> *vṛddhuḍi ārōgyaṁ kṣīṇiñcindi.*
>
> The old man's health deteriorated.

Past (it …): క్షీణించింది, *kṣīṇiñcindi*.

Said of health, of an old building and of a river that runs low. Its subject is a thing, so the past shown is the third person: క్షీణించింది, it declined.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**క్షీణించు** (*kṣīṇiñcu*) is built from Sanskrit *kṣīṇa*, "diminished, wasted away" (Monier-Williams), with the Telugu verb ending -ఇంచు.
