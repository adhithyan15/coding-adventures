## You'll want to know: تصور
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C367-IDRAK367-02]; assesses=[] -->

**تصور** — *taṣawwara* — "to conceive".

> لم أتصور أن الرحلة طويلة إلى هذا الحد.
>
> *lam ataṣawwar anna ar-riḥla ṭawīla ilā hādhā al-ḥadd.*
>
> I did not imagine the journey was this long.

Present: يتصور, *yataṣawwar*.

The noun تصور, *taṣawwur*, an idea, a conception.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**تصور** (*taṣawwara*) is built on the native Arabic root *ṣ-w-r*, "to form, to shape" (Lane). It is Form V of the root of *ṣūra*, "a picture": to form a picture in the mind.
