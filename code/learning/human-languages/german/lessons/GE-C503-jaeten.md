## You'll want to know: jäten
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C503-GARTEN-03]; assesses=[] -->

**jäten** — "to weed".

> Am Samstag jäten wir das Unkraut im Garten.
>
> *On Saturday we pull up the weeds in the garden.*

Regular: *hat gejätet*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*jäten* is inherited from Middle High German *jeten* and Old High German *jetan*, from Proto-Germanic *jedaną* (EtymDB).
