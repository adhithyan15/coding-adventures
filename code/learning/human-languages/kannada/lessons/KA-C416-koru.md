## You'll want to know: ಕೋರು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C416-PRATIJNE416-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ಕೋರು** — *kōru* — "to ask formally".

> ನಾನು ಕಚೇರಿಯಿಂದ ಒಂದು ವಾರದ ರಜೆ ಕೋರಿದೆ.
>
> *nānu kacēriyinda ondu vārada raje kōride.*
>
> I asked the office for a week of leave.

Past (I …): ಕೋರಿದೆ, *kōride*.

Formal. ಕೋರಿಕೆ, a request.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಕೋರು** (*kōru*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 2232 lists Kannada *kōṟu*, *kōru*, "to purpose, to wish, to desire", beside Tamil *kōru*, "to request".
