## You'll want to know: stupeō, stupēre
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C495-MIRA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**stupeō, stupēre** — "to be stunned, be amazed".

> Agricola in urbe magnā stupet.
>
> *The farmer is stunned in the great city.*

*stupeō, stupēre, stupuī*. Struck dumb with wonder or fear. English *stupor* comes from it, and *stupid* from its adjective *stupidus*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**stupeō** is inherited and usually traced to a Proto-Indo-European root meaning "to strike, to stun" (EtymDB). English *stupid* comes from its adjective *stupidus*.
