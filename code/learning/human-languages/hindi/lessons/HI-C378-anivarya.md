## You'll want to know: अनिवार्य
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C378-ZARURAT378-02]; assesses=[] -->

**अनिवार्य** — *anivārya* — "compulsory".

> अगर तुम पहाड़ पर चढ़ोगे तो रस्सी अनिवार्य है।
>
> *agar tum pahāṛ par caṛhoge to rassī anivārya hai.*
>
> If you climb the mountain, a rope is compulsory.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अनिवार्य** (*anivārya*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *anivārya*, "not to be warded off, unavoidable" (Monier-Williams).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
