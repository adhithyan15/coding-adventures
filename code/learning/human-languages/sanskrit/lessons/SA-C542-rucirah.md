## You'll want to know: रुचिरः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C542-UTKRSTA542-04]; assesses=[] -->

**रुचिरः** — *ruciraḥ* — "pleasing; lovely".

> यदि एतत् पुष्पं रुचिरम् अस्ति, तर्हि अहं तत् चिनोमि।
>
> *yadi etat puṣpaṃ ruciram asti, tarhi ahaṃ tat cinomi.*
>
> If this flower is lovely, I'll pick it.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**रुचिरः** (*ruciraḥ*) comes from the root √ruc, "to shine, to please", which also gives *rocate*; MW gives *rucira* as bright, beautiful, and pleasant, liked by someone (MW).
