## You'll want to know: tiānliàng
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C517-LIGHT517-01]; assesses=[] -->

*tiānliàng* — "to get light at dawn".

> *wàipó shuō dōngtiān qī diǎn cái tiānliàng* — Grandma says it doesn't get light until seven in winter.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*tiānliàng* is built from 天 'the sky' and *liàng* 'bright', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *then* and *ljangH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'bright' and 'clear'.

## What you've built
