## You'll want to know: ಸ್ಥಳ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C465-PATTANA465-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸ್ಥಳ** — *sthaḷa* — "a place; a site".

> ಊಟಕ್ಕೆ ನಾವು ಒಂದು ಶಾಂತ ಸ್ಥಳ ಹುಡುಕಿದೆವು.
>
> *ūṭakke nāvu ondu śānta sthaḷa huḍukidevu.*
>
> We looked for a quiet place to eat.

ಸ್ಥಳೀಯ, local, is built on it.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸ್ಥಳ** (*sthaḷa*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *sthala*, "dry land, ground, a place" (CDIAL 13744); Kannada writes its *l* as ಳ.
