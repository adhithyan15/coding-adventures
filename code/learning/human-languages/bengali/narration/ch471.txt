Bengali, chapter 471: Falling Ill.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
অসুখ (ôsukh) — an illness.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for to fall out, then the Bengali for a knot of hair.

You'll want to know: অসুখ.
অসুখ — ôsukh — "an illness".
অসুখের জন্য অরুণ এক সপ্তাহ স্কুলে গেল না। ôsukher jônyô ôrun ek sôptāhô skule gelô nā. Arun missed a week of school because of illness.
অসুখ (ôsukh) করা, to fall ill: আমার অসুখ (ôsukh) করল.

The word, taken apart.
অসুখ (ôsukh) is Sanskrit asukha, "unhappy, painful" (Monier-Williams): a-, "not", on sukha, the সুখ (ease) of an earlier lesson. Bengali made it the everyday word for an illness, a dis-ease.

Lesson 3 of 5.
ভোগা (bhogā) — to suffer from (an illness).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for an illness, then the Bengali for a symptom.

You'll want to know: ভোগা.
ভোগা — bhogā — "to suffer from (an illness)".
দাদু তিন সপ্তাহ ধরে জ্বরে ভুগছেন। dādu tin sôptāhô dhôre jbôre bhugchhen. Grandfather has been suffering from fever for three weeks.
Past (he/she …): তিনি কাশিতে ভুগলেন, tini kāshite bhuglen.
The illness takes -এ or -তে: জ্বরে ভোগা (bhogā), to suffer from fever.

The word, taken apart.
ভোগা (bhogā) is listed by CDIAL for Bengali, with the sense of going through or experiencing something, under a reconstructed form (CDIAL 9523); Bengali uses it above all for suffering an illness.

Lesson 4 of 5.
অজ্ঞান হওয়া (ôgyān hôoyā) — to faint; to lose consciousness.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a symptom, then the Bengali for to suffer from.

You'll want to know: অজ্ঞান হওয়া.
অজ্ঞান হওয়া — ôgyān hôoyā — "to faint; to lose consciousness".
রোদে দাঁড়িয়ে থেকে মহিলাটি অজ্ঞান হয়ে গেলেন। rode dā̃ṛiye theke môhilāṭi ôgyān hôye gelen. After standing in the sun, the woman fainted.
Past (he/she …): তিনি হঠাৎ অজ্ঞান হলেন, tini hôṭhāt ôgyān hôlen.

The word, taken apart.
অজ্ঞান হওয়া (ôgyān hôoyā) is Sanskrit ajñāna, "not knowing" (Monier-Williams), a- on the জ্ঞান (taught) of knowledge, with hôoyā, "to be, to become" (CDIAL 9416): to become senseless.

শয্যাশায়ী (shôjyāshāyī) — bedridden; confined to bed (by illness).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for to suffer from, then the Bengali for to faint.

You'll want to know: শয্যাশায়ী.
শয্যাশায়ী — shôjyāshāyī — "bedridden; confined to bed (by illness)".
অসুখের পরে দাদু এক মাস শয্যাশায়ী ছিলেন। ôsukher pôre dādu ek mās shôjyāshāyī chhilen. After his illness my grandfather was confined to bed for a month.
Another sign of being unwell: মাথা ঘোরা, to feel dizzy (the head goes round): আমার মাথা ঘুরছে.

The word, taken apart.
শয্যাশায়ী (shôjyāshāyī) puts Sanskrit śayyā, "a bed", with śāyin, "lying, resting" (Monier-Williams): lying in bed, and so laid up by illness.
