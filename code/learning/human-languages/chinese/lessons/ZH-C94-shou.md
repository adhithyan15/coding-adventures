## You'll want to know: shòu
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C94-SHOU-01]; assesses=[] -->

*shòu* — "thin; slim".

> *tā hěn gāo, hěn shòu* — He's tall and thin.

Its character comes later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*shòu* is inherited from Middle Chinese *srjuwH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed, through an older dictionary, 'lean'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ZH-LEX-C94-PANG-01] -->

*pàng* and *shòu*, *gāo* and *ǎi*: two pairs that describe a body, each with a plain 很.
