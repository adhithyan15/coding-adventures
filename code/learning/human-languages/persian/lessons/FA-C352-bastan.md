## You'll want to know: باستان
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C352-BASTAN-01]; assesses=[] -->

**باستان** (*bâstân*) — "antiquity, ancient times".

> این بنا از زمان باستان مانده است.
>
> *In banâ az zamân-e bâstân mânde ast.*
>
> This building has survived from ancient times.

*Bâstânshenâs* "an archaeologist".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*bâstân* is a native Persian word that EtymDB glosses "ancient era" without a parent.
