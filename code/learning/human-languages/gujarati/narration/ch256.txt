Gujarati, chapter 256: Paint, photos and knitting.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ચિત્ર (citra) — picture, painting.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for drama, then the Gujarati for film.

You'll want to know: ચિત્ર.
ચિત્ર — citra — "picture, painting".
બાળકે સુંદર ચિત્ર બનાવ્યું. — bāḷake sundar citra banāvyũ — "The child made a beautiful picture."

The word, taken apart.
ચિત્ર (citra) is Sanskrit citrá, "conspicuous, bright, variegated", taken over as a tatsama for a picture (CDIAL 4803).

Lesson 2 of 5.
ચીતરવું (cītarvũ) — to paint, to make pictures.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for film, then the Gujarati for picture.

You'll want to know: ચીતરવું.
ચીતરવું — cītarvũ — "to paint, to make pictures".
તેણે એક મોર ચીતર્યો. — teṇe ek mor cītaryo — "She painted a peacock."

The word, taken apart.
ચીતરવું (cītarvũ) is a tadbhava. It comes from Sanskrit citráyati, "decorates", built on citrá, "bright"; CDIAL lists Gujarati citarvũ (CDIAL 4810).

Lesson 3 of 5.
પીંછી (pī̃chī) — paintbrush.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for picture, then the Gujarati for to paint.

You'll want to know: પીંછી.
પીંછી — pī̃chī — "paintbrush".
મેં નવી પીંછી ખરીદી. — mẽ navī pī̃chī kharīdī — "I bought a new paintbrush."

The word, taken apart.
પીંછી (pī̃chī), a little feather, is built on pī̃ch, "feather", with -ī. CDIAL derives pī̃ch from Sanskrit piccha, "tail-feather" (CDIAL 8151), but lists no pī̃chī.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ગૂંથવું (gū̃thvũ) — to knit, to plait.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for paintbrush, then the Gujarati for photo.

You'll want to know: ગૂંથવું.
ગૂંથવું — gū̃thvũ — "to knit, to plait".
મારી દાદીએ સ્વેટર ગૂંથ્યું. — mārī dādīe sveṭar gū̃thyũ — "My grandmother knitted a sweater."

The word, taken apart.
ગૂંથવું (gū̃thvũ) is a tadbhava. CDIAL lists it, glossed "to knit, plait", under Sanskrit gupháti, "strings together", from a doubtful participle guptha- blended with the Prakrit gaṁthaï, "ties" (CDIAL 4205).
