Bengali, chapter 488: Growing Up and Growing Old.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
শৈশব (shoishôb) — childhood.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for to guard protectively, then the Bengali for to stroke.

You'll want to know: শৈশব.
শৈশব — shoishôb — "childhood".
দাদুর শৈশব গ্রামে কাটল। dādur shoishôb grāme kāṭlô. Grandfather spent his childhood in the village.

The word, taken apart.
শৈশব (shoishôb) is a tatsama, Sanskrit śaiśava, "childhood" (Monier-Williams), from śiśu, the শিশু (taught) of Bengali.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: বেড়ে ওঠা.
বেড়ে ওঠা — beṛe oṭhā — "to grow up".
অরুণ গ্রামে দাদুর কাছে বেড়ে উঠল। ôrun grāme dādur kāchhe beṛe uṭhlô. Arun grew up with his grandfather in the village.
Past (he/she …): তিনি শহরে বেড়ে উঠলেন, tini shôhôre beṛe uṭhlen.

The word, taken apart.
বেড়ে ওঠা (beṛe oṭhā) is beṛe, the -এ form of bāṛā, "to grow", which CDIAL lists for Bengali under Sanskrit vardhate, "grows" (CDIAL 11376), with oṭhā, "to rise" (CDIAL 1793).
