## You'll want to know: कैदी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C290-POLICE290-05]; assesses=[] -->

**कैदी** — *kaidī* — "a prisoner".

> एक कैदी रात्री तुरुंगातून पळाला.
>
> *ek kaidī rātrī turuṅgātūn paḷālā.*
>
> A prisoner ran away from the prison at night.

Masculine.

कैदी keeps its form in the plural: दोन कैदी.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**कैदी** (*kaidī*) is a loan from Persian *qaidī*, 'a captive', usually traced to Arabic *qayd*, 'a fetter'.
