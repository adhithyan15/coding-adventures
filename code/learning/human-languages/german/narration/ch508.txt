German, chapter 508: Sieger und Verlierer.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
siegen — to win, to be victorious.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for tied game, then the German for offside.

You'll want to know: siegen.
siegen — "to win, to be victorious".
Unsere Mannschaft hat mit drei zu null gesiegt. Our team won three-nil.
More formal than gewinnen. Der Sieg — victory.

The word, taken apart.
siegen is built on Sieg "victory" (EtymDB), inherited from Middle High German sige and Old High German sigu, from Proto-Germanic segaz.

Lesson 2 of 5.
der Sieger — winner.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for offside, then the German for to win.

You'll want to know: der Sieger.
der Sieger — "winner".
Am Ende war mein kleiner Bruder der Sieger. In the end my little brother was the winner.
Plural: die Sieger. A woman is die Siegerin.

The word, taken apart.
Sieger is built on siegen with -er; Sieg "victory" is inherited from Old High German sigu, from Proto-Germanic segaz (EtymDB).

Lesson 3 of 5.
der Verlierer — loser.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to win, then the German for winner.

You'll want to know: der Verlierer.
der Verlierer — "loser".
Der Verlierer gratulierte dem Sieger. The loser congratulated the winner.
Plural: die Verlierer. Ein schlechter Verlierer — a bad loser.

The word, taken apart.
Verlierer is built on verlieren "to lose", inherited from Old High German firliosan, from Proto-Germanic fraleusaną (EtymDB), with -er.

Lesson 4 of 5.
der Gegner — opponent.

Warm-up.

You'll want to know: der Gegner.
der Gegner — "opponent".
Der Gegner war stärker als wir. The opponent was stronger than us.
Plural: die Gegner. Also an opponent of an idea: ein Gegner der Reform.

The word, taken apart.
Gegner comes from Middle Low German gēgenēre (EtymDB), built on gegen "against".

Lesson 5 of 5.
das Turnier — tournament.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for loser, then the German for opponent.

You'll want to know: das Turnier.
das Turnier — "tournament".
Bei dem Turnier spielen zwölf Mannschaften. Twelve teams are playing in the tournament.
Plural: die Turniere.

The word, taken apart.
Turnier goes back to Middle High German turnier, usually traced to Old French tornei "a joust", built on torner "to turn", from Latin tornāre; EtymDB lists the German word without a source.
