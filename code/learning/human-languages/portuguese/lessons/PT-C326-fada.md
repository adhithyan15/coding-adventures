## You'll want to know: a fada
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C326-LENDA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**a fada** — "the fairy".

> A fada transformou a abóbora num carro.
>
> *The fairy turned the pumpkin into a carriage.*

*Conto de fadas* — fairy tale.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*fada* goes back to Vulgar Latin *Fāta* "a goddess of fate" (EtymDB), from Latin *fātum* "fate", from *fārī* "to speak".
