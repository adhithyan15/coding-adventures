Bengali, chapter 536: Kinds and Choices.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
সুপারিশ (supārish) — a recommendation.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for well protected, then the Bengali for a fire extinguisher.

You'll want to know: সুপারিশ.
সুপারিশ — supārish — "a recommendation".
ডাক্তার সুপারিশ করলে আমি এই ওষুধটা কিনব। Ḍāktār supārish kôrle āmi ei oshudhṭā kinbô. If the doctor recommends it, I'll buy this medicine.

The word, taken apart.
সুপারিশ (supārish) is usually traced to Persian sifārish, "a commission, a recommendation".

What you've built.
One more word for saying what happens if.

Lesson 5 of 5.
খাপ খাওয়া (khāp khāoyā) — to fit; to suit.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a kind of thing, then the Bengali for a style.

You'll want to know: খাপ খাওয়া.
খাপ খাওয়া — khāp khāoyā — "to fit; to suit".
এই চাবিটা তালায় খাপ খায় না। Ei chābiṭā tālāy khāp khāy nā. This key doesn't fit the lock.
Past (he/she …): সেই ঢাকনা হাঁড়িতে খাপ খেল, sei ḍhāknā hā̃ṛite khāp khelô.

The word, taken apart.
খাপ খাওয়া (khāp khāoyā) joins খাপ, "a sheath, a fit", to খাওয়া, "to eat"; CDIAL lists Bengali khāpā, "to become adjusted", under a reconstructed khappa (CDIAL 3816).
