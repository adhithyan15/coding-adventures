## You'll want to know: erziehen
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C495-ERZIEHUNG-01]; assesses=[] -->

**erziehen** — "to bring up, to raise".

> Sie hat ihre drei Kinder allein erzogen.
>
> *She brought up her three children on her own.*

Irregular: *erzog, hat erzogen*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*erziehen* is built on *er-* and *ziehen* "to pull, to draw", inherited from Old High German *ziohan*, from Proto-Germanic *teuhaną* (EtymDB).
