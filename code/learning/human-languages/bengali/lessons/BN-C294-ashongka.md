## You'll want to know: আশঙ্কা
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C294-SOTORKO294-04]; assesses=[] -->

**আশঙ্কা** — *āshôngkā* — "a fear; an apprehension".

> আবহাওয়া খারাপ, তাই বন্যার আশঙ্কা আছে।
>
> *ābôhāoyā khārāp, tāi bônyār āshôngkā āchhe.*
>
> The weather is bad, so a flood is feared.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**আশঙ্কা** (*āshôngkā*) is Sanskrit *āśaṅkā*, 'fear, apprehension; doubt' (Monier-Williams). It is the fear that something will happen, not fear of a thing in front of you.
