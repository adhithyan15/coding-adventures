# Malayalam A1 mock rubric

Score either A1 mock against
[task-shapes/a1.json](../../task-shapes/a1.json). Reading, listening, writing,
and speaking are separate 100-point papers; every paper must independently
reach 60/100.

## Reading and listening

Reading assigns 35 points to signs/forms, 35 to short messages, and 30 to
personal descriptions. Listening assigns 35 points to announcements, 35 to
short exchanges, and 30 to the personal account. Divide each part's points
equally among its keyed items and retain one decimal place. Play listening
material twice at 90–110 words per minute. No transcript, romanization,
dictionary, or translator is allowed.

## Writing

The practical form is worth 40 points: requested content 16, task fulfilment 8,
Malayalam control 8, and orthographic control 8. The 30–40 word message is worth
60 points: relevant content 20, named reader and purpose 10, organisation 10,
vocabulary and grammar 12, and orthographic control 8. Either traditional or
reformed Malayalam orthography earns full credit when used consistently.
Romanized, translated, non-Malayalam-script, or visibly copied answers earn zero
independent-writing credit.

## Speaking

The personal interview is worth 30 points, the one-minute prepared description
30, and the transactional role-play 40. Within each part award 35% for task
fulfilment and relevant content, 25% for interaction and repair, 25% for
Malayalam vocabulary and grammar, and 15% for pronunciation and fluency.
Natural pauses are acceptable. No written answer script is allowed; paper
preparation notes may contain keywords, not sentences.

## Readiness boundary

The mock is executable but not self-validating. A readiness claim additionally
requires clean timed administration and qualified human review of the paper,
recordings, productive responses, and scoring decisions.
