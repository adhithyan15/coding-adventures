## You'll want to know: சூடு
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C91-WEATHER-02]; assesses=[] -->

**சூடு** (*cūṭu*) — "heat", and of a thing, "hot".

**சூடாக இருக்கிறது** — "it is hot". Tea, a pan, a forehead: anything can be **சூடு**.
