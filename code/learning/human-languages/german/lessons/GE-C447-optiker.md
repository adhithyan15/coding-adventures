## You'll want to know: der Optiker
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C447-FRISEUR-03]; assesses=[] -->

**der Optiker** — "the optician".

> Beim Optiker sucht er eine neue Brille aus.
>
> *At the optician's he chooses new glasses.*

Same form in the plural. A woman: *die Optikerin*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Optiker* is built on *Optik* 'optics', a learned word from Greek *optikós* 'of sight'. EtymDB does not list the German word.
