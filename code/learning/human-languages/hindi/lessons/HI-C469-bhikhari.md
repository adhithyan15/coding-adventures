## You'll want to know: भिखारी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C469-UDAR469-05]; assesses=[] -->

**भिखारी** — *bhikhārī* — "a beggar".

> मंदिर के सामने एक भिखारी बैठा था।
>
> *mandir ke sāmne ek bhikhārī baiṭhā thā.*
>
> A beggar was sitting in front of the temple.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**भिखारी** (*bhikhārī*) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Hindi *bhikhārī*, "mendicant", under Sanskrit *bhikṣācara*, "one who goes about begging" (CDIAL 9486). The feminine is *bhikhārin*.
