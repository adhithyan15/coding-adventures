## You'll want to know: पिधानम्
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C514-PARIVESAYATI514-05]; assesses=[] -->

**पिधानम्** — *pidhānam* — "a lid".

> पात्रस्य पिधानं कुत्र अस्ति?
>
> *pātrasya pidhānaṃ kutra asti?*
>
> Where is the lid of the pot?

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पिधानम्** (*pidhānam*) is built on *pi*, a short form of *api*, "on", and the root √dhā, "to put"; MW gives *pidhāna* as a covering, and a cover or lid (MW).
