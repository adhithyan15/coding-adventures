Bengali, chapter 476: Out of Breath.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
দম (dôm) — breath; stamina.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for to swell, then the Bengali for to stumble.

You'll want to know: দম.
দম — dôm — "breath; stamina".
সাঁতার কাটলে দম বাড়ে। sā̃tār kāṭle dôm bāṛe. Swimming builds up your stamina.
দম (dôm) নেওয়া, to take a breather.

The word, taken apart.
দম (dôm) is usually traced to Persian dam, "breath".

You'll want to know: হেঁচকি.
হেঁচকি — hẽchki — "a hiccup; hiccups".
তাড়াতাড়ি খেলে আমার হেঁচকি হয়। tāṛātāṛi khele āmār hẽchki hôy. If I eat too fast I get hiccups.

The word, taken apart.
হেঁচকি (hẽchki) is an imitative word, its sound standing for the catch of a hiccup. No dictionary we use records an older form, so its history is not settled.

ঢেকুর — ḍhekur — "a burp; a belch".
ভারী খাবারের পরে দাদু ঢেকুর তুললেন। bhārī khābārer pôre dādu ḍhekur tullen. After the heavy meal my grandfather burped.
ঢেকুর (ḍhekur) তোলা, to burp.

The word, taken apart.
ঢেকুর (ḍhekur) is an imitative word for a belch; it shows the new letter ঢ. No dictionary we use records an older form, so its history is not settled.

Lesson 4 of 5.

You'll want to know: হাঁপানো.
হাঁপানো — hā̃pāno — "to pant; to be out of breath".
তাড়াতাড়ি হেঁটে এসে দাদু হাঁপাচ্ছিলেন। tāṛātāṛi hẽṭe ese dādu hā̃pāchchhilen. Grandfather was panting after walking fast.
Past (he/she …): তিনি হাঁপালেন, tini hā̃pālen.

The word, taken apart.
হাঁপানো (hā̃pāno) is listed by CDIAL for Bengali under a reconstructed root for panting (CDIAL 13973); হাঁপানি, asthma, is its noun.

পেটব্যথায় রোগীটা সারারাত কাতরাল। peṭbæthāy rogīṭā sārārāt kātrālô. The patient groaned all night with a stomach ache.

কাতরানো (kātrāno) is the verb of kātôr, "distressed", Sanskrit kātara, "timid, despairing, agitated" (Monier-Williams).
