Persian, chapter 392: Likely or unlikely.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
احتمال (ehtemâl) — a probability, a chance.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for even though, then the Persian for to imagine.

You'll want to know: احتمال.
احتمال (ehtemâl) — "a probability, a chance".
احتمال باران امروز زیاد است. ehtemâl-e bârân emruz ziyâd ast. There is a good chance of rain today.
Ehtemâl dârad ke + subjunctive — it is likely that. The adverb is ehtemâlan.

The word, taken apart.
احتمال (ehtemâl) is usually traced to Arabic iḥtimāl "likelihood, what can be borne", on the root ḥ-m-l "to carry", the same source as ehtemâlan "probably".

Lesson 2 of 5.
احتمال دادن (ehtemâl dâdan) — to think likely, to suspect.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to imagine, then the Persian for a probability.

You'll want to know: احتمال دادن.
احتمال دادن (ehtemâl dâdan) — "to think likely, to suspect".
من احتمال میدهم که او دیر بیاید. man ehtemâl midaham ke u dir biyâyad. I think he will probably come late.
Ehtemâl + dâdan (stem deh-); ke + subjunctive follows.

The word, taken apart.
احتمال دادن (ehtemâl dâdan) is literally "to give a likelihood": ehtemâl is usually traced to Arabic iḥtimāl, and dâdan "to give" is traced by EtymDB to PIE deh₃-.

Lesson 3 of 5.
امکان (emkân) — a possibility; a facility.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a probability, then the Persian for to think likely.

You'll want to know: امکان.
امکان (emkân) — "a possibility; a facility".
امکان دارد که فردا برف بیاید. emkân dârad ke fardâ barf biyâyad. It may snow tomorrow.
Emkân dârad ke + subjunctive — it is possible that. Plural emkânât, facilities.

The word, taken apart.
امکان (emkân) is usually traced to Arabic imkān "possibility", from the same Arabic family as mumkin, the source of Persian momken "possible".

Lesson 4 of 5.
بعید (ba'id) — unlikely; far-fetched.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to think likely, then the Persian for a possibility.

You'll want to know: بعید.
بعید (ba'id) — "unlikely; far-fetched".
بعید است که او امروز بیاید. ba'id ast ke u emruz biyâyad. It is unlikely that he will come today.
Ba'id ast ke + subjunctive. Its first meaning is "far".

The word, taken apart.
بعید (ba'id) is usually traced to Arabic ba'īd "far, remote": an unlikely thing is far from happening.

Lesson 5 of 5.
قطعی (qat'i) — definite, final.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a possibility, then the Persian for unlikely.

You'll want to know: قطعی.
قطعی (qat'i) — "definite, final".
تاریخ سفر هنوز قطعی نیست. târikh-e safar hanuz qat'i nist. The date of the trip is not definite yet.
From qat' "cutting" + -i: a cut-and-dried decision.

The word, taken apart.
قطعی (qat'i) is usually traced to Arabic qaṭ'ī "decisive", built on qaṭ' "cutting": a definite answer cuts off doubt.
