## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[TE-LEX-C317-ILOGA-01, TE-SCRIPT-RECOG-174, TE-GRAMMAR-C317-FIRST-THEN-01, TE-GRAMMAR-C317-CIVARIKI-01, TE-GRAMMAR-C317-CONJUNCTIVE-01, TE-GRAMMAR-C317-AFTER-DOING-01, TE-SKILL-C317-READ-STORY-01, TE-GRAMMAR-C316-PAST-SIMPLE-01, TE-GRAMMAR-C316-SCENE-EVENT-01, TE-GRAMMAR-C316-PAST-HABITUAL-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s each]
- [YOU SAY: First I drank tea. After that I went to the town.]
- [YOU SAY: I came home and had a meal. In the meantime it rained.]
- [YOU SAY: After seeing the film, we went home. In the end we slept.]
- [YOU SAY: from the reading, what the writer was doing on the bus, and what he used to do every day]
- [YOU WRITE: **ఈలోగా**, then **ఈ పుస్తకం**, "this book"]

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[TE-LEX-C317-ILOGA-01, TE-SCRIPT-RECOG-174, TE-GRAMMAR-C317-FIRST-THEN-01, TE-GRAMMAR-C317-CIVARIKI-01, TE-GRAMMAR-C317-CONJUNCTIVE-01, TE-GRAMMAR-C317-AFTER-DOING-01, TE-SKILL-C317-READ-STORY-01] -->

[PAUSE 3s] "First"? (**మొదట**.) "After that"? (**ఆ తరువాత**.) "In the end"? (**చివరికి**.) "Having
come"? (**వచ్చి**.) "In the meantime"? (**ఈలోగా**, which opens with **ఈ**.) In the reading, what did
Grandmother do in the meantime? (**She cooked a meal.**)
