## You'll want to know: とまる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C199-DEPART199-04]; assesses=[] -->

**とまる** — *tomaru* — "to come to a stop; to stop moving".

> **でんしゃが えきに とまります。** — *densha ga eki ni tomarimasu* — "The train stops at the station."

Polite **とまります**, *tomarimasu*; a godan verb.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**とまる** (*tomaru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi, and its older origin is not settled (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
