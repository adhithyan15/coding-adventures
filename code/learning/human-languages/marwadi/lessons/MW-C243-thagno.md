## You'll want to know: ठगणो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C243-CHOR243-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ठगणो** — *ṭhagṇo* — "to cheat; to swindle".

> वो आदमी घणो ठगै है।
>
> *vo ādmī ghaṇo ṭhagai hai.*
>
> That man cheats a lot.

Past (I …): म्हैं गाहक कोनी ठग्या, *mhaĩ gāhak konī ṭhagyā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ठगणो** (*ṭhagṇo*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*ṭhagg*, "cheat". CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry; it lists Hindi *ṭhagnā*, "to cheat, rob" (CDIAL 5489).
