Portuguese, chapter 55: Enter, Go Out, Come Back, Study, Travel.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
entrar — to go in, to enter.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for a ticket, then the Portuguese for a map.

You'll want to know: entrar.
entrar — "to go in, to enter".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: entrar]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: entrar, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say entrar]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Portuguese for a ticket, then the Portuguese for a map, then say entrar again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does entrar mean? ("To go in, to enter".) Say it once more.
