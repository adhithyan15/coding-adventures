## You'll want to know: तत्पर
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C553-DHYEY553-05]; assesses=[] -->

**तत्पर** — *tatpar* — "prompt; ready to act".

> हा दुकानदार ग्राहकांच्या सेवेसाठी नेहमी तत्पर असतो.
>
> *hā dukāndār grāhkāñcyā sevesāṭhī nehmī tatpar asto.*
>
> This shopkeeper is always ready to serve his customers.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**तत्पर** (*tatpar*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *tatpara*, "intent on that" (Monier-Williams).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
