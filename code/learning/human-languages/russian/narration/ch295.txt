Russian, chapter 295: Fire.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
пожар (pozhár) — a fire (a blaze).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to confess, then the Russian for to acquit.

You'll want to know: пожар.
пожар (pozhár) — "a fire (a blaze)".
В доме напротив был пожар. V dóme naprótiv byl pozhár. There was a fire in the building opposite.
A fire that destroys. Пожарный — a firefighter.

The word, taken apart.
пожар (pozhár) is built on по- and жар "heat", from the same Slavic root as гореть (gorét) "to burn"; EtymDB lists пожар (pozhár) with no parent.

Lesson 2 of 5.
дым (dym) — smoke.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to acquit, then the Russian for a fire.

You'll want to know: дым.
дым (dym) — "smoke".
Из окна шёл чёрный дым. Iz okná shyol chyórnyy dym. Black smoke was coming out of the window.
Нет дыма без огня — there is no smoke without fire.

The word, taken apart.
дым (dym) is inherited from Proto-Slavic dymŭ "smoke", from PIE dʰuh₂mós "smoke" (EtymDB); Latin fūmus "smoke" is a cousin.

Lesson 3 of 5.
гореть (gorét) — to burn, to be on fire.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a fire, then the Russian for smoke.

You'll want to know: гореть.
гореть (gorét) — "to burn, to be on fire".
В лесу горела трава. V lesú goréla travá. Grass was burning in the forest.
Imperfective; present горю, горишь. Perfective сгореть (sgorét) — to burn down. Of a light: свет горит — the light is on.

The word, taken apart.
гореть (gorét) is inherited from Proto-Slavic gorěti "to burn", which EtymDB traces to PIE gʷʰer- "warm, hot".

Lesson 4 of 5.
загораться (zagorátsya) — to catch fire.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for smoke, then the Russian for to burn.

You'll want to know: загораться.
загораться (zagorátsya) — "to catch fire".
От свечи загорелась штора. Ot svechí zagorélas shtóra. The curtain caught fire from a candle.
Imperfective; perfective загореться (zagorétsya). Also of lights coming on.

The word, taken apart.
загораться (zagorátsya) is built on за-, which marks a start, and гореть (gorét) "to burn", inherited from Proto-Slavic gorěti (EtymDB): to start burning.

Lesson 5 of 5.
огнетушитель (ognetushítel) — a fire extinguisher.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to burn, then the Russian for to catch fire.

You'll want to know: огнетушитель.
огнетушитель (ognetushítel) — "a fire extinguisher".
Огнетушитель висит на стене. Ognetushítel visít na stené. The fire extinguisher hangs on the wall.
Masculine; genitive огнетушителя.

The word, taken apart.
огнетушитель (ognetushítel) joins огонь "fire", inherited through Old East Slavic ognĭ "fire" (EtymDB), and тушить "to put out": a fire-quencher.
