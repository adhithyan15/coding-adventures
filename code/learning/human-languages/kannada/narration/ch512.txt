Kannada, chapter 512: Serious Illness.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ರಕ್ತದೊತ್ತಡ (raktadottaḍa) — blood pressure.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a symptom, then the Kannada for a vaccine.

You'll want to know: ರಕ್ತದೊತ್ತಡ.
ರಕ್ತದೊತ್ತಡ — raktadottaḍa — "blood pressure".
ಉಪ್ಪು ಹೆಚ್ಚು ತಿಂದರೆ ರಕ್ತದೊತ್ತಡ ಏರುತ್ತದೆ. uppu heccu tindare raktadottaḍa ēruttade. If you eat a lot of salt, your blood pressure rises.
ರಕ್ತ, blood + ಒತ್ತಡ, pressure, joined with a ದ.

The word, taken apart.
ರಕ್ತದೊತ್ತಡ (raktadottaḍa) joins Sanskrit rakta, "red; blood" (CDIAL 10539), and the native ottaḍa, "pressure", from ottu, "to press" (DEDR 1021).

Lesson 2 of 5.
ಹೃದಯಾಘಾತ (hṛdayāghāta) — a heart attack.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a vaccine, then the Kannada for blood pressure.

You'll want to know: ಹೃದಯಾಘಾತ.
ಹೃದಯಾಘಾತ — hṛdayāghāta — "a heart attack".
ಹೃದಯಾಘಾತ ಆದ ಮೇಲೆ ಅಪ್ಪ ಸಿಗರೇಟು ಬಿಟ್ಟರು. hṛdayāghāta āda mēle appa sigarēṭu biṭṭaru. After his heart attack, father gave up cigarettes.
ಹೃದಯ, the heart + ಆಘಾತ, a blow: the word for a shock, now a blow to the heart.

The word, taken apart.
ಹೃದಯಾಘಾತ (hṛdayāghāta) is a learned compound built from Sanskrit hṛdaya, "the heart", and āghāta, "a blow" (Monier-Williams).

ರಕ್ತಸ್ರಾವ (raktasrāva) is a learned compound built from Sanskrit rakta, "blood" (CDIAL 10539), and srāva, "a flowing" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 4 of 5.
ಪಾರ್ಶ್ವವಾಯು (pārśvavāyu) — a stroke; paralysis.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a heart attack, then the Kannada for bleeding.

You'll want to know: ಪಾರ್ಶ್ವವಾಯು.
ಪಾರ್ಶ್ವವಾಯು — pārśvavāyu — "a stroke; paralysis".
ಪಾರ್ಶ್ವವಾಯುವಿನಿಂದ ಅಜ್ಜನ ಎಡಗೈ ಚಲಿಸುವುದಿಲ್ಲ. pārśvavāyuvininda ajjana eḍagai calisuvudilla. Since his stroke, grandfather's left hand does not move.
Literally side-wind: ಪಾರ್ಶ್ವ, a side + ವಾಯು, air, the old name for a disorder of the body.

The word, taken apart.
ಪಾರ್ಶ್ವವಾಯು (pārśvavāyu) is a learned compound built from Sanskrit pārśva, "the side, the ribs" (CDIAL 8118), and vāyu, "wind" (CDIAL 11544): a "wind" that strikes one side of the body.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ಪ್ರಜ್ಞೆ ತಪ್ಪು (prajñe tappu) — to faint; to pass out.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for bleeding, then the Kannada for a stroke.

You'll want to know: ಪ್ರಜ್ಞೆ ತಪ್ಪು.
ಪ್ರಜ್ಞೆ ತಪ್ಪು — prajñe tappu — "to faint; to pass out".
ಬಿಸಿಲಿನಲ್ಲಿ ನಿಂತಿದ್ದ ಹುಡುಗನಿಗೆ ಪ್ರಜ್ಞೆ ತಪ್ಪಿತು. bisilinalli nintidda huḍuganige prajñe tappitu. The boy who had been standing in the sun fainted.
Past (it …): ಪ್ರಜ್ಞೆ ತಪ್ಪಿತು, prajñe tappitu.
ಪ್ರಜ್ಞೆ is consciousness; the one who faints takes -ಗೆ. Coming round is ಪ್ರಜ್ಞೆ ಬರು.

The word, taken apart.
ಪ್ರಜ್ಞೆ ತಪ್ಪು (prajñe tappu) joins Sanskrit prajñā, "wisdom, awareness" (CDIAL 8515), and the native verb tappu, "to slip, to miss": awareness slips away.
