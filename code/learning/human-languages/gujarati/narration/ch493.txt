Gujarati, chapter 493: Many Generations.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
પેઢી (peḍhī) — a generation.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a grandson, then the Gujarati for a cousin.

You'll want to know: પેઢી.
પેઢી — peḍhī — "a generation".
અમારા કુટુંબની ચાર પેઢી આ ગામમાં રહી છે. amārā kuṭumbnī cār peḍhī ā gāmmā̃ rahī che. Four generations of our family have lived in this village.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
પેઢી (peḍhī) is inherited from Sanskrit pīṭha, "stool, bench"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati word as "step of a ladder" (CDIAL 8222). The sense "a generation", one step in a family line, is probably a later use of the same word.

બુઝુર્ગ — bujhurg — "a respected elder".
ગામના બુઝુર્ગો સાંજે મંદિર પાસે બેસે છે. gāmnā bujhurgo sā̃je mandir pāse bese che. In the evenings the village elders sit near the temple.

Lesson 5 of 6.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a respected elder, then the Gujarati for a household.

You'll want to know: સંતાન.
સંતાન — santān — "offspring; a child".
તેમને બે સંતાન છે, એક દીકરો અને એક દીકરી. temne be santān che, ek dīkro ane ek dīkrī. They have two children, a son and a daughter.
A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
સંતાન (santān) is a tatsama, Sanskrit saṃtāna, "continued succession", as Monier-Williams records it: the line that carries a family on.
