Urdu, chapter 450: Missing Someone.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
تنہا (tanhā) — lonely; alone.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for to cling tightly, then the Urdu for passionate love.

You'll want to know: تنہا.
تنہا — tanhā — "lonely; alone".
شہر میں نانی بالکل تنہا ہیں۔ shahr meṅ nānī bilkul tanhā haiṅ. In the city my granny is all alone.

The word, taken apart.
تنہا (tanhā) is usually traced to Persian tanhā, "alone".

What you've built.
One more word for describing what you lived and felt.

The word, taken apart.
اکیلا (akelā) is inherited from ēkkala, "alone", a form CDIAL reconstructs from the word for "one" (CDIAL 2506).

بہن کی جدائی میں وہ روتی ہے۔ bahan kī judāī meṅ voh rotī hai. She cries because her sister is far away.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
جدائی (judāī) is usually traced to Persian judāī, "separation", built on judā, "apart".

You'll want to know: بسورنا.
بسورنا — bisūrnā — "to whimper; to pull a crying face".
ماں کے جانے پر بچہ بسور رہا تھا۔ māṅ ke jāne par bachchā bisūr rahā thā. When his mother left, the child was whimpering.
Past (she …): وہ دیر تک بسوری, voh der tak bisūrī.

The word, taken apart.
بسورنا (bisūrnā) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin bisūrnā, "to put on a crying face", under viśūratē, "is hurt, is distressed", a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 11941).
