## You'll want to know: pulsus, pulsūs
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C517-VENA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**pulsus, pulsūs** — "a beat, pulse".

> Medicus pulsum vēnārum temptat.
>
> *The doctor tests the beat of the veins.*

Masculine, fourth declension, from *pellō* — to strike, push. *Pulsus vēnārum* — the pulse. English *pulse*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**pulsus** is the action noun of *pellō* "to push, strike" (Lewis & Short; EtymDB). English *pulse* and *repulse* come from the family.
