# ఉపాధి (upādhi) — a livelihood; employment

## You'll want to know: ఉపాధి
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C525-SAMPADANA525-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ఉపాధి** — *upādhi* — "a livelihood; employment".

> పల్లెల్లో యువకులకు ఉపాధి దొరకడం కష్టం.
>
> *pallellō yuvakulaku upādhi dorakaḍaṁ kaṣṭaṁ.*
>
> In the villages it is hard for young people to find work.

ఉపాధి అవకాశాలు, job opportunities.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ఉపాధి** (*upādhi*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *upādhi* (CDIAL 2299), "a point of view, an aim"; in Telugu it means a means of living.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
