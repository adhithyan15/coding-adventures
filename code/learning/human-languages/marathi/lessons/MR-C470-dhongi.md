## You'll want to know: ढोंगी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C470-LABAD470-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ढोंगी** — *ḍhoṅgī* — "hypocritical".

> बाहेर गोड बोलणारा तो माणूस ढोंगी आहे.
>
> *bāher goḍ bolṇārā to māṇūs ḍhoṅgī āhe.*
>
> That man who talks sweetly in public is a hypocrite.

From ढोंग, a sham: ढोंग करणे, to put on an act. Unchanging.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ढोंगी** (*ḍhoṅgī*) is a Marathi adjective built on ढोंग, "a sham", whose origin is not settled.
