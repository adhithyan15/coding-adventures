## You'll want to know: l'esca
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C501-PESCA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**l'esca** — "bait".

> Metto l'esca sull'amo.
>
> *I put the bait on the hook.*

Also a lure in a wider sense: *fare da esca*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*esca* is inherited from Latin *ēsca* "food, bait" (EtymDB), from *edere* "to eat" (Lewis & Short).
