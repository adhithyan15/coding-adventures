## You'll want to know: स्पर्श
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C480-SPARSH480-02]; assesses=[] -->

**स्पर्श** — *sparś* — "a touch".

> माँ के स्पर्श से बच्चा सो गया।
>
> *māṁ ke sparś se baccā so gayā.*
>
> At its mother's touch the baby fell asleep.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**स्पर्श** (*sparś*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *sparśa*, "touch, the sense of touch" (Monier-Williams). It is the noun; the everyday verb is *chūnā*.
