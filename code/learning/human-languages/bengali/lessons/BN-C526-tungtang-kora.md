## You'll want to know: টুংটাং করা
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C526-BAJNA526-05]; assesses=[] -->

**টুংটাং করা** — *ṭungṭāng kôrā* — "to strum; to tinkle".

> সন্ধ্যায় মেয়েটা গিটারে টুংটাং করছিল।
>
> *Sôndhyāy meyeṭā giṭāre ṭungṭāng kôrchhilô.*
>
> In the evening the girl was strumming on the guitar.

Past (he/she …): তিনি সেতারে টুংটাং করলেন, *tini setāre ṭungṭāng kôrlen*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**টুংটাং করা** (*ṭungṭāng kôrā*) joins টুংটাং, an imitative word for the light sound of plucked strings or a small bell, to করা, "to do". No dictionary we use records an older form, so its history is not settled.
