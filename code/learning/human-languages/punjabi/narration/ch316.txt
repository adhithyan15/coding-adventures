Punjabi, chapter 316: Trouble in the Lane.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ਲੜਨਾ (laṛnā) — to fight.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for a piece of jewellery, then the Punjabi for a life.

You'll want to know: ਲੜਨਾ.
ਲੜਨਾ — laṛnā — "to fight".
ਕੱਲ੍ਹ ਗਲੀ ਵਿੱਚ ਦੋ ਮੁੰਡੇ ਲੜੇ। kall galī vich do muṇḍe laṛe. Yesterday two boys fought in the lane.
Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਆਪਣੇ ਦੋਸਤ ਨਾਲ ਲੜਿਆ, maiṁ āpṇe dost nāl laṛiā.

The word, taken apart.
ਲੜਨਾ (laṛnā) is inherited from a reconstructed laḍ, "fight"; CDIAL lists Punjabi laṛṇā, "to fight", under it (CDIAL 10920).

What you've built.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: ਗਾਲ੍ਹ.
ਗਾਲ੍ਹ — gālh — "a swear word".
ਉਸ ਨੇ ਮੈਨੂੰ ਗਾਲ੍ਹ ਕੱਢੀ। us ne mainū̃ gālh kaḍḍhī. He swore at me.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ਗਾਲ੍ਹ (gālh) is inherited from Sanskrit gāli, "abusive speech"; CDIAL lists Punjabi gāl and gālī, "abuse", under it (CDIAL 4145).

The word, taken apart.
