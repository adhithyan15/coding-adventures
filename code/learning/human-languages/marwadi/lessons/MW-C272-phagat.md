## You'll want to know: फगत
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C272-PHAGAT272-01]; assesses=[] -->

**फगत** — *phagat* — "only".

> हूं फगत चाय पीऊं हूं।
>
> *hū̃ phagat chāy pīū̃ hū̃.*
>
> I only drink tea.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**फगत** (*phagat*) is usually traced, through Persian *faqat*, to Arabic *faqaṭ*, "only". It goes right before the thing it limits.
