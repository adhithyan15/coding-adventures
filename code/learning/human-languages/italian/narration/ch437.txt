Italian, chapter 437: Rumore e sporco.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
il baccano — the racket, the uproar.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for to recharge, then the Italian for to burn out.

You'll want to know: il baccano.
il baccano — "the racket, the uproar".
I vicini fanno un baccano tremendo. The neighbours are making a dreadful racket.
Informal; fare baccano — to make a racket.

The word, taken apart.
baccano is built on baccanale 'a wild party', a learned borrowing from Latin bacchānal, a feast of the Greek wine god (EtymDB).

In cucina c'era uno scarafaggio! There was a cockroach in the kitchen!
A good reason to ask for another room.

The word, taken apart.
scarafaggio is usually traced to Latin scarabaeus 'beetle' (Lewis & Short), from Greek kárabos; it is inherited, with a changed ending.
