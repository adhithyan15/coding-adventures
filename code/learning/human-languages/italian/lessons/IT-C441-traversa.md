## You'll want to know: la traversa
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C441-VICOLI-03]; assesses=[] -->

**la traversa** — "the side street; the crossbar".

> La farmacia è nella seconda traversa a destra.
>
> *The chemist's is in the second side street on the right.*

A street that crosses a main one. In football, *la traversa* is the crossbar.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*traversa* is inherited from Latin *trānsversa* 'lying across', from *trānsvertere*, built on *vertere* 'to turn' (Lewis & Short).
