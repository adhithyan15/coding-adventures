## You'll want to know: землетрясение
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C300-NAVODNENIYE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**землетрясение** (*zemletryaséniye*) — "an earthquake".

> Землетрясение было ночью.
>
> *Zemletryaséniye býlo nóchyu.*
>
> The earthquake struck at night.

Neuter.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*землетрясение* joins *земля* "earth", inherited from Proto-Slavic *zemľa*, PIE *dʰéǵʰōm*, and *трясти* "to shake", Proto-Slavic *tręsti* (EtymDB): a shaking of the earth.
