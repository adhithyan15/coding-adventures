# ख़ुश (khuś) — happy, glad

## You'll want to know: ख़ुश
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C284-KHUSH-01]; assesses=[HI-CONCEPT-C02-KHUSHI-01, HI-LEX-C108-FEEL-03, HI-LEX-C71-TODAY-01, HI-LEX-C90-ROUT-01, HI-GRAMMAR-C276-PAST-PERSON-01] -->

**ख़ुश** — *khuś* — "happy, glad".

> **आज मैं बहुत ख़ुश हूँ।**
>
> *āj maiṁ bahut khuś hūṁ.* — "I am very happy today."

**ख़ुश** never changes: **वह ख़ुश है** for him and for her, **हम ख़ुश हैं** for us. It is the word **ख़ुशी** is built on: **ख़ुश**, "happy", and **ख़ुशी** (*khuśī*), "happiness".

With **होना** in the past, it says the gladness came: **वह बहुत ख़ुश हुई** (*vah bahut khuś huī*), "she was very pleased". The one you are pleased with takes **से**: **मैं तुमसे ख़ुश हूँ** (*maiṁ tumse khuś hūṁ*), "I am pleased with you".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ख़ुश** (*khuś*) is usually traced to Persian *khush*, "happy, pleasant". **ख़ुशी** adds **-ई**, the ending of many Hindi nouns of feeling, and **ख़ुशबू**, "fragrance", opens with the same word.
