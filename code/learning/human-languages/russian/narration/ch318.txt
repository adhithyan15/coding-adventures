Russian, chapter 318: Customs and festivities.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
традиция (tradítsiya) — a tradition.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to blow out, then the Russian for to have fun.

You'll want to know: традиция.
традиция (tradítsiya) — "a tradition".
В нашей семье есть старая традиция. V náshey semyé yest stáraya tradítsiya. Our family has an old tradition.
Feminine. По традиции — by tradition.

The word, taken apart.
традиция (tradítsiya) goes back to Latin trāditiō "a handing over" (EtymDB), from trādere "to hand over" (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 2 of 6.
обычай (obýchay) — a custom.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to have fun, then the Russian for a tradition.

You'll want to know: обычай.
обычай (obýchay) — "a custom".
По русскому обычаю гостей встречают хлебом и солью. Po rússkomu obýchayu gostéy vstrecháyut khlébom i sólyu. By Russian custom, guests are welcomed with bread and salt.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
обычай (obýchay) is inherited from Proto-Slavic obvyčajĭ (EtymDB), built on vyknǫti "to get used to", the root of привыкать.

Студенты готовились к новогоднему балу. Studénty gotóvilis k novogódnemu bálu. The students were getting ready for the New Year ball.

The word, taken apart.
бал is usually traced through German Ball or French bal to Late Latin ballāre "to dance"; EtymDB lists no parent.

Lesson 5 of 6.
гулянье (gulyánye) — festivities, a fête.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a rite, then the Russian for a ball.

You'll want to know: гулянье.
гулянье (gulyánye) — "festivities, a fête".
На площади было большое гулянье. Na plóshchadi býlo bolshóye gulyánye. There were big festivities on the square.
Neuter; also spelt гуляние. Народное гулянье (gulyánye) — public festivities.

The word, taken apart.
гулянье (gulyánye) is built on гулять "to stroll, to make merry", an East Slavic verb whose origin is not settled; EtymDB lists no parent.
