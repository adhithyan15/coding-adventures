## You'll want to know: ಕುಸ್ತಿ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C540-KUSTI540-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ಕುಸ್ತಿ** — *kusti* — "wrestling".

> ಜಾತ್ರೆಯಲ್ಲಿ ದೊಡ್ಡ ಕುಸ್ತಿ ಸ್ಪರ್ಧೆ ನಡೆಯಿತು.
>
> *jātreyalli doḍḍa kusti spardhe naḍeyitu.*
>
> A big wrestling contest was held at the fair.

ಕುಸ್ತಿಪಟು, a wrestler.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಕುಸ್ತಿ** (*kusti*) is usually traced to Persian *kushtī*, "wrestling", which is thought to have reached Kannada through Hindustani.
