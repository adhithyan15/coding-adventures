## You'll want to know: o julgamento
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C390-CULPA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**o julgamento** — "the trial; the judgement".

> O julgamento começa na segunda-feira.
>
> *The trial starts on Monday.*

*Ir a julgamento* — to go on trial.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*julgamento* is built on *julgar* "to judge" with *-mento*; *julgar* is inherited from Latin *iūdicāre* "to judge" (EtymDB), on *iūdex* "judge" (Lewis & Short).
