## You'll want to know: 每
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C138-EVERY138-01]; assesses=[] -->

**每** — *měi* — "every; each".

> **我们每个星期六都打扫** *wǒmen měi ge xīngqīliù dōu dǎsǎo* — We clean the house every Saturday.

The new character, in pieces: 每 — a short fall and a short bar on top, then a box: a vertical that turns right along the bottom, a bar that turns down the right side and hooks, a dot, a long bar across, and a dot. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**每** (*měi*) is inherited from Middle Chinese *mwojX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'frequently' and as a particle. 'Every' is not among the glosses, so how it got that sense is not settled.
