## You'll want to know: qīngdàn
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C449-TASTE449-04]; assesses=[] -->

*qīngdàn* — "light and mild (of food)".

> *zhè jiā fànguǎn de cài hěn qīngdàn* — The food at this restaurant is light.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*qīngdàn* is built from *qīng* 'clear' and *dàn* 'thin', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *tshjeng* and *damX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second as a thin taste.
