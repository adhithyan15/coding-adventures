German, chapter 385: Regeln und Ausnahmen.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
die Ausnahme — the exception.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to extend, then the German for to expire.

You'll want to know: die Ausnahme.
die Ausnahme — "the exception".
Normalerweise arbeite ich nicht am Sonntag, aber heute ist eine Ausnahme. Normally I do not work on Sunday, but today is an exception.
Plural: die Ausnahmen. Mit Ausnahme von — with the exception of.

The word, taken apart.
Ausnahme is the noun of ausnehmen "to take out, to except", built on nehmen "to take", Proto-Germanic nemaną (EtymDB): a case taken out of the rule.

What you've built.

Lesson 2 of 6.

You'll want to know: bestrafen.
bestrafen — "to punish".
Der Schüler wurde für die Lüge bestraft. The pupil was punished for the lie.
Inseparable: er bestraft, er hat bestraft. Die Strafe — the punishment, also a fine.

The word, taken apart.
bestrafen is built with be- on strafen "to punish", inherited from Middle High German strāfen (EtymDB); EtymDB lists Dutch straffen as a cousin.

Lesson 3 of 6.
gesetzlich — legal, laid down by law.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for the exception, then the German for to punish.

You'll want to know: gesetzlich.
gesetzlich — "legal, laid down by law".
Der Mindestlohn ist gesetzlich geregelt. The minimum wage is laid down by law.
Gesetzlich verboten — prohibited by law. Der gesetzliche Feiertag — the public holiday.

The word, taken apart.
gesetzlich is built with -lich on Gesetz "law", which EtymDB derives from setzen "to set", Proto-Germanic satjaną: what has been set down.

Lesson 4 of 6.
rechtlich — legally, in law.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to punish, then the German for legal.

You'll want to know: rechtlich.
rechtlich — "legally, in law".
Rechtlich ist das kein Problem. Legally, that is no problem.
Rechtlich is about the law in general; gesetzlich about what a statute says.

The word, taken apart.
rechtlich is built with -lich on Recht "law, right", inherited from Old High German reht and Proto-Germanic rehtaz "right, straight" (EtymDB), a cousin of English right.

Lesson 5 of 6.
die Regelung — the rule (that applies), the arrangement.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for legal, then the German for legally.

You'll want to know: die Regelung.
die Regelung — "the rule (that applies), the arrangement".
Für Kinder unter sechs gibt es eine besondere Regelung. There is a special rule for children under six.
Plural: die Regelungen. From regeln — to regulate, to sort out.

The word, taken apart.
Regelung is the noun of regeln, built on Regel "rule", from Old High German regula and Latin rēgula "a straight piece of wood, a ruler, a rule" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short).
