## You'll want to know: ગૃહકાર્ય
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C245-SPELL245-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ગૃહકાર્ય** — *gṛhkārya* — "homework".

> **હું સાંજે ગૃહકાર્ય કરું છું.** — *hũ sā̃je gṛhkārya karũ chũ* — "I do my homework in the evening."

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ગૃહકાર્ય** (*gṛhkārya*) is a modern learned compound built from two tatsamas: Sanskrit *gṛha*, "house" (CDIAL 4240), and *kārya*, "what is to be done, business" (CDIAL 3078).
