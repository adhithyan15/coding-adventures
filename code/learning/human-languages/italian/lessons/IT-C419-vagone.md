## You'll want to know: il vagone
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C419-CARROZZA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**il vagone** — "the railway carriage, the wagon".

> Il nostro vagone è in fondo al treno.
>
> *Our carriage is at the back of the train.*

*Il vagone letto* — sleeping car; *il vagone merci* — goods wagon.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*vagone* was borrowed from French *wagon*, which took it from English *waggon*, from Dutch *wagen* (EtymDB).
