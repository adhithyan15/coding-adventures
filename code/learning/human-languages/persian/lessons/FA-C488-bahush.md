## You'll want to know: باهوش
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C488-CHARACTER-01]; assesses=[] -->

**باهوش** (*bâhush*) — "clever, intelligent".

> دخترشان خیلی باهوش است.
>
> *dokhtarashân kheyli bâhush ast.*
>
> Their daughter is very clever.

*bâ* 'with' + *hush* 'intelligence, wits'. The opposite in speech is *kamhush*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*bâhush* is built on native Persian *bâ*, 'with', and *hush*, 'wits', which is usually traced to Middle Persian *hōš*. EtymDB has no entry for it.
