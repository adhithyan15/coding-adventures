## You'll want to know: ਨਿਯਮ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C253-RULE253-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ਨਿਯਮ** — *niyam* — "a rule".

> ਬੈਂਕ ਦਾ ਨਿਯਮ ਸਖ਼ਤ ਹੈ।
>
> *baiṅk dā niyam sakht hai.*
>
> The bank's rule is strict.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਨਿਯਮ** (*niyam*) is taken over from Sanskrit *niyama*, "any fixed rule or law", a tatsama (Monier-Williams).
