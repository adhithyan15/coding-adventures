## You'll want to know: معلومات
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C303-INKAR303-04]; assesses=[] -->

**معلومات** — *mālūmāt* — "information".

> صحافی کے پاس نئی معلومات تھیں۔
>
> *sahāfī ke pās naī mālūmāt thīṅ.*
>
> The journalist had new information.

Feminine, used in the plural.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**معلومات** (*mālūmāt*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *maʿlūmāt*, "things known", a plural. Urdu treats it as feminine plural: **نئی معلومات تھیں**.
