## You'll want to know: ആഗോള
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C460-AGOLAM460-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ആഗോള** — *āgōḷa* — "global".

> ആഗോള വിപണിയിൽ എണ്ണയുടെ വില കുറഞ്ഞു.
>
> *āgōḷa vipaṇiyil eṇṇayuṭe vila kuṟaññu.*
>
> The price of oil fell on the global market.

It stands before a noun: ആഗോള പ്രശ്നം, a global problem.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ആഗോള** (*āgōḷa*) is a modern learned word built from Sanskrit *ā-*, "up to, all round", and *gola*, "a ball, a globe" (Monier-Williams): to do with the whole globe.
