## You'll want to know: বন্ধক
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C419-ABHAB419-04]; assesses=[] -->

**বন্ধক** — *bôndhôk* — "a pledge; a mortgage".

> দরকারে বাবা জমিটা বন্ধক রাখলেন।
>
> *dôrkāre bābā jômiṭā bôndhôk rākhlen.*
>
> In his need my father mortgaged the land.

বন্ধক রাখা, to pawn or to mortgage; বন্ধক ছাড়ানো, to redeem a pledge.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**বন্ধক** (*bôndhôk*) is Sanskrit *bandhaka*, "pledging, a pledge" (Monier-Williams), from *bandh*, to bind: a thing bound as a surety.
