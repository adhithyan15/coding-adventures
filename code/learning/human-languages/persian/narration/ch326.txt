Persian, chapter 326: The injured.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
مصدوم (masdum) — injured; an injured person.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to be harmed, then the Persian for a fracture.

You'll want to know: مصدوم.
مصدوم (masdum) — "injured; an injured person".
مصدوم (masdum) را با آمبولانس به بیمارستان بردند. Masdum râ bâ âmbulâns be bimârestân bordand. They took the injured man to hospital by ambulance.
In news reports; plural masdumân or masdumin.

The word, taken apart.
masdum is usually traced to Arabic maṣdūm, "struck", a participle of the same root as ṣadma "a blow"; EtymDB lists no entry.

You'll want to know: جراحت.
جراحت (jerâhat) — "a wound, an injury".
جراحت (jerâhat) او عمیق نبود. Jerâhat-e u 'amiq nabud. His wound was not deep.
Formal; in speech zakhm. The same root gives jarrâh "a surgeon".

The word, taken apart.
jerâhat is usually traced to Arabic jirāḥa, "wounds, wounding"; EtymDB lists no entry.

Lesson 3 of 5.
خونریزی (khunrizi) — bleeding; bloodshed.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for injured, then the Persian for a wound.

You'll want to know: خونریزی.
خونریزی (khunrizi) — "bleeding; bloodshed".
پرستار خونریزی را متوقف کرد. Parastâr khunrizi râ motevaqqef kard. The nurse stopped the bleeding.
khun "blood" + rizi "pouring", from riz-, the present stem of rikhtan.

The word, taken apart.
khunrizi joins khun "blood" and rizi "pouring", from rikhtan "to pour", which EtymDB traces to Middle Persian. All of it is native Persian.

Lesson 4 of 5.
بیهوش شدن (bihush shodan) — to faint, to pass out.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a wound, then the Persian for bleeding.

You'll want to know: بیهوش شدن.
بیهوش شدن (bihush shodan) — "to faint, to pass out".
زن از گرما بیهوش شد. Zan az garmâ bihush shod. The woman fainted from the heat.
bi "without" + hush "consciousness". Present bihush mishavad.

The word, taken apart.
hush "consciousness, mind" is inherited: EtymDB takes it back through Middle Persian to an Indo-European word for "ear". bi "without" and shodan "to become" are native too.

Lesson 5 of 5.
به هوش آمدن (be hush âmadan) — to come round, to regain consciousness.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for bleeding, then the Persian for to faint.

You'll want to know: به هوش آمدن.
به هوش آمدن (be hush âmadan) — "to come round, to regain consciousness".
او بعد از ده دقیقه به هوش آمد. U ba'd az dah daqiqe be hush âmad. She came round after ten minutes.
Literally "to come to consciousness". Present stem â-: be hush miâyad.

The word, taken apart.
hush "consciousness" is inherited from Middle Persian (EtymDB), and âmadan "to come" is native Persian too; EtymDB takes it back to the Indo-European root gʷem- "to step".
