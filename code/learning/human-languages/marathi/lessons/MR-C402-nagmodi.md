## You'll want to know: नागमोडी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C402-GHAT402-04]; assesses=[] -->

**नागमोडी** — *nāgmoḍī* — "winding; zigzag".

> घाटातला नागमोडी रस्ता सुंदर दिसतो.
>
> *ghāṭātlā nāgmoḍī rastā sundar disto.*
>
> The winding road through the ghat looks beautiful.

It does not change for gender: नागमोडी रस्ता, नागमोडी वाट.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**नागमोडी** (*nāgmoḍī*) is a Marathi compound: **नाग**, "a snake", a tatsama from Sanskrit *nāga* (Monier-Williams), and *moḍī*, usually linked with **मोडणे**, "to bend, to break": a road that bends like a snake. How the second part was formed is not settled.
