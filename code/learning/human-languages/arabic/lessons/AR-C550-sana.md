## You'll want to know: صان
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C550-SIYANA550-05]; assesses=[] -->

**صان** — *ṣāna* — "to maintain, to look after (a thing)".

> يصون جدي ساعته القديمة.
>
> *yaṣūnu jaddī sāʿatahu al-qadīma.*
>
> My grandfather takes care of his old watch.

Present: يصون, *yaṣūn*.

The noun صيانة is maintenance.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**صان** (*ṣāna*) is built on the native Arabic root *ṣ-w-n*, "to preserve, to guard" (Lane). Lane gives ṣāna, aor. yaṣūnu, as to keep a thing safe.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
