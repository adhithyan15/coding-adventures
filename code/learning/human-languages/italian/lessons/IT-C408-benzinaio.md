## You'll want to know: il benzinaio
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C408-BENZINAIO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**il benzinaio** — "the petrol station attendant; the petrol station".

> Il benzinaio controlla l'olio.
>
> *The attendant checks the oil.*

In speech *andare dal benzinaio* means to go to the petrol station.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*benzinaio* is built on *benzina* 'petrol', which is usually traced to German *Benzin*, named after benzoin, a resin; the word came into Italian as a borrowing.
