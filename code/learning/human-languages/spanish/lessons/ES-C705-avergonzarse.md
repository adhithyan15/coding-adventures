## You'll want to know: avergonzarse
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C705-ORGULLO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**avergonzarse** — "to be ashamed".

> No te avergüences de llorar.
>
> *Do not be ashamed of crying.*

*Avergonzarse de algo*. Stressed forms take *üe*: *me avergüenzo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*avergonzarse* is built on *vergüenza* "shame" with the prefix *a-*; *vergüenza* is inherited from Latin *verēcundia* (EtymDB).
