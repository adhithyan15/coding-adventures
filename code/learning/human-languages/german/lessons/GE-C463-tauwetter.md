## You'll want to know: das Tauwetter
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C463-RAUREIF-04]; assesses=[] -->

**das Tauwetter** — "the thaw".

> Mit dem Tauwetter werden die Wege nass und matschig.
>
> *With the thaw the paths become wet and muddy.*

No plural. Also figurative, in politics.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Tauwetter* is built from *tauen* 'to thaw' and *Wetter* 'weather', both inherited Germanic words.
