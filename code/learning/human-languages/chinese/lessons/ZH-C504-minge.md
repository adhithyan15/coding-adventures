## You'll want to know: míngē
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C504-SONGS504-04]; assesses=[] -->

*míngē* — "folk song".

> *wǒ zuì xǐhuan tīng Zhōngguó míngē* — Chinese folk songs are my favourite.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*míngē* is built from *mín* 'the people' and 歌 'a song', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *mjin* and *ka* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'the common people'.
