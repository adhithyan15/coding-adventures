## You'll want to know: जुआ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C545-JUA545-01]; assesses=[] -->

**जुआ** — *juā* — "gambling".

> जुआ खेलना एक बुरी आदत है।
>
> *Juā khelnā ek burī ādat hai.*
>
> Gambling is a bad habit.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**जुआ** (*juā*) is a tadbhava, inherited from Sanskrit *dyūta*, "gambling" (CDIAL 6608).
