## You'll want to know: رعایت
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C423-SAUDA423-04]; assesses=[] -->

**رعایت** — *riāyat* — "a discount; a concession".

> دکاندار نے ہمیں رعایت دی۔
>
> *dukāndār ne hameṅ riāyat dī.*
>
> The shopkeeper gave us a discount.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**رعایت** (*riāyat*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *riʿāya*, "regard, consideration"; in a shop it is the price taken off.
