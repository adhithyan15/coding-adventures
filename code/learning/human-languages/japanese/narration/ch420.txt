Japanese, chapter 420: Receipts and Fees.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
てすうりょう (tesūryō) — a handling fee.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for one's full name, then the Japanese for to accept applications.

You'll want to know: てすうりょう.
てすうりょう — tesūryō — "a handling fee".
ぎんこうで てすうりょうを はらいました。 — ginkō de tesūryō o haraimashita — "I paid a handling fee at the bank."

The word, taken apart.
てすうりょう (tesūryō) is built from both layers of the vocabulary: the native Japanese (wago) te, "hand", inherited rather than borrowed; the Sino-Japanese sū, "number" (Middle Chinese srjuH), an on'yomi borrowed from Chinese; and the Sino-Japanese ryō, "fee" (lew), an on'yomi borrowed from Chinese (JMdict; KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).

Lesson 2 of 5.
りょうしゅうしょ (ryōshūsho) — a receipt.

Warm-up.

りょうしゅうしょを ください。 — ryōshūsho o kudasai — "A receipt, please."

The word, taken apart.
りょうしゅうしょ (ryōshūsho) is Sino-Japanese (kango): three Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are ryō, "jurisdiction" (Middle Chinese ljengX), shū, "obtain" (syuw) and sho, "write" (syo) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).

Lesson 4 of 5.
しはらう (shiharau) — to pay a bill; to pay a charge.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for a receipt, then the Japanese for postage.

You'll want to know: しはらう.
しはらう — shiharau — "to pay a bill; to pay a charge".
げんきんで しはらいました。 — genkin de shiharaimashita — "I paid in cash."
Polite しはらいます, shiharaimasu; a godan verb. Past (polite): かいしゃが りょひを しはらいました。, kaisha ga ryohi o shiharaimashita.

The word, taken apart.
しはらう (shiharau) is built from both layers of the vocabulary: the Sino-Japanese shi, "support" (Middle Chinese tsye), an on'yomi borrowed from Chinese, and the native Japanese (wago) harau, "to pay", inherited rather than borrowed (JMdict; KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).

Lesson 5 of 5.
はらいもどす (haraimodosu) — to refund.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for postage, then the Japanese for to pay a bill.

You'll want to know: はらいもどす.
はらいもどす — haraimodosu — "to refund".
うんきゅうに なったので、えきが うんちんを はらいもどします。 — unkyū ni natta node, eki ga unchin o haraimodoshimasu — "The service was cancelled, so the station is refunding the fare."
Polite はらいもどします, haraimodoshimasu; a godan verb. Past (polite): えきが うんちんを はらいもどしました。, eki ga unchin o haraimodoshimashita.

The word, taken apart.
はらいもどす (haraimodosu) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, built from harai, "paying" (from harau) and modosu, "to give back"; its kanji were attached later as kun'yomi (JMdict).
