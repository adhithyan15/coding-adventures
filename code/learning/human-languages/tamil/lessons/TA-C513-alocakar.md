## You'll want to know: ஆலோசகர்
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C513-THOZHILALAR513-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ஆலோசகர்** — *ālōcakar* — "an adviser; a consultant".

> நிறுவனம் ஒரு நிதி ஆலோசகரை அமர்த்தியது.
>
> *niṟuvaṉam oru niti ālōcakarai amarttiyatu.*
>
> The company hired a financial adviser.

Built on ஆலோசனை, advice. The person advised takes -க்கு: அரசுக்கு ஆலோசகர், an adviser to the government.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ஆலோசகர்** (*ālōcakar*) is borrowed from Sanskrit: the stem of *ālocana*, "consideration, reflection" (Monier-Williams), with the Tamil personal ending -அர்.
