Lesson 1 of 5.
ટેકો (ṭeko) — backing; support for a person or a plan.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a refutation, then the Gujarati for to cut someone short.

You'll want to know: ટેકો.
ટેકો — ṭeko — "backing; support for a person or a plan".
આખા ગામે આ યોજનાને ટેકો આપ્યો. ākhā gāme ā yojnāne ṭeko āpyo. The whole village backed this plan.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ટેકો (ṭeko) CDIAL lists the related Gujarati ṭek, "support", under a reconstructed ṭakk, "remain, stop" (CDIAL 5420): what keeps a thing standing.

Lesson 2 of 5.
હિમાયત (himāyat) — advocacy.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to cut someone short, then the Gujarati for backing.

You'll want to know: હિમાયત.
હિમાયત — himāyat — "advocacy".
મીરા છોકરીઓના અભ્યાસની હિમાયત કરે છે. Mīrā chokrīonā abhyāsnī himāyat kare che. Mira speaks up for girls' education.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
હિમાયત (himāyat) is usually traced to Arabic ḥimāyat, "protection", through Persian; Gujarati uses it for speaking up for a cause.

હું તમારા મત સાથે સહમત છું. hũ tamārā mat sāthe sahmat chũ. I agree with your view.

સહમત (sahmat) is built from Sanskrit saha, "with", and mata, "an opinion" (Monier-Williams): of the same opinion.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ઢળવું (ḍhaḷvũ) — to lean towards; to slip down.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for against, then the Gujarati for in agreement.

You'll want to know: ઢળવું.
ઢળવું — ḍhaḷvũ — "to lean towards; to slip down".
આખી ચર્ચા પછી મારો મત તમારી બાજુ ઢળે છે. ākhī carcā pachī māro mat tamārī bāju ḍhaḷe che. After the whole discussion my view leans your way.
Past (it …): તે દીવાલ એક બાજુ ઢળી ગઈ, te dīvāl ek bāju ḍhaḷī gaī.

The word, taken apart.
ઢળવું (ḍhaḷvũ) is inherited from ḍhalati, "bends over, falls", a form CDIAL reconstructs; CDIAL glosses the Gujarati verb "to slip, to be poured out" (CDIAL 5581). Leaning towards an opinion is a later sense.
