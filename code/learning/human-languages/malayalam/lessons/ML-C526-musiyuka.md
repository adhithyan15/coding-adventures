# മുഷിയുക (muṣiyuka) — to grow weary; to get grubby

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Malayalam for to thrill, then the Malayalam for to be amused.

## You'll want to know: മുഷിയുക
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C526-RASAM526-05]; assesses=[] -->

**മുഷിയുക** — *muṣiyuka* — "to grow weary; to get grubby".

> ഒരുപാട് നേരം കാത്തിരുന്ന് ഞങ്ങൾ മുഷിഞ്ഞു.
>
> *orupāṭŭ nēraṁ kāttirunnŭ ñaṅṅaḷ muṣiññu.*
>
> We grew weary of waiting so long.

Past: മുഷിഞ്ഞു, *muṣiññu*.

Said of clothes it means to get grubby: മുഷിഞ്ഞ ഷർട്ട്, a grubby shirt. The past is മുഷിഞ്ഞു.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**മുഷിയുക** (*muṣiyuka*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 4903 gives the Malayalam verb with both senses, to grow weary and to get dirty (DEDR 4903).

## What you've built
