## You'll want to know: سعى
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C371-WASILA371-02]; assesses=[] -->

**سعى** — *saʿā* — "to strive".

> سعى الشاب إلى عمل أفضل.
>
> *saʿā ash-shābb ilā ʿamal afḍal.*
>
> The young man strove for a better job.

Present: يسعى, *yasʿā*.

It takes إلى: سعى إلى ….

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**سعى** (*saʿā*) is built on the native Arabic root *s-ʿ-y*, usually traced to the sense "to run, to strive". *Saʿā* is a Form I verb with a weak last letter, so its present is *yasʿā*.
