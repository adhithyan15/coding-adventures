## You'll want to know: ٹونا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C535-SHAGUN535-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ٹونا** — *ṭonā* — "a magic spell".

> اگر ٹونا سچ ہوتا تو سب امیر ہوتے۔
>
> *agar ṭonā sach hotā to sab amīr hote.*
>
> If spells were real, everyone would be rich.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ٹونا** (*ṭonā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *ṭonā*, "sorcery", under *\*ṭōna*, a form it reconstructs and perhaps links with *śakuna*, "a bird of omen" (CDIAL 5480).
