## You'll want to know: 儿歌
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C504-SONGS504-02]; assesses=[] -->

**儿歌** — *érgē* — "nursery rhyme; children's song".

> **这是我小时候最喜欢的儿歌** *zhè shì wǒ xiǎoshíhou zuì xǐhuan de érgē* — This was my favourite nursery rhyme when I was little.

The new character, in pieces: 歌 — fourteen strokes in two pieces: on the left 可 *kě* twice, one over the other, ten strokes; on the right four strokes. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**儿歌** (*érgē*) is built from 儿 'a child' and 歌 'a song', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *nye* and *ka* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'an infant' and the second 'the voice of a person singing'.
