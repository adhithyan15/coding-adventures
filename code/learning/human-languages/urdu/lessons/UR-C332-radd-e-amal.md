## You'll want to know: رد عمل
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C332-NUQSAN332-03]; assesses=[] -->

**رد عمل** — *radd-e-amal* — "a reaction".

> اس خبر پر لوگوں کا رد عمل کیا تھا؟
>
> *is khabar par logoṅ kā radd-e-amal kyā thā?*
>
> How did people react to this news?

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**رد عمل** (*radd-e-amal*) is built from two loans, each usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic: Arabic *radd*, "a turning back", and Arabic *ʿamal*, "an action". An action sent back.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
