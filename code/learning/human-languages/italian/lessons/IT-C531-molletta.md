## You'll want to know: la molletta
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C531-BUCATO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**la molletta** — "clothes peg; hair clip".

> Mi servono altre mollette per le lenzuola.
>
> *I need more pegs for the sheets.*

Also a hair clip.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*molletta* is a little *molla* "spring" (EtymDB), from *mollare* "to let go", built on Latin *mollis* "soft" (Lewis & Short).
