## You'll want to know: जिल्द
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C341-PANNA341-02]; assesses=[] -->

**जिल्द** — *jild* — "a binding; a cover (of a book)".

> पुरानी किताब की जिल्द टूट गई।
>
> *purānī kitāb kī jild ṭūṭ gaī.*
>
> The old book's binding came apart.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**जिल्द** (*jild*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *jild*, "skin, hide": books were once bound in leather.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
