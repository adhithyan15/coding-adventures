Russian, chapter 31: Back, Tongue, Lip, Face, Blood.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
спина (spiná) — the back.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a shoulder, then the Russian for a knee.

You'll want to know: спина.
спина (spiná) — "the back".
