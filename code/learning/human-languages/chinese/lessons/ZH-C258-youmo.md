## You'll want to know: yōumò
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C258-KIND258-04]; assesses=[] -->

*yōumò* — "humorous; witty (of a person)".

> *wǒ bàba hěn yōumò* — My dad has a great sense of humour.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*yōumò* is usually traced to English *humour*, written with two characters whose sounds come close to it, *yōu* 'deep' and *mò* 'silent'; who first wrote it this way is not documented in what we can open.
