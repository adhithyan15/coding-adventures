## You'll want to know: la culla
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C433-CAMERA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**la culla** — "the cot, the cradle".

> Avete una culla per il bambino?
>
> *Do you have a cot for the baby?*

Figuratively: *la culla della civiltà* — the cradle of civilisation.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*culla* is inherited from Vulgar Latin *cunla* (EtymDB), a little form of Latin *cūnae* 'cradle'.
