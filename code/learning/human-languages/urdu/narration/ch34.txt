Urdu, chapter 34: Letters on the Boat.
3 lessons. The first lesson already needs your eyes or your hands, so save this one for when you have stopped.


Lesson 1 of 3.
ب (be) — the letter that opens بولنا.

Before we start: this one needs your hands, so it is not a driving lesson. You will want a pen and something to write on and your eyes, for letter shapes on the page. You can listen to everything else now — leave the sections called Script you'll notice: ب and Guided Practice until you have stopped, and I will say so again when we reach it.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: write پ and و once each, and name them.
[pause 2 seconds]
Now say بولنا (bolnā), "to speak". You have read it for a long time. This lesson takes one letter out of it and puts it in your hand.

Script you'll notice: ب.
ب — be, the sound b.
It opens بولنا (bolnā), "to speak". You already write پ, the shallow boat with three dots beneath it. ب is the same boat with one dot below.

Writing: ب.
1. start here: sweep the independent be-series bowl from right to left.
2. after one lift, place the single dot below — and only now lift.
Pen lifts: 1.
Stroke order is one attested teaching order; hands and teachers vary. Source: Zer o Zabar, Be, kāf, and short vowels, independent ب (be) handwriting animation and Be instructions (Northwestern University Libraries Open Textbook, 2026).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: write و and ب (be) once each, and name them.
[pause 2 seconds]
Now say تم (tum), "you (familiar)". You have read it for a long time. This lesson takes one letter out of it and puts it in your hand.

Script you'll notice: ت.
ت — te, the sound t, with the tongue against the teeth.
It opens تم (tum), the familiar "you". The same boat again, now with two dots above.

Writing: ت.
1. start here: sweep the independent be-series bowl from right to left.
2. after one lift, place the left dot above the main line.
3. after another lift, place the right dot beside it — and only now lift.
Pen lifts: 2.
Stroke order is one attested teaching order; hands and teachers vary. Source: Zer o Zabar, Te, mīm, jīm, che, and more diacritics, independent ت handwriting animation and Te instructions (Northwestern University Libraries Open Textbook, 2026).

Lesson 3 of 3.
ٹ (ṭe) — the letter that opens ٹھیک.

Before we start: this one needs your hands, so it is not a driving lesson. You will want a pen and something to write on and your eyes, for letter shapes on the page. You can listen to everything else now — leave the sections called Script you'll notice: ٹ and Guided Practice until you have stopped, and I will say so again when we reach it.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: write ب (be) and ت once each, and name them.
[pause 2 seconds]
Now say ٹھیک (ṭhīk), "fine, well". You have read it for a long time. This lesson takes one letter out of it and puts it in your hand.

Script you'll notice: ٹ.
ٹ — ṭe, a t made with the tongue curled back.
It opens ٹھیک (ṭhīk), "fine". Say tum, then ṭhīk: the first t touches the teeth, the second is struck further back. Urdu marks that curled-back sound with a small mark above the boat, where ت has its two dots. The dot under the romanization (ṭ) marks the same thing.

Writing: ٹ.
1. start here: sweep the independent be-series bowl from right to left.
2. after one lift, draw the small retroflex mark downward, back upward, and down again to close its loop — and only now lift.
Pen lifts: 1.
Stroke order is one attested teaching order; hands and teachers vary. Source: Zer o Zabar, Fe, qāf, ṭe, ḍāl, and ṛe, independent ٹ handwriting animation and Ṭe instructions (Northwestern University Libraries Open Textbook, 2026).
