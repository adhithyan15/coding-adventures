## You'll want to know: причаливать
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C425-KACHKA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**причаливать** (*prichálivat*) — "to moor, to dock".

> Паром причаливает к пристани.
>
> *Paróm prichálivayet k prístani.*
>
> The ferry docks at the landing stage.

Imperfective; perfective *причалить*. Takes *к* + dative.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*причаливать* is built from *при-* 'to' and *-чаливать*, from *чалить* 'to tie up (a boat)'. EtymDB lists *чалить* without a source, so its origin is left open.
