## You'll want to know: عذب
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C470-YANBU470-05]; assesses=[] -->

**عذب** — *ʿadhb* — "sweet (of water); fresh".

> ماء الينبوع عذب.
>
> *māʾ al-yanbūʿ ʿadhb.*
>
> The water of the spring is sweet.

Of water, sweet means fresh, not salty.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**عذب** (*ʿadhb*) is built on the native Arabic root *ʿ-dh-b*, "to be sweet (of water)" (Lane).
