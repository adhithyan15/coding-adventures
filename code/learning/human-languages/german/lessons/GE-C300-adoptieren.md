## You'll want to know: adoptieren
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C300-SCHEIDUNG-04]; assesses=[] -->

**adoptieren** — "to adopt".

> Sie adoptierten zwei Kinder.
>
> *They adopted two children.*

Regular, without *ge-*: *hat adoptiert*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*adoptieren* is a learned borrowing from Latin *adoptāre* "to adopt" (EtymDB), built on *optāre* "to choose" (Lewis & Short).
