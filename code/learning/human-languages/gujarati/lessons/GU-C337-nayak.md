## You'll want to know: નાયક
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C337-PANU337-04]; assesses=[] -->

**નાયક** — *nāyak* — "the hero (of a story)".

> વાર્તાનો નાયક એક ગરીબ છોકરો છે.
>
> *vārtāno nāyak ek garīb chokro che.*
>
> The hero of the story is a poor boy.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

is a tatsama, Sanskrit *nāyaka*, "a leader", and in drama "the hero" (Monier-Williams). **વીર** is a brave man; the **નાયક** is the main person of a story.
