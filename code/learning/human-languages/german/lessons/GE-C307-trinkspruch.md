## You'll want to know: der Trinkspruch
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C307-GASTGEBER-05]; assesses=[] -->

**der Trinkspruch** — "the toast (with a drink)".

> Nach dem Essen brachte er einen Trinkspruch aus.
>
> *After the meal he proposed a toast.*

Plural: *die Trinksprüche*. *Einen Trinkspruch ausbringen*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Trinkspruch* joins *trinken* "to drink", Proto-Germanic *drinkaną*, and *Spruch* "saying", Middle High German *spruch* (EtymDB).
