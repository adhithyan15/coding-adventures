## You'll want to know: উপদেশ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C325-UPODESH325-01]; assesses=[] -->

**উপদেশ** — *upôdesh* — "a moral lesson; counsel".

> গল্পের শেষে দাদু একটা উপদেশ দিলেন।
>
> *gôlper sheshe dādu ekṭā upôdesh dilen.*
>
> At the end of the story my grandfather gave us a lesson to remember.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**উপদেশ** (*upôdesh*) is Sanskrit *upadeśa*, 'pointing out; instruction, advice' (Monier-Williams); it has nothing to do with **দেশ**, a country. Everyday advice is **পরামর্শ**.
