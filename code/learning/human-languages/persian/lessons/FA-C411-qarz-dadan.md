## You'll want to know: قرض دادن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C411-QARZ-02]; assesses=[] -->

**قرض دادن** (*qarz dâdan*) — "to lend".

> برادرم ماشینش را به من قرض داد.
>
> *barâdaram mâshinash râ be man qarz dâd.*
>
> My brother lent me his car.

*Qarz* + *dâdan* "to give" (stem *deh-*). *Be kasi qarz dâdan* — to lend to someone.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**قرض دادن** is literally "to give a loan": *qarz* comes from Arabic *qarḍ* "a loan" (EtymDB), and *dâdan* "to give" is traced by EtymDB to PIE *deh₃-*.
