## You'll want to know: माफी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C270-MAPHI270-01]; assesses=[] -->

**माफी** — *māphī* — "pardon".

> हूं माफी मांगूं हूं।
>
> *hū̃ māphī māṅgū̃ hū̃.*
>
> I'm sorry: I ask your pardon.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**माफी** (*māphī*) is usually traced, through Persian *muʿāfī*, "pardon", to Arabic *muʿāf*, "excused".
