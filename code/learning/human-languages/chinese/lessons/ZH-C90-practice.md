## The exchange
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-PERFORMANCE-CAN-01]; assesses=[ZH-LEX-C90-HUI-01, ZH-LEX-C90-NENG-01, ZH-LEX-C90-KEYI-01, ZH-LEX-C90-YOUYONG-01, ZH-LEX-C80-JOB80-01, ZH-LEX-C72-TRAVEL72-02] -->

> **A:** 你会开车吗
>
> *nǐ huì kāichē ma* — Can you drive?
>
> **B:** 会
>
> *huì* — I can.
>
> **A:** 你明天能开车吗
>
> *nǐ míngtiān néng kāichē ma* — Can you drive tomorrow?
>
> **B:** *bù néng, wǒ míngtiān shàngbān* — No, I'm at work tomorrow.
>
> **A:** *nǐ huì yóuyǒng ma* — Can you swim?
>
> **B:** 不会
>
> *bú huì* — I can't.
>
> **A:** *wǒ kěyǐ zuò zhèr ma* — May I sit here?
>
> **B:** *kěyǐ* — Yes, please.

The answer to each is the verb alone: 会, 不能, 不会, *kěyǐ*. Mandarin answers yes or no by saying the verb again.

## What you've built — three cans
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ZH-PERFORMANCE-CAN-01, ZH-SCRIPT-NENG-01, ZH-LEX-C90-HUI-01, ZH-LEX-C90-NENG-01, ZH-LEX-C90-KEYI-01] -->

- **我会开车** *wǒ huì kāichē* — I have learned to drive.
- **我明天不能开车** *wǒ míngtiān bù néng kāichē* — tomorrow I'm not able to.
- *wǒ kěyǐ zuò zhèr ma* — may I sit here?

**Listen.** *Nǐ huì shuō Hànyǔ ma?*

**Speak.** Answer it, then say one thing you cannot do today, and ask permission for something.

[YOU WRITE: 能 — the whole left side first, then the two hooked pieces on the right]
