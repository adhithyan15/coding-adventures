## You'll want to know: મંડપ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C330-GARBO330-01]; assesses=[] -->

**મંડપ** — *manḍap* — "a pavilion (for a festival)".

> ચોકમાં મોટો મંડપ હતો.
>
> *cokmā̃ moṭo manḍap hato.*
>
> There was a big pavilion in the square.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**મંડપ** (*manḍap*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *maṇḍapa*, "an open temporary shed, a pavilion" (CDIAL 9740). It is put up for weddings and festivals.
