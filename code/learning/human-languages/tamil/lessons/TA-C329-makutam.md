# மகுடம் (makuṭam) — a crown

## You'll want to know: மகுடம்
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C329-ARASAVAI329-02]; assesses=[] -->

**மகுடம்** — *makuṭam* — "a crown".

> மன்னரின் மகுடத்தில் பல வைரங்கள் இருந்தன.
>
> *maṉṉariṉ makuṭattil pala vairaṅkaḷ iruntaṉa.*
>
> There were many diamonds in the king's crown.

Also a crowning glory: வெற்றியின் மகுடம்.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**மகுடம்** (*makuṭam*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *mukuṭa*, *makuṭa*, "tiara, crest" (CDIAL 10144); CDIAL notes that the Sanskrit word may itself come from a Dravidian word for "top".
