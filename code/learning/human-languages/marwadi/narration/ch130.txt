Lesson 2 of 5.
नथ (nath) — a nose ring.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for a bangle, then the Marwadi for a forehead ornament.

You'll want to know: नथ.
नथ — nath — "a nose ring".

The word, taken apart.
नथ (nath) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from nasta, "nose". CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry (CDIAL 7031); the match is through Gujarati nath. CDIAL lists Gujarati nath, "nose ring for women", and Hindi nāth, "nose rope", under it.

The word, taken apart.
पायल (pāyal) is a tadbhava, a word inherited and reshaped by sound change. It comes from pādala, "appertaining to the feet", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants. CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry (CDIAL 8066); the match is through Hindi pāyal. It is built on pāda, "foot".
