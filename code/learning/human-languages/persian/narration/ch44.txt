Persian, chapter 44: Dinner, Juice, Cake, Cheese, Milk.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
شام (shâm) — dinner.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for breakfast, then the Persian for lunch.

You'll want to know: شام.
شام — shâm — "dinner".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: shâm]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: shâm, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say shâm]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Persian for breakfast, then the Persian for lunch, then say shâm again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does شام (shâm) mean? ("Dinner".) Say it once more.
