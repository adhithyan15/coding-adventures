## You'll want to know: সম্ভাবনা
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C366-SOMBHABONA366-01]; assesses=[] -->

**সম্ভাবনা** — *sômbhābônā* — "a likelihood; a chance".

> আজ ঝড়ের সম্ভাবনা আছে, তাই বাইরে না গেলে ভালো।
>
> *āj jhôṛer sômbhābônā āchhe, tāi bāire nā gele bhālo.*
>
> There's a chance of a storm today, so it's better not to go out.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**সম্ভাবনা** (*sômbhābônā*) is Sanskrit *sambhāvanā*, supposing or assuming (Monier-Williams), from the same root as সম্ভব, possible. Bengali uses it for how likely a thing is.
