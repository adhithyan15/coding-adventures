# خوشحال بودم (khoshhâl budam) — I was happy, and I became happy

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[FA-LEX-C31-HEALTH31-05, FA-LEX-C129-QUAL129-01, FA-LEX-C32-HEALTH32-01, FA-LEX-C40-VERBS40-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "happy", "tired", "upset". (*Khoshhâl*, *khaste*, *nârâhat*.)
Then "to become". (*Shodan.*)

[PAUSE 1s] You can say what happened on a trip. Describing it means saying
how it felt, and Persian gives a feeling two pasts as well.

## Grammar Lens: the feeling as scene
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-GRAMMAR-FEELING-BUDAM-01]; assesses=[FA-GRAMMAR-SCENE-EVENT-01, FA-LEX-C129-QUAL129-03] -->

The adjective, then *budan* in the past:

> **خوشحال بودم.** — *khoshhâl budam* — I was happy.

*Khaste budam*, I was tired; *nârâhat budim*, we were upset; *shâd bud*, she
was cheerful. The adjective stays the same for every person, and the ending
rides on *bud-*. This is the feeling as background, there for a while.

## Grammar Lens: the feeling as event
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-GRAMMAR-FEELING-SHODAM-01]; assesses=[FA-LEX-C35-VERBS35-03, FA-GRAMMAR-VAGHTI-01, FA-LEX-AZ-DAST-DADAN-01, FA-GRAMMAR-PROBLEM-PAST-01] -->

Swap *budan* for *shodan*, "to become", and the feeling arrives at a moment:

> **خسته شدم.** — *khaste shodam* — I got tired.

> **وقتی قطار را از دست دادم، ناراحت شدم.** — *vaqti qatâr râ az dast dâdam,
> nârâhat shodam* — When I missed the train, I got upset.

The cause can come with *az*, "from", and the infinitive works as a noun:

> **از دیدن شما خوشحال شدم.** — *az didan-e shomâ khoshhâl shodam* — I was
> glad to see you.

Word for word, "from the seeing of you I became happy". It is a thing people
say at the end of a first meeting.

So *khoshhâl budam* is the mood of the whole day, and *khoshhâl shodam* is
the moment the news arrived.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Shodan* is usually traced to Middle Persian *šudan*, "to go". Becoming is
going into a state: *khaste shodam* is "I went tired". *Khoshhâl* is *khosh*,
"good", with *hâl*, "state", the same *hâl* as in *hâl-e shomâ chetor ast?*
