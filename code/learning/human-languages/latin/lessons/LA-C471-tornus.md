## You'll want to know: tornus, tornī
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C471-TORNUS-01]; assesses=[] -->

**tornus, tornī** — "a lathe".

> Faber in officīnā tornum habet et pōcula lignea facit.
>
> *The craftsman has a lathe in his workshop and makes wooden cups.*

Second declension, masculine: *tornus, tornī m.* Also the finish of a poem, as if turned on a lathe.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*tornus* was borrowed from Greek *tornos* 'lathe, a tool for drawing circles' (EtymDB; Lewis & Short), from PIE *terh₁-* 'to rub, to twist'.
