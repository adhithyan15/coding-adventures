## You'll want to know: అంటువ్యాధి
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C513-VYADHI513-02]; assesses=[] -->

**అంటువ్యాధి** — *aṇṭuvyādhi* — "a contagious disease".

> అంటువ్యాధి వచ్చినప్పుడు బడులు మూసేశారు.
>
> *aṇṭuvyādhi vaccinappuḍu baḍulu mūsēśāru.*
>
> When the contagious disease broke out, the schools were shut.

అంటు, contact, + వ్యాధి: a disease that spreads by contact.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**అంటువ్యాధి** (*aṇṭuvyādhi*) joins native *aṇṭu*, "to touch; contact, defilement by touch" (DEDR 120), to Sanskrit *vyādhi*, "disease" (CDIAL 12202).
