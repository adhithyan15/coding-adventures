# ਔਰਤ (aurat) — a woman

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-LEX-C55-FAMILY55-05, PA-GRAMMAR-PAST-AAIA-217] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new word: say *a man*, **ਆਦਮੀ**. Then *the girl came
yesterday*.

## You'll want to know: ਔਰਤ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-AURAT-217]; assesses=[PA-LEX-C55-FAMILY55-05, PA-GRAMMAR-PAST-AAIA-217, PA-LEX-KALL-216] -->

**ਔਰਤ** — *aurat* — "a woman". A feminine noun, so the past that agrees with
her ends like **ਆਈ**:

> **ਔਰਤ ਕੱਲ੍ਹ ਆਈ।** — *aurat kallh āī.* — The woman came yesterday.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਔਰਤ** (*aurat*) is a loan: it is usually traced to Persian *aurat*, "a
woman", a word Persian itself took from Arabic. CDIAL gives no Punjabi form.
