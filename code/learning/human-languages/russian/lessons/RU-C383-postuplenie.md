## You'll want to know: поступление
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C383-POSTUPLENIYE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**поступление** (*postupléniye*) — "admission (to a university); entry".

> Поступление в университет — важный шаг.
>
> *Postupléniye v universitét — vázhnyy shag.*
>
> Getting into university is an important step.

Neuter. From *поступать*, to enter a university. It also means money coming in: *поступление денег*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**поступление** is built on *поступить* "to enter", from *по-* and *ступить* "to step", an inherited Slavic verb: the step into a university.
