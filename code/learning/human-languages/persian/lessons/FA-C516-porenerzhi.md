## You'll want to know: پرانرژی
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C516-FITNESS-04]; assesses=[] -->

**پرانرژی** (*porenerzhi*) — "energetic, full of energy".

> بچه ها صبحها پرانرژی هستند.
>
> *bachchehâ sobhhâ porenerzhi hastand.*
>
> The children are full of energy in the mornings.

*por* 'full' + *enerzhi* 'energy'.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*porenerzhi* is built in Persian on native *por*, 'full', and *enerzhi*, 'energy', which was borrowed from French *énergie* (EtymDB).
