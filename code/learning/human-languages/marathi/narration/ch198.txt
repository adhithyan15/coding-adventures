5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
दाबणे (dābṇe) — to press.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to chat, then the Marathi for to deliver.

You'll want to know: दाबणे.
दाबणे — dābṇe — "to press".

The word, taken apart.
दाबणे (dābṇe) comes from a reconstructed dabb-, "press" (CDIAL 6173). CDIAL reports A. Master's view that it comes from Dravidian.

खोकणे — khokṇe — "to cough".

The word, taken apart.
खोकणे (khokṇe) is a tadbhava, a word inherited and reshaped by sound change. It comes from khokkh, "bark, cough", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 3926).

थरथरणे (thartharṇe) is a tadbhava, a word inherited and reshaped by sound change. It comes from thar, "tremble", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 6092). CDIAL compares Buddhist Sanskrit tharatharāyamāṇa, "trembling".

मत देणे — mat deṇe — "to vote".

The word, taken apart.
मत is Sanskrit mata, "thought, opinion", a tatsama. देणे comes from Sanskrit dadāti, "gives" (CDIAL 6141).
