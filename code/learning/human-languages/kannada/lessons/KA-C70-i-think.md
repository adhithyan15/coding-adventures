# ಅದು ನಿಜ ಅಂತ ಯೋಚಿಸುತ್ತೇನೆ (adu nija anta yōcisuttēne) — I think that is true

## Warm-up

## You'll want to know: ಅದು ನಿಜ ಅಂತ ಯೋಚಿಸುತ್ತೇನೆ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C70-TELL-06]; assesses=[] -->

**ಅದು ನಿಜ ಅಂತ ಯೋಚಿಸುತ್ತೇನೆ** (*adu nija anta yōcisuttēne*) — "I think that is
true."

ಯೋಚಿಸು (*yōcisu*, "to think") has been in this book since the chapter of mind
verbs, and until the last lesson it could not do the one thing thinking is for.
A thought is a whole sentence, and Kannada could not hand it one without ಅಂತ.

Read it in Kannada order: **ಅದು ನಿಜ** (the thought) — **ಅಂತ** (closed off) —
**ಯೋಚಿಸುತ್ತೇನೆ** ("I think").

## Grammar Lens: one frame, and the slot you change
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-PRAGMATICS-C70-TELL-07]; assesses=[] -->

Only the first slot ever changes:

**ಕಾಫಿ ಒಳ್ಳೆಯದು ಅಂತ ಯೋಚಿಸುತ್ತೇನೆ** — I think coffee is good.
**ಅದು ನಿಜ ಅಲ್ಲ ಅಂತ ಯೋಚಿಸುತ್ತೇನೆ** — I think that is not true.

An opinion delivered flat is a claim; the same one with ಅಲ್ವಾ after it is an
invitation.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

Agree, disagree, give a reason, say what you think. That is a conversation.
