# Review — who did it, and what will happen

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[UR-GRAMMAR-C264-PAST-01, UR-GRAMMAR-C264-WAS-02] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "she went home" and "I used to go to school". (*voh ghar gaī*, *maiṅ iskūl jātā thā*.)

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[UR-GRAMMAR-C265-ERGATIVE-01, UR-GRAMMAR-C265-ERGATIVE-02, UR-GRAMMAR-C265-FUTURE-01, UR-GRAMMAR-C265-FUTURE-02, UR-SCRIPT-C265-WAW-HAMZA-01, UR-LEX-ROTI, UR-LEX-C113-THINGS113-03, UR-LEX-BARISH] -->

[PAUSE 2s each]
- [YOU SAY: He ate bread. Sara wrote a letter.]
- [YOU SAY: I did the work, and she gave me tea.]
- [YOU SAY: Sara saw me at the market.]
- [YOU SAY: Tomorrow I will go home. We will go too.]
- [YOU SAY: It will rain. He will probably be at home.]
- [YOU WRITE: **جاؤں** and **آؤ**, then read them aloud]

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[UR-GRAMMAR-C265-ERGATIVE-01, UR-GRAMMAR-C265-ERGATIVE-02, UR-GRAMMAR-C265-FUTURE-01, UR-GRAMMAR-C265-FUTURE-02] -->

[PAUSE 3s] In **سارا نے خط لکھا**, which word sets **لکھا**? (**خط**.) Put **کتاب** in place of **خط**: does the verb change? (Yes: **پڑھی**, **لکھی**.) Now the other way round, the future: "she will go"? (**وہ جائے گی**.) "We will go"? (**ہم جائیں گے**.)
