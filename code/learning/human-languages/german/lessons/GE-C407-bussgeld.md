## You'll want to know: das Bußgeld
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C407-BLITZER-04]; assesses=[] -->

**das Bußgeld** — "the fine (for a traffic offence)".

> Das Bußgeld für zu schnelles Fahren ist hoch.
>
> *The fine for driving too fast is high.*

Plural: *die Bußgelder*. *Der Bußgeldbescheid* is the letter that comes in the post.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Bußgeld* is built from *Buße* 'amends, penance', inherited from Old High German *buoza* (EtymDB), and *Geld* 'money', also an inherited Germanic word.
