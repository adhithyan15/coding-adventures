## You'll want to know: le marais
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C456-RUISSEAU-03]; assesses=[] -->

**le marais** — "the marsh, the swamp".

> Beaucoup d'oiseaux vivent dans le marais.
>
> *Many birds live in the marsh.*

*Les marais salants* — salt marshes.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*marais* comes from Old French *mareis*, from Frankish *marisk* 'marsh' (EtymDB).
