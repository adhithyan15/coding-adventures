## You'll want to know: ぎょうぎ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C327-REIGI327-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ぎょうぎ** — *gyōgi* — "behaviour; manners at table".

> **ぎょうぎが いいので、みんなが ほめました。** — *gyōgi ga ii node, minna ga homemashita* — "She behaves well, so everyone praised her."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ぎょうぎ** (*gyōgi*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *gyō*, "conduct" (Middle Chinese *haengH*) and *gi*, "ceremony" (*ngje*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
