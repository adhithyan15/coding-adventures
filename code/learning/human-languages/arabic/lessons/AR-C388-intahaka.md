## You'll want to know: انتهك
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C388-FASAD388-04]; assesses=[] -->

**انتهك** — *intahaka* — "to violate".

> انتهك الجيران حقنا في النوم.
>
> *intahaka al-jīrān ḥaqqanā fī an-nawm.*
>
> The neighbours violated our right to sleep.

Present: ينتهك, *yantahik*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**انتهك** (*intahaka*) is built on the native Arabic root *n-h-k*, "to wear out, to exhaust" (Lane). It is Form VIII; Lane gives it for violating what is held sacred: to violate.
