Lesson 4 of 5.
Put the noun into one identity statement — he or she is a student.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Retrieve ਉਹ, then ਵਿਦਿਆਰਥੀ (vidiarthii), then ਹੈ.

Grammar Lens.
ਉਹ ਵਿਦਿਆਰਥੀ ਹੈ (oh vidiarthii hai) — oh vidiārthī hai — he or she is a student.
Punjabi ਉਹ is gender-neutral in this frame; the pronoun itself does not mark “he” versus “she.” Keep the noun in the middle and the copula last.

Guided Practice.
