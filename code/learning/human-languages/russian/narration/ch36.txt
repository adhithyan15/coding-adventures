Russian, chapter 36: Children, a Child, Aunt, Grandchildren.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
дети (déti) — children.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a husband, then the Russian for a wife.

You'll want to know: дети.
дети (déti) — "children".

What you've built.
One more word for children, a child, aunt, grandchildren.
