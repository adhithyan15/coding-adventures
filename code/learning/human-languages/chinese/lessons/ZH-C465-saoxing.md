## You'll want to know: 扫兴
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C465-FUN465-02]; assesses=[] -->

**扫兴** — *sǎoxìng* — "deflated; with the fun spoiled".

> **下雨了真扫兴** *xiàyǔ le zhēn sǎoxìng* — It's raining. What a let-down.

The new character, in pieces: 兴 — six strokes in three pieces: at the top a dot, a second dot and a short stroke falling to the left; a long bar across; and under it 八 *bā*, two strokes. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**扫兴** (*sǎoxìng*) is built from 扫 'to sweep' and 兴 'high spirits', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *sawX* and *xingH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription). The dictionary glosses the first 'to sweep away'; it gives the second reading no gloss of its own, so 'high spirits' is the usual explanation, not a settled one.
