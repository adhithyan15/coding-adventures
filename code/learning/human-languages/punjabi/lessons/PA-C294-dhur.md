## You'll want to know: ਧੂੜ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C294-POOR294-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ਧੂੜ** — *dhūṛ* — "dust".

> ਸੜਕ ਉੱਤੇ ਬਹੁਤ ਧੂੜ ਹੈ।
>
> *saṛak utte bahut dhūṛ hai.*
>
> There is a lot of dust on the road.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਧੂੜ** (*dhūṛ*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*dhūḍi*, "dust, powder"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *dhūṛ*, "dust", under it (CDIAL 6835).
