## You'll want to know: 国庆
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C339-NATION339-02]; assesses=[] -->

**国庆** — *Guóqìng* — "National Day".

> **国庆的时候我们回了老家** *Guóqìng de shíhou wǒmen huí le lǎojiā* — Over the national holiday we went back to our home town.

The new character, in pieces: 庆 — six strokes: the roof piece of 店, a dot, a bar and a long stroke falling to the left; under it 大. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**国庆** (*Guóqìng*) is built from 国 'a state' and *qìng* 'to celebrate', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *kwok* and *khjaengH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'to congratulate' and 'good fortune'.
