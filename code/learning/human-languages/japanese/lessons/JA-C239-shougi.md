## You'll want to know: しょうぎ

**しょうぎ** — *shōgi* — "shogi; Japanese chess".

> **きのう ちちと しょうぎを しました。** — *kinō chichi to shōgi o shimashita* — "Yesterday I played shogi with my father."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**しょうぎ** (*shōgi*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *shō*, "general" (Middle Chinese *tsjangH*) and *gi*, "board game" (*gi*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
