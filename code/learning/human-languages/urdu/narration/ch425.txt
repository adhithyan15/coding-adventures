Lesson 1 of 6.
ترازو (tarāzū) — a pair of scales; a balance.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a packet, then the Urdu for to wrap.

You'll want to know: ترازو.
ترازو — tarāzū — "a pair of scales; a balance".
دکاندار ترازو پر سیب تولتا ہے۔ dukāndār tarāzū par seb toltā hai. The shopkeeper weighs apples on the scales.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
ترازو (tarāzū) is usually traced to Persian tarāzū, "a balance, scales".

میں نے دکاندار کا بقایا دیا۔ maiṅ ne dukāndār kā baqāyā diyā. I paid the shopkeeper what I still owed him.

Lesson 5 of 6.
تھمانا (thamānā) — to hand (to someone).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for money still owed, then the Urdu for loose change.

You'll want to know: تھمانا.
تھمانا — thamānā — "to hand (to someone)".
دکاندار نے مجھے تھیلا تھمایا۔ dukāndār ne mujhe thailā thamāyā. The shopkeeper handed me the bag.
Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے چابی تھمائی, us ne chābī thamāī.

The word, taken apart.
تھمانا (thamānā) is built on thāmnā, "to hold, to prop", which CDIAL lists under Sanskrit stambhate, "props, stops" (CDIAL 13683): this is the form for making someone hold a thing.
