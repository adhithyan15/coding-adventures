## You'll want to know: l'éboulement
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C303-SEISME-03]; assesses=[] -->

**l'éboulement** — "the rockfall, the landslide".

> Un éboulement a coupé la route de la montagne.
>
> *A rockfall cut off the mountain road.*

Signs in the mountains warn: *chute de pierres*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*éboulement* is built on *ébouler* "to crumble down"; the origin of that verb is not settled (EtymDB lists no parent).
