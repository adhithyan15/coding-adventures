## You'll want to know: провалить
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C382-EKZAMEN-01]; assesses=[] -->

**провалить** (*provalít*) — "to fail (an exam); to ruin".

> Он провалил экзамен по математике.
>
> *On provalíl ekzámen po matemátike.*
>
> He failed his maths exam.

Perfective; imperfective *проваливать*. Colloquial for exams; the neutral phrase is *не сдать экзамен*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**провалить** is built from *про-* "through" and *валить* "to topple, to knock down": to make a thing fall through. *Провал* is a failure, and literally a hole in the ground.
