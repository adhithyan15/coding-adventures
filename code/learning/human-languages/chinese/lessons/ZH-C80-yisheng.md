## You'll want to know: yīshēng
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C80-JOB80-04]; assesses=[] -->

*yīshēng* — "doctor".

> *wǒ māma shì yīshēng* — My mum is a doctor.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*yīshēng* is built from yī 'healing' and 生, as in 学生 and in xiānsheng, both inherited from Middle Chinese, *'i* and *srjaeng* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription).
