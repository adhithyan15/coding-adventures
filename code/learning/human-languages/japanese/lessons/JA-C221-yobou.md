## You'll want to know: よぼう

**よぼう** — *yobō* — "prevention".

> **よぼうの ちゅうしゃですか。** — *yobō no chūsha desu ka* — "Is it a preventive injection?"

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**よぼう** (*yobō*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *yo*, "beforehand" (Middle Chinese *yoH*) and *bō*, "to ward off" (*bjang*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
