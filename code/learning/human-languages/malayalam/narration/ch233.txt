Lesson 1 of 5.
കണങ്കാൽ (kaṇaṅkāl) — the ankle.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for to flow, then the Malayalam for to unfold.

You'll want to know: കണങ്കാൽ.
കണങ്കാൽ — kaṇaṅkāl — "the ankle".

The word, taken apart.
കണങ്കാൽ (kaṇaṅkāl) is native Dravidian, "the joint of the leg": kaṇ, "a joint, a node" (DEDR 1160, with Tamil kaṇ), and kāl, "leg" (DEDR 1479).

The word, taken apart.
ശ്വാസം (śvāsaṁ) from Sanskrit śvāsa, "breath, breathing", taken over whole as a tatsama, with Malayalam's noun ending -ṁ (CDIAL 12769).
