## You'll want to know: مستقل
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C545-MUSTAQIL545-02]; assesses=[] -->

**مستقل** — *mustaqil* — "permanent; steady".

> اگر نوکری مستقل ہوئی تو ہم نیا گھر لیں گے۔
>
> *agar naukrī mustaqil huī to ham nayā ghar leṅ ge.*
>
> If the job becomes permanent, we'll get a new house.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**مستقل** (*mustaqil*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *mustaqill*, "standing on its own, independent".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
