## You'll want to know: sùcài
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C451-SPECIAL451-04]; assesses=[] -->

*sùcài* — "meatless dish; vegetable dish".

> *zhè jiā fànguǎn yǒu hěn duō sùcài* — This restaurant has lots of meatless dishes.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*sùcài* is built from *sù* 'plain' and 菜 'a dish', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *suH* and *tshojH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription); the sense 'meatless' for the first is not given there.
