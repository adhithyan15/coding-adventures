Telugu, chapter 109: That is exactly why, Bed, Pillow, Blanket, Curtain.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
అందుకే (andukē) — that is exactly why.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for therefore, then the Telugu for because of.

You'll want to know: అందుకే.
అందుకే — andukē — "that is exactly why".

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does అందుకే (andukē) mean? ("That is exactly why".) Say it once more.

(గుడి, guḍi.) A school? (బడి, baḍi.) A library? (గ్రంథాలయం, granthālayaṁ.) A bank? (బ్యాంకు, byāṅku.) A market? (సంత, santa.) A shore? (తీరం, tīraṁ.) An office? (కార్యాలయం, kāryālayaṁ.) A garden?
