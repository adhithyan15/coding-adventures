## You'll want to know: दूतावास
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C420-DUTAVAS420-01]; assesses=[] -->

**दूतावास** — *dūtāvās* — "an embassy".

> मीरा व्हिसासाठी दूतावासात गेली.
>
> *mīrā vhisāsāṭhī dūtāvāsāt gelī.*
>
> Meera went to the embassy for her visa.

Masculine.

दूत is an envoy and आवास a dwelling: the envoy’s house.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**दूतावास** (*dūtāvās*) is a modern word built from Sanskrit *dūta*, "an envoy", and *āvāsa*, "a dwelling" (Monier-Williams).
