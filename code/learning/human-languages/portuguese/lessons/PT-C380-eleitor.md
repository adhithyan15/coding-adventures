## You'll want to know: o eleitor
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C380-POLITICA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**o eleitor** — "the voter".

> Muitos eleitores não votaram este ano.
>
> *Many voters did not vote this year.*

Feminine *a eleitora*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*eleitor* comes from Latin *ēlēctor* "a chooser" (Lewis & Short), on *ēligere* "to pick out"; the *-it-* shows the popular change of *-ct-*.
