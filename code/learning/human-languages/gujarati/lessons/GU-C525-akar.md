## You'll want to know: આકાર
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C525-SHILP525-03]; assesses=[] -->

**આકાર** — *ākār* — "a shape".

> આ પથ્થરનો આકાર ઘણો સુંદર છે.
>
> *ā paththarno ākār ghaṇo sundar che.*
>
> The shape of this stone is very beautiful.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**આકાર** (*ākār*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *ākāra*, "form, figure, shape" (Monier-Williams).
