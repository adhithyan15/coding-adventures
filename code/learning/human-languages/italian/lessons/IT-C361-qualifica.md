## You'll want to know: la qualifica
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C361-CARRIERA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**la qualifica** — "the qualification; the job title".

> Per questo lavoro serve una qualifica tecnica.
>
> *For this job you need a technical qualification.*

Stress: *qualìfica*. On a contract it is your grade or title.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*qualifica* is built on *qualificare*, a learned borrowing from Medieval Latin *quālificāre* "to give a quality to", built from *quālis* "of what kind" and *facere* "to make" (Lewis & Short).
