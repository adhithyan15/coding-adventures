## You'll want to know: ਪਰੋਸਣਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C244-KAM244-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ਪਰੋਸਣਾ** — *parosṇā* — "to serve food".

> ਮਾਤਾ ਮਹਿਮਾਨਾਂ ਨੂੰ ਖਾਣਾ ਪਰੋਸਦੀ ਹੈ।
>
> *mātā mahimānāṁ nū̃ khāṇā parosdī hai.*
>
> Mother serves food to the guests.

Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਖਾਣਾ ਪਰੋਸਿਆ, *maiṁ khāṇā parosiā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਪਰੋਸਣਾ** (*parosṇā*) goes back to Sanskrit *parivēṣati*, "attends, serves food", as a word taken from Hindi: CDIAL lists Punjabi *parosṇā*, "to offer food to a guest", as borrowed from Hindi *parosnā*; the Punjabi form inherited directly is *parīhṇā*, "to serve a meal" (CDIAL 7888).
