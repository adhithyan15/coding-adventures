## Unreleased — Hindi's written marks now declare their taught names

The Chapter 2 writing sequence already teaches **विराम** and everyday **हलंत**
as names for **्**, and teaches **पूर्ण विराम** as the name for **।**. Learners
produce and recall those terms, but both lesson headwords previously declared
only the glyphs. The headwords now pair each mark with its taught Hindi names,
making the terminology visible to reports and generated study artifacts.
