## You'll want to know: l'immigration
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C365-PAUVRETE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**l'immigration** — "the immigration".

> L'immigration est un sujet de débat dans beaucoup de pays.
>
> *Immigration is a subject of debate in many countries.*

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*immigration* is built on Latin *immigrāre* "to move into" and *migrātiō* "a moving from one place to another", from *migrāre* "to move, to wander" (Lewis & Short).
