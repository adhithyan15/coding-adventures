## You'll want to know: valide
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C384-VALABLE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**valide** — "valid; fit, able-bodied".

> Il faut une pièce d'identité valide.
>
> *A valid identity document is needed.*

Of a person: fit, able-bodied.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*valide* is a learned borrowing from Latin *validus* "strong, powerful" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short), from *valēre* "to be strong".
