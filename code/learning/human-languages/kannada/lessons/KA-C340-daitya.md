## You'll want to know: ದೈತ್ಯ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C340-DANTAKATHE340-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ದೈತ್ಯ** — *daitya* — "a giant".

> ಕಥೆಯಲ್ಲಿ ಒಬ್ಬ ದೈತ್ಯ ಬೆಟ್ಟದ ಮೇಲೆ ಇದ್ದನು.
>
> *katheyalli obba daitya beṭṭada mēle iddanu.*
>
> In the story a giant lived on the hill.

Plural: ದೈತ್ಯರು, *daityaru*.

As an adjective it means huge: ದೈತ್ಯ ಮರ, a giant tree.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ದೈತ್ಯ** (*daitya*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *daitya*, the name of a race of demons in the old stories (Monier-Williams); in Kannada it also means a giant.
