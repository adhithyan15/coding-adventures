## You'll want to know: ਸੰਦੂਕ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C247-GHR247-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ਸੰਦੂਕ** — *sandūk* — "a trunk; a storage chest".

> ਸੰਦੂਕ ਵਿੱਚ ਰਜਾਈਆਂ ਹਨ।
>
> *sandūk vich rajāīāṁ han.*
>
> There are quilts in the trunk.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਸੰਦੂਕ** (*sandūk*) is borrowed: it is usually traced to Persian *sandūq*, "a chest, a box".
