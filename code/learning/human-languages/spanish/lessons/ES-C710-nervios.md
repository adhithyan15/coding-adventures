## You'll want to know: los nervios
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C710-NERVIOS-01]; assesses=[] -->

**los nervios** — "nerves".

> Antes del examen tengo nervios.
>
> *I get nervous before the exam.*

*Tener nervios* — to be nervous. *¡Qué nervios!* — I am so nervous!

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*nervios* is the plural of *nervio*, which comes from Latin *nervus* "sinew" (EtymDB); the sense nerve came later, as in English *nerve*.
