## You'll want to know: ひろげる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C161-LAUNDRY161-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ひろげる** — *hirogeru* — "to spread out; to unfold".

> **ふとんを ゆかに ひろげます。** — *futon o yuka ni hirogemasu* — "I spread the futon out on the floor."

Polite **ひろげます**, *hirogemasu*; an ichidan verb.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ひろげる** (*hirogeru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi, and its older origin is not settled (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
