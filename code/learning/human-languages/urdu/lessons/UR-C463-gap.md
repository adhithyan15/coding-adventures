## You'll want to know: گپ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C463-TALK463-02]; assesses=[] -->

**گپ** — *gap* — "idle chat".

> چائے پر دوستوں کی گپ دیر تک چلی۔
>
> *chāy par dostoṅ kī gap der tak chalī.*
>
> Over tea the friends chatted for a long time.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**گپ** (*gap*) is inherited from *gappa*, "tittle-tattle", a form CDIAL reconstructs (CDIAL 4022).
