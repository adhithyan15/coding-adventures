Marathi, chapter 554: Chance and Timing.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
मुद्दाम (muddām) — on purpose.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for without fail, then the Marathi for prompt.

You'll want to know: मुद्दाम.
मुद्दाम — muddām — "on purpose".
मी मुद्दाम उशिरा आलो नाही. mī muddām uśirā ālo nāhī. I did not come late on purpose.

The word, taken apart.
मुद्दाम (muddām) is a Marathi adverb for something done deliberately; its origin is not settled.

Lesson 2 of 5.
आगाऊ (āgāū) — in advance.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for prompt, then the Marathi for on purpose.

You'll want to know: आगाऊ.
आगाऊ — āgāū — "in advance".
सहलीचे पैसे आम्ही आगाऊ भरले. sahlīce paise āmhī āgāū bharle. We paid for the trip in advance.

The word, taken apart.
आगाऊ (āgāū), "ahead of time", is perhaps kin to Marathi agāv, "in front", which CDIAL sets under Sanskrit agra, "top, front" (CDIAL 68). CDIAL lists no आगाऊ itself, so the link is not settled.

Lesson 3 of 5.
ऐनवेळी (ainveḷī) — at the last moment.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for on purpose, then the Marathi for in advance.

You'll want to know: ऐनवेळी.
ऐनवेळी — ainveḷī — "at the last moment".
ऐनवेळी गाडी बिघडली आणि आम्ही उशिरा पोहोचलो. ainveḷī gāḍī bighaḍlī āṇi āmhī uśirā pohoclo. At the last moment the car broke down and we arrived late.

The word, taken apart.
ऐनवेळी (ainveḷī) is a Marathi compound of ऐन, "the very", a loan usually traced to Arabic ʿain, "eye, essence", and वेळ, "time".

Lesson 4 of 5.
बहुतेक (bahutek) — mostly; most.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for in advance, then the Marathi for at the last moment.

You'll want to know: बहुतेक.
बहुतेक — bahutek — "mostly; most".
बहुतेक लोक रविवारी घरी असतात. bahutek lok ravivārī gharī astāt. Most people are at home on Sundays.

The word, taken apart.
बहुतेक (bahutek) is a Marathi compound built from बहुत, "much", and एक, "one". CDIAL lists Marathi bahut, "much, many", under Sanskrit bahutva, "abundance" (CDIAL 9190).

Lesson 5 of 5.
साधारण (sādhāraṇ) — roughly; ordinary.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for at the last moment, then the Marathi for mostly.

You'll want to know: साधारण.
साधारण — sādhāraṇ — "roughly; ordinary".
बस साधारण दहा मिनिटांनी येईल. bas sādhāraṇ dahā miniṭānnī yeīl. The bus will come in roughly ten minutes.

The word, taken apart.
साधारण (sādhāraṇ) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit sādhāraṇa, "common to all, general" (Monier-Williams).
