## You'll want to know: તપાસવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C197-GATE197-02]; assesses=[] -->

**તપાસવું** — *tapāsvũ* — "to check, to examine".

> **ચોકીદાર દરેક થેલી તપાસે છે.** — *cokīdār darek thelī tapāse che* — "The watchman checks every bag."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

The origin of **તપાસવું** (*tapāsvũ*) is not settled. EtymDB links it to an Arabic verb for examining and lists Marathi *tapāsṇe* beside it, but CDIAL has no entry for it, so the Arabic link is unconfirmed (EtymDB).
