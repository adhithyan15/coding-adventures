## You'll want to know: vermeiden
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C347-ZUFALL-04]; assesses=[] -->

**vermeiden** — "to avoid".

> Ich fahre früh los, um den Verkehr zu vermeiden.
>
> *I set off early to avoid the traffic.*

Inseparable and strong: *er vermeidet, er hat vermieden*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*vermeiden* is built with *ver-* on *meiden* "to keep away from", inherited from Old High German *mīdan* and Proto-Germanic *mīþaną* "to avoid" (EtymDB).
