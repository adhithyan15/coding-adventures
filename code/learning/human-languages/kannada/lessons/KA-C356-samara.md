## You'll want to know: ಸಮರ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C356-JANAPADA356-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸಮರ** — *samara* — "a battle; combat".

> ಆ ಸಮರದಲ್ಲಿ ಎರಡು ಸಾಮ್ರಾಜ್ಯಗಳು ಕಾದಾಡಿದವು.
>
> *ā samaradalli eraḍu sāmrājyagaḷu kādāḍidavu.*
>
> In that battle two empires fought each other.

Plural: ಸಮರಗಳು, *samaragaḷu*.

A bookish word; ಸಮರ ಸಾರು, to declare war.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸಮರ** (*samara*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *samara*, "coming together", and so "a hostile encounter, a battle" (Monier-Williams).

One more word for telling what happened.
