French, chapter 96: Never, Now, Then, Far, Near.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
jamais — never; ever.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for the morning, then the French for always; still.

You'll want to know: jamais.
jamais — "never; ever".

The word, taken apart.
jamais from Old French ja mais, "ever more" (EtymDB 2.1), from Latin iam, "already", and magis, "more" (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 2 of 5.
maintenant — now.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for always; still, then the French for never; ever.

You'll want to know: maintenant.
maintenant — "now".

The word, taken apart.
maintenant is the present participle of the verb "to maintain", from Latin manu tenere, "to hold in the hand": the moment at hand. The main- is main, "hand". (EtymDB 2.1).
