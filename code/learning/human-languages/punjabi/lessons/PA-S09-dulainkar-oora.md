# ੂ · ਉ — two pieces met inside a word you already say

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-SCRIPT-BINDI-01, PA-SCRIPT-RECOG-NAANA-01, PA-SCRIPT-RECOG-JA-01, PA-SCRIPT-RECOG-VA-01, PA-SCRIPT-RECOG-RRA-01, PA-SCRIPT-RECOG-GHA-01, PA-SCRIPT-RECOG-DA-01, PA-SCRIPT-RECOG-KHA-01, PA-SCRIPT-RECOG-JHA-01, PA-SCRIPT-RECOG-PAIRIN-01, PA-SCRIPT-RECOG-PA-01, PA-SCRIPT-RECOG-AUNKAR-01, PA-SCRIPT-RECOG-TIPPI-01, PA-LEX-LAINA, PA-ETYMON-LAINA-LABH, PA-LEX-PUCHHNA, PA-ETYMON-PUCHHNA-PRACH] -->

[PAUSE 2s] The word that opens every sentence about what you like is five pieces — and you already have three.

First, say the last things you learned once more, out loud and from
memory:

- [YOU SAY: *laiṇā* · *puchhṇā*]

## Script you'll notice: ੂ · ਉ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-SCRIPT-RECOG-DULAINKAR-01, PA-SCRIPT-RECOG-OORA-01]; assesses=[] -->

> ੂ

**dulaiṅkaṛ** — *-ū*. A mātrā for long *ū*, written below the letter as **two** strokes — the double *auṅkaṛ*.

> ਉ

**ūṛā** — *u*. An **independent** vowel: the letter used when a word *starts* with the vowel and there is no consonant to hang a mātrā on.

Now put them where you have already heard them:

> **ਮ** + **ੈ** + **ਨ** + **ੂ** + **ੰ** → **ਮੈਨੂੰ**
>
> **ਮੈਨੂੰ** — *mainū̃* — **to me**

You are not being asked to write any of this. You are being asked to
**recognise** it the next time it goes past.

## Writing: copy what you can see
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-SCRIPT-RECOG-DULAINKAR-01, PA-SCRIPT-RECOG-OORA-01] -->

Keep ੂ · ਉ in front of you and do not cover any of them. Follow each shape once with a
finger, then copy it once with a pencil, larger than it is printed. Say its
sound as your hand stops.

> This is **observe-and-trace with the model visible**. Nothing in these
> chapters asks you to write a Punjabi word from memory; that ladder starts
> later, and it starts from these same shapes.
