## You'll want to know: પૈડું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C208-HIGHWAY208-04]; assesses=[] -->

**પૈડું** — *paiḍũ* — "wheel".

> **ગાડીનું એક પૈડું ખાડામાં ગયું.** — *gāḍīnũ ek paiḍũ khāḍāmā̃ gayũ* — "One wheel of the car went into the pothole."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**પૈડું** (*paiḍũ*) is a tadbhava. CDIAL lists it, glossed "felly", the rim of a wheel, under Sanskrit *pradhí*, "felly", extended with the suffix *-ḍa-* (CDIAL 8672).
