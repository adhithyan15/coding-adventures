# Bengali B2 mock rubric

Score either B2 mock against [task-shapes/b2.json](../../task-shapes/b2.json).
Reading, listening, writing, and speaking are separate 100-point papers; every
paper must independently reach 60/100. A 240/400 total cannot compensate for a
failed paper, and every section must be attempted.

## Reading and listening

Each of the four reading parts and each of the four listening parts is worth 25
points. Divide each part's points equally among its keyed items and retain one
decimal place. Play every listening part once after one unscored orienting
preview, at 150–170 words per minute. Each form must use multiple speakers and
document at least two genuine regional Bengali voice profiles in its examiner
notes; regional pronunciation is evidence, not comic imitation or romanized
dialect spelling. No transcript, romanization, dictionary, or translator is
allowed.

## Writing

The 100–130 word interaction text is worth 35 points: task fulfilment and
relevant content 10, audience and register 7, organisation and cohesion 6,
Bengali vocabulary and grammar 8, and Bengali-script control 4. The 220–280
word extended text is worth 65 points: task fulfilment and development 20,
organisation and cohesion 15, genre and register control 10, Bengali vocabulary
and grammar 14, and Bengali-script control 6. Script control includes
the matra line, hasanta, and correct side and legibility of vowel signs.
Romanized, translated, non-Bengali-script, or visibly copied answers earn
zero independent-writing credit.

## Speaking

The prepared presentation is worth 35 points, collaborative problem-solving 30,
and defended discussion 35. Within each part award 30% for task fulfilment and
development, 25% for organisation or interaction, 30% for Bengali vocabulary
and grammar, and 15% for pronunciation and fluency. Reward comparison,
qualification, clarification, and repair. No written answer script is allowed;
ten-minute preparation notes may contain keywords, not sentences.

## Readiness boundary

The mock is executable but not self-validating. A readiness claim additionally
requires clean timed administration and qualified human review of the paper,
recordings, productive responses, and scoring decisions.
