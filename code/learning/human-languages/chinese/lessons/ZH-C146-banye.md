## You'll want to know: bànyè
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C146-NIGHT146-03]; assesses=[] -->

*bànyè* — "the middle of the night".

> *wǒ bànyè è le* — I got hungry in the middle of the night.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*bànyè* is built from 半 'half' and *yè* 'night', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *panH* and *yaeH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription).
