## You'll want to know: कालवा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C443-ODHA443-02]; assesses=[] -->

**कालवा** — *kālvā* — "a canal".

> नदीचं पाणी कालव्याने शेतात जातं.
>
> *nadīcaṁ pāṇī kālavyāne śetāt jātaṁ.*
>
> Water from the river goes to the fields by canal.

Masculine.

Before an ending कालवा becomes कालव्या-: कालव्याने, by the canal.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**कालवा** (*kālvā*) is a Marathi word for a dug channel of water; its origin is not settled.
