## You'll want to know: bēiguān
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C465-FUN465-04]; assesses=[] -->

*bēiguān* — "pessimistic".

> *nǐ bié zhème bēiguān* — Don't be so pessimistic.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*bēiguān* is built from *bēi* 'grief' and *guān* 'to view', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *pij* and *kwan* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'pain' and the second 'to look'.

## What you've built
