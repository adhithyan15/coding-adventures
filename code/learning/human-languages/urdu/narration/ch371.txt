Urdu, chapter 371: Danger and Damage.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
بحران (buhrān) — a crisis.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for to smash, then the Urdu for to squash.

You'll want to know: بحران.
بحران — buhrān — "a crisis".
اگر بارش نہیں ہوئی تو پانی کا بحران ہو گا۔ agar bārish nahīṅ huī to pānī kā buhrān ho gā. If it does not rain, there will be a water crisis.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
بحران (buhrān) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic buḥrān, "the crisis of a fever", the turning point of an illness.

Lesson 3 of 5.
جان لیوا (jān-levā) — deadly; fatal.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a crisis, then the Urdu for poison.

You'll want to know: جان لیوا.
جان لیوا — jān-levā — "deadly; fatal".
اگر یہ زہر ہے تو جان لیوا ہو سکتا ہے۔ agar yih zahr hai to jān-levā ho saktā hai. If this is poison, it could be deadly.

The word, taken apart.
جان لیوا (jān-levā) is built from jān, "life", usually traced to Persian jān, "life, soul", and levā, "taking", made on لینا, "to take". Life-taking.

Lesson 4 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for poison, then the Urdu for deadly.

You'll want to know: رسنا.
رسنا — risnā — "to seep; to leak (of a liquid)".
اگر چھت سے پانی رسا تو کمرہ گیلا ہو گا۔ agar chhat se pānī risā to kamrā gīlā ho gā. If water seeps through the roof, the room will get wet.
Past (it …): وہ پانی دیوار سے رسا, voh pānī dīvār se risā.

The word, taken apart.
رسنا (risnā) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin risnā, "to leak out, to drip", under riśyate, a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 10745).

پھٹنا — phaṭnā — "to split open; to burst".
