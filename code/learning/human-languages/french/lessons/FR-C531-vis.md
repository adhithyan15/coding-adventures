## You'll want to know: la vis
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C531-BRICOLAGE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**la vis** — "screw".

> Il manque une vis à cette chaise.
>
> *This chair is missing a screw.*

The *s* is sounded: *vis* rhymes with *fils*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*la vis* is inherited from Latin *vītis* "a vine" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short): the screw is probably named for the spiral of the vine's tendrils.
