Persian, chapter 334: Love and engagement.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
عاشق شدن ('âsheq shodan) — to fall in love.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for middle age, then the Persian for old age.

You'll want to know: عاشق شدن.
عاشق شدن ('âsheq shodan) — "to fall in love".
او در دانشگاه عاشق همکلاسی اش شد. U dar dâneshgâh 'âsheq-e hamkelâsi-ash shod. He fell in love with his classmate at university.
The loved one follows with the ezafe: 'âsheq-e u shod. Present 'âsheq mishavad.

The word, taken apart.
'âsheq "in love, a lover" is borrowed from Arabic 'āshiq, "lover" (EtymDB). shodan "to become" is inherited Persian.

Lesson 2 of 5.
خواستگاری (khâstegâri) — a marriage proposal.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for old age, then the Persian for to fall in love.

You'll want to know: خواستگاری.
خواستگاری (khâstegâri) — "a marriage proposal".
خواستگاری (khâstegâri) در خانه پدر دختر انجام شد. Khâstegâri dar khâne-ye pedar-e dokhtar anjâm shod. The proposal took place at the young woman's family home.
Traditionally the man's family visits the woman's family to ask for her hand. From khâstan "to want, to ask for".

The word, taken apart.
khâstegâri is built on khâste, from the native verb khâstan "to want, to ask for", with the suffixes -gâr and -i: the asking. EtymDB lists no entry.

You'll want to know: خواستگاری کردن.
خواستگاری کردن (khâstegâri kardan) — "to propose marriage".
او یک سال بعد از دختر خواستگاری کرد. U yek sâl ba'd az dokhtar khâstegâri kard. A year later he proposed to the young woman.
The person asked takes az. Present khâstegâri mikonad.

The word, taken apart.
khâstegâri kardan joins the native noun khâstegâri "a proposal", from khâstan "to want", with kardan "to do", inherited Persian.

Lesson 4 of 5.
نامزدی (nâmzadi) — an engagement.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a marriage proposal, then the Persian for to propose marriage.

You'll want to know: نامزدی.
نامزدی (nâmzadi) — "an engagement".
جشن نامزدی آنها در تابستان بود. Jashn-e nâmzadi-ye ânhâ dar tâbestân bud. Their engagement party was in the summer.
From nâmzad "fiancé, fiancée", literally "name-struck": a name given to someone.

The word, taken apart.
nâmzadi adds the suffix -i to nâmzad "fiancé, fiancée", built from nâm "name" and zad, the past stem of zadan "to strike". All of it is native Persian.

عقد ('aqd) — the marriage contract, the wedding ceremony.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to propose marriage, then the Persian for an engagement.

You'll want to know: عقد.
عقد ('aqd) — "the marriage contract, the wedding ceremony".
عقد ('aqd) آنها در یک محضر کوچک بود. 'Aqd-e ânhâ dar yek mahzar-e kuchak bud. Their marriage ceremony was at a small registry office.
The legal and religious signing; sofre-ye 'aqd is the spread laid out for it.

The word, taken apart.
'aqd is borrowed from Arabic 'aqd, "a contract" (EtymDB lists the Arabic word), from a root for tying a knot.
