Marwadi (Marwari), chapter 211: Roomy or Tight.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
माप (māp) — a size; a measure.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for fake, then the Marwadi for to pick out.

You'll want to know: माप.
माप — māp — "a size; a measure".
ये जूती म्हारो माप कोनी। ye jūtī mhāro māp konī. These shoes aren't my size.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
माप (māp) is inherited from Sanskrit māpya, "to be measured". CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry, but lists Hindi māp, "measurement, measure" (CDIAL 10053).

नापणो — nāpṇo — "to measure".
मिस्त्री भींत नापै है। mistrī bhī̃t nāpai hai. The mason measures the wall.
Past (I …): म्हैं कमरो नाप्यो, mhaĩ kamro nāpyo.

नापणो (nāpṇo) is inherited from Sanskrit jñāpyate, "is made known". CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry, but lists Hindi nāpnā, "to measure" (CDIAL 5283).

[pause 2 seconds]

मोकळो — mokḷo — "roomy; loose".
ये कुरतो मोकळो है। ye kurto mokḷo hai. This kurta is roomy.

The word, taken apart.
मोकळो (mokḷo) is inherited from a reconstructed mukna, "loosed"; CDIAL lists Old Marwari mokaï, "sends", and Hindi moklā, "loose, free", under it (CDIAL 10157).

Lesson 5 of 5.
समावणो (samāvṇo) — to fit (into).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for roomy, then the Marwadi for tight.

You'll want to know: समावणो.
समावणो — samāvṇo — "to fit (into)".
म्हारो पग जूती मांय कोनी समावै। mhāro pag jūtī mā̃y konī samāvai. My foot won't fit in the shoe.
Past (it …): वो सामान पेटी मांय समायो, vo sāmān peṭī mā̃y samāyo.

The word, taken apart.
समावणो (samāvṇo) is inherited from Sanskrit sammāti, "is contained in"; CDIAL lists Old Marwari samāi, "fits into, is contained in" (CDIAL 12975).
