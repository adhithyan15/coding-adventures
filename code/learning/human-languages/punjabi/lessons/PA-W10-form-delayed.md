# Hide the bank for one short turn

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-FORM-AGE-PHONE-DATE-CUE-01, PA-FORM-AGE-TWENTY-FIVE-01, PA-FORM-PHONE-B-01, PA-FORM-DATE-B-01] -->

Read the familiar B set once: **ਉਮਰ: ੨੫**, **ਫ਼ੋਨ: ੦੨੫ ੧੨੫**,
**ਤਾਰੀਖ਼: ੨੫/੦੨/੨੦੨੫**. Point to one line at a time.

## Writing — brief delayed copy
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-FORM-AGE-PHONE-DATE-ORDER-01, PA-FORM-AGE-PHONE-DATE-CUE-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: delayed-copy -->

Cover the model. Wait five seconds. The card is **ਖ**. Write the three lines
in the known order, recalling one line at a time. Keep the model
covered until all three lines are finished.

> ਉਮਰ: ______
>
> ਫ਼ੋਨ: ______
>
> ਤਾਰੀਖ਼: ______

## Guided Practice — uncover only to compare
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-FORM-AGE-PHONE-DATE-ORDER-01] -->

Uncover the model. Compare age first, phone second, and date third. This is
still delayed-copy practice, not an independent checkpoint.
