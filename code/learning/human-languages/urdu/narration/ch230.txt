Urdu, chapter 230: Granddaughter, Father-in-law, Mother-in-law, Bridegroom, Bride.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
پوتی (potī) — a granddaughter (son's daughter).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a petrol station, then the Urdu for a seat.

You'll want to know: پوتی.
پوتی — potī — "a granddaughter (son's daughter)".

The word, taken apart.
پوتی (potī) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from pautrī, "son's daughter" (CDIAL 8417). It is the feminine of pautra, "son's son".
