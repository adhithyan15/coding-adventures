Marathi, chapter 182: Discount, Coin, Banknote, Loan, Tax.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
सवलत (savlat) — a discount, a concession.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a widow, then the Marathi for success.

You'll want to know: सवलत.
सवलत — savlat — "a discount, a concession".

The word, taken apart.
सवलत (savlat) is a loan, usually traced through Persian to Arabic suhūla, "ease, facility", whose root is s-h-l (Lane).

Lesson 2 of 5.
नाणे (nāṇe) — a coin.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for success, then the Marathi for a discount.

You'll want to know: नाणे.
नाणे — nāṇe — "a coin".

The word, taken apart.
नाणे (nāṇe) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from nāṇaka, "coin, anything stamped" (CDIAL 7050).

Lesson 4 of 5.
कर्ज (karja) — a loan, a debt.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a coin, then the Marathi for a banknote.

You'll want to know: कर्ज.
कर्ज — karja — "a loan, a debt".

The word, taken apart.
कर्ज (karja) is a loan from Arabic qarḍ, "a loan", which reached Marathi through Persian. Its Arabic root is q-r-ḍ (Lane). Persian and Hindustani say qarz.

कर — kar — "a tax".

The word, taken apart.
कर (kar) is Sanskrit kara, "tax, tribute" (CDIAL 2780). CDIAL takes the Sanskrit word, after Burrow, from Dravidian.
