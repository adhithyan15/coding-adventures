## You'll want to know: chuchoter
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C488-CALINS-04]; assesses=[] -->

**chuchoter** — "to whisper".

> Les deux amies chuchotent au fond de la classe.
>
> *The two friends are whispering at the back of the classroom.*

*Un chuchotement* — a whisper. *Murmurer* is close.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*chuchoter* is usually described as an imitative word: it copies the soft sound of whispering, and the origin is not settled beyond that.
