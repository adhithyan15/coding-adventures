## You'll want to know: બાઝવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C451-VALAG451-03]; assesses=[] -->

**બાઝવું** — *bājhvũ* — "to hold on tight; to grapple".

> નાની દીકરી પિતાના પગને બાઝી પડી.
>
> *nānī dīkrī pitānā pagne bājhī paḍī.*
>
> The little daughter held on tight to her father's leg.

Past (she …): તે પિતાને બાઝી પડી, *te pitāne bājhī paḍī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**બાઝવું** (*bājhvũ*) is inherited from Sanskrit *badhyatē*, "is bound"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb as "to stick close to, catch hold of, fight" (CDIAL 9134).
