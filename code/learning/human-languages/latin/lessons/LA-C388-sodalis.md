## You'll want to know: sodālis, sodālis
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C388-MUNICEPS-04]; assesses=[] -->

**sodālis, sodālis** — "a companion, a comrade; a club member".

> Cum sodālibus cēnāvit.
>
> *He dined with his comrades.*

*sodālis, sodālis* m. and f. Members of a club, a guild or a priestly brotherhood were *sodālēs*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

The origin of *sodālis* is not settled: Lewis & Short compare it with a root meaning "one's own way, custom", but give no sure account.
