## You'll want to know: воспитывать
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C305-VOSPITYVAT-01]; assesses=[] -->

**воспитывать** (*vospítyvat*) — "to bring up, to raise".

> Бабушка воспитала троих внуков.
>
> *Bábushka vospitála troíkh vnúkov.*
>
> Grandma brought up three grandchildren.

Imperfective; perfective *воспитать* (*vospitát*).

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*воспитывать* is usually described as a Church Slavonic formation, from *вос-* "up" and *питать* "to feed, to nourish": to feed up. EtymDB lists no parent.
