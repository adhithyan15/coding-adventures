## You'll want to know: نحت
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C541-FANN541-04]; assesses=[] -->

**نحت** — *naḥata* — "to carve".

> نحت الفنان حصانا من الخشب.
>
> *naḥata al-fannān ḥiṣānan min al-khashab.*
>
> The artist carved a horse out of wood.

Present: ينحت, *yanḥit*.

The noun نحت, read naḥt, is sculpture.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**نحت** (*naḥata*) is built on the native Arabic root *n-ḥ-t*, "to hew, to carve" (Lane). Lane gives yanḥitu as its present.
