## You'll want to know: نضج
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C531-KIBAR531-03]; assesses=[] -->

**نضج** — *naḍija* — "to ripen; to mature".

> نضج التين في آخر الصيف.
>
> *naḍija at-tīn fī ākhir aṣ-ṣayf.*
>
> The figs ripened at the end of the summer.

Present: ينضج, *yanḍaj*.

Of a person it means to grow mature.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**نضج** (*naḍija*) is built on the native Arabic root *n-ḍ-j*, "to be ripe, to be well cooked" (Lane). Nāḍij, ripe, which you know, is its participle.

## What you've built
