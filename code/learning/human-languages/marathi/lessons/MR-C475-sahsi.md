## You'll want to know: साहसी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C475-SAHAS475-01]; assesses=[] -->

**साहसी** — *sāhsī* — "adventurous".

> साहसी मुलांनी डोंगर चढून पाहिला.
>
> *sāhsī mulānnī ḍoṅgar caḍhūn pāhilā.*
>
> The adventurous boys tried climbing the mountain.

From साहस, a daring deed. Unchanging.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**साहसी** (*sāhsī*) is a Marathi adjective built on साहस, "a daring deed", a tatsama from Sanskrit *sāhasa*, "boldness" (CDIAL 13378).
