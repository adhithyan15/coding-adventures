Russian, chapter 38: Work, Sing, Dance, Laugh, Cry.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
работать (rabótat) — to work.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a pupil, then the Russian for work.

You'll want to know: работать.
работать (rabótat) — "to work". The verb of работа.

What you've built.
One more word for work, sing, dance, laugh, cry.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: rabótat]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: rabótat, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say rabótat]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Russian for a pupil, then the Russian for work, then say rabótat again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does работать (rabótat) mean? ("To work".) Say it once more.
