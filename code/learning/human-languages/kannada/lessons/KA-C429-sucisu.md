## You'll want to know: ಸೂಚಿಸು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C429-ASHISU429-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸೂಚಿಸು** — *sūcisu* — "to suggest; to indicate".

> ವೈದ್ಯರು ಸ್ವಲ್ಪ ವಿಶ್ರಾಂತಿ ಸೂಚಿಸಿದರು.
>
> *vaidyaru svalpa viśrānti sūcisidaru.*
>
> The doctor suggested some rest.

Past (I …): ಸೂಚಿಸಿದೆ, *sūciside*.

ಸೂಚನೆ, a notice, is the noun beside it.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸೂಚಿಸು** (*sūcisu*) is built on Sanskrit *sūcana*, "pointing out, indicating" (Monier-Williams), with the Kannada verb ending -*isu*.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
