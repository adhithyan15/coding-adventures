## You'll want to know: die Säule
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C441-FASSADE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**die Säule** — "the pillar".

> Vor dem Theater stehen sechs hohe Säulen.
>
> *There are six tall pillars in front of the theatre.*

Plural: *die Säulen*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Säule* is inherited from Old High German *sūl* 'pillar'; EtymDB lists Gothic *sauls* 'pillar' among its cousins.
