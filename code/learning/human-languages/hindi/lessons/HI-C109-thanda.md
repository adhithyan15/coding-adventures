## You'll want to know: ठंडा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C109-MOOD-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ठंडा** (*ṭhaṇḍā*) — "cold, cool", of a thing.

**ठंडा पानी** — "cold water", the first comfort offered to a guest on a hot day.
