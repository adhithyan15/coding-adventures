## You'll want to know: आदौ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C545-ANUKRAMA545-04]; assesses=[] -->

**आदौ** — *ādau* — "at first".

> यदि आदौ कठिनम् अस्ति, तर्हि अनन्तरं सुकरं भविष्यति।
>
> *yadi ādau kaṭhinam asti, tarhi anantaraṃ sukaraṃ bhaviṣyati.*
>
> If it is hard at first, it will be easy later.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**आदौ** (*ādau*) is the locative of *ādi*, "a beginning", which MW records as beginning, commencement; MW gives no further derivation, so its origin is uncertain (MW).
