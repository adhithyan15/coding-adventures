## You'll want to know: ಎಸಗು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C328-DAROODE328-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ಎಸಗು** — *esagu* — "to commit (a crime)".

> ಅವನು ಹಣಕ್ಕಾಗಿ ಆ ಅಪರಾಧ ಎಸಗಿದನು.
>
> *avanu haṇakkāgi ā aparādha esagidanu.*
>
> He committed that crime for money.

Past (I …): ಎಸಗಿದೆ, *esagide*.

A formal verb for wrong deeds: ಅಪರಾಧ ಎಸಗು, ತಪ್ಪು ಎಸಗು.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಎಸಗು** (*esagu*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 471 lists Kannada *esagu*, "to engage in, undertake, do, perform".
