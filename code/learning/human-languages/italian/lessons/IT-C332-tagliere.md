## You'll want to know: il tagliere
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C332-TAGLIERE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**il tagliere** — "the chopping board".

> Taglia la cipolla sul tagliere.
>
> *Cut the onion on the chopping board.*

Built on *tagliare*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*tagliere* is built on *tagliare* "to cut", from Late Latin *tāliāre* "to cut" (EtymDB).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.
