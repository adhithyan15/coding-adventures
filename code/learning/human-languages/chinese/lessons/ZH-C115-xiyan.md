## You'll want to know: xīyān
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C115-CAREFUL115-02]; assesses=[] -->

*xīyān* — "to smoke".

> *nǐ xīyān ma* — Do you smoke?

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*xīyān* is built from xī 'to breathe in' and yān 'smoke', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *xip* and *'en* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription).
