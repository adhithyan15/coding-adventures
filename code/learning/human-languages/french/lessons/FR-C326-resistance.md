## You'll want to know: la résistance
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C326-LIBERATION-04]; assesses=[] -->

**la résistance** — "the resistance; stamina".

> Ses grands-parents ont fait partie de la Résistance.
>
> *His grandparents were part of the Resistance.*

Also stamina: *avoir de la résistance*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*résistance* is built on *résister*, a learned borrowing from Latin *resistere* "to withstand" (Lewis & Short).
