## You'll want to know: فند
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C365-HAQIQA365-04]; assesses=[] -->

**فند** — *fannada* — "to refute".

> فند المحامي كلام الشاهد.
>
> *fannada al-muḥāmī kalām ash-shāhid.*
>
> The lawyer refuted the witness's statement.

Present: يفند, *yufannid*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**فند** (*fannada*) is built on the native Arabic root *f-n-d*, "to lie, to say what is untrue" (Lane). It is Form II, to show to be untrue: to refute.

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
