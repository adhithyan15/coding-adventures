## You'll want to know: こる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C448-STIFF448-01]; assesses=[] -->

**こる** — *koru* — "to get stiff (of muscles)".

> **しごとで かたが こりました。** — *shigoto de kata ga korimashita* — "My shoulders got stiff from work."

Polite **こります**, *korimasu*; a godan verb. Past (polite): **ちちの かたが こりました。**, *chichi no kata ga korimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**こる** (*koru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji spelling was attached to it later (JMdict).
