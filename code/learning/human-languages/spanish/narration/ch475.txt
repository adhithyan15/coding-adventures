Spanish, chapter 475: En el trabajo.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
profesional — professional.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for to deduct, then the Spanish for the blow.

You'll want to know: profesional.
profesional — "professional".
Buscamos un equipo profesional. We are looking for a professional team.
It is an adjective and a noun: un profesional is a professional, a person who does the job for a living.
