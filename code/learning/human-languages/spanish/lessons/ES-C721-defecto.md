## You'll want to know: el defecto
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C721-CARACTER-04]; assesses=[] -->

**el defecto** — "the fault, the flaw".

> Su mayor defecto es la impaciencia.
>
> *His biggest fault is impatience.*

The opposite is *la virtud* or *la cualidad*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*defecto* is a learned borrowing from Latin *dēfectus* "failure, lack" (EtymDB), from *dēficere* "to fail", built on *dē-* and *facere* "to do".
