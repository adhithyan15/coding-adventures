# Punjabi pre-A1 mock rubric

This shared rubric scores either Punjabi pre-A1 mock against the exact task
envelope in [`task-shapes/pre-a1.json`](../../task-shapes/pre-a1.json). Reading,
listening, writing, and speaking are separate 100-point papers. A learner meets
this form's scoring threshold only by earning at least 60/100 on every paper. A
high score in one skill never compensates for a paper below that threshold.

## Reading — 100 points

<!-- mock-part:prea1-reading-signs-and-words -->
### Signs and words — 40 points

Divide 40 points equally across the six keyed items and retain one decimal
place. Score meaning selection and Gurmukhi recognition only.
<!-- /mock-part:prea1-reading-signs-and-words -->

<!-- mock-part:prea1-reading-phrases-and-notices -->
### Phrases and notices — 30 points

Divide 30 points equally across the four keyed items. Score phrase recognition
and accurate situation matching only.
<!-- /mock-part:prea1-reading-phrases-and-notices -->

<!-- mock-part:prea1-reading-personal-details -->
### Personal details — 30 points

Divide 30 points equally across the four keyed items. Score personal-detail
recognition and accurate selection only. Reading aloud earns no reading credit.
<!-- /mock-part:prea1-reading-personal-details -->

## Listening — 100 points

Play every item twice at 70–90 words per minute with an eight-second answer
pause. Score only the learner's selected answer. Do not show a transcript,
romanization, or answer-bearing picture label.

<!-- mock-part:prea1-listening-sounds-and-words -->
### Sounds and words — 40 points

Divide 40 points equally across the six keyed items and retain one decimal
place. Score sound, tone, and known-word recognition.
<!-- /mock-part:prea1-listening-sounds-and-words -->

<!-- mock-part:prea1-listening-greeting-responses -->
### Greeting responses — 30 points

Divide 30 points equally across the four keyed items. Score communicative
function and appropriate-response selection.
<!-- /mock-part:prea1-listening-greeting-responses -->

<!-- mock-part:prea1-listening-personal-details -->
### Personal details — 30 points

Divide 30 points equally across the four keyed items. Score gist and
personal-detail recognition.
<!-- /mock-part:prea1-listening-personal-details -->

## Writing — 100 points

<!-- mock-part:prea1-writing-delayed-recall -->
### Delayed recall — 20 points

Award 10 points per word: 4 for a recognizable ordered akhar sequence, 3 for
required matras and diacritics, 2 for correct nasal or subjoined marks where
needed, and 1 for legibility.
<!-- /mock-part:prea1-writing-delayed-recall -->

<!-- mock-part:prea1-writing-dictation -->
### Dictation — 40 points

Award 10 points per item: 4 for recognizable sound-to-akhar mapping, 3 for
matras and diacritics, 2 for word boundaries, and 1 for legibility.
<!-- /mock-part:prea1-writing-dictation -->

<!-- mock-part:prea1-writing-bounded-production -->
### Bounded independent production — 40 points

Award 20 points per response: 8 for completing the stated purpose, 6 for
recognizable Gurmukhi word formation, 4 for required marks, and 2 for boundary
control and legibility. A romanized, traced, or visibly copied answer earns zero
because it is not independent writing evidence.
<!-- /mock-part:prea1-writing-bounded-production -->

## Speaking — 100 points

The interlocutor may repeat each prompt once slowly without penalty. Within
each part award 40% for completing the communicative purpose, 25% for
intelligibility to a trained Punjabi speaker, 20% for an expected taught word
or chunk, and 15% for responding without an answer script. A memorized chunk is
valid at pre-A1; a support-language-only response is not Punjabi speaking
evidence.

<!-- mock-part:prea1-speaking-greeting-and-identity -->
### Greeting and identity — 30 points

Score both the appropriate greeting response and the supplied identity.
<!-- /mock-part:prea1-speaking-greeting-and-identity -->

<!-- mock-part:prea1-speaking-familiar-questions -->
### Familiar questions — 40 points

Score recognition, relevance, and intelligibility across the three short
personal responses. Do not require one memorized sentence when another taught
response completes the purpose.
<!-- /mock-part:prea1-speaking-familiar-questions -->

<!-- mock-part:prea1-speaking-short-request -->
### Short request — 30 points

Score whether the pictured-item request is completed with appropriate courtesy
and is intelligible. Do not require the form-specific answer-key example word
for word.
<!-- /mock-part:prea1-speaking-short-request -->

## Readiness boundary

These artifacts make the project-defined mock structurally executable. They do
not by themselves establish pedagogical quality, exam readiness, attainment,
or external equivalence. The repository must still record a clean timed
administration, qualified Punjabi review, standard setting, and book-only
human validation before making a pass or learner-readiness claim.
