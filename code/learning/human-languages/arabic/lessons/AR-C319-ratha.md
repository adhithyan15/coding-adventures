## You'll want to know: رثى
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C319-AZA319-03]; assesses=[] -->

**رثى** — *rathā* — "to lament, to mourn in verse".

> رثى الشاعر صديقه في قصيدة طويلة.
>
> *rathā ash-shāʿir ṣadīqahu fī qaṣīda ṭawīla.*
>
> The poet mourned his friend in a long poem.

Present: يرثي, *yarthī*.

A poem of mourning is رثاء, an elegy.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**رثى** (*rathā*) is built on the native Arabic root *r-th-y* (Lane), "to weep for the dead and recount his good deeds"; *rithāʾ*, the elegy, is one of the oldest kinds of Arabic poem.
