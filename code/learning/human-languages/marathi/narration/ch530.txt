Marathi, chapter 530: Medals and Trophies.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
पदक (padak) — a medal.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a playing field, then the Marathi for to overcome.

You'll want to know: पदक.
पदक — padak — "a medal".
स्पर्धेत अरुणला पदक मिळालं. spardhet aruṇlā padak miḷālaṁ. Arun won a medal in the competition.
Neuter.
सुवर्णपदक, a gold medal.

The word, taken apart.
पदक (padak) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit padaka, a derivative of pada, glossed as a kind of ornament (Monier-Williams); Marathi uses it for a medal.

या स्पर्धेत वीस स्पर्धक आहेत. yā spardhet vīs spardhak āhet. There are twenty contestants in this competition.
Masculine.
Built on स्पर्धा, a competition.

The word, taken apart.
स्पर्धक (spardhak) is built on स्पर्धा, "a competition", from Sanskrit spardhā, "rivalry" (Monier-Williams), with the agent ending -क.

आमच्या शाळेने पहिलं बक्षीस पटकावलं. āmcyā śāḷene pahilaṁ bakṣīs paṭkāvlaṁ. Our school carried off the first prize.

The word, taken apart.
पटकावणे (paṭkāvṇe) is a Marathi verb for seizing a prize; its origin is not settled.
