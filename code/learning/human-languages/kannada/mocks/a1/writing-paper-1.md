# Kannada A1 writing paper 1 — learner sheet

This is one **writing-only** paper for the project-defined Kannada A1
assessment, not a complete four-skill mock or a CEFR certificate. Administer
it outside the lesson sequence. A lesson remains at most five minutes; this
paper uses one continuous **20-minute** clock. Give the learner only this
sheet, not the examiner rubric or an answer model. Support-language directions
are allowed at A1, but write text responses independently in Kannada script;
numerals are fine in the contact-number field.
Do not use romanization, a dictionary, translator, spell-checker, or generative
assistant. Invent safe personal details if you prefer.

Start the clock now. Spend **7 minutes** on Task 1, then move immediately to
Task 2 for the remaining **13 minutes**. Do not stop or restart the clock.
Stop writing at 20 minutes; review only after the sheet is collected.

## Task 1 — practical form (7 minutes; 40 points)

A neighbourhood group is planning a morning walk. Complete **seven** short
fields in Kannada script so the organiser can plan where and when to meet you.
You may use an invented name and contact number. Give a town or neighbourhood,
a day and time you can come, a place where you would start, and the name of a
friend who may join. A short word or phrase is enough for each field; these
labels do not supply sentences to copy.
