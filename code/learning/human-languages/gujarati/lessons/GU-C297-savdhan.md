# સાવધાન (sāvdhān) — careful; on guard

## You'll want to know: સાવધાન
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C297-CHETAV297-02]; assesses=[] -->

**સાવધાન** — *sāvdhān* — "careful; on guard".

> રાત્રે આ રસ્તા પર સાવધાન રહો.
>
> *rātre ā rastā par sāvdhān raho.*
>
> Be careful on this road at night.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**સાવધાન** (*sāvdhān*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *sāvadhāna*, "attentive, heedful" (Monier-Williams). On a sign it is a warning by itself: **સાવધાન!**, look out!
