## You'll want to know: la défense
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C387-PROCES-02]; assesses=[] -->

**la défense** — "the defence".

> L'avocat de la défense a parlé pendant une heure.
>
> *The defence lawyer spoke for an hour.*

*Défense de fumer* — no smoking.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*défense* comes from Late Latin *dēfēnsa* "protection" (EtymDB), from *dēfendere* "to ward off, to defend" (Lewis & Short).
