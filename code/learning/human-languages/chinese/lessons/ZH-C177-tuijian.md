## You'll want to know: tuījiàn
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C177-FULL177-01]; assesses=[] -->

*tuījiàn* — "to recommend".

> *fúwùyuán nǐ tuījiàn shénme cài* — Waiter, which dish do you recommend?

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*tuījiàn* is built from *tuī* 'to push' and *jiàn* 'to put forward', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *thwoj* and *tsenH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'to present' and 'to put forward'.

## What you've built
