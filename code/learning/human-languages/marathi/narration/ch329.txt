Marathi, chapter 329: Swords and Cannons.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
तलवार (talvār) — a sword.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to fight, then the Marathi for a battlefield.

You'll want to know: तलवार.
तलवार — talvār — "a sword".
संग्रहालयात एक जुनी तलवार ठेवली आहे. saṅgrahālayāt ek junī talvār ṭhevlī āhe. An old sword is kept in the museum.
Feminine.
Plural तलवारी.

The word, taken apart.
तलवार (talvār) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Marathi tarvār, 'a one-edged sword', under Sanskrit taravāri (CDIAL 5706); standard Marathi writes it with l for r.

Lesson 3 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

शस्त्र — śastra — "a weapon".
शत्रूने सगळी शस्त्रं खाली ठेवली. śatrūne sagḷī śastraṁ khālī ṭhevlī. The enemy laid down all their weapons.
Neuter.
Plural शस्त्रं; शस्त्र (śastra) खाली ठेवणे, to lay down arms.

The word, taken apart.
शस्त्र (śastra) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit śastra, 'a knife, a sword, a weapon' (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 5 of 5.
घोडदळ (ghoḍdaḷ) — cavalry.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a weapon, then the Marathi for a cannon.

You'll want to know: घोडदळ.
घोडदळ — ghoḍdaḷ — "cavalry".
घोडदळ वेगाने पुढे गेलं. ghoḍdaḷ vegāne puḍhe gelaṁ. The cavalry went forward at speed.
Neuter.
घोडा, a horse, + दळ, a troop.

The word, taken apart.
घोडदळ (ghoḍdaḷ) is built from ghoḍā, 'a horse', and daḷ, 'a troop', from Sanskrit dala, 'a part, a troop' (Monier-Williams).
