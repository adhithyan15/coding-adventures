Kannada, chapter 89: Take Care on the Way.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
ಹುಷಾರು (huṣāru) — take care, careful.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for come in, then the Kannada for welcome.

You'll want to know: ಹುಷಾರು.
ಹುಷಾರು (huṣāru) — "careful, take care". Said at parting it means "look after yourself"; said on its own it can also mean "well again".

What you've built.
A warm word at the door.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: huṣāru]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: huṣāru, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say huṣāru!]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Kannada for come in, then the Kannada for welcome, then say huṣāru again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does ಹುಷಾರು (huṣāru) mean? ("Take care, careful".) Say it once more.


Lesson 2 of 7.
ಜೋಪಾನ (jōpāna) — carefully, safely.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for welcome, then the Kannada for take care.

You'll want to know: ಜೋಪಾನ.
ಜೋಪಾನ (jōpāna) — "carefully, safely".
ಜೋಪಾನವಾಗಿ ಹೋಗಿ (jōpānavāgi hōgi) — "go safely", with the same ‑ಆಗಿ ending as ನಿಧಾನವಾಗಿ.

What you've built.
The second half of a caring goodbye.

Guided Practice.

[your turn — say: jōpāna]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: jōpāna, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say jōpānavāgi hōgi]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Kannada for welcome, then the Kannada for take care, then say jōpāna again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does ಜೋಪಾನ (jōpāna) mean? ("Carefully, safely".) Say it once more.


Lesson 3 of 7.
ತಡವಾಯಿತು (taḍavāyitu) — it's late, it got late.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for take care, then the Kannada for safely.

You'll want to know: ತಡವಾಯಿತು.
ತಡವಾಯಿತು (taḍavāyitu) — "it's late", literally "it has become late". ತಡ (taḍa) is "delay, lateness".

What you've built.
The reason people stand up to leave.

Lesson 4 of 7.
ಹೋಗೋಣ (hōgōṇa) — let's go.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for safely, then the Kannada for it's late.

You'll want to know: ಹೋಗೋಣ.
ಹೋಗೋಣ (hōgōṇa) — "let's go". The ending ‑ಓಣ (-ōṇa) means "let's", the same one you know from ಸಿಗೋಣ (sigōṇa), "let's meet".

What you've built.
A known ending on a known verb.

Guided Practice.

[your turn — say: hōgōṇa]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: hōgōṇa, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say taḍavāyitu, hōgōṇa]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Kannada for safely, then the Kannada for it's late, then say hōgōṇa again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does ಹೋಗೋಣ (hōgōṇa) mean? ("Let's go".) Say it once more.


Lesson 5 of 7.
ರಜೆ (raje) — a holiday, leave.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for it's late, then the Kannada for let's go.

You'll want to know: ರಜೆ.
ರಜೆ (raje) — "a holiday, time off".
ನಾಳೆ ರಜೆ (nāḷe raje) — "tomorrow is a holiday", with ನಾಳೆ, "tomorrow".

What you've built.
A good reason to say goodbye cheerfully.

Guided Practice.

[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: raje, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say nāḷe raje]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Kannada for it's late, then the Kannada for let's go, then say raje again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does ರಜೆ (raje) mean? ("A holiday, leave".) Say it once more.


Lesson 6 of 7.
How are you, really? — a first pass.

Warm-up.
[pause 3 seconds]
Tomorrow is a holiday, and it is time to go. Say both.

Guided Practice.
[pause 2 seconds after each]
[your turn — say: you are tired, you have a cold, a cough and a headache]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: tell a friend to take rest]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: name sadness, worry, fear, anger and laughter]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: you are thirsty and worn out; ask where the hospital is; ask for a tablet]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
Tiredness? (ಆಯಾಸ.) A cold? (ಶೀತ.) A cough? (ಕೆಮ್ಮು.) A headache? (ತಲೆನೋವು.) Rest? (ವಿಶ್ರಾಂತಿ.) Sadness, worry, fear, anger, laughter? (ದುಃಖ, ಚಿಂತೆ, ಭಯ, ಕೋಪ, ನಗು.) Thirst? (ಬಾಯಾರಿಕೆ.) Worn out? (ಸುಸ್ತು.) Sweat? (ಬೆವರು.) Hospital? (ಆಸ್ಪತ್ರೆ.) A tablet? (ಮಾತ್ರೆ.)


Lesson 7 of 7.
Talking it through — a second pass.

Warm-up.
[pause 3 seconds]
Someone speaks too fast. Ask them to speak slowly, and to say it once more.

Guided Practice.
[pause 2 seconds after each]
[your turn — say: a little louder; what is the meaning of this?; please write it down]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: one minute; please wait; please show me the way]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: I didn't hear; I didn't understand]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: really?; nothing; oh no!; is that so?; that's the one!]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: congratulations; an invitation for you; a gift for you; please come in; welcome]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: take care; go safely; it's late; let's go; tomorrow is a holiday]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
Slowly? (ನಿಧಾನವಾಗಿ.) Once more? (ಮತ್ತೊಮ್ಮೆ.) Loudly? (ಜೋರಾಗಿ.) Meaning? (ಅರ್ಥ.) Write it down? (ಬರೆದು ಕೊಡಿ.) One minute? (ಒಂದು ನಿಮಿಷ.) Please wait? (ಕಾಯಿರಿ.) Show me? (ತೋರಿಸಿ.) I didn't hear? (ಕೇಳಿಸಲಿಲ್ಲ.) I didn't understand? (ಅರ್ಥವಾಗಲಿಲ್ಲ.) Really? (ನಿಜವಾಗಲೂ?) Nothing? (ಏನೂ ಇಲ್ಲ.) Oh no? (ಅಯ್ಯೋ.) Is that so? (ಹೌದಾ?) The same? (ಅದೇ.) Congratulations? (ಅಭಿನಂದನೆಗಳು.) Invitation? (ಆಹ್ವಾನ.) Gift? (ಉಡುಗೊರೆ.) Come in? (ಒಳಗೆ ಬನ್ನಿ.) Welcome? (ಸ್ವಾಗತ.) Take care? (ಹುಷಾರು (huṣāru).) Safely? (ಜೋಪಾನ (jōpāna).) It's late? (ತಡವಾಯಿತು (taḍavāyitu).) Let's go? (ಹೋಗೋಣ (hōgōṇa).) Holiday? (ರಜೆ (raje).)
