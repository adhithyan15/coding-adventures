## You'll want to know: خدمات
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C443-ANAM-01]; assesses=[] -->

**خدمات** (*khadamât*) — "services".

> خدمات این هتل خیلی خوب است.
>
> *Khadamât-e in hotel kheyli khub ast.*
>
> This hotel's services are very good.

An Arabic plural of *khedmat* 'service'.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*khadamât* is the Arabic plural of *khedmat* 'service', which EtymDB takes from Arabic *ḫidma* 'service'. EtymDB does not list the plural.
