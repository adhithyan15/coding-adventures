## You'll want to know: les noces
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C489-NOCES-03]; assesses=[] -->

**les noces** — "wedding (celebration)".

> Mes grands-parents ont fêté leurs noces d'or.
>
> *My grandparents celebrated their golden wedding anniversary.*

Usually plural. *Le voyage de noces* — the honeymoon trip.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*les noces* is inherited from Vulgar Latin *noptiae*, a form of Latin *nūptiae* "a wedding", from *nūbere* "to marry" (Lewis & Short).
