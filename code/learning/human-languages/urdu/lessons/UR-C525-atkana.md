# اٹکانا (aṭkānā) — to keep (something) pending; to hold up

## You'll want to know: اٹکانا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C525-MOHLAT525-05]; assesses=[] -->

**اٹکانا** — *aṭkānā* — "to keep (something) pending; to hold up".

> اگر افسر نے فائل اٹکائی تو کام رک جائے گا۔
>
> *agar afsar ne fāil aṭkāī to kām ruk jāe gā.*
>
> If the officer holds up the file, the work will stop.

Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے میری فائل اٹکائی, *us ne merī fāil aṭkāī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**اٹکانا** (*aṭkānā*) is the causative of *aṭaknā*, "to get stuck", which CDIAL lists under *\*aṭṭakk*, a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 182).
