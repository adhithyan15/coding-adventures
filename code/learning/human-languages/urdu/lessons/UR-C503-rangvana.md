## You'll want to know: رنگوانا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C503-SILAN503-04]; assesses=[] -->

**رنگوانا** — *rangvānā* — "to have (something) painted".

> ہم نے برسات کے بعد دیواریں رنگوائیں۔
>
> *ham ne barsāt ke bād dīvāreṅ rangvāīṅ.*
>
> We had the walls painted after the rainy season.

Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے دروازہ رنگوایا, *us ne darvāzā rangvāyā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**رنگوانا** (*rangvānā*) is the causative of *rangnā*, "to colour": to have someone colour a thing. CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *rãgānā*, "to be coloured", under *\*raṅgayati*, "dyes", a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 10570).
