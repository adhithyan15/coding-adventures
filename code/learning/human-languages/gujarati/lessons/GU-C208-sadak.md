## You'll want to know: સડક
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C208-HIGHWAY208-01]; assesses=[] -->

**સડક** — *saḍak* — "main road, paved road".

> **સડક પહોળી હતી.** — *saḍak pahoḷī hatī* — "The road was wide."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**સડક** (*saḍak*) is a tadbhava. CDIAL lists it, glossed "road", under Sanskrit *sṛtí*, "road, path", extended with the suffix *-kk-* (CDIAL 13577).
