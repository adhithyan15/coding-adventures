## You'll want to know: തുഴ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C475-KAPPAL475-05]; assesses=[] -->

**തുഴ** — *tuḻa* — "an oar; a paddle".

> വള്ളക്കാരൻ രണ്ട് തുഴകൾ എടുത്തു.
>
> *vaḷḷakkāran raṇṭŭ tuḻakaḷ eṭuttu.*
>
> The boatman picked up two oars.

Plural: തുഴകൾ, *tuḻakaḷ*.

The verb is തുഴയുക, to row: a തുഴ is what you row with.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**തുഴ** (*tuḻa*) is native Dravidian, the noun of the root of തുഴയുക, to row or paddle (DEDR 3356).
