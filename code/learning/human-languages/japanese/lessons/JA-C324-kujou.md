## You'll want to know: くじょう
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C324-MONKU324-02]; assesses=[] -->

**くじょう** — *kujō* — "a formal complaint".

> **となりの おとが やかましいので、くじょうを 言いました。** — *tonari no oto ga yakamashii node, kujō o iimashita* — "The noise next door was loud, so I made a complaint."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**くじょう** (*kujō*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *ku*, "suffering" (Middle Chinese *khuX*) and *jō*, "circumstances" (*dzjeng*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
