## You'll want to know: اصطدم
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C301-TARIQ301-01]; assesses=[] -->

**اصطدم** — *iṣṭadama* — "to collide, to crash into".

> اصطدمت السيارة بشجرة كبيرة.
>
> *iṣṭadamat as-sayyāra bi-shajara kabīra.*
>
> The car crashed into a big tree.

Present: يصطدم, *yaṣṭadim*.

It takes بـ for what is hit: اصطدم بالجدار.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**اصطدم** (*iṣṭadama*) is built on the native Arabic root *ṣ-d-m* (Lane), "to strike, to knock against". It is Form VIII, where the *t* turns to *ṭ* after *ṣ*: "to strike against each other".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
