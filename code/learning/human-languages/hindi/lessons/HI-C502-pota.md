## You'll want to know: पोता
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C502-POTA502-01]; assesses=[] -->

**पोता** — *potā* — "a grandson (a son's son)".

> दादा अपने पोते के साथ खेल रहे हैं।
>
> *dādā apne pote ke sāth khel rahe haiṁ.*
>
> Grandfather is playing with his grandson.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पोता** (*potā*) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Hindi *potā*, "son's son", under Sanskrit *paútra*, "grandson" (CDIAL 8416). The granddaughter is *potī*.
