## You'll want to know: منتظر
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C401-ISTIQBAL401-02]; assesses=[] -->

**منتظر** — *muntazir* — "waiting (for someone)".

> ہمارا دوست باہر ہمارا منتظر تھا۔
>
> *hamārā dost bāhar hamārā muntazir thā.*
>
> Our friend was outside, waiting for us.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**منتظر** (*muntazir*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *muntaẓir*, "one who waits"; it is a cousin of **انتظار**, "waiting".
