## You'll want to know: ग्राहकः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C430-HATTA430-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ग्राहकः** — *grāhakaḥ* — "a customer".

> सायम् आपणे ग्राहकाः आगच्छन्ति।
>
> *sāyam āpaṇe grāhakāḥ āgacchanti.*
>
> In the evening customers come to the shop.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ग्राहकः** (*grāhakaḥ*) is built on the root √grah, "to take", which also gives *gṛhṇāti*, "takes", with the agent ending *-aka*; MW gives *grāhaka* as one who takes and as a purchaser (MW).
