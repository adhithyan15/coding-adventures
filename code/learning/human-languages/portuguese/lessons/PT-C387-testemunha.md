## You'll want to know: a testemunha
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C387-TRIBUNAL-03]; assesses=[] -->

**a testemunha** — "the witness".

> A testemunha viu o acidente da janela.
>
> *The witness saw the accident from the window.*

Feminine in form even for a man: *ele é a única testemunha*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*testemunha* is usually traced to Latin *testimōnium* "witness, evidence, testimony", built on *testis* "witness" (Lewis & Short); the evidence came to name the person who gives it.
