## You'll want to know: गडगडाट
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C439-VAVTAL439-03]; assesses=[] -->

**गडगडाट** — *gaḍgaḍāṭ* — "a rumble of thunder".

> रात्री ढगांचा गडगडाट ऐकू आला.
>
> *rātrī ḍhagāñcā gaḍgaḍāṭ aikū ālā.*
>
> At night the rumble of thunder could be heard.

Masculine.

ऐकू येणे, to be heard: the sound is the subject.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**गडगडाट** (*gaḍgaḍāṭ*) is an echo word that imitates rolling thunder; its origin is not settled.
