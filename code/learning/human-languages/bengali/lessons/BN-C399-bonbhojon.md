## You'll want to know: বনভোজন
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C399-BHROMON399-02]; assesses=[] -->

**বনভোজন** — *bônbhojôn* — "a picnic".

> শীতে আমরা নদীর পাড়ে বনভোজন করলাম।
>
> *shīte āmrā nôdīr pāṛe bônbhojôn kôrlām.*
>
> In winter we had a picnic on the riverbank.

Winter is the season for it in Bengal, with a cook-pot carried out to a field or a riverbank.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**বনভোজন** (*bônbhojôn*) joins **বন**, "a forest", Sanskrit *vana*, to Sanskrit *bhojana*, "eating, a meal" (Monier-Williams for both): a meal out in the woods.
