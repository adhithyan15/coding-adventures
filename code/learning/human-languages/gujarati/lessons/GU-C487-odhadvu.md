## You'll want to know: ઓઢાડવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C487-CAKRI487-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ઓઢાડવું** — *oḍhāḍvũ* — "to cover someone with a blanket".

> માતાએ બાળકને ધાબળો ઓઢાડ્યો.
>
> *mātāe bāḷakne dhābaḷo oḍhāḍyo.*
>
> The mother tucked a blanket around the child.

Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે દાદીને ધાબળો ઓઢાડ્યો, *teṇe dādīne dhābaḷo oḍhāḍyo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ઓઢાડવું** (*oḍhāḍvũ*) is the causative of **ઓઢવું** (*oḍhvũ*), "to wrap oneself in", which goes back to a reconstructed *\*ōḍḍh-*, "put on, cover" (CDIAL 2547).
