## You'll want to know: ਚਮਕਣਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C286-STORM286-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ਚਮਕਣਾ** — *chamakṇā* — "to flash; to shine".

> ਬੱਦਲਾਂ ਵਿੱਚ ਬਿਜਲੀ ਚਮਕੇਗੀ।
>
> *baddlāṁ vich bijlī chamkegī.*
>
> Lightning will flash in the clouds.

Past (it …): ਉਹ ਬਿਜਲੀ ਚਮਕੀ, *oh bijlī chamkī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਚਮਕਣਾ** (*chamakṇā*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*camakka*, "sudden movement"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *camakṇā*, "to shine", under it (CDIAL 4676).
