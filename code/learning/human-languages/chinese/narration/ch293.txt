Lesson 1 of 6.
zázhì — magazine.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for the people listening to the radio, then the Mandarin for to publish an article.

You'll want to know: zázhì.
zázhì — "magazine".
wǒ mǎi le liǎng běn zázhì — I bought two magazines.
Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
zázhì is built from zá 'mixed' and zhì 'a record', both inherited from Middle Chinese, dzop and tsyiH (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to gather' and 'five colours together' and the second 'to note down': a record of many things.

Script — the hand piece, then a hook, a post and a fork.
报 (bào).
Two pieces, left before right. Count the pieces, not the strokes.
The left piece, three strokes. The hand piece of 打: a bar, a vertical that hooks to the left at the foot, and a stroke rising to the right across it.
The right piece, four strokes. A bar that turns down and hooks back to the left. A long vertical down the left side of the piece. Then the fork: a bar that turns into a sweep down to the lower left, and a long stroke falling to the right.
3 + 4 = seven.
