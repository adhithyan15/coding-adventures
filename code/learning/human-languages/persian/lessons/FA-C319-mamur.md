## You'll want to know: مامور
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C319-MAMUR-01]; assesses=[] -->

**مامور** (*ma'mur*) — "an officer, an agent".

> دو مامور پلیس جلوی در ایستاده بودند.
>
> *Do ma'mur-e polis jelo-ye dar istâde budand.*
>
> Two police officers were standing at the door.

Plural *ma'murân*. Careful print adds a hamza sign over the alef.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ma'mur* is borrowed from Arabic *ma'mūr* (EtymDB), the passive participle "one who is given an order".
