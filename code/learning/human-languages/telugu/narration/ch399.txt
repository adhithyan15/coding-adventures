Telugu, chapter 399: Rights and Duties.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
కర్తవ్యం (kartavyaṁ) — a duty; an obligation.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a punishment, then the Telugu for to ban.

You'll want to know: కర్తవ్యం.
కర్తవ్యం — kartavyaṁ — "a duty; an obligation".
తల్లిదండ్రులను చూసుకోవడం మన కర్తవ్యం. tallidaṇḍrulanu cūsukōvaḍaṁ mana kartavyaṁ. Looking after our parents is our duty.
A higher word than బాధ్యత: a moral duty.

The word, taken apart.
కర్తవ్యం (kartavyaṁ) is borrowed from Sanskrit kartavya, "to be done", and as a noun "that which ought to be done, a duty" (Monier-Williams), from kṛ, "to do".

Lesson 2 of 5.
స్వేచ్ఛ (svēccha) — freedom; liberty.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to ban, then the Telugu for a duty.

You'll want to know: స్వేచ్ఛ.
స్వేచ్ఛ — svēccha — "freedom; liberty".
ప్రతి మనిషికి మాట్లాడే స్వేచ్ఛ ఉండాలి. prati maniṣiki māṭlāḍē svēccha uṇḍāli. Every person should have the freedom to speak.
స్వ, one’s own, + ఇచ్ఛ, wish. స్వేచ్ఛగా, freely.

The word, taken apart.
స్వేచ్ఛ (svēccha) is borrowed from Sanskrit svecchā, "one’s own wish, free will" (Monier-Williams), from sva, "own", and icchā, "wish".

Lesson 3 of 5.
సమానత్వం (samānatvaṁ) — equality.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a duty, then the Telugu for freedom.

You'll want to know: సమానత్వం.
సమానత్వం — samānatvaṁ — "equality".
ఆడవాళ్ళకు మగవాళ్ళకు సమానత్వం ఉండాలి. āḍavāḷḷaku magavāḷḷaku samānatvaṁ uṇḍāli. There should be equality between women and men.
సమాన, equal, + -త్వం, -ness. సమానంగా, equally.

The word, taken apart.
సమానత్వం (samānatvaṁ) is borrowed from Sanskrit samānatva, "equality" (Monier-Williams), from samāna, "same, alike" (CDIAL 13211), and the abstract ending -tva.

Lesson 4 of 5.
అన్యాయం (anyāyaṁ) — injustice; a wrong.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for freedom, then the Telugu for equality.

You'll want to know: అన్యాయం.
అన్యాయం — anyāyaṁ — "injustice; a wrong".
పేదవాళ్ళకు అన్యాయం జరిగింది. pēdavāḷḷaku anyāyaṁ jarigindi. An injustice was done to the poor.
అ-, not, + న్యాయం, justice; న్యాయమైన, fair, which you know, is the same word.

The word, taken apart.
అన్యాయం (anyāyaṁ) is borrowed from Sanskrit anyāya, "unjust or unlawful action" (Monier-Williams), the opposite of nyāya, "rule, justice".

You'll want to know: గౌరవించు.
గౌరవించు — gauraviñcu — "to honour; to respect".
మనం పెద్దలను గౌరవించాలి. manaṁ peddalanu gauraviñcāli. We should respect our elders.
Past (I …): గౌరవించాను, gauraviñcānu.
The verb of గౌరవం, respect, which you know.

The word, taken apart.
గౌరవించు (gauraviñcu) is built on Sanskrit gaurava, "weight", and so "venerableness" (CDIAL 4346), with the Telugu verb ending -ఇంచు.
