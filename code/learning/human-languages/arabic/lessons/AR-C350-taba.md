## You'll want to know: طبعة
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C350-NASHIR350-02]; assesses=[] -->

**طبعة** — *ṭabʿa* — "an edition, a printing".

> صدرت طبعة جديدة من الكتاب.
>
> *ṣadarat ṭabʿa jadīda min al-kitāb.*
>
> A new edition of the book came out.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**طبعة** (*ṭabʿa*) is built on the native Arabic root *ṭ-b-ʿ* (Lane), "to seal, to stamp", the root of the taught *ṭabaʿa*, to print. *Ṭabʿa* is one printing.
