## You'll want to know: सत्ता
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C363-SATTA363-01]; assesses=[] -->

**सत्ता** — *sattā* — "power (political)".

> सत्ता हातात आली की माणूस बदलतो.
>
> *sattā hātāt ālī kī māṇūs badalto.*
>
> When power comes into his hands, a man changes.

Feminine.

सत्तेवर येणे, to come to power.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सत्ता** (*sattā*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *sattā*, "existence, being" (Monier-Williams). Marathi uses it for the power to rule.
