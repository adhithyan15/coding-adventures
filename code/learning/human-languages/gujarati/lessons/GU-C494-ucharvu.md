# ઉછરવું (ucharvũ) — to grow up; to be brought up

## You'll want to know: ઉછરવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C494-BALPAN494-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ઉછરવું** — *ucharvũ* — "to grow up; to be brought up".

> મીરા ગામમાં ઉછરી છે.
>
> *Mīrā gāmmā̃ uchrī che.*
>
> Mira grew up in a village.

Past (he …): તે મામાના ઘરે ઉછર્યો, *te māmānā ghare ucharyo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ઉછરવું** (*ucharvũ*) is inherited from Sanskrit *ucchrīyatē*, "is raised"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb, "to be brought up" (CDIAL 1864). The causative **ઉછેરવું** (*uchervũ*), "to bring up", is its partner.
