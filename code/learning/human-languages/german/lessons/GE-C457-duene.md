## You'll want to know: die Düne
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C457-KUESTE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**die Düne** — "the dune".

> Hinter den Dünen liegt der Strand.
>
> *The beach lies behind the dunes.*

Plural: *die Dünen*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Düne* came into the standard language from Low German, a cousin of Dutch *duin* (EtymDB lists the word); English *dune* came from the same Dutch source.
