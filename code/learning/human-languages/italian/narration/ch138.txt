Lesson 1 of 5.
il futuro — the future.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for recently, then the Italian for to happen.

You'll want to know: il futuro.
il futuro — "the future". Italian builds the future on the infinitive: parlare becomes parlerò — "I will speak" — then parlerai, parlerà.

Lesson 2 of 5.
stasera — this evening.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for to happen, then the Italian for the future.

You'll want to know: stasera.
stasera — "this evening". The time word can stand first: Stasera lavorerò. — "This evening I will work." Tra a length of time gives "in": tra due giorni — "in two days".
