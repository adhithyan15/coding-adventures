## You'll want to know: īnscītia, īnscītiae
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C407-SCIENTIA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**īnscītia, īnscītiae** — "ignorance, lack of skill".

> Īnscītiā nautae nāvis periit.
>
> *The ship was lost through the sailor's lack of skill.*

*īnscītia, īnscītiae* f., built on *īnscītus*, not knowing, from *in-* and *sciō*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*īnscītia* is built on *īnscītus* "unknowing" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short), from *in-* "not" and the root of *sciō* "to know".
