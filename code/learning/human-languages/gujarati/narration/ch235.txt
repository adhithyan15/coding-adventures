Gujarati, chapter 235: The dog that barked.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ભસવું (bhasvũ) — to bark.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for sheep, then the Gujarati for to graze.

You'll want to know: ભસવું.
ભસવું — bhasvũ — "to bark".
રાત્રે કૂતરો ભસ્યો. — rātre kūtro bhasyo — "The dog barked at night."

The word, taken apart.
ભસવું (bhasvũ) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from bhaṣati, "barks, growls" (CDIAL 9423).

Lesson 2 of 5.
કરડવું (karaḍvũ) — to bite.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to graze, then the Gujarati for to bark.

You'll want to know: કરડવું.
કરડવું — karaḍvũ — "to bite".
ગઈકાલે મને કૂતરો કરડ્યો. — gaīkāle mane kūtro karaḍyo — "Yesterday a dog bit me."

The word, taken apart.
The origin of કરડવું (karaḍvũ) is not settled: CDIAL lists no Gujarati form like it, and gives ḍasvũ, "bites, stings", as the Gujarati word under Sanskrit daśati, "bites" (CDIAL 6230).

Lesson 3 of 5.
પાળવું (pāḷvũ) — to keep a pet, to rear; to obey.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to bark, then the Gujarati for to bite.

You'll want to know: પાળવું.
પાળવું — pāḷvũ — "to keep a pet, to rear; to obey".
તેણે એક કૂતરો પાળ્યો. — teṇe ek kūtro pāḷyo — "He kept a dog."

The word, taken apart.
પાળવું (pāḷvũ) is a tadbhava. It comes from Sanskrit pālayati, "guards, protects", and CDIAL glosses the Gujarati verb "to rear, keep" (CDIAL 8129).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: ચાટવું.
ચાટવું — cāṭvũ — "to lick".
તેણે મારો હાથ ચાટ્યો. — teṇe māro hāth cāṭyo — "It licked my hand."

The word, taken apart.
ચાટવું (cāṭvũ) comes from caṭṭ, "lick, taste", a reconstructed form that CDIAL thinks may be imitative; it lists the Gujarati verb, glossed "to lick" (CDIAL 4573).

પૂંછડી (pū̃chḍī) — tail.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to keep a pet, then the Gujarati for to lick.

You'll want to know: પૂંછડી.
પૂંછડી — pū̃chḍī — "tail".
કૂતરાની પૂંછડી લાંબી હતી. — kūtrānī pū̃chḍī lā̃bī hatī — "The dog's tail was long."

The word, taken apart.
પૂંછડી (pū̃chḍī) is a tadbhava. It comes from Sanskrit puccha, "tail", where CDIAL lists Gujarati pũch and puchṛũ, "tail" (CDIAL 8249).
