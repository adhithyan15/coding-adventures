## You'll want to know: مدال
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C343-BARANDE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**مدال** (*medâl*) — "a medal".

> او در مسابقه مدال طلا گرفت.
>
> *U dar mosâbeqe medâl-e talâ gereft.*
>
> She won a gold medal in the competition.

*Medâl-e noqre* is silver, *medâl-e boronz* bronze.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*medâl* is usually traced to French *médaille*, "medal"; EtymDB lists no entry.
