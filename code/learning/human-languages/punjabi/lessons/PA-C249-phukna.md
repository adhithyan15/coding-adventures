## You'll want to know: ਫੂਕਣਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C249-GHR249-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ਫੂਕਣਾ** — *phūkṇā* — "to blow on a fire".

> ਸਵੇਰੇ ਮੈਂ ਚੁੱਲ੍ਹਾ ਫੂਕਦੀ ਹਾਂ।
>
> *savere maiṁ chullhā phūkdī hāṁ.*
>
> In the morning I blow on the stove to get the fire going.

Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਚੁੱਲ੍ਹਾ ਫੂਕਿਆ, *maiṁ chullhā phūkiā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਫੂਕਣਾ** (*phūkṇā*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*phūtka*, "blowing"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *phūkṇā*, "to blow" (CDIAL 9102).
