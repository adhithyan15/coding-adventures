## You'll want to know: le rayon
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C520-LIBRAIRE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**le rayon** — "section (of a shop); shelf; ray".

> Les livres pour enfants sont au rayon du fond.
>
> *The children's books are in the section at the back.*

Also a ray: *un rayon de soleil*. That is a different word with the same form.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

This *rayon*, a shelf or a section, comes from Old French *ree* "honeycomb", a word of Germanic origin. *Rayon* "a ray" is a different word, from Latin *radius*.
