## You'll want to know: しんろ

**しんろ** — *shinro* — "a career path; one's course".

> **せんせいと しんろを はなしあいました。** — *sensei to shinro o hanashiaimashita* — "I talked over my career path with my teacher."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**しんろ** (*shinro*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *shin*, "advance" (Middle Chinese *tsinH*) and *ro*, "path" (*luH*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
