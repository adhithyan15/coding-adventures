## You'll want to know: युनक्ति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C407-YANTA407-05]; assesses=[] -->

**युनक्ति** — *yunakti* — "harnesses; yokes".

> यन्ता रथे अश्वं युनक्ति।
>
> *yantā rathe aśvaṃ yunakti.*
>
> The charioteer harnesses the horse to the chariot.

Past (he …): सः रथे अश्वं युक्तवान्, *saḥ rathe aśvaṃ yuktavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**युनक्ति** (*yunakti*) comes from the root √yuj, "to yoke, to join", which also gives *yojayati*, "joins"; MW gives its participle *yukta* as yoked or harnessed; MW compares Greek *zeugnumi* and Latin *jungo* (MW).
