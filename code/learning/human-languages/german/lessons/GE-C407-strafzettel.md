## You'll want to know: der Strafzettel
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C407-BLITZER-05]; assesses=[] -->

**der Strafzettel** — "the penalty ticket, the fine slip".

> Am Abend findet sie einen Strafzettel am Auto.
>
> *In the evening she finds a ticket on the car.*

Same form in the plural. In everyday speech also *das Knöllchen*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Strafzettel* is built from *Strafe* 'punishment, fine', inherited from Middle High German *strāfe* (EtymDB), and *Zettel* 'slip of paper', which goes back to Medieval Latin *cedula* (EtymDB).
