## You'll want to know: சோதி
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C543-KATTALAI543-05]; assesses=[] -->

**சோதி** — *cōti* — "to test; to check".

> பாதுகாவலர் எல்லாப் பைகளையும் சோதித்தார்.
>
> *pātukāvalar ellāp paikaḷaiyum cōtittār.*
>
> The guard checked all the bags.

Past (I …): சோதித்தேன், *cōtittēṉ*.

What is checked takes -ஐ. The noun is சோதனை (*cōtaṉai*), a test.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**சோதி** (*cōti*) is borrowed from the Sanskrit stem of *śōdhana*, "cleaning, purifying" (CDIAL 12629), made a Tamil verb with -இ.
