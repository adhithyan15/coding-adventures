Japanese, chapter 307: A Call in the Night.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
つながる (tsunagaru) — to be connected; to get through.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for surface mail, then the Japanese for to receive.

You'll want to know: つながる.
つながる — tsunagaru — "to be connected; to get through".
やっと でんわが つながりました。 — yatto denwa ga tsunagarimashita — "At last the call went through."
Polite つながります, tsunagarimasu; a godan verb. Past (polite): でんわが つながりました。, denwa ga tsunagarimashita.

The word, taken apart.
つながる (tsunagaru) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict).

Lesson 3 of 5.
でんごん (dengon) — a message (passed on).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for to be connected, then the Japanese for to join.

You'll want to know: でんごん.
でんごん — dengon — "a message (passed on)".
あにから でんごんが あります。 — ani kara dengon ga arimasu — "There is a message from my brother."

The word, taken apart.
でんごん (dengon) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are den, "transmit" (Middle Chinese drjwen) and gon, "word" (ngjon) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).

Lesson 5 of 5.
かけなおす (kakenaosu) — to call back.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for a message (passed on), then the Japanese for a mobile phone.

You'll want to know: かけなおす.
かけなおす — kakenaosu — "to call back".
あとで かけなおします。 — ato de kakenaoshimasu — "I will call back later."
Polite かけなおします, kakenaoshimasu; a godan verb. Past (polite): ははが でんわを かけなおしました。, haha ga denwa o kakenaoshimashita.

The word, taken apart.
かけなおす (kakenaosu) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, built from kake, "calling" (from kakeru), and naosu, "to do again" (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
