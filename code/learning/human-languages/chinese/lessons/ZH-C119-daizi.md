## You'll want to know: dàizi
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C119-BAGS119-04]; assesses=[] -->

*dàizi* — "bag; sack".

> *nǐ xūyào dàizi ma* — Do you need a bag?

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*dàizi* is built from dài and the noun ending zi, both inherited from Middle Chinese, *dojH* and *tsiX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription); the dictionary glosses the first as a kind of sack.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for everyday things.
