# ડુબાડવું (ḍubāḍvũ) — to submerge; to flood

## Warm-up

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to lay waste, then the Gujarati for to be swept away.

## You'll want to know: ડુબાડવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C304-PUR304-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ડુબાડવું** — *ḍubāḍvũ* — "to submerge; to flood".

> નદીએ આખું ગામ ડુબાડી દીધું.
>
> *nadīe ākhũ gām ḍubāḍī dīdhũ.*
>
> The river flooded the whole village.

Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે હોડી ડુબાડી, *teṇe hoḍī ḍubāḍī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ડુબાડવું** (*ḍubāḍvũ*) is the causative of **ડૂબવું**, to sink, from *\*ḍubb*, "sink", a form CDIAL reconstructs (CDIAL 5561): to make something go under. The long *ū* of **ડૂબવું** shortens in the longer verb.

One more word for telling what happened.
