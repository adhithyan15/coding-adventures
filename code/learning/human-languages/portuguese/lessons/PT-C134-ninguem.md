## You'll want to know: ninguém
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C134-NEG134-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ninguém** — "nobody, no one". **Não vejo ninguém.** — "I don't see anybody." On its own it answers a question: **Ninguém.**
