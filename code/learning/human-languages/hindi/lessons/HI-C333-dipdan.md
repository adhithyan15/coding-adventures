# दीपदान (dīpdān) — the floating of lamps

## You'll want to know: दीपदान
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C333-GHAT333-05]; assesses=[] -->

**दीपदान** — *dīpdān* — "the floating of lamps".

> शाम को घाट पर दीपदान हुआ।
>
> *śām ko ghāṭ par dīpdān huā.*
>
> In the evening lamps were floated on the river at the ghat.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**दीपदान** (*dīpdān*) joins Sanskrit *dīpa*, "a lamp", and *dāna*, "a giving" (Monier-Williams): lamps given to the river, set afloat at dusk.
