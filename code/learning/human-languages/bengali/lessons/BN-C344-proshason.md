## You'll want to know: প্রশাসন
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C344-RASHTRO344-03]; assesses=[] -->

**প্রশাসন** — *prôshāsôn* — "an administration".

> স্থানীয় প্রশাসন রাস্তাটা সারাল।
>
> *sthānīyô prôshāsôn rāstāṭā sārālô.*
>
> The local administration repaired the road.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**প্রশাসন** (*prôshāsôn*) is Sanskrit *praśāsana*, guidance or government (Monier-Williams). Bengali uses it for the officials who carry out the government's work.
