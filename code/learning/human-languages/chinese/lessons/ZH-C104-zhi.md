## You'll want to know: zhǐ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C104-DESK104-04]; assesses=[] -->

*zhǐ* — "paper".

> *qǐng gěi wǒ yì zhāng zhǐ* — Could I have a sheet of paper, please?

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*zhǐ* is inherited from Middle Chinese *tsyeX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), where the dictionary says paper is as smooth as a whetstone, and that in the Later Han it was made from old fishing nets and tree bark.
