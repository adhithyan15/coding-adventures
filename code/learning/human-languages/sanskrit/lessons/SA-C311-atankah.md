## You'll want to know: आतंकः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C311-KIMVADANTI311-02]; assesses=[] -->

**आतंकः** — *ātaṅkaḥ* — "dread; panic".

> किंवदन्त्या जनेषु आतंकः वर्धते।
>
> *kiṃvadantyā janeṣu ātaṅkaḥ vardhate.*
>
> Because of the rumour dread grows among the people.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**आतंकः** (*ātaṅkaḥ*) MW gives *ātaṅka* as disease, and also as affliction of mind, apprehension, fear, without a root in the entry, so its origin is left uncertain here (MW).
