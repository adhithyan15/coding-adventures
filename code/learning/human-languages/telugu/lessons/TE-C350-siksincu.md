# శిక్షించు (śikṣiñcu) — to punish

## You'll want to know: శిక్షించు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C350-NERAM350-05]; assesses=[] -->

**శిక్షించు** — *śikṣiñcu* — "to punish".

> న్యాయమూర్తి నేరస్థుడిని శిక్షించాడు.
>
> *nyāyamūrti nērasthuḍini śikṣiñcāḍu.*
>
> The judge punished the criminal.

Past (I …): శిక్షించాను, *śikṣiñcānu*.

Who is punished takes -ను; a teacher or a parent can శిక్షించు too.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**శిక్షించు** (*śikṣiñcu*) is built on Sanskrit *śikṣā*, "learning, study", which also came to mean "punishment" (CDIAL 12432), with the Telugu verb ending *-iñcu*.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
