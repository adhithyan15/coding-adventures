## You'll want to know: o susto
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C308-SUSTO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**o susto** — "the fright, the scare".

> Que susto! Pensei que estava sozinho em casa.
>
> *What a fright! I thought I was alone in the house.*

*Pregar um susto a alguém* — to give someone a fright.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

The origin of *susto* is not settled; EtymDB lists it only beside Spanish *susto*.
