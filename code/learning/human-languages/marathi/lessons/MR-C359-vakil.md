## You'll want to know: वकील
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C359-KHATLA359-01]; assesses=[] -->

**वकील** — *vakīl* — "a lawyer".

> घराच्या वादासाठी त्याने वकील नेमला.
>
> *gharācyā vādāsāṭhī tyāne vakīl nemlā.*
>
> He appointed a lawyer for the dispute over the house.

Masculine.

Plural वकील; वकिली, the legal profession.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**वकील** (*vakīl*) is a loan from Persian, usually traced to Arabic *wakīl*, "agent, one entrusted".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
