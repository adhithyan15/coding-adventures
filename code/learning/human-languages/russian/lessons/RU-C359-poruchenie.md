## You'll want to know: поручение
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C359-OBYAZANNOSTI-03]; assesses=[] -->

**поручение** (*poruchéniye*) — "an assignment, an errand".

> Начальник дал мне важное поручение.
>
> *Nachálnik dal mne vázhnoye poruchéniye.*
>
> The boss gave me an important assignment.

Neuter. *Выполнить поручение* — to carry out an assignment.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**поручение** is derived from *поручать* "to entrust", from *ручать* "to vouch", from *рука* "hand" (EtymDB): a task placed in someone’s hands.
