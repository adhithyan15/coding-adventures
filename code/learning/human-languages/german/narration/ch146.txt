Lesson 1 of 5.
erklären — to explain.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to recommend, then the German for to decide.

You'll want to know: erklären.
erklären — "to explain".

The word, taken apart.
erklären is er- + the verb "to make clear", built on the word for "clear", Middle High German klār from Latin clārus (EtymDB 2.1). To explain is to make clear.
