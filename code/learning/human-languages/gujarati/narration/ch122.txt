Gujarati, chapter 122: Bear, Jackal, Wolf, Pigeon, Duck.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
રીંછ (rī̃ch) — a bear.

Warm-up.

Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a rabbit, then the Gujarati for a deer.
