Arabic, chapter 119: Glove, Scarf, Lunch, Dinner, Vinegar.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
قفاز (quffāz) — a glove.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a wallet, then the Arabic for an umbrella.

You'll want to know: قفاز.
قفاز — quffāz — "a glove".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: quffāz]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: quffāz, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say quffāz]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Arabic for a wallet, then the Arabic for an umbrella, then say quffāz again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does قفاز (quffāz) mean? ("A glove".) Say it once more.

Lesson 6 of 7.
Wanting and planning — a first pass.

Warm-up.
[pause 3 seconds]
Say the words for dinner and vinegar.

Guided Practice.
[pause 2 seconds after each]
[your turn — say: a tool, to own, to lose, to keep, a talent]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: to long for, an expectation, enthusiasm, a wish, a preference]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: longing, ambition, a purpose, a project, a suggestion]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: interest, to hate, a desire, a dream, hope]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: a goal, a plan, a need, to need, to wish]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
A tool? (أداة, adāt.) To own? (ملك, malaka.) To lose? (خسر, khasira.) To keep? (حفظ, ḥafiẓa.) A talent? (موهبة, mawhiba.) To long for? (اشتاق, ishtāqa.) An expectation? (توقع, tawaqquʿ.) Enthusiasm? (حماس, ḥamās.) A wish? (أمنية, umniyya.) A preference? (تفضيل, tafḍīl.) Longing? (شوق, shawq.) Ambition? (طموح, ṭumūḥ.) A purpose? (غاية, ghāya.) A project? (مشروع, mashrūʿ.) A suggestion? (اقتراح, iqtirāḥ.) Interest? (اهتمام, ihtimām.) To hate? (كره, kariha.) A desire? (رغبة, raghba.) A dream? (حلم, ḥulm.) Hope? (أمل, amal.) A goal? (هدف, hadaf.) A plan? (خطة, khuṭṭa.) A need? (حاجة, ḥāja.) To need? (احتاج, iḥtāja.) To wish? (تمنى, tamannā.)
