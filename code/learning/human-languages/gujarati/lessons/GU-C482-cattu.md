## You'll want to know: ચત્તું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C482-THOKAR482-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ચત્તું** — *cattũ* — "flat on one's back".

> તે ભીની ભોંય પર ચત્તો પડ્યો.
>
> *te bhīnī bhõy par catto paḍyo.*
>
> He fell flat on his back on the wet floor.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ચત્તું** (*cattũ*) goes back to a reconstructed *\*citta-*, "lying on the back"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati word (CDIAL 4800).
