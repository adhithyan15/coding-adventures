## You'll want to know: चिरडणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C341-KADAMBARI341-05]; assesses=[] -->

**चिरडणे** — *ciraḍṇe* — "to crush".

> गोष्टीत हत्तीने राक्षसाला पायाखाली चिरडलं.
>
> *goṣṭīt hattīne rākṣasālā pāyākhālī ciraḍlaṁ.*
>
> In the story the elephant crushed the ogre underfoot.

Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने चिरडलं, *tyāne ciraḍlaṁ*.

Transitive: the one crushed takes -ला.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**चिरडणे** (*ciraḍṇe*) is a Marathi verb of crushing; its origin is not settled.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
