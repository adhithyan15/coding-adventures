## You'll want to know: die Absicht
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C346-ZWECK-02]; assesses=[] -->

**die Absicht** — "the intention".

> Es war nicht meine Absicht, so laut zu sein.
>
> *It was not my intention to be so loud.*

Plural: *die Absichten*. *Mit Absicht* — on purpose.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Absicht* is built from *ab* and *Sicht* "sight", from the family of *sehen* "to see", Old High German *sehan* (EtymDB): the thing you have your eye on.
