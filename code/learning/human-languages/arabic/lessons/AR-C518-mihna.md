## You'll want to know: مهنة
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C518-MIHNA518-01]; assesses=[] -->

**مهنة** — *mihna* — "a profession, a trade".

> مهنة أبي التدريس.
>
> *mihnat abī at-tadrīs.*
>
> My father's profession is teaching.

Plural: مهن, *mihan*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**مهنة** (*mihna*) is built on the native Arabic root *m-h-n*, "to serve, to work" (Lane). Mihna is the work one serves at.
