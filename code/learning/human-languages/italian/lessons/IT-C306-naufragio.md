## You'll want to know: il naufragio
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C306-SALVATAGGIO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**il naufragio** — "the shipwreck".

> Dopo il naufragio sono arrivati su un'isola.
>
> *After the shipwreck they reached an island.*

Also a failure: *il naufragio di un progetto*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*naufragio* is a learned borrowing from Latin *naufragium* (EtymDB), built on *nāvis* "ship" and *frangere* "to break".
