## You'll want to know: مغرب
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C408-SIMT408-05]; assesses=[] -->

**مغرب** — *maghrib* — "the west".

> شام کو سورج مغرب میں چھپتا ہے۔
>
> *shām ko sūraj maghrib meṅ chhuptā hai.*
>
> In the evening the sun goes down in the west.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**مغرب** (*maghrib*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *maghrib*, "the place where the sun sets"; it is also the name of the prayer at sunset.
