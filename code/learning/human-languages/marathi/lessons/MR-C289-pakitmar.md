## You'll want to know: पाकीटमार
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C289-GUNHA289-04]; assesses=[] -->

**पाकीटमार** — *pākīṭmār* — "a pickpocket".

> गर्दीत एका पाकीटमाराने माझं पाकीट मारलं.
>
> *gardīt ekā pākīṭmārāne mājhaṁ pākīṭ mārlaṁ.*
>
> In the crowd a pickpocket stole my wallet.

Masculine.

Built from पाकीट, a wallet, and मार-, from मारणे: पाकीट मारणे, to pick a pocket.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पाकीटमार** (*pākīṭmār*) is a Marathi compound built from *pākīṭ*, 'a wallet', a loan from English *packet*, and *mār-*, the stem of *mārṇe*, 'to strike' (CDIAL 10066).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
