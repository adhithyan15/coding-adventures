# Sanskrit A2 mock rubric

Score either A2 mock against
[`task-shapes/a2.json`](../../task-shapes/a2.json). Reading, listening, writing,
and speaking are independent 100-point papers. A learner passes only by earning
at least **60/100 on every paper** in one administration; a 240-point total
cannot compensate for a failed skill.

## Reading — 100 points

- notices and correspondence: 30 points across 8 keyed items;
- connected everyday text one: 35 points across 8 keyed items;
- connected everyday text two: 35 points across 8 keyed items.

Divide each part evenly and retain one decimal place. Score the keyed meaning,
sequence, purpose, detail, or simple inference. A2 sources use external sandhi;
successful decoding is part of the reading construct.

## Listening — 100 points

- announcements: 30 points across 7 keyed items;
- everyday conversations: 35 points across 7 keyed items;
- connected account: 35 points across 6 keyed items.

Use only the confidential mastered files, twice each, at 110–130 words per
minute and within the task-shape duration bands. Score selected answers only.
Do not provide a transcript, romanization, dictionary, translator, or
learner-controlled replay.

## Writing — 100 points

The 25–35 word functional response is worth 40 points: task fulfilment and
relevant content 14, reader and purpose 8, vocabulary and grammar 8, external
sandhi 5, and Devanagari orthographic control 5. The 70–90 word connected text
is worth 60 points: task fulfilment and development 18, organisation and
cohesion 12, reader and purpose 8, vocabulary and grammar 12, external sandhi
5, and orthographic control 5. A response outside its band loses only content
that cannot be evidenced; there is no automatic length penalty.

Orthographic control includes recognisable ordered letter bodies, vowel signs
and conjuncts, continuous śirorekhā where the word requires it, word-final
visarga or anusvara, and clear word boundaries. Romanized, traced, translated,
copied, or answer-model-dependent work earns no independent-writing credit.
Vedic accent and morphology are outside this assessment.

## Speaking — 100 points

The six-question interview is worth 30 points, the 90–120 second prepared
account 35, and the information-exchange role-play 35. Within each part award
35% for task fulfilment and relevant development, 25% for interaction and
repair, 25% for Sanskrit vocabulary and grammar, and 15% for pronunciation and
fluency. Permit paper keyword notes made during five minutes of preparation,
never a written answer script. Accept a consistent documented pronunciation
tradition. Examiner assistance beyond one same-pace repeat is reflected in
interaction scoring.

## Evidence boundary

These source papers, confidential masters, keys, and rubric are not qualified
Sanskrit recordings, independent human review, standard setting, or book-only
validation. They make no readiness or validated-pass claim. Those artifacts
remain separate prerequisites, and `assessment.json` remains absent until they
exist.
