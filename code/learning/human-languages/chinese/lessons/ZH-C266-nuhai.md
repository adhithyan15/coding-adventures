## You'll want to know: 女孩
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C266-AGE266-04]; assesses=[] -->

**女孩** — *nǚhái* — "girl".

> **这个女孩很喜欢读书** *zhège nǚhái hěn xǐhuan dúshū* — This girl loves studying.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**女孩** (*nǚhái*) is built from 女 'female' and 孩 'a child', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *nrjoX* and *hoj* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription).
