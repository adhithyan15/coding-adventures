## You'll want to know: تضمین کردن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C394-RISK-03]; assesses=[] -->

**تضمین کردن** (*tazmin kardan*) — "to guarantee".

> هیچ کس نمیتواند موفقیت را تضمین کند.
>
> *hich kas nemitavânad movaffaqiyat râ tazmin konad.*
>
> Nobody can guarantee success.

*Tazmin* "guarantee" + *kardan*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**تضمین** is usually traced to Arabic *taḍmīn* "guaranteeing"; Persian says the Arabic *ḍ* as *z*. *Kardan* "to do" is inherited Persian.
