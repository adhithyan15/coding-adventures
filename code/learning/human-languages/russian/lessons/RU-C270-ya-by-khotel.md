# я бы хотел — would, and the polite request

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[RU-LEX-KHOTET-01, RU-LEX-KOFE, RU-GRAMMAR-YESLI-FUTURE-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "I want a coffee". (*Ya khochú kófe.*) Then "if I have
time, I'll ring". (*Yésli búdet vrémya, ya pozvonyú.*) That was a real
plan. Now a softer world: what would be.

## Grammar Lens: бы and the -л form
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-GRAMMAR-BY-PAST-FORM-01]; assesses=[RU-GRAMMAR-PAST-L-GENDER-01, RU-LEX-CHAI, RU-LEX-C88-LOC88-02] -->

**бы** — *by* — is a small word that makes "would". It goes with the
past form of the verb, the *-л* form, and here that form says nothing
about the past:

> **Я бы хотел кофе.** — *ya by khotél kófe* — I'd like a coffee.
> **Я бы хотела чай.** — *ya by khotéla chay* — I'd like tea; a woman
> says it.
> **Я бы пошёл в кино.** — *ya by poshól v kinó* — I'd go to the cinema.

**Бы** can also follow the verb: *Я хотел бы кофе.* It agrees with nothing;
the *-л* form does the agreeing.

## Grammar Lens: the polite request
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-GRAMMAR-BY-POLITE-01]; assesses=[RU-LEX-MOCH-01, RU-LEX-MEDLENNO-01, RU-LEX-GOVORIT, RU-LEX-C174-ACT174-03, RU-LEX-POVTORITE-01, RU-GRAMMAR-PAST-LI-PLURAL-01] -->

*Я хочу* is direct; **я бы хотел** is the polite way to ask for a thing.
For asking someone to do something, Russian says "could you not …":

> **Вы не могли бы говорить медленно?** — *vy ne moglí by govorít médlenno?*
> — Could you speak slowly? With *повторить*: **Вы не могли бы повторить?**

**Могли** is the *-ли* form of *мочь*, for polite *вы*; *повторить* is the
partner of *повторять*. The **не** makes it gentler, not negative.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[RU-GRAMMAR-CHTOBY-01, RU-LEX-BYT] -->

**бы** is, by the usual account, a worn-down old form of **быть**, left
over from a past tense Russian no longer has; EtymDB 2.1 lists *бы*
without a parent. It is the same *бы* that sits inside **чтобы**, "in
order to", a wish fastened to *что*.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[RU-GRAMMAR-BY-POLITE-01, RU-GRAMMAR-BY-PAST-FORM-01] -->

[PAUSE 1s]
- [YOU SAY: *Ya by khotél kófe*, then a woman: *Ya by khotéla kófe.*]
- [YOU SAY: *Ya khotél by chay.* The *by* after the verb]
- [YOU SAY: *Vy ne moglí by govorít médlenno?*]
- [YOU ANSWER: at a café the waiter asks *Shto vy khotítye?* Answer politely with *ya by khotél* or *ya by khotéla*]

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[RU-GRAMMAR-BY-POLITE-01, RU-GRAMMAR-BY-PAST-FORM-01] -->

[PAUSE 3s] "I'd like a coffee", a woman? (***Я бы хотела кофе.***) Which
verb form goes with *бы*? (**The -л form**, but it is not past.)
