## You'll want to know: مسیریاب
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C449-MASIRYAB-01]; assesses=[] -->

**مسیریاب** (*masiryâb*) — "a satnav, a GPS; a route-finding app".

> بدون مسیریاب در این شهر گم میشویم.
>
> *Bedun-e masiryâb dar in shahr gom mishavim.*
>
> Without a satnav we get lost in this town.

*masir* 'route' + *yâb* 'finding', from *yâftan*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*masiryâb* is a modern compound of *masir* 'route', a loan usually traced to Arabic *masīr*, and native *yâb*, the present stem of *yâftan* 'to find'.
