Bengali, chapter 398: Currents and Beaches.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
স্রোত (srot) — a current; a stream.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a bank (of a pond or river), then the Bengali for to moor.

You'll want to know: স্রোত.
স্রোত — srot — "a current; a stream".
নদীর স্রোতে নৌকাটা দ্রুত চলল। nôdīr srote noukāṭā drutô chôllô. The boat moved fast on the river's current.
Also a flow of people or things: মানুষের স্রোত (srot), a stream of people.

The word, taken apart.
স্রোত (srot) is Sanskrit srotas, "the current or bed of a river, a stream" (Monier-Williams), from sru, to flow.

Lesson 2 of 5.
সৈকত (soikôt) — a beach.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for to moor, then the Bengali for a current.

You'll want to know: সৈকত.
সৈকত — soikôt — "a beach".
সন্ধ্যায় আমরা সৈকতে হাঁটলাম। sôndhyāy āmrā soikôte hā̃ṭlām. In the evening we walked on the beach.
The first vowel sign is ৈ, the vowel oi; in talk people also say সমুদ্রের ধার.

The word, taken apart.
সৈকত (soikôt) is Sanskrit saikata, "sandy, made of sand" (Monier-Williams), from sikatā, sand: a beach is the sandy place.

Lesson 4 of 5.
ডুব দেওয়া (ḍub deoyā) — to take a dip; to dive under.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a beach, then the Bengali for a jetty.

You'll want to know: ডুব দেওয়া.
ডুব দেওয়া — ḍub deoyā — "to take a dip; to dive under".
গরমে অরুণ পুকুরে ডুব দিল। gôrôme ôrun pukure ḍub dilô. In the heat Arun took a dip in the pond.
Past (he/she …): তিনি পুকুরে ডুব দিলেন, tini pukure ḍub dilen.
ডুব is a plunge under water; ডোবা, to sink, is the same root.

The word, taken apart.
ডুব দেওয়া (ḍub deoyā) joins ডুব, "a plunge under water", from the reconstructed ḍubb, "sink", under which CDIAL lists Bengali ḍubā, "to sink" (CDIAL 5561), to দেওয়া, to give.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ছিটানো (chhiṭāno) — to splash; to sprinkle.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a jetty, then the Bengali for to take a dip.

You'll want to know: ছিটানো.
ছিটানো — chhiṭāno — "to splash; to sprinkle".
শিশুরা সৈকতে জল ছিটাল। shishurā soikôte jôl chhiṭālô. The children splashed water on the beach.
Past (he/she …): তিনি জল ছিটালেন, tini jôl chhiṭālen.

The word, taken apart.
ছিটানো (chhiṭāno) is inherited: CDIAL lists Bengali chiṭā, "to scatter, to sprinkle", under the reconstructed chiṭ, "a sudden movement, a splash" (CDIAL 5035).
