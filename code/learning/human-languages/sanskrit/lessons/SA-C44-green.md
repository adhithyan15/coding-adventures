## You'll want to know: हरितः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C44-COLOUR-04]; assesses=[] -->

**हरितः** (*haritaḥ*) — "green".

**हरितः** is green — and before it settled it covered yellow-green and gold as well.

English *yellow* and *gold* are its cousins, from **\*ǵhel-**, a root that runs across the whole band from green to yellow. One family, one word, two colours pulled apart by different daughters.

तृणम् is **हरितम्**, and so is a young पत्रम् on a तरुः.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

Green, from a root that could not decide between green and gold.
