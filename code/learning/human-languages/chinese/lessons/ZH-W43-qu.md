## Script — a cross on a bar, and a corner below
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-SCRIPT-QU-01]; assesses=[] -->

> 去

**Two pieces, top before bottom.**

**The top piece, three strokes.** A short horizontal, a vertical through it, then a long horizontal under them: 十 standing on a wide base.

**The bottom piece, two strokes.** A stroke that falls to the left and turns out to the right, then a dot inside the turn.

3 + 2 = five.
