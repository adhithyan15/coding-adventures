## You'll want to know: сбережения
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C395-SBEREZHENIYA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**сбережения** (*sberezhéniya*) — "savings".

> Все сбережения он потратил на учёбу.
>
> *Vse sberezhéniya on potrátil na uchyóbu.*
>
> He spent all his savings on his studies.

Plural. From *сберегать*, to save; *беречь* has the same root.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**сбережения** is built on *сберечь* "to save, to keep", from *беречь* "to protect", inherited from Proto-Slavic *\*berťi* (EtymDB).
