## You'll want to know: آوار
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C331-VIRAN-03]; assesses=[] -->

**آوار** (*âvâr*) — "rubble, debris".

> امدادگرها دو نفر را از زیر آوار بیرون آوردند.
>
> *Emdâdgar-hâ do nafar râ az zir-e âvâr birun âvardand.*
>
> The rescue workers pulled two people out from under the rubble.

*Zir-e âvâr* "under the rubble".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*âvâr* "rubble" is a Persian word whose origin is not settled; EtymDB lists no entry.
