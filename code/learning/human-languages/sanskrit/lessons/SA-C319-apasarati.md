## You'll want to know: अपसरति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C319-VIJAYA319-04]; assesses=[] -->

**अपसरति** — *apasarati* — "retreats; moves away".

> शत्रोः सेना शीघ्रम् अपसरति।
>
> *śatroḥ senā śīghram apasarati.*
>
> The enemy's army quickly retreats.

Past (it …): सेना शीघ्रम् अपसृतवती, *senā śīghram apasṛtavatī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अपसरति** (*apasarati*) is built from *apa*, "away", and the root √sṛ, "to go, run"; MW gives *apa-sṛ* as to slip off from, go away, retreat (MW).
