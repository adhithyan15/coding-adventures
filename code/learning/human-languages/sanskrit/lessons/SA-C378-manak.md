## You'll want to know: मनाक्
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C378-LESHA378-02]; assesses=[] -->

**मनाक्** — *manāk* — "a little; in a small degree".

> यदि सः मनाक् अपि यतते, तर्हि सफलतां प्राप्नोति।
>
> *yadi saḥ manāk api yatate, tarhi saphalatāṃ prāpnoti.*
>
> If he tries even a little, he succeeds.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**मनाक्** (*manāk*) MW says *manāk* probably comes from *manā* and the root √añc ("perceivably"), and gives "a little, slightly, in a small degree" (MW).
