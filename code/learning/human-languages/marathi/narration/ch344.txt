Marathi, chapter 344: Agreeing and Disagreeing.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
मतभेद (matbhed) — a disagreement.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to put forward, then the Marathi for proof.

You'll want to know: मतभेद.
मतभेद — matbhed — "a disagreement".
भावांमध्ये पैशांवरून मतभेद झाले. bhāvāmmadhye paiśāṁvarūn matbhed jhāle. The brothers disagreed over money.
Masculine.
Mostly plural: मतभेद (matbhed) होणे, to disagree; मत + भेद, opinion + split.

The word, taken apart.
मतभेद (matbhed) is built from two tatsamas, Sanskrit mata, "thought, opinion", and bheda, "splitting, difference" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 2 of 5.
सहमत होणे (sahmat hoṇe) — to agree.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for proof, then the Marathi for a disagreement.

You'll want to know: सहमत होणे.
सहमत होणे — sahmat hoṇe — "to agree".
मी तुमच्या मताशी सहमत आहे. mī tumacyā matāśī sahmat āhe. I agree with your view.
Past (he …): तो सहमत झाला, to sahmat jhālā.
The person or view agreed with takes -शी: तुमच्याशी सहमत.

The word, taken apart.
सहमत होणे (sahmat hoṇe) joins सहमत, built from Sanskrit saha, "together", and mata, "opinion" (Monier-Williams), to होणे, "to become".

Lesson 3 of 5.
आक्षेप घेणे (ākṣep gheṇe) — to object.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a disagreement, then the Marathi for to agree.

You'll want to know: आक्षेप घेणे.
आक्षेप घेणे — ākṣep gheṇe — "to object".
अरुणने या योजनेवर आक्षेप घेतला. aruṇne yā yojnevar ākṣep ghetlā. Arun objected to this plan.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने आक्षेप घेतला, tyāne ākṣep ghetlā.
What is objected to takes -वर: योजनेवर आक्षेप घेणे (ākṣep gheṇe).

The word, taken apart.
आक्षेप घेणे (ākṣep gheṇe) joins the tatsama आक्षेप, Sanskrit ākṣepa, "throwing at; objection" (Monier-Williams), to घेणे, "to take".

Lesson 4 of 5.
विसंगती (visaṅgtī) — an inconsistency.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to agree, then the Marathi for to object.

You'll want to know: विसंगती.
विसंगती — visaṅgtī — "an inconsistency".
त्याच्या दोन उत्तरांत विसंगती होती. tyācyā don uttarānt visaṅgtī hotī. There was an inconsistency between his two answers.
Feminine.
Feminine; the adjective is विसंगत, inconsistent.

The word, taken apart.
विसंगती (visaṅgtī) is a learned word built from Sanskrit vi-, "apart", and saṁgati, "coming together" (Monier-Williams): things that do not fit together.

The word, taken apart.
दृष्टिकोन (dṛṣṭikon) is a learned compound built from Sanskrit dṛṣṭi, "sight, view", and koṇa, "corner, angle" (Monier-Williams).
