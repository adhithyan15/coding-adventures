# ಮಂಜುಗಡ್ಡೆ (mañjugaḍḍe) — ice; a block of ice

## You'll want to know: ಮಂಜುಗಡ್ಡೆ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C489-IBBANI489-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ಮಂಜುಗಡ್ಡೆ** — *mañjugaḍḍe* — "ice; a block of ice".

> ಚಳಿಗಾಲದಲ್ಲಿ ಪರ್ವತದ ಮೇಲೆ ಮಂಜುಗಡ್ಡೆ ಇರುತ್ತದೆ.
>
> *caḷigāladalli parvatada mēle mañjugaḍḍe iruttade.*
>
> In winter there is ice on the mountain.

ಮಂಜು, mist or frost, plus ಗಡ್ಡೆ, a lump.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಮಂಜುಗಡ್ಡೆ** (*mañjugaḍḍe*) is a native Kannada compound of ಮಂಜು, "dew, fog, coldness" (DEDR 4641), and *gaḍḍe*, "a mass, a lump" (DEDR 1148).
