## You'll want to know: l'hématome
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C299-CHUTE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**l'hématome** — "the haematoma, the large bruise".

> Elle a un gros hématome sur la jambe.
>
> *She has a big bruise on her leg.*

Masculine: *un hématome*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*hématome* is a learned medical word built on Greek *haîma* "blood" with the suffix *-ōma* (EtymDB lists no parent).
