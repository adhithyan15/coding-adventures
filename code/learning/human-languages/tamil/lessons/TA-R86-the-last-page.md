## What you've built, and what this page does with it
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[TA-LEX-C01-PRACTICE-01] -->

Everything here has come round twice in your reading: once where it was taught,
once on a retrieval page in this last stretch. A second pass is where memory
starts holding without the book, and the widest gap on offer runs from the
opening pages to this one.

## Script: three pieces, one of them yours to draw
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[TA-SCRIPT-RECOG-125, TA-SCRIPT-DRIZZLE-09, TA-SCRIPT-RECOG-124, TA-SCRIPT-OO-SIGN-01, TA-SCRIPT-O-VOWEL-01] -->

**உ** standing · **ி** hanging · **ய** a consonant · **ஒ** standing o · **ோ**
the sign built from two marks.

- [YOU READ: **உ**, and say whether it is a letter or a sign]
- [YOU READ: **ி**, and say what it replaces]
- [YOU READ: **ஒ** and **ோ** — the same vowel standing, then hanging]

## Reading: five words, cold
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[TA-SCRIPT-WRITE-SARI-02, TA-SCRIPT-READ-PEYAR-03, TA-SCRIPT-READ-QUESTION-02, TA-SCRIPT-READ-PO-02, TA-SCRIPT-READ-ONRU-02] -->

**சரி** · **பெயர்** · **இருக்கிறீர்கள்** · **போ** · **ஒன்று**

No glosses this time. Read each one and say what it means before moving on. The
long one in the middle is the test: eight pieces, and you have met every piece.

## The exchange — five of them, unprompted
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[TA-LEX-C01-PRACTICE-01, TA-LEX-C02-PRACTICE-01, TA-LEX-C03-PRACTICE-01, TA-LEX-C04-PRACTICE-01, TA-LEX-C05-PRACTICE-01] -->

The book opened with five, and an examiner opens with the same ones:

1. greet, and answer a thank-you
2. trade names, both sides, closing warmly
3. ask after the person, and reply in full
4. take your leave, promising the next meeting
5. say what you speak, and what you do
