German, chapter 383: Bei der Behörde.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
die Behörde — the authority, the public office.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to break, then the German for the violation.

You'll want to know: die Behörde.
die Behörde — "the authority, the public office".
Für den neuen Ausweis muss ich zur Behörde gehen. For the new identity card I have to go to the authorities.
Plural: die Behörden. In Switzerland people often say das Amt.

The word, taken apart.
Behörde is usually traced to an old verb behören "to belong to", of the family of hören "to hear" and gehören "to belong": the office a matter belongs to. EtymDB has no history for it, so the account is hedged.

Lesson 2 of 5.
der Antrag — the application; the motion.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for the violation, then the German for the authority.

You'll want to know: der Antrag.
der Antrag — "the application; the motion".
Ich habe einen Antrag auf Wohngeld gestellt. I have applied for housing benefit.
Plural: die Anträge. Einen Antrag stellen — to make an application. Der Heiratsantrag — the marriage proposal.

The word, taken apart.
Antrag is inherited from Middle High German antrac (EtymDB), of the family of antragen "to offer", built on tragen "to carry": you carry a request to the office.

Lesson 3 of 5.
beantragen — to apply for.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for the authority, then the German for the application.

You'll want to know: beantragen.
beantragen — "to apply for".
Sie hat ein Visum beantragt. She has applied for a visa.
Inseparable: sie beantragt, sie hat beantragt. It takes a direct object, with no für.

The word, taken apart.
beantragen is built with be- on Antrag "application", from Middle High German antrac (EtymDB), of the family of tragen "to carry".

Lesson 4 of 5.
das Formular — the form (to fill in).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for the application, then the German for to apply for.

You'll want to know: das Formular.
das Formular — "the form (to fill in)".
Das Formular hat zwölf Seiten. The form has twelve pages.
Plural: die Formulare. Ein Formular ausfüllen — to fill in a form.

The word, taken apart.
Formular is usually traced to Latin formulārius, which Lewis & Short give for a lawyer who drew up legal forms, from formula "a small form, a pattern", the diminutive of forma "shape" (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 5 of 5.
die Unterschrift — the signature.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to apply for, then the German for the form.

You'll want to know: die Unterschrift.
die Unterschrift — "the signature".
Auf dem Formular fehlt noch Ihre Unterschrift. Your signature is still missing on the form.
Plural: die Unterschriften. From unterschreiben — to sign.

The word, taken apart.
Unterschrift joins unter "under" to Schrift "writing", from Old High German scrift and Latin scrīptum (EtymDB): the name written under the text.
