## You'll want to know: そうじ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C160-DIRT160-01]; assesses=[] -->

**そうじ** — *sōji* — "cleaning; sweeping".

> **そうじは どようびです。** — *sōji wa doyōbi desu* — "The cleaning is on Saturdays."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**そうじ** (*sōji*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *sō*, "sweep" (Middle Chinese *sawX*) and *ji*, "remove" (*drjo*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
