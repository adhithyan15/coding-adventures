## You'll want to know: कान्तारः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C404-PRANTARA404-02]; assesses=[] -->

**कान्तारः** — *kāntāraḥ* — "a wilderness; a forest track".

> कान्तारे सिंहाः वृकाः च वसन्ति।
>
> *kāntāre siṃhāḥ vṛkāḥ ca vasanti.*
>
> Lions and wolves live in the wilderness.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**कान्तारः** (*kāntāraḥ*) is the word MW gives for a large wood or wilderness and for a difficult road through a forest; MW gives no derivation, so its origin is uncertain (MW).
