## You'll want to know: ひきわけ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C462-CONTEST462-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ひきわけ** — *hikiwake* — "a tie (in a game)".

> **しあいは ひきわけでした。** — *shiai wa hikiwake deshita* — "The match was a tie."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ひきわけ** (*hikiwake*) is a native Japanese compound (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese: *hiki*, "pulling (hiku)" and *wake*, "dividing (wakeru)" (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
