Malayalam, chapter 116: How You Feel Inside.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
സങ്കടം (saṅkaṭaṁ) — sadness.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for dizziness, then the Malayalam for not well.

You'll want to know: സങ്കടം.
സങ്കടം (saṅkaṭaṁ) — "sadness, sorrow". എനിക്ക് സങ്കടമുണ്ട് — "I am sad".

The word, taken apart.
സങ്കടം (saṅkaṭaṁ) from Sanskrit saṅkaṭa, "narrow; a strait, distress", taken over whole as a tatsama, with Malayalam's noun ending -ṁ. A tight place became a sorrow.

What you've built.
A feeling, said with to-me.

The word, taken apart.
ദേഷ്യം (dēṣyaṁ) is Sanskrit dvēṣya, "hateful", from dvēṣa, "hatred" (CDIAL 6696), reshaped: the dv- lost its v, and hatred softened to anger.
