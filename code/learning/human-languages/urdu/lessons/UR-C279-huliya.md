## You'll want to know: حلیہ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C279-GAVAHI279-02]; assesses=[] -->

**حلیہ** — *huliyā* — "a person's description; someone's looks".

> گواہ نے چور کا حلیہ بتایا۔
>
> *gavāh ne chor kā huliyā batāyā.*
>
> The witness described what the thief looked like.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *ḥilya*, "an adornment, the look of a person". The police ask for the **حلیہ**: height, face, clothes.
