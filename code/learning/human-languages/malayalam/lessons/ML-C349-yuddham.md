# യുദ്ധം (yuddhaṁ) — a war; a battle

## You'll want to know: യുദ്ധം
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C349-VARTTA349-02]; assesses=[] -->

**യുദ്ധം** — *yuddhaṁ* — "a war; a battle".

> ആ യുദ്ധം വളരെ കാലം മുൻപ് നടന്നു.
>
> *ā yuddhaṁ vaḷare kālaṁ munpŭ naṭannu.*
>
> That war took place a very long time ago.

With ചെയ്യുക it makes a verb: യുദ്ധം ചെയ്യുക, to fight a war.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**യുദ്ധം** (*yuddhaṁ*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *yuddha*, "fought", and as a noun "a battle", the past participle of the root *yudh*, "to fight" (CDIAL 10499). Like many Sanskrit nouns in -a, it ends in -ം in Malayalam.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
