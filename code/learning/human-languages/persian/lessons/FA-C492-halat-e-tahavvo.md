## You'll want to know: حالت تهوع
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C492-UNWELL-01]; assesses=[] -->

**حالت تهوع** (*hâlat-e tahavvo'*) — "nausea".

> در ماشین حالت تهوع میگیرم.
>
> *dar mâshin hâlat-e tahavvo' migiram.*
>
> I feel sick in the car.

*hâlat* 'state' + *tahavvo'* 'retching'. *hâlat-e tahavvo' dâshtan* — to feel sick.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*hâlat-e tahavvo'* is built from two Arabic loans: *hâlat*, from Arabic *ḥāla*, 'state' (EtymDB), and *tahavvo'*, usually traced to Arabic *tahawwuʿ*, 'retching'.
