# ఏకీభవించు (ēkībhaviñcu) — to agree with

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[TE-LEX-C385-ANGIKARAM385-01, TE-LEX-C385-ANGIKARAM385-02] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Telugu for to accept, then the Telugu for consent.

## You'll want to know: ఏకీభవించు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C385-ANGIKARAM385-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ఏకీభవించు** — *ēkībhaviñcu* — "to agree with".

> నేను మీ అభిప్రాయంతో ఏకీభవిస్తాను.
>
> *nēnu mī abhiprāyantō ēkībhavistānu.*
>
> I agree with your opinion.

Past (I …): ఏకీభవించాను, *ēkībhaviñcānu*.

The person or view you agree with takes -తో: మీతో ఏకీభవిస్తాను.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ఏకీభవించు** (*ēkībhaviñcu*) is built on Sanskrit *ekībhāva*, "the becoming one, coalition" (Monier-Williams), from *eka*, "one", and *bhāva*, "becoming", with the Telugu verb ending -ఇంచు.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
