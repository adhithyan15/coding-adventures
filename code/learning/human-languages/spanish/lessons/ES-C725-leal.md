## You'll want to know: leal
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C725-FIEL-02]; assesses=[] -->

**leal** — "loyal".

> Siempre ha sido leal a sus amigos.
>
> *He has always been loyal to his friends.*

*Leal a* — loyal to.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*leal* is usually traced to Latin *lēgālis* "of the law", built on *lēx* "law"; English *loyal* and *legal* are the same Latin word by two roads.
