## You'll want to know: पेंशन
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C504-BUDHAPA504-03]; assesses=[] -->

**पेंशन** — *peṁśan* — "a pension".

> नाना को सरकार से पेंशन मिलती है।
>
> *nānā ko sarkār se peṁśan miltī hai.*
>
> Grandfather gets a pension from the government.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पेंशन** (*peṁśan*) is borrowed from English *pension*.
