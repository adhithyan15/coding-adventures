## You'll want to know: みおくる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C290-UTSURU290-04]; assesses=[] -->

**みおくる** — *miokuru* — "to see (someone) off".

> **えきで ともだちを みおくりました。** — *eki de tomodachi o miokurimashita* — "I saw my friend off at the station."

Polite **みおくります**, *miokurimasu*; a godan verb. Past (polite): **かぞくが あにを みおくりました。**, *kazoku ga ani o miokurimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**みおくる** (*miokuru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, built from *mi*, "seeing" (from *miru*), and *okuru*, "to send" (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
