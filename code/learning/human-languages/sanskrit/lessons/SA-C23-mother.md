## You'll want to know: माता
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-MOTHER]; assesses=[] -->

**माता** (*mātā*) — "mother".

The stem is **मातृ** (*mātṛ-*) — the same *-ṛ* family as पिता, and the same long reach: Latin *māter*, Greek *mētēr*, Old English *mōdor*, English *mother*, Russian *mat*. Put the father-word into the dual and Sanskrit gives you *pitarau*, 'the two parents', with the mother folded silently inside it.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

Two of the four, and one ending family carrying both.
