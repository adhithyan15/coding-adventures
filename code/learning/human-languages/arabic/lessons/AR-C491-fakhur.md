# فخور (fakhūr) — proud

## You'll want to know: فخور
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C491-FAKHR491-01]; assesses=[] -->

**فخور** — *fakhūr* — "proud".

> الأم فخورة بابنها الطبيب.
>
> *al-umm fakhūra bi-ibnihā aṭ-ṭabīb.*
>
> The mother is proud of her son the doctor.

It takes بـ: فخور بـ, proud of.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**فخور** (*fakhūr*) is built on the native Arabic root *f-kh-r*, "to boast, to glory in" (Lane). Fakhūr is a pattern for a lasting trait: one who takes pride.
