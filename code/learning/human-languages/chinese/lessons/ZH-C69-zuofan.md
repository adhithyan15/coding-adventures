## You'll want to know: 做饭
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C69-MEALS69-01]; assesses=[] -->

**做饭** — *zuòfàn* — "to cook".

> **我妈妈喜欢做饭** *wǒ māma xǐhuan zuòfàn* — My mum loves cooking.

The new character, in pieces: 做 — 亻 on the left, 十 over 口 in the middle, and on the right a four-stroke piece like a hand striking. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**做饭** (*zuòfàn*) is built from 做 'to do, to make' and 饭 'meal'. 饭 is inherited from Middle Chinese *bjonH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription); 做 has no reading in any dictionary we use, so its history is not settled.
