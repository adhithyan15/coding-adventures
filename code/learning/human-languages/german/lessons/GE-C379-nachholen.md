## You'll want to know: nachholen
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C379-BEWERTUNG-04]; assesses=[] -->

**nachholen** — "to make up (for), to catch up on".

> Er war krank und muss die Prüfung nächste Woche nachholen.
>
> *He was ill and has to make up the exam next week.*

Separable: *er holt nach*, *er hat nachgeholt*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*nachholen* is built from *nach* "after" and *holen* "to fetch", from Middle High German *holen* (EtymDB): you fetch what you missed afterwards.
