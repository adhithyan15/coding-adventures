## You'll want to know: улов
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C505-FISHING-05]; assesses=[] -->

**улов** (*ulóv*) — "a catch (of fish)".

> Рыбаки были довольны уловом.
>
> *Rybáki býli dovólny ulóvom.*
>
> The fishermen were pleased with their catch.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*улов* is built on *у-* and the root of *ловить* 'to catch'; EtymDB has no entry.
