Urdu, chapter 188: April, May, June, July, August.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
اپریل (aprail) — April.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for exercise, then the Urdu for the body.

You'll want to know: اپریل.
اپریل — aprail — "April".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: aprail]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: aprail, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say aprail]

[your turn — recall: say the Urdu for exercise, then the Urdu for the body, then say aprail again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does اپریل (aprail) mean? ("April".) Say it once more.

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
To be shy? (شرمانا, sharmānā.) To quarrel? (جھگڑنا, jhagaṛnā.) To celebrate; to win round? (منانا, manānā.) To mix; to introduce? (ملانا, milānā.) To join? (جوڑنا, joṛnā.) To spend? (خرچ کرنا, kharch karnā.) To pay off? (چکانا, chukānā.) To measure? (ناپنا, nāpnā.) To weigh? (تولنا, tolnā.) To check? (جانچنا, jāṅchnā.) To get tired? (تھکنا, thaknā.) To rest? (آرام کرنا, ārām karnā.) To lie down? (لیٹنا, leṭnā.) To stand up? (کھڑا ہونا, khaṛā honā.) To wait? (انتظار کرنا, intizār karnā.) To pay a visit? (ملاقات کرنا, mulāqāt karnā.) To marry? (شادی کرنا, shādī karnā.) To be alive? (جینا, jīnā.) To die? (مرنا, marnā.) To increase? (بڑھانا, baṛhānā.)
