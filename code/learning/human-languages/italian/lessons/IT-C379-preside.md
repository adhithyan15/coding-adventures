## You'll want to know: il preside
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C379-LICEO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**il preside** — "the head teacher, the principal".

> Il preside ha chiamato i miei genitori.
>
> *The head teacher called my parents.*

A woman is *la preside*. The official title today is *dirigente scolastico*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*preside* is a learned borrowing from Latin *praeses, praesidis* "a guardian, a governor" (Lewis & Short), from *praesidēre* "to sit in front", built on *sedēre* "to sit".
