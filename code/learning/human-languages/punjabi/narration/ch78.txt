Punjabi, chapter 78: Bird, Mouse, Lion, Elephant, Monkey.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ਪੰਛੀ (panchhī) — a bird.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for a goat, then the Punjabi for a sheep.

You'll want to know: ਪੰਛੀ.
ਪੰਛੀ — panchhī — "a bird".
