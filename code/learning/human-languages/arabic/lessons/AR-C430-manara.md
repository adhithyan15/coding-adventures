## You'll want to know: منارة
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C430-MARFA430-03]; assesses=[] -->

**منارة** — *manāra* — "a lighthouse; a minaret".

> رأينا ضوء المنارة من بعيد.
>
> *raʾaynā ḍawʾ al-manāra min baʿīd.*
>
> We saw the lighthouse's light from far away.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**منارة** (*manāra*) is built on the native Arabic root *n-w-r*, "light" (Lane). It names a place of light; EtymDB lists Aramaic and Hebrew relatives for a lamp-stand.
