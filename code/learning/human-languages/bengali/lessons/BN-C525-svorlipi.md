## You'll want to know: স্বরলিপি
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C525-SUR525-03]; assesses=[] -->

**স্বরলিপি** — *svôrlipi* — "musical notation".

> মেয়েটা খাতায় গানের স্বরলিপি লিখল।
>
> *Meyeṭā khātāy gāner svôrlipi likhlô.*
>
> The girl wrote down the song's notation in a notebook.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**স্বরলিপি** (*svôrlipi*) joins Sanskrit *svara*, "a note", and *lipi*, "writing, a script" (Monier-Williams): music written down.
