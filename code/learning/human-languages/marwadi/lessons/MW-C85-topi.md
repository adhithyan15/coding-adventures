## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**टोपी** (*ṭopī*) is a tadbhava, a word inherited and reshaped by sound change. It comes from *\*ṭoppa*, "hat, covering", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants. CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry (CDIAL 5481); the match is through Hindi *ṭopī* and Gujarati *ṭopī*. CDIAL records a proposal that it comes from Munda; it is not settled.
