# भवितुम् इच्छामि (bhavitum icchāmi) — what I want to become

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[SA-GRAMMAR-C282-FEELING-01, SA-LEX-ICCHATI-WANT-01, SA-GRAMMAR-INFINITIVE-TUM-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "I am pleased". (*ahaṃ prasannaḥ asmi*.) Then "I want to go". (*ahaṃ gantum icchāmi*.) Now say what you want to become.

## You'll want to know: भवितुम् इच्छामि
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-GRAMMAR-C282-AMBITION-01]; assesses=[SA-LEX-ICCHATI-WANT-01, SA-LEX-C26-ROLE-05, SA-LEX-C83-SCHOOL-03, SA-LEX-C16-PLACE-05] -->

> **अहं वैद्यः भवितुम् इच्छामि।**
>
> *ahaṃ vaidyaḥ bhavitum icchāmi.* — "I want to become a doctor."

**भवितुम्** (*bhavitum*) is the *-tum* form of **भवति**: "to become". What you become stays in the same case as you: **वैद्यः**, like **अहम्**.

> **सः अध्यापकः भवितुम् इच्छति।**
>
> *saḥ adhyāpakaḥ bhavitum icchati.* — "He wants to become a teacher."

> **अहं नगरं गन्तुम् इच्छामि।**
>
> *ahaṃ nagaraṃ gantum icchāmi.* — "I want to go to the city."

## You'll want to know: मम स्वप्नः अस्ति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-GRAMMAR-C282-AMBITION-02]; assesses=[SA-LEX-C212-WEL212-04, SA-LEX-C241-WHY241-04, SA-LEX-C139-FUT139-04] -->

**मम स्वप्नः अस्ति** (*mama svapnaḥ asti*), "my dream is", opens a hope; **यत्** gives what it is:

> **मम स्वप्नः अस्ति,**
>
> *mama svapnaḥ asti,*
>
> **यत् अहं वैद्यः भविष्यामि।**
>
> *yat ahaṃ vaidyaḥ bhaviṣyāmi.* — "My dream is that I will become a doctor."

> **मीरायाः स्वप्नः अस्ति,**
>
> *mīrāyāḥ svapnaḥ asti,*
>
> **यत् सा नगरं गमिष्यति।**
>
> *yat sā nagaraṃ gamiṣyati.* — "Mira's dream is that she will go to the city."

**मीरायाः** (*mīrāyāḥ*) is "Mira's".
