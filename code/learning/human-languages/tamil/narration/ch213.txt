Tamil, chapter 213: Cupboard, Electricity, Rubbish, Toothpaste, Toothbrush.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
அலமாரி (alamāri) — a cupboard.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for well-behaved, then the Tamil for ugly.

You'll want to know: அலமாரி.
அலமாரி — alamāri — "a cupboard".

The word, taken apart.
அலமாரி (alamāri) is a loan from Portuguese armário, "cupboard".

மின்சாரம் (miṉcāram) is a modern coinage: native miṉ, "lightning" (DEDR 4876), and cāram, "essence, strength", from Sanskrit sāra (CDIAL 13355).
