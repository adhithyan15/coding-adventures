## You'll want to know: o pódio
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C511-TORNEIO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**o pódio** — "podium".

> Três atletas subiram ao pódio.
>
> *Three athletes went up on the podium.*

*Subir ao pódio* — to finish in the top three.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*pódio* is a learned borrowing from Latin *podium* (EtymDB), from Greek *pódion* "a base", built on *poús* "foot".
