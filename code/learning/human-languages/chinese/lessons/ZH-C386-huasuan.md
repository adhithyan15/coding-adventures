## You'll want to know: huásuàn
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C386-CHANGE386-04]; assesses=[] -->

*huásuàn* — "good value; worth it".

> *wǒ juéde èrshǒu chē bǐ xīn chē huásuàn* — I think a second-hand car is better value than a new one.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*huásuàn* is built from *huá* and *suàn* 'to count'. The second is inherited from Middle Chinese *swanX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'the number of things'; the dictionary glosses the first 'to row a boat', and how it came into this word is not settled.
