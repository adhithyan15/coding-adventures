## You'll want to know: zhíwèi
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C489-STAFF489-04]; assesses=[] -->

*zhíwèi* — "a position in a firm".

> *tā xiǎng yào jīnglǐ de zhíwèi* — He wants the manager's job.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*zhíwèi* is built from 职 'a post' and *wèi* 'a place', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *tsyik* and *hwijH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'a rank in a row'.
