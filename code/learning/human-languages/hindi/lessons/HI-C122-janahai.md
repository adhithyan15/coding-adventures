# जाना है (jānā hai) — (I) have to go

## You'll want to know: जाना है
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C122-LATE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**जाना है** (*jānā hai*) — "have to go".

**मुझे जाना है** — "I have to go", with the person in **मुझे**. **जाना** is "to go".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

A holiday, it's late, take it easy, leave to go, I have to go.
