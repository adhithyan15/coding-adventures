# Three plans, no copied model

## Warm-up

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[SA-GRAMMAR-C263-FUTURE-FIRST-SINGULAR-02] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Keep the speaker ending in every answer: this round is all “I.”

## Guided Practice

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[SA-VERB-C263-GAMISYAMI-01, SA-GRAMMAR-C263-FUTURE-FIRST-SINGULAR-02, SA-VERB-C264-KARISYAMI-01, SA-VERB-C264-PATHISYAMI-02, SA-PATTERN-C264-NEAR-TERM-PLAN-03] -->

There is no Sanskrit model to copy. Build each answer from the English cue.

[PAUSE 3s each]
- [YOU SAY: tomorrow + I will go — *śvaḥ gamiṣyāmi*]
- [YOU SAY: tomorrow + I will read — *śvaḥ paṭhiṣyāmi*]
- [YOU SAY: day after tomorrow + I will do the work — *paraśvaḥ kāryaṃ kariṣyāmi*]

## Wrap-up Recall

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[SA-VERB-C263-GAMISYAMI-01, SA-VERB-C264-KARISYAMI-01, SA-VERB-C264-PATHISYAMI-02, SA-PATTERN-C264-NEAR-TERM-PLAN-03] -->

[PAUSE 3s] Give the three future forms only: I will go, I will do, I will
read. (*Gamiṣyāmi; kariṣyāmi; paṭhiṣyāmi.*)
