Marathi, chapter 272: Justice, Entitlement, Duty, Responsibility, Opportunity.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
न्याय (nyāy) — justice.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a veranda, then the Marathi for a broom.

You'll want to know: न्याय.
न्याय — nyāy — "justice".

The word, taken apart.
न्याय (nyāy) is Sanskrit nyāya, "rule, manner, method", taken over whole as a learned loan, a tatsama (CDIAL 7614). CDIAL lists the inherited Marathi form as nyāv, "justice".

Lesson 2 of 5.
हक्क (hakka) — an entitlement, a right.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a broom, then the Marathi for justice.

You'll want to know: हक्क.
हक्क — hakka — "an entitlement, a right".

The word, taken apart.
हक्क (hakka) is a loan from Arabic ḥaqq, "truth, a right", which reached Marathi through Persian. Its Arabic root is ḥ-q-q (Lane).

जबाबदारी — jabābdārī — "responsibility".

The word, taken apart.
जबाबदारी (jabābdārī) is jabāb, "answer", from Arabic jawāb, through Persian (Lane, root j-w-b), with Persian -dār, "holder", and -ī: "answer-holding".
