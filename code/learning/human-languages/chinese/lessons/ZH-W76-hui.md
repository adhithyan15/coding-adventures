[YOU READ: each one aloud, with nothing said first — *rén*, *jīn*, *qù*]

## Script — 人 over two bars and a bend
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-SCRIPT-HUI-01]; assesses=[] -->

> 会

**Three parts, top to bottom.**

**人, two strokes**, spread wide as a roof, as on 今.

**Two horizontals** under it, the lower one longer.

**A stroke that falls left and turns right** along the bottom, then **a dot**: the same two strokes that end 去.

2 + 2 + 2 = six.
