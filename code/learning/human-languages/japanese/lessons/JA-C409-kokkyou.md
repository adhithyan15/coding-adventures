## You'll want to know: こっきょう
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C409-CHIIKI409-03]; assesses=[] -->

**こっきょう** — *kokkyō* — "a national border".

> **くるまで こっきょうを こえました。** — *kuruma de kokkyō o koemashita* — "We crossed the border by car."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**こっきょう** (*kokkyō*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *koku*, "country" (Middle Chinese *kwok*) and *kyō*, "boundary" (*kjaengX*), joined as *kokkyō* (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
