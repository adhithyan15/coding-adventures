## You'll want to know: زورق
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C430-MARFA430-04]; assesses=[] -->

**زورق** — *zawraq* — "a dinghy; a small boat".

> خرج صياد في زورق قديم.
>
> *kharaja ṣayyād fī zawraq qadīm.*
>
> A fisherman went out in an old dinghy.

Plural: زوارق, *zawāriq*.

## The word, taken apart
