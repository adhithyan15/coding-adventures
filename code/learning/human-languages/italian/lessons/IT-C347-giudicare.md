## You'll want to know: giudicare
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C347-GIUDIZIO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**giudicare** — "to judge".

> Non giudicare le persone dai vestiti.
>
> *Do not judge people by their clothes.*

*Giudicare qualcuno da qualcosa* — to judge someone by something. A *giudice* judges in court.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*giudicare* is inherited from Latin *iūdicāre* "to judge, to give a ruling" (Lewis & Short), built on *iūdex* "a judge".
