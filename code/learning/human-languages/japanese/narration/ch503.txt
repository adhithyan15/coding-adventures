Lesson 1 of 6.
うたごえ (utagoe) — a singing voice; the sound of singing.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for an actress, then the Japanese for to play (a role).

You'll want to know: うたごえ.
うたごえ — utagoe — "a singing voice; the sound of singing".
となりの へやから うたごえが しました。 — tonari no heya kara utagoe ga shimashita — "There was singing from the next room."

The word, taken apart.
うたごえ (utagoe) is a native Japanese compound (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese: built from uta, "song" and goe, "voice" (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).

Lesson 2 of 6.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for to play (a role), then the Japanese for a singing voice.

You'll want to know: せんりつ.
せんりつ — senritsu — "a melody; a tune".
この うたの せんりつは うつくしいです。 — kono uta no senritsu wa utsukushii desu — "The melody of this song is beautiful."

The word, taken apart.
せんりつ (senritsu) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are sen, "rotation" (Middle Chinese zjwen) and ritsu, "rhythm" (lwit) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).

Lesson 5 of 6.
まう (mau) — to flutter; to dance (in the air).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for a musical note, then the Japanese for a musician.

You'll want to know: まう.
まう — mau — "to flutter; to dance (in the air)".
ゆきが かぜに まっています。 — yuki ga kaze ni matteimasu — "The snow is dancing in the wind."
Polite まいます, maimasu; a godan verb. Past (polite): ゆきが まいました。, yuki ga maimashita.

The word, taken apart.
まう (mau) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi, and its older origin is not settled (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
