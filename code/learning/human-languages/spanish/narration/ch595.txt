Spanish, chapter 595: Mi carrera.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
la carrera — the career; the degree; the race.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for to run, then the Spanish for to manage.

You'll want to know: la carrera.
la carrera — "the career; the degree; the race".
Tiene una larga carrera en la empresa. She has a long career in the company.
In Spain hacer una carrera is to study for a degree.

The word, taken apart.
carrera is usually traced to Late Latin (via) carraria 'cart road', built on carrus 'a wagon' (Lewis & Short); a road became a race and then a career.

You'll want to know: ascender.
ascender — "to be promoted; to rise".
Ascendió a directora en dos años. She was promoted to director in two years.
Stem change e becomes ie: asciendo. Ascender a + post.

The word, taken apart.
ascender is a learned borrowing from Latin ascendere 'to climb up', built from ad and scandere 'to climb' (EtymDB).
