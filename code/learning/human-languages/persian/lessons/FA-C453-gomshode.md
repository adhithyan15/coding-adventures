## You'll want to know: گمشده
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C453-PASGAH-02]; assesses=[] -->

**گمشده** (*gomshode*) — "lost, missing".

> کیف گمشده من را در تاکسی پیدا کردند.
>
> *Kif-e gomshode-ye man râ dar tâksi peydâ kardand.*
>
> They found my lost bag in the taxi.

*Ashyâ-ye gomshode* — lost property.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*gomshode* is built from native *gom* 'lost', which EtymDB lists, and *shode*, the past participle of *shodan* 'to become'.
