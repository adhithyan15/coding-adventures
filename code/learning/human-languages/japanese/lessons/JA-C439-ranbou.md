## You'll want to know: らんぼう
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C439-BRAVE439-04]; assesses=[] -->

**らんぼう** — *ranbō* — "rough; violent".

> **あには うんてんが らんぼうです。** — *ani wa unten ga ranbō desu* — "My elder brother drives roughly."

A na-adjective: な before a noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**らんぼう** (*ranbō*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *ran*, "disorder" (Middle Chinese *lwanH*) and *bō*, "violence" (*bawH*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
