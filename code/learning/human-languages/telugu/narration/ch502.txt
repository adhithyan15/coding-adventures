Telugu, chapter 502: Sighs and Letdowns.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
నిట్టూర్చు (niṭṭūrcu) — to sigh.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to impress, then the Telugu for to cheer on.

You'll want to know: నిట్టూర్చు.
నిట్టూర్చు — niṭṭūrcu — "to sigh".
ఫలితం చూసి అతను నిట్టూర్చాడు. phalitaṁ cūsi atanu niṭṭūrcāḍu. Seeing the result, he sighed.
Past (I …): నిట్టూర్చాను, niṭṭūrcānu.
The noun is నిట్టూర్పు, a sigh.

The word, taken apart.
నిట్టూర్చు (niṭṭūrcu) is native Dravidian: DEDR 3738 lists Telugu niṭṭ-ūrucu, "to sigh", and niṭṭ-ūrupu, "a sigh", in the article of Tamil neṭu, "long": a long breath.

Lesson 2 of 5.
నిరాశపడు (nirāśapaḍu) — to be disappointed.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to cheer on, then the Telugu for to sigh.

You'll want to know: నిరాశపడు.
నిరాశపడు — nirāśapaḍu — "to be disappointed".
వర్షం వల్ల ఆట రద్దు అయింది, పిల్లలు నిరాశపడ్డారు. varṣaṁ valla āṭa raddu ayindi, pillalu nirāśapaḍḍāru. The game was called off because of rain; the children were disappointed.
Past (I …): నిరాశపడ్డాను, nirāśapaḍḍānu.
నిరాశ, disappointment, + పడు. నిరాశపరచు, to disappoint someone.

The word, taken apart.
నిరాశపడు (nirāśapaḍu) is built from నిరాశ, from Sanskrit nirāśa, "without hope, despondent" (Monier-Williams) and the native verb paḍu, "to feel, to suffer" (DEDR 3853).

Lesson 3 of 5.
నిరుత్సాహం (nirutsāhaṁ) — discouragement.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to sigh, then the Telugu for to be disappointed.

You'll want to know: నిరుత్సాహం.
నిరుత్సాహం — nirutsāhaṁ — "discouragement".
మొదటిసారి గెలవకపోయినా ఆమెకు నిరుత్సాహం లేదు. modaṭisāri gelavakapōyinā āmeku nirutsāhaṁ lēdu. Though she did not win the first time, she was not discouraged.
The opposite of ఉత్సాహం, enthusiasm: నిర్-, without.

The word, taken apart.
నిరుత్సాహం (nirutsāhaṁ) is borrowed from Sanskrit nirutsāha, "lacking energy or heart" (Monier-Williams): nir-, "without", and utsāha, "power, firmness" (CDIAL 1882).

Lesson 4 of 5.
తహతహలాడు (tahatahalāḍu) — to yearn; to be restless for.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to be disappointed, then the Telugu for discouragement.

You'll want to know: తహతహలాడు.
తహతహలాడు — tahatahalāḍu — "to yearn; to be restless for".
అమ్మను చూడాలని పాప తహతహలాడింది. ammanu cūḍālani pāpa tahatahalāḍindi. The little girl longed to see her mum.
Past (I …): తహతహలాడాను, tahatahalāḍānu.
An echo word, తహతహ, + ఆడు. -ఆలని, wanting to.

The word, taken apart.
తహతహలాడు (tahatahalāḍu) is built from the doubled echo word తహతహ, "restless longing", and the verb āḍu, "to move" (DEDR 347). The origin of తహతహ is not settled.

Lesson 5 of 5.
కృశించు (kṛśiñcu) — to waste away; to pine.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for discouragement, then the Telugu for to yearn.

You'll want to know: కృశించు.
కృశించు — kṛśiñcu — "to waste away; to pine".
దిగులుతో ఆమె రోజురోజుకీ కృశించింది. digulutō āme rōjurōjukī kṛśiñcindi. From grief she wasted away day by day.
Past (I …): కృశించాను, kṛśiñcānu.
A formal word for growing thin from illness or sorrow.

The word, taken apart.
కృశించు (kṛśiñcu) is built on Sanskrit kṛśa, "lean, emaciated, weak" (Monier-Williams), with the Telugu verb ending -ఇంచు.
