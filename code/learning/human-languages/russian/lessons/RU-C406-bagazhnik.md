## You'll want to know: багажник
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C406-BAGAZHNIK-01]; assesses=[] -->

**багажник** (*bagázhnik*) — "a boot (of a car); a roof rack".

> Чемоданы лежат в багажнике.
>
> *Chemodány lezhát v bagázhnike.*
>
> The suitcases are in the boot.

Built on *багаж* — luggage. In American English, the trunk.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*багажник* is built on *багаж* 'luggage' with the ending *-ник*, the place for it. *Багаж* was borrowed from French *bagage* 'baggage, luggage' (EtymDB); EtymDB does not list *багажник* itself.
