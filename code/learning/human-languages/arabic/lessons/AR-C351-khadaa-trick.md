## You'll want to know: خدع
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C351-KHIDA351-01]; assesses=[] -->

**خدع** — *khadaʿa* — "to deceive, to trick".

> خدع الثعلب الغراب في الحكاية.
>
> *khadaʿa ath-thaʿlab al-ghurāb fī al-ḥikāya.*
>
> The fox tricked the crow in the tale.

Present: يخدع, *yakhdaʿ*.

A trick is خدعة.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**خدع** (*khadaʿa*) is built on the native Arabic root *kh-d-ʿ* (Lane), "to hide a thing; to deceive"; *khidʿa* is a ruse.
