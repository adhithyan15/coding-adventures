## You'll want to know: deshacer
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C668-EQUIPAJE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**deshacer** — "to undo; to unpack".

> Deshago la maleta al llegar.
>
> *I unpack my case when I arrive.*

Conjugated like *hacer*: *deshago*, *deshice*. *Deshacer la maleta* — to unpack.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*deshacer* is built on *hacer* "to make, to do" with *des-* "un-"; *hacer* is inherited from Latin *facere*.
