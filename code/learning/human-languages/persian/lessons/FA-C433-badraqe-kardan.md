## You'll want to know: بدرقه کردن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C433-SAK-03]; assesses=[] -->

**بدرقه کردن** (*badraqe kardan*) — "to see off (someone leaving)".

> مادرم ما را تا ایستگاه بدرقه کرد.
>
> *Mâdaram mâ râ tâ istgâh badraqe kard.*
>
> My mother saw us off at the station.

*badraqe* 'a send-off' + *kardan*. Iranian families often see travellers off with water poured behind them.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*badraqe kardan* is built from *badraqe* 'a send-off, an escort', whose origin is not settled, and *kardan* 'to do', inherited from Middle Persian *kardan* (EtymDB).
