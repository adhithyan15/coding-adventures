## You'll want to know: el síntoma
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C738-CONSULTA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**el síntoma** — "the symptom".

> ¿Qué síntomas tiene?
>
> *What symptoms do you have?*

Masculine, though it ends in *-a*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*síntoma* comes from Late Latin *symptōma*, from Greek *sýmptōma* "a happening, a symptom" (EtymDB).
