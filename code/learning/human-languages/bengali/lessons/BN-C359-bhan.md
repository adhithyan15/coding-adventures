## You'll want to know: ভান
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C359-OJUHAT359-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ভান** — *bhān* — "a pretence".

> অরুণ না শোনার ভান করল।
>
> *ôrun nā shonār bhān kôrlô.*
>
> Arun pretended not to hear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ভান** (*bhān*) is Sanskrit *bhāna*, appearance (Monier-Williams; CDIAL 9451 lists Bengali bhān). Bengali uses it for a false appearance: ভান করা, to pretend.
