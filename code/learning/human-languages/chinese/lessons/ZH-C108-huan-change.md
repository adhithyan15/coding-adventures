## You'll want to know: huàn
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C108-SHOP108-02]; assesses=[] -->

*huàn* — "to change; to exchange".

> *wǒ xiǎng huàn yí ge* — I'd like to change it for another one.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*huàn* is inherited from Middle Chinese *hwanH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'to exchange'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for asking for what I want.
