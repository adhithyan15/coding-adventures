## You'll want to know: ਘਟਣਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C290-WEEK290-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ਘਟਣਾ** — *ghaṭṇā* — "to go down; to decrease".

> ਸ਼ਾਮ ਨੂੰ ਗਰਮੀ ਘਟੇਗੀ।
>
> *shām nū̃ garmī ghaṭegī.*
>
> The heat will go down in the evening.

Past (it …): ਉਹ ਪਾਣੀ ਘਟਿਆ, *oh pāṇī ghaṭiā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਘਟਣਾ** (*ghaṭṇā*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*ghaṭṭati*, "decreases, is wanting"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *ghaṭṇā*, "to decrease", under it (CDIAL 4415).
