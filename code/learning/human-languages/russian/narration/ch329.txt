Russian, chapter 329: Boiling.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
кипятить (kipyatít) — to bring to the boil, to boil.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to grate, then the Russian for a grater.

You'll want to know: кипятить.
кипятить (kipyatít) — "to bring to the boil, to boil".
Бабушка кипятит молоко. Bábushka kipyatít molokó. Grandma is boiling milk.
Imperfective; perfective вскипятить (vskipyatít), кипячу. Кипяток — boiling water.

The word, taken apart.
кипятить (kipyatít) is built on кипеть (kipét) "to boil", inherited from Proto-Slavic kypěti (EtymDB): to make something boil.

Lesson 2 of 5.
кипеть (kipét) — to boil (of a liquid).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a grater, then the Russian for to bring to the boil.

You'll want to know: кипеть.
кипеть (kipét) — "to boil (of a liquid)".
Вода уже кипит. Vodá uzhé kipít. The water is already boiling.
Imperfective; perfective вскипеть (vskipét). Кипятить boils something; кипеть (kipét) is the liquid itself boiling.

The word, taken apart.
кипеть (kipét) is inherited from Proto-Slavic kypěti "to boil", from PIE kwep- "to smoke, to boil, to move violently" (EtymDB).

Lesson 3 of 5.
отваривать (otvárivat) — to cook in water, to boil (food).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to bring to the boil, then the Russian for to boil.

You'll want to know: отваривать.
отваривать (otvárivat) — "to cook in water, to boil (food)".
Я отварил картошку. Ya otvaríl kartóshku. I boiled the potatoes.
Imperfective; perfective отварить (otvarít). Варить — to cook, to boil in general.

The word, taken apart.
отваривать (otvárivat) is built on от- and варить "to boil", inherited from Proto-Slavic variti, which EtymDB traces to PIE wer- "to burn".

Lesson 4 of 5.
крышка (krýshka) — a lid.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to boil, then the Russian for to cook in water.

You'll want to know: крышка.
крышка (krýshka) — "a lid".
Крышка от кастрюли упала на пол. Krýshka ot kastryúli upála na pol. The saucepan lid fell on the floor.
Feminine; genitive plural крышек.

The word, taken apart.
крышка (krýshka) is built on крыть "to cover", an inherited Slavic verb, with the ending -шка: a little cover. EtymDB lists no parent.
