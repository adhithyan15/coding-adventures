# మొహమాటపడు (mohamāṭapaḍu) — to hold back politely; to be too polite to ask

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[TE-LEX-C498-SIGGU498-02, TE-LEX-C498-SIGGU498-03] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Telugu for humiliation, then the Telugu for to be proud.

## You'll want to know: మొహమాటపడు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C498-SIGGU498-04]; assesses=[] -->

**మొహమాటపడు** — *mohamāṭapaḍu* — "to hold back politely; to be too polite to ask".

> అతను మొహమాటపడి ఇంకా అన్నం అడగలేదు.
>
> *atanu mohamāṭapaḍi iṅkā annaṁ aḍagalēdu.*
>
> Out of politeness he did not ask for more rice.

Past (I …): మొహమాటపడ్డాను, *mohamāṭapaḍḍānu*.

The noun మొహమాటం is the polite reluctance to refuse, to ask or to say no.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**మొహమాటపడు** (*mohamāṭapaḍu*) is built from మొహమాటం, the polite reluctance to refuse or to ask, and the native verb *paḍu*, "to feel, to suffer" (DEDR 3853). The origin of మొహమాటం is not settled.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
