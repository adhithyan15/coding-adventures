## You'll want to know: la fisarmonica
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C516-STRUMENTI-04]; assesses=[] -->

**la fisarmonica** — "accordion".

> Alla festa un signore suonava la fisarmonica.
>
> *At the party a man was playing the accordion.*

The plural is *le fisarmoniche*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*fisarmonica* is a modern learned word built from Greek *phŷsa* "bellows, breath" and *armonica* "harmonica", from Greek *harmonía* "harmony" (EtymDB).
