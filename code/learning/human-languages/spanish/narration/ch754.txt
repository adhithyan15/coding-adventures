Spanish, chapter 754: Aficiones tranquilas.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
tejer — to knit, to weave.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for rhythm, then the Spanish for to compose.

You'll want to know: tejer.
tejer — "to knit, to weave".
Mi abuela teje bufandas. My grandmother knits scarves.
Spain also says hacer punto.

The word, taken apart.
tejer is inherited from Latin texere "to weave" (EtymDB); a text is words woven together.
