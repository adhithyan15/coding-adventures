Persian, chapter 58: Wolf, Big, Small, Fresh, Old.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
گرگ (gorg) — a wolf.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a butterfly, then the Persian for an ant.

You'll want to know: گرگ.
گرگ — gorg — "a wolf".

The word, taken apart.
gorg is a native Persian word, inherited rather than borrowed: it continues Middle Persian gurg, "wolf" (EtymDB). EtymDB takes it back to the Indo-European word behind English wolf.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for small, then the Persian for fresh.

You'll want to know: قدیمی.
قدیمی — ghadimi — "old".

The word, taken apart.
ghadimi is ghadim, "old, ancient", with the Persian ending -i. ghadim is borrowed from Arabic qadīm, "old, ancient" (root q-d-m, Lane).
