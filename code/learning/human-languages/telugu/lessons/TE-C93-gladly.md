## You'll want to know: సంతోషంగా
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C93-FINE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**సంతోషంగా** (*santōṣaṁgā*) — "happily, gladly".

It is **సంతోషం**, the "pleased" of your first introductions, with **-గా**. **సంతోషంగా** alone
answers a request: "gladly".
