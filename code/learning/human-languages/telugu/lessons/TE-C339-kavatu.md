# కవాతు (kavātu) — a march; a parade

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[TE-LEX-C339-SAINYAM339-02, TE-LEX-C339-SAINYAM339-03] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Telugu for a soldier, then the Telugu for a commander.

## You'll want to know: కవాతు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C339-SAINYAM339-04]; assesses=[] -->

**కవాతు** — *kavātu* — "a march; a parade".

> సైనికులు మైదానంలో కవాతు చేశారు.
>
> *sainikulu maidānaṁlō kavātu cēśāru.*
>
> The soldiers marched on the parade ground.

కవాతు చేయు, to march in step.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**కవాతు** (*kavātu*) is the Telugu word for a drill march; its origin is not settled.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
