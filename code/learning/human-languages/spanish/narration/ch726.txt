Spanish, chapter 726: Humildes y presumidos.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
humilde — humble; poor.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for to make up, then the Spanish for to pretend.

You'll want to know: humilde.
humilde — "humble; poor".
A pesar de su fama, es muy humilde. Despite her fame, she is very humble.
Una familia humilde is a poor family.

The word, taken apart.
humilde is inherited from Latin humilis "low, lowly" (EtymDB), built on humus "earth, ground".

Lesson 5 of 5.
presumir — to show off, to boast.

You'll want to know: presumir.
presumir — "to show off, to boast".
Le gusta presumir de su coche. He likes to show off his car.
Presumir de algo. Also to presume: se presume inocente.

The word, taken apart.
presumir is a learned borrowing from Latin praesūmere "to take beforehand" (EtymDB), built on sūmere "to take".
