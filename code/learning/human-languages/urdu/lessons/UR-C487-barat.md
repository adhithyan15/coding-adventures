## You'll want to know: بارات
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C487-WEDDING487-03]; assesses=[] -->

**بارات** — *bārāt* — "the wedding procession".

> شام کو دولہا کی بارات آئی۔
>
> *shām ko dūlhā kī bārāt āī.*
>
> In the evening the bridegroom's procession arrived.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**بارات** (*bārāt*) is inherited from Sanskrit *varayātrā*, "the bridegroom's procession" (CDIAL 11322).
