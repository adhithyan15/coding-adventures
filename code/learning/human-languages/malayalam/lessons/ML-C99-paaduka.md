# പാടുക (pāṭuka) — to sing

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ML-LEX-C99-SONG-01, ML-LEX-C98-PLAY-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say *a song*. Then say *to play*.

## You'll want to know
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C99-SING-01]; assesses=[] -->

**പാടുക** — *pāṭuka* — **to sing.**

> **ഞാൻ പാടുന്നു.** — *ñān pāṭunnu* — "I sing."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ML-LEX-C99-SING-01, ML-LEX-C99-SONG-01, ML-LEX-C98-PLAY-01, ML-LEX-C98-GAME-01] -->

**Last chapter the noun sat inside the verb. This pair is not built that way.**

| noun | verb |
|---|---|
| **കളി** | **കളിക്കുക** — the noun is there whole |
| **പാട്ട്** | **പാടുക** — the **ട്ട** thins to a single **ട** |

Say them one after the other and you will hear it: **പാട്ട്** holds the **ṭ**
long, **പാടുക** does not.

**Two words, one pattern between them — and that is all it is.** Do not go
looking for a doubled letter in every noun that sits beside a verb. These two
are the pair this chapter gives you, and the book has not shown you a third.
