## You'll want to know: سنجیدن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C398-AMALI-05]; assesses=[] -->

**سنجیدن** (*sanjidan*) — "to weigh up, to measure".

> قبل از تصمیم همه گزینه ها را سنجید.
>
> *qabl az tasmim hame-ye gozine hâ râ sanjid.*
>
> Before deciding he weighed up all the options.

Present stem *sanj-*: *misanjad*. Also to weigh on scales.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**سنجیدن** is an inherited Persian verb "to weigh, to measure", usually traced to Middle Persian; EtymDB lists it without a parent.
