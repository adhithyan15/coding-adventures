## You'll want to know: обморок
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C485-NAUSEA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**обморок** (*óbmorok*) — "a faint".

> В душном зале одна женщина упала в обморок.
>
> *V dúshnom zále odná zhénshchina upála v óbmorok.*
>
> In the stuffy hall a woman fainted.

Masculine. *Упасть в обморок* — to faint.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*обморок* is built on *об-* and *морок*, which EtymDB glosses 'darkness, cloud, fog': fainting is a darkening of the eyes.
