## You'll want to know: أفاد
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C320-ILAM320-04]; assesses=[] -->

**أفاد** — *afāda* — "to give news; to be of use".

> أفادت الشرطة بأن الطريق مغلق.
>
> *afādat ash-shurṭa bi-anna aṭ-ṭarīq mughlaq.*
>
> The police reported that the road was closed.

Present: يفيد, *yufīd*.

In the news: أفاد مراسلنا بأن …, our correspondent reports that …. Elsewhere: هذا يفيد, this helps.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**أفاد** (*afāda*) is built on the native Arabic root *f-y-d* (Lane), where *fāʾida* is a gain or benefit. Form IV *afāda* is "to give someone a benefit", and so to give him information.
