# గట్టిగా (gaṭṭigā) — loudly; firmly

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[TE-LEX-C86-REPAIR-01, TE-LEX-C86-REPAIR-02] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Telugu for slowly, then the Telugu for once more.

## You'll want to know: గట్టిగా
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C86-REPAIR-03]; assesses=[] -->

**గట్టిగా** (*gaṭṭigā*) — "loudly", and also "firmly, tightly".

**కొంచెం గట్టిగా మాట్లాడండి** — "please speak a little louder". **కొంచెం** softens it, as it
softens any request.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

Slowly and loudly: the two fixes for a sentence you could not catch.
