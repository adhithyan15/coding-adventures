Gujarati, chapter 257: Games and competitions.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
રમત (ramat) — game.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for photo, then the Gujarati for to knit.

You'll want to know: રમત.
રમત — ramat — "game".
અમે મેદાનમાં નવી રમત રમ્યા. — ame medānmā̃ navī ramat ramyā — "We played a new game in the field."

The word, taken apart.
રમત (ramat) is built on the taught ramvũ, "to play", with -at. CDIAL derives ramvũ from Sanskrit rámatē, "takes pleasure in" (CDIAL 10628).

Lesson 3 of 5.
હરીફાઈ (harīphāī) — competition, contest.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for game, then the Gujarati for player.

You'll want to know: હરીફાઈ.
હરીફાઈ — harīphāī — "competition, contest".
તે હરીફાઈમાં જીતી. — te harīphāīmā̃ jītī — "She won the competition."
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
હરીફાઈ (harīphāī) is built on harīph, "rival", with -āī. Harīph is a loan from Arabic ḥarīf, "a fellow-worker, an associate" (Lane), which reached Gujarati through Persian.

Lesson 5 of 5.
જોડાવું (joḍāvũ) — to join.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for competition, then the Gujarati for to take part.

You'll want to know: જોડાવું.
જોડાવું — joḍāvũ — "to join".
તે અમારા વર્ગમાં જોડાયો. — te amārā vargmā̃ joḍāyo — "He joined our class."

The word, taken apart.
જોડાવું (joḍāvũ), to become joined, is built on joḍvũ, "to join, fasten", with the intransitive -ā-. CDIAL lists joḍvũ under yōṭayati, "joins", a reconstructed form in its entry yuṭati, "is joined", and calls the history of the group obscure (CDIAL 10496).
