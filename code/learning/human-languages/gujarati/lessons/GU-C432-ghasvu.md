# ઘસવું (ghasvũ) — to rub; to polish

## You'll want to know: ઘસવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C432-GOLD432-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ઘસવું** — *ghasvũ* — "to rub; to polish".

> તેણે ચાંદીની વાટકી કપડાથી ઘસી.
>
> *teṇe cā̃dīnī vāṭkī kapḍāthī ghasī.*
>
> She rubbed the silver bowl with a cloth.

Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે વાસણ ઘસ્યું, *teṇe vāsaṇ ghasyũ*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ઘસવું** (*ghasvũ*) is a tadbhava of Sanskrit *gharṣati*, "rubs"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb, "to rub, to polish" (CDIAL 4450).
