## You'll want to know: निर्भीक
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C470-SAHAS470-04]; assesses=[] -->

**निर्भीक** — *nirbhīk* — "fearless".

> निर्भीक पत्रकार ने सच लिखा।
>
> *nirbhīk patrakār ne sac likhā.*
>
> The fearless journalist wrote the truth.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**निर्भीक** (*nirbhīk*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *nirbhīka*, "fearless", built on *niḥ*, "without", and *bhī*, "fear" (Monier-Williams), the root that also gives *bhay*.
