Sanskrit, chapter 398: At the River Steps.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
घट्टः (ghaṭṭaḥ) — a bathing ghat; river steps.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a hymn of praise, then the Sanskrit for sings the praises of.

You'll want to know: घट्टः.
घट्टः — ghaṭṭaḥ — "a bathing ghat; river steps".
प्रातः घट्टे यात्रिकाः स्नान्ति। prātaḥ ghaṭṭe yātrikāḥ snānti. In the morning pilgrims bathe at the bathing ghat.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
घट्टः (ghaṭṭaḥ) is the word MW gives for a landing-place, a bathing place or steps by a river, with no derivation; CDIAL notes that a Dravidian origin has been suggested (T. Burrow), so its origin is uncertain; it lives on as Hindi ghāṭ, "a bathing or landing place" (MW; CDIAL 4414).

Lesson 2 of 5.
अवगाहते (avagāhate) — plunges into; bathes in.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for sings the praises of, then the Sanskrit for a bathing ghat.

You'll want to know: अवगाहते.
अवगाहते — avagāhate — "plunges into; bathes in".
यात्रिकः नद्याम् अवगाहते। yātrikaḥ nadyām avagāhate. The pilgrim plunges into the river.
Past (he …): सः नद्याम् अवगाढवान्, saḥ nadyām avagāḍhavān.

The word, taken apart.
अवगाहते (avagāhate) is built from ava, "down", and the root √gāh, "to plunge, to dive into"; MW gives ava-gāh as to plunge into or bathe in, with the participle avagāḍha (MW).

Lesson 3 of 5.
संगमः (saṅgamaḥ) — a confluence of rivers.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a bathing ghat, then the Sanskrit for plunges into.

You'll want to know: संगमः.
संगमः — saṅgamaḥ — "a confluence of rivers".
संगमे जलं शीतलम् अस्ति। saṅgame jalaṃ śītalam asti. The water at the confluence is cool.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
संगमः (saṅgamaḥ) is built from sam, "together", and the root √gam, "to go": a going together; MW gives saṃgama as a meeting and as the confluence of rivers (MW).

नद्याः जलं पावनम् अस्ति। nadyāḥ jalaṃ pāvanam asti. The river's water is purifying.

Lesson 5 of 5.
प्रोक्षति (prokṣati) — sprinkles over; sprinkles water on.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a confluence, then the Sanskrit for purifying.

You'll want to know: प्रोक्षति.
प्रोक्षति — prokṣati — "sprinkles over; sprinkles water on".
पुत्रः पुष्पेषु जलं प्रोक्षति। putraḥ puṣpeṣu jalaṃ prokṣati. The son sprinkles water on the flowers.
Past (he …): सः पुष्पेषु जलं प्रोक्षितवान्, saḥ puṣpeṣu jalaṃ prokṣitavān.

The word, taken apart.
प्रोक्षति (prokṣati) is built from pra, "forth", and the root √ukṣ, "to sprinkle"; MW gives prokṣ as to sprinkle upon or to purify by sprinkling, with the participle prokṣita (MW).
