## You'll want to know: allattare
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C496-NEONATO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**allattare** — "to breastfeed, to nurse".

> Ha allattato il figlio per sei mesi.
>
> *She breastfed her son for six months.*

Built on *latte* "milk".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*allattare* is usually traced to Late Latin *allactāre* "to suckle", built on *lac, lactis* "milk" (Lewis & Short).
