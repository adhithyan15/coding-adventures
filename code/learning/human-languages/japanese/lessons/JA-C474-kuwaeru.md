## You'll want to know: くわえる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C474-PREP474-03]; assesses=[] -->

**くわえる** — *kuwaeru* — "to add".

> **しおを すこし くわえました。** — *shio o sukoshi kuwaemashita* — "I added a little salt."

Polite **くわえます**, *kuwaemasu*; an ichidan verb. Past (polite): **ははが さとうを くわえました。**, *haha ga satō o kuwaemashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**くわえる** (*kuwaeru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji spelling was attached to it later (JMdict).
