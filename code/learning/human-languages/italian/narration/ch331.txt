Italian, chapter 331: Gli ingredienti.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
gli ingredienti — the ingredients.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for the excavations, then the Italian for the archaeologist.

You'll want to know: gli ingredienti.
gli ingredienti — "the ingredients".
Gli ingredienti sono pochi: farina, uova e latte. There are only a few ingredients: flour, eggs and milk.
Singular l'ingrediente.

The word, taken apart.
ingrediente is a learned borrowing from Latin ingrediēns, the present participle of ingredī "to go in" (Lewis & Short): what goes into the dish.

dose is usually traced to Greek dósis "a giving", through Medieval Latin dosis (EtymDB has no Italian entry).

il cucchiaino — "the teaspoon".
Un cucchiaino di zucchero, per favore. A teaspoon of sugar, please.
A little cucchiaio, ‘spoon’.

The word, taken apart.
cucchiaino is a little cucchiaio "spoon", from Latin cochlear "a spoon", first a tool for eating snails, cochlea (Lewis & Short).
