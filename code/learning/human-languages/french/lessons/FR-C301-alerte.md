## You'll want to know: l'alerte
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C301-ALERTE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**l'alerte** — "the alert, the alarm".

> L'alerte a été donnée à trois heures du matin.
>
> *The alarm was raised at three in the morning.*

*Donner l'alerte* — to raise the alarm; *une alerte à la bombe* — a bomb scare.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*alerte* is usually traced to Italian *all'erta* "on the lookout, on the height" (EtymDB lists the French word without a parent).
