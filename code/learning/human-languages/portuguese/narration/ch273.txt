Portuguese, chapter 273: Por etapas.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
a etapa — the stage, the step.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for the period, then the Portuguese for immediately.

You'll want to know: a etapa.
a etapa — "the stage, the step".
A primeira etapa da viagem foi de comboio. The first stage of the journey was by train.
Por etapas — in stages, step by step.

The word, taken apart.
etapa was borrowed from French étape, once a stopping place on a march, which EtymDB traces to a Germanic word stapel "a post, a depot".

fase comes from Greek phásis "an appearance" (EtymDB), first said of the moon as it shows a different face.

Lesson 4 of 5.
a sucessão — the succession, a string (of events).

Warm-up.

Before the new one: say the Portuguese for the phase, then the Portuguese for the sequence.

You'll want to know: a sucessão.
a sucessão — "the succession, a string (of events)".
Foi uma sucessão de erros. It was a string of mistakes.
Also who comes next on a throne or at the head of a firm.

The word, taken apart.
sucessão is a learned borrowing from Latin successiō "a coming after, a following on" (EtymDB), from succēdere "to come after".

Lesson 5 of 5.

O professor escreveu a cronologia no quadro. The teacher wrote the chronology on the board.
Por ordem cronológica — in order of time.

The word, taken apart.
cronologia is a learned word built from Greek khrónos "time" and -logía "an account, a reckoning" (EtymDB traces crónica to the same khrónos).
