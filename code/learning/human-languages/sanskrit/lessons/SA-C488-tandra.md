## You'll want to know: तन्द्रा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C488-PRATIBODHA488-02]; assesses=[] -->

**तन्द्रा** — *tandrā* — "lassitude; drowsiness".

> भोजनात् अनन्तरं मम तन्द्रा भवति।
>
> *bhojanāt anantaraṃ mama tandrā bhavati.*
>
> After a meal I feel drowsy.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**तन्द्रा** (*tandrā*) MW gives "lassitude, exhaustion, laziness"; "drowsiness" is the sense it has in modern use; MW gives no derivation, so its origin is uncertain (MW).
