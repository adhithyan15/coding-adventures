# కుదుపు (kudupu) — a jolt; a shake

## You'll want to know: కుదుపు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C446-KUDUPU446-02]; assesses=[] -->

**కుదుపు** — *kudupu* — "a jolt; a shake".

> బస్సు గుంటలో పడినప్పుడు పెద్ద కుదుపు వచ్చింది.
>
> *bassu guṇṭalō paḍinappuḍu pedda kudupu vaccindi.*
>
> When the bus hit the pothole there was a big jolt.

The same word is a verb, to shake something: సీసాను కుదుపు, to shake a bottle.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**కుదుపు** (*kudupu*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 1705 gives Telugu *kudupu*, "to shake, to jolt", and as a noun "a jolting", in the family of Tamil *kuti*, "to leap".
