Lesson 1 of 6.
开发 (kāifā) — to open up land for use.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for a high plateau, then the Mandarin for to emigrate.

You'll want to know: 开发.
开发 — kāifā — "to open up land for use".
这个山区还没有开发 zhège shānqū hái méiyǒu kāifā — This mountain area hasn't been developed yet.

The word, taken apart.
开发 (kāifā) is built from 开 'to open' and 发 'to set going', both inherited from Middle Chinese, khoj and pjot (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'to start' and 'to raise'.

Lesson 2 of 6.
北部 (běibù) — northern part.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for to emigrate, then the Mandarin for to open up land for use.

You'll want to know: 北部.
北部 — běibù — "northern part".
我们坐火车去中国北部 wǒmen zuò huǒchē qù Zhōngguó běibù — We're taking the train up to the north of China.
The new character, in pieces: 部 (bù) — ten strokes in two pieces: on the left five strokes, a dot, a bar, two short strokes and a long bar, over 口 kǒu; on the right the right piece of 那, two strokes. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

The word, taken apart.
北部 (běibù) is built from 北 'north' and 部 (bù) 'a part', both inherited from Middle Chinese, pok and buX (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second as a body of troops; the sense 'a part of a whole' is not given there.

Lesson 3 of 6.
部 (bù) — five strokes over 口, then the right piece of 那.

Before we start: this one needs your hands, so it is not a driving lesson. You will want a pen and something to write on and your eyes, for letter shapes on the page. Script — five strokes over 口, then the right piece of 那.
部 (bù).
Two pieces, left before right. Count the pieces, not the strokes.
The left piece, eight strokes, in two parts. On top, five strokes: a dot, a bar, then a short dot on the left and a short stroke falling to the left on the right, and a long bar under them. Under them, 口 kǒu, three strokes.
The right piece, two strokes, as on the right of 那: a stroke that runs right, bends back and curls down to a hook, then a long vertical.
5 + 3 + 2 = ten.

[once you have stopped driving — this part needs your eyes or your hands; if you are driving, skip ahead to the next part]
Guided Practice.
[once you have stopped driving — write: 部 (bù), all ten, with the model in front of you]
[once you have stopped driving — cover: write it again from memory, and count the pieces, not the strokes]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
How many strokes in 部 (bù)? (Ten.) What sits under the five strokes on top? (口.) Where has your hand made the right piece before? (On the right of 那.)

Lesson 5 of 6.
bùfen — part; section (of a whole).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for the northern part, then the Mandarin for the western part.

You'll want to know: bùfen.
bùfen — "part; section (of a whole)".
dà bùfen yóukè zhù zài běibù — Most of the visitors are staying in the north.
Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
bùfen is built from 部 (bù) 'a section' and fèn 'a share', both inherited from Middle Chinese, buX and bjunH (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second as a portion that is measured out; in this word it is said light.
