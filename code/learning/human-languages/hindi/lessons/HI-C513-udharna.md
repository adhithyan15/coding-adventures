## You'll want to know: उधड़ना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C513-DARZI513-05]; assesses=[] -->

**उधड़ना** — *udhaṛnā* — "to come unstitched; to come apart".

> मेरी जेब उधड़ गई।
>
> *Merī jeb udhaṛ gaī.*
>
> My pocket has come unstitched.

Past (it …): वह कमीज़ उधड़ गई, *vah kamīz udhaṛ gaī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**उधड़ना** (*udhaṛnā*) is a tadbhava, inherited from Sanskrit *uddharati*, "takes out" (CDIAL 2009). CDIAL gives both Hindi verbs as "to be taken out, be unravelled"; *udhaṛnā* is the one that happens by itself.
