## You'll want to know: o choro
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C477-CHORO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**o choro** — "crying".

> Ouvia-se o choro de um bebé no comboio.
>
> *You could hear a baby crying on the train.*

The noun of *chorar*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*choro* comes from *chorar* "to cry", which is inherited from Latin *plōrāre* "to wail" (Lewis & Short).
