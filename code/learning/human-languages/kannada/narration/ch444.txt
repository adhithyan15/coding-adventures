Kannada, chapter 444: Rules of the Road.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಅನುಸರಿಸು (anusarisu) — to comply with; to go by.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for congestion, then the Kannada for to control.

You'll want to know: ಅನುಸರಿಸು.
ಅನುಸರಿಸು — anusarisu — "to comply with; to go by".
ಎಲ್ಲಾ ಚಾಲಕರು ಸಿಗ್ನಲ್ ಅನುಸರಿಸಬೇಕು. ellā cālakaru signal anusarisabēku. All drivers must go by the signals.
Past (I …): ಅನುಸರಿಸಿದೆ, anusariside.
ನಿಯಮ ಅನುಸರಿಸು (anusarisu), to follow a rule; ಹಿಂಬಾಲಿಸು is to follow behind someone.

The word, taken apart.
ಅನುಸರಿಸು (anusarisu) is built from Sanskrit anusaraṇa, "going after", the noun of anusarati, "runs after, follows" (CDIAL 338), with the verb ending -isu.

ಅವನು ಕೆಂಪು ಸಿಗ್ನಲ್ ಉಲ್ಲಂಘಿಸಿದನು. avanu kempu signal ullaṅghisidanu. He jumped the red light.

The word, taken apart.
ಉಲ್ಲಂಘಿಸು (ullaṅghisu) is built from Sanskrit ullaṅghana, "leaping over, transgressing", from ud- "up, over" and laṅgh, "to leap over" (CDIAL 10905), with the verb ending -isu.

Lesson 3 of 5.
ಪಾಲಿಸು (pālisu) — to obey; to keep (a rule).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to comply with, then the Kannada for to violate.

You'll want to know: ಪಾಲಿಸು.
ಪಾಲಿಸು — pālisu — "to obey; to keep (a rule)".
ನಾವು ಸಂಚಾರ ನಿಯಮಗಳನ್ನು ಪಾಲಿಸಿದೆವು. nāvu sañcāra niyamagaḷannu pālisidevu. We kept to the traffic rules.
Past (I …): ಪಾಲಿಸಿದೆ, pāliside.
Also to look after or rear; ನಿಯಮ ಪಾಲಿಸು (pālisu) is the usual pair.

The word, taken apart.
ಪಾಲಿಸು (pālisu) is built from Sanskrit pālayati, "guards, protects" (CDIAL 8129), with the Kannada verb ending -isu: to keep a rule is to guard it.

Lesson 4 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to violate, then the Kannada for to obey.

You'll want to know: ನಿಷೇಧಿಸು.
ನಿಷೇಧಿಸು — niṣēdhisu — "to ban; to prohibit".
ಉದ್ಯಾನದಲ್ಲಿ ಪ್ಲಾಸ್ಟಿಕ್ ಚೀಲಗಳನ್ನು ನಿಷೇಧಿಸಿದ್ದಾರೆ. udyānadalli plāsṭik cīlagaḷannu niṣēdhisiddāre. Plastic bags have been banned in the park.
Past (I …): ನಿಷೇಧಿಸಿದೆ, niṣēdhiside.
ನಿಷೇಧ is a ban: ಪ್ರವೇಶ ನಿಷೇಧ, no entry.

The word, taken apart.
ನಿಷೇಧಿಸು (niṣēdhisu) is built from Sanskrit niṣēdha, "keeping off, prohibition" (Monier-Williams), with the verb ending -isu.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ಏಕಮುಖ (ēkamukha) — one-way.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to obey, then the Kannada for to ban.

You'll want to know: ಏಕಮುಖ.
ಏಕಮುಖ — ēkamukha — "one-way".
ಇದು ಏಕಮುಖ ರಸ್ತೆ, ಆ ಕಡೆಯಿಂದ ಬರಬಾರದು. idu ēkamukha raste, ā kaḍeyinda barabāradu. This is a one-way road; nobody may come from that side.
ಏಕ, one, plus ಮುಖ, face: facing one way.

The word, taken apart.
ಏಕಮುಖ (ēkamukha) is built from two Sanskrit words: ēka, "one" (CDIAL 2462), and mukha, "mouth, face, entrance" (CDIAL 10158).
