## You'll want to know: eficiente
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C361-STRESS-04]; assesses=[] -->

**eficiente** — "efficient".

> A nova máquina é mais eficiente.
>
> *The new machine is more efficient.*

*A eficiência* — efficiency.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*eficiente* is a learned borrowing from Latin *efficiēns* (EtymDB), the present participle of *efficere* "to bring about", built on *facere* "to make" (Lewis & Short).
