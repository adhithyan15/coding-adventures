## You'll want to know: 音乐会
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C128-MUSIC128-04]; assesses=[] -->

**音乐会** — *yīnyuèhuì* — "concert".

> **明天晚上我们去音乐会** *míngtiān wǎnshang wǒmen qù yīnyuèhuì* — Tomorrow evening we're going to a concert.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**音乐会** (*yīnyuèhuì*) is built from 音乐 'music' and 会 'a gathering', all three parts inherited from Middle Chinese, *'im*, *ngaewk* and *hwajH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription); the dictionary glosses the last 'to come together'.
