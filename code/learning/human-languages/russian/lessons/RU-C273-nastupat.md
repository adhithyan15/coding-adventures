## You'll want to know: наступать
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C273-PROISKHODIT-05]; assesses=[] -->

**наступать** (*nastupát*) — "to come, to set in (of a time); to step on".

> Наступает вечер.
>
> *Nastupáyet vécher.*
>
> Evening is coming on.

Imperfective; perfective *наступить* (*nastupít*): *наступила зима* — winter came. Also *наступить на ногу* — to step on a foot.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*наступать* is built on *на-* and *ступать* "to step", the root of *ступень* "a step", inherited from Proto-Slavic *stǫpenĭ* (EtymDB): to step onto.
