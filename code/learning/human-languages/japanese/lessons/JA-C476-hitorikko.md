## You'll want to know: ひとりっこ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C476-KIN476-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ひとりっこ** — *hitorikko* — "an only child".

> **かれは ひとりっこです。** — *kare wa hitorikko desu* — "He is an only child."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ひとりっこ** (*hitorikko*) is a native Japanese compound (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese: *hitori*, "one person" (*hito*, "one", and *ri*, the count of people), and *ko*, "child" (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
