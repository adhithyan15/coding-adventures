Kannada, chapter 448: Filling the Tank.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಇಂಧನ (indhana) — fuel.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to stall, then the Kannada for to ply.

You'll want to know: ಇಂಧನ.
ಇಂಧನ — indhana — "fuel".
ವಿಮಾನಕ್ಕೆ ತುಂಬಾ ಇಂಧನ ಬೇಕು. vimānakke tumbā indhana bēku. An aeroplane needs a lot of fuel.
Petrol, diesel, gas and firewood are all ಇಂಧನ (indhana).

The word, taken apart.
ಇಂಧನ (indhana) is borrowed from Sanskrit indhana, "lighting, kindling, fuel" (CDIAL 1584), from indh, "to kindle".

Lesson 3 of 5.
ಬಂಕ್ (baṅk) — a fuel station; a petrol pump.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for fuel, then the Kannada for diesel.

You'll want to know: ಬಂಕ್.
ಬಂಕ್ — baṅk — "a fuel station; a petrol pump".
ಹೆದ್ದಾರಿಯ ಪಕ್ಕದ ಬಂಕ್ನಲ್ಲಿ ಪೆಟ್ರೋಲ್ ತುಂಬಿಸಿದೆವು. heddāriya pakkada baṅknalli peṭrōl tumbisidevu. We filled up with petrol at the fuel station beside the highway.
ಪೆಟ್ರೋಲ್ ಬಂಕ್ (baṅk) is the full name.

The word, taken apart.
ಬಂಕ್ (baṅk) is a loan from English bunk, in Indian use, in the phrase petrol bunk, "a fuel station".

Lesson 5 of 5.
ಸವಾರಿ ಮಾಡು (savāri māḍu) — to ride.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a fuel station, then the Kannada for a battery.

You'll want to know: ಸವಾರಿ ಮಾಡು.
ಸವಾರಿ ಮಾಡು — savāri māḍu — "to ride".
ಮಕ್ಕಳು ಕಡಲತೀರದಲ್ಲಿ ಕುದುರೆ ಸವಾರಿ ಮಾಡಿದರು. makkaḷu kaḍalatīradalli kudure savāri māḍidaru. The children rode horses on the beach.
Past (I …): ಸವಾರಿ ಮಾಡಿದೆ, savāri māḍide.
ಕುದುರೆ ಸವಾರಿ, horse riding; ಸವಾರ, a rider.

The word, taken apart.
ಸವಾರಿ ಮಾಡು (savāri māḍu) joins savāri, "a ride", usually traced to Persian savārī, "riding, a mount", through Hindustani, and the native verb ಮಾಡು, to do.
