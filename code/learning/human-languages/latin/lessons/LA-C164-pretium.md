## You'll want to know: pretium, pretiī
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C164-LEG164-03]; assesses=[] -->

**pretium, pretiī** — "a price". **Quantī cōnstat?** — "How much does it cost?" English *price* and *precious* come from it.
