## You'll want to know: ਨਿਭਾਉਣਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C363-WORK363-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ਨਿਭਾਉਣਾ** — *nibhāuṇā* — "to carry out a duty; to keep a promise".

> ਉਹ ਹਮੇਸ਼ਾ ਆਪਣਾ ਵਾਅਦਾ ਨਿਭਾਉਂਦਾ ਹੈ।
>
> *oh hameshā āpṇā vāadā nibhāundā hai.*
>
> He always keeps his promise.

Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਆਪਣਾ ਵਾਅਦਾ ਨਿਭਾਇਆ, *maiṁ āpṇā vāadā nibhāiā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਨਿਭਾਉਣਾ** (*nibhāuṇā*) is inherited from Sanskrit *nirvahati*, "leads out of, accomplishes"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *nibhāuṇā*, "to accomplish", under it (CDIAL 7397).
