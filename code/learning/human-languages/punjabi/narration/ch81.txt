Lesson 1 of 5.
ਸੌਖਾ (saukhā) — easy.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for slowly, then the Punjabi for fast.

You'll want to know: ਸੌਖਾ.
ਸੌਖਾ — saukhā — "easy".

The word, taken apart.
Where ਸੌਖਾ (saukhā) comes from is not settled. CDIAL has no entry for it, though it lists Punjabi suhelā, "easy", under Sanskrit sukha, "easy, pleasant" (CDIAL 13451).

Lesson 2 of 5.
ਮਹਿੰਗਾ (mahiṅgā) — expensive.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for fast, then the Punjabi for easy.

You'll want to know: ਮਹਿੰਗਾ.
ਮਹਿੰਗਾ — mahiṅgā — "expensive".

The word, taken apart.
ਮਹਿੰਗਾ (mahiṅgā) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from mahārgha, "high-priced" (CDIAL 9954). CDIAL lists Punjabi maĩhgā.
