## You'll want to know: аренда
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C398-ARENDA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**аренда** (*arénda*) — "rent, a lease".

> Аренда квартиры в центре очень дорогая.
>
> *Arénda kvartíry v tséntre óchen dorogáya.*
>
> Renting a flat in the centre is very expensive.

Feminine. *Сдавать в аренду* — to let; *брать в аренду* — to rent.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**аренда** was borrowed from Polish *arenda*, from Medieval Latin *arrenda* (EtymDB), which is usually traced to Latin *reddere* "to give back, to pay".
