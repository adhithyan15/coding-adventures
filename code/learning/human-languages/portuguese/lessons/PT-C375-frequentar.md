## You'll want to know: frequentar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C375-ESCOLA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**frequentar** — "to attend; to go to often".

> Ele frequenta aulas de inglês à noite.
>
> *He attends English classes in the evening.*

*Frequentar um curso* — to attend a course.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*frequentar* is a learned borrowing from Latin *frequentāre* "to visit or resort to frequently" (EtymDB, Lewis & Short), on *frequēns* "crowded, frequent".
