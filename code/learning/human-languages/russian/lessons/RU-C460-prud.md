## You'll want to know: пруд
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C460-RUCHEY-04]; assesses=[] -->

**пруд** (*prud*) — "a pond".

> В пруду плавают утки.
>
> *V prudú plávayut útki.*
>
> Ducks are swimming in the pond.

Masculine; *в пруду* — in the pond.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*пруд* is usually counted an inherited Slavic word; EtymDB gives no source but lists Polish *prąd* and Slovak *prúd* 'a current' as its cousins.
