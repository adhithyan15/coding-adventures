## You'll want to know: bōdǎ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C320-PHONE320-02]; assesses=[] -->

*bōdǎ* — "to dial a number".

> *yǒu shì qǐng bōdǎ zhège hàomǎ* — If anything comes up, please call this number.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*bōdǎ* is built from *bō* and 打 'to strike', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *pat* and *taengX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to set in order' and 'to remove' and the second 'to hit'. The sense 'to dial' is modern. Middle Chinese *taengX* would not regularly give dǎ.
