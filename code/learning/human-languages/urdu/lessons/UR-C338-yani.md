## You'll want to know: یعنی
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C338-MISAL338-02]; assesses=[] -->

**یعنی** — *yānī* — "that is to say; in other words".

> وہ پرسوں آئے گا، یعنی اتوار کو۔
>
> *voh parsoṅ āe gā, yānī itvār ko.*
>
> He will come the day after tomorrow, that is, on Sunday.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**یعنی** (*yānī*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *yaʿnī*, "it means".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
