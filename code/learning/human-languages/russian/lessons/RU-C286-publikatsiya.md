## You'll want to know: публикация
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C286-OSVESHCHAT-04]; assesses=[] -->

**публикация** (*publikátsiya*) — "a publication".

> После публикации книга стала известной.
>
> *Pósle publikátsii kníga stála izvéstnoy.*
>
> After publication the book became well known.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*публикация* goes back to Latin *pūblicātiō* "a making public", from *pūblicus* "of the people" (Lewis & Short); EtymDB lists no parent for the Russian word.
