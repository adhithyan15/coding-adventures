## You'll want to know: la papelería
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C680-TIENDAS-02]; assesses=[] -->

**la papelería** — "the stationer’s".

> Venden cuadernos y bolígrafos en la papelería.
>
> *They sell notebooks and pens at the stationer’s.*

For pens, paper and envelopes.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*papelería* is built on *papel* "paper", which came from Catalan *paper*, from Latin *papyrus* (EtymDB).
