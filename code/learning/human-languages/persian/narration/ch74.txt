Persian, chapter 74: Sock, Pocket, Ring, Hat, Shoe.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
جوراب (jurâb) — a sock.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a shirt, then the Persian for trousers.

You'll want to know: جوراب.
جوراب — jurâb — "a sock".

The word, taken apart.
jurâb is usually linked with an older Persian gōrab, and Arabic jawrab, "sock", is the same word, but the details are not settled.

Lesson 3 of 5.
انگشتر (angoshtar) — a ring.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a sock, then the Persian for a pocket.

You'll want to know: انگشتر.
انگشتر — angoshtar — "a ring".

The word, taken apart.
angoshtar is built on angosht, "finger", a native word usually traced to Middle Persian angust: the ring is the finger's piece.
