## You'll want to know: aparar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C452-CUIDADOS-02]; assesses=[] -->

**aparar** — "to trim; to catch (something falling)".

> Só quero aparar a barba.
>
> *I only want my beard trimmed.*

*Aparar a bola* — to catch the ball.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*aparar* goes back to Latin *apparāre* 'to make ready', built on *parāre* 'to prepare' (Lewis & Short).
