Lesson 1 of 5.
கூடாரம் (kūṭāram) — a tent.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for chess, then the Tamil for a competition.

You'll want to know: கூடாரம்.
கூடாரம் — kūṭāram — "a tent".

The word, taken apart.
கூடாரம் (kūṭāram) is a South Indian word: DEDR 1881 lists Malayalam kūṭāram, Kannada guḍāra and Telugu guḍāramu. DEDR also compares Sanskrit (lexicons) kuṭaru and Prakrit guḍḍara, "tent", so which way the word travelled is uncertain.

தராசு — tarācu — "a pair of scales, a balance".

தராசு (tarācu) is a loan from Persian tarāzū, "balance, scales", which reached Tamil through Hindustani.

The word, taken apart.
வைக்கோல் (vaikkōl) is native Dravidian. DEDR 5553 lists Malayalam vai-kkōl, "straw". The first part, vai, is itself "paddy straw".
