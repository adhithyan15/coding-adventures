# मिलकर अच्छा लगा (milkar acchā lagā) — nice to meet you

## You'll want to know: मिलकर अच्छा लगा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C120-FORM-05]; assesses=[] -->

**मिलकर अच्छा लगा** (*milkar acchā lagā*) — "nice to meet you", literally "having met, it felt
good".

It is the everyday cousin of **आपसे मिलकर ख़ुशी हुई**, built on the same **मिलना**, "to meet".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**मिलकर अच्छा लगा** (*milkar acchā lagā*) is "having met, it felt good". *milnā*, "to meet", comes from Sanskrit *milati*, "meets" (CDIAL 10133), and *lagnā*, "to strike one as", from *lagyati*, "adheres to" (CDIAL 10895).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

A signature, an identity, an introduction, a person, and the goodbye after meeting.
