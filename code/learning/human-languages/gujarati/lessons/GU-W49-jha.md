# ઝ — the consonant jha

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-LEX-C280-JHADPI-01, GU-LEX-JAVU, GU-CONCEPT-C02-CHHE-01] -->

- [YOU RECALL: say *fast* — **R1**, one lesson back]
- [YOU RECALL: say **જ**, as in **જવું**, and **છ**, as in **છે**]

**ઝડપી** opens on a sound you have not written yet: *jh*.

## You'll want to know: જ with a breath
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-LEX-CHA] -->

| | plain | with a breath |
|---|---|---|
| *c* | **ચ** | **છ** |
| *j* | **જ** | ? |

The missing letter is **ઝ**, *jha*: say **જ** and let a breath follow. Its print has three parts: a rounded left body, a separate right loop flowing into a lower tail, and a separate short upper stem.

## Script — meet one form
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-SCRIPT-C280-JHA-01]; assesses=[] -->

> **ઝ** — *jha*

Keep **ઝ** visible. Follow the numbered strip: it shows where each part starts.

1. begin at the upper left and circle the rounded left body to the lower left
2. lift once and circle the right loop into its lower tail
3. lift again and descend the short upper stem

Three strokes, two pen lifts: a verified three-stroke teaching-app variant fitted to the bundled printed outline (t30apps.com, ‘Gujarati Alphabet Writing Practice’, version 1.0, ઝ animation, first through third SVG paths).

## Writing — notice and trace
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-SCRIPT-C280-JHA-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: observe-trace -->

Finger-trace **ઝ** once, then air-trace it once. Notice the two lifts: after the left body, and after the loop.

## Writing — copy with the model
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-SCRIPT-C280-JHA-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: guided-copy -->

Copy visible **ઝ** once. Compare only two things: are there three parts, and does the short stem come last? Repair one landmark if needed.

## Writing — recall after hiding
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-SCRIPT-C280-JHA-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: delayed-copy -->
<!-- hl-activity: {"id": "GU-W49-jha-recall", "kind": "text", "assesses": ["GU-SCRIPT-C280-JHA-01"], "prompt": "Hide the model and write the letter jha from memory.", "answer": "ઝ", "accepted": [], "feedback": {"correct": "ઝ — the left body, the right loop and tail, then the short stem.", "incorrect": "Look once at ઝ, hide it, and repair only the part that differs."}, "response_seconds": 20} -->

Hide the model and write *jha* once. Uncover **ઝ**, compare, and stop after one repair.

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-SCRIPT-C280-JHA-01, GU-LEX-C280-JHADPI-01] -->

[PAUSE 3s] Read **જ ઝ ઝડપી** aloud. Which part of **ઝ** is written last? (**The short upper stem.**)
