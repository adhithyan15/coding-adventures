Malayalam, chapter 131: What Is Cooking.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
മീൻ (mīn) — fish.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for a feast, then the Malayalam for payasam.

You'll want to know: മീൻ.
മീൻ (mīn) — "fish", straight from the boats every morning.

What you've built.
The coast's favourite food.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: mīn]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: mīn, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say mīn]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Malayalam for a feast, then the Malayalam for payasam, then say mīn again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does മീൻ (mīn) mean? ("Fish".) Say it once more.
