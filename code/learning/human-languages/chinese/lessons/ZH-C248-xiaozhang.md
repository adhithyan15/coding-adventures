## You'll want to know: 校长
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C248-TERM248-03]; assesses=[] -->

**校长** — *xiàozhǎng* — "head teacher; principal".

> **他是我们学校的校长** *tā shì wǒmen xuéxiào de xiàozhǎng* — He's the head of our school.

The new character, in pieces: 校 — ten strokes in two pieces: on the left the tree piece, four strokes, as on the left of 机; on the right a dot and a bar, two short strokes under them, then a long stroke falling to the left crossed by a long stroke falling to the right. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**校长** (*xiàozhǎng*) is built from 校 'a school' and 长 'the head', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *haewH* and *trjangX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription). The dictionary glosses the second 'big'; it lists the first in the title of an army officer, and the sense 'school' is not given there, so how it came is not settled.
