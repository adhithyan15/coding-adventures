## You'll want to know: معطل
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C525-MOHLAT525-03]; assesses=[] -->

**معطل** — *muattal* — "suspended (for now)".

> اگر طوفان آیا تو ریل گاڑیاں معطل ہوں گی۔
>
> *agar tūfān āyā to rel gāṛiyāṅ muattal hoṅ gī.*
>
> If a storm comes, the trains will be suspended.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**معطل** (*muattal*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *muʿaṭṭal*, "left idle, put out of use".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
