## You'll want to know: plēbs, plēbis
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C345-REX-04]; assesses=[] -->

**plēbs, plēbis** — "the common people".

> Plēbs in forō clāmābat.
>
> *The common people were shouting in the forum.*

Feminine, 3rd declension, singular. The citizens outside the old noble families. English *plebeian*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**plēbs** is linked by Lewis & Short with a root meaning "to fill", as in *plēnus*, "full": the multitude. English *plebeian* comes from its family.
