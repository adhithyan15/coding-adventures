## You'll want to know: ഗതാഗതം
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C472-GATAGATAM472-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ഗതാഗതം** — *gatāgataṁ* — "transport; traffic".

> പുതിയ പാലം ഗതാഗതം എളുപ്പമാക്കി.
>
> *putiya pālaṁ gatāgataṁ eḷuppamākki.*
>
> The new bridge made transport easier.

A formal word for the movement of vehicles and people; it is common in the news.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ഗതാഗതം** (*gatāgataṁ*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *gatāgata*, "going and coming" (Monier-Williams), a compound of *gata*, gone, and *āgata*, come.
