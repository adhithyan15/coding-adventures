## You'll want to know: રહસ્ય
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C364-SHODH364-04]; assesses=[] -->

**રહસ્ય** — *rahasya* — "a mystery; a secret".

> દાક્તરે આ રોગનું રહસ્ય શોધી કાઢ્યું.
>
> *dāktare ā rognũ rahasya śodhī kāḍhyũ.*
>
> The doctor found out the secret of this illness.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**રહસ્ય** (*rahasya*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *rahasya*, "secret, mysterious; a secret, a mystery" (Monier-Williams).
