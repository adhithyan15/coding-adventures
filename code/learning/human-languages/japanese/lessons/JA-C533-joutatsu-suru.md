## You'll want to know: じょうたつする
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C533-WORTH533-05]; assesses=[] -->

**じょうたつする** — *jōtatsu suru* — "to improve (in a skill)".

> **もし まいにち れんしゅうしたら、あねは じょうたつするでしょう。** — *moshi mainichi renshū shitara, ane wa jōtatsu suru deshō* — "If my elder sister practises every day, she will get better."

Polite **じょうたつします**, *jōtatsushimasu*; a する verb. Past (polite): **あにの 日本語が じょうたつしました。**, *ani no nihongo ga jōtatsushimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**じょうたつする** (*jōtatsu suru*) is the Sino-Japanese (kango) noun *jōtatsu* with the native verb *suru*, "to do": two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *jō*, "above" (Middle Chinese *dzyangH*) and *tatsu*, "accomplished" (*dat*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
