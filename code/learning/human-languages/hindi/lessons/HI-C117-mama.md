## You'll want to know: मामा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C117-AUNT-03]; assesses=[] -->

**मामा** (*māmā*) — "uncle", your mother's brother.

Beside **चाचा**, the father's brother, it completes the uncles you meet most.
