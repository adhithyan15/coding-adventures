Kannada, chapter 480: Rocks, Deserts and Plains.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಬಂಡೆ (baṇḍe) — a rock; a boulder.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to sprout, then the Kannada for a swamp.

You'll want to know: ಬಂಡೆ.
ಬಂಡೆ — baṇḍe — "a rock; a boulder".
ನದಿಯ ನಡುವೆ ಒಂದು ದೊಡ್ಡ ಬಂಡೆ ಇದೆ. nadiya naḍuve ondu doḍḍa baṇḍe ide. There is a big rock in the middle of the river.
Plural: ಬಂಡೆಗಳು, baṇḍegaḷu.
ಕಲ್ಲು is a stone; ಬಂಡೆ (baṇḍe) is a big rock or a slab.

The word, taken apart.
ಬಂಡೆ (baṇḍe) is native Dravidian: DEDR 3903 lists Kannada baṇḍe, "a rock, a block or slab of stone".

ಮರುಭೂಮಿಯಲ್ಲಿ ಒಂಟೆಗಳು ನೀರಿಲ್ಲದೆ ತುಂಬಾ ದಿನ ಇರುತ್ತವೆ. marubhūmiyalli oṇṭegaḷu nīrillade tumbā dina iruttave. In the desert camels go many days without water.
ಮರು, a wilderness, plus ಭೂಮಿ, land.

The word, taken apart.
ಮರುಭೂಮಿ (marubhūmi) is built from two Sanskrit words: maru, "a wilderness" (CDIAL 9876a), and bhūmi, "earth, ground" (CDIAL 9557).

Lesson 5 of 5.
ಸವೆ (save) — to wear away; to erode.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for an open plain, then the Kannada for a plateau.

You'll want to know: ಸವೆ.
ಸವೆ — save — "to wear away; to erode".
ತುಂಬಾ ನಡೆದಿದ್ದರಿಂದ ನನ್ನ ಚಪ್ಪಲಿ ಸವೆಯಿತು. tumbā naḍediddarinda nanna cappali saveyitu. From so much walking my sandals wore out.
Past (it …): ಸವೆಯಿತು, saveyitu.
ಸವೆತ, wear or erosion: ಮಣ್ಣಿನ ಸವೆತ, soil erosion.

The word, taken apart.
ಸವೆ (save) is native Dravidian: DEDR 2343 lists Kannada same, save, "to be consumed, to wear away, to be abraded".
