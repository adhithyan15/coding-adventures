## You'll want to know: वधूः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C496-VIVAHA496-02]; assesses=[] -->

**वधूः** — *vadhūḥ* — "a bride".

> वधूः रक्तां शाटिकां धारयति।
>
> *vadhūḥ raktāṃ śāṭikāṃ dhārayati.*
>
> The bride wears a red sari.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**वधूः** (*vadhūḥ*) is traced to √vadh, a form of the root √vah, "to carry", by MW: the one led home; MW gives "a bride, a newly married woman" (MW).
