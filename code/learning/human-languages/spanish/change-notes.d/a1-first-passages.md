## Unreleased — A1: the first three paragraphs of connected Spanish

Chapter 266 closes A1 with three reading lessons — 47, 47 and 61 words — and
they are the first connected passages in a 1,155-lesson track.

- Every word in all three was verified to appear in a lesson with a lower
  sequence number, so the lessons' claim that nothing in them is new is checked
  rather than asserted. Two inflections in the third passage (`cansadas`,
  `trabajan`) had never actually appeared despite their lemmas being taught, and
  were rewritten rather than waved through as derivable.
- Three, not one. A single passage introduced `ES-SKILL-CONNECTED-READING` and
  then never revisited it, which dropped Spanish's attained level from A1 to
  **pre-A1** on the reinforcement criterion. The gate is right: a skill
  introduced once is not taught. Spanish is back to attained A1, in progress at
  A2, with only the pre-existing vocabulary and verb-vocabulary shortfalls.
- The passages carry a correction from the rescued draft: María is feminine, and
  the draft read `Ahora está cansado`. The prose also claimed the book's longest
  previous stretch of Spanish was ten words; it was measured at fourteen to
  seventeen, and the claim now says what was measured — that those long lines
  are drills and lists of verb forms rather than anything a person would say.
