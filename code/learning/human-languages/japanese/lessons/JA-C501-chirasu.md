## You'll want to know: ちらす
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C501-MOON501-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ちらす** — *chirasu* — "to scatter (something); to strew".

> **かぜが さくらの はなを ちらしました。** — *kaze ga sakura no hana o chirashimashita* — "The wind scattered the cherry blossoms."

Polite **ちらします**, *chirashimasu*; a godan verb. Past (polite): **かぜが はなを ちらしました。**, *kaze ga hana o chirashimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ちらす** (*chirasu*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, the transitive partner of *chiru*, "to fall (of leaves)"; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
