## You'll want to know: かいきょう
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C383-SHIMAGUNI383-03]; assesses=[] -->

**かいきょう** — *kaikyō* — "a strait".

> **ふねで かいきょうを わたりました。** — *fune de kaikyō o watarimashita* — "We crossed the strait by boat."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**かいきょう** (*kaikyō*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *kai*, "sea" (Middle Chinese *xojX*) and *kyō*, "gorge" (*heap*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
