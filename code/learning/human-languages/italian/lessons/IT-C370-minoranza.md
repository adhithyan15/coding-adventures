## You'll want to know: la minoranza
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C370-MAGGIORANZA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**la minoranza** — "the minority".

> In questa regione vive una minoranza che parla tedesco.
>
> *A minority who speak German lives in this region.*

*Essere in minoranza* — to be outnumbered.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*minoranza* is built on *minore* "smaller", inherited from Latin *minor* "smaller, less" (Lewis & Short).
