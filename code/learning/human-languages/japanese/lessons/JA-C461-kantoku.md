## You'll want to know: かんとく
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C461-SUMO461-05]; assesses=[] -->

**かんとく** — *kantoku* — "a team manager; a director".

> **かんとくは せんしゅに きびしいです。** — *kantoku wa senshu ni kibishii desu* — "The team manager is strict with the players."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**かんとく** (*kantoku*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *kan*, "oversee" (Middle Chinese *kaem*) and *toku*, "coach" (*towk*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
