## You'll want to know: ઉજાગરો
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C398-DOZE398-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ઉજાગરો** — *ujāgro* — "a sleepless night".

> આખી રાત ટ્રેનમાં મને ઉજાગરો થયો.
>
> *ākhī rāt ṭrenmā̃ mane ujāgro thayo.*
>
> I had a sleepless night on the train.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ઉજાગરો** (*ujāgro*) goes back to *\*ujjāgrat*, "awake", a form CDIAL reconstructs; CDIAL lists the Gujarati word, waking through the night (CDIAL 1665).
