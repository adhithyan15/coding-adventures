## You'll want to know: anunciar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C514-REPORTE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**anunciar** — "to announce".

> El presidente anunció su viaje a Chile.
>
> *The president announced his trip to Chile.*

*El anuncio* is the announcement, and also an advert.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*anunciar* is a learned borrowing from Latin *annūntiāre* 'to make known' (EtymDB), built on *nūntius* 'a messenger'. English *announce* is the same Latin verb through French.
