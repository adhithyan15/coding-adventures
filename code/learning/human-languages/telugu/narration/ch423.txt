Telugu, chapter 423: Rewards and Waste.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ప్రతిఫలం (pratiphalaṁ) — a reward; a return.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a resolve, then the Telugu for to decide firmly.

You'll want to know: ప్రతిఫలం.
ప్రతిఫలం — pratiphalaṁ — "a reward; a return".
కష్టానికి ప్రతిఫలం తప్పకుండా వస్తుంది. kaṣṭāniki pratiphalaṁ tappakuṇḍā vastundi. Hard work is sure to bring its reward.
ప్రతి, in return, + ఫలం, fruit.

The word, taken apart.
ప్రతిఫలం (pratiphalaṁ) is built from Sanskrit prati-, "back, in return", and phala, "fruit, result" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 3 of 5.
అనవసరం (anavasaraṁ) — needless; unnecessary.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a reward, then the Telugu for in vain.

You'll want to know: అనవసరం.
అనవసరం — anavasaraṁ — "needless; unnecessary".
ఆ ప్రయాణం అనవసరం. ā prayāṇaṁ anavasaraṁ. That trip is unnecessary.
అ-, not, + అవసరం, need, which you know. అనవసరంగా, needlessly.

The word, taken apart.
అనవసరం (anavasaraṁ) is borrowed from Sanskrit anavasara, "having no leisure; inopportune" (Monier-Williams), the negative of avasara, "occasion", the word in అవసరం.

Lesson 4 of 5.
సరిపోవు (saripōvu) — to be enough; to suffice.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for in vain, then the Telugu for needless.

You'll want to know: సరిపోవు.
సరిపోవు — saripōvu — "to be enough; to suffice".
నా జీతం ఇంటి ఖర్చులకు సరిపోదు. nā jītaṁ iṇṭi kharculaku saripōdu. My salary is not enough for the household expenses.
Past (it …): సరిపోయింది, saripōyindi.
Its past is shown as సరిపోయింది, it was enough. Also to fit: ఆ చొక్కా నాకు సరిపోయింది, that shirt fitted me.

The word, taken apart.
సరిపోవు (saripōvu) is built from sari, "right, equal", and the native verb pōvu, "to go" (DEDR 4572).

Lesson 5 of 5.
వినియోగించు (viniyōgiñcu) — to make use of; to utilise.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for needless, then the Telugu for to be enough.

You'll want to know: వినియోగించు.
వినియోగించు — viniyōgiñcu — "to make use of; to utilise".
మనం నీటిని జాగ్రత్తగా వినియోగించాలి. manaṁ nīṭini jāgrattagā viniyōgiñcāli. We must use water carefully.
Past (I …): వినియోగించాను, viniyōgiñcānu.
Formal beside ఉపయోగించు, which you know.

The word, taken apart.
వినియోగించు (viniyōgiñcu) is built on Sanskrit viniyoga, "distribution; employment, use, application" (Monier-Williams), with the Telugu verb ending -ఇంచు.
