## You'll want to know: pigritia, pigritiae
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C508-IGNA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**pigritia, pigritiae** — "sloth, laziness".

> Propter pigritiam servus nihil facit.
>
> *Because of his laziness the slave does nothing.*

Feminine, first declension, from *piger* — lazy.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**pigritia** is built on *piger* "lazy, slow" (Lewis & Short; EtymDB), which EtymDB links with *pigeō* "to feel annoyance".
