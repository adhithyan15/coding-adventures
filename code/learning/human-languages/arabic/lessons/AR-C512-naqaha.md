## You'll want to know: نقاهة
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C512-SHIFA512-02]; assesses=[] -->

**نقاهة** — *naqāha* — "convalescence".

> بقي أبي في البيت فترة النقاهة.
>
> *baqiya abī fī al-bayt fatrat an-naqāha.*
>
> My father stayed at home during his convalescence.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**نقاهة** (*naqāha*) is usually traced to the native Arabic root *n-q-h*, "to recover from an illness": the weak days between sickness and full health.
