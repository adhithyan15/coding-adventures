Hindi, chapter 317: The Truth Comes Out.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
रहस्य (rahasya) — a mystery; a secret.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a staff, then the Hindi for a curfew.

You'll want to know: रहस्य.
रहस्य — rahasya — "a mystery; a secret".
चोरी का रहस्य पाँच साल तक नहीं खुला। corī kā rahasya pā̃c sāl tak nahīṃ khulā. The mystery of the theft stayed unsolved for five years.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
रहस्य (rahasya) is Sanskrit rahasya, "secret, private" (Monier-Williams), from rahas, solitude. A mystery खुलता है, opens, when it is solved.

Lesson 2 of 5.
मुखबिर (mukhbir) — an informer.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a curfew, then the Hindi for a mystery.

You'll want to know: मुखबिर.
मुखबिर — mukhbir — "an informer".
एक मुखबिर ने पुलिस को चोर का पता बताया। ek mukhbir ne pulis ko cor kā patā batāyā. An informer told the police where the thief was.
Masculine or feminine, by the person meant.

The word, taken apart.
मुखबिर (mukhbir) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic mukhbir, "one who brings news", the same family as ख़बर, news: someone who passes news to the police.

मेरा अंदाज़ा सही निकला। merā andāzā sahī niklā. My guess turned out to be right.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
अंदाज़ा (andāzā) is usually traced to Persian andāza, "a measure": a guess is measuring by eye. अंदाज़ा लगाना is to guess.

Lesson 4 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for an informer, then the Hindi for a guess.

You'll want to know: चौंकना.
चौंकना — cauṅknā — "to be startled".
सच सुनकर सब लोग चौंक गए। sac sunkar sab log cauṅk gae. Everyone was startled to hear the truth.
Past (she …): वह चौंक गई, vah cauṅk gaī.

The word, taken apart.
चौंकना (cauṅknā) is listed by CDIAL beside चमकना, to flash, under camakka, a sudden movement (CDIAL 4676): a start of surprise is a sudden jerk of the body. Hindi writes it with the anusvara.

Lesson 5 of 5.
हैरान (hairān) — astonished.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a guess, then the Hindi for to be startled.

You'll want to know: हैरान.
हैरान — hairān — "astonished".
मुखबिर का नाम सुनकर दरोगा हैरान रह गया। mukhbir kā nām sunkar darogā hairān rah gayā. The inspector was astonished to hear the informer's name.

The word, taken apart.
हैरान (hairān) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic ḥayrān, "bewildered, at a loss". हैरान रह जाना is to be left speechless with surprise.
