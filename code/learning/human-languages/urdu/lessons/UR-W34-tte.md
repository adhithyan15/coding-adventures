# ٹ (ṭe) — the letter that opens ٹھیک

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[UR-SCRIPT-C34-BE-01, UR-SCRIPT-C34-TE-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: write ب and ت once each, and name them.

[PAUSE 2s] Now say **ٹھیک** (*ṭhīk*), "fine, well".
You have read it for a long time. This lesson takes one letter out of it and puts
it in your hand.

## Script you'll notice: ٹ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-SCRIPT-C34-TTE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ٹ** — *ṭe*, a *t* made with the tongue curled back.

It opens **ٹھیک** (*ṭhīk*), "fine". Say *tum*, then *ṭhīk*: the first *t* touches the
teeth, the second is struck further back. Urdu marks that curled-back sound with a
**small mark above** the boat, where **ت** has its two dots. The dot under the
romanization (*ṭ*) marks the same thing.

## Writing: ٹ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[UR-SCRIPT-C34-TTE-01] -->

- **1.** start here: sweep the independent be-series bowl from right to left
- **2.** after one lift, draw the small retroflex mark downward, back upward, and down again to close its loop — and only now lift

**Pen lifts: 1.**

> Stroke order is one attested teaching order; hands and teachers vary.
> Source: Zer o Zabar, Fe, qāf, ṭe, ḍāl, and ṛe, independent ٹ handwriting animation and Ṭe instructions (Northwestern University Libraries Open Textbook, 2026).
