## You'll want to know: averiado
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C769-GOTERA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**averiado** — "broken down, out of order".

> El ascensor está averiado desde el lunes.
>
> *The lift has been out of order since Monday.*

For machines, with *estar*. *Una avería* is a breakdown.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*averiado* is built on *avería* 'a breakdown, damage'. *Avería* is usually traced to Arabic *ʿawāriyya* 'damaged goods', through Catalan or Italian *avaria*, a word of sailors and merchants.
