## You'll want to know: تنور
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C357-KHAMIR-05]; assesses=[] -->

**تنور** (*tanur*) — "a clay oven, a bread oven".

> نان تازه را از تنور بیرون آورد.
>
> *Nân-e tâze râ az tanur birun âvard.*
>
> He took the fresh bread out of the clay oven.

A round oven of clay or sunk in the ground; *nân-e tanuri* "oven bread".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*tanur* is inherited from Middle Persian *tanūr*, "oven" (EtymDB), an old word that EtymDB takes back to Akkadian; Arabic and Aramaic have it too.
