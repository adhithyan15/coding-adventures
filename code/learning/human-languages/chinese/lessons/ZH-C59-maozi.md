## You'll want to know: màozi
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C59-WEAR59-03]; assesses=[] -->

*màozi* — "hat; cap".

> *wǒ de tóu hěn dà màozi tài xiǎo le* — My head's big; the hat is too small.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*màozi* is built from mào and the noun ending zi, both inherited from Middle Chinese, *mawH* and *tsiX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription); the dictionary glosses the first as a covering for the head.
