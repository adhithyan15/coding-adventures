## You'll want to know: ತವರು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C526-BALAGA526-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ತವರು** — *tavaru* — "a woman's parental home".

> ಹೆರಿಗೆಗೆ ಅಕ್ಕ ತವರಿಗೆ ಬಂದಳು.
>
> *herigege akka tavarige bandaḷu.*
>
> My elder sister came to our parents' home for the birth.

ತವರು ಮನೆ, the house a married woman was born in.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ತವರು** (*tavaru*) is usually traced to the native *tavar*, "those who are one's own people" (DEDR 3162); the path is not settled.
