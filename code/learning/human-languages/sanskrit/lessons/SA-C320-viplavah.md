## You'll want to know: विप्लवः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C320-VIPLAVA320-03]; assesses=[] -->

**विप्लवः** — *viplavaḥ* — "a revolt; an uprising".

> राज्ये विप्लवः अभवत्।
>
> *rājye viplavaḥ abhavat.*
>
> A revolt broke out in the kingdom.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**विप्लवः** (*viplavaḥ*) is built from *vi* and the root √plu, "to float, drift"; MW gives *viplava* as confusion, trouble, disaster, and also tumult, affray, revolt (MW).

## What you've built
