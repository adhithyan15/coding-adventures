## You'll want to know: नांगर
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C408-NAVADI408-05]; assesses=[] -->

**नांगर** — *nāṅgar* — "an anchor; a plough".

> जहाजाने बंदरात नांगर टाकला.
>
> *jahājāne bandarāt nāṅgar ṭāklā.*
>
> The ship dropped anchor in the harbour.

Masculine.

नांगर टाकणे, to drop anchor; on a farm the same word is the plough.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**नांगर** (*nāṅgar*) is inherited in the sense "plough": CDIAL lists Marathi *nā̃gar* under Sanskrit *lāṅgala*, "plough" (CDIAL 11006); how the word also came to mean a ship's anchor is not settled.
