## You'll want to know: स्वयंसेवक
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C294-PUR294-04]; assesses=[] -->

**स्वयंसेवक** — *svayaṁsevak* — "a volunteer".

> शंभर स्वयंसेवकांनी लोकांना जेवण वाटलं.
>
> *śambhar svayaṁsevakānnī lokānnā jevaṇ vāṭlaṁ.*
>
> A hundred volunteers handed out food to people.

Masculine.

A woman volunteer is स्वयंसेविका.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**स्वयंसेवक** (*svayaṁsevak*) is a modern compound built from Sanskrit *svayam*, 'of oneself', and *sevaka*, 'one who serves' (Monier-Williams): one who serves of his own accord.
