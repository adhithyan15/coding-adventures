## You'll want to know: நிரூபி
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C347-ATARAM347-02]; assesses=[] -->

**நிரூபி** — *nirūpi* — "to prove".

> அவர் தன் கருத்தை ஆதாரத்துடன் நிரூபித்தார்.
>
> *avar taṉ karuttai ātārattuṭaṉ nirūpittār.*
>
> He proved his point with evidence.

Past (I …): நிரூபித்தேன், *nirūpittēṉ*.

What is proved takes -ஐ: கருத்தை நிரூபி, to prove a point.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**நிரூபி** (*nirūpi*) is a verb Tamil built on Sanskrit *nirūpaṇa*, "stating, determining; examination" (Monier-Williams), with the verb ending -இ that many Sanskrit loans take, as in சுவாசி, "to breathe".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
