Bengali, chapter 367: Making a Guess.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
অনুমান করা (ônumān kôrā) — to suppose; to infer.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for rare, then the Bengali for to assume.

You'll want to know: অনুমান করা.
অনুমান করা — ônumān kôrā — "to suppose; to infer".
আমি অনুমান করলাম যে বাস দেরি করলে অরুণ ট্যাক্সি নেবে। āmi ônumān kôrlām je bās deri kôrle ôrun ṭæksi nebe. I guessed that if the bus was late, Arun would take a taxi.
Past (he/she …): তিনি ঠিক অনুমান করলেন, tini ṭhik ônumān kôrlen.

The word, taken apart.
অনুমান করা (ônumān kôrā) is Sanskrit anumāna, inference (Monier-Williams), with করা: to reason from what you see to what you cannot.

Lesson 2 of 5.
আন্দাজ করা (āndāj kôrā) — to estimate; to make a rough guess.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for to assume, then the Bengali for to suppose.

You'll want to know: আন্দাজ করা.
আন্দাজ করা — āndāj kôrā — "to estimate; to make a rough guess".
আন্দাজ করো, ট্যাক্সিতে গেলে কত খরচ হবে? āndāj kôro, ṭæksite gele kôtô khôrôch hôbe? Have a guess: how much will it cost if we go by taxi?
Past (he/she …): তিনি দামটা আন্দাজ করলেন, tini dāmṭā āndāj kôrlen.

The word, taken apart.
আন্দাজ করা (āndāj kôrā) is usually traced to Persian andāz, a measure or estimate, with করা: to judge an amount by eye.

Lesson 3 of 5.
আভাস (ābhās) — a hint; an indication.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for to suppose, then the Bengali for to estimate.

You'll want to know: আভাস.
আভাস — ābhās — "a hint; an indication".
আকাশ কালো হলে আমরা ঝড়ের আভাস পাই। ākāsh kālo hôle āmrā jhôṛer ābhās pāi. When the sky turns dark, we get a hint of a storm.

The word, taken apart.
আভাস (ābhās) is Sanskrit ābhāsa, appearance (Monier-Williams). Bengali uses it for the faint appearance of something still to come, a hint.

Lesson 5 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a hint, then the Bengali for an expectation.

You'll want to know: ভবিষ্যৎ.
ভবিষ্যৎ — bhôbishyôt — "the future".
এখন টাকা জমালে ভবিষ্যৎ নিরাপদ হবে। ekhôn ṭākā jômāle bhôbishyôt nirāpôd hôbe. If you save money now, your future will be secure.

The word, taken apart.
ভবিষ্যৎ (bhôbishyôt) is Sanskrit bhaviṣyat, 'what is going to be' (Monier-Williams). The last letter is ৎ, a t with no vowel after it.
