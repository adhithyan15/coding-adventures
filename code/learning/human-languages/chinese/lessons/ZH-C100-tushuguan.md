## You'll want to know: túshūguǎn
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C100-TOWN100-04]; assesses=[] -->

*túshūguǎn* — "library".

> *wǒ qù túshūguǎn kàn shū* — I'm going to the library to read.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*túshūguǎn* is built from tú 'picture', 书 'book' and guǎn 'hall', all inherited from Middle Chinese, *du*, *syo* and *kwanH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription); the dictionary glosses the first 'to plan' and the last as a lodging house for guests.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for asking where things are.
