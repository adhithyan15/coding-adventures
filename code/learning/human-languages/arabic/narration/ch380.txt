Arabic, chapter 380: Justice and fairness.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
عدالة (ʿadāla) — justice.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for an activist, then the Arabic for democracy.

You'll want to know: عدالة.
عدالة — ʿadāla — "justice".
يطالب الناس بالعدالة. yuṭālibu an-nās bi-l-ʿadāla. People are demanding justice.

The word, taken apart.
عدالة (ʿadāla) is built on the native Arabic root ʿ-d-l, "to be equal, straight" (Lane). ʿAdāla is a verbal noun, being fair and upright: justice.

What you've built.
One more word for giving reasons and opinions.

استحق (istaḥaqqa) is built on the native Arabic root ḥ-q-q, "to be true, to be due" (Lane). It is Form X, to seek what is due: to deserve.
