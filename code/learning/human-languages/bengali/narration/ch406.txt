The word, taken apart.
গেস্টহাউস (gesṭhāus) is borrowed from English guesthouse.

Lesson 3 of 5.
ধর্মশালা (dhôrmôshālā) — a rest house for pilgrims.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a guesthouse, then the Bengali for a hostel.

You'll want to know: ধর্মশালা.
ধর্মশালা — dhôrmôshālā — "a rest house for pilgrims".
মন্দিরের পাশের ধর্মশালায় আমরা রাত কাটালাম। mondirer pāsher dhôrmôshālāy āmrā rāt kāṭālām. We spent the night in the rest house beside the temple.
Often free or nearly so, kept by a temple or a charitable trust.

The word, taken apart.
ধর্মশালা (dhôrmôshālā) is Sanskrit dharmaśālā, "a charitable asylum" (Monier-Williams), from dharma, duty and merit, and śālā, a hall: a house kept as an act of merit.

You'll want to know: ঠাঁই.
ঠাঁই — ṭhā̃i — "a place to stay; shelter; room".
রাতে গ্রামের একটা বাড়িতে আমরা ঠাঁই পেলাম। rāte grāmer ekṭā bāṛite āmrā ṭhā̃i pelām. At night we found shelter in a house in the village.
ঠাঁই (ṭhā̃i) নেই, there is no room: the bus was so full there was no ঠাঁই (ṭhā̃i).

The word, taken apart.
ঠাঁই (ṭhā̃i) is inherited: CDIAL lists Bengali ṭhā̃i, "a place", under Sanskrit sthā́man, "a station, a standing place" (CDIAL 13760), from sthā, to stand.

Lesson 5 of 5.
টোকা দেওয়া (ṭokā deoyā) — to tap; to knock lightly.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a rest house for pilgrims, then the Bengali for a place to stay.

You'll want to know: টোকা দেওয়া.
টোকা দেওয়া — ṭokā deoyā — "to tap; to knock lightly".
রাতে কেউ আমাদের দরজায় টোকা দিল। rāte keu āmāder dôrjāy ṭokā dilô. In the night someone tapped on our door.
Past (he/she …): তিনি দরজায় টোকা দিলেন, tini dôrjāy ṭokā dilen.
A light tap with the knuckle; a loud knock is দরজায় ধাক্কা.

The word, taken apart.
টোকা দেওয়া (ṭokā deoyā) joins টোকা, "a light tap", to দেওয়া, to give. টোকা perhaps belongs with the reconstructed ṭōkk, "strike", under which CDIAL lists Bengali ṭokar, "a rap" (CDIAL 5476); the link is hedged.
