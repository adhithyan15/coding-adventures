# آ (alef-e madd) — the alef that opens آمدن

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[FA-SCRIPT-C26-FE-01, FA-SCRIPT-C26-GHAF-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: write ف and ق once each, and name them.

[PAUSE 2s] Now say **آمدن** (*âmadan*), "to come".
You have read it for a long time. This lesson takes one letter out of it and puts
it in your hand.

## Script you'll notice: آ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-SCRIPT-C26-ALEF-MADD-01]; assesses=[] -->

**آ** — *alef* with a **madd**, the long *â* at the start of a word.

It opens **آمدن** (*âmadan*), "to come". It is **ا**, which you already write,
wearing a small wavy **hat**. A plain **ا** at the start of a word says a short
*a*; the hat makes it a long *â*.

## Writing: آ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[FA-SCRIPT-C26-ALEF-MADD-01] -->

Put your pen on آ and follow its line. Copy the shape you can see — slowly,
and larger than it is printed.

> This book does not yet tell you **where to start this letter or which way to
> travel**. It is not written down here until it can be written down with a
> source. Copying what is in front of you needs no such source, and it is how the
> shape gets into your hand in the meantime.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[FA-SCRIPT-C26-FE-01, FA-SCRIPT-C26-GHAF-01, FA-SCRIPT-C26-ALEF-MADD-01] -->

[PAUSE 1s]
- [YOU LOOK: at **آمدن** and point at آ inside it]
- [YOU TRACE: آ three times, saying *â* as you finish each one]
- [YOU WRITE: ق, then آ, from memory]
- [YOU READ: **آمدن** aloud once more]

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[FA-SCRIPT-C26-FE-01, FA-SCRIPT-C26-GHAF-01, FA-SCRIPT-C26-ALEF-MADD-01] -->

[PAUSE 3s] Which letter is this — آ? (**alef-e madd**, *â*.) Which word did it
come from? (**آمدن**, *âmadan*, "to come".)
