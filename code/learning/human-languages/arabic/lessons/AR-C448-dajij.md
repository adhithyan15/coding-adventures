## You'll want to know: ضجيج
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C448-DAJIJ448-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ضجيج** — *ḍajīj* — "noise; a din".

> سمعنا ضجيجا من الشارع في الليل.
>
> *samiʿnā ḍajījan min ash-shāriʿ fī al-layl.*
>
> We heard noise from the street at night.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ضجيج** (*ḍajīj*) is built on the native Arabic root *ḍ-j-j*, "to cry out, to clamour" (Lane).
