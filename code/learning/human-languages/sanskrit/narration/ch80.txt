Sanskrit, chapter 80: Crow, Parrot and Peacock.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
काकः (kākaḥ) — a crow.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a mouse, then the Sanskrit for a cat.

You'll want to know: काकः.
काकः (kākaḥ) — "a crow", a black विहगः. Say it and hear its call.

What you've built.
The bird that names itself.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: kākaḥ]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: kākaḥ, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say kākaḥ]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Sanskrit for a mouse, then the Sanskrit for a cat, then say kākaḥ again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does काकः (kākaḥ) mean? ("A crow".) Say it once more.
