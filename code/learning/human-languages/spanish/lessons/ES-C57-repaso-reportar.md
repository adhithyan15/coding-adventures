## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-GRAMMAR-DECIR-PRETERITE, ES-GRAMMAR-J-STEM-PRETERITE-PLURAL] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Two forms first, because the rest is built on them. *He said.*
(*Dijo*.) *They said.* (*Dijeron* — no *i*.)

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-GRAMMAR-REPORTED-QUE-OBLIGATORY, ES-GRAMMAR-REPORTED-YESNO-SI] -->

Three chants. Say each one, then say what it is for.

[PAUSE 2s] **First.** *Dijo que trabajaba.* — a statement goes in on ***que***,
and *que* may never be dropped.

[PAUSE 2s] **Second.** *Preguntó si trabajaba.* — a yes-or-no question goes in
on ***si***, the same *si* that opens a condition.

[PAUSE 2s] **Third.** *Preguntó dónde trabajaba.* — a question that starts with
an asking word brings **its own joint**, accent and all.

## Grammar Lens: the two things that never change
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-GRAMMAR-REPORTED-BACKSHIFT, ES-GRAMMAR-REPORTED-WH-ACCENT] -->

Whatever the joint, two things are true of all three chants.

[PAUSE 2s] **One.** The inner verb steps back into the imperfect — because a
report says what was going on when somebody spoke.

[PAUSE 2s] **Two.** The question marks go. A report tells; it does not ask. And
yet the accent on *dónde* stays, because that accent was never marking the
sentence — it marks the word that does the asking.
