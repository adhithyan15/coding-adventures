## You'll want to know: o barro
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C504-OLARIA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**o barro** — "clay".

> As crianças fizeram pratos de barro.
>
> *The children made plates out of clay.*

The famous painted roosters are made of *barro*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*barro* comes from Vulgar Latin *barrum* "clay, mud" (EtymDB), probably a word from a language spoken in Iberia before Latin.
