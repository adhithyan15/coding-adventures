## You'll want to know: a galeria
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C522-GALERIA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**a galeria** — "gallery".

> A galeria fica perto do rio.
>
> *The gallery is near the river.*

*Galeria de arte* — art gallery.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*galeria* was borrowed from Italian *galleria* (EtymDB), from Medieval Latin *galeria*, whose deeper origin is not settled.
