# వివక్ష (vivakṣa) — discrimination

## You'll want to know: వివక్ష
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C408-SAMAJAM408-05]; assesses=[] -->

**వివక్ష** — *vivakṣa* — "discrimination".

> ఆడపిల్లల మీద వివక్ష తప్పు.
>
> *āḍapillala mīda vivakṣa tappu.*
>
> Discrimination against girls is wrong.

The group treated unfairly takes మీద or పట్ల.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**వివక్ష** (*vivakṣa*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *vivakṣā*, "the wish to speak; meaning, intention" (Monier-Williams); the sense "discrimination" is the modern Telugu one.
