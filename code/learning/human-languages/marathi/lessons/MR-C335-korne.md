## You'll want to know: कोरणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C335-UTKHANAN335-05]; assesses=[] -->

**कोरणे** — *korṇe* — "to carve; to engrave".

> कारागिरांनी दगडात सुंदर मूर्ती कोरल्या.
>
> *kārāgirānnī dagḍāt sundar mūrtī koralyā.*
>
> The craftsmen carved beautiful figures in the stone.

Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने कोरलं, *tyāne korlaṁ*.

The material takes -त: दगडात कोरणे.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**कोरणे** (*korṇe*) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi *korṇe*, 'to scoop, engrave', under *\*kōrati*, 'scrapes' (CDIAL 3530).
