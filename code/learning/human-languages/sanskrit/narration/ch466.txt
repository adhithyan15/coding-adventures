Sanskrit, chapter 466: Sense and Folly.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
मेधा (medhā) — mental vigour.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for attention, then the Sanskrit for industrious.

You'll want to know: मेधा.
मेधा — medhā — "mental vigour".
बालस्य मेधा तीक्ष्णा अस्ति। bālasya medhā tīkṣṇā asti. The boy has a sharp mind.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
मेधा (medhā) MW gives "mental vigour or power, intelligence, prudence, wisdom"; MW gives no derivation, so its origin is uncertain (MW).

The word, taken apart.
मुह्यति (muhyati) comes from the root √muh, "to become stupefied, to be bewildered", the root of मोहः (MW).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: उन्मादः.
उन्मादः — unmādaḥ — "madness; frenzy".
उन्मादेन सः मार्गे धावति। unmādena saḥ mārge dhāvati. In a frenzy he runs along the road.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
उन्मादः (unmādaḥ) is built from ud, "up", and the root √mad, "to be drunk, to rejoice"; MW gives "insanity, madness", "mania (as illness)" and "intoxication" (MW).
