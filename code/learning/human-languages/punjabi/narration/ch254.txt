Punjabi, chapter 254: A Form at the Counter.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ਫ਼ਾਰਮ (fāram) — a form to fill in.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for to move out of the way, then the Punjabi for an advertisement.

You'll want to know: ਫ਼ਾਰਮ.
ਫ਼ਾਰਮ — fāram — "a form to fill in".
ਬੈਂਕ ਵਿੱਚ ਇਹ ਫ਼ਾਰਮ ਭਰੋ। baiṅk vich eh fāram bharo. Fill in this form at the bank.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ਫ਼ਾਰਮ (fāram) is borrowed from English form.

What you've built.
One more word for reading notices and messages.

ਅਮਨ ਨੌਕਰੀ ਲਈ ਅਰਜ਼ੀ ਭੇਜੇਗਾ। Aman naukrī laī arzī bhejegā. Aman will send an application for the job.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ਅਰਜ਼ੀ (arzī) is a loan, usually traced to Persian arzī, "a petition", itself from Arabic.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ਮਿਟਾਉਣਾ (miṭāuṇā) — to rub out; to erase.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for correct, then the Punjabi for wrong.

You'll want to know: ਮਿਟਾਉਣਾ.
ਮਿਟਾਉਣਾ — miṭāuṇā — "to rub out; to erase".
ਮੈਂ ਗ਼ਲਤ ਸ਼ਬਦ ਮਿਟਾਉਂਦਾ ਹਾਂ। maiṁ ghalat shabad miṭāundā hāṁ. I rub out the wrong word.
Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਗ਼ਲਤ ਸ਼ਬਦ ਮਿਟਾਇਆ, maiṁ ghalat shabad miṭāiā.

The word, taken apart.
ਮਿਟਾਉਣਾ (miṭāuṇā) is the causative partner of miṭnā, "is wiped out", which goes back to Sanskrit mṛṣṭa, "rubbed, washed"; CDIAL lists Punjabi miṭnā under it (CDIAL 10299).
