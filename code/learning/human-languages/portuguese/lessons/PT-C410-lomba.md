## You'll want to know: a lomba
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C410-CURVAS-02]; assesses=[] -->

**a lomba** — "the speed bump; the rise (in a road)".

> Há lombas à porta da escola.
>
> *There are speed bumps outside the school.*

Also a rounded hilltop.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*lomba* is built on *lombo* 'back, loin', which is inherited from Latin *lumbus* 'loin' (Lewis & Short): a hump like an animal's back.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
