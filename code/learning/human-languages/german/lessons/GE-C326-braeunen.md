## You'll want to know: bräunen
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C326-VORHEIZEN-05]; assesses=[] -->

**bräunen** — "to brown".

> Der Käse bräunte im Ofen.
>
> *The cheese browned in the oven.*

Also of skin in the sun: *sich in der Sonne bräunen*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*bräunen* is built on *braun* "brown", Old High German *brūn*, Proto-Germanic *brūnaz* (EtymDB).
