Urdu, chapter 441: Joy and Delight.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
مسرت (masarrat) — delight; joy.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a state of mind, then the Urdu for a mood.

You'll want to know: مسرت.
مسرت — masarrat — "delight; joy".
سالگرہ پر سارا کی مسرت دیکھ کر سب خوش تھے۔ sālgirah par sārā kī masarrat dekh kar sab khush the. Everyone was happy to see Sara's delight on her birthday.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
مسرت (masarrat) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic masarra, "gladness".

Lesson 2 of 5.
لطف (lutf) — enjoyment; pleasure.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a mood, then the Urdu for delight.

You'll want to know: لطف.
لطف — lutf — "enjoyment; pleasure".
ہمیں سفر میں بڑا لطف آیا۔ hameṅ safar meṅ baṛā lutf āyā. We really enjoyed the journey.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
لطف (lutf) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic luṭf, "gentleness, grace"; in Urdu it has come to mean the pleasure you take in something.

Lesson 5 of 5.
امنگ (umang) — a surge of eagerness; high spirits.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for joyful, then the Urdu for brimming over.

You'll want to know: امنگ.
امنگ — umang — "a surge of eagerness; high spirits".
نئے سال کی صبح میرے دل میں نئی امنگ تھی۔ naye sāl kī subah mere dil meṅ naī umang thī. On the first morning of the year my heart was full of fresh eagerness.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
امنگ (umang) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin umaṅg, "elation", under Sanskrit únmajjati, "emerges, comes up" (CDIAL 2110).
