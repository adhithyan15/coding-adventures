## You'll want to know: しゃこ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C395-KOSHOU395-02]; assesses=[] -->

**しゃこ** — *shako* — "a garage; a depot".

> **くるまを しゃこに いれました。** — *kuruma o shako ni iremashita* — "I put the car in the garage."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**しゃこ** (*shako*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *sha*, "car" (Middle Chinese *tsyhae*) and *ko*, "warehouse" (*khuH*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
