# Malayalam A2 mock rubric

Score either A2 mock against [task-shapes/a2.json](../../task-shapes/a2.json).
Reading, listening, writing, and speaking are separate 100-point papers; every
paper must independently reach 60/100. A 240/400 total cannot compensate for a
failed paper, and every section must be attempted.

## Reading and listening

Reading assigns 30 points to notices/correspondence and 35 points to each
connected text. Listening assigns 30 points to announcements and 35 points to
each remaining part. Divide each part's points equally among its keyed items and
retain one decimal place. Play every listening text twice at 110–130 words per
minute. No transcript, romanization, dictionary, or translator is allowed.

## Writing

The 25–35 word functional response is worth 40 points: requested content 16,
reader and purpose 8, Malayalam vocabulary and grammar 8, and orthographic
control 8. The 70–90 word connected message or description is worth 60 points:
task fulfilment and relevant content 20, organisation and cohesion 16,
vocabulary and grammar 16, and orthographic control 8. Either traditional or
reformed Malayalam orthography earns full credit when used consistently.
Romanized, translated, non-Malayalam-script, or visibly copied answers earn
zero independent-writing credit.

## Speaking

The personal interview is worth 30 points, the prepared account 35, and the
information-exchange role-play 35. Within each part award 35% for task
fulfilment and relevant content, 25% for interaction or organisation, 25% for
Malayalam vocabulary and grammar, and 15% for pronunciation and fluency.
Natural pauses and successful repair are acceptable. No written answer script
is allowed; preparation notes may contain keywords, not sentences.

## Readiness boundary

The mock is executable but not self-validating. A readiness claim additionally
requires clean timed administration and qualified human review of the paper,
recordings, productive responses, and scoring decisions.
