## You'll want to know: خیرات
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C322-ROZA322-05]; assesses=[] -->

**خیرات** — *khairāt* — "charity; alms".

> ابو ہر سال خیرات دیتے ہیں۔
>
> *abbū har sāl khairāt dete haiṅ.*
>
> Dad gives to charity every year.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**خیرات** (*khairāt*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *khayrāt*, "good deeds, good things", a plural; in Urdu it is what is given to the poor.
