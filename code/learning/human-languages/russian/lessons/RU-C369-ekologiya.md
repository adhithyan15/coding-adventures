## You'll want to know: экология
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C369-EKOLOGIYA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**экология** (*ekológiya*) — "ecology, the environment".

> Мой брат изучает экологию.
>
> *Moy brat izucháyet ekológiyu.*
>
> My brother studies ecology.

Feminine. In everyday speech, the state of the environment: *плохая экология*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**экология** is usually traced to German *Ökologie*, a learned coinage from Greek *oîkos* "a house, a household" and *lógos* "word, study": the study of the home we live in.
