## You'll want to know: برابر
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C344-BARABAR344-01]; assesses=[] -->

**برابر** — *barābar* — "equal".

> سب انسان برابر ہیں۔
>
> *sab insān barābar haiṅ.*
>
> All human beings are equal.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**برابر** (*barābar*) is usually traced to Persian *barābar*, "equal, level".
