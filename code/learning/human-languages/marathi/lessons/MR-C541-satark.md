## You'll want to know: सतर्क
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C541-SAVADH541-04]; assesses=[] -->

**सतर्क** — *satark* — "alert; watchful".

> जंगलात फिरताना सतर्क राहिलं पाहिजे.
>
> *jaṅglāt phirtānā satark rāhilaṁ pāhije.*
>
> One must stay alert when walking in the forest.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सतर्क** (*satark*) is a tatsama, from Sanskrit *satarka*, built on *tarka*, "reasoning"; it is glossed both as skilled in reasoning and as cautious (Monier-Williams).
