# ನಿರಾಳ (nirāḷa) — relieved; at ease

## You'll want to know: ನಿರಾಳ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C496-ASHE496-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ನಿರಾಳ** — *nirāḷa* — "relieved; at ease".

> ಪರೀಕ್ಷೆ ಮುಗಿದ ಮೇಲೆ ನನಗೆ ನಿರಾಳ ಆಯಿತು.
>
> *parīkṣe mugida mēle nanage nirāḷa āyitu.*
>
> Once the exam was over, I felt relieved.

ನಿರಾಳವಾಗಿ, freely, with a light heart.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ನಿರಾಳ** (*nirāḷa*) is an everyday Kannada word for the ease that follows worry; its origin is not settled.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
