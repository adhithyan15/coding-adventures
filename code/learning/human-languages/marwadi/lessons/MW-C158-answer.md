# हां सा, कोनी सा — answering yes or no

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MW-GRAMMAR-C158-POLAR-01, MW-LEX-HAAN-01, MW-SCRIPT-KONI-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Ask *do you drink tea?* Then say the polite *yes* and the plain *no*:
**हां सा**, **कोनी**.

## Grammar Lens: the answer, then the sentence
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-GRAMMAR-C158-ANSWER-01]; assesses=[MW-LEX-HAAN-01, MW-REGISTER-SAA, MW-SCRIPT-KONI-01, MW-GRAMMAR-C157-KONI-VERB-01] -->

Answer with **हां सा** or **कोनी सा**, then say the sentence back:

> **हां सा, हूं पीऊं हूं।**
>
> *hā̃ sā, hū̃ pīū̃ hū̃.* — Yes, I do.

> **कोनी सा, हूं कोनी पीऊं।**
>
> *konī sā, hū̃ konī pīū̃.* — No, I don't.

**सा** keeps the answer as polite as the question.
