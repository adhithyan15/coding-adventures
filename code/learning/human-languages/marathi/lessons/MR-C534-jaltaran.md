## You'll want to know: जलतरण
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C534-BAHER534-02]; assesses=[] -->

**जलतरण** — *jaltaraṇ* — "swimming as a sport".

> जलतरणाच्या स्पर्धेत मीरा पहिली आली.
>
> *jaltaraṇācyā spardhet mīrā pahilī ālī.*
>
> Meera came first in the swimming competition.

Neuter.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**जलतरण** (*jaltaraṇ*) is a tatsama compound of Sanskrit *jala*, "water", and *taraṇa*, "crossing" (Monier-Williams).
