## You'll want to know: そぼ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C243-KIN243-03]; assesses=[] -->

**そぼ** — *sobo* — "my grandmother; a grandmother".

> **そぼは にくを たべません。** — *sobo wa niku o tabemasen* — "My grandmother does not eat meat."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**そぼ** (*sobo*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *so*, "ancestor" (Middle Chinese *tsuX*) and *bo*, "mother" (*muwX*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
