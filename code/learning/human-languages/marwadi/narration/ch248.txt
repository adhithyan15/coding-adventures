Marwadi (Marwari), chapter 248: Henna and Wedding Finery.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
मेंहदी (mẽhdī) — henna.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for a bride, then the Marwadi for a wedding pavilion.

You'll want to know: मेंहदी.
मेंहदी — mẽhdī — "henna".
बहन हाथ माथै मेंहदी लगावै है। bahan hāth māthai mẽhdī lagāvai hai. My sister puts henna on her hands.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
मेंहदी (mẽhdī) is inherited from Sanskrit mendhī, the henna plant. CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry; it lists Hindi mẽhdī (CDIAL 10328).

परणावणो — parṇāvṇo — "to give in marriage; to marry off".
म्हैं म्हारी बेटी परणाई। mhaĩ mhārī beṭī parṇāī. I gave my daughter in marriage.
Past (I …): म्हैं बेटो परणायो, mhaĩ beṭo parṇāyo.

The word, taken apart.
परणावणो (parṇāvṇo) is inherited from Sanskrit pariṇāpayati, "causes a man to marry"; CDIAL lists Old Marwari paraṇāi, "to give in marriage", under it (CDIAL 7821).

Lesson 3 of 5.
दायजो (dāyjo) — a dowry.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for henna, then the Marwadi for to marry off.

You'll want to know: दायजो.
दायजो — dāyjo — "a dowry".
म्हैं दायजो कोनी लियो। mhaĩ dāyjo konī liyo. I took no dowry.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
दायजो (dāyjo) is inherited from Sanskrit dāyādya, "an inheritance"; CDIAL lists Marwari dāyajo, "dowry", under it (CDIAL 6290). A dowry is probably named as the bride's share of the family's goods.

Lesson 4 of 5.
सजणो (sajṇo) — to dress up; to be adorned.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for to marry off, then the Marwadi for a dowry.

You'll want to know: सजणो.
सजणो — sajṇo — "to dress up; to be adorned".
ब्याव मांय सगळा सजै है। byāv mā̃y sagḷā sajai hai. At a wedding everyone dresses up.
Past (he …): वो छोरो सज्यो, vo chhoro sajyo.

The word, taken apart.
सजणो (sajṇo) is probably inherited from Sanskrit sajjyate, "is made ready"; for a bride or a house, being made ready is being adorned. CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry; it lists Hindi sājnā, "to be prepared" (CDIAL 13093).

Lesson 5 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

सेहरो पीळो अर लाल है। sehro pīḷo ar lāl hai. The bridegroom's garland is yellow and red.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
सेहरो (sehro) is inherited from Sanskrit śekhara, "a chaplet". CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry; it lists Hindi sehrā, "a garland worn at a wedding" (CDIAL 12604).
