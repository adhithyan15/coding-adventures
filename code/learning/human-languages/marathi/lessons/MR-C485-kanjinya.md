# कांजिण्या (kāñjiṇyā) — chickenpox

## You'll want to know: कांजिण्या
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C485-MADHUMEH485-05]; assesses=[] -->

**कांजिण्या** — *kāñjiṇyā* — "chickenpox".

> लहानपणी मला कांजिण्या झाल्या होत्या.
>
> *lahānpaṇī malā kāñjiṇyā jhālyā hotyā.*
>
> I had chickenpox as a child.

Feminine.

Plural and feminine: कांजिण्या होणे, with the dative.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**कांजिण्या** (*kāñjiṇyā*) is a Marathi noun whose origin is not settled.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
