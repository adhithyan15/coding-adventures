## You'll want to know: دوران
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C353-DOWRAN-01]; assesses=[] -->

**دوران** (*dowrân*) — "an era, a period".

> در دوران جنگ زندگی سخت بود.
>
> *Dar dowrân-e jang zendegi sakht bud.*
>
> During the war years life was hard.

*Dowrân-e kudaki* "childhood".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*dowrân* is usually traced to Arabic *dawarān*, "a turning, a cycle"; EtymDB lists no entry.
