## You'll want to know: سخی
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C459-GIVE459-03]; assesses=[] -->

**سخی** — *sakhī* — "open-handed".

> سخی آدمی سب کی مدد کرتا ہے۔
>
> *sakhī ādmī sab kī madad kartā hai.*
>
> An open-handed man helps everyone.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**سخی** (*sakhī*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *sakhī*, "generous".
