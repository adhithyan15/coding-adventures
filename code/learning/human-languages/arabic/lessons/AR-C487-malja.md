## You'll want to know: ملجأ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C487-MALJA487-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ملجأ** — *maljaʾ* — "a shelter; a refuge".

> نام الأطفال في الملجأ حتى الصباح.
>
> *nāma al-aṭfāl fī al-maljaʾ ḥattā aṣ-ṣabāḥ.*
>
> The children slept in the shelter until morning.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ملجأ** (*maljaʾ*) is built on the native Arabic root *l-j-ʾ*, "to take refuge" (Lane). The pattern *mafʿal* makes it the place of refuge.
