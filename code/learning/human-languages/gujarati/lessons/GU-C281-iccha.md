# ઇચ્છા (icchā) — a wish; a desire

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-GRAMMAR-C281-FEEL-NOUN-01, GU-GRAMMAR-C146-POSS-AGREE-01, GU-LEX-C91-COURTESY-03] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say "I'm afraid". (*mane bīk lāge che*.) Then "good wishes". (**શુભેચ્છા**, *śubhecchā*.) Its second half is this lesson's word.

## You'll want to know: ઇચ્છા
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C281-ICCHA-01]; assesses=[GU-LEX-NUMBERS-ONE-TO-FIVE, GU-CONCEPT-C02-SHUN-01] -->

**ઇચ્છા** — *icchā* — "a wish; a desire". It is feminine: **મારી ઇચ્છા**, "my wish".

> **મારી એક ઇચ્છા છે.**
>
> *mārī ek icchā che.* — "I have a wish."

> **તમારી ઇચ્છા શું છે?**
>
> *tamārī icchā śũ che?* — "What is your wish?"

**શુભેચ્છા** is *śubh*, "good, lucky", with **ઇચ્છા**: good wishes.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ઇચ્છા** (*icchā*) is a tatsama: Sanskrit *icchā*, "wish, desire" (Monier-Williams). CDIAL lists an inherited Gujarati verb *ichvũ*, "to wish", under Sanskrit *icchati*, "seeks, wishes" (CDIAL 1556).
