## You'll want to know: beherrschen
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C349-STELLE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**beherrschen** — "to master, to have a good command of; to control".

> Er beherrscht drei Sprachen.
>
> *He has a good command of three languages.*

Inseparable: *er beherrscht, er hat beherrscht*. *Sich beherrschen* — to control yourself.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*beherrschen* is built with *be-* on *herrschen* "to rule", inherited from Middle High German *hērschen* and Old High German *hērisōn* (EtymDB): you rule over a skill.
