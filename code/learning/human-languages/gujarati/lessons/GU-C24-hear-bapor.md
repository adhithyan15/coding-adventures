# બપોર — hear midday first

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-BAPOR-01]; assesses=[GU-LEX-SAVAAR-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say *savār* from **morning**. Now listen twice: **bapor**. It means
**midday**, the middle hours that English also calls the afternoon.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**બપોર** (*bapor*) is usually explained as "two watches": a form of the word for two, and *pahor*, the old watch of about three hours. CDIAL lists Gujarati *pahor* under Sanskrit *prahara*, "a watch", and asks whether the Sanskrit word is itself a reshaping of Persian *pahra*, "watch" (CDIAL 8900).
