## You'll want to know: आज़ाद
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C551-MAJBUR551-02]; assesses=[] -->

**आज़ाद** — *āzād* — "free".

> स्कूल के बाद बच्चे खेलने के लिए आज़ाद हैं।
>
> *Skūl ke bād bacce khelne ke liye āzād haiṁ.*
>
> After school the children are free to play.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**आज़ाद** (*āzād*) is usually traced to Persian *āzād*, "free".
