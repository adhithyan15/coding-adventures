## You'll want to know: حساب
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C418-HISAB418-01]; assesses=[] -->

**حساب** — *hisāb* — "an account; a reckoning".

> دکاندار ہر شام اپنا حساب لکھتا ہے۔
>
> *dukāndār har shām apnā hisāb likhtā hai.*
>
> Every evening the shopkeeper writes up his accounts.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**حساب** (*hisāb*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *ḥisāb*, "counting, reckoning".
