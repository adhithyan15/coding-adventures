## You'll want to know: zulässig
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C382-VERSTOSS-02]; assesses=[] -->

**zulässig** — "permitted, allowed".

> Auf dieser Straße sind nur dreißig Kilometer pro Stunde zulässig.
>
> *On this road only thirty kilometres an hour are permitted.*

Formal. The opposite is *unzulässig*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*zulässig* is built with *-ig* on *zulassen* "to allow", from *lassen* "to let", Old High German *lāzan* (EtymDB): what may be let through.
