Urdu, chapter 468: Smells and Itches.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
مہک (mahak) — a scent.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for to suck, then the Urdu for to spit.

You'll want to know: مہک.
مہک — mahak — "a scent".
کمرے میں پھولوں کی مہک ہے۔ kamre meṅ phūloṅ kī mahak hai. There is a scent of flowers in the room.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
مہک (mahak) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin mahak, "fragrance", under Sanskrit magha (CDIAL 9703).

Lesson 4 of 5.
کھجانا (khujānā) — to scratch an itch.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for perfume, then the Urdu for the sense of touch.

You'll want to know: کھجانا.
کھجانا — khujānā — "to scratch an itch".
مچھر کے کاٹنے پر وہ ہاتھ کھجا رہا تھا۔ machchhar ke kāṭne par voh hāth khujā rahā thā. He was scratching his hand where the mosquito had bitten.
Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے سر کھجایا, us ne sar khujāyā.

The word, taken apart.
کھجانا (khujānā) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin khujānā, "to itch", under Sanskrit kharju, "itching, scratching" (CDIAL 3827); کھجلی, "an itch", is from the same word.
