## You'll want to know: ಜಾಗರೂಕ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C426-HIMMETTU426-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ಜಾಗರೂಕ** — *jāgarūka* — "careful; watchful".

> ರಾತ್ರಿ ವಾಹನ ಓಡಿಸುವಾಗ ಜಾಗರೂಕರಾಗಿ ಇರಬೇಕು.
>
> *rātri vāhana ōḍisuvāga jāgarūkarāgi irabēku.*
>
> One must be careful when driving at night.

ಜಾಗರೂಕತೆ, caution.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಜಾಗರೂಕ** (*jāgarūka*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *jāgarūka*, "wakeful, watchful" (Monier-Williams).
