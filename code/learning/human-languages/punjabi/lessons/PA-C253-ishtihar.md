## You'll want to know: ਇਸ਼ਤਿਹਾਰ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C253-RULE253-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ਇਸ਼ਤਿਹਾਰ** — *ishtihār* — "an advertisement".

> ਅਖ਼ਬਾਰ ਵਿੱਚ ਨੌਕਰੀ ਦਾ ਇਸ਼ਤਿਹਾਰ ਹੈ।
>
> *akhbār vich naukrī dā ishtihār hai.*
>
> There is a job advertisement in the newspaper.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਇਸ਼ਤਿਹਾਰ** (*ishtihār*) is a loan, usually traced through Persian to Arabic *ishtihār*, "making known".
