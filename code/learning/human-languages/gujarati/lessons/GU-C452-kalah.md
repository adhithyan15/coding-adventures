# કલહ (kalah) — strife; a family quarrel

## You'll want to know: કલહ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C452-KHIJ452-05]; assesses=[] -->

**કલહ** — *kalah* — "strife; a family quarrel".

> તે ઘરમાં રોજ કલહ થાય છે.
>
> *te gharmā̃ roj kalah thāy che.*
>
> There is strife in that house every day.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**કલહ** (*kalah*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *kalaha*, "strife, contention, quarrel", as Monier-Williams records it.
