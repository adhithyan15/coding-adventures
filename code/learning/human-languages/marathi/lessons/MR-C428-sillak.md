## You'll want to know: शिल्लक
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C428-SHILLAK428-02]; assesses=[] -->

**शिल्लक** — *śillak* — "a balance; what is left".

> महिनाअखेर माझ्याकडे थोडी शिल्लक राहते.
>
> *mahināakher mājhyākaḍe thoḍī śillak rāhte.*
>
> At the end of the month I have a little left over.

Feminine.

शिल्लक राहणे, to be left over; खात्यातली शिल्लक, the account balance.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**शिल्लक** (*śillak*) is a Marathi word for what remains of money or stock; its origin is not settled.
