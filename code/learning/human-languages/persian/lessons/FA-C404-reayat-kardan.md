# رعایت کردن (re'âyat kardan) — to observe, to comply with

## You'll want to know: رعایت کردن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C404-MOQARRARAT-04]; assesses=[] -->

**رعایت کردن** (*re'âyat kardan*) — "to observe, to comply with".

> همه باید قوانین را رعایت کنند.
>
> *hame bâyad qavânin râ re'âyat konand.*
>
> Everyone must obey the rules.

*Re'âyat* "observance, care" + *kardan*. For rules and laws, and for hygiene too.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**رعایت** is usually traced to Arabic *ri'āya* "care, guarding, observance"; with *kardan* "to do" it means to keep a rule.
