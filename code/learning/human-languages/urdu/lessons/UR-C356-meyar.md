## You'll want to know: معیار
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C356-ISLAH356-02]; assesses=[] -->

**معیار** — *meyār* — "a standard (of quality)".

> اس دکان کا معیار اچھا ہے۔
>
> *is dukān kā meyār achchhā hai.*
>
> This shop keeps a good standard.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**معیار** (*meyār*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *miʿyār*, "a measure, a touchstone".
