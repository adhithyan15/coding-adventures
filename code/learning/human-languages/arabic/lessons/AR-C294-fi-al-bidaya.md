## You'll want to know: في البداية
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C294-FAJATAN294-04]; assesses=[] -->

**في البداية** — *fī al-bidāya* — "at first, in the beginning".

> في البداية كان الطريق سهلا.
>
> *fī al-bidāya kāna aṭ-ṭarīq sahlan.*
>
> At first the road was easy.

It opens a story; ثم and بعد ذلك carry it on.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**في البداية** (*fī al-bidāya*) is the preposition *fī*, "in", with *bidāya*, "a beginning", which is built on the native Arabic root *b-d-ʾ*, "to begin" (Lane), the root of *badaʾa*.
