## You'll want to know: maestitia, maestitiae
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C490-MAER-02]; assesses=[] -->

**maestitia, maestitiae** — "sorrow, dejection".

> Maestitia puellae ex oculīs eius appāret.
>
> *The girl's sorrow shows in her eyes.*

Feminine, first declension, from *maestus* — sad. A close partner of *trīstitia*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**maestitia** is built on *maestus* "sad, sorrowful" with *-itia* (Lewis & Short).
