# A gentle five-form payoff

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-FORM-KARDA-183, PA-FORM-KARDE-183] -->

[PAUSE 3s] Without looking, say the *kar-* pair for one masculine subject and for plural or respectful agreement.

## Guided Practice: four skills, bounded to five forms
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-FORM-KARDA-183, PA-FORM-KARDE-183, PA-FORM-UTTHDA-183, PA-FORM-DEKHDA-183, PA-FORM-SAUNDA-183, PA-GRAMMAR-PRESENT-HABITUAL-GENDER-05] -->

- [YOU HEAR: *utthdaa, dekhdaa, saundaa*; YOU SAY: **ਉੱਠਦਾ, ਦੇਖਦਾ, ਸੌਂਦਾ**]
- [YOU READ: **ਮੈਂ ਕੰਮ ਕਰਦਾ ਹਾਂ। ਉਹ ਕੰਮ ਕਰਦੇ ਹਨ।**]
- [YOU WRITE: cover the model and write **ਕਰਦਾ, ਕਰਦੇ, ਉੱਠਦਾ, ਦੇਖਦਾ, ਸੌਂਦਾ**]

Now point to the ending that distinguishes **ਕਰਦਾ** from **ਕਰਦੇ**. Then say what stayed constant.
