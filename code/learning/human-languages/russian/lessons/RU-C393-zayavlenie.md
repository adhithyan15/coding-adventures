## You'll want to know: заявление
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C393-DOKUMENTY-01]; assesses=[] -->

**заявление** (*zayavléniye*) — "an application (in writing); a statement".

> Он подал заявление на отпуск.
>
> *On pódal zayavléniye na ótpusk.*
>
> He put in a request for leave.

Neuter. A written request to an office or a boss: *заявление на отпуск*. Also a public statement.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**заявление** is built on *заявить* "to declare", from *за-* and *явить* "to show", from Proto-Slavic *\*aviti* "to make visible" (EtymDB).
