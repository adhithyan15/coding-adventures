# સંભવતઃ (sambhavataḥ) — probably

## You'll want to know: સંભવતઃ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C367-SAMBHAV367-01]; assesses=[] -->

**સંભવતઃ** — *sambhavataḥ* — "probably".

> જો ટ્રેન સમયસર હશે તો અમે સંભવતઃ સાંજે પહોંચીશું.
>
> *jo ṭren samaysar haśe to ame sambhavataḥ sā̃je pahõcīśũ.*
>
> If the train is on time, we will probably arrive by evening.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**સંભવતઃ** (*sambhavataḥ*) is built from Sanskrit *sambhava*, "coming about, possibility" (Monier-Williams), and the adverb ending *-taḥ*: by way of what is possible.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
