## You'll want to know: lūsus, lūsūs
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C539-ALEA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**lūsus, lūsūs** — "play, a game".

> Lūsus puerōrum in viā clāmōsus est.
>
> *The children's play in the street is noisy.*

Masculine, fourth declension, from *lūdō* — to play.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**lūsus** is the action noun of *lūdō* "to play" (Lewis & Short; EtymDB). English *illusion* comes from the family, through *illūdō* "to mock".
