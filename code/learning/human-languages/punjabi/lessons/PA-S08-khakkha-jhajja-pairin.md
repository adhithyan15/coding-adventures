# ਖ · ਝ · ੍ — three pieces met inside a word you already say

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-SCRIPT-HA-01, PA-SCRIPT-RECOG-SIHARI-01, PA-SCRIPT-RECOG-BA-01, PA-SCRIPT-RECOG-LA-01, PA-SCRIPT-RECOG-HORA-01, PA-SCRIPT-RECOG-AIRA-01, PA-SCRIPT-RECOG-AARA-01, PA-SCRIPT-RECOG-NAANA-01, PA-SCRIPT-RECOG-JA-01, PA-SCRIPT-RECOG-VA-01, PA-SCRIPT-RECOG-RRA-01, PA-SCRIPT-RECOG-GHA-01, PA-SCRIPT-RECOG-DA-01, PA-LEX-JANNA, PA-CONTRAST-JANA-JANNA, PA-ETYMON-JANNA-KNOW, PA-LEX-SOCHNA, PA-ETYMON-SOCHNA-SHUC] -->

[PAUSE 2s] One new mark today is not a sound at all. It is an instruction about the letter beside it.

First, say the last things you learned once more, out loud and from
memory:

- [YOU SAY: *kivēṁ — tusī̃ kivēṁ ho — maiṁ ṭhīk hāṁ — koī gall nahīṁ* · *jāṇnā* · *sochṇā*]

## Script you'll notice: ਖ · ਝ · ੍
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-SCRIPT-RECOG-KHA-01, PA-SCRIPT-RECOG-JHA-01, PA-SCRIPT-RECOG-PAIRIN-01]; assesses=[] -->

> ਖ

**khakkhā** — *kha*. A consonant, *k* with a clear puff of breath after it — the *k* of English *kin*.

> ਝ

**jhajjā** — *jha*. Another letter whose breath became **pitch**: written breathy, said today with a falling tone.

> ੍

**pairī̃** — *(no vowel)*. Not a letter and not a vowel sign. It is the mark that says **no vowel here** — the consonant before it runs straight into the next one, and in print the second letter is often tucked **underneath** the first.

Now put them where you have already heard them:

> **ਖ** is **ਕ** with breath after it. Set them side by side: **ਕ**  **ਖ**
>
> **ੜ** + **੍** + **ਹ** → **ੜ੍ਹ**
>
> **ੜ੍ਹ** — *ṛh* — **a ṛa with a shrunken *ha* tucked underneath it**

You are not being asked to write any of this. You are being asked to
**recognise** it the next time it goes past.

## Writing: copy what you can see
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-SCRIPT-RECOG-KHA-01, PA-SCRIPT-RECOG-JHA-01, PA-SCRIPT-RECOG-PAIRIN-01] -->

Keep ਖ · ਝ · ੍ in front of you and do not cover any of them. Follow each shape once with a
finger, then copy it once with a pencil, larger than it is printed. Say its
sound as your hand stops.

> This is **observe-and-trace with the model visible**. Nothing in these
> chapters asks you to write a Punjabi word from memory; that ladder starts
> later, and it starts from these same shapes.
