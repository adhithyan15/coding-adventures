French, chapter 484: Aux urgences.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
l'ambulance — ambulance.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for dizziness, then the French for to digest.

You'll want to know: l'ambulance.
l'ambulance — "ambulance".
L'ambulance est arrivée en dix minutes. The ambulance arrived in ten minutes.
Feminine: une ambulance. Appeler une ambulance — to call an ambulance.

The word, taken apart.
l'ambulance is built on ambulant "moving about" (EtymDB), from Latin ambulāre "to walk" (Lewis & Short): first a field hospital that moved with an army.

What you've built.

le secouriste — "first-aider, rescue worker".
Un secouriste s'est occupé du blessé. A first-aider took care of the injured man.
A woman is la secouriste. Les premiers secours — first aid.

The word, taken apart.
le secouriste is built on secours "help", which goes back to Latin succursus, from succurrere "to run to help", built on currere "to run" (Lewis & Short).

You'll want to know: le brancard.
le brancard — "stretcher".
Ils ont porté le joueur sur un brancard. They carried the player on a stretcher.
Also called une civière.

The word, taken apart.
le brancard is built on a northern dialect form of branche "branch" with -ard: first two poles for carrying. Branche comes from Late Latin branca "paw".

Lesson 4 of 5.
la piqûre — injection; sting, bite.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for first-aider, then the French for stretcher.

You'll want to know: la piqûre.
la piqûre — "injection; sting, bite".
L'infirmière m'a fait une piqûre au bras. The nurse gave me an injection in the arm.
Also an insect bite: une piqûre de moustique. Also spelt piqure.

The word, taken apart.
la piqûre is built on piquer "to prick" with -ure. Piquer goes back to a Vulgar Latin piccāre, probably an expressive word.

Lesson 5 of 5.
la salle d'attente — waiting room.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for stretcher, then the French for injection.

You'll want to know: la salle d'attente.
la salle d'attente — "waiting room".
Nous avons passé deux heures dans la salle d'attente. We spent two hours in the waiting room.
At a doctor's, a station or a hospital.

The word, taken apart.
la salle d'attente joins salle "room", from Frankish sal "a hall", and attente "waiting", built on attendre, inherited from Latin attendere "to stretch towards", from tendere (Lewis & Short).
