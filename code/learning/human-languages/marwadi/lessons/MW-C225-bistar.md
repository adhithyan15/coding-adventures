## You'll want to know: बिस्तर
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C225-PACK225-04]; assesses=[] -->

**बिस्तर** — *bistar* — "bedding; a bedroll".

> हूं काल बिस्तर लेसूं।
>
> *hū̃ kāl bistar lesū̃.*
>
> Tomorrow I'll take my bedding.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**बिस्तर** (*bistar*) is usually traced to Persian *bistar*, "a bed, bedding".
