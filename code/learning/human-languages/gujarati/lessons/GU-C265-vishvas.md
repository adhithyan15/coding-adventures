## You'll want to know: વિશ્વાસ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C265-LOVE265-05]; assesses=[] -->

**વિશ્વાસ** — *viśvās* — "trust, faith".

> **શું તમને મારામાં વિશ્વાસ નથી?** — *śũ tamne mārāmā̃ viśvās nathī?* — "Don't you trust me?"

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**વિશ્વાસ** (*viśvās*) is Sanskrit *viśvāsa*, "trust, confidence", taken over whole as a tatsama; CDIAL also lists an inherited Old Gujarati *vīsāsa*, "trust" (CDIAL 11966).
