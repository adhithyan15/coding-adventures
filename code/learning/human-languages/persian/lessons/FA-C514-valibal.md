## You'll want to know: والیبال
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C514-BALL-01]; assesses=[] -->

**والیبال** (*vâlibâl*) — "volleyball".

> دانشجوها در سالن والیبال بازی میکنند.
>
> *dâneshjuhâ dar sâlon vâlibâl bâzi mikonand.*
>
> The students play volleyball in the hall.

*vâlibâl bâzi kardan* — to play volleyball.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*vâlibâl* is a modern loan, usually traced to English *volleyball*. EtymDB has no entry for it.
