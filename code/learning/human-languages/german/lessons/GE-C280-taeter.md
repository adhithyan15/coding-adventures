## You'll want to know: der Täter
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C280-VERBRECHEN-03]; assesses=[] -->

**der Täter** — "the offender, the culprit".

> Der Täter trug eine schwarze Mütze.
>
> *The offender wore a black cap.*

Plural: *die Täter*. Feminine *die Täterin*. The person who did the deed, *die Tat*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Täter* is built on *Tat* "deed", from Old High German *tāt*, Proto-Germanic *dēdiz* (EtymDB), a cousin of English *deed*.
