Lesson 1 of 5.
la racchetta — a racket.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for gold, then the Italian for silver.

You'll want to know: la racchetta.
la racchetta — "a racket".

The word, taken apart.
racchetta from French raquette, "a racket" — borrowed; EtymDB 2.1 traces the French word to Arabic raha, "the palm of the hand". (EtymDB 2.1).

Lesson 3 of 5.
la corsa — a race, a run.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for a racket, then the Italian for a gym.

You'll want to know: la corsa.
la corsa — "a race, a run".

The word, taken apart.
corsa is the past participle of correre, "to run", used as a noun: a run, a race. correre from Latin currere. (EtymDB 2.1).
