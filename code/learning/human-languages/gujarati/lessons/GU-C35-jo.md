# જો (jo) — if

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-JOIN-TETHI-01, GU-JOIN-PAN-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] One chapter back, the word that draws a conclusion. Three chapters
back, at the end of its chapter, the word that means both *but* and *also*.

- [YOU RECALL: *tethī*, then *paṇ*]

Every joining word so far has taken two things that are **true** and put them
together. This one takes something that **might not be**.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-JOIN-JO-01]; assesses=[GU-JOIN-TETHI-01] -->

> **જો** *(something that might be so)* …

**જો** stands at the **front** of the condition, where English also puts *if*.
That is unusual for this book: most of the small words you have met — **માટે**,
**પણ** in its *also* sense, the postpositions — come **after** what they apply
to. **જો** comes first, and that alone tells your ear a condition is starting.
