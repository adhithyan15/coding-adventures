# Close the writing loop for ू, ण and आ

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MW-PERFORMANCE-PRICE-FOUR-SKILL-01, MW-LEX-PAANI-01, MW-SCRIPT-KHA-01] -->

[PAUSE 20s] Recall the three-counter price payoff, say water, then write **ख**.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MW-SCRIPT-UU-MATRA-01, MW-SCRIPT-NNA-01, MW-SCRIPT-AA-INDEPENDENT-01, MW-SCRIPT-II-INDEPENDENT-01, MW-SCRIPT-KITNO-01, MW-SCRIPT-PRICE-QUESTION-01, MW-SCRIPT-TOTAL-QUESTION-01, MW-SCRIPT-PAANI-01, MW-SCRIPT-PACHHE-MILSOO-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: dictation-transcription -->

Without a model, write four single signs from sound cues: **ू**, **ण**, **आ**,
**ई**. This chapter added no sign of its own, so the four it borrowed most are
the four it has to prove.

Then write **कितणो**, **पाणी**, **पाछे मिलसू**, and both price questions from
dictation. Repair only the missed unit and rewrite its word once.
