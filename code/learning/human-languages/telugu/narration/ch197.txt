The word, taken apart.
The origin of వడ్రంగి (vaḍraṅgi) is uncertain. DEDR and CDIAL list no word of this shape meaning "carpenter".

Lesson 4 of 5.
పరిష్కారం (pariṣkāraṁ) — a solution.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a writer, then the Telugu for a problem.

You'll want to know: పరిష్కారం.
పరిష్కారం — pariṣkāraṁ — "a solution".

The word, taken apart.
పరిష్కారం (pariṣkāraṁ) is Sanskrit pariṣkāra, "ornament", taken over whole as a learned loan, a tatsama, with the Telugu noun ending -ṁ (CDIAL 7894). The sense "solution" is the one Telugu gives it.
