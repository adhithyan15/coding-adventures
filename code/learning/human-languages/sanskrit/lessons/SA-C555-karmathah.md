## You'll want to know: कर्मठः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C555-PRERANA555-04]; assesses=[] -->

**कर्मठः** — *karmaṭhaḥ* — "hard-working".

> यदि त्वं कर्मठः असि, तर्हि तव कार्यं सम्यक् भविष्यति।
>
> *yadi tvaṃ karmaṭhaḥ asi, tarhi tava kāryaṃ samyak bhaviṣyati.*
>
> If you are hard-working, your work will go well.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**कर्मठः** (*karmaṭhaḥ*) is built on *karman*, "work, action", with the ending *-ṭha*; MW gives *karmaṭha* as capable of work, clever in work, working diligently (MW).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
