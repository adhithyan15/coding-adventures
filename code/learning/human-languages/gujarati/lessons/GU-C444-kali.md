## You'll want to know: કળી
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C444-BUD444-03]; assesses=[] -->

**કળી** — *kaḷī* — "a bud".

> ગુલાબના છોડ પર નવી કળીઓ આવી.
>
> *gulābnā choḍ par navī kaḷīo āvī.*
>
> New buds came on the rose bush.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**કળી** (*kaḷī*) is a tadbhava of Sanskrit *kali*, "a bud"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati word (CDIAL 2934).
