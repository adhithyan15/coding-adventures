## You'll want to know: навигатор
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C437-ZABLUDITSYA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**навигатор** (*navigátor*) — "a satnav, a navigator".

> Навигатор показал нам дорогу.
>
> *Navigátor pokazál nam dorógu.*
>
> The satnav showed us the way.

Masculine. Also a ship's or a plane's navigator.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*навигатор* is usually traced to Latin *nāvigātor* 'a sailor' (EtymDB lists the Latin word but not the Russian), from *nāvigāre* 'to sail', on *nāvis* 'a ship' (Lewis & Short).
