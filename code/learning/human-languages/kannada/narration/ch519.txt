5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಉದ್ಯಮಿ (udyami) — an entrepreneur; an industrialist.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for retirement, then the Kannada for a contract.

You'll want to know: ಉದ್ಯಮಿ.
ಉದ್ಯಮಿ — udyami — "an entrepreneur; an industrialist".
ಆ ಯುವ ಉದ್ಯಮಿ ಹೊಸ ಕಾರ್ಖಾನೆ ತೆರೆದನು. ā yuva udyami hosa kārkhāne teredanu. That young entrepreneur opened a new factory.
Plural: ಉದ್ಯಮಿಗಳು, udyamigaḷu.
ಉದ್ಯಮ, an industry or enterprise.

The word, taken apart.
ಉದ್ಯಮಿ (udyami) is borrowed from Sanskrit udyamin, "undertaking, persevering" (Monier-Williams), built on udyama, "undertaking, striving after" (CDIAL 2048).

Lesson 2 of 5.
ವ್ಯವಸ್ಥಾಪಕ (vyavasthāpaka) — a manager.

[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a contract, then the Kannada for an entrepreneur.

You'll want to know: ವ್ಯವಸ್ಥಾಪಕ.
ವ್ಯವಸ್ಥಾಪಕ — vyavasthāpaka — "a manager".
ಬ್ಯಾಂಕಿನ ವ್ಯವಸ್ಥಾಪಕರು ನನ್ನ ಅರ್ಜಿಯನ್ನು ಪರಿಶೀಲಿಸಿದರು. byāṅkina vyavasthāpakaru nanna arjiyannu pariśīlisidaru. The bank manager checked my application.
Everyday talk also says ಮ್ಯಾನೇಜರ್. ವ್ಯವಸ್ಥೆ is an arrangement.

The word, taken apart.
ವ್ಯವಸ್ಥಾಪಕ (vyavasthāpaka) is borrowed from Sanskrit vyavasthāpaka, "settling, arranging, establishing" (Monier-Williams): the one who puts things in order.

ವೈದ್ಯರ ಸಹಾಯಕ ನನ್ನ ಹೆಸರು ಕೇಳಿದನು. vaidyara sahāyaka nanna hesaru kēḷidanu. The doctor's assistant asked my name.

The word, taken apart.

Lesson 4 of 5.
ನಿಯೋಜಿಸು (niyōjisu) — to assign; to post.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a manager, then the Kannada for an assistant.

You'll want to know: ನಿಯೋಜಿಸು.
ನಿಯೋಜಿಸು — niyōjisu — "to assign; to post".
ಕಂಪನಿ ಅವಳನ್ನು ಹೊಸ ಶಾಖೆಗೆ ನಿಯೋಜಿಸಿತು. kampani avaḷannu hosa śākhege niyōjisitu. The company posted her to the new branch.
Past (I …): ನಿಯೋಜಿಸಿದೆ, niyōjiside.
The person takes -ಅನ್ನು, the post or task -ಗೆ.

The word, taken apart.
ನಿಯೋಜಿಸು (niyōjisu) is built on Sanskrit niyojana, "enjoining, directing, appointing to" (Monier-Williams), with the verb ending -isu.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ಕೂಡಿಡು (kūḍiḍu) — to save up; to put by.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for an assistant, then the Kannada for to assign.

You'll want to know: ಕೂಡಿಡು.
ಕೂಡಿಡು — kūḍiḍu — "to save up; to put by".
ಹೊಸ ಚೀಲಕ್ಕಾಗಿ ತಂಗಿ ದುಡ್ಡು ಕೂಡಿಟ್ಟಳು. hosa cīlakkāgi taṅgi duḍḍu kūḍiṭṭaḷu. My younger sister saved up money for a new bag.
Past (I …): ಕೂಡಿಟ್ಟೆ, kūḍiṭṭe.
ಕೂಡು, to gather + ಇಡು, to put: to gather together and keep.

The word, taken apart.
ಕೂಡಿಡು (kūḍiḍu) is a native Dravidian compound: kūḍu, "to join, come together" (DEDR 1882), and iḍu, "to put, keep" (DEDR 442).
