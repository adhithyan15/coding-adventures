# A regular meeting by a group

## Grammar Lens: the regular-group form
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-FORM-MILDE-188]; assesses=[PA-PRON-ASI-01, PA-GRAMMAR-MAIN-HAAN-03] -->

> **ਅਸੀਂ ਮਿਲਦੇ ਹਾਂ** — *asiin milde haan* — we meet

The middle word is the measured new form. In this bounded frame it presents a
regular or habitual meeting by a plural group.
