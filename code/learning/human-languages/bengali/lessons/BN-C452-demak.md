## You'll want to know: দেমাক
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C452-DEMAK452-02]; assesses=[] -->

**দেমাক** — *demāk* — "conceit; airs".

> নতুন গাড়ি কিনে প্রতিবেশীর দেমাক বাড়ল।
>
> *nôtun gāṛi kine prôtibeshīr demāk bāṛlô.*
>
> Having bought a new car, the neighbour began to put on airs.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**দেমাক** (*demāk*) is usually traced to Arabic *dimāgh*, "brain", which took on the sense of pride and conceit in Persian.
