## You'll want to know: ਕਿਰਾਇਆ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C262-FEE262-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ਕਿਰਾਇਆ** — *kirāiā* — "a fare; rent".

> ਸ਼ਹਿਰ ਤੱਕ ਬੱਸ ਦਾ ਕਿਰਾਇਆ ਸਸਤਾ ਹੈ।
>
> *shahir tak bass dā kirāiā sastā hai.*
>
> The bus fare to town is cheap.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਕਿਰਾਇਆ** (*kirāiā*) is a loan, usually traced through Persian to Arabic *kirā*, "hire".
