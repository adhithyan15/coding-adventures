## You'll want to know: huāqián
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C188-MONEY188-02]; assesses=[] -->

*huāqián* — "to spend money".

> *wǒ mèimei hěn huì huāqián* — My little sister really knows how to spend money.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*huāqián* is built from *huā*, the word for a flower, here 'to spend', and 钱 'money'. The second is inherited from Middle Chinese *dzjen* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription); the dictionary lists the first only as a flower, so how it came to mean spending is not settled.
