## You'll want to know: ઝૂઝવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C383-PRAYATNA383-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ઝૂઝવું** — *jhūjhvũ* — "to battle on".

> જો તે રોગ સામે ઝૂઝશે તો સાજો થશે.
>
> *jo te rog sāme jhūjhśe to sājo thaśe.*
>
> If he fights the illness, he will get better.

Past (he …): તે છેલ્લે સુધી ઝૂઝ્યો, *te chelle sudhī jhūjhyo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ઝૂઝવું** (*jhūjhvũ*) is inherited from Sanskrit *yúdhyatē*, "fights against"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb, "to fight" (CDIAL 10502).
