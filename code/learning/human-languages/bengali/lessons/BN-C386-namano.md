## You'll want to know: নামানো
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C386-KULI386-04]; assesses=[] -->

**নামানো** — *nāmāno* — "to take down; to unload".

> অরুণ তাক থেকে বাক্সটা নামাল।
>
> *ôrun tāk theke bāksôṭā nāmālô.*
>
> Arun took the box down from the shelf.

Past (he/she …): তিনি বাক্সটা নামালেন, *tini bāksôṭā nāmālen*.

The causing partner of নামা, to get down: you নামো from a bus, and you নামাও a box from a shelf.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**নামানো** (*nāmāno*) is the causing form of **নামা**, "to get down", which CDIAL lists as Bengali *nāmā*, "to descend", under Sanskrit *namrá*, "bent, bowed" (CDIAL 6964).
