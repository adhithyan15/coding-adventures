## You'll want to know: बिन्दुः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C437-PRAVRSH437-04]; assesses=[] -->

**बिन्दुः** — *binduḥ* — "a drop".

> पत्रे जलस्य बिन्दुः अस्ति।
>
> *patre jalasya binduḥ asti.*
>
> There is a drop of water on the leaf.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**बिन्दुः** (*binduḥ*) is the word MW gives for a drop, a dot, a spot; MW gives no derivation, so its origin is uncertain; CDIAL 9240 records its later forms (MW; CDIAL 9240).
