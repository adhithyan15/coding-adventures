Kannada, chapter 343: How to Tell a Story.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ನಿರೂಪಿಸು (nirūpisu) — to narrate; to present.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to surrender, then the Kannada for a cannon.

You'll want to know: ನಿರೂಪಿಸು.
ನಿರೂಪಿಸು — nirūpisu — "to narrate; to present".
ಅಜ್ಜ ಯುದ್ಧದ ಕಥೆಯನ್ನು ಚೆನ್ನಾಗಿ ನಿರೂಪಿಸಿದರು. ajja yuddhada katheyannu cennāgi nirūpisidaru. Grandfather narrated the war story well.
Past (I …): ನಿರೂಪಿಸಿದೆ, nirūpiside.
What is told takes -ಅನ್ನು. A programme host is a ನಿರೂಪಕ.

The word, taken apart.
ನಿರೂಪಿಸು (nirūpisu) is built on Sanskrit nirūpaṇa, "stating, defining" (Monier-Williams), with the Kannada verb ending -isu.

Lesson 2 of 5.
ವರ್ಣಿಸು (varṇisu) — to describe vividly.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a cannon, then the Kannada for to narrate.

You'll want to know: ವರ್ಣಿಸು.
ವರ್ಣಿಸು — varṇisu — "to describe vividly".
ಅವಳು ಹಳ್ಳಿಯ ಜಾತ್ರೆಯನ್ನು ಸುಂದರವಾಗಿ ವರ್ಣಿಸಿದಳು. avaḷu haḷḷiya jātreyannu sundaravāgi varṇisidaḷu. She described the village fair beautifully.
Past (I …): ವರ್ಣಿಸಿದೆ, varṇiside.
ವಿವರಿಸು is to explain; ವರ್ಣಿಸು (varṇisu) is to paint in words.

The word, taken apart.
ವರ್ಣಿಸು (varṇisu) is built on Sanskrit varṇana, "description" (CDIAL 11340), from varṇa, "colour", with the Kannada verb ending -isu.

Lesson 3 of 5.
ಸನ್ನಿವೇಶ (sannivēśa) — a situation; a scene.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to narrate, then the Kannada for to describe vividly.

You'll want to know: ಸನ್ನಿವೇಶ.
ಸನ್ನಿವೇಶ — sannivēśa — "a situation; a scene".
ಆ ಸಿನಿಮಾದಲ್ಲಿ ಒಂದು ತಮಾಷೆಯ ಸನ್ನಿವೇಶ ಇದೆ. ā sinimādalli ondu tamāṣeya sannivēśa ide. There is a funny scene in that film.
Plural: ಸನ್ನಿವೇಶಗಳು, sannivēśagaḷu.
A scene in a story or a film, or a real situation: ಕಷ್ಟದ ಸನ್ನಿವೇಶ (sannivēśa).

The word, taken apart.
ಸನ್ನಿವೇಶ (sannivēśa) is borrowed from Sanskrit saṃniveśa, "entering together; a seat, a position, a situation" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 4 of 5.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ಹಿನ್ನೆಲೆ (hinnele) — a background.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a situation, then the Kannada for an episode.

You'll want to know: ಹಿನ್ನೆಲೆ.
ಹಿನ್ನೆಲೆ — hinnele — "a background".
ಈ ಕಥೆಯ ಹಿನ್ನೆಲೆ ಒಂದು ಹಳೆಯ ಯುದ್ಧ. ī katheya hinnele ondu haḷeya yuddha. This story's background is an old war.
ಹಿನ್, behind, and ನೆಲೆ, ground: what lies behind. ಹಿನ್ನೆಲೆ (hinnele) ಸಂಗೀತ is background music.

The word, taken apart.
ಹಿನ್ನೆಲೆ (hinnele) is a native Dravidian compound: hin, "behind" (DEDR 4205), and nele, "a footing, a place" (DEDR 3675).
