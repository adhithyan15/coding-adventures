# క్షేమం (kṣēmaṁ) — wellbeing, safety

## You'll want to know: క్షేమం
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C93-FINE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**క్షేమం** (*kṣēmaṁ*) — "wellbeing, safety".

**క్షేమంగా ఉన్నారా?** — "are you keeping well?", a warmer, more formal cousin of
**బాగున్నారా?**, heard from elders and read at the top of letters.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

A respectful way to ask after someone.
