Japanese, chapter 281: Word Gets Around.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
うわさ (uwasa) — a rumour; gossip.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for a reporter, then the Japanese for to hand out.

You'll want to know: うわさ.
うわさ — uwasa — "a rumour; gossip".
その うわさは ほんとうですか。 — sono uwasa wa hontō desu ka — "Is the rumour true?"

The word, taken apart.
うわさ (uwasa) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict).

Lesson 2 of 5.
つたわる (tsutawaru) — to be passed on; to spread (of news).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for to hand out, then the Japanese for a rumour.

You'll want to know: つたわる.
つたわる — tsutawaru — "to be passed on; to spread (of news)".
じけんは すぐ まちの みんなに つたわりました。 — jiken wa sugu machi no minna ni tsutawarimashita — "News of the incident soon reached the whole town."
Polite つたわります, tsutawarimasu; a godan verb. Past (polite): うわさが つたわりました。, uwasa ga tsutawarimashita.

The word, taken apart.
つたわる (tsutawaru) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict). It is the intransitive partner of tsutaeru, "to pass on".

Lesson 4 of 5.
わだい (wadai) — a topic of conversation.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for to be passed on, then the Japanese for to spread widely.

You'll want to know: わだい.
わだい — wadai — "a topic of conversation".
その じけんが わだいに なっています。 — sono jiken ga wadai ni natte imasu — "Everyone is talking about the incident."

The word, taken apart.
わだい (wadai) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are wa, "talk" (Middle Chinese hwaejH) and dai, "topic" (dej) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).

Lesson 5 of 5.
おおさわぎ (ōsawagi) — an uproar; a commotion.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for to spread widely, then the Japanese for a topic of conversation.

You'll want to know: おおさわぎ.
おおさわぎ — ōsawagi — "an uproar; a commotion".
えきで おおさわぎに なりました。 — eki de ōsawagi ni narimashita — "There was a great commotion at the station."

The word, taken apart.
おおさわぎ (ōsawagi) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, built from ō, "big", and sawagi, "noise" (from sawagu) (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
