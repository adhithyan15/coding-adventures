## You'll want to know: くぐる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C406-TORII406-02]; assesses=[] -->

**くぐる** — *kuguru* — "to pass under; to go through".

> **とりいを くぐって、じんじゃに はいりました。** — *torii o kugutte, jinja ni hairimashita* — "We passed under the gate and went into the shrine."

Polite **くぐります**, *kugurimasu*; a godan verb. Past (polite): **かんこうきゃくが とりいを くぐりました。**, *kankōkyaku ga torii o kugurimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**くぐる** (*kuguru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict).
