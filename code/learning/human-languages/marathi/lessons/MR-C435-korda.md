## You'll want to know: कोरडा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C435-TANCHAI435-03]; assesses=[] -->

**कोरडा** — *korḍā* — "parched; dry".

> या वर्षी विहीर कोरडी पडली.
>
> *yā varṣī vihīr korḍī paḍlī.*
>
> This year the well went dry.

कोरडा पडणे, to go dry; कोरडी, कोरडं for the other genders.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**कोरडा** (*korḍā*) is a Marathi adjective for land, wells or weather without water; its origin is not settled.
