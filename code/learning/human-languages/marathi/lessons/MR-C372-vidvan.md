## You'll want to know: विद्वान
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C372-SANSHODHAN372-03]; assesses=[] -->

**विद्वान** — *vidvān* — "a scholar; a learned person".

> आजोबा संस्कृतचे मोठे विद्वान होते.
>
> *ājobā saṁskṛtce moṭhe vidvān hote.*
>
> Grandfather was a great scholar of Sanskrit.

Masculine.

Also an adjective, learned: विद्वान माणूस.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**विद्वान** (*vidvān*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *vidvas*, "knowing, wise" (Monier-Williams). Its nominative is *vidvān*.
