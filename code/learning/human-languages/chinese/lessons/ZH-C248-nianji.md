## You'll want to know: niánjí
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C248-TERM248-02]; assesses=[] -->

*niánjí* — "year; grade (at school)".

> *wǒmen niánjí yǒu yìbǎi ge xuésheng* — There are a hundred students in our year.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*niánjí* is built from 年 'a year' and *jí* 'a rank', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *nen* and *kip* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'rank' and quotes an older dictionary for it, 'the order of silk threads'.
