## You'll want to know: अस्थि
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C34-LIMB-03]; assesses=[] -->

**अस्थि** (*asthi*) — "a bone".

**अस्थि** is short, neuter and very old — the same shape as *akṣi*, "eye", which you have already had.

Its cousins line up letter for letter: Latin *os*, *ossis*, behind *osseous* and *ossify*; Greek *osteon*, behind *osteopath*; Avestan *ast*; Hittite *hastai*. Hittite was written down three and a half thousand years ago, which makes this one of the oldest words you can still say out loud.
