## You'll want to know: عمارة
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C454-IMARA454-01]; assesses=[] -->

**عمارة** — *ʿimāra* — "a block of flats; architecture".

> نسكن في عمارة قديمة في وسط المدينة.
>
> *naskunu fī ʿimāra qadīma fī wasaṭ al-madīna.*
>
> We live in an old block of flats in the city centre.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**عمارة** (*ʿimāra*) is built on the native Arabic root *ʿ-m-r*, "to inhabit, to build up" (Lane).
