Marathi, chapter 519: Light at Home.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
विजेरी (vijerī) — a torch; a flashlight.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for automatic, then the Marathi for to spray.

You'll want to know: विजेरी.
विजेरी — vijerī — "a torch; a flashlight".
वीज गेली, म्हणून मी विजेरी लावली. vīj gelī, mhaṇūn mī vijerī lāvlī. The power went, so I switched on a torch.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
विजेरी (vijerī) is built on वीज, "lightning, electricity", inherited from Sanskrit vidyut (CDIAL 11742), with a Marathi ending.

Lesson 3 of 6.
उजेड (ujeḍ) — daylight; light.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a torch, then the Marathi for a chandelier.

You'll want to know: उजेड.
उजेड — ujeḍ — "daylight; light".
सकाळी खोलीत छान उजेड येतो. sakāḷī kholīt chān ujeḍ yeto. In the morning lovely light comes into the room.
Masculine.
उजेड (ujeḍ) येणे, light comes in; उजेड (ujeḍ) पडणे, light falls.

The word, taken apart.
उजेड (ujeḍ) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Marathi ujeḍ, "light", under Sanskrit uddyōta, "flashing, light" (CDIAL 2000).

Lesson 5 of 6.
उजळणे (ujaḷṇe) — to light up; to brighten.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for daylight, then the Marathi for darkness.

You'll want to know: उजळणे.
उजळणे — ujaḷṇe — "to light up; to brighten".
दिवे लागले आणि सगळा रस्ता उजळला. dive lāgle āṇi sagḷā rastā ujaḷlā. The lamps came on and the whole street lit up.
Past (he …): तो रस्ता उजळला, to rastā ujaḷlā.

The word, taken apart.
उजळणे (ujaḷṇe) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Marathi ujaḷṇẽ under Sanskrit ujjvalati, "blazes up, shines" (CDIAL 1671).
