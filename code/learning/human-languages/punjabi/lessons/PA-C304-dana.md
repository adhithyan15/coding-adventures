## You'll want to know: ਦਾਣਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C304-GROW304-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ਦਾਣਾ** — *dāṇā* — "a grain; a seed of grain".

> ਹਰ ਸਿੱਟੇ ਵਿੱਚ ਬਹੁਤ ਦਾਣੇ ਹਨ।
>
> *har siṭṭe vich bahut dāṇe han.*
>
> There are many grains in every ear.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਦਾਣਾ** (*dāṇā*) is a loan, usually traced to Persian *dāna*, "grain, seed".
