## You'll want to know: ईंधण
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C173-CHULHO173-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ईंधण** — *ī̃dhaṇ* — "firewood; fuel".

> ईंधण सूको है।
>
> *ī̃dhaṇ sūko hai.*
>
> The firewood is dry.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ईंधण** (*ī̃dhaṇ*) is inherited from Sanskrit *indhana*, "kindling, fuel"; CDIAL lists Hindi *ī̃dhan* and Gujarati *ĩdhaṇ* (CDIAL 1584). CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry.
