## You'll want to know: نسل
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C385-JAMEE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**نسل** (*nasl*) — "a generation".

> نسل جوان بیشتر با اینترنت آشناست.
>
> *nasl-e javân bishtar bâ internet âshenâst.*
>
> The young generation knows the internet better.

Plural *naslhâ*. Also descent: *az nasl-e* … — descended from … .

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**نسل** was borrowed from Arabic *nasl* "offspring, descendants" (EtymDB); in Persian it is a generation.
