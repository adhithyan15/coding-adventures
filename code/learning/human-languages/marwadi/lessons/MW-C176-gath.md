## You'll want to know: गांठ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C176-GANTH176-03]; assesses=[] -->

**गांठ** — *gā̃ṭh* — "a knot".

> गांठ घणी कड़क है।
>
> *gā̃ṭh ghaṇī kaṛak hai.*
>
> The knot is very tight.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**गांठ** (*gā̃ṭh*) is inherited from Sanskrit *granthi*, "a knot"; CDIAL lists Hindi and Gujarati *gā̃ṭh* (CDIAL 4354). CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry.
