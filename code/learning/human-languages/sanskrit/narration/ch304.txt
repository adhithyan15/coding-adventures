Lesson 1 of 5.
राशिः (rāśiḥ) — a heap; a pile.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for spreads through, then the Sanskrit for withers.

You'll want to know: राशिः.
राशिः — rāśiḥ — "a heap; a pile".
भग्नायाः भित्तेः राशिः मार्गे अस्ति। bhagnāyāḥ bhitteḥ rāśiḥ mārge asti. A heap from the broken wall lies in the road.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
राशिः (rāśiḥ) MW gives rāśi as a heap, mass or pile and calls its derivation doubtful, so its origin is uncertain (MW).

क्रोशति — krośati — "shouts; cries out".
सः नरः उच्चैः क्रोशति। saḥ naraḥ uccaiḥ krośati. The man shouts loudly.
Past (he …): सः उच्चैः क्रुष्टवान्, saḥ uccaiḥ kruṣṭavān.

The word, taken apart.
क्रोशति (krośati) comes from the root √kruś, "to cry out, shout"; MW compares Greek krazō (MW).

Lesson 4 of 5.
विलपति (vilapati) — laments; wails.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for in distress, then the Sanskrit for shouts.

You'll want to know: विलपति.
विलपति — vilapati — "laments; wails".
बालस्य माता विलपति। bālasya mātā vilapati. The boy's mother wails.
Past (she …): सा बहु विलपितवती, sā bahu vilapitavatī.

The word, taken apart.
विलपति (vilapati) is built from vi and the root √lap, "to talk, prate"; MW gives vi-lap as to utter moaning sounds, wail, lament (MW).

Lesson 5 of 5.
निःसारयति (niḥsārayati) — brings out; gets out.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for shouts, then the Sanskrit for laments.

You'll want to know: निःसारयति.
निःसारयति — niḥsārayati — "brings out; gets out".
जनाः तं नरं राशेः निःसारयन्ति। janāḥ taṃ naraṃ rāśeḥ niḥsārayanti. People bring the man out of the heap.
Past (he …): सः नरं निःसारितवान्, saḥ naraṃ niḥsāritavān.

The word, taken apart.
निःसारयति (niḥsārayati) is built from niḥ, "out", and the causative of the root √sṛ, "to go, run"; MW gives niḥ-sārayati as to cause to go out, turn out (MW).
