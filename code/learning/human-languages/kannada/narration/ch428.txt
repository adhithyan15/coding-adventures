Kannada, chapter 428: If and Otherwise.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಒಂದೊಮ್ಮೆ (ondomme) — if ever; should it happen.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to convert, then the Kannada for adequate.

You'll want to know: ಒಂದೊಮ್ಮೆ.
ಒಂದೊಮ್ಮೆ — ondomme — "if ever; should it happen".
ಒಂದೊಮ್ಮೆ ಬಸ್ ತಪ್ಪಿದರೆ ನಾನು ಆಟೋದಲ್ಲಿ ಬರುತ್ತೇನೆ. ondomme bas tappidare nānu āṭōdalli baruttēne. Should I miss the bus, I will come by auto.
It goes with a verb in -ದರೆ, as ಒಂದು ವೇಳೆ does.

The word, taken apart.
ಒಂದೊಮ್ಮೆ (ondomme) is native Dravidian: ondu, "one", and omme, "once" (DEDR 990): at some one time.

What you've built.
One more word for saying what happens if.

Lesson 2 of 5.
ಇಲ್ಲವೇ (illavē) — or else; or.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for adequate, then the Kannada for if ever.

You'll want to know: ಇಲ್ಲವೇ.
ಇಲ್ಲವೇ — illavē — "or else; or".
ಇಂದು ಇಲ್ಲವೇ ನಾಳೆ ಮಳೆ ಬರುತ್ತದೆ. indu illavē nāḷe maḷe baruttade. It will rain today or tomorrow.
Like ಅಥವಾ, but stronger: one or the other.

The word, taken apart.
ಇಲ್ಲವೇ (illavē) is native Dravidian: illa, "it is not", with -ē; DEDR 2559 lists the Kannada family of illa.

Lesson 3 of 5.
ಹೇಗಿದ್ದರೂ (hēgiddarū) — in any case; anyway.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for if ever, then the Kannada for or else.

You'll want to know: ಹೇಗಿದ್ದರೂ.
ಹೇಗಿದ್ದರೂ — hēgiddarū — "in any case; anyway".
ಹೇಗಿದ್ದರೂ ನಾನು ಪೇಟೆಗೆ ಹೋಗುತ್ತೇನೆ, ನಿನ್ನ ಪುಸ್ತಕ ತರುತ್ತೇನೆ. hēgiddarū nānu pēṭege hōguttēne, ninna pustaka taruttēne. I am going to town anyway, so I will bring your book.
ಹೇಗೆ (how) + ಇದ್ದರೂ (even if it is).

The word, taken apart.
ಹೇಗಿದ್ದರೂ (hēgiddarū) is native Dravidian: hēge, "how" (DEDR 5151), and iddarū, "even if it is": however it is.

Lesson 4 of 5.
ಅನ್ವಯ (anvaya) — applicability.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for or else, then the Kannada for in any case.

You'll want to know: ಅನ್ವಯ.
ಅನ್ವಯ — anvaya — "applicability".
ಈ ನಿಯಮ ಎಲ್ಲರಿಗೂ ಅನ್ವಯ ಆಗುತ್ತದೆ. ī niyama ellarigū anvaya āguttade. This rule applies to everyone.
ಅನ್ವಯ (anvaya) ಆಗು / ಅನ್ವಯಿಸು, to apply to.

The word, taken apart.
ಅನ್ವಯ (anvaya) is borrowed from Sanskrit anvaya, "connection, being linked to or concerned with" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 5 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: ಒಟ್ಟಾರೆ.
ಒಟ್ಟಾರೆ — oṭṭāre — "overall; on the whole".
ಒಟ್ಟಾರೆ ಪ್ರವಾಸ ಚೆನ್ನಾಗಿತ್ತು. oṭṭāre pravāsa cennāgittu. Overall the trip was good.

The word, taken apart.
ಒಟ್ಟಾರೆ (oṭṭāre) is native Dravidian, built on oṭṭu, "to come together; a mass, a union" (DEDR 958).
