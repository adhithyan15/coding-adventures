## You'll want to know: 运气
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C529-LUCK529-02]; assesses=[] -->

**运气** — *yùnqi* — "a person's luck (good or bad)".

> **运气好的话我们就能买到票** *yùnqi hǎo dehuà wǒmen jiù néng mǎi dào piào* — With luck, we'll be able to get tickets.

The new character, in pieces: 运 — seven strokes in two pieces: 云 first, four strokes; then the walking piece, three strokes, as in 这, along the left and the foot. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**运气** (*yùnqi*) is built from 运 and 气 'breath', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *hjunH* and *khj+jH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'far', 'to move' and 'to carry'. The sense 'luck' is not given there; the second is said light.
