## You'll want to know: عطیہ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C299-IMDAD299-05]; assesses=[] -->

**عطیہ** — *atiyā* — "a donation; a gift".

> اس نے کیمپ کو عطیہ دیا۔
>
> *us ne kaimp ko atiyā diyā.*
>
> He gave a donation to the camp.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**عطیہ** (*atiyā*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *ʿaṭiyya*, "a gift". It is masculine: **عطیہ دیا**.
