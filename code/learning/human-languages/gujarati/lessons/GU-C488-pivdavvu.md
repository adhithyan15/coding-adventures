## You'll want to know: પીવડાવવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C488-NAVDAV488-05]; assesses=[] -->

**પીવડાવવું** — *pīvḍāvvũ* — "to give someone a drink".

> નર્સે દર્દીને પાણી પીવડાવ્યું.
>
> *narse dardīne pāṇī pīvḍāvyũ.*
>
> The nurse gave the patient some water.

Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે બાળકને દૂધ પીવડાવ્યું, *teṇe bāḷakne dūdh pīvḍāvyũ*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**પીવડાવવું** (*pīvḍāvvũ*) is the causative of **પીવું** (*pīvũ*), "to drink", which is inherited from Sanskrit *pibati*, "drinks" (CDIAL 8209).
