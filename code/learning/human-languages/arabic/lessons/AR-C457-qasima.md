## You'll want to know: قسيمة
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C457-TAKHFID457-02]; assesses=[] -->

**قسيمة** — *qasīma* — "a voucher; a coupon".

> استعملت أمي قسيمة في المتجر.
>
> *istaʿmalat ummī qasīma fī al-matjar.*
>
> My mother used a voucher in the shop.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**قسيمة** (*qasīma*) is built on the native Arabic root *q-s-m*, "to divide" (Lane). It shares its root with *qism*, "a portion, a share" (Lane); the sense of a voucher or a coupon is modern.
