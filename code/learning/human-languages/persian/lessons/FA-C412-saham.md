## You'll want to know: سهام
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C412-SARMAYE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**سهام** (*sahâm*) — "shares, stock".

> او سهام یک شرکت بزرگ را خرید.
>
> *u sahâm-e yek sherkat-e bozorg râ kharid.*
>
> She bought shares in a big company.

The Arabic broken plural of *sahm* "a share, a portion".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**سهام** is an Arabic broken plural of *sahm* "part, portion", which EtymDB lists as the source of Persian *sahm*.
