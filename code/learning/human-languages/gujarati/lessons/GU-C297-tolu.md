## You'll want to know: ટોળું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C297-CHETAV297-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ટોળું** — *ṭoḷũ* — "a gathered crowd; a mob".

> અકસ્માત પછી ત્યાં લોકોનું મોટું ટોળું ભેગું થયું.
>
> *akasmāt pachī tyā̃ lokonũ moṭũ ṭoḷũ bhegũ thayũ.*
>
> After the accident a big crowd gathered there.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ટોળું** (*ṭoḷũ*) comes from *\*ṭōla*, "a division, a party", a form CDIAL reconstructs; CDIAL lists Gujarati *ṭoḷũ* there, glossed "a flock of sheep" (CDIAL 5483). Today it is mostly a crowd of people that gathers at one spot, where **ભીડ** is any crowd.

## What you've built

One more word for telling what happened.
