## You'll want to know: सिरदर्द
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C124-BODY-05]; assesses=[] -->

**सिरदर्द** (*sirdard*) — "a headache".

It is **सिर**, "head", and **दर्द**, "pain", both yours already. **मुझे सिरदर्द है** — "I have a
headache".
