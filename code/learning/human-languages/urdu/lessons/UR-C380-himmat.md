## You'll want to know: ہمت
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C380-MASHQ380-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ہمت** — *himmat* — "nerve; courage".

> اگر ہمت ہے تو آگے آئیے۔
>
> *agar himmat hai to āge āiye.*
>
> If you have the nerve, step forward.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ہمت** (*himmat*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *himma*, "resolve, ambition".
