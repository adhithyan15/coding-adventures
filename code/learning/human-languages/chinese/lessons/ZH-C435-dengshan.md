## You'll want to know: dēngshān
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C435-SUMMIT435-01]; assesses=[] -->

*dēngshān* — "to climb a mountain".

> *zhōumò wǒmen zuò huǒchē qù dēngshān* — At the weekend we're taking the train to go climbing.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*dēngshān* is built from *dēng* 'to ascend' and 山 'a mountain', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *tong* and *srean* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to rise' and 'to go up'.
