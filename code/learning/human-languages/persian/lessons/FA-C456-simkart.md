## You'll want to know: سیم کارت
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C456-PEYK-03]; assesses=[] -->

**سیم کارت** (*simkârt*) — "a SIM card".

> در فرودگاه یک سیم کارت تازه خریدم.
>
> *Dar forudgâh yek simkârt-e tâze kharidam.*
>
> At the airport I bought a new SIM card.

Written like native *sim* 'wire' + *kârt*, but taken from English.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*simkârt* was borrowed from English *SIM card*. It is spelled like native *sim* 'wire', which EtymDB traces to Middle Persian, and *kârt* 'card', from French *carte*.
