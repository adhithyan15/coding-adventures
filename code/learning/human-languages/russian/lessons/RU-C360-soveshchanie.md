## You'll want to know: совещание
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C360-SOVESHCHANIYE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**совещание** (*soveshchániye*) — "a meeting (at work)".

> Совещание начнётся в девять часов.
>
> *Soveshchániye nachnyótsya v dévyat chasóv.*
>
> The meeting will begin at nine o’clock.

Neuter. A meeting at work; a meeting of friends is *встреча*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**совещание** is built on *совещаться* "to confer", on *вещать* "to proclaim", the Church Slavonic form of the root of *ответ*, Old Russian *větŭ* "counsel". The *щ* is the Church Slavonic shape where Russian has *ч*.
