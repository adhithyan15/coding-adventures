## You'll want to know: die Tapete
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C529-RENOVIEREN-02]; assesses=[] -->

**die Tapete** — "wallpaper".

> Die alte Tapete im Flur ist hässlich.
>
> *The old wallpaper in the hall is ugly.*

Plural: *die Tapeten*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Tapete* comes from Medieval Latin *tapeta* "wall carpet" (EtymDB), from Latin *tapēte* "carpet".
