## You'll want to know: die Nachbarschaft
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C498-FREUNDE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**die Nachbarschaft** — "neighbourhood".

> In unserer Nachbarschaft gibt es einen schönen Park.
>
> *There's a nice park in our neighbourhood.*

Also the neighbours themselves: *Die ganze Nachbarschaft hat geholfen.*

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Nachbarschaft* is built on *Nachbar* "neighbour", inherited from Middle High German *nāchbūre* "one who lives near" (EtymDB), with *-schaft*.
