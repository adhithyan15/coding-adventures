# मम स्यूतः नष्टः (mama syūtaḥ naṣṭaḥ) — when things go wrong

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[SA-LEX-C281-AGRIMAH-01, SA-GRAMMAR-C276-PAST-TAVAT-02, SA-LEX-C232-ACT232-04] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "the next train". (*agrimaṃ dhūmayānam*.) Then say "is lost". (*naśyati*.) Now something has gone wrong.

## You'll want to know: lost
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-GRAMMAR-C281-PROBLEM-01]; assesses=[SA-LEX-C101-THINGS101-05, SA-LEX-C232-ACT232-04, SA-LEX-C172-TRV172-02, SA-LEX-C30-WELCOME-04] -->

> **मम स्यूतः नष्टः।**
>
> *mama syūtaḥ naṣṭaḥ.* — "My bag is lost."

**नष्टः** (*naṣṭaḥ*), "lost", is the *-ta* form of **नश्यति** (*naśyati*), "is lost". It describes, so it agrees with the thing: **स्यूतः** is masculine, so **नष्टः**.

> **मम यानपत्रं नष्टम्।**
>
> *mama yānapatraṃ naṣṭam.* — "My ticket is lost."

> **मम माला नष्टा।**
>
> *mama mālā naṣṭā.* — "My garland is lost."

## You'll want to know: missed, and ill
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-GRAMMAR-C281-PROBLEM-02]; assesses=[SA-LEX-C123-THINGS123-05, SA-LEX-C243-QUAL243-02, SA-LEX-SAHAYYAM-KAROTI-HELP, SA-LEX-C29-COURTESY-01] -->

> **धूमयानं गतम्।**
>
> *dhūmayānaṃ gatam.* — "The train has gone."

**गतम्** (*gatam*) is **गतः** for a neuter noun: the train is gone.

> **अहं रुग्णः अस्मि।**
>
> *ahaṃ rugṇaḥ asmi.* — "I am ill."

A woman says **अहं रुग्णा अस्मि** (*ahaṃ rugṇā asmi*). And to ask for help, the polite **-तु** on "does":

> **कृपया साहाय्यं करोतु।**
>
> *kṛpayā sāhāyyaṃ karotu.* — "Please help."
