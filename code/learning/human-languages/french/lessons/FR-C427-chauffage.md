## You'll want to know: le chauffage
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C427-CHAMBRE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**le chauffage** — "the heating".

> En hiver, le chauffage marche toute la journée.
>
> *In winter the heating runs all day.*

*Le chauffage central* — central heating.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*chauffage* is built on *chauffer* 'to heat' with *-age*. *Chauffer* is inherited from Latin *calefacere* 'to make warm', from *calēre* 'to be warm' (Lewis & Short).
