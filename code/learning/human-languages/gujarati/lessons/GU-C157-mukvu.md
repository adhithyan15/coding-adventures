## You'll want to know: મૂકવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C157-PLACE157-02]; assesses=[] -->

**મૂકવું** — *mūkvũ* — "to put, to place".

> **હું ચાવી કબાટમાં મૂકું છું.** — *hũ cāvī kabāṭmā̃ mūkũ chũ* — "I put the key in the cupboard."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**મૂકવું** (*mūkvũ*) is a tadbhava. It comes from *\*mukna*, "loosed", a reconstructed form beside Sanskrit *mukta*, "released"; CDIAL glosses the Gujarati verb "to let go, put, place" (CDIAL 10157).
