Sanskrit, chapter 264: What I will do next.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
करिष्यामि (kariṣyāmi) — I will do.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say “I do,” करोमि, then “I will go,” गमिष्यामि.

You'll want to know: करिष्यामि.
करिष्यामि — kariṣyāmi — means “I will do” or “I will make.” Keep the whole future form together: करिष्यामि.

Taken Apart.
करिष्यामि (kariṣyāmi) comes from the root कृ (kṛ), “do, make,” already met inside करोमि. The future stem appears as करिष्य- (kariṣya-), followed here by first-person -आमि. Whitney, Sanskrit Grammar §§932–935, describes the Sanskrit s-future and its -iṣya- / -sya- formation.

Guided Practice.
[pause 2 seconds after each]
[your turn — say: “I will do” — kariṣyāmi]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: “Tomorrow, I will do the work” — śvaḥ kāryaṃ kariṣyāmi]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: choose “I will go” — gamiṣyāmi; now “I will do” — kariṣyāmi]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
Say “I will do.” (करिष्यामि (kariṣyāmi).)


Lesson 2 of 5.
पठिष्यामि (paṭhiṣyāmi) — I will read.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Retrieve “I will go” and “I will do.” (Gamiṣyāmi; kariṣyāmi.)

You'll want to know: पठिष्यामि.
पठिष्यामि — paṭhiṣyāmi — means “I will read” or “I will study.” Learn the complete form; its future stem is given, not guessed.

Taken Apart.
पठिष्यामि (paṭhiṣyāmi) comes from the root पठ् (paṭh), “read, recite.” Its future here is पठिष्य- (paṭhiṣya-), followed by first-person -आमि. Whitney, Sanskrit Grammar §§932–935, describes the Sanskrit s-future and the -iṣya- / -sya- formation used here.

Guided Practice.
[pause 2 seconds after each]
[your turn — say: “I will read” — paṭhiṣyāmi]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: “Tomorrow, I will read” — śvaḥ paṭhiṣyāmi]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: “I will do; I will read” — kariṣyāmi; paṭhiṣyāmi]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
Say “I will read.” (पठिष्यामि (paṭhiṣyāmi).)


Lesson 3 of 5.
A small plan: when, then what I will do — choose a known future time and say what I will do.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Recall tomorrow, the day after tomorrow, and the three complete forms “I will go,” “I will do,” and “I will read.”

Grammar Lens: time first, plan second.
Make a near-term plan in two small choices: choose श्वः (śvaḥ) or परश्वः (paraśvaḥ), then choose one complete first-person future form.

Guided Practice.
[pause 2 seconds after each]
[your turn — say: “Tomorrow, I will read” — śvaḥ paṭhiṣyāmi]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: “The day after tomorrow, I will go” — paraśvaḥ gamiṣyāmi]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: “Tomorrow, I will do the work” — śvaḥ kāryaṃ kariṣyāmi]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What are the two choices in a small plan? (When; then what I will do.)


Lesson 4 of 5.
Three plans, no copied model — make near-term plans from English cues without a model.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Keep the speaker ending in every answer: this round is all “I.”

Guided Practice.
There is no Sanskrit model to copy. Build each answer from the English cue.
[pause 3 seconds after each]
[your turn — say: tomorrow + I will go — śvaḥ gamiṣyāmi]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: tomorrow + I will read — śvaḥ paṭhiṣyāmi]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: day after tomorrow + I will do the work — paraśvaḥ kāryaṃ kariṣyāmi]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
Give the three future forms only: I will go, I will do, I will read. (Gamiṣyāmi; kariṣyāmi; paṭhiṣyāmi.)


Lesson 5 of 5.
Say what you will do — independently say a near-term plan with a taught future form.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
This is a first-person singular checkpoint. Every answer tells your own near-term plan.

Guided Practice — independent retrieval.
[pause 3 seconds after each]
[your turn — say: You plan to travel tomorrow. Say when and what you will do. — śvaḥ gamiṣyāmi]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: You plan to study tomorrow. Say when and what you will do. — śvaḥ paṭhiṣyāmi]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: You plan to do the work the day after tomorrow. — paraśvaḥ kāryaṃ kariṣyāmi]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: Choose a different one of the three actions and make your own tomorrow plan.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Grammar Lens: the boundary.
These three complete forms make a useful first-person singular ramp. This is not a complete future paradigm. Second person, dual, and plural forms, and the future stems of other roots, wait for later lessons.

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
Without a model, say one true or imagined plan for tomorrow with गमिष्यामि, करिष्यामि (kariṣyāmi), or पठिष्यामि (paṭhiṣyāmi).
