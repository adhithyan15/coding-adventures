# કોડિયું (koḍiyũ) — a clay lamp

## Warm-up

## You'll want to know: કોડિયું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C504-KUMBHAR504-05]; assesses=[] -->

**કોડિયું** — *koḍiyũ* — "a clay lamp".

> દિવાળીમાં અમે બારીમાં કોડિયાં મૂકીએ છીએ.
>
> *divāḷīmā̃ ame bārīmā̃ koḍiyā̃ mūkīe chīe.*
>
> At Diwali we put clay lamps in the windows.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**કોડિયું** (*koḍiyũ*) is filed by CDIAL under Sanskrit *kuṭa*, "a water-pot, a pitcher", and CDIAL lists the Gujarati word as an earthen cup for oil and a wick (CDIAL 3227). The plural is **કોડિયાં**.
