## You'll want to know: प्रमाण
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C391-GHATNE391-04]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रमाण** — *pramāṇ* — "a proportion; a rate".

> शहरात अपघातांचं प्रमाण वाढलं आहे.
>
> *śahrāt apghātāñcaṁ pramāṇ vāḍhlaṁ āhe.*
>
> The rate of accidents in the city has gone up.

Neuter.

Also a standard: प्रमाणभाषा, the standard language.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रमाण** (*pramāṇ*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *pramāṇa*, "measure, standard, proof" (CDIAL 8723).
