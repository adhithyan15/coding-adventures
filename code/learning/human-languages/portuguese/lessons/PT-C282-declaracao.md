## You'll want to know: a declaração
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C282-AFIRMAR-01]; assesses=[] -->

**a declaração** — "the declaration, the public statement".

> O presidente fez uma declaração aos jornalistas.
>
> *The president made a statement to the journalists.*

*Fazer uma declaração* — to make a statement. Also a form you fill in.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*declaração* is a learned borrowing from Latin *dēclārātiō* "a making clear" (EtymDB), from *dēclārāre*, built on *clārus* "clear".
