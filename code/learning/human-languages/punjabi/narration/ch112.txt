Lesson 1 of 5.
ਕਲਾ (kalā) — an art.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for a habit, then the Punjabi for a skill.

You'll want to know: ਕਲਾ.
ਕਲਾ — kalā — "an art".

The word, taken apart.
ਕਲਾ (kalā) is Sanskrit kalā, "any practical art", taken over whole as a learned loan, a tatsama (CDIAL 2927). CDIAL takes the Sanskrit word itself from Dravidian. The inherited Punjabi form is kaḷ, "machine, art".

Lesson 3 of 5.
ਲਿਆਕਤ (liākat) — ability, merit.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for an art, then the Punjabi for a tool.

You'll want to know: ਲਿਆਕਤ.
ਲਿਆਕਤ — liākat — "ability, merit".

The word, taken apart.
ਲਿਆਕਤ (liākat) is a loan from Arabic liyāqa, "fitness, merit", which reached Punjabi through Persian. Its Arabic root is l-y-q (Lane).

Lesson 4 of 5.
ਜਿੱਤਣਾ (jittṇā) — to win.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for a tool, then the Punjabi for ability.

You'll want to know: ਜਿੱਤਣਾ.
ਜਿੱਤਣਾ — jittṇā — "to win".

The word, taken apart.
ਜਿੱਤਣਾ (jittṇā) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from jita, "won, conquered" (CDIAL 5224).
