## You'll want to know: ত্রাণ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C291-TRAN291-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ত্রাণ** — *trān* — "relief; aid".

> নৌকায় করে গ্রামে ত্রাণ এল।
>
> *noukāy kôre grāme trān elô.*
>
> Relief arrived in the village by boat.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ত্রাণ** (*trān*) is Sanskrit *trāṇa*, 'protecting, shelter, help' (Monier-Williams); in the news it is the food and goods sent to the victims of a disaster.
