# आप बोलो हो — what you do

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MW-GRAMMAR-C154-PRES-3SG-01, MW-GRAMMAR-C153-BE-HO-01, MW-SCRIPT-KARO-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say *mother listens to songs*. Then *you are here*, and the request
*do it*, **करो**.

## Grammar Lens: -ो and हो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-GRAMMAR-C154-PRES-POLITE-01]; assesses=[MW-GRAMMAR-C153-BE-HO-01, MW-SCRIPT-KARO-01, MW-SCRIPT-WELLBEING-QUESTION-01] -->

After **आप**, the stem takes **-ो**, the ending of the requests **करो** and
**लावो**, and the sentence ends on **हो**:

> **आप थोड़ु बोलो हो।**
>
> *āp thoṛu bolo ho.* — You speak a little.

> **आप कांई करो हो?**
>
> *āp kāĩ karo ho?* — What do you do?

**करो** alone asks someone to do something. **करो हो** after **आप** says what they
do.
