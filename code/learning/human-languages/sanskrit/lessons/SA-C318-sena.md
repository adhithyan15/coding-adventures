## You'll want to know: सेना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C318-SENA318-01]; assesses=[] -->

**सेना** — *senā* — "an army".

> सेना नगरात् निर्गच्छति।
>
> *senā nagarāt nirgacchati.*
>
> The army marches out of the city.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सेना** (*senā*) comes from the root √si, "to bind", by MW's account; its oldest sense is a missile or spear, and its usual sense an army, an armed force (MW).
