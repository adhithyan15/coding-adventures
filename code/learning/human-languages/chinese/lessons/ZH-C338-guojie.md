## You'll want to know: 过节
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C338-VISIT338-01]; assesses=[] -->

**过节** — *guòjié* — "to celebrate a festival".

> **去年我们回老家过节** *qùnián wǒmen huí lǎojiā guòjié* — Last year we went back to our home town for the festival.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**过节** (*guòjié*) is built from 过 'to pass' and 节 'a festival', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *kwaH* and *tset* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to go beyond' and the second 'a joint of bamboo'. The sense 'a festival' is not given there.
