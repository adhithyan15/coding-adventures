## You'll want to know: chēxiāng
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C402-STATION402-05]; assesses=[] -->

*chēxiāng* — "railway carriage".

> *wǒmen zuò zài sān hào chēxiāng* — We're sitting in carriage three.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*chēxiāng* is built from 车 'vehicle' and *xiāng* 'a side room', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *tsyhae* and *sjang* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'a gallery' and the rooms east and west of a hall.
