## You'll want to know: पल्ली
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C421-PALLI421-01]; assesses=[] -->

**पल्ली** — *pallī* — "a hamlet".

> पर्वतस्य अधः एका पल्ली अस्ति।
>
> *parvatasya adhaḥ ekā pallī asti.*
>
> Below the mountain there is a hamlet.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पल्ली** (*pallī*) is the word MW gives for a small village; CDIAL, after T. Burrow, traces it to Dravidian, and DEDR 4018 sets Tamil *paḷḷi* and Kannada *haḷḷi*, "a hamlet", in one family, so it is usually taken as a loan from Dravidian (MW; CDIAL 7972; DEDR 4018).
