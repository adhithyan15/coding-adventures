## You'll want to know: എഞ്ചിൻ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C471-VANDI471-01]; assesses=[] -->

**എഞ്ചിൻ** — *eñcin* — "an engine".

> വണ്ടിയുടെ എഞ്ചിൻ വളരെ പഴയതാണ്.
>
> *vaṇṭiyuṭe eñcin vaḷare paḻayatāṇŭ.*
>
> The vehicle's engine is very old.

A train engine is also an എഞ്ചിൻ.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**എഞ്ചിൻ** (*eñcin*) was borrowed from English *engine*, the machine that drives a vehicle.
