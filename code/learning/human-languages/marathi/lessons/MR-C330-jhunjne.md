## You'll want to know: झुंजणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C330-SENAPATI330-05]; assesses=[] -->

**झुंजणे** — *jhuñjṇe* — "to battle; to fight hard".

> मूठभर सैनिक मोठ्या सैन्याशी झुंजले.
>
> *mūṭhbhar sainik moṭhyā sainyāśī jhuñjle.*
>
> A handful of soldiers battled a large army.

Past (he …): तो झुंजला, *to jhuñjlā*.

The opponent takes -शी: शत्रूशी झुंजणे.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**झुंजणे** (*jhuñjṇe*) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Marathi *jhuñjṇe*, 'to fight', under Sanskrit *yudhyate*, 'fights against' (CDIAL 10502).
