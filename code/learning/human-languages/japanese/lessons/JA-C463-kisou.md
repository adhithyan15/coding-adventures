## You'll want to know: きそう
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C463-TRAIN463-05]; assesses=[] -->

**きそう** — *kisou* — "to compete; to vie".

> **ふたりの せんしゅが ゆうしょうを きそっています。** — *futari no senshu ga yūshō o kisotte imasu* — "Two players are competing for the championship."

Polite **きそいます**, *kisoimasu*; a godan verb. Past (polite): **がくせいが てんを きそいました。**, *gakusei ga ten o kisoimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**きそう** (*kisou*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji spelling was attached to it later (JMdict).
