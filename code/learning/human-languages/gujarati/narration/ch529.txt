Gujarati, chapter 529: One Stage at a Time.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
તબક્કો (tabakko) — a stage (of a process).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a diary, then the Gujarati for to organise.

You'll want to know: તબક્કો.
તબક્કો — tabakko — "a stage (of a process)".
જો પહેલો તબક્કો સારો જશે તો આપણે બીજો તબક્કો શરૂ કરીશું. jo pahelo tabakko sāro jaśe to āpaṇe bījo tabakko śarū karīśũ. If the first stage goes well, we will start the second stage.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
તબક્કો (tabakko) is usually traced to Arabic ṭabaqa, "a layer, a stage", through Persian.

Lesson 2 of 5.
ક્રમ (kram) — a sequence.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to organise, then the Gujarati for a stage (of a process).

You'll want to know: ક્રમ.
ક્રમ — kram — "a sequence".
જો તમે આ ક્રમ બદલશો તો બધું બગડી જશે. jo tame ā kram badalśo to badhũ bagḍī jaśe. If you change this order, everything will go wrong.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ક્રમ (kram) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit krama, "a step; going, a course" (Monier-Williams): one step after another.

Lesson 4 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a sequence, then the Gujarati for an estimate.

You'll want to know: અધૂરું.
અધૂરું — adhūrũ — "half-finished".
જો કામ અધૂરું રહેશે તો સાહેબ નારાજ થશે. jo kām adhūrũ raheśe to sāheb nārāj thaśe. If the work is left half-finished, the boss will be displeased.

The word, taken apart.
અધૂરું (adhūrũ) comes from ardhapūraka, "half-filled", a form CDIAL reconstructs, and CDIAL lists the Gujarati word as "half-done" (CDIAL 664).

Lesson 5 of 5.
અંતિમ (antim) — final.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for an estimate, then the Gujarati for half-finished.

You'll want to know: અંતિમ.
અંતિમ — antim — "final".
જો અંતિમ દિવસે વરસાદ પડશે તો આપણે મેળો બંધ રાખીશું. jo antim divse varsād paḍśe to āpaṇe meḷo bandh rākhīśũ. If it rains on the final day, we will keep the fair closed.

The word, taken apart.
અંતિમ (antim) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit antima, "last" (Monier-Williams), built on anta, "the end", the word behind અંત.
