# वितळणे (vitaḷṇe) — to melt

## You'll want to know: वितळणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C437-GARVA437-05]; assesses=[] -->

**वितळणे** — *vitaḷṇe* — "to melt".

> उन्हात बर्फ लवकर वितळतो.
>
> *unhāt barpha lavkar vitaḷto.*
>
> Ice melts quickly in the sun.

Past (he …): तो वितळला, *to vitaḷlā*.

Ice, butter and wax वितळतात.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**वितळणे** (*vitaḷṇe*) is a Marathi verb for something solid turning liquid; its origin is not settled.
