## You'll want to know: ಕಸ್ಟಮ್ಸ್
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C439-GADI439-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ಕಸ್ಟಮ್ಸ್** — *kasṭams* — "customs".

> ಕಸ್ಟಮ್ಸ್ ಅಧಿಕಾರಿಗಳು ನಮ್ಮ ಚೀಲಗಳನ್ನು ತೆರೆದರು.
>
> *kasṭams adhikārigaḷu namma cīlagaḷannu teredaru.*
>
> The customs officers opened our bags.

The office at a border or an airport that checks goods.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಕಸ್ಟಮ್ಸ್** (*kasṭams*) is a loan from English *customs*, the duties charged on goods and the office that collects them.
