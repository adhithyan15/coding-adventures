Portuguese, chapter 352: Como reagir.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
a reação — the reaction.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for to entail, then the Portuguese for to trigger.

You'll want to know: a reação.
a reação — "the reaction".
A reação dele foi uma surpresa para todos. His reaction was a surprise to everyone.
AO90 reação; older reacção.

The word, taken apart.
reação is a learned word modelled on French réaction (EtymDB), from Medieval Latin reactiō, built on re- "back" and agere "to do, to act" (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 3 of 5.
o pretexto — the pretext.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for the reaction, then the Portuguese for to react.

You'll want to know: o pretexto.
o pretexto — "the pretext".
Ela usou o trabalho como pretexto para não ir. She used work as a pretext for not going.
Sob o pretexto de — on the pretext of.

The word, taken apart.
pretexto is a learned borrowing from Latin praetextus "an outward appearance, a show" (EtymDB, Lewis & Short), from praetexere "to weave in front", built on texere "to weave".

Lesson 4 of 5.
a desculpa — the excuse; the apology.

You'll want to know: a desculpa.
a desculpa — "the excuse; the apology".
Ele chegou tarde e deu uma desculpa estranha. He arrived late and gave a strange excuse.
Pedir desculpa — to apologise; desculpa! — sorry!

The word, taken apart.
desculpa is built on the verb desculpar "to excuse", from des- and culpa "fault, blame", from Latin culpa "fault" (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 5 of 5.
justificar — to justify.

You'll want to know: justificar.
justificar — "to justify".
Ele tentou justificar a decisão. He tried to justify the decision.
Justificar-se — to explain yourself.

The word, taken apart.
justificar is a learned borrowing from Late Latin iūstificāre "to act justly, to justify" (EtymDB), built on iūstus "fair, righteous" and facere "to make" (Lewis & Short).
