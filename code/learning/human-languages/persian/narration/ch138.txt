Persian, chapter 138: Next, From Now On, a Plan, To Decide, To Hope.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
آینده (âyande) — next, coming; the future.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for once, then the Persian for to spend.

You'll want to know: آینده.
آینده — âyande — "next, coming; the future". The future uses خواهم with the verb's past stem (رفت from رفتن): هفته آینده به مشهد خواهم رفت — hafte-ye âyande be mashhad khâham raft — "Next week I will go to Mashhad."

The word, taken apart.
âyande is ây-, the present stem of âmadan, "to come" (EtymDB), with -ande, "-ing": "coming". The verb is inherited.

Lesson 2 of 5.
از حالا (az hâlâ) — from now on.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to spend, then the Persian for next.

You'll want to know: از حالا.
از حالا — az hâlâ — "from now on". از حالا هر روز ورزش خواهم کرد — az hâlâ har ruz varzesh khâham kard — "From now on I will exercise every day."

The word, taken apart.
az hâlâ is "from now". az, "from", is native: Old Persian had hacā (EtymDB). hâlâ, "now", is borrowed from Arabic ḥālan (root ḥ-w-l, Lane).

Lesson 3 of 5.
طرح (tarh) — a plan, a design.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for next, then the Persian for from now on.

You'll want to know: طرح.
طرح — tarh — "a plan, a design". یک طرح خوب داریم — yek tarh-e khub dârim — "We have a good plan."

The word, taken apart.
tarh is borrowed from Arabic ṭarḥ, "throwing, laying down". Its Arabic root is ṭ-r-ḥ (Lane). Persian uses it for a plan or design.

Lesson 4 of 5.
تصمیم گرفتن (tasmim gereftan) — to decide.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for from now on, then the Persian for a plan.

You'll want to know: تصمیم گرفتن.
تصمیم گرفتن — tasmim gereftan — "to decide". تصمیم گرفتم ورزش کنم — tasmim gereftam varzesh konam — "I decided to exercise."

The word, taken apart.
tasmim gereftan pairs tasmim, borrowed from Arabic taṣmīm, "resolve" (root ṣ-m-m, Lane), with the Persian verb gereftan, "to take". gereftan, "to take", is an inherited verb; EtymDB takes it back to the Indo-European root gʰrebh₂-, "to seize".

Lesson 5 of 5.
امیدوار بودن (omidvâr budan) — to hope.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a plan, then the Persian for to decide.

You'll want to know: امیدوار بودن.
امیدوار بودن — omidvâr budan — "to hope". امیدوارم حالت خوب باشد — omidvâram hâlat khub bâshad — "I hope you are well."

The word, taken apart.
omidvâr budan is "to be hopeful": omid, "hope", with -vâr, "having", and budan, "to be". All are native; omid is usually traced to Middle Persian ummēd.
