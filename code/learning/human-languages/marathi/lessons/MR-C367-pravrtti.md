## You'll want to know: प्रवृत्ती
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C367-PRATHA367-04]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रवृत्ती** — *pravṛttī* — "a tendency".

> प्रश्न टाळण्याची त्याची प्रवृत्ती आहे.
>
> *praśna ṭāḷaṇyācī tyācī pravṛttī āhe.*
>
> He has a tendency to avoid questions.

Feminine.

X करण्याची प्रवृत्ती, a tendency to do X.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रवृत्ती** (*pravṛttī*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *pravṛtti*, "moving forward; inclination, tendency" (Monier-Williams).
