## You'll want to know: हुलिया
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C482-KAD482-02]; assesses=[] -->

**हुलिया** — *huliyā* — "appearance; looks".

> दस साल बाद दोस्त का हुलिया बिलकुल बदल गया था।
>
> *das sāl bād dost kā huliyā bilkul badal gayā thā.*
>
> Ten years on, my friend's looks had changed completely.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**हुलिया** (*huliyā*) is usually traced to Arabic *ḥulya*, "ornament", and so "a description of someone's features", which reached Hindi through Persian.
