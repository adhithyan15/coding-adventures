# ಅವಳಿ (avaḷi) — twins

## You'll want to know: ಅವಳಿ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C526-BALAGA526-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ಅವಳಿ** — *avaḷi* — "twins".

> ನನ್ನ ಅಕ್ಕನಿಗೆ ಅವಳಿ ಮಕ್ಕಳು ಇದ್ದಾರೆ.
>
> *nanna akkanige avaḷi makkaḷu iddāre.*
>
> My elder sister has twins.

ಅವಳಿ-ಜವಳಿ, a pair of twins.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಅವಳಿ** (*avaḷi*) is the Kannada word for twins; its origin is not settled.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
