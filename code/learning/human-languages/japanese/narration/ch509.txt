Lesson 1 of 6.
たからくじ (takarakuji) — a lottery; a lottery ticket.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for luckily, then the Japanese for fate.

You'll want to know: たからくじ.
たからくじ — takarakuji — "a lottery; a lottery ticket".
もし たからくじが あたったら、うちを かいます。 — moshi takarakuji ga atattara, uchi o kaimasu — "If I win the lottery, I will buy a house."

The word, taken apart.
たからくじ (takarakuji) is a native Japanese compound (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese: built from takara, "treasure" and kuji, "lot" (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).

Lesson 3 of 6.
ちゅうせんする (chūsen suru) — to hold a draw; to draw lots.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for a lottery, then the Japanese for drawing lots.

You'll want to know: ちゅうせんする.
ちゅうせんする — chūsen suru — "to hold a draw; to draw lots".
もし せんにん きたら、ちゅうせんします。 — moshi sennin kitara, chūsenshimasu — "If a thousand people come, we will hold a draw."
Polite ちゅうせんします, chūsenshimasu; a する verb. Past (polite): みせが ちゅうせんしました。, mise ga chūsenshimashita.

The word, taken apart.
ちゅうせんする (chūsen suru) is the Sino-Japanese (kango) noun chūsen with the native verb suru, "to do": two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are chū, "pluck" (Middle Chinese trhjuw) and sen, "elect" (sjwenX) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).

Lesson 5 of 6.
あてる (ateru) — to hit (the mark); to guess right.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for to hold a draw, then the Japanese for probability.

You'll want to know: あてる.
あてる — ateru — "to hit (the mark); to guess right".
もし こたえを あてたら、おかしを かいます。 — moshi kotae o atetara, okashi o kaimasu — "If you guess the answer, I will buy you some sweets."
Polite あてます, atemasu; an ichidan verb. Past (polite): おとうとが こたえを あてました。, otōto ga kotae o atemashita.

The word, taken apart.
あてる (ateru) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi, and its older origin is not settled (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
