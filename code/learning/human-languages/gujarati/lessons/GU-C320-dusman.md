## You'll want to know: દુશ્મન
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C320-YUDDH320-04]; assesses=[] -->

**દુશ્મન** — *duśman* — "an enemy".

> દુશ્મને રાત્રે કિલ્લો ઘેર્યો.
>
> *duśmane rātre killo gheryo.*
>
> The enemy surrounded the fort at night.

A masculine or feminine noun, by the person meant.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**દુશ્મન** (*duśman*) is usually traced to Persian *dušman*, "an enemy". As the doer it takes **એ**: **દુશ્મને કિલ્લો ઘેર્યો**.
