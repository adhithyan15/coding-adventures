## You'll want to know: inopīnātus, inopīnāta, inopīnātum
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C375-EXSISTO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**inopīnātus, inopīnāta, inopīnātum** — "unexpected".

> Inopīnātum perīculum nōs terruit.
>
> *An unexpected danger frightened us.*

*inopīnātus, inopīnāta, inopīnātum*: *in-* not + the participle of *opīnor*: not supposed. *Ex inopīnātō*, unexpectedly.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*inopīnātus* is built from *in-* "not" and *opīnātus*, the participle of *opīnor* "to suppose" (Lewis & Short): not supposed beforehand.
