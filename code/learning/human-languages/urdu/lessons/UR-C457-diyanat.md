## You'll want to know: دیانت
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C457-TRUST457-04]; assesses=[] -->

**دیانت** — *diyānat* — "honesty; integrity".

> دکاندار کی دیانت پر سب کو بھروسا ہے۔
>
> *dukāndār kī diyānat par sab ko bharosā hai.*
>
> Everyone trusts the shopkeeper's honesty.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**دیانت** (*diyānat*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *diyāna*, "religion, uprightness".
