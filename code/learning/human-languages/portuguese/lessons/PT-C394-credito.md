## You'll want to know: o crédito
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C394-GARANTIA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**o crédito** — "the credit; the loan".

> Pedimos um crédito ao banco para comprar casa.
>
> *We asked the bank for a loan to buy a house.*

*Crédito à habitação* — mortgage. *A crédito* — on credit.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*crédito* came through Italian *credito* (EtymDB), from Latin *crēditum* "a loan", on *crēdere* "to trust, to lend" (Lewis & Short).
