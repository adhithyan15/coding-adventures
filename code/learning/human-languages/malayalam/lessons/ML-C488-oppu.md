## You'll want to know: ഒപ്പ്
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C488-REKHA488-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ഒപ്പ്** — *oppŭ* — "a signature".

> ഫോമിന്റെ താഴെ നിങ്ങളുടെ ഒപ്പ് വേണം.
>
> *phōminṟe tāḻe niṅṅaḷuṭe oppŭ vēṇaṁ.*
>
> Your signature is needed at the bottom of the form.

You met it in ഒപ്പിടുക, to sign: to put your ഒപ്പ്.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ഒപ്പ്** (*oppŭ*) is native Dravidian; the root means to agree or match (DEDR 924), and a signature is the mark of your agreement.
