Punjabi, chapter 348: Instruments I Like.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ਸਾਜ਼ (sāz) — a musical instrument.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for a drumbeat, then the Punjabi for a clap.

You'll want to know: ਸਾਜ਼.
ਸਾਜ਼ — sāz — "a musical instrument".
ਮੈਨੂੰ ਹਰ ਸਾਜ਼ ਪਸੰਦ ਹੈ। mainū̃ har sāz pasand hai. I like every musical instrument.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ਸਾਜ਼ (sāz) is a loan, usually traced to Persian sāz, "instrument".

Lesson 3 of 5.
ਧੁਨ (dhun) — a tune; a melody.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for a musical instrument, then the Punjabi for a tabla.

You'll want to know: ਧੁਨ.
ਧੁਨ — dhun — "a tune; a melody".
ਉਸ ਗੀਤ ਦੀ ਧੁਨ ਬਹੁਤ ਮਿੱਠੀ ਹੈ। us gīt dī dhun bahut miṭṭhī hai. The tune of that song is very sweet.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ਧੁਨ (dhun) is inherited from Sanskrit dhuni, "roaring, boisterous"; CDIAL lists Punjabi dhun, "tune", under it (CDIAL 6823).

Lesson 5 of 5.
ਗੂੰਜਣਾ (gūnjṇā) — to echo; to ring out.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for a tune, then the Punjabi for music practice.

You'll want to know: ਗੂੰਜਣਾ.
ਗੂੰਜਣਾ — gūnjṇā — "to echo; to ring out".
ਤਬਲੇ ਦੀ ਆਵਾਜ਼ ਕਮਰੇ ਵਿੱਚ ਗੂੰਜਦੀ ਹੈ। table dī āvāz kamre vich gūnjdī hai. The sound of the tabla rings out in the room.
Past (it …): ਉਹ ਗੀਤ ਕਮਰੇ ਵਿੱਚ ਗੂੰਜਿਆ, oh gīt kamre vich gūnjiā.

The word, taken apart.
ਗੂੰਜਣਾ (gūnjṇā) is inherited from Sanskrit guñjati, "hums, buzzes"; CDIAL lists Punjabi gū̃jṇā, "to hum, buzz, growl", under it (CDIAL 4175).
