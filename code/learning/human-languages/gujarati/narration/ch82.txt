Gujarati, chapter 82: Seventeen to Forty.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
સત્તર (sattar) — seventeen.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for fifteen, then the Gujarati for sixteen.

You'll want to know: સત્તર.
સત્તર (sattar) — "seventeen".

What you've built.
One more word for seventeen to forty.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: sattar]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: sattar, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say sattar]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Gujarati for fifteen, then the Gujarati for sixteen, then say sattar again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does સત્તર (sattar) mean? ("Seventeen".) Say it once more.
