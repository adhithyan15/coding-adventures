Urdu, chapter 62: School bag, Money, Map, Lesson, Question.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
بستہ (bastā) — a school bag.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a newspaper, then the Urdu for a school.

You'll want to know: بستہ.
بستہ — bastā — "a school bag".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: bastā]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: bastā, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say bastā]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Urdu for a newspaper, then the Urdu for a school, then say bastā again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does بستہ (bastā) mean? ("A school bag".) Say it once more.
