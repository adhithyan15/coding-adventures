## You'll want to know: ਹਿੱਲਣਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C283-ROAD283-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ਹਿੱਲਣਾ** — *hillṇā* — "to shake; to sway".

> ਪੁਰਾਣੀ ਬੱਸ ਬਹੁਤ ਹਿੱਲਦੀ ਹੈ।
>
> *purāṇī bass bahut hilldī hai.*
>
> The old bus sways a lot.

Past (it …): ਉਹ ਪੁਲ ਹਿੱਲਿਆ, *oh pul hilliā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਹਿੱਲਣਾ** (*hillṇā*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*hillati*, "moves, shakes"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *hillṇā*, "to shake, be moved", under it (CDIAL 14120).
