## You'll want to know: நதி
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C53-LAND-01]; assesses=[] -->

**நதி** (*nadi*) — "a river".

Sanskrit *nadī*, and Telugu prints it identically: నది. Malayalam did not take it for the everyday river and says പുഴ (*puḻa*) instead, which is its own.

Tamil's inherited river is ஆறு — the same shape as the six you already count with. Which of the two is meant comes out of what sits around it, and நதி is the one that leaves no room for doubt.
