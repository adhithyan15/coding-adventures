Hindi, chapter 126: What Did You Say?.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
फिर से (phir se) — once more, over again.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for I don't feel like it, then the Hindi for fun.

You'll want to know: फिर से.
फिर से (phir se) — "once more", "from the start again".
फिर से (phir se) बोलिए — "please say it again", a softer cousin of दोबारा.

What you've built.
A second way to ask again.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: phir se]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: phir se, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say phir se bolie]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Hindi for I don't feel like it, then the Hindi for fun, then say phir se again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does फिर से (phir se) mean? ("Once more, over again".) Say it once more.


Lesson 2 of 5.
समझ नहीं आया (samajh nahīṁ āyā) — I didn't understand.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for fun, then the Hindi for once more.

You'll want to know: समझ नहीं आया.
समझ नहीं आया (samajh nahīṁ āyā) — "I didn't understand", literally "understanding did not come".
समझ is "understanding", from समझना. It comes, or it doesn't.

What you've built.
The honest sentence every learner needs.

Lesson 3 of 5.
क्या कहा? (kyā kahā?) — what did you say?

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for once more, then the Hindi for I didn't understand.

You'll want to know: क्या कहा?
क्या कहा? (kyā kahā?) — "what did you say?".
कहा is the past of कहना, "to say". Short, and fine among friends; with a stranger, add जी.

What you've built.
A quick request to hear it again.

Guided Practice.

[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: kyā kahā?, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: ask kyā kahā?]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Hindi for once more, then the Hindi for I didn't understand, then say kyā kahā? again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does क्या कहा? (kyā kahā?) mean? ("What did you say?".) Say it once more.


Lesson 4 of 5.
मुझे नहीं पता (mujhe nahīṁ patā) — I don't know.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for I didn't understand, then the Hindi for what did you say.

You'll want to know: मुझे नहीं पता.
मुझे नहीं पता (mujhe nahīṁ patā) — "I don't know", literally "to me there is no knowing".
The person sits in मुझे, the way it does for wanting and liking.

What you've built.
An honest answer to a question you cannot answer.

Lesson 5 of 5.
एक बार और (ek bār aur) — one more time.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for what did you say, then the Hindi for I don't know.

You'll want to know: एक बार और.
एक बार और (ek bār aur) — "one more time".
एक, "one", बार, "time", और, "more". एक बार और (ek bār aur) बोलिए — "say it one more time".
