## You'll want to know: buzinar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C403-VIDROS-04]; assesses=[] -->

**buzinar** — "to honk, to sound the horn".

> Os carros buzinam quando o semáforo fica verde.
>
> *The cars honk when the lights turn green.*

Built on *a buzina*, the horn.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*buzinar* is built on *buzina* 'horn', which is inherited from Latin *būcina* 'trumpet' (Lewis & Short).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.
