## You'll want to know: निर्माणम्
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C508-NIRMANA508-03]; assesses=[] -->

**निर्माणम्** — *nirmāṇam* — "construction".

> नगरे नवस्य सेतोः निर्माणं भवति।
>
> *nagare navasya setoḥ nirmāṇaṃ bhavati.*
>
> A new bridge is being built in the city.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**निर्माणम्** (*nirmāṇam*) is built on *nis*, "out", and the root √mā, "to measure, to form"; MW gives *nirmāṇa* as forming, making, building (MW).
