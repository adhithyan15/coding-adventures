Spanish, chapter 490: Buscar trabajo.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
el empleo — the job, employment.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for the town hall, then the Spanish for the postbox.

You'll want to know: el empleo.
el empleo — "the job, employment".
Busco empleo como cocinero. I am looking for a job as a cook.
Oficina de empleo — job centre.

What you've built.
One more word for reading notices, forms and messages.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: el empleo]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: el empleo, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: a sentence of your own with el empleo]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Spanish for the town hall, then the Spanish for the postbox, then say el empleo again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does el empleo mean? ("The job, employment".) Say it once more.
