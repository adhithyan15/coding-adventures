## You'll want to know: il confetto
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C318-RICEVIMENTO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**il confetto** — "the sugared almond".

> A ogni invitato diamo cinque confetti.
>
> *We give each guest five sugared almonds.*

White for weddings, pink or blue for a birth. In Italian *confetti* are sweets, not paper.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*confetto* is inherited from Latin *cōnfectus* "made, prepared" (EtymDB), from *cōnficere*.
