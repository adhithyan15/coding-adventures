## You'll want to know: بند
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C409-QANUN409-03]; assesses=[] -->

**بند** — *band* — "a clause".

> قرأ المحامي كل بند في العقد.
>
> *qaraʾa al-muḥāmī kulla band fī al-ʿaqd.*
>
> The lawyer read every clause in the contract.

Plural: بنود, *bunūd*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**بند** (*band*) was borrowed from Persian *band*, "a tie, a bond" (EtymDB). In Arabic it is an article or clause of a text.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
