## You'll want to know: سرعة
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C437-BUSALA437-02]; assesses=[] -->

**سرعة** — *surʿa* — "speed".

> كانت سرعة القطار كبيرة.
>
> *kānat surʿat al-qiṭār kabīra.*
>
> The train's speed was high.

*bi-surʿa* means quickly.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**سرعة** (*surʿa*) is built on the native Arabic root *s-r-ʿ*, "to be quick" (Lane).
