# स्वीकार करना (svīkār karnā) — to admit, to own up to

## You'll want to know: स्वीकार करना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C349-INKAR349-04]; assesses=[] -->

**स्वीकार करना** — *svīkār karnā* — "to admit, to own up to".

> अरुण ने अपनी ग़लती स्वीकार की।
>
> *Aruṇ ne apnī ġaltī svīkār kī.*
>
> Arun admitted his mistake.

Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने ग़लती स्वीकार की, *usne ġaltī svīkār kī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**स्वीकार करना** (*svīkār karnā*) joins स्वीकार to करना, "to do". स्वीकार is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *svīkāra*, "making one's own, acceptance" (Monier-Williams).
