## You'll want to know: le meurtre
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C290-CRIME-03]; assesses=[] -->

**le meurtre** — "the murder".

> Le roman raconte l'histoire d'un meurtre.
>
> *The novel tells the story of a murder.*

The killer is *le meurtrier*, *la meurtrière*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*meurtre* is built on Old French *murtrir* "to murder", which is usually traced to a Frankish verb related to English *murder* (EtymDB lists no parent).
