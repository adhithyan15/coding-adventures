Gujarati, chapter 264: Friends and neighbours.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
પડોશી (paḍośī) — neighbour.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for mother-in-law, then the Gujarati for nephew.

You'll want to know: પડોશી.
પડોશી — paḍośī — "neighbour".
અમારા પડોશી ઘરે નથી. — amārā paḍośī ghare nathī — "Our neighbours are not at home."

The word, taken apart.
પડોશી (paḍośī) is a tadbhava. It comes from Sanskrit prativēśya, "neighbour"; CDIAL lists Gujarati paṛośī (CDIAL 8599).

Lesson 3 of 5.
બહેનપણી (bahenpaṇī) — female friend, a girl's friend.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for neighbour, then the Gujarati for pal.

You'll want to know: બહેનપણી.
બહેનપણી — bahenpaṇī — "female friend, a girl's friend".
મારી બહેનપણી આજે નથી આવતી. — mārī bahenpaṇī āje nathī āvtī — "My friend is not coming today."
A girl's or a woman's female friend.

The word, taken apart.
બહેનપણી (bahenpaṇī) is built on the taught bahen, "sister", with -paṇī. CDIAL derives bahen from Sanskrit bhaginī, "sister" (CDIAL 9349).

શું તમે મને ઓળખો છો? — śũ tame mane oḷakho cho? — "Do you recognize me?"

The word, taken apart.
ઓળખવું (oḷakhvũ) is a tadbhava. It comes from Sanskrit upalakṣayati, "beholds, characterizes"; CDIAL glosses the Gujarati verb "to know, distinguish, have friendship with" (CDIAL 2226).

Lesson 5 of 5.
ભેટવું (bheṭvũ) — to embrace, to hug.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for female friend, then the Gujarati for to recognize.

You'll want to know: ભેટવું.
ભેટવું — bheṭvũ — "to embrace, to hug".
શું તે તેની માતાને ભેટ્યો? — śũ te tenī mātāne bheṭyo? — "Did he hug his mother?"
The taught noun ભેટ, bheṭ, means a gift; the verb means to embrace.

The word, taken apart.
ભેટવું (bheṭvũ) is a tadbhava. It comes from bhēṭṭ-, a reconstructed form listed under bhiṭ, "meet, throng"; CDIAL glosses the Gujarati verb "to meet" (CDIAL 9490).
