Arabic, chapter 447: Fixing the room.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
دش (dush) — a shower.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to greet warmly, then the Arabic for to recommend.

You'll want to know: دش.
دش — dush — "a shower".
أخذت دشا بعد الرحلة. akhadhtu dushan baʿda ar-riḥla. I took a shower after the trip.

The word, taken apart.
دش (dush) is usually traced to French douche.

Lesson 2 of 5.
مكواة (mikwāh) — a clothes iron.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to recommend, then the Arabic for a shower.

You'll want to know: مكواة.
مكواة — mikwāh — "a clothes iron".
طلبت مكواة من الاستقبال. ṭalabtu mikwāh min al-istiqbāl. I asked reception for an iron.

The word, taken apart.
مكواة (mikwāh) is built on the native Arabic root k-w-y, "to burn with a hot iron" (Lane). The tool pattern mifʿala names the thing one irons with, beside kawā, "to iron".

Lesson 3 of 5.
مجفف (mujaffif) — a dryer; a hairdryer.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a shower, then the Arabic for a clothes iron.

You'll want to know: مجفف.
مجفف — mujaffif — "a dryer; a hairdryer".
لم يعمل مجفف الشعر في الحمام. lam yaʿmal mujaffif ash-shaʿr fī al-ḥammām. The hairdryer in the bathroom did not work.

The word, taken apart.
مجفف (mujaffif) is built on the native Arabic root j-f-f, "to be dry" (Lane). It is the Form II participle, "a thing that dries".

Lesson 4 of 5.
تعطل (taʿaṭṭala) — to break down.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a clothes iron, then the Arabic for a dryer.

You'll want to know: تعطل.
تعطل — taʿaṭṭala — "to break down".
تعطل المصعد في الفندق. taʿaṭṭala al-miṣʿad fī al-funduq. The lift in the hotel broke down.
Present: يتعطل, yataʿaṭṭal.

The word, taken apart.
تعطل (taʿaṭṭala) is built on the native Arabic root ʿ-ṭ-l, "to be idle, without work" (Lane). It is Form V; muʿaṭṭal, "out of order", and ʿuṭla, "a holiday", share the root.

Lesson 5 of 5.
تفقد (tafaqqada) — to check on; to inspect.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a dryer, then the Arabic for to break down.

You'll want to know: تفقد.
تفقد — tafaqqada — "to check on; to inspect".
تفقد العامل الحمام بعد العطل. tafaqqada al-ʿāmil al-ḥammām baʿda al-ʿuṭl. The worker checked on the bathroom after the breakdown.
Present: يتفقد, yatafaqqad.

The word, taken apart.
تفقد (tafaqqada) is built on the native Arabic root f-q-d, "to lose, to miss" (Lane). It is Form V, "to look for what is missing", and so to check on.
