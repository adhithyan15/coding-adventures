# في حال (fī ḥāl) — in case

## You'll want to know: في حال
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C399-SHART399-02]; assesses=[] -->

**في حال** — *fī ḥāl* — "in case".

> في حال تأخر القطار، سأتصل بك.
>
> *fī ḥāl taʾakhkhara al-qiṭār, sa-attaṣilu bik.*
>
> In case the train is late, I will call you.

Also في حالة, *fī ḥālat*: in the event of.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**في حال** (*fī ḥāl*) joins *fī*, "in", with *ḥāl*, "a state, a condition", built on the native Arabic root *ḥ-w-l*, "to change, to turn" (Lane). Literally, in the state that.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
