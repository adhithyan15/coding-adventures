# బలహీనపడు (balahīnapaḍu) — to lose strength; to weaken

## You'll want to know: బలహీనపడు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C478-PEDAGALI478-05]; assesses=[] -->

**బలహీనపడు** — *balahīnapaḍu* — "to lose strength; to weaken".

> తీరం దాటిన తరువాత తుపాను బలహీనపడింది.
>
> *tīraṁ dāṭina taruvāta tupānu balahīnapaḍindi.*
>
> After crossing the coast the storm weakened.

Past (I …): బలహీనపడ్డాను, *balahīnapaḍḍānu*.

బలం, strength, + హీన, lacking, + పడు. Said of storms, of the body and of a currency.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**బలహీనపడు** (*balahīnapaḍu*) is a Telugu verb built from Sanskrit *bala*, "strength", and *hīna*, "lacking, deprived of" (Monier-Williams), with the native పడు, "to undergo" (DEDR 3853).
