# تسلیت گفتن (tasliyat goftan) — to offer condolences

## You'll want to know: تسلیت گفتن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C338-KHATM-03]; assesses=[] -->

**تسلیت گفتن** (*tasliyat goftan*) — "to offer condolences".

> همه همکاران به او تسلیت گفتند.
>
> *Hame-ye hamkârân be u tasliyat goftand.*
>
> All his colleagues offered him their condolences.

The person takes *be*. Present stem *gu-*: *tasliyat miguyam*, in speech *tasliyat migam* "my condolences".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*tasliyat* is usually traced to Arabic *tasliya*, "consolation"; EtymDB lists no entry. *goftan* "to say" is native Persian.
