# ਮੈਂ ਰੋਟੀ ਖਾਧੀ ਅਤੇ ਚਾਹ ਪੀਤੀ — I ate a roti and drank tea

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-GRAMMAR-PAST-OBJECT-219, PA-GRAMMAR-PAST-KHADHA-219, PA-LEX-KADDHNA-219] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say *I drank tea*, *I ate a roti*, then *I took out the book*.

## Grammar Lens: two things, two endings
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-GRAMMAR-PAST-OBJECT-219, PA-GRAMMAR-PAST-KHADHA-219, PA-LEX-KADDHNA-219, PA-SCRIPT-RECOG-DDHA-219] -->

Join two pasts with **ਅਤੇ**. Each one agrees with its own thing:

> **ਮੈਂ ਰੋਟੀ ਖਾਧੀ ਅਤੇ ਚਾਹ ਪੀਤੀ।** — *maiṁ roṭī khādhī ate chāh pītī.* — I ate a roti and drank tea.

> **ਅਸੀਂ ਸੇਬ ਖਾਧਾ ਅਤੇ ਦੁੱਧ ਪੀਤਾ।** — *asīṁ seb khādhā ate duddh pītā.* — We ate an apple and drank milk.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-GRAMMAR-PAST-OBJECT-219, PA-GRAMMAR-PAST-KHADHA-219, PA-SCRIPT-RECOG-DDHA-219] -->

- [YOU HEAR: *I ate an apple and drank tea*] Say **ਮੈਂ ਸੇਬ ਖਾਧਾ ਅਤੇ ਚਾਹ ਪੀਤੀ।**
- [YOU HEAR: *I didn't drink milk*] Say **ਮੈਂ ਦੁੱਧ ਨਹੀਂ ਪੀਤਾ।**
- [YOU READ: **ਕੱਢਣਾ**] Point to **ਢ** and say *ḍha*.
- [YOU WRITE: copy **ਮੈਂ ਰੋਟੀ ਖਾਧੀ ਅਤੇ ਚਾਹ ਪੀਤੀ।** once]
