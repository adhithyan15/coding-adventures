## You'll want to know: o entardecer
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C275-ENTARDECER-02]; assesses=[] -->

**o entardecer** — "the late afternoon, early evening".

> Ao entardecer, as pessoas passeiam junto ao rio.
>
> *In the late afternoon people stroll by the river.*

Also a verb: *entardece* — the afternoon is ending.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*entardecer* is built on *tarde* "afternoon, late", which comes from Latin *tardē* "slowly, late" (Lewis & Short).
