## You'll want to know: सदस्यः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C355-SANSAD355-02]; assesses=[] -->

**सदस्यः** — *sadasyaḥ* — "a member".

> अरुणः सभायाः सदस्यः अस्ति।
>
> *aruṇaḥ sabhāyāḥ sadasyaḥ asti.*
>
> Arun is a member of the assembly.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सदस्यः** (*sadasyaḥ*) is built on *sadas*, "a seat, an assembly", from the root √sad, "to sit"; MW gives *sadasya* "an assessor" and "a member of an assembly" (MW).
