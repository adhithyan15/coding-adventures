# ਉਤਾਰਨਾ (utārnā) — to take down; to unload

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Punjabi for to sway, then the Punjabi for to get off.

## You'll want to know: ਉਤਾਰਨਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C284-OFF284-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ਉਤਾਰਨਾ** — *utārnā* — "to take down; to unload".

> ਅਮਨ ਬੱਸ ਦੀ ਛੱਤ ਤੋਂ ਅਟੈਚੀ ਉਤਾਰੇਗਾ।
>
> *Aman bass dī chhatt ton aṭaichī utāregā.*
>
> Aman will take the suitcase down from the roof of the bus.

Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਅਟੈਚੀ ਉਤਾਰੀ, *maiṁ aṭaichī utārī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਉਤਾਰਨਾ** (*utārnā*) is the causative partner of *utarnā*, inherited from Sanskrit *uttārayati*, "makes descend"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *utārṇā*, "to make descend", under it (CDIAL 1770).
