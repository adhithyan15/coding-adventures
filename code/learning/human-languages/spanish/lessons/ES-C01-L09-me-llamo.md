# me llamo — "I call myself," and a root that shouts

## Warm-up

[PAUSE 2s] How you give your name — and it's not "my name is" at all. It's "I
call myself," and the verb inside it literally means *to shout*.

## You'll want to know first

- [ES-C01-L08 (¿cómo estás?)](./ES-C01-L08-como-estas.md) — your first verb
  was there; here's your second, plus the little word *me*.

## Sounds you'll need

- `ll-y` — the **ll** in *llamo* sounds like English **y**: *YA-mo*, not
  *LA-mo*. → [reference](../pronunciation-reference.md)

## The word, taken apart

**me llamo** is *me* + *llamo* — literally "**I call myself**."

**llamo** — "I call." Infinitive **llamar** ("to call"). Root: Latin
**clamare** ("to cry out, shout"). That *clam-* is a root that shouts all
through English:

- ex**claim**, ex**clam**ation — *ex-* ("out") + *clamare*, to shout **out**.
- pro**claim**, pro**clam**ation — *pro-* ("forth") + *clamare*, to call
  **forth** publicly.
- ac**claim** — *ad-* ("toward") + *clamare*, to shout praise **toward**.
- **claim**, re**claim**, dis**claim**; and **clam**or itself, borrowed
  whole.

Watch the prefix machine at work on one root: *ex-* out, *pro-* forth, *ad-*
toward, *re-* back — snap each onto *-claim* and you get a different, precise
verb. Learn this root once and you've unlocked a shelf of English words *and*
the Spanish verb for "to call."

**me** — myself (here). This little word makes the verb **reflexive** — the
action loops back on the doer. *Llamar* alone is "to call [someone]"; *me
llamo* is "I call **myself**." (More on reflexives later; for now, *me* =
"myself.")

## Why it's said this way

Spanish doesn't say "my name is." It says "I call myself ___" — *me llamo
Ana*. The name isn't presented as a fact you *have* ("my name is") but as
what you *do* — what you call yourself. It's subtly more active, and it's the
completely standard, neutral way to introduce yourself (no formal/informal
worry here).

To ask it back: *¿cómo te llamas?* — "how do you call yourself?" — reusing
*cómo* from the last lesson. So the whole exchange is built from pieces you
now own: *Hola, ¿cómo te llamas? — Me llamo ___, ¿y tú?*
