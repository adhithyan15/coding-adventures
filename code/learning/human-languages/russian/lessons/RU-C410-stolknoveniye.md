## You'll want to know: столкновение
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C410-STOLKNOVENIYE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**столкновение** (*stolknovéniye*) — "a collision; a clash".

> На перекрёстке произошло столкновение двух машин.
>
> *Na perekryóstke proizoshló stolknovéniye dvukh mashín.*
>
> There was a collision between two cars at the crossroads.

Neuter; formal. In speech people say *авария*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*столкновение* is built on *столкнуться* 'to collide', from *с-* 'together', *толкнуть* 'to push' and *-ся*. EtymDB lists *толкать* without a deeper source.
