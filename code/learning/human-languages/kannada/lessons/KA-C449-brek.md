## You'll want to know: ಬ್ರೇಕ್
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C449-CAKRA449-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ಬ್ರೇಕ್** — *brēk* — "a brake".

> ಚಾಲಕ ತಕ್ಷಣ ಬ್ರೇಕ್ ಹಾಕಿದನು.
>
> *cālaka takṣaṇa brēk hākidanu.*
>
> The driver braked at once.

ಬ್ರೇಕ್ ಹಾಕು, to put on the brake.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಬ್ರೇಕ್** (*brēk*) is a loan from English *brake*.
