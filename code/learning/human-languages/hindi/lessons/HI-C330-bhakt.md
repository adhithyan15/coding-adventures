## You'll want to know: भक्त
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C330-MELA330-05]; assesses=[] -->

**भक्त** — *bhakt* — "a devotee".

> हज़ारों भक्त मंदिर के बाहर इकट्ठा हुए।
>
> *hazāroṁ bhakt mandir ke bāhar ikaṭṭhā hue.*
>
> Thousands of devotees gathered outside the temple.

Masculine or feminine, by the person meant.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**भक्त** (*bhakt*) is Sanskrit *bhakta*, "devoted, attached" (Monier-Williams): a person devoted to a god.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
