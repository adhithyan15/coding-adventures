# തുലാവർഷം (tulāvarṣaṁ) — the northeast monsoon

## You'll want to know: തുലാവർഷം
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C507-MAZHA507-03]; assesses=[] -->

**തുലാവർഷം** — *tulāvarṣaṁ* — "the northeast monsoon".

> തുലാവർഷത്തിൽ വൈകുന്നേരം ഇടിമിന്നലോടെ മഴ പെയ്യും.
>
> *tulāvarṣattil vaikunnēraṁ iṭiminnalōṭe maḻa peyyuṁ.*
>
> In the northeast monsoon it rains in the evenings with thunder.

It comes in the month of തുലാം, October to November.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**തുലാവർഷം** (*tulāvarṣaṁ*) is a Malayalam compound built from തുലാം, the month named after Sanskrit *tulā*, the scales, and *varṣa*, rain (Monier-Williams).
