## You'll want to know: léiyǔ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C514-WEATHER514-03]; assesses=[] -->

*léiyǔ* — "thunderstorm".

> *wàipó shuō jīntiān wǎnshang yǒu léiyǔ* — Grandma says there'll be a thunderstorm tonight.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*léiyǔ* is built from *léi* 'thunder' and 雨 'rain', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *lwoj* and *hjuX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which explains the first, after an older book, as yin and yang striking each other.
