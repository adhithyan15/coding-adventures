# وقتی (vaqti) — when, and a story told in order

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[FA-GRAMMAR-BAD-THEN-01, FA-GRAMMAR-DAR-AKHAR-01, FA-LEX-C136-NAFY136-05] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "first", "then", "in the end". (*Avval*, *ba'd*, *dar âkhar*.)
Then ask "when?" (*Che vaqt?*)

## Grammar Lens: vaqti, the joint for a moment
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-GRAMMAR-VAGHTI-01]; assesses=[FA-LEX-C133-ACT133-04, FA-LEX-C54-NATURE54-02, FA-LEX-C184-KONESH184-03, FA-LEX-C71-TOWN71-03, FA-GRAMMAR-SCENE-EVENT-01] -->

**وقتی**, *vaqti*, is "when" in the middle of a story, not in a question. It
ties what follows to a moment:

> **وقتی به هتل رسیدم، باد میوزید.** — *vaqti be hotel residam, bâd mivazid* —
> When I got to the hotel, the wind was blowing.

*Vaqti* takes the event, *residam*, and the rest paints what was going on at
that moment, *mivazid*.

It is *vaqt*, "time", with *-i*: "at the time that". *Vaqt* is the word
inside *che vaqt* and *sar-e vaqt*. The letter **ق** is written *q* here, so
chapter seventy's *ghatâr*, "a train", appears as *qatâr*.

## Grammar Lens: bâ, "with"
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-GRAMMAR-BA-WITH-01]; assesses=[FA-LEX-C70-TOWN70-05] -->

**با**, *bâ*, is "with": *bâ yek dust*, with a friend. For travel it is "by":
*bâ qatâr*, by train. And *bâ ham* is "together".

## Grammar Lens: the order a told story keeps
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-GRAMMAR-STORY-ORDER-01]; assesses=[FA-GRAMMAR-SIMPLE-PAST-01, FA-GRAMMAR-PAST-CONTINUOUS-01, FA-LEX-C290-GOZASHTE-01, FA-LEX-C291-TARIF-KARDAN-01] -->

A story you tell, that you *ta'rif mikonid*, keeps three parts in one order. First
the time and the scene, in *bud* and the *mi-* past: *sâl-e gozashte... havâ
sard bud.* Then the events, in the plain past, strung on *avval*, *ba'd* and
*vaqti*. Then the close, with *dar âkhar*.

> **سال گذشته با قطار به تهران رفتم. وقتی رسیدم، هوا سرد بود. اول به هتل
> رفتم. بعد با یک دوست شام خوردم. در آخر به هتل برگشتم.**
>
> *sâl-e gozashte bâ qatâr be Tehrân raftam. vaqti residam, havâ sard bud.
> avval be hotel raftam. ba'd bâ yek dust shâm khordam. dar âkhar be hotel
> bargashtam.*
>
> Last year I went to Tehran by train. When I arrived, it was cold. First I
> went to the hotel. Then I had dinner with a friend. In the end I went back
> to the hotel.

## Wrap-up Recall

[PAUSE 3s] What does *vaqti* do that *che vaqt* does not? (It joins two
parts of a story and asks nothing.) "Together"? (***Bâ ham.***) What comes
first in a told story? (**The time and the scene.**)
