Arabic, chapter 459: Spending wisely.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
مستهلك (mustahlik) — a consumer.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to take back, then the Arabic for to barter.

You'll want to know: مستهلك.
مستهلك — mustahlik — "a consumer".
يحب المستهلك الأسعار الرخيصة. yuḥibbu al-mustahlik al-asʿār ar-rakhīṣa. The consumer likes cheap prices.

The word, taken apart.
مستهلك (mustahlik) is built on the native Arabic root h-l-k, "to perish" (Lane). It is the Form X participle, "one who uses things up".

ميزانية (mīzāniyya) is built on the native Arabic root w-z-n, "to weigh" (Lane). It is made from mīzān, "scales", with -iyya: money weighed out.

وضعت المشتريات في السيارة. waḍaʿtu al-mushtarayāt fī as-sayyāra. I put the shopping in the car.

مشتريات (mushtarayāt) is built on the native Arabic root sh-r-y, "to buy, to sell" (Lane). It is a plural of the Form VIII passive participle: things bought.

The word, taken apart.
اقتصد (iqtaṣada) is built on the native Arabic root q-ṣ-d, "to aim at; to keep to the middle" (Lane). It is Form VIII, "to be moderate".
