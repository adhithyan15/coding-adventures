## You'll want to know: el pantano
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C689-AGUA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**el pantano** — "the marsh, the swamp; the reservoir (Spain)".

> Este verano el pantano tiene poca agua.
>
> *This summer the reservoir has little water.*

In Spain *el pantano* is often a reservoir behind a dam.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*pantano* was borrowed from Italian *pantano* "swamp" (EtymDB); where the Italian word comes from is not settled.
