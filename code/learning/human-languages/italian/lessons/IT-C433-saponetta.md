## You'll want to know: la saponetta
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C433-CAMERA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**la saponetta** — "the bar of soap".

> In bagno manca la saponetta.
>
> *There is no soap in the bathroom.*

A little *sapone*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*saponetta* is a little *sapone* 'soap', which is inherited from Latin *sāpō, sāpōnis* (EtymDB), itself an early loan from a Germanic language (Lewis & Short).
