## You'll want to know: प्राकारः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C422-PRAKARA422-01]; assesses=[] -->

**प्राकारः** — *prākāraḥ* — "a town wall; a rampart".

> नगरं परितः उन्नतः प्राकारः अस्ति।
>
> *nagaraṃ paritaḥ unnataḥ prākāraḥ asti.*
>
> Around the city there is a high rampart.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**प्राकारः** (*prākāraḥ*) is built from *prā*, a form of *pra*, and the root √kṝ, "to pour out, to heap"; MW gives *prākāra* as a wall or enclosure, a rampart (MW).
