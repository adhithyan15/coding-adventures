## You'll want to know: il vandalo
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C293-SCASSO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**il vandalo** — "the vandal".

> I vandali hanno rotto tutte le finestre della scuola.
>
> *Vandals broke all the school windows.*

Plural *i vandali*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*vandalo* comes from Latin *Vandalus* (EtymDB), the name of a Germanic people who sacked Rome in the year 455.
