# ਨਿਪੁੰਨ (nipunn) — skilled; expert at

## You'll want to know: ਨਿਪੁੰਨ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C362-SKILL362-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ਨਿਪੁੰਨ** — *nipunn* — "skilled; expert at".

> ਉਹ ਲੱਕੜ ਦੇ ਕੰਮ ਵਿੱਚ ਨਿਪੁੰਨ ਹੈ।
>
> *oh lakkaṛ de kamm vich nipunn hai.*
>
> He is skilled at woodwork.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਨਿਪੁੰਨ** (*nipunn*) is taken over from Sanskrit *nipuṇa*, "skilful", a tatsama (Monier-Williams).
