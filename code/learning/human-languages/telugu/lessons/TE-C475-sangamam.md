## You'll want to know: సంగమం
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C475-ANAKATTA475-01]; assesses=[] -->

**సంగమం** — *saṅgamaṁ* — "a confluence".

> రెండు నదుల సంగమం దగ్గర పెద్ద జాతర జరుగుతుంది.
>
> *reṇḍu nadula saṅgamaṁ daggara pedda jātara jarugutundi.*
>
> A big fair is held where the two rivers meet.

The place where two rivers meet; also any meeting of two things.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**సంగమం** (*saṅgamaṁ*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *saṅgama*, "a coming together, a confluence of rivers" (Monier-Williams), from *sam-gam*, "to go together".
