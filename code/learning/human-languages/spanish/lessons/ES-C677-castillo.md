## You'll want to know: el castillo
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C677-MONUMENTO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**el castillo** — "the castle".

> Desde el castillo se ve todo el pueblo.
>
> *The whole town can be seen from the castle.*

*Hacer castillos en el aire* — to build castles in the air.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*castillo* is inherited from Latin *castellum* "a castle, a fort" (Lewis & Short), a little *castrum* "fortified camp".
