Lesson 1 of 5.
ಕಬಡ್ಡಿ (kabaḍḍi) — kabaddi.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a lead, then the Kannada for to contend.

You'll want to know: ಕಬಡ್ಡಿ.
ಕಬಡ್ಡಿ — kabaḍḍi — "kabaddi".
ಹಳ್ಳಿಯ ಹುಡುಗರು ಮೈದಾನದಲ್ಲಿ ಕಬಡ್ಡಿ ಆಡುತ್ತಾರೆ. haḷḷiya huḍugaru maidānadalli kabaḍḍi āḍuttāre. The village boys play kabaddi on the ground.
A raider holds his breath, chants, and tags players on the other side.

The word, taken apart.
ಕಬಡ್ಡಿ (kabaḍḍi) is the name of a team game of south Asia; the origin of the name is not settled.

ಜಾತ್ರೆಯಲ್ಲಿ ದೊಡ್ಡ ಕುಸ್ತಿ ಸ್ಪರ್ಧೆ ನಡೆಯಿತು. jātreyalli doḍḍa kusti spardhe naḍeyitu. A big wrestling contest was held at the fair.

ಕುಸ್ತಿ (kusti) is usually traced to Persian kushtī, "wrestling", which is thought to have reached Kannada through Hindustani.

Lesson 4 of 5.
ಒದೆ (ode) — to kick.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for wrestling, then the Kannada for a goal in sport.

You'll want to know: ಒದೆ.
ಒದೆ — ode — "to kick".
ಹುಡುಗ ಚೆಂಡನ್ನು ಜೋರಾಗಿ ಒದ್ದನು. huḍuga ceṇḍannu jōrāgi oddanu. The boy kicked the ball hard.
Past (I …): ಒದ್ದೆ, odde.
The past ಒದ್ದೆ, I kicked, is spelled like ಒದ್ದೆ, wet; the sentence tells them apart.

The word, taken apart.
ಒದೆ (ode) is native Dravidian: DEDR 616 lists Kannada ode, odi, "to kick, spur", with odd- in the past.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ಗುದ್ದು (guddu) — to punch.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a goal in sport, then the Kannada for to kick.

You'll want to know: ಗುದ್ದು.
ಗುದ್ದು — guddu — "to punch".
ಆಟದಲ್ಲಿ ತಮ್ಮ ನನ್ನ ಬೆನ್ನಿಗೆ ಗುದ್ದಿದನು. āṭadalli tamma nanna bennige guddidanu. In the game my younger brother punched my back.
Past (I …): ಗುದ್ದಿದೆ, guddide.
Also a noun, a punch.

The word, taken apart.
ಗುದ್ದು (guddu) is native Dravidian: DEDR 1850 lists Kannada kuttu, "to beat, strike", and guddu, "to strike with the fist".
