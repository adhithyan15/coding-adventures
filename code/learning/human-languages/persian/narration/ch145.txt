Lesson 1 of 5.
پوزش خواستن (puzesh khâstan) — to apologise.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to continue, then the Persian for to type.

You'll want to know: پوزش خواستن.
پوزش خواستن — puzesh khâstan — "to apologise".

The word, taken apart.
puzesh khâstan is "to ask for pardon": puzesh, "apology", with khâstan, "to ask for". puzesh is native, but its older form is not settled. khâstan, "to ask for", is an inherited verb, from Middle Persian xwāstan (EtymDB).

Lesson 2 of 5.

[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to type, then the Persian for to apologise.

You'll want to know: نگهداری کردن.
نگهداری کردن — negahdâri kardan — "to look after, to take care of".

The word, taken apart.
negahdâri kardan pairs negahdâri, "keeping", with kardan. negahdâri is negah, "look, watch", with dâr, "holding" (from dâshtan), and -i: keeping watch. All the pieces are native. kardan, "to do", is an inherited verb, from Middle Persian kardan (EtymDB).

Lesson 5 of 5.
صدا زدن (sedâ zadan) — to call (someone).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to accompany, then the Persian for to believe.

You'll want to know: صدا زدن.
صدا زدن — sedâ zadan — "to call (someone)".

The word, taken apart.
sedâ zadan pairs sedâ, "voice, sound", with zadan, "to strike". sedâ is borrowed from Arabic ṣadā, "echo" (EtymDB; root ṣ-d-y, Lane). zadan, "to strike", is an inherited verb; it is usually traced to Middle Persian zadan.
