Kannada, chapter 267: Manure, Flowerpot, Pipe, Pump, Gate.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಗೊಬ್ಬರ (gobbara) — manure, fertiliser.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for camphor, then the Kannada for copper.

You'll want to know: ಗೊಬ್ಬರ.
ಗೊಬ್ಬರ — gobbara — "manure, fertiliser".

The word, taken apart.
ಗೊಬ್ಬರ (gobbara) is probably a tadbhava of Sanskrit gōvara, "powdered cowdung" (CDIAL 4316): it is close to Prakrit govvara and Hindi gobar, though CDIAL does not list the Kannada word.

Lesson 2 of 5.
ಕುಂಡ (kuṇḍa) — a flowerpot.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for copper, then the Kannada for manure.

You'll want to know: ಕುಂಡ.
ಕುಂಡ — kuṇḍa — "a flowerpot".

The word, taken apart.
ಕುಂಡ (kuṇḍa) is Sanskrit kuṇḍa, "bowl, waterpot" (CDIAL 3264). DEDR 1669 lists Kannada kuṇḍa, "pit, pool", among native words, and compares the same Sanskrit word; CDIAL 3264 in turn takes the Sanskrit word from Dravidian. So the word may have gone north and come back.
