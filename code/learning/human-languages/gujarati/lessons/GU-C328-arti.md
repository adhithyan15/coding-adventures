## You'll want to know: આરતી
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C328-PUJA328-02]; assesses=[] -->

**આરતી** — *ārtī* — "an aarti (worship with lamps)".

> સાંજે મંદિરમાં આરતી થઈ.
>
> *sā̃je mandirmā̃ ārtī thaī.*
>
> In the evening the aarti was held at the temple.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**આરતી** (*ārtī*) goes back to Sanskrit *ārātrika*, the waving of lamps before an image, and CDIAL lists the Gujarati noun, "the ceremony" (CDIAL 1315). It is feminine: **આરતી થઈ**, the aarti took place.
