## You'll want to know: вратарь
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C509-FOOTBALL-02]; assesses=[] -->

**вратарь** (*vratár*) — "a goalkeeper".

> Вратарь поймал мяч.
>
> *Vratár poymál myach.*
>
> The goalkeeper caught the ball.

Masculine; genitive вратаря.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*вратарь* is built on *врата* with the ending *-арь* (EtymDB); *врата* was borrowed from Church Slavonic, the *-ра-* shape of Proto-Slavic *vorta*, which gave native *ворота* 'gates' (EtymDB).
