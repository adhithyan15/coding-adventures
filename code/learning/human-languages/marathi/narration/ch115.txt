Lesson 1 of 5.
होडी (hoḍī) — a boat.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for an office, then the Marathi for the police.

You'll want to know: होडी.
होडी — hoḍī — "a boat".

The word, taken apart.
होडी (hoḍī) is a tadbhava of Sanskrit hoḍa, "raft, boat", a word from the old word-lists (CDIAL 14174). CDIAL takes it from Dravidian, comparing Kannada ōḍa.

Lesson 5 of 5.
कपडे (kapḍe) — clothes.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a market, then the Marathi for a village.

You'll want to know: कपडे.
कपडे — kapḍe — "clothes".

The word, taken apart.
कपडे (kapḍe) is the plural of kapḍā, "cloth", which goes back, like Marathi kāpaḍ, "cloth", to Sanskrit karpaṭa, "patched garment, rag" (CDIAL 2871). CDIAL reports Przyluski's view that the Sanskrit word comes from Austroasiatic.
