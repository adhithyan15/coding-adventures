## You'll want to know: কাঁচামাল
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C499-UTPADON499-02]; assesses=[] -->

**কাঁচামাল** — *kā̃chāmāl* — "raw material".

> কাঁচামাল না এলে কারখানা থামবে।
>
> *Kā̃chāmāl nā ele kārkhānā thāmbe.*
>
> If the raw material doesn't arrive, the factory will stop.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**কাঁচামাল** (*kā̃chāmāl*) joins কাঁচা, "raw", to মাল, "goods", which is usually traced to Arabic *māl*, "property".
