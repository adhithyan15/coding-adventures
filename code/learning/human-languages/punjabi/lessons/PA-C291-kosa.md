## You'll want to know: ਕੋਸਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C291-WARM291-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ਕੋਸਾ** — *kosā* — "lukewarm".

> ਮੈਂ ਕੋਸੇ ਪਾਣੀ ਨਾਲ ਨਹਾਉਂਦਾ ਹਾਂ।
>
> *maiṁ kose pāṇī nāl nahāundā hāṁ.*
>
> I bathe with lukewarm water.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਕੋਸਾ** (*kosā*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*kōṣma*, "warm"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *kōssā*, "lukewarm", under it (CDIAL 3552).
