## You'll want to know: साकारणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C537-NATYA537-05]; assesses=[] -->

**साकारणे** — *sākārṇe* — "to portray; to bring to life".

> तिने आईची भूमिका खूप छान साकारली.
>
> *tine āīcī bhūmikā khūp chān sākārlī.*
>
> She portrayed the mother beautifully.

Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने भूमिका साकारली, *tyāne bhūmikā sākārlī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**साकारणे** (*sākārṇe*) is built on Sanskrit *sākāra*, "having form" (Monier-Williams): to give a role a shape.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
