## You'll want to know: भ्रष्टाचार
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C369-SANSTHA369-01]; assesses=[] -->

**भ्रष्टाचार** — *bhraṣṭācār* — "corruption".

> लोग भ्रष्टाचार से परेशान हैं।
>
> *log bhraṣṭācār se pareśān haiṁ.*
>
> People are fed up with corruption.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**भ्रष्टाचार** (*bhraṣṭācār*) joins two Sanskrit words, *bhraṣṭa*, "fallen", and *ācāra*, "conduct" (Monier-Williams): conduct that has fallen.
