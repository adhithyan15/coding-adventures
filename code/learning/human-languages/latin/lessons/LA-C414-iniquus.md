## You'll want to know: inīquus, inīqua, inīquum
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C414-INIURIA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**inīquus, inīqua, inīquum** — "unfair, unjust; uneven".

> Lēx inīqua est.
>
> *The law is unfair.*

*inīquus, inīqua, inīquum*, *in-* not + *aequus*, level, fair. English *iniquity*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*inīquus* is built from *in-* "not" and *aequus* "level, fair" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short), the vowel changed inside the compound. English *iniquity* comes from it.
