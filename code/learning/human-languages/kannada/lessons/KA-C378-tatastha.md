## You'll want to know: ತಟಸ್ಥ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C378-NILUVU378-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ತಟಸ್ಥ** — *taṭastha* — "neutral".

> ಜಗಳದಲ್ಲಿ ನಾನು ತಟಸ್ಥವಾಗಿ ಇದ್ದೆ.
>
> *jagaḷadalli nānu taṭasthavāgi idde.*
>
> In the quarrel I stayed neutral.

Word by word it is standing on the bank, out of the stream.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ತಟಸ್ಥ** (*taṭastha*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *taṭastha*, "standing on a bank" (Monier-Williams), from *taṭa*, "a bank", and *stha*, "standing".
