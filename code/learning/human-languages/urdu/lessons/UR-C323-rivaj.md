## You'll want to know: رواج
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C323-RIVAJ323-02]; assesses=[] -->

**رواج** — *rivāj* — "a custom".

> پہلے یہ رواج نہیں تھا۔
>
> *pehle yih rivāj nahīṅ thā.*
>
> This custom did not exist before.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *rawāj*, "currency, being in use": what people do now. **روایت** is old; a **رواج** may be new.
