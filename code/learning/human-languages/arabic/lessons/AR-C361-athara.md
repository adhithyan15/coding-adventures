## You'll want to know: أثار
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C361-QADIYYA361-04]; assesses=[] -->

**أثار** — *athāra* — "to stir up; to raise".

> أثار الخبر اهتمام الناس.
>
> *athāra al-khabar ihtimām an-nās.*
>
> The news stirred up people's interest.

Present: يثير, *yuthīr*.

أثار مشكلة, raised a problem.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**أثار** (*athāra*) is built on the native Arabic root *th-w-r*, "to rise up, to be stirred" (Lane). It is Form IV, to stir up: to raise dust, and so to raise feelings or a question.
