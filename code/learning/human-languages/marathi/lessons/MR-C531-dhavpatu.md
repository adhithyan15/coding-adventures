## You'll want to know: धावपटू
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C531-KHELADU531-03]; assesses=[] -->

**धावपटू** — *dhāvpaṭū* — "a runner; an athlete".

> तो धावपटू रोज सकाळी मैदानावर धावतो.
>
> *to dhāvpaṭū roj sakāḷī maidānāvar dhāvto.*
>
> That runner runs on the ground every morning.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**धावपटू** (*dhāvpaṭū*) is built from धाव, "a run", from धावणे, and the Sanskrit *paṭu*, "skilful" (Monier-Williams).
