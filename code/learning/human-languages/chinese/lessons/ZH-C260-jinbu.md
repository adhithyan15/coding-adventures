## You'll want to know: jìnbù
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C260-SKILL260-05]; assesses=[] -->

*jìnbù* — "to improve; to make progress".

> *nǐ de Hànyǔ jìnbù de hěn kuài* — Your Chinese is improving fast.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*jìnbù* is built from 进 'to go forward' and *bù* 'a step', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *tsinH* and *buH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'forward' and the second 'a pace'.
