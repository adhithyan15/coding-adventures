## You'll want to know: abreisen
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C426-REZEPTION-03]; assesses=[] -->

**abreisen** — "to leave, to depart (at the end of a stay)".

> Wir reisen morgen früh ab.
>
> *We are leaving early tomorrow.*

Separable; Perfekt with *sein*: *sie sind schon abgereist*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*abreisen* is built from *ab* 'off, away' and *reisen* 'to travel', inherited from Old High German *reisōn* 'to set out' (EtymDB).
