# શેકાવું (śekāvũ) — to bake (in the heat); to be roasted

## You'll want to know: શેકાવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C436-HEAT436-05]; assesses=[] -->

**શેકાવું** — *śekāvũ* — "to bake (in the heat); to be roasted".

> આવા તડકામાં અમે શેકાઈ ગયા.
>
> *āvā taḍkāmā̃ ame śekāī gayā.*
>
> We got roasted in sunshine like this.

Past (he …): તે તડકામાં શેકાયો, *te taḍkāmā̃ śekāyo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**શેકાવું** (*śekāvũ*) is the intransitive partner of શેકવું, "to roast", which goes back to *\*sēkk*, "to heat, to roast", a form CDIAL reconstructs (CDIAL 13581).
