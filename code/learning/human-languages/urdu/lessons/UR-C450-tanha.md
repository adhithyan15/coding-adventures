## You'll want to know: تنہا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C450-MISS450-01]; assesses=[] -->

**تنہا** — *tanhā* — "lonely; alone".

> شہر میں نانی بالکل تنہا ہیں۔
>
> *shahr meṅ nānī bilkul tanhā haiṅ.*
>
> In the city my granny is all alone.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**تنہا** (*tanhā*) is usually traced to Persian *tanhā*, "alone".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
