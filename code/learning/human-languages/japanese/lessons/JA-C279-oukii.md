## You'll want to know: おおきい
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C279-KAZAN279-05]; assesses=[] -->

**おおきい** — *ōkii* — "big; large".

> **おおきい じしんが ありました。** — *ōkii jishin ga arimashita* — "There was a big earthquake."

An i-adjective.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**おおきい** (*ōkii*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict).

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
