Lesson 4 of 5.
కూలి (kūli) — daily wages; a labourer.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a list, then the Telugu for half.

You'll want to know: కూలి.
కూలి — kūli — "daily wages; a labourer".

The word, taken apart.
కూలి (kūli) is native Dravidian, one word with Tamil kūli and Kannada kūli (DEDR 1905). DEDR lists Telugu kūli, "wages; hired labourer".

Lesson 5 of 5.
ఉద్యోగి (udyōgi) — an employee.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for half, then the Telugu for daily wages.

You'll want to know: ఉద్యోగి.
ఉద్యోగి — udyōgi — "an employee".

The word, taken apart.
ఉద్యోగి (udyōgi) is Sanskrit udyōgin, "one at work", from udyōga, "exertion" (CDIAL 2055), with the final -n dropped.
