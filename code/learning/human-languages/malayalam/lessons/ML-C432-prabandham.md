# പ്രബന്ധം (prabandhaṁ) — a thesis; a paper

## You'll want to know: പ്രബന്ധം
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C432-GAVESANAM432-03]; assesses=[] -->

**പ്രബന്ധം** — *prabandhaṁ* — "a thesis; a paper".

> അവൻ തന്റെ പ്രബന്ധം സർവകലാശാലയിൽ സമർപ്പിച്ചു.
>
> *avan tanṟe prabandhaṁ sarvakalāśālayil samarppiccu.*
>
> He submitted his thesis to the university.

A research paper read at a meeting is also a പ്രബന്ധം.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**പ്രബന്ധം** (*prabandhaṁ*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *prabandha*, "a band, a connexion" (CDIAL 8695), and in later Sanskrit a connected composition (Monier-Williams).
