## You'll want to know: પ્રવેશ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C196-STOREY196-05]; assesses=[] -->

**પ્રવેશ** — *praveś* — "entry, way in".

> **પ્રવેશ પાછળથી છે.** — *praveś pāchaḷthī che* — "The way in is at the back."

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**પ્રવેશ** (*praveś*) is Sanskrit *praveśa*, "entering, entrance", taken over whole as a learned loan, a tatsama. It shares its root with *práviśati*, "enters" (CDIAL 8803).
