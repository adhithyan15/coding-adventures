# おこす (okosu) — to wake someone; to raise

## You'll want to know: おこす
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C146-SLEEP146-04]; assesses=[] -->

**おこす** — *okosu* — "to wake someone; to raise".

> **ははは まいあさ こどもを おこします。** — *haha wa maiasa kodomo o okoshimasu* — "My mother wakes the children every morning."

Polite **おこします**, *okoshimasu*; a godan verb. Its intransitive partner is **おきます**, *okimasu*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**おこす** (*okosu*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, and built on the same root as *okiru*, "to get up"; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
