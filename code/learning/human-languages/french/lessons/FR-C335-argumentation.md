## You'll want to know: l'argumentation
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C335-DEBAT-04]; assesses=[] -->

**l'argumentation** — "the line of argument".

> Ton argumentation est claire, mais je ne suis pas d'accord.
>
> *Your line of argument is clear, but I do not agree.*

The whole chain of reasons; one single reason is *un argument*. Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*argumentation* is a learned borrowing from Latin *argūmentātiō* "the bringing of proof" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short), from *argūmentum* "proof", built on *arguere* "to make clear".
