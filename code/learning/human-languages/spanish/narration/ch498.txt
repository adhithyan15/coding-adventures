Spanish, chapter 498: El deporte.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
el campeonato — the championship.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for to look after, then the Spanish for friendship.

You'll want to know: el campeonato.
el campeonato — "the championship".
Ganamos el campeonato de baloncesto. We won the basketball championship.
