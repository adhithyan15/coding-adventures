## You'll want to know: ಪ್ರತಿಭೆ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C503-BUDDHI503-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ಪ್ರತಿಭೆ** — *pratibhe* — "talent; flair".

> ಆ ಹುಡುಗನಿಗೆ ಚಿತ್ರ ಬಿಡಿಸುವ ಪ್ರತಿಭೆ ಇದೆ.
>
> *ā huḍuganige citra biḍisuva pratibhe ide.*
>
> That boy has a talent for painting.

ಪ್ರತಿಭಾವಂತ, a talented person.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಪ್ರತಿಭೆ** (*pratibhe*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *pratibhā*, "light, splendour; intelligence, understanding" (Monier-Williams). The final -ā became -ೆ.
