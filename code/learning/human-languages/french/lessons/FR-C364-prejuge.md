## You'll want to know: le préjugé
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C364-JUSTICE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**le préjugé** — "the prejudice".

> Elle a beaucoup de préjugés sur les jeunes.
>
> *She has a lot of prejudices about young people.*

Literally judged in advance: *pré-* + *jugé*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*préjugé* is built from *pré-* "before" and *jugé* "judged", after Latin *praeiūdicium* "a judgement made beforehand" (Lewis & Short), built on *iūdex* "judge".
