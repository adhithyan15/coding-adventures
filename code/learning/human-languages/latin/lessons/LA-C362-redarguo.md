## You'll want to know: redarguō, redarguere
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C362-REFELLO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**redarguō, redarguere** — "to disprove, to show to be wrong".

> Testis falsum dīxit, sed iūdex eum redarguit.
>
> *The witness told a lie, but the judge proved him wrong.*

*redarguō, redarguere, redarguī*. Built on *arguō*: to prove the other side wrong, back to its face.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*redarguō* is built from *red-* "back" and *arguō* "to make clear, to prove" (Lewis & Short): to prove a claim back against the one who made it.
