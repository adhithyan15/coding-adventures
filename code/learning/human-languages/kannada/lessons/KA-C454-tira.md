## You'll want to know: ತೀರ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C454-TEPPA454-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ತೀರ** — *tīra* — "a shore; a bank".

> ತೆಪ್ಪ ಆಚೆಯ ತೀರ ತಲುಪಿತು.
>
> *teppa āceya tīra talupitu.*
>
> The coracle reached the far bank.

ಕಡಲತೀರ, a beach, is the shore of the sea.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ತೀರ** (*tīra*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *tīra*, "a shore, a bank" (CDIAL 5842).
