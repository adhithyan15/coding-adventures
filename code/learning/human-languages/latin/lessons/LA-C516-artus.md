## You'll want to know: artus, artuum
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C516-GUTT-05]; assesses=[] -->

**artus, artuum** — "joints, limbs".

> Frīgus artūs senis rigidōs facit.
>
> *The cold makes the old man's joints stiff.*

Masculine, fourth declension, nearly always plural; dative and ablative *artubus*. English *article*, a little joint, comes from its diminutive.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**artus** belongs with *arma* and a Proto-Indo-European root meaning "to fit together" (Lewis & Short; EtymDB). English *article* comes from its diminutive *articulus* "a little joint".
