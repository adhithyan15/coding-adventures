## You'll want to know: das Kostüm
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C310-KARNEVAL-03]; assesses=[] -->

**das Kostüm** — "the costume; a skirt suit".

> Sie nähte ihr Kostüm selbst.
>
> *She sewed her costume herself.*

Plural: *die Kostüme*. Also a woman's skirt suit.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Kostüm* was borrowed from French *costume*, which came from Italian and goes back to Latin *cōnsuētūdō* "custom" (EtymDB): first the dress of a time and place.
