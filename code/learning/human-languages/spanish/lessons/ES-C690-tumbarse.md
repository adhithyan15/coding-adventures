## You'll want to know: tumbarse
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C690-PLAYA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**tumbarse** — "to lie down".

> Me tumbo en la arena a leer.
>
> *I lie down on the sand to read.*

*Estar tumbado* — to be lying down.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*tumbarse* is built on *tumbar* "to knock down", which is usually taken to imitate the thud of something falling; the origin is not settled.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
