## You'll want to know: wǎngshì
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C344-MEMORY344-03]; assesses=[] -->

*wǎngshì* — "past events; things of the past".

> *nà tiān wǎnshang yéye hé wǒmen shuōqǐ le wǎngshì* — That evening my grandpa talked to us about the old days.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*wǎngshì* is built from *wǎng* 'to go' and 事 'a matter', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *hjwangX* and *dzriH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to go'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
