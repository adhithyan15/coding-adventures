Gujarati, chapter 358: Serving Others.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
સેવા (sevā) — service to others.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a social practice, then the Gujarati for to adopt.

You'll want to know: સેવા.
સેવા — sevā — "service to others".
મીરા દરેક રવિવારે દવાખાનામાં સેવા આપે છે. Mīrā darek ravivāre davākhānāmā̃ sevā āpe che. Mira volunteers at the clinic every Sunday.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
સેવા (sevā) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit sēvā, "attendance, service"; CDIAL lists an older inherited Gujarati sev, "attendance on", from the same word (CDIAL 13595).

પરોપકાર એ સૌથી મોટો ગુણ છે. paropkār e sauthī moṭo guṇ che. Doing good to others is the greatest virtue.

Lesson 5 of 5.
નિભાવવું (nibhāvvũ) — to keep up (a promise or a duty).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for doing good to others, then the Gujarati for cooperation.

You'll want to know: નિભાવવું.
નિભાવવું — nibhāvvũ — "to keep up (a promise or a duty)".
અરુણ હંમેશા પોતાનું વચન નિભાવે છે. Aruṇ hammeśā potānũ vacan nibhāve che. Arun always keeps his word.
Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે પોતાની ફરજ નિભાવી, teṇe potānī pharaj nibhāvī.

The word, taken apart.
નિભાવવું (nibhāvvũ) is inherited from Sanskrit nírvahati, "leads out of, accomplishes"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb (CDIAL 7397).
