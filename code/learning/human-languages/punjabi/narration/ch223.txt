Punjabi, chapter 223: Perhaps Next Week.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
Return to next week — retrieve next week and the next journey by ear.

Warm-up.
[your turn — hear: agle hafte]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
Say ਅਗਲੇ ਹਫ਼ਤੇ, "next week". Then the next journey: ਅਗਲੀ ਯਾਤਰਾ.

Grammar Lens.
You can now say we will go and I will do. A time word at the front tells when: ਕੱਲ੍ਹ, ਅਗਲੇ ਹਫ਼ਤੇ.

Guided Practice.

[your turn — hear: we will go tomorrow]

Lesson 2 of 5.
ਅਗਲੇ ਹਫ਼ਤੇ ਅਸੀਂ ਬਾਜ਼ਾਰ ਜਾਵਾਂਗੇ — next week we will go to the market.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say next week, then we will go to the market.

Grammar Lens: when, then who, then the future.
A plan opens with its time, then names who, and keeps the future verb last:
ਅਗਲੇ ਹਫ਼ਤੇ ਅਸੀਂ ਬਾਜ਼ਾਰ ਜਾਵਾਂਗੇ। — agle hafte asīṁ bāzār jāvāṅge. — Next week we will go to the market.
ਅਗਲੀ ਯਾਤਰਾ ਰੇਲ ਨਾਲ ਹੋਵੇਗੀ। — aglī yātrā rel nāl hovegī. — The next journey will be by train.
ਕੱਲ੍ਹ ਸਵੇਰੇ ਮੈਂ ਕੰਮ ਕਰਾਂਗਾ। — kallh savere maiṁ kamm karāṅgā. — Tomorrow morning I will work.

[your turn — hear: tomorrow morning we will work]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
Say ਕੱਲ੍ਹ ਸਵੇਰੇ ਅਸੀਂ ਕੰਮ ਕਰਾਂਗੇ।.
[once you have stopped driving — read: ਅਗਲੀ ਯਾਤਰਾ ਰੇਲ ਨਾਲ ਹੋਵੇਗੀ।]

[once you have stopped driving — write: copy ਅਗਲੇ ਹਫ਼ਤੇ ਅਸੀਂ ਬਾਜ਼ਾਰ ਜਾਵਾਂਗੇ। (agle hafte asīṁ bāzār jāvāṅge.) once]

Lesson 3 of 5.
ਸ਼ਾਇਦ (shāid) — perhaps.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new word: say next week we will go to the market, then Aman will come.

You'll want to know: ਸ਼ਾਇਦ.
ਸ਼ਾਇਦ — shāid — "perhaps". It usually opens the sentence and softens a plan:
ਸ਼ਾਇਦ ਅਮਨ ਕੱਲ੍ਹ ਆਵੇਗਾ। — shāid Aman kallh āvegā. — Perhaps Aman will come tomorrow.

The word, taken apart.
ਸ਼ਾਇਦ (shāid) is a loan: it is usually traced to Persian shāyad, "it may be; perhaps". CDIAL gives no Punjabi form.

What you've built.
One more word for talking about plans.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: shāid]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — hear: perhaps Aman will come tomorrow]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
Say ਸ਼ਾਇਦ (shāid) ਅਮਨ ਕੱਲ੍ਹ ਆਵੇਗਾ।.
[your turn — recall: say next week, then perhaps]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[once you have stopped driving — write: copy ਸ਼ਾਇਦ (shāid) once from the model]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does ਸ਼ਾਇਦ (shāid) mean? ("Perhaps".) Say it once more.


Lesson 4 of 5.
A four-line plan — a four-line spoken plan: what is sure, and what is perhaps.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say perhaps Aman will come tomorrow. Then next week we will go to the market.

Grammar Lens: sure, and perhaps.
Four lines, each with its time first. ਸ਼ਾਇਦ (shāid) marks the one that is not sure:
ਕੱਲ੍ਹ ਮੈਂ ਬਾਜ਼ਾਰ ਜਾਵਾਂਗੀ। — kallh maiṁ bāzār jāvāṅgī. — Tomorrow I will go to the market.
ਅਮਨ ਕੱਲ੍ਹ ਕੰਮ ਕਰੇਗਾ। — Aman kallh kamm karegā. — Aman will work tomorrow.
ਸ਼ਾਇਦ ਅਸੀਂ ਅਗਲੇ ਹਫ਼ਤੇ ਜਾਵਾਂਗੇ। — shāid asīṁ agle hafte jāvāṅge. — Perhaps we will go next week.
ਅਗਲੀ ਯਾਤਰਾ ਰੇਲ ਨਾਲ ਹੋਵੇਗੀ। — aglī yātrā rel nāl hovegī. — The next journey will be by train.

Guided Practice.
[your turn — hear: tomorrow I will go to the market]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
Say it with the ending that fits you.
[your turn — hear: perhaps we will go next week]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
Say ਸ਼ਾਇਦ ਅਸੀਂ ਅਗਲੇ ਹਫ਼ਤੇ ਜਾਵਾਂਗੇ। (shāid asīṁ agle hafte jāvāṅge.)
[once you have stopped driving — read: the four lines]
Say them aloud, in order.
[once you have stopped driving — write: copy the third line once]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
Say the four lines again without the model.
