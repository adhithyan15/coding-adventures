## You'll want to know: xìnggé
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C258-KIND258-05]; assesses=[] -->

*xìnggé* — "personality; character".

> *wǒ xǐhuan tā de xìnggé* — I like his personality.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*xìnggé* is built from *xìng* 'nature' and *gé* 'a pattern', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *sjengH* and *kaek* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'nature and conduct' and the second 'a model' and 'a measure'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
