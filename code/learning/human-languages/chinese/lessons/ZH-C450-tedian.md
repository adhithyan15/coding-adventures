## You'll want to know: 特点
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C450-LOCAL450-02]; assesses=[] -->

**特点** — *tèdiǎn* — "characteristic; a distinguishing trait".

> **这儿的菜有什么特点** *zhèr de cài yǒu shénme tèdiǎn* — What's special about the food here?

The new character, in pieces: 特 — ten strokes in two pieces: on the left the cow piece, four strokes; on the right the earth piece of 地 over the right piece of 对. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**特点** (*tèdiǎn*) is built from 特 'single, special' and 点 'a point', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *dok* and *temX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first as a bull and 'alone' and the second as a dot made with the brush.
