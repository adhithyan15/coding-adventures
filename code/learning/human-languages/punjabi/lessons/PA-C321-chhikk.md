## You'll want to know: ਛਿੱਕ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C321-COLD321-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ਛਿੱਕ** — *chhikk* — "a sneeze".

> ਮਿਰਚ ਤੋਂ ਮੈਨੂੰ ਛਿੱਕ ਆਈ।
>
> *mirach ton mainū̃ chhikk āī.*
>
> The chilli made me sneeze.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਛਿੱਕ** (*chhikk*) is inherited from Sanskrit *chikkā*, "a sneeze"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *chikk*, "sneeze", under it (CDIAL 5032).
