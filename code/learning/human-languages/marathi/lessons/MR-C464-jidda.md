## You'll want to know: जिद्द
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C464-HIMMAT464-02]; assesses=[] -->

**जिद्द** — *jidda* — "grit; tenacity".

> जिद्दीने अभ्यास करून ती उत्तीर्ण झाली.
>
> *jiddīne abhyās karūn tī uttīrṇa jhālī.*
>
> She studied with grit and passed.

Feminine.

जिद्दीने, doggedly; जिद्दी, tenacious.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**जिद्द** (*jidda*) is a loan from Hindustani *zidd*, "insistence", usually traced to Arabic *didd*, "opposite"; Marathi writes the *z* as ज.
