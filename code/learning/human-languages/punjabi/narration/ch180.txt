Punjabi, chapter 180: People in the Big Room.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
ਲੋਕ (lok) — people.

Warm-up.
[your turn — hear: lok; YOU SAY: ਲੋਕ]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
Start by ear. This one word means people.

Grammar Lens: one group word.
ਲੋਕ — lok — people.
Keep this as one useful word. No new plural rule is needed today.

The word, taken apart.
ਲੋਕ (lok) is Sanskrit loka, "world, people", taken over as a learned loan. Mahan Kosh identifies the Sanskrit word; CDIAL 11119 gives the same meanings.

Guided Practice.
[your turn — hear: lok; YOU SAY: ਲੋਕ]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[once you have stopped driving — read: ਲੋਕ (lok)]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
Say people. (ਲੋਕ (lok).)
Sources: Punjabi University, RCPLT dictionary, lists ਲੋਕ (lok) as people or public. Mahan Kosh identifies Sanskrit loka; Turner gives its senses in CDIAL 11119.


Lesson 2 of 7.
ਉਸ (us) — pointing to that place.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say people once. (ਲੋਕ (lok).)

Grammar Lens: one pointing word.
ਉਸ ਬਾਗ਼ ਵਿੱਚ ਲੋਕ ਹਨ। — us bāġ vic lok han — people are in that garden.
Here ਉਸ (us) points to that garden. Treat the whole line as one place model; it does not teach every form of that.

Guided Practice.
[your turn — hear: the garden model; YOU SAY: ਉਸ (us) ਬਾਗ਼ ਵਿੱਚ ਲੋਕ (lok) ਹਨ।]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[once you have stopped driving — read: ਉਸ (us) ਬਾਗ਼ ਵਿੱਚ ਲੋਕ (lok) ਹਨ।]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
Say that garden, then people. (ਉਸ (us) ਬਾਗ਼, ਲੋਕ (lok).)
Source: Punjabi University, RCPLT dictionary, lists ਉਸ (us) as that in a conjunctive form.


Lesson 3 of 7.
ਥਾਂ (thaan) — one place word.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Retrieve ਉਸ (us) and ਲੋਕ (lok) by ear.

Grammar Lens: one location noun.
ਥਾਂ (thaan) — thā̃ — place.
ਉਸ ਥਾਂ (thaan) — us thā̃ — that place.
Keep this as a two-word pointing phrase, not a new rule for every location noun.

The word, taken apart.
ਥਾਂ (thaan) (thā̃) is a tadbhava, inherited and reshaped by sound change. It comes through Prakrit thāma from Sanskrit sthāman, "station, place" (CDIAL 13760).

Guided Practice.
[your turn — hear: us thā̃; YOU SAY: ਉਸ ਥਾਂ (thaan)]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[once you have stopped driving — read: ਉਸ (us) ਥਾਂ (thaan)]
[your turn — say: ਲੋਕ (lok)]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
Say that place. (ਉਸ (us) ਥਾਂ (thaan).)
Sources: Punjabi University, RCPLT dictionary, gives ਥਾਂ (thaan) as place or location. Turner derives it through Prakrit thāma from Sanskrit sthāman in CDIAL 13760.


Lesson 4 of 7.
ਕਮਰੇ (kamre) — room in one location phrase.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say ਉਸ (us) ਥਾਂ (thaan), then the old room word ਕਮਰਾ.

Grammar Lens: one room phrase.
ਕਮਰਾ — kamrā — a room (the old word).
ਕਮਰੇ ਵਿੱਚ — kamre vic — in the room.
The ending changes inside this one location phrase. Use this model first; do not infer every possible room ending from it.

Guided Practice.
[your turn — hear: kamre vic; YOU SAY: ਕਮਰੇ ਵਿੱਚ]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[once you have stopped driving — read: ਕਮਰੇ (kamre) ਵਿੱਚ]
[your turn — say: ਉਸ (us) ਥਾਂ (thaan)]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
Say in the room, then that place. (ਕਮਰੇ (kamre) ਵਿੱਚ, ਉਸ (us) ਥਾਂ (thaan).)
Source: Punjabi University, library description, uses ਕਮਰੇ (kamre) ਵਿੱਚ in its phrase for 'in the study room'.


Lesson 5 of 7.
ਵੱਡੇ (vadde) — big in one room phrase.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say the old ਵੱਡਾ, then ਕਮਰੇ (kamre) ਵਿੱਚ.

Grammar Lens: one agreeing phrase.
ਵੱਡੇ (vadde) ਕਮਰੇ ਵਿੱਚ — vaḍḍe kamre vic — in the big room.
This is one bounded location phrase. Notice ਵੱਡੇ (vadde) beside ਕਮਰੇ (kamre); a full adjective-agreement table comes later, not here.

Guided Practice.
[your turn — hear: the big-room phrase; YOU SAY: ਵੱਡੇ (vadde) ਕਮਰੇ (kamre) ਵਿੱਚ]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[once you have stopped driving — read: ਵੱਡੇ (vadde) ਕਮਰੇ (kamre) ਵਿੱਚ]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
Say in the big room. (ਵੱਡੇ (vadde) ਕਮਰੇ (kamre) ਵਿੱਚ.)
Source: UK Government, Punjabi fire-safety guide, uses ਵੱਡੇ (vadde) ਘਰ ਵਿੱਚ; Punjabi University, library description, uses ਕਮਰੇ (kamre) ਵਿੱਚ in its room phrase.


Lesson 6 of 7.
That place, its big room — one guided review.

Warm-up.
[pause 3 seconds]
Recall people, that place, then in the big room: ਲੋਕ (lok), ਉਸ (us) ਥਾਂ (thaan), ਵੱਡੇ (vadde) ਕਮਰੇ (kamre) ਵਿੱਚ.

Guided Practice: fit the known pieces.
ਉਸ ਥਾਂ ਦੇ ਵੱਡੇ ਕਮਰੇ ਵਿੱਚ (us thaan de vadde kamre vich) — us thā̃ de vaḍḍe kamre vic — in the big room of that place.
Use the whole phrase as a bounded model. ਦੇ links the already known pieces here; this is not a new possessive paradigm.
[your turn — hear: the place-and-room phrase; YOU SAY: ਉਸ ਥਾਂ ਦੇ ਵੱਡੇ ਕਮਰੇ ਵਿੱਚ (us thaan de vadde kamre vich)]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[once you have stopped driving — read: ਉਸ ਥਾਂ ਦੇ ਵੱਡੇ ਕਮਰੇ ਵਿੱਚ (us thaan de vadde kamre vich)]
[your turn — say: ਲੋਕ (lok)]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
