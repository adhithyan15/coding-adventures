# غ (ghayn) — the letter inside الغد

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[AR-SCRIPT-C48-ALIF-MADDA-01, AR-SCRIPT-C48-ZAY-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: write آ and ز once each, and name them.

[PAUSE 2s] Now say **إلى الغد** (*ilā l-ghad*), "see you tomorrow".
You have read it for a long time. This lesson takes one letter out of it and puts
it in your hand.

## Script you'll notice: غ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-SCRIPT-C48-GHAYN-01]; assesses=[] -->

**غ** — *ghayn*, a *gh* gargled at the back of the throat.

It is inside **إلى الغد** (*ilā l-ghad*), "see you tomorrow". It is **ع** (*ʿayn*),
which you already write, with **one dot above**, the third family where a single
dot turns one sound into another.

## Writing: غ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[AR-SCRIPT-C48-GHAYN-01] -->

- **1.** start here: sweep left from the upper-right tip and shape the open head
- **2.** continue down and around the lower bowl without lifting
- **3.** lift once, then place the upper dot last — and only now lift

**Pen lifts: 1.**

> Stroke order is one attested teaching order; hands and teachers vary.
> Source: Introduction to Arabic: Egyptian Arabic for first-year students, Alphabet ع غ, independent Ghayn at 00:02.4–00:04.0 (University of Oregon Libraries Open Textbook, 2023; accessed 2026-08-23).
