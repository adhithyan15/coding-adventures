## You'll want to know: そふ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C243-KIN243-02]; assesses=[] -->

**そふ** — *sofu* — "my grandfather; a grandfather".

> **そふは もう はたらきません。** — *sofu wa mō hatarakimasen* — "My grandfather no longer works."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**そふ** (*sofu*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *so*, "ancestor" (Middle Chinese *tsuX*) and *fu*, "father" (*bjuX*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
