## You'll want to know: reconciliō, reconciliāre
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C537-IURG-02]; assesses=[] -->

**reconciliō, reconciliāre** — "to reconcile, bring together again".

> Māter frātrēs post iūrgium reconciliat.
>
> *Mother reconciles the brothers after the quarrel.*

*reconciliō, reconciliāre, reconciliāvī, reconciliātum*: *re-* 'again' + *conciliō* 'to bring together'. English *reconcile*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**reconciliō** is built on *re-* "again" and *conciliō* "to bring together", from *concilium* "a gathering" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short). English *reconcile* comes from it.
