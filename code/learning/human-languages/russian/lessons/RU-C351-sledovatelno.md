## You'll want to know: следовательно
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C351-POSKOLKU-05]; assesses=[] -->

**следовательно** (*slédovatelno*) — "therefore, consequently".

> Он не звонил, следовательно, всё в порядке.
>
> *On ne zvoníl, slédovatelno, vsyo v poryádke.*
>
> He has not called, so everything is all right.

A formal link between a premise and a conclusion, set off by commas. In conversation people say *значит*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**следовательно** is built on *следовать* "to follow", from *след* "a track, a trail" (EtymDB): what follows from what came before.
