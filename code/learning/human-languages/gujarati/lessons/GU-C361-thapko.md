## You'll want to know: ઠપકો
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C361-TIKA361-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ઠપકો** — *ṭhapko* — "a telling-off".

> હું મોડો આવ્યો એટલે ગુરુએ મને ઠપકો આપ્યો.
>
> *hũ moḍo āvyo eṭle gurue mane ṭhapko āpyo.*
>
> I came late, so the teacher told me off.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ઠપકો** (*ṭhapko*) CDIAL lists the Gujarati noun, "a rebuke", under a reconstructed *ṭhapp*, "strike, press" (CDIAL 5495).
