# (reading) — six lines, no voice attached

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-SKILL-READ-WORDS] -->

[PAUSE 2s] The last lesson gave you single words. These are whole lines.

[PAUSE 1s] You have said every one of them out loud. None of them is new.

## Reading
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-SKILL-READ-LINES]; assesses=[GU-CONCEPT-C02-MAARUNNAAMCHHE-01, GU-CONCEPT-C03-TAMEKEMCHHO-01] -->

> મારું નામ મીરા છે।
> તમે કેમ છો?
> હું મજામાં છું।
> વાંધો નહીં।
> માફ કરો।
> કાલે મળીશું।

[PAUSE 3s] Read down once, without stopping.

[PAUSE 3s] Again — and this time notice that nobody said them first. Until now
every one of these arrived in your ear before it arrived on the page.

## You'll want to know
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-CONCEPT-C03-MAJAA-01, GU-CONCEPT-C03-VANDHONAHI-01, GU-LEX-MAAF-01, GU-CONCEPT-C04-KAALE-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] These six are not a list. Read in order they are most of a
conversation: a name, a question, an answer, a repair, an apology, a parting.

[PAUSE 2s] That is worth seeing once on the page. Spoken, each of them came
with a voice telling you what it was for. Written, the order has to do that
work instead.

[PAUSE 2s] Six lines, and the order is the whole lesson.
