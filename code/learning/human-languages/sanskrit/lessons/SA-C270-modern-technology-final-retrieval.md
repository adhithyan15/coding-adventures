# Two modern-technology words: round two

## Warm-up

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[SA-LEX-C105-THINGS105-02, SA-LEX-C269-MEDIA-02] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Prepare to retrieve the same two meanings in a fresh order.

## Guided Practice: independent retrieval in a fresh order

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[SA-LEX-C105-THINGS105-02, SA-LEX-C269-MEDIA-02] -->

No Sanskrit model is shown before you answer.

[PAUSE 8s] Say the Sanskrit for these meanings in the reverse order: the
internet, then a computer.

## What you've built: check after answering

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[SA-LEX-C105-THINGS105-02, SA-LEX-C269-MEDIA-02] -->

Now check: **अन्तर्जालम्** (*antarjālam*) is “the internet,” and **संगणकम्**
(*saṅgaṇakam*) is “a computer.”

## Wrap-up Recall: boundary

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[SA-LEX-C105-THINGS105-02, SA-LEX-C269-MEDIA-02] -->

You can retrieve two modern-technology nouns twice without a visible model.
This is not evidence of device use, internet navigation, digital literacy,
technological competence, human validation, learner attainment, or exam
readiness.
