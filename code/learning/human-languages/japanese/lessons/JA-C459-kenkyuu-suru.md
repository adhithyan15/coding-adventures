## You'll want to know: けんきゅうする
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C459-STUDY459-03]; assesses=[] -->

**けんきゅうする** — *kenkyū suru* — "to do research".

> **かれは だいがくで しょくぶつを けんきゅうしています。** — *kare wa daigaku de shokubutsu o kenkyū shite imasu* — "He does research on plants at a university."

Polite **けんきゅうします**, *kenkyūshimasu*; a する verb. Past (polite): **せんせいが どうぶつを けんきゅうしました。**, *sensei ga dōbutsu o kenkyū shimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**けんきゅうする** (*kenkyū suru*) is Sino-Japanese (kango) *kenkyū* with the native verb *suru*, "to do": two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *ken*, "polish" (Middle Chinese *ngen*) and *kyū*, "research" (*kjuwH*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
