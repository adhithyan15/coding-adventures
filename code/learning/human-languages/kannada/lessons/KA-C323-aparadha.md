## You'll want to know: ಅಪರಾಧ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C323-APARADHA323-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ಅಪರಾಧ** — *aparādha* — "a crime; an offence".

> ಅಪರಾಧ ಮಾಡಿದರೆ ಶಿಕ್ಷೆ ಸಿಗುತ್ತದೆ.
>
> *aparādha māḍidare śikṣe siguttade.*
>
> If you commit a crime, you get punished.

One who commits a crime is an ಅಪರಾಧಿ, a criminal. A crime is "done": ಅಪರಾಧ ಮಾಡು.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಅಪರಾಧ** (*aparādha*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *aparādha*, "an offence, a fault" (CDIAL 439).
