## You'll want to know: がっき
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C469-INSTR469-01]; assesses=[] -->

**がっき** — *gakki* — "a musical instrument".

> **あねは がっきが じょうずです。** — *ane wa gakki ga jōzu desu* — "My elder sister is good at instruments."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**がっき** (*gakki*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *ga*, "music" (Middle Chinese *ngaewk*) and *ki*, "instrument" (*khijH*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
