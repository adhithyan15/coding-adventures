## You'll want to know: خلاصه
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C313-AMAR-04]; assesses=[] -->

**خلاصه** (*kholâse*) — "a summary; in short".

> گوینده خلاصه اخبار را خواند.
>
> *Guyande kholâse-ye akhbâr râ khând.*
>
> The newsreader read a summary of the news.

In speech also "in short, anyway": *kholâse, dir residim* "anyway, we arrived late".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*kholâse* is borrowed from Arabic *khulāṣa* (EtymDB lists the Arabic word), usually glossed "the essence, an extract".
