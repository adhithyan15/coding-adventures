## You'll want to know: der Stadtteil
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C439-VIERTEL-01]; assesses=[] -->

**der Stadtteil** — "the district, the part of town".

> In unserem Stadtteil gibt es zwei Schulen.
>
> *There are two schools in our part of town.*

Plural: *die Stadtteile*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Stadtteil* is built from *Stadt* 'town' and *Teil* 'part', inherited from Proto-Germanic *dailą* (EtymDB).
