## You'll want to know: اجڑنا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C315-AJDAD315-05]; assesses=[] -->

**اجڑنا** — *ujaṛnā* — "to be ruined; to become deserted".

> سیلاب کے بعد گاؤں اجڑ گیا۔
>
> *sailāb ke bād gāoṅ ujaṛ gayā.*
>
> After the flood the village was left deserted.

Past (it …): وہ شہر اجڑ گیا, *voh shahr ujaṛ gayā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**اجڑنا** (*ujaṛnā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *ujaṛnā*, "to be uprooted", under a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 1661). A place **اجڑ گیا** when its people left or were driven out.
