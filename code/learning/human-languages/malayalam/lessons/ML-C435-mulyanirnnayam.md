## You'll want to know: മൂല്യനിർണ്ണയം
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C435-BIRUDAM435-05]; assesses=[] -->

**മൂല്യനിർണ്ണയം** — *mūlyanirṇṇayaṁ* — "an evaluation; marking".

> പരീക്ഷയുടെ മൂല്യനിർണ്ണയം അടുത്ത ആഴ്ച തീരും.
>
> *parīkṣayuṭe mūlyanirṇṇayaṁ aṭutta āḻca tīruṁ.*
>
> The marking of the exam will finish next week.

The marking of exam papers is its everyday use.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**മൂല്യനിർണ്ണയം** (*mūlyanirṇṇayaṁ*) is built from Sanskrit *mūlya*, "value, price" (CDIAL 10257), and *nirṇaya*, "settling, decision" (Monier-Williams): deciding the value of a thing.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
