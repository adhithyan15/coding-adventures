## You'll want to know: दुआ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C539-DUA539-02]; assesses=[] -->

**दुआ** — *duā* — "a prayer, a blessing (for someone)".

> दादी ने मेरी सेहत के लिए दुआ की।
>
> *Dādī ne merī sehat ke liye duā kī.*
>
> Grandmother prayed for my health.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**दुआ** (*duā*) is usually traced to Arabic *duʿā*, "a call, a prayer", through Persian.
