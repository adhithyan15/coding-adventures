## You'll want to know: atacar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C530-PELIGRO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**atacar** — "to attack".

> Un perro atacó al cartero.
>
> *A dog attacked the postman.*

It takes *qu* before *e*: *ataqué*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*atacar* was borrowed from Italian *attaccare* 'to attach, to fasten' (EtymDB); to join battle was to fasten on to the enemy.
