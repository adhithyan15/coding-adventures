## You'll want to know: آرایشگاه
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C454-KAFFASH-04]; assesses=[] -->

**آرایشگاه** (*ârâyeshgâh*) — "a hairdresser's, a barber's (shop)".

> خواهرم پنجشنبه به آرایشگاه رفت.
>
> *Khâharam panjshanbe be ârâyeshgâh raft.*
>
> My sister went to the hairdresser's on Thursday.

*ârâyesh* 'adornment' + *gâh* 'place'. The person is an *ârâyeshgar*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ârâyeshgâh* is a native compound of *ârâyesh* 'adornment', from *ârâstan* 'to adorn', and *gâh* 'place'.
