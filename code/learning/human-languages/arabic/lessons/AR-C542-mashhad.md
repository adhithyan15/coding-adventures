## You'll want to know: مشهد
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C542-MASRAH542-02]; assesses=[] -->

**مشهد** — *mashhad* — "a scene; a view".

> بكت أختي في المشهد الأخير.
>
> *bakat ukhtī fī al-mashhad al-akhīr.*
>
> My sister cried at the last scene.

Plural: مشاهد, *mashāhid*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**مشهد** (*mashhad*) is built on the native Arabic root *sh-h-d*, "to be present, to witness" (Lane). Shāhada, to watch, which you know, is from the same root: a mashhad is what is witnessed.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
