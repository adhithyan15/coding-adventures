Arabic, chapter 240: Out of order, Ready, Genuine, Suitable, Excellent.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
معطل (muʿaṭṭal) — out of order.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a memory, then the Arabic for doubt.

You'll want to know: معطل.
معطل — muʿaṭṭal — "out of order".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: muʿaṭṭal]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: muʿaṭṭal, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say muʿaṭṭal]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Arabic for a memory, then the Arabic for doubt, then say muʿaṭṭal again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does معطل (muʿaṭṭal) mean? ("Out of order".) Say it once more.

Sunset? (غروب, ghurūb.) Birth? (ميلاد, mīlād.) Yearly? (سنوي, sanawī.) Late morning? (ضحى, ḍuḥā.) Soon? (قريبا, qarīban.) An alarm clock? (منبه, munabbih.) A calendar? (تقويم, taqwīm.) A museum? (متحف, matḥaf.) A stadium? (ملعب, malʿab.) A pavement; a platform? (رصيف, raṣīf.) A crossroads? (تقاطع, taqāṭuʿ.) An embassy? (سفارة, sifāra.)
