## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**وسط** (*wasaṭ*), "the middle", is an old native noun, listed under the root *w-s-ṭ* (Lane) beside a verb meaning "to sit in the middle".
