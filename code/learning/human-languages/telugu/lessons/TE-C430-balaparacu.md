## You'll want to know: బలపరచు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C430-SAMATULYAM430-01]; assesses=[] -->

**బలపరచు** — *balaparacu* — "to strengthen; to back up".

> ఆ సాక్ష్యం అతని వాదనను బలపరిచింది.
>
> *ā sākṣyaṁ atani vādananu balaparicindi.*
>
> That evidence strengthened his argument.

Past (I …): బలపరిచాను, *balaparicānu*.

బలం, strength, which you know, + -పరచు.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**బలపరచు** (*balaparacu*) is built from Sanskrit *bala*, "power, strength" (CDIAL 9161), the word in బలం, and -పరచు, a native ending that makes a verb (DEDR 3853 lists *paṟacu*, "to cause to feel", beside *paḍu*).
