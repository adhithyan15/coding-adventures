## You'll want to know: invejoso
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C468-INVEJA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**invejoso** — "envious".

> O vizinho invejoso não gosta do nosso carro novo.
>
> *The envious neighbour does not like our new car.*

Built on *a inveja* with *-oso*; a woman is *invejosa*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*invejoso* is built on *inveja* "envy", inherited from Latin *invidia* (Lewis & Short); compare Latin *invidiōsus* "envious".
