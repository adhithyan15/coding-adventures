## You'll want to know: તડકો
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C213-WEATHER213-02]; assesses=[] -->

**તડકો** — *taḍko* — "sunshine, the sun's heat".

> **આવતીકાલે તડકો હશે.** — *āvtīkāle taḍko haśe* — "It will be sunny tomorrow."

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

The origin of **તડકો** (*taḍko*) is not settled. CDIAL lists no Gujarati form like it; its nearest entry, Sanskrit *tāpa*, "heat", gives Old Gujarati *tāvaḍu*, "heat of the sun", but no link to *taḍko* is made there (CDIAL 5767).
