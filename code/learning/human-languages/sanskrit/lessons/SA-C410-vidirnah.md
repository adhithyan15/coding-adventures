## You'll want to know: विदीर्णः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C410-CHIDRA410-04]; assesses=[] -->

**विदीर्णः** — *vidīrṇaḥ* — "split; burst open".

> शकटस्य फलकं विदीर्णम् अस्ति।
>
> *śakaṭasya phalakaṃ vidīrṇam asti.*
>
> The cart's plank is split.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**विदीर्णः** (*vidīrṇaḥ*) is built from *vi*, "apart", and the root √dṝ, "to burst, to split"; MW gives *vidīrṇa* as rent asunder, split, burst open (MW).
