# ঠকানো (ṭhôkāno) — to cheat; to swindle

## You'll want to know: ঠকানো
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C458-SOT458-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ঠকানো** — *ṭhôkāno* — "to cheat; to swindle".

> দোকানদার আমাকে দশ টাকা ঠকাল।
>
> *dokāndār āmāke dôsh ṭākā ṭhôkālô.*
>
> The shopkeeper cheated me out of ten taka.

Past (he/she …): তিনি খদ্দেরকে ঠকালেন, *tini khôdderke ṭhôkālen*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ঠকানো** (*ṭhôkāno*) is listed by CDIAL for Bengali, as deceiving, under a reconstructed *ṭhagg*, "cheat" (CDIAL 5489); *ṭhôkā*, to be cheated, is its plain partner.
