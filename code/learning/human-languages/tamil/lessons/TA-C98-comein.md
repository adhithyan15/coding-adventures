# வாருங்கள் (vāruṅkaḷ) — please come (in)

## You'll want to know: வாருங்கள்
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C98-WELCOME-04]; assesses=[] -->

**வாருங்கள்** (*vāruṅkaḷ*) — "please come", "come in".

It is the verb **வா**, "come", with the respectful **-ங்கள்**. At the door, it is the welcome.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

A welcome at the door.
