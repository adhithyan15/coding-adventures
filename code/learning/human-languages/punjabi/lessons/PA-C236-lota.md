## You'll want to know: ਲੋਟਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C236-KAM236-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ਲੋਟਾ** — *loṭā* — "a small water pot".

> ਲੋਟੇ ਵਿੱਚ ਪਾਣੀ ਹੈ।
>
> *loṭe vich pāṇī hai.*
>
> There is water in the pot.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਲੋਟਾ** (*loṭā*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*lōṭṭa*, "waterpot"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *loṭṭā*, "waterpot" (CDIAL 11133).
