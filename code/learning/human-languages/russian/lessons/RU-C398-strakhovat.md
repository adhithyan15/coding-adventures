## You'll want to know: страховать
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C398-ARENDA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**страховать** (*strakhovát*) — "to insure".

> Мы страхуем дом от пожара.
>
> *My strakhúyem dom ot pozhára.*
>
> We insure the house against fire.

Imperfective; present *страхую, страхуешь*; perfective *застраховать*. *Страховать от* + genitive.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**страховать** is built on *страх* "fear", inherited from Proto-Slavic: to insure is to guard against what you fear.
