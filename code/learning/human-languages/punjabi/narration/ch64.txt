Punjabi, chapter 64: Lentils, Fruit, Apple, Banana, Potato.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ਦਾਲ (dāl) — lentils.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for a fish, then the Punjabi for rice.

You'll want to know: ਦਾਲ.
ਦਾਲ — dāl — "lentils".

The word, taken apart.
ਦਾਲ (dāl) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from dāla, "kind of grain, split peas" (CDIAL 6309). CDIAL asks whether it is the same word as dāla, "splitting".
