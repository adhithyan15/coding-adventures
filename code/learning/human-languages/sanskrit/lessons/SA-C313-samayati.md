## You'll want to know: शमयति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C313-UPADRAVA313-05]; assesses=[] -->

**शमयति** — *śamayati* — "calms; quells".

> आरक्षकाः उपद्रवं शमयन्ति।
>
> *ārakṣakāḥ upadravaṃ śamayanti.*
>
> The police quell the disturbance.

Past (he …): सः कलहं शमितवान्, *saḥ kalahaṃ śamitavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**शमयति** (*śamayati*) comes from the root √śam, "to be quiet", as in *śāmyati*, in its causative form: to make quiet, calm (MW).
