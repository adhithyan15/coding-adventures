## You'll want to know: সবাই
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C277-SAKSHI277-03]; assesses=[] -->

**সবাই** — *sôbāi* — "everyone; everybody".

> আওয়াজ শুনে সবাই বাইরে এল।
>
> *āoyāj shune sôbāi bāire elô.*
>
> When they heard the noise, everyone came outside.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**সবাই** (*sôbāi*) is সব, 'all' — Sanskrit *sarva* (CDIAL 13276, which lists Bengali *sab*) — with the emphatic ending that makes it 'every single one'. সবাই এল, everyone came.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
