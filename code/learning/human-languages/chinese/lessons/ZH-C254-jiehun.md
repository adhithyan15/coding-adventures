## You'll want to know: jiéhūn
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C254-BORN254-02]; assesses=[] -->

*jiéhūn* — "to get married".

> *wǒ gēge jiéhūn le* — My brother has got married.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*jiéhūn* is built from *jié* 'to tie' and *hūn* 'marriage', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *ket* and *xwon* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to tie' and the second 'marriage'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for talking about the past.
