## You'll want to know: വളയുക
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C382-KALAPAM382-03]; assesses=[] -->

**വളയുക** — *vaḷayuka* — "to surround".

> പോലീസ് ആ വീട് വളഞ്ഞു.
>
> *pōlīsŭ ā vīṭŭ vaḷaññu.*
>
> The police surrounded the house.

Past: വളഞ്ഞു, *vaḷaññu*.

The same form also means to bend, as in വളവ്, a bend.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**വളയുക** (*vaḷayuka*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 5313 lists Malayalam *vaḷayuka*, "to surround", with വള, "a ring, a bangle": to make a ring round something.
