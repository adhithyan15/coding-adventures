## You'll want to know: कामयते
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C455-ANURAGA455-02]; assesses=[] -->

**कामयते** — *kāmayate* — "desires".

> सर्वः जनः सुखं कामयते।
>
> *sarvaḥ janaḥ sukhaṃ kāmayate.*
>
> Everyone desires happiness.

Past (he …): सः सुखं कामितवान्, *saḥ sukhaṃ kāmitavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**कामयते** (*kāmayate*) comes from the root √kam, "to love, to desire" (MW).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
