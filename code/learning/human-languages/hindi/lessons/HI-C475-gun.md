## You'll want to know: गुण
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C475-CHARITRA475-04]; assesses=[] -->

**गुण** — *guṇ* — "a virtue; a good quality".

> धैर्य अरुण का सबसे बड़ा गुण है।
>
> *dhairya Aruṇ kā sabse baṛā guṇ hai.*
>
> Patience is Arun's greatest virtue.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**गुण** (*guṇ*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *guṇa*, first "a single strand of a cord" and then "a quality" and "good quality, virtue, merit" (Monier-Williams).
