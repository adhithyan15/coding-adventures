Kannada, chapter 113: Property, Owner, Strength, Wish, Dream.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಆಸ್ತಿ (āsti) — property.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a chance, then the Kannada for a tool.

You'll want to know: ಆಸ್ತಿ.
ಆಸ್ತಿ — āsti — "property".

The word, taken apart.
The origin of ಆಸ್ತಿ (āsti) is uncertain. It is often linked to Sanskrit asti, "it is" (CDIAL 977), so that property is "what one has", but no source settles it.

Lesson 2 of 5.
ಯಜಮಾನ (yajamāna) — an owner, the master.

Warm-up.

Before the new one: say the Kannada for a tool, then the Kannada for property.

You'll want to know: ಯಜಮಾನ.
ಯಜಮಾನ — yajamāna — "an owner, the master".

The word, taken apart.
ಯಜಮಾನ (yajamāna) is Sanskrit yajamāna, "the one who pays for a sacrifice", taken over whole. The patron who paid became the master of the house.

Lesson 4 of 5.
ಆಸೆ (āse) — a wish, a desire.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for an owner, then the Kannada for strength.

You'll want to know: ಆಸೆ.
ಆಸೆ — āse — "a wish, a desire".

The word, taken apart.
ಆಸೆ (āse) comes from Sanskrit āśā, "wish, expectation", reshaped to Kannada sounds: a tadbhava (CDIAL 1456). The ś became s, and the final -ā became Kannada -e.

Lesson 5 of 5.

You'll want to know: ಕನಸು.
ಕನಸು — kanasu — "a dream".

The word, taken apart.
ಕನಸು (kanasu) is native Dravidian, one word with Tamil kaṉavu and Telugu kala (DEDR 1407). DEDR lists kanasu itself, "dream".
