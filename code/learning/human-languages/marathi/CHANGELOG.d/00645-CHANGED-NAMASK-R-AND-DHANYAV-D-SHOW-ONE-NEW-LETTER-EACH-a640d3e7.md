## Changed — namaskār and dhanyavād show one new letter each

The script-ramp report (`ramp.script`, policy `maxNewGlyphsPerLesson: 3`)
counts every target-script shape the first time a lesson body shows it.
It listed MR-C01-namaskar (7) and MR-C01-dhanyavad (4); it now lists no
Marathi lesson.

- **MR-C01-namaskar** (7 → 1) now opens the way Gujarati and Malayalam do:
  romanized heading, the greeting heard and used first, and a detachable
  "Writing — let your finger meet one shape" observe-trace of **न**. It says
  plainly that the whole spelling arrives after the next lessons; the MR-W01
  runway now introduces one shape per lesson and MR-W01-namaskar-read shows the
  whole word. The halant question moves to MR-W01-virama, where it is taught.
  The lesson's skills read `[listening, speaking, writing]`.
- **MR-C01-dhanyavad** (4 → 1) no longer prints the whole word in its heading.
  It shows only **द**, the sign that opens the next word, *duḥkh*; ध, य and व
  first appear in their own MR-W03 letter lessons.
- Writing still starts on lesson one, and script closure stays 0. Regenerated
  book chapters 1 and 2, narration, modality and hashes.
