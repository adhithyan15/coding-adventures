Persian, chapter 379: Leaving a job.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
استعفا دادن (este'fâ dâdan) — to resign.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a bonus, then the Persian for benefits.

You'll want to know: استعفا دادن.
استعفا دادن (este'fâ dâdan) — "to resign".
مدیر شرکت دیروز استعفا داد. modir-e sherkat diruz este'fâ dâd. The company director resigned yesterday.
Este'fâ "resignation" + dâdan (stem deh-). Az kâr este'fâ dâdan — to resign from a job.

The word, taken apart.
استعفا is usually traced to Arabic isti'fā' "asking to be excused, asking to be let off"; with dâdan "to give" you hand in your resignation.

Lesson 2 of 5.
اخراج کردن (ekhrâj kardan) — to dismiss, to fire.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for benefits, then the Persian for to resign.

You'll want to know: اخراج کردن.
اخراج کردن (ekhrâj kardan) — "to dismiss, to fire".
شرکت ده کارمند را اخراج کرد. sherkat dah kârmand râ ekhrâj kard. The company dismissed ten employees.
Also to expel a pupil. The person takes râ.

The word, taken apart.
اخراج is usually traced to Arabic ikhrāj "putting out, sending out", from the same Arabic family as Persian khârej "outside".

Lesson 3 of 5.
اخراج شدن (ekhrâj shodan) — to be dismissed, to be fired.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to resign, then the Persian for to dismiss.

You'll want to know: اخراج شدن.
اخراج شدن (ekhrâj shodan) — "to be dismissed, to be fired".
او به خاطر دیر آمدن اخراج شد. u be khâter-e dir âmadan ekhrâj shod. He was fired for coming late.
The passive of ekhrâj kardan, with shodan (stem shav-).

The word, taken apart.
اخراج شدن (ekhrâj shodan) joins ekhrâj, usually traced to Arabic ikhrāj "putting out", to the inherited Persian verb shodan "to become".

Lesson 4 of 5.
بیکاری (bikâri) — unemployment.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to dismiss, then the Persian for to be dismissed.

You'll want to know: بیکاری.
بیکاری (bikâri) — "unemployment".
بیکاری بین جوانها زیاد است. bikâri beyn-e javânhâ ziyâd ast. Unemployment is high among young people.
From bikâr "unemployed": bi- "without" + kâr "work" + -i.

The word, taken apart.
بیکاری (bikâri) is native Persian, built from bi- "without" + kâr "work" (EtymDB traces it to Middle Persian) + the noun suffix -i.

Lesson 5 of 5.
بازنشستگی (bâzneshastegi) — retirement.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to be dismissed, then the Persian for unemployment.

You'll want to know: بازنشستگی.
بازنشستگی (bâzneshastegi) — "retirement".
پدربزرگم بعد از بازنشستگی بیشتر کتاب میخواند. pedarbozorgam ba'd az bâzneshastegi bishtar ketâb mikhânad. Since he retired, my grandfather reads more.
From bâzneshaste "retired": bâz "back" + neshaste "seated".

The word, taken apart.
بازنشستگی (bâzneshastegi) is native Persian, built from bâz "back" + neshaste, the participle of neshastan "to sit", + -gi. A retired person has sat back down.
