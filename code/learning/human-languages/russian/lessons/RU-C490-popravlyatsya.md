## You'll want to know: поправляться
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C490-RECOVERY-03]; assesses=[] -->

**поправляться** (*popravlyátsya*) — "to get better; to put on weight".

> После болезни она быстро поправляется.
>
> *Pósle bolézni oná býstro popravlyáyetsya.*
>
> After her illness she is getting better quickly.

Imperfective; perfective поправиться (поправлюсь). Also to put on weight.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*поправляться* is the reflexive of *поправлять*, the partner of *поправить* 'to set right', built on *править* 'to put right' and *правый* 'right', which EtymDB traces to Proto-Slavic *pravъ* 'right, correct'.
