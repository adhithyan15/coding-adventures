## You'll want to know: парад
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C316-KARNAVAL-03]; assesses=[] -->

**парад** (*parád*) — "a parade".

> Мы смотрели парад на главной улице.
>
> *My smotréli parád na glávnoy úlitse.*
>
> We watched the parade on the main street.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*парад* is usually traced through French or German *Parade* to a Romance word built on Latin *parāre* "to prepare" (Lewis & Short); EtymDB lists no parent.
