## You'll want to know: こうきょう
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C328-KOUKYOU328-01]; assesses=[] -->

**こうきょう** — *kōkyō* — "public (places, services)".

> **こうきょうの ばしょは みんなが つかうから、ごみを すてないで ください。** — *kōkyō no basho wa minna ga tsukau kara, gomi o sutenaide kudasai* — "Everyone uses public places, so please do not drop rubbish there."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**こうきょう** (*kōkyō*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *kō*, "public" (Middle Chinese *kuwng*) and *kyō*, "together" (*gjowngH*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
