# la página web — a two-thousand-year-old word doing a thirty-year-old job

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-INTERNET, ES-LEX-ORDENADOR] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say *internet* and *el ordenador*. You have the network and you have
the machine. Say what is missing: the thing you actually look at.

## You'll want to know first
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

- [internet](./ES-C393-internet.md) — the network itself.
- [el ordenador](./ES-C393-ordenador.md) — the machine, named in Spanish for
  what it *orders* rather than what it counts.
- [el correo electrónico](./ES-C393-correo-electronico.md) — the other thing

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

- `stress-antepenultimate` — *PÁ-gi-na*, stress on the third syllable from the
  end, which is exactly what the written accent is there to tell you. →
  [reference](../pronunciation-reference.md)
- `g-soft` — the **g** in *página* comes before **i**, so it is the
  throaty sound, not the **g** of *gato*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C422-TECH-01]; assesses=[] -->

**la página web** — "the web page." Two words from two thousand years apart,
and only one of them was borrowed.

**Página** is Latin **pagina**, from **pangere**, "to fasten, to fix in place."
A Roman *pagina* was a column of writing, named for the way vine-growers
fastened shoots to a trellis: a column of text is writing **fixed into a
frame**.

| word | what got fastened |
|---|---|
| **página** | writing, into a column |
| **pacto** (a pact) | two parties, into an agreement |
| **paz** (peace) | the same, after a war |

**Web** is English, taken whole. Spanish did not translate it.

## Grammar Lens: which half carries the gender
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-C422-TECH-01, ES-LEX-CORREO-ELECTRONICO] -->

| name | article | what the second word is |
|---|---|---|
| el correo electrónico | **el** | an adjective, and it agrees with *correo* |
| la página web | **la** | a noun bolted on, and it never moves |

The first word is the real noun and it decides everything. *Web* is a borrowed
noun in an adjective's chair, so it never changes: *dos páginas web*, with no
**-s** on *web*.
