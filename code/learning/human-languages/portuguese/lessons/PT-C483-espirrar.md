## You'll want to know: espirrar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C483-ALERGIA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**espirrar** — "to sneeze".

> Na primavera, o meu pai espirra o dia todo.
>
> *In spring, my father sneezes all day.*

Past: *espirrei*, *espirrou*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*espirrar* is inherited from Latin *exspīrāre* "to breathe out" (EtymDB), built on *spīrāre* "to breathe".
