## Script — a bar, 口, three bars and a long hook
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-SCRIPT-SHI-MATTER-01]; assesses=[] -->

> 事

**One shape, eight strokes, threaded on a long hook.**

**A horizontal across the top.**

**Then a small 口, three strokes,** as you write it.

**Then three bars:** a horizontal that turns down at its right end, a long horizontal across, and a shorter one under it.

**Last, a vertical with a hook,** from the top bar down through everything: the longest stroke.

1 + 3 + 3 + 1 = eight.
