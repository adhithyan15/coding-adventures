## You'll want to know: સીડી
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C301-IMARAT301-02]; assesses=[] -->

**સીડી** — *sīḍī* — "a ladder".

> લોકો સીડીથી નીચે ઊતર્યા.
>
> *loko sīḍīthī nīce ūtaryā.*
>
> People climbed down by the ladder.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

comes from *\*śrīḍhi*, a form CDIAL reconstructs, and CDIAL lists the Gujarati noun, "a ladder" (CDIAL 12709). **દાદર** is a building's staircase; a **સીડી** is a ladder you can carry.
