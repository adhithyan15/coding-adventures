## You'll want to know: īnfīdus, īnfīda, īnfīdum
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C537-IURG-04]; assesses=[] -->

**īnfīdus, īnfīda, īnfīdum** — "faithless, untrustworthy".

> Amīcus īnfīdus omnia, quae dīxī, aliīs nārrat.
>
> *The faithless friend tells others everything I said.*

*in-* 'not' + *fīdus* 'faithful'.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**īnfīdus** is built on *in-* "not" and *fīdus* "faithful", from *fīdō* "to trust" (Lewis & Short).
