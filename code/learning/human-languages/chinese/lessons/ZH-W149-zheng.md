## Script — 一 on top, then the middle of 上 on a long bar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-SCRIPT-ZHENG-01]; assesses=[] -->

> 正

**一 on top, then four strokes, two of them shaped as in 上.**

**一**, a bar across the top.

**A vertical down the middle**, from under the bar to the foot.

**A short bar to its right**, halfway down: with the vertical, the upper part of 上.

**A short vertical on the left**, from the height of that bar down to the foot.

**The long bar along the foot**, last, under everything.

1 + 4 = five.
