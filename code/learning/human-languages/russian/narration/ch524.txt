You'll want to know: детектив.
детектив (detektív) — "a detective story; a detective".
Летом я люблю читать детективы. Létom ya lyublyú chitát detektívy. In the summer I like reading detective stories.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
EtymDB lists детектив (detektív) without a source; it is usually traced to English detective.

Lesson 2 of 6.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to get engrossed in reading, then the Russian for a detective story.

You'll want to know: фантастика.
фантастика (fantástika) — "science fiction".
Мой брат читает только фантастику. Moy brat chitáyet tólko fantástiku. My brother only reads science fiction.
Feminine. Also fantasy, and colloquially something amazing: Это фантастика (fantástika)!

The word, taken apart.
EtymDB lists фантастика (fantástika) without a source; it is usually traced through фантастический to Greek phantastikós 'able to imagine'.

Lesson 4 of 6.
повесть (póvest) — a novella.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for science fiction, then the Russian for a biography.

You'll want to know: повесть.
повесть (póvest) — "a novella".
Эту повесть мы читали в школе. Étu póvest my chitáli v shkóle. We read this novella at school.
Feminine. Longer than рассказ, shorter than роман.

The word, taken apart.
повесть (póvest) is built on по- and весть 'news, tidings', so a story is something told; EtymDB lists повесть (póvest) without a source.

Lesson 5 of 6.
мемуары (memuáry) — memoirs.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a biography, then the Russian for a novella.

You'll want to know: мемуары.
мемуары (memuáry) — "memoirs".
Старый актёр написал мемуары. Stáryy aktyór napisál memuáry. The old actor wrote his memoirs.
Plural only.

The word, taken apart.
мемуары (memuáry) was borrowed from French mémoires 'memoirs' (EtymDB), which is usually traced to Latin memoria 'memory'.
