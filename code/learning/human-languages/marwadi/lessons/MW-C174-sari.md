## You'll want to know: साड़ी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C174-ODHNI174-03]; assesses=[] -->

**साड़ी** — *sāṛī* — "a sari".

> मां नुवी साड़ी पैरै है।
>
> *mā̃ nuvī sāṛī pairai hai.*
>
> Mother wears a new sari.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**साड़ी** (*sāṛī*) is inherited from Sanskrit *śāṭī*, "a strip of cloth"; CDIAL lists Old Marwari *sāḍī*, "a sari", feminine (CDIAL 12381).
