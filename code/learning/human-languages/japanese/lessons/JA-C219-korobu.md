# ころぶ (korobu) — to fall over

## You'll want to know: ころぶ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C219-FALL219-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ころぶ** — *korobu* — "to fall over".

> **こどもは ころびませんでしたか。** — *kodomo wa korobimasen deshita ka* — "Didn't the child fall over?"

Polite **ころびます**, *korobimasu*; a godan verb.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ころぶ** (*korobu*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi, and its older origin is not settled (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
