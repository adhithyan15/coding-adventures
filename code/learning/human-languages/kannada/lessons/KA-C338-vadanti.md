# ವದಂತಿ (vadanti) — a rumour

## You'll want to know: ವದಂತಿ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C338-VADANTI338-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ವದಂತಿ** — *vadanti* — "a rumour".

> ಶಾಲೆ ಮುಚ್ಚುತ್ತಾರೆ ಎಂಬ ವದಂತಿ ಊರಿನಲ್ಲಿ ಇತ್ತು.
>
> *śāle muccuttāre emba vadanti ūrinalli ittu.*
>
> There was a rumour in town that the school would close.

Plural: ವದಂತಿಗಳು, *vadantigaḷu*.

A rumour "spreads": ವದಂತಿ ಹಬ್ಬಿತು. ಎಂಬ, "called, that says", links it to its content.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ವದಂತಿ** (*vadanti*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *vadanti*, a noun that Monier-Williams gives beside *kiṃvadantī*, "the common saying, a rumour", literally "what do they say", from *vad*, "to speak".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
