## You'll want to know: सिक्को
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C193-MONEY193-02]; assesses=[] -->

**सिक्को** — *sikko* — "a coin".

> ये सिक्को भारी है।
>
> *ye sikko bhārī hai.*
>
> This coin is heavy.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सिक्को** (*sikko*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *sikka*, "a die for stamping coins": the coin is named for its stamp.
