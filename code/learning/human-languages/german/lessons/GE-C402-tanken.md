## You'll want to know: tanken
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C402-TANKEN-01]; assesses=[] -->

**tanken** — "to fill up (with fuel), to get petrol".

> Auf dem Weg ans Meer tanken wir einmal.
>
> *On the way to the sea we fill up once.*

*Er tankt*, *er hat getankt*. *Die Tankstelle* is the petrol station.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*tanken* is built on *Tank* 'tank', which was borrowed from English *tank* (EtymDB). EtymDB traces the English word further back to Portuguese *tanque* 'water tank'.
