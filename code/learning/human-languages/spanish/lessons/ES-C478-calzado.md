## You'll want to know: el calzado
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C478-MALETA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**el calzado** — "the footwear".

> Lleva calzado cómodo.
>
> *Wear comfortable footwear.*

A notice word for shoes in general; one shoe is *un zapato*.
