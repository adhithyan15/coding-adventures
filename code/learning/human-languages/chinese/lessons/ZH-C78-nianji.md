## You'll want to know: niánjì
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C78-AGE78-04]; assesses=[] -->

*niánjì* — "age (of a person)".

> *nǐ bàba duō dà niánjì* — How old is your father?

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*niánjì* is built from 年 'year' and jì, both inherited from Middle Chinese, *nen* and *kiX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription); the dictionary glosses the second as a span of twelve years.
