## You'll want to know: أخفى
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C351-KHIDA351-05]; assesses=[] -->

**أخفى** — *akhfā* — "to conceal, to hide".

> أخفت البنت الهدية تحت السرير.
>
> *akhfat al-bint al-hadiyya taḥta as-sarīr.*
>
> The girl hid the present under the bed.

Present: يخفي, *yukhfī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**أخفى** (*akhfā*) is built on the native Arabic root *kh-f-y* (Lane), "to be hidden", the root of the taught *ikhtafā*, to disappear. It is Form IV, "to hide a thing".
