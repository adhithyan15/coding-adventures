## You'll want to know: વકીલ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C289-ADALAT289-02]; assesses=[] -->

**વકીલ** — *vakīl* — "a lawyer".

> વકીલે અદાલતમાં ઘણા પ્રશ્ન પૂછ્યા.
>
> *vakīle adālatmā̃ ghaṇā praśna pūchyā.*
>
> The lawyer asked many questions in court.

A masculine or feminine noun, by the person meant.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**વકીલ** (*vakīl*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *wakīl*, "one who acts for another": the person who speaks for you in court.
