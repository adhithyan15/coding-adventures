## You'll want to know: अविचलः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C393-VIJAYA393-02]; assesses=[] -->

**अविचलः** — *avicalaḥ* — "unshakable; steady".

> यदि मनः अविचलम्, तर्हि भयं न भवति।
>
> *yadi manaḥ avicalam, tarhi bhayaṃ na bhavati.*
>
> If the mind is unshakable, there is no fear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अविचलः** (*avicalaḥ*) is built from *a-*, "not", and *vi-cala*, "moving about", from the root √cal, "to move"; MW gives *a-vicala* "immovable, steady, firm" (MW).
