## You'll want to know: شبکه اجتماعی
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C417-SHABAKE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**شبکه اجتماعی** (*shabake-ye ejtemâ'i*) — "a social network".

> او در یک شبکه اجتماعی عکس میگذارد.
>
> *u dar yek shabake-ye ejtemâ'i 'aks migozârad.*
>
> She posts photos on a social network.

*Shabake* "network" + ezafe + *ejtemâ'i* "social".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**شبکه اجتماعی** joins *shabake*, from Arabic *shabaka* "a net", to *ejtemâ'i*, from Arabic *ijtimā'ī* "social" (both in EtymDB), with a Persian ezafe.
