## You'll want to know: ひかげ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C414-SHITSUDO414-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ひかげ** — *hikage* — "shade (out of the sun)".

> **ひかげで すこし やすみました。** — *hikage de sukoshi yasumimashita* — "I rested a little in the shade."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ひかげ** (*hikage*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, built from *hi*, "sun" and *kage*, "shadow"; its kanji were attached later as kun'yomi (JMdict).
