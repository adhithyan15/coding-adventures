## You'll want to know: نقطه ضعف
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C376-TAQSIR-03]; assesses=[] -->

**نقطه ضعف** (*noqte-ye za'f*) — "a weak point, a weakness".

> نقطه ضعف او بیصبری است.
>
> *noqte-ye za'f-e u bisabri ast.*
>
> His weak point is impatience.

*Noqte* "point" + *za'f* "weakness". The opposite is *noqte-ye qovvat*, a strong point.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**نقطه ضعف** joins two words of Arabic origin with a Persian ezafe: *noqte* from Arabic *nuqṭa* "dot" (EtymDB), and *za'f*, usually traced to Arabic *ḍa'f* "weakness".
