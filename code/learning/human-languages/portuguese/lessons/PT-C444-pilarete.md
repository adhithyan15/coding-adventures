## You'll want to know: o pilarete
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C444-MOBILIARIO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**o pilarete** — "the bollard".

> Há pilaretes para os carros não subirem ao passeio.
>
> *There are bollards to stop cars driving onto the pavement.*

A small form of *o pilar*, the pillar.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*pilarete* is a small form of *pilar* 'pillar', which goes back to Latin *pīla* 'pillar' (Lewis & Short).
