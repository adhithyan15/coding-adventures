## You'll want to know: چیک
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C417-RAQAM417-04]; assesses=[] -->

**چیک** — *chek* — "a cheque".

> میں نے کرایے کے لیے چیک دیا۔
>
> *maiṅ ne kirāye ke liye chek diyā.*
>
> I paid the rent by cheque.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**چیک** (*chek*) is borrowed from English *cheque*.
