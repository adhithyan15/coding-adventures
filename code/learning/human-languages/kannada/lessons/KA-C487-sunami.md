## You'll want to know: ಸುನಾಮಿ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C487-CANDAMARUTA487-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸುನಾಮಿ** — *sunāmi* — "a tsunami".

> ಸುನಾಮಿಯಿಂದ ಕರಾವಳಿಯ ಹಳ್ಳಿಗಳು ನಾಶವಾದವು.
>
> *sunāmiyinda karāvaḷiya haḷḷigaḷu nāśavādavu.*
>
> Coastal villages were destroyed by the tsunami.

A great sea wave raised by an earthquake under the sea.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸುನಾಮಿ** (*sunāmi*) is a loan from English *tsunami*, which English took from Japanese.
