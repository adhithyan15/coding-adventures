## You'll want to know: निश्चित
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C376-ZAHIR376-02]; assesses=[] -->

**निश्चित** — *niścit* — "definite, fixed".

> अगर तारीख़ निश्चित होगी तो मैं टिकट ले लूँगा।
>
> *agar tārīkh niścit hogī to maiṁ ṭikaṭ le lūṅgā.*
>
> If the date is fixed, I'll get a ticket.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**निश्चित** (*niścit*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *niścita*, "ascertained, settled" (Monier-Williams).
