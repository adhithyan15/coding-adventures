## You'll want to know: il certificato
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C389-PRATICA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**il certificato** — "the certificate".

> Se sei malato, devi portare un certificato medico.
>
> *If you are ill, you have to bring a medical certificate.*

*Il certificato di nascita* — the birth certificate.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*certificato* is built on *certificare* "to certify", a learned borrowing from Late Latin *certificāre* "to make certain", built from *certus* "sure" and *facere* "to make" (Lewis & Short).
