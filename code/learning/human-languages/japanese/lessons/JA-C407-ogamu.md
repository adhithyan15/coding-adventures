## You'll want to know: おがむ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C407-SANPAI407-05]; assesses=[] -->

**おがむ** — *ogamu* — "to pray with joined hands".

> **ほんどうの まえで おがみました。** — *hondō no mae de ogamimashita* — "I put my hands together in front of the main hall."

Polite **おがみます**, *ogamimasu*; a godan verb. Past (polite): **そぼが ほんどうで おがみました。**, *sobo ga hondō de ogamimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**おがむ** (*ogamu*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict).
