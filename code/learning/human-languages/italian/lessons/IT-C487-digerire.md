## You'll want to know: digerire
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C487-STOMACO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**digerire** — "to digest".

> Non ho digerito bene la cena.
>
> *I did not digest dinner well.*

Also figurative: *non lo digerisco* — I cannot stand him. An *-isc-* verb.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*digerire* is a learned borrowing from Latin *dīgerere* "to divide, to dissolve" (EtymDB), from *dis-* and *gerere* "to carry" (Lewis & Short).
