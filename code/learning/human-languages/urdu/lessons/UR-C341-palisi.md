## You'll want to know: پالیسی
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C341-HUKMRAN341-02]; assesses=[] -->

**پالیسی** — *pālīsī* — "a policy".

> حکومت کی نئی پالیسی کیا ہے؟
>
> *hukūmat kī naī pālīsī kyā hai?*
>
> What is the government's new policy?

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**پالیسی** (*pālīsī*) is borrowed from English *policy*.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
