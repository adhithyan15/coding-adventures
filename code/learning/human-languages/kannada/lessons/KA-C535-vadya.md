## You'll want to know: ವಾದ್ಯ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C535-VADYA535-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ವಾದ್ಯ** — *vādya* — "a musical instrument".

> ಮದುವೆಯಲ್ಲಿ ವಾದ್ಯಗಳ ಸದ್ದು ಜೋರಾಗಿತ್ತು.
>
> *maduveyalli vādyagaḷa saddu jōrāgittu.*
>
> At the wedding the instruments were loud.

Plural: ವಾದ್ಯಗಳು, *vādyagaḷu*.

ವಾದ್ಯಗಾರ, a player.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ವಾದ್ಯ** (*vādya*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *vādya*, "to be played; music, a musical instrument" (CDIAL 11511).
