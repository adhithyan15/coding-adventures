## You'll want to know: ਸਿੰਗ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C308-FEED308-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ਸਿੰਗ** — *siṅg* — "a horn".

> ਬਲਦ ਦੇ ਸਿੰਗ ਲੰਮੇ ਹਨ।
>
> *balad de siṅg lamme han.*
>
> The ox's horns are long.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਸਿੰਗ** (*siṅg*) is inherited from Sanskrit *śṛṅga*, "horn"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *siṅg* under it (CDIAL 12583).
