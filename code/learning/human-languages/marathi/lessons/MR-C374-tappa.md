## You'll want to know: टप्पा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C374-PRAKRIYA374-02]; assesses=[] -->

**टप्पा** — *ṭappā* — "a phase; a stage".

> हे काम तीन टप्प्यांत होणार आहे.
>
> *he kām tīn ṭappyānt hoṇār āhe.*
>
> This work is going to be done in three phases.

Masculine. Plural: टप्पे, *ṭappe*.

Plural टप्पे; oblique टप्प्या-.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**टप्पा** (*ṭappā*) is a Marathi noun whose origin is not settled.
