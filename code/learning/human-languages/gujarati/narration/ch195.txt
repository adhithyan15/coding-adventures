Gujarati, chapter 195: Lost in town.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ખોવાવું (khovāvũ) — to get lost.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for side, then the Gujarati for to cross.

You'll want to know: ખોવાવું.
ખોવાવું — khovāvũ — "to get lost".
હું શહેરમાં ખોવાઈ ગયો. — hũ śahermā̃ khovāī gayo — "I got lost in the city."

The word, taken apart.
ખોવાવું (khovāvũ) is built on khovũ, "to lose", with the passive ending -āvũ. CDIAL lists khovũ, "to lose, waste, throw away", under Sanskrit kṣapayati, "destroys, throws" (CDIAL 3651).

Lesson 5 of 5.
અંતર (antar) — distance.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to show, then the Gujarati for whereabouts.

You'll want to know: અંતર.
અંતર — antar — "distance".
બંને ગામ વચ્ચે ઘણું અંતર છે. — banne gām vacce ghaṇũ antar che — "The two villages are far apart."

The word, taken apart.
અંતર (antar) is Sanskrit ántara, "interior; interval", taken over whole, a tatsama. CDIAL lists its inherited cousin ā̃tro, "distance, interval" (CDIAL 357).
