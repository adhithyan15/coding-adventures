Lesson 1 of 5.
সাঁতার কাটা (sā̃tār kāṭā) — to swim.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for to sweep, then the Bengali for to boil.

You'll want to know: সাঁতার কাটা.
সাঁতার কাটা — sā̃tār kāṭā — "to swim".

The phrase, taken apart.
সাঁতার কাটা (sā̃tār kāṭā) (sāṁtār kāṭā) is sāṁtār, "a swim", from Sanskrit saṁtāra, "crossing over" (CDIAL 12888), + kāṭā, "to cut", from Sanskrit kartati, "cuts" (CDIAL 2854).

The phrase, taken apart.
