## You'll want to know: der Würfel
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C501-BRETTSPIEL-03]; assesses=[] -->

**der Würfel** — "dice; cube".

> Wer mit dem Würfel eine Sechs wirft, darf anfangen.
>
> *Whoever throws a six with the dice gets to start.*

Plural: *die Würfel*. Also a cube: *der Eiswürfel* — an ice cube. The verb is *würfeln* — to throw dice.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Würfel* is inherited from Middle High German *würfel* and Old High German *wurfil*, from Proto-Germanic *wurpilaz* "die" (EtymDB), built on the verb that is today *werfen* "to throw".
