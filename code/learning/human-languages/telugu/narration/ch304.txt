Hollow? (బోలు, bōlu.) Dim? (మసక, masaka.) Chickpeas? (శనగలు, śanagalu.) Mustard seeds? (ఆవాలు, āvālu.) Coriander leaves? (కొత్తిమీర, kottimīra.) Curry leaves? (కరివేపాకు, karivēpāku.) Buttermilk?
