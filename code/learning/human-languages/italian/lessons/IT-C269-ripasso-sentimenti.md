# Review — how it felt, and what comes next

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[IT-LEX-SPERO-DI-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "I hope to travel". (*Spero di viaggiare.*)

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[IT-GRAMMAR-SENTIMENTO-PASSATO-01, IT-GRAMMAR-CONTENTO-DI-02, IT-LEX-SPERO-DI-01, IT-GRAMMAR-IL-MIO-SOGNO-02, IT-LEX-HO-PERSO-01, IT-LEX-C92-VIAGGIO92-05] -->

An experience told well has two ends. The feeling looks back, in the
imperfetto; the hope looks forward, with *spero di* or *il mio sogno è*.

> *Il viaggio era lungo. Ho perso la coincidenza, quindi ero arrabbiato. Ma
> alla fine ero contento del viaggio. Spero di tornare.*

[PAUSE 2s] Follow the feelings. *Ero arrabbiato* came from an event, *ho
perso*, and *quindi* joined them; *ero contento* closed the story; *spero di
tornare* opened the next one.
