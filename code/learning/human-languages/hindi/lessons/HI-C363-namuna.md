## You'll want to know: नमूना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C363-ADHYAYAN363-03]; assesses=[] -->

**नमूना** — *namūnā* — "a sample".

> डाक्टर ने ख़ून का नमूना लिया।
>
> *ḍākṭar ne khūn kā namūnā liyā.*
>
> The doctor took a blood sample.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**नमूना** (*namūnā*) is usually traced to Persian *namūna*, "a sample, a model".
