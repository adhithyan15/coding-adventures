## You'll want to know: حالما
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C327-LAYLA327-03]; assesses=[] -->

**حالما** — *ḥālamā* — "as soon as".

> حالما وصلت اتصلت بأمي.
>
> *ḥālamā waṣaltu ittaṣaltu bi-ummī.*
>
> As soon as I arrived I called my mother.

A past verb follows it in a story.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**حالما** (*ḥālamā*) is built on *ḥāl*, "a state, the present moment", from the native Arabic root *ḥ-w-l* (Lane), with *mā*: "in the moment that".
