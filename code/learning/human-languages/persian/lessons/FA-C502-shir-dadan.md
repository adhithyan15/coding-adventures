## You'll want to know: شیر دادن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C502-BABIES-05]; assesses=[] -->

**شیر دادن** (*shir dâdan*) — "to breastfeed; to feed (milk)".

> مادر هر سه ساعت به بچه شیر میدهد.
>
> *mâdar har se sâ'at be bachche shir midahad.*
>
> The mother feeds the baby every three hours.

*shir* 'milk' + *dâdan* 'to give'; present stem *deh-*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*shir dâdan* is built on two inherited words: *shir*, 'milk', usually traced to Middle Persian *šīr*, and *dâdan*, 'to give'.
