## You'll want to know: کشمکش
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C529-JHAMELA529-03]; assesses=[] -->

**کشمکش** — *kashmakash* — "an inner struggle; a tug-of-war".

> اگر وہ گھر چھوڑتا تو دل میں کشمکش ہوتی۔
>
> *agar voh ghar chhoṛtā to dil meṅ kashmakash hotī.*
>
> If he were leaving home, he would be torn inside.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**کشمکش** (*kashmakash*) is usually traced to Persian *kashmakash*, "pulling to and fro", built on *kash-*, "to pull".
