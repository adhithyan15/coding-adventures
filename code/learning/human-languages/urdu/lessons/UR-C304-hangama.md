## You'll want to know: ہنگامہ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C304-FASAD304-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ہنگامہ** — *hangāmā* — "an uproar; a commotion".

> بازار میں ہنگامہ ہوا۔
>
> *bāzār meṅ hangāmā huā.*
>
> There was an uproar in the market.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ہنگامہ** (*hangāmā*) is usually traced to Persian *hangāma*, "a crowd, a tumult". It is noise and disorder, which need not turn violent.
