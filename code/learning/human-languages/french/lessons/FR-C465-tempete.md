## You'll want to know: la tempête
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C465-TEMPETE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**la tempête** — "the storm, the gale".

> La tempête a fait tomber des arbres sur la route.
>
> *The storm brought trees down onto the road.*

Stronger than an *orage*: a lot of wind.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*tempête* comes from Old French *tempeste*, from Vulgar Latin *tempesta*, from Latin *tempestās* 'weather, storm', built on *tempus* 'time' (EtymDB).
