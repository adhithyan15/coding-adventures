## You'll want to know: عرقل
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C445-TRAM445-05]; assesses=[] -->

**عرقل** — *ʿarqala* — "to hamper; to hold up".

> عرقل الثلج حركة المرور.
>
> *ʿarqala ath-thalj ḥarakat al-murūr.*
>
> The snow held up the traffic.

Present: يعرقل, *yuʿarqil*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**عرقل** (*ʿarqala*) is a verb with four root letters; the origin is not settled.
