Gujarati, chapter 118: Spice, Turmeric, Cumin, Grass, Island.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
મસાલો (masālo) — spice.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for halwa, then the Gujarati for rice pudding.
[pause 1 second]
Between two sentences paṇ means "but." What does it mean right after a noun? ("Also.")

You'll want to know: મસાલો.
મસાલો (masālo) — "spice".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: masālo]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: masālo, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say masālo]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Gujarati for halwa, then the Gujarati for rice pudding, then say masālo again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does મસાલો (masālo) mean? ("Spice".) Say it once more.
