# ऐ — the vowel you have read since है, standing alone

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[HI-LEX-C280-AISA-01, HI-SCRIPT-AI-MATRA-01, HI-SCRIPT-RECOG-131] -->

[PAUSE 1s] Before the new one: say **ऐसा**, "like this". Then look at **है**. How many strokes sit above its head-line? (Two: **◌ै**, *ai*.)

[PAUSE 2s] That sign has a **standing form**, and **ऐसा** opens with it.

## Script you'll notice: ऐ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-SCRIPT-C280-AI-01]; assesses=[HI-SCRIPT-AI-MATRA-01, HI-SCRIPT-RECOG-131] -->

**ऐ** — *ai*.

It is the **independent vowel**. **◌ै** is how *ai* looks when it rides on a consonant (**है**, **मैं**, **पैसा**); **ऐ** is how it looks when it stands on its own, with no consonant to carry it.

What it is made of:

- the long stem and descending tail of **ए**
- the shorter hooked stem of **ए**
- a separate upper arc
- the top shirorekhā

In print, then, **ऐ** is **ए** with one arc that rises from the right end of the head-line and curves up and over to the left.

## Script: two shapes, one sound
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[HI-SCRIPT-C280-AI-01, HI-SCRIPT-AI-MATRA-01, HI-SCRIPT-RECOG-131] -->

| what you see | where it stands |
|---|---|
| ◌ै | on a consonant: **है**, **मैं** |
| ऐ | alone: **ऐसा** |

Count the strokes above the line. **◌े** has one and **◌ै** two; **ए** has none and **ऐ** one. Miscount, and *e* becomes *ai*, or the other way round.

## Writing: ऐ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[HI-SCRIPT-C280-AI-01] -->

- **1.** descend the long left stem, curve right through the lower shoulder, and sweep down through the tail without lifting
- **2.** lift and descend the shorter right stem into its inward hook
- **3.** lift at the headline junction and sweep the upper arc upward and left
- **4.** lift and draw the top shirorekhā left-to-right

**Pen lifts: 3.** The first two strokes are the ones you write for **ए**; the arc comes third, and the head-line last.

> Verified modern printed teaching form; everyday handwriting may simplify the curves.

> This is one attested teaching order and not a national standard — handwriting
> here is taught with school-to-school variation. Source: Saurmandal, ‘Devanagari ऐ stroke order.svg’, panels 1–4, Wikimedia Commons, 5 August 2023.
