Portuguese, chapter 327: Reis e rainhas.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
o rei — the king.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for the fable, then the Portuguese for the fairy.

You'll want to know: o rei.
o rei — "the king".
O rei vivia num castelo grande. The king lived in a big castle.
Viver como um rei — to live like a king.

The word, taken apart.
rei is inherited from Latin rēx, rēgem "a king" (EtymDB).

O rei sentou-se no trono. The king sat down on the throne.

The word, taken apart.
trono comes down from Old Portuguese (EtymDB) and goes back to Latin thronus, from Greek thrónos "a seat, a chair" (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 5 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: a coroa.
a coroa — "the crown".
A coroa da rainha era de ouro. The queen’s crown was made of gold.
Also the top of the head, and the heads side of a coin.

The word, taken apart.
coroa comes down from Old Portuguese corõa (EtymDB), from Latin corōna "a wreath, a crown", from Greek korṓnē "something curved".
