Bengali, chapter 527: On Stage.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
মঞ্চ (mônchô) — a stage.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a violin, then the Bengali for to strum.

You'll want to know: মঞ্চ.
মঞ্চ — mônchô — "a stage".
নাটকের আগে মঞ্চে আলো জ্বলল। Nāṭôker āge mônche ālo jvôllô. Before the play, the lights came on on the stage.

The word, taken apart.
মঞ্চ (mônchô) is a tatsama, Sanskrit mañca, "a raised platform, a stage" (Monier-Williams).

What you've built.
One more word for describing what you lived and felt.

Lesson 2 of 5.
অভিনয় করা (ôbhinôy kôrā) — to act on stage; to act in a film.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for to strum, then the Bengali for a stage.

You'll want to know: অভিনয় করা.
অভিনয় করা — ôbhinôy kôrā — "to act on stage; to act in a film".
আমার বোন স্কুলের নাটকে অভিনয় করল। Āmār bon skuler nāṭôke ôbhinôy kôrlô. My sister acted in the school play.
Past (he/she …): তিনি সিনেমায় অভিনয় করলেন, tini sinemāy ôbhinôy kôrlen.

The word, taken apart.
অভিনয় করা (ôbhinôy kôrā) joins অভিনয়, Sanskrit abhinaya, the art of showing feeling by look and gesture (Monier-Williams), to করা, "to do".

Lesson 3 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a stage, then the Bengali for to act on stage.

You'll want to know: অভিনেতা.
অভিনেতা — ôbhinetā — "an actor".
এই সিনেমার অভিনেতা বিখ্যাত। Ei sinemār ôbhinetā bikhyātô. The actor in this film is famous.

The word, taken apart.
অভিনেতা (ôbhinetā) is Sanskrit abhinetṛ, "one who brings near" (Monier-Williams); the sense "actor" is modern, made to go with abhinaya, acting.

Lesson 5 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for an actor, then the Bengali for a rehearsal.

You'll want to know: হাততালি.
হাততালি — hāttāli — "applause; clapping".
দর্শকরা অভিনেতাদের জন্য হাততালি দিল। Dôrshôkrā ôbhinetāder jônyô hāttāli dilô. The audience applauded the actors.
With দেওয়া: হাততালি (hāttāli) দেওয়া, to clap.

The word, taken apart.
হাততালি (hāttāli) joins হাত, "hand" (CDIAL 14024), and তালি, "clapping" (CDIAL 5748): the clapping of hands.
