## You'll want to know: óbvio
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C341-PROVA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**óbvio** — "obvious".

> A resposta parecia óbvia a toda a gente.
>
> *The answer seemed obvious to everyone.*

*É óbvio!* — obviously! *Obviamente* — obviously.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*óbvio* is a learned borrowing from Latin *obvius* "in the way, meeting, easy to find" (EtymDB), built from *ob* "towards" and *via* "road": it lies right in your path.
