## You'll want to know: il falco
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C464-SELVATICI-04]; assesses=[] -->

**il falco** — "the falcon, the hawk".

> Il falco vola sopra la valle.
>
> *The falcon flies over the valley.*

*Occhio di falco* — eagle eye.
