# ઈ — independent long ī

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-SCRIPT-II-MATRA-01] -->

Write attached long-*ī* sign **ી** once and say “needs a consonant.” Put it
away. Long *ī* standing alone needs a whole letter.

## Script — meet one form
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-SCRIPT-INDEPENDENT-II-01]; assesses=[] -->

Keep **ઈ** visible. Circle the small upper-left loop to the middle crossing,
continue around the broad lower loop, then rise along the right and curl around
the extended top hook without lifting.

## Writing — notice and trace
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-SCRIPT-INDEPENDENT-II-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: observe-trace -->

Finger-trace **ઈ** once, then air-trace it once. Notice upper loop, middle
crossing, lower loop, extended top curl; no pen lift.

## Writing — copy with the model
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-SCRIPT-INDEPENDENT-II-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: guided-copy -->

Copy visible **ઈ** once. Compare only the middle crossing and top curl; repair
one landmark if needed.

## Writing — recall after hiding
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-SCRIPT-INDEPENDENT-II-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: delayed-copy -->
<!-- hl-activity: {"id":"GU-W07-independent-ii-recall","kind":"text","assesses":["GU-SCRIPT-INDEPENDENT-II-01"],"prompt":"Hide the model and write independent long ii from memory.","answer":"ઈ","accepted":[],"feedback":{"correct":"ઈ — the loops, crossing, and extended curl are intact.","incorrect":"Look once at ઈ, hide it, and repair only the differing landmark."},"response_seconds":20} -->

Hide the model and write independent long *ī* once. Uncover **ઈ**, compare, and
stop after one repair.

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-SCRIPT-INDEPENDENT-II-01, GU-SCRIPT-II-MATRA-01] -->

[PAUSE 3s] Point to the whole vowel in **ઈ ી**, then point to the attached
sign.
