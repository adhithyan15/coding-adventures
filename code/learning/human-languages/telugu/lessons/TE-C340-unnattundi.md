# ఉన్నట్టుండి (unnaṭṭuṇḍi) — out of the blue

## You'll want to know: ఉన్నట్టుండి
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C340-ANUKOKUNDA340-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ఉన్నట్టుండి** — *unnaṭṭuṇḍi* — "out of the blue".

> ఉన్నట్టుండి వర్షం మొదలైంది.
>
> *unnaṭṭuṇḍi varṣaṁ modalaindi.*
>
> Out of the blue it started to rain.

Literally while being as it was: all was calm, and then it was not.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ఉన్నట్టుండి** (*unnaṭṭuṇḍi*) is native Telugu, built twice on *uṇḍu*, "to be, exist" (DEDR 697): while things were as they were.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
