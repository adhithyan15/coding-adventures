## You'll want to know: しょうがつ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C170-NEWYEAR170-03]; assesses=[] -->

**しょうがつ** — *shōgatsu* — "the New Year; January".

> **しょうがつに いなかに かえります。** — *shōgatsu ni inaka ni kaerimasu* — "I will go back to the countryside at New Year."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**しょうがつ** (*shōgatsu*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *shō*, "correct, righteous" (Middle Chinese *tsyeng*) and *gatsu*, "moon, month" (*ngjwot*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
