## You'll want to know: جامعه
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C385-JAMEE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**جامعه** (*jâme'e*) — "a society, a community".

> جوانها آینده جامعه هستند.
>
> *javânhâ âyande-ye jâme'e hastand.*
>
> Young people are the future of society.

Plural *javâme'*, an Arabic broken plural. *Jâme'e-shenâsi* — sociology.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**جامعه** comes from Arabic *jāmi'a* (EtymDB), a word usually explained as what gathers people together; in Persian it is a society.
