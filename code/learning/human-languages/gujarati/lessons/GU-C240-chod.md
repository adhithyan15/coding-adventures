## You'll want to know: છોડ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C240-GARDEN240-01]; assesses=[] -->

**છોડ** — *choḍ* — "plant, shrub".

> **મેં છોડને પાણી આપ્યું.** — *mẽ choḍne pāṇī āpyũ* — "I gave the plant water."

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

The origin of **છોડ** (*choḍ*), "plant", is not settled: CDIAL lists no Gujarati form like it among its words for plants.
