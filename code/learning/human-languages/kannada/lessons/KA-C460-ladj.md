# ಲಾಡ್ಜ್ (lāḍj) — a lodge; a cheap hotel

## You'll want to know: ಲಾಡ್ಜ್
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C460-VASATI460-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ಲಾಡ್ಜ್** — *lāḍj* — "a lodge; a cheap hotel".

> ಬಸ್ ನಿಲ್ದಾಣದ ಹತ್ತಿರ ಒಂದು ಲಾಡ್ಜ್ ಇದೆ.
>
> *bas nildāṇada hattira ondu lāḍj ide.*
>
> There is a lodge near the bus station.

A plain place to sleep; a ಹೋಟೆಲ್ may also serve food.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಲಾಡ್ಜ್** (*lāḍj*) is a loan from English *lodge*.
