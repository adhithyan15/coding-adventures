## You'll want to know: l'antivirus
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C404-BLOCCO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**l'antivirus** — "the antivirus (software)".

> Se hai un buon antivirus, sei più tranquillo.
>
> *If you have a good antivirus, you can feel calmer.*

Masculine, invariable: *l'antivirus, gli antivirus*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*antivirus* is built from the Greek prefix *anti-* "against" and *virus*, from Latin *vīrus* "slime, poison" (Lewis & Short).
