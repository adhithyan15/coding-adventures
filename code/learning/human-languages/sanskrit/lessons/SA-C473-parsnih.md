## You'll want to know: पार्ष्णिः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C473-ANGA473-03]; assesses=[] -->

**पार्ष्णिः** — *pārṣṇiḥ* — "the heel".

> नवं पादत्राणं मम पार्ष्णिं पीडयति।
>
> *navaṃ pādatrāṇaṃ mama pārṣṇiṃ pīḍayati.*
>
> The new shoe hurts my heel.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पार्ष्णिः** (*pārṣṇiḥ*) MW asks, with a question mark, whether it comes from a root √pṛṣ, so its origin is uncertain; it gives "the heel" (MW).
