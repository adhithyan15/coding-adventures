## HL-C372 — Latin's spoken meeting scene was never allowed to become a reading

The reading-reach report identified Latin as the only measurable pre-A1 track
still at zero: **0/2 pre-A1 and 0/3 A1**, despite 160 lessons and a complete
meeting exchange already distributed across the course.

Chapter 58 closes the cheap half of that gap with three readings: six familiar
words, six familiar lines, and a 42-word connected scene. No passage introduces
vocabulary. Instead, the rung changes the learner's job from repeating known
Latin to meeting it first on the page, where macrons, person endings, cases and
demonstrative gender have to carry meaning without an English translation
between them.

Measured after generation and focused validation, Latin now reaches **2/2**
pre-A1 parts and **1/3** A1 parts. The remaining A1 parts require longer
connected passages. That is real connected-reading work rather than another
zero-cost one-word tranche, so the next backlog comparison should weigh it
against tracks whose pre-A1 or A1 rows still remain entirely at zero.
