## You'll want to know: शहनाई
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C531-DHOLAK531-02]; assesses=[] -->

**शहनाई** — *śahnāī* — "a shehnai (a reed pipe)".

> शादी के दिन सुबह से शहनाई बज रही थी।
>
> *Śādī ke din subah se śahnāī baj rahī thī.*
>
> On the wedding day the shehnai had been playing since morning.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**शहनाई** (*śahnāī*) is usually traced to Persian *shahnāy*, "a king's pipe".
