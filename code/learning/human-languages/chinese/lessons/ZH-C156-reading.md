## Reading — the pinyin, after you have tried
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ZH-SKILL-READ-PRICE-LIST-01, ZH-SKILL-READ-WORDS, ZH-LEX-C156-YUAN-01, ZH-TONE-SANDHI-YI] -->

> *shū, shí yuán yì běn*
> *běnzi, liǎng yuán yì běn*
> *dà báicài, sān yuán*
> *shuǐ, yì yuán*
> *shǒujī, jiǔbǎi jiǔshíjiǔ yuán*

> *xīngqīliù hé xīngqītiān*
> *liǎng běn shū shíwǔ yuán*

A price list puts the thing first and its price after, with 一本 for each one where the thing is counted by the book. 一 falls before the rise of yuán: *yì yuán*, as before běn. The advert changes only one thing: the price at the weekend.
