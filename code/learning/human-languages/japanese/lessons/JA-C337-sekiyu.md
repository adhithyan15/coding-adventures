## You'll want to know: せきゆ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C337-SETSUYAKU337-04]; assesses=[] -->

**せきゆ** — *sekiyu* — "petroleum; oil".

> **せきゆは すくなく なると おもいます。** — *sekiyu wa sukunaku naru to omoimasu* — "I think oil will become scarce."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**せきゆ** (*sekiyu*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *seki*, "stone" (Middle Chinese *dzyek*) and *yu*, "oil" (*yuw*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
