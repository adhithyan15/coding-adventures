## You'll want to know: поскольку
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C351-POSKOLKU-01]; assesses=[] -->

**поскольку** (*poskólku*) — "since, as (giving a reason)".

> Поскольку шёл дождь, мы остались дома.
>
> *Poskólku shyol dozhd, my ostális dóma.*
>
> Since it was raining, we stayed at home.

It takes an ordinary clause in the indicative, like *так как*, and sounds a little more formal. It can open the sentence.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**поскольку** is built from *по* and *сколько* "how much", from Proto-Slavic *\*koliko* "how much" (EtymDB): first "in so far as", then "since".
