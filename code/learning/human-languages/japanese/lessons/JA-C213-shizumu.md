## You'll want to know: しずむ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C213-SKY213-03]; assesses=[] -->

**しずむ** — *shizumu* — "to sink; to set (of the sun)".

> **たいようは にしに しずみます。** — *taiyō wa nishi ni shizumimasu* — "The sun sets in the west."

Polite **しずみます**, *shizumimasu*; a godan verb.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**しずむ** (*shizumu*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi, and its older origin is not settled (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
