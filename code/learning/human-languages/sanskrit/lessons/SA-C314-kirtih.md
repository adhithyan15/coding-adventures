## You'll want to know: कीर्तिः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C314-SHAURYA314-03]; assesses=[] -->

**कीर्तिः** — *kīrtiḥ* — "fame; renown".

> तस्य कीर्तिः सर्वत्र प्रसरति।
>
> *tasya kīrtiḥ sarvatra prasarati.*
>
> His fame spreads everywhere.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**कीर्तिः** (*kīrtiḥ*) comes from the root that MW numbers √2. kṛ, "to mention, praise"; MW gives *kīrti* as mention, report and good report, fame (MW).
