## You'll want to know: व्याकुलः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C451-ANUNAYA451-04]; assesses=[] -->

**व्याकुलः** — *vyākulaḥ* — "troubled; perplexed".

> स्यूतः नष्टः, अतः यात्री व्याकुलः अस्ति।
>
> *syūtaḥ naṣṭaḥ, ataḥ yātrī vyākulaḥ asti.*
>
> The bag is lost, so the passenger is troubled.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**व्याकुलः** (*vyākulaḥ*) is built from *vi* and *ākula*, "crowded, confused"; MW gives "bewildered, perplexed, troubled" (MW).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
