Bengali, chapter 360: Speeches and Talks.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
বক্তৃতা (bôktritā) — a speech; a lecture.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for to sidestep, then the Bengali for acceptable.

You'll want to know: বক্তৃতা.
বক্তৃতা — bôktritā — "a speech; a lecture".
কাল কলেজে একটা বক্তৃতা আছে। kāl kôleje ekṭā bôktritā āchhe. There is a lecture at the college tomorrow.

The word, taken apart.
বক্তৃতা (bôktritā) is Sanskrit vaktṛtā, the gift of speech, eloquence (Monier-Williams). Bengali uses it for the speech itself: a lecture or an address.

The word, taken apart.
উপস্থিত (upôsthitô) is Sanskrit upasthita, standing near, present (Monier-Williams). It is the word for being present at a meeting or in class.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: সম্বোধন করা.
সম্বোধন করা — sômbodhôn kôrā — "to address a crowd; to address (someone)".
নেতা মিছিলের লোকদের সম্বোধন করলেন। netā michhiler lokder sômbodhôn kôrlen. The leader addressed the people in the procession.
Past (he/she …): তিনি সভাকে সম্বোধন করলেন, tini sôbhāke sômbodhôn kôrlen.

The word, taken apart.
সম্বোধন করা (sômbodhôn kôrā) is Sanskrit sambodhana, calling to someone (Monier-Williams), with করা: to speak to a person or a crowd directly.
