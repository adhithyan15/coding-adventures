## You'll want to know: しじん
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C504-ART504-02]; assesses=[] -->

**しじん** — *shijin* — "a poet".

> **この しじんは ゆうめいです。** — *kono shijin wa yūmei desu* — "This poet is famous."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**しじん** (*shijin*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *shi*, "poem" (Middle Chinese *syi*) and *jin*, "person" (*nyin*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
