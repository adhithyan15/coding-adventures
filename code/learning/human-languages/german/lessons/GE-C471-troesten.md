## You'll want to know: trösten
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C471-TROST-04]; assesses=[] -->

**trösten** — "to comfort, to console".

> Die Oma tröstet das Kind nach dem Sturz.
>
> *Grandma comforts the child after the fall.*

*Sich trösten mit* — to console yourself with.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*trösten* is built on *Trost* "comfort", which is inherited from Old High German *trōst*, from Proto-Germanic *traustą* (EtymDB).
