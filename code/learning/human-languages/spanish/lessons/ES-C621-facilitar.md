## You'll want to know: facilitar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C621-IMPIDE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**facilitar** — "to make easier; to provide".

> El ordenador facilita el trabajo.
>
> *The computer makes the work easier.*

On forms: *facilite sus datos* — provide your details.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*facilitar* is built on *fácil*, a learned borrowing from Latin *facilis* 'easy to do', from *facere* 'to do' (EtymDB).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.
