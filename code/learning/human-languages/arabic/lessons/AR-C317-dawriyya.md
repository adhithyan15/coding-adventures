## You'll want to know: دورية
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C317-TAWQIF317-04]; assesses=[] -->

**دورية** — *dawriyya* — "a patrol".

> مرت دورية شرطة في الشارع.
>
> *marrat dawriyyat shurṭa fī ash-shāriʿ.*
>
> A police patrol went by in the street.

Also a periodical: مجلة دورية.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**دورية** (*dawriyya*) is built on the native Arabic root *d-w-r* (Lane), "to turn, to go round", the root of *dawr*, a turn. A *dawriyya* makes its round again and again.
