## You'll want to know: el pico
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C686-CIMA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**el pico** — "the peak; the beak".

> Desde el pico se ve el mar.
>
> *The sea can be seen from the peak.*

*Y pico* — and a bit: *las tres y pico*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*pico* is inherited from Latin *beccus* "beak" (EtymDB), which the Romans took from Gaulish.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.
