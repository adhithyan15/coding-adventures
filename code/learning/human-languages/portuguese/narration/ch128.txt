Portuguese, chapter 128: Heavy, Thin, Pleased, Weekend, Date.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
pesado — heavy.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for salty, then the Portuguese for light.

You'll want to know: pesado.
pesado — "heavy".

The word, taken apart.
pesado is the participle of the verb "to weigh", which from Latin pensare, "to weigh, to ponder" (EtymDB 2.1) — the same Latin verb as pensar.

Lesson 4 of 5.
o fim de semana — the weekend.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for thin, then the Portuguese for pleased.
[pause 2 seconds]
The weekend is two days: say sábado and domingo.

You'll want to know: o fim de semana.
o fim de semana — "the weekend".

The word, taken apart.
fim de semana is "end of week": fim from Latin finis, "end" (Lewis & Short), and semana from Late Latin septimana (EtymDB 2.1).

You'll want to know: a data.
a data — "a date".

The word, taken apart.
data from Late Latin data, "given" (EtymDB 2.1) — a learned borrowing from Late Latin. Letters began data, "given", at a place on a day.
