## You'll want to know: वैध
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C543-HOKAR543-04]; assesses=[] -->

**वैध** — *vaidh* — "valid".

> माझं पारपत्र पुढच्या वर्षापर्यंत वैध आहे.
>
> *mājhaṁ pārpatra puḍhcyā varṣāparyant vaidh āhe.*
>
> My passport is valid until next year.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**वैध** (*vaidh*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *vaidha*, "prescribed by rule, legal" (Monier-Williams), built on *vidhi*, "rule".
