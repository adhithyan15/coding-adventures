Sanskrit, chapter 495: Growing Up.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
किशोरः (kiśoraḥ) — a lad.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for permits, then the Sanskrit for childhood.

You'll want to know: किशोरः.
किशोरः — kiśoraḥ — "a lad".
किशोरः क्रीडांगणे कन्दुकेन क्रीडति। kiśoraḥ krīḍāṅgaṇe kandukena krīḍati. The lad plays ball in the playground.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
किशोरः (kiśoraḥ) MW's first sense is "a colt", then "a youth, a lad"; MW gives no derivation, so its origin is uncertain (MW).

Lesson 2 of 5.

Warm-up.

Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for childhood, then the Sanskrit for a lad.

You'll want to know: यौवनम्.
यौवनम् — yauvanam — "youth (the time of life)".
यौवने उत्साहः बहु भवति। yauvane utsāhaḥ bahu bhavati. In youth there is much enthusiasm.
A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
यौवनम् (yauvanam) is built on yuvan, "young", the word behind युवकः; MW gives "youth, youthfulness, adolescence" (MW).

Lesson 3 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: प्रौढः.
प्रौढः — prauḍhaḥ — "grown up".
मम पुत्री इदानीं प्रौढा अस्ति। mama putrī idānīṃ prauḍhā asti. My daughter is grown up now.

The word, taken apart.
प्रौढः (prauḍhaḥ) is built from pra and ūḍha, the ppp of the root √vah of वहति; MW gives "grown up, full-grown, mature" (MW).

Lesson 4 of 5.
वारयति (vārayati) — holds back; forbids.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for youth (the time of life), then the Sanskrit for grown up.

You'll want to know: वारयति.
वारयति — vārayati — "holds back; forbids".
वैद्यः शर्करां वारयति। vaidyaḥ śarkarāṃ vārayati. The physician forbids sugar.
Past (he …): सः शर्करां वारितवान्, saḥ śarkarāṃ vāritavān.

The word, taken apart.
वारयति (vārayati) is built on the causative of the root √vṛ, "to cover, to ward off"; MW's ppp vārita is "warded off, prevented, restrained" (MW).

The word, taken apart.
ब्रह्मचारी (brahmacārī) is built from brahman, "sacred knowledge", and cārin, "practising", from the root √car; MW gives "a young Brāhman who is a student of the Veda, before marriage" (MW).
