Gujarati, chapter 205: A seat on the railway.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
રેલવે (relve) — railway.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to start off, then the Gujarati for to leave.

You'll want to know: રેલવે.
રેલવે — relve — "railway".
રેલવે સ્ટેશન દૂર હતું. — relve sṭeśan dūr hatũ — "The railway station was far away."

The word, taken apart.
રેલવે (relve) is a loan from English railway.

ટ્રેનમાં જગ્યા ન હતી. — ṭrenmā̃ jagyā na hatī — "There was no seat on the train."

Lesson 3 of 5.
ઉતારુ (utāru) — passenger.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for railway, then the Gujarati for seat.

You'll want to know: ઉતારુ.
ઉતારુ — utāru — "passenger".
બસમાં ઘણા ઉતારુ હતા. — basmā̃ ghaṇā utāru hatā — "There were many passengers on the bus."

The word, taken apart.
ઉતારુ (utāru) is built on utār, which CDIAL glosses "ford, fare for journey", with the ending -u. CDIAL lists utār under Sanskrit uttāra, "landing, delivery" (CDIAL 1791).

Lesson 4 of 5.
થોભવું (thobhvũ) — to halt, to pause.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for seat, then the Gujarati for passenger.

You'll want to know: થોભવું.
થોભવું — thobhvũ — "to halt, to pause".
ટ્રેન નાના સ્ટેશન પર થોભી. — ṭren nānā sṭeśan par thobhī — "The train stopped at a small station."

The word, taken apart.
થોભવું (thobhvũ) is a tadbhava. It comes from Sanskrit stōbhatē, "stops, makes stop", and CDIAL glosses the Gujarati verb "to stop, delay" (CDIAL 13724).

Lesson 5 of 5.
ઊતરવું (ūtarvũ) — to get off, to come down.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for passenger, then the Gujarati for to halt.

You'll want to know: ઊતરવું.
ઊતરવું — ūtarvũ — "to get off, to come down".
અમે બીજા સ્ટેશને ઊતર્યા. — ame bījā sṭeśane ūtaryā — "We got off at the second station."

The word, taken apart.
ઊતરવું (ūtarvũ) is a tadbhava. It comes from Sanskrit úttarati, "passes over; alights, descends", and CDIAL glosses the Gujarati verb "to cross, descend, decrease" (CDIAL 1770).
