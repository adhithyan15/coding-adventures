## You'll want to know: तर्क
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C343-MUDDA343-02]; assesses=[] -->

**तर्क** — *tarka* — "logic; reasoning".

> त्याच्या बोलण्यात काहीच तर्क नव्हता.
>
> *tyācyā bolaṇyāt kāhīc tarka navhatā.*
>
> There was no logic at all in what he said.

Masculine.

तर्क लावणे, to reason something out, to guess.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**तर्क** (*tarka*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *tarka*, "conjecture, reasoning" (Monier-Williams). CDIAL lists the same head (CDIAL 5714).
