Arabic, chapter 167: To trip, To slip, To rescue, To escape, To disappear.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
تعثر (taʿaththara) — to trip, to stumble.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to paint, then the Arabic for to hide.

You'll want to know: تعثر.
تعثر — taʿaththara — "to trip, to stumble".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: taʿaththara]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: taʿaththara, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say taʿaththara]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Arabic for to paint, then the Arabic for to hide, then say taʿaththara again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does تعثر (taʿaththara) mean? ("To trip, to stumble".) Say it once more.

Lesson 6 of 7.
Words from chapters 159-162 — a first pass.

[pause 2 seconds after each]
[your turn — say: to wake up, to go up, to arrive, to travel, to cross]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: to taste, to pour, to switch off, to fill, to bathe]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: to sneeze, to breathe, to break, to rest, to feel]

[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
To wake up? (استيقظ, istayqaẓa.) To go up? (صعد, ṣaʿida.) To arrive? (وصل, waṣala.) To travel? (سافر, sāfara.) To cross? (عبر, ʿabara.) To taste? (ذاق, dhāqa.) To pour? (صب, ṣabba.) To switch off? (أطفأ, aṭfaʾa.) To fill? (ملأ, malaʾa.) To bathe? (استحم, istaḥamma.) To sneeze? (عطس, ʿaṭasa.) To breathe? (تنفس, tanaffasa.) To break? (كسر, kasara.) To rest? (استراح, istarāḥa.) To feel? (أحس, aḥassa.) To print? (طبع, ṭabaʿa.) To copy? (نسخ, nasakha.) To move? (انتقل, intaqala.) To register? (سجل, sajjala.) To employ? (وظف, waẓẓafa.)

To finish? (أنهى, anhā.) To hunt? (صاد, ṣāda.) To build? (بنى, banā.) To knock down? (هدم, hadama.) To paint? (دهن, dahana.) To hide? (خبأ, khabbaʾa.) To trip? (تعثر, taʿaththara.) To slip?
