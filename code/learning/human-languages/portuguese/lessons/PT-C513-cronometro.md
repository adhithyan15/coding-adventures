## You'll want to know: o cronómetro
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C513-CORRIDA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**o cronómetro** — "stopwatch".

> O treinador marcou o tempo com o cronómetro.
>
> *The coach timed it with the stopwatch.*

In Brazil: *cronômetro*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*cronómetro* is a learned coinage from Greek *khrónos* "time" and *métron* "measure".
