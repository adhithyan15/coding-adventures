## You'll want to know: der Neid
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C466-NEID-02]; assesses=[] -->

**der Neid** — "envy".

> Er spricht ohne Neid über den Erfolg seines Bruders.
>
> *He talks about his brother's success without envy.*

No plural. *Vor Neid platzen* — to burst with envy.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Neid* is a native Germanic word, usually traced to Old High German *nīd* "hatred, envy"; EtymDB lists the German word without a source.
