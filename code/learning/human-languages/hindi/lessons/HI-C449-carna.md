## You'll want to know: चरना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C449-SIYAR449-04]; assesses=[] -->

**चरना** — *carnā* — "to graze".

> मैदान में गायें चर रही थीं।
>
> *maidān meṁ gāyẽ car rahī thīṁ.*
>
> Cows were grazing in the field.

Past (it …): वह भैंस दिन भर चरी, *vah bhaĩs din bhar carī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**चरना** (*carnā*) is a tadbhava of Sanskrit *carati*, "moves" and also "grazes" (CDIAL 4686), which lists Hindi *carnā*, "to move about, graze".
