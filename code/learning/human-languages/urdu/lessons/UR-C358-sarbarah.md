## You'll want to know: سربراہ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C358-JALSA358-02]; assesses=[] -->

**سربراہ** — *sarbarāh* — "a head of an organisation".

> ادارے کے سربراہ نے جلسے میں بات کی۔
>
> *idāre ke sarbarāh ne jalse meṅ bāt kī.*
>
> The head of the organisation spoke at the rally.

Masculine or feminine, by the person meant.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**سربراہ** (*sarbarāh*) is usually traced to Persian *sarbarāh*, "a manager", built on *sar*, "the head".
