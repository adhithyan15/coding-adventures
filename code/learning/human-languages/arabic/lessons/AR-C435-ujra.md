## You'll want to know: أجرة
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C435-MASAR435-02]; assesses=[] -->

**أجرة** — *ujra* — "a fare; a wage".

> كانت أجرة التاكسي رخيصة.
>
> *kānat ujrat at-tāksī rakhīṣa.*
>
> The taxi fare was cheap.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**أجرة** (*ujra*) is built on the native Arabic root *ʾ-j-r*, "a wage, a reward" (Lane). A fare is the wage paid for the ride.
