# ડાબું (ḍābũ) — left, left-hand

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to waste, then the Gujarati for direction.

## You'll want to know: ડાબું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C193-WAY193-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ડાબું** — *ḍābũ* — "left, left-hand".

> **મંદિર ડાબે છે.** — *mandir ḍābe che* — "The temple is on the left."

**ડાબે**, *ḍābe*, 'on the left'.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ડાબું** (*ḍābũ*) is listed by CDIAL, glossed "left", under *\*ḍavva*, "left-hand, crosswise", and its variant *\*ḍābba*, reconstructed forms. CDIAL calls the group non-Aryan and cites A. Master, who traced it to Dravidian (CDIAL 5539); DEDR sets it beside Dravidian words for left, such as Telugu *ḍā* (DEDR 449).
