Marathi, chapter 525: Pouring and Rinsing.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
कळशी (kaḷśī) — a brass water pot.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to shake off, then the Marathi for to scrub.

You'll want to know: कळशी.
कळशी — kaḷśī — "a brass water pot".
आजीने नळावरून कळशी भरून आणली. ājīne naḷāvarūn kaḷśī bharūn āṇlī. Grandmother filled a water pot at the tap and brought it.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
कळशी (kaḷśī) is a tadbhava, inherited from Sanskrit kalaśa, "waterpot" (CDIAL 2920).

Lesson 3 of 5.
ओतणे (otṇe) — to pour.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a brass water pot, then the Marathi for a clay water pot.

You'll want to know: ओतणे.
ओतणे — otṇe — "to pour".
मी माठातलं पाणी कळशीत ओतलं. mī māṭhātlaṁ pāṇī kaḷśīt otlaṁ. I poured the water from the clay pot into the water pot.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने पाणी ओतलं, tyāne pāṇī otlaṁ.
Where it goes takes -त: कळशीत ओतणे (otṇe).

The word, taken apart.
ओतणे (otṇe) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi otṇẽ, "to pour", under avōtta, "flowed down" (CDIAL 902).

Lesson 4 of 5.
सांडणे (sāṇḍṇe) — to spill.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a clay water pot, then the Marathi for to pour.

You'll want to know: सांडणे.
सांडणे — sāṇḍṇe — "to spill".
घाईत माझ्याकडून चहा सांडला. ghāīt mājhyākaḍūn cahā sāṇḍlā. In my hurry I spilled the tea.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने दूध सांडलं, tyāne dūdh sāṇḍlaṁ.
For an accident Marathi says माझ्याकडून चहा सांडला, the tea spilled from me.

The word, taken apart.
सांडणे (sāṇḍṇe) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi sā̃ḍṇẽ, "to spill, give up", under Sanskrit chardati, "ejects, vomits" (CDIAL 4998), whose basic sense is letting something out.
