## You'll want to know: minorenne
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C309-GENERAZIONE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**minorenne** — "underage; a minor".

> I minorenni non possono entrare.
>
> *Minors cannot come in.*

Also a noun: *un minorenne*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*minorenne* joins *minore* "lesser", from Latin *minor*, and *-enne*, from *annus* "year" (Lewis & Short).
