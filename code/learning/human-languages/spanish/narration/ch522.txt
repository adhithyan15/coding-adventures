Spanish, chapter 522: Ante el juez.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
el juicio — the trial; judgement.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for to find out, then the Spanish for to suspect.

You'll want to know: el juicio.
el juicio — "the trial; judgement".
El juicio empieza el martes. The trial starts on Tuesday.
A mi juicio — in my judgement.

The word, taken apart.
juicio is inherited from Latin iūdicium 'a trial, a judgement' (EtymDB), built on iūdex 'a judge'.

Lesson 2 of 5.
el juez — the judge.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for to suspect, then the Spanish for the trial.

You'll want to know: el juez.
el juez — "the judge".
El juez escuchó a los testigos. The judge listened to the witnesses.
A woman judge is la jueza or la juez. Plural: los jueces.

The word, taken apart.
juez is inherited from Latin iūdex 'a judge' (EtymDB), built on iūs 'law' and dīcere 'to say'. The judge is the one who says what the law is.

Lesson 3 of 5.
juzgar — to judge, to try.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for the trial, then the Spanish for the judge.

You'll want to know: juzgar.
juzgar — "to judge, to try".
Lo juzgaron por robo. They tried him for robbery.
It takes gu before e: juzgué. Also everyday: No me juzgues.

The word, taken apart.
juzgar is inherited from Latin iūdicāre 'to judge', through old Spanish judgar (EtymDB). It is built on iūdex 'a judge'.
