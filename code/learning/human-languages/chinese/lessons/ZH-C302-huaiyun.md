## You'll want to know: huáiyùn
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C302-BIRTH302-01]; assesses=[] -->

*huáiyùn* — "to be pregnant; to be expecting a baby".

> *tā huáiyùn le* — She's pregnant.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*huáiyùn* is built from *huái* 'to hold close' and *yùn* 'to carry a child', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *hweaj* and *yingH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to embrace' and the second with this very word. *yingH* would regularly give yìng; the final of yùn is irregular.
