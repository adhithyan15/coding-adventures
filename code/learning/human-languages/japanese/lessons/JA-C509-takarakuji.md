# たからくじ (takarakuji) — a lottery; a lottery ticket

## You'll want to know: たからくじ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C509-LOTTERY509-01]; assesses=[] -->

**たからくじ** — *takarakuji* — "a lottery; a lottery ticket".

> **もし たからくじが あたったら、うちを かいます。** — *moshi takarakuji ga atattara, uchi o kaimasu* — "If I win the lottery, I will buy a house."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**たからくじ** (*takarakuji*) is a native Japanese compound (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese: built from *takara*, "treasure" and *kuji*, "lot" (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
