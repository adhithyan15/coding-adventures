## You'll want to know: कल
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C383-BHAKIT383-04]; assesses=[] -->

**कल** — *kal* — "a trend; an inclination".

> तरुणांचा कल शहरांकडे आहे.
>
> *taruṇāñcā kal śahrāṅkaḍe āhe.*
>
> Young people are inclined towards the cities.

Masculine.

X कडे कल असणे, to lean towards X.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**कल** (*kal*) is a Marathi noun whose origin is not settled.
