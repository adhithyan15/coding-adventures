## You'll want to know: a prova
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C341-PROVA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**a prova** — "the proof, the evidence; the test".

> Não há nenhuma prova de que ele mentiu.
>
> *There is no proof that he lied.*

*Uma prova* can also be a test at school. *À prova de água* — waterproof.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*prova* is built on *provar* "to prove, to try", which is inherited from Latin *probāre* "to test, to approve, to prove" (Lewis & Short); Old Portuguese already had *prova* (EtymDB).
