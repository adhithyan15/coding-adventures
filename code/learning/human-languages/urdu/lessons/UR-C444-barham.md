## You'll want to know: برہم
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C444-ANGER444-03]; assesses=[] -->

**برہم** — *barham* — "incensed; put out".

> غلط بل دیکھ کر گاہک برہم ہوا۔
>
> *ghalat bil dekh kar gāhak barham huā.*
>
> The customer was incensed when he saw the wrong bill.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**برہم** (*barham*) is usually traced to Persian *barham*, "jumbled together, upset".
