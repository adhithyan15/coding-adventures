## You'll want to know: وحش
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C479-TAZALLUJ479-02]; assesses=[] -->

**وحش** — *waḥsh* — "a wild beast; a monster".

> خاف الطفل من الوحش في القصة.
>
> *khāfa aṭ-ṭifl min al-waḥsh fī al-qiṣṣa.*
>
> The child was afraid of the beast in the story.

Plural: وحوش, *wuḥūsh*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**وحش** (*waḥsh*) is built on the native Arabic root *w-ḥ-sh*, "wild, desolate" (Lane).
