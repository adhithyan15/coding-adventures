## You'll want to know: أطلال
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C318-HADARA318-03]; assesses=[] -->

**أطلال** — *aṭlāl* — "remains of a dwelling, ruins".

> وقف الشاعر على أطلال البيت.
>
> *waqafa ash-shāʿir ʿalā aṭlāl al-bayt.*
>
> The poet stood at the remains of the house.

The old Arabic ode opens with the poet weeping at the أطلال of a camp.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**أطلال** (*aṭlāl*) is built on the native Arabic root *ṭ-l-l* (Lane), where *ṭalal* is the part of a dwelling that remains standing after people leave; *aṭlāl* is its plural.
