## You'll want to know: statēra, statērae
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C464-STATERA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**statēra, statērae** — "a steelyard, a balance with one arm".

> Lanius carnem in statērā pōnit.
>
> *The butcher puts the meat on the steelyard.*

First declension, feminine: *statēra, statērae f.* A beam hung from a hook, with a weight slid along one arm.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*statēra* is usually traced to Greek *statēr*, a weight and a coin, built on *histēmi* 'to make stand, to weigh' (EtymDB). Lewis & Short compare Greek *stathmē* 'rule, balance' instead, so the exact source is not settled.
