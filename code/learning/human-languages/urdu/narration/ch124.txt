Warm-up.

You'll want to know: شاخ.
شاخ — shākh — "a branch".

The word, taken apart.
شاخ (shākh) is a loan, usually traced to Persian shākh, "branch, bough".

Lesson 3 of 5.
جھرنا (jharnā) — a waterfall.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a branch, then the Urdu for a rock.

You'll want to know: جھرنا.
جھرنا — jharnā — "a waterfall".

The word, taken apart.
جھرنا (jharnā) is a tadbhava, a word inherited and reshaped by sound change. It comes from jharati, "flowing down", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 5346).

Lesson 4 of 5.
تالاب (tālāb) — a pond, a pool.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a rock, then the Urdu for a waterfall.

You'll want to know: تالاب.
تالاب — tālāb — "a pond, a pool".

The word, taken apart.
تالاب (tālāb) is usually explained as tāl, "pond", which CDIAL lists under Sanskrit talla, "pond", a village word that may come from Munda (CDIAL 5742), with Persian āb, "water".

You'll want to know: کنواں.
کنواں — kuāṅ — "a well".

The word, taken apart.
کنواں (kuāṅ) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from kūpa, "hole, pit, well" (CDIAL 3400).
