## You'll want to know: सौचिकः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C506-VAYATI506-05]; assesses=[] -->

**सौचिकः** — *saucikaḥ* — "a tailor".

> सौचिकः सूच्या कञ्चुकं सीव्यति।
>
> *saucikaḥ sūcyā kañcukaṃ sīvyati.*
>
> The tailor sews a shirt with a needle.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सौचिकः** (*saucikaḥ*) is built on *sūci*, "a needle", with the ending *-ika*; MW gives *saucika* as one who lives by his needle, a tailor (MW).
