## You'll want to know: extemplō
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C292-REPENTE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**extemplō** — "in an instant, forthwith".

> Extemplō portae clausae sunt.
>
> *In an instant the gates were shut.*

Old and lively in tone: Plautus, Vergil and Livy use it. Plautus also has the longer *extempulō*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**extemplō** comes, says Lewis & Short, from *ex* and *tempulum*, a diminutive of *templum* in its old sense "a marked-out space": out of the very moment.
