## You'll want to know: فرزند
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C501-INLAWS-05]; assesses=[] -->

**فرزند** (*farzand*) — "a child (son or daughter), offspring".

> آنها سه فرزند دارند.
>
> *ânhâ se farzand dârand.*
>
> They have three children.

More formal than *bachche*. Plural *farzandân*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*farzand* is inherited Persian: EtymDB lists it beside Avestan *frazanti-*, 'offspring, progeny' (EtymDB).
