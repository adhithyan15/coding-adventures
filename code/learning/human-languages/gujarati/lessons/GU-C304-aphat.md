## You'll want to know: આફત
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C304-PUR304-04]; assesses=[] -->

**આફત** — *āphat* — "a calamity".

> ગામના લોકો કહે છે કે આ સૌથી મોટી આફત હતી.
>
> *gāmnā loko kahe che ke ā sauthī moṭī āphat hatī.*
>
> The villagers say that this was the worst calamity they have known.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**આફત** (*āphat*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *āfa*, "a blight, a calamity". It is feminine: **મોટી આફત**, a great calamity; **આફત આવી**, disaster struck.
