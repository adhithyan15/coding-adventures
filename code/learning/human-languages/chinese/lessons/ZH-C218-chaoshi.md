## You'll want to know: cháoshī
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C218-WIND218-05]; assesses=[] -->

*cháoshī* — "damp; humid".

> *xiàtiān zhèr hěn cháoshī* — Summers here are very humid.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*cháoshī* is built from *cháo* 'tide' and *shī* 'wet', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *drjew* and *syip* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'tide water' and the second 'soaked with water'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing things.
