### Fixed — forty-three chapters a driver could not start now open on a lesson they can do

Chapters with a drivable prefix of 0 fall from **306 to 263**. The by-type
count stays at 183 and the by-content count falls from **123 to 80**.
Core-drivable lessons rise from 27,560 to **27,669** (93% to 94%). Every one
of the 109 edited lessons now has a `voice` core. No chapter's prefix fell. No
classifier rule changed: the sight cues, the eyes-or-hands detectors and the
prompt and heading gates of the two previous driver-safety entries are as they
were, and all of them pass over the edited lessons. Each lesson was fixed in
one of four ways:

- **Deferred as an authored cue (84 lessons).** The anchor-word template that
  opens many script chapters said "Say it: *chīp*. Look at its shape as you
  say it: the lessons that follow take pieces of it, …". It is now
  "`[YOU LOOK: at its shape as you say it.]` The lessons that follow …" in all
  79 lessons that use it (Marathi 25, Gujarati 12, Malayalam 10, Kannada 9,
  Arabic 7, Bengali 5, Hindi 4, Telugu 3, Japanese, Punjabi, Sanskrit and
  Tamil 1 each). The narration says the look after "once you have stopped
  driving", and the book prints it unchanged in substance. Four of the
  Gujarati ones also opened their warm-up with a plain "write **ર**, **દ** and
  **છ** once each, from memory", which is now a `[YOU WRITE: …]` cue. Five
  Gujarati R4 reviews (GU-R16-*-r4) asked the driver to "point to the item"
  and "uncover **નામ** and read it once". They now ask for the item aloud and
  defer the reading as `[YOU READ: …]`. Their scored listening item was always
  by ear, so the R4 window still closes in the car.
- **A writing step moved into its own detachable section (1 lesson).**
  MR-C01-ho asked for "cover the models, write *da*, *dha*, *ba*" inside
  Guided Practice. That step and its scored activity
  (`MR-C01-ho-script-r2-c`, unchanged) now sit in
  "## Writing — the first return, from spoken names". This is the HL-C41
  interspersed pattern: the book prints the step and signs the lesson `pen`,
  and the car sets it aside. The lesson declares the `writing` skill, and its
  prose now names *dhanyavād* as the activity always did.
- **A pointing phrase that was never about the page (14 lessons).** These
  include a gloss ("the Italian for to look at", which is now quoted as
  "to look at"; HI-C73's "I look at the book and come."; ML-C87's
  "I see the teacher"; the whole of ES-C446-mirar, whose headword means
  *to look at*), a body part ("Look at your collarbone", now "Think of"), a
  figure of speech ("to look at them too hard", "promising to look at you
  again", "in how you look at it", "until you see the middle step", "when you
  see the word on a form", "Now look at *hay* again") and a cross-reference
  ("see the Hindi color lesson"). ZH-C18-hear-kan also told a listener to
  "put the cards away", which is now a `[YOU COVER: …]` cue.
- **A point said for the ear (10 lessons).** These are places where what the
  page showed is equally audible: "Listen for the ending" for Hindi -आ and
  Spanish -o/-e agreement, "Listen to the name: **a-be-ce-de**", "Hear it …
  **para** plus **aguas**", "Take *me duele la cabeza* and find the subject",
  "Take it apart" for Latin *nesciō*, "Notice the shape" for Telugu's dative
  construction, "Listen to the *I* form" for Russian т→ч, and "Listen to the
  middle of that word" for Spanish *aquel*.

The four chapters that the two previous driver-safety entries made
unstartable:

- **Gujarati ch20** opened on GU-R16-naam-r4 and could be started by ear for
  none of its 6 lessons. It is now fully drivable, 6 of 6.
- **Marathi ch4** opened on MR-C01-ho and could be started for none of its 5
  lessons. It is now fully drivable, 5 of 5.
- **Gujarati ch29** (GU-C25-savaar) is **left at 0**. The chapter is "the time
  words reach the page", and each lesson reads a word off the page and is the
  prerequisite of the next. Deferring the reading would leave the opener with
  nothing to do in the car while announcing it as drivable.
- **Urdu ch16** (UR-C16-practice, a 1-lesson chapter) is **left at 0** for the
  same reason. It is "eight words you already knew, now read", and its skills
  are `[reading, writing]`.

Both are counted by content, not by type, because neither lesson is
`type: reading`. They are named in the ceiling's comment as known honest
members.
