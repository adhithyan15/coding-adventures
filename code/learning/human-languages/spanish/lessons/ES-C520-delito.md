## You'll want to know: el delito
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C520-DELITO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**el delito** — "the offence, the crime".

> Robar es un delito.
>
> *Stealing is a crime.*

*Cometer un delito* — to commit an offence.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*delito* is a learned borrowing from Latin *dēlictum* 'a fault, a misdeed' (EtymDB), from *dēlinquere* 'to fall short, to do wrong' (Lewis & Short).
