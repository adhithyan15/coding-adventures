## You'll want to know: desesperado
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C717-SUFRIR-05]; assesses=[] -->

**desesperado** — "desperate".

> Estaba desesperado por encontrar piso.
>
> *He was desperate to find a flat.*

*Estar desesperado por* + infinitive.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*desesperado* is built on *desesperar* "to despair", which EtymDB traces to Latin *dēspērāre* "to lose hope", from *spērāre* "to hope".
