# crēdulus, crēdula, crēdulum — trusting, credulous

## You'll want to know: crēdulus, crēdula, crēdulum
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C498-SPES-05]; assesses=[] -->

**crēdulus, crēdula, crēdulum** — "trusting, credulous".

> Puer crēdulus omnia crēdit, quae audit.
>
> *The trusting boy believes everything he hears.*

Built on *crēdō* — to believe. English *credulous*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**crēdulus** is built on *crēdō* "to believe" with *-ulus* (Lewis & Short; EtymDB), and EtymDB traces *crēdō* to a Proto-Indo-European phrase meaning "to put one’s heart". English *credulous* comes from it.
