## You'll want to know: solucionar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C620-SOLUCION-02]; assesses=[] -->

**solucionar** — "to solve, to sort out".

> Vamos a solucionar el problema.
>
> *We are going to sort out the problem.*

Regular -ar verb; close to *resolver*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*solucionar* is built on *solución*, a learned borrowing from Latin *solutio*, from *solvere* 'to loosen' (EtymDB).
