## You'll want to know: condire
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C337-CONDIRE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**condire** — "to dress, to season".

> Condisco l'insalata con olio e limone.
>
> *I dress the salad with oil and lemon.*

Present like *capire*: *condisco*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*condire* is inherited from Latin *condīre* "to pickle, to season" (EtymDB).
