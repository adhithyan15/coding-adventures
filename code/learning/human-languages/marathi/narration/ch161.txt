Marathi, chapter 161: A Timetable, a Notice, the Rent, an Advert, a Receipt.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
वेळापत्रक (veḷāpatrak) — a timetable.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for from now on, then the Marathi for to plan.

You'll want to know: वेळापत्रक.
वेळापत्रक — veḷāpatrak — "a timetable".
[once you have stopped driving — read: the board — बस: सकाळी आठ वाजता.]
The bus leaves at eight in the morning, so be there before eight.

Wrap-up Recall.
