## You'll want to know: はこぶ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C155-MOVE155-02]; assesses=[] -->

**はこぶ** — *hakobu* — "to carry".

> **ちちと にもつを はこびます。** — *chichi to nimotsu o hakobimasu* — "I carry the luggage with my father."

Polite **はこびます**, *hakobimasu*; a godan verb.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**はこぶ** (*hakobu*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi, and its older origin is not settled (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
