## You'll want to know: घुसणो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C201-TOWN201-05]; assesses=[] -->

**घुसणो** — *ghusṇo* — "to get in; to enter".

> बकरी खेत मांय घुसै है।
>
> *bakrī khet mā̃y ghusai hai.*
>
> The goat keeps getting into the field.

Past (he …): वो मांय घुस्यो, *vo mā̃y ghusyo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**घुसणो** (*ghusṇo*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*ghuss*, "thrust in"; CDIAL lists Marwari *ghusṇo*, "to enter" (CDIAL 4492).
