## You'll want to know: die Motorhaube
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C405-DELLE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**die Motorhaube** — "the bonnet (of a car; US: hood)".

> Der Mechaniker öffnet die Motorhaube und schaut lange hinein.
>
> *The mechanic opens the bonnet and looks inside for a long time.*

Plural: *die Motorhauben*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Motorhaube* is built from *Motor* 'engine', from Latin *mōtor* 'mover' (EtymDB; Lewis & Short), and *Haube* 'cap, hood', an inherited Germanic word for a head covering.
