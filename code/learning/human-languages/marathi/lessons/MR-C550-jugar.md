## You'll want to know: जुगार
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C550-NASHIB550-02]; assesses=[] -->

**जुगार** — *jugār* — "gambling".

> जुगारात त्याने सगळे पैसे घालवले.
>
> *jugārāt tyāne sagḷe paise ghālavle.*
>
> He lost all his money gambling.

Masculine.

जुगार खेळणे, to gamble.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**जुगार** (*jugār*) is usually linked with Sanskrit *dyūtakāra*, "gambler", under which CDIAL lists Prakrit *juāra* and Hindi *juwārī* (CDIAL 6609). CDIAL gives no Marathi form, so the path is not settled.
