Malayalam, chapter 183: To choose, To use, To try, To get, To lose.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
തിരഞ്ഞെടുക്കുക (tiraññeṭukkuka) — to choose.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for to roast, then the Malayalam for to pour.

You'll want to know: തിരഞ്ഞെടുക്കുക.
തിരഞ്ഞെടുക്കുക — tiraññeṭukkuka — "to choose".

The word, taken apart.
തിരഞ്ഞെടുക്കുക (tiraññeṭukkuka) is native Dravidian, "take up having searched": the past of the searching verb (DEDR 3242, with Tamil tirakku) and the verb "to lift, take up" (DEDR 851, with Tamil eḻu, "to rise").

കിട്ടുക (kiṭṭuka) is native Dravidian, one word with Tamil kiṭṭu, Kannada kiṭṭu and Telugu kiṭṭu (DEDR 1538). Its first sense is "to come to hand, to reach".
