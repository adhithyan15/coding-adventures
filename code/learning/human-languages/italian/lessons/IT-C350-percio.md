## You'll want to know: perciò
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C350-SICCOME-03]; assesses=[] -->

**perciò** — "so, therefore".

> Ho perso il treno, perciò sono arrivato tardi.
>
> *I missed the train, so I arrived late.*

A little more formal than *quindi*; very frequent in writing and speech.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*perciò* is built from *per* "for" and *ciò* "this", which comes from Latin *ecce hoc* "look, this" (Lewis & Short): "for this", and so "therefore".
