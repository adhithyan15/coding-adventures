## You'll want to know: au moment où
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C269-QUAND-03]; assesses=[] -->

**au moment où** — "at the moment when, right as".

> Au moment où je sortais, le téléphone a sonné.
>
> *Right as I was going out, the phone rang.*

After *moment*, French says *où*, not *quand*: *au moment où il est entré*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*moment* comes from Latin *mōmentum* (EtymDB), taken over as a learned word; Lewis & Short gloss it "movement, motion", built on *movēre* "to move".
