# ಮುನ್ಸೂಚನೆ (munsūcane) — a forewarning; a forecast

## You'll want to know: ಮುನ್ಸೂಚನೆ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C332-VIPATTU332-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ಮುನ್ಸೂಚನೆ** — *munsūcane* — "a forewarning; a forecast".

> ಹವಾಮಾನ ಇಲಾಖೆ ಮೂರು ದಿನದ ಮುಂಚೆ ಮುನ್ಸೂಚನೆ ಕೊಟ್ಟಿತ್ತು.
>
> *havāmāna ilākhe mūru dinada muñce munsūcane koṭṭittu.*
>
> The weather department had given a warning three days earlier.

ಮುನ್, before, and ಸೂಚನೆ, a notice.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಮುನ್ಸೂಚನೆ** (*munsūcane*) is a Kannada compound of native *mun*, "before, in front" (DEDR 5020), and ಸೂಚನೆ, from Sanskrit *sūcanā*, "an indication, a communication" (Monier-Williams).
