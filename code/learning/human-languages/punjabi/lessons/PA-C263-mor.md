## You'll want to know: ਮੋੜ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C263-TURN263-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ਮੋੜ** — *moṛ* — "a bend in the road; a turning".

> ਅਗਲੇ ਮੋੜ ਉੱਤੇ ਇੱਕ ਦੁਕਾਨ ਹੈ।
>
> *agle moṛ utte ikk dukān hai.*
>
> There is a shop at the next bend.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਮੋੜ** (*moṛ*) is inherited from Sanskrit *muṭati*, "twists"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *moṛ*, "turn in a road", under it (CDIAL 10186).
