## You'll want to know: औसत
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C362-NISHKARSH362-02]; assesses=[] -->

**औसत** — *ausat* — "an average".

> पिछले साल बारिश औसत से कम हुई।
>
> *pichle sāl bāriś ausat se kam huī.*
>
> Last year the rain was below average.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**औसत** (*ausat*) is usually traced to Arabic *awsaṭ*, "middlemost", which reached Hindi through Persian.
