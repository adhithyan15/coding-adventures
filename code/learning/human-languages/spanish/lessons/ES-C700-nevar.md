## You'll want to know: nevar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C700-NIEVE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**nevar** — "to snow".

> En la sierra nieva en enero.
>
> *It snows in the mountains in January.*

The e becomes ie: *nieva*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*nevar* is inherited from Vulgar Latin *nivāre* "to snow" (EtymDB), from Latin *nix, nivis* "snow".
