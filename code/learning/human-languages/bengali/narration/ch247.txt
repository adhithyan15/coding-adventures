ফ্যাকাশে (phækāshe) — pale.

Warm-up.

You'll want to know: ফ্যাকাশে.
ফ্যাকাশে — phækāshe — "pale".

The word, taken apart.
CDIAL has no Bengali row for ফ্যাকাশে (phækāshe), "pale", but it appears to belong with Oriya phakā, "light-coloured", which CDIAL lists under Sanskrit phakka, "cripple", beside phikka, "defective" (CDIAL 9037). The same entry gives Bengali phikā, "weak (of tea), pale".

Lesson 3 of 5.
খাড়া (khāṛā) — steep, upright.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for next, then the Bengali for pale.

You'll want to know: খাড়া.
খাড়া — khāṛā — "steep, upright".

The word, taken apart.
খাড়া (khāṛā) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from khaḍaka, "erect; a post" (CDIAL 3784). CDIAL lists Bengali khāṛā, "standing erect".

The word, taken apart.
CDIAL lists চ্যাপ্টা (chæpṭā), "flattened", as Bengali cepṭā under Sanskrit cipiṭa, "flat, flat-nosed", in its variant cippiṭṭa (CDIAL 4818), and compares capp, "press".

Warm-up.

You'll want to know: চকচকে.
চকচকে — chôkchôke — "shiny".

The word, taken apart.
The origin of চকচকে (chôkchôke) is not settled: CDIAL has no entry for it, and a search by the glosses "shine" and "glitter" finds only words of other shapes, such as Bengali camkāna, "to glitter" (CDIAL 4676).
