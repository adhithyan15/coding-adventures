## You'll want to know: おうさま
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C299-OUSAMA299-01]; assesses=[] -->

**おうさま** — *ōsama* — "a king".

> **むかし、その くにに おうさまが いました。** — *mukashi, sono kuni ni ōsama ga imashita* — "Long ago that country had a king."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**おうさま** (*ōsama*) is a mixed word, native and Sino-Japanese: *ō*, "king", is a Chinese character read with its on'yomi, a reading borrowed from Chinese (Middle Chinese *hjwang*), and *sama*, "polite suffix", is native Japanese (JMdict, KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
