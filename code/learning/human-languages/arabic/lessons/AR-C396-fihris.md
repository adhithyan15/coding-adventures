## You'll want to know: فهرس
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C396-MUSTALAH396-03]; assesses=[] -->

**فهرس** — *fihris* — "an index".

> وجدت الكلمة في فهرس الكتاب.
>
> *wajadtu al-kalima fī fihris al-kitāb.*
>
> I found the word in the book's index.

Plural: فهارس, *fahāris*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**فهرس** (*fihris*) was borrowed from Persian *fihrist*, "a list" (EtymDB); Lane calls it an arabicized word for a catalogue of books (Lane). Today it is an index or table of contents.
