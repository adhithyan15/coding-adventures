Bengali, chapter 85: Trousers, Sock, Pocket, Ring, Cap.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
প্যান্ট (pænṭ) — trousers.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a village, then the Bengali for a city.

You'll want to know: প্যান্ট.
প্যান্ট — pænṭ — "trousers".

The word, taken apart.
প্যান্ট (pænṭ) is a loan from English pants.

Lesson 4 of 5.
আংটি (āngṭi) — a ring.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a sock, then the Bengali for a pocket.

You'll want to know: আংটি.
আংটি — āngṭi — "a ring".

The word, taken apart.
আংটি (āngṭi) is a tadbhava, a word inherited and reshaped by sound change. It comes from aṅguṣṭhiya, "of the thumb", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 138). CDIAL writes the Bengali form āṅṭi.

The word, taken apart.
টুপি (ṭupi) is a tadbhava, a word inherited and reshaped by sound change. It comes from ṭoppa, "hat, covering", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 5481). Late Sanskrit has ṭōpikā, "turban".
