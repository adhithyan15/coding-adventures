Urdu, chapter 353: Theory and Practice.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
نظریہ (nazariyā) — a theory.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for knowledge, then the Urdu for to pick out.

You'll want to know: نظریہ.
نظریہ — nazariyā — "a theory".
یہ نظریہ ابھی ثابت نہیں ہوا۔ yih nazariyā abhī sābit nahīṅ huā. This theory has not been proved yet.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
نظریہ (nazariyā) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic naẓariyya, "a theory", built on naẓar, "looking, consideration".

Lesson 4 of 5.
ٹکنا (ṭiknā) — to hold up; to last.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for an exception, then the Urdu for real.

You'll want to know: ٹکنا.
ٹکنا — ṭiknā — "to hold up; to last".
یہ نظریہ عمل میں نہیں ٹکتا۔ yih nazariyā amal meṅ nahīṅ ṭiktā. This theory does not hold up in practice.
Past (it …): وہ مکان طوفان میں ٹکا, voh makān tūfān meṅ ṭikā.

The word, taken apart.
ٹکنا (ṭiknā) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin ṭiknā, "to stop, to remain", under ṭakk, a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 5420).

Lesson 5 of 5.
منوانا (manvānā) — to win acceptance for; to get (someone) to accept.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for real, then the Urdu for to hold up.

You'll want to know: منوانا.
منوانا — manvānā — "to win acceptance for; to get (someone) to accept".
اس نے اپنی بات سب سے منوائی۔ us ne apnī bāt sab se manvāī. She got everyone to accept her point.
Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے اپنی بات منوائی, us ne apnī bāt manvāī.

The word, taken apart.
منوانا (manvānā) is the causative of mānnā, "to accept, to obey", which is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin mānnā under Sanskrit mányate, "thinks" (CDIAL 9857).
