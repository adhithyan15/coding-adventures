Portuguese, chapter 201: To book, To spend, To save, To order, To dry.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
reservar — to book.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for a magazine, then the Portuguese for a dictionary.

You'll want to know: reservar.
reservar — "to book".

The word, taken apart.
reservar from Latin reservare, "to keep back" (Lewis & Short) — a learned borrowing from Latin.
