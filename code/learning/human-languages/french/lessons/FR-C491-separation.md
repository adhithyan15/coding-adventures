## You'll want to know: la séparation
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C491-RUPTURE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**la séparation** — "separation, parting".

> La séparation a été difficile pour les enfants.
>
> *The separation was hard for the children.*

Also a parting: *une séparation à la gare*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*la séparation* is a learned borrowing from Latin *sēparātiō* "a separation" (EtymDB), from *sēparāre* "to part, to separate" (Lewis & Short).
