## You'll want to know: amplector, amplectī
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C535-AMAN-01]; assesses=[] -->

**amplector, amplectī** — "to embrace, hug".

> Pater fīlium redeuntem amplectitur.
>
> *The father embraces his son as he comes home.*

Deponent: *amplector, amplectī, amplexus sum*: *ambi-* 'around' + *plectō* 'to twine'. Also to welcome an idea.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**amplector** is built on *ambi-* "around" and *plector*, from *plectō* "to twine, plait" (EtymDB): to wind the arms around.
