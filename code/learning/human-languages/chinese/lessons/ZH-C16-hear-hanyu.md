# Hear *hànyǔ* — the Chinese language

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ZH-LEX-HANZI-01] -->

[PAUSE 8s] Say **Chinese character** in Mandarin.

## You'll want to know
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-HANYU-01]; assesses=[] -->

> *hànyǔ* — **the Chinese language; Mandarin**

Keep the known falling *hàn*. Then let *yǔ* dip in third tone. The new written
half waits; today the contrast is heard: *hànzì* is a character, *hànyǔ* is the
language.
