## You'll want to know: तोषयति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C449-VISMAYA449-04]; assesses=[] -->

**तोषयति** — *toṣayati* — "pleases; gratifies".

> पौत्रः मधुरेण गीतेन पितामहं तोषयति।
>
> *pautraḥ madhureṇa gītena pitāmahaṃ toṣayati.*
>
> The grandson pleases his grandfather with a sweet song.

Past (he …): सः पितामहं तोषितवान्, *saḥ pitāmahaṃ toṣitavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**तोषयति** (*toṣayati*) is built on the causative of the root √tuṣ, "to be satisfied, to be pleased", the root of **तुष्यति** and **सन्तोषः**: to make someone pleased; MW gives the causative the senses "to satisfy, please, appease" (MW).
