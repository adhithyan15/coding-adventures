## You'll want to know: آلبوم
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C520-MUSICIANS-05]; assesses=[] -->

**آلبوم** (*âlbom*) — "an album".

> آلبوم تازه این خواننده را خریدم.
>
> *âlbom-e tâze-ye in khânande râ kharidam.*
>
> I bought this singer's new album.

Also a photo album: *âlbom-e 'aks*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*âlbom* is a modern loan, usually traced to French *album*. EtymDB has no entry for the Persian word.
