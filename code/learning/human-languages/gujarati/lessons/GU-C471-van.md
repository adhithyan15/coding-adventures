## You'll want to know: વાન
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C471-VAN471-01]; assesses=[] -->

**વાન** — *vān* — "complexion; skin colour".

> દાદા કહે છે કે મારો વાન મારી માતાનો છે.
>
> *dādā kahe che ke māro vān mārī mātāno che.*
>
> Grandfather says that I have my mother's complexion.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**વાન** (*vān*) is inherited from Sanskrit *varṇa*, "appearance, colour"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati word, "colour" (CDIAL 11338).
