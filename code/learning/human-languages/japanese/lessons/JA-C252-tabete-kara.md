# たべてから (tabete kara) — after doing

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[JA-GRAMMAR-C252-TE-01, JA-GRAMMAR-C252-TE-02, JA-LEX-C35-FOOD35-04] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "I ate breakfast and went out". (*asagohan o tabete, dekakemashita*.) Now make the order matter: first breakfast, and only then out.

## You'll want to know: -てから
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-GRAMMAR-C252-TE-KARA-01]; assesses=[JA-LEX-C152-READY152-03, JA-LEX-C41-SCHOOL41-01] -->

> **あさごはんを たべてから、でかけました。**
>
> *asagohan o tabete kara, dekakemashita.* — "After eating breakfast, I went out."

**から** (*kara*) after the **-て** form makes "after": the first action was over before the second began. **-て** alone lines actions up; **-てから** says one waited for the other.

> **てがみを かいてから、ねました。**
>
> *tegami o kaite kara, nemashita.* — "After writing a letter, I went to bed."

## You'll want to know: plans and habits
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-GRAMMAR-C252-TE-KARA-02]; assesses=[JA-LEX-C147-WASH147-02, JA-LEX-C40-HOME40-03, JA-LEX-C233-SPORT233-01] -->

With the non-past, **-てから** gives the order of a plan or a habit:

> **はを みがいてから、ねます。**
>
> *ha o migaite kara, nemasu.* — "I brush my teeth before I go to bed."

> **しゅくだいを してから、あそびます。**
>
> *shukudai o shite kara, asobimasu.* — "I will play after doing my homework."

Two more words for "and then", which start a new sentence, come next.
