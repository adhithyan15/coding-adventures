# ग्राह्य धरणे (grāhya dharṇe) — to treat as valid

## You'll want to know: ग्राह्य धरणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C350-SVIKAR350-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ग्राह्य धरणे** — *grāhya dharṇe* — "to treat as valid".

> शिक्षकांनी त्याचं दुसरं उत्तरही ग्राह्य धरलं.
>
> *śikṣakānnī tyācaṁ dusraṁ uttarhī grāhya dharlaṁ.*
>
> The teacher accepted his second answer as valid too.

Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने ग्राह्य धरलं, *tyāne grāhya dharlaṁ*.

धरणे, to hold: to hold something as acceptable.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ग्राह्य धरणे** (*grāhya dharṇe*) joins the tatsama ग्राह्य, Sanskrit *grāhya*, "to be seized, to be accepted" (Monier-Williams), to धरणे, "to hold".
