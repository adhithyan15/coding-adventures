## You'll want to know: به هوش آمدن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C326-MASDUM-05]; assesses=[] -->

**به هوش آمدن** (*be hush âmadan*) — "to come round, to regain consciousness".

> او بعد از ده دقیقه به هوش آمد.
>
> *U ba'd az dah daqiqe be hush âmad.*
>
> She came round after ten minutes.

Literally "to come to consciousness". Present stem *â-*: *be hush miâyad*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*hush* "consciousness" is inherited from Middle Persian (EtymDB), and *âmadan* "to come" is native Persian too; EtymDB takes it back to the Indo-European root *gʷem-* "to step".
