# પસંદ કરવું (pasand karvũ) — to choose, to pick

## You'll want to know: પસંદ કરવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C260-GAMES260-05]; assesses=[] -->

**પસંદ કરવું** — *pasand karvũ* — "to choose, to pick".

> **તેણે લાલ રમકડું પસંદ કર્યું.** — *teṇe lāl ramakḍũ pasand karyũ* — "She chose the red toy."

**પસંદ**, *pasand*, alone means liked or chosen.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**પસંદ કરવું** (*pasand karvũ*) is built from *pasand*, "liked, approved", a loan usually traced to Persian *pasand*, and the verb *karvũ*, "to do".
