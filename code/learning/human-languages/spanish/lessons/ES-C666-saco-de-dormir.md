## You'll want to know: el saco de dormir
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C666-CAMPING-01]; assesses=[] -->

**el saco de dormir** — "the sleeping bag".

> Hace frío, lleva un buen saco de dormir.
>
> *It is cold, take a good sleeping bag.*

In Latin America also *la bolsa de dormir*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*saco* is inherited from Latin *saccus* "a sack, a bag" (Lewis & Short; EtymDB), and *dormir* from Latin *dormīre* "to sleep".
