## You'll want to know: تفصیل
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C301-SAHAFI301-04]; assesses=[] -->

**تفصیل** — *tafsīl* — "a detail; a full account".

> اخبار میں حادثے کی تفصیل تھی۔
>
> *akhbār meṅ hādse kī tafsīl thī.*
>
> The paper had the details of the accident.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**تفصیل** (*tafsīl*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *tafṣīl*, "a setting out part by part". **تفصیل سے** is in detail.
