5 lessons. You can do the first one in the car; after that you will want to have stopped.


Lesson 1 of 5.
telefonare — to phone.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for "to show", then the Italian for "to look at".

You'll want to know: telefonare.
telefonare — "to phone".

The word, taken apart.
telefonare — built from telefono, "telephone", with the verb ending -are. EtymDB 2.1 has no row for it; the parts are plain to see.

You'll want to know: perdonare.
perdonare — "to forgive".

The word, taken apart.
perdonare from Late Latin perdonare, "to give completely", from per-, "thoroughly", and donare, "to give" — inherited. EtymDB 2.1 has no row for perdonare; it derives Spanish perdonar from perdonare.
