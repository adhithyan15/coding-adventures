## You'll want to know: ভূত
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C322-RAKKHOSH322-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ভূত** — *bhūt* — "a ghost".

> লোকে বলে যে পুরনো বাড়িটায় একটা ভূত থাকে।
>
> *loke bôle je purôno bāṛiṭāy ekṭā bhūt thāke.*
>
> People say a ghost lives in the old house.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ভূত** (*bhūt*) is Sanskrit *bhūta*, 'become, been, past' (Monier-Williams), and so a being, a spirit: one who has been.
