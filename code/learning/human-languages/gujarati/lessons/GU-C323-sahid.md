## You'll want to know: શહીદ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C323-SHAHID323-01]; assesses=[] -->

**શહીદ** — *śahīd* — "a martyr".

> ગામ આ શહીદોનું ઋણી છે.
>
> *gām ā śahīdonũ ṛṇī che.*
>
> The village owes a debt to these martyrs.

A masculine or feminine noun, by the person meant.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**શહીદ** (*śahīd*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *šahīd*, "a witness", and so "a martyr", one whose death bears witness. Someone **શહીદ થાય છે**, dies for a cause.
