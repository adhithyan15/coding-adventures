## You'll want to know: हाँफना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C403-STAIND403-05]; assesses=[] -->

**हाँफना** — *hā̃phnā* — "to pant".

> सीढ़ियाँ चढ़कर मैं हाँफ रहा था।
>
> *sīṛhiyā̃ caṛhkar maiṁ hā̃ph rahā thā.*
>
> I was panting after climbing the stairs.

Past (he …): वह बहुत हाँफा, *vah bahut hā̃phā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**हाँफना** (*hā̃phnā*) goes back to *\*hamph*, "pant", a form CDIAL reconstructs (CDIAL 13973), which lists Hindi *hā̃phnā*; the word itself sounds like the breathing it names.
