# Review — asking the way

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[UR-GRAMMAR-C269-SHOULD-01, UR-GRAMMAR-C269-SO-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "you should go" and "so I didn't come". (*āp ko jānā chāhiye*, *is liye maiṅ nahīṅ āyā*.)

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[UR-LEX-C270-KITNA-01, UR-LEX-C270-ZARA-01, UR-SCRIPT-C270-ZAL-01, UR-GRAMMAR-C270-DIRECTION-01, UR-GRAMMAR-C270-DIRECTION-02, UR-LEX-C80-TOWN80-01, UR-LEX-C80-TOWN80-04] -->

[PAUSE 2s each]
- [YOU SAY: Excuse me, how do you get to the station?]
- [YOU SAY: How far is it? How much is the fare?]
- [YOU SAY: Go straight on, then turn left after the bridge.]
- [YOU SAY: Could you tell me where the market is?]
- [YOU WRITE: **ذرا** once, and point at ذ inside it]

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[UR-LEX-C270-KITNA-01, UR-GRAMMAR-C270-DIRECTION-01, UR-GRAMMAR-C270-DIRECTION-02] -->

[PAUSE 3s] "How many passengers?" (**کتنے مسافر؟**) Now the other way round: from **مڑیے**, what is the verb? (**مڑنا**.) The polite form of **دینا**? (**دیجیے**.)
