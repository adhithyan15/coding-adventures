# വിഷമിക്കുക (viṣamikkuka) — to be upset; to feel bad

## You'll want to know: വിഷമിക്കുക
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C517-VIKARAM517-05]; assesses=[] -->

**വിഷമിക്കുക** — *viṣamikkuka* — "to be upset; to feel bad".

> ജോലി പോയതിൽ അവൻ വളരെ വിഷമിച്ചു.
>
> *jōli pōyatil avan vaḷare viṣamiccu.*
>
> He was very upset at losing his job.

Past: വിഷമിച്ചു, *viṣamiccu*.

The noun is വിഷമം, distress. What upsets you takes -ഇൽ: ജോലി പോയതിൽ, at the job going.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**വിഷമിക്കുക** (*viṣamikkuka*) is built from Sanskrit *viṣama*, "uneven, difficult, dangerous" (CDIAL 11972), with the Malayalam verb ending -ിക്കുക. To be upset is to be in an uneven, difficult state.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
