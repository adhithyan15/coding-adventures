German, chapter 153: To hike, To change, To throw, To repeat, To pull.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
wandern — to hike.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to promise, then the German for to introduce.

You'll want to know: wandern.
wandern — "to hike".

The word, taken apart.
wandern from Old High German wantarōn from Proto-Germanic wandrōną — inherited, the same verb as English wander. (EtymDB 2.1).
