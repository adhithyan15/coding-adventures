# Review — at the station

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[KA-LEX-C314-AUSADHA-01, KA-SCRIPT-RECOG-175, KA-LEX-C310-SVACCHA-01, KA-SCRIPT-RECOG-174] -->

- [YOU WRITE: **ಔ** and **ಛ**, once each from memory]

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[KA-LEX-C314-SITU-01, KA-GRAMMAR-C314-BUY-TICKET-01, KA-GRAMMAR-C314-WHEN-LEAVES-01, KA-LEX-C314-AUSADHA-01, KA-SCRIPT-RECOG-175, KA-GRAMMAR-C314-MISSED-01, KA-GRAMMAR-C314-LOST-REPORT-01, KA-GRAMMAR-C313-ASK-WAY-01, KA-GRAMMAR-C313-LANDMARK-01, KA-GRAMMAR-C312-KARANA-CLAUSE-01, KA-LEX-C63-HEALTH-03, KA-LEX-C34-TEGEDUKO-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s each]
- [YOU SAY: One ticket to my home town, please. At what time does the bus leave?]
- [YOU SAY: Is this seat free? I would like a seat by the window.]
- [YOU SAY: Oh no, the bus has gone. Where is the station? Is it next to the hotel?]
- [YOU SAY: Because I had a fever, I took medicine.]
- [YOU SAY: My bag is lost. My phone was in the bag.]
- [YOU SAY: I'm not well. I need medicine.]
- [YOU WRITE: **ಔಷಧ** and **ಸೀಟು**]
