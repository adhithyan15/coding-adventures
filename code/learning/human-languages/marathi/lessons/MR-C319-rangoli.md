## You'll want to know: रांगोळी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C319-ROSHNAI319-02]; assesses=[] -->

**रांगोळी** — *rāṅgoḷī* — "rangoli; a floor pattern".

> आईने दारासमोर सुंदर रांगोळी काढली.
>
> *āīne dārāsamor sundar rāṅgoḷī kāḍhlī.*
>
> Mother made a beautiful rangoli in front of the door.

Feminine.

रांगोळी काढणे, to make one with coloured powder.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**रांगोळी** (*rāṅgoḷī*) is the Marathi word for a pattern of coloured powder on the floor; its origin is not settled.
