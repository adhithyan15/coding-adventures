Persian, chapter 460: Window shopping.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ویترین (vitrin) — a shop window; a display case.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for fake, then the Persian for a guarantee.

You'll want to know: ویترین.
ویترین (vitrin) — "a shop window; a display case".
دختر جلوی ویترین ایستاد و کفشها را نگاه کرد. Dokhtar jolow-ye vitrin istâd va kafsh-hâ râ negâh kard. The girl stopped in front of the shop window and looked at the shoes.
Also a glass cabinet at home for the best china.

The word, taken apart.
vitrin was borrowed from French vitrine 'shop window', which EtymDB derives from vitre 'pane of glass'; that goes back to Latin vitrum 'glass' (Lewis & Short).

پاساژ (pâsâzh) — "a shopping arcade, a mall".
در پاساژ یک کافه کوچک هست. Dar pâsâzh yek kâfe-ye kuchek hast. There is a small café in the arcade.
Iranian towns have many pâsâzh, covered buildings full of small shops.

The word, taken apart.

Lesson 3 of 5.
پرو کردن (porov kardan) — to try on (clothes).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a shop window, then the Persian for a shopping arcade.

You'll want to know: پرو کردن.
پرو کردن (porov kardan) — "to try on (clothes)".
قبل از خرید شلوار را پرو کردم. Qabl az kharid shalvâr râ porov kardam. I tried the trousers on before buying them.
porov 'a fitting' + kardan.

The word, taken apart.
porov kardan is built from porov 'a fitting', a loan usually traced to French preuve 'proof, trial', and kardan 'to do', inherited from Middle Persian kardan (EtymDB).

اتاق پرو (otâq-e porov) شلوغ بود و منتظر ماندم. Otâq-e porov sholugh bud va montazer mândam. The fitting room was busy and I waited.
otâq 'room' + porov 'fitting'.

The word, taken apart.
otâq-e porov is built from otâq 'room', a loan usually traced to Turkic otaq 'tent, room', and porov 'a fitting', usually traced to French preuve.

Lesson 5 of 5.
پسندیدن (pasandidan) — to like, to approve of (a thing).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to try on, then the Persian for a fitting room.

You'll want to know: پسندیدن.
پسندیدن (pasandidan) — "to like, to approve of (a thing)".
مادرم این پیراهن را پسندید و خرید. Mâdaram in pirâhan râ pasandid va kharid. My mother liked this shirt and bought it.
Present stem pasand-: mipasandam. Pasand — taste, liking.

The word, taken apart.
pasandidan is inherited and is usually traced to Middle Persian passandīdan 'to approve'. EtymDB does not list it.
