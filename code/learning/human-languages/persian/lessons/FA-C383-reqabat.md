## You'll want to know: رقابت
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C383-TOWLID-03]; assesses=[] -->

**رقابت** (*reqâbat*) — "competition, rivalry".

> رقابت بین دو تیم خیلی سخت بود.
>
> *reqâbat beyn-e do tim kheyli sakht bud.*
>
> The competition between the two teams was very tough.

*Reqâbat kardan* — to compete.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**رقابت** is usually traced to Arabic *raqāba*, from the same Arabic family as *raqīb* "rival" (EtymDB), the source of Persian *raqib*.
