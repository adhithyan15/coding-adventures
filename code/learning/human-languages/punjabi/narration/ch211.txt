Punjabi, chapter 211: Going and Drinking Every Day.
7 lessons. You can do the first one in the car; after that you will want to have stopped.


Lesson 1 of 7.
Return to four familiar verbs — retrieve to go, to eat, to drink and the familiar sleeps form by ear.

Warm-up.
[your turn — hear: jāṇā; khāṇā; pīṇā]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
Say ਜਾਣਾ, ਖਾਣਾ, ਪੀਣਾ.

Grammar Lens.
You already say ਕਰਦਾ, "does", and ਸੌਂਦਾ, "sleeps". Listen to the two middles: kar-dā runs straight into its ending, but saun-dā carries a nasal hum before it. The next lesson gives that hum to three verbs you use every day.

Guided Practice.
[your turn — hear: to go]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
Say ਜਾਣਾ.
[your turn — hear: to eat]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
Say ਖਾਣਾ.
[your turn — hear: to drink]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
Say ਪੀਣਾ.
[your turn — hear: sleeps, of a man]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
Say ਸੌਂਦਾ.

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
Say the four once more, slowly. Nothing new is needed yet.


Lesson 2 of 7.
ਜਾਂਦਾ, ਜਾਂਦੀ — goes, every day.

Before we start: this one needs your eyes, so it is not fully a driving lesson. You will want your eyes or your hands for the steps that ask you to read, point, write or gesture. You can listen to everything else now. I will say so again when we reach it, but leave this section until you have stopped: Guided Practice.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say ਜਾਣਾ, "to go", then ਸੌਂਦਾ, "sleeps". Then every day: ਰੋਜ਼.

Grammar Lens: a stem that ends in a vowel.
Take -ਣਾ off ਜਾਣਾ and the stem ਜਾ ends in a vowel. Before -ਦਾ such a stem takes a nasal, the dot ਂ, as ਸੌਂਦਾ does:
ਜਾ + ਂ + ਦਾ becomes ਜਾਂਦਾ.
ਮੈਂ ਰੋਜ਼ ਸਕੂਲ ਜਾਂਦਾ ਹਾਂ। — maiṁ roz sakūl jāndā hāṁ. — I go to school every day (a man speaking).
ਮੈਂ ਰੋਜ਼ ਸਕੂਲ ਜਾਂਦੀ ਹਾਂ। — maiṁ roz sakūl jāndī hāṁ. — I go to school every day (a woman speaking).
The ending agrees with the one who goes, as in ਮੈਂ ਪੰਜਾਬੀ ਬੋਲਦਾ ਹਾਂ. The helper ਹਾਂ stays at the end.

[once you have stopped driving — this part needs your eyes or your hands; if you are driving, skip ahead to the next part]
Guided Practice.
[your turn — hear: I go home every day, as a man]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
Say ਮੈਂ ਰੋਜ਼ ਘਰ ਜਾਂਦਾ ਹਾਂ।.
[your turn — hear: she goes to school every day]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
Say ਉਹ ਰੋਜ਼ ਸਕੂਲ ਜਾਂਦੀ ਹੈ।.
[once you have stopped driving — read: ਜਾਂਦਾ]
Point to the dot that brings the nasal.
[once you have stopped driving — write: copy ਜਾਂਦਾ once]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
Goes, of a man? (ਜਾਂਦਾ.) Of a woman? (ਜਾਂਦੀ.)

Lesson 4 of 7.
ਖਾਂਦਾ, ਪੀਂਦਾ — eats, drinks.

Before we start: this one needs your eyes, so it is not fully a driving lesson. You will want your eyes or your hands for the steps that ask you to read, point, write or gesture. You can listen to everything else now. I will say so again when we reach it, but leave this section until you have stopped: Guided Practice.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say I go to school every day. Then ਖਾਣਾ and ਪੀਣਾ.

Grammar Lens: ਖਾ and ਪੀ.
ਖਾ and ਪੀ end in vowels too, so they work like ਜਾ: ਖਾਂਦਾ, ਖਾਂਦੀ; ਪੀਂਦਾ, ਪੀਂਦੀ.
ਮੈਂ ਰੋਜ਼ ਰੋਟੀ ਖਾਂਦਾ ਹਾਂ। — maiṁ roz roṭī khāndā hāṁ. — I eat roti every day (a man speaking).
ਉਹ ਰੋਜ਼ ਦੁੱਧ ਪੀਂਦੀ ਹੈ। — uh roz duddh pīndī hai. — She drinks milk every day.

[once you have stopped driving — this part needs your eyes or your hands; if you are driving, skip ahead to the next part]
Guided Practice.
[your turn — hear: I drink water every day, as a woman]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
Say ਮੈਂ ਰੋਜ਼ ਪਾਣੀ ਪੀਂਦੀ ਹਾਂ।.
[your turn — hear: he eats roti every day]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
Say ਉਹ ਰੋਜ਼ ਰੋਟੀ ਖਾਂਦਾ ਹੈ।.
[once you have stopped driving — read: ਪੀਂਦਾ]
Point to ਂ, then say pīndā.
[once you have stopped driving — write: copy ਖਾਂਦਾ once]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
Eats, of a man? (ਖਾਂਦਾ.) Drinks, of a woman? (ਪੀਂਦੀ.)

Lesson 6 of 7.
ਅਸੀਂ ਰੋਜ਼ ਚਾਹ ਪੀਂਦੇ ਹਾਂ — we drink tea every day.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say I drink tea every day. Then the familiar ਅਸੀਂ ਮਿਲਦੇ ਹਾਂ, "we meet".

Grammar Lens: -ਦੇ for more than one.
ਮਿਲਦੇ showed the -ਦੇ ending for ਅਸੀਂ. The vowel stems take it too, nasal and all: ਜਾਂਦੇ, ਖਾਂਦੇ, ਪੀਂਦੇ. ਤੁਸੀਂ takes it as well, with ਹੋ:
ਅਸੀਂ ਰੋਜ਼ ਚਾਹ ਪੀਂਦੇ ਹਾਂ। — asīṁ roz chāh pīnde hāṁ. — We drink tea every day.
ਤੁਸੀਂ ਰੋਜ਼ ਸਕੂਲ ਜਾਂਦੇ ਹੋ। — tusīṁ roz sakūl jānde ho. — You go to school every day.

Guided Practice.
[your turn — hear: we eat roti every day]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
Say ਅਸੀਂ ਰੋਜ਼ ਰੋਟੀ ਖਾਂਦੇ ਹਾਂ।.
[your turn — hear: you go home every day]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
Say ਤੁਸੀਂ ਰੋਜ਼ ਘਰ ਜਾਂਦੇ ਹੋ।.
[once you have stopped driving — read: ਅਸੀਂ ਰੋਜ਼ ਚਾਹ ਪੀਂਦੇ ਹਾਂ। (asīṁ roz chāh pīnde hāṁ.)]
