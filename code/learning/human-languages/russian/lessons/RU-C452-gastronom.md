## You'll want to know: гастроном
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C452-BULOCHNAYA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**гастроном** (*gastronóm*) — "a grocery store, a food shop".

> В гастрономе на углу мы купили сыр и колбасу.
>
> *V gastronóme na uglú my kupíli syr i kolbasú.*
>
> We bought cheese and sausage at the grocery store on the corner.

Masculine. Also an old word for a gourmet.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*гастроном* is usually traced, probably through French *gastronome*, to Greek *gastḗr* 'belly' and *nómos* 'law': first a gourmet, then a food shop. EtymDB does not list it, so the line is hedged.
