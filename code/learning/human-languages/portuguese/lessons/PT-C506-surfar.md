## You'll want to know: surfar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C506-AGUA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**surfar** — "to surf".

> Os meus primos surfam todo o ano.
>
> *My cousins surf all year round.*

Also *fazer surf*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
