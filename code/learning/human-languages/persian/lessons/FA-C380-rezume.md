## You'll want to know: رزومه
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C380-REZUME-01]; assesses=[] -->

**رزومه** (*rezume*) — "a CV, a résumé".

> رزومه خودم را برای سه شرکت فرستادم.
>
> *rezume-ye khodam râ barâye se sherkat ferestâdam.*
>
> I sent my CV to three companies.

A loan from French. A more Persian phrase is *sâbeqe-ye kâri*, a work record.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**رزومه** was borrowed from French *résumé* "a summary", the participle of *résumer*, from Latin *resumere* "to take up again" (Lewis & Short): a CV sums up your working life.
