# நேற்று (nēṟṟu) — yesterday

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[TA-LEX-C73-WHEN-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: what did *iṉṟu* mean?

## You'll want to know: நேற்று
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C73-WHEN-02]; assesses=[] -->

**நேற்று** (*nēṟṟu*) — "yesterday".

"Yesterday" — the third corner of the same triangle: நேற்று, இன்று, நாளை.

The middle is a doubled **ற்ற**, the hard r held long, and it is the sound that most often
catches a new speaker out. Say it slowly: the first ற has a புள்ளி and no vowel, so it leans
straight onto the second.

With three words you can now place any day against the one you are in.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

Two, and the days on either side of today are named.
