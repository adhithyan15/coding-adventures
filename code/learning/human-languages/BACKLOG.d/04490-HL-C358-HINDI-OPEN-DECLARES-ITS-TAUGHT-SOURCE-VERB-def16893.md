## HL-C358 — Hindi open declares its taught source verb

Chapter 81 introduces **खुला** by explicitly deriving it from **खुलना**, glossing
the source verb as “to open” and using that relationship to explain the resulting
state. Its declaration exposes only **खुला**, leaving the taught source verb
looking untaught to the corpus report.

Declare **खुलना** beside **खुला**. This makes the lesson's explicit derivational
teaching visible to reports and downstream study artifacts without adding a form
the lesson does not explain.
