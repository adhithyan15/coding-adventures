# Review — what was going on

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[UR-GRAMMAR-C265-ERGATIVE-01, UR-GRAMMAR-C265-FUTURE-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "Sara wrote a letter" and "I will go". (*Sārā ne khat likhā*, *maiṅ jāūṅ gā*.)

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[UR-GRAMMAR-C266-ONGOING-PAST-01, UR-GRAMMAR-C266-ONGOING-PAST-02, UR-LEX-C266-DAURAN-01, UR-LEX-BARISH, UR-LEX-C87-WORLD87-02, UR-LEX-C113-THINGS113-03] -->

[PAUSE 2s each]
- [YOU SAY: I was writing a letter. Sara was reading.]
- [YOU SAY: It was raining outside.]
- [YOU SAY: When I came home, Sara was sleeping.]
- [YOU SAY: During the journey it was raining.]
- [YOU SAY: He came during the meal.]

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[UR-GRAMMAR-C266-ONGOING-PAST-01, UR-GRAMMAR-C266-ONGOING-PAST-02, UR-LEX-C266-DAURAN-01] -->

[PAUSE 3s] "We were eating"? (**ہم کھا رہے تھے**.) Now the other way round: what does **رہی تھیں** tell you? (Women, and something going on.) In **جب … تو …**, which half is the event? (The **جب** half, in the plain past.) "During the journey"? (**سفر کے دوران**.)
