No Sanskrit model is shown before you answer.

[PAUSE 12s] Imagine that a teacher points to the timetable in a classroom.
Ask when the exam will be and answer that it will be tomorrow. Say what
homework you will do and what lesson you will study. Finish by recalling the
school, student, teacher, mark, and answer words.

## What you've built: boundary

You can now complete one small A1 school-day plan with familiar language. This
is **not** complete classroom discourse, school-system, grading, educational
rights, or future-paradigm coverage, and it is not evidence of exam readiness,
human validation, or learner success.
