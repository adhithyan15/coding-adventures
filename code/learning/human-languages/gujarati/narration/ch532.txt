Lesson 1 of 6.
પોસાવું (posāvũ) — to be within one's means.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a change, then the Gujarati for arrangements.

You'll want to know: પોસાવું.
પોસાવું — posāvũ — "to be within one's means".
જો અમને નવી ગાડી પોસાશે તો અમે તે ખરીદીશું. jo amne navī gāḍī posāśe to ame te kharīdīśũ. If we can afford a new car, we will buy it.
Past (it …): તે ભાડું તેને પોસાયું, te bhāḍũ tene posāyũ.

The word, taken apart.
પોસાવું (posāvũ) is filed by CDIAL under Sanskrit pōṣáyati, "nourishes", beside a Gujarati posvũ, "to nourish" (CDIAL 8410); what one can keep up and pay for is affordable. The person takes ને: મને પોસાય છે.

Lesson 2 of 6.
ગજું (gajũ) — one's capacity.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for arrangements, then the Gujarati for to be within one's means.

You'll want to know: ગજું.
ગજું — gajũ — "one's capacity".
જો મારું ગજું હોત તો હું મોટું ઘર ખરીદત. jo mārũ gajũ hot to hũ moṭũ ghar kharīdat. If I had the means, I would buy a big house.
A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
ગજું (gajũ) is what a person can manage or afford: મારું ગજું નથી, it is beyond me. No dictionary we use records an older form, so its history is not settled.

Lesson 3 of 6.
નભવું (nabhvũ) — to get by.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to be within one's means, then the Gujarati for one's capacity.

You'll want to know: નભવું.
નભવું — nabhvũ — "to get by".
જો પગાર ઓછો હશે તો આપણે ઓછા પૈસામાં નભીશું. jo pagār ocho haśe to āpaṇe ochā paisāmā̃ nabhīśũ. If the pay is low, we will get by on less money.
Past (it …): તે કુટુંબ નભી ગયું, te kuṭumb nabhī gayũ.

The word, taken apart.
નભવું (nabhvũ) is inherited from Sanskrit nírvahati, "carries out, carries through", and CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb, "to endure, continue" (CDIAL 7397).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: અનુકૂળ.
અનુકૂળ — anukūḷ — "convenient".
જો તમને રવિવાર અનુકૂળ હશે તો આપણે ત્યારે મળીશું. jo tamne ravivār anukūḷ haśe to āpaṇe tyāre maḷīśũ. If Sunday suits you, we will meet then.

The word, taken apart.
અનુકૂળ (anukūḷ) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit anukūla, "following the slope of the bank", and so "favourable, suitable" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 5 of 6.
રાજીનામું (rājīnāmũ) — a resignation.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to get by, then the Gujarati for convenient.

You'll want to know: રાજીનામું.
રાજીનામું — rājīnāmũ — "a resignation".
જો સાહેબ મારી વાત નહીં માને તો હું રાજીનામું આપીશ. jo sāheb mārī vāt nahī̃ māne to hũ rājīnāmũ āpīś. If the boss won't listen to me, I will resign.
A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
રાજીનામું (rājīnāmũ) is usually traced to Persian rāzīnāma, "a letter of consent"; in Gujarati it is the letter that gives up a post, and one gives it: રાજીનામું આપવું.
