Italian, chapter 429: Dove dormire.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
l'ostello — the hostel.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for to fasten, then the Italian for the window.

You'll want to know: l'ostello.
l'ostello — "the hostel".
L'ostello costa poco ed è pulito. The hostel is cheap and clean.
L'ostello della gioventù — youth hostel.

The word, taken apart.
ostello was borrowed from Old French ostel 'shelter', which is inherited from Late Latin hospitāle 'guesthouse', built on hospes 'guest, host' (EtymDB).

Lesson 2 of 5.
l'agriturismo — the farm stay, the farmhouse holiday.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for the window, then the Italian for the hostel.

You'll want to know: l'agriturismo.
l'agriturismo — "the farm stay, the farmhouse holiday".
Abbiamo mangiato benissimo in un agriturismo. We ate very well at a farm stay.
A working farm that offers rooms and meals, often with its own produce.

The word, taken apart.
agriturismo is built from agri-, from Latin ager, agri 'field' (Lewis & Short), and turismo, which was borrowed from English tourism (EtymDB).

You'll want to know: la locanda.
la locanda — "the inn".
La locanda è sopra un vecchio ristorante. The inn is above an old restaurant.
A small, old-fashioned place to eat and sleep.

The word, taken apart.
locanda comes from Latin locanda '(a house) to be let' (EtymDB), a form of locāre 'to place, to let', built on locus 'place' (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 4 of 5.
la pensione — the guesthouse; the pension.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for the farm stay, then the Italian for the inn.

You'll want to know: la pensione.
la pensione — "the guesthouse; the pension".
La pensione è a due passi dal mare. The guesthouse is a stone's throw from the sea.
Mezza pensione — half board. It also means a retirement pension: andare in pensione.

The word, taken apart.
pensione is a learned borrowing from Latin pēnsiō 'payment' (Lewis & Short), from pendere 'to weigh out, to pay': first the payment, then the house you paid for.
