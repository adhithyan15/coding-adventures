## You'll want to know: ਸਹੀ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C254-FORM254-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ਸਹੀ** — *sahī* — "correct".

> ਕੀ ਇਹ ਤਾਰੀਖ਼ ਸਹੀ ਹੈ?
>
> *kī eh tārīkh sahī hai?*
>
> Is this date correct?

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਸਹੀ** (*sahī*) is a loan, usually traced through Persian to Arabic *sahīh*, "sound, right".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for reading notices and messages.
