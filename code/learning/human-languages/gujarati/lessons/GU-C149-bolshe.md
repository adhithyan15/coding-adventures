# તે બોલશે — the future for everyone else

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-GRAMMAR-C149-ISH-01, GU-GRAMMAR-C149-ISHUN-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say *I will speak Gujarati*, then *we'll meet tomorrow*.

## Grammar Lens: -શે
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-GRAMMAR-C149-SHE-01]; assesses=[GU-PRON-TE-01, GU-LEX-C145-NEG145-01, GU-LEX-AAVVU, GU-GRAMMAR-C147-E-TIME-01] -->

> **તે આવતીકાલે આવશે.** — *te āvtīkāle āvśe.* — He will come tomorrow. She will come tomorrow.
> **તેઓ ગુજરાતી બોલશે.** — *teo gujarātī bolśe.* — They will speak Gujarati.

**-શે** sits on the bare stem, with no **-ી-** before it: **બોલ-શે**, **આવ-શે**.

## You'll want to know: -શો
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-GRAMMAR-C149-SHO-01]; assesses=[GU-GRAMMAR-C145-PRESENT-O-01, GU-Q-KYAARE-01] -->

**તમે** keeps the **-ો** it has in the present, **બોલો છો**:

> **તમે ક્યારે આવશો?** — *tame kyāre āvśo?* — When will you come?

The future for one person:

| who | will speak |
|---|---|
| હું, તું | બોલીશ |
| તે | બોલશે |

And for more than one, or for respect:

| who | will speak |
|---|---|
| અમે, આપણે | બોલીશું |
| તમે | બોલશો |
| તેઓ | બોલશે |
