## You'll want to know: યોજવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C528-YOJVU528-05]; assesses=[] -->

**યોજવું** — *yojvũ* — "to organise (an event)".

> જો વરસાદ નહીં પડે તો અમે બગીચામાં મેળો યોજીશું.
>
> *jo varsād nahī̃ paḍe to ame bagīcāmā̃ meḷo yojīśũ.*
>
> If it doesn't rain, we will hold a fair in the garden.

Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે મેળો યોજ્યો, *teṇe meḷo yojyo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**યોજવું** (*yojvũ*) is a learned verb built on Sanskrit *yojana*, "joining, arranging" (Monier-Williams), the word behind **યોજના**, a plan.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
