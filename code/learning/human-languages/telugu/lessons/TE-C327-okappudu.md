# ఒకప్పుడు (okappuḍu) — at one time; once

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Telugu for to steal, then the Telugu for to run away.

## You'll want to know: ఒకప్పుడు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C327-RAJYAM327-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ఒకప్పుడు** — *okappuḍu* — "at one time; once".

> ఒకప్పుడు ఇక్కడ ఒక పెద్ద అడవి ఉండేది.
>
> *okappuḍu ikkaḍa oka pedda aḍavi uṇḍēdi.*
>
> At one time there was a big forest here.

Stories often open with ఒకప్పుడు, the way English opens with once upon a time: ఒక, one, and అప్పుడు, then.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ఒకప్పుడు** (*okappuḍu*) is a native Telugu compound: *oka*, "one" (DEDR 990), and *appuḍu*, "at that time" (DEDR 4559).
