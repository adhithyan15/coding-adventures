Russian, chapter 431: Too hot, too cold.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
кондиционер (konditsionér) — an air conditioner; a hair conditioner.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a chambermaid, then the Russian for to make up.

You'll want to know: кондиционер.
кондиционер (konditsionér) — "an air conditioner; a hair conditioner".
В жаркие дни кондиционер работает весь день. V zhárkiye dni konditsionér rabótayet ves den. On hot days the air conditioner runs all day.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
кондиционер (konditsionér) was borrowed from English air conditioner (EtymDB), keeping the second word.

Lesson 2 of 5.
обогреватель (obogrevátel) — a heater.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to make up, then the Russian for an air conditioner.

You'll want to know: обогреватель.
обогреватель (obogrevátel) — "a heater".
Ночью в доме было холодно, поэтому мы включили обогреватель. Nóchyu v dóme býlo khólodno, poétomu my vklyuchíli obogrevátel. At night the house was cold, so we switched on the heater.
Masculine, though it ends in -ь.

The word, taken apart.
обогреватель (obogrevátel) is built on обогревать 'to warm up' with -тель, the thing that does it. Греть is inherited from Proto-Slavic grěti 'to heat, to warm' (EtymDB).

Lesson 3 of 5.
сквозняк (skvoznyák) — a draught (of air).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for an air conditioner, then the Russian for a heater.

You'll want to know: сквозняк.
сквозняк (skvoznyák) — "a draught (of air)".
Из окна дует, в комнате сквозняк. Iz okná dúyet, v kómnate skvoznyák. Air is coming in through the window; there is a draught in the room.
Masculine. Built on сквозь — through.

The word, taken apart.
сквозняк (skvoznyák) is built on сквозь 'through' with -няк: air that blows right through a room. EtymDB lists the word without a deeper source.

Lesson 4 of 5.
проветривать (provétrivat) — to air (a room).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a heater, then the Russian for a draught.

You'll want to know: проветривать.
проветривать (provétrivat) — "to air (a room)".
Каждое утро мама проветривает комнату. Kázhdoye útro máma provétrivayet kómnatu. Every morning Mum airs the room.
Imperfective; perfective проветрить. Built on ветер — wind.

The word, taken apart.
проветривать (provétrivat) is built from про- 'through' and ветр-, the root of ветер 'wind': to let the wind through. EtymDB does not list the verb.

Lesson 5 of 5.
сушить (sushít) — to dry.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a draught, then the Russian for to air.

You'll want to know: сушить.
сушить (sushít) — "to dry".
Мы сушим мокрые вещи на балконе. My súshim mókryye véshchi na balkóne. We dry the wet things on the balcony.
Imperfective; perfective высушить. Present сушу, сушишь. Сушить волосы — to dry one's hair.

The word, taken apart.
сушить (sushít) is inherited from Proto-Slavic sušiti (EtymDB), the 'make dry' verb of сухой 'dry'.
