## You'll want to know: ひろがる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C265-KEMURI265-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ひろがる** — *hirogaru* — "to spread; to widen".

> **かぜが あったら、ひが ひろがるでしょう。** — *kaze ga attara, hi ga hirogaru deshō* — "If there were a wind, the fire would spread."

Polite **ひろがります**, *hirogarimasu*; a godan verb. Past (polite): **ひが ひろがりました。**, *hi ga hirogarimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ひろがる** (*hirogaru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict). It is the intransitive partner of *hirogeru*, "to spread out".
