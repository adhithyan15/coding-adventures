Lesson 1 of 5.
زرنگ (zerang) — clever.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for old, then the Persian for lazy.

You'll want to know: زرنگ.
زرنگ — zerang — "clever".

The word, taken apart.
zerang is a native Persian word, but no source used here gives its older form, and its origin is not settled.

Lesson 2 of 5.
ترسو (tarsu) — timid, cowardly.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for lazy, then the Persian for clever.

You'll want to know: ترسو.
ترسو — tarsu — "timid, cowardly".

The word, taken apart.
tarsu is tars, "fear", with the ending -u, "given to": "fearful". tars belongs with tarsidan, "to fear", an inherited verb usually traced to Middle Persian tarsīdan.

Lesson 4 of 5.
خطرناک (khatarnâk) — dangerous.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for timid, then the Persian for famous.

You'll want to know: خطرناک.
خطرناک — khatarnâk — "dangerous".

The word, taken apart.
khatarnâk is khatar, "danger", with the native ending -nâk, "full of". khatar is borrowed from Arabic ḫaṭar, "danger, risk" (root ḫ-ṭ-r, Lane).
