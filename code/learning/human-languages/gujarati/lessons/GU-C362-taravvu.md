## You'll want to know: તારવવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C362-SANSHODHAN362-05]; assesses=[] -->

**તારવવું** — *tāravvũ* — "to deduce".

> આ પ્રયોગમાંથી મીરાએ એક વાત તારવી.
>
> *ā prayogmā̃thī Mīrāe ek vāt tārvī.*
>
> From this experiment Mira drew one conclusion.

Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે સાચો અર્થ તારવ્યો, *teṇe sāco arth tāravyo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**તારવવું** (*tāravvũ*) is inherited from Sanskrit *tāráyati*, "causes to arrive at, leads across"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb, "to raise, to pluck" (CDIAL 5796). "To deduce", to pluck a conclusion out of the facts, is not among CDIAL's senses.
