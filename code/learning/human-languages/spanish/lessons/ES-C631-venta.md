## You'll want to know: la venta
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C631-REBAJAS-03]; assesses=[] -->

**la venta** — "the sale".

> La casa está en venta.
>
> *The house is for sale.*

*En venta* — for sale.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*venta* is inherited from Latin *vendita* 'sold', the participle of *vendere* 'to sell' (EtymDB).
