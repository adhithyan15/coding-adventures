## You'll want to know: قسمت
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C532-QISMAT532-01]; assesses=[] -->

**قسمت** — *qismat* — "luck; fortune".

> اگر قسمت اچھی ہوئی تو ہم میچ جیتیں گے۔
>
> *agar qismat achchhī huī to ham maich jīteṅ ge.*
>
> If our luck is good, we will win the match.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**قسمت** (*qismat*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *qisma*, "a share, a portion": the share life deals one.
