## You'll want to know: सुळका
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C402-GHAT402-05]; assesses=[] -->

**सुळका** — *suḷkā* — "a rock pinnacle".

> डोंगरावर एक उंच सुळका दिसतो.
>
> *ḍoṅgarāvar ek uñc suḷkā disto.*
>
> A tall rock pinnacle stands out on the hill.

Masculine.

A tall, thin peak of bare rock, loved by rock climbers.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सुळका** (*suḷkā*) is a Marathi word for a tall spike of rock; its origin is not settled.
