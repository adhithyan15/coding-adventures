## You'll want to know: a fase
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C273-ETAPA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**a fase** — "the phase".

> O meu filho está numa fase difícil.
>
> *My son is going through a difficult phase.*

*As fases da lua* — the phases of the moon.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*fase* comes from Greek *phásis* "an appearance" (EtymDB), first said of the moon as it shows a different face.
