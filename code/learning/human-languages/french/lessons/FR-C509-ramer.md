## You'll want to know: ramer
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C509-EAU-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ramer** — "to row".

> Nous avons ramé jusqu'à l'île.
>
> *We rowed to the island.*

Informally, to struggle: *j'ai ramé pour trouver un appartement*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ramer* is linked to *rame* "oar". EtymDB traces it to Old French *raim* "branch", from Latin *rāmus* "a branch"; Latin *rēmus* "an oar" (Lewis & Short) probably shaped the sense, and the origin is not fully settled.
