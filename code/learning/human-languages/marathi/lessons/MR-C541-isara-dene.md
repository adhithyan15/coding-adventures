# इशारा देणे (iśārā deṇe) — to issue a warning

## You'll want to know: इशारा देणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C541-SAVADH541-05]; assesses=[] -->

**इशारा देणे** — *iśārā deṇe* — "to issue a warning".

> हवामान विभागाने मोठ्या पावसाचा इशारा दिला.
>
> *havāmān vibhāgāne moṭhyā pāvsācā iśārā dilā.*
>
> The weather department warned of heavy rain.

Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने इशारा दिला, *tyāne iśārā dilā*.

The danger takes -चा: पावसाचा इशारा देणे.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**इशारा देणे** (*iśārā deṇe*) is इशारा, a loan from Persian *išāra*, "a sign, a hint", usually traced to Arabic, with देणे.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
