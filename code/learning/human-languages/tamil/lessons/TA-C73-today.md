## You'll want to know: இன்று
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C73-WHEN-01]; assesses=[] -->

**இன்று** (*iṉṟu*) — "today".

"Today".

You already have நாள் (*nāḷ*), the day itself, and நாளை (*nāḷai*), tomorrow. இன்று is the day
you are standing in, and it completes the middle of that little set.

This is the careful, written form, and it is the one printed on a page. In fast speech you will
also hear இன்னைக்கு (*iṉṉaikku*) for the same thing — worth recognising, but இன்று is the one
to say.
