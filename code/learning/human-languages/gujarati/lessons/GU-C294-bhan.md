## You'll want to know: ભાન
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C294-LAPAS294-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ભાન** — *bhān* — "consciousness".

> અડધા કલાક પછી તે ભાનમાં આવ્યો.
>
> *aḍdhā kalāk pachī te bhānmā̃ āvyo.*
>
> Half an hour later he came round.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ભાન** (*bhān*) is Sanskrit *bhāna*, "appearance, perception" (Monier-Williams): in Gujarati, being aware of oneself and the world. **ભાનમાં આવ્યો**, he came round; **બેભાન** is its opposite.
