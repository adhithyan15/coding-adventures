## You'll want to know: ह्रसति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C378-LESHA378-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ह्रसति** — *hrasati* — "dwindles; grows less".

> यदि वर्षा न भवति, तर्हि नद्याः जलं ह्रसति।
>
> *yadi varṣā na bhavati, tarhi nadyāḥ jalaṃ hrasati.*
>
> If it does not rain, the river's water dwindles.

Past (it …): जलं ह्रसितवत्, *jalaṃ hrasitavat*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ह्रसति** (*hrasati*) comes from the root √hras, "to become short or small, to be diminished"; MW gives *hrasita* "shortened, curtailed, diminished" (MW).
