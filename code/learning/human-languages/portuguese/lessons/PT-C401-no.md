## You'll want to know: o nó
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C401-AUTOESTRADA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**o nó** — "the junction (of motorways); the knot".

> Saímos da autoestrada no próximo nó.
>
> *We leave the motorway at the next junction.*

Also a knot in a rope or a tie: *dar um nó*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*nó* is inherited from Latin *nōdus* 'knot' (EtymDB): a junction is where roads are tied together.
