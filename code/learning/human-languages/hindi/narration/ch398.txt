Hindi, chapter 398: Passengers and Porters.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
मुसाफ़िर (musāfir) — a traveller; a passenger.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for turning over (in bed), then the Hindi for a stretch (of the body).

You'll want to know: मुसाफ़िर.
मुसाफ़िर — musāfir — "a traveller; a passenger".
ट्रेन में बहुत मुसाफ़िर थे। ṭren meṁ bahut musāfir the. There were a lot of passengers on the train.
Masculine or feminine, by the person meant.

The word, taken apart.
मुसाफ़िर (musāfir) is usually traced to Arabic musāfir, "one who travels", which reached Hindi through Persian; on a train or a bus it is the everyday word for a passenger.

Lesson 2 of 5.
कुली (kulī) — a porter.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a stretch (of the body), then the Hindi for a traveller.

You'll want to know: कुली.
कुली — kulī — "a porter".
कुली ने मेरा सामान उठाया। kulī ne merā sāmān uṭhāyā. The porter picked up my luggage.
Masculine.
Station porters wear a red shirt and a numbered brass badge, so you can find the same one again.

The word, taken apart.
कुली (kulī) is often traced to Tamil kūli, "wages, hire" (DEDR 1905): the porter is the man you pay by the load. Other sources have been proposed, so the link is probable rather than certain.

Lesson 3 of 5.
ढोना (ḍhonā) — to haul; to carry (a heavy load).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a traveller, then the Hindi for a porter.

You'll want to know: ढोना.
ढोना — ḍhonā — "to haul; to carry (a heavy load)".
कुली दिन भर भारी बोरियाँ ढोते हैं। kulī din bhar bhārī boriyā̃ ḍhote haiṁ. Porters haul heavy sacks all day long.
Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने सामान ढोया, usne sāmān ḍhoyā.

The word, taken apart.
ढोना (ḍhonā) is a tadbhava of Sanskrit ḍhaukayati, "brings near" (CDIAL 5610), which lists Hindi ḍhonā, "to convey". Bringing a thing near became carrying it, load after load.

Lesson 4 of 5.
लादना (lādnā) — to load (onto).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a porter, then the Hindi for to haul.

You'll want to know: लादना.
लादना — lādnā — "to load (onto)".
मज़दूरों ने ट्रक पर सामान लादा। mazdūroṁ ne ṭrak par sāmān lādā. The labourers loaded the goods onto the lorry.
Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने गाड़ी पर बोरियाँ लादीं, usne gāṛī par boriyā̃ lādīṁ.

The word, taken apart.
लादना (lādnā) is a tadbhava of Sanskrit lardayati, "loads" (CDIAL 10966); the r has dropped out and the vowel has lengthened in its place.

Lesson 5 of 5.
बिठाना (biṭhānā) — to seat (someone); to make (someone) sit.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for to haul, then the Hindi for to load (onto).

You'll want to know: बिठाना.
बिठाना — biṭhānā — "to seat (someone); to make (someone) sit".
कुली ने दादी को सीट पर बिठाया। kulī ne dādī ko sīṭ par biṭhāyā. The porter sat my grandmother down in a seat.
Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने मेहमान को कुर्सी पर बिठाया, usne mehmān ko kursī par biṭhāyā.

The word, taken apart.
बिठाना (biṭhānā) is the causative partner of बैठना, "to sit", which goes back to Sanskrit upaviśati, "sits down" (CDIAL 2245): you बैठना yourself, and you बिठाना someone else.
