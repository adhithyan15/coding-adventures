## You'll want to know: zhīdào
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C62-FAST62-05]; assesses=[] -->

*zhīdào* — "to know a fact; to be aware".

> *wǒ zhīdào nǐ xiǎng shuō shénme* — I know what you want to say.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*zhīdào* is built from zhī 'to perceive' and dào 'the way', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *trje* and *dawX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription). How the pair came to mean knowing a fact is not settled.
