## You'll want to know: فنان
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C541-FANN541-01]; assesses=[] -->

**فنان** — *fannān* — "an artist".

> رسم الفنان صورة لجدتي.
>
> *rasama al-fannān ṣūra li-jaddatī.*
>
> The artist painted a picture of my grandmother.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**فنان** (*fannān*) is built on the native Arabic root *f-n-n*, "a sort, a kind; a branch" (Lane). Fann, an art, which you know, is from the same root.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
