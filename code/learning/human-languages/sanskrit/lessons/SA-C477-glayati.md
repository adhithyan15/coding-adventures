## You'll want to know: ग्लायति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C477-BHRAMA477-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ग्लायति** — *glāyati* — "grows weary".

> उष्णेन वृद्धः ग्लायति।
>
> *uṣṇena vṛddhaḥ glāyati.*
>
> The old man grows faint in the heat.

Past (he …): सः ग्लानवान्, *saḥ glānavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ग्लायति** (*glāyati*) comes from the root √glai, "to be wearied, to feel faint"; MW's ppp is *glāna* (MW).
