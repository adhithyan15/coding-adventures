## You'll want to know: くふうする
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C481-SKILL481-05]; assesses=[] -->

**くふうする** — *kufū suru* — "to devise; to find a better way".

> **ははは りょうりを くふうします。** — *haha wa ryōri o kufūshimasu* — "My mother keeps finding better ways to cook."

Polite **くふうします**, *kufūshimasu*; a する verb. Past (polite): **ははが りょうりを くふうしました。**, *haha ga ryōri o kufūshimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**くふうする** (*kufū suru*) is the Sino-Japanese (kango) noun *kufū* with the native verb *suru*, "to do": two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *ku*, "craft" (Middle Chinese *kuwng*) and *fū*, "man" (*pju*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
