## You'll want to know: こしょう
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C395-KOSHOU395-01]; assesses=[] -->

**こしょう** — *koshō* — "a breakdown; a fault".

> **くるまが こしょうしました。** — *kuruma ga koshō shimashita* — "The car broke down."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**こしょう** (*koshō*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *ko*, "circumstances" (Middle Chinese *kuH*) and *shō*, "hinder" (*tsyangH*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
