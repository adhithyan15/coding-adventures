## You'll want to know: نشرة
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C296-SAHAFA296-05]; assesses=[] -->

**نشرة** — *nashra* — "a bulletin, a news broadcast".

> شاهد أبي نشرة الأخبار في المساء.
>
> *shāhada abī nashrat al-akhbār fī al-masāʾ.*
>
> My father watched the news bulletin in the evening.

نشرة الأخبار is the news on radio or television.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**نشرة** (*nashra*) is built on the native Arabic root *n-sh-r* (Lane), "to spread out, to unfold", the root of the taught *nashara*, to publish. A *nashra* is what is spread among people.
