## You'll want to know: sufflāmen, sufflāminis
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C430-TEMO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**sufflāmen, sufflāminis** — "a drag shoe, a brake (for a wheel)".

> In clīvō raedārius sufflāmen sub rotā pōnit.
>
> *On the slope the coachman puts the drag shoe under the wheel.*

Third declension, neuter: *sufflāmen, sufflāminis n.* A block or chain that stops a wheel turning on a steep road. Also anything that holds a thing back.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

The origin of *sufflāmen* is not settled. Lewis & Short file it with *sufflō* 'to blow up' without further comment, while EtymDB lists Greek *phalanx* 'log, beam' as a cousin, which points to a word for a wooden block.
