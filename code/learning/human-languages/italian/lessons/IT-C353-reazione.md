## You'll want to know: la reazione
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C353-REAZIONE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**la reazione** — "the reaction".

> La sua reazione mi ha sorpreso.
>
> *Her reaction surprised me.*

*Una reazione forte* — a strong reaction.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*reazione* is built from *re-* "back" and *azione* "action", a learned borrowing from Latin *āctiō* "a doing", from *agere* "to do, to drive" (Lewis & Short).
