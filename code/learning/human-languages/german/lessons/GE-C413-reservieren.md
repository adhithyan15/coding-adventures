## You'll want to know: reservieren
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C413-NACHTZUG-04]; assesses=[] -->

**reservieren** — "to book, to reserve".

> Für die Nacht reserviert er ein Bett im Schlafwagen.
>
> *For the night he books a bed in the sleeping car.*

Past participle without *ge-*: *ich habe reserviert*. *Die Reservierung* — the reservation.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*reservieren* is a learned borrowing from Latin *reservāre* 'to keep back, to save up' (Lewis & Short), probably through French *réserver*.
