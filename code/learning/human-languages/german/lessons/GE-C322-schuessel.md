## You'll want to know: die Schüssel
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C322-ZUTAT-05]; assesses=[] -->

**die Schüssel** — "the bowl".

> Er gab die Eier in eine große Schüssel.
>
> *He put the eggs into a big bowl.*

Plural: *die Schüsseln*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Schüssel* comes from Middle High German *schüzzel* (EtymDB), usually explained as an early loan from Latin *scutella* "a small flat dish, a tray" (EtymDB).
