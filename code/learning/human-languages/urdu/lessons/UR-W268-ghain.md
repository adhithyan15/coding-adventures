# غ (ghain) — the letter that opens غلط

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[UR-SCRIPT-C267-AIN-01, UR-LEX-C268-GHALAT-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: write ع once, and name it. (*ain*.)

[PAUSE 2s] Now say **غلط** (*ghalat*), "wrong". This lesson takes one letter out of it and puts it in your hand.

## Script you'll notice: غ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-SCRIPT-C268-GHAIN-01]; assesses=[] -->

**غ** — *ghain*, the sound *gh*, made far back in the throat.

It opens **غلط** (*ghalat*). You already write **ع**: **غ** is that shape with **one dot** above it. At the start of a word, as here, it is the small open head alone, joined on to the letter after it; standing alone it keeps the deep bowl below the line.

## Writing: غ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[UR-SCRIPT-C268-GHAIN-01] -->

Put your pen on غ and follow its line. Copy the shape you can see — slowly, and larger than it is printed.

> This book does not yet tell you **where to start this letter or which way to
> travel**. It is not written down here until it can be written down with a
> source. Copying what is in front of you needs no such source, and it is how the
> shape gets into your hand in the meantime.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[UR-SCRIPT-C267-AIN-01, UR-SCRIPT-C268-GHAIN-01] -->

[PAUSE 1s]
- [YOU LOOK: at **غلط** and point at غ inside it]
- [YOU TRACE: غ three times, saying *gh* as you finish each one]
- [YOU WRITE: **غلط** once, joined, with the dot above its first letter]
- [YOU READ: **غلط** aloud once more]

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[UR-SCRIPT-C268-GHAIN-01] -->

[PAUSE 3s] Which letter is this — غ? (**ghain**, *gh*.) What tells it from ع? (The dot.) Which word did it come from? (**غلط**, *ghalat*, "wrong".)
