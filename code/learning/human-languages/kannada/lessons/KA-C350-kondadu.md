# ಕೊಂಡಾಡು (koṇḍāḍu) — to extol; to praise highly

## You'll want to know: ಕೊಂಡಾಡು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C350-SANMANA350-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ಕೊಂಡಾಡು** — *koṇḍāḍu* — "to extol; to praise highly".

> ಜನರು ಆ ಶಿಕ್ಷಕರ ಸೇವೆಯನ್ನು ಕೊಂಡಾಡಿದರು.
>
> *janaru ā śikṣakara sēveyannu koṇḍāḍidaru.*
>
> People praised that teacher's service.

Past (I …): ಕೊಂಡಾಡಿದೆ, *koṇḍāḍide*.

Stronger and more public than plain praise: to sing someone's praises.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಕೊಂಡಾಡು** (*koṇḍāḍu*) is usually explained as a native compound of ಕೊಂಡು, from *koḷ*, "to take, to accept" (DEDR 2151), and *āḍu*, "to act, to speak" (DEDR 347).
