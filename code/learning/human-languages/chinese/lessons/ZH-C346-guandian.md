## You'll want to know: guāndiǎn
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C346-THINK346-02]; assesses=[] -->

*guāndiǎn* — "point of view; standpoint".

> *wǒ juéde zhège guāndiǎn hěn yǒu yìsi* — I think that's an interesting point of view.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*guāndiǎn* is built from *guān* and 点 'a point', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *kwan* and *temX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to view' and the second 'a dot'.
