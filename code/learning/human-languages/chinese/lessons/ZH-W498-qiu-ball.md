## Script — the jade piece, then seven strokes
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-SCRIPT-QIU-BALL-01]; assesses=[] -->

> 球

**Two pieces, left before right.**

**The left piece, four strokes**, as on the left of 理: two bars, a vertical through them, then a stroke rising to the right.

**The right piece, seven strokes.** A bar. A long vertical down through it that hooks at the foot. On the left, a dot and a stroke rising to the right. On the right, a short stroke falling to the left and a long stroke falling to the right. Last, the small dot at the top right.

4 + 7 = eleven.
