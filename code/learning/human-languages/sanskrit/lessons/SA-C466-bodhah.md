## You'll want to know: बोधः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C466-MEDHA466-05]; assesses=[] -->

**बोधः** — *bodhaḥ* — "understanding".

> अनुभवेन बोधः वर्धते।
>
> *anubhavena bodhaḥ vardhate.*
>
> Understanding grows with experience.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**बोधः** (*bodhaḥ*) comes from the root √budh, "to wake, to know", the root of **बुद्धिः** and **बुध्यते**; MW gives "knowing, understanding" and "waking" (MW).
