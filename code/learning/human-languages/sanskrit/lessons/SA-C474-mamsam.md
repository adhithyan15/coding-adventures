## You'll want to know: मांसम्
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C474-SIRA474-04]; assesses=[] -->

**मांसम्** — *māṃsam* — "flesh".

> सिंहः मांसं खादति।
>
> *siṃhaḥ māṃsaṃ khādati.*
>
> A lion eats flesh.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**मांसम्** (*māṃsam*) MW gives "flesh, meat", from the Rigveda on; MW gives no derivation, so its origin is uncertain (MW).
