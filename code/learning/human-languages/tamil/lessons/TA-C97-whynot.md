## You'll want to know: ஏன் இல்லை?
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C97-SURE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ஏன் இல்லை?** (*ēṉ illai?*) — "why not?".

**ஏன்** is "why"; with **இல்லை**, "why not?" — as a question, or as a cheerful yes.
