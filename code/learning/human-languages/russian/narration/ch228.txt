Russian, chapter 228: Mistake, Rule, Example, Meaning, First name.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ошибка (oshíbka) — a mistake.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a woman, then the Russian for a boy.

You'll want to know: ошибка.
ошибка (oshíbka) — "a mistake".

The word, taken apart.
ошибка (oshíbka) comes from ошибиться, "to make a mistake", first "to miss, to strike past the target", from шибать, "to strike" (Vasmer).

The word, taken apart.
пример (primér) is при-, "by, against", with мер, from мера, "a measure": something to measure against. Vasmer relates мера to Latin mētior, "I measure".

Lesson 5 of 5.
имя (ímya) — a first name.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for an example, then the Russian for a meaning.

You'll want to know: имя.
имя (ímya) — "a first name".

The word, taken apart.
имя (ímya) from Proto-Slavic jĭmę — inherited. Vasmer lists Old Prussian emmens, Latin nōmen, Gothic namō and Old High German -nuomen: English name is a cousin, from PIE h₁nómṇ.
