Urdu, chapter 345: What We Value.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
قدر (qadr) — worth; esteem.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for oppression, then the Urdu for to provoke.

You'll want to know: قدر.
قدر — qadr — "worth; esteem".
وہ پیسے کی قدر جانتا ہے۔ voh paise kī qadr jāntā hai. He knows the value of money.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
قدر (qadr) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic qadr, "measure, worth".

What you've built.
One more word for giving reasons and opinions.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: qadr]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: qadr, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: voh paise kī qadr jāntā hai. — He knows the value of money.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Urdu for oppression, then the Urdu for to provoke, then say qadr again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does قدر (qadr) mean? ("Worth; esteem".) Say it once more.
