5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
লোভ (lobh) — greed.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a need, then the Bengali for a necessity.

You'll want to know: লোভ.
লোভ — lobh — "greed".

The word, taken apart.
লোভ (lobh) is Sanskrit lobha, "eager desire, greed", taken over whole as a learned loan, a tatsama.

Lesson 3 of 5.
শখ (shôkh) — a hobby.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for greed, then the Bengali for a liking.

You'll want to know: শখ.
শখ — shôkh — "a hobby".

The word, taken apart.
শখ (shôkh) is a loan from Arabic shawq, "longing, desire", which reached Bengali through Persian. Its Arabic root is sh-w-q (Lane). In Bengali it has come to mean "a hobby".
