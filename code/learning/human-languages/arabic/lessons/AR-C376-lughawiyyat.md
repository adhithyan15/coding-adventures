## You'll want to know: لغويات
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C376-ILM376-02]; assesses=[] -->

**لغويات** — *lughawiyyāt* — "linguistics".

> تخصصت أختي في اللغويات.
>
> *takhaṣṣaṣat ukhtī fī al-lughawiyyāt.*
>
> My sister specialized in linguistics.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**لغويات** (*lughawiyyāt*) is a modern plural built on *lugha*, "a language", which Lane files under the native Arabic root *l-gh-w* (Lane). It names the study of language.
