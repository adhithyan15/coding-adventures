## You'll want to know: facultativo
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C381-NORMA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**facultativo** — "optional".

> A visita ao museu é facultativa.
>
> *The museum visit is optional.*

The opposite of *obrigatório*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*facultativo* is built on *faculdade* in its sense "power, permission", from Latin *facultās* "capability, means" (Lewis & Short), with the learned suffix *-ivo*.
