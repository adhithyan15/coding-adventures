## Script — the shape
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-SCRIPT-YA-01]; assesses=[JA-SCRIPT-YO-01] -->

> **や** — *ya*

Three strokes, and the order is the surprise. KanjiVG has them as: **first** the
long sweep, starting low on the left and running up and away to the right;
**then** a short tick at the top; **then** the long stroke that comes down
through the middle and out to the bottom right.

Look at the finished shape and you would guess the descender was drawn first. It
is not. Draw the sweep, then the tick, then the descender.

**よ** is already yours and shares the same first sound. It is built the other
way round — the crossing stroke comes down first there.
