## You'll want to know: वारसा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C308-PIDHI308-03]; assesses=[] -->

**वारसा** — *vārsā* — "heritage; an inheritance".

> हा किल्ला आपला ऐतिहासिक वारसा आहे.
>
> *hā killā āplā aitihāsik vārsā āhe.*
>
> This fort is our historical heritage.

Masculine.

वारसा जपणे, to cherish a heritage.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**वारसा** (*vārsā*) is built on *vāras*, 'an heir', a loan from Persian *vāris*, usually traced to Arabic *wārith*, 'an heir'.
