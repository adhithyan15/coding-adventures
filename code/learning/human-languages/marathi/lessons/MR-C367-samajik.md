## You'll want to know: सामाजिक
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C367-PRATHA367-05]; assesses=[] -->

**सामाजिक** — *sāmājik* — "social".

> ती अनेक सामाजिक कामांत भाग घेते.
>
> *tī anek sāmājik kāmānt bhāg ghete.*
>
> She takes part in many social causes.

Invariable; built on समाज, society.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सामाजिक** (*sāmājik*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *sāmājika*, "relating to an assembly" (Monier-Williams). Marathi builds it on समाज, society.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
