## You'll want to know: はんにん
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C270-HANNIN270-01]; assesses=[] -->

**はんにん** — *hannin* — "a culprit; the criminal".

> **はんにんが にげた とき、月が ひかっていました。** — *hannin ga nigeta toki, tsuki ga hikatte imashita* — "When the culprit got away, the moon was shining."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**はんにん** (*hannin*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *han*, "crime" (Middle Chinese *bjomX*) and *nin*, "person" (*nyin*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
