## You'll want to know: நிரல்
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C518-NIRAL518-01]; assesses=[] -->

**நிரல்** — *niral* — "a computer program".

> என் தம்பி கணினியில் ஒரு சிறிய நிரல் எழுதினான்.
>
> *eṉ tampi kaṇiṉiyil oru ciṟiya niral eḻutiṉāṉ.*
>
> My younger brother wrote a small program on the computer.

The one who writes them is a நிரலாளர், a programmer.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**நிரல்** (*niral*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 3673 gives நிரல் (*niral*), "to be placed in a row, in order"; a program is a row of steps in order.
