Marathi, chapter 518: Machines and Devices.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
यंत्र (yantra) — a machine.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a link, then the Marathi for to store.

You'll want to know: यंत्र.
यंत्र — yantra — "a machine".
हे यंत्र विजेवर चालतं. he yantra vijevar cāltaṁ. This machine runs on electricity.
Neuter.

The word, taken apart.
यंत्र (yantra) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit yantra, "an instrument, a machine" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 5 of 5.
फवारणे (phavārṇe) — to spray.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a robot, then the Marathi for automatic.

You'll want to know: फवारणे.
फवारणे — phavārṇe — "to spray".
यंत्राने शेतावर औषध फवारलं. yantrāne śetāvar auṣadh phavārlaṁ. The machine sprayed the field with pesticide.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने औषध फवारलं, tyāne auṣadh phavārlaṁ.
The noun is फवारा, a spray or a fountain.

The word, taken apart.
फवारणे (phavārṇe) is built on फवारा, "a spray, a fountain", a loan through Persian usually traced to Arabic fawwāra, "fountain".
