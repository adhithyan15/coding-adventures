Kannada, chapter 407: Doing Research.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಸಂಶೋಧನೆ (saṃśōdhane) — research.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a professor, then the Kannada for to instruct.

You'll want to know: ಸಂಶೋಧನೆ.
ಸಂಶೋಧನೆ — saṃśōdhane — "research".
ಅವಳು ಕಾಡಿನ ಹಕ್ಕಿಗಳ ಬಗ್ಗೆ ಸಂಶೋಧನೆ ಮಾಡುತ್ತಾಳೆ. avaḷu kāḍina hakkigaḷa bagge saṃśōdhane māḍuttāḷe. She does research on forest birds.
ಸಂಶೋಧಕ, a researcher.

The word, taken apart.
ಸಂಶೋಧನೆ (saṃśōdhane) is borrowed from Sanskrit saṃśodhana, "completely purifying, correcting" (Monier-Williams).

ನಾನು ಪರೀಕ್ಷೆಯ ಫಲಿತಾಂಶಗಳನ್ನು ವಿಶ್ಲೇಷಿಸಿದೆ. nānu parīkṣeya phalitāṃśagaḷannu viślēṣiside. I analysed the exam results.
Past (I …): ವಿಶ್ಲೇಷಿಸಿದೆ, viślēṣiside.
ವಿಶ್ಲೇಷಣೆ, an analysis.

The word, taken apart.
ವಿಶ್ಲೇಷಿಸು (viślēṣisu) is built on Sanskrit viśleṣa, "loosening, separation" (Monier-Williams), with the Kannada verb ending -isu: to take apart.
