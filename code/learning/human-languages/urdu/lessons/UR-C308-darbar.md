## You'll want to know: دربار
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C308-SHAHENSHAH308-04]; assesses=[] -->

**دربار** — *darbār* — "a royal court".

> دربار میں سب خاموش تھے۔
>
> *darbār meṅ sab khāmosh the.*
>
> Everyone at court was silent.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**دربار** (*darbār*) is usually traced to Persian *darbār*, "a royal court": the hall where the king sat and heard his people.
