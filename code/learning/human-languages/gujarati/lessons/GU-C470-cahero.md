## You'll want to know: ચહેરો
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C470-CAHERO470-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ચહેરો** — *cahero* — "a face".

> મીરાનો ચહેરો ખુશીથી ચમકતો હતો.
>
> *Mīrāno cahero khuśīthī camakto hato.*
>
> Mira's face was shining with happiness.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ચહેરો** (*cahero*) is usually traced to Persian *čihra*, "face, appearance".
