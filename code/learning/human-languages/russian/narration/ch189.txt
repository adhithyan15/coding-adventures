Lesson 1 of 5.
чужой (chuzhóy) — someone else's; unfamiliar.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for possible, then the Russian for complicated.

You'll want to know: чужой.
чужой (chuzhóy) — "someone else's; unfamiliar".

The word, taken apart.
чужой (chuzhóy) from Proto-Slavic tjudjĭ, Old Church Slavonic штуждь. It is usually explained as built on a loan from Gothic þiuda, "a people": "belonging to another people" (Vasmer).

Lesson 2 of 5.
родной (rodnóy) — native; dear.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for complicated, then the Russian for someone else's; unfamiliar.

You'll want to know: родной.
родной (rodnóy) — "native; dear".

The word, taken apart.
родной (rodnóy) is built on род, "kin, family", with -ной: "of one's own kin". Vasmer relates род to Latvian rads, "a relative".

The word, taken apart.
местный (méstnyy) is место, "a place", with -ный: "of the place". Vasmer relates место to Lithuanian mìsti, "to feed oneself", despite a mismatch in accent.

Lesson 4 of 5.
иностранный (inostránnyy) — foreign.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for native; dear, then the Russian for local.

You'll want to know: иностранный.
иностранный (inostránnyy) — "foreign".

The word, taken apart.
иностранный (inostránnyy) is иной, "other", with страна, "a country", the Church Slavonic shape of сторона, "side": "of another land". Vasmer confirms страна as borrowed from Church Slavonic, and relates иной to Latin ūnus, "one".

Lesson 5 of 5.
русский (rússkiy) — Russian.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for local, then the Russian for foreign.

You'll want to know: русский.
русский (rússkiy) — "Russian".

The word, taken apart.
русский (rússkiy) comes from Русь, the old name of the land and its people (Vasmer). Vasmer traces Русь to Old Norse Róþsmenn, "rowers, seafarers", the name of the Scandinavian newcomers, which reached the Slavs through the Finns, who still call Sweden Ruotsi; Trubachev's note cites critics of that account, so it is still debated.
