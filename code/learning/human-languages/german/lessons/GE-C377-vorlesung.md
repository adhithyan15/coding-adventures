## You'll want to know: die Vorlesung
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C377-HOCHSCHULE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**die Vorlesung** — "the lecture".

> Die Vorlesung beginnt um neun Uhr im großen Saal.
>
> *The lecture starts at nine in the big hall.*

Plural: *die Vorlesungen*. From *vorlesen* — to read aloud: lecturers once read from a book.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Vorlesung* is the noun of *vorlesen* "to read aloud", built on *lesen* "to read", inherited from Old High German *lesan* and Proto-Germanic *lesaną* "to gather" (EtymDB).
