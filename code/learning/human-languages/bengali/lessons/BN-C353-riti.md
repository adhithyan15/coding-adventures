## You'll want to know: রীতি
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C353-RITI353-02]; assesses=[] -->

**রীতি** — *rīti* — "a convention; a way of doing things".

> অতিথিকে চা দেওয়া আমাদের রীতি।
>
> *ôtithike chā deoyā āmāder rīti.*
>
> Offering tea to a guest is our custom.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**রীতি** (*rīti*) is Sanskrit *rīti*, a course or way and so usage and custom (Monier-Williams). It is the accepted way of doing something.
