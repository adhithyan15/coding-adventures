## You'll want to know: la parution
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C279-REDIGER-05]; assesses=[] -->

**la parution** — "the publication, the release (of a book or paper)".

> Le livre a eu du succès dès sa parution.
>
> *The book was a success as soon as it came out.*

*La date de parution* — the publication date.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*parution* is built on *paru*, the past participle of *paraître*, which comes from Latin *pārēre* "to appear" (Lewis & Short).
