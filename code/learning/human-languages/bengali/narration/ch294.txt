Bengali, chapter 294: Warnings.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
সতর্ক (sôtôrkô) — alert; on guard.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for destruction, then the Bengali for to be trapped underneath.

You'll want to know: সতর্ক.
সতর্ক — sôtôrkô — "alert; on guard".
রেডিওতে সবাইকে সতর্ক থাকতে বলল। reḍiote sôbāike sôtôrkô thākte bôllô. The radio told everyone to stay alert.

The word, taken apart.
সতর্ক (sôtôrkô) is Sanskrit satarka, 'with reasoning', and so 'cautious' (Monier-Williams): sa-, 'with', and tarka, 'reasoning' — তর্ক, in Bengali, is an argument.

সাবধান — sābdhān — "careful; watch out".
সাবধান, সিঁড়িটা ভাঙা! sābdhān, sĩṛiṭā bhāngā! Careful, the stairs are broken!

The word, taken apart.
সাবধান (sābdhān) is Sanskrit sāvadhāna, 'attentive, careful' (Monier-Williams): sa-, 'with', and avadhāna, 'attention'. Said alone it is a warning: সাবধান!

Lesson 3 of 5.
সংকেত (sôngket) — a signal; a warning sign.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for alert, then the Bengali for careful.

You'll want to know: সংকেত.
সংকেত — sôngket — "a signal; a warning sign".
বন্দরে বিপদের সংকেত দেখে জেলেরা সমুদ্রে গেল না। bôndôre bipôder sôngket dekhe jelerā sômudre gelô nā. When they saw the danger signal at the port, the fishermen didn't go to sea.

The word, taken apart.
সংকেত (sôngket) is Sanskrit saṃketa, 'an agreement, a convention' (Monier-Williams); a storm signal is a sign everyone has agreed to read the same way.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for careful, then the Bengali for a warning sign.

You'll want to know: আশঙ্কা.
আশঙ্কা — āshôngkā — "a fear; an apprehension".
আবহাওয়া খারাপ, তাই বন্যার আশঙ্কা আছে। ābôhāoyā khārāp, tāi bônyār āshôngkā āchhe. The weather is bad, so a flood is feared.

The word, taken apart.
আশঙ্কা (āshôngkā) is Sanskrit āśaṅkā, 'fear, apprehension; doubt' (Monier-Williams). It is the fear that something will happen, not fear of a thing in front of you.

Lesson 5 of 5.
এড়ানো (eṛāno) — to avoid.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a warning sign, then the Bengali for an apprehension.

You'll want to know: এড়ানো.
এড়ানো — eṛāno — "to avoid".
আমরা বড় রাস্তা এড়িয়ে গলি দিয়ে গেলাম। āmrā bôṛo rāstā eṛiye gôli diye gelām. We avoided the main road and went through the lanes.
Past (he/she …): তিনি বিপদ এড়ালেন, tini bipôd eṛālen.

The word, taken apart.
এড়ানো (eṛāno) is filed in CDIAL 2511 under Sanskrit ḗṭatē, 'goes', which lists Bengali eṛāna, 'to avoid', beside eṛā, 'to let go, reject'; how the sense moved is not set out there. The -এ form is এড়িয়ে: এড়িয়ে গেলাম, I steered clear.
