## You'll want to know: जरा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C491-JARA491-01]; assesses=[] -->

**जरा** — *jarā* — "old age".

> जरायां केशाः श्वेताः भवन्ति।
>
> *jarāyāṃ keśāḥ śvetāḥ bhavanti.*
>
> In old age the hair turns white.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**जरा** (*jarā*) comes from the root √jṝ, "to grow old"; MW gives "the act of becoming old, old age" (MW).
