## You'll want to know: inolvidable
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C764-RECUERDO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**inolvidable** — "unforgettable".

> Fue un viaje inolvidable.
>
> *It was an unforgettable trip.*

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*inolvidable* is built on *olvidar* "to forget", with *in-* and *-able*; *olvidar* is inherited from a spoken Latin *oblītāre* (EtymDB).
