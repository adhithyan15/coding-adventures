## You'll want to know: shēntǐ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C50-SLEEP50-04]; assesses=[] -->

*shēntǐ* — "body; health".

> *nǐ shēntǐ hǎo ma* — How's your health?

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*shēntǐ* is built from shēn 'body, self' and tǐ 'body', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *syin* and *thejX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying how you are.
