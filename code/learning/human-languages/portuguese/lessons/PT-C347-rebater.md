## You'll want to know: rebater
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C347-POLEMICA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**rebater** — "to rebut, to counter; to hit back".

> Ela rebateu as críticas com calma.
>
> *She rebutted the criticism calmly.*

Literally to beat back, as with a ball.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*rebater* is built from *re-* "back" and *bater* "to beat", which is inherited from Latin *battuere* "to beat, to strike" (Lewis & Short).
