## You'll want to know: o cemitério
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C315-FUNERAL-04]; assesses=[] -->

**o cemitério** — "the cemetery".

> O cemitério fica ao lado da igreja.
>
> *The cemetery is next to the church.*

Every village has its own.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*cemitério* was borrowed from Late Latin *coemētērium* (EtymDB), from Greek *koimētḗrion* "a sleeping place".
