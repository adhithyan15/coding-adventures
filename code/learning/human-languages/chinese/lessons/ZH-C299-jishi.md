## You'll want to know: jíshí
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C299-RESCUE299-02]; assesses=[] -->

*jíshí* — "in time; promptly; before it was too late".

> *yīshēng lái de hěn jíshí* — The doctor came in good time.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*jíshí* is built from *jí* 'to reach' and 时 'time', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *gip* and *dzyi* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to arrive' and 'to catch up with': to catch the time.

## What you've built
