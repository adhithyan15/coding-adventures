You'll want to know: संतरा.

The word, taken apart.
The origin of संतरा (santarā) is uncertain. It is often linked with the Portuguese town of Sintra, once written Cintra, but no source settles it, and CDIAL has no entry for it.

Lesson 4 of 5.
नारियल (nāriyal) — a coconut.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for an orange, then the Hindi for a watermelon.

You'll want to know: नारियल.
नारियल — nāriyal — "a coconut".

The word, taken apart.
नारियल (nāriyal) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from nārikela, "coconut palm and fruit" (CDIAL 7075). CDIAL, after Bloch, takes the Sanskrit word from a Dravidian compound, comparing Tamil nāri, "fibre", and kēḷi, "coconut palm".
