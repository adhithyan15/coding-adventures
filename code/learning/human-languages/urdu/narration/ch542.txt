Urdu, chapter 542: Anyway.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
بہرحال (baharhāl) — anyway; in any case.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a point (that things come to), then the Urdu for supposing (for argument's sake).

You'll want to know: بہرحال.
بہرحال — baharhāl — "anyway; in any case".
بہرحال، اگر آپ آئے تو ہمیں خوشی ہو گی۔ baharhāl, agar āp āe to hameṅ khushī ho gī. Anyway, if you come, we will be glad.

The word, taken apart.
بہرحال (baharhāl) is usually traced to Persian ba-har-ḥāl, "in every state", built from ba, "in", har, "every", and Arabic ḥāl, "a state".
