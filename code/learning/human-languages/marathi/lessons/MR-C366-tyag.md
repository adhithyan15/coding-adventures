## You'll want to know: त्याग
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C366-MULYA366-04]; assesses=[] -->

**त्याग** — *tyāg* — "renunciation; a sacrifice".

> मुलांसाठी आईने खूप त्याग केला.
>
> *mulāṁsāṭhī āīne khūp tyāg kelā.*
>
> The mother made many sacrifices for her children.

Masculine.

त्याग करणे, to give up: सुखाचा त्याग.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**त्याग** (*tyāg*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *tyāga*, "abandonment, liberality" (CDIAL 5985).
