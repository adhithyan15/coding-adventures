## You'll want to know: ರೈತ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C48-ROLE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ರೈತ** (*raita*) — "a farmer".

ರೈತ came the long way round. Arabic *raʿīyat*, 'the flock, the governed people', travelled through Persian into the administrative language of the Deccan and came out the other side as the word for the man who works the land and pays the revenue on it. English took the same word from the same offices and spelled it *ryot*.

Kannada has an older and homelier word beside it — ಒಕ್ಕಲಿಗ (*okkaliga*), from *okkalu*, a household that farms — but ರೈತ is what a newspaper prints.
