## You'll want to know: 还是
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C154-HAISHI-01]; assesses=[] -->

**还是** — *háishi* — "or", in a question.

> **你要水还是要饭** *nǐ yào shuǐ háishi yào fàn* — Do you want water or food?

> **你去还是他去** *nǐ qù háishi tā qù* — Are you going, or is he?

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**还是** (*háishi*) is built from 还 'still', the character you have written, and 是 'to be'. Both are inherited from Middle Chinese, *hwaen* and *dzyeX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription); 'or' is the pair's own sense, which neither gloss gives.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ZH-LEX-C41-MONEY41-05] -->

还是 goes between the two choices, and the answer picks one: 我要水.

Each choice can keep its own verb: 要水还是要饭. The question needs no 吗: 还是 already makes it one.
