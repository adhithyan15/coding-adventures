# (el cuadro de turnos) — read it, then do it yourself

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-C453-LIMPIEZA-01, ES-LEX-C453-LIMPIEZA-03] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say **la limpieza** and **rotativo**. The notice below puts them
in the same line.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-C453-LIMPIEZA-01, ES-LEX-C453-LIMPIEZA-03, ES-LEX-C453-LIMPIEZA-02] -->

A sheet taped inside the entrance hall. Read it, then answer.

> **TURNOS DE LIMPIEZA — ESCALERA B**

> La **limpieza** del portal es **rotativa**, una semana por vivienda.

> Esta semana: 3º izquierda. La próxima: 3º derecha.

> No se limpia la **instalación** de la caldera. De eso se encarga la empresa.

> Para cambiar de semana, **consulten** el cuadro antes de escribir en él.

[PAUSE 3s]
- [YOU SAY: whose turn it is this week and whose it is next]
- [YOU SAY: the one thing the neighbours do not have to clean]
- [YOU SAY: what you must do before changing your week]

## Why it's said this way
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-C453-LIMPIEZA-02, ES-LEX-C453-LIMPIEZA-04, ES-LEX-C453-LIMPIEZA-01] -->

**La instalación de la caldera** is the noun in its second sense — not the act
of fitting the boiler, which happened years ago, but the pipes and tank it
left behind. Nothing in the sentence announces which reading is meant; *no se
limpia* settles it, because you cannot clean an act.

**Rotativa** agrees with *la limpieza*, and it is doing the work of a whole
English clause: "goes round the flats in turn". One adjective, because Spanish
had a word for the wheel and built on it.

Then **consulten**: *ustedes*, plural and formal, the register of a notice
addressed to every household at once. And *se encarga*, once again the *se*
that lets a sentence say what happens without naming who.

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-C453-LIMPIEZA-01, ES-LEX-C453-LIMPIEZA-02, ES-LEX-C453-LIMPIEZA-03, ES-LEX-C453-LIMPIEZA-04] -->

[PAUSE 3s] Now your turn, out loud and then in writing. Four lines for a
stairwell notice: say the cleaning is on a rota, say whose week it is, say the
boiler installation is not included, and tell people to check the sheet first.

Then check yourself: did *rotativa* agree with *la limpieza*, and did you use
the *ustedes* command?
