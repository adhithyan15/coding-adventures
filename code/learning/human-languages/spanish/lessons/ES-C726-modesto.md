## You'll want to know: modesto
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C726-HUMILDE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**modesto** — "modest".

> Vive en un piso modesto.
>
> *She lives in a modest flat.*

For people and for things: *un sueldo modesto*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*modesto* is a learned borrowing from Latin *modestus* "keeping due measure" (EtymDB), built on *modus* "measure".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.
