## Chapters 70-120: 255 headwords, thirty-six verbs, and Marathi attains pre-A1

Marathi's last pre-A1 gap was vocabulary: it was 251 headwords short.

**The vocabulary.** Fifty-one chapters of five words each, in three runs of
seventeen. Each run closes with two reviews, in chapters 86, 103 and 120. Every
word is chained into the next two lessons. Three runs rather than two keep
each review under the 300-second lesson ceiling: a review covering thirteen
chapters measured 310 seconds.

- **70-86**:
  - the body and health
  - the family on both sides
  - thirty-six verbs
- **87-103**:
  - food
  - the home and school
  - nature and animals
- **104-120**:
  - describing words and colours
  - time and place
  - the town
  - clothes
  - the tens to a thousand

Every headword is written only with letters the reader has already written.
Some candidates were held back because earlier lesson text already uses them:

- **ओठ**, **औषध**, **ढग** and **ऐंशी** appear in the standing-vowel and row
  lessons.
- **काल** appears in **उद्या भेटू**.
- **प्रेम** appears in the liking lessons.
- **भेटणे** appears in **भेटून आनंद झाला** and **भेटू**.

Marathi's forward-reference count stays at 10.
