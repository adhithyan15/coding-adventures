## You'll want to know: ఆతిథ్యం
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C533-SAMSARAM533-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ఆతిథ్యం** — *ātithyaṁ* — "hospitality".

> వాళ్ళ ఆతిథ్యం మాకు ఎంతో నచ్చింది.
>
> *vāḷḷa ātithyaṁ māku entō naccindi.*
>
> We liked their hospitality very much.

Linked to అతిథి, a guest.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ఆతిథ్యం** (*ātithyaṁ*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *ātithya*, "hospitable reception, hospitality" (Monier-Williams), built on *atithi*, "a guest".
