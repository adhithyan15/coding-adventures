## You'll want to know: برابری
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C386-BARABARI-01]; assesses=[] -->

**برابری** (*barâbari*) — "equality".

> آنها برای برابری زن و مرد تلاش میکنند.
>
> *ânhâ barâye barâbari-ye zan o mard talâsh mikonand.*
>
> They work for equality between women and men.

From *barâbar* "equal" + *-i*. The opposite is *nâbarâbari*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**برابری** is native Persian, built on *barâbar* "equal, level" with the noun suffix *-i*.
