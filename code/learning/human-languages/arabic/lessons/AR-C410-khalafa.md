# خالف (khālafa) — to break a rule

## You'll want to know: خالف
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C410-IBAHA410-03]; assesses=[] -->

**خالف** — *khālafa* — "to break a rule".

> خالف الرجل قواعد المكان.
>
> *khālafa ar-rajul qawāʿid al-makān.*
>
> The man broke the rules of the place.

Present: يخالف, *yukhālif*.

Also to differ: خالفه الرأي, disagreed with him.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**خالف** (*khālafa*) is built on the native Arabic root *kh-l-f*, "to come after, behind" (Lane). It is Form III, to go against the one behind or before you: to break a rule, to differ.
