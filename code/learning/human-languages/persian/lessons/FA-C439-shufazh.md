## You'll want to know: شوفاژ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C439-MALAFE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**شوفاژ** (*shufâzh*) — "central heating; a radiator".

> در زمستان شوفاژ اتاق گرم است.
>
> *Dar zemestân shufâzh-e otâq garm ast.*
>
> In winter the radiator in the room is warm.

One word for the system and for each radiator.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*shufâzh* was borrowed from French *chauffage* 'heating' (EtymDB).

## What you've built
