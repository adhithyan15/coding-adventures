# Review — what will happen

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[BN-GRAMMAR-C265-FUTURE-PERSON-01, BN-GRAMMAR-C264-PAST-PERSON-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "Yesterday I went to the market", then "Tomorrow I will go to the station". (*kāl āmi bājāre gelām*; *kāl āmi sṭeshône jābô*.)

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[BN-GRAMMAR-C265-FUTURE-PERSON-01, BN-GRAMMAR-C265-FUTURE-NOT-01, BN-LEX-C265-SHESH-01, BN-SCRIPT-C265-MURDHANYA-SHA-01, BN-GRAMMAR-C264-PAST-STEMS-01, BN-GRAMMAR-C264-WAS-01, BN-LEX-C128-THINGS128-04, BN-LEX-C59-FOOD59-04] -->

[PAUSE 2s each]
- [YOU SAY: Tomorrow we will eat fish. Mira will come too.]
- [YOU SAY: I won't go to the market; I'll stay at home.]
- [YOU SAY: When will the last train leave?]
- [YOU SAY: Yesterday I was tired. Tomorrow I'll rest.]
- [YOU SAY: My work will end in the evening.]
- [YOU WRITE: **শেষ**, then ঘ]

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[BN-GRAMMAR-C265-FUTURE-PERSON-01, BN-GRAMMAR-C265-FUTURE-NOT-01, BN-LEX-C265-SHESH-01, BN-SCRIPT-C265-MURDHANYA-SHA-01, BN-GRAMMAR-C264-PAST-PERSON-01] -->

[PAUSE 3s] Which two people share **-বে**? (**তুমি** and **সে**.) "It will happen"? (**হবে**.) "Will not"? (**না** after the verb.) Which letter ends **শেষ**? (**ষ**.)
