French, chapter 378: Examens et résultats.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
échouer — to fail (an exam); to run aground.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for by heart, then the French for the textbook.

You'll want to know: échouer.
échouer — "to fail (an exam); to run aground".
Il a échoué à l'examen du permis de conduire. He failed his driving test.
Échouer à un examen; the noun is un échec.

The word, taken apart.
échouer first meant to run a ship aground. The origin is not settled.

Lesson 2 of 5.
redoubler — to repeat a year (at school); to increase.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for the textbook, then the French for to fail.

You'll want to know: redoubler.
redoubler — "to repeat a year (at school); to increase".
Elle a redoublé sa première année. She repeated her first year.
Belgium, Switzerland and Québec use the verb without re-. Redoubler d'efforts — to try twice as hard.

The word, taken apart.
redoubler is built from re- and doubler "to double", inherited from Late Latin duplāre (EtymDB), from Latin duplus "double" (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 3 of 5.
le concours — the competitive exam; the competition.

Warm-up.

Before the new one: say the French for to fail, then the French for to repeat a year.

You'll want to know: le concours.
le concours — "the competitive exam; the competition".
Il prépare le concours pour devenir professeur. He is preparing for the competitive exam to become a teacher.
Only a set number pass. The same form in the plural.

The word, taken apart.
concours comes from Latin concursus "a running together" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short), from concurrere "to run together", built on currere "to run".

Lesson 4 of 5.
la moyenne — the average; the pass mark.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for to repeat a year, then the French for the competitive exam.

You'll want to know: la moyenne.
la moyenne — "the average; the pass mark".
J'ai eu la moyenne en maths: dix sur vingt. I got the pass mark in maths: ten out of twenty.
In France, marks are out of twenty; avoir la moyenne is to get ten.

The word, taken apart.
moyenne is the feminine of moyen "middle, average", inherited through Old French moien (EtymDB) from Latin mediānus "in the middle", from medius "middle" (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 5 of 5.
le rattrapage — the resit (exam); catching up.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for the competitive exam, then the French for the average.

You'll want to know: le rattrapage.
le rattrapage — "the resit (exam); catching up".
Il a échoué à l'examen, mais il y a un rattrapage en juillet. He failed the exam, but there is a resit in July.
Belgium: la seconde session. From rattraper, to catch up.

The word, taken apart.
rattrapage is built from rattraper "to catch again, to catch up" and -age (EtymDB). rattraper is re- + attraper "to catch", which rests on trappe "a trap", usually traced to a Frankish word.
