## You'll want to know: बदळणो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C220-PLAN220-04]; assesses=[] -->

**बदळणो** — *badaḷṇo* — "to change; to exchange".

> हूं काल गेलो बदळसूं।
>
> *hū̃ kāl gelo badaḷsū̃.*
>
> Tomorrow I'll change my route.

Past (I …): म्हैं गेलो बदळ्यो, *mhaĩ gelo badaḷyo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**बदळणो** (*badaḷṇo*) is usually traced to Persian *badal*, "an exchange, a substitute", which Persian took from Arabic; Marwadi made a verb of it.
