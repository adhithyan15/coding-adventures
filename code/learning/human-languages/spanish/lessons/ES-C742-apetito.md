## You'll want to know: el apetito
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C742-DIETA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**el apetito** — "appetite".

> No tengo apetito hoy.
>
> *I have no appetite today.*

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*apetito* comes from Latin *appetītus* "desire" (EtymDB), from *appetere* "to seek after", built on *petere* "to aim at".
