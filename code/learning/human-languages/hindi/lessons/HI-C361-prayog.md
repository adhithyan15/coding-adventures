## You'll want to know: प्रयोग
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C361-SHODH361-02]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रयोग** — *prayog* — "an experiment".

> छात्रों ने कक्षा में एक प्रयोग किया।
>
> *chātroṁ ne kakṣā meṁ ek prayog kiyā.*
>
> The students did an experiment in class.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रयोग** (*prayog*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *prayoga*, "use, application" (Monier-Williams).
