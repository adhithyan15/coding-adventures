## You'll want to know: ક્રોધ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C452-KHIJ452-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ક્રોધ** — *krodh* — "wrath; fierce anger".

> ક્રોધમાં તેણે ખોટો જવાબ આપ્યો.
>
> *krodhmā̃ teṇe khoṭo javāb āpyo.*
>
> In his anger he gave a false answer.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ક્રોધ** (*krodh*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *krodha*, "anger, wrath", as Monier-Williams records it; everyday Gujarati says **ગુસ્સો** (*gusso*).
