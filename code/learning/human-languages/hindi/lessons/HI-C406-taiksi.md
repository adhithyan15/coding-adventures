## You'll want to know: टैक्सी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C406-TAIKSI406-01]; assesses=[] -->

**टैक्सी** — *ṭaiksī* — "a taxi".

> हमने स्टेशन से टैक्सी ली।
>
> *hamne sṭeśan se ṭaiksī lī.*
>
> We took a taxi from the station.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**टैक्सी** (*ṭaiksī*) is borrowed from English *taxi*; it is feminine in Hindi, like गाड़ी.
