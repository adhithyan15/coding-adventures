## You'll want to know: considerar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C576-OPINA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**considerar** — "to consider; to regard as".

> Considero que es una buena idea.
>
> *I consider it a good idea.*

*Considerar que* + indicative gives your opinion; *considerar algo importante* is to regard it as important.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*considerar* is a learned borrowing from Latin *considerare* 'to examine closely, to scrutinize', built from *con-* and, by the usual account, *sidus* 'star', as if first to watch the stars (EtymDB; Lewis & Short).
