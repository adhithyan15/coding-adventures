# بحث کرنا (bahas karnā) — to argue; to debate

## You'll want to know: بحث کرنا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C334-BAHAS334-01]; assesses=[] -->

**بحث کرنا** — *bahas karnā* — "to argue; to debate".

> وہ دونوں ہر بات پر بحث کرتے ہیں۔
>
> *voh donoṅ har bāt par bahas karte haiṅ.*
>
> Those two argue about everything.

Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے استاد سے بحث کی, *us ne ustād se bahas kī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**بحث کرنا** (*bahas karnā*) is built from *bahas*, usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *baḥth*, "an inquiry, a discussion", and **کرنا**, "to do".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
