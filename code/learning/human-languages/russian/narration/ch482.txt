Russian, chapter 482: Temple, Eyelid and Jaw.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
висок (visók) — a temple (of the head).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a heel, then the Russian for a palm.

You'll want to know: висок.
висок (visók) — "a temple (of the head)".
У него седые волосы на висках. U negó sedýye vólosy na viskákh. He has grey hair at the temples.
Masculine; genitive виска, plural виски (stress on the end). Not to be confused with виски — whisky.

The word, taken apart.
EtymDB lists висок (visók) without a source; it is usually linked to висеть 'to hang', after the hair that hangs at the temples, but the link is not settled.

Lesson 2 of 5.
затылок (zatýlok) — the back of the head.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a palm, then the Russian for a temple.

You'll want to know: затылок.
затылок (zatýlok) — "the back of the head".
Мяч попал ему в затылок. Myach popál yemú v zatýlok. The ball hit him on the back of the head.
Masculine; genitive затылка.

The word, taken apart.
затылок (zatýlok) is built on за 'behind' and тыл 'back, rear', which is inherited from Proto-Slavic tylъ 'back of the head, back' (EtymDB).

Lesson 4 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for the back of the head, then the Russian for an eyelid.

ноздря (nozdryá) — "a nostril".
У меня заложена правая ноздря. U menyá zalózhena právaya nozdryá. My right nostril is blocked.
Feminine; plural ноздри.

The word, taken apart.
ноздря (nozdryá) is inherited from Proto-Slavic nozdra, which EtymDB traces to PIE néh₂s 'nose', the root of нос.
