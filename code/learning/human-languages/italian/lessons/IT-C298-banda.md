## You'll want to know: la banda
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C298-BANDA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**la banda** — "the gang; the brass band".

> Una banda ha rapinato tre negozi.
>
> *A gang robbed three shops.*

At a town festival, *la banda* is the brass band.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*banda* "a band of people" is usually traced to Provençal *banda* "a troop", which EtymDB derives from a Frankish word *binda* "a bond, a link".
