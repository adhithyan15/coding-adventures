Lesson 1 of 5.
die Kindheit — childhood.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for an interest, then the German for youth.

You'll want to know: die Kindheit.
die Kindheit — "childhood".

The word, taken apart.
Kindheit from Middle High German kintheit from Old High German kindheit (EtymDB 2.1): the word for "child" + -heit, the same ending as English -hood.
