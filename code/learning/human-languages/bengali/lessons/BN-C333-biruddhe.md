## You'll want to know: বিরুদ্ধে
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C333-JUKTI333-04]; assesses=[] -->

**বিরুদ্ধে** — *biruddhe* — "against".

> গ্রামের সবাই নতুন আইনের বিরুদ্ধে।
>
> *grāmer sôbāi nôtun āiner biruddhe.*
>
> Everyone in the village is against the new law.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**বিরুদ্ধে** (*biruddhe*) is Sanskrit *viruddha*, opposed or hindered (Monier-Williams), with the Bengali ending -এ. It follows a word with -এর: আইনের বিরুদ্ধে, against the law.
