## You'll want to know: બદલે
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C537-PASANDGI537-02]; assesses=[] -->

**બદલે** — *badle* — "instead of".

> જો ચા નહીં હોય તો હું ચાને બદલે દૂધ પીશ.
>
> *jo cā nahī̃ hoy to hũ cāne badle dūdh pīś.*
>
> If there's no tea, I'll drink milk instead.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**બદલે** (*badle*) is usually traced to Arabic *badal*, "a substitute", through Persian, in the form that follows **ને**: **ચાને બદલે**, instead of tea. The verb **બદલવું**, to change, belongs with it.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
