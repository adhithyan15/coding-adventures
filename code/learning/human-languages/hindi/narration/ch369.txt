Hindi, chapter 369: Corruption.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
भ्रष्टाचार (bhraṣṭācār) — corruption.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for discipline, then the Hindi for to polish.

You'll want to know: भ्रष्टाचार.
भ्रष्टाचार — bhraṣṭācār — "corruption".
लोग भ्रष्टाचार से परेशान हैं। log bhraṣṭācār se pareśān haiṁ. People are fed up with corruption.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
भ्रष्टाचार (bhraṣṭācār) joins two Sanskrit words, bhraṣṭa, "fallen", and ācāra, "conduct" (Monier-Williams): conduct that has fallen.

Lesson 2 of 5.
घूस (ghūs) — a bribe (everyday).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for to polish, then the Hindi for corruption.

You'll want to know: घूस.
घूस — ghūs — "a bribe (everyday)".
अधिकारी ने घूस ली। adhikārī ne ghūs lī. The official took a bribe.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
घूस (ghūs) is inherited from ghūssā, "a present", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 4499). CDIAL glosses the Hindi word "bribe"; the step from a present to a bribe is not spelled out there.
