## You'll want to know: حرر
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C296-SAHAFA296-04]; assesses=[] -->

**حرر** — *ḥarrara* — "to edit; to set free".

> حررت أختي كتابا عن الطبخ.
>
> *ḥarrarat ukhtī kitāban ʿan aṭ-ṭabkh.*
>
> My sister edited a book about cooking.

Present: يحرر, *yuḥarrir*.

Of people and lands it means to free: حرر الجيش المدينة.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**حرر** (*ḥarrara*) is built on the native Arabic root *ḥ-r-r* (Lane), "to be free". It is Form II, "to make free", and of a text "to write it out clean and correct".
