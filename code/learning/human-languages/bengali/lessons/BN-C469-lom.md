## You'll want to know: লোম
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C469-GHAM469-05]; assesses=[] -->

**লোম** — *lom* — "body hair; fur".

> ভয়ে আমার হাতের লোম খাড়া হয়ে গেল।
>
> *bhôye āmār hāter lom khāṛā hôye gelô.*
>
> The hairs on my arms stood on end with fear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**লোম** (*lom*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *loman*, "hair on the body of animals or people" (CDIAL 11154).
