## You'll want to know: روزہ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C322-ROZA322-01]; assesses=[] -->

**روزہ** — *rozā* — "a fast (from dawn to dusk)".

> اس نے آج روزہ رکھا۔
>
> *us ne āj rozā rakhā.*
>
> He fasted today.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**روزہ** (*rozā*) is usually traced to Persian *rōza*, "a fast", built on *rōz*, "a day", the word inside **روز**. A fast is kept: **روزہ رکھا**.
