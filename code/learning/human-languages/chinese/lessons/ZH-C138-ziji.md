## You'll want to know: zìjǐ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C138-EVERY138-02]; assesses=[] -->

*zìjǐ* — "oneself".

> *wǒ zìjǐ zuòfàn* — I cook for myself.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*zìjǐ* is built from *zì* 'from' and *jǐ* 'self', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *dzijH* and *kiX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'one's own body'.
