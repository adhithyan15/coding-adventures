Sanskrit, chapter 513: Milk and Butter.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
मथ्नाति (mathnāti) — churns.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a winnowing basket, then the Sanskrit for combines.

You'll want to know: मथ्नाति.
मथ्नाति — mathnāti — "churns".
माता दधि मथ्नाति। mātā dadhi mathnāti. Mother churns the curd.
Past (she …): सा दधि मथितवती, sā dadhi mathitavatī.

The word, taken apart.
मथ्नाति (mathnāti) comes from the root √math (also manth), "to stir, to churn", whose present is mathnāti; its past participle is mathita (MW).

Lesson 3 of 5.
घृतम् (ghṛtam) — ghee.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for churns, then the Sanskrit for a churning stick.

You'll want to know: घृतम्.
घृतम् — ghṛtam — "ghee".
नवनीतात् घृतं भवति। navanītāt ghṛtaṃ bhavati. Ghee is made from butter.
A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
घृतम् (ghṛtam) comes from the root √ghṛ, "to sprinkle", as its past participle; MW gives ghṛta as ghee, butter that has been boiled gently and left to cool (MW).

Lesson 4 of 5.
गालयति (gālayati) — strains.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a churning stick, then the Sanskrit for ghee.

You'll want to know: गालयति.
गालयति — gālayati — "strains".
सः क्षीरं वस्त्रेण गालयति। saḥ kṣīraṃ vastreṇa gālayati. He strains the milk through a cloth.
Past (he …): सः क्षीरं गालितवान्, saḥ kṣīraṃ gālitavān.

The word, taken apart.
गालयति (gālayati) comes from the causative of the root √gal, "to drip, to ooze": MW gives gālayati as to make drop, to filter, to strain (MW).

घृतेन ओदनः स्निग्धः भवति। ghṛtena odanaḥ snigdhaḥ bhavati. Ghee makes the rice rich.

The word, taken apart.
स्निग्धः (snigdhaḥ) comes from the root √snih, "to be sticky, to be fond of", as its past participle; MW gives snigdha as sticky, unctuous, smooth (MW).
