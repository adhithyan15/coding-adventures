# Chapter 7 — The first verbs

## Warm-up

[PAUSE 2s] No new words. Three verbs, one engine — build real sentences about

## How to answer — build the sentences
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-CONCEPT-C05-PRACTICE-01]; assesses=[GU-CONCEPT-C03-HUN-01, GU-CONCEPT-C05-BOLVUN-01, GU-CONCEPT-C05-HUNGUJARATIBOLUNCHHUN-01, GU-CONCEPT-C05-KAAMKARVUN-01, GU-CONCEPT-C05-RAHEVUN-01] -->

[PAUSE 1s each]
- [YOU SAY: "I speak Gujarati" — *hũ gujarātī bolũ chhũ*]
- [YOU SAY: "I live in Ahmedabad" — *hũ Amdāvādmā rahũ chhũ*]
- [YOU SAY: "I work" — *hũ kām karũ chhũ*]
- [YOU SAY: "you (resp.) speak Gujarati" — *tame gujarātī bolo chho*]

## Grammar lens — the engine
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-CONCEPT-C03-HUN-01, GU-CONCEPT-C05-BOLVUN-01, GU-CONCEPT-C05-HUNGUJARATIBOLUNCHHUN-01, GU-CONCEPT-C05-KAAMKARVUN-01, GU-CONCEPT-C05-PRACTICE-01, GU-CONCEPT-C05-RAHEVUN-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s]
- [YOU SAY: the ending every infinitive carries (*-vũ*)]
- [YOU SAY: the shape of the present (stem + person + the copula *chhũ/chhe/chho*)]
- [YOU SAY: where the verb sits, and where "in" sits (verb last; *-mā* after the noun)]

## What you've built — roots you now carry
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-CONCEPT-C03-HUN-01, GU-CONCEPT-C05-BOLVUN-01, GU-CONCEPT-C05-HUNGUJARATIBOLUNCHHUN-01, GU-CONCEPT-C05-KAAMKARVUN-01, GU-CONCEPT-C05-PRACTICE-01, GU-CONCEPT-C05-RAHEVUN-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s]
- *bolvũ* "to speak" (native; *bol-*, like Hindi/Punjabi)
- *rahevũ* "to live" ← *rah-* "to remain"
- *karvũ* "to do" ← *√kṛ*; *kām* "work" ← *karma* (both from *kṛ*)
- *gujarātī* ← the *Gurjar* people, who named the land Gujarat

## Wrap-up Recall

[PAUSE 3s] In one breath, tell me your language, your city, and your work in
Gujarati. (*Hũ gujarātī bolũ chhũ. Hũ … rahũ chhũ. Hũ kām karũ chhũ.*)
