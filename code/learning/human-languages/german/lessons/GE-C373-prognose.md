## You'll want to know: die Prognose
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C373-UNVORHERGESEHEN-04]; assesses=[] -->

**die Prognose** — "the forecast, the prognosis".

> Die Prognose für das Wochenende ist gut: viel Sonne.
>
> *The forecast for the weekend is good: lots of sun.*

Plural: *die Prognosen*. *Eine Prognose stellen* — to make a forecast; a doctor's prognosis too.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Prognose* is a learned borrowing from Late Latin *prognōsis*, from Greek *prógnōsis* "foreknowledge", built from *pro* "before" and *gnôsis* "knowing". EtymDB has no entry for the German word.
