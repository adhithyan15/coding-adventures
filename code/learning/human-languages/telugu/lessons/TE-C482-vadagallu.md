# వడగళ్ళు (vaḍagaḷḷu) — hailstones

## Warm-up

## You'll want to know: వడగళ్ళు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C482-HIMAPATAM482-01]; assesses=[] -->

**వడగళ్ళు** — *vaḍagaḷḷu* — "hailstones".

> నిన్న వడగళ్ళ వాన పడింది.
>
> *ninna vaḍagaḷḷa vāna paḍindi.*
>
> Yesterday there was a hailstorm.

Plural: వడగళ్ళు, hailstones; వడగళ్ళ వాన, a hailstorm. -గళ్ళు is the plural of కల్లు, a stone.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**వడగళ్ళు** (*vaḍagaḷḷu*) is a Telugu compound: -గళ్ళు, "stones", from the native కల్లు, "a stone" (DEDR 1298), and వడ-, whose origin is not settled.
