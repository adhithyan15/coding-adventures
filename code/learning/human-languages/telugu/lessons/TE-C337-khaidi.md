## You'll want to know: ఖైదీ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C337-ARESTU337-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ఖైదీ** — *khaidī* — "a prisoner".

> ఖైదీ జైలు నుంచి పారిపోయాడు.
>
> *khaidī jailu nuñci pāripōyāḍu.*
>
> The prisoner escaped from jail.

Plural: ఖైదీలు, *khaidīlu*.

From ఖైదు, prison or custody: ఖైదీ is the one held.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ఖైదీ** (*khaidī*) is a loan from Persian *qaidī*, "a prisoner".
