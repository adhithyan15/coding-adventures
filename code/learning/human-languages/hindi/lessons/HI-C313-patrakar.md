## You'll want to know: पत्रकार
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C313-PATRAKAR313-01]; assesses=[] -->

**पत्रकार** — *patrakār* — "a journalist".

> मीरा पत्रकार बनना चाहती है।
>
> *Mīrā patrakār bannā cāhtī hai.*
>
> Mira wants to become a journalist.

Masculine or feminine, by the person meant.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पत्रकार** (*patrakār*) joins **पत्र**, a letter or a paper, from Sanskrit *pattra*, also spelt *patra*, with *-kāra*, "maker, doer" (Monier-Williams): a modern word for one who makes the papers, a journalist.
