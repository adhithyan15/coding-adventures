## You'll want to know: il remo
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C422-BARCA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**il remo** — "the oar".

> Ho perso un remo nell'acqua.
>
> *I lost an oar in the water.*

*Una barca a remi* — a rowing boat.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*remo* is inherited from Latin *rēmus* 'oar' (EtymDB).
