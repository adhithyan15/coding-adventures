## You'll want to know: la crociera
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C423-TRAGHETTO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**la crociera** — "the cruise".

> I miei genitori vanno in crociera.
>
> *My parents are going on a cruise.*

*La velocità di crociera* — cruising speed.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*crociera* is built on *croce* 'cross', which is inherited from Latin *crux* (Lewis & Short): first a ship crossing back and forth over a stretch of sea.
