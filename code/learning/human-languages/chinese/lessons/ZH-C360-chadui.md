## You'll want to know: chāduì
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C360-NEED360-05]; assesses=[] -->

*chāduì* — "to jump the queue".

> *dàjiā dōu zài páiduì nǐ bù yīnggāi chāduì* — Everyone's queuing; you shouldn't push in.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*chāduì* is built from *chā* and *duì*, both inherited from Middle Chinese, *tsrheap* and *dwojH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to thrust in' and the second 'a troop'.
