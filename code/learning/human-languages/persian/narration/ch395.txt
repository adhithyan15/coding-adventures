Persian, chapter 395: It depends.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
بسته به (baste be) — depending on.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a risk, then the Persian for to take a risk.

You'll want to know: بسته به.
بسته به (baste be) — "depending on".
قیمت بسته به فصل فرق میکند. qeymat baste be fasl farq mikonad. The price varies depending on the season.
Baste "bound" + be "to". It takes a noun or a clause: baste be inke ….

The word, taken apart.
بسته به (baste be) is native Persian, literally "bound to": baste is the participle of bastan "to bind", which EtymDB traces to PIE bʰendʰ- "to bind".

Lesson 2 of 5.
وابسته (vâbaste) — dependent; attached.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to take a risk, then the Persian for depending on.

You'll want to know: وابسته.
وابسته (vâbaste) — "dependent; attached".
او هنوز از نظر مالی به پدرش وابسته است. u hanuz az nazar-e mâli be pedarash vâbaste ast. He still depends on his father for money.
Vâbaste be — dependent on. Also emotionally attached to someone.

The word, taken apart.
وابسته (vâbaste) is native Persian, built from the prefix vâ- + baste "bound", the participle of bastan "to bind" (EtymDB traces it to PIE bʰendʰ-). To depend is to be tied to something.

Lesson 3 of 5.
شرایط (sharâyet) — conditions, circumstances.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for depending on, then the Persian for dependent.

You'll want to know: شرایط.
شرایط (sharâyet) — "conditions, circumstances".
در این شرایط نمیتوانیم سفر کنیم. dar in sharâyet nemitavânim safar konim. In these circumstances we cannot travel.
An Arabic broken plural of shart "condition". It also means terms: sharâyet-e qarârdâd.

The word, taken apart.
شرایط (sharâyet) is an Arabic broken plural of shart, which was borrowed from Arabic sharṭ "condition" (EtymDB).

Lesson 4 of 5.
به محض اینکه (be mahz-e inke) — as soon as.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for dependent, then the Persian for conditions.

You'll want to know: به محض اینکه.
به محض اینکه (be mahz-e inke) — "as soon as".
به محض اینکه رسیدم، به تو زنگ میزنم. be mahz-e inke residam, be to zang mizanam. As soon as I arrive, I will call you.
It takes the past even for a future event: be mahz-e inke residam — as soon as I arrive.

The word, taken apart.
به محض اینکه (be mahz-e inke) is literally "at the mere fact that": mahz is usually traced to Arabic maḥḍ "pure, mere", and be and inke "that" are Persian.

Lesson 5 of 5.
تا زمانی که (tâ zamâni ke) — as long as; until.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for conditions, then the Persian for as soon as.

You'll want to know: تا زمانی که.
تا زمانی که (tâ zamâni ke) — "as long as; until".
تا زمانی که اینجا هستی، میتوانی از اتاق من استفاده کنی. tâ zamâni ke injâ hasti, mitavâni az otâq-e man estefâde koni. As long as you are here, you can use my room.
With a negative verb it means "until": tâ zamâni ke nayâyad — until he comes.

The word, taken apart.
تا زمانی که (tâ zamâni ke) is literally "until the time that": tâ is inherited Persian (EtymDB traces it to Middle Persian "until, so that"), and zamân comes from Arabic zamān "time".
