Arabic, chapter 281: Number, Phone call, Information, Presenter, Actor.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
رقم (raqm) — a number.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a pilot, then the Arabic for a pharmacist.

You'll want to know: رقم.
رقم — raqm — "a number".

The word, taken apart.
رقم (raqm) is built on the native Arabic root r-q-m, "to write, to mark" (Lane). Lane's raqm is a mark of writing, and he notes the "Indian" numerals the Arabs adopted.
