## You'll want to know: ちゅうしょく
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C401-CHOUSHOKU401-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ちゅうしょく** — *chūshoku* — "lunch (a formal word)".

> **ちゅうしょくは えきべんでした。** — *chūshoku wa ekiben deshita* — "Lunch was a station lunch box."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ちゅうしょく** (*chūshoku*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *chū*, "daytime" (Middle Chinese *trjuwH*) and *shoku*, "eat" (*zyik*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
