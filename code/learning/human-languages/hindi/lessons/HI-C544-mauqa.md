## You'll want to know: मौक़ा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C544-KISMAT544-03]; assesses=[] -->

**मौक़ा** — *mauqā* — "a chance; an occasion".

> मुझे मंच पर गाने का मौक़ा मिला।
>
> *Mujhe mañc par gāne kā mauqā milā.*
>
> I got the chance to sing on stage.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**मौक़ा** (*mauqā*) is usually traced to Arabic *mauqiʿ*, "a place where something falls", through Persian. Hindi and Urdu use it for the moment when something can happen.
