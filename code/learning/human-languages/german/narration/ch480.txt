German, chapter 480: Übel und schwindlig.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
die Übelkeit — nausea.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for sweat, then the German for goose bumps.

You'll want to know: die Übelkeit.
die Übelkeit — "nausea".
Auf dem Schiff hatte ich starke Übelkeit. On the ship I felt very sick.
No plural. Mir ist übel — I feel sick.

The word, taken apart.
Übelkeit is built on übel "bad, sick" with -keit; übel is inherited from Old High German ubil, from Proto-Germanic ubilaz (EtymDB), and English evil is a cousin.

Lesson 2 of 5.
übergeben — to be sick, to vomit (sich übergeben); to hand over.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for goose bumps, then the German for nausea.

You'll want to know: übergeben.
übergeben — "to be sick, to vomit (sich übergeben); to hand over".
Im Bus hat sich ein Kind übergeben. A child was sick on the bus.
Inseparable. Reflexive for to vomit: Er hat sich übergeben. Without sich it means to hand over: den Schlüssel übergeben.

The word, taken apart.
übergeben is built on über "over" and geben "to give" (EtymDB), inherited from Old High German geban, from Proto-Germanic gebaną.

Lesson 3 of 5.
der Durchfall — diarrhoea.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for nausea, then the German for to be sick.

You'll want to know: der Durchfall.
der Durchfall — "diarrhoea".
Nach dem Essen im Urlaub hatte er Durchfall. After the food on holiday he had diarrhoea.
No article in Durchfall haben. No plural in everyday use.

The word, taken apart.
Durchfall is built on durch "through" and Fall "fall", which is inherited from Middle High German val and Old High German val (EtymDB).

Lesson 4 of 5.
schwindlig — dizzy.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to be sick, then the German for diarrhoea.

You'll want to know: schwindlig.
schwindlig — "dizzy".
Wenn ich zu schnell aufstehe, wird mir schwindlig. When I stand up too fast, I get dizzy.
With a dative: Mir ist schwindlig. Also spelt schwindelig.

The word, taken apart.
schwindlig is built on Schwindel "giddiness" with -ig; Schwindel comes from schwindeln, inherited from Middle High German swindeln and Old High German swintilōn (EtymDB).

Lesson 5 of 5.
der Kreislauf — circulation; cycle.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for diarrhoea, then the German for dizzy.

You'll want to know: der Kreislauf.
der Kreislauf — "circulation; cycle".
Bei der Hitze hatte meine Oma Probleme mit dem Kreislauf. In the heat my grandma had problems with her circulation.
Plural: die Kreisläufe. Germans often blame der Kreislauf when they feel faint.

The word, taken apart.
Kreislauf is built on Kreis "circle", inherited from Old High German kreiz, and Lauf "run", from laufen, from Proto-Germanic hlaupaną (EtymDB).
