# سجن (sajana) — to imprison, to jail

## You'll want to know: سجن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C330-SIJN330-01]; assesses=[] -->

**سجن** — *sajana* — "to imprison, to jail".

> سجن اللص سنتين.
>
> *sujina al-liṣṣ sanatayn.*
>
> The thief was jailed for two years.

Present: يسجن, *yasjun*.

The noun سجن, *sijn*, a prison, is spelled the same.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**سجن** (*sajana*) is built on the native Arabic root *s-j-n* (Lane), "to shut up, to confine"; *sijn* is the place of it.
