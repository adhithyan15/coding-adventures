## You'll want to know: ഉപസംഹരിക്കുക
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C439-GRANTHAM439-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ഉപസംഹരിക്കുക** — *upasaṁharikkuka* — "to conclude; to wind up".

> ഒരു ചെറിയ കഥയോടെ അദ്ദേഹം പ്രസംഗം ഉപസംഹരിച്ചു.
>
> *oru ceṟiya kathayōṭe addēhaṁ prasaṁgaṁ upasaṁhariccu.*
>
> He concluded the speech with a short story.

Past: ഉപസംഹരിച്ചു, *upasaṁhariccu*.

The noun is ഉപസംഹാരം, the conclusion of a speech or essay.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ഉപസംഹരിക്കുക** (*upasaṁharikkuka*) is built from Sanskrit *upasaṁhāra*, "the act of withdrawing, drawing together" (Monier-Williams), with the Malayalam verb ending -ിക്കുക.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
