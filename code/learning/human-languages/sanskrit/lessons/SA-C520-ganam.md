## You'll want to know: गानम्
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C520-SVARA520-04]; assesses=[] -->

**गानम्** — *gānam* — "singing".

> मम भगिन्याः गानं मधुरम् अस्ति।
>
> *mama bhaginyāḥ gānaṃ madhuram asti.*
>
> My sister's singing is sweet.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**गानम्** (*gānam*) comes from the root √gai, "to sing", which also gives *gāyati*; MW gives *gāna* as singing, a song (MW).
