Urdu, chapter 437: Bushes and Shade.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
جھاڑی (jhāṛī) — a bush.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a petal, then the Urdu for to wilt.

You'll want to know: جھاڑی.
جھاڑی — jhāṛī — "a bush".
سڑک کے کنارے جھاڑیاں ہیں۔ saṛak ke kināre jhāṛiyāṅ haiṅ. There are bushes along the road.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
جھاڑی (jhāṛī) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousins jhāṛ and jhāṛī, "a bush", under jhāṭa, "a forest, an arbour" (CDIAL 5362).

Lesson 5 of 5.
اگنا (ugnā) — to come up (of plants); to grow.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for dense, then the Urdu for moss.

You'll want to know: اگنا.
اگنا — ugnā — "to come up (of plants); to grow".
بارش کے بعد کھیتوں میں گھاس اگی۔ bārish ke bād khetoṅ meṅ ghās ugī. After the rain grass came up in the fields.
Past (it …): وہ پودا اگا, voh paudā ugā.

The word, taken apart.
اگنا (ugnā) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin ugānā, "to cause to spring up", under udgāti, "rises, comes forth", a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 1954); اگانا, "to grow (crops)", is the partner for making things come up.
