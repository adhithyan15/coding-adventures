Kannada, chapter 535: Instruments.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ವಾದ್ಯ (vādya) — a musical instrument.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to hum a tune, then the Kannada for to play an instrument.

You'll want to know: ವಾದ್ಯ.
ವಾದ್ಯ — vādya — "a musical instrument".
ಮದುವೆಯಲ್ಲಿ ವಾದ್ಯಗಳ ಸದ್ದು ಜೋರಾಗಿತ್ತು. maduveyalli vādyagaḷa saddu jōrāgittu. At the wedding the instruments were loud.
Plural: ವಾದ್ಯಗಳು, vādyagaḷu.
ವಾದ್ಯಗಾರ, a player.

The word, taken apart.
ವಾದ್ಯ (vādya) is borrowed from Sanskrit vādya, "to be played; music, a musical instrument" (CDIAL 11511).

Lesson 3 of 5.
ಕೊಳಲು (koḷalu) — a flute.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a musical instrument, then the Kannada for a veena.

You'll want to know: ಕೊಳಲು.
ಕೊಳಲು — koḷalu — "a flute".
ದನ ಕಾಯುವ ಹುಡುಗ ಕೊಳಲು ನುಡಿಸುತ್ತಿದ್ದನು. dana kāyuva huḍuga koḷalu nuḍisuttiddanu. The boy minding the cattle was playing a flute.
Made of bamboo, ಬಿದಿರು.

The word, taken apart.
ಕೊಳಲು (koḷalu) is native Dravidian: DEDR 1818 lists the Kannada word for a flute, a fife, beside a word for a tube, a blow-pipe; ಕೊಳವೆ, a pipe, is that second word.

Lesson 4 of 5.
ಪಿಟೀಲು (piṭīlu) — a violin.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a veena, then the Kannada for a flute.

You'll want to know: ಪಿಟೀಲು.
ಪಿಟೀಲು — piṭīlu — "a violin".
ಸಂಗೀತ ಕಚೇರಿಯಲ್ಲಿ ಅವರು ಪಿಟೀಲು ನುಡಿಸಿದರು. saṅgīta kacēriyalli avaru piṭīlu nuḍisidaru. He played the violin at the concert.
In southern concerts it follows the singer.

The word, taken apart.
ಪಿಟೀಲು (piṭīlu) is the Kannada name of the violin, which came to south India from Europe; the origin of the word is not settled.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ಬಾರಿಸು (bārisu) — to beat a drum; to ring.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: ಬಾರಿಸು.
ಬಾರಿಸು — bārisu — "to beat a drum; to ring".
ದೇವಸ್ಥಾನದಲ್ಲಿ ಒಬ್ಬರು ಗಂಟೆ ಬಾರಿಸಿದರು. dēvasthānadalli obbaru gaṇṭe bārisidaru. Someone rang the bell at the temple.
Past (I …): ಬಾರಿಸಿದೆ, bāriside.
Drums and bells take ಬಾರಿಸು (bārisu); strings and flutes take ನುಡಿಸು.

The word, taken apart.
ಬಾರಿಸು (bārisu) is native Dravidian: DEDR 5380 lists Kannada bāṟisu, "to strike or beat with a whip".
