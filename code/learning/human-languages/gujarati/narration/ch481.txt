Gujarati, chapter 481: Cuts and Scrapes.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
કપાવું (kapāvũ) — to get cut.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to rot, then the Gujarati for to puff up.

You'll want to know: કપાવું.
કપાવું — kapāvũ — "to get cut".
છરીથી મારી આંગળી કપાઈ ગઈ. charīthī mārī ā̃gaḷī kapāī gaī. I cut my finger on the knife.
Past (it …): તે આંગળી કપાઈ ગઈ, te ā̃gaḷī kapāī gaī.

The word, taken apart.
કપાવું (kapāvũ) is the intransitive partner of કાપવું (kāpvũ), "to cut", which goes back to Sanskrit kalpayati, "trims, cuts" (CDIAL 2944).

Lesson 2 of 5.
રુઝાવું (rujhāvũ) — to heal (of a wound).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to puff up, then the Gujarati for to get cut.

You'll want to know: રુઝાવું.
રુઝાવું — rujhāvũ — "to heal (of a wound)".
આ ઘા એક અઠવાડિયામાં રુઝાઈ જશે. ā ghā ek aṭhvāḍiyāmā̃ rujhāī jaśe. This wound will heal in a week.
Past (it …): તે ઘા રુઝાઈ ગયો, te ghā rujhāī gayo.

The word, taken apart.
રુઝાવું (rujhāvũ) is inherited from Sanskrit rudhyatē, "is stopped, is obstructed": a wound that closes. CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb, "to be healed, of a wound" (CDIAL 10779).

Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to get cut, then the Gujarati for to heal.

You'll want to know: ઘવાવું.
ઘવાવું — ghavāvũ — "to be wounded".
ગાડી અથડાઈ ત્યારે ડ્રાઈવર ઘવાયો. gāḍī athḍāī tyāre ḍrāīvar ghavāyo. When the car crashed, the driver was injured.
Past (he …): તે ઘવાયો, te ghavāyo.

The word, taken apart.
ઘવાવું (ghavāvũ) is built on ઘા (ghā), "a wound", which goes back to Sanskrit ghāta, "blow, bruise" (CDIAL 4460), with the verb ending -vāvũ.

You'll want to know: ઝરવું.
ઝરવું — jharvũ — "to ooze; to trickle".
ઘામાંથી હજી લોહી ઝરે છે. ghāmā̃thī hajī lohī jhare che. Blood is still oozing from the wound.
Past (it …): તે લોહી ધીમે ઝર્યું, te lohī dhīme jharyũ.

The word, taken apart.
ઝરવું (jharvũ) goes back to a reconstructed jharati; CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb, "to ooze, drip, shed, melt" (CDIAL 5346).

અરુણ પડી ગયો અને તેનો ઘૂંટણ છોલાઈ ગયો. Aruṇ paḍī gayo ane teno ghū̃ṭaṇ cholāī gayo. Arun fell and grazed his knee.

છોલાવું (cholāvũ) is the intransitive partner of છોલવું (cholvũ), "to peel", which goes back to a reconstructed chōll- (CDIAL 5073): skin that peels off.
