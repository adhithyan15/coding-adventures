## You'll want to know: reconciliar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C497-SEPARACAO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**reconciliar** — "to reconcile; to make up (reconciliar-se)".

> Depois de anos sem se falarem, os dois irmãos reconciliaram-se.
>
> *After years of not speaking, the two brothers made up.*

Everyday Portugal also says *fazer as pazes*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*reconciliar* comes from Latin *reconciliāre* "to bring together again" (EtymDB).
