## You'll want to know: どうぶつえん
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C499-PARK499-02]; assesses=[] -->

**どうぶつえん** — *dōbutsuen* — "a zoo".

> **どうぶつえんで さるを みました。** — *dōbutsuen de saru o mimashita* — "We saw monkeys at the zoo."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**どうぶつえん** (*dōbutsuen*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): three Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *dō*, "move" (Middle Chinese *duwngX*), *butsu*, "thing" (*mjut*) and *en*, "garden" (*hjwon*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
