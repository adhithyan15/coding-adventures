## You'll want to know: жать
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C453-PRIMERKA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**жать** (*zhat*) — "to pinch, to be too tight; to press".

> Новые туфли немного жмут.
>
> *Nóvyye túfli nemnógo zhmut.*
>
> The new shoes pinch a little.

Imperfective. Present *жму, жмёшь, жмут*. *Жать руку* — to shake hands. A different verb *жать* (*жну*) means to reap.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*жать* 'to press, to squeeze' is usually counted an inherited Slavic verb. EtymDB lists it without a source, so its deeper origin is left open here.
