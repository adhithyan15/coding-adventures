## You'll want to know: കുശുമ്പ്
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C523-PAKA523-04]; assesses=[] -->

**കുശുമ്പ്** — *kuśumpŭ* — "petty spite; envy".

> അനിയന്റെ പുതിയ സൈക്കിൾ കണ്ട് അവന് കുശുമ്പ് തോന്നി.
>
> *aniyanṟe putiya saikkiḷ kaṇṭŭ avanŭ kuśumpŭ tōnni.*
>
> He felt a pang of envy at his little brother's new bicycle.

Spoken and a little playful; അസൂയ is the general word for envy.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**കുശുമ്പ്** (*kuśumpŭ*) is a spoken word; DEDR has no clear entry for it, so its origin is not settled.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
