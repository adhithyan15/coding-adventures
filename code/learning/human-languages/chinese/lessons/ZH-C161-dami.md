## You'll want to know: 大米
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C161-RICE161-01]; assesses=[] -->

**大米** — *dàmǐ* — "uncooked rice; rice grain".

> **我去买大米** *wǒ qù mǎi dàmǐ* — I'm going to buy rice.

The new character, in pieces: 米 — six strokes: two short strokes slanting in toward the middle, a horizontal, a vertical through it, then from the crossing a stroke falling to the left and one falling to the right. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**大米** (*dàmǐ*) is built from 大 'big' and 米 'grain', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *daH* and *mejX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second as the seed of a cereal.
