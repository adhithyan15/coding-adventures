## You'll want to know: опровергать
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C280-SOOBSHCHAT-05]; assesses=[] -->

**опровергать** (*oprovergát*) — "to refute, to deny officially".

> Компания опровергает эту информацию.
>
> *Kompániya oprovergáyet étu informátsiyu.*
>
> The company refutes this information.

Imperfective; perfective *опровергнуть* (*oprovérgnut*). Stronger than *отрицать*: to show something is false.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*опровергать* is usually described as a Church Slavonic verb, built on *о-*, *про-* and *вергнуть* "to throw", so "to throw down"; EtymDB lists no parent.
