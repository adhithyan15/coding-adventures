Punjabi, chapter 113: Umbrella, Bowl, Glass, Cooking pot, Comb.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ਛਤਰੀ (chatrī) — an umbrella.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for to win, then the Punjabi for to lose.

You'll want to know: ਛਤਰੀ.
ਛਤਰੀ — chatrī — "an umbrella".
