## You'll want to know: बूझना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C374-ANDAZA374-05]; assesses=[] -->

**बूझना** — *būjhnā* — "to make out, to guess (a riddle)".

> अगर तुमने मेरी पहेली बूझ ली तो मैं हार मान लूँगा।
>
> *agar tumne merī pahelī būjh lī to maiṁ hār mān lūṅgā.*
>
> If you guess my riddle, I'll admit defeat.

Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने पहेली बूझ ली, *usne pahelī būjh lī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**बूझना** (*būjhnā*) is a tadbhava, inherited from Sanskrit *budhyate*, "wakes; understands" (CDIAL 9279).
