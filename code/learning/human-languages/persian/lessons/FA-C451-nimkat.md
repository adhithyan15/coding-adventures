## You'll want to know: نیمکت
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C451-SHOLUGHI-02]; assesses=[] -->

**نیمکت** (*nimkat*) — "a bench".

> پیرمرد روی نیمکت پارک نشسته بود.
>
> *Pirmard ru-ye nimkat-e pârk neshaste bud.*
>
> The old man was sitting on a bench in the park.

Also a school bench or desk.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*nimkat* is a native word that EtymDB lists without older forms. It is usually analysed as *nim* 'half' and *kat* 'couch, bed': a half-couch.
