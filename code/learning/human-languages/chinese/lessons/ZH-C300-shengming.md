## You'll want to know: shēngmìng
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C300-DEATH300-03]; assesses=[] -->

*shēngmìng* — "the life of a living thing".

> *měi ge rén de shēngmìng dōu hěn zhòngyào* — Every person's life matters.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*shēngmìng* is built from 生 'to live' and *mìng*, both inherited from Middle Chinese, *srjaeng* and *mjaengH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to grow' and the second 'to order'; its sense 'a life' is not given there.
