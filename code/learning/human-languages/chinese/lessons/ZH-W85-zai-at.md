## Script — the top of 有, a vertical, then 土
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-SCRIPT-ZAI-AT-01]; assesses=[] -->

> 在

**Three pieces, and two of them you already write.**

**The top of 有, two strokes**: a long horizontal, then a long left-falling stroke from above it, down to the lower left.

**A short vertical** under them, on the left, down from the falling stroke.

**土, three strokes**: a short horizontal, a vertical down through it, and a long bar along the foot. It is 十 standing on a bar.

2 + 1 + 3 = six.
