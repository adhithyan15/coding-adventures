## You'll want to know: la multa
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C477-DINERO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**la multa** — "the fine".

> Me pusieron una multa por aparcar mal.
>
> *They gave me a fine for parking badly.*

*Poner una multa* — to give a fine. *Aparcar* is the word in Spain; in Latin America people say *estacionar*.
