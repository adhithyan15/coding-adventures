## You'll Want to Know — hear and say
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-ICHI]; assesses=[JA-LEX-ICHIDO] -->

> **いち** — *ichi* — **one**

Say **もういちど**. You have asked for one more time since chapter nine, and that
lesson told you what the first two morae were: *ichi* is **one**. Nothing about
the word changes today. It stops being part of a phrase and starts being a
number you can use on its own.

Both signs are yours: **い + ち**.

## Guided Practice — read, copy, recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[JA-LEX-ICHI, JA-LEX-ICHIDO, JA-SCRIPT-I-01, JA-SCRIPT-CHI-01] -->

Read **い | ち**. Copy **いち**, hide it, and write it from the meaning. Then say
**いちど** and point to the part of it that is the number.
