Urdu, chapter 348: Objecting and Persuading.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
اعتراض (etirāz) — an objection.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for to get tangled, then the Urdu for to bring up a topic.

You'll want to know: اعتراض.
اعتراض — etirāz — "an objection".
کیا آپ کو کوئی اعتراض ہے؟ kyā āp ko koī etirāz hai? Do you have any objection?
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
اعتراض (etirāz) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic iʿtirāḍ, "standing across the way; objecting".

What you've built.
One more word for giving reasons and opinions.

Lesson 3 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: نقطہ نظر.
نقطہ نظر — nuqta-e-nazar — "a viewpoint; a point of view".
آپ کا نقطہ نظر کیا ہے؟ āp kā nuqta-e-nazar kyā hai? What is your point of view?
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
نقطہ نظر (nuqta-e-nazar) is built from two loans, each usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic: Arabic nuqṭa, "a dot, a point", and Arabic naẓar, "a look". The point you look from.

دھمکانا — dhamkānā — "to threaten".
اس نے دکاندار کو دھمکایا۔ us ne dukāndār ko dhamkāyā. He threatened the shopkeeper.
Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے مجھے دھمکایا, us ne mujhe dhamkāyā.

The word, taken apart.
دھمکانا (dhamkānā) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin dhamkī, "a threat", under dhammakka, a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 6736).

Lesson 5 of 5.
پھسلانا (phuslānā) — to coax; to wheedle.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a viewpoint, then the Urdu for to threaten.

You'll want to know: پھسلانا.
پھسلانا — phuslānā — "to coax; to wheedle".
اس نے پھسلا کر ابو سے پیسے لیے۔ us ne phuslā kar abbū se paise liye. She coaxed some money out of her father.
Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے بچے کو پھسلایا, us ne bachche ko phuslāyā.

The word, taken apart.
پھسلانا (phuslānā) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin phuslānā, "to cajole", under Sanskrit spṛśáti, "touches, strokes" (CDIAL 13815). To win someone over as one strokes an animal.
