Persian, chapter 191: On time, Appointment, Deadline, Nowruz, Yalda.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
سر وقت (sar-e vaqt) — on time.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for blood pressure, then the Persian for listless.

You'll want to know: سر وقت.
سر وقت — sar-e vaqt — "on time".

The word, taken apart.
sar-e vaqt is "at the head of the time". sar, "head", is inherited (Middle Persian sar, EtymDB); vaqt, "time", is borrowed from Arabic waqt (root w-q-t, Lane).

Lesson 4 of 5.
نوروز (nowruz) — Nowruz, the Persian New Year.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for an appointment, then the Persian for a deadline.

You'll want to know: نوروز.
نوروز — nowruz — "Nowruz, the Persian New Year".

The word, taken apart.
nowruz is now, "new", with ruz, "day" (EtymDB): the new day. Middle Persian already had nōg rōz, "New Year's day" (EtymDB). Both parts are inherited.

Lesson 5 of 5.
یلدا (yaldâ) — Yalda (the longest night, winter solstice).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a deadline, then the Persian for Nowruz.

You'll want to know: یلدا.
یلدا — yaldâ — "Yalda (the longest night, winter solstice)".

The word, taken apart.
yaldâ is usually traced to Syriac yaldā, "birth": the long night was linked with the feast of a birth, the Nativity.
