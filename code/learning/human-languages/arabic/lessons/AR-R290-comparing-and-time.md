# Review — bigger, better, and for how long

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[AR-GRAMMAR-C289-KANA-ACCUSATIVE-01, AR-GRAMMAR-C289-SHAARTU-BI-01, AR-GRAMMAR-C289-WISH-AN-01, AR-GRAMMAR-C289-BECOME-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "I was happy" and "I felt afraid". (*Kuntu saʿīdan*,
*shaʿartu bi-l-khawf*.) Then "I want to become an engineer". (*Urīdu an
uṣbiḥa muhandisan*.)

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[AR-GRAMMAR-C290-ELATIVE-01, AR-GRAMMAR-C290-SUPERLATIVE-01, AR-GRAMMAR-C290-MUNDHU-SPAN-01, AR-GRAMMAR-C290-QABLA-AGO-01, AR-LEX-C289-TAJRIBA289-01] -->

Two lessons, and an experience can be weighed and measured. Weighed with the
shape *aCCaC* and *min*: *akbar min*, *asraʿ min*, *afḍal min*; with *al-* or
before a noun for the most: *al-afḍal*, *akbar madīna*. Measured with
*mundhu* and the present, for what is still going on, and *qabla* with the
past, for "ago".

[PAUSE 2s] Both in one breath:

> **سافرت بالطائرة قبل سنة، وكانت أفضل تجربة.** — *sāfartu bi-ṭ-ṭāʾira qabla
> sana, wa-kānat afḍala tajriba* — I travelled by plane a year ago, and it
> was the best experience.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[AR-GRAMMAR-C290-ELATIVE-01, AR-GRAMMAR-C290-MUNDHU-SPAN-01, AR-LEX-C288-SAFAR288-02, AR-LEX-C282-AMAL282-02, AR-LEX-C281-AMAL281-03] -->

[PAUSE 1s]
- [YOU SAY: "The plane is faster than the train" — *aṭ-ṭāʾira asraʿ min al-qiṭār*]
- [YOU SAY: "This pencil case is smaller" — *hādhihi al-miqlama aṣghar*]
- [YOU SAY: "I have been waiting for the information since the morning" — *antaẓiru al-maʿlūmāt mundhu aṣ-ṣabāḥ*]
- [YOU SAY: something you did a year ago, and how long you have done something else]
