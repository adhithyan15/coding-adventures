## You'll want to know: ಬೆರಗು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C494-BERAGU494-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ಬೆರಗು** — *beragu* — "amazement; astonishment".

> ಜಲಪಾತವನ್ನು ನೋಡಿ ಮಕ್ಕಳು ಬೆರಗಾದರು.
>
> *jalapātavannu nōḍi makkaḷu beragādaru.*
>
> Seeing the waterfall, the children were amazed.

ಬೆರಗಾಗು, to be amazed: the noun joined to ಆಗು.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಬೆರಗು** (*beragu*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 5443 lists Kannada *beṟagu*, "amazement, astonishment, alarm", beside Tamil words for being bewildered.
