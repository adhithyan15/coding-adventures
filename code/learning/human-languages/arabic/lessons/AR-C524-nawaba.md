## You'll want to know: ناوب
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C524-TAAWUN524-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ناوب** — *nāwaba* — "to be on duty; to take turns".

> يناوب الطبيب في المستشفى مرة كل أسبوع.
>
> *yunāwibu aṭ-ṭabīb fī al-mustashfā marra kull usbūʿ.*
>
> The doctor is on duty at the hospital once a week.

Present: يناوب, *yunāwib*.

Munāwaba, a shift, is its verbal noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ناوب** (*nāwaba*) is built on the native Arabic root *n-w-b*, "to come in turn" (Lane). Form III nāwaba is to take one's turn.

## What you've built
