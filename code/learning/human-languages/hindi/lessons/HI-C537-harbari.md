## You'll want to know: हड़बड़ी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C537-KARYAKRAM537-03]; assesses=[] -->

**हड़बड़ी** — *haṛbaṛī* — "a flustered hurry".

> हड़बड़ी में मैं अपना बटुआ घर पर भूल गया।
>
> *Haṛbaṛī meṁ maiṁ apnā baṭuā ghar par bhūl gayā.*
>
> In my hurry I forgot my wallet at home.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**हड़बड़ी** (*haṛbaṛī*) is a tadbhava, inherited from *\*haḍabaḍa*, "confusion", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 13949). Today it is the confusion of someone short of time.
