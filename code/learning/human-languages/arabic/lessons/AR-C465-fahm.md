## You'll want to know: فحم
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C465-TAQA465-04]; assesses=[] -->

**فحم** — *faḥm* — "coal; charcoal".

> شوى أبي اللحم على الفحم.
>
> *shawā abī al-laḥm ʿalā al-faḥm.*
>
> My father grilled the meat over charcoal.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**فحم** (*faḥm*) is built on the native Arabic root *f-ḥ-m*, "charcoal" (Lane). EtymDB lists a related Hebrew word for coal.
