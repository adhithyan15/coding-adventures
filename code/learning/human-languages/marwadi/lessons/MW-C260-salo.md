## You'll want to know: साळो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C260-BAHU260-05]; assesses=[] -->

**साळो** — *sāḷo* — "a wife's brother".

> म्हारो साळो पुलिस मांय है।
>
> *mhāro sāḷo pulis mā̃y hai.*
>
> My wife's brother is in the police.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**साळो** (*sāḷo*) is inherited from Sanskrit *syāla*, "a wife's brother". CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry; it lists Gujarati *sāḷo*, with this book's ळ (CDIAL 13871).
