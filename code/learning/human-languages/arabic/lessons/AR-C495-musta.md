## You'll want to know: مستاء
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C495-INZIAJ495-03]; assesses=[] -->

**مستاء** — *mustāʾ* — "displeased, upset".

> الزبون مستاء من الخدمة.
>
> *az-zabūn mustāʾ min al-khidma.*
>
> The customer is unhappy with the service.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**مستاء** (*mustāʾ*) is built on the native Arabic root *s-w-ʾ*, "to be evil, to be bad" (Lane). Mustāʾ is the participle of Form VIII, istāʾa: one who takes a thing badly.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
