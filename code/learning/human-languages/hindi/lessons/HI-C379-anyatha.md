## You'll want to know: अन्यथा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C379-YADI379-02]; assesses=[] -->

**अन्यथा** — *anyathā* — "otherwise (formal)".

> समय पर किराया दीजिए, अन्यथा घर छोड़ना पड़ेगा।
>
> *samay par kirāyā dījiye, anyathā ghar choṛnā paṛegā.*
>
> Pay the rent on time; otherwise you will have to leave the house.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अन्यथा** (*anyathā*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *anyathā*, "otherwise, in another way" (Monier-Williams).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
