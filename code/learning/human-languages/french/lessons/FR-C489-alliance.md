## You'll want to know: l'alliance
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C489-NOCES-02]; assesses=[] -->

**l'alliance** — "wedding ring; alliance".

> Il porte son alliance à la main gauche.
>
> *He wears his wedding ring on his left hand.*

Feminine. *Une bague de fiançailles* — an engagement ring.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*l'alliance* is built on *allier* "to unite", inherited from Latin *alligāre* "to bind to", built on *ligāre* "to tie" (Lewis & Short).
