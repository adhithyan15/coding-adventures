## You'll want to know: टिकट
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C69-GOING-04]; assesses=[HI-CONCEPT-C37-CHAI-02] -->

**टिकट** (*ṭikaṭ*) — "a ticket".

English *ticket*, and both of its *t* sounds land on retroflex ट for the reason
the last lesson gave. The ि in the middle is the short *i* matra, and it is
written **before** the letter it is sounded after — you learnt that trick when
you first wrote a name.

This is the word you actually say at a window. You already know how to ask for
one thing politely: **एक चाय दीजिए** got you a cup of tea. **एक टिकट दीजिए** is
the same frame with a new word dropped in, and it will get you onto a train.
