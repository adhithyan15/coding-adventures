## You'll want to know: caccabus, caccabī
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C353-FERVEO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**caccabus, caccabī** — "a cooking pot".

> Caccabus super focum pendēbat.
>
> *A cooking pot was hanging over the hearth.*

Masculine, a Greek loan: a round pot of bronze or clay.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**caccabus** was borrowed from Greek *kakkabos*, "a pot, a pan" (EtymDB).
