Gujarati, chapter 335: The Wise Old Man.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
સાધુ (sādhu) — a holy man.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to tempt, then the Gujarati for to wander.

You'll want to know: સાધુ.
સાધુ — sādhu — "a holy man".
ગામ બહાર એક સાધુ રહેતો હતો. gām bahār ek sādhu raheto hato. A holy man lived outside the village.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
સાધુ (sādhu) is a tatsama, Sanskrit sādhu, "straight, good", and as a noun "a holy man" (Monier-Williams). He gives up the world and wanders, or lives apart.

શ્રાપ — śrāp — "a curse".
સાધુએ રાજાને શ્રાપ આપ્યો. sādhue rājāne śrāp āpyo. The holy man put a curse on the king.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
શ્રાપ (śrāp) goes back to Sanskrit śāpa, "a curse" (CDIAL 12393); the Sanskrit word has no r, and how the Gujarati form got one is not settled. A curse is given: શ્રાપ આપ્યો.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a curse, then the Gujarati for a moral.

You'll want to know: કહેવત.
કહેવત — kahevat — "a proverb".
મારા દાદાએ એક કહેવત કહી. mārā dādāe ek kahevat kahī. My grandfather quoted a proverb.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
કહેવત (kahevat) is built on કહેવું, to say, from Sanskrit kathayati, "converses, describes" (CDIAL 2703): a saying everybody knows. It is feminine: એક કહેવત કહી.

Lesson 5 of 5.
સંભળાવવું (sambhaḷāvvũ) — to recite; to tell (a story) aloud.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a moral, then the Gujarati for a proverb.

You'll want to know: સંભળાવવું.
સંભળાવવું — sambhaḷāvvũ — "to recite; to tell (a story) aloud".
સાધુએ બાળકોને એક વાર્તા સંભળાવી. sādhue bāḷkone ek vārtā sambhḷāvī. The holy man told the children a story.
Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે વાર્તા સંભળાવી, teṇe vārtā sambhḷāvī.

The word, taken apart.
સંભળાવવું (sambhaḷāvvũ) is the causative of સાંભળવું, to hear, from saṁbhalati, "attends to", a form CDIAL reconstructs (CDIAL 12962): to make others hear, to tell a story aloud. The long ā of સાંભળવું shortens in the longer verb.
