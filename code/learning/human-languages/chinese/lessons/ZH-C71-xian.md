## You'll want to know: xián
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C71-TASTE71-04]; assesses=[] -->

*xián* — "salty".

> *jīntiān de tāng hěn xián* — Today's soup is salty.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*xián* is inherited from Middle Chinese *heam* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'not bland'; the dictionary writes it with the traditional form of the character.
