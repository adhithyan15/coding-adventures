# झीणो (jhīṇo) — fine (of sand or cloth); thin

## You'll want to know: झीणो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C229-LIGHT229-03]; assesses=[] -->

**झीणो** — *jhīṇo* — "fine (of sand or cloth); thin".

> थळ मांय रेत झीणी है।
>
> *thaḷ mā̃y ret jhīṇī hai.*
>
> The sand in the desert is fine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**झीणो** (*jhīṇo*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*jhīna*, "wasted, thin"; CDIAL lists Old Marwari *jhīṇa*, "thin", and Gujarati *jhīṇũ*, "thin, fine" (CDIAL 5395).
