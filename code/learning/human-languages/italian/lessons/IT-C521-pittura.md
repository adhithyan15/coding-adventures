## You'll want to know: la pittura
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C521-PITTURA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**la pittura** — "painting; paint".

> La pittura mi rilassa molto.
>
> *Painting relaxes me a lot.*

Also wet paint: *pittura fresca*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*pittura* is inherited from Latin *pictūra* "the art of painting" (EtymDB), from *pingere* "to paint".
