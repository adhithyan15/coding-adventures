## You'll want to know: постоянный
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C380-BUDUSHCHEYE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**постоянный** (*postoyánnyy*) — "permanent, constant".

> У него нет постоянной работы.
>
> *U negó net postoyánnoy rabóty.*
>
> He does not have a permanent job.

*Постоянно* — all the time.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**постоянный** is built from *по-* and *стоять* "to stand", inherited from Proto-Slavic *\*stojati* (EtymDB): what stays standing.
