## You'll want to know: けつあつ

**けつあつ** — *ketsuatsu* — "blood pressure".

> **そふは けつあつが たかいです。** — *sofu wa ketsuatsu ga takai desu* — "My grandfather has high blood pressure."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**けつあつ** (*ketsuatsu*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *ketsu*, "blood" (Middle Chinese *xwet*) and *atsu*, "pressure" (*'aep*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
