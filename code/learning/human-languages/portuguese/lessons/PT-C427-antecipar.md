## You'll want to know: antecipar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C427-RESERVA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**antecipar** — "to bring forward; to anticipate".

> Antecipámos o regresso por causa do tempo.
>
> *We brought our return forward because of the weather.*

*Com antecedência* — in advance.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*antecipar* is a learned borrowing from Latin *anticipāre* 'to take beforehand' (EtymDB), from *ante* 'before' and *capere* 'to take'.
