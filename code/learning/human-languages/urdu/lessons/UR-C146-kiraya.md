## You'll want to know: کرایہ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C146-PARH146-03]; assesses=[] -->

**کرایہ** — *kirāyā* — "rent; a fare". The money for a room, or for a ride: **کمرے کا کرایہ** — *kamre kā kirāyā* — "the rent for the room".
