## You'll want to know: illegale
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C390-LEGALE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**illegale** — "illegal".

> Scaricare film senza pagare è illegale.
>
> *Downloading films without paying is illegal.*

*In- + legale*: before *l* the *n* becomes *l*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*illegale* is a learned borrowing from Medieval Latin *illēgālis* "unlawful", built from *in-* "not" and *lēgālis*, on *lēx, lēgis* "law" (Lewis & Short).
