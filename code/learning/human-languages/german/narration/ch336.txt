German, chapter 336: Eine Einigung finden.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
die Einigung — the agreement (that is reached).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to suggest, then the German for to stress.

You'll want to know: die Einigung.
die Einigung — "the agreement (that is reached)".
Nach drei Stunden kamen sie endlich zu einer Einigung. After three hours they finally reached an agreement.
Plural: die Einigungen. It belongs with sich einigen — to come to an agreement.

The word, taken apart.
Einigung is the noun of sich einigen, from einig "at one, united", built on ein "one", inherited from Proto-Germanic ainaz (EtymDB): two sides become one.

Lesson 2 of 5.
nachgeben — to give in, to give way.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to stress, then the German for the agreement.

You'll want to know: nachgeben.
nachgeben — "to give in, to give way".
Am Ende hat meine Schwester nachgegeben, und wir haben den Film gesehen. In the end my sister gave in, and we watched the film.
Separable and strong: sie gibt nach, sie hat nachgegeben. Also of things: Der Boden gibt nach — the ground gives way.

The word, taken apart.
nachgeben is built from nach "after" and geben "to give", inherited from Old High German geban and Proto-Germanic gebaną; EtymDB glosses the compound "to give way, to stretch".

Lesson 3 of 5.
abwägen — to weigh up.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for the agreement, then the German for to give in.

You'll want to know: abwägen.
abwägen — "to weigh up".
Sie wägt die Vorteile und Nachteile genau ab. She weighs up the advantages and disadvantages carefully.
Separable: sie wägt ab. Perfekt: sie hat abgewogen (rarely abgewägt).

The word, taken apart.
abwägen is built on wägen "to weigh", an older form beside wiegen, from Proto-Germanic weganą "to move, to carry, to weigh"; EtymDB lists English weigh and Dutch wegen as its cousins.

Lesson 4 of 5.
einerseits — on the one hand.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to give in, then the German for to weigh up.

You'll want to know: einerseits.
einerseits — "on the one hand".
Einerseits ist die Wohnung schön, auf der anderen Seite ist sie sehr teuer. On the one hand the flat is nice; on the other, it is very expensive.
An adverb: the verb comes right after it, einerseits ist …. It usually has a partner later in the sentence, such as auf der anderen Seite or aber.

The word, taken apart.
einerseits is built from ein "one" and Seite "side", inherited from Old High German sīta and Proto-Germanic sīdōn (EtymDB), with the adverb ending -s: on one side.

Lesson 5 of 5.
andererseits — on the other hand.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to weigh up, then the German for on the one hand.

You'll want to know: andererseits.
andererseits — "on the other hand".
Ich möchte gern ein Auto. Andererseits fahre ich gern Fahrrad. I would like a car. On the other hand, I like cycling.
The partner of einerseits, but it can also stand alone. The verb comes right after it: andererseits fahre ich ….

The word, taken apart.
andererseits joins ander "other" to Seite "side", from Old High German sīta and Proto-Germanic sīdōn (EtymDB), with the adverb ending -s.
