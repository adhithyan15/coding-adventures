Portuguese, chapter 470: Humildes e modestos.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
humilde — humble.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for miserly, then the Portuguese for stingy.

You'll want to know: humilde.
humilde — "humble".
Apesar do sucesso, a cantora continua humilde. Despite her success, the singer is still humble.
Of a family or a house it means poor: uma casa humilde.

The word, taken apart.
humilde is inherited from Latin humilis "low, lowly" (EtymDB), built on humus "earth, ground".

You'll want to know: modesto.

The word, taken apart.

Lesson 5 of 5.
elogiar — to praise.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for modest, then the Portuguese for modesty.

You'll want to know: elogiar.
elogiar — "to praise".
O professor elogiou o meu trabalho. The teacher praised my work.
The noun is o elogio — a compliment, praise.

The word, taken apart.
elogiar is built on elogio "praise", a learned borrowing from Latin ēlogium "a short saying, an inscription on a tomb" (Lewis & Short).
