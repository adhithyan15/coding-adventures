## You'll want to know: れんが
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C488-BUILD488-03]; assesses=[] -->

**れんが** — *renga* — "a brick".

> **この えきは れんがの たてものです。** — *kono eki wa renga no tatemono desu* — "This station is a brick building."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**れんが** (*renga*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *ren*, "refine metals" and *ga*, "tile" (Middle Chinese *ngwaeX*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
