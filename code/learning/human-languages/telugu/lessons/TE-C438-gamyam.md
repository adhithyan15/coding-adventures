## You'll want to know: గమ్యం
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C438-PRAYANAM438-03]; assesses=[] -->

**గమ్యం** — *gamyaṁ* — "a destination".

> మా గమ్యం ఇంకా చాలా దూరంలో ఉంది.
>
> *mā gamyaṁ iṅkā cālā dūraṁlō undi.*
>
> Our destination is still a long way off.

A formal word; in everyday talk people ask where you are going: ఎక్కడికి వెళ్తున్నారు?

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**గమ్యం** (*gamyaṁ*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *gamya*, "to be gone to, reachable", from the root *gam*, "to go" (CDIAL 4032). The place one can go to became the place one is going to.
