## You'll want to know: قصر
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C309-QASR309-02]; assesses=[] -->

**قصر** — *qaṣr* — "a palace".

> زرنا قصرا قديما قرب البحر.
>
> *zurnā qaṣran qadīman qurba al-baḥr.*
>
> We visited an old palace near the sea.

Plural: قصور, *quṣūr*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**قصر** (*qaṣr*) is filed by Lane under the root *q-ṣ-r*, "to confine, to keep within". It was borrowed from Aramaic *qaṣrā* (EtymDB), which is usually traced to Latin *castra*, a camp; the Latin step is not settled.
