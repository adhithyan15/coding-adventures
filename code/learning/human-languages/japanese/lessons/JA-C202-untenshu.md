## You'll want to know: うんてんしゅ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C202-DRIVE202-03]; assesses=[] -->

**うんてんしゅ** — *untenshu* — "a driver".

> **ちちは うんてんしゅです。** — *chichi wa untenshu desu* — "My father is a driver."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**うんてんしゅ** (*untenshu*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): three Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *un*, "to carry" (Middle Chinese *hjunH*), *ten*, "to turn" (*trjwenX*) and *shu*, "hand" (*syuwX*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
