## You'll want to know: あきれる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C431-SHOCK431-03]; assesses=[] -->

**あきれる** — *akireru* — "to be dumbfounded; to be appalled".

> **かれの うそに みんな あきれました。** — *kare no uso ni minna akiremashita* — "Everyone was dumbfounded by his lie."

Polite **あきれます**, *akiremasu*; an ichidan verb. Past (polite): **せんせいが あきれました。**, *sensei ga akiremashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**あきれる** (*akireru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji spelling was attached to it later (JMdict).
