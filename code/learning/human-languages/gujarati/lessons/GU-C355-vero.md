## You'll want to know: વેરો
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C355-VERO355-01]; assesses=[] -->

**વેરો** — *vero* — "a tax".

> દરેક વરસે અમારે ઘરનો વેરો ભરવો પડે છે.
>
> *darek varse amāre gharno vero bharvo paḍe che.*
>
> Every year we have to pay the house tax.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**વેરો** (*vero*) No dictionary we use records an older form, so its history is not settled.
