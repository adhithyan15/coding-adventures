Arabic, chapter 223: To tidy, To sweep, To iron, To fold, To hang.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
رتب (rattaba) — to tidy, to arrange.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for literature, then the Arabic for a merchant, a trader.

You'll want to know: رتب.
رتب — rattaba — "to tidy, to arrange".

The word, taken apart.
رتب (rattaba) is built on the native Arabic root r-t-b, "to be fixed, to be firm" (Lane). Rattaba is Form II, "to set in order".
