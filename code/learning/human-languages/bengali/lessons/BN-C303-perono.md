# পেরোনো (perono) — to pass by; to go past

## You'll want to know: পেরোনো
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C303-SEDIN303-03]; assesses=[] -->

**পেরোনো** — *perono* — "to pass by; to go past".

> বছর পেরোল, কিন্তু রাজকুমার ফিরল না।
>
> *bôchhôr perolô, kintu rājkumār phirlô nā.*
>
> Years went by, but the prince did not return.

Past (he/she …): সেই নৌকা নদী পেরোল, *sei noukā nôdī perolô*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**পেরোনো** (*perono*) is usually explained as built on পার, 'the far bank' — Sanskrit *pāra* (CDIAL 8100) — with the verb ending: to get to the far side of a river, or of a stretch of time.
