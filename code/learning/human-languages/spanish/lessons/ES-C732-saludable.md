## You'll want to know: saludable
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C732-SALUD-01]; assesses=[] -->

**saludable** — "healthy".

> Como de forma saludable.
>
> *I eat in a healthy way.*

For food and habits; a healthy person is *sano*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*saludable* is built on *salud* "health" with the ending *-able*; *salud* comes from Latin *salūs*, *salūtem* "health, safety" (EtymDB).
