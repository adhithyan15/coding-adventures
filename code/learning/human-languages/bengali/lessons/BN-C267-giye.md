# বাড়ি গিয়ে খেলাম (bāṛi giye khelām) — one action, then the next

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[BN-GRAMMAR-C266-SCENE-EVENT-01, BN-GRAMMAR-C13-KORE-02] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "I was reading when Mira came". (*jôkhon āmi bôi pôṛchhilām, tôkhon mīrā elô*.) Then the word for "please", **দয়া করে**: "having done a kindness". Its **করে** is the form of this lesson.

## You'll want to know: the -e form
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-GRAMMAR-C267-CONJUNCTIVE-01]; assesses=[BN-LEX-C82-PLACE82-05, BN-LEX-C128-THINGS128-05, BN-LEX-C49-VERBS49-02, BN-GRAMMAR-C13-KORE-02] -->

> **আমি স্টেশনে গিয়ে টিকিট কিনলাম।**
>
> *āmi sṭeshône giye ṭikiṭ kinlām.* — "I went to the station and bought a ticket."

**গিয়ে** is **যাওয়া** in the *-e* form: "having gone". It finishes one action so the next can start, and the last verb carries the tense for both. Most verbs add **-এ** to the stem: **করা** → **করে** (*kore*), **দেখা** → **দেখে** (*dekhe*), **বসা** → **বসে** (*bose*). An **আ** in the stem becomes **এ**: **আসা** → **এসে** (*ese*), **নামা** → **নেমে** (*neme*), **হাঁটা** → **হেঁটে** (*hẽṭe*). An **এ** said *e*, or an **ও**, rises: **কেনা** → **কিনে** (*kine*). The verbs whose stem ends in a vowel take **-য়ে**: **খাওয়া** → **খেয়ে** (*kheye*), **নেওয়া** → **নিয়ে** (*niye*), **দেওয়া** → **দিয়ে** (*diye*).

The *-e* form can also say how: **হেঁটে গেলাম** (*hẽṭe gelām*), "I went on foot". And it pairs with a second verb that adds a shade rather than a new action: **বসে পড়লাম** (*bose pôṛlām*), "I sat down", with **পড়া** in its other sense, "to fall"; **হারিয়ে গেল**, "it got lost", which you know.

## You'll want to know: one doer
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-GRAMMAR-C267-SAME-DOER-01]; assesses=[BN-LEX-C07-KHAOWA-01, BN-LEX-C59-FOOD59-04, BN-GRAMMAR-C264-PAST-PERSON-01] -->

Both actions belong to the same person: **আমি বাড়ি গিয়ে খেলাম** (*āmi bāṛi giye khelām*), "I went home and ate". When someone else does the second thing, Bengali says two sentences: **আমি বাড়ি গেলাম। মীরা খেল।**

[PAUSE 1s]
- [YOU SAY: "I got off the bus and walked home" — *āmi bās theke neme hẽṭe bāṛi gelām*]
- [YOU SAY: "She ate and went out" — *she kheye bāire gelô*]
- [YOU SAY: "We sat and drank tea" — *āmrā bose chā khelām*]

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[BN-GRAMMAR-C267-CONJUNCTIVE-01, BN-GRAMMAR-C267-SAME-DOER-01] -->

[PAUSE 3s] **যাওয়া** in the *-e* form? (**গিয়ে**.) **খাওয়া**? (**খেয়ে**.) Which verb carries the tense? (The last one.) Can the two actions have two doers? (No: then it is two sentences.)
