## You'll want to know: ਇਤਰਾਜ਼
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C344-ARGUE344-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ਇਤਰਾਜ਼** — *itrāz* — "an objection".

> ਮੈਨੂੰ ਕੋਈ ਇਤਰਾਜ਼ ਨਹੀਂ ਹੈ।
>
> *mainū̃ koī itrāz nahīṁ hai.*
>
> I have no objection.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਇਤਰਾਜ਼** (*itrāz*) is a loan, usually traced through Persian to Arabic *iʿtirāḍ*, "standing across".
