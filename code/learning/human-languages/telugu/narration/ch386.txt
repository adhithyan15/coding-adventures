Telugu, chapter 386: Disagreeing.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
విభేదించు (vibhēdiñcu) — to disagree.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to consent, then the Telugu for backing.

You'll want to know: విభేదించు.
విభేదించు — vibhēdiñcu — "to disagree".
ఆ విషయంలో నేను మీతో విభేదిస్తాను. ā viṣayaṁlō nēnu mītō vibhēdistānu. On that matter I disagree with you.
Past (I …): విభేదించాను, vibhēdiñcānu.
Like ఏకీభవించు, it takes -తో: మీతో విభేదిస్తాను.

The word, taken apart.
విభేదించు (vibhēdiñcu) is built on Sanskrit vibheda, "a breaking asunder, a division" (Monier-Williams), with the Telugu verb ending -ఇంచు.

Lesson 2 of 5.
అభ్యంతరం (abhyantaraṁ) — an objection.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for backing, then the Telugu for to disagree.

You'll want to know: అభ్యంతరం.
అభ్యంతరం — abhyantaraṁ — "an objection".
మీకు ఏమైనా అభ్యంతరం ఉందా? mīku ēmainā abhyantaraṁ undā? Do you have any objection?
Plural: అభ్యంతరాలు, abhyantarālu.
A polite opening: మీకు అభ్యంతరం (abhyantaraṁ) లేకపోతే, if you have no objection.

The word, taken apart.
అభ్యంతరం (abhyantaraṁ) is borrowed from Sanskrit abhyantara, "interior", and as a noun "the inside" (CDIAL 544). In Telugu the word has taken the sense of an objection.

Lesson 3 of 5.
ఆక్షేపించు (ākṣēpiñcu) — to object; to take exception.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to disagree, then the Telugu for an objection.

You'll want to know: ఆక్షేపించు.
ఆక్షేపించు — ākṣēpiñcu — "to object; to take exception".
కొంత మంది ఆ కొత్త నియమాన్ని ఆక్షేపించారు. konta mandi ā kotta niyamānni ākṣēpiñcāru. Some people objected to the new rule.
Past (I …): ఆక్షేపించాను, ākṣēpiñcānu.
Formal and written; in talk people say అభ్యంతరం (abhyantaraṁ) చెప్పు.

The word, taken apart.
ఆక్షేపించు (ākṣēpiñcu) is built on Sanskrit ākṣepa, "a drawing together; a throwing away", and also "reviling, objection" (Monier-Williams), with the Telugu verb ending -ఇంచు.

Lesson 4 of 5.
వ్యతిరేకత (vyatirēkata) — opposition; resistance.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for an objection, then the Telugu for to object.

You'll want to know: వ్యతిరేకత.
వ్యతిరేకత — vyatirēkata — "opposition; resistance".
ఆ ప్రతిపాదనకు చాలా వ్యతిరేకత వచ్చింది. ā pratipādanaku cālā vyatirēkata vaccindi. That proposal met a lot of opposition.
The noun of వ్యతిరేకించు, to oppose, which you know; -త makes an abstract noun.

The word, taken apart.
వ్యతిరేకత (vyatirēkata) is built on Sanskrit vyatireka, "distinction, separateness; contrast" (Monier-Williams), with the abstract ending -త; the verb వ్యతిరేకించు comes from the same word.

Lesson 5 of 5.
తిరస్కరించు (tiraskariñcu) — to reject; to turn down.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to object, then the Telugu for opposition.

You'll want to know: తిరస్కరించు.
తిరస్కరించు — tiraskariñcu — "to reject; to turn down".
కంపెనీ నా దరఖాస్తును తిరస్కరించింది. kampenī nā darakhāstunu tiraskariñcindi. The company rejected my application.
Past (I …): తిరస్కరించాను, tiraskariñcānu.
Firm and formal: it goes with applications, offers and ideas.

The word, taken apart.
తిరస్కరించు (tiraskariñcu) is built on Sanskrit tiraskāra, "a placing aside", and also "disdain" (Monier-Williams), with the Telugu verb ending -ఇంచు.
