## You'll want to know: ਦੀਵਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C234-DIN234-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ਦੀਵਾ** — *dīvā* — "an oil lamp".

> ਕਮਰੇ ਵਿੱਚ ਇੱਕ ਛੋਟਾ ਦੀਵਾ ਹੈ।
>
> *kamre vich ikk chhoṭā dīvā hai.*
>
> There is a small oil lamp in the room.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਦੀਵਾ** (*dīvā*) is inherited from Sanskrit *dīpa*, "lamp"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *dīvā*, "lamp" (CDIAL 6348).
