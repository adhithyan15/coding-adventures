## You'll want to know: पश्चात्ताप
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C457-KHED457-02]; assesses=[] -->

**पश्चात्ताप** — *paścāttāp* — "remorse".

> रागात बोलल्याचा त्याला नंतर पश्चात्ताप झाला.
>
> *rāgāt bollyācā tyālā nantar paścāttāp jhālā.*
>
> Later he felt remorse for speaking in anger.

Masculine.

पश्चात्, after, + ताप, burning: the pain that comes afterwards. पश्चात्ताप होणे, with the dative.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पश्चात्ताप** (*paścāttāp*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *paścāttāpa*, "after-pain, repentance" (Monier-Williams), built on *tāpa*, "heat, pain".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
