Latin, chapter 230: To wake up, To accompany, To add, To announce, To appear.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
expergīscor, expergīscī — to wake up.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Latin for to hold off, then the Latin for to remove.

You'll want to know: expergīscor, expergīscī.
expergīscor, expergīscī — "to wake up".

The word, taken apart.
expergīscor is built on expergō, "to wake up" (Lewis & Short), with -scor, the ending of verbs of becoming; expergō is often explained as ex, "out", with pergō, "to go on".

You'll want to know: comitor, comitārī.
comitor, comitārī — "to accompany".

The word, taken apart.
comitor is built on comes, comitis, "a companion" (Lewis & Short): to be someone's companion. comes is con-, "together", with the root of eō, "to go": one who goes along.
