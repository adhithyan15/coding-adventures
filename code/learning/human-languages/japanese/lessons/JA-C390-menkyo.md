## You'll want to know: めんきょ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C390-KOUSOKU390-04]; assesses=[] -->

**めんきょ** — *menkyo* — "a licence; a driving licence".

> **あには くるまの めんきょを もっています。** — *ani wa kuruma no menkyo o motte imasu* — "My brother has a driving licence."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**めんきょ** (*menkyo*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *men*, "excuse" (Middle Chinese *mjenX*) and *kyo*, "permit" (*xjoX*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
