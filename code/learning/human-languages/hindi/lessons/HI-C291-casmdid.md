## You'll want to know: चश्मदीद
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C291-GAVAHI291-01]; assesses=[] -->

**चश्मदीद** — *caśmdīd* — "an eyewitness".

> अगर कोई चश्मदीद होता तो पुलिस का काम आसान होता।
>
> *agar koī caśmdīd hotā to pulis kā kām āsān hotā.*
>
> If there had been an eyewitness, the police's job would have been easy.

Masculine or feminine, by the person meant.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**चश्मदीद** (*caśmdīd*) is usually traced to Persian *čašm-dīd*, built from *čašm*, "eye", the word behind **चश्मा**, and *dīd*, "seen": a person who saw it with their own eyes.
