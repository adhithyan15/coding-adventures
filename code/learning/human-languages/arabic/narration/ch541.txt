Arabic, chapter 541: At the exhibition.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
فنان (fannān) — an artist.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for passion, then the Arabic for fondness.

You'll want to know: فنان.
فنان — fannān — "an artist".
رسم الفنان صورة لجدتي. rasama al-fannān ṣūra li-jaddatī. The artist painted a picture of my grandmother.

The word, taken apart.
فنان (fannān) is built on the native Arabic root f-n-n, "a sort, a kind; a branch" (Lane). Fann, an art, which you know, is from the same root.

Lesson 2 of 5.
معرض (maʿriḍ) — an exhibition.

Warm-up.

Before the new one: say the Arabic for fondness, then the Arabic for an artist.

You'll want to know: معرض.
معرض — maʿriḍ — "an exhibition".
زرنا معرض الكتاب في المدينة. zurnā maʿriḍ al-kitāb fī al-madīna. We visited the book fair in the city.
Plural: معارض, maʿāriḍ.
معرض (maʿriḍ) الكتاب is a book fair.

The word, taken apart.
معرض (maʿriḍ) is built on the native Arabic root ʿ-r-ḍ, "to appear, to come in the way" (Lane). A maʿriḍ is a place where things are shown.

The noun نحت (naḥata), read naḥt, is sculpture.

The word, taken apart.
نحت (naḥata) is built on the native Arabic root n-ḥ-t, "to hew, to carve" (Lane). Lane gives yanḥitu as its present.
