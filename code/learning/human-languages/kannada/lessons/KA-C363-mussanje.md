# ಮುಸ್ಸಂಜೆ (mussañje) — dusk; twilight

## You'll want to know: ಮುಸ್ಸಂಜೆ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C363-SANJE363-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ಮುಸ್ಸಂಜೆ** — *mussañje* — "dusk; twilight".

> ಮುಸ್ಸಂಜೆಯಲ್ಲಿ ಹಕ್ಕಿಗಳು ಗೂಡಿಗೆ ಹಿಂತಿರುಗುತ್ತವೆ.
>
> *mussañjeyalli hakkigaḷu gūḍige hintiruguttave.*
>
> At dusk the birds go back to their nests.

ಮುನ್, before, and ಸಂಜೆ, evening: the time as evening comes on.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಮುಸ್ಸಂಜೆ** (*mussañje*) is a Kannada compound of native *mun*, "before" (DEDR 5020), and ಸಂಜೆ, "evening", borrowed from Sanskrit *saṃdhyā*, "juncture, twilight" (Monier-Williams).
