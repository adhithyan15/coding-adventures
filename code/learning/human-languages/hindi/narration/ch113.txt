Hindi, chapter 113: Nothing, Not Yet, Maybe.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
कुछ नहीं (kuch nahīṁ) — nothing.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for really, then the Hindi for oh.

You'll want to know: कुछ नहीं.
कुछ नहीं (kuch nahīṁ) — "nothing".
क्या हुआ? — कुछ नहीं (kuch nahīṁ). — "what happened? — nothing". It ends on the नहीं you know.

What you've built.
An answer that closes a question kindly.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: kuch nahīṁ]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: kuch nahīṁ, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say kuch nahīṁ]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Hindi for really, then the Hindi for oh, then say kuch nahīṁ again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does कुछ नहीं (kuch nahīṁ) mean? ("Nothing".) Say it once more.

Lesson 3 of 5.
काफ़ी (kāfī) — enough; quite.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for nothing, then the Hindi for not yet.

You'll want to know: काफ़ी.
काफ़ी (kāfī) — "enough", and before an adjective, "quite".
बस, काफ़ी (kāfī) है — "that's enough", with the बस you know. काफ़ी (kāfī) अच्छा — "quite good".

What you've built.
Enough, and quite.

Lesson 4 of 5.
वही (vahī) — that very one, the same.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for not yet, then the Hindi for enough.

You'll want to know: वही.
वही (vahī) — "that very one", "the same".
It is वह, "that", with ही, "exactly". When someone names what you meant: हाँ, वही (vahī)!

What you've built.
That one, exactly.

Lesson 5 of 5.
हो सकता है (ho saktā hai) — maybe, it could be.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for enough, then the Hindi for that very one.

You'll want to know: हो सकता है.
हो सकता है (ho saktā hai) — "maybe", literally "it can be".
It is होना, "to be", and the सकना of "can". Compare शायद, "perhaps".

What you've built.
Nothing, not yet, enough, that one, maybe.
