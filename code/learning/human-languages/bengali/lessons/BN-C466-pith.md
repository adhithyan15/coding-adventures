## You'll want to know: পিঠ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C466-SHORIR466-04]; assesses=[] -->

**পিঠ** — *piṭh* — "the back (of the body)".

> ভারী ব্যাগটা তুলে বাবার পিঠে ব্যথা হল।
>
> *bhārī byāgṭā tule bābār piṭhe bæthā hôlô.*
>
> Lifting the heavy bag gave my father a backache.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**পিঠ** (*piṭh*) is listed by CDIAL for Bengali, as the back, under Sanskrit *pṛṣṭi* (CDIAL 8370).
