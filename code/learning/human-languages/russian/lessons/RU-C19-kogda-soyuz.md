## Grammar lens — the question word, joining
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-GRAMMAR-KOGDA-CLAUSE-01]; assesses=[RU-LEX-KOGDA-01, RU-GRAMMAR-CHTO-COMPLEMENTISER-01] -->

**что** asked a question and then hung a clause under a verb without changing a
letter. **когда** does exactly the same:

- **когда я читаю, я думаю** — *kagdá ya chitáyu, ya dúmayu* — **when** I read, I
  think.

This time the joining word may go **first**. Russian is happy to lead with the
*when*-half and follow with the main one — and when it does, the comma sits
between the two halves rather than before the joining word.

Turn the halves round and the comma travels with the seam:

- **я думаю, когда я читаю** — I think **when** I read.

Either order is correct and both mean the same thing. What never changes is that
there is a comma at the seam.
