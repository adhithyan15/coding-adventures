ಹಗುರ — hagura — "light (in weight)".

The word, taken apart.
The origin of ಹಗುರ (hagura) is uncertain. Neither DEDR nor CDIAL records it, and no source for it is settled.

Lesson 3 of 5.
ಗಟ್ಟಿ (gaṭṭi) — hard, strong.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for light, then the Kannada for soft.

You'll want to know: ಗಟ್ಟಿ.
ಗಟ್ಟಿ — gaṭṭi — "hard, strong".

The word, taken apart.
ಗಟ್ಟಿ (gaṭṭi) is native Dravidian, one word with Tamil kaṭṭu, Telugu kaṭṭu and Malayalam kaṭṭa (DEDR 1148). DEDR lists gaṭṭi itself, "firmness, hardness", and compares Sanskrit kaṭhina, "hard, firm" (CDIAL 2650).

Lesson 4 of 5.
ಕೊಳಕು (koḷaku) — dirty.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for soft, then the Kannada for hard.

You'll want to know: ಕೊಳಕು.
ಕೊಳಕು — koḷaku — "dirty".

The word, taken apart.
ಕೊಳಕು (koḷaku) is native Dravidian. DEDR 1822 lists the older Kannada koḻaku, "a decayed, putrid state", under koḻe, "to rot", and Tulu koḷaků, "filthy, dirty". DEDR 1822 compares Sanskrit kuṇapa.
