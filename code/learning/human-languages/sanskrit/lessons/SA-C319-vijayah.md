## You'll want to know: विजयः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C319-VIJAYA319-01]; assesses=[] -->

**विजयः** — *vijayaḥ* — "a victory".

> अन्ते सेनायाः विजयः भवति।
>
> *ante senāyāḥ vijayaḥ bhavati.*
>
> In the end the army wins the victory.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**विजयः** (*vijayaḥ*) is built from *vi* and the root √ji, "to conquer, win", as in *jayati*; MW gives *vijaya* as a contest for victory, victory, conquest (MW).
