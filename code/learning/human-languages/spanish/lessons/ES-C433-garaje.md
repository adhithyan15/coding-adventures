## You'll want to know: el garaje
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C433-EDIF-03]; assesses=[ES-LEX-C363-WHERE-41] -->

**el garaje** — the garage, the underground car park of a building.

Said *ga-RA-je*, with the **j** as the throaty *jota*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-C433-EDIF-03] -->

French **garage** came into Spanish and was **respelled**. French writes the
sound at the end with *-ge*; Spanish has no such spelling, so it wrote what it
heard with the letter it uses for that sound: **j**.

| language | spelling |
|---|---|
| French | garage |
| Spanish | garaje |

That habit is worth more than the word. Spanish borrows freely but **respells
to its own rules**, which is why *fútbol*, *champú* and *chófer* look the way
they do. A borrowed word you meet in Spanish has usually been made to spell
itself the Spanish way, so read it aloud by Spanish rules and the original often
appears.
