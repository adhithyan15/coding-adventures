## You'll want to know: o recluso
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C298-RECURSO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**o recluso** — "the inmate, the prisoner (formal)".

> O recluso trabalha na biblioteca da cadeia.
>
> *The inmate works in the prison library.*

Formal and official; in speech, *o preso*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*recluso* is a learned borrowing from Late Latin *reclūsus* "shut up, enclosed", from *reclūdere*, built on *claudere* "to shut" (Lewis & Short).
