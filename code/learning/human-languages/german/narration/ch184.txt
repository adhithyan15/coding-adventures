German, chapter 184: To park, To look after, To pick, To test, To clean.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
parken — to park.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to sew, then the German for to sneeze.

You'll want to know: parken.
parken — "to park".

The word, taken apart.
parken is taken from English to park, built on park, which came from Old French parc, "enclosure" (EtymDB 2.1). A car park is an enclosure.
