Marwadi (Marwari), chapter 279: Games and Contests.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
गोठियो (goṭhiyo) — a friend; a companion.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for a doll, then the Marwadi for to hide.

You'll want to know: गोठियो.
गोठियो — goṭhiyo — "a friend; a companion".
म्हारो गोठियो अठै है। mhāro goṭhiyo aṭhai hai. My friend is here.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
गोठियो (goṭhiyo) is inherited from Sanskrit goṣṭhika, "belonging to a gathering"; CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry, but lists a Gujarati form glossed "friend" (CDIAL 4338a).

Lesson 2 of 5.
चौपड़ (chaupaṛ) — chaupar, a board game.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for to hide, then the Marwadi for a friend.

You'll want to know: चौपड़.
चौपड़ — chaupaṛ — "chaupar, a board game".
दादा चौपड़ खेलै है। dādā chaupaṛ khelai hai. Grandfather plays chaupar.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
चौपड़ (chaupaṛ) is inherited from Sanskrit catuṣpuṭa, "having four folds"; CDIAL lists Marwari copṛī and Old Marwari caupaḍī for the game (CDIAL 4648), played on a cloth with four arms.

Lesson 4 of 5.
होड (hoḍ) — a contest.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for a board game, then the Marwadi for a die for games.

You'll want to know: होड.
होड — hoḍ — "a contest".
आज गांव मांय होड है। āj gā̃v mā̃y hoḍ hai. There's a contest in the village today.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
होड (hoḍ) is inherited from a reconstructed hōḍḍa, "a wager", which CDIAL takes from Dravidian; it lists Old Marwari hoḍa, "wager, rivalry" (CDIAL 14175).
