## You'll want to know: installare
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C400-INSTALLARE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**installare** — "to install".

> Se installi l'aggiornamento, il telefono va più veloce.
>
> *If you install the update, the phone runs faster.*

Also to fit something in a house: *installare una lampada*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*installare* was borrowed from French *installer*, from Medieval Latin *installāre* "to put in a stall, to seat in office", built on *stallum*, a word of Germanic origin; compare Old High German *stal* "place, stall" (EtymDB).
