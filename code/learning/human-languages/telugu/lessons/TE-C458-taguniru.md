# తాగునీరు (tāgunīru) — drinking water

## You'll want to know: తాగునీరు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C458-GADI458-05]; assesses=[] -->

**తాగునీరు** — *tāgunīru* — "drinking water".

> గ్రామంలో తాగునీరు దొరకడం కష్టంగా ఉంది.
>
> *grāmaṁlō tāgunīru dorakaḍaṁ kaṣṭaṅgā undi.*
>
> It is hard to get drinking water in the village.

తాగు, to drink, + నీరు, water: water for drinking. నీళ్ళు is the everyday plural.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**తాగునీరు** (*tāgunīru*) is native Dravidian: తాగు, "to drink" (DEDR 3174), and నీరు, "water" (DEDR 3690).
