## You'll want to know: ਕੱਸਣਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C229-DIN229-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ਕੱਸਣਾ** — *kassṇā* — "to tighten".

> ਮੈਂ ਸਵੇਰੇ ਪੇਟੀ ਕੱਸਦਾ ਹਾਂ।
>
> *maiṁ savere peṭī kassdā hāṁ.*
>
> In the morning I tighten my belt.

Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਗੰਢ ਕੱਸੀ, *maiṁ gaṇḍh kassī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਕੱਸਣਾ** (*kassṇā*) is inherited from Sanskrit *karṣati*, "draws, pulls"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *kassṇā*, "to tighten" (CDIAL 2908).
