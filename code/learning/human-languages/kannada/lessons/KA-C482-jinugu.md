# ಜಿನುಗು (jinugu) — to trickle; to ooze

## You'll want to know: ಜಿನುಗು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C482-TORE482-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ಜಿನುಗು** — *jinugu* — "to trickle; to ooze".

> ಬಂಡೆಯ ಮೇಲಿನಿಂದ ನೀರು ಜಿನುಗುತ್ತಿತ್ತು.
>
> *baṇḍeya mēlininda nīru jinuguttittu.*
>
> Water was trickling down from the top of the rock.

Past (it …): ಜಿನುಗಿತು, *jinugitu*.

Of a thin drizzle too: ಮಳೆ ಜಿನುಗುತ್ತಿದೆ.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಜಿನುಗು** (*jinugu*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 2520 lists Kannada *jinugu*, with the noun "drizzling rain".
