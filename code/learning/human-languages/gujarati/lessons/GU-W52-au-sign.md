# ૌ (au sign) — the au sign in સૌથી

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-LEX-C282-SAUTHI-01, GU-SCRIPT-C45-AI-SIGN-01, GU-SCRIPT-O-MATRA-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say **સૌથી** (*sauthī*), "most of all". Then write **ૈ**, the *ai* sign, and **ો**, the *o* sign, once each.

## Script you'll notice: ૌ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-SCRIPT-C282-AU-SIGN-01]; assesses=[GU-LEX-PAISA-01] -->

**ૌ** — the *au* sign: the **ા** bar on the right plus two stacked strokes above. It sits on **સ** in **સૌથી**.

| one stroke above | two strokes above |
|---|---|
| **ે** *e* | **ૈ** *ai*, as in **પૈસા** |
| **ો** *o*: **ા** with **ે** above | **ૌ** *au*: **ા** with **ૈ** above |

It is written after its consonant: **સ** first, then the bar to its right, then the lower flag, then the upper flag.

## Writing — notice and trace
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-SCRIPT-C282-AU-SIGN-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: observe-trace -->

Follow the numbered strip:

1. start at the top of the bar and draw it straight down
2. without lifting, turn into the short foot to the right
3. lift, then start at the lower flag's upper-left tip and arc it to the right
4. without lifting, curve it down to the right
5. lift, then start at the upper flag's upper-left tip and arc it to the right
6. without lifting, curve it down to the right — and only now lift

Three strokes — six movements, two pen lifts; cited to KanoAI's hand-made Gujarati barakhadi templates. Finger-trace **ૌ** once.

## Writing — copy with the model
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-SCRIPT-C282-AU-SIGN-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: guided-copy -->

Copy **સૌથી** once: **સ**, the bar, the two flags, then **થી**. Compare only two things: are there two flags, and does the lower one come first? Repair one if needed.

## Writing — recall after hiding
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-SCRIPT-C282-AU-SIGN-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: delayed-copy -->
<!-- hl-activity: {"id": "GU-W52-au-sign-recall", "kind": "text", "assesses": ["GU-SCRIPT-C282-AU-SIGN-01"], "prompt": "Hide the model and write the au sign from memory.", "answer": "ૌ", "accepted": [], "feedback": {"correct": "ૌ — the bar, the lower flag, the upper flag.", "incorrect": "Look once at ૌ, hide it, and repair only the part that differs."}, "response_seconds": 20} -->

Hide the model and write the *au* sign once. Uncover **ૌ**, compare, and stop after one repair.

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-SCRIPT-C282-AU-SIGN-01, GU-LEX-C282-SAUTHI-01] -->

[PAUSE 3s] Read **સો** and **સૌથી** aloud. What turns **ો** into **ૌ**? (**A second flag above.**)
