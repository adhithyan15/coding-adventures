Russian, chapter 479: Trust and Betrayal.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
доверие (dovériye) — trust.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to play up, then the Russian for to grumble.

You'll want to know: доверие.
доверие (dovériye) — "trust".
Между нами полное доверие. Mézhdu námi pólnoye dovériye. There is complete trust between us.
Neuter.

The word, taken apart.
доверие (dovériye) is built on до- and вера 'faith', which is inherited from Proto-Slavic věra 'faith, belief' (EtymDB).

Lesson 2 of 5.

You'll want to know: доверять.
доверять (doveryát) — "to trust".
Я доверяю своему врачу. Ya doveryáyu svoyemú vrachú. I trust my doctor.
Imperfective; perfective доверить. Takes the dative: доверять (doveryát) врачу.

The word, taken apart.
доверять (doveryát) is built on до- and the root of вера 'faith', inherited from Proto-Slavic věra (EtymDB).

Lesson 3 of 5.
предавать (predavát) — to betray.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for trust, then the Russian for to trust.

You'll want to know: предавать.
предавать (predavát) — "to betray".
Настоящий друг никогда не предаёт. Nastoyáshchiy drug nikogdá ne predayót. A true friend never betrays you.
Imperfective; present предаю, предаёшь (-ава- becomes -а-). Perfective предать.

The word, taken apart.
предавать (predavát) is built on пред- and давать 'to give', so to betray is to hand someone over; the root is inherited from Proto-Slavic dati 'to give' (EtymDB).

Lesson 5 of 5.

Warm-up.

You'll want to know: искренний.
искренний (ískrenniy) — "sincere".
Она дала мне искренний совет. Oná dalá mne ískrenniy sovét. She gave me sincere advice.
Soft adjective: искренняя, искреннее. The adverb is искренне — sincerely.

The word, taken apart.
искренний (ískrenniy) is inherited from Proto-Slavic iskrьnjь (EtymDB), usually glossed 'near, close'.
