## You'll want to know: اوڑھنا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C406-BASERA406-03]; assesses=[] -->

**اوڑھنا** — *oṛhnā* — "to wrap oneself in (a shawl or a blanket)".

> سردی تھی، اس لیے ہم نے کمبل اوڑھے۔
>
> *sardī thī, is liye ham ne kambal oṛhe.*
>
> It was cold, so we wrapped ourselves in blankets.

Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے چادر اوڑھی, *us ne chādar oṛhī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**اوڑھنا** (*oṛhnā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *oṛhnā*, "to cover", under *ōḍḍh*, a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 2547).
