## You'll want to know: বিকল
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C381-BYOBOSTHA381-03]; assesses=[] -->

**বিকল** — *bikôl* — "out of order; broken down".

> গাড়ি বিকল হলে আমাকে ফোন করো।
>
> *gāṛi bikôl hôle āmāke phon kôro.*
>
> Call me if the car breaks down.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**বিকল** (*bikôl*) is Sanskrit *vikala*, impaired or defective (Monier-Williams). It is the word for a machine or a body part that has stopped working.
