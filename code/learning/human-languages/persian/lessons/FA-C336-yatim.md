## You'll want to know: یتیم
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C336-TALAQ-04]; assesses=[] -->

**یتیم** (*yatim*) — "an orphan".

> او از کودکی یتیم بود.
>
> *U az kudaki yatim bud.*
>
> He had been an orphan since childhood.

Broken plural *aytâm*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*yatim* is borrowed from Arabic *yatīm*, "orphan" (EtymDB).

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
