## You'll want to know: しゅと
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C409-CHIIKI409-02]; assesses=[] -->

**しゅと** — *shuto* — "a capital city".

> **しゅとの ちかてつは いつも こんざつしています。** — *shuto no chikatetsu wa itsumo konzatsu shite imasu* — "The subway in the capital is always crowded."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**しゅと** (*shuto*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *shu*, "neck" (Middle Chinese *syuwX*) and *to*, "capital" (*tu*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
