Bengali, chapter 427: Seasons and Heat.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
গ্রীষ্ম (grīshmô) — summer; the hot season.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for bitterly cold, then the Bengali for to melt.

You'll want to know: গ্রীষ্ম.
গ্রীষ্ম — grīshmô — "summer; the hot season".
গ্রীষ্মে দুপুরে রাস্তা ফাঁকা থাকে। grīshme dupure rāstā phā̃kā thāke. In summer the streets are empty at midday.
The calendar word; in talk people say গরম, the heat.

The word, taken apart.
গ্রীষ্ম (grīshmô) is Sanskrit grīṣma, "summer, the hot season" (Monier-Williams), taken over whole.

Lesson 2 of 5.
শরৎ (shôrôt) — autumn (the season after the rains).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for to melt, then the Bengali for summer.

You'll want to know: শরৎ.
শরৎ — shôrôt — "autumn (the season after the rains)".
শরতে আকাশ নীল আর পরিষ্কার থাকে। shôrôte ākāsh nīl ār pôrishkār thāke. In autumn the sky stays blue and clear.
Before an ending ৎ is written ত: শরতে, in autumn. It is the season of the Durga Puja.

The word, taken apart.
শরৎ (shôrôt) is Sanskrit śarad, "autumn" (Monier-Williams), the season of ripening after the rains.

Lesson 3 of 5.
মৌসুম (mousum) — a season (for a crop or an activity).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for summer, then the Bengali for autumn.

You'll want to know: মৌসুম.
মৌসুম — mousum — "a season (for a crop or an activity)".
আমের মৌসুমে বাজার আমে ভরা। āmer mousume bājār āme bhôrā. In mango season the market is full of mangoes.
The English word monsoon comes from the same Arabic source.

The word, taken apart.
মৌসুম (mousum) is usually traced to Arabic mawsim, "a season, a time of year", through Persian; Portuguese sailors took the word for the trade winds, and it reached English as monsoon.

Lesson 4 of 5.
তাপমাত্রা (tāpmātrā) — temperature.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for autumn, then the Bengali for a season (for a crop or an activity).

You'll want to know: তাপমাত্রা.
তাপমাত্রা — tāpmātrā — "temperature".
আজ শহরের তাপমাত্রা চল্লিশ ডিগ্রি। āj shôhôrer tāpmātrā chôllish ḍigri. Today the temperature in the city is forty degrees.

The word, taken apart.
তাপমাত্রা (tāpmātrā) joins Sanskrit tāpa, "heat", and mātrā, "a measure" (Monier-Williams for both): the measure of heat, a modern coinage.

The word, taken apart.
উত্তাপ (uttāp) is Sanskrit ut-tāpa, "great heat, glow" (Monier-Williams), from ut, up, and tāpa, heat.
