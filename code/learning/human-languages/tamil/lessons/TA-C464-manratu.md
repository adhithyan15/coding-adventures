# மன்றாடு (maṉṟāṭu) — to plead; to beg earnestly

## You'll want to know: மன்றாடு
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C464-CAPALAM464-04]; assesses=[] -->

**மன்றாடு** — *maṉṟāṭu* — "to plead; to beg earnestly".

> வேலை வேண்டும் என்று அவர் முதலாளியிடம் மன்றாடினார்.
>
> *vēlai vēṇṭum eṉṟu avar mutalāḷiyiṭam maṉṟāṭiṉār.*
>
> He pleaded with his employer for work.

Past (I …): மன்றாடினேன், *maṉṟāṭiṉēṉ*.

The person pleaded with takes -இடம்.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

The origin of **மன்றாடு** (*maṉṟāṭu*) is not settled; it ends like verbs built with ஆடு, but the first part is unclear.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
