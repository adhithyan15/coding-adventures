# உட்படு (uṭpaṭu) — to be subject to; to come under

## Warm-up

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Tamil for a representative, then the Tamil for a condition.

## You'll want to know: உட்படு
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C382-NIPANTANAI382-02]; assesses=[] -->

**உட்படு** — *uṭpaṭu* — "to be subject to; to come under".

> இந்த அனுமதி சில நிபந்தனைகளுக்கு உட்பட்டது.
>
> *inta aṉumati cila nipantaṉaikaḷukku uṭpaṭṭatu.*
>
> This permission is subject to some conditions.

Past (I …): உட்பட்டேன், *uṭpaṭṭēṉ*.

With -க்கு: விதிகளுக்கு உட்பட்டு, subject to the rules. உட்பட, including.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**உட்படு** (*uṭpaṭu*) is native: *uḷ*, "inside" (DEDR 698), whose ḷ becomes ட் before p, and *paṭu*, "to undergo": to come inside a rule.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
