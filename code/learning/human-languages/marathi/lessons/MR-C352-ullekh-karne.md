# उल्लेख करणे (ullekh karṇe) — to mention

## You'll want to know: उल्लेख करणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C352-ULLEKH352-01]; assesses=[] -->

**उल्लेख करणे** — *ullekh karṇe* — "to mention".

> अरुणने पत्रात तुमचा उल्लेख केला.
>
> *aruṇne patrāt tumcā ullekh kelā.*
>
> Arun mentioned you in his letter.

Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने उल्लेख केला, *tyāne ullekh kelā*.

What is mentioned takes -चा: तुमचा उल्लेख.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**उल्लेख करणे** (*ullekh karṇe*) joins the tatsama उल्लेख, Sanskrit *ullekha*, "causing to appear clearly" (Monier-Williams), to करणे, "to do".
