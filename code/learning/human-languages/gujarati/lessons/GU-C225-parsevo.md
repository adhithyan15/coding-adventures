## You'll want to know: પરસેવો
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C225-UNWELL225-05]; assesses=[] -->

**પરસેવો** — *parsevo* — "sweat".

> **શું તમને પરસેવો થાય છે?** — *śũ tamne parsevo thāy che?* — "Are you sweating?"

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**પરસેવો** (*parsevo*) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from *prasveda*, "great sweat" (CDIAL 8898).
