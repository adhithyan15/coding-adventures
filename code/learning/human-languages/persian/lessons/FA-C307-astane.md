## You'll want to know: آستانه
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C307-AVAYEL-04]; assesses=[] -->

**آستانه** (*âstâne*) — "the eve, the threshold".

> در آستانه سال نو همه خرید میکردند.
>
> *Dar âstâne-ye sâl-e now hame kharid mikardand.*
>
> On the eve of the new year everyone was out shopping.

*Dar âstâne-ye ...* "on the eve of, on the brink of". Its first meaning is a doorstep.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*âstâne* is inherited from Middle Persian *āstānag* (EtymDB); its first sense is "threshold, doorstep".
