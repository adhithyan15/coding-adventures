## You'll want to know: incolō, incolere
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C456-INCOLA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**incolō, incolere** — "to live in, to inhabit".

> Multī hominēs hanc urbem incolunt.
>
> *Many people live in this city.*

*incolō, incolere, incoluī*, with an accusative of the place lived in.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*incolō* is built from *in-* 'in' and *colō* 'to dwell, to till' (EtymDB; Lewis & Short).
