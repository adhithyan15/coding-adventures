## You'll want to know: ぜんいん
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C332-KENRI332-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ぜんいん** — *zen'in* — "everyone (in a group); all members".

> **ぜんいんが さんせいしたので、きまりました。** — *zen'in ga sansei shita node, kimarimashita* — "Everyone agreed, so it was decided."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ぜんいん** (*zen'in*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *zen*, "all" (Middle Chinese *dzjwen*) and *in*, "member" (*hjwen*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
