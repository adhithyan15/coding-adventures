## You'll want to know: वाटसरू
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C399-VAHAK399-05]; assesses=[] -->

**वाटसरू** — *vāṭsarū* — "a wayfarer; a passer-by".

> एका वाटसरूने आम्हाला रस्ता सांगितला.
>
> *ekā vāṭsarūne āmhālā rastā sāṅgitlā.*
>
> A passer-by told us the way.

Masculine.

An old, warm word for someone on the road, often walking.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**वाटसरू** (*vāṭsarū*) is built on **वाट**, "a path", inherited from Sanskrit *vartman*, "track of a wheel, path" (CDIAL 11366); the ending marks the person on it.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for getting around.
