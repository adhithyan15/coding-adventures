## You'll want to know: фейерверк
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C314-SALYUT-02]; assesses=[] -->

**фейерверк** (*feyervérk*) — "fireworks".

> Мы смотрели фейерверк с балкона.
>
> *My smotréli feyervérk s balkóna.*
>
> We watched the fireworks from the balcony.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*фейерверк* was borrowed from German *Feuerwerk* (EtymDB), from *Feuer* "fire" and *Werk* "work".
