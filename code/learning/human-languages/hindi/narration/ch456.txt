Hindi, chapter 456: Losing One's Temper.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
नाराज़ (nārāz) — cross; displeased.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for to fret, then the Hindi for to hurt someone's feelings.

You'll want to know: नाराज़.
नाराज़ — nārāz — "cross; displeased".
पिता अरुण से नाराज़ थे क्योंकि वह देर से आया। pitā Aruṇ se nārāz the kyoṁki vah der se āyā. Arun's father was cross with him because he came late.

The word, taken apart.
नाराज़ (nārāz) is usually traced to Persian nārāz, "displeased", built on nā-, "not", and a word for "content".

Lesson 2 of 5.
क्रोध (krodh) — wrath; rage.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for to hurt someone's feelings, then the Hindi for cross.

You'll want to know: क्रोध.
क्रोध — krodh — "wrath; rage".
क्रोध में आदमी सही और ग़लत भूल जाता है। krodh meṁ ādmī sahī aur ġalat bhūl jātā hai. In a rage a person forgets right from wrong.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
क्रोध (krodh) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit krodha, "anger, wrath, passion" (Monier-Williams). Everyday speech says ġussā; krodh is the word of books and sermons.

Past (he …): वह चिढ़ गया, vah ciṛh gayā.

The word, taken apart.
चिढ़ना (ciṛhnā) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Hindi ciṛhnā, "to be provoked", under ciḍ, a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 4794).

Lesson 4 of 5.
बिफरना (bipharnā) — to fly into a rage.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for wrath, then the Hindi for to get irritated.

You'll want to know: बिफरना.
बिफरना — bipharnā — "to fly into a rage".
कम पैसे देखकर दुकानदार बिफर गया। kam paise dekhkar dukāndār biphar gayā. Seeing how little money he had been given, the shopkeeper flew into a rage.
Past (he …): वह बिफर गया, vah biphar gayā.

The word, taken apart.
बिफरना (bipharnā) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Hindi bipharnā, "to become enraged", under vispharati, "quivers, trembles", a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 12014): someone shaking with anger. It is an outburst, much stronger than ciṛhnā.

Lesson 5 of 5.
खीझना (khījhnā) — to get fed up; to be vexed.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for to get irritated, then the Hindi for to fly into a rage.

You'll want to know: खीझना.
खीझना — khījhnā — "to get fed up; to be vexed".
रात को फ़ोन तीन बार बजा और मीरा खीझ गई। rāt ko fon tīn bār bajā aur Mīrā khījh gaī. The phone rang three times in the night and Mira got fed up.
Past (she …): वह खीझ गई, vah khījh gaī.

The word, taken apart.
खीझना (khījhnā) is inherited: CDIAL lists Hindi khījnā, "to be irritated", and the causative khijhānā, "to vex", under Sanskrit kṣīyátē, "is destroyed, wanes" (CDIAL 3695); khījhnā is the usual spelling of that plain verb.
