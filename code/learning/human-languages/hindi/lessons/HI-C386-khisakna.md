## You'll want to know: खिसकना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C386-SAHARA386-01]; assesses=[] -->

**खिसकना** — *khisaknā* — "to slip away, to shift".

> अगर तुम दोनों थोड़ा खिसकोगे तो मैं भी बैठ सकूँगा।
>
> *agar tum donõ thoṛā khiskoge to maiṁ bhī baiṭh sakūṅgā.*
>
> If you two shift up a little, I'll be able to sit too.

Past (he …): वह पीछे खिसक गया, *vah pīche khisak gayā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**खिसकना** (*khisaknā*) is inherited from *\*khis*, "slip", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 3888).
