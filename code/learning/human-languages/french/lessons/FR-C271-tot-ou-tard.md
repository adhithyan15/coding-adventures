# tôt ou tard — sooner or later

## You'll want to know: tôt ou tard
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C271-PEU-05]; assesses=[] -->

**tôt ou tard** — "sooner or later".

> Tôt ou tard, il va comprendre.
>
> *Sooner or later, he is going to understand.*

The order is fixed: *tôt* first.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*tard* is inherited from Latin *tardē* "slowly, late" (Lewis & Short), from *tardus* "slow" (EtymDB); *tôt* is usually traced to Latin *tostum* "scorched".
