Tamil, chapter 280: Government, Rule, Fine, Complaint, Athlete.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
அரசு (aracu) — the government.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for an engineer, then the Tamil for a lawyer.
