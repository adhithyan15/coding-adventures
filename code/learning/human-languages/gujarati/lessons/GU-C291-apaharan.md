# અપહરણ (apaharaṇ) — a kidnapping

## You'll want to know: અપહરણ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C291-GHERVU291-01]; assesses=[] -->

**અપહરણ** — *apaharaṇ* — "a kidnapping".

> ગઈકાલે એક વેપારીના દીકરાનું અપહરણ થયું.
>
> *gaīkāle ek vepārīnā dīkrānũ apaharaṇ thayũ.*
>
> Yesterday a merchant's son was kidnapped.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**અપહરણ** (*apaharaṇ*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *apaharaṇa*, "a carrying away, stealing" (Monier-Williams). In Gujarati it is the carrying off of a person: **દીકરાનું અપહરણ થયું**.
