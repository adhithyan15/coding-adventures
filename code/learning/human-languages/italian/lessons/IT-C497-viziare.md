## You'll want to know: viziare
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C497-EDUCARE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**viziare** — "to spoil (a child)".

> I nonni viziano sempre i nipoti.
>
> *Grandparents always spoil their grandchildren.*

Built on *il vizio* "vice, bad habit".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*viziare* is a learned borrowing from Latin *vitiāre* "to spoil, to damage" (EtymDB), from *vitium* "a fault".
