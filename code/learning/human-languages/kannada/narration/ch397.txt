Kannada, chapter 397: Citizens Together.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
ನಾಗರಿಕ (nāgarika) — a citizen.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a legislator, then the Kannada for to endorse.

You'll want to know: ನಾಗರಿಕ.
ನಾಗರಿಕ — nāgarika — "a citizen".
ಪ್ರತಿಯೊಬ್ಬ ನಾಗರಿಕನೂ ತೆರಿಗೆ ಕಟ್ಟಬೇಕು. pratiyobba nāgarikanū terige kaṭṭabēku. Every citizen must pay taxes.
Plural: ನಾಗರಿಕರು, nāgarikaru.
As an adjective it means civil, civilised.

The word, taken apart.
ನಾಗರಿಕ (nāgarika) is borrowed from Sanskrit nāgarika, "born or living in a town, civic" (Monier-Williams), from nagara, "a town".

Lesson 5 of 6.
ಒಗ್ಗೂಡು (oggūḍu) — to unite.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for public, then the Kannada for a community.

You'll want to know: ಒಗ್ಗೂಡು.
ಒಗ್ಗೂಡು — oggūḍu — "to unite".
ಊರಿನ ಜನರು ಕೆರೆ ಉಳಿಸಲು ಒಗ್ಗೂಡಿದರು. ūrina janaru kere uḷisalu oggūḍidaru. The townspeople came together to save the lake.
Past (I …): ಒಗ್ಗೂಡಿದೆ, oggūḍide.
ಒಗ್ಗೂಡಿಸು, to unite others.

The word, taken apart.
ಒಗ್ಗೂಡು (oggūḍu) is native Dravidian, built from oggu, "to be one, to be united" (DEDR 990), and kūḍu, "to join, to come together" (DEDR 1882).
