## You'll want to know: つきみ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C501-MOON501-01]; assesses=[] -->

**つきみ** — *tsukimi* — "moon viewing".

> **あきの ばん、つきみを します。** — *aki no ban, tsukimi o shimasu* — "On autumn evenings we go moon viewing."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**つきみ** (*tsukimi*) is a native Japanese compound (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese: built from *tsuki*, "moon" and *mi*, "looking" (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
