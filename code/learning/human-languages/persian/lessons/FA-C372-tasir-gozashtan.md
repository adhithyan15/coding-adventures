# تاثیر گذاشتن (ta'sir gozâshtan) — to influence, to have an impact

## You'll want to know: تاثیر گذاشتن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C372-TASIR-02]; assesses=[] -->

**تاثیر گذاشتن** (*ta'sir gozâshtan*) — "to influence, to have an impact".

> معلمم روی زندگی من تاثیر گذاشت.
>
> *mo'allemam ruye zendegi-ye man ta'sir gozâsht.*
>
> My teacher had an impact on my life.

*Ta'sir* + *gozâshtan* "to put" (present stem *gozâr-*). Takes *ruye* or *bar*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**تاثیر گذاشتن** is literally "to put an effect": *ta'sir* is usually traced to Arabic *ta'thīr*, and *gozâshtan* "to put, to leave" is a native Persian verb.
