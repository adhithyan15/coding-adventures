## You'll want to know: தேய்
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C406-PETROL406-05]; assesses=[] -->

**தேய்** — *tēy* — "to wear down; to rub".

> இந்த டயர் நன்றாகத் தேய்ந்துவிட்டது.
>
> *inta ṭayar naṉṟākat tēyntuviṭṭatu.*
>
> This tyre has worn right down.

Past (it …): தேய்ந்தது, *tēyntatu*.

With an object it means to rub or scrub: பாத்திரம் தேய், to scrub a pot.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**தேய்** (*tēy*) is native Dravidian (DEDR 3458), "to wear away by rubbing, to wane".
