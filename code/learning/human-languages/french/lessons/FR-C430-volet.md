## You'll want to know: le volet
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C430-BESTIOLES-04]; assesses=[] -->

**le volet** — "the shutter".

> Le matin, elle ouvre les volets de la chambre.
>
> *In the morning she opens the bedroom shutters.*

*Fermer les volets* — to close the shutters.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*volet* is built on *voler* 'to fly' (EtymDB), which is inherited from Latin *volāre*: a panel that swings open like a wing.
