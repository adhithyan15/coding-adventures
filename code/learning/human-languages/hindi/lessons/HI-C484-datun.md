## You'll want to know: दातुन
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C484-SANVARNA484-01]; assesses=[] -->

**दातुन** — *dātun* — "a twig toothbrush".

> गाँव में दादा आज भी दातुन से दाँत साफ़ करते हैं।
>
> *gā̃v meṁ dādā āj bhī dātun se dā̃t sāf karte haiṁ.*
>
> In the village my grandfather still cleans his teeth with a twig.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**दातुन** (*dātun*) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Hindi *dātun*, "tooth stick", under Sanskrit *dantapavana*, "tooth-cleaning" (CDIAL 6157): a twig, often of neem, chewed at one end.
