## You'll want to know: অস্ত্র
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C315-OSTRO315-01]; assesses=[] -->

**অস্ত্র** — *ôstrô* — "a weapon".

> সৈন্যদের হাতে অনেক অস্ত্র ছিল।
>
> *soinyôder hāte ônek ôstrô chhilô.*
>
> The soldiers carried many weapons.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**অস্ত্র** (*ôstrô*) is Sanskrit *astra*, a missile weapon, an arrow, and then any weapon (Monier-Williams).
