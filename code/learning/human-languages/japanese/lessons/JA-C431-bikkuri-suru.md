# びっくりする (bikkuri suru) — to be startled

## You'll want to know: びっくりする
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C431-SHOCK431-02]; assesses=[] -->

**びっくりする** — *bikkuri suru* — "to be startled".

> **かみなりの おとに びっくりしました。** — *kaminari no oto ni bikkuri shimashita* — "I was startled by the sound of thunder."

Polite **びっくりします**, *bikkurishimasu*; a する verb. Past (polite): **ねこが びっくりしました。**, *neko ga bikkuri shimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**びっくりする** (*bikkuri suru*) is a native Japanese mimetic word, *bikkuri*, inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, made a verb with *suru*, "to do"; its older origin is not settled (JMdict).
