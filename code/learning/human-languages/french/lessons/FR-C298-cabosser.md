## You'll want to know: cabosser
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C298-CHOC-05]; assesses=[] -->

**cabosser** — "to dent".

> Le choc a cabossé la voiture.
>
> *The crash dented the car.*

*Une voiture toute cabossée* — a car full of dents.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*cabosser* is usually linked to *bosse* "a bump"; the origin is not settled (EtymDB lists no parent).
