Lesson 1 of 5.
دوش گرفتن (dush gereftan) — to take a shower.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a pencil case, then the Persian for a school report.

You'll want to know: دوش گرفتن.
دوش گرفتن — dush gereftan — "to take a shower".

The word, taken apart.
dush gereftan is "to take a shower": dush is borrowed from French douche, "shower" (EtymDB), and gereftan, "to take", is native. (Persian dush, "shoulder", is a different, native word.) gereftan, "to take", is an inherited verb; EtymDB takes it back to the Indo-European root gʰrebh₂-, "to seize".

Lesson 2 of 5.
برداشتن (bardâshtan) — to pick up, to lift.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a school report, then the Persian for to take a shower.

You'll want to know: برداشتن.
برداشتن — bardâshtan — "to pick up, to lift".

The word, taken apart.
bardâshtan is bar, "up", with dâshtan, "to hold": to hold up. Both are native Persian. dâshtan, "to have", is an inherited verb; it is usually traced to Middle Persian dāštan, "to hold".

Lesson 3 of 5.
تماشا کردن (tamâshâ kardan) — to watch.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to take a shower, then the Persian for to pick up.

You'll want to know: تماشا کردن.
تماشا کردن — tamâshâ kardan — "to watch".

The word, taken apart.
tamâshâ kardan pairs tamâshâ, borrowed from Arabic tamāšā, "walking about together" (root m-š-y, Lane), with the Persian verb kardan, "to do, to make". kardan, "to do", is an inherited verb, from Middle Persian kardan (EtymDB). Persian turned it into "looking on, a show" (EtymDB).
