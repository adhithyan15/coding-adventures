## You'll want to know: ناگ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C327-KHAZANA327-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ناگ** — *nāg* — "a cobra; a serpent".

> خزانے پر ایک ناگ بیٹھا تھا۔
>
> *khazāne par ek nāg baiṭhā thā.*
>
> A cobra sat on the treasure.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

is inherited from Sanskrit *nāga*, "a serpent" (Monier-Williams). In tales the **ناگ** guards treasure.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
