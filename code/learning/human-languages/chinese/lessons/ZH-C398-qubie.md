## You'll want to know: qūbié
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C398-DEPEND398-02]; assesses=[] -->

*qūbié* — "difference".

> *zhè liǎng ge yǒu shénme qūbié* — What's the difference between these two?

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*qūbié* is built from 区 'an area' and *bié* 'to part', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *khju* and *bjet* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'different' and 'to leave'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what would happen.
