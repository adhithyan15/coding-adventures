## You'll want to know: ভাগ্নে
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C482-JETHA482-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ভাগ্নে** — *bhāgne* — "a sister's son; a nephew".

> আমার ছোট ভাগ্নের ক্রিকেট খেলতে খুব ভালো লাগে।
>
> *āmār chhoṭo bhāgner krikeṭ khelte khub bhālo lāge.*
>
> My little nephew really likes playing cricket.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ভাগ্নে** (*bhāgne*) comes from Sanskrit *bhāgineya*, "a sister's son" (CDIAL 9433), worn down in Bengali.
