## You'll want to know: ವಂಶ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C341-VAMSHA341-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ವಂಶ** — *vaṃśa* — "a lineage; a dynasty".

> ಆ ವಂಶದ ರಾಜರು ಮುನ್ನೂರು ವರ್ಷ ಆಳಿದರು.
>
> *ā vaṃśada rājaru munnūru varṣa āḷidaru.*
>
> The kings of that dynasty ruled for three hundred years.

ವಂಶವೃಕ್ಷ is a family tree.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ವಂಶ** (*vaṃśa*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *vaṃśa*, "a bamboo cane", and from its joints, "a line of descent, a race" (Monier-Williams).
