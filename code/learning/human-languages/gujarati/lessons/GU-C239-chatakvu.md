# છટકવું (chaṭakvũ) — to slip away, to escape

## You'll want to know: છટકવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C239-POND239-05]; assesses=[] -->

**છટકવું** — *chaṭakvũ* — "to slip away, to escape".

> **દેડકો મારા હાથથી છટક્યો.** — *deḍko mārā hāththī chaṭakyo* — "The frog slipped out of my hand."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**છટકવું** (*chaṭakvũ*) comes from *\*chaṭṭ*, "scatter", a reconstructed form; CDIAL glosses the Gujarati verb "to slip off" (CDIAL 4968).
