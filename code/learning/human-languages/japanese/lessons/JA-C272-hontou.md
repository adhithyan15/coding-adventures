## You'll want to know: ほんとう
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C272-SAGI272-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ほんとう** — *hontō* — "true; the truth".

> **それは ほんとうですか。** — *sore wa hontō desu ka* — "Is that really so?"

A na-adjective: な before a noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ほんとう** (*hontō*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *hon*, "true" (Middle Chinese *pwonX*) and *tō*, "right" (*tang*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
