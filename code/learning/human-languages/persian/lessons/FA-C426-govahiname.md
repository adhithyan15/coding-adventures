## You'll want to know: گواهینامه
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C426-RAHBANDAN-05]; assesses=[] -->

**گواهینامه** (*govâhinâme*) — "a driving licence; a certificate".

> برادرم پارسال گواهینامه گرفت.
>
> *Barâdaram pârsâl govâhinâme gereft.*
>
> My brother got his driving licence last year.

*govâhi* 'witness, proof' + *nâme* 'letter'. In full: *govâhinâme-ye rânandegi*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*govâhinâme* is a native compound of *govâhi* 'witness, proof', from *govâh* 'a witness', and *nâme* 'letter, document', usually traced to Middle Persian *nāmag*.
