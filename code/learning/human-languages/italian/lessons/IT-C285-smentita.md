## You'll want to know: la smentita
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C285-COMUNICATO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**la smentita** — "the denial (of a report)".

> Il giorno dopo è arrivata la smentita.
>
> *The next day the denial came.*

Official and journalistic: a statement that a story is not true.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*smentita* is built on *smentire* "to give the lie to", from *s-* and *mentire* "to lie", which is inherited from Latin *mentīrī* (Lewis & Short).
