## You'll want to know: causer
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C346-CONSEQUENCE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**causer** — "to cause".

> Le brouillard a causé un accident sur l'autoroute.
>
> *The fog caused an accident on the motorway.*

Another *causer*, informal, means to chat: *causer avec un ami*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*causer* is built on *cause*, which comes from Latin *causa* "cause, reason, lawsuit" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short).
