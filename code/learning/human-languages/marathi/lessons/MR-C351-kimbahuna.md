## You'll want to know: किंबहुना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C351-JODNE351-03]; assesses=[] -->

**किंबहुना** — *kimbahunā* — "in fact; rather".

> तो हुशार आहे, किंबहुना वर्गात सर्वात हुशार आहे.
>
> *to huśār āhe, kimbahunā vargāt sarvāt huśār āhe.*
>
> He is clever; in fact, the cleverest in the class.

Formal: it makes what was said stronger.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**किंबहुना** (*kimbahunā*) is a tatsama phrase, taken whole from Sanskrit *kiṁ*, "what", and *bahunā*, "with more" (Monier-Williams): what need is there to say more.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
