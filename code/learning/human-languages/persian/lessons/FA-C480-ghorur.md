## You'll want to know: غرور
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C480-PRIDE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**غرور** (*ghorur*) — "pride; vanity".

> غرور او دوستانش را ناراحت میکند.
>
> *ghorur-e u dustânash râ nârâhat mikonad.*
>
> His pride upsets his friends.

A noun, usually with a bad sense: too much pride. *ghorur dâshtan* — to be vain.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ghorur* was borrowed from Arabic *ġurūr*, which EtymDB glosses 'pride' (EtymDB).
