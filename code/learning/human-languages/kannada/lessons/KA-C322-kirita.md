# ಕಿರೀಟ (kirīṭa) — a crown

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[KA-LEX-C322-RAJA322-03, KA-LEX-C322-RAJA322-04] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Kannada for to reign, then the Kannada for a throne.

## You'll want to know: ಕಿರೀಟ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C322-RAJA322-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ಕಿರೀಟ** — *kirīṭa* — "a crown".

> ರಾಣಿಯ ಕಿರೀಟ ಚಿನ್ನದ್ದು.
>
> *rāṇiya kirīṭa cinnaddu.*
>
> The queen's crown is made of gold.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಕಿರೀಟ** (*kirīṭa*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *kirīṭa*, "a diadem, a crest" (CDIAL 3176).
