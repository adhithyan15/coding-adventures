## You'll want to know: ただよう
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C381-FUNATABI381-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ただよう** — *tadayou* — "to drift; to float".

> **ちいさい ふねが うみを ただよっています。** — *chiisai fune ga umi o tadayotte imasu* — "A small boat is drifting on the sea."

Polite **ただよいます**, *tadayoimasu*; a godan verb. Past (polite): **ふねが うみを ただよいました。**, *fune ga umi o tadayoimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ただよう** (*tadayou*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict).
