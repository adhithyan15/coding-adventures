## You'll want to know: la costilla
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C737-CUERPO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**la costilla** — "the rib".

> Me duele una costilla al respirar.
>
> *One of my ribs hurts when I breathe.*

*Las costillas de cerdo* — pork ribs.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*costilla* comes from Late Latin *costula* "little rib" (EtymDB), a small form of *costa* "rib, side", the source of *costa* "coast".
