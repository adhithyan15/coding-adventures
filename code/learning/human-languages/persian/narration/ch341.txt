Persian, chapter 341: Parties and presents.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
مهمانی دادن (mehmâni dâdan) — to throw a party, to give a party.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a custom, then the Persian for wild rue.

You'll want to know: مهمانی دادن.
مهمانی دادن (mehmâni dâdan) — "to throw a party, to give a party".
برای تولد خواهرم مهمانی دادیم. Barâ-ye tavallod-e khâharam mehmâni dâdim. We threw a party for my sister's birthday.
Present mehmâni midahad. Mehmâni raftan is "to go to a party".

The word, taken apart.
mehmâni "a party" is built on mehmân "guest", a native Persian word, and dâdan "to give" is inherited Persian: to give a party.

Lesson 2 of 5.
پذیرایی کردن (pazirâyi kardan) — to serve (guests), to entertain.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for wild rue, then the Persian for to throw a party.

You'll want to know: پذیرایی کردن.
پذیرایی کردن (pazirâyi kardan) — "to serve (guests), to entertain".
مادرم با چای و شیرینی از مهمانها پذیرایی کرد. Mâdaram bâ chây o shirini az mehmân-hâ pazirâyi kard. My mother served the guests tea and pastries.
The guests take az. The noun pazirâyi is also "the living room".

The word, taken apart.
pazirâyi "reception" is built on pazir-, the present stem of the native verb paziroftan "to receive"; kardan "to do" is inherited Persian. EtymDB lists no entry.

Lesson 3 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to throw a party, then the Persian for to serve.

You'll want to know: کادو.
کادو (kâdo) — "a present (everyday word)".
برای دوستم یک کادوی کوچک خریدم. Barâ-ye dustam yek kâdo-ye kuchak kharidam. I bought a small present for my friend.
Everyday; hediye is more formal. Kâdo kardan is "to gift-wrap".

The word, taken apart.
kâdo is borrowed from French cadeau, "gift" (EtymDB).

Lesson 4 of 5.
بادکنک (bâdkonak) — a balloon.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to serve, then the Persian for a present.

You'll want to know: بادکنک.
بادکنک (bâdkonak) — "a balloon".
بچه ها با بادکنک بازی میکردند. Bachche-hâ bâ bâdkonak bâzi mikardand. The children were playing with balloons.
bâd "wind, air" + kon- "make" + -ak.

The word, taken apart.
bâdkonak is built from Persian bâd "wind, air", kon-, the present stem of kardan "to make", and the suffix -ak. All of it is native.
