## You'll want to know: કડવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C171-TASTE171-03]; assesses=[] -->

**કડવું** — *kaḍvũ* — "bitter".

> **આ દવા કડવી છે.** — *ā davā kaḍvī che* — "This medicine is bitter."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**કડવું** (*kaḍvũ*) is a tadbhava. It comes from Sanskrit *kaṭu*, *kaṭuka*, "pungent, bitter", and CDIAL lists the Gujarati *kaḍvũ* (CDIAL 2641).
