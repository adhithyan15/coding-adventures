# 不 — four strokes, all of them separate

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ZH-SCRIPT-RI] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Write 日. Middle bar before the bottom.

## Script — the shape
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-SCRIPT-BU]; assesses=[] -->

> 不

Four strokes, in this order:

1. a **top horizontal**, left to right
2. lift, then a **long stroke from the upper centre down to the left**
3. lift, then the **central vertical**, straight down
4. lift, then a **short right-falling dot**

**Three pen lifts, and every stroke stands alone.** Nothing joins here. That is
unusual enough to be worth saying plainly: the boxes you have just written were
one continuous movement in places, and this is the opposite — four deliberate,
separate marks under a single roof.

The commonest beginner's version runs the second and third strokes together into
a corner. They are not a corner. Lift.
