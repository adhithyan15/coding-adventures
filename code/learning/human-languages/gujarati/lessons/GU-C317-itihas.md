## You'll want to know: ઇતિહાસ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C317-ITIHAS317-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ઇતિહાસ** — *itihās* — "history".

> મીરાને ઇતિહાસ ખૂબ ગમે છે.
>
> *Mīrāne itihās khūb game che.*
>
> Mira loves history.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ઇતિહાસ** (*itihās*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *itihāsa*, from *iti ha āsa*, "so indeed it was" (Monier-Williams): the telling of what happened long ago. It is masculine: **ગુજરાતનો ઇતિહાસ**, the history of Gujarat.
