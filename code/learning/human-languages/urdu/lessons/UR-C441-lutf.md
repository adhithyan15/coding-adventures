## You'll want to know: لطف
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C441-JOY441-02]; assesses=[] -->

**لطف** — *lutf* — "enjoyment; pleasure".

> ہمیں سفر میں بڑا لطف آیا۔
>
> *hameṅ safar meṅ baṛā lutf āyā.*
>
> We really enjoyed the journey.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**لطف** (*lutf*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *luṭf*, "gentleness, grace"; in Urdu it has come to mean the pleasure you take in something.
