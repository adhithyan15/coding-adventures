## You'll want to know: ખજાનો
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C334-KHAJANO334-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ખજાનો** — *khajāno* — "a treasure".

> ગુફામાં એક જૂનો ખજાનો હતો.
>
> *guphāmā̃ ek jūno khajāno hato.*
>
> There was an old treasure in the cave.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ખજાનો** (*khajāno*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *khizāna*, "a storehouse, a treasury". It is masculine: **જૂનો ખજાનો**.
