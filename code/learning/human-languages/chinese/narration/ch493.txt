Mandarin Chinese, chapter 493: Extra Lessons.
6 lessons. You can do the first 2 of them in the car; after that you will want to have stopped.


Lesson 1 of 6.
bǔxí — to take extra lessons.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for English (the language), then the Mandarin for a major (at university).

You'll want to know: bǔxí.
bǔxí — "to take extra lessons".
wǒ bǔxí le yì nián shùxué — I had extra maths lessons for a year.
Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
bǔxí is built from bǔ 'to patch' and xí 'to practise', both inherited from Middle Chinese, puX and zip (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to mend clothes' and the second 'to learn'.

Lesson 2 of 6.
家教 (jiājiào) — private tutor.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for a major (at university), then the Mandarin for to take extra lessons.

You'll want to know: 家教.
家教 — jiājiào — "private tutor".
大学的时候我当过家教 dàxué de shíhou wǒ dāng guo jiājiào — At university I worked as a private tutor.
The new character, in pieces: 教 (jiào) — eleven strokes in two pieces: on the left the top of 老 lǎo with 子 zǐ tucked under it, seven strokes; on the right four strokes, two falling to the left, a short bar and a long stroke falling to the right. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

The word, taken apart.
家教 (jiājiào) is built from 家 'a home' and 教 'to teach', both inherited from Middle Chinese, kae and kaewH (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'to instruct'. Here 教 keeps the falling tone, jiào.

Script — the top of lǎo over zǐ, then four strokes.
教 (jiào).
Two pieces, left before right.
The left piece, seven strokes. First the top of 老, four strokes, as you wrote them: a short bar, a vertical through it, a long bar, and a long stroke falling to the left. Then 子 zǐ, three strokes, tucked under it: a bar that turns and falls to the left, a vertical with a hook, and a stroke rising to the right.
The right piece, four strokes: a short stroke falling to the left, a bar, a long stroke falling to the left, and a long stroke falling to the right.
4 + 3 + 4 = eleven.

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]

The word, taken apart.

Lesson 5 of 6.
fǔdǎo — to tutor; to give guidance.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for a private tutor, then the Mandarin for to educate.

You'll want to know: fǔdǎo.
fǔdǎo — "to tutor; to give guidance".
jiějie měi tiān wǎnshang fǔdǎo wǒ zuò zuòyè — My sister helps me with my homework every evening.
Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.
Two third tones meet in this word: fǔ is said with a rising tone before dǎo, and the spelling keeps both marks.

The word, taken apart.
fǔdǎo is built from fǔ 'to help' and dǎo 'to lead', both inherited from Middle Chinese, bjuX and dawH (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to assist' and the second 'to guide'. Both modern third tones are irregular: bjuX and dawH would regularly give fù and dào.
