## You'll want to know: جماہی
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C389-UNGHNA389-04]; assesses=[] -->

**جماہی** — *jamāhī* — "a yawn".

> رات لمبی تھی اور سارا کو جماہی آئی۔
>
> *rāt lambī thī aur sārā ko jamāhī āī.*
>
> The night was very long and Sara yawned.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**جماہی** (*jamāhī*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *jamhāī*, "a yawn", under *jṛmbhāyita*, a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 5266). A yawn comes to you: **سارا کو جماہی آئی**.
