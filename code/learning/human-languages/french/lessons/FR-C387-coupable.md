## You'll want to know: coupable
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C387-PROCES-04]; assesses=[] -->

**coupable** — "guilty".

> Pour le juge, il est coupable.
>
> *For the judge, he is guilty.*

Also a noun: *le coupable* — the culprit.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*coupable* is inherited from Latin *culpābilis* "blameworthy" (EtymDB), from *culpa* "fault, blame" (Lewis & Short).
