Telugu, chapter 202: To discuss, To contact, To check, To register, To practise.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
చర్చించు (carciñcu) — to discuss.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to shiver, then the Telugu for to move.

You'll want to know: చర్చించు.
చర్చించు — carciñcu — "to discuss".

The word, taken apart.
చర్చించు (carciñcu) is Sanskrit carcā, which CDIAL 4695 glosses "repetition", made into a verb with the Telugu ending -iñcu.

Lesson 3 of 5.
తనిఖీ చేయు (tanikhī cēyu) — to check, to inspect.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: తనిఖీ చేయు.
తనిఖీ చేయు — tanikhī cēyu — "to check, to inspect".

The word, taken apart.
The origin of tanikhī, "inspection", the first word of తనిఖీ చేయు (tanikhī cēyu), is uncertain; DEDR and CDIAL do not list it. Cēyu, "to do", is native (DEDR 1957).
