## You'll want to know: রটানো
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C298-UTTEJONA298-02]; assesses=[] -->

**রটানো** — *rôṭāno* — "to spread a rumour; to put about".

> কেউ রটাল যে চোর আবার আসবে।
>
> *keu rôṭālô je chor ābār āsbe.*
>
> Someone put it about that the thief would come back.

Past (he/she …): সেই লোক খবরটা রটাল, *sei lok khôbôrṭā rôṭālô*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**রটানো** (*rôṭāno*) is usually linked with Sanskrit *raṭati*, 'cries, yells' (CDIAL 10590): what is shouted about gets about.
