# आयोजन करणे (āyojan karṇe) — to organise

## You'll want to know: आयोजन करणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C324-SHRADDHA324-03]; assesses=[] -->

**आयोजन करणे** — *āyojan karṇe* — "to organise".

> मित्रांनी त्यांच्या आठवणीत एका सभेचं आयोजन केलं.
>
> *mitrānnī tyāñcyā āṭhavṇīt ekā sabhecaṁ āyojan kelaṁ.*
>
> Friends organised a meeting in his memory.

Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने आयोजन केलं, *tyāne āyojan kelaṁ*.

What is organised takes -चं: स्पर्धेचं आयोजन करणे.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**आयोजन करणे** (*āyojan karṇe*) joins *āyojan*, a tatsama taken from Sanskrit *āyojana*, 'a joining, a bringing together' (Monier-Williams), to *karṇe*, 'to do'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
