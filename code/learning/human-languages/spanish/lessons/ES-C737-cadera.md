## You'll want to know: la cadera
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C737-CUERPO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**la cadera** — "the hip".

> Mi abuela se rompió la cadera.
>
> *My grandmother broke her hip.*

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*cadera* is inherited from Latin *cathedra* "a seat, a chair", through spoken *cathegra*; the Latin came from Greek *kathédra* (EtymDB). The hips are what you sit on.
