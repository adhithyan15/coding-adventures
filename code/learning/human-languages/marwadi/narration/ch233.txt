Marwadi (Marwari), chapter 233: Camel Gear.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
पलाण (palāṇ) — a camel saddle; a pack-saddle.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for a pebble, then the Marwadi for a jujube bush.

You'll want to know: पलाण.
पलाण — palāṇ — "a camel saddle; a pack-saddle".
ऊंट माथै नुवो पलाण है। ū̃ṭ māthai nuvo palāṇ hai. The camel has a new saddle.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
पलाण (palāṇ) is inherited from Sanskrit palyāṇa, "a saddle"; CDIAL lists Hindi palān and Gujarati palāṇ, "a pack-saddle". CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry (CDIAL 7966).

Lesson 2 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for a jujube bush, then the Marwadi for a camel saddle.

You'll want to know: नकेल.
नकेल — nakel — "a camel's nose-peg and rein".
नकेल ढीली है। nakel ḍhīlī hai. The nose-rein is loose.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
नकेल (nakel) is built from a reconstructed nakka, "nose", and Sanskrit kīla, "a peg"; CDIAL lists Hindi nakel, the pin fixed in a camel's nose. CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry (CDIAL 6910).

Lesson 4 of 5.
कसणो (kasṇo) — to tighten; to harness.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for a camel's nose-peg, then the Marwadi for trappings (of a camel or a horse).

You'll want to know: कसणो.
कसणो — kasṇo — "to tighten; to harness".
हूं ऊंट माथै पलाण कसूं हूं। hū̃ ū̃ṭ māthai palāṇ kasū̃ hū̃. I tighten the saddle on the camel.
Past (I …): म्हैं पलाण कस्यो, mhaĩ palāṇ kasyo.

The word, taken apart.
कसणो (kasṇo) is inherited from Sanskrit karṣati, "draws, pulls"; CDIAL lists the Old Marwari verb kasaï, "harnesses, binds", and Hindi kasnā, "to tighten" (CDIAL 2908).

Lesson 5 of 5.
घूघरो (ghūghro) — a small bell (on a harness or an anklet).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for trappings (of a camel or a horse), then the Marwadi for to tighten.

You'll want to know: घूघरो.
घूघरो — ghūghro — "a small bell (on a harness or an anklet)".
हूं घूघरो सुणूं हूं। hū̃ ghūghro suṇū̃ hū̃. I hear the little bell.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
घूघरो (ghūghro) is inherited from a reconstructed ghuṅghura, "a bell", which CDIAL takes to imitate the sound; CDIAL lists Old Marwari ghūghara, "a string of bells" (CDIAL 4477).
