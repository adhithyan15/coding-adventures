# തടവ് (taṭavŭ) — imprisonment; custody

## You'll want to know: തടവ്
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C371-JAIL371-02]; assesses=[] -->

**തടവ്** — *taṭavŭ* — "imprisonment; custody".

> കോടതി അവന് അഞ്ച് വർഷം തടവ് വിധിച്ചു.
>
> *kōṭati avanŭ añcŭ varṣaṁ taṭavŭ vidhiccu.*
>
> The court sentenced him to five years in prison.

Prison terms are counted in years: രണ്ട് വർഷം തടവ്.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**തടവ്** (*taṭavŭ*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 3031 lists Tamil *taṭavu*, "prison", under a root meaning to hinder, to stop. Prison stops a person going anywhere.
