## You'll want to know: ਬਹਿਸ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C344-ARGUE344-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ਬਹਿਸ** — *bahis* — "a debate; a heated discussion".

> ਬਹਿਸ ਨਾ ਕਰੋ।
>
> *bahis nā karo.*
>
> Don't argue.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਬਹਿਸ** (*bahis*) is a loan, usually traced through Persian to Arabic *baḥth*, "discussion".
