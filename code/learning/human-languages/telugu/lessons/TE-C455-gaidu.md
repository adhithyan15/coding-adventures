## You'll want to know: గైడు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C455-PARYATANA455-03]; assesses=[] -->

**గైడు** — *gaiḍu* — "a tour guide".

> గైడు మాకు కోట చరిత్ర చెప్పాడు.
>
> *gaiḍu māku kōṭa caritra ceppāḍu.*
>
> The guide told us the history of the fort.

It takes -ఉ like other English loans. A guidebook is also called a గైడు.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**గైడు** (*gaiḍu*) is a loan from English *guide*, with the Telugu final -ఉ.
