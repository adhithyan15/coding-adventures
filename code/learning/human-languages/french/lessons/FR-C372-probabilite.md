## You'll want to know: la probabilité
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C372-RISQUE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**la probabilité** — "the probability, the likelihood".

> La probabilité de gagner est très faible.
>
> *The probability of winning is very low.*

*Selon toute probabilité* — in all likelihood.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*probabilité* is a learned borrowing from Latin *probābilitās* "likelihood" (Lewis & Short), from *probābilis* "likely", built on *probāre* "to test".
