## You'll want to know: kànfǎ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C256-THINK256-02]; assesses=[] -->

*kànfǎ* — "view; opinion".

> *wǒmen de kànfǎ yíyàng* — We see it the same way.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*kànfǎ* is built from 看 'to look' and *fǎ* 'a rule, a way', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *khanH* and *pjop* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'a rule': a way of looking.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying no and asking.
