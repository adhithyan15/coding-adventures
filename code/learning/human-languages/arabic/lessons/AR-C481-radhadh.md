## You'll want to know: رذاذ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C481-MATAR481-02]; assesses=[] -->

**رذاذ** — *radhādh* — "drizzle; spray".

> كان الرذاذ خفيفا في الصباح.
>
> *kāna ar-radhādh khafīfan fī aṣ-ṣabāḥ.*
>
> The drizzle was light in the morning.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**رذاذ** (*radhādh*) is usually traced to the native Arabic root *r-dh-dh*, "fine rain".
