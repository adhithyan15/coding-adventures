# dipende — the condition you do not have to finish

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[IT-GRAMMAR-SE-PRESENTE-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "if it rains, I stay at home". (*Se piove, resto a casa.*)

[PAUSE 1s] That sentence names its condition. Sometimes you want the
condition without saying what it is.

## You'll want to know: dipende
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-DIPENDE-01]; assesses=[IT-LEX-C179-VERBI179-02] -->

**dipende** — "it depends".

> — *Vai alla festa domani?* — *Dipende.*
> — Are you going to the party tomorrow? — It depends.

You have **dipendere**, "to depend". *Dipende* is its "it" form, and on its
own it is a complete answer: it says a condition exists and declines to say
which. That is exactly why it is so useful to a question you are not ready to
answer.

## Grammar Lens: dipende da
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-GRAMMAR-DIPENDE-DA-02]; assesses=[IT-GRAMMAR-SE-01, IT-LEX-C178-VERBI178-01, IT-LEX-C117-TEMPOLIBERO117-04] -->

Add **da** and you can name the condition after all:

> **Dipende da** te. — It depends on you.
> **Dipende dal** treno. — It depends on the train.

*da* + *il* is **dal**, the same welding as *al* and *del*. English says
*depend **on***; Italian says *dipende **da***, "hangs **from**", which is the
older picture.

Or name it with a whole *se*: *Dipende. Se piove, resto a casa.* The *dipende*
announces a condition; the *se* sentence states it.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**dipendere** comes from Latin *dēpendēre*, "to hang from, to hang down"
(Lewis & Short, under *dependeo*): *dē*, "down from", and *pendēre*, "to hang".
To depend on something is to **hang from** it, and the answer swings until the
condition settles. English **pendant** (a thing that hangs), **pending** (still
hanging) and **suspend** share the root.

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[IT-LEX-DIPENDE-01, IT-GRAMMAR-DIPENDE-DA-02, IT-LEX-C179-VERBI179-02] -->

[PAUSE 3s] What does *dipende* say, and what does it not say? (That there **is**
a condition — not **which**.) What do you add to name it? (***Da***: *dipende
da te*.) What is hanging, in *dēpendēre*? (**The answer**, from the condition.)
