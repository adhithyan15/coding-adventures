# хотя бы (khotyá by) — at least

## You'll want to know: хотя бы
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C376-ISKLYUCHENIYE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**хотя бы** (*khotyá by*) — "at least".

> Он мог бы хотя бы позвонить.
>
> *On mog by khotyá by pozvonít.*
>
> He could at least have called.

It sets a minimum: *хотя бы немного* — at least a little. Not the same as *хотя*, although.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**хотя бы** is built from *хотя*, the old gerund of *хотеть* "to want", and *бы*, the particle of the conditional: wanting it to be so, at the very least.
