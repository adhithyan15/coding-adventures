## You'll want to know: स्वयंवरः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C339-YUVARAJA339-02]; assesses=[] -->

**स्वयंवरः** — *svayaṃvaraḥ* — "a bride's choice of a husband".

> श्वः प्रासादे स्वयंवरः भविष्यति।
>
> *śvaḥ prāsāde svayaṃvaraḥ bhaviṣyati.*
>
> Tomorrow a princess will choose her husband at the palace.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**स्वयंवरः** (*svayaṃvaraḥ*) is built from *svayam*, "self", and *vara*, "choosing", from the root √vṛ; MW gives *svayaṃvara* as self-choice, the choosing of a husband by a princess at an assembly of suitors (MW).
