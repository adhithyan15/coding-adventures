## You'll want to know: el depósito
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C646-GASOLINA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**el depósito** — "the tank; the deposit".

> Llene el depósito, por favor.
>
> *Fill the tank, please.*

At a bank *el depósito* is a deposit.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*depósito* is a learned borrowing from Latin *dēpositum* "something laid down, entrusted", from *dēpōnere* "to put down" (Lewis & Short), built on *pōnere* "to put".
