## You'll want to know: સૂચના
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C198-SIGNS198-02]; assesses=[] -->

**સૂચના** — *sūcnā* — "announcement, notice".

> **પાટિયા પર સૂચના છે.** — *pāṭiyā par sūcnā che* — "There is a notice on the board."

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**સૂચના** (*sūcnā*) is Sanskrit *sūcanā*, "indication, information", taken over whole as a learned loan, a tatsama. CDIAL has no entry for it.
