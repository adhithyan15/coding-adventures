## You'll want to know: અખાડો
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C527-AKHADO527-01]; assesses=[] -->

**અખાડો** — *akhāḍo* — "a wrestling ground".

> અમે રોજ સવારે ગામના અખાડામાં કસરત કરીએ છીએ.
>
> *ame roj savāre gāmnā akhāḍāmā̃ kasrat karīe chīe.*
>
> Every morning we exercise at the village wrestling ground.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**અખાડો** (*akhāḍo*) is inherited from Sanskrit *akṣavāṭa*, "a wrestling ground", and CDIAL lists the Gujarati word (CDIAL 39).
