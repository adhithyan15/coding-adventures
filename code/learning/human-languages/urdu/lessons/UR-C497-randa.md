## You'll want to know: رندا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C497-TAKHTA497-03]; assesses=[] -->

**رندا** — *randā* — "a carpenter's plane".

> اس نے تختے پر رندا چلایا۔
>
> *us ne takhte par randā chalāyā.*
>
> He ran a plane over the plank.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**رندا** (*randā*) is usually traced to Persian *randa*, "a plane", the carpenter's tool.
