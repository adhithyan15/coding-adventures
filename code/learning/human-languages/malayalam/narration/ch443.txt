Malayalam, chapter 443: Effects and Limits.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ബാധിക്കുക (bādhikkuka) — to affect.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for to estimate, then the Malayalam for a consequence.

You'll want to know: ബാധിക്കുക.
ബാധിക്കുക — bādhikkuka — "to affect".
പനി ഗ്രാമത്തിലെ പല കുട്ടികളെയും ബാധിച്ചു. pani grāmattile pala kuṭṭikaḷeyuṁ bādhiccu. The fever affected many children in the village.
Past: ബാധിച്ചു, bādhiccu.
What is affected takes -നെ. Usually something bad: an illness, a flood, a price rise.

The word, taken apart.
ബാധിക്കുക (bādhikkuka) is built from the Sanskrit root bādh, "to press hard, to harass", whose noun bādha is "affliction, hurt, damage" (Monier-Williams), with the Malayalam verb ending -ിക്കുക.

സ്വാധീനിക്കുക — svādhīnikkuka — "to influence".
അമ്മയുടെ വാക്കുകൾ അവന്റെ തീരുമാനത്തെ സ്വാധീനിച്ചു. ammayuṭe vākkukaḷ avanṟe tīrumānatte svādhīniccu. Mother's words influenced his decision.
Past: സ്വാധീനിച്ചു, svādhīniccu.
The noun is സ്വാധീനം, influence.

The word, taken apart.
സ്വാധീനിക്കുക (svādhīnikkuka) is built from Sanskrit svādhīna, "dependent on oneself, under one's own control" (Monier-Williams), with the Malayalam verb ending -ിക്കുക: to bring a thing under one's sway.

Lesson 3 of 5.
ബദൽ (badal) — an alternative; a substitute.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for to affect, then the Malayalam for to influence.

You'll want to know: ബദൽ.
ബദൽ — badal — "an alternative; a substitute".
ആ പദ്ധതിക്ക് ഒരു ബദൽ കണ്ടെത്തണം. ā paddhatikkŭ oru badal kaṇṭettaṇaṁ. An alternative to that plan must be found.
As an adjective it comes first: ബദൽ (badal) വഴി, an alternative route.

The word, taken apart.
ബദൽ (badal) is usually traced to Hindustani badal, from Arabic badal, "a substitute, an exchange".

Lesson 4 of 5.
പരിധി (paridhi) — a limit.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for to influence, then the Malayalam for an alternative.

You'll want to know: പരിധി.
പരിധി — paridhi — "a limit".
എല്ലാത്തിനും ഒരു പരിധി ഉണ്ട്. ellāttinuṁ oru paridhi uṇṭŭ. Everything has a limit.
Within the limit: പരിധിക്കുള്ളിൽ.

The word, taken apart.
പരിധി (paridhi) is borrowed from Sanskrit paridhi, "an enclosure, a fence, a wall" (Monier-Williams): a limit is the fence round a thing.

Lesson 5 of 5.
സമയപരിധി (samayaparidhi) — a deadline; a time limit.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for an alternative, then the Malayalam for a limit.

You'll want to know: സമയപരിധി.
സമയപരിധി — samayaparidhi — "a deadline; a time limit".
അപേക്ഷ നൽകാനുള്ള സമയപരിധി നാളെ തീരും. apēkṣa nalkānuḷḷa samayaparidhi nāḷe tīruṁ. The deadline for applying ends tomorrow.
സമയം, time, and പരിധി (paridhi), a limit.

The word, taken apart.
സമയപരിധി (samayaparidhi) is a Malayalam compound built from Sanskrit samaya, "a meeting; an appointed time" (CDIAL 13185), and paridhi, "an enclosure" (Monier-Williams): the fence round a stretch of time.
