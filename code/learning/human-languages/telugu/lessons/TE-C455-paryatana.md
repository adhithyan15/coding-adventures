## You'll want to know: పర్యటన
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C455-PARYATANA455-02]; assesses=[] -->

**పర్యటన** — *paryaṭana* — "a tour; an official visit".

> మంత్రి పర్యటన వల్ల రోడ్లు మూసివేశారు.
>
> *mantri paryaṭana valla rōḍlu mūsivēśāru.*
>
> The roads were closed because of the minister's visit.

A tour of places, and also the official visit of a leader. పర్యటించు, to tour, is its verb.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**పర్యటన** (*paryaṭana*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *paryaṭana*, "wandering about, roaming through" (Monier-Williams).
