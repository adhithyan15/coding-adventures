## You'll want to know: मस्ती
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C329-RANG329-05]; assesses=[] -->

**मस्ती** — *mastī* — "merriment; high spirits".

> त्योहार के दिन बच्चे सुबह से मस्ती में थे।
>
> *tyohār ke din bacce subah se mastī meṁ the.*
>
> On the day of the festival the children were in high spirits from the morning on.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**मस्ती** (*mastī*) is *mast*, carefree and merry, usually traced to Persian *mast*, "intoxicated", with the noun ending *-ī*: the joy of a day with no work.
