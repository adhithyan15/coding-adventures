Russian, chapter 410: A small accident.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
столкновение (stolknovéniye) — a collision; a clash.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a jack, then the Russian for a car repair shop.

You'll want to know: столкновение.
столкновение (stolknovéniye) — "a collision; a clash".
На перекрёстке произошло столкновение двух машин. Na perekryóstke proizoshló stolknovéniye dvukh mashín. There was a collision between two cars at the crossroads.
Neuter; formal. In speech people say авария.

The word, taken apart.
столкновение (stolknovéniye) is built on столкнуться 'to collide', from с- 'together', толкнуть 'to push' and -ся. EtymDB lists толкать without a deeper source.

Lesson 2 of 6.
сталкиваться (stálkivatsya) — to collide; to come up against.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a car repair shop, then the Russian for a collision.

You'll want to know: сталкиваться.
сталкиваться (stálkivatsya) — "to collide; to come up against".
Зимой на этой дороге часто сталкиваются машины. Zimóy na étoy doróge chásto stálkivayutsya mashíny. In winter cars often collide on this road.
Imperfective; the perfective is столкнуться. Takes с + instrumental: столкнуться с автобусом.

The word, taken apart.
сталкиваться (stálkivatsya) is built from с- 'together', -талкивать, from толкать 'to push', and -ся: to push into each other. EtymDB lists толкать without a deeper source.

Lesson 3 of 6.
врезаться (vrézatsya) — to crash (into).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a collision, then the Russian for to collide.

You'll want to know: врезаться.
врезаться (vrézatsya) — "to crash (into)".
Водитель не увидел столб и врезался в него. Vodítel ne uvídel stolb i vrézalsya v negó. The driver did not notice the post and crashed into it.
Perfective; the imperfective врезаться (vrézatsya) (vrezátsya) has the stress on the а. Takes в + accusative. Future врежусь, врежешься.

The word, taken apart.
врезаться (vrézatsya) is built from в- 'into', резать 'to cut' and -ся: to cut one's way into something. Резать is inherited from Proto-Slavic rězati (EtymDB).

Lesson 4 of 6.
сбивать (sbivát) — to knock down; to knock off.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to collide, then the Russian for to crash.

You'll want to know: сбивать.
сбивать (sbivát) — "to knock down; to knock off".
Сильный ветер сбивал людей с ног. Sílnyy véter sbivál lyudéy s nog. The strong wind was knocking people off their feet.
Imperfective; perfective сбить (собью, собьёшь). Машина сбила собаку — a car hit a dog.

The word, taken apart.
сбивать (sbivát) is built from с- 'down, off' and -бивать, from бить 'to beat'. Бить is inherited from Proto-Slavic biti 'to beat' (EtymDB).

Lesson 5 of 6.
вмятина (vmyátina) — a dent.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to crash, then the Russian for to knock down.

You'll want to know: вмятина.
вмятина (vmyátina) — "a dent".
На двери машины теперь большая вмятина. Na dverí mashíny tepér bolsháya vmyátina. There is now a big dent in the car door.
Feminine. Built on вмять — to press in.

The word, taken apart.
вмятина (vmyátina) is built on вмять 'to press in', from в- 'into' and мять 'to crumple, to knead' (EtymDB lists мять with that sense).
