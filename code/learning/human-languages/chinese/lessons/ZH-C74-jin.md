## You'll want to know: 进
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C74-VISIT74-01]; assesses=[] -->

**进** — *jìn* — "to come in; to enter".

> **他进门了** *tā jìn mén le* — He's come in at the door.

The new character, in pieces: 进 — a four-stroke piece read jǐng, a well, on the road piece of 这. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**进** (*jìn*) is inherited from Middle Chinese *tsinH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'forward' and 'to advance'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for meeting people.
