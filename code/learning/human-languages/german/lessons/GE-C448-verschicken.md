## You'll want to know: verschicken
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C448-POST-02]; assesses=[] -->

**verschicken** — "to send (out)".

> Zu Weihnachten verschickt sie viele Karten.
>
> *At Christmas she sends lots of cards.*

*Verschicken* often means sending many things, or sending something away.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*verschicken* is built from *ver-* and *schicken* 'to send', inherited from Middle High German *schicken* 'to arrange, to put in order' (EtymDB).
