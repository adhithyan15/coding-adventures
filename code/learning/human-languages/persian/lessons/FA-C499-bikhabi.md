## You'll want to know: بیخوابی
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C499-SLEEP-01]; assesses=[] -->

**بیخوابی** (*bikhâbi*) — "insomnia, sleeplessness".

> او از بیخوابی رنج میبرد.
>
> *u az bikhâbi ranj mibarad.*
>
> She suffers from insomnia.

*bi-* 'without' + *khâb* 'sleep' + *-i*. *az chizi ranj bordan* — to suffer from something.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*bikhâbi* is built on native Persian parts: *bi-*, 'without', *khâb*, 'sleep', which EtymDB traces to Middle Persian and on to PIE *swep-*, 'to sleep' (EtymDB), and the ending *-i*.
