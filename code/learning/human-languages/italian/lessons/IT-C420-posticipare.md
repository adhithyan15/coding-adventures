## You'll want to know: posticipare
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C420-RITARDO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**posticipare** — "to postpone, to put back".

> Hanno posticipato la partenza a domani.
>
> *They put the departure back to tomorrow.*

A more formal word than *rimandare*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*posticipare* is a learned formation on Latin *post* 'after' (Lewis & Short), shaped on the model of *anticipare* 'to bring forward'.

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
