## Script — a frame, and five strokes inside it
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-SCRIPT-TU-CHART-01]; assesses=[] -->

> 图

**A frame and what is inside it**, as in 国: the frame opens first, takes its inside, and closes last.

**The frame, three strokes.** A vertical down the left. A bar that turns down the right side. The bar across the foot comes last, after the inside.

**The inside, five strokes.** A short stroke falling to the left. A bar that turns down to the left. A long stroke falling to the right, across it. Then two dots, one under the other.

2 + 5 + 1 = eight.
