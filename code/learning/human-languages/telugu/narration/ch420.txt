Telugu, chapter 420: Making Choices.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ఎంపిక (empika) — a choice; a selection.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for in any case, then the Telugu for to occur.

You'll want to know: ఎంపిక.
ఎంపిక — empika — "a choice; a selection".
ఆ ఉద్యోగానికి ఆమెను తీసుకోవడం మంచి ఎంపిక. ā udyōgāniki āmenu tīsukōvaḍaṁ mañci empika. Taking her on for that job was a good choice.
ఎంపిక (empika) చేయు, to select; ఎంపికైన, selected.

The word, taken apart.
ఎంపిక (empika) is a Telugu noun whose origin is not settled; DEDR does not list it.

Lesson 2 of 5.
ప్రత్యామ్నాయం (pratyāmnāyaṁ) — an alternative.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to occur, then the Telugu for a choice.

You'll want to know: ప్రత్యామ్నాయం.
ప్రత్యామ్నాయం — pratyāmnāyaṁ — "an alternative".
మాకు వేరే ప్రత్యామ్నాయం లేదు. māku vērē pratyāmnāyaṁ lēdu. We have no other alternative.
As an adjective: ప్రత్యామ్నాయ ఏర్పాటు, an alternative arrangement.

The word, taken apart.
ప్రత్యామ్నాయం (pratyāmnāyaṁ) is borrowed from Sanskrit pratyāmnāya, a term of logic for "the proposition restated" (Monier-Williams); the Telugu sense "an alternative" is modern.

Lesson 3 of 5.
పరిగణించు (parigaṇiñcu) — to take into account.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a choice, then the Telugu for an alternative.

You'll want to know: పరిగణించు.
పరిగణించు — parigaṇiñcu — "to take into account".
అతని వయసును పరిగణించి అతన్ని క్షమించారు. atani vayasunu parigaṇiñci atanni kṣamiñcāru. Taking his age into account, they forgave him.
Past (I …): పరిగణించాను, parigaṇiñcānu.
పరిగణనలోకి తీసుకో, to take into consideration.

The word, taken apart.
పరిగణించు (parigaṇiñcu) is built on Sanskrit parigaṇana (Monier-Williams), pari-, "all round", with gaṇana, "counting" (CDIAL 3992), with the Telugu verb ending -ఇంచు.

Lesson 4 of 5.
ఐచ్ఛికం (aicchikaṁ) — optional.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for an alternative, then the Telugu for to take into account.

You'll want to know: ఐచ్ఛికం.
ఐచ్ఛికం — aicchikaṁ — "optional".
ఆ పాఠం ఐచ్ఛికం, తప్పనిసరి కాదు. ā pāṭhaṁ aicchikaṁ, tappanisari kādu. That lesson is optional, not compulsory.
The opposite of తప్పనిసరి, compulsory, which you know.

The word, taken apart.
ఐచ్ఛికం (aicchikaṁ) is borrowed from Sanskrit aicchika, "optional, arbitrary, at will" (Monier-Williams), from icchā, "wish".

Lesson 5 of 5.
వదులుకో (vadulukō) — to let go of; to give up.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to take into account, then the Telugu for optional.

You'll want to know: వదులుకో.
వదులుకో — vadulukō — "to let go of; to give up".
అతను ఆ ఉద్యోగం కోసం తన చదువు వదులుకున్నాడు. atanu ā udyōgaṁ kōsaṁ tana caduvu vadulukunnāḍu. He gave up his studies for that job.
Past (I …): వదులుకున్నాను, vadulukunnānu.
వదులు, to let loose, + -కో: to let a thing go from oneself.

The word, taken apart.
వదులుకో (vadulukō) is native Dravidian: DEDR 5243 lists Telugu vadalu, "to leave, to abandon, to let loose"; -కో, from konu, "to take" (DEDR 2151), turns it back on the doer.
