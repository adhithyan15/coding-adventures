## You'll want to know: atracar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C518-ROBO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**atracar** — "to hold up, to mug; to moor".

> Dos hombres atracaron un banco en el centro.
>
> *Two men held up a bank in the centre.*

*Un atraco* is a hold-up. For a boat, *atracar* means to moor.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

The origin of *atracar* is not settled. The sense 'to hold up, to mug' is often linked to the sailors' verb *atracar* 'to bring a boat alongside'. EtymDB traces a Spanish *atracar* to an Arabic verb, without saying which sense it means.
