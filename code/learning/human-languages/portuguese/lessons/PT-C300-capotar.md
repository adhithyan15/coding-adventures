## You'll want to know: capotar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C300-CAPOTAR-01]; assesses=[] -->

**capotar** — "to overturn, to flip over".

> O carro capotou duas vezes.
>
> *The car flipped over twice.*

Of cars and other vehicles.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*capotar* is usually traced to French *capoter* "to overturn"; EtymDB has no entry, and the origin is not settled.
