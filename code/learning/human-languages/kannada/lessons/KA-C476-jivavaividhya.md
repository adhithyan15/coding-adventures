## You'll want to know: ಜೀವವೈವಿಧ್ಯ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C476-VANYAJIVI476-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ಜೀವವೈವಿಧ್ಯ** — *jīvavaividhya* — "biodiversity".

> ಈ ಕಾಡಿನ ಜೀವವೈವಿಧ್ಯ ತುಂಬಾ ಶ್ರೀಮಂತ.
>
> *ī kāḍina jīvavaividhya tumbā śrīmanta.*
>
> The biodiversity of this forest is very rich.

ಜೀವ, life, plus ವೈವಿಧ್ಯ, variety.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಜೀವವೈವಿಧ್ಯ** (*jīvavaividhya*) is a modern compound built from Sanskrit *jīva*, "a living being, life" (CDIAL 5239), and *vaividhya*, "variety", from *vividha*, "of many kinds".
