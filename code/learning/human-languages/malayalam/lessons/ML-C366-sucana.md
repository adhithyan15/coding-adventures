## You'll want to know: സൂചന
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C366-SUCHANA366-01]; assesses=[] -->

**സൂചന** — *sūcana* — "a clue; a hint".

> പോലീസിന് കള്ളനെക്കുറിച്ച് ഒരു സൂചന കിട്ടി.
>
> *pōlīsinŭ kaḷḷanekkuṟiccŭ oru sūcana kiṭṭi.*
>
> The police got a clue about the thief.

Also a hint or a sign: മഴയുടെ സൂചന, a sign of rain.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**സൂചന** (*sūcana*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *sūcanā*, "pointing out, indication, communication" (Monier-Williams).
