## You'll want to know: casar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C549-CASADO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**casar** — "to marry (someone to someone)".

> Los casó el alcalde.
>
> *The mayor married them.*

Without *se* it is the person who performs the wedding. *Casarse* is to get married.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*casar* is built on *casa* 'house', which is inherited from Latin *casa* 'a hut, a cottage' (Lewis & Short).
