## You'll want to know: ধূর্ত
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C458-SOT458-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ধূর্ত** — *dhūrtô* — "cunning; crafty".

> ধূর্ত শিয়ালটা কাকের রুটি নিয়ে পালাল।
>
> *dhūrtô shiyālṭā kāker ruṭi niye pālālô.*
>
> The crafty jackal ran off with the crow's bread.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ধূর্ত** (*dhūrtô*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *dhūrta*, "cunning, crafty", and as a noun "a rogue, a cheat" (Monier-Williams).
