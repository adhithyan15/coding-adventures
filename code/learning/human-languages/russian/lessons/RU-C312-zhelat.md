## You'll want to know: желать
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C312-POZDRAVLYAT-04]; assesses=[] -->

**желать** (*zhelát*) — "to wish".

> Желаю тебе счастья!
>
> *Zheláyu tebé schástya!*
>
> I wish you happiness!

Imperfective; perfective *пожелать* (*pozhelát*). The person is in the dative, the wish in the genitive: *желаю удачи*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*желать* is inherited from Proto-Slavic *želati* "to wish, to long for" (EtymDB).
