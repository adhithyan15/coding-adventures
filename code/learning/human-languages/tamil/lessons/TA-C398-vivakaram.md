# விவகாரம் (vivakāram) — a matter; an affair

## You'll want to know: விவகாரம்
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C398-POKKU398-05]; assesses=[] -->

**விவகாரம்** — *vivakāram* — "a matter; an affair".

> இந்த விவகாரம் நீதிமன்றத்துக்குப் போனால் நீண்ட காலம் ஆகும்.
>
> *inta vivakāram nītimaṉṟattukkup pōṉāl nīṇṭa kālam ākum.*
>
> If this matter goes to court, it will take a long time.

Often a disputed matter; in speech also a fuss.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**விவகாரம்** (*vivakāram*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *vyavahāra*, "behaviour, custom, business" (CDIAL 12174), whose Prakrit form also means "lawsuit". Tamil writes vya as vi and h as க.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
