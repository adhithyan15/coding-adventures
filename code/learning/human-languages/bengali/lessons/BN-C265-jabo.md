# আমি যাব (āmi jābô) — the future, person by person

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[BN-GRAMMAR-C264-PAST-PERSON-01, BN-GRAMMAR-C264-WAS-01, BN-CONCEPT-C04-KALDEKHAHOBE-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "Yesterday I was at home". (*kāl āmi bāṛite chhilām*.) Then the goodbye from chapter seven: **কাল দেখা হবে**, "see you tomorrow". Its last word, **হবে**, is a future: "it will happen".

## You'll want to know: the future, person by person
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-GRAMMAR-C265-FUTURE-PERSON-01]; assesses=[BN-LEX-C82-PLACE82-05, BN-LEX-C07-JAOWA-01, BN-LEX-C24-AMRA-01, BN-CONCEPT-C02-TUMIAPNI-01, BN-LEX-C23-SHE-01] -->

> **কাল আমি স্টেশনে যাব।**
>
> *kāl āmi sṭeshône jābô.* — "Tomorrow I will go to the station."

With a future, **কাল** is tomorrow. The future puts **-ব-** on the stem, then the person:

- **আমি** / **আমরা** **যাব** (*jābô*) — I will go, we will go
- **তুমি** / **সে** **যাবে** (*jābe*) — you will go; he or she will go
- **আপনি** / **তিনি** **যাবেন** (*jāben*) — with respect
- **তোমরা** / **তারা** **যাবে** — you, to several; they

**তুমি** and **সে** share **-বে** here; the past kept them apart (**গেলে**, **গেল**). The stem is the plain one, not the past's: **করব** (*kôrbô*), **দেখব** (*dekhbô*), **খাব** (*khābô*), **আসব** (*āsbô*), **থাকব** (*thākbô*); **দেওয়া** → **দেব** (*debô*), **নেওয়া** → **নেব** (*nebô*); **হওয়া** → **হবে** (*hôbe*), "will be, will happen". You have said one **-বেন** for a long time: **মাফ করবেন**, "excuse me".

## You'll want to know: will not
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-GRAMMAR-C265-FUTURE-NOT-01]; assesses=[BN-LEX-C84-TOWN84-03, BN-CONCEPT-C05-THAKA-01, BN-LEX-C59-FOOD59-04] -->

"Will not" puts **না** after the verb: **আমি যাব না** (*āmi jābô nā*), "I won't go".

> **কাল আমি বাজারে যাব না, বাড়িতে থাকব।**
>
> *kāl āmi bājāre jābô nā, bāṛite thākbô.* — "Tomorrow I won't go to the market; I'll stay at home."
