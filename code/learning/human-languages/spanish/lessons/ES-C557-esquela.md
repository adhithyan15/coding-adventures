## You'll want to know: la esquela
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C557-LUTO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**la esquela** — "the death notice".

> Leí su esquela en el periódico.
>
> *I read his death notice in the paper.*

A notice in the paper or on a wall telling people about a death and the funeral.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*esquela* is usually traced to Latin *schedula* 'a small sheet of paper' (Lewis & Short), first any short note and later the notice of a death.
