## You'll want to know: сворачивать
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C407-OBGON-03]; assesses=[] -->

**сворачивать** (*svoráchivat*) — "to turn (off a road), to turn (into a street)".

> После моста мы всегда сворачиваем направо.
>
> *Pósle mósta my vsegdá svoráchivayem naprávo.*
>
> After the bridge we always turn right.

Imperfective; perfective *свернуть*. *Свернуть налево* — to turn left.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*сворачивать* is built from *с-* and *-ворачивать*, on the root of *вертеть* 'to turn'. EtymDB traces *вертеть* to Proto-Slavic *vĭrtěti* and the PIE root *wert-* 'to turn'.
