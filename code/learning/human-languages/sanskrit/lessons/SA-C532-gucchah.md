## You'll want to know: गुच्छः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C532-UPAYANA532-02]; assesses=[] -->

**गुच्छः** — *gucchaḥ* — "a bunch of flowers".

> यदि पुष्पाणि सुन्दराणि सन्ति, तर्हि एकं गुच्छं क्रीणामि।
>
> *yadi puṣpāṇi sundarāṇi santi, tarhi ekaṃ gucchaṃ krīṇāmi.*
>
> If the flowers are pretty, I'll buy a bunch.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**गुच्छः** (*gucchaḥ*) is the word MW gives for a bush and for a bunch of flowers, a cluster of blossoms, the same as *gutsa*; MW gives no further derivation, so its origin is uncertain (MW).
