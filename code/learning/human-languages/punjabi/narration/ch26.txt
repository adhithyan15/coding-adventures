Punjabi, chapter 26: Form Retrieval at R2 and R3.
3 lessons. The first lesson already needs your eyes or your hands, so save this one for when you have stopped.


Lesson 1 of 3.
Three fields return at R2 — retrieve the three-field no-model task and its bounded repair passes at R2.

Before we start: this one needs your hands, so it is not a driving lesson. You will want a pen and something to write on. You can listen to everything else now. I will say so again when we reach it, but leave this section until you have stopped: Wrap-up recall.

Warm-up.
No new labels or values appear. Keep every bank closed and recall the order language, residence, work.

Writing — one no-model form.
Complete the three fields for cues B, A, B. Finish the whole attempt before checking anything.

Guided Practice — one bounded repair pass.
Compare selection, spelling, spacing, then placement. Repair only the first differing dimension. If the work row differs, repair that row only.

[once you have stopped driving — this part needs your eyes or your hands; if you are driving, skip ahead to the next part]
Wrap-up recall.
Stop after one form. This is retrieval, not a longer writing test.
[once you have stopped driving — question: With every bank closed, complete language, residence, and work for cues B, A, B; then repair only the first differing dimension.]


Lesson 2 of 3.
Rebuild one work row at R3 — rebuild the known job value and its work-field mapping at R3.

Before we start: this one needs your hands, so it is not a driving lesson. You will want a pen and something to write on, your eyes, for letter shapes on the page and your eyes or your hands for the steps that ask you to read, point, write or gesture. You can listen to everything else now. I will say so again when we reach them, but leave these sections until you have stopped: Warm-up, Script and value recall and Wrap-up recall.

[once you have stopped driving — this part needs your eyes or your hands; if you are driving, skip ahead to the next part]
Warm-up.
Read the three labels in order: ਭਾਸ਼ਾ, ਰਿਹਾਇਸ਼, ਕੰਮ. Point to the work label and stop.

[once you have stopped driving — this part needs your eyes or your hands; if you are driving, skip ahead to the next part]
Script and value recall.
Trace ੌ once above ਨ, making ਨੌ. Then read the known value ਨੌਕਰੀ once.

Writing — one supported work row.
Use the old bank once: A — ਖੇਤੀ and B — ਨੌਕਰੀ.
Choose B. Complete ਕੰਮ: __________. Keep the bank visible for this one attempt.

[once you have stopped driving — this part needs your eyes or your hands; if you are driving, skip ahead to the next part]
Wrap-up recall.
One supported row is enough. The next lesson removes the bank after a short delay.
[once you have stopped driving — question: After naming the third field as work, use visible-bank cue B to complete ਕੰਮ: ______.]


Lesson 3 of 3.
Hide, write, and check one work row at R3 — retrieve one work value after a delay and check its four known controls at R3.

Before we start: this one needs your hands, so it is not a driving lesson. You will want a pen and something to write on and your eyes or your hands for the steps that ask you to read, point, write or gesture. You can listen to everything else now. I will say so again when we reach them, but leave these sections until you have stopped: Warm-up and Wrap-up recall.

[once you have stopped driving — this part needs your eyes or your hands; if you are driving, skip ahead to the next part]
Warm-up.
Read the old work bank A — ਖੇਤੀ, B — ਨੌਕਰੀ. Select B, hide the bank, and count slowly to ten.

Writing — one delayed row.
With the bank hidden, complete ਕੰਮ: __________ for B.

Guided Practice — four checks, no rewrite.
Reveal the model. Check the value piece by piece, the one space after the colon, and that the job value stayed on the work field. Repair only the first difference.

[once you have stopped driving — this part needs your eyes or your hands; if you are driving, skip ahead to the next part]
Wrap-up recall.
Stop after one delayed row. This closes the bounded R3 retrieval pass without adding a new form field.
[once you have stopped driving — question: Select B from the work bank, hide it for ten seconds, write the work row, then name the three checks.]
