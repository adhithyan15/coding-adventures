## You'll want to know: ठरणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C387-GANANA387-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ठरणे** — *ṭharṇe* — "to turn out; to be settled".

> त्याचा निर्णय योग्य ठरला.
>
> *tyācā nirṇay yogya ṭharlā.*
>
> His decision turned out right.

Past (he …): तो ठरला, *to ṭharlā*.

Also to be fixed: लग्न ठरलं; ठरवणे, to decide, is its causative.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ठरणे** (*ṭharṇe*) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi *ṭharṇẽ*, "to be settled", under *\*stharati*, "is firm" (CDIAL 13742).
