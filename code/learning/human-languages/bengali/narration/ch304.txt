Bengali, chapter 304: Festival Time.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
উৎসব (utsôb) — a festival.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for in the course of time, then the Bengali for to commemorate.

You'll want to know: উৎসব.
উৎসব — utsôb — "a festival".
এই গ্রামে বসন্তে একটা বড় উৎসব হয়। ei grāme bôsônte ekṭā bôṛo utsôb hôy. There is a big festival in this village in spring.

The word, taken apart.
উৎসব (utsôb) is Sanskrit utsava, first 'an undertaking, a beginning' and then 'a festival' (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 2 of 5.
পুজো (pujo) — a puja; a worship festival.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for to commemorate, then the Bengali for a festival.

You'll want to know: পুজো.
পুজো — pujo — "a puja; a worship festival".
পুজোর সময় সবাই নতুন জামা পরে। pujor sômôy sôbāi nôtun jāmā pôre. At puja time everyone wears new clothes.

The word, taken apart.
পুজো (pujo) is the spoken form of Sanskrit pūjā, 'honour, worship' (Monier-Williams), which the written word পূজা keeps. In Bengal পুজো alone means the great autumn festival.

Lesson 4 of 5.
প্যান্ডেল (pænḍel) — a pandal; a festival marquee.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a puja, then the Bengali for an idol.

You'll want to know: প্যান্ডেল.
প্যান্ডেল — pænḍel — "a pandal; a festival marquee".
পাড়ার প্যান্ডেলে রাতে অনেক ভিড় হয়। pāṛār pænḍele rāte ônek bhiṛ hôy. At night there's a big crowd at the neighbourhood pandal.

The word, taken apart.
প্যান্ডেল (pænḍel) is Indian English pandal, a marquee of bamboo and cloth, a word usually traced to Tamil pantal, 'a shed'. Its initial প্যা is read pæ.

Lesson 5 of 5.
উদযাপন করা (udjāpôn kôrā) — to celebrate.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for an idol, then the Bengali for a pandal.

You'll want to know: উদযাপন করা.
উদযাপন করা — udjāpôn kôrā — "to celebrate".
গ্রামের মানুষ নাচ আর গান দিয়ে উৎসব উদযাপন করল। grāmer mānush nāch ār gān diye utsôb udjāpôn kôrlô. The villagers celebrated the festival with dancing and songs.
Past (he/she …): তিনি জন্মদিন উদযাপন করলেন, tini jônmôdin udjāpôn kôrlen.

The word, taken apart.
উদযাপন করা (udjāpôn kôrā) puts Sanskrit udyāpana, 'bringing to a conclusion' (Monier-Williams), the closing rite of a vow, before করা; Bengali uses it for celebrating.
