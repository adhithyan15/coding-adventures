## You'll want to know: வருகை
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C400-RAYIL400-03]; assesses=[] -->

**வருகை** — *varukai* — "arrival; a visit".

> விமானத்தின் வருகை ஒரு மணி நேரம் தாமதமானது.
>
> *vimāṉattiṉ varukai oru maṇi nēram tāmatamāṉatu.*
>
> The plane's arrival was an hour late.

வருகை is a noun made from வா, to come: its stem வரு- takes the ending -கை.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**வருகை** (*varukai*) is built on *varu-*, the stem of வா, "to come", with the noun ending *-kai*; DEDR 5270 gathers the family, including வரவு, "coming, income".
