# Ask one question and the ending picks itself

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MR-POSTP-BAROBAR, MR-POSTP-SHII, MR-POSTP-NE-INSTR] -->

[PAUSE 4s] English says *with* three times and means three things. Name the
three Marathi endings that keep them apart.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MR-POSTP-BAROBAR, MR-POSTP-SHII, MR-POSTP-NE-INSTR, MR-GRAMMAR-PRON-OBLIQUE, MR-LEX-MITRA, MR-QUESTION-KON, MR-GRAMMAR-OBLIQUE] -->

[PAUSE 1s]
- [YOU SAY: *mitrābarobar* — alongside]
- [YOU SAY: *mitrāśī* — engaged with]
- [YOU SAY: *ḍoḷyāne* — by means of, which no person takes]
- [YOU SAY: *mājhyābarobar* — and notice the pronoun bent from its possessive]
- [YOU RECALL: ask *koṇ tumchyābarobar āhe?* — who is with you?]

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MR-POSTP-NE-INSTR, MR-GRAMMAR-PRON-OBLIQUE] -->

[PAUSE 3s] One question sorts all three: **is this a companion, a partner in
the act, or a tool?** Which ending does the tool take? (**-ने**.) Which form
of a pronoun feeds all three? (**The possessive, bent to -या**.)
