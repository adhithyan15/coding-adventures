## You'll want to know: कांधो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C251-KANDHO251-02]; assesses=[] -->

**कांधो** — *kā̃dho* — "a shoulder".

> म्हारो कांधो भारी लागै है।
>
> *mhāro kā̃dho bhārī lāgai hai.*
>
> My shoulder feels heavy.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**कांधो** (*kā̃dho*) is inherited from Sanskrit *skandha*, "shoulder". CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry; it lists Hindi *kā̃dhā* (CDIAL 13627).
