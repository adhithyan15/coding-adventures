## You'll want to know: pernoitar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C428-PERNOITAR-02]; assesses=[] -->

**pernoitar** — "to spend the night, to stay overnight".

> Pernoitámos numa aldeia a meio do caminho.
>
> *We spent the night in a village halfway there.*

A little formal; people also say *passar a noite*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*pernoitar* comes from Latin *pernoctāre* 'to spend the night' (EtymDB), reshaped on *noite* 'night', from Latin *noctem*.
