## You'll want to know: jìnzhǐ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C115-CAREFUL115-03]; assesses=[] -->

*jìnzhǐ* — "to forbid; no (on signs)".

> *zhèr jìnzhǐ xīyān* — No smoking here.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*jìnzhǐ* is built from jìn and zhǐ, both inherited from Middle Chinese, *kimH* and *tsyiX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), and the dictionary glosses both 'to stop'.
