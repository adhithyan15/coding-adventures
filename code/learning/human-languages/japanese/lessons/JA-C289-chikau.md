## You'll want to know: ちかう
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C289-KEKKON289-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ちかう** — *chikau* — "to vow; to swear".

> **ふたりは じんじゃで ちかいました。** — *futari wa jinja de chikaimashita* — "The two made their vows at the shrine."

Polite **ちかいます**, *chikaimasu*; a godan verb. Past (polite): **ふたりが ちかいました。**, *futari ga chikaimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ちかう** (*chikau*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict).
