## You'll want to know: تخصص
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C390-KULLIYYA390-02]; assesses=[] -->

**تخصص** — *takhaṣṣuṣ* — "a specialization".

> تخصص أختي هو الكيمياء.
>
> *takhaṣṣuṣ ukhtī huwa al-kīmiyāʾ.*
>
> My sister's specialization is chemistry.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**تخصص** (*takhaṣṣuṣ*) is built on the native Arabic root *kh-ṣ-ṣ*, "to single out" (Lane). *Takhaṣṣuṣ* is the Form V verbal noun, singling oneself out for one thing: a specialization.
