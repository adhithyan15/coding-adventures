## You'll want to know: le crime
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C290-CRIME-01]; assesses=[] -->

**le crime** — "the crime; the murder".

> Ce crime a choqué toute la ville.
>
> *This crime shocked the whole town.*

In French, *un crime* is a serious offence, often a murder.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*crime* is inherited from Latin *crīmen* "a charge, a crime" (EtymDB).
