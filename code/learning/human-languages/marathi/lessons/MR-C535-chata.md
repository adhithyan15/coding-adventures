## You'll want to know: छटा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C535-CHITRA535-05]; assesses=[] -->

**छटा** — *chaṭā* — "a shade; a tint".

> आकाशात निळ्या रंगाच्या खूप छटा होत्या.
>
> *ākāśāt niḷyā raṅgācyā khūp chaṭā hotyā.*
>
> There were many shades of blue in the sky.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**छटा** (*chaṭā*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *chaṭā*, "a mass, a lustre" (Monier-Williams).
