## You'll want to know: मुसाफर
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C216-OUT216-04]; assesses=[] -->

**मुसाफर** — *musāphar* — "a traveller".

> मुसाफर दूर सूं आवै है।
>
> *musāphar dūr sū̃ āvai hai.*
>
> The traveller comes from far away.

A masculine or feminine noun, by the person meant.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**मुसाफर** (*musāphar*) is usually traced to Persian *musāfir*, "a traveller", which Persian took from Arabic; Marwadi says it with *ph* and ends it in *-ar*.
