## You'll want to know: xìnhào
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C198-PHONE198-05]; assesses=[] -->

*xìnhào* — "phone signal".

> *zhèr méiyǒu xìnhào* — There's no signal here.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*xìnhào* is built from *xìn* 'a sign, a message' and 号 'a call, a mark', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *sinH* and *hawH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription). The dictionary glosses the first 'true' and 'proof', and the second 'to call'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for reading notices and messages.
