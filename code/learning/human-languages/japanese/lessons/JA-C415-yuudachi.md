## You'll want to know: ゆうだち
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C415-YUUDACHI415-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ゆうだち** — *yūdachi* — "a sudden evening shower".

> **なつの ごごに ゆうだちが ふりました。** — *natsu no gogo ni yūdachi ga furimashita* — "A sudden shower fell on the summer afternoon."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ゆうだち** (*yūdachi*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, built from *yū*, "evening" and *dachi*, "rising" (from *tatsu*); its kanji were attached later as kun'yomi (JMdict).
