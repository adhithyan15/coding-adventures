## You'll want to know: recitātiō, recitātiōnis
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C549-POEM-04]; assesses=[] -->

**recitātiō, recitātiōnis** — "a public reading".

> Hodiē poēta recitātiōnem in ātriō facit.
>
> *Today the poet gives a reading in the hall.*

Feminine, third declension, from *recitō* — to read aloud. Authors read new work to invited listeners. English *recitation*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**recitātiō** is the action noun of *recitō* "to read aloud", built on *re-* and *citō* "to call" (Lewis & Short). English *recitation* comes from it.
