## You'll want to know: o repórter
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C279-IMPRENSA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**o repórter** — "the reporter".

> Um repórter falou com os moradores.
>
> *A reporter spoke to the residents.*

The same form for a woman: *a repórter*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*repórter* was borrowed from English *reporter* (EtymDB), a word that goes back to Latin *reportāre* "to carry back", from *portāre* "to carry".
