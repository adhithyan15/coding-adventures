## You'll want to know: la nevada
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C700-NIEVE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**la nevada** — "the snowfall".

> Después de la nevada no había autobuses.
>
> *After the snowfall there were no buses.*

*Una gran nevada* — heavy snow.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*nevada* is built on *nevar* "to snow", which is inherited from Vulgar Latin *nivāre*, from *nix, nivis* "snow".
