Punjabi, chapter 285: Back Home Again.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ਪਰਤਣਾ (paratṇā) — to return; to come back.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for last, then the Punjabi for to get to.

You'll want to know: ਪਰਤਣਾ.
ਪਰਤਣਾ — paratṇā — "to return; to come back".
ਅਸੀਂ ਐਤਵਾਰ ਨੂੰ ਸ਼ਹਿਰ ਪਰਤਾਂਗੇ। asīṁ aitvār nū̃ shahir partāṅge. We will return to the city on Sunday.
Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਘਰ ਪਰਤਿਆ, maiṁ ghar partiā.

The word, taken apart.
ਪਰਤਣਾ (paratṇā) is inherited from Sanskrit parivartatē, "turns round"; CDIAL lists Punjabi partaṇā under it only as "to change the side of anything by turning it"; the sense "to return" is the one it gives for Lahnda partaṇ (CDIAL 7872).

Lesson 3 of 5.

ਮੁੱਕਣਾ — mukkṇā — "to come to an end; to run out".
ਛੁੱਟੀ ਪਰਸੋਂ ਮੁੱਕੇਗੀ। chhuṭṭī parsõ mukkegī. The holiday will end the day after tomorrow.
Past (it …): ਉਹ ਸਫ਼ਰ ਮੁੱਕਿਆ, oh safar mukkiā.

ਮੁੱਕਣਾ (mukkṇā) is inherited from a reconstructed mukna, "loosed"; CDIAL lists Punjabi mukkṇā, "to come to an end, run short", under it (CDIAL 10157).
