## You'll want to know: भूमिका
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C537-NATYA537-02]; assesses=[] -->

**भूमिका** — *bhūmikā* — "a role; a part".

> नाटकात अरुणने राजाची भूमिका केली.
>
> *nāṭkāt aruṇne rājācī bhūmikā kelī.*
>
> Arun played the king in the play.

Feminine.

भूमिका करणे, to play a part.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**भूमिका** (*bhūmikā*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *bhūmikā*, "ground", and also "the part an actor takes" (Monier-Williams).
