Mandarin Chinese, chapter 50: Sleeping and Waking.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
shuìjiào — to sleep; to go to bed.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for afternoon, then the Mandarin for good evening.

You'll want to know: shuìjiào.
shuìjiào — "to sleep; to go to bed".
wǒ qù shuìjiào — I'm off to bed.
Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
shuìjiào is built from shuì 'to sleep' and jiào 'sleep', both inherited from Middle Chinese, dzyweH and kaewH (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription); the dictionary glosses the second with this very pair.

What you've built.
One more word for saying how you are.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: shuìjiào]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: shuìjiào, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: wǒ qù shuìjiào — I'm off to bed.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Mandarin for afternoon, then the Mandarin for good evening, then say shuìjiào again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does shuìjiào mean? ("To sleep; to go to bed".) Say it once more.
