## You'll want to know: le pressing
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C447-PRESSING-01]; assesses=[] -->

**le pressing** — "the dry cleaner’s".

> Il dépose sa veste au pressing le lundi.
>
> *He drops his jacket off at the dry cleaner's on Mondays.*

*Le nettoyage à sec* — dry cleaning.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*pressing* was borrowed from English *pressing* 'ironing', a sense English does not use for a shop. EtymDB does not list it.
