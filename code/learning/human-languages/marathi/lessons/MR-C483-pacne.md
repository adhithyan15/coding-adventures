## You'll want to know: पचणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C483-PACAN483-05]; assesses=[] -->

**पचणे** — *pacṇe* — "to be digested".

> रात्रीचं जड जेवण लवकर पचत नाही.
>
> *rātrīcaṁ jaḍ jevaṇ lavkar pacat nāhī.*
>
> A heavy dinner does not digest quickly.

Past (to him …): त्याला पचलं, *tyālā paclaṁ*.

The eater takes the dative: मला पचलं. पचवणे, to digest.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पचणे** (*pacṇe*) is a tadbhava, inherited from Sanskrit *pacyate*; CDIAL lists Marathi *pacṇẽ*, "to be digested" (CDIAL 7654).
