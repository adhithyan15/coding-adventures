## You'll want to know: der Feiertag
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C305-FEIER-03]; assesses=[] -->

**der Feiertag** — "the public holiday".

> Am Feiertag sind alle Geschäfte geschlossen.
>
> *On the public holiday all the shops are closed.*

Plural: *die Feiertage*. *Frohe Feiertage!* — happy holidays!

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Feiertag* joins *Feier*, from Latin *fēriae* "holidays" (EtymDB), and the inherited *Tag* "day".
