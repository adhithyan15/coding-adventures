# ಅಣೆಕಟ್ಟು (aṇekaṭṭu) — a dam

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[KA-LEX-C482-TORE482-05, KA-LEX-C483-JALASHAYA483-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Kannada for to trickle, then the Kannada for a reservoir.

## You'll want to know: ಅಣೆಕಟ್ಟು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C483-JALASHAYA483-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ಅಣೆಕಟ್ಟು** — *aṇekaṭṭu* — "a dam".

> ನದಿಗೆ ಅಡ್ಡವಾಗಿ ದೊಡ್ಡ ಅಣೆಕಟ್ಟು ಕಟ್ಟಿದ್ದಾರೆ.
>
> *nadige aḍḍavāgi doḍḍa aṇekaṭṭu kaṭṭiddāre.*
>
> A big dam has been built across the river.

ಅಣೆ, a dam, plus ಕಟ್ಟು, to build.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಅಣೆಕಟ್ಟು** (*aṇekaṭṭu*) is a native Kannada compound of *aṇe*, "a dam, a dike" (DEDR 122), and ಕಟ್ಟು, "to bind, to build" (DEDR 1147).
