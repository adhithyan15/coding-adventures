## You'll want to know: おんせん
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C398-ONSEN398-01]; assesses=[] -->

**おんせん** — *onsen* — "a hot spring".

> **ふゆは おんせんが いちばん いいです。** — *fuyu wa onsen ga ichiban ii desu* — "In winter a hot spring is the best thing."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**おんせん** (*onsen*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *on*, "warm" (Middle Chinese *'won*) and *sen*, "spring" (*dzjwen*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
