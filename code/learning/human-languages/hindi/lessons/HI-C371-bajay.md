# बजाय (bajāy) — instead of (के बजाय)

## You'll want to know: बजाय
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C371-HALANKI371-03]; assesses=[] -->

**बजाय** — *bajāy* — "instead of (के बजाय)".

> चाय के बजाय दूध लीजिए।
>
> *cāy ke bajāy dūdh lījie.*
>
> Have milk instead of tea.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**बजाय** (*bajāy*) is usually traced to Persian *bajāy*, "in place of".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
