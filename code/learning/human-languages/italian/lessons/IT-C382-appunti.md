## You'll want to know: gli appunti
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C382-RIPASSO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**gli appunti** — "the notes (taken in class)".

> Mi presti i tuoi appunti? Ieri ero malato.
>
> *Can you lend me your notes? I was ill yesterday.*

*Prendere appunti* — to take notes. The singular *appunto* also means a reproach; the adverb *appunto* means exactly.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*appunti* is built on *appuntare* "to jot down, to pin", from *a-* and *punto* "point", which is inherited from Latin *punctum* "a prick, a point" (Lewis & Short).
