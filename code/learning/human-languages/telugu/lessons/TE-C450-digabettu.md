# దిగబెట్టు (digabeṭṭu) — to drop someone off

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[TE-LEX-C450-PARKINGU450-03, TE-LEX-C450-PARKINGU450-04] -->

## You'll want to know: దిగబెట్టు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C450-PARKINGU450-05]; assesses=[] -->

**దిగబెట్టు** — *digabeṭṭu* — "to drop someone off".

> నా స్నేహితుడు నన్ను స్టేషన్ దగ్గర దిగబెట్టాడు.
>
> *nā snēhituḍu nannu sṭēṣan daggara digabeṭṭāḍu.*
>
> My friend dropped me off near the station.

Past (I …): దిగబెట్టాను, *digabeṭṭānu*.

దిగు, to get down, + పెట్టు, to put: to see someone safely to a place.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**దిగబెట్టు** (*digabeṭṭu*) is native Dravidian: దిగు, "to get down" (DEDR 502), and పెట్టు, "to put, to place" (DEDR 4390).
