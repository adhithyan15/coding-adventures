## You'll want to know: 重
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C134-THING134-01]; assesses=[] -->

**重** — *zhòng* — "heavy".

> **这个太重了** *zhège tài zhòng le* — This one is too heavy.

The new character, in pieces: 重 — a short fall and a long bar on top, and under them 里, its vertical running up through the bar. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**重** (*zhòng*) is inherited from Middle Chinese *drjowngX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'much', 'thick' and 'careful'. 'Heavy' is not among the glosses, so the step to it is not settled.
