## You'll want to know: иголка
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C501-SEWING-02]; assesses=[] -->

**иголка** (*igólka*) — "a needle".

> Я укололся иголкой.
>
> *Ya ukolólsya igólkoy.*
>
> I pricked myself with a needle.

Feminine. Everyday word beside the bookish *игла*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*иголка* is a diminutive of *игла* 'needle'; EtymDB lists *иголка* without a source, and *игла* is inherited from Proto-Slavic *jьgъla* (EtymDB).
