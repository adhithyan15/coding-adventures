## You'll want to know: dānxīn
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C125-FEEL125-02]; assesses=[] -->

*dānxīn* — "to worry".

> *nǐ bú yào dānxīn* — Don't you worry.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*dānxīn* is built from *dān* 'to carry on the shoulder' and *xīn* 'heart', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *tam* and *sim* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription). The pair is not given there; a load carried in the heart is the plain picture, but the step is not settled.
