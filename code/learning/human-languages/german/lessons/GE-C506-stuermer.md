## You'll want to know: der Stürmer
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C506-FUSSBALL-02]; assesses=[] -->

**der Stürmer** — "striker, forward".

> Der Stürmer schoss zwei Tore.
>
> *The striker scored two goals.*

Plural: *die Stürmer*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Stürmer* is built on *stürmen* "to storm", from *Sturm* "storm, attack", inherited from Old High German *sturm*, from Proto-Germanic *sturmaz* (EtymDB).
