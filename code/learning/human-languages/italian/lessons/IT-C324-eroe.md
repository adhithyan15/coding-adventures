## You'll want to know: l'eroe
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C324-MITO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**l'eroe** — "the hero".

> L'eroe della storia è un ragazzo povero.
>
> *The hero of the story is a poor boy.*

A woman is *l'eroina*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*eroe* is a learned borrowing from Latin *hērōs*, from Greek *hḗrōs* "a hero, a demigod" (Lewis & Short).
