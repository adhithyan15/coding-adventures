## You'll want to know: रोळो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C250-UCHHAV250-05]; assesses=[] -->

**रोळो** — *roḷo* — "an uproar; a din".

> गळी मांय घणो रोळो है।
>
> *gaḷī mā̃y ghaṇo roḷo hai.*
>
> There is a great din in the lane.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**रोळो** (*roḷo*) is inherited from Sanskrit *rava*, "a roar, a cry"; CDIAL lists Marwari *roḷo*, "tumult", under it (CDIAL 10641).
