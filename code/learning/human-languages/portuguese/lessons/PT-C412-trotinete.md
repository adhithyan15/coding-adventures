## You'll want to know: a trotinete
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C412-RODAS-02]; assesses=[] -->

**a trotinete** — "the scooter (kick or electric)".

> Muitos turistas andam de trotinete no centro.
>
> *Many tourists ride scooters in the centre.*

In Brazil: *o patinete*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*trotinete* was borrowed from French *trottinette*, built on *trottiner* 'to trot along in small steps' with the small-size ending *-ette* (EtymDB).
