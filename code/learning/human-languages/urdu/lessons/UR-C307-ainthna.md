## You'll want to know: اینٹھنا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C307-JHAGRA307-05]; assesses=[] -->

**اینٹھنا** — *ainṭhnā* — "to twist; to wring".

> اس نے چور کا ہاتھ اینٹھا۔
>
> *us ne chor kā hāth ainṭhā.*
>
> He twisted the thief's arm.

Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے رسی اینٹھی, *us ne rassī ainṭhī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**اینٹھنا** (*ainṭhnā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *aĩṭhnā*, "to twist", under Sanskrit *āvēṣṭatē*, "wraps round" (CDIAL 1448).
