## You'll want to know: el capitán
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C659-BARCA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**el capitán** — "the captain".

> El capitán habla con los pasajeros.
>
> *The captain is talking to the passengers.*

Of a ship, a plane or a team.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*capitán* comes from Late Latin *capitāneus* "chief" (EtymDB), built on *caput* "head".
