# অভিযোগ (ôbhijog) — a complaint; an accusation

## You'll want to know: অভিযোগ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C283-OPORADH283-02]; assesses=[] -->

**অভিযোগ** — *ôbhijog* — "a complaint; an accusation".

> দোকানদার থানায় গিয়ে অভিযোগ করল।
>
> *dokāndār thānāy giye ôbhijog kôrlô.*
>
> The shopkeeper went to the police station and made a complaint.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**অভিযোগ** (*ôbhijog*) is Sanskrit *abhiyoga*, 'an application, an earnest effort' (Monier-Williams); the sense 'a complaint, a charge' is the one Bengali uses. অভিযোগ করা, to complain.
