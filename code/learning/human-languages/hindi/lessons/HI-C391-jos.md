## You'll want to know: जोश
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C391-PRAYAS391-03]; assesses=[] -->

**जोश** — *joś* — "enthusiasm, fervour".

> अगर टीम में जोश होगा तो जीत पक्की है।
>
> *agar ṭīm meṁ joś hogā to jīt pakkī hai.*
>
> If the team is fired up, victory is certain.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**जोश** (*joś*) is usually traced to Persian *jōsh*, "boiling, ardour".
