Persian, chapter 318: Serious crime.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
جنایت (jenâyat) — a serious crime, a felony.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to disappear, then the Persian for to be found out.

You'll want to know: جنایت.
جنایت (jenâyat) — "a serious crime, a felony".
این جنایت همه شهر را شوکه کرد. In jenâyat hame-ye shahr râ shoke kard. This crime shocked the whole city.
For violent crimes. jenâyatkâr "a criminal".

The word, taken apart.
jenâyat is borrowed from Arabic jināya, "crime" (EtymDB).

Lesson 3 of 5.
قاتل (qâtel) — a murderer, a killer.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a serious crime, then the Persian for a murder.

You'll want to know: قاتل.
قاتل (qâtel) — "a murderer, a killer".
پلیس هنوز قاتل را پیدا نکرده است. Polis hanuz qâtel râ peydâ nakarde ast. The police have not found the murderer yet.
Same Arabic root as qatl: qâtel is "the one who kills". Plural qâtelân.

The word, taken apart.
qâtel is borrowed from Arabic qātil, "killer" (EtymDB), the active participle of the same root as qatl.

Lesson 4 of 5.
حمله کردن (hamle kardan) — to attack.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a murder, then the Persian for a murderer.

You'll want to know: حمله کردن.
حمله کردن (hamle kardan) — "to attack".
یک سگ بزرگ به پسر حمله کرد. Yek sag-e bozorg be pesar hamle kard. A big dog attacked the boy.
The one attacked takes be. Present hamle mikonad. Hamle-ye qalbi "a heart attack".

The word, taken apart.
hamle is borrowed from Arabic ḥamla (EtymDB lists the Arabic word), usually glossed "a charge, an attack". kardan "to do" is inherited Persian.

Lesson 5 of 5.
اسلحه (aslahe) — a weapon, a gun.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a murderer, then the Persian for to attack.

You'll want to know: اسلحه.
اسلحه (aslahe) — "a weapon, a gun".
مرد اسلحه نداشت. Mard aslahe nadâsht. The man did not have a weapon.
An Arabic plural in origin, used as a singular in Persian. Aslahe-ye sard is a knife or blade.

The word, taken apart.
aslahe is borrowed from Arabic asliḥa (EtymDB), the plural of silāḥ "weapon"; Persian treats it as a singular.
