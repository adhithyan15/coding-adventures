## You'll want to know: стрижка
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C449-STRIZHKA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**стрижка** (*strízhka*) — "a haircut".

> Тебе идёт новая стрижка.
>
> *Tebé idyót nóvaya strízhka.*
>
> The new haircut suits you.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*стрижка* is built on *стричь* 'to cut hair, to shear' with *-ка*. *Стричь* is inherited from Proto-Slavic *striťi* (EtymDB).
