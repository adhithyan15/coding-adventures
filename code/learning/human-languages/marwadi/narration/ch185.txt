Marwadi (Marwari), chapter 185: Spices.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
धणियो (dhaṇiyo) — coriander.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for copper, then the Marwadi for iron.

You'll want to know: धणियो.
धणियो — dhaṇiyo — "coriander".
सबजी मांय धणियो घालो। sabjī mā̃y dhaṇiyo ghālo. Put coriander in the vegetables.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
धणियो (dhaṇiyo) is inherited from Sanskrit dhānaka, "coriander"; CDIAL lists Hindi dhaniyā (CDIAL 6776). CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry.
