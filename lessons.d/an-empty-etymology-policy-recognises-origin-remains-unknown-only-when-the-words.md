# An empty-etymology policy recognises origin remains unknown only when the words are explicit and adjacent

The root-ledger guard classifies a word lesson's provenance block by explicit
phrases such as `origin remains unknown`. A sentence that separates those
words with the headword and other qualifiers can be truthful yet fail the
guard. When authoritative evidence cannot establish provenance, include a
plain adjacent sentence such as `Its origin remains unknown here`, then run
the focused root-ledger test before generating and pushing the chapter.
