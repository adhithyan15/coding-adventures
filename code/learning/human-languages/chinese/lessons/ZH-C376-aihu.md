## You'll want to know: àihù
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C376-PUBLIC376-03]; assesses=[] -->

*àihù* — "to cherish and protect; to treat with care".

> *wǒmen yīnggāi àihù gōngyuán lǐ de huācǎo* — We should look after the plants in the park.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*àihù* is built from *ài* 'to cherish' and *hù* 'to protect', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *'ojH* and *huH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to pity' and the second 'to save' and 'to help'.
