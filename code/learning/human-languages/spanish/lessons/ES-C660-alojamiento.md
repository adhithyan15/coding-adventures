## You'll want to know: el alojamiento
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C660-ALOJAMIENTO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**el alojamiento** — "the accommodation, the lodging".

> El precio incluye el alojamiento.
>
> *The price includes accommodation.*

*Alojamiento y desayuno* — bed and breakfast.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*alojamiento* is built on *alojar* "to lodge", which came from Occitan *alotjar* (EtymDB), built on a Germanic word for a shelter or hut.
