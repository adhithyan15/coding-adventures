## You'll want to know: অবকাশ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C544-SOMOY544-02]; assesses=[] -->

**অবকাশ** — *ôbôkāsh* — "a respite; a break".

> দুপুরে অবকাশ পেলে আমি একটু ঘুমাই।
>
> *Dupure ôbôkāsh pele āmi ekṭu ghumāi.*
>
> If I get a break at midday, I take a short nap.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**অবকাশ** (*ôbôkāsh*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *avakāśa*, "room, space", which Monier-Williams also gives for a stretch of time in between: a gap in the day.
