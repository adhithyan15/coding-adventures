## You'll want to know: каюта
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C424-PAROM-03]; assesses=[] -->

**каюта** (*kayúta*) — "a cabin (on a ship)".

> У нас была маленькая каюта без окна.
>
> *U nas bylá málenkaya kayúta bez okná.*
>
> We had a small cabin with no window.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*каюта* was borrowed from German *Kajüte*, which EtymDB traces to Middle Low German *kajüte*.
