## You'll want to know: اعترف
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C325-SHAHID325-04]; assesses=[] -->

**اعترف** — *iʿtarafa* — "to confess, to admit".

> اعترف اللص بالسرقة.
>
> *iʿtarafa al-liṣṣ bi-s-sariqa.*
>
> The thief confessed to the theft.

Present: يعترف, *yaʿtarif*.

It takes بـ: اعترف بالخطأ, he admitted the mistake.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**اعترف** (*iʿtarafa*) is built on the native Arabic root *ʿ-r-f* (Lane), "to know", the root of the taught *ʿarafa*. It is Form VIII, "to own that one knows": to admit.
