Lesson 1 of 5.
अठै (aṭhai) — here.

Warm-up.

You'll want to know: अठै.
अठै — aṭhai — "here".

The word, taken apart.
The origin of अठै (aṭhai) is not settled; CDIAL has no entry for it. A likely relative is Sanskrit atra, "here", under which CDIAL lists Gujarati atre (CDIAL 228), but that entry does not explain the ṭh of अठै (aṭhai).

Lesson 4 of 5.
गळी (gaḷī) — a lane.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for there, then the Marwadi for a road.

You'll want to know: गळी.
गळी — gaḷī — "a lane".

The word, taken apart.
गळी (gaḷī) is a tadbhava, a word inherited and reshaped by sound change. It comes from galī, "defile, lane", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants. CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry (CDIAL 4085); the match is through Hindi galī. Marwadi, like Gujarati, says the l curled back.
