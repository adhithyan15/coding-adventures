## You'll want to know: sānitās, sānitātis
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C523-ROBU-04]; assesses=[] -->

**sānitās, sānitātis** — "health, soundness".

> Sānitās maius bonum est quam pecūnia.
>
> *Health is a greater good than money.*

Feminine, third declension, from *sānus*. Of the mind, good sense. English *sanity*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**sānitās** is built on *sānus* "sound, healthy" with *-itās* (Lewis & Short; EtymDB). English *sanity* comes from it.
