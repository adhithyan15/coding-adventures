Russian, chapter 298: On the road.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
наезд (nayézd) — a hit (by a vehicle).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to be hurt, then the Russian for to wound.

You'll want to know: наезд.
наезд (nayézd) — "a hit (by a vehicle)".
Вчера на углу был наезд на мальчика. Vcherá na uglú byl nayézd na málchika. Yesterday a boy was hit by a car on the corner.
Наезд на + accusative.

The word, taken apart.
наезд (nayézd) is built on наехать "to run into", from на- and ехать "to go by vehicle", inherited from Proto-Slavic ěxati "to travel" (EtymDB).

пострадавший (postradávshiy) — "an injured person, a casualty".
Пострадавшего отвезли в больницу. Postradávshevo otvezlí v bolnítsu. The injured man was taken to hospital.
A participle that declines like an adjective; a woman is пострадавшая.

The word, taken apart.
пострадавший (postradávshiy) is the past participle of пострадать "to be hurt", from по- and страдать "to suffer"; EtymDB lists no parent.

Lesson 3 of 5.
разбиваться (razbivátsya) — to crash; to break, to smash.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a hit, then the Russian for an injured person.

You'll want to know: разбиваться.
разбиваться (razbivátsya) — "to crash; to break, to smash".
Чашка упала и разбилась. Cháshka upála i razbílas. The cup fell and broke.
Imperfective; perfective разбиться (razbítsya), разобьётся (razobyótsya). Of a car or a plane: to crash.

The word, taken apart.
разбиваться (razbivátsya) is built on раз- "apart" and бить "to beat", which is inherited from Proto-Slavic biti "to beat", from PIE bʰeyH- (EtymDB).

Lesson 4 of 5.
уцелеть (utselét) — to come out unhurt, to survive intact.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for an injured person, then the Russian for to crash.

You'll want to know: уцелеть.
уцелеть (utselét) — "to come out unhurt, to survive intact".
В аварии все уцелели. V avárii vse utseléli. Everyone came out of the accident unhurt.
Perfective. Дом уцелел — the house was left standing.

The word, taken apart.
уцелеть (utselét) is built on у- and целый "whole, unhurt" (EtymDB glosses целый "whole, healthy"): to stay whole.

Lesson 5 of 5.
перевернуться (perevernútsya) — to overturn, to roll over.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to crash, then the Russian for to come out unhurt.

You'll want to know: перевернуться.
перевернуться (perevernútsya) — "to overturn, to roll over".
Машина перевернулась на дороге. Mashína perevernúlas na doróge. The car overturned on the road.
Perfective; imperfective переворачиваться (perevoráchivatsya). Also of a person in bed: to turn over.

The word, taken apart.
перевернуться (perevernútsya) is built on пере- "over" and -вернуть, the one-off form of вертеть "to turn" (EtymDB), inherited from Proto-Slavic vĭrtěti, PIE wert- "to turn"; modern вернуть on its own means "to give back".
