## You'll want to know: il portico
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C445-PIAZZA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**il portico** — "the arcade, the portico".

> Quando piove camminiamo sotto i portici.
>
> *When it rains we walk under the arcades.*

Often plural: *i portici*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*portico* is a learned borrowing from Latin *porticus* 'colonnade, porch' (EtymDB), from *porta* 'gate'.
