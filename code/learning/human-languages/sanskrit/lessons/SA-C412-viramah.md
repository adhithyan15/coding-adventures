## You'll want to know: विरामः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C412-VIRAMA412-01]; assesses=[] -->

**विरामः** — *virāmaḥ* — "a halt; a stop".

> लोकयानस्य विरामः ग्रामे अस्ति।
>
> *lokayānasya virāmaḥ grāme asti.*
>
> The bus has a halt at the village.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**विरामः** (*virāmaḥ*) is built from *vi*, "away", and the root √ram, "to rest, to delight", which also gives *ramate*, "enjoys"; MW gives *virāma* as cessation, end, a stop or pause; a bus halt is that sense in modern use (MW).
