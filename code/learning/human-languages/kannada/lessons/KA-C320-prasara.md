## You'll want to know: ಪ್ರಸಾರ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C320-VARADI320-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ಪ್ರಸಾರ** — *prasāra* — "a broadcast; spreading".

> ಆ ಪಂದ್ಯದ ಪ್ರಸಾರ ಸಂಜೆ ಆರು ಗಂಟೆಗೆ ಶುರುವಾಯಿತು.
>
> *ā pandyada prasāra sañje āru gaṇṭege śuruvāyitu.*
>
> The broadcast of that match began at six in the evening.

Radio and television both have a ಪ್ರಸಾರ. ಪ್ರಸಾರವಾಗು means to be broadcast.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಪ್ರಸಾರ** (*prasāra*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *prasāra*, "spreading out, extension" (CDIAL 8835). A broadcast spreads a programme far and wide.
