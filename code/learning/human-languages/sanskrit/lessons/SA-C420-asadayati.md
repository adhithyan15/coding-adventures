## You'll want to know: आसादयति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C420-PRAPA420-04]; assesses=[] -->

**आसादयति** — *āsādayati* — "comes upon; reaches".

> सायं पथिकः प्रपाम् आसादयति।
>
> *sāyaṃ pathikaḥ prapām āsādayati.*
>
> In the evening the wayfarer comes upon a water shed.

Past (he …): सः प्रपाम् आसादितवान्, *saḥ prapām āsāditavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**आसादयति** (*āsādayati*) is built from *ā* and the causative of the root √sad, "to sit"; MW gives its participle *āsādita* as reached (MW).
