## You'll want to know: مسرحية
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C542-MASRAH542-01]; assesses=[] -->

**مسرحية** — *masraḥiyya* — "a stage play".

> شاهدنا مسرحية مضحكة في المسرح.
>
> *shāhadnā masraḥiyya muḍḥika fī al-masraḥ.*
>
> We watched a funny play at the theatre.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**مسرحية** (*masraḥiyya*) is built on the native Arabic root *s-r-ḥ*, "to send out to pasture" (Lane). Masraḥ, a theatre, which you know, first named a pasture: a wide open place.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
