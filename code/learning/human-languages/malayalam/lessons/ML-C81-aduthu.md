# അടുത്ത് (aṭuttŭ) — near

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ML-LEX-C41-DEIXIS-03, ML-LEX-C41-DEIXIS-04] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say *here*. Say *there*. Then think about what neither of them tells
you: **how far away.**

## You'll want to know: അടുത്ത്
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C81-DISTANCE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**അടുത്ത്** — *aṭuttŭ* — near, close by.

**ഇവിടെ** and **അവിടെ** *point*. They split the world in two — where I am, and
where I am not — and that is all they do. Something *aviṭe* might be across the
room or across an ocean, and the word is the same either way.

**അടുത്ത് measures.** It is the first word in this track that says **how much
space is involved** rather than which side of you it is on.

| | does |
|---|---|
| **ഇവിടെ / അവിടെ** | **point** — this side or that |
| **അടുത്ത്** | **measure** — not much space |

That is a real division, and it is the reason a learner can have owned *here*
and *there* for forty chapters and still be unable to say that the shop is
close.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ML-LEX-C81-DISTANCE-01] -->

The near end of a scale you could previously only point along.
