## You'll want to know: dado que
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C349-VISTOQUE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**dado que** — "given that".

> Dado que o comboio está atrasado, vamos chegar tarde.
>
> *Given that the train is late, we are going to arrive late.*

Takes the indicative; close to *visto que*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*dado que* is built from *dado* "given", the participle of *dar*, inherited from Latin *dare* "to give" (Lewis & Short), and *que*, from Latin *quid* (EtymDB).
