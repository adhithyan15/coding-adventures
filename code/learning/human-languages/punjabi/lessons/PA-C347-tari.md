## You'll want to know: ਤਾੜੀ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C347-DRUM347-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ਤਾੜੀ** — *tāṛī* — "a clap; clapping".

> ਗੀਤ ਤੋਂ ਬਾਅਦ ਸਭ ਲੋਕ ਤਾੜੀਆਂ ਵਜਾਉਂਦੇ ਹਨ।
>
> *gīt ton bāad sabh lok tāṛīāṁ vajāunde han.*
>
> After the song everyone claps.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਤਾੜੀ** (*tāṛī*) is inherited from Sanskrit *tāla*, "clap, rhythm"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *tālī*, "clapping", and Lahnda *tāṛī*, "clapping the hands", under it (CDIAL 5748).
