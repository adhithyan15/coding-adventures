## You'll want to know: die Witwe
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C304-TRAUER-04]; assesses=[] -->

**die Witwe** — "the widow".

> Die Witwe wohnte noch viele Jahre im Haus.
>
> *The widow lived in the house for many more years.*

Plural: *die Witwen*. A man is *der Witwer*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Witwe* is inherited from Old High German *wituwa*, from Proto-Germanic *widuwōn* "widow" (EtymDB), the same source as English *widow*.
