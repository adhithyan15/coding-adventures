## You'll want to know: दरड
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C293-BHUKAMP293-05]; assesses=[] -->

**दरड** — *daraḍ* — "a landslide".

> घाटात दरड कोसळली आणि वाहतूक थांबली.
>
> *ghāṭāt daraḍ kosaḷlī āṇi vāhtūk thāmblī.*
>
> A landslide came down in the pass and the traffic stopped.

Feminine.

दरड कोसळणे, a landslide comes down.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**दरड** (*daraḍ*) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi *daraḍ*, 'a steep bank', under Sanskrit *darad*, 'a bank, a precipice' (CDIAL 6191).
