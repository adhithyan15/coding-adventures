## You'll want to know: جھڑپ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C307-JHAGRA307-02]; assesses=[] -->

**جھڑپ** — *jhaṛap* — "a scuffle; a clash".

> بازار میں جھڑپ ہوئی۔
>
> *bāzār meṅ jhaṛap huī.*
>
> There was a scuffle in the market.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**جھڑپ** (*jhaṛap*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *jhaṛap*, "a fight", under *\*jhaṭ* (CDIAL 5327), the entry that also holds **جھٹکا**.
