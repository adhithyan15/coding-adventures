## You'll want to know: mèngjiàn
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C536-DREAM536-04]; assesses=[] -->

*mèngjiàn* — "to dream of; to see in a dream".

> *wǒ zuótiān wǎnshang mèngjiàn wǒ zhòngjiǎng le* — Last night I dreamt I had won a prize.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*mèngjiàn* is built from *mèng* 'a dream' and 见 'to see', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *mjuwngH* and *kenH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'to look'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what would happen.
