Russian, chapter 258: Writing --- My Own Line.
1 lesson. It needs your eyes or your hands, so save it for when you have stopped.


Lesson 1 of 1.
Your own line — the first writing with no model.

Before we start: this one needs your hands, so it is not a driving lesson. You will want a pen and something to write on. You can listen to all of it now and come back to the parts that need looking at once you have stopped.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Everything you have written so far had something to reach for — a letter to trace, a word to copy, a word you heard.
[pause 1 second]
This does not. You choose the words, from a supply that is closed: only what this book has taught you.

Writing — controlled composition.
[pause 2 seconds]
Write one line about yourself, by hand, on paper. Start with я, then a verb you own with its I-ending, then the rest. End on a dot.
[pause 2 seconds]
Copying hid one thing from you: the model always had the right shapes in it. Now your hand chooses them, and three are worth checking on purpose.
[pause 2 seconds]
л and м — л comes to a point, м has a dip.
[pause 2 seconds]
т — if your hand writes it like an m, give it the bar over the top. The bar is a habit, not a rule, but it keeps the shape apart from ш in a run of humps.
[pause 2 seconds]
я — it faces the other way from Latin R. A hand that knows English will try to turn it round.
[pause 3 seconds]
Write the line. Then read it back and check those three letters before you check anything else.

Wrap-up recall.
[pause 2 seconds]
What tells л from м by hand? (The top — a point or a dip.)
[pause 2 seconds]
And what did you choose here that the earlier lessons chose for you? (The words, and so the shapes.)
