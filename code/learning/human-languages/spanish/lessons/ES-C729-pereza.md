## You'll want to know: la pereza
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C729-PEREZA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**la pereza** — "laziness".

> Me da pereza salir con este frío.
>
> *I cannot be bothered to go out in this cold.*

*Me da pereza* + infinitive — I cannot be bothered to.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*pereza* is usually traced to Latin *pigritia* "laziness", built on *piger* "slow, lazy"; Latin *-itia* became Spanish *-eza*, as in *tristeza*.
