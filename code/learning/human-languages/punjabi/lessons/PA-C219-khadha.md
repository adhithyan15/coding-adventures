# ਖਾਧਾ, ਖਾਧੀ — ate

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-GRAMMAR-PAST-OBJECT-219, PA-LEX-KHANA, PA-LEX-ROTI] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say *I drank tea*. Then *to eat* and *roti*.

## Grammar Lens: a past of its own
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-GRAMMAR-PAST-KHADHA-219]; assesses=[PA-LEX-KHANA, PA-GRAMMAR-PAST-OBJECT-219, PA-LEX-ROTI, PA-LEX-C64-FOOD64-03] -->

**ਖਾਣਾ** has a past of its own, **ਖਾਧਾ**, and it agrees with the food in the same
way:

> **ਮੈਂ ਰੋਟੀ ਖਾਧੀ।** — *maiṁ roṭī khādhī.* — I ate a roti.

> **ਅਸੀਂ ਸੇਬ ਖਾਧਾ।** — *asīṁ seb khādhā.* — We ate an apple.

CDIAL lists Punjabi *khādhā*, "eaten" (CDIAL 3865).
