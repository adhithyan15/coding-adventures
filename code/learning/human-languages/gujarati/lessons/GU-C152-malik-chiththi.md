# (reading) — a note under the door

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-LEX-C152-READ152-03, GU-LEX-C152-READ152-02, GU-SKILL-C151-READ-NOTICE-01, GU-LEX-C103-CAN103-05] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say *how much is the rent?*, then *the repair will be done tomorrow*.
And the word for *owner*: **માલિક**.

[PAUSE 1s] The shop notice spoke to everyone. This note speaks to one person,
Mira, and asks her for something.

## Reading
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-SKILL-C152-READ-NOTE-01]; assesses=[GU-LEX-C152-READ152-01, GU-LEX-C152-READ152-02, GU-LEX-C152-READ152-03, GU-GRAMMAR-C148-YUN-01, GU-GRAMMAR-C148-ERG-PL-01, GU-GRAMMAR-C149-THASHE-01, GU-GRAMMAR-C149-SHE-01, GU-GRAMMAR-C149-JAISH-01, GU-GRAMMAR-C146-POLAR-SHUN-01, GU-GRAMMAR-C145-NA-01] -->

> **ચિઠ્ઠી**
>
> **નમસ્તે મીરા બહેન,**
> **હું અરુણ છું, માલિક.**
> **ગઈકાલે તમે ફોન કર્યો: તમારી બારી ખુલ્લી રહે છે.**
> **આવતીકાલે સવારે મરામત થશે.**
> **એક માણસ આવશે. તે બારી અને દરવાજો જોશે.**
> **માણસ બપોરે જશે.**
> **શું તમે ઘરે હશો? હું પણ આવીશ.**
> **ભાડું પાંચ હજાર રૂપિયા છે.**
> **સોમવારે હું ભાડું લઈશ.**
> **ચિંતા ન કરો.**
> **આભાર, અરુણ**

[PAUSE 3s]
[YOU READ: the note once, straight through, without translating]

[PAUSE 3s]
[YOU READ: it a second time, and find the one line that is a question]

## You'll want to know
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-LEX-C151-READ151-02, GU-CONCEPT-C05-RAHEVUN-01, GU-LEX-C86-JOURNEY-04, GU-LEX-BAARI-01, GU-LEX-C129-THINGS129-03, GU-LEX-MAANAS-01, GU-LEX-C83-NUM3-03, GU-LEX-C57-MARKET-04, GU-CONCEPT-C01-NAMASTE-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] **ખુલ્લી રહે છે** is *stays open*: the window will not shut, and
that is the reason for the note. It runs in time order: yesterday's call in the
past, **ફોન કર્યો**; tomorrow's repair in the future, **થશે**, **આવશે**,
**જશે**; and one question in the middle for Mira to answer.
