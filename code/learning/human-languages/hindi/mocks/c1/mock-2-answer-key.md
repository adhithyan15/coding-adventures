## Writing

The synthesis must accurately distinguish queue disparities and alternative-
evidence/appeal findings from the coalition's justice and scarcity concerns. It
should explain the shared case for help, reasons, auditing, and protected urgent
access while fairly presenting disagreement over points versus lottery and the
need for supply. The essay must develop the visibility paradox across at least
two fields, address sampling, classification, aggregation, authority, and
proportionate claims, and propose responsible evidence principles. Score source
fidelity, selection, integration, intellectual development, organisation,
register, Hindi control, and orthography; no fixed wording is required.

## Speaking

The presentation must distinguish evidence from recommendation, address urgent
safeguards, a 90-day audit, disparity-sensitive measures, consent and data
separation, and explicitly reject unsupported agency-wide conclusions.
Collaborative synthesis must compare the named options and reach explicit
decisions for current implementation, next-round access conditions, technical
help, and documented disagreement. Challenge and defence requires supported
positions, precise qualification or reformulation, and credible conditions for
revision. Score only the learner's own contribution; no fixed position is
required.
