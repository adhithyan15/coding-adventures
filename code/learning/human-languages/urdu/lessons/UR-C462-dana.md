## You'll want to know: دانا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C462-SENSE462-02]; assesses=[] -->

**دانا** — *dānā* — "wise".

> دادا بڑے دانا آدمی ہیں۔
>
> *dādā baṛe dānā ādmī haiṅ.*
>
> My grandfather is a very wise man.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**دانا** (*dānā*) is usually traced to Persian *dānā*, "knowing, wise".
