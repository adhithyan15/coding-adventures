## You'll want to know: दीपयति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C326-DIPAVALI326-02]; assesses=[] -->

**दीपयति** — *dīpayati* — "lights; kindles".

> बालिका गृहस्य द्वारे दीपं दीपयति।
>
> *bālikā gṛhasya dvāre dīpaṃ dīpayati.*
>
> The girl lights a lamp at the door of the house.

Past (she …): सा दीपं दीपितवती, *sā dīpaṃ dīpitavatī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**दीपयति** (*dīpayati*) comes from the root √dīp, "to blaze, shine", as in *dīpa*, "a lamp", in its causative form: to set alight, kindle (MW).
