Portuguese, chapter 360: Subir na empresa.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
a promoção — the promotion; the special offer.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for the staff member, then the Portuguese for to delegate.

You'll want to know: a promoção.
a promoção — "the promotion; the special offer".
Ela recebeu uma promoção depois de dois anos. She got a promotion after two years.
In shops, em promoção — on special offer.

The word, taken apart.
promoção is a learned borrowing from Latin prōmōtiō "advancement, promotion" (EtymDB, Lewis & Short), from prōmovēre "to move forward", on movēre "to move".

Lesson 3 of 5.
o cargo — the post, the position.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for the promotion, then the Portuguese for to promote.

You'll want to know: o cargo.
o cargo — "the post, the position".
Ele ocupa um cargo importante na câmara. He holds an important post at the council.
Ocupar um cargo — to hold a post. A cargo de — in charge of.

The word, taken apart.
cargo is built on carregar "to load", which is usually traced to Late Latin carricāre, on carrus "a wagon" (Lewis & Short): a post is a load you carry.

Lesson 5 of 5.
a qualificação — the qualification.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for the post, then the Portuguese for the rise.

You'll want to know: a qualificação.
a qualificação — "the qualification".
Para este trabalho, é preciso uma boa qualificação. For this job, a good qualification is needed.
Qualificações profissionais — professional qualifications.

The word, taken apart.
qualificação is usually traced to Medieval Latin quālificātiō, a learned word built on quālis "of what sort" and facere "to make" (Lewis & Short).
