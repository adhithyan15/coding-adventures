Gujarati, chapter 369: Fears.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ભય (bhay) — dread.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to bear fruit, then the Gujarati for to turn out.

You'll want to know: ભય.
ભય — bhay — "dread".
જો નદીમાં પૂર આવશે તો ગામને ભય છે. jo nadīmā̃ pūr āvśe to gāmne bhay che. If the river floods, the village is in danger.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ભય (bhay) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit bhaya, "fear, alarm, dread" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 5 of 5.
ડરાવવું (ḍarāvvũ) — to frighten.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for peace of mind, then the Gujarati for a misgiving.

You'll want to know: ડરાવવું.
ડરાવવું — ḍarāvvũ — "to frighten".
જો તમે બાળકને ડરાવશો તો તે રાત્રે નહીં સૂએ. jo tame bāḷakne ḍarāvśo to te rātre nahī̃ sūe. If you frighten the child, he will not sleep tonight.
Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે નાના ભાઈને ડરાવ્યો, teṇe nānā bhāīne ḍarāvyo.

The word, taken apart.
ડરાવવું (ḍarāvvũ) is the causative of ડરવું (ḍarvũ), "to be afraid", from Sanskrit darati, "is frightened" (CDIAL 6190).
