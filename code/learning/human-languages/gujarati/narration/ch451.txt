Gujarati, chapter 451: Holding Close.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
વળગવું (vaḷagvũ) — to cling to; to hug tight.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to captivate, then the Gujarati for pampering.

You'll want to know: વળગવું.
વળગવું — vaḷagvũ — "to cling to; to hug tight".
બાળક બીકથી માતાને વળગી પડ્યું. bāḷak bīkthī mātāne vaḷgī paḍyũ. The child clung to its mother in fear.
Past (he …): તે માતાને વળગી પડ્યો, te mātāne vaḷgī paḍyo.

The word, taken apart.
વળગવું (vaḷagvũ) goes back to a reconstructed vilagyati, "hangs on to"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb, "to adhere to, seize" (CDIAL 11881).
