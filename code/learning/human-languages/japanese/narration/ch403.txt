Japanese, chapter 403: Culture and Places to Visit.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ぶんか (bunka) — culture.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for a local speciality, then the Japanese for to tour.

You'll want to know: ぶんか.
ぶんか — bunka — "culture".
がいこくの ぶんかを まなびたいです。 — gaikoku no bunka o manabitai desu — "I want to learn about other countries' cultures."

The word, taken apart.
ぶんか (bunka) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are bun, "literature" (Middle Chinese mjun) and ka, "change" (xwaeH) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).

Lesson 2 of 5.
みどころ (midokoro) — a highlight; the part worth seeing.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for to tour, then the Japanese for culture.

You'll want to know: みどころ.
みどころ — midokoro — "a highlight; the part worth seeing".
この てらの みどころは にわです。 — kono tera no midokoro wa niwa desu — "The highlight of this temple is its garden."

The word, taken apart.
みどころ (midokoro) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, built from mi, "seeing" (from miru) and dokoro, "place"; its kanji were attached later as kun'yomi (JMdict).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for a study visit, then the Japanese for a place visited on a trip.

You'll want to know: おとずれる.
おとずれる — otozureru — "to visit a place; to call on".
なつに ふるい てらを おとずれました。 — natsu ni furui tera o otozuremashita — "In summer I visited an old temple."
Polite おとずれます, otozuremasu; an ichidan verb. Past (polite): かんこうきゃくが しまを おとずれました。, kankōkyaku ga shima o otozuremashita.

The word, taken apart.
おとずれる (otozureru) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict).
