Marwadi (Marwari), chapter 217: Over the River.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
लांघणो (lā̃ghṇo) — to cross over; to leap over.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for a traveller, then the Marwadi for a camp.

You'll want to know: लांघणो.
लांघणो — lā̃ghṇo — "to cross over; to leap over".
हूं काल नदी लांघसूं। hū̃ kāl nadī lā̃ghsū̃. Tomorrow I'll cross the river.
Past (I …): म्हैं नदी लांघी, mhaĩ nadī lā̃ghī.

The word, taken apart.
लांघणो (lā̃ghṇo) is inherited from Sanskrit laṅghayati, "leaps over"; CDIAL lists Hindi lā̃ghnā, "to jump over", under it. CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry (CDIAL 10905).

Lesson 3 of 5.
बोझ (bojh) — a burden; a heavy load.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for to cross over, then the Marwadi for to climb.

You'll want to know: बोझ.
बोझ — bojh — "a burden; a heavy load".
ऊंट माथै घणो बोझ है। ū̃ṭ māthai ghaṇo bojh hai. The camel is carrying a heavy load.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
बोझ (bojh) is inherited from Sanskrit vahya, "to be carried"; CDIAL lists Hindi bojh, "a load", under it. CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry (CDIAL 11465).

Lesson 5 of 5.
ठैरणो (ṭhairṇo) — to halt; to stay for a while.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for a burden, then the Marwadi for to load.

You'll want to know: ठैरणो.
ठैरणो — ṭhairṇo — "to halt; to stay for a while".
हूं परसूं तांई गांव मांय ठैरसूं। hū̃ parsū̃ tā̃ī gā̃v mā̃y ṭhairsū̃. I'll stop in the village until the day after tomorrow.
Past (he …): वो गांव मांय ठैर्यो, vo gā̃v mā̃y ṭhairyo.

The word, taken apart.
ठैरणो (ṭhairṇo) goes back to a reconstructed stabhira, "fixed, firm"; CDIAL lists Hindi ṭhaharnā, "to stop", and a Gujarati verb "to stay" under it. CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry (CDIAL 13680).
