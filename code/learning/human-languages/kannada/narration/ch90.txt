Kannada, chapter 90: More of the Family.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಮೊಮ್ಮಗ (mommaga) — grandson.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for let's go, then the Kannada for a holiday.

You'll want to know: ಮೊಮ್ಮಗ.
ಮೊಮ್ಮಗ (mommaga) — "grandson". It contains ಮಗ (maga), "son", which you already know: a grandson is a son's or daughter's son.

What you've built.
A known word inside a new one.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: mommaga]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: mommaga, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say mommaga]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Kannada for let's go, then the Kannada for a holiday, then say mommaga again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does ಮೊಮ್ಮಗ (mommaga) mean? ("Grandson".) Say it once more.

Lesson 3 of 5.
ಸಂಬಂಧಿಕ (sambandhika) — a relative.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for grandson, then the Kannada for granddaughter.

You'll want to know: ಸಂಬಂಧಿಕ.
ಸಂಬಂಧಿಕ (sambandhika) — "a relative", from ಸಂಬಂಧ (sambandha), "connection, relationship".
ಅವರು ನಮ್ಮ ಸಂಬಂಧಿಕರು (avaru namma sambandhikaru) — "they are our relatives".

What you've built.
The word for everyone the family tree connects.

What you've built.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ಮಾವ (māva) — uncle (mother's brother); father-in-law.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a relative, then the Kannada for the neighbours.

You'll want to know: ಮಾವ.
ಮಾವ (māva) — "uncle", in particular the mother's brother. The same word also means "father-in-law": in the south the two roles were often the same person.

What you've built.
One word, two relations.
