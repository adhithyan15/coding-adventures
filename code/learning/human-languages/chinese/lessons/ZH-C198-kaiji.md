## You'll want to know: 开机
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C198-PHONE198-02]; assesses=[] -->

**开机** — *kāijī* — "to turn a phone on; to switch on a phone or a computer".

> **你开机了吗** *nǐ kāijī le ma* — Have you switched your phone on?

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**开机** (*kāijī*) is built from 开 'to open' and 机 'machine', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *khoj* and *kj+j* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for reading notices and messages.
