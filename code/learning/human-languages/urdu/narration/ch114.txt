Urdu, chapter 114: Exam, Language, Vegetable, Onion, Garlic.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
امتحان (imtihān) — an exam.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a pencil, then the Urdu for an exercise book.
[pause 3 seconds]
Read the two lines aloud, one after the other: یہ میز ہے۔ وہ دروازہ ہے۔.

You'll want to know: امتحان.
