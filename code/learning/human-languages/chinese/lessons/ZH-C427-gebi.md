## You'll want to know: gébì
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C427-COMEGO427-01]; assesses=[] -->

*gébì* — "next door".

> *wǒ zhù zài gébì* — I'm staying next door.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*gébì* is built from *gé* 'to separate' and *bì* 'a wall', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *keak* and *pek* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to block' and the second 'a wall'.
