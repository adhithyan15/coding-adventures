## You'll want to know: démissionner
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C355-DEMISSION-04]; assesses=[] -->

**démissionner** — "to resign, to quit (a job)".

> Elle a démissionné parce qu'elle n'était pas heureuse.
>
> *She resigned because she was not happy.*

*Démissionner de son poste*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*démissionner* is built on *démission*, from Latin *dēmissiō* "a letting down", from *dēmittere* "to send down", built on *mittere* "to send" (Lewis & Short).
