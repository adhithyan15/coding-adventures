## You'll want to know: o protagonista
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C325-HEROI-02]; assesses=[] -->

**o protagonista** — "the protagonist, the main character".

> O protagonista perde tudo no início da história.
>
> *The protagonist loses everything at the start of the story.*

The same form for a woman: *a protagonista*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*protagonista* goes back to Greek *prōtagōnistḗs* "the first actor", from *prôtos* "first" and *agōnistḗs* "a contestant" (EtymDB has no entry).
