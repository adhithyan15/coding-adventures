## You'll want to know: la integración
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C610-CONVIVIR-03]; assesses=[] -->

**la integración** — "the integration".

> La integración de los inmigrantes lleva tiempo.
>
> *The integration of immigrants takes time.*

*Integrarse* — to fit in.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*integración* is built on *integrar*, a learned borrowing from Latin *integrare* 'to make whole', from *integer* 'whole, untouched' (EtymDB).
