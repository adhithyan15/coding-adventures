# തർക്കം (tarkkaṁ) — a dispute; a quarrel

## You'll want to know: തർക്കം
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C406-VADAM406-02]; assesses=[] -->

**തർക്കം** — *tarkkaṁ* — "a dispute; a quarrel".

> വീടിന്റെ വിലയെക്കുറിച്ച് അവർ തമ്മിൽ തർക്കം ഉണ്ടായി.
>
> *vīṭinṟe vilayekkuṟiccŭ avar tammil tarkkaṁ uṇṭāyi.*
>
> There was a dispute between them about the price of the house.

The verb തർക്കിക്കുക, to argue, is the same word.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**തർക്കം** (*tarkkaṁ*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *tarka*, "inquiry, conjecture, reasoning" (CDIAL 5714). In Malayalam the reasoning became the quarrel it can lead to.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
