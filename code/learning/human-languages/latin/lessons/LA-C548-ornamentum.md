## You'll want to know: ōrnāmentum, ōrnāmentī
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C548-VENU-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ōrnāmentum, ōrnāmentī** — "an ornament, decoration".

> Domina ōrnāmenta aurea gerit.
>
> *The lady wears gold ornaments.*

Neuter, second declension, from *ōrnō* — to equip, adorn. English *ornament*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ōrnāmentum** is built on *ōrnō* "to equip, adorn" with *-mentum* (EtymDB; Lewis & Short). English *ornament* comes from it.
