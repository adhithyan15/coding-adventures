## You'll want to know: il sintomo
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C489-GUARIRE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**il sintomo** — "symptom".

> Quali sono i sintomi dell'influenza?
>
> *What are the symptoms of flu?*

The plural is *i sintomi*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*sintomo* is a learned borrowing, through Late Latin *symptōma*, from Greek *sýmptōma* "a happening, a mishap" (EtymDB, under English *symptom*), from *sympíptein* "to befall".
