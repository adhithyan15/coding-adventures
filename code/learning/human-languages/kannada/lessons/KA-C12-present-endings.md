# ಮಾಡುತ್ತಾನೆ, ಮಾಡುತ್ತಾಳೆ, ಮಾಡುತ್ತಾರೆ — let the ending name the person

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[KA-GRAMMAR-C12-THIRD-PERSON-03, KA-GRAMMAR-C12-HUMAN-NONHUMAN-04, KA-LEX-C05-MAADU-01, KA-GRAMMAR-C05-MATANADU-02] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say “I work”: *nānu kelasa māḍuttēne*. Then say he, she and
they/respectful he or she. Keep *māḍutt-* still and change only the ending.

## Grammar Lens: the ending follows the human pronoun
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-GRAMMAR-C12-PRESENT-THIRD-05]; assesses=[KA-GRAMMAR-C12-THIRD-PERSON-03, KA-GRAMMAR-C12-HUMAN-NONHUMAN-04, KA-LEX-C05-MAADU-01] -->

> *avanu kelasa māḍuttāne.* — He works.

> *avaḷu kelasa māḍuttāḷe.* — She works.

> *avaru kelasa māḍuttāre.* — They work; or one respected person works.

The stem and *-utt-* stay still. The endings *-tāne, -tāḷe, -tāre* tell you
which human pronoun fits. Keep the non-human present ending for a later lesson;
for now, recognise *adu* as the non-human member of the pronoun set.
