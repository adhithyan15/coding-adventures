Russian, chapter 451: The embassy and the post.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
посольство (posólstvo) — an embassy.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to get, then the Russian for to certify.

You'll want to know: посольство.
посольство (posólstvo) — "an embassy".
Мы пошли в посольство за визой. My poshlí v posólstvo za vízoy. We went to the embassy for a visa.
Neuter. Built on посол — an ambassador.

The word, taken apart.
посольство (posólstvo) is built on посол 'an envoy, an ambassador' with -ство. Посол goes with посылать 'to send': one who is sent. EtymDB lists посольство (posólstvo) without a deeper source.

Lesson 2 of 5.
загранпаспорт (zagranpásport) — a passport for travel abroad.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to certify, then the Russian for an embassy.

You'll want to know: загранпаспорт.
загранпаспорт (zagranpásport) — "a passport for travel abroad".
Для поездки за границу нужен загранпаспорт. Dlya poyézdki za granítsu núzhen zagranpásport. You need a passport to travel abroad.
Short for заграничный паспорт; Russians also have an internal passport.

The word, taken apart.
загранпаспорт (zagranpásport) is a short form of заграничный паспорт, from за границей 'abroad', on граница 'a border', and паспорт 'a passport'.

Lesson 3 of 5.
бандероль (banderól) — a small parcel; printed matter (by post).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for an embassy, then the Russian for a passport for travel abroad.

You'll want to know: бандероль.
бандероль (banderól) — "a small parcel; printed matter (by post)".
Я отправил книги бандеролью. Ya otprávil knígi banderólyu. I sent the books as a small parcel.
Feminine. Smaller and cheaper than a посылка.

The word, taken apart.
бандероль (banderól) was borrowed from French banderole, from Italian banderuola (EtymDB), a little banner; the word probably first named the paper band round a packet.

Lesson 4 of 5.

Warm-up.

You'll want to know: получатель.
получатель (poluchátel) — "an addressee, a recipient".
Посылка не дошла до получателя. Posýlka ne doshlá do poluchátelya. The parcel did not reach the addressee.
Masculine. The sender is the отправитель.

The word, taken apart.
получатель (poluchátel) is built on получать 'to receive' with -тель 'one who'. EtymDB lists получатель (poluchátel) without a deeper source.

Lesson 5 of 5.
выдавать (vydavát) — to issue, to hand out.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a small parcel, then the Russian for an addressee.

You'll want to know: выдавать.
выдавать (vydavát) — "to issue, to hand out".
Посольство выдаёт визы за неделю. Posólstvo vydayót vízy za nedélyu. The embassy issues visas within a week.
Imperfective; perfective выдать. Present выдаю, выдаёшь.

The word, taken apart.
выдавать (vydavát) is built from вы- 'out' and давать 'to give', which is inherited from Proto-Slavic dāvati (EtymDB).
