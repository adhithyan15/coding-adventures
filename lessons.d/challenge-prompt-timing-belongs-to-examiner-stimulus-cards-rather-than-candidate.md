# Challenge prompt timing belongs to examiner stimulus cards rather than candidate answers

The task shape assigns 20–75 seconds to each challenge stimulus, but one draft
applied that band to each candidate answer and the other claimed that very
short questions occupied the band. A substring assertion accepted both. Put
natural, expanded stimulus cards in the confidential examiner master, time and
count those prompts there, and reserve the 330–420-second total for the six
candidate responses.
