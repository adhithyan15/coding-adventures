Russian, chapter 81: The Letter Yo.
3 lessons. The first lesson already needs your eyes or your hands, so save this one for when you have stopped.


Lesson 1 of 3.
ё (yo) — the yo inside ребёнок.

Before we start: this one needs your hands, so it is not a driving lesson. You will want a pen and something to write on and your eyes, for letter shapes on the page. You can listen to everything else now — leave the sections called Script you'll notice: ё and Guided Practice until you have stopped, and I will say so again when we reach it.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: write я and ю once each, and name them.
[pause 2 seconds]
Now say ребёнок (rebyónok), "a child". You have read it for a long time. This lesson takes one letter out of it and puts it in your hand.

Script you'll notice: ё.
ё — yo.
It is inside ребёнок (rebyónok), "a child", and in мёд, чёрный and тёплый. It is е with two dots above it, and the syllable that carries it takes the stress. Many printed texts leave the dots off; this book keeps them, so you can see how the word is said.

Writing: ё.
Put your pen on ё and follow its line. Copy the shape you can see — slowly, and larger than it is printed.
This book does not yet tell you where to start this letter or which way to travel. It is not written down here until it can be written down with a source. Copying what is in front of you needs no such source, and it is how the shape gets into your hand in the meantime.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — look: at ребёнок and point at ё (yo) inside it]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[once you have stopped driving — trace: ё three times, saying yo as you finish each one]
[once you have stopped driving — write: ю, then ё (yo), from memory]
[your turn — read: ребёнок aloud once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
Which letter is this — ё? (yo, yo.) Which word did it come from? (ребёнок, rebyónok, "a child".)
