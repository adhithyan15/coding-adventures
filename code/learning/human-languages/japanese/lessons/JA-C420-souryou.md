## You'll want to know: そうりょう
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C420-RYOUSHUUSHO420-03]; assesses=[] -->

**そうりょう** — *sōryō* — "postage; a delivery charge".

> **おみやげの そうりょうは いくらですか。** — *omiyage no sōryō wa ikura desu ka* — "How much is the postage for the souvenirs?"

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**そうりょう** (*sōryō*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *sō*, "send" (Middle Chinese *suwngH*) and *ryō*, "fee" (*lew*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
