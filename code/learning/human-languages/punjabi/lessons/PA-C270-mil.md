## You'll want to know: ਮੀਲ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C270-SEARCH270-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ਮੀਲ** — *mīl* — "a mile".

> ਸਕੂਲ ਘਰ ਤੋਂ ਇੱਕ ਮੀਲ ਦੂਰ ਹੈ।
>
> *sakūl ghar ton ikk mīl dūr hai.*
>
> The school is a mile from home.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਮੀਲ** (*mīl*) is borrowed from English *mile*.
