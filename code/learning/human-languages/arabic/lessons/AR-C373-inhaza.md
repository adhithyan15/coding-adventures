# انحاز (inḥāza) — to take sides

## You'll want to know: انحاز
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C373-MAWQIF373-01]; assesses=[] -->

**انحاز** — *inḥāza* — "to take sides".

> انحاز الحكم إلى الفريق الآخر.
>
> *inḥāza al-ḥakam ilā al-farīq al-ākhar.*
>
> The referee took the side of the other team.

Present: ينحاز, *yanḥāz*.

It takes إلى: انحاز إلى ….

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**انحاز** (*inḥāza*) is built on the native Arabic root *ḥ-w-z*, "to draw together, to gather" (Lane). It is Form VII, to draw oneself aside to one party: to take sides.
