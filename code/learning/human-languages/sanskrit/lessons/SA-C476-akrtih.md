## You'll want to know: आकृतिः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C476-SAKTI476-04]; assesses=[] -->

**आकृतिः** — *ākṛtiḥ* — "a figure; build".

> तस्य आकृतिः पितुः इव अस्ति।
>
> *tasya ākṛtiḥ pituḥ iva asti.*
>
> He is built like his father.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**आकृतिः** (*ākṛtiḥ*) is built from *ā* and the root √kṛ, "to make": how a thing is made; MW gives "form, figure, shape, appearance" (MW).
