## You'll want to know: चूमना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C459-SNEH459-04]; assesses=[] -->

**चूमना** — *cūmnā* — "to kiss".

> माँ ने बच्चे का माथा चूमा।
>
> *māṁ ne bacce kā māthā cūmā.*
>
> The mother kissed the child's forehead.

Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने माथा चूमा, *usne māthā cūmā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**चूमना** (*cūmnā*) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Hindi *cūmnā*, "to kiss", under Sanskrit *cúmbati*, "kisses" (CDIAL 4870).
