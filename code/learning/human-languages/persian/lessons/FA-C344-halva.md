## You'll want to know: حلوا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C344-NAZR-03]; assesses=[] -->

**حلوا** (*halvâ*) — "halva".

> در مراسم ختم حلوا پخش کردند.
>
> *Dar marâsem-e khatm halvâ pakhsh kardand.*
>
> At the memorial service they handed out halva.

Persian halva is flour, butter, sugar and saffron, served at mourning ceremonies.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*halvâ* is borrowed from Arabic *ḥalwā* (EtymDB), which EtymDB derives from *ḥulw* "sweet".
