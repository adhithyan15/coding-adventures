## You'll want to know: पोसना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C503-PIDHI503-05]; assesses=[] -->

**पोसना** — *posnā* — "to nurture; to rear".

> दादी ने हमारे परिवार की तीन पीढ़ियों को पोसा।
>
> *dādī ne hamāre parivār kī tīn pīṛhiyoṁ ko posā.*
>
> Grandmother has nurtured three generations of our family.

Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने बच्चों को पोसा, *usne baccoṁ ko posā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पोसना** (*posnā*) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Hindi *posnā*, "to nourish, rear", under Sanskrit *pōṣáyati*, "nourishes" (CDIAL 8410).
