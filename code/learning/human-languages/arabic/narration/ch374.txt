Arabic, chapter 374: Linking reasons.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
بالتالي (bi-t-tālī) — consequently.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to back down, then the Arabic for to resolve.

You'll want to know: بالتالي.
بالتالي — bi-t-tālī — "consequently".
كان الطريق مغلقا، بالتالي تأخر الجميع. kāna aṭ-ṭarīq mughlaqan, bi-t-tālī taʾakhkhara al-jamīʿ. The road was closed; consequently everyone was late.
Usually with و: وبالتالي, and as a result.

The word, taken apart.
بالتالي (bi-t-tālī) joins bi-, "by", with at-tālī, "the one that follows", built on the native Arabic root t-l-w, "to follow" (Lane). Literally, by what follows.

Lesson 2 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: لهذا السبب.
لهذا السبب — li-hādhā as-sabab — "for this reason".
كان الجو ممطرا، لهذا السبب بقينا في المنزل. kāna al-jaww mumṭiran, li-hādhā as-sabab baqīnā fī al-manzil. The weather was rainy; for this reason we stayed at home.

The word, taken apart.
لهذا السبب (li-hādhā as-sabab) is a phrase: li-, "for", hādhā, "this", and as-sabab, "the reason", built on the native Arabic root s-b-b, "a rope; a means to an end" (Lane).

Lesson 3 of 5.
بما أن (bi-mā anna) — seeing that; since.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for consequently, then the Arabic for for this reason.

You'll want to know: بما أن.
بما أن — bi-mā anna — "seeing that; since".
بما أن اليوم عطلة، سننام متأخرين. bi-mā anna al-yawm ʿuṭla, sa-nanāmu mutaʾakhkhirīn. Seeing that today is a holiday, we will sleep late.
It gives a reason both speakers already accept.

The word, taken apart.
بما أن (bi-mā anna) is built from three native Arabic function words: bi-, "by", mā, "what", and anna, "that". The deeper origin of such particles is not settled.

Lesson 4 of 5.
حيث إن (ḥaythu inna) — given that.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for for this reason, then the Arabic for seeing that.

You'll want to know: حيث إن.
حيث إن — ḥaythu inna — "given that".
حيث إن الطريق طويل، سنخرج مبكرا. ḥaythu inna aṭ-ṭarīq ṭawīl, sa-nakhruju mubakkiran. Given that the road is long, we will leave early.
Formal; in speech بما أن (bi-mā anna) or لأن.

The word, taken apart.
حيث إن (ḥaythu inna) joins two native Arabic particles: ḥaythu, "where", and inna, "indeed, truly". The deeper origin of such particles is not settled.

Lesson 5 of 5.
لكي (li-kay) — in order to.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for seeing that, then the Arabic for given that.

You'll want to know: لكي.
لكي — li-kay — "in order to".
يدرس أخي كل ليلة لكي ينجح. yadrusu akhī kulla layla li-kay yanjaḥa. My brother studies every night in order to pass.
The verb after it takes the subjunctive: لكي ينجح, li-kay yanjaḥa.

The word, taken apart.
لكي (li-kay) joins the native Arabic particles li-, "for", and kay, "so that", which learners met on its own earlier. The deeper origin of such particles is not settled.
