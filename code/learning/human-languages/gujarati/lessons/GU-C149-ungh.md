# ઊંઘ (ūṅgh) — sleepiness, sleep

## You'll want to know: ઊંઘ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C149-FUT149-01]; assesses=[GU-GRAMMAR-C146-DATIVE-SG-01, GU-LEX-C54-MOVE-04] -->

**ઊંઘ** (*ūṅgh*) — "sleepiness, sleep".

Gujarati does not say *I am sleepy*. Sleep comes *to* you, the way a fever does:

> **મને ઊંઘ આવે છે.** — *mane ūṅgh āve che.* — I'm sleepy. Word for word: *to me, sleep comes*.
> **આજે રાત્રે સારી ઊંઘ આવશે.** — *āje rātre sārī ūṅgh āvśe.* — Tonight there will be good sleep.

**ઊંઘ** is feminine, so *good* bends to **સારી**. **સૂવું**, *to sleep*, is lying
down to sleep; **ઊંઘ** is the sleep itself.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ઊંઘ** (*ūṅgh*) is inherited. CDIAL reconstructs an Indo-Aryan *uṅghā*,
"sleep", and lists the Gujarati word under it, beside Hindi *ūṅgh* (CDIAL 1633).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word, and sleep joins likes and fevers among the things that happen *to*
someone.
