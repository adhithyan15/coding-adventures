# 〜に よります (ni yorimasu) — it depends on

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[JA-GRAMMAR-C260-IF-01, JA-LEX-C207-WEATHER207-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "if it is fine tomorrow, I will go to the mountains". (*moshi ashita haretara, yama ni ikimasu*.) Then "the weather". (*tenki*.)

## You'll want to know: 〜に よります
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-GRAMMAR-C261-DEPENDS-01]; assesses=[JA-LEX-C207-WEATHER207-01, JA-LEX-C41-SCHOOL41-02] -->

> **てんきに よります。**
>
> *tenki ni yorimasu.* — "It depends on the weather."

What it depends on takes **に**, and **よります** (*yorimasu*) follows. This **よる** is "to depend on", a different verb from the **よる** of **よります** "to drop in", though the two sound the same; both are godan verbs.

> **それは じかんに よります。**
>
> *sore wa jikan ni yorimasu.* — "That depends on the time."

## You'll want to know: なにに よりますか
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-GRAMMAR-C261-DEPENDS-02]; assesses=[JA-LEX-C207-WEATHER207-04, JA-GRAMMAR-C260-TARA-01] -->

To ask, put **なに** (*nani*), "what", before **に**:

> **なにに よりますか。**
>
> *nani ni yorimasu ka.* — "What does it depend on?"

The answer often goes on with **-たら**:

> **てんきに よります。**
>
> *tenki ni yorimasu.* — "It depends on the weather."

> **はれたら、いきます。**
>
> *haretara, ikimasu.* — "If it is fine, I will go."
