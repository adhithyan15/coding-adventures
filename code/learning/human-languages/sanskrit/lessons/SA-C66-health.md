## You'll want to know: आरोग्यम्
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C66-UNWELL-05]; assesses=[] -->

**आरोग्यम्** (*ārogyam*) — "health": **आ-**, then **रोग**, "illness", with **-य** after
it — the state of the body once the illness is gone.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

What you wish someone who is ill.
