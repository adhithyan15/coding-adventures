## You'll want to know: crēditor, crēditōris
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C418-AESALIENUM-03]; assesses=[] -->

**crēditor, crēditōris** — "a creditor, a lender".

> Crēditor pecūniam suam exspectat.
>
> *The creditor is waiting for his money.*

*crēditor, crēditōris* m., built on *crēdō*, to trust, to lend. English *creditor*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*crēditor* is built on *crēdō* "to trust, to lend" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short), from its participle *crēditus*. English *creditor* comes from it.
