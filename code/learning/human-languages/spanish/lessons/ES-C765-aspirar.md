## You'll want to know: aspirar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C765-ILUSION-05]; assesses=[] -->

**aspirar** — "to aspire; to breathe in; to vacuum".

> Aspira a ser médica.
>
> *She aspires to be a doctor.*

*Aspirar a* — to aspire to. Also to vacuum: *aspirar la alfombra*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*aspirar* is a learned borrowing from Latin *aspīrāre* "to breathe upon" (EtymDB), built on *spīrāre* "to breathe".
