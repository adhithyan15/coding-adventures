**அறிவியல்** — *aṟiviyal* — "science".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**அறிவியல்** (*aṟiviyal*) is native: *aṟivu*, "knowledge" (DEDR 314), and *iyal*, "nature, a field of study" (DEDR 471): "the study of knowledge".
