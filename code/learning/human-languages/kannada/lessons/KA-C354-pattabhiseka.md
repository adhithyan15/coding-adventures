# ಪಟ್ಟಾಭಿಷೇಕ (paṭṭābhiṣēka) — a coronation

## You'll want to know: ಪಟ್ಟಾಭಿಷೇಕ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C354-SAMRAJYA354-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ಪಟ್ಟಾಭಿಷೇಕ** — *paṭṭābhiṣēka* — "a coronation".

> ಹೊಸ ರಾಜನ ಪಟ್ಟಾಭಿಷೇಕ ಅರಮನೆಯಲ್ಲಿ ನಡೆಯಿತು.
>
> *hosa rājana paṭṭābhiṣēka aramaneyalli naḍeyitu.*
>
> The new king's coronation took place in the palace.

ಪಟ್ಟ, a crown band, and ಅಭಿಷೇಕ, an anointing.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಪಟ್ಟಾಭಿಷೇಕ** (*paṭṭābhiṣēka*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *paṭṭābhiṣeka*, "consecration of a tiara" (Monier-Williams), from *paṭṭa* "a band" and *abhiṣeka* "anointing".
