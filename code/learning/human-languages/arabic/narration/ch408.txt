Arabic, chapter 408: Gains and losses.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
استفاد (istafāda) — to benefit.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to avert, then the Arabic for to arise unexpectedly.

You'll want to know: استفاد.
استفاد — istafāda — "to benefit".
استفاد الطلاب من الرحلة. istafāda aṭ-ṭullāb min ar-riḥla. The students benefited from the trip.
Present: يستفيد, yastafīd.
It takes من: استفاد (istafāda) من ….

The word, taken apart.
استفاد (istafāda) is built on the native Arabic root f-y-d, "to accrue, to come as gain" (Lane). It is Form X, to seek gain from a thing: to benefit.

What you've built.
One more word for saying what happens if.

يتمتع المواطنون بحقوق كثيرة. yatamattaʿu al-muwāṭinūn bi-ḥuqūq kathīra. Citizens enjoy many rights.
Present: يتمتع, yatamattaʿ.

The word, taken apart.
تمتع (tamattaʿa) is built on the native Arabic root m-t-ʿ, "to have the use of" (Lane). It is Form V, to take the use of a thing for oneself: to enjoy it. ممتع, enjoyable, is from the same root.

Lesson 3 of 5.
حرم (ḥarama) — to deprive.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to benefit, then the Arabic for to enjoy a right.

You'll want to know: حرم.
حرم — ḥarama — "to deprive".
حرمت الحمى الطفل من المدرسة. ḥaramat al-ḥummā aṭ-ṭifl min al-madrasa. The fever deprived the child of school.
Present: يحرم, yaḥrim.
It takes من for what is lost: حرمه من ….

The word, taken apart.
حرم (ḥarama) is built on the native Arabic root ḥ-r-m, "to be forbidden" (Lane). Ḥarama is a Form I verb, to make a thing forbidden to someone: to deprive.

تهتم الدولة برفاهية المواطنين. tahtammu ad-dawla bi-rafāhiyyat al-muwāṭinīn. The state cares about the well-being of citizens.

رفاهية (rafāhiyya) is built on the native Arabic root r-f-h, "to live in ease" (Lane). Rafāhiyya is an abstract noun from rafāh, ease of life: well-being.
