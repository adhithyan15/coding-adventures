Lesson 2 of 5.
പ്രധാനപ്പെട്ട (pradhānappeṭṭa) — important.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for success, then the Malayalam for urgent.

You'll want to know: പ്രധാനപ്പെട്ട.
പ്രധാനപ്പെട്ട — pradhānappeṭṭa — "important".

The word, taken apart.
പ്രധാനപ്പെട്ട (pradhānappeṭṭa) joins Sanskrit pradhāna, "chief, principal", to native peṭṭa, a form of the verb "to fall into, happen" (DEDR 3853).

Lesson 4 of 5.
താൽക്കാലിക (tālkkālika) — temporary.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for important, then the Malayalam for safe.

You'll want to know: താൽക്കാലിക.
താൽക്കാലിക — tālkkālika — "temporary".

The word, taken apart.
താൽക്കാലിക (tālkkālika) from Sanskrit tātkālika, "lasting for that time", taken over whole as a tatsama. Its parts are tat, "that", and kāla, "time".

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: നേരിട്ട്.

The word, taken apart.
നേരിട്ട് (nēriṭṭŭ) is native Dravidian: nēr, "straight, direct" (DEDR 3770, with Tamil nēr), and iṭṭu, "having put" (DEDR 442).
