## You'll want to know: спасать
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C296-SPASATEL-02]; assesses=[] -->

**спасать** (*spasát*) — "to save, to rescue".

> Пожарный спас ребёнка.
>
> *Pozhárnyy spas rebyónka.*
>
> The firefighter saved a child.

Imperfective; perfective *спасти* (*spastí*), past *спас*, *спасла*. *Спасите!* — help!

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*спасать* is the imperfective of *спасти*, inherited through Old East Slavic *sŭpasti*, from *sŭ-* "with" and *pasti* "to pasture, to herd", PIE *peh₂-* "to guard, to feed" (EtymDB); the same verb stands behind *спасибо*.
