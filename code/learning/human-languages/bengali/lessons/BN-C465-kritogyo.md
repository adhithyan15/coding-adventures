## You'll want to know: কৃতজ্ঞ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C465-BIBEK465-04]; assesses=[] -->

**কৃতজ্ঞ** — *kritôgyô* — "grateful".

> তোমার সাহায্যের জন্য আমি কৃতজ্ঞ।
>
> *tomār sāhājjer jônyô āmi kritôgyô.*
>
> I am grateful for your help.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**কৃতজ্ঞ** (*kritôgyô*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *kṛtajña*, "knowing what has been done (for you)", so "grateful" (Monier-Williams).
