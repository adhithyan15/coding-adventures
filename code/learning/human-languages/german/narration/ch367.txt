German, chapter 367: Vielleicht.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
eventuell — possibly (not eventually); perhaps.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for if applicable, then the German for at least.

You'll want to know: eventuell.
eventuell — "possibly (not eventually); perhaps".
Eventuell komme ich später, ich weiß es noch nicht. I may possibly come later; I do not know yet.
A false friend: eventuell means possibly, not eventually. Eventually is irgendwann.

The word, taken apart.
eventuell was borrowed from French éventuel "possible", a learned formation on Latin ēventus "an outcome, an event", from ēvenīre "to come out, to happen" (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 2 of 5.
möglicherweise — maybe, possibly.

You'll want to know: möglicherweise.
möglicherweise — "maybe, possibly".
Möglicherweise ist der Zug schon weg. Maybe the train has already gone.
Built like glücklicherweise: möglich + -erweise. The verb comes right after it.

The word, taken apart.
möglicherweise is built from möglich "possible", of the family of mögen and Proto-Germanic maganą "to be able" (EtymDB), and Weise "way, manner".

Lesson 3 of 5.
zumindest — at any rate, at least.

Warm-up.

You'll want to know: zumindest.
zumindest — "at any rate, at least".
Zumindest hat er sich entschuldigt. At any rate, he apologized.
Close to wenigstens. The verb comes right after it: zumindest hat er ….

The word, taken apart.
zumindest grew out of zum mindesten "at the least", built on minder "less", from Old High German minniro and Proto-Germanic minnizô (EtymDB).

Lesson 4 of 5.
immerhin — after all, still (on the bright side).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for maybe, then the German for at any rate.

You'll want to know: immerhin.
immerhin — "after all, still (on the bright side)".
Wir haben verloren. Immerhin haben wir ein Tor geschossen. We lost. Still, we scored a goal.
It names the good side of a bad result. The verb comes right after it.

The word, taken apart.
immerhin joins immer "always", from Middle High German immer (EtymDB), to hin "onward, away": whatever happens, this much stands.

Lesson 5 of 5.
womöglich — perhaps even, quite possibly.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for at any rate, then the German for after all.

You'll want to know: womöglich.
womöglich — "perhaps even, quite possibly".
Der Himmel ist ganz dunkel, womöglich gibt es heute noch ein Gewitter. The sky is very dark; there may even be a thunderstorm today.
Often with a hint of worry: something unwelcome might happen. The verb comes right after it.

The word, taken apart.
womöglich joins wo "where" and möglich "possible", of the family of mögen and Proto-Germanic maganą "to be able" (EtymDB): "if at all possible", then "perhaps even".
