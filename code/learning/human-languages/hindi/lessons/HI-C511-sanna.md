## You'll want to know: सानना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C511-KUMHAR511-02]; assesses=[] -->

**सानना** — *sānnā* — "to mix (with water); to knead (clay)".

> कुम्हार मिट्टी को पानी में सानता है।
>
> *Kumhār miṭṭī ko pānī meṁ sāntā hai.*
>
> The potter mixes the clay with water.

Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने मिट्टी सानी, *usne miṭṭī sānī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सानना** (*sānnā*) is a tadbhava, inherited from Sanskrit *saṁdadhāti*, "places on, combines" (CDIAL 12898). CDIAL gives the Hindi verb as "to mix, knead".
