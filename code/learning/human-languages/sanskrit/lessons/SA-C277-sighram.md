# शीघ्रम् (śīghram) — quickly

## You'll want to know: शीघ्रम्
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C277-SIGHRAM-01]; assesses=[SA-LEX-AGACCHATI-COME, SA-LEX-C71-MOVE-01, SA-LEX-C16-PLACE-01] -->

**शीघ्रम्** — *śīghram* — "quickly".

> **शीघ्रम् आगच्छतु।**
>
> *śīghram āgacchatu.* — "Please come quickly."

> **सः शीघ्रं धावति।**
>
> *saḥ śīghraṃ dhāvati.* — "He runs quickly."

> **सा शीघ्रं गृहं गतवती।**
>
> *sā śīghraṃ gṛhaṃ gatavatī.* — "She went home quickly."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**शीघ्रम्** (*śīghram*) is the neuter of **शीघ्र** (*śīghra*), "quick, swift", said as an adverb, as **प्रथमम्** is. Monier-Williams calls its derivation doubtful, so its origin is uncertain. It lives on as Sindhi *sigho*, "quick" (CDIAL 12484).
