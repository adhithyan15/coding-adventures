## You'll want to know: выживать
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C302-ZADYKHATSYA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**выживать** (*vyzhivát*) — "to survive".

> Он выжил после аварии.
>
> *On výzhil pósle avárii.*
>
> He survived the accident.

Imperfective; perfective *выжить* (*výzhit*), *выживу*, stressed on *вы-*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*выживать* is built on *вы-* "out" and *жить* "to live", an inherited Slavic verb from PIE *gʷeyh₃-* "to live": to live through.
