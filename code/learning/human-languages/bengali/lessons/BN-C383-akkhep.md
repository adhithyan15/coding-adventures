## You'll want to know: আক্ষেপ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C383-SARTHOK383-04]; assesses=[] -->

**আক্ষেপ** — *ākkhep* — "a regret".

> আগে চেষ্টা করলে আজ এই আক্ষেপ থাকত না।
>
> *āge cheshṭā kôrle āj ei ākkhep thāktô nā.*
>
> If I had tried earlier, I would not have this regret today.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**আক্ষেপ** (*ākkhep*) is Sanskrit *ākṣepa*, among whose senses Monier-Williams gives reproach and harsh speech. The Bengali sense, regret for what might have been, is not one the dictionary gives.
