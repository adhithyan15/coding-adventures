## You'll want to know: निर्दिशति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C357-SAMITI357-05]; assesses=[] -->

**निर्दिशति** — *nirdiśati* — "directs; instructs".

> अध्यक्षः सदस्यान् निर्दिशति।
>
> *adhyakṣaḥ sadasyān nirdiśati.*
>
> The president directs the members.

Past (he …): सः सदस्यान् निर्दिष्टवान्, *saḥ sadasyān nirdiṣṭavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**निर्दिशति** (*nirdiśati*) is built on *nir*, "out", and the root √diś, "to point"; MW gives *nir-√diś* "to point to, to show", and *nir-diṣṭa* "pointed out, declared, enjoined" (MW).
