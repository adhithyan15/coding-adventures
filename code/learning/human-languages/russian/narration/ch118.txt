Russian, chapter 118: Squirrel, Owl, Whale, Dolphin, Elbow.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
белка (bélka) — a squirrel.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a frog, then the Russian for a tortoise.

You'll want to know: белка.
белка (bélka) — "a squirrel".

The word, taken apart.
белка (bélka) comes from белый, "white": Old Russian chronicles speak of бела веверица, "a white squirrel", fur paid as tribute; Vasmer calls the link proven.

Lesson 2 of 5.
сова (sová) — an owl.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a tortoise, then the Russian for a squirrel.

You'll want to know: сова.
сова (sová) — "an owl".

The word, taken apart.
сова (sová) from Proto-Slavic sova — inherited. Vasmer relates it to Welsh cuan, "night owl", Old High German hûwo, "owl", and Lithuanian šaũkti, "to cry out": the owl as the hooter.
