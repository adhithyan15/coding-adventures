## You'll want to know: wánjù
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C269-YOUNG269-05]; assesses=[] -->

*wánjù* — "toy".

> *zhège wánjù hěn yǒu yìsi* — This toy is a lot of fun.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*wánjù* is built from *wán* 'to play' and *jù*, both inherited from Middle Chinese, *ngwanH* and *gjuH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to toy with' and the second 'to provide'. Middle Chinese *ngwanH* would regularly give wàn; the rising tone of wán is irregular.
