## You'll want to know: shùlín
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C516-TREES516-04]; assesses=[] -->

*shùlín* — "woods; a grove".

> *wǒ hé sūnnǚ zài shùlín lǐ zǒu le liǎng ge xiǎoshí* — My granddaughter and I walked in the woods for two hours.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*shùlín* is built from 树 'a tree' and *lín* 'a wood', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *dzyuH* and *lim* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which says, after an older dictionary, that land out in the open is called *lim*, and after another, that flat land with stands of trees is.
