# más despacio — the request that makes the last sentence solvable

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-NO-ENTIENDO-01, ES-LEX-POR-FAVOR] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Everything so far assumed you understood what was said to you. This
chapter is for when you did not.

## You'll want to know first
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-MAS-DESPACIO-01]; assesses=[ES-LEX-NO-ENTIENDO-01, ES-LEX-POR-FAVOR] -->

**Más despacio, por favor.** — "More slowly, please."

*No entiendo* stops the conversation. This restarts it. Together they are the
whole repair kit: say what went wrong, then say what would fix it.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-ETYMON-SPATIUM-02]; assesses=[ES-LEX-MAS-DESPACIO-01] -->

*Despacio* comes from **de spatio** — "of space." Latin **spatium** is English
**space**, and also **spatial**.

Doing something *de spatio* meant doing it with room around it, unhurried. The
sense drifted from space into time, which is a journey English made too: we
still say we need *space* when we mean we need *time*.
