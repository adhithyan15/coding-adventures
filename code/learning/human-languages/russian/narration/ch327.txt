Russian, chapter 327: Treasure and finds.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
археолог (arkheológ) — an archaeologist.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a prince of old Rus, then the Russian for to found.

You'll want to know: археолог.
археолог (arkheológ) — "an archaeologist".
Археолог работал в пустыне два года. Arkheológ rabótal v pustýne dva góda. The archaeologist worked in the desert for two years.
Археология — archaeology.

The word, taken apart.
археолог (arkheológ) is built on Greek arkhaîos "ancient", from arkhḗ "beginning", and lógos "word, account" (EtymDB): one who studies ancient things.

находка (nakhódka) — "a find, a discovery".
Эта находка удивила учёных. Éta nakhódka udivíla uchyónykh. This find amazed the scientists.

The word, taken apart.
находка (nakhódka) (EtymDB: "a find, a discovery") is built on находить "to find", from на- and ходить "to go", Proto-Slavic xoditi (EtymDB): what you come upon.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a dig, then the Russian for a find.

You'll want to know: сокровище.
сокровище (sokróvishche) — "a treasure".
Пираты спрятали сокровище на острове. Piráty spryátali sokróvishche na óstrove. The pirates hid the treasure on an island.
Neuter; often plural сокровища.

The word, taken apart.
сокровище (sokróvishche) is usually traced to сокровенный "hidden", built on со- and the root of крыть "to cover": what is kept hidden. EtymDB lists no parent.

Warm-up.

В саду старого дома нашли клад. V sadú stárovo dóma nashlí klad. A hoard was found in the garden of an old house.

The word, taken apart.
клад (klad) is built on the root of класть "to put, to lay", which EtymDB traces to PIE kleh₂- "to put, to lay out": what was laid away.
