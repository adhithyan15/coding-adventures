## You'll want to know: みなおす
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C513-PLAN513-02]; assesses=[] -->

**みなおす** — *minaosu* — "to look over again; to review".

> **もし もんだいが あったら、よていを みなおします。** — *moshi mondai ga attara, yotei o minaoshimasu* — "If there is a problem, I will review the schedule."

Polite **みなおします**, *minaoshimasu*; a godan verb. Past (polite): **せんせいが しけんを みなおしました。**, *sensei ga shiken o minaoshimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**みなおす** (*minaosu*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, built from *miru*, "to see", and *naosu*, "to fix"; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
