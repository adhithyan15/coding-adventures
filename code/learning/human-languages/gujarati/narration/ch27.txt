Gujarati, chapter 27: Village and Shop Complete Ten.
6 lessons. You can do the first 1 of them in the car; after that you will want to have stopped.


Lesson 1 of 6.
ગામ (gām) — hear village first.

Warm-up.
Recall shahar, city. Now listen twice: gām. It means village.

Guided Practice.
Choose city or village as you hear shahar or gām, then say gām once.

Wrap-up Recall.
Source: Cambridge: village.
[question — say your answer, then pause 8 seconds]
What does gām mean?


Lesson 2 of 6.
ગામ (gām) — village on the page.

Before we start: this one needs your hands, so it is not a driving lesson. You can listen to all of it now and come back to the parts that need looking at once you have stopped.

Warm-up.
Say gām from village, then look: ગામ — gām — village.

Guided Practice.
Study ગામ (gām) for five seconds, hide it, and write it once. Check only after the word is complete.

Wrap-up Recall.
Source: Cambridge: village.
[question — say your answer, then pause 18 seconds]
Write village after hiding the model.


Lesson 3 of 6.
દુકાન (dukān) — hear shop first.

Warm-up.
Listen twice: dukān. It means shop. Hear the short u before the long final ā.

Guided Practice.
Point to the shop when you hear dukān, then say dukān from the meaning.

Wrap-up Recall.
Source: Cambridge: shop.
[question — say your answer, then pause 8 seconds]
What does dukān mean?

Lesson 5 of 6.
Retrieve the ten-word map — ten concrete words in mixed retrieval.

Before we start: this one needs your hands, so it is not a driving lesson. You can listen to all of it now and come back to the parts that need looking at once you have stopped.

Warm-up.
[pause 20 seconds]
Recall the old five cards, then city, school, road, village, and shop.

Guided Practice.
Hear ten cards in mixed order, say each from its meaning, read all ten, then write village and shop without looking.

Wrap-up Recall.
Sources: Cambridge: village and shop.
[question — say your answer, then pause 25 seconds]
Write village and shop in that order.


Lesson 6 of 6.
Payoff — ten words in four skills.

Before we start: this one needs your hands, so it is not a driving lesson. You can listen to all of it now and come back to the parts that need looking at once you have stopped.

Warm-up.
[pause 20 seconds]
Name the first five words, then the five new map words.

Guided Practice.
Identify ten heard words, produce ten words from meaning cues, match ten printed cards, and write four words named aloud. Pass listening, speaking, reading, and writing separately; one strong skill cannot compensate for another.

Wrap-up Recall.
Sources: Cambridge: city, school, road, village, and shop.
[question — say your answer, then pause 45 seconds]
After all four skills pass, write city, school, road, village, and shop in order.
