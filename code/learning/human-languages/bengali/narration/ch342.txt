Bengali, chapter 342: In Court.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
উকিল (ukil) — a lawyer.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for corruption, then the Bengali for an injustice.

You'll want to know: উকিল.
উকিল — ukil — "a lawyer".
আমাদের একজন ভালো উকিল দরকার। āmāder ekjôn bhālo ukil dôrkār. We need a good lawyer.

The word, taken apart.
উকিল (ukil) is usually traced to Arabic wakīl, one who acts for another, through Persian. The lawyer is the one who speaks for you.

Lesson 2 of 5.
বিচার করা (bichār kôrā) — to try a case; to judge.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for an injustice, then the Bengali for a lawyer.

You'll want to know: বিচার করা.
বিচার করা — bichār kôrā — "to try a case; to judge".
আদালত এই মামলার বিচার করবে। ādālôt ei māmlār bichār kôrbe. The court will try this case.
Past (he/she …): তিনি মামলাটার বিচার করলেন, tini māmlāṭār bichār kôrlen.

The word, taken apart.
বিচার করা (bichār kôrā) is Sanskrit vicāra, consideration or examination (Monier-Williams), with করা. The judge, বিচারক, is the one who does it.

রায় — rāy — "a verdict; a judgment".
আদালত কাল রায় দেবে। ādālôt kāl rāy debe. The court will give its verdict tomorrow.

The word, taken apart.
রায় (rāy) is Persian rāy, an opinion or judgment, usually traced to Arabic raʾy. A court gives it with দেওয়া.

Lesson 5 of 5.
মীমাংসা করা (mīmāngsā kôrā) — to settle a dispute; to resolve.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a verdict, then the Bengali for a formal complaint.

You'll want to know: মীমাংসা করা.
মীমাংসা করা — mīmāngsā kôrā — "to settle a dispute; to resolve".
দুই পরিবার ঝগড়াটার মীমাংসা করল। dui pôribār jhôgṛāṭār mīmāngsā kôrlô. The two families settled the quarrel.
Past (he/she …): তিনি মামলাটার মীমাংসা করলেন, tini māmlāṭār mīmāngsā kôrlen.

The word, taken apart.
মীমাংসা করা (mīmāngsā kôrā) is Sanskrit mīmāṃsā, deep reflection or investigation (Monier-Williams), with করা. The Bengali sense, settling a question or a dispute, is not one the dictionary gives; the settled matter takes -এর: ঝগড়ার মীমাংসা.
