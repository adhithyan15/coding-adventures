## You'll want to know: ऋते
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C545-ANUKRAMA545-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ऋते** — *ṛte* — "except".

> यदि माम् ऋते सर्वे आगच्छन्ति, तर्हि अहं दुःखी भविष्यामि।
>
> *yadi mām ṛte sarve āgacchanti, tarhi ahaṃ duḥkhī bhaviṣyāmi.*
>
> If everyone comes but me, I will be sad.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ऋते** (*ṛte*) is the word MW gives for excepting, without, unless, and reports as perhaps a case form of the past participle of the root √ṛ, "to go", so its origin is uncertain (MW).
