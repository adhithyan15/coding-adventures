## You'll want to know: कर्णधार
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C529-SAMNA529-02]; assesses=[] -->

**कर्णधार** — *karṇadhār* — "a team captain".

> मीरा आमच्या संघाची कर्णधार आहे.
>
> *mīrā āmcyā saṅghācī karṇadhār āhe.*
>
> Meera is our team's captain.

Masculine.

One word for a man or a woman.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**कर्णधार** (*karṇadhār*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *karṇadhāra*, "helmsman" (Monier-Williams): the captain steers the team.
