## You'll want to know: ਕਟੋਰਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C236-KAM236-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ਕਟੋਰਾ** — *kaṭorā* — "a deep bowl".

> ਕਟੋਰੇ ਵਿੱਚ ਦਾਲ ਹੈ।
>
> *kaṭore vich dāl hai.*
>
> There is dal in the deep bowl.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਕਟੋਰਾ** (*kaṭorā*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*kaṭṭōra*, "cup"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *kaṭorā*, "small open-mouthed cup" (CDIAL 2648).
