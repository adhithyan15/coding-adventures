Marathi, chapter 143: Tortoise, Crocodile, Deer, Rabbit, Wolf.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
कासव (kāsav) — a tortoise.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for an owl, then the Marathi for a frog.

You'll want to know: कासव.
कासव — kāsav — "a tortoise".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: kāsav]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: kāsav, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say kāsav]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Marathi for an owl, then the Marathi for a frog, then say kāsav again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does कासव (kāsav) mean? ("A tortoise".) Say it once more.
