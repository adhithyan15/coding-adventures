## You'll want to know: અવલોકન
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C364-SHODH364-02]; assesses=[] -->

**અવલોકન** — *avlokan* — "an observation".

> મીરાએ પોતાનાં અવલોકનો એક ચોપડીમાં નોંધ્યાં.
>
> *Mīrāe potānā̃ avlokno ek copḍīmā̃ nõdhyā̃.*
>
> Mira wrote her observations down in a notebook.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**અવલોકન** (*avlokan*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *avalokana*, "seeing, observing" (Monier-Williams).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
