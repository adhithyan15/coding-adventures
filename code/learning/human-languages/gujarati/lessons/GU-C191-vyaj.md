## You'll want to know: વ્યાજ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C191-LEND191-02]; assesses=[] -->

**વ્યાજ** — *vyāj* — "interest on money".

> **આ ખાતામાં વ્યાજ ઓછું છે.** — *ā khātāmā̃ vyāj ochũ che* — "This account pays little interest."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

The origin of **વ્યાજ** (*vyāj*) is not settled. It matches Sanskrit *vyāja*, "pretext", in form, but CDIAL has no entry for *vyāja* and no Gujarati word for interest, so the link is unconfirmed.
