## You'll want to know: la probabilidad
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C613-REQUIERE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**la probabilidad** — "the probability, the likelihood".

> La probabilidad es baja.
>
> *The likelihood is low.*

*Con toda probabilidad* — in all likelihood.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*probabilidad* is built on *probable*, a learned borrowing from Latin *probabilis*, from *probare* 'to test' (Lewis & Short).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.
