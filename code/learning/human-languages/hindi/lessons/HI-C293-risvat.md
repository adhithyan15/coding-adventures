## You'll want to know: रिश्वत
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C293-THAGI293-03]; assesses=[] -->

**रिश्वत** — *riśvat* — "a bribe".

> अगर वह रिश्वत माँगेगा, तो मैं पुलिस को बताऊँगा।
>
> *agar vah riśvat mā̃gegā, to maiṁ pulis ko batāūṅgā.*
>
> If he asks for a bribe, I will tell the police.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**रिश्वत** (*riśvat*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *rishwa*, "a bribe". It is feminine: **उसने रिश्वत माँगी**, he asked for a bribe.
