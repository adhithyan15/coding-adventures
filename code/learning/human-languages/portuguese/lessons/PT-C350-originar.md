## You'll want to know: originar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C350-ORIGEM-04]; assesses=[] -->

**originar** — "to give rise to, to cause".

> A tempestade originou cortes de luz em toda a região.
>
> *The storm caused power cuts across the whole region.*

*Originar-se* — to originate.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*originar* is built on *origem* "origin", from Latin *orīgō, orīginis* "beginning, source", on *orīrī* "to rise" (Lewis & Short).
