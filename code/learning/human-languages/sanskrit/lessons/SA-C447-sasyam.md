## You'll want to know: सस्यम्
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C447-KEDARA447-02]; assesses=[] -->

**सस्यम्** — *sasyam* — "a crop".

> केदारेषु हरितं सस्यं वर्धते।
>
> *kedāreṣu haritaṃ sasyaṃ vardhate.*
>
> A green crop grows in the flooded fields.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सस्यम्** (*sasyam*) is the word MW gives for corn, grain, the fruit of the field, a crop; MW gives no derivation, so its origin is uncertain (MW; CDIAL 13295).
