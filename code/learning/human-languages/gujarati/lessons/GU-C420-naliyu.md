## You'll want to know: નળિયું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C420-HOUSE420-02]; assesses=[] -->

**નળિયું** — *naḷiyũ* — "a roof tile (a curved clay tile)".

> તોફાનમાં ઘરનાં ઘણાં નળિયાં તૂટી ગયાં.
>
> *tophānmā̃ gharnā̃ ghaṇā̃ naḷiyā̃ tūṭī gayā̃.*
>
> Many roof tiles of the house broke in the storm.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**નળિયું** (*naḷiyũ*) seems to be built on નળ, a pipe, which goes back to Sanskrit *naḍa*, "a reed" (CDIAL 6936): a curved tile is like half a pipe.
