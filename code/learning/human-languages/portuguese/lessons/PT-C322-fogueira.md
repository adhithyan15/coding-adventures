## You'll want to know: a fogueira
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C322-FOGUETE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**a fogueira** — "the bonfire".

> Na praia, fizemos uma fogueira e cantámos.
>
> *On the beach we made a bonfire and sang.*

People jump over *fogueiras* on some June nights.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*fogueira* comes down from Old Portuguese (EtymDB) and goes back to Vulgar Latin *focāria*, built on Latin *focus* "a hearth".
