## You'll want to know: o andebol
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C512-PAVILHAO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**o andebol** — "handball".

> Joga andebol no clube do bairro.
>
> *He plays handball at the local club.*

In Brazil: *o handebol*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*andebol* was borrowed from English *handball* (EtymDB), adapted to Portuguese spelling.
