## You'll want to know: ತೇವ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C484-TAPAMANA484-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ತೇವ** — *tēva* — "dampness; moisture".

> ಮಳೆಗಾಲದಲ್ಲಿ ಗೋಡೆಗಳಲ್ಲಿ ತೇವ ಇರುತ್ತದೆ.
>
> *maḷegāladalli gōḍegaḷalli tēva iruttade.*
>
> In the rainy season there is damp in the walls.

ತೇವದ ಬಟ್ಟೆ, damp clothes.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ತೇವ** (*tēva*) is a Kannada word for dampness whose origin is not settled.
