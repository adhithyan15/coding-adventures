## You'll want to know: la saison
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C523-SERIES-02]; assesses=[] -->

**la saison** — "season".

> La deuxième saison de la série sort en mars.
>
> *The second season of the series comes out in March.*

Also the seasons of the year, and *la haute saison* for tourism.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*la saison* is inherited from Latin *satiō*, *satiōnem* "a sowing" (EtymDB), from *serere* "to sow" (Lewis & Short): first the time for sowing.
