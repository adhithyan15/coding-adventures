पारितोषिकम् (pāritoṣikam) — a prize.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for an item, then the Sanskrit for a tool.

You'll want to know: पारितोषिकम्.
पारितोषिकम् — pāritoṣikam — "a prize".

The word, taken apart.
pāritoṣika comes from paritoṣa, "delight, satisfaction", built on pari- and the root √tuṣ, "to be pleased" (MW). It first meant "gratifying", and then "a reward", the thing that pleases.

Lesson 2 of 5.
निमन्त्रणम् (nimantraṇam) — an invitation.

You'll want to know: निमन्त्रणम्.
निमन्त्रणम् — nimantraṇam — "an invitation".

The word, taken apart.
nimantraṇa is built on ni- and mantrayate, "speaks, takes counsel", a verb MW makes from mantra, which is built on the root √man, "to think". MW glosses it "invitation, a summons", and Hindi took it over as nimantraṇ.

Lesson 3 of 5.
सभा (sabhā) — an assembly, a meeting.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a prize, then the Sanskrit for an invitation.

You'll want to know: सभा.
सभा — sabhā — "an assembly, a meeting".

The word, taken apart.
sabhā is of unknown derivation, MW says, though probably connected with sa-, "together". MW compares Gothic sibja, "kinship", and English gossip, which MW also cites in its older form god-sib. It is a Rigvedic word for an assembly or hall (CDIAL 13167).

Lesson 4 of 5.
गोष्ठी (goṣṭhī) — a discussion group.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for an invitation, then the Sanskrit for an assembly.

You'll want to know: गोष्ठी.
गोष्ठी — goṣṭhī — "a discussion group".

The word, taken apart.
goṣṭhī comes from goṣṭha, a cow-pen, built on go, "cow", and stha, "standing" (MW). The cow-pen became a meeting place, then an assembly, then a talk (CDIAL 4339). Marathi goṭh now means "a tale".

You'll want to know: मेलकः.
मेलकः — melakaḥ — "a fair, a gathering".

The word, taken apart.
melaka is mela, "meeting", built on the root √mil, "to meet" (MW), with the suffix -ka. It lives on as Hindi melā, "fair" (CDIAL 10331).
