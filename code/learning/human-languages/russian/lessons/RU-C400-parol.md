## You'll want to know: пароль
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C400-BEZOPASNOST-01]; assesses=[] -->

**пароль** (*paról*) — "a password".

> Я забыл пароль от почты.
>
> *Ya zabýl paról ot póchty.*
>
> I have forgotten my email password.

Masculine. *Пароль от* + genitive — the password for.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**пароль** was borrowed from French *parole* "a word, a word of honour" (EtymDB), which is usually traced to Latin *parabola* "a comparison" (Lewis & Short), later "a saying": first the word a sentry asks for.
