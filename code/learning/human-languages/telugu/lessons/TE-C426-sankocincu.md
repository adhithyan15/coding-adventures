# సంకోచించు (saṅkōciñcu) — to hold back; to hesitate

## You'll want to know: సంకోచించు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C426-ARAMBHAM426-01]; assesses=[] -->

**సంకోచించు** — *saṅkōciñcu* — "to hold back; to hesitate".

> ఆమె నిజం చెప్పడానికి సంకోచించింది.
>
> *āme nijaṁ ceppaḍāniki saṅkōciñcindi.*
>
> She hesitated to tell the truth.

Past (I …): సంకోచించాను, *saṅkōciñcānu*.

What you hesitate over takes -డానికి. Close to వెనకాడు, which you know.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**సంకోచించు** (*saṅkōciñcu*) is built on Sanskrit *saṁkoca*, "contraction, shrinking; shyness" (Monier-Williams), with the Telugu verb ending -ఇంచు.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
