## You'll want to know: तबाही
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C304-TOOFAN304-05]; assesses=[] -->

**तबाही** — *tabāhī* — "destruction; havoc".

> तूफ़ान ने पूरे शहर में तबाही की।
>
> *tūfān ne pūre śahar meṁ tabāhī kī.*
>
> The storm wreaked havoc across the whole city.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**तबाही** (*tabāhī*) is usually traced to Persian *tabāhī*, "ruin", from *tabāh*, ruined. A storm or a war **तबाही करता है**, or **तबाही मचाता है**.
