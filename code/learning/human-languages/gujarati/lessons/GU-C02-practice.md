# Chapter 2 — The introduction exchange

## Warm-up

[PAUSE 2s] No new words. The whole introduction, assembled from atoms you now own.

## The exchange

[PAUSE 1s each]
- [YOU SAY: "My name is Mira." — *mārũ nām Mira chhe.*]
- [YOU SAY: "What's your name?" — *tamārũ nām shũ chhe?*]
- [YOU SAY: "My name is Arun." — *mārũ nām Arun chhe.*]
- [YOU SAY: "Pleased to meet you." — *tamne maḷīne ānand thayo.*]

## The atoms

[PAUSE 2s]
- [YOU SAY: "name," "my," "is" (*nām*, *mārũ*, *chhe*)]
- [YOU SAY: the two "you" levels (*tũ* / *tame*)]
- [YOU SAY: "what," and where the verb sits (*shũ*; the verb is last)]

## Roots you now carry

[PAUSE 2s]
- *nām* ← Sanskrit *nāman* → English **name**
- *mārũ* ← *ma-* → English **my**; agrees in three genders (*māro/mārī/mārũ*)
- *chhe* — Gujarati's own copula (not Hindi's *hai*)
- *shũ* ← *ka-* → English *what*; *tũ* ← *\*tū* → *thou*

## Wrap-up Recall

[PAUSE 3s] Introduce yourself and ask a stranger's name in Gujarati. (*Mārũ nām …
chhe. Tamārũ nām shũ chhe?*)
