## You'll want to know: ゆき
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C207-WEATHER207-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ゆき** — *yuki* — "snow".

> **らいしゅう ゆきが ふります。** — *raishū yuki ga furimasu* — "It will snow next week."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ゆき** (*yuki*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi, and its older origin is not settled (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
