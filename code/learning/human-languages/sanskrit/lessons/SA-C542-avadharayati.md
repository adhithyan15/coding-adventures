## You'll want to know: अवधारयति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C542-UTKRSTA542-02]; assesses=[] -->

**अवधारयति** — *avadhārayati* — "makes sure".

> यदि भवान् मार्गं न जानाति, तर्हि पूर्वम् अवधारयतु।
>
> *yadi bhavān mārgaṃ na jānāti, tarhi pūrvam avadhārayatu.*
>
> If you don't know the way, make sure of it first.

Past (he …): सः मार्गम् अवधारितवान्, *saḥ mārgam avadhāritavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अवधारयति** (*avadhārayati*) is built on *ava*, "down", and the causative of the root √dhṛ, "to hold", which also gives *dhārayati*; MW gives *avadhārayati* as to consider, to ascertain, to determine accurately (MW).
