## You'll want to know: పరామర్శించు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C519-CIKITSA519-05]; assesses=[] -->

**పరామర్శించు** — *parāmarśiñcu* — "to visit the sick; to inquire after".

> ఆసుపత్రిలో ఉన్న స్నేహితుడిని మేము పరామర్శించాము.
>
> *āsupatrilō unna snēhituḍini mēmu parāmarśiñcāmu.*
>
> We visited our friend in hospital.

Past (I …): పరామర్శించాను, *parāmarśiñcānu*.

Also calling on a family in mourning to share their grief.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**పరామర్శించు** (*parāmarśiñcu*) is built on Sanskrit *parāmarśa* (Monier-Williams), first "a seizing, a touching", with the Telugu verb ending -ఇంచు; in Telugu it means going to see someone who is ill or grieving.
