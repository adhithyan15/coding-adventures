## You'll want to know: ડિગ્રી
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C216-HEAT216-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ડિગ્રી** — *ḍigrī* — "degree".

> **કાલે ચાળીસ ડિગ્રી હશે.** — *kāle cāḷīs ḍigrī haśe* — "It will be forty degrees tomorrow."

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ડિગ્રી** (*ḍigrī*) is a loan from English *degree*, for heat and for a university degree alike.
