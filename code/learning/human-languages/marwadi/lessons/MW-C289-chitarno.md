## You'll want to know: चीतरणो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C289-MASIN289-05]; assesses=[] -->

**चीतरणो** — *chītarṇo* — "to paint a picture".

> छोरी भींत माथै मोर चीतरै है।
>
> *chhorī bhī̃t māthai mor chītrai hai.*
>
> The girl paints a peacock on the wall.

Past (I …): म्हैं मोर चीतर्यो, *mhaĩ mor chītaryo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**चीतरणो** (*chītarṇo*) is inherited from Sanskrit *citrakāra*, "a painter"; CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry, but lists Hindi *citārnā*, "to paint" (CDIAL 4807).
