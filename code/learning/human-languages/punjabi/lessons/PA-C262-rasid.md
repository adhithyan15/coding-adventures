## You'll want to know: ਰਸੀਦ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C262-FEE262-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ਰਸੀਦ** — *rasīd* — "a receipt".

> ਫ਼ੀਸ ਦੀ ਰਸੀਦ ਮੇਰੀ ਜੇਬ ਵਿੱਚ ਹੈ।
>
> *fīs dī rasīd merī jeb vich hai.*
>
> The receipt for the fee is in my pocket.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਰਸੀਦ** (*rasīd*) is a loan, usually traced to Persian *rasīd*, "arrived; a receipt".
