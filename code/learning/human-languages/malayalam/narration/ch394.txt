Malayalam, chapter 394: Property and Inheritance.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
സ്വത്ത് (svattŭ) — property; possessions.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for a stone inscription, then the Malayalam for to commemorate.

You'll want to know: സ്വത്ത്.
സ്വത്ത് — svattŭ — "property; possessions".
മുത്തച്ഛന്റെ സ്വത്ത് മക്കൾക്ക് കിട്ടി. muttacchanṟe svattŭ makkaḷkkŭ kiṭṭi. The children got grandfather's property.
Land, a house, money: everything one owns. സമ്പത്ത് is wealth in general.

The word, taken apart.
സ്വത്ത് (svattŭ) is usually traced to Sanskrit svatva, "proprietary right, property" (Monier-Williams), from sva, "own".

Lesson 2 of 5.
അവകാശി (avakāśi) — an heir.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for to commemorate, then the Malayalam for property.

You'll want to know: അവകാശി.
അവകാശി — avakāśi — "an heir".
അവൻ ആ വീടിന്റെ ഒരേയൊരു അവകാശി ആണ്. avan ā vīṭinṟe orēyoru avakāśi āṇŭ. He is the only heir to that house.
From അവകാശം, a right: one who has the right to inherit.

The word, taken apart.
അവകാശി (avakāśi) is built on അവകാശം (avakāśaṁ), "a right", from Sanskrit avakāśa, "space, room; opportunity" (CDIAL 728), with the person ending -ി.

Lesson 3 of 5.
ഒസ്യത്ത് (osyattŭ) — a will; a testament.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for property, then the Malayalam for an heir.

You'll want to know: ഒസ്യത്ത്.
ഒസ്യത്ത് — osyattŭ — "a will; a testament".
മരിക്കുന്നതിന് മുൻപ് അദ്ദേഹം ഒരു ഒസ്യത്ത് എഴുതി. marikkunnatinŭ munpŭ addēhaṁ oru osyattŭ eḻuti. Before he died he wrote a will.
A will is written or made: ഒസ്യത്ത് (osyattŭ) എഴുതുക.

The word, taken apart.
ഒസ്യത്ത് (osyattŭ) is usually traced to Arabic waṣiyya, "a bequest". It belongs to the language of courts and land deeds.

Lesson 5 of 5.
പൈതൃകം (paitṛkaṁ) — heritage; an inheritance.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for a will, then the Malayalam for a widow.

You'll want to know: പൈതൃകം.
പൈതൃകം — paitṛkaṁ — "heritage; an inheritance".
ആ പഴയ വീട് ഞങ്ങളുടെ കുടുംബത്തിന്റെ പൈതൃകം ആണ്. ā paḻaya vīṭŭ ñaṅṅaḷuṭe kuṭumbattinṟe paitṛkaṁ āṇŭ. That old house is our family's heritage.
What the fathers left: a house, a craft, a custom.

The word, taken apart.
പൈതൃകം (paitṛkaṁ) is borrowed from Sanskrit paitṛka, "belonging to a father, paternal, ancestral" (Monier-Williams), from pitṛ, "a father".
