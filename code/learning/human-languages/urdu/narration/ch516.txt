Urdu, chapter 516: Carving and Calligraphy.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
خطاطی (khattātī) — calligraphy.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a masterpiece, then the Urdu for to show through.

You'll want to know: خطاطی.
خطاطی — khattātī — "calligraphy".
میرے استاد خطاطی سکھاتے ہیں۔ mere ustād khattātī sikhāte haiṅ. My teacher teaches calligraphy.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
خطاطی (khattātī) is built on khattāt, "a calligrapher", usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic khaṭṭāṭ, with the noun ending -ī.

Lesson 4 of 5.
کندہ کرنا (kandā karnā) — to engrave.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for carved or painted decoration, then the Urdu for a figurine.

You'll want to know: کندہ کرنا.
کندہ کرنا — kandā karnā — "to engrave".
سنار نے انگوٹھی پر نام کندہ کیا۔ sunār ne angūṭhī par nām kandā kiyā. The goldsmith engraved a name on the ring.
Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے پتھر پر ایک لفظ کندہ کیا, us ne patthar par ek lafz kandā kiyā.

The word, taken apart.
کندہ کرنا (kandā karnā) is built from kandā, usually traced to Persian kanda, "dug, carved", from kandan, "to dig", and karnā, "to do".
