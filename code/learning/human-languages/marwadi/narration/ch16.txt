Marwadi (Marwari), chapter 16: Wind, Cloud, and Rain: No New Signs.
9 lessons. All 9 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 9.
Hear havā — wind.

Warm-up.
[pause 22 seconds]
Recall the five-word travel payoff, say home, then write नाम, पत्नी, and बाजार.

You'll want to know.
havā — wind.
Listen once, then say the two light beats. Keep the meaning in your ear; the spelling waits for the next lesson even though every sign is already familiar.

Wrap-up Recall.
Source: Marwari Pathshala, Lesson 7.
[question — say your answer, then pause 10 seconds]
What weather word does havā name?


Lesson 2 of 9.
हवा (havā) — wind on the page.

Warm-up.
[pause 18 seconds]
Say wind and money, write घर, then form ह, व, and ा once.

You'll want to know.
ह + वा becomes हवा — havā — wind.

Guided Practice.
Look, cover, wait five seconds, and write हवा (havā). No new sign has been added.

Wrap-up Recall.
Source: Marwari Pathshala, Lesson 7.
[question — say your answer, then pause 12 seconds]
From havā, write the weather word.


Lesson 3 of 9.
Hear bādal — cloud.

Warm-up.
[pause 22 seconds]
Say and write wind, then recall temple, hand, maternal grandmother, and the ै mark in है.

You'll want to know.
bādal — cloud; clouds.
Hear the long first vowel. Say the sky word once without looking at any script.

Wrap-up Recall.
Source: Marwari Pathshala, Lesson 7.
[question — say your answer, then pause 10 seconds]
What does bādal name in the sky?


Lesson 4 of 9.
बादल (bādal) — cloud on the page.

Warm-up.
[pause 25 seconds]
Say cloud, wind, and market. Write नाना and the known my-name line ending in है, then recall ब, ा, द, and ल.

You'll want to know.
बा + दल becomes बादल — bādal — cloud.

Guided Practice.
Look, cover, wait five seconds, and write the two chunks. Check that only the first syllable carries ा.

Wrap-up Recall.
Source: Marwari Pathshala, Lesson 7.
[question — say your answer, then pause 12 seconds]
From bādal, write the sky word.


Lesson 5 of 9.

You'll want to know.
barsāt — rain; rainfall.
Listen for the long final vowel. Say the weather word once from a rain cue.

Wrap-up Recall.
Source: Marwari Pathshala, Lesson 7.
[question — say your answer, then pause 10 seconds]
What weather does barsāt name?


Lesson 6 of 9.
बरसात (barsāt) — rain on the page.

Warm-up.
[pause 24 seconds]
Say rain and wind, recall the travel payoff, write थ, then form ब, र, स, ा, and त.

You'll want to know.
बर + सात becomes बरसात — barsāt — rain.

Guided Practice.
Look, cover, wait five seconds, and write बरसात (barsāt). Check that ा sits only in the final chunk.

Wrap-up Recall.
Source: Marwari Pathshala, Lesson 7.
[question — say your answer, then pause 12 seconds]
From barsāt, write the rain word.


Lesson 7 of 9.
Retrieve wind, cloud, and rain — wind, cloud, and rain.

Warm-up.
[pause 22 seconds]
Write घ, ज, बच्चा, and थारो, then say wife.

Guided Practice.
Hear the three words in mixed order, say each from its meaning, match three printed cards, then write all three from sound.

Wrap-up Recall.
Source: Marwari Pathshala, Lesson 7.
[question — say your answer, then pause 30 seconds]
Write wind, cloud, and rain in order.


Lesson 8 of 9.
Payoff — three weather words in four skills.

Warm-up.
[pause 25 seconds]
Say and write market and home, recall the twelve-label family payoff, then give the meanings of kāĩ and hai from the known name exchange.

Guided Practice.
Identify three heard words.
Produce three words from meaning cues.
Match three printed cards to meanings.
Write all three heard words without a model.
Pass each skill separately.

Wrap-up Recall.
Source: Marwari Pathshala, Lesson 7.
[question — say your answer, then pause 36 seconds]
Complete all four scored checks, then write wind, cloud, and rain.


Lesson 9 of 9.
Close the weather loop — close the three-word weather loop.

Warm-up.
[pause 22 seconds]
Write घ, ज, थ, and क, then name the four weather-payoff skills you just passed.

Guided Practice.
Without a model, hear one word, say its meaning, read the other two in mixed order, then write all three from meaning cues.

Wrap-up Recall.
Source: Marwari Pathshala, Lesson 7.
[question — say your answer, then pause 36 seconds]
After the four-skill pass, write wind, cloud, and rain without a model.
