## You'll want to know: かたみち
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C205-TICKET205-04]; assesses=[] -->

**かたみち** — *katamichi* — "one way; a single ticket".

> **かたみちの きっぷを ください。** — *katamichi no kippu o kudasai* — "A one-way ticket, please."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**かたみち** (*katamichi*) is native Japanese (wago), built from *kata*, "one side, one of a pair", and *michi*, "road", both inherited rather than borrowed (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
