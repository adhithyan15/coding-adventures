Urdu, chapter 497: The Carpenter's Bench.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
تختہ (takhtā) — a plank; a board.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for to bore (a hole), then the Urdu for sandpaper.

You'll want to know: تختہ.
تختہ — takhtā — "a plank; a board".
بڑھئی نے ایک لمبا تختہ خریدا۔ baṛhaī ne ek lambā takhtā kharīdā. The carpenter bought a long plank.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
تختہ (takhtā) is usually traced to Persian takhta, "a board, a plank".

Lesson 4 of 5.

You'll want to know: ٹھونکنا.
ٹھونکنا — ṭhoṅknā — "to knock in; to hammer (a nail)".
اس نے ہتھوڑے سے دیوار میں کیل ٹھونکی۔ us ne hathauṛe se dīvār meṅ kīl ṭhoṅkī. He hammered a nail into the wall.
Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے تختے میں کیل ٹھونکی, us ne takhte meṅ kīl ṭhoṅkī.

The word, taken apart.
ٹھونکنا (ṭhoṅknā) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin ṭhoknā, "to knock, to make firm", under ṭhōkk, "knock", a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 5513).

Lesson 5 of 5.
ریتنا (retnā) — to file (metal or wood).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a carpenter's plane, then the Urdu for to knock in.

You'll want to know: ریتنا.
ریتنا — retnā — "to file (metal or wood)".
مستری نے چابی کے دانت ریتے۔ mistrī ne chābī ke dānt rete. The mechanic filed down the teeth of the key.
Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے کیل ریتی, us ne kīl retī.

The word, taken apart.
ریتنا (retnā) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousins retī, "sand, a file", and retnā, "to file", under Sanskrit rētra (CDIAL 10816).
