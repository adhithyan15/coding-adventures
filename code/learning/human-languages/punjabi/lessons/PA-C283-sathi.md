## You'll want to know: ਸਾਥੀ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C283-ROAD283-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ਸਾਥੀ** — *sāthī* — "a companion; a comrade".

> ਸਫ਼ਰ ਵਿੱਚ ਅਮਨ ਮੇਰਾ ਸਾਥੀ ਹੋਵੇਗਾ।
>
> *safar vich Aman merā sāthī hovegā.*
>
> Aman will be my companion on the journey.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਸਾਥੀ** (*sāthī*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*sārthin*, "having part in a company"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *sāthī*, "comrade", under it (CDIAL 13366).
