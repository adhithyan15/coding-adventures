## You'll want to know: نسیم
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C479-RANGINKAMAN-02]; assesses=[] -->

**نسیم** (*nasim*) — "a breeze".

> عصر نسیم خنکی از دریا میوزید.
>
> *'Asr nasim-e khonaki az daryâ mivazid.*
>
> In the afternoon a cool breeze was blowing from the sea.

Also a given name, both for boys and girls.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*nasim* was borrowed from Arabic *nasīm*, which EtymDB lists; it is usually glossed 'a gentle breeze'.
