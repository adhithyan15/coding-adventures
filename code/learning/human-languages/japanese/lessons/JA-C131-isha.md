## You'll want to know: いしゃ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C131-ISHA]; assesses=[JA-SCRIPT-SHI-01, JA-SCRIPT-I-01] -->

**いしゃ** — *isha* — "a doctor".

The small sign is spent at once. **し** with **ゃ** after it is one beat, *sha*, so
the word is two beats: *i–sha*.
