## You'll want to know: воспитание
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C497-UPBRINGING-01]; assesses=[] -->

**воспитание** (*vospitániye*) — "upbringing".

> Хорошее воспитание видно сразу.
>
> *Khorósheye vospitániye vídno srázu.*
>
> A good upbringing shows at once.

Neuter. The verbs are *воспитывать / воспитать* — to bring up.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*воспитание* is built on *воспитать* 'to bring up', from *вос-* and *питать* 'to feed, to nourish'; EtymDB has no entry for the noun.
