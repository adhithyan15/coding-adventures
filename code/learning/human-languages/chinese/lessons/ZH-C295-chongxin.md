## You'll want to know: 重新
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C295-AGAIN295-02]; assesses=[] -->

**重新** — *chóngxīn* — "anew; over again from the start".

> **你得重新写** *nǐ děi chóngxīn xiě* — You'll have to write it over again.

The new character, in pieces: 新 — thirteen strokes in three pieces: top left, the piece on the left of 站, five strokes; under it a bar, a vertical with a hook, a short sweep and a dot; on the right two strokes falling to the left, a bar and a long vertical. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**重新** (*chóngxīn*) is built from 重 'to repeat' and 新 'new', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *drjowng* and *sin* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first, in this reading, 'to double' and 'to layer'; the same character read *drjowngX*, glossed 'much' and 'thick', is the source of zhòng 'heavy'.
