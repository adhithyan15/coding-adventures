## You'll want to know: yīntiān
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C136-WEATHER136-02]; assesses=[] -->

*yīntiān* — "overcast day".

> *jīntiān shì yīntiān yǒudiǎnr lěng* — It's overcast today, and a bit cold.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*yīntiān* is built from *yīn* 'shade, dark' and 天 'sky', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *'im* and *then* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription). The first has the same old form as the 音 of 音乐 but is a different word.
