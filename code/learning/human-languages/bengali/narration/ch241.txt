Lesson 1 of 5.
হারিয়ে যাওয়া (hāriye jāoyā) — to get lost.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for to sink, then the Bengali for to float.

You'll want to know: হারিয়ে যাওয়া.
হারিয়ে যাওয়া — hāriye jāoyā — "to get lost".

The phrase, taken apart.
হারিয়ে যাওয়া (hāriye jāoyā) is hāriye, "losing", a form of hārā, "to lose", from Sanskrit hārayati, "loses" (CDIAL 14061), + jāoyā, "to go", from Sanskrit yāti, "goes" (CDIAL 10452).

Lesson 4 of 5.
জানানো (jānāno) — to inform.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for to find, then the Bengali for to notice.

You'll want to know: জানানো.
জানানো — jānāno — "to inform".

The word, taken apart.
জানানো (jānāno) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from jānāti, "knows" (CDIAL 5193). CDIAL lists Bengali jānā, "to know"; jānāno is its causing form, "to make know".
