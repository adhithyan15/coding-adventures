Italian, chapter 484: Una gamba ingessata.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
la radiografia — X-ray.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for to trip, then the Italian for to cut oneself.

You'll want to know: la radiografia.
la radiografia — "X-ray".
Il medico mi ha fatto una radiografia al braccio. The doctor X-rayed my arm.
Fare una radiografia — to have an X-ray. In speech often le lastre.

The word, taken apart.
radiografia is a modern learned word built from Latin radius "a ray" (Lewis & Short) and Greek -graphía "writing" (EtymDB).

You'll want to know: il gesso.
il gesso — "plaster cast; chalk".
Porto il gesso al braccio per un mese. I am wearing a cast on my arm for a month.
At school, il gesso is the chalk for the blackboard.

The word, taken apart.
gesso is inherited from Latin gypsum "chalk, plaster" (EtymDB), from Greek gýpsos.

In ospedale mi hanno ingessato la gamba. At the hospital they put my leg in a cast.
