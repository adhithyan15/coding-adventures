## You'll want to know: befördern
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C301-KUENDIGEN-03]; assesses=[] -->

**befördern** — "to promote; to transport".

> Nach zwei Jahren wurde sie befördert.
>
> *After two years she was promoted.*

Also 'to transport': *Pakete befördern*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*befördern* is built on *fördern* "to further", from Middle High German *vürdern* (EtymDB), a cousin of English *further*.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
