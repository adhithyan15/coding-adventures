Lesson 2 of 5.
വള്ളംകളി (vaḷḷaṁkaḷi) — a snake-boat race.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for information, then the Malayalam for a joke.

You'll want to know: വള്ളംകളി.
വള്ളംകളി — vaḷḷaṁkaḷi — "a snake-boat race".

The word, taken apart.
വള്ളംകളി (vaḷḷaṁkaḷi) is native Dravidian, "boat play": vaḷḷam, "canoe, boat" (DEDR 5315, with Tamil vaḷḷam), and kaḷi, "play, game" (DEDR 1374). DEDR 1374 compares Sanskrit kalyā and kaḍ.

Lesson 3 of 5.
കണി (kaṇi) — the Vishu kani, the first auspicious sight of the year.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for a joke, then the Malayalam for a snake-boat race.

You'll want to know: കണി.
കണി — kaṇi — "the Vishu kani, the first auspicious sight of the year".

The word, taken apart.
കണി (kaṇi) is native Dravidian, "the sight": it belongs with the seeing verb, kāṇuka (DEDR 1443, with Tamil kāṇ).

You'll want to know: കൈനീട്ടം.
കൈനീട്ടം — kainīṭṭaṁ — "a Vishu gift of money".

The word, taken apart.
കൈനീട്ടം (kainīṭṭaṁ) is native Dravidian, "the stretching out of the hand": kai, "hand" (DEDR 2023), and a noun from the verb "to stretch out, hold out" (DEDR 3692, with Tamil nīḷ).

പടക്കം — paṭakkaṁ — "a firecracker".

The word, taken apart.
പടക്കം (paṭakkaṁ) is native Dravidian, one word with Tamil paṭapaṭa (DEDR 3841). DEDR 3841 lists Malayalam paṭakkam, "a cracker", itself; the root imitates the crack.
