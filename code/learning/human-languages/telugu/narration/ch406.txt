Telugu, chapter 406: Corruption and Accountability.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
అవినీతి (avinīti) — corruption.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a budget, then the Telugu for a loan.

You'll want to know: అవినీతి.
అవినీతి — avinīti — "corruption".
అవినీతి వల్ల దేశానికి చాలా నష్టం జరుగుతుంది. avinīti valla dēśāniki cālā naṣṭaṁ jarugutundi. Corruption does a lot of harm to the country.
అ-, not, + వినీతి, good conduct.

The word, taken apart.
అవినీతి (avinīti) is built from Sanskrit a-, "not", and vinīti, "training, good behaviour" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 2 of 5.
లంచం (lañcaṁ) — a bribe.

Warm-up.

Before the new one: say the Telugu for a loan, then the Telugu for corruption.

You'll want to know: లంచం.
లంచం — lañcaṁ — "a bribe".
ఆ అధికారి లంచం అడిగాడు. ā adhikāri lañcaṁ aḍigāḍu. That officer asked for a bribe.
Plural: లంచాలు, lañcālu.
లంచం (lañcaṁ) ఇచ్చు, to give a bribe; లంచం (lañcaṁ) తీసుకో, to take one.

The word, taken apart.
లంచం (lañcaṁ) is borrowed from Sanskrit lañcā, "a present, a bribe" (CDIAL 10914).

Lesson 3 of 5.
పారదర్శకత (pāradarśakata) — transparency.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for corruption, then the Telugu for a bribe.

You'll want to know: పారదర్శకత.
పారదర్శకత — pāradarśakata — "transparency".
ప్రభుత్వ పనుల్లో పారదర్శకత ఉండాలి. prabhutva panullō pāradarśakata uṇḍāli. There should be transparency in the work of government.
పార, the far side, + దర్శక, showing, + -త.

The word, taken apart.
పారదర్శకత (pāradarśakata) is built on Sanskrit pāradarśaka, "showing the opposite shore" (Monier-Williams), with the abstract ending -త; the sense "transparency" is modern.

Lesson 4 of 5.
ఆరోపణ (ārōpaṇa) — an allegation; a charge.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a bribe, then the Telugu for transparency.

You'll want to know: ఆరోపణ.
ఆరోపణ — ārōpaṇa — "an allegation; a charge".
అతని మీద అవినీతి ఆరోపణలు వచ్చాయి. atani mīda avinīti ārōpaṇalu vaccāyi. Allegations of corruption were made against him.
Plural: ఆరోపణలు, ārōpaṇalu.
The person accused takes మీద: అతని మీద ఆరోపణ (ārōpaṇa).

The word, taken apart.
ఆరోపణ (ārōpaṇa) is borrowed from Sanskrit āropaṇa, "placing or fixing on" (Monier-Williams), of the family of āropa, "charging with, attributing to".

You'll want to know: ఆరోపించు.
ఆరోపించు — ārōpiñcu — "to allege; to accuse".
అతను డబ్బు తీసుకున్నాడని ఆమె ఆరోపించింది. atanu ḍabbu tīsukunnāḍani āme ārōpiñcindi. She alleged that he had taken the money.
Past (I …): ఆరోపించాను, ārōpiñcānu.
What is alleged is a clause ending in అని.

The word, taken apart.
ఆరోపించు (ārōpiñcu) is built on Sanskrit āropa, "charging with; assigning or attributing to" (Monier-Williams), with the Telugu verb ending -ఇంచు.
