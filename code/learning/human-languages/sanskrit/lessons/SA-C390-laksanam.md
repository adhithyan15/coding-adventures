# लक्षणम् (lakṣaṇam) — a sign of something to come; a mark

## You'll want to know: लक्षणम्
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C390-LAKSHANA390-01]; assesses=[] -->

**लक्षणम्** — *lakṣaṇam* — "a sign of something to come; a mark".

> यदि ज्वरस्य लक्षणम् अस्ति, तर्हि वैद्यं पश्यतु।
>
> *yadi jvarasya lakṣaṇam asti, tarhi vaidyaṃ paśyatu.*
>
> If there is a sign of fever, see a doctor.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**लक्षणम्** (*lakṣaṇam*) comes from the root √lakṣ, "to mark, to perceive"; MW gives *lakṣaṇa* "a mark, a sign, a symbol, a token, a characteristic" (MW).
