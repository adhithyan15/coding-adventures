## You'll want to know: মনোযোগ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C362-MONOJOG362-01]; assesses=[] -->

**মনোযোগ** — *mônojog* — "attention; concentration".

> মনোযোগ দিয়ে শোনো।
>
> *mônojog diye shono.*
>
> Listen carefully.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**মনোযোগ** (*mônojog*) is a learned compound of Sanskrit *manas*, mind, and *yoga*, joining (Monier-Williams): joining your mind to the task. The compound itself is not in the dictionary. মনোযোগ দিয়ে means 'carefully, with attention'.
