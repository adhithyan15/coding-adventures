## You'll want to know: la huelga
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C490-EMPLEO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**la huelga** — "the strike".

> Mañana hay huelga de trenes.
>
> *Tomorrow there is a train strike.*

*Estar en huelga* — to be on strike.
