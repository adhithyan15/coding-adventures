## You'll want to know: பராமரி
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C497-VALARPPU497-04]; assesses=[] -->

**பராமரி** — *parāmari* — "to look after; to maintain".

> அவள் வயதான பெற்றோரைப் பராமரித்தாள்.
>
> *avaḷ vayatāṉa peṟṟōraip parāmarittāḷ.*
>
> She looked after her elderly parents.

Past (I …): பராமரித்தேன், *parāmarittēṉ*.

Of people, houses and machines: வீட்டைப் பராமரி, to maintain a house.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

The origin of **பராமரி** (*parāmari*) is not settled. It looks like a Sanskrit loan made into a Tamil verb in -இ, perhaps from *parāmarśa*, "seizing; consideration" (Monier-Williams), but the link is uncertain.
