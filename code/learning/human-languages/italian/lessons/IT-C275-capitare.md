## You'll want to know: capitare
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C275-ACCADERE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**capitare** — "to happen to someone; to turn up".

> Mi è capitato di incontrarlo in treno.
>
> *I happened to meet him on the train.*

*Capita!* — these things happen. Takes *essere*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*capitare* is inherited from Vulgar Latin *capitāre* "to come to a head, to arrive", built on Latin *caput* "head" (EtymDB).
