French, chapter 384: Des papiers en règle.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
expirer — to expire; to breathe out.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for the admission, then the French for to exclude.

You'll want to know: expirer.
expirer — "to expire; to breathe out".
Mon passeport expire au mois de mai. My passport expires in May.
Of breathing: inspirer, then expirer.

The word, taken apart.
expirer is a learned borrowing from Latin exspīrāre "to breathe out, to die away" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short), built on spīrāre "to breathe".

Lesson 2 of 5.

You'll want to know: valable.
valable — "acceptable, valid; worthwhile".
Ce billet est valable une semaine. This ticket is valid for a week.
Une excuse valable — a good excuse. One form for both genders.

The word, taken apart.
valable is built from valoir "to be worth" and -able (EtymDB). valoir is inherited from Latin valēre "to be strong, to be worth" (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 4 of 5.
renouveler — to renew.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for acceptable, then the French for valid.

You'll want to know: renouveler.
renouveler — "to renew".
Je dois renouveler ma carte d'identité. I have to renew my identity card.
Je renouvelle, with ll before a silent ending.

The word, taken apart.
renouveler is built from re- and Old French noveler "to make new", from Latin novellus "young, new", a small form of novus "new" (Lewis & Short).
