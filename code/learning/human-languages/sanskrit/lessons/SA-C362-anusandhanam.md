## You'll want to know: अनुसन्धानम्
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C362-ANUSANDHANA362-01]; assesses=[] -->

**अनुसन्धानम्** — *anusandhānam* — "research; investigation".

> वैज्ञानिकः रोगस्य अनुसन्धानं करोति।
>
> *vaijñānikaḥ rogasya anusandhānaṃ karoti.*
>
> The scientist is doing research on the disease.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अनुसन्धानम्** (*anusandhānam*) is built on *anu*, *sam* and the root √dhā, "to put"; MW gives *anu-saṃdhāna* "investigation, inquiry, searching into, close inspection" (MW).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
