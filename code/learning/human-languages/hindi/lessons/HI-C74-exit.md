## You'll want to know: निकास
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C74-SIGNS-04]; assesses=[HI-LEX-C49-LEAVE-04] -->

**निकास** (*nikās*) — "exit".

You have most of this word already. **निकलना**, "to set out, to go out", has been
yours since the chapter on leaving; निकास is what that doing word turns into when
you need a noun. Going out, named.

Watch the vowel change as the noun is made: निक**ल**ना has a short *a*, निक**ा**स
takes the long ा. Hindi lengthens a vowel to build a name out of an action often
enough that you should expect it rather than be surprised.

निकास is painted opposite प्रवेश over every second doorway in a station, and the
two are read together or not at all.
