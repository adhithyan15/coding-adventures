# Seven forms in six small phrases

## Guided Practice: keep each form beside its noun
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-FORM-MERI-185, PA-FORM-MERE-185, PA-FORM-TUHADI-185, PA-FORM-APNI-185, PA-FORM-APNE-185, PA-FORM-SADA-185, PA-FORM-SADE-185, PA-LEX-C71-HOME71-02, PA-LEX-DOST, PA-TRAVEL-JOURNEY-01, PA-LEX-C67-FOOD67-03] -->

Read slowly:

- **ਮੇਰੀ ਕਿਤਾਬ** — my book
- **ਮੇਰੇ ਦੋਸਤ** — my friends
- **ਤੁਹਾਡੀ ਯਾਤਰਾ** — your journey
- **ਆਪਣੀ ਕਿਤਾਬ** — one's own book
- **ਆਪਣੇ ਦੋਸਤ** — one's own friends
- **ਸਾਡਾ ਘਰ, ਸਾਡੇ ਘਰ** — our home, our homes

[YOU HEAR: the six phrases in mixed order; YOU SAY: each matching owner and noun]
