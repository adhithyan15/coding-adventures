## You'll want to know: der Untertitel
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C524-FILM-04]; assesses=[] -->

**der Untertitel** — "subtitle".

> Ich schaue Filme lieber mit Untertiteln.
>
> *I prefer watching films with subtitles.*

Plural: *die Untertitel*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Untertitel* is built on *unter* "under" and *Titel* "title", which goes back to Latin *titulus* "inscription, label" (Lewis & Short).
