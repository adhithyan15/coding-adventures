Spanish, chapter 757: Campeones y medallas.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
el campeón — the champion.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for the referee, then the Spanish for to draw.

You'll want to know: el campeón.
el campeón — "the champion".
Somos los campeones de la liga. We are the league champions.
La campeona for a woman.

The word, taken apart.
campeón was borrowed from Italian campione (EtymDB), from Late Latin campiō "a fighter in the field", built on campus "field".

The word, taken apart.
torneo is built on tornear, which is usually traced to Old French tornoier "to joust", from Latin tornāre "to turn": knights wheeled their horses.
