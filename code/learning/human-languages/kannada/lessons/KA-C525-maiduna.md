# ಮೈದುನ (maiduna) — husband's younger brother

## You'll want to know: ಮೈದುನ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C525-NENTARU525-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ಮೈದುನ** — *maiduna* — "husband's younger brother".

> ನನ್ನ ಮೈದುನ ಎಂಜಿನಿಯರ್ ಆಗಿ ಕೆಲಸ ಮಾಡುತ್ತಾನೆ.
>
> *nanna maiduna eñjiniyar āgi kelasa māḍuttāne.*
>
> My husband's younger brother works as an engineer.

Also a wife's brother in some families.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಮೈದುನ** (*maiduna*) is a Kannada kinship word for a younger brother-in-law; its origin is not settled.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
