Telugu, chapter 254: State, Area, Direction, East, West.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
రాష్ట్రం (rāṣṭraṁ) — a state.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for in the past, then the Telugu for dawn.

You'll want to know: రాష్ట్రం.
రాష్ట్రం — rāṣṭraṁ — "a state".

The word, taken apart.
రాష్ట్రం (rāṣṭraṁ) is Sanskrit rāṣṭra, "kingdom, country", taken over whole as a learned loan, a tatsama, with the Telugu noun ending -ṁ (CDIAL 10721).

ప్రాంతం (prāntaṁ) is Sanskrit prānta, "tip, point; end, edge", taken over whole as a learned loan, a tatsama, with the Telugu noun ending -ṁ (CDIAL 8940). The sense "region" is the one Telugu gives it.

Lesson 5 of 5.
పడమర (paḍamara) — west.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a direction, then the Telugu for east.

You'll want to know: పడమర.
పడమర — paḍamara — "west".

The word, taken apart.
పడమర (paḍamara) is native Dravidian. DEDR 3852 lists paḍamara, "the west", under paḍu, "to fall, lie", which Kannada uses for the setting sun.
