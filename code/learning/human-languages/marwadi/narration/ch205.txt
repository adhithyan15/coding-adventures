Lesson 2 of 5.
चूकणो (chūkṇo) — to miss (a bus); to slip up.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for a map, then the Marwadi for a ticket.

You'll want to know: चूकणो.
चूकणो — chūkṇo — "to miss (a bus); to slip up".
हूं रोज बस चूकूं हूं। hū̃ roj bas chūkū̃ hū̃. I miss the bus every day.
Past (I …): हूं चूक्यो, hū̃ chūkyo.

The word, taken apart.
चूकणो (chūkṇo) is inherited from a reconstructed cukk, "fall short of, stop"; CDIAL lists Marwari cūkṇo, "to be completed", and Gujarati cukvũ, "to fail, miss" (CDIAL 4848). चुकावणो, "to pay", is its causative.

Lesson 3 of 5.
भाड़ो (bhāṛo) — a fare; hire.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for a ticket, then the Marwadi for to miss.

You'll want to know: भाड़ो.
भाड़ो — bhāṛo — "a fare; hire".
भाड़ो कितणो है? bhāṛo kitṇo hai? How much is the fare?
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
भाड़ो (bhāṛo) is inherited from a reconstructed bhārta, "pertaining to servants", by way of Middle Indo-Aryan bhāṭa, "wages". CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry, but lists Sindhi bhāṛo, "hire", and Hindi bhāṛā, "hire, fare, rent" (CDIAL 9468).

Lesson 4 of 5.
अटकणो (aṭakṇo) — to get stuck.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for to miss, then the Marwadi for a fare.

You'll want to know: अटकणो.
अटकणो — aṭakṇo — "to get stuck".
बस भीड़ मांय अटकै है। bas bhīṛ mā̃y aṭkai hai. The bus gets stuck in the crowd.
Past (I …): हूं अटक्यो, hū̃ aṭakyo.

The word, taken apart.
अटकणो (aṭakṇo) is inherited from a reconstructed aṭṭakk, "obstruct, stop"; CDIAL lists Marwari aṭakṇo, "to remain", and Hindi aṭaknā, "to stick" (CDIAL 182).
