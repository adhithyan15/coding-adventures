# (reading) — six words, nothing said first

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[UR-LEX-PANI] -->

[PAUSE 2s] You have written every letter in every one of these. Not one word is
new, and not one shape inside them is new either.

[PAUSE 1s] What is new is that nobody says them before you read them.

## Reading
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-SKILL-READ-WORDS]; assesses=[UR-LEX-KAMRA, UR-LEX-SHAHR] -->

> پانی
> کام
> شہر
> کمرہ
> ماں
> لاہور

[PAUSE 3s] Read down the list once. Do not take each word apart — look at the
whole shape and let it arrive.

[PAUSE 3s] Again, and notice which came at once and which you had to unpick.
Both are fine. The difference is what practice removes.

## You'll want to know
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[UR-LEX-KAAM, UR-LEX-MAAN, UR-LEX-LAHORE] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Urdu joins its letters, so a word is not five shapes standing in a
row. It is one shape.

[PAUSE 2s] That is why reading a word cold is a different skill from writing
it. Writing, you make the joins one at a time and know what is coming. Reading,
the joins are already made and the whole word has to land at once.

[PAUSE 2s] Six is enough for one sitting.
