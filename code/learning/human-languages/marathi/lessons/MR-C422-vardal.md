## You'll want to know: वर्दळ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C422-MANDAI422-05]; assesses=[] -->

**वर्दळ** — *vardaḷ* — "bustle; coming and going".

> स्टेशनबाहेर दिवसभर वर्दळ असते.
>
> *sṭeśanbāher divasbhar vardaḷ aste.*
>
> Outside the station people come and go all day.

Feminine.

A feminine noun: वर्दळ कमी झाली, the bustle died down.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**वर्दळ** (*vardaḷ*) is a Marathi word for steady coming and going; its origin is not settled.
