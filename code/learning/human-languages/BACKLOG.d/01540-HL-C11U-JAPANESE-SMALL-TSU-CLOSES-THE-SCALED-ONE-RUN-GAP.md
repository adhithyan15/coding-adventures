## HL-C11U — Japanese small tsu closes the scaled one-run gap

After Urdu retroflex **ٹ** landed, Japanese small **っ** led the measured queue
at **4 affected realizations**. Unicode 17 identifies U+3063 as HIRAGANA LETTER
SMALL TU, alias small tsu, while Sirgazil's 24-frame CC0 animation demonstrates
the full-size **つ** movement as one uninterrupted pen-down run.

The Japanese inventory now records the sokuon's one mora of closure before a
doubled following consonant and fits that source-backed movement to the bundled
small-tsu outline. The record explicitly says the animation demonstrates **つ**,
not an independently animated **っ**, so the size adaptation remains visible.
This removes all 4 affected realizations for **っ** and reranks Arabic **ظ**
first at **4**.
