## You'll want to know: violar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C382-PROIBIDO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**violar** — "to break, to violate (a rule, a law)".

> O motorista violou as regras de trânsito.
>
> *The driver broke the traffic rules.*

Also, in a grave sense, to rape; for rules many speakers prefer *infringir* or *desrespeitar*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*violar* comes from Latin *violāre* "to treat with violence, to violate" (EtymDB, Lewis & Short), through Old Portuguese *violar*; it is built on *vīs* "strength, force".
