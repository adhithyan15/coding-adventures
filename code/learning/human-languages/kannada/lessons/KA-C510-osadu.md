# ಒಸಡು (osaḍu) — the gums

## You'll want to know: ಒಸಡು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C510-NOVU510-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ಒಸಡು** — *osaḍu* — "the gums".

> ಒಸಡಿನಿಂದ ರಕ್ತ ಬರುತ್ತಿದೆ ಅಂತ ಅವನು ವೈದ್ಯರಿಗೆ ಹೇಳಿದನು.
>
> *osaḍininda rakta baruttide anta avanu vaidyarige hēḷidanu.*
>
> He told the doctor that his gums were bleeding.

Plural: ಒಸಡುಗಳು, *osaḍugaḷu*.

Often in the plural, ಒಸಡುಗಳು.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಒಸಡು** (*osaḍu*) is a native Kannada word for the gums; its origin is not settled.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
