## You'll want to know: der Kratzer
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C405-DELLE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**der Kratzer** — "the scratch".

> Auf der Tür ist ein langer Kratzer.
>
> *There is a long scratch on the door.*

Same form in the plural.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Kratzer* is built on the verb *kratzen* 'to scratch' (EtymDB), which is inherited from Old High German *krazzōn*.
