# Return to four familiar verbs

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-LEX-JANA, PA-LEX-KHANA, PA-LEX-C56-FAMILY56-02, PA-FORM-SAUNDA-183] -->

[YOU HEAR: *jāṇā; khāṇā; pīṇā*] Say **ਜਾਣਾ · ਖਾਣਾ · ਪੀਣਾ**.

## Grammar Lens
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-FORM-SAUNDA-183, PA-FORM-KARDA-183, PA-GRAMMAR-PRESENT-HABITUAL-GENDER-05] -->

You already say **ਕਰਦਾ**, "does", and **ਸੌਂਦਾ**, "sleeps". Listen to the two
middles: *kar-dā* runs straight into its ending, but *saun-dā* carries a nasal
hum before it. The next lesson gives that hum to three verbs you use every day.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-LEX-JANA, PA-LEX-KHANA, PA-LEX-C56-FAMILY56-02, PA-FORM-SAUNDA-183] -->

- [YOU HEAR: *to go*] Say **ਜਾਣਾ**.
- [YOU HEAR: *to eat*] Say **ਖਾਣਾ**.
- [YOU HEAR: *to drink*] Say **ਪੀਣਾ**.
- [YOU HEAR: *sleeps, of a man*] Say **ਸੌਂਦਾ**.

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-LEX-JANA, PA-LEX-KHANA, PA-LEX-C56-FAMILY56-02, PA-FORM-SAUNDA-183] -->

[PAUSE 3s] Say the four once more, slowly. Nothing new is needed yet.
