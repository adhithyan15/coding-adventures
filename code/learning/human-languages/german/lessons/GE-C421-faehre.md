## You'll want to know: die Fähre
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C421-FAEHRE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**die Fähre** — "the ferry".

> Die Fähre fährt jede Stunde zur Insel.
>
> *The ferry goes to the island every hour.*

Plural: *die Fähren*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Fähre* is built on the inherited verb *fahren* 'to travel' (EtymDB lists *Fähre*); English *ferry* comes from the same Germanic family.
