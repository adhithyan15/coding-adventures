## You'll want to know: దూత
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C362-SANDHI362-05]; assesses=[] -->

**దూత** — *dūta* — "an envoy; a messenger".

> రాజు శత్రువు దగ్గరకు దూతను పంపాడు.
>
> *rāju śatruvu daggaraku dūtanu pampāḍu.*
>
> The king sent an envoy to the enemy.

Plural: దూతలు, *dūtalu*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**దూత** (*dūta*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *dūta*, "a messenger" (CDIAL 6488).
