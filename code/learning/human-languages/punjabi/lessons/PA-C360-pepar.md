## You'll want to know: ਪੇਪਰ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C360-EXAM360-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ਪੇਪਰ** — *pepar* — "an exam paper".

> ਅੱਜ ਦਾ ਪੇਪਰ ਔਖਾ ਸੀ।
>
> *ajj dā pepar aukhā sī.*
>
> Today's paper was hard.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਪੇਪਰ** (*pepar*) is borrowed from English *paper*.
