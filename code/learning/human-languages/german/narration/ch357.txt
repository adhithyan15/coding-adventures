German, chapter 357: Die Gewerkschaft.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
die Gewerkschaft — the trade union.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to cope with, then the German for to fail.

You'll want to know: die Gewerkschaft.
die Gewerkschaft — "the trade union".
Die Gewerkschaft fordert mehr Lohn für die Arbeiter. The union is demanding higher pay for the workers.
Plural: die Gewerkschaften. When talks fail, a union may call a strike: Die Gewerkschaft ruft zum Streik auf.

The word, taken apart.
Gewerkschaft joins Gewerk "trade, craft", from Werk "work", Old High German werc, Proto-Germanic werką (EtymDB), to the ending -schaft. It is usually said to have named a mining company before it named a workers' union.

Lesson 2 of 5.
der Betriebsrat — the works council.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to fail, then the German for the trade union.

You'll want to know: der Betriebsrat.
der Betriebsrat — "the works council".
Der Betriebsrat spricht mit dem Chef über die Arbeitszeiten. The works council talks to the boss about working hours.
Plural: die Betriebsräte. Also a member of it: Sie ist Betriebsrätin.

The word, taken apart.
Betriebsrat joins Betrieb "firm", from betreiben "to run", to Rat "council, advice", inherited from Old High German rāt and Proto-Germanic rēdaz (EtymDB).

Lesson 3 of 5.
die Forderung — the demand.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for the trade union, then the German for the works council.

You'll want to know: die Forderung.
die Forderung — "the demand".
Die Forderungen der Arbeiter sind klar: mehr Geld und weniger Stunden. The workers' demands are clear: more money and fewer hours.
Plural: die Forderungen. From fordern — to demand. A sum someone owes you is also eine Forderung.

The word, taken apart.
Forderung is the noun of fordern "to demand". EtymDB has no history for the verb; it is usually traced to Old High German fordarōn and the family of vorder "front": you call for a thing to come forward.

Lesson 4 of 5.
unterstützen — to support.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for the works council, then the German for the demand.

You'll want to know: unterstützen.
unterstützen — "to support".
Meine Eltern unterstützen mich beim Studium. My parents support me during my studies.
Inseparable: sie unterstützt, sie hat unterstützt.

The word, taken apart.
unterstützen is inherited from Middle High German understüzzen and Old High German untarstuzzen (EtymDB), from unter "under" and stützen "to prop up": you put a prop under someone.

Lesson 5 of 5.
die Unterstützung — the support.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for the demand, then the German for to support.

You'll want to know: die Unterstützung.
die Unterstützung — "the support".
Ohne die Unterstützung meiner Freunde hätte ich das nicht geschafft. Without my friends' support I would not have managed it.
No plural in everyday use. Mit Unterstützung von — with the support of.

The word, taken apart.
Unterstützung is the noun of unterstützen, which is inherited from Old High German untarstuzzen (EtymDB), from unter "under" and stützen "to prop up".
