# చీలిపోవు (cīlipōvu) — to split; to split apart

## You'll want to know: చీలిపోవు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C490-CIRUGU490-04]; assesses=[] -->

**చీలిపోవు** — *cīlipōvu* — "to split; to split apart".

> భూకంపంలో గోడ రెండుగా చీలిపోయింది.
>
> *bhūkampaṁlō gōḍa reṇḍugā cīlipōyindi.*
>
> In the earthquake the wall split in two.

Past (it …): చీలిపోయింది, *cīlipōyindi*.

Said of wood, of walls and of a group that breaks in two. Its subject is a thing, so the past shown is the third person: చీలిపోయింది, it split.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**చీలిపోవు** (*cīlipōvu*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 1622 lists Telugu *cīlu*, "to break, to split, to crack", here with పోవు, "to go" (DEDR 4572).
