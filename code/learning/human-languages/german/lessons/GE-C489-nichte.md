## You'll want to know: die Nichte
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C489-VERWANDTE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**die Nichte** — "niece".

> Meine Nichte hat mir ein Bild gemalt.
>
> *My niece painted me a picture.*

Plural: *die Nichten*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Nichte* was borrowed from Middle Low German *nichte* (EtymDB), the northern form of an old Germanic word for a niece.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.
