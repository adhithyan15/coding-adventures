## You'll want to know: दम्पती
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C501-SAMBANDHA501-04]; assesses=[] -->

**दम्पती** — *dampatī* — "husband and wife".

> दम्पती प्रतिदिनं सह खादतः।
>
> *dampatī pratidinaṃ saha khādataḥ.*
>
> The couple eat together every day.

A masculine noun, used in the dual.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**दम्पती** (*dampatī*) is built from *dam*, "house", and **पतिः**, "master"; MW gives the dual "the two masters, husband and wife" (MW).
