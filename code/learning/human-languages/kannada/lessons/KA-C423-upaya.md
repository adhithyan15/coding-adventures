## You'll want to know: ಉಪಾಯ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C423-HANTA423-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ಉಪಾಯ** — *upāya* — "a trick; a way out".

> ಮಳೆಯಿಂದ ತಪ್ಪಿಸಿಕೊಳ್ಳಲು ಅವನಿಗೆ ಒಂದು ಉಪಾಯ ಹೊಳೆಯಿತು.
>
> *maḷeyinda tappisikoḷḷalu avanige ondu upāya hoḷeyitu.*
>
> A way to escape the rain came to his mind.

ಉಪಾಯ ಹೊಳೆ, for an idea to strike.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಉಪಾಯ** (*upāya*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *upāya*, "coming near; a means, a way" (CDIAL 2308).
