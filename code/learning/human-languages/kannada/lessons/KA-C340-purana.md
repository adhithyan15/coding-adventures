# ಪುರಾಣ (purāṇa) — a myth; an old legend

## You'll want to know: ಪುರಾಣ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C340-DANTAKATHE340-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ಪುರಾಣ** — *purāṇa* — "a myth; an old legend".

> ಅಜ್ಜಿ ಮಲಗುವ ಮುಂಚೆ ಒಂದು ಪುರಾಣ ಕಥೆ ಹೇಳಿದರು.
>
> *ajji malaguva muñce ondu purāṇa kathe hēḷidaru.*
>
> Before bed Grandmother told an old legend.

Plural: ಪುರಾಣಗಳು, *purāṇagaḷu*.

The old Sanskrit story collections are the ಪುರಾಣಗಳು. In talk, ಪುರಾಣ can also be a long, boring tale.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಪುರಾಣ** (*purāṇa*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *purāṇa*, "ancient, belonging to olden times" (CDIAL 8283), then the name of collections of old tales.
