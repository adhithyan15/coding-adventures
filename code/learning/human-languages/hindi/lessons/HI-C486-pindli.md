## You'll want to know: पिंडली
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C486-DUKHNA486-02]; assesses=[] -->

**पिंडली** — *piṇḍlī* — "the calf of the leg".

> दौड़ने के बाद अरुण की पिंडली में दर्द है।
>
> *dauṛne ke bād Aruṇ kī piṇḍlī meṁ dard hai.*
>
> Arun's calf aches after running.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पिंडली** (*piṇḍlī*) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Hindi *piṇḍlī*, "calf of leg, shin", beside *piṇḍā*, "lump", under Sanskrit *píṇḍa* (CDIAL 8168): the rounded lump of muscle at the back of the leg.
