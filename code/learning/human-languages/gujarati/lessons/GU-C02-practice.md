## The word, taken apart — the atoms
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-CONCEPT-C02-ANAND-01, GU-CONCEPT-C02-CHHE-01, GU-CONCEPT-C02-MAARUN-01, GU-CONCEPT-C02-MAARUNNAAMCHHE-01, GU-CONCEPT-C02-NAAM-01, GU-CONCEPT-C02-PRACTICE-01, GU-CONCEPT-C02-SHUN-01, GU-CONCEPT-C02-TAMARUNNAAMSHUNCHHE-01, GU-CONCEPT-C02-TUTAME-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s]
- [YOU SAY: "name," "my," "is" (*nām*, *mārũ*, *chhe*)]
- [YOU SAY: the two "you" levels (*tũ* / *tame*)]
- [YOU SAY: "what," and where the verb sits (*shũ*; the verb is last)]

## What you've built — roots you now carry
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-CONCEPT-C02-ANAND-01, GU-CONCEPT-C02-CHHE-01, GU-CONCEPT-C02-MAARUN-01, GU-CONCEPT-C02-MAARUNNAAMCHHE-01, GU-CONCEPT-C02-NAAM-01, GU-CONCEPT-C02-PRACTICE-01, GU-CONCEPT-C02-SHUN-01, GU-CONCEPT-C02-TAMARUNNAAMSHUNCHHE-01, GU-CONCEPT-C02-TUTAME-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s]
- *nām* ← Sanskrit *nāman* → English **name**
- *mārũ* ← *ma-* → English **my**; agrees in three genders (*māro/mārī/mārũ*)
- *chhe* — Gujarati's own copula (not Hindi's *hai*)
- *shũ* ← *ka-* → English *what*; *tũ* ← *\*tū* → *thou*
