## You'll want to know: శిక్షణ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C526-NIRVAHANA526-05]; assesses=[] -->

**శిక్షణ** — *śikṣaṇa* — "training".

> కొత్త ఉద్యోగులకు ఒక నెల శిక్షణ ఇచ్చారు.
>
> *kotta udyōgulaku oka nela śikṣaṇa iccāru.*
>
> The new employees were given a month of training.

శిక్షణ ఇచ్చు, to train someone; శిక్షణ పొందు, to be trained. Not శిక్ష, a punishment.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**శిక్షణ** (*śikṣaṇa*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *śikṣaṇa*, "the act of learning; teaching" (CDIAL 12429).
