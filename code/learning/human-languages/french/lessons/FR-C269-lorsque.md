## You'll want to know: lorsque
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C269-QUAND-01]; assesses=[] -->

**lorsque** — "when (careful style)".

> Lorsque je suis arrivé, la fête avait déjà commencé.
>
> *When I arrived, the party had already started.*

It means *quand* in a time clause, a little more written. Before a vowel it becomes *lorsqu'*: *lorsqu'il pleut*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*lorsque* joins *lors* "then" and *que* "that". *Lors* is traced to Latin *illā hōrā* "at that hour" (EtymDB), from *hōra* "hour".
