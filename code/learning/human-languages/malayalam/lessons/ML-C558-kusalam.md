# കുശലം (kuśalaṁ) — pleasantries; small talk

## You'll want to know: കുശലം
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C558-VARDHAKYAM558-01]; assesses=[] -->

**കുശലം** — *kuśalaṁ* — "pleasantries; small talk".

> വഴിയിൽ കണ്ട അയൽക്കാരനോട് അച്ഛൻ കുശലം ചോദിച്ചു.
>
> *vaḻiyil kaṇṭa ayalkkāranōṭŭ acchan kuśalaṁ cōdiccu.*
>
> Father asked after the neighbour he met on the road.

കുശലം ചോദിക്കുക, to ask after someone, is the set phrase.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**കുശലം** (*kuśalaṁ*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *kuśala*, "right, proper, healthy" (CDIAL 3365): to ask the കുശലം is to ask whether all is well.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
