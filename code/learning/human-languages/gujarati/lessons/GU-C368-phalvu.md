## You'll want to know: ફળવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C368-NASIB368-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ફળવું** — *phaḷvũ* — "to bear fruit".

> જો તમે સતત મહેનત કરશો તો તે ફળશે.
>
> *jo tame satat mahenat karśo to te phaḷśe.*
>
> If you keep working hard, it will pay off.

Past (it …): તે કેરીનો છોડ ફળ્યો, *te kerīno choḍ phaḷyo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ફળવું** (*phaḷvũ*) is inherited from Sanskrit *phálati*, "bears fruit"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb (CDIAL 9057).
