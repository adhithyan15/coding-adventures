## You'll want to know: chàbuduō
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C205-FAR205-04]; assesses=[] -->

*chàbuduō* — "about; almost".

> *cóng zhèr dào chēzhàn chàbuduō liǎng gōnglǐ* — From here to the station is almost two kilometres.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

不 loses its tone in the middle of this word: chàbuduō.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*chàbuduō* is built from *chà* 'short of', 不 'not' and *duō* 'much', all inherited from Middle Chinese, *tsrhae*, *pjut* and *ta* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription): not much short.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for asking where things are.
