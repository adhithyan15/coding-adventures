## You'll want to know: ವಿಧಿಸು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C372-VANAVASA372-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ವಿಧಿಸು** — *vidhisu* — "to impose (a penalty)".

> ನ್ಯಾಯಾಧೀಶರು ಕಳ್ಳನಿಗೆ ದಂಡ ವಿಧಿಸಿದರು.
>
> *nyāyādhīśaru kaḷḷanige daṇḍa vidhisidaru.*
>
> The judge imposed a fine on the thief.

Past (I …): ವಿಧಿಸಿದೆ, *vidhiside*.

Punishments, fines, taxes and rules are imposed; the person takes -ಗೆ.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ವಿಧಿಸು** (*vidhisu*) is built on Sanskrit *vidhi*, "a rule, an ordinance", and "fate" (CDIAL 11755), with the Kannada verb ending -*isu*.
