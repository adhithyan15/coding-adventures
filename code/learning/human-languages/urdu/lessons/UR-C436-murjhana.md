## You'll want to know: مرجھانا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C436-QUDRAT436-05]; assesses=[] -->

**مرجھانا** — *murjhānā* — "to wilt; to fade".

> گرمی میں پودے مرجھا گئے۔
>
> *garmī meṅ paude murjhā gae.*
>
> The plants wilted in the heat.

Past (it …): وہ پھول مرجھا گیا, *voh phūl murjhā gayā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**مرجھانا** (*murjhānā*) is an everyday word whose source the dictionaries we use do not give: CDIAL has no entry for it. No dictionary we use records an older form, so its history is not settled.
