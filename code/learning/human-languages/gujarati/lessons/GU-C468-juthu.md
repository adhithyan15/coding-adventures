## You'll want to know: જૂઠું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C468-JUTHU468-01]; assesses=[] -->

**જૂઠું** — *jūṭhũ* — "untruthful; lying".

> જૂઠું બોલવું સારું નથી.
>
> *jūṭhũ bolvũ sārũ nathī.*
>
> Telling lies is not good.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**જૂઠું** (*jūṭhũ*) goes back to a reconstructed *\*jhūṭṭha-*, "false"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati word, "false" (CDIAL 5407).
