## You'll want to know: trasgredire
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C386-VIOLAZIONE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**trasgredire** — "to break (a rule), to transgress".

> Da ragazzo mi piaceva trasgredire le regole.
>
> *As a boy I liked breaking the rules.*

Conjugated with *-isc-*: *trasgredisco*. *Trasgressivo* — provocative, rule-breaking.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*trasgredire* is a learned borrowing from Latin *trānsgredī* "to step across" (Lewis & Short), built from *trāns* "across" and *gradī* "to step": you step over the line.
