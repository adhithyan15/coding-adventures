## You'll want to know: విరామం
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C440-ALASYAM440-04]; assesses=[] -->

**విరామం** — *virāmaṁ* — "an interval; a break".

> రెండు గంటల ప్రయాణం తరువాత పది నిమిషాల విరామం ఉంది.
>
> *reṇḍu gaṇṭala prayāṇaṁ taruvāta padi nimiṣāla virāmaṁ undi.*
>
> After two hours on the road there is a ten-minute break.

A film has an interval, విరామం, too. In Sanskrit grammar *virāma* is also the name of the sign ్, which takes away a consonant's vowel.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**విరామం** (*virāmaṁ*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *virāma*, "cessation, a stop, a pause" (Monier-Williams), from *vi-ram*, "to stop".
