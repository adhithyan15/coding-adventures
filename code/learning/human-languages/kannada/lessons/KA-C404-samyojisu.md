# ಸಂಯೋಜಿಸು (saṃyōjisu) — to coordinate; to combine

## You'll want to know: ಸಂಯೋಜಿಸು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C404-SADASYA404-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸಂಯೋಜಿಸು** — *saṃyōjisu* — "to coordinate; to combine".

> ಅವಳು ಶಾಲೆಯ ಕಾರ್ಯಕ್ರಮವನ್ನು ಸಂಯೋಜಿಸಿದಳು.
>
> *avaḷu śāleya kāryakramavannu saṃyōjisidaḷu.*
>
> She coordinated the school event.

Past (I …): ಸಂಯೋಜಿಸಿದೆ, *saṃyōjiside*.

ಸಂಯೋಜಕ, a coordinator.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸಂಯೋಜಿಸು** (*saṃyōjisu*) is built on Sanskrit *saṃyojana*, "the act of joining or uniting" (Monier-Williams), with the Kannada verb ending -*isu*.
