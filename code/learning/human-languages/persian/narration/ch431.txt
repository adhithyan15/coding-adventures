Persian, chapter 431: Flying.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
پرواز (parvâz) — a flight.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to be late, then the Persian for a loudspeaker.

You'll want to know: پرواز.
پرواز (parvâz) — "a flight".
پرواز (parvâz) ما ساعت شش صبح است. Parvâz-e mâ sâ'at-e shesh-e sobh ast. Our flight is at six in the morning.
Parvâz-e mostaqim — a direct flight.

The word, taken apart.
parvâz is a native Persian word, which EtymDB lists with the sense 'flight'. It is usually linked with par 'feather, wing', though EtymDB gives no older form.

پرواز کردن (parvâz kardan) — "to fly".
هواپیما بالای ابرها پرواز میکند. Havâpeymâ bâlâ-ye abr-hâ parvâz mikonad. The plane flies above the clouds.
parvâz + kardan. Birds and planes both parvâz mikonand.

The word, taken apart.
parvâz kardan is built from native parvâz 'flight', which EtymDB lists, and kardan 'to do', inherited from Middle Persian kardan (EtymDB).

Lesson 3 of 5.
فرود آمدن (forud âmadan) — to land (of a plane or a bird).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a flight, then the Persian for to fly.

You'll want to know: فرود آمدن.
فرود آمدن (forud âmadan) — "to land (of a plane or a bird)".
هواپیما در باران فرود آمد. Havâpeymâ dar bârân forud âmad. The plane landed in the rain.
forud 'down' + âmadan 'to come'. Present: forud miâyad. Taking off is boland shodan.

The word, taken apart.
forud âmadan is built from native forud 'down, below' and âmadan 'to come', which EtymDB traces to the PIE root gʷem- 'to step'.

Lesson 4 of 5.
هواپیمایی (havâpeymâyi) — an airline; aviation.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to fly, then the Persian for to land.

You'll want to know: هواپیمایی.
هواپیمایی (havâpeymâyi) — "an airline; aviation".
برادرم در یک شرکت هواپیمایی کار میکند. Barâdaram dar yek sherkat-e havâpeymâyi kâr mikonad. My brother works for an airline.
Built on havâpeymâ 'aeroplane' + -i. An airline company is sherkat-e havâpeymâyi.

The word, taken apart.
havâpeymâyi is built on havâpeymâ 'aeroplane', from havâ 'air', borrowed from Arabic hawāʾ (EtymDB), and native peymâ 'measuring, crossing', with the ending -i.

The word, taken apart.
kârt-e parvâz is built from kârt 'card', which EtymDB takes from French carte, and native parvâz 'flight'.
