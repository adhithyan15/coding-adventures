# ఉత్సాహపరచు (utsāhaparacu) — to cheer on; to enthuse

## You'll want to know: ఉత్సాహపరచు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C501-NAVVU501-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ఉత్సాహపరచు** — *utsāhaparacu* — "to cheer on; to enthuse".

> అమ్మ మాటలు నన్ను ఉత్సాహపరిచాయి.
>
> *amma māṭalu nannu utsāhaparicāyi.*
>
> Mum's words cheered me on.

Past (I …): ఉత్సాహపరిచాను, *utsāhaparicānu*.

ఉత్సాహం, enthusiasm, + -పరచు, to make someone feel it.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ఉత్సాహపరచు** (*utsāhaparacu*) is built from ఉత్సాహం, from Sanskrit *utsāha*, "power, firmness; joy" (CDIAL 1882), and *paracu*, which DEDR 3853 lists as "to cause to feel".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
