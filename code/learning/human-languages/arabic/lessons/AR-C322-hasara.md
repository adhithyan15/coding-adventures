## You'll want to know: حاصر
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C322-QALA322-04]; assesses=[] -->

**حاصر** — *ḥāṣara* — "to besiege, to surround".

> حاصر الجيش القلعة شهرا.
>
> *ḥāṣara al-jaysh al-qalʿa shahran.*
>
> The army besieged the castle for a month.

Present: يحاصر, *yuḥāṣir*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**حاصر** (*ḥāṣara*) is built on the native Arabic root *ḥ-ṣ-r* (Lane), "to confine, to hem in". It is Form III, "to hem in an enemy".
