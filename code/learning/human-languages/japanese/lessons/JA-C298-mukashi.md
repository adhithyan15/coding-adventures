## You'll want to know: むかし
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C298-MUKASHI298-01]; assesses=[] -->

**むかし** — *mukashi* — "long ago; the old days".

> **むかし、やまに おじいさんが すんでいました。** — *mukashi, yama ni ojiisan ga sunde imashita* — "Long ago an old man lived in the mountains."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**むかし** (*mukashi*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict).
