## You'll want to know: محو الأمية
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C398-TALIM398-04]; assesses=[] -->

**محو الأمية** — *maḥw al-ummiyya* — "literacy".

> تعمل الجمعية على محو الأمية في القرى.
>
> *taʿmalu al-jamʿiyya ʿalā maḥw al-ummiyya fī al-qurā.*
>
> The association works for literacy in the villages.

Literally the wiping out of illiteracy; أمي, *ummī*, is one who cannot read.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**محو الأمية** (*maḥw al-ummiyya*) joins *maḥw*, "wiping out", built on the native Arabic root *m-ḥ-w*, and *ummiyya*, "illiteracy", from *ummī*, built on *ʾ-m-m* (Lane): one still as his mother bore him, unable to read.
