## You'll want to know: jiěshì
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C268-EXAM268-05]; assesses=[] -->

*jiěshì* — "to explain".

> *zhège dá'àn nǐ néng jiěshì ma* — Can you explain this answer?

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*jiěshì* is built from *jiě* and *shì*, both inherited from Middle Chinese, *keaX* and *syek* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to explain' and 'to untie', and the second 'to let go' and 'to untie'.
