[PAUSE 3s] Why does Gujarati *be* start with a **b** where Sanskrit had *dve*, and what happened to the **r** of *traṇ*? (The **dv-** cluster collapsed to **bb-** and then **b-**; the **r** was restored later by learned borrowing, not inherited.)
