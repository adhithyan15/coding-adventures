Marwadi (Marwari), chapter 243: A Thief at the Fair.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
चोरी (chorī) — a theft.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for a potsherd, then the Marwadi for to sink.

You'll want to know: चोरी.
चोरी — chorī — "a theft".
चोरी माड़ी बात है। chorī māṛī bāt hai. Stealing is a bad thing.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
चोरी (chorī) is inherited from Sanskrit caurikā, "theft"; CDIAL lists Marwari corī under it (CDIAL 4937).

Lesson 3 of 5.
खोसणो (khosṇo) — to snatch away.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for a theft, then the Marwadi for to rob.

You'll want to know: खोसणो.
खोसणो — khosṇo — "to snatch away".
म्हैं थारी कलम कोनी खोसी। mhaĩ thārī kalam konī khosī. I did not snatch your pen.
Past (I …): म्हैं टोपी खोसी, mhaĩ ṭopī khosī.

The word, taken apart.
खोसणो (khosṇo) is inherited from a reconstructed skōṣati, "plucks out". CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry; it lists Hindi khosnā, "to pluck out". The Marwari sense, "to snatch", is probably a later turn of that meaning (CDIAL 13661).

Lesson 4 of 5.
गमणो (gamṇo) — to go missing; to get lost.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for to rob, then the Marwadi for to snatch away.

You'll want to know: गमणो.
गमणो — gamṇo — "to go missing; to get lost".
म्हारी अंगूठी गमी। mhārī aṅgūṭhī gamī. My ring went missing.
Past (it …): वो रुपियो गम्यो, vo rupiyo gamyo.

The word, taken apart.
गमणो (gamṇo) is inherited from Sanskrit gamyate, "one goes". CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry; it lists Hindi gamnā, "to be lost" (CDIAL 4033). The taught गमावणो (gamāvṇo), "to lose", is its causative partner.
