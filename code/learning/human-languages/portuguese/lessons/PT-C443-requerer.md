## You'll want to know: requerer
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C443-SERVICOS-05]; assesses=[] -->

**requerer** — "to apply for; to require".

> Requeri um cartão novo na câmara municipal.
>
> *I applied for a new card at the town hall.*

*O requerimento* — the application form.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*requerer* is inherited from Latin *requīrere* 'to seek, to ask for' (EtymDB), built on *quaerere* 'to seek'.
