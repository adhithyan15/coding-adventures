## You'll want to know: ਮਲਣਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C227-DIN227-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ਮਲਣਾ** — *malṇā* — "to rub".

> ਮੈਂ ਹੱਥਾਂ ਉੱਤੇ ਸਾਬਣ ਮਲਦਾ ਹਾਂ।
>
> *maiṁ hatthāṁ utte sābaṇ maldā hāṁ.*
>
> I rub soap on my hands.

Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਸਾਬਣ ਮਲਿਆ, *maiṁ sābaṇ maliā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਮਲਣਾ** (*malṇā*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*marati*, "crushes, rubs"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *maḷnā*, "to rub" (CDIAL 9870).
