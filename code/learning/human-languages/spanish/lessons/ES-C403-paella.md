# la paella — the pan that named the dish

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-COCINAR, ES-LEX-VERDURA] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say *to cook* and *the vegetables*. (*Cocinar, la verdura*.)

## Sounds you'll need
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

- *pa-E-ya* for most speakers: keep three clear beats, with the stress on **e**.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-PAELLA]; assesses=[] -->

**la paella** — “paella,” the Valencian rice dish.

The dish took the name of its pan. Valencian **paella** came through Old French
**paele** from Latin **patella**, a small pan or dish. Catalan also has native
**padella**; the different middle consonant is evidence that **paella** made the
French detour rather than wearing down inside Catalan.
