## You'll want to know: प्रबलः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C439-PAVANA439-03]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रबलः** — *prabalaḥ* — "strong; violent".

> समुद्रे प्रबलः वायुः वहति।
>
> *samudre prabalaḥ vāyuḥ vahati.*
>
> A violent wind blows over the sea.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रबलः** (*prabalaḥ*) is built from *pra*, "very", and *bala*, "strength"; MW gives *prabala* as strong, mighty and violent (MW).
