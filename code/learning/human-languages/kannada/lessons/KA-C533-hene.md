## You'll want to know: ಹೆಣೆ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C533-KASUTI533-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ಹೆಣೆ** — *heṇe* — "to knit; to plait".

> ಅಜ್ಜಿ ಚಳಿಗಾಲಕ್ಕೆ ಉಣ್ಣೆಯ ಸ್ವೆಟರ್ ಹೆಣೆದರು.
>
> *ajji caḷigālakke uṇṇeya sveṭar heṇedaru.*
>
> Grandmother knitted a woollen sweater for the winter.

Past (I …): ಹೆಣೆದೆ, *heṇede*.

Hair too: ಜಡೆ ಹೆಣೆ, to plait a braid.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಹೆಣೆ** (*heṇe*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 4160 lists Kannada *peṇe*, "to tie together, intertwine, twist, plait, braid". Old p- became h-.
