## You'll want to know: सुस्त
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C472-PHURTI472-02]; assesses=[] -->

**सुस्त** — *sust* — "sluggish; slack".

> गर्मी में सब लोग सुस्त हो जाते हैं।
>
> *garmī meṁ sab log sust ho jāte haiṁ.*
>
> In the heat everyone becomes sluggish.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सुस्त** (*sust*) is usually traced to Persian *sust*, "weak, slack".
