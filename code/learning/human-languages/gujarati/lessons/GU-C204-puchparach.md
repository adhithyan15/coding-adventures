## You'll want to know: પૂછપરછ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C204-TIMES204-02]; assesses=[] -->

**પૂછપરછ** — *pūchparach* — "enquiry, enquiries".

> **મેં પૂછપરછમાં સમય પૂછ્યો.** — *mẽ pūchparachmā̃ samay pūchyo* — "I asked the time at the enquiry desk."

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**પૂછપરછ** (*pūchparach*) is built on *pūch*, "asking", doubled with a rhyming partner. CDIAL lists Gujarati *puch-parach*, "questioning", under Sanskrit *pṛcchā*, "asking" (CDIAL 8354), the noun beside *pṛccháti*, "asks", which gave *pūchvũ*, "to ask" (CDIAL 8352).
