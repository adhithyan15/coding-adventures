## You'll want to know: طلبکار
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C411-QARZ-05]; assesses=[] -->

**طلبکار** (*talabkâr*) — "a creditor; owed money".

> طلبکارها هر روز به او زنگ میزنند.
>
> *talabkârhâ har ruz be u zang mizanand.*
>
> His creditors call him every day.

*Talab* "claim" + *-kâr*. Of a person who acts as if everyone owes him: *talabkâr ast*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**طلبکار** joins *talab* "claim", from Arabic *ṭalab* "seeking", to the Persian agent suffix *-kâr*: the one who has a claim.
