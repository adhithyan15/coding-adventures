# નિવારવું (nivārvũ) — to avert

## You'll want to know: નિવારવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C374-AVRODH374-05]; assesses=[] -->

**નિવારવું** — *nivārvũ* — "to avert".

> જો આપણે હાથ ધોઈશું તો ઘણા રોગ નિવારી શકીશું.
>
> *jo āpaṇe hāth dhoīśũ to ghaṇā rog nivārī śakīśũ.*
>
> If we wash our hands, we can prevent many illnesses.

Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે મોટી આપત્તિ નિવારી, *teṇe moṭī āpatti nivārī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**નિવારવું** (*nivārvũ*) is inherited from Sanskrit *nivārayati*, "wards off, restrains"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb, "to turn back, to avert" (CDIAL 7419).

One more word for saying what happens if.
