## You'll want to know: વણકર
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C506-TANTANO506-01]; assesses=[] -->

**વણકર** — *vaṇkar* — "a weaver".

> આ ગામના વણકર સુંદર સાડીઓ બનાવે છે.
>
> *ā gāmnā vaṇkar sundar sāḍīo banāve che.*
>
> The weavers of this village make beautiful saris.

A masculine or feminine noun, by the person meant.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**વણકર** (*vaṇkar*) is built on *vaṇvũ*, "to weave", which CDIAL lists under *\*vināti* (CDIAL 11773), with the ending *-kar*, "one who does".
