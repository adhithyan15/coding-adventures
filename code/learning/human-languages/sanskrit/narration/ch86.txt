Sanskrit, chapter 86: Friday to Sunday, and the Time.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
शुक्रवासरः (śukravāsaraḥ) — Friday.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for Wednesday, then the Sanskrit for Thursday.

You'll want to know: शुक्रवासरः.
शुक्रवासरः (śukravāsaraḥ) — "Friday", the day of शुक्र, Venus.

What you've built.
Venus's day.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: śukravāsaraḥ]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: śukravāsaraḥ, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say śukravāsaraḥ]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Sanskrit for Wednesday, then the Sanskrit for Thursday, then say śukravāsaraḥ again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]

Lesson 5 of 5.

क्षणः (kṣaṇaḥ) — "a moment". एकः क्षणः — "one moment", said with a raised hand.
