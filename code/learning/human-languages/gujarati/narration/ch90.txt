Gujarati, chapter 90: Weddings, Birthdays and Work.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
લગ્ન (lagna) — a wedding.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to study, then the Gujarati for to teach.

You'll want to know: લગ્ન.
લગ્ન (lagna) — "a wedding".

The word, taken apart.
લગ્ન (lagna) is Sanskrit lagna, "sticking to, fixed on", taken over whole as a learned loan, a tatsama (CDIAL 10893). The sense "wedding" is usually explained through its later use for the auspicious moment fixed for the rite.

One more word for weddings, birthdays and work.
