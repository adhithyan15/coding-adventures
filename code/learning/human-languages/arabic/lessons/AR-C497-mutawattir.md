## You'll want to know: متوتر
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C497-TAWATTUR497-01]; assesses=[] -->

**متوتر** — *mutawattir* — "tense, nervous".

> كانت أختي متوترة قبل المقابلة.
>
> *kānat ukhtī mutawattira qabla al-muqābala.*
>
> My sister was nervous before the interview.

The noun توتر, stress, shares the root.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**متوتر** (*mutawattir*) is built on the native Arabic root *w-t-r*, "a bowstring" (Lane). Mutawattir is the participle of Form V: drawn tight, like a bowstring.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
