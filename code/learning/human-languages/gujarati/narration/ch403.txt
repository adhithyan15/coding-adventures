Gujarati, chapter 403: On the Highway.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ધોરીમાર્ગ (dhorīmārg) — a highway; a main road.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a bus conductor, then the Gujarati for to drive a cart.

You'll want to know: ધોરીમાર્ગ.
ધોરીમાર્ગ — dhorīmārg — "a highway; a main road".
આ ધોરીમાર્ગ પર ઘણી ટ્રકો ચાલે છે. ā dhorīmārg par ghaṇī ṭrako cāle che. Many trucks run on this highway.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ધોરીમાર્ગ (dhorīmārg) is built from ધોરી, "main", which CDIAL lists under Sanskrit dhaurēya, "a beast of burden; foremost" (CDIAL 6884), and Sanskrit mārga, "a road": the road that carries the load.

Lesson 4 of 5.
ફંટાવું (phanṭāvũ) — to branch off (of a road).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a mile, then the Gujarati for a kilometre.

You'll want to know: ફંટાવું.
ફંટાવું — phanṭāvũ — "to branch off (of a road)".
આગળ જઈને રસ્તો બે બાજુ ફંટાય છે. āgaḷ jaīne rasto be bāju phanṭāy che. Further on, the road branches off two ways.
Past (it …): તે રસ્તો ફંટાયો, te rasto phanṭāyo.

The word, taken apart.
ફંટાવું (phanṭāvũ) goes back to phāṇṭ, "to separate by beating or cutting", a form CDIAL reconstructs; CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb, "to be divided" (CDIAL 9071).
