# నేరస్థుడు (nērasthuḍu) — a criminal; a culprit

## You'll want to know: నేరస్థుడు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C350-NERAM350-02]; assesses=[] -->

**నేరస్థుడు** — *nērasthuḍu* — "a criminal; a culprit".

> పోలీసులు నేరస్థుడిని పట్టుకున్నారు.
>
> *pōlīsulu nērasthuḍini paṭṭukunnāru.*
>
> The police caught the criminal.

Plural: నేరస్థులు, *nērasthulu*.

నేరం with the Sanskrit -స్థ, standing in: one who stands in a crime.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**నేరస్థుడు** (*nērasthuḍu*) is a mixed compound: native *nēram*, "crime" (DEDR 3775), with the Sanskrit ending *-stha*, "standing in, being in" (Monier-Williams).
