## You'll want to know: взлёт
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C421-VZLYOT-02]; assesses=[] -->

**взлёт** (*vzlyot*) — "take-off".

> Во время взлёта нельзя вставать.
>
> *Vo vrémya vzlyóta nelzyá vstavát.*
>
> You may not stand up during take-off.

Masculine. *Взлётная полоса* — the runway.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*взлёт* is inherited from Proto-Slavic *vŭzletŭ* (EtymDB), built from *vŭz-* 'up' and the root of *летать* 'to fly'.
