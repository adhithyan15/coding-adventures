## Fixed — chapters 43 and 44 practise chapter 32's రా and చూడు instead of teaching them a second time

TE-C43-come (రా, "come") and TE-C44-look (చూడు, "look") taught again words that Chapter 32 already teaches in the same sense: TE-C32-raa (`TE-LEX-C32-RAA-01`) and TE-C32-cuudu (`TE-LEX-C32-CUUDU-01`, "to see, to look"). Each introduced a second atom, `TE-LEX-COME` and `TE-LEX-LOOK`, so the track counted each word twice.

- **TE-C43-come and TE-C44-look now introduce nothing.** Each requires and practises its Chapter 32 atom and names its Chapter 32 lesson in `reviews_of`. Their input blocks now say the learner has already met the word, without naming a chapter number. TE-C43-come repeats the earlier warning that the endings attach to *vaccu-*, not *rā*. TE-C44-look used to call చూడు "the same three-way stem" as Kannada *nōḍu*, which contradicts TE-C32-cuudu's point that the two verbs are unrelated. It now says the two share the same three meanings, and that the verbs themselves are not related.
- **Everything that practised the duplicate atoms now practises the Chapter 32 atoms.** That covers TE-C43-sit, TE-C44-listen, TE-C143-lecu, the chapter 43 and 44 payoffs in `chapters.d/0043.json` and `chapters.d/0044.json`, and the A1 inventory probes in `core/exam-inventory-telugu-a1.json` (TE-A1-V-12, TE-A1-F-29, TE-A1-X-11, TE-A1-X-28 and TE-A1-X-49). TE-A1-X-11 already probed `TE-LEX-C32-CUUDU-01`, so its `TE-LEX-LOOK` probe was dropped rather than listed twice.
