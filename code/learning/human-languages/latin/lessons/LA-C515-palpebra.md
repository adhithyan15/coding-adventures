## You'll want to know: palpebra, palpebrae
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C515-VULT-05]; assesses=[] -->

**palpebra, palpebrae** — "eyelid".

> Puer fessus palpebrās claudit.
>
> *The tired boy closes his eyelids.*

Feminine, first declension; usually plural. English *palpebral*, in medicine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**palpebra** is built on *palpō* "to stroke, touch softly" (EtymDB), with an ending for a tool: the soft lid that strokes the eye.
