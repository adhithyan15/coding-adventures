# অন্তত (ôntôtô) — at least

## You'll want to know: অন্তত
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C373-EKEBARE373-03]; assesses=[] -->

**অন্তত** — *ôntôtô* — "at least".

> অন্তত দশজন এলে সভা হবে।
>
> *ôntôtô dôshjôn ele sôbhā hôbe.*
>
> The meeting will go ahead if at least ten people come.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**অন্তত** (*ôntôtô*) is Sanskrit *antatas*, 'from the end', which Monier-Williams also gives as 'in the lowest way': counting from the bottom, at the very least.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
