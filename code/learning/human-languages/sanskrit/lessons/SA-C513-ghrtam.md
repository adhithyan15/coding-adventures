## You'll want to know: घृतम्
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C513-MATHNATI513-03]; assesses=[] -->

**घृतम्** — *ghṛtam* — "ghee".

> नवनीतात् घृतं भवति।
>
> *navanītāt ghṛtaṃ bhavati.*
>
> Ghee is made from butter.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**घृतम्** (*ghṛtam*) comes from the root √ghṛ, "to sprinkle", as its past participle; MW gives *ghṛta* as ghee, butter that has been boiled gently and left to cool (MW).
