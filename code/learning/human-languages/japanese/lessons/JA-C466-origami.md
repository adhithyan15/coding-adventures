## You'll want to know: おりがみ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C466-ARTS466-04]; assesses=[] -->

**おりがみ** — *origami* — "paper folding; origami".

> **むすめは おりがみが じょうずです。** — *musume wa origami ga jōzu desu* — "My daughter is good at paper folding."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**おりがみ** (*origami*) is a native Japanese compound (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese: *ori*, "folding (oru)" and *gami*, "paper (kami)" (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
