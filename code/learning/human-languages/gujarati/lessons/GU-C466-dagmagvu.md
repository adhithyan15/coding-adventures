# ડગમગવું (ḍagmagvũ) — to wobble; to waver

## You'll want to know: ડગમગવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C466-SHAURYA466-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ડગમગવું** — *ḍagmagvũ* — "to wobble; to waver".

> તોફાનમાં પણ તેની હિંમત ડગમગી નહીં.
>
> *tophānmā̃ paṇ tenī himmat ḍagmagī nahī̃.*
>
> Even in the storm his courage did not waver.

Past (it …): તે હોડી ડગમગી, *te hoḍī ḍagmagī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ડગમગવું** (*ḍagmagvũ*) goes back to a reconstructed *\*ḍag-*, "tremble"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb, "to tremble" (CDIAL 5522).
