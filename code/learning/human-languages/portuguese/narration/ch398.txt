6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
o ficheiro — the computer file.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for to uninstall, then the Portuguese for to update.

You'll want to know: o ficheiro.
o ficheiro — "the computer file".
Perdi o ficheiro com o trabalho todo. I lost the file with all the work.
European Portuguese; in Brazil o arquivo.

The word, taken apart.
ficheiro is built on ficha "index card", borrowed from French fiche "peg, mark" (EtymDB), on ficher "to fasten", which is usually traced to Latin fīgere "to fix" (Lewis & Short).

copiar is built on cópia "copy", from Medieval Latin copia "transcript" (EtymDB), from Latin cōpia "plenty, abundance" (Lewis & Short): a copy multiplies a text.

The word, taken apart.
guardar is inherited from Medieval Latin wardāre, which took it from Frankish wardōn "to keep, to guard, to save" (EtymDB).

Lesson 4 of 6.

Warm-up.

Before the new one: say the Portuguese for to copy, then the Portuguese for to save.

You'll want to know: o anexo.
o anexo — "the attachment; the annex".
O anexo é muito grande para enviar. The attachment is too big to send.
Also an annex to a building or a document.

The word, taken apart.
anexo is a learned borrowing from Latin annexus "a connection" (EtymDB), from annectere "to tie on", built on nectere "to bind" (Lewis & Short).
