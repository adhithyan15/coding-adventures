## You'll want to know: dēngtǎ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C444-ISLAND444-04]; assesses=[] -->

*dēngtǎ* — "lighthouse".

> *wǎnshang chuán wǎng dēngtǎ kāi* — At night the boats steer for the lighthouse.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*dēngtǎ* is built from *dēng* 'a lamp' and *tǎ* 'a tower', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *tong* and *thap* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first as a lamp and the second as a pagoda.
