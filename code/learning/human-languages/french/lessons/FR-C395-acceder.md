## You'll want to know: accéder
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C395-COMPTE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**accéder** — "to access, to reach".

> Sans mot de passe, on ne peut pas accéder au site.
>
> *Without a password, you cannot get into the website.*

*Accéder à*, with *à*. *J'accède*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*accéder* is a learned borrowing from Latin *accēdere* "to go toward, to approach" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short), built on *cēdere* "to go".
