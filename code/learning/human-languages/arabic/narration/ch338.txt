Arabic, chapter 338: Remembering the dead.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
تأبين (taʾbīn) — a eulogy, a memorial tribute.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for an heir, then the Arabic for a widow.

You'll want to know: تأبين.
تأبين — taʾbīn — "a eulogy, a memorial tribute".
حضر الكتاب حفل تأبين الشاعر. ḥaḍara al-kuttāb ḥafl taʾbīn ash-shāʿir. The writers attended the poet’s memorial.

The word, taken apart.
تأبين (taʾbīn) is built on the native Arabic root ʾ-b-n (Lane), where Form II abbana is "to praise a person after his death"; taʾbīn is its verbal noun.

ضريح (ḍarīḥ) is built on the native Arabic root ḍ-r-ḥ (Lane), "to dig a trench for the dead"; a ḍarīḥ is first that grave, then a built tomb.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: وفاة.
وفاة — wafāh — "a death, a passing".
بكى الجميع بعد وفاة (wafāh) الجدة. bakā al-jamīʿ baʿda wafāt al-jadda. Everyone wept after the grandmother’s death.
The polite noun beside موت.

The word, taken apart.
وفاة (wafāh) is built on the native Arabic root w-f-y (Lane), "to be complete, to fulfil". Wafāh is a life brought to its full term.

مقبرة — maqbara — "a cemetery".
زرنا المقبرة صباح العيد. zurnā al-maqbara ṣabāḥ al-ʿīd. We visited the cemetery on the morning of the holiday.
Plural: مقابر, maqābir.

The word, taken apart.
مقبرة (maqbara) is built on the native Arabic root q-b-r (Lane), "to bury". The mafʿala pattern names a place: "the place of graves".
