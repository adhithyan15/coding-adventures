Spanish, chapter 642: En ruta.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
la circunvalación — the ring road, the bypass.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for the journey, then the Spanish for to stop.

You'll want to know: la circunvalación.
la circunvalación — "the ring road, the bypass".
Por la circunvalación no pasas por el centro. On the ring road you do not go through the centre.
In some Spanish cities it is called la ronda.

The word, taken apart.
circunvalación is a learned borrowing built on Latin circumvallāre "to surround with a wall" (Lewis & Short), from circum "around" and vallum "rampart".

Lesson 3 of 5.
el peaje — the toll.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for the ring road, then the Spanish for the hard shoulder.

You'll want to know: el peaje.
el peaje — "the toll".
En esta autopista hay que pagar peaje. You have to pay a toll on this motorway.
Una autopista de peaje — a toll motorway.

The word, taken apart.
peaje is usually traced to Old French péage, from Medieval Latin pedāticum "a toll for passing through", built on Latin pēs, pedis "a foot" (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 5 of 5.
el tramo — the stretch, the section (of a road or route).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for the toll, then the Spanish for the pothole.

You'll want to know: el tramo.
el tramo — "the stretch, the section (of a road or route)".
Este tramo está en obras. This stretch has roadworks.
Un tramo de escalera — a flight of stairs.

The word, taken apart.
tramo is traced in EtymDB to Latin trāmes "a cross-way, a by-path" (Lewis & Short). Some sources link it to trama "weft" instead, so the origin is not settled.
