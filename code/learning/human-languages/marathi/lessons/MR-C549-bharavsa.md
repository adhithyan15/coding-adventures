## You'll want to know: भरवसा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C549-SANRAKSHAN549-05]; assesses=[] -->

**भरवसा** — *bharavsā* — "reliance; trust".

> मला माझ्या मित्रांवर पूर्ण भरवसा आहे.
>
> *malā mājhyā mitrāṁvar pūrṇa bharavsā āhe.*
>
> I fully rely on my friends.

Masculine.

The one trusted takes -वर: मित्रांवर भरवसा.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**भरवसा** (*bharavsā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi *bharvasā*, "confidence", under *\*bharavaśya*, "dependence on support" (CDIAL 9398).
