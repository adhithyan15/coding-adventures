## You'll want to know: लजाना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C461-SANKOCH461-04]; assesses=[] -->

**लजाना** — *lajānā* — "to blush; to be bashful".

> सब लोगों के सामने अपना नाम सुनकर मीरा लजा गई।
>
> *sab logoṁ ke sāmne apnā nām sunkar Mīrā lajā gaī.*
>
> Mira blushed when she heard her name in front of everyone.

Past (she …): वह लजा गई, *vah lajā gaī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**लजाना** (*lajānā*) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Hindi *lajānā*, "to shame, be ashamed", under Sanskrit *lajjátē*, "is ashamed" (CDIAL 10909).
