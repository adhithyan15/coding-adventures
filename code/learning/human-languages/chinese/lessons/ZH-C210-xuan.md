## You'll want to know: xuǎn
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C210-STYLE210-05]; assesses=[] -->

*xuǎn* — "to choose".

> *nǐ xuǎn nǎge yánsè* — Which colour are you choosing?

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*xuǎn* is inherited from Middle Chinese *sjwenX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'to choose'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for asking for what I want.
