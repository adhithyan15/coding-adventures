## You'll want to know: le cadet
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C490-PARRAIN-05]; assesses=[] -->

**le cadet** — "younger (sibling); youngest".

> Mon frère cadet a trois ans de moins que moi.
>
> *My younger brother is three years younger than me.*

A woman is *la cadette*. *C'est le cadet de mes soucis* — that is the least of my worries.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*le cadet* was borrowed from Gascon *capdet* "little chief", a title for younger sons serving as officers; it goes back to Latin *capitellum*, from *caput* "head" (Lewis & Short).
