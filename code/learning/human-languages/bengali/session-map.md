# Session Map — Bengali Chapters 1–5

Same spaced-repetition schedule as the other tracks (a word from session *N*
resurfaces at *N+1, N+3, N+7, N+15*). Lessons named by slug; this map is the
authoritative order.

**There is no reading course.** The Bengali script is learned *through* the
words: each lesson's *"The letters in this word"* section introduces exactly the
letters that word needs.

## Chapter 1 — Greetings

| Session | Lesson | Word | Root / hook |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | nomoshkar | নমস্কার | "hello" ← Sanskrit *namaskāra*; the *a→ô*, *s→sh* fingerprint |
| 2 | dhonnobad | ধন্যবাদ | "thanks" ← *dhanya*+*vāda*; *a→ô* + *v→b* (no "v" in Bengali) |
| 3 | hyan-na | হ্যাঁ / না | "yes / no"; the *chandrabindu* nasal; *nā* ← PIE *ne |
| 4 | achchha | আচ্ছা | "okay / I see"; the standalone vowel আ and চ্ছ conjunct |
| 5 | ashi | আসি | "I'll come again" goodbye; স = "sh"; no gender on the verb |
| 6 | practice | (recap) | the five greetings + the sound-fingerprint |

## Chapter 2 — Introducing Yourself

| Session | Lesson | Word | Root / hook |
|---|---|---|---|
| 7 | naam | নাম | "name" ← Sanskrit *nāman* → English *name* |
| 8 | amar | আমার | "my" ← first-person *ām-*; **no gender** (unlike Hindi *merā/merī*) |
| 9 | amar-naam | আমার নাম … | **"my name is…"**; the **zero copula** (no "is") |
| 10 | tumi-apni | তুমি / আপনি | **"you"** — three levels (tui/tumi/āpni); *tumi* ← *\*tū* → *thou* |
| 11 | ki | কি | "what" ← *ka-* → English *what/who* |
| 12 | tomar-naam-ki | তোমার নাম কি? | **"what's your name?"** (still no "is") |
| 13 | alaap | আলাপ করে ভালো লাগলো | "pleased to meet you"; *ālāp* ← Sanskrit *ālāpa* (a rāga's opening) |
| 14 | practice | (dialogue) | the whole exchange |

## Chapter 3 — How Are You

| Session | Lesson | Word | Root / hook |
|---|---|---|---|
| 15 | kemon | কেমন | "how" ← the interrogative *k-* |
| 16 | tumi-kemon-achho | তুমি কেমন আছো? | **"how are you?"**; the verb *āchhā* — copula returns for state |
| 17 | ami | আমি | "I" ← Sanskrit *asmi* → English **am** |
| 18 | bhalo | ভালো | "well" ← Sanskrit *bhadra*; reply *āmi bhālo āchhi* |
| 19 | kono-bepar-na | কোনো ব্যাপার না | "no matter / you're welcome"; *nā* ← PIE *ne |
| 20 | practice | (dialogue) | the whole how-are-you exchange |

## Chapter 4 — Farewells

| Session | Lesson | Word | Root / hook |
|---|---|---|---|
| 21 | abar | আবার | "again" — turns a parting into a promise |
| 22 | dekha-hobe | দেখা হবে | "(we) will meet" = "a seeing will happen"; the impersonal future |
| 23 | abar-dekha-hobe | আবার দেখা হবে | **"we'll meet again"**; the fuller form of Ch.1's *āshi* |
| 24 | kal-dekha-hobe | কাল দেখা হবে | "see you tomorrow"; *kāl* = tomorrow *and* yesterday ← *kāla* |
| 25 | practice | (dialogue) | the farewells |

## Chapter 5 — The First Verbs

| Session | Lesson | Word | Root / hook |
|---|---|---|---|
| 26 | bola | বলা | "to speak"; the *-i* "I" ending; **never changes for gender** |
| 27 | ami-bangla-boli | আমি বাংলা বলি | **"I speak Bengali"**; *bôngo* → the Ganges delta |
| 28 | thaka | থাকা | "to live" ← Sanskrit *sthā* → English *stand/stay/state* |
| 29 | kaj-kora | কাজ করা | "to work" (noun + *kôrā*) ← √kṛ, the root of *nômoshkar* |
| 30 | practice | (dialogue) | three verbs, one gender-free engine |
