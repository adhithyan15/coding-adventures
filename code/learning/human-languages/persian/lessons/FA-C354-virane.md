## You'll want to know: ویرانه
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C354-KASHF-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ویرانه** (*virâne*) — "a ruin, ruins".

> از قلعه فقط یک ویرانه مانده است.
>
> *Az qal'e faqat yek virâne mânde ast.*
>
> Only a ruin is left of the castle.

From *virân* "ruined" + *-e*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*virâne* adds the suffix *-e* to *virân* "desolate, ruined", a native Persian word that EtymDB lists without a parent.

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
