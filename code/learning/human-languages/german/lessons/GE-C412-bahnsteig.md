## You'll want to know: der Bahnsteig
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C412-BAHNSTEIG-01]; assesses=[] -->

**der Bahnsteig** — "the platform (at a station)".

> Unser Zug fährt heute von einem anderen Bahnsteig.
>
> *Our train leaves from a different platform today.*

Plural: *die Bahnsteige*. The track is *das Gleis*. In Switzerland: *der Perron*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Bahnsteig* is built from *Bahn* 'track, railway' and *Steig* 'steep path', from *steigen* 'to climb', inherited from Old High German *stīgan* (EtymDB).
