## You'll want to know: らいしゅう
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C141-RAISHUU]; assesses=[] -->

**らいしゅう** — *raishū* — "next week": **せんしゅう**, **こんしゅう**,
**らいしゅう**, last week, this week, next week.

Japanese has no separate future tense. The polite present **ます** says
what will happen too, and a time word places it:

> **らいしゅう はたらきます。** — *raishū hatarakimasu* — "I will work next
> week."
