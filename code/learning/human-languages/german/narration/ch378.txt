German, chapter 378: Die Hausarbeit.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
die Hausarbeit — the term paper; housework.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for the degree programme, then the German for the internship.

You'll want to know: die Hausarbeit.
die Hausarbeit — "the term paper; housework".
Bis Ende März muss ich eine Hausarbeit über die Industrie schreiben. By the end of March I have to write a term paper about industry.
Plural: die Hausarbeiten. It also means housework: die Hausarbeit teilen.

The word, taken apart.
Hausarbeit joins Haus "house" to Arbeit "work", Old High German arbeit, Proto-Germanic arbaidiz (EtymDB): work done at home.

Lesson 2 of 5.
das Referat — the class talk, the oral report.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for the internship, then the German for the term paper.

You'll want to know: das Referat.
das Referat — "the class talk, the oral report".
Morgen halte ich ein Referat über Wale. Tomorrow I am giving a talk in class about whales.
Plural: die Referate. Ein Referat halten — to give a talk. In an office, das Referat is also a section.

The word, taken apart.
Referat comes from the Latin verb form referat "let him report", from referre "to carry back, to bring back word" (Lewis & Short). It is usually said to have been first a note on a document, then the report itself.

Lesson 3 of 5.
der Aufsatz — the essay, the composition.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for the term paper, then the German for the class talk.

You'll want to know: der Aufsatz.
der Aufsatz — "the essay, the composition".
Im Deutschunterricht haben wir einen Aufsatz über unsere Ferien geschrieben. In German class we wrote an essay about our holidays.
Plural: die Aufsätze. Also a top part set on something: der Aufsatz of a cupboard.

The word, taken apart.
Aufsatz is usually traced to Middle High German ūfsaz, of the family of aufsetzen "to set up, to draft", built on setzen "to set": a text you set down.

Lesson 4 of 5.
die Zusammenfassung — the summary.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for the class talk, then the German for the essay.

You'll want to know: die Zusammenfassung.
die Zusammenfassung — "the summary".
Am Ende des Kapitels steht eine kurze Zusammenfassung. At the end of the chapter there is a short summary.
Plural: die Zusammenfassungen.

The word, taken apart.
Zusammenfassung is the noun of zusammenfassen, built from zusammen "together" and fassen "to grasp", Old High German fazzōn, Proto-Germanic fatōną (EtymDB): you grasp it all together.

The word, taken apart.
