## You'll want to know: prōdō, prōdere
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C302-SPECULATOR-04]; assesses=[] -->

**prōdō, prōdere** — "to betray; to hand down".

> Servus dominum hostibus prōdidit.
>
> *The slave betrayed his master to the enemy.*

*prōdō, prōdere, prōdidī, prōditum*: to give forth. Also 'to hand down': *memoriae prōdere*, to record for later ages.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**prōdō** is built on *dō*, "to give", with *prō*, "forth" (Lewis & Short): to give forth, to give up, and so to betray.
