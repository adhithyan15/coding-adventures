## You'll want to know: ચોપડી
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C243-SCHOOL243-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ચોપડી** — *copḍī* — "schoolbook, small book".

> **આ મારી નવી ચોપડી છે.** — *ā mārī navī copḍī che* — "This is my new schoolbook."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

The origin of **ચોપડી** (*copḍī*) is not settled. CDIAL has no entry for it, and our other sources do not trace it further.
