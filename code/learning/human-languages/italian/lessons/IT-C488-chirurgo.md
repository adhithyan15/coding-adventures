## You'll want to know: il chirurgo
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C488-OSPEDALE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**il chirurgo** — "surgeon".

> Il chirurgo dice che l'operazione è andata bene.
>
> *The surgeon says the operation went well.*

The plural is *i chirurghi*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*chirurgo* is a learned borrowing from Latin *chīrurgus* (EtymDB), from Greek *kheirourgós* "working with the hands", from *kheír* "hand" and *érgon* "work".
