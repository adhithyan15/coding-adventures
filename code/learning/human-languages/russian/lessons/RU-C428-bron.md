## You'll want to know: бронь
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C428-NOCHLEG-05]; assesses=[] -->

**бронь** (*bron*) — "a booking, a reservation".

> У нас есть бронь на два номера.
>
> *U nas yest bron na dva nómera.*
>
> We have a booking for two rooms.

Feminine; colloquial for *бронирование*. Its cousin *броня* means armour.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*бронь* goes with *броня* 'armour', which EtymDB traces through Old East Slavic *brŭnja* to Proto-Slavic *brŭnja* and on to Gothic *brunjō* 'a breastplate': a booking shields a place for you.
