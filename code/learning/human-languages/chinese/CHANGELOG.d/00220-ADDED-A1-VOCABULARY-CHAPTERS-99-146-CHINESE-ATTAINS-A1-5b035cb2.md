## Added — the A1 vocabulary, chapters 99-146; Chinese attains A1

- Two tranches of 24 chapters of five words each (240 words, 85 verbs), every
  chapter on an A1 spine node, with reviews closing each block.
- 24 writing lessons, 洗 菜 用 本 站 试 网 外 边 出 心 包 (chapters 99-119) and 以 些 音 吵
  病 春 千 扫 重 节 每 半 (chapters 126-140), each directly after the word whose
  headword first shows the character, with Hanzi Writer Data's stroke order and a
  stroke-by-stroke strip.
- 39 words are headed in characters the reader writes; 201 are taught by ear with
  pinyin headwords. Examples print hanzi only for written characters.
- The independent reviews rewrote examples that needed untaught grammar (在 + place
  before another verb, the benefactive 给我 + verb) or an untaught measure word,
  replaced transparent combinations (下雪, 新年) with 打雷 and 过年, and kept every
  origin to what the Guangyun gives: 订 is dengX 'to discuss and settle', 复 shares
  bjuwk-return with the Japanese 復習, and 吵 'noisy' is a different word from 炒
  'to stir-fry' with its own slug.
- 夜里 realizes TIME-NIGHT, which leaves SPINE-TIME-OF-DAY's omits.
- 少's and 吵's last stroke is now described as starting at the middle of the
  right side, as Hanzi Writer's medians have it.
- With chapters 85-146 Chinese attains A1: 641 headwords and 172 verbs at or below
  A1, all twelve A1 spine nodes and both writing stages.
