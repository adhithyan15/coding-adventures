## You'll want to know: भँवर
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C305-BAADH305-05]; assesses=[] -->

**भँवर** — *bhãvar* — "a whirlpool".

> भँवर में एक नाव डूब गई।
>
> *bhãvar meṁ ek nāv ḍūb gaī.*
>
> A boat went down in the whirlpool.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

is listed by CDIAL under Sanskrit *bhramara*, "moving unsteadily, revolving" (CDIAL 9650), beside *bhramaraka*, a spinning top or a whirlpool, of the family of *bhram*, to whirl about. Water that turns round and round is a **भँवर**.
