## You'll want to know: 真心
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C358-MANNERS358-03]; assesses=[] -->

**真心** — *zhēnxīn* — "sincere; from the bottom of your heart".

> **我真心觉得你说得有道理** *wǒ zhēnxīn juéde nǐ shuō de yǒu dàoli* — I honestly think you've got a point.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**真心** (*zhēnxīn*) is built from 真 'true' and 心 'the heart', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *tsyin* and *sim* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'true, as against false'.
