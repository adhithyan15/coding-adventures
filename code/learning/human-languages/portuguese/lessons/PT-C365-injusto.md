## You'll want to know: injusto
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C365-JUSTICA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**injusto** — "unfair, unjust".

> Acho injusto pagar mais pelo mesmo trabalho.
>
> *I think it is unfair to pay more for the same work.*

Feminine *injusta*. The opposite of *justo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*injusto* is a learned borrowing from Latin *iniūstus* "unjust" (Lewis & Short), built from *in-* "not" and *iūstus* "fair, righteous".
