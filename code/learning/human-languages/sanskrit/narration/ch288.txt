Sanskrit, chapter 288: At the Watch-house.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
अपराधः (aparādhaḥ) — an offence; a crime.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for stays awake, then the Sanskrit for an uproar.

You'll want to know: अपराधः.
अपराधः — aparādhaḥ — "an offence; a crime".
चौर्यम् अपराधः अस्ति। cauryam aparādhaḥ asti. Theft is an offence.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
अपराधः (aparādhaḥ) is built from apa, "away", and the root √rādh, "to succeed": MW gives apa-rādh as to miss one's aim and to offend, and aparādha as an offence (MW).

Lesson 2 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for an uproar, then the Sanskrit for an offence.

You'll want to know: सन्देहः.
सन्देहः — sandehaḥ — "a suspicion; a doubt".
मम सन्देहः अस्ति। mama sandehaḥ asti. I have a suspicion.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
सन्देहः (sandehaḥ) is built from sam, "together", and the root √dih, "to smear, heap up"; MW's first sense is a mass stuck together, and its usual sense doubt or uncertainty, and a suspicion is its everyday modern use (MW).

तर्जयति — tarjayati — "threatens".
चौरः बालं तर्जयति। cauraḥ bālaṃ tarjayati. The thief threatens the boy.
Past (he …): सः बालं तर्जितवान्, saḥ bālaṃ tarjitavān.

The word, taken apart.
तर्जयति (tarjayati) comes from the root √tarj, "to threaten, scold"; tarjayati is its causative, which MW gives with the same sense (MW).
