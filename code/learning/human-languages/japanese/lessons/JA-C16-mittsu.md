> **みっつ** — *mi-t-tsu* — **three things**

Every sign is already yours: **み**, the small **っ**, and **つ**.

The small **っ** is the sign you met in **ゆっくり**: it holds the mouth shut for
one beat. **みっつ** is three beats, and the middle one is silence.

**さん** is *three*. **みっつ** is *three things*.
