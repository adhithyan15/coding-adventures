## You'll want to know: सामान्य
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C389-BEHTAR389-04]; assesses=[] -->

**सामान्य** — *sāmānya* — "normal, usual".

> अगर बुख़ार सामान्य से ज़्यादा होगा तो डाक्टर को बुलाओ।
>
> *agar bukhār sāmānya se zyādā hogā to ḍākṭar ko bulāo.*
>
> If the fever is above normal, call the doctor.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सामान्य** (*sāmānya*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *sāmānya*, "common, general" (Monier-Williams).
