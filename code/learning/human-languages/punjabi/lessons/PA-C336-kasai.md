## You'll want to know: ਕਸਾਈ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C336-TRADE336-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ਕਸਾਈ** — *kasāī* — "a butcher".

> ਕਸਾਈ ਦੀ ਦੁਕਾਨ ਬਾਜ਼ਾਰ ਦੇ ਕੋਨੇ ਉੱਤੇ ਹੈ।
>
> *kasāī dī dukān bāzār de kone utte hai.*
>
> The butcher's shop is on the corner of the bazaar.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਕਸਾਈ** (*kasāī*) is a loan, usually traced through Persian to Arabic *qaṣṣāb*, "a butcher".
