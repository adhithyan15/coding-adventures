## You'll want to know: вытирать
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C529-CLEANING-05]; assesses=[] -->

**вытирать** (*vytirát*) — "to wipe; to dry".

> После ужина брат моет тарелки, а я их вытираю.
>
> *Pósle úzhina brat móyet tarélki, a ya ikh vytiráyu.*
>
> After dinner my brother washes the plates and I dry them.

Imperfective; perfective вытереть (вытру; past вытер). *Вытирать пыль* — to dust.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*вытирать* is built on *вы-* and the root of *тереть* 'to rub'; EtymDB has no entry.
