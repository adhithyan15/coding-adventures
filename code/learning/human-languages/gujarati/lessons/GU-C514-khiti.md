# ખીંટી (khī̃ṭī) — a wall peg

## You'll want to know: ખીંટી
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C514-DHILU514-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ખીંટી** — *khī̃ṭī* — "a wall peg".

> મારી છત્રી ખીંટી પર છે.
>
> *mārī chatrī khī̃ṭī par che.*
>
> My umbrella is on the peg.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ખીંટી** (*khī̃ṭī*) is filed by CDIAL under Sanskrit *kīla*, "a stake, a peg", with the Gujarati word glossed "a stopper" (CDIAL 3202); on a wall it is a peg to hang things on.
