Lesson 1 of 5.
মাঝি (mājhi) — a boatman.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for abroad, then the Bengali for to make a crossing.

You'll want to know: মাঝি.
মাঝি — mājhi — "a boatman".
বুড়ো মাঝি নৌকায় বসে গান গাইছিল। buṛo mājhi noukāy bôse gān gāichhilô. The old boatman sat in the boat, singing.
The মাঝি (mājhi)'s river songs are a whole branch of Bengali folk music.

The word, taken apart.
মাঝি (mājhi) is inherited: CDIAL lists Bengali māji, "a steersman", under the reconstructed majjhika, "a boatman" (CDIAL 9714). It is not filed under mádhya, middle, the source of মাঝ.

Lesson 3 of 5.
নোঙর (nongôr) — an anchor.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a boatman, then the Bengali for an oar.

You'll want to know: নোঙর.
নোঙর — nongôr — "an anchor".
বন্দরের কাছে জাহাজটা নোঙর ফেলল। bôndôrer kāchhe jāhājṭā nongôr phellô. The ship dropped anchor near the port.
নোঙর (nongôr) ফেলা, to drop anchor; নোঙর (nongôr) তোলা, to weigh anchor.

The word, taken apart.
নোঙর (nongôr) is usually traced to Persian langar, "an anchor"; Bengali turned the first l into n.

Lesson 4 of 5.
ছই (chhôi) — the hood of a country boat.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for an oar, then the Bengali for an anchor.

You'll want to know: ছই.
ছই — chhôi — "the hood of a country boat".
রোদে আমরা নৌকার ছইয়ের নিচে বসলাম। rode āmrā noukār chhôiyer niche bôslām. In the sun we sat under the boat's hood.
The curved roof of bamboo and matting over the middle of a river boat.

The word, taken apart.
ছই (chhôi) is inherited: CDIAL lists Bengali chai, "the thatched covering of a boat or cart", under Sanskrit chadís, "a cover, a roof" (CDIAL 4980).

Lesson 5 of 5.
বাওয়া (bāoyā) — to row; to steer (a boat).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for an anchor, then the Bengali for the hood of a country boat.

You'll want to know: বাওয়া.
বাওয়া — bāoyā — "to row; to steer (a boat)".
মাঝি সারারাত নৌকা বাইল। mājhi sārārāt noukā bāilô. The boatman rowed the boat all night.
Past (he/she …): তিনি নৌকা বাইলেন, tini noukā bāilen.
The stem is বা-: আমি বাই, সে বায়, সে বাইল.

The word, taken apart.
বাওয়া (bāoyā) is inherited: CDIAL lists Bengali baoyā and bahā, "to carry, to row, to steer", under Sanskrit váhati, "carries, conveys" (CDIAL 11453).
