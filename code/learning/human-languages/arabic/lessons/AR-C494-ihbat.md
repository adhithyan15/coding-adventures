# إحباط (iḥbāṭ) — frustration

## You'll want to know: إحباط
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C494-KAABA494-03]; assesses=[] -->

**إحباط** — *iḥbāṭ* — "frustration".

> شعر الفريق بالإحباط بعد الخسارة.
>
> *shaʿara al-farīq bi-l-iḥbāṭ baʿda al-khasāra.*
>
> The team felt frustrated after the loss.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**إحباط** (*iḥbāṭ*) is built on the native Arabic root *ḥ-b-ṭ*, "to come to nothing, to be made void" (Lane). Iḥbāṭ is the feeling when an effort comes to nothing.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
