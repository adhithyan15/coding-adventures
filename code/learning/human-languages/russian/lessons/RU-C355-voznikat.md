## You'll want to know: возникать
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C355-SLUCHAYNO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**возникать** (*voznikát*) — "to arise, to come up".

> На работе часто возникают новые проблемы.
>
> *Na rabóte chásto voznikáyut nóvyye problémy.*
>
> New problems often come up at work.

Imperfective; perfective *возникнуть* (past *возник, возникла*). Of questions, problems and ideas.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**возникать** was taken from Church Slavonic *vŭzniknǫti* (EtymDB), built from *воз-* "up" and *никнуть* "to bow down, to droop": something that rises up into view.
