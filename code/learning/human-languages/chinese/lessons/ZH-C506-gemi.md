## You'll want to know: gēmí
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C506-BAND506-05]; assesses=[] -->

*gēmí* — "fan (of a singer)".

> *wǒ shì nàge gēshǒu zuì dà de gēmí* — I'm that singer's biggest fan.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*gēmí* is built from 歌 'a song' and *mí* 'to be led astray', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *ka* and *mej* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'to be confused'.
