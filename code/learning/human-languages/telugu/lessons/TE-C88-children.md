## You'll want to know: పిల్లలు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C88-PEOPLE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**పిల్లలు** (*pillalu*) — "children".

**మీకు పిల్లలు ఉన్నారా?** — "do you have children?", literally "to you, are there children?".
The **-లు** at the end is the plural you met in your very first lesson.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

The question that follows a name in almost every first conversation.
