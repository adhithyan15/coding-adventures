## You'll want to know: धमकावणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C300-LACH300-01]; assesses=[] -->

**धमकावणे** — *dhamkāvṇe* — "to threaten".

> त्या गुंडाने दुकानदाराला धमकावलं.
>
> *tyā guṇḍāne dukāndārālā dhamkāvlaṁ.*
>
> That thug threatened the shopkeeper.

Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने धमकावलं, *tyāne dhamkāvlaṁ*.

The person threatened takes -ला; the noun is धमकी, a threat.

## The word, taken apart
