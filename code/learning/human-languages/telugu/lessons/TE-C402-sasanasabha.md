# శాసనసభ (śāsanasabha) — a legislative assembly

## You'll want to know: శాసనసభ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C402-MANTRI402-01]; assesses=[] -->

**శాసనసభ** — *śāsanasabha* — "a legislative assembly".

> కొత్త చట్టం గురించి శాసనసభలో చర్చ జరిగింది.
>
> *kotta caṭṭaṁ guriñci śāsanasabhalō carca jarigindi.*
>
> There was a debate in the assembly about the new law.

శాసన, law-making, + సభ, an assembly. Its members are శాసనసభ్యులు.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**శాసనసభ** (*śāsanasabha*) is built from Sanskrit *śāsana*, "instructing; a command, a royal edict" (CDIAL 12420), and *sabhā*, "an assembly, a hall" (CDIAL 13167).
