# Review — the scene, and what cut into it

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[AR-GRAMMAR-C283-PRESENT-PERSONS-01, AR-GRAMMAR-C283-PAST-PERSONS-01, AR-LEX-C283-MADI283-01, AR-GRAMMAR-C283-KANA-IMPERFECT-01, AR-LEX-C156-MADI156-03] -->

Four lessons, one stem, and three shapes of it. The present marks
the person at the front: *aktubu, taktubu, yaktubu, naktubu*. The past
marks it at the back: *katabtu, katabat, katabnā, katabū*. And *kāna* in
front of the present gives the past that is still running: *kuntu aktubu*.

[PAUSE 2s] *Al-māḍī* sets the time:

> **كنت أعمل في المكتب الأسبوع الماضي.** — *kuntu aʿmalu fī al-maktab
> al-usbūʿa al-māḍī* — Last week I was working at the office.

[PAUSE 2s] And *ḥīnamā* joins the scene to the event that cut into it:

> **كانت تكتب قصة حينما جاء أخوها.** — *kānat taktubu qiṣṣa ḥīnamā jāʾa
> akhūhā* — She was writing a story when her brother came.
