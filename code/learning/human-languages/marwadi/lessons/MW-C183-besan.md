## You'll want to know: बेसण
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C183-HATH183-05]; assesses=[] -->

**बेसण** — *besaṇ* — "gram flour".

> बेसण पीळो है।
>
> *besaṇ pīḷo hai.*
>
> Gram flour is yellow.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**बेसण** (*besaṇ*) is inherited from Sanskrit *vesana*, a kind of flour; CDIAL lists Gujarati *vesaṇ* and Hindi *besan*, "gram flour" (CDIAL 12133). CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry.
