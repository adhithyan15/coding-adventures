# ુ — the attached short-u vowel sign

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-SCRIPT-II-MATRA-01] -->

Write the long-ī sign once from memory. Check that its curl sits to the right.

## Script — meet one form
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-SCRIPT-U-MATRA-01]; assesses=[] -->

Keep **ુ** visible. It is the attached short-u vowel sign, pronounced *-u*. This is the short-u vowel sign. It hangs below its consonant as a small hook. The position below, not beside or above, identifies the mark.

## Writing — notice and trace
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-SCRIPT-U-MATRA-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: observe-trace -->

Finger-trace **ુ** once, then air-trace it once. Notice a below-letter hook; one small hook below.

## Writing — copy with the model
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-SCRIPT-U-MATRA-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: guided-copy -->

Copy visible **ુ** once. Compare only the landmark named above and repair it once if needed.

## Writing — recall after hiding
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-SCRIPT-U-MATRA-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: delayed-copy -->
<!-- hl-activity: {"id":"GU-W03-u-matra-recall","kind":"text","assesses":["GU-SCRIPT-U-MATRA-01"],"prompt":"Hide the model and write the attached short-u vowel sign from memory.","answer":"ુ","accepted":[],"feedback":{"correct":"ુ — the taught form is intact.","incorrect":"Look once at ુ, hide it, and repair only the differing landmark."},"response_seconds":20} -->

Hide the model and write *-u* once. Uncover **ુ**, compare, and stop after one repair.

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-SCRIPT-U-MATRA-01] -->

[PAUSE 3s] Name the form, its position or main landmark, and its pen-lift count.
