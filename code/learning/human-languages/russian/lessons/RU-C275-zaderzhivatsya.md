## You'll want to know: задерживаться
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C275-ZATYAGIVATSYA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**задерживаться** (*zadérzhivatsya*) — "to be delayed, to stay late".

> Папа сегодня задерживается на работе.
>
> *Pápa sevódnya zadérzhivayetsya na rabóte.*
>
> Dad is staying late at work today.

Imperfective; the perfective is *задержаться* (*zaderzhátsya*): *поезд задержался на час* — the train was an hour late.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*задерживаться* is built on *за-* and *держать* "to hold", which is inherited from Proto-Slavic *dĭržati* "to hold", from PIE *dʰer-* (EtymDB): to be held back.
