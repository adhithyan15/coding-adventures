Punjabi, chapter 309: News from Home.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ਖ਼ਬਰ (khabar) — a piece of news.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for to take out, then the Punjabi for perhaps.

You'll want to know: ਖ਼ਬਰ.
ਖ਼ਬਰ — khabar — "a piece of news".
ਅਮਨ ਨੇ ਮੈਨੂੰ ਇੱਕ ਚੰਗੀ ਖ਼ਬਰ ਦਿੱਤੀ। Aman ne mainū̃ ikk chaṅgī khabar dittī. Aman gave me a piece of good news.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ਖ਼ਬਰ (khabar) is a loan, usually traced through Persian to Arabic khabar, "news, a report".

What you've built.
One more word for saying what happened.

Guided Practice.

[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: khabar, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: Aman ne mainū̃ ikk chaṅgī khabar dittī. — Aman gave me a piece of good news.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Punjabi for to take out, then the Punjabi for perhaps, then say khabar again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does ਖ਼ਬਰ (khabar) mean? ("A piece of news".) Say it once more.

Lesson 4 of 5.
ਰੌਲਾ (raulā) — a din; a lot of noise.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for an accident, then the Punjabi for a rumour.

You'll want to know: ਰੌਲਾ.
ਰੌਲਾ — raulā — "a din; a lot of noise".
ਹਾਦਸੇ ਤੋਂ ਬਾਅਦ ਸੜਕ ਉੱਤੇ ਬਹੁਤ ਰੌਲਾ ਸੀ। hādse ton bāad saṛak utte bahut raulā sī. After the accident there was a lot of noise on the road.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ਰੌਲਾ (raulā) is inherited from Sanskrit rava, "a roar, a cry"; CDIAL lists Punjabi raulā, "noise", under it (CDIAL 10641).

Past (it …): ਉਹ ਬਿੱਲੀ ਸਰਦੀ ਵਿੱਚ ਮਰੀ, oh billī sardī vich marī.

ਮਰਨਾ (marnā) is inherited from Sanskrit marate, "will die"; CDIAL lists Punjabi marnā, "dies", under it (CDIAL 9871).
