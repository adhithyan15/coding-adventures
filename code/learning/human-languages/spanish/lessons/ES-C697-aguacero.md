## You'll want to know: el aguacero
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C697-LLUVIA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**el aguacero** — "the downpour".

> De repente cayó un aguacero.
>
> *Suddenly there was a downpour.*

Heavier than *un chubasco*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*aguacero* is built on *agua* "water" (EtymDB), which is inherited from Latin *aqua*.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.
