## You'll want to know: das Bargeld
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C449-KASSE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**das Bargeld** — "the cash".

> Auf dem Markt zahlt man oft mit Bargeld.
>
> *At the market people often pay in cash.*

No plural. *Bar zahlen* — to pay cash.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Bargeld* is built from *bar* 'bare, ready', an inherited Germanic word that is a cousin of English *bare* (EtymDB), and *Geld* 'money'.
