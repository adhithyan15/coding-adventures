## You'll want to know: vomō, vomere
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C525-TUSS-02]; assesses=[] -->

**vomō, vomere** — "to vomit, be sick".

> Nauta in nāve vomit.
>
> *The sailor is sick on the ship.*

*vomō, vomere, vomuī, vomitum*. Also of a volcano spewing fire. English *vomit*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**vomō** is inherited from a Proto-Indo-European root meaning "to vomit", and Greek *emeo* is a cousin (EtymDB; Lewis & Short). English *vomit* comes from it.
