## You'll want to know: प्रकटयति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C292-CARA292-05]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रकटयति** — *prakaṭayati* — "reveals".

> यदि प्रमाणं भविष्यति, तर्हि सः प्रकटयिष्यति।
>
> *yadi pramāṇaṃ bhaviṣyati, tarhi saḥ prakaṭayiṣyati.*
>
> If there is proof, then he will reveal it.

Past (he …): सः रहस्यं प्रकटितवान्, *saḥ rahasyaṃ prakaṭitavān*.

Future: प्रकटयिष्यति, *prakaṭayiṣyati*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रकटयति** (*prakaṭayati*) is built on *prakaṭa*, "evident, manifest"; MW gives *prakaṭayati* as a verb made from it, to manifest or disclose (MW).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
