German, chapter 333: Widersprechen.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
widersprechen — to contradict, to disagree with.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for especially since, then the German for to explain in detail.

You'll want to know: widersprechen.
widersprechen — "to contradict, to disagree with".
Ich muss dir widersprechen: Der Film war gar nicht langweilig. I have to contradict you: the film was not boring at all.
Inseparable, with the dative: ich widerspreche dir, ich habe ihm widersprochen. Conjugated like sprechen: er widerspricht.

The word, taken apart.
widersprechen is inherited through Middle High German widersprechen from Old High German widarsprehhan (EtymDB), built from wider "against" and sprechen "to speak": to speak against.

Lesson 2 of 5.
der Widerspruch — the contradiction; the objection.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to explain in detail, then the German for to contradict.

You'll want to know: der Widerspruch.
der Widerspruch — "the contradiction; the objection".
In seiner Geschichte gibt es einen klaren Widerspruch. There is a clear contradiction in his story.
Plural: die Widersprüche. Ohne Widerspruch — without objection.

The word, taken apart.
Widerspruch is the noun that goes with widersprechen, built from wider "against" and Spruch "saying", from the family of sprechen (EtymDB).

Lesson 3 of 5.
der Einwand — the objection.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to contradict, then the German for the contradiction.

You'll want to know: der Einwand.
der Einwand — "the objection".
Mein Vater hatte einen Einwand: Die Reise war ihm zu teuer. My father had an objection: the trip was too expensive for him.
Plural: die Einwände. Einen Einwand gegen etwas haben — to have an objection to something.

The word, taken apart.
Einwand is formed from einwenden "to object" (EtymDB), built on wenden "to turn", inherited from Old High German wenten and Proto-Germanic wandijaną (EtymDB): something you turn against an idea.

Lesson 4 of 5.
einwenden — to object, to say against.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for the contradiction, then the German for the objection.

You'll want to know: einwenden.
einwenden — "to object, to say against".
Sie wendet ein, dass der Weg zu weit ist. She objects that the way is too far.
Separable: sie wendet ein. Perfekt: sie hat eingewandt or eingewendet; both are correct.

The word, taken apart.
einwenden is built from ein and wenden "to turn", inherited from Old High German wenten and Proto-Germanic wandijaną (EtymDB).

Lesson 5 of 5.
bestreiten — to deny, to dispute.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for the objection, then the German for to object.

You'll want to know: bestreiten.
bestreiten — "to deny, to dispute".
Der Junge bestreitet, dass er das Fenster kaputt gemacht hat. The boy denies that he broke the window.
Inseparable and strong: er bestreitet, er hat bestritten. Das kann man nicht bestreiten — that cannot be denied.

The word, taken apart.
bestreiten is inherited from Middle High German bestrīten "to fight against" (EtymDB), built on strīten, today streiten "to quarrel": you fight a claim.
