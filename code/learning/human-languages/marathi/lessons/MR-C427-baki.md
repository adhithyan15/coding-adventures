## You'll want to know: बाकी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C427-VYAJ427-04]; assesses=[] -->

**बाकी** — *bākī* — "the remainder; the rest".

> मी अर्धे पैसे दिले, बाकी उद्या देईन.
>
> *mī ardhe paise dile, bākī udyā deīn.*
>
> I paid half the money; I will give the rest tomorrow.

Feminine.

Also the balance of an account: बाकी किती?

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**बाकी** (*bākī*) is a loan through Persian, usually traced to Arabic *bāqī*, "remaining".
