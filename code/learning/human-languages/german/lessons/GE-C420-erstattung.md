## You'll want to know: die Erstattung
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C420-STORNO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**die Erstattung** — "the refund".

> Die Erstattung kommt nach zwei Wochen auf das Konto.
>
> *The refund reaches the account after two weeks.*

Plural: *die Erstattungen*. Also *die Rückerstattung*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Erstattung* is built on *erstatten* 'to refund' with *-ung*; the verb comes from *er-* and *statten* 'to put in place', from *Statt* 'place'.
