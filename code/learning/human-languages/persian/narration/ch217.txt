Persian, chapter 217: To speak, To work, To play, To walk, To get on.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
حرف زدن (harf zadan) — to speak, to talk.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for an electrician, then the Persian for a tailor.

You'll want to know: حرف زدن.
حرف زدن — harf zadan — "to speak, to talk".

The word, taken apart.
harf zadan pairs harf, borrowed from Arabic ḥarf, "letter, word" (root ḥ-r-f, Lane), with the Persian verb zadan, "to strike". zadan, "to strike", is an inherited verb; it is usually traced to Middle Persian zadan.

Warm-up.

You'll want to know: سوار شدن.
سوار شدن — savâr shodan — "to get on (a vehicle)".

The word, taken apart.
savâr shodan is "to become a rider". savâr is inherited: Old Persian had asabāra-, "horseman" (EtymDB), built on the word for "horse". shodan, "to become", is an inherited verb; it is usually traced to Middle Persian šudan, "to go".
