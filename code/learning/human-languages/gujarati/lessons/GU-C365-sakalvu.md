## You'll want to know: સાંકળવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C365-SIDDHANT365-05]; assesses=[] -->

**સાંકળવું** — *sā̃kaḷvũ* — "to link".

> ગુરુ નવો પાઠ જૂના પાઠ સાથે સાંકળે છે.
>
> *guru navo pāṭh jūnā pāṭh sāthe sā̃kḷe che.*
>
> The teacher links the new lesson to the old one.

Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે બંને વિચાર સાંકળ્યા, *teṇe banne vicār sā̃kaḷyā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**સાંકળવું** (*sā̃kaḷvũ*) is built on *sā̃kaḷ*, "a chain", which CDIAL traces to Sanskrit *śṛṅkhala*, "a chain" (CDIAL 12580): to chain two things together.

## What you've built

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
