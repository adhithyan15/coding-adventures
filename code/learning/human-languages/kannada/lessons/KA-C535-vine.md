## You'll want to know: ವೀಣೆ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C535-VADYA535-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ವೀಣೆ** — *vīṇe* — "a veena".

> ವೀಣೆಯ ನಾದ ತುಂಬಾ ಮಧುರ.
>
> *vīṇeya nāda tumbā madhura.*
>
> The sound of the veena is very sweet.

A long-necked string instrument of the south.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ವೀಣೆ** (*vīṇe*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *vīṇā*, "a lute" (CDIAL 12048); the final -ā became -ೆ.
