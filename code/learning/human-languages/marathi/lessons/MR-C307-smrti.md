## You'll want to know: स्मृती
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C307-ANT307-05]; assesses=[] -->

**स्मृती** — *smṛtī* — "a remembrance; memory".

> आजोबांच्या स्मृती अजून ताज्या आहेत.
>
> *ājobāñcyā smṛtī ajūn tājyā āhet.*
>
> Memories of Grandfather are still fresh.

Feminine.

स्मृतीत, in memory. आठवण is the everyday word.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**स्मृती** (*smṛtī*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *smṛti*, 'remembrance, memory' (Monier-Williams).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
