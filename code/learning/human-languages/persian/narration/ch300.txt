Persian, chapter 300: Suddenly and at once.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ناگهان (nâgahân) — suddenly, all of a sudden.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for an invoice, then the Persian for a cash machine.

You'll want to know: ناگهان.
ناگهان (nâgahân) — "suddenly, all of a sudden".
ناگهان (nâgahân) برق رفت و خانه تاریک شد. Nâgahân barq raft va khâne târik shod. Suddenly the power went off and the house went dark.
Neutral and common in stories; in everyday speech Iranians also say yekhow. It usually opens the clause.

The word, taken apart.
nâgahân is inherited: EtymDB derives it from nâgah (also nâgâh), which continues Middle Persian anāgāh, "unaware, unwitting". A sudden thing comes on you unawares.

Lesson 2 of 5.
بلافاصله (belâfâsele) — immediately, right away.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a cash machine, then the Persian for suddenly.

You'll want to know: بلافاصله.
بلافاصله (belâfâsele) — "immediately, right away".
پلیس بلافاصله به محل رسید. Polis belâfâsele be mahal resid. The police arrived at the scene immediately.
Literally "without a gap". Belâfâsele ba'd az means "right after": belâfâsele ba'd az shâm "right after dinner".

The word, taken apart.
belâfâsele is an Arabic phrase taken over whole: bi- "with", lā "no" and fāṣila "an interval, a gap" (EtymDB lists Arabic fāṣila). Persian uses it as a single adverb.

Lesson 3 of 5.
بی درنگ (bi derang) — without delay, at once.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for suddenly, then the Persian for immediately.

You'll want to know: بی درنگ.
بی درنگ (bi derang) — "without delay, at once".
او بی درنگ به آمبولانس زنگ زد. U bi derang be âmbulâns zang zad. She called an ambulance without delay.
Formal and literary; in speech fowran or zud is more usual. Built from bi "without" and derang "delay".

The word, taken apart.
bi derang is built from bi "without" and derang "a delay, a pause", which is inherited from Middle Persian dērang, "a period, a duration" (EtymDB).

Lesson 4 of 5.
همین که (hamin ke) — the moment that, as soon as.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for immediately, then the Persian for without delay.

You'll want to know: همین که.
همین که (hamin ke) — "the moment that, as soon as".
همین که (hamin ke) در را باز کردم، گربه فرار کرد. Hamin ke dar râ bâz kardam, gorbe farâr kard. The moment I opened the door, the cat ran away.
It opens a clause, with the past for a past event: hamin ke residam "as soon as I arrived".

The word, taken apart.
hamin ke joins hamin "this very", built on ham "same, also" and in "this", with the linking word ke "that". All of its parts are inherited Persian words.

Lesson 5 of 5.
اتفاقی (ettefâqi) — by chance, accidentally.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for without delay, then the Persian for the moment that.

You'll want to know: اتفاقی.
اتفاقی (ettefâqi) — "by chance, accidentally".
دیروز اتفاقی دوست مدرسه ام را در بازار دیدم. Diruz ettefâqi dust-e madrese-am râ dar bâzâr didam. Yesterday I ran into my school friend at the bazaar by chance.
Also an adjective: yek didâr-e ettefâqi "a chance meeting". Built on ettefâq "an event".

The word, taken apart.
ettefâqi adds the Persian suffix -i to ettefâq "an event", which is borrowed from Arabic ittifāq (EtymDB). In Arabic the word is usually glossed "agreement, coincidence".
