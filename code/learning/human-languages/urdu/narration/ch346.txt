Urdu, chapter 346: Serving Others.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
خدمت (khidmat) — service to others.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a principle, then the Urdu for a creed.

You'll want to know: خدمت.
خدمت — khidmat — "service to others".
وہ ہسپتال میں مریضوں کی خدمت کرتی ہے۔ voh haspatāl meṅ marīzoṅ kī khidmat kartī hai. She looks after patients at the hospital.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
خدمت (khidmat) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic khidma, "service".

Lesson 3 of 6.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for service to others, then the Urdu for selflessness.

You'll want to know: لالچ.
لالچ — lālach — "greed".
لالچ بری بات ہے۔ lālach burī bāt hai. Greed is a bad thing.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
لالچ (lālach) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin lālac, "cupidity", under Sanskrit lālitya, "amorousness" (CDIAL 11029); لالچی, "greedy", is built on it.

Lesson 5 of 6.
جتانا (jatānā) — to make known; to make a show of (a favour).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for greed, then the Urdu for welfare.

You'll want to know: جتانا.
جتانا — jatānā — "to make known; to make a show of (a favour)".
اس نے اپنی خدمت کبھی نہیں جتائی۔ us ne apnī khidmat kabhī nahīṅ jatāī. She never made a show of her service.
Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے اپنا حق جتایا, us ne apnā haq jatāyā.

The word, taken apart.
جتانا (jatānā) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin jatānā, "to make known", under Sanskrit jñaptá, "instructed" (CDIAL 5273).
