## You'll want to know: जादू
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C338-PARI338-02]; assesses=[] -->

**जादू** — *jādū* — "magic".

> परी ने जादू से पत्थर को फूल बनाया।
>
> *parī ne jādū se patthar ko phūl banāyā.*
>
> The fairy used magic to turn a stone into a flower.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**जादू** (*jādū*) is usually traced to Persian *jādū*, "magic". **जादू से** is by magic.
