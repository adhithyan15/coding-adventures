# അതിനെക്കാൾ (atinekkāḷ) — than that

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ML-LEX-C42-ADJ-01, ML-LEX-C58-ANSWER-01, ML-GRAMMAR-C89-NOMINAL-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "big", "more", and "a good one". (*valiya*, *kūṭutal*, *nallatŭ*.)

## Grammar Lens: X-നെക്കാൾ, "than X"
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-GRAMMAR-C346-EKKAL-01]; assesses=[ML-GRAMMAR-C89-NOMINAL-01, ML-LEX-C166-QUAL166-02, ML-LEX-C158-THINGS158-02, ML-LEX-C121-TRAVEL-03, ML-CONCEPT-C34-ISHTAM-01, ML-CONCEPT-C39-KAAPI-01, ML-CONCEPT-C39-CHAAYA-01] -->

Malayalam has no "bigger". It says "than that, this is big". Put the thing you compare against in
the object form, **അവനെ**, **അതിനെ**, **എന്നെ**, and add **-ക്കാൾ**:

> **ഇത് അതിനെക്കാൾ വലുതാണ്.**
>
> *itŭ atinekkāḷ valutāṇŭ.* — "This is bigger than that."

> **ട്രെയിൻ ബസിനെക്കാൾ വേഗത്തിൽ പോകും.**
>
> *ṭreyin basinekkāḷ vēgattil pōkuṁ.* — "The train goes faster than the bus."

> **എനിക്ക് ചായയെക്കാൾ കാപ്പിയാണ് ഇഷ്ടം.**
>
> *enikkŭ cāyayekkāḷ kāppiyāṇŭ iṣṭaṁ.* — "I like coffee more than tea."

You will also see it spelt **-ഏക്കാൾ**, as in **അതിനേക്കാൾ**; it is read the same way.

## Grammar Lens: ഏറ്റവും, "the most"
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-GRAMMAR-C346-ETTAVUM-01]; assesses=[ML-LEX-C42-ADJ-01, ML-LEX-C128-HOME-01, ML-LEX-C135-TOWN-05, ML-GRAMMAR-C89-NOMINAL-01] -->

Put **ഏറ്റവും** before the quality, and it is the top of the scale:

> **ഇത് ഗ്രാമത്തിലെ ഏറ്റവും വലിയ വീടാണ്.**
>
> *itŭ grāmattile ēṟṟavuṁ valiya vīṭāṇŭ.* — "This is the biggest house in the village."

**-ലെ** is "in, of" before a noun. **ഏറ്റവും നല്ലത്** is "the best one".
