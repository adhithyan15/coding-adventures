Tamil, chapter 360: Right and Wrong.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
நியாயம் (niyāyam) — fairness; what is right.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for uncertainty, then the Tamil for to reject.

You'll want to know: நியாயம்.
நியாயம் — niyāyam — "fairness; what is right".
அவருக்கு நடந்தது நியாயம் இல்லை. avarukku naṭantatu niyāyam illai. What happened to him is not fair.
நியாயமான, fair: நியாயமான விலை, a fair price.

The word, taken apart.
நியாயம் (niyāyam) is borrowed from Sanskrit nyāya, "rule, method" (CDIAL 7614); Pali has ñāya, "justice". Tamil breaks the cluster ny with a vowel: niyāyam.

Lesson 2 of 5.
நீதி (nīti) — justice.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for to reject, then the Tamil for fairness.

You'll want to know: நீதி.
நீதி — nīti — "justice".
பாதிக்கப்பட்ட மக்களுக்கு நீதி கிடைக்க வேண்டும். pātikkappaṭṭa makkaḷukku nīti kiṭaikka vēṇṭum. The people who were harmed must get justice.
நீதி (nīti) கேள், to demand justice. நீதிபதி, a judge, is நீதி (nīti) + பதி, master.

The word, taken apart.
நீதி (nīti) is borrowed from Sanskrit nīti, "conduct, guidance" (CDIAL 7546), from the root nī, "to lead"; Sinhala niya means "law".

Lesson 3 of 5.
அறம் (aṟam) — ethics; virtue.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for fairness, then the Tamil for justice.

You'll want to know: அறம்.
அறம் — aṟam — "ethics; virtue".
அறம் இல்லாத வாழ்க்கை பயனற்றது. aṟam illāta vāḻkkai payaṉaṟṟatu. A life without virtue is worthless.
An old Tamil word; அறவழி, the path of virtue. நீதி (nīti) is justice between people, அறம் (aṟam) the good in a person.

The word, taken apart.
அறம் (aṟam) is native Dravidian: DEDR 311 lists Tamil aṟam, "moral duty, virtue", and aṟavaṉ, "one who is virtuous".

Lesson 5 of 5.
சாக்கு (cākku) — an excuse; a pretext.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for ethics, then the Tamil for blame.

You'll want to know: சாக்கு.
சாக்கு — cākku — "an excuse; a pretext".
தாமதமாக வந்ததற்கு அவன் ஒரு சாக்கு சொன்னான். tāmatamāka vantataṟku avaṉ oru cākku coṉṉāṉ. He made an excuse for coming late.
சாக்குப்போக்கு, excuses, an echo pair. சாக்கு (cākku), a sack, sounds the same but is another word.

The word, taken apart.
சாக்கு (cākku) is native Dravidian: DEDR 2428 lists Tamil cākku, "excuse, pretext", with Kannada sāku and Telugu sāku, "pretence, plea".
