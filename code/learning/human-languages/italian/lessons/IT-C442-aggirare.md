## You'll want to know: aggirare
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C442-SALITA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**aggirare** — "to go round, to get round".

> Aggiriamo il centro per evitare il traffico.
>
> *We go round the centre to avoid the traffic.*

Also to get round a problem or a rule.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*aggirare* is built from *a-* and *girare* 'to turn', which is inherited from Latin *gyrāre*, from Greek *gŷros* 'circle' (Lewis & Short).

## What you've built

One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.
