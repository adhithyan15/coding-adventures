## You'll want to know: કીડી
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C238-HOUSE238-04]; assesses=[] -->

**કીડી** — *kīḍī* — "ant".

> **કીડીઓ ગોળ પાસે આવી.** — *kīḍīo goḷ pāse āvī* — "The ants came to the jaggery."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**કીડી** (*kīḍī*) is a tadbhava. It comes from Sanskrit *kīṭa*, "insect, worm", and CDIAL glosses the Gujarati word "ant" (CDIAL 3193).
