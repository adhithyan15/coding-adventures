## You'll want to know: ruǎnwò
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C407-PULLIN407-02]; assesses=[] -->

*ruǎnwò* — "soft sleeper".

> *ruǎnwò bǐ yìngzuò guì* — A soft sleeper costs more than a hard seat.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ruǎnwò* is built from *ruǎn* 'soft' and *wò* 'to lie down', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *nywenX* and *ngwaH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'soft' and the second 'to sleep'.
