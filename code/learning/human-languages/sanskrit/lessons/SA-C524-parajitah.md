## You'll want to know: पराजितः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C524-SPARDHA524-04]; assesses=[] -->

**पराजितः** — *parājitaḥ* — "beaten; defeated".

> मीरा पराजिता न अस्ति।
>
> *mīrā parājitā na asti.*
>
> Mira is not beaten.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पराजितः** (*parājitaḥ*) is built on *parā*, "away", and the root √ji, "to conquer", as its past participle; MW gives *parājita* as conquered, defeated, overthrown (MW).
