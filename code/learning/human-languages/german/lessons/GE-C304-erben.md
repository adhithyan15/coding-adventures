## You'll want to know: erben
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C304-TRAUER-05]; assesses=[] -->

**erben** — "to inherit".

> Sie erbte das Haus ihrer Großmutter.
>
> *She inherited her grandmother's house.*

*Das Erbe* — the inheritance.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*erben* is an inherited Germanic verb, which EtymDB traces to Proto-Germanic *arbijaną* "to inherit".
