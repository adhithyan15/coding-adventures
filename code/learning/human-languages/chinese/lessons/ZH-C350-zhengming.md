## You'll want to know: zhèngmíng
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C350-FACTS350-02]; assesses=[] -->

*zhèngmíng* — "to prove".

> *nǐ néng zhèngmíng nǐ shuō de huà ma* — Can you prove what you say?

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*zhèngmíng* is built from *zhèng* and 明 'bright', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *tsyingH* and *mjaeng* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to verify' and the second 'bright'.
