Kannada, chapter 363: Dusk Falls.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಮುಸ್ಸಂಜೆ (mussañje) — dusk; twilight.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to demolish, then the Kannada for a statue.

You'll want to know: ಮುಸ್ಸಂಜೆ.
ಮುಸ್ಸಂಜೆ — mussañje — "dusk; twilight".
ಮುಸ್ಸಂಜೆಯಲ್ಲಿ ಹಕ್ಕಿಗಳು ಗೂಡಿಗೆ ಹಿಂತಿರುಗುತ್ತವೆ. mussañjeyalli hakkigaḷu gūḍige hintiruguttave. At dusk the birds go back to their nests.
ಮುನ್, before, and ಸಂಜೆ, evening: the time as evening comes on.

The word, taken apart.
ಮುಸ್ಸಂಜೆ (mussañje) is a Kannada compound of native mun, "before" (DEDR 5020), and ಸಂಜೆ, "evening", borrowed from Sanskrit saṃdhyā, "juncture, twilight" (Monier-Williams).

ಮೂಡು — mūḍu — "to rise; to appear".
ಬೆಟ್ಟದ ಹಿಂದೆ ಚಂದ್ರ ಮೂಡಿತು. beṭṭada hinde candra mūḍitu. The moon rose behind the hill.
Past (it …): ಮೂಡಿತು, mūḍitu.
The sun, the moon, a doubt or a smile ಮೂಡು (mūḍu): ಮನಸ್ಸಿನಲ್ಲಿ ಪ್ರಶ್ನೆ ಮೂಡಿತು.

The word, taken apart.
ಮೂಡು (mūḍu) is native Dravidian: DEDR 5035 lists Kannada mūḍu, "to arise, to originate, to become visible", and mūḍa, "the east".

The word, taken apart.
ಮೊಳಗು (moḷagu) is native Dravidian: DEDR 4989 lists the older Kannada form of this verb, "to sound as musical instruments, to roar, to thunder".

Lesson 4 of 5.
ಹೊರಹೊಮ್ಮು (horahommu) — to emerge; to come out.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to rise, then the Kannada for to resound.

You'll want to know: ಹೊರಹೊಮ್ಮು.
ಹೊರಹೊಮ್ಮು — horahommu — "to emerge; to come out".
ಕತ್ತಲೆಯಿಂದ ಒಂದು ಬೆಳಕು ಹೊರಹೊಮ್ಮಿತು. kattaleyinda ondu beḷaku horahommitu. A light came out of the darkness.
Past (it …): ಹೊರಹೊಮ್ಮಿತು, horahommitu.
Also of results and talents: ಹೊಸ ಆಟಗಾರ ಹೊರಹೊಮ್ಮಿದನು.

The word, taken apart.
ಹೊರಹೊಮ್ಮು (horahommu) is built on native hora, "outside" (DEDR 4333), and hommu, "to rise, to spring up, to come forth", which DEDR 4482 lists only with a query, so its source is not settled.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ಗೋಚರಿಸು (gōcarisu) — to become visible; to show up.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to resound, then the Kannada for to emerge.

You'll want to know: ಗೋಚರಿಸು.
ಗೋಚರಿಸು — gōcarisu — "to become visible; to show up".
ಮಂಜು ಕರಗಿದ ಮೇಲೆ ಬೆಟ್ಟ ಗೋಚರಿಸಿತು. mañju karagida mēle beṭṭa gōcarisitu. After the mist melted away, the hill came into view.
Past (it …): ಗೋಚರಿಸಿತು, gōcarisitu.
A bookish verb; in talk people say ಕಾಣಿಸು.

The word, taken apart.
ಗೋಚರಿಸು (gōcarisu) is built on Sanskrit gocara, "pasture ground", and so "range, scope, what lies within the senses" (Monier-Williams), with the Kannada verb ending -isu.
