## You'll want to know: investigar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C521-PISTA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**investigar** — "to investigate".

> La policía investiga el caso.
>
> *The police are investigating the case.*

It takes *gu* before *e*: *investigué*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*investigar* is a learned borrowing from Latin *investīgāre* 'to track, to search after' (EtymDB), built on *vestīgium* 'a footprint'. To investigate is to follow the footprints.
