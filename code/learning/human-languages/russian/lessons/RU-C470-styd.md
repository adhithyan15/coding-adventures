## You'll want to know: стыд
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C470-SHAME-01]; assesses=[] -->

**стыд** (*styd*) — "shame".

> От стыда он не мог сказать ни слова.
>
> *Ot stydá on ne mog skazát ni slóva.*
>
> He couldn't say a word for shame.

Masculine; genitive стыда. *Мне стыдно* — I feel ashamed.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*стыд* is inherited from Proto-Slavic *stydъ* (EtymDB); it is usually linked to the root of *стыть* 'to grow cold'.
