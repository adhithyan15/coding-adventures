## You'll want to know: યોદ્ધો
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C322-HUMLO322-03]; assesses=[] -->

**યોદ્ધો** — *yoddho* — "a warrior".

> ગામના યોદ્ધા બહાદુર હતા.
>
> *gāmnā yoddhā bahādur hatā.*
>
> The village's warriors were brave.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

goes back to Sanskrit *yoddhṛ*, "a fighter, a warrior" (Monier-Williams), made into a Gujarati noun in *-o*: **યોદ્ધો**, plural **યોદ્ધા**.
