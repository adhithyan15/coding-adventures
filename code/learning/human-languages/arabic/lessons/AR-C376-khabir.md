## You'll want to know: خبير
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C376-ILM376-04]; assesses=[] -->

**خبير** — *khabīr* — "an expert".

> سأل الصحفي خبيرا في التاريخ.
>
> *saʾala aṣ-ṣaḥafī khabīran fī at-tārīkh.*
>
> The journalist asked an expert on history.

Plural: خبراء, *khubarāʾ*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**خبير** (*khabīr*) is built on the native Arabic root *kh-b-r*, "to know thoroughly, to test" (Lane). A *khabīr* knows a thing from the inside: an expert.
