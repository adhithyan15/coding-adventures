## You'll want to know: 报考
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C494-GAOKAO494-03]; assesses=[] -->

**报考** — *bàokǎo* — "to enter for an exam; to apply to a school by exam".

> *wǒ xiǎng bàokǎo Běijīng de dàxué* — I want to apply to universities in Beijing.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**报考** (*bàokǎo*) is built from 报 'to report' and 考 'to examine', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *pawH* and *khawX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to report'.
