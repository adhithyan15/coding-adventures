# సాహసం (sāhasaṁ) — daring; boldness

## You'll want to know: సాహసం
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C508-DHAIRYAM508-04]; assesses=[] -->

**సాహసం** — *sāhasaṁ* — "daring; boldness".

> రాత్రి ఒంటరిగా అడవిలో నడవడం సాహసమే.
>
> *rātri oṇṭarigā aḍavilō naḍavaḍaṁ sāhasamē.*
>
> Walking alone in the forest at night is daring indeed.

సాహసంగా, boldly; సాహసి, a daring person.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**సాహసం** (*sāhasaṁ*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *sāhasa*, "boldness; aggression" (CDIAL 13378).
