## You'll want to know: لپٹنا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C318-EIDMORNING318-05]; assesses=[] -->

**لپٹنا** — *lipaṭnā* — "to cling; to hug".

> بچی ماں سے لپٹ گئی۔
>
> *bachchī māṅ se lipaṭ gaī.*
>
> The little girl clung to her mother.

Past (she …): وہ لپٹ گئی, *voh lipaṭ gaī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**لپٹنا** (*lipaṭnā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *lipaṭnā*, "to stick", under Sanskrit *lipyatē* (CDIAL 11061). The person you cling to takes **سے**: **ماں سے لپٹ گئی**.
