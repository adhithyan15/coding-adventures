## You'll want to know: shāndǐng
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C435-SUMMIT435-02]; assesses=[] -->

*shāndǐng* — "top of a mountain; summit".

> *cóng zhèr wǎng qián zǒu shí fēnzhōng jiù dào shāndǐng le* — Walk on for ten minutes from here and you'll be at the top.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*shāndǐng* is built from 山 'a mountain' and *dǐng* 'the top', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *srean* and *tengX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second as the crown of the head.
