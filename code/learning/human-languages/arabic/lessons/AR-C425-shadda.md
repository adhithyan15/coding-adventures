# شد (shadda) — to tighten; to fasten

## You'll want to know: شد
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C425-RAKIB425-04]; assesses=[] -->

**شد** — *shadda* — "to tighten; to fasten".

> شد الراكب حزامه قبل الهبوط.
>
> *shadda ar-rākib ḥizāmahu qabla al-hubūṭ.*
>
> The passenger fastened his belt before landing.

Present: يشد, *yashudd*.

A doubled verb: *shadadtu*, "I tightened", unfolds the two d's.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**شد** (*shadda*) is built on the native Arabic root *sh-d-d*, "to be firm; to bind tightly" (Lane). It is the plain verb of a doubled root.
