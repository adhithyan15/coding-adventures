## You'll want to know: ぺらぺら
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-ANCHOR-PERAPERA]; assesses=[JA-SCRIPT-RA-01] -->

**ぺらぺら** — *perapera* — "fluent, fluently": it says that someone speaks a
language with ease. It is one of the many doubled words Japanese uses for a
sound or a manner: *pera*, and *pera* again.

Four beats: *pe–ra–pe–ra*. **ら** you already write. The other sign, twice,
is **へ** with the small circle, so *he* becomes *pe*. The next lesson
writes it.
