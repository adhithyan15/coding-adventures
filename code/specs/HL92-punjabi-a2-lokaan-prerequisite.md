# HL92 — Punjabi A2 `ਲੋਕਾਂ` prerequisite

Issue: #18029

The confidential direct Punjabi A2 mock audit against exact merged main
`f49ed80b41b7ca3403103d81088a7701c1263457` finds `ਲੋਕਾਂ` four times without
an instructional owner: three learner-paper occurrences and one confidential
examiner occurrence. Punjabi already teaches direct plural `ਲੋਕ`; the missing
step is its plural form before a postposition.

Chapter 192 closes that narrow form gap without editing either mock. The ramp
first retrieves `ਲੋਕ` and familiar postpositions, then directly owns `ਲੋਕਾਂ`
inside one bounded `ਲੋਕਾਂ ਨਾਲ` frame. A separate retrieval lesson stabilizes
the written form before later lessons move it through familiar `ਲਈ` and `ਤੋਂ`
frames. The chapter ends with a four-skill contrast between standalone `ਲੋਕ`
and pre-postposition `ਲੋਕਾਂ`.

`ਲੋਕਾਂ` has one direct owner and at least two later retrieval lessons. All
supporting Punjabi must occur before the chapter, and the target must not leak
into authored, generated-book, or narration copy before its owner. The chapter
must not present a broad noun-declension table or imply that every Punjabi noun
changes the same way. Examiner-only `ਸ਼ੁਰੂ` and all other mock gaps stay out of
scope. This is a prerequisite tranche, not mock-readiness or attainment
evidence. Authored and generated ownership remains sharded by language,
chapter, lesson, or test case; no shared aggregate is introduced.
