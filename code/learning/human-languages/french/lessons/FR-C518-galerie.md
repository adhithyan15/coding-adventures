## You'll want to know: la galerie
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C518-EXPOSITION-03]; assesses=[] -->

**la galerie** — "gallery; arcade".

> Cette galerie vend les tableaux de jeunes artistes.
>
> *This gallery sells paintings by young artists.*

*Une galerie marchande* — a shopping arcade.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*la galerie* was borrowed from Italian *galleria*, from Medieval Latin *galeria*. The earlier origin is not settled.
