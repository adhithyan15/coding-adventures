## You'll want to know: まず
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C171-ORDER171-02]; assesses=[] -->

**まず** — *mazu* — "first of all; to begin with".

> **あしたは まず かおを あらいます。** — *ashita wa mazu kao o araimasu* — "Tomorrow I will wash my face first of all."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**まず** (*mazu*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, and its older origin is not settled. It is usually written in kana; its kanji is a later kun'yomi (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
