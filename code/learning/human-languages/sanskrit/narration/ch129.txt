Sanskrit, chapter 129: Cool, New, Ancient, Brave, Clever.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
शीतलः (śītalaḥ) — cool.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for sour, then the Sanskrit for bitter.

You'll want to know: शीतलः.
शीतलः — śītalaḥ — "cool".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: śītalaḥ]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: śītalaḥ, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say śītalaḥ]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Sanskrit for sour, then the Sanskrit for bitter, then say śītalaḥ again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does शीतलः (śītalaḥ) mean? ("Cool".) Say it once more.

[your turn — say: silver, glass, a cart, a pot, a cot]

[your turn — say: a seat, a bicycle, a vehicle, a ship, a train]

(वीणा, vīṇā.) A cloth? (पट्टः, paṭṭaḥ.) A jewel? (रत्नम्, ratnam.) Gold? (सुवर्णम्, suvarṇam.) Silver? (रजतम्, rajatam.) Glass? (काचः, kācaḥ.) A cart? (शकटम्, śakaṭam.) A pot? (भाण्डम्, bhāṇḍam.) A cot? (खट्वा, khaṭvā.) A seat? (पीठम्, pīṭham.) A bicycle? (द्विचक्रिका, dvicakrikā.) A vehicle? (यानम्, yānam.) A ship? (नौयानम्, nauyānam.) A train? (धूमयानम्, dhūmayānam.) A cloth? (पटः, paṭaḥ.) A lake? (सरोवरः, sarovaraḥ.) A riverbank? (नदीतीरम्, nadītīram.) A cuckoo? (कोकिलः, kokilaḥ.) A sparrow? (चाटकः, cāṭakaḥ.)
