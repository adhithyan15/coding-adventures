Bengali, chapter 82: Behind, Shop, Road, Lane, Station.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
পিছনে (pichhône) — behind.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for below, then the Bengali for in front.

You'll want to know: পিছনে.
পিছনে — pichhône — "behind".

The word, taken apart.
পিছনে (pichhône) is a form of pichhôn, "the back". CDIAL lists Bengali pichā, "the rear", under paśca, "hinder part", crossed with Sanskrit pṛṣṭha, "back" (CDIAL 7990).

You'll want to know: দোকান.
দোকান — dokān — "a shop".

The word, taken apart.
দোকান (dokān) is a loan from Arabic dukkān, "shop", which reached Bengali through Persian. Its Arabic root is d-k-n (Lane).

Lesson 4 of 5.
গলি (gôli) — a lane.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a shop, then the Bengali for a road.

You'll want to know: গলি.
গলি — gôli — "a lane".

The word, taken apart.
গলি (gôli) is a tadbhava, a word inherited and reshaped by sound change. It comes from galī, "defile, lane", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 4085).
