## You'll want to know: मामूली
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C388-PARYAPT388-04]; assesses=[] -->

**मामूली** — *māmūlī* — "minor, ordinary".

> अगर चोट मामूली है तो पट्टी काफ़ी है।
>
> *agar coṭ māmūlī hai to paṭṭī kāfī hai.*
>
> If the injury is minor, a bandage is enough.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**मामूली** (*māmūlī*) is usually traced to Arabic *maʿmūlī*, "customary", which reached Hindi through Persian.
