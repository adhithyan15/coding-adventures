## You'll want to know: освобождать
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C324-POBEDA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**освобождать** (*osvobozhdát*) — "to free, to liberate; to vacate".

> Солдаты освободили город.
>
> *Soldáty osvobodíli górod.*
>
> The soldiers liberated the town.

Imperfective; perfective *освободить* (*osvobodít*), *освобожу*. Also to free up: *освободить место*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*освобождать* is built on *о-* and *свобода* "freedom", in the Church Slavonic shape *-жд-*; EtymDB lists *освободить* with no parent.
