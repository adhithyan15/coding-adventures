## You'll want to know: jiǎngjià
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C193-PRICE193-04]; assesses=[] -->

*jiǎngjià* — "to haggle".

> *zhèr néng jiǎngjià ma* — Can you haggle here?

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*jiǎngjià* is built from *jiǎng* 'to talk over' and *jià* 'price', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *kaewngX* and *kaeH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to tell' and 'to discuss': to talk the price over.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for asking for what I want.
