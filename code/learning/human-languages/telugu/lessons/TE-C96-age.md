## You'll want to know: వయసు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C96-NAMES-03]; assesses=[] -->

**వయసు** (*vayasu*) — "age".

You have asked it already, inside **నీ వయసెంత?**. Now the word stands alone: **మీ వయసు ఎంత?** —
"what is your age?", the respectful form.
