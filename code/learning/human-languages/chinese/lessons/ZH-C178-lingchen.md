## You'll want to know: língchén
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C178-DAY178-05]; assesses=[] -->

*língchén* — "the small hours; before dawn".

> *wǒ língchén sì diǎn qǐchuáng le* — I got up at four in the morning.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*língchén* is built from *líng* and *chén*, both inherited from Middle Chinese, *ling* and *dzyin* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription). The dictionary glosses the first 'ice' and the second 'early' and 'dawn'; how the first came into the word is not settled.
