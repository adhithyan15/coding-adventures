Persian, chapter 186: To knit, To shave, To drip, To shiver, To crawl.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
بافتن (bâftan) — to knit, to weave.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to borrow, then the Persian for to lend.

You'll want to know: بافتن.
بافتن — bâftan — "to knit, to weave".

The word, taken apart.
bâftan is a native Persian verb, inherited rather than borrowed: EtymDB traces it to the Middle Persian stem waf-, "to weave", and it is usually set beside English weave as one Indo-European root.
