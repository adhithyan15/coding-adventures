## You'll want to know: أناني
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C500-GHURUR500-03]; assesses=[] -->

**أناني** — *anānī* — "selfish".

> الطفل الأناني لا يشارك أخاه في ألعابه.
>
> *aṭ-ṭifl al-anānī lā yushāriku akhāhu fī alʿābihi.*
>
> The selfish child does not share his toys with his brother.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**أناني** (*anānī*) is a modern Arabic word, usually traced to the pronoun *anā*, "I", with the adjective ending *-ī*: a person always saying I. The noun *anāniyya* is selfishness.
