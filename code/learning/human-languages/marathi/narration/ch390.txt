Marathi, chapter 390: On the Whole.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
एकूण (ekūṇ) — in total; overall.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for purely, then the Marathi for usually.

You'll want to know: एकूण.
एकूण — ekūṇ — "in total; overall".
वर्गात एकूण तीस मुलं आहेत. vargāt ekūṇ tīs mulaṁ āhet. There are thirty children in the class in all.
Also an adjective: एकूण (ekūṇ) खर्च, the total cost.

The word, taken apart.
एकूण (ekūṇ) is a Marathi word whose origin is not settled.

Lesson 2 of 5.
एकंदरीत (ekandarīt) — on the whole.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for usually, then the Marathi for in total.

You'll want to know: एकंदरीत.
एकंदरीत — ekandarīt — "on the whole".
एकंदरीत सहल छान झाली. ekandarīt sahal chān jhālī. On the whole, the trip went well.
एकंदर, whole, + -ईत.

The word, taken apart.
एकंदरीत (ekandarīt) is built on एकंदर, "whole, total", with the ending -ईत, "in"; the origin of एकंदर is not settled.

Lesson 3 of 5.
तत्त्व (tattva) — a principle.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for in total, then the Marathi for on the whole.

You'll want to know: तत्त्व.
तत्त्व — tattva — "a principle".
खोटं न बोलणं हे त्याचं तत्त्व आहे. khoṭaṁ na bolṇaṁ he tyācaṁ tattva āhe. Not telling lies is his principle.
Neuter.
तत्त्वतः, in principle.

The word, taken apart.
तत्त्व (tattva) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit tattva, "real state, truth" (CDIAL 5642), from tat, "that": thatness.

Lesson 5 of 5.
व्यतिरिक्त (vyatirikta) — apart from; besides.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a principle, then the Marathi for particular.

You'll want to know: व्यतिरिक्त.
व्यतिरिक्त — vyatirikta — "apart from; besides".
मराठीच्या व्यतिरिक्त त्याला आणखी दोन भाषा येतात. marāṭhīcyā vyatirikta tyālā āṇkhī don bhāṣā yetāt. Apart from Marathi he knows two more languages.
After -च्या, or attached: मराठीव्यतिरिक्त.

The word, taken apart.
व्यतिरिक्त (vyatirikta) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit vyatirikta, "left apart, excluded" (Monier-Williams).
