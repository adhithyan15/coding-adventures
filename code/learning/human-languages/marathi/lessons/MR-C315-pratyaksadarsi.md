## You'll want to know: प्रत्यक्षदर्शी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C315-ANK315-01]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रत्यक्षदर्शी** — *pratyakṣadarśī* — "an eyewitness".

> एका प्रत्यक्षदर्शीने पोलिसांना सगळं सांगितलं.
>
> *ekā pratyakṣadarśīne polisānnā sagḷaṁ sāṅgitlaṁ.*
>
> An eyewitness told the police everything.

Masculine.

प्रत्यक्ष, before the eyes, + -दर्शी, one who sees.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रत्यक्षदर्शी** (*pratyakṣadarśī*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *pratyakṣadarśin*, 'one who has seen with his own eyes' (Monier-Williams).
