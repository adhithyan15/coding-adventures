# నిషేధించు (niṣēdhiñcu) — to ban; to prohibit

## You'll want to know: నిషేధించు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C398-CATTAM398-05]; assesses=[] -->

**నిషేధించు** — *niṣēdhiñcu* — "to ban; to prohibit".

> పాఠశాలలో ఫోన్లను నిషేధించారు.
>
> *pāṭhaśālalō phōnlanu niṣēdhiñcāru.*
>
> They banned phones at the school.

Past (I …): నిషేధించాను, *niṣēdhiñcānu*.

The noun is నిషేధం, a ban.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**నిషేధించు** (*niṣēdhiñcu*) is built on Sanskrit *niṣedha*, "warding off, hindering, prohibition" (Monier-Williams), with the Telugu verb ending -ఇంచు.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
