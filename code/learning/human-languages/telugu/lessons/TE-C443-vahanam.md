# వాహనం (vāhanaṁ) — a vehicle

## You'll want to know: వాహనం
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C443-VAHANAM443-01]; assesses=[] -->

**వాహనం** — *vāhanaṁ* — "a vehicle".

> ఆ రోడ్డు మీద పెద్ద వాహనాలు వెళ్ళకూడదు.
>
> *ā rōḍḍu mīda pedda vāhanālu veḷḷakūḍadu.*
>
> Big vehicles must not go on that road.

Plural: వాహనాలు, *vāhanālu*.

Plural వాహనాలు, the -ం noun plural in -ాలు. It covers every vehicle, from a bicycle to a lorry.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**వాహనం** (*vāhanaṁ*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *vāhana*, "any vehicle or draught animal", from the root *vah*, "to carry" (CDIAL 11610).
