Gujarati, chapter 470: Face and Hair.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
ચહેરો (cahero) — a face.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for sluggish, then the Gujarati for steady effort.

You'll want to know: ચહેરો.
ચહેરો — cahero — "a face".
મીરાનો ચહેરો ખુશીથી ચમકતો હતો. Mīrāno cahero khuśīthī camakto hato. Mira's face was shining with happiness.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ચહેરો (cahero) is usually traced to Persian čihra, "face, appearance".

દાદી રોજ સવારે મીરાનો ચોટલો ગૂંથે છે. dādī roj savāre Mīrāno coṭlo gū̃the che. Every morning my grandmother plaits Mira's hair.

Lesson 4 of 6.
લટ (laṭ) — a lock of hair.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for an eyelash, then the Gujarati for a plait.

You'll want to know: લટ.
લટ — laṭ — "a lock of hair".
પવનથી તેના કપાળ પર એક લટ આવી ગઈ. pavanthī tenā kapāḷ par ek laṭ āvī gaī. The wind blew a lock of hair onto her forehead.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
લટ (laṭ) probably goes back to a reconstructed laṭṭa-, "hanging, tangled", under which CDIAL lists the Gujarati laṭiyā̃, "matted locks" (CDIAL 10918).

મીરાની પાંથી વચ્ચે છે. Mīrānī pā̃thī vacce che. Mira parts her hair in the middle.
