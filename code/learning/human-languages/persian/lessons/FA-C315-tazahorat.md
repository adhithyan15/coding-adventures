## You'll want to know: تظاهرات
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C315-KHABARFOWRI-04]; assesses=[] -->

**تظاهرات** (*tazâhorât*) — "a demonstration, a protest march".

> دیروز جلوی دانشگاه تظاهرات بود.
>
> *Diruz jelo-ye dâneshgâh tazâhorât bud.*
>
> Yesterday there was a demonstration outside the university.

Plural in form, but one event. Built on Arabic *taẓāhur* "a public show".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*tazâhorât* is usually traced to Arabic *taẓāhurāt*, the plural of *taẓāhur* "a public show"; EtymDB lists no entry.
