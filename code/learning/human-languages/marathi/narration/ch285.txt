Marathi, chapter 285: Bigger, Biggest, and How Long.
3 lessons. All 3 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 3.
-पेक्षा (-pekṣā) — than, and the most.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say "I want to go to Pune". (malā puṇyālā jāycaṁ āhe.) Then "fast". (jalad.)

You'll want to know: -पेक्षा, 'than'.
गाडी बसपेक्षा जलद आहे. gāḍī baspekṣā jalad āhe. — "The train is faster than the bus."
-पेक्षा (-pekṣā) goes on the thing you compare against, in its bent form: घर becomes घरापेक्षा (gharāpekṣā), चहा becomes चहापेक्षा (cahāpekṣā). The adjective stays as it is; Marathi needs no "-er". For a quantity, add जास्त (jāsta, "more") or कमी, "less": मला चहापेक्षा दूध जास्त आवडतं (malā cahāpekṣā dūdh jāsta āvaḍtaṁ), "I like milk more than tea".

You'll want to know: सर्वात, 'the most'.
मीरा वर्गात सर्वात उंच आहे. mīrā vargāt sarvāt uñc āhe. — "Meera is the tallest in the class."
सर्वात (sarvāt) is "of all": सर्वात मोठा (sarvāt moṭhā), "the biggest"; सर्वात जवळ (sarvāt javaḷ), "the nearest".

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: "The train is faster than the bus" — gāḍī baspekṣā jalad āhe]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: "I like milk more than tea" — malā cahāpekṣā dūdh jāsta āvaḍtaṁ]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: "Meera is the tallest in the class" — mīrā vargāt sarvāt uñc āhe]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[question — say your answer, then pause 24 seconds]
Say that the train is faster than the bus, then that Meera is the tallest.

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
"Than the house"? (घरापेक्षा.) "More"? (जास्त.) "The biggest"? (सर्वात मोठा.)


Lesson 2 of 3.
तीन वर्षांपासून (tīn varṣāmpāsūn) — how long.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say "the train is faster than the bus". (gāḍī baspekṣā jalad āhe.) Then "from" and "until". (pāsūn, paryant.)

You'll want to know: -पासून, 'for (and still)'.
मी तीन वर्षांपासून पुण्यात राहतो. mī tīn varṣāmpāsūn puṇyāt rāhto. — "I have lived in Pune for three years."
Marathi keeps the present: the living still goes on. -पासून goes on the stretch of time, in its bent plural: वर्ष becomes वर्षांपासून (varṣāmpāsūn), तास becomes तासांपासून (tāsāmpāsūn). To ask: तुम्ही किती दिवसांपासून इथे आहात? (tumhī kitī divsāmpāsūn ithe āhāt?), "How long have you been here?"

You'll want to know: from … until, and all … long.
मी सकाळपासून संध्याकाळपर्यंत काम केलं. mī sakāḷpāsūn sandhyākāḷparyant kām kelaṁ. — "I worked from morning until evening."
-पासून … -पर्यंत frames a stretch with two ends. -भर, as in दिवसभर, fills it: दिवसभर पाऊस पडत होता (divasbhar pāūs paḍat hotā), "it rained all day". A bare number of hours needs no ending: मी दोन तास वाचत होतो (mī don tās vācat hoto).

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: "I have lived in Pune for three years" — mī tīn varṣāmpāsūn puṇyāt rāhto]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: "How long have you been here?" — tumhī kitī divsāmpāsūn ithe āhāt?]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: "I worked from morning until evening" — mī sakāḷpāsūn sandhyākāḷparyant kām kelaṁ]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[question — say your answer, then pause 24 seconds]
Say that you have lived in Pune for three years, then that it rained all day.

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
"For three years"? (तीन वर्षांपासून (tīn varṣāmpāsūn).) Which tense goes with it, for something still going on? (The present: राहतो.) "From morning until evening"? (सकाळपासून संध्याकाळपर्यंत.)


Lesson 3 of 3.
Review — comparing and time.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say "I got angry" and "I want to become a teacher". (malā rāg ālā, malā śikṣak vhāycaṁ āhe.) Ask what time the Pune train leaves. (puṇyācī gāḍī kitī vājtā nighte?) Then ऋतू and ज्ञान, and the letters ऋ and ञ.

Guided Practice.
[pause 2 seconds after each]
[your turn — say: In my opinion the train is better than the bus.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: I like the monsoon season most, with ऋतू.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: I have lived in Pune for three years.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: Yesterday it rained all day. I read from morning until evening.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: how long you have been learning Marathi, with -पासून]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
"Than"? (-पेक्षा (-pekṣā).) "The most"? (सर्वात.) "For three years"? (तीन वर्षांपासून (tīn varṣāmpāsūn).) "All day"? (दिवसभर.)
