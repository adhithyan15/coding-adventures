## You'll want to know: ترانه
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C519-SINGING-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ترانه** (*tarâne*) — "a song (popular)".

> این ترانه را از رادیو شنیدم.
>
> *in tarâne râ az râdiyo shenidam.*
>
> I heard this song on the radio.

A popular song; *âhang* is the tune, *tarâne* the song with words.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*tarâne* is usually taken as a native Persian word for a song, but EtymDB lists it without a source, and its origin is not settled.
