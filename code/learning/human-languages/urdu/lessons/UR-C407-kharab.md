## You'll want to know: خراب
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C407-MARAMMAT407-01]; assesses=[] -->

**خراب** — *kharāb* — "out of order; bad".

> کمرے کا پنکھا خراب ہے۔
>
> *kamre kā pankhā kharāb hai.*
>
> The fan in the room isn't working.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *kharāb*, "ruin, desolation"; in Urdu a broken fan, spoilt food and bad weather are all **خراب**.
