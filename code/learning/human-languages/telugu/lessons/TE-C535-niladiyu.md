# నిలదీయు (niladīyu) — to call to account; to confront

## You'll want to know: నిలదీయు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C535-EGATALI535-04]; assesses=[] -->

**నిలదీయు** — *niladīyu* — "to call to account; to confront".

> డబ్బు గురించి గ్రామస్థులు అధికారిని నిలదీశారు.
>
> *ḍabbu guriñci grāmasthulu adhikārini niladīśāru.*
>
> The villagers confronted the official about the money.

Past (I …): నిలదీశాను, *niladīśānu*.

To stop someone and demand an answer.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**నిలదీయు** (*niladīyu*) is a native compound: *nilucu*, "to stand, to stop" (DEDR 3675), and *tīyu*, "to draw, to pull" (DEDR 3407): to pull someone up short.
