## You'll want to know: condiscipulus, condiscipulī
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C401-PRAECEPTOR-05]; assesses=[] -->

**condiscipulus, condiscipulī** — "a schoolfellow, a fellow pupil".

> Condiscipulī in eādem scholā sedēbant.
>
> *The schoolfellows sat in the same school.*

*condiscipulus, condiscipulī* m., *con-* with + *discipulus*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*condiscipulus* is built from *con-* "with" and *discipulus* "a pupil" (Lewis & Short).
