# ಹಣದುಬ್ಬರ (haṇadubbara) — inflation

## You'll want to know: ಹಣದುಬ್ಬರ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C421-BEDIKE421-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ಹಣದುಬ್ಬರ** — *haṇadubbara* — "inflation".

> ಹಣದುಬ್ಬರದಿಂದ ಎಲ್ಲದರ ಬೆಲೆ ಏರಿತು.
>
> *haṇadubbaradinda elladara bele ēritu.*
>
> With inflation the price of everything went up.

Word by word: money swelling.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಹಣದುಬ್ಬರ** (*haṇadubbara*) is a modern Kannada word built from *haṇa*, "money", and *ubbara*, "a swelling", from native *ubbu*, "to swell" (DEDR 666).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
