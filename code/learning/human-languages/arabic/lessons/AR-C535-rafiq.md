## You'll want to know: رفيق
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C535-RIFAQ535-01]; assesses=[] -->

**رفيق** — *rafīq* — "a companion".

> كان الكلب رفيق جدي في الجبل.
>
> *kāna al-kalb rafīq jaddī fī al-jabal.*
>
> The dog was my grandfather's companion in the mountains.

Plural: رفاق, *rifāq*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**رفيق** (*rafīq*) is built on the native Arabic root *r-f-q*, "to be gentle; to be a companion" (Lane). A rafīq goes along with you.

## What you've built

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
