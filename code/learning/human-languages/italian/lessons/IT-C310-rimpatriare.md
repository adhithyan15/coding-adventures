## You'll want to know: rimpatriare
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C310-TRASFERIRSI-05]; assesses=[] -->

**rimpatriare** — "to return to one's country; to repatriate".

> Dopo vent'anni all'estero è rimpatriato.
>
> *After twenty years abroad he went back home.*

Built on *patria*, ‘homeland’.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*rimpatriare* is built on *patria* "homeland", from Latin *patria*, built on *pater* "father" (Lewis & Short).
