## You'll want to know: انگارا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C287-AAG287-01]; assesses=[] -->

**انگارا** — *angārā* — "an ember; a live coal".

> چولہے میں ایک انگارا تھا۔
>
> *chūlhe meṅ ek angārā thā.*
>
> There was an ember in the stove.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**انگارا** (*angārā*) is inherited from Sanskrit *áṅgāra*, "glowing charcoal" (CDIAL 125), where CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *ãgārā*.
