Marathi, chapter 521: Cracks and Repairs.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
भेग (bheg) — a fissure; a crack in the ground.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a drain, then the Marathi for to get blocked.

You'll want to know: भेग.
भेग — bheg — "a fissure; a crack in the ground".
उन्हाळ्यात शेतात मोठ्या भेगा पडल्या. unhāḷyāt śetāt moṭhyā bhegā paḍlyā. In the summer big cracks opened in the field.
Feminine. Plural: भेगा, bhegā.
भेग (bheg) पडणे, a crack opens.

The word, taken apart.
भेग (bheg) is the word for a split in dry earth or a wall; its origin is not settled.

Lesson 2 of 5.
तडकणे (taḍakṇe) — to crack.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to get blocked, then the Marathi for a fissure.

You'll want to know: तडकणे.
तडकणे — taḍakṇe — "to crack".
खाली पडल्यावर आरसा तडकला. khālī paḍlyāvar ārsā taḍaklā. The mirror cracked when it fell.
Past (he …): तो आरसा तडकला, to ārsā taḍaklā.

The word, taken apart.
तडकणे (taḍakṇe) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi taḍakṇẽ, "to crack", with the sound word traṭ, "crackle" (CDIAL 5988).

Lesson 3 of 5.
गोंद (gond) — glue; gum.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a fissure, then the Marathi for to crack.

You'll want to know: गोंद.
गोंद — gond — "glue; gum".
मी तुटलेलं खेळणं गोंदाने चिकटवलं. mī tuṭlelaṁ kheḷṇaṁ gondāne cikṭavlaṁ. I stuck the broken toy together with glue.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
गोंद (gond) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi gõd, "gum", beside Hindi gõd, under Sanskrit gundra, the name of a reed (CDIAL 4199). How a reed's name came to mean gum is not explained there.

Lesson 4 of 5.

You'll want to know: करवत.
करवत — karvat — "a saw".
सुताराने करवतीने लाकूड कापलं. sutārāne karavtīne lākūḍ kāplaṁ. The carpenter cut the wood with a saw.
Feminine.
The oblique is करवती-: करवतीने, with a saw.

The word, taken apart.
करवत (karvat) is a tadbhava, inherited from Sanskrit karapattra, "saw" (CDIAL 2795).

Lesson 5 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for glue, then the Marathi for a saw.

You'll want to know: लिंपणे.
लिंपणे — limpṇe — "to plaster; to smear over".
गवंड्याने भिंतीतली भेग मातीने लिंपली. gavaṇḍyāne bhintītlī bheg mātīne limplī. The mason plastered the crack in the wall with mud.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने भिंत लिंपली, tyāne bhint limplī.

The word, taken apart.
लिंपणे (limpṇe) is a tadbhava, inherited from Sanskrit limpati, "smears" (CDIAL 11066).
