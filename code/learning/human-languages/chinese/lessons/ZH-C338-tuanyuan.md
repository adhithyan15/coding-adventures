## You'll want to know: tuányuán
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C338-VISIT338-03]; assesses=[] -->

*tuányuán* — "to be reunited as a family".

> *guònián de shíhou jiārén dōu huílai tuányuán* — At the new year the whole family comes back together.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*tuányuán* is built from *tuán* and *yuán*, both inherited from Middle Chinese, *dwan* and *hjwen* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first with this very word and the second 'the round body of the heavens'.
