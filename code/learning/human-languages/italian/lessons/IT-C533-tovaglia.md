## You'll want to know: la tovaglia
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C533-TAVOLA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**la tovaglia** — "tablecloth".

> Per la festa mettiamo la tovaglia bianca.
>
> *For the party we put on the white tablecloth.*

*Una tovaglia a quadri* — a checked tablecloth.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*tovaglia* comes from a Germanic word, Frankish *thwahillja* "a cloth" (EtymDB, under English *towel*), the same source as English *towel*.
