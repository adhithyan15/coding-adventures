Kannada, chapter 411: Terms and Conditions.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
ಷರತ್ತು (ṣarattu) — a condition; a term.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for above all, then the Kannada for not only that.

You'll want to know: ಷರತ್ತು.
ಷರತ್ತು — ṣarattu — "a condition; a term".
ಒಂದು ಷರತ್ತಿನ ಮೇಲೆ ನಾನು ಒಪ್ಪುತ್ತೇನೆ. ondu ṣarattina mēle nānu opputtēne. I agree on one condition.
ಷರತ್ತಿನ ಮೇಲೆ, on condition. ಷರತ್ತುಗಳು, terms.

The word, taken apart.
ಷರತ್ತು (ṣarattu) is a loan from Arabic sharṭ, "a condition", which reached Kannada through Hindustani.

What you've built.
One more word for saying what happens if.

ಎರಡು ಕಂಪನಿಗಳು ಒಂದು ಒಪ್ಪಂದ ಮಾಡಿಕೊಂಡವು. eraḍu kampanigaḷu ondu oppanda māḍikoṇḍavu. The two companies made an agreement.

Lesson 3 of 6.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a condition, then the Kannada for an agreement.

You'll want to know: ಒಡಂಬಡಿಕೆ.
ಒಡಂಬಡಿಕೆ — oḍambaḍike — "a pact; a treaty".
ಎರಡು ದೇಶಗಳ ನಡುವೆ ಹೊಸ ಒಡಂಬಡಿಕೆ ಆಯಿತು. eraḍu dēśagaḷa naḍuve hosa oḍambaḍike āyitu. A new treaty was made between the two countries.
More formal than ಒಪ್ಪಂದ (oppanda).

The word, taken apart.

Lesson 4 of 6.
ಈಡೇರಿಸು (īḍērisu) — to fulfil.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for an agreement, then the Kannada for a pact.

You'll want to know: ಈಡೇರಿಸು.
ಈಡೇರಿಸು — īḍērisu — "to fulfil".
ಅಪ್ಪ ನನ್ನ ಆಸೆಯನ್ನು ಈಡೇರಿಸಿದರು. appa nanna āseyannu īḍērisidaru. Father fulfilled my wish.
Past (I …): ಈಡೇರಿಸಿದೆ, īḍēriside.
ಈಡೇರು, to come true: ಆಸೆ ಈಡೇರಿತು.

The word, taken apart.
ಈಡೇರಿಸು (īḍērisu) is built as the causative of īḍēru, "to be fulfilled"; its origin is not settled.

Lesson 5 of 6.
ಬಾಧ್ಯತೆ (bādhyate) — an obligation.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a pact, then the Kannada for to fulfil.

You'll want to know: ಬಾಧ್ಯತೆ.
ಬಾಧ್ಯತೆ — bādhyate — "an obligation".
ಸಾಲ ತೀರಿಸುವುದು ನನ್ನ ಬಾಧ್ಯತೆ. sāla tīrisuvudu nanna bādhyate. Paying off the loan is my obligation.
ಬಾಧ್ಯತೆ (bādhyate) ಇರು, to be bound to do.

The word, taken apart.
ಬಾಧ್ಯತೆ (bādhyate) is built on Sanskrit bādhya, "to be pressed hard" (Monier-Williams), with -tā, "-ness": what presses on one.

What you've built.
