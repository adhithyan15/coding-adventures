## You'll want to know: der Wendepunkt
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C301-KUENDIGEN-05]; assesses=[] -->

**der Wendepunkt** — "the turning point".

> Diese Reise war ein Wendepunkt in meinem Leben.
>
> *This journey was a turning point in my life.*

Plural: *die Wendepunkte*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Wendepunkt* joins *wenden* "to turn", Old High German *wenten*, Proto-Germanic *wandijaną* (EtymDB), and *Punkt* "point", from Latin *punctum*.
