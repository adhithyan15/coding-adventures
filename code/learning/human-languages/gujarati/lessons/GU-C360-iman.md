# ઈમાન (īmān) — integrity

## You'll want to know: ઈમાન
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C360-IMAN360-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ઈમાન** — *īmān* — "integrity".

> સાચો માણસ પૈસા માટે પોતાનું ઈમાન વેચતો નથી.
>
> *sāco māṇas paisā māṭe potānũ īmān vecto nathī.*
>
> An honest person does not sell his integrity for money.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ઈમાન** (*īmān*) is usually traced to Arabic *īmān*, "faith", through Persian; in Gujarati it is the faith one keeps with others.
