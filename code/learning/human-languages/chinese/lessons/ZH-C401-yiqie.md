## You'll want to know: yíqiè
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C401-FUTURE401-05]; assesses=[] -->

*yíqiè* — "everything; all".

> *zhǐyào nǐ zài yíqiè dōu hǎo* — As long as you're here, everything's fine.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

Tone: 一 is said yí here, rising, because qiè after it is a falling tone.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*yíqiè* is built from 一 'one' and *qiè*, both inherited from Middle Chinese, *'jit* and *tshejH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second, in this tone, 'the many': all of them as one.
