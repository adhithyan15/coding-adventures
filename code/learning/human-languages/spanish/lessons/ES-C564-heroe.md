## You'll want to know: el héroe
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C564-HEROE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**el héroe** — "the hero".

> El bombero fue el héroe del día.
>
> *The firefighter was the hero of the day.*

A woman is *la heroína*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*héroe* is a learned borrowing from Latin *hērōs*, which comes from Greek *hḗrōs* 'a demigod, a hero' (EtymDB).
