## You'll want to know: भ्रमणध्वनी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C516-PHON516-02]; assesses=[] -->

**भ्रमणध्वनी** — *bhramaṇdhvanī* — "a mobile phone".

> मीराचा भ्रमणध्वनी बसमध्ये हरवला.
>
> *mīrācā bhramaṇdhvanī basmadhye haravlā.*
>
> Meera's mobile phone got lost on the bus.

Masculine.

In speech people say मोबाईल; भ्रमणध्वनी is the formal word, a sound that roams.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**भ्रमणध्वनी** (*bhramaṇdhvanī*) is a modern tatsama coinage from Sanskrit *bhramaṇa*, "roaming", and *dhvani*, "sound" (Monier-Williams).
