## You'll want to know: असली
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C434-SHUDDH434-01]; assesses=[] -->

**असली** — *aslī* — "real, genuine".

> यह असली चाँदी है।
>
> *yah aslī cā̃dī hai.*
>
> This is real silver.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**असली** (*aslī*) is usually traced to Arabic *aṣlī*, "original", from *aṣl*, "a root", through Persian: the thing itself, not a copy.
