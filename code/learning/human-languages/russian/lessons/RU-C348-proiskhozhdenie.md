## You'll want to know: происхождение
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C348-POVOD-05]; assesses=[] -->

**происхождение** (*proiskhozhdéniye*) — "origin, descent".

> Он русский по происхождению.
>
> *On rússkiy po proiskhozhdéniyu.*
>
> He is Russian by origin.

A neuter noun. *По происхождению* — by origin, by birth. From *происходить*, to come from; the *жд* is the Church Slavonic softening of *д*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**происхождение** is built on *происходить* "to come from", made of *про-*, *из-* "out of" and *ходить* "to go", inherited from Proto-Slavic *\*xoditi*. The *жд* is the Church Slavonic way of softening *д*.
