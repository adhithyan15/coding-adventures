Russian, chapter 40: Sleep, Sit, Stand, Run, Play.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
спать (spat) — to sleep.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to sell, then the Russian for to swim.

You'll want to know: спать.
спать (spat) — "to sleep".

What you've built.
One more word for sleep, sit, stand, run, play.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: spat]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: spat, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say spat]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Russian for to sell, then the Russian for to swim, then say spat again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does спать (spat) mean? ("To sleep".) Say it once more.
