## You'll want to know: সিলমোহর
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C493-OPHIS493-03]; assesses=[] -->

**সিলমোহর** — *silmohôr* — "an official seal; a rubber stamp".

> কেরানি ফর্মে সিলমোহর লাগাল।
>
> *Kerāni phôrme silmohôr lāgālô.*
>
> The clerk stamped the form with the official seal.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**সিলমোহর** (*silmohôr*) probably joins English *seal* to মোহর, "a seal", usually traced to Persian *muhr*: the same thing said twice, in two languages.
