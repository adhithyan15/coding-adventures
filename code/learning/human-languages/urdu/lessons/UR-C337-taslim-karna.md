# تسلیم کرنا (taslīm karnā) — to acknowledge; to admit

## You'll want to know: تسلیم کرنا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C337-TASDIQ337-02]; assesses=[] -->

**تسلیم کرنا** — *taslīm karnā* — "to acknowledge; to admit".

> آخر اس نے سچ تسلیم کر لیا۔
>
> *ākhir us ne sach taslīm kar liyā.*
>
> In the end he admitted the truth.

Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے بات تسلیم کی, *us ne bāt taslīm kī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**تسلیم کرنا** (*taslīm karnā*) is built from *taslīm*, usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *taslīm*, "handing over; assent", and **کرنا**, "to do".
