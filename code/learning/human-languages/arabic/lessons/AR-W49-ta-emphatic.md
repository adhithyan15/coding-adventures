# ط (ṭāʾ) — the letter that opens طعام

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[AR-SCRIPT-C48-ZAY-01, AR-SCRIPT-C48-GHAYN-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: write ز and غ once each, and name them.

[PAUSE 2s] Now say **طعام** (*ṭaʿām*), "food".
You have read it for a long time. This lesson takes one letter out of it and puts
it in your hand.

## Script you'll notice: ط
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-SCRIPT-C49-TA-EMPHATIC-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ط** — *ṭāʾ*, a heavy *t*.

It opens **طعام** (*ṭaʿām*), "food". A closed **oval** on the line, and a **tall
upright** drawn last, from the top down.

## Writing: ط
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[AR-SCRIPT-C49-TA-EMPHATIC-01] -->

- **1.** start here: loop counterclockwise around the closed oval
- **2.** finish left along the baseline without lifting
- **3.** lift once, then draw the tall upright top-to-bottom — and only now lift

**Pen lifts: 1.**

> Stroke order is one attested teaching order; hands and teachers vary.
> Source: Introduction to Arabic: Egyptian Arabic for first-year students, Alphabet ط ظ, independent emphatic Taa at 00:01.2–00:03.0 (University of Oregon Libraries Open Textbook, 2023; accessed 2026-08-23).
