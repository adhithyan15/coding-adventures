## You'll want to know: затылок
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C482-HEAD-02]; assesses=[] -->

**затылок** (*zatýlok*) — "the back of the head".

> Мяч попал ему в затылок.
>
> *Myach popál yemú v zatýlok.*
>
> The ball hit him on the back of the head.

Masculine; genitive затылка.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*затылок* is built on *за* 'behind' and *тыл* 'back, rear', which is inherited from Proto-Slavic *tylъ* 'back of the head, back' (EtymDB).
