## You'll want to know: اقترح
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C359-NAZAR359-04]; assesses=[] -->

**اقترح** — *iqtaraḥa* — "to suggest".

> اقترح المعلم رحلة إلى المتحف.
>
> *iqtaraḥa al-muʿallim riḥla ilā al-matḥaf.*
>
> The teacher suggested a trip to the museum.

Present: يقترح, *yaqtariḥ*.

The noun اقتراح, *iqtirāḥ*, a suggestion, comes from it.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**اقترح** (*iqtaraḥa*) is built on the native Arabic root *q-r-ḥ*, "to wound" (Lane). It is Form VIII. Its modern sense, to put an idea forward, is usually traced to an older one, to bring out something new.

## What you've built

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
