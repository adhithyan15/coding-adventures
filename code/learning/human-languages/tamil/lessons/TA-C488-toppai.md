## You'll want to know: தொப்பை
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C488-EDAI488-02]; assesses=[] -->

**தொப்பை** — *toppai* — "a paunch; a pot belly".

> உடற்பயிற்சி செய்யாததால் மாமாவுக்குத் தொப்பை வந்தது.
>
> *uṭaṟpayiṟci ceyyātatāl māmāvukkut toppai vantatu.*
>
> Uncle got a paunch because he did not exercise.

Everyday and a little teasing.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

The origin of **தொப்பை** (*toppai*) is not settled; we could not find it in DEDR.

## What you've built

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
