# পুতুলনাচ (putulnāch) — a puppet show

## You'll want to know: পুতুলনাচ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C311-MELA311-02]; assesses=[] -->

**পুতুলনাচ** — *putulnāch* — "a puppet show".

> মেলায় সন্ধ্যায় একটা পুতুলনাচ হল।
>
> *melāy sôndhyāy ekṭā putulnāch hôlô.*
>
> There was a puppet show at the fair in the evening.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**পুতুলনাচ** (*putulnāch*) is built from **পুতুল**, a doll, and **নাচ**, a dance: dolls that dance on strings.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
