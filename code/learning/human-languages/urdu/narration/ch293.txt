Urdu, chapter 293: The Water Rose.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
گاؤں (gāoṅ) — a village.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for destruction, then the Urdu for an effect.

You'll want to know: گاؤں.
گاؤں — gāoṅ — "a village".
ہمارا گاؤں دریا کے کنارے ہے۔ hamārā gāoṅ daryā ke kināre hai. Our village is on the bank of the river.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
گاؤں (gāoṅ) is inherited from Sanskrit grāma, "a village" (CDIAL 4368), where CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin gā̃u. Urdu writes it with ؤ and the nasal ں.

بہنا — bahnā — "to flow; to be swept away".
پانی گلیوں میں بہہ رہا تھا۔ pānī galiyoṅ meṅ bah rahā thā. Water was flowing through the lanes.
Past (it …): وہ پل بہہ گیا, voh pul bah gayā.

The word, taken apart.
بہنا (bahnā) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin bahnā, "flows, blows", under Sanskrit váhati (CDIAL 11453). Its stem is written بہہ: بہہ گیا, was swept away.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a village, then the Urdu for to flow.

You'll want to know: ڈوبنا.
ڈوبنا — ḍūbnā — "to sink; to drown".
تمام گاؤں پانی میں ڈوب گیا۔ tamām gāoṅ pānī meṅ ḍūb gayā. The whole village went under water.
Past (it …): وہ کشتی ڈوب گئی, voh kashtī ḍūb gaī.

The word, taken apart.
ڈوبنا (ḍūbnā) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin ḍūbnā, "to sink", under ḍubb, a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 5561). A person who ڈوب گیا drowned.
