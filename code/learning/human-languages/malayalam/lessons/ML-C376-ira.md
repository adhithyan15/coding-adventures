## You'll want to know: ഇര
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C376-JEEVAN376-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ഇര** — *ira* — "a victim; prey".

> തട്ടിപ്പിന്റെ ഇരകൾ പോലീസിൽ പരാതി കൊടുത്തു.
>
> *taṭṭippinṟe irakaḷ pōlīsil parāti koṭuttu.*
>
> The victims of the fraud complained to the police.

Plural: ഇരകൾ, *irakaḷ*.

First the prey of an animal; in the news, a victim.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ഇര** (*ira*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 490 lists Malayalam *ira*, "food of birds, snakes", beside Tamil *irai*, "prey". The news uses it for a person something bad happened to.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
