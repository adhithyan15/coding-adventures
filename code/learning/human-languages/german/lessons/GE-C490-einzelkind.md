## You'll want to know: das Einzelkind
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C490-SCHWIEGER-05]; assesses=[] -->

**das Einzelkind** — "only child".

> Als Einzelkind hatte ich mein eigenes Zimmer.
>
> *As an only child I had my own room.*

Plural: *die Einzelkinder*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Einzelkind* is built on *einzeln* "single" and *Kind* "child", inherited from Middle High German *kint* and Old High German *kind* (EtymDB).
