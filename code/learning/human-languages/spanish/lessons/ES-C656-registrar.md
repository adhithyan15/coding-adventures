## You'll want to know: registrar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C656-FRONTERA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**registrar** — "to search; to register, to record".

> La policía registró el coche.
>
> *The police searched the car.*

*Registrarse* is to check in or sign up: *registrarse en el hotel*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*registrar* is built on *registro* "register" (EtymDB), which comes from Medieval Latin *registrum*, from *regesta* "things recorded", built on *gerere* "to carry, to carry out".
