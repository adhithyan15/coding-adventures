Punjabi, chapter 321: A Cough and a Cold.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ਜ਼ੁਕਾਮ (zukām) — a head cold.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for the pulse, then the Punjabi for an injection.

You'll want to know: ਜ਼ੁਕਾਮ.
ਜ਼ੁਕਾਮ — zukām — "a head cold".
ਮੈਨੂੰ ਜ਼ੁਕਾਮ ਹੈ ਅਤੇ ਮੈਂ ਅੱਜ ਘਰ ਵਿੱਚ ਹਾਂ। mainū̃ zukām hai ate maiṁ ajj ghar vich hāṁ. I have a cold, and I am at home today.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ਜ਼ੁਕਾਮ (zukām) is a loan, usually traced through Persian to Arabic zukām, "a cold, catarrh".

Lesson 2 of 5.
ਖੰਘ (khaṅgh) — a cough.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for an injection, then the Punjabi for a head cold.

You'll want to know: ਖੰਘ.
ਖੰਘ — khaṅgh — "a cough".
ਬੱਚੇ ਨੂੰ ਰਾਤ ਨੂੰ ਬਹੁਤ ਖੰਘ ਸੀ। bachche nū̃ rāt nū̃ bahut khaṅgh sī. The child had a bad cough at night.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ਖੰਘ (khaṅgh) goes back to a reconstructed khaṅkh, "cough, hawk", an imitative form; CDIAL lists Punjabi khaṅgh, "cough", under it (CDIAL 3763).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for a head cold, then the Punjabi for a cough.

You'll want to know: ਛਿੱਕ.
ਛਿੱਕ — chhikk — "a sneeze".
ਮਿਰਚ ਤੋਂ ਮੈਨੂੰ ਛਿੱਕ ਆਈ। mirach ton mainū̃ chhikk āī. The chilli made me sneeze.

Lesson 4 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for a cough, then the Punjabi for a sneeze.

You'll want to know: ਸਾਹ.
ਸਾਹ — sāh — "breath".
ਪੌੜੀ ਤੋਂ ਬਾਅਦ ਮੇਰਾ ਸਾਹ ਤੇਜ਼ ਸੀ। pauṛī ton bāad merā sāh tez sī. After the stairs I was breathing fast.
