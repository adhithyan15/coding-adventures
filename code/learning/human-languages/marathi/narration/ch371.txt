Marathi, chapter 371: Passing and Failing.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
उत्तीर्ण होणे (uttīrṇa hoṇe) — to pass (an exam).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a symposium, then the Marathi for a speaker.

You'll want to know: उत्तीर्ण होणे.
उत्तीर्ण होणे — uttīrṇa hoṇe — "to pass (an exam)".
अरुण पहिल्या प्रयत्नात परीक्षा उत्तीर्ण झाला. aruṇ pahilyā prayatnāt parīkṣā uttīrṇa jhālā. Arun passed the exam at the first attempt.
Past (he …): तो उत्तीर्ण झाला, to uttīrṇa jhālā.
Formal; what is passed is the object, with no ending: परीक्षा उत्तीर्ण होणे (uttīrṇa hoṇe).

The word, taken apart.
उत्तीर्ण होणे (uttīrṇa hoṇe) joins the tatsama उत्तीर्ण, Sanskrit uttīrṇa, "crossed over, landed" (Monier-Williams), to होणे, "to become": to get across.

Lesson 2 of 5.
नापास होणे (nāpās hoṇe) — to fail (an exam).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a speaker, then the Marathi for to pass.

You'll want to know: नापास होणे.
नापास होणे — nāpās hoṇe — "to fail (an exam)".
तो गणितात नापास झाला. to gaṇitāt nāpās jhālā. He failed in maths.
Past (he …): तो नापास झाला, to nāpās jhālā.
The school subject takes -त: गणितात नापास, failed in maths.

The word, taken apart.
नापास होणे (nāpās hoṇe) joins नापास, a hybrid of the Persian prefix nā-, "not", and a loan from English pass, to होणे, "to become".

Lesson 3 of 5.
उजळणी करणे (ujaḷṇī karṇe) — to revise.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to pass, then the Marathi for to fail.

You'll want to know: उजळणी करणे.
उजळणी करणे — ujaḷṇī karṇe — "to revise".
परीक्षेआधी मी सगळ्या धड्यांची उजळणी केली. parīkṣeādhī mī sagaḷyā dhaḍyāñcī ujaḷṇī kelī. Before the exam I revised all the lessons.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने उजळणी केली, tyāne ujaḷṇī kelī.
What is revised takes -ची: धड्यांची उजळणी.

The word, taken apart.
उजळणी करणे (ujaḷṇī karṇe) joins उजळणी, "brightening up again", built on the Marathi verb उजळणे, "to polish, kindle", inherited from Sanskrit ujjvalati, "blazes up" (CDIAL 1671), to करणे.

Warm-up.

You'll want to know: घोकणे.
घोकणे — ghokṇe — "to cram; to learn by rote".
न समजता उत्तरं घोकून काही उपयोग नाही. na samajtā uttaraṁ ghokūn kāhī upyog nāhī. There is no use cramming answers without understanding them.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने घोकलं, tyāne ghoklaṁ.
घोकंपट्टी, rote learning.

The word, taken apart.
घोकणे (ghokṇe) is a Marathi verb whose origin is not settled.

Lesson 5 of 5.
प्रमाणपत्र (pramāṇpatra) — a certificate.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to revise, then the Marathi for to cram.

You'll want to know: प्रमाणपत्र.
प्रमाणपत्र — pramāṇpatra — "a certificate".
स्पर्धेनंतर सगळ्यांना प्रमाणपत्र मिळालं. spardhenantar sagaḷyānnā pramāṇpatra miḷālaṁ. After the competition everyone got a certificate.
Neuter.
Literally a letter of proof.

The word, taken apart.
प्रमाणपत्र (pramāṇpatra) is a learned compound built from Sanskrit pramāṇa, "measure, proof" (CDIAL 8723), and patra, "leaf, letter".
