## You'll want to know: ترفیع
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C380-REZUME-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ترفیع** (*tarfi'*) — "a promotion".

> خواهرم امسال ترفیع گرفت.
>
> *khâharam emsâl tarfi' gereft.*
>
> My sister got a promotion this year.

*Tarfi' gereftan* — to be promoted.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ترفیع** is usually traced to Arabic *tarfī'* "raising, lifting up": a promotion lifts you to a higher post.
