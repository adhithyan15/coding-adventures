5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
حرفه (herfe) — a profession.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a vacuum cleaner, then the Persian for a washing machine.

You'll want to know: حرفه.
حرفه — herfe — "a profession".

The word, taken apart.
herfe is borrowed from Arabic ḥirfa, "trade, occupation". Its Arabic root is ḥ-r-f (Lane).

Lesson 3 of 5.
کارخانه (kârkhâne) — a factory.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a profession, then the Persian for a company.

You'll want to know: کارخانه.
کارخانه — kârkhâne — "a factory".

The word, taken apart.
kârkhâne is built from kâr, "work", and khâne, "house": the work-house. Both are inherited: kâr continues Middle Persian kār (EtymDB), and khâne is usually traced to xānag.

You'll want to know: حقوق.
حقوق — hoquq — "a salary".

The word, taken apart.
hoquq is borrowed from Arabic ḥuqūq, "rights, dues". Its Arabic root is ḥ-q-q (Lane). It is the plural of ḥaqq; Persian uses "dues" for a salary.
