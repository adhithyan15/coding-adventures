Portuguese, chapter 339: Chegar a acordo.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
o acordo — the agreement, the deal.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for the objection, then the Portuguese for to contradict.

You'll want to know: o acordo.
o acordo — "the agreement, the deal".
Depois de duas horas, chegámos a um acordo. After two hours, we reached an agreement.
Estar de acordo — to agree; de acordo! — agreed, OK.

The word, taken apart.
acordo is built on the verb acordar in its old sense "to agree", usually traced to Vulgar Latin accordāre, a formation on Latin cor, cordis "heart" (Lewis & Short): hearts that beat together.

What you've built.

Lesson 2 of 5.
concordar — to agree.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for to contradict, then the Portuguese for the agreement.

You'll want to know: concordar.
concordar — "to agree".
Concordo contigo: é melhor sair cedo. I agree with you: it is better to leave early.
Concordar com alguém — to agree with someone; concordar em + infinitive — to agree to do something.

The word, taken apart.
concordar is a learned borrowing from Latin concordāre "to agree together, to be of one mind" (EtymDB, Lewis & Short), from concors, built from con- "together" and cor, cordis "heart".

Lesson 3 of 5.
admitir — to admit; to let in.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for the agreement, then the Portuguese for to agree.

You'll want to know: admitir.
admitir — "to admit; to let in".
Tenho de admitir que o teu plano era melhor. I have to admit that your plan was better.
It also means to let in or accept: admitir novos sócios — to take in new members.

The word, taken apart.
admitir is a learned borrowing from Latin admittere "to let in, to allow" (EtymDB), built from ad "to" and mittere "to send".

Lesson 4 of 5.
o consenso — the consensus, the general agreement.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for to agree, then the Portuguese for to admit.

You'll want to know: o consenso.
o consenso — "the consensus, the general agreement".
Não houve consenso na reunião. There was no consensus at the meeting.
Chegar a um consenso — to reach a consensus.

The word, taken apart.
consenso is a learned borrowing from Latin cōnsēnsus "agreement, accordance, unanimity" (Lewis & Short), from cōnsentīre, built from con- "together" and sentīre "to feel".
