## You'll want to know: ताबडतोब
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C302-KSHAN302-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ताबडतोब** — *tābaḍtob* — "immediately; at once".

> रुग्णवाहिका ताबडतोब घटनास्थळी पोहोचली.
>
> *rugṇavāhikā tābaḍtob ghaṭnāsthaḷī pohoclī.*
>
> The ambulance reached the scene at once.

It keeps one form: ताबडतोब निघणे, to set off at once.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ताबडतोब** (*tābaḍtob*) is a Marathi word for at once; its origin is not settled.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
