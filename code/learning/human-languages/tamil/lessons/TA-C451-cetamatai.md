# சேதமடை (cētamaṭai) — to get damaged

## You'll want to know: சேதமடை
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C451-VALAKAM451-05]; assesses=[] -->

**சேதமடை** — *cētamaṭai* — "to get damaged".

> வெள்ளத்தில் பல வீடுகள் சேதமடைந்தன.
>
> *veḷḷattil pala vīṭukaḷ cētamaṭaintaṉa.*
>
> Many houses were damaged in the flood.

Past (it …): சேதமடைந்தது, *cētamaṭaintatu*.

சேதம், damage, + அடை, to get.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**சேதமடை** (*cētamaṭai*) is built from சேதம், "damage", borrowed from Sanskrit *cheda*, "cutting, loss" (CDIAL 5064), and அடை, "to reach, to get" (DEDR 79).

One more word for getting around.
