## You'll want to know: مزاج
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C440-FEEL440-05]; assesses=[] -->

**مزاج** — *mizāj* — "a mood; a temperament".

> آج ابو کا مزاج اچھا ہے۔
>
> *āj abbū kā mizāj achchhā hai.*
>
> Dad is in a good mood today.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**مزاج** (*mizāj*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *mizāj*, "a mixture", and so the blend that makes up a person's temper.
