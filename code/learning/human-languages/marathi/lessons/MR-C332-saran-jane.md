## You'll want to know: शरण जाणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C332-SHAURYA332-05]; assesses=[] -->

**शरण जाणे** — *śaraṇ jāṇe* — "to surrender".

> शेवटी शत्रू राजाला शरण गेला.
>
> *śevṭī śatrū rājālā śaraṇ gelā.*
>
> In the end the enemy surrendered to the king.

Past (he …): तो शरण गेला, *to śaraṇ gelā*.

The one surrendered to takes -ला: राजाला शरण जाणे.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**शरण जाणे** (*śaraṇ jāṇe*) joins *śaraṇ*, a tatsama taken from Sanskrit *śaraṇa*, 'shelter, refuge' (Monier-Williams), to *jāṇe*, 'to go': to go for refuge.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
