# placeō, placēre — to please, be pleasing

## You'll want to know: placeō, placēre
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C499-VOLU-05]; assesses=[] -->

**placeō, placēre** — "to please, be pleasing".

> Haec domus nova uxōrī meae placet.
>
> *This new house pleases my wife.*

*placeō, placēre, placuī, placitum*, with the dative: *mihi placet* — I like it; it seems good to me. English *please* comes from it.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**placeō** is a native Latin verb, a cousin of *plācō* "to calm" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short). English *please* and *pleasure* come from it through Old French.
