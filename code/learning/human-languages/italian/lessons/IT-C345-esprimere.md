## You'll want to know: esprimere
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C345-AFFERMARE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**esprimere** — "to express".

> Non è facile esprimere quello che sento.
>
> *It is not easy to express what I feel.*

Past participle *espresso* — the same word as the coffee, pressed out. *Esprimersi* — to express yourself.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*esprimere* is a learned borrowing from Latin *exprimere* "to press out, to wring" (EtymDB), built from *ex-* "out of" and *premere* "to press": the thought is squeezed out into words.
