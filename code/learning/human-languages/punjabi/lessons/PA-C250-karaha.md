## You'll want to know: ਕੜਾਹਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C250-GHR250-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ਕੜਾਹਾ** — *kaṛāhā* — "a large pan".

> ਕੜਾਹੇ ਵਿੱਚ ਬਹੁਤ ਹਲਵਾ ਹੈ।
>
> *kaṛāhe vich bahut halvā hai.*
>
> There is a lot of halwa in the big pan.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਕੜਾਹਾ** (*kaṛāhā*) is inherited from Sanskrit *kaṭāha*, "boiler, saucepan with handles"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *kaṛāhā*, "shallow pan for boiling sugarcane juice" (CDIAL 2638).
