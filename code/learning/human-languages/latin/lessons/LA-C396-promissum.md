## You'll want to know: prōmissum, prōmissī
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C396-POLLICEOR-01]; assesses=[] -->

**prōmissum, prōmissī** — "a promise".

> Prōmissum suum servāvit.
>
> *He kept his promise.*

*prōmissum, prōmissī* n., the participle of *prōmittō*: a thing sent forward. *Prōmissum servāre*, to keep a promise. English *promise*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*prōmissum* is the participle of *prōmittō* "to send forward, to promise" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short), from *prō* and *mittō* "to send". English *promise* comes from it through French.
