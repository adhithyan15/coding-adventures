## You'll want to know: ചക്രം
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C471-VANDI471-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ചക്രം** — *cakraṁ* — "a wheel".

> ബസിന്റെ ഒരു ചക്രം ചെളിയിൽ താഴ്ന്നു.
>
> *basinṟe oru cakraṁ ceḷiyil tāḻnnu.*
>
> One of the bus's wheels sank into the mud.

Plural: ചക്രങ്ങൾ, *cakraṅṅaḷ*.

A two-wheeler, a scooter or a motorbike, is an ഇരുചക്രവാഹനം, a two-wheeled vehicle.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ചക്രം** (*cakraṁ*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *cakra*, "a wheel; a circle" (CDIAL 4538).
