## You'll want to know: تصور
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C547-TASAVVUR547-01]; assesses=[] -->

**تصور** — *tasavvur* — "imagination; a picture in the mind".

> اگر تصور نہ ہوتا تو کوئی نئی چیز نہ بنتی۔
>
> *agar tasavvur na hotā to koī naī chīz na bantī.*
>
> Without imagination nothing new would ever be made.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**تصور** (*tasavvur*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *taṣawwur*, "forming an image in the mind".
