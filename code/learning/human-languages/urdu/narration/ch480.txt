Urdu, chapter 480: Looking After Someone.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
عیادت کرنا (iyādat karnā) — to visit the sick.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a surgeon, then the Urdu for a stretcher.

You'll want to know: عیادت کرنا.
عیادت کرنا — iyādat karnā — "to visit the sick".
ہم نے ہسپتال جا کر دادا کی عیادت کی۔ ham ne haspatāl jā kar dādā kī iyādat kī. We went to the hospital to visit my grandfather.
Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے مریض کی عیادت کی, us ne marīz kī iyādat kī.

The word, taken apart.
عیادت کرنا (iyādat karnā) is built from عیادت, usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic ʿiyāda, "visiting the sick", and کرنا, "to do".

What you've built.

Lesson 4 of 5.
لٹانا (liṭānā) — to lay (someone) down.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for nursing the sick, then the Urdu for looking after.

You'll want to know: لٹانا.
لٹانا — liṭānā — "to lay (someone) down".
نرس نے مریض کو بستر پر لٹایا۔ nars ne marīz ko bistar par liṭāyā. The nurse laid the patient on the bed.
Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے بچے کو لٹایا, us ne bachche ko liṭāyā.

The word, taken apart.
لٹانا (liṭānā) is the causative of لیٹنا, "to lie down", which CDIAL lists under Sanskrit lēṭyáti (CDIAL 11109).

The word, taken apart.
اوڑھانا (oṛhānā) is the causative of oṛhnā, "to wrap oneself in", inherited from ōḍḍh, a form CDIAL reconstructs (CDIAL 2547).
