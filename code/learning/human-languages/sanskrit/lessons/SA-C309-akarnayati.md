## You'll want to know: आकर्णयति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C309-VRITTANTA309-03]; assesses=[] -->

**आकर्णयति** — *ākarṇayati* — "listens to".

> पिता प्रातः वार्ताम् आकर्णयति।
>
> *pitā prātaḥ vārtām ākarṇayati.*
>
> Father listens to the news in the morning.

Past (he …): सः वार्ताम् आकर्णितवान्, *saḥ vārtām ākarṇitavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**आकर्णयति** (*ākarṇayati*) is built on *ā* and *karṇa*, "the ear": MW gives *ākarṇayati* as a verb made from them, to give ear to, listen (MW).
