## You'll want to know: નહેર
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C440-STREAM440-04]; assesses=[] -->

**નહેર** — *naher* — "a canal".

> નહેરનું પાણી ખેતરો સુધી પહોંચે છે.
>
> *nahernũ pāṇī khetro sudhī pahõce che.*
>
> The canal water reaches the fields.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**નહેર** (*naher*) is usually traced to Arabic *nahr*, "a river, a stream", which came through Persian: a river dug by hand.
