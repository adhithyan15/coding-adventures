## You'll want to know: अस्पताल
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C107-DOCTOR-02]; assesses=[] -->

**अस्पताल** (*aspatāl*) — "a hospital".

It is English *hospital*, reshaped to fit Hindi sounds. **अस्पताल कहाँ है?** — "where is the
hospital?"
