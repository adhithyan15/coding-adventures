## You'll want to know: छँटना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C440-GHIRNA440-04]; assesses=[] -->

**छँटना** — *chãṭnā* — "to clear (of clouds); to thin out".

> बारिश के बाद बादल छँट गए।
>
> *bāriś ke bād bādal chãṭ gae.*
>
> After the rain the clouds cleared.

Past (it …): वह कोहरा छँट गया, *vah kohrā chãṭ gayā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**छँटना** (*chãṭnā*) goes back to *\*chaṇṭ*, "scatter", a form CDIAL reconstructs (CDIAL 4970), which lists Hindi *chā̃ṭnā*, "to sift, select"; clouds that छँट जाते are scattered and thinned away.
