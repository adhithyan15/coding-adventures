## You'll want to know: थाणो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C201-TOWN201-03]; assesses=[] -->

**थाणो** — *thāṇo* — "a police station".

> थाणो चौक कनै है।
>
> *thāṇo chauk kanai hai.*
>
> The police station is near the square.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**थाणो** (*thāṇo*) is inherited from Sanskrit *sthāna*, "a place". CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry, but lists Hindi *thānā* and Gujarati *thāṇũ*, "police station" (CDIAL 13753).
