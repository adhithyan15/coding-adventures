## You'll want to know: trauen
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C477-MUT-05]; assesses=[] -->

**trauen** — "to dare (sich trauen); to trust".

> Er traut sich nicht, vom Sprungturm zu springen.
>
> *He doesn't dare to jump off the diving tower.*

Reflexive for to dare: *sich trauen*. With a dative it means to trust: *Ich traue ihm nicht.*

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*trauen* is inherited from Old High German *trūwēn* "to trust, to believe" (EtymDB), a Germanic verb related to *treu* "faithful".
