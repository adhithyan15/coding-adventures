## You'll want to know: होड
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C279-GOTHIYO279-04]; assesses=[] -->

**होड** — *hoḍ* — "a contest".

> आज गांव मांय होड है।
>
> *āj gā̃v mā̃y hoḍ hai.*
>
> There's a contest in the village today.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**होड** (*hoḍ*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*hōḍḍa*, "a wager", which CDIAL takes from Dravidian; it lists Old Marwari *hoḍa*, "wager, rivalry" (CDIAL 14175).
