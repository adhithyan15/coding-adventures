## You'll want to know: પરેજી
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C485-DAKHAL485-05]; assesses=[] -->

**પરેજી** — *parejī* — "a diet restriction".

> ઓપરેશન પછી દાદાએ તીખા ભોજનની પરેજી પાળી.
>
> *opreśan pachī dādāe tīkhā bhojannī parejī pāḷī.*
>
> After the operation my grandfather kept off spicy food.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**પરેજી** (*parejī*) is usually traced to Persian *parhēz*, "abstinence, keeping away".
