# استفاد (istafāda) — to benefit

## You'll want to know: استفاد
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C408-MASLAHA408-01]; assesses=[] -->

**استفاد** — *istafāda* — "to benefit".

> استفاد الطلاب من الرحلة.
>
> *istafāda aṭ-ṭullāb min ar-riḥla.*
>
> The students benefited from the trip.

Present: يستفيد, *yastafīd*.

It takes من: استفاد من ….

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**استفاد** (*istafāda*) is built on the native Arabic root *f-y-d*, "to accrue, to come as gain" (Lane). It is Form X, to seek gain from a thing: to benefit.
