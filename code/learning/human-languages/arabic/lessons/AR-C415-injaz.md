## You'll want to know: إنجاز
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C415-INJAZ415-01]; assesses=[] -->

**إنجاز** — *injāz* — "an achievement".

> كان الجسر الجديد إنجازا كبيرا.
>
> *kāna al-jisr al-jadīd injāzan kabīran.*
>
> The new bridge was a great achievement.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**إنجاز** (*injāz*) is built on the native Arabic root *n-j-z*, "to be accomplished, finished" (Lane). *Injāz* is the Form IV verbal noun of *anjaza*, to accomplish: an achievement.
