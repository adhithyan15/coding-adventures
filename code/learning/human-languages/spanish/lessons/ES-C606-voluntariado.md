## You'll want to know: el voluntariado
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C606-SOLIDAR-02]; assesses=[] -->

**el voluntariado** — "the volunteering; the volunteers".

> Hago voluntariado los fines de semana.
>
> *I do volunteering at weekends.*

It also means all the volunteers together.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*voluntariado* is built on *voluntario*, a learned borrowing from Latin *voluntarius* 'willing', from *voluntas* and *velle* 'to want' (EtymDB).
