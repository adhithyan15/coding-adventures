## You'll want to know: gùkè
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C202-TILL202-04]; assesses=[] -->

*gùkè* — "customer".

> *zhèr de gùkè dōu hěn niánqīng* — The customers here are all young.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*gùkè* is built from *gù* 'to look round' and *kè* 'a guest', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *kuH* and *khaek* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to look back'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for asking for what I want.
