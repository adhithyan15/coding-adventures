## You'll want to know: आपत्तिः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C299-GHOSHANA299-05]; assesses=[] -->

**आपत्तिः** — *āpattiḥ* — "a misfortune".

> सहसा ग्रामे आपत्तिः आगच्छति।
>
> *sahasā grāme āpattiḥ āgacchati.*
>
> Suddenly misfortune comes to the village.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**आपत्तिः** (*āpattiḥ*) is built from *ā* and the root √pad, "to fall": MW gives *āpatti* as happening, incurring and misfortune or calamity (MW).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
