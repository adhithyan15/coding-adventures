Arabic, chapter 103: Beside, Left, Middle, Corner, North.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
بجانب (bi-jānib) — beside, next to.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for behind, then the Arabic for between.

You'll want to know: بجانب.
بجانب — bi-jānib — "beside, next to".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: bi-jānib]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: bi-jānib, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say bi-jānib]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Arabic for behind, then the Arabic for between, then say bi-jānib again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does بجانب (bi-jānib) mean? ("Beside, next to".) Say it once more.
