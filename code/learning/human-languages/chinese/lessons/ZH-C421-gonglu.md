## You'll want to know: 公路
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C421-EXPRESS421-01]; assesses=[] -->

**公路** — *gōnglù* — "highway; main road".

> *zhè tiáo gōnglù hěn cháng* — This main road is very long.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**公路** (*gōnglù*) is built from 公 'public' and 路 'a road', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *kuwng* and *luH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription).

One more word for getting around.
