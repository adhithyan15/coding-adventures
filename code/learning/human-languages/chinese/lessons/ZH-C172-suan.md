## You'll want to know: suān
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C172-TASTE172-02]; assesses=[] -->

*suān* — "sour".

> *zhège píngguǒ hěn suān* — This apple is sour.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*suān* is inherited from Middle Chinese *swan* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'vinegar': the taste of vinegar.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for asking for what I want.
