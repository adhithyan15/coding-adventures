Kannada, chapter 220: Lid, Cupboard, Fridge, Fan, Rubbish.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಮುಚ್ಚಳ (muccaḷa) — a lid.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for rough, then the Kannada for sticky.

You'll want to know: ಮುಚ್ಚಳ.
ಮುಚ್ಚಳ — muccaḷa — "a lid".

The word, taken apart.
ಮುಚ್ಚಳ (muccaḷa) is native Dravidian, from muccu, "to close, cover", and DEDR 4915 lists muccaḷa, "covering, lid", itself; the entry also has Tamil muccu and Telugu mūyu.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ಕಸ (kasa) — rubbish, trash.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a fridge, then the Kannada for a fan.

You'll want to know: ಕಸ.
ಕಸ — kasa — "rubbish, trash".

The word, taken apart.
ಕಸ (kasa) is native Dravidian, one word with Tamil kacaṭu, Telugu kasaṭu and Malayalam kacci (DEDR 1088). DEDR lists kasa, kasavu, "rubbish, sweepings". DEDR 1088 compares Sanskrit kaccara.
