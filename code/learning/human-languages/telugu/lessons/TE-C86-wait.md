## You'll want to know: ఆగండి
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C86-REPAIR-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ఆగండి** (*āgaṇḍi*) — "please wait", or "please stop".

It is the verb **ఆగు** with the polite **-అండి**. **ఆగండి, ఒక్క నిమిషం** — "wait, one minute".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

Wait, slower, louder, once more: enough to hold a conversation you cannot yet follow.
