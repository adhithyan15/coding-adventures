## You'll want to know: लिलाव
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C429-LILAV429-01]; assesses=[] -->

**लिलाव** — *lilāv* — "an auction".

> जुन्या गाड्यांचा लिलाव उद्या आहे.
>
> *junyā gāḍyāñcā lilāv udyā āhe.*
>
> The auction of old cars is tomorrow.

Masculine.

लिलाव करणे, to auction; लिलावात घेणे, to buy at auction.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**लिलाव** (*lilāv*) is a loan from Portuguese *leilão*, "an auction", from the trade of the west coast.
