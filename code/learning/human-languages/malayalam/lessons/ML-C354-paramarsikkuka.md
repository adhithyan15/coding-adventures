## You'll want to know: പരാമർശിക്കുക
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C354-PRASAMGAM354-05]; assesses=[] -->

**പരാമർശിക്കുക** — *parāmarśikkuka* — "to mention; to refer to".

> പ്രസംഗത്തിൽ അവൾ അമ്മയെ പരാമർശിച്ചു.
>
> *prasaṁgattil avaḷ ammaye parāmarśiccu.*
>
> In the speech she mentioned her mother.

Past: പരാമർശിച്ചു, *parāmarśiccu*.

The noun is പരാമർശം. What is mentioned takes the object ending: അപകടത്തെ പരാമർശിച്ചു, mentioned the accident.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**പരാമർശിക്കുക** (*parāmarśikkuka*) is built from Sanskrit *parāmarśa*, "seizing, taking hold of" (Monier-Williams), with the Malayalam verb ending -ിക്കുക: to mention a thing is to take hold of it in words.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
