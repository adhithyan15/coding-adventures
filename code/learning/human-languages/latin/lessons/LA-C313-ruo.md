## You'll want to know: ruō, ruere
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C313-RUINA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ruō, ruere** — "to rush; to fall down".

> Aqua dē monte ruēbat.
>
> *Water was rushing down from the mountain.*

*ruō, ruere, ruī, rutum*: any violent movement, forward or down.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ruō** is an inherited verb (EtymDB). English *ruin* comes from its noun *ruīna*.
