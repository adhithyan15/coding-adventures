# えきまえ (ekimae) — the area in front of a station

## You'll want to know: えきまえ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C372-EKIMAE372-01]; assesses=[] -->

**えきまえ** — *ekimae* — "the area in front of a station".

> **えきまえに ゆうびんきょくが あります。** — *ekimae ni yūbinkyoku ga arimasu* — "There is a post office in front of the station."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**えきまえ** (*ekimae*) is built from both layers of the vocabulary: the Sino-Japanese *eki*, "station" (Middle Chinese *yek*), an on'yomi borrowed from Chinese, and the native Japanese (wago) *mae*, "front", inherited rather than borrowed (JMdict; KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
