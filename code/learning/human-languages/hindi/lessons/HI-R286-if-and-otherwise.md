# Review — if, and otherwise

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[HI-GRAMMAR-C286-AGAR-TO-01, HI-LEX-C286-VARNA-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "if it rains, we will stay at home". (*agar bāriś hogī, to ham ghar par raheṅge*.) Then "or else". (*varnā*.)

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[HI-GRAMMAR-C286-AGAR-TO-01, HI-GRAMMAR-C286-IF-NOW-01, HI-GRAMMAR-C286-IF-PERFECTIVE-01, HI-GRAMMAR-C286-NAHIN-TO-01, HI-LEX-C286-VARNA-01, HI-LEX-C248-THG248-03, HI-LEX-C49-LEAVE-04, HI-GRAMMAR-C284-HOPE-DREAM-01, HI-GRAMMAR-C281-PADNA-MUST-01, HI-GRAMMAR-C285-SINCE-FOR-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s each]
- [YOU SAY: I hope it won't rain tomorrow. If it rains, we will stay at home.]
- [YOU SAY: If you have time, come to the market with me.]
- [YOU SAY: If I miss the bus, I'll have to come by train.]
- [YOU SAY: It has been raining since the morning. If it doesn't stop, we will stay at home.]
- [YOU SAY: Eat now, otherwise you'll be hungry later.]
- [YOU SAY: Leave early, or you'll be late.]

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[HI-GRAMMAR-C286-AGAR-TO-01, HI-GRAMMAR-C286-IF-NOW-01, HI-GRAMMAR-C286-IF-PERFECTIVE-01, HI-GRAMMAR-C286-NAHIN-TO-01, HI-LEX-C286-VARNA-01] -->

[PAUSE 3s] "If it rains, we will stay at home"? (**अगर बारिश होगी, तो हम घर पर रहेंगे**.) "If you have time, come"? (**अगर समय है, तो आइए**.) "If I miss the bus"? (**अगर मेरी बस छूट गई**.) "Otherwise"? (**नहीं तो**, or **वरना**.)
