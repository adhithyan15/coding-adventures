## You'll want to know: دفن کردن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C337-MARG-04]; assesses=[] -->

**دفن کردن** (*dafn kardan*) — "to bury".

> او را در روستای خودش دفن کردند.
>
> *U râ dar rustâ-ye khodash dafn kardand.*
>
> They buried him in his own village.

Present *dafn mikonad*; the passive is *dafn shod*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*dafn* is borrowed from Arabic *dafn*, "burial" (EtymDB lists the Arabic word). *kardan* "to do" is inherited Persian.
