## You'll want to know: polvilhar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C333-BOLO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**polvilhar** — "to sprinkle, to dust".

> No fim, ele polvilha o bolo com açúcar.
>
> *At the end he dusts the cake with sugar.*

Of a powder: sugar, flour, cinnamon.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*polvilhar* is built on *polvilho* "fine powder", a word built on *pó* "dust", from Latin *pulvis* "dust" (Lewis & Short).
