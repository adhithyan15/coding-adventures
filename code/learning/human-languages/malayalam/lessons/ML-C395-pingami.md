## You'll want to know: പിൻഗാമി
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C395-PURVIKAN395-02]; assesses=[] -->

**പിൻഗാമി** — *pingāmi* — "a successor".

> മാനേജർ തന്റെ പിൻഗാമിയെ പരിചയപ്പെടുത്തി.
>
> *mānējar tanṟe pingāmiye paricayappeṭutti.*
>
> The manager introduced his successor.

പിൻ, after, + -ഗാമി, one who goes: the one who comes after.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**പിൻഗാമി** (*pingāmi*) joins the native പിൻ (*pin*), "behind, after" (DEDR 4205), and Sanskrit *gāmin*, "going" (CDIAL 4134): the one who goes after.
