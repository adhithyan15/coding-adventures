## You'll want to know: शौर्य
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C332-SHAURYA332-03]; assesses=[] -->

**शौर्य** — *śaurya* — "bravery".

> सैनिकांच्या शौर्यामुळे गाव वाचलं.
>
> *sainikāñcyā śauryāmuḷe gāv vāclaṁ.*
>
> The village was saved by the soldiers' bravery.

Neuter.

शौर्य दाखवणे, to show bravery.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**शौर्य** (*śaurya*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *śaurya*, 'heroism, valour' (Monier-Williams), built on *śūra*, 'a hero'.
