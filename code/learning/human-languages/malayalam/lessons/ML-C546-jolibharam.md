# ജോലിഭാരം (jōlibhāraṁ) — a workload

## You'll want to know: ജോലിഭാരം
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C546-VYAVASAYAM546-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ജോലിഭാരം** — *jōlibhāraṁ* — "a workload".

> മാസാവസാനം ഓഫീസിൽ ജോലിഭാരം കൂടും.
>
> *māsāvasānaṁ ōphīsil jōlibhāraṁ kūṭuṁ.*
>
> At the end of the month the workload at the office goes up.

ജോലി work + ഭാരം weight; ഭാരമുള്ള, heavy, is already familiar.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ജോലിഭാരം** (*jōlibhāraṁ*) is a Malayalam compound built from ജോലി, "work", and ഭാരം, from Sanskrit *bhāra*, "a burden, a weight" (CDIAL 9459).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
