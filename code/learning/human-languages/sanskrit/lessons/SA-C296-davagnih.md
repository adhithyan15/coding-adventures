## You'll want to know: दावाग्निः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C296-DAVAGNI296-01]; assesses=[] -->

**दावाग्निः** — *dāvāgniḥ* — "a forest fire".

> पर्वते दावाग्निः ज्वलति।
>
> *parvate dāvāgniḥ jvalati.*
>
> A forest fire is burning on the hill.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**दावाग्निः** (*dāvāgniḥ*) is built from *dāva*, "a blaze, a forest blaze", and *agni*, "fire"; MW gives *dāvāgni* as fire in a forest (MW).
