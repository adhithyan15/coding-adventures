## You'll want to know: ڈھیلا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C427-POSHAK427-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ڈھیلا** — *ḍhīlā* — "loose; slack".

> یہ پتلون ڈھیلی ہے۔
>
> *yih patlūn ḍhīlī hai.*
>
> These trousers are loose.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ڈھیلا** (*ḍhīlā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *ḍhīlā*, "slack, loose", under *ḍhila*, a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 5590).
