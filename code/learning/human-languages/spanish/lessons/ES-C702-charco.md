## You'll want to know: el charco
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C702-MOJARSE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**el charco** — "the puddle".

> Los niños saltan en los charcos.
>
> *The children jump in the puddles.*

*Cruzar el charco* — to cross the Atlantic.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

The origin of *charco* is not settled; it may come from a pre-Roman word.
