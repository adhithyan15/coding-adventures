## You'll want to know: चरः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C292-CARA292-01]; assesses=[] -->

**चरः** — *caraḥ* — "a spy; a secret agent".

> राजा चरं प्रेषयति।
>
> *rājā caraṃ preṣayati.*
>
> The king sends a spy.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**चरः** (*caraḥ*) comes from the root √car, "to move, go about"; MW gives *cara* as a spy or secret agent (MW).
