## You'll want to know: অনুযোগ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C444-OBHIMAN444-02]; assesses=[] -->

**অনুযোগ** — *ônujog* — "a gentle complaint; a reproach (to someone close)".

> মীরার অনুযোগ, অরুণ সবসময় দেরি করে আসে।
>
> *mīrār ônujog, ôrun sôbsômôy deri kôre āse.*
>
> Mira's complaint is that Arun always turns up late.

অনুযোগ করা, to complain to someone close. অভিযোগ is a formal complaint or a charge; অনুযোগ stays inside the family and among friends.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**অনুযোগ** (*ônujog*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *anuyoga*, which Monier-Williams gives for questioning and also for reproof; Bengali uses it for the milder complaint made to someone you are close to.
