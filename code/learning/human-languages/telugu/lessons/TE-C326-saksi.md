## You'll want to know: సాక్షి
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C326-SANGHATANA326-01]; assesses=[] -->

**సాక్షి** — *sākṣi* — "a witness".

> సాక్షి పోలీసుతో మాట్లాడాడు.
>
> *sākṣi pōlīsutō māṭlāḍāḍu.*
>
> The witness spoke with the police.

Plural: సాక్షులు, *sākṣulu*.

A ప్రత్యక్ష సాక్షి is an eyewitness, one who saw it happen. A whole case can turn on one: it depends on the witness, సాక్షి మీద ఆధారపడి ఉంటుంది.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**సాక్షి** (*sākṣi*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *sākṣin*, "a witness" (CDIAL 13321). CDIAL takes the Sanskrit word apart as *sa-* "with" and *akṣi* "eye": the witness is the one who was there with his own eyes.
