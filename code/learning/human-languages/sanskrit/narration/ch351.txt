Sanskrit, chapter 351: Right and Wrong.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
सत्कर्म (satkarma) — a virtuous act.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for gives back, then the Sanskrit for atonement.

You'll want to know: सत्कर्म.
सत्कर्म — satkarma — "a virtuous act".
सेवा सत्कर्म अस्ति। sevā satkarma asti. Service is a virtuous act.
A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
सत्कर्म (satkarma) is built from sat, "good", and karman, "an act", from the root √kṛ; MW gives sat-karman "a good work, a virtuous act" (MW).

अनुवर्तते — anuvartate — "conforms to; follows (a custom)".
पुत्रः धर्मस्य मार्गम् अनुवर्तते। putraḥ dharmasya mārgam anuvartate. The son follows the path of dharma.
Past (he …): सः धर्मम् अनुवृत्तवान्, saḥ dharmam anuvṛttavān.

The word, taken apart.
अनुवर्तते (anuvartate) is built on anu, "after", and the root √vṛt, "to turn, to be"; MW gives anu-√vṛt "to follow" and "to obey, to respect, to imitate" (MW).

Lesson 5 of 5.
मर्यादा (maryādā) — the bounds of propriety.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for conforms to, then the Sanskrit for abides by.

You'll want to know: मर्यादा.
मर्यादा — maryādā — "the bounds of propriety".
बालः मर्यादां न त्यजेत्। bālaḥ maryādāṃ na tyajet. A child should not overstep what is proper.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
मर्यादा (maryādā) MW gives maryādā "a limit, a boundary" and "the bounds of morality and propriety"; MW doubts whether it comes from maryā with dā or with ādā, so its origin is uncertain (MW).
