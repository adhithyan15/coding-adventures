## You'll want to know: die Hochschule
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C377-HOCHSCHULE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**die Hochschule** — "the university, the college".

> Meine Schwester studiert an einer Hochschule für Musik.
>
> *My sister studies at a college of music.*

Plural: *die Hochschulen*. A general word for universities and colleges; *die Fachhochschule* is a university of applied sciences.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Hochschule* joins *hoch* "high", Old High German *hōh* (EtymDB), to *Schule* "school", from Old High German *scuola*, Latin *schola* and Greek *scholḗ* "leisure, learned talk" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short).
