## You'll want to know: મહેતલ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C530-TAKID530-03]; assesses=[] -->

**મહેતલ** — *mahetal* — "extra time".

> જો સાહેબ મને એક અઠવાડિયાની મહેતલ આપશે તો હું કામ પતાવી દઈશ.
>
> *jo sāheb mane ek aṭhvāḍiyānī mahetal āpśe to hũ kām patāvī daīś.*
>
> If the boss gives me a week's extra time, I'll get the work finished.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**મહેતલ** (*mahetal*) is usually traced to Arabic *muhla*, "a respite, a delay granted", through Persian.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
