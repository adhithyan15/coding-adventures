Spanish, chapter 557: De luto.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
el luto — mourning.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for the grave, then the Spanish for the cemetery.

You'll want to know: el luto.
el luto — "mourning".
Toda la familia iba de luto. The whole family was in mourning.
Ir de luto — to wear black for mourning.

The word, taken apart.
luto is inherited from Latin lūctus 'grief, mourning' (EtymDB), built on lūgēre 'to mourn'.

Lesson 2 of 5.
la esquela — the death notice.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for the cemetery, then the Spanish for mourning.

You'll want to know: la esquela.
la esquela — "the death notice".
Leí su esquela en el periódico. I read his death notice in the paper.
A notice in the paper or on a wall telling people about a death and the funeral.

The word, taken apart.
esquela is usually traced to Latin schedula 'a small sheet of paper' (Lewis & Short), first any short note and later the notice of a death.

Lesson 3 of 5.
el pésame — the condolences.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for mourning, then the Spanish for the death notice.

You'll want to know: el pésame.
el pésame — "the condolences".
Le di el pésame a la viuda. I offered my condolences to the widow.
Dar el pésame — to offer condolences. Mi más sentido pésame — my deepest sympathy.

The word, taken apart.
pésame is built on the phrase me pesa 'it weighs on me, I am sorry', from pesar 'to weigh', which comes from Latin pēnsāre 'to weigh', built on pendere 'to weigh, to hang' (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 4 of 5.
el ataúd — the coffin.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for the death notice, then the Spanish for the condolences.

You'll want to know: el ataúd.
el ataúd — "the coffin".
Seis amigos llevaron el ataúd. Six friends carried the coffin.
In Latin America el féretro is also used.

The word, taken apart.
ataúd was borrowed from Arabic tābūt 'a box, a coffin' (EtymDB), with the Arabic article joined to the front.

Lesson 5 of 5.
la herencia — the inheritance.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for the condolences, then the Spanish for the coffin.

You'll want to know: la herencia.
la herencia — "the inheritance".
Recibió una casa en herencia. He inherited a house.
Heredar is to inherit.

The word, taken apart.
herencia is usually traced to Latin haerentia 'things that cling, belongings', from haerēre 'to stick'. It was later pulled towards heredar 'to inherit', from Latin hērēs 'heir'.
