## You'll want to know: చెవుడు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C516-INDRIYAM516-03]; assesses=[] -->

**చెవుడు** — *cevuḍu* — "deafness".

> ముసలితనంలో ఆయనకు కొంచెం చెవుడు వచ్చింది.
>
> *musalitanaṁlō āyanaku koñceṁ cevuḍu vaccindi.*
>
> In old age he became a little deaf.

Built on చెవి, ear. చెవిటివాడు, a deaf man.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**చెవుడు** (*cevuḍu*) is built on the native చెవి, "ear" (DEDR 1977, with Tamil *cevi*), with a noun ending; DEDR does not list *cevuḍu* itself.
