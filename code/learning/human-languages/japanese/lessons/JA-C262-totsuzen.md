## You'll want to know: とつぜん
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C262-JIKO262-04]; assesses=[] -->

**とつぜん** — *totsuzen* — "suddenly; all of a sudden".

> **とつぜん くるまが とまりました。** — *totsuzen kuruma ga tomarimashita* — "All of a sudden the car stopped."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**とつぜん** (*totsuzen*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *totsu*, "protruding" (Middle Chinese *thwot*) and *zen*, "so" (*nyen*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
