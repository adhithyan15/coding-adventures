## You'll want to know: ぜんぶ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C184-GOODS184-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ぜんぶ** — *zenbu* — "all of it; everything, in all".

> **ぜんぶで せんえんです。** — *zenbu de sen'en desu* — "It comes to a thousand yen in all."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ぜんぶ** (*zenbu*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *zen*, "whole" (Middle Chinese *dzjwen*) and *bu*, "part, section" (*buX*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
