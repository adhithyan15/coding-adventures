## You'll want to know: liánlei
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C540-NOWONDER540-02]; assesses=[] -->

*liánlei* — "to drag someone into trouble".

> *yàoshi wǒ chūshì le jiù huì liánlei dàjiā* — If anything happens to me, everyone will be dragged into it.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*liánlei* is built from *lián* 'to join' and *lei*, both inherited from Middle Chinese, *ljen* and *ljweH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to join, to continue' and the second as being caught up in another's offence. The second is said light here.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what would happen.
