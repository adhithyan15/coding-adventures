## You'll want to know: りかいする
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C320-TACHIBA320-04]; assesses=[] -->

**りかいする** — *rikai suru* — "to comprehend; to understand fully".

> **くわしく せつめいしたので、みんな りかいしました。** — *kuwashiku setsumei shita node, minna rikai shimashita* — "I explained it in detail, so everyone understood."

Polite **りかいします**, *rikaishimasu*; a する verb. Past (polite): **みんなが りかいしました。**, *minna ga rikai shimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**りかいする** (*rikai suru*) is built from a Sino-Japanese noun and the native verb *suru*, "to do". The noun, *rikai*, is two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese: *ri*, "reason" (Middle Chinese *liX*) and *kai*, "understanding" (*keaX*) (KANJIDIC2; JMdict; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
