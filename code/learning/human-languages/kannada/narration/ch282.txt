Kannada, chapter 282: Accident, Ambulance, Stress, Nurse, Surprise.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಅಪಘಾತ (apaghāta) — an accident.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to fail, then the Kannada for to get stuck.

You'll want to know: ಅಪಘಾತ.
ಅಪಘಾತ — apaghāta — "an accident".

The word, taken apart.
ಅಪಘಾತ (apaghāta) is Sanskrit apaghāta, "a blow, a striking away".

Lesson 3 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for an accident, then the Kannada for an ambulance.

You'll want to know: ಒತ್ತಡ.
ಒತ್ತಡ — ottaḍa — "stress, pressure".

The word, taken apart.
ಒತ್ತಡ (ottaḍa) is native Dravidian, one word with Telugu ottu and Malayalam ottanam (DEDR 1021). DEDR lists ottaḍa, "pressure", beside ottu, "to press".

Lesson 4 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for an ambulance, then the Kannada for stress.

You'll want to know: ದಾದಿ.
ದಾದಿ — dādi — "a nurse".

The word, taken apart.
Where ದಾದಿ (dādi) comes from is uncertain. It may belong with Sanskrit dhātrī, "nurse" (CDIAL 6774), but no source records the Kannada word.
