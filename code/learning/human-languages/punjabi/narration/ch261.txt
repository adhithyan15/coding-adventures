Punjabi, chapter 261: The Train Timetable.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ਅੱਧਾ (addhā) — half.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for one and a half, then the Punjabi for two and a half.

You'll want to know: ਅੱਧਾ.
ਅੱਧਾ — addhā — "half".
ਮੈਂ ਅੱਧਾ ਘੰਟਾ ਸਟੇਸ਼ਨ ਉੱਤੇ ਉਡੀਕਦਾ ਹਾਂ। maiṁ addhā ghaṇṭā saṭeshan utte uḍīkdā hāṁ. I wait half an hour at the station.

The word, taken apart.
ਅੱਧਾ (addhā) is inherited from Sanskrit ardha, "half"; CDIAL lists Punjabi addhā, "half", under it (CDIAL 644).

What you've built.
One more word for reading notices and messages.

Lesson 2 of 5.
ਪਲੇਟਫ਼ਾਰਮ (paleṭfāram) — a railway platform.

[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for two and a half, then the Punjabi for half.

You'll want to know: ਪਲੇਟਫ਼ਾਰਮ.
ਪਲੇਟਫ਼ਾਰਮ — paleṭfāram — "a railway platform".
ਰੇਲ ਦੂਜੇ ਪਲੇਟਫ਼ਾਰਮ ਉੱਤੇ ਆਵੇਗੀ। rel dūje paleṭfāram utte āvegī. The train will come in at the second platform.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ਪਲੇਟਫ਼ਾਰਮ (paleṭfāram) is borrowed from English platform.

Lesson 3 of 5.
ਠਹਿਰਨਾ (ṭhahirnā) — to halt; to stop for a while.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for half, then the Punjabi for a railway platform.

You'll want to know: ਠਹਿਰਨਾ.
ਠਹਿਰਨਾ — ṭhahirnā — "to halt; to stop for a while".
ਰੇਲ ਹਰ ਸਟੇਸ਼ਨ ਉੱਤੇ ਦਸ ਮਿੰਟ ਠਹਿਰਦੀ ਹੈ। rel har saṭeshan utte das minṭ ṭhahirdī hai. The train halts for ten minutes at every station.
Past (it …): ਉਹ ਰੇਲ ਸਟੇਸ਼ਨ ਉੱਤੇ ਠਹਿਰੀ, oh rel saṭeshan utte ṭhahirī.

The word, taken apart.
ਠਹਿਰਨਾ (ṭhahirnā) is inherited from a reconstructed stabhira, "fixed, firm"; CDIAL lists Punjabi ṭhahirnā, "to stop, be settled", under it (CDIAL 13680).

Lesson 4 of 5.
ਚੱਲਣਾ (challṇā) — to set off; to go, of a bus or train.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for a railway platform, then the Punjabi for to halt.

You'll want to know: ਚੱਲਣਾ.
ਚੱਲਣਾ — challṇā — "to set off; to go, of a bus or train".
ਪਹਿਲੀ ਬੱਸ ਸਵੇਰੇ ਛੇ ਵਜੇ ਚੱਲਦੀ ਹੈ। pahilī bass savere chhe vaje challdī hai. The first bus sets off at six in the morning.
Past (it …): ਉਹ ਬੱਸ ਅੱਠ ਵਜੇ ਚੱਲੀ, oh bass aṭṭh vaje challī.

The word, taken apart.
ਚੱਲਣਾ (challṇā) is inherited from a reconstructed calyati, "goes"; CDIAL lists Punjabi callṇā, "to go, move", under it (CDIAL 4721).

Lesson 5 of 5.
ਰਵਾਨਾ (ravānā) — departing; leaving, with ਹੋਣਾ.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for to halt, then the Punjabi for to set off.

You'll want to know: ਰਵਾਨਾ.
ਰਵਾਨਾ — ravānā — "departing; leaving, with ਹੋਣਾ".
ਰੇਲ ਸਾਢੇ ਦਸ ਵਜੇ ਰਵਾਨਾ ਹੁੰਦੀ ਹੈ। rel sāḍhe das vaje ravānā hundī hai. The train leaves at half past ten.

The word, taken apart.
ਰਵਾਨਾ (ravānā) is a loan, usually traced to Persian ravāna, "going, setting out".
