# ખસવું (khasvũ) — to move aside, to shift

## You'll want to know: ખસવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C199-QUEUE199-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ખસવું** — *khasvũ* — "to move aside, to shift".

> **તે બાજુમાં ખસ્યો.** — *te bājumā̃ khasyo* — "He moved aside."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ખસવું** (*khasvũ*) is a tadbhava. CDIAL lists it, glossed "to slide, move, go to a distance", under *\*khasati*, "falls, slips", a reconstructed form (CDIAL 3856).
