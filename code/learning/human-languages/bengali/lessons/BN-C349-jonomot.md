## You'll want to know: জনমত
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C349-PROTIBAD349-03]; assesses=[] -->

**জনমত** — *jônômôt* — "public opinion".

> জনমত সরকারের বিরুদ্ধে।
>
> *jônômôt sôrkārer biruddhe.*
>
> Public opinion is against the government.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**জনমত** (*jônômôt*) is a modern compound of Sanskrit *jana*, people, and *mata*, an opinion (Monier-Williams): what the public thinks.
