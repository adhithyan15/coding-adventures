## You'll want to know: gāngqín
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C110-SKILL110-02]; assesses=[] -->

*gāngqín* — "piano".

> *tā jiā yǒu gāngqín* — Her family has a piano.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*gāngqín* is built from gāng 'steel' and qín, a stringed instrument, both inherited from Middle Chinese, *kang* and *gim* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription); the dictionary describes the second as an instrument of five strings, later seven. The piano is a modern sense of the pair.
