Persian, chapter 425: On the motorway.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
اتوبان (otobân) — a motorway (US: a freeway).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to pull over, then the Persian for safety.

You'll want to know: اتوبان.
اتوبان (otobân) — "a motorway (US: a freeway)".
از این شهر تا پایتخت اتوبان است. Az in shahr tâ pâytakht otobân ast. There is a motorway from this town to the capital.
The more formal word is bozorgrâh.

The word, taken apart.
otobân was borrowed from German Autobahn 'motorway' (EtymDB), a compound of Auto 'car' and Bahn 'way'.

Lesson 2 of 5.
عوارضی ('avârezi) — a toll booth, a toll gate.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for safety, then the Persian for a motorway.

You'll want to know: عوارضی.
عوارضی ('avârezi) — "a toll booth, a toll gate".
در عوارضی ماشینها کمی صبر میکنند. Dar 'avârezi mâshin-hâ kami sabr mikonand. At the toll gate the cars wait a little.
Built on 'avârez 'tolls, charges', an Arabic broken plural, + -i.

The word, taken apart.
'avârezi is built from 'avârez 'tolls, charges', a loan usually traced to the Arabic plural ʿawāriḍ 'things that come up, extra charges', and the Persian ending -i. EtymDB does not list it.

Lesson 3 of 5.
شانه خاکی (shâne-khâki) — a hard shoulder (of a road).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a motorway, then the Persian for a toll booth.

You'll want to know: شانه خاکی.
شانه خاکی (shâne-khâki) — "a hard shoulder (of a road)".
ماشین خراب را در شانه خاکی دیدیم. Mâshin-e kharâb râ dar shâne-khâki didim. We saw the broken-down car on the hard shoulder.
Literally 'earthy shoulder': shâne 'shoulder' + khâki 'of earth'.

The word, taken apart.
shâne-khâki is a native compound: shâne 'shoulder' is inherited from Middle Persian šānag (EtymDB), and khâki 'earthy' is built on khâk 'earth', from Middle Persian xāk (EtymDB).

Lesson 4 of 5.
ترافیک (terâfik) — traffic; a traffic jam.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a toll booth, then the Persian for a hard shoulder.

You'll want to know: ترافیک.
ترافیک (terâfik) — "traffic; a traffic jam".
صبحها در اتوبان ترافیک سنگین است. Sobh-hâ dar otobân terâfik sangin ast. In the mornings the traffic on the motorway is heavy.
Dar terâfik mândan — to be stuck in traffic.

The word, taken apart.
terâfik was borrowed from a European language: it is usually traced to English traffic or French trafic. EtymDB does not list the Persian word.

Lesson 5 of 5.
منحرف شدن (monharef shodan) — to swerve, to veer off; to go astray.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a hard shoulder, then the Persian for traffic.

You'll want to know: منحرف شدن.
منحرف شدن (monharef shodan) — "to swerve, to veer off; to go astray".
ماشین روی برف منحرف شد. Mâshin ru-ye barf monharef shod. The car swerved on the snow.
monharef 'turned aside' + shodan. Also figurative: to go off the right path.

The word, taken apart.
monharef shodan is built from monharef 'turned aside', a loan usually traced to Arabic munḥarif, and the native verb shodan 'to become'. EtymDB does not list it.
