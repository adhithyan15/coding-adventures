## You'll want to know: दृष्टिकोण
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C360-APATTI360-03]; assesses=[] -->

**दृष्टिकोण** — *dṛṣṭikoṇ* — "a point of view".

> हर आदमी का अपना दृष्टिकोण होता है।
>
> *har ādmī kā apnā dṛṣṭikoṇ hotā hai.*
>
> Everyone has their own point of view.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**दृष्टिकोण** (*dṛṣṭikoṇ*) joins two Sanskrit words, *dṛṣṭi*, "sight", and *koṇa*, "an angle, a corner" (Monier-Williams): the angle one looks from. The compound is a modern one.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
