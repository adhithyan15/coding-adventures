# మన్నించండి (manniñcaṇḍi) — please pardon me

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[TE-LEX-C93-FINE-04, TE-LEX-C93-FINE-05] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Telugu for gladly, then the Telugu for the body.

## You'll want to know: మన్నించండి
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C94-POLITE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**మన్నించండి** (*manniñcaṇḍi*) — "please pardon me".

It does the work of **క్షమించండి**, and is the gentler, native word beside it. Either answers
a mistake; **మన్నించండి** is the one to use with an elder.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

A second apology, softer than the first.
