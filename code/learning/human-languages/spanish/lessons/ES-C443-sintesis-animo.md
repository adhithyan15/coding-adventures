# (un mensaje antes del examen) — read it, then do it yourself

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-C443-ANIMO-04, ES-LEX-C443-ANIMO-03] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say *concentrarse* and *acercarse*. Both are in the message you are
about to read, and one of them is about a date rather than a person.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-C443-ANIMO-01, ES-LEX-C443-ANIMO-03, ES-LEX-C443-ANIMO-04] -->

Read both, then answer.

**1 — de Marta**

> Se **acerca** el examen y no me puedo **concentrar** en nada. Llevo dos días
> con dolor de cabeza: me **duele** por la mañana y se me pasa por la tarde.
> ¿Tú cómo lo llevas?

**2 — la respuesta**

> Yo igual. Me **arrepiento** de no haber empezado en septiembre. Pero me
> **interesa** de verdad la asignatura, así que algo se me quedará. Nos vemos el
> jueves y lo repasamos juntos.

[PAUSE 3s]
- [YOU SAY: what is making Marta anxious]
- [YOU SAY: when her headache comes and when it goes]
- [YOU SAY: what the second writer regrets, and what she says in spite of it]

## Why it's said this way
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-C443-ANIMO-01, ES-LEX-C443-ANIMO-02, ES-LEX-C443-ANIMO-05] -->

Four of the five verbs in those messages put the writer in the object, not the
subject. **Se acerca** el examen, **me duele** la cabeza, **me interesa** la
asignatura, **me arrepiento** — in every one, something else is doing the acting
and the writer is on the receiving end.

That is not a coincidence of these two messages. It is how Spanish talks about
inner states by default: the feeling arrives, the date approaches,
the subject interests. English reaches for *I am* plus an adjective — *I am
worried*, *I am interested* — and puts the person in charge of a state they did
not choose.

Neither is more accurate. But if you translate your English shape word for word
you will produce *estoy interesado en* where a Spanish speaker says *me
interesa*, and the sentence will be understood and will not sound like theirs.

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-C443-ANIMO-01, ES-LEX-C443-ANIMO-02, ES-LEX-C443-ANIMO-03, ES-LEX-C443-ANIMO-04, ES-LEX-C443-ANIMO-05] -->

[PAUSE 3s] Now your turn, out loud and then in writing. Five sentences about a
week before something difficult: say the date is getting close, say what hurts,
say you cannot concentrate, say what you regret, and say what still interests
you.

Then check yourself: did you write *me interesa*, or did you reach for *estoy
interesado*?
