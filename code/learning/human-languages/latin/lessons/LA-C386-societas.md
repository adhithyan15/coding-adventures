## You'll want to know: societās, societātis
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C386-SOCIETAS-01]; assesses=[] -->

**societās, societātis** — "a partnership, an alliance; society".

> Cum frātre societātem fēcit.
>
> *He went into partnership with his brother.*

*societās, societātis* f., built on *socius*, a partner, an ally. A business partnership in Roman law. English *society*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*societās* is built on *socius* "a partner, an ally" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short), a word kin to *sequor* "to follow": one who goes along with you. English *society* comes from it.
