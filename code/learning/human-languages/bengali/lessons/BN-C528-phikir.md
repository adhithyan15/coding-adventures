## You'll want to know: ফিকির
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C528-UPAY528-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ফিকির** — *phikir* — "a way out; a scheme to manage".

> একটা ফিকির পেলে আমরা ঝামেলাটা এড়াতে পারব।
>
> *Ekṭā phikir pele āmrā jhāmelāṭā eṛāte pārbô.*
>
> If we find a way out, we'll be able to get around the trouble.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ফিকির** (*phikir*) is usually traced to Arabic *fikr*, "thought", through Persian: a clever thought that gets you out of a fix.
