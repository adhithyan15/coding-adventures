## You'll want to know: zhùsù
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C425-HOTEL425-01]; assesses=[] -->

*zhùsù* — "accommodation; a place to stay".

> *zhèr de zhùsù hěn guì* — Accommodation is expensive here.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*zhùsù* is built from *zhù* 'to stay' and *sù* 'to lodge for the night', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *drjuH* and *sjuwk* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to stop' and the second 'a lodging'.
