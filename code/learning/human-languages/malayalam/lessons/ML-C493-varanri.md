# വാറന്റി (vāṟanṟi) — a warranty

## You'll want to know: വാറന്റി
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C493-VANGAL493-03]; assesses=[] -->

**വാറന്റി** — *vāṟanṟi* — "a warranty".

> ആ ഫോണിന് ഒരു വർഷത്തെ വാറന്റി ഉണ്ട്.
>
> *ā phōṇinŭ oru varṣatte vāṟanṟi uṇṭŭ.*
>
> That phone has a one-year warranty.

Keep the bill: the shop asks for it when you claim the warranty.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**വാറന്റി** (*vāṟanṟi*) was borrowed from English *warranty*, a maker's promise to repair a fault.
