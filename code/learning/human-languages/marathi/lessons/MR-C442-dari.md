## You'll want to know: दरी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C442-SHIKHAR442-02]; assesses=[] -->

**दरी** — *darī* — "a valley; a gorge".

> शिखरावरून खाली खोल दरी दिसते.
>
> *śikharāvarūn khālī khol darī diste.*
>
> From the summit you can see a deep valley below.

Feminine.

खोल दरी, a deep valley.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**दरी** (*darī*) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi *darī*, "chasm", under Sanskrit *dara*, *darī*, "a cleft, a cave" (CDIAL 6188).
