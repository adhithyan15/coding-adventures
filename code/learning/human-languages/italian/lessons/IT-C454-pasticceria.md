## You'll want to know: la pasticceria
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C454-ALIMENTARI-04]; assesses=[] -->

**la pasticceria** — "the cake shop, the pastry shop".

> La domenica compriamo le paste in pasticceria.
>
> *On Sundays we buy pastries at the cake shop.*

*Le paste* here are pastries, not pasta.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*pasticceria* is built on *pasticcio* 'pie', from *pasta* 'dough', which is inherited from Late Latin *pasta*, from Greek *pastá* 'barley porridge' (EtymDB).
