## You'll want to know: xiàzài
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C109-SCREEN109-05]; assesses=[] -->

*xiàzài* — "to download".

> *nǐ xiàzài le ma* — Have you downloaded it?

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*xiàzài* is built from 下 'down' and zài 'to carry', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *haeX* and *dzojH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'to go down' and 'to transport'. A modern word.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for everyday things.
