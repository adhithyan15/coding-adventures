## You'll want to know: پہچاننا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C279-GAVAHI279-01]; assesses=[] -->

**پہچاننا** — *pahchānnā* — "to recognize".

> اگر میں قریب ہوتا تو اسے پہچانتا۔
>
> *agar maiṅ qarīb hotā to use pahchāntā.*
>
> If I had been closer, I would have recognized him.

Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے چور کو پہچانا, *us ne chor ko pahchānā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**پہچاننا** (*pahchānnā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *pahcānnā* under Sanskrit *pratyabhijānāti*, "recognizes" (CDIAL 8637). A person recognized takes **کو**, and the verb stays masculine: **چور کو پہچانا**.
