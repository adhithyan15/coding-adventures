## You'll want to know: やわらかい
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C496-SOFT496-01]; assesses=[] -->

**やわらかい** — *yawarakai* — "soft; tender".

> **この にくは やわらかいです。** — *kono niku wa yawarakai desu* — "This meat is tender."

An i-adjective.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**やわらかい** (*yawarakai*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi, and its older origin is not settled (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
