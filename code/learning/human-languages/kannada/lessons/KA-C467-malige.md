## You'll want to know: ಮಳಿಗೆ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C467-MARUKATTE467-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ಮಳಿಗೆ** — *maḷige* — "a store; a shop unit".

> ಈ ಬೀದಿಯಲ್ಲಿ ಬಟ್ಟೆಯ ಮಳಿಗೆಗಳು ಇವೆ.
>
> *ī bīdiyalli baṭṭeya maḷigegaḷu ive.*
>
> There are clothes stores on this street.

Plural: ಮಳಿಗೆಗಳು, *maḷigegaḷu*.

A large shop or a showroom; ಅಂಗಡಿ is any shop.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಮಳಿಗೆ** (*maḷige*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 4757 lists Kannada *maḷige*, "a warehouse, a shop".
