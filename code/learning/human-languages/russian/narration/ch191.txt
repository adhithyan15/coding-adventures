Lesson 1 of 5.
батарея (bataréya) — a radiator; a battery.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a toilet, then the Russian for shampoo.

You'll want to know: батарея.
батарея (bataréya) — "a radiator; a battery".

The word, taken apart.
батарея (bataréya) — from 1697; it came from German Batterie or French batterie, from battre, "to beat" (Vasmer): first a group of guns, then a row of cells or heating pipes.

розетка (rozétka) — "a socket".

The word, taken apart.
розетка (rozétka) came through German Rosette or straight from French rosette, "a little rose", from rose, "a rose" (Vasmer): an electric socket was a round rose-shaped fitting.

Lesson 4 of 5.
зарядка (zaryádka) — a charger; morning exercises.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a socket, then the Russian for a wire.

You'll want to know: зарядка.
зарядка (zaryádka) — "a charger; morning exercises".

The word, taken apart.
зарядка (zaryádka) is built on зарядить, "to charge, to load", за- with the root of ряд, "a row, an order", and снаряд, "equipment"; зарядка (zaryádka) also means "morning exercises", charging the body.
