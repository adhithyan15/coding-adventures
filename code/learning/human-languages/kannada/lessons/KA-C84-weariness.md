# ಸುಸ್ತು (sustu) — weariness, feeling worn out

## You'll want to know: ಸುಸ್ತು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C84-BODY-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸುಸ್ತು** (*sustu*) — "weariness", the everyday word for feeling worn out.

**ಸುಸ್ತಾಗಿದೆ** (*sustāgide*) — "I'm worn out". **ಆಯಾಸ** is a little more formal;
**ಸುಸ್ತು** is what you hear at home.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

The everyday cousin of āyāsa.
