## You'll want to know: commeō, commeāre
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C460-TURBA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**commeō, commeāre** — "to go back and forth, to come and go".

> Mercātōrēs inter portum et forum commeant.
>
> *Merchants go back and forth between the harbour and the forum.*

*commeō, commeāre, commeāvī, commeātum*. The noun *commeātus* is the coming and going, then supplies and leave.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*commeō* is built from *con-* and *meō* 'to go, to pass' (EtymDB; Lewis & Short).
