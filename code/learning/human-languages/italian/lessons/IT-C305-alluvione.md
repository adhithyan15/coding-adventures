## You'll want to know: l'alluvione
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C305-ALLUVIONE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**l'alluvione** — "the flood".

> Dopo l'alluvione la piazza era piena di fango.
>
> *After the flood the square was full of mud.*

Feminine: *un'alluvione*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*alluvione* is a learned borrowing from Latin *alluviō* "an overflowing" (Lewis & Short), from *alluere* "to wash against".
