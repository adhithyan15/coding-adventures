## You'll want to know: bān
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C107-MOVE107-03]; assesses=[] -->

*bān* — "to move house; to move (something heavy)".

> *nǐ néng bāng wǒ bān zhuōzi ma* — Can you help me move the table?

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*bān* has no older form on record under this character. The dictionary lists the same sound, *pan* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), under an older character glossed 'to transport', which is probably the same word, though that is not settled.
