# आखरी — final, on the page

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-SCRIPT-AAKHRI-01]; assesses=[MW-LEX-AAKHRI-01, MW-SCRIPT-AABHAAR-01, MW-SCRIPT-GARMI-01, MW-LEX-GARMI-01] -->

[PAUSE 22s] Say *final*, then write formal thanks and heat.

## You'll want to know
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MW-SCRIPT-AA-INDEPENDENT-01, MW-SCRIPT-KHA-01, MW-SCRIPT-RA-01, MW-SCRIPT-II-MATRA-01, MW-SCRIPT-DIKHAVO-01] -->

> **आ** + **ख** + **री** → **आखरी** — *ākhrī* — final

Four signs, all secure. The word opens on the standing **आ** of **आभार**, and
its second sign is the **ख** you traced for **दिखावो**. Write **दिखावो** and
**आखरी** one under the other and check that the **ख** is drawn the same way in
both.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MW-SCRIPT-AAKHRI-01, MW-SCRIPT-KHA-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: delayed-copy -->

Look at **आखरी**, cover it, wait five seconds, and write it from the sound.
Check the standing **आ** is at full height and the **ी** reaches above the
headstroke.
