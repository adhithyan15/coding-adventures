## You'll want to know: 全家
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C381-FAMILY381-02]; assesses=[] -->

**全家** — *quánjiā* — "the whole family".

> **今天我们全家一起打扫房子** *jīntiān wǒmen quánjiā yìqǐ dǎsǎo fángzi* — Today the whole family is cleaning the house together.

The new character, in pieces: 全 — six strokes: a wide roof like 人, then a bar, a second bar, a vertical down from the first through the second, and a long bar at the foot. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**全家** (*quánjiā*) is built from 全 'whole' and 家 'a home', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *dzjwen* and *kae* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'complete'.
