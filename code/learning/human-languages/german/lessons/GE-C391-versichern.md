## You'll want to know: versichern
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C391-STEUER-05]; assesses=[] -->

**versichern** — "to insure; to assure".

> Wir haben das Haus gegen Feuer versichert.
>
> *We have insured the house against fire.*

Inseparable: *er versichert, er hat versichert*. Also to assure: *Ich versichere dir, dass …* *Die Versicherung* — the insurance.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*versichern* is built with *ver-* on *sicher* "safe", inherited from Old High German *sihhur*, which goes back to Latin *sēcūrus* "free from care" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short): you make a thing safe.
