## Script — the speech piece, then guǒ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-SCRIPT-KE-LESSON-01]; assesses=[] -->

> 课

**Two pieces, left before right.**

**讠, two strokes**, as in 说 and 话.

**Guǒ, eight strokes**: a box like 日, its left side, the top that turns down, the middle bar and the bar that closes it; then a long horizontal, a vertical down through the box and the bar, a left-falling stroke and a stroke falling to the right.

2 + 8 = ten.
