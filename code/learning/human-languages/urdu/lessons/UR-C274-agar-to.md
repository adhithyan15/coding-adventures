# اگر … تو (agar … to) — if, then

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[UR-LEX-C159-VAJAH159-03, UR-GRAMMAR-C269-SO-02, UR-GRAMMAR-C270-DIRECTION-02] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "if". (*agar*.) Then "then I'll stay at home". (*to maiṅ ghar par rahūṅ gā*.) **اگر** opens a condition; **تو** opens what follows.

## You'll want to know: اگر … تو
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-GRAMMAR-C274-IF-01]; assesses=[UR-LEX-C159-VAJAH159-03, UR-LEX-C78-TIME78-01, UR-LEX-ANA, UR-LEX-C51-VERBS51-02, UR-LEX-C47-VERBS47-04] -->

> **اگر وقت ہے،**
>
> *agar vaqt hai,*
>
> **تو آئیے۔**
>
> *to āiye.* — "If you have time, come."

**اگر** (*agar*), "if", opens the condition; **تو** (*to*), "then", opens the result. In talk, **تو** often drops out.

> **اگر آپ چاہتے ہیں،**
>
> *agar āp chāhte haiṅ,*
>
> **تو ہم چلیں گے۔**
>
> *to ham chaleṅ ge.* — "If you like, we'll go."

## You'll want to know: ورنہ, or else
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-GRAMMAR-C274-IF-02]; assesses=[UR-LEX-C159-VAJAH159-05, UR-LEX-C77-TIME77-03, UR-LEX-C271-CHHUTNA-01, UR-GRAMMAR-C265-FUTURE-01] -->

> **جلدی چلیے،**
>
> *jaldī chaliye,*
>
> **ورنہ گاڑی چھوٹ جائے گی۔**
>
> *varnā gāṛī chhūṭ jāe gī.* — "Hurry, or we'll miss the train."

**ورنہ** (*varnā*), "otherwise", turns the condition round: do this, or that will happen.
