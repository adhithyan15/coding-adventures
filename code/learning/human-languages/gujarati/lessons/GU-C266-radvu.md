## You'll want to know: રડવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C266-FEELINGS266-04]; assesses=[] -->

**રડવું** — *raḍvũ* — "to cry, to weep".

> **શું બાળક રડે છે?** — *śũ bāḷak raḍe che?* — "Is the baby crying?"

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**રડવું** (*raḍvũ*) is a tadbhava. It comes from Sanskrit *raṭati*, "cries, yells"; CDIAL lists Gujarati *raṛvũ*, "to weep" (CDIAL 10590).
