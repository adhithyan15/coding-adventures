## You'll want to know: اکاؤنٹ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C417-RAQAM417-01]; assesses=[] -->

**اکاؤنٹ** — *akāunṭ* — "a bank account".

> میرا اکاؤنٹ اس بینک میں ہے۔
>
> *merā akāunṭ is baink meṅ hai.*
>
> My account is with this bank.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**اکاؤنٹ** (*akāunṭ*) is borrowed from English *account*.
