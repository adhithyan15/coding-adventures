## You'll want to know: мешать
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C433-SHUM-02]; assesses=[] -->

**мешать** (*meshát*) — "to disturb, to bother; to stir".

> Музыка соседей мешает нам спать.
>
> *Múzyka sosédey mesháyet nam spat.*
>
> The neighbours' music keeps us from sleeping.

Imperfective; perfective *помешать*. Takes the dative: *мешать соседям*. Also to stir: *мешать суп*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*мешать* is usually traced to an old Slavic verb meaning 'to mix', which fits *мешать суп* 'to stir soup'; to disturb is to mix things up for someone. EtymDB lists the word without a source.
