**il ritorno** — "the return".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ritorno** is the noun of *ritornare*, "to come back": *ri-* plus *tornare*, "to return" ← Latin *tornare*, "to turn". (EtymDB 2.1 for *tornare*)
