## You'll want to know: یوگا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C516-FITNESS-02]; assesses=[] -->

**یوگا** (*yogâ*) — "yoga".

> مادرم هفته ای سه بار یوگا میکند.
>
> *mâdaram hafte-i se bâr yogâ mikonad.*
>
> My mother does yoga three times a week.

*yogâ kardan* — to do yoga.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*yogâ* is a modern loan, usually traced through English to Sanskrit *yoga*, 'yoking, joining'. EtymDB has no entry for the Persian word.
