## You'll want to know: пожар
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C295-POZHAR-01]; assesses=[] -->

**пожар** (*pozhár*) — "a fire (a blaze)".

> В доме напротив был пожар.
>
> *V dóme naprótiv byl pozhár.*
>
> There was a fire in the building opposite.

A fire that destroys. *Пожарный* — a firefighter.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*пожар* is built on *по-* and *жар* "heat", from the same Slavic root as *гореть* "to burn"; EtymDB lists *пожар* with no parent.
