## Guided Practice: every verb, three ways
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[FR-GRAM-IMPERATIF-01, FR-GRAM-FUTUR-PROCHE-01, FR-GRAM-NE-CHUTE-01, FR-LEX-PARLER-01, FR-GRAM-ER-PRESENT-02, FR-LEX-TRAVAILLER-06, FR-ETYMON-TRAVAILLER-07, FR-LEX-HABITER-04, FR-ETYMON-HABITER-05, FR-LEX-AIMER-09, FR-LEX-DEMANDER-04, FR-FALSEFRIEND-DEMANDER-06, FR-LEX-MARCHER-07, FR-SEMANTICS-MARCHER-WORK-09, FR-LEX-COURIR-10, FR-LEX-LIRE-06, FR-LEX-ECRIRE-08, FR-LEX-ENTENDRE-01, FR-LEX-DORMIR-04, FR-LEX-FINIR-01] -->

[PAUSE 4s] Every verb the book owns, commanded, put in the near future, and
refused.

- [YOU SAY: commanded — "Parle ! Travaille ! Écoute ! Marche ! Demande !"]
- [YOU SAY: about to happen — "Je vais lire. Je vais écrire. Je vais dormir. Je
  vais courir."]
- [YOU SAY: refused, in speech — "Je comprends pas. J'entends pas. Je finis pas."]
- [YOU SAY: the false friend, still false — *demander* asks, it does not demand]
- [YOU SAY: the one that means both walk and work — *marcher*]

## Guided Practice: the distant band — on, and the whole table
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[FR-LEX-ON-01, FR-GRAM-FUTUR-PROCHE-01, FR-GRAM-IR-PRESENT-02, FR-LEX-FINIR-01, FR-LEX-PAIN-01, FR-LEX-VIN-05, FR-LEX-CAFE-01, FR-LEX-THE-03, FR-LEX-LAIT-05, FR-LEX-EAU-03, FR-LEX-FROMAGE-01, FR-LEX-SEL-05, FR-LEX-SUCRE-07, FR-LEX-BEURRE-03, FR-LEX-OEUF-07, FR-GRAM-DU-PARTITIF-07, FR-GRAM-DES-01, FR-GRAM-DE-LA-01, FR-GRAM-AU-AUX-01, FR-LEX-ILYA-01, FR-GRAM-CEST-01, FR-LEX-PARCEQUE-01, FR-LEX-MAIS-03, FR-LEX-ET-01, FR-LEX-OU-CONJ-02, FR-LEX-FRANCAIS-09, FR-LEX-ICI-01, FR-LEX-LABAS-03] -->

[PAUSE 4s] The whole table, with *on* in front of it and the joiners between.

- [YOU SAY: "On va au café. On prend du pain et du fromage."]
- [YOU SAY: "Il y a du café et du thé, mais il n'y a pas de lait."]
- [YOU SAY: "On finit le pain, parce qu'on a faim."]
- [YOU SAY: the whole table in the plural — "des œufs, du sel, du sucre, du
  beurre, de l'eau"]
- [YOU SAY: "On parle français ici, mais on parle anglais là-bas."]
- [YOU SAY: the three cousins of *on* — French *on*, German *man*, English *one*]

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[FR-GRAM-IMPERATIF-01, FR-GRAM-FUTUR-PROCHE-01, FR-GRAM-IR-PRESENT-02, FR-GRAM-NE-CHUTE-01, FR-LEX-ON-01] -->

[PAUSE 4s] How do you build a command? (**The *tu* form without its pronoun** —
and without its *-s* on an *-er* verb.) What two pieces make the near future?
(***Aller*** **plus a plain verb**.) What grows in the plural of *finir*? (**An
extra *-iss-***.) Which half of the negative does speech drop? (**The *ne***.)
And what was *on* in Latin? (***Homo***, "man".)
