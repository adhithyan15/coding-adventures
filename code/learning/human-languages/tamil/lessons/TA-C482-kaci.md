## You'll want to know: கசி
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C482-KAYAM482-05]; assesses=[] -->

**கசி** — *kaci* — "to ooze; to seep".

> காயத்திலிருந்து இரத்தம் கசிந்தது.
>
> *kāyattiliruntu irattam kacintatu.*
>
> Blood oozed from the wound.

Past (it …): கசிந்தது, *kacintatu*.

Also of damp walls and of tears.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**கசி** (*kaci*) is native Dravidian (DEDR 1091), of moisture seeping through a wall or eyes welling up.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
