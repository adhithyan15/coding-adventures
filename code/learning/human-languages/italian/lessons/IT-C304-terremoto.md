## You'll want to know: il terremoto
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C304-TERREMOTO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**il terremoto** — "the earthquake".

> Il terremoto è durato venti secondi.
>
> *The earthquake lasted twenty seconds.*

A lively child is also *un terremoto*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*terremoto* is inherited from Latin *terrae mōtus* "a moving of the earth" (EtymDB).
