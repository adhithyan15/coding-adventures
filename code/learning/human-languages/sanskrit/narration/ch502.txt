Sanskrit, chapter 502: In the Workshop.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
कर्मशाला (karmaśālā) — a workshop.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for radio, then the Sanskrit for the internet.

You'll want to know: कर्मशाला.
कर्मशाला — karmaśālā — "a workshop".
मम पिता कर्मशालायां कार्यं करोति। mama pitā karmaśālāyāṃ kāryaṃ karoti. My father works in a workshop.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
कर्मशाला (karmaśālā) is built from karman, "work", and śālā, "a hall, a room"; MW records karmaśālā as the room where the day's work is done, a workshop (MW).

तक्षकः — takṣakaḥ — "a carpenter".
तक्षकः नूतनाम् आसन्दीं रचयति। takṣakaḥ nūtanām āsandīṃ racayati. The carpenter makes a new chair.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
तक्षकः (takṣakaḥ) comes from the root √takṣ, "to pare, to fashion by cutting", with the agent ending -aka; MW gives takṣaka the sense "a carpenter" (MW).

Lesson 3 of 5.
तक्षति (takṣati) — pares; shapes wood.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a workshop, then the Sanskrit for a carpenter.

You'll want to know: तक्षति.
तक्षति — takṣati — "pares; shapes wood".
तक्षकः दारु तक्षति। takṣakaḥ dāru takṣati. The carpenter shapes the wood.
Past (he …): सः दारु तष्टवान्, saḥ dāru taṣṭavān.

The word, taken apart.
तक्षति (takṣati) comes from the root √takṣ, "to form by cutting, to chisel"; its past participle is taṣṭa, and KRM lists the past taṣṭavān used here (MW).
