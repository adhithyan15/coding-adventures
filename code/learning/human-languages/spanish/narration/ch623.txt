el castigo — the punishment.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for to force, then the Spanish for the obligation.

You'll want to know: el castigo.
el castigo — "the punishment".
El castigo fue muy duro. The punishment was very harsh.
Un castigo justo — a fair punishment.

The word, taken apart.
castigo is built on castigar, inherited from Latin castigare 'to correct, to chastise', built on castus 'pure' (EtymDB).

Lesson 3 of 6.
la sanción — the penalty, the sanction.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for the punishment, then the Spanish for to punish.

You'll want to know: la sanción.
la sanción — "the penalty, the sanction".
El club recibió una sanción. The club received a sanction.
A formal word for a punishment or fine.

The word, taken apart.
sanción is a learned borrowing from Latin sanctio 'a decree, the penalty clause of a law', built on sancire 'to make sacred, to decree' (EtymDB).
