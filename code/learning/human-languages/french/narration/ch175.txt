French, chapter 175: To kiss, To greet, To thank, To invite, To park.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
embrasser — to kiss.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for a meeting, then the French for a contract.

You'll want to know: embrasser.
embrasser — "to kiss".

The word, taken apart.
embrasser — built from em-, "into", and bras, "arm": to take into the arms, now to kiss.

The word, taken apart.
garer from Old French garer, warer, "to shelter, to guard" (EtymDB 2.1), from a Germanic verb meaning "to watch over" — borrowed: to park is to put in shelter.
