## Changed — ఆ and ఇ are written without lifting the pen, and ఏ lifts once

Native Telugu writers draw ఆ and ఇ in one stroke and ఏ in two (HP Labs
India's LipiTk Telugu recognizer: 99%, 98% and 89% of its stored
prototypes; counts and shares only). Their strips and steps now do too.
ఆ (TE-S166) runs on from the bowl up into the right lobe, turns it
clockwise and comes back down through the crossing to draw the bar
leftward, with no lift. ఇ (TE-S135) starts at the free tip of the
upper-left lobe, draws both upper parts from left to right, comes down the
right side, sweeps left under the bowl and back along its top, and ends
down the tail. ఏ (TE-S163) draws its body as ఎ does, round the loop and on
up the outer arch, and lifts only for the hook above. The lessons'
prose follows the numbered strips and needed no change.
