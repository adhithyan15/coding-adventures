# ધ — high entry, broad lower body, separate spine, dha

## Warm-up

## Script — meet the shape
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-SCRIPT-DHA-01]; assesses=[] -->

Keep **ધ** visible. It is **dha**. Begin at the high left entry, descend through
the upper turn and narrow middle, then sweep around the broad lower body into
the right shoulder. Lift once; descend the tall right spine and turn through its foot.
