## You'll want to know: સંકલ્પ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C382-DHYEY382-02]; assesses=[] -->

**સંકલ્પ** — *sankalp* — "a resolution".

> જો તમારો સંકલ્પ મજબૂત હશે તો તમે જૂની ટેવ છોડી શકશો.
>
> *jo tamāro sankalp majbūt haśe to tame jūnī ṭev choḍī śakśo.*
>
> If your resolve is strong, you will be able to give up an old habit.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**સંકલ્પ** (*sankalp*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *saṃkalpa*, "a conception of the mind, a resolve" (Monier-Williams).

One more word for saying what happens if.
