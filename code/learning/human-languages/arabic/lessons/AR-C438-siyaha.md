## You'll want to know: سياحة
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C438-SIYAHA438-01]; assesses=[] -->

**سياحة** — *siyāḥa* — "tourism".

> تعمل أختي في السياحة.
>
> *taʿmalu ukhtī fī as-siyāḥa.*
>
> My sister works in tourism.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**سياحة** (*siyāḥa*) is built on the native Arabic root *s-y-ḥ*, "to flow; to roam the land" (Lane). It is the verbal noun of *sāḥa*, "to travel about".
