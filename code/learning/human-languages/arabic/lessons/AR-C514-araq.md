## You'll want to know: أرق
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C514-TAAB514-01]; assesses=[] -->

**أرق** — *araq* — "insomnia".

> عانى أبي من الأرق قبل السفر.
>
> *ʿānā abī min al-araq qabla as-safar.*
>
> My father suffered from insomnia before the trip.

Do not mix it up with عرق, ʿaraq, sweat.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**أرق** (*araq*) is built on the native Arabic root *ʾ-r-q*, "to be sleepless" (Lane). Araq is sleeplessness at night.
