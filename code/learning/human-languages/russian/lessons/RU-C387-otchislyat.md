## You'll want to know: отчислять
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C387-ZNANIYA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**отчислять** (*otchislyát*) — "to expel (from a university); to deduct".

> Студента отчислили за прогулы.
>
> *Studénta otchíslili za progúly.*
>
> The student was expelled for missing classes.

Imperfective; perfective *отчислить*. It also means to deduct or transfer money: *отчислять налоги*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**отчислять** is built from *от-* and *число* "number", inherited from Proto-Slavic *\*čislo* (EtymDB): to take a name off the count.
