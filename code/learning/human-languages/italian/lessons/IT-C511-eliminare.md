## You'll want to know: eliminare
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C511-RISULTATI-05]; assesses=[] -->

**eliminare** — "to knock out, to eliminate".

> La nostra squadra è stata eliminata ai quarti.
>
> *Our team was knocked out in the quarter-finals.*

*Essere eliminati* — to be knocked out.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*eliminare* is a learned borrowing from Latin *ēlīmināre* "to put out of doors" (Lewis & Short), from *ē-* and *līmen* "threshold".
