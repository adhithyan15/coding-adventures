## You'll want to know: जाम
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C404-TRAIFIK404-02]; assesses=[] -->

**जाम** — *jām* — "a traffic jam".

> पुल पर बड़ा जाम था।
>
> *pul par baṛā jām thā.*
>
> There was a big jam on the bridge.

Masculine.

जाम लगना, for a jam to build up: सड़क पर जाम लगा है.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**जाम** (*jām*) is borrowed from English *jam*, a road packed so tight that nothing moves.
