Marathi, chapter 405: Planes and Runways.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
विमान (vimān) — an aeroplane.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to brush against, then the Marathi for to crowd thickly.

You'll want to know: विमान.
विमान — vimān — "an aeroplane".
आमचं विमान वेळेवर उतरलं. āmcaṁ vimān veḷevar utarlaṁ. Our plane landed on time.
Neuter.
विमानाने, by plane; विमानतळ, the airport, is built on it.

The word, taken apart.
विमान (vimān) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit vimāna, which names the flying chariot of the old stories (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 3 of 5.
धावपट्टी (dhāvpaṭṭī) — a runway.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for an aeroplane, then the Marathi for a flight.

You'll want to know: धावपट्टी.
धावपट्टी — dhāvpaṭṭī — "a runway".
विमान धावपट्टीवर उभं आहे. vimān dhāvpaṭṭīvar ubhaṁ āhe. The plane is standing on the runway.
Feminine.
धाव is a run and पट्टी a strip: the strip planes run along.

The word, taken apart.
धावपट्टी (dhāvpaṭṭī) is a Marathi compound of धाव, "a run", inherited from dhāva (CDIAL 6800), and पट्टी, "a strip".

Lesson 4 of 5.
सीमाशुल्क (sīmāśulka) — border customs duty.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a flight, then the Marathi for a runway.

You'll want to know: सीमाशुल्क.
सीमाशुल्क — sīmāśulka — "border customs duty".
विमानतळावर आम्ही सीमाशुल्क भरलं. vimāntaḷāvar āmhī sīmāśulka bharlaṁ. We paid customs duty at the airport.
Neuter.
The official word on forms and boards; the older word is जकात.

The word, taken apart.
सीमाशुल्क (sīmāśulka) is a modern word built from Sanskrit sīmā, "boundary", and śulka, "toll, price" (Monier-Williams): the toll paid at the border.
