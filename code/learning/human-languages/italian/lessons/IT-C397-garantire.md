## You'll want to know: garantire
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C397-GARANZIA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**garantire** — "to guarantee".

> Ti garantisco che domani è pronto.
>
> *I guarantee you it will be ready tomorrow.*

Conjugated with *-isc-*: *garantisco*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*garantire* is built on *garante* "guarantor", from French *garant*, a word of Germanic, probably Frankish, origin; EtymDB sets it beside Old High German *werento* "guarantor" (EtymDB), but the exact form behind it is not settled.
