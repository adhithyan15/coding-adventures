## You'll want to know: विमर्शः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C364-VISHESHAJNA364-02]; assesses=[] -->

**विमर्शः** — *vimarśaḥ* — "deliberation".

> विमर्शं विना निर्णयं न करोतु।
>
> *vimarśaṃ vinā nirṇayaṃ na karotu.*
>
> Make no decision without deliberation.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**विमर्शः** (*vimarśaḥ*) is built on *vi* and the root √mṛś, "to touch, to consider"; MW gives *vi-marśa* "consideration, deliberation, critical test, examination" (MW).
