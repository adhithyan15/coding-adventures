# Session Map — Gujarati Chapters 1–5

Same spaced-repetition schedule as the other tracks (a word from session *N*
resurfaces at *N+1, N+3, N+7, N+15*). Lessons named by slug; this map is the
authoritative order.

**There is no reading course.** The Gujarati script — the "headless" script,
Devanagari without the top line — is learned *through* the words: each word
lesson introduces exactly the letters that word needs.

## Chapter 1 — Greetings

| Session | Lesson | Word | Root / hook |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | namaste | નમસ્તે | "hello" ← Sanskrit *namas*+*te*; the headless script, no top line |
| 2 | aabhaar | આભાર | "thanks" ← *ā*+*bhṛ* "to bear"; cousin of English *bear*, Portuguese *obrigado* |
| 3 | haa-naa | હા / ના | "yes / no"; *nā* ← PIE *ne (English *no*) |
| 4 | saarun | સારું | "good/okay" ← Sanskrit *sāra*; the **three genders** *sāro/sārī/sārũ* |
| 5 | aavjo | આવજો | "goodbye" = "come again," polite imperative of *āvvũ* "to come" |
| 6 | practice | (recap) | the five greetings + top line + three genders |

## Chapter 2 — Introducing Yourself

| Session | Lesson | Word | Root / hook |
|---|---|---|---|
| 7 | naam | નામ | "name" ← PIE *h₃nómn̥* (English *name*) |
| 8 | maarun | મારું | "my" ← *ma-* (English *me/my*); neuter to agree with *nām* |
| 9 | chhe | છે | "is" — Gujarati's **own** copula, not Hindi *hai*; verb goes last |
| 10 | maarun-naam-chhe | મારું નામ … છે | "my name is…" assembled |
| 11 | tu-tame | તું / તમે | familiar / respectful "you"; courtesy-by-plural |
| 12 | shun | શું | "what"; the odd *sh-* cousin in a *k-* question family |
| 13 | tamarun-naam-shun-chhe | તમારું નામ શું છે? | "what's your name?" assembled |
| 14 | anand | આનંદ | "joy" ← Sanskrit *ānanda*; "pleased to meet you" |
| 15 | practice | (recap) | the whole introduction exchange |

## Chapter 3 — How Are You
