Russian, chapter 528: Consoles and Bloggers.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
приставка (pristávka) — a games console; a prefix.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to switch, then the Russian for a screen adaptation.

You'll want to know: приставка.
приставка (pristávka) — "a games console; a prefix".
На день рождения сын получил игровую приставку. Na den rozhdéniya syn poluchíl igrovúyu pristávku. For his birthday my son got a games console.
Feminine. Игровая приставка (pristávka) — a games console. In grammar, a prefix.

The word, taken apart.
приставка (pristávka) is built on приставить 'to put next to, to attach', from ставить 'to stand, to put'; EtymDB lists the word without a source.
