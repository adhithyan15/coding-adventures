Punjabi, chapter 339: No, Thank You.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ਇਨਕਾਰ (inkār) — a refusal; saying no.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for an order, then the Punjabi for to interrupt.

You'll want to know: ਇਨਕਾਰ.
ਇਨਕਾਰ — inkār — "a refusal; saying no".
ਮੈਂ ਇਨਕਾਰ ਨਹੀਂ ਕਰਦਾ ਹਾਂ। maiṁ inkār nahīṁ kardā hāṁ. I am not refusing.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ਇਨਕਾਰ (inkār) is a loan, usually traced through Persian to Arabic inkār, "denial".

What you've built.
One more word for asking and answering.

[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Punjabi for an order, then the Punjabi for to interrupt, then say inkār again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Lesson 2 of 5.
ਟਲਣਾ (ṭalṇā) — to back out; to go back on one's word.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for to interrupt, then the Punjabi for a refusal.

You'll want to know: ਟਲਣਾ.
ਟਲਣਾ — ṭalṇā — "to back out; to go back on one's word".
ਮੇਰਾ ਦੋਸਤ ਆਪਣੀ ਗੱਲ ਤੋਂ ਨਹੀਂ ਟਲਦਾ ਹੈ। merā dost āpṇī gall ton nahīṁ ṭaldā hai. My friend does not go back on his word.
Past (he …): ਉਹ ਆਪਣੀ ਗੱਲ ਤੋਂ ਟਲਿਆ, oh āpṇī gall ton ṭaliā.

The word, taken apart.
ਟਲਣਾ (ṭalṇā) is inherited from Sanskrit ṭalati, "moves aside" (a sense CDIAL infers); CDIAL lists Punjabi ṭalṇā, "to slip away", under it (CDIAL 5450). CDIAL does not give the sense "to back out".

Lesson 5 of 5.
ਅੜਨਾ (aṛnā) — to dig one's heels in; to refuse to budge.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for an excuse, then the Punjabi for by no means.

You'll want to know: ਅੜਨਾ.
ਅੜਨਾ — aṛnā — "to dig one's heels in; to refuse to budge".
ਬੱਚਾ ਅੜਦਾ ਹੈ ਅਤੇ ਰੋਟੀ ਨਹੀਂ ਖਾਂਦਾ ਹੈ। bachchā aṛdā hai ate roṭī nahīṁ khāndā hai. The child digs his heels in and won't eat his food.
Past (he …): ਉਹ ਆਪਣੀ ਗੱਲ ਉੱਤੇ ਅੜਿਆ, oh āpṇī gall utte aṛiā.

The word, taken apart.
ਅੜਨਾ (aṛnā) is inherited from a reconstructed aḍ, "obstruct, stop"; CDIAL lists Punjabi aṛnā, "to stop, stick fast", under it (CDIAL 187).
