## You'll want to know: destruir
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C529-BOMBA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**destruir** — "to destroy".

> El fuego destruyó tres casas.
>
> *The fire destroyed three houses.*

It takes a *y*: *destruyo*, *destruyó*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*destruir* comes from Latin *dēstruere* 'to tear down, to destroy' (EtymDB), the reverse of *struere* 'to pile up, to build'.
