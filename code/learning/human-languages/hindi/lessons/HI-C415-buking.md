## You'll want to know: बुकिंग
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C415-RAJISTAR415-03]; assesses=[] -->

**बुकिंग** — *buking* — "a booking".

> मेरी बुकिंग तीन रात की है।
>
> *merī buking tīn rāt kī hai.*
>
> My booking is for three nights.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**बुकिंग** (*buking*) is borrowed from English *booking*; it is feminine in Hindi.
