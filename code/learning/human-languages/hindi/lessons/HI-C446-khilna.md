## You'll want to know: खिलना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C446-PRAKRITI446-04]; assesses=[] -->

**खिलना** — *khilnā* — "to bloom; to open (of a flower)".

> वसंत में बगीचे में फूल खिलते हैं।
>
> *vasant meṁ bagīce meṁ phūl khilte haiṁ.*
>
> In spring the flowers bloom in the garden.

Past (it …): वह फूल सुबह खिला, *vah phūl subah khilā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**खिलना** (*khilnā*) goes back to *\*khiḍ*, *\*khil*, "be open, expand", forms CDIAL reconstructs (CDIAL 3882), which lists Hindi *khilnā*, "to open, bloom". A face खिलता too, when it lights up.
