## You'll want to know: अवधानम्
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C465-UDYAMIN465-04]; assesses=[] -->

**अवधानम्** — *avadhānam* — "attention".

> अवधानेन पाठं शृणोतु।
>
> *avadhānena pāṭhaṃ śṛṇotu.*
>
> Listen to the lesson with attention.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अवधानम्** (*avadhānam*) is built from *ava*, "down", and the root √dhā, "to put": putting the mind down on a thing; MW gives "attention, attentiveness" (MW).
