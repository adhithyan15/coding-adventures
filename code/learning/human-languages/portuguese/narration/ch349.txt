Portuguese, chapter 349: Visto que e dado que.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
visto que — since, seeing that.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for the factor, then the Portuguese for the consequence.

You'll want to know: visto que.
visto que — "since, seeing that".
Visto que está a chover, ficamos em casa. Since it is raining, we are staying at home.
Takes the indicative. A little formal; in speech como at the start of a sentence.

The word, taken apart.
visto que is built from visto "seen", the participle of ver, inherited from Latin vidēre "to see" (Lewis & Short), and que, from Latin quid (EtymDB): "it being seen that".

Lesson 3 of 5.
uma vez que — seeing as, since.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for since, then the Portuguese for given that.

You'll want to know: uma vez que.
uma vez que — "seeing as, since".
Uma vez que já estás aqui, podes ajudar-me. Since you are already here, you can help me.
With the indicative it gives a reason. With the subjunctive it means once or provided that.

The word, taken apart.
uma vez que is built from uma vez "once", with vez inherited from Latin vicis "change, turn, place" (Lewis & Short), and que.

Lesson 4 of 5.
consequentemente — consequently, as a result.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for given that, then the Portuguese for seeing as.

You'll want to know: consequentemente.
consequentemente — "consequently, as a result".
Perdi o autocarro e, consequentemente, cheguei atrasado. I missed the bus and, as a result, I arrived late.
Formal; in speech por isso.

The word, taken apart.
consequentemente is built on consequente with the adverb ending -mente; consequente is a learned borrowing from Latin cōnsequēns "following", on sequī "to follow" (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 5 of 5.
devido a — due to, because of.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for seeing as, then the Portuguese for consequently.

You'll want to know: devido a.
devido a — "due to, because of".
O voo chegou atrasado devido ao nevoeiro. The flight arrived late due to the fog.
Devido a + noun: devido à chuva. With a verb: devido a ter chovido.

The word, taken apart.
devido a is built on devido "owed, due", the participle of dever, inherited from Latin dēbēre "to owe" (Lewis & Short): what is owed to a cause.
