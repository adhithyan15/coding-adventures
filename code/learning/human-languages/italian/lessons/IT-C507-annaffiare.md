## You'll want to know: annaffiare
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C507-ORTO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**annaffiare** — "to water (plants)".

> Chi annaffia le piante quando siamo in vacanza?
>
> *Who waters the plants when we are on holiday?*

Also spelt *innaffiare*. The watering can is *l'annaffiatoio*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*annaffiare* is inherited from Vulgar Latin *inafflāre* "to blow onto, to sprinkle" (EtymDB), from Latin *afflāre*, built on *flāre* "to blow".
