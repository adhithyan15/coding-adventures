Lesson 1 of 5.
क्वचित (kvacit) — rarely.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for patience, restraint, then the Marathi for luck, fate.

You'll want to know: क्वचित.
क्वचित — kvacit — "rarely".

The word, taken apart.
क्वचित (kvacit) is Sanskrit kvacit, "somewhere, at times", a tatsama: kva, "where", with -cit, "some-". CDIAL has no entry for it.

Lesson 2 of 5.
मग (mag) — then, after that.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for luck, fate, then the Marathi for rarely.

You'll want to know: मग.
मग — mag — "then, after that".

The word, taken apart.
मग (mag) is inherited. CDIAL lists Marathi mag, "then, after", as an adverbial use of Sanskrit mārga, "track, road" (CDIAL 10071).

Lesson 4 of 5.
अजून (ajūn) — still, yet.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for then, then the Marathi for finally.

You'll want to know: अजून.
अजून — ajūn — "still, yet".

The word, taken apart.
अजून (ajūn) is a tadbhava, a word inherited and reshaped by sound change. It comes from adyāhnaḥ, "this very day", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 244). It is adya, "today", and ahnaḥ, "of the day". CDIAL glosses the Marathi word "even now".
