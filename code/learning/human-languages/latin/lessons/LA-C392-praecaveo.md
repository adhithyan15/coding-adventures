## You'll want to know: praecaveō, praecavēre
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C392-CAVEO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**praecaveō, praecavēre** — "to take precautions, to guard against beforehand".

> Ab īnsidiīs praecāvit.
>
> *He took precautions against the ambush.*

*praecaveō, praecavēre, praecāvī, praecautum*: *prae-* beforehand + *caveō*. English *precaution*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*praecaveō* is built from *prae-* "before" and *caveō* "to beware" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short). English *precaution* comes from its family.
