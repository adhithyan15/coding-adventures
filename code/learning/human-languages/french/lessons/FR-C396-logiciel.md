## You'll want to know: le logiciel
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C396-LOGICIEL-04]; assesses=[] -->

**le logiciel** — "the software, the program".

> Ce logiciel est gratuit.
>
> *This software is free.*

Québec also says *le programme*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*logiciel* is usually described as a French coinage of the twentieth century from *logique* "logic" and *-iel*, made to replace English *software*. *logique* comes from Greek *logikḗ* "the art of reasoning".
