## You'll want to know: liàng
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C179-WASH179-03]; assesses=[] -->

*liàng* — "to hang out to dry".

> *jīntiān tiānqì hěn hǎo nǐ qù liàng yīfu* — The weather's nice today: go and hang the washing out.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*liàng* No dictionary we use records an older form, so its history is not settled.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what I do.
