## You'll want to know: કાટમાળ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C305-BHUKAMP305-05]; assesses=[] -->

**કાટમાળ** — *kāṭmāḷ* — "rubble".

> લોકોએ કાટમાળ નીચેથી એક બાળકને કાઢ્યું.
>
> *lokoe kāṭmāḷ nīcethī ek bāḷakne kāḍhyũ.*
>
> People pulled a child out from under the rubble.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**કાટમાળ** (*kāṭmāḷ*) No dictionary we use records an older form, so its history is not settled. It is what is left of a building that has fallen: broken walls, stones and wood.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
