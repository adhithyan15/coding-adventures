# ಕಿರಿಯ (kiriya) — younger; junior

## You'll want to know: ಕಿರಿಯ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C526-BALAGA526-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ಕಿರಿಯ** — *kiriya* — "younger; junior".

> ನನ್ನ ಕಿರಿಯ ಮಗ ಶಾಲೆಗೆ ಹೋಗುತ್ತಾನೆ.
>
> *nanna kiriya maga śālege hōguttāne.*
>
> My younger son goes to school.

The opposite is ಹಿರಿಯ, elder.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಕಿರಿಯ** (*kiriya*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 1594 lists Kannada *kiṟi*, "small, little", with *kiṟiya*, "younger".
