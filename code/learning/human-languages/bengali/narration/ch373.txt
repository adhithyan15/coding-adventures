Bengali, chapter 373: All or Part.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
একেবারে (ekebāre) — completely; absolutely.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a level, then the Bengali for an amount.

You'll want to know: একেবারে.
একেবারে — ekebāre — "completely; absolutely".
এই রাস্তায় গেলে আমরা একেবারে ঠিক সময়ে পৌঁছাব। ei rāstāy gele āmrā ekebāre ṭhik sômôye poũchhābô. If we take this road, we'll arrive exactly on time.

The word, taken apart.
একেবারে (ekebāre) is এক, one (CDIAL 2462), with বার, a time or turn, and the Bengali -এ: 'all at one go', so completely. With না it means 'not at all'.

Lesson 3 of 5.
অন্তত (ôntôtô) — at least.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for absolutely, then the Bengali for complete.

You'll want to know: অন্তত.
অন্তত — ôntôtô — "at least".
অন্তত দশজন এলে সভা হবে। ôntôtô dôshjôn ele sôbhā hôbe. The meeting will go ahead if at least ten people come.

The word, taken apart.
অন্তত (ôntôtô) is Sanskrit antatas, 'from the end', which Monier-Williams also gives as 'in the lowest way': counting from the bottom, at the very least.

Lesson 4 of 5.
প্রায় (prāy) — almost; nearly.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for complete, then the Bengali for at least.

You'll want to know: প্রায়.
প্রায় — prāy — "almost; nearly".
কাজ প্রায় শেষ; কাল এলে দেখবে। kāj prāy shesh; kāl ele dekhbe. The work is almost finished; if you come tomorrow, you'll see.

The word, taken apart.
প্রায় (prāy) is Sanskrit prāya, 'for the most part, almost' (Monier-Williams). With the ending -ই it gives the taught প্রায়ই, often.

Lesson 5 of 5.
আংশিক (āngshik) — partial; in part.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for at least, then the Bengali for nearly.

You'll want to know: আংশিক.
আংশিক — āngshik — "partial; in part".
জল কম থাকলে কারখানা আংশিক বন্ধ থাকবে। jôl kôm thākle kārkhānā āngshik bôndhô thākbe. If water is short, the factory will be partly shut.

The word, taken apart.
আংশিক (āngshik) is built on Sanskrit aṃśa, a share or part (Monier-Williams), with the adjective ending -ika: 'in part'. The adjective itself is a later formation.
