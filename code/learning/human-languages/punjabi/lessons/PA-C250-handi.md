## You'll want to know: ਹਾਂਡੀ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C250-GHR250-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ਹਾਂਡੀ** — *hāṇḍī* — "a clay cooking pot".

> ਹਾਂਡੀ ਚੁੱਲ੍ਹੇ ਉੱਤੇ ਹੈ।
>
> *hāṇḍī chullhe utte hai.*
>
> The clay pot is on the stove.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਹਾਂਡੀ** (*hāṇḍī*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*hāṇḍa*, "pot"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *hā̃ḍī*, "cooking pot" (CDIAL 14050).
