Sanskrit, chapter 105: Television, Computer, Machine, Fan, Box.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
दूरदर्शनम् (dūradarśanam) — a television.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a shirt, then the Sanskrit for a telephone.

You'll want to know: दूरदर्शनम्.
दूरदर्शनम् — dūradarśanam — "a television".

The word, taken apart.
dūradarśana is built from dūra "far" and darśana "seeing", from the root √dṛś "see" (MW): "far-seeing". It is a modern coinage, made the way English television was made from Greek tele "far" + Latin visio "seeing".

You'll want to know: संगणकम्.
संगणकम् — saṅgaṇakam — "a computer".

The word, taken apart.
saṅgaṇaka is built from sam "together" and the root √gaṇ "count, reckon" (MW), with the agent suffix -aka: "the one that reckons it all up". It is a modern coinage for the computer, made the way English made computer from compute.

Lesson 3 of 5.
यन्त्रम् (yantram) — a machine.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a television, then the Sanskrit for a computer.

You'll want to know: यन्त्रम्.
यन्त्रम् — yantram — "a machine".

The word, taken apart.
yantra comes from the root √yam "hold, restrain" with the tool suffix -tra (CDIAL 10412): in the Rigveda it is a controlling device, a prop or a fetter, and later any contrivance or machine (MW). Punjabi jandrā "padlock" comes from it (CDIAL 10412).

Lesson 4 of 5.
व्यजनम् (vyajanam) — a fan.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a computer, then the Sanskrit for a machine.

You'll want to know: व्यजनम्.
व्यजनम् — vyajanam — "a fan".

The word, taken apart.
vyajana "a fan" comes from the root √vīj, also written vyaj, "to fan, to cool by fanning" (MW; CDIAL 12043). Its twin vījana lives on as Hindi bījnā "fan" (CDIAL 12043).

Lesson 5 of 5.
पेटिका (peṭikā) — a box.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a machine, then the Sanskrit for a fan.

You'll want to know: पेटिका.
पेटिका — peṭikā — "a box".

The word, taken apart.
peṭikā is peṭaka "a little basket, a box" with the feminine suffix -ikā, and MW takes peṭaka from piṭaka "basket". CDIAL 8164 calls the family non-Aryan, and DEDR 4388 sets it beside Dravidian words for a box; Marathi peṭī "box" belongs to it (CDIAL 8164).
