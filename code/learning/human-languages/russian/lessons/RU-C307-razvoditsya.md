## You'll want to know: разводиться
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C307-RAZVOD-02]; assesses=[] -->

**разводиться** (*razvodítsya*) — "to get divorced".

> Его родители развелись давно.
>
> *Yevó rodíteli razvelís davnó.*
>
> His parents divorced long ago.

Imperfective; the perfective is *развестись* (*razvestís*), past *развёлся* (*razvyólsya*), *развелась*. It takes *с* + instrumental.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*разводиться* is built on *раз-* "apart" and *водить* "to lead", inherited from Proto-Slavic *voditi* (EtymDB), with the reflexive *-ся*: to be led apart.
