Punjabi, chapter 104: Jeth, Harh, Savan, Bhadon, Assu.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ਜੇਠ (jeṭh) — Jeth, a Punjabi month.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for Chet, then the Punjabi for Visakh.

You'll want to know: ਜੇਠ.
ਜੇਠ — jeṭh — "Jeth, a Punjabi month".

The word, taken apart.
ਜੇਠ (jeṭh) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from jyaiṣṭha, "the month May-June" (CDIAL 5293).
