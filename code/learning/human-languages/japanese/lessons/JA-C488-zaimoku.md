## You'll want to know: ざいもく
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C488-BUILD488-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ざいもく** — *zaimoku* — "timber; lumber".

> **くるまで ざいもくを はこびます。** — *kuruma de zaimoku o hakobimasu* — "We carry the timber by car."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ざいもく** (*zaimoku*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *zai*, "lumber" and *moku*, "tree" (Middle Chinese *muwk*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
