## You'll want to know: वरदान
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C335-DEVTA335-03]; assesses=[] -->

**वरदान** — *vardān* — "a boon".

> देवता ने राक्षस को एक वरदान दिया।
>
> *devtā ne rākṣas ko ek vardān diyā.*
>
> The god granted the demon a boon.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**वरदान** (*vardān*) is Sanskrit *varadāna* (Monier-Williams), *vara*, a wish or a choice, and *dāna*, a giving: a wish granted by a god.
