## You'll want to know: أزمة
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C386-TAAWUN386-05]; assesses=[] -->

**أزمة** — *azma* — "a crisis".

> مرت البلاد بأزمة صعبة.
>
> *marrat al-bilād bi-azma ṣaʿba.*
>
> The country went through a hard crisis.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**أزمة** (*azma*) is built on the native Arabic root *ʾ-z-m*, "to bite hard; to be severe" (Lane). An *azma* is a time of hardship: a crisis.
