## You'll want to know: ненадолго
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C278-VPERVYYE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ненадолго** (*nenadólgo*) — "for a short while, not for long".

> Она зашла ненадолго и скоро ушла.
>
> *Oná zashlá nenadólgo i skóro ushlá.*
>
> She dropped in for a short while and soon left.

The opposite is *надолго* — for a long time.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ненадолго* is built on *не*, *на* and *долго* "for long", the adverb of *долгий* "long", which is inherited from Proto-Slavic *dĭlgŭ* (EtymDB).
