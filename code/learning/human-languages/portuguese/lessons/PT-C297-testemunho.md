## You'll want to know: o testemunho
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C297-TRIBUNAL-03]; assesses=[] -->

**o testemunho** — "the testimony, the evidence of a witness".

> O testemunho dela ajudou o juiz.
>
> *Her testimony helped the judge.*

Also any proof: *um testemunho de amizade*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*testemunho* comes from Latin *testimōnium* "testimony" (EtymDB), from *testis* "a witness".
