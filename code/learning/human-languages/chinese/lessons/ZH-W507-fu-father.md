## Script — two short strokes, then two long ones
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-SCRIPT-FU-FATHER-01]; assesses=[] -->

> 父

**Two short strokes on top**: on the left a stroke falling to the left, on the right a dot falling to the right.

**Then two long strokes that cross**: a long stroke falling to the left, then a long stroke falling to the right, like the legs of 人 set wide.

2 + 2 = four.
