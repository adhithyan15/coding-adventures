## You'll want to know: indignātiō, indignātiōnis
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C514-CONT-04]; assesses=[] -->

**indignātiō, indignātiōnis** — "indignation, outrage".

> Cīvēs magnā cum indignātiōne clāmant.
>
> *The citizens shout with great indignation.*

Feminine, third declension, from *indignor*. English *indignation*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**indignātiō** is the action noun of *indignor* "to be indignant" (Lewis & Short; EtymDB). English *indignation* comes from it.
