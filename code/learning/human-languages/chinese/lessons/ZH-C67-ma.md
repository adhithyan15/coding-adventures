## You'll want to know: 马
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C67-ANIMALS67-01]; assesses=[] -->

**马** — *mǎ* — "horse".

> **我喜欢白马** *wǒ xǐhuan bái mǎ* — I love white horses.

The new character, in pieces: 马 — a turn, a zigzag that hooks, and a bar along the bottom: the right-hand piece of 吗 and 妈. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**马** (*mǎ*) is inherited from Middle Chinese *maeX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription); the dictionary explains its old character as the shape of a head, a mane, a tail and four legs.
