## You'll want to know: ভ্যান
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C388-TRAM388-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ভ্যান** — *bhæn* — "a van; a cycle van".

> একটা ভ্যান আমাদের মালপত্র স্টেশনে নিয়ে গেল।
>
> *ekṭā bhæn āmāder mālpôtrô sṭeshône niye gelô.*
>
> A van took our luggage to the station.

In a small town a ভ্যান is often a pedalled tricycle with a flat wooden bed.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ভ্যান** (*bhæn*) is borrowed from English *van*; Bengali has no v, so the word begins with ভ, and the y-phala gives the English vowel æ.
