## You'll want to know: dàodǐ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C372-ANYWAY372-02]; assesses=[] -->

*dàodǐ* — "on earth (in a question)".

> *nǐ dàodǐ xiǎng shuō shénme* — What on earth are you trying to say?

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*dàodǐ* is built from 到 'to arrive' and *dǐ*, both inherited from Middle Chinese, *tawH* and *tejX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to arrive' and the second 'below'.
