Portuguese, chapter 443: Serviços públicos.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
a esquadra — the police station; the squadron.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for the cloister, then the Portuguese for to wander.

You'll want to know: a esquadra.
a esquadra — "the police station; the squadron".
Fui à esquadra porque me roubaram a carteira. I went to the police station because my wallet was stolen.
In Brazil: a delegacia.

The word, taken apart.
esquadra was borrowed from Italian squadra 'square, team', which goes back to Vulgar Latin exquadrāre 'to make square', on quadrus 'square' (Lewis & Short).

What you've built.
One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.

Lesson 3 of 5.
a conservatória — the registry office.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for the police station, then the Portuguese for the barracks.

You'll want to know: a conservatória.
a conservatória — "the registry office".
Casaram na conservatória da cidade. They got married at the town registry office.
Births, marriages and deaths are recorded at a conservatória do registo civil.

The word, taken apart.
conservatória is built on conservar 'to keep', from Latin cōnservāre 'to preserve' (EtymDB): the office that keeps the records.

Lesson 4 of 5.
denunciar — to report (a crime); to denounce.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for the barracks, then the Portuguese for the registry office.

You'll want to know: denunciar.
denunciar — "to report (a crime); to denounce".
Denunciámos o roubo na esquadra. We reported the theft at the police station.
Also to expose someone: denunciar um colega.

The word, taken apart.
denunciar is a learned borrowing from Latin dēnūntiāre 'to announce, to give notice' (EtymDB), on nūntius 'messenger'.
