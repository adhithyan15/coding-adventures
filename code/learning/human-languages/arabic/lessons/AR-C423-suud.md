## You'll want to know: صعود
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C423-BAWWABA423-04]; assesses=[] -->

**صعود** — *ṣuʿūd* — "boarding; going up".

> بدأ الصعود بعد التأخير.
>
> *badaʾa aṣ-ṣuʿūd baʿda at-taʾkhīr.*
>
> Boarding began after the delay.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**صعود** (*ṣuʿūd*) is built on the native Arabic root *ṣ-ʿ-d*, "to go up, to ascend" (Lane). It is the verbal noun of *ṣaʿida*, "to go up".
