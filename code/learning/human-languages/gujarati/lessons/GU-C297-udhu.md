## You'll want to know: ઊંધું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C297-CHETAV297-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ઊંધું** — *ū̃dhũ* — "upside down".

> અકસ્માત પછી ગાડી ઊંધી પડી હતી.
>
> *akasmāt pachī gāḍī ū̃dhī paḍī hatī.*
>
> After the accident the car lay upside down.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ઊંધું** (*ū̃dhũ*) goes back to Sanskrit *avamūrdha*, "head down", and CDIAL lists the Gujarati word, "upside down" (CDIAL 804). It agrees like an adjective: **ઊંધી ગાડી**, **ઊંધું વાસણ**.
