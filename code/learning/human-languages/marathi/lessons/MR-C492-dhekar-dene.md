# ढेकर देणे (ḍhekar deṇe) — to burp

## You'll want to know: ढेकर देणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C492-UCAKI492-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ढेकर देणे** — *ḍhekar deṇe* — "to burp".

> पोटभर जेवल्यावर आजोबांनी ढेकर दिली.
>
> *poṭbhar jevlyāvar ājobānnī ḍhekar dilī.*
>
> After a full meal Grandfather burped.

Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने ढेकर दिली, *tyāne ḍhekar dilī*.

ढेकर, a burp, is feminine in everyday use (ढेकर आली, ढेकर दिली), though CDIAL marks it masculine. ढेकर येणे, with the dative.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ढेकर देणे** (*ḍhekar deṇe*) is a Marathi phrase built on ढेकर, "a burp", which is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi *ḍhẽkar*, "belch", under *\*ḍakkāra* (CDIAL 5521); देणे is the light verb.
