French, chapter 288: Attention aux escrocs.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
le pickpocket — the pickpocket.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for the forced entry, then the French for the loot.

You'll want to know: le pickpocket.
le pickpocket — "the pickpocket".
Un pickpocket m'a pris mon téléphone dans le métro. A pickpocket took my phone on the underground.
Borrowed from English. Signs warn: attention aux pickpockets.

The word, taken apart.
pickpocket was borrowed from English pickpocket (EtymDB), one who picks pockets.

What you've built.
One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.

Lesson 2 of 5.
l'arnaque — the scam, the rip-off (informal).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for the loot, then the French for the pickpocket.

You'll want to know: l'arnaque.
l'arnaque — "the scam, the rip-off (informal)".
Ce restaurant est une vraie arnaque. This restaurant is a real rip-off.
Informal. The verb is arnaquer, to rip off.

The word, taken apart.
arnaque is built on the slang verb arnaquer "to rip off" (EtymDB); the origin of the verb is not settled.

Lesson 3 of 5.
l'escroc — the swindler, the con artist.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for the pickpocket, then the French for the scam.

You'll want to know: l'escroc.
l'escroc — "the swindler, the con artist".
L'escroc se faisait passer pour un policier. The con artist was passing himself off as a police officer.
The final c is silent.

The word, taken apart.
escroc was borrowed from Italian scrocco "sponging, living at others' cost" (EtymDB).

Lesson 4 of 5.
escroquer — to swindle, to con.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for the scam, then the French for the swindler.

You'll want to know: escroquer.
escroquer — "to swindle, to con".
Il a escroqué des personnes âgées. He swindled elderly people.
Escroquer quelqu'un de cent euros — to con someone out of a hundred euros.

The word, taken apart.
escroquer was borrowed from Italian scroccare "to sponge, to get by trickery" (EtymDB).

Lesson 5 of 5.
l'escroquerie — the fraud, the swindle.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for the swindler, then the French for to swindle.

You'll want to know: l'escroquerie.
l'escroquerie — "the fraud, the swindle".
Il a été arrêté pour escroquerie. He was arrested for fraud.
The formal and legal word; l'arnaque is the everyday one.

The word, taken apart.
escroquerie is built on escroquer "to swindle" (EtymDB), which was borrowed from Italian scroccare.
