Marathi, chapter 512: Made in a Factory.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
कारखाना (kārkhānā) — a factory.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a business partner, then the Marathi for to invest.

You'll want to know: कारखाना.
कारखाना — kārkhānā — "a factory".
आमच्या शहराजवळ साखरेचा कारखाना आहे. āmcyā śahrājvaḷ sākhrecā kārkhānā āhe. There is a sugar factory near our town.
Masculine. Plural: कारखाने, kārkhāne.

The word, taken apart.
कारखाना (kārkhānā) is a loan from Persian kārkhāna, "workshop", from kār, "work", and khāna, "house".

उत्पादन (utpādan) — production; output.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to invest, then the Marathi for a factory.

You'll want to know: उत्पादन.
उत्पादन — utpādan — "production; output".
या कारखान्यात रोज हजार सायकलींचं उत्पादन होतं. yā kārkhānyāt roj hajār sāyaklīñcaṁ utpādan hotaṁ. A thousand bicycles are made in this factory every day.
Neuter.
उत्पादन (utpādan) होणे, to be produced.

The word, taken apart.
उत्पादन (utpādan) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit utpādana, "bringing forth, producing" (Monier-Williams).

पुरवठा (puravṭhā) — a supply.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for production, then the Marathi for goods.

You'll want to know: पुरवठा.
पुरवठा — puravṭhā — "a supply".
कारखान्याला कच्च्या मालाचा पुरवठा कमी पडला. kārkhānyālā kaccyā mālācā puravṭhā kamī paḍlā. The factory ran short of raw materials.
Masculine.
पुरवठा (puravṭhā) कमी पडणे, to run short.

The word, taken apart.
पुरवठा (puravṭhā) is built on पुरवणे, "to provide", the causative of पुरणे, "to suffice", inherited from Sanskrit pūrayati, "fills" (CDIAL 8335).

Lesson 5 of 5.
खपणे (khapṇe) — to sell well; to toil.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for goods, then the Marathi for a supply.

You'll want to know: खपणे.
खपणे — khapṇe — "to sell well; to toil".
सणांच्या दिवसांत सगळा माल खपला. saṇāñcyā divsānt sagḷā māl khaplā. In the festival days all the goods sold out.
Past (he …): तो माल लवकर खपला, to māl lavkar khaplā.
The goods are the subject: माल (māl) खपतो, the goods sell.

The word, taken apart.
खपणे (khapṇe) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Marathi khapṇẽ, "to be consumed, to work", under Sanskrit kṣapyatē, "is destroyed" (CDIAL 3655); goods that sell are used up.
