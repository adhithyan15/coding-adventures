# こ — two strokes, and the greeting begins

## Script — the shape
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-SCRIPT-KO-01]; assesses=[] -->

> こ

The simplest sign you have met. Two short horizontal strokes, stacked, the lower
one longer and slightly curved:

1. the **upper** stroke, short, curving down a little at its right end
2. the **lower** stroke, wider, sweeping right and lifting slightly

Read **ko**: a clean *k*, then the *o* of English *more* shortened.

Nothing joins them. The gap between the two strokes is part of the shape — close
it and you have a different sign.
