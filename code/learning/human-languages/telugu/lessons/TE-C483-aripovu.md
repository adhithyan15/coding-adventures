# ఆరిపోవు (āripōvu) — to go out; to die down

## You'll want to know: ఆరిపోవు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C483-AVIRI483-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ఆరిపోవు** — *āripōvu* — "to go out; to die down".

> గాలికి దీపం ఆరిపోయింది.
>
> *gāliki dīpaṁ āripōyindi.*
>
> The lamp went out in the wind.

Past (it …): ఆరిపోయింది, *āripōyindi*.

ఆరు, to cool, to go out, + పోవు. A fire, a lamp or wet clothes drying: బట్టలు ఆరిపోయాయి. Its subject is a thing, so the past shown is the third person: ఆరిపోయింది, it went out.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ఆరిపోవు** (*āripōvu*) is native Dravidian: ఆరు, "to cool, to be put out, to dry" (DEDR 404), with పోవు, "to go" (DEDR 4572).
