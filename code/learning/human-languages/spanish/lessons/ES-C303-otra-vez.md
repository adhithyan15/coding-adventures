## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C303-REPAIR-03]; assesses=[] -->

**otra vez** — "again."

Word by word it is "another turn." **Vez** is Latin *vicem*, and *vicem* meant a
**turn taken in rotation** — your go, coming round again.

English holds that exact word in several places. *Vice versa* is "the turn having
been changed." A *vicar* stands in somebody else's turn. A *vice-*president takes
the turn of the president.

So *otra vez* does not say "repeat." It says **another go round** — a kinder
thing to ask of a stranger, and it pairs with what you already have.
