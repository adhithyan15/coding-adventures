## You'll want to know: el roble
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C693-ARBOLES-05]; assesses=[] -->

**el roble** — "the oak tree".

> Comimos a la sombra de un roble.
>
> *We ate in the shade of an oak tree.*

*Fuerte como un roble* — as strong as an ox.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*roble* is inherited from Latin *rōbur* "an oak tree; hardness, strength" (EtymDB).
