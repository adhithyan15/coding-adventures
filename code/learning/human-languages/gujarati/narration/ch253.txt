Gujarati, chapter 253: Hobbies and spare hours.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
શોખ (śokh) — hobby, keen interest.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for uniform, then the Gujarati for to retire.

You'll want to know: શોખ.
શોખ — śokh — "hobby, keen interest".
મારા દાદાને ગાવાનો શોખ હતો. — mārā dādāne gāvāno śokh hato — "My grandfather was fond of singing."

The word, taken apart.
શોખ (śokh) is a loan from Arabic shawq, "longing, desire", which reached Gujarati through Persian. Its Arabic root is sh-w-q (Lane).

Lesson 3 of 5.
માણવું (māṇvũ) — to enjoy, to relish.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for hobby, then the Gujarati for leisure.

You'll want to know: માણવું.
માણવું — māṇvũ — "to enjoy, to relish".
અમે ગઈકાલે રજા માણી. — ame gaīkāle rajā māṇī — "We enjoyed the holiday yesterday."
Not the taught માનવું, mānvũ, "to believe".

The word, taken apart.
માણવું (māṇvũ) is a tadbhava. CDIAL lists it, glossed "to enjoy", under Sanskrit mānáyati, "esteems, honours" (CDIAL 10047).

Lesson 5 of 5.
અજમાવવું (ajmāvvũ) — to try out, to test.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to enjoy, then the Gujarati for to get bored.

You'll want to know: અજમાવવું.
અજમાવવું — ajmāvvũ — "to try out, to test".
મેં નવો શોખ અજમાવ્યો. — mẽ navo śokh ajmāvyo — "I tried out a new hobby."

The word, taken apart.
અજમાવવું (ajmāvvũ) is a loan, usually traced to Persian āzmā-, the stem of āzmūdan, "to test, to try", made into a Gujarati verb with -āv-. CDIAL has no entry for it.
