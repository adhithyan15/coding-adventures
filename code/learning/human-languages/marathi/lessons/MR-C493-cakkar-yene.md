# चक्कर येणे (cakkar yeṇe) — to feel dizzy

## You'll want to know: चक्कर येणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C493-TOL493-02]; assesses=[] -->

**चक्कर येणे** — *cakkar yeṇe* — "to feel dizzy".

> उन्हात उभं राहिल्यामुळे तिला चक्कर आली.
>
> *unhāt ubhaṁ rāhilyāmuḷe tilā cakkar ālī.*
>
> She felt dizzy from standing in the sun.

Past (to him …): त्याला चक्कर आली, *tyālā cakkar ālī*.

Dative. चक्कर also means a round: चक्कर मारणे, to go for a spin.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**चक्कर येणे** (*cakkar yeṇe*) is a Marathi phrase built on चक्कर, a loan from Hindustani *cakkar*, "a whirl", which goes back to Sanskrit *cakra*, "wheel" (CDIAL 4538); येणे is the light verb.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
