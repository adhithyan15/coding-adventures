Kannada, chapter 355: Digging Up the Past.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಉತ್ಖನನ (utkhanana) — an excavation.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a royal court, then the Kannada for a prince.

You'll want to know: ಉತ್ಖನನ.
ಉತ್ಖನನ — utkhanana — "an excavation".
ಉತ್ಖನನದಲ್ಲಿ ಹಳೆಯ ಮಡಕೆಗಳು ಸಿಕ್ಕಿದವು. utkhananadalli haḷeya maḍakegaḷu sikkidavu. Old pots were found in the excavation.
The careful digging done to find old things.

The word, taken apart.
ಉತ್ಖನನ (utkhanana) is borrowed from Sanskrit utkhanana, "digging or tearing out" (Monier-Williams).

ಪುರಾತತ್ವ — purātatva — "archaeology".
ಅವಳು ಕಾಲೇಜಿನಲ್ಲಿ ಪುರಾತತ್ವ ಓದುತ್ತಿದ್ದಾಳೆ. avaḷu kālējinalli purātatva ōduttiddāḷe. She is studying archaeology at college.
ಪುರಾತತ್ವ (purātatva) ಇಲಾಖೆ is the department that looks after old monuments.

The word, taken apart.
ಪುರಾತತ್ವ (purātatva) is borrowed from Sanskrit, a modern compound of purā, "formerly, of old" (Monier-Williams), and tattva, "the real state, truth" (CDIAL 5642).

ಬೆಟ್ಟದ ಮೇಲೆ ಹಳೆಯ ಕೋಟೆಯ ಅವಶೇಷಗಳು ಇವೆ. beṭṭada mēle haḷeya kōṭeya avaśēṣagaḷu ive. There are the ruins of an old fort on the hill.
Plural: ಅವಶೇಷಗಳು, avaśēṣagaḷu.
Usually plural: ಅವಶೇಷಗಳು, what is left of a building or a city.

The word, taken apart.
ಅವಶೇಷ (avaśēṣa) is borrowed from Sanskrit avaśeṣa, "leavings, remainder" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 4 of 5.
ಶಾಸನ (śāsana) — an inscription; an edict.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for archaeology, then the Kannada for ruins.

You'll want to know: ಶಾಸನ.
ಶಾಸನ — śāsana — "an inscription; an edict".
ಈ ಕಲ್ಲಿನ ಶಾಸನ ಸಾವಿರ ವರ್ಷ ಹಳೆಯದು. ī kallina śāsana sāvira varṣa haḷeyadu. This stone inscription is a thousand years old.
Plural: ಶಾಸನಗಳು, śāsanagaḷu.
Old kings cut their orders and gifts into stone: ಶಿಲಾಶಾಸನ.

The word, taken apart.
ಶಾಸನ (śāsana) is borrowed from Sanskrit śāsana, "a command", and "a royal edict" (CDIAL 12420), from śās, "to order".

Lesson 5 of 5.
ಹಸ್ತಪ್ರತಿ (hastaprati) — a manuscript.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for ruins, then the Kannada for an inscription.

You'll want to know: ಹಸ್ತಪ್ರತಿ.
ಹಸ್ತಪ್ರತಿ — hastaprati — "a manuscript".
ಗ್ರಂಥಾಲಯದಲ್ಲಿ ತಾಳೆಗರಿಯ ಹಳೆಯ ಹಸ್ತಪ್ರತಿಗಳು ಇವೆ. granthālayadalli tāḷegariya haḷeya hastapratigaḷu ive. There are old palm-leaf manuscripts in the library.
Plural: ಹಸ್ತಪ್ರತಿಗಳು, hastapratigaḷu.
ಹಸ್ತ, hand, and ಪ್ರತಿ, a copy: a copy made by hand.

The word, taken apart.
ಹಸ್ತಪ್ರತಿ (hastaprati) is borrowed from Sanskrit, a modern compound of hasta, "the hand" (CDIAL 14024), and prati, "towards, against" (CDIAL 8540), used in Kannada for "a copy".
