## You'll want to know: étendre
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C526-LINGE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**étendre** — "to hang out (washing); to spread out".

> Elle étend le linge sur le balcon.
>
> *She hangs the washing out on the balcony.*

*S'étendre* — to lie down, to stretch out.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*étendre* is inherited, through Old French *estendre*, from Latin *extendere* "to stretch out" (EtymDB), built on *tendere* "to stretch" (Lewis & Short).
