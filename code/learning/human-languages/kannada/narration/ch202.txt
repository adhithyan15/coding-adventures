Kannada, chapter 202: To complain, To register, To continue, To contact, To discuss.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ದೂರು ಕೊಡು (dūru koḍu) — to complain, to lodge a complaint.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to remember, then the Kannada for to print.

You'll want to know: ದೂರು ಕೊಡು.
ದೂರು ಕೊಡು — dūru koḍu — "to complain, to lodge a complaint".

The word, taken apart.
ದೂರು ಕೊಡು (dūru koḍu) is native Dravidian: dūru, "blame, complaint", which DEDR 3397 lists as dūṟu, and koḍu, "to give" (DEDR 2053): "to give a complaint".

The word, taken apart.
ಮುಂದುವರಿಸು (munduvarisu) begins with native mundu, "ahead, onward" (DEDR 5020). DEDR does not explain the second part, -varisu.
