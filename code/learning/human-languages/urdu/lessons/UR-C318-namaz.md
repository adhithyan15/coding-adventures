## You'll want to know: نماز
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C318-EIDMORNING318-01]; assesses=[] -->

**نماز** — *namāz* — "the set prayer".

> صبح سب نے نماز پڑھی۔
>
> *subah sab ne namāz paṛhī.*
>
> In the morning everyone said the prayer.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**نماز** (*namāz*) is usually traced to Persian *namāz*, "a bowing, a prayer". It is said, in Urdu, by reading it: **نماز پڑھی**.
