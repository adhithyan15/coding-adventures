## You'll want to know: اصلاح
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C356-ISLAH356-01]; assesses=[] -->

**اصلاح** — *islāh* — "a reform; a correction".

> اسکولوں میں اصلاح کی ضرورت ہے۔
>
> *iskūloṅ meṅ islāh kī zarūrat hai.*
>
> The schools need reform.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**اصلاح** (*islāh*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *iṣlāḥ*, "putting right".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
