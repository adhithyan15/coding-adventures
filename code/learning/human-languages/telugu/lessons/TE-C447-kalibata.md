## You'll want to know: కాలిబాట
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C447-SIGNALU447-04]; assesses=[] -->

**కాలిబాట** — *kālibāṭa* — "a footpath; a pavement".

> పిల్లలు కాలిబాట మీదే నడవాలి.
>
> *pillalu kālibāṭa mīdē naḍavāli.*
>
> Children should walk only on the pavement.

కాలి, of the foot, + బాట, a path. In a town it is the pavement beside the road.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**కాలిబాట** (*kālibāṭa*) is a Telugu compound: కాలి, the oblique of కాలు, "foot, leg", native Dravidian (DEDR 1479), and బాట, "a path", usually traced to Sanskrit *vartman*, "a track", through Prakrit (CDIAL 11366).
