# تفاجأ (tafājaʾa) — to be surprised

## You'll want to know: تفاجأ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C490-DAHSHA490-04]; assesses=[] -->

**تفاجأ** — *tafājaʾa* — "to be surprised".

> تفاجأت عندما رأيت صديقي في المطار.
>
> *tafājaʾtu ʿindamā raʾaytu ṣadīqī fī al-maṭār.*
>
> I was surprised when I saw my friend at the airport.

Present: يتفاجأ, *yatafājaʾ*.

It takes بـ for the cause: تفاجأ بالخبر.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**تفاجأ** (*tafājaʾa*) is built on the native Arabic root *f-j-ʾ*, "to come upon suddenly" (Lane). Tafājaʾa is to be come upon suddenly: to be taken by surprise.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
