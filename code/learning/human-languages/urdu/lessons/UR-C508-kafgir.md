## You'll want to know: کفگیر
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C508-DHAKKAN508-05]; assesses=[] -->

**کفگیر** — *kafgīr* — "a skimmer (a flat spoon)".

> کفگیر چولہے کے پاس ہے۔
>
> *kafgīr chūlhe ke pās hai.*
>
> The skimmer is next to the stove.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**کفگیر** (*kafgīr*) is usually traced to Persian *kafgīr*, "a skimmer", built from *kaf*, "foam", and *gīr-*, "to take".
