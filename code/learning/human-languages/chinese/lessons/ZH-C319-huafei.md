## You'll want to know: huàfèi
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C319-CALL319-05]; assesses=[] -->

*huàfèi* — "phone charges".

> *zhège yuè huàfèi yìbǎi kuài* — This month's phone bill is a hundred yuan.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*huàfèi* is built from 话 'talk' and *fèi* 'a cost', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *hwaejH* and *phj+jH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'to use up'.
