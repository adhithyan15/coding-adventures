## You'll want to know: dànǎo
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C477-INSIDE477-03]; assesses=[] -->

*dànǎo* — "brain".

> *shuìjiào de shíhou dànǎo yě zài gōngzuò* — Even while you sleep, your brain is working.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*dànǎo* is built from 大 'big' and *nǎo* 'the brain', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *dajH* and *nawX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second as the brain in the head.
