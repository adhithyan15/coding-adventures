## You'll want to know: смерч
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C300-NAVODNENIYE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**смерч** (*smerch*) — "a tornado, a whirlwind".

> Смерч сломал много деревьев.
>
> *Smerch slomál mnógo derévyev.*
>
> The tornado broke a lot of trees.

Masculine; genitive *смерча*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*смерч* is an old East Slavic word whose origin is not settled; EtymDB lists no parent.
