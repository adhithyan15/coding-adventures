Persian, chapter 340: Nowruz visits.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
عیدی ('eydi) — New Year money, a holiday gift.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for oleaster, then the Persian for a goldfish.

You'll want to know: عیدی.
عیدی ('eydi) — "New Year money, a holiday gift".
پدربزرگ به همه بچه ها عیدی داد. Pedarbozorg be hame-ye bachche-hâ 'eydi dâd. Grandfather gave all the children their New Year money.
Usually crisp new banknotes, given by the elders. 'Eydi gereftan "to get one's New Year gift".

The word, taken apart.
'eydi adds the Persian suffix -i to 'eyd "festival", which is borrowed from Arabic 'īd (EtymDB).

Lesson 2 of 5.
دید و بازدید (did o bâzdid) — a round of visits (at the New Year).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a goldfish, then the Persian for New Year money.

You'll want to know: دید و بازدید.
دید و بازدید (did o bâzdid) — "a round of visits (at the New Year)".
در روزهای عید دید و بازدید داریم. Dar ruz-hâ-ye 'eyd did o bâzdid dârim. During the New Year holidays we go round visiting family.
Literally "seeing and seeing back": you visit, then they return the visit. The elders are visited first.

The word, taken apart.
did "a seeing" and bâzdid "a return visit" (bâz "back" + did) are both built on the past stem of the native verb didan "to see".

Lesson 3 of 5.
آیین (âyin) — a rite, a ritual.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for New Year money, then the Persian for a round of visits.

You'll want to know: آیین.
آیین (âyin) — "a rite, a ritual".
پریدن از روی آتش یک آیین قدیمی است. Paridan az ru-ye âtash yek âyin-e qadimi ast. Jumping over a fire is an old ritual.
It also means "a religion, a creed". Âyin-e Nowruz "the Nowruz rites".

The word, taken apart.
âyin is a native Persian word that EtymDB glosses "custom" with no parent; it is usually traced to Middle Persian ēwēn, "manner, custom".

Lesson 4 of 5.
رسم (rasm) — a custom, a tradition.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a round of visits, then the Persian for a rite.

You'll want to know: رسم.
رسم (rasm) — "a custom, a tradition".
در خانواده ما رسم است که شب عید همه دور هم جمع شوند. Dar khânevâde-ye mâ rasm ast ke shab-e 'eyd hame dor-e ham jam' shavand. In our family it is the custom for everyone to get together on New Year's Eve.
Broken plural rosum: rasm-o rosum "customs and traditions". In Arabic the word means "a drawing".

The word, taken apart.
rasm is borrowed from Arabic rasm, "a drawing, a picture" (EtymDB); in Persian it came to mean a custom, a traced-out way of doing things.

Lesson 5 of 5.
اسپند (espand) — wild rue (seeds burned for luck).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a rite, then the Persian for a custom.

You'll want to know: اسپند.
اسپند (espand) — "wild rue (seeds burned for luck)".
مادربزرگم برای ما اسپند دود کرد. Mâdarbozorgam barâ-ye mâ espand dud kard. My grandmother burned wild rue for us.
The seeds are burned on hot coals against the evil eye, at weddings and at the New Year.

The word, taken apart.
espand "wild rue" is a native Persian word, usually traced to Middle Persian spand; EtymDB lists no entry.
