Sanskrit, chapter 452: Worry and Despair.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
उद्वेगः (udvegaḥ) — anxiety.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for troubled, then the Sanskrit for anguish.

You'll want to know: उद्वेगः.
उद्वेगः — udvegaḥ — "anxiety".
परीक्षायाः पूर्वं छात्रस्य उद्वेगः भवति। parīkṣāyāḥ pūrvaṃ chātrasya udvegaḥ bhavati. A student feels anxious before an exam.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
उद्वेगः (udvegaḥ) is built from ud, "up", and the root √vij, "to tremble"; MW gives "trembling" and "agitation, anxiety" (MW).

वर्षा न भवति, अतः कृषकः हताशः अस्ति। varṣā na bhavati, ataḥ kṛṣakaḥ hatāśaḥ asti. There is no rain, so the farmer has lost hope.

हताशः (hatāśaḥ) is built from hata, "struck, destroyed", from the root √han, and आशा, "hope": one whose hopes are struck down; MW gives "whose hopes are destroyed, desperate" (MW).

Lesson 3 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for anxiety, then the Sanskrit for despairing.

You'll want to know: विषीदति.
विषीदति — viṣīdati — "loses heart".
कार्यं कठिनम् अस्ति, किन्तु अरुणः न विषीदति। kāryaṃ kaṭhinam asti, kintu aruṇaḥ na viṣīdati. The work is hard, but Arun does not lose heart.
Past (he …): सः विषण्णवान्, saḥ viṣaṇṇavān.

The word, taken apart.
विषीदति (viṣīdati) is built from vi and the root √sad, "to sit, to sink", the verb behind विषादः; MW gives "to be dejected, despond, despair" (MW).

विलम्बेन यात्री व्यग्रः भवति। vilambena yātrī vyagraḥ bhavati. The passenger grows agitated at the delay.

The word, taken apart.
व्यग्रः (vyagraḥ) is built from vi, "apart", and agra, "a point": a mind with no single point; MW gives "distracted" and "bewildered, agitated, alarmed" (MW).

Lesson 5 of 5.
दीनः (dīnaḥ) — wretched; dejected.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for loses heart, then the Sanskrit for agitated.

You'll want to know: दीनः.
दीनः — dīnaḥ — "wretched; dejected".
दीनः याचकः मार्गे तिष्ठति। dīnaḥ yācakaḥ mārge tiṣṭhati. A wretched beggar stands by the road.

The word, taken apart.
दीनः (dīnaḥ) MW asks, with a question mark, whether it comes from a root √dī, so its origin is uncertain; its first sense is "scarce, scanty", then "depressed, afflicted, sad" and "miserable, wretched" (MW).
