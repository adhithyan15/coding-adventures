## You'll want to know: врезаться
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C410-STOLKNOVENIYE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**врезаться** (*vrézatsya*) — "to crash (into)".

> Водитель не увидел столб и врезался в него.
>
> *Vodítel ne uvídel stolb i vrézalsya v negó.*
>
> The driver did not notice the post and crashed into it.

Perfective; the imperfective *врезаться* (*vrezátsya*) has the stress on the а. Takes *в* + accusative. Future *врежусь, врежешься*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*врезаться* is built from *в-* 'into', *резать* 'to cut' and *-ся*: to cut one's way into something. *Резать* is inherited from Proto-Slavic *rězati* (EtymDB).
