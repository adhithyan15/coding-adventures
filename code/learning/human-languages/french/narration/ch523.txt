French, chapter 523: Une série à suivre.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
l'épisode — episode.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for biography, then the French for tale.

You'll want to know: l'épisode.
l'épisode — "episode".
J'ai regardé trois épisodes hier soir. I watched three episodes last night.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
l'épisode comes from Greek epeisódion "an added part" (EtymDB), built on epí "upon" and eísodos "an entrance".

Lesson 2 of 5.

You'll want to know: la saison.
la saison — "season".
La deuxième saison de la série sort en mars. The second season of the series comes out in March.
Also the seasons of the year, and la haute saison for tourism.

The word, taken apart.
la saison is inherited from Latin satiō, satiōnem "a sowing" (EtymDB), from serere "to sow" (Lewis & Short): first the time for sowing.

Lesson 3 of 5.
le suspense — suspense.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for episode, then the French for season.

You'll want to know: le suspense.
le suspense — "suspense".
Ce film garde le suspense jusqu'à la fin. This film keeps up the suspense to the end.
Said the English way or sus-pense. A different word from en suspens "pending".

The word, taken apart.
le suspense was borrowed from English suspense, which goes back to Latin suspendere "to hang up", built on pendere "to hang" (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 4 of 5.
la plateforme — (streaming) platform; platform.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for season, then the French for suspense.

You'll want to know: la plateforme.
la plateforme — "(streaming) platform; platform".
Cette série est disponible sur une plateforme. This series is available on a streaming platform.
Also a station platform in some contexts, but that is usually le quai. Older spelling plate-forme.

The word, taken apart.
la plateforme is built from plat "flat" and forme "shape", from Latin forma (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 5 of 5.
palpitant — thrilling, exciting.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for suspense, then the French for (streaming) platform.

You'll want to know: palpitant.
palpitant — "thrilling, exciting".
Le dernier épisode était palpitant. The last episode was thrilling.
Built on palpiter "to throb".

The word, taken apart.
palpitant is the present participle of palpiter "to throb", a learned borrowing from Latin palpitāre "to flutter, to throb", from palpāre "to touch softly" (Lewis & Short).
