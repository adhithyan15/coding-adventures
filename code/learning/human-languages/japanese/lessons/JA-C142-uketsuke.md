## You'll want to know: うけつけ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C142-UKETSUKE]; assesses=[] -->

**うけつけ** — *uketsuke* — "reception", "a reception desk": where you
check in at a hospital, an office or a school.
On a real sign this word is usually printed in kanji, which this book has
not reached yet; the reading is the same.

> **うけつけで なまえを かいて ください。** — *uketsuke de namae o kaite
> kudasai* — "Please write your name at reception."

**かく**, *kaku*, "to write", becomes **かいて** before **ください**.
The notice tells you what to do: go to the desk, and write your name.
