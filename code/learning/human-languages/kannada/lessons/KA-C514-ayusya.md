## You'll want to know: ಆಯುಷ್ಯ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C514-PATHYA514-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ಆಯುಷ್ಯ** — *āyuṣya* — "lifespan; long life".

> ಒಳ್ಳೆಯ ಆಹಾರದಿಂದ ಆಯುಷ್ಯ ಹೆಚ್ಚುತ್ತದೆ.
>
> *oḷḷeya āhāradinda āyuṣya heccuttade.*
>
> Good food lengthens life.

Elders bless with ನೂರು ವರ್ಷ ಆಯುಷ್ಯ, a hundred years of life.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಆಯುಷ್ಯ** (*āyuṣya*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *āyuṣya*, "giving long life; long life" (CDIAL 1292a).
