Bengali, chapter 434: Nature and the Environment.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
প্রকৃতি (prôkriti) — nature; the natural world.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for to sway, then the Bengali for to babble (of water).

You'll want to know: প্রকৃতি.
প্রকৃতি — prôkriti — "nature; the natural world".
গ্রামে এসে মীরা প্রকৃতির শান্তি পেল। grāme ese mīrā prôkritir shānti pelô. Coming to the village, Mira found the peace of nature.
Also a person's nature, temperament.

The word, taken apart.
প্রকৃতি (prôkriti) is Sanskrit prakṛti, "the original or natural form of a thing, nature" (Monier-Williams), taken over whole.

Lesson 2 of 5.
পরিবেশ (pôribesh) — the environment; surroundings.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for to babble (of water), then the Bengali for nature.

You'll want to know: পরিবেশ.
পরিবেশ — pôribesh — "the environment; surroundings".
কারখানার ধোঁয়া পরিবেশের ক্ষতি করে। kārkhānār dhõyā pôribesher khôti kôre. Factory smoke harms the environment.
Also the surroundings of a place: শান্ত পরিবেশ (pôribesh), a calm setting.

The word, taken apart.
পরিবেশ (pôribesh) is Sanskrit pariveṣa, "a surrounding, a circle, a halo" (Monier-Williams); the sense "the environment" is a modern use.

Lesson 4 of 5.
নষ্ট করা (nôshṭô kôrā) — to spoil; to waste; to ruin.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for the environment, then the Bengali for pollution.

You'll want to know: নষ্ট করা.
নষ্ট করা — nôshṭô kôrā — "to spoil; to waste; to ruin".
বন্যা খেতের ধান নষ্ট করল। bônyā kheter dhān nôshṭô kôrlô. The flood ruined the rice in the fields.
Past (he/she …): সেই বন্যা ধান নষ্ট করল, sei bônyā dhān nôshṭô kôrlô.
নষ্ট alone is spoiled, gone bad: খাবারটা নষ্ট, the food has gone off. সময় নষ্ট করা (nôshṭô kôrā), to waste time.

The word, taken apart.
নষ্ট করা (nôshṭô kôrā) joins Sanskrit naṣṭa, "lost, perished, destroyed" (Monier-Williams), to করা, to do.

আমাদের বন আর নদী সংরক্ষণ করতে হবে। āmāder bôn ār nôdī sôngrôkkhôn kôrte hôbe. We must conserve our forests and rivers.

সংরক্ষণ করা (sôngrôkkhôn kôrā) joins Sanskrit saṃrakṣaṇa, "guarding, keeping, preservation" (Monier-Williams), to করা, to do.
