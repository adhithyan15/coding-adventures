## You'll want to know: o ferido
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C307-FERIDO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**o ferido** — "the injured person, the wounded".

> O ferido foi levado para o hospital.
>
> *The injured man was taken to hospital.*

*Feridos ligeiros* — minor injuries; *feridos graves* — serious ones.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ferido* comes from Latin *ferītus* "struck" (EtymDB), from *ferīre* "to strike".
