## You'll want to know: خروپف کردن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C499-SLEEP-02]; assesses=[] -->

**خروپف کردن** (*khoropof kardan*) — "to snore".

> پدربزرگ شبها بلند خروپف میکند.
>
> *pedarbozorg shabhâ boland khoropof mikonad.*
>
> Grandfather snores loudly at night.

*khoropof* copies the sound: *khor* breathing in, *pof* breathing out. + *kardan*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*khoropof* is usually explained as an imitative word built on the sound of snoring, *khor* and *pof* joined by *o*, 'and'. *kardan*, 'to do', is inherited Persian.
