Persian, chapter 325: Falls and injuries.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
زمین خوردن (zamin khordan) — to fall over, to fall down.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to collide, then the Persian for to overturn.

You'll want to know: زمین خوردن.
زمین خوردن (zamin khordan) — "to fall over, to fall down".
پیرمرد روی یخ زمین خورد. Pirmard ru-ye yakh zamin khord. The old man fell over on the ice.
Literally "to hit the ground". Present zamin mikhorad.

The word, taken apart.
zamin "ground, earth" and khordan "to eat, to strike" are both native Persian; EtymDB takes khordan back to Middle Persian xwar-.

Lesson 2 of 5.
پیچ خوردن (pich khordan) — to get twisted, to sprain.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to overturn, then the Persian for to fall over.

You'll want to know: پیچ خوردن.
پیچ خوردن (pich khordan) — "to get twisted, to sprain".
پایم در کوه پیچ خورد. Pâyam dar kuh pich khord. I twisted my ankle on the mountain.
The body part is the subject: dastam pich khord "I sprained my wrist". Present pich mikhorad.

The word, taken apart.
pich "a twist, a turn" is a native Persian word that EtymDB lists without a parent; khordan "to eat, to strike" is inherited Persian.

دو نفر در این حادثه آسیب دیدند. Do nafar dar in hâdese âsib didand. Two people were hurt in this accident.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to get twisted, then the Persian for to get hurt.

You'll want to know: صدمه دیدن.
صدمه دیدن (sadame didan) — "to be harmed, to suffer damage".
ماشین در تصادف خیلی صدمه دید. Mâshin dar tasâdof kheyli sadame did. The car was badly damaged in the crash.
Sadame zadan is "to harm". Close in meaning to âsib didan.

The word, taken apart.
sadame is borrowed from Arabic ṣadma (EtymDB lists the Arabic word), usually glossed "a blow, a shock". didan "to see" is native Persian.

Lesson 5 of 5.
شکستگی (shekastegi) — a fracture, a break.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to get hurt, then the Persian for to be harmed.

You'll want to know: شکستگی.
شکستگی (shekastegi) — "a fracture, a break".
دکتر گفت شکستگی پایش جدی نیست. Doktor goft shekastegi-ye pâyash jeddi nist. The doctor said the break in his leg was not serious.
From shekaste "broken", the participle of shekastan "to break".

The word, taken apart.
shekastegi is built on shekaste "broken", the participle of the native verb shekastan "to break", with the noun suffix -gi.
