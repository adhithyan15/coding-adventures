Lesson 1 of 6.
まんいち (man'ichi) — in the unlikely event; by any chance.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for a countermeasure, then the Japanese for disaster preparedness.

You'll want to know: まんいち.
まんいち — man'ichi — "in the unlikely event; by any chance".
まんいち おくれたら、でんわして ください。 — man'ichi okuretara, denwa shite kudasai — "If by any chance you are late, please phone."

The word, taken apart.
まんいち (man'ichi) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are man, "ten thousand" (Middle Chinese mjonH) and ichi, "one" ('jit) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).

Lesson 3 of 6.
かくにんする (kakunin suru) — to confirm; to check.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for in the unlikely event, then the Japanese for insurance.

You'll want to know: かくにんする.
かくにんする — kakunin suru — "to confirm; to check".
まちがえたら、もう いちど かくにんして ください。 — machigaetara, mō ichido kakunin shite kudasai — "If you make a mistake, please check it again."
Polite かくにんします, kakuninshimasu; a する verb. Past (polite): てんいんが よやくを かくにんしました。, ten'in ga yoyaku o kakunin shimashita.

The word, taken apart.
かくにんする (kakunin suru) is built from a Sino-Japanese noun and the native verb suru, "to do". The noun, kakunin, is two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese: kaku, "firm" (Middle Chinese khaewk) and nin, "recognize" (nyinH) (KANJIDIC2; JMdict; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).

You'll want to know: ねんのため.
ねんのため — nen no tame — "to be on the safe side; to make sure".
ねんのため、ついたら でんわして ください。 — nen no tame, tsuitara denwa shite kudasai — "To be on the safe side, please phone when you arrive."

The word, taken apart.
ねんのため (nen no tame) is built from the Sino-Japanese nen, "sense" or attention, an on'yomi borrowed from Chinese (Middle Chinese nemH), and the native no tame, "for the sake of": "for the sake of care" (KANJIDIC2; JMdict; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
