## You'll want to know: विभिन्नः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C541-DVIDHA541-05]; assesses=[] -->

**विभिन्नः** — *vibhinnaḥ* — "different".

> यदि मतानि विभिन्नानि सन्ति, तर्हि वयं पुनः चिन्तयामः।
>
> *yadi matāni vibhinnāni santi, tarhi vayaṃ punaḥ cintayāmaḥ.*
>
> If our opinions differ, we think again.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**विभिन्नः** (*vibhinnaḥ*) is built on *vi*, "apart", and the root √bhid, "to split", which also gives *bhinatti*, as its past participle; MW gives *vibhinna* as split, divided, and various (MW).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
