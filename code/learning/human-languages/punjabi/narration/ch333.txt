Punjabi, chapter 333: The Barber and the Washerman.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ਨਾਈ (nāī) — a barber.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for a woman's female friend, then the Punjabi for to call out to someone.

You'll want to know: ਨਾਈ.
ਨਾਈ — nāī — "a barber".
ਨਾਈ ਹਰ ਸਵੇਰ ਆਪਣੀ ਦੁਕਾਨ ਖੋਲ੍ਹਦਾ ਹੈ। nāī har saver āpṇī dukān kholhdā hai. The barber opens his shop every morning.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ਨਾਈ (nāī) is inherited from Sanskrit nāpita, "a barber"; CDIAL lists Punjabi nāī, "barber", under it (CDIAL 13792).

Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਆਪਣੀ ਦਾੜ੍ਹੀ ਮੁੰਨੀ, maiṁ āpṇī dāṛhī munnī.

Lesson 3 of 5.
ਧੋਬੀ (dhobī) — a washerman.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for a barber, then the Punjabi for to shave.

You'll want to know: ਧੋਬੀ.
ਧੋਬੀ — dhobī — "a washerman".
ਧੋਬੀ ਹਰ ਹਫ਼ਤੇ ਸਾਡੇ ਕੱਪੜੇ ਧੋਂਦਾ ਹੈ। dhobī har hafte sāḍe kappṛe dhondā hai. The washerman washes our clothes every week.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ਧੋਬੀ (dhobī) goes back to a reconstructed dhauvati, "washes", as a word taken from Hindi: CDIAL lists Punjabi dhobī under it among the forms that spread from Hindi dhobī, "washerman" (CDIAL 6886).

Lesson 4 of 5.
ਮੋਚੀ (mochī) — a cobbler.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for to shave, then the Punjabi for a washerman.

You'll want to know: ਮੋਚੀ.
ਮੋਚੀ — mochī — "a cobbler".
ਮੋਚੀ ਬਾਜ਼ਾਰ ਵਿੱਚ ਜੁੱਤੀਆਂ ਸਿਉਂਦਾ ਹੈ। mochī bāzār vich juttīāṁ siundā hai. The cobbler stitches shoes in the market.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ਮੋਚੀ (mochī) is inherited from Sanskrit mocika, "a tanner and cobbler"; CDIAL lists Punjabi mocī, "cobbler", under it (CDIAL 10349). CDIAL traces the word for a shoe behind it to Iranian.
