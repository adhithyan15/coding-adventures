Japanese, chapter 346: This Time and Next Time.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
こんど (kondo) — next time; this time.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for to shift a time, then the Japanese for to bring forward.

You'll want to know: こんど.
こんど — kondo — "next time; this time".
こんど じかんが あったら、うちに きて ください。 — kondo jikan ga attara, uchi ni kite kudasai — "Next time you have a moment, please come to my house."

The word, taken apart.
こんど (kondo) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are kon, "now" (Middle Chinese kim) and do, "degrees" (duH) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).

What you've built.
One more word for saying what happens if.
