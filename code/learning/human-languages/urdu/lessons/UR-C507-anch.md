## You'll want to know: آنچ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C507-ROTI507-05]; assesses=[] -->

**آنچ** — *āṅch* — "the heat of a flame".

> دال کو دھیمی آنچ پر پکائیے۔
>
> *dāl ko dhīmī āṅch par pakāiye.*
>
> Cook the lentils on a low flame, please.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**آنچ** (*āṅch*) is inherited from Sanskrit *arcis*, "a flame, a ray" (CDIAL 635).
