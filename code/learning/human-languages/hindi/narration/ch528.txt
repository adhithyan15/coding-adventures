Hindi, chapter 528: Craft and Carving.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
तराशना (tarāśnā) — to carve; to cut into shape.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a line (drawn), then the Hindi for to colour.

You'll want to know: तराशना.
तराशना — tarāśnā — "to carve; to cut into shape".
कारीगर ने पत्थर तराशकर एक हाथी बनाया। Kārīgar ne patthar tarāśkar ek hāthī banāyā. The craftsman carved the stone into an elephant.
Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने पत्थर तराशा, usne patthar tarāśā.

The word, taken apart.
तराशना (tarāśnā) is usually traced to Persian tarāsh, "cutting", the stem of tarāshīdan, "to shave, to carve", with the Hindi verb ending -nā.

महल के दरवाज़ों पर सुंदर नक़्क़ाशी है। Mahal ke darvāzoṁ par sundar naqqāśī hai. There is beautiful carving on the palace doors.

The word, taken apart.
नक़्क़ाशी (naqqāśī) is usually traced to Arabic naqqāsh, "a painter, an engraver", through Persian. Hindi adds -ī for the work itself.

Lesson 4 of 5.
गोदना (godnā) — to prick, to tattoo.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for carving, then the Hindi for a craft.

You'll want to know: गोदना.
गोदना — godnā — "to prick, to tattoo".
मेले में एक औरत लोगों के हाथ पर फूल गोद रही थी। Mele meṁ ek aurat logoṁ ke hāth par phūl god rahī thī. At the fair a woman was tattooing flowers on people's hands.
Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने हाथ पर नाम गोदा, usne hāth par nām godā.

The word, taken apart.
गोदना (godnā) is a tadbhava, inherited from khōdd, "dig", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 3934). CDIAL gives the Hindi verb as "to pierce, hoe".

Lesson 5 of 5.
प्रदर्शनी (pradarśanī) — an exhibition.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a craft, then the Hindi for to prick, to tattoo.

You'll want to know: प्रदर्शनी.
प्रदर्शनी — pradarśanī — "an exhibition".
संग्रहालय में चित्रों की एक प्रदर्शनी लगी है। Saṅgrahālay meṁ citroṁ kī ek pradarśanī lagī hai. There is an exhibition of paintings at the museum.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
प्रदर्शनी (pradarśanī) is a modern formation built on Sanskrit pradarśana, "look, appearance; showing" (Monier-Williams), with the feminine -ī: a place where things are shown.
