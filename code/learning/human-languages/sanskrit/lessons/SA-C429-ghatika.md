## You'll want to know: घटिका
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C429-UDGHATANA429-02]; assesses=[] -->

**घटिका** — *ghaṭikā* — "a clock; a ghatika of twenty-four minutes".

> आपणस्य भित्तौ घटिका अस्ति।
>
> *āpaṇasya bhittau ghaṭikā asti.*
>
> There is a clock on the wall of the shop.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**घटिका** (*ghaṭikā*) is built on *ghaṭī*, "a small water-jar": MW gives *ghaṭikā* as a water-jar, as a period of twenty-four minutes once measured by such a jar, and as the Indian gong clock; a clock or a watch on the wall is that sense in modern use (MW).
