## You'll want to know: acarretar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C351-IMPACTO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**acarretar** — "to entail, to bring about".

> Esse erro acarretou muitos problemas à empresa.
>
> *That mistake brought many problems to the company.*

Formal; close to *implicar*. *Acarretar custos* — to entail costs.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*acarretar* first meant "to carry by cart": it is built on *carreta* "small cart", from *carro*, inherited from Latin *carrus* "a wagon" (Lewis & Short).
