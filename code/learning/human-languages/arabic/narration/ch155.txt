Arabic, chapter 155: Necessary, Ugly, Precious, Hot, Crowded.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
ضروري (ḍarūrī) — necessary.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for strong, then the Arabic for weak.

You'll want to know: ضروري.
ضروري — ḍarūrī — "necessary".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: ḍarūrī]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: ḍarūrī, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say ḍarūrī]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Arabic for strong, then the Arabic for weak, then say ḍarūrī again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does ضروري (ḍarūrī) mean? ("Necessary".) Say it once more.

Lesson 6 of 7.
Describing words — a first pass.

Warm-up.
[pause 3 seconds]
Say the words for hot and crowded.

Guided Practice.
[pause 2 seconds after each]
[your turn — say: pleased, spacious, deep, round, sharp]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: dark, colourful, elegant, strange, enjoyable]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: delicious, low, high, wide, narrow]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: thin, fat, wet, dry, dirty]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
Pleased? (مسرور, masrūr.) Spacious? (واسع, wāsiʿ.) Deep? (عميق, ʿamīq.) Round? (مستدير, mustadīr.) Sharp? (حاد, ḥādd.) Dark? (مظلم, muẓlim.) Colourful? (ملون, mulawwan.) Elegant? (أنيق, anīq.) Strange? (غريب, gharīb.) Enjoyable? (ممتع, mumtiʿ.) Delicious? (لذيذ, ladhīdh.) Low? (منخفض, munkhafiḍ.) High? (مرتفع, murtafiʿ.) Wide? (عريض, ʿarīḍ.) Narrow? (ضيق, ḍayyiq.) Thin? (نحيف, naḥīf.) Fat? (سمين, samīn.) Wet? (مبلل, muballal.) Dry? (جاف, jāff.) Dirty? (متسخ, mutassikh.)

Wrap-up Recall.

Sour? (حامض, ḥāmiḍ.) Bitter? (مر, murr.) Soft? (ناعم, nāʿim.) Hard? (صلب, ṣulb.) Clever? (ذكي, dhakī.) Lazy? (كسول, kasūl.) Brave? (شجاع, shujāʿ.) Thirsty? (عطشان, ʿaṭshān.) Fresh? (طازج, ṭāzaj.) Ripe? (ناضج, nāḍij.) Full? (مليء, malīʾ.) Young? (شاب, shābb.) Old? (عجوز, ʿajūz.) Strong? (قوي, qawī.) Weak? (ضعيف, ḍaʿīf.) Necessary? (ضروري, ḍarūrī.) Ugly? (قبيح, qabīḥ.) Precious? (ثمين, thamīn.) Hot? (ساخن, sākhin.) Crowded? (مزدحم, muzdaḥim.)
