## You'll want to know: curiosare
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C451-PASSEGGIATA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**curiosare** — "to browse, to have a look round; to snoop".

> Entriamo a curiosare in libreria.
>
> *Let us go into the bookshop for a look round.*

*Curioso* — curious.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*curiosare* is built on *curioso*, a learned borrowing from Latin *cūriōsus* 'careful, inquisitive' (EtymDB), from *cūra* 'care'.
