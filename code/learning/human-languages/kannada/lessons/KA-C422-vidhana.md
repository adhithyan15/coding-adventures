## You'll want to know: ವಿಧಾನ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C422-MANADANDA422-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ವಿಧಾನ** — *vidhāna* — "a method; a procedure".

> ಅಮ್ಮನ ಅಡುಗೆಯ ವಿಧಾನ ಬೇರೆ.
>
> *ammana aḍugeya vidhāna bēre.*
>
> Mother's way of cooking is different.

ವಿಧಾನಸಭೆ, the legislative assembly.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ವಿಧಾನ** (*vidhāna*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *vidhāna*, "arranging, regulating, a rule" (CDIAL 11754).
