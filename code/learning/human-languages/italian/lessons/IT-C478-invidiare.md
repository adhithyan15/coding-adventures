## You'll want to know: invidiare
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C478-VERGOGNA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**invidiare** — "to envy".

> Ti invidio: vai in vacanza al mare!
>
> *I envy you: you are going on holiday to the seaside!*

*Invidiare qualcosa a qualcuno*: *gli invidio la casa* — I envy him his house.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*invidiare* is built on *invidia* "envy", from Latin *invidia* (Lewis & Short), and so goes back to *vidēre* "to see".
