## You'll want to know: tolerar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C610-CONVIVIR-02]; assesses=[] -->

**tolerar** — "to tolerate, to put up with".

> No tolero las mentiras.
>
> *I do not tolerate lies.*

Close to *aguantar*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*tolerar* is a learned borrowing from Latin *tolerare* 'to carry, to endure' (EtymDB).
