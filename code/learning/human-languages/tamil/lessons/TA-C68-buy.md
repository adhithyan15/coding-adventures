## You'll want to know: வாங்கு
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C68-SHOP-04]; assesses=[] -->

**வாங்கு** (*vāṅgu*) — "to buy, to get".

The verb that makes a shop work.

It is also the ordinary Tamil verb for **receiving**. Buying something and being handed
something are the same act here, and one word covers both — so வாங்கு is as useful at a
doorway as at a counter.

Set it against கொடு (*koṭu*), "give", which you already have. One hand out, one hand in, and
the pair covers most of what happens over a counter.
