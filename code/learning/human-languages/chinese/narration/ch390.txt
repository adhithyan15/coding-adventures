Mandarin Chinese, chapter 390: As We Agreed.
6 lessons. You can do the first 2 of them in the car; after that you will want to have stopped.


Lesson 1 of 6.
quèdìng — to be certain; to settle on.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for as a precaution, then the Mandarin for to fall due.

You'll want to know: quèdìng.
quèdìng — "to be certain; to settle on".
wǒ hái bú quèdìng míngtiān néng bu néng qù — I'm not sure yet whether I can go tomorrow.
Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
quèdìng is built from què 'firm' and dìng 'settled', both inherited from Middle Chinese, khaewk and dengH (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'steadfast' and the second 'calm'.

约定 — yuēdìng — "to agree on a time; to fix (a time or place) together".

约定 (yuēdìng) is built from 约 'to bind' and 定 (dìng) 'settled', both inherited from Middle Chinese, 'jak and dengH (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'calm'.

Lesson 3 of 6.
定 (dìng) — the roof of 安 over the foot of 是.

Before we start: this one needs your hands, so it is not a driving lesson. You will want a pen and something to write on and your eyes, for letter shapes on the page. You can listen to everything else now. 安 是.
[once you have stopped driving — read: each one aloud, with nothing said first — ān, shì]

[once you have stopped driving — this part needs your eyes or your hands; if you are driving, skip ahead to the next part]
Script — the roof of 安 over the foot of 是.
定 (dìng).
Two pieces, top before bottom.
The roof, three strokes, as on top of 安: a dot, a short stroke down on the left, and a bar that turns down and hooks.
Under it, five strokes, the foot of 是. A bar. A short vertical down from its middle. A short bar to the right of the vertical. Then a stroke falling to the left, and a long stroke falling to the right that sweeps along the foot.
3 + 5 = eight.

[once you have stopped driving — this part needs your eyes or your hands; if you are driving, skip ahead to the next part]
Guided Practice.
[once you have stopped driving — write: 定 (dìng), all eight, with the model in front of you]
[once you have stopped driving — cover: write it again from memory, and count the pieces, not the strokes]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
How many strokes in 定 (dìng)? (Eight.) What is on top? (The roof of 安.) Where have you made the bottom piece before? (At the foot of 是.)

Lesson 4 of 6.
定期 (dìngqī) — at regular intervals; periodically.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for to be certain, then the Mandarin for to agree on a time.

You'll want to know: 定期.
定期 — dìngqī — "at regular intervals; periodically".
你最好定期去看医生 nǐ zuìhǎo dìngqī qù kàn yīshēng — You'd better see a doctor regularly.

The word, taken apart.
定期 (dìngqī) is built from 定 (dìng) 'settled' and 期 'a set time', both inherited from Middle Chinese, dengH and gi (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'a limit'. The level tone of qī is irregular, as in 星期.

Lesson 6 of 6.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: 大约.
大约 — dàyuē — "approximately; roughly (a time or number)".
yàoshi bù dǔchē wǒ dàyuē bā diǎn dào — If there's no traffic, I'll be there at about eight.

The word, taken apart.
大约 (dàyuē) is built from 大 'big' and 约 'to bind', both inherited from Middle Chinese, dajH and 'jak (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'to tie up' and 'sparing'. How the pair came to mean 'roughly' is not settled.
