## You'll want to know: ఐక్యత
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C409-AIKYATA409-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ఐక్యత** — *aikyata* — "unity".

> ఐక్యత లేకపోతే మనం గెలవలేం.
>
> *aikyata lēkapōtē manaṁ gelavalēṁ.*
>
> Without unity we cannot win.

ఐక్యంగా, united, together.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ఐక్యత** (*aikyata*) is built on Sanskrit *aikya*, "oneness" (CDIAL 2533), from *eka*, "one", with the abstract ending -త.
