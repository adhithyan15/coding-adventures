## You'll want to know: 出错
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C356-MISS356-03]; assesses=[] -->

**出错** — *chūcuò* — "to slip up; to go wrong".

> **他工作从来不出错** *tā gōngzuò cónglái bù chūcuò* — He never slips up at work.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**出错** (*chūcuò*) is built from 出 'to come out' and 错 'wrong', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *tsyhwit* and *tshak* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to go forward' and 'to appear', and the second 'mixed'.
