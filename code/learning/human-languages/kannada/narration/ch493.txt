Kannada, chapter 493: Anger and Mockery.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಸಿಟ್ಟಾಗು (siṭṭāgu) — to get annoyed; to lose your temper.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for delight, then the Kannada for satisfaction.

You'll want to know: ಸಿಟ್ಟಾಗು.
ಸಿಟ್ಟಾಗು — siṭṭāgu — "to get annoyed; to lose your temper".
ತಡವಾಗಿ ಬಂದಿದ್ದಕ್ಕೆ ಅಪ್ಪನಿಗೆ ನನ್ನ ಮೇಲೆ ಸಿಟ್ಟಾಯಿತು. taḍavāgi bandiddakke appanige nanna mēle siṭṭāyitu. Father got annoyed with me for coming late.
Past (it …): ಸಿಟ್ಟಾಯಿತು, siṭṭāyitu.
The one who gets annoyed takes -ಗೆ, the target … ಮೇಲೆ. ಸಿಟ್ಟು, a temper, is the everyday word beside ಕೋಪ.

The word, taken apart.
ಸಿಟ್ಟಾಗು (siṭṭāgu) is built from siṭṭu, "a temper, anger", and ಆಗು, "to become"; the origin of siṭṭu is not settled.

Lesson 2 of 5.
ಕೆಣಕು (keṇaku) — to provoke; to needle.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for satisfaction, then the Kannada for to get annoyed.

You'll want to know: ಕೆಣಕು.
ಕೆಣಕು — keṇaku — "to provoke; to needle".
ಅಣ್ಣ ತಂಗಿಯನ್ನು ಕೆಣಕಿದಾಗ ಅವಳು ಅತ್ತಳು. aṇṇa taṅgiyannu keṇakidāga avaḷu attaḷu. When the elder brother needled his younger sister, she cried.
Past (I …): ಕೆಣಕಿದೆ, keṇakide.
The one provoked takes -ಅನ್ನು. Close to ರೇಗಿಸು, but meaner.

The word, taken apart.
ಕೆಣಕು (keṇaku) is native Dravidian: DEDR 1944 lists Kannada keṇaku, "to irritate, provoke, by words or deeds".

Lesson 4 of 5.
ಹತಾಶೆ (hatāśe) — frustration; despair.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to provoke, then the Kannada for to ridicule.

You'll want to know: ಹತಾಶೆ.
ಹತಾಶೆ — hatāśe — "frustration; despair".
ಮೂರು ಸಲ ಸೋತ ಮೇಲೆ ಅವನಿಗೆ ಹತಾಶೆ ಆಯಿತು. mūru sala sōta mēle avanige hatāśe āyitu. After losing three times, he felt frustrated.
From ಹತ, struck down, and ಆಶೆ, hope: a hope that has been knocked down.

The word, taken apart.
ಹತಾಶೆ (hatāśe) is built from Sanskrit hatāśa, "whose hopes are destroyed, desperate" (Monier-Williams): hata, "struck", and āśā, "hope".

Lesson 5 of 5.
ಉದ್ವೇಗ (udvēga) — agitation; excitement.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to ridicule, then the Kannada for frustration.

You'll want to know: ಉದ್ವೇಗ.
ಉದ್ವೇಗ — udvēga — "agitation; excitement".
ಉದ್ವೇಗದಲ್ಲಿ ಅವನು ಜೋರಾಗಿ ಮಾತಾಡಿದನು. udvēgadalli avanu jōrāgi mātāḍidanu. In his agitation he spoke loudly.
ಉದ್ವೇಗಕ್ಕೆ ಒಳಗಾಗು, to get worked up.

The word, taken apart.
ಉದ್ವೇಗ (udvēga) is borrowed from Sanskrit udvega, "trembling, shaking; agitation, anxiety" (Monier-Williams), from ud-, "up", and vega, "force".
