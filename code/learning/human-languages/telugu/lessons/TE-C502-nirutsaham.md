# నిరుత్సాహం (nirutsāhaṁ) — discouragement

## You'll want to know: నిరుత్సాహం
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C502-NIRASA502-03]; assesses=[] -->

**నిరుత్సాహం** — *nirutsāhaṁ* — "discouragement".

> మొదటిసారి గెలవకపోయినా ఆమెకు నిరుత్సాహం లేదు.
>
> *modaṭisāri gelavakapōyinā āmeku nirutsāhaṁ lēdu.*
>
> Though she did not win the first time, she was not discouraged.

The opposite of ఉత్సాహం, enthusiasm: నిర్-, without.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**నిరుత్సాహం** (*nirutsāhaṁ*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *nirutsāha*, "lacking energy or heart" (Monier-Williams): *nir-*, "without", and *utsāha*, "power, firmness" (CDIAL 1882).
