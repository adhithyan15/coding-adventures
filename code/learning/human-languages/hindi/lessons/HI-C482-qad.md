## You'll want to know: क़द
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C482-KAD482-01]; assesses=[] -->

**क़द** — *qad* — "height; stature".

> अरुण का क़द अपने पिता से लंबा है।
>
> *Aruṇ kā qad apne pitā se lambā hai.*
>
> Arun is taller than his father.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**क़द** (*qad*) is usually traced to Arabic *qadd*, "stature, height", which reached Hindi through Persian.
