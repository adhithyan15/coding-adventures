## You'll want to know: شاهد
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C406-QAZI-03]; assesses=[] -->

**شاهد** (*shâhed*) — "a witness".

> دو شاهد تصادف را دیدند.
>
> *do shâhed tasâdof râ didand.*
>
> Two witnesses saw the accident.

Plural *shâhedân*; the broken plural *shavâhed* means evidence.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**شاهد** comes from Arabic *shāhid* "witness" (EtymDB), one who was present and saw.
