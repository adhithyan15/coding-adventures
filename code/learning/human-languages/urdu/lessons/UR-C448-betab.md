## You'll want to know: بیتاب
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C448-CURIOUS448-04]; assesses=[] -->

**بیتاب** — *betāb* — "eager; impatient".

> بچے چھٹی کے لیے بیتاب ہیں۔
>
> *bachche chhuṭṭī ke liye betāb haiṅ.*
>
> The children can't wait for the holiday.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**بیتاب** (*betāb*) is usually traced to Persian *bētāb*, "without patience": *bē*, "without", and *tāb*, "endurance".
