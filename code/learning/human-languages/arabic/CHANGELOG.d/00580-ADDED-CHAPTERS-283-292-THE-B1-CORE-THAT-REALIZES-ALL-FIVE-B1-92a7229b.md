## Added — chapters 283-292, the B1 core that realizes all five B1 spine nodes

- 45 lessons at sequences 16400-16840, two chapters per B1 node. The track
  had the present for "I" and a handful of past forms, so the core builds the
  verb system a story needs: the present for every person (*yaktubu*), the
  past for every person (*katabtu ... katabū*), *kāna*, *kāna* with the
  present as scene against the plain past as event, the subjunctive after
  *an*, the future with *sa-* / *sawfa* and *lan*, and the imperative.
- SPINE-NARRATE-EVENTS (283-284): *kāna yaktubu* (ASPECT-ONGOING-PAST);
  *awwalan, thumma, baʿda dhālika, fī an-nihāya* (CONNECTIVE-THEN); *ʿindamā*
  and *baʿda an* with the past (NARRATIVE-SEQUENCE); the verb *ḥakā*; a
  reading of about ninety words, AR-C284-qissat-rihla, using only taught
  words.
- SPINE-GIVE-REASONS (285-286): *raʾy*, *fī raʾyī*, *aʿtaqidu anna* and
  *aẓunnu anna* (OPINION-I-THINK); *li-dhālika* and *fa-* against *li-anna*
  (CONNECTIVE-SO); *an adhhaba*, *yajibu an*, *yanbaghī an*, *kāna yajibu an*
  (MODAL-SHOULD); *bi-sabab* against *li-anna*, *as-sababu huwa anna*
  (EXPLANATION-GIVE).
- SPINE-HANDLE-TRAVEL (287-288): *min faḍlik, kayfa aṣilu ilā ...?*
  (DIRECTION-ASK) and the directions as orders; *tadhkirat dhahāb wa-iyāb*
  and three questions at the counter (TRANSPORT-TICKET); *fātanī al-qiṭār*,
  *ḍāʿat ḥaqībatī*, *mādhā yajibu an aʿmala?* (PROBLEM-REPORT); *ṭāʾira*,
  *masʾūl*.
- SPINE-DESCRIBE-EXPERIENCE (289-290): *kuntu saʿīdan* (*kāna* and the
  accusative) and *shaʿartu bi-* (ADJECTIVE-FEELING); *tajriba*; *atamannā
  an*, *aḥlumu bi-*, *urīdu an uṣbiḥa* (AMBITION-EXPRESS); the elative *akbar
  min*, *akbar madīna*, *afḍal* (COMPARISON-BASIC); *mundhu* with the present
  and *qabla* for "ago" (TIME-DURATION).
- SPINE-EXPRESS-CONDITION (291-292): *idhā* and *in* with the past
  (CONNECTIVE-IF); the result clause and its *fa-* (CONDITIONAL-REAL); the
  verb *iʿtamada* and *yaʿtamidu ʿalā*, *ḥasaba* (CONDITION-DEPENDS); *law ...
  la-* and *layta* (MODAL-WOULD); a six-line guided writing task,
  AR-W292-sitta-sutur (connected composition).
- Two letter lessons, each after the word that holds its letter: ؤ
  (AR-W285-hamza-on-waw, after **سؤال**) and ئ (AR-W288-hamza-on-yaa, after
  **طائرة**). Both are compositions of a seat and the hamza mark that
  `data/scripts/arabic.json` already models, so no inventory or owner change
  is needed; the lessons use the track's copy-the-shape wording and claim no
  stroke order.
- Ten new headwords: *kāna*, *ḥakā* and *iʿtamada* (verbs), *raʾy*, *suʾāl*,
  *ṭāʾira*, *masʾūl*, *tajriba*, and the phrases *kayfa aṣilu ilā* and
  *tadhkirat dhahāb wa-iyāb*. Etymologies cite Lane's Lexicon by root; the
  modern senses of *ṭāʾira* and *masʾūl* are hedged.
- The five B1 spine ledgers no longer omit any concept. Pins: Arabic lessons
  1383 -> 1428; the root ledger 941 -> 944 roots; Arabic leaves the A2 reach
  list and joins the tracks whose reach is above A2 (attained stays A2 until
  the B1 vocabulary lands).
