## You'll want to know: riskieren
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C369-RISIKO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**riskieren** — "to risk".

> Er hat alles riskiert und seine eigene Firma gegründet.
>
> *He risked everything and started his own company.*

Perfekt: *hat riskiert*, with no *ge-*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*riskieren* was borrowed from French *risquer* "to risk", from *risque* "risk", which EtymDB traces to Old Italian *risco*.
