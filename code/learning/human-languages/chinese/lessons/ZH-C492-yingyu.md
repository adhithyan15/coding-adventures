## You'll want to know: Yīngyǔ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C492-SUBJECTS492-04]; assesses=[] -->

*Yīngyǔ* — "English (the language)".

> *wǒ xué le liù nián Yīngyǔ* — I studied English for six years.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Yīngyǔ* is built from *Yīng*, short for the name of England, and 语 'speech', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *'jaeng* and *ngjoX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'a flower'; the first stands for the country only in modern use.
