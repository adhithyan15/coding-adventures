## You'll want to know: സുതാര്യമായ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C430-AZHIMATI430-04]; assesses=[] -->

**സുതാര്യമായ** — *sutāryamāya* — "transparent".

> ഭരണം സുതാര്യമായിരിക്കണം.
>
> *bharaṇaṁ sutāryamāyirikkaṇaṁ.*
>
> Administration must be transparent.

It is said of glass and of honest government alike.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**സുതാര്യമായ** (*sutāryamāya*) is a modern learned Malayalam word whose origin is not settled.
