## You'll want to know: दोषः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C293-NYAYALAYA293-01]; assesses=[] -->

**दोषः** — *doṣaḥ* — "guilt; a fault".

> चौरस्य दोषः स्पष्टः अस्ति।
>
> *caurasya doṣaḥ spaṣṭaḥ asti.*
>
> The thief's guilt is clear.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**दोषः** (*doṣaḥ*) comes from the root √duṣ, "to become bad"; MW gives *doṣa* as a fault or defect and also as an offence or guilt (MW).
