## You'll want to know: چور
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C276-CHORI276-01]; assesses=[] -->

**چور** — *chor* — "a thief".

> چور کھڑکی سے آیا۔
>
> *chor khiṛkī se āyā.*
>
> The thief came in through the window.

Masculine or feminine, by the person meant.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**چور** (*chor*) is inherited from Sanskrit *cōrá*, "thief" (CDIAL 4931). It names a man or a woman: the verb shows which, **چور آیا** or **چور آئی**.
