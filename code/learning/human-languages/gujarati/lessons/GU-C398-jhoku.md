## You'll want to know: ઝોકું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C398-DOZE398-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ઝોકું** — *jhokũ* — "a nap; a nod of the head in sleep".

> બપોરે દાદાને ટ્રેનમાં ઝોકું આવ્યું.
>
> *bapore dādāne ṭrenmā̃ jhokũ āvyũ.*
>
> In the afternoon grandfather nodded off on the train.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ઝોકું** (*jhokũ*) seems to belong with the Gujarati verb *jhukvũ*, "to bend", which CDIAL lists under *\*jhukkati*, "stoops", a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 5399): the head bends forward as you doze. CDIAL gives no form for ઝોકું itself, so the link is hedged.
