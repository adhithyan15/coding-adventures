## You'll want to know: ತಾಪಮಾನ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C484-TAPAMANA484-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ತಾಪಮಾನ** — *tāpamāna* — "temperature".

> ಇವತ್ತು ತಾಪಮಾನ ನಿನ್ನೆಗಿಂತ ಹೆಚ್ಚು.
>
> *ivattu tāpamāna ninneginta heccu.*
>
> Today the temperature is higher than yesterday.

ತಾಪ, heat, plus ಮಾನ, a measure.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ತಾಪಮಾನ** (*tāpamāna*) is a modern compound built from Sanskrit words: *tāpa*, "heat, glow" (CDIAL 5767), and *māna*, "a measure, a standard" (CDIAL 10041).
