Punjabi, chapter 219: I Drank the Tea.
8 lessons. You can do the first one in the car; after that you will want to have stopped.


Lesson 1 of 8.
Return to a familiar did — retrieve the familiar I did the work, to drink and to eat by ear.

Warm-up.
[your turn — hear: maiṁ kamm kītā]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
Say ਮੈਂ ਕੰਮ ਕੀਤਾ, "I did the work".

Grammar Lens.
In ਮੈਂ ਕੰਮ ਕੀਤਾ, ਕੀਤਾ agrees with the work, ਕੰਮ, not with the speaker: a woman says it the same way. Hold that next to two verbs that are also done to something: ਪੀਣਾ, ਖਾਣਾ.

Guided Practice.
[your turn — hear: I did the work]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
Say ਮੈਂ ਕੰਮ ਕੀਤਾ.
[your turn — hear: to drink]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
Say ਪੀਣਾ.
[your turn — hear: to eat]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
Say ਖਾਣਾ.

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
Who decides the ending of ਕੀਤਾ? (The work, ਕੰਮ.)


Lesson 2 of 8.
ਪੀਤਾ, ਪੀਤੀ — drank.

Before we start: this one needs your eyes, so it is not fully a driving lesson. You will want your eyes or your hands for the steps that ask you to read, point, write or gesture. You can listen to everything else now. I will say so again when we reach it, but leave this section until you have stopped: Guided Practice.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say I did the work. Then tea and milk.

Grammar Lens: the thing decides.
ਪੀਣਾ works like ਕੀਤਾ: after ਮੈਂ, ਅਸੀਂ or ਤੁਸੀਂ, its past agrees with what was drunk. ਚਾਹ is a feminine word, ਦੁੱਧ a masculine one:
ਮੈਂ ਚਾਹ ਪੀਤੀ। — maiṁ chāh pītī. — I drank tea.
ਮੈਂ ਦੁੱਧ ਪੀਤਾ। — maiṁ duddh pītā. — I drank milk.
A man and a woman say both lines the same way.

[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Lesson 4 of 8.
ਖਾਧਾ, ਖਾਧੀ — ate.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say I drank tea. Then to eat and roti.

Grammar Lens: a past of its own.
ਖਾਣਾ has a past of its own, ਖਾਧਾ, and it agrees with the food in the same way:
ਮੈਂ ਰੋਟੀ ਖਾਧੀ। — maiṁ roṭī khādhī. — I ate a roti.
ਅਸੀਂ ਸੇਬ ਖਾਧਾ। — asīṁ seb khādhā. — We ate an apple.
CDIAL lists Punjabi khādhā, "eaten" (CDIAL 3865).

Guided Practice.

[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

[your turn — hear: I ate an apple and drank milk]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

[once you have stopped driving — read: ਮੈਂ ਰੋਟੀ ਖਾਧੀ।]

Lesson 5 of 8.
ਕੱਢਣਾ (kaḍḍhṇā) — to take out.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say I ate a roti, then a book.

You'll want to know: ਕੱਢਣਾ.
ਕੱਢਣਾ — kaḍḍhṇā — "to take out". It is done to something, so its past agrees with the thing:
ਮੈਂ ਕਿਤਾਬ ਕੱਢੀ। — maiṁ kitāb kaḍḍhī. — I took out the book.

The word, taken apart.
ਕੱਢਣਾ (kaḍḍhṇā) is inherited: CDIAL lists Punjabi kaḍḍhṇā, "to pull out", under a reconstructed kaḍḍhati, "pulls, draws" (CDIAL 2660).

What you've built.
One more word for talking about the past.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: kaḍḍhṇā]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — hear: I took out the book]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
Say ਮੈਂ ਕਿਤਾਬ ਕੱਢੀ।.
[your turn — recall: say to eat, to drink, then to take out]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[once you have stopped driving — write: copy ਕੱਢਣਾ (kaḍḍhṇā) once from the model]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does ਕੱਢਣਾ (kaḍḍhṇā) mean? ("To take out".) Say it once more.

Script you'll notice: ਢ.
ਕ + ੱ + ਢ (ḍha) + ਣ + ਾ becomes ਕੱਢਣਾ (kaḍḍhṇā).
ਢ — ḍha — a Gurmukhi consonant.
Keep ਢ (ḍha) in view. It is the third piece of ਕੱਢਣਾ (kaḍḍhṇā), after the doubling sign ੱ. It belongs to the same row as ਡ, which you know.
No stroke order is taught here: the track does not yet have a verified source for one.

Writing: copy what you can see.
Keep ਢ in front of you. Trace its printed shape once with a finger, then copy it once with a pencil while the model stays visible. Say ḍha when you finish.
This is observe-and-copy with the model visible, not writing from memory.

[once you have stopped driving — look: at ਢ; YOU SAY: ḍha]
[once you have stopped driving — read: ਕੱਢਣਾ (kaḍḍhṇā), then point to ਢ (ḍha)]
[once you have stopped driving — write: copy ਢ (ḍha) once while the model remains visible]

Before we start: this one needs your eyes, so it is not fully a driving lesson. You will want your eyes or your hands for the steps that ask you to read, point, write or gesture. Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say I drank tea, I ate a roti, then I took out the book.

Grammar Lens: two things, two endings.
Join two pasts with ਅਤੇ. Each one agrees with its own thing:
ਮੈਂ ਰੋਟੀ ਖਾਧੀ ਅਤੇ ਚਾਹ ਪੀਤੀ। — maiṁ roṭī khādhī ate chāh pītī. — I ate a roti and drank tea.
ਅਸੀਂ ਸੇਬ ਖਾਧਾ ਅਤੇ ਦੁੱਧ ਪੀਤਾ। — asīṁ seb khādhā ate duddh pītā. — We ate an apple and drank milk.
