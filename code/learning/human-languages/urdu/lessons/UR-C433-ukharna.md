## You'll want to know: اکھڑنا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C433-TILA433-05]; assesses=[] -->

**اکھڑنا** — *ukhaṛnā* — "to be uprooted; to come loose".

> طوفان میں ایک پرانا درخت اکھڑ گیا۔
>
> *tūfān meṅ ek purānā darakht ukhaṛ gayā.*
>
> An old tree was uprooted in the storm.

Past (it …): وہ پودا اکھڑ گیا, *voh paudā ukhaṛ gayā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**اکھڑنا** (*ukhaṛnā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *ukhaṛnā*, "to be rooted up", under *utskarati*, a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 1895).
