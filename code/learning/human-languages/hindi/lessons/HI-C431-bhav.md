## You'll want to know: भाव
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C431-SAUDA431-01]; assesses=[] -->

**भाव** — *bhāv* — "the going rate; a price per unit".

> आज आलू का भाव क्या है?
>
> *āj ālū kā bhāv kyā hai?*
>
> What is the price of potatoes today?

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**भाव** (*bhāv*) is Sanskrit *bhāva*, "being, a state, a condition" (Monier-Williams); in Hindi markets it came to mean the state of prices on the day, a sense the dictionary does not give. The same word also means a feeling.
