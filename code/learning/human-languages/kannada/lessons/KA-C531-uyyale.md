# ಉಯ್ಯಾಲೆ (uyyāle) — a swing

## You'll want to know: ಉಯ್ಯಾಲೆ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C531-ATIKE531-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ಉಯ್ಯಾಲೆ** — *uyyāle* — "a swing".

> ಉದ್ಯಾನದಲ್ಲಿ ಮಕ್ಕಳು ಉಯ್ಯಾಲೆ ಆಡಿದರು.
>
> *udyānadalli makkaḷu uyyāle āḍidaru.*
>
> The children played on the swings in the park.

ಉಯ್ಯಾಲೆ ಆಡು, to swing.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಉಯ್ಯಾಲೆ** (*uyyāle*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 731 lists Kannada *uyyal(u)*, *uyyāle*, "a swing", beside Tamil *ūñcal* and Telugu *uyyāla*.
