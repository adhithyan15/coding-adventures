## You'll want to know: ताळी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C276-BANSLI276-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ताळी** — *tāḷī* — "a clap".

> सगळा ताळी बजावै है।
>
> *sagḷā tāḷī bajāvai hai.*
>
> Everyone claps.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ताळी** (*tāḷī*) is inherited from Sanskrit *tāla*, "a clap, rhythm"; CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry, but lists Gujarati *tāḷī*, "clapping" (CDIAL 5748). You clap with **बजावणो**: **ताळी बजावो**.
