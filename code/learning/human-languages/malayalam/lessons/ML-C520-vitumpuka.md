# വിതുമ്പുക (vitumpuka) — to sob; to choke up

## You'll want to know: വിതുമ്പുക
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C520-ANUKAMPA520-05]; assesses=[] -->

**വിതുമ്പുക** — *vitumpuka* — "to sob; to choke up".

> അച്ഛൻ യാത്ര പറഞ്ഞപ്പോൾ കുട്ടി വിതുമ്പി.
>
> *acchan yātra paṟaññappōḷ kuṭṭi vitumpi.*
>
> When Father said goodbye, the child began to sob.

Past: വിതുമ്പി, *vitumpi*.

Crying held back, with trembling lips; louder crying is കരയുക.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**വിതുമ്പുക** (*vitumpuka*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 5399 gives the Malayalam verb with the sense of starting to cry, as a child does (DEDR 5399).
