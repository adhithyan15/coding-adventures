## You'll want to know: शोभा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C326-DIPAVALI326-05]; assesses=[] -->

**शोभा** — *śobhā* — "splendour; beauty".

> दीपैः नगरस्य शोभा वर्धते।
>
> *dīpaiḥ nagarasya śobhā vardhate.*
>
> With the lamps the splendour of the city grows.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**शोभा** (*śobhā*) is usually traced to the root √śubh, "to shine", as in *śobhate*; MW gives *śobhā* as splendour, brilliance, beauty, grace (MW).
