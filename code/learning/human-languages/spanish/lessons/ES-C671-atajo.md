## You'll want to know: el atajo
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C671-LEJOS-03]; assesses=[] -->

**el atajo** — "the short cut".

> Por este atajo llegamos antes.
>
> *We get there sooner by this short cut.*

*Coger un atajo* — to take a short cut.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*atajo* is built on *atajar* "to cut short", from *tajar* "to cut" (EtymDB), which comes from Late Latin *taliāre* "to cut".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
