## You'll want to know: ابر
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C431-ABR431-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ابر** — *abr* — "a rain cloud; cloud (formal)".

> آسمان پر ابر ہے۔
>
> *āsmān par abr hai.*
>
> The sky is overcast.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ابر** (*abr*) is usually traced to Persian *abr*, "a cloud"; it is a more formal word than **بادل**.
