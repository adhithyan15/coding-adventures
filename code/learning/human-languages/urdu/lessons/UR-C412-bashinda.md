## You'll want to know: باشندہ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C412-QASBA412-03]; assesses=[] -->

**باشندہ** — *bāshindā* — "an inhabitant; a resident".

> وہ اسی قصبے کا باشندہ ہے۔
>
> *voh usī qasbe kā bāshindā hai.*
>
> He comes from that very town.

Masculine or feminine, by the person meant.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**باشندہ** (*bāshindā*) is usually traced to Persian *bāshinda*, "one who lives somewhere".
