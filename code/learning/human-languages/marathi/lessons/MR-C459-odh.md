## You'll want to know: ओढ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C459-ODH459-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ओढ** — *oḍh* — "a longing; a pull".

> सुट्टीत गावाची ओढ लागते.
>
> *suṭṭīt gāvācī oḍh lāgte.*
>
> In the holidays one longs for the village.

Feminine.

ओढ लागणे, to feel drawn to, with the genitive: घराची ओढ. It belongs with ओढणे, to pull.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ओढ** (*oḍh*) is a Marathi noun beside ओढणे, "to pull", which goes back to the reconstructed *\*avakaḍḍhati*, "pulls away" (CDIAL 14245).
