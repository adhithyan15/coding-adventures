## You'll want to know: आवर्तः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C415-SROTAS415-02]; assesses=[] -->

**आवर्तः** — *āvartaḥ* — "a whirlpool".

> नद्याः मध्ये आवर्तः अस्ति।
>
> *nadyāḥ madhye āvartaḥ asti.*
>
> There is a whirlpool in the middle of the river.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**आवर्तः** (*āvartaḥ*) is built from *ā* and the root √vṛt, "to turn": a turning round; MW gives *āvarta* as a whirlpool (MW).
