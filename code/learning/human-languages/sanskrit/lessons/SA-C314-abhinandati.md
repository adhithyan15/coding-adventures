## You'll want to know: अभिनन्दति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C314-SHAURYA314-05]; assesses=[] -->

**अभिनन्दति** — *abhinandati* — "congratulates; hails".

> राजा वीरम् अभिनन्दति।
>
> *rājā vīram abhinandati.*
>
> The king congratulates the hero.

Past (he …): सः वीरम् अभिनन्दितवान्, *saḥ vīram abhinanditavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अभिनन्दति** (*abhinandati*) is built from *abhi* and the root √nand, "to rejoice", as in *nandati*; MW gives *abhi-nand* as to rejoice at, welcome, greet, hail, praise (MW).
