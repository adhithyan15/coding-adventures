## You'll want to know: löschen
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C293-BRENNEN-04]; assesses=[] -->

**löschen** — "to put out, to extinguish; to delete".

> Die Feuerwehr löschte das Feuer in einer Stunde.
>
> *The fire brigade put out the fire in an hour.*

On a computer it means to delete: *ein Foto löschen*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*löschen* is inherited from Middle High German *leschen*, Old High German *lescan* (EtymDB). The old verb is usually glossed "to go out, to die down"; later it came to mean "to make go out".
