## You'll want to know: مهمل
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C503-TABA503-05]; assesses=[] -->

**مهمل** — *muhmil* — "careless".

> كسر العامل المهمل الزجاج.
>
> *kasara al-ʿāmil al-muhmil az-zujāj.*
>
> The careless worker broke the glass.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**مهمل** (*muhmil*) is built on the native Arabic root *h-m-l*, "to leave neglected" (Lane). Muhmil is the participle of Form IV: one who leaves things neglected.
