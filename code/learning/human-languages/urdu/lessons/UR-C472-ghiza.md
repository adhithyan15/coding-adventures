## You'll want to know: غذا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C472-STOMACH472-05]; assesses=[] -->

**غذا** — *ghizā* — "food; nourishment".

> مریض کو ہلکی غذا دیجیے۔
>
> *marīz ko halkī ghizā dījiye.*
>
> Give the patient light food.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**غذا** (*ghizā*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *ghidhāʾ*, "nourishment".
