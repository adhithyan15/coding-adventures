## You'll want to know: rever
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C378-REVER-01]; assesses=[] -->

**rever** — "to revise, to go over; to see again".

> Revi a matéria toda no fim de semana.
>
> *I revised all the material at the weekend.*

Conjugated like *ver*: *revejo, revês, revê*. In Portugal it is the usual word for revising for exams.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*rever* is built from *re-* "again" and *ver* "to see", inherited from Latin *vidēre* "to see"; compare Latin *revidēre* "to see again, to go to see again" (Lewis & Short).
