# گمشدہ (gumshudā) — missing; lost

## Warm-up

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Urdu for to be separated, then the Urdu for to lose one's way.

## You'll want to know: گمشدہ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C298-BICHHAR298-03]; assesses=[] -->

**گمشدہ** — *gumshudā* — "missing; lost".

> گمشدہ بچہ صبح مل گیا۔
>
> *gumshudā bachchā subah mil gayā.*
>
> The missing child was found in the morning.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**گمشدہ** (*gumshudā*) is usually traced to Persian *gum-shuda*, built from *gum*, "lost", and *shuda*, "become". It is the word on notices for missing people.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
