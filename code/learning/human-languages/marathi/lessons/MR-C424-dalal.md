## You'll want to know: दलाल
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C424-VIKRI424-04]; assesses=[] -->

**दलाल** — *dalāl* — "a broker; an agent".

> दलालाने आम्हाला भाड्याचं घर दाखवलं.
>
> *dalālāne āmhālā bhāḍyācaṁ ghar dākhavlaṁ.*
>
> The broker showed us a house for rent.

Masculine.

दलाली is his fee.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**दलाल** (*dalāl*) is a loan through Persian, usually traced to Arabic *dallāl*, "a broker, a guide".
