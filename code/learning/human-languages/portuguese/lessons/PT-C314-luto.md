## You'll want to know: o luto
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C314-FALECER-04]; assesses=[] -->

**o luto** — "mourning".

> A família está de luto.
>
> *The family is in mourning.*

*Estar de luto* — to be in mourning; people often wear black.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*luto* is inherited from Latin *lūctus* "grief, sorrow" (EtymDB).
