## You'll want to know: كأس
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C547-BUTULA547-03]; assesses=[] -->

**كأس** — *kaʾs* — "a trophy; a cup".

> رفع الفريق الكأس بعد المباراة.
>
> *rafaʿa al-farīq al-kaʾs baʿda al-mubārā.*
>
> The team lifted the trophy after the match.

It is also a drinking glass.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**كأس** (*kaʾs*) is built on the native Arabic root *k-ʾ-s*, "a cup, a drinking vessel" (Lane). Lane gives kaʾs as a cup with drink in it.
