## You'll want to know: त्यार
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C220-PLAN220-02]; assesses=[] -->

**त्यार** — *tyār* — "ready".

> हूं अबार त्यार हूं।
>
> *hū̃ abār tyār hū̃.*
>
> I'm ready now.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**त्यार** (*tyār*) is usually traced to Persian *taiyār*, "ready", which Persian took from Arabic; Marwadi has worn it down to one syllable.
