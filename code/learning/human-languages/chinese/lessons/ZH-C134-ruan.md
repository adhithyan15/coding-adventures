## You'll want to know: ruǎn
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C134-THING134-02]; assesses=[] -->

*ruǎn* — "soft".

> *zhè zhāng chuáng hěn ruǎn* — This bed is soft.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ruǎn* is inherited from Middle Chinese *nywenX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'soft', under an older way of writing the character.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
