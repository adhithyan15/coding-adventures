## You'll want to know: मृन्मयः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C505-MRD505-02]; assesses=[] -->

**मृन्मयः** — *mṛnmayaḥ* — "earthen".

> कुम्भकारः मृन्मयानि पात्राणि रचयति।
>
> *kumbhakāraḥ mṛnmayāni pātrāṇi racayati.*
>
> The potter makes earthen pots.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**मृन्मयः** (*mṛnmayaḥ*) is built on *mṛd*, "clay", with *-maya*, "made of"; MW gives *mṛnmaya*, so spelt (MW calls *mṛṇmaya* a wrong reading), as made of earth or clay, earthen (MW).

## What you've built
