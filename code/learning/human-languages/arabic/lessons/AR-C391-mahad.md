## You'll want to know: معهد
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C391-BAHTH391-04]; assesses=[] -->

**معهد** — *maʿhad* — "an institute".

> درست الموسيقى في معهد صغير.
>
> *darastu al-mūsīqā fī maʿhad ṣaghīr.*
>
> I studied music at a small institute.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**معهد** (*maʿhad*) is built on the native Arabic root *ʿ-h-d*, "to enjoin; to know a place" (Lane). A *maʿhad* is a place one knows and comes back to; in modern use, an institute.
