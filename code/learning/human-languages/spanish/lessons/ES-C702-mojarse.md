## You'll want to know: mojarse
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C702-MOJARSE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**mojarse** — "to get wet".

> Me mojé porque no llevaba paraguas.
>
> *I got wet because I had no umbrella.*

Without *se*, *mojar* is to wet something.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*mojarse* is built on *mojar* "to wet", which is usually traced to Vulgar Latin *molliāre* "to soften", from *mollis* "soft": wet things go soft.
