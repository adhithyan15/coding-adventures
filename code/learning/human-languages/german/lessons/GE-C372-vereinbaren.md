## You'll want to know: vereinbaren
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C372-VEREINBARUNG-01]; assesses=[] -->

**vereinbaren** — "to agree on, to arrange (formally)".

> Wir haben einen Termin für Dienstag vereinbart.
>
> *We have arranged an appointment for Tuesday.*

Inseparable: *wir vereinbaren, wir haben vereinbart*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*vereinbaren* is inherited from Middle High German *vereinbæren* (EtymDB), built on *einbære* "united", from *ein* "one", Proto-Germanic *ainaz*: two sides become one.
