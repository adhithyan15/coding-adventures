## You'll want to know: 本意
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C352-AGREE352-04]; assesses=[] -->

**本意** — *běnyì* — "what one really meant; original intention".

> **这不是我的本意** *zhè bú shì wǒ de běnyì* — That's not what I meant.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**本意** (*běnyì*) is built from 本 'the root' and 意 'intent', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *pwonX* and *'iH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'the root, as against the branches' and the second 'will'.
