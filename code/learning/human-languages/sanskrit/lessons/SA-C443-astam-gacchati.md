# अस्तं गच्छति (astaṃ gacchati) — sets in the west; goes down (of the sun)

## You'll want to know: अस्तं गच्छति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C443-ASTA443-02]; assesses=[] -->

**अस्तं गच्छति** — *astaṃ gacchati* — "sets in the west; goes down (of the sun)".

> सूर्यः पश्चिमायाम् अस्तं गच्छति।
>
> *sūryaḥ paścimāyām astaṃ gacchati.*
>
> The sun sets in the west.

Past (it …): सूर्यः अस्तं गतवान्, *sūryaḥ astaṃ gatavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अस्तं गच्छति** (*astaṃ gacchati*) is built from *astam*, "home, the setting of the sun", and the root √gam, "to go": to go home; MW gives *astam* with √gam as to set, of the sun; CDIAL 976 gives *astam eti*, "goes home, sets", from the Rigveda on (MW; CDIAL 976).
