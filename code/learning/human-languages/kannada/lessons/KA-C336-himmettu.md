# ಹಿಮ್ಮೆಟ್ಟು (himmeṭṭu) — to retreat; to fall back

## Warm-up

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Kannada for a warrior, then the Kannada for to fight.

## You'll want to know: ಹಿಮ್ಮೆಟ್ಟು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C336-SAINYA336-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ಹಿಮ್ಮೆಟ್ಟು** — *himmeṭṭu* — "to retreat; to fall back".

> ದೊಡ್ಡ ಸೈನ್ಯ ಬರುವುದನ್ನು ನೋಡಿ ಅವರು ಹಿಮ್ಮೆಟ್ಟಿದರು.
>
> *doḍḍa sainya baruvudannu nōḍi avaru himmeṭṭidaru.*
>
> Seeing a large army coming, they fell back.

Past (I …): ಹಿಮ್ಮೆಟ್ಟಿದೆ, *himmeṭṭide*.

ಹಿಂ, back, and ಮೆಟ್ಟು, to step: to step back.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಹಿಮ್ಮೆಟ್ಟು** (*himmeṭṭu*) is a native Dravidian compound: *him*, "behind, back" (DEDR 4205), and *meṭṭu*, "to put the foot down, to step" (DEDR 5057).
