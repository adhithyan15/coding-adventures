## You'll want to know: ゆうかん
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C439-BRAVE439-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ゆうかん** — *yūkan* — "courageous; brave".

> **おまわりさんは ゆうかんでした。** — *omawarisan wa yūkan deshita* — "The police officer was brave."

A na-adjective: な before a noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ゆうかん** (*yūkan*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *yū*, "courage" (Middle Chinese *yowngX*) and *kan*, "daring" (*kamX*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
