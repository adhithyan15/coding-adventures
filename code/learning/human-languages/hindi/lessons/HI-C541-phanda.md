## You'll want to know: फंदा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C541-RUKAVAT541-04]; assesses=[] -->

**फंदा** — *phandā* — "a noose; a snare".

> किसान ने चूहों के लिए एक फंदा रखा।
>
> *Kisān ne cūhoṁ ke liye ek phandā rakhā.*
>
> The farmer set a snare for the rats.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**फंदा** (*phandā*) is probably related to Hindi *phā̃d* and *phā̃s*, "a noose, a net", which CDIAL files under Sanskrit *spāśa*, "a fetter, a noose" (CDIAL 13813); CDIAL does not list *phandā* itself.
