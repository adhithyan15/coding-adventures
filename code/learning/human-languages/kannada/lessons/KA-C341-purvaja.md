## You'll want to know: ಪೂರ್ವಜ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C341-VAMSHA341-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ಪೂರ್ವಜ** — *pūrvaja* — "an ancestor".

> ನಮ್ಮ ಪೂರ್ವಜರು ಈ ಊರಿಗೆ ಬಂದು ಮನೆ ಕಟ್ಟಿದರು.
>
> *namma pūrvajaru ī ūrige bandu mane kaṭṭidaru.*
>
> Our ancestors came to this village and built houses.

Plural: ಪೂರ್ವಜರು, *pūrvajaru*.

ಪೂರ್ವ, before, and ಜ, born: one born before.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಪೂರ್ವಜ** (*pūrvaja*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *pūrvaja*, "born before, former, ancient" (Monier-Williams).
