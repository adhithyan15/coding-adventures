## You'll want to know: il padrino
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C308-BATTESIMO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**il padrino** — "the godfather".

> Mio zio è il mio padrino.
>
> *My uncle is my godfather.*

A little *padre*, ‘father’.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*padrino* is inherited from Vulgar Latin *patrīnus* (EtymDB), built on *pater* "father".
