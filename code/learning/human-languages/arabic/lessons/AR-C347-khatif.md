## You'll want to know: خاطف
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C347-KHATF347-05]; assesses=[] -->

**خاطف** — *khāṭif* — "a kidnapper".

> هرب الخاطف بالسيارة.
>
> *haraba al-khāṭif bi-s-sayyāra.*
>
> The kidnapper got away in the car.

Plural خاطفون. As an adjective it is swift: زيارة خاطفة, a flying visit.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**خاطف** (*khāṭif*) is built on the native Arabic root *kh-ṭ-f* (Lane), "to snatch". *Khāṭif* is the active participle, "one who snatches".
