# చింతించు (cintiñcu) — to worry; to regret

## You'll want to know: చింతించు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C494-BHAVAM494-02]; assesses=[] -->

**చింతించు** — *cintiñcu* — "to worry; to regret".

> నాన్న డబ్బు గురించి చాలా చింతించాడు.
>
> *nānna ḍabbu guriñci cālā cintiñcāḍu.*
>
> Dad worried a lot about money.

Past (I …): చింతించాను, *cintiñcānu*.

What you worry about takes గురించి. The noun is చింత, worry or care.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**చింతించు** (*cintiñcu*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *cintayati*, "thinks; cares for" (CDIAL 4815), and takes the Telugu ending -ఇంచు, which turns a Sanskrit stem into a verb. Thinking too much about a thing becomes worrying about it.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
