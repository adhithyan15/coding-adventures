# நிலைகுலை (nilaikulai) — to fall apart

## Warm-up

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Tamil for a curfew, then the Tamil for to stall.

## You'll want to know: நிலைகுலை
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C300-AVASARAM300-04]; assesses=[] -->

**நிலைகுலை** — *nilaikulai* — "to fall apart".

> அந்தச் செய்தியைக் கேட்டு நான் நிலைகுலைந்தேன்.
>
> *antac ceytiyaik kēṭṭu nāṉ nilaikulaintēṉ.*
>
> I fell apart when I heard that news.

Past (I …): நிலைகுலைந்தேன், *nilaikulaintēṉ*.

From நிலை, a steady state, and குலை, to be upset.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**நிலைகுலை** (*nilaikulai*) is a Tamil compound built from நிலை, "standing, a steady state" (DEDR 3675), and குலை, "to be upset, thrown into confusion" (DEDR 1806).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
