## You'll want to know: il commissariato
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C294-PATTUGLIA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**il commissariato** — "the police station".

> Il commissariato è in fondo alla strada.
>
> *The police station is at the end of the street.*

The local office of *la polizia*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*commissariato* is built on *commissario* "commissioner", usually traced to Medieval Latin *commissārius*, built on Latin *committere* "to entrust" (Lewis & Short).
