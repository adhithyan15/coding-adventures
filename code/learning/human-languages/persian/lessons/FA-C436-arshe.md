## You'll want to know: عرشه
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C436-ESKELE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**عرشه** (*'arshe*) — "a deck (of a ship)".

> مسافران روی عرشه ایستاده بودند.
>
> *Mosâferân ru-ye 'arshe istâde budand.*
>
> The passengers were standing on the deck.

*Ru-ye 'arshe* — on deck.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*'arshe* was borrowed from Arabic and is usually traced to Arabic *ʿarš* 'a roof, a raised platform, a throne'. EtymDB does not list it.
