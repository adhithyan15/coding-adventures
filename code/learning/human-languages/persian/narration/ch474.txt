Persian, chapter 474: Rain.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
باریدن (bâridan) — to rain, to fall (of rain or snow).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to change, then the Persian for below zero.

You'll want to know: باریدن.
باریدن (bâridan) — "to rain, to fall (of rain or snow)".
تمام شب باران بارید. Tamâm-e shab bârân bârid. It rained all night.
Present stem bâr-: bârân mibârad — it is raining. Snow too: barf bârid.

The word, taken apart.
bâridan is a native verb on the same stem as bârân 'rain', which is usually traced to Middle Persian wārān. EtymDB does not list the verb.

Lesson 2 of 5.
رگبار (ragbâr) — a downpour, a heavy shower.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for below zero, then the Persian for to rain.

You'll want to know: رگبار.
رگبار (ragbâr) — "a downpour, a heavy shower".
یک رگبار تند آمد و همه خیس شدیم. Yek ragbâr-e tond âmad va hame khis shodim. A heavy shower came and we all got wet.
Also a burst of gunfire.

The word, taken apart.
ragbâr is usually analysed as native rag 'vein, streak' and bâr 'raining', the stem of bâridan: rain falling in streaks. EtymDB does not list it.

You'll want to know: نم نم.
نم نم (namnam) — "a drizzle; drizzling".
صبح باران نم نم میبارید. Sobh bârân namnam mibârid. In the morning it was drizzling.
Bârân-e namnam — light rain.

The word, taken apart.
namnam doubles native nam 'damp, moisture', which EtymDB lists and traces to Middle Persian.

Lesson 4 of 5.
بند آمدن (band âmadan) — to stop (of rain, a flow).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a downpour, then the Persian for a drizzle.

You'll want to know: بند آمدن.
بند آمدن (band âmadan) — "to stop (of rain, a flow)".
بعد از یک ساعت باران بند آمد. Ba'd az yek sâ'at bârân band âmad. After an hour the rain stopped.
band 'tie, block' + âmadan. Also of bleeding or of a voice.

The word, taken apart.
band âmadan is built from native band 'a tie, a block', from the root of bastan (PIE bʰendʰ-, EtymDB), and âmadan 'to come', from PIE gʷem- (EtymDB).

Lesson 5 of 5.
سیلاب (seylâb) — floodwater, a flood.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a drizzle, then the Persian for to stop.

You'll want to know: سیلاب.
سیلاب (seylâb) — "floodwater, a flood".
سیلاب (seylâb) پل روستا را خراب کرد. Seylâb pol-e rustâ râ kharâb kard. The floodwater destroyed the village bridge.
seyl 'flood' + âb 'water'.

The word, taken apart.
seylâb is built from seyl 'flood', borrowed from Arabic sayl, and native âb 'water', from Middle Persian āb (EtymDB). EtymDB lists seylâb itself without a source.
