# تمهل (tamahhala) — to slow down; to take one's time

## You'll want to know: تمهل
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C445-TRAM445-04]; assesses=[] -->

**تمهل** — *tamahhala* — "to slow down; to take one's time".

> تمهل أبي عند المنعطف.
>
> *tamahhala abī ʿinda al-munʿaṭaf.*
>
> My father slowed down at the bend.

Present: يتمهل, *yatamahhal*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**تمهل** (*tamahhala*) is built on the native Arabic root *m-h-l*, "to act gently, without haste" (Lane). It is Form V.
