## You'll want to know: چنانچه
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C391-FARZ-03]; assesses=[] -->

**چنانچه** (*chenânche*) — "if (formal), in case".

> چنانچه هوا خوب باشد، مسابقه برگزار میشود.
>
> *chenânche havâ khub bâshad, mosâbeqe bargozâr mishavad.*
>
> If the weather is good, the match will be held.

Formal and written, like *agar*; it takes the subjunctive.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**چنانچه** is a native Persian compound built from *chenân* "so, in that way" and *che* "what": in such a way that, if.
