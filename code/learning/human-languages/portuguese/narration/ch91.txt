Portuguese, chapter 91: Underground, Tram, Journey, Holidays, Bicycle.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
o metro — the underground.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for a bakery, then the Portuguese for a butcher's.

You'll want to know: o metro.
o metro — "the underground". In Brazil, o metrô.

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: o metro]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: o metro, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say o metro]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Portuguese for a bakery, then the Portuguese for a butcher's, then say o metro again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does o metro mean? ("The underground".) Say it once more.
