## You'll want to know: سلاح
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C326-SILAH326-01]; assesses=[] -->

**سلاح** — *silāḥ* — "a weapon".

> كان مع الحارس سلاح صغير.
>
> *kāna maʿa al-ḥāris silāḥ ṣaghīr.*
>
> The guard had a small weapon.

Plural: أسلحة, *asliḥa*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**سلاح** (*silāḥ*) is built on the native Arabic root *s-l-ḥ* (Lane), where *silāḥ* names the arms of war and *musallaḥ*, armed, is its participle.
