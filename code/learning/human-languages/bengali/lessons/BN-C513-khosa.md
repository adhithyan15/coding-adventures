## You'll want to know: খোসা
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C513-KOTA513-02]; assesses=[] -->

**খোসা** — *khosā* — "a peel; a husk".

> আমি আলুর খোসা বালতিতে ফেললাম।
>
> *Āmi ālur khosā bāltite phellām.*
>
> I threw the potato peel into the bucket.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**খোসা** (*khosā*) comes from a reconstructed *khosa*; CDIAL lists Bengali *khosā*, "husk" (CDIAL 3948).
