## You'll want to know: ભેદવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C550-NISHAN550-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ભેદવું** — *bhedvũ* — "to pierce (a target)".

> જો અરુણ પહેલા તીરથી નિશાન ભેદત તો તે જીતી જાત.
>
> *jo Aruṇ pahelā tīrthī niśān bhedat to te jītī jāt.*
>
> If Arun had hit the target with the first arrow, he would have won.

Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે નિશાન ભેદ્યું, *teṇe niśān bhedyũ*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ભેદવું** (*bhedvũ*) goes back to Sanskrit *bhid*, "to split, to pierce" (Monier-Williams).
