# கருவூலம் (karuvūlam) — a treasury

## You'll want to know: கருவூலம்
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C330-PERARASU330-03]; assesses=[] -->

**கருவூலம்** — *karuvūlam* — "a treasury".

> மன்னரின் கருவூலத்தில் நிறையத் தங்கம் இருந்தது.
>
> *maṉṉariṉ karuvūlattil niṟaiyat taṅkam iruntatu.*
>
> There was a lot of gold in the king's treasury.

Today also a government treasury.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**கருவூலம்** (*karuvūlam*), "a treasury", is a Tamil word whose origin is not settled in the sources at hand.
