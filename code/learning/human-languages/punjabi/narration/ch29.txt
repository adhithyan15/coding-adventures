Punjabi, chapter 29: Build One Fictional Age Field.
14 lessons. The first lesson already needs your eyes or your hands, so save this one for when you have stopped.


Lesson 1 of 14.
ਉ (u) — one new shape for the age label.

Before we start: this one needs your hands, so it is not a driving lesson. You will want a pen and something to write on and your eyes, for letter shapes on the page. You can listen to everything else now — leave the section called Script — one tiny step until you have stopped, and I will say so again when we reach it.

Warm-up.
Write the familiar ਮ and ਰ once each. The age label needs only one new letter before those known pieces can return.

Script — one tiny step.
Trace ਉ slowly twice. It is the independent vowel u. Do not assemble a word yet.

Guided Practice.
Cover the model, write ਉ (u) once, and repair only the first shape difference.

Wrap-up recall.
Stop after one new letter.
[question — say your answer, then pause 12 seconds]
Which new independent Gurmukhi vowel did you trace?
