# если бы — the condition that is not so

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[RU-GRAMMAR-BY-PAST-FORM-01, RU-GRAMMAR-BY-POLITE-01, RU-GRAMMAR-PERFECTIVE-FUTURE-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "I'd like a coffee". (*Ya by khotél kófe.*) Then the real
plan: "if I have time, I'll ring". (*Yésli búdet vrémya, ya pozvonyú.*)

## Grammar Lens: у меня было, I had
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-GRAMMAR-U-MENYA-BYLO-01]; assesses=[RU-LEX-MENYA-ZOVUT, RU-GRAMMAR-BYL-PAST-01, RU-LEX-C112-THINGS112-01, RU-LEX-C69-TIME2-05] -->

You have said **у меня проблема**, "at me, a problem". "I have time" is
built the same way: **у меня есть время**. In the past, the thing you had
is the subject, and **было** agrees with it: *время* is neuter, so **у
меня было время** — *u menyá býlo vrémya* — I had time.

## Grammar Lens: бы on both sides
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-GRAMMAR-YESLI-BY-01]; assesses=[RU-GRAMMAR-YESLI-CLAUSE-01, RU-LEX-C232-ACT232-03, RU-LEX-POMOGAT, RU-LEX-ZNAT] -->

> **Если бы у меня было время, я бы позвонил.** — *yésli by u menyá býlo
> vrémya, ya by pozvoníl* — If I had time, I would ring.
> **Если бы я знал, я бы помог.** — *yésli by ya znal, ya by pomóg* — If I
> had known, I would have helped.

**Бы** stands on both sides, each time with the past form: *знал*,
*позвонил*, and *помог*, one of the few without an *л*. Russian marks
no time here: the first can mean "if I had time now" or "if I had had
time then", and the story around it decides. **Помог**, "helped", is the
past of *помочь*, the partner of *помогать*.

## Why it's said this way: real, or not real
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[RU-GRAMMAR-YESLI-FUTURE-01, RU-GRAMMAR-PERFECTIVE-FUTURE-01, RU-GRAMMAR-BY-PAST-FORM-01] -->

> **Если будет время, я позвоню.** — It may well happen: a plan.
> **Если бы было время, я бы позвонил.** — It is not so: a wish, or a
> regret.

[PAUSE 3s] "I had time"? (***У меня было время.***) A plan or a wish:
*если бы было время*? (**A wish**: it is not so.) How many times does *бы*
appear? (**Twice**, once on each side.)
