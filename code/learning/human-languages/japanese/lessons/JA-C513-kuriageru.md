## You'll want to know: くりあげる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C513-PLAN513-03]; assesses=[] -->

**くりあげる** — *kuriageru* — "to bring forward (a date)".

> **もし じかんが あったら、かいぎを くりあげます。** — *moshi jikan ga attara, kaigi o kuriagemasu* — "If there is time, we will bring the meeting forward."

Polite **くりあげます**, *kuriagemasu*; an ichidan verb. Past (polite): **がっこうが しけんを くりあげました。**, *gakkō ga shiken o kuriagemashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**くりあげる** (*kuriageru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi, and its older origin is not settled (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
