Mandarin Chinese, chapter 77: Thanks and Replies.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
不客气 (bú kèqi) — you're welcome; don't mention it.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for good night, then the Mandarin for bye-bye.

You'll want to know: 不客气.
不客气 — bú kèqi — "you're welcome; don't mention it".
不客气请进 bú kèqi qǐng jìn — Don't mention it. Do come in!
bù changes its tone here, rising to bú before the falling kè: bú kèqi.

The word, taken apart.
不客气 (bú kèqi) is built from 不 'not' and kèqi 'polite, formal', from Middle Chinese khaek 'guest' and khj+jH 'breath' (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription): no need to be formal.

What you've built.
One more word for thanking and answering thanks.

[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Lesson 2 of 5.
máfan — to trouble; to bother.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for bye-bye, then the Mandarin for don't mention it.

You'll want to know: máfan.
máfan — "to trouble; to bother".
bù hǎoyìsi máfan nǐ — Sorry to trouble you.
Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
máfan is built from má and fán, both inherited from Middle Chinese, mae and bjon (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription). The dictionary glosses the first as hemp and the second as 'toil'; how the pair came to mean putting somebody to trouble is not settled.

Lesson 4 of 5.
fēicháng — extremely; very.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for to trouble somebody, then the Mandarin for to thank.

You'll want to know: fēicháng.
fēicháng — "extremely; very".
fēicháng gǎnxiè — Thank you so much.
Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
fēicháng is built from fēi 'not' and cháng, both inherited from Middle Chinese, pj+j and dzyang (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription). The dictionary glosses the first as 'it is not so'; the second as 'usual' is not given there, so 'out of the ordinary' is likely but not settled.
