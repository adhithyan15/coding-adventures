Arabic, chapter 398: Schooling.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
تعليم (taʿlīm) — education.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a syllabus, then the Arabic for training.

You'll want to know: تعليم.
تعليم — taʿlīm — "education".
التعليم مجاني في هذا البلد. at-taʿlīm majjānī fī hādhā al-balad. Education is free in this country.

The word, taken apart.
تعليم (taʿlīm) is built on the native Arabic root ʿ-l-m, "to know" (Lane). Taʿlīm is the Form II verbal noun, making someone know: teaching, education.

روضة — rawḍa — "a kindergarten".
يذهب ابني إلى الروضة كل صباح. yadhhabu ibnī ilā ar-rawḍa kulla ṣabāḥ. My son goes to kindergarten every morning.

The word, taken apart.
روضة (rawḍa) is built on the native Arabic root r-w-ḍ (Lane), where it names a green meadow or garden. Modern Arabic made it the kindergarten, a garden of children.

Lesson 4 of 5.
محو الأمية (maḥw al-ummiyya) — literacy.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for upbringing, then the Arabic for a kindergarten.

You'll want to know: محو الأمية.
محو الأمية — maḥw al-ummiyya — "literacy".
تعمل الجمعية على محو الأمية في القرى. taʿmalu al-jamʿiyya ʿalā maḥw al-ummiyya fī al-qurā. The association works for literacy in the villages.
Literally the wiping out of illiteracy; أمي, ummī, is one who cannot read.

The word, taken apart.
محو الأمية (maḥw al-ummiyya) joins maḥw, "wiping out", built on the native Arabic root m-ḥ-w, and ummiyya, "illiteracy", from ummī, built on ʾ-m-m (Lane): one still as his mother bore him, unable to read.
