# कोस (kos) — a kos (about two miles)

## You'll want to know: कोस
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C408-MIL408-02]; assesses=[] -->

**कोस** — *kos* — "a kos (about two miles)".

> दादा कहते थे कि नदी तीन कोस दूर है।
>
> *dādā kahte the ki nadī tīn kos dūr hai.*
>
> Grandfather used to say that the river is three kos away.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**कोस** (*kos*) is a tadbhava of Sanskrit *krośa*, "a shout", and then "the distance a shout carries" (CDIAL 3611), which lists Hindi *kos*, about two miles.
