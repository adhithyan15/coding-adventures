## You'll want to know: うらむ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C302-YUUREI302-03]; assesses=[] -->

**うらむ** — *uramu* — "to bear a grudge against".

> **ゆうれいは その おとこを うらんでいました。** — *yūrei wa sono otoko o urande imashita* — "The ghost held a grudge against the man."

Polite **うらみます**, *uramimasu*; a godan verb. Past (polite): **おんなが おとこを うらみました。**, *onna ga otoko o uramimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**うらむ** (*uramu*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict).
