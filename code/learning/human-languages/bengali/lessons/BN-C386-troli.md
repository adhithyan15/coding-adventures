## You'll want to know: ট্রলি
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C386-KULI386-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ট্রলি** — *ṭrôli* — "a trolley".

> বিমানবন্দরে আমরা একটা ট্রলি নিলাম।
>
> *bimānbôndôre āmrā ekṭā ṭrôli nilām.*
>
> We took a trolley at the airport.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ট্রলি** (*ṭrôli*) is borrowed from English *trolley*, the wheeled cart of airports and stations.
