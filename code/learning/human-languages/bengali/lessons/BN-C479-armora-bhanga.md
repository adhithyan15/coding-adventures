# আড়মোড়া ভাঙা (āṛmoṛā bhāngā) — to stretch on waking; to stretch one's limbs

## You'll want to know: আড়মোড়া ভাঙা
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C479-GHUM479-05]; assesses=[] -->

**আড়মোড়া ভাঙা** — *āṛmoṛā bhāngā* — "to stretch on waking; to stretch one's limbs".

> সকালে জেগে বিড়ালটা আড়মোড়া ভাঙল।
>
> *sôkāle jege biṛālṭā āṛmoṛā bhānglô.*
>
> Waking in the morning, the cat had a long stretch.

Past (he/she …): তিনি জেগে আড়মোড়া ভাঙলেন, *tini jege āṛmoṛā bhānglen*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**আড়মোড়া ভাঙা** (*āṛmoṛā bhāngā*) is *āṛmoṛā*, "a stretch of the limbs", whose origin is not settled, with *bhāngā*, "to break" (CDIAL 9353): to break out of the stiffness of sleep.
