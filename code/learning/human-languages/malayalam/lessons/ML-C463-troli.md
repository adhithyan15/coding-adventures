# ട്രോളി (ṭrōḷi) — a luggage trolley

## You'll want to know: ട്രോളി
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C463-VIMANAM463-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ട്രോളി** — *ṭrōḷi* — "a luggage trolley".

> വിമാനത്താവളത്തിൽ ഞാൻ ബാഗുകൾ ഒരു ട്രോളിയിൽ വച്ചു.
>
> *vimānattāvaḷattil ñān bāgukaḷ oru ṭrōḷiyil vaccu.*
>
> At the airport I put the bags on a trolley.

A shopping trolley in a supermarket is a ട്രോളി too.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ട്രോളി** (*ṭrōḷi*) was borrowed from English *trolley*, a cart on wheels for bags.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for getting around.
