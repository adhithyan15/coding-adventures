Marathi, chapter 438: Showers and Drizzle.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
रिमझिम (rimjhim) — a drizzle.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to freeze, then the Marathi for to melt.

You'll want to know: रिमझिम.
रिमझिम — rimjhim — "a drizzle".
सकाळपासून रिमझिम चालू आहे. sakāḷpāsūn rimjhim cālū āhe. It has been drizzling since morning.
Feminine.
Also an adverb: रिमझिम (rimjhim) पाऊस, gentle rain.

The word, taken apart.
रिमझिम (rimjhim) is an echo word that imitates light, steady rain; its origin is not settled.

Lesson 2 of 5.
मुसळधार (musaḷdhār) — torrential.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to melt, then the Marathi for a drizzle.

You'll want to know: मुसळधार.
मुसळधार — musaḷdhār — "torrential".
काल रात्री मुसळधार पाऊस पडला. kāl rātrī musaḷdhār pāūs paḍlā. Last night it rained in torrents.
मुसळ is a pestle and धार a stream: rain as thick as pestles.

The word, taken apart.
मुसळधार (musaḷdhār) is built from मुसळ, "a pestle", inherited from Sanskrit musala (CDIAL 10223), and धार, "a stream".

Lesson 3 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a drizzle, then the Marathi for torrential.

You'll want to know: वळीव.
वळीव — vaḷīv — "a pre-monsoon storm".
उन्हाळ्याच्या शेवटी वळवाचा पाऊस येतो. unhāḷyācyā śevṭī vaḷvācā pāūs yeto. At the end of summer the pre-monsoon storms come.
Masculine.
Before an ending वळीव (vaḷīv) becomes वळवा-: वळवाचा पाऊस.

The word, taken apart.
वळीव (vaḷīv) names the sudden thunderstorm of late summer before the monsoon; its origin is not settled.

Lesson 4 of 5.
बरसणे (barasṇe) — to pour down.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for torrential, then the Marathi for a pre-monsoon storm.

You'll want to know: बरसणे.
बरसणे — barasṇe — "to pour down".
ढग गडगडले आणि पाऊस बरसला. ḍhag gaḍgaḍle āṇi pāūs baraslā. The clouds rumbled and the rain poured down.
Past (he …): तो बरसला, to baraslā.
Rain बरसतो; it sounds more poetic than पडणे.

The word, taken apart.
बरसणे (barasṇe) belongs with Sanskrit varṣati, "it rains", where CDIAL lists Marathi varasṇẽ (CDIAL 11394); the form with b- matches Hindi barasnā, and how it reached Marathi is not settled.

Lesson 5 of 5.
भिजणे (bhijṇe) — to get wet.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a pre-monsoon storm, then the Marathi for to pour down.

You'll want to know: भिजणे.
भिजणे — bhijṇe — "to get wet".
छत्री नव्हती म्हणून मी भिजलो. chatrī navhatī mhaṇūn mī bhijlo. I had no umbrella, so I got wet.
Past (he …): तो भिजला, to bhijlā.
A woman says मी भिजले; ओलाचिंब भिजणे (bhijṇe), to get soaked.

The word, taken apart.
भिजणे (bhijṇe) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi bhij̈ṇẽ, "to be wet", under bhiyajyate, "is smeared, gets wet" (CDIAL 9502).
