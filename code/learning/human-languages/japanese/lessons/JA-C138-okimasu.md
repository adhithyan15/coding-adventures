## You'll want to know: おきます
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C138-OKIMASU]; assesses=[] -->

**おきます** — *okimasu* — "I get up", or "she gets up", "we get up":
Japanese leaves out who does it when that is clear. It says what someone
does, as a habit or as a plan.

Every verb in this chapter ends in **ます**, *masu*. That ending makes the
polite present, the form you use with anyone you do not know well. A word
list gives the dictionary form instead, here **おきる**, *okiru*. To make
the polite form of a verb in *-iru* or *-eru* like this one, take off the
last **る** and add **ます**: *oki-ru* becomes *oki-masu*.

> **まいあさ おきます。** — *maiasa okimasu* — "I get up every morning."
