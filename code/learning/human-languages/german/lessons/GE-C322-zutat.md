## You'll want to know: die Zutat
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C322-ZUTAT-01]; assesses=[] -->

**die Zutat** — "the ingredient".

> Für den Kuchen braucht man nur fünf Zutaten.
>
> *For the cake you only need five ingredients.*

Plural: *die Zutaten*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Zutat* is built on *zutun* "to add", from *tun* "to do, to put", Old High German *tuon* (EtymDB): what is put in.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.
