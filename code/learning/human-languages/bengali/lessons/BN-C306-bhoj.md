## You'll want to know: ভোজ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C306-OTITHI306-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ভোজ** — *bhoj* — "a feast".

> বিয়ের রাতে একটা বড় ভোজ হল।
>
> *biyer rāte ekṭā bôṛo bhoj hôlô.*
>
> There was a big feast on the night of the wedding.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ভোজ** (*bhoj*) comes down from Sanskrit *bhojya*, 'to be eaten; a meal' (CDIAL 9631, which lists Bengali *bhoj*, 'a feast').
