## You'll want to know: ਸਿਲ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C240-KAM240-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ਸਿਲ** — *sil* — "a grinding stone".

> ਸਿਲ ਭਾਰੀ ਪੱਥਰ ਹੈ।
>
> *sil bhārī patthar hai.*
>
> The grinding stone is a heavy stone.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਸਿਲ** (*sil*) is inherited from Sanskrit *śilā*, "rock, crag"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *sil*, "stone slab used for grinding spices" (CDIAL 12459).
