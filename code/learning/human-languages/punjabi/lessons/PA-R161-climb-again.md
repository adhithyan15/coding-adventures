# An old word for climbing

Keep the previous word for swimming separate. Retrieve just one other
familiar action from its meaning; no movement is required.

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-LEX-C149-ACT149-02] -->

Hear **to climb**. [PAUSE 4s] Say the earlier Punjabi word before the
model: **ਚੜ੍ਹਨਾ** (*caṛhnā*).

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-LEX-C149-ACT149-02] -->

Hear **ਚੜ੍ਹਨਾ**. [PAUSE 4s] Give its old meaning, **to climb**.
Then hear **to climb** and say **ਚੜ੍ਹਨਾ** before the model.
This is the same word you already learned, not a new form.

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-LEX-C149-ACT149-02] -->

Hear **to climb** once more. [PAUSE 4s] Recall **ਚੜ੍ਹਨਾ**.
If it slips away, hear the model and retry once. This untimed
return is separate from scored A1 production.
