## You'll want to know: ಸಹಿಷ್ಣುತೆ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C541-VYAYAMA541-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸಹಿಷ್ಣುತೆ** — *sahiṣṇute* — "endurance; tolerance".

> ದೂರ ಓಡಲು ಸಹಿಷ್ಣುತೆ ಬೇಕು.
>
> *dūra ōḍalu sahiṣṇute bēku.*
>
> Running far takes endurance.

Also tolerance of other views.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸಹಿಷ್ಣುತೆ** (*sahiṣṇute*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *sahiṣṇutā*, from *sahiṣṇu*, "bearing, enduring" (Monier-Williams).
