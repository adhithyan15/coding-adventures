6 lessons. You can do the first 2 of them in the car; after that you will want to have stopped.


Lesson 1 of 6.
回电 (huídiàn) — to call back; to return a call.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for a reply to a letter, then the Mandarin for to pass on a message.

You'll want to know: 回电.
回电 — huídiàn — "to call back; to return a call".
我在开会一会儿给你回电 wǒ zài kāihuì yíhuìr gěi nǐ huídiàn — I'm in a meeting; I'll call you back in a moment.

The word, taken apart.
回电 (huídiàn) is built from 回 'to turn back' and 电 'electricity', both inherited from Middle Chinese, hwoj and denH (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'lightning'.

The new character, in pieces: 信 (xìn) — nine strokes: the person piece 亻 on the left; on the right a dot, three bars and 口. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

Script — the person piece, then a dot, three bars and 口.
信 (xìn).
Two pieces, left before right. Count the pieces, not the strokes.
The left piece, 亻, two strokes. A stroke falling to the left, then a vertical down to the foot, as in 你.
The right piece, seven strokes, top to bottom. A dot. Three bars, the top one the longest. Then 口 at the foot: a short vertical, a bar that runs right and turns down, and the bar that closes it.
2 + 7 = nine.

[once you have stopped driving — this part needs your eyes or your hands; if you are driving, skip ahead to the next part]
Guided Practice.
[once you have stopped driving — write: 信 (xìn), all nine, with the model in front of you]
[once you have stopped driving — cover: write it again from memory, and count the pieces, not the strokes]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
How many strokes in 信 (xìn)? (Nine.) What is the left piece? (亻, the person piece.) What closes the right piece at the foot? (口.)
