## You'll want to know: أخرج
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C545-SHIR545-05]; assesses=[] -->

**أخرج** — *akhraja* — "to direct films; to bring out".

> أخرج صديقي فيلما قصيرا.
>
> *akhraja ṣadīqī fīlman qaṣīran.*
>
> My friend directed a short film.

Present: يخرج, *yukhrij*.

Its first sense is to take out; the director, مخرج, is spelled like makhraj, an exit.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**أخرج** (*akhraja*) is built on the native Arabic root *kh-r-j*, "to go out" (Lane). Kharaja, to go out, which you know, is from the same root; Form IV is to bring out.
