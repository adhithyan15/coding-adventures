## You'll want to know: વલોવવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C521-VALONU521-03]; assesses=[] -->

**વલોવવું** — *valovvũ* — "to churn".

> સવારે દાદીએ દહીં વલોવીને માખણ કાઢ્યું.
>
> *savāre dādīe dahī̃ valovīne mākhaṇ kāḍhyũ.*
>
> In the morning my grandmother churned the curd and took out the butter.

Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે દહીં વલોવ્યું, *teṇe dahī̃ valovyũ*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**વલોવવું** (*valovvũ*) is filed by CDIAL under Sanskrit *vilōbhayati*, "perplexes, agitates", and CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb, "to churn" (CDIAL 11913a).
