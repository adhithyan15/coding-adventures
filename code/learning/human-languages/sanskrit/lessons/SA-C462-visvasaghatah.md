## You'll want to know: विश्वासघातः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C462-SATHA462-05]; assesses=[] -->

**विश्वासघातः** — *viśvāsaghātaḥ* — "a betrayal of trust".

> मित्रस्य विश्वासघातः महत् दुःखं ददाति।
>
> *mitrasya viśvāsaghātaḥ mahat duḥkhaṃ dadāti.*
>
> A friend's betrayal brings great sorrow.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**विश्वासघातः** (*viśvāsaghātaḥ*) is built from **विश्वासः**, "trust", and *ghāta*, "a blow, a killing", from the root √han; MW gives "violation of trust, treachery" (MW).
