# Practice — going, approaching events, and ownership

## Grammar Lens: the complete small chapter frame
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-IR, ES-GRAMMAR-IR-VERB-PRESENT-SINGULAR, ES-LEX-A, ES-GRAMMAR-NEAR-FUTURE-IR-A-INFINITIVE, ES-LEX-POSSESSIVES-MI-TU-SU, ES-GRAMMAR-POSSESSIVE-BEFORE-NOUN, ES-LEX-HABLAR, ES-LEX-COMER, ES-LEX-VIVIR, ES-LEX-CAFE] -->

- **ir**, singular: **voy · vas · va**.
- near future: **voy a hablar · vas a comer · va a vivir**.
- ownership before one singular known noun: **mi café · tu café · su café**.

Plural person forms of *ir* and possessives before plural nouns remain deferred.

## The word, taken apart — retrieve the history
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-ETYMON-IRE-VADERE, ES-SEMANTIC-MOTION-TOWARD-FUTURE, ES-ETYMON-MEUS-TUUS-SUUS] -->

- **ir** continues Latin *īre*, while **voy, vas, va** continue *vādere*.
- **a** continues Latin *ad*, “toward”; motion toward a goal became motion toward
  a coming event.
- **mi, tu, su** continue Latin *meus, tuus, suus*.
