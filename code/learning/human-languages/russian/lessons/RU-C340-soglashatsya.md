## You'll want to know: соглашаться
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C340-SOGLASIYE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**соглашаться** (*soglashátsya*) — "to agree".

> Я не всегда соглашаюсь с отцом.
>
> *Ya ne vsegdá soglasháyus s ottsóm.*
>
> I do not always agree with my father.

Imperfective; perfective *согласиться*. *Соглашаться с* + instrumental (with a person or a view), *на* + accusative (to accept an offer).

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**соглашаться** is built on *глас* "voice", the Church Slavonic form of native *голос*; both come from Proto-Slavic *\*golsŭ* "voice" (EtymDB). The *-ла-* is the Church Slavonic shape where Russian has *-оло-*: to agree is to speak with one voice.
