## You'll want to know: условие
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C370-USLOVIYE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**условие** (*uslóviye*) — "a condition".

> У меня одно условие: мы вернёмся до десяти.
>
> *U menyá odnó uslóviye: my vernyómsya do desyatí.*
>
> I have one condition: we come back before ten.

Neuter. The plural *условия* also means terms or circumstances: *условия работы*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**условие** is built on the old verb *условиться* "to agree on terms", from *у-* and *слово* "word", inherited from Proto-Slavic *\*slovo* (EtymDB). A condition is something settled in words.
