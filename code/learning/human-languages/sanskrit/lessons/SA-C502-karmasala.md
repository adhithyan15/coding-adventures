## You'll want to know: कर्मशाला
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C502-KARMASALA502-01]; assesses=[] -->

**कर्मशाला** — *karmaśālā* — "a workshop".

> मम पिता कर्मशालायां कार्यं करोति।
>
> *mama pitā karmaśālāyāṃ kāryaṃ karoti.*
>
> My father works in a workshop.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**कर्मशाला** (*karmaśālā*) is built from *karman*, "work", and *śālā*, "a hall, a room"; MW records *karmaśālā* as the room where the day's work is done, a workshop (MW).
