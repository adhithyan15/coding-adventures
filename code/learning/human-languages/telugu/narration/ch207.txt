Telugu, chapter 207: To cool, To tap, To pluck, To till, To grow.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
చల్లార్చు (callārcu) — to cool (something) down.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to tread on, then the Telugu for to hit.

You'll want to know: చల్లార్చు.
చల్లార్చు — callārcu — "to cool (something) down".

The word, taken apart.
చల్లార్చు (callārcu) is native Dravidian: calla, "cool, cold", one word with Kannada caḷi, "cold" (DEDR 2408), and ārcu, "to cool, extinguish", one word with Tamil āṟṟu, "to cool" (DEDR 404).

The word, taken apart.
తట్టు (taṭṭu) is native Dravidian, one word with Tamil taṭṭu and Kannada taṭṭu (DEDR 3039). DEDR lists Telugu taṭṭu, "to strike, knock, pat". DEDR compares Indo-Aryan words for striking, such as Sanskrit tāḍayati, "strikes" (CDIAL 5752).
