## You'll want to know: উচিত
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C269-UCHIT-01]; assesses=[BN-LEX-C45-HEALTH45-02, BN-LEX-C81-PLACE81-01, BN-LEX-C07-JAOWA-01, BN-LEX-C44-BODY44-02] -->

**উচিত** — *uchit* — "ought to; proper".

> **তোমার ডাক্তারের কাছে যাওয়া উচিত।**
>
> *tomār ḍāktārer kāchhe jāoyā uchit.* — "You ought to go to the doctor."

The one who ought to is in the **-র** form, **তোমার**, as in **তোমার মনে হয়**; the action is the dictionary form, **যাওয়া**; **উচিত** comes last. Word for word: "your going to the doctor is proper".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**উচিত** (*uchit*) is a tatsama: Sanskrit *ucita*, which Monier-Williams gives as "customary" and "proper, suitable". CDIAL has no entry for it.
