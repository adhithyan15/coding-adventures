# ചൂടാകുക (cūṭākuka) — to get hot

## You'll want to know: ചൂടാകുക
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C509-CUTU509-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ചൂടാകുക** — *cūṭākuka* — "to get hot".

> ഉച്ചയായപ്പോൾ കാറിന്റെ ഉള്ള് വല്ലാതെ ചൂടായി.
>
> *uccayāyappōḷ kāṟinṟe uḷḷŭ vallāte cūṭāyi.*
>
> By noon the inside of the car got very hot.

Past: ചൂടായി, *cūṭāyi*.

ചൂട്, heat, plus ആകുക, to become. A person can also ചൂടാകുക: get angry.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ചൂടാകുക** (*cūṭākuka*) is a Malayalam verb built from the native ചൂട്, heat (DEDR 2654), and ആകുക, to become.
