# கோட்பாடு (kōṭpāṭu) — a theory; a doctrine

## You'll want to know: கோட்பாடு
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C358-KOTPATU358-01]; assesses=[] -->

**கோட்பாடு** — *kōṭpāṭu* — "a theory; a doctrine".

> இந்தக் கோட்பாடு எனக்குப் புரியவில்லை.
>
> *intak kōṭpāṭu eṉakkup puriyavillai.*
>
> I do not understand this theory.

கோட்பாட்டை, the theory as an object: a noun in -டு doubles it to -ட்டு- before a case ending.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**கோட்பாடு** (*kōṭpāṭu*) is native: *kōḷ*, "taking, opinion, tenet", the noun of *koḷ*, "to take, to hold" (DEDR 2151), with *pāṭu*, the noun of *paṭu*: "what is held to be so".
