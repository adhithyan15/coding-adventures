Marathi, chapter 342: Why Things Happen.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
हेतू (hetū) — a motive; an intention.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for dance, then the Marathi for a stage.

You'll want to know: हेतू.
हेतू — hetū — "a motive; an intention".
त्याच्या मदतीमागचा हेतू चांगला होता. tyācyā madtīmāgcā hetū cāṅglā hotā. The motive behind his help was good.
Masculine.
हेतूने, with the intention: शिकण्याच्या हेतूने, with the aim of learning.

The word, taken apart.
हेतू (hetū) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit hetu, "impulse, motive, cause" (Monier-Williams). Marathi lengthens the final vowel.

Lesson 5 of 5.
निर्माण होणे (nirmāṇ hoṇe) — to come about; to arise.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a consequence, then the Marathi for an explanation.

You'll want to know: निर्माण होणे.
निर्माण होणे — nirmāṇ hoṇe — "to come about; to arise".
या निर्णयामुळे नवा प्रश्न निर्माण झाला. yā nirṇayāmuḷe navā praśna nirmāṇ jhālā. A new question arose because of this decision.
Past (he …): तो निर्माण झाला, to nirmāṇ jhālā.
Intransitive: what arises is the subject. निर्माण करणे is to create.

The word, taken apart.
निर्माण होणे (nirmāṇ hoṇe) joins the tatsama निर्माण, Sanskrit nirmāṇa, "measuring, making" (CDIAL 7368), to होणे, "to become": to come into being.
