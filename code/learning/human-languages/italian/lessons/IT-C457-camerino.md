## You'll want to know: il camerino
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C457-CASSA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**il camerino** — "the fitting room; the dressing room".

> Posso provarlo in camerino?
>
> *Can I try it on in the fitting room?*

In a theatre, *il camerino* is the actors' dressing room.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*camerino* is a little *camera* 'room', which is inherited from Latin *camera* 'vault, arched roof' (EtymDB).
