# Review — asking the way, and following it

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[AR-GRAMMAR-C286-YAJIBU-01, AR-GRAMMAR-C286-YANBAGHI-01, AR-GRAMMAR-C286-BI-SABAB-01, AR-GRAMMAR-C286-LAN-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "I must go now" and "you ought to rest". (*Yajibu an adhhaba
al-ān*, *yanbaghī an tastarīḥa*.) Then "I won't go, because of the rain".
(*Lan adhhaba, bi-sabab al-maṭar*.)

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[AR-LEX-C287-TARIQ287-01, AR-GRAMMAR-C287-IMPERATIVE-01, AR-GRAMMAR-C287-DIRECTIONS-01, AR-CONCEPT-C06-MIN-FADLIK-01] -->

Two lessons, and both halves of a conversation in the street. Your half:
*min faḍlik, kayfa aṣilu ilā …?*, with *ayna* and *hal … baʿīd?* beside it.
Their half: orders, the present with its *ta-* taken off, strung together with
*thumma*.

[PAUSE 2s] One exchange, whole:

> — *min faḍlik, kayfa aṣilu ilā al-funduq?* — *idhhab mustaqīman, thumma
> khudh ash-shāriʿa al-awwala ʿalā al-yasār.* — *hal huwa baʿīd?* — *lā,
> qarīb min hunā.*

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[AR-GRAMMAR-C287-IMPERATIVE-01, AR-GRAMMAR-C287-DIRECTIONS-01, AR-LEX-C204-MAKAN204-05, AR-LEX-C92-TOWN92-04, AR-LEX-C235-MAKAN235-01, AR-LEX-C103-LOC103-04, AR-LEX-C93-TOWN93-04] -->

[PAUSE 1s]
- [YOU SAY: "Excuse me, how do I get to the hotel?" — *min faḍlik, kayfa aṣilu ilā al-funduq?*]
- [YOU SAY: "Where is the museum?" — *ayna al-matḥaf?*]
- [YOU SAY: "Go straight on, then take the first street on the left"]
- [YOU SAY: "The station is at the corner" — *al-maḥaṭṭa ʿinda az-zāwiya*]
- [YOU SAY: the way from your door to the centre of your own city, in three orders]
