Urdu, chapter 514: Needle and Pattern.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
اون (ūn) — wool.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a picnic, then the Urdu for an album.

You'll want to know: اون.
اون — ūn — "wool".
سردیوں میں ہم اون کے سویٹر پہنتے ہیں۔ sardiyoṅ meṅ ham ūn ke sveṭar pahante haiṅ. In winter we wear woollen jumpers.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
اون (ūn) is inherited from Sanskrit ūrṇā, "wool" (CDIAL 2424).

Lesson 2 of 5.

You'll want to know: پرونا.
پرونا — pironā — "to thread (a needle); to string (beads)".
نانی نے سوئی میں دھاگا پرویا۔ nānī ne sūī meṅ dhāgā piroyā. Grandmother threaded the needle.
Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے سوئی میں دھاگا پرویا, us ne sūī meṅ dhāgā piroyā.

The word, taken apart.
پرونا (pironā) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin pironā, "to string pearls, to thread a needle", under Sanskrit parivayati (CDIAL 7869).

Lesson 3 of 5.
کاڑھنا (kāṛhnā) — to embroider.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for wool, then the Urdu for to thread (a needle).

You'll want to know: کاڑھنا.
کاڑھنا — kāṛhnā — "to embroider".
امی نے دوپٹے پر پھول کاڑھے۔ ammī ne dupaṭṭe par phūl kāṛhe. Mum embroidered flowers on the scarf.
Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے تکیے پر پھول کاڑھا, us ne takiye par phūl kāṛhā.

The word, taken apart.
کاڑھنا (kāṛhnā) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin kāṛhnā, "to pull out", under kaḍḍhati, "pulls, draws" (CDIAL 2660); the sense "to embroider", drawing a thread through cloth, is a later one that CDIAL does not record.
