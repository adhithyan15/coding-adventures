## You'll want to know: رمز عبور
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C416-KARBAR-02]; assesses=[] -->

**رمز عبور** (*ramz-e 'obur*) — "a password".

> رمز عبورم را فراموش کردم.
>
> *ramz-e 'oburam râ farâmush kardam.*
>
> I forgot my password.

*Ramz* "code" + *'obur* "passing". Also *gozarvâzhe*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**رمز عبور** is literally "the code for passing": *ramz* was borrowed from Arabic *ramz* "cypher, code" (EtymDB), and *'obur* is usually traced to Arabic *'ubūr* "crossing".
