## You'll want to know: ならう
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C224-SCHOOL224-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ならう** — *narau* — "to take lessons; to learn from a teacher".

> **いもうとは せんせいに うたを ならいます。** — *imōto wa sensei ni uta o naraimasu* — "My younger sister takes singing lessons from a teacher."

Polite **ならいます**, *naraimasu*; a godan verb.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ならう** (*narau*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
