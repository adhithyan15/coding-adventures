## You'll want to know: தூண்
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C522-PAZHAYAVEEDU522-04]; assesses=[] -->

**தூண்** — *tūṇ* — "a pillar".

> கோயிலின் தூண்களில் அழகான சிலைகள் இருக்கின்றன.
>
> *kōyiliṉ tūṇkaḷil aḻakāṉa cilaikaḷ irukkiṉṟaṉa.*
>
> There are beautiful statues on the temple pillars.

Plural: தூண்கள், *tūṇkaḷ*.

The plural is தூண்கள். கம்பம் (*kampam*) is a thinner post or pole.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**தூண்** (*tūṇ*) stands in DEDR's appendix of words shared with Indo-Aryan (DEDR App. 44), beside Sanskrit *sthūṇā*, "a post, a pillar" (CDIAL 13774); which way the word travelled is not settled.
