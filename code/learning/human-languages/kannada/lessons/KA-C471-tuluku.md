# ತುಳುಕು (tuḷuku) — to spill over; to brim over

## You'll want to know: ತುಳುಕು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C471-GADDALA471-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ತುಳುಕು** — *tuḷuku* — "to spill over; to brim over".

> ಹಬ್ಬದ ದಿನ ಬಸ್ಸುಗಳು ಜನರಿಂದ ತುಳುಕುತ್ತಿದ್ದವು.
>
> *habbada dina bassugaḷu janarinda tuḷukuttiddavu.*
>
> On the festival day the buses were overflowing with people.

Past (it …): ತುಳುಕಿತು, *tuḷukitu*.

Of a full pot, a crowded bus or a full heart.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ತುಳುಕು** (*tuḷuku*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 3361 lists Kannada *tuḷaku*, "to be scattered in drops, to spill, to run over", and *tuḷuku*.
