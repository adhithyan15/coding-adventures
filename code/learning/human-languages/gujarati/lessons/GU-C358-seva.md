## You'll want to know: સેવા
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C358-SEVA358-01]; assesses=[] -->

**સેવા** — *sevā* — "service to others".

> મીરા દરેક રવિવારે દવાખાનામાં સેવા આપે છે.
>
> *Mīrā darek ravivāre davākhānāmā̃ sevā āpe che.*
>
> Mira volunteers at the clinic every Sunday.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**સેવા** (*sevā*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *sēvā*, "attendance, service"; CDIAL lists an older inherited Gujarati *sev*, "attendance on", from the same word (CDIAL 13595).
