## You'll want to know: blinken
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C400-SICHT-05]; assesses=[] -->

**blinken** — "to indicate (in a car); to flash, to twinkle".

> Er blinkt rechts und fährt in die kleine Straße.
>
> *He indicates right and turns into the little street.*

*Er blinkt*, *er hat geblinkt*. Stars also *blinken*: they twinkle.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*blinken* is inherited: EtymDB traces it through Middle Low German *blinken* to Proto-Germanic *blinkaną* 'to shine, to gleam'. English *blink* is a cousin.
