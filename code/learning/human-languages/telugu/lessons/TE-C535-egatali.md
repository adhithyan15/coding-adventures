## You'll want to know: ఎగతాళి
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C535-EGATALI535-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ఎగతాళి** — *egatāḷi* — "mockery; ridicule".

> కొత్త అబ్బాయిని తరగతిలో ఎగతాళి చేశారు.
>
> *kotta abbāyini taragatilō egatāḷi cēśāru.*
>
> They made fun of the new boy in class.

ఎగతాళి చేయు, to make fun of.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ఎగతాళి** (*egatāḷi*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 767 lists Telugu *egatāḷi*, "a joke, mockery", and marks Tamil *ekattāḷi* as taken from Telugu.
