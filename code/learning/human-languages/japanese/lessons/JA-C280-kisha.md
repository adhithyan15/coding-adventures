## You'll want to know: きしゃ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C280-KIJI280-04]; assesses=[] -->

**きしゃ** — *kisha* — "a reporter; a journalist".

> **きしゃが けいさつに しつもんしました。** — *kisha ga keisatsu ni shitsumon shimashita* — "A reporter put questions to the police."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**きしゃ** (*kisha*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *ki*, "account" (Middle Chinese *kiH*) and *sha*, "person" (*tsyaeX*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
