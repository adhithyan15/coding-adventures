## You'll want to know: نبرد
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C351-SEPAH-02]; assesses=[] -->

**نبرد** (*nabard*) — "a battle, combat".

> نبرد سه روز طول کشید.
>
> *Nabard se ruz tul keshid.*
>
> The battle went on for three days.

Literary; in speech *jang*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*nabard* is a native Persian word that EtymDB glosses "combat, battle" without a parent.
