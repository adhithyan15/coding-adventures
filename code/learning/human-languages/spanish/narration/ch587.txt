Spanish, chapter 587: Está claro.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
comprobar — to check, to verify.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for the pretext, then the Spanish for to show.

You'll want to know: comprobar.
comprobar — "to check, to verify".
Comprueba el precio antes de pagar. Check the price before paying.
Stem change o becomes ue: compruebo.

The word, taken apart.
comprobar is inherited from Latin comprobare 'to confirm, to approve', built on probare 'to test' (EtymDB).
