Sanskrit, chapter 364: Ask an Expert.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
विशेषज्ञः (viśeṣajñaḥ) — an expert.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for studies, then the Sanskrit for practises.

You'll want to know: विशेषज्ञः.
विशेषज्ञः — viśeṣajñaḥ — "an expert".
रोगस्य विषये विशेषज्ञं पृच्छतु। rogasya viṣaye viśeṣajñaṃ pṛcchatu. Ask an expert about the illness.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
विशेषज्ञः (viśeṣajñaḥ) is built from viśeṣa, "a distinction", and jña, "knowing"; MW gives viśeṣa-jña "knowing distinctions, judicious", and "an expert" is its modern sense (MW).

विमर्शं विना निर्णयं न करोतु। vimarśaṃ vinā nirṇayaṃ na karotu. Make no decision without deliberation.

Lesson 4 of 5.
निबन्धः (nibandhaḥ) — an essay; a composition.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for deliberation, then the Sanskrit for a commentary.

You'll want to know: निबन्धः.
निबन्धः — nibandhaḥ — "an essay; a composition".
छात्रः धर्मस्य विषये निबन्धं लिखति। chātraḥ dharmasya viṣaye nibandhaṃ likhati. The student writes an essay about dharma.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
निबन्धः (nibandhaḥ) is built on ni, "down", and the root √bandh, "to bind"; MW gives ni-bandha "binding" and "any literary composition or work", and "an essay" is the sense modern Sanskrit gives it (MW).

Lesson 5 of 5.
निरूपयति (nirūpayati) — investigates; determines.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a commentary, then the Sanskrit for an essay.

You'll want to know: निरूपयति.
निरूपयति — nirūpayati — "investigates; determines".
विशेषज्ञः रोगस्य कारणं निरूपयति। viśeṣajñaḥ rogasya kāraṇaṃ nirūpayati. The expert determines the cause of the illness.
Past (he …): सः कारणं निरूपितवान्, saḥ kāraṇaṃ nirūpitavān.

The word, taken apart.
निरूपयति (nirūpayati) is built on ni and rūpa, "form", as a verb; MW gives ni-rūpita "observed, considered, weighed, ascertained, determined" (MW).
