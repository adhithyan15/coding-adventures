# বীর (bīr) — a hero; a warrior

## You'll want to know: বীর
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C317-BIR317-01]; assesses=[] -->

**বীর** — *bīr* — "a hero; a warrior".

> সেই যুদ্ধের বীরদের গল্প সবাই জানে।
>
> *sei juddher bīrder gôlpo sôbāi jāne.*
>
> Everyone knows the stories of the heroes of that war.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**বীর** (*bīr*) is Sanskrit *vīra*, 'a man; a hero, a chief' (Monier-Williams); the ী is long in writing.
