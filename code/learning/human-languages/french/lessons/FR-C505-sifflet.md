## You'll want to know: le sifflet
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C505-TERRAIN-02]; assesses=[] -->

**le sifflet** — "whistle".

> Au coup de sifflet, les joueurs sont sortis.
>
> *At the whistle, the players went off.*

*Le coup de sifflet final* — the final whistle.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*le sifflet* is built on *siffler* "to whistle", which goes back to Late Latin *sīfilāre*, a form of Latin *sībilāre* "to hiss, to whistle" (Lewis & Short).
