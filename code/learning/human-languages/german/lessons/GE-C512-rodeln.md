## You'll want to know: rodeln
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C512-WINTERSPORT-02]; assesses=[] -->

**rodeln** — "to toboggan".

> Am Sonntag sind wir im Wald gerodelt.
>
> *On Sunday we went tobogganing in the woods.*

Perfekt with *sein* or *haben*. Common in southern Germany and Austria; elsewhere also *Schlitten fahren*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*rodeln* is a word from the south of the German-speaking area; its origin is not settled, and EtymDB lists it without a source.
