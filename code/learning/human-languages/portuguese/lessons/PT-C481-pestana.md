## You'll want to know: a pestana
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C481-ROSTO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**a pestana** — "eyelash".

> O bebé tem pestanas muito compridas.
>
> *The baby has very long eyelashes.*

In Brazil: *o cílio*. *Queimar as pestanas* — to study late into the night.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*pestana* is already found in Old Portuguese (EtymDB), but its origin is not settled.
