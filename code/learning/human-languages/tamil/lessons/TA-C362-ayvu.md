## You'll want to know: ஆய்வு
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C362-ARAYCCI362-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ஆய்வு** — *āyvu* — "a study; an inspection".

> இந்த ஆய்வு மூன்று ஆண்டுகள் நடந்தது.
>
> *inta āyvu mūṉṟu āṇṭukaḷ naṭantatu.*
>
> This study went on for three years.

ஆய்வு செய், to study, to inspect. ஆய்வாளர், a researcher.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ஆய்வு** (*āyvu*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 363 lists Tamil *āy*, "to search, examine, select", and *āyvu*, "investigation, research".
