Tamil, chapter 398: Trends and Challenges.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
போக்கு (pōkku) — a trend; a tendency.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for personal, then the Tamil for rational thought.

You'll want to know: போக்கு.
போக்கு — pōkku — "a trend; a tendency".
கைப்பேசியில் படிக்கும் போக்கு இப்போது அதிகரித்து வருகிறது. kaippēciyil paṭikkum pōkku ippōtu atikarittu varukiṟatu. The trend of reading on phones is growing now.
Also a way of behaving: அவன் போக்கு (pōkku) சரியில்லை, his ways are not right.

The word, taken apart.
போக்கு (pōkku) is native Dravidian: DEDR 4572 lists Tamil pō, "to go", and pōkku, "going; to cause to go": the way things are going.

Lesson 2 of 5.
சவால் (cavāl) — a challenge.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for rational thought, then the Tamil for a trend.

You'll want to know: சவால்.
சவால் — cavāl — "a challenge".
தண்ணீர் இல்லாவிட்டால் விவசாயம் ஒரு பெரிய சவால். taṇṇīr illāviṭṭāl vivacāyam oru periya cavāl. Without water, farming is a big challenge.
சவால் (cavāl) விடு, to throw down a challenge.

The word, taken apart.
சவால் (cavāl) is usually traced to Arabic suʾāl, "a question, a demand", which reached Tamil through Hindustani, where it also means a challenge.

Lesson 3 of 5.
சாதனை (cātaṉai) — an achievement; a record.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for a trend, then the Tamil for a challenge.

You'll want to know: சாதனை.
சாதனை — cātaṉai — "an achievement; a record".
இந்த முறை வென்றால் அவள் புதிய சாதனை படைப்பாள். inta muṟai veṉṟāl avaḷ putiya cātaṉai paṭaippāḷ. If she wins this time, she will set a new record.
சாதனை (cātaṉai) படை, to set a record; சாதனையாளர், an achiever.

The word, taken apart.
சாதனை (cātaṉai) is borrowed from Sanskrit sādhana, "furthering, accomplishing, completion" (CDIAL 13334), with the Tamil ending -ஐ.

Lesson 4 of 5.
நிலைத்தன்மை (nilaittaṉmai) — stability.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for a challenge, then the Tamil for an achievement.

You'll want to know: நிலைத்தன்மை.
நிலைத்தன்மை — nilaittaṉmai — "stability".
அரசியலில் நிலைத்தன்மை இருந்தால் நாடு வளரும். araciyalil nilaittaṉmai iruntāl nāṭu vaḷarum. If there is political stability, the country will grow.
நிலை, a firm standing + தன்மை, nature.

The word, taken apart.
நிலைத்தன்மை (nilaittaṉmai) is native: nilai, "a standing, firmness" (DEDR 3675), and taṉmai, "nature, quality" (DEDR 3196): the quality of standing firm.

Lesson 5 of 5.
விவகாரம் (vivakāram) — a matter; an affair.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for an achievement, then the Tamil for stability.

You'll want to know: விவகாரம்.
விவகாரம் — vivakāram — "a matter; an affair".
இந்த விவகாரம் நீதிமன்றத்துக்குப் போனால் நீண்ட காலம் ஆகும். inta vivakāram nītimaṉṟattukkup pōṉāl nīṇṭa kālam ākum. If this matter goes to court, it will take a long time.
Often a disputed matter; in speech also a fuss.

The word, taken apart.
விவகாரம் (vivakāram) is borrowed from Sanskrit vyavahāra, "behaviour, custom, business" (CDIAL 12174), whose Prakrit form also means "lawsuit". Tamil writes vya as vi and h as க.
