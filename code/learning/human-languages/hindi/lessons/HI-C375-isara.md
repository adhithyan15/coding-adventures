## You'll want to know: इशारा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C375-ASHA375-04]; assesses=[] -->

**इशारा** — *iśārā* — "a hint, a signal".

> अगर मैं इशारा करूँगा तो अरुण दरवाज़ा खोलेगा।
>
> *agar maiṁ iśārā karūṅgā to Aruṇ darvāzā kholegā.*
>
> If I give a signal, Arun will open the door.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**इशारा** (*iśārā*) is usually traced to Arabic *ishāra*, "a sign, a gesture", which reached Hindi through Persian.
