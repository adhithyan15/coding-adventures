# ೬ — the digit for āru

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[KA-SCRIPT-RECOG-144] -->

[PAUSE 1s] Before the new shape: **೫** — which number is it?

[PAUSE 2s] One digit this time, and you already know its name.

## Script you'll notice: ೬
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-SCRIPT-RECOG-145]; assesses=[] -->

**೬** — **6**, the digit for **ಆರು** (*āru*), "six".

A digit carries no sound of its own — it stands for a number, and you read
it aloud with the word you learned when you learned to count.

## Writing: ೬ — copy what you see
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[KA-SCRIPT-RECOG-145] -->

Put your pen on ೬ and follow its line. Copy the shape in front of you —
slowly, and larger than it is printed.

> This book does not yet tell you **where to start the character or which way to
> travel**. That is taught with real variation from school to school, and it is
> not written down here until it can be written down with a source. Copying what
> you can see needs no such source.
