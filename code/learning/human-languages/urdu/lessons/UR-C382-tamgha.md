## You'll want to know: تمغہ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C382-INAM382-03]; assesses=[] -->

**تمغہ** — *tamghā* — "a medal".

> اگر اس نے دوڑ جیتی تو اسے تمغہ ملے گا۔
>
> *agar us ne dauṛ jītī to use tamghā mile gā.*
>
> If she wins the race, she will get a medal.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**تمغہ** (*tamghā*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Turkish *tamga*, "a seal, a stamp": a stamped token of honour.
