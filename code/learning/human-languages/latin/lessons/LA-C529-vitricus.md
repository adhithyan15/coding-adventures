## You'll want to know: vitricus, vitricī
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C529-NOVE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**vitricus, vitricī** — "a stepfather".

> Vitricus puerum ut fīlium suum amat.
>
> *The stepfather loves the boy like his own son.*

Masculine, second declension.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**vitricus** is a native Latin noun, and its origin is not settled; neither Lewis & Short nor EtymDB gives a source.
