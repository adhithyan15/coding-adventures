## You'll want to know: ಮಿತಿ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C438-TUKA438-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ಮಿತಿ** — *miti* — "a limit".

> ಸಾಮಾನಿನ ತೂಕಕ್ಕೆ ಒಂದು ಮಿತಿ ಇದೆ.
>
> *sāmānina tūkakke ondu miti ide.*
>
> There is a limit on the weight of the luggage.

ವೇಗದ ಮಿತಿ is a speed limit.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಮಿತಿ** (*miti*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *miti*, "measuring, measure, weight" (Monier-Williams), from the root *mā*, "to measure".
