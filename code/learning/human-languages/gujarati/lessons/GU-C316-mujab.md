## You'll want to know: મુજબ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C316-MUJAB316-03]; assesses=[] -->

**મુજબ** — *mujab* — "according to".

> છાપાના અહેવાલ મુજબ ચાર લોકો ઘાયલ થયા.
>
> *chāpānā ahevāl mujab cār loko ghāyal thayā.*
>
> According to the newspaper report, four people were hurt.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**મુજબ** (*mujab*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *mūjib*, "a cause, a ground". It follows its noun: **અહેવાલ મુજબ**, according to the report.
