## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-RECTO]; assesses=[] -->

**recto** — "straight."

It comes from Latin **rectus**, also "straight." English **rectilinear** keeps
the straight-line meaning, and every **rectangle** is built from straight
sides and right angles.

For a route, put it after the action:

- **seguir recto** — continue straight.

Do not let the exam's near-neighbour replace it:

- **recto** — straight;
- **resto** — the rest, the remainder.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

You can combine an action and a quality into the direction *seguir recto*.
