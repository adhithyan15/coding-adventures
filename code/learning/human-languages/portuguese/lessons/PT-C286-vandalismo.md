## You'll want to know: o vandalismo
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C286-DELITO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**o vandalismo** — "vandalism".

> O vandalismo na estação custou muito dinheiro.
>
> *The vandalism at the station cost a lot of money.*

*Atos de vandalismo* — acts of vandalism.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*vandalismo* is built on *vândalo* with *-ismo*, an ending that comes from Greek *-ismós* through Latin (EtymDB has *vândalo*).
