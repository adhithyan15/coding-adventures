## You'll want to know: il volontario
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C367-VOLONTARIO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**il volontario** — "the volunteer".

> I volontari portano da mangiare agli anziani.
>
> *The volunteers bring food to the elderly.*

A woman is *la volontaria*. As an adjective: voluntary.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*volontario* is a learned borrowing from Latin *voluntārius* "willing, of free will" (Lewis & Short), from *voluntās* "will", built on *velle* "to want".
