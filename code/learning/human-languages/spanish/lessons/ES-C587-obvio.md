## You'll want to know: obvio
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C587-EVIDENTE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**obvio** — "obvious".

> La respuesta es obvia.
>
> *The answer is obvious.*

*Es obvio que* + indicative.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*obvio* is a learned borrowing from Latin *obvius* 'standing in the way', built from *ob* and *via* 'road': the obvious is what you meet on the road (EtymDB).
