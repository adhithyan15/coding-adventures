## You'll want to know: 上个月
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C148-SHANGGEYUE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**上个月** — *shàng ge yuè* — "last month".

> **上个月我去了中国** *shàng ge yuè wǒ qù le Zhōngguó* — Last month I went to China.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**上个月** (*shàng ge yuè*) is built from 上 'above; before', 个 and 月. 上 is inherited from Middle Chinese *dzyangH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription); the Guangyun glosses this reading 'ruler', so 'above', and 'last' in a row of months, are assumed here, not quoted.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ZH-LEX-C148-XIAGEYUE-01, ZH-GRAMMAR-LE-COMPLETION-01] -->

上 and 下 point through time as through a list read from the top: the month above is the one before, the month below the one after.

上个月 looks back, so it takes 了 when you say what happened: 上个月, 我, 去了, 中国.
