## You'll want to know: निखरना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C389-BEHTAR389-05]; assesses=[] -->

**निखरना** — *nikharnā* — "to brighten, to come out at its best".

> अगर तुम रोज़ अभ्यास करोगे तो तुम्हारा गाना निखर जाएगा।
>
> *agar tum roz abhyās karoge to tumhārā gānā nikhar jāegā.*
>
> If you practise every day, your singing will come into its own.

Past (it …): वह रंग धूप में निखर गया, *vah raṅg dhūp meṁ nikhar gayā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**निखरना** (*nikharnā*) is inherited from *\*niḥkṣarati*, "flows out", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 7095). CDIAL glosses the Hindi verb "to become clear".
