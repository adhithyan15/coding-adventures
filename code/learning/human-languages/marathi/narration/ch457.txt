Marathi, chapter 457: Regret and Tears.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
खेद (khed) — regret.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to get flustered, then the Marathi for to be bashful.

You'll want to know: खेद.
खेद — khed — "regret".
त्याला आपल्या चुकीचा खेद वाटला. tyālā āplyā cukīcā khed vāṭlā. He regretted his mistake.
Masculine.
Formal: खेद (khed) वाटणे, to regret, with the dative and the genitive of the thing.

The word, taken apart.
खेद (khed) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit kheda, "lassitude, pain" (CDIAL 3919); in Marathi it is regret.

Lesson 2 of 6.
पश्चात्ताप (paścāttāp) — remorse.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to be bashful, then the Marathi for regret.

You'll want to know: पश्चात्ताप.
पश्चात्ताप — paścāttāp — "remorse".
रागात बोलल्याचा त्याला नंतर पश्चात्ताप झाला. rāgāt bollyācā tyālā nantar paścāttāp jhālā. Later he felt remorse for speaking in anger.
Masculine.
पश्चात्, after, + ताप, burning: the pain that comes afterwards. पश्चात्ताप (paścāttāp) होणे, with the dative.

The word, taken apart.
पश्चात्ताप (paścāttāp) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit paścāttāpa, "after-pain, repentance" (Monier-Williams), built on tāpa, "heat, pain".

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for remorse, then the Marathi for a sob.

You'll want to know: हळहळणे.
हळहळणे — haḷhaḷṇe — "to feel sorry; to lament".
जुनं झाड तोडलं म्हणून आजी हळहळली. junaṁ jhāḍ toḍlaṁ mhaṇūn ājī haḷhaḷlī. Grandmother felt sorry that the old tree was cut down.
Past (he …): तो हळहळला, to haḷhaḷlā.
Sorrow for a loss: हळहळ वाटणे, to feel a pang of regret.

The word, taken apart.
हळहळणे (haḷhaḷṇe) is a Marathi verb built on a doubled syllable; its origin is not settled.

Lesson 5 of 6.
मुसमुसणे (musmusṇe) — to sob quietly.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a sob, then the Marathi for to feel sorry.

You'll want to know: मुसमुसणे.
मुसमुसणे — musmusṇe — "to sob quietly".
खोलीत बसून मुलगी मुसमुसत होती. kholīt basūn mulgī musmusat hotī. The girl sat in the room, sobbing quietly.
Past (he …): तो मुसमुसला, to musmuslā.
Quieter than रडणे: sniffs heard through a door.

The word, taken apart.
मुसमुसणे (musmusṇe) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi musmusṇẽ, "to sob", under the expressive root muss (CDIAL 10227).
