## You'll want to know: うで
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C215-BODY215-03]; assesses=[] -->

**うで** — *ude* — "an arm".

> **ちちは うでが つよいです。** — *chichi wa ude ga tsuyoi desu* — "My father has strong arms."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**うで** (*ude*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi, and its older origin is not settled (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
