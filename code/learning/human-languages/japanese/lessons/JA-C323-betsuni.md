## You'll want to know: べつに
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C323-ENRYO323-05]; assesses=[] -->

**べつに** — *betsuni* — "not particularly".

> **べつに いそがないから、ゆっくり して ください。** — *betsuni isoganai kara, yukkuri shite kudasai* — "There is no particular hurry, so take your time."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**べつに** (*betsuni*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): one Chinese character read with its on'yomi, reading borrowed from Chinese. It is *betsu*, "separate" (Middle Chinese *bjet*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription). The native particle *ni* after it makes the adverb (JMdict).
