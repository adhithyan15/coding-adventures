# ಗಲಭೆ (galabhe) — a riot; a disturbance

## You'll want to know: ಗಲಭೆ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C339-GALABHE339-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ಗಲಭೆ** — *galabhe* — "a riot; a disturbance".

> ಸಂತೆಯಲ್ಲಿ ಸಣ್ಣ ಜಗಳ ದೊಡ್ಡ ಗಲಭೆ ಆಯಿತು.
>
> *santeyalli saṇṇa jagaḷa doḍḍa galabhe āyitu.*
>
> A small quarrel at the market became a big riot.

Plural: ಗಲಭೆಗಳು, *galabhegaḷu*.

In the news: ಗಲಭೆ ನಡೆಯಿತು, there was a riot.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಗಲಭೆ** (*galabhe*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 1310 lists Kannada *galabe*, "hubbub, clamour", and *galabili*, "disorder", beside Telugu *galaba*, "confusion, noise".
