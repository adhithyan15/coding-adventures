## You'll want to know: ちょきん
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C186-SAVE186-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ちょきん** — *chokin* — "savings; money put aside".

> **ぎんこうに ちょきんが あります。** — *ginkō ni chokin ga arimasu* — "I have savings in the bank."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ちょきん** (*chokin*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *cho*, "store up" (Middle Chinese *trjoX*) and *kin*, "gold, money" (*kim*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
