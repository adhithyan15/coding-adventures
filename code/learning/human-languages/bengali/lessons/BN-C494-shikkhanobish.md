## You'll want to know: শিক্ষানবিশ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C494-KAJKHOJA494-03]; assesses=[] -->

**শিক্ষানবিশ** — *shikkhānôbish* — "an apprentice; a trainee".

> আমি একটা কারখানায় শিক্ষানবিশ হলাম।
>
> *Āmi ekṭā kārkhānāy shikkhānôbish hôlām.*
>
> I became an apprentice at a factory.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**শিক্ষানবিশ** (*shikkhānôbish*) joins Sanskrit *śikṣā*, "learning" (Monier-Williams), to an ending probably from Persian *navīs*, "one who writes": a learner at a desk.
