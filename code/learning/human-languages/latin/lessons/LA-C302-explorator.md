## You'll want to know: explōrātor, explōrātōris
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C302-SPECULATOR-02]; assesses=[] -->

**explōrātor, explōrātōris** — "a scout".

> Explōrātōrēs nūntiāvērunt hostēs prope flūmen esse.
>
> *The scouts reported that the enemy were near the river.*

Masculine, 3rd declension. In Caesar the *explōrātōrēs* went ahead of the army.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**explōrātor** is built on *explōrō*, "to search out" (Lewis & Short), with the ending *-tor* for the one who does it.
