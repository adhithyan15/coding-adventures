# वह करेगा (vah karegā) — the future, for every person

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[HI-LEX-C88-FUT-01, HI-LEX-C88-FUT-03, HI-LEX-C88-FUT-04, HI-LEX-C88-FUT-05] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "I will do", then "he will come", "they will come" and "you will come". (*karūṅgā*, *āegā*, *āeṅge*, *āoge*.) They are four cells of one grid. This lesson fills in the rest.

## You'll want to know: the whole grid
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-GRAMMAR-C277-FUTURE-PERSON-01]; assesses=[HI-CONCEPT-C05-KARNA-01, HI-LEX-C69-GOING-05, HI-CONCEPT-C02-AAPTUM-01, HI-LEX-C86-PRON-01, HI-LEX-C86-PRON-02, HI-LEX-C56-ROAD-03] -->

> **कल मैं बाज़ार जाऊँगा।**
>
> *kal maiṁ bāzār jāūṅgā.* — "Tomorrow I will go to the market."

| who | करना | जाना |
|---|---|---|
| **मैं** | **करूँगा** / **करूँगी** | **जाऊँगा** / **जाऊँगी** |
| **तुम** | **करोगे** / **करोगी** | **जाओगे** / **जाओगी** |
| **आप** | **करेंगे** / **करेंगी** | **जाएँगे** / **जाएँगी** |
| **वह** | **करेगा** / **करेगी** | **जाएगा** / **जाएगी** |
| **हम** | **करेंगे** | **जाएँगे** |
| **वे** | **करेंगे** / **करेंगी** | **जाएँगे** / **जाएँगी** |

After the stem comes a vowel for the person: **ऊँ** for I, **ओ** for **तुम**, **ए** for he or she, **एँ** for several or for **आप**. Then **-गा**, **-गी** or **-गे**, which follows the gender as **था**, **थी**, **थे** do. A man says **करूँगा**, a woman **करूँगी**.

**लेना** and **देना** shorten the stem: **मैं लूँगा** (*maiṁ lūṅgā*), **वह लेगा** (*vah legā*), **हम लेंगे** (*ham leṅge*); **मैं दूँगा** (*maiṁ dūṅgā*), **वह देगा** (*vah degā*). **पीना** gives **वह पिएगा** (*vah piegā*).

"Will not" puts **नहीं** in front: **मैं नहीं जाऊँगा** (*maiṁ nahīṁ jāūṅgā*).

## You'll want to know: होगा, will be
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-GRAMMAR-C277-WILL-BE-01]; assesses=[HI-LEX-C87-PAST-01, HI-LEX-C87-PAST-02, HI-LEX-C184-THG184-05, HI-LEX-C185-THG185-02, HI-CONCEPT-C39-DOST-01] -->

> **कल बारिश होगी।**
>
> *kal bāriś hogī.* — "It will rain tomorrow."

**है** and **था** have a future too: **होगा** (*hogā*), **होगी** (*hogī*), **होंगे** (*hoṅge*). It follows its subject as **था**, **थी**, **थे** do. **बारिश** is feminine, so **होगी**.

- **वह घर पर होगा** (*vah ghar par hogā*) — he will be at home
- **कल ठंड होगी** (*kal ṭhaṇḍ hogī*) — it will be cold tomorrow
- **मेरे दोस्त वहाँ होंगे** (*mere dost vahā̃ hoṅge*) — my friends will be there
