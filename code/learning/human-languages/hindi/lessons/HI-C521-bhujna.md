## You'll want to know: भूँजना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C521-TADKA521-05]; assesses=[] -->

**भूँजना** — *bhū̃jnā* — "to roast; to parch (dry, in a pan)".

> दादी कड़ाही में मूँगफली भूँज रही हैं।
>
> *Dādī kaṛāhī meṁ mū̃gphalī bhū̃j rahī haiṁ.*
>
> Grandmother is roasting peanuts in the wok.

Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने मूँगफली भूँजी, *usne mū̃gphalī bhū̃jī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**भूँजना** (*bhū̃jnā*) is a tadbhava, inherited from *\*bhṛñjati*, "parches", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 9586). CDIAL gives the Hindi verb as "to parch, fry".
