Marathi, chapter 280: What I Think, and So.
3 lessons. All 3 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 3.
मला वाटतं की … (malā vāṭtaṁ kī …) — what I think.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say "I feel fine", with the verb that puts you in the dative. (malā baraṁ vāṭtaṁ.) Then "Meera says that Marathi is easy". (mīrā mhaṇte kī marāṭhī sopaṁ āhe.)
[pause 1 second]
Put the two together and you have an opinion.

You'll want to know: मला वाटतं की …
मला वाटतं की उद्या पाऊस पडेल. malā vāṭtaṁ kī udyā pāūs paḍel. — "I think it will rain tomorrow."
Word for word: "to me it seems that tomorrow rain will fall". The thinker stands in the dative, as with आवडणे; की opens the opinion, and the opinion is a whole sentence. To ask: तुम्हाला काय वाटतं? (tumhālā kāy vāṭtaṁ?), "What do you think?" In the past: मला वाटलं की … (malā vāṭlaṁ kī …), "I thought that …". "I don't think so": मला असं वाटत नाही (malā asaṁ vāṭat nāhī), with असं (asaṁ, "so, like this").

You'll want to know: माझ्या मते, 'in my opinion'.
माझ्या मते हा चित्रपट चांगला आहे. mājhyā mate hā citrapaṭ cāṅglā āhe. — "In my opinion this film is good."
मते is मत, "an opinion", the word inside मत देणे, "to vote", with an ending; माझ्या is माझा bent to fit it. It sits at the front of the sentence, and it sounds firmer than मला वाटतं.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: "I think it will rain tomorrow" — malā vāṭtaṁ kī udyā pāūs paḍel]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: "What do you think?" — tumhālā kāy vāṭtaṁ?]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: "In my opinion this film is good" — mājhyā mate hā citrapaṭ cāṅglā āhe]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[question — say your answer, then pause 24 seconds]
Say that you think it will rain tomorrow, then ask what your listener thinks.

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
"I think that"? (मला वाटतं की.) "I thought that"? (मला वाटलं की.) "In my opinion"? (माझ्या मते.) "What do you think?" (तुम्हाला काय वाटतं?)


Lesson 2 of 3.
म्हणून (mhaṇūn) — so, and because of that.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say "because", the word that answers का. (kāraṇ.) Then "it was raining". (pāūs paḍat hotā.)

You'll want to know: म्हणून, 'so'.
पाऊस पडत होता, म्हणून मी घरी राहिलो. pāūs paḍat hotā, mhaṇūn mī gharī rāhilo. — "It was raining, so I stayed at home."
कारण gives the reason after the event; म्हणून (mhaṇūn) gives the result after the reason. Same two facts, opposite order: मी घरी राहिलो, कारण पाऊस पडत होता (mī gharī rāhilo, kāraṇ pāūs paḍat hotā).
म्हणून (mhaṇūn) is built like करून: the -ऊन form of म्हणणे, "to say".

You'll want to know: त्यामुळे, 'because of that'.
काल खूप पाऊस होता, त्यामुळे आम्ही बाहेर गेलो नाही. kāl khūp pāūs hotā, tyāmuḷe āmhī bāher gelo nāhī. — "Yesterday there was a lot of rain, so we didn't go out."
त्यामुळे (tyāmuḷe) is त्या, "that", and -मुळे, "because of". It leans on the cause: "because of that". Where म्हणून (mhaṇūn) often marks a choice someone made, त्यामुळे marks what came of something.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: "It was raining, so I stayed at home" — pāūs paḍat hotā, mhaṇūn mī gharī rāhilo]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: "I stayed at home, because it was raining" — mī gharī rāhilo, kāraṇ pāūs paḍat hotā]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: "Yesterday there was a lot of rain, so we didn't go out" — kāl khūp pāūs hotā, tyāmuḷe āmhī bāher gelo nāhī]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[question — say your answer, then pause 30 seconds]
Say that it was raining, so you stayed at home; then say it the other way round with kāraṇ.

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
Which word comes after the reason? (म्हणून (mhaṇūn).) Which comes before it? (कारण.) "Because of that"? (त्यामुळे.)


Lesson 3 of 3.
Review — opinion and result.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say "after school I had tea; after that I went home and slept". (śāḷenantar mī cahā ghetlā. tyānantar mī gharī jāūn jhoplo.) Then name the season Arun likes, with ऋतू.

Guided Practice.
[pause 2 seconds after each]
[your turn — say: I think Meera will come tomorrow.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: In my opinion this play is good.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: It was raining, so I didn't go to school.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: Yesterday there was a lot of rain, so the river was full.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: which season you like, and why, with ऋतू and कारण]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
"I think that"? (मला वाटतं की.) "So"? (म्हणून (mhaṇūn).) "Because of that"? (त्यामुळे.) Which letter starts ऋतू? (ऋ.)
