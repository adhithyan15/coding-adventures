## You'll want to know: तिकीटघर
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C398-TIKITGHAR398-01]; assesses=[] -->

**तिकीटघर** — *tikīṭghar* — "a ticket office".

> तिकीटघरासमोर मोठी रांग होती.
>
> *tikīṭgharāsamor moṭhī rāṅg hotī.*
>
> There was a long queue in front of the ticket office.

Neuter.

घर at the end of a compound is a room or building for one job: तिकीटघर, a ticket house.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**तिकीटघर** (*tikīṭghar*) is a Marathi compound of **तिकीट**, a loan from English *ticket*, and **घर**, "house", inherited from Sanskrit *ghara* (CDIAL 4428).
