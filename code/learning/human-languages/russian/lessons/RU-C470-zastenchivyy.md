## You'll want to know: застенчивый
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C470-SHAME-05]; assesses=[] -->

**застенчивый** (*zasténchivyy*) — "shy".

> Застенчивый студент редко задаёт вопросы.
>
> *Zasténchivyy studént rédko zadayót voprósy.*
>
> The shy student rarely asks questions.

Shy by nature. Short forms: застенчив, застенчива.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*застенчивый* is usually traced to *за стеной* 'behind a wall', a person who keeps out of sight; EtymDB lists the word without a source, so the link is not settled.
