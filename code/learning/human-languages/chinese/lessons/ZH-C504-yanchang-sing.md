## You'll want to know: yǎnchàng
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C504-SONGS504-05]; assesses=[] -->

*yǎnchàng* — "to perform a song (on stage)".

> *nàge gēshǒu lái wǒmen xuéxiào yǎnchàng* — That singer is coming to sing at our school.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*yǎnchàng* is built from *yǎn* 'to perform' and *chàng* 'to sing', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *yenX* and *tsyhangH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to spread wide' and the second 'to strike up a song'; the sense 'to perform' is not given for the first there.
