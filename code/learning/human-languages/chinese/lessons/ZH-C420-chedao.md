## You'll want to know: 车道
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C420-PARK420-02]; assesses=[] -->

**车道** — *chēdào* — "traffic lane".

> *wǎng yòubian de chēdào kāi* — Move over into the right-hand lane.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**车道** (*chēdào*) is built from 车 'vehicle' and 道 'a road', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *tsyhae* and *dawX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'a road' and 'reason'.
