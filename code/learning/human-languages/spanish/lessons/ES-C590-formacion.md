## You'll want to know: la formación
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C590-VACANTE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**la formación** — "the training, the education".

> Buscamos a alguien con formación técnica.
>
> *We are looking for someone with technical training.*

*Formación profesional* (FP) is vocational training in Spain.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*formación* is a learned borrowing from Latin *formatio* 'a shaping', built on *formare* and *forma* 'shape' (EtymDB).
