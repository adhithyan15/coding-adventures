## You'll want to know: خط سير
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C437-BUSALA437-05]; assesses=[] -->

**خط سير** — *khaṭṭ sayr* — "an itinerary".

> كتب المرشد خط سير الرحلة.
>
> *kataba al-murshid khaṭṭ sayr ar-riḥla.*
>
> The guide wrote out the itinerary of the trip.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**خط سير** (*khaṭṭ sayr*) is built from two native Arabic words: *khaṭṭ*, "a line", on the root *kh-ṭ-ṭ* (Lane), and *sayr*, "going", on the root *s-y-r* (Lane): the line a journey follows.
