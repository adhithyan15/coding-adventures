## You'll want to know: دفاع
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C334-MUHAKAMA334-03]; assesses=[] -->

**دفاع** — *difāʿ* — "the defence".

> تكلم محامي الدفاع طويلا.
>
> *takallama muḥāmī ad-difāʿ ṭawīlan.*
>
> The defence lawyer spoke for a long time.

In sport and in war too: خط الدفاع, the line of defence.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**دفاع** (*difāʿ*) is built on the native Arabic root *d-f-ʿ* (Lane), "to push". *Difāʿ* is the verbal noun of Form III *dāfaʿa*, "to push back".
