Arabic, chapter 138: Eagle, Frog, Cheek, Forehead, Eyebrow.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
نسر (nasr) — an eagle.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for an owl, then the Arabic for a crow.

You'll want to know: نسر.
نسر — nasr — "an eagle".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: nasr]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: nasr, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say nasr]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Arabic for an owl, then the Arabic for a crow, then say nasr again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does نسر (nasr) mean? ("An eagle".) Say it once more.
