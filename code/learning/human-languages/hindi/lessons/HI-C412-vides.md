## You'll want to know: विदेश
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C412-VIDESH412-01]; assesses=[] -->

**विदेश** — *videś* — "abroad; a foreign country".

> मेरा भाई विदेश में काम करता है।
>
> *merā bhāī videś meṁ kām kartā hai.*
>
> My brother works abroad.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**विदेश** (*videś*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *videśa*, "another country, abroad" (Monier-Williams), from *vi-*, "apart", and *deśa*, "country"; विदेशी, foreign, is its adjective.
