## You'll want to know: 过去
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C150-GUOQU-01]; assesses=[] -->

**过去** — *guòqù* — "in the past; to go over (there)".

> **过去 我家在大学里边** *guòqù, wǒ jiā zài dàxué lǐbian* — In the past, our home was on the university grounds.

> **我过去** *wǒ guòqù* — I'll go over there.

The next lesson puts the first character in your hand. It is the guò of *guònián* and *nánguò*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**过去** (*guòqù*) is built from 过 'to pass' and 去 'to go'. Both are inherited from Middle Chinese, *kwaH* and *khjoH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'to go beyond; to exceed' and 'to leave'. 'The past', time that has gone by, is the pair's own sense.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ZH-LEX-C126-OFTEN126-03, ZH-LEX-C45-TODAY45-04] -->

过去 and 以前 both look back. 以前 is before some point; 过去 is the past at large, set against now, *xiànzài*.

As a verb, 过去 is going over there, away from the speaker.
