# अस्ति — cover the model, keep the join

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[SA-LEX-ASTI] -->

Look at **अस्ति** for five seconds. Say *asti*, “is,” and point to the conjunct
**स्त**. Then cover the whole word and every romanized answer.

## Writing — delayed conjunct copy
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[SA-LEX-ASTI, SA-SCRIPT-RECOG-03, SA-SCRIPT-RECOG-05, SA-SCRIPT-RECOG-107, SA-SCRIPT-RECOG-108, SA-SCRIPT-RECOG-109] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: delayed-copy -->

Wait ten seconds. With no visible answer, write **अस्ति** from memory. Do not
split **स** and **त** with a vowel: preserve the joined **स्त** in the middle.

Only after the word is complete, uncover **अस्ति**. Compare the independent
vowel, the conjunct, and the short-*i* sign. Repair only the first difference,
then read the word and give its meaning.

## Wrap-up recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[SA-LEX-ASTI] -->

Why does **स** not say *sa* inside this word? (**Its vowel is removed so it can
join त in स्त.**)
