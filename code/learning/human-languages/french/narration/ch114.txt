French, chapter 114: Toothpaste, Shampoo, Comb, Bin, Broom.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
le dentifrice — toothpaste.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for soap, then the French for a toothbrush.

You'll want to know: le dentifrice.
le dentifrice — "toothpaste".

The word, taken apart.
dentifrice from Latin dentifricium, "tooth powder", from dens, "tooth", and fricare, "to rub" — a learned borrowing. (EtymDB 2.1).
