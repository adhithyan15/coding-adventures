Marathi, chapter 557: Means and Notice.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
कुवत (kuvat) — capacity; means.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for dire, then the Marathi for confusion.

You'll want to know: कुवत.
कुवत — kuvat — "capacity; means".
घर घेण्याची आमची अजून कुवत नाही. ghar gheṇyācī āmcī ajūn kuvat nāhī. We do not yet have the means to buy a house.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
कुवत (kuvat) is a loan through Persian usually traced to Arabic quwwa, "strength".

Lesson 2 of 7.
आवाका (āvākā) — scope; reach.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for confusion, then the Marathi for capacity.

You'll want to know: आवाका.
आवाका — āvākā — "scope; reach".
हे काम माझ्या आवाक्याबाहेर आहे. he kām mājhyā āvākyābāher āhe. This job is beyond my reach.
Masculine.
The oblique is आवाक्या-: आवाक्याबाहेर, beyond reach.

The word, taken apart.
आवाका (āvākā) is a Marathi word for the reach of one's ability; its origin is not settled.

Lesson 4 of 7.
पूर्वसूचना (pūrvasūcanā) — advance notice.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for scope, then the Marathi for thrift.

You'll want to know: पूर्वसूचना.
पूर्वसूचना — pūrvasūcanā — "advance notice".
कोणतीही पूर्वसूचना न देता वीज गेली. koṇtīhī pūrvasūcanā na detā vīj gelī. The power went off without any advance notice.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
पूर्वसूचना (pūrvasūcanā) is a tatsama compound of Sanskrit pūrva, "before", and sūcanā, "indication" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 5 of 7.
अनुमती (anumatī) — formal permission.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for thrift, then the Marathi for advance notice.

You'll want to know: अनुमती.
अनुमती — anumatī — "formal permission".
शिक्षकांची अनुमती घेऊन मी वर्गाबाहेर गेलो. śikṣakāñcī anumatī gheūn mī vargābāher gelo. With the teacher's permission I went out of the class.
Feminine.
अनुमती (anumatī) is the formal word beside परवानगी.

The word, taken apart.
अनुमती (anumatī) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit anumati, "assent, permission" (Monier-Williams).
