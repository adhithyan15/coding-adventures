## You'll want to know: ਹਾਲ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C319-HEALTH319-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ਹਾਲ** — *hāl* — "how things stand".

> ਤੁਹਾਡੇ ਪਰਿਵਾਰ ਦਾ ਕੀ ਹਾਲ ਹੈ?
>
> *tuhāḍe parivār dā kī hāl hai?*
>
> How is your family doing?

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਹਾਲ** (*hāl*) is a loan, usually traced through Persian to Arabic *ḥāl*, "the present state".
