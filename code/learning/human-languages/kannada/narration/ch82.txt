Kannada, chapter 82: Not Feeling Well.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಆಯಾಸ (āyāsa) — tiredness.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for fever, then the Kannada for pain.

You'll want to know: ಆಯಾಸ.
ಆಯಾಸ (āyāsa) — "tiredness", the heavy feeling at the end of a long day.
ನನಗೆ ಆಯಾಸ ಆಗಿದೆ (nanage āyāsa āgide) — "I am tired", literally "to me tiredness has happened". The person sits in ನನಗೆ, "to me", the same way ನನಗೆ ಹಸಿವು says "I am hungry".

What you've built.
A first word for how the body feels at the end of the day.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: āyāsa]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: āyāsa, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say nanage āyāsa āgide]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Kannada for fever, then the Kannada for pain, then say āyāsa again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does ಆಯಾಸ (āyāsa) mean? ("Tiredness".) Say it once more.


Lesson 2 of 5.

You'll want to know: ಶೀತ.
ಶೀತ (śīta) — "a cold", the kind with a runny nose. The word also means "cold, chill".
ನನಗೆ ಶೀತ ಆಗಿದೆ (nanage śīta āgide) — "I have a cold".

What you've built.
An illness, said the same way as tiredness.

Lesson 3 of 5.
ಕೆಮ್ಮು (kemmu) — a cough.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for tiredness, then the Kannada for a cold.

You'll want to know: ಕೆಮ್ಮು.
ಕೆಮ್ಮು (kemmu) — "a cough", and also the verb "to cough".
ಕೆಮ್ಮು ಇದೆ (kemmu ide) — "there is a cough", the plain way to tell a doctor.

What you've built.
A second complaint for the doctor.

Lesson 4 of 5.
ತಲೆನೋವು (talenōvu) — a headache.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a cold, then the Kannada for a cough.

You'll want to know: ತಲೆನೋವು.
ತಲೆನೋವು (talenōvu) — "a headache". It is two words you already know, joined: ತಲೆ (tale), "head", and ನೋವು (nōvu), "pain".
ನನಗೆ ತಲೆನೋವು (nanage talenōvu) — "I have a headache".

What you've built.
Two known words make a new one: head + pain.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ವಿಶ್ರಾಂತಿ (viśrānti) — rest.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a cough, then the Kannada for a headache.

You'll want to know: ವಿಶ್ರಾಂತಿ.
ವಿಶ್ರಾಂತಿ (viśrānti) — "rest".
ವಿಶ್ರಾಂತಿ ತೆಗೆದುಕೊಳ್ಳಿ (viśrānti tegedukoḷḷi) — "please take rest", what you say to a friend who is unwell. The verb is ತೆಗೆದುಕೊ, "take", which you already know.

What you've built.
The answer to tiredness, a cold and a headache.
