## You'll want to know: retirar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C628-BANCO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**retirar** — "to withdraw, to take out; to remove".

> Retiro dinero del cajero.
>
> *I take money out of the cash machine.*

In speech people often say *sacar dinero*. *Retirarse* — to retire, to withdraw.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*retirar* is built on *tirar* 'to pull, to throw' with *re-*; *tirar* is usually traced to a Germanic verb meaning 'to tear' (EtymDB), but its origin is not settled.
