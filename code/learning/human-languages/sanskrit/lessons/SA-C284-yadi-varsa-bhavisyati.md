# यदि वर्षा भविष्यति (yadi varṣā bhaviṣyati) — if it happens, it will follow

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[SA-GRAMMAR-C284-IF-01, SA-GRAMMAR-C275-FUTURE-01, SA-GRAMMAR-C275-FUTURE-STEM-02] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "if it rains, I stay at home". (*yadi varṣā bhavati, tarhi ahaṃ gṛhe tiṣṭhāmi*.) That is what happens every time. Now say what will happen tomorrow.

## You'll want to know: the future in both halves
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-GRAMMAR-C284-CONDITIONAL-REAL-01]; assesses=[SA-LEX-C76-WEATHER-01, SA-LEX-C139-FUT139-04, SA-LEX-C86-WEEKEND-04, SA-LEX-C37-COURTESY-03] -->

For one real thing ahead, both halves take the future, as Lanman's vocabulary shows for **यदि**:

> **यदि वर्षा भविष्यति,**
>
> *yadi varṣā bhaviṣyati,*
>
> **तर्हि अहं गृहे स्थास्यामि।**
>
> *tarhi ahaṃ gṛhe sthāsyāmi.* — "If it rains, I will stay at home."

> **यदि समयः भविष्यति,**
>
> *yadi samayaḥ bhaviṣyati,*
>
> **तर्हि अहम् आगमिष्यामि।**
>
> *tarhi aham āgamiṣyāmi.* — "If there is time, I will come."

> **यदि भवान् आगमिष्यति,**
>
> *yadi bhavān āgamiṣyati,*
>
> **तर्हि मीरा प्रसन्ना भविष्यति।**
>
> *tarhi mīrā prasannā bhaviṣyati.* — "If you come, Mira will be pleased."

## You'll want to know: if …, then do this
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-GRAMMAR-C284-CONDITIONAL-REAL-02]; assesses=[SA-GRAMMAR-C279-SHOULD-01, SA-LEX-C243-QUAL243-02, SA-LEX-C26-ROLE-05, SA-LEX-C257-TRV257-02] -->

After a condition in the present, the second half can ask or advise, with **-तु** or **-एत्**:

> **यदि भवान् रुग्णः अस्ति,**
>
> *yadi bhavān rugṇaḥ asti,*
>
> **तर्हि वैद्यं गच्छतु।**
>
> *tarhi vaidyaṃ gacchatu.* — "If you are ill, please go to the doctor."

> **यदि धूमयानं गतम्,**
>
> *yadi dhūmayānaṃ gatam,*
>
> **तर्हि भवान् लोकयानेन गच्छेत्।**
>
> *tarhi bhavān lokayānena gacchet.* — "If the train has gone, you should go by bus."

**लोकयानेन** (*lokayānena*) is "by bus": *-ena*, "by", as in **मतेन**.
