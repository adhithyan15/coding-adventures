Lesson 1 of 5.
автобус (avtóbus) — a bus.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a theatre, then the Russian for a restaurant.

You'll want to know: автобус.
автобус (avtóbus) — "a bus".

The word, taken apart.
автобус (avtóbus) is an international word of the early 1900s: авто-, from Greek autós, "self", as in automobile, with бус, cut from Latin omnibus, "for everyone".

Lesson 2 of 5.
поезд (póyezd) — a train.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a restaurant, then the Russian for a bus.

You'll want to know: поезд.
поезд (póyezd) — "a train".

The word, taken apart.
поезд (póyezd) is по- with езд, from ездить, "to ride, to travel", and езда, "a ride" (Vasmer). It first meant "a journey", then "a procession of carriages", then a train.

Lesson 3 of 5.
самолёт (samolyót) — an aeroplane.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a bus, then the Russian for a train.

You'll want to know: самолёт.
самолёт (samolyót) — "an aeroplane". "Self-flier".

The word, taken apart.
самолёт (samolyót) is сам, "self", with -лёт, "flight", from летать, "to fly": "a self-flier". The fairy-tale flying carpet is the ковёр-самолёт (samolyót), "carpet-plane".

Lesson 4 of 5.
машина (mashína) — a car.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a train, then the Russian for an aeroplane.

You'll want to know: машина.
машина (mashína) — "a car".

The word, taken apart.
машина (mashína) — from Peter the Great's time; it came through German Maschine from French machine, Latin māchina, "a device" (Vasmer). The sense "car" is modern.

Lesson 5 of 5.
мост (most) — a bridge.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for an aeroplane, then the Russian for a car.

You'll want to know: мост.
мост (most) — "a bridge".

The word, taken apart.
мост (most) from Proto-Slavic mostŭ, "bridge; a plank floor" — inherited. Vasmer finds a Germanic source unreliable but compares Old High German mast, "a pole", so the deeper origin is open.
