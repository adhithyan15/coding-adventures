## Grammar Lens: the table, filled in

| | near | far |
|---|---|---|
| respectfully | இவர் | அவர் |
| he / she | — | அவன் / அவள் |
| more than one | — | அவர்கள் |

**The far column was empty and its pattern had been taught.** That is the
shape of this chapter: the pointing system arrived long ago, correct and
complete as a rule, with only half its cells ever handed over.

## Grammar Lens: the question behind each choice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[TA-LEX-C85-NAAM-NAANGAL-01, TA-LEX-C85-AVAN-AVAL-01] -->

| before you say | ask yourself |
|---|---|
| he or she | how close is this person to me |
| we | is my listener inside it |

Both questions are ones English lets a speaker skip. Tamil asks them every time,
and neither has a safe default you can lean on forever — though a learner in
doubt about the first is better off one shade too polite.
