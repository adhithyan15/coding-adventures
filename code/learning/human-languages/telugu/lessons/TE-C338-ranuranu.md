# రానురాను (rānurānu) — as time went by

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[TE-LEX-C337-ARESTU337-05, TE-LEX-C338-KRAMANGA338-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Telugu for the accused, then the Telugu for gradually.

## You'll want to know: రానురాను
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C338-KRAMANGA338-02]; assesses=[] -->

**రానురాను** — *rānurānu* — "as time went by".

> రానురాను ఆ పల్లె పట్టణంగా మారింది.
>
> *rānurānu ā palle paṭṭaṇaṅgā mārindi.*
>
> As time went by, that village grew into a town.

రాను, coming, said twice: as the days keep coming.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**రానురాను** (*rānurānu*) is native Telugu: the verb *rā*, "to come" (DEDR 5270), said twice.
