## You'll want to know: غار
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C492-GHIRA492-05]; assesses=[] -->

**غار** — *ghāra* — "to be jealous".

> تغار أختي عندما يلعب أبي مع أخي.
>
> *taghāru ukhtī ʿindamā yalʿabu abī maʿa akhī.*
>
> My sister gets jealous when my father plays with my brother.

Present: يغار, *yaghār*.

It takes من or على: غار منه. The same letters also spell غار, ghār, a cave, from another root.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**غار** (*ghāra*) is built on the native Arabic root *gh-y-r*, "jealousy, a jealous care for one's own" (Lane). It is the verb that goes with ghīra and ghayūr.
