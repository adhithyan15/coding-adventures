## You'll want to know: செரி
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C487-UNAVU487-04]; assesses=[] -->

**செரி** — *ceri* — "to digest".

> இரவு உணவு இன்னும் செரிக்கவில்லை.
>
> *iravu uṇavu iṉṉum cerikkavillai.*
>
> Dinner has not been digested yet.

Past (it …): செரித்தது, *cerittatu*.

The food is the subject, so the past is செரித்தது. செரிமானம், digestion.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**செரி** (*ceri*) is a Tamil verb whose origin is not settled; DEDR has no entry for it that we can point to.
