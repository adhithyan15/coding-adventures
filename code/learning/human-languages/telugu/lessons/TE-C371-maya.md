## You'll want to know: మాయ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C371-MAYA371-02]; assesses=[] -->

**మాయ** — *māya* — "magic; an illusion".

> మాంత్రికుడు మాయతో ఒక కోట సృష్టించాడు.
>
> *māntrikuḍu māyatō oka kōṭa sṛṣṭiñcāḍu.*
>
> The sorcerer created a castle by magic.

మాయ చేయు, to work magic; also a trick.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**మాయ** (*māya*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *māyā*, "supernatural power, skill", and "illusion" (CDIAL 10060).
