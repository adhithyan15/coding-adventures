## You'll want to know: lo scooter
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C416-MOTORINO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**lo scooter** — "the scooter (motor scooter)".

> Con lo scooter giro la città in dieci minuti.
>
> *With the scooter I get round the city in ten minutes.*

Bigger than *il motorino*; plural *gli scooter*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*scooter* was borrowed from English *scooter* (EtymDB), built on *scoot* 'to dart off'.
