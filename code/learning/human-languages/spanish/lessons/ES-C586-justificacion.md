## You'll want to know: la justificación
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C586-JUSTIF-02]; assesses=[] -->

**la justificación** — "the justification".

> No hay justificación para eso.
>
> *There is no justification for that.*

Often after *no hay*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*justificación* is a learned borrowing from Late Latin *iustificatio*, built on *iustificare* 'to make right', from *iustus* (EtymDB).
