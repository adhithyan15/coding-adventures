## You'll want to know: el conductor
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C640-VIAJAR-05]; assesses=[] -->

**el conductor** — "the driver".

> El conductor del autobús es muy amable.
>
> *The bus driver is very kind.*

A woman is *la conductora*. In Spain the verb is *conducir*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*conductor* is a learned borrowing from Latin *conductor*, which meant "one who hires, a contractor" (Lewis & Short); it is built on *condūcere* "to bring together", from *dūcere* "to lead" (EtymDB).
