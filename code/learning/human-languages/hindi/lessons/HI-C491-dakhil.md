## You'll want to know: दाख़िल
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C491-DAKHIL491-01]; assesses=[] -->

**दाख़िल** — *dākhil* — "admitted; entered".

> तेज़ बुख़ार की वजह से दादा अस्पताल में दाख़िल हैं।
>
> *tez bukhār kī vajah se dādā aspatāl meṁ dākhil haiṁ.*
>
> Grandfather is in hospital with a high fever.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**दाख़िल** (*dākhil*) is usually traced to Arabic *dākhil*, "entering, inside", which reached Hindi through Persian. *aspatāl meṁ dākhil honā* is to be admitted to hospital.
