## You'll want to know: vindicta, vindictae
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C532-LIBT-03]; assesses=[] -->

**vindicta, vindictae** — "the rod of freedom (in freeing a slave); vengeance".

> Magistrātus servum vindictā tangit, et servus līber fit.
>
> *The magistrate touches the slave with the rod, and the slave becomes free.*

Feminine, first declension. In one way of freeing a slave, he was touched with this rod before a magistrate. Later it also means vengeance; English *vindictive*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**vindicta** is built on *vindicō* "to lay claim to, set free" (Lewis & Short; EtymDB), from *vindex* "a defender". English *vindictive* comes from its later sense, vengeance.
