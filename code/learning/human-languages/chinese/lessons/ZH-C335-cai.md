## You'll want to know: cāi
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C335-LANTERN335-03]; assesses=[] -->

*cāi* — "to guess".

> *wǒ cāi le sān cì dōu bú duì* — I guessed three times and got it wrong each time.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*cāi* is inherited from Middle Chinese *tshoj* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses it 'to suspect' and 'to resent'. The sense 'to guess' is not given there.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
