## You'll want to know: اعلان
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C290-AILAN290-02]; assesses=[] -->

**اعلان** — *elān* — "an announcement".

> ذرا سنیے، ایک اعلان ہے۔
>
> *zarā suniye, ek elān hai.*
>
> Listen, please: there is an announcement.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**اعلان** (*elān*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *iʿlān*, "a making known". **اعلان کرنا** is to announce.
