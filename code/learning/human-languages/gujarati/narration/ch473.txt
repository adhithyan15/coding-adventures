Gujarati, chapter 473: From Hip to Toe.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
જાંઘ (jā̃gh) — a thigh.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for an intestine, then the Gujarati for the stomach.

You'll want to know: જાંઘ.
જાંઘ — jā̃gh — "a thigh".
પડ્યા પછી મારી જાંઘ પર સોજો આવ્યો. paḍyā pachī mārī jā̃gh par sojo āvyo. After I fell, my thigh swelled up.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
જાંઘ (jā̃gh) is inherited from Sanskrit jaṅghā, "shank, ankle to knee"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati word, "thigh" (CDIAL 5082).

What you've built.
One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
