## You'll want to know: арестовывать
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C292-DOPROS-02]; assesses=[] -->

**арестовывать** (*arestóvyvat*) — "to arrest".

> Его арестовали в аэропорту.
>
> *Yevó arestováli v aeroportú.*
>
> He was arrested at the airport.

Imperfective; perfective *арестовать* (*arestovát*). *Арест* — an arrest.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*арестовывать* is built on *арест* "an arrest", which is usually traced through German *Arrest* to Medieval Latin *arrestum* "a stopping"; EtymDB lists no parent.
