Lesson 1 of 5.
శ్మశానం (śmaśānaṁ) — a cremation ground.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a funeral procession, then the Telugu for a funeral.

You'll want to know: శ్మశానం.
శ్మశానం — śmaśānaṁ — "a cremation ground".
అంతిమయాత్ర శ్మశానం చేరింది. antimayātra śmaśānaṁ cērindi. The funeral procession reached the cremation ground.

The word, taken apart.
శ్మశానం (śmaśānaṁ) is borrowed from Sanskrit śmaśāna, "a place for burning the dead" (CDIAL 12658).

Lesson 2 of 5.
సమాధి (samādhi) — a grave; a tomb.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a funeral, then the Telugu for a cremation ground.

You'll want to know: సమాధి.
సమాధి — samādhi — "a grave; a tomb".
ఆ పాత సమాధి దగ్గర ఒక చెట్టు ఉంది. ā pāta samādhi daggara oka ceṭṭu undi. There is a tree near that old tomb.
Plural: సమాధులు, samādhulu.

The word, taken apart.
సమాధి (samādhi) is borrowed from Sanskrit samādhi (Monier-Williams), whose first sense is "putting together, joining"; in Telugu it names a tomb.

Lesson 3 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a cremation ground, then the Telugu for a grave.

You'll want to know: నివాళి.
నివాళి — nivāḷi — "a tribute".
విద్యార్థులు తమ మాస్టారికి నివాళి అర్పించారు. vidyārthulu tama māsṭāriki nivāḷi arpiñcāru. The students paid tribute to their teacher.
నివాళి (nivāḷi) అర్పించు, to pay tribute: the phrase every news report uses.

The word, taken apart.
నివాళి (nivāḷi) is a Telugu word for a tribute, an offering of honour; its origin is not settled.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a grave, then the Telugu for a tribute.

You'll want to know: వర్ధంతి.
వర్ధంతి — vardhanti — "a death anniversary".
నిన్న తాతగారి మొదటి వర్ధంతి జరిగింది. ninna tātagāri modaṭi vardhanti jarigindi. Yesterday was grandfather's first death anniversary.
Families remember the dead on this day each year.

The word, taken apart.
వర్ధంతి (vardhanti) comes from Sanskrit, but its exact source is not settled.

Lesson 5 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a tribute, then the Telugu for a death anniversary.

You'll want to know: వీలునామా.
వీలునామా — vīlunāmā — "a will; a testament".
తాతగారు ఇల్లు నా పేరు మీద వీలునామా రాశారు. tātagāru illu nā pēru mīda vīlunāmā rāśāru. Grandfather wrote a will leaving the house to me.
వీలునామా (vīlunāmā) రాయు, to make a will.

The word, taken apart.
వీలునామా (vīlunāmā) ends in Persian nāma, "a writing, a deed", as several loans for documents do; the origin of the first part is not settled.
