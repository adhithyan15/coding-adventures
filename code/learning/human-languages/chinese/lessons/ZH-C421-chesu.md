## You'll want to know: 车速
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C421-EXPRESS421-03]; assesses=[] -->

**车速** — *chēsù* — "speed of a vehicle".

> **高速的车速很快** *gāosù de chēsù hěn kuài* — Cars go fast on the expressway.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**车速** (*chēsù*) is built from 车 'vehicle' and 速 'speed', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *tsyhae* and *suwk* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription).
