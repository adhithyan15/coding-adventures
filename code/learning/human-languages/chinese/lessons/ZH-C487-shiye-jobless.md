## You'll want to know: shīyè
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C487-CAREER487-04]; assesses=[] -->

*shīyè* — "to lose one's job; to be out of work".

> *tā shīyè le bàn nián jīngshen hěn bù hǎo* — He was out of work for six months and was in very low spirits.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*shīyè* is built from *shī* 'to lose' and 业 'work', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *syit* and *ngjaep* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to err' and 'to let slip'.
