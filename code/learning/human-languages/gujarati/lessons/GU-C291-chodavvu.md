# છોડાવવું (choḍāvvũ) — to set free; to get released

## You'll want to know: છોડાવવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C291-GHERVU291-05]; assesses=[] -->

**છોડાવવું** — *choḍāvvũ* — "to set free; to get released".

> રાત્રે પોલીસે છોકરાને છોડાવ્યો.
>
> *rātre polīse chokrāne choḍāvyo.*
>
> During the night the police freed the boy.

Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે છોકરાને છોડાવ્યો, *teṇe chokrāne choḍāvyo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**છોડાવવું** (*choḍāvvũ*) is the causative of **છોડવું**, to let go, which CDIAL sets under Sanskrit *kṣōṭayati* (CDIAL 3747): to make someone be let go, and so to get a captive freed.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
