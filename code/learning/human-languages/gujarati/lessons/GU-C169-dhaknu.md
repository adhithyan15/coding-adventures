# ઢાંકણું (ḍhā̃kṇũ) — lid

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-LEX-C169-DISH169-03, GU-LEX-C169-DISH169-04] -->

## You'll want to know: ઢાંકણું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C169-DISH169-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ઢાંકણું** — *ḍhā̃kṇũ* — "lid".

> **તપેલીનું ઢાંકણું ક્યાં છે?** — *tapelīnũ ḍhā̃kṇũ kyā̃ che?* — "Where is the lid of the pot?"

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ઢાંકણું** (*ḍhā̃kṇũ*) is a tadbhava, kin to *ḍhā̃kvũ*, "to cover". CDIAL lists it, glossed "cover, lid", under *\*ḍhakk*, *\*ḍhaṅk*, "cover" (CDIAL 5574).
