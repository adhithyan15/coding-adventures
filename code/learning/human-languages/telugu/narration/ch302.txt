Telugu, chapter 302: Ox, Calf, Sheep, Wolf, Crocodile.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ఎద్దు (eddu) — an ox, a bull.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a taxi, then the Telugu for petrol.

You'll want to know: ఎద్దు.
ఎద్దు — eddu — "an ox, a bull".

The word, taken apart.
ఎద్దు (eddu) is native Dravidian, one word with Tamil erutu and Kannada eddu (DEDR 815). DEDR lists Telugu eddu, "ox, bull".
