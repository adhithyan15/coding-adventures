## You'll want to know: روشنی
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C288-CHIRAGH288-04]; assesses=[] -->

**روشنی** — *raushnī* — "light; brightness".

> آگ کی روشنی دور تک تھی۔
>
> *āg kī raushnī dūr tak thī.*
>
> The light of the fire could be seen far off.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**روشنی** (*raushnī*) is usually traced to Persian *raushanī*, "brightness", from *raushan*, "bright". It is feminine: **روشنی تھی**.
