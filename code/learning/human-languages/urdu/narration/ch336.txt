Urdu, chapter 336: Proof.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ثابت کرنا (sābit karnā) — to prove.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for in agreement, then the Urdu for a side in a dispute.

You'll want to know: ثابت کرنا.
ثابت کرنا — sābit karnā — "to prove".
آپ اپنی بات کیسے ثابت کریں گے؟ āp apnī bāt kaise sābit kareṅ ge? How will you prove what you say?
Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے اپنی بات ثابت کی, us ne apnī bāt sābit kī.

The word, taken apart.
ثابت کرنا (sābit karnā) is built from sābit, usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic thābit, "firm, established", and کرنا, "to do": to make a claim stand firm.

Lesson 3 of 5.
الزام (ilzām) — an accusation; a charge.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for to prove, then the Urdu for firm.

You'll want to know: الزام.
الزام — ilzām — "an accusation; a charge".
یہ الزام بالکل غلط ہے۔ yih ilzām bilkul ghalat hai. This accusation is completely wrong.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
الزام (ilzām) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic ilzām, "binding someone to something"; the sense "a charge laid on someone" is the one Urdu uses.
