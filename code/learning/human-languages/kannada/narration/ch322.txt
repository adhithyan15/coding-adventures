Kannada, chapter 322: The King and the Queen.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ರಾಜ (rāja) — a king.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a verdict, then the Kannada for a punishment.

You'll want to know: ರಾಜ.
ರಾಜ — rāja — "a king".
ನಾನು ರಾಜ ಆಗಿದ್ದರೆ ಎಲ್ಲರಿಗೂ ಮನೆ ಕಟ್ಟಿಸುತ್ತಿದ್ದೆ. nānu rāja āgiddare ellarigū mane kaṭṭisuttidde. If I were king, I would have houses built for everyone.
Plural: ರಾಜರು, rājaru.
The land a king rules is his ರಾಜ್ಯ, and he lives in an ಅರಮನೆ, a palace.

The word, taken apart.
ರಾಜ (rāja) is borrowed from Sanskrit rājan, "a chieftain, a king" (CDIAL 10679), in its compound form rāja.

Lesson 2 of 5.
ರಾಣಿ (rāṇi) — a queen.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a punishment, then the Kannada for a king.

You'll want to know: ರಾಣಿ.
ರಾಣಿ — rāṇi — "a queen".
ರಾಣಿ ಅರಮನೆಯ ತೋಟದಲ್ಲಿ ನಡೆಯುತ್ತಿದ್ದಳು. rāṇi aramaneya tōṭadalli naḍeyuttiddaḷu. The queen was walking in the palace garden.
Plural: ರಾಣಿಯರು, rāṇiyaru.

The word, taken apart.
ರಾಣಿ (rāṇi) goes back to Sanskrit rājñī, "a queen" (CDIAL 10692), the feminine of rājan; the spoken languages of the north smoothed it to rāṇī, and Kannada took that form.

Lesson 3 of 5.
ಆಳು (āḷu) — to reign; to rule.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a king, then the Kannada for a queen.

You'll want to know: ಆಳು.
ಆಳು — āḷu — "to reign; to rule".
ಆ ರಾಜ ನಲವತ್ತು ವರ್ಷ ಈ ನಾಡನ್ನು ಆಳಿದನು. ā rāja nalavattu varṣa ī nāḍannu āḷidanu. That king ruled this land for forty years.
Past (I …): ಆಳಿದೆ, āḷide.
What is ruled takes -ಅನ್ನು. A different ಆಳು (āḷu), a noun, means a servant or a worker.

The word, taken apart.
ಆಳು (āḷu) is native Dravidian: DEDR 5157 lists Kannada āḷ, "to possess, govern, rule", beside Tamil āḷ, "to rule, reign over".

Lesson 4 of 5.
ಸಿಂಹಾಸನ (siṃhāsana) — a throne.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a queen, then the Kannada for to reign.

You'll want to know: ಸಿಂಹಾಸನ.
ಸಿಂಹಾಸನ — siṃhāsana — "a throne".
ರಾಜ ಸಿಂಹಾಸನದ ಮೇಲೆ ಕುಳಿತಿದ್ದನು. rāja siṃhāsanada mēle kuḷitiddanu. The king was sitting on the throne.
The lion seat, as ಸಿಂಹ is a lion. ಸಿಂಹಾಸನ (siṃhāsana) ಏರು, to climb the throne, is to become king.

The word, taken apart.
ಸಿಂಹಾಸನ (siṃhāsana) is borrowed from Sanskrit siṃhāsana, "the lion's seat, the king's seat, a throne" (Monier-Williams), from siṃha "a lion" and āsana "a seat".
