## You'll want to know: اقتبس
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C395-MARJI395-03]; assesses=[] -->

**اقتبس** — *iqtabasa* — "to quote".

> اقتبس الصحفي جملة من الخطاب.
>
> *iqtabasa aṣ-ṣaḥafī jumla min al-khiṭāb.*
>
> The journalist quoted a sentence from the speech.

Present: يقتبس, *yaqtabis*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**اقتبس** (*iqtabasa*) is built on the native Arabic root *q-b-s*, "to take fire from" (Lane). Lane gives the root for taking a flame from a fire, and also for taking knowledge: Form VIII came to mean to quote.
