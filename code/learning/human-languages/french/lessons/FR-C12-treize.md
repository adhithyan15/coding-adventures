# treize — thirteen

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

[PAUSE 2s] With the **-ze** identified, the rest of the welded teens stop being
words to memorise and start being sums you can read.

## Sounds you'll need
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-TREIZE-04]; assesses=[] -->

- The **ei** is the open **è** you met in *père* and *frère* — *treh*, not
  *tray*.
- `z-voiced` — the buzzed **z** again.
- Together: **treize** = *TREHZ*, one syllable, with the throat **r** at the
  front.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**treize** is Latin **trēdecim** — *trēs* ("three") plus *decem* ("ten").

Read it as a sum now rather than a word:

**trei-** is *trois*, worn down · **-ze** is *decem*, worn down

Say **trois**, then **treize**, and the front half answers for itself. You are
not learning a thirteenth arbitrary noise; you are reading *three* and *ten*
stuck together and then rubbed smooth.
