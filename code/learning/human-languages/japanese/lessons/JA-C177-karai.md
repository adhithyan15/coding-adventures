## You'll want to know: からい
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C177-TASTE177-01]; assesses=[] -->

**からい** — *karai* — "spicy; hot, salty".

> **この りょうりは からいです。** — *kono ryōri wa karai desu* — "This dish is spicy."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**からい** (*karai*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
