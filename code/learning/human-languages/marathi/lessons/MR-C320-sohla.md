## You'll want to know: सोहळा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C320-UTSAV320-03]; assesses=[] -->

**सोहळा** — *sohḷā* — "a grand ceremony; a celebration".

> शाळेच्या शताब्दीचा सोहळा थाटात झाला.
>
> *śāḷecyā śatābdīcā sohḷā thāṭāt jhālā.*
>
> The school's centenary celebration was held in style.

Masculine.

Plural सोहळे.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सोहळा** (*sohḷā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi *sohḷā*, 'a festive occasion', under *\*śubhakāla*, 'an auspicious time' (CDIAL 12533).
