# మాసిపోవు (māsipōvu) — to get grubby; to get soiled

## You'll want to know: మాసిపోవు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C490-CIRUGU490-03]; assesses=[] -->

**మాసిపోవు** — *māsipōvu* — "to get grubby; to get soiled".

> రెండు రోజుల ప్రయాణంలో నా బట్టలు మాసిపోయాయి.
>
> *reṇḍu rōjula prayāṇaṁlō nā baṭṭalu māsipōyāyi.*
>
> After two days on the road my clothes were grubby.

Past (it …): మాసిపోయింది, *māsipōyindi*.

Said of clothes, walls and hands that dirt has dulled. మాసిన బట్టలు, dirty clothes. Its subject is a thing, so the past shown is the third person: మాసిపోయింది, it got grubby.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**మాసిపోవు** (*māsipōvu*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 4792 lists Telugu *māsi*, "dirt", and *māyu*, "to become dirty, soiled", here with పోవు, "to go" (DEDR 4572).
