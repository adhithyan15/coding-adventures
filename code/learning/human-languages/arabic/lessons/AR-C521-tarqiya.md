## You'll want to know: ترقية
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C521-MUKAFAA521-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ترقية** — *tarqiya* — "a promotion".

> حصلت أختي على ترقية في عملها.
>
> *ḥaṣalat ukhtī ʿalā tarqiya fī ʿamalihā.*
>
> My sister got a promotion at work.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ترقية** (*tarqiya*) is built on the native Arabic root *r-q-y*, "to go up, to climb" (Lane). Tarqiya is the verbal noun of Form II: raising someone up.
