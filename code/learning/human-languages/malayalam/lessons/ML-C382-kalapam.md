# കലാപം (kalāpaṁ) — a riot

## You'll want to know: കലാപം
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C382-KALAPAM382-01]; assesses=[] -->

**കലാപം** — *kalāpaṁ* — "a riot".

> കലാപം കാരണം കടകൾ അടച്ചു.
>
> *kalāpaṁ kāraṇaṁ kaṭakaḷ aṭaccu.*
>
> Shops closed because of the riot.

Also an uproar at a meeting.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

The origin of **കലാപം** (*kalāpaṁ*) in the sense of a riot is not settled. In the news it is violent disorder in a town.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
