## You'll want to know: सरोवर
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C443-ODHA443-04]; assesses=[] -->

**सरोवर** — *sarovar* — "a large lake".

> सरोवराच्या काठावर खूप पक्षी असतात.
>
> *sarovarācyā kāṭhāvar khūp pakṣī astāt.*
>
> There are lots of birds on the shore of the lake.

Neuter.

A literary word, bigger than a तलाव.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सरोवर** (*sarovar*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *sarovara*, "a lake, a big pond" (Monier-Williams).
