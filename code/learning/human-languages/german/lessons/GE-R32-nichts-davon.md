# Nichts davon — the chapter, run cold

## Warm-up

[PAUSE 4s] Two questions before you say anything. Which negative goes in front

## Guided Practice: everything on the table, cancelled
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GE-LEX-KEIN-01, GE-LEX-KEINE-01, GE-LEX-KEINEN-01, GE-LEX-BROT-01, GE-ETYMON-BROT-BREAD-01, GE-LEX-WASSER-01, GE-ETYMON-WASSER-WATER-01, GE-LEX-WEIN-01, GE-ETYMON-WEIN-VINUM-01, GE-LEX-KAFFEE-02, GE-LEX-TEE-02, GE-LEX-MILCH-02] -->

[PAUSE 1s] Six things this book has put in front of you to eat and drink. Say
each one's article first, then cancel it.

- [YOU SAY: *das* Brot → "kein Brot"]
- [YOU SAY: *das* Wasser → "kein Wasser"]
- [YOU SAY: *der* Wein → "kein Wein", and having it, "keinen Wein"]
- [YOU SAY: *der* Kaffee → "kein Kaffee", "keinen Kaffee"]
- [YOU SAY: *der* Tee → "kein Tee", "keinen Tee"]
- [YOU SAY: *die* Milch → "keine Milch", which does not move]

## Guided Practice: the distant band — the people and the face
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GE-LEX-KEIN-01, GE-LEX-KEINE-01, GE-LEX-KEINEN-01, GE-LEX-HUND-02, GE-LEX-KATZE-04, GE-LEX-BRUDER-01, GE-LEX-SCHWESTER-01, GE-LEX-MUTTER-01, GE-LEX-VATER-01, GE-LEX-FREUND-02, GE-SOUND-FREUND-03, GE-ETYMON-FREUND-04, GE-LEX-FREUNDIN-02, GE-GRAMMAR-SUFFIX-IN-03, GE-ETYMON-SUFFIX-IN-04, GE-LEX-FAMILIE-02, GE-SOUND-FAMILIE-03, GE-ETYMON-FAMILIE-04, GE-LEX-AUGE-02, GE-SOUND-AUGE-03, GE-ETYMON-AUGE-04, GE-LEX-OHR-02, GE-SOUND-OHR-03, GE-ETYMON-OHR-04, GE-LEX-MUND-02, GE-SOUND-MUND-03, GE-ETYMON-MUND-04, GE-LEX-NASE-02] -->

[PAUSE 4s] Now reach further back — the animals, the household, and the four
words on your face. Nothing here is new; the negative in front of it is.

- [YOU SAY: the two animals — "kein Hund" and "keine Katze"]
- [YOU SAY: the household, having none of them — "keinen Bruder, keine
  Schwester, keine Mutter, keinen Vater"]
- [YOU SAY: the pair the *-in* suffix made — "keinen Freund, keine Freundin"]
- [YOU SAY: the collective — "keine Familie"]
- [YOU SAY: the four on your face — "kein Auge, kein Ohr, keinen Mund, keine
  Nase"]
- [YOU SAY: which of those four is a *die*-word, and prove it by its negative
  — "*die* Nase, keine Nase"]

## Guided Practice: the verbs, refused
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GE-GRAMMAR-NICHT-STELLUNG-01, GE-GRAMMAR-KEIN-VS-NICHT-01, GE-LEX-NICHT-01, GE-LEX-GUT-02, GE-LEX-MUEDE-01, GE-LEX-ZEIT-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s]
- [YOU SAY: the verb cancelled, so *nicht* goes last — "Ich verstehe nicht."]
- [YOU SAY: the quality cancelled, so *nicht* leads it — "Das ist nicht gut."]
- [YOU SAY: "Ich bin nicht müde."]
- [YOU SAY: the noun, so *kein* — "Ich habe keine Zeit."]
- [YOU SAY: both in one breath — "Ich habe keine Zeit und ich verstehe nicht."]

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GE-LEX-KEIN-01, GE-LEX-KEINE-01, GE-LEX-KEINEN-01, GE-ETYMON-KEIN-NICHT-EIN-02, GE-GRAMMAR-KEIN-VS-NICHT-01, GE-GRAMMAR-NICHT-STELLUNG-01] -->

[PAUSE 4s] Say the three shapes and what each stands in front of. (***Kein***
before a *der* or *das* word, ***keine*** before a *die* word and every plural,
***keinen*** before a *der* word that is being had.) What two pieces is *kein*
built from? (**Ni** and **ein** — "not one".) And when does the other negative go
to the end of the sentence? (When what it cancels is **the verb**.) Next: the
question that has no question word in it.
