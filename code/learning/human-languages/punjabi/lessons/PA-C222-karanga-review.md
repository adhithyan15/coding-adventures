# Hear and say will do again

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-GRAMMAR-FUT-CONSONANT-222, PA-GRAMMAR-FUT-YOU-THEY-222] -->

[YOU HEAR: *karāṅgā; karegā; karoge; karange*] Say all four.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-GRAMMAR-FUT-CONSONANT-222, PA-GRAMMAR-FUT-YOU-THEY-222, PA-GRAMMAR-FUT-1PL-221] -->

- [YOU HEAR: *Aman will work tomorrow*] Say **ਅਮਨ ਕੱਲ੍ਹ ਕੰਮ ਕਰੇਗਾ।**
- [YOU HEAR: *what will you do?*] Say **ਤੁਸੀਂ ਕੀ ਕਰੋਗੇ?**
- [YOU HEAR: *we will go home*] Say **ਅਸੀਂ ਘਰ ਜਾਵਾਂਗੇ।**
- [YOU READ: **ਉਹ ਕੰਮ ਕਰਨਗੇ।**] Give its meaning.
- [YOU WRITE: cover the model and write **ਮੈਂ ਕੰਮ ਕਰਾਂਗਾ।** or its woman's form]
