## You'll want to know: સૂર્યોદય
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C218-SUN218-02]; assesses=[] -->

**સૂર્યોદય** — *sūryoday* — "sunrise".

> **આવતીકાલે સૂર્યોદય વહેલો થશે.** — *āvtīkāle sūryoday vahelo thaśe* — "Sunrise will be early tomorrow."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**સૂર્યોદય** (*sūryoday*) is Sanskrit *sūryodaya*, "sunrise", built from *sūrya*, "sun", and *udaya*, "rising", and taken over whole as a learned loan, a tatsama (CDIAL 13574, 1931).
