## You'll want to know: 门口
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C74-VISIT74-03]; assesses=[] -->

**门口** — *ménkǒu* — "doorway; entrance".

> **门口有一个客人** *ménkǒu yǒu yí ge kèrén* — There's a guest at the door.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**门口** (*ménkǒu*) is built from 门 'door' and 口 'mouth', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *mwon* and *khuwX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription): the mouth of the door, where you go in.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for meeting people.
