## You'll want to know: শঙ্খ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C310-SHONKHO310-01]; assesses=[] -->

**শঙ্খ** — *shôngkhô* — "a conch shell".

> সন্ধ্যায় মন্দিরে কেউ শঙ্খ বাজাল।
>
> *sôndhyāy mondire keu shôngkhô bājālô.*
>
> In the evening someone blew a conch at the temple.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**শঙ্খ** (*shôngkhô*) is Sanskrit *śaṅkha*, a shell, above all the conch (Monier-Williams). Its ঙ্খ is *ngkh*; everyday speech wears the word down to শাঁখ.
