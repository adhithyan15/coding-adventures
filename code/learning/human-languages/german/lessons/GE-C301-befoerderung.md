## You'll want to know: die Beförderung
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C301-KUENDIGEN-04]; assesses=[] -->

**die Beförderung** — "the promotion; transport".

> Die Beförderung kam ganz unerwartet.
>
> *The promotion came quite unexpectedly.*

Plural: *die Beförderungen*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Beförderung* is built on *befördern*, from *fördern* "to further", Middle High German *vürdern* (EtymDB).
