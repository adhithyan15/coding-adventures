Marathi, chapter 475: Daring and Fearful.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
साहसी (sāhsī) — adventurous.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a mess, then the Marathi for discipline.

You'll want to know: साहसी.
साहसी — sāhsī — "adventurous".
साहसी मुलांनी डोंगर चढून पाहिला. sāhsī mulānnī ḍoṅgar caḍhūn pāhilā. The adventurous boys tried climbing the mountain.
From साहस, a daring deed. Unchanging.

The word, taken apart.
साहसी (sāhsī) is a Marathi adjective built on साहस, "a daring deed", a tatsama from Sanskrit sāhasa, "boldness" (CDIAL 13378).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for adventurous, then the Marathi for chicken-hearted.

You'll want to know: बिनधास्त.
बिनधास्त — bindhāsta — "carefree; unafraid".
परीक्षा संपल्यावर मुली बिनधास्त खेळल्या. parīkṣā samplyāvar mulī bindhāsta kheḷlyā. When the exams were over the girls played without a care.
बिन, without, + धास्त, dread, the word in धास्ती. Also an adverb.

The word, taken apart.
बिनधास्त (bindhāsta) is a Marathi compound of Hindustani bin, "without", and धास्त, "dread", the word in धास्ती, whose origin is not settled.

Lesson 4 of 5.
बेफिकीर (bephikīr) — unconcerned; heedless.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for chicken-hearted, then the Marathi for carefree.

You'll want to know: बेफिकीर.
बेफिकीर — bephikīr — "unconcerned; heedless".
तो खर्चाबद्दल बेफिकीर आहे. to kharcābaddal bephikīr āhe. He is heedless about spending.
बे-, without, + फिकीर, worry. Unchanging.

The word, taken apart.
बेफिकीर (bephikīr) is a loan from Persian befikr, "without worry", built on be-, "without", and fikr, "thought, worry", usually traced to Arabic.

Lesson 5 of 5.
भिणे (bhiṇe) — to be scared of; to fear.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for carefree, then the Marathi for unconcerned.

You'll want to know: भिणे.
भिणे — bhiṇe — "to be scared of; to fear".
माझी बहीण कुत्र्यांना भिते. mājhī bahīṇ kutryānnā bhite. My sister is afraid of dogs.
Past (he …): तो भ्याला, to bhyālā.
What is feared takes -ला: कुत्र्याला भिणे (bhiṇe). The past is irregular: तो भ्याला.

The word, taken apart.
भिणे (bhiṇe) is a tadbhava, inherited from Sanskrit bibheti, "fears"; CDIAL lists Marathi bhiṇẽ, "to fear" (CDIAL 9241).
