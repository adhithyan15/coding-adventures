## You'll want to know: il cavaliere
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C327-BATTAGLIA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**il cavaliere** — "the knight; the rider".

> Il cavaliere ha salvato la principessa.
>
> *The knight saved the princess.*

Also a gentleman, and a woman's dance partner.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*cavaliere* came through Provençal *cavalier* (EtymDB) from Late Latin *caballārius* "horseman", built on *caballus* "horse".
