## You'll want to know: aufregen
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C468-WUT-04]; assesses=[] -->

**aufregen** — "to get worked up (sich aufregen); to upset".

> Mein Opa regt sich über jede Kleinigkeit auf.
>
> *My grandpa gets worked up about every little thing.*

Separable and mostly reflexive: *sich über etwas aufregen*. *Das regt mich auf!* — That makes me mad!

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*aufregen* is built on *auf* "up" and *regen* "to stir, to move", which goes back to Middle High German *regen* "to excite, to move" (EtymDB).
