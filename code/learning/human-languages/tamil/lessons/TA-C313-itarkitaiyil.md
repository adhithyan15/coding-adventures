# இதற்கிடையில் (itaṟkiṭaiyil) — meanwhile

## You'll want to know: இதற்கிடையில்
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C313-NERAM313-01]; assesses=[] -->

**இதற்கிடையில்** — *itaṟkiṭaiyil* — "meanwhile".

> இதற்கிடையில், காவலர்கள் வீட்டுக்கு வந்தார்கள்.
>
> *itaṟkiṭaiyil, kāvalarkaḷ vīṭṭukku vantārkaḷ.*
>
> Meanwhile, the police came to the house.

இது + இடை, the space between, + -இல்: in the meantime.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**இதற்கிடையில்** (*itaṟkiṭaiyil*) is a Tamil phrase grown into one word, built from இதற்கு, "to this", and இடையில், "in between", from native இடை, "space, interval" (DEDR 434).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
