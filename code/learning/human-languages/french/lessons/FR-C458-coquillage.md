## You'll want to know: le coquillage
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C458-LITTORAL-05]; assesses=[] -->

**le coquillage** — "the seashell; the shellfish".

> La petite fille ramasse des coquillages sur la plage.
>
> *The little girl collects shells on the beach.*

*Les coquillages* are also shellfish to eat.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*coquillage* is built on *coquille* 'shell' (EtymDB lists it), which goes back to Vulgar Latin *conchilia*, from Latin *concha* 'shellfish, shell'.
