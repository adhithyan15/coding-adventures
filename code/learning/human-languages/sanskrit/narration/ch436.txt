Sanskrit, chapter 436: Clouds and Showers.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
अभ्रम् (abhram) — a bank of cloud; a cloud.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for barter, then the Sanskrit for a storehouse.

You'll want to know: अभ्रम्.
अभ्रम् — abhram — "a bank of cloud; a cloud".
आकाशे कृष्णम् अभ्रम् अस्ति। ākāśe kṛṣṇam abhram asti. There is a dark cloud in the sky.
A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
अभ्रम् (abhram) is the word MW gives for a cloud and cloudy weather; MW reports a reading of it as ab-bhra, "water-bearer"; MW compares Greek ombros and Latin imber, "rain" (MW).

Lesson 2 of 5.
वृष्टिः (vṛṣṭiḥ) — rainfall; a shower.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a storehouse, then the Sanskrit for a bank of cloud.

You'll want to know: वृष्टिः.
वृष्टिः — vṛṣṭiḥ — "rainfall; a shower".
अद्य बहु वृष्टिः भविष्यति। adya bahu vṛṣṭiḥ bhaviṣyati. Today there will be a lot of rainfall.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
वृष्टिः (vṛṣṭiḥ) is built on the root √vṛṣ, "to rain", which also gives varṣati, "rains", with the ending -ti; MW gives vṛṣṭi as rain (MW).

Lesson 3 of 5.
प्रवर्षति (pravarṣati) — starts to pour; begins to rain.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a bank of cloud, then the Sanskrit for rainfall.

You'll want to know: प्रवर्षति.
प्रवर्षति — pravarṣati — "starts to pour; begins to rain".
सायम् अभ्रं सहसा प्रवर्षति। sāyam abhraṃ sahasā pravarṣati. In the evening the cloud suddenly pours down rain.
Past (it …): अभ्रं सहसा प्रवृष्टवत्, abhraṃ sahasā pravṛṣṭavat.

The word, taken apart.
प्रवर्षति (pravarṣati) is built from pra, "forth", and the root √vṛṣ, "to rain", which also gives varṣati, "rains"; MW gives pra-vṛṣ as to begin to rain, to rain, with the participle pravṛṣṭa (MW).

Lesson 4 of 5.
क्लिद्यति (klidyati) — gets soaked; gets wet.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for rainfall, then the Sanskrit for starts to pour.

You'll want to know: क्लिद्यति.
क्लिद्यति — klidyati — "gets soaked; gets wet".
छत्रं विना बालः वर्षायां क्लिद्यति। chatraṃ vinā bālaḥ varṣāyāṃ klidyati. Without an umbrella the boy gets soaked in the rain.
Past (he …): सः वर्षायां क्लिन्नवान्, saḥ varṣāyāṃ klinnavān.

The word, taken apart.
क्लिद्यति (klidyati) comes from the root √klid, "to be or become wet"; MW gives the participle klinna, "moistened, wet" (MW).
