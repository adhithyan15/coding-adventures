## You'll want to know: ਕਾਰਨ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C342-MEAN342-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ਕਾਰਨ** — *kāran* — "a reason; a cause".

> ਕੀ ਉਸ ਦਾ ਕੋਈ ਕਾਰਨ ਹੈ?
>
> *kī us dā koī kāran hai?*
>
> Is there a reason for that?

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਕਾਰਨ** (*kāran*) is inherited from Sanskrit *kāraṇa*, "cause"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *kāraṇ*, "cause", under it (CDIAL 3057).
