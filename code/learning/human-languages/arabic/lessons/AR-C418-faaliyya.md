## You'll want to know: فعالية
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C418-TAQWIYA418-04]; assesses=[] -->

**فعالية** — *faʿʿāliyya* — "effectiveness".

> أثبتت الدراسة فعالية الدواء.
>
> *athbatat ad-dirāsa faʿʿāliyyat ad-dawāʾ.*
>
> The study proved the effectiveness of the medicine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**فعالية** (*faʿʿāliyya*) is built on the native Arabic root *f-ʿ-l*, "to do, to act" (Lane). *Faʿʿāliyya* is a modern abstract noun from *faʿʿāl*, very active: effectiveness.
