## You'll want to know: छिद्र
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C432-BHOK432-02]; assesses=[] -->

**छिद्र** — *chidra* — "a small hole; a pore".

> बादलीच्या तळाला एक छिद्र आहे.
>
> *bādlīcyā taḷālā ek chidra āhe.*
>
> There is a small hole in the bottom of the bucket.

Neuter.

More formal and smaller than भोक.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**छिद्र** (*chidra*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *chidra*, "torn open; a hole" (Monier-Williams).
