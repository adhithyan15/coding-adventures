## You'll want to know: ਤਕਰਾਰ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C344-ARGUE344-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ਤਕਰਾਰ** — *takrār* — "a dispute; a row".

> ਅਸੀਂ ਪੈਸੇ ਲਈ ਤਕਰਾਰ ਨਹੀਂ ਕਰਦੇ ਹਾਂ।
>
> *asīṁ paise laī takrār nahīṁ karde hāṁ.*
>
> We don't have rows about money.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਤਕਰਾਰ** (*takrār*) is a loan, usually traced through Persian to Arabic *takrār*, "repetition".
