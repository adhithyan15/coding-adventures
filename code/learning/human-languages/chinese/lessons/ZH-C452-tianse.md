## You'll want to know: 天色
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C452-AFTERMEAL452-04]; assesses=[] -->

**天色** — *tiānsè* — "the light of the sky; how dark the sky is getting".

> **天色不早了我们坐车回酒店** *tiānsè bù zǎo le wǒmen zuò chē huí jiǔdiàn* — It's getting late, so we're heading back to the hotel.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**天色** (*tiānsè*) is built from 天 'the sky' and 色 'a colour', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *then* and *srik* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription).
