# സഹോദരി (sahōdari) — a sister

## Warm-up

## You'll want to know: സഹോദരി
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C554-MATAPITA554-01]; assesses=[] -->

**സഹോദരി** — *sahōdari* — "a sister".

> അവന്റെ സഹോദരി ഒരു നഴ്സാണ്.
>
> *avanṟe sahōdari oru naḻsāṇŭ.*
>
> His sister is a nurse.

At home, ചേച്ചി or അനിയത്തി.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**സഹോദരി** (*sahōdari*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *sahodarī*, the feminine of *sahodara*, "born of the same womb" (Monier-Williams).
