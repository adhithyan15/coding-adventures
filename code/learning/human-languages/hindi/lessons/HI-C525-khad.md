## You'll want to know: खाद
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C525-PAUDHA525-03]; assesses=[] -->

**खाद** — *khād* — "manure; fertiliser".

> खेत में हर साल खाद डालिए।
>
> *Khet meṁ har sāl khād ḍāliye.*
>
> Put manure on the field every year.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**खाद** (*khād*) is a tadbhava, inherited from Sanskrit *khāta*, "dug up" (CDIAL 3862). CDIAL gives the Hindi word as "manure".
