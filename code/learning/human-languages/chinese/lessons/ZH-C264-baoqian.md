## You'll want to know: bàoqiàn
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C264-REFUSE264-03]; assesses=[] -->

*bàoqiàn* — "sorry; regretful".

> *hěn bàoqiàn wǒ míngtiān méiyǒu shíjiān* — I'm very sorry, I don't have time tomorrow.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*bàoqiàn* is built from *bào* 'to hold' and *qiàn*, both inherited from Middle Chinese, *bawX* and *kheamH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to hold'. For the second it gives a sunken mouth, and in other readings 'not eating your fill'; the sense of being sorry is not given there, so how it came is not settled.
