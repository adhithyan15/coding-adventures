## You'll want to know: puisque
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C347-PUISQUE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**puisque** — "since, as (a known reason)".

> Puisque tu es là, tu peux m'aider.
>
> *Since you are here, you can help me.*

Takes the indicative: *puisque tu es là*. The reason is one both people already know. Before *il* or *elle*: *puisqu'il*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*puisque* is built from *puis* "then, afterwards" and *que*. *puis* is usually traced to a Vulgar Latin form of *post* "after" (Lewis & Short).
