## You'll want to know: مل
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C494-KAABA494-05]; assesses=[] -->

**مل** — *malla* — "to get bored (of)".

> مل الأطفال من الانتظار في السيارة.
>
> *malla al-aṭfāl min al-intiẓār fī as-sayyāra.*
>
> The children got bored of waiting in the car.

Present: يمل, *yamall*.

It takes من. The noun ملل, boredom, shares the root.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**مل** (*malla*) is built on the native Arabic root *m-l-l*, "to be weary of a thing" (Lane). Its last two root letters are the same, so they write as one doubled letter.

## What you've built
