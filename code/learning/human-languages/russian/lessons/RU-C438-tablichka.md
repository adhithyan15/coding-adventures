## You'll want to know: табличка
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C438-UKAZATEL-02]; assesses=[] -->

**табличка** (*tablíchka*) — "a sign, a plaque, a nameplate".

> На двери висела табличка «Закрыто».
>
> *Na dverí viséla tablíchka «Zakrýto».*
>
> A sign saying Closed hung on the door.

Feminine; a diminutive of *таблица*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*табличка* is a diminutive of *таблица* (EtymDB lists it without a source), which is usually traced to Latin *tabula* 'a board, a plank' (Lewis & Short).
