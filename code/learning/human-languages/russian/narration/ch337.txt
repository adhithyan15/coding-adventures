Russian, chapter 337: My point of view.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
точка зрения (tóchka zréniya) — a point of view.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a task, then the Russian for experience.

You'll want to know: точка зрения.
точка зрения (tóchka zréniya) — "a point of view".
С моей точки зрения, это хорошая идея. S moyéy tóchki zréniya, éto khoróshaya idéya. From my point of view, it is a good idea.
Feminine, after точка: genitive точки зрения. С моей точки зрения — from my point of view.

The word, taken apart.
точка is usually traced to the verb ткнуть "to poke, to jab": a dot is the mark a jab leaves (EtymDB lists Lithuanian taškas "a dot" beside it). зрение "sight" is built on the old verb зреть "to look", so a point of view is literally the point you look from.

Lesson 2 of 5.
позиция (pozítsiya) — a position, a stance.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for experience, then the Russian for a point of view.

You'll want to know: позиция.
позиция (pozítsiya) — "a position, a stance".
Моя позиция проста: надо больше читать. Moyá pozítsiya prostá: nádo bólshe chitát. My position is simple: we need to read more.
Feminine. Занимать позицию — to take a position. It is also a place in a list or a team: первая позиция (pozítsiya).

The word, taken apart.
позиция (pozítsiya) is usually traced, through western European languages, to Latin positiō "a placing, a position" (Lewis & Short), which is built on pōnere "to put, to place". A stance is where you have placed yourself.

Lesson 3 of 5.
взгляд (vzglyad) — a view, an opinion; a look.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a point of view, then the Russian for a position.

You'll want to know: взгляд.
взгляд (vzglyad) — "a view, an opinion; a look".
У нас разные взгляды на жизнь. U nas ráznyye vzglyády na zhizn. We have different views on life.
Masculine. На мой взгляд (vzglyad) — in my view. It is also a glance: быстрый взгляд (vzglyad).

The word, taken apart.
взгляд (vzglyad) is inherited from Proto-Slavic vŭzględŭ (EtymDB), built from the prefix vŭz- "up" and ględěti "to look", the verb that survives in глядеть. A view began as a look upwards.

Lesson 4 of 5.
заявлять (zayavlyát) — to declare, to state publicly.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a position, then the Russian for a view.

You'll want to know: заявлять.
заявлять (zayavlyát) — "to declare, to state publicly".
Директор заявляет, что завод не закроют. Diréktor zayavlyáyet, chto zavód ne zakróyut. The director states that the factory will not be closed.
Imperfective; perfective заявить (заявлю, заявишь). Formal: officials and newspapers заявляют, while among friends people говорят.

The word, taken apart.
заявлять (zayavlyát) is built from за- and являть "to show, to reveal", the verb behind явный "obvious". Явить comes through Old East Slavic javiti from Proto-Slavic aviti "to make visible" (EtymDB): to declare is to make a thing plain.

Lesson 5 of 5.
считаться (schitátsya) — to be considered, to be regarded as.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a view, then the Russian for to declare.

You'll want to know: считаться.
считаться (schitátsya) — "to be considered, to be regarded as".
Этот район считается самым тихим в городе. Étot rayón schitáyetsya sámym tíkhim v górode. This district is considered the quietest in the city.
An imperfective verb. It takes the instrumental: считается лучшим. Считаться с + instrumental — to take into account.

The word, taken apart.
считаться (schitátsya) is the reflexive of считать, built from с- and читать. Читать shares its root with число "number", from Proto-Slavic čislo, built on čisti (EtymDB): to be considered is to be counted as something.
