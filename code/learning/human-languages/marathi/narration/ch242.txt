Marathi, chapter 242: Leather, Metal, Brick, Fine thread, Ladder.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
चामडे (cāmḍe) — leather.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a lawn, then the Marathi for a footpath.

You'll want to know: चामडे.
चामडे — cāmḍe — "leather".

The word, taken apart.
चामडे (cāmḍe) is built on Marathi cām, "hide, leather", which comes from Sanskrit carman, "hide, skin" (CDIAL 4701).

Lesson 3 of 5.
वीट (vīṭ) — a brick.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for leather, then the Marathi for metal.

You'll want to know: वीट.
वीट — vīṭ — "a brick".

The word, taken apart.
वीट (vīṭ) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from iṣṭakā, "brick" (CDIAL 1600). CDIAL lists Marathi vīṭ beside īṭ.
