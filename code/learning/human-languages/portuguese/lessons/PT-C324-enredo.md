## You'll want to know: o enredo
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C324-CONTO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**o enredo** — "the plot".

> O enredo do filme é muito complicado.
>
> *The plot of the film is very complicated.*

Built on *enredar*, ‘to tangle up’.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*enredo* is built on *enredar* "to catch in a net, to entangle", from *rede* "a net", which is inherited from Latin *rēte* "a net" (Lewis & Short).
