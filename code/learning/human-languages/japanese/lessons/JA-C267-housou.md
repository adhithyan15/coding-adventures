## You'll want to know: ほうそう
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C267-MAIGO267-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ほうそう** — *hōsō* — "an announcement; a broadcast".

> **えきで まいごの ほうそうが ありました。** — *eki de maigo no hōsō ga arimashita* — "At the station they announced a lost child."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ほうそう** (*hōsō*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *hō*, "release" (Middle Chinese *pjangH*) and *sō*, "send" (*suwngH*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
