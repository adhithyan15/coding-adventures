## You'll want to know: persuāsiō, persuāsiōnis
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C369-HORTOR-03]; assesses=[] -->

**persuāsiō, persuāsiōnis** — "persuasion; a conviction".

> Persuāsiōne, nōn vī, vincere mālō.
>
> *I prefer to win by persuasion, not by force.*

*persuāsiō, persuāsiōnis* f., the noun of *persuādeō*. English *persuasion*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*persuāsiō* is the noun built on *persuādeō* "to persuade" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short), itself *per-* "thoroughly" with *suādeō* "to advise". English *persuasion* comes from it.
