## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਸੱਪ** (*sapp*) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from *sarpa*, "snake" (CDIAL 13271).
