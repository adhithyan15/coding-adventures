# पिच्छिलः (picchilaḥ) — slippery

## You'll want to know: पिच्छिलः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C436-ABHRA436-05]; assesses=[] -->

**पिच्छिलः** — *picchilaḥ* — "slippery".

> वृष्टेः अनन्तरं मार्गः पिच्छिलः भवति।
>
> *vṛṣṭeḥ anantaraṃ mārgaḥ picchilaḥ bhavati.*
>
> After the shower the road becomes slippery.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पिच्छिलः** (*picchilaḥ*) is the word MW gives for slimy, slippery; MW gives no derivation, so its origin is uncertain (MW).
