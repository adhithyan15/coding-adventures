## You'll want to know: der Betrug
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C285-BETRUG-02]; assesses=[] -->

**der Betrug** — "the fraud, the deception".

> Der Betrug kam nach einem Jahr ans Licht.
>
> *The fraud came to light after a year.*

No plural in most uses.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Betrug* comes from Middle High German *betroc*, Old High German *bitrog* "delusion" (EtymDB), from the same root as *betrügen*.
