Russian, chapter 495: Expecting a Baby.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
беременность (berémennost) — pregnancy.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to be unfaithful, then the Russian for a parting.

You'll want to know: беременность.
беременность (berémennost) — "pregnancy".
Во время беременности она много гуляла. Vo vrémya berémennosti oná mnógo gulyála. During her pregnancy she walked a lot.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
беременность (berémennost) is built on беременная 'pregnant', from the older беремя 'a load, an armful', usually linked to брать 'to take'; EtymDB has no entry.

Lesson 2 of 5.
роддом (roddóm) — a maternity hospital.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a parting, then the Russian for pregnancy.

You'll want to know: роддом.
роддом (roddóm) — "a maternity hospital".
Папа встретил маму и сестрёнку у роддома. Pápa vstrétil mámu i sestryónku u roddóma. Dad met Mum and my baby sister at the maternity hospital.
Masculine. Short for родильный дом.

The word, taken apart.
роддом (roddóm) is a clipped compound of родильный 'birth-', from родить 'to give birth', and дом 'house'; EtymDB has no entry.

Lesson 3 of 5.
декрет (dekrét) — maternity leave; a decree.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for pregnancy, then the Russian for a maternity hospital.

You'll want to know: декрет.
декрет (dekrét) — "maternity leave; a decree".
После рождения сына она год была в декрете. Pósle rozhdéniya sýna oná god bylá v dekréte. After her son was born she was on maternity leave for a year.
Masculine. Colloquial for декретный отпуск. Its older sense is a decree.

The word, taken apart.
декрет (dekrét) was borrowed from Latin decretum 'a decree' (EtymDB), from dēcernere 'to decide' (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 4 of 5.
коляска (kolyáska) — a pram; a wheelchair.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a maternity hospital, then the Russian for maternity leave.

You'll want to know: коляска.
коляска (kolyáska) — "a pram; a wheelchair".
Каждый день мы гуляем с коляской в парке. Kázhdyy den my gulyáyem s kolyáskoy v párke. Every day we go for a walk in the park with the pram.
Feminine. Детская коляска (kolyáska) — a pram; инвалидная коляска (kolyáska) — a wheelchair.

The word, taken apart.
EtymDB lists коляска (kolyáska) without a source; it is usually traced through Polish kolaska to the root of колесо 'wheel'.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ползать (pólzat) — to crawl.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for maternity leave, then the Russian for a pram.

You'll want to know: ползать.
ползать (pólzat) — "to crawl".
Наш сын уже ползает по всей квартире. Nash syn uzhé pólzayet po vsey kvartíre. Our son already crawls all over the flat.
Imperfective, in many directions; the one-way verb is ползти.

The word, taken apart.
ползать (pólzat) is inherited from Proto-Slavic pьlzati (EtymDB).
