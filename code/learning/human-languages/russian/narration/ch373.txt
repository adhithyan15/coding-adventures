Russian, chapter 373: Playing it safe.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
рисковать (riskovát) — to take a risk, to risk.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a chance, then the Russian for a risk.

You'll want to know: рисковать.
рисковать (riskovát) — "to take a risk, to risk".
Я не хочу рисковать своими деньгами. Ya ne khochú riskovát svoími dengámi. I do not want to risk my money.
Imperfective; the present is рискую, рискуешь; perfective рискнуть. Рисковать + instrumental — to risk something.

The word, taken apart.
рисковать (riskovát) "to risk" (EtymDB) is built on риск, which is usually traced, through French risque, to Italian risco.

Lesson 2 of 5.
опасаться (opasátsya) — to fear, to be wary of.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a risk, then the Russian for to take a risk.

You'll want to know: опасаться.
опасаться (opasátsya) — "to fear, to be wary of".
Врачи опасаются новой эпидемии. Vrachí opasáyutsya nóvoy epidémii. Doctors fear a new epidemic.
Imperfective only. It takes the genitive: опасаться (opasátsya) дождя. More formal than бояться.

The word, taken apart.
опасаться (opasátsya) is usually traced to о- and пасти "to guard, to watch over", inherited from Proto-Slavic pasti (EtymDB): to be wary is to keep watch. Опасный "dangerous" is built on it.

Lesson 3 of 5.
избегать (izbegát) — to avoid.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to take a risk, then the Russian for to fear.

You'll want to know: избегать.
избегать (izbegát) — "to avoid".
Он избегает трудных разговоров. On izbegáyet trúdnykh razgovórov. He avoids difficult conversations.
Imperfective; perfective избежать, conjugated like бежать. It takes the genitive: избегать (izbegát) ошибок.

The word, taken apart.
избегать (izbegát) is built from из- "out of" and бегать "to run", from Proto-Slavic běžati: to run out of a thing’s way.

Lesson 4 of 5.
угрожать (ugrozhát) — to threaten.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to fear, then the Russian for to avoid.

You'll want to know: угрожать.
угрожать (ugrozhát) — "to threaten".
Сильный ветер угрожает старым деревьям. Sílnyy véter ugrozháyet stárym derévyam. The strong wind threatens the old trees.
Imperfective. Угрожать кому чем: the person threatened goes in the dative. The noun is угроза, a threat.

The word, taken apart.
угрожать (ugrozhát) is built from у- and гроза "a thunderstorm", which in older Russian also meant terror and threat; з softens to ж in the verb.

Lesson 5 of 5.
предотвращать (predotvrashchát) — to prevent.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to avoid, then the Russian for to threaten.

You'll want to know: предотвращать.
предотвращать (predotvrashchát) — "to prevent".
Эти правила предотвращают аварии. Éti právila predotvrashcháyut avárii. These rules prevent accidents.
Imperfective; the perfective is предотвратить, with предотвращу, предотвратишь. Formal.

The word, taken apart.
предотвращать (predotvrashchát) is built from пред- "before", от- "away" and вращать "to turn", the Church Slavonic twin of native ворочать: to turn a danger away in advance. The -ра- and the щ are both Church Slavonic shapes.
