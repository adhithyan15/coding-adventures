> ै

**ai**, roughly the vowel of English *cat* in most of Hindi. It sits **above** the
head-line as **two** short strokes.

Its single-stroke relative is the **e** mātrā, and the difference is exactly one
stroke — one stroke for *e*, two for *ai*. Miscount and you have a different word.

This one is worth the attention because of where it turns up: **है**, the word for
*is*, ends in it, and that word appears in almost every sentence you will ever
read.
