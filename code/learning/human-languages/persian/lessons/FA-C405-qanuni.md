## You'll want to know: قانونی
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C405-QANUNI-01]; assesses=[] -->

**قانونی** (*qânuni*) — "legal, lawful".

> این کار کاملا قانونی است.
>
> *in kâr kâmelan qânuni ast.*
>
> This is completely legal.

*Qânun* "law" + *-i*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**قانونی** is *qânun* "law" with the Persian adjective suffix *-i*. *Qânun* comes from Arabic *qānūn*, which was taken from Greek *kanōn* "rule, measuring rod".
