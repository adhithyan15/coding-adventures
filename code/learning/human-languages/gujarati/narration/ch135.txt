Gujarati, chapter 135: Thirsty, Ill, Fresh, Ripe, Raw.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
તરસ્યું (tarasyũ) — thirsty.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for sad, then the Gujarati for hungry.
