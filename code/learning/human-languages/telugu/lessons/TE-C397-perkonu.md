# పేర్కొను (pērkonu) — to mention; to state

## You'll want to know: పేర్కొను
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C397-SANKSIPTAM397-04]; assesses=[] -->

**పేర్కొను** — *pērkonu* — "to mention; to state".

> ఆ ఉత్తరంలో అతను తన కారణాలు పేర్కొన్నాడు.
>
> *ā uttaraṁlō atanu tana kāraṇālu pērkonnāḍu.*
>
> In that letter he stated his reasons.

Past (I …): పేర్కొన్నాను, *pērkonnānu*.

Formal and written; in talk, చెప్పు.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**పేర్కొను** (*pērkonu*) is native Dravidian, a compound of *pēru*, "a name" (DEDR 4410), and *konu*, "to take" (DEDR 2151): to name a thing.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
