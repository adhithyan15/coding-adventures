# வெளியிடு (veḷiyiṭu) — to bring out; to publish; to release

## You'll want to know: வெளியிடு
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C306-THALAIPPU306-05]; assesses=[] -->

**வெளியிடு** — *veḷiyiṭu* — "to bring out; to publish; to release".

> நாளிதழ் அவருடைய கடிதத்தை வெளியிட்டது.
>
> *nāḷitaḻ avaruṭaiya kaṭitattai veḷiyiṭṭatu.*
>
> The newspaper published his letter.

Past (I …): வெளியிட்டேன், *veḷiyiṭṭēṉ*.

வெளி, outside + இடு, to put: to put out.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**வெளியிடு** (*veḷiyiṭu*) is a Tamil compound built from வெளி, "outside, the open, public" (DEDR 5498), and இடு, "to put".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
