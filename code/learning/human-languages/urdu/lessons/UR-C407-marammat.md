## You'll want to know: مرمت
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C407-MARAMMAT407-04]; assesses=[] -->

**مرمت** — *marammat* — "a repair".

> مستری نے پنکھے کی مرمت کی۔
>
> *mistrī ne pankhe kī marammat kī.*
>
> The mechanic repaired the fan.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**مرمت** (*marammat*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *marammat*, "a mending"; **مرمت کرنا** is to repair.
