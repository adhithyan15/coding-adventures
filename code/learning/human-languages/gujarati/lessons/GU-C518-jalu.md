## You'll want to know: જાળું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C518-PASTI518-02]; assesses=[] -->

**જાળું** — *jāḷũ* — "a cobweb".

> છતના ખૂણામાં કરોળિયાનું જાળું છે.
>
> *chatnā khūṇāmā̃ karoḷiyānũ jāḷũ che.*
>
> There is a spider's web in the corner of the ceiling.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**જાળું** (*jāḷũ*) is inherited from Sanskrit *jāla*, "a net", and CDIAL lists the Gujarati word, "a net, a cobweb" (CDIAL 5213).
