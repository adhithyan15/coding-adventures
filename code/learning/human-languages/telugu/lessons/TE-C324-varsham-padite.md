# వర్షం పడితే (varṣaṁ paḍitē) — if it rains, and if not

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[TE-GRAMMAR-C324-TE-CONDITIONAL-01, TE-GRAMMAR-C324-OKAVELA-TE-01, TE-LEX-C176-KARANAM176-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "if you come, I will come too" (*nuvvu vastē nēnu kūḍā vastānu*). Then "otherwise".
(*lēkapōtē*.)

## You'll want to know: a real condition
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-GRAMMAR-C324-REAL-CONDITION-01]; assesses=[TE-LEX-C170-SAMAYAM170-01, TE-LEX-C312-PANI312-03, TE-LEX-C04-REEPU-KALUDDAAM-01] -->

> **రేపు వర్షం పడితే నేను రాను.**
>
> *rēpu varṣaṁ paḍitē nēnu rānu.* — "If it rains tomorrow, I will not come."

> **సమయం ఉంటే సినిమాకి వెళ్దాం.**
>
> *samayaṁ uṇṭē sinimāki veḷdāṁ.* — "If there is time, let's go to a film."

A real condition is one that may well happen. The main verb is the form you know for what will happen
(**వస్తాను**, **వెళ్తాను**), its "not" (**రాను** (*rānu*, "I will not come")), or a suggestion with **-దాం**
(**వెళ్దాం** (*veḷdāṁ*, "let's go")).

## You'll want to know: -అకపోతే, if not
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-GRAMMAR-C324-NEGATIVE-CONDITION-01]; assesses=[TE-LEX-C90-LEAVE-02, TE-LEX-C105-LOC105-02, TE-LEX-C176-KARANAM176-01, TE-GRAMMAR-C321-MISSED-01] -->

> **నువ్వు త్వరగా రాకపోతే, బస్సు వెళ్ళిపోతుంది.**
>
> *nuvvu tvaragā rākapōtē, bassu veḷḷipōtundi.* — "If you do not come quickly, the bus will go."

**-అక** + **పోతే**: **రాకపోతే**, "if (you) don't come"; **చేయకపోతే**, "if (you) don't do"; **తినకపోతే**,
"if (you) don't eat". **లేకపోతే**, which you know as "otherwise", is the same shape on **లే-**, "not be": "if
not".

> **త్వరగా రా, లేకపోతే బస్సు వెళ్ళిపోతుంది.**
>
> *tvaragā rā, lēkapōtē bassu veḷḷipōtundi.* — "Come quickly, otherwise the bus will go."
