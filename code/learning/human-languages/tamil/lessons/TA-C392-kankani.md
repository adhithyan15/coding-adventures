# கண்காணி (kaṇkāṇi) — to monitor; to keep watch over

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[TA-LEX-C392-ULAL392-03, TA-LEX-C392-ULAL392-04] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Tamil for frankly, then the Tamil for indirectly.

## You'll want to know: கண்காணி
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C392-ULAL392-05]; assesses=[] -->

**கண்காணி** — *kaṇkāṇi* — "to monitor; to keep watch over".

> தேர்தல் அதிகாரிகள் வாக்குச்சாவடிகளைக் கண்காணித்தார்கள்.
>
> *tērtal atikārikaḷ vākkuccāvaṭikaḷaik kaṇkāṇittārkaḷ.*
>
> The election officials kept watch over the polling booths.

Past (I …): கண்காணித்தேன், *kaṇkāṇittēṉ*.

கண், eye + காணி, the watcher: கண்காணிப்பு, supervision.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**கண்காணி** (*kaṇkāṇi*) is native: *kaṇ*, "eye" (DEDR 1159), and *kāṇi*, "one who sees", from *kāṇ*, "to see, investigate" (DEDR 1443): to keep an eye on.
