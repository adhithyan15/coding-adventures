## You'll want to know: محكمة
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C305-MAHKAMA305-01]; assesses=[] -->

**محكمة** — *maḥkama* — "a court of law".

> في المدينة محكمة كبيرة.
>
> *fī al-madīna maḥkama kabīra.*
>
> There is a big court in the city.

Plural: محاكم, *maḥākim*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**محكمة** (*maḥkama*) is built on the native Arabic root *ḥ-k-m* (Lane), "to restrain from wrong, to judge". The *mafʿala* pattern names a place: "the place of judging".
