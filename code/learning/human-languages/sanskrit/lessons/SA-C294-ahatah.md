## You'll want to know: आहतः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C294-SANGHATTA294-04]; assesses=[] -->

**आहतः** — *āhataḥ* — "hurt; injured".

> सः वृद्धः आहतः अस्ति।
>
> *saḥ vṛddhaḥ āhataḥ asti.*
>
> That old man is hurt.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**आहतः** (*āhataḥ*) is built from *ā* and the root √han, "to strike"; MW gives *āhata* as struck, beaten, hurt (MW).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
