## You'll want to know: উদ্দেশ্য
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C331-UDDESHYO331-01]; assesses=[] -->

**উদ্দেশ্য** — *uddeshyô* — "a purpose; an aim".

> এই মিটিংয়ের উদ্দেশ্য কী?
>
> *ei miṭingyer uddeshyô kī?*
>
> What is this meeting for?

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**উদ্দেশ্য** (*uddeshyô*) is Sanskrit *uddeśya*, the thing one points at or has in view (Monier-Williams). Bengali uses it for the purpose behind a plan or a meeting.
