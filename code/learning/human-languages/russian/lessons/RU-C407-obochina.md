## You'll want to know: обочина
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C407-OBGON-05]; assesses=[] -->

**обочина** (*obóchina*) — "a hard shoulder, a roadside".

> Сломанная машина стояла на обочине.
>
> *Slómannaya mashína stoyála na obóchine.*
>
> A broken-down car was standing on the hard shoulder.

Feminine. *Съехать на обочину* — to pull over onto the shoulder.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*обочина* is usually traced to the old adverb *обочь* 'alongside', built on *бок* 'a side': the strip at the side of the road. EtymDB does not list the word, so the line is hedged.
