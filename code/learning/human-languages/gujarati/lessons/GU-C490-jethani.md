## You'll want to know: જેઠાણી
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C490-SASRA490-04]; assesses=[] -->

**જેઠાણી** — *jeṭhāṇī* — "a husband's elder brother's wife".

> જેઠાણી રોજ મને રસોડામાં મદદ કરે છે.
>
> *jeṭhāṇī roj mane rasoḍāmā̃ madad kare che.*
>
> My husband's elder brother's wife helps me in the kitchen every day.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**જેઠાણી** (*jeṭhāṇī*) goes back to a reconstructed *\*jyēṣṭhajāni-*, "senior wife, elder brother's wife", built from *jyēṣṭha*, "eldest", and *jāni*, "wife"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati word (CDIAL 5287).
