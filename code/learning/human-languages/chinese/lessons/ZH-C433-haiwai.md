## You'll want to know: 海外
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C433-OVERSEAS433-02]; assesses=[] -->

**海外** — *hǎiwài* — "overseas; beyond the sea".

> *hěn duō hǎiwài yóukè zhù zài zhè jiā jiǔdiàn* — A lot of visitors from overseas stay at this hotel.

The new character, in pieces: 海 — ten strokes in two pieces: on the left the water piece of 洗, three strokes; on the right 每 měi, seven strokes. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**海外** (*hǎiwài*) is built from 海 'the sea' and 外 'outside', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *xojX* and *ngwajH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first, after an older dictionary, as the great pool that takes in a hundred rivers, and the second 'outside'.
