## You'll want to know: diskriminieren
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C360-VORURTEIL-04]; assesses=[] -->

**diskriminieren** — "to discriminate against".

> Die Firma darf niemanden wegen seiner Herkunft diskriminieren.
>
> *The firm may not discriminate against anyone because of their origin.*

Perfekt: *hat diskriminiert*. It takes a direct object: *jemanden diskriminieren*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*diskriminieren* is a learned borrowing from Latin *discrīmināre* "to divide, to separate", from *discrīmen* "that which divides" (Lewis & Short): you set people apart.
