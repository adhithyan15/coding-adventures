## You'll want to know: bàituō
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C457-HELP457-02]; assesses=[] -->

*bàituō* — "to ask a favour of someone".

> *bàituō nǐ bāng wǒ ná xíngli* — Could you carry my luggage for me, please?

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*bàituō* is built from *bài* 'to bow' and *tuō* 'to entrust', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *peajH* and *thak* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'to entrust'.
