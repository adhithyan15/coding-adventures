## You'll want to know: নৌকা
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C271-NOUKA-01]; assesses=[BN-LEX-C66-NATURE66-03, BN-LEX-C237-ACT237-04, BN-GRAMMAR-C264-PAST-PERSON-01, BN-LEX-C128-THINGS128-03] -->

**নৌকা** — *noukā* — "a boat".

> **আমরা নৌকায় নদী পার হলাম।**
>
> *āmrā noukāy nôdī pār hôlām.* — "We crossed the river by boat."

**নৌকায়**, with **-য়**, is "in a boat, by boat", as **বাসে** is "by bus". A **নৌকা** is small, rowed or sailed on a river; the big ship is **জাহাজ**, which you know.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**নৌকা** (*noukā*) is a tatsama: Sanskrit *naukā*, which Monier-Williams gives as "a small boat or ship". Beside it Monier-Williams lists *nau*, "a ship, boat".
