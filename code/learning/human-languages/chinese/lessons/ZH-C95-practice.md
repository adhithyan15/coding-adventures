## The exchange
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-PERFORMANCE-WHY-01]; assesses=[ZH-LEX-C95-WEISHENME-01, ZH-LEX-C95-DUCHE-01, ZH-LEX-C95-YOUSHI-01, ZH-LEX-C95-WANG-01, ZH-LEX-C83-WAIT83-05] -->

> **A:** *nǐ wèishénme chídào* — Why are you late?
>
> **B:** *zǎoshang dǔchē* — The traffic this morning.
>
> **A:** 你明天去吗
>
> *nǐ míngtiān qù ma* — Are you going tomorrow?
>
> **B:** 我不去
>
> *wǒ bú qù* — I'm not going.
>
> **A:** 为什么
>
> *wèishénme* — Why?
>
> **B:** *wǒ yǒushì* — I've got something on.
>
> **A:** *nǐ de zuòyè ne* — And your homework?
>
> **B:** *wǒ wàng le* — I forgot.

为什么 can stand alone as a whole question, as A's fifth line does. Each answer here is a reason with nothing in front of it; the next chapter gives the word that introduces one.

## What you've built — asking why
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ZH-PERFORMANCE-WHY-01, ZH-SCRIPT-WEI-FOR-01, ZH-LEX-C95-WEISHENME-01, ZH-LEX-C95-YOUSHI-01, ZH-LEX-C95-DUCHE-01] -->

- **你为什么不去** *nǐ wèishénme bú qù* — why aren't you going?
- **为什么** *wèishénme* — why?
- *wǒ yǒushì* — something has come up.
- *zǎoshang dǔchē* — the traffic this morning.

**Listen.** *Nǐ wèishénme bù chī fàn?*

**Speak.** Answer it with any reason you have, then ask somebody why.
