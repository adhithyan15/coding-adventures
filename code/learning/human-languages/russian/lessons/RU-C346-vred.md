## You'll want to know: вред
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C346-PLYUSY-04]; assesses=[] -->

**вред** (*vred*) — "harm".

> Курение приносит вред здоровью.
>
> *Kuréniye prinósit vred zdoróvyu.*
>
> Smoking does harm to your health.

Masculine. *Без вреда для* + genitive. *Вредный* (harmful) is built on it.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**вред** was borrowed from Church Slavonic *vrědŭ*, from Proto-Slavic *\*verdŭ* (EtymDB). The *-ре-* is the Church Slavonic shape; the native Russian development of the same word survives in dialect *веред* "a boil".
