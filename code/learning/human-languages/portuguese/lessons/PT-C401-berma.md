## You'll want to know: a berma
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C401-AUTOESTRADA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**a berma** — "the hard shoulder, the verge".

> O carro parou na berma com um problema no motor.
>
> *The car stopped on the hard shoulder with an engine problem.*

In Brazil: *o acostamento*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*berma* was borrowed from French *berme*, which came from Dutch *berm* 'bank, raised edge' (EtymDB).
