Lesson 1 of 7.
कादंबरी (kādambarī) — a novel.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for thrilling, then the Marathi for to wrestle with.

You'll want to know: कादंबरी.
कादंबरी — kādambarī — "a novel".
मी सुट्टीत एक मोठी कादंबरी वाचली. mī suṭṭīt ek moṭhī kādambarī vāclī. I read a long novel during the holidays.
Feminine.
कादंबरीकार, a novelist.

The word, taken apart.
कादंबरी (kādambarī) comes from Sanskrit Kādambarī, the title of a celebrated prose romance (Monier-Williams); Marathi made the title the word for every novel.

अविस्मरणीय — avismaraṇīy — "unforgettable".
ती सहल माझ्यासाठी अविस्मरणीय होती. tī sahal mājhyāsāṭhī avismaraṇīy hotī. That trip was unforgettable for me.

The word, taken apart.
अविस्मरणीय (avismaraṇīy) is built from the Sanskrit prefix a-, 'not', and vismaraṇīya, 'to be forgotten' (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 5 of 7.
चिरडणे (ciraḍṇe) — to crush.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for wondrous, then the Marathi for unforgettable.

You'll want to know: चिरडणे.
चिरडणे — ciraḍṇe — "to crush".
गोष्टीत हत्तीने राक्षसाला पायाखाली चिरडलं. goṣṭīt hattīne rākṣasālā pāyākhālī ciraḍlaṁ. In the story the elephant crushed the ogre underfoot.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने चिरडलं, tyāne ciraḍlaṁ.
Transitive: the one crushed takes -ला.

The word, taken apart.
चिरडणे (ciraḍṇe) is a Marathi verb of crushing; its origin is not settled.
