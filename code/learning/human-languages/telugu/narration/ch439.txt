Telugu, chapter 439: Booking a Berth.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
రిజర్వేషను (rijarvēṣanu) — a reservation.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a route, then the Telugu for to set foot in.

You'll want to know: రిజర్వేషను.
రిజర్వేషను — rijarvēṣanu — "a reservation".
పండుగ రోజుల్లో రైలులో రిజర్వేషను దొరకడం కష్టం. paṇḍuga rōjullō railulō rijarvēṣanu dorakaḍaṁ kaṣṭaṁ. In the festival season it is hard to get a reservation on the train.
Like most English loans in Telugu it ends in -ఉ: రిజర్వేషను (rijarvēṣanu). It goes with చేయు: రిజర్వేషను (rijarvēṣanu) చేయించాను, I had a seat reserved.

The word, taken apart.
రిజర్వేషను (rijarvēṣanu) is a loan from English reservation, a seat kept for one passenger. Telugu adds its usual -ఉ to the final consonant.

నాకు కింది బెర్తు కావాలి. nāku kindi bertu kāvāli. I would like the lower berth.
The berths on a sleeper coach are కింది, the lower, మధ్య, the middle, and పై, the upper.

The word, taken apart.

Lesson 4 of 5.
బోగీ (bōgī) — a railway coach.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a booking, then the Telugu for a sleeper berth.

You'll want to know: బోగీ.
బోగీ — bōgī — "a railway coach".
మా బోగీ రైలు చివర ఉంది. mā bōgī railu civara undi. Our coach is at the end of the train.
Plural: బోగీలు, bōgīlu.
The coaches of a train are its బోగీలు; the number of yours is on the ticket.

The word, taken apart.
బోగీ (bōgī) is a loan from English bogie, which in Indian English names a railway carriage.

Lesson 5 of 5.
కిరాయి (kirāyi) — a fare; hire charge.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a sleeper berth, then the Telugu for a railway coach.

You'll want to know: కిరాయి.
కిరాయి — kirāyi — "a fare; hire charge".
ఆటో కిరాయి ఎంత? āṭō kirāyi enta? How much is the auto fare?
It is the charge for hiring a vehicle, and also the rent of a room or a house.

The word, taken apart.
కిరాయి (kirāyi) is a loan from Arabic kirāʾ, "hire, rent", which reached Telugu through Persian and Hindustani kirāya.
