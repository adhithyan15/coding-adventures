## You'll want to know: ফোলা
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C475-KHOTO475-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ফোলা** — *pholā* — "to swell".

> মচকানোর পরে গোড়ালিটা ফুলে গেল।
>
> *môchkānor pôre goṛāliṭā phule gelô.*
>
> After the sprain the ankle swelled up.

Past (he/she …): সেই রোগীর গাল ফুলল, *sei rogīr gāl phullô*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ফোলা** (*pholā*) is listed by CDIAL for Bengali, as swelling and blossoming, under Sanskrit *phullati*, "opens out like a flower" (CDIAL 9093).
