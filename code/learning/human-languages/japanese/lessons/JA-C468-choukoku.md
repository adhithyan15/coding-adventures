## You'll want to know: ちょうこく
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C468-WORKS468-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ちょうこく** — *chōkoku* — "a sculpture; carving".

> **こうえんに いしの ちょうこくが あります。** — *kōen ni ishi no chōkoku ga arimasu* — "There is a stone sculpture in the park."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ちょうこく** (*chōkoku*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *chō*, "carve" (Middle Chinese *tew*) and *koku*, "time" (*khok*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
