## You'll want to know: जमाव
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C298-GONDHAL298-04]; assesses=[] -->

**जमाव** — *jamāv* — "a mob; a gathered crowd".

> अपघाताच्या ठिकाणी मोठा जमाव जमला.
>
> *apghātācyā ṭhikāṇī moṭhā jamāv jamlā.*
>
> A large crowd gathered at the scene of the accident.

Masculine.

Built on जमणे, to gather. गर्दी is any crowd; जमाव is one that has gathered round something.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**जमाव** (*jamāv*) is built on *jamṇe*, 'to gather', which CDIAL lists, as Marathi 'to collect', under Sanskrit *yamyate* (CDIAL 10428).
