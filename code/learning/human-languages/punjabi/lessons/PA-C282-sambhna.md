## You'll want to know: ਸਾਂਭਣਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C282-BAGS282-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ਸਾਂਭਣਾ** — *sāmbhṇā* — "to keep safe; to look after".

> ਮੈਂ ਟਿਕਟਾਂ ਸਾਂਭਾਂਗਾ।
>
> *maiṁ ṭikṭāṁ sāmbhāṅgā.*
>
> I will keep the tickets safe.

Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਟਿਕਟ ਸਾਂਭੀ, *maiṁ ṭikaṭ sāmbhī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਸਾਂਭਣਾ** (*sāmbhṇā*) is inherited from Sanskrit *saṁbhṛta*, "collected"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *sāmbhṇā*, "to support, take care of", under it (CDIAL 12970).
