## You'll want to know: der Ehemann
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C299-EHE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**der Ehemann** — "the husband".

> Ihr Ehemann arbeitet als Koch.
>
> *Her husband works as a cook.*

Plural: *die Ehemänner*. In speech, *mein Mann* is more common.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Ehemann* joins *Ehe* "marriage", Middle High German *ē* (EtymDB), and the inherited *Mann* "man".
