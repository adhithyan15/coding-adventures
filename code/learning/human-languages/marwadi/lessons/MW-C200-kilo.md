## You'll want to know: किलो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C200-WEIGH200-03]; assesses=[] -->

**किलो** — *kilo* — "a kilo".

> दो किलो चावळ लावो।
>
> *do kilo chāvaḷ lāvo.*
>
> Bring two kilos of rice.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**किलो** (*kilo*) is borrowed from English *kilo*, short for kilogram.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for reading notices and messages.
