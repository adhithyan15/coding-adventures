## You'll want to know: голосовать
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C366-VYBORY-02]; assesses=[] -->

**голосовать** (*golosovát*) — "to vote".

> Мы голосовали за нового мэра.
>
> *My golosováli za nóvogo méra.*
>
> We voted for a new mayor.

Imperfective; the present is *голосую, голосуешь*; the perfective is *проголосовать*. *Голосовать за* + accusative, *против* + genitive.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**голосовать** is built on *голос* "voice", inherited through Old East Slavic *golosŭ* from Proto-Slavic *\*golsŭ* "voice" (EtymDB). The *-оло-* is the native Russian shape: to vote is to give your voice.
