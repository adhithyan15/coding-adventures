## You'll want to know: le romancier
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C519-ROMAN-01]; assesses=[] -->

**le romancier** — "novelist".

> Ce romancier écrit un livre par an.
>
> *This novelist writes a book a year.*

A woman is *la romancière*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*le romancier* is built on *roman* "novel", from Old French *romanz*, a story told in the common Romance tongue rather than in Latin.
