## You'll want to know: 进来
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C183-INOUT183-02]; assesses=[] -->

**进来** — *jìnlái* — "to come inside; to come in".

> **请进来你的房间在这儿** *qǐng jìnlái nǐ de fángjiān zài zhèr* — Come on in: your room is here.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**进来** (*jìnlái*) is built from 进 'to go in' and 来 'to come', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *tsinH* and *loj* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription): in and towards here.
