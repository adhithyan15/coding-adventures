## You'll want to know: धक्का
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C452-DHAKKA452-01]; assesses=[] -->

**धक्का** — *dhakkā* — "a shock; a push".

> ती बातमी ऐकून आम्हाला मोठा धक्का बसला.
>
> *tī bātmī aikūn āmhālā moṭhā dhakkā baslā.*
>
> Hearing that news gave us a big shock.

Masculine.

धक्का बसणे, to get a shock, with the dative; धक्का देणे, to push.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**धक्का** (*dhakkā*) is inherited from the expressive root *\*dhakk*, "push, strike" (CDIAL 6701), under which CDIAL lists Marathi *dhakā*, "push".
