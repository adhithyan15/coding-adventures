Urdu, chapter 412: Towns and Villages.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
قصبہ (qasbā) — a small town.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a basement, then the Urdu for beside.

You'll want to know: قصبہ.
قصبہ — qasbā — "a small town".
ہمارا قصبہ شہر سے دور ہے۔ hamārā qasbā shahr se dūr hai. Our little town is a long way from the city.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
قصبہ (qasbā) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic qaṣaba, "a chief town, a citadel"; in Urdu it is a town smaller than a شہر.

Lesson 2 of 5.
آبادی (ābādī) — a population; a settlement.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for beside, then the Urdu for a small town.

You'll want to know: آبادی.
آبادی — ābādī — "a population; a settlement".
اس قصبے کی آبادی کم ہے۔ is qasbe kī ābādī kam hai. Not many people live in this little town.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
آبادی (ābādī) is built on ābād, "inhabited, flourishing", usually traced to Persian ābād, with the ending -ī.

Lesson 4 of 5.
دیہات (dehāt) — the countryside; villages.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a population, then the Urdu for an inhabitant.

You'll want to know: دیہات.
دیہات — dehāt — "the countryside; villages".
دیہات میں لوگ کھیتوں میں کام کرتے ہیں۔ dehāt meṅ log khetoṅ meṅ kām karte haiṅ. In the countryside people work in the fields.
Masculine, used in the plural.

The word, taken apart.
دیہات (dehāt) is built on Persian deh, "a village", with the Arabic plural ending -āt: it is usually traced to Persian dehāt, "villages".

Lesson 5 of 5.
پڑوس (paṛos) — next door; the neighbourhood.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for an inhabitant, then the Urdu for the countryside.

You'll want to know: پڑوس.
پڑوس — paṛos — "next door; the neighbourhood".
ہمارے پڑوس میں ایک درزی رہتا ہے۔ hamāre paṛos meṅ ek darzī rahtā hai. A tailor lives next door to us.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
پڑوس (paṛos) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin paṛos, "the neighbourhood", under Sanskrit prátivēśa (CDIAL 8598); پڑوسی, "a neighbour", belongs with it.
