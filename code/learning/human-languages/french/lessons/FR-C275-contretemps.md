## You'll want to know: le contretemps
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C275-DELAI-04]; assesses=[] -->

**le contretemps** — "the hitch, the mishap".

> Un petit contretemps nous a mis en retard.
>
> *A small hitch made us late.*

*À contretemps* — at the wrong moment, out of step.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*contretemps* joins *contre* "against", from Latin *contrā*, and *temps* "time", from Latin *tempus* (Lewis & Short): something that goes against the timing.
