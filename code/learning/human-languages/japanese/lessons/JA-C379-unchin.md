## You'll want to know: うんちん

**うんちん** — *unchin* — "a fare".

> **みなとまでの うんちんは いくらですか。** — *minato made no unchin wa ikura desu ka* — "How much is the fare to the harbour?"

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**うんちん** (*unchin*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *un*, "carry" (Middle Chinese *hjunH*) and *chin*, "fare" (*nrimH*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
