Punjabi, chapter 117: Rubbish, Basket, Box, Clay water pot, Candle.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ਕੂੜਾ (kūṛā) — rubbish.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for a bucket, then the Punjabi for a broom.

You'll want to know: ਕੂੜਾ.
ਕੂੜਾ — kūṛā — "rubbish".

The word, taken apart.
ਕੂੜਾ (kūṛā) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from kūṭa, "heap, multitude" (CDIAL 3392). CDIAL takes the Sanskrit word, probably, from Dravidian; Punjabi kūṛā is "sweepings, dirt".

ਟੋਕਰੀ (ṭokrī) is a tadbhava, a word inherited and reshaped by sound change. It comes from ṭokka, "basket", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 5477). CDIAL lists Punjabi ṭokrā, with an added -r-.

Lesson 3 of 5.
ਡੱਬਾ (ḍabbā) — a box.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for rubbish, then the Punjabi for a basket.

You'll want to know: ਡੱਬਾ.
ਡੱਬਾ — ḍabbā — "a box".

The word, taken apart.
ਡੱਬਾ (ḍabbā) matches Hindi ḍabbā, "box", which CDIAL lists under a reconstructed ḍabba, "box", a word it calls non-Aryan, perhaps Dravidian (CDIAL 5528). Its Punjabi forms there are ḍabb and ḍibbā.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ਮੋਮਬੱਤੀ (mombattī) — a candle.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for a box, then the Punjabi for a clay water pot.

You'll want to know: ਮੋਮਬੱਤੀ.
ਮੋਮਬੱਤੀ — mombattī — "a candle".

The word, taken apart.
ਮੋਮਬੱਤੀ (mombattī) joins words from two sources: mom, "wax", usually traced to Persian mom, and battī, "wick, lamp", from Sanskrit varti (CDIAL 11359). A candle is a "wax lamp".
