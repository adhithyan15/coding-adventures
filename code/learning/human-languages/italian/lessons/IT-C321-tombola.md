## You'll want to know: la tombola
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C321-PRESEPE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**la tombola** — "the tombola, the family bingo game".

> Dopo cena giochiamo a tombola.
>
> *After dinner we play tombola.*

Numbers from 1 to 90; *fare tombola* is to fill the whole card.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*tombola* is usually traced to *tombolare* "to tumble", from the numbers tumbling out of the bag; *tombolare* itself is usually explained as a sound word (EtymDB has no entry).
