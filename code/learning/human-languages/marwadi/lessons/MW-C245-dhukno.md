# ढूकणो (ḍhūkṇo) — to draw near; to arrive at the door

## You'll want to know: ढूकणो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C245-TEDNO245-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ढूकणो** — *ḍhūkṇo* — "to draw near; to arrive at the door".

> पावणो घर कनै ढूक्यो।
>
> *pāvṇo ghar kanai ḍhūkyo.*
>
> The guest drew near the house.

Past (he …): वो पावणो ढूक्यो, *vo pāvṇo ḍhūkyo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ढूकणो** (*ḍhūkṇo*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*ḍhukyati*, "approaches"; CDIAL lists the Old Marwari partner *ḍhukāvaï*, "brings near", and Hindi *ḍhūknā*, "to approach", under it (CDIAL 5592).
