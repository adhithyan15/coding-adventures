## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**مصنع** (*maṣnaʿ*) is built on the native Arabic root *ṣ-n-ʿ*, "to make, to fashion" (Lane). *Maṣnaʿ* is the *ma-* place noun, "a place of making".
