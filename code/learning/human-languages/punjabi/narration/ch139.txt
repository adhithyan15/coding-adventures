Lesson 1 of 5.
ਤਨਖ਼ਾਹ (tankhāh) — a salary.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for a class, then the Punjabi for a teacher.

You'll want to know: ਤਨਖ਼ਾਹ.
ਤਨਖ਼ਾਹ — tankhāh — "a salary".

The word, taken apart.
ਤਨਖ਼ਾਹ (tankhāh) is a loan, usually traced to Persian tankhwāh, "salary".

Lesson 3 of 5.
ਤਰਖਾਣ (tarkhāṇ) — a carpenter.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for a salary, then the Punjabi for a tailor.

You'll want to know: ਤਰਖਾਣ.
ਤਰਖਾਣ — tarkhāṇ — "a carpenter".

The word, taken apart.
ਤਰਖਾਣ (tarkhāṇ) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from takṣan, "carpenter" (CDIAL 5621). CDIAL notes its intrusive r.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ਘੁਮਿਆਰ (ghumiār) — a potter.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for a carpenter, then the Punjabi for a blacksmith.

You'll want to know: ਘੁਮਿਆਰ.
ਘੁਮਿਆਰ — ghumiār — "a potter".

The word, taken apart.
Where ਘੁਮਿਆਰ (ghumiār) comes from is not fully settled. CDIAL lists Punjabi kumhār, "potter", under Sanskrit kumbhakāra, "potter", literally "pot-maker" (CDIAL 3310), but it does not list ghumiār, which looks like the same word.
