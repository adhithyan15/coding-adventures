# தீக்கிரையாகு (tīkkiraiyāku) — to burn down; to be gutted by fire

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[TA-LEX-C299-THEE299-01, TA-LEX-C299-THEE299-02] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Tamil for to catch fire, then the Tamil for to blaze.

## You'll want to know: தீக்கிரையாகு
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C299-THEE299-03]; assesses=[] -->

**தீக்கிரையாகு** — *tīkkiraiyāku* — "to burn down; to be gutted by fire".

> பழைய வீடு முழுவதும் தீக்கிரையானது.
>
> *paḻaiya vīṭu muḻuvatum tīkkiraiyāṉatu.*
>
> The whole old house was gutted by fire.

Past (it …): தீக்கிரையானது, *tīkkiraiyāṉatu*.

Literally to become food for the fire: தீ + இரை, prey.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**தீக்கிரையாகு** (*tīkkiraiyāku*) is a Tamil compound built from தீ, "fire" (DEDR 3266), இரை, "food, prey" (DEDR 490), and ஆகு, "to become": to become the fire's prey.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
