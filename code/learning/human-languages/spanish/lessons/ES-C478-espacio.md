## You'll want to know: el espacio
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C478-MALETA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**el espacio** — "the space".

> El espacio es pequeño pero tiene luz.
>
> *The space is small but it has light.*

More formal than *sitio*, and the word for outer space too.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
