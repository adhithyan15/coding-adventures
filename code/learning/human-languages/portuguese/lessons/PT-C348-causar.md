## You'll want to know: causar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C348-EFEITO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**causar** — "to cause".

> O acidente causou muito trânsito.
>
> *The accident caused a lot of traffic.*

*Causar problemas* — to cause problems.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*causar* is built on *causa* "cause" with the verb ending *-ar* (EtymDB); *causa* comes from Latin *causa* "reason, cause, lawsuit" (Lewis & Short).
