You'll want to know: malgré.
malgré — "despite".

The word, taken apart.
malgré from Middle French maugré, from Old French mal, "bad", and gré, "liking", from Latin gratum, "pleasing" (EtymDB 2.1): against one's liking.

Lesson 2 of 5.
malheureusement — unfortunately.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for on the left, then the French for despite.

You'll want to know: malheureusement.
malheureusement — "unfortunately".

The word, taken apart.
malheureusement — heureux, "lucky", with mal-, "badly", in front and the adverb ending -ment: unluckily. Heureux is built on Old French eur, "luck", from Latin augurium, "an omen", and mal- from Latin male, "badly" (EtymDB 2.1).
