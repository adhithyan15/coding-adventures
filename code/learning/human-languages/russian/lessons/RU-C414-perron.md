## You'll want to know: перрон
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C414-PERRON-01]; assesses=[] -->

**перрон** (*perrón*) — "a station platform".

> Мы ждали поезд на перроне.
>
> *My zhdáli póyezd na perróne.*
>
> We waited for the train on the platform.

Masculine. *На перроне* — on the platform.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*перрон* was borrowed from French *perron* (EtymDB), a stone landing with steps outside a building, built on *pierre* 'stone'.
