Marwadi (Marwari), chapter 67: Game, Letter, Tree, Leaf, Hill.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
खेल (khel) — a game.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for an answer, then the Marwadi for a word.

You'll want to know: खेल.
खेल — khel — "a game".

The word, taken apart.
खेल (khel) is a tadbhava, a word inherited and reshaped by sound change. It comes from khel, "play", or from khell, forms CDIAL reconstructs from their descendants. CDIAL lists Old Marwari khelaï under khel (CDIAL 3918). It is the noun of the verb khelṇo, "to play". CDIAL says that if Sanskrit krīḍati, "plays", is of non-Aryan origin, these forms may share its source.

Lesson 5 of 5.
डूंगर (ḍū̃gar) — a hill, a mountain.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for a tree, then the Marwadi for a leaf.

You'll want to know: डूंगर.
डूंगर — ḍū̃gar — "a hill, a mountain".

The word, taken apart.
डूंगर (ḍū̃gar) is a tadbhava, a word inherited and reshaped by sound change. It comes from ḍuṅga, "hill", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants. CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry (CDIAL 5423); the match is through Hindi ḍū̃gar and Gujarati ḍũgar. CDIAL sets it in a large group of reconstructed words for "hill" headed ṭakka, and explains the -r as an added suffix. CDIAL takes the variety of these forms as a sign of a non-Aryan, perhaps Munda, source.
