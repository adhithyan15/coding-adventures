Urdu, chapter 442: A Surprise.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
تعجب (tājjub) — astonishment.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for brimming over, then the Urdu for a surge of eagerness.

You'll want to know: تعجب.
تعجب — tājjub — "astonishment".
اس خبر پر سب کو تعجب ہوا۔ is khabar par sab ko tājjub huā. Everyone was astonished at this news.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
تعجب (tājjub) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic taʿajjub, "wondering at something".

Lesson 5 of 5.
اچنبھا (achambhā) — an amazing thing; astonishment.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for dumbfounded, then the Urdu for a wonder.

You'll want to know: اچنبھا.
اچنبھا — achambhā — "an amazing thing; astonishment".
اس کے لیے سمندر ایک اچنبھا تھا۔ us ke liye samandar ek achambhā thā. For her the sea was an amazing sight.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
اچنبھا (achambhā) is inherited from ācchambha, a form CDIAL reconstructs for "stiffness" and so "astonishment", the state of being frozen with surprise (CDIAL 1079).
