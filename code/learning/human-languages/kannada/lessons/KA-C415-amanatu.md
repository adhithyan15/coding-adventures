# ಅಮಾನತು (amānatu) — suspension

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[KA-LEX-C414-GARISHTHA414-04, KA-LEX-C414-GARISHTHA414-05] -->

## You'll want to know: ಅಮಾನತು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C415-AMANATU415-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ಅಮಾನತು** — *amānatu* — "suspension".

> ಲಂಚ ತೆಗೆದುಕೊಂಡ ಅಧಿಕಾರಿಯನ್ನು ಅಮಾನತು ಮಾಡಿದರು.
>
> *lañca tegedukoṇḍa adhikāriyannu amānatu māḍidaru.*
>
> The official who took a bribe was suspended.

ಅಮಾನತು ಮಾಡು, to suspend from a job.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಅಮಾನತು** (*amānatu*) is usually traced to Arabic *amānat*, "a trust, a deposit", through Hindustani: a post held in trust, not used.
