## You'll want to know: ਸੱਟ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C322-HURT322-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ਸੱਟ** — *saṭṭ* — "an injury".

> ਮੇਰੀ ਲੱਤ ਉੱਤੇ ਇੱਕ ਵੱਡੀ ਸੱਟ ਹੈ।
>
> *merī latt utte ikk vaḍḍī saṭṭ hai.*
>
> I have a big injury on my leg.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਸੱਟ** (*saṭṭ*) is inherited from Sanskrit *sṛṣṭi*, "emission, creation"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *saṭṭ*, "a blow, a wound", under it (CDIAL 13579). How the sense moved is not explained there.
