# சித்தப்பா (cittappā) — the father's younger brother

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[TA-LEX-C101-GRAND-03, TA-LEX-C101-GRAND-04] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Tamil for an uncle, then the Tamil for an aunt.

## You'll want to know: சித்தப்பா
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C101-GRAND-05]; assesses=[] -->

**சித்தப்பா** (*cittappā*) — your father's younger brother.

It ends in **அப்பா**, "father": he is the *little father*.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

Grandparents, uncle, aunt, and a little father.
