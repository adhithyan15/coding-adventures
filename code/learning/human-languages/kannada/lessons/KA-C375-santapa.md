# ಸಂತಾಪ (santāpa) — condolence; grief

## You'll want to know: ಸಂತಾಪ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C375-SHIBIRA375-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸಂತಾಪ** — *santāpa* — "condolence; grief".

> ಊರಿನವರು ಸಂತ್ರಸ್ತರ ಕುಟುಂಬಗಳಿಗೆ ಸಂತಾಪ ತಿಳಿಸಿದರು.
>
> *ūrinavaru santrastara kuṭumbagaḷige santāpa tiḷisidaru.*
>
> The townspeople offered condolences to the victims' families.

A condolence meeting is a ಸಂತಾಪ ಸಭೆ.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸಂತಾಪ** (*santāpa*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *saṃtāpa*, "great heat", and "affliction, pain, sorrow" (Monier-Williams).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
