## You'll want to know: منزعج
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C495-INZIAJ495-02]; assesses=[] -->

**منزعج** — *munzaʿij* — "annoyed".

> الأب منزعج من صوت التلفاز.
>
> *al-ab munzaʿij min ṣawt at-tilfāz.*
>
> The father is annoyed by the noise of the television.

It takes من for the cause.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**منزعج** (*munzaʿij*) is built on the native Arabic root *z-ʿ-j*, "to disquiet, to unsettle" (Lane). Munzaʿij is the participle of Form VII.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
