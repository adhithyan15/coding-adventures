## You'll want to know: decorar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C378-REVER-04]; assesses=[] -->

**decorar** — "to learn by heart, to memorise; to decorate".

> Decorei o poema para a aula.
>
> *I learnt the poem by heart for the class.*

In Portugal *decorar* very often means to memorise; it also means to decorate a room.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*decorar* "to decorate" comes from Latin *decorāre* "to adorn" (EtymDB); the sense "to learn by heart" is usually linked with *de cor* "by heart", on Latin *cor* "heart" (Lewis & Short).
