Mandarin Chinese, chapter 333: New Year's Eve.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
春晚 (Chūnwǎn) — the Spring Festival Gala.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for how absurd, then the Mandarin for the human world below.

You'll want to know: 春晚.
春晚 — Chūnwǎn — "the Spring Festival Gala".
每年春节我们都在家看春晚 měinián Chūnjié wǒmen dōu zài jiā kàn Chūnwǎn — Every lunar new year we have the gala on at home.

The word, taken apart.
春晚 (Chūnwǎn) is built from 春 'spring' and 晚 'evening', a short form of the longer name of the Spring Festival evening show. Both are inherited from Middle Chinese, tsyhwin and mjonX (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which calls the first the first of the four seasons and glosses the second 'dusk'.

Chúxī — "lunar New Year's Eve".
Chúxī de shíhou wǒmen dōu huí jiā — On the eve of the lunar new year we all go home.
Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
Chúxī is built from chú 'to clear away' and xī 'dusk', both inherited from Middle Chinese, drjo and zjek (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'steps' and 'to remove', and the second 'dusk'.

Lesson 3 of 5.
niányèfàn — New Year's Eve dinner.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for the Spring Festival Gala on TV, then the Mandarin for lunar New Year's Eve.

You'll want to know: niányèfàn.
niányèfàn — "New Year's Eve dinner".
Chúxī de wǎnshang wǒmen zài nǎinai jiā chī le niányèfàn — On the last night of the year we had the reunion dinner at my grandma's.
Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
niányèfàn is built from 年 'a year', yè 'night' and 饭 'a meal', all three inherited from Middle Chinese, nen, yaeH and bjonH (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'evening' and says of the third that grain was first cooked into it.
