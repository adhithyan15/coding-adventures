## You'll want to know: упрямый
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C478-STUBBORN-01]; assesses=[] -->

**упрямый** (*upryámyy*) — "stubborn".

> Упрямый ребёнок не хотел есть суп.
>
> *Upryámyy rebyónok ne khotél yest sup.*
>
> The stubborn child wouldn't eat the soup.

Short forms: упрям, упряма.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

EtymDB lists *упрямый* without a source; it is usually linked to *упереться* 'to dig one's heels in', but the origin is not settled.
