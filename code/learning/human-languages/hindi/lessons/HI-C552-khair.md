## You'll want to know: ख़ैर
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C552-BAHARHAAL552-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ख़ैर** — *khair* — "well; anyway (moving on)".

> ख़ैर, अब चलिए, देर हो रही है।
>
> *Khair, ab calie, der ho rahī hai.*
>
> Well, let's go now; it's getting late.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ख़ैर** (*khair*) is usually traced to Arabic *khayr*, "good, well-being", through Persian.
