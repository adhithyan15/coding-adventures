## You'll want to know: rassurer
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C470-FRAYEUR-05]; assesses=[] -->

**rassurer** — "to reassure".

> Le médecin a rassuré mes parents.
>
> *The doctor reassured my parents.*

*Je suis rassuré* — I feel reassured.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*rassurer* comes from Old French *rasseürer* (EtymDB), built from *re-* and *asseürer* "to make safe", which goes back to Latin *sēcūrus* "free from care" (Lewis & Short).
