## You'll want to know: شاحنة
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C445-TRAM445-02]; assesses=[] -->

**شاحنة** — *shāḥina* — "a lorry; a truck".

> وقفت شاحنة كبيرة في وسط الطريق.
>
> *waqafat shāḥina kabīra fī wasaṭ aṭ-ṭarīq.*
>
> A big lorry stopped in the middle of the road.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**شاحنة** (*shāḥina*) is built on the native Arabic root *sh-ḥ-n*, "to load (a ship)" (Lane). It is an active participle: the vehicle that carries loads; *shāḥin*, "a charger", shares the root.
