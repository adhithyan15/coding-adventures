## You'll want to know: ચોરી
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C285-CHOR285-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ચોરી** — *corī* — "a theft".

> ગઈકાલે રાત્રે અમારી ગલીમાં ચોરી થઈ.
>
> *gaīkāle rātre amārī galīmā̃ corī thaī.*
>
> There was a theft in our lane last night.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

is inherited from Sanskrit *caurikā*, "theft" (CDIAL 4937). **ચોર** is the person and **ચોરી** what the person does; a theft takes place with **થવું**: **ચોરી થઈ**.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
