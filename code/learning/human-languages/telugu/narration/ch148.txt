Telugu, chapter 148: To protect, To burn, To put out, To sing, To sell.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
కాపాడు (kāpāḍu) — to protect, to save.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to win, then the Telugu for to hide.

You'll want to know: కాపాడు.
కాపాడు — kāpāḍu — "to protect, to save".

The word, taken apart.
కాపాడు (kāpāḍu) is native Dravidian. DEDR 1416 lists kāpāḍu, "to protect, save", under kācu, "to guard", with Tamil kā, "to guard". DEDR compares Prakrit kāhila, "cowherd".
