Marwadi (Marwari), chapter 264: Smith, Potter and Carpenter.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
घड़णो (ghaṛṇo) — to shape; to forge (metal or clay).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for a washerman, then the Marwadi for a goldsmith.

You'll want to know: घड़णो.
घड़णो — ghaṛṇo — "to shape; to forge (metal or clay)".
सुनार अंगूठी घड़ै है। sunār aṅgūṭhī ghaṛai hai. The goldsmith shapes a ring.
Past (I …): म्हैं कड़ो घड़्यो, mhaĩ kaṛo ghaṛyo.

The word, taken apart.
घड़णो (ghaṛṇo) is inherited from Sanskrit ghaṭate, "is busy with"; CDIAL lists Old Marwari ghaṛī, "formed", under it (CDIAL 4407).

Lesson 2 of 5.

[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: लुहार.
लुहार — luhār — "a blacksmith".
लुहार कुल्हाड़ी बणावै है। luhār kulhāṛī baṇāvai hai. The blacksmith makes an axe.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
लुहार (luhār) is inherited from Sanskrit lohakāra, "blacksmith". CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry; it lists Hindi luhār (CDIAL 11159).

Lesson 3 of 5.
ठोकणो (ṭhokṇo) — to hammer; to knock in.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for to shape (metal or clay), then the Marwadi for a blacksmith.

You'll want to know: ठोकणो.
ठोकणो — ṭhokṇo — "to hammer; to knock in".
लुहार खीलो ठोकै है। luhār khīlo ṭhokai hai. The blacksmith hammers in a nail.
Past (I …): म्हैं खीलो ठोक्यो, mhaĩ khīlo ṭhokyo.

The word, taken apart.
ठोकणो (ṭhokṇo) is inherited from a reconstructed ṭhōkk, "knock". CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry; it lists Hindi ṭhoknā, "to knock, make firm" (CDIAL 5513).

Lesson 5 of 5.
सुथार (suthār) — a carpenter.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for to hammer, then the Marwadi for a potter.

You'll want to know: सुथार.
सुथार — suthār — "a carpenter".
सुथार किवाड़ बणावै है। suthār kivāṛ baṇāvai hai. The carpenter makes a door.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
सुथार (suthār) is inherited from Sanskrit sūtradhāra, "the holder of the measuring line", a carpenter. CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry; it lists Gujarati suthār and Hindi sutār (CDIAL 13563).
