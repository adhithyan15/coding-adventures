## You'll want to know: じつりょく
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C460-GOAL460-03]; assesses=[] -->

**じつりょく** — *jitsuryoku* — "real ability".

> **あの せんしゅは じつりょくが あります。** — *ano senshu wa jitsuryoku ga arimasu* — "That player has real ability."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**じつりょく** (*jitsuryoku*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *jitsu*, "reality" (Middle Chinese *zyit*) and *ryoku*, "power" (*lik*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
