## You'll want to know: húzi
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C233-LOOKS233-03]; assesses=[] -->

*húzi* — "beard; moustache".

> *wǒ bàba yǒu húzi* — My dad has a beard.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*húzi* is built from *hú* 'beard' and the noun ending 子, inherited from Middle Chinese *tsiX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription). No dictionary we use records an older form of the first, so its history is not settled.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing things.
