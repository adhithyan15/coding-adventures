## You'll want to know: মোটর
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C504-JONTRO504-05]; assesses=[] -->

**মোটর** — *moṭôr* — "a motor".

> এই পাখার মোটরটা নতুন।
>
> *Ei pākhār moṭôrṭā nôtun.*
>
> This fan's motor is new.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**মোটর** (*moṭôr*) is borrowed from English *motor*.
