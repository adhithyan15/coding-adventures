Gujarati, chapter 64: Flower, Mountain and Forest.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ફૂલ (phūl) — a flower.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a garden, then the Gujarati for an address.

You'll want to know: ફૂલ.
ફૂલ (phūl) — "a flower".

The word, taken apart.
ફૂલ (phūl) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from phulla, "expanded, blown (of flowers)" (CDIAL 9092).

Lesson 4 of 5.
જંગલ (jaṅgal) — a forest, the jungle.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a leaf, then the Gujarati for a mountain.

You'll want to know: જંગલ.
જંગલ (jaṅgal) — "a forest, the jungle".

The word, taken apart.
જંગલ (jaṅgal) goes back to Sanskrit jāṅgala, "arid, sparingly grown with trees" (CDIAL 5177). CDIAL notes that the noun spread across the modern languages in the form of the Hindi area, and that some forms support a non-Aryan origin.
