## You'll want to know: চাঁদা
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C417-NOT417-05]; assesses=[] -->

**চাঁদা** — *chā̃dā* — "a contribution; a subscription".

> পুজোর জন্য পাড়ার ছেলেরা চাঁদা তুলল।
>
> *pujor jônyô pāṛār chhelerā chā̃dā tullô.*
>
> The local boys collected contributions for the Puja.

চাঁদা তোলা, to collect contributions; চাঁদা দেওয়া, to give one.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**চাঁদা** (*chā̃dā*) is usually traced to Persian *chanda*, a word built on *chand*, "how much, some"; how it came to mean a contribution is not set out here.
