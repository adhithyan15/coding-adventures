Arabic, chapter 154: Full, Young, Old, Strong, Weak.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
مليء (malīʾ) — full.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for fresh, then the Arabic for ripe.

You'll want to know: مليء.
مليء — malīʾ — "full".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: malīʾ]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: malīʾ, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say malīʾ]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Arabic for fresh, then the Arabic for ripe, then say malīʾ again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does مليء (malīʾ) mean? ("Full".) Say it once more.
