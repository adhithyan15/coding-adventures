## You'll want to know: pànduàn
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C350-FACTS350-04]; assesses=[] -->

*pànduàn* — "to make a judgement; to judge".

> *wǒ bù néng pànduàn tā shuō de shì bu shì shìshí* — I can't judge whether what he says is true.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*pànduàn* is built from *pàn* and *duàn*, both inherited from Middle Chinese, *phanH* and *dwanX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to split' and 'to divide', and the second 'to cut off'; it also lists the second in the departing tone, glossed 'to decide'.
