## You'll want to know: 冷静
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C466-CALM466-02]; assesses=[] -->

**冷静** — *lěngjìng* — "calm; cool-headed".

> **你太高兴了先冷静一点儿** *nǐ tài gāoxìng le xiān lěngjìng yìdiǎnr* — You're getting carried away. Calm down a little first.

The new character, in pieces: 静 — fourteen strokes in two pieces: on the left the right piece of 情, a top of four strokes over 月 *yuè*; on the right a short stroke falling to the left, a bar that turns into a stroke falling to the left, a stroke that runs right and turns down, two bars and a vertical with a hook. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**冷静** (*lěngjìng*) is built from 冷 'cold' and 静 'still', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *lengX* and *dzjengX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'cold' and the second 'at peace'. The dictionary lists 冷 under three readings, two of them glossed 'cold'; *lengX* is one of those two.
