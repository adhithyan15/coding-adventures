## You'll want to know: رشوت
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C281-THAG281-03]; assesses=[] -->

**رشوت** — *rishvat* — "a bribe".

> داروغے نے رشوت مانگی۔
>
> *dāroghe ne rishvat māṅgī.*
>
> The inspector asked for a bribe.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**رشوت** (*rishvat*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *rishwa*, "a bribe". It is feminine, so the verb agrees: **رشوت مانگی**.
