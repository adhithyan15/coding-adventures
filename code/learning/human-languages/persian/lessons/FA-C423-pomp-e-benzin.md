## You'll want to know: پمپ بنزین
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C423-BENZIN-01]; assesses=[] -->

**پمپ بنزین** (*pomp-e benzin*) — "a petrol station (US: gas station)".

> در این جاده پمپ بنزین کم است.
>
> *Dar in jâdde pomp-e benzin kam ast.*
>
> There are few petrol stations on this road.

Literally 'petrol pump'. In speech also *pomp benzin*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*pomp-e benzin* is built from *pomp* 'pump', a European loan usually traced to French *pompe*, and *benzin* 'petrol', usually traced to French *benzine*. EtymDB does not list the compound.
