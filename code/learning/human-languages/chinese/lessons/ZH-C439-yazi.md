## You'll want to know: yāzi
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C439-COUNTRY439-04]; assesses=[] -->

*yāzi* — "duck".

> *nǎinai jiā yǒu shí zhī yāzi* — Grandma keeps ten ducks.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*yāzi* is built from *yā* 'a duck' and the noun ending *zi*. The first is inherited from Middle Chinese *'aep* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed as a water bird.
