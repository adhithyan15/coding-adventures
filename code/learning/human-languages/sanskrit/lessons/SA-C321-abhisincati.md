## You'll want to know: अभिषिञ्चति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C321-ABHISHEKA321-04]; assesses=[] -->

**अभिषिञ्चति** — *abhiṣiñcati* — "anoints; consecrates".

> पुरोहितः तं जलेन अभिषिञ्चति।
>
> *purohitaḥ taṃ jalena abhiṣiñcati.*
>
> The priest anoints him with water.

Past (he …): सः तं जलेन अभिषिक्तवान्, *saḥ taṃ jalena abhiṣiktavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अभिषिञ्चति** (*abhiṣiñcati*) is built from *abhi* and the root √sic, "to sprinkle"; MW gives *abhi-ṣic* as to sprinkle and to consecrate, anoint, appoint by consecration (MW).

## What you've built

One more word for telling what happened.
