## You'll want to know: शिबिरम्
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C306-SHIBIRA306-01]; assesses=[] -->

**शिबिरम्** — *śibiram* — "a camp; a tent".

> आर्ताः जनाः शिबिरे वसन्ति।
>
> *ārtāḥ janāḥ śibire vasanti.*
>
> People in distress live in the camp.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**शिबिरम्** (*śibiram*) MW gives *śibira* as a royal camp, a tent, any tent, without a derivation, so its origin is uncertain (MW).
