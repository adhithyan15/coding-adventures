## You'll want to know: जनयति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C340-KARANA340-05]; assesses=[] -->

**जनयति** — *janayati* — "gives rise to; produces".

> क्रोधः दुःखं जनयति।
>
> *krodhaḥ duḥkhaṃ janayati.*
>
> Anger gives rise to sorrow.

Past (he …): सः भयं जनितवान्, *saḥ bhayaṃ janitavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**जनयति** (*janayati*) comes from the root √jan, "to be born", in its causative form, "to cause to be born, to produce"; MW gives *janita* "born" (MW), and KRM gives the participle *janita* for the root.
