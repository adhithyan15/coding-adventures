Japanese, chapter 271: Caught at Last.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
つかまる (tsukamaru) — to be caught; to be arrested.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for that (one), then the Japanese for to touch.

You'll want to know: つかまる.
つかまる — tsukamaru — "to be caught; to be arrested".
はんにんは えきで つかまりました。 — hannin wa eki de tsukamarimashita — "The culprit was arrested at the station."
Polite つかまります, tsukamarimasu; a godan verb. Past (polite): はんにんが つかまりました。, hannin ga tsukamarimashita.

The word, taken apart.
つかまる (tsukamaru) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict).

Lesson 2 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for to touch, then the Japanese for to be caught.

You'll want to know: おいかける.
おいかける — oikakeru — "to chase; to run after".
おまわりさんが どろぼうを おいかけました。 — omawarisan ga dorobō o oikakemashita — "A police officer ran after the thief."
Polite おいかけます, oikakemasu; an ichidan verb. Past (polite): いぬが ねこを おいかけました。, inu ga neko o oikakemashita.

The word, taken apart.
おいかける (oikakeru) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, built from oi, "chasing" (from ou), and kakeru, "to set about" (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
