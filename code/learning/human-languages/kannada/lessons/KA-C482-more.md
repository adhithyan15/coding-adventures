## You'll want to know: ಮೊರೆ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C482-TORE482-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ಮೊರೆ** — *more* — "to rumble; to hum".

> ಮಳೆಗಾಲದಲ್ಲಿ ಜಲಪಾತ ಜೋರಾಗಿ ಮೊರೆಯುತ್ತದೆ.
>
> *maḷegāladalli jalapāta jōrāgi moreyuttade.*
>
> In the rainy season the waterfall roars loudly.

Past (it …): ಮೊರೆಯಿತು, *moreyitu*.

Of rushing water, bees and engines; a steady low sound.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಮೊರೆ** (*more*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 4973 lists Kannada *more*, "to hum, to buzz, to murmur, to gurgle", beside *moraḷ*, "to growl".
