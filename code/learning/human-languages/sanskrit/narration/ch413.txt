Sanskrit, chapter 413: Packing for the Road.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
उन्नयति (unnayati) — raises up; lifts.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for passes by, then the Sanskrit for a kos.

You'll want to know: उन्नयति.
उन्नयति — unnayati — "raises up; lifts".
भारवाहः भूमेः भारम् उन्नयति। bhāravāhaḥ bhūmeḥ bhāram unnayati. The porter lifts the load from the ground.
Past (he …): सः भारम् उन्नीतवान्, saḥ bhāram unnītavān.

The word, taken apart.
उन्नयति (unnayati) is built from ud, "up", and the root √nī, "to lead", which also gives nayati, "leads"; MW gives un-nī as to lead up and to raise or lift up, with the participle unnīta (MW).

Lesson 2 of 5.
सन्नह्यति (sannahyati) — gets ready; equips oneself.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a kos, then the Sanskrit for raises up.

You'll want to know: सन्नह्यति.
सन्नह्यति — sannahyati — "gets ready; equips oneself".
यात्री प्रातः यात्रायै सन्नह्यति। yātrī prātaḥ yātrāyai sannahyati. The traveller gets ready for the journey in the morning.
Past (he …): सः यात्रायै सन्नद्धवान्, saḥ yātrāyai sannaddhavān.

The word, taken apart.
सन्नह्यति (sannahyati) is built from sam, "together", and the root √nah, "to bind, to tie": MW gives saṃ-nah as to bind together or gird on and to arm oneself or prepare for a task, with the participle saṃnaddha, "girt, equipped" (MW).

Lesson 4 of 5.
प्रापयति (prāpayati) — delivers; takes to.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for gets ready, then the Sanskrit for a sack.

You'll want to know: प्रापयति.
प्रापयति — prāpayati — "delivers; takes to".
भारवाहः गोणीं धूमयानं प्रापयति। bhāravāhaḥ goṇīṃ dhūmayānaṃ prāpayati. The porter takes the sack to the train.
Past (he …): सः गोणीं प्रापितवान्, saḥ goṇīṃ prāpitavān.

The word, taken apart.
प्रापयति (prāpayati) is built from pra, "forth", and the causative of the root √āp, "to reach", which gives prāpnoti, "reaches"; MW gives its participle prāpita as conveyed or brought to (MW).
