Malayalam, chapter 136: By the Water.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
കടൽ (kaṭal) — the sea.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for a bridge, then the Malayalam for a village.

You'll want to know: കടൽ.
കടൽ (kaṭal) — "the sea". Kerala's whole west side is on it.

What you've built.
Where the fish come from.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: kaṭal]

[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does കടൽ (kaṭal) mean? ("The sea".) Say it once more.
