## You'll want to know: a franqueza
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C471-MENTIRA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**a franqueza** — "frankness".

> Com toda a franqueza, não gostei do filme.
>
> *In all frankness, I did not like the film.*

*Com franqueza* — frankly. The adjective is *franco*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*franqueza* is built on *franco* "frank", which came from French *franc* (EtymDB), the name of the Franks, Latin *Franci* (Lewis & Short); the sense "free, open" is usually explained by the Franks being the free men of their realm.
