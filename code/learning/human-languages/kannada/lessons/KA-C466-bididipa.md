# ಬೀದಿದೀಪ (bīdidīpa) — a streetlight

## You'll want to know: ಬೀದಿದೀಪ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C466-UPANAGARA466-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ಬೀದಿದೀಪ** — *bīdidīpa* — "a streetlight".

> ನಮ್ಮ ಬೀದಿಯ ಬೀದಿದೀಪ ಒಂದು ವಾರದಿಂದ ಉರಿಯುತ್ತಿಲ್ಲ.
>
> *namma bīdiya bīdidīpa ondu vāradinda uriyuttilla.*
>
> The streetlight on our street has not been on for a week.

ಬೀದಿ, a street, plus ದೀಪ, a lamp.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಬೀದಿದೀಪ** (*bīdidīpa*) is a Kannada compound built from ಬೀದಿ, "a street", from Sanskrit *vīthi*, "a row, a road" (Monier-Williams), and ದೀಪ, "a lamp" (CDIAL 6348).
