## You'll want to know: rural
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C684-CIUDADANO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**rural** — "rural, country (as an adjective)".

> Pasamos una semana en una casa rural.
>
> *We spent a week in a country cottage.*

In Spain *una casa rural* is a holiday cottage in the country.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*rural* is a learned borrowing from Latin *rūrālis* "of the countryside" (EtymDB), from *rūs* "the country".
