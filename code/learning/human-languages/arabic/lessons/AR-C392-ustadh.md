## You'll want to know: أستاذ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C392-MUHADIR392-02]; assesses=[] -->

**أستاذ** — *ustādh* — "a professor".

> أستاذ الأدب رجل لطيف.
>
> *ustādh al-adab rajul laṭīf.*
>
> The literature professor is a kind man.

Plural: أساتذة, *asātidha*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**أستاذ** (*ustādh*) was borrowed from Persian *ustād*, "a master, a teacher" (EtymDB). In Arabic it is both a polite title and a university professor.
