# tribūnal, tribūnālis — the judgement seat, a raised platform

## You'll want to know: tribūnal, tribūnālis
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C411-TRIBUNAL-01]; assesses=[] -->

**tribūnal, tribūnālis** — "the judgement seat, a raised platform".

> Praetor in tribūnālī sedēbat.
>
> *The praetor sat on the judgement seat.*

*tribūnal, tribūnālis* n.: the raised platform where a magistrate sat to judge. English *tribunal*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*tribūnal* is built on *tribūnus* "a tribune" (Lewis & Short), who takes his name from *tribus* "a tribe": the platform of the tribunes. English *tribunal* comes from it.
