## You'll want to know: el lujo
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C633-RIQUEZA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**el lujo** — "the luxury".

> Un coche así es un lujo.
>
> *A car like that is a luxury.*

*De lujo* — luxury, deluxe: *un hotel de lujo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*lujo* is a learned borrowing from Latin *luxus* 'excess, extravagance' (EtymDB).
