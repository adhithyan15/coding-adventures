## You'll want to know: కల్పన
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C372-KATHAKUDU372-04]; assesses=[] -->

**కల్పన** — *kalpana* — "imagination; fiction".

> ఆ కథ పూర్తిగా కల్పన.
>
> *ā katha pūrtigā kalpana.*
>
> That story is pure imagination.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**కల్పన** (*kalpana*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *kalpanā*, "making, preparing" (Monier-Williams); in Telugu it is what the mind makes up.
