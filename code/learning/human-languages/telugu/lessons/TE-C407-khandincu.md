# ఖండించు (khaṇḍiñcu) — to condemn; to denounce

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Telugu for a protest, then the Telugu for a movement.

## You'll want to know: ఖండించు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C407-NIRASANA407-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ఖండించు** — *khaṇḍiñcu* — "to condemn; to denounce".

> పెద్దలు ఆ పనిని గట్టిగా ఖండించారు.
>
> *peddalu ā panini gaṭṭigā khaṇḍiñcāru.*
>
> The elders strongly condemned that act.

Past (I …): ఖండించాను, *khaṇḍiñcānu*.

Also to refute: ఆ వాదనను ఖండించు, to refute that argument.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ఖండించు** (*khaṇḍiñcu*) is built on Sanskrit *khaṇḍana*, "breaking" (CDIAL 3796), with the Telugu verb ending -ఇంచు.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
