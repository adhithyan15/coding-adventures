## You'll want to know: थेगळो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C177-PHAD177-04]; assesses=[] -->

**थेगळो** — *thegḷo* — "a patch (of cloth)".

> मां थेगळो सीवै है।
>
> *mā̃ thegḷo sīvai hai.*
>
> Mother sews on a patch.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**थेगळो** (*thegḷo*) comes from a reconstructed *\*thigga*, "a rag"; CDIAL lists Hindi *theglī*, "a patch of cloth" (CDIAL 6096). CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry.
