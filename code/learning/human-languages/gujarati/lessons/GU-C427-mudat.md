## You'll want to know: મુદત
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C427-SAVE427-04]; assesses=[] -->

**મુદત** — *mudat* — "a fixed term; a period (of time)".

> આ થાપણની મુદત પાંચ વરસની છે.
>
> *ā thāpaṇnī mudat pā̃c varasnī che.*
>
> The term of this deposit is five years.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**મુદત** (*mudat*) is usually traced to Arabic *mudda*, "a space of time", which came through Persian.
