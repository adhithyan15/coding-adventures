## You'll want to know: खंदक
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C331-VEDHA331-03]; assesses=[] -->

**खंदक** — *khandak* — "a moat; a trench".

> किल्ल्याभोवती पाण्याने भरलेला खंदक होता.
>
> *killyābhovtī pāṇyāne bharlelā khandak hotā.*
>
> There was a moat full of water round the fort.

Masculine.

खंदक खणणे, to dig a trench.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**खंदक** (*khandak*) is a loan from Persian *xandaq*, usually traced to Arabic *khandaq*, 'a trench, a moat'.
