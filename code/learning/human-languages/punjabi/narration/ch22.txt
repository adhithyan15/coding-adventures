Punjabi, chapter 22: Three Known Lines, One Small Form.
4 lessons. The first lesson already needs your eyes or your hands, so save this one for when you have stopped.


Lesson 1 of 4.
Put three known labels on one small form — read and place the known language, residence, and work labels.

Before we start: this one needs your hands, so it is not a driving lesson. You will want a pen and something to write on and your eyes, for letter shapes on the page. You can listen to everything else now — leave the section called Script — one placement pass until you have stopped, and I will say so again when we reach it.

Warm-up.
Read the three labels once: ਭਾਸ਼ਾ, ਰਿਹਾਇਸ਼, ਕੰਮ. Each label is old; only seeing all three together is new.

Script — one placement pass.
Point from the first blank line to the third. Place ਭਾਸ਼ਾ first, ਰਿਹਾਇਸ਼ second, and ਕੰਮ third. Do not choose or write any value.

Guided Practice.
Cover the labels. Say which known label belongs on the middle line, then reveal and check only its placement.

Wrap-up recall.
Stop after the labels are in place. Values arrive in the next lesson.
[question — say your answer, then pause 12 seconds]
Write the known label that belongs on the middle line.


Lesson 2 of 4.
Select three values before writing — select one value from each already practised closed bank.

Before we start: this one needs your hands, so it is not a driving lesson. You will want a pen and something to write on and your eyes, for letter shapes on the page. You can listen to everything else now — leave the section called Script — one cue per field until you have stopped, and I will say so again when we reach it.

Warm-up.
Read each closed practice bank once:
ਭਾਸ਼ਾ: A — ਪੰਜਾਬੀ, B — ਹਿੰਦੀ ਰਿਹਾਇਸ਼: A — ਪਿੰਡ, B — ਸ਼ਹਿਰ ਕੰਮ: A — ਖੇਤੀ, B — ਨੌਕਰੀ.

Script — one cue per field.
For the cue row A, B, A, point to the selected language, residence, and work values in that order. Do not copy them.

Guided Practice.
Cover all three banks and name the selections once. Reveal and check only selection; spelling and form placement wait.

Wrap-up recall.
Selection is now ready for a two-line writing bridge.
[question — say your answer, then pause 18 seconds]
For A, B, A, write the selected value sequence.


Lesson 3 of 4.
Join two supported lines first — join the known language and residence fields with both banks visible.

Before we start: this one needs your hands, so it is not a driving lesson. You will want a pen and something to write on. You can listen to all of it now and come back to the parts that need looking at once you have stopped.

Warm-up.
Keep the language and residence banks visible. Read the cue pair A, B and point to ਪੰਜਾਬੀ, then ਸ਼ਹਿਰ.

Writing — two models remain visible.
Complete only these two lines:
A — ਭਾਸ਼ਾ: __________ B — ਰਿਹਾਇਸ਼: __________.
Copy the selected values while their banks remain visible. This is supported entry, not independent writing evidence.

Guided Practice.
Check the first line, then the second. Stop before adding a work line.

Wrap-up recall.
The third line joins only after two lines feel small.
[question — say your answer, then pause 10 seconds]
How many lines belong in this bridge?


Lesson 4 of 4.
Add the third supported line — join all three known fields while every closed bank remains visible.

Before we start: this one needs your hands, so it is not a driving lesson. You will want a pen and something to write on. You can listen to all of it now and come back to the parts that need looking at once you have stopped.

Warm-up.
Keep all three old banks visible. Select the cue row A, B, A without writing.

Writing — all models remain visible.
Complete one language line, one residence line, and one work line. Copy from the visible banks. This is supported entry, not independent writing evidence.

Guided Practice — one pass at a time.
Check selection on all three lines. Then check spelling. Then check spacing and placement. Do not mix the passes.

Wrap-up recall.
Close the banks before beginning the separate repair lessons.
[question — say your answer, then pause 10 seconds]
Does this visible-bank practice count as independent writing evidence?
