## You'll want to know: pasear
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C674-PASEO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**pasear** — "to go for a walk, to stroll".

> Por la tarde paseamos por el puerto.
>
> *In the evening we stroll round the harbour.*

*Pasear al perro* — to walk the dog.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*pasear* is built on *paso* "step" (EtymDB), which is inherited from Latin *passus* "a step".
