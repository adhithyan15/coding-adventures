# અટકાવવું (aṭkāvvũ) — to hold back; to prevent

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-LEX-C299-BUM299-02, GU-LEX-C299-BUM299-03] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a shout, then the Gujarati for to bring to a stop.

## You'll want to know: અટકાવવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C299-BUM299-04]; assesses=[] -->

**અટકાવવું** — *aṭkāvvũ* — "to hold back; to prevent".

> મીરાએ હાથ પકડીને બાળકને અટકાવ્યું.
>
> *Mīrāe hāth pakḍīne bāḷakne aṭkāvyũ.*
>
> Mira took the child's hand and held him back.

Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે બાળકને અટકાવ્યું, *teṇe bāḷakne aṭkāvyũ*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**અટકાવવું** (*aṭkāvvũ*) is the causative of **અટકવું**, to stop, from *\*aṭṭakk*, "obstruct, stop", a form CDIAL reconstructs (CDIAL 182): to make someone stop before something happens.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
