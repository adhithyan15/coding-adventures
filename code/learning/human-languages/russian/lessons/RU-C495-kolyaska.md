## You'll want to know: коляска
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C495-EXPECTING-04]; assesses=[] -->

**коляска** (*kolyáska*) — "a pram; a wheelchair".

> Каждый день мы гуляем с коляской в парке.
>
> *Kázhdyy den my gulyáyem s kolyáskoy v párke.*
>
> Every day we go for a walk in the park with the pram.

Feminine. *Детская коляска* — a pram; *инвалидная коляска* — a wheelchair.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

EtymDB lists *коляска* without a source; it is usually traced through Polish *kolaska* to the root of *колесо* 'wheel'.
