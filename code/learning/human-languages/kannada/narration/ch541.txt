Kannada, chapter 541: Fitness.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಕಸರತ್ತು (kasarattu) — a workout; physical exercise.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to kick, then the Kannada for to punch.

You'll want to know: ಕಸರತ್ತು.
ಕಸರತ್ತು — kasarattu — "a workout; physical exercise".
ಪ್ರತಿದಿನ ಬೆಳಗ್ಗೆ ಅಪ್ಪ ಅರ್ಧ ಗಂಟೆ ಕಸರತ್ತು ಮಾಡುತ್ತಾರೆ. pratidina beḷagge appa ardha gaṇṭe kasarattu māḍuttāre. Every morning father works out for half an hour.
Also a clever trick or stunt.

The word, taken apart.
ಕಸರತ್ತು (kasarattu) is usually traced to Persian kasrat, "exercise", which reached Kannada through Hindustani.

ಪರ್ವತಾರೋಹಣ — parvatārōhaṇa — "mountaineering".
ನನ್ನ ಅಣ್ಣನಿಗೆ ಪರ್ವತಾರೋಹಣ ತುಂಬಾ ಇಷ್ಟ. nanna aṇṇanige parvatārōhaṇa tumbā iṣṭa. My elder brother loves mountaineering.
ಪರ್ವತ, mountain + ಆರೋಹಣ, climbing.

The word, taken apart.
ಪರ್ವತಾರೋಹಣ (parvatārōhaṇa) is a learned compound built from Sanskrit parvata, "rock, mountain" (CDIAL 7945), and ārohaṇa, "ascending" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 4 of 5.
ಬಿಲ್ಲುಗಾರಿಕೆ (billugārike) — archery.

[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for endurance, then the Kannada for mountaineering.

You'll want to know: ಬಿಲ್ಲುಗಾರಿಕೆ.
ಬಿಲ್ಲುಗಾರಿಕೆ — billugārike — "archery".
ಅವಳು ಬಿಲ್ಲುಗಾರಿಕೆಯಲ್ಲಿ ಪದಕ ಗೆದ್ದಳು. avaḷu billugārikeyalli padaka geddaḷu. She won a medal in archery.
ಬಿಲ್ಲು, a bow + -ಗಾರಿಕೆ, the craft of.

The word, taken apart.
ಬಿಲ್ಲುಗಾರಿಕೆ (billugārike) is a Kannada compound of ಬಿಲ್ಲು, "a bow", and -gārike, "the craft of"; the origin of the suffix is not settled.

ಪ್ರೇಕ್ಷಕರು ಆಟಗಾರರನ್ನು ಜೋರಾಗಿ ಹುರಿದುಂಬಿಸಿದರು. prēkṣakaru āṭagārarannu jōrāgi huridumbisidaru. The spectators cheered the players on loudly.
Past (I …): ಹುರಿದುಂಬಿಸಿದೆ, huridumbiside.
The ones cheered take -ಅನ್ನು.

The word, taken apart.
ಹುರಿದುಂಬಿಸು (huridumbisu) is a native Kannada verb, "to fire up, to cheer on"; its origin is not settled.
