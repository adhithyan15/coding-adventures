# శ్మశానం (śmaśānaṁ) — a cremation ground

## You'll want to know: శ్మశానం
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C345-SMRTI345-01]; assesses=[] -->

**శ్మశానం** — *śmaśānaṁ* — "a cremation ground".

> అంతిమయాత్ర శ్మశానం చేరింది.
>
> *antimayātra śmaśānaṁ cērindi.*
>
> The funeral procession reached the cremation ground.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**శ్మశానం** (*śmaśānaṁ*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *śmaśāna*, "a place for burning the dead" (CDIAL 12658).
