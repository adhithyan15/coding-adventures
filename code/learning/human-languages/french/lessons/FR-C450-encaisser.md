## You'll want to know: encaisser
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C450-CAISSE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**encaisser** — "to cash in, to take (money); to take (a blow)".

> Le commerçant encaisse le paiement.
>
> *The shopkeeper takes the payment.*

Informal: *je ne peux pas l'encaisser* — I cannot stand him.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*encaisser* is built from *en-* and *caisse* 'box, till', which goes back to Latin *capsa* (Lewis & Short): to put the money in the till.
