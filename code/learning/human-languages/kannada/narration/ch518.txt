Lesson 1 of 6.
ನೇಮಿಸು (nēmisu) — to appoint; to hire.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to achieve, then the Kannada for to gain.

You'll want to know: ನೇಮಿಸು.
ನೇಮಿಸು — nēmisu — "to appoint; to hire".
ಕಂಪನಿ ಹತ್ತು ಹೊಸ ನೌಕರರನ್ನು ನೇಮಿಸಿತು. kampani hattu hosa naukararannu nēmisitu. The company hired ten new employees.
Past (I …): ನೇಮಿಸಿದೆ, nēmiside.
The one appointed takes -ಅನ್ನು. ನೇಮಕ is an appointment.

The word, taken apart.
ನೇಮಿಸು (nēmisu) is built on nēma, "a fixed rule or arrangement", a Kannada form of Sanskrit niyama, "restraint, fixed law" (CDIAL 7250), with the verb ending -isu.

Lesson 2 of 6.
ವಜಾ ಮಾಡು (vajā māḍu) — to dismiss; to sack.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to gain, then the Kannada for to appoint.

You'll want to know: ವಜಾ ಮಾಡು.
ವಜಾ ಮಾಡು — vajā māḍu — "to dismiss; to sack".
ತಡವಾಗಿ ಬರುತ್ತಿದ್ದ ನೌಕರನನ್ನು ಕಂಪನಿ ವಜಾ ಮಾಡಿತು. taḍavāgi baruttidda naukaranannu kampani vajā māḍitu. The company sacked the employee who kept coming late.
Past (I …): ವಜಾ ಮಾಡಿದೆ, vajā māḍide.
Also of a court case: ಅರ್ಜಿ ವಜಾ ಆಯಿತು, the petition was dismissed.

The word, taken apart.
ವಜಾ ಮಾಡು (vajā māḍu) joins vajā, a word that came into Kannada through the language of offices and courts, with ಮಾಡು, "to do"; the earlier source of vajā is not settled.

Lesson 3 of 6.
ನಿರುದ್ಯೋಗ (nirudyōga) — unemployment.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to appoint, then the Kannada for to dismiss.

You'll want to know: ನಿರುದ್ಯೋಗ.
ನಿರುದ್ಯೋಗ — nirudyōga — "unemployment".
ಹಳ್ಳಿಗಳಲ್ಲಿ ನಿರುದ್ಯೋಗ ದೊಡ್ಡ ಸಮಸ್ಯೆ. haḷḷigaḷalli nirudyōga doḍḍa samasye. Unemployment is a big problem in the villages.
ನಿರ್, without + ಉದ್ಯೋಗ, a job. ನಿರುದ್ಯೋಗಿ, an unemployed person.

The word, taken apart.
ನಿರುದ್ಯೋಗ (nirudyōga) is a learned compound built from Sanskrit nir-, "without", and udyoga, "undertaking, exertion" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 4 of 6.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to dismiss, then the Kannada for unemployment.

You'll want to know: ನಿವೃತ್ತಿ.
ನಿವೃತ್ತಿ — nivṛtti — "retirement".
ಅರವತ್ತನೇ ವಯಸ್ಸಿನಲ್ಲಿ ಅಪ್ಪ ನಿವೃತ್ತಿ ಪಡೆದರು. aravattanē vayassinalli appa nivṛtti paḍedaru. Father retired at the age of sixty.
ನಿವೃತ್ತ, retired.

The word, taken apart.
ನಿವೃತ್ತಿ (nivṛtti) is borrowed from Sanskrit nivṛtti, "returning; ceasing, abstaining" (Monier-Williams), from ni-, "back", and vṛt, "to turn".

You'll want to know: ಗುತ್ತಿಗೆ.
ಗುತ್ತಿಗೆ — guttige — "a contract; a lease".
ರಸ್ತೆ ಕೆಲಸದ ಗುತ್ತಿಗೆ ಒಂದು ಕಂಪನಿಗೆ ಸಿಕ್ಕಿತು. raste kelasada guttige ondu kampanige sikkitu. The road-building contract went to a company.

The word, taken apart.
ಗುತ್ತಿಗೆ (guttige) is a Kannada word for a contract or lease; its origin is not settled.
