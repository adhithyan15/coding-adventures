## You'll want to know: ভ্যাপসা
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C428-ARDRO428-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ভ্যাপসা** — *bhæpsā* — "muggy; close and sticky".

> বৃষ্টির আগে ভ্যাপসা গরম ছিল।
>
> *brishṭir āge bhæpsā gôrôm chhilô.*
>
> Before the rain it was muggy and hot.

Also of a smell: musty, stale.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ভ্যাপসা** (*bhæpsā*) is an expressive word for close, damp heat; its origin is not settled.

One more word for getting around.
