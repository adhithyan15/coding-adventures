## You'll want to know: recapitare
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C458-POSTA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**recapitare** — "to deliver (mail)".

> Il postino ha recapitato il pacco al vicino.
>
> *The postman delivered the parcel to the neighbour.*

*Il recapito* — the address, the contact details.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*recapitare* is built on *recapito* 'address, delivery'; the deeper origin is not settled.
