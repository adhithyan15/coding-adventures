## You'll want to know: おみやげ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C189-PARCEL189-05]; assesses=[] -->

**おみやげ** — *omiyage* — "souvenir; gift brought back from a trip".

> **ともだちに おみやげを かいます。** — *tomodachi ni omiyage o kaimasu* — "I buy a souvenir for a friend."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**おみやげ** (*omiyage*) is built from polite *o-* and native *miyage*, written with two kanji for "local product" that are read as a whole; the older origin of *miyage* is not settled (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
