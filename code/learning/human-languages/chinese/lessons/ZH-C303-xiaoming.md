## You'll want to know: 小名
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C303-NAME303-02]; assesses=[] -->

**小名** — *xiǎomíng* — "childhood pet name".

> **你的小名是什么** *nǐ de xiǎomíng shì shénme* — What was your pet name as a child?

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**小名** (*xiǎomíng*) is built from 小 'small' and 名 'a name', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *sjewX* and *mjieng* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription): the small name, used at home.
