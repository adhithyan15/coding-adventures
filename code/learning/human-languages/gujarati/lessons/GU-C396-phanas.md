## You'll want to know: ફાનસ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C396-GUARD396-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ફાનસ** — *phānas* — "a lantern".

> રાત્રે ગાર્ડ હાથમાં ફાનસ રાખે છે.
>
> *rātre gārḍ hāthmā̃ phānas rākhe che.*
>
> At night the guard keeps a lantern in his hand.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ફાનસ** (*phānas*) is usually traced to Persian *fānūs*, "a lantern", a word Persian itself took from Greek.
