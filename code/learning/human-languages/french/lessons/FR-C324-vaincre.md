## You'll want to know: vaincre
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C324-CONQUETE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**vaincre** — "to defeat, to overcome".

> L'armée a vaincu l'ennemi en trois jours.
>
> *The army defeated the enemy in three days.*

Irregular: *il a vaincu*, *nous vainquons*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*vaincre* comes from Old French *veintre* (EtymDB), inherited from Latin *vincere* "to conquer" (Lewis & Short).
