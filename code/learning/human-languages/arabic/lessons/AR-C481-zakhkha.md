## You'll want to know: زخة
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C481-MATAR481-01]; assesses=[] -->

**زخة** — *zakhkha* — "a shower of rain".

> نزلت زخة مطر قصيرة.
>
> *nazalat zakhkhat maṭar qaṣīra.*
>
> A short shower of rain fell.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**زخة** (*zakhkha*) names a short, sudden shower; the origin is not settled.
