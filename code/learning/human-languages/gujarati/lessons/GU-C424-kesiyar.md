## You'll want to know: કેશિયર
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C424-COUNTER424-01]; assesses=[] -->

**કેશિયર** — *keśiyar* — "a cashier".

> બેંકના કેશિયરે મને નવી નોટો આપી.
>
> *beṅknā keśiyre mane navī noṭo āpī.*
>
> The bank cashier gave me new notes.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**કેશિયર** (*keśiyar*) is borrowed from English *cashier*.
