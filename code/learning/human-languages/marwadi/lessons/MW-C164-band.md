# बंद (band) — closed

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MW-LEX-C164-NOTICE164-01, MW-LEX-C91-TIME91-02, MW-LEX-C92-TIME92-02] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say *the shop is open*. Then *Monday* and *Sunday*:
**सोमवार**, **रविवार**.

## You'll want to know: बंद
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C164-NOTICE164-02]; assesses=[MW-SCRIPT-DUKAN-01, MW-LEX-C91-TIME91-02] -->

**बंद** — *band* — "closed".

> **सोमवार दुकान बंद है।**
>
> *somvār dukān band hai.* — On Monday the shop is closed.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**बंद** (*band*) is a loan, usually traced to Persian *band*, "shut, bound".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MW-LEX-C164-NOTICE164-02, MW-LEX-C164-NOTICE164-01] -->

**बंद** does not change: **घर बंद है।**, **दुकान बंद है।** Its opposite does:
**खुलो**, **खुली**.
