Lesson 1 of 5.
समानता (samāntā) — equality.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a verdict, then the Marathi for a claim.

You'll want to know: समानता.
समानता — samāntā — "equality".
स्त्री आणि पुरुषांमध्ये समानता असली पाहिजे. strī āṇi puruṣāmmadhye samāntā aslī pāhije. There should be equality between women and men.
Feminine.
समान, equal, + -ता, -ness.

The word, taken apart.
समानता (samāntā) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit samānatā, "equality, sameness" (Monier-Williams).

भेदभाव — bhedbhāv — "discrimination".
आमचे शिक्षक कोणताही भेदभाव करत नाहीत. āmce śikṣak koṇtāhī bhedbhāv karat nāhīt. Our teachers show no discrimination at all.

भेदभाव (bhedbhāv) करणे, to discriminate.

The word, taken apart.
भेदभाव (bhedbhāv) is a learned compound built from Sanskrit bheda, "splitting, difference", and bhāva, "state, attitude" (Monier-Williams).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for discrimination, then the Marathi for inequality.

You'll want to know: दारिद्र्य.
दारिद्र्य — dāridrya — "poverty".
शिक्षणामुळे अनेक कुटुंबं दारिद्र्यातून बाहेर आली. śikṣaṇāmuḷe anek kuṭumbaṁ dāridryātūn bāher ālī. Through education many families came out of poverty.
Neuter.
Neuter and formal; in speech गरिबी, from गरीब, poor.

The word, taken apart.
दारिद्र्य (dāridrya) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit dāridrya, "poverty" (CDIAL 6297), built on daridra, "poor".
