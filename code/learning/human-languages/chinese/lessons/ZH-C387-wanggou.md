## You'll want to know: wǎnggòu
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C387-MONEY387-04]; assesses=[] -->

*wǎnggòu* — "to shop online".

> *wǒ juéde wǎnggòu bǐ qù shāngdiàn piányi* — I think shopping online is cheaper than going to the shops.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*wǎnggòu* is built from *wǎng* 'a net' and *gòu* 'to buy', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *mjangX* and *kuwH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'to buy back'. The word is a modern short form.
