Spanish, chapter 488: Encuentros y despedidas.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
casarse — to get married.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for to relax, then the Spanish for to have fun.

You'll want to know: casarse.
casarse — "to get married".
Se casan en junio. They are getting married in June.
It goes with con: casarse con alguien.

What you've built.
One more word for reading notices, forms and messages.
