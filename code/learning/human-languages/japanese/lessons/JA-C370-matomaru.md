## You'll want to know: まとまる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C370-MATOMARU370-01]; assesses=[] -->

**まとまる** — *matomaru* — "to be settled; to come together".

> **いけんが まとまったら、ていあんして ください。** — *iken ga matomattara, teian shite kudasai* — "Once your ideas come together, please make a proposal."

Polite **まとまります**, *matomarimasu*; a godan verb. Past (polite): **いけんが まとまりました。**, *iken ga matomarimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**まとまる** (*matomaru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, and its older origin is not settled. JMdict notes that it is usually written in kana (JMdict).
