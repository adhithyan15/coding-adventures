## You'll want to know: नियन्त्रणम्
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C376-YOGYA376-04]; assesses=[] -->

**नियन्त्रणम्** — *niyantraṇam* — "control; restraint".

> यदि चालकस्य नियन्त्रणं न अस्ति, तर्हि भयम् अस्ति।
>
> *yadi cālakasya niyantraṇaṃ na asti, tarhi bhayam asti.*
>
> If the driver has no control, there is danger.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**नियन्त्रणम्** (*niyantraṇam*) is built on *ni* and the root √yantr, "to restrain, to bind"; MW gives *ni-yantraṇa* "restraining, checking, governing" (MW).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
