# மனப்பான்மை (maṉappāṉmai) — an attitude

## You'll want to know: மனப்பான்மை
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C350-KANNOTTAM350-03]; assesses=[] -->

**மனப்பான்மை** — *maṉappāṉmai* — "an attitude".

> அவனுடைய மனப்பான்மை சரியில்லை.
>
> *avaṉuṭaiya maṉappāṉmai cariyillai.*
>
> His attitude is not right.

நல்ல மனப்பான்மை, a good attitude.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**மனப்பான்மை** (*maṉappāṉmai*) is a compound: *maṉam*, "mind", is borrowed from Sanskrit *manas* (CDIAL 9822), and native *pāṉmai*, "portion; nature" (DEDR 4097), gives "the nature of the mind".
