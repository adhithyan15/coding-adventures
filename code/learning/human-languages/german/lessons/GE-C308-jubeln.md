## You'll want to know: jubeln
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C308-ANSTOSSEN-03]; assesses=[] -->

**jubeln** — "to cheer, to rejoice".

> Die Leute jubelten, als die Mannschaft gewann.
>
> *People cheered when the team won.*

Regular: *jubelte*, *hat gejubelt*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*jubeln* is built on *Jubel* "rejoicing", from Middle High German *jūbel*, which came from Latin *iūbilus* "a shout of joy" (EtymDB).
