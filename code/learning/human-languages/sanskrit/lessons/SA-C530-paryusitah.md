# पर्युषितः (paryuṣitaḥ) — stale

## You'll want to know: पर्युषितः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C530-BHOJYA530-05]; assesses=[] -->

**पर्युषितः** — *paryuṣitaḥ* — "stale".

> यदि अन्नं पर्युषितम् अस्ति, तर्हि अहं न खादामि।
>
> *yadi annaṃ paryuṣitam asti, tarhi ahaṃ na khādāmi.*
>
> If the food is stale, I do not eat it.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पर्युषितः** (*paryuṣitaḥ*) is built on *pari* and the root √vas, "to stay, to pass the night", as its past participle: kept overnight; MW gives *paryuṣita* as not fresh, stale (MW).
