Japanese, chapter 197: City Hall, Squares and Factories.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
しやくしょ (shiyakusho) — city hall; municipal office.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for map, then the Japanese for to go round.

You'll want to know: しやくしょ.
しやくしょ — shiyakusho — "city hall; municipal office".
しやくしょは えきの みなみに あります。 — shiyakusho wa eki no minami ni arimasu — "City hall is south of the station."

The word, taken apart.
しやくしょ (shiyakusho) is Sino-Japanese (kango): three Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are shi, "market, city" (Middle Chinese dzyiX), yaku, "duty, office" (ywek) and sho, "place" (srjoX) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).

Lesson 2 of 7.
ひろば (hiroba) — town square; open space, plaza.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for to go round, then the Japanese for city hall.

You'll want to know: ひろば.
ひろば — hiroba — "town square; open space, plaza".
ひろばで まつりが あります。 — hiroba de matsuri ga arimasu — "There is a festival in the square."

The word, taken apart.
ひろば (hiroba) is native Japanese, built from hiro, the stem of hiroi, "wide", and ba, "place", both inherited rather than borrowed (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).

Lesson 4 of 7.
たてる (tateru) — to build; to put up.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for town square, then the Japanese for factory.

You'll want to know: たてる.
たてる — tateru — "to build; to put up".
えきの まえに ひゃっかてんを たてます。 — eki no mae ni hyakkaten o tatemasu — "They will build a department store in front of the station."
Polite たてます, tatemasu; an ichidan verb.

The word, taken apart.
たてる (tateru) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).

Lesson 5 of 7.
みつかる (mitsukaru) — to be found; to turn up.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for factory, then the Japanese for to build.

You'll want to know: みつかる.
みつかる — mitsukaru — "to be found; to turn up".
こうばんで さいふが みつかりました。 — kōban de saifu ga mitsukarimashita — "My wallet turned up at the police box."
Polite みつかります, mitsukarimasu; a godan verb. Its transitive partner みつける is taught already.

The word, taken apart.
みつかる (mitsukaru) is a native Japanese word, inherited rather than borrowed, built on the same stem as mitsukeru, "to find" (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
