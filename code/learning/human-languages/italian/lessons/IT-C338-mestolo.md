## You'll want to know: il mestolo
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C338-FRUSTA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**il mestolo** — "the ladle".

> Aggiungi l'acqua un mestolo alla volta.
>
> *Add the water one ladleful at a time.*

Wooden or steel; *un mestolo di brodo* is a ladleful of broth.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*mestolo* is built on *mestare* "to stir" (EtymDB), from Vulgar Latin *mixtāre*, built on *miscēre* "to mix".
