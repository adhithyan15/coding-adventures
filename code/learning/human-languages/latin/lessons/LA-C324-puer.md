## You'll want to know: puer, puerī
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C324-PUER-02]; assesses=[] -->

**puer, puerī** — "a boy; a child; a slave boy".

> Puer in hortō lūdēbat.
>
> *The boy was playing in the garden.*

Masculine, 2nd declension; the *e* stays in every form. Also a young slave of any age; the plural *puerī* can mean 'children'.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**puer** is inherited from Proto-Indo-European *\*ph₂weros* (EtymDB), from a root meaning "few, small". English *puerile*, "childish", comes from it.
