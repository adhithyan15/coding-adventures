## You'll want to know: ਵਿਗਿਆਨ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C358-SUBJ358-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ਵਿਗਿਆਨ** — *vigiān* — "science".

> ਮੇਰੀ ਭੈਣ ਵਿਗਿਆਨ ਪੜ੍ਹਦੀ ਹੈ।
>
> *merī bhaiṇ vigiān paṛhdī hai.*
>
> My sister studies science.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਵਿਗਿਆਨ** (*vigiān*) is taken over from Sanskrit *vijñāna*, "understanding, knowledge", and also "science", a tatsama (Monier-Williams).
