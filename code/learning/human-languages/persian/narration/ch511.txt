Persian, chapter 511: Outdoor Pastimes.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
اسکی (eski) — skiing; a ski.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to have a good time, then the Persian for to become a member.

You'll want to know: اسکی.
اسکی (eski) — "skiing; a ski".
زمستانها برای اسکی به کوه میرویم. zemestânhâ barâ-ye eski be kuh miravim. In winter we go to the mountains to ski.
eski kardan — to ski. Persian puts e- before the sk- cluster.

The word, taken apart.
eski was borrowed from French ski, which EtymDB traces to Norwegian ski and Old Norse skíð, 'stick of wood, snowshoe' (EtymDB).

Lesson 2 of 6.
چادر زدن (châdor zadan) — to pitch a tent, to camp.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to become a member, then the Persian for skiing.

You'll want to know: چادر زدن.
چادر زدن (châdor zadan) — "to pitch a tent, to camp".
ما کنار رودخانه چادر زدیم. mâ kenâr-e rudkhâne châdor zadim. We pitched a tent by the river.
châdor 'tent; veil' + zadan 'to strike'; present stem zan-.

The word, taken apart.
châdor is inherited: EtymDB traces it to Middle Persian čādur, 'sheet, veil' (EtymDB). A tent is a sheet you pitch, and zadan, 'to strike', is inherited too.

Lesson 3 of 6.
اسب سواری (asb savâri) — horse riding.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for skiing, then the Persian for to pitch a tent.

You'll want to know: اسب سواری.
اسب سواری (asb savâri) — "horse riding".
او در روستا اسب سواری یاد گرفت. u dar rustâ asb savâri yâd gereft. She learned to ride in the village.
asb 'horse' + savâri 'riding' (savâr 'mounted').

The word, taken apart.
asb savâri is built on two native words. asb, 'horse', is inherited, and EtymDB lists Latin equus and Greek hippos as cousins (EtymDB); savâri is built on savâr, 'mounted'.

Lesson 4 of 6.
پیاده روی (piyâde ravi) — walking (for exercise), a walk.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to pitch a tent, then the Persian for horse riding.

You'll want to know: پیاده روی.
پیاده روی (piyâde ravi) — "walking (for exercise), a walk".
پدربزرگم هر صبح پیاده روی میکند. pedarbozorgam har sobh piyâde ravi mikonad. My grandfather goes walking every morning.
piyâde 'on foot' + ravi 'going'. Not piyâderow, the pavement.

The word, taken apart.
piyâde ravi is built on native Persian parts: piyâde, 'on foot', which EtymDB traces to Middle Persian (EtymDB), and ravi, from rav-, the present stem of raftan, 'to go'.

Lesson 5 of 6.
قایقرانی (qâyeqrâni) — rowing, boating.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for horse riding, then the Persian for walking.

You'll want to know: قایقرانی.
قایقرانی (qâyeqrâni) — "rowing, boating".
تابستان در دریاچه قایقرانی کردیم. tâbestân dar daryâche qâyeqrâni kardim. In summer we went boating on the lake.
qâyeq 'boat' + rân 'driving' (stem of rândan) + -i.

The word, taken apart.
qâyeqrâni is built in Persian on qâyeq, 'boat', a loan usually traced to Turkic qayıq, and rân, the stem of inherited rândan, 'to drive'.
