## You'll want to know: 欢送
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C336-SEEOFF336-02]; assesses=[] -->

**欢送** — *huānsòng* — "to give a send-off".

> **我们开了一个晚会欢送他** *wǒmen kāi le yí ge wǎnhuì huānsòng tā* — We held a party to see him off.

The new character, in pieces: 送 — nine strokes: 关 with its last stroke shrunk to a dot, inside the walking piece of 这, which wraps it from the left and runs along the foot. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**欢送** (*huānsòng*) is built from 欢 'glad' and *sòng* 'to send', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *xwan* and *suwngH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'joy' and the second 'to send away'.
