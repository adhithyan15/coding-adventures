## You'll want to know: yùndòngyuán
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C501-WINNERS501-01]; assesses=[] -->

*yùndòngyuán* — "athlete".

> *wǒ xiǎoshíhou xiǎng dāng yùndòngyuán* — When I was a kid I wanted to be an athlete.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*yùndòngyuán* is built from *yùndòng* 'sport' and *yuán* 'a member', all inherited from Middle Chinese, *hjunH*, *duwngX* and *hjwen* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to move' and the third 'a count of things'.
