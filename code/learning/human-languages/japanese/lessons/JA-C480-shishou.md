## You'll want to know: ししょう
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C480-MAKE480-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ししょう** — *shishō* — "a master (of a craft); a teacher".

> **ししょうは でしに しごとを おしえます。** — *shishō wa deshi ni shigoto o oshiemasu* — "The master teaches the apprentice the work."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ししょう** (*shishō*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *shi*, "expert" (Middle Chinese *srij*) and *shō*, "artisan" (*dzjangH*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
