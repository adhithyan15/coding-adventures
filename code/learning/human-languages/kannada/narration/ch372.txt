Kannada, chapter 372: Exile and Punishment.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ವನವಾಸ (vanavāsa) — exile in the forest.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a mansion, then the Kannada for to set foot.

You'll want to know: ವನವಾಸ.
ವನವಾಸ — vanavāsa — "exile in the forest".
ಕಥೆಯಲ್ಲಿ ರಾಜಕುಮಾರ ಹನ್ನೆರಡು ವರ್ಷ ವನವಾಸ ಮಾಡಿದನು. katheyalli rājakumāra hanneraḍu varṣa vanavāsa māḍidanu. In the story the prince spent twelve years in exile in the forest.
The epics are full of ವನವಾಸ (vanavāsa); today it can mean any long hard time away.

The word, taken apart.
ವನವಾಸ (vanavāsa) is borrowed from Sanskrit vanavāsa, "dwelling in the forest" (CDIAL 11265a), from vana "forest" and vāsa "dwelling".

ವಿಧಿಸು — vidhisu — "to impose (a penalty)".
ನ್ಯಾಯಾಧೀಶರು ಕಳ್ಳನಿಗೆ ದಂಡ ವಿಧಿಸಿದರು. nyāyādhīśaru kaḷḷanige daṇḍa vidhisidaru. The judge imposed a fine on the thief.
Past (I …): ವಿಧಿಸಿದೆ, vidhiside.
Punishments, fines, taxes and rules are imposed; the person takes -ಗೆ.

The word, taken apart.
ವಿಧಿಸು (vidhisu) is built on Sanskrit vidhi, "a rule, an ordinance", and "fate" (CDIAL 11755), with the Kannada verb ending -isu.

Lesson 3 of 5.
ಗಡೀಪಾರು (gaḍīpāru) — banishment; deportation.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for exile in the forest, then the Kannada for to impose.

You'll want to know: ಗಡೀಪಾರು.
ಗಡೀಪಾರು — gaḍīpāru — "banishment; deportation".
ನ್ಯಾಯಾಲಯ ಆ ರೌಡಿಗೆ ಗಡೀಪಾರು ಶಿಕ್ಷೆ ವಿಧಿಸಿತು. nyāyālaya ā rauḍige gaḍīpāru śikṣe vidhisitu. The court sentenced that thug to banishment.
ಗಡೀಪಾರು (gaḍīpāru) ಮಾಡು, to banish someone from a district or a country.

The word, taken apart.
ಗಡೀಪಾರು (gaḍīpāru) is usually traced to ಗಡಿ, "a border, a limit" (DEDR 1109), and ಪಾರು, "across, beyond", from Sanskrit pāra, "the further bank" (CDIAL 8100): sent beyond the border.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ಕಳ್ಳತನ (kaḷḷatana) — theft.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for banishment, then the Kannada for former.

You'll want to know: ಕಳ್ಳತನ.
ಕಳ್ಳತನ — kaḷḷatana — "theft".
ಊರಿನಲ್ಲಿ ಕಳ್ಳತನ ಹೆಚ್ಚಾದಾಗ ಪೊಲೀಸರು ಗಸ್ತು ಶುರು ಮಾಡಿದರು. ūrinalli kaḷḷatana heccādāga polīsaru gastu śuru māḍidaru. When thefts increased in the town, the police began patrols.
ಕಳ್ಳ, a thief, with -ತನ, "-ness, the doing of": ಕಳ್ಳತನ (kaḷḷatana) ಮಾಡು, to steal.

The word, taken apart.
ಕಳ್ಳತನ (kaḷḷatana) is a native Dravidian noun: kaḷḷa, "a thief" (DEDR 1372), with the abstract ending -tana.
