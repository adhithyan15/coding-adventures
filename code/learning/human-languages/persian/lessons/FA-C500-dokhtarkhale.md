## You'll want to know: دخترخاله
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C500-UNCLES-04]; assesses=[] -->

**دخترخاله** (*dokhtarkhâle*) — "a maternal cousin (daughter of a mother's sister)".

> دخترخاله من دانشجوی پزشکی است.
>
> *dokhtarkhâle-ye man dâneshju-ye pezeshki ast.*
>
> My cousin is a medical student.

*dokhtar* 'daughter' + *khâle* 'maternal aunt'.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*dokhtarkhâle* is built in Persian on native *dokhtar*, 'daughter', and *khâle*, 'maternal aunt', which was borrowed from Arabic *ḫāla*.
