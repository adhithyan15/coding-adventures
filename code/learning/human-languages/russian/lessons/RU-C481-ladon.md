## You'll want to know: ладонь
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C481-BACK-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ладонь** (*ladón*) — "a palm (of the hand)".

> Ребёнок держал бабочку на ладони.
>
> *Rebyónok derzhál bábochku na ladóni.*
>
> The child held a butterfly on his palm.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

EtymDB lists *ладонь* without a source; it is usually traced to older *долонь*, from Proto-Slavic *dolnь*. Church Slavonic *длань* is the same word with *-ла-*: EtymDB traces *длань* through Church Slavonic to Proto-Slavic *dolnь* 'palm'.
