## You'll want to know: বিদেশ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C395-BIMAN395-04]; assesses=[] -->

**বিদেশ** — *bidesh* — "abroad; a foreign country".

> মীরার ভাই বিদেশে থাকে।
>
> *mīrār bhāi bideshe thāke.*
>
> Mira's brother lives abroad.

বিদেশে, abroad (being there); বিদেশে যাওয়া, to go abroad. বিদেশি, foreign, is built on it.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**বিদেশ** (*bidesh*) is Sanskrit *videśa*, "another country, abroad" (Monier-Williams), from *vi-*, apart, and *deśa*, a country: the land away from home.
