Gujarati, chapter 522: Pound, Grate and Soak.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ખાયણી (khāyṇī) — a mortar (for pounding).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to boil over, then the Gujarati for to whisk.

You'll want to know: ખાયણી.
ખાયણી — khāyṇī — "a mortar (for pounding)".
દાદીની ખાયણી પથ્થરની છે. dādīnī khāyṇī paththarnī che. Grandmother's mortar is made of stone.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ખાયણી (khāyṇī) is filed by CDIAL under Sanskrit khaṇḍana, "breaking", with the Gujarati word for a mortar (CDIAL 3796).

Lesson 2 of 5.
ખાંડવું (khā̃ḍvũ) — to pound.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to whisk, then the Gujarati for a mortar.

You'll want to know: ખાંડવું.
ખાંડવું — khā̃ḍvũ — "to pound".
દાદીએ ખાયણીમાં આદુ ખાંડ્યું. dādīe khāyṇīmā̃ ādu khā̃ḍyũ. Grandmother pounded the ginger in the mortar.
Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે આદુ ખાંડ્યું, teṇe ādu khā̃ḍyũ.

The word, taken apart.
ખાંડવું (khā̃ḍvũ) is inherited from Sanskrit kháṇḍatē, "breaks", and CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb, "to break, pound" (CDIAL 3795). Sugar, ખાંડ, comes from the same root: sugar in pieces.

છીણવું — chīṇvũ — "to grate".
મીરાએ ગાજર છીણ્યું. Mīrāe gājar chīṇyũ. Mira grated the carrot.
Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે ગાજર છીણ્યું, teṇe gājar chīṇyũ.

The word, taken apart.
છીણવું (chīṇvũ) is filed by CDIAL under Sanskrit chinátti, "cuts off", with a Gujarati chiṇvũ, "to slit, peel" (CDIAL 5045); grating is probably the same cutting, into fine shreds.

Lesson 4 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to pound, then the Gujarati for to grate.

You'll want to know: પલાળવું.
પલાળવું — palāḷvũ — "to soak".
રાત્રે મગ પાણીમાં પલાળો. rātre mag pāṇīmā̃ palāḷo. Soak the mung beans in water overnight.
Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે મગ પલાળ્યા, teṇe mag palāḷyā.

The word, taken apart.
પલાળવું (palāḷvũ) is the causative partner of પલળવું, "to get soaked", and CDIAL lists it under pralālayatē as "to make wet, soak" (CDIAL 8759).
