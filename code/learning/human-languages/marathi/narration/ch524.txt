Marathi, chapter 524: Cleaning Day.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
कचरा (kacrā) — rubbish; litter.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a mat, then the Marathi for to roll up.

You'll want to know: कचरा.
कचरा — kacrā — "rubbish; litter".
उद्यानात खूप कचरा पडला होता. udyānāt khūp kacrā paḍlā hotā. There was a lot of litter lying in the park.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
कचरा (kacrā) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Marathi kacrā, "rubbish", under Sanskrit kaccara, "dirty, vile" (CDIAL 2615).

Lesson 2 of 5.
केरसुणी (kersuṇī) — a grass broom.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to roll up, then the Marathi for rubbish.

You'll want to know: केरसुणी.
केरसुणी — kersuṇī — "a grass broom".
आईने केरसुणीने खोली झाडली. āīne kersuṇīne kholī jhāḍlī. Mother swept the room with a grass broom.
Feminine.
Softer than a झाडू, for sweeping indoors.

The word, taken apart.
केरसुणी (kersuṇī) is a compound of केर, "sweepings", and सुणी; the origin of both parts is not settled.

Lesson 3 of 5.
पोतेरे (potere) — a floor cloth.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for rubbish, then the Marathi for a grass broom.

You'll want to know: पोतेरे.
पोतेरे — potere — "a floor cloth".
न्हाणीघराबाहेर एक जुनं पोतेरं पडलं होतं. nhāṇīgharābāher ek junaṁ poteraṁ paḍlaṁ hotaṁ. An old floor cloth lay outside the bathroom.
Neuter.
The dictionary writes पोतेरे (potere); a sentence says पोतेरं, like जुनं.

The word, taken apart.
पोतेरे (potere) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi potrẽ, "a rag for smearing", under Sanskrit pōta, "cloth" (CDIAL 8400).

You'll want to know: झटकणे.
झटकणे — jhaṭakṇe — "to shake off; to dust".
आजीने गच्चीवर चादर झटकली. ājīne gaccīvar cādar jhaṭaklī. Grandmother shook out the sheet on the terrace.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने चादर झटकली, tyāne cādar jhaṭaklī.

The word, taken apart.
झटकणे (jhaṭakṇe) is inherited: CDIAL sets Marathi jhaṭakṇẽ among the words of sudden movement under jhaṭ (CDIAL 5327).
