Portuguese, chapter 53: Phone, Umbrella, Money, Present, Watch.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
o telefone — a phone.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for a pencil, then the Portuguese for a bag.

You'll want to know: o telefone.
