## You'll want to know: সংশোধন করা
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C382-NISHTHA382-05]; assesses=[] -->

**সংশোধন করা** — *sôngshodhôn kôrā* — "to correct; to amend".

> ভুল থাকলে প্রবন্ধটা সংশোধন করো।
>
> *bhul thākle prôbôndhôṭā sôngshodhôn kôro.*
>
> If there are mistakes, correct the essay.

Past (he/she …): তিনি আইনটা সংশোধন করলেন, *tini āinṭā sôngshodhôn kôrlen*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**সংশোধন করা** (*sôngshodhôn kôrā*) is Sanskrit *saṃśodhana*, thorough cleansing (Monier-Williams), with করা. Bengali cleans out errors: to correct a text or amend a law.
