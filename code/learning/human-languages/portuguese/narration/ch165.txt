Portuguese, chapter 165: Leather, Metal, Brick, Sack, Thread.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
o couro — leather.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for a pebble, then the Portuguese for earth, land.

You'll want to know: o couro.
o couro — "leather".

The word, taken apart.
couro from Latin corium, "hide, skin" (EtymDB 2.1) — inherited. Old Portuguese said coiro.
