## You'll want to know: પ્રામાણિક
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C270-NATURE270-03]; assesses=[] -->

**પ્રામાણિક** — *prāmāṇik* — "honest".

> **તે વેપારી પ્રામાણિક નથી.** — *te vepārī prāmāṇik nathī* — "That trader is not honest."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**પ્રામાણિક** (*prāmāṇik*) is Sanskrit *prāmāṇika*, "founded on authority", built on *pramāṇa*, "measure, proof", and taken over as a tatsama (CDIAL 8949).
