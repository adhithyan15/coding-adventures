## You'll want to know: うたごえ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C503-SONG503-01]; assesses=[] -->

**うたごえ** — *utagoe* — "a singing voice; the sound of singing".

> **となりの へやから うたごえが しました。** — *tonari no heya kara utagoe ga shimashita* — "There was singing from the next room."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**うたごえ** (*utagoe*) is a native Japanese compound (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese: built from *uta*, "song" and *goe*, "voice" (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
