## You'll want to know: સોગંદ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C540-BALJABRI540-05]; assesses=[] -->

**સોગંદ** — *sogand* — "an oath".

> જો તમે સોગંદ ખાશો તો જ હું માનીશ.
>
> *jo tame sogand khāśo to ja hũ mānīś.*
>
> Only if you swear will I believe you.

A masculine noun, used in the plural.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**સોગંદ** (*sogand*) is usually traced to Persian *saugand*, "an oath". Gujarati "eats" an oath: **સોગંદ ખાવા**.
