Persian, chapter 225: To memorise, To moan, To yawn, To vomit, To swell up.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
حفظ کردن (hefz kardan) — to memorise.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to separate, then the Persian for to peel.

You'll want to know: حفظ کردن.
حفظ کردن — hefz kardan — "to memorise".

[pause 3 seconds]
