## You'll want to know: دنده
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C420-FARMAN-03]; assesses=[] -->

**دنده** (*dande*) — "a gear (of a car); a rib".

> این ماشین پنج دنده دارد.
>
> *In mâshin panj dande dârad.*
>
> This car has five gears.

Also a rib of the body. *Dande-ye 'aqab* — reverse gear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*dande* is a native Persian word for a rib, later also for the teeth and gears of a wheel. Its older history is not settled, and EtymDB does not list it.
