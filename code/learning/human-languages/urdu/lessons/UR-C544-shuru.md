## You'll want to know: شروع
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C544-AGHAZ544-02]; assesses=[] -->

**شروع** — *shurū* — "the start (shurū karnā: to begin)".

> اگر میچ وقت پر شروع ہوا تو ہم دیکھیں گے۔
>
> *agar maich vaqt par shurū huā to ham dekheṅ ge.*
>
> If the match starts on time, we'll watch it.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**شروع** (*shurū*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *shurūʿ*, "setting about, beginning".
