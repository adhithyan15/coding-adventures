# ऋ — the vowel letter at the start of ऋतू

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MR-LEX-C279-RTU-01, MR-SCRIPT-RU-MATRA-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say **ऋतू**, "a season". Then find the sign **ृ** in **नृत्य**, "dance", and say its sound: *ru*.

One new character comes next: the first letter of **ऋतू**.

## Script
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-SCRIPT-VOCALIC-R-01]; assesses=[MR-SCRIPT-RU-MATRA-01] -->

> ऋ

**ऋ** is the standing form of the sign **ृ**. The sign hangs on a consonant (**नृत्य**, **हृदय**); the letter starts a word (**ऋतू**). Its careful transliteration is *ṛ*; Marathi says it *ru*. Keep it apart from **र**, the consonant *ra*: **ऋ** is a vowel letter, and it stands alone.

## Writing: ऋ — guided copy
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MR-SCRIPT-VOCALIC-R-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: guided-copy -->

Keep **ऋ** in front of you for this first pass.

- **1.** start at the open upper-left tip, sweep right through the short shoulder, then turn down-left through the diagonal hook
- **2.** lift at the middle body's left edge, loop anticlockwise round its little ball, then run down to the right and swing the lower bowl left, round the bottom, and out to the right without lifting
- **3.** lift and draw the right stem top-to-bottom
- **4.** lift and draw the top shirorekhā left-to-right

**Pen lifts: 3.** This is one sourced learner order; hands and teachers vary. Source: Saurmandal, ‘Devanagari ऋ stroke order.svg’, panels 1–4, Wikimedia Commons, 5 August 2023.

[PAUSE 1s] Copy **ऋ** twice beside the model, saying *ru* as you finish each one. Then write **ऋतू** once.
