5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
le CV — the CV, the résumé.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for to lessen, then the French for to make up for.

You'll want to know: le CV.
le CV — "the CV, the résumé".
J'ai envoyé mon CV à dix entreprises. I sent my CV to ten companies.
Said cé-vé: short for Latin curriculum vitae. Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
CV is short for curriculum vitae, Latin for "a running of life": curriculum "a running, a course", from currere "to run", and vīta "life" (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 2 of 5.
le candidat — the candidate, the applicant.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for to make up for, then the French for the CV.

You'll want to know: le candidat.
le candidat — "the candidate, the applicant".
Il y a vingt candidats pour ce poste. There are twenty applicants for this job.
Feminine candidate. Also in elections.

The word, taken apart.
candidat comes from Latin candidātus "clothed in white" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short), from candidus "dazzling white": in Rome, people seeking office wore a whitened toga.

Lesson 3 of 5.

You'll want to know: la candidature.
la candidature — "the application (for a job)".
J'ai envoyé ma candidature hier soir. I sent my application last night.
Poser sa candidature — to apply.

The word, taken apart.
candidature is built from candidat and the suffix -ure. candidat comes from Latin candidātus "clothed in white", from candidus "white" (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 5 of 5.
l'embauche — the hiring, being taken on.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for the application, then the French for to apply.

You'll want to know: l'embauche.
l'embauche — "the hiring, being taken on".
Après l'entretien, il a eu une promesse d'embauche. After the interview, he got a written job offer.
Une promesse d'embauche — a written job offer. Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
embauche is formed from the verb meaning to take on a worker (EtymDB), built on an old word bauche "workshop". The origin of bauche is not settled.
