## You'll want to know: gēnběn
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C538-CAUSE538-05]; assesses=[] -->

*gēnběn* — "not in the least; (not) at all".

> *jíshǐ nǐ shuō le tā yě gēnběn bù tīng* — Even if you tell him, he won't listen at all.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*gēnběn* is built from *gēn* 'a root' and 本 'the base of a tree', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *kon* and *pwonX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'roots': down to the roots.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what would happen.
