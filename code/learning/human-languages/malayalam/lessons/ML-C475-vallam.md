# വള്ളം (vaḷḷaṁ) — a country boat

## You'll want to know: വള്ളം
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C475-KAPPAL475-04]; assesses=[] -->

**വള്ളം** — *vaḷḷaṁ* — "a country boat".

> രാവിലെ ആ വള്ളം കായലിലൂടെ പോയി.
>
> *rāvile ā vaḷḷaṁ kāyalilūṭe pōyi.*
>
> In the morning that boat went along the backwaters.

You met it inside വള്ളംകളി, the boat race. A boatman is a വള്ളക്കാരൻ.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**വള്ളം** (*vaḷḷaṁ*) is native Dravidian; DEDR gives the Malayalam word as a canoe hollowed from one trunk (DEDR 5315).
