## Grammar Lens: the verb, 得, then how well
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-GRAMMAR-DE-COMPLEMENT-01]; assesses=[ZH-LEX-C62-FAST62-01, ZH-ORTHO-HANYU-01, ZH-LEX-HEN-01, ZH-LEX-C152-DE-01] -->

> **After a verb, 得 brings in how well or how fast it is done. The adjective after 得 usually takes 很.**

> **你说得很好** *nǐ shuō de hěn hǎo* — You speak well.
> **他说得很快** *tā shuō de hěn kuài* — He talks fast.

With an object, say the verb twice: once with the object, once with 得:

> **他说汉语说得很快** *tā shuō Hànyǔ shuō de hěn kuài* — He speaks Chinese fast.

很 here is light, as it is in 你很好: it carries the sentence more than it means *very*.
