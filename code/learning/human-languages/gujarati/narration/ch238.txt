Gujarati, chapter 238: Creatures in the house.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
બિલાડી (bilāḍī) — cat.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to flutter, then the Gujarati for to fly up.

You'll want to know: બિલાડી.
બિલાડી — bilāḍī — "cat".
બિલાડી રાત્રે ઘરમાં આવી. — bilāḍī rātre gharmā̃ āvī — "The cat came into the house at night."

The word, taken apart.
બિલાડી (bilāḍī) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from biḍāla, "cat" (CDIAL 9237).

ગરોળી બારી પાસે હતી. — garoḷī bārī pāse hatī — "The lizard was near the window."

ગરોળી (garoḷī) is inherited. CDIAL lists the Gujarati gharoḷī, "house lizard", under gharagolī, a reconstructed form built on ghara, "house" (CDIAL 4431).

Lesson 3 of 5.
કરોળિયો (karoḷiyo) — spider.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for cat, then the Gujarati for house lizard.

You'll want to know: કરોળિયો.
કરોળિયો — karoḷiyo — "spider".
છત પાસે એક કરોળિયો હતો. — chat pāse ek karoḷiyo hato — "There was a spider near the ceiling."

The word, taken apart.
કરોળિયો (karoḷiyo) is a tadbhava. CDIAL lists it, glossed "spider", under Sanskrit kolika, "weaver", so the spider is named for its weaving (CDIAL 3535).

લટકવું (laṭakvũ) — to hang, to dangle.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for spider, then the Gujarati for ant.

You'll want to know: લટકવું.
લટકવું — laṭakvũ — "to hang, to dangle".
કરોળિયો હવામાં લટક્યો. — karoḷiyo havāmā̃ laṭakyo — "The spider hung in the air."

The word, taken apart.
લટકવું (laṭakvũ) comes from laṭṭa, "hanging, tangled", a reconstructed form; CDIAL glosses the Gujarati verb "to hang down" (CDIAL 10918).
