## You'll want to know: gōngrén
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C80-JOB80-02]; assesses=[] -->

*gōngrén* — "worker".

> *wǒ bàba shì gōngrén* — My dad is a worker.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*gōngrén* is built from gōng 'craft, work' and 人 'person', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *kuwng* and *nyin* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word about people.
