## You'll want to know: भाग्य
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C550-NASHIB550-03]; assesses=[] -->

**भाग्य** — *bhāgya* — "good fortune".

> अशी आई मिळणं हे माझं भाग्य आहे.
>
> *aśī āī miḷṇaṁ he mājhaṁ bhāgya āhe.*
>
> Having a mother like this is my good fortune.

Neuter.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**भाग्य** (*bhāgya*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *bhāgya*, "one's lot, fortune" (Monier-Williams).
