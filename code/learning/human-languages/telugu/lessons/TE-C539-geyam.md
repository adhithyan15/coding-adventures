## You'll want to know: గేయం
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C539-PADYAM539-04]; assesses=[] -->

**గేయం** — *gēyaṁ* — "a lyric; a song text".

> ఆ కవి రాసిన గేయం బాగుంది.
>
> *ā kavi rāsina gēyaṁ bāgundi.*
>
> The lyric that poet wrote is good.

Formal: verse written to be sung.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**గేయం** (*gēyaṁ*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *gēya*, "to be sung; singing, a song" (CDIAL 4249).
