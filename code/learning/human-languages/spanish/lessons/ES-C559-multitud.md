## You'll want to know: la multitud
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C559-DESFILE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**la multitud** — "the crowd".

> Una multitud esperaba en la plaza.
>
> *A crowd was waiting in the square.*

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*multitud* is a learned borrowing from Latin *multitūdō* 'a great number' (EtymDB), built on *multus* 'much, many'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.
