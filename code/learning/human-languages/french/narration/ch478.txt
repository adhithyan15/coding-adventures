French, chapter 478: Le visage de près.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
le sourcil — eyebrow.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for toe, then the French for hip.

You'll want to know: le sourcil.
le sourcil — "eyebrow".
Il lève les sourcils quand il est surpris. He raises his eyebrows when he is surprised.
Froncer les sourcils — to frown.

The word, taken apart.
le sourcil is inherited from Latin supercilium "eyebrow" (EtymDB), built from super "above" and cilium "eyelid" (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 5 of 5.

You'll want to know: la tempe.
la tempe — "temple (side of the head)".
Quand je suis fatigué, j'ai mal aux tempes. When I am tired, my temples ache.
A temple for worship is un temple, a different word.

The word, taken apart.
la tempe is inherited from Vulgar Latin tempula (EtymDB), from Latin tempora "the temples of the head" (Lewis & Short), a different word from tempus "time".
