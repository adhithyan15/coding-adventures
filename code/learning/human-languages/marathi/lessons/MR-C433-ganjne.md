## You'll want to know: गंजणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C433-TADA433-03]; assesses=[] -->

**गंजणे** — *gañjṇe* — "to rust".

> पावसात सायकल गंजली.
>
> *pāvsāt sāykal gañjlī.*
>
> The bicycle rusted in the rain.

Past (he …): तो गंजला, *to gañjlā*.

The noun is गंज, rust: गंज चढणे, rust sets in.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**गंजणे** (*gañjṇe*) is built on **गंज**, "rust", whose origin is not settled.
