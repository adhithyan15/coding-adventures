## You'll want to know: kěndìng
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C352-AGREE352-02]; assesses=[] -->

*kěndìng* — "for sure; without doubt".

> *wǒ juéde tā kěndìng huì tóngyì* — I'm sure he'll agree.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*kěndìng* is built from two parts, both inherited from Middle Chinese, *khongX* and *dengH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to permit' and the second 'to settle'.
