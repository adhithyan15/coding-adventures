## You'll want to know: ゆうしょう
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C235-BALL235-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ゆうしょう** — *yūshō* — "a championship win; overall victory".

> **せんげつ あにの がっこうが ゆうしょうしました。** — *sengetsu ani no gakkō ga yūshō shimashita* — "Last month my elder brother's school won the championship."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ゆうしょう** (*yūshō*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *yū*, "excel" (Middle Chinese *'juw*) and *shō*, "win" (*syingH*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
