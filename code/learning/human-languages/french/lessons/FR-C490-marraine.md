## You'll want to know: la marraine
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C490-PARRAIN-02]; assesses=[] -->

**la marraine** — "godmother".

> Ma marraine habite à la campagne.
>
> *My godmother lives in the country.*

Also a sponsor: *la marraine d'une association*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*la marraine* is inherited, through Old French *marrene*, from Vulgar Latin *mātrīna* (EtymDB), built on *māter* "mother" (Lewis & Short).
