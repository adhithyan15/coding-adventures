# दृढः (dṛḍhaḥ) — firm

## You'll want to know: दृढः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C278-DRDHAH-01]; assesses=[SA-LEX-C43-COURTESY-04, SA-LEX-C16-PLACE-01, SA-LEX-C256-WRK256-03] -->

**दृढः** — *dṛḍhaḥ* — "firm". It agrees with its noun: **दृढः** for a masculine noun, **दृढा** (*dṛḍhā*) for a feminine one, **दृढम्** (*dṛḍham*) for a neuter one.

> **मम दृढः विश्वासः अस्ति।**
>
> *mama dṛḍhaḥ viśvāsaḥ asti.* — "I have a firm belief."

> **गृहं दृढम् अस्ति।**
>
> *gṛhaṃ dṛḍham asti.* — "The house is solid."

> **मम निर्णयः दृढः।**
>
> *mama nirṇayaḥ dṛḍhaḥ.* — "My decision is firm."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**दृढः** (*dṛḍhaḥ*) comes from **दृढ** (*dṛḍha*), "fixed, firm, strong", in Monier-Williams; CDIAL 6508 sets it under the root √dṛṃh and lists Hindi *diṛh*, "firm", among its descendants.
