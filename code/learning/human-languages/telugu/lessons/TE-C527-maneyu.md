# మానేయు (mānēyu) — to quit; to stop

## You'll want to know: మానేయు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C527-VIJAYAM527-05]; assesses=[] -->

**మానేయు** — *mānēyu* — "to quit; to stop".

> అన్నయ్య పాత ఉద్యోగం మానేశాడు.
>
> *annayya pāta udyōgaṁ mānēśāḍu.*
>
> My elder brother quit his old job.

Past (I …): మానేశాను, *mānēśānu*.

With a job, a habit or study: చదువు మానేయు, to drop out of school.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**మానేయు** (*mānēyu*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 4811 lists Telugu *mānu*, "to cease, to stop, to give up, to quit"; *vēyu*, "to put", makes the stopping final.
