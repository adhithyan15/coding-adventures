## You'll want to know: la spugna
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C534-PIATTI-03]; assesses=[] -->

**la spugna** — "sponge".

> Lavo le pentole con la spugna.
>
> *I wash the pans with the sponge.*

*Gettare la spugna* — to throw in the towel.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*spugna* is inherited from Latin *spongia* "a sponge" (EtymDB), from Greek *spongiá*.
