## You'll want to know: ചൂട്
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C118-WEATHER-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ചൂട്** (*cūṭŭ*) — "heat", and also "hot". **നല്ല ചൂട്** — "it's really hot".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

The opposite of taṇuppŭ.
