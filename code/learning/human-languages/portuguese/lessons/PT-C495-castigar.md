## You'll want to know: castigar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C495-EDUCAR-03]; assesses=[] -->

**castigar** — "to punish".

> Os pais castigaram o filho: uma semana sem televisão.
>
> *The parents punished their son: a week without television.*

*Ficar de castigo* — to be grounded.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*castigar* is inherited, through Old Portuguese, from Latin *castīgāre* "to correct, to punish" (EtymDB).
