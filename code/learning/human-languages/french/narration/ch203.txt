Lesson 1 of 5.
la défaite — a defeat.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for a small bag, then the French for a rope.

You'll want to know: la défaite.
la défaite — "a defeat".

The word, taken apart.
défaite is the past participle of the verb for "to undo", used as a noun: an undoing. Old French had desfait, "undone" (EtymDB 2.1); the verb is built on Latin facere, "to do".
