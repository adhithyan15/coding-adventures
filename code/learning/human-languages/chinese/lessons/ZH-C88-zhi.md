## You'll want to know: zhī
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C88-ZHI-01]; assesses=[] -->

*zhī* — a measure word for animals, and for one of a pair.

> *liǎng zhī māo* — two cats

> *yì zhī shǒu* — one hand

Its character comes later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*zhī* is inherited from Middle Chinese *tsyek* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'one'; the Guangyun, quoting an older dictionary, describes the old character as a hand holding a single bird.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

Cats, dogs, birds: animals take *zhī*. So does one of a pair, such as a hand: *yì zhī shǒu*.
