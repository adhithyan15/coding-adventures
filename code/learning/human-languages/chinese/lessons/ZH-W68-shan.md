## Script — three peaks on a base
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-SCRIPT-SHAN-01]; assesses=[] -->

> 山

**One piece, three strokes, the middle first.**

**The tall vertical** in the middle.

**A vertical down the left that turns** and runs right along the bottom.

**A vertical down the right**, standing on the base.
