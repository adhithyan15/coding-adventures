## You'll want to know: نفض
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C550-SIYANA550-03]; assesses=[] -->

**نفض** — *nafaḍa* — "to shake out (dust)".

> نفضت أمي السجادة في الشرفة.
>
> *nafaḍat ummī as-sajjāda fī ash-shurfa.*
>
> My mother shook out the carpet on the balcony.

Present: ينفض, *yanfuḍ*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**نفض** (*nafaḍa*) is built on the native Arabic root *n-f-ḍ*, "to shake a thing to clear it" (Lane). Lane gives nafaḍa, aor. yanfuḍu, as to shake a cloth so that what is on it falls off.
