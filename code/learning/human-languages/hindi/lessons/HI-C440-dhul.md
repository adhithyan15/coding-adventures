## You'll want to know: धूल
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C440-GHIRNA440-01]; assesses=[] -->

**धूल** — *dhūl* — "dust".

> आँधी के बाद हर चीज़ पर धूल थी।
>
> *ā̃dhī ke bād har cīz par dhūl thī.*
>
> After the dust storm everything was covered in dust.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**धूल** (*dhūl*) comes from Sanskrit *dhūli*, "dust, powder", which CDIAL files under *\*dhūḍi* (CDIAL 6835) with Hindi *dhūl*, "dust".
