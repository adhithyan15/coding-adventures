The word, taken apart.
బూట్లు (būṭlu) is a loan from English boots.

Lesson 2 of 5.
గొలుసు (golusu) — a chain, a necklace.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for buttermilk, then the Telugu for shoes.

You'll want to know: గొలుసు.
గొలుసు — golusu — "a chain, a necklace".

The word, taken apart.
గొలుసు (golusu) is native Dravidian, one word with Tamil kolucu and Kannada golasu (DEDR 2134). DEDR lists Telugu golusu, "a chain".

Lesson 3 of 5.
చెవిపోగులు (cevipōgulu) — earrings.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for shoes, then the Telugu for a chain.

You'll want to know: చెవిపోగులు.
చెవిపోగులు — cevipōgulu — "earrings".

The word, taken apart.
చెవిపోగులు (cevipōgulu) is native cevi, "ear", one word with Tamil cevi (DEDR 1977), and pōgu, "earring", which DEDR 4545 lists with a query.

Lesson 5 of 5.
చేతిరుమాలు (cētirumālu) — a handkerchief.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for earrings, then the Telugu for a button.

You'll want to know: చేతిరుమాలు.
చేతిరుమాలు — cētirumālu — "a handkerchief".

The word, taken apart.
చేతిరుమాలు (cētirumālu) is native cēti, "of the hand", and rumālu, a loan from Persian rūmāl, "handkerchief", through Hindustani.
