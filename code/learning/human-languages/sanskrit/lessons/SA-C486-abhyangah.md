## You'll want to know: अभ्यंगः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C486-UPAVASA486-04]; assesses=[] -->

**अभ्यंगः** — *abhyaṅgaḥ* — "an oil massage".

> शीते तैलस्य अभ्यंगः सुखं ददाति।
>
> *śīte tailasya abhyaṅgaḥ sukhaṃ dadāti.*
>
> In the cold an oil massage brings comfort.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अभ्यंगः** (*abhyaṅgaḥ*) is built from *abhi* and the root √añj, "to anoint"; MW gives "rubbing with unctuous substances, inunction" (MW).
