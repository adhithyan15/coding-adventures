## You'll want to know: baptiser
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C309-BAPTEME-03]; assesses=[] -->

**baptiser** — "to christen, to baptise; to name".

> Le prêtre a baptisé l'enfant dimanche.
>
> *The priest christened the child on Sunday.*

Also to give a name: *ils ont baptisé leur bateau*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*baptiser* comes through Old French *baptisier* (EtymDB) from Church Latin *baptizāre*, a borrowing from Greek *baptízein* "to dip".
