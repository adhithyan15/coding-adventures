Urdu, chapter 443: Sadness.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
غم (gham) — grief.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a wonder, then the Urdu for an amazing thing.

You'll want to know: غم.
غم — gham — "grief".
اس کے دل میں بڑا غم ہے۔ us ke dil meṅ baṛā gham hai. There is great grief in his heart.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
غم (gham) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic ghamm, "grief, distress".

وہ اپنی ناکامی پر کڑھتا ہے۔ voh apnī nākāmī par kuṛhtā hai. He frets over his failure.
Past (he …): وہ اس بات پر کڑھا, voh is bāt par kuṛhā.

The word, taken apart.
کڑھنا (kuṛhnā) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin kuṛhnā, "to be annoyed, to grieve", under Sanskrit kruddhá, "irritated" (CDIAL 3598).

The word, taken apart.
افسردہ (afsurdā) is usually traced to Persian afsurda, "frozen, withered": a downcast person is pictured as withered.
