## You'll want to know: તકરાર
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C342-VIVAD342-02]; assesses=[] -->

**તકરાર** — *takrār* — "a squabble".

> બાળકો રમકડા માટે તકરાર કરતાં હતાં.
>
> *bāḷko ramakḍā māṭe takrār kartā̃ hatā̃.*
>
> The children were squabbling over a toy.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**તકરાર** (*takrār*) is usually traced to Arabic *takrār*, "repetition", through Persian; Gujarati uses it for a petty quarrel, a sense the donor word does not have.
