## You'll want to know: رگ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C464-ORGAN464-05]; assesses=[] -->

**رگ** — *rag* — "a vein".

> نرس نے رگ سے خون لیا۔
>
> *nars ne rag se khūn liyā.*
>
> The nurse took blood from a vein.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**رگ** (*rag*) is usually traced to Persian *rag*, "vein, sinew".
