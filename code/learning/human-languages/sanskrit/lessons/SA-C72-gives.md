## You'll want to know: ददाति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C72-HOUSEWORK-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ददाति** (*dadāti*) — "(he, she) gives". **मह्यम्** + **ददाति**: "gives to me".

## What you've built

The verb of every gift.
