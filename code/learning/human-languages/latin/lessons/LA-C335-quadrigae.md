## You'll want to know: quadrīgae, quadrīgārum
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C335-AURIGA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**quadrīgae, quadrīgārum** — "a four-horse chariot".

> Quadrīgae per circum volābant.
>
> *The four-horse chariots flew round the circus.*

Feminine, plural: four horses under one yoke.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**quadrīgae** is a shortened *quadriiugae*, from *quattuor*, "four", and *iugum*, "yoke" (Lewis & Short): four together.
