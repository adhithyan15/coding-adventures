## You'll want to know: īnstruō, īnstruere
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C400-DOCEO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**īnstruō, īnstruere** — "to equip, to draw up; to instruct".

> Imperātor aciem īnstrūxit.
>
> *The general drew up his battle line.*

*īnstruō, īnstruere, īnstrūxī, īnstrūctum*: to build in, to equip, then to fit someone out with knowledge. English *instruct* and *instrument*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*īnstruō* is built from *in* and *struō* "to pile up, to build" (Lewis & Short). English *instruct* and *instrument* come from it.
