Kannada, chapter 486: Late Rains and Hail.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಹಿಂಗಾರು (hiṅgāru) — the late monsoon; the retreating rains.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a downpour, then the Kannada for to get soaked.

You'll want to know: ಹಿಂಗಾರು.
ಹಿಂಗಾರು — hiṅgāru — "the late monsoon; the retreating rains".
ಹಿಂಗಾರು ಮಳೆ ಮುಂಗಾರಿನ ನಂತರ ಬರುತ್ತದೆ. hiṅgāru maḷe muṅgārina nantara baruttade. The late monsoon rain comes after the early monsoon.
Its partner is ಮುಂಗಾರು, the early rains.

The word, taken apart.
ಹಿಂಗಾರು (hiṅgāru) is a native Kannada compound of pin, him, "behind, later" (DEDR 4205), and kār, "the rainy season" (DEDR 1278).

ಬಿರುಗಾಳಿಯ ಜೊತೆಗೆ ಆಲಿಕಲ್ಲು ಬಿದ್ದಿತು. birugāḷiya jotege ālikallu bidditu. Hail fell along with the storm.

ಬೆಳಗ್ಗೆ ನಗರದ ಮೇಲೆ ಮಬ್ಬು ಇತ್ತು. beḷagge nagarada mēle mabbu ittu. In the morning there was a haze over the city.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for hail, then the Kannada for haze.

You'll want to know: ಮುಸುಕು.
ಮುಸುಕು — musuku — "to shroud; to cover over".
ಬೆಳಗ್ಗೆ ಬೆಟ್ಟಗಳನ್ನು ಮಂಜು ಮುಸುಕಿತು. beḷagge beṭṭagaḷannu mañju musukitu. In the morning mist shrouded the hills.
Past (it …): ಮುಸುಕಿತು, musukitu.
As a noun, a veil: ಮುಸುಕು (musuku) ಹಾಕು, to cover one's head.

The word, taken apart.
ಮುಸುಕು (musuku) is native Dravidian: DEDR 5030 lists Kannada musuku, musugu, "to cover, to hide".

Lesson 5 of 5.
ಹಿಮಪಾತ (himapāta) — snowfall.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for haze, then the Kannada for to shroud.

You'll want to know: ಹಿಮಪಾತ.
ಹಿಮಪಾತ — himapāta — "snowfall".
ಹಿಮಪಾತದಿಂದ ಬೆಟ್ಟದ ರಸ್ತೆ ಮುಚ್ಚಿತು. himapātadinda beṭṭada raste muccitu. The mountain road was closed by snowfall.
ಹಿಮ, snow, plus ಪಾತ, a fall.

The word, taken apart.
ಹಿಮಪಾತ (himapāta) is built from two Sanskrit words: hima, "cold, frost, snow" (CDIAL 14096), and pāta, "a fall" (CDIAL 8052).
