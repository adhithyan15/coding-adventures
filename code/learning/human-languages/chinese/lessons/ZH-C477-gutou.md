## You'll want to know: gǔtou
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C477-INSIDE477-02]; assesses=[] -->

*gǔtou* — "bone".

> *gǒu zuì xǐhuan chī gǔtou* — Dogs love chewing bones more than anything.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*gǔtou* is built from *gǔ* 'bone' and 头, a noun ending here, both inherited from Middle Chinese, *kwot* and *duw* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which explains the first, after an older book, as the core of the flesh; the second is said light.
