## You'll want to know: спотыкаться
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C459-POKHOD-05]; assesses=[] -->

**спотыкаться** (*spotykátsya*) — "to stumble, to trip".

> В темноте я часто спотыкаюсь о камни.
>
> *V temnoté ya chásto spotykáyus o kámni.*
>
> In the dark I often trip over stones.

Imperfective; perfective *споткнуться*. Takes *о* + accusative.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*спотыкаться* is built from *с-*, *по-*, *тыкать* 'to poke, to knock' and *-ся* (EtymDB): to knock one's foot against something.
