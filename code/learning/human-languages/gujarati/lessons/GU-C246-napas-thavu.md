## You'll want to know: નાપાસ થવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C246-MARKS246-03]; assesses=[] -->

**નાપાસ થવું** — *nāpās thavũ* — "to fail an exam".

> **તે ગણિતમાં નાપાસ થાય છે.** — *te gaṇitmā̃ nāpās thāy che* — "He fails in maths."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**નાપાસ થવું** (*nāpās thavũ*) is built from *nāpās*, "not passed", with the verb *thavũ*, "to become". *Nāpās* joins *nā*, "not", usually traced to Persian, to a loan from English *pass*.
