## You'll want to know: लाज
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C257-RIS257-03]; assesses=[] -->

**लाज** — *lāj* — "modesty; shame".

> छोरी घणी लाज करै है।
>
> *chhorī ghaṇī lāj karai hai.*
>
> The girl is very modest.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**लाज** (*lāj*) is inherited from Sanskrit *lajjā*, "shame, modesty"; CDIAL lists Marwari *lāj* under it (CDIAL 10910).
