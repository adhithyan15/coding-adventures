## You'll want to know: o comunicado
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C282-AFIRMAR-04]; assesses=[] -->

**o comunicado** — "the press release, the official notice".

> O clube divulgou um comunicado no site.
>
> *The club put out a press release on its website.*

A short official text sent to the media.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*comunicado* is the past participle of *comunicar* used as a noun; the verb comes from Latin *commūnicāre* "to share", built on *commūnis* "common" (Lewis & Short).
