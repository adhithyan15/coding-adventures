## You'll want to know: коллектив
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C357-SOTRUDNIK-05]; assesses=[] -->

**коллектив** (*kollektív*) — "a team, the staff".

> У нас дружный коллектив.
>
> *U nas drúzhnyy kollektív.*
>
> We have a close-knit team.

Masculine. The people one works with, taken together.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**коллектив** is usually traced, through German, to Latin *collēctīvus* "gathered together", from *colligere* "to gather together" (Lewis & Short).
