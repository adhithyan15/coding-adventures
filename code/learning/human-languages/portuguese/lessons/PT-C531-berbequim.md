## You'll want to know: o berbequim
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C531-BRICOLAGE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**o berbequim** — "(electric) drill".

> Fez um furo na parede com o berbequim.
>
> *He drilled a hole in the wall with the drill.*

In Brazil: *a furadeira*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*berbequim* is usually traced to French *vilebrequin*, a hand drill; the origin of the French word is not settled.
