## You'll want to know: chǎnshēng
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C538-CAUSE538-04]; assesses=[] -->

*chǎnshēng* — "to arise; to come about".

> *yàoshi bù shuō qīngchu jiù huì chǎnshēng wùhuì* — If you don't make it clear, misunderstandings will arise.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*chǎnshēng* is built from *chǎn* 'to bear' and 生 'to be born', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *sreanX* and *srjaeng* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to give birth'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what would happen.
