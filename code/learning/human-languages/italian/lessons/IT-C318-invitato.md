## You'll want to know: l'invitato
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C318-RICEVIMENTO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**l'invitato** — "the guest".

> Al matrimonio ci sono duecento invitati.
>
> *There are two hundred guests at the wedding.*

The past participle of *invitare* used as a noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*invitato* is the past participle of *invitare*, from Latin *invītāre* "to invite" (Lewis & Short).
