# बाजार सूं — from

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MW-GRAMMAR-C156-POSTP-LOC-01, MW-GRAMMAR-C155-VSTEM-MORE-01, MW-LEX-C96-LOC96-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say *the shop is in the market*. Then *he comes every day*, and the
question word *where*, **कठै**.

## Grammar Lens: सूं
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-GRAMMAR-C156-POSTP-SU-01]; assesses=[MW-GRAMMAR-C155-VSTEM-MORE-01, MW-SCRIPT-BAJAR-01] -->

**सूं** after a place says where someone comes *from*:

> **वो बाजार सूं आवै है।**
>
> *vo bājār sū̃ āvai hai.* — He comes from the market.

> **हूं दुकान सूं आऊं हूं।**
>
> *hū̃ dukān sū̃ āū̃ hū̃.* — I come from the shop.

Old Marwari wrote it *-su*: *talā-su*, "from below" (CDIAL 5731).

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MW-GRAMMAR-C156-POSTP-SU-01, MW-LEX-C96-LOC96-01, MW-GRAMMAR-C154-PRES-POLITE-01, MW-SCRIPT-WELLBEING-QUESTION-01] -->

Ask with **कठै** in front of it:

> **आप कठै सूं आवो हो?**
>
> *āp kaṭhai sū̃ āvo ho?* — Where do you come from?
