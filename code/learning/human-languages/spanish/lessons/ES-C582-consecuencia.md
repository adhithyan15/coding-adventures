## You'll want to know: la consecuencia
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C582-CONSEC-01]; assesses=[] -->

**la consecuencia** — "the consequence".

> Todo lo que haces tiene consecuencias.
>
> *Everything you do has consequences.*

*Como consecuencia* — as a result.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*consecuencia* is a learned borrowing from Latin *consequentia*, built on *consequi* 'to follow closely' (EtymDB).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.
