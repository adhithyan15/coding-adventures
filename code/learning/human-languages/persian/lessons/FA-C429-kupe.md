## You'll want to know: کوپه
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C429-RAHAHAN-04]; assesses=[] -->

**کوپه** (*kupe*) — "a compartment (in a train)".

> در کوپه ما چهار تخت بود.
>
> *Dar kupe-ye mâ chahâr takht bud.*
>
> There were four berths in our compartment.

Night trains in Iran have four- or six-berth *kupe*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*kupe* was borrowed from French and is usually traced to French *coupé*, first a closed carriage. EtymDB does not list the Persian word.
