রোবট — robôṭ — "a robot".

স্টেশনের দরজাটা স্বয়ংক্রিয়। Sṭeshôner dôrjāṭā svôyôngkriyô. The station's door is automatic.

The word, taken apart.
স্বয়ংক্রিয় (svôyôngkriyô) joins Sanskrit svayam, "by oneself", and kriyā, "doing, action" (Monier-Williams): working by itself.

Lesson 4 of 5.
জং ধরা (jông dhôrā) — to rust.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for automatic, then the Bengali for a generator.

You'll want to know: জং ধরা.
জং ধরা — jông dhôrā — "to rust".
বৃষ্টিতে পেরেকগুলোতে জং ধরল। Brishṭite perekgulote jông dhôrlô. The nails rusted in the rain.
Past (he/she …): সেই সাইকেলে জং ধরল, sei sāikele jông dhôrlô.

The word, taken apart.
জং ধরা (jông dhôrā) joins জং, "rust", usually traced to Persian zang, to ধরা, "to catch, to take hold": rust takes hold.
