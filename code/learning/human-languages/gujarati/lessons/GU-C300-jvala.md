## You'll want to know: જ્વાળા
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C300-AAG300-03]; assesses=[] -->

**જ્વાળા** — *jvāḷā* — "a flame".

> આગની જ્વાળાઓ છત સુધી પહોંચી.
>
> *āgnī jvāḷāo chat sudhī pahõcī.*
>
> The flames reached the ceiling.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**જ્વાળા** (*jvāḷā*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *jvālā*, "a flame, a blaze" (Monier-Williams). It is feminine, and fire comes in the plural: **જ્વાળાઓ**, flames.
