Lesson 1 of 5.
کود دادن (kud dâdan) — to fertilise.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to vomit, then the Persian for to swell up.

You'll want to know: کود دادن.
کود دادن — kud dâdan — "to fertilise".

The word, taken apart.
kud dâdan is "to give manure": kud, "manure, heap", is a native word (EtymDB). dâdan, "to give", is an inherited verb; EtymDB takes it back to the Indo-European root deh₃-, "to give".

Lesson 5 of 5.
آبکشی کردن (âbkeshi kardan) — to rinse.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to let go, then the Persian for to scrub.

You'll want to know: آبکشی کردن.
آبکشی کردن — âbkeshi kardan — "to rinse".

The word, taken apart.
âbkeshi kardan pairs âbkeshi, "rinsing", with kardan. âbkeshi is âb, "water" (EtymDB), with kesh, "drawing", and -i. All are native Persian. kardan, "to do", is an inherited verb, from Middle Persian kardan (EtymDB).
