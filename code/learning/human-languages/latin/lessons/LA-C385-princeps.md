## You'll want to know: prīnceps, prīncipis
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C385-REGO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**prīnceps, prīncipis** — "the first, a leader; the emperor".

> Prīnceps cīvitātis erat.
>
> *He was the leading man of the state.*

*prīnceps, prīncipis* m.: *prīmus* + *capiō*, the one who takes first place. As an adjective it means first. English *prince* and *principal*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*prīnceps* is built from *prīmus* "first" and *capiō* "to take" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short): the one who takes first place. English *prince* and *principal* come from it.
