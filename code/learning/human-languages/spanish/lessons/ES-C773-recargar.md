## You'll want to know: recargar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C773-COBERTURA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**recargar** — "to top up; to recharge".

> Tengo que recargar el móvil antes del viaje.
>
> *I have to top up my phone before the trip.*

It takes *gu* before *e*: *recargué*. It also means to recharge a battery.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*recargar* is built on *cargar* 'to load, to charge' with *re-* 'again'. *Cargar* comes from Late Latin *carricāre* 'to load a cart', from *carrus* 'a cart' (Lewis & Short).
