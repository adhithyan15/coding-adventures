Guided Practice.
[pause 2 seconds after each]
[your turn — say: nowhere, from all sides, here and there, upwards, downwards]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: even as, as, if only, on the contrary, at least]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: wonderful, different, similar, fair, unjust]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: fat, clever, special, former, nearest]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
Nowhere? (nusquam.) From all sides? (undique.) Here and there? (passim.) Upwards? (sūrsum.) Downwards? (deorsum.) Even as? (sīcut.) As? (velut.) If only? (utinam.) On the contrary? (immō.) At least? (saltem.) Wonderful? (mīrus, mīra, mīrum.) Different? (dīversus, dīversa, dīversum.) Similar? (similis, simile.) Fair? (iūstus, iūsta, iūstum.) Unjust? (iniūstus, iniūsta, iniūstum.) Fat? (pinguis, pingue.) Clever? (callidus, callida, callidum.) Special? (praecipuus, praecipua, praecipuum.) Former? (prīstinus, prīstina, prīstinum.) Nearest? (proximus, proxima, proximum.)
