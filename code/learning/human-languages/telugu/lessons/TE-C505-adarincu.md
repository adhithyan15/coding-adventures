# ఆదరించు (ādariñcu) — to welcome warmly; to cherish

## You'll want to know: ఆదరించు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C505-KARUNA505-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ఆదరించు** — *ādariñcu* — "to welcome warmly; to cherish".

> కొత్తగా వచ్చిన కోడలిని అత్త ఆదరించింది.
>
> *kottagā vaccina kōḍalini atta ādariñcindi.*
>
> The mother-in-law warmly welcomed her new daughter-in-law.

Past (I …): ఆదరించాను, *ādariñcānu*.

The noun ఆదరణ is a warm reception or support.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ఆదరించు** (*ādariñcu*) is built on Sanskrit *ādara*, "respect" (CDIAL 1142), with the Telugu verb ending -ఇంచు.
