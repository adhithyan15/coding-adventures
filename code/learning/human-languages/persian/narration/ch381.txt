Persian, chapter 381: Roles at work.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
سرپرست (sarparast) — a supervisor; a guardian.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a trainee, then the Persian for an internship.

You'll want to know: سرپرست.
سرپرست (sarparast) — "a supervisor; a guardian".
سرپرست ما هر صبح کارها را تقسیم میکند. sarparast-e mâ har sobh kârhâ râ taqsim mikonad. Our supervisor shares out the jobs every morning.
Sar "head" + parast, from parastidan "to serve, to tend". Also the guardian of a child.

The word, taken apart.
سرپرست (sarparast) is a native Persian compound built from sar "head" + parast, which EtymDB traces to a Middle Persian verb "to serve, to minister". A supervisor is the one who looks after the head of the work.

Lesson 2 of 5.
معاون (mo'âven) — a deputy, an assistant manager.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for an internship, then the Persian for a supervisor.

You'll want to know: معاون.
معاون (mo'âven) — "a deputy, an assistant manager".
معاون مدرسه با والدین صحبت کرد. mo'âven-e madrese bâ vâledeyn sohbat kard. The deputy head of the school talked with the parents.
Plural mo'âvenân. Mo'âven-e vazir — a deputy minister.

The word, taken apart.
معاون (mo'âven) is usually traced to Arabic mu'āwin "helper, assistant": the person who helps the head.

Lesson 3 of 5.
کارشناس (kârshenâs) — an expert, an analyst.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a supervisor, then the Persian for a deputy.

You'll want to know: کارشناس.
کارشناس (kârshenâs) — "an expert, an analyst".
یک کارشناس خانه را دید و قیمت گذاشت. yek kârshenâs khâne râ did va qeymat gozâsht. An expert looked at the house and set a price.
Kâr "work" + shenâs "knowing". Kârshenâsi is also a bachelor's degree.

The word, taken apart.
کارشناس (kârshenâs) is native Persian, built from kâr "work" + shenâs-, the present stem of shenâkhtan "to know", which EtymDB traces through Old Persian to PIE ǵneh₃- "to know".

Lesson 4 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a deputy, then the Persian for an expert.

You'll want to know: متخصص.
متخصص (motakhasses) — "a specialist (doctor), an expert".
دکتر مرا پیش یک متخصص قلب فرستاد. doktor marâ pish-e yek motakhasses-e qalb ferestâd. The doctor sent me to a heart specialist.
Above all for doctors and skilled trades. Plural motakhassesân.

The word, taken apart.
متخصص (motakhasses) is usually traced to Arabic mutakhaṣṣiṣ "specialised", from the same Arabic family as khosusi "particular, private".

Lesson 5 of 5.
وظیفه (vazife) — a duty, a task.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for an expert, then the Persian for a specialist.

You'll want to know: وظیفه.
وظیفه (vazife) — "a duty, a task".
کمک به مردم وظیفه ماست. komak be mardom vazife-ye mâst. Helping people is our duty.
Plural vazâyef, an Arabic broken plural. Vazife dâshtan — to be obliged to.

The word, taken apart.
وظیفه (vazife) was borrowed from Arabic waẓīfa "task, duty, office" (EtymDB).
