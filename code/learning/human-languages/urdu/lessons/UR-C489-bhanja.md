# بھانجا (bhāṅjā) — a nephew (a sister's son)

## You'll want to know: بھانجا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C489-KIN489-04]; assesses=[] -->

**بھانجا** — *bhāṅjā* — "a nephew (a sister's son)".

> میرا بھانجا دس سال کا ہے۔
>
> *merā bhāṅjā das sāl kā hai.*
>
> My sister's son is ten years old.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**بھانجا** (*bhāṅjā*) is inherited from Sanskrit *bhāginēya*, "sister's son" (CDIAL 9433).
