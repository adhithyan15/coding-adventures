## You'll want to know: ਥਣ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C307-BUFF307-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ਥਣ** — *thaṇ* — "a teat; an udder".

> ਮੱਝ ਦੇ ਥਣਾਂ ਵਿੱਚ ਬਹੁਤ ਦੁੱਧ ਹੈ।
>
> *majjh de thaṇāṁ vich bahut dudh hai.*
>
> There is a lot of milk in the buffalo's teats.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਥਣ** (*thaṇ*) is inherited from Sanskrit *stana*, "female breast, udder"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *thaṇ*, "teat", under it (CDIAL 13666).
