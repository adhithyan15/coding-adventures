## You'll want to know: впоследствии
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C274-POSTEPENNO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**впоследствии** (*vposlédstvii*) — "later on, subsequently".

> Впоследствии он стал известным врачом.
>
> *Vposlédstvii on stal izvéstnym vrachóm.*
>
> Later on he became a well-known doctor.

Formal and written. In speech: *потом*, *позже*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*впоследствии* is built on *в* and *последствие* "a consequence", from *след* "a track, a trace" (EtymDB): what comes in the footsteps of an event.
