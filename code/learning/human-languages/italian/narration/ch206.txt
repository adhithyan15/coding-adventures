Italian, chapter 206: To cool down, To fill, To empty, To count, To measure.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
raffreddare — to cool down.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for to stop, then the Italian for to halt.

You'll want to know: raffreddare.
raffreddare — "to cool down".

The word, taken apart.
raffreddare — built on freddo, "cold", with ra-: to make cold. freddo goes back to Latin frigidus, "cold". EtymDB 2.1 has no row for the verb.
