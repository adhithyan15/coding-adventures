Hindi, chapter 358: Serving Others.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
सेवा (sevā) — service (to others).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for belief, then the Hindi for to adopt.

You'll want to know: सेवा.
सेवा — sevā — "service (to others)".
वह बीमारों की सेवा करती है। vah bīmāroṁ kī sevā kartī hai. She looks after the sick.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
सेवा (sevā) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit sevā, "service, attendance" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 4 of 6.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for renunciation, then the Hindi for self-interest.

You'll want to know: लालच.
लालच — lālac — "greed".
लालच बुरी चीज़ है। lālac burī cīz hai. Greed is a bad thing.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
लालच (lālac) is a tadbhava, inherited from Sanskrit lālitya, "amorousness" (CDIAL 11029). CDIAL lists the Hindi lālac, "cupidity", under this entry; how a word for amorousness came to mean greed is not spelled out there.

Lesson 5 of 6.
जताना (jatānā) — to show, to make known (a feeling).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for self-interest, then the Hindi for greed.

You'll want to know: जताना.
जताना — jatānā — "to show, to make known (a feeling)".
मीरा ने अपना दुख नहीं जताया। Mīrā ne apnā dukh nahīṃ jatāyā. Mira didn't show her sorrow.
Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने अपना प्यार जताया, usne apnā pyār jatāyā.

The word, taken apart.
जताना (jatānā) is a tadbhava, inherited from Sanskrit jñapta, "instructed" (CDIAL 5273). CDIAL glosses the Hindi verb "to make known".
