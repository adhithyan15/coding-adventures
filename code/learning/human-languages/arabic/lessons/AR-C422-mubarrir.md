## You'll want to know: مبرر
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C422-MAWDUI422-03]; assesses=[] -->

**مبرر** — *mubarrir* — "a justification; a pretext".

> لا يوجد مبرر لهذا التأخير.
>
> *lā yūjadu mubarrir li-hādhā at-taʾkhīr.*
>
> There is no justification for this delay.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**مبرر** (*mubarrir*) is built on the native Arabic root *b-r-r*, "to be true, to be good" (Lane). *Mubarrir* is the Form II active participle, what shows a thing to be right: a justification, a pretext.
