**ちゅうしゃ** — *chūsha* — "an injection".

> **ちゅうしゃは いたいですか。** — *chūsha wa itai desu ka* — "Does the injection hurt?"

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ちゅうしゃ** (*chūsha*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *chū*, "to pour" (Middle Chinese *tsyuH*) and *sha*, "to shoot" (*zyaeH*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
