## You'll want to know: కచేరీ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C543-KACERI543-01]; assesses=[] -->

**కచేరీ** — *kacērī* — "a concert; a recital".

> శనివారం సాయంత్రం సంగీత కచేరీ జరిగింది.
>
> *śanivāraṁ sāyantraṁ saṅgīta kacērī jarigindi.*
>
> There was a music concert on Saturday evening.

Also an old word for a government office.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**కచేరీ** (*kacērī*) came into Telugu through Hindustani *kacahrī*, "a court, an office"; its earlier origin is not settled.
