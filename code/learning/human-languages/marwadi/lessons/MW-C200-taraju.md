## You'll want to know: तराजू
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C200-WEIGH200-01]; assesses=[] -->

**तराजू** — *tarājū* — "a pair of scales".

> तराजू दुकान मांय है।
>
> *tarājū dukān mā̃y hai.*
>
> The scales are in the shop.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**तराजू** (*tarājū*) is usually traced to Persian *tarāzū*, "balance, scales".
