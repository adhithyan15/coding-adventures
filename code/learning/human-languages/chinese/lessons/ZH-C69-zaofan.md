## You'll want to know: 早饭
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C69-MEALS69-02]; assesses=[] -->

**早饭** — *zǎofàn* — "breakfast".

> **我七点吃早饭** *wǒ qī diǎn chī zǎofàn* — I have breakfast at seven.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**早饭** (*zǎofàn*) is built from zǎo 'morning' and 饭 'meal', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *tsawX* and *bjonH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription).
