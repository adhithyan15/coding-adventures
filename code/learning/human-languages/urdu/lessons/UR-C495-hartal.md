## You'll want to know: ہڑتال
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C495-AJRAT495-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ہڑتال** — *haṛtāl* — "a strike (stopping work)".

> کل سے کارخانے میں ہڑتال ہے۔
>
> *kal se kārkhāne meṅ haṛtāl hai.*
>
> There has been a strike at the factory since yesterday.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ہڑتال** (*haṛtāl*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *haṛtāl*, "closing shops in protest", under Sanskrit *haṭṭa*, "a market" (CDIAL 13944).
