## You'll want to know: la caridad
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C606-SOLIDAR-04]; assesses=[] -->

**la caridad** — "the charity".

> Dan dinero a obras de caridad.
>
> *They give money to charity.*

*Pedir caridad* — to beg.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*caridad* comes from Latin *cāritās*, *cāritātem* 'dearness, love', built on *cārus* 'dear' (Lewis & Short).
