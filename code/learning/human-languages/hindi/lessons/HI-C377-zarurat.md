## You'll want to know: ज़रूरत
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C377-HALAT377-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ज़रूरत** — *zarūrat* — "a need".

> अगर तुम्हें पैसों की ज़रूरत होगी तो मुझे बताओ।
>
> *agar tumhẽ paisoṁ kī zarūrat hogī to mujhe batāo.*
>
> If you need money, tell me.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ज़रूरत** (*zarūrat*) is usually traced to Arabic *ḍarūra*, "necessity", which reached Hindi through Persian.
