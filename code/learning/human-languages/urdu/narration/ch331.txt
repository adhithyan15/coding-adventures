Urdu, chapter 331: What Caused It.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
سبب (sabab) — a cause.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a fruit seller, then the Urdu for a greengrocer.

You'll want to know: سبب.
سبب — sabab — "a cause".
اس حادثے کا سبب کیا تھا؟ is hādse kā sabab kyā thā? What was the cause of this accident?
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
سبب (sabab) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic sabab, "a cause, a means".

Lesson 2 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a greengrocer, then the Urdu for a cause.

You'll want to know: بنیاد.
بنیاد — bunyād — "a basis; a foundation".
آپ کی بات کی بنیاد کیا ہے؟ āp kī bāt kī bunyād kyā hai? What is your point based on?
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
بنیاد (bunyād) is usually traced to Persian bunyād, "a foundation"; Urdu uses it for the ground an argument stands on as well as for the base of a building.

[pause 2 seconds]

ذمہ دار — zimme-dār — "responsible".
سارا اپنے کام کی ذمہ دار ہے۔ sārā apne kām kī zimme-dār hai. Sara is responsible for her own work.

The word, taken apart.
ذمہ دار (zimme-dār) is usually traced to Persian zimmadār, "answerable, in charge": zimma, a charge one carries, with -dār, "holding".

You'll want to know: جڑنا.
جڑنا — juṛnā — "to be joined; to be linked".
یہ سڑک اس پل سے جڑتی ہے۔ yih saṛak is pul se juṛtī hai. This road links up with this bridge.
Past (it …): وہ پل سڑک سے جڑ گیا, voh pul saṛak se juṛ gayā.

The word, taken apart.
جڑنا (juṛnā) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin juṛnā, "to be joined", under yuṭati, a form it reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 10496).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

The word, taken apart.
وابستہ (vābastā) is usually traced to Persian vābasta, "tied back, attached", built on basta, "tied".
