## You'll want to know: કરવત
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C502-SUTHAR502-01]; assesses=[] -->

**કરવત** — *karvat* — "a saw".

> સુથાર કરવતથી લાકડું કાપે છે.
>
> *suthār karvatthī lākḍũ kāpe che.*
>
> The carpenter cuts the wood with a saw.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**કરવત** (*karvat*) is inherited from Sanskrit *karapattra*, "a saw", and CDIAL lists the Gujarati word (CDIAL 2795).
