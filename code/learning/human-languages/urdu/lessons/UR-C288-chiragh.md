## You'll want to know: چراغ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C288-CHIRAGH288-01]; assesses=[] -->

**چراغ** — *chirāgh* — "an oil lamp".

> رات کو چراغ گر گیا۔
>
> *rāt ko chirāgh gir gayā.*
>
> The lamp fell over in the night.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**چراغ** (*chirāgh*) is usually traced to Persian *chirāgh*, "a lamp". It is the small clay lamp with oil and a wick.
