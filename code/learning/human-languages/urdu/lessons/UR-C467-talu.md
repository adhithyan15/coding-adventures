## You'll want to know: تالو
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C467-MOUTH467-01]; assesses=[] -->

**تالو** — *tālū* — "the palate".

> تالو منہ کی چھت ہے۔
>
> *tālū muṅh kī chhat hai.*
>
> The palate is the roof of the mouth.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**تالو** (*tālū*) is inherited from Sanskrit *tālu*, "palate" (CDIAL 5803).
