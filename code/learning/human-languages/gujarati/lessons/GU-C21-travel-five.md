# Payoff — five words in four skills

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-PERFORMANCE-PLACES-THREE-FOUR-SKILL-01, GU-LEX-HAATH-01, GU-LEX-PAISA-01] -->

[PAUSE 15s] Recall the three-place score, then say hand and money.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-PERFORMANCE-TRAVEL-FIVE-FOUR-SKILL-01]; assesses=[GU-LEX-BAJAR-01, GU-SCRIPT-BAJAR-01, GU-LEX-GHAR-01, GU-SCRIPT-GHAR-01, GU-LEX-MANDIR-01, GU-SCRIPT-MANDIR-01, GU-LEX-HAATH-01, GU-SCRIPT-HAATH-01, GU-LEX-PAISA-01, GU-SCRIPT-PAISA-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: dictation-transcription -->

1. Identify five heard words.
2. Produce five words from meaning cues.
3. Match five printed cards to meanings.
4. Write three heard words without a model.

Pass each skill separately. A strong score in one skill cannot replace a miss
in another.
