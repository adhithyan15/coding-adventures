# पछाड़ना (pachāṛnā) — to throw down; to overpower

## You'll want to know: पछाड़ना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C323-VIJAY323-02]; assesses=[] -->

**पछाड़ना** — *pachāṛnā* — "to throw down; to overpower".

> एक सिपाही ने दो दुश्मनों को पछाड़ा।
>
> *ek sipāhī ne do duśmanoṁ ko pachāṛā.*
>
> One soldier overpowered two enemies.

Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने दुश्मन को पछाड़ा, *usne duśman ko pachāṛā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पछाड़ना** (*pachāṛnā*) is inherited from *\*pracchaṭ*, a form CDIAL reconstructs (CDIAL 8493), with the sense of knocking down. It is the wrestler's word: to throw the other to the ground, and so to beat them.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
