## You'll want to know: بزاز
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C458-DASTFORUSH-03]; assesses=[] -->

**بزاز** (*bazzâz*) — "a draper, a cloth merchant".

> بزاز پارچه را با متر اندازه گرفت.
>
> *Bazzâz pârche râ bâ metr andâze gereft.*
>
> The draper measured the cloth with a tape.

An old word; today often *pârche-forush*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*bazzâz* was borrowed from Arabic *bazzāz* 'draper, cloth merchant' (EtymDB).
