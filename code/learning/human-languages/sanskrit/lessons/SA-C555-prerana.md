## You'll want to know: प्रेरणा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C555-PRERANA555-01]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रेरणा** — *preraṇā* — "inspiration; urging".

> यदि गुरोः प्रेरणा अस्ति, तर्हि शिष्यः उत्तमं कार्यं करोति।
>
> *yadi guroḥ preraṇā asti, tarhi śiṣyaḥ uttamaṃ kāryaṃ karoti.*
>
> With a teacher's urging, a pupil does fine work.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रेरणा** (*preraṇā*) is built on *pra*, "forth", and the root √īr, "to set in motion"; MW gives *preraṇa*, also *preraṇā*, as setting in motion, urging, inciting; "inspiration" is its modern use (MW).
