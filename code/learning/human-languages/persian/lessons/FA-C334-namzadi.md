## You'll want to know: نامزدی
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C334-ASHEQ-04]; assesses=[] -->

**نامزدی** (*nâmzadi*) — "an engagement".

> جشن نامزدی آنها در تابستان بود.
>
> *Jashn-e nâmzadi-ye ânhâ dar tâbestân bud.*
>
> Their engagement party was in the summer.

From *nâmzad* "fiancé, fiancée", literally "name-struck": a name given to someone.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*nâmzadi* adds the suffix *-i* to *nâmzad* "fiancé, fiancée", built from *nâm* "name" and *zad*, the past stem of *zadan* "to strike". All of it is native Persian.
