# अग्रिमः (agrimaḥ) — next

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[SA-GRAMMAR-C281-TICKET-02] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say "when will the train leave?" (*dhūmayānaṃ kadā gamiṣyati?*) That one has gone. You want the next.

## You'll want to know: अग्रिमः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C281-AGRIMAH-01]; assesses=[SA-LEX-C123-THINGS123-05, SA-LEX-C85-WEEK-01, SA-LEX-C18-ASK-01] -->

**अग्रिमः** — *agrimaḥ* — "next", the one coming up. It agrees with its noun: **अग्रिमम्** (*agrimam*) for a neuter noun such as **धूमयानम्**.

> **अग्रिमं धूमयानं कदा?**
>
> *agrimaṃ dhūmayānaṃ kadā?* — "When is the next train?"

**अग्रिमः सप्ताहः** (*agrimaḥ saptāhaḥ*) is "next week".

> **अहम् अग्रिमे सप्ताहे गमिष्यामि।**
>
> *aham agrime saptāhe gamiṣyāmi.* — "I will go next week."

**अग्रिमे सप्ताहे** (*agrime saptāhe*) is "in the next week": *-e* says "in", for a time as for a place.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अग्रिमः** (*agrimaḥ*) is built on **अग्र** (*agra*), "the foremost point, the front": Monier-Williams gives *agrima* as "being in front, preceding". The next train is the one in front. CDIAL 92 lists Punjabi *aggiõ*, "first".
