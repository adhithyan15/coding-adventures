# ಹಮಾಲಿ (hamāli) — a porter

## You'll want to know: ಹಮಾಲಿ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C436-HAMALI436-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ಹಮಾಲಿ** — *hamāli* — "a porter".

> ಹಮಾಲಿ ನನ್ನ ಎರಡು ಸೂಟ್ಕೇಸ್ಗಳನ್ನು ಹೊತ್ತನು.
>
> *hamāli nanna eraḍu sūṭkēsgaḷannu hottanu.*
>
> The porter carried my two suitcases.

Plural: ಹಮಾಲಿಗಳು, *hamāligaḷu*.

At stations porters wear a red shirt and a numbered badge; ಕೂಲಿ also names them.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಹಮಾಲಿ** (*hamāli*) is usually traced to Arabic *ḥammāl*, "a carrier, a porter", which reached Kannada through Hindustani or Marathi.
