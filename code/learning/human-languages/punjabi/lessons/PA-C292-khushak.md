## You'll want to know: ਖ਼ੁਸ਼ਕ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C292-DAMP292-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ਖ਼ੁਸ਼ਕ** — *khushak* — "arid; dry in the air".

> ਸਿਆਲ ਵਿੱਚ ਹਵਾ ਖ਼ੁਸ਼ਕ ਹੁੰਦੀ ਹੈ।
>
> *siāl vich havā khushak hundī hai.*
>
> In winter the air is dry.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਖ਼ੁਸ਼ਕ** (*khushak*) is a loan, usually traced to Persian *khushk*, "dry".
