## You'll want to know: der Hausmeister
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C532-WG-04]; assesses=[] -->

**der Hausmeister** — "caretaker, janitor".

> Der Hausmeister repariert die Tür im Keller.
>
> *The caretaker is repairing the door in the cellar.*

Plural: *die Hausmeister*. In Switzerland: *der Hauswart*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Hausmeister* is built on *Haus* "house" and *Meister* "master", which came through Old High German *meistar* from Latin *magister* (EtymDB).
