## You'll want to know: ਖਾਲ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C299-WATER299-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ਖਾਲ** — *khāl* — "a watercourse; a field channel".

> ਖਾਲ ਵਿੱਚ ਪਾਣੀ ਖੇਤ ਤੱਕ ਜਾਂਦਾ ਹੈ।
>
> *khāl vich pāṇī khet tak jāndā hai.*
>
> In the channel the water goes to the field.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਖਾਲ** (*khāl*) is inherited from Sanskrit *khalla*, "trench, creek, canal"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *khāl*, "hollow, gutter, inlet", under it (CDIAL 3849).
