## You'll want to know: ਐਲਬਮ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C356-PHOTO356-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ਐਲਬਮ** — *ailbam* — "a photo album".

> ਸਾਡੀਆਂ ਫ਼ੋਟੋਆਂ ਐਲਬਮ ਵਿੱਚ ਹਨ।
>
> *sāḍīāṁ foṭoāṁ ailbam vich han.*
>
> Our photos are in the album.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਐਲਬਮ** (*ailbam*) is borrowed from English *album*.
