Telugu, chapter 441: Papers at the Border.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
పాస్పోర్టు (pāspōrṭu) — a passport.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for an interval, then the Telugu for to hurry up.

You'll want to know: పాస్పోర్టు.
పాస్పోర్టు — pāspōrṭu — "a passport".
విదేశాలకు వెళ్ళాలంటే పాస్పోర్టు కావాలి. vidēśālaku veḷḷālaṇṭē pāspōrṭu kāvāli. To go abroad you need a passport.
It ends in -ఉ, like most English loans in Telugu: పాస్పోర్టు (pāspōrṭu), బస్సు.

The word, taken apart.
పాస్పోర్టు (pāspōrṭu) is a loan from English passport, with the usual Telugu final -ఉ.

Lesson 2 of 5.
గుర్తింపు (gurtimpu) — identification; recognition.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to hurry up, then the Telugu for a passport.

You'll want to know: గుర్తింపు.
గుర్తింపు — gurtimpu — "identification; recognition".
నా గుర్తింపు కార్డు సంచిలో ఉంది. nā gurtimpu kārḍu sañcilō undi. My identity card is in the bag.
గుర్తు, a mark, a sign, + the noun ending -ఇంపు. గుర్తింపు (gurtimpu) కార్డు is an identity card; the word also means being recognised for good work.

The word, taken apart.
గుర్తింపు (gurtimpu) is native Dravidian, built on గుర్తు, "a mark, a sign", which belongs to the family of Tamil kuṟi, "a mark" (DEDR 1847), with the noun ending -ఇంపు.

Lesson 3 of 5.
సుంకం (suṅkaṁ) — a customs duty; a toll.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a passport, then the Telugu for identification.

You'll want to know: సుంకం.
సుంకం — suṅkaṁ — "a customs duty; a toll".
విదేశీ సరుకుల మీద సుంకం ఎక్కువ. vidēśī sarukula mīda suṅkaṁ ekkuva. The duty on foreign goods is high.
పన్ను is a tax in general; సుంకం (suṅkaṁ) is the duty paid on goods that cross a border or a toll gate.

The word, taken apart.
సుంకం (suṅkaṁ) is borrowed from Sanskrit śulka, "price, toll", in its Middle Indo-Aryan shape suṅka (CDIAL 12543); CDIAL notes that the Dravidian forms come from that spoken shape.

Lesson 4 of 5.
భద్రత (bhadrata) — security; safety.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for identification, then the Telugu for a customs duty.

You'll want to know: భద్రత.
భద్రత — bhadrata — "security; safety".
విమానాశ్రయంలో భద్రత కోసం సామాను తనిఖీ చేశారు. vimānāśrayaṁlō bhadrata kōsaṁ sāmānu tanikhī cēśāru. At the airport they checked the luggage for security.
The ending -త makes an abstract noun of a Sanskrit adjective: భద్ర, safe, gives భద్రత (bhadrata), safety.

The word, taken apart.
భద్రత (bhadrata) is borrowed from Sanskrit bhadratā (Monier-Williams), the abstract noun of bhadra, "fortunate, good" (CDIAL 9377). In Telugu it came to mean safety and security.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ధ్రువపత్రం (dhruvapatraṁ) — a certificate.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a customs duty, then the Telugu for security.

You'll want to know: ధ్రువపత్రం.
ధ్రువపత్రం — dhruvapatraṁ — "a certificate".
పాస్పోర్టు కోసం నా ధ్రువపత్రాలు అన్నీ ఇచ్చాను. pāspōrṭu kōsaṁ nā dhruvapatrālu annī iccānu. I handed in all my certificates for the passport.
Plural: ధ్రువపత్రాలు, dhruvapatrālu.
ధ్రువ, firm, + పత్రం, a document: a paper that makes a fact firm. Plural ధ్రువపత్రాలు, the -ం noun plural in -ాలు.

The word, taken apart.
ధ్రువపత్రం (dhruvapatraṁ) is built from two words borrowed from Sanskrit: dhruva, "fixed, firm" (CDIAL 6892), and patra, "a leaf, a written document" (Monier-Williams).
