## You'll want to know: مستقل
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C418-TAQWIYA418-05]; assesses=[] -->

**مستقل** — *mustaqill* — "independent".

> أصبح أخي مستقلا عن أسرته.
>
> *aṣbaḥa akhī mustaqillan ʿan usratih.*
>
> My brother became independent of his family.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**مستقل** (*mustaqill*) is built on the native Arabic root *q-l-l*, "to be few; to lift" (Lane). Lane gives Form X for one who manages his affairs by himself alone; *mustaqill*, its active participle, is independent.
