## You'll want to know: वैज्ञानिक
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C361-SHODH361-03]; assesses=[] -->

**वैज्ञानिक** — *vaijñānik* — "a scientist".

> वैज्ञानिक नई दवा पर काम कर रहे हैं।
>
> *vaijñānik naī davā par kām kar rahe haiṁ.*
>
> Scientists are working on a new medicine.

Masculine or feminine, by the person meant.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**वैज्ञानिक** (*vaijñānik*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *vaijñānika*, "proficient, learned" (Monier-Williams). The sense of a scientist is a modern one that Monier-Williams does not give.
