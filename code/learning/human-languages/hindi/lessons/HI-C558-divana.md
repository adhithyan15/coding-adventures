## You'll want to know: दीवाना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C558-KHVAHISH558-02]; assesses=[] -->

**दीवाना** — *dīvānā* — "crazy (about); mad (for)".

> मीरा पुराने गानों की दीवानी है।
>
> *Mīrā purāne gānoṁ kī dīvānī hai.*
>
> Mira is crazy about old songs.

Agrees like an adjective: दीवाना, दीवानी, दीवाने.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**दीवाना** (*dīvānā*) is usually traced to Persian *dīvāna*, "mad, possessed"; Hindi uses it for someone wild about a thing or a person.
