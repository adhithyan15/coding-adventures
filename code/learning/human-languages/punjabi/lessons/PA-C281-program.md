# ਪ੍ਰੋਗਰਾਮ (progrām) — a plan; a programme

## Warm-up

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Punjabi for to take out, then the Punjabi for perhaps.

## You'll want to know: ਪ੍ਰੋਗਰਾਮ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C281-PLAN281-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ਪ੍ਰੋਗਰਾਮ** — *progrām* — "a plan; a programme".

> ਛੁੱਟੀ ਦਾ ਪ੍ਰੋਗਰਾਮ ਕੀ ਹੈ?
>
> *chhuṭṭī dā progrām kī hai?*
>
> What is the plan for the holiday?

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਪ੍ਰੋਗਰਾਮ** (*progrām*) is borrowed from English *programme*.
