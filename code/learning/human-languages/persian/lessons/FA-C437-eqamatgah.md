## You'll want to know: اقامتگاه
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C437-MOSAFERKHANE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**اقامتگاه** (*eqâmatgâh*) — "lodgings, a place to stay; a residence".

> اقامتگاه ما نزدیک دریا بود.
>
> *Eqâmatgâh-e mâ nazdik-e daryâ bud.*
>
> Our lodgings were near the sea.

*Eqâmatgâh-e bumgardi* — a guesthouse in a village home, now common in the countryside.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*eqâmatgâh* is built from *eqâmat* 'a stay', a loan usually traced to Arabic *iqāma* 'staying, residing', and native *gâh* 'place'. EtymDB does not list it.
