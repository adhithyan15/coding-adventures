## You'll want to know: ちゃんと
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C330-CHANTO330-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ちゃんと** — *chanto* — "as one should; properly".

> **びょうきに なるから、ちゃんと てを あらって ください。** — *byōki ni naru kara, chanto te o aratte kudasai* — "You will get ill, so wash your hands properly."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ちゃんと** (*chanto*) is a native mimetic word, one of the Japanese words that picture a manner or a feeling in sound; JMdict marks it as mimetic. It is inherited, not borrowed, and its older origin is not settled (JMdict).
