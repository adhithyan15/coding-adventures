## You'll want to know: منجر شدن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C370-PAYAMAD-04]; assesses=[] -->

**منجر شدن** (*monjar shodan*) — "to lead to, to result in".

> این اشتباه به یک دعوای بزرگ منجر شد.
>
> *in eshtebâh be yek da'vâ-ye bozorg monjar shod.*
>
> This mistake led to a big quarrel.

Takes *be*: *be chizi monjar shodan*. Formal. Stem *shav-*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**منجر** is usually traced to Arabic *munjarr* "drawn along"; with the Persian verb *shodan* "to become", one thing is drawn along into another.
