## You'll want to know: بدهکار
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C411-QARZ-04]; assesses=[] -->

**بدهکار** (*bedehkâr*) — "in debt, owing; a debtor".

> من هنوز به تو بدهکارم.
>
> *man hanuz be to bedehkâram.*
>
> I still owe you.

*Be kasi bedehkâr budan* — to owe someone. The opposite is *talabkâr*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**بدهکار** is native Persian, built from *bedeh* "owing", built on *deh-*, the present stem of *dâdan* "to give", + the agent suffix *-kâr*.
