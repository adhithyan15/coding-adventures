Urdu, chapter 515: Pictures and Painters.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
تصویر (tasvīr) — a picture.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a thimble, then the Urdu for a fringe.

You'll want to know: تصویر.
تصویر — tasvīr — "a picture".
دیوار پر دادا کی ایک پرانی تصویر ہے۔ dīvār par dādā kī ek purānī tasvīr hai. There is an old picture of grandfather on the wall.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
تصویر (tasvīr) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic taṣwīr, "a picturing, a portrait".

اس مصور نے پہاڑوں کی تصویریں بنائیں۔ is musavvir ne pahāṛoṅ kī tasvīreṅ banāīṅ. This painter made pictures of the mountains.
Masculine or feminine, by the person meant.

The word, taken apart.
مصور (musavvir) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic muṣawwir, "one who gives form".

Lesson 4 of 5.
شاہکار (shāhkār) — a masterpiece.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a painter (an artist), then the Urdu for a sketch.

You'll want to know: شاہکار.
شاہکار — shāhkār — "a masterpiece".
یہ تصویر اس مصور کا شاہکار ہے۔ yih tasvīr is musavvir kā shāhkār hai. This picture is the painter's masterpiece.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
شاہکار (shāhkār) is usually traced to Persian shāhkār, built from shāh, "a king", and kār, "work": a work fit for a king.

Lesson 5 of 5.

Warm-up.

You'll want to know: جھلکنا.
جھلکنا — jhalaknā — "to show through; to glint".
پانی میں چاند جھلکتا ہے۔ pānī meṅ chā̃d jhalaktā hai. The moon glints in the water.
Past (it …): وہ چاند پانی میں جھلکا, voh chā̃d pānī meṅ jhalkā.

The word, taken apart.
جھلکنا (jhalaknā) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin jhalaknā, "to sparkle", under jhal, "flash", a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 5352).
