## Grammar Lens: 坐 and a vehicle, then 去
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-GRAMMAR-TICKET-01]; assesses=[ZH-LEX-C44-PLACES44-04, ZH-LEX-C72-TRAVEL72-01, ZH-LEX-C88-ZHANG-01, ZH-LEX-C73-STATION73-05, ZH-LEX-C48-CLOCK48-01, ZH-LEX-C88-LIANG-01] -->

> **坐 and a vehicle, then 去 or 来: that is how you travel.**

> **我们坐火车去** *wǒmen zuò huǒchē qù* — We're going by train.
> **我们坐八点的火车** *wǒmen zuò bā diǎn de huǒchē* — We're taking the eight o'clock train.

坐 is the *zuò* you know, to sit and to travel by; the next lesson puts it in your hand. The vehicle comes between 坐 and the verb of going.

Tickets are counted with *zhāng*, as flat things are: *mǎi liǎng zhāng qù Běijīng de piào*, two tickets to Beijing.
