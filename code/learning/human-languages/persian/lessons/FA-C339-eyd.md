## You'll want to know: عید
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C339-EYD-01]; assesses=[] -->

**عید** (*'eyd*) — "a festival, a holiday; the New Year".

> برای عید همه لباس نو میخرند.
>
> *Barâ-ye 'eyd hame lebâs-e now mikharand.*
>
> For the New Year everyone buys new clothes.

Said alone, *'eyd* usually means Nowruz, the Persian New Year. *'Eyd mobârak!* "Happy holiday!"

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*'eyd* is borrowed from Arabic *'īd*, "holiday, feast" (EtymDB).
