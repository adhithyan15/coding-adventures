## You'll want to know: হোস্টেল
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C406-THAI406-02]; assesses=[] -->

**হোস্টেল** — *hosṭel* — "a hostel".

> অরুণ কলেজের হোস্টেলে থাকে।
>
> *ôrun kôlejer hosṭele thāke.*
>
> Arun lives in the college hostel.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**হোস্টেল** (*hosṭel*) is borrowed from English *hostel*, most often a students' or workers' hall of residence.
