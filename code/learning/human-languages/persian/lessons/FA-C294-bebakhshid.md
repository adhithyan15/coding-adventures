# ببخشید (bebakhshid) — excuse me, and the way to the station

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[FA-LEX-C221-KONESH221-01, FA-LEX-C85-DEF85-02, FA-GRAMMAR-MIKHAHAM-SUBJ-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "to forgive", then "where?". (*Bakhshidan*, *kojâ*.) Then "I
want to go". (*Mikhâham beravam.*)

[PAUSE 1s] On the street the first word you need is the one that stops a
stranger politely.

## You'll want to know: ببخشید
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-BEBAKHSHID-01]; assesses=[FA-LEX-C70-TOWN70-02, FA-GRAMMAR-CHI-AST-FUSION, FA-LEX-C67-TIME67-05] -->

**ببخشید** — *bebakhshid* — "excuse me", before a question; "sorry", after a
bump.

It is *bakhshidan*, "to forgive", with *be-* and the *shomâ* ending, the
shape a polite request takes: "forgive". Persian stops a stranger by asking
pardon first.

> **ببخشید، ایستگاه کجاست؟** — *bebakhshid, istgâh kojâst?* — Excuse me,
> where is the station?

*Kojâst* is *kojâ* with *ast* fused on, the way *chi* and *ast* became
*chist*. The answer may start **نزدیک است**, *nazdik ast*, "it's near".

## Grammar Lens: chetor mitavânam be ... beravam?
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-GRAMMAR-CHETOR-MITAVANAM-01]; assesses=[FA-LEX-C89-CAN89-01, FA-LEX-CHETOR, FA-GRAMMAR-SUBJUNCTIVE-BE-01, FA-LEX-C88-LOC88-05] -->

*Tavânestan*, "to be able", has the present stem **tavân-**: *mitavânam*, I
can; *mitavânid*, you can. Like *mikhâham*, it takes the subjunctive after
it. Put *chetor*, "how", in front and you have the way:

> **ببخشید، چطور میتوانم به ایستگاه بروم؟** — *bebakhshid, chetor mitavânam
> be istgâh beravam?* — Excuse me, how can I get to the station?

Swap the place and the question still works: *be muze*, to the museum; *be
hotel*, to the hotel.

## The phrase, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[FA-LEX-C221-KONESH221-01] -->

*bakhshidan* is native Persian. EtymDB 2.1 takes it back to a Middle Persian
verb written *bhš-* in Pahlavi letters, and lists it in two senses, "to give,
to grant" and "to forgive". Forgiving is a kind of granting, and
*bebakhshid* asks for it in advance.
