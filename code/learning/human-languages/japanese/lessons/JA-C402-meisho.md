## You'll want to know: めいしょ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C402-KANKOU402-03]; assesses=[] -->

**めいしょ** — *meisho* — "a famous sight; a place of interest".

> **この まちの めいしょを まわりました。** — *kono machi no meisho o mawarimashita* — "We went round the sights of this town."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**めいしょ** (*meisho*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *mei*, "noted" (Middle Chinese *mjieng*) and *sho*, "place" (*srjoX*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
