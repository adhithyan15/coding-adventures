# ಬಹುಕಾಲ (bahukāla) — a long time

## You'll want to know: ಬಹುಕಾಲ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C331-HINDE331-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ಬಹುಕಾಲ** — *bahukāla* — "a long time".

> ಅಜ್ಜ ಬಹುಕಾಲ ಆ ಹಳ್ಳಿಯಲ್ಲಿ ಇದ್ದರು.
>
> *ajja bahukāla ā haḷḷiyalli iddaru.*
>
> Grandfather lived in that village for a long time.

A formal phrase; in talk people say ತುಂಬಾ ಸಮಯ.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಬಹುಕಾಲ** (*bahukāla*) is borrowed from Sanskrit, a compound of *bahu*, "much, many" (CDIAL 9187), and *kāla*, "time" (Monier-Williams).
