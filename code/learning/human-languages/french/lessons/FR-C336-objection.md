## You'll want to know: l'objection
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C336-DESACCORD-03]; assesses=[] -->

**l'objection** — "the objection".

> Pas d'objection ? Alors nous commençons.
>
> *No objection? Then we start.*

*Faire une objection* — to raise an objection. Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*objection* is a learned borrowing from Late Latin *obiectiō* "a throwing against, an objection", from *obicere* "to throw before" (Lewis & Short), built on *iacere* "to throw".
