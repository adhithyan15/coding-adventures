## You'll want to know: कैवणो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C268-KAIVNO268-01]; assesses=[] -->

**कैवणो** — *kaivṇo* — "to say".

> वो साची बात कैवै है।
>
> *vo sāchī bāt kaivai hai.*
>
> He tells the truth.

Past (I …): म्हैं एक सबद कोनी कैयो, *mhaĩ ek sabad konī kaiyo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**कैवणो** (*kaivṇo*) is inherited from Sanskrit *kathayati*, "converses with, describes"; CDIAL lists the Old Marwari forms *kahaï* and *kai* under it (CDIAL 2703).
