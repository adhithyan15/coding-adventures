## You'll want to know: سوجھنا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C380-MASHQ380-04]; assesses=[] -->

**سوجھنا** — *sūjhnā* — "to occur to (someone); to strike".

> اگر مجھے کوئی حل سوجھا تو میں بتاؤں گا۔
>
> *agar mujhe koī hal sūjhā to maiṅ batāūṅ gā.*
>
> If a solution occurs to me, I will tell you.

Past (it …): وہ خیال اچانک سوجھا, *voh khayāl achānak sūjhā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**سوجھنا** (*sūjhnā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *sūjhnā*, "to be perceptible, to occur to", under Sanskrit *śúdhyati*, "becomes clear" (CDIAL 12527); **سجھانا**, "to suggest", is its partner.
