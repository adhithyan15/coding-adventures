## You'll want to know: a conservatória
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C443-SERVICOS-03]; assesses=[] -->

**a conservatória** — "the registry office".

> Casaram na conservatória da cidade.
>
> *They got married at the town registry office.*

Births, marriages and deaths are recorded at *a conservatória do registo civil*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*conservatória* is built on *conservar* 'to keep', from Latin *cōnservāre* 'to preserve' (EtymDB): the office that keeps the records.
