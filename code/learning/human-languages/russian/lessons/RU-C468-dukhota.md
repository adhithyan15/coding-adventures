## You'll want to know: духота
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C468-ZHARA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**духота** (*dukhotá*) — "stuffiness, close heat".

> В вагоне была ужасная духота.
>
> *V vagóne bylá uzhásnaya dukhotá.*
>
> It was terribly stuffy in the carriage.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*духота* is built on *дух* 'breath, air' with *-ота*: heavy air one can hardly breathe. EtymDB does not list it.
