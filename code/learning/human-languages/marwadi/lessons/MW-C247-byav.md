## You'll want to know: ब्याव
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C247-BYAV247-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ब्याव** — *byāv* — "a wedding; a marriage".

> आज गांव मांय ब्याव है।
>
> *āj gā̃v mā̃y byāv hai.*
>
> There is a wedding in the village today.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ब्याव** (*byāv*) is inherited from Sanskrit *vivāha*, "marriage"; CDIAL lists Old Marwari *byāha* under it (CDIAL 11920).
