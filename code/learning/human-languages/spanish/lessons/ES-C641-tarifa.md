## You'll want to know: la tarifa
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C641-TAXI-03]; assesses=[] -->

**la tarifa** — "the fare, the rate; the price list".

> La tarifa de noche es más cara.
>
> *The night fare is more expensive.*

For a phone or for electricity too: *una tarifa plana* is a flat rate.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*tarifa* comes from Arabic *taʿrīfa* "a notice, a list of rates" (EtymDB), from the verb *ʿarrafa* "to make known".
