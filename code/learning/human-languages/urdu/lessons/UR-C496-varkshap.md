## You'll want to know: ورکشاپ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C496-VARKSHAP496-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ورکشاپ** — *varkshāp* — "a workshop".

> ہماری ورکشاپ اسٹیشن کے قریب ہے۔
>
> *hamārī varkshāp isṭeshan ke qarīb hai.*
>
> Our workshop is near the station.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ورکشاپ** (*varkshāp*) is borrowed from English *workshop*.
