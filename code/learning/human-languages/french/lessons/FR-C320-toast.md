## You'll want to know: le toast
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C320-TOAST-02]; assesses=[] -->

**le toast** — "the toast (with a drink); toasted bread".

> Le père de la mariée a porté un toast.
>
> *The bride's father proposed a toast.*

*Porter un toast à quelqu'un* — to drink to someone. Also a slice of toasted bread.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*toast* is usually traced to English *toast* (EtymDB lists no parent), which comes from Old French *toster* "to grill", built on Latin *tostus* "roasted", from *torrēre* (Lewis & Short).
