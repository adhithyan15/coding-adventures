## You'll want to know: बेपत्ता
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C291-SUTKA291-04]; assesses=[] -->

**बेपत्ता** — *bepattā* — "missing".

> तो मुलगा दोन दिवसांपासून बेपत्ता आहे.
>
> *to mulgā don divsāmpāsūn bepattā āhe.*
>
> That boy has been missing for two days.

बेपत्ता keeps one form: बेपत्ता मुलगी, बेपत्ता माणूस. पत्ता is an address: with no address, no trace.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**बेपत्ता** (*bepattā*) is built from the Persian prefix *be-*, 'without', and *pattā*, 'an address, a trace'; the origin of *pattā* itself is not settled.
