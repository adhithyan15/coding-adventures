## You'll want to know: cōnclūdō, cōnclūdere
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C364-CONCLUDO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**cōnclūdō, cōnclūdere** — "to shut in; to conclude, to infer".

> Ex hīs rēbus cōnclūdō eum mentīrī.
>
> *From these facts I conclude that he is lying.*

*cōnclūdō, cōnclūdere, cōnclūsī, cōnclūsum*. Built on *claudō*, to shut: to shut an argument up tight. English *conclude*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*cōnclūdō* is built from *con-* and *claudō* "to shut" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short), the vowel changed inside the compound. English *conclude* comes from it.
