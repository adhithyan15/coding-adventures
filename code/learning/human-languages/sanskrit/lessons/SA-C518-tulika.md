## You'll want to know: तूलिका
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C518-TULIKA518-01]; assesses=[] -->

**तूलिका** — *tūlikā* — "a paintbrush".

> चित्रकारः नूतनां तूलिकां क्रीणाति।
>
> *citrakāraḥ nūtanāṃ tūlikāṃ krīṇāti.*
>
> The painter buys a new brush.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**तूलिका** (*tūlikā*) is the word MW gives as the same as *tūli*, a painter's brush; MW gives no further derivation, so its origin is uncertain (MW).
