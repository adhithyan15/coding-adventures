## You'll want to know: péi
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C163-WEEKEND163-02]; assesses=[] -->

*péi* — "to keep someone company; to go along with".

> *zhōumò wǒ péi māma qù mǎi dōngxi* — At the weekend I'm going shopping with my mum.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*péi* is inherited from Middle Chinese *bwoj* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'to attend at someone's side'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for talking about plans.
