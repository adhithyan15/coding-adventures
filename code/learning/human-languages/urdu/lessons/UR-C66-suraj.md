## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**سورج** (*sūraj*) is usually traced to Sanskrit *sūrya*, "sun". CDIAL lists that word, but no Hindi or Urdu form under it (CDIAL 13574).
