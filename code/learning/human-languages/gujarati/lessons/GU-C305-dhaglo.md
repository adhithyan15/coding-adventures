## You'll want to know: ઢગલો
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C305-BHUKAMP305-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ઢગલો** — *ḍhaglo* — "a heap".

> ભૂકંપ પછી રસ્તા પર પથ્થરનો ઢગલો હતો.
>
> *bhūkamp pachī rastā par paththarno ḍhaglo hato.*
>
> After the earthquake there was a heap of stones on the road.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ઢગલો** (*ḍhaglo*) comes from *\*ḍhikka*, a form CDIAL reconstructs, where it lists Gujarati *ḍhaglo*, "a heap" (CDIAL 5585). It is masculine: **પથ્થરનો ઢગલો**, a heap of stones.
