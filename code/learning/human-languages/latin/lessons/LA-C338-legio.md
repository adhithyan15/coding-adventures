## You'll want to know: legiō, legiōnis
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C338-LEGIO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**legiō, legiōnis** — "a legion".

> Decima legiō fortissima erat.
>
> *The tenth legion was the bravest.*

Feminine, 3rd declension, from *legō*, 'to choose': a picked levy, about five thousand men. English *legion*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**legiō** is built on *legō*, "to gather, to choose" (Lewis & Short): a body of chosen soldiers. English *legion* comes from it through French.
