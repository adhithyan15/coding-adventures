Marathi, chapter 549: Losses and Protection.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
भरपाई देणे (bharpāī deṇe) — to compensate.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to sort out, then the Marathi for to waver.

You'll want to know: भरपाई देणे.
भरपाई देणे — bharpāī deṇe — "to compensate".
विमा कंपनीने पुरात बुडालेल्या गाडीची भरपाई दिली. vimā kampanīne purāt buḍālelyā gāḍīcī bharpāī dilī. The insurance company paid for the car that sank in the flood.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने भरपाई दिली, tyāne bharpāī dilī.
What is lost takes -ची: गाडीची भरपाई देणे (bharpāī deṇe).

The word, taken apart.
भरपाई देणे (bharpāī deṇe) is भरपाई, "making good", built on भरणे, "to fill", inherited from Sanskrit bharati (CDIAL 9397), with देणे.

Lesson 2 of 5.

Warm-up.

You'll want to know: मुकणे.
मुकणे — mukṇe — "to miss out on".
आजारपणामुळे तो अंतिम सामन्याला मुकला. ājārpaṇāmuḷe to antim sāmnyālā muklā. Because of illness he missed the final match.
Past (he …): तो संधीला मुकला, to sandhīlā muklā.
What is missed takes -ला: संधीला मुकणे (mukṇe).

The word, taken apart.
मुकणे (mukṇe) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi mukṇẽ, "to lose", under mukna, "loosed" (CDIAL 10157).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

The word, taken apart.
