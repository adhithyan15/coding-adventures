## You'll want to know: der Schlittschuh
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C512-WINTERSPORT-03]; assesses=[] -->

**der Schlittschuh** — "ice skate".

> Im Winter laufen wir auf dem See Schlittschuh.
>
> *In winter we go ice-skating on the lake.*

Plural: *die Schlittschuhe*. *Schlittschuh laufen* — to skate.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Schlittschuh* is built on a first part related to *Schlitten* "sledge" and *Schuh* "shoe", inherited from Old High German *scuoh*, from Proto-Germanic *skōhaz* (EtymDB).
