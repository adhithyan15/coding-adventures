## You'll want to know: परिखा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C422-PRAKARA422-03]; assesses=[] -->

**परिखा** — *parikhā* — "a moat".

> प्राकारात् बहिः गभीरा परिखा अस्ति।
>
> *prākārāt bahiḥ gabhīrā parikhā asti.*
>
> Outside the rampart there is a deep moat.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**परिखा** (*parikhā*) is built from *pari*, "around", and the root √khan, "to dig", which also gives *khanati*, "digs"; MW gives *parikhā* as a moat or trench round a town or fort (MW).
