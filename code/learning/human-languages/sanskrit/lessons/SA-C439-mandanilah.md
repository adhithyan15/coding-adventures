## You'll want to know: मन्दानिलः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C439-PAVANA439-05]; assesses=[] -->

**मन्दानिलः** — *mandānilaḥ* — "a gentle breeze".

> सायं नदीतीरे मन्दानिलः वहति।
>
> *sāyaṃ nadītīre mandānilaḥ vahati.*
>
> In the evening a gentle breeze blows on the riverbank.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**मन्दानिलः** (*mandānilaḥ*) is built from *manda*, "slow, gentle", and *anila*, "wind, air"; MW gives the compound as a gentle breeze, a zephyr (MW).
