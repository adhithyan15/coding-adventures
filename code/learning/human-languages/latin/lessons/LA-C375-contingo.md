## You'll want to know: contingō, contingere
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C375-EXSISTO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**contingō, contingere** — "to touch; to happen (to), to fall to one's lot".

> Mihi contigit ut eum vidērem.
>
> *I had the good luck to see him.*

*contingō, contingere, contigī, contactum*. Built on *tangō*, to touch. *Contingit* + dative, often of good luck. English *contact* and *contingent*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*contingō* is built from *con-* and *tangō* "to touch" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short), the vowel weakened inside the compound: what touches you is what happens to you. English *contact* and *contingent* come from it.
