# અભિમાન (abhimān) — conceit

## You'll want to know: અભિમાન
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C359-IJJAT359-04]; assesses=[] -->

**અભિમાન** — *abhimān* — "conceit".

> તેને પોતાના પૈસાનું ઘણું અભિમાન છે.
>
> *tene potānā paisānũ ghaṇũ abhimān che.*
>
> He is very proud of his money.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**અભિમાન** (*abhimān*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *abhimāna*, "a high opinion of oneself, pride" (Monier-Williams).
