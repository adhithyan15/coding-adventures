# نجا (najā) — to survive, to escape

## You'll want to know: نجا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C310-NAJDA310-02]; assesses=[] -->

**نجا** — *najā* — "to survive, to escape".

> نجا كل الركاب من الحادث.
>
> *najā kull ar-rukkāb min al-ḥādith.*
>
> All the passengers survived the accident.

Present: ينجو, *yanjū*.

It takes من: نجا من الموت.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**نجا** (*najā*) is built on the native Arabic root *n-j-w* (Lane), "to escape, to be safe"; *najāt*, rescue, is its verbal noun.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
