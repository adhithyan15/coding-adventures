## You'll want to know: विरमति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C374-PRAMADA374-05]; assesses=[] -->

**विरमति** — *viramati* — "ceases; stops".

> यदि वर्षा विरमति, तर्हि अहं बहिः गमिष्यामि।
>
> *yadi varṣā viramati, tarhi ahaṃ bahiḥ gamiṣyāmi.*
>
> If the rain stops, I will go out.

Past (it …): वर्षा विरतवती, *varṣā viratavatī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**विरमति** (*viramati*) is built on *vi* and the root √ram, "to rest"; MW gives *vi-√ram* "to stop, to pause, to cease, to come to an end" (MW).
