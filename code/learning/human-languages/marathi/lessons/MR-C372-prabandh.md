## You'll want to know: प्रबंध
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C372-SANSHODHAN372-04]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रबंध** — *prabandh* — "a thesis; a dissertation".

> तिने इतिहासावर प्रबंध लिहिला.
>
> *tine itihāsāvar prabandh lihilā.*
>
> She wrote a thesis on history.

Masculine.

प्रबंधाचा विषय, the topic of a thesis.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रबंध** (*prabandh*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *prabandha*, "band, connexion" (CDIAL 8695), later a connected composition.
