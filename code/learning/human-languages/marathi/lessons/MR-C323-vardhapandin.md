## You'll want to know: वर्धापनदिन
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C323-NIROP323-04]; assesses=[] -->

**वर्धापनदिन** — *vardhāpandin* — "an anniversary".

> आज आमच्या कंपनीचा पन्नासावा वर्धापनदिन आहे.
>
> *āj āmacyā kampnīcā pannāsāvā vardhāpandin āhe.*
>
> Today is our company's fiftieth anniversary.

Masculine.

वर्धापन, a birthday rite, + दिन, a day.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**वर्धापनदिन** (*vardhāpandin*) is built from Sanskrit *vardhāpana*, a rite kept on a birthday (Monier-Williams), and *dina*, 'a day'.
