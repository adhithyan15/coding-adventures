## You'll want to know: ける
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C235-BALL235-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ける** — *keru* — "to kick".

> **おとうとは にわで まりを けりました。** — *otōto wa niwa de mari o kerimashita* — "My younger brother kicked a ball in the garden."

Polite **けります**, *kerimasu*; a godan verb.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ける** (*keru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
