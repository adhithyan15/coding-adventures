## You'll want to know: displiceō, displicēre
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C514-CONT-05]; assesses=[] -->

**displiceō, displicēre** — "to displease".

> Haec verba mihi valdē displicent.
>
> *These words displease me greatly.*

*displiceō, displicēre, displicuī, displicitum*, with the dative; the opposite of *placeō*. English *displease*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**displiceō** is built on *dis-* "apart, not" and *placeō* "to please" (Lewis & Short; EtymDB). English *displease* comes from it through Old French.
