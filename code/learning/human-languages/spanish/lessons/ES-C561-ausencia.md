## You'll want to know: la ausencia
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C561-PARTIDA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**la ausencia** — "the absence".

> Durante su ausencia, su hermana cuidó la casa.
>
> *During his absence, his sister looked after the house.*

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ausencia* is a learned borrowing from Latin *absentia* 'absence' (Lewis & Short), from *abesse* 'to be away', built on *ab* and *esse* 'to be'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.
