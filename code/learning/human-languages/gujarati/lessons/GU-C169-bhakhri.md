# ભાખરી (bhākhrī) — bhakhri, thick flatbread

## You'll want to know: ભાખરી
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C169-DISH169-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ભાખરી** — *bhākhrī* — "bhakhri, thick flatbread".

> **દાદા સવારે ભાખરી ખાય છે.** — *dādā savāre bhākhrī khāy che* — "Grandfather eats bhakhri in the morning."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ભાખરી** (*bhākhrī*) is inherited. CDIAL lists the kindred masculine *bhākhro*, "lump of bread", under *\*bhakkha*, "lump", a reconstructed form, though not *bhākhrī* itself (CDIAL 9330).
