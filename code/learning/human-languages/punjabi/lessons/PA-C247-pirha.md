## You'll want to know: ਪੀੜ੍ਹਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C247-GHR247-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ਪੀੜ੍ਹਾ** — *pīṛhā* — "a low stool".

> ਦਾਦੀ ਪੀੜ੍ਹੇ ਉੱਤੇ ਬੈਠਦੀ ਹੈ।
>
> *dādī pīṛhe utte baiṭhdī hai.*
>
> Grandmother sits on a low stool.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਪੀੜ੍ਹਾ** (*pīṛhā*) is inherited from Sanskrit *pīṭha*, "stool, bench"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *pīṛhā*, "stool" (CDIAL 8222).
