Russian, chapter 289: Robbery and attack.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ограбление (ograbléniye) — a robbery.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a pickpocket, then the Russian for a break-in.

You'll want to know: ограбление.
ограбление (ograbléniye) — "a robbery".
Ограбление произошло ночью. Ograbléniye proizoshló nóchyu. The robbery happened at night.
Neuter. Ограбление банка — a bank robbery.

The word, taken apart.
ограбление (ograbléniye) is built on ограбить, the perfective of грабить (grábit) "to rob", which is inherited from Proto-Slavic grabiti (EtymDB).

Lesson 3 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a robbery, then the Russian for a robber.

You'll want to know: грабить.
грабить (grábit) — "to rob".
Они грабили магазины в центре. Oní grábili magazíny v tséntre. They robbed shops in the centre.
Imperfective; perfective ограбить (ográbit): его ограбили — he was robbed. Present граблю, грабишь.

The word, taken apart.
грабить (grábit) is inherited from Proto-Slavic grabiti (EtymDB), usually glossed in its old sense "to grab, to rake together".

Lesson 4 of 5.

Warm-up.

Before the new one: say the Russian for a robber, then the Russian for to rob.

You'll want to know: нападение.
нападение (napadéniye) — "an attack".
Нападение было неожиданным. Napadéniye býlo neozhídannym. The attack was unexpected.
Нападение на + accusative — an attack on.

The word, taken apart.
нападение (napadéniye) is built on нападать (napadát) "to attack", from на- "on" and падать "to fall", inherited from Proto-Slavic padati (EtymDB): a falling upon.
