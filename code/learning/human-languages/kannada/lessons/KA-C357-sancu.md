## You'll want to know: ಸಂಚು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C357-VANCHANE357-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸಂಚು** — *sañcu* — "a plot; a conspiracy".

> ಅರಮನೆಯಲ್ಲಿ ಕೆಲವರು ರಾಜನ ವಿರುದ್ಧ ಸಂಚು ಮಾಡಿದರು.
>
> *aramaneyalli kelavaru rājana viruddha sañcu māḍidaru.*
>
> In the palace some people plotted against the king.

Plural: ಸಂಚುಗಳು, *sañcugaḷu*.

A plot is "made": ಸಂಚು ಮಾಡು, to plot.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸಂಚು** (*sañcu*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 2293 lists Kannada *sañcu*, "an expedient, an artifice, a stratagem, an intrigue".

## What you've built

One more word for telling what happened.
