# ಪತ್ತೆ ಹಚ್ಚು (patte haccu) — to track down; to detect

## Warm-up

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Kannada for a wedding party, then the Kannada for to go missing.

## You'll want to know: ಪತ್ತೆ ಹಚ್ಚು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C326-KANE326-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ಪತ್ತೆ ಹಚ್ಚು** — *patte haccu* — "to track down; to detect".

> ಪೊಲೀಸರು ಎರಡು ದಿನದಲ್ಲಿ ಮಗುವನ್ನು ಪತ್ತೆ ಹಚ್ಚಿದರು.
>
> *polīsaru eraḍu dinadalli maguvannu patte haccidaru.*
>
> The police tracked down the child in two days.

Past (I …): ಪತ್ತೆ ಹಚ್ಚಿದೆ, *patte haccide*.

ಪತ್ತೆ is a trace; ಪತ್ತೆಯಾಗು means to be found.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಪತ್ತೆ ಹಚ್ಚು** (*patte haccu*) is a Kannada phrase verb built from ಪತ್ತೆ, "a trace, a whereabouts", and ಹಚ್ಚು, "to apply, to light". The origin of ಪತ್ತೆ is not settled.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
