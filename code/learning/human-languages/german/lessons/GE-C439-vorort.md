## You'll want to know: der Vorort
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C439-VIERTEL-03]; assesses=[] -->

**der Vorort** — "the suburb".

> Viele Familien ziehen in einen Vorort mit Garten.
>
> *Many families move to a suburb with a garden.*

Plural: *die Vororte*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Vorort* is built from *vor* 'before, in front' and *Ort* 'place', inherited from Old High German *ort* 'point, edge' (EtymDB).
