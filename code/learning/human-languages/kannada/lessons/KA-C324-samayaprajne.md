# ಸಮಯಪ್ರಜ್ಞೆ (samayaprajñe) — presence of mind

## You'll want to know: ಸಮಯಪ್ರಜ್ಞೆ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C324-BENKI324-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸಮಯಪ್ರಜ್ಞೆ** — *samayaprajñe* — "presence of mind".

> ಹುಡುಗಿಯ ಸಮಯಪ್ರಜ್ಞೆಯಿಂದ ಎಲ್ಲರೂ ಬೆಂಕಿಯಿಂದ ತಪ್ಪಿಸಿಕೊಂಡರು.
>
> *huḍugiya samayaprajñeyinda ellarū beṅkiyinda tappisikoṇḍaru.*
>
> Thanks to the girl's presence of mind, everyone escaped the fire.

Quick, calm thinking in danger: ಸಮಯಪ್ರಜ್ಞೆ ತೋರಿಸು, to show presence of mind.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸಮಯಪ್ರಜ್ಞೆ** (*samayaprajñe*) is borrowed from Sanskrit, a compound of *samaya*, "a meeting, an occasion, time" (CDIAL 13185), and *prajñā*, "wisdom, awareness" (CDIAL 8515).
