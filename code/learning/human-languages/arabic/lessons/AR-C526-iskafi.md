## You'll want to know: إسكافي
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C526-HIRAF526-05]; assesses=[] -->

**إسكافي** — *iskāfī* — "a cobbler".

> أصلح الإسكافي حذاء أبي.
>
> *aṣlaḥa al-iskāfī ḥidhāʾ abī.*
>
> The cobbler repaired my father's shoe.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**إسكافي** (*iskāfī*) is an old Arabic word for a shoemaker or cobbler. Lane gives *iskāf*, a maker of boots or shoes, under *s-k-f*; the origin is not settled.
