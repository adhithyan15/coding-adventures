# വിടപറയുക (viṭapaṟayuka) — to say goodbye

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Malayalam for to bid farewell, then the Malayalam for to see off.

## You'll want to know: വിടപറയുക
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C390-YATRAYAYAPPU390-04]; assesses=[] -->

**വിടപറയുക** — *viṭapaṟayuka* — "to say goodbye".

> കുട്ടികൾ മുത്തശ്ശിയോട് വിടപറഞ്ഞു.
>
> *kuṭṭikaḷ muttaśśiyōṭŭ viṭapaṟaññu.*
>
> The children said goodbye to grandmother.

Past: വിടപറഞ്ഞു, *viṭapaṟaññu*.

The person takes -ഓട്. In the news, also to pass away.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**വിടപറയുക** (*viṭapaṟayuka*) is a native Dravidian compound of വിട (*viṭa*), "leave, a farewell" (DEDR 5393), and പറയുക (*paṟayuka*), "to say" (DEDR 4031).
