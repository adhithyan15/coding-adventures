## You'll want to know: उस्ताद
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C507-DAFTAR507-03]; assesses=[] -->

**उस्ताद** — *ustād* — "a master (of a craft or an art); a teacher".

> मेरे उस्ताद ने मुझे लकड़ी का काम सिखाया।
>
> *Mere ustād ne mujhe lakṛī kā kām sikhāyā.*
>
> My master taught me woodwork.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**उस्ताद** (*ustād*) is usually traced to Persian *ustād*, "a master". In Hindi it is the master of a craft or an art, and the teacher who passes it on.
