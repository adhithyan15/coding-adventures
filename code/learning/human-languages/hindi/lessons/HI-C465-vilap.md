## You'll want to know: विलाप
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C465-VILAP465-05]; assesses=[] -->

**विलाप** — *vilāp* — "wailing; lamentation".

> बेटी का विलाप सुनकर सब लोग रो पड़े।
>
> *beṭī kā vilāp sunkar sab log ro paṛe.*
>
> Hearing the daughter's wailing, everyone broke down in tears.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**विलाप** (*vilāp*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *vilāpa*, "lamentation", from *vi-lap*, "to wail" (Monier-Williams).
