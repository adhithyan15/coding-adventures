## You'll want to know: il narratore
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C323-FAVOLA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**il narratore** — "the narrator, the storyteller".

> Il narratore parla in prima persona.
>
> *The narrator speaks in the first person.*

A woman is *la narratrice*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*narratore* is a learned borrowing from Latin *nārrātor* "a storyteller" (Lewis & Short).
