## You'll want to know: заучивать
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C384-UCHYOBA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**заучивать** (*zaúchivat*) — "to memorise, to learn by heart".

> Мы заучиваем стихи к уроку.
>
> *My zaúchivayem stikhí k uróku.*
>
> We learn poems by heart for the lesson.

Imperfective; perfective *заучить*. *Заучивать слова* — to memorise words.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**заучивать** is built from *за-* and *учить* "to teach, to learn", inherited from Proto-Slavic *\*učiti*: to learn a thing until it sticks.
