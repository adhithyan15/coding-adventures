## You'll want to know: گشت
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C319-MAMUR-02]; assesses=[] -->

**گشت** (*gasht*) — "a patrol".

> گشت پلیس هر شب از کوچه ما رد میشود.
>
> *Gasht-e polis har shab az kuche-ye mâ radd mishavad.*
>
> A police patrol goes through our street every night.

From *gashtan* "to go round". *Gasht zadan* is "to stroll about".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*gasht* is the past stem of the native verb *gashtan* "to turn, to go round", used as a noun. EtymDB lists it without a parent.
