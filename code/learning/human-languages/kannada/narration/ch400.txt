Kannada, chapter 400: Public Money.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಬಜೆಟ್ (bajeṭ) — a budget.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to distribute, then the Kannada for rural.

You'll want to know: ಬಜೆಟ್.
ಬಜೆಟ್ — bajeṭ — "a budget".
ಈ ಸಲದ ಬಜೆಟ್ ರೈತರಿಗೆ ಒಳ್ಳೆಯದು. ī salada bajeṭ raitarige oḷḷeyadu. This year's budget is good for farmers.
ಮುಂಗಡ ಪತ್ರ is the formal Kannada name.

The word, taken apart.
ಬಜೆಟ್ (bajeṭ) is a loan from English budget.

Lesson 2 of 5.
ಆದಾಯ (ādāya) — income.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for rural, then the Kannada for a budget.

You'll want to know: ಆದಾಯ.
ಆದಾಯ — ādāya — "income".
ಅವರ ತಿಂಗಳ ಆದಾಯ ಕಡಿಮೆ. avara tiṅgaḷa ādāya kaḍime. Their monthly income is low.
ಆದಾಯ (ādāya) ತೆರಿಗೆ, income tax.

The word, taken apart.
ಆದಾಯ (ādāya) is borrowed from Sanskrit ādāya, "taking, seizing" (Monier-Williams), from ā-dā, "to take": what one receives.

Lesson 4 of 5.
ವಿನಿಯೋಗಿಸು (viniyōgisu) — to utilise; to spend.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for income, then the Kannada for a subsidy.

You'll want to know: ವಿನಿಯೋಗಿಸು.
ವಿನಿಯೋಗಿಸು — viniyōgisu — "to utilise; to spend".
ಪಂಚಾಯಿತಿ ಹಣವನ್ನು ರಸ್ತೆಗಾಗಿ ವಿನಿಯೋಗಿಸಿತು. pañcāyiti haṇavannu rastegāgi viniyōgisitu. The village council spent the money on roads.
Past (I …): ವಿನಿಯೋಗಿಸಿದೆ, viniyōgiside.
ವಿನಿಯೋಗ, use or allocation.

The word, taken apart.
ವಿನಿಯೋಗಿಸು (viniyōgisu) is built on Sanskrit viniyoga, "apportionment, distribution, use" (Monier-Williams), with the Kannada verb ending -isu.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ವ್ಯರ್ಥ ಮಾಡು (vyartha māḍu) — to waste.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a subsidy, then the Kannada for to utilise.

You'll want to know: ವ್ಯರ್ಥ ಮಾಡು.
ವ್ಯರ್ಥ ಮಾಡು — vyartha māḍu — "to waste".
ನಾನು ಇಡೀ ದಿನ ಫೋನಿನಲ್ಲಿ ಸಮಯ ವ್ಯರ್ಥ ಮಾಡಿದೆ. nānu iḍī dina phōninalli samaya vyartha māḍide. I wasted the whole day on the phone.
Past (I …): ವ್ಯರ್ಥ ಮಾಡಿದೆ, vyartha māḍide.
ವ್ಯರ್ಥ alone means in vain: ವ್ಯರ್ಥವಾಯಿತು, it was in vain.

The word, taken apart.
ವ್ಯರ್ಥ ಮಾಡು (vyartha māḍu) is built from Sanskrit vyartha, "useless, vain" (Monier-Williams), from vi- "without" and artha "purpose", with māḍu, "to do".
