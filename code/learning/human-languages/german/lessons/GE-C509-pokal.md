## You'll want to know: der Pokal
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C509-WETTKAMPF-03]; assesses=[] -->

**der Pokal** — "trophy, cup".

> Der Kapitän hob den Pokal in die Luft.
>
> *The captain lifted the trophy into the air.*

Plural: *die Pokale*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Pokal* was borrowed from Italian *boccale* "jug" (EtymDB), from Late Latin *baucalis*, from Greek: a drinking cup before it was a prize.
