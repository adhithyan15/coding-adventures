## You'll want to know: gōngjiāochē
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C106-STOP106-03]; assesses=[] -->

*gōngjiāochē* — "city bus".

> *gōngjiāochē lái le* — Here comes the bus.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*gōngjiāochē* is built from gōng 'public', jiāo 'to cross' and 车 'vehicle', all inherited from Middle Chinese, *kuwng*, *kaew* and *tsyhae* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription). The bus is a modern sense of the whole.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for asking where things are.
