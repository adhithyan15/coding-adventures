## You'll want to know: تکلیف
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C401-MORUR-05]; assesses=[] -->

**تکلیف** (*taklif*) — "homework, an assignment; a duty".

> بچه ها بعد از شام تکلیفشان را انجام دادند.
>
> *bachche hâ ba'd az shâm taklifeshân râ anjâm dâdand.*
>
> The children did their homework after dinner.

Plural *takâlif*, an Arabic broken plural. *Taklif-e shab* — homework.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**تکلیف** is usually traced to Arabic *taklīf* "imposing a task, a charge".
