## You'll want to know: आक्रमति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C290-DASYU290-03]; assesses=[] -->

**आक्रमति** — *ākramati* — "attacks".

> दस्युः पथिकम् आक्रमति।
>
> *dasyuḥ pathikam ākramati.*
>
> The robber attacks the traveller.

Past (he …): सः पथिकम् आक्रान्तवान्, *saḥ pathikam ākrāntavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**आक्रमति** (*ākramati*) is built from *ā*, "towards", and the root √kram, "to step, stride"; MW gives *ā-kram* as to step near, seize and attack (MW).
