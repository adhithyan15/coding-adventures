# One whole word for all

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Recall the English idea “all,” then listen for one new Punjabi word.

## You'll want to know
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-SABH-205]; assesses=[] -->

> **ਸਭ** — *sabh* — all; everyone; everything here

Hear and say the whole word before reading it. Keep **ਸਭ** whole. In this
chapter it carries one beginner idea: all, understood as everyone or everything
in the small frame that follows. The lesson does not open every grammatical use.

## The word, taken apart — where the trail runs out
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-LEX-SABH-205] -->

Punjabi University Patiala's lexical statistics lists **ਸਭ** as an indefinite
pronoun with the meanings “all / every / whole.” Its pronoun grammar also lists
the word among plural indefinite pronouns and glosses it “all.” Its number
introduction independently shows that the same form can serve an indefinite
numeral role.
Sources: [Punjabi University lexical statistics](https://www.learnpunjabi.org/statistics.html),
[Punjabi University pronoun grammar](https://www.learnpunjabi.org/Pronoun.html),
and [Punjabi University number introduction](https://www.learnpunjabi.org/numbers_intro.html).

Those sources support the present meaning, but they do not establish a deeper
history. The Punjabi origin remains unknown in the sources used here. Make no
historical derivation claim; remember the whole form.
