## You'll want to know: ശാപം
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C403-PRETHAM403-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ശാപം** — *śāpaṁ* — "a curse".

> മാന്ത്രികന്റെ ശാപം കാരണം രാജകുമാരൻ ഒരു തവളയായി.
>
> *māntrikanṟe śāpaṁ kāraṇaṁ rājakumāran oru tavaḷayāyi.*
>
> Because of the magician's curse the prince became a frog.

The opposite of a blessing, അനുഗ്രഹം.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ശാപം** (*śāpaṁ*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *śāpa*, "a curse, abuse" (CDIAL 12393).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
