## You'll want to know: とびだす
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C201-ROAD201-04]; assesses=[] -->

**とびだす** — *tobidasu* — "to rush out; to jump out".

> **こどもが みちに とびだします。** — *kodomo ga michi ni tobidashimasu* — "A child rushes out into the street."

Polite **とびだします**, *tobidashimasu*; a godan verb.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**とびだす** (*tobidasu*) is native Japanese (wago), built from *tobi*, "flying, leaping", and *dasu*, "to put out", both inherited rather than borrowed (JMdict).
