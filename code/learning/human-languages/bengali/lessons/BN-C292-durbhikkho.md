# দুর্ভিক্ষ (durbhikkhô) — a famine

## You'll want to know: দুর্ভিক্ষ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C292-KHORA292-02]; assesses=[] -->

**দুর্ভিক্ষ** — *durbhikkhô* — "a famine".

> দাদু একটা দুর্ভিক্ষের গল্প বললেন।
>
> *dādu ekṭā durbhikkher gôlpo bôllen.*
>
> Grandfather told a story about a famine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**দুর্ভিক্ষ** (*durbhikkhô*) is Sanskrit *durbhikṣa*, 'dearth, famine' (Monier-Williams): *dur-*, 'bad', and *bhikṣā*, 'alms', literally a bad time for alms.
