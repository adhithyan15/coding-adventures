## You'll want to know: ફાયદો
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C371-FAYDO371-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ફાયદો** — *phāydo* — "a benefit".

> જો તમે વહેલા ટિકિટ ખરીદશો તો ફાયદો થશે.
>
> *jo tame vahelā ṭikiṭ kharīdśo to phāydo thaśe.*
>
> If you buy the tickets early, you will save.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ફાયદો** (*phāydo*) is usually traced to Arabic *fāʾida*, "a benefit, a profit", through Persian.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
