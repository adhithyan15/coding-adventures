Kannada, chapter 94: At the Table.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ತಿಂಡಿ (tiṇḍi) — a snack, a light meal.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a wound, then the Kannada for vomiting.

You'll want to know: ತಿಂಡಿ.
ತಿಂಡಿ (tiṇḍi) — "a snack, a light meal", also the word for breakfast. It comes from ತಿನ್ನು (tinnu), "to eat".
ತಿಂಡಿ ಆಯ್ತಾ? (tiṇḍi āytā?) — "have you had breakfast?"

What you've built.
Something to eat, from the verb to eat.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: tiṇḍi]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: tiṇḍi, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: ask tiṇḍi āytā?]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Kannada for a wound, then the Kannada for vomiting, then say tiṇḍi again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does ತಿಂಡಿ (tiṇḍi) mean? ("A snack, a light meal".) Say it once more.
