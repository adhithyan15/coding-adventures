## You'll want to know: ગળવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C226-BREATH226-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ગળવું** — *gaḷvũ* — "to swallow".

> **શું બાળક દવા ગળે છે?** — *śũ bāḷak davā gaḷe che?* — "Does the child swallow the medicine?"

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ગળવું** (*gaḷvũ*) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from *galati*, "swallows", beside *gilati* (CDIAL 4075).
