Arabic, chapter 479: A day in the hills.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
تزلج (tazallaja) — to ski; to skate.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for fur, then the Arabic for to graze.

You'll want to know: تزلج.
تزلج — tazallaja — "to ski; to skate".
تزلجنا على الثلج في الشتاء. tazallajnā ʿalā ath-thalj fī ash-shitāʾ. We skied on the snow in winter.
Present: يتزلج, yatazallaj.

The word, taken apart.
تزلج (tazallaja) is usually traced to the native Arabic root z-l-j, "to slip, to slide". It is Form V.

What you've built.
One more word for getting around.

Lesson 3 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to ski, then the Arabic for a wild beast.

You'll want to know: كيس.
كيس — kīs — "a plastic bag; a sack".
وضعنا الطعام في كيس كبير. waḍaʿnā aṭ-ṭaʿām fī kīs kabīr. We put the food in a big bag.
Plural: أكياس, akyās.
ḥaqība is a bag one carries; kīs is a sack or a plastic bag.

The word, taken apart.
كيس (kīs) is given by Lane as a purse for money; the origin of the word is not settled.

تبادلنا العناوين في نهاية الرحلة. tabādalnā al-ʿanāwīn fī nihāyat ar-riḥla. We exchanged addresses at the end of the trip.

تبادل (tabādala) is built on the native Arabic root b-d-l, "to change, to replace" (Lane). It is Form VI.

Lesson 5 of 5.
حزم (ḥazama) — to pack (luggage).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a plastic bag, then the Arabic for to exchange.

You'll want to know: حزم.
حزم — ḥazama — "to pack (luggage)".
حزمت أمتعتي قبل الرحلة بيوم. ḥazamtu amtiʿatī qabla ar-riḥla bi-yawm. I packed my luggage a day before the trip.
Present: يحزم, yaḥzim.

The word, taken apart.
حزم (ḥazama) is built on the native Arabic root ḥ-z-m, "to bind, to tie up a bundle" (Lane). It shares its root with ḥizām, "a belt".
