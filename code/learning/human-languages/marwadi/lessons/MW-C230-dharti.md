## You'll want to know: धरती
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C230-SUN230-03]; assesses=[] -->

**धरती** — *dhartī* — "the earth; the ground".

> आज धरती ताती है।
>
> *āj dhartī tātī hai.*
>
> Today the ground is hot.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**धरती** (*dhartī*) is inherited from Sanskrit *dharitrī*, "she who carries", a name for the earth; CDIAL lists Old Marwari *dharatī*, "the earth" (CDIAL 6750).
