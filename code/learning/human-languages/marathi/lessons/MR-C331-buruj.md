## You'll want to know: बुरुज
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C331-VEDHA331-05]; assesses=[] -->

**बुरुज** — *buruj* — "a bastion; a fort tower".

> बुरुजावरून संपूर्ण गाव दिसतं.
>
> *burujāvarūn sampūrṇa gāv distaṁ.*
>
> The whole village can be seen from the bastion.

Masculine.

बुरुजावर, on the bastion.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**बुरुज** (*buruj*) is a loan from Persian *burj*, usually traced to Arabic *burj*, 'a tower'.
