# ઘ (gha) — the letter that opens ઘર

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-SCRIPT-TE-01, GU-SCRIPT-TTHA-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: write તે and ઠ once each, and name them.

[PAUSE 2s] Now say **ઘર** (*ghar*), "home".
You have read it for a long time. This lesson takes one letter out of it and puts
it in your hand.

## Script you'll notice: ઘ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-SCRIPT-C45-GHA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ઘ** — *gha*, a *g* with a puff of breath after it.

It opens **ઘર** (*ghar*), "home". Say *gar*, then *ghar*: the second has the breath.

## Writing: ઘ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-SCRIPT-C45-GHA-01] -->

- **1.** start here: circle the upper lobe, turn through the middle, and round the lower body
- **2.** lift, then descend the right spine and turn through its lower foot — and only now lift

**Pen lifts: 1.**

> Stroke order is one attested teaching order; hands and teachers vary.
> Source: t30apps.com, ‘Gujarati Alphabet Writing Practice’, version 1.0, ઘ animation, first and second SVG paths.
