# પીગળવું (pīgaḷvũ) — to soften with pity; to melt

## You'll want to know: પીગળવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C459-KOMAL459-05]; assesses=[] -->

**પીગળવું** — *pīgaḷvũ* — "to soften with pity; to melt".

> બાળકનાં આંસુ જોઈને પિતાનું હૃદય પીગળી ગયું.
>
> *bāḷaknā̃ ā̃su joīne pitānũ hṛday pīgḷī gayũ.*
>
> Seeing the child's tears, the father's heart melted.

Past (it …): તે બરફ પીગળી ગયો, *te baraph pīgḷī gayo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**પીગળવું** (*pīgaḷvũ*) is inherited from Sanskrit *pragalati*, "drips"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb, "to melt, be dissolved" (CDIAL 8468).
