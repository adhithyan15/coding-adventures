## You'll want to know: てつ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C488-BUILD488-04]; assesses=[] -->

**てつ** — *tetsu* — "iron".

> **てつの なべは おもいです。** — *tetsu no nabe wa omoi desu* — "An iron pot is heavy."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**てつ** (*tetsu*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): one Chinese character read with its on'yomi, reading borrowed from Chinese. It is *tetsu*, "iron" (Middle Chinese *thet*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
