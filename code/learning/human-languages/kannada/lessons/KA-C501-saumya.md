## You'll want to know: ಸೌಮ್ಯ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C501-SAUMYA501-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸೌಮ್ಯ** — *saumya* — "gentle; mild".

> ನಮ್ಮ ವೈದ್ಯರು ತುಂಬಾ ಸೌಮ್ಯ ಸ್ವಭಾವದವರು.
>
> *namma vaidyaru tumbā saumya svabhāvadavaru.*
>
> Our doctor has a very gentle nature.

Also mild weather or a mild taste.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸೌಮ್ಯ** (*saumya*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *saumya*, "relating to soma", and later "cool and mild" (CDIAL 13620).
