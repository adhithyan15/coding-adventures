## You'll want to know: zhēnglùn
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C348-TALK348-03]; assesses=[] -->

*zhēnglùn* — "to argue over a point; to debate".

> *tāmen zài zhēnglùn zhège wèntí* — They're arguing over this question.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*zhēnglùn* is built from *zhēng* and *lùn*, both inherited from Middle Chinese, *tsreang* and *lwon* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to compete' and the second 'to discuss'.
