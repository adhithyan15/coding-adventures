## You'll want to know: あげる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C309-OKURU309-02]; assesses=[] -->

**あげる** — *ageru* — "to give (to someone else)".

> **いもうとに かばんを あげました。** — *imōto ni kaban o agemashita* — "I gave my little sister a bag."

Polite **あげます**, *agemasu*; an ichidan verb. Past (polite): **あねが こどもに おかしを あげました。**, *ane ga kodomo ni okashi o agemashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**あげる** (*ageru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict).
