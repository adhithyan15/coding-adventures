Hindi, chapter 471: Manners.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
विनम्र (vinamra) — polite; humble.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for fearless, then the Hindi for a risk.

You'll want to know: विनम्र.
विनम्र — vinamra — "polite; humble".
मीरा बड़ों से हमेशा विनम्र होकर बात करती है। Mīrā baṛoṁ se hameśā vinamra hokar bāt kartī hai. Mira always speaks politely to her elders.

The word, taken apart.
विनम्र (vinamra) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit vinamra, "bent down, submissive, humble, modest" (Monier-Williams): someone who bows a little.

बदतमीज़ — badtamīz — "rude; ill-mannered".
वह लड़का बहुत बदतमीज़ है, बड़ों का आदर नहीं करता। vah laṛkā bahut badtamīz hai, baṛoṁ kā ādar nahīṃ kartā. That boy is very rude; he doesn't respect his elders.

The word, taken apart.
बदतमीज़ (badtamīz) is usually traced to Persian bad, "bad", joined to Arabic tamīz, "discernment, good manners", which came through Persian: having bad manners.

You'll want to know: शिष्टाचार.

मेहमान के आने पर खड़े होना शिष्टाचार है। mehmān ke āne par khaṛe honā śiṣṭācār hai. Standing up when a guest arrives is good manners.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
शिष्टाचार (śiṣṭācār) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit śiṣṭācāra, "the conduct of the learned, good manners" (Monier-Williams), built on śiṣṭa, "well taught", and ācāra, "conduct".

Lesson 5 of 5.
चिढ़ाना (ciṛhānā) — to tease.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for consideration, then the Hindi for etiquette.

You'll want to know: चिढ़ाना.
चिढ़ाना — ciṛhānā — "to tease".
बड़ा भाई मीरा को उसके नाम पर चिढ़ाता है। baṛā bhāī Mīrā ko uske nām par ciṛhātā hai. Mira's big brother teases her about her name.
Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने बहन को चिढ़ाया, usne bahan ko ciṛhāyā.

The word, taken apart.
चिढ़ाना (ciṛhānā) is the causative partner of ciṛhnā, "to be provoked", which CDIAL lists under ciḍ (CDIAL 4794): to make someone cross on purpose, usually in fun.
