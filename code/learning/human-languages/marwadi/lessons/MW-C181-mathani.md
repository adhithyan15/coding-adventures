## You'll want to know: मथाणी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C181-BILOVO181-03]; assesses=[] -->

**मथाणी** — *mathāṇī* — "a churning stick".

> मथाणी दही मांय है।
>
> *mathāṇī dahī mā̃y hai.*
>
> The churning stick is in the curd.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**मथाणी** (*mathāṇī*) is inherited from Sanskrit *manthāna*, "a churning stick"; CDIAL lists Old Marwari *mathāṇī* (CDIAL 9842).
