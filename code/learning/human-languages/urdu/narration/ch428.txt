Urdu, chapter 428: Drops and Showers.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
بوند (būṅd) — a drop.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a handkerchief, then the Urdu for to look good (on someone).

You'll want to know: بوند.
بوند — būṅd — "a drop".
بارش کی پہلی بوند میرے ہاتھ پر گری۔ bārish kī pehlī būṅd mere hāth par girī. The first drop of rain fell on my hand.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
بوند (būṅd) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin bū̃d, "a drop", under Sanskrit bindú, "a drop, a spot" (CDIAL 9240).
