Russian, chapter 476: Fright and Courage.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
испуг (ispúg) — a fright.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for pride, then the Russian for to boast.

You'll want to know: испуг.
испуг (ispúg) — "a fright".
От испуга кошка прыгнула на шкаф. Ot ispúga kóshka prýgnula na shkaf. The cat jumped onto the wardrobe in fright.
Masculine. The adjective is испуганный — frightened.

The word, taken apart.
испуг (ispúg) is built on ис- and the root of пугать 'to frighten'; EtymDB lists пугать without a source, so its further origin is not settled.

Lesson 2 of 6.
пугаться (pugátsya) — to get frightened.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to boast, then the Russian for a fright.

You'll want to know: пугаться.
пугаться (pugátsya) — "to get frightened".
Мой сын пугается грома. Moy syn pugáyetsya gróma. My son gets frightened of thunder.
Imperfective; perfective испугаться. Takes the genitive: пугаться (pugátsya) грома.

The word, taken apart.
пугаться (pugátsya) is the reflexive of пугать 'to frighten, to scare'; EtymDB lists пугать without a source, so the origin is not settled.

Lesson 3 of 6.
вздрагивать (vzdrágivat) — to flinch.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a fright, then the Russian for to get frightened.

You'll want to know: вздрагивать.
вздрагивать (vzdrágivat) — "to flinch".
Собака вздрагивает от каждого звука. Sobáka vzdrágivayet ot kázhdogo zvúka. The dog flinches at every sound.
Imperfective; perfective вздрогнуть (once). Related to дрожать — to tremble.

The word, taken apart.
вздрагивать (vzdrágivat) is built on вз- and the root of дрогнуть 'to quiver' and дрожать 'to tremble'; EtymDB lists these words without a source.

Lesson 4 of 6.
трус (trus) — a coward.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to get frightened, then the Russian for to flinch.

You'll want to know: трус.
трус (trus) — "a coward".
В начале фильма герой был трусом. V nachále fílma geróy byl trúsom. At the start of the film the hero was a coward.
Masculine. The adjective is трусливый — cowardly.

The word, taken apart.
EtymDB lists трус (trus) without a source; it is usually linked to трусить 'to shake, to tremble', so a coward is someone who trembles.

Lesson 5 of 6.
смелость (smélost) — courage, boldness.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to flinch, then the Russian for a coward.

You'll want to know: смелость.
смелость (smélost) — "courage, boldness".
Нужно много смелости, чтобы петь на сцене. Núzhno mnógo smélosti, chtóby pet na stséne. It takes a lot of courage to sing on stage.
Feminine. Built on смелый — bold, brave.

The word, taken apart.
смелость (smélost) is built on смелый 'bold' with -ость (EtymDB); смелый is inherited from Old East Slavic sъmělъ (EtymDB).
