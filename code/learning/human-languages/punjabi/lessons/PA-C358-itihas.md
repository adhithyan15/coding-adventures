## You'll want to know: ਇਤਿਹਾਸ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C358-SUBJ358-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ਇਤਿਹਾਸ** — *itihās* — "history".

> ਮੈਨੂੰ ਇਤਿਹਾਸ ਦੀ ਹਰ ਕਹਾਣੀ ਪਸੰਦ ਹੈ।
>
> *mainū̃ itihās dī har kahāṇī pasand hai.*
>
> I like every story from history.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਇਤਿਹਾਸ** (*itihās*) is taken over from Sanskrit *itihāsa*, "history, traditional accounts", a tatsama (Monier-Williams).
