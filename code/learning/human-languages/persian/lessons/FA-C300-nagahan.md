## You'll want to know: ناگهان
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C300-NAGAHAN-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ناگهان** (*nâgahân*) — "suddenly, all of a sudden".

> ناگهان برق رفت و خانه تاریک شد.
>
> *Nâgahân barq raft va khâne târik shod.*
>
> Suddenly the power went off and the house went dark.

Neutral and common in stories; in everyday speech Iranians also say *yekhow*. It usually opens the clause.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*nâgahân* is inherited: EtymDB derives it from *nâgah* (also *nâgâh*), which continues Middle Persian *anāgāh*, "unaware, unwitting". A sudden thing comes on you unawares.
