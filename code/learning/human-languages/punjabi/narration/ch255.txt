Punjabi, chapter 255: Letters and Stamps.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ਡਾਕ (ḍāk) — the post; the mail.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for wrong, then the Punjabi for to rub out.

You'll want to know: ਡਾਕ.
ਡਾਕ — ḍāk — "the post; the mail".
ਡਾਕ ਸਵੇਰੇ ਦਸ ਵਜੇ ਆਉਂਦੀ ਹੈ। ḍāk savere das vaje āundī hai. The post comes at ten in the morning.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ਡਾਕ (ḍāk) goes back to a reconstructed ḍakka, "relay, post"; CDIAL gives no Punjabi form, but lists Hindi ḍāk, "relay, post" (CDIAL 5519).

What you've built.
One more word for reading notices and messages.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: ḍāk]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: ḍāk, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: ḍāk savere das vaje āundī hai. — The post comes at ten in the morning.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Punjabi for wrong, then the Punjabi for to rub out, then say ḍāk again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does ਡਾਕ (ḍāk) mean? ("The post; the mail".) Say it once more.


Lesson 2 of 5.
ਲਿਫ਼ਾਫ਼ਾ (lifāfā) — an envelope.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for to rub out, then the Punjabi for the post.

You'll want to know: ਲਿਫ਼ਾਫ਼ਾ.
ਲਿਫ਼ਾਫ਼ਾ — lifāfā — "an envelope".
ਚਿੱਠੀ ਲਿਫ਼ਾਫ਼ੇ ਵਿੱਚ ਰੱਖੋ। chiṭṭhī lifāfe vich rakkho. Put the letter in the envelope.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ਲਿਫ਼ਾਫ਼ਾ (lifāfā) is a loan, usually traced through Persian to Arabic lifāfa, "a wrapper".

Lesson 5 of 5.
ਛਾਪਣਾ (chhāpṇā) — to print.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for an office stamp, then the Punjabi for paper.

You'll want to know: ਛਾਪਣਾ.
ਛਾਪਣਾ — chhāpṇā — "to print".
ਦਫ਼ਤਰ ਹਰ ਹਫ਼ਤੇ ਫ਼ਾਰਮ ਛਾਪਦਾ ਹੈ। daftar har hafte fāram chhāpdā hai. The office prints forms every week.
Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਫ਼ਾਰਮ ਛਾਪਿਆ, maiṁ fāram chhāpiā.

The word, taken apart.
ਛਾਪਣਾ (chhāpṇā) goes back to a reconstructed chapp, "press, cover, hide"; CDIAL lists Punjabi chāpṇā, "to print", under it, as a word taken from Hindi chāpnā (CDIAL 4994).
