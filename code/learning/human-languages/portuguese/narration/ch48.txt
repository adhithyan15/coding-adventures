Portuguese, chapter 48: House, Kitchen, Window, Table, Chair.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
a casa — a house, home.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for dinner, then the Portuguese for a lemon.

You'll want to know: a casa.
a casa — "a house, home". em casa: at home.

The word, taken apart.
casa from Latin casa, "hut, cottage" (EtymDB 2.1) — inherited.
