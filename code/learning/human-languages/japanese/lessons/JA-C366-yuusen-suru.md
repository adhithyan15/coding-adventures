## You'll want to know: ゆうせんする
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C366-SAIAKU366-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ゆうせんする** — *yūsen suru* — "to put first; to give priority to".

> **しけんが あったら、べんきょうを ゆうせんします。** — *shiken ga attara, benkyō o yūsen shimasu* — "If there is an exam, I put studying first."

Polite **ゆうせんします**, *yūsenshimasu*; a する verb. Past (polite): **ははは かぞくを ゆうせんしました。**, *haha wa kazoku o yūsen shimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ゆうせんする** (*yūsen suru*) is built from a Sino-Japanese noun and the native verb *suru*, "to do". The noun, *yūsen*, is two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese: *yū*, "excel" (Middle Chinese *'juw*) and *sen*, "ahead" (*sen*) (KANJIDIC2; JMdict; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
