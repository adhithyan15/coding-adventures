# Start with three forms you know

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-LEX-MERA-02, PA-PHRASE-YOUR-NAME-02, PA-LEX-C107-DEF107-03] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say **ਮੇਰਾ**, **ਤੁਹਾਡਾ**, then **ਆਪਣਾ**.

## Grammar Lens: the ending will follow the owned noun
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-LEX-MERA-02, PA-PHRASE-YOUR-NAME-02, PA-LEX-C107-DEF107-03] -->

These familiar forms all end in *-aa*. Keep their meanings steady: my,
respectful your, and one's own. The next steps will change one ending at a time
to agree with a familiar noun.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-LEX-MERA-02, PA-PHRASE-YOUR-NAME-02, PA-LEX-C107-DEF107-03] -->

- [YOU HEAR: *meraa*; YOU SAY: **ਮੇਰਾ**]
- [YOU HEAR: *tuhaadaa*; YOU SAY: **ਤੁਹਾਡਾ**]
- [YOU HEAR: *aapnaa*; YOU SAY: **ਆਪਣਾ**]
- [YOU READ: **ਮੇਰਾ, ਤੁਹਾਡਾ, ਆਪਣਾ**]

## Wrap-up Recall

[PAUSE 3s] Which form means my? Respectful your? One's own?
