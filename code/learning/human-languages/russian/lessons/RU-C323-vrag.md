## You'll want to know: враг
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C323-VOYNA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**враг** (*vrag*) — "an enemy".

> Враг подошёл к городу.
>
> *Vrag podoshyól k górodu.*
>
> The enemy approached the town.

Genitive *врага*. The opposite of *друг*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*враг* is a Church Slavonic word, *vragŭ* "foe" (EtymDB), from Proto-Slavic *vorgŭ* "enemy"; the native Russian shape is the old *ворог*.
