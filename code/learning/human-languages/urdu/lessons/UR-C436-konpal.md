## You'll want to know: کونپل
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C436-QUDRAT436-03]; assesses=[] -->

**کونپل** — *koṅpal* — "a new shoot; an opening bud".

> بہار میں درختوں پر کونپلیں آتی ہیں۔
>
> *bahār meṅ darakhtoṅ par koṅpaleṅ ātī haiṅ.*
>
> In spring new shoots appear on the trees.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**کونپل** (*koṅpal*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *kõpal*, "an opening bud", under Sanskrit *kuḍmalá*, "a bud" (CDIAL 3250).
