## You'll want to know: लुभाना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C339-RAJKUMAR339-05]; assesses=[] -->

**लुभाना** — *lubhānā* — "to lure; to tempt".

> डायन ने मिठाई से बच्चों को लुभाया।
>
> *ḍāyan ne miṭhāī se baccoṁ ko lubhāyā.*
>
> The witch lured the children with sweets.

Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने बच्चे को लुभाया, *usne bacce ko lubhāyā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**लुभाना** (*lubhānā*) goes back to Sanskrit *lubhyati*, "is eager, longs for" (CDIAL 11086): to make someone want something so much that they follow.
