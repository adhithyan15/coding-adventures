## You'll want to know: てきとう
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C440-CAREFUL440-05]; assesses=[] -->

**てきとう** — *tekitō* — "suitable; offhand".

> **この ばしょは てきとうです。** — *kono basho wa tekitō desu* — "This place is suitable."

A na-adjective: な before a noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**てきとう** (*tekitō*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *teki*, "suitable" (Middle Chinese *syek*) and *tō*, "appropriate" (*tangH*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
