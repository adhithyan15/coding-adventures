## You'll want to know: दसवाँ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C76-ORDINAL-05]; assesses=[] -->

**दसवाँ** (*dasvā̃*) — "**tenth**".

**दसवाँ दिन** (*dasvā̃ din*), **दसवीं किताब** (*dasvī̃ kitāb*).

That is the ten. Say them in order — *pahlā, dūsrā, tīsrā, cauthā, pā̃cvā̃,
chaṭhā, sātvā̃, āṭhvā̃, nauvā̃, dasvā̃* — and count what you actually had to learn:
**five words and one ending**.

And ten is not a boundary. **ग्यारहवाँ** is eleventh, **बीसवाँ** twentieth,
**सौवाँ** hundredth. There is no second list above ten and no third above a
hundred; the ending you learned at five reaches every number you will ever say.
