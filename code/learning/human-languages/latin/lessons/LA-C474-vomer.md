## You'll want to know: vōmer, vōmeris
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C474-ARO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**vōmer, vōmeris** — "a ploughshare".

> Vōmer ex ferrō est et terram dūram frangit.
>
> *The ploughshare is made of iron and breaks the hard ground.*

Third declension, masculine: *vōmer, vōmeris m.* (also *vōmis*). The iron blade of the plough.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

The origin of *vōmer* is not settled: Lewis & Short give the sense but no source, and EtymDB lists it without a parent. English keeps *vomer* for a bone of the nose.
