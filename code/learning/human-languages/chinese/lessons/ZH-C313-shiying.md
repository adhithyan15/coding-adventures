## You'll want to know: shìyìng
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C313-MOVE313-05]; assesses=[] -->

*shìyìng* — "to adapt; to adjust to new surroundings".

> *wǒ hái bú shìyìng zhèr de shēnghuó* — I'm not used to life here yet.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*shìyìng* is built from *shì* 'to go to' and 应 'to answer', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *syek* and *'ingH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to go to' and 'pleased' and the second 'to respond'.
