Italian, chapter 318: Gli invitati.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
l'invito — the invitation.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for the corkscrew, then the Italian for the banquet.

You'll want to know: l'invito.
l'invito — "the invitation".
Grazie per l'invito! Thank you for the invitation!
Built on invitare.

The word, taken apart.
invito is built on invitare "to invite", from Latin invītāre (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 4 of 5.
il rinfresco — the refreshments, the drinks party.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for the guest, then the Italian for the reception.

You'll want to know: il rinfresco.
il rinfresco — "the refreshments, the drinks party".
Dopo il concerto c'è un piccolo rinfresco. After the concert there are some refreshments.
Built on fresco, ‘fresh’.

The word, taken apart.
rinfresco is built on rinfrescare "to refresh", from fresco "fresh", which EtymDB traces through Medieval Latin friscus to a Germanic word.

Lesson 5 of 5.
il confetto — the sugared almond.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for the reception, then the Italian for the refreshments.

You'll want to know: il confetto.
il confetto — "the sugared almond".
A ogni invitato diamo cinque confetti. We give each guest five sugared almonds.
White for weddings, pink or blue for a birth. In Italian confetti are sweets, not paper.

The word, taken apart.
confetto is inherited from Latin cōnfectus "made, prepared" (EtymDB), from cōnficere.
