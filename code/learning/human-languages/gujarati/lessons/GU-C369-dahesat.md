# દહેશત (daheśat) — terror

## You'll want to know: દહેશત
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C369-BHAY369-02]; assesses=[] -->

**દહેશત** — *daheśat* — "terror".

> જો વાઘ જંગલમાંથી બહાર આવશે તો ગામમાં દહેશત ફેલાશે.
>
> *jo vāgh jangalmā̃thī bahār āvśe to gāmmā̃ daheśat phelāśe.*
>
> If the tiger comes out of the forest, terror will spread through the village.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**દહેશત** (*daheśat*) is usually traced to Persian *dahshat*, "terror, amazement", a word of Arabic origin.
