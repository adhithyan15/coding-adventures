## You'll want to know: かぞく
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-ANCHOR-KAZOKU]; assesses=[JA-SCRIPT-KA-01, JA-SCRIPT-KU-01] -->

**かぞく** — *kazoku* — "a family", the people you live with or grew up
with. Said of your own, it means *my family*.

Three beats: *ka–zo–ku*. **か** and **く** you already write. The middle sign
is **そ** with the two short strokes of the voicing mark at its upper right,
so *so* becomes *zo*. The next lesson writes it.
