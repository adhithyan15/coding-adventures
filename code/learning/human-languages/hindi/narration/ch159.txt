Hindi, chapter 159: Full, To sleep, To wake up, To run, To put.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
भरा (bharā) — full.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for low, then the Hindi for empty.

You'll want to know: भरा.
भरा — bharā — "full".

The word, taken apart.
भरा (bharā) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from bharati, "bears, fills" (CDIAL 9397). bharā, "full", is the past form of bharnā, "to fill".
