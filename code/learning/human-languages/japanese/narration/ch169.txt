Japanese, chapter 169: Sometimes, Rarely and Every Month.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
ときどき (tokidoki) — sometimes.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for to put off, then the Japanese for to be put off.

You'll want to know: ときどき.
ときどき — tokidoki — "sometimes".
らいねんは ときどき うみに いきます。 — rainen wa tokidoki umi ni ikimasu — "Next year I will sometimes go to the sea."

The word, taken apart.
ときどき (tokidoki) is native Japanese, built from toki, "time", said twice with the second one voiced to doki: time and again. It is inherited rather than borrowed (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).

The word, taken apart.
たまに (tamani) is a native Japanese word, inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, and its older origin is not settled. It is usually written in kana (JMdict).

Lesson 3 of 6.
めったに (mettani) — rarely (with a negative verb).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for sometimes, then the Japanese for once in a while.

You'll want to know: めったに.
めったに — mettani — "rarely (with a negative verb)".
ちちは めったに やすみません。 — chichi wa mettani yasumimasen — "My father rarely takes a day off."

The word, taken apart.
めったに (mettani) is built from metta and the native particle ni. Metta is written with two characters read with their on'yomi, metsu, "destroy" (Middle Chinese mjiet), and ta, "many" (ta); whether it was borrowed from Chinese or the characters were chosen for their sound is not settled (JMdict, KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).

Lesson 4 of 6.
まいつき (maitsuki) — every month.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for once in a while, then the Japanese for rarely.

You'll want to know: まいつき.
まいつき — maitsuki — "every month".
らいねんは まいつき ははに あいます。 — rainen wa maitsuki haha ni aimasu — "Next year I will see my mother every month."

The word, taken apart.
まいつき (maitsuki) is a mixed word, built from Sino-Japanese mai, "every", borrowed from Chinese (Middle Chinese mwojX), and native tsuki, "moon, month" (JMdict, KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).

Lesson 5 of 6.
くりかえす (kurikaesu) — to repeat; to do again.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for rarely, then the Japanese for every month.

You'll want to know: くりかえす.
くりかえす — kurikaesu — "to repeat; to do again".
らいしゅう もういちど くりかえします。 — raishū mō ichido kurikaeshimasu — "Next week I will do it over again."
Polite くりかえします, kurikaeshimasu; a godan verb.

The word, taken apart.
くりかえす (kurikaesu) is native Japanese, built from kuri, "reeling, winding", and kaesu, "to send back", both inherited rather than borrowed (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
