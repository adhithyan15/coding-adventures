# கீழே (kīḻē) — down, below

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[TA-LEX-C66-WHICHWAY-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say the word for "up", and the instruction you built with it.

## You'll want to know: கீழே
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C66-WHICHWAY-02]; assesses=[TA-LEX-C66-WHICHWAY-01] -->

**கீழே** (*kīḻē*) — "down, below". The other half of the pair, and built by the
same two pieces: **கீழ்** (*kīḻ*, "the bottom") plus the **-ஏ** you met one
lesson ago.

**கீழே பார்** (*kīḻē pār*) — "look down". Same slot, same verb, opposite
direction.

## Sounds you'll need
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[TA-LEX-C66-WHICHWAY-02] -->

The middle of this word is **ழ** (*ḻ*), the retroflex glide you first met inside
**மகிழ்ச்சி** and again in **தமிழ்** itself. Curl the tongue back and let the
air pass beside it; it is not an English *l* and it is not an English *r*.

The vowel in front of it is **long**: *kīḻ*, not *kiḻ*. Tamil separates words by
vowel length, so the length is doing work here, not decoration.
