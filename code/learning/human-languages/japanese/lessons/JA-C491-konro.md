## You'll want to know: こんろ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C491-STOVE491-03]; assesses=[] -->

**こんろ** — *konro* — "a cooking stove".

> **こんろで ゆを わかします。** — *konro de yu o wakashimasu* — "I boil water on the stove."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**こんろ** (*konro*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *kon*, "shine" and *ro*, "hearth" (Middle Chinese *lu*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
