Spanish, chapter 751: Mis aficiones.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
la afición — the hobby; liking; fans.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for the colleague, then the Spanish for support.

You'll want to know: la afición.
la afición — "the hobby; liking; fans".
Mi afición es la fotografía. My hobby is photography.
Tener afición a — to be keen on. La afición is also a team's fans.

The word, taken apart.
afición comes from Latin affectiō "disposition, affection" (EtymDB).

Lesson 2 of 6.
el pasatiempo — the pastime.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for support, then the Spanish for the hobby.

You'll want to know: el pasatiempo.
el pasatiempo — "the pastime".
Leer es mi pasatiempo favorito. Reading is my favourite pastime.
Plural los pasatiempos — also puzzles in a newspaper.

The word, taken apart.
pasatiempo is built on pasar "to pass" and tiempo "time". Pasar is usually traced to a spoken Latin verb built on passus "step", and tiempo comes from Latin tempus "time" (EtymDB).
