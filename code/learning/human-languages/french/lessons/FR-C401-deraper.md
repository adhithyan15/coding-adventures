## You'll want to know: déraper
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C401-CONDUITE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**déraper** — "to skid".

> Avec la pluie, la voiture a dérapé dans la descente.
>
> *Because of the rain the car skidded on the way down.*

Also figurative: *la conversation a dérapé* — the talk got out of hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*déraper* is usually traced to Provençal *derapar*, first said of an anchor pulled loose from the sea bed, built on a Germanic verb for seizing. EtymDB links it with Germanic cousins such as Old Norse *hreppa* 'to grasp' (EtymDB).
