# ఒక్క నిమిషం (okka nimiṣaṁ) — one minute, a moment

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[TE-LEX-C86-REPAIR-02, TE-LEX-C86-REPAIR-03] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Telugu for once more, then the Telugu for loudly.

## You'll want to know: ఒక్క నిమిషం
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C86-REPAIR-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ఒక్క నిమిషం** (*okka nimiṣaṁ*) — "one minute", said the way English says "a moment".

**నిమిషం** is "a minute", and **ఒక్క** is "a single one", a close relative of **ఒకటి**, the "one"
you count with.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

A way to buy time while you find the word.
