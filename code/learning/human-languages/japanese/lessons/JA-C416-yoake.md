## You'll want to know: よあけ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C416-YOAKE416-01]; assesses=[] -->

**よあけ** — *yoake* — "dawn; daybreak".

> **よあけに やどを でました。** — *yoake ni yado o demashita* — "We left the inn at dawn."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**よあけ** (*yoake*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, built from *yo*, "night" and *ake*, "opening" (from *akeru*); its kanji were attached later as kun'yomi (JMdict).
