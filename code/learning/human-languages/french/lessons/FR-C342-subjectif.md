## You'll want to know: subjectif
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C342-JUGEMENT-04]; assesses=[] -->

**subjectif** — "subjective".

> C'est un avis très subjectif.
>
> *It is a very subjective view.*

Feminine *subjective*: *une impression subjective*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*subjectif* comes from Latin *subiectīvus* (EtymDB), formed on *subiectus*, the participle of *subicere* "to throw under, to place under" (Lewis & Short), built on *iacere* "to throw".
