## You'll want to know: de hecho
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C589-MATIZA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**de hecho** — "in fact".

> De hecho, ya lo sabía.
>
> *In fact, I already knew it.*

It adds a stronger point or a correction.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*de hecho* is built on *hecho*, inherited from Latin *factus* 'done, made', the participle of *facere* (EtymDB).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.
