6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
आंधी (ā̃dhī) — a dust storm.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for to thunder, then the Marwadi for a spell of steady rain.

You'll want to know: आंधी.
आंधी — ā̃dhī — "a dust storm".
जेठ मांय आंधी आवै है। jeṭh mā̃y ā̃dhī āvai hai. Dust storms come in early summer.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
आंधी (ā̃dhī) is inherited from Sanskrit andhikā, "night", perhaps for the darkness a dust storm brings; CDIAL lists Hindi and Gujarati ā̃dhī, "a dust storm". CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry (CDIAL 390).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for a spell of steady rain, then the Marwadi for a dust storm.

You'll want to know: भीजणो.
भीजणो — bhījṇo — "to get wet".
हूं काल बरसात मांय भीजसूं। hū̃ kāl barsāt mā̃y bhījsū̃. Tomorrow I'll get wet in the rain.
Past (he …): वो बरसात मांय भीज्यो, vo barsāt mā̃y bhījyo.

The word, taken apart.
भीजणो (bhījṇo) goes back to a reconstructed bhiyajyate, "is smeared"; CDIAL lists Hindi bhījnā and Gujarati bhijvũ, "to be wet", under it. CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry (CDIAL 9502).

Lesson 5 of 6.
धूंवर (dhū̃var) — fog; mist.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for dew, then the Marwadi for to shiver.

You'll want to know: धूंवर.
धूंवर — dhū̃var — "fog; mist".
आज दिनूगै धूंवर है। āj dinūgai dhū̃var hai. There's fog this morning.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
धूंवर (dhū̃var) is inherited from Sanskrit dhūmarī, "mist, fog", which CDIAL sets beside a reconstructed dhūmara, "smoky", from dhūma, "smoke" (CDIAL 6852); CDIAL lists Gujarati dhũvar, "fog". CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry (CDIAL 6853).
