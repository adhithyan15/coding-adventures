Bengali, chapter 422: Weighing and Testing.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
দাঁড়িপাল্লা (dā̃ṛipāllā) — a pair of scales; a balance.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for wholesale, then the Bengali for to stock up.

You'll want to know: দাঁড়িপাল্লা.
দাঁড়িপাল্লা — dā̃ṛipāllā — "a pair of scales; a balance".
মুদি দাঁড়িপাল্লায় ডাল ওজন করলেন। mudi dā̃ṛipāllāy ḍāl ojôn kôrlen. The grocer weighed the lentils on the scales.
The hanging scales of a market stall: a beam, the দাঁড়ি, with two pans, the পাল্লা.

The word, taken apart.
দাঁড়িপাল্লা (dā̃ṛipāllā) joins দাঁড়ি, which CDIAL lists as Bengali ḍā̃ṛi, "the beam of a balance", under Sanskrit daṇḍá, "a stick" (CDIAL 6128), to পাল্লা, "a scale-pan", whose own origin is not settled.

You'll want to know: খাঁটি.
খাঁটি — khā̃ṭi — "pure; genuine; unmixed".
এই দোকানের মধু খাঁটি। ei dokāner môdhu khā̃ṭi. The honey in this shop is pure.
Of food, unmixed with anything cheaper; of a person, honest through and through.

The word, taken apart.
খাঁটি (khā̃ṭi) is the everyday word for pure, unmixed goods; its origin is not settled.

Lesson 4 of 5.
গ্রাহক (grāhôk) — a customer; a subscriber.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for pure (unmixed), then the Bengali for a shopping list.

You'll want to know: গ্রাহক.
গ্রাহক — grāhôk — "a customer; a subscriber".
দোকানে নতুন গ্রাহকদের জন্য ছাড় আছে। dokāne nôtun grāhôkder jônyô chhāṛ āchhe. The shop has a discount for new customers.
খদ্দের is the everyday word; গ্রাহক (grāhôk) is what banks, shops and the post office say.

The word, taken apart.
গ্রাহক (grāhôk) is Sanskrit grāhaka, "one who receives, a purchaser" (Monier-Williams), from grah, to take.

Lesson 5 of 5.
পরখ করা (pôrôkh kôrā) — to test; to try out.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a shopping list, then the Bengali for a customer (formal).

You'll want to know: পরখ করা.
পরখ করা — pôrôkh kôrā — "to test; to try out".
কেনার আগে আমি আমটা টিপে পরখ করলাম। kenār āge āmi āmṭā ṭipe pôrôkh kôrlām. Before buying, I pressed the mango to test it.
Past (he/she …): তিনি জিনিসটা পরখ করলেন, tini jinisṭā pôrôkh kôrlen.

The word, taken apart.
পরখ করা (pôrôkh kôrā) joins পরখ, which CDIAL lists as Bengali parakh, "an examination", under Sanskrit párīkṣatē, "observes, examines" (CDIAL 7904), to করা, to do; পরীক্ষা, an exam, is the same word taken whole.
