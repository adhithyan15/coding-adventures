## You'll want to know: der Studiengang
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C377-HOCHSCHULE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**der Studiengang** — "the degree programme, the programme of study".

> Mein Studiengang dauert sechs Semester.
>
> *My degree programme lasts six semesters.*

Plural: *die Studiengänge*. *Einen Studiengang wählen* — to choose a course.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Studiengang* joins *Studium* "study", from Latin *studium* "zeal, study" (Lewis & Short), to *Gang* "course, walk", Old High German *gang* (EtymDB): the path your studies follow.
