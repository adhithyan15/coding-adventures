## You'll want to know: saupoudrer
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C332-ASSAISONNER-03]; assesses=[] -->

**saupoudrer** — "to sprinkle, to dust".

> Il saupoudre le gâteau de sucre.
>
> *He dusts the cake with sugar.*

*Saupoudrer de* — to dust with.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*saupoudrer* is built from *sel* "salt" and *poudrer* "to powder" (EtymDB); *poudre* goes back to Latin *pulvis* "dust".
