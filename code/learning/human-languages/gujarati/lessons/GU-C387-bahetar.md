## You'll want to know: બહેતર
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C387-BAHETAR387-01]; assesses=[] -->

**બહેતર** — *bahetar* — "better".

> જો તમે વહેલા સૂશો તો તમારી તબિયત બહેતર થશે.
>
> *jo tame vahelā sūśo to tamārī tabiyat bahetar thaśe.*
>
> If you go to bed early, your health will improve.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**બહેતર** (*bahetar*) is usually traced to Persian *behtar*, "better".
