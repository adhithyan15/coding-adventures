## You'll want to know: वर्गणी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C430-MOBADLA430-03]; assesses=[] -->

**वर्गणी** — *vargaṇī* — "a subscription; a contribution".

> उत्सवासाठी सगळ्यांनी वर्गणी दिली.
>
> *utsavāsāṭhī sagaḷyānnī vargaṇī dilī.*
>
> Everyone gave a contribution for the festival.

Feminine.

A share each member pays: मासिक वर्गणी, a monthly subscription.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**वर्गणी** (*vargaṇī*) is a Marathi word for a share paid into a common fund; its origin is not settled.
