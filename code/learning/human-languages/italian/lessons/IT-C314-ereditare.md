## You'll want to know: ereditare
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C314-EREDITA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ereditare** — "to inherit".

> Ho ereditato la casa di mia zia.
>
> *I inherited my aunt's house.*

Also for looks: *ha ereditato gli occhi del padre*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ereditare* is inherited from Late Latin *hērēditāre* (EtymDB), built on *hērēs* "heir".

## What you've built
