## You'll want to know: divulgar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C280-PUBLICAR-03]; assesses=[] -->

**divulgar** — "to make public, to spread (news)".

> A empresa divulgou os resultados ontem.
>
> *The company made the results public yesterday.*

Formal; frequent on the news.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*divulgar* is a learned borrowing from Latin *dīvulgāre* "to spread among the people" (EtymDB), built on *vulgus* "the common people".
