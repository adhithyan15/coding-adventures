## You'll want to know: ધકેલવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C209-BREAKDOWN209-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ધકેલવું** — *dhakelvũ* — "to push".

> **અમે ગાડી ધકેલી.** — *ame gāḍī dhakelī* — "We pushed the car."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ધકેલવું** (*dhakelvũ*) is a tadbhava. CDIAL lists it, glossed "to push", under *\*dhakk*, "push, strike", a reconstructed form (CDIAL 6701).
