## You'll want to know: l'antifurto
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C293-SCASSO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**l'antifurto** — "the burglar alarm, the anti-theft device".

> L'antifurto della macchina ha suonato tutta la notte.
>
> *The car alarm went off all night.*

Also an adjective: *un sistema antifurto*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*antifurto* joins Greek *anti-* "against" with *furto* "theft", which is inherited from Latin *fūrtum* (Lewis & Short).
