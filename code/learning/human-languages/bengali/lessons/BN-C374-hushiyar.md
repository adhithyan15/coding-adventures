## You'll want to know: হুঁশিয়ার
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C374-PROTIRODH374-01]; assesses=[] -->

**হুঁশিয়ার** — *hũshiyār* — "watchful; on one's guard".

> রাতে বাইরে গেলে হুঁশিয়ার থেকো।
>
> *rāte bāire gele hũshiyār theko.*
>
> Be on your guard if you go out at night.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**হুঁশিয়ার** (*hũshiyār*) is usually traced to Persian *hushyār*, alert or wide awake. As a single word it is also a warning: হুঁশিয়ার! Watch out!
