Tamil, chapter 90: Laughter and Tears.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
சிரிப்பு (sirippu) — laughter.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for calm, then the Tamil for shivering.

You'll want to know: சிரிப்பு.
சிரிப்பு (sirippu) — "laughter", and "a smile".
சிரிப்பு (sirippu) வருகிறது — "I feel like laughing", literally "laughter is coming".

What you've built.
Laughter, which comes to you.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: sirippu]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: sirippu, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say sirippu varukiṟatu]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Tamil for calm, then the Tamil for shivering, then say sirippu again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does சிரிப்பு (sirippu) mean? ("Laughter".) Say it once more.

Lesson 5 of 5.
பசிக்கிறது (pasikkiṟatu) — (I) am hungry.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for tears, then the Tamil for alone.

You'll want to know: பசிக்கிறது.
பசிக்கிறது (pasikkiṟatu) — "I am hungry", literally "it hungers".
It is பசி, "hunger", made into a verb. எனக்குப் பசிக்கிறது (pasikkiṟatu) — "I'm hungry".

What you've built.
Laughter, crying, tears, alone, and hunger as a verb.
