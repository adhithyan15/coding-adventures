## You'll want to know: resultar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C350-ORIGEM-03]; assesses=[] -->

**resultar** — "to result; to work out".

> O acidente resultou de um erro do motorista.
>
> *The accident resulted from the driver's mistake.*

*Resultar de* — to result from; *resultar em* — to lead to. Also to work: *o plano resultou*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*resultar* comes from Medieval Latin *resultāre* (EtymDB), Latin *resultāre* "to spring or leap back, to rebound" (Lewis & Short), built on *saltāre* "to jump".
