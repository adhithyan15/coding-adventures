## You'll want to know: ಕೊಳಲು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C535-VADYA535-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ಕೊಳಲು** — *koḷalu* — "a flute".

> ದನ ಕಾಯುವ ಹುಡುಗ ಕೊಳಲು ನುಡಿಸುತ್ತಿದ್ದನು.
>
> *dana kāyuva huḍuga koḷalu nuḍisuttiddanu.*
>
> The boy minding the cattle was playing a flute.

Made of bamboo, ಬಿದಿರು.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಕೊಳಲು** (*koḷalu*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 1818 lists the Kannada word for a flute, a fife, beside a word for a tube, a blow-pipe; ಕೊಳವೆ, a pipe, is that second word.
