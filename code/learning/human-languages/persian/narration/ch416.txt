Persian, chapter 416: Accounts and passwords.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
کاربر (kârbar) — a user.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a percent, then the Persian for a spendthrift.

You'll want to know: کاربر.
کاربر (kârbar) — "a user".
این برنامه میلیونها کاربر دارد. in barnâme milyunhâ kârbar dârad. This app has millions of users.
Kâr "work" + bar "carrying". Nâm-e kârbari — a username.

The word, taken apart.
کاربر (kârbar) is a modern native coinage built from kâr "work" + bar "carrying", from bar-, the present stem of bordan "to carry". A user is one who puts a tool to work.

Lesson 2 of 5.
رمز عبور (ramz-e 'obur) — a password.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a spendthrift, then the Persian for a user.

You'll want to know: رمز عبور.
رمز عبور (ramz-e 'obur) — "a password".
رمز عبورم را فراموش کردم. ramz-e 'oburam râ farâmush kardam. I forgot my password.
Ramz "code" + 'obur "passing". Also gozarvâzhe.

The word, taken apart.
رمز عبور (ramz-e 'obur) is literally "the code for passing": ramz was borrowed from Arabic ramz "cypher, code" (EtymDB), and 'obur is usually traced to Arabic 'ubūr "crossing".

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a user, then the Persian for a password.

You'll want to know: ذخیره کردن.
ذخیره کردن (zakhire kardan) — "to save, to store".
عکسها را در کامپیوتر ذخیره کردم. 'akshâ râ dar kâmpyuter zakhire kardam. I saved the photos on the computer.
Zakhire "store, reserve" + kardan. Also to store food or water.

The word, taken apart.
ذخیره was borrowed from Arabic dhakhīra "store, reserve" (EtymDB); Persian says the Arabic dh as z.

You'll want to know: حذف کردن.
حذف کردن (hazf kardan) — "to delete, to remove".
او پیام را حذف کرد. u payâm râ hazf kard. She deleted the message.
Hazf "removal" + kardan. Also to drop a course or a player.

The word, taken apart.
حذف is usually traced to Arabic ḥadhf "cutting off, dropping"; Persian says the Arabic dh as z.

Lesson 5 of 5.
به روز رسانی (be ruz resâni) — an update.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to save, then the Persian for to delete.

You'll want to know: به روز رسانی.
به روز رسانی (be ruz resâni) — "an update".
کامپیوتر من یک به روز رسانی تازه گرفت. kâmpyuter-e man yek be ruz resâni-ye tâze gereft. My computer got a new update.
Be ruz "up to date" + resâni "bringing", from resândan. Be ruz resâni kardan — to update.

The word, taken apart.
به روز رسانی (be ruz resâni) is a modern native coinage, literally "bringing to the day": ruz "day" is traced by EtymDB through Old Persian, and resâni is built on resân-, the causative stem of residan "to arrive".
