## You'll want to know: el boxeo
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C758-DEPORTES-02]; assesses=[] -->

**el boxeo** — "boxing".

> No me gusta ver el boxeo.
>
> *I do not like watching boxing.*

The boxer is *el boxeador*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*boxeo* is built on *boxear*, which was borrowed from English *box* "to fight with the fists" (EtymDB).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.
