## You'll want to know: die Schlucht
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C453-GIPFEL-05]; assesses=[] -->

**die Schlucht** — "the gorge, the ravine".

> Unten in der tiefen Schlucht ist ein kleiner Fluss.
>
> *Down in the deep gorge there is a small river.*

Plural: *die Schluchten*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Schlucht* came into the standard language from Low German; it is usually linked to *schlucken* 'to swallow', and EtymDB lists Old Norse *slok* 'gully, channel' as a cousin.
