# थ — one breathed consonant

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MW-LEX-THARO-01, MW-LEX-HAAN-01, MW-SCRIPT-HAAN-01] -->

[PAUSE 10s] Read **हां**, say **yes**, then hear *thāro*, point away, and
say **your**.

## Script
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-SCRIPT-THA-01]; assesses=[] -->

> थ

Read **थ** as *tha*: the tongue begins near the teeth and a small breath follows.
It is one new shape. The remaining signs in *thāro* are already known.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MW-SCRIPT-THA-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: observe-trace -->

Keep **थ** visible. Finger-trace it once, then trace one printed model once.
Say *tha* after your hand stops.
