## You'll want to know: explotar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C529-BOMBA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**explotar** — "to explode; to exploit".

> La bombona de gas explotó.
>
> *The gas canister exploded.*

It also means to exploit: *explotar a los trabajadores*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*explotar* was borrowed from French *exploiter* 'to make use of' (EtymDB). The sense 'to explode' is usually explained as a later Spanish growth, pulled along by *explosión*.
