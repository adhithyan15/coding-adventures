## You'll want to know: دنده عوض کردن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C420-FARMAN-04]; assesses=[] -->

**دنده عوض کردن** (*dande 'avaz kardan*) — "to change gear".

> او قبل از پیچ دنده عوض میکند.
>
> *U qabl az pich dande 'avaz mikonad.*
>
> He changes gear before the bend.

*dande* 'gear' + *'avaz* 'a change, an exchange' + *kardan*. *'Avaz kardan* on its own means to change or swap.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*dande 'avaz kardan* is built from native *dande* 'gear', *'avaz* 'exchange', a loan usually traced to Arabic *ʿiwaḍ* 'a substitute', and *kardan* 'to do', inherited from Middle Persian *kardan* (EtymDB).
