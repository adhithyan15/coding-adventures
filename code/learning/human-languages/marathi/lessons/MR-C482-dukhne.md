## You'll want to know: दुखणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C482-DUKH482-02]; assesses=[] -->

**दुखणे** — *dukhṇe* — "to ache; to hurt".

> जास्त वेळ बसल्यामुळे माझी पाठ दुखते.
>
> *jāsta veḷ baslyāmuḷe mājhī pāṭh dukhte.*
>
> My back aches from sitting too long.

Past (to him …): त्याला दुखलं, *tyālā dukhlaṁ*.

The aching part is the subject: माझं डोकं दुखतं. With the dative and no part named: मला खूप दुखलं, it hurt me a lot. दुखणं, an ailment.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**दुखणे** (*dukhṇe*) is a tadbhava, inherited from Sanskrit *duḥkhati*, "pains"; CDIAL lists Marathi *dukhṇẽ* (CDIAL 6376).

## What you've built

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
