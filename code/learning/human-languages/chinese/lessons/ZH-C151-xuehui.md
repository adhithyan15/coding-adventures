## You'll want to know: 学会
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C151-XUEHUI-01]; assesses=[] -->

**学会** — *xuéhuì* — "to learn (and now be able to)".

> **我学会开车了** *wǒ xuéhuì kāichē le* — I've learned to drive.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**学会** (*xuéhuì*) is built from 学 'to study' and 会 'can'. Both are inherited from Middle Chinese, *haewk* and *hwajH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'to awaken to; to learn' and 'to come together'. 会 as 'can' is a later sense than that gloss, assumed here, not quoted.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ZH-GRAMMAR-RESULT-COMPLEMENT-01, ZH-LEX-C90-HUI-01, ZH-GRAMMAR-LE-CHANGED-STATE-01] -->

学 is the studying; 会 is the result: now you can. With 了 at the end it is news: 我学会开车了.

Without the result, **我学开车** *wǒ xué kāichē* says only that you are studying it.
