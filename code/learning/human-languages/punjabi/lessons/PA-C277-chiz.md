## You'll want to know: ਚੀਜ਼
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C277-CHOOSE277-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ਚੀਜ਼** — *chīz* — "a thing; an item".

> ਇਹ ਚੀਜ਼ ਬਹੁਤ ਮਹਿੰਗੀ ਹੈ।
>
> *eh chīz bahut mahiṅgī hai.*
>
> This item is very expensive.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਚੀਜ਼** (*chīz*) is a loan, usually traced to Persian *chīz*, "a thing".
