## You'll want to know: वही
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C113-NOTHING-04]; assesses=[] -->

**वही** (*vahī*) — "that very one", "the same".

It is **वह**, "that", with **ही**, "exactly". When someone names what you meant: **हाँ, वही!**

## What you've built
