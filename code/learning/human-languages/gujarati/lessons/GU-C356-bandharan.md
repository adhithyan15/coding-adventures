## You'll want to know: બંધારણ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C356-BANDHARAN356-01]; assesses=[] -->

**બંધારણ** — *bandhāraṇ* — "a constitution".

> આપણું બંધારણ દરેક નાગરિકને સમાન હક આપે છે.
>
> *āpṇũ bandhāraṇ darek nāgrikne samān hak āpe che.*
>
> Our constitution gives every citizen equal rights.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**બંધારણ** (*bandhāraṇ*) CDIAL lists Gujarati *bandhāraṇ*, "arrangement, construction", under Sanskrit *bandhakaraṇa*, "binding" (CDIAL 9138); "a constitution" is its modern sense, the frame a state is built on.
