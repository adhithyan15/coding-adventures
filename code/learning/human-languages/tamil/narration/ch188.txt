Tamil, chapter 188: Thorn, Lotus, Jasmine, Neem tree, Coconut palm.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
முள் (muḷ) — a thorn.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for a wolf, then the Tamil for a leopard.

You'll want to know: முள்.
முள் — muḷ — "a thorn".

The word, taken apart.
முள் (muḷ) is native Dravidian. DEDR 4995 lists Malayalam muḷ, Kannada muḷ and Telugu mullu.
