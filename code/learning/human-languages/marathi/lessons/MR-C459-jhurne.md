## You'll want to know: झुरणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C459-ODH459-05]; assesses=[] -->

**झुरणे** — *jhurṇe* — "to pine".

> दूर गेलेल्या मुलासाठी आई झुरत होती.
>
> *dūr gelelyā mulāsāṭhī āī jhurat hotī.*
>
> The mother was pining for her son who had gone far away.

Past (he …): तो झुरला, *to jhurlā*.

Slow and wearing: एखाद्यासाठी झुरणे, to pine for someone.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**झुरणे** (*jhurṇe*) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi *jhurṇẽ*, "to waste away", under the reconstructed *\*jhurati* (CDIAL 5405).
