## You'll want to know: ஊகி
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C349-CINTI349-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ஊகி** — *ūki* — "to guess; to infer".

> அவன் பதிலைச் சரியாக ஊகித்தான்.
>
> *avaṉ patilaic cariyāka ūkittāṉ.*
>
> He guessed the answer correctly.

Past (I …): ஊகித்தேன், *ūkittēṉ*.

What is guessed takes -ஐ: பதிலை ஊகி, to guess the answer.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ஊகி** (*ūki*) is a verb built on Sanskrit *ūha*, "consideration", Prakrit "reasoning, guess" (CDIAL 2443), with the loan-verb ending -இ.
