## You'll want to know: آمدنی
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C420-AAMDANI420-01]; assesses=[] -->

**آمدنی** — *āmdanī* — "an income".

> اس کی آمدنی کم ہے۔
>
> *us kī āmdanī kam hai.*
>
> He doesn't earn much.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**آمدنی** (*āmdanī*) is usually traced to Persian *āmadanī*, "what comes in", from the verb for "to come".
