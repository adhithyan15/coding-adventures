Bengali, chapter 384: Rails and Carriages.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
রেল (rel) — a railway; the railways.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a female singer, then the Bengali for a female dancer.

You'll want to know: রেল.
রেল — rel — "a railway; the railways".
আমার বাবা রেলে কাজ করতেন। āmār bābā rele kāj kôrten. My father used to work on the railways.
রেলে, on the railways; রেলের, of the railway: রেলের টিকিট, a railway ticket.

The word, taken apart.
রেল (rel) is English rail, taken over in the age of steam for the line and then for the whole railway.

Lesson 2 of 5.
কামরা (kāmrā) — a compartment (of a train); a chamber.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a female dancer, then the Bengali for a railway.

You'll want to know: কামরা.
কামরা — kāmrā — "a compartment (of a train); a chamber".
ট্রেনের এই কামরাটা খালি। ṭrener ei kāmrāṭā khāli. This compartment of the train is empty.
With the classifier: কামরাটা, the compartment; মহিলাদের কামরা (kāmrā), the ladies' compartment.

The word, taken apart.
কামরা (kāmrā) is usually traced to Portuguese câmara, "a room, a chamber", one of the words the Portuguese left along the Bengal coast; in Bengali it settled on the rooms of a train.

বার্থ — bārth — "a berth (on a train)".
রাতের ট্রেনে আমার বার্থটা ওপরে ছিল। rāter ṭrene āmār bārthṭā opôre chhilô. On the night train my berth was the upper one.

The word, taken apart.
বার্থ (bārth) is borrowed from English berth, a sleeping place on a ship or train; the English th is said with থ.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a compartment, then the Bengali for a berth.

You'll want to know: লোকাল.
লোকাল — lokāl — "a local train; a stopping train".
লোকাল ট্রেন সব স্টেশনে থামে। lokāl ṭren sôb sṭeshône thāme. The local train stops at every station.
On its own লোকাল (lokāl) is the train: লোকালে এলাম, I came on the local.

The word, taken apart.
লোকাল (lokāl) is borrowed from English local, short for local train, the slow suburban train that stops everywhere.

Lesson 5 of 5.
হুইসল দেওয়া (huisôl deoyā) — to blow a whistle (of a train or a guard).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a berth, then the Bengali for a local train.

You'll want to know: হুইসল দেওয়া.
হুইসল দেওয়া — huisôl deoyā — "to blow a whistle (of a train or a guard)".
ট্রেনটা হুইসল দিয়ে স্টেশন ছাড়ল। ṭrenṭā huisôl diye sṭeshôn chhāṛlô. The train blew its whistle and left the station.
Past (he/she …): সেই ট্রেন হুইসল দিল, sei ṭren huisôl dilô.
Used of the engine's whistle and of the guard's whistle on the platform.

The word, taken apart.
হুইসল দেওয়া (huisôl deoyā) joins whistle, borrowed from English, to দেওয়া, to give: you give a whistle in Bengali as you give a push.
