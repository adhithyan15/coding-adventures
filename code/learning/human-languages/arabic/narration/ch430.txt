Arabic, chapter 430: In the harbour.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
مرفأ (marfaʾ) — a harbour.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to sail, then the Arabic for to dock.

You'll want to know: مرفأ.
مرفأ — marfaʾ — "a harbour".
كانت قوارب الصيادين في المرفأ. kānat qawārib aṣ-ṣayyādīn fī al-marfaʾ. The fishermen's boats were in the harbour.

The word, taken apart.
مرفأ (marfaʾ) is usually traced to the native Arabic root r-f-ʾ, "to bring a ship close to the shore". The pattern mafʿal makes it a place.

Lesson 2 of 6.
مرسى (marsā) — an anchorage; a mooring.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to dock, then the Arabic for a harbour.

You'll want to know: مرسى.
مرسى — marsā — "an anchorage; a mooring".
وجد القبطان مرسى آمنا للقارب. wajada al-qubṭān marsā āminan li-l-qārib. The captain found a safe anchorage for the boat.

The word, taken apart.
مرسى (marsā) is built on the native Arabic root r-s-w, "to be firm, fixed" (Lane). It is the place-noun of rasā, "to anchor".

Lesson 3 of 6.
منارة (manāra) — a lighthouse; a minaret.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a harbour, then the Arabic for an anchorage.

You'll want to know: منارة.
منارة — manāra — "a lighthouse; a minaret".
رأينا ضوء المنارة من بعيد. raʾaynā ḍawʾ al-manāra min baʿīd. We saw the lighthouse's light from far away.

The word, taken apart.
منارة (manāra) is built on the native Arabic root n-w-r, "light" (Lane). It names a place of light; EtymDB lists Aramaic and Hebrew relatives for a lamp-stand.

Lesson 5 of 6.
جدف (jaddafa) — to row.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a lighthouse, then the Arabic for a dinghy.

You'll want to know: جدف.
جدف — jaddafa — "to row".
جدف أخي نحو الجزيرة. jaddafa akhī naḥwa al-jazīra. My brother rowed towards the island.
Present: يجدف, yujaddif.
An oar is mijdāf.

The word, taken apart.
جدف (jaddafa) is usually traced to the native Arabic root j-d-f, "to row". It is Form II.
