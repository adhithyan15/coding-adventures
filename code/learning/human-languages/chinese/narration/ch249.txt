Lesson 1 of 6.
yùxí — to prepare a lesson (before class).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for senior high school, then the Mandarin for a pupil's parent.

You'll want to know: yùxí.
yùxí — "to prepare a lesson (before class)".
míngtiān de kè nǐ yùxí le ma — Have you prepared tomorrow's lesson?
Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
yùxí is built from yù 'beforehand' and xí 'to practise', both inherited from Middle Chinese, yoH and zip (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first, among other senses, 'before', and the second 'to learn'.

Lesson 3 of 6.
读音 (dúyīn) — pronunciation; how a character is read.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for to prepare a lesson, then the Mandarin for to finish school for the day.

You'll want to know: 读音.
读音 — dúyīn — "pronunciation; how a character is read".
这个字的读音是什么 zhège zì de dúyīn shì shénme — How do you say this character?
The new character, in pieces: 读 (dú) — ten strokes: the speech piece 讠 on the left; on the right a short 十 over 买. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

The word, taken apart.
读音 (dúyīn) is built from 读 (dú) 'to read aloud' and 音 'a sound', both inherited from Middle Chinese, duwk and 'im (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to recite' and quotes an older dictionary for the second: 'sound'.

Script — the speech piece, then 十 over 买.
读 (dú).
Two pieces, left before right.
The left piece, 讠, two strokes, as in 说 and 请: a dot, then a short bar to the right that turns down and flicks up to the right, all one stroke.
The right piece, eight strokes, top to bottom. First a short 十: a bar and a vertical through it. Then 买, six strokes, as you wrote it: a bar that turns down at its right end and hooks, two short dots, then a long bar, a stroke falling to the left, and a short dot at the foot.
2 + 8 = ten.

Guided Practice.

[once you have stopped driving — cover: write it again from memory, and count the pieces, not the strokes]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
How many strokes in 读 (dú)? (Ten.) Which piece is on the left? (讠, the speech piece.) What sits under 十? (买.)
