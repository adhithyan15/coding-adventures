Spanish, chapter 597: Aprender y evaluar.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
el aprendizaje — the learning; the apprenticeship.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for the faculty, then the Spanish for the master's degree.

You'll want to know: el aprendizaje.
el aprendizaje — "the learning; the apprenticeship".
El aprendizaje de un idioma lleva tiempo. Learning a language takes time.
A noun for the process of learning.

The word, taken apart.
aprendizaje is built on aprendiz 'apprentice', from aprender, inherited from Latin apprehendere 'to seize, to grasp' (EtymDB).

What you've built.
One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.

You'll want to know: evaluar.
evaluar — "to assess, to evaluate".
El profesor evalúa el trabajo de todo el año. The teacher assesses the work of the whole year.
Written accent in evalúo, evalúas, evalúa.

The word, taken apart.
evaluar was borrowed from French évaluer, built on value 'worth', from Latin valere 'to be strong, to be worth' (EtymDB).
