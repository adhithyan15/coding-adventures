# रूसणो (rūsṇo) — to sulk; to take offence

## You'll want to know: रूसणो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C257-RIS257-02]; assesses=[] -->

**रूसणो** — *rūsṇo* — "to sulk; to take offence".

> छोटी बहन रोज रूसै है।
>
> *chhoṭī bahan roj rūsai hai.*
>
> My little sister sulks every day.

Past (he …): वो बेटो रूस्यो, *vo beṭo rūsyo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**रूसणो** (*rūsṇo*) is inherited from Sanskrit *ruṣyati*, "is angry"; CDIAL lists Old Marwari *rūsaï*, "to get angry", under it (CDIAL 10794).
