## You'll want to know: o nascimento
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C309-NASCIMENTO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**o nascimento** — "the birth".

> O nascimento da minha filha mudou a minha vida.
>
> *My daughter’s birth changed my life.*

*Data de nascimento* — date of birth.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*nascimento* is built on *nascer* "to be born", which goes back to Latin *nāscī* "to be born" (Lewis & Short), with the ending *-mento*.
