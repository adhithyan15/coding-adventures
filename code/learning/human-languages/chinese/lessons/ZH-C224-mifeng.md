## You'll want to know: mìfēng
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C224-BLOOM224-05]; assesses=[] -->

*mìfēng* — "bee; honeybee".

> *huāyuán lǐ de huā kāi le mìfēng yě lái le* — The flowers in the garden are out, and the bees have come too.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*mìfēng* is built from *mì* 'honey' and *fēng* 'bee', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *mjit* and *phjowng* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first as the food bees make and explains the second, after an older book, as a flying insect that stings.
