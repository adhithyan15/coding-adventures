## You'll want to know: تلوث
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C464-TALAWWUTH464-01]; assesses=[] -->

**تلوث** — *talawwuth* — "pollution".

> التلوث في المدينة مشكلة كبيرة.
>
> *at-talawwuth fī al-madīna mushkila kabīra.*
>
> Pollution in the city is a big problem.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**تلوث** (*talawwuth*) is built on the native Arabic root *l-w-th*, "to soil, to stain" (Lane). It is the verbal noun of Form V, "becoming soiled".
