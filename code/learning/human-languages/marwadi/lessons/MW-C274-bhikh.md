## You'll want to know: भीख
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C274-SAHARO274-05]; assesses=[] -->

**भीख** — *bhīkh* — "alms".

> वो भीख मांगै है।
>
> *vo bhīkh māṅgai hai.*
>
> He begs for alms.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**भीख** (*bhīkh*) is inherited from Sanskrit *bhikṣā*, "alms"; CDIAL lists Old Marwari *bhīkha*, "alms" (CDIAL 9485).
