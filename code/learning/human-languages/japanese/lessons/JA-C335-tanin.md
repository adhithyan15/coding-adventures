## You'll want to know: たにん

**たにん** — *tanin* — "other people; a stranger".

> **たにんの かさを つかわないで ください。** — *tanin no kasa o tsukawanaide kudasai* — "Please do not use other people's umbrellas."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**たにん** (*tanin*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *ta*, "other" (Middle Chinese *tha*) and *nin*, "person" (*nyin*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
