## You'll want to know: आदरातिथ्य
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C414-ATITHI414-02]; assesses=[] -->

**आदरातिथ्य** — *ādarātithya* — "hospitality".

> त्यांच्या आदरातिथ्याने आम्ही खूश झालो.
>
> *tyāñcyā ādarātithyāne āmhī khūś jhālo.*
>
> We were delighted by their hospitality.

Neuter.

आदर, respect, joined to आतिथ्य, guest care.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**आदरातिथ्य** (*ādarātithya*) is built from **आदर**, "respect", and Sanskrit *ātithya*, "proper for a guest, hospitality" (Monier-Williams).
