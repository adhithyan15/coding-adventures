## You'll want to know: ревновать
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C469-JEALOUSY-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ревновать** (*revnovát*) — "to be jealous".

> Она ревнует мужа к его коллегам.
>
> *Oná revnúyet múzha k yegó kollégam.*
>
> She gets jealous of her husband's colleagues.

Imperfective; the present is ревную, ревнуешь (-ова- becomes -у-). *Ревновать кого к кому* — to be jealous about someone because of someone. Perfective приревновать.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ревновать* is built with *-овать* on the stem it shares with *ревность* 'jealousy'; EtymDB lists the verb without a source and links the noun to Church Slavonic *rĭvenie* 'strife'.
