# قبول کرنا (qabūl karnā) — to own up to; to accept

## You'll want to know: قبول کرنا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C337-TASDIQ337-04]; assesses=[] -->

**قبول کرنا** — *qabūl karnā* — "to own up to; to accept".

> اس نے میرا مشورہ قبول نہیں کیا۔
>
> *us ne merā mashvarā qabūl nahīṅ kiyā.*
>
> He did not accept my advice.

Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے مشورہ قبول کیا, *us ne mashvarā qabūl kiyā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**قبول کرنا** (*qabūl karnā*) is built from *qabūl*, usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *qabūl*, "acceptance", and **کرنا**, "to do".

## What you've built

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
