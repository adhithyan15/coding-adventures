## You'll want to know: 买
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C40-FRUIT40-01]; assesses=[] -->

**买** — *mǎi* — "to buy".

> **你们买水吗** *nǐmen mǎi shuǐ ma* — Are you buying water?

The new character, in pieces: 买 — a lid with two dots under it, and below them a shape like 大 with its last stroke cut down to a dot. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**买** (*mǎi*) is inherited from Middle Chinese *meaX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'to trade at market'.
