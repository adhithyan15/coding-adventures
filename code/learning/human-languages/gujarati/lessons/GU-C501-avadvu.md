## You'll want to know: આવડવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C501-KARIGAR501-04]; assesses=[] -->

**આવડવું** — *āvaḍvũ* — "to know how (to do something)".

> મીરાને લાકડાનું કામ આવડે છે.
>
> *Mīrāne lākḍānũ kām āvḍe che.*
>
> Mira knows how to do woodwork.

Past (it …): તે કામ તેને આવડી ગયું, *te kām tene āvḍī gayũ*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**આવડવું** (*āvaḍvũ*) takes the person who has the skill with **ને**, and the skill itself is the subject: **મને તરતાં આવડે છે**, I know how to swim. CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb, "to come to someone's knowledge", under Sanskrit *āpatati*, "flies towards; befalls" (CDIAL 1193); the step to knowing a skill is not explained there.
