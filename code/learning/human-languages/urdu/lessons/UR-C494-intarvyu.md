## You'll want to know: انٹرویو
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C494-ROZGAR494-01]; assesses=[] -->

**انٹرویو** — *inṭarvyū* — "an interview (for a job)".

> کل میرا ایک کمپنی میں انٹرویو ہے۔
>
> *kal merā ek kampanī meṅ inṭarvyū hai.*
>
> Tomorrow I have an interview at a company.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**انٹرویو** (*inṭarvyū*) is borrowed from English *interview*.
