## You'll want to know: ಪಗಡೆ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C531-ATIKE531-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ಪಗಡೆ** — *pagaḍe* — "a dice game".

> ಹಬ್ಬದ ದಿನ ಅಜ್ಜಿ ಮತ್ತು ಅತ್ತೆ ಪಗಡೆ ಆಡಿದರು.
>
> *habbada dina ajji mattu atte pagaḍe āḍidaru.*
>
> On the festival day grandmother and aunt played pagade.

A board game played with cowries or long dice.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಪಗಡೆ** (*pagaḍe*) is the name of an old board game played with dice; DEDR 1461 records the game, but the origin of the name is not settled.
