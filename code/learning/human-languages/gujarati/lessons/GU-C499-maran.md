## You'll want to know: મરણ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C499-MARAN499-01]; assesses=[] -->

**મરણ** — *maraṇ* — "a death".

> દાદાના મરણથી આખા કુટુંબને દુઃખ થયું.
>
> *dādānā maraṇthī ākhā kuṭumbne duḥkh thayũ.*
>
> The whole family grieved at my grandfather's death.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**મરણ** (*maraṇ*) is inherited from Sanskrit *maraṇa*, "dying"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati word (CDIAL 9869).
