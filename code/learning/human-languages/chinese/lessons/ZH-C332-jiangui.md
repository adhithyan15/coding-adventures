## You'll want to know: 见鬼
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C332-GHOST332-04]; assesses=[] -->

**见鬼** — *jiànguǐ* — "how absurd; curse it".

> **见鬼了我的手机不见了** *jiànguǐ le wǒ de shǒujī bú jiàn le* — What on earth? My phone's gone!

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**见鬼** (*jiànguǐ*) is built from 见 'to see' and 鬼 'a ghost', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *kenH* and *kjw+jX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to look'. The exclamation is not given there.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
