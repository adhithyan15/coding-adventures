## You'll want to know: l'uragano
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C469-TEMPESTA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**l'uragano** — "the hurricane".

> L'uragano ha distrutto molte case.
>
> *The hurricane destroyed many houses.*

Also a person who sweeps in like one.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*uragano* was borrowed from Spanish *huracán* (EtymDB), which took it from a language of the Caribbean.
