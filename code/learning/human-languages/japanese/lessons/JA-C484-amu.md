## You'll want to know: あむ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C484-THREAD484-02]; assesses=[] -->

**あむ** — *amu* — "to knit".

> **ははは ふゆの ぼうしを あみます。** — *haha wa fuyu no bōshi o amimasu* — "My mother knits a winter hat."

Polite **あみます**, *amimasu*; a godan verb. Past (polite): **ははが てぶくろを あみました。**, *haha ga tebukuro o amimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**あむ** (*amu*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi, and its older origin is not settled (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
