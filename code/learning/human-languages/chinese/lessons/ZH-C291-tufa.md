## You'll want to know: 突发
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C291-INVENT291-04]; assesses=[] -->

**突发** — *tūfā* — "to break out suddenly; sudden".

> *zhè shì yí ge tūfā shìjiàn* — This is a sudden, unexpected event.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**突发** (*tūfā*) is built from 突 'to burst out' and 发 'to start', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *thwot* and *pjot* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'the look of something coming out'.
