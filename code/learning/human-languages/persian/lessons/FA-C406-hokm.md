## You'll want to know: حکم
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C406-QAZI-04]; assesses=[] -->

**حکم** (*hokm*) — "a verdict, a ruling".

> قاضی حکم را خواند.
>
> *qâzi hokm râ khând.*
>
> The judge read out the verdict.

Plural *ahkâm*, an Arabic broken plural. Also an order or a decree.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**حکم** is usually traced to Arabic *ḥukm* "judgement, ruling", from the same Arabic family as *ḥukūma* "government".
