## You'll want to know: échouer
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C378-ECHOUER-01]; assesses=[] -->

**échouer** — "to fail (an exam); to run aground".

> Il a échoué à l'examen du permis de conduire.
>
> *He failed his driving test.*

*Échouer à un examen*; the noun is *un échec*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*échouer* first meant to run a ship aground. The origin is not settled.
