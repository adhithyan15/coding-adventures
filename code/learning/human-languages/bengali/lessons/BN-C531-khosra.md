## You'll want to know: খসড়া
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C531-KHOSRA531-01]; assesses=[] -->

**খসড়া** — *khôsṛā* — "a draft (a first version)".

> আমি পরিকল্পনার একটা খসড়া বানালাম।
>
> *Āmi pôrikôlpônār ekṭā khôsṛā bānālām.*
>
> I made a draft of the plan.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**খসড়া** (*khôsṛā*) is usually traced to an Arabic word *khasra*, a rough record, which came through Persian.
