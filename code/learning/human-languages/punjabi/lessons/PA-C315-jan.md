## You'll want to know: ਜਾਨ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C315-BANDIT315-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ਜਾਨ** — *jān* — "a life".

> ਹਮਲੇ ਵਿੱਚ ਸਾਡੀ ਜਾਨ ਬਚੀ।
>
> *hamle vich sāḍī jān bachī.*
>
> We came through the attack alive.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਜਾਨ** (*jān*) is a loan, usually traced to Persian *jān*, "life, soul".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happened.
