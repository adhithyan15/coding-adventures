## You'll want to know: నిల్చో
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-STAND]; assesses=[] -->

**నిల్చో** (*nilcō*) — "stand".

Its pair. Telugu, like the other Dravidian languages, makes the bare stem do the work of a command with nothing added.
