## You'll want to know: はなび
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C293-HANABI293-01]; assesses=[] -->

**はなび** — *hanabi* — "fireworks".

> **なつは かわで はなびが あります。** — *natsu wa kawa de hanabi ga arimasu* — "In summer there are fireworks by the river."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**はなび** (*hanabi*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, built from *hana*, "flower", and *bi*, "fire" (from *hi*) (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
