Marathi, chapter 460: Affection.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
माया (māyā) — affection; tender love.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to long for, then the Marathi for to pine.

You'll want to know: माया.
माया — māyā — "affection; tender love".
आजीची माया सगळ्या मुलांवर असते. ājīcī māyā sagḷyā mulāṁvar aste. Grandmother's affection is on all the children.
Feminine.
Feminine; मायाळू, loving. In other settings माया (māyā) also means illusion.

The word, taken apart.
माया (māyā) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit māyā (CDIAL 10060), which CDIAL glosses "supernatural power, skill; illusion" and, in the lexicons, "compassion".

Lesson 3 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for affection, then the Marathi for warmth of feeling.

You'll want to know: आपुलकी.
आपुलकी — āpulkī — "fellow feeling".
नव्या शाळेत सगळ्यांनी माझं आपुलकीने स्वागत केलं. navyā śāḷet sagḷyānnī mājhaṁ āpulkīne svāgat kelaṁ. At the new school everyone welcomed me warmly.
Feminine.
Built on आपला, one's own: treating someone as one's own.

The word, taken apart.
आपुलकी (āpulkī) is usually traced to आपुला, an older form of आपला, "one's own", with the noun ending -kī.

Warm-up.

You'll want to know: लाडका.
लाडका — lāḍkā — "beloved; pet".
ही मांजर आमची लाडकी आहे. hī māñjar āmcī lāḍkī āhe. This cat is our darling.
Agrees: लाडका (lāḍkā) मुलगा, लाडकी मुलगी, लाडकं बाळ.

The word, taken apart.
लाडका (lāḍkā) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi lāḍkā, "darling", under the reconstructed laḍikka, "child" (CDIAL 10924).
