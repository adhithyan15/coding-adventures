## You'll want to know: ചുഴി
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C476-KATATTU476-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ചുഴി** — *cuḻi* — "a whirlpool; an eddy".

> പുഴയുടെ നടുവിൽ അപകടകരമായ ഒരു ചുഴി ഉണ്ട്.
>
> *puḻayuṭe naṭuvil apakaṭakaramāya oru cuḻi uṇṭŭ.*
>
> There is a dangerous whirlpool in the middle of the river.

It comes from the same root as ചുഴലുക, to whirl round.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ചുഴി** (*cuḻi*) is native Dravidian, from the root of ചുഴലുക, to whirl or revolve (DEDR 2698).
