Telugu, chapter 488: Wholesale and Stock.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
టోకు (ṭōku) — wholesale.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to be available, then the Telugu for to sell out.

You'll want to know: టోకు.
టోకు — ṭōku — "wholesale".
అతను టోకు ధరకు బియ్యం కొంటాడు. atanu ṭōku dharaku biyyaṁ koṇṭāḍu. He buys rice at the wholesale price.
టోకు (ṭōku) వ్యాపారి, a wholesale dealer; its opposite is చిల్లర, retail.

The word, taken apart.
టోకు (ṭōku) is a market word for wholesale whose origin is not settled.

Lesson 2 of 5.
నిల్వ (nilva) — a stock; a store.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to sell out, then the Telugu for wholesale.

You'll want to know: నిల్వ.
నిల్వ — nilva — "a stock; a store".
దుకాణంలో పంచదార నిల్వ అయిపోయింది. dukāṇaṁlō pañcadāra nilva ayipōyindi. The shop has run out of its stock of sugar.
నిల్వ (nilva) చేయు, to store. నిల్వ (nilva) పచ్చడి is a pickle made to keep.

The word, taken apart.
నిల్వ (nilva) is native Dravidian, of the family of నిలుచు, "to stand, to remain, to be preserved" (DEDR 3675): what remains is the stock.

Lesson 3 of 5.
గిడ్డంగి (giḍḍaṅgi) — a storehouse; a godown.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for wholesale, then the Telugu for a stock.

You'll want to know: గిడ్డంగి.
గిడ్డంగి — giḍḍaṅgi — "a storehouse; a godown".
రైతులు ధాన్యాన్ని గిడ్డంగిలో దాచారు. raitulu dhānyānni giḍḍaṅgilō dācāru. The farmers stored the grain in the warehouse.
A government warehouse for grain is a గిడ్డంగి (giḍḍaṅgi).

The word, taken apart.
గిడ్డంగి (giḍḍaṅgi) is native Dravidian: DEDR 1525 lists Telugu giḍḍaṅgi, "a storehouse, a godown", with Tamil kiṭaṅku, "a storehouse".

Lesson 4 of 5.
కొరత (korata) — a shortage.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a stock, then the Telugu for a storehouse.

You'll want to know: కొరత.
కొరత — korata — "a shortage".
వేసవిలో నీటి కొరత ఉంది. vēsavilō nīṭi korata undi. In summer there is a shortage of water.
నీటి కొరత (korata), a water shortage; డబ్బు కొరత (korata), a lack of money.

The word, taken apart.
కొరత (korata) is native Dravidian: DEDR 1851 lists Telugu koṟata, "deficiency, want", in the family of kuṟu, "short, small".

Lesson 5 of 5.
వేలం (vēlaṁ) — an auction.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a storehouse, then the Telugu for a shortage.

You'll want to know: వేలం.
వేలం — vēlaṁ — "an auction".
పాత బస్సులను వేలంలో అమ్మారు. pāta bassulanu vēlaṁlō ammāru. The old buses were sold at auction.
వేలం (vēlaṁ) వేయు, to auction; వేలంపాట, the bidding.

The word, taken apart.
వేలం (vēlaṁ) is usually traced to Portuguese leilão, "an auction", from the trade of the Portuguese on the Indian coasts.
