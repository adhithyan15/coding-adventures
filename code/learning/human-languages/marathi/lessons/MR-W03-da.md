# द — dental da

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

## Script

> द

Keep the tongue at the teeth and add voice: *da*. Descend the short stem; lift
for the outer body, inward curl, and down-right tail; finish the headline.
