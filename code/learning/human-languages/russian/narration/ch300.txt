Russian, chapter 300: Natural disasters.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
наводнение (navodnéniye) — a flood.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for the person on duty, then the Russian for to call.

You'll want to know: наводнение.
наводнение (navodnéniye) — "a flood".
После дождей началось наводнение. Pósle dozhdéy nachalós navodnéniye. After the rains a flood began.
Neuter.

The word, taken apart.
наводнение (navodnéniye) is built on наводнить "to flood", from вода "water", inherited from Proto-Slavic voda "water" (EtymDB); English water is a cousin.

Lesson 5 of 5.
затоплять (zatoplyát) — to flood, to submerge.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: затоплять.
затоплять (zatoplyát) — "to flood, to submerge".
Вода затопила улицы. Vodá zatopíla úlitsy. The water flooded the streets.
Imperfective; perfective затопить (zatopít). Careful: затопить печь means to light a stove.

The word, taken apart.
затоплять (zatoplyát) is built on за- and топить "to sink, to drown", inherited from Proto-Slavic topiti (EtymDB).
