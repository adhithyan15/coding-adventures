Persian, chapter 450: The town centre.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
مرکز (markaz) — a centre.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to pass, then the Persian for the back streets.

You'll want to know: مرکز.
مرکز (markaz) — "a centre".
هتل ما در مرکز شهر است. Hotel-e mâ dar markaz-e shahr ast. Our hotel is in the town centre.
Arabic plural marâkez. Markaz-e kharid — a shopping centre.

The word, taken apart.
markaz was borrowed from Arabic markaz 'centre', which EtymDB lists.

Lesson 2 of 5.
حومه (hume) — the outskirts, the suburbs.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for the back streets, then the Persian for a centre.

You'll want to know: حومه.
حومه (hume) — "the outskirts, the suburbs".
خانواده ما در حومه شهر زندگی میکند. Khânevâde-ye mâ dar hume-ye shahr zendegi mikonad. Our family lives on the outskirts of the town.
Usually hume-ye shahr.

The word, taken apart.
hume was borrowed from Arabic ḥawma, which EtymDB lists; it is usually glossed 'the area around, the edge'.

Lesson 3 of 5.
برج (borj) — a tower; a tower block.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a centre, then the Persian for the outskirts.

You'll want to know: برج.
برج (borj) — "a tower; a tower block".
از بالای برج تمام شهر را دیدیم. Az bâlâ-ye borj tamâm-e shahr râ didim. From the top of the tower we saw the whole town.
Arabic plural boruj. Also a sign of the zodiac.

The word, taken apart.
borj was borrowed from Arabic burj 'tower', which EtymDB traces through Syriac to Greek pýrgos 'tower'.

Lesson 4 of 5.
مجسمه (mojassame) — a statue.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for the outskirts, then the Persian for a tower.

You'll want to know: مجسمه.
مجسمه (mojassame) — "a statue".
وسط میدان یک مجسمه بزرگ است. Vasat-e meydân yek mojassame-ye bozorg ast. There is a big statue in the middle of the square.
Built on jesm 'body': a thing given a body.

The word, taken apart.
mojassame was borrowed from Arabic mujassama (EtymDB glosses it 'maquette'), a word built on jism 'body'.

Lesson 5 of 5.
فواره (favvâre) — a fountain.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: فواره.
فواره (favvâre) — "a fountain".
بچه ها کنار فواره پارک بازی میکردند. Bachche-hâ kenâr-e favvâre-ye pârk bâzi mikardand. The children were playing by the fountain in the park.
A spring in the ground is cheshme; favvâre sends water up.

The word, taken apart.
favvâre was borrowed from Arabic fawwāra (EtymDB), a word for water that gushes up.
