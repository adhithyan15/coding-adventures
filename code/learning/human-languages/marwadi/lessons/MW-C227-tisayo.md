## You'll want to know: तिसायो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C227-WELL227-03]; assesses=[] -->

**तिसायो** — *tisāyo* — "thirsty".

> ऊंट तिसायो है।
>
> *ū̃ṭ tisāyo hai.*
>
> The camel is thirsty.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**तिसायो** (*tisāyo*) is inherited from Sanskrit *tṛṣā*, "thirst"; CDIAL lists Hindi *tisāyā*, "thirsty", under it. CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry (CDIAL 5936).
