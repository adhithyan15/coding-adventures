## You'll want to know: a denúncia
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C297-TRIBUNAL-01]; assesses=[] -->

**a denúncia** — "the tip-off, the report to the police".

> A polícia recebeu uma denúncia anónima.
>
> *The police received an anonymous tip-off.*

*Fazer uma denúncia* — to report someone.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*denúncia* is a learned word built on Latin *dēnūntiāre* "to announce, to give notice", from *nūntius* "a messenger" (Lewis & Short).
