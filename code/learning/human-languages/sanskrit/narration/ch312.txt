Sanskrit, chapter 312: Claims and Denials.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
प्रवक्ता (pravaktā) — a spokesman.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for doubts, then the Sanskrit for a misunderstanding.

You'll want to know: प्रवक्ता.
प्रवक्ता — pravaktā — "a spokesman".
प्रवक्ता सभायां वदति। pravaktā sabhāyāṃ vadati. The spokesman speaks at the meeting.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
प्रवक्ता (pravaktā) is built from pra, "forth", and vaktṛ, "a speaker", from the root √vac, "to speak"; MW gives pravaktṛ as one who tells or relates, an announcer, and a spokesman is its modern use (MW).

पत्रिका न्यायाधीशे आरोपं प्रकाशयति। patrikā nyāyādhīśe āropaṃ prakāśayati. The newspaper publishes an allegation against the judge.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
आरोपः (āropaḥ) is built from ā and the causative of the root √ruh, "to rise", as in āropayati; MW gives āropa as imposing a burden, charging with, attributing to, and an allegation is its modern use (MW).

निराकरोति — nirākaroti — "denies; rejects".
न्यायाधीशः आरोपं दृढं निराकरोति। nyāyādhīśaḥ āropaṃ dṛḍhaṃ nirākaroti. The judge firmly denies the allegation.
Past (he …): सः आरोपं निराकृतवान्, saḥ āropaṃ nirākṛtavān.

The word, taken apart.
निराकरोति (nirākaroti) is built from nir, ā and the root √kṛ, "to do"; MW gives nir-ā-kṛ as to drive away, repudiate, reject and contradict (MW).

Lesson 4 of 5.
अनृतम् (anṛtam) — a lie; an untruth.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for an allegation, then the Sanskrit for denies.

You'll want to know: अनृतम्.
अनृतम् — anṛtam — "a lie; an untruth".
सः आरोपः अनृतम् अस्ति। saḥ āropaḥ anṛtam asti. That allegation is a lie.
A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
अनृतम् (anṛtam) is built from an, "not", and ṛta, "right, true"; MW gives anṛta as not true and, as a noun, falsehood, lying (MW).

You'll want to know: सन्दिग्धः.

प्रवक्तुः उत्तरं सन्दिग्धम् अस्ति। pravaktuḥ uttaraṃ sandigdham asti. The spokesman's answer is doubtful.

The word, taken apart.
सन्दिग्धः (sandigdhaḥ) is built from sam and the root √dih, like sandeha; MW gives saṃdigdha as smeared over, confused, and questionable (MW).
