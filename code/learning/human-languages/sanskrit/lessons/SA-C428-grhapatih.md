## You'll want to know: गृहपतिः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C428-GRHAPATI428-01]; assesses=[] -->

**गृहपतिः** — *gṛhapatiḥ* — "the master of the house".

> गृहपतिः अतिथिम् आह्वयति।
>
> *gṛhapatiḥ atithim āhvayati.*
>
> The master of the house calls in the guest.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**गृहपतिः** (*gṛhapatiḥ*) is built from *gṛha*, "a house", and *pati*, "a lord, a master"; MW gives the compound as the master of a house, a householder (MW).
