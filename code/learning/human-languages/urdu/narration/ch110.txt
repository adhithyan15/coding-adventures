Urdu, chapter 110: Television, Computer, Mobile phone, Phone, Glasses.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
ٹی وی (ṭīvī) — a television.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a drinking glass, then the Urdu for a fridge.

You'll want to know: ٹی وی.
ٹی وی — ṭīvī — "a television".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: ṭīvī]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: ṭīvī, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say ṭīvī]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Urdu for a drinking glass, then the Urdu for a fridge, then say ṭīvī again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does ٹی وی (ṭīvī) mean? ("A television".) Say it once more.

Lesson 6 of 7.
Places, can and want — a first pass.

Warm-up.
[pause 3 seconds]
Say the words for a phone and glasses.

Guided Practice.
[pause 2 seconds after each]
[your turn — say: a crossroads, around, with, a market, a police station]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: a palace, a park, a cinema, a field, a door]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: a kitchen, a bathroom, a ticket, an office, a library]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: an aeroplane, a train, near; with, to be able to, to manage to]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: a skill, able, is needed, a wish, a need]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
A crossroads? (چوراہا, chaurāhā.) Around? (آس پاس, ās pās.) With? (ساتھ, sāth.) A market? (بازار, bāzār.) A police station? (تھانہ, thānā.) A palace? (محل, mahal.) A park? (پارک, pārk.) A cinema? (سینما, sinemā.) A field? (میدان, maidān.) A door? (دروازہ, darvāzā.) A kitchen? (باورچی خانہ, bāvarchīkhānā.) A bathroom? (باتھ روم, bāthrūm.) A ticket? (ٹکٹ, ṭikaṭ.) An office? (دفتر, daftar.) A library? (کتب خانہ, kutubkhānā.) An aeroplane? (ہوائی جہاز, hawāī jahāz.) A train? (ریل گاڑی, rel gāṛī.) Near; with? (پاس, pās.) To be able to? (سکنا, saknā.) To manage to? (پانا, pānā.) A skill? (ہنر, hunar.) Able? (قابل, qābil.) Is needed? (چاہیے, chāhiye.) A wish? (خواہش, khvāhish.) A need? (ضرورت, zarūrat.)
