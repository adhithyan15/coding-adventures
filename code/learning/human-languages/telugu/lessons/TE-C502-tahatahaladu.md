# తహతహలాడు (tahatahalāḍu) — to yearn; to be restless for

## You'll want to know: తహతహలాడు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C502-NIRASA502-04]; assesses=[] -->

**తహతహలాడు** — *tahatahalāḍu* — "to yearn; to be restless for".

> అమ్మను చూడాలని పాప తహతహలాడింది.
>
> *ammanu cūḍālani pāpa tahatahalāḍindi.*
>
> The little girl longed to see her mum.

Past (I …): తహతహలాడాను, *tahatahalāḍānu*.

An echo word, తహతహ, + ఆడు. -ఆలని, wanting to.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**తహతహలాడు** (*tahatahalāḍu*) is built from the doubled echo word తహతహ, "restless longing", and the verb *āḍu*, "to move" (DEDR 347). The origin of తహతహ is not settled.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
