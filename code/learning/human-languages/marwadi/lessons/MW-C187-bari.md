## You'll want to know: बारी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C187-KOTHO187-03]; assesses=[] -->

**बारी** — *bārī* — "a small window".

> रसोई मांय एक छोटी बारी है।
>
> *rasoī mā̃y ek chhoṭī bārī hai.*
>
> There's a small window in the kitchen.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**बारी** (*bārī*) is inherited from Sanskrit *dvāra*, "a door"; CDIAL lists Old Marwari *bāra*, "a door", and Gujarati *bārī*, "a window" (CDIAL 6663).
