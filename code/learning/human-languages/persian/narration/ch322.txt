Persian, chapter 322: Verdict and release.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
محکوم شدن (mahkum shodan) — to be sentenced, to be convicted.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to give away, then the Persian for guilty.

You'll want to know: محکوم شدن.
محکوم شدن (mahkum shodan) — "to be sentenced, to be convicted".
مرد به دو سال زندان محکوم شد. Mard be do sâl zendân mahkum shod. The man was sentenced to two years in prison.
The punishment takes be. Present mahkum mishavad.

The word, taken apart.
mahkum is usually traced to Arabic maḥkūm, "judged, ruled on"; EtymDB lists no entry. shodan "to become" is inherited Persian.

Lesson 3 of 5.
زندانی (zendâni) — a prisoner.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to be sentenced, then the Persian for to be acquitted.

You'll want to know: زندانی.
زندانی (zendâni) — "a prisoner".
زندانی (zendâni) ها هر روز یک ساعت در حیاط بودند. Zendâni-hâ har ruz yek sâ'at dar hayât budand. The prisoners spent an hour in the yard every day.
zendân "prison" + -i. Also an adjective, "imprisoned".

The word, taken apart.
zendâni adds the suffix -i to zendân "prison", a native Persian word; EtymDB links zendân to Old Armenian zndan but gives it no parent, so its deeper origin is not settled.

Lesson 4 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: وثیقه.
وثیقه (vasiqe) — "bail, a security".
او با وثیقه آزاد شد. U bâ vasiqe âzâd shod. He was released on bail.
Also a guarantee for a loan. Written with ث.

The word, taken apart.
vasiqe is usually traced to Arabic wathīqa, "a document, a pledge"; EtymDB lists no entry.

Lesson 5 of 5.
آزاد شدن (âzâd shodan) — to be released, to be freed.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a prisoner, then the Persian for bail.

You'll want to know: آزاد شدن.
آزاد شدن (âzâd shodan) — "to be released, to be freed".
زندانی (zendâni) ها صبح آزاد شدند. Zendâni-hâ sobh âzâd shodand. The prisoners were released in the morning.
Present âzâd mishavad. Âzâd kardan is "to set free".

The word, taken apart.
âzâd "free" is inherited: EtymDB traces it through Middle Persian to Old Persian āzāta-, "noble, free". shodan "to become" is inherited too.
