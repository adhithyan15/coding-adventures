## You'll want to know: non appena
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C274-FRATTEMPO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**non appena** — "as soon as".

> Non appena sono arrivato, ha cominciato a piovere.
>
> *As soon as I arrived, it started to rain.*

It means the same as *appena* here; the *non* adds nothing negative.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*appena* is inherited from Latin *ad paene*, built on *paene* "almost" (EtymDB); *non* comes from Latin *nōn* "not".
