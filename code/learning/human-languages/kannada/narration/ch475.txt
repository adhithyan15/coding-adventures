Lesson 1 of 6.
ಅರಣ್ಯ (araṇya) — woodland; a forest.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to forsake, then the Kannada for solar power.

You'll want to know: ಅರಣ್ಯ.
ಅರಣ್ಯ — araṇya — "woodland; a forest".
ಅರಣ್ಯ ಇಲಾಖೆ ಹೊಸ ಮರಗಳನ್ನು ನೆಟ್ಟಿತು. araṇya ilākhe hosa maragaḷannu neṭṭitu. The forest department planted new trees.
A formal word; ಕಾಡು is the everyday forest.

The word, taken apart.
ಅರಣ್ಯ (araṇya) is borrowed from Sanskrit araṇya, "foreign land, wilderness, a forest" (CDIAL 600).

Lesson 2 of 6.
ಮಳೆಕಾಡು (maḷekāḍu) — a rainforest.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for solar power, then the Kannada for woodland.

You'll want to know: ಮಳೆಕಾಡು.
ಮಳೆಕಾಡು — maḷekāḍu — "a rainforest".
ಮಳೆಕಾಡಿನಲ್ಲಿ ಸೂರ್ಯನ ಬೆಳಕು ನೆಲ ಮುಟ್ಟುವುದಿಲ್ಲ. maḷekāḍinalli sūryana beḷaku nela muṭṭuvudilla. In a rainforest the sunlight does not reach the ground.
ಮಳೆ, rain, plus ಕಾಡು, forest.

The word, taken apart.
ಮಳೆಕಾಡು (maḷekāḍu) is a native Kannada compound of ಮಳೆ, "rain" (DEDR 4753), and ಕಾಡು, "a forest" (DEDR 1438).

You'll want to know: ಕಾಡ್ಗಿಚ್ಚು.
ಕಾಡ್ಗಿಚ್ಚು — kāḍgiccu — "a forest fire".
ಕಳೆದ ಬೇಸಿಗೆಯಲ್ಲಿ ಈ ಬೆಟ್ಟದಲ್ಲಿ ಕಾಡ್ಗಿಚ್ಚು ಆಯಿತು. kaḷeda bēsigeyalli ī beṭṭadalli kāḍgiccu āyitu. Last summer there was a forest fire on this hill.
ಕಾಡು, forest, plus ಕಿಚ್ಚು, fire: the k turns to g in the join.

The word, taken apart.
ಕಾಡ್ಗಿಚ್ಚು (kāḍgiccu) is a native Kannada compound of ಕಾಡು, "a forest" (DEDR 1438), and kiccu, "fire" (DEDR 1514).

Lesson 4 of 6.
ಕಡಿ (kaḍi) — to fell; to chop.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a rainforest, then the Kannada for a forest fire.

You'll want to know: ಕಡಿ.
ಕಡಿ — kaḍi — "to fell; to chop".
ರಸ್ತೆ ವಿಸ್ತರಿಸಲು ಅವರು ತುಂಬಾ ಮರಗಳನ್ನು ಕಡಿದರು. raste vistarisalu avaru tumbā maragaḷannu kaḍidaru. To widen the road they cut down a lot of trees.
Past (I …): ಕಡಿದೆ, kaḍide.
ಮರ ಕಡಿ (kaḍi), to fell a tree; ಕತ್ತರಿಸು is to cut with a blade.

The word, taken apart.
ಕಡಿ (kaḍi) is native Dravidian: DEDR 1125 lists Kannada kaḍi, "to cut, to chop, to fell".

Lesson 5 of 6.
ಸಂರಕ್ಷಿಸು (saṃrakṣisu) — to conserve; to protect.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a forest fire, then the Kannada for to fell.

You'll want to know: ಸಂರಕ್ಷಿಸು.
ಸಂರಕ್ಷಿಸು — saṃrakṣisu — "to conserve; to protect".
ನಾವು ಕಾಡುಗಳನ್ನು ಸಂರಕ್ಷಿಸಬೇಕು. nāvu kāḍugaḷannu saṃrakṣisabēku. We must conserve our forests.
Past (I …): ಸಂರಕ್ಷಿಸಿದೆ, saṃrakṣiside.
ಸಂರಕ್ಷಣೆ is conservation; ಕಾಪಾಡು is the everyday verb.

The word, taken apart.
ಸಂರಕ್ಷಿಸು (saṃrakṣisu) is built from Sanskrit saṃrakṣaṇa, "guarding, preservation, protection" (Monier-Williams), with the verb ending -isu.
