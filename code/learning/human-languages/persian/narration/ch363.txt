Persian, chapter 363: Claims and proof.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ادعا (edde'â) — a claim.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for logical, then the Persian for to convince.

You'll want to know: ادعا.
ادعا (edde'â) — "a claim".
این ادعا درست نیست. in edde'â dorost nist. This claim is not true.
Plural edde'âhâ. Formal; also a claim in court.

The word, taken apart.
ادعا (edde'â) is usually traced to Arabic iddi'ā' "a claim, a pretension"; Persian drops the final hamza.

Lesson 2 of 5.
ادعا کردن (edde'â kardan) — to claim.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to convince, then the Persian for a claim.

You'll want to know: ادعا کردن.
ادعا کردن (edde'â kardan) — "to claim".
او ادعا میکند که آن مرد را دیده است. u edde'â mikonad ke ân mard râ dide ast. He claims that he has seen that man.
Edde'â "a claim" + kardan (stem kon-), followed by ke and a clause.

The word, taken apart.
ادعا کردن (edde'â kardan) joins edde'â, usually traced to Arabic iddi'ā' "a claim", to the inherited Persian verb kardan "to do".

Lesson 3 of 5.
اثبات کردن (esbât kardan) — to prove.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a claim, then the Persian for to claim.

You'll want to know: اثبات کردن.
اثبات کردن (esbât kardan) — "to prove".
او نمیتواند حرفش را اثبات کند. u nemitavânad harfash râ esbât konad. He cannot prove what he says.
Esbât "proof" + kardan. Formal; everyday speech prefers sâbet kardan. In Persian, ث sounds like s.

The word, taken apart.
اثبات comes from Arabic ithbāt "proving, establishing" (EtymDB); Persian says the Arabic th as s. With kardan "to do" it becomes a verb.

Lesson 4 of 5.
ثابت شدن (sâbet shodan) — to be proven.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to claim, then the Persian for to prove.

You'll want to know: ثابت شدن.
ثابت شدن (sâbet shodan) — "to be proven".
حرف او ثابت شد. harf-e u sâbet shod. What she said was proven.
Sâbet "fixed, firm" + shodan "to become" (present stem shav-). Sâbet kardan — to prove.

The word, taken apart.
ثابت comes from Arabic thābit "firm, constant, unchanging" (EtymDB), from the same Arabic family as ithbāt. A claim that is proven has become firm.

Lesson 5 of 5.
شواهد (shavâhed) — evidence.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to prove, then the Persian for to be proven.

You'll want to know: شواهد.
شواهد (shavâhed) — "evidence".
شواهد زیادی وجود دارد. shavâhed-e ziyâdi vojud dârad. There is a lot of evidence.
An Arabic broken plural of shâhed "a witness; a piece of evidence".

The word, taken apart.
شواهد (shavâhed) is usually traced to Arabic shawāhid, a broken plural of shāhid "a witness" (EtymDB lists shāhid as the source of Persian shâhed). Evidence is what bears witness.
