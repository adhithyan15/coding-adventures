## You'll want to know: el bonobús
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C650-LINEA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**el bonobús** — "the bus pass (for several trips)".

> Con el bonobús el viaje sale más barato.
>
> *With a bus pass the trip works out cheaper.*

A Spain word; it holds ten trips, or a month of travel.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*bonobús* is built from *bono* "voucher", from Latin *bonus* "good", and *bus*, short for *autobús*, from Latin *omnibus* "for all".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.
