## You'll want to know: именины
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C317-IMENINNIK-02]; assesses=[] -->

**именины** (*imeníny*) — "a name day".

> Сегодня у мамы именины.
>
> *Sevódnya u mámy imeníny.*
>
> Today is Mum's name day.

Plural only: the feast of the saint whose name you bear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*именины* is built on the stem *имен-* of *имя* "name" (EtymDB), a cousin of Latin *nōmen* and English *name*.
