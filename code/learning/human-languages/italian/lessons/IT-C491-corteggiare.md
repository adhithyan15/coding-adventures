## You'll want to know: corteggiare
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C491-INNAMORATI-02]; assesses=[] -->

**corteggiare** — "to court, to woo".

> L'ha corteggiata per mesi prima del primo appuntamento.
>
> *He courted her for months before the first date.*

Built on *corte* "court": at first it meant to serve at a court.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*corteggiare* is built on *corte* "court", inherited from Latin *cohors, cohortis* "an enclosed yard" (Lewis & Short).
