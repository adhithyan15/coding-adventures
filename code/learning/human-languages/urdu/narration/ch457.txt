Urdu, chapter 457: Honest and False.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
مخلص (mukhlis) — sincere.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for grace, then the Urdu for cruelty.

You'll want to know: مخلص.
مخلص — mukhlis — "sincere".
وہ ایک مخلص دوست ہے۔ voh ek mukhlis dost hai. He is a sincere friend.

The word, taken apart.
مخلص (mukhlis) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic mukhliṣ, "sincere, devoted".

Lesson 3 of 5.
اعتبار کرنا (etibār karnā) — to believe someone; to trust their word.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: اعتبار کرنا.
اعتبار کرنا — etibār karnā — "to believe someone; to trust their word".
میں نے اس کی بات کا اعتبار کیا۔ maiṅ ne us kī bāt kā etibār kiyā. I took him at his word.
Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے دوست کا اعتبار کیا, us ne dost kā etibār kiyā.

The word, taken apart.
اعتبار کرنا (etibār karnā) is built from اعتبار, usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic iʿtibār, "regard, credit", and کرنا, "to do".
