# مجھے لگتا ہے (mujhe lagtā hai) — what I think

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[UR-LEX-C268-LAGNA-01, UR-LEX-C268-KHAYAL-01, UR-GRAMMAR-C265-FUTURE-02] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "I'm hungry". (*mujhe bhūk lagī hai*.) Then "it will rain". (*bārish ho gī*.) Join the two verbs, and you have an opinion.

## You'll want to know: مجھے لگتا ہے کہ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-GRAMMAR-C268-OPINION-01]; assesses=[UR-GRAMMAR-KI-COMPLEMENTISER, UR-LEX-BARISH, UR-GRAMMAR-C265-FUTURE-01] -->

> **مجھے لگتا ہے۔**
>
> *mujhe lagtā hai.* — "I think so."

Word for word, "it seems to me". **کہ** (*ki*), "that", hangs the thought on it:

> **مجھے لگتا ہے کہ بارش ہو گی۔**
>
> *mujhe lagtā hai ki bārish ho gī.* — "I think it will rain."

> **مجھے لگتا ہے کہ وہ نہیں آئے گا۔**
>
> *mujhe lagtā hai ki voh nahīṅ āe gā.* — "I don't think he'll come."

## You'll want to know: in my view
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-GRAMMAR-C268-OPINION-02]; assesses=[UR-LEX-C231-CHIZ231-01, UR-LEX-THIK, UR-LEX-C268-GHALAT-01, UR-LEX-C268-KHAYAL-01] -->

> **میرے خیال میں یہ ٹھیک ہے۔**
>
> *mere khayāl meṅ yih ṭhīk hai.* — "In my view, this is right."

> **میری رائے میں یہ غلط ہے۔**
>
> *merī rāe meṅ yih ghalat hai.* — "In my opinion, this is wrong."

**خیال** is masculine, so **میرے** (*mere*); **رائے** (*rāe*) is feminine, so **میری** (*merī*). To ask:

> **آپ کا کیا خیال ہے؟**
>
> *āp kā kyā khayāl hai?* — "What do you think?"
