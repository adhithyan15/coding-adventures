## You'll want to know: બલ્બ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C517-VIJLI517-04]; assesses=[] -->

**બલ્બ** — *balb* — "a light bulb".

> ઓરડાનો બલ્બ ઊડી ગયો.
>
> *orḍāno balb ūḍī gayo.*
>
> The bulb in the room has blown.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**બલ્બ** (*balb*) is borrowed from English *bulb*. A bulb that blows "flies off": **બલ્બ ઊડી ગયો**.
