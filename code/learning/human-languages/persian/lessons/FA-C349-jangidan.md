## You'll want to know: جنگیدن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C349-KHIYANAT-03]; assesses=[] -->

**جنگیدن** (*jangidan*) — "to fight".

> سربازها تا صبح جنگیدند.
>
> *Sarbâz-hâ tâ sobh jangidand.*
>
> The soldiers fought until morning.

*jang* "war" + the verb suffix *-idan*. Present *mijangad*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*jangidan* is built from Persian *jang* "war", a native noun, turned into a verb with the suffix *-idan*.

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
