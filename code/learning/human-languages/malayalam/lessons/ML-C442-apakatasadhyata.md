# അപകടസാധ്യത (apakaṭasādhyata) — a risk

## You'll want to know: അപകടസാധ്യത
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C442-APAKATAM442-01]; assesses=[] -->

**അപകടസാധ്യത** — *apakaṭasādhyata* — "a risk".

> ആ പാലത്തിൽ അപകടസാധ്യത കൂടുതലാണ്.
>
> *ā pālattil apakaṭasādhyata kūṭutalāṇŭ.*
>
> The risk on that bridge is high.

അപകടം, danger, and സാധ്യത, a possibility: the possibility of danger.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**അപകടസാധ്യത** (*apakaṭasādhyata*) is a Malayalam compound: അപകടം, "an accident, danger", built from Sanskrit *apa-*, "away, ill", with a second part whose source is uncertain, and സാധ്യത, "a possibility", from Sanskrit *sādhya* (Monier-Williams).
