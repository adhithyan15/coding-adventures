# धाकटा (dhākṭā) — younger

## You'll want to know: धाकटा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C497-THORLA497-01]; assesses=[] -->

**धाकटा** — *dhākṭā* — "younger".

> माझा धाकटा भाऊ शाळेत जातो.
>
> *mājhā dhākṭā bhāū śāḷet jāto.*
>
> My younger brother goes to school.

Agrees: धाकटा भाऊ, धाकटी बहीण.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**धाकटा** (*dhākṭā*) is a Marathi adjective whose origin is not settled.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
