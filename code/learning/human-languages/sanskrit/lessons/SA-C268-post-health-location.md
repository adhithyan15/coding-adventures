# One small four-service location exchange

## Warm-up

[PAUSE 2s] Say “financial bank,” then “here,” “there,” “where?”, and “is.”

## You'll want to know: four complete turns

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-PATTERN-C268-SERVICE-LOCATION-02]; assesses=[SA-LEX-C120-THINGS120-02, SA-LEX-C268-SERVICE-01, SA-LEX-C84-PLACES-02, SA-LEX-C238-PPL238-04, SA-LEX-C14-DEIXIS-03, SA-LEX-C14-DEIXIS-04, SA-LEX-C14-DEIXIS-06, SA-LEX-ASTI] -->

Keep the familiar question frame whole and change only its first word:

- **पत्रालयः कुत्र अस्ति? पत्रालयः तत्र अस्ति।** — Where is the post office?
  The post office is there.
- **चिकित्सालयः कुत्र अस्ति? चिकित्सालयः अत्र अस्ति।** — Where is the
  hospital? The hospital is here.
- **वित्तकोषः कुत्र अस्ति? वित्तकोषः तत्र अस्ति।** — Where is the financial
  bank? The financial bank is there.
- **आरक्षकः कुत्र अस्ति? आरक्षकः अत्र अस्ति।** — Where is the policeman? The
  policeman is here.

These four complete turns are the whole pattern. Do not use them to infer a
new declension or a broader directions system.
