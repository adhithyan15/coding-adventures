## Guided Practice: five turns

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[SA-LEX-C24-ASK-03, SA-LEX-C74-MARKET-01, SA-LEX-C74-MARKET-03, SA-LEX-C87-NUMBERS-03, SA-LEX-C185-WHY185-01, SA-LEX-C248-THG248-03, SA-LEX-DHANYAVADA, SA-GRAMMAR-C265-DADATU-01, SA-PATTERN-C265-POLITE-ORDER-02, SA-VERB-C266-KRINAMI-01, SA-PATTERN-C266-MARKET-EXCHANGE-02] -->

Build the exchange one complete turn at a time:

1. **अहं फलं क्रीणामि।** — *Ahaṃ phalaṃ krīṇāmi.* — I buy fruit.
2. **मूल्यं कियत्?** — *Mūlyaṃ kiyat?* — How much is the price?
3. **विंशतिः रूप्यकाणि।** — *Viṃśatiḥ rūpyakāṇi.* — Twenty rupees.
4. **कृपया मह्यं फलं ददातु।** — *Kṛpayā mahyaṃ phalaṃ dadātu.* — Please give me fruit.
5. **धन्यवादः।** — *Dhanyavādaḥ.* — Thank you.

[PAUSE 2s] Say the five turns once without breaking the exchange apart.
