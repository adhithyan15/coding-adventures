## You'll want to know: தூங்கு
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C71-DAY-02]; assesses=[] -->

**தூங்கு** (*tūṅgu*) — "to sleep".

The other end of the day — and the verb behind a noun you already own.

தூக்கம் (*tūkkam*) is sleep, the thing. தூங்கு is sleeping, the doing. Tamil makes that pair
again and again, and once you can hear it you get two words for the price of one: the noun
தூக்கம் sits still, and the verb தூங்கு does something.

Its **ங்க** is the same nasal-plus-க you say inside வாங்கு.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

Two verbs, and the day has both ends.
