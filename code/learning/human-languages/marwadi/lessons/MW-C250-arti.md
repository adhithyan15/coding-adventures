## You'll want to know: आरती
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C250-UCHHAV250-04]; assesses=[] -->

**आरती** — *ārtī* — "the waving of a lamp in worship".

> मां आथण आरती करै है।
>
> *mā̃ āthaṇ ārtī karai hai.*
>
> Mother waves the lamp in worship in the evening.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**आरती** (*ārtī*) is inherited from Sanskrit *ārātrika*, the waving of a lamp before an image at night. CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry; it lists Hindi and Gujarati *ārtī* (CDIAL 1315).
