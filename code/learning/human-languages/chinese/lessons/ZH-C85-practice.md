## The exchange
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-PERFORMANCE-LOCATION-01]; assesses=[ZH-LEX-C85-ZAI-01, ZH-LEX-C85-NAR-01, ZH-LEX-C85-LIBIAN-01, ZH-LEX-C85-WAIBIAN-01, ZH-LEX-NE-01, ZH-LEX-C43-GO43-05] -->

> **A:** 我在家 你呢
>
> *wǒ zài jiā, nǐ ne* — I'm at home. And you?
>
> **B:** *wǒ zài xuéxiào* — I'm at school.
>
> **A:** 你的书呢
>
> *nǐ de shū ne* — Where's your book?
>
> **B:** *zài bāo lǐbian* — In my bag.
>
> **A:** *māo ne* — And the cat?
>
> **B:** *māo zài wàibian* — The cat's outside.
>
> **A:** 他呢
>
> *tā ne* — And him?
>
> **B:** 他在那儿
>
> *tā zài nàr* — He's over there.

*nǐ de shū ne* asks where the book is: 呢 after a thing, with nothing else, means *and where is it?* The answer drops the book and starts at 在.

## What you've built — one pattern, four places
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ZH-PERFORMANCE-LOCATION-01, ZH-SCRIPT-ZAI-AT-01, ZH-LEX-C85-ZAI-01, ZH-LEX-C57-HOME57-05] -->

One pattern, a person or a thing, then 在, then the place:

- **我在这儿** *wǒ zài zhèr* — I'm here.
- **他在那儿** *tā zài nàr* — he's there.
- *shū zài bāo lǐbian* — the book is in the bag.
- *māo zài wàibian* — the cat is outside.

**Listen.** *Nǐ de shǒujī ne?*

**Speak.** Place yourself, then somebody you know, then your phone.

[YOU WRITE: 在 — the top of 有, a short vertical, then 十 on a long bar]
