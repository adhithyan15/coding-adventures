## You'll want to know: anxieux
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C476-INQUIETUDE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**anxieux** — "anxious".

> Avant un examen, je suis toujours anxieux.
>
> *Before an exam I am always anxious.*

Feminine *anxieuse*. Stronger than *inquiet*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*anxieux* is a learned borrowing from Latin *anxius* "troubled" (EtymDB), from *angere* "to squeeze, to distress" (Lewis & Short).
