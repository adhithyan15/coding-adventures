## You'll want to know: ਕਿਆਰੀ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C302-SOW302-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ਕਿਆਰੀ** — *kiārī* — "a garden bed; a bed in a field".

> ਮਾਤਾ ਜੀ ਕਿਆਰੀ ਵਿੱਚ ਸਾਗ ਬੀਜਦੇ ਹਨ।
>
> *mātā jī kiārī vich sāg bījde han.*
>
> Mother sows greens in the garden bed.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਕਿਆਰੀ** (*kiārī*) is inherited from Sanskrit *kēdāra*, "field"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *kiārī*, "bed in a field or garden", under it (CDIAL 3463).
