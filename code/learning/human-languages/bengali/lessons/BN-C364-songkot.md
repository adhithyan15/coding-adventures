# সংকট (sôngkôṭ) — a crisis

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Bengali for to come through, then the Bengali for difficult.

## You'll want to know: সংকট
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C364-SONGKOT364-02]; assesses=[] -->

**সংকট** — *sôngkôṭ* — "a crisis".

> দেশে এখন জলের সংকট।
>
> *deshe ekhôn jôler sôngkôṭ.*
>
> There is a water crisis in the country now.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**সংকট** (*sôngkôṭ*) is Sanskrit *saṃkaṭa*, narrow and so dangerous or critical (Monier-Williams). In Bengali it is the noun, a crisis or a severe shortage.
