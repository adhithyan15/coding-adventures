# ಶಿಫಾರಸು ಮಾಡು (śiphārasu māḍu) — to recommend

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[KA-LEX-C402-LANCHA402-03, KA-LEX-C402-LANCHA402-04] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Kannada for transparent, then the Kannada for to conceal.

## You'll want to know: ಶಿಫಾರಸು ಮಾಡು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C402-LANCHA402-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ಶಿಫಾರಸು ಮಾಡು** — *śiphārasu māḍu* — "to recommend".

> ಮ್ಯಾನೇಜರ್ ಲಂಚ ಪಡೆದು ಅವನನ್ನು ಕೆಲಸಕ್ಕೆ ಶಿಫಾರಸು ಮಾಡಿದನು.
>
> *myānējar lañca paḍedu avanannu kelasakke śiphārasu māḍidanu.*
>
> The manager took a bribe and recommended him for the job.

Past (I …): ಶಿಫಾರಸು ಮಾಡಿದೆ, *śiphārasu māḍide*.

For what: -ಗೆ. ಶಿಫಾರಸು alone is a recommendation.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಶಿಫಾರಸು ಮಾಡು** (*śiphārasu māḍu*) is built from *śiphārasu*, a loan from Hindustani *sifāriś*, "a recommendation", which comes from Persian, with *māḍu*, "to do".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
