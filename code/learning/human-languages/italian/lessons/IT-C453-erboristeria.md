## You'll want to know: l'erboristeria
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C453-NEGOZI-04]; assesses=[] -->

**l'erboristeria** — "the herbalist's".

> In erboristeria vendono tisane e piante.
>
> *At the herbalist's they sell herbal teas and plants.*

*L'erborista* — the herbalist.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*erboristeria* is built on *erba* 'herb, grass', which is inherited from Latin *herba* (EtymDB).
