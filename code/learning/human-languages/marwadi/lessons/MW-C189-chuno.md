## You'll want to know: चूनो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C189-THANBHO189-03]; assesses=[] -->

**चूनो** — *chūno* — "lime (for whitewash)".

> भींत माथै नुवो चूनो है।
>
> *bhī̃t māthai nuvo chūno hai.*
>
> There's fresh whitewash on the wall.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**चूनो** (*chūno*) is inherited from Sanskrit *cūrṇa*, "powder"; CDIAL lists Hindi *cūnā*, "lime", and an Old Marwari form meaning "flour" (CDIAL 4889).
