## You'll want to know: лишать
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C392-OGRANICHENIYA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**лишать** (*lishát*) — "to deprive of, to take away".

> Боль лишает его сна.
>
> *Bol lisháyet yegó sna.*
>
> The pain keeps him from sleeping.

Imperfective; perfective *лишить*. *Лишать кого чего*: the thing taken goes in the genitive.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**лишать** is usually linked with *лишний* "extra, too many" and *лишь* "only", on an old Slavic root meaning excess: to take away what someone has.
