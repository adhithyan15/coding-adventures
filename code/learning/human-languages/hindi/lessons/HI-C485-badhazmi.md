## You'll want to know: बदहज़मी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C485-CHAKKAR485-01]; assesses=[] -->

**बदहज़मी** — *badhazmī* — "indigestion".

> ज़्यादा मिठाई खाकर अरुण को बदहज़मी हो गई।
>
> *zyādā miṭhāī khākar Aruṇ ko badhazmī ho gaī.*
>
> Arun got indigestion from eating too many sweets.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**बदहज़मी** (*badhazmī*) is usually traced to Persian *bad-hazmī*, "indigestion", which joins Persian *bad*, "bad", to Arabic *haḍm*, "digestion", said *hazm* in Persian.
