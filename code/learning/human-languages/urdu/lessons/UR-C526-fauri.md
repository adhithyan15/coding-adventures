## You'll want to know: فوری
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C526-MIAD526-03]; assesses=[] -->

**فوری** — *faurī* — "urgent; immediate".

> اگر کام فوری ہے تو میں آج ہی کروں گا۔
>
> *agar kām faurī hai to maiṅ āj hī karūṅ gā.*
>
> If the job is urgent, I will do it today.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**فوری** (*faurī*) is built on *faur*, "at once", usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *fawr*, "haste", with the ending *-ī*.
