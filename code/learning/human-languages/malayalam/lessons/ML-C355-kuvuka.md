## You'll want to know: കൂവുക
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C355-PATRAPRAVARTTAKAN355-05]; assesses=[] -->

**കൂവുക** — *kūvuka* — "to boo; to hoot".

> കളി തോറ്റപ്പോൾ ആളുകൾ കൂവി.
>
> *kaḷi tōṟṟappōḷ āḷukaḷ kūvi.*
>
> When the game was lost, people booed.

Past: കൂവി, *kūvi*.

The same verb is the crowing of a cock at dawn: കോഴി കൂവി. At a speech it is a jeer.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**കൂവുക** (*kūvuka*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 1868 groups it with Tamil *kū*, a cry, a call. Malayalam uses it for a cock crowing and for a crowd jeering.
