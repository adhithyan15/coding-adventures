Persian, chapter 311: Sources and details.

Lesson 1 of 5.
منبع (manba') — a source.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to let someone know, then the Persian for to inform.

You'll want to know: منبع.
منبع (manba') — "a source".
این خبر از یک منبع مطمئن بود. In khabar az yek manba'-e motma'en bud. This news came from a reliable source.
Broken plural manâbe'. Its first meaning is a spring of water.

The word, taken apart.
manba' is borrowed from Arabic manba', "spring, source" (EtymDB).

Lesson 2 of 5.
به گفته (be gofte-ye) — according to.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to inform, then the Persian for a source.

You'll want to know: به گفته.
به گفته (be gofte-ye) — "according to".
به گفته (be gofte-ye) پلیس، کسی آسیب ندید. Be gofte-ye polis, kasi âsib nadid. According to the police, nobody was hurt.
It takes the ezafe: be gofte-ye u "according to him". gofte "what was said" is the participle of goftan.

The word, taken apart.
be gofte-ye is built from be "to" and gofte "what was said", the participle of goftan "to say". All of it is native Persian.

Lesson 3 of 5.
به نقل از (be naql az) — quoting, citing.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a source, then the Persian for according to.

You'll want to know: به نقل از.
به نقل از (be naql az) — "quoting, citing".
روزنامه به نقل از وزیر نوشت که قیمت ها بالا میرود. Ruznâme be naql az vazir nevesht ke qeymat-hâ bâlâ miravad. Quoting the minister, the newspaper wrote that prices were going up.
naql "transfer, relaying" is the same noun as in naql kardan "to relate".

The word, taken apart.
naql is borrowed from Arabic naql, "transport, transfer" (EtymDB); be "to" and az "from" are inherited Persian.

Lesson 4 of 5.
جزئیات (joz'iyât) — details, the details.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for according to, then the Persian for quoting.

You'll want to know: جزئیات.
جزئیات (joz'iyât) — "details, the details".
پلیس جزئیات بیشتری نگفت. Polis joz'iyât-e bishtari nagoft. The police gave no further details.
An Arabic plural built on joz'i "partial, minor" (from joz' "a part"), written with the hamza seat ئ.

The word, taken apart.
joz'iyât is borrowed from Arabic juz'iyyāt, "details" (EtymDB), a plural built on juz' "a part".
