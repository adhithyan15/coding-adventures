Lesson 1 of 5.
શહીદ (śahīd) — a martyr.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to slay, then the Gujarati for to fight on.

You'll want to know: શહીદ.
શહીદ — śahīd — "a martyr".
ગામ આ શહીદોનું ઋણી છે. gām ā śahīdonũ ṛṇī che. The village owes a debt to these martyrs.
A masculine or feminine noun, by the person meant.

The word, taken apart.
શહીદ (śahīd) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic šahīd, "a witness", and so "a martyr", one whose death bears witness. Someone શહીદ થાય છે, dies for a cause.

Lesson 2 of 5.
બલિદાન (balidān) — a sacrifice.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to fight on, then the Gujarati for a martyr.

You'll want to know: બલિદાન.
બલિદાન — balidān — "a sacrifice".
સૈનિકોના બલિદાનથી ગામ બચી ગયું. sainikonā balidānthī gām bacī gayũ. The soldiers' sacrifice saved the village.
A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
બલિદાન (balidān) is Sanskrit balidāna, "the presenting of an offering" (Monier-Williams), built from bali, an offering, and dāna, a giving. In Gujarati it is giving up one's life or comfort for others.

Lesson 3 of 5.
વિજય (vijay) — a victory.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a martyr, then the Gujarati for a sacrifice.

You'll want to know: વિજય.
વિજય — vijay — "a victory".
છેવટે રાજાની સેનાનો વિજય થયો. chevṭe rājānī senāno vijay thayo. In the end the king's army won.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
વિજય (vijay) is a tatsama, Sanskrit vijaya, "victory, conquest" (Monier-Williams). It happens with થવું: સેનાનો વિજય થયો, the army won.

Lesson 4 of 5.
વેર (ver) — enmity; a feud.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a sacrifice, then the Gujarati for a victory.

You'll want to know: વેર.
વેર — ver — "enmity; a feud".
બંને રાજાઓ વચ્ચે જૂનું વેર હતું. banne rājāo vacce jūnũ ver hatũ. There was an old feud between the two kings.
A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
વેર (ver) is inherited from Sanskrit vaíra, "enmity", and CDIAL lists the Gujarati noun, "enmity, revenge" (CDIAL 12142). It is neuter: જૂનું વેર, an old feud.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: પડકારવું.
પડકારવું — paḍkārvũ — "to challenge".
યોદ્ધાએ દુશ્મનને પડકાર્યો. yoddhāe duśmanne paḍkāryo. The warrior challenged the enemy.
Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે દુશ્મનને પડકાર્યો, teṇe duśmanne paḍkāryo.

The word, taken apart.
પડકારવું (paḍkārvũ) No dictionary we use records an older form, so its history is not settled. The one challenged takes ને, and the verb agrees with him: દુશ્મનને પડકાર્યો.
