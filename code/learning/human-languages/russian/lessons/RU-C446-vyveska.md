## You'll want to know: вывеска
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C446-VITRINA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**вывеска** (*výveska*) — "a shop sign, a signboard".

> Над дверью висит новая вывеска.
>
> *Nad dvéryu visít nóvaya výveska.*
>
> A new sign hangs over the door.

Feminine; the stress is on *вы-* (*vý-*).

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*вывеска* is built on *вывесить* 'to hang out', from *вы-* 'out' and *весить*, *вешать* 'to hang': a sign hung outside a shop. EtymDB does not list the word.
