## You'll want to know: వ్యక్తిత్వం
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C507-SVABHAVAM507-02]; assesses=[] -->

**వ్యక్తిత్వం** — *vyaktitvaṁ* — "personality".

> ఆమె వ్యక్తిత్వం అందరికీ నచ్చుతుంది.
>
> *āme vyaktitvaṁ andarikī naccutundi.*
>
> Everyone likes her personality.

Built on వ్యక్తి, a person, + -త్వం, -ness.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**వ్యక్తిత్వం** (*vyaktitvaṁ*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *vyaktitva*, "distinctness, individuality, personality" (Monier-Williams), built on *vyakti*, "an individual".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
