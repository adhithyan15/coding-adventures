## You'll want to know: চুকানো
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C540-MASHUL540-05]; assesses=[] -->

**চুকানো** — *chukāno* — "to settle; to finish off (a matter)".

> আমরা ঝামেলাটা আজই চুকালাম।
>
> *Āmrā jhāmelāṭā āji chukālām.*
>
> We settled the trouble that very day.

Past (he/she …): তিনি দোকানের ধার চুকালেন, *tini dokāner dhār chukālen*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**চুকানো** (*chukāno*) comes from a reconstructed *cukk*, "stop"; CDIAL lists Bengali *cukāna*, "to get rid of, to finish, to settle" (CDIAL 4848).
