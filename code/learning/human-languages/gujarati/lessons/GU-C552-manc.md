## You'll want to know: મંચ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C552-MANCH552-01]; assesses=[] -->

**મંચ** — *manc* — "a stage".

> જો મંચ તૈયાર હશે તો નાટક સમયસર શરૂ થશે.
>
> *jo manc taiyār haśe to nāṭak samaysar śarū thaśe.*
>
> If the stage is ready, the play will start on time.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**મંચ** (*manc*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *mañca*, "a raised platform, a stage" (Monier-Williams).
