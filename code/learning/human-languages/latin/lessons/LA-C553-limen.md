## You'll want to know: līmen, līminis
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C553-LIMN-03]; assesses=[] -->

**līmen, līminis** — "a threshold, doorstep".

> Nova nūpta līmen nōn tangit.
>
> *The new bride does not touch the threshold.*

Neuter, third declension. A bride was carried over the threshold, so as not to stumble on it. English *subliminal*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**līmen** is a native Latin noun; Lewis & Short connects it with *līmus* "sidelong", a crossbeam, but the origin is not settled. English *subliminal* comes from it.
