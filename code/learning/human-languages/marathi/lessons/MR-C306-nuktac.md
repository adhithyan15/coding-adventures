## You'll want to know: नुकताच
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C306-KRAMASH306-05]; assesses=[] -->

**नुकताच** — *nuktāc* — "lately; a moment ago".

> तो नुकताच गावाहून परत आला.
>
> *to nuktāc gāvāhūn parat ālā.*
>
> He has recently come back from the village.

It agrees like an adjective: तो नुकताच आला, ती नुकतीच आली, ते नुकतंच आलं.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**नुकताच** (*nuktāc*) is a Marathi word for lately, made with the emphatic *-c*; its origin is not settled.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
