# ಹೂಡು (hūḍu) — to invest

## You'll want to know: ಹೂಡು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C401-ABHIVRIDDHI401-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ಹೂಡು** — *hūḍu* — "to invest".

> ನಾನು ಉಳಿಸಿದ ಹಣವನ್ನು ಒಂದು ಅಂಗಡಿಯಲ್ಲಿ ಹೂಡಿದೆ.
>
> *nānu uḷisida haṇavannu ondu aṅgaḍiyalli hūḍide.*
>
> I invested the money I had saved in a shop.

Past (I …): ಹೂಡಿದೆ, *hūḍide*.

ಹೂಡಿಕೆ, an investment. Also to yoke: ಎತ್ತು ಹೂಡು.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಹೂಡು** (*hūḍu*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 4361 lists Kannada *pūḍu*, "to join, to tie to, to yoke, to fix, to arrange"; old *p-* became *h-*, and "to put money to work" is the modern sense.
