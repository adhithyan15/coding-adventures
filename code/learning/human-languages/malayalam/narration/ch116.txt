Malayalam, chapter 116: How You Feel Inside.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
സങ്കടം (saṅkaṭaṁ) — sadness.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for dizziness, then the Malayalam for not well.

You'll want to know: സങ്കടം.
സങ്കടം (saṅkaṭaṁ) — "sadness, sorrow". എനിക്ക് സങ്കടമുണ്ട് — "I am sad".

What you've built.
A feeling, said with to-me.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: saṅkaṭaṁ]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: saṅkaṭaṁ, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say enikkŭ saṅkaṭamuṇṭŭ]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Malayalam for dizziness, then the Malayalam for not well, then say saṅkaṭaṁ again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does സങ്കടം (saṅkaṭaṁ) mean? ("Sadness".) Say it once more.
