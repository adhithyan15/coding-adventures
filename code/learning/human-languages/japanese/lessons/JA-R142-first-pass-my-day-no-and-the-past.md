## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[JA-LEX-C138-OKIMASU, JA-LEX-C138-HATARAKIMASU, JA-LEX-C138-ARUKIMASU, JA-LEX-C138-KAERIMASU, JA-LEX-C138-NEMASU, JA-LEX-C139-DESUKA, JA-LEX-C139-CHIGAIMASU, JA-LEX-C139-JANAI, JA-LEX-C139-NANIMO, JA-LEX-C139-DAREMO, JA-LEX-C139-DOKONIMO, JA-LEX-C140-SENSHUU, JA-LEX-C140-SENGETSU, JA-LEX-C140-KYONEN, JA-LEX-C140-SAKKI, JA-LEX-C140-OWARIMASHITA] -->

[PAUSE 2s each] Say each one in Japanese.

- [YOU SAY: I get up, I work, I walk, I go home, I go to bed — politely]
- [YOU SAY: is it ...?, no, that is not right, is not (plainly)]
- [YOU SAY: nothing, nobody, nowhere — each with a negative verb]
- [YOU SAY: last week, last month, last year, a moment ago, it is over]

Then say three sentences: what you did last week (**ました**), what you
did not do last year (**ませんでした**), and a question about tomorrow
(**ますか**).

[PAUSE 3s] I get up? (**おきます**, *okimasu*.) I work? (**はたらきます**, *hatarakimasu*.) I walk? (**あるきます**, *arukimasu*.) I go home? (**かえります**, *kaerimasu*.) I go to bed? (**ねます**, *nemasu*.) Is it ...? (**ですか**, *desu ka*.) No, that is not right? (**ちがいます**, *chigaimasu*.) Is not? (**じゃない**, *ja nai*.) Nothing? (**なにも**, *nani mo*.) Nobody? (**だれも**, *dare mo*.) Nowhere? (**どこにも**, *doko ni mo*.) Last week? (**せんしゅう**, *senshū*.) Last month? (**せんげつ**, *sengetsu*.) Last year? (**きょねん**, *kyonen*.) A moment ago? (**さっき**, *sakki*.) It is over? (**おわりました**, *owarimashita*.)
