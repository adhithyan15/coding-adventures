Russian, chapter 93: The Letters Shcha and E.
4 lessons. The first lesson already needs your eyes or your hands, so save this one for when you have stopped.


Lesson 1 of 4.
э (e) — the e that opens этот.

Before we start: this one needs your hands, so it is not a driving lesson. You will want a pen and something to write on and your eyes, for letter shapes on the page. You can listen to everything else now — leave the sections called Script you'll notice: э and Guided Practice until you have stopped, and I will say so again when we reach it.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: write я and ё once each, and name them.
[pause 2 seconds]
Now say этот (étot), "this". You have read it for a long time. This lesson takes one letter out of it and puts it in your hand.

Script you'll notice: э.
э — e.
It opens этот (étot), "this", and эта. It looks like a с turned to face the other way, with a short bar across the middle. It is the plain e sound, with no y in front of it, which is why it is mostly found at the start of a word.

Writing: э.
Put your pen on э and follow its line. Copy the shape you can see — slowly, and larger than it is printed.
This book does not yet tell you where to start this letter or which way to travel. It is not written down here until it can be written down with a source. Copying what is in front of you needs no such source, and it is how the shape gets into your hand in the meantime.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — look: at этот and point at э inside it]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[once you have stopped driving — trace: э three times, saying e as you finish each one]
[once you have stopped driving — write: ё, then э, from memory]
[your turn — read: этот aloud once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
Which letter is this — э? (e, e.) Which word did it come from? (этот, étot, "this".)

Now say площадь (plóshchad), "a square". You have read it for a long time. This lesson takes one letter out of it and puts it in your hand.

Script you'll notice: щ.
щ (shcha) — shch.
It is inside площадь (plóshchad), "a square". It is ш with a small tail at the bottom right, and it sounds like a long, soft sh.

Put your pen on щ (shcha) and follow its line. Copy the shape you can see — slowly, and larger than it is printed.
This book does not yet tell you where to start this letter or which way to travel. It is not written down here until it can be written down with a source. Copying what is in front of you needs no such source, and it is how the shape gets into your hand in the meantime.

Lesson 3 of 4.
