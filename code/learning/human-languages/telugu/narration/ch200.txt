Telugu, chapter 200: To save, To choose, To try, To serve, To chew.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
పొదుపు చేయు (podupu cēyu) — to save (money).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to fold, then the Telugu for to arrange.

You'll want to know: పొదుపు చేయు.
పొదుపు చేయు — podupu cēyu — "to save (money)".

The word, taken apart.
The origin of podupu, "saving", the first word of పొదుపు చేయు (podupu cēyu), is uncertain: DEDR 4509 lists podupu only as "to imbed". Cēyu, "to do", is native (DEDR 1957).

Lesson 2 of 5.
ఎంచుకో (eñcukō) — to choose.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to arrange, then the Telugu for to save.

You'll want to know: ఎంచుకో.
ఎంచుకో — eñcukō — "to choose".

The word, taken apart.
ఎంచుకో (eñcukō) is native Dravidian: eñcu, "to count, consider, judge", one word with Tamil eṇṇu, "to think, count" (DEDR 793), with -konu, "for oneself".

Lesson 4 of 5.
వడ్డించు (vaḍḍiñcu) — to serve (food).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to choose, then the Telugu for to try.

You'll want to know: వడ్డించు.
వడ్డించు — vaḍḍiñcu — "to serve (food)".

The word, taken apart.
The origin of వడ్డించు (vaḍḍiñcu) is uncertain. It may come from Sanskrit vardhayati, "cuts, divides", whose Oriya and Marathi descendants mean "to serve out food" (CDIAL 11381), but no source says so.
