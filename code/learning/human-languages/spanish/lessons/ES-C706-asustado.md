## You'll want to know: asustado
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C706-SUSTO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**asustado** — "frightened, scared".

> La niña está asustada.
>
> *The little girl is frightened.*

Used with *estar*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*asustado* is the participle of *asustar*, which is built on *susto* "fright"; for *susto* the origin is not settled.
