## You'll want to know: стоимость
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C395-SBEREZHENIYA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**стоимость** (*stóimost*) — "cost, value".

> Стоимость билета — тысяча рублей.
>
> *Stóimost biléta — týsyacha rubléy.*
>
> The ticket costs a thousand roubles.

Feminine. From *стоить*, to cost.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**стоимость** is built on *стоить* "to cost", which perhaps came through Polish and copies the sense of Latin *cōnstāre* "to stand firm; to cost", by a usual account; it goes with *стоять* "to stand".
