Arabic, chapter 543: Making music.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
لحن (laḥn) — a melody, a tune.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to applaud, then the Arabic for acting.

You'll want to know: لحن.
لحن — laḥn — "a melody, a tune".
غنت البنت لحنا جميلا. ghannat al-bint laḥnan jamīlan. The girl sang a beautiful tune.
Plural: ألحان, alḥān.
In grammar لحن (laḥn) also means a mistake in speech.

The word, taken apart.
لحن (laḥn) is built on the native Arabic root l-ḥ-n, "to speak with a tone; to make a mistake in speech" (Lane). Lane gives laḥn both as a tone of voice and as a fault in speaking.

إيقاع (īqāʿ) — a rhythm, a beat.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for acting, then the Arabic for a melody.

You'll want to know: إيقاع.
إيقاع — īqāʿ — "a rhythm, a beat".
رقص الأطفال على إيقاع الموسيقى. raqaṣa al-aṭfāl ʿalā īqāʿ al-mūsīqā. The children danced to the rhythm of the music.

The word, taken apart.
إيقاع (īqāʿ) is built on the native Arabic root w-q-ʿ, "to fall, to happen" (Lane). Īqāʿ is the verbal noun of Form IV: making the beats fall.

Lesson 3 of 6.
عزف (ʿazafa) — to play an instrument.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a melody, then the Arabic for a rhythm.

You'll want to know: عزف.
عزف — ʿazafa — "to play an instrument".
عزفت أختي أغنية قديمة. ʿazafat ukhtī ughniya qadīma. My sister played an old song.
Present: يعزف, yaʿzif.
It takes على for the instrument.

The word, taken apart.
عزف (ʿazafa) is built on the native Arabic root ʿ-z-f, "to turn away; to make a sound" (Lane). Lane gives yaʿzifu as its present.

You'll want to know: جوقة.
جوقة — jawqa — "a choir".
تغني ابنتي في جوقة (jawqa) المدرسة. tughannī ibnatī fī jawqat al-madrasa. My daughter sings in the school choir.

The word, taken apart.
جوقة (jawqa) is a word for a band or company of people, and today a choir; the origin is not settled.
