## You'll want to know: el logro
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C766-RETO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**el logro** — "the achievement".

> Terminar la carrera fue un gran logro.
>
> *Finishing my degree was a great achievement.*

The verb is *lograr*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*logro* comes from Latin *lucrum* "gain, profit" (EtymDB), through *lograr*, from *lucrārī* "to gain"; in old Spanish *logro* also meant interest on a loan.
