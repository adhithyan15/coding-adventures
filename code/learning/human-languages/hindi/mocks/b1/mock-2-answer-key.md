## Writing

Task 1 must explain the Wednesday work conflict, request the Thursday group,
state an acceptable fallback if it is full, and ask when a decision will
arrive. Task 2 must identify likely users of a shared workspace, develop a
benefit and a risk or cost, propose a practical rule or trial, and make a
reasoned recommendation for local readers. Apply the rubric rather than
matching fixed wording. Scorer-only reference openings:

- **नमस्ते। मुझे बुधवार के पाठ्यक्रम में स्थान मिला है, लेकिन मेरे काम का समय…**
- **हमारे क्षेत्र में साझा कार्यस्थल उन लोगों के लिए उपयोगी हो सकता है जो…**

Do not show these stems to the learner.

## Speaking

The interview requires extended, relevant answers with reasons or examples.
The account must sustain approximately three minutes and connect the earlier
state, reason for change, reactions, problem-solving, and present evaluation.
The collaborative task must compare choices and reach an explicit six-week
trial plan. The follow-up must express, support, clarify, and respond to views.
Score each learner's own task development, interaction and repair, Hindi
control, organisation, and intelligibility; no fixed wording is required.
