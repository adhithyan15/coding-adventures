Marathi, chapter 83: To lift, To pull, To push, To hide, To win.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
उचलणे (uchalṇe) — to lift.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to teach, then the Marathi for to make.

You'll want to know: उचलणे.
उचलणे — uchalṇe — "to lift".

The word, taken apart.
उचलणे (uchalṇe) is a tadbhava, a word inherited and reshaped by sound change. It comes from uccalyati, "goes up", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 1642). CDIAL builds it on Sanskrit uccalati, "springs up".

Lesson 5 of 5.
जिंकणे (jiṅkṇe) — to win.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to push, then the Marathi for to hide.

You'll want to know: जिंकणे.
जिंकणे — jiṅkṇe — "to win".

The word, taken apart.
जिंकणे (jiṅkṇe) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from jita, "won, conquered" (CDIAL 5224). CDIAL lists Marathi jĩkṇẽ, "to conquer", from the Middle Indo-Aryan form jikka-.
