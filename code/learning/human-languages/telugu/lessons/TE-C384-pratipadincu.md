# ప్రతిపాదించు (pratipādiñcu) — to propose

## You'll want to know: ప్రతిపాదించు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C384-SUCANA384-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ప్రతిపాదించు** — *pratipādiñcu* — "to propose".

> ఆమె సమావేశంలో ఒక కొత్త పద్ధతి ప్రతిపాదించింది.
>
> *āme samāvēśaṁlō oka kotta paddhati pratipādiñcindi.*
>
> She proposed a new method at the meeting.

Past (I …): ప్రతిపాదించాను, *pratipādiñcānu*.

Formal: meetings, committees and papers. In everyday talk సూచించు does the work.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ప్రతిపాదించు** (*pratipādiñcu*) is built on Sanskrit *pratipādana*, "giving, presenting" (Monier-Williams), with the Telugu verb ending -ఇంచు; Telugu uses it for putting an idea forward.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
