## You'll want to know: だいぶつ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C407-SANPAI407-03]; assesses=[] -->

**だいぶつ** — *daibutsu* — "a great statue of the Buddha".

> **この てらの だいぶつは たかいです。** — *kono tera no daibutsu wa takai desu* — "The great statue at this temple is tall."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**だいぶつ** (*daibutsu*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *dai*, "large" (Middle Chinese *dajH*) and *butsu*, "Buddha" (*bjut*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
