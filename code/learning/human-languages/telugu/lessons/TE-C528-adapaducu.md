# ఆడపడుచు (āḍapaḍucu) — a husband's sister

## Warm-up

## You'll want to know: ఆడపడుచు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C528-TOBUTTUVU528-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ఆడపడుచు** — *āḍapaḍucu* — "a husband's sister".

> పండుగకు ఆడపడుచు ఇంటికి వచ్చింది.
>
> *paṇḍugaku āḍapaḍucu iṇṭiki vaccindi.*
>
> My husband's sister came home for the festival.

Also a married daughter of the house visiting home.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ఆడపడుచు** (*āḍapaḍucu*) is built from ఆడ, "female", and native *paḍucu*, "a girl", which DEDR 3840 lists with *paḍãti*, "a woman".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
