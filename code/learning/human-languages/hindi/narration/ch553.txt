Hindi, chapter 553: In the Right Order.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
सिवा (sivā) — except (के सिवा).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for well, then the Hindi for but (in a lighter register).

You'll want to know: सिवा.
सिवा — sivā — "except (के सिवा)".
रविवार के सिवा दुकान हर दिन खुली रहती है। Ravivār ke sivā dukān har din khulī rahtī hai. The shop is open every day except Sunday.

The word, taken apart.
सिवा (sivā) is usually traced to Arabic siwā, "except", through Persian.

What you've built.
One more word for saying what happens if.

Lesson 3 of 5.
क्रम (kram) — order; sequence.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for except (के सिवा (sivā)), then the Hindi for a series.

You'll want to know: क्रम.
क्रम — kram — "order; sequence".
शब्दों का क्रम बदलिए। Śabdoṁ kā kram badliye. Change the order of the words.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
क्रम (kram) is a tatsama, taken whole from Sanskrit krama, "a step; order, succession" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 4 of 5.
चरण (caraṇ) — a stage, a phase.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a series, then the Hindi for order.

You'll want to know: चरण.
चरण — caraṇ — "a stage, a phase".
योजना का पहला चरण पूरा हो गया। Yojnā kā pahlā caraṇ pūrā ho gayā. The first stage of the plan is complete.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
चरण (caraṇ) is a tatsama, taken whole from Sanskrit caraṇa, "a foot; a step" (Monier-Williams). A step of a plan is a stage of it.
