## You'll want to know: كربون
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C463-MUNAKH463-05]; assesses=[] -->

**كربون** — *karbūn* — "carbon".

> في الهواء كربون كثير فوق المدينة.
>
> *fī al-hawāʾ karbūn kathīr fawqa al-madīna.*
>
> There is a lot of carbon in the air above the city.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**كربون** (*karbūn*) is usually traced to French *carbone*, from Latin *carbo*, "coal".
