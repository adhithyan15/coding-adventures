# স্যাঁতসেঁতে (sæ̃tsẽte) — damp; dank

## You'll want to know: স্যাঁতসেঁতে
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C428-ARDRO428-04]; assesses=[] -->

**স্যাঁতসেঁতে** — *sæ̃tsẽte* — "damp; dank".

> বর্ষায় ঘরের দেয়াল স্যাঁতসেঁতে হয়।
>
> *bôrshāy ghôrer deyāl sæ̃tsẽte hôy.*
>
> In the monsoon the walls of the house get damp.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**স্যাঁতসেঁতে** (*sæ̃tsẽte*) is inherited: CDIAL lists Bengali *sẽt-sẽtiyā*, "damp, moist", under the reconstructed *saṁtimita* (CDIAL 12891); the doubled shape is an expressive repetition.
