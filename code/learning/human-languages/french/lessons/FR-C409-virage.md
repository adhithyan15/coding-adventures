## You'll want to know: le virage
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C409-VIRAGE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**le virage** — "the bend, the curve (in a road)".

> Le bus ralentit dans le virage.
>
> *The bus slows down on the bend.*

*Un virage dangereux* — a dangerous bend.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*virage* is built on *virer* 'to turn' with *-age*. The origin of *virer* is not settled; it is often linked to Latin *gyrare* 'to turn round'.
