## You'll want to know: りょうし
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C490-SEA490-01]; assesses=[] -->

**りょうし** — *ryōshi* — "a fisherman".

> **りょうしは あさ はやく うみに でます。** — *ryōshi wa asa hayaku umi ni demasu* — "Fishermen go out to sea early in the morning."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**りょうし** (*ryōshi*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *ryō*, "fishing" and *shi*, "expert" (Middle Chinese *srij*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
