Gujarati, chapter 189: Wrapping up an order.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
લપેટવું (lapeṭvũ) — to wrap.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for goods, then the Gujarati for thing.

You'll want to know: લપેટવું.
લપેટવું — lapeṭvũ — "to wrap".
દુકાનદાર ભેટ કાગળમાં લપેટે છે. — dukāndār bheṭ kāgaḷmā̃ lapeṭe che — "The shopkeeper wraps the gift in paper."

The word, taken apart.
લપેટવું (lapeṭvũ) is a tadbhava. It comes from lappēṭṭ, "wrap", a reconstructed form, and CDIAL glosses the Gujarati verb "to wrap, fold, wind" (CDIAL 10942).

Lesson 2 of 5.
મંગાવવું (mangāvvũ) — to order, to send for.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for thing, then the Gujarati for to wrap.

You'll want to know: મંગાવવું.
મંગાવવું — mangāvvũ — "to order, to send for".
અમે હોટેલમાંથી ચા મંગાવીએ છીએ. — ame hoṭelmā̃thī cā mangāvīe chīe — "We order tea from the hotel."
માગવું asks for a thing; મંગાવવું (mangāvvũ) has it brought.

The word, taken apart.
મંગાવવું (mangāvvũ) is built on māgvũ, "to ask for", with the causative ending -āvvũ: "to have something brought". māgvũ is a tadbhava from Sanskrit mā́rgati, "seeks, requests" (CDIAL 10074).

Lesson 4 of 5.
બચવું (bacvũ) — to be left over, to be spared.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to order, then the Gujarati for to run out.

You'll want to know: બચવું.
બચવું — bacvũ — "to be left over, to be spared".
મારી પાસે દસ રૂપિયા બચે છે. — mārī pāse das rūpiyā bace che — "I have ten rupees left over."
બચાવવું saves a thing; બચવું (bacvũ) is what is left or spared.

The word, taken apart.
બચવું (bacvũ) goes back to Sanskrit váñcati, "moves; escapes from", but CDIAL marks the Gujarati verb, glossed "to escape", as borrowed from Hindi bacnā, "to escape"; the taught bacāvvũ, "to save", is its causative (CDIAL 11208).

Lesson 5 of 5.
સ્વીકારવું (svīkārvũ) — to accept.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to run out, then the Gujarati for to be left over.

You'll want to know: સ્વીકારવું.
સ્વીકારવું — svīkārvũ — "to accept".
આ દુકાન સિક્કા સ્વીકારે છે. — ā dukān sikkā svīkāre che — "This shop accepts coins."

The word, taken apart.
સ્વીકારવું (svīkārvũ) is built on Sanskrit svīkāra, "acceptance, making one's own", from sva, "own", and kāra, "making": a learned word with the Gujarati verb ending -vũ. CDIAL has no entry for it.
