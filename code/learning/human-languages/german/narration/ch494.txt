German, chapter 494: Zanken und versöhnen.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
zanken — to squabble (sich zanken).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for feeling of safety, then the German for solidarity.

You'll want to know: zanken.
zanken — "to squabble (sich zanken)".
Die Geschwister zanken sich um das letzte Stück Kuchen. The brother and sister are squabbling over the last piece of cake.
Mostly reflexive: sich zanken um + accusative.

The word, taken apart.
The origin of zanken is not settled; EtymDB lists the German word without a source.

Lesson 3 of 5.
vertragen — to get along (sich vertragen); to tolerate.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to squabble, then the German for to sulk.

You'll want to know: vertragen.
vertragen — "to get along (sich vertragen); to tolerate".
Meine Katze und mein Hund vertragen sich gut. My cat and my dog get along well.
Reflexive for to get along: sich vertragen. Without sich it means to tolerate: Ich vertrage keine Milch.

The word, taken apart.
vertragen is inherited from Middle High German vertragen and Old High German fartragan "to bear, to stand" (EtymDB), built on tragen "to carry", from Proto-Germanic draganą.

Lesson 4 of 5.
versöhnen — to reconcile (sich versöhnen).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to sulk, then the German for to get along.

You'll want to know: versöhnen.
versöhnen — "to reconcile (sich versöhnen)".
Nach einer Woche haben sich die Freundinnen wieder versöhnt. After a week the two friends made up again.
Reflexive: sich versöhnen mit — to make up with.

The word, taken apart.
versöhnen comes from Middle High German versüenen (EtymDB), built on suone "atonement, settlement", today die Sühne.

Lesson 5 of 5.
die Versöhnung — reconciliation.

Warm-up.

You'll want to know: die Versöhnung.
die Versöhnung — "reconciliation".
Zur Versöhnung kaufte er ihr Blumen. To make up, he bought her flowers.
Plural: die Versöhnungen.

The word, taken apart.
Versöhnung is built on versöhnen with -ung; the verb comes from Middle High German versüenen (EtymDB).
