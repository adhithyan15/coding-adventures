## You'll want to know: enterrar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C556-ENTIERRO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**enterrar** — "to bury".

> Lo enterraron en su pueblo.
>
> *They buried him in his home village.*

Stem change: *entierro*, *entierras*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*enterrar* is inherited from spoken Latin *\*interrāre* 'to put in the earth' (EtymDB), built on *in* and *terra* 'earth'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
