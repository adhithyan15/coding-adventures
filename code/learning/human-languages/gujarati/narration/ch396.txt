Gujarati, chapter 396: The Guard's Lantern.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ગાર્ડ (gārḍ) — a guard (on a train or at a gate).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to cram, then the Gujarati for to seat.

You'll want to know: ગાર્ડ.
ગાર્ડ — gārḍ — "a guard (on a train or at a gate)".
ટ્રેનના છેલ્લા ડબ્બામાં ગાર્ડ બેસે છે. ṭrennā chellā ḍabbāmā̃ gārḍ bese che. The guard sits in the last carriage of the train.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ગાર્ડ (gārḍ) is borrowed from English guard: on the railway, the guard who rides at the back of the train.

Lesson 2 of 5.
ફાનસ (phānas) — a lantern.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to seat, then the Gujarati for a guard.

You'll want to know: ફાનસ.
ફાનસ — phānas — "a lantern".
રાત્રે ગાર્ડ હાથમાં ફાનસ રાખે છે. rātre gārḍ hāthmā̃ phānas rākhe che. At night the guard keeps a lantern in his hand.
A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
ફાનસ (phānas) is usually traced to Persian fānūs, "a lantern", a word Persian itself took from Greek.

Lesson 3 of 5.
સાંકળ (sā̃kaḷ) — a chain.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a guard, then the Gujarati for a lantern.

You'll want to know: સાંકળ.
સાંકળ — sā̃kaḷ — "a chain".
મીરાએ બારણાની સાંકળ ખોલી. Mīrāe bārṇānī sā̃kaḷ kholī. Mira undid the chain on the door.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
સાંકળ (sā̃kaḷ) is a tadbhava of Sanskrit śṛṅkhala, "a chain"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati word (CDIAL 12580). On a train it is the chain you pull to stop it.

You'll want to know: વાગવું.
વાગવું — vāgvũ — "to ring; to sound (of a bell or a drum)".
મંદિરમાં સાંજે ઢોલ વાગે છે. mandirmā̃ sā̃je ḍhol vāge che. In the temple a drum sounds in the evening.
Past (it …): તે ઢોલ વાગ્યો, te ḍhol vāgyo.

The word, taken apart.
વાગવું (vāgvũ) is a tadbhava of Sanskrit vādyatē, "is made to sound" (CDIAL 11513); વગાડવું, to play an instrument, is its causative partner.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ઘંટ (ghanṭ) — a large bell.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a chain, then the Gujarati for to ring.

You'll want to know: ઘંટ.
ઘંટ — ghanṭ — "a large bell".
સ્ટેશન પર ઘંટ વાગ્યો અને ટ્રેન ઊપડી. sṭeśan par ghanṭ vāgyo ane ṭren ūpḍī. The bell rang at the station and the train set off.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ઘંટ (ghanṭ) is a tadbhava of Sanskrit ghaṇṭā, "a bell" (CDIAL 4421): the big bell of a temple or a station. ઘંટડી is the small one.
