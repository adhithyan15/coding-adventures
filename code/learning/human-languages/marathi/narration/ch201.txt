7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.

To be enough? (पुरणे, purṇe.) To burn? (जळणे, jaḷṇe.) To knock? (ठोठावणे, ṭhoṭhāvṇe.) To compare? (तुलना करणे, tulnā karṇe.) To go for a walk? (फिरायला जाणे, phirāylā jāṇe.) To fold? (घडी करणे, ghaḍī karṇe.) To prepare? (तयारी करणे, tayārī karṇe.) To introduce? (ओळख करून देणे, oḷakh karūn deṇe.) To lend? (उसने देणे, usne deṇe.)
