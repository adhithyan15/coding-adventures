## You'll want to know: 全天
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C396-NOMATTER396-04]; assesses=[] -->

**全天** — *quántiān* — "all day; round the clock".

> **要是晚上要用钱这儿全天都能取钱** *yàoshi wǎnshang yào yòng qián zhèr quántiān dōu néng qǔqián* — If you need cash at night, you can take money out here at any hour.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**全天** (*quántiān*) is built from 全 'whole' and 天 'a day', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *dzjwen* and *then* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription).

One more word for saying what would happen.
