# কম (kôm) — less; little

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[BN-LEX-C370-KHUB370-02, BN-LEX-C370-KHUB370-03] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Bengali for extremely, then the Bengali for too much.

## You'll want to know: কম
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C370-KHUB370-04]; assesses=[] -->

**কম** — *kôm* — "less; little".

> দাম কম হলে আমি এটা কিনব।
>
> *dām kôm hôle āmi eṭā kinbô.*
>
> If the price is lower, I'll buy this.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**কম** (*kôm*) is usually traced to Persian *kam*, little: the opposite of বেশি.

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
