## You'll want to know: collezionare
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C505-COLLEZIONI-01]; assesses=[] -->

**collezionare** — "to collect (as a hobby)".

> Da bambino collezionavo francobolli.
>
> *As a child I collected stamps.*

For things kept as a hobby; to pick up, say *raccogliere*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*collezionare* is built on *collezione* "collection", a learned borrowing from Latin *collēctiō* "a gathering" (EtymDB), from *colligere* "to gather".
