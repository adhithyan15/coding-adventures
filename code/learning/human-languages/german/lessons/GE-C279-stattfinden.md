## You'll want to know: stattfinden
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C279-EREIGNIS-03]; assesses=[] -->

**stattfinden** — "to take place".

> Das Konzert fand im Park statt.
>
> *The concert took place in the park.*

Separable: *es findet statt*, *es hat stattgefunden*. For planned events.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*stattfinden* joins the old noun *Statt* "place" and *finden* "to find", a cousin of English *find* (EtymDB): to find its place.
