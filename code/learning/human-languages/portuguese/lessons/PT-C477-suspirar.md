## You'll want to know: suspirar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C477-CHORO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**suspirar** — "to sigh".

> O meu pai suspira sempre que vê a conta da luz.
>
> *My father sighs every time he sees the electricity bill.*

*Suspirar por* — to long for.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*suspirar* is inherited, through Old Portuguese *suspirar* (EtymDB), from Latin *suspīrāre* "to draw a deep breath, to sigh" (Lewis & Short), built on *spīrāre* "to breathe".
