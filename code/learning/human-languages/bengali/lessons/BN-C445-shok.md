## You'll want to know: শোক
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C445-SHOK445-01]; assesses=[] -->

**শোক** — *shok* — "grief; mourning".

> দাদুর মৃত্যুর পরে পুরো পরিবার শোকে ছিল।
>
> *dādur mrityur pôre puro pôribār shoke chhilô.*
>
> After my grandfather's death the whole family was in mourning.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**শোক** (*shok*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *śoka*, "burning", and so "sorrow, grief" (Monier-Williams).
