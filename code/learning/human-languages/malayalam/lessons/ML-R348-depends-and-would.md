# Review — it depends, and what would have been

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ML-LEX-C348-AGHOSHAM-01, ML-SCRIPT-C348-GHA-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "a celebration". (*āghōṣaṁ*.) Write **ഘ** once from memory, then **ആഘോഷം**.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ML-GRAMMAR-C348-DEPENDS-01, ML-LEX-C348-AGHOSHAM-01, ML-SCRIPT-C348-GHA-01, ML-GRAMMAR-C348-UNREAL-IF-01, ML-GRAMMAR-C348-WOULD-01, ML-GRAMMAR-C347-REAL-CONDITION-01, ML-GRAMMAR-C347-ENKIL-VERB-01, ML-GRAMMAR-C346-EKKAL-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s each]
- [YOU SAY: "Will you come tomorrow? — It depends on the weather."]
- [YOU SAY: "If it does not rain, I will come. If it rains, I will not."]
- [YOU SAY: "Onam is the biggest celebration in Kerala."]
- [YOU SAY: "If I had money, I would go."]
- [YOU SAY: "If you had come earlier, we would have caught the bus."]
- [YOU SAY: "If I had known, I would have called. I should have gone."]
- [YOU WRITE: **ആഘോഷം**]
