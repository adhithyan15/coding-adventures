German, chapter 520: Krimis und Biografien.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
der Krimi — crime novel, crime film.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for sculptor, then the German for work of art.

You'll want to know: der Krimi.
der Krimi — "crime novel, crime film".
Am Strand lese ich gern einen spannenden Krimi. On the beach I like reading an exciting crime novel.
Plural: die Krimis. Short for der Kriminalroman.

The word, taken apart.
Krimi is short for Kriminalroman "crime novel", built on a learned word from Latin crīmen "charge, crime" (Lewis & Short).

What you've built.
One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.

Lesson 4 of 5.

das Sachbuch — "non-fiction book".
Zum Geburtstag bekam ich ein Sachbuch über Vögel. For my birthday I got a non-fiction book about birds.
Plural: die Sachbücher. Fiction is die Belletristik.

The word, taken apart.
Sachbuch is built on Sache "thing, matter", inherited from Old High German sahha, and Buch "book", from Old High German buoh (EtymDB).

Lesson 5 of 5.
der Bösewicht — villain.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for short story, then the German for non-fiction book.

You'll want to know: der Bösewicht.
der Bösewicht — "villain".
Im Film ist der Bösewicht am Ende im Gefängnis. In the film the villain ends up in prison.
Plural: die Bösewichte. Half joking when said of real people.

The word, taken apart.
Bösewicht is built on böse "evil", from Proto-Germanic bausijaz, and Wicht "creature, wretch", inherited from Old High German wiht (EtymDB).
