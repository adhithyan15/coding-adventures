## You'll want to know: سیلاب
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C474-RAGBAR-05]; assesses=[] -->

**سیلاب** (*seylâb*) — "floodwater, a flood".

> سیلاب پل روستا را خراب کرد.
>
> *Seylâb pol-e rustâ râ kharâb kard.*
>
> The floodwater destroyed the village bridge.

*seyl* 'flood' + *âb* 'water'.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*seylâb* is built from *seyl* 'flood', borrowed from Arabic *sayl*, and native *âb* 'water', from Middle Persian *āb* (EtymDB). EtymDB lists *seylâb* itself without a source.
