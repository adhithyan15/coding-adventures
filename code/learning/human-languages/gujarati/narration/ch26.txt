Gujarati, chapter 26: City, School, and Road.
7 lessons. You can do the first one in the car; after that you will want to have stopped.


Lesson 1 of 7.
શહેર (shahar) — hear city first.

Warm-up.
Listen twice: shahar. It means city. Hold only the sound and meaning; the Gujarati spelling waits for the next small step.

Guided Practice.
Picture a city when you hear shahar, then say shahar once from the English cue.

Wrap-up Recall.
