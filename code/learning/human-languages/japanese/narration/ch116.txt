Japanese, chapter 116: Trout, Crucian carp, Young yellowtail, Pacific saury, Short-neck clam.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ます (masu) — a trout.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for a sea cucumber, then the Japanese for a sweetfish.

You'll want to know: ます.
ます — masu — "a trout".

The word, taken apart.
ます (masu) is a native Japanese word, inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, and its older origin is not settled. It is usually written in kana; its kanji, when used, is a kun'yomi attached later (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
