## You'll want to know: تصادف کردن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C324-HADESE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**تصادف کردن** (*tasâdof kardan*) — "to crash, to have a collision".

> ماشین ما با یک تاکسی تصادف کرد.
>
> *Mâshin-e mâ bâ yek tâksi tasâdof kard.*
>
> Our car crashed into a taxi.

The other vehicle takes *bâ*. Present *tasâdof mikonad*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*tasâdof* is borrowed from Arabic *taṣāduf* (EtymDB), usually glossed "a collision, a chance meeting". *kardan* "to do" is inherited Persian.
