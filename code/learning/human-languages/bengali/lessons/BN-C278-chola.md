## You'll want to know: চলা
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C278-DHAKKA278-02]; assesses=[] -->

**চলা** — *chôlā* — "to move; to go along".

> বাসটা দ্রুত চলছিল, তাই হঠাৎ থামতে পারল না।
>
> *bāsṭā drutô chôlchhilô, tāi hôṭhāt thāmte pārlô nā.*
>
> The bus was going fast, so it couldn't stop suddenly.

Past (he/she …): সেই বাস চলল, *sei bās chôllô*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**চলা** (*chôlā*) comes down from Sanskrit *calati*, 'goes away; shakes' (CDIAL 4715, which lists Bengali *calā*, 'to move, walk'). It is the plain partner of **চালানো**: a driver চালায়, and the car চলে.
