## You'll want to know: کوک کردن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C518-PLAYING-05]; assesses=[] -->

**کوک کردن** (*kuk kardan*) — "to tune (an instrument); to wind (a clock)".

> او قبل از کنسرت تارش را کوک کرد.
>
> *u qabl az konsert târash râ kuk kard.*
>
> He tuned his tar before the concert.

*kuk* 'tuning' + *kardan*. *kuk budan* — to be in tune, and in speech 'to be in good form'.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*kuk* means 'tuning' and 'winding', but no source used here gives its history, and its origin is not settled. *kardan* is inherited Persian.
