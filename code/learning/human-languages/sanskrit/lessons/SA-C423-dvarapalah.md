## You'll want to know: द्वारपालः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C423-PRAVESHA423-05]; assesses=[] -->

**द्वारपालः** — *dvārapālaḥ* — "a gatekeeper".

> द्वारपालः प्रवेशे तिष्ठति।
>
> *dvārapālaḥ praveśe tiṣṭhati.*
>
> The gatekeeper stands at the entrance.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**द्वारपालः** (*dvārapālaḥ*) is built from *dvāra*, "a door, a gate", and *pāla*, "a guard", from the root √pā, "to protect"; MW gives the compound as a doorkeeper (MW).
