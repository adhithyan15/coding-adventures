Arabic, chapter 317: Stopped by the police.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
قبض (qabaḍa) — to seize, to grasp.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for rubble, then the Arabic for ruins.

You'll want to know: قبض.
قبض — qabaḍa — "to seize, to grasp".
قبضت الشرطة على اللص في السوق. qabaḍat ash-shurṭa ʿalā al-liṣṣ fī as-sūq. The police caught the thief in the market.
Present: يقبض, yaqbiḍ.
قبض (qabaḍa) على: to arrest. قبض (qabaḍa) راتبه: he drew his pay.

The word, taken apart.
قبض (qabaḍa) is built on the native Arabic root q-b-ḍ (Lane), "to take a thing in the closed hand"; qabḍa is a fist.

Lesson 2 of 5.
احتجز (iḥtajaza) — to detain, to hold.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for ruins, then the Arabic for to seize.

You'll want to know: احتجز.
احتجز — iḥtajaza — "to detain, to hold".
احتجز الموظف جواز سفري ساعة. iḥtajaza al-muwaẓẓaf jawāz safarī sāʿa. The official held my passport for an hour.
Present: يحتجز, yaḥtajiz.

The word, taken apart.
احتجز (iḥtajaza) is built on the native Arabic root ḥ-j-z (Lane), "to keep apart, to hold back". It is Form VIII, "to hold back for oneself"; a hotel booking, ḥajz, is the same root.

Lesson 3 of 5.
أوقف (awqafa) — to stop someone; to arrest.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to seize, then the Arabic for to detain.

You'll want to know: أوقف.
أوقف — awqafa — "to stop someone; to arrest".
أوقف الشرطي السيارة عند الجسر. awqafa ash-shurṭī as-sayyāra ʿinda al-jisr. The policeman stopped the car at the bridge.
Present: يوقف, yūqif.
توقف is to stop oneself; أوقف (awqafa) is to stop someone or something.

The word, taken apart.
أوقف (awqafa) is built on the native Arabic root w-q-f (Lane), "to stand still", the root of tawaqqafa, to stop. It is Form IV, "to make stand still".

Lesson 4 of 5.
دورية (dawriyya) — a patrol.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to detain, then the Arabic for to stop someone.

You'll want to know: دورية.
دورية — dawriyya — "a patrol".
مرت دورية (dawriyya) شرطة في الشارع. marrat dawriyyat shurṭa fī ash-shāriʿ. A police patrol went by in the street.
Also a periodical: مجلة دورية (dawriyya).

The word, taken apart.
دورية (dawriyya) is built on the native Arabic root d-w-r (Lane), "to turn, to go round", the root of dawr, a turn. A dawriyya makes its round again and again.
