# ਮੇਰੇ — my, with plural friends

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-LEX-MERA-02, PA-LEX-DOST] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say **ਮੇਰਾ**, then **ਦੋਸਤ**.

## Grammar Lens: use the *-e* agreement form
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-FORM-MERE-185]; assesses=[PA-LEX-MERA-02] -->

> **ਮੇਰੇ** — *mere* — my, with plural or oblique masculine nouns

The owner is still “me.” The ending changes to *-e*. In **ਮੇਰੇ ਦੋਸਤ**, the
form goes with friends in the plural.
