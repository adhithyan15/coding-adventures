# సంసారం (saṁsāraṁ) — family life

## You'll want to know: సంసారం
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C533-SAMSARAM533-02]; assesses=[] -->

**సంసారం** — *saṁsāraṁ* — "family life".

> పెద్ద సంసారం నడపడం సులభం కాదు.
>
> *pedda saṁsāraṁ naḍapaḍaṁ sulabhaṁ kādu.*
>
> Running a big family is not easy.

Also one's family itself: మా సంసారం.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**సంసారం** (*saṁsāraṁ*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *saṁsāra*, "secular life, the world" (CDIAL 13036).
