## You'll want to know: le sourcil
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C478-VISAGE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**le sourcil** — "eyebrow".

> Il lève les sourcils quand il est surpris.
>
> *He raises his eyebrows when he is surprised.*

*Froncer les sourcils* — to frown.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*le sourcil* is inherited from Latin *supercilium* "eyebrow" (EtymDB), built from *super* "above" and *cilium* "eyelid" (Lewis & Short).
