## You'll want to know: विसर्जयति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C331-NADITIRA331-03]; assesses=[] -->

**विसर्जयति** — *visarjayati* — "immerses; lets go".

> उत्सवस्य अन्ते जनाः दीपान् नद्यां विसर्जयन्ति।
>
> *utsavasya ante janāḥ dīpān nadyāṃ visarjayanti.*
>
> At the end of the festival people set lamps afloat on the river.

Past (he …): सः दीपं नद्यां विसर्जितवान्, *saḥ dīpaṃ nadyāṃ visarjitavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**विसर्जयति** (*visarjayati*) is built from *vi* and the causative of the root √sṛj, "to let go, send forth"; MW gives *vi-sṛj* as to send or pour forth, let go, and the immersion of an image is its festival use (MW).
