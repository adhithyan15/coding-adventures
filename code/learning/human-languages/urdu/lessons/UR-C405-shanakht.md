## You'll want to know: شناخت
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C405-RAJISTAR405-02]; assesses=[] -->

**شناخت** — *shanākht* — "identification; identity".

> کاؤنٹر پر شناخت کے لیے پاسپورٹ دیجیے۔
>
> *kāunṭar par shanākht ke liye pāsporṭ dījiye.*
>
> Please show your passport at the desk for identification.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**شناخت** (*shanākht*) is usually traced to Persian *shinākht*, "knowing, recognition".
