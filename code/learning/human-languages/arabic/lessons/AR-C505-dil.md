## You'll want to know: ضلع
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C505-ADHM505-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ضلع** — *ḍilʿ* — "a rib".

> كسر اللاعب ضلعا في المباراة.
>
> *kasara al-lāʿib ḍilʿan fī al-mubārā.*
>
> The player broke a rib in the match.

Plural: أضلاع, *aḍlāʿ*.

In geometry ضلع is also the side of a triangle.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ضلع** (*ḍilʿ*) is built on the native Arabic root *ḍ-l-ʿ*, "a rib; to be crooked" (Lane). Lane links the rib to the sense of being bent.

## What you've built

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
