## You'll want to know: o homicídio
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C286-DELITO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**o homicídio** — "the homicide, the killing".

> A polícia acha que foi um homicídio.
>
> *The police think it was a homicide.*

A news and court word; in speech, *o assassinato*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*homicídio* is a learned borrowing from Latin *homicīdium* "a killing of a person", from *homō* "a human being" and *caedere* "to cut down, to kill" (Lewis & Short).
