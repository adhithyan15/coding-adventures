Waits? (प्रतीक्षते, pratīkṣate.) Follows? (अनुगच्छति, anugacchati.) Goes out? (निर्गच्छति, nirgacchati.) Dries up? (शुष्यति, śuṣyati.) Floats? (प्लवते, plavate.) Begins? (आरभते, ārabhate.) Finishes? (समापयति, samāpayati.) Hands over? (प्रयच्छति, prayacchati.) Covers? (पिदधाति, pidadhāti.) Plants? (आरोपयति, āropayati.) Drives? (चालयति, cālayati.) Embraces? (आलिंगति, āliṅgati.) Kisses? (चुम्बति, cumbati.)
