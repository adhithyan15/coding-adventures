## You'll want to know: mascherarsi
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C319-CARNEVALE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**mascherarsi** — "to dress up in costume".

> Mi sono mascherato da gatto.
>
> *I dressed up as a cat.*

*Mascherarsi da* + a character.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*mascherarsi* is built on *maschera* "mask", from Medieval Latin *masca* (EtymDB).
