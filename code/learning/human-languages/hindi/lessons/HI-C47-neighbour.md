## You'll want to know: पड़ोसी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C47-ROLE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**पड़ोसी** (*paṛosī*) — "a neighbour".

It sits beside *paṛos*, the neighbourhood, and its *-ī* sounds like the ending that makes आदमी out of Adam. The history is a little different. CDIAL derives *paṛosī* from *\*prativeśiya*, a by-form it reconstructs beside Sanskrit *prativeśya*, "neighbour" (CDIAL 8599), so the *-ī* continues that old *-iya*. The place-word *paṛos* comes separately from Sanskrit *prativeśa*, "neighbouring" (CDIAL 8598).

Both are built on *prati-*, "towards, facing", and the verb *viś-*, "to enter, to settle". CDIAL prefers that verb to the older idea of a compound with *veśa*, "dwelling".
