## You'll want to know: chīkuī
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C539-BLAME539-04]; assesses=[] -->

*chīkuī* — "to come off worse; to lose out".

> *rúguǒ nǐ bù tīng wǒ de jiù huì chīkuī* — If you don't listen to me, you'll come off worse.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*chīkuī* is built from 吃 'to eat' and *kuī* 'a loss'. For the first the dictionary has *khek*, glossed 'to eat', under another spelling; the second is inherited from Middle Chinese *khjwe* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'to be lacking': to swallow a loss.
