## You'll want to know: شمل
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C405-ISTITHNA405-03]; assesses=[] -->

**شمل** — *shamila* — "to include".

> تشمل الرحلة زيارة المتحف.
>
> *tashmalu ar-riḥla ziyārat al-matḥaf.*
>
> The trip includes a visit to the museum.

Present: يشمل, *yashmal*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**شمل** (*shamila*) is built on the native Arabic root *sh-m-l*, "to comprise, to cover" (Lane). *Shamila* is a Form I verb, to cover all of a thing: to include.
