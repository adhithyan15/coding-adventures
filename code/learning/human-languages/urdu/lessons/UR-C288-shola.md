## You'll want to know: شعلہ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C288-CHIRAGH288-02]; assesses=[] -->

**شعلہ** — *sholā* — "a flame".

> شعلے چھت تک پہنچے۔
>
> *shole chhat tak pahuṅche.*
>
> The flames reached the roof.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**شعلہ** (*sholā*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *shuʿla*, "a flame". The plural is **شعلے**.
