# મંજૂરી (manjūrī) — approval

## You'll want to know: મંજૂરી
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C354-ADHIKARI354-03]; assesses=[] -->

**મંજૂરી** — *manjūrī* — "approval".

> સરકારે આ યોજનાને મંજૂરી આપી.
>
> *sarkāre ā yojnāne manjūrī āpī.*
>
> The government approved this plan.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**મંજૂરી** (*manjūrī*) is usually traced to Persian *manzūrī*, "approval", from Arabic *manẓūr*, "seen, looked upon with favour".
