Lesson 1 of 5.
цена (tsená) — a price.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a drawing, then the Russian for life.

You'll want to know: цена.
цена (tsená) — "a price".

The word, taken apart.
цена (tsená) from Proto-Slavic cěna — inherited. Vasmer relates it to Lithuanian kainà, "price", Avestan kaēnā-, "retribution", and Greek poinḗ, "penalty", the source of English pain.

You'll want to know: концерт.
концерт (kontsért) — "a concert".

The word, taken apart.
концерт (kontsért) came through German Konzert from Italian concerto, "a contest (of voices)", from Latin concertāre, "to contend"; under Peter the Great it meant "an agreement" (Vasmer).

Lesson 4 of 5.
праздник (prázdnik) — a holiday, a celebration.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for football, then the Russian for a concert.

You'll want to know: праздник.
праздник (prázdnik) — "a holiday, a celebration".

The word, taken apart.
праздник (prázdnik) is built on праздный, "idle, free", which Vasmer calls Church Slavonic, by its -ра-, beside native порожний, "empty": a holiday is a day free of work.

Lesson 5 of 5.
туман (tumán) — fog.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a concert, then the Russian for a holiday.

You'll want to know: туман.
туман (tumán) — "fog".

The word, taken apart.
туман (tumán) comes from Turkic tuman, "fog, gloom", as in Kazakh and Kirghiz, Turkish duman; Vasmer thinks the Turkic words probably go back to Avestan dunman-, "mist".
