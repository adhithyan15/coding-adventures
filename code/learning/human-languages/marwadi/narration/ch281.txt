Marwadi (Marwari), chapter 281: Clay, Wood and Mud.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
चाक (chāk) — a potter's wheel.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for a target, then the Marwadi for to take aim.

You'll want to know: चाक.
चाक — chāk — "a potter's wheel".
चाक घणो भारी है। chāk ghaṇo bhārī hai. The potter's wheel is very heavy.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
चाक (chāk) is inherited from Sanskrit cakra, "a wheel; a potter's wheel"; CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry, but lists Hindi cāk, "any kind of wheel", and Gujarati cāk, "wheel, potter's wheel" (CDIAL 4538).

Lesson 2 of 5.
घुमावणो (ghumāvṇo) — to turn (a wheel); to spin.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for to take aim, then the Marwadi for a potter's wheel.

You'll want to know: घुमावणो.
घुमावणो — ghumāvṇo — "to turn (a wheel); to spin".
काको चाक घुमावै है। kāko chāk ghumāvai hai. My uncle turns the potter's wheel.
Past (I …): म्हैं चाक घुमायो, mhaĩ chāk ghumāyo.

The word, taken apart.
घुमावणो (ghumāvṇo) is the causative of घूमणो, to go round: it is built on that verb and means "to make go round". CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry, but lists Hindi ghumnā, "to revolve", under a reconstructed ghummati (CDIAL 4485).

Lesson 3 of 5.
थापणो (thāpṇo) — to pat into shape.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for a potter's wheel, then the Marwadi for to spin a wheel.

You'll want to know: थापणो.
थापणो — thāpṇo — "to pat into shape".
मां छाणो थापै है। mā̃ chhāṇo thāpai hai. Mother pats out a dung cake.
Past (I …): म्हैं छाणो थाप्यो, mhaĩ chhāṇo thāpyo.

The word, taken apart.
थापणो (thāpṇo) is inherited from a reconstructed thapp, "slap, pat"; CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry, but lists Hindi thāpnā, "to pat", and Gujarati thāpvũ, "to flatten by patting" (CDIAL 6091).
