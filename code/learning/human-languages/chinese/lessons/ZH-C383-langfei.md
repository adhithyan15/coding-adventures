## You'll want to know: làngfèi
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C383-ENERGY383-02]; assesses=[] -->

*làngfèi* — "to waste".

> *xǐzǎo de shíhou bú yào làngfèi shuǐ* — Don't waste water when you have a shower.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*làngfèi* is built from *làng* 'a wave' and *fèi* 'to use up', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *langH* and *phj+jH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'a wave; reckless' and the second 'to spend down'.
