Marathi, chapter 107: True, Green, Yellow, Red, Blue.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
खरे (khare) — true.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for thin, then the Marathi for light.

You'll want to know: खरे.
खरे — khare — "true".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: khare]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: khare, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say khare]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Marathi for thin, then the Marathi for light, then say khare again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does खरे (khare) mean? ("True".) Say it once more.
