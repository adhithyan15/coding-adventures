## You'll want to know: die Braut
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C298-VERLOBUNG-03]; assesses=[] -->

**die Braut** — "the bride".

> Die Braut trug ein langes weißes Kleid.
>
> *The bride wore a long white dress.*

Plural: *die Bräute*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Braut* is an inherited Germanic word, and EtymDB lists English *bride* as its cousin.
