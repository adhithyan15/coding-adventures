## You'll want to know: لنگڑانا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C473-ACHE473-04]; assesses=[] -->

**لنگڑانا** — *laṅgṛānā* — "to limp".

> چوٹ کے بعد وہ لنگڑا رہا تھا۔
>
> *choṭ ke bād voh laṅgṛā rahā thā.*
>
> He was limping after the injury.

Past (he …): وہ لنگڑایا, *voh laṅgṛāyā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**لنگڑانا** (*laṅgṛānā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *lãgṛānā*, "to limp", under *lakka*, "defective", with Sanskrit *laṅga*, "lame" (CDIAL 10877).
