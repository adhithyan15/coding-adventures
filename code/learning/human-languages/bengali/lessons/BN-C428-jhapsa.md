## You'll want to know: ঝাপসা
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C428-ARDRO428-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ঝাপসা** — *jhāpsā* — "hazy; blurred".

> কুয়াশায় দূরের পাহাড় ঝাপসা দেখাল।
>
> *kuyāshāy dūrer pāhāṛ jhāpsā dekhālô.*
>
> In the fog the distant hills looked hazy.

Also of sight: চোখে ঝাপসা দেখা, to see things blurred.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ঝাপসা** (*jhāpsā*) is an expressive word for what cannot be seen clearly; its origin is not settled.
