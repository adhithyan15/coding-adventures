Punjabi, chapter 256: The Postman's Round.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ਡਾਕੀਆ (ḍākīā) — a postman.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for paper, then the Punjabi for to print.

You'll want to know: ਡਾਕੀਆ.
ਡਾਕੀਆ — ḍākīā — "a postman".
ਡਾਕੀਆ ਰੋਜ਼ ਸਾਡੀ ਗਲੀ ਵਿੱਚ ਆਉਂਦਾ ਹੈ। ḍākīā roz sāḍī galī vich āundā hai. The postman comes to our lane every day.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ਡਾਕੀਆ (ḍākīā) is built on ḍāk, "the post", with the ending -īā for the person who does a job; for ḍāk, CDIAL gives no Punjabi form (CDIAL 5519).

What you've built.
One more word for reading notices and messages.

Lesson 3 of 5.
ਪਹੁੰਚਾਉਣਾ (pahunchāuṇā) — to deliver; to take something to a place.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for a postman, then the Punjabi for a parcel.

You'll want to know: ਪਹੁੰਚਾਉਣਾ.
ਪਹੁੰਚਾਉਣਾ — pahunchāuṇā — "to deliver; to take something to a place".
ਡਾਕੀਆ ਚਿੱਠੀਆਂ ਸਾਡੇ ਘਰ ਪਹੁੰਚਾਉਂਦਾ ਹੈ। ḍākīā chiṭṭhīāṁ sāḍe ghar pahunchāundā hai. The postman delivers the letters to our house.
Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਪਾਰਸਲ ਪਹੁੰਚਾਇਆ, maiṁ pārsal pahunchāiā.

The word, taken apart.
ਪਹੁੰਚਾਉਣਾ (pahunchāuṇā) is the causative partner of pahũcṇā, "to arrive"; CDIAL lists Punjabi pahũcṇā under Sanskrit prabhūta, "much, great" (CDIAL 8716).

Lesson 4 of 5.
ਘੱਲਣਾ (ghallṇā) — to send off; to send.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for a parcel, then the Punjabi for to deliver.

You'll want to know: ਘੱਲਣਾ.
ਘੱਲਣਾ — ghallṇā — "to send off; to send".
ਮੈਂ ਹਰ ਮਹੀਨੇ ਪਿੰਡ ਚਿੱਠੀ ਘੱਲਦਾ ਹਾਂ। maiṁ har mahīne piṇḍ chiṭṭhī ghalldā hāṁ. Every month I send a letter to the village.
Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਅਮਨ ਨੂੰ ਪਾਰਸਲ ਘੱਲਿਆ, maiṁ Aman nū̃ pārsal ghalliā.

The word, taken apart.
ਘੱਲਣਾ (ghallṇā) is inherited from a reconstructed ghalyati, "pours"; CDIAL lists Punjabi ghallṇā, "to send", under it (CDIAL 4457).

Lesson 5 of 5.
ਪਾੜਨਾ (pāṛnā) — to tear.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for to deliver, then the Punjabi for to send off.

You'll want to know: ਪਾੜਨਾ.
ਪਾੜਨਾ — pāṛnā — "to tear".
ਲਿਫ਼ਾਫ਼ਾ ਖੋਲ੍ਹੋ ਪਰ ਚਿੱਠੀ ਨਾ ਪਾੜੋ। lifāfā kholho par chiṭṭhī nā pāṛo. Open the envelope, but do not tear the letter.
Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਪੁਰਾਣਾ ਕਾਗ਼ਜ਼ ਪਾੜਿਆ, maiṁ purāṇā kāghaz pāṛiā.

The word, taken apart.
ਪਾੜਨਾ (pāṛnā) is inherited from Sanskrit pāṭayati, "splits"; CDIAL lists Punjabi pāṛnā, "to split, tear", under it (CDIAL 8032).
