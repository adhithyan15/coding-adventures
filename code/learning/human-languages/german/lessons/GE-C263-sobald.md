## You'll want to know: sobald
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C263-NACHDEM-03]; assesses=[] -->

**sobald** — "as soon as".

> Sobald der Regen aufhörte, spielten die Kinder im Garten.
>
> *As soon as the rain stopped, the children played in the garden.*

The verb goes to the end. For the future the present is enough: *Sobald ich Zeit habe, rufe ich an*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*sobald* joins *so* and *bald* "soon". *Bald* is inherited and is a cousin of English *bold* (EtymDB), from an older sense "bold, quick".
