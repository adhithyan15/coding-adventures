# ಸಂತೈಸು (santaisu) — to comfort; to console

## You'll want to know: ಸಂತೈಸು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C337-NIDHANA337-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸಂತೈಸು** — *santaisu* — "to comfort; to console".

> ನೆರೆಯವರು ಅಳುತ್ತಿದ್ದ ಅಜ್ಜಿಯನ್ನು ಸಂತೈಸಿದರು.
>
> *nereyavaru aḷuttidda ajjiyannu santaisidaru.*
>
> The neighbours consoled the grandmother, who was crying.

Past (I …): ಸಂತೈಸಿದೆ, *santaiside*.

The person comforted takes -ಅನ್ನು.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸಂತೈಸು** (*santaisu*) is usually traced to Sanskrit *sāntva*, "consolation, gentle words" (Monier-Williams), with the Kannada verb ending -*isu*.
