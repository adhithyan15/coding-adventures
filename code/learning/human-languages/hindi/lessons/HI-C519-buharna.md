## You'll want to know: बुहारना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C519-DHOOL519-04]; assesses=[] -->

**बुहारना** — *buhārnā* — "to sweep (with a broom)".

> दादी हर सुबह आँगन बुहारती हैं।
>
> *Dādī har subah āṁgan buhārtī haiṁ.*
>
> Grandmother sweeps the courtyard every morning.

Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने कमरा बुहारा, *usne kamrā buhārā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**बुहारना** (*buhārnā*) is a tadbhava: CDIAL files it under Sanskrit *bahukāra*, "effecting much", whose feminine *bahukarī* is "a broom", and gives the Hindi verb as "to sweep" (CDIAL 9188).
