German, chapter 444: Noch mehr Läden.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
der Baumarkt — the DIY store, the hardware store.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for the chemist, then the German for the health food shop.

You'll want to know: der Baumarkt.
der Baumarkt — "the DIY store, the hardware store".
Im Baumarkt kaufen wir Farbe für das Bad. We buy paint for the bathroom at the DIY store.
Plural: die Baumärkte.

The word, taken apart.
Baumarkt is built from Bau 'building', from bauen, inherited from Proto-Germanic būaną (EtymDB), and Markt, from Latin mercātus (EtymDB).

What you've built.

Plural: die Schreibwarengeschäfte. Die Schreibwaren — paper, pens and so on.

The word, taken apart.
Schreibwarengeschäft is built from schreiben 'to write', an early borrowing from Latin scrībere (Lewis & Short), Ware 'goods', from Middle Low German ware (EtymDB), and Geschäft 'shop'.

Lesson 5 of 5.
kleben — to stick, to glue.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for the bookshop, then the German for the flower shop.

You'll want to know: kleben.
kleben — "to stick, to glue".
Sie klebt das kaputte Bild wieder zusammen. She glues the broken picture back together.
Der Kleber — the glue. Das klebt — that is sticky.

The word, taken apart.
kleben is inherited from Old High German klebēn and Proto-Germanic klibjaną 'to stick' (EtymDB). English cleave 'to cling' is a cousin.
