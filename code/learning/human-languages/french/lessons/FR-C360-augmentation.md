## You'll want to know: l'augmentation
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C360-CARRIERE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**l'augmentation** — "the pay rise, the increase".

> J'ai demandé une augmentation à mon patron.
>
> *I asked my boss for a pay rise.*

From *augmenter*. Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*augmentation* comes from Late Latin *augmentātiō* "an increase" (EtymDB), from *augēre* "to increase" (Lewis & Short).
