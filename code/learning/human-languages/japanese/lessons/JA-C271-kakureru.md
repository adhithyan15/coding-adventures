## You'll want to know: かくれる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C271-TSUKAMARU271-04]; assesses=[] -->

**かくれる** — *kakureru* — "to hide (oneself)".

> **はんにんは やまに かくれていました。** — *hannin wa yama ni kakurete imashita* — "The culprit had been hiding in the hills."

Polite **かくれます**, *kakuremasu*; an ichidan verb. Past (polite): **ねこが かくれました。**, *neko ga kakuremashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**かくれる** (*kakureru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict).
