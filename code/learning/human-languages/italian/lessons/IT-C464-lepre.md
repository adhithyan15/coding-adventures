## You'll want to know: la lepre
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C464-SELVATICI-03]; assesses=[] -->

**la lepre** — "the hare".

> Una lepre attraversa il campo.
>
> *A hare is crossing the field.*

Bigger than *il coniglio*, the rabbit.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*lepre* is inherited from Latin *lepus, leporem* 'hare' (EtymDB).
