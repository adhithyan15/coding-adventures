You'll want to know: स्तूपः.
स्तूपः — stūpaḥ — "a stupa; a mound".
पर्वतस्य समीपे प्राचीनः स्तूपः अस्ति। parvatasya samīpe prācīnaḥ stūpaḥ asti. There is an ancient stupa near the hill.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
स्तूपः (stūpaḥ) MW gives stūpa as a knot of hair, a top, and a heap of earth or bricks, especially a Buddhist monument, and reports two old derivations, so its origin is uncertain (MW).

Lesson 3 of 5.

[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a stupa, then the Sanskrit for a rampart.

You'll want to know: अतीतम्.
अतीतम् — atītam — "the past".
इतिहासः अतीतं वर्णयति। itihāsaḥ atītaṃ varṇayati. History describes the past.
A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
अतीतम् (atītam) is built from ati, "beyond", and ita, "gone", from the root √i, "to go"; MW gives atīta as gone by, past, and as a noun the past (MW).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a rampart, then the Sanskrit for the past.

You'll want to know: उत्खनति.
उत्खनति — utkhanati — "digs up; excavates".
वैज्ञानिकाः भूमिम् उत्खनन्ति। vaijñānikāḥ bhūmim utkhananti. The scientists dig up the ground.
Past (he …): सः भूमिम् उत्खातवान्, saḥ bhūmim utkhātavān.

The word, taken apart.
उत्खनति (utkhanati) is built from ud, "up, out", and the root √khan, "to dig", as in khanati; MW gives ut-khan as to dig up or out, excavate (MW).
