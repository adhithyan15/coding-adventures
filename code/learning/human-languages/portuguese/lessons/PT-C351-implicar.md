## You'll want to know: implicar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C351-IMPACTO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**implicar** — "to involve, to entail; to imply".

> Mudar de casa implica muito trabalho.
>
> *Moving house involves a lot of work.*

Informally, *implicar com alguém* — to pick on someone.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*implicar* is a learned borrowing from Latin *implicāre* "to fold into, to entangle" (EtymDB, Lewis & Short), built from *in* and *plicāre* "to fold"; the inherited form of the same Latin verb is *empregar*.
