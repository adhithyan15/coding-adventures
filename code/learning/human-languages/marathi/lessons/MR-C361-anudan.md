## You'll want to know: अनुदान
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C361-ARTHIK361-04]; assesses=[] -->

**अनुदान** — *anudān* — "a subsidy; a grant".

> या शाळेला सरकारकडून अनुदान मिळतं.
>
> *yā śāḷelā sarkārkaḍūn anudān miḷtaṁ.*
>
> This school gets a grant from the government.

Neuter.

Neuter; अनुदानित, aided (school).

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अनुदान** (*anudān*) is a learned word built from Sanskrit *anu-*, "along, after", and *dāna*, "giving" (Monier-Williams).
