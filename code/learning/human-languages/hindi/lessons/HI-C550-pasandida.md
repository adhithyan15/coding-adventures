## You'll want to know: पसंदीदा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C550-CHAYAN550-04]; assesses=[] -->

**पसंदीदा** — *pasandīdā* — "favourite".

> आम मेरा पसंदीदा फल है।
>
> *Ām merā pasandīdā phal hai.*
>
> The mango is my favourite fruit.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पसंदीदा** (*pasandīdā*) is usually traced to Persian *pasandīda*, "approved, chosen".
