Tamil, chapter 357: On the Other Hand.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
எனினும் (eṉiṉum) — nevertheless; however.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for a compromise, then the Tamil for a heated argument.

You'll want to know: எனினும்.
எனினும் — eṉiṉum — "nevertheless; however".
மழை பெய்தது, எனினும் போட்டி நடந்தது. maḻai peytatu, eṉiṉum pōṭṭi naṭantatu. It rained. Nevertheless, the match took place.
A formal written word; the everyday one is இருந்தாலும். It opens the second sentence, with a comma.

The word, taken apart.
எனினும் (eṉiṉum) is native: it is eṉ, "to say" (DEDR 868), with the concessive ending -இனும், "even if": "even if it is said so".

Lesson 2 of 5.
மறுபுறம் (maṟupuṟam) — on the other hand.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for a heated argument, then the Tamil for nevertheless.

You'll want to know: மறுபுறம்.
மறுபுறம் — maṟupuṟam — "on the other hand".
இந்தக் கைப்பேசி மலிவானது, மறுபுறம் இது மெதுவாக வேலை செய்கிறது. intak kaippēci malivāṉatu, maṟupuṟam itu metuvāka vēlai ceykiṟatu. This phone is cheap; on the other hand, it works slowly.
ஒருபுறம் … மறுபுறம் (maṟupuṟam), on the one hand … on the other.

The word, taken apart.
மறுபுறம் (maṟupuṟam) is native: maṟu, "another, other" (DEDR 4766), and puṟam, "side, outside" (DEDR 4333): the other side.

Lesson 3 of 5.
தவிர (tavira) — except; apart from.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for nevertheless, then the Tamil for on the other hand.

You'll want to know: தவிர.
தவிர — tavira — "except; apart from".
ஞாயிறு தவிர எல்லா நாட்களிலும் கடை திறந்திருக்கும். ñāyiṟu tavira ellā nāṭkaḷilum kaṭai tiṟantirukkum. The shop is open every day except Sunday.
It follows the noun: X தவிர (tavira), except X; அதைத் தவிர (tavira), apart from that.

The word, taken apart.
தவிர (tavira) is native Dravidian: it is the infinitive of tavir, "to abstain, leave, avoid" (DEDR 3113), the verb தவிர் taught already: "leaving aside".

Lesson 4 of 5.
அதேபோல் (atēpōl) — likewise; in the same way.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for on the other hand, then the Tamil for except.

You'll want to know: அதேபோல்.
அதேபோல் — atēpōl — "likewise; in the same way".
அவன் கடினமாகப் படித்தான், அதேபோல் அவன் தங்கையும் படித்தாள். avaṉ kaṭiṉamākap paṭittāṉ, atēpōl avaṉ taṅkaiyum paṭittāḷ. He studied hard, and likewise so did his younger sister.
அதே, that same, + போல், like: அதேபோல் (atēpōl) செய், do it the same way.

The word, taken apart.
அதேபோல் (atēpōl) is native: atē, "that very", from atu, "that" (DEDR 1), and pōl, "to resemble; like" (DEDR 4597).

Lesson 5 of 5.
வேறுபடு (vēṟupaṭu) — to differ; to vary.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for except, then the Tamil for likewise.

You'll want to know: வேறுபடு.
வேறுபடு — vēṟupaṭu — "to differ; to vary".
இந்த இரண்டு கருத்துகளும் பல வழிகளில் வேறுபடுகின்றன. inta iraṇṭu karuttukaḷum pala vaḻikaḷil vēṟupaṭukiṉṟaṉa. These two views differ in many ways.
Past (I …): வேறுபட்டேன், vēṟupaṭṭēṉ.
From what: X-இலிருந்து வேறுபடு (vēṟupaṭu). The noun is வேறுபாடு, a difference.

The word, taken apart.
வேறுபடு (vēṟupaṭu) is native Dravidian: DEDR 5548 lists Tamil vēṟu, "other, different", and vēṟu-paṭu, "to become different", with paṭu, "to undergo".
