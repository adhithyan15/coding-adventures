## You'll want to know: चालवणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C399-VAHAK399-03]; assesses=[] -->

**चालवणे** — *cālavṇe* — "to drive; to run".

> माझे काका बस चालवतात.
>
> *mājhe kākā bas cālavtāt.*
>
> My uncle drives a bus.

Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने चालवलं, *tyāne cālavlaṁ*.

Not the same as चालणे, to walk: चालवणे is to make something go, so गाडी चालवणे is to drive and दुकान चालवणे is to run a shop.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**चालवणे** (*cālavṇe*) is built on **चालणे**, "to walk, to go", which CDIAL lists as Marathi *ċālṇẽ* under *\*calyati*, "goes" (CDIAL 4721), as its causative: to make a vehicle or a business go.
