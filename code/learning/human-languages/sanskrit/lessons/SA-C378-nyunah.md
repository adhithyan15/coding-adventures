## You'll want to know: न्यूनः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C378-LESHA378-04]; assesses=[] -->

**न्यूनः** — *nyūnaḥ* — "less; falling short".

> यदि अन्नं न्यूनम्, तर्हि अहं न खादिष्यामि।
>
> *yadi annaṃ nyūnam, tarhi ahaṃ na khādiṣyāmi.*
>
> If there is too little food, I will not eat.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**न्यूनः** (*nyūnaḥ*) is built from *ni* and *ūna*, "wanting, deficient"; MW gives *ny-ūna* "less, diminished, defective, deficient" (MW).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
