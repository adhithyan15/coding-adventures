Hindi, chapter 547: Being Careful.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
चौकन्ना (caukannā) — alert, watchful.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for good fortune, then the Hindi for chance.

You'll want to know: चौकन्ना.
चौकन्ना — caukannā — "alert, watchful".
जंगल में हिरन हमेशा चौकन्ना रहता है। Jaṅgal meṁ hiran hameśā caukannā rahtā hai. In the forest a deer is always on the alert.

The word, taken apart.
चौकन्ना (caukannā) is a tadbhava, inherited from Sanskrit catuṣkarṇa, "heard by four ears" (CDIAL 4630). CDIAL gives the Hindi word as "vigilant, circumspect": all ears.

Lesson 4 of 5.
भनक (bhanak) — an inkling; a whisper (of news).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for safekeeping, then the Hindi for watch.

You'll want to know: भनक.
भनक — bhanak — "an inkling; a whisper (of news)".
मुझे उसकी योजना की भनक लग गई। Mujhe uskī yojnā kī bhanak lag gaī. I got wind of his plan.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
भनक (bhanak) is a tadbhava, inherited from bhan, "hum, buzz", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 9382). CDIAL gives the Hindi word only as "hum, buzz"; the sense "an inkling", news that reaches you as a faint buzz, is a later Hindi use CDIAL does not give.
