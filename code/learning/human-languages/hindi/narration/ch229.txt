You'll want to know: मोड़ना.
मोड़ना — moṛnā — "to bend, to turn".

The word, taken apart.
मोड़ना (moṛnā) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from muṭati, "twists" (CDIAL 10186).

Lesson 3 of 5.
फैलाना (phailānā) — to spread (something).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for to bend, then the Hindi for to gather up.

You'll want to know: फैलाना.
फैलाना — phailānā — "to spread (something)".

The word, taken apart.
फैलाना (phailānā) is the causing form of phailnā, "to spread". CDIAL lists phailnā in two places: under a reconstructed praghirati, "flows forth" (CDIAL 8486), and under Sanskrit prathita, "spread out" (CDIAL 8651).
