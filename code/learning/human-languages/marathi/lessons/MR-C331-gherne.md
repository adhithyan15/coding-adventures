## You'll want to know: घेरणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C331-VEDHA331-02]; assesses=[] -->

**घेरणे** — *gherṇe* — "to surround".

> सैन्याने किल्ल्याला चारी बाजूंनी घेरलं.
>
> *sainyāne killyālā cārī bājūnnī gherlaṁ.*
>
> The army surrounded the fort on all sides.

Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने घेरलं, *tyāne gherlaṁ*.

What is surrounded takes -ला: किल्ल्याला घेरणे.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**घेरणे** (*gherṇe*) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi *gherṇe*, 'to surround', under *\*ghir*, 'go round' (CDIAL 4474).
