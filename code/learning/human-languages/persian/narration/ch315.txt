Persian, chapter 315: Breaking news and the press.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
خبر فوری (khabar-e fowri) — breaking news.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to hold, then the Persian for to take part.

You'll want to know: خبر فوری.
خبر فوری (khabar-e fowri) — "breaking news".
تلویزیون یک خبر فوری پخش کرد. Televiziyon yek khabar-e fowri pakhsh kard. The television broadcast a piece of breaking news.
fowri "urgent", from fowr "at once".

The word, taken apart.
khabar "news" is borrowed from Arabic khabar (EtymDB); fowri "urgent" adds the Persian -i to fowr "at once", usually traced to Arabic fawr.

Lesson 2 of 6.
روزنامه نگار (ruznâme negâr) — a journalist.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to take part, then the Persian for breaking news.

You'll want to know: روزنامه نگار.
روزنامه نگار (ruznâme negâr) — "a journalist".
او بیست سال روزنامه نگار بود. U bist sâl ruznâme negâr bud. She was a journalist for twenty years.
ruznâme "newspaper" + negâr "one who writes or paints", from negâshtan.

The word, taken apart.
ruznâme negâr is built from Persian ruznâme "newspaper" (ruz "day" + nâme "letter") and negâr-, the stem of negâshtan "to write, to paint". All of it is native.

Lesson 3 of 6.
سرمقاله (sarmaqâle) — an editorial, a leading article.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for breaking news, then the Persian for a journalist.

You'll want to know: سرمقاله.
سرمقاله (sarmaqâle) — "an editorial, a leading article".
سرمقاله (sarmaqâle) امروز درباره آموزش بود. Sarmaqâle-ye emruz darbâre-ye âmuzesh bud. Today's editorial was about education.
sar "head" + maqâle "article": the head article of the paper.

The word, taken apart.
sarmaqâle joins native Persian sar "head" and maqâle "an article", which is borrowed from Arabic maqāla, "article, essay" (EtymDB).

Lesson 4 of 6.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a journalist, then the Persian for an editorial.

You'll want to know: تظاهرات.
تظاهرات (tazâhorât) — "a demonstration, a protest march".
دیروز جلوی دانشگاه تظاهرات بود. Diruz jelo-ye dâneshgâh tazâhorât bud. Yesterday there was a demonstration outside the university.
Plural in form, but one event. Built on Arabic taẓāhur "a public show".

The word, taken apart.
tazâhorât is usually traced to Arabic taẓāhurāt, the plural of taẓāhur "a public show"; EtymDB lists no entry.

Lesson 5 of 6.
شعار (sho'âr) — a slogan, a chant.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for an editorial, then the Persian for a demonstration.

You'll want to know: شعار.
شعار (sho'âr) — "a slogan, a chant".
مردم در خیابان شعار میدادند. Mardom dar khiyâbân sho'âr midâdand. People were chanting slogans in the street.
With dâdan: sho'âr dâdan "to chant slogans".

The word, taken apart.
sho'âr is borrowed from Arabic shi'ār (EtymDB lists the Arabic word), usually glossed "a sign, a motto".
