Telugu, chapter 525: Making a Living.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
ఉపాధి (upādhi) — a livelihood; employment.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a skilled person, then the Telugu for initiative.

You'll want to know: ఉపాధి.
ఉపాధి — upādhi — "a livelihood; employment".
పల్లెల్లో యువకులకు ఉపాధి దొరకడం కష్టం. pallellō yuvakulaku upādhi dorakaḍaṁ kaṣṭaṁ. In the villages it is hard for young people to find work.
ఉపాధి (upādhi) అవకాశాలు, job opportunities.

The word, taken apart.
ఉపాధి (upādhi) is borrowed from Sanskrit upādhi (CDIAL 2299), "a point of view, an aim"; in Telugu it means a means of living.

Lesson 3 of 6.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a livelihood, then the Telugu for earnings.

You'll want to know: ఆదాయం.
ఆదాయం — ādāyaṁ — "income".
పంట బాగా పండితే రైతుల ఆదాయం పెరుగుతుంది. paṇṭa bāgā paṇḍitē raitula ādāyaṁ perugutundi. If the harvest is good, the farmers' income rises.
The opposite of ఖర్చు, expense.

The word, taken apart.
ఆదాయం (ādāyaṁ) is usually traced to Sanskrit ādāya, "taking, seizing" (Monier-Williams); compare Sanskrit āya, "arrival; income" (CDIAL 1283).

Lesson 5 of 6.
శ్రమించు (śramiñcu) — to toil; to labour.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for income, then the Telugu for a wage.

You'll want to know: శ్రమించు.
శ్రమించు — śramiñcu — "to toil; to labour".
ఆ పుస్తకం కోసం రచయిత రెండేళ్ళు శ్రమించారు. ā pustakaṁ kōsaṁ racayita reṇḍēḷḷu śramiñcāru. The writer toiled for two years on that book.
Past (I …): శ్రమించాను, śramiñcānu.
The noun is శ్రమ, toil.

The word, taken apart.
శ్రమించు (śramiñcu) is built on Sanskrit śrama, "labour; fatigue" (CDIAL 12682), with the Telugu verb ending -ఇంచు.
