## You'll want to know: વાસવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C417-POL417-05]; assesses=[] -->

**વાસવું** — *vāsvũ* — "to shut (a door)".

> રાત્રે દાદાએ ડેલીનું બારણું વાસ્યું.
>
> *rātre dādāe ḍelīnũ bārṇũ vāsyũ.*
>
> At night grandfather shut the door of the gateway.

Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે બારણું વાસ્યું, *teṇe bārṇũ vāsyũ*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**વાસવું** (*vāsvũ*) No dictionary we use records an older form, so its history is not settled.
