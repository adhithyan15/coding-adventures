# உடனடியாக (uṭaṉaṭiyāka) — immediately

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[TA-LEX-C312-VIRIVAKA312-05, TA-LEX-C313-NERAM313-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Tamil for oral tradition, then the Tamil for meanwhile.

## You'll want to know: உடனடியாக
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C313-NERAM313-02]; assesses=[] -->

**உடனடியாக** — *uṭaṉaṭiyāka* — "immediately".

> தீயணைப்பு வீரர்கள் உடனடியாக வந்தார்கள்.
>
> *tīyaṇaippu vīrarkaḷ uṭaṉaṭiyāka vantārkaḷ.*
>
> The firefighters came immediately.

Stronger and more formal than உடனே.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**உடனடியாக** (*uṭaṉaṭiyāka*) is built on native உடன், "together with, at once" (DEDR 945), with அடி and the adverb ending -ஆக.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
