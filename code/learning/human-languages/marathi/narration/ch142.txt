Marathi, chapter 142: Sparrow, Parrot, Pigeon, Owl, Frog.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
चिमणी (cimṇī) — a sparrow.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a storm, then the Marathi for a crow.

You'll want to know: चिमणी.
चिमणी — cimṇī — "a sparrow".

What you've built.

Guided Practice.

[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: cimṇī, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does चिमणी (cimṇī) mean? ("A sparrow".) Say it once more.
