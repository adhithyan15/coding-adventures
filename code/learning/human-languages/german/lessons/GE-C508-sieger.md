## You'll want to know: der Sieger
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C508-SIEGER-02]; assesses=[] -->

**der Sieger** — "winner".

> Am Ende war mein kleiner Bruder der Sieger.
>
> *In the end my little brother was the winner.*

Plural: *die Sieger*. A woman is *die Siegerin*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Sieger* is built on *siegen* with *-er*; *Sieg* "victory" is inherited from Old High German *sigu*, from Proto-Germanic *segaz* (EtymDB).
