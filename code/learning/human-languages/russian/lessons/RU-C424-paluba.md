## You'll want to know: палуба
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C424-PAROM-02]; assesses=[] -->

**палуба** (*páluba*) — "a deck (of a ship)".

> Вечером все пассажиры сидели на палубе.
>
> *Vécherom vse passazhíry sidéli na pálube.*
>
> In the evening all the passengers sat on deck.

Feminine; the stress is on the first syllable.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

The origin of *палуба* is not settled; EtymDB lists the word without a source.
