## You'll want to know: 假日
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C338-VISIT338-04]; assesses=[] -->

**假日** — *jiàrì* — "holiday; day off".

> **祝你假日快乐** *zhù nǐ jiàrì kuàilè* — Have a happy holiday!

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**假日** (*jiàrì*) is built from 假 'leave' and 日 'a day', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *kaeH* and *nyit* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first in this falling tone 'a holiday from work'.
