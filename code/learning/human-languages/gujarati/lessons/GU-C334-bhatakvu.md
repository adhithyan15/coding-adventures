## You'll want to know: ભટકવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C334-KHAJANO334-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ભટકવું** — *bhaṭakvũ* — "to wander (lost)".

> ચોર ઘણા દિવસ જંગલમાં ભટક્યો.
>
> *cor ghaṇā divas jangalmā̃ bhaṭakyo.*
>
> The thief wandered in the forest for many days.

Past (he …): તે જંગલમાં ભટક્યો, *te jangalmā̃ bhaṭakyo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

is listed by CDIAL under *\*bhaṭ*, "sudden movement or noise", glossed "to blaze, start" (CDIAL 9365), while CDIAL files the Hindi cousin *bhaṭaknā*, "to wander", under Sanskrit *bhraṣṭá*, "fallen" (CDIAL 9655); which line the wandering sense belongs to is not settled. **રખડવું** is to roam; **ભટકવું** is to wander without finding the way.
