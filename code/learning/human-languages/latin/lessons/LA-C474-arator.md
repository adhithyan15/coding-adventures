## You'll want to know: arātor, arātōris
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C474-ARO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**arātor, arātōris** — "a ploughman, a farmer".

> Arātor bovēs ad agrum dūcit.
>
> *The ploughman leads the oxen to the field.*

Third declension, masculine: *arātor, arātōris m.*, from *arō* with *-tor*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*arātor* is built on *arō* 'to plough' with the suffix *-tor* 'one who' (EtymDB; Lewis & Short).
