## You'll want to know: trescientos
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C477-DINERO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**trescientos** — "three hundred".

> El billete cuesta trescientos euros.
>
> *The ticket costs three hundred euros.*

It agrees with the noun: *trescientas personas*.
