## You'll want to know: ಹೆಲಿಕಾಪ್ಟರ್
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C438-TUKA438-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ಹೆಲಿಕಾಪ್ಟರ್** — *helikāpṭar* — "a helicopter".

> ಹೆಲಿಕಾಪ್ಟರ್ ಬೆಟ್ಟದ ಮೇಲೆ ಇಳಿಯಿತು.
>
> *helikāpṭar beṭṭada mēle iḷiyitu.*
>
> The helicopter landed on the hill.

ಇಳಿ, to get down, also means to land.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಹೆಲಿಕಾಪ್ಟರ್** (*helikāpṭar*) is a loan from English *helicopter*.
