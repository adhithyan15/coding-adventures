# దిగులు (digulu) — gloom; low spirits; worry

## You'll want to know: దిగులు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C494-BHAVAM494-01]; assesses=[] -->

**దిగులు** — *digulu* — "gloom; low spirits; worry".

> అమ్మ దూరంగా ఉంటే నాకు దిగులుగా ఉంటుంది.
>
> *amma dūraṅgā uṇṭē nāku digulugā uṇṭundi.*
>
> When mum is far away, I feel low.

Said with the dative: నాకు దిగులుగా ఉంది, I feel low.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**దిగులు** (*digulu*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 3202 lists Telugu *digulu*, "fear, alarm", beside Tamil *tikil*, "fright, terror". In everyday Telugu the fear has softened into a heavy, anxious sadness.
