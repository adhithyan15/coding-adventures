Marwadi (Marwari), chapter 181: Curd and Butter.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
जमावणो (jamāvṇo) — to set curd.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for a hanging net, then the Marwadi for a flat basket.

You'll want to know: जमावणो.
जमावणो — jamāvṇo — "to set curd".
मां रात मांय दही जमावै है। mā̃ rāt mā̃y dahī jamāvai hai. Mother sets the curd at night.
Past (I …): म्हैं दही जमायो, mhaĩ dahī jamāyo.

The word, taken apart.
जमावणो (jamāvṇo) is built on Sanskrit yamyate, "is held fast"; CDIAL lists Old Marwari jamaï, "is collected", and Hindi jamānā (CDIAL 10428). Milk that sets holds fast as curd.

दादी दिनूगै दही बिलोवै है। dādī dinūgai dahī bilovai hai. Grandmother churns the curd early in the morning.

बिलोवणो (bilovṇo) is inherited from Sanskrit vilobhayati, "perplexes, stirs up"; CDIAL lists Old Marwari bilovaṇā, "churnings" (CDIAL 11913a).

Lesson 4 of 5.
गाळणो (gāḷṇo) — to strain.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for to churn, then the Marwadi for a churning stick.

You'll want to know: गाळणो.
गाळणो — gāḷṇo — "to strain".
मां दूध गाळै है। mā̃ dūdh gāḷai hai. Mother strains the milk.
Past (I …): म्हैं पाणी गाळ्यो, mhaĩ pāṇī gāḷyo.

The word, taken apart.
गाळणो (gāḷṇo) is inherited from Sanskrit gālayati, "filters, strains"; CDIAL lists Gujarati gāḷvũ and Hindi gārnā (CDIAL 4144). CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry.
