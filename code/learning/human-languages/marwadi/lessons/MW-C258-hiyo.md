## You'll want to know: हियो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C258-CHINTA258-05]; assesses=[] -->

**हियो** — *hiyo* — "the heart (as the seat of feeling)".

> आज म्हारो हियो राजी है।
>
> *āj mhāro hiyo rājī hai.*
>
> My heart is glad today.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**हियो** (*hiyo*) is inherited from Sanskrit *hṛdaya*, "heart"; CDIAL lists Marwari *hīyo* under it (CDIAL 14152).
