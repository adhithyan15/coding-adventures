Persian, chapter 509: Games.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
پازل (pâzel) — a jigsaw puzzle.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a handicraft, then the Persian for pottery.

You'll want to know: پازل.
پازل (pâzel) — "a jigsaw puzzle".
ما شبها با هم پازل درست میکنیم. mâ shabhâ bâ ham pâzel dorost mikonim. We do jigsaws together in the evenings.
pâzel dorost kardan — to do a jigsaw.

The word, taken apart.
pâzel is a modern loan, usually traced to English puzzle. EtymDB has no entry for it.

Lesson 2 of 5.
تخته نرد (takhte nard) — backgammon.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for pottery, then the Persian for a jigsaw puzzle.

You'll want to know: تخته نرد.
تخته نرد (takhte nard) — "backgammon".
پدربزرگ و پدرم عصرها تخته نرد بازی میکنند. pedarbozorg va pedaram 'asrhâ takhte nard bâzi mikonand. Grandfather and my father play backgammon in the afternoons.
takhte 'board' + nard. Often shortened to takhte.

The word, taken apart.
takhte nard joins inherited takhte, which EtymDB traces to a Middle Persian word for 'tablet, plank, (chess) board' (EtymDB), and nard, the game's own name, whose origin is not settled.

Lesson 3 of 5.
ورق (varaq) — a playing card; a sheet (of paper).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a jigsaw puzzle, then the Persian for backgammon.

You'll want to know: ورق.
ورق (varaq) — "a playing card; a sheet (of paper)".
یک دسته ورق در کیف من بود. yek daste varaq dar kif-e man bud. There was a pack of cards in my bag.
yek daste varaq — a pack of cards. Also a sheet of paper or metal.

The word, taken apart.
varaq comes from Arabic waraq (EtymDB), a word for leaves and sheets of paper; Persian took the sheet and, from it, the playing card.

Lesson 4 of 5.
تاس (tâs) — a die (pl. dice).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for backgammon, then the Persian for a playing card.

You'll want to know: تاس.
تاس (tâs) — "a die (pl. dice)".
او تاس را انداخت و شش آمد. u tâs râ andâkht va shesh âmad. He threw the die and got a six.
tâs andâkhtan or tâs rikhtan — to roll the dice.

The word, taken apart.
tâs is the Persian word for a die. EtymDB's only entry under this spelling is tâs, 'cup', listed without a source, and the origin of the word for a die is not settled.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ورق بازی کردن (varaq bâzi kardan) — to play cards.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a playing card, then the Persian for a die.

You'll want to know: ورق بازی کردن.
ورق بازی کردن (varaq bâzi kardan) — "to play cards".
شب یلدا با فامیل ورق بازی کردیم. shab-e yaldâ bâ fâmil varaq bâzi kardim. On Yalda night we played cards with the family.
varaq 'card' + bâzi kardan 'to play'.

The word, taken apart.
varaq bâzi kardan is built in Persian on varaq, from Arabic waraq (EtymDB), and the native phrase bâzi kardan, 'to play'.
