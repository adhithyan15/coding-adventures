Persian, chapter 529: Readers and Critics.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
نویسنده (nevisande) — a writer, an author.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a publisher, then the Persian for a translator.

You'll want to know: نویسنده.
نویسنده (nevisande) — "a writer, an author".
این نویسنده برای بچه ها داستان مینویسد. in nevisande barâ-ye bachchehâ dâstân minevisad. This writer writes stories for children.
nevis- (stem of neveshtan) + -ande 'one who'.

The word, taken apart.
nevisande is built on native Persian nevis-, the present stem of neveshtan, 'to write', which EtymDB traces to Middle Persian (EtymDB), with the ending -ande, 'one who'.

منتقدها از فیلم تازه او تعریف کردند. montaqedhâ az film-e tâze-ye u ta'rif kardand. Critics praised his new film.
Plural montaqedân or montaqedhâ. naqd is criticism.

The word, taken apart.
montaqed comes from Arabic muntaqid (EtymDB), a word from the same root as naqd, 'criticism'.

Lesson 3 of 5.
نقد کردن (naqd kardan) — to review, to critique.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a writer, then the Persian for a critic.

You'll want to know: نقد کردن.
نقد کردن (naqd kardan) — "to review, to critique".
او فیلمها را در مجله نقد میکند. u filmhâ râ dar majalle naqd mikonad. He reviews films in a magazine.
naqd 'criticism, review' + kardan. The same word naqd also means 'cash'.

The word, taken apart.
naqd is a loan, usually traced to Arabic naqd, 'ready money; testing coins'; testing a coin is usually taken as the picture behind testing a book or a film. EtymDB has no entry for it. kardan is inherited Persian.

Lesson 4 of 5.

Before the new one: say the Persian for a critic, then the Persian for to review.

You'll want to know: مطالعه کردن.
مطالعه کردن (motâle'e kardan) — "to study (closely), to read".
او هر شب قبل از خواب مطالعه میکند. u har shab qabl az khâb motâle'e mikonad. She reads every night before bed.
motâle'e 'study, reading' + kardan. More serious than khândan.

The word, taken apart.
motâle'e is a loan, usually traced to Arabic muṭālaʿa, 'perusal, close reading'. EtymDB has no entry for it. kardan is inherited Persian.

Lesson 5 of 5.
کرم کتاب (kerm-e ketâb) — a bookworm.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to review, then the Persian for to study.

You'll want to know: کرم کتاب.
کرم کتاب (kerm-e ketâb) — "a bookworm".
خواهرم کرم کتاب است و هر هفته یک رمان میخواند. khâharam kerm-e ketâb ast va har hafte yek român mikhânad. My sister is a bookworm and reads a novel every week.
kerm 'worm' + ketâb 'book', as in English.

The word, taken apart.
kerm-e ketâb joins inherited kerm, 'worm', which EtymDB traces to Proto-Iranian and sets beside Lithuanian kirmis, 'worm' (EtymDB), and ketâb, 'book', from Arabic.
