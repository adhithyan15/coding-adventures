## You'll want to know: a batalha
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C329-BATALHA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**a batalha** — "the battle".

> A batalha durou um dia inteiro.
>
> *The battle lasted a whole day.*

Also any hard struggle: *uma batalha contra a doença*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*batalha* comes from Late Latin *battālia* "fighting, fencing" (EtymDB), built on *battuere* "to beat".
