## You'll want to know: کولر
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C439-MALAFE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**کولر** (*kuler*) — "an air conditioner; an air cooler".

> کولر اتاق کار نمیکرد و هوا گرم بود.
>
> *Kuler-e otâq kâr nemikard va havâ garm bud.*
>
> The room's air conditioner wasn't working and it was hot.

In Iran the *kuler-e âbi* cools the air with water; the *kuler-e gâzi* is a true air conditioner.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*kuler* was borrowed from English and is usually traced to English *cooler*. EtymDB does not list the Persian word.
