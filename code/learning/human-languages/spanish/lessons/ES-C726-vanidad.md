## You'll want to know: la vanidad
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C726-HUMILDE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**la vanidad** — "vanity".

> Su vanidad no tiene límites.
>
> *His vanity knows no limits.*

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*vanidad* is usually traced to Latin *vānitās* "emptiness", built on *vānus* "empty".
