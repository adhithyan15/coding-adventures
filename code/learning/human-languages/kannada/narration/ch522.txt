Kannada, chapter 522: Craftspeople.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಕಮ್ಮಾರ (kammāra) — a blacksmith.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for hard work, then the Kannada for rivalry.

You'll want to know: ಕಮ್ಮಾರ.
ಕಮ್ಮಾರ — kammāra — "a blacksmith".
ಕಮ್ಮಾರ ಬೆಂಕಿಯಲ್ಲಿ ಕಬ್ಬಿಣವನ್ನು ಕಾಯಿಸಿದನು. kammāra beṅkiyalli kabbiṇavannu kāyisidanu. The blacksmith heated the iron in the fire.
Plural: ಕಮ್ಮಾರರು, kammāraru.
His forge is the ಕುಲುಮೆ.

The word, taken apart.
ಕಮ್ಮಾರ (kammāra) goes back to Sanskrit karmāra, "blacksmith" (CDIAL 2898), through a Prakrit form in which rm became mm.

Lesson 2 of 5.
ಅಕ್ಕಸಾಲಿಗ (akkasāliga) — a goldsmith.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for rivalry, then the Kannada for a blacksmith.

You'll want to know: ಅಕ್ಕಸಾಲಿಗ.
ಅಕ್ಕಸಾಲಿಗ — akkasāliga — "a goldsmith".
ಅಕ್ಕಸಾಲಿಗ ಮದುವೆಗೆ ಚಿನ್ನದ ಸರ ಮಾಡಿದನು. akkasāliga maduvege cinnada sara māḍidanu. The goldsmith made a gold necklace for the wedding.
Plural: ಅಕ್ಕಸಾಲಿಗರು, akkasāligaru.
The ending -ಇಗ marks the worker, as in many trade names.

The word, taken apart.
ಅಕ್ಕಸಾಲಿಗ (akkasāliga) is the Kannada name of the goldsmith; its origin is not settled.

Lesson 3 of 5.
ನೇಕಾರ (nēkāra) — a weaver.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a blacksmith, then the Kannada for a goldsmith.

You'll want to know: ನೇಕಾರ.
ನೇಕಾರ — nēkāra — "a weaver".
ನೇಕಾರರು ಕೈಮಗ್ಗದಲ್ಲಿ ರೇಷ್ಮೆ ಸೀರೆ ನೇಯುತ್ತಾರೆ. nēkāraru kaimaggadalli rēṣme sīre nēyuttāre. Weavers weave silk saris on hand looms.
Plural: ನೇಕಾರರು, nēkāraru.
The verb is ನೇಯು, to weave.

The word, taken apart.
ನೇಕಾರ (nēkāra) is native Dravidian: DEDR 3745 lists Kannada nēy, nēyu, "to weave", and agent nouns such as nēyikāṟa, "a weaver".

Lesson 4 of 5.
ಶಿಲ್ಪಿ (śilpi) — a sculptor.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a goldsmith, then the Kannada for a weaver.

You'll want to know: ಶಿಲ್ಪಿ.
ಶಿಲ್ಪಿ — śilpi — "a sculptor".
ಶಿಲ್ಪಿ ಕಲ್ಲಿನಲ್ಲಿ ಸುಂದರ ಆನೆಯನ್ನು ಮಾಡಿದನು. śilpi kallinalli sundara āneyannu māḍidanu. The sculptor made a beautiful elephant in stone.
Plural: ಶಿಲ್ಪಿಗಳು, śilpigaḷu.
ಶಿಲ್ಪ is a sculpture.

The word, taken apart.
ಶಿಲ್ಪಿ (śilpi) is borrowed from Sanskrit śilpin, "skilled in art; an artificer" (CDIAL 12471), built on śilpa, "artistic work".

Lesson 5 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: ಕೆತ್ತು.
ಕೆತ್ತು — kettu — "to carve; to engrave".
ಬಡಗಿ ಮರದ ಬಾಗಿಲಿನ ಮೇಲೆ ಹೂವುಗಳನ್ನು ಕೆತ್ತಿದನು. baḍagi marada bāgilina mēle hūvugaḷannu kettidanu. The carpenter carved flowers on the wooden door.
Past (I …): ಕೆತ್ತಿದೆ, kettide.
ಕೆತ್ತನೆ, a carving.

The word, taken apart.
ಕೆತ್ತು (kettu) is native Dravidian: DEDR 1953 lists Kannada kettu, "to pare, chip, engrave, carve".
