## You'll want to know: کوٹنا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C510-CHHALNI510-02]; assesses=[] -->

**کوٹنا** — *kūṭnā* — "to pound".

> دادی نے کالی مرچ کوٹی۔
>
> *dādī ne kālī mirch kūṭī.*
>
> Grandmother pounded the black pepper.

Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے مسالہ کوٹا, *us ne masālā kūṭā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**کوٹنا** (*kūṭnā*) is inherited from Sanskrit *kuṭṭayati*, "pounds, threshes" (CDIAL 3241).
