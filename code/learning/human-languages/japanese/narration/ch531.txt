Japanese, chapter 531: On Purpose or Not.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
わざと (wazato) — on purpose; deliberately.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for about, then the Japanese for (not) at all.

You'll want to know: わざと.
わざと — wazato — "on purpose; deliberately".
もし わざと まけたら、みんな おこるでしょう。 — moshi wazato maketara, minna okoru deshō — "If you lose on purpose, everyone will be angry."

The word, taken apart.
わざと (wazato) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, and usually written in kana; its older origin is not settled (JMdict).

もし あまい ものが あったら、おとうとは つい たべるでしょう。 — moshi amai mono ga attara, otōto wa tsui taberu deshō — "If there were sweet things about, my younger brother would eat them without meaning to."

The word, taken apart.
つい (tsui) is usually counted a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese: JMdict gives this adverb no kanji spelling, and no dictionary we use records an older form, so its history is not settled (JMdict).

Lesson 3 of 5.
むやみに (muyamini) — recklessly; without thinking.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for on purpose, then the Japanese for without meaning to.

You'll want to know: むやみに.
むやみに — muyamini — "recklessly; without thinking".
もし むやみに くすりを のんだら、あぶないでしょう。 — moshi muyamini kusuri o nondara, abunai deshō — "If you take medicine without thinking, it will probably be dangerous."

The word, taken apart.
むやみに (muyamini) is usually counted a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, with the particle ni; JMdict marks it as usually written in kana and its kanji spelling as ateji, characters chosen for their sound, and no dictionary we use records an older form, so its history is not settled (JMdict).

Lesson 4 of 5.
のぞましい (nozomashii) — desirable; best.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for without meaning to, then the Japanese for recklessly.

You'll want to know: のぞましい.
のぞましい — nozomashii — "desirable; best".
のぞましい ほうほうは ばあいに よります。 — nozomashii hōhō wa baai ni yorimasu — "The best method depends on the case."
An i-adjective.

The word, taken apart.
のぞましい (nozomashii) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, built on the same stem as nozomu, "to wish for"; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
