Punjabi, chapter 245: Our Courtyard House.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
ਵਿਹੜਾ (vihṛā) — a courtyard.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for to serve food, then the Punjabi for to mash.

You'll want to know: ਵਿਹੜਾ.
ਵਿਹੜਾ — vihṛā — "a courtyard".
ਸਾਡੇ ਘਰ ਦਾ ਵਿਹੜਾ ਵੱਡਾ ਹੈ। sāḍe ghar dā vihṛā vaḍḍā hai. Our house has a big courtyard.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ਵਿਹੜਾ (vihṛā) is inherited from Sanskrit vēṣṭa, "band, enclosure"; CDIAL lists Punjabi veṛhā, "enclosure, courtyard" (CDIAL 12130).
