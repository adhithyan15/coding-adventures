## You'll want to know: comprometerse
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C547-NOVIOS-03]; assesses=[] -->

**comprometerse** — "to get engaged; to commit oneself".

> Se comprometieron en Navidad.
>
> *They got engaged at Christmas.*

*Comprometerse a* + infinitive is to promise to: *Me comprometo a ayudar.*

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*comprometerse* comes from Latin *comprōmittere* 'to promise each other' (Lewis & Short), built on *com-* and *prōmittere* 'to promise', from *mittere* 'to send'.
