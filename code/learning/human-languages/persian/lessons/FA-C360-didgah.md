## You'll want to know: دیدگاه
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C360-AQIDE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**دیدگاه** (*didgâh*) — "a point of view, a viewpoint".

> از دیدگاه پدرم، این کار خطرناک است.
>
> *az didgâh-e pedaram, in kâr khatarnâk ast.*
>
> From my father's point of view, this is dangerous.

*Az didgâh-e* … — from the point of view of … . Plural *didgâhhâ*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**دیدگاه** is a native Persian compound built from *did* "sight", the past stem of *didan* "to see", and *-gâh* "place". A point of view is the place you look from.
