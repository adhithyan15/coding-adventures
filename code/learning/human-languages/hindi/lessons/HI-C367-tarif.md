# तारीफ़ (tārīf) — praise

## You'll want to know: तारीफ़
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C367-TARIF367-02]; assesses=[] -->

**तारीफ़** — *tārīf* — "praise".

> शिक्षक ने मीरा की तारीफ़ की।
>
> *śikṣak ne Mīrā kī tārīf kī.*
>
> The teacher praised Mira.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**तारीफ़** (*tārīf*) is usually traced to Arabic *taʿrīf*, "making known, description", which reached Hindi through Persian. The sense of praise is a Persian and Hindi one.
