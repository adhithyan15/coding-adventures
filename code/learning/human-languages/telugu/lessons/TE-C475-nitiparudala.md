# నీటిపారుదల (nīṭipārudala) — irrigation

## You'll want to know: నీటిపారుదల
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C475-ANAKATTA475-04]; assesses=[] -->

**నీటిపారుదల** — *nīṭipārudala* — "irrigation".

> ఆ ఆనకట్ట వల్ల నీటిపారుదల సులభమైంది.
>
> *ā ānakaṭṭa valla nīṭipārudala sulabhamaindi.*
>
> Thanks to that dam, irrigation became easy.

నీటి, of water, + పారుదల, flowing: making water flow to the fields.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**నీటిపారుదల** (*nīṭipārudala*) is native Dravidian: నీటి, from నీరు, "water" (DEDR 3690), and పారుదల, "flowing", from పారు, "to run, to flow" (DEDR 3963).
