## You'll want to know: a fraude
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C289-BURLA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**a fraude** — "the fraud".

> A polícia descobriu uma fraude com cartões bancários.
>
> *The police uncovered a fraud with bank cards.*

*Fraude fiscal* — tax fraud.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*fraude* is a learned borrowing from Latin *fraus, fraudis* "deceit, harm" (Lewis & Short).
