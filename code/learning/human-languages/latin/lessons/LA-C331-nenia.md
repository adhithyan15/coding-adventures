## You'll want to know: nēnia, nēniae
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C331-LUCTUS-02]; assesses=[] -->

**nēnia, nēniae** — "a dirge, a funeral song".

> Fēminae nēniam trīstem canēbant.
>
> *The women were singing a sad dirge.*

Feminine, also spelled *naenia*. Hired women sang it in praise of the dead; later also a lullaby or a silly song.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**nēnia** is a word whose origin is not settled (Lewis & Short).
