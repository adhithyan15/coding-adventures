## You'll want to know: شامل
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C419-IJABI419-05]; assesses=[] -->

**شامل** — *shāmil* — "comprehensive".

> قرأت تقريرا شاملا عن المدينة.
>
> *qaraʾtu taqrīran shāmilan ʿan al-madīna.*
>
> I read a comprehensive report about the city.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**شامل** (*shāmil*) is built on the native Arabic root *sh-m-l*, "to comprise, to cover" (Lane). *Shāmil* is the active participle of *shamila*, to include: covering everything, comprehensive.
