# ਡੇਢ (ḍeḍh) — one and a half

## You'll want to know: ਡੇਢ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C260-TIME260-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ਡੇਢ** — *ḍeḍh* — "one and a half".

> ਸਟੇਸ਼ਨ ਘਰ ਤੋਂ ਡੇਢ ਘੰਟਾ ਦੂਰ ਹੈ।
>
> *saṭeshan ghar ton ḍeḍh ghaṇṭā dūr hai.*
>
> The station is an hour and a half from home.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਡੇਢ** (*ḍeḍh*) is inherited from Sanskrit *dvyardha*, "one and a half"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *ḍeḍh*, "one and a half", under it (CDIAL 6698).
