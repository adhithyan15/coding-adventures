Gujarati, chapter 129: Lane, Park, Gate, Pen, Pencil.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ગલી (galī) — a lane.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a library, then the Gujarati for a town square.

You'll want to know: ગલી.
ગલી (galī) — "a lane".

The word, taken apart.
ગલી (galī) matches Hindi galī, "lane", which CDIAL lists under a reconstructed galī, "defile, lane" (CDIAL 4085). The Gujarati word CDIAL lists there is gālī, "valley".
