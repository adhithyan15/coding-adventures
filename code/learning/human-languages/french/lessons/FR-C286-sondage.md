## You'll want to know: le sondage
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C286-CHIFFRES-01]; assesses=[] -->

**le sondage** — "the poll, the survey".

> Un sondage montre que les jeunes lisent moins.
>
> *A poll shows that young people read less.*

*Un sondage d'opinion* — an opinion poll.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*sondage* is built on *sonder* "to sound, to probe", from *sonde* "a sounding line", which is usually traced to Old English *sund* "water, sea"; the origin is not fully settled (EtymDB lists no parent).
