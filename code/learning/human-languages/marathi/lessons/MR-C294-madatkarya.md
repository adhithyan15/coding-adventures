## You'll want to know: मदतकार्य
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C294-PUR294-03]; assesses=[] -->

**मदतकार्य** — *madatkārya* — "relief work".

> पूर ओसरल्यावर मदतकार्य सुरू झालं.
>
> *pūr osralyāvar madatkārya surū jhālaṁ.*
>
> Once the flood went down, relief work began.

Neuter.

Built from मदत, help, and Sanskrit kārya, work.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**मदतकार्य** (*madatkārya*) is a Marathi compound built from *madat*, 'help', a loan usually traced to Arabic *madad*, and Sanskrit *kārya*, 'work, a task' (Monier-Williams).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
