## You'll want to know: संपादक
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C313-PATRAKAR313-04]; assesses=[] -->

**संपादक** — *sampādak* — "an editor".

> संपादक ने ख़बर को दो बार पढ़ा।
>
> *sampādak ne khabar ko do bār paṛhā.*
>
> The editor read the story twice.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**संपादक** (*sampādak*) is Sanskrit *sampādaka*, "one who brings about, who accomplishes" (Monier-Williams). The sense of editor is modern: the one who gets the paper ready.
