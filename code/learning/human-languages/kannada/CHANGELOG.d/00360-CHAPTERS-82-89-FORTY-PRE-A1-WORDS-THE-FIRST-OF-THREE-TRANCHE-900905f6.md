## Chapters 82-89: forty pre-A1 words, the first of three tranches toward pre-A1

With the atom budget fixed in `KA-C08`, vocabulary is Kannada's only pre-A1
blocker. This tranche adds forty headwords on pre-A1 nodes:

| chapters | node | words |
|---|---|---|
| 82-84 | CHECK-WELLBEING | tiredness, a cold, cough, headache, rest; sadness, worry, fear, anger, laughter; thirst, worn out, sweat, hospital, tablet |
| 85-86 | POLITE-REQUEST-REPAIR | slowly, once more, loudly, meaning, write it down; one minute, please wait, show me, I didn't hear, I didn't understand |
| 87 | RESPOND-BASIC | really?, nothing, oh no!, is that so?, the same |
| 88 | COURTESY-THANK | congratulations, invitation, gift, come in, welcome |
| 89 | TAKE-LEAVE | take care, safely, it's late, let's go, holiday |

The tranche is chained like the Telugu, Hindi and Tamil tranches: each word
lesson practises the two before it, and chapter 89 closes with two reviews.
Where a word is built from pieces already taught, the lesson says so:

- ತಲೆನೋವು is ತಲೆ + ನೋವು.
- ಅರ್ಥವಾಗಲಿಲ್ಲ starts with ಅರ್ಥ.
- ಹೋಗೋಣ carries the ‑ಓಣ of ಸಿಗೋಣ.

Every new word uses only glyphs the track already teaches. It avoids ಛ, ಔ and
ಙ, which are still untaught.
