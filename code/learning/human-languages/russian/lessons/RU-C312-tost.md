## You'll want to know: тост
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C312-POZDRAVLYAT-05]; assesses=[] -->

**тост** (*tost*) — "a toast (with drinks)".

> Отец произнёс тост за молодых.
>
> *Otéts proiznyós tost za molodýkh.*
>
> Father proposed a toast to the newlyweds.

*Поднять тост за* + accusative. Also toasted bread.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*тост* was borrowed from English *toast* (EtymDB), from Old French *toster* "to roast", from Latin *tostus* "roasted", the participle of *torrēre* "to parch".
