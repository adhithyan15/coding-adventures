## You'll want to know: اعتبار
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C414-ARZ-02]; assesses=[] -->

**اعتبار** (*e'tebâr*) — "credit; validity".

> اعتبار این کارت تا آخر ماه است.
>
> *e'tebâr-e in kârt tâ âkhar-e mâh ast.*
>
> This card is valid until the end of the month.

Also trust or standing: *e'tebâr dâshtan*. *Kârt-e e'tebâri* — a credit card.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**اعتبار** comes from Arabic *i'tibār* "regard, consideration" (EtymDB): credit is the regard others have for you.
