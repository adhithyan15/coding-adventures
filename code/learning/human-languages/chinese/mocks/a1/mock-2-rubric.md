# Chinese A1 project mock — form 2 scoring rubric

This original rubric applies only to form 2, not an official HSK exam.
Assess Standard Mandarin and simplified characters at a gentle A1 floor.
Administer reading for 17 minutes, listening for 15 minutes, writing for 15
minutes, and individual speaking for 8 minutes after 2 separate preparation
minutes. Play each listening item twice.
No dictionary, pinyin aid, translator, answer bank, or copyable model. Score
four skills separately out of 100; pass needs **60/100 in each skill**.
Never compensate a deficient skill with another skill's points.

## Reading — 100 points

<!-- mock-part:a1-reading-signs-and-form-fields -->
Items 1–10: 5 points for each keyed answer, 0 otherwise. No partial credit.
<!-- /mock-part:a1-reading-signs-and-form-fields -->

<!-- mock-part:a1-reading-short-messages -->
Items 11–20: 5 points for each keyed answer, 0 otherwise. The Chinese message,
not the English choices, is the assessed reading source.
<!-- /mock-part:a1-reading-short-messages -->

## Listening — 100 points

<!-- mock-part:a1-listening-familiar-cues -->
Items 21–30: 5 points for each keyed answer, 0 otherwise. Verify the recorded
cue is 2–10 seconds and played twice at a consistent pace.
<!-- /mock-part:a1-listening-familiar-cues -->

<!-- mock-part:a1-listening-short-exchanges -->
Items 31–40: 5 points for each keyed answer, 0 otherwise. Verify each recorded
exchange is 4–18 seconds and played twice.
<!-- /mock-part:a1-listening-short-exchanges -->

## Writing — 100 points

Require independent production. Never show calibration examples. Accept
comprehensible A1 alternatives, personal names in their usual script, and
minor character slips that preserve meaning. English-only or pinyin-only
answers do not demonstrate Chinese character production.

<!-- mock-part:a1-writing-practical-form -->
Item 41, five fields × 8 = 40 points. For each field, award 2 each for
(1) relevant content, (2) comprehensible and appropriately organised response,
(3) A1 vocabulary/grammar/register, and (4) readable characters and punctuation
or an ordinary personal name. A blank or English-only field scores 0 for that
field. Truthful yes/no answers are equally valid.
<!-- /mock-part:a1-writing-practical-form -->

<!-- mock-part:a1-writing-short-message -->
Item 42, 60 points, four equally weighted dimensions of 15 each: (1) task
fulfilment and the four requested moves, (2) comprehensible, organised
2–4-clause connection, (3) A1 vocabulary/grammar/register, and (4) legible
characters and punctuation. Do not require one wording. Copying the form
above without an independently formed message cannot earn points for missing
connection or task moves.
<!-- /mock-part:a1-writing-short-message -->

## Speaking — 100 points

Record and score the learner independently. Permit one slow repeat without
penalty. Accent variation is acceptable if meaning remains clear. Do not
credit examiner-provided phrases to the learner.

<!-- mock-part:a1-speaking-personal-exchange -->
Items 43–47, 8 points each = 40. Each earns 2 for (1) on-task content,
(2) comprehensible interaction or asking back where requested, (3) A1
vocabulary/grammar/register, and (4) tones, pronunciation and fluency. Target
2–15 seconds per response. Silence is 0.
<!-- /mock-part:a1-speaking-personal-exchange -->

<!-- mock-part:a1-speaking-rehearsed-role-play -->
Item 48, 60 points, four equally weighted dimensions of 15 each: (1) task
content—greeting, university-today question, response and exit question;
(2) comprehensible connected interaction; (3) A1 vocabulary/grammar/register;
and (4) tones, pronunciation and fluency. Target 20–45 seconds. A monologue
cannot earn the interaction points it does not evidence.
<!-- /mock-part:a1-speaking-rehearsed-role-play -->

Two trained raters independently score writing and speaking and retain item
marks and reasons. If a skill total differs by more than 2 points, or one
rater would pass and the other fail, a third rater adjudicates before reporting.
Qualified independent Chinese-language review is required before any
mock-ready claim. Even both mocks passed do not establish learner-proven
readiness without the preregistered book-only pilot required by the assessment
specification. This rubric cannot establish official HSK equivalence.
