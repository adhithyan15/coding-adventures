# ぬれる (nureru) — to get wet

## You'll want to know: ぬれる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C161-LAUNDRY161-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ぬれる** — *nureru* — "to get wet".

> **あめで くつが ぬれます。** — *ame de kutsu ga nuremasu* — "My shoes get wet in the rain."

Polite **ぬれます**, *nuremasu*; an ichidan verb.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ぬれる** (*nureru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi, and its older origin is not settled (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
