## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**घी** (*ghī*) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from *ghṛta*, "clarified butter, ghee" (CDIAL 4501).
