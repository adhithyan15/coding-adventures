## You'll want to know: l'argile
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C517-SCULPTURE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**l'argile** — "clay".

> Les enfants font des petits pots en argile.
>
> *The children are making little pots out of clay.*

Feminine. *La terre glaise* is a close word.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*l'argile* comes from Latin *argilla* (EtymDB), a borrowing from Greek *árgillos* "white clay", related to *argós* "white".
