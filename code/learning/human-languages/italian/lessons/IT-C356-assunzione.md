## You'll want to know: l'assunzione
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C356-CANDIDATURA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**l'assunzione** — "the hiring, the taking on (of staff)".

> L'azienda ha fermato le assunzioni per un anno.
>
> *The company stopped hiring for a year.*

Built on *assumere* "to hire". *Un'assunzione a tempo indeterminato* — a permanent job.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*assunzione* comes from Latin *assūmptiō* "a taking up, an adoption" (EtymDB), from *assūmere* "to take to oneself", built on *sūmere* "to take".
