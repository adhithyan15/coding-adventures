Marathi, chapter 534: Into the Outdoors.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
गिर्यारोहण (giryārohaṇ) — mountaineering.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to bend, then the Marathi for to leap.

You'll want to know: गिर्यारोहण.
गिर्यारोहण — giryārohaṇ — "mountaineering".
माझ्या भावाला गिर्यारोहणाची आवड आहे. mājhyā bhāvālā giryārohaṇācī āvaḍ āhe. My brother loves mountaineering.
Neuter.

The word, taken apart.
गिर्यारोहण (giryārohaṇ) is a tatsama compound of Sanskrit giri, "mountain", and ārohaṇa, "climbing" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 5 of 5.
शेकोटी (śekoṭī) — a campfire; a bonfire.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a camp, then the Marathi for a tent.

You'll want to know: शेकोटी.
शेकोटी — śekoṭī — "a campfire; a bonfire".
रात्री सगळे शेकोटीभोवती बसले. rātrī sagḷe śekoṭībhovtī basle. At night everyone sat around the campfire.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
शेकोटी (śekoṭī) is built on शेकणे, "to warm oneself at a fire": CDIAL lists Marathi śekṇẽ under sēkk, "heat, foment, roast" (CDIAL 13581). The ending -ओटी is not explained there.
