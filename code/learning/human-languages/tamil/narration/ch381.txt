Tamil, chapter 381: One Nation.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
தேசம் (tēcam) — a nation.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for to contribute, then the Tamil for to cooperate.

You'll want to know: தேசம்.
தேசம் — tēcam — "a nation".
அவர் தேசத்துக்காகத் தன் வாழ்க்கையைக் கொடுத்தார். avar tēcattukkākat taṉ vāḻkkaiyaik koṭuttār. He gave his life for the nation.
தேசிய, national: தேசியக் கொடி, the national flag.

The word, taken apart.
தேசம் (tēcam) is borrowed from Sanskrit deśa, "point, region; province, country" (CDIAL 6547). Tamil writes the ś as ச.

Lesson 2 of 5.
அகதி (akati) — a refugee.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for to cooperate, then the Tamil for a nation.

You'll want to know: அகதி.
அகதி — akati — "a refugee".
போரால் பல குடும்பங்கள் அகதிகளாக இங்கே வந்தன. pōrāl pala kuṭumpaṅkaḷ akatikaḷāka iṅkē vantaṉa. Because of the war, many families came here as refugees.
Plural: அகதிகள், akatikaḷ.
அகதியாக, as a refugee.

The word, taken apart.
அகதி (akati) is borrowed from Sanskrit agati, "not going; without resource, helpless" (Monier-Williams): one who has no place to go.

Lesson 3 of 5.
சின்னம் (ciṉṉam) — a symbol; an emblem.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for a nation, then the Tamil for a refugee.

You'll want to know: சின்னம்.
சின்னம் — ciṉṉam — "a symbol; an emblem".
புறா அமைதியின் சின்னம். puṟā amaitiyiṉ ciṉṉam. The dove is a symbol of peace.
X-இன் சின்னம் (ciṉṉam), a symbol of X. In elections, each party has its சின்னம் (ciṉṉam).

The word, taken apart.
சின்னம் (ciṉṉam) is borrowed from Sanskrit cihna, "mark, sign" (CDIAL 4833); Pali has cinha. Tamil reshapes the cluster hn as ன்ன.

Lesson 4 of 5.
ஒன்றிணை (oṉṟiṇai) — to unite; to bring together.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for a refugee, then the Tamil for a symbol.

You'll want to know: ஒன்றிணை.
ஒன்றிணை — oṉṟiṇai — "to unite; to bring together".
அந்தத் தலைவர் மக்களை ஒன்றிணைத்தார். antat talaivar makkaḷai oṉṟiṇaittār. That leader united the people.
Past (I …): ஒன்றிணைத்தேன், oṉṟiṇaittēṉ.
Transitive with -ஐ and the past in -த்த-; people unite with -ந்த-: ஒன்றிணைந்தார்கள்.

The word, taken apart.
ஒன்றிணை (oṉṟiṇai) is native: oṉṟu, "one" (DEDR 990), and iṇai, "to join, unite" (DEDR 457): to join into one.
