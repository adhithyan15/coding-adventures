## You'll want to know: फुप्फुसः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C470-PHUPPHUSA470-01]; assesses=[] -->

**फुप्फुसः** — *phupphusaḥ* — "the lungs".

> धूमः फुप्फुसं पीडयति।
>
> *dhūmaḥ phupphusaṃ pīḍayati.*
>
> Smoke harms the lungs.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**फुप्फुसः** (*phupphusaḥ*) MW records *phupphusa* (Suśruta) and *phuphusa*, "the lungs"; MW gives no derivation, so its origin is uncertain (MW).
