Mandarin Chinese, chapter 317: Looking Back.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
qīngnián — young people; youth.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for life from birth to death, then the Mandarin for as you would naturally expect.

You'll want to know: qīngnián.
qīngnián — "young people; youth".
tā shì yí ge hěn yǒu nénglì de qīngnián — He's a very capable young man.
Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
qīngnián is built from qīng 'green' and 年 'years', both inherited from Middle Chinese, tsheng and nen (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first as the colour of the east: green years.

What you've built.

Lesson 5 of 7.
事先 (shìxiān) — beforehand; in advance.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for a marriage, then the Mandarin for a household.

You'll want to know: 事先.
事先 — shìxiān — "beforehand; in advance".
他又没有事先说我们都生气了 tā yòu méiyǒu shìxiān shuō, wǒmen dōu shēngqì le — Once again he didn't say so beforehand, and we were all cross.

The word, taken apart.
事先 (shìxiān) is built from 事 'a matter' and 先 'before', both inherited from Middle Chinese, dzriH and sen (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'first and after': before the matter.
