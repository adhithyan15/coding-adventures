## You'll want to know: résulter
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C349-INFLUENCE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**résulter** — "to result (from)".

> Cet accident résulte d'une erreur humaine.
>
> *This accident results from human error.*

*Il en résulte que* — the result is that. Mostly in the third person.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*résulter* is a learned borrowing from Latin *resultāre* "to spring back, to rebound" (Lewis & Short), built on *saltāre* "to leap".
