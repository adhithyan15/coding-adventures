Lesson 1 of 5.
пустыня (pustýnya) — a desert.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a rock, a cliff, then the Russian for a wave.

You'll want to know: пустыня.
пустыня (pustýnya) — "a desert".

The word, taken apart.
пустыня (pustýnya) is built on пустой, "empty", Proto-Slavic pustŭ (Vasmer): a desert is the empty land.

The word, taken apart.
пещера (peshchéra) — Church Slavonic, says Vasmer, beside the East Slavic печора; he notes the link with печь, "a stove, an oven". The -щ- against -ч- marks the church form.

Lesson 5 of 5.
осёл (osyól) — a donkey.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a cave, then the Russian for a waterfall.

You'll want to know: осёл.
осёл (osyól) — "a donkey".

The word, taken apart.
осёл (osyól) from Old Russian osĭlŭ, borrowed from Gothic asilus, from Latin asinus, "ass", and fitted to Slavic words in -ĭlŭ such as козёл, "billy goat" (Vasmer).
