## You'll want to know: くばる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C280-KIJI280-05]; assesses=[] -->

**くばる** — *kubaru* — "to hand out; to distribute".

> **えきで しんぶんを くばっていました。** — *eki de shinbun o kubatte imashita* — "They were handing out newspapers at the station."

Polite **くばります**, *kubarimasu*; a godan verb. Past (polite): **こどもが しんぶんを くばりました。**, *kodomo ga shinbun o kubarimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**くばる** (*kubaru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict).
