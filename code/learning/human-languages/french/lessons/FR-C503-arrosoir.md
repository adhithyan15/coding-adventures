## You'll want to know: l'arrosoir
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C503-JARDIN-05]; assesses=[] -->

**l'arrosoir** — "watering can".

> L'enfant remplit l'arrosoir au robinet.
>
> *The child fills the watering can at the tap.*

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*l'arrosoir* is built on *arroser* "to water" with *-oir*, the ending for a tool; *arroser* goes back to Latin *rōs* "dew" (Lewis & Short).
