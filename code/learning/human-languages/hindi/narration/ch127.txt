Hindi, chapter 127: Not at All.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
मालूम नहीं (mālūm nahīṁ) — don't know.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for I don't know, then the Hindi for one more time.

You'll want to know: मालूम नहीं.
मालूम नहीं (mālūm nahīṁ) — "don't know", "no idea".
मालूम is "known". It says what मुझे नहीं पता says, with a different word for knowing.

What you've built.
A second I don't know.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: mālūm nahīṁ]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: mālūm nahīṁ, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say mālūm nahīṁ]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Hindi for I don't know, then the Hindi for one more time, then say mālūm nahīṁ again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does मालूम नहीं (mālūm nahīṁ) mean? ("Don't know".) Say it once more.

Lesson 3 of 7.

You'll want to know: सचमुच.
सचमुच (sacmuc) — "truly, really".
It opens on the सच you know. सचमुच (sacmuc)? — "really?", a stronger सच में?.

Lesson 4 of 7.
अच्छा जी (acchā jī) — is that so?; all right then.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for not at all, then the Hindi for truly.

You'll want to know: अच्छा जी.
अच्छा जी (acchā jī) — "is that so?", or "all right then", by tone.
अच्छा is the "good" you know; alone it already means "oh, I see". जी makes it polite.

What you've built.
A polite way to show you are listening.

Lesson 5 of 7.
चलेगा (calegā) — that'll do, that works.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for truly, then the Hindi for is that so.

You'll want to know: चलेगा.
चलेगा (calegā) — "that'll do", "that works", literally "it will go".
It is चलना, "to go", in the future. Offered a choice, चलेगा (calegā) accepts it without fuss.

What you've built.
Don't know, not at all, truly, is that so, that'll do.

Lesson 6 of 7.
When you are lost, and short answers — a second pass.

Warm-up.
[pause 3 seconds]
You did not understand. Say so, and ask for it once more.

Grammar Lens: two ways to say I don't know.
[a table of 2 rows, read aloud]
phrase: मुझे नहीं पता. literally: to me, no knowing.
phrase: मालूम नहीं (mālūm nahīṁ). literally: not known.
Both keep the knower out of the subject slot, the way Hindi does for wanting.

Guided Practice.
[pause 2 seconds after each]
[your turn — say: ask what someone said; ask for it one more time]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say you don't know, two ways]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say "not at all"; then "truly?"]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: show you are listening politely; accept a plan with "that'll do"]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
Once more? (फिर से.) Didn't understand? (समझ नहीं आया.) What did you say? (क्या कहा?) I don't know? (मुझे नहीं पता.) One more time? (एक बार और.) Don't know? (मालूम नहीं (mālūm nahīṁ).) Not at all? (बिलकुल नहीं (bilkul nahīṁ).) Truly? (सचमुच (sacmuc).) Is that so? (अच्छा जी (acchā jī).) That'll do? (चलेगा (calegā).)


Lesson 7 of 7.
What the body needs, and how you feel — a second pass.

Warm-up.
[pause 3 seconds]
You have walked all afternoon. Say you are hungry, and thirsty.

Grammar Lens: things that come to you.
[a table of 3 rows, read aloud]
phrase: नींद आ रही है. literally: sleep is coming.
phrase: पसीना आ रहा है. literally: sweat is coming.
phrase: मज़ा आया. literally: fun came.
Sleep, sweat and fun all come to you, on आना.

Guided Practice.
[pause 2 seconds after each]
[your turn — say: say you are sleepy; say you have a headache]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say you are sweating a lot]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: ask a nervous friend to stay calm; say you feel restless]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say you don't feel like it today; then that you enjoyed it]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: a friend offers a plan: "is that so?" then "that'll do"]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
Hungry? (भूखा.) Thirsty? (प्यासा.) Sleepy? (नींद आ रही है.) Sweat? (पसीना.) A headache? (सिरदर्द.) Calm? (शांत.) Nervousness? (घबराहट.) Restless? (बेचैन.) Not in the mood? (मन नहीं है.) Fun? (मज़ा.)
