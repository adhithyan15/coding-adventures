## You'll want to know: گھمانا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C397-FASLA397-05]; assesses=[] -->

**گھمانا** — *ghumānā* — "to show (someone) around; to turn round".

> ایک دوست نے ہمیں شہر گھمایا۔
>
> *ek dost ne hameṅ shahr ghumāyā.*
>
> A friend showed us round the city.

Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے چابی گھمائی, *us ne chābī ghumāī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**گھمانا** (*ghumānā*) is built on **گھومنا**, "to go round", which comes from *ghummati*, "revolves", a form CDIAL reconstructs (CDIAL 4485): it is the form for making something, or someone, go round.
