## You'll want to know: क्षिप्रम्
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C367-SAMBHAVA367-04]; assesses=[] -->

**क्षिप्रम्** — *kṣipram* — "swiftly; without delay".

> यदि वायुः वहति, तर्हि क्षिप्रं वर्षा भविष्यति।
>
> *yadi vāyuḥ vahati, tarhi kṣipraṃ varṣā bhaviṣyati.*
>
> If the wind blows, rain will come swiftly.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**क्षिप्रम्** (*kṣipram*) comes from the root √kṣip, "to throw" (CDIAL 3688 sets *kṣiprá* under it); MW gives *kṣipra* "springing" and "quick, speedy, swift", and *kṣipram* "quickly, immediately" (MW).
