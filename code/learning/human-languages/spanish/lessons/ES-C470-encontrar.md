# encontrar — to find

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-BUSCAR, ES-LEX-C470-PARAGUAS-02] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say **buscar**. You have had the looking for a long time. Say **el
mango**. You still have no word for the moment the looking succeeds.

## You'll want to know: encontrar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C470-PARAGUAS-03]; assesses=[ES-LEX-BUSCAR] -->

**encontrar** — to find.

> No lo **encuentro** por ninguna parte.

*Buscar* and *encontrar* are the two halves of one search, and Spanish keeps
them strictly apart:

| what you do | what happens |
|---|---|
| **buscar** — look for | the effort |
| **encontrar** — find | the result |

*Busco mi paraguas* is work in progress. *He encontrado mi paraguas* is over.
You can *buscar* all afternoon and never *encontrar*.

## Grammar Lens: the o that breaks
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-C470-PARAGUAS-03, ES-LEX-C451-TURNO-04] -->

You wrote *encuentro* above, not *encontro*. That is
[mostrar](./ES-C451-mostrar.md)'s rule arriving again, unchanged: push the
stress back onto the stem and the **o** breaks into **ue**.

| stress on the stem | stress on the ending |
|---|---|
| enc**ue**ntro, enc**ue**ntras | encontr**a**mos |
| enc**ue**ntra, enc**ue**ntran | encontr**á**is |

The infinitive keeps its **o** because the stress is on the ending there —
*encon-TRAR*. Nothing about the verb is irregular once you know where the
weight falls.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-C470-PARAGUAS-03] -->

**en-** plus **contra**: to come up **against** something.

That is the whole picture, and it is more honest than the English word.
*Find* says nothing about how. *Encontrar* says you went along and the thing
was in your way.

English has the same word by a different road: **encounter**, borrowed from
French, is *in* plus *contra* exactly. To encounter somebody is to come
against them — which is why an encounter can be a meeting or a fight, and
*un encuentro* in Spanish is both as well.

So *encontrarse con alguien* is to run into somebody. The *contra* has not
gone anywhere.
