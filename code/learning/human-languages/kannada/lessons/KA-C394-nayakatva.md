## You'll want to know: ನಾಯಕತ್ವ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C394-RAJAKIYA394-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ನಾಯಕತ್ವ** — *nāyakatva* — "leadership".

> ಅವಳ ನಾಯಕತ್ವದಲ್ಲಿ ತಂಡ ಗೆದ್ದಿತು.
>
> *avaḷa nāyakatvadalli taṇḍa gedditu.*
>
> Under her leadership the team won.

Built on the Sanskrit word for a leader, with -ತ್ವ, -ship.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ನಾಯಕತ್ವ** (*nāyakatva*) is built on Sanskrit *nāyaka*, "a guide, a leader" (Monier-Williams), with -*tva*, "-ship".
