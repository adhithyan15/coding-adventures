## You'll want to know: उफाळणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C385-CHIGHALNE385-02]; assesses=[] -->

**उफाळणे** — *uphāḷṇe* — "to surge up; to flare up".

> जुन्या गोष्टींवरून पुन्हा भांडण उफाळलं.
>
> *junyā goṣṭīṁvarūn punhā bhāṇḍaṇ uphāḷlaṁ.*
>
> The quarrel flared up again over old matters.

Past (he …): तो उफाळला, *to uphāḷlā*.

Of flames, boiling water and feelings.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**उफाळणे** (*uphāḷṇe*) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Marathi *uphāḷṇẽ*, "to boil or bubble up, rise (of flame)", under Sanskrit *utphalati*, "springs open, bursts" (CDIAL 1837).
