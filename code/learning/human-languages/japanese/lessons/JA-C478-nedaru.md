## You'll want to know: ねだる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C478-GROW478-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ねだる** — *nedaru* — "to pester for; to beg for".

> **すえっこが おかしを ねだっています。** — *suekko ga okashi o nedatte imasu* — "The youngest child is pestering for sweets."

Polite **ねだります**, *nedarimasu*; a godan verb. Past (polite): **むすこが おかしを ねだりました。**, *musuko ga okashi o nedarimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ねだる** (*nedaru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese. JMdict marks it as usually written in kana; its kanji spelling borrows characters meaning "strong" and "ask", chosen for the meaning and not the sound (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
