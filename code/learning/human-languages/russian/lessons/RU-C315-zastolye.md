## You'll want to know: застолье
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C315-UGOSHCHENIYE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**застолье** (*zastólye*) — "a feast, a long festive meal".

> Застолье продолжалось до утра.
>
> *Zastólye prodolzhálos do utrá.*
>
> The feast went on until morning.

Neuter.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*застолье* is built on *за* "at, behind" and *стол* "table", inherited from Proto-Slavic *stolŭ* (EtymDB): sitting at table.
