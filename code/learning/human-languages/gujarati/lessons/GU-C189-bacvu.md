# બચવું (bacvũ) — to be left over, to be spared

## You'll want to know: બચવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C189-ORDER189-04]; assesses=[] -->

**બચવું** — *bacvũ* — "to be left over, to be spared".

> **મારી પાસે દસ રૂપિયા બચે છે.** — *mārī pāse das rūpiyā bace che* — "I have ten rupees left over."

**બચાવવું** saves a thing; **બચવું** is what is left or spared.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**બચવું** (*bacvũ*) goes back to Sanskrit *váñcati*, "moves; escapes from", but CDIAL marks the Gujarati verb, glossed "to escape", as borrowed from Hindi *bacnā*, "to escape"; the taught *bacāvvũ*, "to save", is its causative (CDIAL 11208).
