## You'll want to know: 春天
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C131-SEASON131-01]; assesses=[] -->

**春天** — *chūntiān* — "spring".

> **我喜欢春天** *wǒ xǐhuan chūntiān* — I love spring.

The new character, in pieces: 春 — the three bars of 三, a 人 whose two strokes spread across them, and 日 underneath. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**春天** (*chūntiān*) is built from 春 'spring' and 天 'sky, day', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *tsyhwin* and *then* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription); the dictionary glosses the first 'the head of the four seasons'.
