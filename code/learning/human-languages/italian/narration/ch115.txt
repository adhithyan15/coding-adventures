Italian, chapter 115: Homework, Answer, Problem, Sentence, Page.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
i compiti — homework.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for a board, then the Italian for a rubber.
[pause 3 seconds]
[once you have stopped driving — read: the two lines aloud, as one run — Il cinema è dietro. Il teatro è davanti.]

You'll want to know: i compiti.
i compiti — "homework".

The word, taken apart.
compiti is the plural of compito, "a task" from Latin computus, "a reckoning" — inherited. (EtymDB 2.1).

You'll want to know: la risposta.
la risposta — "an answer".

The word, taken apart.
risposta is "a thing answered": a form of rispondere, "to answer", used as a noun. rispondere from Latin respondere, "to reply". (EtymDB 2.1).
