Bengali, chapter 516: Free Time.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
বিনোদন (binodôn) — entertainment.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for leftover from a meal, then the Bengali for to rinse after a meal.

You'll want to know: বিনোদন.
বিনোদন — binodôn — "entertainment".
আমার বিনোদন বই পড়া। Āmār binodôn bôi pôṛā. Reading books is my entertainment.

The word, taken apart.
বিনোদন (binodôn) is a tatsama, Sanskrit vinodana, "diversion, pastime" (Monier-Williams).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for spare time, then the Bengali for cycling.

You'll want to know: মগ্ন.
মগ্ন — môgnô — "absorbed (in something)".
মেয়েটা গল্পের বইয়ে মগ্ন ছিল। Meyeṭā gôlper bôiye môgnô chhilô. The girl was absorbed in a storybook.

The word, taken apart.
মগ্ন (môgnô) is a tatsama, Sanskrit magna, "sunk, plunged", from majj, "to sink" (Monier-Williams): sunk deep in what you are doing.
