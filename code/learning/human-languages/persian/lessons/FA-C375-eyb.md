## You'll want to know: عیب
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C375-MAZIYYAT-02]; assesses=[] -->

**عیب** (*'eyb*) — "a flaw, a defect".

> تنها عیب این ماشین رنگ آن است.
>
> *tanhâ 'eyb-e in mâshin rang-e ân ast.*
>
> The only flaw of this car is its colour.

*'Eyb nadârad* — never mind, it doesn't matter. Plural *'oyub*, an Arabic broken plural.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**عیب** is usually traced to Arabic *'ayb* "fault, blemish"; in Persian it is a flaw in a thing or in a person.
