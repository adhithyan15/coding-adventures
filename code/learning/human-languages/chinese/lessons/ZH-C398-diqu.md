## You'll want to know: 地区
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C398-DEPEND398-03]; assesses=[] -->

**地区** — *dìqū* — "region; area (of a country)".

> **这个地区有一百万人口** *zhège dìqū yǒu yìbǎi wàn rénkǒu* — This region has a million people.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**地区** (*dìqū*) is built from 地 'the ground' and 区 'an area', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *dijH* and *khju* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'the land'.
