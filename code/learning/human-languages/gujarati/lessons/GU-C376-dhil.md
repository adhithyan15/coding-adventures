## You'll want to know: ઢીલ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C376-BHUL376-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ઢીલ** — *ḍhīl* — "slackness; a delay".

> જો તમે ઢીલ કરશો તો ટ્રેન છૂટી જશે.
>
> *jo tame ḍhīl karśo to ṭren chūṭī jaśe.*
>
> If you dawdle, you will miss the train.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ઢીલ** (*ḍhīl*) CDIAL lists Gujarati *ḍhīl*, "loose, slack", under a reconstructed *ḍhila* (CDIAL 5590); "a delay", slack in doing a thing, is the noun's sense.
