# el deporte — recreation that became sport

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-GIMNASIO] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Recall the place for exercise: **el gimnasio**. What you may do
there is **deporte**, sport.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-DEPORTE]; assesses=[] -->

**Deporte** belongs to an older **deportar** family that once meant recreation
or amusement. English **sport** hides a useful cousin: it is the shortened form
of **disport**, which came through French from the same Latin **deportāre**
family. The histories narrowed toward organised physical activity.

## Grammar Lens: hacer deporte
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-DEPORTE] -->

Spanish commonly says **hacer deporte**, literally “to do sport,” for taking
exercise. No article is needed in that general expression. Compare **el
deporte es importante**, where the noun is the subject and keeps **el**.
