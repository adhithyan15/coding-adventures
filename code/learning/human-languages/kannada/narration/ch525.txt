Lesson 1 of 6.
ಭಾವ (bhāva) — a brother-in-law.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for mother's elder sister, then the Kannada for a nephew.

You'll want to know: ಭಾವ.
ಭಾವ — bhāva — "a brother-in-law".
ನನ್ನ ಅಕ್ಕನ ಗಂಡ, ಅಂದರೆ ನನ್ನ ಭಾವ, ವೈದ್ಯರು. nanna akkana gaṇḍa, andare nanna bhāva, vaidyaru. My elder sister's husband, that is, my brother-in-law, is a doctor.
An elder sister's husband, or a husband's elder brother.

The word, taken apart.
ಭಾವ (bhāva), the kinship word, is usually traced to Sanskrit bhāvuka, "a sister's husband" (Monier-Williams). It is a different word from bhāva, "being, feeling".

Lesson 4 of 6.
ಸೋದರಸಂಬಂಧಿ (sōdarasambandhi) — a cousin.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for husband's sister, then the Kannada for husband's younger brother.

You'll want to know: ಸೋದರಸಂಬಂಧಿ.
ಸೋದರಸಂಬಂಧಿ — sōdarasambandhi — "a cousin".
ಮದುವೆಯಲ್ಲಿ ನನ್ನ ಎಲ್ಲಾ ಸೋದರಸಂಬಂಧಿಗಳನ್ನು ನೋಡಿದೆ. maduveyalli nanna ellā sōdarasambandhigaḷannu nōḍide. I saw all my cousins at the wedding.
Plural: ಸೋದರಸಂಬಂಧಿಗಳು, sōdarasambandhigaḷu.
Everyday talk often names the exact tie instead: ಮಾವನ ಮಗ, an uncle's son.

The word, taken apart.
ಸೋದರಸಂಬಂಧಿ (sōdarasambandhi) is a learned compound built from Sanskrit sodara, "born of the same womb", and sambandha, "close connection; a relative" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 5 of 6.
ಸಹೋದರ (sahōdara) — a brother.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for husband's younger brother, then the Kannada for a cousin.

You'll want to know: ಸಹೋದರ.
ಸಹೋದರ — sahōdara — "a brother".
ನನಗೆ ಇಬ್ಬರು ಸಹೋದರರು ಇದ್ದಾರೆ. nanage ibbaru sahōdararu iddāre. I have two brothers.
Plural: ಸಹೋದರರು, sahōdararu.
Formal for ಅಣ್ಣ or ತಮ್ಮ; a sister is ಸಹೋದರಿ.

The word, taken apart.
ಸಹೋದರ (sahōdara) is borrowed from Sanskrit sahodara, "co-uterine, born of the same womb" (Monier-Williams): saha, "with", and udara, "womb".
