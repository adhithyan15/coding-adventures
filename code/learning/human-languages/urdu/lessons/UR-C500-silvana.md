## You'll want to know: سلوانا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C500-SILAI500-02]; assesses=[] -->

**سلوانا** — *silvānā* — "to have (clothes) sewn".

> میں نے درزی سے نیا کرتا سلوایا۔
>
> *maiṅ ne darzī se nayā kurtā silvāyā.*
>
> I had a new kurta made by the tailor.

Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے نئی شلوار سلوائی, *us ne naī shalvār silvāī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**سلوانا** (*silvānā*) is the second causative of *sīnā*, "to sew", which CDIAL lists under Sanskrit *sīvyati*, "sews" (CDIAL 13444): to have someone sew.
