Telugu, chapter 101: Above, Below, Inside, Outside, Behind.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
పైన (paina) — above, on top.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for near, then the Telugu for far; distance.

You'll want to know: పైన.
పైన — paina — "above, on top".

The word, taken apart.
పైన (paina) is native Dravidian: pai, "top; on, above", one word with Kannada pai, "upper" (DEDR 4451), with the ending -na.
