## You'll want to know: mǎnyì
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C252-FEEL252-05]; assesses=[] -->

*mǎnyì* — "satisfied; pleased".

> *nǐ mǎnyì ma* — Are you happy with it?

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*mǎnyì* is built from *mǎn* 'full' and *yì* 'intent', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *manX* and *'iH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'full' and the second 'will'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing things.
