Marathi, chapter 432: Holes and Stains.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
भोक (bhok) — a hole.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to burst, then the Marathi for to rip.

You'll want to know: भोक.
भोक — bhok — "a hole".
माझ्या खिशाला भोक पडलं आहे. mājhyā khiśālā bhok paḍlaṁ āhe. There is a hole in my pocket.
Neuter.
भोक (bhok) पडणे, a hole appears; the thing with the hole takes -ला.

The word, taken apart.
भोक (bhok) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi bhok, "hole", under bhōkka, "hollow" (CDIAL 9624).

Lesson 2 of 5.
छिद्र (chidra) — a small hole; a pore.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to rip, then the Marathi for a hole.

You'll want to know: छिद्र.
छिद्र — chidra — "a small hole; a pore".
बादलीच्या तळाला एक छिद्र आहे. bādlīcyā taḷālā ek chidra āhe. There is a small hole in the bottom of the bucket.
Neuter.
More formal and smaller than भोक (bhok).

The word, taken apart.
छिद्र (chidra) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit chidra, "torn open; a hole" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 3 of 5.
डाग (ḍāg) — a stain; a spot.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a hole, then the Marathi for a small hole.

You'll want to know: डाग.
डाग — ḍāg — "a stain; a spot".
शर्टवर चहाचा डाग पडला. śarṭvar cahācā ḍāg paḍlā. A tea stain got on the shirt.
Masculine.
डाग (ḍāg) पडणे, a stain appears; डाग (ḍāg) काढणे, to remove one.

The word, taken apart.
डाग (ḍāg) is a loan, usually traced to Persian dāġ, "a brand, a mark".

[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a small hole, then the Marathi for a stain.

You'll want to know: विस्कटणे.
विस्कटणे — viskaṭṇe — "to get messed up".
धावताना तिचे केस विस्कटले. dhāvtānā tice kes viskaṭle. Her hair got messed up as she ran.
Past (he …): तो विस्कटला, to viskaṭlā.
Hair, papers and plans can all विस्कटणे (viskaṭṇe).

The word, taken apart.
विस्कटणे (viskaṭṇe) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi viskaṭṇẽ, "to disorder, derange", under viṣvaskati, "goes astray" (CDIAL 11995).

Lesson 5 of 5.
पाझरणे (pājharṇe) — to ooze.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a stain, then the Marathi for to get messed up.

You'll want to know: पाझरणे.
पाझरणे — pājharṇe — "to ooze".
भिंतीतून पाणी पाझरत होतं. bhintītūn pāṇī pājhrat hotaṁ. Water was oozing through the wall.
Past (he …): तो पाझरला, to pājharlā.
The source takes -तून: भिंतीतून, through the wall.

The word, taken apart.
पाझरणे (pājharṇe) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi pājharṇẽ, "to drip down", under prajjharati, "flows" (CDIAL 8512).
