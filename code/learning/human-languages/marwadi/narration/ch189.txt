Marwadi (Marwari), chapter 189: Walls and Doorways.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
थांभो (thā̃bho) — a pillar.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for a hut, then the Marwadi for a thatched shelter.

You'll want to know: थांभो.
थांभो — thā̃bho — "a pillar".
थांभो लांबो है। thā̃bho lā̃bo hai. The pillar is tall.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
थांभो (thā̃bho) is inherited from Sanskrit stambha, "a pillar, a post"; CDIAL lists Old Marwari thāma, "a pillar" (CDIAL 13682).

Lesson 3 of 5.
चूनो (chūno) — lime (for whitewash).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for a pillar, then the Marwadi for a brick.

You'll want to know: चूनो.
चूनो — chūno — "lime (for whitewash)".
भींत माथै नुवो चूनो है। bhī̃t māthai nuvo chūno hai. There's fresh whitewash on the wall.

चूनो (chūno) is inherited from Sanskrit cūrṇa, "powder"; CDIAL lists Hindi cūnā, "lime", and an Old Marwari form meaning "flour" (CDIAL 4889).

Lesson 5 of 5.
चौबारो (chaubāro) — an open-sided room; a room open on all sides.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for whitewash, then the Marwadi for a threshold.

You'll want to know: चौबारो.
चौबारो — chaubāro — "an open-sided room; a room open on all sides".
चौबारो बड़ो है। chaubāro baṛo hai. The open room is big.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
चौबारो (chaubāro) is built from Sanskrit catur, "four", and dvāra, "door"; CDIAL lists Hindi caubārā and Sindhi caubāro, a room or house open on four sides (CDIAL 4611). CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry.
