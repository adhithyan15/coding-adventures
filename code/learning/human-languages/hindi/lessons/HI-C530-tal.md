## You'll want to know: ताल
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C530-SANGEET530-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ताल** — *tāl* — "a beat; a rhythmic cycle".

> मीरा ताल के साथ हाथ हिला रही है।
>
> *Mīrā tāl ke sāth hāth hilā rahī hai.*
>
> Mira is moving her hand with the beat.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ताल** (*tāl*) is a tatsama, taken whole from Sanskrit *tāla*, "clapping the hands; musical time or measure" (Monier-Williams).
