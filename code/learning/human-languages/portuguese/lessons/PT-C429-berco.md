## You'll want to know: o berço
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C429-QUARTO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**o berço** — "the cot, the cradle".

> Pedimos um berço para o bebé.
>
> *We asked for a cot for the baby.*

*Nascer em berço de ouro* — to be born with a silver spoon in your mouth.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*berço* comes from Vulgar Latin *berciolum* 'cradle' (EtymDB), probably from a Gaulish word; the details are not settled.
