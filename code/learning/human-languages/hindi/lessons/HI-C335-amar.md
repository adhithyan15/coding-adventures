## You'll want to know: अमर
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C335-DEVTA335-04]; assesses=[] -->

**अमर** — *amar* — "immortal".

> वरदान से राक्षस अमर हो गया।
>
> *vardān se rākṣas amar ho gayā.*
>
> The boon made the demon immortal.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अमर** (*amar*) is Sanskrit *amara*, "undying" (Monier-Williams), *a-*, not, and the family of *mṛ*, to die, the root of **मरना**.
