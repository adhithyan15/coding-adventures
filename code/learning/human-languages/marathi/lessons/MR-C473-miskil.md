## You'll want to know: मिश्किल
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C473-KHODKAR473-02]; assesses=[] -->

**मिश्किल** — *miśkil* — "impish; playful".

> आजोबा मिश्किल हसले.
>
> *ājobā miśkil hasle.*
>
> Grandfather smiled impishly.

Of a smile or a remark with a twinkle: मिश्किल नजर.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**मिश्किल** (*miśkil*) is a Marathi adjective whose origin is not settled.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
