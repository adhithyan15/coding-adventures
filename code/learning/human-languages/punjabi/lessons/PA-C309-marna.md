## You'll want to know: ਮਰਨਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C309-NEWS309-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ਮਰਨਾ** — *marnā* — "to die".

> ਪਿਛਲੇ ਹਫ਼ਤੇ ਸਾਡਾ ਕੁੱਤਾ ਮਰਿਆ ਅਤੇ ਬੱਚੇ ਬਹੁਤ ਰੋਏ।
>
> *pichhle hafte sāḍā kuttā mariā ate bachche bahut roe.*
>
> Our dog died last week, and the children cried a lot.

Past (it …): ਉਹ ਬਿੱਲੀ ਸਰਦੀ ਵਿੱਚ ਮਰੀ, *oh billī sardī vich marī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਮਰਨਾ** (*marnā*) is inherited from Sanskrit *marate*, "will die"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *marnā*, "dies", under it (CDIAL 9871).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happened.
