## You'll want to know: रोपणो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C238-SOW238-03]; assesses=[] -->

**रोपणो** — *ropṇo* — "to plant (a seedling or a tree)".

> हूं काल खेत कनै रूंख रोपसूं।
>
> *hū̃ kāl khet kanai rū̃kh ropsū̃.*
>
> Tomorrow I'll plant a tree by the field.

Past (I …): म्हैं रूंख रोप्यो, *mhaĩ rū̃kh ropyo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**रोपणो** (*ropṇo*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*rupati*, "makes a hole, plants", beside Sanskrit *ropayati*, "plants"; CDIAL lists the Old Marwari verb *ropaï*, "plants" (CDIAL 10783).
