## You'll want to know: سہنا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C458-PATIENCE458-05]; assesses=[] -->

**سہنا** — *sahnā* — "to bear; to suffer patiently".

> دادی نے بڑا دکھ سہا۔
>
> *dādī ne baṛā dukh sahā.*
>
> My grandmother bore great sorrow.

Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے غم سہا, *us ne gham sahā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**سہنا** (*sahnā*) is inherited from Sanskrit *sáhatē*, "bears, suffers" (CDIAL 13304).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
