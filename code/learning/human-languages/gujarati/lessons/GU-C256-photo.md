## You'll want to know: ફોટો
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C256-PAINT256-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ફોટો** — *phoṭo* — "photo".

> **મેં દરિયાનો ફોટો લીધો.** — *mẽ dariyāno phoṭo līdho* — "I took a photo of the sea."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
