Arabic, chapter 306: A wedding.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
عرس (ʿurs) — a wedding feast, a wedding.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a defendant, then the Arabic for to pass judgment.

You'll want to know: عرس.
عرس — ʿurs — "a wedding feast, a wedding".
حضرنا عرس ابن عمي في القرية. ḥaḍarnā ʿurs ibn ʿammī fī al-qarya. We went to my cousin’s wedding in the village.
Plural: أعراس, aʿrās.
The taught عروس, bride, and عريس, groom, are the same root.

The word, taken apart.
عرس (ʿurs) is built on the native Arabic root ʿ-r-s (Lane), the root of the taught ʿarūs, a bride, and ʿarīs, a groom. ʿUrs is the feast held for them.

Lesson 2 of 5.
خطب (khaṭaba) — to propose marriage; to make a speech.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to pass judgment, then the Arabic for a wedding feast.

You'll want to know: خطب.
خطب — khaṭaba — "to propose marriage; to make a speech".
خطب أخي جارتنا الشهر الماضي. khaṭaba akhī jāratanā ash-shahr al-māḍī. My brother asked our neighbour to marry him last month.
Present: يخطب, yakhṭub.
خطب (khaṭaba) فتاة: to ask for her hand. خطب (khaṭaba) في الناس: to speak to a crowd.

The word, taken apart.
خطب (khaṭaba) is built on the native Arabic root kh-ṭ-b (Lane), "to address, to speak to". Lane gives both senses: khiṭba, asking for a woman in marriage, and khuṭba, a speech.

Lesson 4 of 5.
زف (zaffa) — to escort a bride; to bring good news.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to propose marriage, then the Arabic for an engagement to marry.

You'll want to know: زف.
زف — zaffa — "to escort a bride; to bring good news".
زف الأهل العروس إلى بيتها الجديد. zaffa al-ahl al-ʿarūs ilā baytihā al-jadīd. The family led the bride to her new home.
Present: يزف, yazuff.
The taught زفاف, a wedding, is its verbal noun. زف (zaffa) إليه الخبر: he brought him the good news.

The word, taken apart.
زف (zaffa) is built on the native Arabic root z-f-f (Lane), where zaffa al-ʿarūs is to conduct the bride to her husband with music and pageantry; zifāf is its verbal noun.

Lesson 5 of 5.
زغرد (zaghrada) — to ululate for joy.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for an engagement to marry, then the Arabic for to escort a bride.

You'll want to know: زغرد.
زغرد — zaghrada — "to ululate for joy".
زغردت النساء عندما دخلت العروس. zaghradat an-nisāʾ ʿindamā dakhalat al-ʿarūs. The women ululated when the bride came in.
Present: يزغرد, yuzaghrid.
The cry itself is زغرودة; plural زغاريد.

The word, taken apart.
زغرد (zaghrada) is built on the native Arabic four-letter root z-gh-r-d (Lane), first said of the bray a camel rolls in its throat, then of the trilling cry women raise at a wedding.
