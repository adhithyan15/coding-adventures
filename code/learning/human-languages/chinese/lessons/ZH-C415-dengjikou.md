## You'll want to know: dēngjīkǒu
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C415-TERMINAL415-05]; assesses=[] -->

*dēngjīkǒu* — "departure gate".

> *qù shí hào dēngjīkǒu zěnme zǒu* — How do I get to gate ten?

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*dēngjīkǒu* is built from *dēngjī* 'to board a plane' and 口 'an opening', all inherited from Middle Chinese, *tong*, *kj+j* and *khuwX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for getting around.
