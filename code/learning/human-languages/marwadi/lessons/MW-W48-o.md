# ओ (o) — the letter that opens ओळखणो

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MW-SCRIPT-A-INDEPENDENT-01, MW-SCRIPT-O-MATRA-01, MW-LEX-C158-VERB158-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: write **अ** once, and name it. Then say **छोरो**
(*chhoro*): its *o* is the sign **ो**.

[PAUSE 2s] Now say **ओळखणो** (*oḷakhṇo*), "to recognise". It opens on the vowel *o*
standing alone, and this lesson puts that letter in your hand.

## Script you'll notice: ओ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-SCRIPT-C158-O-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ओ** — *o*.

It opens **ओळखणो**. It is built on **अ**: the joined bowls, the shoulder and the
inner stem, then a trailing stem, as **आ** has, and above it a separate arc, the
same small arc the sign **ो** carries.

## Writing: ओ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MW-SCRIPT-C158-O-01] -->

- **1.** curve right around the upper bowl and continue down and around the lower bowl without lifting
- **2.** lift and sweep the middle shoulder right
- **3.** without lifting, climb up the inner stem to the headline
- **4.** descend the inner stem
- **5.** lift and descend the trailing stem
- **6.** lift at the trailing stem's headline junction and sweep the upper arc upward and left
- **7.** lift and draw the top shirorekhā left-to-right

**Pen lifts: 4.**

> The source shows six movements; native writers draw ओ as five pen-down strokes (60% of HP Labs India's native-writer samples, 49 of 82). This ductus follows the six movements in order and direction, lifting four times: the middle shoulder runs on, climbing the inner stem and descending it, and every other movement starts with a lift.

> Stroke order is one attested teaching order; hands and teachers vary.
> Source: Saurmandal, ‘Devanagari ओ stroke order.svg’, panels 1–6, Wikimedia Commons, 5 August 2023.
