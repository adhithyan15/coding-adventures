## You'll want to know: ریاض
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C518-RIYAZ518-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ریاض** — *riyāz* — "practice (in music)".

> سارا ہر صبح دو گھنٹے ریاض کرتی ہے۔
>
> *Sārā har subah do ghanṭe riyāz kartī hai.*
>
> Sara practises for two hours every morning.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ریاض** (*riyāz*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *riyāḍa*, "exercise, training"; Urdu uses it for a musician's daily practice.
