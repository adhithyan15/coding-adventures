## You'll want to know: अंशः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C433-AYA433-03]; assesses=[] -->

**अंशः** — *aṃśaḥ* — "a portion; a part".

> सः धनस्य अंशं मित्राय ददाति।
>
> *saḥ dhanasya aṃśaṃ mitrāya dadāti.*
>
> He gives a portion of the money to his friend.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अंशः** (*aṃśaḥ*) probably comes from the root √aś, "to obtain", as MW gives it; MW gives *aṃśa* as a share, a portion, a part (MW).
