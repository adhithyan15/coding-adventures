## You'll want to know: обжечься
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C302-ZADYKHATSYA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**обжечься** (*obzhéchsya*) — "to burn oneself, to get burnt".

> Я обжёгся горячим чаем.
>
> *Ya obzhyógsya goryáchim cháyem.*
>
> I burnt myself on hot tea.

Perfective; imperfective *обжигаться* (*obzhigátsya*). Past *обжёгся* (*obzhyógsya*), *обожглась*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*обжечься* is built on *об-* and *жечь* "to burn", inherited through Old East Slavic *žeči* (EtymDB), with the reflexive *-ся*.
