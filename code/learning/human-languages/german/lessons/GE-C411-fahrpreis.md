## You'll want to know: der Fahrpreis
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C411-FAHRSCHEIN-03]; assesses=[] -->

**der Fahrpreis** — "the fare".

> Für Kinder ist der Fahrpreis halb so hoch.
>
> *For children the fare is half as much.*

Plural: *die Fahrpreise*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Fahrpreis* is built from *fahren* 'to travel' and *Preis* 'price', which is usually traced through Old French *pris* to Latin *pretium* 'price, value' (Lewis & Short).
