## You'll want to know: il nubifragio
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C469-TEMPESTA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**il nubifragio** — "the cloudburst, the downpour".

> Il nubifragio ha allagato le strade.
>
> *The cloudburst flooded the streets.*

A lot of rain in a short time.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*nubifragio* is a learned borrowing from Medieval Latin *nūbifragium* (EtymDB), from *nūbēs* 'cloud' and *frangere* 'to break': the cloud breaks.
