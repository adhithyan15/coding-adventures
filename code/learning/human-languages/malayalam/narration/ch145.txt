Malayalam, chapter 145: Wish, Dream, Hope, Aim, Plan.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ആഗ്രഹം (āgrahaṁ) — a wish, a desire.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for a cause, then the Malayalam for on account of.
