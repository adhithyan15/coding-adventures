## You'll want to know: survivre
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C305-NAUFRAGE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**survivre** — "to survive".

> Tous les passagers ont survécu.
>
> *All the passengers survived.*

*Survivre à* — to survive something: *survivre à un accident*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*survivre* is inherited from Late Latin *supervīvere* "to outlive" (EtymDB), from *super* "over" and *vīvere* "to live".
