## You'll want to know: fēngxiǎn
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C389-INCASE389-03]; assesses=[] -->

*fēngxiǎn* — "risk".

> *yàoshi fēngxiǎn tài dà wǒmen jiù bú zuò le* — If the risk is too great, we won't do it.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*fēngxiǎn* is built from 风 'the wind' and *xiǎn* 'dangerous', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *pjuwng* and *xjemX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'perilous' and 'hard to pass'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what would happen.
