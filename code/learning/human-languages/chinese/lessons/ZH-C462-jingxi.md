## You'll want to know: jīngxǐ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C462-SURPRISE462-01]; assesses=[] -->

*jīngxǐ* — "pleasant surprise".

> *zhè shì wǒ jīnnián zuì dà de jīngxǐ* — This is the biggest surprise I've had all year.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*jīngxǐ* is built from *jīng* 'to startle' and 喜 'joy', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *kjaeng* and *xiX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'fear' and, after an older book, a startled horse, and the second 'joy'.
