## You'll want to know: paddeln
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C511-WASSERSPORT-04]; assesses=[] -->

**paddeln** — "to paddle".

> Wir paddeln mit dem Boot über den See.
>
> *We paddle across the lake in the boat.*

Perfekt with *sein* for a trip: *Wir sind über den See gepaddelt.*

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*paddeln* was borrowed from English *to paddle*; EtymDB has no entry for the German verb.
