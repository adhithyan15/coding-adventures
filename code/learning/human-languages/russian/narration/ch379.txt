Russian, chapter 379: A fallback plan.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
запасной (zapasnóy) — spare, reserve.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for an alternative, then the Russian for final.

You'll want to know: запасной.
запасной (zapasnóy) — "spare, reserve".
У меня есть запасной ключ. U menyá yest zapasnóy klyuch. I have a spare key.
Запасной выход — an emergency exit. Запасной план — a fallback plan.

The word, taken apart.
запасной (zapasnóy) is built on запас "a store, a supply", which is usually traced to пасти "to guard, to keep": what is kept in reserve.

Lesson 2 of 5.
в любом случае (v lyubóm slúchaye) — in any case, anyway.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for final, then the Russian for spare.

You'll want to know: в любом случае.
в любом случае (v lyubóm slúchaye) — "in any case, anyway".
В любом случае я тебе позвоню. V lyubóm slúchaye ya tebé pozvonyú. In any case I will call you.
Любой means any. It closes an argument: whatever happens.

The word, taken apart.
в любом случае (v lyubóm slúchaye) is "in any case": любой "any", on the root of любить "to love", and случай "a case", from Proto-Slavic sŭlučajĭ (EtymDB).

Lesson 3 of 5.
колебаться (kolebátsya) — to hesitate, to waver.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for spare, then the Russian for in any case.

You'll want to know: колебаться.
колебаться (kolebátsya) — "to hesitate, to waver".
Я долго колебался, но потом согласился. Ya dólgo kolebálsya, no potóm soglasílsya. I hesitated for a long time, but then I agreed.
Imperfective; present колеблюсь, колеблешься. Literally to sway: пламя колеблется.

The word, taken apart.
колебаться (kolebátsya) is inherited from Proto-Slavic kolěbati (EtymDB), usually glossed "to rock, to sway"; a person who hesitates sways from one side to the other.

Lesson 4 of 5.
решаться (reshátsya) — to make up one’s mind, to dare.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for in any case, then the Russian for to hesitate.

You'll want to know: решаться.
решаться (reshátsya) — "to make up one’s mind, to dare".
Он никак не решается сказать ей правду. On nikák ne resháyetsya skazát yey právdu. He cannot bring himself to tell her the truth.
Imperfective; perfective решиться. Решаться на + accusative, or + infinitive. Not the same as решать, to solve or decide.

The word, taken apart.
решаться (reshátsya) is the reflexive of решать "to decide", inherited from Proto-Slavic rěšiti "to untie, to loosen": to make up your mind is to untie yourself from doubt.

Lesson 5 of 5.
в зависимости от (v zavísimosti ot) — depending on.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to hesitate, then the Russian for to make up one’s mind.

You'll want to know: в зависимости от.
в зависимости от (v zavísimosti ot) — "depending on".
В зависимости от погоды мы поедем на море или в горы. V zavísimosti ot pogódy my poyédem na móre íli v góry. Depending on the weather, we will go to the sea or to the mountains.
От + genitive: в зависимости от (v zavísimosti ot) цены.

The word, taken apart.
в зависимости от (v zavísimosti ot) is built on зависимость "dependence", from зависеть "to depend", made of за- and висеть "to hang": one thing hangs on another.
