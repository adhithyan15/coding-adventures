Gujarati, chapter 417: The Pol.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
પોળ (poḷ) — a pol (a gated old-city neighbourhood).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for the outskirts of a village, then the Gujarati for to travel about.

You'll want to know: પોળ.
પોળ — poḷ — "a pol (a gated old-city neighbourhood)".
જૂના શહેરની પોળમાં સાંકડી ગલીઓ છે. jūnā śahernī poḷmā̃ sā̃kḍī galīo che. The pol in the old city has narrow lanes.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
પોળ (poḷ) is a tadbhava of Sanskrit pratōlī, "a main street; a town gate"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati word, "a street" (CDIAL 8633). A પોળ is a street of houses shut in by its own gate.

ડેલી પાસે ચોકીદાર બેઠો છે. ḍelī pāse cokīdār beṭho che. A watchman is sitting by the gateway.

Lesson 3 of 5.

સાંજે દાદી ઓટલા પર બેસે છે. sā̃je dādī oṭlā par bese che. In the evening grandmother sits on the front platform.

ઓટલો (oṭlo) goes back to ōṭṭā, a form CDIAL reconstructs; CDIAL lists the Gujarati word, a raised platform to sit on (CDIAL 2544).

Lesson 4 of 5.
ખડકી (khaḍkī) — a small gated lane (inside a pol).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for an entrance gateway, then the Gujarati for a raised platform.

You'll want to know: ખડકી.
ખડકી — khaḍkī — "a small gated lane (inside a pol)".
અમારી ખડકીમાં પાંચ ઘર છે. amārī khaḍkīmā̃ pā̃c ghar che. There are five houses in our little lane.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ખડકી (khaḍkī) is a tadbhava of Sanskrit khaṭakkikā, "a side door"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati word, "a courtyard, a lane" (CDIAL 3770). The name of the small gate seems to have passed to the lane behind it.

Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે બારણું વાસ્યું, teṇe bārṇũ vāsyũ.

The word, taken apart.
વાસવું (vāsvũ) No dictionary we use records an older form, so its history is not settled.
