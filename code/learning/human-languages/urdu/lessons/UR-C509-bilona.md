## You'll want to know: بلونا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C509-CHHACHH509-02]; assesses=[] -->

**بلونا** — *bilonā* — "to churn".

> دادی نے مٹکے میں دہی بلویا۔
>
> *dādī ne maṭke meṅ dahī biloyā.*
>
> Grandmother churned the yoghurt in a clay pot.

Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے دودھ بلویا, *us ne dūdh biloyā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**بلونا** (*bilonā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *bilonā*, "to churn", under *\*virapati*, a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 11844).
