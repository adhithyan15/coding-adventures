## You'll want to know: ਰਿਕਸ਼ਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C272-CROWD272-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ਰਿਕਸ਼ਾ** — *rikshā* — "a rickshaw".

> ਸਟੇਸ਼ਨ ਦੇ ਬਾਹਰ ਬਹੁਤ ਰਿਕਸ਼ੇ ਹਨ।
>
> *saṭeshan de bāhar bahut rikshe han.*
>
> There are many rickshaws outside the station.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਰਿਕਸ਼ਾ** (*rikshā*) is borrowed from English *rickshaw*.
