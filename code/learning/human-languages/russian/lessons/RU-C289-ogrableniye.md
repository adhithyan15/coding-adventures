## You'll want to know: ограбление
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C289-OGRABLENIYE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ограбление** (*ograbléniye*) — "a robbery".

> Ограбление произошло ночью.
>
> *Ograbléniye proizoshló nóchyu.*
>
> The robbery happened at night.

Neuter. *Ограбление банка* — a bank robbery.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ограбление* is built on *ограбить*, the perfective of *грабить* "to rob", which is inherited from Proto-Slavic *grabiti* (EtymDB).
