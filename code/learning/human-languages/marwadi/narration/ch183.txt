Marwadi (Marwari), chapter 183: Small Things Around the House.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
कड़छी (kaṛchhī) — a ladle.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for to temper, then the Marwadi for to serve food.

You'll want to know: कड़छी.
कड़छी — kaṛchhī — "a ladle".
कड़छी कड़ाही मांय है। kaṛchhī kaṛāhī mā̃y hai. The ladle is in the wok.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
कड़छी (kaṛchhī) comes from a reconstructed kaṭacchu, "a ladle"; CDIAL lists Hindi and Gujarati kaṛchī, "an iron ladle" (CDIAL 2633). CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry.

Lesson 3 of 5.
अंगोछो (aṅgochho) — a shoulder cloth.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for a ladle, then the Marwadi for a basket.

You'll want to know: अंगोछो.
अंगोछो — aṅgochho — "a shoulder cloth".
म्हारो अंगोछो गीलो है। mhāro aṅgochho gīlo hai. My shoulder cloth is wet.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
अंगोछो (aṅgochho) is inherited from Sanskrit aṅgoñcha, "a towel"; CDIAL lists Hindi ãgochā and Sindhi aṅgocho (CDIAL 139). CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry.

Lesson 4 of 5.
काजळ (kājaḷ) — kohl.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for a basket, then the Marwadi for a shoulder cloth.

You'll want to know: काजळ.
काजळ — kājaḷ — "kohl".
छोरी आंख मांय काजळ लगावै है। chhorī ā̃kh mā̃y kājaḷ lagāvai hai. The girl puts kohl in her eyes.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
काजळ (kājaḷ) is inherited from Sanskrit kajjala, "lamp-black", a word CDIAL thinks may come from Dravidian or Munda; it lists Gujarati kājaḷ and Hindi kājal (CDIAL 2622). CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry.
