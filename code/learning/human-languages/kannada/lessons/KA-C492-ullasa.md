# ಉಲ್ಲಾಸ (ullāsa) — cheerfulness; high spirits

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Kannada for regret, then the Kannada for remorse.

## You'll want to know: ಉಲ್ಲಾಸ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C492-HARSHA492-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ಉಲ್ಲಾಸ** — *ullāsa* — "cheerfulness; high spirits".

> ಬೆಳಗಿನ ತಣ್ಣನೆಯ ಗಾಳಿಯಿಂದ ಮನಸ್ಸಿಗೆ ಉಲ್ಲಾಸ ಆಯಿತು.
>
> *beḷagina taṇṇaneya gāḷiyinda manassige ullāsa āyitu.*
>
> The cool morning breeze lifted my spirits.

ಉಲ್ಲಾಸದಿಂದ, cheerfully.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಉಲ್ಲಾಸ** (*ullāsa*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *ullāsa*, "light, splendour; a coming forth; joy, merriness" (Monier-Williams).

## What you've built

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
