## You'll want to know: أسر
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C333-BAHR333-05]; assesses=[] -->

**أسر** — *asara* — "to capture, to take prisoner".

> أسر القراصنة البحارة.
>
> *asara al-qarāṣina al-baḥḥāra.*
>
> The pirates took the sailors prisoner.

Present: يأسر, *yaʾsir*.

A prisoner of war is أسير. Of beauty: أسر قلبه, it captivated him.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**أسر** (*asara*) is built on the native Arabic root *ʾ-s-r* (Lane), "to bind with a thong". Lane links the taught *usra*, a family, to it: the kin who bind a man and stand with him.
