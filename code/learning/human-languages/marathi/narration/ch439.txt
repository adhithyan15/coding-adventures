Marathi, chapter 439: Storms and Thunder.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
चक्रीवादळ (cakrīvādaḷ) — a cyclone.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to pour down, then the Marathi for to get wet.

You'll want to know: चक्रीवादळ.
चक्रीवादळ — cakrīvādaḷ — "a cyclone".
चक्रीवादळामुळे गावात खूप पडझड झाली. cakrīvādaḷāmuḷe gāvāt khūp paḍjhaḍ jhālī. The cyclone brought down a lot in the village.
Neuter.
चक्री, whirling, and वादळ, a storm.

The word, taken apart.
चक्रीवादळ (cakrīvādaḷ) is built from cakrī, "whirling", on Sanskrit cakra, "a wheel" (CDIAL 4538), and वादळ, "a storm".

Lesson 2 of 5.
वावटळ (vāvṭaḷ) — a whirlwind.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to get wet, then the Marathi for a cyclone.

You'll want to know: वावटळ.
वावटळ — vāvṭaḷ — "a whirlwind".
शेतात अचानक वावटळ उठली. śetāt acānak vāvṭaḷ uṭhlī. Suddenly a whirlwind rose in the field.
Feminine.
वावटळ (vāvṭaḷ) उठणे, a whirlwind rises.

The word, taken apart.
वावटळ (vāvṭaḷ) is a Marathi word for a spinning pillar of wind and dust; its origin is not settled.

Lesson 3 of 5.
गडगडाट (gaḍgaḍāṭ) — a rumble of thunder.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a cyclone, then the Marathi for a whirlwind.

You'll want to know: गडगडाट.
गडगडाट — gaḍgaḍāṭ — "a rumble of thunder".
रात्री ढगांचा गडगडाट ऐकू आला. rātrī ḍhagāñcā gaḍgaḍāṭ aikū ālā. At night the rumble of thunder could be heard.
Masculine.
ऐकू येणे, to be heard: the sound is the subject.

The word, taken apart.
गडगडाट (gaḍgaḍāṭ) is an echo word that imitates rolling thunder; its origin is not settled.

Lesson 4 of 5.
घोंगावणे (ghoṅgāvṇe) — to howl; to roar.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a whirlwind, then the Marathi for a rumble of thunder.

You'll want to know: घोंगावणे.
घोंगावणे — ghoṅgāvṇe — "to howl; to roar".
रात्रभर वारा घोंगावत होता. rātrabhar vārā ghoṅgāvat hotā. The wind howled all night.
Past (he …): तो घोंगावला, to ghoṅgāvlā.
For strong wind and for swarming bees.

The word, taken apart.
घोंगावणे (ghoṅgāvṇe) is an echo verb for the roar of wind or the drone of bees; its origin is not settled.

Lesson 5 of 5.
कडाडणे (kaḍāḍṇe) — to crackle; to crash.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a rumble of thunder, then the Marathi for to howl.

You'll want to know: कडाडणे.
कडाडणे — kaḍāḍṇe — "to crackle; to crash".
आकाशात वीज कडाडली. ākāśāt vīj kaḍāḍlī. Lightning crashed across the sky.
Past (he …): तो कडाडला, to kaḍāḍlā.
Lightning कडाडते; a fire can कडाडणे (kaḍāḍṇe) too.

The word, taken apart.
कडाडणे (kaḍāḍṇe) is inherited, from the family of Sanskrit kaṭakaṭā, "a grating sound", where CDIAL lists Marathi kaḍāḍ, "crackling" (CDIAL 2632).
