## You'll want to know: えんぶん
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C496-SOFT496-04]; assesses=[] -->

**えんぶん** — *enbun* — "salt content".

> **この みそしるは えんぶんが おおいです。** — *kono misoshiru wa enbun ga ōi desu* — "This miso soup has a lot of salt in it."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**えんぶん** (*enbun*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *en*, "salt" (Middle Chinese *yem*) and *bun*, "part" (*bjunH*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
