# Combien ? — the chapter, run cold

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[FR-LEX-BEAUCOUP-01, FR-LEX-UNPEU-02, FR-LEX-TRES-03, FR-LEX-ASSEZ-04, FR-LEX-COMBIEN-06] -->

[PAUSE 4s] Four measures. Three take a bare *de* in at least one position and
one never does. Sort them, then say the one rule that decides it.

## Guided Practice: the answers combien was waiting for
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[FR-LEX-COMBIEN-06, FR-LEX-BEAUCOUP-01, FR-LEX-UNPEU-02, FR-LEX-ASSEZ-04, FR-LEX-DE-PREP-01, FR-GRAM-PAS-DE-01, FR-LEX-CAFE-01, FR-LEX-PAIN-01, FR-LEX-EAU-03, FR-LEX-LAIT-05, FR-LEX-VIN-05, FR-LEX-FROMAGE-01, FR-LEX-SUCRE-07, FR-LEX-SEL-05, FR-LEX-BEURRE-03, FR-LEX-OEUF-07, FR-GRAM-DU-PARTITIF-07, FR-GRAM-DES-01] -->

[PAUSE 4s] **Combien ?** has had no answer since it was taught. Now it has four.

- [YOU SAY: the whole table, measured — "beaucoup de café, un peu de lait, assez
  de pain, pas de vin"]
- [YOU SAY: "beaucoup de fromage, un peu de sucre, assez de sel, pas de beurre"]
- [YOU SAY: with the plural — "beaucoup d'œufs", "pas d'œufs"]
- [YOU SAY: the four shapes of *de* in one breath — "du café, pas de café,
  beaucoup de café, un peu de café"]

## Guided Practice: the distant band — qualities, measured
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[FR-LEX-TRES-03, FR-LEX-ASSEZ-04, FR-LEX-BON-03, FR-GRAM-ADJ-AGREEMENT-04, FR-GRAM-FEMININ-E-01, FR-GRAM-ADJ-APRES-NOM-10, FR-LEX-BIEN-02, FR-LEX-VERT-02, FR-LEX-BLANC-03, FR-LEX-NOIR-01, FR-LEX-ROUGE-06, FR-LEX-BLEU-08, FR-LEX-JAUNE-04, FR-LEX-VIN-05, FR-LEX-PAIN-01, FR-GRAM-CEST-01, FR-LEX-CAVA-08] -->

[PAUSE 4s] Every adjective the book owns, intensified — and no *de* anywhere.

- [YOU SAY: "très bon, très bien, assez bon"]
- [YOU SAY: the feminine — "très bonne", and the consonant it wakes]
- [YOU SAY: the colours — "très rouge, très blanc, assez noir, très vert"]
- [YOU SAY: with the noun behind — "un vin très rouge", "un pain très blanc"]
- [YOU SAY: the everyday exchange — "Ça va ? — Très bien, merci."]

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[FR-LEX-BEAUCOUP-01, FR-LEX-UNPEU-02, FR-LEX-TRES-03, FR-LEX-ASSEZ-04, FR-LEX-DE-PREP-01, FR-LEX-MONSIEUR-01] -->

[PAUSE 4s] Which of the four measures refuses *de*? (***Très***.) Which one takes
it before a noun and not before an adjective? (***Assez***.) Why do quantity
words take a bare *de* at all? (**They are already measuring**, so the measuring
article drops.) And what two words are inside *Monsieur*?
