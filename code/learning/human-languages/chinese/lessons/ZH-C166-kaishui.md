## You'll want to know: 开水
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C166-TEA166-01]; assesses=[] -->

**开水** — *kāishuǐ* — "boiled water; boiling water".

> **中午我们吃米饭喝开水** *zhōngwǔ wǒmen chī mǐfàn hē kāishuǐ* — At midday we have rice and drink boiled water.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**开水** (*kāishuǐ*) is built from 开 'to open', here 'to come to the boil', and 水 'water', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *khoj* and *sywijX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription); the boiling sense is not given there.
