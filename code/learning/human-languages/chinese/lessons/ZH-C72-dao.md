## You'll want to know: dào
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C72-TRAVEL72-05]; assesses=[] -->

*dào* — "to arrive; to reach".

> *huǒchē hěn màn shí diǎn dào* — The train is slow; it gets in at ten.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*dào* is inherited from Middle Chinese *tawH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'to reach'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for times and partings.
