## You'll want to know: સાચવવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C250-COMPUTER250-04]; assesses=[] -->

**સાચવવું** — *sācavvũ* — "to keep safe, to save".

> **હું મારું કામ કમ્પ્યૂટરમાં સાચવું છું.** — *hũ mārũ kām kampyūṭarmā̃ sācavũ chũ* — "I save my work on the computer."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**સાચવવું** (*sācavvũ*) is a tadbhava. CDIAL lists it, glossed "to preserve", under Sanskrit *sájati*, "is attached, clings", as built on an analogical Middle Indo-Aryan stem *\*ṣacc-* (CDIAL 13085).
