## You'll want to know: héshì
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C108-SHOP108-05]; assesses=[] -->

*héshì* — "right; suitable; a good fit".

> *zhè shuāng xié hěn héshì* — These shoes fit well.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*héshì* is built from hé and shì, both inherited from Middle Chinese, *hop* and *syek* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription); the dictionary glosses the first 'to agree, to match' and the second 'good', among other senses.
