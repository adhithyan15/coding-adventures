## You'll want to know: lēx, lēgis
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C308-IUDEX-04]; assesses=[] -->

**lēx, lēgis** — "a law".

> Populus novam lēgem probāvit.
>
> *The people approved the new law.*

Feminine, 3rd declension. A *lēx* was a bill passed by the people's vote. English *legal* comes from it.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**lēx** is inherited from Proto-Italic *\*lēg-* (EtymDB); Lewis & Short connect it, with doubt, with a root meaning "to bind". English *legal* and *legislate* come from it.
