Hindi, chapter 332: Old Customs.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
परंपरा (paramparā) — a tradition.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a blessed offering (of food), then the Hindi for a festive dish.

You'll want to know: परंपरा.
परंपरा — paramparā — "a tradition".
यह परंपरा सौ साल पुरानी है। yah paramparā sau sāl purānī hai. This tradition is a hundred years old.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
परंपरा (paramparā) is Sanskrit paramparā, "an unbroken series, one after another" (Monier-Williams): what passes from each generation to the next.

[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: रिवाज.
रिवाज — rivāj — "a custom".
हमारे गाँव में त्योहार पर नए कपड़े पहनने का रिवाज है। hamāre gā̃v meṁ tyohār par nae kapṛe pahanne kā rivāj hai. In our village it is the custom to wear new clothes for a festival.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
रिवाज (rivāj) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic rawāj, "currency, being in use": what is current in a place. का रिवाज है is it is the custom to.
