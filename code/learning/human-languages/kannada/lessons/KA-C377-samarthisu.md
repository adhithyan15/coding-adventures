# ಸಮರ್ಥಿಸು (samarthisu) — to justify; to defend

## You'll want to know: ಸಮರ್ಥಿಸು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C377-TARKA377-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸಮರ್ಥಿಸು** — *samarthisu* — "to justify; to defend".

> ಅವಳು ತನ್ನ ನಿರ್ಧಾರವನ್ನು ಸಮರ್ಥಿಸಿದಳು.
>
> *avaḷu tanna nirdhāravannu samarthisidaḷu.*
>
> She defended her decision.

Past (I …): ಸಮರ್ಥಿಸಿದೆ, *samarthiside*.

What you defend takes -ಅನ್ನು. ಸಮರ್ಥಿಸಿಕೊ, to defend oneself.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸಮರ್ಥಿಸು** (*samarthisu*) is built on Sanskrit *samartha*, "fit, able, forcible" (CDIAL 13189), with the Kannada verb ending -*isu*: to show that something is fit.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
