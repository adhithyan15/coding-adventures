# வளைகுடா (vaḷaikuṭā) — a bay; a gulf

## You'll want to know: வளைகுடா
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C435-KATALORAM435-02]; assesses=[] -->

**வளைகுடா** — *vaḷaikuṭā* — "a bay; a gulf".

> வளைகுடாவில் அலைகள் அமைதியாக இருந்தன.
>
> *vaḷaikuṭāvil alaikaḷ amaitiyāka iruntaṉa.*
>
> The waves in the bay were calm.

வளை, to bend, + குடா, a curve: where the coast bends in.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**வளைகுடா** (*vaḷaikuṭā*) is built from வளை, "to bend" (DEDR 5314), and குடா, "a bend, a curve" (DEDR 2054).
