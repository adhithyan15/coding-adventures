# Marathi A2 mock rubric

Score either form against [`task-shapes/a2.json`](../../task-shapes/a2.json).
Reading, listening, writing, and speaking are four separate 100-point papers.
The learner must attempt every section and reach 60/100 on **every** paper.
The 240/400 headline sum is a reporting convenience only: there is **no compensatory total** for a failed skill paper.

<!-- mock-part:a2-reading-notices-and-correspondence -->
## Reading: notices and correspondence — 30 points

Award the 30 points equally across items 1–8. Credit only the keyed purpose,
explicit detail, or required reader action.
<!-- /mock-part:a2-reading-notices-and-correspondence -->

<!-- mock-part:a2-reading-connected-everyday-text-one -->
## Reading: connected everyday text one — 35 points

Award the 35 points equally across items 9–16 for gist, sequence, explicit
detail, and simple inference.
<!-- /mock-part:a2-reading-connected-everyday-text-one -->

<!-- mock-part:a2-reading-connected-everyday-text-two -->
## Reading: connected everyday text two — 35 points

Award the 35 points equally across items 17–24 for main idea, paragraph
purpose, explicit detail, and simple inference.
<!-- /mock-part:a2-reading-connected-everyday-text-two -->

<!-- mock-part:a2-listening-announcements -->
## Listening: announcements — 30 points

Award the 30 points equally across items 1–7. The complete part has two plays
at 110–130 words per minute; the learner may take notes but may not see a
transcript.
<!-- /mock-part:a2-listening-announcements -->

<!-- mock-part:a2-listening-everyday-conversations -->
## Listening: everyday conversations — 35 points

Award the 35 points equally across items 8–14 for gist, speaker intention,
specific detail, and outcome recognition. The complete part has two plays.
<!-- /mock-part:a2-listening-everyday-conversations -->

<!-- mock-part:a2-listening-connected-account -->
## Listening: connected account — 35 points

Award the 35 points equally across items 15–20 for gist, event sequence,
specific detail, and attitude recognition. The complete part has two plays.
<!-- /mock-part:a2-listening-connected-account -->

<!-- mock-part:a2-writing-functional-response -->
## Writing: functional response — 40 points

The independent 25–35-word response earns: requested content 16, reader and
purpose awareness 8, Marathi vocabulary and grammar 8, and orthographic
control 8. Copied prompt language is not independent production.
<!-- /mock-part:a2-writing-functional-response -->

<!-- mock-part:a2-writing-connected-message-or-description -->
## Writing: connected message or description — 60 points

The independent 70–90-word response earns: task fulfilment and relevant
content 20, organisation and cohesion 16, Marathi vocabulary and grammar 16,
and orthographic control 8.
<!-- /mock-part:a2-writing-connected-message-or-description -->

<!-- mock-part:a2-speaking-personal-interview -->
## Speaking: personal interview — 30 points

Across the six short responses award 35% for relevant task fulfilment, 25% for
interaction, 25% for Marathi vocabulary and grammar, and 15% for pronunciation
and fluency.
<!-- /mock-part:a2-speaking-personal-interview -->

<!-- mock-part:a2-speaking-prepared-account -->
## Speaking: prepared account — 35 points

For 90–120 seconds of speech award 35% for relevant task fulfilment, 25% for
organisation and cohesion, 25% for Marathi vocabulary and grammar, and 15% for
pronunciation and fluency. Notes made in preparation may contain keywords, not
sentences.
<!-- /mock-part:a2-speaking-prepared-account -->

<!-- mock-part:a2-speaking-information-exchange-role-play -->
## Speaking: information exchange — 35 points

For the 120–180-second exchange award 35% for relevant task fulfilment, 25% for
interaction and repair, 25% for Marathi vocabulary and grammar, and 15% for
pronunciation and fluency.
<!-- /mock-part:a2-speaking-information-exchange-role-play -->

## Evidence boundary

Accept a documented regional Marathi form when its meaning is clear and it is
used consistently. Romanization, a dictionary, a translator, a spell-checker,
or a written speaking script is forbidden by the project contract. This pair
of source-bounded project forms does not establish exam readiness or external
certification. It does not establish recording provenance, qualified recordings,
independent human validation, standard setting, learner readiness, or learner attainment.
