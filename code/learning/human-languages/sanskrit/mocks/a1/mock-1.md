# Sanskrit A1 mock 1 — learner paper

Give this paper to the learner. Keep
[`mock-1-examiner-script.md`](mock-1-examiner-script.md) confidential. Collect
the Reading and Listening sheets before Writing so that no visible Sanskrit
can become a copyable answer model. The written papers take 58 minutes. The
speaking paper takes 10 minutes after five minutes of preparation. Sanskrit is
unjoined at word boundaries at A1; correct external sandhi is accepted but not
required.

Read the sources and choose A, B, or C.

## Writing — 20 minutes

Keep one timer running through both tasks. Write only in Devanagari. Do not use
romanization, a dictionary, a translator, a spell-checker, or any visible
Sanskrit model.

1. Practical form — 7 minutes. Complete all seven fields for a new local
   reading group. Give short personal or fictional details. The unscored
   example shows only the response type: “Age: 24”.

   | field | response |
   |---|---|
   | name | __________ |
   | city or place | __________ |
   | language spoken | __________ |
   | everyday work or study | __________ |
   | one food or drink you like | __________ |
   | one person in your family | __________ |
   | one activity you like | __________ |

2. Named-reader message — 13 minutes. Your reader is Mira. Write a **30–40
   word message** to help Mira recognise you and arrange a meeting tomorrow.
   Greet her, introduce yourself, say where you live and what you do, mention
   one thing you like, ask where she is, give a meeting place and time, and
   close politely.

## Speaking — 10 minutes

You have five minutes of preparation for parts 2 and 3. Notes may contain only
keywords, not a written answer script. The examiner conducts the confidential
prompts in Sanskrit and records the complete paper.

1. Personal interview — answer five familiar questions.
2. Prepared description — speak for one minute about a familiar place.
3. Transactional role-play — ask for a book, check its price, and arrange to return it tomorrow.
