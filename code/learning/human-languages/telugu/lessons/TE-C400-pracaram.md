# ప్రచారం (pracāraṁ) — a campaign; publicity

## You'll want to know: ప్రచారం
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C400-ENNIKA400-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ప్రచారం** — *pracāraṁ* — "a campaign; publicity".

> ఎన్నికల ప్రచారం నిన్నటితో ముగిసింది.
>
> *ennikala pracāraṁ ninnaṭitō mugisindi.*
>
> The election campaign ended yesterday.

ప్రచారం చేయు, to campaign, to spread the word.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ప్రచారం** (*pracāraṁ*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *pracāra*, "roaming; coming forth, manifestation" (Monier-Williams); in Telugu it is the spreading of a word or a cause.
