Urdu, chapter 381: Success.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
کامیاب (kāmyāb) — successful.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for to occur to, then the Urdu for to get down to.

You'll want to know: کامیاب.
کامیاب — kāmyāb — "successful".
اگر تم محنت کرو گے تو کامیاب ہو گے۔ agar tum mehnat karo ge to kāmyāb ho ge. If you work hard, you will succeed.

The word, taken apart.
کامیاب (kāmyāb) is usually traced to Persian kāmyāb, "attaining one's wish"; کامیابی, "success", is built on it.
