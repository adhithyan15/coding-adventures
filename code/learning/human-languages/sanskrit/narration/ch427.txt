Sanskrit, chapter 427: A Night's Lodging.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
वसतिः (vasatiḥ) — a night's stay; a lodging for the night.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for stays on, then the Sanskrit for a place of refuge.

You'll want to know: वसतिः.
वसतिः — vasatiḥ — "a night's stay; a lodging for the night".
पथिकः ग्रामे वसतिं याचते। pathikaḥ grāme vasatiṃ yācate. The wayfarer asks for a night's stay in the village.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
वसतिः (vasatiḥ) is built on the root √vas, "to dwell, to stay", which also gives vasati, "dwells"; MW gives the noun vasati as staying, especially overnight, a dwelling (MW).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a tent, then the Sanskrit for an encampment.

You'll want to know: आस्तृणाति.
आस्तृणाति — āstṛṇāti — "spreads out".
पथिकः भूमौ कम्बलम् आस्तृणाति। pathikaḥ bhūmau kambalam āstṛṇāti. The wayfarer spreads a blanket on the ground.
Past (he …): सः कम्बलम् आस्तीर्णवान्, saḥ kambalam āstīrṇavān.

The word, taken apart.
आस्तृणाति (āstṛṇāti) is built from ā and the root √stṛ, "to spread, to strew", which also gives āstaraṇa, "a carpet, a spread"; MW gives ā-stṛ as to spread or strew over, with the participle āstīrṇa, "spread" (MW).
