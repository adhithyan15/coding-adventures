# ದಂಗೆ (daṅge) — a revolt; an uprising

## You'll want to know: ದಂಗೆ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C352-DANGE352-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ದಂಗೆ** — *daṅge* — "a revolt; an uprising".

> ಜನರು ಆ ರಾಜನ ವಿರುದ್ಧ ದಂಗೆ ಎದ್ದರು.
>
> *janaru ā rājana viruddha daṅge eddaru.*
>
> The people rose in revolt against that king.

One "rises" in revolt: ದಂಗೆ ಏಳು.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ದಂಗೆ** (*daṅge*) is usually traced to Hindustani *daṅgā*, "a riot, a disturbance"; its earlier history is not settled.
