# நினைவுநாள் (niṉaivunāḷ) — a memorial day

## You'll want to know: நினைவுநாள்
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C324-ANJALI324-03]; assesses=[] -->

**நினைவுநாள்** — *niṉaivunāḷ* — "a memorial day".

> இன்று என் தாத்தாவின் நினைவுநாள்.
>
> *iṉṟu eṉ tāttāviṉ niṉaivunāḷ.*
>
> Today is my grandfather's memorial day.

The yearly day of a death, kept in memory.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**நினைவுநாள்** (*niṉaivunāḷ*) is a Tamil compound built from நினைவு, "remembrance" (DEDR 3683), and நாள், "day" (DEDR 3656).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
