## You'll want to know: der Verteidiger
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C506-FUSSBALL-03]; assesses=[] -->

**der Verteidiger** — "defender".

> Der Verteidiger stoppte den Stürmer kurz vor dem Tor.
>
> *The defender stopped the striker shortly before the goal.*

Plural: *die Verteidiger*. Also a defence lawyer.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Verteidiger* is built on *verteidigen* "to defend", usually traced to Middle High German *vertagedingen* "to plead before a court"; EtymDB lists the verb without a source.
