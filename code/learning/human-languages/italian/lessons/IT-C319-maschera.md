## You'll want to know: la maschera
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C319-CARNEVALE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**la maschera** — "the mask".

> Mio figlio vuole una maschera da leone.
>
> *My son wants a lion mask.*

Also a stock character of old comedy: *le maschere della commedia*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*maschera* comes from Medieval Latin *masca* "mask, disguise" (EtymDB); the deeper origin is not settled.
