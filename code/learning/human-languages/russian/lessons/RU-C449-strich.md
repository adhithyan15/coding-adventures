## You'll want to know: стричь
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C449-STRIZHKA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**стричь** (*strich*) — "to cut (hair), to trim".

> Мастер стрижёт меня уже пять лет.
>
> *Máster strizhyót menyá uzhé pyat let.*
>
> The hairdresser has been cutting my hair for five years now.

Imperfective; the perfective is *постричь*. Present *стригу, стрижёшь, стригут*; past *стриг*. *Стричься* — to have one's hair cut.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*стричь* is inherited from Proto-Slavic *striťi* (EtymDB), which EtymDB takes back to a PIE root *streyg-*.
