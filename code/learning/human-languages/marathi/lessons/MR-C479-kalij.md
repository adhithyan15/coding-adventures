## You'll want to know: काळीज
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C479-NADI479-05]; assesses=[] -->

**काळीज** — *kāḷīj* — "the innermost heart; the liver".

> ती बातमी ऐकून माझं काळीज तुटलं.
>
> *tī bātmī aikūn mājhaṁ kāḷīj tuṭlaṁ.*
>
> My heart broke when I heard that news.

Neuter.

Neuter: माझं काळीज. काळजाचा तुकडा, a darling. काळजी, care, is often linked with it.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**काळीज** (*kāḷīj*) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Marathi *kāḷīj*, "liver, heart", under Sanskrit *kāleyaka* (CDIAL 3103). काळजी, care, is often linked with it, but CDIAL does not list it there.

## What you've built

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
