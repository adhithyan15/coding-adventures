## You'll want to know: দিগন্ত
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C401-DIK401-05]; assesses=[] -->

**দিগন্ত** — *digôntô* — "the horizon".

> সৈকত থেকে আমরা দিগন্তে একটা জাহাজ দেখলাম।
>
> *soikôt theke āmrā digônte ekṭā jāhāj dekhlām.*
>
> From the beach we saw a ship on the horizon.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**দিগন্ত** (*digôntô*) is Sanskrit *dig-anta*, "the end of the horizon, a far distance" (Monier-Williams), from *diś*, a quarter of the sky, and *anta*, an end.
