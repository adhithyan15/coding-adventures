## You'll want to know: જગ્યા
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C205-RAIL205-02]; assesses=[] -->

**જગ્યા** — *jagyā* — "seat, place, room".

> **ટ્રેનમાં જગ્યા ન હતી.** — *ṭrenmā̃ jagyā na hatī* — "There was no seat on the train."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**જગ્યા** (*jagyā*) is a loan, usually traced to Persian *jāygāh*, "place". CDIAL has no entry for it.
