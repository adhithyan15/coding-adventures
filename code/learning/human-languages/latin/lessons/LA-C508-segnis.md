## You'll want to know: segnis, segne
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C508-IGNA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**segnis, segne** — "sluggish, slow".

> Asinus segnis lentē ambulat.
>
> *The sluggish donkey walks slowly.*

Two endings. *Nōn segniter* — not slackly, with energy.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**segnis** is a native Latin adjective; Lewis & Short reports that it is commonly linked with *sequor* "to follow", but the origin is not settled.
