## You'll want to know: जृम्भते
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C475-SVEDA475-02]; assesses=[] -->

**जृम्भते** — *jṛmbhate* — "yawns".

> रात्रौ श्रान्तः बालः जृम्भते।
>
> *rātrau śrāntaḥ bālaḥ jṛmbhate.*
>
> At night the tired child yawns.

Past (he …): सः जृम्भितवान्, *saḥ jṛmbhitavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**जृम्भते** (*jṛmbhate*) comes from the root √jṛmbh, "to open the mouth, to yawn" (MW).
