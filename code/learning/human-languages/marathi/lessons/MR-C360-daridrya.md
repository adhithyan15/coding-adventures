## You'll want to know: दारिद्र्य
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C360-SAMANTA360-04]; assesses=[] -->

**दारिद्र्य** — *dāridrya* — "poverty".

> शिक्षणामुळे अनेक कुटुंबं दारिद्र्यातून बाहेर आली.
>
> *śikṣaṇāmuḷe anek kuṭumbaṁ dāridryātūn bāher ālī.*
>
> Through education many families came out of poverty.

Neuter.

Neuter and formal; in speech गरिबी, from गरीब, poor.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**दारिद्र्य** (*dāridrya*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *dāridrya*, "poverty" (CDIAL 6297), built on *daridra*, "poor".
