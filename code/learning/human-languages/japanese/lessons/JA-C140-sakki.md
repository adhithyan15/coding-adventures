## You'll want to know: さっき
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C140-SAKKI]; assesses=[] -->

**さっき** — *sakki* — "a moment ago", "a little while ago": earlier
today, not long before now. The small **っ** holds the beat before *ki*.

> **さっき おきました。** — *sakki okimashita* — "I got up a little while
> ago."
