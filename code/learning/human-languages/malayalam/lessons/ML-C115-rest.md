## You'll want to know: വിശ്രമം
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C115-UNWELL-03]; assesses=[] -->

**വിശ്രമം** (*viśramaṁ*) — "rest".

**വിശ്രമിക്കൂ** (*viśramikkū*) — "please rest", what you tell a friend who is unwell.
