## You'll want to know: پہلوان
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C520-AKHARA520-01]; assesses=[] -->

**پہلوان** — *pahalvān* — "a wrestler".

> پہلوان روز صبح دودھ پیتا ہے۔
>
> *pahalvān roz subah dūdh pītā hai.*
>
> The wrestler drinks milk every morning.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**پہلوان** (*pahalvān*) is usually traced to Persian *pahlavān*, "a hero, a champion".
