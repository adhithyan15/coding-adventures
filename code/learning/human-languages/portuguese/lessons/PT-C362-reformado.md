## You'll want to know: reformado
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C362-ORDENADO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**reformado** — "retired".

> A minha mãe é reformada: era professora.
>
> *My mother is retired: she was a teacher.*

European Portuguese; in Brazil *aposentado*. Also a noun: *os reformados*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*reformado* is the participle of *reformar*, a learned borrowing from Latin *refōrmāre* "to shape again, to transform" (EtymDB, Lewis & Short).
