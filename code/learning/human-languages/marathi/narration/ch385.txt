Marathi, chapter 385: When Things Get Worse.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
चिघळणे (cighaḷṇe) — to worsen; to fester.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a tight spot, then the Marathi for a test.

You'll want to know: चिघळणे.
चिघळणे — cighaḷṇe — "to worsen; to fester".
वेळेवर बोलणं झालं नाही, म्हणून वाद चिघळला. veḷevar bolṇaṁ jhālaṁ nāhī, mhaṇūn vād cighaḷlā. Nobody talked in time, so the dispute festered.
Past (he …): तो चिघळला, to cighaḷlā.
Of a wound or a quarrel left alone.

The word, taken apart.
चिघळणे (cighaḷṇe) is a Marathi verb whose origin is not settled.

Lesson 2 of 5.
उफाळणे (uphāḷṇe) — to surge up; to flare up.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a test, then the Marathi for to worsen.

You'll want to know: उफाळणे.
उफाळणे — uphāḷṇe — "to surge up; to flare up".
जुन्या गोष्टींवरून पुन्हा भांडण उफाळलं. junyā goṣṭīṁvarūn punhā bhāṇḍaṇ uphāḷlaṁ. The quarrel flared up again over old matters.
Past (he …): तो उफाळला, to uphāḷlā.
Of flames, boiling water and feelings.

The word, taken apart.
उफाळणे (uphāḷṇe) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Marathi uphāḷṇẽ, "to boil or bubble up, rise (of flame)", under Sanskrit utphalati, "springs open, bursts" (CDIAL 1837).

Lesson 3 of 5.
निवळणे (nivaḷṇe) — to calm down; to clear.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to worsen, then the Marathi for to surge up.

You'll want to know: निवळणे.
निवळणे — nivaḷṇe — "to calm down; to clear".
दोन दिवसांनी गावातील तणाव निवळला. don divsānnī gāvātīl taṇāv nivaḷlā. After two days the tension in the village eased.
Past (he …): तो निवळला, to nivaḷlā.
Of muddy water as well: पाणी निवळलं, the water cleared.

The word, taken apart.
निवळणे (nivaḷṇe) is a verb built on Marathi nivaḷ, "pure, clear", which CDIAL lists under Sanskrit nirmala, "spotless, clean" (CDIAL 7366).

Lesson 4 of 5.
टळणे (ṭaḷṇe) — to be averted.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to surge up, then the Marathi for to calm down.

You'll want to know: टळणे.
टळणे — ṭaḷṇe — "to be averted".
थोडक्यात अपघात टळला. thoḍkyāt apghāt ṭaḷlā. An accident was narrowly averted.
Past (he …): तो टळला, to ṭaḷlā.
Intransitive: the danger is the subject; टाळणे, to avoid, is its causative partner.

The word, taken apart.
टळणे (ṭaḷṇe) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi ṭaḷṇẽ, "to pass off", under ṭalati, "moves aside" (CDIAL 5450), beside ṭāḷṇẽ, "to avoid".

Lesson 5 of 5.
सामोरे जाणे (sāmore jāṇe) — to confront; to face.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to calm down, then the Marathi for to be averted.

You'll want to know: सामोरे जाणे.
सामोरे जाणे — sāmore jāṇe — "to confront; to face".
ती अडचणींना धैर्याने सामोरी गेली. tī aḍcaṇīnnā dhairyāne sāmorī gelī. She faced the difficulties bravely.
Past (he …): तो सामोरा गेला, to sāmorā gelā.
What is faced takes -ला, and सामोरा agrees with the subject: तो सामोरा गेला, ती सामोरी गेली.

The word, taken apart.
सामोरे जाणे (sāmore jāṇe) joins सामोरे, "in front", inherited from Sanskrit saṁmukha, "facing" (CDIAL 12982 lists Marathi sāmorā), to जाणे, "to go": to go up face to face.
