# ਕੱਲ੍ਹ (kall) — yesterday; tomorrow

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-GRAMMAR-PAST-GIA-216, PA-LEX-KALL] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new sense: say *Aman went home*, then the farewell
**ਕੱਲ੍ਹ ਮਿਲਾਂਗੇ**, "we will meet tomorrow".

## You'll want to know: ਕੱਲ੍ਹ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-KALL-216]; assesses=[PA-GRAMMAR-PAST-GIA-216, PA-FORM-NAME-AMAN-01, PA-LEX-C67-FOOD67-03] -->

**ਕੱਲ੍ਹ** — *kall* — "yesterday; tomorrow". It names the day one step from
today, either way; the verb tells you which.

> **ਅਮਨ ਕੱਲ੍ਹ ਘਰ ਗਿਆ।** — *Aman kallh ghar giā.* — Aman went home yesterday.

With a went form it is yesterday; in **ਕੱਲ੍ਹ ਮਿਲਾਂਗੇ** it is tomorrow.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਕੱਲ੍ਹ** (*kall*) is inherited from Sanskrit *kālya*, "at dawn; tomorrow":
CDIAL lists Punjabi *kall*, "yesterday, tomorrow" (CDIAL 3104).

One more word for talking about the past.
