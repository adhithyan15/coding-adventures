## You'll want to know: غش کردن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C492-UNWELL-03]; assesses=[] -->

**غش کردن** (*ghash kardan*) — "to faint".

> او در گرما غش کرد.
>
> *u dar garmâ ghash kard.*
>
> She fainted in the heat.

*ghash* 'a faint' + *kardan*. Colloquially also 'to laugh till you drop': *az khande ghash kard*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ghash* is a loan, usually traced to Arabic *ġašy*, 'a fainting fit'. EtymDB has no entry for it. *kardan*, 'to do', is inherited Persian.
