## You'll want to know: أدرك
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C367-IDRAK367-01]; assesses=[] -->

**أدرك** — *adraka* — "to realize".

> أدركت أنني نسيت مفتاحي.
>
> *adraktu annanī nasītu miftāḥī.*
>
> I realized that I had forgotten my key.

Present: يدرك, *yudrik*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**أدرك** (*adraka*) is built on the native Arabic root *d-r-k*, "to overtake, to reach" (Lane). It is Form IV, to catch up with: a thought catches up with you, and you realize.

## What you've built
