# मी गेलो (mī gelo) — the past, person by person

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MR-LEX-C275-VASTU275-04, MR-LEX-C275-VASTU275-05, MR-PHRASE-BHETUN-ANAND-JHALA] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the last two things you named, dance and a stage. (*nṛtya*, *raṅgmañc*.) Then the line you have said since your first meeting: **भेटून आनंद झाला** (*bheṭūn ānand jhālā*).

[PAUSE 1s] Its last word, **झाला**, is a past: "(gladness) came about".

## You'll want to know: the past, person by person
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-GRAMMAR-C276-PAST-PERSON-01]; assesses=[MR-LEX-MI, MR-LEX-TU-TUMHI, MR-LEX-TO-TI-TE, MR-LEX-JANE, MR-GRAMMAR-PRESENT-GENDER, MR-GRAMMAR-RESPECT-BY-PLURAL] -->

> **काल मी पुण्याला गेलो.**
>
> *kāl mī puṇyālā gelo.* — "Yesterday I went to Pune."

**काल** (*kāl*, "yesterday"). **गेलो** is **जाणे** in the past: the stem **गे-**, then **-ल-**, then an ending for person and gender, as in **बोलतो / बोलते**:

- **मी** **गेलो** (*gelo*) — I went; a woman says **मी** **गेले** (*gele*)
- **तू** **गेलास** (*gelās*) — you went, to a friend; to a woman, **गेलीस** (*gelīs*)
- **तो** **गेला** (*gelā*) — he went; **ती** **गेली** (*gelī*) — she went
- **आम्ही** (*āmhī*, "we") **गेलो** — we went
- **तुम्ही** **गेलात** (*gelāt*) — you went, with respect or to several
- **ते** **गेले** (*gele*) — they went; **त्या** (*tyā*, "they, all women") **गेल्या** (*gelyā*)

**-ा**, **-ी**, **-ं** are the endings of **माझा**, **माझी**, **माझं**. The Marathi past behaves like an adjective that agrees with whoever went.

## You'll want to know: the past stems
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-GRAMMAR-C276-PAST-STEMS-01]; assesses=[MR-LEX-YENE, MR-LEX-BASNE, MR-LEX-JHOPNE, MR-LEX-RAHNE, MR-LEX-C84-VERBS84-04, MR-GRAMMAR-NEGATE-NAHI] -->

Most verbs put **-ल-** straight onto the stem: **बसणे** → **मी बसलो** (*mī baslo*), **झोपणे** → **मी झोपलो** (*mī jhoplo*), **पडणे** → **तो पडला** (*to paḍlā*), "he fell". A few add a vowel first: **राहणे** → **मी राहिलो** (*mī rāhilo*), "I stayed"; **निघणे** → **मी निघालो** (*mī nighālo*), "I set out".

Two more change the stem itself:

- **येणे** → **मी आलो** (*mī ālo*), **ती आली** (*tī ālī*) — came
- **होणे** (*hoṇe*, "to become, to happen") → **तो झाला** (*to jhālā*), **ते झालं** (*te jhālaṁ*) — became, happened

"Did not" keeps the past form and puts **नाही** last, as you do with the present: **मी गेलो नाही** (*mī gelo nāhī*), "I didn't go".
