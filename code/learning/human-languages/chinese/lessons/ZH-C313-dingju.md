## You'll want to know: dìngjū
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C313-MOVE313-03]; assesses=[] -->

*dìngjū* — "to settle down; to make a place your home".

> *tāmen zài Běijīng dìngjū le* — They've settled in Beijing.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*dìngjū* is built from *dìng* 'calm' and *jū* 'to dwell', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *dengH* and *kjo* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'at peace' and the second 'to dwell'.

## What you've built
