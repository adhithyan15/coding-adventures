## You'll want to know: लूटणो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C243-CHOR243-02]; assesses=[] -->

**लूटणो** — *lūṭṇo* — "to rob; to plunder".

> रात मांय कोई गळी मांय लूटै है।
>
> *rāt mā̃y koī gaḷī mā̃y lūṭai hai.*
>
> Someone robs people in the lane at night.

Past (I …): म्हैं पैसा कोनी लूट्या, *mhaĩ paisā konī lūṭyā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**लूटणो** (*lūṭṇo*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*luṭṭati*, "plunders"; CDIAL lists Old Marwari *lūṭaï*, "plunders", under it (CDIAL 11078).
