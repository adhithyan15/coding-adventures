Portuguese, chapter 60: Dog, Cat, Horse, Cow, Pig.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
o cão — a dog.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for a desert, then the Portuguese for fire.

You'll want to know: o cão.
o cão — "a dog". Also o cachorro in Brazil.

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: o cão]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: o cão, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say o cão]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Portuguese for a desert, then the Portuguese for fire, then say o cão again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does o cão mean? ("A dog".) Say it once more.
