## You'll want to know: la contravention
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C408-RADAR-01]; assesses=[] -->

**la contravention** — "the fine (for a driving offence), the ticket".

> Il a reçu une contravention pour excès de vitesse.
>
> *He got a ticket for speeding.*

Informally *un PV*, short for *procès-verbal*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*contravention* is a learned formation on Late Latin *contrāvenīre* 'to oppose, to go against', from *contrā* 'against' and *venīre* 'to come' (Lewis & Short).
