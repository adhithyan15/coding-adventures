## You'll want to know: پیدا ہونا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C483-BABY483-03]; assesses=[] -->

**پیدا ہونا** — *paidā honā* — "to be born".

> میرا بھائی جنوری میں پیدا ہوا۔
>
> *merā bhāī janvarī meṅ paidā huā.*
>
> My brother was born in January.

Past (she …): وہ لاہور میں پیدا ہوئی, *voh lāhaur meṅ paidā huī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**پیدا ہونا** (*paidā honā*) is built from **پیدا**, usually traced to Persian *paidā*, "appearing, born", and **ہونا**, "to be".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
