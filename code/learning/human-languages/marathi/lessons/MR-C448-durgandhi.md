## You'll want to know: दुर्गंधी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C448-DURGANDHI448-02]; assesses=[] -->

**दुर्गंधी** — *durgandhī* — "a stench".

> सांडपाण्यामुळे गल्लीत दुर्गंधी पसरली.
>
> *sāṇḍpāṇyāmuḷe gallīt durgandhī pasarlī.*
>
> A stench spread through the lane because of the sewage.

Feminine.

A feminine noun; the opposite is सुगंध, a fragrance.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**दुर्गंधी** (*durgandhī*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *durgandha*, "a bad smell, a stink" (Monier-Williams).
