## You'll want to know: தொப்பி
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C67-WEAR-05]; assesses=[] -->

**தொப்பி** (*toppi*) — "a cap, a hat".

The last of the five, and the highest one: a தொப்பி sits மேலே, on the தலை.

Its **ொ** is the short o sign you met long before this chapter, and its doubled **ப்ப** works
exactly like the doubled ட you took apart in சட்டை — a புள்ளி closing the first letter onto
the second.

With this one the run has both ends: தொப்பி at the top, செருப்பு at the floor.
