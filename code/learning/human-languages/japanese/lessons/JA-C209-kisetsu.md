## You'll want to know: きせつ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C209-SEASON209-03]; assesses=[] -->

**きせつ** — *kisetsu* — "a season".

> **はるは いい きせつです。** — *haru wa ii kisetsu desu* — "Spring is a good season."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**きせつ** (*kisetsu*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *ki*, "season" (Middle Chinese *kjwijH*) and *setsu*, "joint, period" (*tset*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
