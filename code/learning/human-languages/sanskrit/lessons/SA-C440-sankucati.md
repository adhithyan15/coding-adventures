## You'll want to know: संकुचति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C440-HIMA440-04]; assesses=[] -->

**संकुचति** — *saṅkucati* — "huddles up; shrinks".

> शीते बालः कम्बले संकुचति।
>
> *śīte bālaḥ kambale saṅkucati.*
>
> In the cold the boy huddles up in a blanket.

Past (he …): सः कम्बले संकुचितवान्, *saḥ kambale saṅkucitavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**संकुचति** (*saṅkucati*) is built from *sam*, "together", and the root √kuc, "to contract, to bend"; MW gives *saṃ-kuc* as to contract or shrink, and its participle *saṃkucita* as crouching, cowering (MW).
