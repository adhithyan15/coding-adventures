## You'll want to know: మభ్యపెట్టు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C354-KUTRA354-02]; assesses=[] -->

**మభ్యపెట్టు** — *mabhyapeṭṭu* — "to mislead; to hoodwink".

> అతను అబద్ధాలతో అందరినీ మభ్యపెట్టాడు.
>
> *atanu abaddhālatō andarinī mabhyapeṭṭāḍu.*
>
> He misled everyone with lies.

Past (I …): మభ్యపెట్టాను, *mabhyapeṭṭānu*.

To cloud someone's mind; a written-style word.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**మభ్యపెట్టు** (*mabhyapeṭṭu*) ends in native *peṭṭu*, "to put" (DEDR 4390); the origin of the first part is not settled.
