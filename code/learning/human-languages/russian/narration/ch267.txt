Russian, chapter 267: Glad, Tired, Hopeful.
3 lessons. All 3 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 3.
я был рад, я устал, мне было скучно (ya byl rad, ya ustál, mne býlo skúchno) — how it felt.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say "joy", "happy" and "sad". (Rádost. Schastlívyy. Grústnyy.) Then "I've missed the train". (Ya opozdál na póyezd.) A trip has problems, and it has feelings.

Grammar Lens: рад, glad.
рад — rad — "glad". It is a short adjective: it does not stand before a noun, only after the person, and it agrees: рад for a man, рада (ráda) for a woman, рады (rády) for more than one.
Я рад, что ты позвонил. — ya rad, shto ty pozvoníl — I'm glad you rang. Я была рада, что ты позвонил. — ya bylá ráda, shto ty pozvoníl — I was glad you rang.
In the present there is no verb; in the past, был agrees with it.

Grammar Lens: я устал, I'm tired.
Я устал. — ya ustál — I'm tired. A woman: Я устала.
This is the perfective past of устать, the partner of уставать: "I have got tired". The result is the state you are in now, so a past form says "I am tired" today. Усталый is the adjective for a tired face or a tired voice.

Grammar Lens: мне было скучно.
How a thing felt goes on the "to me" form, with an -о word and было:
На концерте мне было скучно. — na kontsérte mne býlo skúchno — I was bored at the concert. Мне было интересно и весело. — mne býlo interésno i véselo — It was interesting, and I had fun.
Скучно is said skúshno: the ч is said as ш in this word.

The word, taken apart.
рад goes back to Proto-Slavic radŭ, "glad" (EtymDB 2.1). Радость, "joy", is the same root with a noun ending: the gladness itself.

Lesson 2 of 3.
мечтать (mechtát) — to dream of, to long to.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say "I was glad" and "I had fun". (Ya byl rad. Mne býlo véselo.) Then "to hope". (Nadéyatsya.) A trip told in the past leaves something for the future: a hope, a dream.

You'll want to know: мечтать.
мечтать — mechtát — "to dream of, to long to". It takes an infinitive, as хотеть does:
Я мечтаю путешествовать. — ya mechtáyu puteshéstvovat — I dream of travelling.
Or о and a thing, with the thing's ending changed:
Я мечтаю о море. — ya mechtáyu o móre — I dream of the sea.
This is a waking dream, a wish. A dream at night is сон, the word you have for sleep.

Grammar Lens: я надеюсь, что …
A dream may never come; a hope is about what may well happen. Я надеюсь, что … takes a whole clause, and a hope about tomorrow puts it in the future:
Я надеюсь, что погода будет хорошая. — ya nadéyus, shto pogóda búdet khoróshaya — I hope the weather will be good.
Будет, "will be", is the future of быть: the буду inside будущий. Short and spoken: Надеюсь! — "I hope so!"

The word, taken apart.
мечтать (mechtát) is inherited from Proto-Slavic mĭčĭtati, and the noun мечта, "a dream, a wish", from mĭčĭta, "dream" (EtymDB 2.1). EtymDB derives that noun from a Proto-Slavic verb mikati and gives it no meaning; by the usual account it is the verb of blinking and flickering, so a dream was first a flicker before the eyes.

Wrap-up Recall.

"I dream of travelling"? (Я мечтаю путешествовать.) A dream at night? (Сон.) "I hope the weather will be good"? (Я надеюсь, что погода будет хорошая.)


Lesson 3 of 3.
Review — how it felt, and what comes next.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say "I'm glad", as a man and as a woman. (Ya rad. Ya ráda.) Then "I'm tired". (Ya ustál. Ya ustála.)

What you've built.
Two lessons, and an experience now has a feeling and a future. Рад, рада, рады, with был in the past. Я устал, a past form for a state now. Мне было with an -о word for how a thing felt. And two ways forward: я надеюсь, что … будет, and я мечтаю with an infinitive or о.
[pause 2 seconds]
A day at a museum, in three lines:
В музее мне было интересно. Вечером я устала, но я была рада. Я мечтаю вернуться туда.
Вернуться, the partner of возвращаться, came with Anna's passport: "I dream of going back there".

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: V muzéye mne býlo interésno.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: Ya opozdál na póyezd, no mne né bylo grústno.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: Ya nadéyus, shto pogóda búdet khoróshaya.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — answer: Kak býlo na kontsérte? "How was the concert?" Answer with mne býlo and one feeling]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
"It was interesting for me"? (Мне было интересно.) "I dream of the sea"? (Я мечтаю о море.) A hope about tomorrow: which verb form? (Будет, the future.)
