## You'll want to know: ਝਾੜਨਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C235-KAM235-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ਝਾੜਨਾ** — *jhāṛnā* — "to dust; to shake out".

> ਮੈਂ ਕੱਪੜੇ ਨਾਲ ਅਲਮਾਰੀ ਝਾੜਦਾ ਹਾਂ।
>
> *maiṁ kappṛe nāl almārī jhāṛdā hāṁ.*
>
> I dust the cupboard with a cloth.

Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਦਰੀ ਝਾੜੀ, *maiṁ darī jhāṛī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਝਾੜਨਾ** (*jhāṛnā*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*jhāṭayati*, "shakes down"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *jhāṛṇā*, "to shake off, thresh, sweep" (CDIAL 5328).
