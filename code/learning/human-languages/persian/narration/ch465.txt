Persian, chapter 465: On the farm.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
مزرعه (mazra'e) — a farm; a field (of crops).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a down payment, then the Persian for to overcharge.

You'll want to know: مزرعه.
مزرعه (mazra'e) — "a farm; a field (of crops)".
پدربزرگم یک مزرعه کوچک در روستا دارد. Pedarbozorgam yek mazra'e-ye kuchek dar rustâ dârad. My grandfather has a small farm in the village.
Arabic plural mazâre'.

The word, taken apart.
mazra'e was borrowed from Arabic mazraʿa (EtymDB), a place where seed is sown.

Lesson 3 of 5.
شالیزار (shâlizâr) — a rice paddy, a paddy field.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a farm, then the Persian for wheat.

You'll want to know: شالیزار.
شالیزار (shâlizâr) — "a rice paddy, a paddy field".
در شمال شالیزارهای سبز زیادی هست. Dar shomâl shâlizâr-hâ-ye sabz-e ziyâdi hast. In the north there are many green rice paddies.
shâli 'rice in the husk' + -zâr 'a place full of'.

The word, taken apart.
shâlizâr is built from shâli 'rice in the husk' and the native ending -zâr 'a place full of', as in golzâr 'a rose garden'. The origin of shâli is not settled, and EtymDB does not list it.

در پاییز در تاکستان انگور میچینند. Dar pâyiz dar tâkestân angur michinand. In autumn they pick grapes in the vineyard.
tâk 'vine' + -estân 'place of'.

The word, taken apart.
tâkestân is a native compound of tâk 'vine' and the ending -estân 'place of', as in bimârestân 'hospital'. EtymDB does not list it.

کشاورز زمین را با تراکتور شخم زد. Keshâvarz zamin râ bâ terâktor shokhm zad. The farmer ploughed the land with a tractor.
shokhm 'ploughing' + zadan. Present stem zan-: shokhm mizanad.

The word, taken apart.
shokhm zadan is built from shokhm 'ploughing', whose origin is not settled, and the native verb zadan 'to strike'.
