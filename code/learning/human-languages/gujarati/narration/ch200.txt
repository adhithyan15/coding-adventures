Gujarati, chapter 200: At the post office.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
ટપાલી (ṭapālī) — postman.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to sneak in, then the Gujarati for to move aside.

You'll want to know: ટપાલી.
ટપાલી — ṭapālī — "postman".
ટપાલી સવારે ટપાલ લાવે છે. — ṭapālī savāre ṭapāl lāve che — "The postman brings the post in the morning."
Built on ટપાલ, ṭapāl, 'post'.

The word, taken apart.
ટપાલી (ṭapālī) is built on ṭapāl, "post, mail", with the agent ending -ī. The origin of ṭapāl is not settled: neither CDIAL nor DEDR has an entry for it.

નાખવું — nākhvũ — "to put in, to drop in".
તે પત્ર ટપાલપેટીમાં નાખે છે. — te patra ṭapālpeṭīmā̃ nākhe che — "She drops the letter into the postbox."

The word, taken apart.
નાખવું (nākhvũ) is a tadbhava. CDIAL lists it, glossed "to throw, put on, leave", under Sanskrit naṅkṣyati, "will perish" (CDIAL 6930).

Lesson 5 of 6.

Warm-up.

You'll want to know: નોંધાવવું.
નોંધાવવું — nõdhāvvũ — "to register, to book".
મેં પત્ર નોંધાવ્યો. — mẽ patra nõdhāvyo — "I had the letter registered."

The word, taken apart.
નોંધાવવું (nõdhāvvũ) is the causative of nõdhvũ, "to note down, enter in a book", which CDIAL lists under nibandhati, beside Sanskrit níbadhnāti, "binds on; writes down" (CDIAL 7223).
