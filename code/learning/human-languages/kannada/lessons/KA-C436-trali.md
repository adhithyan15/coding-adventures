# ಟ್ರಾಲಿ (ṭrāli) — a trolley

## You'll want to know: ಟ್ರಾಲಿ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C436-HAMALI436-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ಟ್ರಾಲಿ** — *ṭrāli* — "a trolley".

> ವಿಮಾನ ನಿಲ್ದಾಣದಲ್ಲಿ ಟ್ರಾಲಿ ಉಚಿತ.
>
> *vimāna nildāṇadalli ṭrāli ucita.*
>
> At the airport the trolley is free.

A luggage trolley at a station, or a hand cart in a shop.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಟ್ರಾಲಿ** (*ṭrāli*) is a loan from English *trolley*, a small cart pushed by hand.
