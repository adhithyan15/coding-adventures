## You'll want to know: 护送
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C443-CLEAN443-04]; assesses=[] -->

**护送** — *hùsòng* — "to escort".

> *tiān hēi le cūnzhǎng hùsòng wǒmen guò hé* — It got dark, so the village head saw us safely across the river.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**护送** (*hùsòng*) is built from 护 'to guard' and 送 'to see someone off', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *huH* and *suwngH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'to send'.
