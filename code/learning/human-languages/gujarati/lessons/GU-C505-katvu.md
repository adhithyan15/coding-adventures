## You'll want to know: કાંતવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C505-RENTIYO505-04]; assesses=[] -->

**કાંતવું** — *kā̃tvũ* — "to spin (thread)".

> દાદી રેંટિયા પર રૂ કાંતતી હતી.
>
> *dādī rẽṭiyā par rū kā̃ttī hatī.*
>
> Grandmother used to spin cotton on the spinning wheel.

Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે દોરો કાંત્યો, *teṇe doro kā̃tyo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**કાંતવું** (*kā̃tvũ*) is inherited from Sanskrit *kṛntati*, "spins", and CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb (CDIAL 3433).
