## You'll want to know: danificar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C300-CAPOTAR-04]; assesses=[] -->

**danificar** — "to damage".

> A chuva danificou o telhado.
>
> *The rain damaged the roof.*

Formal; in speech, *estragar*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*danificar* is a learned word from Late Latin *damnificāre* "to injure", from *damnum* "loss" and *facere* "to make" (Lewis & Short).
