# сначала, потом, затем, наконец — the steps of a story

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[RU-LEX-C150-VREMYA150-03, RU-LEX-C74-WHEN-05, RU-LEX-C150-VREMYA150-04] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "at first", "later, then" and "at last". (*Snachála.
Potóm. Nakonéts.*) Three step words you have had for a while. Now they
get a story to carry.

## You'll want to know: затем
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-ZATEM-01]; assesses=[RU-LEX-C74-WHEN-05] -->

**затем** — *zatém* — "after that, then". It means much the same as
**потом**, and sounds a little more careful: it is common in writing, in
instructions and in a story told with some care. Between them, a story
need not say **потом** twice in a row.

## Grammar Lens: one finished step, then the next
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-GRAMMAR-PERFECTIVE-CHAIN-01]; assesses=[RU-GRAMMAR-PAST-PERFECTIVE-RESULT-01, RU-LEX-C140-ACT140-02, RU-LEX-C192-VESHCH192-01, RU-LEX-C232-ACT232-03, RU-LEX-C37-PEOPLE-05, RU-LEX-IDTI] -->

> **Сначала я позавтракал, потом прочитал газету.** — *snachála ya
> pozávtrakal, potóm prochitál gazétu* — First I had breakfast, then I
> read the paper.
> **Затем я позвонил маме. Наконец я пошёл на работу.** — *zatém ya
> pozvoníl máme. nakonéts ya poshól na rabótu* — After that I rang Mum.
> At last I set off for work.

Every verb is perfective, so every step is done before the next one
starts. *Позавтракал* is *завтракать* with *по-*. **Пошёл**, "set off", is the
past of *пойти*, the perfective partner of *идти*.

## Why it's said this way: the same steps, as a habit
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[RU-GRAMMAR-PAST-IMPERFECTIVE-PROCESS-01, RU-LEX-C240-VREMYA240-05] -->

> **Обычно я сначала завтракал, потом читал газету.** — *obýchno ya
> snachála závtrakal, potóm chitál gazétu* — Usually I had breakfast
> first, then read the paper.

Imperfective verbs and **обычно**: not one morning, but every morning.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[RU-LEX-C82-DEF82-03] -->

**затем** is *за*, "behind, after", with *тем*, a form of **тот**,
"that": "after that". EtymDB 2.1 has no entry for it, so this is the plain
reading of its parts. **Тот** sits on the Proto-Indo-European pointing
stem *\*to-*, the same one behind English *that* and *the*.
