## You'll want to know: تنافس
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C546-MUBARA546-05]; assesses=[] -->

**تنافس** — *tanāfasa* — "to compete".

> تنافس الفريقان على البطولة.
>
> *tanāfasa al-farīqān ʿalā al-buṭūla.*
>
> The two teams competed for the championship.

Present: يتنافس, *yatanāfas*.

It takes على for the prize.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**تنافس** (*tanāfasa*) is built on the native Arabic root *n-f-s*, "a soul; to be precious" (Lane). Form VI tanāfasa is to vie with one another for a precious thing.
