Gujarati, chapter 26: City, School, and Road.
7 lessons. You can do the first 1 of them in the car; after that you will want to have stopped.


Lesson 1 of 7.
શહેર (shahar) — hear city first.

Warm-up.
Listen twice: shahar. It means city. Hold only the sound and meaning; the Gujarati spelling waits for the next small step.

Guided Practice.
Point to the city picture when you hear shahar, then say shahar once from the English cue.

Wrap-up Recall.
Source: Cambridge: city.

Lesson 7 of 7.
Payoff — a three-word route.

Before we start: this one needs your hands, so it is not a driving lesson. You can listen to all of it now and come back to the parts that need looking at once you have stopped.

Warm-up.
[pause 15 seconds]
Say city, school, and road in Gujarati.

Guided Practice.
Identify three heard words, say all three from meaning cues, match three printed cards, then write two words named aloud. Pass each skill separately.

Wrap-up Recall.
Sources: Cambridge: city, school, and road.
[question — say your answer, then pause 35 seconds]
After all four skills pass, write city, school, and road in order.
