## You'll want to know: ਝਗੜਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C316-QUARREL316-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ਝਗੜਾ** — *jhagṛā* — "a quarrel".

> ਪੈਸੇ ਬਾਰੇ ਇੱਕ ਵੱਡਾ ਝਗੜਾ ਹੋਇਆ।
>
> *paise bāre ikk vaḍḍā jhagṛā hoiā.*
>
> There was a big quarrel about money.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਝਗੜਾ** (*jhagṛā*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*jhagaḍ*, "quarrel"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *jhagṛā*, "quarrel", under it (CDIAL 5321).
