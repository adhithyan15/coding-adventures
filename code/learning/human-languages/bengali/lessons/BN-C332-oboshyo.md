# অবশ্য (ôbôshyô) — of course; mind you

## You'll want to know: অবশ্য
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C332-SUTORANG332-04]; assesses=[] -->

**অবশ্য** — *ôbôshyô* — "of course; mind you".

> হোটেলটা দামি, অবশ্য খাবার দুর্দান্ত।
>
> *hoṭelṭā dāmi, ôbôshyô khābār durdāntô.*
>
> The hotel is expensive, though mind you, the food is excellent.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**অবশ্য** (*ôbôshyô*) is Sanskrit *avaśyam*, 'necessarily' (Monier-Williams). Bengali uses it to grant a point while keeping your own: 'of course', 'mind you'.
