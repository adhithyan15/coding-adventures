## You'll want to know: پڑوس
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C412-QASBA412-05]; assesses=[] -->

**پڑوس** — *paṛos* — "next door; the neighbourhood".

> ہمارے پڑوس میں ایک درزی رہتا ہے۔
>
> *hamāre paṛos meṅ ek darzī rahtā hai.*
>
> A tailor lives next door to us.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**پڑوس** (*paṛos*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *paṛos*, "the neighbourhood", under Sanskrit *prátivēśa* (CDIAL 8598); **پڑوسی**, "a neighbour", belongs with it.
