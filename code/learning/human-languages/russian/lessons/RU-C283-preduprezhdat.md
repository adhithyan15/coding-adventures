## You'll want to know: предупреждать
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C283-UTVERZHDAT-05]; assesses=[] -->

**предупреждать** (*preduprezhdát*) — "to warn".

> Мать предупредила детей о грозе.
>
> *Mat predupredíla detéy o grozé.*
>
> Mother warned the children about the storm.

Imperfective; perfective *предупредить* (*predupredít*), *я предупрежу*. The person is in the accusative, the danger with *о*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*предупреждать* is built on *пред-* "before" and *упредить* "to get ahead of", in the Church Slavonic shape of the root seen in native *перед*: to tell before it happens. EtymDB lists no parent.
