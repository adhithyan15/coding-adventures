## You'll want to know: اتصال
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C417-SHABAKE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**اتصال** (*ettesâl*) — "a connection".

> اتصال اینترنت امروز خیلی کند است.
>
> *ettesâl-e internet emruz kheyli kond ast.*
>
> The internet connection is very slow today.

*Ettesâl dâshtan* — to be connected.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**اتصال** was borrowed from Arabic *ittiṣāl* "connection, contact" (EtymDB).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.
