## You'll want to know: linter, lintris
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C435-NAVIGIUM-04]; assesses=[] -->

**linter, lintris** — "a punt, a small flat boat; a trough".

> Agricolae lintribus flūmen trānseunt.
>
> *The farmers cross the river in punts.*

Third declension, feminine: *linter, lintris f.* A flat river boat; also a wooden tub or trough.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

The origin of *linter* is not settled: Lewis & Short connect it, doubtfully, with Greek *plynō* 'to wash', a washtub before a boat, and EtymDB lists it without a parent.
