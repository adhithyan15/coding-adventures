## You'll want to know: भिगोना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C329-RANG329-02]; assesses=[] -->

**भिगोना** — *bhigonā* — "to drench; to soak".

> बच्चों ने बाल्टी के पानी से अरुण को भिगोया।
>
> *baccoṁ ne bālṭī ke pānī se Aruṇ ko bhigoyā.*
>
> The children drenched Arun with a bucket of water.

Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने कपड़ा भिगोया, *usne kapṛā bhigoyā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**भिगोना** (*bhigonā*) is the causative of *bhīgnā*, to get wet, which CDIAL 9500 sets under *\*bhiyagna*: to make someone or something wet through.
