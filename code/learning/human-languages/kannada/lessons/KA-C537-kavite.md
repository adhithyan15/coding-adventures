## You'll want to know: ಕವಿತೆ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C537-KAVYA537-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ಕವಿತೆ** — *kavite* — "a poem".

> ಮಗಳು ಶಾಲೆಯಲ್ಲಿ ಒಂದು ಕವಿತೆ ಓದಿದಳು.
>
> *magaḷu śāleyalli ondu kavite ōdidaḷu.*
>
> My daughter read a poem at school.

Also ಕವನ.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಕವಿತೆ** (*kavite*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *kavitā*, "poetry" (Monier-Williams), built on *kavi*, "a poet".

## What you've built

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
