German, chapter 381: Vorschriften.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
die Vorschrift — the regulation.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for the registration, then the German for to withdraw.

You'll want to know: die Vorschrift.
die Vorschrift — "the regulation".
Nach der Vorschrift muss man im Labor eine Schutzbrille tragen. According to the regulations you have to wear goggles in the lab.
Plural: die Vorschriften. Dienst nach Vorschrift — work-to-rule.

The word, taken apart.
Vorschrift goes with vorschreiben "to lay down", built on Schrift "writing", from Old High German scrift and Latin scrīptum (EtymDB): a rule written out in front of you.

Lesson 2 of 5.
vorschreiben — to lay down (a rule), to prescribe.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to withdraw, then the German for the regulation.

You'll want to know: vorschreiben.
vorschreiben — "to lay down (a rule), to prescribe".
Das Gesetz schreibt vor, dass Kinder im Auto einen Kindersitz brauchen. The law lays down that children need a child seat in the car.
Separable and strong: es schreibt vor, es hat vorgeschrieben. Du kannst mir nichts vorschreiben — you cannot tell me what to do.

The word, taken apart.
vorschreiben is built from vor "before" and schreiben, from Old High German scrīban, a very early borrowing from Latin scrībere (EtymDB); EtymDB lists English scribe as a cousin.

Lesson 3 of 5.
beachten — to observe, to pay attention to.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for the regulation, then the German for to lay down.

You'll want to know: beachten.
beachten — "to observe, to pay attention to".
Viele Autofahrer beachten das Tempolimit nicht. Many drivers do not observe the speed limit.
Inseparable: er beachtet, er hat beachtet. Etwas nicht beachten — to ignore something.

The word, taken apart.
beachten is inherited from Middle High German beahten and Old High German biahtōn (EtymDB), built on Acht "attention", Old High German ahta (EtymDB): you pay attention.

Lesson 4 of 5.
einhalten — to keep to, to comply with.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to lay down, then the German for to observe.

You'll want to know: einhalten.
einhalten — "to keep to, to comply with".
Wir müssen die Frist einhalten. We have to meet the deadline.
Separable and strong: er hält ein, er hat eingehalten. Ein Versprechen einhalten — to keep a promise.

The word, taken apart.
einhalten is built from ein and halten "to hold", inherited from Proto-Germanic haldaną (EtymDB): you hold to what was agreed.

Lesson 5 of 5.
verpflichtet — obliged, required.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to observe, then the German for to keep to.

You'll want to know: verpflichtet.
verpflichtet — "obliged, required".
Der Arbeitgeber ist verpflichtet, den Lohn pünktlich zu zahlen. The employer is obliged to pay wages on time.
Zu etwas verpflichtet sein — to be obliged to do something. From verpflichten — to oblige.

The word, taken apart.
verpflichtet is the participle of verpflichten, built on Pflicht "duty", inherited from Old High German pfliht and Proto-Germanic plihtiz (EtymDB).
