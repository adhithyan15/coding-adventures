## You'll want to know: clāmor, clāmōris
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C321-MINAE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**clāmor, clāmōris** — "a shout, shouting".

> Subitō clāmor ingēns in viā ortus est.
>
> *Suddenly a huge shout went up in the street.*

Masculine, 3rd declension, from *clāmō* (taught). *Clāmōrem tollere*: to raise a shout. English *clamour*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**clāmor** is built on *clāmō*, "to shout" (EtymDB). English *clamour* comes from it through French.
