Urdu, chapter 281: Cheats.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ٹھگنا (ṭhagnā) — to cheat; to swindle.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for to tie up, then the Urdu for a looter.

You'll want to know: ٹھگنا.
ٹھگنا — ṭhagnā — "to cheat; to swindle".
اس نے مجھے ٹھگا۔ us ne mujhe ṭhagā. He cheated me.
Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے دکاندار کو ٹھگا, us ne dukāndār ko ṭhagā.

The word, taken apart.
ٹھگنا (ṭhagnā) is inherited from ṭhagg, a form CDIAL reconstructs (CDIAL 5489), which also lists the noun ṭhag, a swindler. English thug was taken from that noun.

You'll want to know: دھوکا.
دھوکا — dhokā — "a deception; a trick".
اگر یہ دھوکا ہے تو پولیس بلائیے۔ agar yih dhokā hai to polīs bulāiye. If this is a trick, call the police.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
دھوکا (dhokā) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin dhokā, "deceit", under dhrōkṣa, a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 6894). دھوکا دینا is to deceive someone.

Lesson 3 of 5.
رشوت (rishvat) — a bribe.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for to cheat, then the Urdu for a deception.

You'll want to know: رشوت.
رشوت — rishvat — "a bribe".
داروغے نے رشوت مانگی۔ dāroghe ne rishvat māṅgī. The inspector asked for a bribe.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
رشوت (rishvat) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic rishwa, "a bribe". It is feminine, so the verb agrees: رشوت مانگی.

Warm-up.

Before the new one: say the Urdu for a deception, then the Urdu for a bribe.

You'll want to know: بدمعاش.
بدمعاش — badmāsh — "a crook; a rascal".
وہ آدمی بدمعاش ہے۔ voh ādmī badmāsh hai. That man is a crook.
Masculine or feminine, by the person meant.

The word, taken apart.
بدمعاش (badmāsh) is usually traced to Persian bad, "bad", and Arabic maʿāsh, "a living": one who lives by bad means.

یہ نوٹ جعلی ہے۔ yih noṭ jālī hai. This banknote is forged.

The word, taken apart.
is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic jaʿl, "a making, a fabrication", with the adjective ending -ī. نقلی is any copy; a جعلی (jālī) note is one made to pass as real.
