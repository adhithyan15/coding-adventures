Arabic, chapter 293: Reporters and statements.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
مراسل (murāsil) — a correspondent, a reporter.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a sentence, then the Arabic for a barber.

You'll want to know: مراسل.
مراسل — murāsil — "a correspondent, a reporter".
كتب المراسل عن المطر في القرية. kataba al-murāsil ʿan al-maṭar fī al-qarya. The correspondent wrote about the rain in the village.

The word, taken apart.
مراسل (murāsil) is built on the native Arabic root r-s-l (Lane), the root of arsala, "to send". Murāsil is the Form III participle, "one who writes back and forth": a correspondent.

بيان (bayān) is built on the native Arabic root b-y-n (Lane), "to be apart; to become clear", the root of bayna, "between". A bayān makes a thing clear.

Lesson 3 of 5.

[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a correspondent, then the Arabic for a statement.

You'll want to know: صحيفة.
صحيفة — ṣaḥīfa — "a gazette, a newspaper".
تقرأ أمي صحيفة كل صباح. taqraʾu ummī ṣaḥīfa kulla ṣabāḥ. My mother reads a newspaper every morning.
Plural: صحف, ṣuḥuf.

The word, taken apart.
صحيفة (ṣaḥīfa) is built on the native Arabic root ṣ-ḥ-f (Lane), where it names a written leaf or page. Modern Arabic gave the old word the newspaper.

Lesson 5 of 5.
صرح (ṣarraḥa) — to declare, to state.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a gazette, then the Arabic for to announce.

You'll want to know: صرح.
صرح — ṣarraḥa — "to declare, to state".
صرح المدير بأن المكتب مغلق. ṣarraḥa al-mudīr bi-anna al-maktab mughlaq. The manager stated that the office was closed.
Present: يصرح, yuṣarriḥ.
It takes بـ before what is stated: صرح (ṣarraḥa) بأن …

The word, taken apart.
صرح (ṣarraḥa) is built on the native Arabic root ṣ-r-ḥ (Lane), "pure, unmixed, clear". It is Form II, "to make clear": to say a thing plainly.
