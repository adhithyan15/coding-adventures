Kannada, chapter 490: Fear and Shyness.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಹೆದರು (hedaru) — to be afraid; to fear.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for chalk, then the Kannada for a ruler.

You'll want to know: ಹೆದರು.
ಹೆದರು — hedaru — "to be afraid; to fear".
ಮಗು ಕತ್ತಲೆಗೆ ಹೆದರುತ್ತದೆ. magu kattalege hedaruttade. The child is afraid of the dark.
Past (I …): ಹೆದರಿದೆ, hedaride.
What you fear takes -ಗೆ: ಕತ್ತಲೆಗೆ ಹೆದರು (hedaru), to be afraid of the dark. The noun for the feeling is ಭಯ.

The word, taken apart.
ಹೆದರು (hedaru) is native Dravidian: DEDR 4401 lists Kannada bedaṟu and hedaṟu, "to be alarmed, to take fright", with a noun meaning "fright" beside them. The b- and h- forms are two shapes of one old verb.

Lesson 2 of 5.
ನಾಚು (nācu) — to feel shy; to be bashful.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a ruler, then the Kannada for to be afraid.

You'll want to know: ನಾಚು.
ನಾಚು — nācu — "to feel shy; to be bashful".
ಹೊಸ ಅತಿಥಿಯನ್ನು ನೋಡಿ ಹುಡುಗಿ ನಾಚಿದಳು. hosa atithiyannu nōḍi huḍugi nācidaḷu. Seeing the new guest, the girl felt shy.
Past (I …): ನಾಚಿದೆ, nācide.
The noun beside it is ನಾಚಿಕೆ, shyness: the verb is the feeling happening, the noun is the feeling itself.

The word, taken apart.
ನಾಚು (nācu) is native Dravidian: DEDR 3639 gives Kannada nāṇcu, nāñcu and nācu, "to become abashed", beside a noun for bashfulness and modesty.

Lesson 3 of 5.
ಆತಂಕ (ātaṅka) — anxiety; dread.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to be afraid, then the Kannada for to feel shy.

You'll want to know: ಆತಂಕ.
ಆತಂಕ — ātaṅka — "anxiety; dread".
ಪರೀಕ್ಷೆಗೆ ಮೊದಲು ನನಗೆ ಆತಂಕ ಆಯಿತು. parīkṣege modalu nanage ātaṅka āyitu. Before the exam I felt anxious.
A feeling takes the dative of the person: ನನಗೆ ಆತಂಕ (ātaṅka) ಆಯಿತು, I grew anxious.

The word, taken apart.
ಆತಂಕ (ātaṅka) is borrowed from Sanskrit ātaṅka, which Monier-Williams gives first as a sickness of the body and then as affliction of mind, disquiet and fear (Monier-Williams). Kannada keeps the second sense: the dread before something happens.

Lesson 4 of 5.
ಮುಜುಗರ (mujugara) — embarrassment; awkwardness.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to feel shy, then the Kannada for anxiety.

You'll want to know: ಮುಜುಗರ.
ಮುಜುಗರ — mujugara — "embarrassment; awkwardness".
ಎಲ್ಲರ ಮುಂದೆ ಹಾಡುವಾಗ ನನಗೆ ಮುಜುಗರ ಆಯಿತು. ellara munde hāḍuvāga nanage mujugara āyitu. Singing in front of everyone, I felt embarrassed.
Sharper than ನಾಚಿಕೆ: the discomfort of an awkward moment, often caused by someone else.

The word, taken apart.
ಮುಜುಗರ (mujugara) is an everyday Kannada word; its origin is not settled, and none of the dictionaries we can open lists it.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ಕಳವಳ (kaḷavaḷa) — unease; agitation.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for anxiety, then the Kannada for embarrassment.

You'll want to know: ಕಳವಳ.
ಕಳವಳ — kaḷavaḷa — "unease; agitation".
ರಾತ್ರಿ ಮಗ ಬರಲಿಲ್ಲ, ಅಮ್ಮನಿಗೆ ಕಳವಳ ಆಯಿತು. rātri maga baralilla, ammanige kaḷavaḷa āyitu. The son had not come by night; his mother grew uneasy.
A restless worry that will not settle; ಕಳವಳಗೊಳ್ಳು, to grow uneasy.

The word, taken apart.
ಕಳವಳ (kaḷavaḷa) is native Dravidian: DEDR 1306 lists Kannada kaḷakaḷa and kaḷavaḷike, "agitation of the mind, distress", and the verb kaḷavaḷisu, "to be agitated".
