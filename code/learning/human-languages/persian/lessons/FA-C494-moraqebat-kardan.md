## You'll want to know: مراقبت کردن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C494-CARE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**مراقبت کردن** (*morâqebat kardan*) — "to look after, to take care of".

> او از مادر پیرش مراقبت میکند.
>
> *u az mâdar-e pirash morâqebat mikonad.*
>
> She looks after her elderly mother.

*az kasi morâqebat kardan*. *morâqeb bâsh* — take care.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*morâqebat* is a loan, usually traced to Arabic *murāqaba*, 'watching over'. EtymDB has no entry for it. *kardan* is inherited Persian.
