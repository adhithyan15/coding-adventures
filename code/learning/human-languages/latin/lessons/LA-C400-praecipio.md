## You'll want to know: praecipiō, praecipere
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C400-DOCEO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**praecipiō, praecipere** — "to instruct, to advise; to take beforehand".

> Medicus mihi praecēpit ut quiēscerem.
>
> *The doctor told me to rest.*

*praecipiō, praecipere, praecēpī*; *prae* + *capiō*: to take beforehand, then to lay down a rule: *praecipiō tibi ut*, I instruct you to. English *precept*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*praecipiō* is built from *prae* "before" and *capiō* "to take" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short): to take in advance, then to tell in advance. English *precept* comes from it.
