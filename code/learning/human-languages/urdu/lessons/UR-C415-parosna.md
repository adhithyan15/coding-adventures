## You'll want to know: پروسنا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C415-DHABA415-05]; assesses=[] -->

**پروسنا** — *parosnā* — "to serve (food)".

> ڈھابے والے نے ہمیں گرم روٹی پروسی۔
>
> *ḍhābe vāle ne hameṅ garm roṭī parosī.*
>
> The man at the eatery served us hot bread.

Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے کھانا پروسا, *us ne khānā parosā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**پروسنا** (*parosnā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *parosnā*, "to offer food to a guest", under Sanskrit *párivēṣati* (CDIAL 7888).
