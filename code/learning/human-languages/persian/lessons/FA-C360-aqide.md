## You'll want to know: عقیده
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C360-AQIDE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**عقیده** (*'aqide*) — "an opinion, a belief".

> عقیده من این است که او راست میگوید.
>
> *'aqide-ye man in ast ke u râst miguyad.*
>
> My opinion is that he is telling the truth.

Plural *'aqâyed*, an Arabic broken plural. *Be 'aqide-ye man* — in my opinion, a little more formal than *be nazar-e man*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**عقیده** was borrowed from Arabic *'aqīda* "a creed, a firm belief" (EtymDB). A belief, in this picture, is something you have tied fast.
