## You'll want to know: ஒடுக்கு
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C371-CAMATTUVAM371-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ஒடுக்கு** — *oṭukku* — "to oppress; to suppress".

> அந்த அரசன் தன் மக்களை ஒடுக்கினான்.
>
> *anta aracaṉ taṉ makkaḷai oṭukkiṉāṉ.*
>
> That king oppressed his people.

Past (I …): ஒடுக்கினேன், *oṭukkiṉēṉ*.

Who is oppressed takes -ஐ. The noun is ஒடுக்குமுறை, oppression.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ஒடுக்கு** (*oṭukku*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 954 lists Tamil *oṭuṅku*, "to be restrained, shrink", and its causative *oṭukku*, "to restrain, repress".
