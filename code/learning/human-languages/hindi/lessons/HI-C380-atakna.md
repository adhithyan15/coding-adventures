## You'll want to know: अटकना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C380-HAL380-04]; assesses=[] -->

**अटकना** — *aṭaknā* — "to get stuck, to be held up".

> अगर दरवाज़ा अटकेगा तो उसे ज़ोर से धकेलो।
>
> *agar darvāzā aṭkegā to use zor se dhakelo.*
>
> If the door sticks, push it hard.

Past (it …): वह दरवाज़ा अटक गया, *vah darvāzā aṭak gayā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अटकना** (*aṭaknā*) is inherited from *\*aṭṭakk*, "obstruct, stop", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 182).
