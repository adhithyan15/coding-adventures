## You'll want to know: a avenida
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C437-RUAS-01]; assesses=[] -->

**a avenida** — "the avenue".

> A avenida principal tem muitas lojas.
>
> *The main avenue has a lot of shops.*

Shortened to *Av.* in addresses.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*avenida* was borrowed from Spanish *avenida*, which came from French *avenue* 'way of approach' (EtymDB), on Latin *advenīre* 'to arrive'.
