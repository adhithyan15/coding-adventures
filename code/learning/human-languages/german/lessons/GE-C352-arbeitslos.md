## You'll want to know: arbeitslos
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C352-ARBEITSLOS-02]; assesses=[] -->

**arbeitslos** — "unemployed, out of work".

> Nach der Kündigung war er drei Monate arbeitslos.
>
> *After he was given notice, he was out of work for three months.*

*Der Arbeitslose, die Arbeitslose* — the unemployed person, declined like an adjective.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*arbeitslos* joins *Arbeit* "work", Proto-Germanic *arbaidiz* (EtymDB), to *-los* "without", from Old High German *lōs* "loose, free, lacking" (EtymDB).
