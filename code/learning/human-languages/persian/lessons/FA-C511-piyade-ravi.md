## You'll want to know: پیاده روی
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C511-OUTDOORS-04]; assesses=[] -->

**پیاده روی** (*piyâde ravi*) — "walking (for exercise), a walk".

> پدربزرگم هر صبح پیاده روی میکند.
>
> *pedarbozorgam har sobh piyâde ravi mikonad.*
>
> My grandfather goes walking every morning.

*piyâde* 'on foot' + *ravi* 'going'. Not *piyâderow*, the pavement.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*piyâde ravi* is built on native Persian parts: *piyâde*, 'on foot', which EtymDB traces to Middle Persian (EtymDB), and *ravi*, from *rav-*, the present stem of *raftan*, 'to go'.
