## You'll want to know: o barqueiro
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C418-BARCO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**o barqueiro** — "the boatman, the ferryman".

> O barqueiro leva as pessoas de uma margem à outra.
>
> *The boatman takes people from one bank to the other.*

Built on *o barco*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*barqueiro* is built on *barco* 'boat' with *-eiro*, a person who works with it; the family goes back to Late Latin *barca* (Lewis & Short).
