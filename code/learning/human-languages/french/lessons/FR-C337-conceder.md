## You'll want to know: concéder
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C337-COMPROMIS-05]; assesses=[] -->

**concéder** — "to concede, to grant".

> Je te concède ce point : tu avais raison.
>
> *I grant you that point: you were right.*

*Je concède*, like *céder*. Formal; in speech, *je reconnais que* is common.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*concéder* comes from Latin *concēdere* "to withdraw, to grant" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short), a strengthened form of *cēdere* "to go, to yield".
