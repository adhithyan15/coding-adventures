## You'll want to know: lo stereotipo
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C365-GIUSTIZIA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**lo stereotipo** — "the stereotype".

> Gli stereotipi sugli italiani sono tanti: pizza, mandolino...
>
> *There are many stereotypes about Italians: pizza, mandolin...*

Stress: *stereòtipo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*stereotipo* is usually traced to French *stéréotype*, a printing word built from Greek *stereós* "solid" and *týpos* "a blow, an impression", the word behind Latin *typus* "a figure, an image" (Lewis & Short): a fixed plate that prints the same image again and again.
