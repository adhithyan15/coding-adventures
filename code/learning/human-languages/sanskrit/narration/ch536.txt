Sanskrit, chapter 536: A Stroke of Luck.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
अदृष्टम् (adṛṣṭam) — the unseen; fate.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a breaking, then the Sanskrit for one's word.

You'll want to know: अदृष्टम्.
अदृष्टम् — adṛṣṭam — "the unseen; fate".
यदि अदृष्टं साहाय्यं करोति, तर्हि सः जयति। yadi adṛṣṭaṃ sāhāyyaṃ karoti, tarhi saḥ jayati. If fate helps him, he wins.
A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
अदृष्टम् (adṛṣṭam) is built on a, "not", and dṛṣṭa, "seen", from the root √dṛś, "to see": the unseen; MW gives adṛṣṭa as what is beyond observation, the merit or demerit of one's deeds, fate (MW).

यदि भवितव्यता एवम् अस्ति, तर्हि किं करोमि? yadi bhavitavyatā evam asti, tarhi kiṃ karomi? If that is what must be, what can I do?

भवितव्यता (bhavitavyatā) is built on bhavitavya, "what is to be", from the root √bhū, "to be", with the abstract ending -tā; MW gives bhavitavyatā as inevitable consequence, necessity, destiny (MW).

Lesson 3 of 5.
संयोगः (saṃyogaḥ) — a coincidence; a conjunction.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for the unseen, then the Sanskrit for what must be.

You'll want to know: संयोगः.
संयोगः — saṃyogaḥ — "a coincidence; a conjunction".
यदि संयोगः भवति, तर्हि त्वां श्वः द्रक्ष्यामि। yadi saṃyogaḥ bhavati, tarhi tvāṃ śvaḥ drakṣyāmi. If chance brings us together, I'll see you tomorrow.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
संयोगः (saṃyogaḥ) is built on sam, "together", and yoga, "joining", from the root √yuj; MW gives saṃyoga as conjunction, connection, union; "a coincidence" is its modern use (MW).

Warm-up.

You'll want to know: यदृच्छया.
यदृच्छया — yadṛcchayā — "by chance".
यदि यदृच्छया मार्गे मित्रं पश्यामि, तर्हि मोदे। yadi yadṛcchayā mārge mitraṃ paśyāmi, tarhi mode. If I happen to meet a friend on the road, I am glad.

The word, taken apart.
यदृच्छया (yadṛcchayā) is built from yad, "what, whatever", and ṛcchā, as MW gives it; MW records yadṛcchā as self-will, accident, chance, and yadṛcchayā as by accident (MW).

Lesson 5 of 5.
सम्पद्यते (sampadyate) — turns out well.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a coincidence, then the Sanskrit for by chance.

You'll want to know: सम्पद्यते.
सम्पद्यते — sampadyate — "turns out well".
यदि सर्वं सम्यक् सम्पद्यते, तर्हि वयम् उत्सवं करिष्यामः। yadi sarvaṃ samyak sampadyate, tarhi vayam utsavaṃ kariṣyāmaḥ. If everything turns out well, we will celebrate.
Past (it …): कार्यं सम्पन्नवत्, kāryaṃ sampannavat.

The word, taken apart.
सम्पद्यते (sampadyate) is built on sam, "together, well", and the root √pad, "to fall, to go"; MW gives sampad as to fall out well, to turn out well, to succeed; its past participle is sampanna (MW).
