## You'll want to know: 打车
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C409-TRAFFIC409-05]; assesses=[] -->

**打车** — *dǎchē* — "to take a taxi".

> **下雨的时候不好打车** *xià yǔ de shíhou bù hǎo dǎchē* — When it rains it's hard to get a taxi.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**打车** (*dǎchē*) is built from 打 and 车 'vehicle', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *taengX* and *tsyhae* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to strike'. The sense 'to hire' in this word is not given there, and *taengX* would regularly give a different final, so dǎ is irregular.
