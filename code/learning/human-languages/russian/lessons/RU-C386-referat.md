## You'll want to know: реферат
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C386-DOKLAD-02]; assesses=[] -->

**реферат** (*referát*) — "an essay, a summary paper".

> Студенты пишут реферат по литературе.
>
> *Studénty píshut referát po literatúre.*
>
> The students are writing an essay on literature.

Masculine. A short paper that sums up what sources say.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**реферат** was borrowed from German *Referat* (EtymDB), which is usually traced to Latin *referat* "let him report", a form of *referre* "to bring back, to report" (Lewis & Short).
