## You'll want to know: શાહુકાર
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C428-DEBT428-04]; assesses=[] -->

**શાહુકાર** — *śāhukār* — "a moneylender".

> ગામનો શાહુકાર ઘણું વ્યાજ લે છે.
>
> *gāmno śāhukār ghaṇũ vyāj le che.*
>
> The village moneylender charges a lot of interest.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**શાહુકાર** (*śāhukār*) is built on શાહુ, a banker, which goes back to Sanskrit *sādhu*, "good, honest" (CDIAL 13337), with -કાર, "one who does".
