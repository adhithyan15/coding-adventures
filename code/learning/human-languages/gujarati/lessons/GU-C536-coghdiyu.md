# ચોઘડિયું (coghḍiyũ) — a choghadiyu (a lucky or unlucky part of the day)

## You'll want to know: ચોઘડિયું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C536-MUHURT536-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ચોઘડિયું** — *coghḍiyũ* — "a choghadiyu (a lucky or unlucky part of the day)".

> જો ચોઘડિયું સારું હશે તો દાદા સવારે દુકાન ખોલશે.
>
> *jo coghḍiyũ sārũ haśe to dādā savāre dukān kholśe.*
>
> If the time of day is lucky, my grandfather will open the shop in the morning.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ચોઘડિયું** (*coghḍiyũ*) is usually explained as built from *co-*, "four", and *ghaḍī*, an old unit of time, from Sanskrit *ghaṭikā* (Monier-Williams): the day is cut into such periods, each good or bad for beginning a task.
