## You'll want to know: xuānchuáncè
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C454-INFO454-05]; assesses=[] -->

*xuānchuáncè* — "brochure; leaflet".

> *fúwùtái yǒu hěn duō miǎnfèi de xuānchuáncè* — The information desk has lots of free brochures.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*xuānchuáncè* is built from *xuānchuán* 'to spread the word' and *cè* 'a booklet', all inherited from Middle Chinese, *sjwen*, *drjwen* and *tsrheak* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to spread', the second 'to turn' and the last as a set of bamboo slips.
