## You'll want to know: shèng
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C451-SPECIAL451-05]; assesses=[] -->

*shèng* — "to be left over".

> *shèng le hěn duō cài wǒmen dǎbāo* — There's a lot of food left, so we'll take it away.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*shèng* is inherited from Middle Chinese *zyingH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed as what is long and to spare.
