# Return to the reader and the clock

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-A1-FORM-MIXED-CUES-01, PA-A1-FORM-TIMED-01] -->

Keep the previous lesson's six-field first attempt. In one minute, point to a line
whose card mark you selected correctly and a line you would repair. Do not
rewrite the timed page; its first-attempt evidence stays as it was.

## Writing — one small timed opening
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-A1-MESSAGE-PACING-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: controlled-composition -->

Close the earlier message pages. As Aman, write to Manan in Gurmukhi. In
ninety seconds, greet your named reader and ask one familiar wellbeing
question. Choose your own taught words; no sample sentence is visible. Stop
on time and keep the first attempt.

## Guided Practice — inspect one boundary
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-A1-MESSAGE-PACING-01] -->

After the timer, check whether Manan is clearly the reader. Then check the
question mark and spacing. A repair belongs on another page and is not timed
evidence. This opening is not a complete A1 mock.

## Wrap-up recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-A1-MESSAGE-PACING-01] -->

The complete paper still needs a fresh purpose, a 30–40-word message, and a
twenty-minute form-plus-message clock. Those conditions belong to the two
separate mocks, not this short return.
