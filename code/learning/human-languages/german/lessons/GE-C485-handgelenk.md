## You'll want to know: das Handgelenk
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C485-GELENKE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**das Handgelenk** — "wrist".

> Sie trägt eine Uhr am linken Handgelenk.
>
> *She wears a watch on her left wrist.*

Plural: *die Handgelenke*. *Das Gelenk* — joint.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Handgelenk* is built on *Hand* and *Gelenk* "joint", inherited from Middle High German *gelenke* and Old High German *gilenki* "joint, bend, waist" (EtymDB).
