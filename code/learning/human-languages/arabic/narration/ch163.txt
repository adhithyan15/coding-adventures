Arabic, chapter 163: To celebrate, To invite, To marry, To shake hands with, To hug.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
احتفل (iḥtafala) — to celebrate.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to register, then the Arabic for to employ.

You'll want to know: احتفل.
احتفل — iḥtafala — "to celebrate".

The word, taken apart.
احتفل (iḥtafala) is built on the native Arabic root ḥ-f-l, "to gather, to fill" (Lane). Iḥtafala is Form VIII, "to gather for an occasion", to celebrate.

Lesson 3 of 5.
تزوج (tazawwaja) — to marry.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to celebrate, then the Arabic for to invite.

You'll want to know: تزوج.
تزوج — tazawwaja — "to marry".

The word, taken apart.
تزوج (tazawwaja) is built on the native Arabic root z-w-j, "one of a pair, a mate" (Lane). Tazawwaja is Form V, "to take a mate", built from zawj, which is usually traced through Aramaic to Greek zeugos, "yoke, pair" (EtymDB).
