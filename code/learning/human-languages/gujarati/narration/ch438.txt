Gujarati, chapter 438: Dawn and Dusk.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ઉજાસ (ujās) — the light of daybreak; brightness.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to congeal, then the Gujarati for to shiver all over.

You'll want to know: ઉજાસ.
ઉજાસ — ujās — "the light of daybreak; brightness".
સવારે બારીમાંથી ઓરડામાં ઉજાસ આવ્યો. savāre bārīmā̃thī orḍāmā̃ ujās āvyo. In the morning light came into the room through the window.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ઉજાસ (ujās) goes back to ujjhāṣa, "shining", a form CDIAL reconstructs; CDIAL lists the Gujarati word, "brightness, light" (CDIAL 1678).

Lesson 2 of 5.
સંધ્યા (sandhyā) — dusk; the evening twilight.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to shiver all over, then the Gujarati for the light of daybreak.

You'll want to know: સંધ્યા.
સંધ્યા — sandhyā — "dusk; the evening twilight".
સંધ્યાનું આકાશ ગુલાબી છે. sandhyānũ ākāś gulābī che. The sky at dusk is pink.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
સંધ્યા (sandhyā) is a tatsama, Sanskrit saṁdhyā, "a joining", the meeting of day and night (Monier-Williams).

ધુમ્મસમાં બધું ધૂંધળું લાગે છે. dhummasmā̃ badhũ dhū̃dhḷũ lāge che. In the fog everything looks hazy.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ટમટમવું (ṭamṭamvũ) — to twinkle.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for dim, then the Gujarati for hazy.

You'll want to know: ટમટમવું.
ટમટમવું — ṭamṭamvũ — "to twinkle".
રાત્રે આકાશમાં તારા ટમટમે છે. rātre ākāśmā̃ tārā ṭamaṭme che. At night the stars twinkle in the sky.
Past (it …): તે તારો ટમટમ્યો, te tāro ṭamṭamyo.

The word, taken apart.
ટમટમવું (ṭamṭamvũ) doubles a sound, as words that name a flicker often do. No dictionary we use records an older form, so its history is not settled.
