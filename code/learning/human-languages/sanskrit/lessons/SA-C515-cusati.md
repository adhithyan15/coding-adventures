## You'll want to know: चूषति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C515-SVADA515-05]; assesses=[] -->

**चूषति** — *cūṣati* — "sucks".

> बालः आम्रं चूषति।
>
> *bālaḥ āmraṃ cūṣati.*
>
> The child sucks the mango.

Past (he …): सः आम्रं चूषितवान्, *saḥ āmraṃ cūṣitavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**चूषति** (*cūṣati*) comes from the root √cūṣ, "to suck, to suck out"; its past participle is *cūṣita* (MW).
