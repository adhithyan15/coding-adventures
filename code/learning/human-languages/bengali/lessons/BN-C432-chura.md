## You'll want to know: চূড়া
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C432-CHURA432-01]; assesses=[] -->

**চূড়া** — *chūṛā* — "a peak; a summit; a top".

> আমরা ভোরে পাহাড়ের চূড়ায় উঠলাম।
>
> *āmrā bhore pāhāṛer chūṛāy uṭhlām.*
>
> We climbed to the summit at dawn.

Also the top of anything tall: মন্দিরের চূড়া, the spire of a temple.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**চূড়া** (*chūṛā*) is Sanskrit *cūḍā*, "the hair on the top of the head, a crest" (Monier-Williams); the sense of a mountain top is a natural step, though Monier-Williams gives the head first.
