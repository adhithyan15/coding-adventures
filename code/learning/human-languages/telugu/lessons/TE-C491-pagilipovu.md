# పగిలిపోవు (pagilipōvu) — to crack; to burst

## You'll want to know: పగిలిపోవు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C491-TUPPU491-04]; assesses=[] -->

**పగిలిపోవు** — *pagilipōvu* — "to crack; to burst".

> చేతి నుంచి పడి గ్లాసు పగిలిపోయింది.
>
> *cēti nuñci paḍi glāsu pagilipōyindi.*
>
> The glass fell from my hand and broke.

Past (it …): పగిలిపోయింది, *pagilipōyindi*.

Said of glass, pots, pipes and lips in the cold. Its subject is a thing, so the past shown is the third person: పగిలిపోయింది, it broke.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**పగిలిపోవు** (*pagilipōvu*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 3808 lists Telugu *pagulu*, "to break, to crack, to burst", here with పోవు, "to go" (DEDR 4572).
