## You'll want to know: うろうろ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C396-YUKISAKI396-05]; assesses=[] -->

**うろうろ** — *urōro* — "wandering restlessly".

> **えきの まえで うろうろしていました。** — *eki no mae de urōro shite imashita* — "I was hanging about in front of the station."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**うろうろ** (*urōro*) is a native mimetic word that pictures restless, back-and-forth wandering: an imitative Japanese form, inherited rather than borrowed, whose older origin is not settled (JMdict).
