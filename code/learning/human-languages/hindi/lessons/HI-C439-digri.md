## You'll want to know: डिग्री
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C439-TAP439-04]; assesses=[] -->

**डिग्री** — *ḍigrī* — "a degree".

> आज दोपहर को चालीस डिग्री गर्मी थी।
>
> *āj dopahar ko cālīs ḍigrī garmī thī.*
>
> It was forty degrees this afternoon.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**डिग्री** (*ḍigrī*) is borrowed from English *degree*, of heat and also of a university; it is feminine.
