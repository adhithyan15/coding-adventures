## You'll want to know: सुख
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C450-MAN450-03]; assesses=[] -->

**सुख** — *sukh* — "happiness; comfort".

> सुख पैशात नसतं, मनात असतं.
>
> *sukh paiśāt nastaṁ, manāt astaṁ.*
>
> Happiness is not in money, it is in the mind.

Neuter.

Neuter, the opposite of दुःख. सुखी, happy and content.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सुख** (*sukh*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *sukha*, "comfort, happiness" (CDIAL 13451).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
