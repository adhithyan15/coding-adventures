## You'll want to know: táo
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C301-ESCAPE301-01]; assesses=[] -->

*táo* — "to run away; to flee".

> *fángzi zháohuǒ le, dàjiā kuài táo* — The house is on fire, everyone run!

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*táo* is inherited from Middle Chinese *daw* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses it 'to go away', 'to avoid' and 'to flee'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
