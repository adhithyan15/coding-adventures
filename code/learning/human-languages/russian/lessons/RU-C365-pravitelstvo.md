## You'll want to know: правительство
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C365-VLAST-02]; assesses=[] -->

**правительство** (*pravítelstvo*) — "the government".

> Правительство обещало повысить пенсии.
>
> *Pravítelstvo obeshchálo povýsit pénsii.*
>
> The government promised to raise pensions.

Neuter. The cabinet that runs a country; *власти* is broader.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**правительство** is built on *правитель* "a ruler", from *править* "to rule, to steer", from *правый* "right", Proto-Slavic *\*pravŭ* "right, correct" (EtymDB).
