# એન્જિન (enjin) — an engine (of a train)

## You'll want to know: એન્જિન
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C393-ENGINE393-01]; assesses=[] -->

**એન્જિન** — *enjin* — "an engine (of a train)".

> આ ટ્રેનનું એન્જિન ઘણું જૂનું છે.
>
> *ā ṭrennũ enjin ghaṇũ jūnũ che.*
>
> The engine of this train is very old.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**એન્જિન** (*enjin*) is borrowed from English *engine*; on the railway it is the engine at the front that pulls the train.
