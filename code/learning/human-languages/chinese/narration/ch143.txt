Mandarin Chinese, chapter 143: Clock Words.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
miǎo — second (of time).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for to sort out, then the Mandarin for to water the plants.

You'll want to know: miǎo.
miǎo — "second (of time)".
qǐng děng wǔ miǎo — Please wait five seconds.
Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
miǎo is inherited from Middle Chinese mjiewX (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'the beard of grain'. The tiny unit of time is not given there; the step from something very fine is the likely one, but it is not settled.

What you've built.
One more word for times of day.

Lesson 2 of 5.
fēnzhōng — minute (a length of time).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for to water the plants, then the Mandarin for second (of time).

You'll want to know: fēnzhōng.
fēnzhōng — "minute (a length of time)".
shí fēnzhōng yǐhòu wǒmen zǒu — We're leaving in ten minutes.
Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
fēnzhōng is built from fēn, the minute of the clock, and zhōng 'bell', both inherited from Middle Chinese, pjun and tsyowng (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription); the dictionary glosses the second as a musical instrument.

Lesson 3 of 5.
chà — to be short of; to (before the hour).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for second (of time), then the Mandarin for minute (a length of time).

You'll want to know: chà.
chà — "to be short of; to (before the hour)".
xiànzài chà wǔ fēn bā diǎn — It's five to eight now.
Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
chà is inherited from Middle Chinese, where the Guangyun lists several readings; tsrhae (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription) is glossed 'to choose' and 'amiss'. None of them gives the fourth tone of chà by the regular sound change, so how this reading came is not settled.

Lesson 4 of 5.
zhǔnshí — on time; punctual.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for minute (a length of time), then the Mandarin for to be short of.

You'll want to know: zhǔnshí.
zhǔnshí — "on time; punctual".
huǒchē hěn zhǔnshí — The trains run on time.
Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
zhǔnshí is built from zhǔn 'level, a standard', the zhǔn of zhǔnbèi, and 时 'time', both inherited from Middle Chinese, tsywinX and dzyi (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription): true to the time.

Lesson 5 of 5.
半天 (bàntiān) — a long while; half a day.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for to be short of, then the Mandarin for punctual.

You'll want to know: 半天.
半天 — bàntiān — "a long while; half a day".
wǒ děng le bàntiān — I waited for ages.

The word, taken apart.
半天 (bàntiān) is built from 半 'half' and 天 'day', both inherited from Middle Chinese, panH and then (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription): half a day, and in everyday talk, often with a sigh, a long while.
