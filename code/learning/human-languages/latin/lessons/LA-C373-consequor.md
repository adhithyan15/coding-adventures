## You'll want to know: cōnsequor, cōnsequī
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C373-EFFECTUS-02]; assesses=[] -->

**cōnsequor, cōnsequī** — "to follow; to achieve, to obtain".

> Quod voluit, cōnsecūtus est.
>
> *He achieved what he wanted.*

Deponent: *cōnsequor, cōnsequī, cōnsecūtus sum*. *Cōnsequitur ut*, it follows that. English *consequence*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*cōnsequor* is built from *con-* and *sequor* "to follow" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short): to follow all the way to the goal. English *consequence* comes from its family.
