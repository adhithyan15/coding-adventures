## You'll want to know: der Bildhauer
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C519-GALERIE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**der Bildhauer** — "sculptor".

> Der Bildhauer arbeitet mit Holz und Stein.
>
> *The sculptor works with wood and stone.*

Plural: *die Bildhauer*. A woman is *die Bildhauerin*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Bildhauer* is built on *Bild* "image", from Proto-Germanic *biliþją*, and *hauen* "to hew", inherited from Old High German *houwan* (EtymDB): someone who hews images.
