## You'll want to know: карманник
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C288-KRAZHA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**карманник** (*karmánnik*) — "a pickpocket".

> В автобусе работал карманник.
>
> *V avtóbuse rabótal karmánnik.*
>
> A pickpocket was at work on the bus.

Genitive *карманника*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*карманник* is built on *карман* "a pocket", which is usually traced to a Turkic word for a purse; EtymDB lists *карман* "pocket" with no parent.
