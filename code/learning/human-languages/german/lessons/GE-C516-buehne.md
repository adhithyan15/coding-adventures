## You'll want to know: die Bühne
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C516-BUEHNE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**die Bühne** — "stage".

> Die Band kommt um neun Uhr auf die Bühne.
>
> *The band comes on stage at nine.*

Plural: *die Bühnen*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Bühne* is inherited from Middle High German *büne* "wooden stage, platform, ceiling" (EtymDB).
