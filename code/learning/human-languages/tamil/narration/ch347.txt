Tamil, chapter 347: Evidence and Logic.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ஆதாரம் (ātāram) — evidence; proof; a source.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for a complaint, then the Tamil for an athlete.

You'll want to know: ஆதாரம்.
ஆதாரம் — ātāram — "evidence; proof; a source".
உங்களிடம் இதற்கு ஆதாரம் இருக்கிறதா? uṅkaḷiṭam itaṟku ātāram irukkiṟatā? Do you have evidence for this?
Plural: ஆதாரங்கள், ātāraṅkaḷ.
With -உடன், a -அம் noun takes its oblique -அத்து-: ஆதாரத்துடன், with evidence.

The word, taken apart.
ஆதாரம் (ātāram) is borrowed from Sanskrit ādhāra, "support, substratum" (CDIAL 1165). Tamil gives the final -a the noun ending -அம், and "what holds a claim up" became "evidence".

Lesson 2 of 5.
நிரூபி (nirūpi) — to prove.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for an athlete, then the Tamil for evidence.

You'll want to know: நிரூபி.
நிரூபி — nirūpi — "to prove".
அவர் தன் கருத்தை ஆதாரத்துடன் நிரூபித்தார். avar taṉ karuttai ātārattuṭaṉ nirūpittār. He proved his point with evidence.
Past (I …): நிரூபித்தேன், nirūpittēṉ.
What is proved takes -ஐ: கருத்தை நிரூபி (nirūpi), to prove a point.

The word, taken apart.
நிரூபி (nirūpi) is a verb Tamil built on Sanskrit nirūpaṇa, "stating, determining; examination" (Monier-Williams), with the verb ending -இ that many Sanskrit loans take, as in சுவாசி, "to breathe".

Lesson 3 of 5.
தர்க்கம் (tarkkam) — logic; reasoning.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for evidence, then the Tamil for to prove.

You'll want to know: தர்க்கம்.
தர்க்கம் — tarkkam — "logic; reasoning".
அவன் சொன்னதில் தர்க்கம் இல்லை. avaṉ coṉṉatil tarkkam illai. There is no logic in what he said.
தர்க்கமாக, logically: an -அம் noun + -ஆக makes the adverb.

The word, taken apart.
தர்க்கம் (tarkkam) is borrowed from Sanskrit tarka, "inquiry; conjecture" (CDIAL 5714), the name Indian thought gave to the study of reasoning.

Lesson 4 of 5.
அடிப்படை (aṭippaṭai) — a basis; a foundation; basic.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for to prove, then the Tamil for logic.

You'll want to know: அடிப்படை.
அடிப்படை — aṭippaṭai — "a basis; a foundation; basic".
இந்த முடிவின் அடிப்படை என்ன? inta muṭiviṉ aṭippaṭai eṉṉa? What is the basis of this decision?
Before another noun it means basic: அடிப்படை (aṭippaṭai) உரிமை, a basic right.

The word, taken apart.
அடிப்படை (aṭippaṭai) is a native Tamil compound built from aṭi, "foot, base, bottom" (DEDR 72), and paṭai, "layer, stratum" (DEDR 3852): the bottom layer that the rest stands on.

Lesson 5 of 5.
விளக்கம் (viḷakkam) — an explanation.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for logic, then the Tamil for a basis.

You'll want to know: விளக்கம்.
விளக்கம் — viḷakkam — "an explanation".
ஆசிரியர் தெளிவான விளக்கம் கொடுத்தார். āciriyar teḷivāṉa viḷakkam koṭuttār. The teacher gave a clear explanation.
Its verb is விளக்கு, to explain, the same word as விளக்கு, a lamp: an explanation sheds light.

The word, taken apart.
விளக்கம் (viḷakkam) is native Dravidian: DEDR 5496 lists Tamil viḷakku, "to make bright, to explain", and viḷakkam, "clearness, light", in the family of veḷ, "white, bright".
