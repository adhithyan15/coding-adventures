## You'll want to know: la fedeltà
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C499-LITIGI-04]; assesses=[] -->

**la fedeltà** — "faithfulness, loyalty".

> Per me la fedeltà è importante in una coppia.
>
> *For me faithfulness is important in a couple.*

The adjective is *fedele*; the wedding ring is *la fede*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*fedeltà* is inherited from Latin *fidēlitātem* "faithfulness" (EtymDB), from *fidēs* "trust".
