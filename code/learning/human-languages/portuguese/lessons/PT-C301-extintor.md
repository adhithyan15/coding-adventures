## You'll want to know: o extintor
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C301-INCENDIO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**o extintor** — "the fire extinguisher".

> O empregado apagou o fogo com o extintor.
>
> *The employee put out the fire with the extinguisher.*

Built on *extinguir*, ‘to put out’.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*extintor* is a learned word from Latin *exstinctor* "one who puts out", from *exstinguere* "to put out, to quench" (Lewis & Short).
