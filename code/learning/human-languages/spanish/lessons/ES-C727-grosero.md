## You'll want to know: grosero
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C727-SENSIBLE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**grosero** — "rude, coarse".

> No seas grosero con la camarera.
>
> *Do not be rude to the waitress.*

Also vulgar: *una palabra grosera*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*grosero* is built on *grueso* "thick", which is inherited from Latin *grossus* "thick, coarse" (EtymDB); rough cloth became rough manners.
