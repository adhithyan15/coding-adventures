## You'll want to know: आशीर्वाद
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C317-AHER317-02]; assesses=[] -->

**आशीर्वाद** — *āśīrvād* — "a blessing".

> लग्नानंतर त्यांनी आजीचे आशीर्वाद घेतले.
>
> *lagnānantar tyānnī ājīce āśīrvād ghetle.*
>
> After the wedding they took Grandma's blessings.

Masculine.

आशीर्वाद देणे, to bless; आशीर्वाद घेणे, to seek a blessing.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**आशीर्वाद** (*āśīrvād*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *āśīrvāda*, 'a benediction' (Monier-Williams): *āśis*, 'a wish, a blessing', and *vāda*, 'a saying'.
