German, chapter 315: Ritter und Riesen.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
der Ritter — the knight.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for the curse, then the German for the dragon.

You'll want to know: der Ritter.
der Ritter — "the knight".
Der Ritter ritt auf einem weißen Pferd. The knight rode a white horse.
Plural: die Ritter. Built on reiten, to ride.

The word, taken apart.
Ritter comes from Middle High German ritter, shaped after Middle Dutch ridder (EtymDB), built on the root of reiten "to ride": a rider.

You'll want to know: der König.
der König — "the king".
Der König hatte drei Töchter. The king had three daughters.
Plural: die Könige. Die Königin — the queen.

The word, taken apart.
König goes back to Proto-Germanic kuningaz "king", which EtymDB builds from kunją "kin, family" and the ending -ingaz "one who comes from": a man of the noble kin.
