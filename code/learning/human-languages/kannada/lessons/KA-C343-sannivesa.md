## You'll want to know: ಸನ್ನಿವೇಶ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C343-NIRUPANE343-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸನ್ನಿವೇಶ** — *sannivēśa* — "a situation; a scene".

> ಆ ಸಿನಿಮಾದಲ್ಲಿ ಒಂದು ತಮಾಷೆಯ ಸನ್ನಿವೇಶ ಇದೆ.
>
> *ā sinimādalli ondu tamāṣeya sannivēśa ide.*
>
> There is a funny scene in that film.

Plural: ಸನ್ನಿವೇಶಗಳು, *sannivēśagaḷu*.

A scene in a story or a film, or a real situation: ಕಷ್ಟದ ಸನ್ನಿವೇಶ.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸನ್ನಿವೇಶ** (*sannivēśa*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *saṃniveśa*, "entering together; a seat, a position, a situation" (Monier-Williams).
