## You'll want to know: heurter
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C411-ACCIDENT-02]; assesses=[] -->

**heurter** — "to bump into, to knock against; to offend".

> En reculant, il a heurté un poteau.
>
> *While reversing he bumped into a post.*

Also figurative: *heurter quelqu'un* — to hurt someone's feelings.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*heurter* comes from Old French *hurter* 'to ram', which is built on Frankish *hūrt* 'battering ram' (EtymDB).
