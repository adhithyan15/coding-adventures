## You'll want to know: ಕಿರುಗುಟ್ಟು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C462-KOTHADI462-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ಕಿರುಗುಟ್ಟು** — *kiruguṭṭu* — "to creak; to squeak".

> ರಾತ್ರಿ ಕೊಠಡಿಯ ಹಳೆಯ ಬಾಗಿಲು ಕಿರುಗುಟ್ಟಿತು.
>
> *rātri koṭhaḍiya haḷeya bāgilu kiruguṭṭitu.*
>
> At night the old door of the room creaked.

Past (it …): ಕಿರುಗುಟ್ಟಿತು, *kiruguṭṭitu*.

Of doors, brakes, sandals and birds: a thin, sharp sound.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಕಿರುಗುಟ್ಟು** (*kiruguṭṭu*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 1590 lists Kannada *kiṟuguṭṭu*, *kirguṭṭu* among words for crying out and shrill sounds.
