Mandarin Chinese, chapter 429: Home Again.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
lǚtú — journey.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for the plan for a trip, then the Mandarin for an official receipt.

You'll want to know: lǚtú.
lǚtú — "journey".
lǚtú hěn cháng wǒmen dōu hěn lèi — It was a long journey, and we're all tired.
Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
lǚtú is built from 旅 'to travel' and tú 'a road', both inherited from Middle Chinese, ljoX and du (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription); the dictionary glosses the first as a body of troops and the second 'a road'.

What you've built.
One more word for getting around.

Lesson 3 of 7.
dāchē — to get a lift.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for the journey as a whole, then the Mandarin for on one's way.

You'll want to know: dāchē.
dāchē — "to get a lift".
wǒmen dāchē huí jiā — We got a lift home.
Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
dāchē is built from dā 'to join on' and 车 'vehicle', both inherited from Middle Chinese, thap and tsyhae (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription); the dictionary glosses the first only as a word for touching, so the sense 'to ride along' is not given there.
