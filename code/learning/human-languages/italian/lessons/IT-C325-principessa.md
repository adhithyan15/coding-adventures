## You'll want to know: la principessa
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C325-REGNO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**la principessa** — "the princess".

> La principessa vive in un castello.
>
> *The princess lives in a castle.*

Built on *principe* with *-essa*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*principessa* is *principe* with *-essa*, from Late Latin *-issa*; the root is Latin *prīnceps* "chief" (EtymDB).
