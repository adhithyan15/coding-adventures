## You'll want to know: দূরবিন
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C523-BAIRE523-02]; assesses=[] -->

**দূরবিন** — *dūrbin* — "binoculars; a telescope".

> আমি দূরবিনে দূরের পাহাড় দেখলাম।
>
> *Āmi dūrbine dūrer pāhāṛ dekhlām.*
>
> I looked at the far-off mountains through binoculars.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**দূরবিন** (*dūrbin*) is usually traced to Persian *dūrbīn*, "far-seeing": *dūr*, "far", and *bīn*, "seeing".
