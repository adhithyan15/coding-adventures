## You'll want to know: اعزاز
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C382-INAM382-02]; assesses=[] -->

**اعزاز** — *ezāz* — "an honour; an award".

> اگر اسے یہ اعزاز ملا تو سب خوش ہوں گے۔
>
> *agar use yih ezāz milā to sab khush hoṅ ge.*
>
> If she receives this award, everyone will be happy.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**اعزاز** (*ezāz*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *iʿzāz*, "honouring", of the same family as **عزت**, "honour".
