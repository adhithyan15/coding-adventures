## You'll want to know: स्वारी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C333-MOHIM333-02]; assesses=[] -->

**स्वारी** — *svārī* — "a military campaign; a ride".

> राजाने उत्तरेकडे स्वारी केली.
>
> *rājāne uttarekaḍe svārī kelī.*
>
> The king led a campaign to the north.

Feminine.

स्वारी करणे, to march on a place.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**स्वारी** (*svārī*) is a loan from Persian *savārī*, 'riding, a mounted expedition', built on *savār*, 'a rider'.
