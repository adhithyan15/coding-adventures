# Malayalam B2 mock rubric

Score either B2 mock against [task-shapes/b2.json](../../task-shapes/b2.json).
Reading, listening, writing, and speaking are separate 100-point papers; every
paper must independently reach 60/100. A 240/400 total cannot compensate for a
failed paper, and every section must be attempted.

## Reading and listening

Each of the four reading parts and each of the four listening parts is worth 25
points. Divide the points equally among keyed items and retain one decimal
place. Play every listening text twice at 130–150 words per minute. No
transcript, romanization, dictionary, or translator is allowed.

## Writing

The 100–130 word interaction text is worth 35 points: task fulfilment 10,
audience and register 7, organisation and cohesion 6, Malayalam vocabulary and
grammar 8, and orthographic control 4. The 220–280 word extended text is worth
65 points: task fulfilment and development 20, organisation and cohesion 15,
genre and register control 10, Malayalam vocabulary and grammar 14, and
orthographic control 6. Either traditional or reformed Malayalam orthography
earns full credit when used consistently. Romanized, translated,
non-Malayalam-script, or visibly copied answers earn zero independent-writing
credit.

## Speaking

The presentation is worth 35 points, collaborative problem-solving 30, and
defended discussion 35. Within each part award 30% for task fulfilment and
development, 25% for organisation or interaction, 30% for Malayalam vocabulary
and grammar, and 15% for pronunciation and fluency. Reward comparison,
qualification, clarification, and repair. No written answer script is allowed;
ten-minute preparation notes may contain keywords, not sentences.

## Readiness boundary

The mock is executable but not self-validating. A readiness claim additionally
requires clean timed administration and qualified human review of the paper,
recordings, productive responses, and scoring decisions.
