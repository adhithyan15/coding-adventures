## Script — the sign piece, then the earth piece
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-SCRIPT-SHE-SOCIETY-01]; assesses=[] -->

> 社

**Two pieces, left before right.** Count the pieces, not the strokes.

**The left piece, the sign piece, four strokes.** A short dot at the top. A bar that runs right, turns and falls to the lower left, all one stroke. A vertical down from under it. Then a short dot to the right of the vertical.

**The right piece, the earth piece, three strokes,** as at the foot of 在: a short bar, a vertical down through it, then a long bar across the foot.

4 + 3 = seven.
