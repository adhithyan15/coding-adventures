## You'll want to know: बिखेरणो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C178-UNGH178-04]; assesses=[] -->

**बिखेरणो** — *bikherṇo* — "to scatter".

> दादी बाजरो बिखेरै है।
>
> *dādī bājro bikherai hai.*
>
> Grandmother scatters millet.

Past (I …): म्हैं बाजरो बिखेर्यो, *mhaĩ bājro bikheryo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**बिखेरणो** (*bikherṇo*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*viṣkirati*, "scatters"; CDIAL lists Old Marwari *bakheraï*, "strews" (CDIAL 11985).
