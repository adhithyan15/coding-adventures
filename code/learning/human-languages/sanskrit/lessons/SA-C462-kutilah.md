## You'll want to know: कुटिलः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C462-SATHA462-02]; assesses=[] -->

**कुटिलः** — *kuṭilaḥ* — "crooked; devious".

> कुटिलः जनः सरलं मित्रं वञ्चयति।
>
> *kuṭilaḥ janaḥ saralaṃ mitraṃ vañcayati.*
>
> A devious man deceives his straightforward friend.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**कुटिलः** (*kuṭilaḥ*) MW's first sense is "bent, crooked, curved", and then "dishonest, fraudulent"; MW gives no derivation, so its origin is uncertain (MW).
