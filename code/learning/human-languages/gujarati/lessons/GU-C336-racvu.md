## You'll want to know: રચવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C336-LEKHAK336-03]; assesses=[] -->

**રચવું** — *racvũ* — "to compose".

> કવિએ નદી પર એક ગીત રચ્યું.
>
> *kavie nadī par ek gīt racyũ.*
>
> The poet composed a song about the river.

Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે ગીત રચ્યું, *teṇe gīt racyũ*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**રચવું** (*racvũ*) is built on Sanskrit *racana*, "arranging, composing" (Monier-Williams), from the root *rac*, to fashion: to put words together into a song or a poem.
