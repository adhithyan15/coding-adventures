## You'll want to know: かりる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C187-BANK187-04]; assesses=[] -->

**かりる** — *kariru* — "to borrow; to rent".

> **あにに おかねを かります。** — *ani ni okane o karimasu* — "I borrow money from my older brother."

Polite **かります**, *karimasu*; an ichidan verb. Its partner **かす**, "to lend", is taught already.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**かりる** (*kariru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
