## You'll want to know: লঙ্ঘন করা
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C341-ONYAY341-01]; assesses=[] -->

**লঙ্ঘন করা** — *lôngghôn kôrā* — "to violate; to break (a law, an agreement)".

> কোম্পানিটা চুক্তি লঙ্ঘন করল।
>
> *kompāniṭā chukti lôngghôn kôrlô.*
>
> The company broke the contract.

Past (he/she …): তিনি নিয়মটা লঙ্ঘন করলেন, *tini niyômṭā lôngghôn kôrlen*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**লঙ্ঘন করা** (*lôngghôn kôrā*) is Sanskrit *laṅghana*, leaping over and so overstepping, a violation (Monier-Williams), with করা: to step across the line a law draws.
