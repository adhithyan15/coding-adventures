Gujarati, chapter 277: Opinions and reasons.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
મત (mat) — an opinion; a view.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say "I think that …", the A2 way. (વિચારું છું કે.) Then "your", with respect. (તમારું.)

You'll want to know: મત.
મત — mat — "an opinion; a view". It is masculine: તમારો મત, "your opinion".
તમારો મત શું છે? tamāro mat śũ che? — "What is your opinion?"
The answer often opens on મારા મતે (mārā mate), "in my opinion": મત (mat) takes the -ે of સવારે, and મારો turns to મારા before it.
મારા મતે ગુજરાતી અઘરી નથી. mārā mate gujarātī agharī nathī. — "In my opinion Gujarati is not hard."

The word, taken apart.
મત (mat) is a tatsama: Sanskrit mata, "thought, held to be", and as a noun "an opinion" (Monier-Williams), taken into Gujarati as it was. It belongs with the Sanskrit verb man-, "to think".

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: mat, then tamāro mat]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: "What is your opinion?" — tamāro mat śũ che?]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: "In my opinion he is a good doctor" — mārā mate te sāro dāktar che]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does મત (mat) mean? ("An opinion".) "In my opinion"? (મારા મતે.) Why તમારો, not તમારું? (મત (mat) is masculine.)


Lesson 2 of 5.
મને લાગે છે કે … (mane lāge che ke …) — I think that …

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say "in my opinion". (mārā mate.) Then the verb લાગવું: "to cost; to seem". Its second sense carries this lesson.

Grammar Lens: it seems to me.
મને લાગે છે કે વરસાદ પડશે. mane lāge che ke varsād paḍśe. — "I think it will rain."
Word for word: "to me it seems that it will rain". The thinker takes -ને, as with મને તાવ છે: તમને, તેને, અરુણને. કે hands over the thought, which keeps its own verb.
In the past, લાગ્યું: મને લાગ્યું કે તે નહીં આવે (mane lāgyũ ke te nahī̃ āve), "I thought he wouldn't come". "I don't think so" is મને નથી લાગતું (mane nathī lāgtũ).
હું વિચારું છું કે … is thinking something over. મને લાગે છે કે … (mane lāge che ke …) gives an opinion.

You'll want to know: asking for a view.
તમને શું લાગે છે? tamne śũ lāge che? — "What do you think?"
Two ways to ask: તમને શું લાગે છે? and તમારો મત (mat) શું છે? Two ways to answer: મને લાગે છે કે … (mane lāge che ke …) and મારા મતે …: મારા મતે ટ્રેન મોડી આવશે (mārā mate ṭren moḍī āvśe).

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: "I thought he wouldn't come" — mane lāgyũ ke te nahī̃ āve]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: "I don't think so" — mane nathī lāgtũ]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[question — say your answer, then pause 22 seconds]
Ask a friend what they think, then say that you think it will rain.

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
"I think it will rain"? (મને લાગે છે કે વરસાદ પડશે.) "What do you think?" (તમને શું લાગે છે?) Who takes -ને in it? (The thinker.)


Lesson 3 of 5.
કારણ (kāraṇ) — a reason; a cause.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: ask "why?" and answer "because …". (કેમ? … કેમકે ….) Then "I think that …". (mane lāge che ke …)

You'll want to know: કારણ.
કારણ — kāraṇ — "a reason; a cause". It is neuter: તેનું કારણ, "the reason for it".
તેનું કારણ શું છે? tenũ kāraṇ śũ che? — "What is the reason for it?"
મારી પાસે બે કારણ છે. mārī pāse be kāraṇ che. — "I have two reasons."

The word, taken apart.
કારણ (kāraṇ) is a tatsama: Sanskrit kāraṇa, "cause" (CDIAL 3057), "what makes a thing happen", from the root kṛ-, "to do", that also stands behind કરવું.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: kāraṇ, then tenũ kāraṇ]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: "What is the reason for it?" — tenũ kāraṇ śũ che?]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: "I have two reasons" — mārī pāse be kāraṇ che]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does કારણ (kāraṇ) mean? ("A reason; a cause".) Its gender? (Neuter: તેનું કારણ (kāraṇ).)


Lesson 4 of 5.
કારણ કે, -ને લીધે (kāraṇ ke, -ne līdhe) — because, because of.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say "a reason". (kāraṇ.) Then "late". (મોડું.)

Grammar Lens: કારણ કે, and a clause.
હું મોડો આવ્યો, કારણ કે ટ્રેન મોડી હતી. hũ moḍo āvyo, kāraṇ ke ṭren moḍī hatī. — "I came late, because the train was late."
કારણ કે (kāraṇ ke) works like કેમકે: what happened first, then the reason, a whole clause with its own verb. મોડો follows the man who came, મોડી the train.

You'll want to know: -ને લીધે, and a noun.
વરસાદને લીધે અમે ઘરે રહ્યા. varsādne līdhe ame ghare rahyā. — "Because of the rain, we stayed at home."
When the reason is a single thing, a noun, it takes -ને લીધે (-ne līdhe), "because of", or -ને કારણે (-ne kāraṇe), the same thing with the noun you have:
[a table of 3 rows, read aloud]
the reason is: a clause: the train was late. say: …, કારણ (kāraṇ) કે ટ્રેન મોડી હતી.
the reason is: a noun: the rain. say: વરસાદને લીધે …
the reason is: a noun: the crowd. say: ભીડને કારણે …

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: "Because of the crowd, we were late" — bhīḍne kāraṇe amne moḍũ thayũ]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: "She won't come, because she has a fever" — te nahī̃ āve, kāraṇ ke tene tāv che]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[question — say your answer, then pause 26 seconds]
Say that you came late because the train was late, then that because of the rain you stayed at home.

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
"Because the train was late"? (કારણ (kāraṇ) કે ટ્રેન મોડી હતી.) "Because of the rain"? (વરસાદને લીધે.) Which one takes a noun? (-ને લીધે.)


Lesson 5 of 5.
Review — opinions and reasons.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say "I went home and ate". (hũ ghare jaīne jamyo.) Then "first we drank tea, then we went to the market". (pahelā̃ ame cā pīdhī, pachī ame bajār gayā.)

Guided Practice.
[pause 2 seconds after each]
[your turn — say: What do you think? What is your opinion?]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: I think it will rain. In my opinion the train will come late.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: I came late, because the train was late.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: Because of the rain, we stayed at home. Because of the rain, I won't go.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: What is the reason for it? I have two reasons.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
"In my opinion"? (મારા મતે.) "I don't think so"? (મને નથી લાગતું.) Which takes a clause, કારણ (kāraṇ) કે or -ને લીધે? (કારણ (kāraṇ) કે.)
