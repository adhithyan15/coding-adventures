## You'll want to know: 雨
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C66-WEATHER66-02]; assesses=[] -->

**雨** — *yǔ* — "rain".

> **请问明天有雨吗** *qǐngwèn míngtiān yǒu yǔ ma* — Excuse me, will it rain tomorrow?

The new character, in pieces: 雨 — a lid, a frame with a line down its middle, and four drops inside. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**雨** (*yǔ*) is inherited from Middle Chinese *hjuX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which the dictionary explains, through an older book, as water coming down from the clouds.
