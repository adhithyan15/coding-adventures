## You'll want to know: まんぞく
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C435-CONTENT435-01]; assesses=[] -->

**まんぞく** — *manzoku* — "satisfied; contented".

> **この ばんごはんに まんぞくしました。** — *kono bangohan ni manzoku shimashita* — "I was satisfied with this dinner."

A na-adjective: な before a noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**まんぞく** (*manzoku*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *man*, "full" (Middle Chinese *manX*) and *zoku*, "be sufficient" (*tsjowk*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
