Arabic, chapter 162: To print, To copy, To move, To register, To employ.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
طبع (ṭabaʿa) — to print.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to rest, then the Arabic for to feel.

You'll want to know: طبع.
طبع — ṭabaʿa — "to print".

The word, taken apart.
طبع (ṭabaʿa) is built on the native Arabic root ṭ-b-ʿ, "to seal, to stamp" (Lane). Ṭabaʿa is to stamp, hence to print.

Lesson 4 of 5.
سجل (sajjala) — to register, to record.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to copy, then the Arabic for to move.

You'll want to know: سجل.
سجل — sajjala — "to register, to record".

The word, taken apart.
سجل (sajjala) is a Form II verb made from sijill, "a written record, a deed", which Lane lists under s-j-l. Sijill is usually traced to Greek sigillion, from Latin sigillum, "seal"; Lane gives no foreign source.

وظف (waẓẓafa) is built on the native Arabic root w-ẓ-f, "a daily allowance" (Lane). Waẓẓafa is Form II, "to assign a fixed portion", hence "to employ".
