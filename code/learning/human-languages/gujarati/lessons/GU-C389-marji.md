## You'll want to know: મરજી
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C389-NIRNAY389-02]; assesses=[] -->

**મરજી** — *marjī* — "one's own will".

> જો તમારી મરજી હશે તો આપણે કાલે જઈશું.
>
> *jo tamārī marjī haśe to āpaṇe kāle jaīśũ.*
>
> If you are willing, we will go tomorrow.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**મરજી** (*marjī*) is usually traced to Arabic *marḍī*, "what pleases", through Persian *marzī*.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
