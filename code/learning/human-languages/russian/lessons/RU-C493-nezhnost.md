## You'll want to know: нежность
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C493-SWEETHEARTS-04]; assesses=[] -->

**нежность** (*nézhnost*) — "tenderness".

> Бабушка смотрела на внука с нежностью.
>
> *Bábushka smotréla na vnúka s nézhnostyu.*
>
> Grandma gazed at her grandson with tenderness.

Feminine. Built on *нежный* — tender, gentle.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*нежность* is built on *нежный* 'tender' with *-ость*; EtymDB has no entry, and *нежный* is usually traced to Proto-Slavic *něžьnъ*.
