# ರಣರಂಗ (raṇaraṅga) — a battlefield

## You'll want to know: ರಣರಂಗ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C344-AYUDHA344-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ರಣರಂಗ** — *raṇaraṅga* — "a battlefield".

> ಯುದ್ಧದ ನಂತರ ರಣರಂಗ ಮೌನವಾಗಿತ್ತು.
>
> *yuddhada nantara raṇaraṅga maunavāgittu.*
>
> After the war the battlefield was silent.

ರಣ, battle, and ರಂಗ, a stage: the stage of battle.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ರಣರಂಗ** (*raṇaraṅga*) is borrowed from Sanskrit, a compound of *raṇa*, "battle, war", and *raṅga*, "a stage, an arena" (Monier-Williams).
