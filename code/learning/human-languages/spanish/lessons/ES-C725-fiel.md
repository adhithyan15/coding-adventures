## You'll want to know: fiel
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C725-FIEL-01]; assesses=[] -->

**fiel** — "faithful, loyal".

> Mi perro es muy fiel.
>
> *My dog is very faithful.*

Plural *fieles*; *los fieles* are the faithful in a church.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*fiel* is inherited from Latin *fidēlis* "faithful" (EtymDB), built on *fidēs* "faith, trust".
