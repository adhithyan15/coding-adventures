## Writing lessons print their first stroke-order filmstrips

Nine of the 85 writing lessons now open their Writing (or Script) block with
a filmstrip: র, ঁ, ব, খ, এ, ও, থ, ঞ and ঃ (BN-W01-ra, BN-W02-chandrabindu,
BN-W03-ba, BN-W03-kha, BN-W04-e-indep, BN-W04-o-indep, BN-W05-tha,
BN-W06-nya, BN-W41-bisarga). The order each strip shows is the majority of
native Bengali writers' pen traces in HP Labs India's LipiTk Bangla
recognizer, with the counts in each citation, fitted to the printed Noto
Sans Bengali letter.

The track's script now has an inventory, `data/scripts/bengali.json`: its
30 letters, 11 signs and 10 digits, each `sound` taken from the letter's own
lesson. ন and the other letters whose traces split on where the headline
goes (or on how many strokes to use) stay undrawn; the inventory notes say
why. Book only: narration, modality and the lessons themselves do not
change.
