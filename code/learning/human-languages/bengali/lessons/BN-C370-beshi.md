# বেশি (beshi) — more; too much

## You'll want to know: বেশি
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C370-KHUB370-03]; assesses=[] -->

**বেশি** — *beshi* — "more; too much".

> বেশি খেলে পেট ব্যথা করবে।
>
> *beshi khele peṭ bæthā kôrbe.*
>
> If you eat too much, you'll get a stomach ache.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**বেশি** (*beshi*) is usually traced to Persian *bēsh*, more. It means 'more' after -এর চেয়ে and 'too much' on its own.
