Russian, chapter 445: Lamps and benches.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
фонарь (fonár) — a street lamp; a torch, a lantern.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a fortress, then the Russian for a city hall.

You'll want to know: фонарь.
фонарь (fonár) — "a street lamp; a torch, a lantern".
Вечером на улице зажигаются фонари. Vécherom na úlitse zazhigáyutsya fonarí. In the evening the street lamps come on.
Masculine; the stress moves: фонаря (fonaryá), фонари (fonarí). Карманный фонарь (fonár) — a torch.

The word, taken apart.
фонарь (fonár) goes back to Byzantine Greek phanárion 'a lamp, a torch' (EtymDB).

Lesson 2 of 5.
скамейка (skaméyka) — a bench.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a city hall, then the Russian for a street lamp.

You'll want to know: скамейка.
скамейка (skaméyka) — "a bench".
Бабушки сидят на скамейке у подъезда. Bábushki sidyát na skaméyke u podyézda. The grandmothers are sitting on the bench by the entrance.
Feminine; a diminutive of скамья.

The word, taken apart.
скамейка (skaméyka) is a diminutive of скамья 'a bench', which EtymDB takes from Byzantine Greek skamnía. EtymDB lists скамейка (skaméyka) itself without a source.

урна (úrna) — "a litter bin; an urn".
Он бросил старую газету в урну. On brósil stáruyu gazétu v úrnu. He threw the old newspaper into the bin.
Feminine. Урна для голосования — a ballot box.

The word, taken apart.
урна (úrna) was borrowed from Latin urna 'a vessel, a jar' (EtymDB).

Lesson 4 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: киоск.
киоск (kiósk) — "a kiosk, a news stand".
В киоске продают газеты и билеты. V kióske prodayút gazéty i biléty. The kiosk sells newspapers and tickets.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
киоск (kiósk) came from French kiosque (EtymDB), which goes back through Turkish köşk 'a pavilion' to Persian.

Lesson 5 of 5.
клумба (klúmba) — a flower bed.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a litter bin, then the Russian for a kiosk.

You'll want to know: клумба.
клумба (klúmba) — "a flower bed".
Перед школой большая клумба с розами. Péred shkóloy bolsháya klúmba s rózami. There is a big flower bed with roses in front of the school.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
клумба (klúmba) is usually traced to English clump 'a cluster of plants'. EtymDB lists the word beside a Polish cousin, klomb, but gives no source.
