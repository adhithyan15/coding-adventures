## You'll want to know: കൊട്ടുക
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C561-VADYAM561-03]; assesses=[] -->

**കൊട്ടുക** — *koṭṭuka* — "to beat a drum".

> ഉത്സവത്തിന് അവർ ചെണ്ട കൊട്ടി.
>
> *utsavattinŭ avar ceṇṭa koṭṭi.*
>
> They played the chenda drum at the festival.

Past: കൊട്ടി, *koṭṭi*.

Also to knock or tap; കൈ കൊട്ടുക is to clap.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**കൊട്ടുക** (*koṭṭuka*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 2063 gives it as to beat so as to make a sound, as on a drum or a bell (DEDR 2063).
