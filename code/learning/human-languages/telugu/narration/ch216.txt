7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.

(నిరాశ, nirāśa.) Boredom? (విసుగు, visugu.) Pining? (బెంగ, beṅga.) Numbness? (తిమ్మిరి, timmiri.) Hiccups? (ఎక్కిళ్ళు, ekkiḷḷu.) Snoring? (గురక, guraka.) Sunstroke? (వడదెబ్బ, vaḍadebba.) Steam?
