## You'll want to know: reivindicar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C358-GREVE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**reivindicar** — "to lay claim to, to demand (as a right)".

> Os trabalhadores reivindicam salários mais altos.
>
> *The workers are demanding higher wages.*

Formal, of rights and pay; in speech *pedir*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*reivindicar* is formed from Latin *rēs, reī* "thing" and *vindicāre* "to lay claim to, to avenge" (EtymDB, Lewis & Short): to claim a thing as yours.
