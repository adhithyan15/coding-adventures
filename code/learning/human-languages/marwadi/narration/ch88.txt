Lesson 1 of 5.
रंग (raṅg) — a colour.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for a hundred thousand, then the Marwadi for the world.

You'll want to know: रंग.
रंग — raṅg — "a colour".

The word, taken apart.
रंग (raṅg) has two possible sources that may have merged: Sanskrit raṅga, "dye, colour", under which CDIAL lists Hindi rā̃g, "dye" (CDIAL 10560), and Persian rang, "colour".

Lesson 3 of 5.
जातरा (jātrā) — a journey.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for a colour, then the Marwadi for a song.

You'll want to know: जातरा.
जातरा — jātrā — "a journey".

The word, taken apart.
जातरा (jātrā) is a semi-tatsama: Sanskrit yātrā, "journey", borrowed after the old sound changes had run their course and then lightly reshaped (CDIAL 10456). It is built on the root yā, "to go"; Marwadi says the y as j and splits the tr with a vowel.

Lesson 4 of 5.
छुट्टी (chhuṭṭī) — a holiday.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for a song, then the Marwadi for a journey.

You'll want to know: छुट्टी.
छुट्टी — chhuṭṭī — "a holiday".

The word, taken apart.
छुट्टी (chhuṭṭī) is a tadbhava, a word inherited and reshaped by sound change. It comes from kṣuṭyate, "is released", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants. CDIAL lists Marwari chuṭṇo under it (CDIAL 3707). The Marwari row is the verb "to get loose"; CDIAL marks Hindi and Gujarati chuṭṭī, "leave", as taken from Punjabi.
