Italian, chapter 370: La maggioranza.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
la maggioranza — the majority.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for to elect, then the Italian for the voter.

You'll want to know: la maggioranza.
la maggioranza — "the majority".
La maggioranza degli studenti è d'accordo. The majority of the students agree.
La maggioranza of parliament is the governing side.

The word, taken apart.
maggioranza is built on maggiore "greater", inherited from Latin māior "greater" (Lewis & Short), the comparative of magnus "great".

la democrazia — "the democracy".
In una democrazia tutti possono votare. In a democracy everyone can vote.

The word, taken apart.
democrazia is a learned borrowing from Medieval Latin dēmocratia, from Greek dēmokratía "rule by the people" (EtymDB), built from dêmos "the people" and krátos "strength, power".

il deputato — "the member of parliament, the deputy".
Il deputato ha votato contro la riforma. The member of parliament voted against the reform.

The word, taken apart.
deputato is a learned borrowing from Late Latin dēputātus "assigned, sent", from dēputāre "to allot, to assign" (Lewis & Short), built on putāre "to reckon".
