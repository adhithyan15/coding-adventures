## You'll want to know: rebrousser
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C436-RACCOURCI-05]; assesses=[] -->

**rebrousser** — "to turn back (rebrousser chemin)".

> La route était fermée, alors nous avons rebroussé chemin.
>
> *The road was closed, so we turned back.*

Almost always in *rebrousser chemin* — to retrace one's steps.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*rebrousser* is usually traced to *rebours* 'against the grain', from Late Latin *reburrus* 'with hair standing up'; the details are not settled, and EtymDB does not list it.
