## You'll want to know: sauvegarder
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C397-FICHIER-05]; assesses=[] -->

**sauvegarder** — "to save (a file); to safeguard".

> Il a oublié de sauvegarder son travail.
>
> *He forgot to save his work.*

*Une sauvegarde* — a backup.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*sauvegarder* is built from *sauve* "safe", from Latin *salvus* "safe" (Lewis & Short), and *garder* "to keep", usually traced to a Frankish word.
