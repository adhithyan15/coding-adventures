## You'll want to know: broder
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C496-COUTURE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**broder** — "to embroider".

> Elle a brodé des fleurs sur un coussin.
>
> *She embroidered flowers on a cushion.*

*La broderie* — embroidery. Informally, to embellish a story.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*broder* goes back to Old French *brosder*, a word of Germanic origin; its form was later drawn towards *bord* "edge".
