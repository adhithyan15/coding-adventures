## You'll want to know: सिंहासन
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C320-SAMRAT320-03]; assesses=[] -->

**सिंहासन** — *siṁhāsan* — "a throne".

> राजा सोने के सिंहासन पर बैठा।
>
> *rājā sone ke siṁhāsan par baiṭhā.*
>
> The king sat on a golden throne.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सिंहासन** (*siṁhāsan*) is Sanskrit *siṁhāsana*, *siṁha*, a lion, and *āsana*, a seat (Monier-Williams): the lion-seat of a king.
