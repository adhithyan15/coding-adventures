French, chapter 216: Around, Everywhere, Elsewhere, Up there, Suburbs.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
autour — around.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for a syrup, then the French for an ointment.

You'll want to know: autour.
autour — "around".

The word, taken apart.
autour — au and the word for "a turn", from Latin tornus, "a lathe": round about. (EtymDB 2.1).

You'll want to know: la banlieue.
la banlieue — "the suburbs".

The word, taken apart.
banlieue from Medieval Latin banleuca: ban, "a lord's rule", a Frankish word, and leuca, "a league", a Gaulish one — the land within a league of a town. (EtymDB 2.1).
