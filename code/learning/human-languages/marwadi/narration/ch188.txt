Marwadi (Marwari), chapter 188: Around the Courtyard.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
फूल (phūl) — a flower.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for a corner, then the Marwadi for a loft.

You'll want to know: फूल.
फूल — phūl — "a flower".
ये फूल लाल है। ye phūl lāl hai. This flower is red.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
फूल (phūl) is inherited from Sanskrit phulla, "blown (of a flower)"; CDIAL lists Old Marwari phūla (CDIAL 9092).

खेत मांय एक छोटी झूंपड़ी है। khet mā̃y ek chhoṭī jhū̃pṛī hai. There's a small hut in the field.

झूंपड़ी (jhū̃pṛī) comes from a reconstructed jhuppa, "a hut"; CDIAL lists Gujarati jhũpṛī and Hindi jhopṛī (CDIAL 5403). CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry.
