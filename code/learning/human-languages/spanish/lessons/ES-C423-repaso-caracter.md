# ocho palabras de carácter — eight words, three endings, two pairs

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-C423-CHAR-01, ES-LEX-C423-CHAR-06] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Name as many of the eight as you can before reading the table. Aim
for six.

## Grammar Lens: the ending decides whether the word can move
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-C423-CHAR-01, ES-LEX-C423-CHAR-02, ES-LEX-C423-CHAR-03, ES-LEX-C423-CHAR-04, ES-LEX-C423-CHAR-05, ES-LEX-C423-CHAR-06, ES-LEX-C380-COUNT-36, ES-LEX-C380-COUNT-37] -->

All eight, sorted by what their last letters allow:

| word | meaning | feminine |
|---|---|---|
| simpático | likeable | simpática |
| antipático | unpleasant | antipática |
| serio | earnest | seria |
| tímido | shy | tímida |
| trabajador | hard-working | trabajadora |
| alegre | cheerful | alegre |
| inteligente | clever | inteligente |
| sociable | outgoing | sociable |

Read the third column against the first. Where the two differ, the word moves
for a woman; where they are identical, it does not.

Five move and three do not, and you can predict which from the spelling alone:
a word ending in **-o** changes, a word ending in **-e** does not, and **-dor**
is the consonant ending that changes anyway.

## Grammar Lens: two of them are matched opposites
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-C423-CHAR-01, ES-LEX-C423-CHAR-05, ES-LEX-C423-CHAR-06, ES-LEX-C380-COUNT-36, ES-LEX-C380-COUNT-37] -->

Four of the eight come in pairs, which halves what there is to remember:

| one end | other end | what the pair measures |
|---|---|---|
| simpático | antipático | how you are to be around |
| tímido | sociable | how you meet strangers |
| alegre | serio | how you carry yourself |

Three scales, six words. *Inteligente* and *trabajador* stand alone.
