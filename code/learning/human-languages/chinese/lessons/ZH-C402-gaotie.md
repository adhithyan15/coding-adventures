# gāotiě — high-speed railway

## You'll want to know: gāotiě
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C402-STATION402-03]; assesses=[] -->

*gāotiě* — "high-speed railway".

> *wǒmen zuò gāotiě qù Běijīng* — We're taking the high-speed train to Beijing.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*gāotiě* is built from 高 'high' and *tiě* 'iron', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *kaw* and *thet* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second, after an older dictionary, 'black metal'. It is a short form of a longer name meaning a high-speed iron road.
