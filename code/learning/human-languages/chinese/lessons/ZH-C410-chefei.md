## You'll want to know: chēfèi
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C410-COACH410-02]; assesses=[] -->

*chēfèi* — "fare for a ride".

> *zuò chūzūchē qù jīchǎng chēfèi shì duōshao qián* — How much is the taxi fare to the airport?

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*chēfèi* is built from 车 'vehicle' and *fèi* 'a cost', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *tsyhae* and *phj+jH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'to use up'.
