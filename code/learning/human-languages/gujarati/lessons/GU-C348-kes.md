# કેસ (kes) — a court case

## You'll want to know: કેસ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C348-KES348-02]; assesses=[] -->

**કેસ** — *kes* — "a court case".

> આ કેસ ત્રણ વરસથી ચાલે છે.
>
> *ā kes traṇ varasthī cāle che.*
>
> This case has been going on for three years.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**કેસ** (*kes*) is borrowed from English *case*, in its legal sense.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
