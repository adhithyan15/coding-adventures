## You'll want to know: il mutuo
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C394-BILANCIO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**il mutuo** — "the mortgage, the (bank) loan".

> Se la banca ci dà il mutuo, compriamo la casa.
>
> *If the bank gives us the mortgage, we will buy the house.*

*Pagare il mutuo* — to pay the mortgage. As an adjective it means mutual.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*mutuo* is a learned borrowing from Latin *mūtuum* "a loan" (Lewis & Short), from *mūtuus* "borrowed, lent; in return, mutual".
