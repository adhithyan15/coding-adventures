## You'll want to know: el vecindario
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C684-CIUDADANO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**el vecindario** — "the neighbourhood; the residents".

> El vecindario es muy tranquilo.
>
> *The neighbourhood is very quiet.*

Common in Latin America; Spain often says *el barrio*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*vecindario* is built on *vecino* "neighbour", inherited from Latin *vīcīnus* (EtymDB), from *vīcus* "a village, a street".
