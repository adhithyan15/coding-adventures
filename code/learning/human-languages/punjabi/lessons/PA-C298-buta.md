## You'll want to know: ਬੂਟਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C298-THORN298-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ਬੂਟਾ** — *būṭā* — "a bush; a plant".

> ਘਰ ਦੇ ਸਾਹਮਣੇ ਇੱਕ ਹਰਾ ਬੂਟਾ ਹੈ।
>
> *ghar de sāhmaṇe ikk harā būṭā hai.*
>
> There is a green bush in front of the house.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਬੂਟਾ** (*būṭā*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*būṭa*, "bush, plant"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *būṭā*, "bush, flower", under it (CDIAL 9297).
