# Chapter 16 checkpoint — the months, Ugadi, ಉ and ೮

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Nothing new here. The months and the new year out loud first, then
one word, one letter and one digit on the page.

## Guided Practice — the months
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[KA-LEX-C16-TINGALUGALU-01, KA-PRAGMATICS-C16-TINGALUGALU-02] -->

[PAUSE 1s]
- [YOU SAY: the twelve months, from the first]

(*Caitra, Vaiśākha, Jyēṣṭha, Āṣāḍha, Śrāvaṇa, Bhādrapada, Āśvayuja, Kārtīka,
Mārgaśira, Puṣya, Māgha, Phālguṇa*.)

- Is this calendar Kannada's own, the way Tamil's and Malayalam's are theirs?
  (No: it is the pan-Indian lunisolar calendar Hindi uses too.)
- When does Ugadi fall? (On *Chaitra Śukla Pratipada*, the first day of the
  bright fortnight of Chaitra.)

## Guided Practice — one more word from this chapter
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[KA-LEX-ANCHOR-UTTARA] -->

- What is "an answer", and also "north"? (*Uttara*.)

## Script — ಉ and ೮
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[KA-SCRIPT-RECOG-126, KA-SCRIPT-RECOG-147, KA-LEX-ANCHOR-UTTARA] -->

- **ಉತ್ತರ** is *uttara*. Which character opens it, and why does it stand on
  its own there? (**ಉ**, *u*: the word starts on a vowel, so the vowel takes
  its standing shape.)
- Where would the same *u* take its riding shape, **ು**? (After a consonant,
  as at the end of **ಹೌದು**.)
- Which number is **೮**, and which word does it stand for? (**8**, *eṇṭu*.)

[YOU READ: **ಉತ್ತರ** aloud, and point to the ಉ in it]

[YOU READ: **೮** aloud as *eṇṭu*]

## Writing — from sound

<!-- hl-writing-stage: dictation-transcription -->

[YOU CHECK: against the key — **ಉ**, **೮**]

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[KA-PRAGMATICS-C16-TINGALUGALU-02] -->

[PAUSE 3s] Which month opens the year, and which festival opens it? (*Caitra*;
Ugadi.) That is the chapter: a calendar shared with Hindi, a word that means
both answer and north, and the vowel and the digit that sit inside it.
