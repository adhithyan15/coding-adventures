## You'll want to know: die Strophe
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C514-MELODIE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**die Strophe** — "verse, stanza".

> Wir singen nur die erste Strophe.
>
> *We only sing the first verse.*

Plural: *die Strophen*. The chorus is *der Refrain*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Strophe* comes from Latin *stropha*, from Greek *strophḗ* "a turning" (EtymDB).
