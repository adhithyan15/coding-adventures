## You'll want to know: ಮೂಲ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C385-PRABHAVA385-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ಮೂಲ** — *mūla* — "a source; a root".

> ಈ ನದಿಯ ಮೂಲ ಬೆಟ್ಟದಲ್ಲಿದೆ.
>
> *ī nadiya mūla beṭṭadallide.*
>
> This river's source is in the hills.

ಮೂಲತಃ, originally.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಮೂಲ** (*mūla*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *mūla*, "a root, a base, an origin" (CDIAL 10250).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
