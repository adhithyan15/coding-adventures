## You'll want to know: interveniō, intervenīre
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C298-EVENIO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**interveniō, intervenīre** — "to come along, to intervene".

> Pater intervēnit et fābulam interrūpit.
>
> *Father came along and broke off the story.*

*interveniō, intervenīre, intervēnī, interventum*, often with the dative: *sermōnī intervēnit*, he broke in on the talk.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**interveniō** is built on *veniō*, "to come", with *inter*, "between" (Lewis & Short): to come in during something. English *intervene* comes from it.
