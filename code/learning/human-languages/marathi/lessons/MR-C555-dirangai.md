## You'll want to know: दिरंगाई
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C555-GHAI555-02]; assesses=[] -->

**दिरंगाई** — *diraṅgāī* — "a needless delay; dilatoriness".

> कामात दिरंगाई केली तर दंड भरावा लागतो.
>
> *kāmāt diraṅgāī kelī tar daṇḍ bharāvā lāgto.*
>
> If you put off the work, you have to pay a fine.

Feminine.

दिरंगाई करणे, to drag one's feet; दिरंगाई होणे, a delay happens.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**दिरंगाई** (*diraṅgāī*) is a loan, usually traced to Persian *dirang*, "delay, tarrying", with the Marathi noun ending -आई; the path into Marathi is not recorded in our sources.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
