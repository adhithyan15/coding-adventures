## You'll want to know: il soffritto
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C334-SOFFRITTO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**il soffritto** — "the soffritto, the gently fried base of onion and vegetables".

> Il soffritto è la base di molte ricette.
>
> *Soffritto is the base of many recipes.*

Onion, carrot and celery, chopped and fried slowly.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*soffritto* is the past participle of *soffriggere* "to fry gently", from Latin *frīgere* "to fry" (EtymDB).
