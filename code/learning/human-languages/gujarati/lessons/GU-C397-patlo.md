# પાટલો (pāṭlo) — a low wooden seat; a bench

## You'll want to know: પાટલો
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C397-DOOR397-03]; assesses=[] -->

**પાટલો** — *pāṭlo* — "a low wooden seat; a bench".

> દાદી પાટલા પર બેસીને જમે છે.
>
> *dādī pāṭlā par besīne jame che.*
>
> Grandmother sits on a low wooden seat to eat.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**પાટલો** (*pāṭlo*) is a tadbhava of Sanskrit *paṭṭa*, "a slab, a board"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati word, "a bench" (CDIAL 7699). પાટિયું, a plank, comes from the same word.
