Gujarati, chapter 305: When the Earth Shook.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
હલવું (halvũ) — to shake; to move.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a calamity, then the Gujarati for a crisis.

You'll want to know: હલવું.
હલવું — halvũ — "to shake; to move".
રાત્રે અચાનક ધરતી હલી. rātre acānak dhartī halī. Suddenly in the night the ground shook.
Past (it …): તે મકાન હલ્યું, te makān halyũ.

The word, taken apart.
હલવું (halvũ) comes from hallati, "moves, shakes", a form CDIAL reconstructs, where it lists Gujarati hālvũ, "to move, shake" (CDIAL 14018). હલાવવું, to stir, is its causative: you shake a thing, but the ground હલે છે by itself.

Lesson 2 of 5.
ઢગલો (ḍhaglo) — a heap.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a crisis, then the Gujarati for to shake.

You'll want to know: ઢગલો.
ઢગલો — ḍhaglo — "a heap".
ભૂકંપ પછી રસ્તા પર પથ્થરનો ઢગલો હતો. bhūkamp pachī rastā par paththarno ḍhaglo hato. After the earthquake there was a heap of stones on the road.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ઢગલો (ḍhaglo) comes from ḍhikka, a form CDIAL reconstructs, where it lists Gujarati ḍhaglo, "a heap" (CDIAL 5585). It is masculine: પથ્થરનો ઢગલો, a heap of stones.

Lesson 3 of 5.
ધસવું (dhasvũ) — to give way; to cave in.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to shake, then the Gujarati for a heap.

You'll want to know: ધસવું.
ધસવું — dhasvũ — "to give way; to cave in".
ભૂકંપમાં જૂનું મકાન ધસી પડ્યું. bhūkampmā̃ jūnũ makān dhasī paḍyũ. The old building caved in during the earthquake.
Past (it …): તે મકાન ધસી પડ્યું, te makān dhasī paḍyũ.

The word, taken apart.
ધસવું (dhasvũ) is inherited from Sanskrit dhváṁsati, "falls to pieces", and CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb, "to push through, be undermined" (CDIAL 6896). With પડવું it is a building giving way: ધસી પડ્યું.

You'll want to know: દટાવું.
દટાવું — daṭāvũ — "to be buried (under something)".
ભૂકંપમાં ઘણા લોકો મકાનો નીચે દટાઈ ગયા. bhūkampmā̃ ghaṇā loko makāno nīce daṭāī gayā. In the earthquake many people were buried under buildings.
Past (he …): તે માણસ દટાઈ ગયો, te māṇas daṭāī gayo.

The word, taken apart.
દટાવું (daṭāvũ) is the intransitive partner of dāṭvũ, to bury, which CDIAL lists under draṭ, "press", a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 6618): to be pressed down out of sight. People and things દટાઈ જાય છે under earth or fallen walls.

કાટમાળ — kāṭmāḷ — "rubble".
લોકોએ કાટમાળ નીચેથી એક બાળકને કાઢ્યું. lokoe kāṭmāḷ nīcethī ek bāḷakne kāḍhyũ. People pulled a child out from under the rubble.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
કાટમાળ (kāṭmāḷ) No dictionary we use records an older form, so its history is not settled. It is what is left of a building that has fallen: broken walls, stones and wood.
