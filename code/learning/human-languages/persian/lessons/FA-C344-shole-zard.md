## You'll want to know: شله زرد
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C344-NAZR-04]; assesses=[] -->

**شله زرد** (*shole zard*) — "saffron rice pudding".

> مادرم برای نذری شله زرد پخت.
>
> *Mâdaram barâ-ye nazri shole zard pokht.*
>
> My mother cooked saffron rice pudding as votive food.

Rice, sugar, saffron and rosewater, decorated with cinnamon. *shole* "a soft rice dish" + *zard* "yellow".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*shole zard* joins *shole* "a soft rice dish" and *zard* "yellow, golden", a native Persian word that EtymDB lists without a parent.
