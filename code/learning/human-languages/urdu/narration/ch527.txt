Urdu, chapter 527: Busy or Free.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
مصروف (masrūf) — busy.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a break in work, then the Urdu for complete.

You'll want to know: مصروف.
مصروف — masrūf — "busy".
اگر آپ مصروف ہیں تو میں کل آؤں گا۔ agar āp masrūf haiṅ to maiṅ kal āūṅ gā. If you're busy, I'll come tomorrow.

The word, taken apart.
مصروف (masrūf) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic maṣrūf, "turned towards something, taken up with it".

What you've built.
One more word for saying what happens if.

Lesson 5 of 5.
اوبنا (ūbnā) — to get bored; to be fed up.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for available, then the Urdu for an excuse.

You'll want to know: اوبنا.
اوبنا — ūbnā — "to get bored; to be fed up".
اگر بارش نہیں رکی تو بچے اوب جائیں گے۔ agar bārish nahīṅ rukī to bachche ūb jāeṅ ge. If the rain doesn't stop, the children will get bored.
Past (he …): وہ انتظار سے اوب گیا, voh intizār se ūb gayā.

The word, taken apart.
اوبنا (ūbnā) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin ūbnā, "to be agitated, to be bewildered", under Sanskrit udvijatē, "shudders, recoils" (CDIAL 2087); the everyday sense "to be bored, to be fed up" is a later one that CDIAL does not record.
