Gujarati, chapter 81: Eleven to Sixteen.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
અગિયાર (agiyār) — eleven.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a festival, then the Gujarati for Holi.

You'll want to know: અગિયાર.
અગિયાર (agiyār) — "eleven".

What you've built.
One more word for eleven to sixteen.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: agiyār]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: agiyār, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say agiyār]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Gujarati for a festival, then the Gujarati for Holi, then say agiyār again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does અગિયાર (agiyār) mean? ("Eleven".) Say it once more.
