# حك (ḥakka) — to scratch

## You'll want to know: حك
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C508-JILD508-03]; assesses=[] -->

**حك** — *ḥakka* — "to scratch".

> حك الولد رأسه وفكر.
>
> *ḥakka al-walad raʾsahu wa-fakkara.*
>
> The boy scratched his head and thought.

Present: يحك, *yaḥukk*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**حك** (*ḥakka*) is built on the native Arabic root *ḥ-k-k*, "to rub, to scratch" (Lane). Its last two root letters are the same, so they write as one doubled letter.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
