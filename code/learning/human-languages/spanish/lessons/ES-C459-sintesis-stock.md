# (cerrado por inventario) — read it, then do it yourself

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-C459-STOCK-02, ES-LEX-C459-STOCK-03] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say **el inventario** and **habitual**. The door below uses one to
excuse breaking the other.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-C459-STOCK-01, ES-LEX-C459-STOCK-02, ES-LEX-C459-STOCK-03] -->

A sheet taped to a shop door. Read it, then answer.

> **CERRADO POR INVENTARIO**

> Solo esta mañana. Abrimos a las 16:00.

> El **horario habitual** se recupera mañana.

> La **sección** de deportes permanece abierta por la puerta lateral.

[PAUSE 3s]
- [YOU SAY: how long the shop is shut, and when it opens]
- [YOU SAY: what is open anyway, and how to get in]
- [YOU SAY: when things go back to normal]

## Why it's said this way
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-C459-STOCK-03, ES-LEX-C459-STOCK-01, ES-LEX-C459-STOCK-02] -->

**El horario habitual se recupera mañana** is the line worth taking apart.
*Habitual* rather than *normal*, because the shop is saying *this is the
pattern we keep*, not *this is not strange*. And *se recupera* rather than
*recuperamos*: the timetable comes back by itself, nobody is thanked for it.

**Permanece abierta** agrees with *la sección*, feminine — a small check that
catches people out, because *deportes* is masculine and plural and sits right
next to the verb. Spanish agrees with the head noun, not with the nearest one.

And the heading is the frame you now know: *cerrado por* plus a bare noun. A
reader takes it in from the pavement without reading the rest.

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-C459-STOCK-01, ES-LEX-C459-STOCK-02, ES-LEX-C459-STOCK-03] -->

[PAUSE 3s] Now your turn, out loud and then in writing. Four lines for a shop
door: say you are closed for stocktaking, say when you reopen, say the usual
hours come back tomorrow, and say which section stays open.

Then check yourself: did you use the *cerrado por* frame with no article, and
did the adjective agree with *la sección* rather than with *deportes*?
