## You'll want to know: конфетти
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C314-SALYUT-03]; assesses=[] -->

**конфетти** (*konfettí*) — "confetti".

> Конфетти было везде.
>
> *Konfettí býlo vezdé.*
>
> There was confetti everywhere.

Neuter and indeclinable.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*конфетти* was borrowed from Italian *confetti* (EtymDB), the plural of *confetto* "a sugared sweet", once thrown at carnivals; it shares its source, Latin *conficere* "to prepare", with *конфета*.
