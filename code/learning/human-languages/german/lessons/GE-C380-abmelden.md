## You'll want to know: abmelden
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C380-ZULASSUNG-05]; assesses=[] -->

**abmelden** — "to withdraw, to deregister (often sich abmelden); to log off".

> Ich habe mich vom Tanzkurs abgemeldet, weil ich keine Zeit habe.
>
> *I withdrew from the dance class because I have no time.*

Separable, often reflexive: *ich melde mich ab*, *ich habe mich abgemeldet*. Also to log off a computer.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*abmelden* is built from *ab* "off" and *melden* "to report", inherited from Old High German *meldōn* and Proto-Germanic *melþōną* (EtymDB): you report yourself off a list.
