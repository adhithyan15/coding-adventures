## You'll want to know: দম
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C476-DOM476-01]; assesses=[] -->

**দম** — *dôm* — "breath; stamina".

> সাঁতার কাটলে দম বাড়ে।
>
> *sā̃tār kāṭle dôm bāṛe.*
>
> Swimming builds up your stamina.

দম নেওয়া, to take a breather.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**দম** (*dôm*) is usually traced to Persian *dam*, "breath".
