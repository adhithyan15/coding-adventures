## You'll want to know: schluchzen
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C471-TROST-02]; assesses=[] -->

**schluchzen** — "to sob".

> Das Mädchen schluchzte leise in seinem Zimmer.
>
> *The girl was sobbing quietly in her room.*

Stronger than *weinen*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*schluchzen* is usually explained as an imitative word beside *schlucken* "to swallow"; EtymDB lists it without a source, so the details are not settled.
