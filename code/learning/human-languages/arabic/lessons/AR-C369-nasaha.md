## You'll want to know: نصح
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C369-NASIHA369-01]; assesses=[] -->

**نصح** — *naṣaḥa* — "to advise".

> نصحني الطبيب بالراحة.
>
> *naṣaḥanī aṭ-ṭabīb bi-r-rāḥa.*
>
> The doctor advised me to rest.

Present: ينصح, *yanṣaḥ*.

The advice comes after بـ or أن: نصحه بـ …, نصحه أن ….

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**نصح** (*naṣaḥa*) is built on the native Arabic root *n-ṣ-ḥ*, "to be sincere" (Lane). *Naṣaḥa* is a Form I verb: to give sincere counsel, to advise.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
