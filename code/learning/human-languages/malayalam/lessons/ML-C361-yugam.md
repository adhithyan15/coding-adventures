## You'll want to know: യുഗം
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C361-YUGAM361-01]; assesses=[] -->

**യുഗം** — *yugaṁ* — "an era; an age".

> ഇത് ഇന്റർനെറ്റിന്റെ യുഗമാണ്.
>
> *itŭ inṟarneṟṟinṟe yugamāṇŭ.*
>
> This is the age of the internet.

In old tales a യുഗം is one of the great ages of the world; today it is any era.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**യുഗം** (*yugaṁ*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *yuga*, first "a yoke, a pair" (CDIAL 10482), and in later Sanskrit also an age of the world (Monier-Williams).
