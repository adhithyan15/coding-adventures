## You'll want to know: jadis
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C272-JADIS-01]; assesses=[] -->

**jadis** — "long ago, in olden days".

> Jadis, cette rue était un marché.
>
> *Long ago, this street was a market.*

Literary and a little old-fashioned; fairy tales like it.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*jadis* comes from Old French *ja a dis* "it is days already" (EtymDB), with *ja* from Latin *iam* "already" and *dis* from Latin *diēs* "day".
