Bengali, chapter 453: Courage and Nerve.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
আত্মবিশ্বাস (ātmôbishvās) — self-confidence.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for to show off, then the Bengali for to be humiliated.

You'll want to know: আত্মবিশ্বাস.
আত্মবিশ্বাস — ātmôbishvās — "self-confidence".
গান শেখার পরে মীরার আত্মবিশ্বাস বাড়ল। gān shekhār pôre mīrār ātmôbishvās bāṛlô. After learning to sing, Mira's self-confidence grew.

The word, taken apart.
আত্মবিশ্বাস (ātmôbishvās) puts Sanskrit ātman, "self", in front of viśvāsa, "trust" (Monier-Williams), the বিশ্বাস of বিশ্বাস করা (taught), to believe: trust in yourself.

Warm-up.

Before the new one: say the Bengali for self-confidence, then the Bengali for morale.

You'll want to know: কাপুরুষ.
কাপুরুষ — kāpurush — "a coward".
বিপদ দেখে পালানো কাপুরুষের কাজ। bipôd dekhe pālāno kāpurusher kāj. Running away at the sight of danger is a coward's act.
ভীতু (taught) is timid; কাপুরুষ (kāpurush) names the man himself, and it stings.

The word, taken apart.
কাপুরুষ (kāpurush) is a tatsama, Sanskrit kāpuruṣa, "a contemptible man, a coward" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 4 of 6.
রুখে দাঁড়ানো (rukhe dā̃ṛāno) — to stand up to; to resist.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for morale, then the Bengali for a coward.

You'll want to know: রুখে দাঁড়ানো.
রুখে দাঁড়ানো — rukhe dā̃ṛāno — "to stand up to; to resist".
মীরা একা ডাকাতদের সামনে রুখে দাঁড়াল। mīrā ekā ḍākātder sāmne rukhe dā̃ṛālô. Mira stood up to the robbers on her own.
Past (he/she …): তিনি চোরের সামনে রুখে দাঁড়ালেন, tini chorer sāmne rukhe dā̃ṛālen.

The word, taken apart.
রুখে দাঁড়ানো (rukhe dā̃ṛāno) is rukhe, the -এ form of rokhā, "to hold back, to resist". CDIAL lists a Bengali rokā, to hinder (CDIAL 10827), and a rukhā, to be angry (CDIAL 10800); which of them this is, is not settled. দাঁড়ানো (taught), to stand, probably belongs with Sanskrit daṇḍa, a stick (CDIAL 6128).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a coward, then the Bengali for to stand up to.

You'll want to know: পিছিয়ে যাওয়া.
পিছিয়ে যাওয়া — pichhiye jāoyā — "to back down; to fall behind".
মীরা ভয় পেল, কিন্তু পিছিয়ে গেল না। mīrā bhôy pelô, kintu pichhiye gelô nā. Mira was afraid, but she did not back down.
Past (he/she …): তিনি শেষে পিছিয়ে গেলেন, tini sheshe pichhiye gelen.

The word, taken apart.
পিছিয়ে যাওয়া (pichhiye jāoyā) is pichhiye, the -এ form of pichhāno, "to move back", built on the word for the rear that CDIAL lists for Bengali (CDIAL 7990), the পিছ of পিছনে (taught), behind; jāoyā is "to go" (CDIAL 10452).
