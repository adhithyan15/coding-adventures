Mandarin Chinese, chapter 208: Getting Things Fixed.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
shīfu — skilled tradesman; master (and how you address one).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for to take a photo, then the Mandarin for tourist sight.

You'll want to know: shīfu.
shīfu — "skilled tradesman; master (and how you address one)".
shīfu wǒ de diànnǎo huài le — Excuse me, my computer is broken.
Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
shīfu is built from shī 'a teacher', the shī of lǎoshī, and fu 'a tutor', both inherited from Middle Chinese, srij and pjuH (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'to assist'. In the word the second loses its tone.

What you've built.

Lesson 2 of 5.
jiǎnchá — to check; to examine.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for tourist sight, then the Mandarin for skilled tradesman.

You'll want to know: jiǎnchá.
jiǎnchá — "to check; to examine".
yīshēng jiǎnchále wǒ de yǎnjing — The doctor examined my eyes.
Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
jiǎnchá is built from two words for looking into things, both inherited from Middle Chinese, kjemX and dzrae (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription). The dictionary glosses the first as the seal on a document and 'to check'; it glosses the second as a raft, so how it came to mean examining is not settled.

Lesson 3 of 5.
ānzhuāng — to install; to put in.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for skilled tradesman, then the Mandarin for to examine.

You'll want to know: ānzhuāng.
ānzhuāng — "to install; to put in".
tāmen zài ānzhuāng diànshì — They're installing the television.
Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
ānzhuāng is built from ān 'to settle' and zhuāng 'to fit out', both inherited from Middle Chinese, 'an and tsrjang (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'calm' and the second 'to pack up' and 'to dress'. Putting a machine in place is not given there, so how the pair came to it is not settled.
