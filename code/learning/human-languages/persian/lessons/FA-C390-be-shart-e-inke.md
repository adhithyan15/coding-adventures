## You'll want to know: به شرط اینکه
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C390-SHART-01]; assesses=[] -->

**به شرط اینکه** (*be shart-e inke*) — "provided that, on condition that".

> میآیم، به شرط اینکه زود برگردیم.
>
> *miâyam, be shart-e inke zud bargardim.*
>
> I will come, provided that we come back early.

It takes the subjunctive: *be shart-e inke* + *bargardim*. With a noun: *be shart-e* … .

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**به شرط اینکه** is literally "on the condition that": *shart* was borrowed from Arabic *sharṭ* "condition" (EtymDB), and *be* "to" and *inke* "that" (*in* "this" + *ke* "that") are Persian.
