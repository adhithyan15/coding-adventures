## You'll want to know: la calzada
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C647-CRUZAR-03]; assesses=[] -->

**la calzada** — "the roadway, the carriageway".

> No juegues en la calzada.
>
> *Do not play in the road.*

The part of the street for cars; people walk beside it.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*calzada* is usually traced to Vulgar Latin *(via) calciāta* "a paved road", built on Latin *calx, calcis* "limestone" (Lewis & Short); the details are not settled.
