# م — a loop, and a tail below the line

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[FA-SCRIPT-SIN-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Write س. Three teeth, then the bowl.

## Script — the shape
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-SCRIPT-MIM-01]; assesses=[] -->

> م

A small **closed loop** sitting on the line, and from it a **tail** dropping
straight down **below** the line.

The loop is small and round; keep it tight, or it starts to look like other
letters you will meet later. The tail is what makes mim unmistakable standing on
its own — very few letters drop below the line, and your eye will learn to use
that. Joined into the middle of a word the tail is trimmed away and the loop is
what survives, which is the same trimming you will meet everywhere.

One movement, no lift.

Its name is **mim** and it says **m**.
