## You'll want to know: die Wäscheklammer
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C527-WAESCHE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**die Wäscheklammer** — "clothes peg".

> Für das große Laken braucht man viele Wäscheklammern.
>
> *For the big sheet you need lots of pegs.*

Plural: *die Wäscheklammern*. *Die Klammer* — clip; bracket.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Wäscheklammer* is built on *Wäsche* "washing" and *Klammer* "clip", inherited from Middle High German *klammer* (EtymDB).
