## You'll want to know: ていきけん
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C373-KAISATSU373-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ていきけん** — *teikiken* — "a commuter pass".

> **ちちは ていきけんで でんしゃに のります。** — *chichi wa teikiken de densha ni norimasu* — "My father rides the train on a commuter pass."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ていきけん** (*teikiken*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): three Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *tei*, "fix" (Middle Chinese *dengH*), *ki*, "period" (*gi*) and *ken*, "ticket" (*khjwonH*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
