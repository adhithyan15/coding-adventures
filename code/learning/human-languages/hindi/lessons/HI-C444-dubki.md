## You'll want to know: डुबकी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C444-BANDARGAH444-05]; assesses=[] -->

**डुबकी** — *ḍubkī* — "a dip (in water)".

> ठंडे पानी में एक डुबकी के बाद मैं काँप रहा था।
>
> *ṭhaṇḍe pānī meṁ ek ḍubkī ke bād maiṁ kā̃p rahā thā.*
>
> After one dip in the cold water I was shivering.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**डुबकी** (*ḍubkī*) is built on the stem of डूबना, "to sink", from *\*ḍubb*, "sink", a form CDIAL reconstructs (CDIAL 5561); CDIAL does not list the noun itself.
