## You'll want to know: ಊರ್ಜಿತ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C425-SVIKARISU425-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ಊರ್ಜಿತ** — *ūrjita* — "valid; in force".

> ಈ ಪಾಸ್ ಇನ್ನೂ ಒಂದು ತಿಂಗಳು ಊರ್ಜಿತ.
>
> *ī pās innū ondu tiṅgaḷu ūrjita.*
>
> This pass is valid for one more month.

ಊರ್ಜಿತವಾಗು, to stay in force.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಊರ್ಜಿತ** (*ūrjita*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *ūrjita*, "endowed with strength, powerful" (Monier-Williams).
