## You'll want to know: મિજાજ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C452-KHIJ452-03]; assesses=[] -->

**મિજાજ** — *mijāj* — "temper; mood".

> આજે સાહેબનો મિજાજ ખરાબ છે.
>
> *āje sāhebno mijāj kharāb che.*
>
> The boss is in a bad mood today.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**મિજાજ** (*mijāj*) is usually traced to Arabic *mizāj*, "temperament, mixture", which reached Gujarati through Persian.
