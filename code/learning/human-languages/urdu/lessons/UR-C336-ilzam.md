## You'll want to know: الزام
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C336-SABIT336-03]; assesses=[] -->

**الزام** — *ilzām* — "an accusation; a charge".

> یہ الزام بالکل غلط ہے۔
>
> *yih ilzām bilkul ghalat hai.*
>
> This accusation is completely wrong.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**الزام** (*ilzām*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *ilzām*, "binding someone to something"; the sense "a charge laid on someone" is the one Urdu uses.
