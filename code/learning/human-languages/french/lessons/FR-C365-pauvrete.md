## You'll want to know: la pauvreté
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C365-PAUVRETE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**la pauvreté** — "the poverty".

> Beaucoup de familles vivent dans la pauvreté.
>
> *Many families live in poverty.*

From *pauvre*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*pauvreté* is inherited through Old French *poverté* from Latin *paupertās* "poverty" (EtymDB), from *pauper* "poor" (Lewis & Short).
