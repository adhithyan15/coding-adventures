## You'll want to know: il finestrino
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C428-VOLO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**il finestrino** — "the window (of a car, train or plane)".

> Preferisco il posto vicino al finestrino.
>
> *I prefer the window seat.*

At home it is *la finestra*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*finestrino* is a little *finestra* 'window', which is inherited from Latin *fenestra* (EtymDB).
