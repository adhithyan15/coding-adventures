Arabic, chapter 464: Pollution.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
تلوث (talawwuth) — pollution.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for an emission, then the Arabic for carbon.

You'll want to know: تلوث.
تلوث — talawwuth — "pollution".
التلوث في المدينة مشكلة كبيرة. at-talawwuth fī al-madīna mushkila kabīra. Pollution in the city is a big problem.

The word, taken apart.
تلوث (talawwuth) is built on the native Arabic root l-w-th, "to soil, to stain" (Lane). It is the verbal noun of Form V, "becoming soiled".

What you've built.
One more word for getting around.
