## You'll want to know: જાન
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C497-KANKOTRI497-03]; assesses=[] -->

**જાન** — *jān* — "the groom's wedding party".

> સાંજે જાન ગામમાં આવી.
>
> *sā̃je jān gāmmā̃ āvī.*
>
> In the evening the groom's wedding party arrived in the village.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**જાન** (*jān*) is inherited from Sanskrit *janya*, "belonging to a people" and "a friend of the bridegroom"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati word, "bridegroom's procession" (CDIAL 5116).
