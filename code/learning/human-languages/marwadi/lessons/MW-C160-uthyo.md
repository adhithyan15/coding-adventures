# उठ्यो, बैठ्यो — got up, sat down

## Grammar Lens: -यो on a consonant stem
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-GRAMMAR-C160-PAST-YO-01]; assesses=[MW-LEX-C52-VERBS52-03, MW-LEX-C52-VERBS52-01, MW-LEX-C52-VERBS52-04, MW-LEX-C110-ACT110-03] -->

A stem that ends in a consonant takes **-्यो** in the past: **उठ**, **उठ्यो**;
**बैठ**, **बैठ्यो**; **चाल**, **चाल्यो**; **पूग**, **पूग्यो**.

> **हूं दिनूगै उठ्यो।**
>
> *hū̃ dinūgai uṭhyo.* — I got up early in the morning.

For a woman it is **-ी**, and for more than one **-्या**:

> **मां दिनूगै उठी।**
>
> *mā̃ dinūgai uṭhī.* — Mother got up early in the morning.

> **सगळा बैठ्या।**
>
> *sagḷā baiṭhyā.* — Everyone sat down.

CDIAL lists Old Marwari *ūṭhyo*, "stood up" (CDIAL 1907).
