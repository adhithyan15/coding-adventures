## You'll want to know: رونق
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C317-EID317-05]; assesses=[] -->

**رونق** — *raunaq* — "liveliness; festive bustle".

> چاند رات کو بازار میں رونق تھی۔
>
> *chā̃d rāt ko bāzār meṅ raunaq thī.*
>
> On the night of the new moon the market was full of life.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**رونق** (*raunaq*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *rawnaq*, "brightness, beauty"; in Urdu it is the life a crowd gives a place.
