# HL60 — Sanskrit A1 modern-technology retrieval

## Status

Proposed for issue #17562 under #13415 and #13295.

## Problem

The project-defined Sanskrit A1 point `SA-A1-M-13` says that computer
vocabulary is absent. That note is stale. The corpus already owns
**संगणकम्** (*saṅgaṇakam*, “a computer”) as
`SA-LEX-C105-THINGS105-02`, and chapter 269 owns sourced modern
**अन्तर्जालम्** (*antarjālam*, “internet”) as
`SA-LEX-C269-MEDIA-02`.

The missing evidence is not another word. It is a small, explicit retrieval
probe that joins those two existing owners without inflating two nouns into a
claim of digital skill.

## Contract

Append one Sanskrit-owned chapter after chapter 269 with two lessons:

1. independently retrieve computer and internet from English meanings without
   a visible Sanskrit model; and
2. independently retrieve the same two meanings in the reverse order, again
   without a visible Sanskrit model.

Both lessons introduce nothing. They require and practise the two direct
owners. The second lesson is the chapter payoff and the exact inventory probe.

## Knowledge ownership

This chapter owns no new atom. Its exact `SA-A1-M-13` probe is:

- `SA-LEX-C105-THINGS105-02`
- `SA-LEX-C269-MEDIA-02`

The learner-facing prose may show the two Sanskrit forms only after the
model-free response cue. The first cue asks for computer and then internet;
the second asks for internet and then computer.

## Ownership and sharding

Lesson files, chapter ledger, curriculum extension/path/membership owners,
book target, generated modality/book/narration owners, corpus evidence, and
the Sanskrit changelog entry must be Sanskrit-owned or single-record additive
files. The one inventory point and existing Sanskrit aggregate pins are the
only same-track files whose measured contract changes. No cross-language
mutable aggregate may be introduced.

## Evidence boundary

This slice closes one project-defined editorial A1 point. It proves two
model-free retrievals of two modern-technology nouns. It does not prove device
use, internet navigation, digital literacy, technological competence, learner
attainment, human validation, or exam readiness. The A1 inventory remains
partial.

## Acceptance

- A Sanskrit-owned red-before-green test pins both lessons, their order,
  exact ownership, duration ceilings, chapter payoff, and exact inventory
  probe.
- Both lessons introduce nothing and show no Sanskrit model before the learner
  answers.
- Existing lessons remain the sole owners of both atoms and their source
  boundaries remain authoritative.
- Sanskrit A1 coverage moves from 152/164 to 153/164, with 11 unmapped and
  zero partial points.
- Corpus, curriculum, shard, document-shard, modality, generated-book,
  narration, lesson, affected-package, and full book checks pass.
- Independent security and data-integrity review passes on the exact pushed
  head before every push.
