## You'll want to know: چمٹنا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C449-LOVE449-04]; assesses=[] -->

**چمٹنا** — *chimaṭnā* — "to cling tightly".

> بچہ ڈر کر ماں سے چمٹ گیا۔
>
> *bachchā ḍar kar māṅ se chimaṭ gayā.*
>
> The child took fright and clung to his mother.

Past (she …): وہ ماں سے چمٹ گئی, *voh māṅ se chimaṭ gaī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**چمٹنا** (*chimaṭnā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *cimaṭnā*, "to embrace closely", under *cimb*, "pinch", a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 4822).
