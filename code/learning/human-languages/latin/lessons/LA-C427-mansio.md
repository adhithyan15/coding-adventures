## You'll want to know: mānsiō, mānsiōnis
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C427-MANSIO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**mānsiō, mānsiōnis** — "an overnight stop, a halting place; a stay".

> Prīmā mānsiōne viātōrēs cēnant et dormiunt.
>
> *At the first halting place the travellers have dinner and sleep.*

Third declension, feminine: *mānsiō, mānsiōnis f.* A journey could be counted in *mānsiōnēs*, day stages. English *mansion* comes from it.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*mānsiō* is built on *maneō* 'to stay, to remain' (EtymDB; Lewis & Short), through its participle *mānsus*. English *mansion* comes from it through French.
