## You'll want to know: اختیار
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C339-IKHTIYAR339-01]; assesses=[] -->

**اختیار** — *ikhtiyār* — "authority; a power (to decide)".

> یہ میرے اختیار میں نہیں ہے۔
>
> *yih mere ikhtiyār meṅ nahīṅ hai.*
>
> This is not in my power.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**اختیار** (*ikhtiyār*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *ikhtiyār*, "choice": the power to choose.
