## You'll want to know: تنکا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C433-TILA433-04]; assesses=[] -->

**تنکا** — *tinkā* — "a straw; a blade of grass".

> ہوا میں تنکے اڑ رہے تھے۔
>
> *hawā meṅ tinke uṛ rahe the.*
>
> Bits of straw were blowing about in the wind.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**تنکا** (*tinkā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *tinkā*, "a blade of grass, a straw", under Sanskrit *tṛṇa*, "grass" (CDIAL 5906).
