## You'll want to know: ਸੁਨਿਆਰ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C335-METAL335-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ਸੁਨਿਆਰ** — *suniār* — "a goldsmith".

> ਸੁਨਿਆਰ ਸੋਨੇ ਦੀ ਮੁੰਦਰੀ ਬਣਾਉਂਦਾ ਹੈ।
>
> *suniār sone dī mundrī baṇāundā hai.*
>
> The goldsmith makes a gold ring.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਸੁਨਿਆਰ** (*suniār*) is inherited from Sanskrit *suvarṇakāra*, "a goldsmith"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *suneār*, "goldsmith", under it (CDIAL 13520).
