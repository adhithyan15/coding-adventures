## You'll want to know: くらい
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C158-LIGHTS158-05]; assesses=[] -->

**くらい** — *kurai* — "dark".

> **この へやは くらいです。** — *kono heya wa kurai desu* — "This room is dark."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**くらい** (*kurai*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi, and its older origin is not settled (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
