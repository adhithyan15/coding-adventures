## You'll want to know: причинять
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C353-USHCHERB-02]; assesses=[] -->

**причинять** (*prichinyát*) — "to cause (harm)".

> Я не хотел причинять тебе боль.
>
> *Ya ne khotél prichinyát tebé bol.*
>
> I did not want to cause you pain.

Imperfective; the perfective is *причинить*. It goes with bad things: *причинять боль, вред, ущерб*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**причинять** is the verb made from *причина* "a cause", which is usually traced to *при-* and *чин* "order, rank", the root of *чинить* "to make, to mend".
