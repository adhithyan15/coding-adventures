Mandarin Chinese, chapter 398: It Depends.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
tàidu — attitude.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for in an awkward spot, then the Mandarin for an excuse (a poor reason).

You'll want to know: tàidu.
tàidu — "attitude".
néng bu néng chénggōng yào kàn nǐ de tàidu — Whether you succeed depends on your attitude.
Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
tàidu is built from tài 'a bearing' and dù 'a measure', both inherited from Middle Chinese, thojH and duH (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'manner' and the second 'a rule'. In the word the second loses its tone.

What you've built.
One more word for saying what would happen.

Lesson 4 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for the difference, then the Mandarin for a region of a country.

You'll want to know: 现成.
现成 — xiànchéng — "ready-made".
要是没时间做饭就买现成的 yàoshi méi shíjiān zuòfàn jiù mǎi xiànchéng de — If there's no time to cook, buy something ready-made.

The word, taken apart.
现成 (xiànchéng) is built from 现 'to appear' and 成 'complete', both inherited from Middle Chinese, henH and dzyeng (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to show' and the second 'finished': already there and done.

Lesson 5 of 5.
qǔjuéyú — to depend on (a factor); to hinge on.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for a region of a country, then the Mandarin for ready-made.

You'll want to know: qǔjuéyú.
qǔjuéyú — "to depend on (a factor); to hinge on".
wǒmen qù bu qù qǔjuéyú tiānqì — Whether we go hinges on the weather.
Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
qǔjuéyú is built from 取 'to take', jué 'to decide' and yú 'by', all inherited from Middle Chinese, tshjuX, kwet and 'jo (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'to break through' and 'to cut' and lists the third as a grammar word: to take its decision from something.
