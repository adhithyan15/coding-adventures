## You'll want to know: لعنت
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C326-LANAT326-01]; assesses=[] -->

**لعنت** — *lānat* — "a curse".

> سب نے اس پر لعنت بھیجی۔
>
> *sab ne us par lānat bhejī.*
>
> Everyone cursed him.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**لعنت** (*lānat*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *laʿna*, "a curse". A curse is sent: **لعنت بھیجی**.
