## You'll want to know: die Delle
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C405-DELLE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**die Delle** — "the dent".

> Nach dem Parken hat das Auto eine kleine Delle.
>
> *After parking, the car has a small dent.*

Plural: *die Dellen*. Also *die Beule*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Delle* is inherited from Middle High German *telle* 'a hollow, a dip in the ground' (EtymDB).
