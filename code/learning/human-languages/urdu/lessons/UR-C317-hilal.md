## You'll want to know: ہلال
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C317-EID317-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ہلال** — *hilāl* — "the new crescent moon".

> شام کو آسمان پر ہلال نظر آیا۔
>
> *shām ko āsmān par hilāl nazar āyā.*
>
> In the evening the new moon was seen in the sky.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ہلال** (*hilāl*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *hilāl*, "the new moon": the thin first crescent that tells people the month has begun.
