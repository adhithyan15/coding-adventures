## You'll want to know: 因为
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C96-YINWEI-01]; assesses=[] -->

**因为** — *yīnwèi* — "because".

> **我不去 因为我没有车** *wǒ bú qù, yīnwèi wǒ méiyǒu chē* — I'm not going, because I don't have a car.

The next lesson puts the first character in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**因为** (*yīnwèi*) is built from 因 'to follow from' and 为 'for'. 因 is inherited from Middle Chinese *'jin* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'to rely on; to follow from'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ZH-LEX-C87-MEIYOU-01] -->

One reason, in one clause, after what it explains: 我不去, 因为我没有车. It can also come first: 因为我没有车, 我不去.
