German, chapter 114: Blanket, Towel, Soap, Toothbrush, Toothpaste.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
die Decke — a blanket; a ceiling.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for a curtain, then the German for a cushion, a pillow.

You'll want to know: die Decke.
die Decke — "a blanket; a ceiling".

The word, taken apart.
Decke from Old High German decchī, from the verb "to cover" from Proto-Germanic þakjaną, built on þaką, "roof" (EtymDB 2.1) — a cousin of Dach and English thatch.

Lesson 2 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for a cushion, a pillow, then the German for a blanket; a ceiling.

You'll want to know: das Handtuch.
das Handtuch — "a towel".

The word, taken apart.
Handtuch is Hand + the word for "cloth", Old High German tuoh from Proto-Germanic dōkaz (EtymDB 2.1): a cloth for the hands.
