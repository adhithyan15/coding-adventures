Telugu, chapter 536: Free Time.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
వనభోజనం (vanabhōjanaṁ) — a picnic; a meal outdoors.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to call to account, then the Telugu for to deceive.

You'll want to know: వనభోజనం.
వనభోజనం — vanabhōjanaṁ — "a picnic; a meal outdoors".
ఆదివారం మేము స్నేహితులతో కలిసి వనభోజనానికి వెళ్ళాం. ādivāraṁ mēmu snēhitulatō kalisi vanabhōjanāniki veḷḷāṁ. On Sunday we went on a picnic with friends.
వనం, a grove, + భోజనం, a meal: a meal eaten out under the trees.

The word, taken apart.
వనభోజనం (vanabhōjanaṁ) is a learned compound built from Sanskrit vana, "a forest, a grove" (CDIAL 11258), and bhojana, "feeding, a meal" (Monier-Williams).

తాతయ్యకు పుస్తకాలు చదవడమే కాలక్షేపం. tātayyaku pustakālu cadavaḍamē kālakṣēpaṁ. Reading books is grandpa's pastime.

కాలక్షేపం (kālakṣēpaṁ) is a learned compound built from Sanskrit kāla, "time" (CDIAL 3084), and kṣēpa, "sending, throwing" (CDIAL 3738): sending the time on its way.

Lesson 4 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a pastime, then the Telugu for gardening.

You'll want to know: అల్లిక.
అల్లిక — allika — "knitting; weaving".
అమ్మమ్మ ఖాళీ సమయంలో అల్లిక చేస్తుంది. ammamma khāḷī samayaṁlō allika cēstundi. Grandma knits in her free time.
The noun of అల్లు, to knit.

The word, taken apart.
అల్లిక (allika) is native Dravidian: DEDR 260 lists Telugu allu, "to plait, to braid, to weave", with Tamil allu, "to knit".
