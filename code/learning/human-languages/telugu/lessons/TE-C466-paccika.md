## You'll want to know: పచ్చిక
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C466-CAURASTA466-03]; assesses=[] -->

**పచ్చిక** — *paccika* — "a lawn; grassland".

> పిల్లలు పచ్చిక మీద ఆడుకుంటున్నారు.
>
> *pillalu paccika mīda āḍukuṇṭunnāru.*
>
> The children are playing on the lawn.

Fresh green grass for sitting or grazing. పచ్చ, green, is in the word.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**పచ్చిక** (*paccika*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 3821 lists Telugu *paccika*, "grass", beside *pacca*, "green".
