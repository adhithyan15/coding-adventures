Lesson 1 of 5.
la guerre — war.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for a butcher's, then the French for a large river.

You'll want to know: la guerre.
la guerre — "war".

The word, taken apart.
guerre from Old French guerre from Medieval Latin werra (EtymDB 2.1), from Frankish werra, "strife" — borrowed from the Franks; Germanic w- became French gu-.

Lesson 3 of 5.
l'armée — an army.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for war, then the French for peace.

You'll want to know: l'armée.
l'armée — "an army".

The word, taken apart.
armée is the past participle of the verb for "to arm", used as a noun: a force that is armed. (EtymDB 2.1 links it to Medieval Latin armata, "an armed force".)
