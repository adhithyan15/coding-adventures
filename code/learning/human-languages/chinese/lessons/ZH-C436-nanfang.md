## You'll want to know: 南方
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C436-SOUTH436-02]; assesses=[] -->

**南方** — *nánfāng* — "the south of the country; southern China".

> *dōngtiān wǒmen zuò huǒchē qù nánfāng* — In winter we take the train down south.

The new character, in pieces: 方 — four strokes: a dot, a long bar, a stroke that turns down and hooks, and a long stroke falling to the left. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**南方** (*nánfāng*) is built from 南 'south' and 方 'a quarter', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *nom* and *pjang* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second as the four quarters of the world.
