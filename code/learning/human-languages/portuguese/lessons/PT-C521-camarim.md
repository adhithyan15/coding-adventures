## You'll want to know: o camarim
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C521-GENEROS-05]; assesses=[] -->

**o camarim** — "(theatre) dressing room".

> Depois do espetáculo, fomos ao camarim cumprimentar a atriz.
>
> *After the show, we went to the dressing room to greet the actress.*

Plural *camarins*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*camarim* was borrowed from Italian *camerino* "a little room" (EtymDB), from Latin *camera* "a vaulted room".
