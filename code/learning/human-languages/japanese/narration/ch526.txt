Japanese, chapter 526: Progress.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
しんぽする (shinpo suru) — to progress; to advance.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for to compare (to), then the Japanese for sooner or later.

You'll want to know: しんぽする.
しんぽする — shinpo suru — "to progress; to advance".
もし ぎじゅつが しんぽしたら、くるまは はやく なるでしょう。 — moshi gijutsu ga shinpo shitara, kuruma wa hayaku naru deshō — "If technology advances, cars will get faster."
Polite しんぽします, shinposhimasu; a する verb. Past (polite): ぎじゅつが しんぽしました。, gijutsu ga shinposhimashita.

The word, taken apart.
しんぽする (shinpo suru) is the Sino-Japanese (kango) noun shinpo with the native verb suru, "to do": two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are shin, "advance" (Middle Chinese tsinH) and po, "walk" (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).

どんどん (dondon) — rapidly; steadily (and fast).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for to improve (something), then the Japanese for to turn for the better.

You'll want to know: どんどん.
どんどん — dondon — "rapidly; steadily (and fast)".
もし あめが ふったら、かわの みずが どんどん ふえるでしょう。 — moshi ame ga futtara, kawa no mizu ga dondon fueru deshō — "If it rains, the river will rise fast."

The word, taken apart.
どんどん (dondon) is a native mimetic word, imitating a sound rather than borrowed from Chinese: JMdict's first sense is the noise of drumming, and its later senses are "rapidly" and "steadily"; no dictionary we use records an older form, so its history is not settled (JMdict).
