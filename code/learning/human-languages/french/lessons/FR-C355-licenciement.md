## You'll want to know: le licenciement
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C355-DEMISSION-02]; assesses=[] -->

**le licenciement** — "the dismissal, the layoff".

> Il a reçu sa lettre de licenciement.
>
> *He received his letter of dismissal.*

*Un licenciement économique* — a layoff for economic reasons.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*licenciement* is built from *licencier* and the suffix *-ment*. The verb rests on *licence*, from Latin *licentia* "freedom, licence", from *licēre* "to be allowed" (Lewis & Short).
