## You'll want to know: jìshù
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C260-SKILL260-04]; assesses=[] -->

*jìshù* — "skill; technique".

> *tā de jìshù hěn hǎo* — He's very skilled.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*jìshù* is built from *jì* 'a craft' and *shù*, both inherited from Middle Chinese, *gjeX* and *zywit* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'skill' and the second with this very word. Middle Chinese *zywit* would regularly give a rising tone, shú; the falling tone of shù is irregular.
