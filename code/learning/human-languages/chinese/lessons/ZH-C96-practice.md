## The exchange
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-PERFORMANCE-BECAUSE-01]; assesses=[ZH-LEX-C96-YINWEI-01, ZH-LEX-C96-SUOYI-01, ZH-LEX-C96-SHUFU-01, ZH-LEX-C95-WEISHENME-01, ZH-LEX-C90-NENG-01] -->

> **A:** 你为什么不吃饭
>
> *nǐ wèishénme bù chī fàn* — Why aren't you eating?
>
> **B:** *yīnwèi wǒ bú è* — Because I'm not hungry.
>
> **A:** 你为什么不去上课
>
> *nǐ wèishénme bú qù shàngkè* — Why aren't you going to class?
>
> **B:** *yīnwèi wǒ bù shūfu, suǒyǐ wǒ bú qù* — I'm not feeling well, so I'm not going.
>
> **A:** 你明天能来吗
>
> *nǐ míngtiān néng lái ma* — Can you come tomorrow?
>
> **B:** 能
>
> *néng* — I can.

为什么 asks; 因为 answers, with one reason in one clause; *suǒyǐ* carries the reason on to what happens.

## What you've built — a question and its reason
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ZH-PERFORMANCE-BECAUSE-01, ZH-SCRIPT-YIN-01, ZH-LEX-C96-YINWEI-01, ZH-LEX-C96-SUOYI-01, ZH-LEX-C95-WEISHENME-01] -->

- **你为什么不去** *nǐ wèishénme bú qù* — why aren't you going?
- **因为我没有车** *yīnwèi wǒ méiyǒu chē* — because I don't have a car.
- *yīnwèi wǒ hěn lèi, suǒyǐ wǒ bú qù* — I'm tired, so I'm not going.

**Listen.** *Nǐ wèishénme xuéxí Hànyǔ?*

**Speak.** Answer it with 因为 and one reason of your own.
