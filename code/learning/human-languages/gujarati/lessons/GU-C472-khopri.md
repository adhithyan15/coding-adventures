## You'll want to know: ખોપરી
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C472-NAS472-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ખોપરી** — *khoprī* — "the skull".

> ખોપરી મગજને બચાવે છે.
>
> *khoprī magajne bacāve che.*
>
> The skull protects the brain.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ખોપરી** (*khoprī*) goes back to a reconstructed *\*khōppa-*, "coconut shell"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati word, "skull" (CDIAL 3936): the shell of the head.
