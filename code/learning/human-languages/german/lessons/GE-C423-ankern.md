## You'll want to know: ankern
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C423-ANKER-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ankern** — "to anchor, to lie at anchor".

> Das Boot ankert vor dem Strand.
>
> *The boat is anchored off the beach.*

*Es ankert*, *es hat geankert*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ankern* is built on *Anker* 'anchor', an early borrowing from Latin *ancora* (EtymDB; Lewis & Short).
