## You'll want to know: آشیانہ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C438-CHONCH438-03]; assesses=[] -->

**آشیانہ** — *āshiyānā* — "a nest".

> چڑیا نے درخت پر آشیانہ بنایا۔
>
> *chiṛyā ne darakht par āshiyānā banāyā.*
>
> The sparrow built a nest in the tree.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**آشیانہ** (*āshiyānā*) is usually traced to Persian *āshiyāna*, "a nest"; poets also use it for a home.
