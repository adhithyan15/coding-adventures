## You'll want to know: il regno
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C325-REGNO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**il regno** — "the kingdom; the reign".

> Il regno era piccolo ma ricco.
>
> *The kingdom was small but rich.*

Also the time a ruler reigns: *durante il suo regno*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*regno* is inherited from Latin *rēgnum* "royal power, a kingdom" (EtymDB).
