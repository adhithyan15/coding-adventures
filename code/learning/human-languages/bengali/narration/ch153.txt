Bengali, chapter 153: To discuss, To praise, To scold, To thank, To put up with.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
আলোচনা করা (ālochônā kôrā) — to discuss.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for to comb, then the Bengali for to bite.

You'll want to know: আলোচনা করা.
আলোচনা করা — ālochônā kôrā — "to discuss".

The phrase, taken apart.
আলোচনা করা (ālochônā kôrā) is ālochônā, "discussion", Sanskrit ālocanā, "consideration", taken over whole, + kôrā, "to do", from Sanskrit karoti, "does" (CDIAL 2814).

Lesson 3 of 5.
বকা (bôkā) — to scold.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for to discuss, then the Bengali for to praise.

You'll want to know: বকা.
বকা — bôkā — "to scold".

The word, taken apart.
বকা (bôkā) is a tadbhava, a word inherited and reshaped by sound change. It comes from bakk, "chatter", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 9117). CDIAL lists Bengali bakā, "to prattle".
