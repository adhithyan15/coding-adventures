# permitir — let something pass through

## Warm-up

## Sounds you'll need
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

- *per-mi-TEER*: keep the two **r** sounds light and stress the final syllable.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-PERMITIR]; assesses=[] -->

**permitir** means “to permit” or “to allow.” It continues Latin **permittere**,
“to let through,” built from **per-**, “through,” and **mittere**, “to send or
let go.” English *permit* belongs to the same family.

On a public sign, **está permitido** means “it is permitted,” while **no está
permitido** means “it is not permitted.” Treat that whole sign formula as one
useful chunk here.
