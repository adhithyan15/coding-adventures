## You'll want to know: विजेता
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C534-MAICH534-04]; assesses=[] -->

**विजेता** — *vijetā* — "a winner; a champion".

> विजेता टीम ख़ुशी से नाच रही थी।
>
> *Vijetā ṭīm khuśī se nāc rahī thī.*
>
> The winning team was dancing for joy.

Masculine or feminine, by the person meant.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**विजेता** (*vijetā*) is a tatsama, taken whole from Sanskrit *vijetṛ*, "a vanquisher, a conqueror" (Monier-Williams). Hindi takes the Sanskrit nominative form *vijetā*.
