## You'll want to know: enfadado
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C707-ENFADO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**enfadado** — "angry, annoyed".

> Mi jefe está enfadado conmigo.
>
> *My boss is angry with me.*

The usual word in Spain: *estar enfadado con alguien*. Latin America mostly uses another word, coming up next.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*enfadado* is built on *enfadar*, which is usually traced to Portuguese *enfadar*, made from *fado* "fate", from Latin *fātum*.
