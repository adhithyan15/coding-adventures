## You'll want to know: વાસી
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C172-STALE172-02]; assesses=[] -->

**વાસી** — *vāsī* — "stale".

> **આ રોટલી વાસી છે.** — *ā roṭlī vāsī che* — "This rotli is stale."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**વાસી** (*vāsī*) is a tadbhava. CDIAL lists it, glossed "stale", under Sanskrit *vāsita*, "infused, scented" (CDIAL 11604).
