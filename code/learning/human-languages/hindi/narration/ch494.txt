Hindi, chapter 494: Looking After Someone.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
तीमारदारी (tīmārdārī) — nursing the sick.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for cancer, then the Hindi for to stiffen.

You'll want to know: तीमारदारी.
तीमारदारी — tīmārdārī — "nursing the sick".
बीमारी में मीरा ने अपनी माँ की बहुत तीमारदारी की। bīmārī meṁ Mīrā ne apnī māṁ kī bahut tīmārdārī kī. Mira nursed her mother devotedly through her illness.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
तीमारदारी (tīmārdārī) is usually traced to Persian tīmārdārī, "tending the sick", built on tīmār, "care of the sick", and -dārī, "keeping". tīmārdār is the person at the bedside.

गोद (god) — a lap.

गोद — god — "a lap".
बच्चा माँ की गोद में सो गया। baccā māṁ kī god meṁ so gayā. The baby fell asleep in its mother's lap.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
गोद (god) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Hindi god, "lap", under gōddī, a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 4284). god lenā, "to take into one's lap", is to adopt a child.

Lesson 3 of 5.
लिटाना (liṭānā) — to lay someone down.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for nursing the sick, then the Hindi for a lap.

You'll want to know: लिटाना.
लिटाना — liṭānā — "to lay someone down".
नर्स ने मरीज़ को बिस्तर पर लिटाया। nars ne marīz ko bistar par liṭāyā. The nurse laid the patient down on the bed.
Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने बच्चे को लिटाया, usne bacce ko liṭāyā.

The word, taken apart.
लिटाना (liṭānā) is the causative partner of leṭnā, "to lie down", which CDIAL lists under Sanskrit lēṭyáti (CDIAL 11109); the vowel shortens before the causative ending.

Lesson 4 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a lap, then the Hindi for to lay someone down.

You'll want to know: सेंकना.
सेंकना — sẽknā — "to warm (a sore part); to toast".
माँ ने गरम पानी की बोतल से मेरा पेट सेंका। māṁ ne garam pānī kī botal se merā peṭ sẽkā. Mother warmed my stomach with a hot-water bottle.
Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने घुटना सेंका, usne ghuṭnā sẽkā.

The word, taken apart.
सेंकना (sẽknā) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Hindi sẽknā, "to warm, foment, toast", under sēkk, a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 13581). Bread is toasted and a sore knee warmed with the same verb.

ओढ़ाना — oṛhānā — "to cover someone (with a blanket)".
माँ ने बच्चे को कंबल ओढ़ाया। māṁ ne bacce ko kambal oṛhāyā. Mother tucked a blanket round the child.
Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने कंबल ओढ़ाया, usne kambal oṛhāyā.

The word, taken apart.
ओढ़ाना (oṛhānā) is the causative partner of oṛhnā, "to cover oneself", which CDIAL lists under ōḍḍh, a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 2547): to wrap someone else up.
