## You'll want to know: أخاف
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C489-KHAWF489-05]; assesses=[] -->

**أخاف** — *akhāfa* — "to frighten".

> أخافني صوت الرعد في الليل.
>
> *akhāfanī ṣawt ar-raʿd fī al-layl.*
>
> The sound of the thunder frightened me in the night.

Present: يخيف, *yukhīf*.

Do not confuse it with أخاف read akhāfu, I am afraid, the present of خاف.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**أخاف** (*akhāfa*) is built on the native Arabic root *kh-w-f*, "fear" (Lane). It is Form IV of khāfa, to be afraid: to make someone afraid. Mukhīf, frightening, is its participle.
