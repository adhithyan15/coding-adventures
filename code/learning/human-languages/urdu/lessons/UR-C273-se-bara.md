# سے بڑا (se baṛā) — bigger than, the biggest

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[UR-LEX-C69-ANIMALS69-05, UR-LEX-C70-ADJ70-01, UR-GRAMMAR-C272-FEELING-02] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "big" and "small". (*baṛā*, *chhoṭā*.) Then "I liked this city". (*mujhe yih shahr achchhā lagā*.) Now compare two cities.

## You'll want to know: bigger than
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-GRAMMAR-C273-COMPARISON-01]; assesses=[UR-LEX-C69-ANIMALS69-05, UR-LEX-C71-ADJ71-05, UR-LEX-C202-CHIZ202-04, UR-LEX-C80-TOWN80-03, UR-LEX-C195-SIFAT195-03, UR-LEX-PASAND] -->

> **لاہور بڑا ہے۔**
>
> *Lāhaur baṛā hai.* — "Lahore is big."

> **یہ گھر اس گھر سے بڑا ہے۔**
>
> *yih ghar us ghar se baṛā hai.* — "This house is bigger than that one."

Urdu adds no "-er" here. **سے** (*se*), "from, than", goes after the thing compared, and the word stays as it is:

> **بس ٹیکسی سے سستی ہے۔**
>
> *bas ṭaiksī se sastī hai.* — "The bus is cheaper than a taxi."

**بس** is feminine: **سستی**. **زیادہ** (*zyādā*) and **کم** (*kam*) say more and less:

> **مجھے چائے زیادہ پسند ہے۔**
>
> *mujhe chāy zyādā pasand hai.* — "I like tea more."

## You'll want to know: the most
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-GRAMMAR-C273-COMPARISON-02]; assesses=[UR-LEX-C89-DEF89-01, UR-LEX-C70-ADJ70-03, UR-LEX-C70-ADJ70-05, UR-LEX-C45-FAMILY45-02] -->

> **یہ سب سے اچھا ہے۔**
>
> *yih sab se achchhā hai.* — "This is the best."

> **وہ سب سے لمبی لڑکی ہے۔**
>
> *voh sab se lambī laṛkī hai.* — "She is the tallest girl."

**سب سے** (*sab se*), "than all", makes the most: **سب سے بڑا**, **سب سے سستا**.
