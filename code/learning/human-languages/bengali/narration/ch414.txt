Bengali, chapter 414: At the Bank.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ব্যাংক (bængk) — a bank.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for dirt, then the Bengali for junk.

You'll want to know: ব্যাংক.
ব্যাংক — bængk — "a bank".
মীরা ব্যাংকে টাকা জমা দিল। mīrā bængke ṭākā jômā dilô. Mira put money into the bank.

The word, taken apart.
ব্যাংক (bængk) is borrowed from English bank; the word-initial y-phala gives the English vowel æ.

What you've built.
One more word for getting around.

Lesson 5 of 5.
নগদ (nôgôd) — cash; in cash.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a cashier, then the Bengali for a cheque.

You'll want to know: নগদ.
নগদ — nôgôd — "cash; in cash".
দোকানদার নগদ টাকা চাইলেন। dokāndār nôgôd ṭākā chāilen. The shopkeeper asked for cash.
Before a noun it means paid in hand: নগদ (nôgôd) টাকা, cash; নগদে, in cash.

The word, taken apart.
নগদ (nôgôd) is usually traced to Arabic naqd, "ready money", which reached Bengali through Persian.
