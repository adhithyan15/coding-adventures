## You'll want to know: o palco
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C519-ENSAIO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**o palco** — "stage".

> Os atores entraram no palco.
>
> *The actors came on stage.*

*Subir ao palco* — to go on stage.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*palco* was borrowed from Italian *palco* "a platform, a stage" (EtymDB), a word of Germanic origin.
