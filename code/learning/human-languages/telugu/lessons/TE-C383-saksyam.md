# సాక్ష్యం (sākṣyaṁ) — evidence; testimony

## You'll want to know: సాక్ష్యం
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C383-VADANA383-02]; assesses=[] -->

**సాక్ష్యం** — *sākṣyaṁ* — "evidence; testimony".

> ఆ మాటకు సాక్ష్యం ఏమిటి?
>
> *ā māṭaku sākṣyaṁ ēmiṭi?*
>
> What is the evidence for that statement?

సాక్ష్యం చెప్పు, to give evidence, to testify.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**సాక్ష్యం** (*sākṣyaṁ*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *sākṣya*, "evidence" (CDIAL 13322), and also "testimony, attestation" (Monier-Williams).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
