## You'll want to know: cōlō, cōlāre
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C355-CONDIO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**cōlō, cōlāre** — "to strain, to filter".

> Vīnum per linteum cōlāvit.
>
> *He strained the wine through a cloth.*

*cōlō, cōlāre, cōlāvī, cōlātum*, from *cōlum*, 'a strainer'. Not the verb *colō, colere*, 'to farm'. English *colander*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**cōlō** is built on *cōlum*, "a strainer, a colander" (EtymDB, Lewis & Short). English *colander* comes from its family.
