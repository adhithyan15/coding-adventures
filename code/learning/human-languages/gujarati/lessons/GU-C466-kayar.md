## You'll want to know: કાયર
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C466-SHAURYA466-01]; assesses=[] -->

**કાયર** — *kāyar* — "cowardly".

> કાયર માણસ જોખમ જોઈને ભાગી જાય છે.
>
> *kāyar māṇas jokham joīne bhāgī jāy che.*
>
> A coward runs away at the sight of danger.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**કાયર** (*kāyar*) is inherited from Sanskrit *kātara*, "confused, discouraged, cowardly"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati word, "timid" (CDIAL 3027).
