## You'll want to know: терминал
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C422-VYLET-04]; assesses=[] -->

**терминал** (*terminál*) — "a terminal (at an airport); a card machine".

> Между терминалами ходит автобус.
>
> *Mézhdu terminálami khódit avtóbus.*
>
> A bus runs between the terminals.

Masculine. Also a card reader in a shop: *оплатить через терминал*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*терминал* is usually traced to English *terminal*, which EtymDB derives through French from Late Latin *terminālis*, on *terminus* 'a boundary, an end'. The Russian word is not in EtymDB.
