## You'll want to know: ならぶ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C183-STAFF183-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ならぶ** — *narabu* — "to line up; to queue".

> **きゃくが みせの まえに ならびます。** — *kyaku ga mise no mae ni narabimasu* — "Customers line up in front of the shop."

Polite **ならびます**, *narabimasu*; a godan verb.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ならぶ** (*narabu*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
