## You'll want to know: der Zuschauer
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C510-TRIBUENE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**der Zuschauer** — "spectator; viewer".

> Im Stadion waren fünfzigtausend Zuschauer.
>
> *There were fifty thousand spectators in the stadium.*

Plural: *die Zuschauer*. Also a TV viewer.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Zuschauer* is built on *zuschauen* "to watch", from *schauen* "to look", inherited from Old High German *scouwōn*, from Proto-Germanic *skawwōną* (EtymDB).
