## Script — the left of 说, then the right of 孩
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-SCRIPT-GAI-01]; assesses=[] -->

> 该

**Two pieces, and you write both already.**

**The left of 说, two strokes**: a dot, then a short horizontal that turns down and flicks up to the right.

**The right of 孩, six strokes**: a dot at the top; a horizontal under it; a stroke that falls to the lower left and turns up to the right; a long left-falling stroke; a shorter left-falling stroke under it; and a dot at the lower right.

2 + 6 = eight.
