## You'll want to know: ਕੜਛੀ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C242-KAM242-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ਕੜਛੀ** — *kaṛchhī* — "a ladle".

> ਕੜਛੀ ਪਤੀਲੇ ਵਿੱਚ ਹੈ।
>
> *kaṛchhī patīle vich hai.*
>
> The ladle is in the cooking pot.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਕੜਛੀ** (*kaṛchhī*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*kaṭacchu*, "ladle"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *kaṛchī*, "iron ladle" (CDIAL 2633).
