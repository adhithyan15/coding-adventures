## You'll want to know: cōngming
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C123-PEOPLE123-01]; assesses=[] -->

*cōngming* — "clever; bright".

> *wǒ mèimei hěn cōngming* — My little sister is very bright.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*cōngming* is built from *cōng* and *míng*, both inherited from Middle Chinese, *tshuwng* and *mjaeng* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription). The dictionary glosses the first 'to hear' and 'to discern', and the second 'bright'.
