Lesson 2 of 5.
bagnato — wet.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for safe; sure, then the Italian for dangerous.

You'll want to know: bagnato.
bagnato — "wet".

The word, taken apart.
bagnato is the past participle of the verb for "to wet, to bathe", which from Vulgar Latin baneare, from Late Latin balneare, "to bathe". (EtymDB 2.1).

You'll want to know: morbido.
morbido — "soft".

The word, taken apart.
morbido from Latin morbidus, "sickly", from morbus, "disease" — a learned borrowing that softened in meaning. EtymDB 2.1 has no row for morbido; it derives French morbide and Spanish mórbido from morbidus.
