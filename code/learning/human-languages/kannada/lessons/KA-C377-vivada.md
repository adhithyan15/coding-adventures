## You'll want to know: ವಿವಾದ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C377-TARKA377-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ವಿವಾದ** — *vivāda* — "a controversy; a dispute".

> ಹೊಸ ನಿಯಮದ ಬಗ್ಗೆ ದೊಡ್ಡ ವಿವಾದ ಆಯಿತು.
>
> *hosa niyamada bagge doḍḍa vivāda āyitu.*
>
> There was a big controversy about the new rule.

ವಿವಾದಾತ್ಮಕ, controversial.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ವಿವಾದ** (*vivāda*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *vivāda*, "a dispute, a quarrel, a lawsuit" (Monier-Williams), from *vi-* "apart" and *vad* "to speak".
