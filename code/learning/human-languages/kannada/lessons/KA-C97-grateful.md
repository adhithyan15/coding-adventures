# ಆಭಾರಿ (ābhāri) — grateful, obliged

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[KA-LEX-C97-GRATEFUL-01, KA-LEX-C97-GRATEFUL-02] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Kannada for pardon, then the Kannada for an introduction.

## You'll want to know: ಆಭಾರಿ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C97-GRATEFUL-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ಆಭಾರಿ** (*ābhāri*) — "grateful, obliged".

**ನಾನು ನಿಮಗೆ ಆಭಾರಿ** (*nānu nimage ābhāri*) — "I am grateful to you", a deeper
thanks than **ಧನ್ಯವಾದ**.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

A thank-you with more weight.
