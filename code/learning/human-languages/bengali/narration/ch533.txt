Bengali, chapter 533: By Chance.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
দৈবাৎ (doibāt) — by chance.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a forecast, then the Bengali for an astrologer.

You'll want to know: দৈবাৎ.
দৈবাৎ — doibāt — "by chance".
বাজারে দৈবাৎ আমার পুরনো বন্ধুর সঙ্গে দেখা হল। Bājāre doibāt āmār purôno bôndhur sôngge dækhā hôlô. By chance I met my old friend at the market.

The word, taken apart.
দৈবাৎ (doibāt) is the Sanskrit form daivāt, "from fate", of daiva, "coming from the gods, fate" (Monier-Williams): by the gods' doing.

কাকতালীয় (kāktālīyô) — coincidental.

You'll want to know: কাকতালীয়.
কাকতালীয় — kāktālīyô — "coincidental".
আমাদের জন্মদিন একই দিনে, এটা কাকতালীয়। Āmāder jônmôdin eki dine, eṭā kāktālīyô. Our birthdays fall on the same day; it's a coincidence.

The word, taken apart.
কাকতালীয় (kāktālīyô) is Sanskrit kākatālīya (Monier-Williams), from a fable: a crow lands on a palm tree at the very moment a fruit falls, and the two events only look connected.

Warm-up.

Before the new one: say the Bengali for by chance, then the Bengali for coincidental.

You'll want to know: মওকা.
মওকা — môokā — "a lucky break; an opening".
দোকানে ছাড় ছিল, আমি মওকা বুঝে একটা জ্যাকেট কিনলাম। Dokāne chhāṛ chhilô, āmi môokā bujhe ekṭā jækeṭ kinlām. There was a discount at the shop; I saw my chance and bought a jacket.

The word, taken apart.
মওকা (môokā) is usually traced to Arabic mawqiʿ, "a place, an occasion", through Persian.

Lesson 4 of 5.
ফসকানো (phôskāno) — to slip away; to be missed (a chance).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for coincidental, then the Bengali for a lucky break.

You'll want to know: ফসকানো.
ফসকানো — phôskāno — "to slip away; to be missed (a chance)".
এই সুযোগ ফসকালে তুমি আর পাবে না। Ei sujog phôskāle tumi ār pābe nā. If you let this chance slip, you won't get it again.
Past (he/she …): সেই সুযোগ ফসকাল, sei sujog phôskālô.

The word, taken apart.
ফসকানো (phôskāno) probably belongs with ফসকা, "loose", which CDIAL lists as Bengali phaskā under a reconstructed phass, "loose" (CDIAL 9068): what slips from a loose grip, a ball or a chance.
