Italian, chapter 403: Sui social.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
pubblicare — to publish, to post.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for to browse, then the Italian for the link.

You'll want to know: pubblicare.
pubblicare — "to publish, to post".
Se pubblichi la foto, tutti la vedono. If you post the photo, everyone sees it.
For books too: pubblicare un romanzo.

The word, taken apart.
pubblicare is a learned borrowing from Latin pūblicāre "to make public" (Lewis & Short), from pūblicus "of the people, public".

Lesson 3 of 5.
inoltrare — to forward (a message); to submit.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for to publish, then the Italian for to comment.

You'll want to know: inoltrare.
inoltrare — "to forward (a message); to submit".
Ti inoltro il messaggio della professoressa. I will forward you the teacher's message.
In offices, inoltrare una domanda is to submit an application.

The word, taken apart.
inoltrare is built from in- and oltre "beyond, further", inherited from Latin ultrā "beyond" (Lewis & Short): to send something further on.

Lesson 4 of 5.
l'email — the email.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for to comment, then the Italian for to forward.

You'll want to know: l'email.
l'email — "the email".
Se non rispondi all'email, ti chiamo. If you do not answer the email, I will call you.
Feminine: un'email, le email. Also la mail or la posta elettronica.

The word, taken apart.
email was borrowed from English e-mail, short for electronic mail; mail goes back through Middle English male to Anglo-Norman male (EtymDB), usually explained as a word for a bag.

Lesson 5 of 5.
la casella — the inbox, the mailbox; the box (on a form).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for to forward, then the Italian for the email.

You'll want to know: la casella.
la casella — "the inbox, the mailbox; the box (on a form)".
Controlla la casella: ti ho scritto ieri. Check your inbox: I wrote to you yesterday.
La casella di posta — the inbox. On a form, barrare la casella — tick the box.

The word, taken apart.
casella is a little casa "house", inherited from Latin casa "a hut, a cottage" (Lewis & Short): a small compartment, then a small box for letters.
