## You'll want to know: મુસીબત
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C374-AVRODH374-03]; assesses=[] -->

**મુસીબત** — *musībat* — "trouble".

> જો તમે મોડું કરશો તો મુસીબતમાં પડશો.
>
> *jo tame moḍũ karśo to musībatmā̃ paḍśo.*
>
> If you are late, you will land in trouble.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**મુસીબત** (*musībat*) is usually traced to Arabic *muṣība*, "a misfortune", through Persian.

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
