German, chapter 100: Belt, Jacket, Border, World, Language.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
der Gürtel — a belt.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for a cap, then the German for a sock.

You'll want to know: der Gürtel.
der Gürtel — "a belt".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.

[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does der Gürtel mean? ("A belt".) Say it once more.
