## You'll want to know: hòuchēshì
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C403-TRAVELLER403-01]; assesses=[] -->

*hòuchēshì* — "station waiting room".

> *nǐ zuò zài hòuchēshì děng wǒ* — Sit in the waiting room and wait for me.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*hòuchēshì* is built from 候车 'to wait for a train' and *shì* 'a room', all inherited from Middle Chinese, *huwH*, *tsyhae* and *syit* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the last 'a room'.
