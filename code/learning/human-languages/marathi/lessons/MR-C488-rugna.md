## You'll want to know: रुग्ण
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C488-RUGNA488-01]; assesses=[] -->

**रुग्ण** — *rugṇa* — "a sick person; a patient".

> दवाखान्यात सकाळपासून रुग्णांची रांग होती.
>
> *davākhānyāt sakāḷpāsūn rugṇāñcī rāṅg hotī.*
>
> There was a queue of patients at the clinic from the morning.

Masculine.

As in रुग्णालय, a hospital, and रुग्णवाहिका, an ambulance.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**रुग्ण** (*rugṇa*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *rugṇa*, "broken, diseased" (CDIAL 10760).
