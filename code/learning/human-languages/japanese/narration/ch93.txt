Lesson 1 of 5.
まちどおしい (machidooshii) — long-awaited.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for a request, then the Japanese for a desire.

You'll want to know: まちどおしい.
まちどおしい — machidooshii — "long-awaited".

The word, taken apart.
まちどおしい (machidooshii) (machidōshii) is native: machi, from matsu, "to wait", with dōshii, from tōi, "far", voiced: the waiting feels far (JMdict). It is inherited rather than borrowed.

Lesson 3 of 5.
ちゅうもん (chuumon) — an order.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for long-awaited, then the Japanese for a prayer.

You'll want to know: ちゅうもん.
ちゅうもん — chuumon — "an order".

The word, taken apart.
ちゅうもん (chuumon) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are chū, "pour; note" (Middle Chinese tsyuH) and mon, "writing" (mjun) (Unihan, KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
