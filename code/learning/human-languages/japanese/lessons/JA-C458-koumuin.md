## You'll want to know: こうむいん
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C458-CAREER458-02]; assesses=[] -->

**こうむいん** — *kōmuin* — "a civil servant".

> **ちちは しやくしょの こうむいんです。** — *chichi wa shiyakusho no kōmuin desu* — "My father is a civil servant at the city hall."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**こうむいん** (*kōmuin*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): three Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *kō*, "public" (Middle Chinese *kuwng*), *mu*, "duties" (*mjuH*), and *in*, "member" (*hjwen*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
