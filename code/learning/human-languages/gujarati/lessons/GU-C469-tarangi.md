## You'll want to know: તરંગી
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C469-TARANGI469-02]; assesses=[] -->

**તરંગી** — *tarangī* — "whimsical".

> તરંગી મામા રોજ નવી યોજના બનાવે છે.
>
> *tarangī māmā roj navī yojnā banāve che.*
>
> My whimsical uncle makes a new plan every day.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**તરંગી** (*tarangī*) is built on *taraṅg*, "a wave", from Sanskrit *taraṃga*, "a wave, a billow" (Monier-Williams), with *-ī*: a mind that rises and falls like waves.
