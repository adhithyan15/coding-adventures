## You'll want to know: vidēlicet
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C366-PROINDE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**vidēlicet** — "evidently, clearly; namely".

> Domum rediit, vidēlicet fessus.
>
> *He went home, tired, evidently.*

*Vidēlicet* = *vidēre licet*, one may see. English *viz.* is its abbreviation.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*vidēlicet* is contracted from *vidēre licet* "one may see" (Lewis & Short). English *videlicet* and its short form *viz.* were borrowed from it (EtymDB).
