## You'll want to know: домкрат
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C409-POLOMKA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**домкрат** (*domkrát*) — "a jack (for a car)".

> Без домкрата колесо не поменять.
>
> *Bez domkráta kolesó ne pomenyát.*
>
> You cannot change a wheel without a jack.

Masculine; the stress is on the last syllable.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*домкрат* was borrowed from Dutch *dommekracht* (EtymDB), a word usually explained as *dom* 'dumb' and *kracht* 'strength': a tool that lifts by plain force.
