## You'll want to know: पाबंद
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C543-VACHAN543-05]; assesses=[] -->

**पाबंद** — *pāband* — "bound (by a rule or a time)".

> वह समय का बहुत पाबंद है।
>
> *Vah samay kā bahut pāband hai.*
>
> He always keeps to time.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पाबंद** (*pāband*) is usually traced to Persian *pāband*, "with feet bound, fettered".
