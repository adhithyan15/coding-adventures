## You'll want to know: प्रतिनिधी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C355-MATDAN355-02]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रतिनिधी** — *pratinidhī* — "a representative; a delegate".

> आमच्या भागाचा प्रतिनिधी लोकांचे प्रश्न मांडतो.
>
> *āmacyā bhāgācā pratinidhī lokāñce praśna māṇḍto.*
>
> Our area's representative puts forward people's problems.

Masculine.

Used for a woman too: ती आमची प्रतिनिधी आहे; लोकप्रतिनिधी, an elected representative.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रतिनिधी** (*pratinidhī*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *pratinidhi*, "a substitute, a representative" (Monier-Williams). Marathi lengthens the final vowel.
