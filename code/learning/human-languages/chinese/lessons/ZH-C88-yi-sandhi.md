## Sounds you'll need — 一 moves
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-RULE-TONE-SANDHI-YI-01]; assesses=[ZH-TONE-SANDHI-YI, ZH-LEX-GE-01] -->

一 has three spoken tones, and the next syllable picks one.

**Counting, and inside numbers and dates, it stays level**: *yī, èr, sān*; **星期一** *xīngqīyī*.

**Before a fourth tone it rises**: **一个** *yí ge*. 个 is fourth tone, even when it is said light.

**Before the other three tones it falls**: **一天** *yì tiān*, **一年** *yì nián*, **一百** *yìbǎi*.

This book writes the tone you say, as it writes *bú shì* for 不是.
