## You'll want to know: откладывать
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C275-ZATYAGIVATSYA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**откладывать** (*otkládyvat*) — "to put off, to postpone; to save up".

> Они откладывали поездку три раза.
>
> *Oní otkládyvali poyézdku tri ráza.*
>
> They put off the trip three times.

Imperfective; perfective *отложить* (*otlozhít*). Also "to put money aside": *откладывать деньги*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*откладывать* is built on *от-* "away" and the stem *клад-* of *класть* "to put", an inherited Slavic verb (Proto-Slavic *klasti*): to put to one side. EtymDB lists no parent for the compound.
