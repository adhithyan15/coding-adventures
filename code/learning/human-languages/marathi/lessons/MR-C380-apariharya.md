## You'll want to know: अपरिहार्य
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C380-ANIVARYA380-03]; assesses=[] -->

**अपरिहार्य** — *aparihārya* — "unavoidable".

> पावसामुळे थोडा उशीर अपरिहार्य होता.
>
> *pāvsāmuḷe thoḍā uśīr aparihārya hotā.*
>
> Because of the rain, a little delay was unavoidable.

Invariable; अ + परिहार्य, not avoidable.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अपरिहार्य** (*aparihārya*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *aparihārya*, "not to be avoided" (Monier-Williams).
