## You'll want to know: le dédommagement
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C433-DEDOMMAGEMENT-04]; assesses=[] -->

**le dédommagement** — "the compensation".

> L'hôtel nous a offert un repas en dédommagement.
>
> *The hotel offered us a meal by way of compensation.*

*En dédommagement* — as compensation.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*dédommagement* is built on *dédommager* with *-ment* (EtymDB). *Dédommager* comes from *dommage* 'harm', from Latin *damnum* 'loss' (Lewis & Short).
