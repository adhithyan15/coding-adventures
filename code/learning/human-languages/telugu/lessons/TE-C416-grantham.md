## You'll want to know: గ్రంథం
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C416-VYASAM416-03]; assesses=[] -->

**గ్రంథం** — *granthaṁ* — "a scholarly book; a treatise".

> ఆ గ్రంథం వంద సంవత్సరాల కిందట రాశారు.
>
> *ā granthaṁ vanda saṁvatsarāla kindaṭa rāśāru.*
>
> That treatise was written a hundred years ago.

Plural: గ్రంథాలు, *granthālu*.

గ్రంథాలయం, a library, which you know, is built on it.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**గ్రంథం** (*granthaṁ*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *grantha*, "a knot", and later "a book" (CDIAL 4350), from *granth*, "to tie, to string together".
