## You'll want to know: అంతర్జాలం
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C459-PHALAHARAM459-04]; assesses=[] -->

**అంతర్జాలం** — *antarjālaṁ* — "the web; the internet".

> ఆ సత్రంలో అంతర్జాలం లేదు.
>
> *ā satraṁlō antarjālaṁ lēdu.*
>
> There is no internet at that inn.

A formal word made for the internet; in speech people mostly say ఇంటర్నెట్.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**అంతర్జాలం** (*antarjālaṁ*) is a modern Telugu coinage built from two Sanskrit words: *antar*, "between, inside", and *jāla*, "a net" (CDIAL 5213).
