## You'll want to know: なくす
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C153-OUTING153-04]; assesses=[] -->

**なくす** — *nakusu* — "to lose; to mislay".

> **いもうとは よく かさを なくします。** — *imōto wa yoku kasa o nakushimasu* — "My younger sister often loses her umbrella."

Polite **なくします**, *nakushimasu*; a godan verb.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**なくす** (*nakusu*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, and its older origin is not settled. It is usually written in kana; its kanji is a later kun'yomi (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
