Italian, chapter 299: Lo scontro.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
lo scontro — the crash, the collision; the clash.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for the attack, then the Italian for to threaten.

You'll want to know: lo scontro.
lo scontro — "the crash, the collision; the clash".
Sull'autostrada c'è stato uno scontro tra due camion. There was a crash between two lorries on the motorway.
Also a clash of ideas or of teams.

The word, taken apart.
scontro is built on scontrare, a form of incontrare "to meet", from Latin in contrā "against" (EtymDB).

Lesson 4 of 5.
ammaccare — to dent, to bruise.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for to collide, then the Italian for to crash into something.

You'll want to know: ammaccare.
ammaccare — "to dent, to bruise".
Ho ammaccato la macchina nel parcheggio. I dented the car in the car park.
Ammaccato — dented; for fruit, bruised.

The word, taken apart.
ammaccare is traced in EtymDB to Latin maccāre "to bruise, to crush", a word whose own origin is not settled.

Il vento ha danneggiato il tetto. The wind damaged the roof.
Present danneggio, danneggi, danneggia.

The word, taken apart.
danneggiare is built on danno "damage", which is inherited from Latin damnum "loss, harm" (Lewis & Short).
