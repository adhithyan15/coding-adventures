## You'll want to know: جهت
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C446-JAHAT-01]; assesses=[] -->

**جهت** (*jahat*) — "a direction; a side".

> ما در جهت اشتباه رانندگی میکردیم.
>
> *Mâ dar jahat-e eshtebâh rânandegi mikardim.*
>
> We were driving in the wrong direction.

Arabic plural *jahât*. *Be in jahat* — for this reason.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*jahat* was borrowed from Arabic and is usually traced to Arabic *jiha* 'side, direction'. EtymDB does not list it.
