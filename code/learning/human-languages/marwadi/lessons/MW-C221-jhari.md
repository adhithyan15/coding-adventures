## You'll want to know: झड़ी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C221-RAIN221-05]; assesses=[] -->

**झड़ी** — *jhaṛī* — "a spell of steady rain".

> सावण मांय झड़ी लागै है।
>
> *sāvaṇ mā̃y jhaṛī lāgai hai.*
>
> In the monsoon month the rain sets in for days.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**झड़ी** (*jhaṛī*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*jhaḍī*, "a rainstorm"; CDIAL lists Marwari *jhaṛī*, "continuous heavy rain", and points to a Dravidian word for fine steady rain (CDIAL 5329).
