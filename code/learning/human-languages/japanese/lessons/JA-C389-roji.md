## You'll want to know: ろじ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C389-OODOORI389-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ろじ** — *roji* — "an alley; a lane".

> **ろじに ちいさい みせが あります。** — *roji ni chiisai mise ga arimasu* — "There is a small shop in the alley."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ろじ** (*roji*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *ro*, "path" (Middle Chinese *luH*) and *ji*, "ground" (*dijH*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
