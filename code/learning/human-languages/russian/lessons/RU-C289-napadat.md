## You'll want to know: нападать
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C289-OGRABLENIYE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**нападать** (*napadát*) — "to attack".

> Собака напала на почтальона.
>
> *Sobáka napála na pochtalóna.*
>
> The dog attacked the postman.

Imperfective; perfective *напасть* (*napást*), past *напал*. It takes *на* + accusative.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*нападать* is built on *на-* "on" and *падать* "to fall", which is inherited from Proto-Slavic *padati* (EtymDB): to fall upon.
