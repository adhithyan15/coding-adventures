## You'll want to know: ویران شدن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C331-VIRAN-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ویران شدن** (*virân shodan*) — "to be destroyed, to be ruined".

> در زلزله صدها خانه ویران شد.
>
> *Dar zelzele sad-hâ khâne virân shod.*
>
> Hundreds of houses were destroyed in the earthquake.

Present *virân mishavad*. *Virân* "ruined" is also an adjective.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*virân* "desolate, ruined" is a native Persian word that EtymDB lists without a parent; *shodan* "to become" is inherited Persian.
