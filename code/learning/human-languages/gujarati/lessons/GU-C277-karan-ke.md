# કારણ કે, -ને લીધે (kāraṇ ke, -ne līdhe) — because, because of

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-LEX-C277-KARAN-01, GU-JOIN-KEMKE-01, GU-LEX-C147-PAST147-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "a reason". (*kāraṇ*.) Then "late". (**મોડું**.)

## Grammar Lens: કારણ કે, and a clause
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-GRAMMAR-C277-KARAN-KE-01]; assesses=[GU-LEX-C85-TRAVEL-02, GU-LEX-AAVVU, GU-CONCEPT-C03-HUN-01] -->

> **હું મોડો આવ્યો, કારણ કે ટ્રેન મોડી હતી.**
>
> *hũ moḍo āvyo, kāraṇ ke ṭren moḍī hatī.* — "I came late, because the train was late."

**કારણ કે** (*kāraṇ ke*) works like **કેમકે**: what happened first, then the reason, a whole clause with its own verb. **મોડો** follows the man who came, **મોડી** the train.

## You'll want to know: -ને લીધે, and a noun
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-GRAMMAR-C277-NE-LIDHE-01]; assesses=[GU-LEX-VARSAAD-01, GU-LEX-C199-QUEUE199-01, GU-CONCEPT-C05-RAHEVUN-01] -->

> **વરસાદને લીધે અમે ઘરે રહ્યા.**
>
> *varsādne līdhe ame ghare rahyā.* — "Because of the rain, we stayed at home."

When the reason is a single thing, a noun, it takes **-ને લીધે** (*-ne līdhe*), "because of", or **-ને કારણે** (*-ne kāraṇe*), the same thing with the noun you have:

| the reason is | say |
|---|---|
| a clause: the train was late | **…, કારણ કે ટ્રેન મોડી હતી** |
| a noun: the rain | **વરસાદને લીધે …** |
| a noun: the crowd | **ભીડને કારણે …** |
