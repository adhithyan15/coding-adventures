# رأي (raʾy) — an opinion

## Warm-up

qaraʾtu, thumma katabtu*.) Then "I told the story when he came". (*Ḥakaytu
al-qiṣṣa ʿindamā jāʾa*.)

[PAUSE 1s] A story says what happened. The next node asks what you **think**

## You'll want to know: رأي
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C285-RAY285-01]; assesses=[AR-LEX-C184-SHAY184-05, AR-LEX-C146-QUAL146-01] -->

**رأي** — *raʾy* — "an opinion, a view".

> **ما رأيك في الفيلم؟** — *mā raʾyuka fī al-fīlm?* — What do you think of
> the film?

> **في رأيي، الفيلم مفيد.** — *fī raʾyī, al-fīlm mufīd* — In my opinion,
> the film is useful.

*Raʾyī* is "my opinion", with the *-ī* of "my". *Raʾyuka* is "your opinion"
to a man; to a woman it is *raʾyuki*. *Fī*, "in", says what the opinion is
about.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[AR-CONCEPT-C29-RAA-01] -->

**رأي** (*raʾy*) is built on the native Arabic root *r-a-y*, "to see"
(Lane): the root of *raʾā*, "he saw", from chapter twenty-nine. Lane gives
*raʾā fī al-amr raʾyan* for forming a view of a matter. An opinion, in
Arabic, is what you see in something.
