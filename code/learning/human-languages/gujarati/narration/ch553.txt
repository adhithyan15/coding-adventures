5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
નગારું (nagārũ) — a kettledrum.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to make ring, then the Gujarati for to get going.

You'll want to know: નગારું.
નગારું — nagārũ — "a kettledrum".
જો તહેવાર હશે તો અરુણ મંદિરમાં નગારું વગાડશે. jo tahevār haśe to Aruṇ mandirmā̃ nagārũ vagāḍśe. If there is a festival, Arun will play the kettledrum at the temple.
A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
નગારું (nagārũ) is usually traced to Arabic naqqāra, "a kettledrum", through Persian.

Lesson 3 of 5.
લલકારવું (lalkārvũ) — to sing out.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a kettledrum, then the Gujarati for a shehnai.

You'll want to know: લલકારવું.
લલકારવું — lalkārvũ — "to sing out".
જો મીરા ગીત લલકારશે તો બધા સાથે ગાશે. jo Mīrā gīt lalkārśe to badhā sāthe gāśe. If Mira sings out a song, everyone will sing along.
Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે ગીત લલકાર્યું, teṇe gīt lalkāryũ.

The word, taken apart.
લલકારવું (lalkārvũ) is built on lalkār, "a challenge, a shout", which CDIAL files under lallakka (CDIAL 10973): to sing out loud and clear.

Lesson 4 of 5.
રણકવું (raṇakvũ) — to chime.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a shehnai, then the Gujarati for to sing out.

You'll want to know: રણકવું.
રણકવું — raṇakvũ — "to chime".
જો ઘંટડી રણકશે તો તરત બારણું ખોલો. jo ghanṭaḍī raṇakśe to tarat bārṇũ kholo. If the bell rings, open the door at once.
Past (it …): તે ઘંટડી રણકી, te ghanṭaḍī raṇkī.

The word, taken apart.
રણકવું (raṇakvũ) is imitative, the ring of a bell or of coins in the word itself. CDIAL lists Gujarati raṇko, "a tinkling", and raṇraṇvũ, "to tinkle", under Sanskrit raṇa, "noise" (CDIAL 10596); the verb itself is not listed there.

Lesson 5 of 5.
થનગનવું (thanganvũ) — to be itching to dance.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to sing out, then the Gujarati for to chime.

You'll want to know: થનગનવું.
થનગનવું — thanganvũ — "to be itching to dance".
જો ગરબાનું સંગીત શરૂ થશે તો બાળકો થનગનશે. jo garbānũ sangīt śarū thaśe to bāḷko thanganśe. If the garba music starts, the children will be itching to dance.
Past (he …): તે થનગન્યો, te thanganyo.

The word, taken apart.
થનગનવું (thanganvũ) is imitative, the tapping of eager feet. No dictionary we use records an older form, so its history is not settled.
