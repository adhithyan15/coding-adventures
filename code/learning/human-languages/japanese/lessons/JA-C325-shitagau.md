## You'll want to know: したがう
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C325-SHITAGAU325-01]; assesses=[] -->

**したがう** — *shitagau* — "to obey; to follow".

> **せんせいの いけんに したがった ほうが いいです。** — *sensei no iken ni shitagatta hō ga ii desu* — "You should follow the teacher's advice."

Polite **したがいます**, *shitagaimasu*; a godan verb. Past (polite): **がくせいは したがいました。**, *gakusei wa shitagaimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**したがう** (*shitagau*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, and its older origin is not settled. Its kanji was attached to it later as a kun'yomi, a native reading given to a Chinese character (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
