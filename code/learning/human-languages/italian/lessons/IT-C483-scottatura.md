## You'll want to know: la scottatura
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C483-FERITE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**la scottatura** — "burn; sunburn".

> Sono stato al sole troppo a lungo e ora ho una scottatura.
>
> *I stayed in the sun too long and now I have sunburn.*

From *scottarsi* — to get burnt.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*scottatura* is built on *scottare* "to scald", usually traced to Latin *excoctāre*, from *coquere* "to cook" (Lewis & Short).
