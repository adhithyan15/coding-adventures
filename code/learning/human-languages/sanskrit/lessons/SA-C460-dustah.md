## You'll want to know: दुष्टः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C460-SVABHAVA460-05]; assesses=[] -->

**दुष्टः** — *duṣṭaḥ* — "wicked; corrupt".

> दुष्टः बालः शुनकं ताडयति।
>
> *duṣṭaḥ bālaḥ śunakaṃ tāḍayati.*
>
> The wicked boy beats the dog.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**दुष्टः** (*duṣṭaḥ*) is built on the root √duṣ, "to become bad, to spoil": spoilt; MW gives "spoilt, corrupted" and "bad, wicked" (MW).
