## You'll want to know: основывать
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C326-PREDOK-05]; assesses=[] -->

**основывать** (*osnóvyvat*) — "to found".

> Город основали в двенадцатом веке.
>
> *Górod osnováli v dvenádtsatom véke.*
>
> The town was founded in the twelfth century.

Imperfective; perfective *основать* (*osnovát*). *Основатель* — a founder.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*основывать* is built on *о-* and *основа* "a base", inherited from Proto-Slavic *osnova* (EtymDB), first the warp threads on a loom, a cousin of *сновать* "to warp, to dart to and fro".
