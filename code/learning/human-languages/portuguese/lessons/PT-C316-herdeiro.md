## You'll want to know: o herdeiro
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C316-HERANCA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**o herdeiro** — "the heir".

> O filho mais velho é o único herdeiro.
>
> *The eldest son is the only heir.*

A woman is *a herdeira*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*herdeiro* comes down from Old Portuguese *erdeiro* (EtymDB) and goes back to Latin *hērēditārius*, built on *hērēs* "an heir" (Lewis & Short).
