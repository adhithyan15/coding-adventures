## You'll want to know: féizào
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C105-BATH105-04]; assesses=[] -->

*féizào* — "soap".

> *yòng féizào xǐ shǒu* — Wash your hands with soap.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*féizào* is built from féi 'fat' and zào, both inherited from Middle Chinese, *bj+j* and *dzawX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription). The dictionary glosses the first 'fleshy', and the second as black silk, among other senses; how the pair came to mean soap is not settled.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for everyday things.
