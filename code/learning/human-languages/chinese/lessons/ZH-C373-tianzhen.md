## You'll want to know: 天真
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C373-PROSCONS373-04]; assesses=[] -->

**天真** — *tiānzhēn* — "naive; innocent".

> **虽然你说得有理但是你太天真了** *suīrán nǐ shuō de yǒulǐ dànshì nǐ tài tiānzhēn le* — You may have a point, but you're too naive.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**天真** (*tiānzhēn*) is built from 天 'the sky' and 真 'true', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *then* and *tsyin* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'the dark heaven above' and the second 'true, as against false'. How the pair came to mean naive is not settled.
