Spanish, chapter 569: La lucha.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
luchar — to fight, to struggle.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for the queen, then the Spanish for to conquer.

You'll want to know: luchar.
luchar — "to fight, to struggle".
Lucharon por sus derechos. They fought for their rights.
Luchar por — to fight for. Luchar contra — to fight against.

The word, taken apart.
luchar is inherited from Latin luctārī 'to wrestle', through spoken Latin luctāre (EtymDB).

What you've built.
One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.

Lesson 2 of 5.

You'll want to know: derrotar.
derrotar — "to defeat".
Nuestro club derrotó al favorito. Our club beat the favourite.
Derrotar a alguien — to defeat someone.

The word, taken apart.
derrotar is built on derrota (EtymDB), which goes back to Latin dīrumpere 'to break apart', from rumpere 'to break'.

Lesson 3 of 5.
vencer — to beat, to win; to expire.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for to fight, then the Spanish for to defeat.

You'll want to know: vencer.
vencer — "to beat, to win; to expire".
Vencimos por dos a uno. We won two-one.
It takes z: venzo. On a document it means to expire: El plazo vence el lunes.

The word, taken apart.
vencer is inherited from Latin vincere 'to conquer' (EtymDB), through old Spanish vencer.

You'll want to know: defender.
defender — "to defend".
Defendió a su hermano en el colegio. He stood up for his brother at school.
Stem change: defiendo. Defenderse — to defend yourself; also to get by.

defender is inherited from Latin dēfendere 'to ward off, to defend' (EtymDB).
