# ನಂದಿಸು (nandisu) — to extinguish; to put out

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[KA-LEX-C324-BENKI324-02, KA-LEX-C324-BENKI324-03] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Kannada for to catch fire, then the Kannada for presence of mind.

## You'll want to know: ನಂದಿಸು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C324-BENKI324-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ನಂದಿಸು** — *nandisu* — "to extinguish; to put out".

> ಅಗ್ನಿಶಾಮಕರು ಒಂದು ಗಂಟೆಯಲ್ಲಿ ಬೆಂಕಿಯನ್ನು ನಂದಿಸಿದರು.
>
> *agniśāmakaru ondu gaṇṭeyalli beṅkiyannu nandisidaru.*
>
> The firefighters put out the fire in an hour.

Past (I …): ನಂದಿಸಿದೆ, *nandiside*.

Fire and a lamp are put out: ದೀಪವನ್ನು ನಂದಿಸು. A fire going out by itself is ನಂದು.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ನಂದಿಸು** (*nandisu*) is native Dravidian: the causative in -*isu* of *nandu*, "to be quenched, to go out", which DEDR 3590 lists for Kannada.
