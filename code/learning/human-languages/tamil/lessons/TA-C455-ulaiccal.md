# உளைச்சல் (uḷaiccal) — anguish; mental strain

## You'll want to know: உளைச்சல்
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C455-KAVALAI455-02]; assesses=[] -->

**உளைச்சல்** — *uḷaiccal* — "anguish; mental strain".

> வேலையை இழந்த பிறகு அவருக்கு மன உளைச்சல் ஏற்பட்டது.
>
> *vēlaiyai iḻanta piṟaku avarukku maṉa uḷaiccal ēṟpaṭṭatu.*
>
> After losing his job he suffered mental anguish.

Mostly in மன உளைச்சல், mental anguish, with மனம் "mind".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**உளைச்சல்** (*uḷaiccal*) is native Dravidian, built on உளை *uḷai*, "to ache, to suffer pain", which DEDR 2702 lists with a query.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
