# ಹಂಬಲಿಸು (hambalisu) — to long for; to yearn

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[KA-LEX-C495-MAMATE495-04, KA-LEX-C495-MAMATE495-05] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Kannada for motherly love, then the Kannada for care.

## You'll want to know: ಹಂಬಲಿಸು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C496-ASHE496-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ಹಂಬಲಿಸು** — *hambalisu* — "to long for; to yearn".

> ವಿದೇಶದಲ್ಲಿದ್ದ ಮಗ ಅಮ್ಮನ ಅಡುಗೆಗಾಗಿ ಹಂಬಲಿಸುತ್ತಿದ್ದನು.
>
> *vidēśadallidda maga ammana aḍugegāgi hambalisuttiddanu.*
>
> The son living abroad used to long for his mother's cooking.

Past (I …): ಹಂಬಲಿಸಿದೆ, *hambaliside*.

What you long for takes -ಗಾಗಿ. The noun is ಹಂಬಲ, a longing.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಹಂಬಲಿಸು** (*hambalisu*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 3931 lists Kannada *pambal*, "ardent desire", and *pambalisu*, "to desire ardently". Old Kannada p- became h- in the modern language.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
