German, chapter 63: Neighbour, Guest, Grandson, Pupil, Pupil.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
der Nachbar — a neighbour.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for an uncle, then the German for an aunt.

You'll want to know: der Nachbar.
der Nachbar — "a neighbour".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: der Nachbar]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: der Nachbar, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say der Nachbar]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the German for an uncle, then the German for an aunt, then say der Nachbar again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does der Nachbar mean? ("A neighbour".) Say it once more.
