## You'll want to know: speciōsus, speciōsa, speciōsum
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C548-VENU-02]; assesses=[] -->

**speciōsus, speciōsa, speciōsum** — "good-looking, handsome".

> Iuvenis speciōsus est, sed superbus.
>
> *The young man is good-looking, but proud.*

From *speciēs* — appearance. Later also showy but false: English *specious*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**speciōsus** is built on *speciēs* "appearance, look", from *speciō* "to behold" (Lewis & Short). English *specious* comes from it, with the later sense "fair on the surface".
