## You'll want to know: খুব
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C370-KHUB370-01]; assesses=[] -->

**খুব** — *khub* — "very".

> তুমি এলে আমি খুব খুশি হব।
>
> *tumi ele āmi khub khushi hôbô.*
>
> I'll be very happy if you come.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**খুব** (*khub*) is usually traced to Persian *khūb*, good or fine. In Bengali it became the plain word for 'very'.
