## You'll want to know: మూసివేత
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C469-SAMME469-02]; assesses=[] -->

**మూసివేత** — *mūsivēta* — "a closure; a shutdown".

> వంతెన మూసివేత వల్ల చుట్టూ తిరిగి వెళ్ళాం.
>
> *vantena mūsivēta valla cuṭṭū tirigi veḷḷāṁ.*
>
> Because the bridge was closed we went the long way round.

మూసి, having shut, + -వేత, a putting: a closing that lasts.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**మూసివేత** (*mūsivēta*) is native Dravidian: మూసి, from మూయు, "to cover, to shut" (DEDR 4915), and -వేత, the noun of వేయు, "to put" (DEDR 5549).
