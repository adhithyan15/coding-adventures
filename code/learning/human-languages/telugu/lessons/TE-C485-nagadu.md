## You'll want to know: నగదు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C485-NAGADU485-02]; assesses=[] -->

**నగదు** — *nagadu* — "cash".

> ఆ దుకాణంలో నగదు మాత్రమే తీసుకుంటారు.
>
> *ā dukāṇaṁlō nagadu mātramē tīsukuṇṭāru.*
>
> That shop takes cash only.

నగదు రూపంలో, in cash.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**నగదు** (*nagadu*) is a loan from Arabic *naqd*, "ready money, cash", which came into Telugu through Persian and Hindustani.
