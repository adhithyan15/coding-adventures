## You'll want to know: bestrafen
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C385-AUSNAHME-02]; assesses=[] -->

**bestrafen** — "to punish".

> Der Schüler wurde für die Lüge bestraft.
>
> *The pupil was punished for the lie.*

Inseparable: *er bestraft, er hat bestraft*. *Die Strafe* — the punishment, also a fine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*bestrafen* is built with *be-* on *strafen* "to punish", inherited from Middle High German *strāfen* (EtymDB); EtymDB lists Dutch *straffen* as a cousin.
