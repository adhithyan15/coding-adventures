## You'll want to know: ढलना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C385-BIKHAR385-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ढलना** — *ḍhalnā* — "to set (of the sun), to wane".

> अगर सूरज ढल जाएगा तो रास्ता नहीं दिखेगा।
>
> *agar sūraj ḍhal jāegā to rāstā nahīṃ dikhegā.*
>
> If the sun sets, we won't be able to see the path.

Past (it …): वह सूरज ढल गया, *vah sūraj ḍhal gayā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ढलना** (*ḍhalnā*) is inherited from *\*ḍhalati*, "bends over, falls", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 5581).
