## You'll want to know: تبرئه شدن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C322-MAHKUM-02]; assesses=[] -->

**تبرئه شدن** (*tabra'e shodan*) — "to be acquitted, to be cleared".

> او بعد از یک سال تبرئه شد.
>
> *U ba'd az yek sâl tabra'e shod.*
>
> She was cleared after a year.

Written with the hamza seat ئ. Present *tabra'e mishavad*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*tabra'e* is usually traced to Arabic *tabri'a*, "a clearing of guilt"; EtymDB lists no entry. *shodan* "to become" is inherited Persian.
