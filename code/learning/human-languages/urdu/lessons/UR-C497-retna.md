## You'll want to know: ریتنا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C497-TAKHTA497-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ریتنا** — *retnā* — "to file (metal or wood)".

> مستری نے چابی کے دانت ریتے۔
>
> *mistrī ne chābī ke dānt rete.*
>
> The mechanic filed down the teeth of the key.

Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے کیل ریتی, *us ne kīl retī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ریتنا** (*retnā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousins *retī*, "sand, a file", and *retnā*, "to file", under Sanskrit *rētra* (CDIAL 10816).
