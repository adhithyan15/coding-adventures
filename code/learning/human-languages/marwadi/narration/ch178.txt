Lesson 1 of 6.
ऊंघणो (ū̃ghṇo) — to doze.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for a patch, then the Marwadi for the end of a sari.

You'll want to know: ऊंघणो.
ऊंघणो — ū̃ghṇo — "to doze".
दादा कुरसी माथै ऊंघै है। dādā kursī māthai ū̃ghai hai. Grandfather dozes in the chair.
Past (he …): वो कुरसी माथै ऊंघ्यो, vo kursī māthai ū̃ghyo.

The word, taken apart.
ऊंघणो (ū̃ghṇo) comes from a reconstructed uṅghati, "sleeps"; CDIAL lists Hindi ū̃ghnā, "to be drowsy", and Gujarati ũghvũ (CDIAL 1632). CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry.

Warm-up.

Before the new one: say the Marwadi for to doze, then the Marwadi for to peep.

You'll want to know: बीणणो.
बीणणो — bīṇṇo — "to pick over (grain)".
दादी बाजरो बीणै है। dādī bājro bīṇai hai. Grandmother picks over the millet.
Past (I …): म्हैं बाजरो बीण्यो, mhaĩ bājro bīṇyo.

The word, taken apart.
बीणणो (bīṇṇo) is inherited from Sanskrit vicinoti, "picks out, chooses"; CDIAL lists Hindi bīnnā, "to pick up, choose" (CDIAL 11686). CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry.

You'll want to know: सूंपणो.
सूंपणो — sū̃pṇo — "to hand over".
चाबी सूंपो। chābī sū̃po. Hand over the key, please.
Past (I …): म्हैं चाबी सूंपी, mhaĩ chābī sū̃pī.

The word, taken apart.
सूंपणो (sū̃pṇo) is inherited from Sanskrit samarpayati, "delivers over"; CDIAL lists Old Marwari sopaï, "hands over" (CDIAL 13192).
