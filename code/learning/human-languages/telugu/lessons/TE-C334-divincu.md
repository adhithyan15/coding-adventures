# దీవించు (dīviñcu) — to bless

## You'll want to know: దీవించు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C334-JANANAM334-05]; assesses=[] -->

**దీవించు** — *dīviñcu* — "to bless".

> అమ్మమ్మ పసికందును దీవించింది.
>
> *ammamma pasikandunu dīviñcindi.*
>
> Grandmother blessed the newborn.

Past (I …): దీవించాను, *dīviñcānu*.

Its noun is దీవెన, a blessing: elders bless, the young bow.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**దీవించు** (*dīviñcu*) is a Telugu verb whose origin is not settled; DEDR has no entry for it.
