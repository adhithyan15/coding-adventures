# समर्घः (samarghaḥ) — low-priced

## You'll want to know: समर्घः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C431-ARGHA431-03]; assesses=[] -->

**समर्घः** — *samarghaḥ* — "low-priced".

> हट्टे शाकं समर्घम् अस्ति।
>
> *haṭṭe śākaṃ samargham asti.*
>
> Vegetables are low-priced at the bazaar.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**समर्घः** (*samarghaḥ*) is built from *sam* and *argha*, "price"; MW gives *samargha* as cheap (MW).
