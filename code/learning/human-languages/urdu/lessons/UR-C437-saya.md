## You'll want to know: سایہ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C437-JHARI437-02]; assesses=[] -->

**سایہ** — *sāyā* — "shade; a shadow".

> گرمی میں ہم درخت کے سائے میں بیٹھے۔
>
> *garmī meṅ ham darakht ke sāe meṅ baiṭhe.*
>
> In the heat we sat in the shade of a tree.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**سایہ** (*sāyā*) is usually traced to Persian *sāya*, "shade, a shadow".
