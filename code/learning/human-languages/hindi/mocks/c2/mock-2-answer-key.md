## Writing

The synthesis must distinguish the analytics unit's substantial but uneven use,
cost, connection, and measurement findings from the coalition's chain-of-journey,
workplace-power, safety, care, and labour concerns and the essay's account of the
night-time public realm, categorisation, testimony, and spare capacity. It should
state what the trial cannot causally or universally establish and reframe the
evidence for fair public discussion rather than a verdict. The curatorial essay
must develop and qualify the copy/original claim across two practices, distinguish
material authenticity, technique, use, authorship and authority, address credit,
consent, extraction, visible alteration, and unequal accountability, and end with
a workable museum rule. Score fidelity, synthesis, reframing, conceptual control,
genre, organisation, register, Hindi control, and orthography; no fixed wording
or position is required.

## Speaking

The presentation must distinguish source evidence from recommendation and mediate
inherited consent, uneven transcription, identification risk, research and teaching
value, tiered access, binding but internally plural community governance, family
and collective claims, correction, revenue and benefit, and specific synthetic-
voice risk. A viable first-year policy identifies access tiers, decision owners,
review, withdrawal and dissent paths, and promises the laboratory can keep.
Negotiation must address accessible first-phase scope, temporary service, archive
conditions, quiet and youth uses, donor limits, scheduling, owners, and a dated
stop/review rule without relegating one group indefinitely. Defence must respond
to all selected challenges, identify evidence capable of changing a tier, and
reformulate faithfully for each named audience. Score only the learner's own
contribution; no fixed policy is required.
