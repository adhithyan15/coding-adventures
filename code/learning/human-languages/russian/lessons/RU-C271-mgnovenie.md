## You'll want to know: мгновение
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C271-VNEZAPNO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**мгновение** (*mgnovéniye*) — "an instant, a moment".

> На одно мгновение все замолчали.
>
> *Na odnó mgnovéniye vse zamolcháli.*
>
> For an instant everyone fell silent.

A neuter noun. *В одно мгновение* — in an instant; *в последнее мгновение* — at the last moment.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*мгновение* is inherited through Old East Slavic *mĭgnovenije* from Proto-Slavic *mĭgnovenĭje*, built on *mĭgnǫti* "to blink", a cousin of *мигать* "to blink" (EtymDB).
