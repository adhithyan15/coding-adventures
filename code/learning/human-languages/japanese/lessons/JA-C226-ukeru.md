## You'll want to know: うける
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C226-EXAM226-04]; assesses=[] -->

**うける** — *ukeru* — "to take an exam; to receive".

> **あには らいねん しけんを うけます。** — *ani wa rainen shiken o ukemasu* — "My elder brother takes an exam next year."

Polite **うけます**, *ukemasu*; an ichidan verb.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**うける** (*ukeru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
