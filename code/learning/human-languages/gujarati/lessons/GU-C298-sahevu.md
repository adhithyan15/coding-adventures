## You'll want to know: સહેવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C298-MRUTYU298-05]; assesses=[] -->

**સહેવું** — *sahevũ* — "to bear; to endure".

> માતાએ મોટું દુઃખ સહ્યું.
>
> *mātāe moṭũ duḥkh sahyũ.*
>
> The mother bore a great sorrow.

Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે દુઃખ સહ્યું, *teṇe duḥkh sahyũ*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**સહેવું** (*sahevũ*) is inherited from Sanskrit *sáhatē*, "conquers; bears", and CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb, "bears" (CDIAL 13304). Its past is **સહ્યું**, as **કહેવું** gives **કહ્યું**.
