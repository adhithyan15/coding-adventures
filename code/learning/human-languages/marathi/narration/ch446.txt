Marathi, chapter 446: Wildlife Sanctuaries.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
वन्यजीव (vanyajīv) — wildlife.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to blossom, then the Marathi for to wither.

You'll want to know: वन्यजीव.
वन्यजीव — vanyajīv — "wildlife".
या जंगलात खूप वन्यजीव आहेत. yā jaṅgalāt khūp vanyajīv āhet. There is a lot of wildlife in this forest.
Masculine.
A formal word for the wild animals and birds of a region.

The word, taken apart.
वन्यजीव (vanyajīv) is a modern word built from Sanskrit vanya, "growing or living in a forest", and jīva, "a living being" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 2 of 5.
अभयारण्य (abhayāraṇya) — a sanctuary.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to wither, then the Marathi for wildlife.

You'll want to know: अभयारण्य.
अभयारण्य — abhayāraṇya — "a sanctuary".
अभयारण्यात शिकार करायला बंदी आहे. abhayāraṇyāt śikār karāylā bandī āhe. Hunting is banned in the sanctuary.
Neuter.
अभय, without fear, and अरण्य, a forest.

The word, taken apart.
अभयारण्य (abhayāraṇya) is a modern word built from Sanskrit abhaya, "without fear, safe", and araṇya, "a forest" (Monier-Williams): a forest where animals are safe.

Lesson 4 of 5.
डरकाळी फोडणे (ḍarkāḷī phoḍṇe) — to roar.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a sanctuary, then the Marathi for a leopard.

You'll want to know: डरकाळी फोडणे.
डरकाळी फोडणे — ḍarkāḷī phoḍṇe — "to roar".
जंगलात वाघाने डरकाळी फोडली. jaṅgalāt vāghāne ḍarkāḷī phoḍlī. In the forest the tiger roared.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने डरकाळी फोडली, tyāne ḍarkāḷī phoḍlī.
डरकाळी, a roar, is feminine, so the past is डरकाळी फोडली.

The word, taken apart.
डरकाळी फोडणे (ḍarkāḷī phoḍṇe) joins डरकाळी, "a roar", whose origin is not settled, to फोडणे, "to break open": to let out a roar.

Lesson 5 of 5.
चरणे (carṇe) — to graze.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a leopard, then the Marathi for to roar.

You'll want to know: चरणे.
चरणे — carṇe — "to graze".
डोंगरावर गायी चरत होत्या. ḍoṅgarāvar gāyī carat hotyā. Cows were grazing on the hill.
Past (he …): तो चरला, to carlā.
Animals चरतात; to take them grazing is चरायला नेणे.

The word, taken apart.
चरणे (carṇe) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi ċarṇẽ, "to move about, graze", under Sanskrit carati, "moves, grazes" (CDIAL 4686).
