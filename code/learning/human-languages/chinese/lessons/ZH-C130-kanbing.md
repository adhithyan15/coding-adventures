## You'll want to know: 看病
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C130-ILL130-05]; assesses=[] -->

**看病** — *kànbìng* — "to see a doctor".

> **我病了我去看病** *wǒ bìng le wǒ qù kànbìng* — I'm ill, so I'm off to the doctor.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**看病** (*kànbìng*) is built from 看 'to look at' and 病 'illness', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *khanH* and *bjaengH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription): to have an illness looked at.
