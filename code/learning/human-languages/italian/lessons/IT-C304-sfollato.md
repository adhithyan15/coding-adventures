## You'll want to know: lo sfollato
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C304-TERREMOTO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**lo sfollato** — "the evacuee, the displaced person".

> Gli sfollati dormono nella palestra della scuola.
>
> *The evacuees are sleeping in the school gym.*

From *sfollare*, to empty of people.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*sfollato* is the past participle of *sfollare* "to empty of people", built on *folla* "crowd", which is usually traced to a Vulgar Latin *fullāre* "to tread cloth", built on *fullo* "a fuller" (Lewis & Short).
