Spanish, chapter 633: Ricos y pobres.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
rico — rich; tasty.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for to pay in, then the Spanish for the pension.

You'll want to know: rico.
rico — "rich; tasty".
Es un país rico. It is a rich country.
With ser it means rich; with estar it means tasty: la sopa está muy rica.

The word, taken apart.
rico was borrowed from Gothic reiks 'mighty, powerful', from Germanic rīkijaz (EtymDB); being powerful came to mean having money.
