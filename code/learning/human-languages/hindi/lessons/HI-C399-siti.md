## You'll want to know: सीटी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C399-LAIN399-04]; assesses=[] -->

**सीटी** — *sīṭī* — "a whistle".

> ट्रेन ने लंबी सीटी दी।
>
> *ṭren ne lambī sīṭī dī.*
>
> The train gave a long whistle.

Feminine.

सीटी देना or सीटी बजाना, to blow a whistle.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सीटी** (*sīṭī*) goes back to *\*sīṭṭa*, "whistle", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants and treats as imitative of the sound itself (CDIAL 13427).
