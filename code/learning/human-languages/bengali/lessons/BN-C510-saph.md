## You'll want to know: সাফ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C510-SAPHAI510-03]; assesses=[] -->

**সাফ** — *sāph* — "clean; cleared away".

> ঝড়ের পরে আমরা উঠোনটা সাফ করলাম।
>
> *Jhôṛer pôre āmrā uṭhonṭā sāph kôrlām.*
>
> After the storm we cleared the courtyard.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**সাফ** (*sāph*) is usually traced to Arabic *ṣāf*, "pure, clear", through Persian.
