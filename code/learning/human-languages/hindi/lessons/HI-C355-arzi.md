## You'll want to know: अर्ज़ी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C355-VIBHAG355-02]; assesses=[] -->

**अर्ज़ी** — *arzī* — "an application, a petition".

> मैंने नौकरी के लिए अर्ज़ी दी।
>
> *maiṁne naukrī ke liye arzī dī.*
>
> I applied for the job.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अर्ज़ी** (*arzī*) is usually traced to Persian *ʿarzī*, "a petition". The word is usually said to be built on Arabic *ʿarḍ*, "a presentation".
