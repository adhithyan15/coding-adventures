## You'll want to know: pigro
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C472-DIFETTI-02]; assesses=[] -->

**pigro** — "lazy".

> La domenica sono pigro e resto a letto fino a tardi.
>
> *On Sundays I am lazy and stay in bed until late.*

The noun is *la pigrizia* "laziness".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*pigro* is inherited from Latin *piger* "reluctant, slow" (EtymDB), related to *pigēre* "to feel reluctant" (Lewis & Short).
