## You'll want to know: aposentar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C317-REFORMA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**aposentar** — "to retire (usually aposentar-se)".

> A professora aposentou-se aos sessenta e seis anos.
>
> *The teacher retired at sixty-six.*

Mostly *aposentar-se*, the usual word in Brazil; in Portugal *reformar-se* is more common, though civil servants such as teachers *aposentam-se*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*aposentar* comes down from Old Portuguese *apousentar* "to lodge, to give rest" (EtymDB), built on *pousar* "to rest", from Latin *pausāre* "to stop" (Lewis & Short).
