Urdu, chapter 101: Palace, Park, Cinema, Field, Door.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
محل (mahal) — a palace.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a market, then the Urdu for a police station.

You'll want to know: محل.
محل — mahal — "a palace".

The word, taken apart.
محل (mahal) is a loan from Arabic maḥall, "a place of alighting, a quarter", which reached Urdu through Persian. Its Arabic root is ḥ-l-l (Lane). In Urdu it came to mean "a palace".

Lesson 5 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a cinema, then the Urdu for a field.

You'll want to know: دروازہ.
دروازہ — darvāzā — "a door".

The word, taken apart.
دروازہ (darvāzā) is a loan, usually traced to Persian darvāza, "gate, door". Its first part, Persian dar, "door", goes back to the same ancient root as English door and Sanskrit dvāra.
