Gujarati, chapter 295: Too Fast on the Bend.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
વળાંક (vaḷā̃k) — a bend (in a road).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a slope, then the Gujarati for consciousness.

You'll want to know: વળાંક.
વળાંક — vaḷā̃k — "a bend (in a road)".
વળાંક પર ગાડી ખૂબ ઝડપી હતી. vaḷā̃k par gāḍī khūb jhaḍpī hatī. The car was going very fast on the bend.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
વળાંક (vaḷā̃k) is built on વળવું, to turn, from Sanskrit válati, "turns" (CDIAL 11405), with the ending -āṅk: the place where a road turns. વળાંક પર ધીમે ચલાવો, drive slowly on the bend.

બેદરકાર (bedarkār) — careless.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a bend, then the Gujarati for a brake.

You'll want to know: બેદરકાર.
બેદરકાર — bedarkār — "careless".
બેદરકાર ડ્રાઈવરને લીધે આ અકસ્માત થયો. bedarkār ḍrāīvarne līdhe ā akasmāt thayo. This accident happened because of a careless driver.

The word, taken apart.
બેદરકાર (bedarkār) is usually traced to Persian bē-, "without", and darkār, "needed, wanted": someone who does not care what is needed. બેદરકાર ડ્રાઈવર, a careless driver.

Lesson 4 of 5.
દંડ (danḍ) — a fine (penalty).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a brake, then the Gujarati for careless.

You'll want to know: દંડ.
દંડ — danḍ — "a fine (penalty)".
પોલીસે ડ્રાઈવરને હજાર રૂપિયાનો દંડ કર્યો. polīse ḍrāīvarne hajār rūpiyāno danḍ karyo. The police fined the driver a thousand rupees.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
દંડ (danḍ) is a tatsama, Sanskrit daṇḍa, "a stick, a rod", the rod of punishment; its verb daṇḍáyati means "chastises, fines" (CDIAL 6136). A fine is made with કરવું: દંડ કર્યો.

Lesson 5 of 5.
કચડવું (kacaḍvũ) — to crush; to run over.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for careless, then the Gujarati for a fine.

You'll want to know: કચડવું.
કચડવું — kacaḍvũ — "to crush; to run over".
ગાડીએ રસ્તા પર એક કૂતરાને કચડી નાખ્યો. gāḍīe rastā par ek kūtrāne kacḍī nākhyo. The car ran over a dog on the road.
Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે કીડી કચડી, teṇe kīḍī kacḍī.

The word, taken apart.
કચડવું (kacaḍvũ) probably belongs with kacarvũ, "to press", which CDIAL sets under kacc, "crush, press", a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 2611). A wheel that goes over something કચડી નાખે છે, crushes it.
