# હરખાવું (harkhāvũ) — to rejoice; to be delighted

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-LEX-C272-PROMISE272-05, GU-LEX-C447-HARAKH447-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to hope, then the Gujarati for delight.

## You'll want to know: હરખાવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C447-HARAKH447-02]; assesses=[] -->

**હરખાવું** — *harkhāvũ* — "to rejoice; to be delighted".

> પરિણામ સાંભળીને મીરા હરખાઈ ગઈ.
>
> *pariṇām sā̃bhḷīne Mīrā harkhāī gaī.*
>
> Mira was delighted when she heard the result.

Past (she …): તે હરખાઈ ગઈ, *te harkhāī gaī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**હરખાવું** (*harkhāvũ*) is built on **હરખ** (*harakh*), "delight", with the verb ending *-āvũ*; the noun probably goes back to Sanskrit *harṣa*, "joy" (Monier-Williams).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
