## You'll want to know: ஒன்றுமில்லை
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C95-RIGHT-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ஒன்றுமில்லை** (*oṉṟumillai*) — "nothing", "it's nothing".

**ஒன்று** is "one", and **இல்லை** is "no": *not even one*.
