## You'll want to know: તૈયાર થવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C155-READY155-02]; assesses=[] -->

**તૈયાર થવું** — *taiyār thavũ* — "to get ready".

> **હું સવારે જલદી તૈયાર થાઉં છું.** — *hũ savāre jaldī taiyār thāũ chũ* — "I get ready quickly in the morning."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**તૈયાર થવું** (*taiyār thavũ*) is built from *taiyār*, "ready", a loan from Persian *tayyār* (EtymDB lists the Persian word), and the inherited verb *thavũ*, "to become".
