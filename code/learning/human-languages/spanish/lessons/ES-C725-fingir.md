## You'll want to know: fingir
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C725-FIEL-05]; assesses=[] -->

**fingir** — "to pretend, to fake".

> Fingió que no me veía.
>
> *He pretended not to see me.*

*Fingir que* + indicative. Spelling: *finjo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*fingir* comes from Latin *fingere* "to shape, to invent, to feign" (EtymDB); English *fiction* and *feign* share it.
