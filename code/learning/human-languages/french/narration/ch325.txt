French, chapter 325: Les traces du passé.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
l'historien — the historian.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for the conquest, then the French for to defeat.

You'll want to know: l'historien.
l'historien — "the historian".
Les historiens ne sont pas d'accord sur cette date. Historians do not agree on this date.
Feminine: l'historienne.

The word, taken apart.
historien is built on histoire, from Latin historia, a borrowing from Greek historía "inquiry, knowledge" (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 4 of 5.
l'archéologue — the archaeologist.

l'archéologue — "the archaeologist".
Les archéologues ont trouvé des pièces très anciennes. The archaeologists found some very old coins.
The science is l'archéologie.

The word, taken apart.
archéologue is a learned word built from Greek arkhaîos "ancient" and -logos "one who studies" (EtymDB lists no parent).

Lesson 5 of 5.
la fouille — the dig, the excavation; a search.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for heritage, then the French for the archaeologist.

You'll want to know: la fouille.
la fouille — "the dig, the excavation; a search".
Les fouilles ont duré trois ans. The dig went on for three years.
Also a search, by the police or at an airport.

The word, taken apart.
fouille is built on fouiller "to dig, to search", usually traced to Vulgar Latin fodiculāre (EtymDB lists no parent), built on Latin fodere "to dig" (Lewis & Short).
