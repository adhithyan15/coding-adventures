## You'll want to know: o ritual
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C320-CERIMONIA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**o ritual** — "the ritual".

> Tomar café depois do almoço é um ritual para ele.
>
> *Having coffee after lunch is a ritual for him.*

Also an adjective: *uma dança ritual*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ritual* is a learned borrowing from Latin *rītuālis* "of rites", from *rītus* "a rite, a custom" (Lewis & Short).
