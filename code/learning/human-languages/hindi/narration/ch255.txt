बहू (bahū) — a daughter-in-law.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a mother-in-law, then the Hindi for a father-in-law.

You'll want to know: बहू.
बहू — bahū — "a daughter-in-law".

The word, taken apart.
बहू (bahū) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from vadhū, "bride, young wife" (CDIAL 11250).

Lesson 5 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a husband's younger brother, then the Hindi for a husband's sister.

You'll want to know: जीजा.
जीजा — jījā — "a sister's husband".

The word, taken apart.
जीजा (jījā) is a tadbhava, a word inherited and reshaped by sound change. It comes from jījja, "a nursery word for breast, mother, sister", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 5232). CDIAL glosses Hindi jījā "sister's husband".
