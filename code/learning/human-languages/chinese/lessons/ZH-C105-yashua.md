## You'll want to know: yáshuā
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C105-BATH105-02]; assesses=[] -->

*yáshuā* — "toothbrush".

> *wǒ de yáshuā zài nǎr* — Where's my toothbrush?

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*yáshuā* is built from yá 'tooth' and shuā 'brush', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *ngae* and *srwaet* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription): the two parts of shuāyá, the other way round, make the thing.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for everyday things.
