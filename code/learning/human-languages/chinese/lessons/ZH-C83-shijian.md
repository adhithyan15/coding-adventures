## You'll want to know: shíjiān
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C83-WAIT83-01]; assesses=[] -->

*shíjiān* — "time; free time".

> *nǐ míngtiān yǒu shíjiān ma* — Are you free tomorrow?

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*shíjiān* is built from shí 'time' and jiān 'a gap', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *dzyi* and *kean* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription); the dictionary glosses the first with another word for a time, and lists the second under an older form of the character.
