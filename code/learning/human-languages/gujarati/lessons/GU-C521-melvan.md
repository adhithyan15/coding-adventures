# મેળવણ (meḷvaṇ) — a curd starter

## You'll want to know: મેળવણ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C521-VALONU521-01]; assesses=[] -->

**મેળવણ** — *meḷvaṇ* — "a curd starter".

> દહીં માટે દૂધમાં એક ચમચી મેળવણ નાખો.
>
> *dahī̃ māṭe dūdhmā̃ ek camcī meḷvaṇ nākho.*
>
> For curd, put a spoonful of starter in the milk.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**મેળવણ** (*meḷvaṇ*) is built on **મેળવવું**, "to get; to mix in", with the ending *-aṇ*: the spoonful of old curd mixed into milk to set it. CDIAL files the family under Sanskrit *miláti*, "meets" (CDIAL 10133).
