## You'll want to know: copier
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C397-FICHIER-03]; assesses=[] -->

**copier** — "to copy".

> Il a copié le texte dans un nouveau document.
>
> *He copied the text into a new document.*

At school, *copier* also means to cheat by copying.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*copier* comes from Medieval Latin *copiāre* "to transcribe" (EtymDB), from *cōpia* "plenty, abundance" (Lewis & Short): to make more of a text.
