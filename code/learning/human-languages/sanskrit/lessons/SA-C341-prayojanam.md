## You'll want to know: प्रयोजनम्
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C341-PRAYOJANA341-01]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रयोजनम्** — *prayojanam* — "a purpose".

> एतस्य कार्यस्य प्रयोजनं किम्?
>
> *etasya kāryasya prayojanaṃ kim?*
>
> What is the purpose of this work?

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रयोजनम्** (*prayojanam*) is built on *pra* and the root √yuj, "to join, to use"; MW gives *pra-yojana* the senses "occasion, motive, purpose, aim" (MW).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
