Lesson 1 of 5.
il cerotto — a plaster.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for to collect, then the Italian for to turn to.

You'll want to know: il cerotto.
il cerotto — "a plaster".

The word, taken apart.
cerotto from Latin cerotum, "a wax plaster", from Greek keros, "beeswax" — inherited. (EtymDB 2.1).

Lesson 2 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for to turn to, then the Italian for a plaster.

You'll want to know: l'ambulanza.
l'ambulanza — "an ambulance".

The word, taken apart.
ambulanza from French ambulance — borrowed. EtymDB 2.1 has no row for ambulanza; it builds the French word on ambulant, "walking", from Latin ambulare, "to walk": a field hospital that moved.
