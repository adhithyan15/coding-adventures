Lesson 1 of 7.
音乐 (yīnyuè) — music.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for a few, then the Mandarin for like this.

You'll want to know: 音乐.
音乐 — yīnyuè — "music".
我喜欢音乐 wǒ xǐhuan yīnyuè — I love music.
The new character, in pieces: 音 (yīn) — on top a five-stroke piece — a dot, a short bar, two short strokes leaning toward each other, and a long bar — and under it 日. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

The word, taken apart.
音乐 (yīnyuè) is built from 音 (yīn) 'sound' and 乐, here said yuè, both inherited from Middle Chinese, 'im and ngaewk (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription); the dictionary glosses the second with the whole word, 'music'.

Script — five strokes over 日.
音 (yīn).
Two pieces, top to bottom.
The top piece, five strokes. A dot at the top, a short horizontal under it, a short stroke on the left falling to the lower right, a short stroke on the right falling to the lower left, then a long horizontal across the whole width.
日, four strokes, the four your hand makes in 日: the left side, the top that turns down the right side, the middle bar, and the bar that closes the bottom.
5 + 4 = nine.

[once you have stopped driving — this part needs your eyes or your hands; if you are driving, skip ahead to the next part]
Guided Practice.
[once you have stopped driving — write: 音 (yīn), all nine, with the model in front of you]
[once you have stopped driving — cover: write it again from memory, and count the pieces, not the strokes]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
How many pieces in 音 (yīn)? (Two.) Which stroke ends the top piece? (The long horizontal.) What sits underneath? (日.)
Source: Hanzi Writer Data 音 (yīn), pinned stroke paths and medians.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
gē is inherited from Middle Chinese ka (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription); the dictionary quotes an older book: the voice of a person is called song.

You'll want to know: 音乐会.
音乐会 — yīnyuèhuì — "concert".
明天晚上我们去音乐会 míngtiān wǎnshang wǒmen qù yīnyuèhuì — Tomorrow evening we're going to a concert.

The word, taken apart.
音乐会 (yīnyuèhuì) is built from 音乐 (yīnyuè) 'music' and 会 'a gathering', all three parts inherited from Middle Chinese, 'im, ngaewk and hwajH (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription); the dictionary glosses the last 'to come together'.
