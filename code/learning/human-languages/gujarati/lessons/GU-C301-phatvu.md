## You'll want to know: ફાટવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C301-IMARAT301-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ફાટવું** — *phāṭvũ* — "to burst; to split".

> ગરમીથી દીવાલ ફાટી ગઈ.
>
> *garmīthī dīvāl phāṭī gaī.*
>
> The heat split the wall.

Past (it …): તે કપડું ફાટી ગયું, *te kapḍũ phāṭī gayũ*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ફાટવું** (*phāṭvũ*) belongs with Sanskrit *sphāṭayati*, "causes to split", where CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb, "to be split" (CDIAL 13825). Cloth tears and walls crack with it: **ફાટી ગયું**.
