## You'll want to know: remar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C659-BARCA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**remar** — "to row".

> Remamos hasta la isla.
>
> *We rowed to the island.*

*El remo* is the oar.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*remar* is built on *remo* "oar" (EtymDB), inherited from Latin *rēmus* "an oar" (Lewis & Short).
