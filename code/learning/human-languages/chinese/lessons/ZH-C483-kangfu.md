## You'll want to know: kāngfù
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C483-BETTER483-02]; assesses=[] -->

*kāngfù* — "to get well again; to convalesce".

> *tā kāngfù le xià ge yuè kěyǐ shàngbān* — He has recovered and can go back to work next month.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*kāngfù* is built from *kāng* 'at ease' and *fù* 'to return', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *khang* and *bjuwk* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'peaceful' and 'happy' and the second 'to return'.
