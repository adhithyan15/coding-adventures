## You'll want to know: عشق
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C486-JOY-04]; assesses=[] -->

**عشق** (*'eshq*) — "love (passionate)".

> عشق او به موسیقی از کودکی شروع شد.
>
> *'eshq-e u be musiqi az kudaki shoru' shod.*
>
> His love of music began in childhood.

Strong love, for a person or a passion. For liking, Persian says *dust dâshtan*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*'eshq* was borrowed from Arabic *ʿišq*, 'love, passion' (EtymDB).
