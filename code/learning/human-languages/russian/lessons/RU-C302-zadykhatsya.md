## You'll want to know: задыхаться
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C302-ZADYKHATSYA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**задыхаться** (*zadykhátsya*) — "to choke, to suffocate; to be out of breath".

> В дыму мы задыхались.
>
> *V dymú my zadykhális.*
>
> We were choking in the smoke.

Imperfective; perfective *задохнуться* (*zadokhnútsya*). Also after running: *он бежал и задыхался*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*задыхаться* is built on *за-*, *дыхать* "to breathe" and *-ся* (EtymDB), from the Slavic root of *дышать* and *дух* "breath".
