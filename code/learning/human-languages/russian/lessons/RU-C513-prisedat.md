## You'll want to know: приседать
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C513-TRAINING-03]; assesses=[] -->

**приседать** (*prisedát*) — "to squat".

> Тренер сказал нам приседать медленно.
>
> *Tréner skazál nam prisedát médlenno.*
>
> The coach told us to squat slowly.

Imperfective; perfective присесть (присяду), which also means to sit down for a moment.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*приседать* is built on *при-* and the root of *сесть* 'to sit down'; EtymDB lists the verb without a source.
