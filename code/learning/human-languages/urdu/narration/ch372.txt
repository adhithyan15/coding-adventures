Urdu, chapter 372: Heat and Cold.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
جمنا (jamnā) — to freeze; to set.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for to seep, then the Urdu for to split open.

You'll want to know: جمنا.
جمنا — jamnā — "to freeze; to set".
اگر رات کو سردی ہوئی تو پانی جم جائے گا۔ agar rāt ko sardī huī to pānī jam jāe gā. If it is cold tonight, the water will freeze.
Past (it …): وہ دودھ جم گیا, voh dūdh jam gayā.

The word, taken apart.
جمنا (jamnā) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin jamnā, "to coagulate, to freeze", under Sanskrit yamyátē, "is held fast" (CDIAL 10428).

[pause 2 seconds]

The word, taken apart.
درمیانہ (darmiyānā) is usually traced to Persian darmiyāna, "middle", built on darmiyān, "between".
