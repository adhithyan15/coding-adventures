## You'll want to know: le renouvellement
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C384-VALABLE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**le renouvellement** — "the renewal".

> Le renouvellement du passeport prend un mois.
>
> *Renewing a passport takes a month.*

Spelled with *ll*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*renouvellement* is built from *renouveler* and *-ment* (EtymDB). The verb rests on Latin *novellus* "new", from *novus* "new" (Lewis & Short).
