## You'll want to know: क़स्बा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C421-BASTI421-02]; assesses=[] -->

**क़स्बा** — *qasbā* — "a small town".

> मेरे नाना एक छोटे क़स्बे में रहते हैं।
>
> *mere nānā ek choṭe qasbe meṁ rahte haiṁ.*
>
> My grandfather lives in a small town.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**क़स्बा** (*qasbā*) is usually traced to Arabic *qaṣaba*, "a chief town, a fortress", which came through Persian; in Hindi a क़स्बा is bigger than a village and smaller than a city.
