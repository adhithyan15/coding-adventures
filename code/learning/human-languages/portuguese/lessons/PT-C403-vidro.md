## You'll want to know: o vidro
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C403-VIDROS-01]; assesses=[] -->

**o vidro** — "the glass (material); the car window".

> Ela abre o vidro do carro porque está calor.
>
> *She opens the car window because it is hot.*

*Uma garrafa de vidro* — a glass bottle. A drinking glass is *um copo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*vidro* is inherited from Latin *vitrum* 'glass' (EtymDB).
