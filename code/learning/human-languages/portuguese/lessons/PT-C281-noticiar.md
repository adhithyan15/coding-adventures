## You'll want to know: noticiar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C281-RELATAR-05]; assesses=[] -->

**noticiar** — "to announce in the news, to report".

> A televisão noticiou a chegada do presidente.
>
> *Television reported the president’s arrival.*

What the media do with an event.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*noticiar* is built on *notícia* "news", from Latin *nōtitia* "knowledge, a notice" (Lewis & Short).
