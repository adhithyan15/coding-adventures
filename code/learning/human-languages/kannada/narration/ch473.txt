Kannada, chapter 473: Our Environment.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಪರಿಸರ (parisara) — the environment; surroundings.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for shelter, then the Kannada for a helpline.

You'll want to know: ಪರಿಸರ.
ಪರಿಸರ — parisara — "the environment; surroundings".
ಪ್ಲಾಸ್ಟಿಕ್ ಪರಿಸರಕ್ಕೆ ಒಳ್ಳೆಯದಲ್ಲ. plāsṭik parisarakke oḷḷeyadalla. Plastic is not good for the environment.
ಪರಿಸರ (parisara) ದಿನ, environment day.

The word, taken apart.
ಪರಿಸರ (parisara) is borrowed from Sanskrit parisara, "adjacent, lying around" (Monier-Williams); modern Kannada uses it for the environment.

ಮಾಲಿನ್ಯ (mālinya) is borrowed from Sanskrit mālinya, "foulness, dirtiness, impurity" (Monier-Williams), from malina, "dirty".

The word, taken apart.
ಕಲುಷಿತಗೊಳಿಸು (kaluṣitagoḷisu) is built from Sanskrit kaluṣita, "foul, impure" (Monier-Williams), and the Kannada verb-maker -goḷisu: to make foul.

Lesson 4 of 5.
ಮಸಿ (masi) — soot.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for pollution, then the Kannada for to pollute.

You'll want to know: ಮಸಿ.
ಮಸಿ — masi — "soot".
ಹಳೆಯ ಬಸ್ಸಿನ ಹೊಗೆಯಿಂದ ಗೋಡೆಗೆ ಮಸಿ ಹಿಡಿಯಿತು. haḷeya bassina hogeyinda gōḍege masi hiḍiyitu. The smoke of an old bus left soot on the wall.
In older Kannada it was also ink.

The word, taken apart.
ಮಸಿ (masi) is native Dravidian: DEDR 5101 lists Kannada masi, "soot, lampblack, ink", beside Tamil mai. Sanskrit maṣi, masi, "lampblack, ink", was itself taken from Dravidian, as Turner notes (CDIAL 9920).

Lesson 5 of 5.
ಇಂಗಾಲ (iṅgāla) — carbon.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to pollute, then the Kannada for soot.

You'll want to know: ಇಂಗಾಲ.
ಇಂಗಾಲ — iṅgāla — "carbon".
ಮರಗಳು ಗಾಳಿಯಿಂದ ಇಂಗಾಲವನ್ನು ಹೀರುತ್ತವೆ. maragaḷu gāḷiyinda iṅgālavannu hīruttave. Trees take in carbon from the air.
In everyday Kannada a burnt-out coal; in science, carbon.

The word, taken apart.
ಇಂಗಾಲ (iṅgāla) comes from Sanskrit aṅgāra, "glowing charcoal", through the variant iṅgāla (CDIAL 125).
