Urdu, chapter 75: Evening, Week, Month, Year, Hour.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
شام (shām) — evening.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a day, then the Urdu for night.

You'll want to know: شام.
شام — shām — "evening".

The word, taken apart.
شام (shām) is a loan, usually traced to Persian shām, "evening". It only looks like Sanskrit śyāma, "dark", and is a different word.

Lesson 5 of 5.
گھنٹہ (ghaṇṭā) — an hour.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a month, then the Urdu for a year.

You'll want to know: گھنٹہ.
گھنٹہ — ghaṇṭā — "an hour".

The word, taken apart.
گھنٹہ (ghaṇṭā) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from ghaṇṭā, "bell" (CDIAL 4421). Clocks once marked the hour by striking a bell, and CDIAL glosses the Hindi word "bell, clock, hour".
