## You'll want to know: అన్యాయం
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C399-KARTAVYAM399-04]; assesses=[] -->

**అన్యాయం** — *anyāyaṁ* — "injustice; a wrong".

> పేదవాళ్ళకు అన్యాయం జరిగింది.
>
> *pēdavāḷḷaku anyāyaṁ jarigindi.*
>
> An injustice was done to the poor.

అ-, not, + న్యాయం, justice; న్యాయమైన, fair, which you know, is the same word.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**అన్యాయం** (*anyāyaṁ*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *anyāya*, "unjust or unlawful action" (Monier-Williams), the opposite of *nyāya*, "rule, justice".
