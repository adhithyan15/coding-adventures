# हम स्टेशन पर मिलेंगे — the sentence the paper actually prints

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[HI-LEX-C86-PRON-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say **हम**. Until this chapter the track could not say it, and a
reading item that opens with it was unreadable from the first word.

## Reading
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[HI-LEX-C86-PRON-01, HI-LEX-C86-PRON-05, HI-LEX-C86-PRON-02] -->

Read aloud. Every word has been taught:

> हम स्टेशन पर मिलेंगे।
> वे भी आ रहे हैं।

Two sentences. Note what is **not** in the first one: no word for *the* in front
of *sṭeśan*, because there is none to put there. An English speaker reads "we
will meet at **the** station" and supplies the article themselves.

## Grammar Lens: choosing between the obliques in a real question
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[HI-LEX-C86-PRON-03, HI-LEX-C86-PRON-04] -->

| asking | form |
|---|---|
| a friend | तुम्हें क्या पसंद है? |
| a stranger | आपको क्या पसंद है? |

The frame is the one the track has had since its liking chapter. What is new is
that the pronoun in it can now change — it was never a fixed phrase, and the
first word was always a pronoun in a case.
