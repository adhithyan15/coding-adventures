## You'll want to know: आमाशयः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C470-PHUPPHUSA470-04]; assesses=[] -->

**आमाशयः** — *āmāśayaḥ* — "the stomach".

> आमाशयः भोजनं पचति।
>
> *āmāśayaḥ bhojanaṃ pacati.*
>
> The stomach digests food.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**आमाशयः** (*āmāśayaḥ*) is built from *āma*, "raw, undigested", and *āśaya*, "a resting place"; MW gives "the receptacle of the undigested food, the stomach" (MW).
