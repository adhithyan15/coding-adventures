## You'll want to know: べんしょうする
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C364-SONGAI364-02]; assesses=[] -->

**べんしょうする** — *benshō suru* — "to pay for damage; to compensate".

> **おさらを わったら、べんしょうして ください。** — *osara o wattara, benshō shite kudasai* — "If you break a plate, please pay for it."

Polite **べんしょうします**, *benshōshimasu*; a する verb. Past (polite): **きゃくが べんしょうしました。**, *kyaku ga benshō shimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**べんしょうする** (*benshō suru*) is built from a Sino-Japanese noun and the native verb *suru*, "to do". The noun, *benshō*, is two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese: *ben*, "distinguish" and *shō*, "reparation" (KANJIDIC2; JMdict).
