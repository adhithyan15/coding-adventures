Persian, chapter 374: Getting better.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
بهبود (behbud) — an improvement; recovery.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to reduce, then the Persian for to grow.

You'll want to know: بهبود.
بهبود (behbud) — "an improvement; recovery".
حال پدربزرگ رو به بهبود است. hâl-e pedarbozorg ru be behbud ast. Grandfather's health is improving.
Beh "good, better" + bud "being". For health and for situations alike.

The word, taken apart.
بهبود (behbud) is a native Persian compound built from beh "good, better" and bud, the past stem of budan "to be": a better state of being.

Lesson 2 of 5.
بهبود یافتن (behbud yâftan) — to improve, to recover.

Warm-up.

Before the new one: say the Persian for to grow, then the Persian for an improvement.

You'll want to know: بهبود یافتن.
بهبود یافتن (behbud yâftan) — "to improve, to recover".
بعد از دو هفته حالش بهبود یافت. ba'd az do hafte hâlash behbud yâft. After two weeks he recovered.
Formal; yâftan has the stem yâb-. In speech, behtar shodan.

The word, taken apart.
بهبود یافتن (behbud yâftan) is native Persian, built from behbud "improvement" with the verb yâftan "to find", a formal way to say that something got better.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for an improvement, then the Persian for to improve.

You'll want to know: پیشرفت.
پیشرفت (pishraft) — "progress".
پیشرفت تو در زبان فارسی عالی است. pishraft-e to dar zabân-e fârsi 'âli ast. Your progress in Persian is excellent.
Pish "forward" + raft "went". Plural pishrafthâ.

The word, taken apart.
پیشرفت (pishraft) is a native Persian compound built from pish "forward, before" and raft, the past stem of raftan "to go". Progress is going forward.

Lesson 5 of 5.
عقب ماندن ('aqab mândan) — to fall behind, to lag.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for progress, then the Persian for to make progress.

You'll want to know: عقب ماندن.
عقب ماندن ('aqab mândan) — "to fall behind, to lag".
او به خاطر بیماری از درسها عقب ماند. u be khâter-e bimâri az darshâ 'aqab mând. Because of his illness he fell behind with his lessons.
'aqab "back" + mândan "to stay" (present stem mân-). Takes az.

The word, taken apart.
عقب ماندن ('aqab mândan) is literally "to stay behind": 'aqab "back" is usually traced to Arabic 'aqab "heel", and mândan "to stay" is inherited Persian (EtymDB traces it to Middle Persian).
