## You'll want to know: فوضى
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C324-KHATAR324-05]; assesses=[] -->

**فوضى** — *fawḍā* — "chaos, disorder".

> كانت الغرفة في فوضى كبيرة.
>
> *kānat al-ghurfa fī fawḍā kabīra.*
>
> The room was in a terrible mess.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**فوضى** (*fawḍā*) is built on the native Arabic root *f-w-ḍ* (Lane), where *qawm fawḍā* is a people who are all equal, with no chief over them: everyone at once.
