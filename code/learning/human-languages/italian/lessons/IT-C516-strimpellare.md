## You'll want to know: strimpellare
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C516-STRUMENTI-05]; assesses=[] -->

**strimpellare** — "to strum, to play badly".

> Strimpello un po' la chitarra, ma non so suonare bene.
>
> *I strum the guitar a bit, but I cannot play well.*

Playing without much skill, for fun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

The origin of *strimpellare* is not settled; it is usually taken to imitate the sound of plucked strings.
