Russian, chapter 353: Damage and harm.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ущерб (ushchérb) — damage, loss.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for an excuse, then the Russian for responsible.

You'll want to know: ущерб.
ущерб (ushchérb) — "damage, loss".
Пожар причинил большой ущерб. Pozhár prichiníl bolshóy ushchérb. The fire caused great damage.
Masculine. Нанести ущерб (ushchérb) — to cause damage. Луна на ущербе — the moon is waning.

The word, taken apart.
ущерб (ushchérb) is usually traced to у- and the root of щерба, щербина "a notch, a gap": damage is a piece broken out of something.

Lesson 2 of 5.
причинять (prichinyát) — to cause (harm).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for responsible, then the Russian for damage.

You'll want to know: причинять.
причинять (prichinyát) — "to cause (harm)".
Я не хотел причинять тебе боль. Ya ne khotél prichinyát tebé bol. I did not want to cause you pain.
Imperfective; the perfective is причинить. It goes with bad things: причинять (prichinyát) боль, вред, ущерб (ushchérb).

The word, taken apart.
причинять (prichinyát) is the verb made from причина "a cause", which is usually traced to при- and чин "order, rank", the root of чинить "to make, to mend".

Lesson 3 of 5.
вредить (vredít) — to harm, to do damage to.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for damage, then the Russian for to cause.

You'll want to know: вредить.
вредить (vredít) — "to harm, to do damage to".
Сладкое вредит зубам. Sládkoye vredít zubám. Sweet things harm your teeth.
Imperfective; present врежу, вредишь; perfective навредить. It takes the dative.

The word, taken apart.
вредить (vredít) is the verb made from вред "harm", which Russian took from Church Slavonic vrědŭ, from Proto-Slavic verdŭ (EtymDB).

Lesson 4 of 5.
страдать (stradát) — to suffer.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to cause, then the Russian for to harm.

You'll want to know: страдать.
страдать (stradát) — "to suffer".
Он страдает от головной боли. On stradáyet ot golovnóy bóli. He suffers from headaches.
Imperfective. Страдать от + genitive (from a cause), страдать (stradát) + instrumental (from an illness).

The word, taken apart.
страдать (stradát) belongs with страда "hard farm work, harvest time" (EtymDB lists both, and glosses the verb "to suffer, to drudge"). Toil and suffering sit side by side in the one word.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ухудшать (ukhudshát) — to make worse.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to harm, then the Russian for to suffer.

You'll want to know: ухудшать.
ухудшать (ukhudshát) — "to make worse".
Стресс ухудшает сон. Stress ukhudsháyet son. Stress makes your sleep worse.
Imperfective; perfective ухудшить. Ухудшаться — to get worse.

The word, taken apart.
ухудшать (ukhudshát) is built on худший "worse", from худой "thin, poor, bad", inherited from Proto-Slavic xudŭ (EtymDB).
