## You'll want to know: शेषः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C298-KSHATI298-05]; assesses=[] -->

**शेषः** — *śeṣaḥ* — "what is left; the remainder".

> गृहस्य शेषः केवलं भस्म अस्ति।
>
> *gṛhasya śeṣaḥ kevalaṃ bhasma asti.*
>
> All that is left of the house is ash.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**शेषः** (*śeṣaḥ*) comes from the root √śiṣ, "to leave, leave remaining"; MW gives *śeṣa* as the remainder, what is left (MW).
