## You'll want to know: امانت
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C531-QAUL531-01]; assesses=[] -->

**امانت** — *amānat* — "something left in one's keeping".

> اگر اس نے امانت لوٹائی تو میں خوش ہوں گا۔
>
> *agar us ne amānat lauṭāī to maiṅ khush hūṅ gā.*
>
> If he gives back what I left with him, I will be glad.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**امانت** (*amānat*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *amāna*, "trustworthiness; a thing entrusted".
