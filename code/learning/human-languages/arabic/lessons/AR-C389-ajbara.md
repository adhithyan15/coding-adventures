## You'll want to know: أجبر
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C389-NUFUDH389-04]; assesses=[] -->

**أجبر** — *ajbara* — "to force".

> أجبر المطر الناس على البقاء في البيت.
>
> *ajbara al-maṭar an-nās ʿalā al-baqāʾ fī al-bayt.*
>
> The rain forced people to stay at home.

Present: يجبر, *yujbir*.

It takes على: أجبره على ….

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**أجبر** (*ajbara*) is built on the native Arabic root *j-b-r*, "to set a broken bone" (Lane). It is Form IV; Lane gives it for compelling someone: to force.
