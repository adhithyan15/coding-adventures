## You'll want to know: manifestare
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C364-PROTESTA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**manifestare** — "to demonstrate; to show (a feeling)".

> Migliaia di persone manifestano in piazza.
>
> *Thousands of people are demonstrating in the square.*

Also to show: *manifestare la propria gioia*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*manifestare* is a learned borrowing from Latin *manifestāre* "to make plain, to show" (Lewis & Short), from *manifestus* "evident, plain" (EtymDB).
