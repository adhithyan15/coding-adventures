## You'll want to know: مشرف
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C392-MUHADIR392-04]; assesses=[] -->

**مشرف** — *mushrif* — "a supervisor".

> ساعدني المشرف في بحثي.
>
> *sāʿadanī al-mushrif fī baḥthī.*
>
> The supervisor helped me with my research.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**مشرف** (*mushrif*) is built on the native Arabic root *sh-r-f*, "to be high, noble" (Lane). *Mushrif* is the Form IV active participle: one who oversees, a supervisor.
