# સાસરું (sāsrũ) — the in-laws' home

## You'll want to know: સાસરું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C492-PIYAR492-02]; assesses=[] -->

**સાસરું** — *sāsrũ* — "the in-laws' home".

> લગ્ન પછી દીકરી સાસરે ગઈ.
>
> *lagna pachī dīkrī sāsre gaī.*
>
> After the wedding the daughter went to her in-laws' home.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**સાસરું** (*sāsrũ*) is inherited from Sanskrit *śvāśura*, "belonging to the father-in-law"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati word, "father-in-law's house" (CDIAL 12767).
