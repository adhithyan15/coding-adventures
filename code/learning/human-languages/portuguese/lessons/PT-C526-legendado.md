## You'll want to know: legendado
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C526-SERIES-04]; assesses=[] -->

**legendado** — "subtitled".

> Prefiro ver os filmes legendados.
>
> *I prefer to watch films with subtitles.*

Subtitles are *as legendas*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*legendado* is built on *legenda* "a caption, a subtitle", from Latin *legenda* "things to be read" (EtymDB), from *legere* "to read".
