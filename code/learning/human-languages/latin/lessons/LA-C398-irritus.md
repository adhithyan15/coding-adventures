## You'll want to know: irritus, irrita, irritum
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C398-PRAESTO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**irritus, irrita, irritum** — "void, invalid; in vain".

> Cōnsilia eius irrita fuērunt.
>
> *His plans came to nothing.*

*irritus, irrita, irritum* (also *inritus*): *in-* not + *ratus*, fixed. *Irritum fierī*, to come to nothing.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*irritus* is built from *in-* "not" and *ratus* "fixed, valid" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short): not settled, so void.
