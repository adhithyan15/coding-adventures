Telugu, chapter 485: Paying Up.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
చెల్లింపు (cellimpu) — a payment.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a concession, then the Telugu for to haggle.

You'll want to know: చెల్లింపు.
చెల్లింపు — cellimpu — "a payment".
బిల్లు చెల్లింపు కోసం కౌంటరుకు వెళ్ళాను. billu cellimpu kōsaṁ kauṇṭaruku veḷḷānu. I went to the counter to pay the bill.
The noun of చెల్లించు, to pay. ఆన్లైన్ చెల్లింపు (cellimpu), an online payment.

The word, taken apart.
చెల్లింపు (cellimpu) is native Dravidian, the noun of చెల్లించు, "to pay", from చెల్లు, "to pass, to be current, to be paid" (DEDR 2781).

Lesson 2 of 6.
నగదు (nagadu) — cash.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to haggle, then the Telugu for a payment.

You'll want to know: నగదు.
నగదు — nagadu — "cash".
ఆ దుకాణంలో నగదు మాత్రమే తీసుకుంటారు. ā dukāṇaṁlō nagadu mātramē tīsukuṇṭāru. That shop takes cash only.
నగదు (nagadu) రూపంలో, in cash.

The word, taken apart.
నగదు (nagadu) is a loan from Arabic naqd, "ready money, cash", which came into Telugu through Persian and Hindustani.

మారకం — mārakaṁ — "an exchange rate".
ఇవాళ డాలరు మారకం రేటు పెరిగింది. ivāḷa ḍālaru mārakaṁ rēṭu perigindi. Today the dollar exchange rate went up.

Lesson 5 of 6.
ఎగ్గొట్టు (eggoṭṭu) — to dodge; to skip paying.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for money owed, then the Telugu for an exchange rate.

You'll want to know: ఎగ్గొట్టు.
ఎగ్గొట్టు — eggoṭṭu — "to dodge; to skip paying".
అతను అద్దె ఎగ్గొట్టి వెళ్ళిపోయాడు. atanu adde eggoṭṭi veḷḷipōyāḍu. He went off without paying the rent.
Past (I …): ఎగ్గొట్టాను, eggoṭṭānu.
A colloquial word: to dodge a debt, a class or a duty.

The word, taken apart.
ఎగ్గొట్టు (eggoṭṭu) is a Telugu compound: -గొట్టు, from the native కొట్టు, "to strike" (DEDR 2063), and a first part whose origin is not settled.
