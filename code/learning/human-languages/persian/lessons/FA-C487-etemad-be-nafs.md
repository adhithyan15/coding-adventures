## You'll want to know: اعتماد به نفس
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C487-HOPE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**اعتماد به نفس** (*e'temâd be nafs*) — "self-confidence".

> او بعد از این مسابقه اعتماد به نفس بیشتری دارد.
>
> *u ba'd az in mosâbeqe e'temâd be nafs-e bishtari dârad.*
>
> After this contest he has more self-confidence.

*e'temâd* 'trust' + *be* 'in' + *nafs* 'self': trust in oneself.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*e'temâd be nafs* is built in Persian from two Arabic loans: *e'temâd*, usually traced to Arabic *iʿtimād*, 'reliance', and *nafs*, borrowed from Arabic *nafs*, 'self, soul' (EtymDB), joined by Persian *be*.
