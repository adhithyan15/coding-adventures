# ਸੌਂਦਾ — he sleeps

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-LEX-C56-FAMILY56-05, PA-FORM-DEKHDA-183] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Recall *saunaa*, “to sleep,” then read **ਦੇਖਦਾ**.

## Grammar Lens: one small sound change
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-FORM-SAUNDA-183]; assesses=[] -->

> **ਸੌਂਦਾ** — *saundaa* — sleeps, for one masculine subject

Hear the nasal in the middle and keep the familiar ending. In a complete sentence: **ਉਹ ਸੌਂਦਾ ਹੈ** — “He sleeps.”
