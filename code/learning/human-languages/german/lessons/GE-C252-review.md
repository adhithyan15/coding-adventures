# Review — the scene, and what cut into it

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GE-GRAMMAR-PRAETERITUM-STARK-01, GE-GRAMMAR-PRAETERITUM-TE-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "I went", "I came" and "I saw". (*Ich ging. Ich kam. Ich
sah.*) Then "I waited". (*Ich wartete.*)

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GE-GRAMMAR-ERZAEHL-PRAETERITUM-01, GE-GRAMMAR-WAEHREND-HINTERGRUND-02, GE-ETYMON-ABLAUT-02] -->

Three lessons, and the past of a story is in your hands. Two ways to make
it: the *-te* of *sagte* and *wartete*, and the vowel change of *ging*,
*kam*, *sah*, *fuhr*, *nahm*, *stand* and *las*. One form for "I read" and
"I was reading", with the sentence around it deciding which. And
**während** to hold a scene open while something happens.

[PAUSE 2s] Then the moment itself: **als** for one occasion, **wenn** for
every time.

> *Während ich las, klingelte das Telefon.*
> *Als ich in Berlin ankam, regnete es.*

The second one is worth hearing twice. *Ankommen* splits in a main clause:
*ich kam an*. After *als*, the verb goes to the end, and the prefix joins it
again: *als ich in Berlin* **ankam**. Then verb, comma, verb: *ankam,
regnete*.
