## You'll want to know: el barro
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C702-MOJARSE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**el barro** — "the mud; clay".

> Tengo los zapatos llenos de barro.
>
> *My shoes are covered in mud.*

*Una olla de barro* — a clay pot.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*barro* comes from Vulgar Latin *barrum* "mud, clay" (EtymDB); the deeper origin is not settled, and it may be pre-Roman.
