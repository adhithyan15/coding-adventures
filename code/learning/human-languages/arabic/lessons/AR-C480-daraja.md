## You'll want to know: درجة
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C480-NASHRA480-02]; assesses=[] -->

**درجة** — *daraja* — "a degree; a class (of travel)".

> كانت درجة الحرارة مرتفعة اليوم.
>
> *kānat darajat al-ḥarāra murtafiʿa al-yawm.*
>
> The temperature was high today.

Plural: درجات, *darajāt*.

*darajat al-ḥarāra* is the temperature; *ad-daraja al-ūlā* is first class.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**درجة** (*daraja*) is built on the native Arabic root *d-r-j*, "to go step by step" (Lane). A *daraja* is a step.
