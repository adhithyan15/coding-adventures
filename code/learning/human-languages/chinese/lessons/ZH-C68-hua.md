## You'll want to know: huā
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C68-NATURE68-04]; assesses=[] -->

*huā* — "flower".

> *māma xǐhuan huā* — Mum loves flowers.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*huā* is inherited from Middle Chinese *xwae* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which the dictionary notes as the everyday form, in common use, of an older word for blossom.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for everyday answers.
