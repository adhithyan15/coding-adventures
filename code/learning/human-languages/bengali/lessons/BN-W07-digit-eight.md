## Script
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-SCRIPT-DIGIT-EIGHT-01]; assesses=[BN-SCRIPT-DIGIT-SIX-01] -->

> ৮

That is the Bengali digit **eight**: an upright stroke with a single small bowl
at the bottom right — much closer to a lower-case **b** than to anything numeric.

Look hard at it and notice what it is **not**. It has **one** bowl. **8** has
**two**, stacked. Whatever the two-loop shape means in Bengali, it does not mean
eight — and something has to mean it, because the shape exists.

That is the next lesson, and it is the one place in this chapter where the digits
you already read will tell you a lie.
