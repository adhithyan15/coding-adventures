## You'll want to know: کلکسیون
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C507-HOBBIES-05]; assesses=[] -->

**کلکسیون** (*koleksiyon*) — "a collection".

> او یک کلکسیون تمبر قدیمی دارد.
>
> *u yek koleksiyon-e tambr-e qadimi dârad.*
>
> He has a collection of old stamps.

Plural *koleksiyonhâ*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*koleksiyon* is a modern loan, usually traced to French *collection*. EtymDB has no entry for the Persian word.
