## You'll want to know: la libération
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C326-LIBERATION-02]; assesses=[] -->

**la libération** — "the liberation, the release".

> La libération de la ville a été une grande fête.
>
> *The liberation of the town was a great celebration.*

*La libération d'un otage* — the release of a hostage.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*libération* is a learned borrowing from Latin *līberātiō* "a setting free, a release" (EtymDB).
