## You'll want to know: રંદો
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C503-RANDO503-01]; assesses=[] -->

**રંદો** — *rando* — "a carpenter's plane".

> સુથાર રંદાથી લાકડું સીધું કરે છે.
>
> *suthār randāthī lākḍũ sīdhũ kare che.*
>
> The carpenter makes the wood straight with a plane.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**રંદો** (*rando*) is usually traced to Persian *randa*, "a carpenter's plane". Before **થી** it becomes **રંદાથી**.
