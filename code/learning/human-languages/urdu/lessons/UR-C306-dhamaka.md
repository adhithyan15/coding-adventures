## You'll want to know: دھماکا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C306-DHAMAKA306-01]; assesses=[] -->

**دھماکا** — *dhamākā* — "a bang; a blast".

> دھماکا ذرا دور ہوا۔
>
> *dhamākā zarā dūr huā.*
>
> The blast happened a little way off.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**دھماکا** (*dhamākā*) is probably imitative, built on the sound *dham*; CDIAL sets Hindi *dhamādham*, "noisily", under a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 6735).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
