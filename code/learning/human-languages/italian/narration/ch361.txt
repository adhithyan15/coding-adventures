Italian, chapter 361: Fare carriera.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
l'aumento — the rise, the increase; the pay rise.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for the extension, then the Italian for the delivery.

You'll want to know: l'aumento.
l'aumento — "the rise, the increase; the pay rise".
Ho chiesto un aumento al mio capo. I asked my boss for a pay rise.
In aumento — rising: i prezzi sono in aumento.

The word, taken apart.
aumento is a learned borrowing from Latin augmentum "an increase, growth" (EtymDB), built on augēre "to increase".

Lesson 2 of 5.
la qualifica — the qualification; the job title.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for the delivery, then the Italian for the rise.

You'll want to know: la qualifica.
la qualifica — "the qualification; the job title".
Per questo lavoro serve una qualifica tecnica. For this job you need a technical qualification.
Stress: qualìfica. On a contract it is your grade or title.

The word, taken apart.
qualifica is built on qualificare, a learned borrowing from Medieval Latin quālificāre "to give a quality to", built from quālis "of what kind" and facere "to make" (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 3 of 5.
la competenza — the skill, the competence.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for the rise, then the Italian for the qualification.

You'll want to know: la competenza.
la competenza — "the skill, the competence".
Ha grandi competenze in matematica. She has great skills in mathematics.
Often plural: le competenze on a CV. Non è di mia competenza — it is not my area.

The word, taken apart.
competenza is a learned borrowing from Late Latin competentia "agreement, fitness" (EtymDB), from competere "to meet, to be suitable", built on petere "to seek".

Lesson 4 of 5.
la formazione — the training, the education; the line-up.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: la formazione.
la formazione — "the training, the education; the line-up".
L'azienda offre un mese di formazione ai nuovi dipendenti. The company offers new employees a month of training.
La formazione of a team is its line-up.

The word, taken apart.
formazione is a learned borrowing from Latin fōrmātiō "a forming, a shaping" (Lewis & Short), from fōrma "shape, form": training gives a shape.

Lesson 5 of 5.
il consulente — the consultant, the adviser.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: il consulente.
il consulente — "the consultant, the adviser".
Abbiamo chiamato un consulente per organizzare il lavoro. We called in a consultant to organise the work.
One form for a man or a woman. Consulenza is the advice or the service.

The word, taken apart.
consulente is a learned borrowing from Latin cōnsulēns, present participle of cōnsulere "to take counsel, to consult" (Lewis & Short).
