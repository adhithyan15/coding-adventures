## You'll want to know: l'edicolante
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C452-EDICOLA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**l'edicolante** — "the newsagent".

> L'edicolante mi tiene da parte la rivista.
>
> *The newsagent keeps the magazine aside for me.*

The person who runs *l'edicola*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*edicolante* is built on *edicola*, a learned borrowing from Latin *aedicula* 'small shrine', from *aedēs* 'temple' (EtymDB).
