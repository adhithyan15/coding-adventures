## You'll want to know: ನೃತ್ಯ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C532-GITA532-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ನೃತ್ಯ** — *nṛtya* — "a dance form; dancing".

> ಅಕ್ಕ ಹತ್ತು ವರ್ಷದಿಂದ ನೃತ್ಯ ಕಲಿಯುತ್ತಿದ್ದಾಳೆ.
>
> *akka hattu varṣadinda nṛtya kaliyuttiddāḷe.*
>
> My elder sister has been learning dance for ten years.

Formal and artistic; everyday dancing is ಕುಣಿತ.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ನೃತ್ಯ** (*nṛtya*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *nṛtya*, "dancing, acting, gesture" (Monier-Williams).
