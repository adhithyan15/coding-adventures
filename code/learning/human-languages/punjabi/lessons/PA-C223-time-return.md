# Return to next week

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-FORM-AGLE-184, PA-FORM-HAFTE-184, PA-FORM-AGLI-184, PA-TRAVEL-JOURNEY-01] -->

[YOU HEAR: *agle hafte*] Say **ਅਗਲੇ ਹਫ਼ਤੇ**, "next week". Then *the next
journey*: **ਅਗਲੀ ਯਾਤਰਾ**.

## Grammar Lens
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-GRAMMAR-FUT-1PL-221, PA-GRAMMAR-FUT-CONSONANT-222] -->

You can now say *we will go* and *I will do*. A time word at the front tells
when: **ਕੱਲ੍ਹ**, **ਅਗਲੇ ਹਫ਼ਤੇ**.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-FORM-AGLE-184, PA-FORM-HAFTE-184, PA-GRAMMAR-FUT-1PL-221] -->

- [YOU HEAR: *next week*] Say **ਅਗਲੇ ਹਫ਼ਤੇ**.
- [YOU HEAR: *we will go tomorrow*] Say **ਅਸੀਂ ਕੱਲ੍ਹ ਜਾਵਾਂਗੇ**.
- [YOU HEAR: *the journey will be by train*] Say **ਯਾਤਰਾ ਰੇਲ ਨਾਲ ਹੋਵੇਗੀ**.

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-FORM-AGLE-184, PA-FORM-HAFTE-184] -->

[PAUSE 3s] Say *next week* and *tomorrow* once more.
