## You'll want to know: justificar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C586-JUSTIF-01]; assesses=[] -->

**justificar** — "to justify".

> Tienes que justificar los gastos.
>
> *You have to justify the expenses.*

Spelling: *justifiqué*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*justificar* is a learned borrowing from Late Latin *iustificare* 'to make right', built on *iustus* 'right, lawful' and *facere* 'to make' (EtymDB).
