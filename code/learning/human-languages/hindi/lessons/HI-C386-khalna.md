## You'll want to know: खलना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C386-SAHARA386-02]; assesses=[] -->

**खलना** — *khalnā* — "to be missed, to bother (someone)".

> अगर तुम चले जाओगे तो मुझे बहुत खलेगा।
>
> *agar tum cale jāoge to mujhe bahut khalegā.*
>
> If you go away, I'll really miss you.

Past (it …): वह बात उसे बहुत खली, *vah bāt use bahut khalī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**खलना** (*khalnā*) is a tadbhava, inherited from Sanskrit *skhalati*, "stumbles" (CDIAL 13663). CDIAL glosses the Hindi verb "to be bad, to be inimical"; the sense of being missed is not among its glosses.
