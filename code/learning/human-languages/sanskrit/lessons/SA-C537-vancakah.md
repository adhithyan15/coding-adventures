## You'll want to know: वञ्चकः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C537-PASAKA537-03]; assesses=[] -->

**वञ्चकः** — *vañcakaḥ* — "a cheat".

> यदि सः वञ्चकः अस्ति, तर्हि अहं न क्रीडामि।
>
> *yadi saḥ vañcakaḥ asti, tarhi ahaṃ na krīḍāmi.*
>
> If he is a cheat, I won't play.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**वञ्चकः** (*vañcakaḥ*) comes from the causative of the root √vañc, "to go crookedly", which means "to deceive", with the agent ending *-aka*; MW gives *vañcaka* as deceiving, a deceiver, a cheat (MW).
