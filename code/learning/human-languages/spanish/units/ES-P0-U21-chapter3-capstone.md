# Unit 21: Chapter 3 Capstone — Describing Your Family

## Warm-up

[PAUSE 2s] Articles, the gender pattern and its exceptions, adjective
agreement, colors, family words — one description, all five recombined.

## New Material

No new vocabulary — pure recombination.

> "**Mi madre es alta.**" — My mother is tall. *(alta, feminine agreement)*
> "**Mi padre es pequeño.**" — My father is short. *(pequeño, masculine)*
> "**Mi hermana tiene el pelo negro.**" — My sister has black hair.
> "**La casa de mi familia es grande y azul.**" — My family's house is big
> and blue. *(grande/azul — neither changes by gender)*

## Wrap-up Recall

[PAUSE 3s] Of everything in Chapter 3, which single fact is most likely to
trip you up a year from now? For most learners it's *el agua* — grammatically
feminine, sounds masculine. Worth over-reviewing.
