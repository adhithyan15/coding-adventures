# Saying no: a four-skill payoff

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-GRAMMAR-NEG-HABITUAL-212, PA-GRAMMAR-NEG-COPULA-212] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say *I don't drink tea*, then *the garden is not open*.

## Guided Practice: four skills
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-GRAMMAR-NEG-HABITUAL-212, PA-GRAMMAR-NEG-COPULA-212, PA-GRAMMAR-KHANDA-PINDA-211, PA-GRAMMAR-VOWEL-STEM-211, PA-STATUS-BAND-CLOSED-01] -->

- [YOU HEAR: *I don't eat roti*] Say it with the ending that fits you.
- [YOU HEAR: *the garden is not closed*] Say **ਬਾਗ਼ ਬੰਦ ਨਹੀਂ ਹੈ।**
- [YOU READ: **ਉਹ ਸਕੂਲ ਨਹੀਂ ਜਾਂਦੀ ਹੈ।**] Give its meaning: she doesn't go to school.
- [YOU WRITE: cover the line and write **ਮੈਂ ਦੁੱਧ ਨਹੀਂ ਪੀਂਦਾ ਹਾਂ।** or its woman's form]

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-GRAMMAR-NEG-HABITUAL-212, PA-GRAMMAR-NEG-COPULA-212] -->

[PAUSE 3s] Say two true things you don't do, and one place that is not open
now.
