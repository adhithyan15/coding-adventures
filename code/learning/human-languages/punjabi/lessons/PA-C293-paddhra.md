# ਪੱਧਰਾ (paddhrā) — level; flat

## You'll want to know: ਪੱਧਰਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C293-LAND293-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ਪੱਧਰਾ** — *paddhrā* — "level; flat".

> ਪਿੰਡ ਦੇ ਨੇੜੇ ਜ਼ਮੀਨ ਪੱਧਰੀ ਹੈ।
>
> *piṇḍ de neṛe zamīn paddhrī hai.*
>
> Near the village the land is level.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਪੱਧਰਾ** (*paddhrā*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*paddhara*, "straight, level"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *paddharā*, "smooth and even", under it (CDIAL 7767).
