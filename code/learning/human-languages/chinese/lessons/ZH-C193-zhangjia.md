## You'll want to know: zhǎngjià
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C193-PRICE193-03]; assesses=[] -->

*zhǎngjià* — "to get dearer; to go up in price".

> *niúnǎi zhǎngjià le* — Milk has gone up.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*zhǎngjià* is built from *zhǎng* 'to rise' and *jià* 'price'. The second is inherited from Middle Chinese *kaeH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription). The dictionary glosses the first as water in flood, with a level and a departing tone only, so the rising tone of zhǎng is not the regular outcome, and its history is not settled.
