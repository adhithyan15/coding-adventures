Tamil, chapter 91: Sun, Heat, and Sleep.
4 lessons. All 4 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 4.
வெயில் (veyil) — sunshine, the heat of the sun.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for alone, then the Tamil for I am hungry.

You'll want to know: வெயில்.
வெயில் (veyil) — "sunshine", the heat of the sun.
வெயில் (veyil) அதிகம் — "the sun is strong", literally "the sunshine is much".

What you've built.
The sun's heat.

Guided Practice.

[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: veyil, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say veyil atikam]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Tamil for alone, then the Tamil for I am hungry, then say veyil again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does வெயில் (veyil) mean? ("Sunshine, the heat of the sun".) Say it once more.


Lesson 2 of 4.
சூடு (cūṭu) — heat; hot.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for I am hungry, then the Tamil for sunshine.

You'll want to know: சூடு.
சூடு (cūṭu) — "heat", and of a thing, "hot".
சூடாக இருக்கிறது — "it is hot". Tea, a pan, a forehead: anything can be சூடு (cūṭu).

What you've built.
Heat, of a day or a thing.

Lesson 3 of 4.
தூக்கம் வருகிறது (tūkkam varukiṟatu) — (I) am sleepy.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for sunshine, then the Tamil for heat.

You'll want to know: தூக்கம் வருகிறது.
தூக்கம் வருகிறது (tūkkam varukiṟatu) — "I am sleepy", literally "sleep is coming".
தூக்கம் is the sleep you know, on the coming frame of laughter and tears.

What you've built.
Sleep, which comes to you.

Lesson 4 of 4.
நன்றாக (naṉṟāka) — well, fine.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for heat, then the Tamil for I am sleepy.

You'll want to know: நன்றாக.
நன்றாக (naṉṟāka) — "well, fine".
நான் நன்றாக (naṉṟāka) இருக்கிறேன் — "I am well", the everyday answer to how are you.

What you've built.
Sun, heat, sleepy, and an answer for good days.
