# دو سال سے (do sāl se) — how long, and since when

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[UR-LEX-C273-SUBAH-01, UR-LEX-C75-TIME75-04, UR-LEX-DO] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "the morning" and "a year". (*subah*, *sāl*.) Then "two". (*do*.)

## You'll want to know: since, and for
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-GRAMMAR-C273-DURATION-01]; assesses=[UR-LEX-C75-TIME75-04, UR-LEX-YAHAN, UR-LEX-C50-VERBS50-04, UR-LEX-C41-HEALTH41-02, UR-LEX-C76-TIME76-03] -->

> **دو سال سے۔**
>
> *do sāl se.* — "For two years."

> **میں دو سال سے یہاں رہتا ہوں۔**
>
> *maiṅ do sāl se yahāṅ rahtā hūṅ.* — "I've lived here for two years."

**سے** (*se*), "from", after a time says since when, or for how long. Urdu keeps the present: the living began two years ago and goes on.

> **وہ کل سے بیمار ہے۔**
>
> *voh kal se bīmār hai.* — "She's been ill since yesterday."

## You'll want to know: from … to, all day
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-GRAMMAR-C273-DURATION-02]; assesses=[UR-LEX-C75-TIME75-01, UR-LEX-C189-VAQT189-01, UR-LEX-C74-ADJ74-04, UR-LEX-C74-ADJ74-05, UR-LEX-C273-SUBAH-01] -->

> **میں صبح سے شام تک کام کرتا ہوں۔**
>
> *maiṅ subah se shām tak kām kartā hūṅ.* — "I work from morning to evening."

**سے** opens the span and **تک** (*tak*), "up to", closes it. **بھر** (*bhar*), "full", after a time word fills it:

> **کل دن بھر بارش ہوئی۔**
>
> *kal din bhar bārish huī.* — "Yesterday it rained all day."

**رات بھر** (*rāt bhar*) is "all night".
