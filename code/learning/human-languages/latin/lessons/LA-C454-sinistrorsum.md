## You'll want to know: sinistrōrsum
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C454-DEXTRORSUM-02]; assesses=[] -->

**sinistrōrsum** — "to the left, towards the left".

> Ad compitum sinistrōrsum flectimus et ad flūmen venīmus.
>
> *At the crossroads we turn to the left and come to the river.*

An adverb, also *sinistrōrsus*, built like *dextrōrsum*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*sinistrōrsum* is a shortened *sinistrō-vorsum*, built from *sinister* 'left' and *vorsum* 'turned', from *vertō* 'to turn' (Lewis & Short).
