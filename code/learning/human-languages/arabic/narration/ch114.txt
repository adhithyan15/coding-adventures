Arabic, chapter 114: Goal, Plan, Need, To need, To wish.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
هدف (hadaf) — a goal, an aim.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a dream, then the Arabic for hope.

You'll want to know: هدف.
هدف — hadaf — "a goal, an aim".
