## You'll want to know: die Reue
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C478-REUE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**die Reue** — "remorse, regret".

> Der Täter zeigte keine Reue.
>
> *The culprit showed no remorse.*

No plural.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Reue* is inherited from Old High German *riuwa*, from Proto-Germanic *hrewwō* "pain, sadness, regret" (EtymDB); English *rue* is a cousin.
