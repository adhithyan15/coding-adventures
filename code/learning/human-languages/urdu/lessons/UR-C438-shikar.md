## You'll want to know: شکار
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C438-CHONCH438-05]; assesses=[] -->

**شکار** — *shikār* — "prey; hunting".

> بلی کا شکار ایک چوہا تھا۔
>
> *billī kā shikār ek chūhā thā.*
>
> The cat's prey was a mouse.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**شکار** (*shikār*) is usually traced to Persian *shikār*, "hunting, the chase"; in Urdu it is both the hunt and the animal hunted.
