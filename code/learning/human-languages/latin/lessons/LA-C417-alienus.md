## You'll want to know: aliēnus, aliēna, aliēnum
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C417-POSSESSIO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**aliēnus, aliēna, aliēnum** — "belonging to another; foreign; unfavourable".

> Rem aliēnam cupere nōn dēbēmus.
>
> *We should not desire what belongs to another.*

*aliēnus, aliēna, aliēnum*, built on *alius*, other. The opposite of *suus* and *proprius*. English *alien*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*aliēnus* is built on *alius* "other" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short). English *alien* comes from it through French.
