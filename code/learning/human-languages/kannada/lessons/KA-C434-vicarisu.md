# ವಿಚಾರಿಸು (vicārisu) — to enquire; to ask after

## You'll want to know: ವಿಚಾರಿಸು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C434-VILAMBA434-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ವಿಚಾರಿಸು** — *vicārisu* — "to enquire; to ask after".

> ನಾನು ನಿಲ್ದಾಣದಲ್ಲಿ ರೈಲಿನ ಸಮಯ ವಿಚಾರಿಸಿದೆ.
>
> *nānu nildāṇadalli railina samaya vicāriside.*
>
> I enquired about the train times at the station.

Past (I …): ವಿಚಾರಿಸಿದೆ, *vicāriside*.

What is asked about takes -ಅನ್ನು or no ending; ಆರೋಗ್ಯ ವಿಚಾರಿಸು, to ask after someone's health.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ವಿಚಾರಿಸು** (*vicārisu*) is built from Sanskrit *vicāra*, "deliberation, examination, investigation" (Monier-Williams), with the verb ending -*isu*. The noun ವಿಚಾರ, an idea or a matter, comes from the same Sanskrit word.
