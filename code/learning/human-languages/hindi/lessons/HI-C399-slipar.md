## You'll want to know: स्लीपर
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C399-LAIN399-03]; assesses=[] -->

**स्लीपर** — *slīpar* — "a sleeper carriage; sleeper class".

> मेरा टिकट स्लीपर में है।
>
> *merā ṭikaṭ slīpar meṁ hai.*
>
> My ticket is for the sleeper class.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**स्लीपर** (*slīpar*) is borrowed from English *sleeper*, short for a sleeper carriage, where the seats fold out into berths for the night.
