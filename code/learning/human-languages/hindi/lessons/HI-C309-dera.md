## You'll want to know: डेरा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C309-RAHAT309-03]; assesses=[] -->

**डेरा** — *ḍerā* — "an encampment; a temporary shelter".

> बाढ़ के बाद लोग नदी के किनारे डेरे में रहे।
>
> *bāṛh ke bād log nadī ke kināre ḍere meṁ rahe.*
>
> After the flood people lived in a camp on the riverbank.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**डेरा** (*ḍerā*) is inherited from *\*ḍēra*, a form CDIAL reconstructs (CDIAL 5564), which gathers words for tents and resting places. **डेरे में रहना** is to live in a camp or a shelter, away from home.
