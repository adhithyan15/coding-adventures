## You'll want to know: महसूस करना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C452-BHAVNA452-04]; assesses=[] -->

**महसूस करना** — *mahsūs karnā* — "to feel".

> मीरा ने कंधे में दर्द महसूस किया।
>
> *Mīrā ne kandhe meṁ dard mahsūs kiyā.*
>
> Mira felt a pain in her shoulder.

Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने दर्द महसूस किया, *usne dard mahsūs kiyā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**महसूस करना** (*mahsūs karnā*) joins *mahsūs*, usually traced to Arabic *maḥsūs*, "perceived, felt", which came through Persian, with *karnā*, "to do": to do the feeling of something. The thing felt is the object, so the perfective agrees with it.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
