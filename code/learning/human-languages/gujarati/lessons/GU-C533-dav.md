## You'll want to know: દાવ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C533-PASO533-03]; assesses=[] -->

**દાવ** — *dāv* — "a turn (in a game)".

> જો હવે મારો દાવ હોત તો હું જીતી જાત.
>
> *jo have māro dāv hot to hũ jītī jāt.*
>
> If it were my turn now, I would win.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**દાવ** (*dāv*) is filed by CDIAL under Sanskrit *dātu*, "a share", with the Gujarati word glossed "a turn, a throw" (CDIAL 6258).
