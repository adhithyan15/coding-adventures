Russian, chapter 483: Scratches and Grazes.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
царапина (tsarápina) — a scratch.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a nostril, then the Russian for a jaw.

You'll want to know: царапина.
царапина (tsarápina) — "a scratch".
После игры с кошкой у меня на руке царапина. Pósle igrý s kóshkoy u menyá na ruké tsarápina. After playing with the cat I've got a scratch on my hand.
Feminine. The verb is царапать / поцарапать — to scratch.

The word, taken apart.
царапина (tsarápina) is built on царапать 'to scratch', which EtymDB lists without a source; it is usually taken as imitative.

Lesson 2 of 5.
ушибиться (ushibítsya) — to hurt oneself (by a knock).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a jaw, then the Russian for a scratch.

You'll want to know: ушибиться.
ушибиться (ushibítsya) — "to hurt oneself (by a knock)".
Ребёнок упал и сильно ушибился. Rebyónok upál i sílno ushibílsya. The child fell and hurt himself badly.
Perfective; past ушибся, ушиблась. Imperfective ушибаться. The noun is ушиб — a bruise.

The word, taken apart.
ушибиться (ushibítsya) is the reflexive of ушибить 'to bruise', built on у- and шибить 'to strike'; EtymDB lists ушибить without a source and has no entry for шибить.

Lesson 3 of 5.
порез (poréz) — a cut.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a scratch, then the Russian for to hurt oneself.

You'll want to know: порез.
порез (poréz) — "a cut".
Мама промыла порез и заклеила его пластырем. Máma promýla poréz i zakléila yegó plástyrem. Mum washed the cut and put a plaster on it.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
порез (poréz) is built on по- and the root of резать 'to cut', which is inherited from Old East Slavic and Proto-Slavic rězati (EtymDB).

Lesson 5 of 5.
заживать (zazhivát) — to heal (of a wound).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a cut, then the Russian for a graze.

You'll want to know: заживать.
заживать (zazhivát) — "to heal (of a wound)".
Рана заживает очень медленно. Rána zazhiváyet óchen médlenno. The wound is healing very slowly.
Imperfective; perfective зажить (заживёт). Of a wound or a cut: рана заживает.

The word, taken apart.
заживать (zazhivát) is built on за- and жить 'to live', which is inherited from Proto-Slavic žiti (EtymDB): a wound that heals comes back to life.
