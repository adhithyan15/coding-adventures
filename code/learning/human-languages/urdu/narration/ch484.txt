Urdu, chapter 484: Bringing Up a Child.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
پرورش کرنا (parvarish karnā) — to raise a child; to bring up.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for birth, then the Urdu for a newborn.

You'll want to know: پرورش کرنا.
پرورش کرنا — parvarish karnā — "to raise a child; to bring up".
نانی نے میری پرورش کی۔ nānī ne merī parvarish kī. My grandmother brought me up.
Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے بیٹی کی پرورش کی, us ne beṭī kī parvarish kī.

The word, taken apart.
پرورش کرنا (parvarish karnā) is built from پرورش, usually traced to Persian parvarish, "nurture", and کرنا, "to do".

Lesson 2 of 5.

[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a newborn, then the Urdu for to raise a child.

You'll want to know: لوری.
لوری — lorī — "a lullaby".
ماں رات کو لوری گاتی ہے۔ māṅ rāt ko lorī gātī hai. The mother sings a lullaby at night.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
لوری (lorī) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin lorī under lōḍa, "tossing, swinging", a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 11136): the song of the rocking cradle.

دادی بچے کو جھلا رہی تھیں۔ dādī bachche ko jhulā rahī thīṅ. My grandmother was rocking the baby.
