## You'll want to know: la fase
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C277-DURATA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**la fase** — "the phase, the stage".

> Siamo nella prima fase del lavoro.
>
> *We are in the first phase of the work.*

Also the phases of the moon: *le fasi della luna*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*fase* is a learned borrowing from Greek *phásis* "appearance" (EtymDB), first said of the changing look of the moon.
