## You'll want to know: कचरो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C171-BASAN171-05]; assesses=[] -->

**कचरो** — *kachro* — "rubbish".

> रसोई मांय कचरो कोनी।
>
> *rasoī mā̃y kachro konī.*
>
> There's no rubbish in the kitchen.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**कचरो** (*kachro*) is inherited from Sanskrit *kaccara*, "dirty"; CDIAL lists Hindi *kacrā*, "sweepings, dirt" (CDIAL 2615). CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry.
