## You'll want to know: பனை
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C434-THOPPU434-04]; assesses=[] -->

**பனை** — *paṉai* — "a palmyra palm".

> கடற்கரையோரம் உயரமான பனைமரங்கள் நிற்கின்றன.
>
> *kaṭaṟkaraiyōram uyaramāṉa paṉaimaraṅkaḷ niṟkiṉṟaṉa.*
>
> Tall palmyra palms stand along the shore.

பனைமரம் is the whole tree; பனையோலை, its leaf.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**பனை** (*paṉai*) is native Dravidian (DEDR 4037), "the palmyra palm".
