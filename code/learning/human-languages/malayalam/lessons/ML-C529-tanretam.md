# തന്റേടം (tanṟēṭaṁ) — self-assurance; boldness

## You'll want to know: തന്റേടം
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C529-VIVEKAM529-02]; assesses=[] -->

**തന്റേടം** — *tanṟēṭaṁ* — "self-assurance; boldness".

> ഒറ്റയ്ക്ക് വിദേശത്ത് പോകാൻ അവൾക്ക് തന്റേടം ഉണ്ട്.
>
> *oṟṟaykkŭ vidēśattŭ pōkān avaḷkkŭ tanṟēṭaṁ uṇṭŭ.*
>
> She has the nerve to go abroad on her own.

Praise for a confident person; തന്റേടി can also mean a cheeky one.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**തന്റേടം** (*tanṟēṭaṁ*) seems to begin with തൻ, "self", a native word (DEDR 3196); the rest of the word is not settled.
