Marathi, chapter 305: Last Year and Before.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
गतवर्षी (gatvarṣī) — last year.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to make a stir, then the Marathi for to spread.

You'll want to know: गतवर्षी.
गतवर्षी — gatvarṣī — "last year".
गतवर्षी आमच्या गावात मोठी जत्रा भरली होती. gatvarṣī āmacyā gāvāt moṭhī jatrā bharlī hotī. Last year a big fair was held in our village.
Formal; in speech मागच्या वर्षी. The letter ञ hides inside ज्ञ: ज्ञान, knowledge.

The word, taken apart.
गतवर्षी (gatvarṣī) is built from Sanskrit gata, 'gone', and varṣa, 'a year' (Monier-Williams), in the Marathi locative: in the year gone by.

What you've built.

मागील — māgīl — "previous; the last".
मागील बैठकीत हा प्रश्न आला होता. māgīl baiṭhkīt hā praśna ālā hotā. This question came up at the previous meeting.
Formal; it keeps one form: मागील (māgīl) आठवडा, मागील (māgīl) बैठक.

The word, taken apart.
मागील (māgīl) is built on māg, 'a track; behind', which CDIAL lists as Marathi 'road, track' under Sanskrit mārga (CDIAL 10071), with the ending -īl.

Lesson 4 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for previous, then the Marathi for before that.

You'll want to know: कालांतराने.
कालांतराने — kālāntarāne — "over time; as time went by".
कालांतराने गावाचं शहर झालं. kālāntarāne gāvācaṁ śahar jhālaṁ. As time went by, the village became a town.
Built from Sanskrit kāla, time, and antara, an interval, with -ने: as time went by.

The word, taken apart.
कालांतराने (kālāntarāne) is built on Sanskrit kālāntara, 'an interval of time' (Monier-Williams), with the Marathi instrumental -ne.

Lesson 5 of 5.
लोटणे (loṭṇe) — to elapse; to push.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for before that, then the Marathi for over time.

You'll want to know: लोटणे.
लोटणे — loṭṇe — "to elapse; to push".
त्या घटनेला आता दहा वर्षं लोटली. tyā ghaṭnelā ātā dahā varṣaṁ loṭlī. Ten years have now passed since that event.
Past (he …): तो लोटला, to loṭlā.
Of time, with -ला: घटनेला वर्षं लोटली, years have passed since the event. Also to push: दार लोटणे (loṭṇe).

The word, taken apart.
लोटणे (loṭṇe) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi loṭṇẽ, 'to roll, wallow', under lōrtati, 'rolls' (CDIAL 11156). The senses 'to push' and, of time, 'to pass' are most likely the same verb: time rolls on.
