## You'll want to know: श्राद्धम्
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C491-JARA491-05]; assesses=[] -->

**श्राद्धम्** — *śrāddham* — "a rite for the ancestors".

> पुत्रः पितुः श्राद्धं करोति।
>
> *putraḥ pituḥ śrāddhaṃ karoti.*
>
> The son performs the rite for his late father.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**श्राद्धम्** (*śrāddham*) is built on *śraddhā*, "faith"; MW gives "a ceremony in honour and for the benefit of dead relatives" (MW).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
