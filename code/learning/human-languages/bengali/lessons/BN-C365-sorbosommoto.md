## You'll want to know: সর্বসম্মত
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C365-SOMJHOTA365-05]; assesses=[] -->

**সর্বসম্মত** — *sôrbôsômmôtô* — "unanimous".

> সিদ্ধান্তটা সর্বসম্মত ছিল।
>
> *siddhāntôṭā sôrbôsômmôtô chhilô.*
>
> The decision was unanimous.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**সর্বসম্মত** (*sôrbôsômmôtô*) is Sanskrit *sarva*, all, with *sammata*, agreed or approved (Monier-Williams): agreed by everyone.
