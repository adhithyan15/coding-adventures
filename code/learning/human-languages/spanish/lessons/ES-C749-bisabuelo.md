## You'll want to know: el bisabuelo
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C749-EDADES-04]; assesses=[] -->

**el bisabuelo** — "the great-grandfather".

> Mi bisabuelo vivió cien años.
>
> *My great-grandfather lived to a hundred.*

*Los bisabuelos* — great-grandparents.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*bisabuelo* is built on Latin *bis* "twice" and *abuelo* "grandfather", which is usually traced to Latin *aviolus*, from *avus* "grandfather".
