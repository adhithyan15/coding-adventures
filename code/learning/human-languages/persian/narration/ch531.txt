Persian, chapter 531: On Screen.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
مستند (mostanad) — a documentary.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for tragic, then the Persian for funny.

You'll want to know: مستند.
مستند (mostanad) — "a documentary".
او مستندهای طبیعت را دوست دارد. u mostanadhâ-ye tabi'at râ dust dârad. He likes nature documentaries.
Also an adjective 'documented': film-e mostanad.

The word, taken apart.
mostanad is a loan, usually traced to Arabic mustanad, 'a document, something relied on'. EtymDB has no entry for it.

Lesson 3 of 5.
زیرنویس (zirnevis) — subtitles.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a documentary, then the Persian for a cartoon.

You'll want to know: زیرنویس.
زیرنویس (zirnevis) — "subtitles".
من فیلمهای خارجی را با زیرنویس میبینم. man filmhâ-ye khâreji râ bâ zirnevis mibinam. I watch foreign films with subtitles.
zir 'under' + nevis 'writing'.

The word, taken apart.
zirnevis is built on native Persian zir, 'under', usually traced to Middle Persian azēr, and nevis, the present stem of neveshtan, 'to write': under-writing.

دوبله (duble) — "dubbed; dubbing".
پدربزرگ فیلمهای دوبله را بیشتر دوست دارد. pedarbozorg filmhâ-ye duble râ bishtar dust dârad. Grandfather prefers dubbed films.

duble is a modern loan, usually traced to French doublé, 'doubled'. EtymDB has no entry for it.
