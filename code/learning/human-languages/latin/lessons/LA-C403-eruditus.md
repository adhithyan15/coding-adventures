# ērudītus, ērudīta, ērudītum — educated, learned

## You'll want to know: ērudītus, ērudīta, ērudītum
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C403-DOCTUS-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ērudītus, ērudīta, ērudītum** — "educated, learned".

> Fēmina ērudīta multōs librōs lēgerat.
>
> *The educated woman had read many books.*

*ērudītus, ērudīta, ērudītum*, the participle of *ērudiō*. English *erudite*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ērudītus* is the participle of *ērudiō* "to educate" (Lewis & Short), from *ē* and *rudis* "rough". English *erudite* comes from it (EtymDB).
