# ش (shīn) — the letter that opens شكرا

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[AR-CONCEPT-W10-AYN-01, AR-CONCEPT-W11-HA-AND-TA-MARBUTA-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: write ع and ه once each, and name them.

[PAUSE 2s] Now say **شكرا** (*shukran*), "thank you".
You have read it for a long time. This lesson takes one letter out of it and puts
it in your hand.

## Script you'll notice: ش
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-SCRIPT-C46-SHIN-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ش** — *shīn*, the sound *sh*.

It opens **شكرا** (*shukran*), "thank you", one of the first words in this book.
You already write **س** (*sīn*): three small teeth and a bowl. **ش** is the same
body with **three dots above**. One skeleton, and the dots decide the sound.

## Writing: ش
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[AR-SCRIPT-C46-SHIN-01] -->

- **1.** start here: shape the three close teeth from right to left
- **2.** flow directly into the final bowl without lifting
- **3.** lift, then place the lower-left dot
- **4.** lift again, then place the lower-right dot
- **5.** lift a third time, then place the centered upper dot — and only now lift

**Pen lifts: 3.**

> Stroke order is one attested teaching order; hands and teachers vary.
> Source: Introduction to Arabic: Egyptian Arabic for first-year students, Alphabet: س ش ص ض, independent Shiin at 00:00.7–00:03.0 (University of Oregon Libraries Open Textbook, 2023).
