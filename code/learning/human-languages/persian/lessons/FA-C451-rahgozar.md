## You'll want to know: رهگذر
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C451-SHOLUGHI-04]; assesses=[] -->

**رهگذر** (*rahgozar*) — "a passer-by".

> یک رهگذر راه ایستگاه را به ما نشان داد.
>
> *Yek rahgozar râh-e istgâh râ be mâ neshân dâd.*
>
> A passer-by showed us the way to the station.

*rah* (a short form of *râh*) + *gozar* 'passing'.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*rahgozar* is a native compound of *rah*, a short form of *râh* 'road' (Middle Persian *rāh*, EtymDB), and *gozar*, the present stem of *gozashtan* 'to pass'.
