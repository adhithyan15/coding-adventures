## You'll want to know: pedalare
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C416-MOTORINO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**pedalare** — "to pedal, to cycle".

> Abbiamo pedalato per venti chilometri.
>
> *We cycled twenty kilometres.*

In speech, *pedala!* also means get moving!

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*pedalare* is built on *pedale* 'pedal', a learned borrowing from Latin *pedālis* 'of the foot' (EtymDB), from *pēs* 'foot'.
