## You'll want to know: дворник
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C406-BAGAZHNIK-05]; assesses=[] -->

**дворник** (*dvórnik*) — "a windscreen wiper; a caretaker, a street sweeper".

> Шёл дождь, и дворники работали всю дорогу.
>
> *Shyol dozhd, i dvórniki rabótali vsyu dorógu.*
>
> It was raining, and the wipers worked all the way.

The first meaning is a person who sweeps a yard; the wipers sweep the glass.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*дворник* is built on *двор* 'a yard' (EtymDB), which is inherited from Proto-Slavic *dvorŭ* 'a court'. EtymDB glosses *дворник* as the one who keeps the yard and the pavement; the wiper that sweeps the glass took the name later.
