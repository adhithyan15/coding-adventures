Arabic, chapter 53: Fever, Hospital, Ill, Pain, Medicine.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
حمى (ḥummā) — a fever.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for the back, then the Arabic for the heart.

You'll want to know: حمى.
حمى — ḥummā — "a fever".

The word, taken apart.
حمى (ḥummā) is built on the native Arabic root ḥ-m-m, "to be hot, to heat" (Lane). Fever is named on the root of heat, like ḥammām, "a hot bath".
