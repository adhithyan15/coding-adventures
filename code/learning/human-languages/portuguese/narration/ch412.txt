Portuguese, chapter 412: Em duas rodas.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
a motorizada — the moped.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for the mileage, then the Portuguese for the excess.

You'll want to know: a motorizada.
a motorizada — "the moped".
O meu avô ia para o trabalho de motorizada. My grandfather used to go to work by moped.
A mota is a bigger motorbike. In Brazil: a mobilete.

The word, taken apart.
motorizada is built on motor 'engine', a learned borrowing from Latin mōtor 'mover', from movēre 'to move' (Lewis & Short).

What you've built.

Muitos turistas andam de trotinete no centro. Many tourists ride scooters in the centre.

trotinete was borrowed from French trottinette, built on trottiner 'to trot along in small steps' with the small-size ending -ette (EtymDB).

o selim — "the saddle (of a bicycle)".
O selim da bicicleta está muito alto. The bicycle saddle is too high.
A horse's saddle is a sela.

The word, taken apart.
selim is a small form of sela 'saddle', which is inherited from Latin sella 'seat' (EtymDB).
