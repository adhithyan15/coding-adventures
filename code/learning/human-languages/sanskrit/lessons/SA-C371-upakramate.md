## You'll want to know: उपक्रमते
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C371-VIKALPA371-05]; assesses=[] -->

**उपक्रमते** — *upakramate* — "sets about; undertakes".

> यदि योजना सज्जा अस्ति, तर्हि सः कार्यम् उपक्रमते।
>
> *yadi yojanā sajjā asti, tarhi saḥ kāryam upakramate.*
>
> If the plan is ready, he sets about the work.

Past (he …): सः कार्यम् उपक्रान्तवान्, *saḥ kāryam upakrāntavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**उपक्रमते** (*upakramate*) is built on *upa*, "towards", and the root √kram, "to step"; MW gives *upa-√kram* "to approach" and "to set about, to undertake, to begin" (MW).
