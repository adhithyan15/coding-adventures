## You'll want to know: चुलत
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C497-THORLA497-04]; assesses=[] -->

**चुलत** — *culat* — "of a paternal uncle; cousin on the father's side".

> माझा चुलत भाऊ आणि मी एकाच वर्गात आहोत.
>
> *mājhā culat bhāū āṇi mī ekāc vargāt āhot.*
>
> My cousin and I are in the same class.

Unchanging: चुलत भाऊ, चुलत बहीण, the children of a father's brother.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**चुलत** (*culat*) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi *cultā*, "father's brother", under the reconstructed *\*culla*, "small" (CDIAL 4877).
