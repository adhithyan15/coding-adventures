Spanish, chapter 636: Decir la verdad.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
mentir — to lie, to tell lies.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for the consistency, then the Spanish for willing.

You'll want to know: mentir.
mentir — "to lie, to tell lies".
No me mientas. Do not lie to me.
Like sentir: miento, mintió.

The word, taken apart.
mentir is inherited from Latin mentiri 'to lie, to invent', usually linked with mens 'mind' (EtymDB).

Lesson 3 of 5.
engañar — to deceive, to cheat.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for to lie, then the Spanish for the lie.

You'll want to know: engañar.
engañar — "to deceive, to cheat".
El vendedor me engañó. The seller cheated me.
Engañar a someone. It can also mean to be unfaithful.

The word, taken apart.
engañar is usually traced to spoken Latin ingannare 'to mock, to trick'; EtymDB has no parent for it, and the origin is not settled.
