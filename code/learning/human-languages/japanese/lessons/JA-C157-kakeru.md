## You'll want to know: かける
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C157-TABLE157-04]; assesses=[] -->

**かける** — *kakeru* — "to hang; to hang up".

> **うわぎを いすに かけます。** — *uwagi o isu ni kakemasu* — "I hang my jacket on the chair."

Polite **かけます**, *kakemasu*; an ichidan verb.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**かける** (*kakeru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi, and its older origin is not settled (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
