## You'll want to know: el exceso
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C668-EQUIPAJE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**el exceso** — "the excess".

> Pagué cincuenta euros por exceso de equipaje.
>
> *I paid fifty euros for excess baggage.*

*Exceso de equipaje* — excess baggage.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*exceso* is a learned borrowing from Latin *excessus* "a going out, a departure" (Lewis & Short), from *excēdere* "to go beyond", built on *cēdere* "to go".
