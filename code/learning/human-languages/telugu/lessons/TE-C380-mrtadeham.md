# మృతదేహం (mṛtadēhaṁ) — a dead body

## You'll want to know: మృతదేహం
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C380-KRITAM380-04]; assesses=[] -->

**మృతదేహం** — *mṛtadēhaṁ* — "a dead body".

> నది ఒడ్డున ఒక మృతదేహం కనిపించింది.
>
> *nadi oḍḍuna oka mṛtadēhaṁ kanipiñcindi.*
>
> A dead body was found on the riverbank.

Plural: మృతదేహాలు, *mṛtadēhālu*.

మృత, dead, and దేహం, a body: a news word.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**మృతదేహం** (*mṛtadēhaṁ*) is borrowed from Sanskrit: *mṛta*, "dead", and *deha*, "the body" (Monier-Williams).
