## You'll want to know: obsōnium, obsōniī
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C350-OBSONO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**obsōnium, obsōniī** — "food to go with bread, provisions".

> Obsōnium in culīnam portāvit: piscēs et holera.
>
> *He carried the provisions into the kitchen: fish and vegetables.*

Neuter. Anything eaten with bread, very often fish.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**obsōnium** was borrowed from Greek *opsōnion*, "provisions, what is eaten with bread" (EtymDB).
