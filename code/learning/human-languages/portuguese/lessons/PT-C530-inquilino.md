## You'll want to know: o inquilino
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C530-SENHORIO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**o inquilino** — "tenant".

> Os inquilinos do segundo andar são muito simpáticos.
>
> *The tenants on the second floor are very friendly.*

A woman is *a inquilina*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*inquilino* is a learned borrowing from Latin *inquilīnus* "a tenant, a lodger" (EtymDB).
