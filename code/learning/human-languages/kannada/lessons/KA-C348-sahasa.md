## You'll want to know: ಸಾಹಸ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C348-NIDHI348-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸಾಹಸ** — *sāhasa* — "an adventure; a daring act".

> ಕಾಡಿನಲ್ಲಿ ನಮ್ಮ ಮೂರು ದಿನದ ಸಾಹಸ ಮರೆಯಲು ಆಗುವುದಿಲ್ಲ.
>
> *kāḍinalli namma mūru dinada sāhasa mareyalu āguvudilla.*
>
> Our three-day adventure in the forest is impossible to forget.

Plural: ಸಾಹಸಗಳು, *sāhasagaḷu*.

A daring person is a ಸಾಹಸಿ.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸಾಹಸ** (*sāhasa*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *sāhasa*, "boldness, rashness" (CDIAL 13378).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
