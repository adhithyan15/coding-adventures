Sanskrit, chapter 149: Worker, Labourer, Official, Clerk, Leader.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
कर्मकरः (karmakaraḥ) — a worker, a labourer.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a fisherman, then the Sanskrit for a cowherd.

You'll want to know: कर्मकरः.
कर्मकरः — karmakaraḥ — "a worker, a labourer".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: karmakaraḥ]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: karmakaraḥ, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say karmakaraḥ]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Sanskrit for a fisherman, then the Sanskrit for a cowherd, then say karmakaraḥ again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does कर्मकरः (karmakaraḥ) mean? ("A worker, a labourer".) Say it once more.

Lesson 6 of 7.
Words from chapters 141-144 — a first pass.

Warm-up.
[pause 3 seconds]
Say the words for a clerk and a leader.

Guided Practice.
[pause 2 seconds after each]
[your turn — say: drinks, dwells, moves about, touches, leaves]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: is pleased, gets tired, composes, shows, worships]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: comes back, lies down, sits, is afraid, meditates]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: shares, weighs, flies up, puts in, joins]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
Drinks? (पिबति, pibati.) Dwells? (वसति, vasati.) Moves about? (चरति, carati.) Touches? (स्पृशति, spṛśati.) Leaves? (त्यजति, tyajati.) Is pleased? (तुष्यति, tuṣyati.) Gets tired? (श्राम्यति, śrāmyati.) Composes? (रचयति, racayati.) Shows? (दर्शयति, darśayati.) Worships? (पूजयति, pūjayati.) Comes back? (प्रत्यागच्छति, pratyāgacchati.) Lies down? (शेते, śete.) Sits? (आस्ते, āste.) Is afraid? (बिभेति, bibheti.) Meditates? (ध्यायति, dhyāyati.) Shares? (भजति, bhajati.) Weighs? (तोलयति, tolayati.) Flies up? (उत्पतति, utpatati.) Puts in? (निक्षिपति, nikṣipati.) Joins? (योजयति, yojayati.)
