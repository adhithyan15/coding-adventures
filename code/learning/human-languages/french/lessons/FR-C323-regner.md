## You'll want to know: régner
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C323-REGNE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**régner** — "to reign, to rule".

> Ce roi a régné pendant quarante ans.
>
> *This king reigned for forty years.*

Figurative: *le silence régnait* — silence reigned.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*régner* comes from Old French *reignier*, from Latin *rēgnāre* "to rule" (EtymDB), built on *rēgnum* "kingship", from *rēx* "king" (Lewis & Short).
