## You'll want to know: যতক্ষণ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C368-SHORTO368-04]; assesses=[] -->

**যতক্ষণ** — *jôtôkkhôn* — "as long as".

> ঝড় না থামলে আমরা যতক্ষণ দরকার এখানে থাকব।
>
> *jhôṛ nā thāmle āmrā jôtôkkhôn dôrkār ekhāne thākbô.*
>
> If the storm doesn't stop, we'll stay here as long as we need to.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**যতক্ষণ** (*jôtôkkhôn*) is যত, 'as much as', with Sanskrit *kṣaṇa*, a moment (Monier-Williams): for as many moments as. It is often answered by ততক্ষণ, 'for that long'.
