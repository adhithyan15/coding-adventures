## You'll want to know: ಹಿಂಸೆ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C365-HODEDATA365-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ಹಿಂಸೆ** — *hiṃse* — "violence; cruelty".

> ಗಲಭೆಯಲ್ಲಿ ತುಂಬಾ ಹಿಂಸೆ ನಡೆಯಿತು.
>
> *galabheyalli tumbā hiṃse naḍeyitu.*
>
> There was a lot of violence in the riot.

ಹಿಂಸೆ ಕೊಡು is to torment; ಅಹಿಂಸೆ is non-violence.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಹಿಂಸೆ** (*hiṃse*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *hiṃsā*, "injury, harm, hurt" (Monier-Williams).
