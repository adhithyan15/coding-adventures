## You'll want to know: bàn
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C48-CLOCK48-03]; assesses=[] -->

*bàn* — "half".

> *bā diǎn bàn* — Half past eight.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*bàn* is inherited from Middle Chinese *panH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'a thing divided in the middle'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for times and partings.
