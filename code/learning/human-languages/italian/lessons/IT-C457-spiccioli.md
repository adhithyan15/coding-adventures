## You'll want to know: gli spiccioli
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C457-CASSA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**gli spiccioli** — "the small change, the coins".

> Non ho spiccioli per il caffè.
>
> *I have no change for the coffee.*

The singular *lo spicciolo* is rare.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*spiccioli* is the plural of *spicciolo* 'small coin'. The origin is not settled; it is often linked to *spicciare* 'to sort out'.
