## You'll want to know: yáokòngqì
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C171-SWITCH171-03]; assesses=[] -->

*yáokòngqì* — "remote control".

> *yáokòngqì zài nǎr* — Where's the remote?

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*yáokòngqì* is built from *yáo* 'far', *kòng* 'to control' and *qì* 'device'. The last is inherited from Middle Chinese *khijH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'a vessel'; the first two are listed there as *yew*, glossed 'far', and *khuwngH*, glossed 'to pull': a device that pulls from far away.
