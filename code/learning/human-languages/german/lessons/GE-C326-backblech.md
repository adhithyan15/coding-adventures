## You'll want to know: das Backblech
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C326-VORHEIZEN-03]; assesses=[] -->

**das Backblech** — "the baking tray".

> Die Kekse lagen auf dem Backblech.
>
> *The biscuits lay on the baking tray.*

Plural: *die Backbleche*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Backblech* joins *backen* "to bake", a cousin of English *bake* (EtymDB), and *Blech* "sheet metal", an inherited Germanic word.
