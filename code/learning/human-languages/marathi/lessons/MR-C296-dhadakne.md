## You'll want to know: धडकणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C296-DHADAK296-01]; assesses=[] -->

**धडकणे** — *dhaḍakṇe* — "to collide; to crash".

> भरधाव गाडी झाडावर धडकली.
>
> *bhardhāv gāḍī jhāḍāvar dhaḍaklī.*
>
> The speeding car crashed into a tree.

Past (he …): तो धडकला, *to dhaḍaklā*.

What is hit takes -वर or -ला: भिंतीवर धडकणे, to crash into a wall.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**धडकणे** (*dhaḍakṇe*) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi *dhaḍakṇe*, 'to throb', under *\*dhaḍ*, 'throb, beat' (CDIAL 6711); the sense of a crash grows from the thud.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
