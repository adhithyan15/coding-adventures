## You'll want to know: हुंदका
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C457-KHED457-03]; assesses=[] -->

**हुंदका** — *hundkā* — "a sob".

> निरोप घेताना तिला हुंदका आला.
>
> *nirop ghetānā tilā hundkā ālā.*
>
> A sob caught in her throat as she said goodbye.

Masculine.

हुंदका येणे, a sob comes, with the dative; हुंदके देणे, to sob.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**हुंदका** (*hundkā*) is a Marathi noun whose origin is not settled.

## What you've built
