Kannada, chapter 489: Cold Nights and Clear Skies.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
ಇಬ್ಬನಿ (ibbani) — dew.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a crack, then the Kannada for to subside.

You'll want to know: ಇಬ್ಬನಿ.
ಇಬ್ಬನಿ — ibbani — "dew".
ಬೆಳಗ್ಗೆ ಹುಲ್ಲಿನ ಮೇಲೆ ಇಬ್ಬನಿ ಇತ್ತು. beḷagge hullina mēle ibbani ittu. In the morning there was dew on the grass.
Drops on leaves at dawn after a cold night.

The word, taken apart.
ಇಬ್ಬನಿ (ibbani) is native Dravidian: DEDR 522 lists Kannada ibbani, "fog, mist, dew".

Lesson 2 of 7.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to subside, then the Kannada for dew.

You'll want to know: ಕುಳಿರ್ಗಾಳಿ.
ಕುಳಿರ್ಗಾಳಿ — kuḷirgāḷi — "a chill wind".
ರಾತ್ರಿ ಬೆಟ್ಟದ ಮೇಲೆ ಕುಳಿರ್ಗಾಳಿ ಬೀಸುತ್ತಿತ್ತು. rātri beṭṭada mēle kuḷirgāḷi bīsuttittu. At night a chill wind was blowing on the hill.
ಕುಳಿರ್, cold, plus ಗಾಳಿ, wind.

The word, taken apart.
ಕುಳಿರ್ಗಾಳಿ (kuḷirgāḷi) is a native Kannada compound of kuḷir, "to be cool or cold; coldness" (DEDR 1834), and ಗಾಳಿ, "wind" (DEDR 1499).

ಚಳಿಗಾಲದಲ್ಲಿ ಪರ್ವತದ ಮೇಲೆ ಮಂಜುಗಡ್ಡೆ ಇರುತ್ತದೆ. caḷigāladalli parvatada mēle mañjugaḍḍe iruttade. In winter there is ice on the mountain.

[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a chill wind, then the Kannada for ice.

You'll want to know: ಹೆಪ್ಪುಗಟ್ಟು.
ಹೆಪ್ಪುಗಟ್ಟು — heppugaṭṭu — "to freeze; to set".
ರಾತ್ರಿ ಚಳಿಗೆ ಬಕೆಟ್ನ ನೀರು ಹೆಪ್ಪುಗಟ್ಟಿತು. rātri caḷige bakeṭna nīru heppugaṭṭitu. In the cold of the night the water in the bucket froze.
Past (it …): ಹೆಪ್ಪುಗಟ್ಟಿತು, heppugaṭṭitu.
In the kitchen it is milk setting into curd: ಹೆಪ್ಪು is the starter.

The word, taken apart.
ಹೆಪ್ಪುಗಟ್ಟು (heppugaṭṭu) is a native Kannada compound of heppu, "a curdling agent" (DEDR 4421), and ಕಟ್ಟು, "to gather, to harden" (DEDR 1148).

ಮಿನುಗು (minugu) — to twinkle; to glitter.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for ice, then the Kannada for to freeze.

You'll want to know: ಮಿನುಗು.
ಮಿನುಗು — minugu — "to twinkle; to glitter".
ಕತ್ತಲೆಯಲ್ಲಿ ಆಕಾಶದಲ್ಲಿ ಚುಕ್ಕಿಗಳು ಮಿನುಗುತ್ತಿದ್ದವು. kattaleyalli ākāśadalli cukkigaḷu minuguttiddavu. In the dark the stars were twinkling in the sky.
Past (it …): ಮಿನುಗಿತು, minugitu.
Of stars, lights and jewels.

The word, taken apart.
ಮಿನುಗು (minugu) is native Dravidian: DEDR 4876 lists Kannada miṇuku, minuku, minugu, "to glitter, to sparkle", the family of ಮಿಂಚು, lightning.
