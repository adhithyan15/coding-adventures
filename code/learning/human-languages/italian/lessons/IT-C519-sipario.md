## You'll want to know: il sipario
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C519-TEATRO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**il sipario** — "curtain (in a theatre)".

> Si alza il sipario e comincia lo spettacolo.
>
> *The curtain rises and the show begins.*

Only for the theatre; at home, *la tenda*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*sipario* comes from Latin *sīparium* "a stage curtain" (EtymDB), from Greek *síparos* "a topsail".
