## You'll want to know: うちこむ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C533-WORTH533-04]; assesses=[] -->

**うちこむ** — *uchikomu* — "to throw oneself (into)".

> **もし べんきょうに うちこんだら、いもうとは しけんに うかるでしょう。** — *moshi benkyō ni uchikondara, imōto wa shiken ni ukaru deshō* — "If my younger sister throws herself into her studies, she will pass the exam."

Polite **うちこみます**, *uchikomimasu*; a godan verb. Past (polite): **あねが れんしゅうに うちこみました。**, *ane ga renshū ni uchikomimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**うちこむ** (*uchikomu*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi, and its older origin is not settled (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
