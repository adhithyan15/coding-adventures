## You'll want to know: معقول
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C354-MAQUL354-01]; assesses=[] -->

**معقول** — *māqūl* — "reasonable".

> یہ قیمت معقول ہے۔
>
> *yih qīmat māqūl hai.*
>
> This price is reasonable.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**معقول** (*māqūl*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *maʿqūl*, "understood; rational".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
