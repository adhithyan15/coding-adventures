## You'll want to know: o mestrado
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C377-DOCENTE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**o mestrado** — "the master's degree".

> Ele está a fazer um mestrado em engenharia.
>
> *He is doing a master's degree in engineering.*

*Fazer um mestrado* — to do a master's.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*mestrado* is built on *mestre* "master" with *-ado* (EtymDB); *mestre* is inherited from Latin *magister* "master, teacher" (Lewis & Short).
