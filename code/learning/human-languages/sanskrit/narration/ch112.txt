Lesson 1 of 5.
पद्मम् (padmam) — a lotus.

Warm-up.

You'll want to know: पद्मम्.
पद्मम् — padmam — "a lotus".

The word, taken apart.
MW links padma "a lotus" to pad with a query mark, so its origin is uncertain. It lives on in Kashmiri pam-pōś "lotus" (CDIAL 7769).

Lesson 2 of 5.
नक्षत्रम् (nakṣatram) — a star.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a flower, then the Sanskrit for a lotus.

You'll want to know: नक्षत्रम्.
नक्षत्रम् — nakṣatram — "a star".

The word, taken apart.
nakṣatra "a star", and later a lunar mansion, is old, in the Rigveda; MW thinks it probably comes from the root √nakṣ "approach, reach". It lives on as Hindi nakhat "star, constellation" (CDIAL 6913).

Lesson 3 of 5.
सूर्यः (sūryaḥ) — the sun.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a lotus, then the Sanskrit for a star.

You'll want to know: सूर्यः.
सूर्यः — sūryaḥ — "the sun".

The word, taken apart.
sūrya "the sun" is inherited from Indo-European (EtymDB): MW sets its base svar "sun, light" beside Latin sol and Greek hēlios, the source of English solar and helium. Hindi sūraj "sun" comes from it (EtymDB).

Lesson 4 of 5.
भल्लूकः (bhallūkaḥ) — a bear.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a star, then the Sanskrit for the sun.

You'll want to know: भल्लूकः.
भल्लूकः — bhallūkaḥ — "a bear".

The word, taken apart.
bhallūka "a bear" has a disputed origin: CDIAL 9415 reports EWA's view that it may be Indo-European with an expressive -ll-, and Przyluski's that it is Austroasiatic. It lives on as Hindi bhālū "bear" (CDIAL 9415).

Lesson 5 of 5.
शृगालः (śṛgālaḥ) — a jackal.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for the sun, then the Sanskrit for a bear.

You'll want to know: शृगालः.
शृगालः — śṛgālaḥ — "a jackal".

The word, taken apart.
MW calls śṛgāla "a jackal" (also written sṛgāla) of doubtful derivation, so its origin is uncertain; it is old, in the Śatapatha Brāhmaṇa. It lives on as Hindi siyār and Bengali siyāl "jackal" (CDIAL 12578).
