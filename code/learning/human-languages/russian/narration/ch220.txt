Lesson 1 of 5.
планшет (planshét) — a tablet (computer).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a vacuum cleaner, then the Russian for an oven.

You'll want to know: планшет.
планшет (planshét) — "a tablet (computer)".

The word, taken apart.
планшет (planshét) comes from French planchette, "a small board, a drawing board", from planche, "a plank".

пульт (pult) — "a remote control".

The word, taken apart.
пульт (pult) came through German Pult, "a desk", older pulpet, from Latin pulpitum, "a platform" (Vasmer). A remote is a little control desk.
