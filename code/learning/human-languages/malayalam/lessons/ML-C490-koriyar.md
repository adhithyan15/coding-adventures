## You'll want to know: കൊറിയർ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C490-TAPAL490-02]; assesses=[] -->

**കൊറിയർ** — *koṟiyar* — "a courier".

> പുസ്തകങ്ങൾ കൊറിയറിൽ ഇന്നലെ എത്തി.
>
> *pustakaṅṅaḷ koṟiyaṟil innale etti.*
>
> The books came by courier yesterday.

It names both the company and the parcel it brings.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**കൊറിയർ** (*koṟiyar*) was borrowed from English *courier*, a firm that delivers parcels.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for getting around.
