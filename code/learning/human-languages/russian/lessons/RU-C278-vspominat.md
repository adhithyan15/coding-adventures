## You'll want to know: вспоминать
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C278-VPERVYYE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**вспоминать** (*vspominát*) — "to recall, to remember".

> Бабушка часто вспоминает свою деревню.
>
> *Bábushka chásto vspomináyet svoyú derévnyu.*
>
> Grandma often recalls her village.

Imperfective; perfective *вспомнить* (*vspómnit*). It takes the accusative or *о* + prepositional. *Помнить* is to keep in memory; *вспоминать* is to bring something back to mind.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*вспоминать* is built on *вс-* "up" and *помнить* "to remember", from the Slavic root of *память* "memory"; EtymDB lists no parent for the compound.
