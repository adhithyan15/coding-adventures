## You'll want to know: a madrasta
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C492-FAMILIA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**a madrasta** — "stepmother".

> A minha madrasta trata-me como uma filha.
>
> *My stepmother treats me like a daughter.*

A neutral word in everyday use, though fairy tales made *a madrasta má* a stock villain.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*madrasta* is usually traced to Late Latin *mātrastra* "stepmother", built on *māter* "mother" (Lewis & Short).
