## You'll want to know: venēficus, venēficī
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C307-VENENUM-02]; assesses=[] -->

**venēficus, venēficī** — "a poisoner; a sorcerer".

> Venēficus herbās perīculōsās in hortō alēbat.
>
> *The poisoner grew dangerous herbs in his garden.*

Masculine; the feminine *venēfica* is the witch of Roman stories.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**venēficus** joins *venēnum*, "poison", and *faciō*, "to make" (EtymDB, Lewis & Short): a maker of poisons and charms.
