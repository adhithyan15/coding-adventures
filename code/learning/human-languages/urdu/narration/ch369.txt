Urdu, chapter 369: If Only.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
کاش (kāsh) — if only; I wish.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for to get stuck, then the Urdu for to deal with.

You'll want to know: کاش.
کاش — kāsh — "if only; I wish".
کاش میرے پاس وقت ہوتا! kāsh mere pās vaqt hotā! If only I had the time!

The word, taken apart.
کاش (kāsh) is usually traced to Persian kāsh, "would that".

Lesson 4 of 5.
ستانا (satānā) — to pester; to torment.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a longing, then the Urdu for a wistful regret.

You'll want to know: ستانا.
ستانا — satānā — "to pester; to torment".
اگر تم بلی کو ستاؤ گے تو وہ کاٹے گی۔ agar tum billī ko satāo ge to voh kāṭe gī. If you pester the cat, she will bite.
Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے بلی کو ستایا, us ne billī ko satāyā.

The word, taken apart.
ستانا (satānā) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin satānā, "to persecute, to annoy", under Sanskrit saṃtāpayati, "inflames" (CDIAL 12886).

Lesson 5 of 5.
گنوانا (ganvānā) — to waste; to throw away (time, a chance).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a wistful regret, then the Urdu for to pester.

You'll want to know: گنوانا.
گنوانا — ganvānā — "to waste; to throw away (time, a chance)".
اگر تم وقت نہ گنواتے تو امتحان پاس کرتے۔ agar tum vaqt na ganvāte to imtihān pās karte. If you had not wasted time, you would have passed the exam.
Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے وقت گنوایا, us ne vaqt ganvāyā.

The word, taken apart.
گنوانا (ganvānā) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin gãwānā, "to squander, to lose", under Sanskrit gamáyati, "makes go" (CDIAL 4028).
