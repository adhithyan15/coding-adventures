German, chapter 408: Wer fährt zuerst.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
die Vorfahrt — the right of way.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for the fine, then the German for the penalty ticket.

You'll want to know: die Vorfahrt.
die Vorfahrt — "the right of way".
An dieser Kreuzung hat der Bus Vorfahrt. At this junction the bus has right of way.
Vorfahrt achten — give way, on a sign. In Austria: der Vorrang; in Switzerland: der Vortritt.

The word, taken apart.
Vorfahrt is built from vor 'before, ahead' and Fahrt 'drive', from the inherited verb fahren (EtymDB).

Lesson 2 of 5.
überholen — to overtake; to overhaul.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for the penalty ticket, then the German for the right of way.

You'll want to know: überholen.
überholen — "to overtake; to overhaul".
Der LKW überholt uns auf der Landstraße. The lorry overtakes us on the country road.
Inseparable: er überholt, er hat überholt. Überholen also means to overhaul a machine.

The word, taken apart.
überholen is built from über 'over, past' and holen 'to fetch'; EtymDB lists Middle High German überholn, which is usually said to have first meant fetching someone across a river.

Lesson 3 of 5.
drängeln — to tailgate; to push in, to jostle.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for the right of way, then the German for to overtake.

You'll want to know: drängeln.
drängeln — "to tailgate; to push in, to jostle".
Hinter uns drängelt ein schwarzes Auto. A black car is tailgating us.
Drängeln is also pushing in a queue: sich vordrängeln.

The word, taken apart.
drängeln is built on drängen 'to push, to press', inherited from Old High German drengen and Proto-Germanic þrangwijaną (EtymDB). English throng is usually counted as a cousin.

Lesson 4 of 5.
der Gegenverkehr — the oncoming traffic.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to overtake, then the German for to tailgate.

You'll want to know: der Gegenverkehr.
der Gegenverkehr — "the oncoming traffic".
In der Kurve kommt plötzlich Gegenverkehr. On the bend there is suddenly oncoming traffic.
No plural.

The word, taken apart.
Gegenverkehr is built from gegen 'against, towards' and Verkehr 'traffic', from verkehren 'to go to and fro', built on kehren 'to turn'. The parts are inherited Germanic words.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ausweichen — to swerve, to get out of the way; to avoid.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to tailgate, then the German for the oncoming traffic.

You'll want to know: ausweichen.
ausweichen — "to swerve, to get out of the way; to avoid".
Die Fahrerin weicht einer Katze aus. The driver swerves to avoid a cat.
Separable, with the dative; Perfekt with sein: sie ist der Katze ausgewichen.

The word, taken apart.
ausweichen is built from aus 'out' and weichen 'to give way, to yield', inherited from Old High German wīchan and Proto-Germanic wīkwaną (EtymDB).
