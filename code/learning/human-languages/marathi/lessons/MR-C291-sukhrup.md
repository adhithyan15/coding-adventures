## You'll want to know: सुखरूप
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C291-SUTKA291-03]; assesses=[] -->

**सुखरूप** — *sukhrūp* — "safe and sound".

> सगळे प्रवासी सुखरूप घरी पोहोचले.
>
> *sagḷe pravāsī sukhrūp gharī pohocle.*
>
> All the passengers reached home safe and sound.

सुखरूप keeps one form: तो सुखरूप आहे, ती सुखरूप आहे.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सुखरूप** (*sukhrūp*) is built from Sanskrit *sukha*, 'ease, well-being', and *rūpa*, 'form' (Monier-Williams): in a good state.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
