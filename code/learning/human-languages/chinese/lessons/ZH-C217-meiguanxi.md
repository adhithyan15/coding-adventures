## You'll want to know: méi guānxi
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C217-FINE217-01]; assesses=[] -->

*méi guānxi* — "it doesn't matter; never mind".

> *méi guānxi wǒmen míngtiān qù* — Never mind, we'll go tomorrow.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*méi guānxi* is built from 没 'not have' and *guānxi* 'a connection', all three parts inherited from Middle Chinese, *mwot*, *kwaen* and *kejH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription): no connection to worry about.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying no and asking.
