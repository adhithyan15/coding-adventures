## You'll want to know: সড়ক
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C390-SOROK390-01]; assesses=[] -->

**সড়ক** — *sôṛôk* — "a highway; a main road".

> নতুন সড়কে বাস দ্রুত চলে।
>
> *nôtun sôṛôke bās drutô chôle.*
>
> Buses go fast on the new highway.

রাস্তা is any road; সড়ক is a main road between towns, the word on maps: জাতীয় সড়ক, a national highway.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**সড়ক** (*sôṛôk*) probably belongs with Sanskrit *sṛtí*, "a road, a path", the entry where CDIAL sets Bengali *saṛak*, "a road" (CDIAL 13577); the Hindi word is filed under a second entry as well (CDIAL 12269), so the link is hedged.
