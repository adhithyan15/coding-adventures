## You'll want to know: chóngzi
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C242-FARMANIMAL242-04]; assesses=[] -->

*chóngzi* — "insect; bug".

> *wǒ nǚ'ér bú pà chóngzi* — My daughter isn't afraid of bugs.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*chóngzi* is built from *chóng* 'insect' and the noun ending 子, both inherited from Middle Chinese, *drjuwng* and *tsiX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which says, after an older dictionary, that creatures with feet are called by the first.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for everyday things.
