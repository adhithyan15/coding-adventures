## You'll want to know: شاعر
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C545-SHIR545-01]; assesses=[] -->

**شاعر** — *shāʿir* — "a poet".

> كتب الشاعر قصيدة عن البحر.
>
> *kataba ash-shāʿir qaṣīda ʿan al-baḥr.*
>
> The poet wrote a poem about the sea.

Plural: شعراء, *shuʿarāʾ*.

Shiʿr, poetry, has the same letters as shaʿr, hair.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**شاعر** (*shāʿir*) is built on the native Arabic root *sh-ʿ-r*, "to know, to perceive" (Lane). Shuʿūr, a feeling, which you know, is from the same root: a poet is one who perceives.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
