## You'll want to know: संविधान
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C358-SANVIDHAN358-01]; assesses=[] -->

**संविधान** — *saṁvidhān* — "a constitution".

> संविधानाने प्रत्येक नागरिकाला समान हक्क दिले आहेत.
>
> *saṁvidhānāne pratyek nāgarikālā samān hakka dile āhet.*
>
> The constitution has given every citizen equal rights.

Neuter.

Neuter; also राज्यघटना.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**संविधान** (*saṁvidhān*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *saṁvidhāna*, "arrangement, disposition" (Monier-Williams).
