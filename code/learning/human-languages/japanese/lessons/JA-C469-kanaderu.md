## You'll want to know: かなでる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C469-INSTR469-04]; assesses=[] -->

**かなでる** — *kanaderu* — "to play music".

> **かのじょは ふえで うたを かなでました。** — *kanojo wa fue de uta o kanademashita* — "She played a song on the flute."

Polite **かなでます**, *kanademasu*; an ichidan verb. Past (polite): **せんせいが ふえを かなでました。**, *sensei ga fue o kanademashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**かなでる** (*kanaderu*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji spelling was attached to it later (JMdict).
