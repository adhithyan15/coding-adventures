# दौहित्रः (dauhitraḥ) — a daughter's son

## You'll want to know: दौहित्रः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C499-SVASR499-05]; assesses=[] -->

**दौहित्रः** — *dauhitraḥ* — "a daughter's son".

> मातामहः दौहित्रेण सह क्रीडति।
>
> *mātāmahaḥ dauhitreṇa saha krīḍati.*
>
> Grandfather plays with his daughter's son.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**दौहित्रः** (*dauhitraḥ*) is built on *duhitṛ*, "a daughter" (**दुहिता**), by lengthening its first vowel; MW gives "a daughter's son" (MW).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
