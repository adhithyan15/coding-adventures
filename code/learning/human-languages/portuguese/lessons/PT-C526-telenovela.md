## You'll want to know: a telenovela
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C526-SERIES-03]; assesses=[] -->

**a telenovela** — "soap opera".

> A minha avó não perde a telenovela das nove.
>
> *My grandmother never misses the nine o'clock soap.*

Often shortened to *a novela*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*telenovela* is built from *tele-*, from Greek *tēle* "far", and *novela*, from Italian *novella* "a tale" (EtymDB).
