## You'll want to know: पूर
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C294-PUR294-01]; assesses=[] -->

**पूर** — *pūr* — "a flood".

> नदीला पूर आल्यास हा पूल बंद होईल.
>
> *nadīlā pūr ālyās hā pūl band hoīl.*
>
> Should the river flood, this bridge will be closed.

Masculine.

नदीला पूर येणे, the river floods; आल्यास, should it come.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पूर** (*pūr*) goes back to Sanskrit *pūra*, 'filling; a flood': CDIAL lists the Marathi word, as *purā*, 'filling; flood', under it (CDIAL 8330).
