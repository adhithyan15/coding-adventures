## You'll want to know: صرف
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C366-IJAZAT366-01]; assesses=[] -->

**صرف** — *sirf* — "only".

> اگر صرف ایک ٹکٹ ہے تو آپ جائیے۔
>
> *agar sirf ek ṭikaṭ hai to āp jāiye.*
>
> If there is only one ticket, you go.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**صرف** (*sirf*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *ṣirf*, "pure, unmixed": that and nothing else.
