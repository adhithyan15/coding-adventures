## You'll want to know: bùfen
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C438-NORTH438-04]; assesses=[] -->

*bùfen* — "part; section (of a whole)".

> *dà bùfen yóukè zhù zài běibù* — Most of the visitors are staying in the north.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*bùfen* is built from 部 'a section' and *fèn* 'a share', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *buX* and *bjunH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second as a portion that is measured out; in this word it is said light.
