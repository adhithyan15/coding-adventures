# سکڑنا (sikuṛnā) — to shrink; to huddle up

## You'll want to know: سکڑنا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C429-THAND429-04]; assesses=[] -->

**سکڑنا** — *sikuṛnā* — "to shrink; to huddle up".

> سردی میں بچے کمبل میں سکڑ گئے۔
>
> *sardī meṅ bachche kambal meṅ sikuṛ gae.*
>
> In the cold the children huddled up under the blanket.

Past (it …): وہ کرتا سکڑ گیا, *voh kurtā sikuṛ gayā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**سکڑنا** (*sikuṛnā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousins *sãkaṛnā* and *sikaṛnā*, "to be contracted", under Sanskrit *saṃkuṭati*, "bends oneself together" (CDIAL 12825).
