## You'll want to know: सतरा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-SATRA]; assesses=[MR-LEX-NUMBERS-ONE-TO-FIVE] -->

> **सतरा** — *satrā* — **17**

Sanskrit **सप्तदश** (*saptadaśa*), *seven-ten*. **सत-** is the **सात** of two
chapters ago and **-रा** is the ten. From here the pattern finishes itself.
