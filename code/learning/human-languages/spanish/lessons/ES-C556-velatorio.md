## You'll want to know: el velatorio
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C556-ENTIERRO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**el velatorio** — "the wake; the funeral parlour".

> Fuimos al velatorio por la noche.
>
> *We went to the wake in the evening.*

In Latin America *el velorio* is common.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*velatorio* is built on *velar* 'to stay awake, to keep watch over', which is inherited from Latin *vigilāre* 'to stay awake' (EtymDB). Family and friends stay up with the dead.
