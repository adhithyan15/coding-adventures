## You'll want to know: இயற்று
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C325-OLAI325-05]; assesses=[] -->

**இயற்று** — *iyaṟṟu* — "to compose".

> அவர் இந்த நூலை இயற்றினார்.
>
> *avar inta nūlai iyaṟṟiṉār.*
>
> He composed this work.

Past (I …): இயற்றினேன், *iyaṟṟiṉēṉ*.

Of composing a poem or a book, in written Tamil.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**இயற்று** (*iyaṟṟu*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 471 gives it beside இயல், "to be possible", as "to do, effect, cause to act".
