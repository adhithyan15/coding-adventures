# (la excursión del sábado) — read it, then do it yourself

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-C463-GUIA-01, ES-LEX-C463-GUIA-02] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say **organizar** and **el guía**. The notice below has one doing
the other.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-C463-GUIA-01, ES-LEX-C463-GUIA-02, ES-LEX-C463-GUIA-03] -->

A message to a walking group. Read it, then answer.

> Hola a todos. La excursión del sábado la **organiza** el centro cívico.
>
> Viene un **guía** del pueblo, así que no hace falta llevar mapa.
>
> Salimos a las ocho. Si a alguien le **importa** madrugar, hay otro grupo a
> las once, pero ese no lleva guía.

[PAUSE 3s]
- [YOU SAY: who is putting the trip on, and who is coming with it]
- [YOU SAY: why you do not need a map]
- [YOU SAY: the difference between the two groups]

## Why it's said this way
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-C463-GUIA-03, ES-LEX-C463-GUIA-01] -->

**La excursión del sábado la organiza el centro cívico** puts the object first
and then repeats it as **la**. That doubling is not a mistake and not emphasis
for its own sake: Spanish allows the object to lead the sentence, and when it
does, a pronoun must hold its place beside the verb. English would say "the
Saturday trip is organised by the civic centre" and reach for a passive;
Spanish reorders instead.

**Si a alguien le importa madrugar** is *importar* in its natural habitat. The
subject is *madrugar* — getting up early is what does the mattering — and *a
alguien le* marks the person it matters to. Word for word: "if getting up early
matters to anyone".

Note *le* even though *alguien* is already named. Spanish doubles the indirect
object almost always, and leaving the *le* out would sound incomplete.

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-C463-GUIA-01, ES-LEX-C463-GUIA-02, ES-LEX-C463-GUIA-03] -->

[PAUSE 3s] Now your turn, out loud and then in writing. Four lines to a group:
say who is organising the outing, say a guide is coming, give the time, and ask
whether an early start matters to anyone.

Then check yourself: did *importar* agree with the thing rather than the
person, and did you keep the *le*?
