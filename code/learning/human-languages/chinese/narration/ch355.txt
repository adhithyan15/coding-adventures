Mandarin Chinese, chapter 355: Thanks, but No.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
xièjué — to decline politely.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for what something is about, then the Mandarin for to have a serious talk.

You'll want to know: xièjué.
xièjué — "to decline politely".
wǒ qǐng tā chī fàn tā xièjué le — I invited him to dinner, but he politely declined.
Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
xièjué is built from 谢 and jué, both inherited from Middle Chinese, zjaeH and dzjwet (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to take leave' and the second 'to cut off'.

Lesson 3 of 5.
kěn — to be willing to; to agree to.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for to decline politely, then the Mandarin for a kind thought.

You'll want to know: kěn.
kěn — "to be willing to; to agree to".
tā bù kěn gàosu wǒ wèishénme — He won't tell me why.
Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
kěn is inherited from Middle Chinese khongX (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses it 'to permit' and, quoting an older dictionary, 'the flesh between the bones'. How it came to mean being willing is not settled.

Lesson 5 of 5.
当面 (dāngmiàn) — to someone's face; in person.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for to be willing, then the Mandarin for to deny.

You'll want to know: 当面.
当面 — dāngmiàn — "to someone's face; in person".
nǐ zuìhǎo dāngmiàn gēn tā shuō — You'd better say it to his face.

The word, taken apart.
当面 (dāngmiàn) is built from 当 and 面 'the face', both inherited from Middle Chinese, tang and mjienH (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to match' and 'to preside', and the second 'to face' and 'in front'.
