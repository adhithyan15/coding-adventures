# A meal in a familiar place

## Warm-up

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[SA-LEX-C247-THG247-03, SA-GRAMMAR-C259-PLURAL-POSSESSORS-03, SA-VERB-C264-KARISYAMI-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say “armour,” then contrast “my” with “our,” and finish with “I
will do.” (*Kavacam; mama, asmākam; kariṣyāmi.*)

## Grammar Lens: bring the meal words back

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[SA-LEX-C97-LOC97-02, SA-LEX-C160-THG160-01, SA-LEX-KHADATI-EAT, SA-LEX-ANNAM-FOOD, SA-LEX-PANIYAM-WATER, SA-LEX-KSHIRAM-MILK] -->

Nothing here is new. **भोजनालयः** (*bhojanālayaḥ*) is a restaurant;
**भोजनम्** (*bhojanam*) is a meal or food. You already know **खादति**
(*khādati*), “eats,” along with **अन्नम्** (*annam*), food, **पानीयम्**
(*pānīyam*), water, and **क्षीरम्** (*kṣīram*), milk.

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[SA-LEX-C97-LOC97-02, SA-LEX-C160-THG160-01, SA-LEX-KHADATI-EAT, SA-LEX-ANNAM-FOOD, SA-LEX-PANIYAM-WATER, SA-LEX-KSHIRAM-MILK] -->

[PAUSE 2s each]
- [YOU SAY: “restaurant” — *bhojanālayaḥ*]
- [YOU SAY: “meal” — *bhojanam*]
- [YOU SAY: “eats food” — *annaṃ khādati*]
- [YOU CONTRAST: water, then milk — *pānīyam; kṣīram*]
