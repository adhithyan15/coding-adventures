## You'll want to know: साहस
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C470-SAHAS470-01]; assesses=[] -->

**साहस** — *sāhas* — "daring; boldness".

> रात में अकेले जंगल में जाने के लिए साहस चाहिए।
>
> *rāt meṁ akele jaṅgal meṁ jāne ke liye sāhas cāhiye.*
>
> It takes daring to go into the forest alone at night.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**साहस** (*sāhas*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *sāhasa*, "boldness, daring", also "rashness" (Monier-Williams).
