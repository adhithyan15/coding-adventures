## You'll want to know: развод
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C307-RAZVOD-01]; assesses=[] -->

**развод** (*razvód*) — "a divorce".

> После развода он уехал в другой город.
>
> *Pósle razvóda on uyékhal v drugóy górod.*
>
> After the divorce he moved to another town.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*развод* is built on *разводить* "to lead apart", from *раз-* and *водить* "to lead", inherited from Proto-Slavic *voditi* (EtymDB).
