Marwadi (Marwari), chapter 226: Sand Hills.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
टीबो (ṭībo) — a sand hill; a dune.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for bedding, then the Marwadi for a woollen blanket.

You'll want to know: टीबो.
टीबो — ṭībo — "a sand hill; a dune".
गांव कनै एक टीबो है। gā̃v kanai ek ṭībo hai. There's a sand hill near the village.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
टीबो (ṭībo) is inherited from a reconstructed ṭappa, "a hill", one of several shapes CDIAL gives for it; CDIAL lists Hindi ṭībā, "a hill, a sandbank", and a Gujarati cousin. CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry (CDIAL 5446).

Lesson 2 of 5.
ऊंचो (ū̃cho) — high; tall.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for a woollen blanket, then the Marwadi for a sand hill.

You'll want to know: ऊंचो.
ऊंचो — ū̃cho — "high; tall".
टीबो घणो ऊंचो है। ṭībo ghaṇo ū̃cho hai. The sand hill is very high.

The word, taken apart.
ऊंचो (ū̃cho) is inherited from Sanskrit ucca, "tall, lofty"; CDIAL lists Old Marwari uco, "high" (CDIAL 1634). नीचो, low, is its opposite.

Lesson 3 of 5.
सूनो (sūno) — lonely; deserted.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for a sand hill, then the Marwadi for high.

You'll want to know: सूनो.
सूनो — sūno — "lonely; deserted".
रात गेलो सूनो है। rāt gelo sūno hai. At night the path is lonely.

The word, taken apart.
सूनो (sūno) is inherited from Sanskrit śūnya, "empty" (CDIAL 12567, which lists Marwari sūno). It is said of a place with nobody in it.

वो गांव उजाड़ है। vo gā̃v ujāṛ hai. That village is deserted.

उजाड़ (ujāṛ) is inherited from a reconstructed ujjaṭa, "uprooted, laid waste"; CDIAL lists Old Marwari ujāṛi, "a deserted place" (CDIAL 1660b).
