# लांघणो (lā̃ghṇo) — to cross over; to leap over

## You'll want to know: लांघणो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C217-CROSS217-01]; assesses=[] -->

**लांघणो** — *lā̃ghṇo* — "to cross over; to leap over".

> हूं काल नदी लांघसूं।
>
> *hū̃ kāl nadī lā̃ghsū̃.*
>
> Tomorrow I'll cross the river.

Past (I …): म्हैं नदी लांघी, *mhaĩ nadī lā̃ghī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**लांघणो** (*lā̃ghṇo*) is inherited from Sanskrit *laṅghayati*, "leaps over"; CDIAL lists Hindi *lā̃ghnā*, "to jump over", under it. CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry (CDIAL 10905).
