Gujarati, chapter 384: Success.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
સફળ (saphaḷ) — successful.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to set about, then the Gujarati for to battle on.

You'll want to know: સફળ.
સફળ — saphaḷ — "successful".
જો આ યોજના સફળ થશે તો આખા ગામને ફાયદો થશે. jo ā yojnā saphaḷ thaśe to ākhā gāmne phāydo thaśe. If this plan succeeds, the whole village will benefit.

The word, taken apart.
સફળ (saphaḷ) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit saphala, "having fruit, fruitful" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 4 of 5.
ફાવવું (phāvvũ) — to get one's way; to succeed.

[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for advancement, then the Gujarati for development.

You'll want to know: ફાવવું.
ફાવવું — phāvvũ — "to get one's way; to succeed".
જો તે છેતરવાની કોશિશ કરશે તો તે નહીં ફાવે. jo te chetarvānī kośiś karśe to te nahī̃ phāve. If he tries to cheat, he will not get away with it.
Past (he …): તે પોતાની યોજનામાં ફાવ્યો, te potānī yojnāmā̃ phāvyo.

The word, taken apart.
ફાવવું (phāvvũ) CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb, "to find convenient, to succeed", under a reconstructed sparvati, "releases, wins, succeeds" (CDIAL 13808).

Lesson 5 of 5.
પહોંચાડવું (pahõcāḍvũ) — to deliver.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for development, then the Gujarati for to get one's way.

You'll want to know: પહોંચાડવું.
પહોંચાડવું — pahõcāḍvũ — "to deliver".
જો ટપાલી સમયસર આવશે તો તે પત્ર આજે પહોંચાડશે. jo ṭapālī samaysar āvśe to te patra āje pahõcāḍśe. If the postman comes on time, he will deliver the letter today.
Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે પાર્સલ ઘરે પહોંચાડ્યું, teṇe pārsal ghare pahõcāḍyũ.

The word, taken apart.
પહોંચાડવું (pahõcāḍvũ) is the causative of પહોંચવું (pahõcvũ), "to arrive", which CDIAL lists under Sanskrit prábhūta, "much, great" (CDIAL 8716): to make a thing arrive.
