## Changed — every Kannada word lesson now says where its word comes from

All 1,345 Kannada word lessons that lacked an etymology now carry one (HL24 §9). Before this change, 1,330 had no `etymology_hook` and 1,281 had no visible etymology. Now no lesson lacks either.

- **Shape.** 1,280 lessons gained a `## The word, taken apart` block (`## The phrase, taken apart` in nine phrase lessons) with `<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->`. The block sits after the section that introduces the word. Each lesson also has a matching `etymology_hook` of 120 characters or fewer. 64 lessons whose body already stated an origin gained only the hook, plus one sentence in an existing section where the origin was not yet visible. KA-C06-dative-subject's hook was a grammar note; it now gives the etymology of *gottu*, and one sentence in the existing section says it.
- **Sources.** Native words cite the *Dravidian Etymological Dictionary* (Burrow & Emeneau, DEDR) by entry number, for example "(DEDR 4411)". Indo-Aryan loans that DEDR lists in its appendix are cited as "DEDR App. N". Sanskrit tatsamas and tadbhavas cite Turner's *Comparative Dictionary of the Indo-Aryan Languages* (CDIAL) by entry number. Both were read locally from the moli-mandala/data (Jambu) files. DEDR was read from the full article texts, not the shortened table, because the table labels some Kota lines as Kannada. Every number was checked by script against those files. So was every Kannada form cited from DEDR (it must sit under "Ka." in the full article) and every cognate (it must sit under the language named). No source text is copied: each statement is our own sentence. English loans and Perso-Arabic loans that came through Hindustani name the donor without a number.
- **Hedges kept.** Wherever a cited DEDR or CDIAL article hedges, the lesson keeps the hedge, and a script checks this. That covers "?", "probably", "Cf. Skt.", "< IA", "may be reborrowings from IA", appendix loans, and CDIAL's own "← Drav." or "non-Aryan" notes. For example, KA-C45-grandfather and -grandmother say DEDR 50 calls *ajja* and *ajji* "probably" Indo-Aryan loans (Prakrit *ajja*, Sanskrit *ārya*, CDIAL 1347). KA-C258-majjige says DEDR 4630 queries it as an Indo-Aryan loan. KA-C267-kunda says CDIAL 3264 takes Sanskrit *kuṇḍa* itself from Dravidian. KA-C305-kalavida says the same of *kalā* (CDIAL 2927).
- **Tatsama and tadbhava.** A word is called a tatsama only when it keeps the Sanskrit form. The ending changes Kannada always makes still count as tatsama: *-e* for final *-ā*, an added *-u*, a shortened final *-ī*. Words reshaped in their sounds are tadbhavas, for example *nantara*, *nāṇya*, *tuḷasi*, *suddi* (*śuddhi*), *huṇṇime* (*pūrṇimā*), *kumbāra* (*kumbhakāra*).
- **Tallies (1,345 lessons, by the origin each hook names).** 644 native Dravidian. 381 Sanskrit: 143 tatsama, 32 tadbhava, and 206 built from Sanskrit parts or with a hedged Sanskrit source. 79 English. 69 Persian or Arabic through Hindustani. 4 DEDR appendix loans. 3 other (Portuguese *mestre* for *mēstri*, Malay through English *godown*, an exclamation). 97 built from parts of mixed origin, such as *sākuprāṇi* (native *sāku* plus Sanskrit *prāṇin*). 68 of uncertain origin. The added text cites DEDR 1,864 times (520 distinct entries) and CDIAL 777 times (359 distinct entries).
- **Uncertain origins.** Before a lesson says "uncertain", both DEDR and CDIAL were searched by form (allowing Kannada *p-*/*h-*, *v-*/*b-*, *ḻ*/*ḷ*, *ṟ*/*r*) and by gloss. That search turned up sources for nine words first marked uncertain: *baḷe* (DEDR 5313), *jaraḍi* (DEDR 2370), *oyyu* (DEDR 984), *ese* (DEDR 805), *iḍli* (DEDR 455), *kandu* (DEDR 1410), *aṅgai* (DEDR 7), *aḍḍa* (DEDR 83) and *kaḍa* (DEDR 1113). Five more now have hedged leads: *sambaḷa* (perhaps Sanskrit *śambala*, CDIAL 12315), *haṇḍe* and *kallaṅgaḍi* (northern words in CDIAL 14050 and 3100), *seke* (CDIAL 13581) and *huṇḍi* (CDIAL 14138). The rest say plainly that neither source records them, and give the usual account as a hedge where there is one. Examples: *sari* (usually Sanskrit *sadṛśa* through Prakrit, CDIAL 13120), *sante*, *palya*, *hubbu*, *nepa*, *lakōṭe*.
- **Rejected rows.** A same-spelling entry was never accepted on spelling alone. Rejected: DEDR 2360 *sari* "to move" for *sari* "correct"; DEDR 3941 *pesaṟ* "green gram" for *hesaru* "name" (DEDR 4410); DEDR 5350 *bā* "to swell" for *bā* "come" (DEDR 5270); DEDR 604 *uṇṇe* "tick" for *uṇṇe* "wool" (CDIAL 2424); DEDR 29 *aṅka* "fight" for *aṅka* "mark" (CDIAL 100); DEDR 4362 *huṇḍi* "hamlet"; DEDR 2735 *cūpu* "sight" for *cūpu* "point"; DEDR App. 2 *agga* "rope" for *agga* "cheap". In CDIAL: 12251 (*śakti* "spear") for 12250 (*śakti* "power"); 5619 (*takṣaṇa*) for 5640 (*tatkṣaṇa*); 9054 (*phalaka* "shield") for 9053 ("board"). The Kota line *puḷy* that the shortened DEDR table labels "Ka." in 4322 is not cited as Kannada.
- **Corrections to existing text.** KA-C01-sari no longer calls *sari* native; its origin is uncertain. KA-C01-illa says DEDR 2559 files Telugu *lē-*, *lēdu* with *il-*; the table had said "a different root". KA-C03-paravaagilla glosses *paravā* as Persian *parvā*, "care, concern", through Hindustani. It also stops saying Telugu "breaks away". KA-C07-ondu and KA-C07-entu stop saying Telugu will not share the word. DEDR 990 files Telugu *oka* and DEDR 784 files Telugu *enimidi* in the same entries. KA-C07-ondu's gloss changed to match. KA-C44-speak says *māta* is native (DEDR 4834); it had said "from Sanskrit". KA-C174-aramane says DEDR 201 derives *arasu* from Sanskrit *rājan*.
- **Forward references and glyphs.** A script checked that every Kannada word in the new text is taught by that point or glossed beside it. It also checked that no cited form uses a mark the book font cannot set: a macron with a breve, a spacing ˜ or ˘, or a long vowel with a nasal mark. Such forms were left out or given in a plain spelling.
- **Roots.** 1,283 lessons gained `roots:` slugs: 1,150 distinct slugs, 70 reused from the corpus and 1,080 new. Six slugs (for DEDR 333, 2017, 4205 and 4719, and CDIAL 11683 and 8035) were changed to the spelling another track already uses. `check:root-slug-splits` reports no new split.
- **Durations.** 23 lessons had `duration.max_seconds` raised to their computed length. All are under 300 s. Eight went over because of the new text: KA-C06-dative-subject 290→294, KA-C67-and-too 210→220, KA-C68-me-neither 210→214, KA-C68-neither 210→235, KA-C69-why 210→220, KA-C71-say-again 210→226, KA-C73-a-flower 210→227, KA-C74-second 220→229. Fifteen were already over their declared length before this change and are now declared honestly (their raises also cover the new block text): KA-C07-ombattu 180→198, KA-C32-baa 250→294, KA-C68-is-not 210→266, KA-C69-is-it 210→240, KA-C69-isnt-it 210→272, KA-C70-because 210→272, KA-C70-i-think 210→259, KA-C70-quotative 210→268, KA-C71-which 210→285, KA-C72-do-you-like 210→258, KA-C72-prefer 210→255, KA-C72-want 210→271, KA-C72-when-clause 210→273, KA-C73-can 210→281, KA-C73-in-order-to 210→229.
