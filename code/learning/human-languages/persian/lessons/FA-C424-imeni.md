## You'll want to know: ایمنی
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C424-SORAT-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ایمنی** (*imeni*) — "safety".

> کمربند برای ایمنی مسافران مهم است.
>
> *Kamarband barâ-ye imeni-ye mosâferân mohem ast.*
>
> The belt matters for the safety of the passengers.

*Kamarband-e imeni* — a seat belt. Built on *imen* 'safe' + *-i*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*imeni* is built from *imen* 'safe, secure' and the noun ending *-i*. *Imen* is usually traced to Arabic *āmin* 'safe, secure'; EtymDB does not list *imen* or *imeni*.
