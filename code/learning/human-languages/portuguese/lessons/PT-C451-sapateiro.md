## You'll want to know: o sapateiro
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C451-ARRANJOS-01]; assesses=[] -->

**o sapateiro** — "the cobbler, the shoemaker".

> O sapateiro mudou as solas das minhas botas.
>
> *The cobbler replaced the soles of my boots.*

Built on *o sapato*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*sapateiro* is built on *sapato* 'shoe' with *-eiro*. *Sapato* comes from Old Portuguese *çapato* (EtymDB); its earlier origin is not settled.
