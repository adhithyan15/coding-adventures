## Script — the hand piece, then a hook, a post and a fork
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-SCRIPT-BAO-REPORT-01]; assesses=[] -->

> 报

**Two pieces, left before right.** Count the pieces, not the strokes.

**The left piece, three strokes.** The hand piece of 打: a bar, a vertical that hooks to the left at the foot, and a stroke rising to the right across it.

**The right piece, four strokes.** A bar that turns down and hooks back to the left. A long vertical down the left side of the piece. Then the fork: a bar that turns into a sweep down to the lower left, and a long stroke falling to the right.

3 + 4 = seven.
