Sanskrit, chapter 491: Old Age and Death.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
जरा (jarā) — old age.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a wrinkle, then the Sanskrit for appearance.

You'll want to know: जरा.
जरा — jarā — "old age".
जरायां केशाः श्वेताः भवन्ति। jarāyāṃ keśāḥ śvetāḥ bhavanti. In old age the hair turns white.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
जरा (jarā) comes from the root √jṝ, "to grow old"; MW gives "the act of becoming old, old age" (MW).

Lesson 5 of 5.
श्राद्धम् (śrāddham) — a rite for the ancestors.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for death, then the Sanskrit for funeral rites.

You'll want to know: श्राद्धम्.
श्राद्धम् — śrāddham — "a rite for the ancestors".
पुत्रः पितुः श्राद्धं करोति। putraḥ pituḥ śrāddhaṃ karoti. The son performs the rite for his late father.
A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
श्राद्धम् (śrāddham) is built on śraddhā, "faith"; MW gives "a ceremony in honour and for the benefit of dead relatives" (MW).
