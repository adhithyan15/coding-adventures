## You'll want to know: تأرجح
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C539-LUAB539-05]; assesses=[] -->

**تأرجح** — *taʾarjaḥa* — "to swing".

> تأرجحت البنت على الأرجوحة.
>
> *taʾarjaḥat al-bint ʿalā al-urjūḥa.*
>
> The girl swung on the swing.

Present: يتأرجح, *yataʾarjaḥ*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**تأرجح** (*taʾarjaḥa*) goes with *urjūḥa*, "a swing", built on the native Arabic root *r-j-ḥ*, "to outweigh, to go down (of a scale pan)" (Lane).
