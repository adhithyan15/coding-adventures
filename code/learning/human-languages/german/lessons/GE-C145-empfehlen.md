## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**empfehlen** ← Middle High German *enpfelhen*, "to entrust to someone" (EtymDB 2.1) — inherited, built from *ent-* and an old verb for "to hand over". To recommend is to entrust.
