## You'll want to know: batir
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C768-COCINAR-04]; assesses=[] -->

**batir** — "to beat, to whisk".

> Batí tres huevos para la tortilla.
>
> *I beat three eggs for the omelette.*

*Batir un récord* — to break a record.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*batir* comes from Late Latin *battere* 'to beat' (EtymDB), a form of Latin *battuere*. *Batido* 'milkshake' is its participle.
