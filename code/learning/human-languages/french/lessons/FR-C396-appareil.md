## You'll want to know: l'appareil
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C396-LOGICIEL-03]; assesses=[] -->

**l'appareil** — "the device, the appliance".

> Cet appareil ne marche plus.
>
> *This device does not work any more.*

*Un appareil photo* — a camera. On the phone, *qui est à l'appareil ?* — who is speaking? Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*appareil* is formed on *appareiller* "to prepare, to fit out" (EtymDB), from a Vulgar Latin *appariculāre*, built on Latin *parāre* "to prepare" (Lewis & Short).
