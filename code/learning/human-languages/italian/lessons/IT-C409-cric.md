## You'll want to know: il cric
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C409-GOMMA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**il cric** — "the jack (for a car)".

> Senza il cric non possiamo cambiare la ruota.
>
> *Without the jack we cannot change the wheel.*

It sounds like the clicking it makes. Plural unchanged: *i cric*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*cric* came from French *cric*, a word that seems to imitate the click of the mechanism; the deeper origin is not settled.
