## You'll want to know: el bañador
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C690-PLAYA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**el bañador** — "the swimsuit, the swimming trunks".

> No olvides el bañador.
>
> *Do not forget your swimsuit.*

A Spain word; Latin America says *el traje de baño*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*bañador* is built on *bañar* "to bathe", from *baño* "bath", inherited from Latin *balneum*.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
