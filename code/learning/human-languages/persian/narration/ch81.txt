Persian, chapter 81: Saturday, Sunday, Monday, Wednesday, Thursday.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
شنبه (shanbe) — Saturday.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for winter, then the Persian for a season.

You'll want to know: شنبه.
شنبه — shanbe — "Saturday".

The word, taken apart.
shanbe is the Sabbath word: it is usually traced through Middle Persian šambad to the Aramaic and Hebrew word for the Sabbath. Saturday was the Sabbath, and the next weekdays are counted from it.

Lesson 2 of 5.
یکشنبه (yekshanbe) — Sunday.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a season, then the Persian for Saturday.

You'll want to know: یکشنبه.
یکشنبه — yekshanbe — "Sunday".

The word, taken apart.
yekshanbe is yek, "one", with shanbe, "Saturday" (EtymDB): the first day after the Sabbath. yek is inherited; shanbe is the Sabbath word, usually traced through Middle Persian to Aramaic.
