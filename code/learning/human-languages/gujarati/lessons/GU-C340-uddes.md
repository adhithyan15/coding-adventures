## You'll want to know: ઉદ્દેશ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C340-HETU340-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ઉદ્દેશ** — *uddeś* — "a purpose".

> આ યોજનાનો ઉદ્દેશ ગામમાં પાણી લાવવાનો છે.
>
> *ā yojnāno uddeś gāmmā̃ pāṇī lāvvāno che.*
>
> The aim of this plan is to bring water to the village.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ઉદ્દેશ** (*uddeś*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *uddeśa*, "pointing at, an aim" (Monier-Williams).

## What you've built
