# బయటపెట్టు (bayaṭapeṭṭu) — to expose; to bring out

## You'll want to know: బయటపెట్టు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C354-KUTRA354-03]; assesses=[] -->

**బయటపెట్టు** — *bayaṭapeṭṭu* — "to expose; to bring out".

> విలేకరి ఆ కుట్రను బయటపెట్టాడు.
>
> *vilēkari ā kuṭranu bayaṭapeṭṭāḍu.*
>
> The reporter exposed the plot.

Past (I …): బయటపెట్టాను, *bayaṭapeṭṭānu*.

బయట, outside, with పెట్టు, to put: to put a secret outside.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**బయటపెట్టు** (*bayaṭapeṭṭu*) is a native compound: *bayaṭa*, "outside", which sits beside *bayalu*, "the outside" (DEDR 3940), and *peṭṭu*, "to put" (DEDR 4390).
