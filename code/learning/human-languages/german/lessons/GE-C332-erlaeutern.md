## You'll want to know: erläutern
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C332-BEGRUENDEN-05]; assesses=[] -->

**erläutern** — "to explain in detail, to clarify".

> Die Ärztin hat mir die Ergebnisse genau erläutert.
>
> *The doctor explained the results to me in detail.*

More formal than *erklären*. Inseparable: *sie erläutert, sie hat erläutert*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*erläutern* is built on *lauter* "clear, pure", inherited from Middle High German *lūter*, Old High German *hlūttar* and Proto-Germanic *hlūtraz* "clear, clean" (EtymDB): you make a thing clear.
