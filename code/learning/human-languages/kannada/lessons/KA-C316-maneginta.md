# ಮನೆಗಿಂತ (maneginta) — bigger than, best of all

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[KA-LEX-C42-ADJ-01, KA-LEX-C42-ADJ-02, KA-LEX-C58-REPLY-02, KA-GRAMMAR-C315-WISH-BEKU-ANTA-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "big", "small" and "more". (*doḍḍa*, *cikka*, *heccu*.) Then "I would love to go to Mysuru one day." (*nanage ondu dina maisūrige hōgabēku anta āse ide*.)

## You'll want to know: -ಗಿಂತ, than
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-GRAMMAR-C316-KINTA-01]; assesses=[KA-LEX-C103-HOME-01, KA-LEX-C100-FIND-03, KA-LEX-C111-LOC111-02, KA-LEX-C64-JOIN-04, KA-LEX-C39-KAAPI-01, KA-LEX-C39-CHAHA-01, KA-LEX-C34-ISHTA-01, KA-LEX-C54-TREE-01, KA-GRAMMAR-C06-DATIVE-SUBJECT-02] -->

> **ಈ ಮನೆ ಆ ಮನೆಗಿಂತ ದೊಡ್ಡದು.**
>
> *ī mane ā maneginta doḍḍadu.* — "This house is bigger than that house."

The thing you measure against takes **-ಗಿಂತ**, "than". It is the "to" ending with **-ಇಂತ** on it: **ಮನೆಗೆ** → **ಮನೆಗಿಂತ**; **ನನಗೆ** → **ನನಗಿಂತ** (*nanaginta*, "than me"). Where "to" is **-ಕ್ಕೆ**, "than" is **-ಕ್ಕಿಂತ**: **ಮರಕ್ಕೆ** → **ಮರಕ್ಕಿಂತ** (*marakkinta*, "than the tree").

The adjective needs no "-er". **ದೊಡ್ಡ** stays **ದೊಡ್ಡ**; at the end of the sentence it takes **-ದು**: **ದೊಡ್ಡದು** (*doḍḍadu*, "(it is) big"), **ಚಿಕ್ಕದು** (*cikkadu*, "(it is) small"), like the **ಒಳ್ಳೆಯದು** of "it is good".

> **ಬಸ್ಸಿಗಿಂತ ರೈಲು ಬೇಗ ಹೋಗುತ್ತದೆ.**
>
> *bassiginta railu bēga hōguttade.* — "The train goes faster than the bus."

> **ಕಾಫಿಗಿಂತ ಚಹಾ ನನಗೆ ಹೆಚ್ಚು ಇಷ್ಟ.**
>
> *kāphiginta cahā nanage heccu iṣṭa.* — "I like tea more than coffee."

With a liking, **ಹೆಚ್ಚು**, "more", says how much more.

## You'll want to know: ಎಲ್ಲಕ್ಕಿಂತ, most of all
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-GRAMMAR-C316-MOST-01]; assesses=[KA-LEX-C102-THISWAY-03, KA-LEX-C173-STHALA173-04, KA-LEX-C42-ADJ-02, KA-LEX-C64-JOIN-04] -->

> **ಇದು ಎಲ್ಲಕ್ಕಿಂತ ಚಿಕ್ಕ ಕೋಣೆ.**
>
> *idu ellakkinta cikka kōṇe.* — "This is the smallest room."

**ಎಲ್ಲಕ್ಕಿಂತ** is **ಎಲ್ಲ**, "all", the **ಎಲ್ಲಾ** you know, with **-ಕ್ಕಿಂತ**: "than all", so "most". For people it is **ಎಲ್ಲರಿಗಿಂತ** (*ellariginta*, "than everyone"):

> **ಅವಳು ಎಲ್ಲರಿಗಿಂತ ಬೇಗ ಬಂದಳು.**
>
> *avaḷu ellariginta bēga bandaḷu.* — "She came earliest of all."
