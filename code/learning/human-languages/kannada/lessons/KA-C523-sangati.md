# ಸಂಗಾತಿ (saṅgāti) — a partner; a companion

## You'll want to know: ಸಂಗಾತಿ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C523-SAMSARA523-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸಂಗಾತಿ** — *saṅgāti* — "a partner; a companion".

> ಅಜ್ಜಿ ಅಜ್ಜನ ಐವತ್ತು ವರ್ಷದ ಸಂಗಾತಿ.
>
> *ajji ajjana aivattu varṣada saṅgāti.*
>
> Grandmother has been grandfather's partner for fifty years.

A life partner, and also a comrade: ಪ್ರಯಾಣದ ಸಂಗಾತಿ, a travel companion.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸಂಗಾತಿ** (*saṅgāti*) is built on Sanskrit *saṅga*, "contact, company" (Monier-Williams); the origin of the ending is not settled.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
