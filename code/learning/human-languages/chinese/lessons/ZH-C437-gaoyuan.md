## You'll want to know: gāoyuán
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C437-LAND437-04]; assesses=[] -->

*gāoyuán* — "plateau; highland".

> *xīnán yǒu hěn duō gāoyuán* — There are a lot of high plateaus in the southwest.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*gāoyuán* is built from 高 'high' and *yuán* 'broad flat land', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *kaw* and *ngjwon* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'high' and 'lofty'.
