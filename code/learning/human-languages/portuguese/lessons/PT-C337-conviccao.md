## You'll want to know: a convicção
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C337-ARGUMENTAR-05]; assesses=[] -->

**a convicção** — "the firm belief, the conviction".

> Ele falou com muita convicção.
>
> *He spoke with great conviction.*

*Com convicção* — firmly, with conviction.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*convicção* is a learned borrowing from Latin *convictiō* "demonstration, proof" (EtymDB, Lewis & Short), built on *convincere* "to prove".
