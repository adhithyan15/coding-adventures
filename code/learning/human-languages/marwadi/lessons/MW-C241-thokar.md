## You'll want to know: ठोकर
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C241-FALL241-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ठोकर** — *ṭhokar* — "a stumble; a knock against the foot".

> काल सड़क माथै ठोकर लागी।
>
> *kāl saṛak māthai ṭhokar lāgī.*
>
> I stumbled on the road yesterday.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ठोकर** (*ṭhokar*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*ṭhōkk*, "knock". CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry; it lists Hindi *ṭhokar*, "blow" (CDIAL 5513).
