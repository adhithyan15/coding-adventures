## You'll want to know: غش
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C470-BREATH470-04]; assesses=[] -->

**غش** — *ghash* — "a faint; a swoon".

> تیز دھوپ میں اسے غش آ گیا۔
>
> *tez dhūp meṅ use ghash ā gayā.*
>
> He fainted in the strong sun.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**غش** (*ghash*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *ghashy*, "a covering over", and so a swoon.
