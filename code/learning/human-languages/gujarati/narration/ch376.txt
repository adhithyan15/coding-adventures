Gujarati, chapter 376: Mistakes and Slips.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ભૂલ (bhūl) — a mistake.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to get done, then the Gujarati for to provide.

You'll want to know: ભૂલ.
ભૂલ — bhūl — "a mistake".
જો હિસાબમાં એક પણ ભૂલ હશે તો આખું પરિણામ ખોટું આવશે. jo hisābmā̃ ek paṇ bhūl haśe to ākhũ pariṇām khoṭũ āvśe. If there is even one mistake in the accounts, the whole result will come out wrong.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ભૂલ (bhūl) shares its source with ભૂલવું (bhūlvũ), "to forget": CDIAL lists both under a reconstructed bhull, "err, forget" (CDIAL 9538).

Lesson 4 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for negligence, then the Gujarati for a defect.

You'll want to know: ઢીલ.
ઢીલ — ḍhīl — "slackness; a delay".
જો તમે ઢીલ કરશો તો ટ્રેન છૂટી જશે. jo tame ḍhīl karśo to ṭren chūṭī jaśe. If you dawdle, you will miss the train.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ઢીલ (ḍhīl) CDIAL lists Gujarati ḍhīl, "loose, slack", under a reconstructed ḍhila (CDIAL 5590); "a delay", slack in doing a thing, is the noun's sense.

Lesson 5 of 5.
સુધરવું (sudharvũ) — to get better.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: સુધરવું.
સુધરવું — sudharvũ — "to get better".
જો તમે રોજ અભ્યાસ કરશો તો તમારા ગુણ સુધરશે. jo tame roj abhyās karśo to tamārā guṇ sudharśe. If you study every day, your marks will improve.
Past (he …): તે છોકરો ઘણો સુધર્યો, te chokro ghaṇo sudharyo.

The word, taken apart.
સુધરવું (sudharvũ) is the intransitive partner of સુધારવું (sudhārvũ), "to mend"; CDIAL lists both under a reconstructed śuddhakāra, "making clean" (CDIAL 12521).
