Arabic, chapter 396: Terms and theses.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
مصطلح (muṣṭalaḥ) — a term.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to read up on, then the Arabic for to reflect.

You'll want to know: مصطلح.
مصطلح — muṣṭalaḥ — "a term".
هذا مصطلح علمي جديد. hādhā muṣṭalaḥ ʿilmī jadīd. This is a new scientific term.

The word, taken apart.
مصطلح (muṣṭalaḥ) is built on the native Arabic root ṣ-l-ḥ, "to be good, right" (Lane). Muṣṭalaḥ is the Form VIII passive participle, what people have agreed on: a term.

Lesson 3 of 5.
فهرس (fihris) — an index.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a term, then the Arabic for a definition.

You'll want to know: فهرس.
فهرس — fihris — "an index".
وجدت الكلمة في فهرس الكتاب. wajadtu al-kalima fī fihris al-kitāb. I found the word in the book's index.
Plural: فهارس, fahāris.

The word, taken apart.
فهرس (fihris) was borrowed from Persian fihrist, "a list" (EtymDB); Lane calls it an arabicized word for a catalogue of books (Lane). Today it is an index or table of contents.

كتب الباحث أطروحة عن الهجرة. kataba al-bāḥith uṭrūḥa ʿan al-hijra. The researcher wrote a thesis on migration.

أطروحة (uṭrūḥa) is built on the native Arabic root ṭ-r-ḥ, "to throw, to cast" (Lane). An uṭrūḥa is a thing put forward, thrown out for discussion: a thesis.

منهج (manhaj) — a curriculum.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for an index, then the Arabic for a thesis.

You'll want to know: منهج.
منهج — manhaj — "a curriculum".
غيرت المدرسة منهج العلوم. ghayyarat al-madrasa manhaj al-ʿulūm. The school changed the science curriculum.
Plural: مناهج, manāhij.

The word, taken apart.
منهج (manhaj) is built on the native Arabic root n-h-j, "to be plain, open (of a road)" (Lane). A manhaj is a plain road: a method, and in schools a curriculum.
