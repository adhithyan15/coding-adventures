## You'll want to know: исполнять
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C518-REHEARSAL-01]; assesses=[] -->

**исполнять** (*ispolnyát*) — "to perform; to carry out".

> Певица исполняет старые народные песни.
>
> *Pevítsa ispolnyáyet stáryye naródnyye pésni.*
>
> The singer performs old folk songs.

Imperfective; perfective исполнить. Also *исполнять мечту* — to make a dream come true.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*исполнять* is built on *ис-* and *полный* 'full', so to perform is to fulfil; EtymDB lists the verb without a source.
