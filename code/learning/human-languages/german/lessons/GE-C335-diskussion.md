## You'll want to know: die Diskussion
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C335-DISKUSSION-01]; assesses=[] -->

**die Diskussion** — "the discussion".

> Nach dem Film gab es eine lange Diskussion.
>
> *After the film there was a long discussion.*

Plural: *die Diskussionen*. It goes with the verb *diskutieren*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Diskussion* was borrowed from Latin *discussiō* (EtymDB), from *discutere* "to strike apart, to scatter", which later came to mean "to examine" (Lewis & Short).
