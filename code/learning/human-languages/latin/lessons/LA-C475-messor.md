## You'll want to know: messor, messōris
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C475-MESSIS-02]; assesses=[] -->

**messor, messōris** — "a reaper, a harvester".

> Messōrēs sub sōle ardentī frūmentum metunt.
>
> *The reapers cut the grain under the burning sun.*

Third declension, masculine: *messor, messōris m.*, from *metō* with the agent ending *-or*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*messor* is built on *metō* 'to reap' with the agent ending *-or*, on *mess-*, the stem of its supine (Lewis & Short).
