## You'll want to know: さいきん
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C527-TIME527-03]; assesses=[] -->

**さいきん** — *saikin* — "recently; these days".

> **さいきんの てんきは ばしょに よります。** — *saikin no tenki wa basho ni yorimasu* — "The weather lately depends on where you are."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**さいきん** (*saikin*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *sai*, "utmost" (Middle Chinese *tswajH*) and *kin*, "near" (*gj+nX*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
