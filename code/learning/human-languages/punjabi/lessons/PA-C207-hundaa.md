## Grammar Lens: direct owner
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-FORM-HUNDA-207]; assesses=[PA-LEX-HONA, PA-GRAMMAR-PRESENT-HABITUAL-GENDER-05] -->

> **ਹੁੰਦਾ** — *hundaa* — happens; masculine singular habitual form

Hear and say the whole form before reading it. Keep **ਹੁੰਦਾ** whole. This
chapter uses it only for one repeated event in the small frame that follows.
It does not open the wider habitual agreement system.

## The form, taken apart — source boundary
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-FORM-HUNDA-207, PA-LEX-HONA, PA-GRAMMAR-PRESENT-HABITUAL-GENDER-05] -->

Punjabi University Patiala's lexical statistics identifies **ਹੁੰਦਾ** as the
masculine singular non-perfect form of **ਹੋ**, with continuous or habitual
use. Its reference grammar gives **ਹੋ** as the stem of **ਹੋਣਾ** and lists
**ਹੁੰਦਾ** as Present II. Punjabi University lesson 13 describes the agreeing
*-dā* construction with a present auxiliary, and its verb reference identifies
**ਹੈ** as the third-person singular present auxiliary.
Sources: [Punjabi University lexical statistics](https://www.learnpunjabi.org/statistics.html),
[Punjabi University reference grammar](https://pt.learnpunjabi.org/assets/A%20Reference%20Grammar_Final.pdf),
[Punjabi University lesson 13](https://pt.learnpunjabi.org/lesson13.aspx?l=13),
and [Punjabi University verb reference](https://www.learnpunjabi.org/verb.html).

Those sources support the modern form. Reuse the history already attached to
**ਹੋਣਾ**; do not invent a second etymology for an inflected form. This chapter
teaches the habitual reading only, not generic “is,” a progressive,
counterfactual “would,” a past form, or a feminine or plural form.
