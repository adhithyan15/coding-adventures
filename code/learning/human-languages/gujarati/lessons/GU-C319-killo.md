## You'll want to know: કિલ્લો
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C319-KILLO319-01]; assesses=[] -->

**કિલ્લો** — *killo* — "a fort".

> ટેકરી પર એક જૂનો કિલ્લો છે.
>
> *ṭekrī par ek jūno killo che.*
>
> There is an old fort on the hill.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**કિલ્લો** (*killo*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *qalʿa*, "a fortress". It is masculine: **જૂનો કિલ્લો**, an old fort.
