## You'll want to know: a anca
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C482-CORPO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**a anca** — "hip".

> A avó partiu a anca numa queda.
>
> *Grandma broke her hip in a fall.*

In Brazil the usual word is *o quadril*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*anca* comes from Vulgar Latin *hanca* (EtymDB), which was taken from a Germanic word for the hip.
