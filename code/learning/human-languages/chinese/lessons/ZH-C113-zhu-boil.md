## You'll want to know: zhǔ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C113-COOK113-02]; assesses=[] -->

*zhǔ* — "to boil; to cook in water".

> *wǒmen zhǔ jiǎozi* — We're boiling dumplings.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*zhǔ* is inherited from Middle Chinese *tsyoX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), listed under a variant form of the character and glossed 'to cook'.
