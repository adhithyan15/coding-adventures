## You'll want to know: naufragar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C328-NAVEGAR-03]; assesses=[] -->

**naufragar** — "to be shipwrecked, to sink".

> O barco naufragou perto da costa.
>
> *The boat sank near the coast.*

Also of plans: *o projeto naufragou*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*naufragar* is a learned borrowing from Latin *naufragāre* "to suffer shipwreck", built on *nāvis* "a ship" and *frangere* "to break" (Lewis & Short).
