# જઈશ, થશે, હશે — the short verbs

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-GRAMMAR-C149-SHE-01, GU-GRAMMAR-C149-SHO-01, GU-LEX-JAVU, GU-LEX-LEVU] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say *she will come tomorrow*, then ask *when will you come?* And two
short verbs: **જવું**, *to go*, and **લેવું**, *to take*.

## Grammar Lens: જવું, લેવું, કરવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-GRAMMAR-C149-JAISH-01]; assesses=[GU-LEX-JAVU, GU-LEX-LEVU, GU-CONCEPT-C05-KAAMKARVUN-01, GU-LEX-BAJAR-01] -->

**જવું** and **લેવું** have short stems, and they put an **ઈ** where the long
verbs have **-ી-**:

- **હું જઈશ** — *jaīś* — I will go; **અમે જઈશું**; **તે જશે**; **તમે જશો**;
- **હું લઈશ** — *laīś* — I will take; **તે લેશે**.

**કરવું** is regular: **હું કરીશ**, **અમે કરીશું**, **તે કરશે**.

> **હું આવતીકાલે બજાર જઈશ.** — *hũ āvtīkāle bajār jaīś.* — I'll go to the market tomorrow.

## You'll want to know: થશે and હશે
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-GRAMMAR-C149-THASHE-01]; assesses=[GU-GRAMMAR-C147-THAYO-01, GU-LEX-HOVU, GU-LEX-C147-PAST147-01, GU-GRAMMAR-C147-HATO-01] -->

**થવું**, *to happen*, and **હોવું**, *to be*, are heard most often with **-શે**:

> **મોડું થશે.** — *moḍũ thaśe.* — It will be late, word for word *lateness will happen*.
> **તે ઘરે હશે.** — *te ghare haśe.* — She will be at home.

**હશે** is the future of **છે**: **છે** now, **હતો** before, **હશે** later. With
**તમે** it is **હશો**: **શું તમે ઘરે હશો?**, *will you be at home?* Said about
right now, **હશે** is a guess: **તે ઘરે હશે** can also mean *she must be at home*.
