## You'll want to know: پیشکش
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C547-TASAVVUR547-04]; assesses=[] -->

**پیشکش** — *peshkash* — "an offer".

> اگر یہ پیشکش اچھی ہوئی تو میں قبول کروں گا۔
>
> *agar yih peshkash achchhī huī to maiṅ qabūl karūṅ gā.*
>
> If the offer is good, I will accept it.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**پیشکش** (*peshkash*) is usually traced to Persian *pēshkash*, "something offered", built from *pēsh*, "before", and *kash-*, "to draw".
