Sanskrit, chapter 74: At the Market.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
क्रीणाति (krīṇāti) — buys.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for she calls, then the Sanskrit for he bows.

You'll want to know: क्रीणाति.
क्रीणाति (krīṇāti) — "(he, she) buys". फलं क्रीणाति — "buys fruit".

What you've built.
The verb of the market.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: krīṇāti]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: krīṇāti, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say phalaṁ krīṇāti]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Sanskrit for she calls, then the Sanskrit for he bows, then say krīṇāti again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does क्रीणाति (krīṇāti) mean? ("Buys".) Say it once more.
