## You'll want to know: গৃহহীন
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C290-DHEU290-04]; assesses=[] -->

**গৃহহীন** — *grihôhīn* — "homeless".

> বন্যার পরে অনেক পরিবার গৃহহীন হল।
>
> *bônyār pôre ônek pôribār grihôhīn hôlô.*
>
> After the flood many families were left homeless.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**গৃহহীন** (*grihôhīn*) joins Sanskrit *gṛha*, 'a house, a home', to *hīna*, 'left, deprived of' (Monier-Williams): without a house. It is a word of the news more than of talk.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
