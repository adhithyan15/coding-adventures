## You'll want to know: કોથળો
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C433-OUTFIT433-03]; assesses=[] -->

**કોથળો** — *kothḷo* — "a sack".

> હમાલ ઘઉંનો ભારે કોથળો ઊંચકે છે.
>
> *hamāl ghaũno bhāre kothḷo ū̃cke che.*
>
> The porter lifts a heavy sack of wheat.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**કોથળો** (*kothḷo*) goes back to *\*kōtthala*, a form CDIAL reconstructs; CDIAL lists the Gujarati word, "a large bag" (CDIAL 3511).
