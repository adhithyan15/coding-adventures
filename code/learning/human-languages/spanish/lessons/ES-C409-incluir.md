# incluir — close it inside the whole

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-RESERVAR] -->

[PAUSE 2s] You can **reservar** a room. Now ask what comes inside the price:
**¿Incluye el desayuno?**, “Does it include breakfast?”

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-INCLUIR]; assesses=[] -->

Spanish borrowed **incluir** from Latin **inclūdere**, literally “close in”:
**in**, “in,” plus **claudere**, “close.” English **include**, **exclude** and
**conclude** share the same learned family. Something included is enclosed
inside the whole.

## Grammar Lens: the useful third-person form
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-INCLUIR] -->

The infinitive is **incluir**, but hotel messages often use **incluye**: **el
precio incluye el desayuno**. In a question, keep the same form and lift the
intonation: **¿Incluye el desayuno?**
