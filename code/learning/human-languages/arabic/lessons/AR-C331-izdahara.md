## You'll want to know: ازدهر
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C331-IMBARATUR331-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ازدهر** — *izdahara* — "to flourish, to thrive".

> ازدهرت التجارة في المدينة.
>
> *izdaharat at-tijāra fī al-madīna.*
>
> Trade flourished in the city.

Present: يزدهر, *yazdahir*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ازدهر** (*izdahara*) is built on the native Arabic root *z-h-r* (Lane), "to shine; to blossom", the root of *zahra*, a flower. It is Form VIII, where the *t* turns to *d* after *z*.
