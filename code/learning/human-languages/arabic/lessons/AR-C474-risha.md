## You'll want to know: ريشة
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C474-SAQR474-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ريشة** — *rīsha* — "a feather; a quill".

> وجد الطفل ريشة بيضاء.
>
> *wajada aṭ-ṭifl rīsha bayḍāʾ.*
>
> The child found a white feather.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ريشة** (*rīsha*) is built on the native Arabic root *r-y-sh*, "feathers" (Lane). It is one of *rīsh*, "feathers".
