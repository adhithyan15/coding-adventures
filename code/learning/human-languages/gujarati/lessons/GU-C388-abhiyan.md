## You'll want to know: અભિયાન
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C388-UPAKRAM388-03]; assesses=[] -->

**અભિયાન** — *abhiyān* — "a public drive; a campaign".

> જો આ અભિયાન ચાલશે તો ગામ ચોખ્ખું રહેશે.
>
> *jo ā abhiyān cālśe to gām cokhkhũ raheśe.*
>
> If this campaign keeps going, the village will stay clean.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**અભિયાન** (*abhiyān*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *abhiyāna*, "coming near, approaching" (Monier-Williams); "a campaign" is its modern sense.
