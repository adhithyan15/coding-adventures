Marathi, chapter 447: Herons and Kites.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
बगळा (bagḷā) — a heron; an egret.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to roar, then the Marathi for to graze.

You'll want to know: बगळा.
बगळा — bagḷā — "a heron; an egret".
शेतात पांढरे बगळे बसले आहेत. śetāt pāṇḍhre bagḷe basle āhet. White egrets are sitting in the field.
Masculine. Plural: बगळे, bagḷe.
The plural is बगळे.

The word, taken apart.
बगळा (bagḷā) belongs with Sanskrit baka, "a heron" (CDIAL 9115); how the Marathi form was shaped is not settled.

Lesson 2 of 5.
घार (ghār) — a black kite.

[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: घार.
घार — ghār — "a black kite".
आकाशात एक घार उडत होती. ākāśāt ek ghār uḍat hotī. A kite was flying in the sky.
Feminine.
A feminine noun: घार (ghār) उडत होती.

The word, taken apart.
घार (ghār) is the Marathi word for the black kite, the brown bird of prey seen over every town; its origin is not settled.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: गिधाड.
गिधाड — gidhāḍ — "a vulture".
आजकाल गिधाडं फार कमी दिसतात. ājkāl gidhāḍaṁ phār kamī distāt. These days vultures are seldom seen.
Neuter.
A neuter noun: one गिधाड (gidhāḍ), many गिधाडं.

The word, taken apart.
गिधाड (gidhāḍ) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi gidhāḍ, "vulture, kite", under Sanskrit gṛdhra, "greedy; a vulture" (CDIAL 4233).

Lesson 4 of 5.
किलबिलणे (kilbilṇe) — to chirp.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a black kite, then the Marathi for a vulture.

You'll want to know: किलबिलणे.
किलबिलणे — kilbilṇe — "to chirp".
पहाटे झाडावर पक्षी किलबिलतात. pahāṭe jhāḍāvar pakṣī kilbiltāt. At dawn the birds chirp in the tree.
Past (he …): तो किलबिलला, to kilbillā.
The noun is किलबिल, the chirping of many birds.

The word, taken apart.
किलबिलणे (kilbilṇe) is an echo verb built on kilbil, the twitter of small birds; its origin is not settled.

Lesson 5 of 5.
स्थलांतर (sthalāntar) — migration.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a vulture, then the Marathi for to chirp.

You'll want to know: स्थलांतर.
स्थलांतर — sthalāntar — "migration".
हिवाळ्यात अनेक पक्षी इथे स्थलांतर करतात. hivāḷyāt anek pakṣī ithe sthalāntar kartāt. In winter many birds migrate here.
Neuter.
स्थलांतर (sthalāntar) करणे, to migrate; also people moving for work.

The word, taken apart.
स्थलांतर (sthalāntar) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit sthalāntara, "another place" (Monier-Williams): going over to another place.
