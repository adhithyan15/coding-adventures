## You'll want to know: बिसूरना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C499-KISHOR499-05]; assesses=[] -->

**बिसूरना** — *bisūrnā* — "to whimper; to sulk tearfully".

> छोटा भाई मिठाई के लिए बिसूर रहा था।
>
> *choṭā bhāī miṭhāī ke liye bisūr rahā thā.*
>
> My little brother was whimpering for a sweet.

Past (he …): वह दिन भर बिसूरा, *vah din bhar bisūrā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**बिसूरना** (*bisūrnā*) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Hindi *bisūrnā*, "to put on a crying face", under *\*viśūratē*, a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 11941).
