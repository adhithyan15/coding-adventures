## You'll want to know: в случае
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C370-USLOVIYE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**в случае** (*v slúchaye*) — "in case of, in the event of".

> В случае дождя концерт будет в зале.
>
> *V slúchaye dozhdyá kontsért búdet v zále.*
>
> In case of rain the concert will be in the hall.

*В случае* + genitive: *в случае дождя*. Before a clause, *в случае если…*

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**в случае** is *в* "in" with the prepositional of *случай* "an event, a chance", inherited from Proto-Slavic *\*sŭlučajĭ*, from *\*lučiti*, tentatively "to find, to reach" (EtymDB).
