## You'll want to know: পাসবই
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C415-SUD415-02]; assesses=[] -->

**পাসবই** — *pāsbôi* — "a passbook (for a bank account)".

> বাবা পাসবই খুলে সুদের টাকা দেখলেন।
>
> *bābā pāsbôi khule suder ṭākā dekhlen.*
>
> Father opened the passbook and looked at the interest.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**পাসবই** (*pāsbôi*) joins English *pass*, from *passbook*, to the Bengali **বই**, "a book": half borrowed and half translated.
