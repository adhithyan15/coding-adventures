## You'll want to know: はいたつ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C305-YUUBIN305-04]; assesses=[] -->

**はいたつ** — *haitatsu* — "a delivery".

> **ゆうびんの はいたつは ごぜんです。** — *yūbin no haitatsu wa gozen desu* — "The post is delivered in the morning."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**はいたつ** (*haitatsu*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *hai*, "distribute" (Middle Chinese *phwojH*) and *tatsu*, "arrive" (*dat*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
