## You'll want to know: તંગી
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C380-PURTU380-02]; assesses=[] -->

**તંગી** — *tangī* — "a shortage".

> જો ઉનાળામાં વરસાદ મોડો આવશે તો પાણીની તંગી પડશે.
>
> *jo unāḷāmā̃ varsād moḍo āvśe to pāṇīnī tangī paḍśe.*
>
> If the rain comes late this summer, there will be a water shortage.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**તંગી** (*tangī*) is usually traced to Persian *tangī*, "narrowness, want".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
