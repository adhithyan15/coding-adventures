## You'll want to know: 飞快
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C413-FLY413-05]; assesses=[] -->

**飞快** — *fēikuài* — "very fast; as fast as flying".

> **时间过得飞快** *shíjiān guò de fēikuài* — Time flies.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**飞快** (*fēikuài*) is built from 飞 'to fly' and 快 'fast', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *pj+j* and *khwaejH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'to one's liking'; the sense 'fast' is not given there.
