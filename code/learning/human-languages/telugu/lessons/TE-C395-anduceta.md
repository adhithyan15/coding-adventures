# అందుచేత (anducēta) — for that reason; therefore

## You'll want to know: అందుచేత
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C395-KANUKA395-02]; assesses=[] -->

**అందుచేత** — *anducēta* — "for that reason; therefore".

> అతనికి జ్వరం వచ్చింది, అందుచేత పనికి రాలేదు.
>
> *ataniki jvaraṁ vaccindi, anducēta paniki rālēdu.*
>
> He had a fever, and for that reason he did not come to work.

అందు, in that, + చేత, by: by that. A cousin of అందువల్ల, which you know.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**అందుచేత** (*anducēta*) is native Telugu: *andu*, "in that, there", built on the far-pointing base *a-* (DEDR 1), and *cēta*, "by", the ending that marks the means.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
