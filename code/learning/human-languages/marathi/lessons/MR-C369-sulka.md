## You'll want to know: शुल्क
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C369-VIDYAPITH369-04]; assesses=[] -->

**शुल्क** — *śulka* — "a fee".

> महाविद्यालयाचं शुल्क दरवर्षी वाढतं.
>
> *mahāvidyālyācaṁ śulka darvarṣī vāḍhtaṁ.*
>
> The college fee goes up every year.

Neuter.

Formal; in speech फी.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**शुल्क** (*śulka*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *śulka*, "price, value; tax, toll" (CDIAL 12543).
