## You'll want to know: どきどきする
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C427-NERVES427-02]; assesses=[] -->

**どきどきする** — *dokidoki suru* — "to have a pounding heart".

> **しけんの まえは むねが どきどきします。** — *shiken no mae wa mune ga dokidoki shimasu* — "Before an exam my heart pounds."

Polite **どきどきします**, *dokidokishimasu*; a する verb. Past (polite): **かのじょの むねが どきどきしました。**, *kanojo no mune ga dokidoki shimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**どきどきする** (*dokidoki suru*) is a native Japanese mimetic word, *dokidoki*, inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, made a verb with *suru*, "to do"; its older origin is not settled (JMdict).
