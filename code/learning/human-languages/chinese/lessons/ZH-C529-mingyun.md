## You'll want to know: mìngyùn
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C529-LUCK529-05]; assesses=[] -->

*mìngyùn* — "fate; destiny".

> *jíshǐ mìngyùn bù hǎo wǒmen yě yào nǔlì* — Even if fate is against us, we have to keep trying.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*mìngyùn* is built from *mìng* and 运 'luck', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *mjaengH* and *hjunH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to order' and 'to send': what has been ordered for you.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what would happen.
