# ಆನಂದಿಸು (ānandisu) — to enjoy

## You'll want to know: ಆನಂದಿಸು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C492-HARSHA492-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ಆನಂದಿಸು** — *ānandisu* — "to enjoy".

> ಮಕ್ಕಳು ಕಡಲತೀರದಲ್ಲಿ ಆಟವನ್ನು ಆನಂದಿಸಿದರು.
>
> *makkaḷu kaḍalatīradalli āṭavannu ānandisidaru.*
>
> The children enjoyed playing on the beach.

Past (I …): ಆನಂದಿಸಿದೆ, *ānandiside*.

What you enjoy takes -ಅನ್ನು. The ending -ಇಸು makes a verb of the Sanskrit noun ಆನಂದ, bliss.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಆನಂದಿಸು** (*ānandisu*) is built from Sanskrit *ānanda*, "happiness" (CDIAL 1172), with the Kannada ending -*isu* that turns a Sanskrit noun into a verb.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
