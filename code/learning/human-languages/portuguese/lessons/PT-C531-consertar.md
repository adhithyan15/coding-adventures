## You'll want to know: consertar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C531-BRICOLAGE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**consertar** — "to repair, to fix".

> O meu pai consertou a cadeira partida.
>
> *My father fixed the broken chair.*

Not *concertar*, which means to arrange or to agree.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*consertar* is usually traced to Latin *cōnsertus* "joined together", from *cōnserere* "to join" (Lewis & Short).
