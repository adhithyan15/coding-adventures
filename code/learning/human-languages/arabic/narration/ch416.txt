Arabic, chapter 416: Instead and besides.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
تبنى (tabannā) — to adopt.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to arrive at, then the Arabic for a level.

You'll want to know: تبنى.
تبنى — tabannā — "to adopt".
تبنت الأسرة طفلا صغيرا. tabannat al-usra ṭiflan ṣaghīran. The family adopted a small child.
Present: يتبنى, yatabannā.
Also to adopt an idea: تبنى (tabannā) الفكرة.

The word, taken apart.
تبنى (tabannā) is built on the native Arabic root b-n-y, "to build" (Lane). It is Form V, to take someone as one's son, ibn, a word Lane files under this root: to adopt.

Lesson 2 of 5.
بدلا من (badalan min) — instead of.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: بدلا من.
بدلا من — badalan min — "instead of".
شربت الشاي بدلا من القهوة. sharibtu ash-shāy badalan min al-qahwa. I drank tea instead of coffee.

The word, taken apart.
بدلا من (badalan min) joins badalan, "as a substitute", built on the native Arabic root b-d-l, "to change, to put in place of" (Lane), with min, "from": instead of.

Lesson 3 of 5.
بالإضافة إلى (bi-l-iḍāfa ilā) — in addition to.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to adopt, then the Arabic for instead of.

You'll want to know: بالإضافة إلى.
بالإضافة إلى — bi-l-iḍāfa ilā — "in addition to".
اشتريت خبزا بالإضافة إلى الحليب. ishtaraytu khubzan bi-l-iḍāfa ilā al-ḥalīb. I bought bread in addition to milk.

The word, taken apart.
بالإضافة إلى (bi-l-iḍāfa ilā) joins bi-, "with", al-iḍāfa, "the adding", built on the native Arabic root ḍ-y-f, "to be a guest; to join to" (Lane), and ilā, "to": in addition to. أضاف, to add, is from the same root.

Lesson 4 of 5.

[pause 2 seconds]

تأمين — taʾmīn — "insurance".
عندي تأمين صحي. ʿindī taʾmīn ṣiḥḥī. I have health insurance.

The word, taken apart.
تأمين (taʾmīn) is built on the native Arabic root ʾ-m-n, "to be safe, secure" (Lane). Taʾmīn is the Form II verbal noun, making safe: insurance. آمن, safe, is from the same root.

Lesson 5 of 5.
سوى (siwā) — apart from; except.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for in addition to, then the Arabic for insurance.

You'll want to know: سوى.
سوى — siwā — "apart from; except".
لم يحضر سوى ثلاثة طلاب. lam yaḥḍur siwā thalāthat ṭullāb. No one came apart from three students.

The word, taken apart.
سوى (siwā) is built on the native Arabic root s-w-y, "to be equal, level" (Lane). Siwā is an old noun of the root, the like of, other than: apart from, except.
