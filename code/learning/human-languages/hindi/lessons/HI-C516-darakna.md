## You'll want to know: दरकना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C516-RISNA516-02]; assesses=[] -->

**दरकना** — *daraknā* — "to crack, to split".

> भूकंप के बाद दीवार दरक गई।
>
> *Bhūkamp ke bād dīvār darak gaī.*
>
> After the earthquake the wall cracked.

Past (it …): वह शीशा दरक गया, *vah śīśā darak gayā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**दरकना** (*daraknā*) is a tadbhava, inherited from Sanskrit *darayati*, "causes to split" (CDIAL 6192). CDIAL gives the Hindi verb as "to be torn".
