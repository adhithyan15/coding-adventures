## You'll want to know: ちょうせんする
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C460-GOAL460-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ちょうせんする** — *chōsen suru* — "to take on a challenge".

> **むすこは たかい やまに ちょうせんしたいです。** — *musuko wa takai yama ni chōsen shitai desu* — "My son wants to take on a high mountain."

Polite **ちょうせんします**, *chōsenshimasu*; a する verb. Past (polite): **せんしゅが ちょうせんしました。**, *senshu ga chōsen shimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ちょうせんする** (*chōsen suru*) is Sino-Japanese (kango) *chōsen* with the native verb *suru*, "to do": two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *chō*, "challenge" (Middle Chinese *thew*) and *sen*, "battle" (*tsyenH*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
