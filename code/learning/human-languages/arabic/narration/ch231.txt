Arabic, chapter 231: Treatment, Prescription, Clinic, Pharmacy, Nurse.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
علاج (ʿilāj) — treatment.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to divide, then the Arabic for to kick.

You'll want to know: علاج.
علاج — ʿilāj — "treatment".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]

[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say ʿilāj]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Arabic for to divide, then the Arabic for to kick, then say ʿilāj again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does علاج (ʿilāj) mean? ("Treatment".) Say it once more.

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
To tidy? (رتب, rattaba.) To sweep? (كنس, kanasa.) To iron? (كوى, kawā.) To fold? (طوى, ṭawā.) To hang? (علق, ʿallaqa.) To earn? (كسب, kasaba.) To borrow? (اقترض, iqtaraḍa.) To lend? (أقرض, aqraḍa.) To change? (غير, ghayyara.) To hurry? (أسرع, asraʿa.) To pull; to withdraw? (سحب, saḥaba.) To prepare? (حضر, ḥaḍḍara.) To repair? (أصلح, aṣlaḥa.) To use? (استعمل, istaʿmala.) To bring? (أحضر, aḥḍara.) To set off? (انطلق, inṭalaqa.) To charge? (شحن, shaḥana.) To apologise? (اعتذر, iʿtadhara.) To inform? (أخبر, akhbara.) To agree? (اتفق, ittafaqa.)
