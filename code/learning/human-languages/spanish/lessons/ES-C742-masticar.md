## You'll want to know: masticar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C742-DIETA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**masticar** — "to chew".

> Hay que masticar bien la comida.
>
> *You have to chew your food well.*

Spelling: *mastiqué*. *Mascar* is the older, everyday twin.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*masticar* is a learned borrowing from Late Latin *masticāre* "to chew" (EtymDB); the inherited form of the same word is *mascar*.
