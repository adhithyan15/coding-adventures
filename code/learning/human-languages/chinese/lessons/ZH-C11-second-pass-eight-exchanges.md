# Eight exchanges, one meeting

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ZH-DIALOGUE-GREET, ZH-DIALOGUE-PART] -->

[PAUSE 3s] Every chapter so far has ended on a two-voice exchange, and you have
practised each one on its own. From memory: the one that opens a meeting, and
the one that closes it.

## You'll want to know: they were always one conversation
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ZH-DIALOGUE-GREET, ZH-DIALOGUE-INTRODUCE, ZH-DIALOGUE-ASK, ZH-DIALOGUE-IDENTIFY, ZH-DIALOGUE-NEGATE, ZH-DIALOGUE-THANK, ZH-DIALOGUE-POLITE, ZH-DIALOGUE-PART] -->

Eight exchanges arrived one per chapter. Put them in the order a meeting uses
them and this book's whole first act is there:

| the move | what it added |
|---|---|
| greet | 你好, both voices |
| introduce, ask | a name given, a name asked for |
| identify, negate | saying it is so, and saying it is not |
| thank, be polite | 谢谢, and the reply that follows it |
| part | 再见 |

Nothing in that column is new. What is new is that **no chapter ever asked you
to run them one after another**, and a meeting is exactly that: eight small
moves with no pause between them for looking anything up.

## Guided Practice: the whole meeting, no stops
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ZH-DIALOGUE-GREET, ZH-DIALOGUE-INTRODUCE, ZH-DIALOGUE-ASK, ZH-DIALOGUE-IDENTIFY, ZH-DIALOGUE-THANK, ZH-DIALOGUE-PART] -->

[PAUSE 1s]
- [YOU SAY: both voices of the greeting]
- [YOU SAY: give your name, then ask for theirs]
- [YOU SAY: answer a yes-or-no question, once affirming and once denying]
- [YOU SAY: thank them, then give the reply you would receive]
- [YOU SAY: take your leave]

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ZH-DIALOGUE-GREET, ZH-DIALOGUE-NEGATE, ZH-DIALOGUE-IDENTIFY, ZH-DIALOGUE-PART, ZH-DIALOGUE-INTRODUCE, ZH-DIALOGUE-ASK, ZH-DIALOGUE-THANK, ZH-DIALOGUE-POLITE] -->

[PAUSE 3s] Which exchange opens a meeting, and which closes it? (**你好** and **再见**.) Which pair is a yes against a no? (**Identify** and **negate**.) What did the thanks exchange add beyond the word itself? (**The reply** that comes back.) And what is new here, when every piece is old? (**Running them together**, with no stop.)
