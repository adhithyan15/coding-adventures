## You'll want to know: il borseggiatore
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C291-SCIPPO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**il borseggiatore** — "the pickpocket".

> Attenzione ai borseggiatori sull'autobus!
>
> *Watch out for pickpockets on the bus!*

Built on *borsa*, ‘bag’: someone who goes through your bag.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*borseggiatore* is built on *borseggiare* "to pick pockets" (EtymDB), from *borsa* "bag", which is usually traced to Late Latin *bursa* "a purse".
