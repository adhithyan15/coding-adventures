## You'll want to know: مکمل
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C526-MIAD526-05]; assesses=[] -->

**مکمل** — *mukammal* — "complete; finished".

> اگر کام مکمل ہوا تو ہم کل چھٹی کریں گے۔
>
> *agar kām mukammal huā to ham kal chhuṭṭī kareṅ ge.*
>
> If the work is finished, we will take tomorrow off.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**مکمل** (*mukammal*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *mukammal*, "made complete".
