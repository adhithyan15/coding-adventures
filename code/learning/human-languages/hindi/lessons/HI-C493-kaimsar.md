## You'll want to know: कैंसर
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C493-DAMA493-04]; assesses=[] -->

**कैंसर** — *kaiṁsar* — "cancer".

> डाक्टर ने कहा कि दादा को कैंसर नहीं है।
>
> *ḍākṭar ne kahā ki dādā ko kaiṁsar nahīṃ hai.*
>
> The doctor said my grandfather doesn't have cancer.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**कैंसर** (*kaiṁsar*) is borrowed from English *cancer*.
