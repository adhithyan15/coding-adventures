## You'll want to know: ارمان
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C369-KASH369-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ارمان** — *armān* — "a longing; a cherished wish".

> اگر وہ آتا تو میرا ارمان نکل جاتا۔
>
> *agar voh ātā to merā armān nikal jātā.*
>
> If he had come, my wish would have come true.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ارمان** (*armān*) is usually traced to Persian *armān*, "a longing, a hope".
