Persian, chapter 371: For this reason.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
به دلیل (be dalil-e) — because of, due to.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to lead to, then the Persian for to conclude.

You'll want to know: به دلیل.
به دلیل (be dalil-e) — "because of, due to".
جاده به دلیل برف بسته شد. jâdde be dalil-e barf baste shod. The road was closed because of snow.
Takes a noun with ezafe; with a clause, be dalil-e inke ….

The word, taken apart.
به دلیل (be dalil-e) joins the Persian preposition be to dalil, from Arabic dalīl (EtymDB glosses it "guide"): by reason of.

Lesson 2 of 5.
به همین دلیل (be hamin dalil) — for this reason, that is why.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to conclude, then the Persian for because of.

You'll want to know: به همین دلیل.
به همین دلیل (be hamin dalil) — "for this reason, that is why".
کارش را دوست نداشت، به همین دلیل دنبال یک کار تازه است. kârash râ dust nadâsht, be hamin dalil donbâl-e yek kâr-e tâze ast. He did not like his job; that is why he is looking for a new one.
Be + hamin "this very" + dalil "reason". It opens a clause that states a result.

The word, taken apart.
به همین دلیل (be hamin dalil) joins the native words be "to" and hamin "this very" to dalil, from Arabic dalīl (EtymDB): for this very reason.

Lesson 3 of 5.
از این رو (az in ru) — hence, for this reason.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for because of, then the Persian for for this reason.

You'll want to know: از این رو.
از این رو (az in ru) — "hence, for this reason".
قیمتها بالا رفت، از این رو مردم کمتر خرید کردند. qeymathâ bâlâ raft, az in ru mardom kamtar kharid kardand. Prices went up; hence people bought less.
Formal, mostly written. Az "from" + in "this" + ru "face, side".

The word, taken apart.
از این رو (az in ru) is native Persian, literally "from this face": az "from" (EtymDB traces it to Old Persian), in "this" and ru "face, side".

Lesson 4 of 5.
لذا (lezâ) — therefore (formal).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for for this reason, then the Persian for hence.

You'll want to know: لذا.
لذا (lezâ) — "therefore (formal)".
هوا خیلی بد بود، لذا جلسه برگزار نشد. havâ kheyli bad bud, lezâ jalase bargozâr nashod. The weather was very bad; therefore the meeting was not held.
Formal and written; in speech barâye hamin. A whole clause follows. ذ sounds like z.

The word, taken apart.
لذا (lezâ) is usually traced to the Arabic phrase li-dhā "for this"; Persian says the Arabic dh as z.

Lesson 5 of 5.
با توجه به (bâ tavajjoh be) — considering, given.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for hence, then the Persian for therefore.

You'll want to know: با توجه به.
با توجه به (bâ tavajjoh be) — "considering, given".
با توجه به قیمت، این ماشین خوب است. bâ tavajjoh be qeymat, in mâshin khub ast. Considering the price, this car is good.
Bâ "with" + tavajjoh "attention" + be "to". It takes a noun; with a clause, bâ tavajjoh be inke ….

The word, taken apart.
با توجه به (bâ tavajjoh be) is literally "with attention to": bâ and be are Persian, and tavajjoh "attention" is usually traced to Arabic tawajjuh "turning towards".
