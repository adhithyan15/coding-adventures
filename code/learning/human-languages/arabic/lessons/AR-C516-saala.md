## You'll want to know: سعل
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C516-HARAKA516-04]; assesses=[] -->

**سعل** — *saʿala* — "to have a cough".

> سعل المريض كثيرا في الليل.
>
> *saʿala al-marīḍ kathīran fī al-layl.*
>
> The patient coughed a lot in the night.

Present: يسعل, *yasʿul*.

The noun سعال, a cough, shares the root.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**سعل** (*saʿala*) is built on the native Arabic root *s-ʿ-l*, "to cough" (Lane). Suʿāl, a cough, which you know, is its noun.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
