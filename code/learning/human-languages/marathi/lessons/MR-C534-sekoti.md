## You'll want to know: शेकोटी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C534-BAHER534-05]; assesses=[] -->

**शेकोटी** — *śekoṭī* — "a campfire; a bonfire".

> रात्री सगळे शेकोटीभोवती बसले.
>
> *rātrī sagḷe śekoṭībhovtī basle.*
>
> At night everyone sat around the campfire.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**शेकोटी** (*śekoṭī*) is built on शेकणे, "to warm oneself at a fire": CDIAL lists Marathi *śekṇẽ* under *\*sēkk*, "heat, foment, roast" (CDIAL 13581). The ending -ओटी is not explained there.

## What you've built
