## You'll want to know: قصه گو
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C345-QESSE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**قصه گو** (*qesse gu*) — "a storyteller".

> قصه گو داستان را با صدای بلند تعریف کرد.
>
> *Qesse gu dâstân râ bâ sedâ-ye boland ta'rif kard.*
>
> The storyteller told the story in a loud voice.

*qesse* "tale" + *gu*, the present stem of *goftan* "to say".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*qesse* "tale" is borrowed from Arabic *qiṣṣa* (EtymDB), and *gu-* is the present stem of the native verb *goftan* "to say": a tale-sayer.
