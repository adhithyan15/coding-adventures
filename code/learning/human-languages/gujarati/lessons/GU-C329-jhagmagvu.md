# ઝગમગવું (jhagmagvũ) — to sparkle

## You'll want to know: ઝગમગવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C329-RANGOLI329-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ઝગમગવું** — *jhagmagvũ* — "to sparkle".

> દિવાળીની રાત્રે આખું શહેર દીવાથી ઝગમગતું હતું.
>
> *divāḷīnī rātre ākhũ śaher dīvāthī jhagmagtũ hatũ.*
>
> On Diwali night the whole city sparkled with lamps.

Past (it …): તે શહેર ઝગમગ્યું, *te śaher jhagmagyũ*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ઝગમગવું** (*jhagmagvũ*) is an imitative word: CDIAL sets the Hindi cousin *jagmag*, "glitter", under *\*jag*, "glitter", a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 5076), but does not list the Gujarati verb. The doubled sound *jhag-mag* imitates light that flickers and shines.
