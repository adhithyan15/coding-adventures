## You'll want to know: наказывать
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C294-NAKAZANIYE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**наказывать** (*nakázyvat*) — "to punish".

> Родители редко наказывали сына.
>
> *Rodíteli rédko nakázyvali sýna.*
>
> The parents rarely punished their son.

Imperfective; perfective *наказать* (*nakazát*), *накажу*, *накажешь*. The reason takes *за* + accusative: *наказать за ложь*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*наказывать* is built on *на-* and *казать* "to show, to tell", inherited from Proto-Slavic *kazati* (EtymDB): first "to instruct".
