# 月 — the moon, four strokes

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[JA-LEX-C252-TSUKIHI-01, JA-SCRIPT-KANJI-NICHI-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say *tsukihi*, the passing of time. Its second sign is **日**, which you write. Today the first.

## Script — the shape
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-SCRIPT-KANJI-TSUKI-01]; assesses=[JA-SCRIPT-KANJI-NICHI-01] -->

> **月** — *tsuki*

Its parts:

- a tall frame, open at the bottom
- two short bars inside

Four strokes, three lifts:

1. draw the left side from the top down, sweeping a little to the left at the foot
2. lift, draw the top from left to right, then without lifting turn down the right side and hook left at its foot
3. lift, draw the upper inside bar from left to right
4. lift, draw the lower inside bar from left to right

Like **日** it is taller than it is wide and has bars inside, but it is open at the bottom, and its first stroke sweeps away to the left. Here it is read **つき** (*tsuki*), as in **月日**. Kanji have more than one reading; this lesson asks only for the one in the word you know.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[JA-SCRIPT-KANJI-TSUKI-01, JA-LEX-C252-TSUKIHI-01] -->

1. Find **月** in **月日** and say *tsukihi*.
2. Copy **月** three times, saying *tsuki* at each one.
3. Find it once more, in **月日は はやいです** (*tsukihi wa hayai desu*), and say the line.
