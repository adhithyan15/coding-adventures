## You'll want to know: ندم
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C493-NADAM493-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ندم** — *nadima* — "to regret".

> ندم الولد لأنه كذب على أمه.
>
> *nadima al-walad li-annahu kadhaba ʿalā ummihi.*
>
> The boy was sorry because he had lied to his mother.

Present: يندم, *yandam*.

It takes على: ندم على ما قال.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ندم** (*nadima*) is built on the native Arabic root *n-d-m*, "to repent, to regret" (Lane). The noun nadam is regret.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
