## You'll want to know: le citoyen
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C362-CITOYEN-01]; assesses=[] -->

**le citoyen** — "the citizen".

> Chaque citoyen a le droit de voter.
>
> *Every citizen has the right to vote.*

Feminine *citoyenne*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*citoyen* is inherited from Old French *citoien* (EtymDB), built on *cité* "city", from Latin *cīvitās* "citizenship, a state", from *cīvis* "citizen" (Lewis & Short).
