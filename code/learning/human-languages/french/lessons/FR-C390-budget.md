## You'll want to know: le budget
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C390-BUDGET-01]; assesses=[] -->

**le budget** — "the budget".

> Notre budget pour les vacances est de mille euros.
>
> *Our holiday budget is a thousand euros.*

The final *t* is silent.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*budget* was borrowed from English *budget* (EtymDB), which came from Old French *bougette* "a little leather bag", a small form of *bouge* "bag". *bouge* goes back to Late Latin *bulga* "a leather bag", a Gaulish word (EtymDB; Lewis & Short).
