6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
あいさつ (aisatsu) — a greeting.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for a delivery, then the Japanese for after a long time.

You'll want to know: あいさつ.
あいさつ — aisatsu — "a greeting".
あさ、となりの おばあさんに あいさつしました。 — asa, tonari no obāsan ni aisatsu shimashita — "In the morning I said hello to the old lady next door."

The word, taken apart.
あいさつ (aisatsu) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are ai, "push open" (Middle Chinese 'eajX) and satsu, "draw close" (tsat) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).

よろしく (yoroshiku) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, and it is usually written in kana (JMdict).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for a greeting, then the Japanese for kind regards.

You'll want to know: ねんがじょう.
ねんがじょう — nengajō — "a New Year's card".
しょうがつに ねんがじょうが とどきました。 — shōgatsu ni nengajō ga todokimashita — "Greeting cards arrived for the new year."

The word, taken apart.
ねんがじょう (nengajō) is Sino-Japanese (kango): three Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are nen, "year" (Middle Chinese nen), ga, "congratulations" (haH) and jō, "form" (dzrjangH) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).

Lesson 4 of 6.
ふなびん (funabin) — surface mail; sea mail.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for kind regards, then the Japanese for a New Year's card.

You'll want to know: ふなびん.
ふなびん — funabin — "surface mail; sea mail".
はこを ふなびんで おくりました。 — hako o funabin de okurimashita — "I sent the box by sea mail."

The word, taken apart.
ふなびん (funabin) is a mixed word, native and Sino-Japanese: funa, "ship", is native Japanese, the form fune takes in compounds, and bin, "letter", is a Chinese character read with its on'yomi, a reading borrowed from Chinese (Middle Chinese bjienH) (JMdict, KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).

Lesson 5 of 6.
もらう (morau) — to receive; to get.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for a New Year's card, then the Japanese for surface mail.

You'll want to know: もらう.
もらう — morau — "to receive; to get".
そふから ねんがじょうを もらいました。 — sofu kara nengajō o moraimashita — "I got a greeting card from my grandfather."
Polite もらいます, moraimasu; a godan verb. Past (polite): いもうとが はがきを もらいました。, imōto ga hagaki o moraimashita.

The word, taken apart.
もらう (morau) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict).
