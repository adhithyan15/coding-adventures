# ਪਿਘਲਣਾ (pighalṇā) — to melt

## You'll want to know: ਪਿਘਲਣਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C296-SLIP296-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ਪਿਘਲਣਾ** — *pighalṇā* — "to melt".

> ਧੁੱਪ ਵਿੱਚ ਬਰਫ਼ ਪਿਘਲਦੀ ਹੈ।
>
> *dhupp vich baraf pighaldī hai.*
>
> Ice melts in the sunshine.

Past (it …): ਉਹ ਬਰਫ਼ ਪਿਘਲੀ, *oh baraf pighlī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਪਿਘਲਣਾ** (*pighalṇā*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*praghirati*, "flows forth"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *pighalnā*, "to be dissolved, be melted", under it (CDIAL 8486).
