Urdu, chapter 520: In the Wrestling Pit.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
پہلوان (pahalvān) — a wrestler.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a gathering (for music or poetry), then the Urdu for to make (someone) dance.

You'll want to know: پہلوان.
پہلوان — pahalvān — "a wrestler".
پہلوان روز صبح دودھ پیتا ہے۔ pahalvān roz subah dūdh pītā hai. The wrestler drinks milk every morning.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
پہلوان (pahalvān) is usually traced to Persian pahlavān, "a hero, a champion".

پہلوان کے پاس دو بھاری مگدر ہیں۔ pahalvān ke pās do bhārī mugdar haiṅ. The wrestler has two heavy clubs.

Lesson 3 of 5.
داؤ (dāo) — a move (in wrestling).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a wrestler, then the Urdu for an Indian club (for exercise).

You'll want to know: داؤ.
داؤ — dāo — "a move (in wrestling)".
استاد نے اسے ایک نیا داؤ سکھایا۔ ustād ne use ek nayā dāo sikhāyā. His trainer taught him a new move.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
داؤ (dāo) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin dā̃w, "a turn", under Sanskrit dātu, "a share" (CDIAL 6258); the same entry also mentions the sense "a move in a game", so the step from "a turn" to "a move" is an old one.

Lesson 4 of 5.
بھڑانا (bhiṛānā) — to pit against each other.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for an Indian club (for exercise), then the Urdu for a move (in wrestling).

You'll want to know: بھڑانا.
بھڑانا — bhiṛānā — "to pit against each other".
استاد نے دونوں پہلوانوں کو بھڑایا۔ ustād ne donoṅ pahalvānoṅ ko bhiṛāyā. The trainer pitted the two wrestlers against each other.
Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے دو لڑکوں کو بھڑایا, us ne do laṛkoṅ ko bhiṛāyā.

The word, taken apart.
بھڑانا (bhiṛānā) is the causative of bhiṛnā, "to clash": CDIAL lists the Hindi cousins bhiṛnā and bhiṛānā, "to bring together", under bhiṭ, "meet, throng", a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 9490).
