## You'll want to know: સમુદ્ર
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C442-SEA442-01]; assesses=[] -->

**સમુદ્ર** — *samudra* — "the ocean; the sea (formal)".

> સમુદ્રનું પાણી ખારું છે.
>
> *samudranũ pāṇī khārũ che.*
>
> The water of the ocean is salty.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**સમુદ્ર** (*samudra*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *samudra*, "a gathering together of waters", the sea (Monier-Williams); speech says દરિયો.
