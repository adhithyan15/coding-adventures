## You'll want to know: doctrīna, doctrīnae
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C407-SCIENTIA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**doctrīna, doctrīnae** — "teaching, learning; a doctrine".

> Vir magnae doctrīnae erat.
>
> *He was a man of great learning.*

*doctrīna, doctrīnae* f., built on *doctor*, a teacher, from *doceō*. English *doctrine*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*doctrīna* is built on *doctor* "a teacher" (EtymDB), which comes from *doceō* "to teach" (Lewis & Short). English *doctrine* comes from it.
