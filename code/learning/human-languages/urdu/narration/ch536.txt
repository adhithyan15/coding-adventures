Urdu, chapter 536: Being Careful.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
محتاط (mohtāt) — careful; cautious.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for an astrologer, then the Urdu for a horoscope.

You'll want to know: محتاط.
محتاط — mohtāt — "careful; cautious".
اگر آپ محتاط ہوتے تو یہ حادثہ نہ ہوتا۔ agar āp mohtāt hote to yih hādsā na hotā. If you had been careful, this accident would not have happened.

The word, taken apart.
محتاط (mohtāt) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic muḥtāṭ, "on one's guard".

[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a horoscope, then the Urdu for cautious.

You'll want to know: چوکنا.
چوکنا — chaukannā — "alert; on guard".
اگر کھلاڑی چوکنا ہے تو گیند نہیں چھوٹے گی۔ agar khilāṛī chaukannā hai to gend nahīṅ chhūṭe gī. If the player is alert, the ball won't slip past him.

The word, taken apart.
چوکنا (chaukannā) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin caukannā, "vigilant", under Sanskrit catuṣkarṇa, "heard by four ears" (CDIAL 4630): with both ears pricked up.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for alert, then the Urdu for a warning.

You'll want to know: آڑ.
آڑ — āṛ — "cover (to shelter behind)".
اگر بارش ہوئی تو ہم درخت کی آڑ لیں گے۔ agar bārish huī to ham darakht kī āṛ leṅ ge. If it rains, we'll shelter under a tree.
