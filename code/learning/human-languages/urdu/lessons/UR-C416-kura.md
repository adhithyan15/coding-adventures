## You'll want to know: کوڑا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C416-KURA416-01]; assesses=[] -->

**کوڑا** — *kūṛā* — "rubbish".

> گلی میں کوڑا نہیں پھینکنا چاہیے۔
>
> *galī meṅ kūṛā nahīṅ pheṅknā chāhiye.*
>
> You shouldn't throw rubbish in the lane.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**کوڑا** (*kūṛā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *kūṛā*, "a dust heap", under Sanskrit *kūṭa*, "a heap" (CDIAL 3392).
