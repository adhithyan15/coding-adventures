## You'll want to know: anexar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C398-FICHEIRO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**anexar** — "to attach (a file); to annex".

> Anexei o meu currículo à mensagem.
>
> *I attached my CV to the message.*

*Em anexo* — attached, enclosed.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*anexar* is built on *anexo* "attachment", from Latin *annexus* "a connection", on *nectere* "to bind" (Lewis & Short).
