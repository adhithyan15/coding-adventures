## You'll want to know: अतिथी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C414-ATITHI414-01]; assesses=[] -->

**अतिथी** — *atithī* — "an honoured guest".

> समारंभाला मोठे अतिथी आले होते.
>
> *samārambhālā moṭhe atithī āle hote.*
>
> Distinguished guests had come to the ceremony.

Masculine.

More formal than पाहुणा; the plural is the same: एक अतिथी, अनेक अतिथी.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अतिथी** (*atithī*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *atithi*, "a guest" (Monier-Williams).
