## You'll want to know: контракт
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C363-PEREGOVORY-02]; assesses=[] -->

**контракт** (*kontrákt*) — "a contract".

> Он подписал контракт на три года.
>
> *On podpisál kontrákt na tri góda.*
>
> He signed a three-year contract.

Masculine. *Работать по контракту* — to work on a contract. Close to *договор*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**контракт** was borrowed from German *Kontrakt*, from Latin *contractus* (EtymDB) "a drawing together, an agreement", from *contrahere* "to draw together" (Lewis & Short).
