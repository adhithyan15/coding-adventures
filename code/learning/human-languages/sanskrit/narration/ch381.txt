Sanskrit, chapter 381: Usually and Especially.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
सामान्यतः (sāmānyataḥ) — generally; as a rule.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for as much as one likes, then the Sanskrit for expands.

You'll want to know: सामान्यतः.
सामान्यतः — sāmānyataḥ — "generally; as a rule".
सामान्यतः, यदि अहं श्रान्तः, तर्हि शीघ्रं स्वपिमि। sāmānyataḥ, yadi ahaṃ śrāntaḥ, tarhi śīghraṃ svapimi. Generally, if I am tired, I fall asleep quickly.

The word, taken apart.
सामान्यतः (sāmānyataḥ) is built on sāmānya, "common, general", with the ending -tas; MW gives sāmānya-tas "in general, generally" (MW).

Lesson 5 of 5.
संक्षिपति (saṅkṣipati) — sums up; shortens.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for in every way, then the Sanskrit for repeatedly.

You'll want to know: संक्षिपति.
संक्षिपति — saṅkṣipati — "sums up; shortens".
यदि समयः अल्पः, तर्हि वक्ता भाषणं संक्षिपति। yadi samayaḥ alpaḥ, tarhi vaktā bhāṣaṇaṃ saṅkṣipati. If time is short, the speaker shortens the speech.
Past (he …): सः भाषणं संक्षिप्तवान्, saḥ bhāṣaṇaṃ saṅkṣiptavān.

The word, taken apart.
संक्षिपति (saṅkṣipati) is built on sam, "together", and the root √kṣip, "to throw"; MW gives saṃ-√kṣip "to throw or heap together" and "to concentrate", and "to shorten" is how modern Sanskrit uses it (MW).
