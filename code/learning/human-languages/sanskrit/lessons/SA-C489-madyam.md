## You'll want to know: मद्यम्
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C489-VYASANA489-05]; assesses=[] -->

**मद्यम्** — *madyam* — "liquor".

> मद्यं यकृत् पीडयति।
>
> *madyaṃ yakṛt pīḍayati.*
>
> Liquor harms the liver.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**मद्यम्** (*madyam*) comes from the root √mad, "to be drunk, to rejoice"; MW gives "any intoxicating drink, liquor, wine" (MW).
