Italian, chapter 283: In redazione.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
la redazione — the newsroom, the editorial staff.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for the headline, then the Italian for the article.

You'll want to know: la redazione.
la redazione — "the newsroom, the editorial staff".
In redazione lavorano venti persone. Twenty people work in the newsroom.
Also the writing of a text: la redazione di un documento.

The word, taken apart.
redazione is usually traced to French rédaction, built on Latin redāctus, the past participle of redigere "to bring back, to put in order" (Lewis & Short).

il cronista — "the news reporter".
Il cronista ha parlato con i vicini. The reporter spoke to the neighbours.
A woman is la cronista: same form.

The word, taken apart.
cronista is built on cronaca "chronicle, news" with the ending -ista; the root is Greek khrónos "time" (EtymDB).

l'inviato — "the correspondent, the reporter sent out".
L'inviato del giornale è arrivato ieri. The paper's correspondent arrived yesterday.
The past participle of inviare, ‘to send’; un inviato speciale is a special correspondent.

The word, taken apart.
inviato is the past participle of inviare "to send", usually traced to Late Latin inviāre "to put on the way", built on via "way" (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 4 of 5.
l'intervista — the interview.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for the news reporter, then the Italian for the correspondent.

You'll want to know: l'intervista.
l'intervista — "the interview".
L'intervista è durata un'ora. The interview lasted an hour.
Fare un'intervista a qualcuno — to interview someone.

The word, taken apart.
intervista is usually said to be modelled on English interview, which goes back to French entrevue "a meeting", from entrevoir "to glimpse"; the root is Latin vidēre "to see" (Lewis & Short).
