## You'll want to know: допустим
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C371-GIPOTEZA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**допустим** (*dopústim*) — "let us say, say".

> Допустим, ты прав. Что тогда?
>
> *Dopústim, ty prav. Chto togdá?*
>
> Let us say you are right. What then?

A form of *допустить*, to allow: let us allow that… It also gives an example: *допустим, в пятницу*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**допустим** is a form of *допустить* "to allow, to admit", built from *до-* and *пустить* "to let go", which is usually linked with *пустой* "empty".
