## You'll want to know: sūnnǚ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C510-GRAND510-03]; assesses=[] -->

*sūnnǚ* — "granddaughter".

> *nǎinai měi tiān sòng sūnnǚ qù shàngxué* — Grandma takes her granddaughter to school every day.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*sūnnǚ* is built from *sūn* 'a grandchild' and 女 'a daughter', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *swon* and *nrjoX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription).
