## You'll want to know: つかる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C398-ONSEN398-04]; assesses=[] -->

**つかる** — *tsukaru* — "to soak (in water)".

> **あつい ゆに ゆっくり つかりました。** — *atsui yu ni yukkuri tsukarimashita* — "I had a long soak in the hot water."

Polite **つかります**, *tsukarimasu*; a godan verb. Past (polite): **ちちが おんせんに つかりました。**, *chichi ga onsen ni tsukarimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**つかる** (*tsukaru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict).
