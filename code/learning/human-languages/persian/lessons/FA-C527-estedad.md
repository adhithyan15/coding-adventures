## You'll want to know: استعداد
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C527-TALENT-01]; assesses=[] -->

**استعداد** (*este'dâd*) — "talent, aptitude".

> او در موسیقی استعداد زیادی دارد.
>
> *u dar musiqi este'dâd-e ziyâdi dârad.*
>
> He has a great talent for music.

*dar chizi este'dâd dâshtan* — to have a gift for something.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*este'dâd* is a loan, usually traced to Arabic *istiʿdād*, 'readiness, aptitude'. EtymDB has no entry for it.
