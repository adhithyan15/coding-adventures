## You'll want to know: ਤਜਵੀਜ਼
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C345-DECIDE345-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ਤਜਵੀਜ਼** — *tajvīz* — "a proposal; a plan put forward".

> ਉਸ ਦੀ ਤਜਵੀਜ਼ ਬੁਰੀ ਨਹੀਂ ਹੈ।
>
> *us dī tajvīz burī nahīṁ hai.*
>
> His proposal is not a bad one.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਤਜਵੀਜ਼** (*tajvīz*) is a loan, usually traced through Persian to Arabic *tajwīz*, "allowing".
