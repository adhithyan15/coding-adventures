# ਸੋਮਵਾਰ — Monday

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

[YOU HEAR: *somvaar*; YOU SAY: **ਸੋਮਵਾਰ**] Start with the sound. It means *Monday*.

## Grammar Lens: one weekday
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-WEEKDAY-SOMVAR-01]; assesses=[] -->

> **ਸੋਮਵਾਰ** — *somvār* — Monday

Keep it as one calendar label. A full week comes slowly, one day at a time.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Somvār* joins Sanskrit *soma*, "moon", with *vāra*, "day of the week" (CDIAL 11547): the moon day.
