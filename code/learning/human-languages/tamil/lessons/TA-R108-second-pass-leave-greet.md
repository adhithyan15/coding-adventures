# Hello and goodbye, the everyday way — a second pass

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[TA-LEX-C108-GREET-01, TA-LEX-C108-GREET-02] -->

[PAUSE 3s] You meet an old friend around lunchtime. Say two things.

## Grammar Lens: written and spoken
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[TA-LEX-C108-GREET-04, TA-LEX-C108-GREET-05] -->

| spoken | written |
|---|---|
| **நல்லா** | **நன்றாக** |
| **இருக்கீங்க** | **இருக்கிறீர்கள்** |

Spoken Tamil wears the written forms short. Both are right; they are for different places.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[TA-LEX-C107-LEAVE-01, TA-LEX-C107-LEAVE-02, TA-LEX-C107-LEAVE-03, TA-LEX-C107-LEAVE-04, TA-LEX-C107-LEAVE-05, TA-LEX-C108-GREET-01, TA-LEX-C108-GREET-02, TA-LEX-C108-GREET-03, TA-LEX-C108-GREET-04, TA-LEX-C108-GREET-05] -->

[PAUSE 2s each]
- [YOU SAY: ask a friend how they are, the spoken way; then what's new]
- [YOU SAY: at the door: shall we leave? see you later]
- [YOU SAY: wish a traveller a good journey; ask them to come again]
- [YOU SAY: take your leave formally]

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[TA-LEX-C107-LEAVE-01, TA-LEX-C107-LEAVE-02, TA-LEX-C107-LEAVE-03, TA-LEX-C107-LEAVE-04, TA-LEX-C107-LEAVE-05, TA-LEX-C108-GREET-01, TA-LEX-C108-GREET-02, TA-LEX-C108-GREET-03, TA-LEX-C108-GREET-04, TA-LEX-C108-GREET-05] -->

[PAUSE 3s] I take my leave? (**விடைபெறுகிறேன்**.) Shall we leave? (**கிளம்பலாமா?**) See you
later? (**அப்புறம் பார்க்கலாம்**.) A good journey? (**இனிய பயணம்**.) Come again? (**மீண்டும்
வாருங்கள்**.) Have you eaten? (**சாப்பிட்டீர்களா?**) Long time? (**ரொம்ப நாளாச்சு**.) What's
new? (**என்ன விசேஷம்?**) Are you well? (**நல்லா இருக்கீங்களா?**) How are you? (**எப்படி
இருக்கீங்க?**)
