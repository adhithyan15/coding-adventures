## You'll want to know: xīngjí
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C426-ROOM426-04]; assesses=[] -->

*xīngjí* — "hotel star rating".

> *zhè jiā jiǔdiàn de xīngjí hěn gāo* — This hotel has a lot of stars.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*xīngjí* is built from 星 'a star' and *jí* 'a rank', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *seng* and *kip* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription).
