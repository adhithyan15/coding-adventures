## You'll want to know: せぼね
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C442-INSIDE442-04]; assesses=[] -->

**せぼね** — *sebone* — "the spine; the backbone".

> **そふの せぼねは まがっています。** — *sofu no sebone wa magatte imasu* — "My grandfather's spine is bent."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**せぼね** (*sebone*) is a native Japanese compound (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese: *se*, "back" and *bone*, "bone (hone)" (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
