Marathi, chapter 468: Gentle Natures.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
मृदू (mṛdū) — soft-hearted; gentle.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to stay alive, then the Marathi for to wobble.

You'll want to know: मृदू.
मृदू — mṛdū — "soft-hearted; gentle".
आमच्या शिक्षिका स्वभावाने मृदू आहेत. āmcyā śikṣikā svabhāvāne mṛdū āhet. Our teacher is gentle by nature.
Unchanging: मृदू (mṛdū) आवाज, a gentle voice. Sanskrit writes the word with a short final vowel.

The word, taken apart.
मृदू (mṛdū) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit mṛdu, "soft, weak" (CDIAL 10292), with the final vowel lengthened as Marathi writes it.

Lesson 3 of 5.
हळवा (haḷvā) — tender-hearted.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for soft-hearted, then the Marathi for mild.

You'll want to know: हळवा.
हळवा — haḷvā — "tender-hearted".
माझी बहीण खूप हळवी आहे, लगेच रडते. mājhī bahīṇ khūp haḷvī āhe, lagec raḍte. My sister is very tender-hearted; she cries at once.
Agrees: हळवा (haḷvā), हळवी, हळवं. हळवं मन, a sensitive heart.

The word, taken apart.
हळवा (haḷvā) is usually traced to Sanskrit laghu, "light, quick": CDIAL lists Marathi haḷū, "light", and haḷvā, "ripening quickly", under it (CDIAL 10896). The sense "tender-hearted" is not given there, so the link is not certain.

Lesson 4 of 5.
संवेदनशील (saṁvedanśīl) — sensitive.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for mild, then the Marathi for tender-hearted.

You'll want to know: संवेदनशील.
संवेदनशील — saṁvedanśīl — "sensitive".
संवेदनशील माणूस लवकर दुखावतो. saṁvedanśīl māṇūs lavkar dukhāvto. A sensitive person is quickly hurt.
संवेदना, feeling, + शील, of the nature of. Unchanging.

The word, taken apart.
संवेदनशील (saṁvedanśīl) is a learned compound built from two Sanskrit words, saṃvedana, "perceiving, feeling", and śīla, "habit, nature" (Monier-Williams).
