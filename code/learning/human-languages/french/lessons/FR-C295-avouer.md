## You'll want to know: avouer
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C295-AVEUX-01]; assesses=[] -->

**avouer** — "to confess, to admit".

> Il a fini par avouer.
>
> *In the end he confessed.*

*Je dois avouer que…* — I must admit that…

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*avouer* is inherited through Old French *avoer* from Latin *advocāre* "to call to, to call upon" (EtymDB).
