Arabic, chapter 205: Nature, Dust, Mud, Borders, Planet.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
طبيعة (ṭabīʿa) — nature.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a capital city, then the Arabic for where.

You'll want to know: طبيعة.
طبيعة — ṭabīʿa — "nature".

The word, taken apart.
طبيعة (ṭabīʿa) is built on the native Arabic root ṭ-b-ʿ, "to seal, to stamp" (Lane). Ṭabīʿa is the impress stamped on a thing, its natural disposition: nature.

Lesson 5 of 5.
كوكب (kawkab) — a planet.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for mud, then the Arabic for borders.

You'll want to know: كوكب.
كوكب — kawkab — "a planet".

The word, taken apart.
كوكب (kawkab) is listed by Lane under k-w-k-b, where he notes the grammarians' dispute over its letters and suggests, in his own note, that it came from Hebrew kōkhāv, "star". The two are better taken as Semitic cousins.
