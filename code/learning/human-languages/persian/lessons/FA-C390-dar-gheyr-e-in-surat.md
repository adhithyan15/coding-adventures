# در غیر این صورت (dar gheyr-e in surat) — otherwise, if not

## You'll want to know: در غیر این صورت
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C390-SHART-05]; assesses=[] -->

**در غیر این صورت** (*dar gheyr-e in surat*) — "otherwise, if not".

> او باید پول را تا فردا بدهد، در غیر این صورت جریمه میشود.
>
> *u bâyad pul râ tâ fardâ bedahad, dar gheyr-e in surat jarime mishavad.*
>
> He must pay by tomorrow; otherwise he will be fined.

A formal *vagarna*; a whole clause follows.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**در غیر این صورت** is literally "in other than this form": *gheyr* comes from Arabic *ghayr* "other than" and *surat* from Arabic *ṣūra* "form" (both in EtymDB), held together by the Persian words *dar* "in" and *in* "this".
