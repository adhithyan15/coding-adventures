## You'll want to know: témoigner
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C389-ENQUETE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**témoigner** — "to testify, to give evidence".

> Elle a accepté de témoigner au procès.
>
> *She agreed to testify at the trial.*

*Témoigner de* — to show, to bear witness to.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*témoigner* comes from Old French *tesmoignier* (EtymDB), formed on *tesmoing* "witness", from Latin *testimōnium* "testimony", built on *testis* "witness" (Lewis & Short).
