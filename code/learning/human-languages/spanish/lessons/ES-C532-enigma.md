## You'll want to know: el enigma
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C532-SECRETO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**el enigma** — "the enigma, the puzzle".

> Su desaparición sigue siendo un enigma.
>
> *His disappearance is still a puzzle.*

Masculine although it ends in *-a*: *un enigma*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*enigma* is a learned borrowing from Latin *aenigma*, which comes from Greek *aínigma* 'a riddle' (EtymDB).
