## You'll want to know: concordare
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C342-COMPROMESSO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**concordare** — "to agree (on), to settle together".

> Abbiamo concordato il prezzo con il proprietario.
>
> *We agreed on the price with the owner.*

*Concordare con qualcuno* — to agree with someone; *concordare una data* — to agree on a date. A little more formal than *essere d'accordo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*concordare* is a learned borrowing from Latin *concordāre* "to agree, to be of one mind" (Lewis & Short), from *concors* "of one heart", built from *con-* "together" and *cor, cordis* "heart".
