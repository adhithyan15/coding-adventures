## You'll want to know: frequentare
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C379-LICEO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**frequentare** — "to attend; to go around with (people)".

> Se vuoi passare l'esame, devi frequentare le lezioni.
>
> *If you want to pass the exam, you have to attend the lessons.*

Also of people: *frequentare brutte compagnie* — to keep bad company.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*frequentare* is a learned borrowing from Latin *frequentāre* "to visit often, to crowd" (Lewis & Short), from *frequēns* "crowded, frequent".
