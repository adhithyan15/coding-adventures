Gujarati, chapter 25: Hand and Money Join the Map.
7 lessons. You can do the first 3 of them in the car; after that you will want to have stopped.


Lesson 1 of 7.
હાથ (hāth) — hear hand first.

Warm-up.
Say home. Now listen twice: hāth means hand. Keep its long vowel and final puff distinct from ghar.

Guided Practice.
Touch your hand when you hear hāth, then say hāth once from the meaning.

Wrap-up Recall.
Source: Gujarati Wiktionary word index.

Lesson 3 of 7.
પૈસા (paisā) — hear money first.

Warm-up.
Say hand. Now listen twice: paisā means money. Do not read the Gujarati shape yet; one vowel sign still needs its own tiny lesson.

Guided Practice.
Choose money or hand as you hear paisā and hāth. Then say paisā from the meaning cue.

Wrap-up Recall.

Lesson 4 of 7.
ૈ (ai vowel sign) — one sign for ai.

Before we start: this one needs your hands, so it is not a driving lesson. You will want a pen and something to write on. You can listen to all of it now and come back to the parts that need looking at once you have stopped.

Warm-up.
The attached sign ૈ (ai vowel sign) carries the ai sound. It sits above a consonant; by itself here it is only a writing shape, not a word.

Guided Practice.
Watch the model: make the lower high arc from left to right, lift, then add the second higher arc from left to right. Trace ૈ (ai vowel sign) twice and copy it once.

Wrap-up Recall.
Stroke-order source: t30apps Gujarati Alphabet Writing Practice, fitted to the bundled Noto Sans Gujarati form documented in data/scripts/gujarati.json.
[question — say your answer, then pause 12 seconds]
Copy the attached ai sign once.

Lesson 6 of 7.
Retrieve five concrete words — five concrete place, body, and money words.

Warm-up.
[pause 15 seconds]
Recall the three-place checkpoint, then say hand and money.

Guided Practice.
Hear all five in mixed order, say each from its meaning, read the five printed cards, then write the two words named aloud.

Wrap-up Recall.
Sources: Gujarati Wiktionary word index, Cambridge: temple, and Cambridge: money.

Lesson 7 of 7.
Payoff — five words in four skills.

Warm-up.
[pause 15 seconds]
Recall the three-place score, then say hand and money.

Guided Practice.
Identify five heard words.
Produce five words from meaning cues.
Match five printed cards to meanings.
Write three heard words without a model.
Pass each skill separately. A strong score in one skill cannot replace a miss in another.

Wrap-up Recall.
Sources: Gujarati Wiktionary word index, Cambridge: temple, and Cambridge: money.
[question — say your answer, then pause 45 seconds]
After all four skills pass separately, write the five words in order.
