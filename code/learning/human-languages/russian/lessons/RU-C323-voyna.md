## You'll want to know: война
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C323-VOYNA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**война** (*voyná*) — "a war".

> Война продолжалась пять лет.
>
> *Voyná prodolzhálas pyat let.*
>
> The war went on for five years.

Feminine; plural *войны*. *На войне* — in the war.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*война* is inherited through Old East Slavic *voina* from Proto-Slavic *vojĭna* "war", built on *vojĭ* "a soldier" (EtymDB).
