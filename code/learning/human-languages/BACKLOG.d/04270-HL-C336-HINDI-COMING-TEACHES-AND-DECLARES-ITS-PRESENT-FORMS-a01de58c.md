## HL-C336 — Hindi coming teaches and declares its present forms

After the action-chain repair, **आता** is the highest-ranked independent modern
Hindi form in the glossed-but-never-taught queue. Chapter 73 already models and
assesses **आता हूँ** as the final verb in an action chain, but Chapter 42's
introduction to **आना** stops at the infinitive and asks learners only to
repeat that dictionary form.

Derive **आता**, **आती**, and **आते** in Chapter 42 using the present-form pattern
learners already know, practise and recall the set, and declare the complete
family as the lesson headword. This closes the gap before the later assessed
use and makes the forms visible to reports and downstream cards.
