## You'll want to know: le mollet
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C477-JAMBE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**le mollet** — "calf (of the leg)".

> Le cycliste a des mollets très forts.
>
> *The cyclist has very strong calves.*

The soft back part of the lower leg.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*le mollet* is built on *mol*, the old form of *mou* "soft", with the small ending *-et* (EtymDB); *mou* is inherited from Latin *mollis* "soft" (Lewis & Short).
