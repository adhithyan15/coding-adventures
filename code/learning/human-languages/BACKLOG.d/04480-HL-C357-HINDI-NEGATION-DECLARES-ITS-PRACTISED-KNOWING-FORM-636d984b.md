## HL-C357 — Hindi negation declares its practised knowing form

Chapter 7 teaches sentence negation through **मैं नहीं जानता**, glosses the
whole sentence as “I do not know,” and asks the learner to produce it in guided
practice and recall it again at wrap-up. Its declaration exposes only **नहीं**,
leaving **जानता** looking untaught to the corpus report.

Declare **जानता** beside **नहीं**. This makes the lesson's repeatedly modelled
and practised masculine present form visible to reports and downstream study
artifacts without inventing forms the lesson does not present.
