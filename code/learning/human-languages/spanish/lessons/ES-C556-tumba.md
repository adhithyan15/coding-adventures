## You'll want to know: la tumba
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C556-ENTIERRO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**la tumba** — "the grave, the tomb".

> Pusimos flores en su tumba.
>
> *We put flowers on his grave.*

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*tumba* comes from Late Latin *tumba*, taken from Greek *týmbos* 'a burial mound' (EtymDB).
