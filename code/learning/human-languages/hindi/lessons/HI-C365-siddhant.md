## You'll want to know: सिद्धांत
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C365-SIDDHANT365-01]; assesses=[] -->

**सिद्धांत** — *siddhānt* — "a principle, a theory".

> यह सिद्धांत किताब में अच्छा लगता है।
>
> *yah siddhānt kitāb meṁ acchā lagtā hai.*
>
> This theory looks good in a book.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सिद्धांत** (*siddhānt*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *siddhānta*, "an established conclusion" (Monier-Williams).
