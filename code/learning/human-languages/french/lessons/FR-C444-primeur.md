## You'll want to know: le primeur
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C444-COMMERCES-05]; assesses=[] -->

**le primeur** — "the greengrocer; early produce".

> Chez le primeur, les fraises sont déjà arrivées.
>
> *At the greengrocer's the strawberries are already in.*

*Les primeurs* are the first fruit and vegetables of the year.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*primeur* is built on the old adjective *prime* 'first', from Latin *prīmus* (Lewis & Short): the first produce of the year. EtymDB does not list it.
