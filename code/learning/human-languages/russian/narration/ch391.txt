Russian, chapter 391: Laws of the land.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
законодательство (zakonodátelstvo) — legislation, the laws (of a country).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a right, then the Russian for responsibility.

You'll want to know: законодательство.
законодательство (zakonodátelstvo) — "legislation, the laws (of a country)".
В законодательстве есть новые правила. V zakonodátelstve yest nóvyye právila. There are new rules in the legislation.
Neuter. All the laws of a country taken together.

The word, taken apart.
законодательство (zakonodátelstvo) is built from закон "a law" and -датель, from дать "to give", with the suffix -ство: the giving of laws.

Lesson 2 of 5.
конституция (konstitútsiya) — a constitution.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for responsibility, then the Russian for legislation.

You'll want to know: конституция.
конституция (konstitútsiya) — "a constitution".
Конституция — главный закон страны. Konstitútsiya — glávnyy zakón straný. The constitution is the main law of the country.
Feminine. It also means a person’s build.

The word, taken apart.
конституция (konstitútsiya) is usually traced, through western languages, to Latin cōnstitūtiō "an arrangement, a decree" (Lewis & Short), from cōnstituere "to set up, to establish".

Lesson 3 of 5.
гражданство (grazhdánstvo) — citizenship.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for legislation, then the Russian for a constitution.

You'll want to know: гражданство.
гражданство (grazhdánstvo) — "citizenship".
У неё двойное гражданство. U neyó dvoynóye grazhdánstvo. She has dual citizenship.
A neuter noun. Двойное гражданство (grazhdánstvo) — dual citizenship. From гражданин, a citizen.

The word, taken apart.
гражданство (grazhdánstvo) is built on гражданин "a citizen", on град, the Church Slavonic form of native город "a town", Proto-Slavic gordŭ. The -ра- is the Church Slavonic shape, -оро- the native one.

Warm-up.

юридический (yuridícheskiy) — "of the law, legal".
Мне нужна юридическая помощь. Mne nuzhná yuridícheskaya pómoshch. I need legal help.
Юридический факультет — a law faculty.

The word, taken apart.
юридический (yuridícheskiy) is usually traced to Latin iūridicus "of the administering of justice", from iūs "right, law" and dīcere "to say" (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 5 of 5.
ограничение (ogranichéniye) — a restriction, a limit.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for citizenship, then the Russian for of the law.

You'll want to know: ограничение.
ограничение (ogranichéniye) — "a restriction, a limit".
Есть ограничения по возрасту. Yest ogranichéniya po vózrastu. There are age restrictions.
Neuter. Ограничение скорости — a speed limit.

The word, taken apart.
ограничение (ogranichéniye) is built on ограничить "to restrict", from граница "a border", which is built on грань "an edge": to restrict is to draw a border round a thing.
