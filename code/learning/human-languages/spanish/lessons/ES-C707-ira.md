## You'll want to know: la ira
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C707-ENFADO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**la ira** — "anger, wrath".

> Habló con ira.
>
> *He spoke with anger.*

A strong, written word; in conversation people say *rabia* or *enfado*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ira* comes from Latin *īra* "anger" (EtymDB), the source of English *ire* and *irate* too.
