## You'll want to know: челюсть
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C482-HEAD-05]; assesses=[] -->

**челюсть** (*chélyust*) — "a jaw".

> После удара у него болела челюсть.
>
> *Pósle udára u negó boléla chélyust.*
>
> After the blow his jaw hurt.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*челюсть* is inherited from Proto-Slavic *čeľustь* (EtymDB).
