## You'll want to know: डोंगररांग
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C442-SHIKHAR442-05]; assesses=[] -->

**डोंगररांग** — *ḍoṅgarrāṅg* — "a mountain range".

> पश्चिमेला एक मोठी डोंगररांग आहे.
>
> *paścimelā ek moṭhī ḍoṅgarrāṅg āhe.*
>
> There is a great mountain range to the west.

Feminine.

डोंगर, a hill, and रांग, a row.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**डोंगररांग** (*ḍoṅgarrāṅg*) is built from **डोंगर**, "a hill", and **रांग**, "a row, a line", both of uncertain origin.
