## Script — 口 beside the whole of 那
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-SCRIPT-NA-WHERE-01]; assesses=[] -->

> 哪

**Two characters you own, side by side.** Nothing in it is new.

**口, three strokes**, small, on the left: the left side, the top that turns down the right side, the bottom bar.

**那, six strokes**, whole, as you write it alone: the hooked turn across the top and down the right, the upper bar, the lower bar, the left-falling stroke through them, then the ear on the right, across, back, round and hooking, and its long vertical.

3 + 6 = nine.
