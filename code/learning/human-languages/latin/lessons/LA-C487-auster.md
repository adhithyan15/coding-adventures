## You'll want to know: auster, austrī
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C487-AQUILO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**auster, austrī** — "the south wind; the south".

> Auster calidus nūbēs et pluviam fert.
>
> *The warm south wind brings clouds and rain.*

Second declension, masculine: *auster, austrī m.* English *austral* comes from it.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*auster* is inherited; EtymDB lists English *east* and Latin *aurōra* 'dawn' as cousins, while Lewis & Short derive it from a root meaning 'to burn', the hot wind. English *austral* comes from it.
