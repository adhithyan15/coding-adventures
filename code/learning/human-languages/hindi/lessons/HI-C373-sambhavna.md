## You'll want to know: संभावना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C373-MUMKIN373-03]; assesses=[] -->

**संभावना** — *sambhāvnā* — "a likelihood, a possibility".

> अगर बादल रहेंगे तो बारिश की संभावना है।
>
> *agar bādal raheṅge to bāriś kī sambhāvnā hai.*
>
> If the clouds stay, there is a chance of rain.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**संभावना** (*sambhāvnā*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *sambhāvanā*, "bringing about; supposing" (Monier-Williams).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
