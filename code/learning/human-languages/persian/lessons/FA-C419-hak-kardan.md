## You'll want to know: هک کردن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C419-HARIM-02]; assesses=[] -->

**هک کردن** (*hak kardan*) — "to hack".

> کسی حساب ایمیل او را هک کرد.
>
> *kasi hesâb-e imeyl-e u râ hak kard.*
>
> Someone hacked his email account.

An English loan + *kardan*. *Hak shodan* — to be hacked.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**هک کردن** joins the English loan *hack* to the inherited Persian verb *kardan* "to do".
