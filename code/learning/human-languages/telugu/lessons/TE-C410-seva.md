## You'll want to know: సేవ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C410-SEVA410-01]; assesses=[] -->

**సేవ** — *sēva* — "service; selfless work".

> ఆమె పేదవాళ్ళకు సేవ చేస్తుంది.
>
> *āme pēdavāḷḷaku sēva cēstundi.*
>
> She serves the poor.

సేవ చేయు, to serve; ప్రజా సేవ, public service.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**సేవ** (*sēva*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *sevā*, "going to; service, attendance; devotion" (Monier-Williams).
