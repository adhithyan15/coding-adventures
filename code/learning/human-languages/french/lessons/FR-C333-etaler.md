## You'll want to know: étaler
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C333-PETRIR-04]; assesses=[] -->

**étaler** — "to roll out, to spread".

> Il étale la pâte avec le rouleau à pâtisserie.
>
> *He rolls out the dough with the rolling pin.*

Also to spread butter: *étaler du beurre sur le pain*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*étaler* is built on Old French *estal* "a place, a stall", which is usually traced to a Frankish word (EtymDB lists no parent): to lay out on a stall.
