has been the whole of what you can count.

## You'll want to know: सहा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-SAHA]; assesses=[MR-LEX-NUMBERS-ONE-TO-FIVE] -->

> **सहा** — *sahā* — **6**

Sanskrit **षष्** (*ṣaṣ*) is the ancestor, and it is the same word as Latin
*sex* and English **six**. The opening consonant softened on its way into
Marathi; everything else is recognisably there.
