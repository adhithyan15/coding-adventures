## You'll want to know: generoso
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C723-GENEROSO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**generoso** — "generous".

> Mi tío es muy generoso con todos.
>
> *My uncle is very generous to everyone.*

*Ser generoso con alguien*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*generoso* comes from Latin *generōsus* "of noble birth" (EtymDB), built on *genus* "birth, family"; noble birth was expected to give freely.
