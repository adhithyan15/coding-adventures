## You'll want to know: निवास
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C413-NIVAS413-01]; assesses=[] -->

**निवास** — *nivās* — "lodging; a residence".

> सहलीत आमचा निवास एका जुन्या वाड्यात होता.
>
> *sahalīt āmcā nivās ekā junyā vāḍyāt hotā.*
>
> On the trip we stayed in an old mansion.

Masculine.

A formal word on signs and forms: निवास आणि जेवण, board and lodging.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**निवास** (*nivās*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *nivāsa*, "dwelling, abode", from *ni-vas*, "to dwell" (Monier-Williams).
