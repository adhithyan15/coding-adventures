## You'll want to know: न्यायालयः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C349-NYAYALAYA349-01]; assesses=[] -->

**न्यायालयः** — *nyāyālayaḥ* — "a court of law".

> अधिवक्ता प्रातः न्यायालयं गच्छति।
>
> *adhivaktā prātaḥ nyāyālayaṃ gacchati.*
>
> The lawyer goes to court in the morning.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**न्यायालयः** (*nyāyālayaḥ*) is a modern word built from *nyāya*, "rule, right manner", and *ālaya*, "a house, an abode"; MW records the parts but not the compound (MW).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
