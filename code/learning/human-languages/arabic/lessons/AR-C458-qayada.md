## You'll want to know: قايض
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C458-DAMAN458-05]; assesses=[] -->

**قايض** — *qāyaḍa* — "to barter".

> قايض المزارع القمح بالملح.
>
> *qāyaḍa al-muzāriʿ al-qamḥ bi-l-milḥ.*
>
> The farmer bartered the wheat for salt.

Present: يقايض, *yuqāyiḍ*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**قايض** (*qāyaḍa*) is usually traced to the native Arabic root *q-y-ḍ*, "to give in exchange". It is Form III.
