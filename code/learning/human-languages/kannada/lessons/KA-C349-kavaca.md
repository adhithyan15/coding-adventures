# ಕವಚ (kavaca) — armour

## You'll want to know: ಕವಚ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C349-KHADGA349-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ಕವಚ** — *kavaca* — "armour".

> ಸೈನಿಕರು ಕಬ್ಬಿಣದ ಕವಚ ತೊಟ್ಟಿದ್ದರು.
>
> *sainikaru kabbiṇada kavaca toṭṭiddaru.*
>
> The soldiers wore iron armour.

Also any protective cover: a phone case is a ಕವಚ too.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಕವಚ** (*kavaca*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *kavaca*, "a coat of mail, a jacket" (CDIAL 2957).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
