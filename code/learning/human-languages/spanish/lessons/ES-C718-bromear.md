## You'll want to know: bromear
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C718-SONRISA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**bromear** — "to joke".

> No te preocupes, estoy bromeando.
>
> *Do not worry, I am joking.*

*Estar bromeando* — to be joking.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*bromear* is built on *broma* "joke" with the verb ending *-ear*; *broma* comes from Greek *brôma* "food" (EtymDB).
