## You'll want to know: লিফট
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C403-GHOR403-03]; assesses=[] -->

**লিফট** — *liphṭ* — "a lift".

> লিফটটা বন্ধ ছিল, তাই আমরা হেঁটে উঠলাম।
>
> *liphṭṭā bôndhô chhilô, tāi āmrā hẽṭe uṭhlām.*
>
> The lift was out of order, so we walked up.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**লিফট** (*liphṭ*) is borrowed from English *lift*, with ফ for f and ট for t.
