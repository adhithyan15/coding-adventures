## You'll want to know: la lagartija
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C694-ANIMALES-05]; assesses=[] -->

**la lagartija** — "the small lizard, the wall lizard".

> Una lagartija toma el sol en la pared.
>
> *A lizard is sunning itself on the wall.*

A bigger one is *un lagarto*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*lagartija* is built on *lagarto* "lizard", which is inherited from Latin *lacertus* "a lizard" (Lewis & Short; EtymDB).
