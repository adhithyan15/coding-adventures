Lesson 1 of 5.
ವರಾಂಡ (varāṇḍa) — a veranda.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a basement, then the Kannada for a cabinet.

You'll want to know: ವರಾಂಡ.
ವರಾಂಡ — varāṇḍa — "a veranda".
ಸಂಜೆ ಅಜ್ಜ ವರಾಂಡದಲ್ಲಿ ಕುಳಿತು ಪತ್ರಿಕೆ ಓದುತ್ತಾರೆ. sañje ajja varāṇḍadalli kuḷitu patrike ōduttāre. In the evening grandfather sits on the veranda and reads the paper.
A roofed space along the front of the house; ಜಗಲಿ is the raised one.

The word, taken apart.
ವರಾಂಡ (varāṇḍa) is a loan from English veranda.

Lesson 4 of 5.
ಪೀಠೋಪಕರಣ (pīṭhōpakaraṇa) — furniture.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a lantern, then the Kannada for a cushion.

You'll want to know: ಪೀಠೋಪಕರಣ.
ಪೀಠೋಪಕರಣ — pīṭhōpakaraṇa — "furniture".
ಹೊಸ ಮನೆಗೆ ನಾವು ಹೊಸ ಪೀಠೋಪಕರಣ ತಂದೆವು. hosa manege nāvu hosa pīṭhōpakaraṇa tandevu. We brought new furniture for the new house.
ಪೀಠ, a seat + ಉಪಕರಣ, an implement.

The word, taken apart.
ಪೀಠೋಪಕರಣ (pīṭhōpakaraṇa) is a modern learned compound built from Sanskrit pīṭha, "a stool, a bench" (CDIAL 8222), and upakaraṇa, "an implement" (CDIAL 2138).
