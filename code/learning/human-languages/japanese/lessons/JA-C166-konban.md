## You'll want to know: こんばん

**こんばん** — *konban* — "this evening".

> **こんばん ともだちと あいます。** — *konban tomodachi to aimasu* — "I am meeting a friend this evening."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**こんばん** (*konban*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *kon*, "now" (Middle Chinese *kim*) and *ban*, "nightfall, evening" (*mjonX*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
