Telugu, chapter 450: Parking and Dropping Off.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
పార్కింగు (pārkiṅgu) — parking.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to seize up, then the Telugu for to get blocked.

You'll want to know: పార్కింగు.
పార్కింగు — pārkiṅgu — "parking".
ఆ దుకాణం దగ్గర పార్కింగు లేదు. ā dukāṇaṁ daggara pārkiṅgu lēdu. There is no parking near that shop.
A parking place is పార్కింగు (pārkiṅgu) స్థలం.

The word, taken apart.
పార్కింగు (pārkiṅgu) is a loan from English parking, with the Telugu final -ఉ.

Lesson 2 of 5.
నిలుపు (nilupu) — to pull up; to park.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to get blocked, then the Telugu for parking.

You'll want to know: నిలుపు.
నిలుపు — nilupu — "to pull up; to park".
అతను కారును చెట్టు కింద నిలిపాడు. atanu kārunu ceṭṭu kinda nilipāḍu. He parked the car under a tree.
Past (I …): నిలిపాను, nilipānu.
The causative of నిలుచు, to stand: to make something stand. It also means to stop a process.

The word, taken apart.
నిలుపు (nilupu) is native Dravidian: DEDR 3675 lists Telugu nilupu, "to cause to stand, to stop", beside nilucu, "to stand".

Lesson 3 of 5.
ఎక్కించు (ekkiñcu) — to put aboard; to load.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for parking, then the Telugu for to pull up.

You'll want to know: ఎక్కించు.
ఎక్కించు — ekkiñcu — "to put aboard; to load".
నాన్న పిల్లలను బస్సు ఎక్కించాడు. nānna pillalanu bassu ekkiñcāḍu. Father put the children on the bus.
Past (I …): ఎక్కించాను, ekkiñcānu.
The causative of ఎక్కు, to climb on, to board: to make someone or something get on.

The word, taken apart.
ఎక్కించు (ekkiñcu) is native Dravidian: DEDR 766 lists Telugu ekkincu, "to cause to ascend", beside ekku, "to climb, to get on".

Lesson 4 of 5.
దించు (diñcu) — to unload; to lower.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to pull up, then the Telugu for to put aboard.

You'll want to know: దించు.
దించు — diñcu — "to unload; to lower".
కూలీలు లారీ నుంచి బస్తాలు దించారు. kūlīlu lārī nuñci bastālu diñcāru. The labourers unloaded the sacks from the lorry.
Past (I …): దించాను, diñcānu.
The causative of దిగు, to get down: to bring something down. బరువు దించు (diñcu), to put down a load.

The word, taken apart.
దించు (diñcu) is native Dravidian: DEDR 502 lists Telugu dincu beside digu, "to descend, to get off".

Lesson 5 of 5.
దిగబెట్టు (digabeṭṭu) — to drop someone off.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to put aboard, then the Telugu for to unload.

You'll want to know: దిగబెట్టు.
దిగబెట్టు — digabeṭṭu — "to drop someone off".
నా స్నేహితుడు నన్ను స్టేషన్ దగ్గర దిగబెట్టాడు. nā snēhituḍu nannu sṭēṣan daggara digabeṭṭāḍu. My friend dropped me off near the station.
Past (I …): దిగబెట్టాను, digabeṭṭānu.
దిగు, to get down, + పెట్టు, to put: to see someone safely to a place.

The word, taken apart.
దిగబెట్టు (digabeṭṭu) is native Dravidian: దిగు, "to get down" (DEDR 502), and పెట్టు, "to put, to place" (DEDR 4390).
