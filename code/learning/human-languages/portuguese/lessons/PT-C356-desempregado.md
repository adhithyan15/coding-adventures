## You'll want to know: desempregado
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C356-DESPEDIMENTO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**desempregado** — "unemployed, out of work".

> O meu primo está desempregado há seis meses.
>
> *My cousin has been out of work for six months.*

Also a noun: *os desempregados*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*desempregado* is built from *des-* "not" and *empregado* "employed", the participle of *empregar*, which is inherited from Latin *implicāre* "to fold into, to involve" (EtymDB).
