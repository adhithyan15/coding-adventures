## Chapters 157-198: 210 A2 headwords, fifty of them verbs

The first of four Telugu A2 vocabulary tranches: forty-two chapters of five
words, in five runs, each closed by two review lessons. The word list was
drafted by theme, checked against every headword the course already teaches,
and reviewed by a native-level reader; romanizations write the anusvara before
a stop as the matching nasal, as the course does. Every headword avoids the
letters the course has not taught (ఈ ఊ ఓ ఔ), and words an earlier lesson
already uses in its text were left out. Chapter 171's last word re-reads ఛాయ
and ఝరి, the R4 revisit of the ఛ and ఝ letter atoms. Level gate: 350 headwords
and 9 verbs short of A2.
