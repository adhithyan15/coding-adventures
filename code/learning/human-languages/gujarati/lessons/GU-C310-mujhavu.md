# મૂંઝાવું (mū̃jhāvũ) — to be at a loss

## You'll want to know: મૂંઝાવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C310-TUKDI310-04]; assesses=[] -->

**મૂંઝાવું** — *mū̃jhāvũ* — "to be at a loss".

> જંગલમાં બે રસ્તા જોઈને ટુકડી મૂંઝાઈ ગઈ.
>
> *jangalmā̃ be rastā joīne ṭukḍī mū̃jhāī gaī.*
>
> When the squad saw two paths in the forest, it was at a loss.

Past (he …): તે મૂંઝાઈ ગયો, *te mū̃jhāī gayo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**મૂંઝાવું** (*mū̃jhāvũ*) is inherited from Sanskrit *múhyati*, "is bewildered", and CDIAL lists Gujarati *mujhāvũ*, "to be perplexed" (CDIAL 10230). It is not knowing what to do next: **મૂંઝાઈ ગઈ**.
