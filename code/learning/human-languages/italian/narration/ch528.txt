Italian, chapter 528: Una serie da vedere.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
la serie — series.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for comic, then the Italian for poet.

You'll want to know: la serie.
la serie — "series".
Stiamo guardando una serie poliziesca. We are watching a detective series.
The same form in the plural: le serie. Una serie TV.

The word, taken apart.
serie is a learned borrowing from Latin seriēs "a row, a chain" (Lewis & Short), from serere "to join".

Ho visto tre episodi di fila. I watched three episodes in a row.

The word, taken apart.
episodio is a learned borrowing from Greek epeisódion "a part added between choral songs" (EtymDB, under English episode), from eísodos "an entrance".
