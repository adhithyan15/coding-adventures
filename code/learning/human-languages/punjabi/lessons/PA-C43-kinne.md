> **ਕਿੰਨੇ** — *kinne* — **how many**, **how much**

The same **ਕ** one last time, now asking about quantity. With it the family this
book named when it taught *kivēṁ* is finally complete on the page:

- **ਕੀ** what · **ਕਿਵੇਂ** how · **ਕਿਉਂ** why · **ਕੌਣ** who · **ਕਦੋਂ** when ·
- **ਕਿੱਥੇ** where · **ਕਿੰਨੇ** how many

Two of those you had before this run. Five arrived in the last four chapters,
and only one of the five cost a new letter.
