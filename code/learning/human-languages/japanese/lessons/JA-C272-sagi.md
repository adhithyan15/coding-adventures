## You'll want to know: さぎ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C272-SAGI272-01]; assesses=[] -->

**さぎ** — *sagi* — "a fraud; a swindle".

> **年じゅう さぎの でんわが あります。** — *nenjū sagi no denwa ga arimasu* — "Swindlers phone all year round."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**さぎ** (*sagi*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *sa*, "deceive" (Middle Chinese *tsraeH*) and *gi*, "cheat" (*khi*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
