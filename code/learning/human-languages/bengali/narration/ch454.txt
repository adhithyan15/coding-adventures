Bengali, chapter 454: Shyness and Hesitation.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
সংকোচ (sôngkoch) — reluctance; bashful hesitation.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for to stand up to, then the Bengali for to back down.

You'll want to know: সংকোচ.
সংকোচ — sôngkoch — "reluctance; bashful hesitation".
সংকোচ না করে তোমার প্রশ্নটা জিজ্ঞাসা করো। sôngkoch nā kôre tomār prôshnôṭā jigyāsā kôro. Don't hold back: ask your question.
সংকোচ (sôngkoch) is the shrinking-back of a guest or a newcomer who does not want to impose: সংকোচ (sôngkoch) করবেন না, please don't hold back.

The word, taken apart.
সংকোচ (sôngkoch) is a tatsama, Sanskrit saṃkoca, "contraction, shrinking together" (Monier-Williams): shrinking back.

দ্বিধা — dvidhā — "doubt; being in two minds".
নতুন চাকরি নিয়ে অরুণের মনে দ্বিধা ছিল। nôtun chākri niye ôruner mône dvidhā chhilô. Arun was in two minds about the new job.

The word, taken apart.
দ্বিধা (dvidhā) is a tatsama, Sanskrit dvidhā, "in two ways, divided" (Monier-Williams); Bengali turned the adverb into a noun, a mind split in two.

The word, taken apart.
আড়ষ্ট (āṛôshṭô) is a Bengali word for stiffness, of the body or of the manner; its origin is not settled.

Lesson 4 of 5.
জড়সড় (jôṛôsôṛô) — huddled up; shrinking (with shyness or cold).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for being in two minds, then the Bengali for stiff and awkward.

You'll want to know: জড়সড়.
জড়সড় — jôṛôsôṛô — "huddled up; shrinking (with shyness or cold)".
শীতে কুকুরটা জড়সড় হয়ে বসে ছিল। shīte kukurṭā jôṛôsôṛô hôye bôse chhilô. The dog sat huddled up in the cold.

The word, taken apart.
জড়সড় (jôṛôsôṛô) is a rhyming doubling whose first half probably belongs with Sanskrit jaḍa, "dull, numb with cold", under which CDIAL lists Bengali forms (CDIAL 5090).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for stiff and awkward, then the Bengali for huddled up.

You'll want to know: ইতস্তত করা.
ইতস্তত করা — itôstôtô kôrā — "to hesitate".
দরজার সামনে অরুণ ইতস্তত করল। dôrjār sāmne ôrun itôstôtô kôrlô. At the door Arun hesitated.
Past (he/she …): তিনি দরজায় ইতস্তত করলেন, tini dôrjāy itôstôtô kôrlen.

The word, taken apart.
ইতস্তত করা (itôstôtô kôrā) is Sanskrit itastatas, "here and there, hither and thither" (Monier-Williams), with kôrā, "to do" (CDIAL 2814): going this way and that.
