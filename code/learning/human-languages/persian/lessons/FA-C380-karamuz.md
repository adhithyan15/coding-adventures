## You'll want to know: کارآموز
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C380-REZUME-04]; assesses=[] -->

**کارآموز** (*kârâmuz*) — "a trainee, an intern".

> سه کارآموز در دفتر ما کار میکنند.
>
> *se kârâmuz dar daftar-e mâ kâr mikonand.*
>
> Three trainees work in our office.

*Kâr* "work" + *âmuz*, the present stem of *âmukhtan* "to learn". Plural *kârâmuzân*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**کارآموز** is a native Persian compound built from *kâr* "work" + *âmuz-*, the present stem of *âmukhtan* "to learn, to teach" (EtymDB traces it to Middle Persian). A trainee is a work-learner.
