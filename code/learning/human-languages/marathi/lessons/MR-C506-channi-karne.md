## You'll want to know: छाननी करणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C506-KACHERI506-05]; assesses=[] -->

**छाननी करणे** — *chānnī karṇe* — "to scrutinize".

> लिपिकाने सगळ्या कागदपत्रांची छाननी केली.
>
> *lipikāne sagḷyā kāgadpatrāñcī chānnī kelī.*
>
> The clerk scrutinized all the papers.

Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने अर्जाची छाननी केली, *tyāne arjācī chānnī kelī*.

What is checked takes -ची: अर्जाची छाननी करणे.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**छाननी करणे** (*chānnī karṇe*) is छाननी, "close sifting, scrutiny", with करणे; the origin of छाननी is not settled.
