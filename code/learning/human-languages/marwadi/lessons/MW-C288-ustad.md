## You'll want to know: उस्ताद
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C288-KAM288-03]; assesses=[] -->

**उस्ताद** — *ustād* — "a master of a craft".

> म्हारो उस्ताद घणो हुसियार है।
>
> *mhāro ustād ghaṇo husiyār hai.*
>
> My master is very clever.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**उस्ताद** (*ustād*) is usually traced to Persian *ustād*, "a master": the one who has the skill and teaches it.
