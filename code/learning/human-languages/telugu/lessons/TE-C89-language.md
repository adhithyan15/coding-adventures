## You'll want to know: భాష
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C89-ABOUT-02]; assesses=[] -->

**భాష** (*bhāṣa*) — "a language".

**మీకు ఏ భాషలు వచ్చు?** — "which languages do you know?". The knower sits in the dative, as in
**నాకు తెలుగు వచ్చు**: a language is something that comes to you.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

A way to ask what someone speaks, in the frame you learned for Telugu itself.
