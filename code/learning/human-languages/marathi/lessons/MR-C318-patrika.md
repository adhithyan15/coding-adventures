## You'll want to know: पत्रिका
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C318-BARSE318-05]; assesses=[] -->

**पत्रिका** — *patrikā* — "an invitation card".

> त्यांनी लग्नाची पत्रिका घरी आणून दिली.
>
> *tyānnī lagnācī patrikā gharī āṇūn dilī.*
>
> They brought the wedding invitation card to our home.

Feminine.

लग्नपत्रिका, a wedding card; also a small journal.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पत्रिका** (*patrikā*) is built on Sanskrit *patra*, 'a leaf, a letter' (Monier-Williams), with the small-thing ending *-ikā*.

## What you've built
