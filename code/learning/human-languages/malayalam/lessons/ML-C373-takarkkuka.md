# തകർക്കുക (takarkkuka) — to smash; to wreck

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Malayalam for to attack, then the Malayalam for to loot.

## You'll want to know: തകർക്കുക
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C373-AKRAMANAM373-05]; assesses=[] -->

**തകർക്കുക** — *takarkkuka* — "to smash; to wreck".

> കാറ്റ് ഗ്രാമത്തിലെ വീടുകൾ തകർത്തു.
>
> *kāṟṟŭ grāmattile vīṭukaḷ takarttu.*
>
> The wind wrecked the houses of the village.

Past: തകർത്തു, *takarttu*.

The partner of തകരുക, to collapse: one smashes, the other is smashed.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**തകർക്കുക** (*takarkkuka*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 2999 lists Malayalam *takarkka*, "to smash, crush, demolish", the active partner of തകരുക (*takaruka*).

## What you've built

One more word for telling what happened.
