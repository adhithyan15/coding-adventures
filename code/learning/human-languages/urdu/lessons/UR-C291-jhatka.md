## You'll want to know: جھٹکا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C291-ZALZALA291-02]; assesses=[] -->

**جھٹکا** — *jhaṭkā* — "a jolt; a tremor".

> پہلا جھٹکا ہلکا تھا۔
>
> *pehlā jhaṭkā halkā thā.*
>
> The first jolt was mild.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**جھٹکا** (*jhaṭkā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *jhaṭkā*, "a jerk", under *\*jhaṭ*, a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 5327).
