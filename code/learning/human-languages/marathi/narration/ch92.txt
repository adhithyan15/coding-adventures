Lesson 2 of 5.
ताट (tāṭ) — a plate.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a key, then the Marathi for a cup.

You'll want to know: ताट.
ताट — tāṭ — "a plate".

The word, taken apart.
ताट (tāṭ) goes back to taṭṭa, "pot", a form CDIAL reconstructs; it glosses Marathi tāṭ "rimmed metal dining plate" (CDIAL 5631). DEDR sets Marathi tāṭ beside Dravidian words for a flat plate, such as Tamil taṭṭam, "eating plate" (DEDR 3035).

Lesson 4 of 5.
सुरी (surī) — a knife.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a plate, then the Marathi for a spoon.

You'll want to know: सुरी.
सुरी — surī — "a knife".

The word, taken apart.
सुरी (surī) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from kṣurī, "knife, dagger" (CDIAL 3727). It is built on kṣura, "razor".
