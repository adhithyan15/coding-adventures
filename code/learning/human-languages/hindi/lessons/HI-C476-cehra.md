## You'll want to know: चेहरा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C476-CHEHRA476-01]; assesses=[] -->

**चेहरा** — *cehrā* — "a face".

> धूप में मीरा का चेहरा लाल हो गया।
>
> *dhūp meṁ Mīrā kā cehrā lāl ho gayā.*
>
> Mira's face went red in the sun.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**चेहरा** (*cehrā*) is usually traced to Persian *chihra*, "face, countenance". *mũh* is the face you wash; *cehrā* is the face you see.
