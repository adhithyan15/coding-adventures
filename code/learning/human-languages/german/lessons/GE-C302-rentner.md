## You'll want to know: der Rentner
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C302-RUHESTAND-03]; assesses=[] -->

**der Rentner** — "the pensioner".

> Viele Rentner sitzen am Morgen im Park.
>
> *Many pensioners sit in the park in the morning.*

Plural: *die Rentner*. Feminine *die Rentnerin*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Rentner* is built on *Rente* "pension", borrowed from French *rente* and usually traced to Latin *reddere* "to give back" (Lewis & Short).
