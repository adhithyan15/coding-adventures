## You'll want to know: রাক্ষস
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C322-RAKKHOSH322-01]; assesses=[] -->

**রাক্ষস** — *rākkhôs* — "an ogre; a demon who eats people".

> রাক্ষসটা রাতে গ্রামে এসে মানুষ খেত।
>
> *rākkhôsṭā rāte grāme ese mānush khetô.*
>
> The ogre used to come to the village at night and eat people.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**রাক্ষস** (*rākkhôs*) is Sanskrit *rākṣasa*, a Rakṣas, a malignant demon of the old epics (Monier-Williams). Its ক্ষ is read *kkh*.
