## You'll want to know: નગર
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C423-TOWN423-01]; assesses=[] -->

**નગર** — *nagar* — "a town; a city (formal)".

> આ નગર નદીના કિનારે છે.
>
> *ā nagar nadīnā kināre che.*
>
> This town is on the bank of the river.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**નગર** (*nagar*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *nagara*, "a town, a city" (Monier-Williams); it ends many place names.
