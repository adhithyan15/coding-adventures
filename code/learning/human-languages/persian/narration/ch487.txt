6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
ناامید (nâomid) — disappointed; hopeless.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for love, then the Persian for to hate.

You'll want to know: ناامید.
ناامید (nâomid) — "disappointed; hopeless".
او از نتیجه امتحان ناامید شد. u az natije-ye emtehân nâomid shod. He was disappointed with the exam result.
nâ- 'not' + omid 'hope'. The noun is nâomidi.

The word, taken apart.
nâomid is built on native Persian pieces: the prefix nâ-, 'not', and omid, 'hope', which is usually traced to Middle Persian ummēd.

Lesson 2 of 6.

You'll want to know: خوشبین.
خوشبین (khoshbin) — "optimistic".
پدرم همیشه به آینده خوشبین است. pedaram hamishe be âyande khoshbin ast. My father is always optimistic about the future.
khosh 'good' + bin 'seeing' (stem of didan). What you are hopeful about takes be.

The word, taken apart.
khoshbin is built on native Persian parts: khosh, 'good, pleasant', and bin, the present stem of didan, 'to see': someone who sees things well.

بدبین (badbin) — "pessimistic; distrustful".
او به همه بدبین است. u be hame badbin ast. He distrusts everyone.
bad 'bad' + bin 'seeing'. be kasi badbin budan — to distrust someone.

The word, taken apart.
badbin is built on native Persian bad, 'bad', and bin, the present stem of didan, 'to see'. Persian bad looks like English bad, but they are not related.

Lesson 4 of 6.
اعتماد به نفس (e'temâd be nafs) — self-confidence.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for optimistic, then the Persian for pessimistic.

You'll want to know: اعتماد به نفس.
اعتماد به نفس (e'temâd be nafs) — "self-confidence".
او بعد از این مسابقه اعتماد به نفس بیشتری دارد. u ba'd az in mosâbeqe e'temâd be nafs-e bishtari dârad. After this contest he has more self-confidence.
e'temâd 'trust' + be 'in' + nafs 'self': trust in oneself.

The word, taken apart.
e'temâd be nafs is built in Persian from two Arabic loans: e'temâd, usually traced to Arabic iʿtimād, 'reliance', and nafs, borrowed from Arabic nafs, 'self, soul' (EtymDB), joined by Persian be.

Lesson 5 of 6.
شجاعت (shojâ'at) — courage, bravery.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for pessimistic, then the Persian for self-confidence.

You'll want to know: شجاعت.
شجاعت (shojâ'at) — "courage, bravery".
آتش نشان با شجاعت بچه را نجات داد. âtashneshân bâ shojâ'at bachche râ nejât dâd. The firefighter bravely saved the child.
bâ shojâ'at — bravely. The adjective is shojâ' 'brave'.

The word, taken apart.
shojâ'at is a loan, usually traced to Arabic šajāʿa, 'courage'. Its partner shojâ', 'brave', was borrowed from Arabic šujāʿ (EtymDB).
