## You'll want to know: 好意
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C355-REFUSE355-02]; assesses=[] -->

**好意** — *hǎoyì* — "good intentions; a kind thought".

> *xièxie nǐ de hǎoyì wǒ zìjǐ néng huí jiā* — Thanks for the kind thought, but I can get home on my own.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**好意** (*hǎoyì*) is built from 好 'good' and 意 'intent', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *xawX* and *'iH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'good' and the second 'will'.
