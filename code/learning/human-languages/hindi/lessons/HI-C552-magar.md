## You'll want to know: मगर
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C552-BAHARHAAL552-05]; assesses=[] -->

**मगर** — *magar* — "but (in a lighter register); yet".

> मैं बहुत थका था, मगर मैंने खाना बनाया।
>
> *Maiṁ bahut thakā thā, magar maiṁne khānā banāyā.*
>
> I was very tired, but I cooked the meal.

Means the same as लेकिन; common in speech and in songs.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**मगर** (*magar*) is usually traced to Persian *magar*, "but, unless".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
