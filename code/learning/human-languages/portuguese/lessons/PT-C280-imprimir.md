## You'll want to know: imprimir
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C280-PUBLICAR-01]; assesses=[] -->

**imprimir** — "to print".

> O jornal imprime cem mil exemplares por dia.
>
> *The paper prints a hundred thousand copies a day.*

Also on a home printer: *imprimir um bilhete*. The past participle is *impresso*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*imprimir* is a learned borrowing from Latin *imprimere* "to press into, to stamp", built on *premere* "to press" (Lewis & Short).
