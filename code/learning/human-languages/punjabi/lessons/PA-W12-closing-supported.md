# Two small units at the end

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-PHRASE-SEE-YOU-AGAIN-04] -->

Read the everyday ending once. Then recall the old courtesy word
**ਧੰਨਵਾਦ**. They do different jobs: thanks first, parting second.

## Writing — supported closing
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-PHRASE-SEE-YOU-AGAIN-04] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: guided-copy -->

Copy **ਧੰਨਵਾਦ। ਫਿਰ ਮਿਲਾਂਗੇ।** as two short units. Put one space after the
first sentence mark and one between **ਫਿਰ** and **ਮਿਲਾਂਗੇ**. No space goes
before either mark.

## Guided Practice — read the order
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-PHRASE-SEE-YOU-AGAIN-04] -->

Point to the word that thanks the reader. Point to the words that promise a
later meeting. Keep this order; the next lesson will put an opening and this
closing on separate lines.
