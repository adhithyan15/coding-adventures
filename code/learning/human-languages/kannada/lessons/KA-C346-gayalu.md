# ಗಾಯಾಳು (gāyāḷu) — an injured person; a casualty

## You'll want to know: ಗಾಯಾಳು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C346-SPHOTA346-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ಗಾಯಾಳು** — *gāyāḷu* — "an injured person; a casualty".

> ಸ್ಫೋಟದ ನಂತರ ಗಾಯಾಳುಗಳನ್ನು ಆಸ್ಪತ್ರೆಗೆ ಕರೆದುಕೊಂಡು ಹೋದರು.
>
> *sphōṭada nantara gāyāḷugaḷannu āspatrege karedukoṇḍu hōdaru.*
>
> After the explosion the injured were taken to hospital.

Plural: ಗಾಯಾಳುಗಳು, *gāyāḷugaḷu*.

ಗಾಯ, a wound, and ಆಳು, a person: one who is hurt. A news word after accidents and blasts.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಗಾಯಾಳು** (*gāyāḷu*) is a Kannada compound of ಗಾಯ, "a wound", which goes back to Sanskrit *ghāta*, "a blow, a bruise" (CDIAL 4460), and the native Dravidian *āḷ*, "a person" (DEDR 399): a wounded person.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
