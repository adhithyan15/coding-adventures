## You'll want to know: el arroyo
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C689-AGUA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**el arroyo** — "the stream, the brook".

> Cruzamos el arroyo por un puente.
>
> *We crossed the stream by a bridge.*

Smaller than *un río*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*arroyo* is usually traced to a pre-Roman word recorded in Latin as *arrugia* "a mine gallery"; the origin is not settled.
