# റോഡ് (ṟōḍŭ) — a road

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ML-LEX-C55-GROUND-04, ML-LEX-C155-THINGS155-03, ML-LEX-C121-TRAVEL-04] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Malayalam for "a way, a path", then "a street", then "a
station". (*vaḻi*, *teruvŭ*, *sṟṟēṣan*.)

## You'll want to know: റോഡ്
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C343-RODU-01]; assesses=[ML-LEX-C121-TRAVEL-04, ML-LEX-C42-ADJ-01, ML-LEX-C55-GROUND-04] -->

**റോഡ്** — *ṟōḍŭ* — "a road".

> **ഈ റോഡ് സ്റ്റേഷനിലേക്ക് പോകുന്നു.**
>
> *ī ṟōḍŭ sṟṟēṣanilēkkŭ pōkunnu.* — "This road goes to the station."

> **വലിയ റോഡിൽ ഒരുപാട് ബസുകൾ ഉണ്ട്.**
>
> *valiya ṟōḍil orupāṭŭ basukaḷ uṇṭŭ.* — "There are a lot of buses on the big road."

**വഴി** is the way or route you take; **റോഡ്** is the road itself; **തെരുവ്** is a street lined
with houses and shops. **-ലേക്ക്** on **സ്റ്റേഷൻ** says "towards, to".

Its last consonant is new. The next lesson takes it out of this word and puts it in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**റോഡ്** (*ṟōḍŭ*) is borrowed from English *road*. Malayalam hears the English *r* as its own **റ**
and the English *d* as its curled-back **ഡ**, and closes the word with the half-u, *ŭ*, as it does
with **ടിക്കറ്റ്** and **സീറ്റ്**.
