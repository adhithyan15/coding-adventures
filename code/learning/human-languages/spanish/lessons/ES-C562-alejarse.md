## You'll want to know: alejarse
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C562-VOLVER-05]; assesses=[] -->

**alejarse** — "to move away, to go off".

> El coche se alejó despacio.
>
> *The car moved slowly away.*

*Alejarse de* — to move away from.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*alejarse* is built on *lejos* 'far', which comes from Latin *laxius* 'more loosely, further apart', from *laxus* 'loose' (Lewis & Short), with *a-* and *-ar*.
