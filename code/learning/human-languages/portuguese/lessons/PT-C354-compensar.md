## You'll want to know: compensar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C354-INFLUENCIA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**compensar** — "to make up for, to compensate; to be worth it".

> Trabalhei no sábado para compensar o dia que perdi.
>
> *I worked on Saturday to make up for the day I lost.*

*Compensa!* — it is worth it.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*compensar* is a learned borrowing from Latin *compēnsāre* "to weigh several things with one another, to balance" (EtymDB, Lewis & Short), built on *pēnsāre* "to weigh", from *pendere*.
