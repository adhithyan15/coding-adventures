## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C309-KIN-17]; assesses=[] -->

**el apodo** --- "the nickname."

It comes from *apodar*, "to nickname," and behind that sits Latin **putare**.
*Putare* first meant to **prune** a tree --- to cut it back into shape. From
trimming a tree it moved to trimming a problem, and so to *reckon*, *judge*,
*consider*.

English is full of the pruned version. To *compute* is to reckon together. To
*dispute* is to reckon apart. Your *reputation* is what people reckon about you
over and over. And to *amputate* is the oldest sense back again, cutting off.

So an *apodo* is a name somebody reckoned up for you --- a judgement, trimmed to
one word. Which is exactly what a nickname is.

Set it beside what you already have. Your *nombre* was given to you, your
*apellido* was inherited, and your *apodo* was decided by other people.

## What you've built
