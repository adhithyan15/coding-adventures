## You'll want to know: खुश
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C451-MOOD451-03]; assesses=[] -->

**खुश** — *khuś* — "pleased; glad".

> बक्षीस मिळाल्यावर मुली खुश झाल्या.
>
> *bakṣīs miḷālyāvar mulī khuś jhālyā.*
>
> The girls were pleased when they got the prize.

Unchanging; खुश होणे, to become pleased. A Persian word beside the Sanskrit आनंदी.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**खुश** (*khuś*) is a loan from Persian *khush*, "happy, pleasant"; Marathi writes the Persian *kh* with ख.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
