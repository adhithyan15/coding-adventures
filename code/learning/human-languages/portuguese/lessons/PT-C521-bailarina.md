## You'll want to know: a bailarina
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C521-GENEROS-04]; assesses=[] -->

**a bailarina** — "ballerina, dancer".

> A bailarina dançou sozinha no palco.
>
> *The ballerina danced alone on the stage.*

A man is *o bailarino*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*bailarina* comes from Italian *ballerina* (EtymDB), built on a verb from Late Latin *ballāre* "to dance".
