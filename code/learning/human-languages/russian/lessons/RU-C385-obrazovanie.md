## You'll want to know: образование
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C385-OBRAZOVANIYE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**образование** (*obrazovániye*) — "education".

> У неё хорошее образование.
>
> *U neyó khorósheye obrazovániye.*
>
> She has a good education.

Neuter. *Высшее образование* — higher education. It also means formation: *образование льда*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**образование** is built on *образовать* "to form, to shape", from *образ* "a shape, an image", inherited from Proto-Slavic *\*obrazŭ* (EtymDB). Education gives a mind its shape.
