## You'll want to know: یکساں
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C537-MUTABADIL537-02]; assesses=[] -->

**یکساں** — *yaksāṅ* — "alike; the same".

> اگر دونوں راستے یکساں ہیں تو کوئی بھی لیجیے۔
>
> *agar donoṅ rāste yaksāṅ haiṅ to koī bhī lījiye.*
>
> If both routes are the same, take either one.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**یکساں** (*yaksāṅ*) is usually traced to Persian *yaksān*, "alike", built from *yak*, "one", and *sān*, "manner".
