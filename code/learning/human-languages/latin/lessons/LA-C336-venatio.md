# vēnātiō, vēnātiōnis — a hunt; a wild-beast show

## You'll want to know: vēnātiō, vēnātiōnis
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C336-GLADIATOR-04]; assesses=[] -->

**vēnātiō, vēnātiōnis** — "a hunt; a wild-beast show".

> Māne vēnātiō erat, post merīdiem gladiātōrēs.
>
> *In the morning there was a wild-beast hunt, in the afternoon the gladiators.*

Feminine, from *vēnor*, 'to hunt' (taught). In the arena, hunters fought lions and bears.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**vēnātiō** is built on *vēnor*, "to hunt" (Lewis & Short): hunting, and the meat of the hunt. English *venison* comes from it through French.
