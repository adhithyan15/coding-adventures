Hindi, chapter 107: At the Doctor.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
डाक्टर (ḍākṭar) — a doctor.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for ill, then the Hindi for how one is feeling.

You'll want to know: डाक्टर.
डाक्टर (ḍākṭar) — "a doctor", English borrowed whole.
डाक्टर (ḍākṭar) कहाँ हैं? — "where is the doctor?", with the respectful plural हैं for a person you are asking after.

What you've built.
The person to ask for.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: ḍākṭar]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: ḍākṭar, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: ask ḍākṭar kahā̃ haiṁ?]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Hindi for ill, then the Hindi for how one is feeling, then say ḍākṭar again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does डाक्टर (ḍākṭar) mean? ("A doctor".) Say it once more.

Lesson 3 of 5.

[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a doctor, then the Hindi for a hospital.

You'll want to know: चोट.
चोट (coṭ) — "an injury, a hurt". It is feminine.
मुझे चोट (coṭ) लगी है — "I have been hurt", literally "a hurt has struck me".

What you've built.
A way to say you are hurt.

Lesson 4 of 5.
कमज़ोरी (kamzorī) — weakness.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a hospital, then the Hindi for an injury.

You'll want to know: कमज़ोरी.
कमज़ोरी (kamzorī) — "weakness".
It opens on the कम you know, "little": weakness is having little strength. कमज़ोरी (kamzorī) लग रही है — "I feel weak".

What you've built.
Weakness, built on a word you know.

Lesson 5 of 5.
सेहत (sehat) — health, fitness.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for an injury, then the Hindi for weakness.

You'll want to know: सेहत.
सेहत (sehat) — "health", the long-term kind.
तबीयत is how you feel today; सेहत (sehat) is how healthy you are. सेहत (sehat) अच्छी है — "the health is good".

What you've built.
A doctor, a hospital, an injury, weakness, and health itself.

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does सेहत (sehat) mean? ("Health, fitness".) Say it once more.
