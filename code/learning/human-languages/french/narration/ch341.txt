French, chapter 341: Je m'explique.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
le malentendu — the misunderstanding.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for likely, then the French for unlikely.

You'll want to know: le malentendu.
le malentendu — "the misunderstanding".
Excuse-moi, c'est un malentendu: je parlais de l'autre réunion. Sorry, it is a misunderstanding: I was talking about the other meeting.
From mal + entendu: badly heard.

The word, taken apart.
malentendu is built from mal "badly" and entendu, the past participle of entendre, which comes from Latin intendere "to stretch toward, to turn the mind to" (Lewis & Short).

What you've built.

Lesson 2 of 6.
clarifier — to clarify.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for unlikely, then the French for the misunderstanding.

You'll want to know: clarifier.
clarifier — "to clarify".
Je voudrais clarifier un point avant de commencer. I would like to clarify one point before starting.
Clarifier la situation — to make things clear.

The word, taken apart.
clarifier is a learned borrowing from Late Latin clārificāre (EtymDB), which Lewis & Short gloss "to make illustrious, to make famous"; it is built on clārus "clear, bright", and French took it in the sense to make clear.

Lesson 3 of 6.
préciser — to specify, to make precise.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for the misunderstanding, then the French for to clarify.

You'll want to know: préciser.
préciser — "to specify, to make precise".
Il a précisé l'heure de la réunion dans son message. He specified the time of the meeting in his message.
Préciser que — to point out exactly. From précis, precise.

The word, taken apart.
préciser is built on précis "precise" (EtymDB), from Latin praecīsus "cut off, abridged", the participle of praecīdere "to cut off in front" (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 5 of 6.
éclaircir — to clear up, to make clearer.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for to specify, then the French for the precision.

You'll want to know: éclaircir.
éclaircir — "to clear up, to make clearer".
Nous devons éclaircir ce problème avant lundi. We need to clear up this problem before Monday.
A -ir verb like finir. Of the sky too: le ciel s'éclaircit.

The word, taken apart.
éclaircir is built from é- and clair "clear", which is inherited from Latin clārus "clear, bright" (Lewis & Short).
