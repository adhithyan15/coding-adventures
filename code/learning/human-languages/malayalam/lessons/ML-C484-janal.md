## You'll want to know: ജനൽ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C484-VELLAM484-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ജനൽ** — *janal* — "a window".

> തീവണ്ടിയുടെ ജനലിലൂടെ ഞാൻ വയലുകൾ കണ്ടു.
>
> *tīvaṇṭiyuṭe janalilūṭe ñān vayalukaḷ kaṇṭu.*
>
> Through the train window I saw the paddy fields.

Also spelt ജനാല. A window seat is a ജനലിനടുത്തുള്ള സീറ്റ്.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ജനൽ** (*janal*) was borrowed from Portuguese *janela*, a window, one of the words the Portuguese left on the Malabar coast.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for getting around.
