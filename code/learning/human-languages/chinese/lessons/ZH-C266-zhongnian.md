## You'll want to know: 中年
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C266-AGE266-05]; assesses=[] -->

**中年** — *zhōngnián* — "middle age".

> *tā bù lǎo shì zhōngnián rén* — He isn't old; he's middle-aged.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**中年** (*zhōngnián*) is built from 中 'middle' and 年 'a year', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *trjuwng* and *nen* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription).

## What you've built

One more word for what I have and can do.
