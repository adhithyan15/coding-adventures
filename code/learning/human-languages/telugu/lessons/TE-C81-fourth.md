## You'll want to know: నాలుగవ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C81-ORDINAL-05]; assesses=[] -->

**నాలుగవ** (*nālugava*) — "**fourth**".

**నాలుగు** → **నాలుగవ**. Four syllables, and the swap is the same one: the final
**u** goes, **-వ** arrives.

**నాలుగవ సంవత్సరం** (*nālugava saṁvatsaraṁ*) — the fourth year.

This is the longest of the ten and it is no harder than the shortest, which is the
practical point of a regular ending. Once you can count, you can order.
