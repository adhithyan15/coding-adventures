## You'll want to know: le détour
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C436-RACCOURCI-02]; assesses=[] -->

**le détour** — "the detour, the roundabout way".

> Nous avons fait un détour pour voir le château.
>
> *We made a detour to visit the castle.*

*Sans détour* — straight out, frankly.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*détour* comes from Old French *destour*, built on *détourner* 'to turn away' (EtymDB), from *tourner*, which goes back to Latin *tornāre* 'to turn on a lathe'.
