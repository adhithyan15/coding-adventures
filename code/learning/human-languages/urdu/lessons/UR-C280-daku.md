## You'll want to know: ڈاکو
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C280-DAKU280-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ڈاکو** — *ḍākū* — "a bandit".

> رات کو سڑک پر ڈاکو تھے۔
>
> *rāt ko saṛak par ḍākū the.*
>
> There were bandits on the road at night.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *ḍākū*, "robber", under *\*ḍākka*, a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 5543). A **ڈاکو** robs with a gang, on the roads; a **چور** steals alone, in secret.
