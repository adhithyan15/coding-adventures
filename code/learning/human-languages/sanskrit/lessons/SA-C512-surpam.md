## You'll want to know: शूर्पम्
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C512-CHURIKA512-04]; assesses=[] -->

**शूर्पम्** — *śūrpam* — "a winnowing basket".

> माता शूर्पेण तण्डुलान् शोधयति।
>
> *mātā śūrpeṇa taṇḍulān śodhayati.*
>
> Mother cleans the rice with a winnowing basket.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**शूर्पम्** (*śūrpam*) is the word MW records for a winnowing basket, the wicker tray that is shaken to fan the husk away; MW gives no derivation, so its origin is uncertain (MW).
