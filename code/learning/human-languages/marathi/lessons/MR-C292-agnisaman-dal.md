## You'll want to know: अग्निशमन दल
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C292-AAG292-05]; assesses=[] -->

**अग्निशमन दल** — *agniśaman dal* — "a fire brigade".

> अग्निशमन दलाच्या गाड्या दहा मिनिटांत आल्या.
>
> *agniśaman dalācyā gāḍyā dahā miniṭānt ālyā.*
>
> The fire brigade arrived within ten minutes.

Neuter.

दल is neuter, and so is the whole: अग्निशमन दलाला फोन करणे, to call the fire brigade.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अग्निशमन दल** (*agniśaman dal*) is a modern compound built from Sanskrit *agni*, 'fire', *śamana*, 'quelling', and *dala*, 'a troop' (Monier-Williams).
