## You'll want to know: મુક્ત
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C350-KARAR350-03]; assesses=[] -->

**મુક્ત** — *mukt* — "free of; released (from a duty, a debt or a worry)".

> બધું બિલ ચૂકવ્યા પછી તે ચિંતાથી મુક્ત થયો.
>
> *badhũ bil cūkavyā pachī te cintāthī mukt thayo.*
>
> Once he had paid the whole bill he was free of worry.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**મુક્ત** (*mukt*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *mukta*, "loosened, set free" (Monier-Williams).
