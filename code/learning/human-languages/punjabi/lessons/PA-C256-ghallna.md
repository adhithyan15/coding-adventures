# ਘੱਲਣਾ (ghallṇā) — to send off; to send

## You'll want to know: ਘੱਲਣਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C256-DAK256-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ਘੱਲਣਾ** — *ghallṇā* — "to send off; to send".

> ਮੈਂ ਹਰ ਮਹੀਨੇ ਪਿੰਡ ਚਿੱਠੀ ਘੱਲਦਾ ਹਾਂ।
>
> *maiṁ har mahīne piṇḍ chiṭṭhī ghalldā hāṁ.*
>
> Every month I send a letter to the village.

Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਅਮਨ ਨੂੰ ਪਾਰਸਲ ਘੱਲਿਆ, *maiṁ Aman nū̃ pārsal ghalliā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਘੱਲਣਾ** (*ghallṇā*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*ghalyati*, "pours"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *ghallṇā*, "to send", under it (CDIAL 4457).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for reading notices and messages.
