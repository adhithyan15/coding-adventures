## You'll want to know: as análises
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C487-CONSULTA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**as análises** — "(medical) tests".

> O médico mandou-me fazer análises ao sangue.
>
> *The doctor sent me for blood tests.*

*Fazer análises* — to have tests done. Singular *a análise* — an analysis.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*análise* is a learned word, usually traced through Medieval Latin to Greek *análysis* "a loosening, a breaking up".
