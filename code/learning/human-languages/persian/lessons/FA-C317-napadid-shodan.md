## You'll want to know: ناپدید شدن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C317-TAQIB-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ناپدید شدن** (*nâpadid shodan*) — "to disappear, to vanish".

> گربه ما دو روز ناپدید شد.
>
> *Gorbe-ye mâ do ruz nâpadid shod.*
>
> Our cat disappeared for two days.

*nâ-* "not" + *padid* "visible". Present *nâpadid mishavad*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*nâpadid* joins the native prefix *nâ-* "not" and *padid* "visible"; *shodan* "to become" is inherited Persian. EtymDB lists no entry.
