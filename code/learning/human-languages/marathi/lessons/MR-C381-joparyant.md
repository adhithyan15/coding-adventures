## You'll want to know: जोपर्यंत
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C381-JOPARYANT381-01]; assesses=[] -->

**जोपर्यंत** — *joparyant* — "as long as; until".

> जोपर्यंत तू येत नाहीस, मी जेवणार नाही.
>
> *joparyant tū yet nāhīs, mī jevṇār nāhī.*
>
> I won't eat until you come.

With a negative it means until: जोपर्यंत … नाही; a तो- clause often answers it.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**जोपर्यंत** (*joparyant*) is built from जो, "which", and -पर्यंत, "up to", from Sanskrit *paryanta*, "limit, edge" (Monier-Williams): up to the point which.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
