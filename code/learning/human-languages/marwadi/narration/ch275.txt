Marwadi (Marwari), chapter 275: Well Done!.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
सरावणो (sarāvṇo) — to praise.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for mercy, then the Marwadi for alms.

You'll want to know: सरावणो.
सरावणो — sarāvṇo — "to praise".
सगळा गीत सरावै है। sagḷā gīt sarāvai hai. Everyone praises the song.
Past (I …): म्हैं गीत सरायो, mhaĩ gīt sarāyo.

The word, taken apart.
सरावणो (sarāvṇo) is inherited from Sanskrit ślāghate, "praises"; CDIAL lists Old Marwari sarāi, "praises" (CDIAL 12734).

Lesson 2 of 5.
साबास (sābās) — well done.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for alms, then the Marwadi for to praise.

You'll want to know: साबास.
साबास — sābās — "well done".
साबास, छोरी! sābās, chhorī! Well done, girl!

The word, taken apart.
साबास (sābās) is usually traced to Persian shābāsh, "bravo"; it is said on its own, as praise.

Lesson 3 of 5.
इनाम (inām) — a prize.

[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for to praise, then the Marwadi for well done.

You'll want to know: इनाम.
इनाम — inām — "a prize".
छोरी इनाम जीतै है। chhorī inām jītai hai. The girl wins a prize.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
इनाम (inām) is usually traced, through Persian inʿām, to Arabic inʿām, "a gift, a favour".

Lesson 4 of 5.
जस (jas) — credit; renown.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for well done, then the Marwadi for a prize.

You'll want to know: जस.
जस — jas — "credit; renown".
यो जस थारो है। yo jas thāro hai. The credit is yours.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
जस (jas) is inherited from Sanskrit yaśas, "splendour, renown"; CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry, but lists Hindi jas, "fame" (CDIAL 10443).

Lesson 5 of 5.
राय (rāy) — an opinion.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for a prize, then the Marwadi for credit for a deed.

You'll want to know: राय.
राय — rāy — "an opinion".
थारी राय कांई है? thārī rāy kāĩ hai? What's your opinion?
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
राय (rāy) is usually traced, through Persian rāy, to Arabic raʾy, "opinion, view". It is feminine: थारी राय, your opinion.
