# Review — tell it back

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[IT-GRAMMAR-POI-01, IT-GRAMMAR-ALLA-FINE-02] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "first", "then" and "in the end". (*Prima, poi, alla fine.*)

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[IT-GRAMMAR-STORIA-IN-ORDINE-01, IT-SKILL-READ-NARRATIVE-01, IT-GRAMMAR-SCENA-EVENTO-01] -->

You have read a story. Now you tell one, which is the same skill turned round:
a reader sorts verbs into scene and event, and a teller has to choose them.

[PAUSE 2s] The journey from the reading, in five cues. Answer each one in
Italian, out loud, in a full sentence. Choose the past as you go: is this cue
the scene, or something that happened?

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[IT-GRAMMAR-IMPERFETTO-01, IT-LEX-ERA-01, IT-LEX-C-ERA-02, IT-LEX-FACEVA-01, IT-LEX-C187-TEMPO187-04, IT-GRAMMAR-POI-01, IT-GRAMMAR-ALLA-FINE-02] -->

[PAUSE 1s]
- [YOU SAY: the weather that morning (*Ieri mattina faceva freddo e pioveva.*)]
- [YOU SAY: where you were, and who was with you (*Ero alla stazione, e il mio amico era lì.*)]
- [YOU SAY: the first thing you did, then the second (*Prima abbiamo aspettato al binario. Poi abbiamo preso un caffè.*)]
- [YOU SAY: what cut into your talk (*Mentre parlavamo, hanno annunciato il treno.*)]
- [YOU SAY: where you were in the end, and how you felt (*Alla fine eravamo al mare. Ero stanco, ma ero contento.*)]

[PAUSE 3s] Now tell the whole journey once more, in your own order and your
own words, without the cues.

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[IT-GRAMMAR-STORIA-IN-ORDINE-01, IT-ETYMON-RACCONTARE-02, IT-ETYMON-POI-03, IT-GRAMMAR-SCENA-EVENTO-01] -->

[PAUSE 3s] Which of your five cues were scene? (The weather, where you were,
the talk that was running, how you felt.) Which were events? (Waiting, the
coffee, the announcement.) Which Latin word is behind *poi*? (***Post***.) And
what are you doing, literally, when you *raccontare*? (**Counting it out** —
*contare*.)
