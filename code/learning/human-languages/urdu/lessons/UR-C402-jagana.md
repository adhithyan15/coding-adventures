## You'll want to know: جگانا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C402-MUSAFAT402-05]; assesses=[] -->

**جگانا** — *jagānā* — "to wake (someone) up".

> کنڈکٹر نے صبح سب مسافروں کو جگایا۔
>
> *kanḍakṭar ne subah sab musāfiroṅ ko jagāyā.*
>
> In the morning the conductor woke all the passengers.

Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے سارا کو جگایا, *us ne sārā ko jagāyā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**جگانا** (*jagānā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists Hindi *jagānā* beside *jāgnā*, "to wake", under Sanskrit *jāgrati*, "are awake" (CDIAL 5175).
