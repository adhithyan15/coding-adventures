Persian, chapter 475: Storms.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
رعد و برق (ra'd-o barq) — a thunderstorm; thunder and lightning.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to stop, then the Persian for floodwater.

You'll want to know: رعد و برق.
رعد و برق (ra'd-o barq) — "a thunderstorm; thunder and lightning".
دیشب رعد و برق شدیدی بود. Dishab ra'd-o barq-e shadidi bud. Last night there was a heavy thunderstorm.
ra'd 'thunder' + o 'and' + barq 'lightning'.

The word, taken apart.
ra'd-o barq joins two Arabic loans with Persian o 'and': ra'd from Arabic raʿd 'thunder' and barq from Arabic barq 'flashing, lightning' (EtymDB lists both).

صاعقه (sâ'eqe) به درخت بلند حیاط خورد. Sâ'eqe be derakht-e boland-e hayât khord. Lightning struck the tall tree in the courtyard.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a thunderstorm, then the Persian for a lightning strike.

You'll want to know: غرش کردن.
غرش کردن (ghorresh kardan) — "to roar, to rumble (of thunder, a lion, an engine)".
آسمان غرش کرد و باران شروع شد. Âsmân ghorresh kard va bârân shoru' shod. The sky rumbled and the rain began.
ghorresh 'a roar', from ghorridan 'to roar', + kardan.

The word, taken apart.
ghorresh kardan is built from ghorresh 'a roar', from ghorridan 'to roar', whose origin is not settled, and kardan 'to do', inherited from Middle Persian kardan (EtymDB).

Lesson 4 of 6.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a lightning strike, then the Persian for to roar.

You'll want to know: تندباد.
تندباد (tondbâd) — "a gale, a strong gust of wind".
تندباد (tondbâd) چند درخت را انداخت. Tondbâd chand derakht râ andâkht. The gale blew down several trees.
tond 'fast, strong' + bâd 'wind'.

The word, taken apart.
tondbâd is a native compound of tond 'fast, violent', usually traced to Middle Persian tund, and bâd 'wind', usually traced to Middle Persian wād.

در بیابان یک گردباد کوچک دیدیم. Dar biyâbân yek gerdbâd-e kuchek didim. We saw a small whirlwind in the desert.
