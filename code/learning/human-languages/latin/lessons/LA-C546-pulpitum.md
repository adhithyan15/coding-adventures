## You'll want to know: pulpitum, pulpitī
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C546-CAVE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**pulpitum, pulpitī** — "a stage, platform".

> Histriō in pulpitum ascendit.
>
> *The actor climbs onto the stage.*

Neuter, second declension. A wooden platform for actors or speakers. English *pulpit*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**pulpitum** is a Latin word whose origin is not settled; Lewis & Short gives none. English *pulpit* comes from it.
