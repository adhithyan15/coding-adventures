## You'll want to know: la pompe
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C403-POMPE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**la pompe** — "the pump".

> La pompe numéro trois ne marche pas.
>
> *Pump number three is not working.*

*La pompe à essence* is the petrol pump; *une pompe à vélo* is a bicycle pump.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

The origin of *pompe* is not settled. It is usually linked to Middle Low German *pumpe* 'pump' (EtymDB) and may imitate the sound of the pump.
