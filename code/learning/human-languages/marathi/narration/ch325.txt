Marathi, chapter 325: A King and His Court.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
राजा (rājā) — a king.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to go off, then the Marathi for to compose.

You'll want to know: राजा.
राजा — rājā — "a king".
एकेकाळी या प्रदेशात एक दयाळू राजा होता. ekekāḷī yā pradeśāt ek dayāḷū rājā hotā. Once upon a time there was a kind king in this region.
Masculine. Plural: राजे, rāje.
Plural राजे.

The word, taken apart.
राजा (rājā) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit rājan, 'a king', in its nominative form rājā (Monier-Williams).

You'll want to know: राजवाडा.
राजवाडा — rājvāḍā — "a palace".
जुन्या राजवाड्यात आता संग्रहालय आहे. junyā rājvāḍyāt ātā saṅgrahālay āhe. There is now a museum in the old palace.
Masculine.
The oblique is राजवाड्या-: राजवाड्यात, in the palace.

The word, taken apart.
राजवाडा (rājvāḍā) is built from rāj-, 'royal', and vāḍā, 'a mansion, an enclosure', which goes back to Sanskrit vāṭa, 'an enclosure' (CDIAL 11480).

Lesson 5 of 5.
राज्य करणे (rājya karṇe) — to reign.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a palace, then the Marathi for a royal court.

You'll want to know: राज्य करणे.
राज्य करणे — rājya karṇe — "to reign".
त्या राजाने चाळीस वर्षं राज्य केलं. tyā rājāne cāḷīs varṣaṁ rājya kelaṁ. That king reigned for forty years.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने राज्य केलं, tyāne rājya kelaṁ.
The land ruled takes -वर: देशावर राज्य करणे (rājya karṇe).

The word, taken apart.
राज्य करणे (rājya karṇe) joins rājya, a tatsama taken from Sanskrit rājya, 'kingship, a kingdom' (Monier-Williams), to karṇe, 'to do'.
