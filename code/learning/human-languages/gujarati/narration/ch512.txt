Gujarati, chapter 512: Looking for a Job.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
પદ (pad) — a job post.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a mechanic, then the Gujarati for a clerk.

You'll want to know: પદ.
પદ — pad — "a job post".
આ પદ માટે ઘણી અરજીઓ આવી. ā pad māṭe ghaṇī arjīo āvī. Many applications came in for this post.
A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
પદ (pad) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit pada, "a step, a footing; a position" (Monier-Williams); in an office it is a post one is appointed to.

Lesson 3 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a job post, then the Gujarati for a job interview.

વેતન — vetan — "a wage".
આ કારખાનામાં મજૂરોનું વેતન ઓછું છે. ā kārkhānāmā̃ majūronũ vetan ochũ che. The workers' wages in this factory are low.
A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
વેતન (vetan) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit vetana, "wages, hire, salary" (Monier-Williams). It is the formal word beside the everyday પગાર.

Lesson 5 of 5.
બઢતી (baḍhtī) — a promotion (at work).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a wage, then the Gujarati for a career.

You'll want to know: બઢતી.
બઢતી — baḍhtī — "a promotion (at work)".
અરુણને આ વરસે બઢતી મળી. Aruṇne ā varse baḍhtī maḷī. Arun got a promotion this year.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
બઢતી (baḍhtī) is probably borrowed from Hindi baṛhtī, "an increase", built on baṛhnā, "to increase", which CDIAL lists under Sanskrit várdhatē, "grows" (CDIAL 11376). CDIAL gives Gujarati's own verb from the same root, વધવું, there too.
