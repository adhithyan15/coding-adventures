Lesson 1 of 6.
しだいに (shidaini) — gradually.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for beyond expectation, then the Japanese for sure.

You'll want to know: しだいに.
しだいに — shidaini — "gradually".
はるに なったら、しだいに あたたかく なるでしょう。 — haru ni nattara, shidaini atatakaku naru deshō — "When spring comes, it will gradually get warmer."

The word, taken apart.
しだいに (shidaini) is Sino-Japanese (kango), with the particle ni: two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are shi, "next" (Middle Chinese tshijH) and dai, "No." (dejH) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).

Lesson 4 of 6.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for little by little, then the Japanese for step by step.

You'll want to know: ようやく.
ようやく — yōyaku — "finally (after a long wait); at last".
あと いちにち まったら、ようやく やすみに なるでしょう。 — ato ichinichi mattara, yōyaku yasumi ni naru deshō — "If I wait one more day, I will finally have a holiday."

The word, taken apart.
ようやく (yōyaku) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, and usually written in kana; its older origin is not settled (JMdict).

Lesson 5 of 6.
ずいぶん (zuibun) — very; a good deal.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for step by step, then the Japanese for finally (after a long wait).

You'll want to know: ずいぶん.
ずいぶん — zuibun — "very; a good deal".
もし まいにち あるいたら、そふは ずいぶん つよく なるでしょう。 — moshi mainichi aruitara, sofu wa zuibun tsuyoku naru deshō — "If my grandfather walks every day, he will get a good deal stronger."

The word, taken apart.
ずいぶん (zuibun) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese, though JMdict marks it as usually written in kana. They are zui, "follow" (Middle Chinese zjwe) and bun, "part" (bjunH); how the two came to mean "very" no dictionary we use records (KANJIDIC2, JMdict; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
