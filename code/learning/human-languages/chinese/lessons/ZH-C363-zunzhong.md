## You'll want to know: zūnzhòng
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C363-PUBLIC363-01]; assesses=[] -->

*zūnzhòng* — "to show respect for; to respect".

> *wǒmen yīnggāi zūnzhòng biérén de xiǎngfǎ* — We should respect other people's ideas.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*zūnzhòng* is built from *zūn* and 重 'heavy', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *tswon* and *drjowngX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'honoured, as against humble' and the second 'much' and 'careful'.
