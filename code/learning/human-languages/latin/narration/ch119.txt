Lesson 6 of 7.
Can, want and why — a first pass.

Warm-up.
[pause 3 seconds]
Say the words for next to and opposite.

Guided Practice.
[pause 2 seconds after each]
[your turn — say: to be able, to dare, to have to, to be used to, power]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: a chance, a skill, it is necessary, it is allowed, to not want]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: to prefer, to desire, to wish for, to miss, a wish]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: a desire, hope, a longing, why?, why?]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: for, for, for that reason, and so, a reason]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
To be able? (queō, quīre.) To dare? (audeō, audēre.) To have to? (dēbeō, dēbēre.) To be used to? (soleō, solēre.) Power? (potestās, potestātis.) A chance? (facultās, facultātis.) A skill? (ars, artis.) It is necessary? (oportet.) It is allowed? (licet.) To not want? (nōlō, nōlle.) To prefer? (mālō, mālle.) To desire? (cupiō, cupere.) To wish for? (optō, optāre.) To miss? (dēsīderō, dēsīderāre.) A wish? (voluntās, voluntātis.) A desire? (cupiditās, cupiditātis.) Hope? (spēs, speī.) A longing? (dēsīderium, dēsīderiī.) Why?? (quārē.) Why?? (quamobrem.) For? (nam.) For? (enim.) For that reason? (ideō.) And so? (itaque.) A reason? (causa, causae.)
