# ಉಪನಗರ (upanagara) — a suburb

## You'll want to know: ಉಪನಗರ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C466-UPANAGARA466-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ಉಪನಗರ** — *upanagara* — "a suburb".

> ನಾವು ನಗರದ ಹೊರಗಿನ ಒಂದು ಉಪನಗರದಲ್ಲಿ ಮನೆ ಹುಡುಕಿದೆವು.
>
> *nāvu nagarada horagina ondu upanagaradalli mane huḍukidevu.*
>
> We looked for a house in a suburb outside the city.

ಉಪ-, near or secondary, plus ನಗರ, a city.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಉಪನಗರ** (*upanagara*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *upanagara*, "a suburb" (Monier-Williams), from *upa-*, "near", and *nagara*, "a city".
