# મટાડવું (maṭāḍvũ) — to cure

## You'll want to know: મટાડવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C484-ILAJ484-04]; assesses=[] -->

**મટાડવું** — *maṭāḍvũ* — "to cure".

> આ દવા તાવ મટાડે છે.
>
> *ā davā tāv maṭāḍe che.*
>
> This medicine cures fever.

Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે રોગ મટાડ્યો, *teṇe rog maṭāḍyo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**મટાડવું** (*maṭāḍvũ*) is the causative of **મટવું** (*maṭvũ*), "to be cured", which CDIAL lists, "to be rubbed out", under Sanskrit *mṛṣṭa*, "rubbed" (CDIAL 10299).
