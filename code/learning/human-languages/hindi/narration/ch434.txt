Hindi, chapter 434: Pure Gold and Touchstones.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
असली (aslī) — real, genuine.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a weekly market, then the Hindi for to hand (to).

You'll want to know: असली.
असली — aslī — "real, genuine".
यह असली चाँदी है। yah aslī cā̃dī hai. This is real silver.

The word, taken apart.
असली (aslī) is usually traced to Arabic aṣlī, "original", from aṣl, "a root", through Persian: the thing itself, not a copy.

Lesson 3 of 5.
रगड़ना (ragaṛnā) — to rub.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for real, then the Hindi for pure.

You'll want to know: रगड़ना.
रगड़ना — ragaṛnā — "to rub".
सुनार ने सोने को पत्थर पर रगड़ा। sunār ne sone ko patthar par ragaṛā. The goldsmith rubbed the gold on a stone.
Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने हाथ रगड़े, usne hāth ragaṛe.

The word, taken apart.
रगड़ना (ragaṛnā) goes back to ragg, "rub", a form CDIAL reconstructs (CDIAL 10558), which lists Hindi ragaṛnā. Goldsmiths test gold by the streak it leaves when rubbed on a dark stone.

Lesson 5 of 5.
कसौटी (kasauṭī) — a touchstone; a test.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for to rub, then the Hindi for to suit.

You'll want to know: कसौटी.
कसौटी — kasauṭī — "a touchstone; a test".
सुनार कसौटी पर सोना रगड़कर देखता है। sunār kasauṭī par sonā ragaṛkar dekhtā hai. The goldsmith tests gold by rubbing it on a touchstone.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
कसौटी (kasauṭī) is a tadbhava of Sanskrit kaṣapaṭṭikā, "a touchstone" (CDIAL 2973), which lists Hindi kasauṭī; CDIAL suspects the Sanskrit spelling is itself a learned remaking of an older form. The goldsmith's dark stone also stands for any real test: समय की कसौटी, the test of time.
