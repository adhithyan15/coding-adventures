# چھانا (chhānā) — to spread over; to cover (of clouds or mist)

## You'll want to know: چھانا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C431-ABR431-03]; assesses=[] -->

**چھانا** — *chhānā* — "to spread over; to cover (of clouds or mist)".

> شام کو آسمان پر ابر چھا گیا۔
>
> *shām ko āsmān par abr chhā gayā.*
>
> In the evening cloud spread across the sky.

Past (it …): وہ کہرا شہر پر چھا گیا, *voh kohrā shahr par chhā gayā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**چھانا** (*chhānā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *chānā*, "to thatch, to spread around", under Sanskrit *chādáyati*, "covers" (CDIAL 5018).
