## You'll want to know: a narrativa
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C324-CONTO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**a narrativa** — "the narrative, the story as told".

> A narrativa começa numa aldeia pequena.
>
> *The narrative begins in a small village.*

Also an adjective: *um texto narrativo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*narrativa* is built on *narrativo* (EtymDB), from Latin *nārrātīvus*, from *nārrāre* "to tell".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
