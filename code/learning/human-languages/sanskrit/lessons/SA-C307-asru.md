## You'll want to know: अश्रु
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C307-VIYOGA307-02]; assesses=[] -->

**अश्रु** — *aśru* — "a tear".

> मातुः नेत्रात् अश्रु पतति।
>
> *mātuḥ netrāt aśru patati.*
>
> A tear falls from the mother's eye.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अश्रु** (*aśru*) MW reports that it is supposed to stand for *daśru*, from the root √daṃś, and MW compares Greek *dakru*, Latin *lacryma* and English *tear*; that origin is not settled (MW).
