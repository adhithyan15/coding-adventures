## You'll want to know: भागीदार
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C511-DHANDA511-04]; assesses=[] -->

**भागीदार** — *bhāgīdār* — "a business partner".

> दोन मित्र या व्यवसायात भागीदार आहेत.
>
> *don mitra yā vyavsāyāt bhāgīdār āhet.*
>
> Two friends are partners in this business.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**भागीदार** (*bhāgīdār*) is built on भाग, "a share", from Sanskrit *bhāga* (Monier-Williams), with the Persian ending *-dār*, "holder": the one who holds a share.
