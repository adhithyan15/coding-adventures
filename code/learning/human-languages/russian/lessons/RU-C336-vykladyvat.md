## You'll want to know: выкладывать
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C336-GOTOVO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**выкладывать** (*vykládyvat*) — "to lay out, to put out".

> Она выложила печенье на тарелку.
>
> *Oná výlozhila pechénye na tarélku.*
>
> She put the biscuits out on a plate.

Imperfective; perfective *выложить* (*výlozhit*). In speech also "to tell everything": *выложить всё* (*výlozhit vsyo*).

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*выкладывать* is built on *вы-* "out" and the stem *клад-* of *класть* "to put", an inherited Slavic verb; EtymDB lists no parent.
