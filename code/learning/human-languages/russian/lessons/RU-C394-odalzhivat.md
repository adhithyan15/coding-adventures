## You'll want to know: одалживать
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C394-DOLG-03]; assesses=[] -->

**одалживать** (*odálzhivat*) — "to lend; to borrow".

> Я часто одалживаю брату деньги.
>
> *Ya chásto odálzhivayu brátu déngi.*
>
> I often lend my brother money.

Imperfective; perfective *одолжить*. *Одалживать кому* — to lend to; *одалживать у кого* — to borrow from.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**одалживать** is the imperfective of *одолжить*, built on *долг* "a debt", Proto-Slavic *\*dŭlgŭ* (EtymDB): to put someone in your debt. The *о* turns to *а* in the imperfective, as in *спрашивать*.
