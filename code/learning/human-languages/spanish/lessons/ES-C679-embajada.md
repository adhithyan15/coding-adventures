## You'll want to know: la embajada
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C679-SERVICIOS-02]; assesses=[] -->

**la embajada** — "the embassy".

> Perdí el pasaporte y llamé a la embajada.
>
> *I lost my passport and phoned the embassy.*

The person is *el embajador*, *la embajadora*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*embajada* came from Occitan *ambaissada* "embassy" (EtymDB), from Late Latin *ambactia*, built on Gaulish *ambactos* "a servant, a messenger".
