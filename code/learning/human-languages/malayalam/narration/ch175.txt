Malayalam, chapter 175: Love, Sell, Send, Throw, Climb.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
സ്നേഹിക്കുക (snēhikkuka) — to love.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for to find, then the Malayalam for to forget.

You'll want to know: സ്നേഹിക്കുക.
സ്നേഹിക്കുക — snēhikkuka — "to love".

The word, taken apart.
സ്നേഹിക്കുക (snēhikkuka) is built on Sanskrit snēha, "oiliness; tenderness, love" (CDIAL 13802), with the ending that makes a Malayalam verb.
