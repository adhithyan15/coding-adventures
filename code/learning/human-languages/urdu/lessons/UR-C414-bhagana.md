# بھگانا (bhagānā) — to chase off; to drive away

## You'll want to know: بھگانا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C414-GARD414-05]; assesses=[] -->

**بھگانا** — *bhagānā* — "to chase off; to drive away".

> گارڈ نے کتوں کو بھگایا۔
>
> *gārḍ ne kuttoṅ ko bhagāyā.*
>
> The guard chased the dogs away.

Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے بلی کو بھگایا, *us ne billī ko bhagāyā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**بھگانا** (*bhagānā*) is built on **بھاگنا**, "to run away", which CDIAL lists under Sanskrit *bhajyate*, "is broken", and its past form *bhagna*, "routed" (CDIAL 9361): this is the form for putting someone to flight.
