## You'll want to know: चत्वरम्
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C421-PALLI421-03]; assesses=[] -->

**चत्वरम्** — *catvaram* — "a town square".

> सायं चत्वरे बालाः क्रीडन्ति।
>
> *sāyaṃ catvare bālāḥ krīḍanti.*
>
> In the evening children play in the square.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**चत्वरम्** (*catvaram*) is the word MW gives for a square place, a place where many ways meet; MW gives no derivation, so its origin is uncertain (MW).
