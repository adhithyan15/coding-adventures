## You'll want to know: సన్నివేశం
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C541-NATAKAM541-02]; assesses=[] -->

**సన్నివేశం** — *sannivēśaṁ* — "a scene".

> సినిమాలో చివరి సన్నివేశం చూసి అందరూ ఏడ్చారు.
>
> *sinimālō civari sannivēśaṁ cūsi andarū ēḍcāru.*
>
> Everyone cried at the last scene of the film.

A scene in a film or a play; also a situation.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**సన్నివేశం** (*sannivēśaṁ*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *saṁniveśa*, "settling down together; a position, a situation" (Monier-Williams).

## What you've built

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
