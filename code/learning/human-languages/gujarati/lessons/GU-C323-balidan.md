## You'll want to know: બલિદાન
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C323-SHAHID323-02]; assesses=[] -->

**બલિદાન** — *balidān* — "a sacrifice".

> સૈનિકોના બલિદાનથી ગામ બચી ગયું.
>
> *sainikonā balidānthī gām bacī gayũ.*
>
> The soldiers' sacrifice saved the village.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**બલિદાન** (*balidān*) is Sanskrit *balidāna*, "the presenting of an offering" (Monier-Williams), built from *bali*, an offering, and *dāna*, a giving. In Gujarati it is giving up one's life or comfort for others.
