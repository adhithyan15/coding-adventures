## You'll want to know: しょうぶ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C462-CONTEST462-01]; assesses=[] -->

**しょうぶ** — *shōbu* — "a contest; a match".

> **あには おとうとと しょうぶを しました。** — *ani wa otōto to shōbu o shimashita* — "My elder brother had a contest with my younger brother."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**しょうぶ** (*shōbu*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *shō*, "victory" (Middle Chinese *syingH*) and *bu*, "defeat" (*bjuwX*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
