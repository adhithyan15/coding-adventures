## You'll want to know: पाणिग्रहणम्
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C497-KANYADANA497-02]; assesses=[] -->

**पाणिग्रहणम्** — *pāṇigrahaṇam* — "taking the bride's hand".

> पाणिग्रहणम् अग्नेः समीपे भवति।
>
> *pāṇigrahaṇam agneḥ samīpe bhavati.*
>
> The taking of hands happens beside the fire.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पाणिग्रहणम्** (*pāṇigrahaṇam*) is built from *pāṇi*, "the hand", and *grahaṇa*, "taking", from the root √grah of **गृह्णाति**; MW gives it as *pāṇi-graha*, "taking the bride by the hand, marriage" (MW).
