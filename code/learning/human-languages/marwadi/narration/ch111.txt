Marwadi (Marwari), chapter 111: To want, Wish, Mind, Hope, Demand.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
चावणो (chāvṇo) — to want.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for to fly, then the Marwadi for to weigh.

You'll want to know: चावणो.
चावणो — chāvṇo — "to want".

The word, taken apart.
चावणो (chāvṇo) is a tadbhava, a word inherited and reshaped by sound change. It comes from cāh, "see, look for, desire", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants. CDIAL lists Old Marwari cāhaï under it (CDIAL 4775). CDIAL calls the further source of this reconstructed verb uncertain.

Lesson 5 of 5.
मांग (mā̃g) — a demand.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for the mind, then the Marwadi for hope.

You'll want to know: मांग.
मांग — mā̃g — "a demand".

The word, taken apart.
मांग (mā̃g) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from mārgya, "to be sought". CDIAL lists Old Marwari māṃga under it (CDIAL 10078). मांग (mā̃g) is the noun of māṅgṇo, "to ask for"; the Old Marwari row means "betrothed".
