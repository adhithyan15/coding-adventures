## You'll want to know: मुक़दमा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C295-ADALAT295-02]; assesses=[] -->

**मुक़दमा** — *muqadmā* — "a court case; a lawsuit".

> अदालत में चोर का मुक़दमा दो साल से चल रहा है।
>
> *adālat meṁ cor kā muqadmā do sāl se cal rahā hai.*
>
> The thief's case has been going on in court for two years.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**मुक़दमा** (*muqadmā*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *muqaddama*, "what is put in front", and so a case laid before a court. A case **चलता है**: it runs on.
