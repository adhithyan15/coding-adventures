Lesson 1 of 5.
साधनम् (sādhanam) — an instrument, a means.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a knot, then the Sanskrit for a small stool.

You'll want to know: साधनम्.
साधनम् — sādhanam — "an instrument, a means".

The word, taken apart.
sādhana is built on the root √sādh, "to go straight to a goal, accomplish" (MW). In the Rigveda it means "furthering", and later "completion" and "means" (CDIAL 13334): the instrument that gets something done.

bhāra is built on the root √bhṛ, "to bear, carry" (MW), the root of bharati, "bears": a load is what is borne. It lives on as Hindi bhār, "load" (CDIAL 9459).

Lesson 5 of 5.
पत्रिका (patrikā) — a newspaper, a magazine.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for goods for sale, then the Sanskrit for a shopkeeper.

You'll want to know: पत्रिका.
पत्रिका — patrikā — "a newspaper, a magazine".

The word, taken apart.
patrikā is pattra, "wing-feather, leaf, leaf of a book" (CDIAL 7733), with the suffix -ikā: a little leaf, a letter or document (MW). Its use for a newspaper or magazine is modern. Pattra lives on as Hindi pattā, "leaf".
