## You'll want to know: ਤਲਣਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C242-KAM242-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ਤਲਣਾ** — *talṇā* — "to fry".

> ਮੈਂ ਪਕੌੜੇ ਤੇਲ ਵਿੱਚ ਤਲਦੀ ਹਾਂ।
>
> *maiṁ pakauṛe tel vich taldī hāṁ.*
>
> I fry the pakoras in oil.

Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਪਕੌੜੇ ਤਲੇ, *maiṁ pakauṛe tale*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਤਲਣਾ** (*talṇā*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*talati*, "fries"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *talṇā*, "to fry, cook in ghee" (CDIAL 5736).
