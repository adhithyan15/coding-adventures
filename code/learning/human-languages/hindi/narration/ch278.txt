Hindi, chapter 278: What Was Going On.
3 lessons. All 3 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 3.
मैं पढ़ रहा था (maiṁ paṛh rahā thā) — what was going on.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say "I am doing" and "he was". (maiṁ kar rahā hūṁ, vah thā.) Then "she came". (vah āī.) The first two make this lesson; the third will interrupt it.

You'll want to know: was …-ing.
मैं किताब पढ़ रहा था। maiṁ kitāb paṛh rahā thā. — "I was reading a book."
You know कर रहा हूँ, "I am doing". Put था where हूँ stood and the same action moves into the past. Both रहा and था follow the one doing it:
मैं पढ़ रहा था (maiṁ paṛh rahā thā) — I was reading; a woman says मैं पढ़ रही थी (maiṁ paṛh rahī thī).
वह सो रहा था (vah so rahā thā) — he was sleeping; वह सो रही थी (vah so rahī thī) — she was sleeping.
बारिश हो रही थी (bāriś ho rahī thī) — it was raining.
There is no ने here, even with a thing: मैं खाना खा रहा था, never मैंने. ने belongs to the plain past alone.

Grammar Lens: a scene and an event.
जब मैं खाना खा रहा था, तब मीरा आई। jab maiṁ khānā khā rahā thā, tab Mīrā āī. — "When I was eating, Mira came."
A story needs two kinds of past. खा रहा था is the scene: it was going on, and the sentence does not say when it began or ended. आई is the event: it happened, once, inside the scene. जब … तब …, the frame you know for "when … then …", holds the two together.
जब बारिश हो रही थी, तब बिजली गई। (jab bāriś ho rahī thī, tab bijlī gaī.) — While it was raining, the power went.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: "What were you doing yesterday evening?" — tum kal śām ko kyā kar rahe the?]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: "We were drinking tea when the power went" — jab ham cāy pī rahe the, tab bijlī gaī]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[question — say your answer, then pause 24 seconds]
Say that you were reading a book when Arun came.

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
"I was reading"? (मैं पढ़ रहा था (maiṁ paṛh rahā thā), or पढ़ रही थी.) "It was raining"? (बारिश हो रही थी.) In जब मैं खाना खा रहा था, तब मीरा आई, which is the scene and which is the event? (खा रहा था is the scene; आई is the event.) Why no ने in मैं खाना खा रहा था? (ने belongs to the plain past alone.)


Lesson 2 of 3.
पिछला (pichlā) — last, previous.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say "I was reading a book". (maiṁ kitāb paṛh rahā thā.) Then the words for a week and a year. (haftā, sāl.)

You'll want to know: पिछला.
पिछला — pichlā — "last, previous": the one before this one.
पिछले हफ़्ते मैं गाँव गया। pichle hafte maiṁ gā̃v gayā. — "Last week I went to the village."
पिछला is an adjective, and it agrees: पिछला हफ़्ता (pichlā haftā), "last week", but पिछली बार (pichlī bār), "last time", because बार is feminine.
Said as a time, "last week" or "last year" takes the form you met in बच्चे को: पिछले हफ़्ते, पिछले साल.
पिछले साल हम बहुत व्यस्त थे। pichle sāl ham bahut vyast the. — "Last year we were very busy."

The word, taken apart.
पिछला (pichlā) has no row of its own in CDIAL. It is usually connected with पीछे (pīche), "behind", which CDIAL traces to paśca, "hinder part" (CDIAL 7990). The last week, on that picture, is the one behind you.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: pichlā, then pichle hafte]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: "Last week I went to the village" — pichle hafte maiṁ gā̃v gayā]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: "Last time it was raining" — pichlī bār bāriś ho rahī thī]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does पिछला (pichlā) mean? ("Last, previous".) "Last year"? (पिछले साल.) "Last time"? (पिछली बार: बार is feminine.)


Lesson 3 of 3.
Review — the scene and the event.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say "I was reading a book". (maiṁ kitāb paṛh rahā thā.) Then "last week" and "last time". (pichle hafte, pichlī bār.)

Guided Practice.
[pause 2 seconds after each]
[your turn — say: Last week I went to the village.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: What were you doing yesterday evening? I was cooking.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: When we were drinking tea, Arun came.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: It was raining, and suddenly the power went.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: Last year Mira read many books.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
Which past is the scene? (रहा था: बारिश हो रही थी.) Which is the event? (The plain past: मीरा आई.) "Last year she read many books"? (पिछले साल उसने बहुत किताबें पढ़ीं: ने, and पढ़ीं for the books.)
