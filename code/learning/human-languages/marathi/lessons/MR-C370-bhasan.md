## You'll want to know: भाषण
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C370-VYAKHYAN370-03]; assesses=[] -->

**भाषण** — *bhāṣaṇ* — "a speech".

> मुख्य पाहुण्यांनी छोटं भाषण केलं.
>
> *mukhya pāhuṇyānnī choṭaṁ bhāṣaṇ kelaṁ.*
>
> The chief guest gave a short speech.

Neuter.

भाषण करणे or देणे, to make a speech.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**भाषण** (*bhāṣaṇ*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *bhāṣaṇa*, "speaking, talk" (Monier-Williams).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
