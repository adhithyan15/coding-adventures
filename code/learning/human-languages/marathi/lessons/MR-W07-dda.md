# ड — the curl, with the voice on

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

Fingers on the throat, tongue curled back. Say *ṭa*, then switch the voice on.

## Script
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-SCRIPT-DDA-01]; assesses=[MR-SCRIPT-TTHA-01, MR-SCRIPT-TTA-01] -->

> ड

**ड** is *ḍa*, the voiced member of the curled row — the same relationship
**ग** has to **क**.

Marathi needs it for *ḍoḷā*, "eye", a word that also carries the retroflex
**ळ** you learned in the doorway chapters. Draw the top stroke, then the body
that curves down and closes to the left under the headline.

## Writing — delayed copy
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MR-SCRIPT-DDA-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: delayed-copy -->

Look at **ड** for five seconds, cover it, and write it once. Uncover and set it
beside **ठ**: one closes into a ring, the other does not.
