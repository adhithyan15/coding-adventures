## You'll want to know: modeste
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C473-ORGUEIL-04]; assesses=[] -->

**modeste** — "modest".

> Elle a gagné le prix, mais elle reste modeste.
>
> *She won the prize, but she remains modest.*

Also simple, small: *un salaire modeste*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*modeste* is a learned borrowing from Latin *modestus* "keeping within measure", from *modus* "measure" (Lewis & Short).
