## You'll want to know: બૂમ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C299-BUM299-02]; assesses=[] -->

**બૂમ** — *būm* — "a shout".

> લોકોએ બૂમો મારી અને ચોર નાસી ગયો.
>
> *lokoe būmo mārī ane cor nāsī gayo.*
>
> People shouted and the thief ran off.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**બૂમ** (*būm*) No dictionary we use records an older form, so its history is not settled. A shout is given with **મારવું**: **બૂમ મારી**, called out loudly.
