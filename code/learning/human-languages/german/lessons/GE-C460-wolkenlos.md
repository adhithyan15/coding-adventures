## You'll want to know: wolkenlos
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C460-WETTERBERICHT-03]; assesses=[] -->

**wolkenlos** — "cloudless".

> Am Nachmittag ist der Himmel wolkenlos.
>
> *In the afternoon the sky is cloudless.*

*-los* means without: *arbeitslos*, *kostenlos*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*wolkenlos* is built from *Wolke* 'cloud', inherited from Old High German *wolkan* (EtymDB), and *-los* 'without', from Proto-Germanic *lausaz* 'loose, free'.
