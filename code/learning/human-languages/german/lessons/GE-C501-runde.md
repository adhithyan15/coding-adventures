## You'll want to know: die Runde
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C501-BRETTSPIEL-05]; assesses=[] -->

**die Runde** — "round".

> Nach drei Runden hatte meine Schwester gewonnen.
>
> *After three rounds my sister had won.*

Plural: *die Runden*. *Eine Runde ausgeben* — to buy a round of drinks.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Runde* is built on *rund* "round", which came through Middle High German *runt* from Old French *ront* (EtymDB), itself from Latin *rotundus*.
