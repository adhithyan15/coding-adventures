## You'll want to know: નિહાળવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C315-UDGHATAN315-05]; assesses=[] -->

**નિહાળવું** — *nihāḷvũ* — "to watch; to behold".

> ઘણા લોકોએ ઉદ્ઘાટન નિહાળ્યું.
>
> *ghaṇā lokoe udghāṭan nihāḷyũ.*
>
> Many people watched the opening.

Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે આકાશ નિહાળ્યું, *teṇe ākāś nihāḷyũ*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**નિહાળવું** (*nihāḷvũ*) goes back to Sanskrit *nibhālayati*, "perceives, sees", and CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb, "to see" (CDIAL 7228). It is to watch with attention, a fuller word than **જોવું**.
