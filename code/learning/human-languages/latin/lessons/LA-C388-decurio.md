## You'll want to know: decuriō, decuriōnis
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C388-MUNICEPS-02]; assesses=[] -->

**decuriō, decuriōnis** — "a town councillor; a cavalry officer".

> Decuriōnēs dē aquaeductū dēlīberāvērunt.
>
> *The town councillors deliberated about the aqueduct.*

*decuriō, decuriōnis* m. In a town the *decuriōnēs* formed the council; in the army a *decuriō* led ten horsemen. Built on *decem*, ten.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*decuriō* is built on *decuria* "a group of ten" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short), which comes from *decem* "ten".
