## You'll want to know: 发光
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C517-LIGHT517-03]; assesses=[] -->

**发光** — *fāguāng* — "to give off light; to shine".

> **星星为什么会发光** *xīngxing wèishénme huì fāguāng* — Why do the stars shine?

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**发光** (*fāguāng*) is built from 发 'to send out' and 光 'light', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *pjot* and *kwang* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to start' and 'to open out' and the second 'bright'.
