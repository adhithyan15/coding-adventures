## You'll want to know: तन्त्री
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C521-VADAKA521-02]; assesses=[] -->

**तन्त्री** — *tantrī* — "a string of an instrument".

> वीणायाः एका तन्त्री जीर्णा अस्ति।
>
> *vīṇāyāḥ ekā tantrī jīrṇā asti.*
>
> One string of the veena is worn out.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**तन्त्री** (*tantrī*) comes from the root √tan, "to stretch", like *tantu*, "a thread"; MW gives *tantrī* as the wire or string of a lute (MW).
