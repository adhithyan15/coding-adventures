## You'll want to know: 四季
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C515-SEASONS515-02]; assesses=[] -->

**四季** — *sìjì* — "the four seasons".

> **这儿四季都有花** *zhèr sìjì dōu yǒu huā* — There are flowers here all year round.

The new character, in pieces: 季 — eight strokes in two pieces: on top the grain piece, five strokes, as on the left of 秋, but spread wide; and 子 at the foot, three strokes. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**四季** (*sìjì*) is built from 四 'four' and 季, both inherited from Middle Chinese, *sijH* and *kjwijH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription). The dictionary glosses the second as the youngest of brothers and 'young'; how it came to mean a season, as in 季节, is not settled.
