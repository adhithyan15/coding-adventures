## You'll want to know: рейс
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C419-REGISTRATSIYA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**рейс** (*reys*) — "a flight; a trip (of a bus or a ship)".

> Наш рейс в два часа дня.
>
> *Nash reys v dva chasá dnya.*
>
> Our flight is at two in the afternoon.

Masculine. *Рейс отменили* — the flight was cancelled.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*рейс* was borrowed from Dutch *reis* 'a journey, travel' (EtymDB); in Russian it names one trip of a plane, a ship or a bus.
