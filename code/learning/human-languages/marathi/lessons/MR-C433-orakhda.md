# ओरखडा (orakhḍā) — a scrape; a scratch

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Marathi for to get messed up, then the Marathi for to ooze.

## You'll want to know: ओरखडा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C433-TADA433-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ओरखडा** — *orakhḍā* — "a scrape; a scratch".

> गाडीच्या दारावर मोठा ओरखडा आहे.
>
> *gāḍīcyā dārāvar moṭhā orakhḍā āhe.*
>
> There is a big scratch on the car door.

Masculine.

ओरखडा पडणे, a scratch appears.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ओरखडा** (*orakhḍā*) is a Marathi word for a long, thin scratch; its origin is not settled.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for getting around.
