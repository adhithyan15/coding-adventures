## You'll want to know: લાંચ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C292-KAUBHAND292-03]; assesses=[] -->

**લાંચ** — *lā̃c* — "a bribe".

> એક સિપાઈએ ઠગ પાસેથી લાંચ લીધી.
>
> *ek sipāīe ṭhag pāsethī lā̃c līdhī.*
>
> A constable took a bribe from the swindler.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**લાંચ** (*lā̃c*) goes back to Sanskrit *lañcā*, "a present, a bribe", and CDIAL lists the Gujarati noun (CDIAL 10914). It is feminine: **લાંચ લીધી**, took a bribe.
