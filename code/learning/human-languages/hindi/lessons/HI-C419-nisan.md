## You'll want to know: निशान
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C419-TIRAHA419-03]; assesses=[] -->

**निशान** — *niśān* — "a mark left behind; a sign; a scar".

> दीवार पर लाल निशान है, वहाँ से दाएँ मुड़िए।
>
> *dīvār par lāl niśān hai, vahā̃ se dāẽ muṛiye.*
>
> There is a red mark on the wall; turn right there.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**निशान** (*niśān*) is usually traced to Persian *nišān*, "a sign, a mark": a scar, a stain, a mark to steer by.
