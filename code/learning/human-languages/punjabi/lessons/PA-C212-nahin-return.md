# Return to a familiar no

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-LEX-HAAN-NAHIN-01, PA-NEG-NOT-UNDERSTOOD-01] -->

[YOU HEAR: *nahīṁ*] Say **ਨਹੀਂ**. Then the whole sentence you have used since
the early lessons: **ਮੈਨੂੰ ਸਮਝ ਨਹੀਂ ਆਈ**, "I did not understand".

## Grammar Lens
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-NEG-CLAUSE-01, PA-GRAMMAR-KHANDA-PINDA-211] -->

You learned that **ਨਹੀਂ** goes directly before the verb and that nothing else
in the sentence moves. Hold that idea next to a sentence you can say now:
**ਮੈਂ ਚਾਹ ਪੀਂਦਾ ਹਾਂ**.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-LEX-HAAN-NAHIN-01, PA-NEG-NOT-UNDERSTOOD-01, PA-GRAMMAR-KHANDA-PINDA-211] -->

- [YOU HEAR: *no*] Say **ਨਹੀਂ**.
- [YOU HEAR: *I did not understand*] Say **ਮੈਨੂੰ ਸਮਝ ਨਹੀਂ ਆਈ**.
- [YOU HEAR: *I drink tea, as a man*] Say **ਮੈਂ ਚਾਹ ਪੀਂਦਾ ਹਾਂ**.

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-LEX-HAAN-NAHIN-01, PA-GRAMMAR-KHANDA-PINDA-211] -->

[PAUSE 3s] Say *no*, then *I drink tea*. The next lesson joins them.
