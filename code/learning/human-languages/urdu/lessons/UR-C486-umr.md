## You'll want to know: عمر
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C486-GROWUP486-01]; assesses=[] -->

**عمر** — *umr* — "age".

> میری بہن کی عمر دس سال ہے۔
>
> *merī bahan kī umr das sāl hai.*
>
> My sister is ten years old.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**عمر** (*umr*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *ʿumr*, "life, a lifetime".
