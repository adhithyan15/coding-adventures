# ಆರಾಮ (ārāma) — ease, comfort

## You'll want to know: ಆರಾಮ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C93-WEATHER-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ಆರಾಮ** (*ārāma*) — "ease, comfort, rest".

**ಆರಾಮಾಗಿದ್ದೀರಾ?** (*ārāmāgiddīrā?*) — "are you well, are you at ease?", a warm
way to ask how someone is.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

The feeling that comes after rest.
