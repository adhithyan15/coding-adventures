## You'll want to know: शपथः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C349-NYAYALAYA349-05]; assesses=[] -->

**शपथः** — *śapathaḥ* — "an oath".

> सः सत्यस्य शपथं करोति।
>
> *saḥ satyasya śapathaṃ karoti.*
>
> He swears an oath to tell the truth.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**शपथः** (*śapathaḥ*) comes from the root √śap, "to curse, to swear"; MW gives *śapatha* "an oath, a vow" (MW).
