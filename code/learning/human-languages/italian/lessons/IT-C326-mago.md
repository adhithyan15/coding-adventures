## You'll want to know: il mago
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C326-MAGIA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**il mago** — "the wizard, the magician".

> Il mago ha fatto sparire il coniglio.
>
> *The magician made the rabbit disappear.*

Also an expert: *un mago dei computer*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*mago* is inherited from Latin *magus* (EtymDB), from Greek *mágos*, a Persian priest.
