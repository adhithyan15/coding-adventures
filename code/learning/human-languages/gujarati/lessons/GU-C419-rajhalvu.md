## You'll want to know: રઝળવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C419-STREET419-05]; assesses=[] -->

**રઝળવું** — *rajhaḷvũ* — "to wander aimlessly; to be left roaming".

> રાત્રે શેરીમાં કૂતરા રઝળે છે.
>
> *rātre śerīmā̃ kūtrā rajhḷe che.*
>
> At night dogs wander the street.

Past (he …): તે આખો દિવસ રઝળ્યો, *te ākho divas rajhaḷyo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**રઝળવું** (*rajhaḷvũ*) No dictionary we use records an older form, so its history is not settled.
