## You'll want to know: el sobrepeso
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C742-DIETA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**el sobrepeso** — "excess weight, being overweight".

> El médico dice que tengo sobrepeso.
>
> *The doctor says I am overweight.*

*Tener sobrepeso* — to be overweight.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*sobrepeso* is built on *sobre* "over", from Latin *super*, and *peso* "weight", from Latin *pēnsum* "something weighed".
