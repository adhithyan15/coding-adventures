## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-JOIN-LEKIN-01]; assesses=[PA-JOIN-PAR-01] -->

> **ਲੇਕਿਨ** — *lekin* — **but**

The same job, the same position, and a completely different history. **ਲੇਕਿਨ**
is Arabic **lākin**, carried into Punjabi through Persian — the road that also
brought this book several of its greetings and farewells.

So Punjabi has two words for *but*: one that grew here and one that arrived. You
can use either. They are not in competition and neither is more correct.

What they are is a **pair**, and this book has been quietly teaching you another
one since its first chapter without ever pointing at it. The next lesson but one
is about that.
