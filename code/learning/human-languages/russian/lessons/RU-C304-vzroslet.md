## You'll want to know: взрослеть
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C304-DETSTVO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**взрослеть** (*vzroslét*) — "to grow up".

> Дети быстро взрослеют.
>
> *Déti býstro vzrosléyut.*
>
> Children grow up fast.

Imperfective; perfective *повзрослеть* (*povzroslét*). *Взрослый* — an adult.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*взрослеть* is built on *взрослый* "adult", from *вз-* "up" and the root *рос-* of *расти* "to grow": grown up. EtymDB lists no parent.
