## You'll want to know: दस्तख़त
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C429-PARCHI429-03]; assesses=[] -->

**दस्तख़त** — *dastkhat* — "a signature (everyday)".

> यहाँ अपने दस्तख़त कीजिए।
>
> *yahā̃ apne dastkhat kījiye.*
>
> Please sign here.

Masculine.

Usually plural in agreement: मैंने दस्तख़त किए, I signed.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**दस्तख़त** (*dastkhat*) is usually traced to Persian *dastkhaṭṭ*, from *dast*, "hand", and *khaṭṭ*, "writing": your hand's own mark. हस्ताक्षर is the formal word.
