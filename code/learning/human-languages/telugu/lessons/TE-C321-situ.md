# సీటు (sīṭu) — a seat

## You'll want to know: సీటు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C321-SITU-01]; assesses=[TE-LEX-C67-WALL-05, TE-LEX-C80-WISH-01, TE-LEX-C100-LOC100-04] -->

**సీటు** — *sīṭu* — "a seat", on a bus or a train, or in a hall.

> **ఈ సీటు ఖాళీగా ఉందా?**
>
> *ī sīṭu khāḷīgā undā?* — "Is this seat free?"

> **నాకు కిటికీ దగ్గర సీటు కావాలి.**
>
> *nāku kiṭikī daggara sīṭu kāvāli.* — "I would like a seat by the window."

**ఖాళీగా** (*khāḷīgā*, "empty, free") with **ఉందా?**, "is it?": the **-ఆ** question again. Two seats are
**రెండు సీట్లు** (*reṇḍu sīṭlu*), the way two tickets are **టిక్కెట్లు**.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**సీటు** (*sīṭu*) is a loan from English *seat*, with the **-ఉ** that Telugu gives most borrowed nouns, as in
**బస్సు** and **ఫోను**.
