Marathi, chapter 419: Neighbourhoods.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
वस्ती (vastī) — a settlement; a neighbourhood.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a small village, then the Marathi for a taluka.

You'll want to know: वस्ती.
वस्ती — vastī — "a settlement; a neighbourhood".
डोंगराखाली एक लहान वस्ती आहे. ḍoṅgarākhālī ek lahān vastī āhe. There is a small settlement at the bottom of the hill.
Feminine.
Also the people who live there: सगळी वस्ती (vastī) जमली, the whole neighbourhood gathered.

The word, taken apart.
वस्ती (vastī) belongs with Marathi vasṇẽ, "to settle", inherited from Sanskrit vasati, "stays, dwells" (CDIAL 11435); how the noun was formed is not settled.

Lesson 3 of 6.
पेठ (peṭh) — an old town quarter.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a settlement, then the Marathi for a housing colony.

You'll want to know: पेठ.
पेठ — peṭh — "an old town quarter".
पुण्यात अनेक जुन्या पेठा आहेत. puṇyāt anek junyā peṭhā āhet. Pune has many old quarters.
Feminine. Plural: पेठा, peṭhā.
In Pune each old quarter is a पेठ (peṭh); the plural is पेठा.

The word, taken apart.
पेठ (peṭh) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi peṭh, "town, quarter of a town, market", under Sanskrit pratiṣṭhā, "foundation, homestead" (CDIAL 8608).

बोळ — boḷ — "a narrow lane".
या बोळातून बाजारात लवकर पोहोचता येतं. yā boḷātūn bājārāt lavkar pohoctā yetaṁ. You can reach the market quickly through this lane.
Masculine.
Narrower than a गल्ली, often a short cut.

The word, taken apart.
बोळ (boḷ) is a Marathi word for a narrow passage between houses; its origin is not settled.

चाळ — cāḷ — "a tenement".
शहरात अजून काही जुन्या चाळी आहेत. śahrāt ajūn kāhī junyā cāḷī āhet. There are still some old tenements in the city.
Feminine. Plural: चाळी, cāḷī.
A long building of one-room homes along a shared balcony; the plural is चाळी.

The word, taken apart.
चाळ (cāḷ) is a Marathi word for a long tenement block; its origin is not settled.
