# Hear *zhōngwén* — Chinese

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ZH-SCRIPT-ZHONG-01, ZH-PERFORMANCE-LANGUAGE-IDENTITY-THREE-FOUR-SKILL-01] -->

[PAUSE 16s] From the last chapter, say **汉语, 汉字, 字** for **Chinese
language, Chinese character, written character**. Then say the sound of 中:
*zhōng*.

## You'll want to know
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-ZHONGWEN-01]; assesses=[] -->

> *zhōngwén* — **Chinese; the Chinese language**

Keep the level first tone in *zhōng*. Let *wén* rise in second tone. The new
second character stays hidden for now; this step is only sound and meaning.
