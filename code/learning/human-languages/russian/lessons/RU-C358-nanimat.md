## You'll want to know: нанимать
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C358-VAKANSIYA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**нанимать** (*nanimát*) — "to hire, to take on".

> Фирма нанимает новых сотрудников.
>
> *Fírma nanimáyet nóvykh sotrúdnikov.*
>
> The firm is hiring new employees.

Imperfective; perfective *нанять* (*найму, наймёшь*). An older use is to rent: *нанять квартиру*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**нанимать** is built from *на-* and *-нимать*, from an old verb "to take" that also stands in *снимать* and *занимать*. To hire someone is to take them on.
