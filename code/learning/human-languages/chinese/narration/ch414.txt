Mandarin Chinese, chapter 414: In the Cabin.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
chéngwùyuán — flight attendant; cabin crew.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for to come down onto the runway, then the Mandarin for as fast as flying.

You'll want to know: chéngwùyuán.
chéngwùyuán — "flight attendant; cabin crew".
chéngwùyuán zhǎohuí le wǒ de shǒujī — The flight attendant found my phone for me.
Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
chéngwùyuán is built from chéng 'to ride', wù 'a task' and yuán 'a member of staff', all inherited from Middle Chinese, zying, mjuH and hjwen (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'business, affairs'.

Lesson 3 of 5.
jīngjìcāng — economy class.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for a flight attendant, then the Mandarin for next to the window.

You'll want to know: jīngjìcāng.
jīngjìcāng — "economy class".
wǒmen zuò jīngjìcāng qù Běijīng — We're flying to Beijing in economy.
Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
jīngjìcāng is built from jīngjì 'economy' and cāng 'a cabin'. The first two parts are inherited from Middle Chinese, keng and tsejH (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'constant' and 'to cross a river'; no dictionary we use records an older form of the third, so its history is not settled.

Lesson 4 of 5.
yùnjī — to get airsick.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for next to the window, then the Mandarin for economy class.

You'll want to know: yùnjī.
yùnjī — "to get airsick".
wǒ zuò fēijī de shíhou huì yùnjī — I get airsick when I fly.
Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
yùnjī is built from yùn 'dizzy' and 机 'a plane'. The second is inherited from Middle Chinese kj+j (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription); the dictionary glosses the first, hjunH there, as a halo round the sun or moon, so how it came to mean dizziness is not settled.
