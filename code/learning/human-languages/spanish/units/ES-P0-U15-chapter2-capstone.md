# Unit 15: Chapter 2 Capstone — Asking for Help

## Warm-up

[PAUSE 2s] Everything from this chapter, recombined into one real
situation: you're lost and need directions and the time.

## New Material

No new vocabulary — pure recombination.

> "**Perdón, ¿qué hora es?**" — Excuse me, what time is it?
> "**Son las tres y media.**" — It's half past three.
> "**Gracias. ¿Dónde está la estación?**" — Thanks. Where's the station?
> "**No entiendo. ¿Puede repetir, más despacio por favor?**" — I don't
> understand. Can you repeat that, more slowly please?

## Guided Practice

- [YOU SAY: say you don't understand and ask for a repeat, more slowly]
