Persian, chapter 154: Store, Supermarket, Butcher's shop, Café, Doctor's surgery.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
فروشگاه (forushgâh) — a store, a shop.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for finally, then the Persian for weekly.

You'll want to know: فروشگاه.
فروشگاه — forushgâh — "a store, a shop".

The word, taken apart.
forushgâh is built from forush, "selling" (from forukhtan, "to sell"), and gâh, "place": the selling place. Both are inherited; forukhtan is usually traced to Middle Persian frōxtan, and gâh to gāh.

Lesson 3 of 5.
قصابی (qassâbi) — a butcher's shop.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a store, then the Persian for a supermarket.

You'll want to know: قصابی.
قصابی — qassâbi — "a butcher's shop".

The word, taken apart.
qassâbi is qassâb, "butcher", with the ending -i, "shop of". qassâb is borrowed from Arabic qaṣṣāb, "butcher" (root q-ṣ-b, Lane).

Lesson 4 of 5.
کافه (kâfe) — a café.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a supermarket, then the Persian for a butcher's shop.

You'll want to know: کافه.
کافه — kâfe — "a café".

The word, taken apart.
kâfe is borrowed from French café (EtymDB), which goes back through Italian and Turkish to Arabic qahwa, "coffee": the same word as ghahve, home again by a long road.
