Arabic, chapter 224: To earn, To borrow, To lend, To change, To hurry.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
كسب (kasaba) — to earn.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to fold, then the Arabic for to hang.

You'll want to know: كسب.
كسب — kasaba — "to earn".

The word, taken apart.
كسب (kasaba) is built on the native Arabic root k-s-b, "to gain, to earn" (Lane). Kasaba is the plain verb, "he earned".
