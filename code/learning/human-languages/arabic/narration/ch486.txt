Arabic, chapter 486: Changing weather.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
تقلب (taqallaba) — to keep changing; to fluctuate.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to shine, then the Arabic for to grow dark.

You'll want to know: تقلب.
تقلب — taqallaba — "to keep changing; to fluctuate".
يتقلب الطقس كثيرا في الربيع. yataqallabu aṭ-ṭaqs kathīran fī ar-rabīʿ. The weather changes a lot in spring.
Present: يتقلب, yataqallab.

The word, taken apart.
تقلب (taqallaba) is built on the native Arabic root q-l-b, "to turn over" (Lane). It is Form V, "to turn this way and that".

What you've built.
One more word for getting around.

Guided Practice.

[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: yataqallabu aṭ-ṭaqs kathīran fī ar-rabīʿ. — The weather changes a lot in spring.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Arabic for to shine, then the Arabic for to grow dark, then say taqallaba again]

Lesson 5 of 6.
تعرض (taʿarraḍa) — to be exposed (to); to suffer.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to fall steadily, then the Arabic for refreshing.

You'll want to know: تعرض.
تعرض — taʿarraḍa — "to be exposed (to); to suffer".
تعرض المسافرون للمطر. taʿarraḍa al-musāfirūn li-l-maṭar. The travellers were caught in the rain.
Present: يتعرض, yataʿarraḍ.
It takes li-: taʿarraḍa li-, "to be exposed to".

The word, taken apart.
تعرض (taʿarraḍa) is built on the native Arabic root ʿ-r-ḍ, "to show, to present" (Lane). It is Form V, "to put oneself in the way of".
