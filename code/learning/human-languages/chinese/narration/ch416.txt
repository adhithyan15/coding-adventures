6 lessons. You can do the first 2 of them in the car; after that you will want to have stopped.


Lesson 1 of 6.
yánwù — to be delayed; to be held up.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for the security check at an airport, then the Mandarin for a departure gate.

You'll want to know: yánwù.
yánwù — "to be delayed; to be held up".
hángbān yánwù le wǒmen yào zài jīchǎng zài děng liǎng ge xiǎoshí — The flight's delayed: we have to wait at the airport two more hours.
Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
yánwù is built from yán 'to draw out' and wù 'a mistake', both inherited from Middle Chinese, yen and nguH (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to stretch, to lengthen' and the second 'an error'.

Lesson 2 of 6.
场 (chǎng) — measure word for a rainstorm; measure word for a game or a show.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for a departure gate, then the Mandarin for to be held up.

You'll want to know: 场.
场 — chǎng — "measure word for a rainstorm; measure word for a game or a show".
今天有一场大雨 jīntiān yǒu yì chǎng dà yǔ — There's a big storm today.
The new character, in pieces: 场 (chǎng) — six strokes in two pieces: on the left the earth piece of 地, a bar, a vertical and a bar rising to the right; on the right a bar that turns down and zigzags to a hook, then two strokes falling to the left. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

The word, taken apart.
场 (chǎng) is inherited from Middle Chinese drjang (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed as a place for offerings to the spirits and a threshing floor; how it came to count spells of rain and games is not settled. drjang would regularly give a rising tone, cháng; the falling-rising tone of the measure word is irregular.

What you've built.
One more word for getting around.

Script — the earth piece, then a zigzag and two sweeps.
场 (chǎng).
Two pieces, left before right. Count the pieces, not the strokes.
The left piece, three strokes, as on the left of 地: a short bar, a vertical down through it, then a bar that rises to the right at the foot.
The right piece, three strokes. A bar to the right that turns down, zigzags and ends in a hook to the left: one stroke. Then two strokes falling to the left inside it, the second longer.
3 + 3 = six.

[once you have stopped driving — this part needs your eyes or your hands; if you are driving, skip ahead to the next part]
Guided Practice.
[once you have stopped driving — write: 场 (chǎng), all six, with the model in front of you]
[once you have stopped driving — cover: write it again from memory, and count the pieces, not the strokes]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
How many strokes in 场 (chǎng)? (Six.) What is the left piece? (The earth piece, as in 地.) How many strokes falling to the left at the end? (Two.)
