Lesson 1 of 5.
умирать (umirát) — to die.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to check, then the Russian for to fill in.

You'll want to know: умирать.
умирать (umirát) — "to die".

The word, taken apart.
умирать (umirát) is у- with -мирать, from мереть, "to die", Proto-Slavic merti, which Vasmer relates to Lithuanian mìrti and Latin morī, "to die".

The word, taken apart.
стучать (stuchát) comes from стук, "a knock" (Vasmer), which he compares with sound words like Czech ťukati, "to tap", and French toc.

Lesson 3 of 5.
ремонтировать (remontírovat) — to renovate, to repair.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to die, then the Russian for to knock.

You'll want to know: ремонтировать.
ремонтировать (remontírovat) — "to renovate, to repair".

The word, taken apart.
ремонтировать (remontírovat) is ремонт, "repair", made into a verb. ремонт comes from French remonte, "a fresh supply of horses" (Vasmer); under Peter the Great it meant supplying horses to regiments.

You'll want to know: переезжать.
переезжать (pereyezzhát) — "to move (house)".

The word, taken apart.
переезжать (pereyezzhát) is пере-, "across", with -езжать, from ездить, "to travel": to move from one home to another.
