Telugu, chapter 407: Protest and Reform.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
నిరసన (nirasana) — a protest.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for an allegation, then the Telugu for to allege.

You'll want to know: నిరసన.
నిరసన — nirasana — "a protest".
విద్యార్థులు కళాశాల ముందు నిరసన చేశారు. vidyārthulu kaḷāśāla mundu nirasana cēśāru. The students held a protest in front of the college.
Plural: నిరసనలు, nirasanalu.
నిరసన (nirasana) తెలుపు, to register a protest.

The word, taken apart.
నిరసన (nirasana) is borrowed from Sanskrit nirasana, "casting out, driving away, rejecting" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 2 of 5.
ఉద్యమం (udyamaṁ) — a movement; a campaign for change.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to allege, then the Telugu for a protest.

You'll want to know: ఉద్యమం.
ఉద్యమం — udyamaṁ — "a movement; a campaign for change".
ఆ గ్రామంలో నీళ్ళ కోసం ఒక ఉద్యమం మొదలైంది. ā grāmaṁlō nīḷḷa kōsaṁ oka udyamaṁ modalaindi. A movement for water began in that village.
Plural: ఉద్యమాలు, udyamālu.
A long campaign by many people. ఉద్యమకారుడు, an activist.

The word, taken apart.
ఉద్యమం (udyamaṁ) is borrowed from Sanskrit udyama, "the act of raising", and "undertaking, striving after, zeal" (CDIAL 2048).

Lesson 3 of 5.
ఖండించు (khaṇḍiñcu) — to condemn; to denounce.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a protest, then the Telugu for a movement.

You'll want to know: ఖండించు.
ఖండించు — khaṇḍiñcu — "to condemn; to denounce".
పెద్దలు ఆ పనిని గట్టిగా ఖండించారు. peddalu ā panini gaṭṭigā khaṇḍiñcāru. The elders strongly condemned that act.
Past (I …): ఖండించాను, khaṇḍiñcānu.
Also to refute: ఆ వాదనను ఖండించు (khaṇḍiñcu), to refute that argument.

The word, taken apart.
ఖండించు (khaṇḍiñcu) is built on Sanskrit khaṇḍana, "breaking" (CDIAL 3796), with the Telugu verb ending -ఇంచు.

Lesson 4 of 5.
డిమాండు (ḍimāṇḍu) — a claim; a demand.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a movement, then the Telugu for to condemn.

You'll want to know: డిమాండు.
డిమాండు — ḍimāṇḍu — "a claim; a demand".
ఉద్యోగుల డిమాండు ఎక్కువ జీతం. udyōgula ḍimāṇḍu ekkuva jītaṁ. The employees’ demand is higher pay.
Plural: డిమాండ్లు, ḍimāṇḍlu.
Like బస్సు, it ends in -ఉ. డిమాండు (ḍimāṇḍu) చేయు, to demand.

The word, taken apart.
డిమాండు (ḍimāṇḍu) is a loan from English demand, with the final -ఉ that English loans often take.

Lesson 5 of 5.
సంస్కరణ (saṁskaraṇa) — a reform.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to condemn, then the Telugu for a claim.

You'll want to know: సంస్కరణ.
సంస్కరణ — saṁskaraṇa — "a reform".
విద్యా రంగంలో కొత్త సంస్కరణలు వచ్చాయి. vidyā raṅgaṁlō kotta saṁskaraṇalu vaccāyi. New reforms have come in education.
Plural: సంస్కరణలు, saṁskaraṇalu.
Mostly in the plural, సంస్కరణలు.

The word, taken apart.
సంస్కరణ (saṁskaraṇa) is borrowed from Sanskrit saṁskaraṇa, "the act of putting together, preparing" (Monier-Williams); Telugu uses it for putting a system right.
