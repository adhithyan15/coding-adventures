Hindi, chapter 277: What Will Happen.
2 lessons. Both can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 2.
वह करेगा (vah karegā) — the future, for every person.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say "I will do", then "he will come", "they will come" and "you will come". (karūṅgā, āegā, āeṅge, āoge.) They are four cells of one grid. This lesson fills in the rest.

You'll want to know: the whole grid.
कल मैं बाज़ार जाऊँगा। kal maiṁ bāzār jāūṅgā. — "Tomorrow I will go to the market."
[a table of 6 rows, read aloud]
who: मैं. करना: करूँगा / करूँगी. जाना: जाऊँगा / जाऊँगी.
who: तुम. करना: करोगे / करोगी. जाना: जाओगे / जाओगी.
who: आप. करना: करेंगे / करेंगी. जाना: जाएँगे / जाएँगी.
who: वह. करना: करेगा / करेगी. जाना: जाएगा / जाएगी.
who: हम. करना: करेंगे. जाना: जाएँगे.
who: वे. करना: करेंगे / करेंगी. जाना: जाएँगे / जाएँगी.
After the stem comes a vowel for the person: ऊँ for I, ओ for तुम, ए for he or she, एँ for several or for आप. Then -गा, -गी or -गे, which follows the gender as था, थी, थे do. A man says करूँगा, a woman करूँगी.
लेना and देना shorten the stem: मैं लूँगा (maiṁ lūṅgā), वह लेगा (vah legā), हम लेंगे (ham leṅge); मैं दूँगा (maiṁ dūṅgā), वह देगा (vah degā). पीना gives वह पिएगा (vah piegā).
"Will not" puts नहीं in front: मैं नहीं जाऊँगा (maiṁ nahīṁ jāūṅgā).

You'll want to know: होगा, will be.
कल बारिश होगी। kal bāriś hogī. — "It will rain tomorrow."
है and था have a future too: होगा (hogā), होगी (hogī), होंगे (hoṅge). It follows its subject as था, थी, थे do. बारिश is feminine, so होगी.
वह घर पर होगा (vah ghar par hogā) — he will be at home.
कल ठंड होगी (kal ṭhaṇḍ hogī) — it will be cold tomorrow.
मेरे दोस्त वहाँ होंगे (mere dost vahā̃ hoṅge) — my friends will be there.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: "Tomorrow I will go to the market" — kal maiṁ bāzār jāūṅgā]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: "What will you do?", to a friend — tum kyā karoge?]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[question — say your answer, then pause 22 seconds]
Say that she will come tomorrow, and that it will be cold.

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
"He will do"? (करेगा.) "We will go"? (जाएँगे.) "I will take"? (लूँगा, or लूँगी.) "It will rain"? (बारिश होगी: होगी, because बारिश is feminine.)


Lesson 2 of 2.
Review — what will happen.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say "Yesterday I went to the market". (kal maiṁ bāzār gayā.) Now move it: "Tomorrow I will go to the market". (kal maiṁ bāzār jāūṅgā.)

Guided Practice.
[pause 2 seconds after each]
[your turn — say: Tomorrow I will go to the market, and then I will come home.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: Will you come?, to a friend. Yes, I will come.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: The train will come at five o'clock.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: It will be cold tomorrow, and it will rain.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: Arun and Mira will eat at home.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
"You will go", to a friend? (जाओगे, or जाओगी.) "He will give"? (देगा.) "It will be"? (होगा, or होगी for a feminine thing.) "The train will come"? (ट्रेन आएगी: ट्रेन is feminine.)
