## You'll want to know: でしょう
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C141-DESHOU]; assesses=[] -->

**でしょう** — *deshō* — "probably", "it will ..., I expect". It closes a
sentence about something you think will happen but cannot be sure of, the
way a weather forecast does.

> **あしたは すずしいでしょう。** — *ashita wa suzushii deshō* — "It will
> probably be cool tomorrow."

Now you can talk about what comes next: what will happen (**ます** with a
time word), what you mean to do (**つもり**), and what will probably
happen (**でしょう**).
