Malayalam, chapter 97: Beautiful, and Not.
4 lessons. All 4 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 4.
സുന്ദരം (sundaraṁ) — beautiful.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say this is easy. You built that sentence one chapter ago.

You'll want to know.
സുന്ദരം — sundaraṁ — beautiful.
ഇത് സുന്ദരമാണ്. — itŭ sundaramāṇŭ — "This is beautiful."

What you've built.
Read what you did there. The word ends in ം, so it welds onto ആണ് the way the last chapter's two words did, and you did it without being told.
[a table of 3 rows, read aloud]
ഇത് എളുപ്പമാണ്, this is easy.
ഇത് പ്രയാസമാണ്, this is difficult.
ഇത് സുന്ദരമാണ്, this is beautiful.
That is what a frame is for. The chapter before this one cost you a rule; this one cost you a word, and the weld came free.

Lesson 2 of 4.
ഭംഗി (bhaṁgi) — beauty.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say this is beautiful. Then say child — you have had that word a long time.

You'll want to know.
ഭംഗി — bhaṁgi — beauty.
ഇത് ഭംഗിയാണ്. — itŭ bhaṁgiyāṇŭ — "This is beautiful."
Read the gloss again. The word means beauty, but the sentence does not say this is beauty — it says this is beautiful, and it is the way you are most likely to hear it said.

The word, taken apart.
Two different things are happening here, and only one of them is new.
[a table of 2 rows, read aloud]
സുന്ദരം (sundaraṁ) becomes സുന്ദരമാണ്, the m was already there.
ഭംഗി (bhaṁgi) becomes ഭംഗിയാണ്, a യ is genuinely added.
In സുന്ദരമാണ് nothing is added at all. The ം is the sound m, and it stays; only its writing moves, because the bare sign cannot carry a vowel. You were told that last chapter.
ഭംഗി (bhaṁgi) is the other case, and you have met the reason before. It ends in a vowel, ആണ് begins with one, and Malayalam will not let two vowels sit together — so a യ slides in between them. The joining chapter said exactly that about അരിയും.
What is new is only where it happens. ഇത് എന്റെ കുട്ടിയാണ് — this is my child — has the same യ, for the same reason.

Lesson 3 of 4.
ഇത് സുന്ദരമല്ല (itŭ sundaramalla) — this is not beautiful.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say this is beautiful. Then say the word that denies being what you were called.

Grammar Lens: the denial welds too.
ഇത് സുന്ദരമല്ല. — itŭ sundaramalla — "This is not beautiful."
You already know how that was built. അല്ല is the negative of ആണ്, and it welds on the same way:
[a table of 2 rows, read aloud]
സുന്ദരം (sundaraṁ) + ആണ്, സുന്ദരമാണ് — this is beautiful.
സുന്ദരം (sundaraṁ) + അല്ല, സുന്ദരമല്ല — this is not beautiful.
Same മ, same reason: the m was always there and now has a vowel after it, so it is written with the full letter.
When you first met അല്ല it was written apart — ഞാൻ അധ്യാപകൻ അല്ല, I am not a teacher. That was a choice about writing, not a different grammar. Weld them and you get ഞാൻ അധ്യാപകനല്ല — ñān adhyāpakanalla — which is as ordinary as the other.
And look at what the ൻ does there. You were shown this at അധ്യാപകനെ: a chillu cannot carry a vowel, so when a vowel arrives behind it the full letter comes back — ൻ becomes ന. It happens here too: ഇത് എന്റെ മകനാണ്, this is my son.
A consonant does not. നിങ്ങൾക്ക് keeps its ൾ; the vowel is what forces the change.
And do not carry any of it across to the ം, which does different things for different endings, as you saw last chapter.

What you've built.
Both ends of the same line, from one new word and one you already had.
This is how the far end of most qualities will arrive for a while: not by a second word, but by denying the first one. It is what a learner can build today, and it is what a speaker often says anyway.

Lesson 4 of 4.
(beautiful, and not) (sundaravuṁ allāthatuṁ enna ōrmma) — from cold.

Warm-up.
[pause 3 seconds]
Close the lessons before this one. Say this is beautiful, then deny it.

Grammar Lens: two different events.
[a table of 4 rows, read aloud]
word: എളുപ്പം. joined to ആണ്: എളുപ്പമാണ്. what happened: the m was already there.
word: സുന്ദരം (sundaraṁ). joined to ആണ്: സുന്ദരമാണ്. what happened: the m was already there.
word: കുട്ടി. joined to ആണ്: കുട്ടിയാണ്. what happened: a യ was added.
word: ഭംഗി (bhaṁgi). joined to ആണ്: ഭംഗിയാണ്. what happened: a യ was added.
The two halves are not the same event. In the top pair nothing is added — the m was always in the word, and only its writing moves once a vowel sits behind it. In the bottom pair a യ genuinely appears, because the word ends in a vowel and two vowels will not sit together.
And what attaches matters too, not only how the word ended: the same സുന്ദരം (sundaraṁ) gives സുന്ദരവും when the and ending arrives.

What you've built.
ഇത് സുന്ദരമാണ്. — this is beautiful. ഇത് സുന്ദരമല്ല (itŭ sundaramalla). — this is not beautiful.
The second is not a second word. അല്ല is the negative of ആണ് and welds the same way — same മ, same reason.
So one new quality gave you both ends of a line. That is how the far end of most qualities will arrive for a while — by denying the near one, which is both what you can build today and what a speaker often says anyway.
