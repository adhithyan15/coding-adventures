# よく — how fully?

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[JA-LEX-SUMIMASEN, JA-SCRIPT-MI-01, JA-SCRIPT-ME-01] -->

[PAUSE 12s] Say *sumimasen*. Point to **み** and **め** inside it.

## You'll want to know
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-YOKU]; assesses=[] -->

> **よく** — *yo-ku* — well; fully

Two morae. *Yoku* is the adverbial partner of *yoi*, “good.” In the coming line,
it says that understanding did not happen **well** or **fully**. It does not
itself mean “understand.”

You already write **よ**. Treat **く** as visible support for now; its own tiny
writing lesson follows.
