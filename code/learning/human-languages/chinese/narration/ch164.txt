Mandarin Chinese, chapter 164: Getting Ready.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
dǎban — to dress up; to get dressed up.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for next time, then the Mandarin for all together.

You'll want to know: dǎban.
dǎban — "to dress up; to get dressed up".
tā zài dǎban wǒmen děng yíhuìr — She's getting ready; let's wait a moment.
Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
dǎban is built from 打 'to strike' and ban, both inherited from Middle Chinese, taengX and peanH (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second with this very word, 'to dress up'. Dǎ is not the sound taengX would regularly give, and in the word the second loses its tone.

Lesson 3 of 5.
chuīfēngjī — hair dryer.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for to dress up, then the Mandarin for to wash one's hair.

You'll want to know: chuīfēngjī.
chuīfēngjī — "hair dryer".
xǐtóu yǐhòu wǒ yòng chuīfēngjī — After washing my hair I use the hair dryer.
Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
chuīfēngjī is built from chuī 'to blow', fēng 'wind' and 机 'machine'. The last two are inherited from Middle Chinese, pjuwng and kj+j (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription); the first is listed there as tsyhwe, glossed 'to breathe out': the machine that blows wind.

Lesson 5 of 5.
guāliǎn — to have a shave; to shave one's face.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for a hair dryer, then the Mandarin for to put on make-up.

You'll want to know: guāliǎn.
guāliǎn — "to have a shave; to shave one's face".
bàba měitiān zǎoshang guāliǎn — Dad shaves every morning.
Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
guāliǎn is built from guā 'to scrape' and liǎn 'face'. The first is inherited from Middle Chinese kwaet (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'to scrape and pare'; the dictionary glosses the second, leamX, as the name of a kind of stew, so how it came to mean face is not settled.
