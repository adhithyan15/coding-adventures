## You'll want to know: ચૂસવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C474-CAVVU474-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ચૂસવું** — *cūsvũ* — "to suck".

> બાળક અંગૂઠો ચૂસે છે.
>
> *bāḷak angūṭho cūse che.*
>
> The baby is sucking its thumb.

Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે કેરી ચૂસી, *teṇe kerī cūsī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ચૂસવું** (*cūsvũ*) is inherited from Sanskrit *cūṣati*, "sucks"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb (CDIAL 4898).
