## You'll want to know: けむり
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C265-KEMURI265-01]; assesses=[] -->

**けむり** — *kemuri* — "smoke".

> **けむりで そらが くらく なりました。** — *kemuri de sora ga kuraku narimashita* — "The sky went dark with smoke."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**けむり** (*kemuri*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
