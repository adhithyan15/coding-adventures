Hindi, chapter 327: Ancestors.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
पूर्वज (pūrvaj) — an ancestor.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for an inscription (on stone), then the Hindi for remains.

You'll want to know: पूर्वज.
पूर्वज — pūrvaj — "an ancestor".
हमारे पूर्वज सौ साल पहले इस गाँव में आए। hamāre pūrvaj sau sāl pahle is gā̃v meṁ āe. Our ancestors came to this village a hundred years ago.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
पूर्वज (pūrvaj) is Sanskrit pūrvaja, "born before" (Monier-Williams), pūrva, earlier, and -ja, born: those who came before us.

Lesson 2 of 6.
बसना (basnā) — to settle; to make a home.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for remains, then the Hindi for an ancestor.

You'll want to know: बसना.
बसना — basnā — "to settle; to make a home".
वे नदी के किनारे बसे। ve nadī ke kināre base. They settled on the riverbank.
Past (it …): वह परिवार यहाँ बसा, vah parivār yahā̃ basā.

The word, taken apart.
बसना (basnā) goes back to Sanskrit vasati, "stays, dwells" (CDIAL 11435). A family बसता है in a place and stays; a town बसता है when people come to live there.

Lesson 4 of 6.
युग (yug) — an age; an era.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for to settle, then the Hindi for a family line.

You'll want to know: युग.
युग — yug — "an age; an era".
वह युग अब बीत गया। vah yug ab bīt gayā. That age has passed now.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
युग (yug) is Sanskrit yuga (Monier-Williams), a yoke and then an age of the world. Hindi uses it for any long period: आधुनिक युग, the modern age.

Lesson 5 of 6.
उजड़ना (ujaṛnā) — to be ruined; to become deserted.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a family line, then the Hindi for an age.

You'll want to know: उजड़ना.
उजड़ना — ujaṛnā — "to be ruined; to become deserted".
अकाल के बाद पूरा गाँव उजड़ गया। akāl ke bād pūrā gā̃v ujaṛ gayā. After the famine the whole village was deserted.
Past (it …): वह गाँव उजड़ गया, vah gā̃v ujaṛ gayā.

The word, taken apart.
उजड़ना (ujaṛnā) is inherited from ujjaṭati, "is uprooted", a form CDIAL reconstructs (CDIAL 1661). A place उजड़ता है when its people leave and it falls empty.
