Bengali, chapter 119: Sherbet, Halwa, Biscuit, Dhoti, Long shirt.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
শরবত (sôrbôt) — a sherbet.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for puffed rice, then the Bengali for coffee.

You'll want to know: শরবত.
শরবত — sôrbôt — "a sherbet".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: sôrbôt]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: sôrbôt, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say sôrbôt]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Bengali for puffed rice, then the Bengali for coffee, then say sôrbôt again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does শরবত (sôrbôt) mean? ("A sherbet".) Say it once more.
