## You'll want to know: umplanen
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C372-VEREINBARUNG-05]; assesses=[] -->

**umplanen** — "to change your plans".

> Wegen des Regens mussten wir umplanen.
>
> *Because of the rain we had to change our plans.*

Separable: *wir planen um*, *wir haben umgeplant*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*umplanen* is built from *um* "around, anew" and *planen* "to plan", from *Plan*, borrowed from French *plan* and going back to Latin *plānus* "flat" (EtymDB).
