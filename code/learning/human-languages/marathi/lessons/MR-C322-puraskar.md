## You'll want to know: पुरस्कार
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C322-PURASKAR322-01]; assesses=[] -->

**पुरस्कार** — *puraskār* — "an award".

> तिला तिच्या कामासाठी मोठा पुरस्कार मिळाला.
>
> *tilā ticyā kāmāsāṭhī moṭhā puraskār miḷālā.*
>
> She received a big award for her work.

Masculine.

पुरस्कार मिळणे, to receive an award, with the receiver in -ला: तिला. The letter ञ hides inside ज्ञ: ज्ञान, knowledge.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पुरस्कार** (*puraskār*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *puraskāra*, 'placing in front; honouring' (Monier-Williams).
