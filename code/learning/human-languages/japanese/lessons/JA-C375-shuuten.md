## You'll want to know: しゅうてん
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C375-SHUUDEN375-03]; assesses=[] -->

**しゅうてん** — *shūten* — "the terminus; the last stop".

> **この でんしゃの しゅうてんは みなとです。** — *kono densha no shūten wa minato desu* — "This train's last stop is the harbour."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**しゅうてん** (*shūten*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *shū*, "end" (Middle Chinese *tsyuwng*) and *ten*, "point" (*temX*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
