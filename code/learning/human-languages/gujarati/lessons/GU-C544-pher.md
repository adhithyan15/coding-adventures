## You'll want to know: ફેર
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C544-TAPHAVAT544-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ફેર** — *pher* — "a difference".

> જો તમે બંને ગીતો સાંભળશો તો તમે ફેર ઓળખી શકશો.
>
> *jo tame banne gīto sā̃bhaḷśo to tame pher oḷkhī śakśo.*
>
> If you listen to both songs, you'll be able to tell the difference.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ફેર** (*pher*) is filed by CDIAL under *\*phirati*, "turns", with a Gujarati *pher*, "a turn" (CDIAL 9078); how the word came to mean a difference is not explained there.
