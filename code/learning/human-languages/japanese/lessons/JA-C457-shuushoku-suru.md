## You'll want to know: しゅうしょくする
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C457-JOBHUNT457-01]; assesses=[] -->

**しゅうしょくする** — *shūshoku suru* — "to find a job".

> **あねは ぎんこうに しゅうしょくしました。** — *ane wa ginkō ni shūshoku shimashita* — "My elder sister got a job at a bank."

Polite **しゅうしょくします**, *shūshokushimasu*; a する verb. Past (polite): **あにが かいしゃに しゅうしょくしました。**, *ani ga kaisha ni shūshoku shimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**しゅうしょくする** (*shūshoku suru*) is Sino-Japanese (kango) *shūshoku* with the native verb *suru*, "to do": two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *shū*, "settle" (Middle Chinese *dzjuwH*) and *shoku*, "employment" (*tsyik*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
