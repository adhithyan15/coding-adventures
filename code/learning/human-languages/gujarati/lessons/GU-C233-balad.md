## You'll want to know: બળદ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C233-WORK233-04]; assesses=[] -->

**બળદ** — *baḷad* — "ox, bullock".

> **બળદ ખેતરમાં ગયો.** — *baḷad khetarmā̃ gayo* — "The ox went to the field."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**બળદ** (*baḷad*) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from *balivarda*, "ox, bull" (CDIAL 9176).
