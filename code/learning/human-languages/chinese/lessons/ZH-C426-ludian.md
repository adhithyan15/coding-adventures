## You'll want to know: 旅店
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C426-ROOM426-03]; assesses=[] -->

**旅店** — *lǚdiàn* — "inn; small hotel".

> **这家旅店很小** *zhè jiā lǚdiàn hěn xiǎo* — This inn is very small.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**旅店** (*lǚdiàn*) is built from 旅 'to travel' and 店 'an inn', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *ljoX* and *temH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription); the dictionary glosses the first as a body of troops, so the sense 'to travel' is not given there.
