## You'll want to know: l'enterrement
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C314-FUNERAILLES-03]; assesses=[] -->

**l'enterrement** — "the burial, the funeral".

> Beaucoup de gens sont venus à l'enterrement.
>
> *Many people came to the funeral.*

The everyday word for a funeral.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*enterrement* is built on *enterrer* "to bury", which is inherited from Vulgar Latin *interrāre*, built on *terra* "earth" (EtymDB).
