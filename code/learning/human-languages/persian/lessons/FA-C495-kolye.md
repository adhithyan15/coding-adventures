## You'll want to know: کلیه
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C495-ORGANS-04]; assesses=[] -->

**کلیه** (*kolye*) — "a kidney".

> آب زیاد برای کلیه خوب است.
>
> *âb-e ziyâd barâ-ye kolye khub ast.*
>
> Plenty of water is good for the kidneys.

Plural *kolyehâ*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*kolye* was borrowed from Arabic *kulya*, 'kidney' (EtymDB).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.
