## You'll want to know: le lecteur
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C521-AUTEURS-01]; assesses=[] -->

**le lecteur** — "reader; player (for discs)".

> Ce journal a beaucoup de lecteurs jeunes.
>
> *This newspaper has a lot of young readers.*

A woman is *la lectrice*. *Un lecteur de DVD* — a DVD player.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*le lecteur* is a learned borrowing from Latin *lēctor* "a reader" (EtymDB), from *legere* "to gather, to read" (Lewis & Short).
