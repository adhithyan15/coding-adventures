## You'll want to know: ಸಿದ್ಧಾಂತ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C407-SAMSHODHANE407-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸಿದ್ಧಾಂತ** — *siddhānta* — "a theory; a doctrine".

> ಈ ಸಿದ್ಧಾಂತ ಇನ್ನೂ ಸಾಬೀತಾಗಿಲ್ಲ.
>
> *ī siddhānta innū sābītāgilla.*
>
> This theory has not been proved yet.

ಸೈದ್ಧಾಂತಿಕ, theoretical.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸಿದ್ಧಾಂತ** (*siddhānta*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *siddhānta*, "an established end, a conclusion" (CDIAL 13404).
