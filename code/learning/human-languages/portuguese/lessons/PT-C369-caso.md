## You'll want to know: caso
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C369-CONDICAO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**caso** — "in case, if (+ subjunctive); the case".

> Caso chova, o jantar é dentro de casa.
>
> *If it rains, dinner is indoors.*

*Caso* + present subjunctive: *caso chova*. As a noun, *o caso* is the case: *neste caso*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*caso* comes from Latin *cāsus* "a falling, an event, a chance" (EtymDB, Lewis & Short), built on *cadere* "to fall": what befalls you.
