# Keep the clocked page; repair elsewhere

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-A1-TIMED-REPAIR-01] -->

Put the previous message's first attempt beside a new blank page. Keep the
first attempt unchanged. Close the older examples: there is no model or
copyable answer for this small repair.

## Guided Practice — one separate correction
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-A1-TIMED-REPAIR-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: controlled-composition -->

Inspect one thing at a time: named reader and purpose, sentence order,
Punjabi word choice and agreement, then Gurmukhi spelling, spaces, and
punctuation. Choose just one line you would improve. Write that corrected
line on the new page in Gurmukhi and name the one change you made. If the
first attempt has no error in that line, choose a line whose meaning could
be clearer; do not invent a mistake.

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-A1-TIMED-REPAIR-01] -->

The separate line helps the next attempt; it never replaces or raises the
score of the timed page. This return is not a complete A1 mock. The two
full papers, calibration, and human validation remain separate gates.
