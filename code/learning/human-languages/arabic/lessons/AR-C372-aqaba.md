## You'll want to know: عقبة
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C372-AQABA372-01]; assesses=[] -->

**عقبة** — *ʿaqaba* — "an obstacle".

> كانت اللغة أكبر عقبة أمامه.
>
> *kānat al-lugha akbar ʿaqaba amāmah.*
>
> Language was the biggest obstacle in his way.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**عقبة** (*ʿaqaba*) is built on the native Arabic root *ʿ-q-b*, "to come after, to follow" (Lane). *ʿAqaba* first named a steep mountain road; in modern use it is any obstacle.
