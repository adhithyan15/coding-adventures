## You'll want to know: ਗੁੱਤ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C231-DIN231-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ਗੁੱਤ** — *gutt* — "a plait of hair".

> ਕੁੜੀ ਦੀ ਗੁੱਤ ਲੰਮੀ ਹੈ।
>
> *kuṛī dī gutt lammī hai.*
>
> The girl's plait is long.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਗੁੱਤ** (*gutt*) comes from the same root as ਗੁੰਦਣਾ, Sanskrit *guphati*, "strings together": CDIAL lists Punjabi *gundṇā*, "to braid hair", under it, but has no Punjabi row for the noun; it lists only Lahnda *gutt*, "plait of hair hanging down woman's back" (CDIAL 4205).
