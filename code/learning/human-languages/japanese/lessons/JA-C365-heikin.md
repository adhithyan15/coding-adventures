## You'll want to know: へいきん
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C365-KEIZAI365-05]; assesses=[] -->

**へいきん** — *heikin* — "an average".

> **へいきんを けいさんしたら、おしえて ください。** — *heikin o keisan shitara, oshiete kudasai* — "When you have worked out the average, please tell me."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**へいきん** (*heikin*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *hei*, "even" (Middle Chinese *bjaeng*) and *kin*, "level" (*kjwin*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
