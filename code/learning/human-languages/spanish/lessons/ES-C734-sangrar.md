## You'll want to know: sangrar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C734-HERIDA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**sangrar** — "to bleed".

> Me sangra la nariz.
>
> *My nose is bleeding.*

Works like *doler*: *me sangra la rodilla*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*sangrar* is inherited from Latin *sanguināre* "to bleed" (EtymDB), built on *sanguis* "blood".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.
