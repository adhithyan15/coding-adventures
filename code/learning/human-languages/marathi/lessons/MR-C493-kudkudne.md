# कुडकुडणे (kuḍkuḍṇe) — to shiver with cold

## You'll want to know: कुडकुडणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C493-TOL493-04]; assesses=[] -->

**कुडकुडणे** — *kuḍkuḍṇe* — "to shiver with cold".

> थंडीत रस्त्यावरचा कुत्रा कुडकुडत होता.
>
> *thaṇḍīt rastyāvarcā kutrā kuḍkuḍat hotā.*
>
> The street dog was shivering in the cold.

Past (he …): तो कुडकुडला, *to kuḍkuḍlā*.

Of cold, where थरथरणे also covers fear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**कुडकुडणे** (*kuḍkuḍṇe*) is a Marathi verb built on a doubled syllable; its origin is not settled.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
