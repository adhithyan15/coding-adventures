## You'll want to know: صراف آلي
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C453-QIST453-03]; assesses=[] -->

**صراف آلي** — *ṣarrāf ālī* — "a cash machine".

> سحبت مالا من الصراف الآلي.
>
> *saḥabtu mālan min aṣ-ṣarrāf al-ālī.*
>
> I took money out of the cash machine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**صراف آلي** (*ṣarrāf ālī*) is a modern coinage built from two native Arabic words (Lane gives both roots): *ṣarrāf*, "a money changer", on *ṣ-r-f*, and *ālī*, "mechanical", from *āla*, "a machine", on *ʾ-w-l*.
