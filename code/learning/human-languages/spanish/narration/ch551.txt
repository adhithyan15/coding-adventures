6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
la acogida — the welcome, the reception.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for the widower, then the Spanish for the widow.

You'll want to know: la acogida.
la acogida — "the welcome, the reception".
La acogida en el pueblo fue muy buena. The welcome in the village was very good.
Un centro de acogida is a shelter for refugees or homeless people.

The word, taken apart.
acogida is built on acoger 'to take in, to welcome' (EtymDB), which is inherited from spoken Latin accolligere, built on colligere 'to gather together'.

What you've built.
One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.

Lesson 2 of 6.

You'll want to know: instalarse.
instalarse — "to settle in, to move in".
Nos instalamos en un piso pequeño. We settled into a small flat.
Without se: instalar is to install.

The word, taken apart.
instalarse was borrowed from French installer (EtymDB), from Medieval Latin installāre 'to put into a seat', built on stallum 'a stall, a seat', a Germanic word.

Lesson 4 of 6.
emigrar — to emigrate.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for to settle in, then the Spanish for to settle.

You'll want to know: emigrar.
emigrar — "to emigrate".
Mis abuelos emigraron a Alemania. My grandparents emigrated to Germany.
Emigrar is leaving your country; inmigrar is coming into a new one.

The word, taken apart.
emigrar is a learned borrowing from Latin ēmigrāre 'to move out, to depart' (EtymDB), built on ē 'out' and migrāre 'to move'.
