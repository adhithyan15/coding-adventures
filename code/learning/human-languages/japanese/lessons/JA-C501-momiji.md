## You'll want to know: もみじ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C501-MOON501-03]; assesses=[] -->

**もみじ** — *momiji* — "autumn leaves; a maple".

> **あきの やまは もみじが きれいです。** — *aki no yama wa momiji ga kirei desu* — "The autumn leaves on the mountains are beautiful."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**もみじ** (*momiji*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi, and its older origin is not settled (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
