## You'll want to know: ਗਾਗਰ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C250-GHR250-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ਗਾਗਰ** — *gāgar* — "a water pitcher".

> ਮੈਂ ਗਾਗਰ ਪਾਣੀ ਨਾਲ ਭਰਦੀ ਹਾਂ।
>
> *maiṁ gāgar pāṇī nāl bhardī hāṁ.*
>
> I fill the pitcher with water.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਗਾਗਰ** (*gāgar*) is inherited from Sanskrit *gargara*, "churn, waterpot"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *gāgar*, "waterpot" (CDIAL 4043).
