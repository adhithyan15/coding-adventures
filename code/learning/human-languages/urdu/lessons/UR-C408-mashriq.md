## You'll want to know: مشرق
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C408-SIMT408-04]; assesses=[] -->

**مشرق** — *mashriq* — "the east".

> سورج مشرق سے نکلتا ہے۔
>
> *sūraj mashriq se nikaltā hai.*
>
> The sun rises in the east.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**مشرق** (*mashriq*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *mashriq*, "the place where the sun rises".
