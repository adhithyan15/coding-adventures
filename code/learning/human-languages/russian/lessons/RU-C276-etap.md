## You'll want to know: этап
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C276-DESYATILETIYE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**этап** (*etáp*) — "a stage, a phase".

> Первый этап работы закончен.
>
> *Pérvyy etáp rabóty zakónchen.*
>
> The first stage of the work is finished.

Masculine. *На этом этапе* — at this stage.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*этап* was borrowed from French *étape* "a stage, a stopping place" (EtymDB), from Old French *estaple* "a warehouse, a market", itself from Middle Dutch *stapel* "a pillar, a market" (EtymDB).
