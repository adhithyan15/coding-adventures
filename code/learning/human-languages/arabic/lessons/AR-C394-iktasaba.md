## You'll want to know: اكتسب
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C394-ITQAN394-02]; assesses=[] -->

**اكتسب** — *iktasaba* — "to acquire".

> اكتسب الموظف خبرة كبيرة.
>
> *iktasaba al-muwaẓẓaf khibra kabīra.*
>
> The employee gained a lot of experience.

Present: يكتسب, *yaktasib*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**اكتسب** (*iktasaba*) is built on the native Arabic root *k-s-b*, "to gain, to earn" (Lane). It is Form VIII of *kasaba*, to earn: to acquire for oneself.
