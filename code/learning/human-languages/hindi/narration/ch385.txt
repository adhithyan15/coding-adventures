Hindi, chapter 385: If Things Go Wrong.
5 lessons. You can do the first 3 of them in the car; after that you will want to have stopped.


Lesson 1 of 5.
छितराना (chitrānā) — to scatter, to strew.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for to soften, then the Hindi for moderate.

You'll want to know: छितराना.
छितराना — chitrānā — "to scatter, to strew".
अगर तुम आँगन में चावल छितराओगे तो चिड़ियाँ आएँगी। agar tum āṁgan meṁ cāval chitrāoge to ciṛiyā̃ āeṅgī. If you scatter rice in the courtyard, the birds will come.
Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने बीज छितराए, usne bīj chitrāe.

The word, taken apart.
छितराना (chitrānā) is inherited from chitr, "scatter, sprinkle", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 5040).

अगर बारिश होगी तो दीवार का रंग मिट जाएगा। agar bāriś hogī to dīvār kā raṅg miṭ jāegā. If it rains, the colour on the wall will fade.

The word, taken apart.

Lesson 3 of 5.
लताड़ना (latāṛnā) — to tell off, to scold harshly.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for to strew, then the Hindi for to be wiped out.

You'll want to know: लताड़ना.
लताड़ना — latāṛnā — "to tell off, to scold harshly".
अगर तुमने फिर कमरा गंदा किया तो माँ तुम्हें लताड़ेगी। agar tumne phir kamrā gandā kiyā to māṁ tumhẽ latāṛegī. If you make the room dirty again, your mother will tell you off.
Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने मुझे लताड़ा, usne mujhe latāṛā.

The word, taken apart.
लताड़ना (latāṛnā) is inherited from lattā, "a kick", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 10931). CDIAL glosses the Hindi verb "to spurn"; telling someone off, the everyday sense, is not among its glosses.
