## You'll want to know: نیت
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C547-TASAVVUR547-02]; assesses=[] -->

**نیت** — *niyyat* — "an intention (in one's heart)".

> اگر نیت اچھی ہے تو راستہ مل جائے گا۔
>
> *agar niyyat achchhī hai to rāstā mil jāe gā.*
>
> If your heart is in the right place, you'll find a way.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**نیت** (*niyyat*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *niyya*, "an intention".
