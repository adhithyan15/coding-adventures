## You'll want to know: جھولنا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C321-MELA321-01]; assesses=[] -->

**جھولنا** — *jhūlnā* — "to swing".

> بچی رسی پر جھول رہی تھی۔
>
> *bachchī rassī par jhūl rahī thī.*
>
> The little girl was swinging on a rope.

Past (she …): وہ جھولی, *voh jhūlī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**جھولنا** (*jhūlnā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *jhūlnā*, "to swing", under a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 5406).
