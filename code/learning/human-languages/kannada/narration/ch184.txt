(ಅಕಸ್ಮಾತ್, akasmāt.) If you want? (ಬೇಕಾದರೆ, bēkādare.) In case? (ಯಾವುದಕ್ಕೂ, yāvudakkū.) By the way? (ಅಂದಹಾಗೆ, andahāge.) Fresh? (ತಾಜಾ, tājā.) Stale? (ಹಳಸಿದ, haḷasida.) Raw? (ಹಸಿ, hasi.) Bland? (ಸಪ್ಪೆ, sappe.) Expensive? (ದುಬಾರಿ, dubāri.)
