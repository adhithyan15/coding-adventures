# Practice — the past you actually say

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

[PAUSE 2s] One tense, built from two things you already had. Run it, then take it
apart again.

## Guided Practice: build it
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[FR-GRAM-PASSE-COMPOSE-02, FR-GRAM-PARTICIPE-ER-01] -->

[PAUSE 1s]
- [YOU SAY: "j'ai parlé, tu as parlé, il a parlé, nous avons parlé, vous avez parlé, ils ont parlé"]
- [YOU SAY: with your other verbs — "j'ai travaillé, nous avons habité"]
- [YOU SAY: what moves and what does not — "avoir changes; the participle never does"]

[REPEAT x2] "j'ai parlé, tu as parlé, il a parlé"

## Guided Practice: the three endings

- [YOU SAY: the Latin — "-ātum, -ītum, -ūtum"; and English "finite, acute, statute"]

## Guided Practice: the sound trap and the fossil
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[FR-PHON-PARLER-PARLE-PARLEZ-03, FR-GRAM-ACCORD-PARTICIPE-07, FR-GRAM-PASSE-COMPOSE-ORIGINE-06] -->

[PAUSE 1s]
- [YOU SAY: the three that sound alike — "parler, parlé, parlez"]
- [YOU SAY: the old meaning — "I have letters, written ones"]
- [YOU SAY: the fossil — "Les lettres que j'ai écrites"]

## What you've built so far
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[FR-GRAM-PASSE-COMPOSE-02, FR-GRAM-PASSE-COMPOSE-ORIGINE-06] -->

- **a past tense**, made of a verb you already knew and one unchanging word.
- **three participle endings**, one per verb class, two inherited and one spread
  by analogy.
- **an explanation for the *have***: the construction began as plain possession —
  *I have letters, written ones* — and hardened into a tense.
- **a fossil you can point at**: the agreement in *écrites* is an adjective
  ending that has outlived the grammar that required it.

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[FR-GRAM-PASSE-COMPOSE-02, FR-GRAM-PARTICIPE-IR-RE-04, FR-PHON-PARLER-PARLE-PARLEZ-03, FR-GRAM-ACCORD-PARTICIPE-07] -->

[PAUSE 3s] Say "we spoke." (**Nous avons parlé**.) Which half changes with the
person? (***avoir***.) Give the three participle vowels. (**-é, -i, -u**.) Which
three forms of *parler* sound identical? (***parler, parlé, parlez***.) When does
the participle agree? (When the **object comes first**.) Next: the other past
tense — the one you will read and never say.
