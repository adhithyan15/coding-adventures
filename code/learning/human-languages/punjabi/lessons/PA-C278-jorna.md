## You'll want to know: ਜੋੜਨਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C278-STOCK278-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ਜੋੜਨਾ** — *joṛnā* — "to add up; to join".

> ਮੈਂ ਕਾਗ਼ਜ਼ ਉੱਤੇ ਸਭ ਪੈਸੇ ਜੋੜਦਾ ਹਾਂ।
>
> *maiṁ kāghaz utte sabh paise joṛdā hāṁ.*
>
> I add up all the money on paper.

Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਹਿਸਾਬ ਜੋੜਿਆ, *maiṁ hisāb joṛiā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਜੋੜਨਾ** (*joṛnā*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*yuṭati*, "is joined"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *joṛṇā*, "to join", under it (CDIAL 10496).
