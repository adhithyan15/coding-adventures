## You'll want to know: renverser
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C298-CHOC-01]; assesses=[] -->

**renverser** — "to knock over, to run over; to spill".

> Une voiture a renversé un enfant à vélo.
>
> *A car knocked over a child on a bike.*

Also to spill: *renverser du café*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*renverser* is built on Old French *enverser* "to turn upside down", from *envers* "reverse", which is inherited from Latin *inversus*, from *vertere* "to turn" (Lewis & Short).
