## You'll want to know: সারাংশ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C328-PRISHTHA328-04]; assesses=[] -->

**সারাংশ** — *sārāngshô* — "a summary".

> ক্লাসে আমরা গল্পটার সারাংশ লিখলাম।
>
> *klāse āmrā gôlpoṭār sārāngshô likhlām.*
>
> In class we wrote a summary of the story.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**সারাংশ** (*sārāngshô*) joins Sanskrit *sāra*, 'the core, the essence', to *aṃśa*, 'a share, a part' (Monier-Williams): the part that holds the core.
