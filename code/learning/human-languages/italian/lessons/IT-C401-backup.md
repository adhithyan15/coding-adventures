## You'll want to know: il backup
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C401-FILE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**il backup** — "the backup (copy)".

> Se fai un backup ogni settimana, non perdi niente.
>
> *If you make a backup every week, you will not lose anything.*

Masculine, invariable. Also *la copia di sicurezza*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*backup* was borrowed from English *backup* (EtymDB), built from *back* and *up*: something kept in support.
