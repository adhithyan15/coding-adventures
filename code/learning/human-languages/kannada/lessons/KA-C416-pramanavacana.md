# ಪ್ರಮಾಣವಚನ (pramāṇavacana) — an oath of office

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[KA-LEX-C416-PRATIJNE416-03, KA-LEX-C416-PRATIJNE416-04] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Kannada for a reward, then the Kannada for to ask formally.

## You'll want to know: ಪ್ರಮಾಣವಚನ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C416-PRATIJNE416-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ಪ್ರಮಾಣವಚನ** — *pramāṇavacana* — "an oath of office".

> ಹೊಸ ಮಂತ್ರಿಗಳು ಪ್ರಮಾಣವಚನ ತೆಗೆದುಕೊಂಡರು.
>
> *hosa mantrigaḷu pramāṇavacana tegedukoṇḍaru.*
>
> The new ministers took the oath of office.

ಪ್ರಮಾಣ (proof, oath) + ವಚನ (word).

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಪ್ರಮಾಣವಚನ** (*pramāṇavacana*) is built from Sanskrit *pramāṇa*, "a standard, a proof" (CDIAL 8723), and *vacana*, "speech, a word" (CDIAL 11199).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
