## You'll want to know: स्पर्धते
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C385-SPARDHA385-01]; assesses=[] -->

**स्पर्धते** — *spardhate* — "competes".

> यदि मीरा स्पर्धते, तर्हि सा प्रथमा भविष्यति।
>
> *yadi mīrā spardhate, tarhi sā prathamā bhaviṣyati.*
>
> If Mira competes, she will come first.

Past (she …): सा स्पर्धितवती, *sā spardhitavatī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**स्पर्धते** (*spardhate*) comes from the root √spardh, "to contend, to vie"; MW gives *spardhita* "contending or competing together" (MW).

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
