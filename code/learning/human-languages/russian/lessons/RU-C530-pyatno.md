## You'll want to know: пятно
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C530-LAUNDRY-03]; assesses=[] -->

**пятно** (*pyatnó*) — "a stain, a spot".

> На рубашке осталось пятно от кофе.
>
> *Na rubáshke ostálos pyatnó ot kófe.*
>
> There's a coffee stain left on the shirt.

Neuter; plural пятна.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

EtymDB lists *пятно* without a source, and the origin is not settled.
