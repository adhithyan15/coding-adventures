## You'll want to know: ઝાપટું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C434-SHOWER434-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ઝાપટું** — *jhāpṭũ* — "a sharp shower (of rain)".

> બપોરે વરસાદનું એક ઝાપટું આવ્યું.
>
> *bapore varsādnũ ek jhāpṭũ āvyũ.*
>
> In the afternoon there was a sharp shower.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ઝાપટું** (*jhāpṭũ*) goes back to *\*jhapp*, "a sudden movement", a form CDIAL reconstructs; CDIAL lists the Gujarati word, "a sharp shower of rain" (CDIAL 5336).
