Arabic, chapter 63: To throw, To fly, To swim, To make, To enter.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
رمى (ramā) — to throw (he threw).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to wear, then the Arabic for to leave.

You'll want to know: رمى.
رمى — ramā — "to throw (he threw)".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: ramā]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: ramā, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say ramā]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Arabic for to wear, then the Arabic for to leave, then say ramā again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does رمى (ramā) mean? ("To throw (he threw)".) Say it once more.
