# निःश्वासः (niḥśvāsaḥ) — a sigh

## You'll want to know: निःश्वासः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C450-VISADA450-05]; assesses=[] -->

**निःश्वासः** — *niḥśvāsaḥ* — "a sigh".

> पिता श्रान्तः, सः निःश्वासं मुञ्चति।
>
> *pitā śrāntaḥ, saḥ niḥśvāsaṃ muñcati.*
>
> Father is tired; he lets out a sigh.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**निःश्वासः** (*niḥśvāsaḥ*) is built from *niḥ*, "out", and the root √śvas, "to breathe", the root of **श्वासः**: a breath let out (MW).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
