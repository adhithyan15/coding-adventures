## You'll want to know: die Krise
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C277-STREIK-05]; assesses=[] -->

**die Krise** — "the crisis".

> Das Land war in einer tiefen Krise.
>
> *The country was in a deep crisis.*

Plural: *die Krisen*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Krise* was borrowed through Latin *crisis* from Greek *krisis* "a decision, the turning point of an illness"; EtymDB lists no parent, so this follows the usual account.
