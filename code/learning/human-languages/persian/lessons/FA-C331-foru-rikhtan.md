## You'll want to know: فرو ریختن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C331-VIRAN-02]; assesses=[] -->

**فرو ریختن** (*foru rikhtan*) — "to collapse, to cave in".

> سقف مدرسه قدیمی فرو ریخت.
>
> *Saqf-e madrese-ye qadimi foru rikht.*
>
> The roof of the old school collapsed.

*foru* "down" + *rikhtan* "to pour". Present stem *riz-*: *foru mirizad*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*foru* "down" is native Persian, and *rikhtan* "to pour" is inherited from Middle Persian (EtymDB): to pour down.

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
