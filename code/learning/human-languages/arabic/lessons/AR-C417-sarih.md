## You'll want to know: صريح
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C417-WUDUH417-02]; assesses=[] -->

**صريح** — *ṣarīḥ* — "frank".

> كان صديقي صريحا معي.
>
> *kāna ṣadīqī ṣarīḥan maʿī.*
>
> My friend was frank with me.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**صريح** (*ṣarīḥ*) is built on the native Arabic root *ṣ-r-ḥ*, "pure, unmixed, clear" (Lane). *Ṣarīḥ* is what is pure and unmixed: plain, frank.
