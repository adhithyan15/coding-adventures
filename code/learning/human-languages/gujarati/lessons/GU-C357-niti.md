## You'll want to know: નીતિ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C357-MULYA357-03]; assesses=[] -->

**નીતિ** — *nīti* — "a policy".

> સરકારની નવી નીતિ નાના વેપારીઓ માટે સારી છે.
>
> *sarkārnī navī nīti nānā vepārīo māṭe sārī che.*
>
> The government's new policy is good for small traders.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**નીતિ** (*nīti*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *nīti*, "leading, guidance, conduct" (Monier-Williams).
