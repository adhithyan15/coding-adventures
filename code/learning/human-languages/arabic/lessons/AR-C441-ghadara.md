## You'll want to know: غادر
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C441-ISTIQBAL441-04]; assesses=[] -->

**غادر** — *ghādara* — "to depart from; to leave (a place)".

> غادرنا الفندق في الصباح.
>
> *ghādarnā al-funduq fī aṣ-ṣabāḥ.*
>
> We left the hotel in the morning.

Present: يغادر, *yughādir*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**غادر** (*ghādara*) is built on the native Arabic root *gh-d-r*, "to leave behind" (Lane). It is Form III; the plain verb *ghadara* means to betray.
