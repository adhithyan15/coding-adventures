## You'll want to know: لوڈو
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C534-BISAT534-05]; assesses=[] -->

**لوڈو** — *luḍo* — "ludo (a board game)".

> اگر بارش ہوئی تو ہم گھر میں لوڈو کھیلیں گے۔
>
> *agar bārish huī to ham ghar meṅ luḍo kheleṅ ge.*
>
> If it rains, we'll play ludo at home.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**لوڈو** (*luḍo*) is borrowed from English *ludo*.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
