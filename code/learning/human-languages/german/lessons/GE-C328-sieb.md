## You'll want to know: das Sieb
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C328-SIEB-01]; assesses=[] -->

**das Sieb** — "the sieve, the colander".

> Er goss die Nudeln in ein Sieb.
>
> *He poured the pasta into a colander.*

Plural: *die Siebe*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Sieb* is inherited from Old High German *sib*, from Proto-Germanic *sibi* "sieve" (EtymDB), the same source as English *sieve*.
