# How are you, honestly — a second pass

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[TE-LEX-C84-UNWELL-01, TE-LEX-C84-UNWELL-02, TE-LEX-C84-UNWELL-03] -->

[PAUSE 3s] You are not well today. Say that you have a fever, that your head aches, and that
you are tired.

## Grammar Lens: two ways a feeling reaches you
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[TE-LEX-C84-UNWELL-01, TE-LEX-C84-UNWELL-03, TE-LEX-C85-FEEL-01, TE-LEX-C85-FEEL-03, TE-LEX-C85-FEEL-04, TE-LEX-C85-FEEL-05] -->

| it comes to you | it is so, to you |
|---|---|
| **నాకు జ్వరం వచ్చింది** | **నాకు అలసటగా ఉంది** |
| **నాకు కోపం వచ్చింది** | **నాకు భయంగా ఉంది** |

Both keep the person in the dative **నాకు**. One says the thing arrived; the other says how it
is with you now. **చలి** and **బాధ** take the right-hand frame too.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[TE-LEX-C84-UNWELL-01, TE-LEX-C84-UNWELL-02, TE-LEX-C84-UNWELL-03, TE-LEX-C84-UNWELL-04, TE-LEX-C84-UNWELL-05, TE-LEX-C85-FEEL-01, TE-LEX-C85-FEEL-02, TE-LEX-C85-FEEL-03, TE-LEX-C85-FEEL-04, TE-LEX-C85-FEEL-05, TE-LEX-C87-ANSWER-04, TE-LEX-C87-ANSWER-05] -->

[PAUSE 2s each]
- [YOU SAY: you are thirsty; ask for water]
- [YOU SAY: it is cold today; then, it is very hot today]
- [YOU SAY: tell a friend with a stomach ache to rest]
- [YOU SAY: a friend says they are sad; react with "really?", then "I see"]

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[TE-LEX-C84-UNWELL-01, TE-LEX-C84-UNWELL-02, TE-LEX-C84-UNWELL-03, TE-LEX-C84-UNWELL-04, TE-LEX-C84-UNWELL-05, TE-LEX-C85-FEEL-01, TE-LEX-C85-FEEL-02, TE-LEX-C85-FEEL-03, TE-LEX-C85-FEEL-04, TE-LEX-C85-FEEL-05, TE-LEX-C87-ANSWER-04, TE-LEX-C87-ANSWER-05] -->

[PAUSE 3s] A fever? (**జ్వరం**.) A pain? (**నొప్పి**.) Tiredness? (**అలసట**.) Thirst?
(**దాహం**.) Rest? (**విశ్రాంతి**.) Cold, and heat? (**చలి**, **వేడి**.) Fear, anger, sorrow?
(**భయం**, **కోపం**, **బాధ**.) Really? (**నిజంగా**.) I see? (**అలాగా**.)
