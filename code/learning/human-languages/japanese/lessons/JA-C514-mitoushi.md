## You'll want to know: みとおし
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C514-GUESS514-05]; assesses=[] -->

**みとおし** — *mitōshi* — "an outlook; a forecast".

> **みとおしは てんきに よります。** — *mitōshi wa tenki ni yorimasu* — "The outlook depends on the weather."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**みとおし** (*mitōshi*) is a native Japanese compound (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese: built from *mi*, "seeing" and *tōshi*, "passing through" (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
