## You'll want to know: કેડી
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C404-PATH404-02]; assesses=[] -->

**કેડી** — *keḍī* — "a footpath (across fields); a trail".

> ખેતરની વચ્ચે એક નાની કેડી છે.
>
> *khetarnī vacce ek nānī keḍī che.*
>
> There is a little trail through the middle of the field.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**કેડી** (*keḍī*) No dictionary we use records an older form, so its history is not settled.
