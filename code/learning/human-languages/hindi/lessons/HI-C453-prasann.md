## You'll want to know: प्रसन्न
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C453-ANAND453-05]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रसन्न** — *prasann* — "pleased; cheerful".

> अरुण का काम देखकर उसके पिता प्रसन्न हुए।
>
> *Aruṇ kā kām dekhkar uske pitā prasann hue.*
>
> Arun's father was pleased when he saw his work.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रसन्न** (*prasann*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *prasanna*, "clear, bright" and also "soothed, pleased" (Monier-Williams). It is a more formal word than *khuś*.
