## You'll want to know: だいじ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C369-JUUYOU369-02]; assesses=[] -->

**だいじ** — *daiji* — "important; precious".

> **けがを したら、やすむ ことが だいじでしょう。** — *kega o shitara, yasumu koto ga daiji deshō* — "If you get hurt, resting will be the important thing."

A na-adjective: な before a noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**だいじ** (*daiji*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *dai*, "large" (Middle Chinese *dajH*) and *ji*, "matter" (*dzriH*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
