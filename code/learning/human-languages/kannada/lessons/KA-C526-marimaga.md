# ಮರಿಮಗ (marimaga) — a great-grandson

## You'll want to know: ಮರಿಮಗ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C526-BALAGA526-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ಮರಿಮಗ** — *marimaga* — "a great-grandson".

> ಅಜ್ಜಿ ತಮ್ಮ ಮರಿಮಗನನ್ನು ಎತ್ತಿಕೊಂಡು ನಕ್ಕರು.
>
> *ajji tamma marimaganannu ettikoṇḍu nakkaru.*
>
> Grandmother picked up her great-grandson and laughed.

ಮರಿ, a young one + ಮಗ, son. A great-granddaughter is ಮರಿಮಗಳು.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಮರಿಮಗ** (*marimaga*) is a Kannada compound built from *mari*, "a young one", and *maga*, "son, male child" (DEDR 4616).
