## You'll want to know: دليل
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C308-TAHQIQ308-04]; assesses=[] -->

**دليل** — *dalīl* — "evidence, proof; a guide".

> وجد المحقق دليلا في الغرفة.
>
> *wajada al-muḥaqqiq dalīlan fī al-ghurfa.*
>
> The investigator found a piece of evidence in the room.

Plural: أدلة, *adilla*.

Also a guidebook or a guide: دليل سياحي.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**دليل** (*dalīl*) is built on the native Arabic root *d-l-l* (Lane), "to direct, to show the way". A *dalīl* is what points the way to a place, or to the truth.
