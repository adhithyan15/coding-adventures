## You'll want to know: すいへいせん
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C382-KAIGAN382-04]; assesses=[] -->

**すいへいせん** — *suiheisen* — "the horizon (over the sea)".

> **すいへいせんに ゆうひが しずみました。** — *suiheisen ni yūhi ga shizumimashita* — "The evening sun sank below the horizon."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**すいへいせん** (*suiheisen*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): three Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *sui*, "water" (Middle Chinese *sywijX*), *hei*, "flat" (*bjaeng*) and *sen*, "line" (*sjenH*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
