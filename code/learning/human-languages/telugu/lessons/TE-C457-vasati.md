## You'll want to know: వసతి
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C457-VASATI457-01]; assesses=[] -->

**వసతి** — *vasati* — "lodging; accommodation".

> పండుగ రోజుల్లో హోటళ్ళలో వసతి దొరకలేదు.
>
> *paṇḍuga rōjullō hōṭaḷḷalō vasati dorakalēdu.*
>
> In the festival days we could not find lodging in the hotels.

Also a hostel for students: విద్యార్థి వసతి గృహం.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**వసతి** (*vasati*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *vasati*, "staying, dwelling", from *vas*, "to stay" (CDIAL 11435).
