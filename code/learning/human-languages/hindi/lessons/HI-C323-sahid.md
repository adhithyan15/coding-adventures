## You'll want to know: शहीद
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C323-VIJAY323-04]; assesses=[] -->

**शहीद** — *śahīd* — "a martyr".

> युद्ध में सौ सिपाही शहीद हुए।
>
> *yuddh meṁ sau sipāhī śahīd hue.*
>
> A hundred soldiers fell in the war.

Masculine or feminine, by the person meant.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**शहीद** (*śahīd*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *shahīd*, "a witness" and then one who dies for a cause. **शहीद होना** is to fall for one's country.
