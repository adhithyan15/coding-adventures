## You'll want to know: प्रसूते
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C492-JANMA492-02]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रसूते** — *prasūte* — "gives birth".

> धेनुः वत्सं प्रसूते।
>
> *dhenuḥ vatsaṃ prasūte.*
>
> The cow gives birth to a calf.

Past (she …): सा पुत्रं प्रसूतवती, *sā putraṃ prasūtavatī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रसूते** (*prasūte*) is built from *pra* and the root √sū, "to beget, to bear"; MW gives "to procreate, bring forth" (MW).
