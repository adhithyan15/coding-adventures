## You'll want to know: رکاوٹ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C368-HAL368-02]; assesses=[] -->

**رکاوٹ** — *rukāvaṭ* — "an obstacle".

> اگر رکاوٹ آئی تو ہم دوسرا راستہ لیں گے۔
>
> *agar rukāvaṭ āī to ham dūsrā rāstā leṅ ge.*
>
> If something blocks us, we will take another route.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**رکاوٹ** (*rukāvaṭ*) is built on **رکنا**, "to stop", which CDIAL lists beside *roknā* under *\*rokk*, "stop", a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 10827); *-āvaṭ* makes the noun: what stops you.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
