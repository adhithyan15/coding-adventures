## You'll want to know: ಮೂರನೆಯ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C74-ORDINAL-03]; assesses=[KA-LEX-C07-MUURU-01, KA-GRAMMAR-C74-ORDINAL-02] -->

**ಮೂರನೆಯ** (*mūraneya*) — "**third**".

You have already met the machine that made it. **ಮೂರು** loses its final **-ು**
and takes **-ಅನೆಯ**:

| cardinal | ordinal |
|---|---|
| **ಎರಡು** (*eraḍu*) | **ಎರಡನೆಯ** (*eraḍaneya*) |
| **ಮೂರು** (*mūru*) | **ಮೂರನೆಯ** (*mūraneya*) |

**ಮೂರನೆಯ ದಿನ** (*mūraneya dina*), the third day. **ಮೂರನೆಯ ಮರ** (*mūraneya mara*),
the third tree.

This is the second word and it needed nothing new. That is what an exceptionless
ending buys you: from here on, a number you can say is a position you can say.
