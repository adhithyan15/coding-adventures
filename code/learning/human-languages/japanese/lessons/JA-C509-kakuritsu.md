## You'll want to know: かくりつ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C509-LOTTERY509-04]; assesses=[] -->

**かくりつ** — *kakuritsu* — "probability; the odds".

> **しあいは あめの かくりつに よります。** — *shiai wa ame no kakuritsu ni yorimasu* — "The match depends on the chance of rain."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**かくりつ** (*kakuritsu*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *kaku*, "assurance" (Middle Chinese *khaewk*) and *ritsu*, "ratio" (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
