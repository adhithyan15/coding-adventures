## You'll want to know: ઝૂરવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C381-PASTAVO381-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ઝૂરવું** — *jhūrvũ* — "to long for someone; to pine".

> જો દીકરો શહેરમાં રહેવા જશે તો માતા તેના માટે ઝૂરશે.
>
> *jo dīkro śahermā̃ rahevā jaśe to mātā tenā māṭe jhūrśe.*
>
> If her son goes to live in the city, the mother will pine for him.

Past (he …): તે ઘર માટે ઘણું ઝૂર્યો, *te ghar māṭe ghaṇũ jhūryo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ઝૂરવું** (*jhūrvũ*) CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb, "to languish", under a reconstructed *jhūrati*, "wastes away" (CDIAL 5409).
