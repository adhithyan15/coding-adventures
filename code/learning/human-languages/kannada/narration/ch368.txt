Lesson 1 of 6.
ಕ್ರಮೇಣ (kramēṇa) — gradually.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to destroy, then the Kannada for to disappear.

You'll want to know: ಕ್ರಮೇಣ.
ಕ್ರಮೇಣ — kramēṇa — "gradually".
ಕ್ರಮೇಣ ಹಳ್ಳಿ ಒಂದು ಪಟ್ಟಣವಾಯಿತು. kramēṇa haḷḷi ondu paṭṭaṇavāyitu. Gradually the village became a town.
A formal adverb; in talk people say ನಿಧಾನವಾಗಿ or ಸ್ವಲ್ಪ ಸ್ವಲ್ಪವಾಗಿ.

The word, taken apart.
ಕ್ರಮೇಣ (kramēṇa) is borrowed from Sanskrit krameṇa, "in regular course, gradually, by degrees" (Monier-Williams), a case form of krama, "a step, an order".

ಯುದ್ಧದ ತರುವಾಯ ದೇಶ ನಿಧಾನವಾಗಿ ಬೆಳೆಯಿತು. yuddhada taruvāya dēśa nidhānavāgi beḷeyitu. After the war the country grew slowly.
A written partner of ನಂತರ: ಊಟದ ತರುವಾಯ (taruvāya), after the meal.

You'll want to know: ಸರಣಿ.
ಸರಣಿ — saraṇi — "a series; a string".
ಆ ತಿಂಗಳು ಊರಿನಲ್ಲಿ ಕಳ್ಳತನಗಳ ಸರಣಿ ನಡೆಯಿತು. ā tiṅgaḷu ūrinalli kaḷḷatanagaḷa saraṇi naḍeyitu. That month there was a series of thefts in the town.
Plural: ಸರಣಿಗಳು, saraṇigaḷu.

The word, taken apart.
ಸರಣಿ (saraṇi) is borrowed from Sanskrit saraṇi, "a road, a path, a straight or continuous line" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 4 of 6.
ಘಟ್ಟ (ghaṭṭa) — a turning point; a stage.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for thereafter, then the Kannada for a series.

You'll want to know: ಘಟ್ಟ.
ಘಟ್ಟ — ghaṭṭa — "a turning point; a stage".
ಆ ಪರೀಕ್ಷೆ ಅವಳ ಜೀವನದ ಮುಖ್ಯ ಘಟ್ಟ. ā parīkṣe avaḷa jīvanada mukhya ghaṭṭa. That exam is a turning point in her life.
Also a hill slope or a mountain pass: ಘಟ್ಟದ ದಾರಿ, a road over the hills.

The word, taken apart.
ಘಟ್ಟ (ghaṭṭa) matches Sanskrit ghaṭṭa, "a landing place, a quay, a ferry" (CDIAL 4414). CDIAL and DEDR 1147 both compare it with Dravidian words for a bank or a dam, such as Kannada gaṭṭu, so its first source is not settled; "a slope" and "a stage" are later senses.

Lesson 5 of 6.
ಜರುಗು (jarugu) — to take place; to shift.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a series, then the Kannada for a turning point.

You'll want to know: ಜರುಗು.
ಜರುಗು — jarugu — "to take place; to shift".
ಸಭೆ ಶಾಂತವಾಗಿ ಜರುಗಿತು. sabhe śāntavāgi jarugitu. The meeting took place peacefully.
Past (it …): ಜರುಗಿತು, jarugitu.
Events take place: ಸಮಾರಂಭ ಜರುಗಿತು. It also means to move aside, as ಸರಿ does.

The word, taken apart.
ಜರುಗು (jarugu) is native Dravidian: DEDR 2360 lists Kannada jarugu beside Tamil cari, "to slip away, to slide, to give way".
