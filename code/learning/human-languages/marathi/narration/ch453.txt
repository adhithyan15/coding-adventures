Marathi, chapter 453: Losing Your Temper.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
तापट (tāpaṭ) — hot-tempered.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to panic, then the Marathi for to be stunned.

You'll want to know: तापट.
तापट — tāpaṭ — "hot-tempered".
आमचे मामा थोडे तापट आहेत. āmce māmā thoḍe tāpaṭ āhet. Our uncle is a bit hot-tempered.
Unchanging. Built on ताप, heat or fever: a temper that heats up fast. Close to रागीट.

The word, taken apart.
तापट (tāpaṭ) is a Marathi derivation of ताप, "heat, fever", which goes back to Sanskrit tāpa, "heat, glow" (CDIAL 5767), with the ending -aṭ.

Lesson 2 of 5.
संतापणे (santāpṇe) — to be enraged; to fly into a rage.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to be stunned, then the Marathi for hot-tempered.

You'll want to know: संतापणे.
संतापणे — santāpṇe — "to be enraged; to fly into a rage".
खोटं ऐकून वडील संतापले. khoṭaṁ aikūn vaḍīl santāple. Father flew into a rage when he heard the lie.
Past (he …): तो संतापला, to santāplā.
Stronger than रागावणे. The noun संताप, rage: त्याला संताप आला.

The word, taken apart.
संतापणे (santāpṇe) is a Marathi verb built on संताप, "rage", a tatsama from Sanskrit saṁtāpa, "burning heat, affliction" (CDIAL 12885).

Lesson 3 of 5.
चिडणे (ciḍṇe) — to get irritated.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for hot-tempered, then the Marathi for to be enraged.

You'll want to know: चिडणे.
चिडणे — ciḍṇe — "to get irritated".
भाऊ खूप प्रश्न विचारतो, म्हणून ती चिडते. bhāū khūp praśna vicārto, mhaṇūn tī ciḍte. Her brother asks a lot of questions, so she gets irritated.
Past (he …): तो चिडला, to ciḍlā.
The person one is cross with takes -वर: ती माझ्यावर चिडली. चिडवणे, to tease.

The word, taken apart.
चिडणे (ciḍṇe) is a tadbhava, inherited from the reconstructed root ciḍ, "be angry"; CDIAL lists Marathi ciḍṇẽ, "to be provoked" (CDIAL 4794).

थकलेला दुकानदार ग्राहकावर खेकसला. thaklelā dukāndār grāhkāvar khekaslā. The tired shopkeeper snapped at a customer.

The word, taken apart.

कुरकुरणे — kurkurṇe — "to grumble".
जेवण थंड होतं म्हणून आजोबा कुरकुरले. jevaṇ thaṇḍ hotaṁ mhaṇūn ājobā kurkurle. Grandfather grumbled because the food was cold.

कुरकुर, grumbling: कुरकुर करणे, to keep grumbling.

The word, taken apart.
कुरकुरणे (kurkurṇe) is a Marathi verb built by doubling a syllable, कुर-कुर; its origin is not settled.
