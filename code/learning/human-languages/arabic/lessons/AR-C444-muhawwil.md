## You'll want to know: محول
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C444-KAHRABA444-04]; assesses=[] -->

**محول** — *muḥawwil* — "an adapter; a transformer".

> احتجت إلى محول لشاحن الهاتف.
>
> *iḥtajtu ilā muḥawwil li-shāḥin al-hātif.*
>
> I needed an adapter for the phone charger.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**محول** (*muḥawwil*) is built on the native Arabic root *ḥ-w-l*, "to change, to shift" (Lane). It is the Form II participle, "a thing that converts".
