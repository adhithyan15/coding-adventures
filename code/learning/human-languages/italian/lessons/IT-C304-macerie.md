## You'll want to know: le macerie
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C304-TERREMOTO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**le macerie** — "the rubble, the debris".

> Sotto le macerie c'è ancora qualcuno.
>
> *There is still someone under the rubble.*

Mostly plural.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*macerie* is a learned borrowing from Latin *māceria* "a garden wall" (Lewis & Short): first a dry-stone wall, later its fallen stones.
