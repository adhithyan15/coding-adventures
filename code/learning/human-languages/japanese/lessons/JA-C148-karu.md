## You'll want to know: かる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C148-HAIR148-02]; assesses=[] -->

**かる** — *karu* — "to trim; to mow".

> **ちちは にわの くさを かります。** — *chichi wa niwa no kusa o karimasu* — "My father mows the grass in the garden."

Polite **かります**, *karimasu*; a godan verb.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**かる** (*karu*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi, and its older origin is not settled (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
