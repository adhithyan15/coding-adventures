Arabic, chapter 436: Around the station.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
مرآب (mirʾāb) — a garage; a car park.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a pedestrian crossing, then the Arabic for to turn around.

You'll want to know: مرآب.
مرآب — mirʾāb — "a garage; a car park".
ترك أبي السيارة في المرآب. taraka abī as-sayyāra fī al-mirʾāb. My father left the car in the garage.

The word, taken apart.
مرآب (mirʾāb) is a modern coinage, usually traced to the native root r-ʾ-b, "to repair, to mend" (Lane), in the tool-and-place pattern mifʿāl: a place where cars are kept and seen to.

Lesson 3 of 5.
شباك (shubbāk) — a ticket window; a window.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a garage, then the Arabic for a kiosk.

You'll want to know: شباك.
شباك — shubbāk — "a ticket window; a window".
اشترى أبي التذاكر من الشباك. ishtarā abī at-tadhākir min ash-shubbāk. My father bought the tickets at the ticket window.
Plural: شبابيك, shabābīk.

The word, taken apart.
شباك (shubbāk) is built on the native Arabic root sh-b-k, "to interlace, to entwine" (Lane). A shubbāk was first a latticed window.

Lesson 5 of 5.
تنقل (tanaqqala) — to travel about; to move from place to place.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a ticket window, then the Arabic for a porter.

You'll want to know: تنقل.
تنقل — tanaqqala — "to travel about; to move from place to place".
تنقلنا بين المدن بالقطار. tanaqqalnā bayna al-mudun bi-l-qiṭār. We travelled between the cities by train.
Present: يتنقل, yatanaqqal.

The word, taken apart.
تنقل (tanaqqala) is built on the native Arabic root n-q-l, "to move from place to place" (Lane). It is Form V, "to keep moving on".
