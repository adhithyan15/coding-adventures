# ◌ि (i kī mātrā) — the short i sign in शुक्रिया

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[HI-SCRIPT-DA-RETROFLEX-01, HI-SCRIPT-RECOG-140] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: write ड and ़ once each, and name them.

[PAUSE 2s] Now say **शुक्रिया** (*shukriyā*), "thanks".
You have read it for a long time. This lesson takes one letter out of it and puts
it in your hand.

## Script you'll notice: ◌ि
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-SCRIPT-C128-I-SIGN-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ि** — the short *i* sign.

It is inside **शुक्रिया** (*shukriyā*), "thanks", one of the first words in this
book. Look at **क्रि**: the sign stands **before** the consonants and is said
**after** them, *kri*. It is the one Hindi vowel sign written ahead of the sound it
follows.

## Writing: ◌ि
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[HI-SCRIPT-C128-I-SIGN-01] -->

Put your pen on ◌ि and follow its line. Copy the shape you can see — slowly,
and larger than it is printed.

> This book does not yet tell you **where to start this letter or which way to
> travel**. It is not written down here until it can be written down with a
> source. Copying what is in front of you needs no such source, and it is how the
> shape gets into your hand in the meantime.
