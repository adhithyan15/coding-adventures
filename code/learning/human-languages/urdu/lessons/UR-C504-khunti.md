## You'll want to know: کھونٹی
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C504-SVICH504-04]; assesses=[] -->

**کھونٹی** — *khūṅṭī* — "a peg (on a wall)".

> اس کا تھیلا کھونٹی پر ہے۔
>
> *us kā thailā khūṅṭī par hai.*
>
> Her bag is hanging on the peg.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**کھونٹی** (*khūṅṭī*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *khū̃ṭī*, "a small peg", under *\*khuṭṭa*, a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 3893).
