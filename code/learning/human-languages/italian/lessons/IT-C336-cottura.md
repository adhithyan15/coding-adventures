## You'll want to know: la cottura
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C336-FORNO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**la cottura** — "the cooking; the cooking time".

> La cottura dura un'ora.
>
> *The cooking takes an hour.*

*Il tempo di cottura* — cooking time; *a metà cottura* — halfway through.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*cottura* is inherited from Latin *coctūra* "a cooking" (EtymDB), from *coquere* "to cook".
