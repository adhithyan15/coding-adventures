## You'll want to know: सन्तरति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C415-SROTAS415-03]; assesses=[] -->

**सन्तरति** — *santarati* — "swims across; crosses over".

> बालः नदीं सन्तरति।
>
> *bālaḥ nadīṃ santarati.*
>
> The boy swims across the river.

Past (he …): सः नदीं सन्तीर्णवान्, *saḥ nadīṃ santīrṇavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सन्तरति** (*santarati*) is built from *sam* and the root √tṝ, "to cross", which also gives *tarati*, "swims, crosses"; MW gives *saṃ-tṝ* as to cross or pass through, with the participle *saṃtīrṇa* (MW).
