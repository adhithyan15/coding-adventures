You'll want to know: দাদু.
দাদু — dādu — "a grandfather".

The word, taken apart.
দাদু (dādu) is a pet form of dādā, "grandfather", which CDIAL lists under dādda, "father or other elderly relative", a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 6261).

Lesson 2 of 5.
দিদিমা (didimā) — a grandmother (mother's mother).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a daughter; a girl, then the Bengali for a grandfather.

You'll want to know: দিদিমা.
দিদিমা — didimā — "a grandmother (mother's mother)".

The word, taken apart.
দিদিমা (didimā) is didi + mā. CDIAL lists didi, "elder sister", and notes that Bengali also uses it for "grandmother", under diddā, "elder sister", a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 6327); mā, "mother", is Sanskrit mātṛ (CDIAL 10016).

Lesson 3 of 5.
কাকা (kākā) — an uncle (father's brother).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a grandfather, then the Bengali for a grandmother.

You'll want to know: কাকা.
কাকা — kākā — "an uncle (father's brother)".

The word, taken apart.
কাকা (kākā) is a tadbhava, a word inherited and reshaped by sound change. It comes from kākka, "senior male relative", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 2998). CDIAL takes it from Dravidian, comparing Kannada kakka, "uncle".

You'll want to know: মাসি.
মাসি — māshi — "an aunt (mother's sister)".

The word, taken apart.
মাসি (māshi) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from mātuḥṣvasṛ, "mother's sister" (CDIAL 10001). It is mātṛ, "mother", + svasṛ, "sister".
