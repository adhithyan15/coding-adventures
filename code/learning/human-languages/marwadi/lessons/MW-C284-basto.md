## You'll want to know: बस्तो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C284-BASTO284-01]; assesses=[] -->

**बस्तो** — *basto* — "a school bag".

> म्हारो बस्तो भारी है।
>
> *mhāro basto bhārī hai.*
>
> My school bag is heavy.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**बस्तो** (*basto*) is usually traced to Persian *basta*, "a bundle"; Marwadi gives it the -ो ending of a masculine noun.
