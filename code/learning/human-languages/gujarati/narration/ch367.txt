Gujarati, chapter 367: Probably.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
સંભવતઃ (sambhavataḥ) — probably.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a conjecture, then the Gujarati for to want.

You'll want to know: સંભવતઃ.
સંભવતઃ — sambhavataḥ — "probably".
જો ટ્રેન સમયસર હશે તો અમે સંભવતઃ સાંજે પહોંચીશું. jo ṭren samaysar haśe to ame sambhavataḥ sā̃je pahõcīśũ. If the train is on time, we will probably arrive by evening.

The word, taken apart.
સંભવતઃ (sambhavataḥ) is built from Sanskrit sambhava, "coming about, possibility" (Monier-Williams), and the adverb ending -taḥ: by way of what is possible.

Lesson 2 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to want, then the Gujarati for probably.

You'll want to know: અચૂક.
અચૂક — acūk — "without fail".
જો તમે આ દવા લેશો તો તાવ અચૂક મટશે. jo tame ā davā leśo to tāv acūk maṭśe. If you take this medicine, the fever will go without fail.

The word, taken apart.
અચૂક (acūk) is built from a-, "not", and cūk, "a slip, a miss", from a reconstructed cukk, "to fall short" (CDIAL 4848): with no miss.

Lesson 5 of 5.

You'll want to know: આંકવું.
આંકવું — ā̃kvũ — "to assess".
જો તમે ઘરની કિંમત ઓછી આંકશો તો ખોટ જશે. jo tame gharnī kimmat ochī ā̃kśo to khoṭ jaśe. If you value the house too low, you will make a loss.
Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે ઘરની કિંમત આંકી, teṇe gharnī kimmat ā̃kī.

The word, taken apart.
આંકવું (ā̃kvũ) is inherited from Sanskrit aṅkáyati, "marks"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb, "to mark, to appraise" (CDIAL 104).
