Punjabi, chapter 113: Umbrella, Bowl, Glass, Cooking pot, Comb.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ਛਤਰੀ (chatrī) — an umbrella.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for to win, then the Punjabi for to lose.

You'll want to know: ਛਤਰੀ.
ਛਤਰੀ — chatrī — "an umbrella".

The word, taken apart.
ਛਤਰੀ (chatrī) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from chattra, "parasol" (CDIAL 4972).

Lesson 2 of 5.
ਕੌਲੀ (kaulī) — a bowl.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for to lose, then the Punjabi for an umbrella.

You'll want to know: ਕੌਲੀ.
ਕੌਲੀ — kaulī — "a bowl".

The word, taken apart.
ਕੌਲੀ (kaulī) is inherited. CDIAL lists it, "little brass bowl", under kapalla, a form beside Sanskrit kapāla, "skull, cranium; cup, jar, bowl" (CDIAL 2744). CDIAL thinks the variant forms show at least some Austroasiatic influence.

Lesson 4 of 5.
ਪਤੀਲਾ (patīlā) — a cooking pot.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for a bowl, then the Punjabi for a glass.
[pause 1 second]
Say "I work." (Maiṁ kamm kardā hāṁ: the noun kamm, "work," plus karnā, "to do.")

You'll want to know: ਪਤੀਲਾ.
ਪਤੀਲਾ — patīlā — "a cooking pot".

The word, taken apart.
ਪਤੀਲਾ (patīlā) matches Hindi patīlā, "copper pot", which CDIAL lists under Sanskrit pātra, "drinking vessel, dish" (CDIAL 8055). CDIAL gives no Punjabi patīlā.

ਕੰਘੀ (kanghī) is inherited. CDIAL lists it under kaṅkaśa, a variant of Sanskrit kaṅkata, "comb", a word of doubtful Indo-European origin, and says Hindi kaṅghī was taken from Punjabi (CDIAL 2598).
