## You'll want to know: しゃない
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C379-SHARYOU379-04]; assesses=[] -->

**しゃない** — *shanai* — "inside the train".

> **しゃないで でんわを しないで ください。** — *shanai de denwa o shinaide kudasai* — "Please do not make phone calls on the train."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**しゃない** (*shanai*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *sha*, "car" (Middle Chinese *tsyhae*) and *nai*, "inside" (*nwojH*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
