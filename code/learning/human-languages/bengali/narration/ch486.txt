Bengali, chapter 486: Raising Children.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
লালনপালন (lālônpālôn) — upbringing; raising (a child).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a baby, then the Bengali for a cradle.

You'll want to know: লালনপালন.
লালনপালন — lālônpālôn — "upbringing; raising (a child)".
বাবার মৃত্যুর পরে মামা অরুণের লালনপালন করলেন। bābār mrityur pôre māmā ôruner lālônpālôn kôrlen. After his father's death, Arun's uncle brought him up.

The word, taken apart.
লালনপালন (lālônpālôn) pairs Sanskrit lālana, "caressing, fondling", with pālana, "guarding, nourishing" (Monier-Williams); পালা (taught), to rear, is from the second.

Lesson 2 of 5.
প্রশ্রয় দেওয়া (prôshrôy deoyā) — to indulge; to spoil (a child).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a cradle, then the Bengali for upbringing.

You'll want to know: প্রশ্রয় দেওয়া.
প্রশ্রয় দেওয়া — prôshrôy deoyā — "to indulge; to spoil (a child)".
দিদিমা সবসময় নাতিকে প্রশ্রয় দেন। didimā sôbsômôy nātike prôshrôy den. Grandmother always indulges her grandson.
Past (he/she …): তিনি নাতনিকে প্রশ্রয় দিলেন, tini nātnike prôshrôy dilen.

The word, taken apart.
প্রশ্রয় দেওয়া (prôshrôy deoyā) is Sanskrit praśraya, "leaning on, a resting place", and also affection (Monier-Williams); Bengali uses it for indulgence, a sense the dictionary does not give, with deoyā, "to give" (CDIAL 6141): to give a child something to lean on.

Lesson 3 of 5.
চোষা (choshā) — to suck.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for upbringing, then the Bengali for to spoil (a child).

You'll want to know: চোষা.
চোষা — choshā — "to suck".
বাচ্চাটা বোতল থেকে দুধ চুষে খাচ্ছে। bāchchāṭā botôl theke dudh chushe khāchchhe. The baby is sucking milk from its bottle.
Past (he/she …): সেই শিশু বোতল থেকে দুধ চুষল, sei shishu botôl theke dudh chushlô.
The stem vowel rises in the verb forms: চুষি, I suck; চুষল, sucked.

The word, taken apart.
চোষা (choshā) comes down from Sanskrit cūṣati, "sucks" (CDIAL 4898, which lists Bengali cusā, "to suck"); the forms চুষি and চুষছে keep the old u.

Lesson 4 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for to spoil (a child), then the Bengali for to suck.

You'll want to know: ঘুম পাড়ানো.
ঘুম পাড়ানো — ghum pāṛāno — "to put to sleep (a child)".
বাবা গান গেয়ে মেয়েকে ঘুম পাড়ালেন। bābā gān geye meyeke ghum pāṛālen. Father sang his daughter to sleep.
Past (he/she …): তিনি শিশুকে ঘুম পাড়ালেন, tini shishuke ghum pāṛālen.

The word, taken apart.
ঘুম পাড়ানো (ghum pāṛāno) is ঘুম, sleep, with pāṛāno, "to lay down", from the verb CDIAL lists for Bengali as felling and laying down, under Sanskrit pātayati (CDIAL 8053).

আমার ভাইঝি এখন হামাগুড়ি দেয়। āmār bhāijhi ekhôn hāmāguṛi dey. My niece crawls now.
