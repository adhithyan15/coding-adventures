## You'll want to know: पारखणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C376-MULYAMAPAN376-02]; assesses=[] -->

**पारखणे** — *pārakhṇe* — "to assess; to judge the worth of".

> माणसं पारखायला वेळ लागतो.
>
> *māṇsaṁ pārkhāylā veḷ lāgto.*
>
> It takes time to judge people.

Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने पारखलं, *tyāne pārakhlaṁ*.

पारख, the eye of a judge: त्याला सोन्याची पारख आहे.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पारखणे** (*pārakhṇe*) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Marathi *pārakhṇẽ*, "to examine", under Sanskrit *parīkṣate*, "observes, examines" (CDIAL 7904); परीक्षा, an exam, is its learned cousin.
