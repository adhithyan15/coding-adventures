Arabic, chapter 441: At reception.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
استقبال (istiqbāl) — reception; a welcome.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to accompany, then the Arabic for to give a lift.

You'll want to know: استقبال.
استقبال — istiqbāl — "reception; a welcome".
أعطاني موظف الاستقبال المفتاح. aʿṭānī muwaẓẓaf al-istiqbāl al-miftāḥ. The receptionist gave me the key.

The word, taken apart.
استقبال (istiqbāl) is built on the native Arabic root q-b-l, "to be in front of; to accept" (Lane). It is the verbal noun of Form X, "going out to meet".

Lesson 2 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to give a lift, then the Arabic for reception.

You'll want to know: نزيل.
نزيل — nazīl — "a hotel guest; a lodger".
طلب النزيل غرفة بعيدة عن الشارع. ṭalaba an-nazīl ghurfa baʿīda ʿan ash-shāriʿ. The guest asked for a room far from the street.
Plural: نزلاء, nuzalāʾ.

The word, taken apart.
نزيل (nazīl) is built on the native Arabic root n-z-l, "to alight, to stop at a place" (Lane). A nazīl is one who has stopped somewhere for the night.
