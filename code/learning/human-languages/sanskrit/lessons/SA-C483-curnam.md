## You'll want to know: चूर्णम्
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C483-GUTIKA483-02]; assesses=[] -->

**चूर्णम्** — *cūrṇam* — "a powder".

> वैद्यः उदरपीडायै चूर्णं ददाति।
>
> *vaidyaḥ udarapīḍāyai cūrṇaṃ dadāti.*
>
> The physician gives a powder for the stomach ache.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**चूर्णम्** (*cūrṇam*) is traced to the root √carv, "to grind with the teeth", by MW, which gives "powder, flour" (MW).
