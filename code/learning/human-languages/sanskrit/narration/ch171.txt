Lesson 1 of 5.
सन्देशः (sandeśaḥ) — a message.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a livelihood, then the Sanskrit for service.

You'll want to know: सन्देशः.
सन्देशः — sandeśaḥ — "a message".

The word, taken apart.
sandeśa comes from sam-√diś "point out, direct, send word", the root √diś "point, show" with sam "together" (MW): "a communication, a message, an errand".

Lesson 4 of 5.
कार्यक्रमः (kāryakramaḥ) — a programme, an event.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a convenience, then the Sanskrit for an arrangement.

You'll want to know: कार्यक्रमः.
कार्यक्रमः — kāryakramaḥ — "a programme, an event".

The word, taken apart.
kāryakrama is built from kārya "work to be done", from √kṛ "do", and krama "a step, an order", from √kram "stride" (MW): "the order of things to be done". The pairing for a programme or event is modern.

You'll want to know: प्रतियोगिता.
प्रतियोगिता — pratiyogitā — "a competition".

The word, taken apart.
pratiyogitā is built from pratiyogin "counterpart, rival", from prati-√yuj "fasten against", with the abstract suffix -tā "-ness" (MW). MW gives it as "correlation" in philosophy; "competition" is a modern use.
