## You'll want to know: 出入
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C405-GATES405-03]; assesses=[] -->

**出入** — *chūrù* — "to go in and out".

> **旅客从这个门出入** *lǚkè cóng zhège mén chūrù* — Passengers go in and out through this gate.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**出入** (*chūrù*) is built from 出 'to go out' and 入 'to go in', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *tsyhwit* and *nyip* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription).
