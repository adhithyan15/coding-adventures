(**当年**, *dāngnián*.) In one's early years? (**早年**, *zǎonián*.) In one's later years? (**晚年**, *wǎnnián*.) All one's life? (**一生**, *yìshēng*.) To look back fondly on? (*huáiniàn*.) A teenage boy?
