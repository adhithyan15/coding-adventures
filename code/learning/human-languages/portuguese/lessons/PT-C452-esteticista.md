## You'll want to know: a esteticista
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C452-CUIDADOS-03]; assesses=[] -->

**a esteticista** — "the beautician".

> A esteticista trabalha no salão ao lado.
>
> *The beautician works in the salon next door.*

Built on *a estética*, beauty care.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*esteticista* is built on *estética*, a learned borrowing from Greek *aisthētikós* 'of the senses', on *aisthánesthai* 'to perceive' (EtymDB).
