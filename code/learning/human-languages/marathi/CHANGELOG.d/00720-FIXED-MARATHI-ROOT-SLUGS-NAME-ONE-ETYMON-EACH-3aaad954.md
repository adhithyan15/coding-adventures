## Fixed — Marathi root slugs name one etymon each

- **MR-C76-bal** moves from `sanskrit-bala` to `sanskrit-bala-child`, the slug Gujarati, Kannada and Sanskrit use. *Bāḷ* is *bālá* "young, boy" (CDIAL 9216); `sanskrit-bala` is *bála* "strength" (CDIAL 9161).
- **MR-C70-pay** and **MR-C241-payvat** move from `sanskrit-pada` to `sanskrit-pada-foot`. *Pāy* is *pā́da* "foot" (CDIAL 8056); `sanskrit-pada` is *padá* "footstep" (CDIAL 7747).
- **MR-C103-nava** moves from `sanskrit-nava` to `sanskrit-nava-new` (*náva¹*, CDIAL 6983). Bare `sanskrit-nava` was also used for *náva²* "nine".
- **MR-C55-nau** no longer says nine is "the same word as new". Its gloss and block said *nava* "nine" also meant "new" and that nine is "the new number". CDIAL gives *náva²* "nine" (6984) and *náva¹* "new" (6983) separate entries, and English *nine* and *new* are different words too. The block now says the two only sound alike.
