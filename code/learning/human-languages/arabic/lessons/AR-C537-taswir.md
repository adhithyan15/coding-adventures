## You'll want to know: تصوير
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C537-HIWAYA537-03]; assesses=[] -->

**تصوير** — *taṣwīr* — "photography".

> هواية أختي التصوير.
>
> *hiwāyat ukhtī at-taṣwīr.*
>
> My sister's hobby is photography.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**تصوير** (*taṣwīr*) is built on the native Arabic root *ṣ-w-r*, "to form, to shape" (Lane). Ṣūra, a picture, and ṣawwara, to photograph, which you know, share the root.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
