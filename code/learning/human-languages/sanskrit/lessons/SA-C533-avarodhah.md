## You'll want to know: अवरोधः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C533-ANTARAYA533-02]; assesses=[] -->

**अवरोधः** — *avarodhaḥ* — "an obstruction".

> यदि नद्याम् अवरोधः भवति, तर्हि जलं न प्रवहति।
>
> *yadi nadyām avarodhaḥ bhavati, tarhi jalaṃ na pravahati.*
>
> If the river is blocked, the water does not flow.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अवरोधः** (*avarodhaḥ*) is built on *ava*, "down", and the root √rudh, "to obstruct"; MW gives *avarodha* as hindrance, obstruction (MW).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
