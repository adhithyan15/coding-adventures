# સળગવું (saḷagvũ) — to catch fire, to burn

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-LEX-C221-DROUGHT221-02, GU-LEX-C221-DROUGHT221-03] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to fall off, then the Gujarati for fire.

## You'll want to know: સળગવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C221-DROUGHT221-04]; assesses=[] -->

**સળગવું** — *saḷagvũ* — "to catch fire, to burn".

> **સૂકું ઘાસ તરત સળગશે.** — *sūkũ ghās tarat saḷagśe* — "Dry grass will catch fire at once."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**સળગવું** (*saḷagvũ*) is a tadbhava. CDIAL lists it, glossed "to take fire", under Sanskrit *saṁlagna*, "closely attached", so the fire is what takes hold (CDIAL 12999).
