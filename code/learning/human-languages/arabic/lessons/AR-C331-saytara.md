## You'll want to know: سيطر
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C331-IMBARATUR331-02]; assesses=[] -->

**سيطر** — *sayṭara* — "to control, to take control".

> سيطر رجال الإطفاء على الحريق.
>
> *sayṭara rijāl al-iṭfāʾ ʿalā al-ḥarīq.*
>
> The firefighters brought the fire under control.

Present: يسيطر, *yusayṭir*.

It takes على: سيطر على.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**سيطر** (*sayṭara*) is filed under the root *s-ṭ-r* (Lane), the root of *saṭr*, a line, with *muṣayṭir*, an overseer. Its earlier history, perhaps through Aramaic, is not settled.
