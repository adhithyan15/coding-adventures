## You'll want to know: पवनः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C439-PAVANA439-01]; assesses=[] -->

**पवनः** — *pavanaḥ* — "a breeze".

> सायं शीतलः पवनः वहति।
>
> *sāyaṃ śītalaḥ pavanaḥ vahati.*
>
> In the evening a cool breeze blows.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पवनः** (*pavanaḥ*) comes from the root √pū, "to purify": MW gives *pavana*, "the purifier", as wind, breeze, air; it lives on as Punjabi *pauṇ*, "wind" (MW; CDIAL 7978).
