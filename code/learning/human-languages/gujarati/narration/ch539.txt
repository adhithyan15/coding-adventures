Gujarati, chapter 539: Making Up My Mind.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
મક્કમ (makkam) — resolute.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to mull over, then the Gujarati for a settlement.

You'll want to know: મક્કમ.
મક્કમ — makkam — "resolute".
જો તમે મક્કમ રહેશો તો તમે જીતશો. jo tame makkam raheśo to tame jītśo. If you stay firm, you will win.

The word, taken apart.
મક્કમ (makkam) is usually traced to Arabic muḥkam, "made firm, strong", through Persian.

Lesson 2 of 5.
ઠરાવવું (ṭharāvvũ) — to settle on (a decision).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a settlement, then the Gujarati for resolute.

You'll want to know: ઠરાવવું.
ઠરાવવું — ṭharāvvũ — "to settle on (a decision)".
અમે ઠરાવ્યું છે કે જો વરસાદ પડશે તો અમે નહીં જઈએ. ame ṭharāvyũ che ke jo varsād paḍśe to ame nahī̃ jaīe. We have decided that if it rains we won't go.
Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે ઠરાવ્યું, teṇe ṭharāvyũ.

The word, taken apart.
ઠરાવવું (ṭharāvvũ) is the causative of ઠરવું, "to settle, to become fixed", which CDIAL lists under stharati, "is firm" (CDIAL 13742): to make a decision firm.

Lesson 3 of 5.
કસોટી (kasoṭī) — a touchstone; a hard test.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for resolute, then the Gujarati for to settle on.

You'll want to know: કસોટી.
કસોટી — kasoṭī — "a touchstone; a hard test".
જો તમે આ કસોટીમાં મક્કમ રહેશો તો તમને આ નોકરી મળશે. jo tame ā kasoṭīmā̃ makkam raheśo to tamne ā nokrī maḷśe. If you stand firm through this test, you will get this job.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
કસોટી (kasoṭī) is first the touchstone, the dark stone on which gold is rubbed to show its worth, and so any hard test. CDIAL lists the Gujarati word, "a touchstone", under Sanskrit kaṣapaṭṭikā (CDIAL 2973); the sense of a trial it does not give.

Lesson 4 of 5.
ઝંપલાવવું (jhamplāvvũ) — to take the plunge.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to settle on, then the Gujarati for a hard test.

You'll want to know: ઝંપલાવવું.
ઝંપલાવવું — jhamplāvvũ — "to take the plunge".
જો હું ત્યારે ધંધામાં ઝંપલાવત તો આજે હું અમીર હોત. jo hũ tyāre dhandhāmā̃ jhamplāvat to āje hũ amīr hot. If I had taken the plunge into business then, I would be rich today.
Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે ધંધામાં ઝંપલાવ્યું, teṇe dhandhāmā̃ jhamplāvyũ.

The word, taken apart.
ઝંપલાવવું (jhamplāvvũ) is perhaps related to a reconstructed jhapp, "sudden movement", with a variant jhamp-, "a jump", under which CDIAL lists a Gujarati verb, "to throw headlong" (CDIAL 5336); this form is not listed, so its history is not settled. One plunges into water, or into a venture.

Lesson 5 of 5.
નિશ્ચય (niścay) — resolve (a firm decision).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a hard test, then the Gujarati for to take the plunge.

You'll want to know: નિશ્ચય.
નિશ્ચય — niścay — "resolve (a firm decision)".
જો તમારો નિશ્ચય મક્કમ હશે તો તમે આ કામ પતાવી દેશો. jo tamāro niścay makkam haśe to tame ā kām patāvī deśo. If your resolve is firm, you will finish this work.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
નિશ્ચય (niścay) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit niścaya, "certainty; resolve, a fixed intention" (Monier-Williams).
