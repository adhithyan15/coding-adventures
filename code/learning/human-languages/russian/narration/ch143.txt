Russian, chapter 143: To fly, To ride, To walk, To come, To leave.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
летать (letát) — to fly.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to catch, then the Russian for to push.

You'll want to know: летать.
летать (letát) — "to fly".

What you've built.

Guided Practice.

[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does летать (letát) mean? ("To fly".) Say it once more.
