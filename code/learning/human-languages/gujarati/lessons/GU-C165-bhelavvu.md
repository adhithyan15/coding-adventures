## You'll want to know: ભેળવવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C165-STIR165-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ભેળવવું** — *bheḷavvũ* — "to mix".

> **તે લોટમાં પાણી ભેળવે છે.** — *te loṭmā̃ pāṇī bheḷave che* — "He mixes water into the flour."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ભેળવવું** (*bheḷavvũ*) is a tadbhava. CDIAL lists it, glossed "to mix", under *\*bhiṭ*, *\*bhēl*, "meet, throng", a group of reconstructed forms (CDIAL 9490).
