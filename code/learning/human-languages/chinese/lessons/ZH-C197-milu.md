## You'll want to know: mílù
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C197-WAY197-03]; assesses=[] -->

*mílù* — "to lose your way; to get lost".

> *wǒ mílù le* — I'm lost.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*mílù* is built from *mí* 'confused' and 路 'road', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *mej* and *luH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to be bewildered'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for asking where things are.
