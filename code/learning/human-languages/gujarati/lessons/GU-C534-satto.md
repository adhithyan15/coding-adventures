## You'll want to know: સટ્ટો
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C534-JUGAR534-02]; assesses=[] -->

**સટ્ટો** — *saṭṭo* — "a speculative bet".

> જો તમે સટ્ટામાં પૈસા નાખશો તો કદાચ બધું જશે.
>
> *jo tame saṭṭāmā̃ paisā nākhśo to kadāc badhũ jaśe.*
>
> If you put money into speculation, you may lose it all.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**સટ્ટો** (*saṭṭo*) is a bet on prices or results. CDIAL lists Gujarati *sāṭũ*, "a bargain", and Panjabi *saṭṭī*, "an exchange market", under a reconstructed *\*saṭṭa*, "exchange" (CDIAL 13101); it does not list this word, so its history is not settled.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
