Arabic, chapter 261: To melt, To freeze, To park, To queue, To hand out.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ذاب (dhāba) — to melt.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to stir, then the Arabic for to wrap.

You'll want to know: ذاب.
ذاب — dhāba — "to melt".

The word, taken apart.
ذاب (dhāba) is built on the native Arabic root dh-w-b, "to melt" (Lane). Dhāba is the plain verb, "it melted".
