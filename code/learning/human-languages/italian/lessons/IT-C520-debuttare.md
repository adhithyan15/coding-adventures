## You'll want to know: debuttare
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C520-RECITARE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**debuttare** — "to make one's debut".

> Ha debuttato a teatro a vent'anni.
>
> *She made her stage debut at twenty.*

The noun is *il debutto*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*debuttare* was borrowed from French *débuter* "to make the first move" (EtymDB), built on *but* "a target".
