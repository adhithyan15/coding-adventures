Lesson 3 of 5.
দাড়ি (dāṛi) — a beard.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for vomiting, then the Bengali for an itch.

You'll want to know: দাড়ি.
দাড়ি — dāṛi — "a beard".

The word, taken apart.
দাড়ি (dāṛi) is a tadbhava, a word inherited and reshaped by sound change. It comes from dāṁṣṭra, "fang, tusk, beard", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 6250). CDIAL lists Bengali dāṛi, "beard, chin".

Before the new one: say the Bengali for an itch, then the Bengali for a beard.

You'll want to know: গোঁফ.
গোঁফ — gõph — "a moustache".

The word, taken apart.
গোঁফ (gõph) is a tadbhava, a word inherited and reshaped by sound change. It comes from guppha, "something strung together", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 4203). CDIAL lists Bengali gõph, "moustache", beside Sanskrit gumpha, "a whisker".
