## You'll want to know: la fantascienza
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C527-GENERI-02]; assesses=[] -->

**la fantascienza** — "science fiction".

> Mio figlio legge solo fantascienza.
>
> *My son only reads science fiction.*

Built from *fantasia* and *scienza*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*fantascienza* is a modern word built from *fantasia* "fantasy", from Greek *phantasía*, and *scienza* "science", from Latin *scientia* "knowledge" (Lewis & Short).
