# في اليوم التالي (fī al-yawm at-tālī) — the next day

## You'll want to know: في اليوم التالي
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C299-ATHNA299-03]; assesses=[] -->

**في اليوم التالي** — *fī al-yawm at-tālī* — "the next day".

> في اليوم التالي عاد المسافر إلى بيته.
>
> *fī al-yawm at-tālī ʿāda al-musāfir ilā baytihi.*
>
> The next day the traveller went back home.

At night: في الليلة التالية, the following night.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**في اليوم التالي** (*fī al-yawm at-tālī*) is "on the following day": *tālī* is the participle of *talā*, "to follow", built on the native Arabic root *t-l-w* (Lane).
