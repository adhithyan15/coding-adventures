## You'll want to know: ひる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C166-TIMEOFDAY166-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ひる** — *hiru* — "noon; the daytime".

> **あしたは ひるに ともだちと あいます。** — *ashita wa hiru ni tomodachi to aimasu* — "Tomorrow I will meet a friend at noon."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ひる** (*hiru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi, and its older origin is not settled (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
