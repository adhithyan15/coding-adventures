# A curriculum extension must not assign an A1 stage to an A2 spine node

A late Sanskrit slice closed an A1 assessment-inventory point by extending
`SPINE-TALK-ABOUT-FUTURE`, then labelled the new extension `stage: A1`. That
spine node is an A2 node. The mismatch made the level gate reinterpret the
track and drop Sanskrit from structurally complete A2 to pre-A1 with a
seemingly unrelated reinforcement blocker.

The level of the inventory debt is not automatically the placement level of
new lessons. Before writing an extension, inspect the existing path for the
chosen spine node and keep its stage. A later lesson may repair an earlier
assessment gap without pretending the lesson itself belongs earlier in the
ramp.
