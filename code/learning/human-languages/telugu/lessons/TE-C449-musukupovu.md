# మూసుకుపోవు (mūsukupōvu) — to get blocked; to close up

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[TE-LEX-C449-ADDANKI449-03, TE-LEX-C449-ADDANKI449-04] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Telugu for to stop dead, then the Telugu for to seize up.

## You'll want to know: మూసుకుపోవు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C449-ADDANKI449-05]; assesses=[] -->

**మూసుకుపోవు** — *mūsukupōvu* — "to get blocked; to close up".

> చెత్త వల్ల కాలువ మూసుకుపోయింది.
>
> *cetta valla kāluva mūsukupōyindi.*
>
> The canal got blocked with rubbish.

Past (it …): మూసుకుపోయింది, *mūsukupōyindi*.

మూయు, to close, + -కో + పోవు. A blocked nose is ముక్కు మూసుకుపోయింది. Its subject is a thing, so the past shown is the third person: మూసుకుపోయింది, it got blocked.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**మూసుకుపోవు** (*mūsukupōvu*) is native Dravidian: మూయు, "to cover, to shut" (DEDR 4915), with the self-directed -కో and పోవు, "to go" (DEDR 4572).
