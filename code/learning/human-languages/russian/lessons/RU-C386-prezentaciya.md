## You'll want to know: презентация
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C386-DOKLAD-03]; assesses=[] -->

**презентация** (*prezentátsiya*) — "a presentation; a launch".

> Презентация длилась двадцать минут.
>
> *Prezentátsiya dlilás dvádtsat minút.*
>
> The presentation lasted twenty minutes.

Feminine. *Подготовить презентацию* — to prepare a presentation. Also a launch: *презентация книги*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**презентация** is usually traced, through western languages, to Late Latin *praesentātiō* "a showing", from *praesentāre* "to show", built on *praesēns* "present, at hand" (Lewis & Short).
