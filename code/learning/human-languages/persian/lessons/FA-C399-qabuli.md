## You'll want to know: قبولی
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C399-KONKUR-03]; assesses=[] -->

**قبولی** (*qabuli*) — "a pass (in an exam)".

> همه برای قبولی او خوشحال شدند.
>
> *hame barâye qabuli-ye u khoshhâl shodand.*
>
> Everyone was happy that she passed.

From *qabul* "accepted" + *-i*. *Qabul shodan* — to pass.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**قبولی** is built on *qabul* "accepted", usually traced to Arabic *qabūl* "acceptance", with the Persian noun suffix *-i*.
