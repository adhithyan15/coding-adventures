## You'll want to know: شکست
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C521-MAICH521-05]; assesses=[] -->

**شکست** — *shikast* — "a defeat".

> ہماری ٹیم کو پہلے میچ میں شکست ہوئی۔
>
> *hamārī ṭīm ko pehle maich meṅ shikast huī.*
>
> Our team was beaten in the first match.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**شکست** (*shikast*) is usually traced to Persian *shikast*, "a breaking; a defeat", from *shikastan*, "to break".
