## You'll want to know: तेली
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C263-TELI263-03]; assesses=[] -->

**तेली** — *telī* — "an oil-presser".

> तेली तेल बेचै है।
>
> *telī tel bechai hai.*
>
> The oil-presser sells oil.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**तेली** (*telī*) is inherited from Sanskrit *tailika*, "oilman"; CDIAL lists Marwari *telī* under it (CDIAL 5963).
