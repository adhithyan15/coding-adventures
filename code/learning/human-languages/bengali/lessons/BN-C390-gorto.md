## You'll want to know: গর্ত
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C390-SOROK390-03]; assesses=[] -->

**গর্ত** — *gôrtô* — "a hole; a pothole".

> গাড়িটা একটা গর্তে পড়ল।
>
> *gāṛiṭā ekṭā gôrte pôṛlô.*
>
> The car went into a pothole.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**গর্ত** (*gôrtô*) is Sanskrit *garta*, "a hollow, a hole, a pit" (Monier-Williams), taken over whole as a tatsama.
