## You'll want to know: प्राणायामः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C486-UPAVASA486-02]; assesses=[] -->

**प्राणायामः** — *prāṇāyāmaḥ* — "breath control".

> पिता प्रतिदिनं प्राणायामं करोति।
>
> *pitā pratidinaṃ prāṇāyāmaṃ karoti.*
>
> Father does breath control every day.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**प्राणायामः** (*prāṇāyāmaḥ*) is built from *prāṇa*, "breath", and *āyāma*, "restraint", from *ā* and the root √yam; MW names it as the "breath-exercises" of the Saṃdhyā (MW).
