## You'll want to know: ईमान
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C357-MULYA357-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ईमान** — *īmān* — "integrity, good faith".

> उसने पैसे के लिए अपना ईमान नहीं बेचा।
>
> *usne paise ke liye apnā īmān nahīṃ becā.*
>
> He didn't sell his integrity for money.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ईमान** (*īmān*) is usually traced to Arabic *īmān*, "faith", which reached Hindi through Persian.

## What you've built
