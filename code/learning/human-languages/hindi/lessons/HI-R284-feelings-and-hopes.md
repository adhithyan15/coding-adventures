# Review — feelings and hopes

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[HI-LEX-C284-KHUSH-01, HI-GRAMMAR-C284-FEELING-DATIVE-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "I am happy" and "I'm afraid". (*maiṁ khuś hūṁ*, *mujhe ḍar lagtā hai*.)

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[HI-LEX-C284-KHUSH-01, HI-GRAMMAR-C284-FEELING-DATIVE-01, HI-GRAMMAR-C284-FEELING-BE-01, HI-GRAMMAR-C284-WANT-TO-BECOME-01, HI-GRAMMAR-C284-HOPE-DREAM-01, HI-LEX-C283-AGLA-01, HI-LEX-C56-ROAD-02] -->

[PAUSE 2s each]
- [YOU SAY: I'm afraid of dogs. I'm cold.]
- [YOU SAY: I liked your village very much.]
- [YOU SAY: Why are you sad? What happened?]
- [YOU SAY: What do you want to become? I want to become a doctor.]
- [YOU SAY: I hope that next year I will go to the village.]

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[HI-LEX-C284-KHUSH-01, HI-GRAMMAR-C284-FEELING-DATIVE-01, HI-GRAMMAR-C284-FEELING-BE-01, HI-GRAMMAR-C284-WANT-TO-BECOME-01, HI-GRAMMAR-C284-HOPE-DREAM-01] -->

[PAUSE 3s] "I liked it"? (**मुझे अच्छा लगा**.) "I got angry"? (**मुझे गुस्सा आया**.) "She is happy"? (**वह ख़ुश है**: **ख़ुश** does not change.) "I want to become …"? (**मैं … बनना चाहता हूँ**.) "I hope that …"? (**मुझे उम्मीद है कि …**, then the future.)
