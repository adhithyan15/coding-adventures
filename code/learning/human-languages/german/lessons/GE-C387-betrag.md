## You'll want to know: der Betrag
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C387-KONTO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**der Betrag** — "the amount, the sum".

> Der Betrag ist bis Ende des Monats fällig.
>
> *The amount is due by the end of the month.*

Plural: *die Beträge*. *Der Gesamtbetrag* — the total.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Betrag* is the noun of *betragen* "to amount to", built with *be-* on *tragen* "to carry", Proto-Germanic *draganą* (EtymDB): what a bill carries.
