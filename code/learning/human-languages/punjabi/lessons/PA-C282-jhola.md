## You'll want to know: ਝੋਲਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C282-BAGS282-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ਝੋਲਾ** — *jholā* — "a cloth shoulder bag".

> ਝੋਲੇ ਵਿੱਚ ਪਾਣੀ ਦੀ ਬੋਤਲ ਹੈ।
>
> *jhole vich pāṇī dī botal hai.*
>
> There is a bottle of water in the shoulder bag.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਝੋਲਾ** (*jholā*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*jhōla*, "bag"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *jholā* under it (CDIAL 5415).
