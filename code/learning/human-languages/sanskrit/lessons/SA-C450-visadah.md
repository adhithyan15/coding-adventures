## You'll want to know: विषादः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C450-VISADA450-01]; assesses=[] -->

**विषादः** — *viṣādaḥ* — "dejection".

> परीक्षायाः परिणामेन छात्रस्य विषादः अभवत्।
>
> *parīkṣāyāḥ pariṇāmena chātrasya viṣādaḥ abhavat.*
>
> The student was dejected by the exam result.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**विषादः** (*viṣādaḥ*) is built from *vi*, "apart", and the root √sad, "to sit, to sink": a sinking of the heart; MW gives "dejection, depression, despondency" (MW).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
