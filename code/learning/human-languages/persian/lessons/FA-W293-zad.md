# ض (zâd) — the letter inside توضیح

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[FA-SCRIPT-C25-SAD-01, FA-SCRIPT-C290-ZAL-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: write ص and ذ once each, and name them.

[PAUSE 2s] Now say **توضیح دادن** (*towzih dâdan*), "to explain". You met it a
moment ago. This lesson takes one letter out of it and puts it in your hand.

## Script you'll notice: ض
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-SCRIPT-C293-ZAD-01]; assesses=[FA-LEX-C293-TOWZIH-DADAN-01] -->

**ض** — *zâd*, the sound *z*.

It is the third letter of **توضیح**. The **و** before it offers nothing to the
left, so **ض** starts a fresh run, **ضیح**, in its opening shape.

It is **ص**, from chapter twenty-five, with **one dot above**: the same wide
oval body and the same trailing bowl. That makes four ways to write *z*:
**ز**, **ذ**, **ض** and **ظ**, and Persian says all four alike. Arabic keeps
them apart in sound; Persian keeps them apart only in spelling, so each word
has to be learned with its own *z*.

## Writing: ض
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[FA-SCRIPT-C293-ZAD-01] -->

Put your pen on ض and follow its line. Copy the shape you can see — slowly,
and larger than it is printed.

> This book does not yet tell you **where to start this letter or which way to
> travel**. It is not written down here until it can be written down with a
> source. Copying what is in front of you needs no such source, and it is how the
> shape gets into your hand in the meantime.

## Wrap-up Recall
