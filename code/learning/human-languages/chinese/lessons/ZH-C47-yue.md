## You'll want to know: yuè
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C47-DATE47-02]; assesses=[] -->

*yuè* — "month; moon".

> *zhège yuè wǒ hěn máng* — I'm busy this month.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*yuè* is inherited from Middle Chinese *ngjwot* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), where the dictionary quotes an old saying that the moon is the essence of water.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for times and partings.
