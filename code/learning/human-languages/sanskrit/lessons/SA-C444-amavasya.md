## You'll want to know: अमावास्या
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C444-CANDRA444-02]; assesses=[] -->

**अमावास्या** — *amāvāsyā* — "the new-moon night".

> अमावास्यायाम् आकाशे चन्द्रः न अस्ति।
>
> *amāvāsyāyām ākāśe candraḥ na asti.*
>
> On the new-moon night there is no moon in the sky.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अमावास्या** (*amāvāsyā*) is built from *amā*, "at home, together", and the root √vas, "to dwell": the night when sun and moon dwell together; MW gives *amāvāsyā* as the night of new moon; it lives on as Hindi *amāwas*, "the night of the new moon" (MW; CDIAL 565).
