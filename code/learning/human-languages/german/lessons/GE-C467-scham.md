## You'll want to know: die Scham
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C467-SCHAM-01]; assesses=[] -->

**die Scham** — "shame".

> Vor Scham wurde sie ganz rot.
>
> *She went bright red with shame.*

No plural. *Vor Scham* — with shame.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Scham* is inherited from Old High German *scama*, from Proto-Germanic *skamō* "shame" (EtymDB); English *shame* is a cousin.
