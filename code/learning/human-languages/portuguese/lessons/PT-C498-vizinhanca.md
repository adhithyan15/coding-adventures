## You'll want to know: a vizinhança
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C498-CONVIVIO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**a vizinhança** — "neighbourhood; the neighbours".

> Toda a vizinhança veio à festa.
>
> *All the neighbours came to the party.*

Built on *o vizinho*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*vizinhança* is built on *vizinho* "neighbour", which is inherited from Latin *vīcīnus* "near, neighbouring" (Lewis & Short).
