## You'll want to know: क्षयः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C479-VYADHI479-03]; assesses=[] -->

**क्षयः** — *kṣayaḥ* — "a wasting disease".

> क्षयः फुप्फुसस्य रोगः अस्ति।
>
> *kṣayaḥ phupphusasya rogaḥ asti.*
>
> Consumption is a disease of the lungs.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**क्षयः** (*kṣayaḥ*) comes from the root √kṣi, "to waste away"; among MW's senses are "loss, waste" and "consumption, phthisis" (MW).
