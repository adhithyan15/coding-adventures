## You'll want to know: ડાહ્યું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C464-SANU464-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ડાહ્યું** — *ḍāhyũ* — "well-behaved; sensible".

> ડાહ્યું બાળક મહેમાનો સામે શાંત બેઠું.
>
> *ḍāhyũ bāḷak mahemāno sāme śānt beṭhũ.*
>
> The well-behaved child sat quietly in front of the guests.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ડાહ્યું** (*ḍāhyũ*) goes back to Sanskrit *dāsa*, "a knowing man", a word of the old word lists; CDIAL lists the Gujarati word, "wise" (CDIAL 6317).
