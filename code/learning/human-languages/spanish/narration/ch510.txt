Spanish, chapter 510: Entre personas.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
disculparse — to apologise.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for hope, then the Spanish for the agreement.

You'll want to know: disculparse.
disculparse — "to apologise".
Se disculpó por llegar tarde. He apologised for arriving late.
It goes with por: disculparse por algo.

What you've built.
One more word for reading notices, forms and messages.

Lesson 2 of 5.
fiarse — to trust.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for the agreement, then the Spanish for to apologise.

You'll want to know: fiarse.
fiarse — "to trust".
No me fío de él. I do not trust him.
It goes with de: fiarse de alguien. Note the accent in me fío. In much of Latin America, confiar en is more common.
