## You'll want to know: grasen
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C459-BAUERNHOF-04]; assesses=[] -->

**grasen** — "to graze".

> Die Schafe grasen auf der Wiese.
>
> *The sheep are grazing in the meadow.*

*Es grast*, *sie haben gegrast*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*grasen* is built on *Gras* 'grass', inherited from Proto-Germanic *grasą* (EtymDB lists the verb).
