## You'll want to know: نجومی
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C535-SHAGUN535-04]; assesses=[] -->

**نجومی** — *najūmī* — "an astrologer".

> اگر نجومی سب جانتا تو وہ خود امیر ہوتا۔
>
> *agar najūmī sab jāntā to voh khud amīr hotā.*
>
> If the astrologer knew everything, he would be rich himself.

Masculine or feminine, by the person meant.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**نجومی** (*najūmī*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *nujūm*, "stars", the plural of *najm*, with the ending *-ī*, "one who".
