# ಕೊಚ್ಚಿಹೋಗು (koccihōgu) — to be washed away

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[KA-LEX-C351-KARYA351-03, KA-LEX-C351-KARYA351-04] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Kannada for to crash against, then the Kannada for to engulf.

## You'll want to know: ಕೊಚ್ಚಿಹೋಗು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C351-KARYA351-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ಕೊಚ್ಚಿಹೋಗು** — *koccihōgu* — "to be washed away".

> ಪ್ರವಾಹದಲ್ಲಿ ಹಳೆಯ ಸೇತುವೆ ಕೊಚ್ಚಿಹೋಯಿತು.
>
> *pravāhadalli haḷeya sētuve koccihōyitu.*
>
> The old bridge was washed away in the flood.

Past (it …): ಕೊಚ್ಚಿಹೋಯಿತು, *koccihōyitu*.

Things carried off by water: ಮನೆಗಳು ಕೊಚ್ಚಿಹೋದವು, houses were swept away.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಕೊಚ್ಚಿಹೋಗು** (*koccihōgu*) is a Kannada compound of ಕೊಚ್ಚಿ and *hōgu*, "to go" (DEDR 4572); DEDR 1859 lists a Kannada *koccu*, "to cut away", but whether ಕೊಚ್ಚಿ is that verb is not settled.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
