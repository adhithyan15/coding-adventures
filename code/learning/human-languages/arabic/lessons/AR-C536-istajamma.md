## You'll want to know: استجم
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C536-SAYD536-04]; assesses=[] -->

**استجم** — *istajamma* — "to unwind".

> استجم أبي في القرية أسبوعا.
>
> *istajamma abī fī al-qarya usbūʿan.*
>
> My father unwound in the village for a week.

Present: يستجم, *yastajimm*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**استجم** (*istajamma*) is built on the native Arabic root *j-m-m*, "to rest, to gather again (of water in a well)" (Lane). Form X istajamma is to rest until one's strength comes back.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
