Gujarati, chapter 258: Bat and ball.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ક્રિકેટ (krikeṭ) — cricket.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to take part, then the Gujarati for to join.

You'll want to know: ક્રિકેટ.
ક્રિકેટ — krikeṭ — "cricket".
અમે રવિવારે ક્રિકેટ રમ્યા. — ame ravivāre krikeṭ ramyā — "We played cricket on Sunday."

The word, taken apart.
ક્રિકેટ (krikeṭ) is a loan from English cricket, the game.

Lesson 2 of 5.
દડો (daḍo) — ball.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to join, then the Gujarati for cricket.

You'll want to know: દડો.
દડો — daḍo — "ball".
છોકરાએ દડો ફેંક્યો. — chokrāe daḍo phẽkyo — "The boy threw the ball."

The word, taken apart.
દડો (daḍo) is inherited. EtymDB traces it to Old Gujarati daḍau, "ball", and CDIAL lists Old Gujarati daïḍaü and Gujarati deṛo, "leather ball", under Sanskrit dṛ́ti, "leather bag" (CDIAL 6511).

Lesson 4 of 5.
મારવું (mārvũ) — to hit, to strike.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for ball, then the Gujarati for bat.

You'll want to know: મારવું.
મારવું — mārvũ — "to hit, to strike".
તેણે દડો દૂર માર્યો. — teṇe daḍo dūr māryo — "He hit the ball far."

The word, taken apart.
મારવું (mārvũ) is a tadbhava. It comes from Sanskrit māráyati, "kills"; CDIAL glosses the Gujarati verb "to strike, kill" (CDIAL 10066).

Lesson 5 of 5.
ઊછળવું (ūchaḷvũ) — to bounce, to leap up.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for bat, then the Gujarati for to hit.

You'll want to know: ઊછળવું.
ઊછળવું — ūchaḷvũ — "to bounce, to leap up".
દડો દીવાલથી ઊછળ્યો. — daḍo dīvālthī ūchaḷyo — "The ball bounced off the wall."

The word, taken apart.
ઊછળવું (ūchaḷvũ) is a tadbhava. It comes from ut-śalati, "springs up", a reconstructed form beside Sanskrit ucchalati; CDIAL writes the Gujarati verb uchaḷvũ, "to leap" (CDIAL 1843).
