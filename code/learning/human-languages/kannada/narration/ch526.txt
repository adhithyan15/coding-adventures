Kannada, chapter 526: Kin.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಬಂಧು (bandhu) — kin; a relative.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a cousin, then the Kannada for a brother.

You'll want to know: ಬಂಧು.
ಬಂಧು — bandhu — "kin; a relative".
ಹಬ್ಬಕ್ಕೆ ಎಲ್ಲಾ ಬಂಧುಗಳು ನಮ್ಮ ಮನೆಗೆ ಬಂದರು. habbakke ellā bandhugaḷu namma manege bandaru. All our relatives came to our house for the festival.
Plural: ಬಂಧುಗಳು, bandhugaḷu.
ಬಂಧು (bandhu)-ಬಳಗ, relatives and friends.

The word, taken apart.
ಬಂಧು (bandhu) is borrowed from Sanskrit bandhu, "kinsman" (CDIAL 9144), from bandh, "to bind".

Lesson 2 of 5.
ತವರು (tavaru) — a woman's parental home.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a brother, then the Kannada for kin.

You'll want to know: ತವರು.
ತವರು — tavaru — "a woman's parental home".
ಹೆರಿಗೆಗೆ ಅಕ್ಕ ತವರಿಗೆ ಬಂದಳು. herigege akka tavarige bandaḷu. My elder sister came to our parents' home for the birth.
ತವರು (tavaru) ಮನೆ, the house a married woman was born in.

The word, taken apart.
ತವರು (tavaru) is usually traced to the native tavar, "those who are one's own people" (DEDR 3162); the path is not settled.

Lesson 4 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a woman's parental home, then the Kannada for twins.

You'll want to know: ಮರಿಮಗ.
ಮರಿಮಗ — marimaga — "a great-grandson".
ಅಜ್ಜಿ ತಮ್ಮ ಮರಿಮಗನನ್ನು ಎತ್ತಿಕೊಂಡು ನಕ್ಕರು. ajji tamma marimaganannu ettikoṇḍu nakkaru. Grandmother picked up her great-grandson and laughed.
ಮರಿ, a young one + ಮಗ, son. A great-granddaughter is ಮರಿಮಗಳು.

The word, taken apart.
ಮರಿಮಗ (marimaga) is a Kannada compound built from mari, "a young one", and maga, "son, male child" (DEDR 4616).

Lesson 5 of 5.
ಕಿರಿಯ (kiriya) — younger; junior.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for twins, then the Kannada for a great-grandson.

You'll want to know: ಕಿರಿಯ.
ಕಿರಿಯ — kiriya — "younger; junior".
ನನ್ನ ಕಿರಿಯ ಮಗ ಶಾಲೆಗೆ ಹೋಗುತ್ತಾನೆ. nanna kiriya maga śālege hōguttāne. My younger son goes to school.
The opposite is ಹಿರಿಯ, elder.

The word, taken apart.
ಕಿರಿಯ (kiriya) is native Dravidian: DEDR 1594 lists Kannada kiṟi, "small, little", with kiṟiya, "younger".
