## You'll want to know: anhängen
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C393-DATEI-05]; assesses=[] -->

**anhängen** — "to attach; to add on".

> Ich habe die Fotos an die E-Mail angehängt.
>
> *I attached the photos to the email.*

Separable: *ich hänge an*, *ich habe angehängt*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*anhängen* is built from *an* "on" and *hängen* "to hang", Old High German *hengen*, Proto-Germanic *hangijaną* "to cause to hang" (EtymDB).
