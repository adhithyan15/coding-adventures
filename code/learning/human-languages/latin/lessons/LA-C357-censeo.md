# cēnseō, cēnsēre — to give an opinion, to propose; to assess

## You'll want to know: cēnseō, cēnsēre
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C357-SENTENTIA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**cēnseō, cēnsēre** — "to give an opinion, to propose; to assess".

> Ego cēnseō pācem meliōrem esse quam bellum.
>
> *In my opinion peace is better than war.*

*cēnseō, cēnsēre, cēnsuī, cēnsum*. The verb of a senator stating his view in the senate, and of the officer who counted and valued the citizens. English *census* comes from it.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*cēnseō* is inherited from Proto-Italic *\*kensēō* "to assess, to judge" (EtymDB), and has a Sanskrit cousin meaning "to declare". English *census* comes from it.
