## You'll want to know: die Wetterlage
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C460-WETTERBERICHT-02]; assesses=[] -->

**die Wetterlage** — "the weather situation, the general weather".

> Die Wetterlage bleibt bis Sonntag stabil.
>
> *The weather will stay settled until Sunday.*

Plural: *die Wetterlagen*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Wetterlage* is built from *Wetter* 'weather' and *Lage* 'position, situation', from *liegen* 'to lie' (EtymDB lists *Wetterlage*).
