Sanskrit, chapter 217: Red, Reddish, Tawny, Equal, Uneven.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
रक्तः (raktaḥ) — red.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for wise, then the Sanskrit for intelligent.

You'll want to know: रक्तः.
रक्तः — raktaḥ — "red".

The word, taken apart.
rakta is the past participle of the root √rañj, "to be coloured, dyed" (MW): "dyed", then "red", then "blood" (CDIAL 10539). It lives on as Hindi rātā, "dyed, red".

Lesson 2 of 5.
अरुणः (aruṇaḥ) — reddish, dawn-red.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for intelligent, then the Sanskrit for red.

You'll want to know: अरुणः.
अरुणः — aruṇaḥ — "reddish, dawn-red".

The word, taken apart.
aruṇa is the Rigvedic colour of dawn, "reddish, tawny" (CDIAL 616). MW takes it from the root √ṛ, while CDIAL suggests, with a question mark, a stem aru-, so the derivation is not settled. Hindi keeps it as arun.

Lesson 3 of 5.
कपिलः (kapilaḥ) — tawny, brown.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for red, then the Sanskrit for reddish.

You'll want to know: कपिलः.
कपिलः — kapilaḥ — "tawny, brown".

The word, taken apart.
kapila is a Rigvedic colour word, "brown, tawny, reddish" (CDIAL 2750). MW says it is more probably connected with kapi, "monkey": monkey-coloured. Gujarati koḷũ, "reddish brown", comes from it.

Lesson 5 of 5.
विषमः (viṣamaḥ) — uneven, odd.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for tawny, then the Sanskrit for equal.

You'll want to know: विषमः.
विषमः — viṣamaḥ — "uneven, odd".

The word, taken apart.
viṣama is built on vi-, "apart", and sama, "even, equal" (MW): "uneven, unequal", and so "odd", "difficult" (CDIAL 11972). The s of sama turns to ṣ after vi-.
