# ਮੇਰੀ — my, with a feminine noun

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-LEX-MERA-02, PA-LEX-C71-HOME71-02] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Read **ਮੇਰਾ**, then say **ਕਿਤਾਬ**.

## Grammar Lens: change the agreement ending
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-FORM-MERI-185]; assesses=[PA-LEX-MERA-02] -->

> **ਮੇਰੀ** — *merii* — my, agreeing with a feminine noun

The ending changes from *-aa* to *-ii*. Keep the owner the same. In
**ਮੇਰੀ ਕਿਤਾਬ**, the form agrees with **ਕਿਤਾਬ**.
