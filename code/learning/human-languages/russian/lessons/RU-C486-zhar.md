## You'll want to know: жар
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C486-FEVER-01]; assesses=[] -->

**жар** (*zhar*) — "a fever, a high temperature; heat".

> У ребёнка жар, надо вызвать врача.
>
> *U rebyónka zhar, nádo výzvat vrachá.*
>
> The child has a fever; we need to call the doctor.

Masculine. *У него жар* — he has a high temperature.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

EtymDB lists *жар* without a source; it is usually traced to Proto-Slavic *žarъ* 'heat', related to *гореть* 'to burn' and *жарить* 'to fry'.
