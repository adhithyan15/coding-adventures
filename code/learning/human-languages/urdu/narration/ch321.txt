Urdu, chapter 321: At the Fair.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
جھولنا (jhūlnā) — to swing.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for to drench, then the Urdu for to sway.

You'll want to know: جھولنا.
جھولنا — jhūlnā — "to swing".
بچی رسی پر جھول رہی تھی۔ bachchī rassī par jhūl rahī thī. The little girl was swinging on a rope.
Past (she …): وہ جھولی, voh jhūlī.

The word, taken apart.
جھولنا (jhūlnā) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin jhūlnā, "to swing", under a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 5406).

Lesson 2 of 5.
جلوس (julūs) — a procession.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for to sway, then the Urdu for to swing.

You'll want to know: جلوس.
جلوس — julūs — "a procession".
سڑک پر ایک لمبا جلوس تھا۔ saṛak par ek lambā julūs thā. There was a long procession in the road.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
جلوس (julūs) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic julūs, "a sitting", once the sitting of a ruler in state; how it came to mean a procession is thought to run through the royal parades.

ہجوم (hujūm) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic hujūm, "a rushing in, an onset"; in Urdu it is the crowd itself.

Lesson 4 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a procession, then the Urdu for a crowd.

You'll want to know: اکٹھا.
اکٹھا — ikaṭṭhā — "gathered together".
سب لوگ میدان میں اکٹھے ہوئے۔ sab log maidān meṅ ikaṭṭhe hue. Everyone gathered on the field.

The word, taken apart.
اکٹھا (ikaṭṭhā) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin ekṭhā, "standing together", under Sanskrit ēkasthá (CDIAL 2480). It agrees like an adjective: اکٹھے ہوئے.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

میلے میں بندر کا تماشا تھا۔ mele meṅ bandar kā tamāshā thā. There was a monkey show at the fair.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
تماشا (tamāshā) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic tamāshā, "a walking about together", and so a sight people gather to watch.
