# কণ্ঠ (kônṭhô) — the voice; the throat

## You'll want to know: কণ্ঠ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C467-THOT467-04]; assesses=[] -->

**কণ্ঠ** — *kônṭhô* — "the voice; the throat".

> গায়িকার কণ্ঠ সুন্দর।
>
> *gāyikār kônṭhô sundôr.*
>
> The singer has a beautiful voice.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**কণ্ঠ** (*kônṭhô*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *kaṇṭha*, "the throat, the neck" and "the voice" (Monier-Williams).
