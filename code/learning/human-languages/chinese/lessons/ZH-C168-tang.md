## You'll want to know: táng
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C168-COOK168-05]; assesses=[] -->

*táng* — "sugar; sweets".

> *zhège tāng yào fàng yìdiǎnr táng* — This soup needs a little sugar in it.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*táng* is inherited from Middle Chinese *dang* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'malt syrup'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
