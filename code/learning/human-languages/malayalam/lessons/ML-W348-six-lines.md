# Six lines about a trip

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ML-GRAMMAR-C339-SCENE-EVENT-01, ML-SKILL-C340-READ-STORY-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] The last A2 writing task asked for a connected message about a familiar day. This one asks for six
lines, and they tell a story.

[PAUSE 1s] Pick one reader: a friend, by name.

## You'll want to know
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ML-GRAMMAR-C340-ATINUSESHAM-01, ML-GRAMMAR-C340-ENNITTU-01, ML-GRAMMAR-C340-APPOL-01, ML-GRAMMAR-C342-ATUKONDU-CLAUSE-01, ML-GRAMMAR-C345-FELT-01, ML-GRAMMAR-C348-UNREAL-IF-01, ML-GRAMMAR-C348-WOULD-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] A told story is not a list of events. It has a **scene**, in **ആയിരുന്നു** and the
**-ഉകയായിരുന്നു** past. It has **events** in the plain past, kept in order with **ആദ്യം**,
**എന്നിട്ട്**, **-അപ്പോൾ**, **അതിനുശേഷം** and **ഒടുവിൽ**. It has at least one **reason**, with
**-അതുകൊണ്ട്** or **കാരണം**, and a **feeling**, with **എനിക്ക് … തോന്നി**.

[PAUSE 2s] A good one ends by looking at what might have been: **… -ഇരുന്നെങ്കിൽ … -ഉമായിരുന്നു**.

## Writing — connected composition
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ML-GRAMMAR-C339-AYIRUNNU-01, ML-GRAMMAR-C339-PAST-PROGRESSIVE-01, ML-LEX-C340-IYITE-01, ML-LEX-C68-ORDINAL-08, ML-LEX-C196-SAMAYAM196-03, ML-GRAMMAR-C340-ENNITTU-01, ML-GRAMMAR-C340-APPOL-01, ML-GRAMMAR-C340-ATINUSESHAM-01, ML-GRAMMAR-C342-ATUKONDU-CLAUSE-01, ML-LEX-C73-KAARANAM-01, ML-GRAMMAR-C345-FELT-01, ML-GRAMMAR-C345-FEELING-SCENE-EVENT-01, ML-GRAMMAR-C348-UNREAL-IF-01, ML-GRAMMAR-C348-WOULD-01, ML-GRAMMAR-C343-ASK-WAY-01, ML-GRAMMAR-C344-MISSED-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: connected-composition -->

[PAUSE 2s] On paper, write **six lines** to your reader about a trip, real or invented, one line for
each of these:

1. the scene: when it was, with **ഈയിടെ** or **കഴിഞ്ഞ**, and what it was like, with **ആയിരുന്നു**;
2. where you went and what you were doing on the way, in the **-ഉകയായിരുന്നു** past;
3. the first thing that happened, with **ആദ്യം**;
4. what happened next, with **എന്നിട്ട്** or **-അപ്പോൾ**, and one thing that went wrong;
5. how it ended and how you felt, with **ഒടുവിൽ** and **എനിക്ക് … തോന്നി**, and a reason;
6. one line about what might have been, with **-ഇരുന്നെങ്കിൽ … -ഉമായിരുന്നു**.

[PAUSE 3s] There is no model on this page. Use only words you already write. The whole exercise is
choosing the right past for each line, and joining them.

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ML-GRAMMAR-C339-SCENE-EVENT-01, ML-GRAMMAR-C339-PAST-PROGRESSIVE-01, ML-GRAMMAR-C348-UNREAL-IF-01, ML-SCRIPT-C340-II-01, ML-SCRIPT-C344-AU-SIGN-01, ML-SCRIPT-C345-VISARGA-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Now read your lines as you read the village story earlier in this core. Mark every verb **s**
for scene or **e** for event. Line two should be mostly **s**; lines three and four mostly **e**.

[PAUSE 2s] Then three spelling checks. A word that starts with long *ī* starts with **ഈ**, not **ഇ**.
The *au* of **സൗകര്യം** is the tail **ൗ** after its consonant. **ദുഃഖം** keeps its two rings, **ഃ**.

[PAUSE 2s] Last, read line six alone. Does the if-clause end in **-ഇരുന്നെങ്കിൽ**, and the main verb
in **-ഉമായിരുന്നു**?
