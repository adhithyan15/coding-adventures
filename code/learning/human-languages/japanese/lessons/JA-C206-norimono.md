## You'll want to know: のりもの
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C206-TRAFFIC206-01]; assesses=[] -->

**のりもの** — *norimono* — "a vehicle; a means of transport".

> **でんしゃや ふねは のりものです。** — *densha ya fune wa norimono desu* — "Trains and boats are vehicles."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**のりもの** (*norimono*) is native Japanese (wago), built from *nori*, "riding", and *mono*, "thing", both inherited rather than borrowed (JMdict).
