## You'll want to know: ревнивый
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C469-JEALOUSY-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ревнивый** (*revnívyy*) — "jealous (by nature)".

> Мой муж очень ревнивый.
>
> *Moy muzh óchen revnívyy.*
>
> My husband is very jealous.

Describes a jealous character. Short forms: ревнив, ревнива.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ревнивый* is built on the stem of *ревность* 'jealousy' with the adjective ending *-ивый*; EtymDB lists the adjective without a source.
