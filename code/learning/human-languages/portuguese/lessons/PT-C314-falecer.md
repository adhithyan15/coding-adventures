## You'll want to know: falecer
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C314-FALECER-02]; assesses=[] -->

**falecer** — "to pass away".

> O avô dela faleceu no domingo.
>
> *Her grandfather passed away on Sunday.*

A gentle, formal way to say *morrer*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*falecer* goes back to Vulgar Latin *fallēscere* (EtymDB), from Latin *fallere* "to deceive, to fail": life fails.
