## You'll want to know: অভিনেতা
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C527-MONCHO527-03]; assesses=[] -->

**অভিনেতা** — *ôbhinetā* — "an actor".

> এই সিনেমার অভিনেতা বিখ্যাত।
>
> *Ei sinemār ôbhinetā bikhyātô.*
>
> The actor in this film is famous.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**অভিনেতা** (*ôbhinetā*) is Sanskrit *abhinetṛ*, "one who brings near" (Monier-Williams); the sense "actor" is modern, made to go with *abhinaya*, acting.
