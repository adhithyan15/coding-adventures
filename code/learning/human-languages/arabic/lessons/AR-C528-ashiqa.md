## You'll want to know: عشق
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C528-ZAWAJ528-05]; assesses=[] -->

**عشق** — *ʿashiqa* — "to love passionately".

> يعشق جدي الموسيقى القديمة.
>
> *yaʿshaqu jaddī al-mūsīqā al-qadīma.*
>
> My grandfather adores old music.

Present: يعشق, *yaʿshaq*.

The noun عشق is passionate love.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**عشق** (*ʿashiqa*) is built on the native Arabic root *ʿ-sh-q*, "to love passionately" (Lane). Lane gives yaʿshaqu as its present.
