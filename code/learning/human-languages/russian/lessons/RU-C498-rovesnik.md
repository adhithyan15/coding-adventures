## You'll want to know: ровесник
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C498-FRIENDS-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ровесник** (*rovésnik*) — "someone of the same age".

> Мы с ним ровесники.
>
> *My s nim rovésniki.*
>
> He and I are the same age.

Masculine; feminine ровесница.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ровесник* was borrowed from Polish *rówieśnik* 'a person of the same age' (EtymDB).
