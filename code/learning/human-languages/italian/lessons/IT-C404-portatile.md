## You'll want to know: il portatile
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C404-BLOCCO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**il portatile** — "the laptop".

> Porto il portatile in vacanza, se devo lavorare.
>
> *I take my laptop on holiday, if I have to work.*

As an adjective, portable: *un televisore portatile*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*portatile* is built on *portare* "to carry", inherited from Latin *portāre* "to carry" (Lewis & Short): a computer you can carry.
