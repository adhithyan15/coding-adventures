## You'll want to know: l'impero
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C328-IMPERO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**l'impero** — "the empire".

> L'impero è durato cinque secoli.
>
> *The empire lasted five centuries.*

Built on Latin *imperium*, ‘command’.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*impero* is inherited from Latin *imperium* "command, power, empire" (EtymDB), from *imperāre* "to command".
