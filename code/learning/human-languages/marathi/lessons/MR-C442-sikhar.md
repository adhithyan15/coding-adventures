## You'll want to know: शिखर
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C442-SHIKHAR442-01]; assesses=[] -->

**शिखर** — *śikhar* — "a peak; a summit".

> आम्ही पहाटे शिखरावर पोहोचलो.
>
> *āmhī pahāṭe śikharāvar pohoclo.*
>
> We reached the summit at dawn.

Neuter.

Also the top of a temple, and the height of success: यशाचं शिखर.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**शिखर** (*śikhar*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *śikhara*, "a peak, a summit, a pinnacle" (Monier-Williams).
