# ಜಮಾಯಿಸು (jamāyisu) — to gather; to assemble

## You'll want to know: ಜಮಾಯಿಸು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C471-GADDALA471-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ಜಮಾಯಿಸು** — *jamāyisu* — "to gather; to assemble".

> ಅಪಘಾತದ ನಂತರ ರಸ್ತೆಯಲ್ಲಿ ಜನ ಜಮಾಯಿಸಿದರು.
>
> *apaghātada nantara rasteyalli jana jamāyisidaru.*
>
> After the accident people gathered on the road.

Past (I …): ಜಮಾಯಿಸಿದೆ, *jamāyiside*.

Said of a crowd that forms.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಜಮಾಯಿಸು** (*jamāyisu*) is usually traced to an Arabic word for gathering, through Hindustani *jamā*, "gathered, collected", with the Kannada verb ending -*isu*. ಜಮಾ ಮಾಡು, to deposit, comes from the same word.
