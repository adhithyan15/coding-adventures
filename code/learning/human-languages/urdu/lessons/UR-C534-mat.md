## You'll want to know: مات
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C534-BISAT534-04]; assesses=[] -->

**مات** — *māt* — "checkmate".

> اگر آپ نے بادشاہ نہیں بچایا تو مات ہو گی۔
>
> *agar āp ne bādshāh nahīṅ bachāyā to māt ho gī.*
>
> If you don't save your king, it will be checkmate.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**مات** (*māt*) is usually traced to Persian *māt*, "helpless, beaten", as in *shāh māt*, "the king is helpless".
