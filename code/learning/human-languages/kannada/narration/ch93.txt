Kannada, chapter 93: Hot, Cold and Hurt.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ತಲೆಸುತ್ತು (talesuttu) — dizziness.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a job, then the Kannada for English.

You'll want to know: ತಲೆಸುತ್ತು.
ತಲೆಸುತ್ತು (talesuttu) — "dizziness", literally "head-spinning": ತಲೆ (tale), "head", with ಸುತ್ತು (suttu), "to go round".
ನನಗೆ ತಲೆಸುತ್ತು (nanage talesuttu) — "I feel dizzy", built like ನನಗೆ ತಲೆನೋವು.

What you've built.
Head, and a turn, make one more head word.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: talesuttu]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: talesuttu, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say nanage talesuttu]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Kannada for a job, then the Kannada for English, then say talesuttu again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does ತಲೆಸುತ್ತು (talesuttu) mean? ("Dizziness".) Say it once more.

You'll want to know: ಸೆಕೆ.
ಸೆಕೆ (seke) — "sultry, sticky heat", the heat that makes you ಬೆವರು, "sweat".
ತುಂಬಾ ಸೆಕೆ (tumbā seke) — "it's very hot and sticky".
