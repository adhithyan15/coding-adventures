Marwadi (Marwari), chapter 229: Light and White.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
उजास (ujās) — daylight; brightness.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for cool, then the Marwadi for to dry up.

You'll want to know: उजास.
उजास — ujās — "daylight; brightness".
तड़कै उजास आवै है। taṛkai ujās āvai hai. At daybreak the light comes.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
उजास (ujās) is inherited from a reconstructed ujjhāṣa, "shining"; CDIAL lists Gujarati ujās, "brightness, light", under it. CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry (CDIAL 1678).

Lesson 3 of 5.

Warm-up.

Before the new one: say the Marwadi for daylight, then the Marwadi for bright.

You'll want to know: झीणो.
झीणो — jhīṇo — "fine (of sand or cloth); thin".
थळ मांय रेत झीणी है। thaḷ mā̃y ret jhīṇī hai. The sand in the desert is fine.

The word, taken apart.
झीणो (jhīṇo) is inherited from a reconstructed jhīna, "wasted, thin"; CDIAL lists Old Marwari jhīṇa, "thin", and Gujarati jhīṇũ, "thin, fine" (CDIAL 5395).

Lesson 4 of 5.
धोळो (dhoḷo) — white.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for bright, then the Marwadi for fine.

You'll want to know: धोळो.
धोळो — dhoḷo — "white".
म्हारो कुरतो धोळो है। mhāro kurto dhoḷo hai. My kurta is white.

The word, taken apart.
धोळो (dhoḷo) is inherited from Sanskrit dhavala, "dazzling white"; CDIAL lists Hindi dhaulā, "white", and a Gujarati cousin. CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry (CDIAL 6767).

Lesson 5 of 5.
चमकणो (chamakṇo) — to shine; to glitter.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for fine, then the Marwadi for white.

You'll want to know: चमकणो.
चमकणो — chamakṇo — "to shine; to glitter".
रात तारो चमकै है। rāt tāro chamkai hai. At night a star shines.
Past (it …): वो तारो चमक्यो, vo tāro chamkyo.

The word, taken apart.
चमकणो (chamakṇo) goes back to a reconstructed camakka, "a sudden movement"; CDIAL lists Hindi camaknā and Gujarati camakvũ, "to glitter". CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry (CDIAL 4676). चमकीलो, shiny, is built on it.
