7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
ધર્મશાળા (dharmaśāḷā) — rest house, pilgrims' lodge.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for pilgrimage, then the Gujarati for vehicle.

You'll want to know: ધર્મશાળા.
ધર્મશાળા — dharmaśāḷā — "rest house, pilgrims' lodge".
અમે રાત્રે ધર્મશાળામાં રહ્યા. — ame rātre dharmaśāḷāmā̃ rahyā — "We spent the night at a rest house."

The word, taken apart.
ધર્મશાળા (dharmaśāḷā) is Sanskrit dharmaśālā, "charitable asylum", built from dhárma, "duty, charity", and śā́lā, "hall", taken over with Gujarati -ḷ-, a semi-tatsama (CDIAL 6760a).

Lesson 2 of 7.
ભોમિયો (bhomiyo) — guide, one who knows the way.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for vehicle, then the Gujarati for rest house.

You'll want to know: ભોમિયો.
ભોમિયો — bhomiyo — "guide, one who knows the way".
ભોમિયાએ અમને રસ્તો બતાવ્યો. — bhomiyāe amne rasto batāvyo — "The guide showed us the way."

The word, taken apart.
ભોમિયો (bhomiyo) is built on bhom, "land", with the agent ending -iyo: the one who knows the land. CDIAL lists Gujarati bhom, "a piece of land, field", under Sanskrit bhaumika, "being in the earth", a derivative of bhū́mi, "earth" (CDIAL 9642).

Lesson 3 of 7.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for rest house, then the Gujarati for guide.

You'll want to know: રાહદારી.
રાહદારી — rāhdārī — "pedestrian, passer-by".
એક રાહદારીએ મને સરનામું કહ્યું. — ek rāhdārīe mane sarnāmũ kahyũ — "A passer-by told me the address."

The word, taken apart.
રાહદારી (rāhdārī) is built from rāh, "road, way", usually traced to Persian rāh, "road", the Persian suffix -dār, "holder", and the ending -ī: one who keeps to the road.

ટ્રેન લાંબી સુરંગમાંથી ગઈ. — ṭren lā̃bī surangmā̃thī gaī — "The train went through a long tunnel."

The word, taken apart.

Lesson 5 of 7.
મુસાફરખાનું (musāpharkhānũ) — waiting room.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for pedestrian, then the Gujarati for tunnel.

You'll want to know: મુસાફરખાનું.
મુસાફરખાનું — musāpharkhānũ — "waiting room".
મુસાફરખાનું ભરેલું હતું. — musāpharkhānũ bharelũ hatũ — "The waiting room was full."
Built on મુસાફર, musāphar, 'traveller'.

The word, taken apart.
મુસાફરખાનું (musāpharkhānũ) is built from musāphar, "traveller", a loan from Arabic musāfir that reached Gujarati through Persian, and khānũ, "house, room", from Persian khāna. The Arabic root is s-f-r, "to journey" (Lane).
