Lesson 1 of 5.
ಉಚಿತ (ucita) — free of charge.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for bland, then the Kannada for expensive.

You'll want to know: ಉಚಿತ.
ಉಚಿತ — ucita — "free of charge".

The word, taken apart.
ಉಚಿತ (ucita) is Sanskrit ucita, "fitting, customary". The sense "free of charge" grew in Kannada, from what is given as is fitting.

ಕಡ್ಡಾಯ (kaḍḍāya) is native Dravidian, one word with Tamil kaṭṭāyam and Telugu kaḍḍāyamu (DEDR 1157). DEDR lists kaḍḍāya itself, "force, compulsion".
