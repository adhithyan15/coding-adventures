## You'll want to know: a consequência
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C348-EFEITO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**a consequência** — "the consequence".

> Ele não pensou nas consequências.
>
> *He did not think about the consequences.*

*Em consequência de* — as a result of.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*consequência* is a learned borrowing from Latin *cōnsequentia* "a consequence, a natural succession" (EtymDB, Lewis & Short), from *cōnsequī* "to follow after", built on *sequī* "to follow".
