## You'll want to know: تذكار
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C440-TIDHKAR440-02]; assesses=[] -->

**تذكار** — *tidhkār* — "a souvenir; a keepsake".

> اشتريت تذكارا صغيرا لأختي.
>
> *ishtaraytu tidhkāran ṣaghīran li-ukhtī.*
>
> I bought a small souvenir for my sister.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**تذكار** (*tidhkār*) is built on the native Arabic root *dh-k-r*, "to remember" (Lane). A *tidhkār* is a thing that makes one remember.
