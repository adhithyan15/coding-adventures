## You'll want to know: dubitare
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C343-DUBBIO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**dubitare** — "to doubt".

> Dubito che arrivi in orario.
>
> *I doubt that he will arrive on time.*

*Dubitare che* takes the subjunctive. *Dubitare di qualcuno* — not to trust someone.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*dubitare* is a learned borrowing from Latin *dubitāre* "to waver, to be uncertain, to doubt" (EtymDB), related to *dubius* "doubtful", a word linked with *duo* "two": a mind pulled two ways.
