## You'll want to know: उत्तारयति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C511-KASTHA511-03]; assesses=[] -->

**उत्तारयति** — *uttārayati* — "takes down; lifts off".

> माता चुल्ल्याः पात्रम् उत्तारयति।
>
> *mātā cullyāḥ pātram uttārayati.*
>
> Mother takes the pot off the stove.

Past (she …): सा पात्रम् उत्तारितवती, *sā pātram uttāritavatī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**उत्तारयति** (*uttārayati*) is built on *ud*, "up, out", and the causative of the root √tṝ, "to cross over", which also gives *tarati*; MW gives the causative *uttārayati* as to make come out, to take down, to take off (MW).
