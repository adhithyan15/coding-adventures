## You'll want to know: xìn
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C118-SEND118-03]; assesses=[] -->

*xìn* — "letter (in the post)".

> *wǒ xiǎng gěi péngyou xiě xìn* — I'd like to write to a friend.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*xìn* is inherited from Middle Chinese *sinH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'trust' and 'proof'; how it came to mean a letter is not settled.
