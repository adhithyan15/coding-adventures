## You'll want to know: 语音
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C320-PHONE320-05]; assesses=[] -->

**语音** — *yǔyīn* — "voice message; speech sounds".

> *wǒ tīng le māma de yǔyīn* — I listened to my mum's voice message.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**语音** (*yǔyīn*) is built from 语 'speech' and 音 'a sound', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *ngjoX* and *'im* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to discuss' and the second 'a sound'. A recorded message is a modern sense.
