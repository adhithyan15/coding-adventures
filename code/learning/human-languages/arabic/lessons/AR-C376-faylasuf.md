## You'll want to know: فيلسوف
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C376-ILM376-03]; assesses=[] -->

**فيلسوف** — *faylasūf* — "a philosopher".

> قرأت كتابا لفيلسوف قديم.
>
> *qaraʾtu kitāban li-faylasūf qadīm.*
>
> I read a book by an ancient philosopher.

Plural فلاسفة, *falāsifa*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**فيلسوف** (*faylasūf*) was borrowed from Greek *philosophos*, "a lover of wisdom" (EtymDB). Its sister word is *falsafa*, philosophy.
