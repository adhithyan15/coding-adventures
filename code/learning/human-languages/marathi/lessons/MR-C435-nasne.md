## You'll want to know: नासणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C435-TANCHAI435-05]; assesses=[] -->

**नासणे** — *nāsṇe* — "to go bad".

> उन्हामुळे दूध नासलं.
>
> *unhāmuḷe dūdh nāslaṁ.*
>
> The milk went bad in the heat.

Past (he …): तो नासला, *to nāslā*.

Milk, fruit and cooked food नासतात.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**नासणे** (*nāsṇe*) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi *nāsṇẽ*, "to be spoilt", under Sanskrit *naśyati*, "is lost, perishes" (CDIAL 7027).
