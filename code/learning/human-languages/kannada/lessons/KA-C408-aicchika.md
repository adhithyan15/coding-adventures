## You'll want to know: ಐಚ್ಛಿಕ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C408-PATHYAKRAMA408-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ಐಚ್ಛಿಕ** — *aicchika* — "optional; elective".

> ಶಾಲೆಯಲ್ಲಿ ಕನ್ನಡ ಕಡ್ಡಾಯ, ಸಂಗೀತ ಐಚ್ಛಿಕ.
>
> *śāleyalli kannaḍa kaḍḍāya, saṅgīta aicchika.*
>
> At school Kannada is compulsory and music is optional.

Its opposite is ಕಡ್ಡಾಯ, compulsory. ಐಚ್ಛಿಕ ವಿಷಯ, an elective subject.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಐಚ್ಛಿಕ** (*aicchika*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *aicchika*, "optional, arbitrary, at will" (Monier-Williams), an adjective formed from *icchā*, "a wish".
