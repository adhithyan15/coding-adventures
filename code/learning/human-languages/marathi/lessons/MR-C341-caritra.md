## You'll want to know: चरित्र
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C341-KADAMBARI341-02]; assesses=[] -->

**चरित्र** — *caritra* — "a biography".

> तिने एका खेळाडूचं चरित्र लिहिलं.
>
> *tine ekā kheḷāḍūcaṁ caritra lihilaṁ.*
>
> She wrote the biography of a sportsman.

Neuter.

चरित्र लिहिणे, to write a life story.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**चरित्र** (*caritra*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *caritra*, 'behaviour, deeds, adventures' (Monier-Williams).
