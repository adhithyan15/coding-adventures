# ഭേദഗതി ചെയ്യുക (bhēdagati ceyyuka) — to amend

## Warm-up

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Malayalam for to implement, then the Malayalam for to pass a law.

## You'll want to know: ഭേദഗതി ചെയ്യുക
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C423-NAYAM423-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ഭേദഗതി ചെയ്യുക** — *bhēdagati ceyyuka* — "to amend".

> സർക്കാർ ആ നിയമം ഭേദഗതി ചെയ്തു.
>
> *sarkkār ā niyamaṁ bhēdagati ceytu.*
>
> The government amended that law.

Past: ഭേദഗതി ചെയ്തു, *bhēdagati ceytu*.

The noun ഭേദഗതി is an amendment.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ഭേദഗതി ചെയ്യുക** (*bhēdagati ceyyuka*) is a phrase verb built on the noun ഭേദഗതി, "an amendment", a Malayalam compound of Sanskrit *bheda*, "breaking, a change" (Monier-Williams), and *gati*, "going" (CDIAL 4009); ചെയ്യുക, "to do", makes it a verb.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
