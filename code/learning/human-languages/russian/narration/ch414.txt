Russian, chapter 414: On the platform.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
перрон (perrón) — a station platform.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a rate, then the Russian for a concession.

You'll want to know: перрон.
перрон (perrón) — "a station platform".
Мы ждали поезд на перроне. My zhdáli póyezd na perróne. We waited for the train on the platform.
Masculine. На перроне — on the platform.

The word, taken apart.
перрон (perrón) was borrowed from French perron (EtymDB), a stone landing with steps outside a building, built on pierre 'stone'.

Lesson 2 of 5.
платформа (platfórma) — a platform.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a concession, then the Russian for a station platform.

You'll want to know: платформа.
платформа (platfórma) — "a platform".
Поезд приходит на вторую платформу. Póyezd prikhódit na vtorúyu platfórmu. The train comes in at platform two.
Feminine. Поезд стоит у третьей платформы — the train is at platform three.

The word, taken apart.
платформа (platfórma) is usually traced to French plate-forme, from plat 'flat' and forme 'a form' (EtymDB lists the French word); the Russian word is not in EtymDB.

Lesson 4 of 5.
отправление (otpravléniye) — departure.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a platform, then the Russian for a departures board.

You'll want to know: отправление.
отправление (otpravléniye) — "departure".
До отправления поезда осталось пять минут. Do otpravléniya póyezda ostálos pyat minút. There are five minutes left before the train departs.
Neuter; formal, used on boards and in announcements. Почтовое отправление (otpravléniye) — an item of mail.

The word, taken apart.
отправление (otpravléniye) is built on отправлять 'to send off' with the ending -ение; EtymDB lists it with the sense 'departure'.

Lesson 5 of 5.
прибытие (pribýtiye) — arrival.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a departures board, then the Russian for departure.

You'll want to know: прибытие.
прибытие (pribýtiye) — "arrival".
Время прибытия — десять часов. Vrémya pribýtiya — désyat chasóv. The arrival time is ten o'clock.
Neuter; formal. In speech people say приезд.

The word, taken apart.
прибытие (pribýtiye) is built on прибыть 'to arrive', from при- 'to, near' and быть 'to be'. EtymDB lists прибыть without a deeper source.
