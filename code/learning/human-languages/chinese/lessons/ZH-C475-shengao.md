## You'll want to know: 身高
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C475-BODY475-04]; assesses=[] -->

**身高** — *shēngāo* — "a person's height; body height".

> **你身高多少** *nǐ shēngāo duōshao* — How tall are you?

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**身高** (*shēngāo*) is built from 身 'body' and 高 'high', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *syin* and *kaw* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'oneself' and the second 'high'.
