## You'll want to know: 找到
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C197-WAY197-02]; assesses=[] -->

**找到** — *zhǎodào* — "to find".

> **我找到书店了** *wǒ zhǎodào shūdiàn le* — I've found the bookshop.

The new character, in pieces: 找 — seven strokes: on the left the hand piece of 打; on the right a bar, a long slanting hook, a stroke falling to the left across it, and a dot. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**找到** (*zhǎodào*) is built from 找 'to look for' and 到 'to arrive': looking that gets there. 到 is inherited from Middle Chinese *tawH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription); no dictionary we use records an older form of 找, so its history is not settled.

## What you've built
