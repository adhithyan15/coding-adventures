## You'll want to know: die Wange
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C484-GESICHT-02]; assesses=[] -->

**die Wange** — "cheek".

> Das Baby hat rote Wangen.
>
> *The baby has red cheeks.*

Plural: *die Wangen*. In speech also *die Backe*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Wange* is inherited from Middle High German *wange* and Old High German *wanga*, from Proto-Germanic *wangô* "cheek" (EtymDB).
