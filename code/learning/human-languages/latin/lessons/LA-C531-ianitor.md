## You'll want to know: iānitor, iānitōris
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C531-IANI-02]; assesses=[] -->

**iānitor, iānitōris** — "a doorkeeper".

> Iānitor ad iānuam sedet et hospitēs salūtat.
>
> *The doorkeeper sits by the door and greets the guests.*

Masculine, third declension, from *iānua* — door. English *janitor*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**iānitor** is built on *iānua* "door" with *-tor* (Lewis & Short; EtymDB). English *janitor* comes from it.
