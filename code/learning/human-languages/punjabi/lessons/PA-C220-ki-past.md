# ਕੀ ਤੁਸੀਂ ਰੋਟੀ ਖਾਧੀ? — did you eat the roti?

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-GRAMMAR-POLAR-KI-214, PA-GRAMMAR-PAST-KHADHA-219] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Ask *do you drink tea?* Then say *you ate a roti*.

## Grammar Lens: ਕੀ in the past
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-GRAMMAR-POLAR-KI-214, PA-GRAMMAR-POLAR-ANSWER-214, PA-GRAMMAR-PAST-KHADHA-219, PA-GRAMMAR-PAST-OBJECT-219, PA-GRAMMAR-PAST-NEG-218] -->

**ਕੀ** turns a past sentence into a question the same way it turns a present one:

> **ਕੀ ਤੁਸੀਂ ਰੋਟੀ ਖਾਧੀ?** — *kī tusīṁ roṭī khādhī?* — Did you eat the roti?

> **ਹਾਂ ਜੀ, ਮੈਂ ਰੋਟੀ ਖਾਧੀ।** — *hāṁ jī, maiṁ roṭī khādhī.* — Yes, I ate the roti.

> **ਨਹੀਂ ਜੀ, ਮੈਂ ਅਜੇ ਰੋਟੀ ਨਹੀਂ ਖਾਧੀ।** — *nahīṁ jī, maiṁ aje roṭī nahīṁ khādhī.* — No, I haven't eaten the roti yet.
