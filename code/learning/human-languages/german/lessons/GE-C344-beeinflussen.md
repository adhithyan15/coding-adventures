## You'll want to know: beeinflussen
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C344-EINFLUSS-01]; assesses=[] -->

**beeinflussen** — "to influence".

> Das Wetter beeinflusst meine Laune.
>
> *The weather influences my mood.*

Inseparable: *es beeinflusst, es hat beeinflusst*. It takes a direct object.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*beeinflussen* is built on *Einfluss* "influence", literally an "inflow", from *ein* "in" and *Fluss* "flow, river". *Einfluss* is usually explained as a rendering of Latin *influxus* "a flowing in"; EtymDB has no history for it.
