## You'll want to know: دلچسپ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C448-CURIOUS448-02]; assesses=[] -->

**دلچسپ** — *dilchasp* — "interesting".

> یہ کتاب بڑی دلچسپ ہے۔
>
> *yih kitāb baṛī dilchasp hai.*
>
> This book is very interesting.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**دلچسپ** (*dilchasp*) is usually traced to Persian *dilchasp*, "that clings to the heart": **دل**, "heart", and *chasp*, "sticking".
