## You'll want to know: వంశం
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C357-VAMSAM357-01]; assesses=[] -->

**వంశం** — *vaṁśaṁ* — "a lineage; a dynasty".

> ఆ రాజు గొప్ప వంశంలో పుట్టాడు.
>
> *ā rāju goppa vaṁśaṁlō puṭṭāḍu.*
>
> That king was born into a great dynasty.

Plural: వంశాలు, *vaṁśālu*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**వంశం** (*vaṁśaṁ*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *vaṁśa*, "a bamboo", and also "a family, a lineage" (CDIAL 11175).
