## You'll want to know: воевать
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C324-POBEDA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**воевать** (*voyevát*) — "to wage war, to fight".

> Его дедушка воевал четыре года.
>
> *Yevó dédushka voyevál chetýre góda.*
>
> His grandfather fought in the war for four years.

Imperfective; present *воюю*, *воюешь*. *Воевать с* + instrumental.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*воевать* is built on *воин* "a warrior", from Proto-Slavic *vojinŭ*, with the verb ending *-евать* (EtymDB).
