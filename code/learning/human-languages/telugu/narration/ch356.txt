Telugu, chapter 356: Celebrations.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ఉత్సవం (utsavaṁ) — a public celebration; a festival.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a birth anniversary, then the Telugu for a felicitation.

You'll want to know: ఉత్సవం.
ఉత్సవం — utsavaṁ — "a public celebration; a festival".
పట్టణంలో పెద్ద ఉత్సవం జరిగింది. paṭṭaṇaṁlō pedda utsavaṁ jarigindi. A big celebration took place in the town.
Plural: ఉత్సవాలు, utsavālu.

The word, taken apart.
ఉత్సవం (utsavaṁ) is borrowed from Sanskrit utsava, "an enterprise", and later "a festival" (CDIAL 1876).

Before the new one: say the Telugu for a public celebration, then the Telugu for to felicitate.

You'll want to know: గృహప్రవేశం.
గృహప్రవేశం — gṛhapravēśaṁ — "a housewarming".
మా కొత్త ఇంటి గృహప్రవేశం ఆదివారం జరిగింది. mā kotta iṇṭi gṛhapravēśaṁ ādivāraṁ jarigindi. The housewarming of our new house was on Sunday.
గృహం, a house, and a Sanskrit word for entering: the first entry into a new home.

The word, taken apart.
గృహప్రవేశం (gṛhapravēśaṁ) is borrowed from Sanskrit: gṛha, "a house", and praveśa, "an entering" (Monier-Williams).
