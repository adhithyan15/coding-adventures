## You'll want to know: పెనుగులాడు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C378-KOTLATA378-04]; assesses=[] -->

**పెనుగులాడు** — *penugulāḍu* — "to grapple; to wrestle free".

> ఆమె దొంగతో పెనుగులాడింది.
>
> *āme doṅgatō penugulāḍindi.*
>
> She grappled with the thief.

Past (I …): పెనుగులాడాను, *penugulāḍānu*.

With whom takes -తో.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**పెనుగులాడు** (*penugulāḍu*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 4160 lists Telugu *penãgu*, "to be twisted, to struggle, fight", here with *āḍu*.
