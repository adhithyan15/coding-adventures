## You'll want to know: o caiaque
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C506-AGUA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**o caiaque** — "kayak".

> No verão andamos de caiaque no rio.
>
> *In summer we go kayaking on the river.*

*Andar de caiaque* — to go kayaking.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*caiaque* is usually traced to an Inuit word for a small hunting boat, probably through French or English.
