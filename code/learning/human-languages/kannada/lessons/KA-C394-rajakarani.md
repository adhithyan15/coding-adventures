## You'll want to know: ರಾಜಕಾರಣಿ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C394-RAJAKIYA394-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ರಾಜಕಾರಣಿ** — *rājakāraṇi* — "a politician".

> ಆ ರಾಜಕಾರಣಿ ಹಳ್ಳಿಗಳಿಗೆ ಹೋಗಿ ಜನರ ಜೊತೆ ಮಾತಾಡಿದನು.
>
> *ā rājakāraṇi haḷḷigaḷige hōgi janara jote mātāḍidanu.*
>
> That politician went to the villages and talked with people.

Plural: ರಾಜಕಾರಣಿಗಳು, *rājakāraṇigaḷu*.

ರಾಜಕಾರಣ, politics, + -ಇ, the one who does it.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ರಾಜಕಾರಣಿ** (*rājakāraṇi*) is a modern Kannada word built on *rājakāraṇa*, "politics", from Sanskrit *rāja*, "king", and *kāraṇa*, "cause, means" (CDIAL 3057).
