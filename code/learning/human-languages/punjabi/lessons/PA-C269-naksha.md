## You'll want to know: ਨਕਸ਼ਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C269-WAY269-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ਨਕਸ਼ਾ** — *nakshā* — "a map".

> ਮੇਰੇ ਫ਼ੋਨ ਵਿੱਚ ਸ਼ਹਿਰ ਦਾ ਨਕਸ਼ਾ ਹੈ।
>
> *mere fon vich shahir dā nakshā hai.*
>
> There is a map of the town on my phone.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਨਕਸ਼ਾ** (*nakshā*) is a loan, usually traced to Persian *naqsha*, "a map, a plan", itself from Arabic.
