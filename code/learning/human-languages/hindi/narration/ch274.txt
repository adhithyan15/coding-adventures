Hindi, chapter 274: A heartbeat, Breath, An itch, A burning feeling, A swelling.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
धड़कन (dhaṛkan) — a heartbeat.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a disease, then the Hindi for an illness.

You'll want to know: धड़कन.
धड़कन — dhaṛkan — "a heartbeat".

The word, taken apart.
धड़कन (dhaṛkan) is a tadbhava, a word inherited and reshaped by sound change. It comes from dhaḍ, "throb, beat", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 6711). dhaṛkan is the noun of dhaṛaknā, "to throb".

The word, taken apart.
खुजली (khujlī) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from kharju, "itching, scab" (CDIAL 3827). CDIAL takes the word from Dravidian. The Sanskrit word is known only from the old word-lists.
