## Unreleased — Hindi taking now declares its stem and present forms

Chapter 35 already taught **ले**, **लेता**, **लेती**, and **लेते**, but its
headword exposed only **लेना** and the related noun **लाभ**. The lesson now
declares the complete taught family, spells out the regular derivation,
practises respectful **आप लेते हैं**, and recalls all four forms for reports
and generated study artifacts. Chapter 4 now builds **मिले** as one spelling
chunk instead of presenting its internal **ले** syllable like a standalone
word, preventing that script exercise from becoming a false vocabulary
forward reference.
