Punjabi, chapter 353: Out to Play.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ਲਾਟੂ (lāṭū) — a spinning top.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for a game piece, then the Punjabi for a prize.

You'll want to know: ਲਾਟੂ.
ਲਾਟੂ — lāṭū — "a spinning top".
ਮੇਰੀ ਛੋਟੀ ਭੈਣ ਕੋਲ ਇੱਕ ਲਾਲ ਲਾਟੂ ਹੈ। merī chhoṭī bhaiṇ kol ikk lāl lāṭū hai. My little sister has a red spinning top.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ਲਾਟੂ (lāṭū) is inherited from Sanskrit laṭati, "shakes" (a sense CDIAL infers); CDIAL lists Punjabi lāṭū, "spinning top", under it, with a query (CDIAL 10916).

Lesson 2 of 5.
ਮੁਕਾਬਲਾ (mukāblā) — a contest; a competition.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for a prize, then the Punjabi for a spinning top.

You'll want to know: ਮੁਕਾਬਲਾ.
ਮੁਕਾਬਲਾ — mukāblā — "a contest; a competition".
ਅੱਜ ਸਕੂਲ ਵਿੱਚ ਇੱਕ ਮੁਕਾਬਲਾ ਹੈ। ajj sakūl vich ikk mukāblā hai. There is a contest at school today.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ਮੁਕਾਬਲਾ (mukāblā) is a loan, usually traced through Persian to Arabic muqābala, "facing each other".

ਬੱਚੇ ਪਾਣੀ ਵਿੱਚ ਕੁੱਦਦੇ ਹਨ ਅਤੇ ਤਰਦੇ ਹਨ। bachche pāṇī vich kuddde han ate tarde han. The children jump into the water and swim.
Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਪਾਣੀ ਵਿੱਚ ਕੁੱਦਿਆ, maiṁ pāṇī vich kuddiā.

The word, taken apart.
ਕੁੱਦਣਾ (kuddṇā) is inherited from Sanskrit kūrdati, "leaps, jumps"; CDIAL lists Punjabi kuddṇā, "to leap", under it (CDIAL 3412).
