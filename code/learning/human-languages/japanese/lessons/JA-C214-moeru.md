# もえる (moeru) — to burn

## You'll want to know: もえる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C214-STORM214-04]; assesses=[] -->

**もえる** — *moeru* — "to burn".

> **かみは よく もえます。** — *kami wa yoku moemasu* — "Paper burns well."

Polite **もえます**, *moemasu*; an ichidan verb.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**もえる** (*moeru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi, and its older origin is not settled (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
