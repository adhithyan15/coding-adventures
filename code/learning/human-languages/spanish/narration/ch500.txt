Spanish, chapter 500: Vivir juntos.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
respetar — to respect.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for to feel like, then the Spanish for to share.

You'll want to know: respetar.
respetar — "to respect".
Respeta el horario de descanso. Respect the quiet hours.
The verb behind el respeto.

What you've built.
One more word for reading notices, forms and messages.

Guided Practice.

[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: respetar, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: a sentence of your own with respetar]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Spanish for to feel like, then the Spanish for to share, then say respetar again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does respetar mean? ("To respect".) Say it once more.
