## You'll want to know: dehnen
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C505-TRAINING-04]; assesses=[] -->

**dehnen** — "to stretch".

> Vor dem Laufen dehnt sie ihre Beine.
>
> *Before running she stretches her legs.*

*Sich dehnen* — to stretch yourself.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*dehnen* is inherited from Old High German *denen* "to stretch" (EtymDB).
