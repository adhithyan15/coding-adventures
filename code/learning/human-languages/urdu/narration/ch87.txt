Urdu, chapter 87: Song, Journey, Holiday, Love, Music.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
گیت (gīt) — a song.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for the world, then the Urdu for a colour.

You'll want to know: گیت.
گیت — gīt — "a song".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: gīt]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: gīt, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say gīt]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Urdu for the world, then the Urdu for a colour, then say gīt again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does گیت (gīt) mean? ("A song".) Say it once more.
