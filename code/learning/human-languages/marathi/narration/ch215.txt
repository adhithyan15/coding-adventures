Marathi, chapter 215: Player, Coach, Supporter, Arrival, Departure.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
खेळाडू (kheḷāḍū) — a player.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for wool, then the Marathi for silk.

You'll want to know: खेळाडू.
खेळाडू — kheḷāḍū — "a player".

The word, taken apart.
खेळाडू (kheḷāḍū) is खेळ, "play", with the ending -āḍū, "one who". CDIAL lists Marathi kheḷ under a reconstructed khēl-, "play" (CDIAL 3918).

चाहता — cāhtā — "a supporter, a fan".

The word, taken apart.
चाहता (cāhtā) is "one who likes", from Marathi cāhṇẽ, "to love, like", which CDIAL lists under a reconstructed cāh-, "see, look for, desire", whose source it calls uncertain (CDIAL 4775).
