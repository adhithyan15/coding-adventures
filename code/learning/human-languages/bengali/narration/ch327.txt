Bengali, chapter 327: Books to Remember.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
উপন্যাস (upônyās) — a novel.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a hidden treasure, then the Bengali for to vanish.

You'll want to know: উপন্যাস.
উপন্যাস — upônyās — "a novel".
আমি ছুটিতে একটা লম্বা উপন্যাস পড়লাম। āmi chhuṭite ekṭā lômbā upônyās pôṛlām. I read a long novel during the holidays.

The word, taken apart.
উপন্যাস (upônyās) is Sanskrit upanyāsa, 'a putting down, a placing near' (Monier-Williams); the sense 'a novel' is the modern Bengali one.

Lesson 3 of 5.
গ্রন্থ (grônthô) — a volume; a book (formal).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a novel, then the Bengali for a biography.

You'll want to know: গ্রন্থ.
গ্রন্থ — grônthô — "a volume; a book (formal)".
লাইব্রেরিতে কবির পুরনো গ্রন্থগুলো আছে। lāibrerite kôbir purôno grônthôgulo āchhe. The library has the poet's old volumes.

The word, taken apart.
গ্রন্থ (grônthô) is Sanskrit grantha, 'tying, binding, stringing together' (Monier-Williams): leaves strung together into a book. It is the formal word; বই is the everyday one.

Warm-up.

Before the new one: say the Bengali for a volume, then the Bengali for a chapter of a book.

You'll want to know: প্রকাশক.
প্রকাশক — prôkāshôk — "a publisher".
প্রকাশক লেখকের নতুন উপন্যাস ছাপলেন। prôkāshôk lekhôker nôtun upônyās chhāplen. The publisher printed the writer's new novel.

The word, taken apart.
প্রকাশক (prôkāshôk) is Sanskrit prakāśaka, 'clear, shining; well known' (Monier-Williams), the agent noun beside প্রকাশ করা; the sense 'a publisher' is the modern one.
