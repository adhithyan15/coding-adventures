## You'll want to know: o escândalo
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C284-ESCANDALO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**o escândalo** — "the scandal".

> O escândalo saiu em todos os jornais.
>
> *The scandal was in all the papers.*

*Que escândalo!* — how shocking!

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*escândalo* is a learned borrowing from Latin *scandalum* (EtymDB), from Greek *skándalon* "a trap, a stumbling block".
