## You'll want to know: mōs, mōris
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C386-SOCIETAS-03]; assesses=[] -->

**mōs, mōris** — "a custom, a way; (plural) character, morals".

> Hic mōs antīquus est.
>
> *This is an old custom.*

*mōs, mōris* m. The plural *mōrēs* is a person's ways, his character. *Mōs maiōrum*, the custom of the ancestors. English *moral* and *morals*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

The origin of *mōs* is not settled; Lewis & Short give no sure account. English *moral* comes from its adjective *mōrālis*.
