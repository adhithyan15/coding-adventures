## You'll want to know: resgatar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C306-RESGATE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**resgatar** — "to rescue; to ransom".

> Os bombeiros resgataram um gato da árvore.
>
> *The firefighters rescued a cat from the tree.*

Also to cash in: *resgatar um prémio*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*resgatar* is built on *resgate* (EtymDB), from Spanish *rescatar* "to rescue", which goes back to Vulgar Latin *recaptāre*, from Latin *captāre* "to catch".
