## You'll want to know: сознание
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C297-TRAVMA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**сознание** (*soznániye*) — "consciousness".

> Он потерял сознание на минуту.
>
> *On poteryál soznániye na minútu.*
>
> He lost consciousness for a minute.

*Потерять сознание* — to faint; *прийти в сознание* — to come round.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*сознание* is built on *сознать* "to realize", from *со-* and *знать* "to know", inherited from Proto-Slavic *znati*, PIE *ǵneh₃-* (EtymDB).
