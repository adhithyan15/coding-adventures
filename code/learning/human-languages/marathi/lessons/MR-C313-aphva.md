## You'll want to know: अफवा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C313-AFVA313-01]; assesses=[] -->

**अफवा** — *aphvā* — "a rumour".

> गावात भूकंपाची खोटी अफवा पसरली.
>
> *gāvāt bhūkampācī khoṭī aphvā pasarlī.*
>
> A false rumour of an earthquake spread through the village.

Feminine.

अफवा पसरणे, a rumour spreads; plural अफवा.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अफवा** (*aphvā*) is a loan from Persian *afvāh*, usually traced to Arabic *afwāh*, 'mouths': talk that goes from mouth to mouth.
