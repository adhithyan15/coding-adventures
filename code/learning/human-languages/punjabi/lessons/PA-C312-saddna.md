## You'll want to know: ਸੱਦਣਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C312-VISIT312-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ਸੱਦਣਾ** — *saddṇā* — "to invite".

> ਪਿਛਲੇ ਹਫ਼ਤੇ ਅਸੀਂ ਅਮਨ ਨੂੰ ਰੋਟੀ ਲਈ ਸੱਦਿਆ।
>
> *pichhle hafte asīṁ Aman nū̃ roṭī laī saddiā.*
>
> Last week we invited Aman for a meal.

Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਆਪਣੇ ਦੋਸਤ ਨੂੰ ਘਰ ਸੱਦਿਆ, *maiṁ āpṇe dost nū̃ ghar saddiā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਸੱਦਣਾ** (*saddṇā*) is inherited from Sanskrit *śabdayati*, "calls"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *saddṇā*, "called", under it (CDIAL 12300).

## What you've built
