## You'll want to know: ریتم
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C519-SINGING-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ریتم** (*ritm*) — "rhythm".

> بچه ها با ریتم موسیقی دست میزدند.
>
> *bachchehâ bâ ritm-e musiqi dast mizadand.*
>
> The children were clapping along to the music.

Plural *ritmhâ*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ritm* is a modern loan, usually traced to French *rythme*, 'rhythm'. EtymDB has no entry for the Persian word.
