## You'll want to know: patrouiller
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C291-GENDARMES-03]; assesses=[] -->

**patrouiller** — "to patrol".

> Des policiers patrouillent dans le parc la nuit.
>
> *Police officers patrol the park at night.*

*Patrouiller dans un quartier* — to patrol a neighbourhood.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*patrouiller* comes from Old French *patrouiller* "to paddle about, to tramp in mud" (EtymDB), built on *patte* "paw", whose origin is not settled.
