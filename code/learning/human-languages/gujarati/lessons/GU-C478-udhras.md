## You'll want to know: ઉધરસ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C478-KAPH478-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ઉધરસ** — *udhras* — "a lingering cough; a cough".

> દાદીને આખી રાત ઉધરસ આવી.
>
> *dādīne ākhī rāt udhras āvī.*
>
> Grandmother coughed all night.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ઉધરસ** (*udhras*) goes back to a reconstructed *\*uddhrasā-*, "coughing up"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati word, "cough" (CDIAL 2030).
