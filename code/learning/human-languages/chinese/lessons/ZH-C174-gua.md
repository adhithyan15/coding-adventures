## You'll want to know: guà
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C174-JOBS174-01]; assesses=[] -->

*guà* — "to hang; to hang up".

> *mén hòubian kěyǐ guà dàyī* — You can hang your coat behind the door.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*guà* is inherited from Middle Chinese *kweaH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'to hang', under a popular way of writing it.
