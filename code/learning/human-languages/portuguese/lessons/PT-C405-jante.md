## You'll want to know: a jante
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C405-PNEU-04]; assesses=[] -->

**a jante** — "the wheel rim".

> A jante ficou riscada quando bati no passeio.
>
> *The rim got scratched when I hit the kerb.*

In Brazil: *o aro* or *a roda*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*jante* was borrowed from French *jante* 'wheel rim', which EtymDB traces to Vulgar Latin *gambita*; the earlier history of that word is not settled.
