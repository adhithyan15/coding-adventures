## You'll want to know: فرصة
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C421-FURSA421-01]; assesses=[] -->

**فرصة** — *furṣa* — "a chance".

> كانت الرحلة فرصة جيدة للتعلم.
>
> *kānat ar-riḥla furṣa jayyida li-t-taʿallum.*
>
> The trip was a good chance to learn.

Plural: فرص, *furaṣ*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**فرصة** (*furṣa*) is built on the native Arabic root *f-r-ṣ*, "a turn, an opportunity" (Lane). A *furṣa* is one's turn, the right moment: a chance.
