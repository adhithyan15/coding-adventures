## You'll want to know: મૂર્તિ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C319-KILLO319-04]; assesses=[] -->

**મૂર્તિ** — *mūrti* — "a statue; an idol".

> કિલ્લામાં પથ્થરની એક સુંદર મૂર્તિ છે.
>
> *killāmā̃ paththarnī ek sundar mūrti che.*
>
> There is a beautiful stone statue in the fort.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**મૂર્તિ** (*mūrti*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *mūrti*, "a solid body, a form, an image" (Monier-Williams). It is a statue of a person, and in a temple the image of a god.
