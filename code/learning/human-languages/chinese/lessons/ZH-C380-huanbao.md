## You'll want to know: huánbǎo
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C380-AIR380-05]; assesses=[] -->

*huánbǎo* — "eco-friendly".

> *zuò dìtiě bǐ kāichē huánbǎo* — Taking the underground is greener than driving.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*huánbǎo* is built from *huán*, the first syllable of *huánjìng* 'the environment', and *bǎo* 'to keep', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *hwaen* and *pawX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'to guard': a short form of 'protecting the environment'.
