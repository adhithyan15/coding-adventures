## You'll want to know: છોલવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C166-CHOP166-02]; assesses=[] -->

**છોલવું** — *cholvũ* — "to peel".

> **હું બટાકા છોલું છું.** — *hũ baṭākā cholũ chũ* — "I peel the potatoes."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**છોલવું** (*cholvũ*) is a tadbhava. It comes from *\*chōll*, "to peel, skin", a reconstructed form that Burrow, cited in CDIAL, traces to a Dravidian source; CDIAL also compares Sanskrit *challi*, "bark" (CDIAL 5073).
