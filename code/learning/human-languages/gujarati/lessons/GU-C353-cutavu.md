# ચૂંટાવું (cū̃ṭāvũ) — to be elected

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-LEX-C353-MATDAN353-03, GU-LEX-C353-MATDAN353-04] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Gujarati for voting, then the Gujarati for a candidate.

## You'll want to know: ચૂંટાવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C353-MATDAN353-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ચૂંટાવું** — *cū̃ṭāvũ* — "to be elected".

> આ વખતે એક જુવાન ઉમેદવાર ચૂંટાશે.
>
> *ā vakhte ek juvān umedvār cū̃ṭāśe.*
>
> This time a young candidate will be elected.

Past (he …): તે ઉમેદવાર ચૂંટાયો, *te umedvār cū̃ṭāyo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ચૂંટાવું** (*cū̃ṭāvũ*) is the *-āvũ* partner of **ચૂંટવું** (*cū̃ṭvũ*), "to pick", which CDIAL traces to Sanskrit *cuṭáti*, "cuts off" (CDIAL 4857): to be picked by the voters.
