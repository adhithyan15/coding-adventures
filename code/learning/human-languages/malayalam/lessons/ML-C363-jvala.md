# ജ്വാല (jvāla) — a flame

## You'll want to know: ജ്വാല
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C363-JVALA363-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ജ്വാല** — *jvāla* — "a flame".

> മെഴുകുതിരിയുടെ ജ്വാല കാറ്റിൽ ഇളകി.
>
> *meḻukutiriyuṭe jvāla kāṟṟil iḷaki.*
>
> The candle's flame shook in the wind.

A literary and news word; plural ജ്വാലകൾ.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ജ്വാല** (*jvāla*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *jvālā*, "a flame, a blaze" (Monier-Williams), from the verb *jval*, "to burn brightly".
