## You'll want to know: bàba
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C32-FAMILY32-02]; assesses=[] -->

*bàba* — "dad; father".

> *wǒ bàba xǐhuan kàn shū* — My dad likes reading.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*bàba* is inherited from Middle Chinese *baX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), where the character is glossed 'father'. Spoken, it is doubled, like the word for mum.
