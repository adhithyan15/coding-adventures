Marathi, chapter 501: Hugs and Pats.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
स्पर्श (sparśa) — a touch.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to pamper, then the Marathi for to adopt.

You'll want to know: स्पर्श.
स्पर्श — sparśa — "a touch".
आईच्या स्पर्शाने बाळ शांत झालं. āīcyā sparśāne bāḷ śānt jhālaṁ. The baby calmed down at its mother's touch.
Masculine.
स्पर्श (sparśa) करणे, to touch.

The word, taken apart.
स्पर्श (sparśa) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit sparśa, "touch" (CDIAL 13809).

Lesson 2 of 5.
मिठी मारणे (miṭhī mārṇe) — to hug.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to adopt, then the Marathi for a touch.

You'll want to know: मिठी मारणे.
मिठी मारणे — miṭhī mārṇe — "to hug".
मित्राला पाहून त्याने मिठी मारली. mitrālā pāhūn tyāne miṭhī mārlī. When he saw his friend he gave him a hug.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने मिठी मारली, tyāne miṭhī mārlī.
मिठी, a hug; the one hugged takes -ला: आईला मिठी मारणे (miṭhī mārṇe).

The word, taken apart.
मिठी मारणे (miṭhī mārṇe) is a Marathi phrase built on मिठी, "a hug", whose origin is not settled, with मारणे as the light verb.

मुलीने मांजराला गोंजारलं. mulīne māñjrālā goñjārlaṁ. The girl stroked the cat.

Lesson 4 of 5.
थोपटणे (thopaṭṇe) — to pat.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to hug, then the Marathi for to stroke.

You'll want to know: थोपटणे.
थोपटणे — thopaṭṇe — "to pat".
आई बाळाला थोपटून झोपवते. āī bāḷālā thopṭūn jhopavte. Mother pats the baby to sleep.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने बाळाला थोपटलं, tyāne bāḷālā thopaṭlaṁ.
पाठ थोपटणे (thopaṭṇe), to pat someone on the back.

The word, taken apart.
थोपटणे (thopaṭṇe) is usually traced to the expressive thapp, "slap, pat", under which CDIAL lists Marathi thābṇẽ, "to pat" (CDIAL 6091).
