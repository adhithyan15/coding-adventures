## You'll want to know: جهیزیه
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C335-ARUSI-05]; assesses=[] -->

**جهیزیه** (*jahiziye*) — "a trousseau, the bride's household goods".

> مادرش جهیزیه را از یک سال پیش خریده بود.
>
> *Mâdarash jahiziye râ az yek sâl pish kharide bud.*
>
> Her mother had bought the trousseau a year before.

The furniture and household things that the bride's family provides.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*jahiziye* is built on *jahiz* "a trousseau", which EtymDB derives from Arabic *jihāz*, "equipment, outfit", with the ending *-iye*.
