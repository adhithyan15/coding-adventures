Persian, chapter 454: Repairs around town.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
تعمیرکار (ta'mirkâr) — a repairman, a repairer.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for the traffic police, then the Persian for to ring a number.

You'll want to know: تعمیرکار.
تعمیرکار (ta'mirkâr) — "a repairman, a repairer".
تعمیرکار (ta'mirkâr) یخچال ما را درست کرد. Ta'mirkâr yakhchâl-e mâ râ dorost kard. The repairman fixed our fridge.
ta'mir 'repair' + kâr 'worker'.

The word, taken apart.
ta'mirkâr is built from ta'mir 'repair', a loan usually traced to Arabic taʿmīr, and native kâr 'work, worker'.
