Japanese, chapter 227: Mistakes and Second Thoughts.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
まちがえる (machigaeru) — to make a mistake; to get wrong.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for to take an exam, then the Japanese for a mark.

You'll want to know: まちがえる.
まちがえる — machigaeru — "to make a mistake; to get wrong".
おとうとは よく なまえを まちがえます。 — otōto wa yoku namae o machigaemasu — "My younger brother often gets names wrong."
Polite まちがえます, machigaemasu; an ichidan verb.

The word, taken apart.
まちがえる (machigaeru) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed: it is built from ma, "interval, space", and chigaeru, the partner of chigau, "to differ" (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
