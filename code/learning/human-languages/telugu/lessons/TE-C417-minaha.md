# మినహా (minahā) — except for; apart from

## You'll want to know: మినహా
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C417-NIBANDHANA417-04]; assesses=[] -->

**మినహా** — *minahā* — "except for; apart from".

> ఆదివారం మినహా ప్రతి రోజు దుకాణం తెరిచి ఉంటుంది.
>
> *ādivāraṁ minahā prati rōju dukāṇaṁ terici uṇṭundi.*
>
> The shop is open every day except Sunday.

It follows the noun it leaves out: ఆదివారం మినహా, except Sunday.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**మినహా** (*minahā*) is usually traced to Hindustani *minhā*, "deducted, left out", a word of Arabic origin.
