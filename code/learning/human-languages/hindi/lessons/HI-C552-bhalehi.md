# भले ही (bhale hī) — even though; even if

## You'll want to know: भले ही
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C552-BAHARHAAL552-02]; assesses=[] -->

**भले ही** — *bhale hī* — "even though; even if".

> भले ही रास्ता लंबा है, फिर भी हम चलकर जाएँगे।
>
> *Bhale hī rāstā lambā hai, phir bhī ham calkar jāeṅge.*
>
> Even though the way is long, we will go on foot.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**भले ही** (*bhale hī*) is *bhale*, a form of *bhalā*, "good", which CDIAL derives from Sanskrit *bhalla*, "auspicious" (CDIAL 9408), with the emphatic *hī*: "well and good, even so".
