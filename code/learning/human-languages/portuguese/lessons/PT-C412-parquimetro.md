## You'll want to know: o parquímetro
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C412-RODAS-05]; assesses=[] -->

**o parquímetro** — "the parking meter".

> Pago o estacionamento no parquímetro.
>
> *I pay for the parking at the meter.*

Many cities now have an app as well.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*parquímetro* is built on English *parking* and *-metro* 'measurer', from Greek *métron* 'measure' (EtymDB).
