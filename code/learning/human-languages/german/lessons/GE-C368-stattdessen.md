## You'll want to know: stattdessen
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C368-STATTDESSEN-01]; assesses=[] -->

**stattdessen** — "instead".

> Der Film war ausverkauft. Stattdessen sind wir essen gegangen.
>
> *The film was sold out. Instead, we went out to eat.*

The verb comes right after it: *stattdessen sind wir …*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*stattdessen* joins *statt* "instead of", from *Statt* "place", Old High German *stat*, to *dessen* "of that": in the place of that. EtymDB has no entry for the compound.
