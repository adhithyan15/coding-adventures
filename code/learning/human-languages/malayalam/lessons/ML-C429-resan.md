## You'll want to know: റേഷൻ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C429-KSEMAM429-02]; assesses=[] -->

**റേഷൻ** — *ṟēṣan* — "a ration".

> റേഷൻ കടയിൽ ഇന്ന് അരി വന്നു.
>
> *ṟēṣan kaṭayil innŭ ari vannu.*
>
> Rice came to the ration shop today.

The ration shop, റേഷൻ കട, sells rice and sugar at fixed prices.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**റേഷൻ** (*ṟēṣan*) was borrowed from English *ration*.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
