## You'll want to know: জঙ্গল
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C432-CHURA432-05]; assesses=[] -->

**জঙ্গল** — *jônggôl* — "a forest; a jungle; overgrown land".

> গ্রামের পিছনে একটা বড় জঙ্গল আছে।
>
> *grāmer pichhône ekṭā bôṛo jônggôl āchhe.*
>
> Behind the village there is a big forest.

বন is a forest in general; জঙ্গল is wild and thick, and also an overgrown patch of weeds.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**জঙ্গল** (*jônggôl*) is inherited: CDIAL lists Bengali *jaṅgal*, "a forest", under Sanskrit *jāṅgala*, "arid, sparsely wooded; a desert region" (CDIAL 5177); the turn from dry land to thick forest happened in the modern languages, and English *jungle* is the same word.
