Russian, chapter 157: Light blue, Sweet, Sour, Tasty, Comfortable.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
голубой (golubóy) — light blue.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for dirty, then the Russian for empty.

You'll want to know: голубой.
голубой (golubóy) — "light blue".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: golubóy]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: golubóy, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say golubóy]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Russian for dirty, then the Russian for empty, then say golubóy again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does голубой (golubóy) mean? ("Light blue".) Say it once more.
