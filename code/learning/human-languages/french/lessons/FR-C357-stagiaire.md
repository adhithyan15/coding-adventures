## You'll want to know: le stagiaire
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C357-STAGE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**le stagiaire** — "the intern, the trainee".

> Le stagiaire travaille avec moi depuis lundi.
>
> *The intern has been working with me since Monday.*

The same form for a man or a woman: *une stagiaire*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*stagiaire* is built from *stage* and *-aire*. *stage* comes from Medieval Latin *stagium* "a period of residence" (EtymDB), usually linked to Latin *stāre* "to stand".
