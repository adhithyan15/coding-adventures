## You'll want to know: भीड़
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C204-OUT204-02]; assesses=[] -->

**भीड़** — *bhīṛ* — "a crowd".

> आज बाजार मांय घणी भीड़ है।
>
> *āj bājār mā̃y ghaṇī bhīṛ hai.*
>
> The market is very crowded today.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**भीड़** (*bhīṛ*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*bhiṭ*, "meet, throng"; CDIAL lists Old Marwari *bheṭi*, "meeting", and Hindi *bhīṛ*, "crowd", under it (CDIAL 9490).
