## You'll want to know: छलकना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C382-JALDBAZI382-03]; assesses=[] -->

**छलकना** — *chalaknā* — "to spill over, to slop".

> गिलास धीरे से उठाओ, वरना पानी छलक जाएगा।
>
> *gilās dhīre se uṭhāo, varnā pānī chalak jāegā.*
>
> Lift the glass slowly, or the water will spill.

Past (it …): वह दूध छलक गया, *vah dūdh chalak gayā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**छलकना** (*chalaknā*) is inherited from *\*chala*, "a splash", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 5002).
