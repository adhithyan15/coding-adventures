## You'll want to know: लुटणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C297-CHOR297-04]; assesses=[] -->

**लुटणे** — *luṭṇe* — "to loot; to plunder".

> रस्त्यात चार माणसांनी प्रवाशांना लुटलं.
>
> *rastyāt cār māṇsānnī pravāśānnā luṭlaṁ.*
>
> On the road four men robbed the travellers.

Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने लुटलं, *tyāne luṭlaṁ*.

The victims take -ला: प्रवाशांना लुटणे; goods are the object: दुकान लुटणे.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**लुटणे** (*luṭṇe*) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi *luṭṇe*, 'plunders', under *\*luṭṭati*, 'plunders' (CDIAL 11078).
