## You'll want to know: سیاست
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C409-HOKUMAT-02]; assesses=[] -->

**سیاست** (*siyâsat*) — "politics; a policy".

> پدرم به سیاست علاقه دارد.
>
> *pedaram be siyâsat 'alâqe dârad.*
>
> My father is interested in politics.

Plural *siyâsathâ*, policies. *Siyâsatmadâr* — a politician.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**سیاست** was borrowed from Arabic *siyāsa* "policy, politics" (EtymDB).
