# Put ਸਨ after a familiar plural habit

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-FORM-SAN-187, PA-PRON-OH-01, PA-LEX-KAMM-05, PA-FORM-KARDE-183] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say **ਉਹ ਕੰਮ ਕਰਦੇ**, then retrieve **ਸਨ**.

## Grammar Lens: a familiar habit moved into the past
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-FORM-SAN-187, PA-FORM-KARDE-183] -->

> **ਉਹ ਕੰਮ ਕਰਦੇ ਸਨ** — *oh kamm karde san* — they used to work

The familiar plural habitual predicate stays before **ਸਨ**. The final copula
moves the routine into the past.
