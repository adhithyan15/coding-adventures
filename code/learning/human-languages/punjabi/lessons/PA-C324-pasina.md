## You'll want to know: ਪਸੀਨਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C324-BREATH324-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ਪਸੀਨਾ** — *pasīnā* — "sweat".

> ਗਰਮੀ ਵਿੱਚ ਮੈਨੂੰ ਬਹੁਤ ਪਸੀਨਾ ਆਉਂਦਾ ਹੈ।
>
> *garmī vich mainū̃ bahut pasīnā āundā hai.*
>
> In the heat I sweat a lot.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਪਸੀਨਾ** (*pasīnā*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*prasvidana*, "sweating"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *pasīṇā*, "sweat", under it (CDIAL 8895).
