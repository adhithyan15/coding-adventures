# امید (ummīd) — hope

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[UR-LEX-C272-BANNA-01, UR-GRAMMAR-C268-OPINION-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say "I think she will come". (*mujhe lagtā hai ki voh āe gī*.) Now say it as a hope.

## You'll want to know: امید
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C272-UMMID-01]; assesses=[UR-GRAMMAR-KI-COMPLEMENTISER, UR-GRAMMAR-C265-FUTURE-01] -->

**امید** — *ummīd* — "hope". It is feminine.

> **مجھے امید ہے۔**
>
> *mujhe ummīd hai.* — "I hope so."

> **مجھے امید ہے کہ وہ آئے گی۔**
>
> *mujhe ummīd hai ki voh āe gī.* — "I hope she'll come."

Word for word, "to me there is hope": **مجھے**, then **کہ** (*ki*) and what you hope, in the future.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**امید** (*ummīd*) is usually traced to Persian *ummēd*, "hope".
