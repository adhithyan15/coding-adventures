Persian, chapter 535: A Cosy Home.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
شومینه (shumine) — a fireplace.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a cushion, then the Persian for a quilt.

You'll want to know: شومینه.
شومینه (shumine) — "a fireplace".
شبهای زمستان کنار شومینه مینشینیم. shabhâ-ye zemestân kenâr-e shumine mineshinim. On winter nights we sit by the fireplace.
An open fireplace; a stove is bokhâri.

The word, taken apart.
shumine is a modern loan, usually traced to French cheminée, 'fireplace'. EtymDB has no entry for it.

Lesson 2 of 6.
رختخواب (rakhtekhâb) — bedding; a bed (on the floor).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a quilt, then the Persian for a fireplace.

You'll want to know: رختخواب.
رختخواب (rakhtekhâb) — "bedding; a bed (on the floor)".
مادر رختخوابها را پهن کرد. mâdar rakhtekhâbhâ râ pahn kard. Mother laid out the bedding.
rakht 'clothes, things' + khâb 'sleep'. rakhtekhâb pahn kardan — to make up a bed on the floor.

The word, taken apart.
rakhtekhâb is built on two native Persian words, as EtymDB records: rakht, 'clothes, gear', and khâb, 'sleep' (EtymDB).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: دنج.
دنج (denj) — "cosy, quiet (of a place)".
این کافه یک جای دنج است. in kâfe yek jâ-ye denj ast. This café is a cosy spot.
Of a quiet, tucked-away place, not of a person.

The word, taken apart.
denj is an everyday Persian word for a quiet, tucked-away place, but EtymDB has no entry for it, and its origin is not settled.

Lesson 4 of 6.
خانه دار (khânedâr) — a homemaker.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for bedding, then the Persian for cosy.

You'll want to know: خانه دار.
خانه دار (khânedâr) — "a homemaker".
مادرم خانه دار است و پدرم کارمند است. mâdaram khânedâr ast va pedaram kârmand ast. My mother is a homemaker and my father is an office worker.
khâne 'house' + dâr 'keeping'. khânedâri — housekeeping.

The word, taken apart.
khânedâr is built on native Persian khâne, 'house', which EtymDB traces to Middle Persian (EtymDB), and dâr, the stem of dâshtan, 'to have, to keep'.

Lesson 5 of 6.
کدبانو (kadbânu) — a capable housewife; the lady of the house.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for cosy, then the Persian for a homemaker.

You'll want to know: کدبانو.
کدبانو (kadbânu) — "a capable housewife; the lady of the house".
مادربزرگ کدبانو است و همه چیز را خودش درست میکند. mâdarbozorg kadbânu ast va hame chiz râ khodash dorost mikonad. Grandmother is a wonderful homemaker and makes everything herself.
Warm praise. Built on kad 'house' and bânu 'lady'.

The word, taken apart.
kadbânu is a native Persian compound, usually explained as kad, 'house', and bânu, 'lady': the lady of the house. EtymDB has no entry for the compound.
