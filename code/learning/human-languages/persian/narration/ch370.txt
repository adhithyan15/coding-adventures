Persian, chapter 370: Results and consequences.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
پیامد (payâmad) — a consequence, an outcome.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to bring about, then the Persian for owing to.

You'll want to know: پیامد.
پیامد (payâmad) — "a consequence, an outcome".
این تصمیم پیامدهای مهمی داشت. in tasmim payâmadhâ-ye mohemmi dâsht. This decision had important consequences.
Native and formal: pay "trace, footstep" + âmad "came". Plural payâmadhâ.

The word, taken apart.
پیامد (payâmad) is a native Persian compound built from pay "footstep, trace" and âmad, the past stem of âmadan "to come", which EtymDB traces to PIE gʷem- "to step": what comes in the footsteps of an act.

Lesson 2 of 5.
عواقب ('avâqeb) — repercussions, bad consequences.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for owing to, then the Persian for a consequence.

You'll want to know: عواقب.
عواقب ('avâqeb) — "repercussions, bad consequences".
او به عواقب کارش فکر نکرد. u be 'avâqeb-e kârash fekr nakard. He did not think about the consequences of his action.
An Arabic broken plural of 'âqebat "end, outcome". In nearly every case the consequences are bad ones.

The word, taken apart.
عواقب ('avâqeb) is usually traced to Arabic 'awāqib, a broken plural of 'āqiba "end, outcome": what follows at the end.

Lesson 3 of 5.
در نتیجه (dar natije) — as a result, consequently.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a consequence, then the Persian for repercussions.

You'll want to know: در نتیجه.
در نتیجه (dar natije) — "as a result, consequently".
او زیاد کار کرد و در نتیجه بیمار شد. u ziyâd kâr kard va dar natije bimâr shod. He worked too much, and as a result he fell ill.
Dar "in" + natije "result". A whole clause follows; dar natije-ye + noun means "as a result of".

The word, taken apart.
در نتیجه (dar natije) joins the inherited Persian preposition dar "in" to natije, from Arabic natīja "result" (EtymDB): in the result.

Lesson 4 of 5.
منجر شدن (monjar shodan) — to lead to, to result in.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for repercussions, then the Persian for as a result.

You'll want to know: منجر شدن.
منجر شدن (monjar shodan) — "to lead to, to result in".
این اشتباه به یک دعوای بزرگ منجر شد. in eshtebâh be yek da'vâ-ye bozorg monjar shod. This mistake led to a big quarrel.
Takes be: be chizi monjar shodan. Formal. Stem shav-.

The word, taken apart.
منجر is usually traced to Arabic munjarr "drawn along"; with the Persian verb shodan "to become", one thing is drawn along into another.

Lesson 5 of 5.
نتیجه گرفتن (natije gereftan) — to conclude, to infer.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for as a result, then the Persian for to lead to.

You'll want to know: نتیجه گرفتن.
نتیجه گرفتن (natije gereftan) — "to conclude, to infer".
از حرفهای او نتیجه گرفتم که خسته است. az harfhâ-ye u natije gereftam ke khaste ast. From what he said I concluded that he was tired.
Natije "result" + gereftan "to take" (present stem gir-). Also to get results.

The word, taken apart.
نتیجه گرفتن (natije gereftan) is literally "to take a result": natije comes from Arabic natīja "result" (EtymDB), and gereftan "to take" is the inherited Persian verb.
