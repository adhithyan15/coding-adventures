## You'll want to know: مایکروویو
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C538-APPLIANCES-02]; assesses=[] -->

**مایکروویو** (*mâykroviv*) — "a microwave (oven)".

> مایکروویو ما خراب شد.
>
> *mâykroviv-e mâ kharâb shod.*
>
> Our microwave broke down.

Plural *mâykrovivhâ*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*mâykroviv* is a modern loan, usually traced to English *microwave*. EtymDB has no entry for it.
