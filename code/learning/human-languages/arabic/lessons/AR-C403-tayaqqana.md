# تيقن (tayaqqana) — to make certain

## You'll want to know: تيقن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C403-IRTIYAB403-01]; assesses=[] -->

**تيقن** — *tayaqqana* — "to make certain".

> تيقن الطبيب من سلامة الطفل.
>
> *tayaqqana aṭ-ṭabīb min salāmat aṭ-ṭifl.*
>
> The doctor made certain that the child was safe.

Present: يتيقن, *yatayaqqan*.

It takes من: تيقن من ….

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**تيقن** (*tayaqqana*) is built on the native Arabic root *y-q-n*, usually traced to the sense "to be sure". It is Form V, to make oneself sure: to make certain.
