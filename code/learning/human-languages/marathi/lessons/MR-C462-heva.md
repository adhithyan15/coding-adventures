## You'll want to know: हेवा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C462-HEVA462-01]; assesses=[] -->

**हेवा** — *hevā* — "envy".

> मित्राच्या नव्या सायकलचा त्याला हेवा वाटला.
>
> *mitrācyā navyā sāykalcā tyālā hevā vāṭlā.*
>
> He envied his friend's new bicycle.

Masculine.

हेवा वाटणे, to envy: the dative for the one who envies, the genitive for the thing. Milder than मत्सर.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**हेवा** (*hevā*) is a Marathi noun whose origin is not settled.
