## You'll want to know: چھلکانا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C509-CHHACHH509-05]; assesses=[] -->

**چھلکانا** — *chhalkānā* — "to spill (a liquid)".

> بچے نے میز پر دودھ چھلکایا۔
>
> *bachche ne mez par dūdh chhalkāyā.*
>
> The child spilt milk on the table.

Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے پانی چھلکایا, *us ne pānī chhalkāyā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**چھلکانا** (*chhalkānā*) is the causative of *chhalaknā*, "to be spilt", which CDIAL lists under *\*chala*, a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 5002).
