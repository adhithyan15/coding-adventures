## You'll want to know: دمہ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C475-LONG475-02]; assesses=[] -->

**دمہ** — *damā* — "asthma".

> دمے کے مریض کو سانس لینے میں مشکل ہوتی ہے۔
>
> *dame ke marīz ko sāṅs lene meṅ mushkil hotī hai.*
>
> Someone with asthma finds it hard to breathe.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**دمہ** (*damā*) is usually traced to Persian *dama*, "asthma", beside *dam*, "breath" (**دم**).
