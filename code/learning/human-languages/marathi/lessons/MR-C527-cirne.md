## You'll want to know: चिरणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C527-SHIJAVNE527-01]; assesses=[] -->

**चिरणे** — *cirṇe* — "to chop; to slice".

> भाजीसाठी मी दोन कांदे चिरले.
>
> *bhājīsāṭhī mī don kānde cirle.*
>
> I chopped two onions for the vegetable dish.

Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने कांदा चिरला, *tyāne kāndā cirlā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**चिरणे** (*cirṇe*) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi *cirṇẽ*, "to split", under *\*cīrayati*, "splits" (CDIAL 4844).
