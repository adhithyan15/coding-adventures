# బేరమాడు (bēramāḍu) — to haggle

## You'll want to know: బేరమాడు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C484-AMMAKAM484-05]; assesses=[] -->

**బేరమాడు** — *bēramāḍu* — "to haggle".

> అమ్మ కూరగాయల ధర కోసం బేరమాడింది.
>
> *amma kūragāyala dhara kōsaṁ bēramāḍindi.*
>
> Mother haggled over the price of the vegetables.

Past (I …): బేరమాడాను, *bēramāḍānu*.

బేరం, a bargain, + ఆడు, to play: to play at bargaining.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**బేరమాడు** (*bēramāḍu*) is built on బేరం, "a bargain", whose origin is not settled, with the native ఆడు, "to play, to do".
