Urdu, chapter 459: Generous and Mean.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
فیاض (fayyāz) — generous; bountiful.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for to snap at, then the Urdu for to bear.

You'll want to know: فیاض.
فیاض — fayyāz — "generous; bountiful".
ہمارے پڑوسی بڑے فیاض ہیں۔ hamāre paṛosī baṛe fayyāz haiṅ. Our neighbours are very generous.

The word, taken apart.
فیاض (fayyāz) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic fayyāḍ, "overflowing, bountiful".

Lesson 2 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: خود غرض.
خود غرض — khud gharaz — "selfish".
وہ خود غرض ہے، کسی کی مدد نہیں کرتا۔ voh khud gharaz hai, kisī kī madad nahīṅ kartā. He is selfish and helps nobody.

The word, taken apart.
خود غرض (khud gharaz) is built from خود, "oneself", usually traced to Persian khud, and gharaz, "an aim", usually traced to Arabic gharaḍ: someone whose aim is himself.
