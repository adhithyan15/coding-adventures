Portuguese, chapter 127: Narrow, Angry, Bitter, Salty, Light.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
estreito — narrow.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for round, then the Portuguese for wide.

You'll want to know: estreito.
estreito — "narrow".
