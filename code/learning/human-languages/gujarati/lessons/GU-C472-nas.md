## You'll want to know: નસ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C472-NAS472-01]; assesses=[] -->

**નસ** — *nas* — "a vein; a nerve".

> દાક્તરે મારા હાથની નસ તપાસી.
>
> *dāktare mārā hāthnī nas tapāsī.*
>
> The doctor examined the vein in my arm.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**નસ** (*nas*) is inherited from Sanskrit *snasā*, "tendon, muscle"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati word, "vein, nerve, tendon" (CDIAL 13784).
