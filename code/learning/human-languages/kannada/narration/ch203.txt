Kannada, chapter 203: To grow, To bloom, To wilt, To blow, To melt.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಬೆಳೆಸು (beḷesu) — to grow (plants), to raise.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to contact, then the Kannada for to discuss.

You'll want to know: ಬೆಳೆಸು.
ಬೆಳೆಸು — beḷesu — "to grow (plants), to raise".

The word, taken apart.
ಬೆಳೆಸು (beḷesu) is native Dravidian, from beḷe, "to grow", and DEDR 5437 lists beḷasu, "to cause to grow", itself; the entry also has Tamil viḷai.

ಬೀಸು (bīsu) is native Dravidian, one word with Tamil vīcu and Telugu vīcu (DEDR 5450). DEDR lists bīsu, "to swing, fan, blow as the wind": the old v- has become b-. DEDR 5450 compares Sanskrit vīj.
