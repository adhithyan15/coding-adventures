## You'll want to know: 人口
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C374-SOCIETY374-01]; assesses=[] -->

**人口** — *rénkǒu* — "population".

> *zhège chéngshì de rénkǒu yǒu yìqiān wàn* — This city has a population of ten million.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**人口** (*rénkǒu*) is built from 人 'a person' and 口 'a mouth', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *nyin* and *khuwX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'what a person speaks and eats with'. Counting the mouths of a place to mean its people is not given there.
