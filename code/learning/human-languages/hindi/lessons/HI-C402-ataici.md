## You'll want to know: अटैची
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C402-GATHRI402-02]; assesses=[] -->

**अटैची** — *aṭaicī* — "a suitcase".

> मेरी अटैची बहुत भारी है।
>
> *merī aṭaicī bahut bhārī hai.*
>
> My suitcase is very heavy.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अटैची** (*aṭaicī*) is borrowed from English *attaché*, short for *attaché case*; in India it names any small suitcase, and it is feminine.
