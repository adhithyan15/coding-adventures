# పెళ్ళిరోజు (peḷḷirōju) — a wedding anniversary

## You'll want to know: పెళ్ళిరోజు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C355-VARSHIKAM355-02]; assesses=[] -->

**పెళ్ళిరోజు** — *peḷḷirōju* — "a wedding anniversary".

> నిన్న అమ్మానాన్నల పెళ్ళిరోజు.
>
> *ninna ammānānnala peḷḷirōju.*
>
> Yesterday was my parents' wedding anniversary.

పెళ్ళి, a wedding, and రోజు, a day: the day of the wedding, each year.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**పెళ్ళిరోజు** (*peḷḷirōju*) is a Telugu compound: native *peḷḷi*, "a marriage" (DEDR 4395), and *rōju*, "a day".
