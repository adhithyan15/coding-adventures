# അഴിമുഖം (aḻimukhaṁ) — an estuary; a river mouth

## You'll want to know: അഴിമുഖം
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C497-TIRAM497-02]; assesses=[] -->

**അഴിമുഖം** — *aḻimukhaṁ* — "an estuary; a river mouth".

> പുഴ കടലിൽ ചേരുന്നത് അഴിമുഖത്താണ്.
>
> *puḻa kaṭalil cērunnatŭ aḻimukhattāṇŭ.*
>
> The river meets the sea at the estuary.

അഴി, an opening to the sea, plus മുഖം, a face: the face of the river towards the sea.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**അഴിമുഖം** (*aḻimukhaṁ*) is a Malayalam compound built from അഴി, a bar or opening where a river meets the sea, whose origin is not settled, and മുഖം, a face, from Sanskrit *mukha* (CDIAL 10158).
