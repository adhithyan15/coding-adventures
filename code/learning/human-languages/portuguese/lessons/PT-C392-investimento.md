## You'll want to know: o investimento
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C392-INVESTIR-02]; assesses=[] -->

**o investimento** — "the investment".

> Comprar casa é um bom investimento.
>
> *Buying a house is a good investment.*

*Fazer um investimento* — to make an investment.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*investimento* is built on *investir* "to invest" with *-mento*; the verb comes from Latin *investīre* "to clothe, to cover" (EtymDB), on *vestis* "garment".
