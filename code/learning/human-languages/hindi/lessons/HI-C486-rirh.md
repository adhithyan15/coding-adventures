## You'll want to know: रीढ़
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C486-DUKHNA486-04]; assesses=[] -->

**रीढ़** — *rīṛh* — "the spine; the backbone".

> भारी बोझ उठाने से मज़दूर की रीढ़ में दर्द है।
>
> *bhārī bojh uṭhāne se mazdūr kī rīṛh meṁ dard hai.*
>
> The labourer's back hurts from lifting heavy loads.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**रीढ़** (*rīṛh*) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Hindi *rīṛh*, "backbone", under Sanskrit *rīḍhaka-* (CDIAL 10749a). *rīṛh kī haḍḍī*, "the bone of the back", is the spine itself.
