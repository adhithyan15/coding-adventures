## You'll want to know: ほうりつ

**ほうりつ** — *hōritsu* — "a law".

> **ほうりつが かわったので、ぜいきんが たかく なりました。** — *hōritsu ga kawatta node, zeikin ga takaku narimashita* — "The law changed, so taxes went up."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ほうりつ** (*hōritsu*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *hō*, "law" (Middle Chinese *pjop*) and *ritsu*, "regulation" (*lwit*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
