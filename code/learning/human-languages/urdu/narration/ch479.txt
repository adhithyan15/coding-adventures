Urdu, chapter 479: In Hospital.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
داخل کرنا (dākhil karnā) — to admit to hospital.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for pus, then the Urdu for to pick at.

You'll want to know: داخل کرنا.
داخل کرنا — dākhil karnā — "to admit to hospital".
ڈاکٹروں نے دادا کو ہسپتال میں داخل کیا۔ ḍākṭaroṅ ne dādā ko haspatāl meṅ dākhil kiyā. The doctors admitted my grandfather to hospital.
Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے مریض کو داخل کیا, us ne marīz ko dākhil kiyā.

The word, taken apart.
داخل کرنا (dākhil karnā) is built from داخل, usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic dākhil, "entering", and کرنا, "to do".

What you've built.
One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
