Kannada, chapter 517: Getting Ahead.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಬಡ್ತಿ (baḍti) — a promotion.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for an expert, then the Kannada for expertise.

You'll want to know: ಬಡ್ತಿ.
ಬಡ್ತಿ — baḍti — "a promotion".
ಈ ವರ್ಷ ನನ್ನ ಅಕ್ಕನಿಗೆ ಬಡ್ತಿ ಸಿಕ್ಕಿತು. ī varṣa nanna akkanige baḍti sikkitu. This year my elder sister got a promotion.
ಬಡ್ತಿ (baḍti) ಕೊಡು, to promote; ಬಡ್ತಿ (baḍti) ಪಡೆ, to be promoted.

The word, taken apart.
ಬಡ್ತಿ (baḍti) is usually traced to Hindustani baṛhtī, "growth, increase"; the path into Kannada is not settled.

ಪ್ರಗತಿ — pragati — "progress".
ಊರಿನಲ್ಲಿ ರಸ್ತೆ ಕೆಲಸ ಪ್ರಗತಿಯಲ್ಲಿದೆ. ūrinalli raste kelasa pragatiyallide. Road work in the town is in progress.

The word, taken apart.
ಪ್ರಗತಿ (pragati) is built from Sanskrit pra-, "forward", and gati, "going, progress" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 3 of 5.
ಮಹತ್ವಾಕಾಂಕ್ಷೆ (mahatvākāṅkṣe) — ambition.

You'll want to know: ಮಹತ್ವಾಕಾಂಕ್ಷೆ.
ಮಹತ್ವಾಕಾಂಕ್ಷೆ — mahatvākāṅkṣe — "ambition".
ಅವನಿಗೆ ದೊಡ್ಡ ವಿಜ್ಞಾನಿ ಆಗುವ ಮಹತ್ವಾಕಾಂಕ್ಷೆ ಇದೆ. avanige doḍḍa vijñāni āguva mahatvākāṅkṣe ide. He has the ambition of becoming a great scientist.
ಮಹತ್ವ, greatness + ಆಕಾಂಕ್ಷೆ, desire.

The word, taken apart.
ಮಹತ್ವಾಕಾಂಕ್ಷೆ (mahatvākāṅkṣe) is a learned compound built from Sanskrit mahattva, "greatness" (CDIAL 9933), and ākāṅkṣā, "desire, wish" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 4 of 5.
ಸಾಧಿಸು (sādhisu) — to achieve.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for progress, then the Kannada for ambition.

You'll want to know: ಸಾಧಿಸು.
ಸಾಧಿಸು — sādhisu — "to achieve".
ಕಷ್ಟಪಟ್ಟು ಅವಳು ತನ್ನ ಕನಸನ್ನು ಸಾಧಿಸಿದಳು. kaṣṭapaṭṭu avaḷu tanna kanasannu sādhisidaḷu. Through hard work she achieved her dream.
Past (I …): ಸಾಧಿಸಿದೆ, sādhiside.
The noun is ಸಾಧನೆ, an achievement.

The word, taken apart.
ಸಾಧಿಸು (sādhisu) is built on Sanskrit sādh-, "to accomplish", whose noun sādhana means "furthering, completion" (CDIAL 13334), with the verb ending -isu.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ಗಳಿಸು (gaḷisu) — to gain; to earn.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for ambition, then the Kannada for to achieve.

You'll want to know: ಗಳಿಸು.
ಗಳಿಸು — gaḷisu — "to gain; to earn".
ಅವನು ಊರಿನಲ್ಲಿ ಒಳ್ಳೆಯ ಹೆಸರು ಗಳಿಸಿದನು. avanu ūrinalli oḷḷeya hesaru gaḷisidanu. He earned a good name in the town.
Past (I …): ಗಳಿಸಿದೆ, gaḷiside.
Money, fame or marks: ಅಂಕ ಗಳಿಸು (gaḷisu), to score marks.

The word, taken apart.
ಗಳಿಸು (gaḷisu) is a native Kannada verb, "to gain, to earn"; its origin is not settled.
