## You'll want to know: इजाजत
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C267-RAJA267-01]; assesses=[] -->

**इजाजत** — *ijājat* — "permission".

> म्हैं इजाजत ली अर घरै गयो।
>
> *mhaĩ ijājat lī ar gharai gayo.*
>
> I got permission and went home.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**इजाजत** (*ijājat*) is usually traced, through Persian *ijāzat*, to Arabic *ijāza*, "permission". It is feminine, so **म्हैं इजाजत ली**, I got permission.
