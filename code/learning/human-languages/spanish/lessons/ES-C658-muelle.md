## You'll want to know: el muelle
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C658-BARCO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**el muelle** — "the quay, the dock; the spring (metal)".

> El barco espera en el muelle.
>
> *The boat is waiting at the quay.*

A second *muelle* is a metal spring.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*muelle* for a quay comes from Catalan *moll*, from Latin *mōlēs* "a mass; a dam, a pier" (Lewis & Short). The *muelle* that means a spring is a different word, from Latin *mollis* "soft".
