## You'll want to know: die Landung
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C418-BORD-05]; assesses=[] -->

**die Landung** — "the landing".

> Die Landung war ruhig und pünktlich.
>
> *The landing was smooth and on time.*

Plural: *die Landungen*. *Landen* — to land.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Landung* is built on *landen* 'to land', from *Land* 'land', inherited from Proto-Germanic *landą* (EtymDB).
