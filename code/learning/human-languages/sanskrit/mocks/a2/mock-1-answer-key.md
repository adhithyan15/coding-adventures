## Writing

Task 1 must identify the broken fastening, request a remedy, and ask when to
visit. Task 2 must contrast the old routine with a change, give two results,
and state a recommendation. Apply the analytic rubric, including external
sandhi, endings, and headline control; no fixed wording is required and no
scorer-only stem may be shown to the learner.

## Speaking

The interview needs six relevant answers with reasons or details. The account
must sustain 90–120 seconds with a clear sequence. The role-play must identify
both booking problems, obtain missing information, compare two offers, choose
one, and confirm date and meal. Accept one documented pronunciation tradition
when consistently applied and score the recording independently.
