Lesson 1 of 6.
দুর্যোগ (durjog) — a natural disaster.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a commitment, then the Bengali for to shrink back.

You'll want to know: দুর্যোগ.
দুর্যোগ — durjog — "a natural disaster".
দুর্যোগ (durjog) হলে আমরা স্কুলে আশ্রয় নেব। Durjog hôle āmrā skule āshrôy nebô. If disaster strikes, we will take shelter in the school.

The word, taken apart.
দুর্যোগ (durjog) is Sanskrit duryoga, "a bad contrivance" (Monier-Williams): dur-, "bad", and yoga, "joining"; the sense "a natural disaster" is the modern language's own.
