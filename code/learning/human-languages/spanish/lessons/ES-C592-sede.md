## You'll want to know: la sede
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C592-EMPRESA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**la sede** — "the head office, the headquarters".

> La sede de la empresa está en Madrid.
>
> *The company's head office is in Madrid.*

*La sede central* — the head office.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*sede* is a learned borrowing from Latin *sedes* 'seat, home' (EtymDB), built on *sedere* 'to sit'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.
