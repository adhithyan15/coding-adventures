## You'll want to know: ઉજ્જડ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C390-PAK390-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ઉજ્જડ** — *ujjaḍ* — "barren".

> જો આપણે જંગલ કાપીશું તો આ ધરતી ઉજ્જડ થઈ જશે.
>
> *jo āpaṇe jaṅgal kāpīśũ to ā dhartī ujjaḍ thaī jaśe.*
>
> If we cut down the forest, this land will turn barren.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ઉજ્જડ** (*ujjaḍ*) CDIAL lists the Gujarati word, "waterless, barren", under a reconstructed *ujjaṭa-*, "uprooted, laid waste" (CDIAL 1660b).
