Spanish, chapter 630: La economía.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
la economía — the economy; economics.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for the investment, then the Spanish for to finance.

You'll want to know: la economía.
la economía — "the economy; economics".
La economía va mejor. The economy is doing better.
Also the subject at university.

The word, taken apart.
economía is a learned borrowing from Latin oeconomia, taken from Greek oikonomia 'household management', built from oikos 'house' and nomos 'custom, rule' (EtymDB).

Lesson 3 of 5.
la exportación — the export.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for the economy, then the Spanish for economic.

You'll want to know: la exportación.
la exportación — "the export".
La exportación de aceite crece. Olive oil exports are growing.
Exportar — to export; la importación — the import.

The word, taken apart.
exportación is built on exportar, a learned borrowing from Latin exportare 'to carry out', from ex and portare 'to carry' (EtymDB).
