## You'll want to know: o cavalete
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C505-PINTURA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**o cavalete** — "easel".

> Pôs o cavalete perto da janela para ter luz.
>
> *She put the easel near the window to get light.*

Literally "a little horse": it stands on legs.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*cavalete* is usually traced to Italian *cavalletto* "a little horse, a trestle", from Latin *caballus* "horse" (Lewis & Short).
