Sanskrit, chapter 544: Each in Turn.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
परस्परम् (parasparam) — each other.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for insistence, then the Sanskrit for by oneself.

You'll want to know: परस्परम्.
परस्परम् — parasparam — "each other".
यदि भ्रातरौ परस्परं स्निह्यतः, तर्हि माता प्रसन्ना भवति। yadi bhrātarau parasparaṃ snihyataḥ, tarhi mātā prasannā bhavati. If the two brothers love each other, my mother is happy.

The word, taken apart.
परस्परम् (parasparam) is built from para, "other", said twice, the first time in its nominative paras; MW gives paraspara as mutual, each other's (MW).

Lesson 2 of 5.
युगपत् (yugapat) — at the same time.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for by oneself, then the Sanskrit for each other.

You'll want to know: युगपत्.
युगपत् — yugapat — "at the same time".
यदि सर्वे युगपत् वदन्ति, तर्हि अहं न अवगच्छामि। yadi sarve yugapat vadanti, tarhi ahaṃ na avagacchāmi. If everyone talks at the same time, I don't understand.

The word, taken apart.
युगपत् (yugapat) is built from yuga, "a yoke", and pad, "a foot": side by side in one yoke; MW gives yugapad as together, at the same time (MW).

Lesson 4 of 5.
पृथक् (pṛthak) — separately.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for at the same time, then the Sanskrit for together.

You'll want to know: पृथक्.
पृथक् — pṛthak — "separately".
यदि पुस्तकानि पृथक् सन्ति, तर्हि तानि पेटिकायां स्थापयतु। yadi pustakāni pṛthak santi, tarhi tāni peṭikāyāṃ sthāpayatu. If the books are lying about separately, put them in the box.

The word, taken apart.
पृथक् (pṛthak) is built on the root √pṛth (or prath), "to spread", and añc, "to turn", as MW gives it: spread apart; MW gives pṛthak as widely apart, separately, singly (MW).

Lesson 5 of 5.
सम्प्रति (samprati) — at present.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for together, then the Sanskrit for separately.

You'll want to know: सम्प्रति.
सम्प्रति — samprati — "at present".
यदि सम्प्रति वर्षा भवति, तर्हि वयं पश्चात् गमिष्यामः। yadi samprati varṣā bhavati, tarhi vayaṃ paścāt gamiṣyāmaḥ. If it is raining now, we will go later.

The word, taken apart.
सम्प्रति (samprati) is built on sam and prati, "towards, against"; MW gives samprati as directly opposite, at the right time, and now, at present (MW).
