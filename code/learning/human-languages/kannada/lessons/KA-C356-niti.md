## You'll want to know: ನೀತಿ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C356-JANAPADA356-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ನೀತಿ** — *nīti* — "a moral; a principle".

> ಈ ಕಥೆಯ ನೀತಿ ಏನು?
>
> *ī katheya nīti ēnu?*
>
> What is the moral of this story?

ನೀತಿ ಕಥೆ is a fable. ನೀತಿ also means a policy: ಸರ್ಕಾರದ ನೀತಿ.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ನೀತಿ** (*nīti*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *nīti*, "conduct, guidance, wise behaviour" (CDIAL 7546), from *nī*, "to lead".
