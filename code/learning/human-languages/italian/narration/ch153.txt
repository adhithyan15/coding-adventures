Italian, chapter 153: Rarely, At once, Finally, Meanwhile, By now.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
raramente — rarely.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for sleep, then the Italian for a dream.

You'll want to know: raramente.
raramente — "rarely".

The word, taken apart.
raramente — built from raro, "rare" from Latin rarus, "thin, scattered" (EtymDB 2.1), with -mente, the adverb ending, which began as Latin mente, "with a mind".
