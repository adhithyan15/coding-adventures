## You'll want to know: समझणो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C157-VERB157-01]; assesses=[MW-GRAMMAR-C154-PRES-1SG-01, MW-GRAMMAR-C157-KONI-VERB-01, MW-SCRIPT-THODU-01] -->

**समझणो** — *samajhṇo* — "to understand".

> **हूं थोड़ु समझूं हूं।**
>
> *hū̃ thoṛu samajhū̃ hū̃.* — I understand a little.

> **हूं कोनी समझूं।**
>
> *hū̃ konī samajhū̃.* — I don't understand.

It holds a letter you have not written yet: *jh*, a **ज** with a breath after it.
The next lesson puts it in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**समझणो** (*samajhṇo*) is inherited from Sanskrit *sambudhyate*, "wakes up,
understands". CDIAL lists Old Marwari *samajhaï*, "understands" (CDIAL 12959).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MW-LEX-C157-VERB157-01, MW-GRAMMAR-C154-PRES-3SG-01, MW-GRAMMAR-C154-PRES-POLITE-01] -->

Its stem **समझ** ends in a consonant, so it goes like **बोलणो**: **समझूं हूं**,
**समझै है**, **समझो हो**.
