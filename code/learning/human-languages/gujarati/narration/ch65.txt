Gujarati, chapter 65: Sea, Lake and Well.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
દરિયો (dariyo) — the sea.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a forest, then the Gujarati for a stone.

You'll want to know: દરિયો.
દરિયો (dariyo) — "the sea".

What you've built.
One more word for sea, lake and well.

Guided Practice.

[your turn — say: dariyo]

[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does દરિયો (dariyo) mean? ("The sea".) Say it once more.
