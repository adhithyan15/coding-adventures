Marwadi (Marwari), chapter 219: Guests Are Coming.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
पधारणो (padhārṇo) — to come (said with respect).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for to depart, then the Marwadi for to take to a place.

You'll want to know: पधारणो.
पधारणो — padhārṇo — "to come (said with respect)".
आप काल पधारो हो? āp kāl padhāro ho? Will you be coming tomorrow?
Past (he …): वो घरै पधार्यो, vo gharai padhāryo.

The word, taken apart.
पधारणो (padhārṇo) is inherited from a reconstructed paddhārayati, "steps"; CDIAL lists the Old Marwari verb padhāraï, "comes, goes" (CDIAL 7768). It is the polite word for a guest's coming.

Lesson 2 of 5.
भेट (bheṭ) — a gift (brought on a visit); a meeting.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for to take to a place, then the Marwadi for to come (said with respect).

You'll want to know: भेट.
भेट — bheṭ — "a gift (brought on a visit); a meeting".
हूं काल भेट लासूं। hū̃ kāl bheṭ lāsū̃. Tomorrow I'll bring a gift.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
भेट (bheṭ) is inherited from a reconstructed bhiṭ, "meet"; CDIAL lists Old Marwari bheṭi, "a meeting", and Hindi bheṭī and Sindhi bheṭa, "a present" (CDIAL 9490): the gift you bring to a meeting.

Lesson 3 of 5.
हफ्तो (haphto) — a week.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for to come (said with respect), then the Marwadi for a gift.

You'll want to know: हफ्तो.
हफ्तो — haphto — "a week".
हूं एक हफ्तो गांव मांय रैसूं। hū̃ ek haphto gā̃v mā̃y raisū̃. I'll stay a week in the village.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
हफ्तो (haphto) is usually traced to Persian hafta, "a week", from haft, "seven"; Marwadi ends it in -o like its own nouns.

Lesson 4 of 5.
तीज (tīj) — the Teej festival; the third day of a lunar fortnight.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for a gift, then the Marwadi for a week.

You'll want to know: तीज.
तीज — tīj — "the Teej festival; the third day of a lunar fortnight".
परसूं तीज है। parsū̃ tīj hai. The day after tomorrow is Teej.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
तीज (tīj) is inherited from Sanskrit tṛtīyā, "the third day of the lunar fortnight" (CDIAL 5920, which lists Marwari tīj). The women's festival of Teej falls on such a third day, in the rains.

Lesson 5 of 5.
उतावळो (utāvḷo) — in a hurry; hasty.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for a week, then the Marwadi for the Teej festival.

You'll want to know: उतावळो.
उतावळो — utāvḷo — "in a hurry; hasty".
हूं उतावळो हूं क्यूंकै बस आथण छूटै है। hū̃ utāvḷo hū̃ kyū̃kai bas āthaṇ chhūṭai hai. I'm in a hurry because the bus leaves in the evening.

The word, taken apart.
उतावळो (utāvḷo) goes back to a reconstructed uttāpala, "impetuous", built on Sanskrit uttāpa, "heat"; CDIAL lists a Gujarati word for impetuous under it. CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry (CDIAL 1788).
