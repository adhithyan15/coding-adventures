Portuguese, chapter 520: Estreias.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
o cenário — set, scenery; setting.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for audience, then the Portuguese for applause.

You'll want to know: o cenário.
o cenário — "set, scenery; setting".
O cenário da peça era uma cozinha antiga. The play's set was an old kitchen.
Also a setting or a scenario: um cenário bonito.

The word, taken apart.
cenário is built on Latin scaena "a stage, a scene" (EtymDB), from Greek skēnḗ "a tent, a stage building".

Lesson 4 of 5.
a estreia — premiere, opening night.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for to stage, then the Portuguese for staging.

You'll want to know: a estreia.
a estreia — "premiere, opening night".
A estreia do filme é na sexta-feira. The film's premiere is on Friday.
Also a debut: a estreia na seleção.

The word, taken apart.
estreia is inherited, through Old Portuguese estrẽa, from Latin strēna "a sign, an omen", and so a New Year gift that brought luck (EtymDB).

estrear is inherited from Old Portuguese estrẽar (EtymDB), built like estreia on Latin strēna "an omen, a lucky gift".
