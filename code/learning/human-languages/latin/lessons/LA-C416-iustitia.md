## You'll want to know: iūstitia, iūstitiae
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C416-IUSTITIA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**iūstitia, iūstitiae** — "justice".

> Iūstitia fundāmentum cīvitātis est.
>
> *Justice is the foundation of the state.*

*iūstitia, iūstitiae* f., built on *iūstus*, fair and right. English *justice*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*iūstitia* is built on *iūstus* "righteous, fair" (EtymDB), which comes from *iūs* "right, law" (Lewis & Short). English *justice* comes from it through French.
