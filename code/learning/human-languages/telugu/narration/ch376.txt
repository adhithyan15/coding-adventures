Telugu, chapter 376: How It Began and Ended.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
స్పందన (spandana) — a response; a reaction.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to submit a report, then the Telugu for to respond.

You'll want to know: స్పందన.
స్పందన — spandana — "a response; a reaction".
ఆ పుస్తకానికి మంచి స్పందన వచ్చింది. ā pustakāniki mañci spandana vaccindi. That book got a good response.
The noun of స్పందించు.

The word, taken apart.
స్పందన (spandana) is borrowed from Sanskrit spandana (Monier-Williams), from spand, "to quiver, throb".

Lesson 3 of 5.
ముగింపు (mugimpu) — an ending; a conclusion.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a response, then the Telugu for a start.

You'll want to know: ముగింపు.
ముగింపు — mugimpu — "an ending; a conclusion".
కానీ కథ ముగింపు నాకు నచ్చలేదు. kānī katha mugimpu nāku naccalēdu. But I did not like the story's ending.
The noun of ముగించు, to finish.

The word, taken apart.
ముగింపు (mugimpu) is native Dravidian: DEDR 4891 lists Telugu mugimpu, "finishing, end", with mugincu, "to finish", and Tamil muki, "to end".

Lesson 4 of 5.
ఘట్టం (ghaṭṭaṁ) — an episode; a phase.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a start, then the Telugu for an ending.

You'll want to know: ఘట్టం.
ఘట్టం — ghaṭṭaṁ — "an episode; a phase".
కథలో ఆ ఘట్టం నాకు బాగా గుర్తుంది. kathalō ā ghaṭṭaṁ nāku bāgā gurtundi. I remember that episode of the story well.
Plural: ఘట్టాలు, ghaṭṭālu.
Also a landing place; in a story, a stage of it.

The word, taken apart.
ఘట్టం (ghaṭṭaṁ) is borrowed from Sanskrit ghaṭṭa, "a landing place, a quay" (CDIAL 4414); CDIAL gives its basic sense as a passage.

Lesson 5 of 5.
యాదృచ్ఛికంగా (yādṛcchikaṅgā) — by chance.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for an ending, then the Telugu for an episode.

You'll want to know: యాదృచ్ఛికంగా.
యాదృచ్ఛికంగా — yādṛcchikaṅgā — "by chance".
మేము యాదృచ్ఛికంగా ఒకే రైలులో కలిశాము. mēmu yādṛcchikaṅgā okē railulō kaliśāmu. We met by chance on the same train.
A bookish word; అనుకోకుండా says much the same in speech.

The word, taken apart.
యాదృచ్ఛికంగా (yādṛcchikaṅgā) is built on Sanskrit yādṛcchika, "spontaneous, accidental" (Monier-Williams), with the Telugu adverb ending -gā.
