## You'll want to know: उपचारः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C482-CIKITSA482-04]; assesses=[] -->

**उपचारः** — *upacāraḥ* — "medical treatment".

> उचितेन उपचारेण आतुरः स्वस्थः भवति।
>
> *ucitena upacāreṇa āturaḥ svasthaḥ bhavati.*
>
> With the right treatment the patient gets well.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**उपचारः** (*upacāraḥ*) is built from *upa*, "near", and the root √car, "to move"; MW gives "attendance, service" and "attendance on a patient, medical practice" (MW).
