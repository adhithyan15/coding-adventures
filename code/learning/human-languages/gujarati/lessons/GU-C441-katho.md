# કાંઠો (kā̃ṭho) — a riverbank; the bank of a lake

## You'll want to know: કાંઠો
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C441-LAKE441-04]; assesses=[] -->

**કાંઠો** — *kā̃ṭho* — "a riverbank; the bank of a lake".

> નદીના કાંઠે બાળકો રમે છે.
>
> *nadīnā kā̃ṭhe bāḷko rame che.*
>
> Children play on the river bank.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**કાંઠો** (*kā̃ṭho*) is a tadbhava of Sanskrit *kaṇṭha*, "the throat", and so a rim or edge; CDIAL lists the Gujarati word, "a bank, a coast" (CDIAL 2680).
