Italian, chapter 522: Marmo e argilla.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
scolpire — to sculpt, to carve.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for easel, then the Italian for watercolour.

You'll want to know: scolpire.
scolpire — "to sculpt, to carve".
Ha scolpito una piccola statua nel legno. He carved a small statue out of wood.
An -isc- verb: scolpisco.

The word, taken apart.
scolpire comes from Latin sculpere "to carve, to chisel" (EtymDB).
