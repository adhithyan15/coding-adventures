## You'll want to know: shēngzhí
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C489-STAFF489-05]; assesses=[] -->

*shēngzhí* — "to get promoted".

> *tā gōngzuò le wǔ nián cái shēngzhí* — He worked five years before he was promoted.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*shēngzhí* is built from *shēng* 'to rise' and 职 'a post', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *sying* and *tsyik* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first as a measure of grain and 'to complete'; the sense 'to rise' is not given there.
