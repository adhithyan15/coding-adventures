## You'll want to know: yóuxì
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C141-FUN141-02]; assesses=[] -->

*yóuxì* — "game".

> *wǒ érzi hěn xǐhuan wán yóuxì* — My son loves playing games.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*yóuxì* is built from *yóu* 'to roam' and *xì* 'to play, to tease', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *yuw* and *xjeH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription).
