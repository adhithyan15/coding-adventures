## You'll want to know: одиночество
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C477-LONGING-03]; assesses=[] -->

**одиночество** (*odinóchestvo*) — "loneliness".

> После переезда он долго страдал от одиночества.
>
> *Pósle pereyézda on dólgo stradál ot odinóchestva.*
>
> After the move he suffered from loneliness for a long time.

Neuter.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*одиночество* is built on *одинокий* 'lonely', from *один* 'one', which is inherited from Proto-Slavic *edinъ* (EtymDB).
