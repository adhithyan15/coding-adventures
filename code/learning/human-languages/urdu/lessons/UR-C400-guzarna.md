## You'll want to know: گزارنا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C400-PARDES400-04]; assesses=[] -->

**گزارنا** — *guzārnā* — "to spend (time)".

> سارا نے پردیس میں دو سال گزارے۔
>
> *sārā ne pardes meṅ do sāl guzāre.*
>
> Sara spent two years abroad.

Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے وہاں ایک ہفتہ گزارا, *us ne vahāṅ ek haftā guzārā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**گزارنا** (*guzārnā*) is usually traced to Persian *guzār*, "a passing"; **گزشتہ**, "last, previous", is a time that has passed.
