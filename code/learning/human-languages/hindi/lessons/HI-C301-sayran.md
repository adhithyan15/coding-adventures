## You'll want to know: सायरन
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C301-BUJHI301-01]; assesses=[] -->

**सायरन** — *sāyran* — "a siren".

> सड़क पर सायरन बज रहा था।
>
> *saṛak par sāyran baj rahā thā.*
>
> A siren was sounding on the road.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सायरन** (*sāyran*) is borrowed from English *siren*, taken into Hindi as a masculine noun. Like a bell, a siren **बजता है**.
