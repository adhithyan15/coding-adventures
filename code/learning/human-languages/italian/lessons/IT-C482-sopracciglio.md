## You'll want to know: il sopracciglio
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C482-VISO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**il sopracciglio** — "eyebrow".

> Quando è sorpreso alza un sopracciglio.
>
> *When he is surprised he raises an eyebrow.*

An irregular plural: *le sopracciglia*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*sopracciglio* is inherited from Latin *supercilium* "eyebrow" (Lewis & Short), built on *super* "above" and *cilium* "eyelid".
