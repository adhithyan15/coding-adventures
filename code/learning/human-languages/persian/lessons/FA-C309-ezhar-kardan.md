## You'll want to know: اظهار کردن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C309-SOKHANGU-05]; assesses=[] -->

**اظهار کردن** (*ezhâr kardan*) — "to state, to declare".

> مرد اظهار کرد که چیزی ندیده است.
>
> *Mard ezhâr kard ke chizi nadide ast.*
>
> The man stated that he had not seen anything.

Formal, mostly in reports. Present *ezhâr mikonad*. *ezhâr-e nazar* "a comment".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ezhâr* is borrowed from Arabic *iẓhār* (EtymDB), usually glossed "a showing, a making plain". *kardan* "to do" is inherited Persian.
