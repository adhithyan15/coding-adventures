Marwadi (Marwari), chapter 21: Bus, Vehicle, and Cart: No New Signs.
9 lessons. All 9 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 9.
Hear bas — bus.

Warm-up.
[pause 20 seconds]
Recall the seven-word shopping payoff, then say expensive and price.

You'll want to know.
bas — bus.
Hear it twice, picture a bus, and say it once. Keep the spelling covered until the sound and meaning are easy.

Wrap-up Recall.
Source: Marwari Pathshala, Lesson 10.

What vehicle does bas name?

Lesson 8 of 9.
Payoff — three transport words in four skills.

Warm-up.
[pause 16 seconds]
[once you have stopped driving — write: the word for husband]
Then say goods.

Guided Practice.
Identify three heard words.
Produce three words from meaning cues.
[once you have stopped driving — read: three printed cards and match them to meanings]
[once you have stopped driving — write: all three heard words without a model]
Pass each skill separately. Timetables, tickets, and location phrases remain later work.

Wrap-up Recall.
Source: Marwari Pathshala, Lesson 10.
[question — say your answer, then pause 36 seconds]
Complete all four scored checks, then write the three words.
