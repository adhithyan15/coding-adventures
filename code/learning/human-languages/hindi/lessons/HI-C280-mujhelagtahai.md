# मुझे लगता है कि … (mujhe lagtā hai ki …) — what I think

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[HI-LEX-C280-LAGNA-01, HI-LEX-C280-AISA-01, HI-JOIN-KI-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "I think so". (**मुझे ऐसा लगता है**.) Then **कि**, "that", the word that turns a sentence into the thing you think or know.

## You'll want to know: मुझे लगता है कि
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-GRAMMAR-C280-LAGTA-KI-01]; assesses=[HI-LEX-C280-LAGNA-01, HI-JOIN-KI-01, HI-LEX-C71-TODAY-01, HI-LEX-C184-THG184-05, HI-GRAMMAR-C277-FUTURE-PERSON-01] -->

> **मुझे लगता है कि आज बारिश होगी।**
>
> *mujhe lagtā hai ki āj bāriś hogī.* — "I think it will rain today."

Word for word, "it seems to me that …". The thinker takes **मुझे**, and **लगता है** stays as it is whatever follows **कि**.

- **आपको क्या लगता है?** (*āpko kyā lagtā hai?*) — What do you think?
- **तुम्हें क्या लगता है?** (*tumhẽ kyā lagtā hai?*) — the same, to a friend
- **मुझे नहीं लगता कि वह आएगा।** (*mujhe nahīṁ lagtā ki vah āegā.*) — I don't think he will come. With **नहीं**, the **है** drops.
- **मुझे लगा कि तुम नहीं आओगे।** (*mujhe lagā ki tum nahīṁ āoge.*) — I thought you wouldn't come.

## You'll want to know: in my opinion
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-GRAMMAR-C280-RAY-MEIN-01]; assesses=[HI-LEX-C206-THG206-02, HI-LEX-C206-THG206-03, HI-CONCEPT-C34-SOCHNA-01, HI-LEX-C80-EASE-01] -->

> **मेरी राय में हिंदी आसान है।**
>
> *merī rāy meṁ hindī āsān hai.* — "In my opinion Hindi is easy."

**राय** is feminine, so **मेरी राय में**, "in my opinion". **विचार** is masculine: **मेरे विचार से** (*mere vicār se*) says the same, a little more formally. To ask: **आपकी क्या राय है?** (*āpkī kyā rāy hai?*), "What is your opinion?"

**मैं सोचता हूँ कि …** (*maiṁ soctā hūṁ ki …*) puts the thinker in charge: it is for a thought you are turning over, not for an impression.
