## You'll want to know: rūsticitās, rūsticitātis
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C506-COMI-04]; assesses=[] -->

**rūsticitās, rūsticitātis** — "country manners, boorishness".

> Rūsticitātem eius urbānī rīdent.
>
> *City people laugh at his country manners.*

Feminine, third declension, from *rūsticus*. The opposite of *urbānitās*. English *rusticity*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**rūsticitās** is built on *rūsticus* "of the country", from *rūs* "countryside" (Lewis & Short). English *rusticity* comes from it.
