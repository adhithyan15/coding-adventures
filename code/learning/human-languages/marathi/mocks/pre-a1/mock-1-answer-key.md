## Speaking

<!-- mock-part:prea1-speaking-greeting-and-identity -->
1. Accept **नमस्कार** or an equally appropriate taught Marathi greeting.
2. Accept a chosen identity in a comprehensible frame such as
   **माझं नाव … आहे.**
<!-- /mock-part:prea1-speaking-greeting-and-identity -->
<!-- mock-part:prea1-speaking-familiar-questions -->
3. Accept a relevant state such as **मी बरा आहे** or **मी बरी आहे**.
4. Accept a comprehensible place response such as **मी पुण्यात राहतो** or
   **मी पुण्यात राहते**.
5. Accept a relevant yes/no response or a short frame such as
   **मी मराठी बोलतो / बोलते**.
<!-- /mock-part:prea1-speaking-familiar-questions -->
<!-- mock-part:prea1-speaking-short-request -->
6. Accept a comprehensible polite water request such as **पाणी, कृपया** or
   **पाणी द्या, कृपया**.

Apply the speaking rubric to the whole part. These examples are not scripts the
learner must reproduce word for word. Consistent taught gender and regional
choices remain acceptable.
<!-- /mock-part:prea1-speaking-short-request -->
