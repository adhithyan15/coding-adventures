## You'll want to know: ينبوع
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C470-YANBU470-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ينبوع** — *yanbūʿ* — "a spring (of water)".

> شربنا من ينبوع في الجبل.
>
> *sharibnā min yanbūʿ fī al-jabal.*
>
> We drank from a spring in the mountain.

Plural: ينابيع, *yanābīʿ*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ينبوع** (*yanbūʿ*) is built on the native Arabic root *n-b-ʿ*, "to well up, to gush" (Lane). EtymDB traces the word back to Proto-Semitic.
