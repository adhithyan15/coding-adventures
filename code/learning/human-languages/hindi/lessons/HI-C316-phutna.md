## You'll want to know: फूटना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C316-UPADRAV316-03]; assesses=[] -->

**फूटना** — *phūṭnā* — "to burst; to shatter".

> पत्थर लगने से बस का शीशा फूट गया।
>
> *patthar lagne se bas kā śīśā phūṭ gayā.*
>
> A stone hit the bus and its glass shattered.

Past (it …): वह बोतल फूट गई, *vah botal phūṭ gaī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**फूटना** (*phūṭnā*) is inherited from *\*sphuṭyati*, "bursts open", a form CDIAL reconstructs (CDIAL 13845). Glass, a pot or a bubble **फूटता है**; a quarrel can **फूट पड़ना**, break out.
