## You'll want to know: ānwèi
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C314-FUNERAL314-03]; assesses=[] -->

*ānwèi* — "to comfort; to console".

> *wǒ qù ānwèi tā* — I'm going to comfort her.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ānwèi* is built from *ān* 'calm' and *wèi* 'to soothe', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *'an* and *'jw+jH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second with this very word.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
