## You'll want to know: બાજી
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C533-PASO533-04]; assesses=[] -->

**બાજી** — *bājī* — "a round (of a game)".

> જો તું આ બાજી જીતીશ તો આપણે બીજી બાજી રમીશું.
>
> *jo tũ ā bājī jītīś to āpaṇe bījī bājī ramīśũ.*
>
> If you win this round, we'll play another.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**બાજી** (*bājī*) is usually traced to Persian *bāzī*, "play, a game".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
