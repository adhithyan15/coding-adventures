## You'll want to know: શેરી
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C419-STREET419-01]; assesses=[] -->

**શેરી** — *śerī* — "a street; a lane (in a town)".

> અમારી શેરીમાં બધાં ઘર જૂનાં છે.
>
> *amārī śerīmā̃ badhā̃ ghar jūnā̃ che.*
>
> All the houses in our street are old.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**શેરી** (*śerī*) No dictionary we use records an older form, so its history is not settled.
