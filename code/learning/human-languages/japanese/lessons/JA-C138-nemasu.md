## You'll want to know: ねます
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C138-NEMASU]; assesses=[] -->

**ねます** — *nemasu* — "I go to bed", "I sleep".

Its dictionary form is **ねる**, *neru*, and it works like **おきる**: take
off **る** and add **ます**. *ne-ru* becomes *ne-masu*.

> **いま ねます。** — *ima nemasu* — "I am going to bed now."

Now you have a whole day, and the two ways to find the stem:

| dictionary form | polite present | how |
|---|---|---|
| **おきる** *okiru* | **おきます** *okimasu* | drop *-ru* |
| **ねる** *neru* | **ねます** *nemasu* | drop *-ru* |
| **はたらく** *hataraku* | **はたらきます** *hatarakimasu* | *-u* becomes *-i* |
| **あるく** *aruku* | **あるきます** *arukimasu* | *-u* becomes *-i* |
| **かえる** *kaeru* | **かえります** *kaerimasu* | *-u* becomes *-i* |
