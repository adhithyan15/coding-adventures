The corrected post-chapter-405 audit leaves 26 objective items failing under

Chapter 406 starts tranche C with **el cliente**, **el documento**, **el dato**,
**el impreso**, and **rellenar**. The bundle closes four measured items:
`cliente` appears in both mocks, `documento` closes the remaining ID-document

until all of that item's requirements are taught.

The etymology ladder keeps the meanings reconstructable: Latin *cliens* becomes
the modern customer, *documentum* is evidence that shows, *datum* is something
given, and *impressus* is something pressed into paper. The transparent Spanish
construction **re- + llenar** then turns those nouns into the practical phrase
**rellenar el impreso con los datos**.
