## You'll want to know: amāns, amantis
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C535-AMAN-03]; assesses=[] -->

**amāns, amantis** — "a lover".

> Amāns sub fenestrā puellae cantat.
>
> *The lover sings under the girl's window.*

The present participle of *amō*, used as a noun. *Amantēs* — lovers.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**amāns** is the present participle of *amō* "to love", used as a noun (Lewis & Short). English *amorous* belongs to the same family.
