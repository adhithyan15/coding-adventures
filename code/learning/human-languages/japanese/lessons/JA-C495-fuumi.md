## You'll want to know: ふうみ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C495-SMELL495-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ふうみ** — *fūmi* — "flavour; taste".

> **この みそしるは ふうみが いいです。** — *kono misoshiru wa fūmi ga ii desu* — "This miso soup has a good flavour."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ふうみ** (*fūmi*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *fū*, "wind" (Middle Chinese *pjuwng*) and *mi*, "flavor" (*mj+jH*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
