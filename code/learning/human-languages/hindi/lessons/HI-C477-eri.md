## You'll want to know: एड़ी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C477-HATHELI477-05]; assesses=[] -->

**एड़ी** — *eṛī* — "a heel".

> नए जूतों से मेरी एड़ी में दर्द है।
>
> *nae jūtoṁ se merī eṛī meṁ dard hai.*
>
> My heel hurts because of my new shoes.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**एड़ी** (*eṛī*) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Hindi *eṛī*, "heel", under *\*aḍḍi*, a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 191).
