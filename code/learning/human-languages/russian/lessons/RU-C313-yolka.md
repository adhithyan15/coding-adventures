## You'll want to know: ёлка
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C313-YOLKA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ёлка** (*yólka*) — "a New Year tree; a fir tree".

> Перед Новым годом мы купили ёлку.
>
> *Péred Nóvym gódom my kupíli yólku.*
>
> Before New Year we bought a tree.

The Russian holiday tree, put up for New Year. *Ель* is the tree in the forest.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ёлка* (EtymDB *jolka*) is built on *ель* "spruce, fir", inherited from Proto-Slavic *jedlĭ*, with the ending *-ка*.
