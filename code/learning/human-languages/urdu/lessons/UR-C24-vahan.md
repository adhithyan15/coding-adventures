# وہاں (vahāṅ) — there

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[UR-LEX-YAHAN, UR-LEX-VOH, UR-LEX-KAHAN] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say **here**. Then say **that**. Now guess this lesson's word before
you read on.

## You'll want to know: وہاں
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-VAHAN]; assesses=[UR-LEX-YAHAN, UR-LEX-VOH] -->

> **وہ وہاں ہے** — *voh vahāṅ hai* — **he is there.**

If you guessed it, that is the lesson. The frame is closed:

| head | thing | place |
|---|---|---|
| **ک-** asking | **کیا** | **کہاں** |
| **یہ-** near | **یہ** | **یہاں** |
| **وہ-** far | **وہ** | **وہاں** |

Six words, three heads, two frames — and every one of them was predictable from
the shape of the others.

## Grammar Lens: what a place word can now do
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[UR-LEX-VAHAN, UR-LEX-YAHAN, UR-GRAMMAR-NAHIN-VERB, UR-GRAMMAR-MEIN-POST, UR-GRAMMAR-IS-OBLIQUE, UR-LEX-KAMRA] -->

These two words and the postpositions of the last chapter answer the same
question at different resolutions:

- **وہاں** — *there*, gesturing
- **اس کمرے میں** — *in that room*, naming

And the denial you already own works on both, because **نہیں** goes in front of
the verb wherever the verb is:

> **وہ وہاں نہیں ہے** — *voh vahāṅ nahīṅ hai* — **he is not there.**
