# تھمنا (thamnā) — to let up; to stop (of rain or wind)

## You'll want to know: تھمنا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C431-ABR431-05]; assesses=[] -->

**تھمنا** — *thamnā* — "to let up; to stop (of rain or wind)".

> دوپہر تک بارش تھم گئی۔
>
> *dopahar tak bārish tham gaī.*
>
> By midday the rain had let up.

Past (it …): وہ طوفان تھم گیا, *voh tūfān tham gayā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *thāmnā*, "to prop, to stop", under Sanskrit *stambhate* (CDIAL 13683); **تھمنا** is the form for something that stops by itself, **تھمانا** the one for handing something over.
