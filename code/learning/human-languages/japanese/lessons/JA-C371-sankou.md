## You'll want to know: さんこう
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C371-JOUDAN371-02]; assesses=[] -->

**さんこう** — *sankō* — "a reference; something to learn from".

> **この ほんを よんだら、さんこうに なるでしょう。** — *kono hon o yondara, sankō ni naru deshō* — "If you read this book, you will probably find it useful."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**さんこう** (*sankō*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *san*, "participate" and *kō*, "consider" (Middle Chinese *khawX*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
