# घ — voice and breath together

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

You have the puff, and you have the voice. Now do both in one sound.

## Script
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-SCRIPT-GHA-01]; assesses=[MR-SCRIPT-GA-01, MR-SCRIPT-KHA-01] -->

> घ

**घ** is *gha*: voiced like **ग**, breathy like **ख**. Four letters now sit at
the same place in your mouth, and only voice and breath tell them apart:

**क** plain · **ख** breath · **ग** voice · **घ** voice and breath.

You already say this letter. *Kāḷjī ghyā*, "take care", ends in it, and so does
*gheṇe*, "to take". Draw the left bowl, then the right stem, then the headline.

## Writing — delayed copy
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MR-SCRIPT-GHA-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: delayed-copy -->

Look at **घ** for five seconds, cover it, and write it once. Uncover and check
it against **ग**: the bowl on the left is what you are looking for.
