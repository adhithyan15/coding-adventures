## You'll want to know: मापना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C361-SHODH361-04]; assesses=[] -->

**मापना** — *māpnā* — "to gauge, to measure (precisely)".

> दर्ज़ी ने मेरी कमर मापी।
>
> *darzī ne merī kamar māpī.*
>
> The tailor measured my waist.

Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने कमर मापी, *usne kamar māpī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**मापना** (*māpnā*) is inherited from *\*māpyate*, "is measured", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 10054).
