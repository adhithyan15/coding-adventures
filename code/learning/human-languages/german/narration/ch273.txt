German, chapter 273: Bekannt machen.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
veröffentlichen — to publish.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for the journalist, then the German for the front page.

You'll want to know: veröffentlichen.
veröffentlichen — "to publish".
Die Zeitung veröffentlichte das Foto am Montag. The newspaper published the photo on Monday.
Built on öffentlich, already known. Perfekt: hat veröffentlicht.

The word, taken apart.
veröffentlichen is built on öffentlich "public", from offen "open", an inherited Germanic word; EtymDB lists no parent for the verb: to make open to all.

What you've built.
One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.

erwähnen — "to mention".
Er erwähnte den Unfall mit keinem Wort. He did not say a single word about the accident.
It takes a direct object: Sie erwähnte ihn nicht.

The word, taken apart.
erwähnen is built on wähnen "to suppose", from Old High German wānen (EtymDB), a cousin of old English ween "to think".

Lesson 3 of 5.
äußern — to express, to voice; reflexive: to comment.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to publish, then the German for to mention.

You'll want to know: äußern.
äußern — "to express, to voice; reflexive: to comment".
Sie äußerte ihre Meinung ganz offen. She voiced her opinion quite openly.
Reflexive, it means to comment: Er wollte sich nicht äußern.

The word, taken apart.
äußern comes from Middle High German iuzern (EtymDB), built on außen "outside", Old High German ūzana: to bring something out.

Lesson 4 of 5.
ankündigen — to announce.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to mention, then the German for to express.

You'll want to know: ankündigen.
ankündigen — "to announce".
Die Firma kündigte neue Preise an. The company announced new prices.
Separable: sie kündigt an, sie hat angekündigt.

The word, taken apart.
ankündigen is built on kündigen, from kund "known", an inherited word with the Gothic cousin kunþs "known" (EtymDB): to make known ahead.

Lesson 5 of 5.
bekanntgeben — to make public, to announce officially.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to express, then the German for to announce.

You'll want to know: bekanntgeben.
bekanntgeben — "to make public, to announce officially".
Die Schule gab das Ergebnis am Freitag bekannt. The school made the result public on Friday.
Separable: sie gibt bekannt, sie hat bekanntgegeben.

The word, taken apart.
bekanntgeben joins bekannt "known" and geben "to give", from Old High German geban, Proto-Germanic gebaną (EtymDB): to give out as known.
