## You'll want to know: मध्यम
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C384-BARF384-05]; assesses=[] -->

**मध्यम** — *madhyam* — "medium, moderate".

> अगर गैस मध्यम नहीं होगी तो खाना जल जाएगा।
>
> *agar gais madhyam nahīṃ hogī to khānā jal jāegā.*
>
> If the gas isn't on medium, the food will burn.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**मध्यम** (*madhyam*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *madhyama*, "in the middle" (Monier-Williams).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
