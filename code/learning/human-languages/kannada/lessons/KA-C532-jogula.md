## You'll want to know: ಜೋಗುಳ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C532-GITA532-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ಜೋಗುಳ** — *jōguḷa* — "a lullaby".

> ಅಮ್ಮ ಜೋಗುಳ ಹಾಡಿದಾಗ ಮಗು ಮಲಗಿತು.
>
> *amma jōguḷa hāḍidāga magu malagitu.*
>
> When the mother sang a lullaby, the baby fell asleep.

Built on ಜೋ ಜೋ, the soothing sound sung over a cradle.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಜೋಗುಳ** (*jōguḷa*) is native Dravidian, built on Kannada *jō*, "hush!", the word sung in lullabies (DEDR 2869).
