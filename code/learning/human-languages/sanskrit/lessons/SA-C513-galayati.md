## You'll want to know: गालयति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C513-MATHNATI513-04]; assesses=[] -->

**गालयति** — *gālayati* — "strains".

> सः क्षीरं वस्त्रेण गालयति।
>
> *saḥ kṣīraṃ vastreṇa gālayati.*
>
> He strains the milk through a cloth.

Past (he …): सः क्षीरं गालितवान्, *saḥ kṣīraṃ gālitavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**गालयति** (*gālayati*) comes from the causative of the root √gal, "to drip, to ooze": MW gives *gālayati* as to make drop, to filter, to strain (MW).
