# Two things at once

## Wrap-up Recall

[PAUSE 3s] Which joiner turns a statement into an offer once the voice rises?
(**বা** — two things and a choice between them.) How many **না** does a double

which of this chapter's joiners cannot stand on its own? (**-ও**.)
