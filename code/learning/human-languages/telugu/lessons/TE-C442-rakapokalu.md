# రాకపోకలు (rākapōkalu) — comings and goings; traffic

## You'll want to know: రాకపోకలు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C442-PATTA442-03]; assesses=[] -->

**రాకపోకలు** — *rākapōkalu* — "comings and goings; traffic".

> వరద వల్ల ఆ రోడ్డు మీద రాకపోకలు నిలిచాయి.
>
> *varada valla ā rōḍḍu mīda rākapōkalu nilicāyi.*
>
> Because of the flood, traffic on that road stopped.

రాక, coming, + పోక, going, + the plural -లు. The news uses it for the traffic on a road.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**రాకపోకలు** (*rākapōkalu*) is native Dravidian: రాక, "coming", the noun of రా, "to come" (DEDR 5270), and పోక, "going", the noun of పోవు, "to go" (DEDR 4572), with the plural -లు.
