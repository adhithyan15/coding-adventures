Telugu, chapter 151: A Word for Shade — Then Its First Letter.
3 lessons. You can do the first 1 of them in the car; after that you will want to have stopped.


Lesson 1 of 3.
ఛాయ (chāya) — shade, shadow.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Point once to the rare ఙ you met in వాఙ్మయం. Now imagine the cool patch beneath a tree. This lesson gives that patch a name, using the familiar య at the end and a different uncommon letter at the beginning.

You'll want to know: ఛాయ.
ఛాయ — chāya — "shade" or "shadow".
Say it in two gentle pieces: chā, ya. The first sound is an airy ch; the second is the familiar య sound. Read the word as ఛా-య.
ఛాయ (chāya) is a learned word with a wider range than one dark shape. In literary or careful language it can also name a reflected image or a tinge of colour. Keep the first meaning small today: shade or shadow.

What you've built.
A useful image comes before an unfamiliar letter. You can say the whole word before the next lesson asks your hand to take its first shape apart.

Guided Practice.

[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: chāya, as one word]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — read: ఛాయ (chāya) — shade or shadow]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — find: the familiar య at the end]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]

Lesson 2 of 3.
ఛ (cha) — the airy first letter in ఛాయ (chāya).

Before we start: this one needs your hands, so it is not a driving lesson. You will want a pen and something to write on and your eyes, for letter shapes on the page. You can listen to everything else now — leave the sections called Script you'll notice: ఛ and Guided Practice until you have stopped, and I will say so again when we reach it.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say ఛాయ (chāya) once. What does it mean? ("Shade" or "shadow.")
[pause 1 second]
Now slow the first piece: chā. The base letter carrying that airy ch is today's whole task.

Script you'll notice: ఛ.
ఛ — cha.
In ఛాయ (chāya), the long ా sign turns the base sound into ఛా. Remove that vowel sign and the base letter is ఛ (cha).
This aspirated letter is uncommon in everyday Telugu vocabulary. That is why the book gave you ఛాయ (chāya) before asking you to practise the shape.

Writing: ఛ — five movements, two runs.
Follow the filmstrip slowly. Movements 1–4 stay joined: draw the short upper bar, turn around the left bowl, sweep around the broad outer bowl, and continue through the upper flourish. Lift once. Movement 5 draws the separate stem downward below the middle.
The numbered route is one attested school-style order; Telugu handwriting can vary. Keep the two pen-down runs distinct and let the long curves stay round.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[once you have stopped driving — trace: ఛ (cha) once, following the five numbered movements]
[your turn — copy: ఛ (cha) twice without tracing]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — build: ఛ (cha) + ా = ఛా]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — read: ఛాయ (chāya)]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[question — say your answer, then pause 12 seconds]
Which base letter begins ఛాయ (chāya)?

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
Which letter is this — ఛ (cha)? What word gave it to you? (ఛాయ (chāya).)


Lesson 3 of 3.
ఛాయ (chāya) — meaning and shape together.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Picture the cool patch beneath a tree. Say the Telugu word for it. (ఛాయ (chāya).)

Guided Practice.
Read ఛాయ (chāya) once. Cover it, then write only its first base letter: ఛ (cha).
Now uncover the word and check two things: the broad curved body and the short separate stem beneath it. Add the long ా sign only after the base letter is steady.

[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does ఛాయ (chāya) mean, and which base letter begins it? ("Shade" or "shadow"; ఛ (cha).)
