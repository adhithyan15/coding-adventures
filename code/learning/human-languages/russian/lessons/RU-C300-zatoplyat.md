## You'll want to know: затоплять
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C300-NAVODNENIYE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**затоплять** (*zatoplyát*) — "to flood, to submerge".

> Вода затопила улицы.
>
> *Vodá zatopíla úlitsy.*
>
> The water flooded the streets.

Imperfective; perfective *затопить* (*zatopít*). Careful: *затопить печь* means to light a stove.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*затоплять* is built on *за-* and *топить* "to sink, to drown", inherited from Proto-Slavic *topiti* (EtymDB).
