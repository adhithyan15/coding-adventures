# उजळणे (ujaḷṇe) — to light up; to brighten

## You'll want to know: उजळणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C519-UJED519-05]; assesses=[] -->

**उजळणे** — *ujaḷṇe* — "to light up; to brighten".

> दिवे लागले आणि सगळा रस्ता उजळला.
>
> *dive lāgle āṇi sagḷā rastā ujaḷlā.*
>
> The lamps came on and the whole street lit up.

Past (he …): तो रस्ता उजळला, *to rastā ujaḷlā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**उजळणे** (*ujaḷṇe*) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Marathi *ujaḷṇẽ* under Sanskrit *ujjvalati*, "blazes up, shines" (CDIAL 1671).
