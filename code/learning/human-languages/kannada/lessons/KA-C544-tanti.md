## You'll want to know: ತಂತಿ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C544-VIDYUT544-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ತಂತಿ** — *tanti* — "a wire; a string".

> ಬಟ್ಟೆ ಒಣಗಿಸಲು ಅಮ್ಮ ತಂತಿ ಕಟ್ಟಿದರು.
>
> *baṭṭe oṇagisalu amma tanti kaṭṭidaru.*
>
> Mother tied up a wire to dry the clothes.

Also a string of the veena, and in older talk a telegram.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ತಂತಿ** (*tanti*) goes back to Sanskrit *tantrī*, "the string of a lute" (CDIAL 5667).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
