Punjabi, chapter 275: Too Loose, Too Tight.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ਢਿੱਲਾ (ḍhillā) — loose; slack.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for a size, then the Punjabi for a pair.

You'll want to know: ਢਿੱਲਾ.
ਢਿੱਲਾ — ḍhillā — "loose; slack".
ਇਹ ਕੁੜਤਾ ਮੈਨੂੰ ਢਿੱਲਾ ਹੈ। eh kuṛtā mainū̃ ḍhillā hai. This kurta is loose on me.

The word, taken apart.
ਢਿੱਲਾ (ḍhillā) is inherited from a reconstructed ḍhila, "loose, slack"; CDIAL lists Punjabi ḍhillā, "slack, slow, late", under it (CDIAL 5590).

Lesson 3 of 5.
ਫੱਬਣਾ (phabbṇā) — to suit; to look good on someone.

[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for loose, then the Punjabi for tight.

You'll want to know: ਫੱਬਣਾ.
ਫੱਬਣਾ — phabbṇā — "to suit; to look good on someone".
ਨੀਲਾ ਕੁੜਤਾ ਅਮਨ ਨੂੰ ਬਹੁਤ ਫੱਬਦਾ ਹੈ। nīlā kuṛtā Aman nū̃ bahut phabbdā hai. The blue kurta really suits Aman.
Past (it …): ਉਹ ਕੁੜਤਾ ਅਮਨ ਨੂੰ ਫੱਬਿਆ, oh kuṛtā Aman nū̃ phabbiā.

The word, taken apart.
ਫੱਬਣਾ (phabbṇā) is inherited from a reconstructed sparvati, "releases, succeeds, is fitting"; CDIAL lists Punjabi phabbṇā, "to look well, be profitable", under it (CDIAL 13808).
