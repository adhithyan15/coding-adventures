## You'll want to know: ڈھکن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C508-DHAKKAN508-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ڈھکن** — *ḍhakkan* — "a lid".

> دیگچی کا ڈھکن کہاں ہے؟
>
> *degchī kā ḍhakkan kahāṅ hai?*
>
> Where is the lid of the cooking pot?

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ڈھکن** (*ḍhakkan*) is inherited from *\*ḍhakk*, "cover", a form CDIAL reconstructs, which also gives the Hindi cousin *ḍhā̃knā*, "to cover" (CDIAL 5574).
