## You'll want to know: ਮਜ਼ਾਕ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C355-RIDDLE355-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ਮਜ਼ਾਕ** — *mazāk* — "a joke; joking".

> ਮੇਰਾ ਦੋਸਤ ਬਹੁਤ ਮਜ਼ਾਕ ਕਰਦਾ ਹੈ।
>
> *merā dost bahut mazāk kardā hai.*
>
> My friend jokes a lot.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਮਜ਼ਾਕ** (*mazāk*) is a loan, usually traced through Persian to Arabic *madhāq*, "taste".
