## You'll want to know: প্রতারণা
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C285-HUMKI285-05]; assesses=[] -->

**প্রতারণা** — *prôtārônā* — "fraud; deception".

> একটা লোক প্রতারণা করে গ্রামের মানুষের টাকা নিল।
>
> *ekṭā lok prôtārônā kôre grāmer mānusher ṭākā nilô.*
>
> A man tricked the villagers out of their money.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**প্রতারণা** (*prôtārônā*) is Sanskrit *pratāraṇa*, from the causative of *pra-√tṝ*, which Monier-Williams gives both as 'ferrying across' and as 'deceiving': taking someone across, and taking them in.
