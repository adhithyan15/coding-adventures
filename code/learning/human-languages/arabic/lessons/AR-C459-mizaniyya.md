## You'll want to know: ميزانية
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C459-MIZANIYYA459-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ميزانية** — *mīzāniyya* — "a budget".

> كانت ميزانية الرحلة صغيرة.
>
> *kānat mīzāniyyat ar-riḥla ṣaghīra.*
>
> The budget for the trip was small.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ميزانية** (*mīzāniyya*) is built on the native Arabic root *w-z-n*, "to weigh" (Lane). It is made from *mīzān*, "scales", with *-iyya*: money weighed out.
