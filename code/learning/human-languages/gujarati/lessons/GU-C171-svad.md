## You'll want to know: સ્વાદ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C171-TASTE171-01]; assesses=[] -->

**સ્વાદ** — *svād* — "taste, flavour".

> **આ દાળનો સ્વાદ સારો છે.** — *ā dāḷno svād sāro che* — "This dal tastes good."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**સ્વાદ** (*svād*) is Sanskrit *svāda*, "taste, flavour", taken over whole as a learned loan, a tatsama. CDIAL lists the Sanskrit word but no Gujarati form (CDIAL 13923).
