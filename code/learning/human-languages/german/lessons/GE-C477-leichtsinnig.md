## You'll want to know: leichtsinnig
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C477-MUT-04]; assesses=[] -->

**leichtsinnig** — "reckless, careless".

> Ohne Helm Fahrrad zu fahren ist leichtsinnig.
>
> *Riding a bike without a helmet is reckless.*

The noun: *der Leichtsinn* — recklessness.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*leichtsinnig* is built on *leicht* "light" and *Sinn* "mind, sense", which is inherited from Old High German *sin*, from Proto-Germanic *sinnaz* (EtymDB): a light mind.
