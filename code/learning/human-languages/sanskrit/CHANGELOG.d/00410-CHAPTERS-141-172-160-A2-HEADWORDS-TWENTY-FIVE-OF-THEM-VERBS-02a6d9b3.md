## Chapters 141-172: 160 A2 headwords, twenty-five of them verbs

The first of four Sanskrit A2 vocabulary tranches. Thirty-two chapters of five
words each, generated in four runs of at most nine chapters, and each run closes
with two reviews.

- **Chapters 141-145: twenty-five verbs** (`SPINE-NAME-EVERYDAY-ACTIONS`), including
  **पिबति** (drinks), **वसति** (dwells), **स्पृशति** (touches), **दर्शयति**
  (shows), **प्रत्यागच्छति** (comes back), **शेते** (lies down), **बिभेति** (is
  afraid), **तोलयति** (weighs) and **क्रन्दति** (cries out).
- **Chapters 146-149: people** (`SPINE-EXCHANGE-NAMES`): grandchildren and
  grandparents (**पौत्रः**, **पितामही**, **मातामहः**), a household
  (**स्वामी**, **सेवकः**), the court (**राज्ञी**, **मन्त्री**), and work
  (**नापितः**, **धीवरः**, **श्रमिकः**, **लिपिकः**).
- **Chapters 150-151: well-being** (`SPINE-CHECK-WELLBEING`): **भेषजम्**,
  **व्रणः**, **उदरपीडा**, and the dispositions **उत्साहः**, **धैर्यम्**,
  **आलस्यम्**.
- **Chapter 152: time** (**कालः**, **तिथिः**, **दिनांकः**, **शताब्दी**).
- **Chapter 153: place** (**इतः**, **ततः**, **परितः**).
- **Chapter 154: conditions and concessions** (**यदि**, **चेत्**, **तथापि**,
  **यद्यपि**, **अथवा**).
- **Chapters 155-157: fifteen qualities**, such as **नूतनः**, **उष्णः**,
  **शान्तः**, **कुपितः**, **श्रान्तः** and **क्षुधितः**.
- **Chapters 158-172: seventy-five nouns** (`SPINE-NAME-EVERYDAY-THINGS`):
  - writing things (**लेखनी**, **मसी**);
  - spices (**जीरकम्**, **हिंगु**);
  - meals (**भोजनम्**, **व्यञ्जनम्**);
  - music (**वेणुः**, **मृदंगः**);
  - money (**वेतनम्**, **ऋणम्**, **लाभः**);
  - occasions (**उत्सवः**, **विवाहः**);
  - trees (**अश्वत्थः**, **निम्बः**, **वटः**);
  - school (**अध्यायः**, **अक्षरम्**, **शब्दकोशः**);
  - work (**कार्यम्**, **वृत्तिः**);
  - travel (**यात्री**, **यानपत्रम्**, **पर्यटकः**).
- **Recall prompts stay unambiguous.** Many A2 words are near-synonyms of words
  the book already teaches. Their glosses lead with a word of their own, so no
  recall prompt ever has two right answers: **शीतः** is "chilly", not "cold",
  which **शीतम्** already owns; **दिनांकः** is "a calendar date"; **वसति** is
  "dwells".
- **Anusvāra spelling before velars.** Words such as **अंकः**, **शंखः** and
  **दिनांकः** are spelled with the anusvāra, as the book already spells
  **मंगलवासरः**, and not with the conjunct ङ्. The Devanagari script data has
  no source-backed row for ङ yet, so a headword that used it would fail
  validation.
- **Pins:** the Sanskrit lesson-content budget goes from 776 to 944.
- **Level gate at A2:** vocabulary 559 short -> 399; verb vocabulary 48 short
  -> 23.
