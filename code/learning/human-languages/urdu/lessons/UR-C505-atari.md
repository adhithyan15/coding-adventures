## You'll want to know: اٹاری
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C505-ATARI505-03]; assesses=[] -->

**اٹاری** — *aṭārī* — "a loft; an attic".

> ہمارا پرانا سامان اٹاری میں ہے۔
>
> *hamārā purānā sāmān aṭārī meṅ hai.*
>
> Our old things are up in the loft.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**اٹاری** (*aṭārī*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *aṭārī*, "a thatched upper building", under Sanskrit *aṭṭāla*, "a watch-tower" (CDIAL 185).
