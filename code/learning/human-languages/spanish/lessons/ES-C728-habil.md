## You'll want to know: hábil
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C728-SABIO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**hábil** — "skilful, clever".

> Es muy hábil con las manos.
>
> *He is very good with his hands.*

*Día hábil* — a working day.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*hábil* is a learned borrowing from Latin *habilis* "apt, skilful" (EtymDB), from *habēre* "to hold"; English *able* is its inherited French form.
