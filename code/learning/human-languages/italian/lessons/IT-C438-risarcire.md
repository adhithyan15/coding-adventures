## You'll want to know: risarcire
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C438-RECLAMO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**risarcire** — "to compensate, to pay damages".

> La compagnia deve risarcire i passeggeri.
>
> *The company must compensate the passengers.*

*Il risarcimento* — compensation. Conjugated like *finire*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*risarcire* is inherited from Latin *resarcīre* 'to patch or mend again' (EtymDB), from *sarcīre* 'to mend'.
