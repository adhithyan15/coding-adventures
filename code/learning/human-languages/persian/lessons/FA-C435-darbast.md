## You'll want to know: دربست
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C435-DARBAST-01]; assesses=[] -->

**دربست** (*darbast*) — "a private hire (a taxi taken for yourself alone)".

> تا فرودگاه دربست گرفتیم.
>
> *Tâ forudgâh darbast gereftim.*
>
> We took a private taxi to the airport.

A shared taxi picks up several people on a fixed route; *darbast* means you hire the whole car.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*darbast* is usually analysed as a native compound of *dar* 'in' and *bast* 'closed', the past stem of *bastan* 'to tie, to close', which EtymDB traces to the PIE root *bʰendʰ-*: the whole car, closed to others.
