## You'll want to know: વાયર
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C517-VIJLI517-01]; assesses=[] -->

**વાયર** — *vāyar* — "an electric wire".

> પંખાનો વાયર જૂનો થઈ ગયો છે.
>
> *pankhāno vāyar jūno thaī gayo che.*
>
> The fan's wire has got old.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**વાયર** (*vāyar*) is borrowed from English *wire*.
