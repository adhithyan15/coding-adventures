# когда and пока — the scene, and what cut into it

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[RU-GRAMMAR-PAST-IMPERFECTIVE-PROCESS-01, RU-GRAMMAR-PAST-PERFECTIVE-RESULT-01, RU-GRAMMAR-KOGDA-CLAUSE-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "yesterday I was reading a book", then "yesterday I read
the book to the end". (*Vcherá ya chitál knígu. Vcherá ya prochitál
knígu.*)

[PAUSE 1s] And the hinge from the present: *kogdá ya chitáyu, ya dúmayu*.
**Когда** joins two halves with a comma at the seam. In the past, the
aspect on each side tells the listener how the two halves fit together.

## Grammar Lens: one going on, one cutting in
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-GRAMMAR-KOGDA-EVENT-01]; assesses=[RU-LEX-KOGDA-01, RU-LEX-C232-ACT232-03, RU-LEX-BRAT-NOUN, RU-PUNCT-ZAPYATAYA-01, RU-LEX-C52-SCHOOL-01] -->

> **Когда я читал, позвонил брат.** — *kogdá ya chitál, pozvoníl brat* —
> When I was reading, my brother rang.

The reading is imperfective: it was going on. **Позвонил**, the perfective
of *звонить*, is one call that cut into it.

Put a perfective on **both** sides and the events come one after the other:

> **Когда я прочитал книгу, я позвонил брату.** — *kogdá ya prochitál
> knígu, ya pozvoníl brátu* — When I had finished the book, I rang my
> brother.

First the book was finished, then the call. *Брату* is *брат* with
**-у**: a man you ring takes that ending, and Mum would be *маме*.

## Grammar Lens: пока, for two things at once
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-GRAMMAR-POKA-WHILE-01]; assesses=[RU-LEX-POKA, RU-LEX-C40-VERB3-01, RU-LEX-CHITAT] -->

The goodbye **пока** has a second job: "while, for as long as". Inside a
story it usually takes the imperfective, and it lays two scenes side by
side:

> **Пока я читал, брат спал.** — *poká ya chitál, brat spal* — While I was
> reading, my brother was asleep.

Neither half is finished; both went on through the same hour.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[RU-ETYMON-POKA-KAKOI] -->

**пока** is *по-* with an old pronoun meaning "which, what kind", the
ancestor of *какой*, on the question-root PIE *\*kʷo-* of *как* and of
English *who* and *when*. By the usual account "for as long as" is its old
sense, and the goodbye, "for now", grew out of it in the twentieth
century. **Когда** comes off the same root.
