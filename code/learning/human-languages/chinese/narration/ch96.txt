You'll want to know: 因为.
因为 — yīnwèi — "because".
我不去 因为我没有车 wǒ bú qù, yīnwèi wǒ méiyǒu chē — I'm not going, because I don't have a car.
The next lesson puts the first character in your hand.

The word, taken apart.
因为 (yīnwèi) is built from 因 (yīn) 'to follow from' and 为 'for'. 因 (yīn) is inherited from Middle Chinese 'jin (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'to rely on; to follow from'.

What you've built.
One reason, in one clause, after what it explains: 我不去, 因为我没有车. It can also come first: 因为我没有车, 我不去.

[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: 因为我没有车 我不去 — yīnwèi wǒ méiyǒu chē, wǒ bú qù — Because I don't have a car, I'm not going.]

Script — 大 inside the frame of 国.
因 (yīn).
A frame and what it holds, and the frame is closed last, after its contents, as in 国.
The frame, two strokes first: its left side, then its top turning down the right side.
大, three strokes, inside: the bar, the left-falling stroke, the short right-falling stroke.
The bottom bar, last, closing the frame.
2 + 3 + 1 = six.

[once you have stopped driving — this part needs your eyes or your hands; if you are driving, skip ahead to the next part]
Guided Practice.
[once you have stopped driving — write: 因 (yīn), all six, with the model in front of you]
[once you have stopped driving — cover: write it again from memory, and count the pieces, not the strokes]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
How many strokes in 因 (yīn)? (Six.) What is inside the frame? (大.) When is the frame closed? (Last, as in 国.)

Lesson 3 of 5.
suǒyǐ — so; therefore.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: read the last lesson's character, then two words, one of which ends like this one.
[once you have stopped driving — read: 因为, aloud — yīnwèi]
[your turn — recall: say lèi, tired — R4, recognition at distance]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say kěyǐ, may — R2]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

You'll want to know: suǒyǐ.
suǒyǐ — "so; therefore".
yīnwèi wǒ hěn lèi, suǒyǐ wǒ bú qù — I'm tired, so I'm not going.
Two dips in a row: spoken, the first rises, suóyǐ. It is written suǒyǐ.
Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
suǒyǐ is built from suǒ 'a place; that which' and yǐ 'to use', the yǐ of kěyǐ. Both are inherited from Middle Chinese, srjoX and yiX (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), srjoX glossed 'a place'. The regular change from srjoX would give shǔ, not suǒ, so why it is said suǒ is not settled.

What you've built.
因为 gives the reason, suǒyǐ what follows from it. Mandarin often says both: yīnwèi …, suǒyǐ …. Either half can also stand alone.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: suǒyǐ — and hear it as suóyǐ]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: yīnwèi wǒ hěn lèi, suǒyǐ wǒ bú qù — I'm tired, so I'm not going.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say kěyǐ, then suǒyǐ, and hear the same yǐ]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does suǒyǐ mean? ("So; therefore".) Which half gives the reason, 因为 (yīnwèi) or suǒyǐ? (因为 (yīnwèi).)

You'll want to know: shūfu.
shūfu — "comfortable; well".
wǒ bù shūfu — I'm not feeling well.
zhège yǐzi hěn shūfu — This chair is comfortable.
Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
shūfu is built from shū 'to stretch; to ease' and fu, the fu of yīfu. Both are inherited from Middle Chinese, syo and bjuwk (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), syo glossed 'slow; to stretch out'.

What you've built.
wǒ bù shūfu is the gentle way to say you are unwell, and a reason nobody argues with: yīnwèi wǒ bù shūfu.

Lesson 5 of 5.
为什么 — 因为 (wèishénme — yīnwèi).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
The chapter closes here, with every why answered.
[your turn — recall: say shūfu, comfortable — R1, one lesson back]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say è, hungry — R4, recognition at distance]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say 上课 — shàngkè, to go to class — R2]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

The exchange.
A: 你为什么不吃饭 nǐ wèishénme bù chī fàn — Why aren't you eating? B: yīnwèi wǒ bú è — Because I'm not hungry. A: 你为什么不去上课 nǐ wèishénme bú qù shàngkè — Why aren't you going to class? B: yīnwèi wǒ bù shūfu, suǒyǐ wǒ bú qù — I'm not feeling well, so I'm not going. A: 你明天能来吗 nǐ míngtiān néng lái ma — Can you come tomorrow? B: 能 néng — I can.
为什么 asks; 因为 (yīnwèi) answers, with one reason in one clause; suǒyǐ carries the reason on to what happens.

Reading — the exchange at once.
[once you have stopped driving — read: the exchange through once without stopping, then answer the question below]
[question — say your answer, then pause 13 seconds]
Why isn't B going to class?

What you've built — a question and its reason.
你为什么不去 nǐ wèishénme bú qù — why aren't you going?
因为我没有车 yīnwèi wǒ méiyǒu chē — because I don't have a car.
yīnwèi wǒ hěn lèi, suǒyǐ wǒ bú qù — I'm tired, so I'm not going.
Listen. Nǐ wèishénme xuéxí Hànyǔ?
Speak. Answer it with 因为 (yīnwèi) and one reason of your own.
