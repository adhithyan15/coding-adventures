## You'll want to know: کارنامہ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C381-KAMYAB381-02]; assesses=[] -->

**کارنامہ** — *kārnāmā* — "a feat; an achievement".

> اگر اس نے یہ کیا تو بڑا کارنامہ ہو گا۔
>
> *agar us ne yih kiyā to baṛā kārnāmā ho gā.*
>
> If she does this, it will be a great feat.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**کارنامہ** (*kārnāmā*) is usually traced to Persian *kārnāma*, "a record of deeds": *kār*, "a deed", and *nāma*, "a writing".
