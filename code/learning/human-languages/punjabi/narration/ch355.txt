Punjabi, chapter 355: Riddles and Jokes.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ਪਹੇਲੀ (pahelī) — a riddle.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for a queen, then the Punjabi for magic.

You'll want to know: ਪਹੇਲੀ.
ਪਹੇਲੀ — pahelī — "a riddle".
ਮੇਰੇ ਦਾਦੇ ਕੋਲ ਬਹੁਤ ਪਹੇਲੀਆਂ ਹਨ। mere dāde kol bahut pahelīāṁ han. My grandfather knows lots of riddles.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ਪਹੇਲੀ (pahelī) is inherited from Sanskrit prahelikā, "riddle"; CDIAL lists Punjabi parhelī, "riddle", and pahelī under it (CDIAL 8911).

Lesson 2 of 5.

You'll want to know: ਬੁੱਝਣਾ.
ਬੁੱਝਣਾ — bujjhṇā — "to solve a riddle; to guess".
ਮੇਰੀ ਭੈਣ ਹਰ ਪਹੇਲੀ ਬੁੱਝਦੀ ਹੈ। merī bhaiṇ har pahelī bujjhdī hai. My sister solves every riddle.
Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਪਹੇਲੀ ਬੁੱਝੀ, maiṁ pahelī bujjhī.

The word, taken apart.
ਬੁੱਝਣਾ (bujjhṇā) is inherited from Sanskrit budhyate, "is awakened, understands"; CDIAL lists Punjabi bujjhṇā, "to guess, solve", under it (CDIAL 9279).

Lesson 3 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for a riddle, then the Punjabi for to solve a riddle.

You'll want to know: ਮਜ਼ਾਕ.
ਮਜ਼ਾਕ — mazāk — "a joke; joking".
ਮੇਰਾ ਦੋਸਤ ਬਹੁਤ ਮਜ਼ਾਕ ਕਰਦਾ ਹੈ। merā dost bahut mazāk kardā hai. My friend jokes a lot.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ਮਜ਼ਾਕ (mazāk) is a loan, usually traced through Persian to Arabic madhāq, "taste".

Lesson 4 of 5.
ਹਸਾਉਣਾ (hasāuṇā) — to make someone laugh.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for to solve a riddle, then the Punjabi for a joke.

You'll want to know: ਹਸਾਉਣਾ.
ਹਸਾਉਣਾ — hasāuṇā — "to make someone laugh".
ਅਮਨ ਸਭ ਨੂੰ ਹਸਾਉਂਦਾ ਹੈ। Aman sabh nū̃ hasāundā hai. Aman makes everyone laugh.
Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਬੱਚਿਆਂ ਨੂੰ ਹਸਾਇਆ, maiṁ bachchiāṁ nū̃ hasāiā.

The word, taken apart.
ਹਸਾਉਣਾ (hasāuṇā) is the causative partner of hassṇā, "to laugh", which goes back to a reconstructed hasyate, "one laughs"; CDIAL lists Punjabi hassṇā under it (CDIAL 14048).

Lesson 5 of 5.
ਛੇੜਨਾ (chheṛnā) — to tease.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for a joke, then the Punjabi for to make someone laugh.

You'll want to know: ਛੇੜਨਾ.
ਛੇੜਨਾ — chheṛnā — "to tease".
ਮੇਰਾ ਭਰਾ ਮੈਨੂੰ ਹਰ ਰੋਜ਼ ਛੇੜਦਾ ਹੈ। merā bharā mainū̃ har roz chheṛdā hai. My brother teases me every day.
Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਭੈਣ ਨੂੰ ਛੇੜਿਆ, maiṁ bhaiṇ nū̃ chheṛiā.

The word, taken apart.
ਛੇੜਨਾ (chheṛnā) is inherited from a reconstructed ciḍ, "be angry", with cheḍ, "make angry"; CDIAL lists Punjabi cheṛnā, "to provoke", under it (CDIAL 4794).
