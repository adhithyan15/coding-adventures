## You'll want to know: ویسا

**ویسا** — *vaisā* — "like that".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ویسا** (*vaisā*) is the far partner of *aisā*, "such, like this", which comes from Sanskrit *īdṛśa*, "such" (CDIAL 1611). CDIAL has no entry for *vaisā* itself, but it notes under *tādṛśa*, "suchlike", that *aisā* and *waisā* were shaped after Hindi *taisā*, "such" (CDIAL 5760). Where the *v-* came from it does not say.
