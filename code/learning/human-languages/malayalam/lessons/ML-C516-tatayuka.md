# തടയുക (taṭayuka) — to block; to stop

## You'll want to know: തടയുക
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C516-TATASSAM516-04]; assesses=[] -->

**തടയുക** — *taṭayuka* — "to block; to stop".

> പോലീസ് പാലത്തിൽ വണ്ടികൾ തടഞ്ഞു.
>
> *pōlīsŭ pālattil vaṇṭikaḷ taṭaññu.*
>
> The police stopped the vehicles at the bridge.

Past: തടഞ്ഞു, *taṭaññu*.

What is stopped takes no ending or -എ: വണ്ടികൾ തടഞ്ഞു.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**തടയുക** (*taṭayuka*) is native Dravidian, "to be obstructed; to stop" (DEDR 3031).
