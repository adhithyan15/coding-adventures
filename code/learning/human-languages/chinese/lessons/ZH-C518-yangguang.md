## You'll want to know: 阳光
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C518-SUN518-02]; assesses=[] -->

**阳光** — *yángguāng* — "sunshine".

> **这个季节的阳光最好** *zhège jìjié de yángguāng zuì hǎo* — The sunshine is at its best at this time of year.

The new character, in pieces: 阳 — six strokes in two pieces: on the left the ear piece, two strokes, as on the right of 那; on the right 日, four strokes. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**阳光** (*yángguāng*) is built from 阳 and 光 'light', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *yang* and *kwang* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription). The dictionary glosses the first as the yang of yin and yang and, after an older book, 'high and bright'; the light of the yang is the sun's.
