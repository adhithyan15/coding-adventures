Marwadi (Marwari), chapter 91: Afterwards, Monday, Tuesday, Thursday, Friday.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
पछै (pachhai) — afterwards, later.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for dawn, then the Marwadi for the day after tomorrow; the day before yesterday.

You'll want to know: पछै.
पछै — pachhai — "afterwards, later".

The word, taken apart.
पछै (pachhai) is a tadbhava, a word inherited and reshaped by sound change. It comes from paśca, "hinder part", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants. CDIAL lists Old Marwari pāchaï under it (CDIAL 7990). CDIAL says this form was drawn from Sanskrit paścāt, "behind, afterwards".

Lesson 2 of 5.
सोमवार (somvār) — Monday.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for the day after tomorrow; the day before yesterday, then the Marwadi for afterwards.

You'll want to know: सोमवार.
सोमवार — somvār — "Monday".

The word, taken apart.
सोमवार (somvār) is a Sanskrit compound, a tatsama: soma, "the moon" (CDIAL 13608), and vāra, "day of the week", whose inherited Old Marwari form is bāra, "turn, time" (CDIAL 11547).

Lesson 5 of 5.
सुकरवार (sukarvār) — Friday.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for Tuesday, then the Marwadi for Thursday.

You'll want to know: सुकरवार.
सुकरवार — sukarvār — "Friday".

The word, taken apart.
सुकरवार (sukarvār) is a Sanskrit compound, lightly reshaped, a semi-tatsama: śukra, "bright", also the planet Venus (CDIAL 12506), and vāra, "day of the week" (CDIAL 11547). Marwadi says the ś as s and splits the kr with a vowel.
