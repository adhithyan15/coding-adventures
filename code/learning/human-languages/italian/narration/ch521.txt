Italian, chapter 521: Pennelli e colori.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
la pittura — painting; paint.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for to make one's debut, then the Italian for director.

You'll want to know: la pittura.
la pittura — "painting; paint".
La pittura mi rilassa molto. Painting relaxes me a lot.
Also wet paint: pittura fresca.

The word, taken apart.
pittura is inherited from Latin pictūra "the art of painting" (EtymDB), from pingere "to paint".

What you've built.
One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.

Lesson 2 of 5.
il pennello — paintbrush.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for director, then the Italian for painting.

You'll want to know: il pennello.
il pennello — "paintbrush".
Lavo i pennelli con l'acqua calda. I wash the brushes in hot water.
A pennello — perfectly: ti sta a pennello.

The word, taken apart.
pennello is inherited from Vulgar Latin pēnicellus "a little brush" (Lewis & Short has pēnicillus), a little form of a word for a tail.

Lesson 4 of 5.

il cavalletto — "easel; stand".
Metto la tela sul cavalletto. I put the canvas on the easel.
A little cavallo "horse"; any frame on legs.

The word, taken apart.
cavalletto is a little cavallo "horse", inherited from Latin caballus "a nag" (Lewis & Short): a frame on legs.
