## You'll want to know: маршрут
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C417-MARSHRUTKA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**маршрут** (*marshrút*) — "a route".

> Этот автобус ходит по новому маршруту.
>
> *Étot avtóbus khódit po nóvomu marshrútu.*
>
> This bus runs on a new route.

Masculine. *Маршрут* is the planned way; *дорога* the road itself.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*маршрут* was borrowed from German *Marschroute* 'a line of march', built from *Marsch* 'march' and *Route* 'route' (EtymDB).
