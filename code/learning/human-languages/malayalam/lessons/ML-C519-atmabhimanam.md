# ആത്മാഭിമാനം (ātmābhimānaṁ) — self-respect

## You'll want to know: ആത്മാഭിമാനം
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C519-SAMADHANAM519-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ആത്മാഭിമാനം** — *ātmābhimānaṁ* — "self-respect".

> ആ വാക്കുകൾ അവന്റെ ആത്മാഭിമാനത്തെ വേദനിപ്പിച്ചു.
>
> *ā vākkukaḷ avanṟe ātmābhimānatte vēdanippiccu.*
>
> Those words hurt his self-respect.

ആത്മ- self + അഭിമാനം pride: pride in oneself, already familiar as അഭിമാനം.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ആത്മാഭിമാനം** (*ātmābhimānaṁ*) is a learned compound built from Sanskrit *ātman*, "self" (Monier-Williams), and *abhimāna*, "pride" (CDIAL 524).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
