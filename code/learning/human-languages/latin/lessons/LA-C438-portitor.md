## You'll want to know: portitor, portitōris
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C438-CONSCENDO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**portitor, portitōris** — "a ferryman, a boatman; a customs officer".

> Portitor viātōrēs ad alteram rīpam vehit.
>
> *The ferryman carries the travellers to the other bank.*

Third declension, masculine: *portitor, portitōris m.* Two words in one spelling: a ferryman, and the officer who takes harbour dues.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

Lewis & Short list two words *portitor*: the customs officer, built on *portus* 'harbour', and the carrier or ferryman, from the root of *portō* 'to carry'. EtymDB does not list it.
