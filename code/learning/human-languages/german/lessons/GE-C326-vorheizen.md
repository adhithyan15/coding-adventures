## You'll want to know: vorheizen
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C326-VORHEIZEN-01]; assesses=[] -->

**vorheizen** — "to preheat".

> Er heizte den Ofen auf hundertachtzig Grad vor.
>
> *He preheated the oven to a hundred and eighty degrees.*

Separable: *er heizt vor*, *er hat vorgeheizt*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*vorheizen* is built on *heizen* "to heat", from Old High German *heizen*, Proto-Germanic *haitijaną* "to make hot" (EtymDB), with *vor* "before".
