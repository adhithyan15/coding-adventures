## You'll want to know: распродажа
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C454-RASPRODAZHA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**распродажа** (*rasprodázha*) — "a sale (clearance)".

> В январе во всех магазинах распродажа.
>
> *V yanvaré vo vsekh magazínakh rasprodázha.*
>
> In January there is a sale in all the shops.

Feminine. *На распродаже* — in the sale.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*распродажа* is built from *рас-* 'off, all over' and *продажа* 'a sale', from *продать* 'to sell', on *дать* 'to give'. EtymDB lists *продажа* without a deeper source.
