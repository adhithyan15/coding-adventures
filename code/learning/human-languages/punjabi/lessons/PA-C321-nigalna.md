## You'll want to know: ਨਿਗਲਣਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C321-COLD321-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ਨਿਗਲਣਾ** — *nigalṇā* — "to swallow".

> ਮੈਂ ਦਵਾਈ ਪਾਣੀ ਨਾਲ ਨਿਗਲਦੀ ਹਾਂ।
>
> *maiṁ davāī pāṇī nāl nigaldī hāṁ.*
>
> I swallow the medicine with water.

Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਦਵਾਈ ਨਿਗਲੀ, *maiṁ davāī niglī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਨਿਗਲਣਾ** (*nigalṇā*) is inherited from Sanskrit *nigalati*, "swallows"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *nigalṇā*, "to swallow", under it (CDIAL 7163).
