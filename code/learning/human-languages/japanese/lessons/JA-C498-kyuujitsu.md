## You'll want to know: きゅうじつ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C498-REST498-01]; assesses=[] -->

**きゅうじつ** — *kyūjitsu* — "a day off; a holiday".

> **きゅうじつは うちで やすみます。** — *kyūjitsu wa uchi de yasumimasu* — "On my days off I rest at home."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**きゅうじつ** (*kyūjitsu*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *kyū*, "rest" (Middle Chinese *xjuw*) and *jitsu*, "day" (*nyit*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
