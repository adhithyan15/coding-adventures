## You'll want to know: कवटी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C478-HAD478-01]; assesses=[] -->

**कवटी** — *kavṭī* — "the skull".

> संग्रहालयात एका प्राचीन माणसाची कवटी होती.
>
> *saṅgrahālayāt ekā prācīn māṇsācī kavṭī hotī.*
>
> There was the skull of an ancient man in the museum.

Feminine.

Feminine; also the shell of a coconut.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**कवटी** (*kavṭī*) is a Marathi noun whose origin is not settled.
