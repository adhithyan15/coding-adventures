## You'll want to know: lunatique
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C469-HUMEUR-03]; assesses=[] -->

**lunatique** — "moody".

> Mon frère est lunatique : un jour il rit, un jour il boude.
>
> *My brother is moody: one day he laughs, the next he sulks.*

Not like English *lunatic*: it means moody, full of ups and downs.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*lunatique* is a learned borrowing from Late Latin *lūnāticus* "moonstruck" (Lewis & Short), built on *lūna* "the moon": moods were thought to follow its phases.
