## You'll want to know: वज़न
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C411-VAZAN411-01]; assesses=[] -->

**वज़न** — *vazan* — "weight".

> मेरे सामान का वज़न बीस किलो है।
>
> *mere sāmān kā vazan bīs kilo hai.*
>
> My luggage weighs twenty kilos.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**वज़न** (*vazan*) is usually traced to Arabic *wazn*, "weight, weighing", through Persian; Hindi breaks up the final zn with a vowel.
