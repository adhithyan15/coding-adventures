# ਔ — one vowel from the word for a woman

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-LEX-AURAT-217] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say **ਔਰਤ**, "a woman", the word from the previous lesson.

## Script you'll notice: ਔ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-SCRIPT-RECOG-AU-217]; assesses=[PA-LEX-AURAT-217, PA-SCRIPT-INDEPENDENT-A-01, PA-SCRIPT-AU-MATRA-01] -->

> **ਅ** + **ੌ** → **ਔ**

> **ਔ** — *au* — an independent Gurmukhi vowel

You know **ਅ** and the sign **ੌ**. Together they make the standing vowel **ਔ**,
the first letter of **ਔਰਤ**.

No stroke order is taught here: the track does not yet have a verified source
for one.

## Writing: copy what you can see
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-SCRIPT-RECOG-AU-217] -->

Keep **ਔ** in front of you. Trace its printed shape once with a finger, then
copy it once with a pencil while the model stays visible. Say *au* when you
finish.

> This is observe-and-copy with the model visible, not writing from memory.

- [YOU LOOK: at **ਔ**; YOU SAY: *au*]

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-LEX-AURAT-217, PA-SCRIPT-RECOG-AU-217] -->

[PAUSE 3s] Point to **ਔ**, say *au*, then say the whole word **ਔਰਤ**.
