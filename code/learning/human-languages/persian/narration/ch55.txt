Persian, chapter 55: Horse, Cow, Goat, Sheep, Bird.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
اسب (asb) — a horse.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a dog, then the Persian for a cat.

You'll want to know: اسب.
اسب — asb — "a horse".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: asb]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: asb, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say asb]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Persian for a dog, then the Persian for a cat, then say asb again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does اسب (asb) mean? ("A horse".) Say it once more.
