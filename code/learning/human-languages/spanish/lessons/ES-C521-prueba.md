## You'll want to know: la prueba
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C521-PISTA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**la prueba** — "the proof, the evidence; the test".

> No hay pruebas contra él.
>
> *There is no evidence against him.*

*Una prueba* is also a test: *una prueba de inglés*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*prueba* is built on *probar* 'to test, to try', which is inherited from Latin *probāre* 'to test, to prove' (Lewis & Short).
