## You'll want to know: ammaccare
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C299-SCONTRO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ammaccare** — "to dent, to bruise".

> Ho ammaccato la macchina nel parcheggio.
>
> *I dented the car in the car park.*

*Ammaccato* — dented; for fruit, bruised.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ammaccare* is traced in EtymDB to Latin *maccāre* "to bruise, to crush", a word whose own origin is not settled.
