## You'll want to know: 见报
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C293-PAPER293-03]; assesses=[] -->

**见报** — *jiànbào* — "to appear in the papers; to make the papers".

> **我们学校见报了** *wǒmen xuéxiào jiànbào le* — Our school was in the paper.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**见报** (*jiànbào*) is built from 见 'to see' and 报 'a newspaper', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *kenH* and *pawH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to view': to be seen in the paper.
