## You'll want to know: विरह
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C460-BICHHAD460-01]; assesses=[] -->

**विरह** — *virah* — "the pain of separation".

> पति के विरह में पत्नी बहुत उदास थी।
>
> *pati ke virah meṁ patnī bahut udās thī.*
>
> Parted from her husband, the wife was very sad.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**विरह** (*virah*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *viraha*, "parting, separation, esp. of lovers" (Monier-Williams). Songs and poems are full of it.
