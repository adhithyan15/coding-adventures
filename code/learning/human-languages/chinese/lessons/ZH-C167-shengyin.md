## You'll want to know: shēngyīn
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C167-PAST167-05]; assesses=[] -->

*shēngyīn* — "sound; voice; noise".

> *wàibian yǒu shénme shēngyīn* — What's that noise outside?

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*shēngyīn* is built from *shēng* and 音, both inherited from Middle Chinese, *syeng* and *'im* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first with this very word and the second as sound made with measure.

## What you've built
