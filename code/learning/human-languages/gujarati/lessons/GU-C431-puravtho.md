# પુરવઠો (puravṭho) — a supply (of goods, water or power)

## You'll want to know: પુરવઠો
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C431-STOCK431-02]; assesses=[] -->

**પુરવઠો** — *puravṭho* — "a supply (of goods, water or power)".

> ગામમાં પાણીનો પુરવઠો ઓછો છે.
>
> *gāmmā̃ pāṇīno puravṭho ocho che.*
>
> The water supply in the village is low.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**પુરવઠો** (*puravṭho*) seems to belong with the verb CDIAL lists for Gujarati, *pūrvũ*, "to fill", and Marathi *puraviṇẽ*, "to supply to the full", under Sanskrit *pūrayati*, "fills" (CDIAL 8335); CDIAL gives no form for પુરવઠો itself, so the link is hedged.
