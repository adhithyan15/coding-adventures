## You'll want to know: shēngxué
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C497-SELFSTUDY497-05]; assesses=[] -->

*shēngxué* — "to go on to a higher school; to go on to further study".

> *gāozhōng bìyè yǐhòu tā méiyǒu shēngxué* — After senior high he didn't go on to further study.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*shēngxué* is built from *shēng* 'to rise' and 学 'to learn', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *sying* and *haewk* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription); the sense 'to rise' is not given for the first there.
