# ૃ — a small below-consonant sign, -ṛ

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-SCRIPT-DHA-01, GU-SCRIPT-BHA-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Point to **ભ**, then **ધ**. Say *bha*, then *dha*. Do not copy either.

## Script — meet the shape
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-SCRIPT-VOCALIC-R-MATRA-01]; assesses=[] -->

Keep **ૃ** visible. This is not a free-standing letter. It is a small vowel sign
placed **below a consonant**, mainly in Sanskrit loans, and it gives vocalic
*ṛ*. Keep **ભૃ** visible: the new information is only the small sign below **ભ**.

## Writing — notice and trace
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-SCRIPT-VOCALIC-R-MATRA-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: observe-trace -->

Finger-trace the visible **ૃ** once. No stroke-order claim is being added here;
copy the printed model's small below-consonant shape.

## Writing — copy with the model
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-SCRIPT-VOCALIC-R-MATRA-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: guided-copy -->

Keep **ભ** visible. Copy only **ૃ** beneath it once, making **ભૃ**. Point to the
base, then the sign, and say *bhṛ*.

## Writing — recall after hiding
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-SCRIPT-VOCALIC-R-MATRA-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: delayed-copy -->
<!-- hl-activity: {"id":"GU-W02-vocalic-r-recall","kind":"text","assesses":["GU-SCRIPT-VOCALIC-R-MATRA-01"],"prompt":"Keep ભ visible but hide the sign. Add the Gujarati vocalic-r sign to make bhṛ.","answer":"ભૃ","accepted":["ૃ"],"feedback":{"correct":"ભૃ — the small vocalic-r sign sits below the consonant.","incorrect":"Look once at ભૃ, hide only the sign, and add the small below-consonant mark."},"response_seconds":20} -->

Hide only the sign while **ભ** remains visible. Add it once, then compare with **ભૃ**.

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-SCRIPT-VOCALIC-R-MATRA-01] -->

[PAUSE 3s] Is **ૃ** independent? (**No; it attaches below a consonant.**) Where
will it appear next? (**Only in the old root bhṛ.**)
