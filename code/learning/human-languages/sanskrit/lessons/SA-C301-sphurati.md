## You'll want to know: स्फुरति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C301-CAKRAVATA301-04]; assesses=[] -->

**स्फुरति** — *sphurati* — "flashes; flickers".

> मेघेषु विद्युत् स्फुरति।
>
> *megheṣu vidyut sphurati.*
>
> Lightning flashes in the clouds.

Past (it …): विद्युत् स्फुरितवती, *vidyut sphuritavatī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**स्फुरति** (*sphurati*) comes from the root √sphur, "to dart, quiver, flash"; MW compares English *spur* and *spurn* (MW).
