## You'll want to know: また
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C275-TSUNAMI275-05]; assesses=[] -->

**また** — *mata* — "once more; again".

> **また つなみが くるでしょう。** — *mata tsunami ga kuru deshō* — "Another tsunami will probably come."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**また** (*mata*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, and it is usually written in kana (JMdict).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
