Persian, chapter 346: Telling the story.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ماجرا (mâjarâ) — what happened, an affair; an adventure.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a storyteller, then the Persian for a narrator.

You'll want to know: ماجرا.
ماجرا (mâjarâ) — "what happened, an affair; an adventure".
او همه ماجرا را برای پلیس گفت. U hame-ye mâjarâ râ barâ-ye polis goft. He told the police the whole story of what happened.
Mâjarâ chi bud? "What was it all about?" Mâjarâju is "an adventurer".

The word, taken apart.
mâjarâ is usually explained as the Arabic phrase mā jarā, "what took place"; EtymDB lists the Persian word without a parent.

Lesson 2 of 5.
روایت (revâyat) — an account, a narrative.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a narrator, then the Persian for what happened.

You'll want to know: روایت.
روایت (revâyat) — "an account, a narrative".
روایت او با روایت پلیس فرق داشت. Revâyat-e u bâ revâyat-e polis farq dâsht. His account differed from the police account.
Be revâyat-e... "according to...". Also a traditional report of a saying.

The word, taken apart.
revâyat is usually traced to Arabic riwāya, "narration, transmission"; EtymDB lists no entry.

Lesson 3 of 5.
روایت کردن (revâyat kardan) — to narrate, to recount.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for what happened, then the Persian for an account.

You'll want to know: روایت کردن.
روایت کردن (revâyat kardan) — "to narrate, to recount".
فیلم داستان یک خانواده را روایت میکند. Film dâstân-e yek khânevâde râ revâyat mikonad. The film tells the story of a family.
Present revâyat mikonad. More literary than ta'rif kardan.

The word, taken apart.
revâyat kardan joins revâyat "an account", usually traced to Arabic riwāya, with kardan "to do", inherited Persian.

Lesson 4 of 5.
نقل کردن (naql kardan) — to relate, to pass on (a story).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for an account, then the Persian for to narrate.

You'll want to know: نقل کردن.
نقل کردن (naql kardan) — "to relate, to pass on (a story)".
پدرم این خاطره را بارها نقل کرده است. Pedaram in khâtere râ bâr-hâ naql karde ast. My father has told this memory many times.
Also "to quote": az u naql kard "he quoted him". Present naql mikonad.

The word, taken apart.
naql is borrowed from Arabic naql, "transport, transfer" (EtymDB): a story carried from one teller to the next. kardan "to do" is inherited Persian.

Lesson 5 of 5.
یادآوری کردن (yâdâvari kardan) — to remind.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to narrate, then the Persian for to relate.

You'll want to know: یادآوری کردن.
یادآوری کردن (yâdâvari kardan) — "to remind".
منشی جلسه فردا را به ما یادآوری کرد. Monshi jalase-ye fardâ râ be mâ yâdâvari kard. The secretary reminded us about tomorrow's meeting.
The thing takes râ, the person be. yâd "memory" + âvari "bringing".

The word, taken apart.
yâdâvari joins yâd "memory" and âvari "bringing", from âvardan "to bring": bringing to mind. All of it is native Persian.
