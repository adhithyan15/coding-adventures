# Review — how it felt, and what comes next

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[RU-GRAMMAR-RAD-SHORT-01, RU-GRAMMAR-USTAL-STATE-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "I'm glad", as a man and as a woman. (*Ya rad. Ya ráda.*)
Then "I'm tired". (*Ya ustál. Ya ustála.*)

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[RU-GRAMMAR-MNE-BYLO-01, RU-LEX-MECHTAT-01, RU-GRAMMAR-NADEYUS-CHTO-01, RU-LEX-C78-TOWN2-03, RU-LEX-C126-QUAL126-04, RU-LEX-C176-ACT176-01, RU-LEX-C216-KACH216-04, RU-LEX-C184-MESTO184-03] -->

Two lessons, and an experience now has a feeling and a future. **Рад**,
**рада**, **рады**, with *был* in the past. **Я устал**, a past form for
a state now. **Мне было** with an *-о* word for how a thing felt. And two
ways forward: **я надеюсь, что** … *будет*, and **я мечтаю** with an
infinitive or **о**.

[PAUSE 2s] A day at a museum, in three lines:

> **В музее мне было интересно. Вечером я устала, но я была рада.
> Я мечтаю вернуться туда.**

*Вернуться*, the partner of *возвращаться*, came with Anna's passport:
"I dream of going back there".
