# (perder la cita) — read it, then do it yourself

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-C464-CITA-01, ES-LEX-C464-CITA-03] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say **recordar** and **fallar**. The first message does one; the
second admits the other.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-C464-CITA-01, ES-LEX-C464-CITA-02, ES-LEX-C464-CITA-03] -->

Two messages, a day apart. Read both, then answer.

**1 — de la clínica**

> Le **recordamos** su cita con la **dentista** el jueves a las 10:15. Para
> anular, llame con 24 horas de antelación.

**2 — el jueves por la tarde**

> Buenas tardes. Siento no haber ido esta mañana; me **falló** el despertador y
> no me acordé hasta las once. ¿Puedo pedir otra cita?

[PAUSE 3s]
- [YOU SAY: when the appointment was, and who with]
- [YOU SAY: what you must do to cancel, and how far ahead]
- [YOU SAY: the two things that went wrong]

## Why it's said this way
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-C464-CITA-03, ES-LEX-C464-CITA-01] -->

The apology uses **both** verbs for remembering and they are doing different
work. **Me falló el despertador** — the alarm clock is the subject; it let the
writer down. Then **no me acordé** — *acordarse*, the reflexive one, for the
person's own failure to recall. Spanish separates the thing that failed from
the person who forgot, and the apology is more honest for it.

**La dentista**, feminine, so the clinic is naming a particular woman. And
*con la dentista*, not *con el dentista* — had the message said *el*, you would
know it was a man, since the noun itself never tells you.

**Siento no haber ido** is the standard apology frame: *sentir* plus a perfect
infinitive. Not *siento que no fui*, which a learner reaches for and a Spanish
speaker does not.

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-C464-CITA-01, ES-LEX-C464-CITA-02, ES-LEX-C464-CITA-03] -->

[PAUSE 3s] Now your turn, out loud and then in writing. Four lines: as the
clinic, remind somebody of a dental appointment and say how to cancel; then as
the patient, apologise for missing it, say what let you down, and ask for
another.

Then check yourself: did *fallar* go to the thing and *acordarse* to the
person, and did *acordarse* keep its *de*?
