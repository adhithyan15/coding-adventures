## You'll want to know: aplaudir
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C759-ESCENARIO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**aplaudir** — "to applaud, to clap".

> Al final, todos aplaudieron.
>
> *At the end, everyone clapped.*

*El aplauso* — applause.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*aplaudir* is a learned borrowing from Latin *applaudere* "to clap the hands together" (EtymDB), built on *plaudere* "to strike, to clap".
