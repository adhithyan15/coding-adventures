## You'll want to know: indignor, indignārī
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C493-IRA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**indignor, indignārī** — "to be indignant, resent".

> Cīvēs indignantur, quod tribūtum magnum est.
>
> *The citizens are indignant that the tax is large.*

Deponent: *indignor, indignārī, indignātus sum*. To count a thing unworthy, and be angry at it. English *indignant*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**indignor** is built on *indignus* "unworthy" (Lewis & Short; EtymDB). English *indignant* comes from its present participle.
