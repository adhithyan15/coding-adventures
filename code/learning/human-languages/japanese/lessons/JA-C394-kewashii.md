## You'll want to know: けわしい
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C394-SAKAMICHI394-02]; assesses=[] -->

**けわしい** — *kewashii* — "steep; rugged".

> **やまの みちは けわしいです。** — *yama no michi wa kewashii desu* — "The mountain path is steep."

An i-adjective.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**けわしい** (*kewashii*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict).
