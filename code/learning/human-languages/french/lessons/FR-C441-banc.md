## You'll want to know: le banc
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C441-MOBILIER-01]; assesses=[] -->

**le banc** — "the bench".

> Deux vieux messieurs lisent sur un banc.
>
> *Two old men are reading on a bench.*

*Un banc de poissons* — a shoal of fish.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*banc* comes from a Germanic word for 'bench' (EtymDB), and English *bench* is a cousin.
