## You'll want to know: la morte
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C313-LUTTO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**la morte** — "death".

> La morte del nonno ci ha cambiato la vita.
>
> *Grandfather's death changed our lives.*

*Annoiarsi a morte* — to be bored to death.
