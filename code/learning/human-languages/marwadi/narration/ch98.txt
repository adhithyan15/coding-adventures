Marwadi (Marwari), chapter 98: Right, Town square, Crossroads, Turn, Path.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
जीमणो (jīmaṇo) — right (the side).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for opposite, then the Marwadi for left.

You'll want to know: जीमणो.
जीमणो — jīmaṇo — "right (the side)".

The word, taken apart.
जीमणो (jīmaṇo) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from jemana, "eating". CDIAL lists Old Marwari jīmaṇa under it (CDIAL 5268). The Old Marwari row means "food, meal", and Old Gujarati jimaṇaüṃ means "to the righthand side": the right is the eating hand.

The word, taken apart.
चौक (chauk) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from catuṣka, "set of four; square courtyard". CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry (CDIAL 4629); the match is through Hindi cauk. It is built on catur, "four".

Lesson 3 of 5.
चौराहो (chaurāho) — a crossroads.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for right, then the Marwadi for a town square.

You'll want to know: चौराहो.
चौराहो — chaurāho — "a crossroads".

The word, taken apart.
चौराहो (chaurāho) is a meeting of four roads: chau-, "four", as in chauk (CDIAL 4629), with rāh, "road", usually traced to Persian rāh, "road, way". CDIAL has no entry for the compound.

Lesson 4 of 5.
मोड़ (moṛ) — a turn, a bend.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for a town square, then the Marwadi for a crossroads.

You'll want to know: मोड़.
मोड़ — moṛ — "a turn, a bend".

The word, taken apart.
मोड़ (moṛ) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from muṭati, "twists". CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry (CDIAL 10186); the match is through Hindi moṛnā and Gujarati moṛvũ. It is the noun of the verb "to turn, to bend". CDIAL notes that the Sanskrit verb partly ran together with a look-alike it takes from Dravidian.
