Marwadi (Marwari), chapter 193: Rupees and Coins.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
रुपियो (rupiyo) — a rupee.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for to print, then the Marwadi for to mend.

You'll want to know: रुपियो.
रुपियो — rupiyo — "a rupee".
म्हारी जेब मांय एक रुपियो है। mhārī jeb mā̃y ek rupiyo hai. I have one rupee in my pocket.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
रुपियो (rupiyo) is inherited from Sanskrit rūpya, "bearing a stamp; silver"; CDIAL lists Marwari rūpo, "silver", under it, and sets up a second form, rūpiya-, which the coin's name probably continues (CDIAL 10805).

Lesson 2 of 5.
सिक्को (sikko) — a coin.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for to mend, then the Marwadi for a rupee.

You'll want to know: सिक्को.
सिक्को — sikko — "a coin".
ये सिक्को भारी है। ye sikko bhārī hai. This coin is heavy.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
सिक्को (sikko) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic sikka, "a die for stamping coins": the coin is named for its stamp.

Lesson 5 of 5.
चुकावणो (chukāvṇo) — to pay; to settle (a bill).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for a banknote, then the Marwadi for in cash.

You'll want to know: चुकावणो.
चुकावणो — chukāvṇo — "to pay; to settle (a bill)".
आप पैसा अबार चुकावो। āp paisā abār chukāvo. Please pay now.
Past (I …): म्हैं पैसा चुकाया, mhaĩ paisā chukāyā.

The word, taken apart.
चुकावणो (chukāvṇo) is the causative of a verb inherited from a reconstructed cukk, "fall short of, stop": CDIAL lists Marwari cūkṇo, "to be completed", and Hindi cukānā, "to settle" (CDIAL 4848). To pay a bill is to make it complete.
