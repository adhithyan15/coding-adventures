## You'll want to know: mercanteggiare
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C457-CASSA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**mercanteggiare** — "to haggle, to bargain".

> Al mercato si può mercanteggiare un po'.
>
> *At the market you can haggle a bit.*

More common in speech: *tirare sul prezzo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*mercanteggiare* is built on *mercante* 'merchant', which is inherited from a form of Latin *mercārī* 'to trade', from *merx* 'goods' (Lewis & Short).
