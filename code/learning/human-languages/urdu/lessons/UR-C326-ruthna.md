# روٹھنا (rūṭhnā) — to sulk; to take offence

## You'll want to know: روٹھنا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C326-LANAT326-04]; assesses=[] -->

**روٹھنا** — *rūṭhnā* — "to sulk; to take offence".

> بہن ہم سے روٹھ گئی۔
>
> *bahan ham se rūṭh gaī.*
>
> My sister went off in a sulk with us.

Past (she …): وہ روٹھ گئی, *voh rūṭh gaī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**روٹھنا** (*rūṭhnā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *rūṭhnā*, "to be offended", under Sanskrit *ruṣṭa*, "angry" (CDIAL 10791). The person you sulk with takes **سے**.
