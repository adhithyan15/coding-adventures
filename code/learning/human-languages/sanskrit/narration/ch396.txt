Lesson 1 of 5.
ध्वनति (dhvanati) — sounds; rings out.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for takes leave, then the Sanskrit for a wandering ascetic.

You'll want to know: ध्वनति.
ध्वनति — dhvanati — "sounds; rings out".
मन्दिरे शंखः ध्वनति। mandire śaṅkhaḥ dhvanati. A conch sounds in the temple.
Past (it …): शंखः ध्वनितवान्, śaṅkhaḥ dhvanitavān.

The word, taken apart.
ध्वनति (dhvanati) comes from the root √dhvan, "to sound", which also gives dhvani, "a sound"; KRM gives its participle dhvanita (MW).

Lesson 2 of 5.
दर्शनम् (darśanam) — a sight of the deity; seeing.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a wandering ascetic, then the Sanskrit for rings out.

You'll want to know: दर्शनम्.
दर्शनम् — darśanam — "a sight of the deity; seeing".
यात्रिकाः प्रातः मन्दिरे दर्शनं लभन्ते। yātrikāḥ prātaḥ mandire darśanaṃ labhante. In the morning the pilgrims are granted a sight of the deity in the temple.
A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
दर्शनम् (darśanam) is built on the root √dṛś, "to see", with the ending -ana; MW gives darśana as seeing, sight and an audience, and the sight of a deity at a shrine is that sense in pilgrims' use (MW).

Lesson 3 of 5.
प्रतिमा (pratimā) — an image of a deity.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for rings out, then the Sanskrit for a sight of the deity.

You'll want to know: प्रतिमा.
प्रतिमा — pratimā — "an image of a deity".
मन्दिरे सुन्दरी प्रतिमा अस्ति। mandire sundarī pratimā asti. There is a beautiful image in the temple.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
प्रतिमा (pratimā) is built from prati, "against, in return", and the root √mā, "to measure": a thing measured against another, a likeness; MW gives pratimā as an image, a figure of a god (MW).

Lesson 4 of 5.
गर्भगृहम् (garbhagṛham) — an inner sanctum; the inner room of a temple.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a sight of the deity, then the Sanskrit for an image of a deity.

You'll want to know: गर्भगृहम्.
गर्भगृहम् — garbhagṛham — "an inner sanctum; the inner room of a temple".
प्रतिमा गर्भगृहे तिष्ठति। pratimā garbhagṛhe tiṣṭhati. The image stands in the inner sanctum.
A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
गर्भगृहम् (garbhagṛham) is built from garbha, "the womb, the inside", and gṛha, "a house"; MW gives the compound as an inner room and as the sanctuary of a temple where the image stands (MW).

Lesson 5 of 5.
भक्तः (bhaktaḥ) — a devotee.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for an image of a deity, then the Sanskrit for an inner sanctum.

You'll want to know: भक्तः.
भक्तः — bhaktaḥ — "a devotee".
भक्तः प्रतिमां नमति। bhaktaḥ pratimāṃ namati. The devotee bows to the image.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
भक्तः (bhaktaḥ) comes from the root √bhaj, "to share, to worship", which also gives bhajati, "shares, worships": MW gives bhakta as devoted or attached to, and as a worshipper (MW).
