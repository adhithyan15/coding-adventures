Punjabi, chapter 358: What We Study.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ਵਿਸ਼ਾ (vishā) — a school subject; a topic.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for a slate, then the Punjabi for a page.

You'll want to know: ਵਿਸ਼ਾ.
ਵਿਸ਼ਾ — vishā — "a school subject; a topic".
ਸਕੂਲ ਵਿੱਚ ਮੇਰੇ ਛੇ ਵਿਸ਼ੇ ਹਨ। sakūl vich mere chhe vishe han. I have six subjects at school.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ਵਿਸ਼ਾ (vishā) is taken over from Sanskrit viṣaya, "sphere, scope", a tatsama (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 5 of 5.
ਪੜ੍ਹਾਉਣਾ (paṛhāuṇā) — to teach a subject; to give lessons.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for science, then the Punjabi for history.

You'll want to know: ਪੜ੍ਹਾਉਣਾ.
ਪੜ੍ਹਾਉਣਾ — paṛhāuṇā — "to teach a subject; to give lessons".
ਮੇਰੀ ਮਾਤਾ ਸਕੂਲ ਵਿੱਚ ਗਣਿਤ ਪੜ੍ਹਾਉਂਦੀ ਹੈ। merī mātā sakūl vich gaṇit paṛhāundī hai. My mother teaches mathematics at a school.
Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਗਣਿਤ ਪੜ੍ਹਾਇਆ, maiṁ gaṇit paṛhāiā.

The word, taken apart.
ਪੜ੍ਹਾਉਣਾ (paṛhāuṇā) is the causative partner of paṛhnā, from Sanskrit paṭhati, "recites, repeats aloud"; CDIAL lists Punjabi paṛhāuṇā, "teaches", under it (CDIAL 7712).
