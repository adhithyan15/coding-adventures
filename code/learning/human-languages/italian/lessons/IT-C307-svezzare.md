## You'll want to know: svezzare
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C307-NASCITA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**svezzare** — "to wean".

> Abbiamo svezzato il bambino a sei mesi.
>
> *We weaned the baby at six months.*

Literally to take out of a habit: *vezzo* is an old word for habit.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*svezzare* is built on *vezzo* "habit", from Latin *vitium* "a fault" (Lewis & Short), with *s-* "out of".
