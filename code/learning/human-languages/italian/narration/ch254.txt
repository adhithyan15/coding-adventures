Lesson 2 of 5.
il volante — a steering wheel.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for a form, then the Italian for a vehicle seat.

You'll want to know: il volante.
il volante — "a steering wheel".

The word, taken apart.
volante is the present participle of volare, "to fly", used as a noun, after French volant: the wheel that spins. volare from Latin volare. (EtymDB 2.1).
