## You'll want to know: भूकम्पः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C300-BHUKAMPA300-01]; assesses=[] -->

**भूकम्पः** — *bhūkampaḥ* — "an earthquake".

> ह्यः रात्रौ भूकम्पः अभवत्।
>
> *hyaḥ rātrau bhūkampaḥ abhavat.*
>
> There was an earthquake last night.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**भूकम्पः** (*bhūkampaḥ*) is built from *bhū*, "the earth", and *kampa*, "trembling"; MW gives *bhūkampa* as an earthquake (MW).

## What you've built
