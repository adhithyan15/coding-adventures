## You'll want to know: undicht
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C428-ABFLUSS-04]; assesses=[] -->

**undicht** — "leaky, not watertight".

> Das Dach über dem Bad ist undicht.
>
> *The roof over the bathroom leaks.*

*Dicht* means tight, sealed; *undicht* is its opposite.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*undicht* is built from *un-* 'not' and *dicht* 'tight, dense', an inherited Germanic word (EtymDB lists *dicht*).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.
