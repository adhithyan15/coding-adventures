## You'll want to know: ఉపకారవేతనం
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C414-UPANYASAM414-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ఉపకారవేతనం** — *upakāravētanaṁ* — "a scholarship".

> ఆమెకు చదువు కోసం ఉపకారవేతనం వచ్చింది.
>
> *āmeku caduvu kōsaṁ upakāravētanaṁ vaccindi.*
>
> She got a scholarship for her studies.

With వచ్చు: ఉపకారవేతనం వచ్చింది, a scholarship came (to her).

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ఉపకారవేతనం** (*upakāravētanaṁ*) is built from Sanskrit *upakāra*, "help, use" (CDIAL 2141), and *vetana*, "wages, hire" (Monier-Williams): pay given as help.
