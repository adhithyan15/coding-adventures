## You'll want to know: सासू
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C259-SASU259-01]; assesses=[] -->

**सासू** — *sāsū* — "a mother-in-law".

> म्हारी सासू घणी भली है।
>
> *mhārī sāsū ghaṇī bhalī hai.*
>
> My mother-in-law is very kind.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सासू** (*sāsū*) is inherited from Sanskrit *śvaśrū*, "a husband's or wife's mother". CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry; it lists Hindi *sāsū* (CDIAL 12759).
