# ਈ ਇ ਉ ਠ — the pieces that were met once

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-SCRIPT-INDEPENDENT-I-01, PA-SCRIPT-INDEPENDENT-U-01] -->

[PAUSE 3s] Two standing vowels. Read **ਇ**, then **ਉ**, and say which vowel each
one carries when nothing precedes it.

## Script: four standing shapes and one mark
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-SCRIPT-RECOG-EERI-01, PA-SCRIPT-INDEPENDENT-I-01, PA-SCRIPT-INDEPENDENT-U-01, PA-SCRIPT-TTHA-01, PA-SCRIPT-HORA-01] -->

| shape | name | what it is |
|---|---|---|
| **ਈ** | īṛī | a standing long **ī** |
| **ਇ** | iṛī | a standing short **i** |
| **ਉ** | ūṛā | a standing **u** |
| **ਠ** | ṭhaṭṭhā | a consonant — the curled retroflex |
| **ੋ** | horā | a **mark**, not a letter: long **o**, written above |

The first three are the ones that sit at the front of a word with no consonant
to lean on. The last is the opposite kind of thing entirely — it cannot stand
anywhere, and it goes above the letter it belongs to.
