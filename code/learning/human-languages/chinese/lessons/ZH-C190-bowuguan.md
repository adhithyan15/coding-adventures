## You'll want to know: bówùguǎn
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C190-TOWN190-05]; assesses=[] -->

*bówùguǎn* — "museum".

> *bówùguǎn jīntiān bù kāimén* — The museum is closed today.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*bówùguǎn* is built from *bó* 'broad', *wù* 'a thing' and *guǎn* 'a lodging, a hall', all inherited from Middle Chinese, *pak*, *mjut* and *kwanH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription): a hall of all kinds of things.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for asking where things are.
