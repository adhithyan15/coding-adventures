## You'll want to know: रिझाना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C375-ASHA375-05]; assesses=[] -->

**रिझाना** — *rijhānā* — "to charm, to win over".

> अगर तुम अच्छा गाना गाओगे तो लोगों को रिझा लोगे।
>
> *agar tum acchā gānā gāoge to logoṁ ko rijhā loge.*
>
> If you sing a good song, you'll win people over.

Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने लोगों को रिझाया, *usne logoṁ ko rijhāyā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**रिझाना** (*rijhānā*) is a tadbhava, inherited from Sanskrit *ṛdhati*, "thrives" (CDIAL 2457). CDIAL glosses the Hindi *rijhānā* "to enchant".
