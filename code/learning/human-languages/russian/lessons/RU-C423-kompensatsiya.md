## You'll want to know: компенсация
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C423-ZADERZHKA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**компенсация** (*kompensátsiya*) — "compensation".

> За задержку пассажиры получили компенсацию.
>
> *Za zadérzhku passazhíry poluchíli kompensátsiyu.*
>
> The passengers got compensation for the delay.

Feminine; formal.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*компенсация* is usually traced through French *compensation* to Latin *compensātiō* 'a balancing' (EtymDB lists both), from *compensāre* 'to weigh one thing against another' (Lewis & Short).
