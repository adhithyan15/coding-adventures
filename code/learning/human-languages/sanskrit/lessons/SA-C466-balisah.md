## You'll want to know: बालिशः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C466-MEDHA466-04]; assesses=[] -->

**बालिशः** — *bāliśaḥ* — "childish; foolish".

> वृद्धः अपि सः बालिशः अस्ति।
>
> *vṛddhaḥ api saḥ bāliśaḥ asti.*
>
> Though old, he is childish.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**बालिशः** (*bāliśaḥ*) MW gives "young, childish, puerile, ignorant, simple, foolish" and "a fool, a simpleton"; MW gives no derivation, so its origin is uncertain (MW).
