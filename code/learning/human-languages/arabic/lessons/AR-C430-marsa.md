## You'll want to know: مرسى
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C430-MARFA430-02]; assesses=[] -->

**مرسى** — *marsā* — "an anchorage; a mooring".

> وجد القبطان مرسى آمنا للقارب.
>
> *wajada al-qubṭān marsā āminan li-l-qārib.*
>
> The captain found a safe anchorage for the boat.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**مرسى** (*marsā*) is built on the native Arabic root *r-s-w*, "to be firm, fixed" (Lane). It is the place-noun of *rasā*, "to anchor".
