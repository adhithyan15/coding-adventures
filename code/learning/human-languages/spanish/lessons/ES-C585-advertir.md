## You'll want to know: advertir
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C585-PROPONE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**advertir** — "to warn; to notice".

> Te advierto que es difícil.
>
> *I am warning you that it is difficult.*

Like *sentir*: *advierto, advirtió*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*advertir* is inherited from Latin *advertere* 'to turn towards, to give attention', built from *ad* and *vertere* 'to turn' (EtymDB).
