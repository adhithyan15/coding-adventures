## You'll want to know: はいゆう
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C458-CAREER458-03]; assesses=[] -->

**はいゆう** — *haiyū* — "an actor".

> **あの はいゆうの えいがを みました。** — *ano haiyū no eiga o mimashita* — "I saw a film with that actor."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**はいゆう** (*haiyū*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *hai*, "actor" (Middle Chinese *beaj*) and *yū*, "actor" (*'juw*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
