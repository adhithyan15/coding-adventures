Telugu, chapter 354: A Plot Uncovered.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
కుట్ర (kuṭra) — a conspiracy; a plot.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to appear suddenly, then the Telugu for to detect.

You'll want to know: కుట్ర.
కుట్ర — kuṭra — "a conspiracy; a plot".
రాజు మీద ఒక కుట్ర జరిగింది. rāju mīda oka kuṭra jarigindi. There was a plot against the king.
కుట్ర (kuṭra) పన్ను, to hatch a plot.

The word, taken apart.
కుట్ర (kuṭra) is a Telugu word for a secret plot; its origin is not settled.

అతను అబద్ధాలతో అందరినీ మభ్యపెట్టాడు. atanu abaddhālatō andarinī mabhyapeṭṭāḍu. He misled everyone with lies.
Past (I …): మభ్యపెట్టాను, mabhyapeṭṭānu.
To cloud someone's mind; a written-style word.

The word, taken apart.
మభ్యపెట్టు (mabhyapeṭṭu) ends in native peṭṭu, "to put" (DEDR 4390); the origin of the first part is not settled.

Lesson 3 of 5.
బయటపెట్టు (bayaṭapeṭṭu) — to expose; to bring out.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a conspiracy, then the Telugu for to mislead.

You'll want to know: బయటపెట్టు.
బయటపెట్టు — bayaṭapeṭṭu — "to expose; to bring out".
విలేకరి ఆ కుట్రను బయటపెట్టాడు. vilēkari ā kuṭranu bayaṭapeṭṭāḍu. The reporter exposed the plot.
Past (I …): బయటపెట్టాను, bayaṭapeṭṭānu.
బయట, outside, with పెట్టు, to put: to put a secret outside.

The word, taken apart.
బయటపెట్టు (bayaṭapeṭṭu) is a native compound: bayaṭa, "outside", which sits beside bayalu, "the outside" (DEDR 3940), and peṭṭu, "to put" (DEDR 4390).

Lesson 4 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to mislead, then the Telugu for to expose.

You'll want to know: కప్పిపుచ్చు.
కప్పిపుచ్చు — kappipuccu — "to cover up; to hush up".
అధికారులు ఆ తప్పును కప్పిపుచ్చారు. adhikārulu ā tappunu kappipuccāru. The officials covered up the mistake.
Past (I …): కప్పిపుచ్చాను, kappipuccānu.
Built on కప్పు, to cover.

The word, taken apart.
కప్పిపుచ్చు (kappipuccu) is a native compound built on kappu, "to cover, conceal" (DEDR 1221), which also gives కప్పు, a roof.

Lesson 5 of 5.
గూఢచారి (gūḍhacāri) — a spy.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to expose, then the Telugu for to cover up.

You'll want to know: గూఢచారి.
గూఢచారి — gūḍhacāri — "a spy".
శత్రువు గూఢచారి కోటలోకి వచ్చాడు. śatruvu gūḍhacāri kōṭalōki vaccāḍu. An enemy spy came into the fort.
Plural: గూఢచారులు, gūḍhacārulu.
గూఢ, hidden, and చారి, one who goes about.

The word, taken apart.
గూఢచారి (gūḍhacāri) is borrowed from Sanskrit: gūḍha, "hidden" (CDIAL 4223), and cārin, "moving; a spy" (CDIAL 4765).
