## You'll want to know: вскоре
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C272-ZARANEE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**вскоре** (*vskóre*) — "soon after, shortly".

> Вскоре пошёл снег.
>
> *Vskóre poshyól sneg.*
>
> Soon after, it began to snow.

It looks back from a point in the story: soon after that. *Вскоре после обеда* — shortly after lunch.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*вскоре* is built on *в* and *скоро* "quickly", from *скорый* "fast", which is inherited through Old East Slavic *skorŭ* from Proto-Slavic *skorŭ* "fast" (EtymDB).
