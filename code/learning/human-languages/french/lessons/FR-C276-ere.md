## You'll want to know: l'ère
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C276-EPOQUE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**l'ère** — "the era, the age".

> Nous vivons dans l'ère d'internet.
>
> *We live in the internet age.*

Feminine: *une nouvelle ère*, a new era.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ère* was borrowed from Late Latin *aera* "a counted number, an era" (EtymDB).
