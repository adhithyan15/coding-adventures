## Script — the left of 站 on top, then 日 and 心
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-SCRIPT-YI-IDEA-01]; assesses=[] -->

> 意

**Three pieces, and you write every one of them already.**

**The left of 站, five strokes**, here on top and wider: the dot, the bar, a dot on the left, a left-falling stroke on the right, and a long horizontal. In 站 that last stroke rises; here it lies flat and runs the whole width.

**日, four strokes**, in the middle.

**心, four strokes**, at the foot: the left dot, the curve along the foot that hooks up, the middle dot, the right dot.

5 + 4 + 4 = thirteen.
