# તે (te) — he, she, it

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-PERFORMANCE-CONDITION-01, GU-CONCEPT-C03-HUN-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] From the chapter behind you, a condition. And from the third chapter
of this book, the word for **I**.

- [YOU RECALL: *jo … to …*, and *hũ*]

You have been able to talk about yourself since chapter 3 and about the person
in front of you since chapter 2. You have never been able to talk about anybody
**absent**.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-PRON-TE-01]; assesses=[GU-CONCEPT-C03-HUN-01] -->

> **તે** — *te* — **he**, **she**, or **it**

One word, three English ones. Gujarati's third person does not make you choose a
gender before you speak — which is a real convenience, and occasionally a real
ambiguity.

**તે** also means *that*, pointing at something away from both speakers. The
pronoun and the pointing word are the same word, which is where the **તો** of the
last chapter came from as well.
