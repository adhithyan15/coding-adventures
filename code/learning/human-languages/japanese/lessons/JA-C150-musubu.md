## You'll want to know: むすぶ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C150-GLOVES150-04]; assesses=[] -->

**むすぶ** — *musubu* — "to tie; to knot".

> **くつの ひもを むすびます。** — *kutsu no himo o musubimasu* — "I tie my shoelaces."

Polite **むすびます**, *musubimasu*; a godan verb.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**むすぶ** (*musubu*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi, and its older origin is not settled (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
