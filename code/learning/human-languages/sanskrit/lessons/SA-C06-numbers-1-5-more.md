## You'll want to know: numbers have gender
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-GRAMMAR-NUMERAL-GENDER, SA-HISTORY-INDO-ARYAN-NEUTER-FORMS]; assesses=[] -->

**Sanskrit numbers have gender.** Look back at the last column of the table in the last lesson. These
same words, in Hindi, Marathi, Bengali, Gujarati, and Punjabi, mostly
descend from the **neuter** forms — *trī́ṇi* and *catvā́ri*, not *tráyaḥ*
and *catvā́raḥ*. Keep that column in view; the daughter languages inherit from it.
