## You'll want to know: دبانا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C481-BETTER481-04]; assesses=[] -->

**دبانا** — *dabānā* — "to press gently".

> بیٹی نے ابو کا سر دبایا۔
>
> *beṭī ne abbū kā sar dabāyā.*
>
> The daughter gently pressed her father's head.

Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے کندھا دبایا, *us ne kandhā dabāyā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**دبانا** (*dabānā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *dābnā*, "to press", under *dabb*, "press", a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 6173).
