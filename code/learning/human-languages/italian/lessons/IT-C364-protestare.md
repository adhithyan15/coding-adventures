## You'll want to know: protestare
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C364-PROTESTA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**protestare** — "to protest".

> I cittadini protestano contro il nuovo parcheggio.
>
> *The citizens are protesting against the new car park.*

*Protestare contro qualcosa*. Stress: *protèsto*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*protestare* is a learned borrowing from Latin *prōtestārī* "to declare publicly" (Lewis & Short), built from *prō* "before" and *testārī* "to bear witness", on *testis* "a witness".
