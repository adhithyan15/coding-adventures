## You'll want to know: सेकणो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C173-CHULHO173-05]; assesses=[] -->

**सेकणो** — *sekṇo* — "to toast (on a fire or a griddle)".

> मां आग माथै पापड़ सेकै है।
>
> *mā̃ āg māthai pāpaṛ sekai hai.*
>
> Mother toasts a papad over the fire.

Past (I …): म्हैं रोटी सेकी, *mhaĩ roṭī sekī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सेकणो** (*sekṇo*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*sekk*, "heat, roast"; CDIAL lists Old Marwari *sekaï*, "heats, roasts" (CDIAL 13581).
