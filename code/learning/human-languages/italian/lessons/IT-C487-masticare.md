## You'll want to know: masticare
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C487-STOMACO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**masticare** — "to chew".

> Mastica bene prima di mandare giù.
>
> *Chew well before you swallow.*

*La gomma da masticare* — chewing gum.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*masticare* is inherited from Late Latin *masticāre* "to chew" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short), usually linked to Greek *mastikhân* "to gnash the teeth".
