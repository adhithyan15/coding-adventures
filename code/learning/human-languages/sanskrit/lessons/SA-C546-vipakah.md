## You'll want to know: विपाकः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C546-VIPAKA546-05]; assesses=[] -->

**विपाकः** — *vipākaḥ* — "a consequence".

> यदि कर्म उत्तमम् अस्ति, तर्हि विपाकः अपि उत्तमः।
>
> *yadi karma uttamam asti, tarhi vipākaḥ api uttamaḥ.*
>
> If a deed is good, its outcome is good too.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**विपाकः** (*vipākaḥ*) is built on *vi* and the root √pac, "to cook, to ripen", which also gives *pacati*; MW gives *vipāka* as ripening, maturing, the result or consequence of actions (MW).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
