Marathi, chapter 321: Guests and a Feast.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
पंगत (paṅgat) — a row of diners.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a pilgrimage, then the Marathi for an auspicious time.

You'll want to know: पंगत.
पंगत — paṅgat — "a row of diners".
लग्नात पाहुण्यांच्या पंगती बसल्या. lagnāt pāhuṇyāñcyā paṅgtī basalyā. At the wedding the guests sat down in rows to eat.
Feminine. Plural: पंगती, paṅgtī.
पंगत (paṅgat) बसणे, a row sits down to a meal; plural पंगती.

The word, taken apart.
पंगत (paṅgat) is usually traced to Sanskrit paṅkti, 'a row, a troop'. CDIAL lists the inherited Marathi pā̃t, 'a row', under that word (CDIAL 7646) but not paṅgat itself, so its path is not settled.

You'll want to know: पाहुणचार.
पाहुणचार — pāhuṇcār — "hosting guests; hospitality".
त्या गावातल्या लोकांचा पाहुणचार आम्ही कधीच विसरणार नाही. tyā gāvātalyā lokāñcā pāhuṇcār āmhī kadhīc visarṇār nāhī. We will never forget the hospitality of the people of that village.
Masculine.
Built on पाहुणा, a guest.

The word, taken apart.
पाहुणचार (pāhuṇcār) is built on pāhuṇā, 'a guest', inherited from Sanskrit prāhuṇa (CDIAL 8973), with the ending -cār.

Lesson 3 of 5.
स्नेहसंमेलन (snehsammelan) — a get-together; an annual social.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a row of diners, then the Marathi for hosting guests.

You'll want to know: स्नेहसंमेलन.
स्नेहसंमेलन — snehsammelan — "a get-together; an annual social".
शाळेचं वार्षिक स्नेहसंमेलन पुढच्या आठवड्यात आहे. śāḷecaṁ vārṣik snehsammelan puḍhacyā āṭhvaḍyāt āhe. The school's annual get-together is next week.
Neuter.
स्नेह, affection, + संमेलन, a gathering.

The word, taken apart.
स्नेहसंमेलन (snehsammelan) is a modern compound built from Sanskrit sneha, 'affection', and sammelana, 'a meeting, a gathering' (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 4 of 5.
उपस्थित राहणे (upasthit rāhṇe) — to attend; to be present.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for hosting guests, then the Marathi for a get-together.

You'll want to know: उपस्थित राहणे.
उपस्थित राहणे — upasthit rāhṇe — "to attend; to be present".
कार्यक्रमाला गावातले सगळे लोक उपस्थित राहिले. kāryakramālā gāvātle sagḷe lok upasthit rāhile. All the people of the village attended the event.
Past (he …): तो उपस्थित राहिला, to upasthit rāhilā.
The event takes -ला: सभेला उपस्थित राहणे (upasthit rāhṇe).

The word, taken apart.
उपस्थित राहणे (upasthit rāhṇe) joins upasthit, a tatsama taken from Sanskrit upasthita, 'present, near at hand' (Monier-Williams), to rāhṇe, 'to stay, to be'.

Lesson 5 of 5.
हजेरी (hajerī) — attendance; presence.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a get-together, then the Marathi for to attend.

You'll want to know: हजेरी.
हजेरी — hajerī — "attendance; presence".
गुरुजींनी वर्गात हजेरी घेतली. gurujīnnī vargāt hajerī ghetlī. The teacher took attendance in class.
Feminine.
हजेरी (hajerī) घेणे, to take attendance; हजेरी (hajerī) लावणे, to put in an appearance.

The word, taken apart.
हजेरी (hajerī) is a loan from Persian hāzirī, 'attendance', usually traced to Arabic ḥāḍir, 'present'.
