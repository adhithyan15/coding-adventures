Persian, chapter 321: Owning up.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
قسم خوردن (qasam khordan) — to swear, to take an oath.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a fingerprint, then the Persian for an interrogation.

You'll want to know: قسم خوردن.
قسم خوردن (qasam khordan) — "to swear, to take an oath".
او قسم خورد که چیزی ندیده است. U qasam khord ke chizi nadide ast. He swore that he had not seen anything.
Literally "to eat an oath". Present qasam mikhorad.

The word, taken apart.
qasam "an oath" is borrowed from Arabic qasam (EtymDB). khordan "to eat" is inherited, from Middle Persian xwar- (EtymDB).

Lesson 2 of 5.
شهادت دادن (shahâdat dâdan) — to testify, to give evidence.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for an interrogation, then the Persian for to swear.

You'll want to know: شهادت دادن.
شهادت دادن (shahâdat dâdan) — "to testify, to give evidence".
همسایه به نفع او شهادت داد. Hamsâye be naf'-e u shahâdat dâd. The neighbour testified in his favour.
shahâdat "witnessing" also means "martyrdom" in religious use. Present shahâdat midahad.

The word, taken apart.
shahâdat is borrowed from Arabic shahāda, "witnessing" (EtymDB). dâdan "to give" is inherited Persian.

Lesson 3 of 5.
اقرار کردن (eqrâr kardan) — to admit, to own up.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to swear, then the Persian for to testify.

You'll want to know: اقرار کردن.
اقرار کردن (eqrâr kardan) — "to admit, to own up".
پسر اقرار کرد که پنجره را شکسته است. Pesar eqrâr kard ke panjere râ shekaste ast. The boy admitted that he had broken the window.
Present eqrâr mikonad. Close in meaning to "to confess".

The word, taken apart.
eqrâr is borrowed from Arabic iqrār (EtymDB lists the Arabic word), usually glossed "acknowledgement". kardan "to do" is inherited Persian.

Lesson 4 of 5.
لو دادن (low dâdan) — to give away, to betray (a secret or a person).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to testify, then the Persian for to admit.

You'll want to know: لو دادن.
لو دادن (low dâdan) — "to give away, to betray (a secret or a person)".
دوستش او را لو داد. Dustash u râ low dâd. His friend gave him away.
Colloquial. Present low midahad. Compare low raftan "to be found out".

The word, taken apart.
dâdan "to give" is inherited Persian. The origin of low "revealed, exposed" is not settled; EtymDB lists no entry.

Lesson 5 of 5.
گناهکار (gonâhkâr) — guilty; a guilty person.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to admit, then the Persian for to give away.

You'll want to know: گناهکار.
گناهکار (gonâhkâr) — "guilty; a guilty person".
همه فکر میکردند او گناهکار است. Hame fekr mikardand u gonâhkâr ast. Everyone thought he was guilty.
gonâh "sin, fault" + -kâr "doer".

The word, taken apart.
gonâhkâr joins gonâh "sin, fault" and the suffix -kâr "doer", both native Persian. EtymDB lists gonâh without a parent.
