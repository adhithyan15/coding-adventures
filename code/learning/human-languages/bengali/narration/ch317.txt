Bengali, chapter 317: Courage and Sacrifice.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
বীর (bīr) — a hero; a warrior.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for to hold out, then the Bengali for to fall back.

You'll want to know: বীর.
বীর — bīr — "a hero; a warrior".
সেই যুদ্ধের বীরদের গল্প সবাই জানে। sei juddher bīrder gôlpo sôbāi jāne. Everyone knows the stories of the heroes of that war.

The word, taken apart.
বীর (bīr) is Sanskrit vīra, 'a man; a hero, a chief' (Monier-Williams); the ী is long in writing.

You'll want to know: আত্মত্যাগ.
আত্মত্যাগ — ātmôtyāg — "self-sacrifice".
দেশের জন্য সৈন্যদের আত্মত্যাগ আমরা মনে রাখব। desher jônyô soinyôder ātmôtyāg āmrā mône rākhbô. We will remember the soldiers' sacrifice for their country.

The word, taken apart.
আত্মত্যাগ (ātmôtyāg) joins Sanskrit ātman, 'self', to tyāga, 'giving up, abandoning' (Monier-Williams): giving up oneself.

Lesson 3 of 5.
শহিদ (shôhid) — a martyr.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a warrior, then the Bengali for self-sacrifice.

You'll want to know: শহিদ.
শহিদ — shôhid — "a martyr".
স্বাধীনতার যুদ্ধে অনেক মানুষ শহিদ হলেন। svādhīnôtār juddhe ônek mānush shôhid hôlen. Many people gave their lives in the war of independence.

The word, taken apart.
শহিদ (shôhid) is usually traced through Persian to Arabic shahīd, 'a witness', and so one who bears witness with his life. শহিদ হওয়া, to die for a cause.

You'll want to know: আত্মসমর্পণ করা.
আত্মসমর্পণ করা — ātmôsômôrpôn kôrā — "to surrender".
অবশেষে শত্রুরা আত্মসমর্পণ করল। ôbôsheshe shôtrurā ātmôsômôrpôn kôrlô. At last the enemy surrendered.
Past (he/she …): তিনি আত্মসমর্পণ করলেন, tini ātmôsômôrpôn kôrlen.

The word, taken apart.
আত্মসমর্পণ করা (ātmôsômôrpôn kôrā) puts Sanskrit ātman, 'self', and samarpaṇa, 'handing completely over' (Monier-Williams), before করা: to hand yourself over.

পরাজয় (pôrājôy) is Sanskrit parājaya, 'loss, defeat' (Monier-Williams), with parā-, 'away', before jaya, victory — জয়.
