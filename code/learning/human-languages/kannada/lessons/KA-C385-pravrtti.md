# ಪ್ರವೃತ್ತಿ (pravṛtti) — a tendency; a trend

## You'll want to know: ಪ್ರವೃತ್ತಿ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C385-PRABHAVA385-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ಪ್ರವೃತ್ತಿ** — *pravṛtti* — "a tendency; a trend".

> ಯುವಕರಲ್ಲಿ ನಗರಕ್ಕೆ ಹೋಗುವ ಪ್ರವೃತ್ತಿ ಹೆಚ್ಚಾಗಿದೆ.
>
> *yuvakaralli nagarakke hōguva pravṛtti heccāgide.*
>
> Among young people the trend of moving to the city has grown.

A verb in -ುವ comes before it: ಹೋಗುವ ಪ್ರವೃತ್ತಿ.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಪ್ರವೃತ್ತಿ** (*pravṛtti*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *pravṛtti*, "coming forth, activity, a course of action" (Monier-Williams).
