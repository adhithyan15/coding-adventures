## You'll want to know: せいり
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C159-TIDY159-05]; assesses=[] -->

**せいり** — *seiri* — "sorting out; putting in order".

> **へやの せいりは たのしいです。** — *heya no seiri wa tanoshii desu* — "Sorting out my room is fun."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**せいり** (*seiri*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *sei*, "put in order" (Middle Chinese *tsyengX*) and *ri*, "reason, arrangement" (*liX*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
