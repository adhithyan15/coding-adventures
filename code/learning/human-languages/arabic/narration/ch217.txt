Lesson 1 of 5.
ذيل (dhayl) — a tail.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a promise, then the Arabic for fog.

You'll want to know: ذيل.
ذيل — dhayl — "a tail".

The word, taken apart.
ذيل (dhayl) is built on the native Arabic root dh-y-l, "a tail, a train" (Lane). An old noun with its own root in Lane.

سماعة (sammāʿa) is built on the native Arabic root s-m-ʿ, "to hear" (Lane). Sammāʿa is a modern faʿʿāla word, "the hearer": headphones.
