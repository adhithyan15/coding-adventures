# The whole A2 core — a second pass

## Warm-up

[PAUSE 3s] Say *a note*, then *a repair*.

## Guided Practice: the note
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-LEX-C152-READ152-01, GU-LEX-C152-READ152-02, GU-LEX-C152-READ152-03, GU-SKILL-C152-READ-NOTE-01, GU-SKILL-C151-READ-NOTICE-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s each]
- [YOU SAY: *I wrote her a note*; *the repair will be done tomorrow*; *how much is the rent?*]
- [YOU ANSWER: what did Arun's note ask Mira? (Whether she will be at home tomorrow morning.)]
- [YOU ANSWER: a notice or a note: which one speaks to one person? (A note: **ચિઠ્ઠી**.)]

## Guided Practice: one sweep
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-GRAMMAR-C145-NEGATE-01, GU-GRAMMAR-C145-KOI-NATHI-01, GU-GRAMMAR-C146-KE-NAHI-01, GU-GRAMMAR-C146-POSS-PL-01, GU-GRAMMAR-C147-THAYO-01, GU-LEX-C147-PAST147-01, GU-GRAMMAR-C148-ERGATIVE-01, GU-GRAMMAR-C148-ERG-SG-01, GU-GRAMMAR-C148-DHUN-01, GU-GRAMMAR-C148-YUN-01, GU-GRAMMAR-C149-SHE-01, GU-GRAMMAR-C149-SHO-01, GU-GRAMMAR-C150-VAANO-Q-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s each]
- [YOU SAY: *I don't speak Gujarati*; *nobody is here*; *are you coming or not?*]
- [YOU SAY: *their brother*; *I got worried*; *I came late*; *I did the work*]
- [YOU SAY: *I drank tea*; *she ate rice*; *he took the medicine*]
- [YOU SAY: *they will speak Gujarati*; *when will you come?*; *what are you going to do tomorrow?*]
