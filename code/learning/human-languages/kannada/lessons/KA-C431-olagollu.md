## You'll want to know: ಒಳಗೊಳ್ಳು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C431-TIRMANISU431-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ಒಳಗೊಳ್ಳು** — *oḷagoḷḷu* — "to include".

> ಪ್ರವಾಸದ ಬೆಲೆ ಊಟವನ್ನೂ ಒಳಗೊಂಡಿತ್ತು.
>
> *pravāsada bele ūṭavannū oḷagoṇḍittu.*
>
> The price of the trip included meals.

Past (it …): ಒಳಗೊಂಡಿತು, *oḷagoṇḍitu*.

Mostly of things: ಒಳಗೊಂಡಿದೆ, it includes.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಒಳಗೊಳ್ಳು** (*oḷagoḷḷu*) is native Dravidian, built from *oḷ*, "the inside" (DEDR 698), and *koḷ*, "to take" (DEDR 2151): to take inside.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
