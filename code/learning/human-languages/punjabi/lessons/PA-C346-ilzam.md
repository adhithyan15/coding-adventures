## You'll want to know: ਇਲਜ਼ਾਮ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C346-SORRY346-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ਇਲਜ਼ਾਮ** — *ilzām* — "an accusation; a charge".

> ਇਹ ਇਲਜ਼ਾਮ ਸੱਚਾ ਨਹੀਂ ਹੈ।
>
> *eh ilzām sachchā nahīṁ hai.*
>
> This accusation is not true.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਇਲਜ਼ਾਮ** (*ilzām*) is a loan, usually traced through Persian to Arabic *ilzām*, "binding someone".
