## You'll want to know: 职场
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C491-BOSS491-04]; assesses=[] -->

**职场** — *zhíchǎng* — "the workplace; the world of work".

> *wǒ juéde zhíchǎng bǐ xuéxiào lèi* — I find working life more tiring than school.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**职场** (*zhíchǎng*) is built from 职 'a post' and 场 'a ground', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *tsyik* and *drjang* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'a threshing floor'; the modern third tone of chǎng is irregular for *drjang*.
