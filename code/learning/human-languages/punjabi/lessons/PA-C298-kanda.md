## You'll want to know: ਕੰਡਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C298-THORN298-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ਕੰਡਾ** — *kaṇḍā* — "a thorn".

> ਮੇਰੇ ਪੈਰ ਵਿੱਚ ਕੰਡਾ ਹੈ।
>
> *mere pair vich kaṇḍā hai.*
>
> There is a thorn in my foot.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਕੰਡਾ** (*kaṇḍā*) is inherited from Sanskrit *kaṇṭa*, "thorn"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *kaṇḍā* under it (CDIAL 2668).
