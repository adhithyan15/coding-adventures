## You'll want to know: तोलणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C376-MULYAMAPAN376-01]; assesses=[] -->

**तोलणे** — *tolṇe* — "to weigh up".

> निर्णय घेण्यापूर्वी त्याने दोन्ही बाजू तोलल्या.
>
> *nirṇay gheṇyāpūrvī tyāne donhī bājū tolalyā.*
>
> Before deciding, he weighed up both sides.

Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने तोललं, *tyāne tollaṁ*.

Also to weigh on scales.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**तोलणे** (*tolṇe*) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi *tol*, "a weight for weighing", under Sanskrit *tōlya*, "to be weighed" (CDIAL 5980), and the verb is built on that noun.
