Sanskrit, chapter 457: Laughter and Jokes.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
स्मयते (smayate) — smiles.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for massages, then the Sanskrit for a secluded place.

You'll want to know: स्मयते.
स्मयते — smayate — "smiles".
शिशुः मातरं पश्यति, स्मयते च। śiśuḥ mātaraṃ paśyati, smayate ca. The baby sees its mother and smiles.
Past (she …): सा स्मितवती, sā smitavatī.

The word, taken apart.
स्मयते (smayate) comes from the root √smi, "to smile", the root of विस्मयः (MW).

विनोदः (vinodaḥ) is built from vi and the root √nud, "to push", the root of नुदति: driving away dullness; MW gives "removal" first and then "diversion, pastime, pleasure" (MW).
