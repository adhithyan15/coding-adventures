## You'll want to know: गुटिका
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C483-GUTIKA483-01]; assesses=[] -->

**गुटिका** — *guṭikā* — "a pill".

> प्रतिदिनं द्वे गुटिके खादतु।
>
> *pratidinaṃ dve guṭike khādatu.*
>
> Take two pills every day.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**गुटिका** (*guṭikā*) MW's first sense is "a small globe or ball" and then "a pill"; MW gives no derivation, so its origin is uncertain (MW).
