The word, taken apart.
post is a loan, usually traced to French poste, "post". (EtymDB lists two other Persian words spelled post, "low" and "flour", which are different.)

Lesson 3 of 5.
باجه (bâje) — a counter window (bank, post).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a coach terminal, then the Persian for the post.

You'll want to know: باجه.
باجه — bâje — "a counter window (bank, post)".

The word, taken apart.
bâje is the word for a counter window. It is sometimes linked with bâj, "toll, tribute", as the window where dues were paid, but no source used here confirms that, and its origin is not settled.

The word, taken apart.
dare is a native Persian word, inherited rather than borrowed; it is usually traced to Middle Persian darrag, "valley".

Lesson 5 of 5.

You'll want to know: قله.
قله — qolle — "a peak, a summit".

The word, taken apart.
