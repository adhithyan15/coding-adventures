## You'll want to know: альтернатива
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C378-DOGOVORILIS-04]; assesses=[] -->

**альтернатива** (*alternatíva*) — "an alternative".

> У нас нет альтернативы.
>
> *U nas net alternatívy.*
>
> We have no alternative.

Feminine. *Альтернатива* + dative, or *для*: *альтернатива машине*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**альтернатива** is usually traced to French *alternative*, built on Latin *alternus* "by turns", from *alter* "the other of two" (Lewis & Short).
