## You'll want to know: das Volksfest
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C312-VOLKSFEST-01]; assesses=[] -->

**das Volksfest** — "the public festival, the folk festival".

> Im Herbst gibt es in unserer Stadt ein großes Volksfest.
>
> *In autumn there is a big public festival in our town.*

Plural: *die Volksfeste*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Volksfest* joins *Volk* "people", Old High German *folc* (EtymDB), and *Fest* "festival", from Latin *festum* "feast".
