Coverage note: this map currently stops at Chapter 5 while canonical lessons
continue through Chapter 31. `HL-M04` records the work to schedule every later
lesson, including the four new vocabulary → support sequences, without
pretending this partial map is already complete.

**Writing grows inside useful words, in tiny steps.** First hear and use a word.
Then meet no more than three shapes at once. A small, already meaningful unit
moves through tracing, guided copy, delayed copy, and dictation before the
whole word is ever produced. Chapter 1 begins with that complete gentle runway
for *namaskāram*; later words keep adding only the shapes they need.
