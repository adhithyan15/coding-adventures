## You'll want to know: svoltare
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C440-INDICAZIONI-03]; assesses=[] -->

**svoltare** — "to turn (into a street)".

> Svolta a destra dopo la farmacia.
>
> *Turn right after the chemist's.*

*La svolta* — the turn, and a turning point.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*svoltare* is built from *s-* and *voltare* 'to turn', which is inherited from Vulgar Latin *volvitāre*, built on *volvere* 'to roll' (Lewis & Short).
