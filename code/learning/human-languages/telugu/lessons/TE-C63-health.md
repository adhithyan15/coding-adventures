## You'll want to know: ఆరోగ్యం
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C63-HEALTH-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ఆరోగ్యం** (*ārōgyaṁ*) — "health".

ఆరోగ్యం is health, and it is the word before it with a small piece stuck on the front. The piece means "not", and రోగ is the illness: health here is what is left when the illness is gone.

That is a real difference in outlook and worth a moment. English health is something to be built up. ఆరోగ్యం is a subtraction.

మీ ఆరోగ్యం ఎలా ఉంది? is what a house asks an *atithi* who has been ill. It is more particular than మీరు ఎలా ఉన్నారు?, and it expects a real answer.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

Five: ఆకలి, నిద్ర, దగ్గు, రోగం, ఆరోగ్యం. Enough to say what is wrong and to ask after somebody else.
