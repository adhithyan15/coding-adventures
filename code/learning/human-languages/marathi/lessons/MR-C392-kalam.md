## You'll want to know: कलम
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C392-KALAM392-01]; assesses=[] -->

**कलम** — *kalam* — "a clause; a section (of a law)".

> करारातील हे कलम मला मान्य नाही.
>
> *karārātīl he kalam malā mānya nāhī.*
>
> I do not accept this clause in the contract.

Neuter.

Neuter: हे कलम.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**कलम** (*kalam*) is a loan from Persian, usually traced to Arabic *qalam*, "pen": an item set down in writing.

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
