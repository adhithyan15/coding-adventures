## You'll want to know: અનાથ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C500-VARSO500-03]; assesses=[] -->

**અનાથ** — *anāth* — "an orphan".

> અમારા ગામમાં અનાથ બાળકો માટે એક ઘર છે.
>
> *amārā gāmmā̃ anāth bāḷko māṭe ek ghar che.*
>
> In our village there is a home for orphaned children.

A masculine or feminine noun, by the person meant.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**અનાથ** (*anāth*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *anātha*, "having no master or protector", as Monier-Williams records it.
