Hindi, chapter 308: The Rescue.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
बेड़ा (beṛā) — a raft; a fleet.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for to take out, then the Hindi for in a sorry state.

You'll want to know: बेड़ा.
बेड़ा — beṛā — "a raft; a fleet".
लोगों ने लकड़ी का एक बेड़ा बनाया और बच्चों को पानी से निकाला। logoṁ ne lakṛī kā ek beṛā banāyā aur baccoṁ ko pānī se nikālā. People built a wooden raft and got the children out of the water.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
goes back to Sanskrit bēḍā, "a boat" (CDIAL 9308), where CDIAL gives Hindi beṛā, a boat or a raft. In a flood a बेड़ा is anything that floats and carries people; with ships, it is a fleet.

Lesson 2 of 5.
गोताख़ोर (gotākhor) — a diver.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for in a sorry state, then the Hindi for a raft.

You'll want to know: गोताख़ोर.
गोताख़ोर — gotākhor — "a diver".
गोताख़ोर कितने बजे पहुँचेंगे? gotākhor kitne baje pahũceṅge? What time will the divers get here?
Masculine or feminine, by the person meant.

The word, taken apart.
गोताख़ोर (gotākhor) is usually traced to Persian ġōṭa-xōr, built from ġōṭa, a plunge, and -xōr, one who takes: someone who dives under the water.

Lesson 3 of 5.
ज़िंदा (zindā) — alive.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a raft, then the Hindi for a diver.

You'll want to know: ज़िंदा.
ज़िंदा — zindā — "alive".
गोताख़ोरों ने नदी से एक आदमी को ज़िंदा निकाला। gotākhoroṁ ne nadī se ek ādmī ko zindā nikālā. The divers pulled a man out of the river alive.

The word, taken apart.
ज़िंदा (zindā) is usually traced to Persian zinda, "alive, living". It does not change for a woman or for many: वह ज़िंदा है, वे ज़िंदा हैं.

Lesson 4 of 5.
छुड़ाना (chuṛānā) — to set free; to get loose.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a diver, then the Hindi for alive.

You'll want to know: छुड़ाना.
छुड़ाना — chuṛānā — "to set free; to get loose".
अरुण ने रस्सी काटकर बकरी को छुड़ाया। Aruṇ ne rassī kāṭkar bakrī ko chuṛāyā. Arun cut the rope and set the goat free.
Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने बकरी को छुड़ाया, usne bakrī ko chuṛāyā.

The word, taken apart.
छुड़ाना (chuṛānā) is the causative of छूटना, to be released, which CDIAL 3707 sets under kṣuṭyatē, "is released": to make something come loose.

Lesson 5 of 5.
लपकना (lapaknā) — to dart; to spring forward.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for alive, then the Hindi for to set free.

You'll want to know: लपकना.
लपकना — lapaknā — "to dart; to spring forward".
मीरा ने लपककर बच्चे को पकड़ा। Mīrā ne lapakkar bacce ko pakṛā. Mira darted forward and caught the child.
Past (he …): वह लपका, vah lapkā.

The word, taken apart.
लपकना (lapaknā) is inherited from lappa, a form CDIAL reconstructs for leaping (CDIAL 10939). It is a quick spring towards something: लपककर पकड़ा, sprang and caught.
