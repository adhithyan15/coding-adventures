Telugu, chapter 377: Disaster.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
విపత్తు (vipattu) — a disaster; a calamity.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for an episode, then the Telugu for by chance.

You'll want to know: విపత్తు.
విపత్తు — vipattu — "a disaster; a calamity".
ఆ విపత్తులో చాలా ఇళ్ళు పోయాయి. ā vipattulō cālā iḷḷu pōyāyi. Many houses were lost in that disaster.
Plural: విపత్తులు, vipattulu.
ప్రకృతి విపత్తు (vipattu), a natural disaster.

The word, taken apart.
విపత్తు (vipattu) is borrowed from Sanskrit vipatti, "misfortune, failure" (CDIAL 11788).

Lesson 2 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for by chance, then the Telugu for a disaster.

You'll want to know: అలజడి.
అలజడి — alajaḍi — "a commotion; unrest".
రాత్రి వీధిలో అలజడి మొదలైంది. rātri vīdhilō alajaḍi modalaindi. A commotion started in the street at night.

The word, taken apart.
అలజడి (alajaḍi) is native Dravidian: DEDR 236 lists Telugu alajaḍi, "sorrow, affliction, calamity", under the word for distress; today it names a stir of unrest.

గస్తీ (gastī) తిరుగు, to go on patrol.
