## Grammar Lens: complete statement
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-FORM-KITA-190, PA-LEX-MAIN-03, PA-LEX-KAMM-05, PA-LEX-KARNA-05] -->

> **ਮੈਂ ਕੰਮ ਕੀਤਾ** — *main kamm kiitaa* — I did the work

Read the familiar speaker, familiar work noun, and completed form as one
bounded frame. In this transitive perfective frame, **ਕੀਤਾ** agrees with the
unmarked masculine-singular object **ਕੰਮ**, not the speaker.
