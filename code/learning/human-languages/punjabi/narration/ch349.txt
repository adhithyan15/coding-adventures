Punjabi, chapter 349: Singers and Songs.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ਗਾਇਕ (gāik) — a singer.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for music practice, then the Punjabi for to echo.

You'll want to know: ਗਾਇਕ.
ਗਾਇਕ — gāik — "a singer".
ਉਹ ਗਾਇਕ ਬਹੁਤ ਮਿੱਠਾ ਗਾਉਂਦਾ ਹੈ। oh gāik bahut miṭṭhā gāundā hai. That singer sings very sweetly.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ਗਾਇਕ (gāik) is taken over from Sanskrit gāyaka, "a singer", a tatsama (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 2 of 5.
ਵੰਝਲੀ (vanjhlī) — a flute; a simple pipe.

Before the new one: say the Punjabi for to echo, then the Punjabi for a singer.

You'll want to know: ਵੰਝਲੀ.
ਵੰਝਲੀ — vanjhlī — "a flute; a simple pipe".
ਮੈਨੂੰ ਵੰਝਲੀ ਦੀ ਧੁਨ ਪਸੰਦ ਹੈ। mainū̃ vanjhlī dī dhun pasand hai. I like the tune of the flute.

The word, taken apart.
ਵੰਝਲੀ (vanjhlī) is inherited from Sanskrit vaṁśī, "flute, pipe"; CDIAL lists Punjabi vañjhlī, "pipe, flute", under it (CDIAL 11180).

Lesson 3 of 5.
ਵਾਜਾ (vājā) — a harmonium; a musical instrument.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for a singer, then the Punjabi for a flute.

You'll want to know: ਵਾਜਾ.
ਵਾਜਾ — vājā — "a harmonium; a musical instrument".
ਮੇਰਾ ਦਾਦਾ ਵਾਜਾ ਵਜਾਉਂਦਾ ਹੈ ਅਤੇ ਗਾਉਂਦਾ ਹੈ। merā dādā vājā vajāundā hai ate gāundā hai. My grandfather plays the harmonium and sings.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ਵਾਜਾ (vājā) is inherited from Sanskrit vādya, "musical instrument"; CDIAL lists Punjabi vājjā, "musical instrument", under it (CDIAL 11511). CDIAL does not give the narrower sense "harmonium".

Lesson 5 of 5.
