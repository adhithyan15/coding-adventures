Punjabi, chapter 176: This, Here, and In a Place.
3 lessons. You can do the first 2 of them in the car; after that you will want to have stopped.


Lesson 1 of 3.
ਇਹ (eh) — this, near here.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Put one familiar contrast back in your voice. Say ਇੱਥੇ (itthe, here), then ਉੱਥੇ (utthe, there). Ask ਕਿੱਥੇ? (kitthe?, where?). Now say ਕਿਤਾਬ (kitāb, book), and the old distant pointer ਉਹ (oh, that).

You'll want to know: ਇਹ.
ਇਹ — eh — this, near here ਉਹ — oh — that, farther away.
You have known ਉਹ from its earlier lesson as he, she, it. That lesson also told you it points to that. Nothing new is being added to ਉਹ now. The new half of the pair is ਇਹ: use eh for a nearby thing.
The same near/far choice already lives in ਇੱਥੇ / ਉੱਥੇ, here / there. Keep the pairs aligned:
[a table of 2 rows, read aloud]
near: ਇਹ (eh) — this. farther away: ਉਹ — that.
near: ਇੱਥੇ — here. farther away: ਉੱਥੇ — there.
Wiktionary traces ਇਹ (eh) to Sanskrit eṣa, “this”.

What you've built.
Keep ਹੈ at the end, where Punjabi already taught you to put the verb:
ਇਹ ਕਿਤਾਬ ਹੈ। — eh kitāb hai. — This is a book. ਉਹ ਕਿਤਾਬ ਹੈ। — oh kitāb hai. — That is a book.
Only the first word changes. The book and the verb stay still.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: for a nearby thing, ਇਹ (eh); for a farther thing, ਉਹ]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: ਇਹ (eh) ਕਿਤਾਬ ਹੈ]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — switch: make it distant — ਉਹ ਕਿਤਾਬ ਹੈ]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
Near thing? (ਇਹ, eh.) Farther thing? (ਉਹ, oh.) Near place? (ਇੱਥੇ, itthe.) Farther place? (ਉੱਥੇ, utthe.)
Source: Wiktionary: ਇਹ (eh).


Lesson 2 of 3.
ਵਿੱਚ (vich) — in, at.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Near and farther: ਇਹ (eh) / ਉਹ. Now bring back two familiar places: ਘਰ (ghar, home) and ਸਕੂਲ (sakūl, school). Ask ਕਿੱਥੇ? (kitthe?, where?).

You'll want to know: ਵਿੱਚ.
ਵਿੱਚ — vich — in, at.
English puts in before a place. Punjabi puts ਵਿੱਚ (vich) after it. That is why it is called a postposition: the place comes first.
ਘਰ ਵਿੱਚ — ghar vich — at home, in the house ਸਕੂਲ ਵਿੱਚ — sakūl vich — at school, in the school.
Do not add a verb yet. This step owns only the place chunk: noun, then ਵਿੱਚ (vich).

The word, taken apart.
ਵਿੱਚ (vich) is inherited through Old Punjabi vici and Prakrit vicca from a form reconstructed as Sanskrit vīcya, “middle” (CDIAL 12042). Hindi bīc, “middle”, is a cousin. The old idea of being in the middle became Punjabi's ordinary word for being in a place.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: ਘਰ ਵਿੱਚ (vich)]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: ਸਕੂਲ ਵਿੱਚ (vich)]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — answer: after ਕਿੱਥੇ?, choose one familiar place chunk]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[once you have stopped driving — check: did the place come before ਵਿੱਚ (vich)?]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
How do you say at home? (ਘਰ ਵਿੱਚ (vich).) At school? (ਸਕੂਲ ਵਿੱਚ (vich).) Which comes first, the place or ਵਿੱਚ (vich)? (The place.)
Sources: Wiktionary: ਵਿੱਚ (vich); CDIAL 12042.


Lesson 3 of 3.
A thing, a place, then ਹੈ — the book is at home - a thing, a location, then hai.

Before we start: this one needs your eyes, so it is not fully a driving lesson. You will want your eyes or your hands for the steps that ask you to read, point, write or gesture. You can listen to everything else now. I will say so again when we reach it, but leave this section until you have stopped: Guided Practice.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say the two pointing pairs: ਇਹ (eh) / ਉਹ and ਇੱਥੇ / ਉੱਥੇ. Ask ਕਿੱਥੇ?. Then say ਘਰ ਵਿੱਚ (vich) and leave ਹੈ waiting; Punjabi keeps its verb for the end.

Grammar Lens: a thing, a place, then ਹੈ.
ਕਿਤਾਬ ਘਰ ਵਿੱਚ ਹੈ (kitab ghar vich hai)। kitāb ghar vich hai. The book is at home.
The frame has three pieces:
the thing that is present — ਕਿਤਾਬ;
its place — ਘਰ ਵਿੱਚ (vich); and.
ਹੈ at the end.
Punjabi does not need a separate word matching English there in there is. The placed thing plus ਹੈ states that the thing is present in that location.

Grammar Lens: pointing without a postposition.
When the location itself is here or there, the familiar place word can stand directly before ਹੈ:
ਦੁਕਾਨ ਇੱਥੇ ਹੈ। — dukān itthe hai. — The shop is here. ਦੁਕਾਨ ਉੱਥੇ ਹੈ। — dukān utthe hai. — The shop is there.
The second sentence is the short practical answer this chapter is building: ਦੁਕਾਨ ਉੱਥੇ ਹੈ.

[once you have stopped driving — this part needs your eyes or your hands; if you are driving, skip ahead to the next part]
Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — ask: ਕਿਤਾਬ ਕਿੱਥੇ ਹੈ?]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — answer: ਕਿਤਾਬ ਘਰ ਵਿੱਚ ਹੈ (kitab ghar vich hai)।]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — ask: ਦੁਕਾਨ ਕਿੱਥੇ ਹੈ?]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — answer: ਦੁਕਾਨ ਉੱਥੇ ਹੈ।]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — switch: point near and answer ਦੁਕਾਨ ਇੱਥੇ ਹੈ।]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
Ask where the book is and answer that it is at home. Then ask where the shop is and answer that it is there. Keep ਹੈ last.
