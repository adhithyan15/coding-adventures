# ఝంకారం — meaning and shape together

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[TE-LEX-C152-BUZZ-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Imagine the steady sound of a bee. Say the learned Telugu word for
that buzz or hum. (**ఝంకారం**.)

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[TE-LEX-C152-BUZZ-01, TE-SCRIPT-RECOG-173] -->

<!-- hl-activity: {"id":"TE-R152-jhankaram-recall-write","kind":"text","assesses":["TE-LEX-C152-BUZZ-01","TE-SCRIPT-RECOG-173"],"prompt":"Write the first base letter of ఝంకారం, then say what the whole word means.","answer":"ఝ — a buzz or hum","accepted":["ఝ, buzz","ఝ, hum","jha, buzz","jha, hum"],"feedback":{"correct":"ఝ begins ఝంకారం — a buzz or hum.","incorrect":"Begin with ఝ; the whole word ఝంకారం means a buzz or hum."},"response_seconds":15} -->

Read **ఝంకారం** once. Cover it, then write only its first base letter: **ఝ**.

Now uncover the word and check two things: the three round joined bowls and the
short separate stem beneath the right side. Add **ం** only after the base
letter is steady.

- [YOU WRITE: **ఝ** once from memory]
- [YOU BUILD: **ఝ** + **ం** = **ఝం**]
- [YOU SAY: “a buzz or hum”]
