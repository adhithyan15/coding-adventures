## You'll want to know: нарушать
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C388-ZAKON-04]; assesses=[] -->

**нарушать** (*narushát*) — "to break (a rule), to violate".

> Он часто нарушает правила.
>
> *On chásto narusháyet právila.*
>
> He often breaks the rules.

Imperfective; perfective *нарушить*. *Нарушать закон, правила, тишину*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**нарушать** is built from *на-* and *рушить* "to pull down, to wreck", an inherited Slavic verb: to break a rule is to knock part of it down.
