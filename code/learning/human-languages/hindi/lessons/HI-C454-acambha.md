## You'll want to know: अचंभा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C454-ACHAMBHA454-03]; assesses=[] -->

**अचंभा** — *acambhā* — "astonishment; a marvel".

> यह अचंभे की बात है कि दादा अस्सी साल के हैं और रोज़ दौड़ते हैं।
>
> *yah acambhe kī bāt hai ki dādā assī sāl ke haiṁ aur roz dauṛte haiṁ.*
>
> It's a marvel that my grandfather is eighty and still runs every day.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अचंभा** (*acambhā*) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Hindi *acambhā*, "wonderful; wonder", under *\*ācchambha*, a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 1079).
