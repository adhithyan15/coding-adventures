# براءة (barāʾa) — innocence

## You'll want to know: براءة
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C334-MUHAKAMA334-05]; assesses=[] -->

**براءة** — *barāʾa* — "innocence".

> أعلن القاضي براءة الشاب.
>
> *aʿlana al-qāḍī barāʾat ash-shābb.*
>
> The judge declared the young man innocent.

A patent is براءة اختراع.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**براءة** (*barāʾa*) is built on the native Arabic root *b-r-ʾ* (Lane), "to be free, clear of a thing". *Barāʾa* is being clear of blame.
