German, chapter 337: Sich irren.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
irren — to be mistaken (mostly reflexive, sich irren); to wander.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for on the one hand, then the German for on the other hand.

You'll want to know: irren.
irren — "to be mistaken (mostly reflexive, sich irren); to wander".
Ich habe mich geirrt: Das Konzert ist am Sonntag, nicht am Samstag. I was mistaken: the concert is on Sunday, not Saturday.
Mostly reflexive: sich irren, ich habe mich geirrt. Irren ist menschlich — to err is human.

The word, taken apart.
irren is inherited from Old High German irren and Proto-Germanic irzijaną "to delude, to mislead" (EtymDB), a cousin of Latin errāre "to wander": to be mistaken is to wander off the path.

Lesson 2 of 6.
der Irrtum — the mistake, the error.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for on the other hand, then the German for to be mistaken.

You'll want to know: der Irrtum.
der Irrtum — "the mistake, the error".
Das war ein Irrtum: Die Rechnung ist schon bezahlt. That was an error: the bill has already been paid.
Plural: die Irrtümer. Im Irrtum sein — to be mistaken.

The word, taken apart.
Irrtum is inherited through Middle High German irretuom from Old High German irrituom (EtymDB), built on irren "to err" with the noun ending -tum.

Lesson 3 of 6.
der Zweifel — the doubt.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to be mistaken, then the German for the mistake.

You'll want to know: der Zweifel.
der Zweifel — "the doubt".
Ich habe keinen Zweifel: Sie ist die beste Spielerin. I have no doubt: she is the best player.
Plural: die Zweifel. Ohne Zweifel — without doubt.

The word, taken apart.
Zweifel is inherited from Middle High German zwīvel, Old High German zwīfal and Proto-Germanic twīflaz (EtymDB), built on the word for "two": a mind split in two.

Lesson 4 of 6.
zweifeln — to have doubts.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for the mistake, then the German for the doubt.

You'll want to know: zweifeln.
zweifeln — "to have doubts".
Ich zweifle daran, dass der Bus pünktlich kommt. I have doubts that the bus will come on time.
An etwas zweifeln — to have doubts about something. Ich zweifle drops the e of zweifele.

The word, taken apart.
zweifeln is inherited from Middle High German zwīvelen (EtymDB), the verb of Zweifel "doubt", which is built on the word for "two".

Lesson 5 of 6.
bezweifeln — to doubt (something), to question.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for the doubt, then the German for to have doubts.

You'll want to know: bezweifeln.
bezweifeln — "to doubt (something), to question".
Der Arzt bezweifelt, dass das Medikament hilft. The doctor doubts that the medicine helps.
Inseparable, with a direct object: Ich bezweifle das. Zweifeln takes an; bezweifeln takes the thing itself.

The word, taken apart.
bezweifeln is built with be- on zweifeln, the verb of Zweifel "doubt", which goes back to Proto-Germanic twīflaz (EtymDB).
