Hindi, chapter 533: In the Wrestling Pit.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
कुश्ती (kuśtī) — wrestling; a bout.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a gathering (for music or poetry), then the Hindi for to make dance.

You'll want to know: कुश्ती.
कुश्ती — kuśtī — "wrestling; a bout".
गाँव के मेले में कुश्ती होती है। Gā̃v ke mele meṁ kuśtī hotī hai. There is wrestling at the village fair.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
कुश्ती (kuśtī) is usually traced to Persian kushtī, "wrestling".

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a wrestler, then the Hindi for a wrestling ground.

You'll want to know: लड़ाना.
लड़ाना — laṛānā — "to pit against; to make fight".
बच्चों ने छत पर दो पतंगें लड़ाईं। Baccoṁ ne chat par do pataṅgeṁ laṛāīṁ. On the roof the children pitted two kites against each other.
Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने दो पहलवानों को लड़ाया, usne do pahalvānoṁ ko laṛāyā.

The word, taken apart.
लड़ाना (laṛānā) is the causative of laṛnā, "to fight", from laḍ, "fight", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 10920).

Lesson 5 of 5.
पैंतरा (paĩtrā) — a stance; a move (in wrestling).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a wrestling ground, then the Hindi for to pit against.

You'll want to know: पैंतरा.
पैंतरा — paĩtrā — "a stance; a move (in wrestling)".
पहलवान (pahalvān) ने एक नया पैंतरा दिखाया। Pahalvān ne ek nayā paĩtrā dikhāyā. The wrestler showed off a new move.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
पैंतरा (paĩtrā) is a tadbhava, inherited from Sanskrit pādāntara, "the space of one step" (CDIAL 8073). CDIAL gives the Hindi word as paitrā, "a way of placing the feet when turning in fencing or wrestling".
