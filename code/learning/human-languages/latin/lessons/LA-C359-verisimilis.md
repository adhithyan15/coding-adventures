## You'll want to know: vērisimilis, vērisimile
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C359-PONDERO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**vērisimilis, vērisimile** — "probable, like the truth".

> Nōn est vērisimile eum hoc fēcisse.
>
> *It is not likely that he did this.*

*vērisimilis, vērisimile*, also written as two words, *vērī similis*, like the truth. *Vērisimile est* + accusative and infinitive. English *verisimilitude*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*vērisimilis* grew out of the phrase *vērī similis* "like the truth" (Lewis & Short), from *vērus* "true" and *similis* "like". English *verisimilitude* comes from it.
