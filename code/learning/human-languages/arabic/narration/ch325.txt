Arabic, chapter 325: Witnesses and oaths.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
شهد (shahida) — to witness; to testify.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a whistle, then the Arabic for chaos.

You'll want to know: شهد.
شهد — shahida — "to witness; to testify".
شهدت المدينة مطرا شديدا أمس. shahidat al-madīna maṭaran shadīdan ams. The city saw heavy rain yesterday.
Present: يشهد, yashhad.
شهد (shahida) على or شهد (shahida) بـ: to testify. A witness is شاهد.

The word, taken apart.
شهد (shahida) is built on the native Arabic root sh-h-d (Lane), "to be present at a thing and see it"; shahāda is testimony, and a certificate.

Lesson 2 of 5.
أقسم (aqsama) — to swear.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for chaos, then the Arabic for to witness.

You'll want to know: أقسم.
أقسم — aqsama — "to swear".
أقسم الشاهد أن كلامه صحيح. aqsama ash-shāhid anna kalāmahu ṣaḥīḥ. The witness swore that what he said was true.
Present: يقسم, yuqsim.
The oath itself is قسم, qasam.

The word, taken apart.
أقسم (aqsama) is built on the native Arabic root q-s-m (Lane), "to divide", the root of qism, a part. Lane gives qasam as an oath, and Form IV aqsama, to swear.

You'll want to know: حلف.
حلف — ḥalafa — "to take an oath".
حلف الموظف أمام المحكمة. ḥalafa al-muwaẓẓaf amāma al-maḥkama. The employee took an oath before the court.
Present: يحلف, yaḥlif.
An ally, one bound by oath, is حليف.

The word, taken apart.
حلف (ḥalafa) is built on the native Arabic root ḥ-l-f (Lane), "to swear"; ḥilf is a pact sworn between tribes, and ḥalīf an ally.

Lesson 4 of 5.
اعترف (iʿtarafa) — to confess, to admit.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to swear, then the Arabic for to take an oath.

You'll want to know: اعترف.
اعترف — iʿtarafa — "to confess, to admit".
اعترف اللص بالسرقة. iʿtarafa al-liṣṣ bi-s-sariqa. The thief confessed to the theft.
Present: يعترف, yaʿtarif.
It takes بـ: اعترف (iʿtarafa) بالخطأ, he admitted the mistake.

The word, taken apart.
اعترف (iʿtarafa) is built on the native Arabic root ʿ-r-f (Lane), "to know", the root of the taught ʿarafa. It is Form VIII, "to own that one knows": to admit.

Lesson 5 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to take an oath, then the Arabic for to confess.

You'll want to know: أنكر.
أنكر — ankara — "to deny".
أنكر المتهم كل شيء. ankara al-muttaham kull shayʾ. The defendant denied everything.
Present: ينكر, yunkir.

The word, taken apart.
أنكر (ankara) is built on the native Arabic root n-k-r (Lane), "not to know a thing"; nakira is "to find strange". It is Form IV, "to disown".
