## You'll want to know: imāgō, imāginis
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C547-IMAG-01]; assesses=[] -->

**imāgō, imāginis** — "an image, likeness, portrait".

> In ātriō imāginēs maiōrum sunt.
>
> *In the hall there are portraits of the ancestors.*

Feminine, third declension. Also the wax masks of ancestors kept in the hall. English *image*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**imāgō** is usually linked with *imitor* "to copy" (Lewis & Short; EtymDB). English *image* and *imagine* come from it.
