## You'll want to know: まるめる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C497-MEAL497-04]; assesses=[] -->

**まるめる** — *marumeru* — "to roll into a ball".

> **ごはんを まるめて、おにぎりを つくります。** — *gohan o marumete, onigiri o tsukurimasu* — "I roll the rice into balls to make rice balls."

Polite **まるめます**, *marumemasu*; an ichidan verb. Past (polite): **こどもが ねんどを まるめました。**, *kodomo ga nendo o marumemashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**まるめる** (*marumeru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi, and its older origin is not settled (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
