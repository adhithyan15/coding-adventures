# ಕಾಡ್ಗಿಚ್ಚು (kāḍgiccu) — a forest fire

## You'll want to know: ಕಾಡ್ಗಿಚ್ಚು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C475-ARANYA475-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ಕಾಡ್ಗಿಚ್ಚು** — *kāḍgiccu* — "a forest fire".

> ಕಳೆದ ಬೇಸಿಗೆಯಲ್ಲಿ ಈ ಬೆಟ್ಟದಲ್ಲಿ ಕಾಡ್ಗಿಚ್ಚು ಆಯಿತು.
>
> *kaḷeda bēsigeyalli ī beṭṭadalli kāḍgiccu āyitu.*
>
> Last summer there was a forest fire on this hill.

ಕಾಡು, forest, plus ಕಿಚ್ಚು, fire: the k turns to g in the join.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಕಾಡ್ಗಿಚ್ಚು** (*kāḍgiccu*) is a native Kannada compound of ಕಾಡು, "a forest" (DEDR 1438), and *kiccu*, "fire" (DEDR 1514).
