Tamil, chapter 118: Stove, Vessel, Plate, Cup, Spoon.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
அடுப்பு (aṭuppu) — a stove.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for a tool, then the Tamil for a possession.

You'll want to know: அடுப்பு.
அடுப்பு — aṭuppu — "a stove".

The word, taken apart.
அடுப்பு (aṭuppu) is native Dravidian. DEDR 76 lists Malayalam aṭuppu. It comes from aṭu, "to cook": the stove is the cooking place. The entry compares Sanskrit aṭṭa, "dried".

Lesson 3 of 5.
தட்டு (taṭṭu) — a plate.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for a stove, then the Tamil for a vessel.
[pause 2 seconds]
Count with care: oṉṟu, mūṉṟu, with the hard ṉṟ; onpatu, one short of pattu; then பதினொன்று to இருபது, two tens.

You'll want to know: தட்டு.
தட்டு — taṭṭu — "a plate".

The word, taken apart.
தட்டு (taṭṭu) is native Dravidian. DEDR 3035 lists Malayalam taṭṭu, "a flat plate". The entry compares Pali taṭṭaka, "a flat bowl".
