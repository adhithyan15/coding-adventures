## You'll want to know: publier
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C398-RESEAU-05]; assesses=[] -->

**publier** — "to post, to publish".

> Elle a publié une photo de son chat.
>
> *She posted a photo of her cat.*

Of books too: *publier un roman*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*publier* is inherited from Latin *pūblicāre* "to make public" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short), from *pūblicus* "of the people".
