## You'll want to know: जणो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C262-JANO262-01]; assesses=[] -->

**जणो** — *jaṇo* — "a person (when counting)".

> घर मांय पांच जणा है।
>
> *ghar mā̃y pāṅch jaṇā hai.*
>
> There are five people in the house.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**जणो** (*jaṇo*) is inherited from Sanskrit *jana*, "person"; CDIAL lists Marwari *jaṇo* under it (CDIAL 5098).
