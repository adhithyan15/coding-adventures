## You'll want to know: vomir
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C483-NAUSEE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**vomir** — "to vomit, to be sick".

> Le petit a vomi dans la voiture.
>
> *The little one was sick in the car.*

A regular *-ir* verb: *nous vomissons*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*vomir* is inherited from Vulgar Latin *vomīre*, a form of Latin *vomere* "to vomit" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short).
