## You'll want to know: बाळगणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C346-KHATRI346-01]; assesses=[] -->

**बाळगणे** — *bāḷagṇe* — "to entertain (a hope); to keep".

> त्याने निकालाबद्दल मोठी आशा बाळगली होती.
>
> *tyāne nikālābaddal moṭhī āśā bāḷaglī hotī.*
>
> He had cherished high hopes about the result.

Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने आशा बाळगली, *tyāne āśā bāḷaglī*.

आशा बाळगणे, to cherish a hope; राग बाळगणे, to bear a grudge.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**बाळगणे** (*bāḷagṇe*) is a Marathi verb whose origin is not settled.
