## You'll want to know: عدو
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C535-RIFAQ535-02]; assesses=[] -->

**عدو** — *ʿaduww* — "an enemy".

> القط والفأر عدوان في القصص.
>
> *al-qiṭṭ wa-l-faʾr ʿaduwwān fī al-qiṣaṣ.*
>
> The cat and the mouse are enemies in stories.

Plural: أعداء, *aʿdāʾ*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**عدو** (*ʿaduww*) is built on the native Arabic root *ʿ-d-w*, "to run, to pass beyond" (Lane). An ʿaduww is one who runs against you, who goes beyond what is right.
