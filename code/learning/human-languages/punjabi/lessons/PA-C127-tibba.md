## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਟਿੱਬਾ** (*ṭibbā*) is inherited. CDIAL lists it, "mound, sandbank, hill", under *\*ṭibba*, one of the variant forms it groups under *\*ṭappa*, "hill" (CDIAL 5446).
