# Review — on the street, asking the way

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[FA-LEX-BEBAKHSHID-01, FA-GRAMMAR-CHETOR-MITAVANAM-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Stop someone and ask the way to the museum. (*Bebakhshid, chetor
mitavânam be muze beravam?*)

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[FA-LEX-DAST-E-RAST-01, FA-GRAMMAR-POLITE-IMPERATIVE-01, FA-LEX-C230-MAKAN230-04, FA-LEX-C88-LOC88-05] -->

Two lessons, and a whole exchange. You open with *bebakhshid*, ask with
*chetor mitavânam be ... beravam?* or *... kojâst?*, and the answer comes
back in the polite instruction: *mostaqim beravid*, *be dast-e chap
bepichid*.

[PAUSE 2s] Here is one exchange, start to finish:

> — *bebakhshid, muze kojâst?*
> — *mostaqim beravid. sar-e chahârrâh be dast-e râst bepichid. muze dast-e
> chap ast.*
> — *mostaqim, ba'd dast-e râst, ba'd dast-e chap. mamnun.*

The third line is the habit worth keeping: say the directions back, and any
mistake shows up while the stranger is still there.
