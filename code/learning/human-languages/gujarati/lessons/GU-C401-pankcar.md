## You'll want to know: પંક્ચર
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C401-TYRE401-02]; assesses=[] -->

**પંક્ચર** — *pankcar* — "a puncture".

> મારી સાઈકલમાં પંક્ચર પડ્યું.
>
> *mārī sāīkalmā̃ pankcar paḍyũ.*
>
> My bicycle got a puncture.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**પંક્ચર** (*pankcar*) is borrowed from English *puncture*.
