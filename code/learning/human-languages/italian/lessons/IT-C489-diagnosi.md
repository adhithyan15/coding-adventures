## You'll want to know: la diagnosi
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C489-GUARIRE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**la diagnosi** — "diagnosis".

> La diagnosi è chiara: è solo un raffreddore.
>
> *The diagnosis is clear: it is only a cold.*

The same form in the plural: *le diagnosi*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*diagnosi* is a learned borrowing, through Latin *diagnōsis*, from Greek *diágnōsis* (EtymDB, under English *diagnosis*), from *diagignṓskein* "to discern, to tell apart".
