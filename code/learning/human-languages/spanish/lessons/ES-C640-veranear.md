## You'll want to know: veranear
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C640-VIAJAR-01]; assesses=[] -->

**veranear** — "to spend the summer (on holiday)".

> Veraneamos en la costa todos los años.
>
> *We spend every summer on the coast.*

Built on *el verano*. *El veraneo* is the summer holiday.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*veranear* is built on *verano* "summer", which is inherited from Vulgar Latin *vērānum (tempus)*, built on Latin *vēr* "spring" (EtymDB).
