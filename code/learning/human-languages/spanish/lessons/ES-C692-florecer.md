## You'll want to know: florecer
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C692-SEMBRAR-05]; assesses=[] -->

**florecer** — "to flower, to bloom; to flourish".

> Los árboles florecen en primavera.
>
> *The trees blossom in spring.*

It adds a z: *florezco*, *florezca*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*florecer* is inherited from Latin *flōrēscere* "to begin to bloom" (EtymDB), from *flōs, flōris* "flower".
