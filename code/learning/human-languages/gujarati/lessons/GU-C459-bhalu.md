## You'll want to know: ભલું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C459-KOMAL459-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ભલું** — *bhalũ* — "good-hearted; kind".

> અમારા પડોશી ભલા માણસ છે.
>
> *amārā paḍośī bhalā māṇas che.*
>
> Our neighbour is a kind man.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ભલું** (*bhalũ*) is inherited from Sanskrit *bhalla*, "auspicious", a rustic form of *bhadra*, "good"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati word (CDIAL 9408).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
