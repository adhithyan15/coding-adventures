# متقاعد (mutaqāʿid) — retired; a pensioner

## You'll want to know: متقاعد
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C522-AMAL522-04]; assesses=[] -->

**متقاعد** — *mutaqāʿid* — "retired; a pensioner".

> جارنا معلم متقاعد.
>
> *jārunā muʿallim mutaqāʿid.*
>
> Our neighbour is a retired teacher.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**متقاعد** (*mutaqāʿid*) is built on the native Arabic root *q-ʿ-d*, "to sit" (Lane). Mutaqāʿid is the participle of Form VI.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
