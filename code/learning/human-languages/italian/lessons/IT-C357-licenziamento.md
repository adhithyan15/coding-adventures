## You'll want to know: il licenziamento
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C357-DIMISSIONI-01]; assesses=[] -->

**il licenziamento** — "the dismissal, the sacking".

> Dopo il licenziamento ha aperto un ristorante.
>
> *After being dismissed he opened a restaurant.*

Built on *licenziare* "to dismiss". *La lettera di licenziamento* — the dismissal letter.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*licenziamento* is built on *licenziare* "to dismiss", from *licenza* "leave, permission", a learned borrowing from Latin *licentia* "freedom, permission" (Lewis & Short), from *licēre* "to be allowed".
