## You'll want to know: schnarchen
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C486-MUEDE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**schnarchen** — "to snore".

> Mein Mann schnarcht so laut, dass ich nicht schlafen kann.
>
> *My husband snores so loudly that I can't sleep.*

Regular: *hat geschnarcht*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*schnarchen* is inherited from Middle High German *snarchen* (EtymDB), probably an imitative word; English *snore* and *snort* are of the same kind.
