## You'll want to know: অটো
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C388-TRAM388-03]; assesses=[] -->

**অটো** — *ôṭo* — "an autorickshaw".

> স্টেশন থেকে আমরা অটোতে বাড়ি ফিরলাম।
>
> *sṭeshôn theke āmrā ôṭote bāṛi phirlām.*
>
> We came home from the station by auto.

The three-wheeler with a motor; a রিকশা is pulled or pedalled.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**অটো** (*ôṭo*) is borrowed from English *auto*, clipped from *autorickshaw*; the first letter is অ, said as an open ô.
