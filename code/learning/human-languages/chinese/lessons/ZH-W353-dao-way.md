## Script — a head piece, then the walking piece
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-SCRIPT-DAO-WAY-01]; assesses=[] -->

> 道

**Two pieces: the head piece first, the walking piece last**, although it sits on the left and along the foot. Count the pieces, not the strokes.

**The head piece, nine strokes.** A short stroke falling to the right and a short stroke falling to the left, like two horns. A horizontal under them. A short left-falling stroke. Then a narrow box: a vertical, a horizontal that turns down, two short horizontals inside and one closing the foot.

**The walking piece, three strokes**, as in 这: a dot, a stroke that zigzags down, then a long sweep along the foot to the right, under the head piece.

9 + 3 = twelve.
