## You'll want to know: jíhé
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C434-GROUP434-02]; assesses=[] -->

*jíhé* — "to gather; to assemble; to meet up as a group".

> *míngtiān zǎoshang bā diǎn wǒmen zài jiǔdiàn dàtáng jíhé* — Tomorrow at eight in the morning we meet in the hotel lobby.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*jíhé* is built from *jí* 'to gather' and *hé* 'to join', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *dzip* and *hop* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to gather' and the second 'to join'.
