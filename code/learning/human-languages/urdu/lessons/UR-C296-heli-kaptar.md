## You'll want to know: ہیلی کاپٹر
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C296-BACHAO296-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ہیلی کاپٹر** — *helī kāpṭar* — "a helicopter".

> ہیلی کاپٹر نے لوگوں کو بچایا۔
>
> *helī kāpṭar ne logoṅ ko bachāyā.*
>
> The helicopter rescued people.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ہیلی کاپٹر** (*helī kāpṭar*) is borrowed from English *helicopter*. Urdu writes it as two words.
