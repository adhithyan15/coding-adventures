## You'll want to know: உதவித்தொகை
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C368-KALVIYARIVU368-03]; assesses=[] -->

**உதவித்தொகை** — *utavittokai* — "a scholarship; a grant".

> அவளுக்குப் படிப்பதற்கு உதவித்தொகை கிடைத்தது.
>
> *avaḷukkup paṭippataṟku utavittokai kiṭaittatu.*
>
> She got a scholarship for her studies.

உதவி, help, + a word for a sum of money.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**உதவித்தொகை** (*utavittokai*) is native: *utavi*, "help, aid", from *utavu* (DEDR 609), and *tokai*, "collection, sum, total" (DEDR 3476).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
