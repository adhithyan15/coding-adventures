## You'll want to know: यादी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C551-YADI551-02]; assesses=[] -->

**यादी** — *yādī* — "a list".

> आईने बाजारातून आणायच्या सामानाची यादी केली.
>
> *āīne bājārātūn āṇāycyā sāmānācī yādī kelī.*
>
> Mother made a list of the things to bring from the market.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**यादी** (*yādī*) is a loan from Persian *yādī*, "a memorandum", built on *yād*, "memory".
