## You'll want to know: అప్పట్లో
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C338-KRAMANGA338-05]; assesses=[] -->

**అప్పట్లో** — *appaṭlō* — "in those days".

> అప్పట్లో ఇక్కడ బస్సులు లేవు.
>
> *appaṭlō ikkaḍa bassulu lēvu.*
>
> In those days there were no buses here.

A spoken form: అప్పట్లో looks back at a whole period, అప్పుడు at one moment.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**అప్పట్లో** (*appaṭlō*) is native Telugu: *appaṭi*, "of that time", from *appuḍu*, "then" (DEDR 4559), with the locative *-lō*, "in".
