## You'll want to know: 口试
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C268-EXAM268-02]; assesses=[] -->

**口试** — *kǒushì* — "oral exam".

> **明天我们学校有口试** *míngtiān wǒmen xuéxiào yǒu kǒushì* — Our school has oral exams tomorrow.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**口试** (*kǒushì*) is built from 口 'the mouth' and 试 'a test', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *khuwX* and *syiH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'to use'. The sense 'a test' is not given there.
