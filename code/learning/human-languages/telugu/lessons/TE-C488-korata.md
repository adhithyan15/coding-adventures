## You'll want to know: కొరత
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C488-TOKU488-04]; assesses=[] -->

**కొరత** — *korata* — "a shortage".

> వేసవిలో నీటి కొరత ఉంది.
>
> *vēsavilō nīṭi korata undi.*
>
> In summer there is a shortage of water.

నీటి కొరత, a water shortage; డబ్బు కొరత, a lack of money.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**కొరత** (*korata*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 1851 lists Telugu *koṟata*, "deficiency, want", in the family of *kuṟu*, "short, small".
