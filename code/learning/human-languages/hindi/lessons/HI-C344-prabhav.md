## You'll want to know: प्रभाव
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C344-PARINAM344-02]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रभाव** — *prabhāv* — "an influence".

> बच्चों पर दोस्तों का बहुत प्रभाव होता है।
>
> *baccoṁ par dostoṁ kā bahut prabhāv hotā hai.*
>
> Friends have a great influence on children.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रभाव** (*prabhāv*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *prabhāva*, "might, power" (Monier-Williams).
