Portuguese, chapter 111: Driver, Lawyer, Engineer, Firefighter, Postman.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
o motorista — a driver.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for a farmer, then the Portuguese for a baker.

You'll want to know: o motorista.
o motorista — "a driver".

The word, taken apart.
motorista is built from the word for a motor and -ista, "one who". That word from Latin motor, "one that moves", from movere, "to move".
