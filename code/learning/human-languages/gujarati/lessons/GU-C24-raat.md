## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-SCRIPT-RAAT-01, GU-LEX-RAAT-01, GU-SCRIPT-RA-01, GU-SCRIPT-AA-MATRA-01, GU-SCRIPT-TA-01] -->
<!-- hl-activity: {"id":"GU-C24-raat-reading","kind":"text","assesses":["GU-SCRIPT-RAAT-01","GU-LEX-RAAT-01"],"prompt":"Read this word aloud and give its meaning: રાત.","answer":"rāt — night","accepted":["rat night","rāt night"],"feedback":{"correct":"You read rāt from signs you already knew.","incorrect":"Name the three signs one at a time, then read the word again."},"response_seconds":15} -->

Uncover **રાત** once. Nothing in it is new: **ર** was taught in the courtesy
chapter, the **ા** stroke in the very first one, and **ત** beside it. Name the
three parts aloud in order, then read the whole word once.
