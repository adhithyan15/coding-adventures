## You'll want to know: untersuchen
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C487-HAUSARZT-03]; assesses=[] -->

**untersuchen** — "to examine".

> Die Ärztin untersucht mein Knie.
>
> *The doctor examines my knee.*

Inseparable: *hat untersucht*. The noun: *die Untersuchung*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*untersuchen* is built on *unter* "under" and *suchen* "to seek", inherited from Old High German *suohhen*, from Proto-Germanic *sōkijaną* (EtymDB).
