## You'll want to know: അണുബാധ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C532-ROGAM532-02]; assesses=[] -->

**അണുബാധ** — *aṇubādha* — "an infection".

> മുറിവിൽ അണുബാധ ഉണ്ടായി.
>
> *muṟivil aṇubādha uṇṭāyi.*
>
> The wound got infected.

അണു a germ + ബാധ an affliction. അണു also means an atom.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**അണുബാധ** (*aṇubādha*) is a learned compound built from Sanskrit *aṇu*, "fine, minute" (CDIAL 193), and *bādha*, "affliction, distress" (Monier-Williams).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
