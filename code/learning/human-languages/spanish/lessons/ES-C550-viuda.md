## You'll want to know: la viuda
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C550-DIVORCIO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**la viuda** — "the widow".

> La viuda vive sola en el pueblo.
>
> *The widow lives alone in the village.*

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*viuda* is inherited from Latin *vidua* 'a widow' (EtymDB), the feminine of *viduus* 'bereaved'.
