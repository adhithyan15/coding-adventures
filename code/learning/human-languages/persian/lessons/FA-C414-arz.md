## You'll want to know: ارز
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C414-ARZ-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ارز** (*arz*) — "foreign currency".

> نرخ ارز امروز بالا رفت.
>
> *nerkh-e arz emruz bâlâ raft.*
>
> The exchange rate went up today.

Different from *arzesh* "value". *Arz-e khâreji* — foreign currency.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
