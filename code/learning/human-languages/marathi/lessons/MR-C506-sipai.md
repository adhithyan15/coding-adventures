## You'll want to know: शिपाई
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C506-KACHERI506-02]; assesses=[] -->

**शिपाई** — *śipāī* — "an office attendant; a peon".

> शिपायाने सगळ्यांना चहा आणून दिला.
>
> *śipāyāne sagḷyānnā cahā āṇūn dilā.*
>
> The office attendant brought everyone tea.

Masculine.

The oblique is शिपाया-: शिपायाने, शिपायाला.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**शिपाई** (*śipāī*) is a loan from Persian *sipāhī*, "soldier"; in a Marathi office it names the attendant who carries files and tea.
