## You'll want to know: بیننده
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C312-MONTASHER-04]; assesses=[] -->

**بیننده** (*binande*) — "a viewer".

> میلیون ها بیننده این برنامه را تماشا کردند.
>
> *Milyon-hâ binande in barnâme râ tamâshâ kardand.*
>
> Millions of viewers watched this programme.

*bin-*, the present stem of *didan* "to see", + *-ande*. Plural *binandegân* or *binande-hâ*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*binande* is *bin-*, the present stem of *didan* "to see", with the native suffix *-ande* "one who". EtymDB lists no entry.
