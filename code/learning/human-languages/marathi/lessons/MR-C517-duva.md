## You'll want to know: दुवा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C517-JAL517-04]; assesses=[] -->

**दुवा** — *duvā* — "a link; a connecting link".

> शिक्षकांनी सहलीच्या चित्रफितीचा दुवा पाठवला.
>
> *śikṣakānnī sahlīcyā citraphitīcā duvā pāṭhavlā.*
>
> The teacher sent the link to the video of the trip.

Masculine.

Once a link of a chain; now also a link on a web page.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**दुवा** (*duvā*) is the Marathi word for a link, of a chain or of a web page; its origin is not settled.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
