## You'll want to know: 进球
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C498-PITCH498-05]; assesses=[] -->

**进球** — *jìnqiú* — "to score a goal".

> **进球的时候我感觉很开心** *jìnqiú de shíhou wǒ gǎnjué hěn kāixīn* — When I score, I feel great.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**进球** (*jìnqiú*) is built from 进 'to go in' and 球 'a ball', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *tsinH* and *gjuw* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'forward'.
