## You'll want to know: पुञ्जः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C510-AVAKARA510-04]; assesses=[] -->

**पुञ्जः** — *puñjaḥ* — "a pile; a heap".

> गृहस्य बहिः अवकरस्य पुञ्जः अस्ति।
>
> *gṛhasya bahiḥ avakarasya puñjaḥ asti.*
>
> There is a heap of rubbish outside the house.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पुञ्जः** (*puñjaḥ*) is the word MW records for a heap, a mass, a multitude; MW gives no derivation, so its origin is uncertain (MW).
