## You'll want to know: die Zustimmung
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C334-ZUSTIMMEN-02]; assesses=[] -->

**die Zustimmung** — "the approval, the consent".

> Für den Ausflug brauchen die Kinder die Zustimmung der Eltern.
>
> *For the trip the children need their parents' consent.*

*Zustimmung finden* — to meet with approval.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Zustimmung* is the noun of *zustimmen*, formed with *-ung*; the verb is made from *Stimme* "voice, vote" (EtymDB).
