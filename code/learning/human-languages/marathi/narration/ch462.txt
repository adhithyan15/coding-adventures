Marathi, chapter 462: Envy and Greed.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
हेवा (hevā) — envy.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to hurt someone's feelings, then the Marathi for to belittle.

You'll want to know: हेवा.
हेवा — hevā — "envy".
मित्राच्या नव्या सायकलचा त्याला हेवा वाटला. mitrācyā navyā sāykalcā tyālā hevā vāṭlā. He envied his friend's new bicycle.
Masculine.
हेवा (hevā) वाटणे, to envy: the dative for the one who envies, the genitive for the thing. Milder than मत्सर.

The word, taken apart.
हेवा (hevā) is a Marathi noun whose origin is not settled.

Lesson 3 of 5.
किळस (kiḷas) — disgust.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for envy, then the Marathi for hatred.

You'll want to know: किळस.
किळस — kiḷas — "disgust".
घाणेरडं पाणी पाहून मला किळस आली. ghāṇerḍaṁ pāṇī pāhūn malā kiḷas ālī. Seeing the filthy water, I felt disgust.
Feminine.
किळस (kiḷas) येणे, to feel disgust, with the dative; किळसवाणा, disgusting.

The word, taken apart.
किळस (kiḷas) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Marathi verb kiḷasṇẽ, "to loathe", under Sanskrit kliśyate, "is troubled" (CDIAL 3623).

Lesson 4 of 5.
मोह (moh) — temptation.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for hatred, then the Marathi for disgust.

You'll want to know: मोह.
मोह — moh — "temptation".
लाडू पाहून मला खाण्याचा मोह झाला. lāḍū pāhūn malā khāṇyācā moh jhālā. Seeing the ladoos, I was tempted to eat one.
Masculine.
मोह (moh) होणे, to be tempted, with the dative; मोह (moh) टाळणे, to resist temptation.

The word, taken apart.
मोह (moh) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit moha, "bewilderment" (CDIAL 10360); in Marathi it is the pull of temptation.
