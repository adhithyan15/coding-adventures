## You'll want to know: la brisa
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C772-MONTE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**la brisa** — "the breeze".

> Arriba sopla una brisa fresca.
>
> *Up there a cool breeze blows.*

*La brisa del mar* — the sea breeze.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*brisa* comes from old Spanish *briza* (EtymDB), which named the northeast wind. Where *briza* came from is not settled; French *brise* and English *breeze* are related words.
