## You'll want to know: flâner
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C438-FLANER-01]; assesses=[] -->

**flâner** — "to stroll, to wander about".

> Le dimanche, j'aime flâner au marché.
>
> *On Sundays I like strolling round the market.*

*Un flâneur* — someone who strolls with no aim.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*flâner* came into French from a Norman dialect word, which is usually traced to Old Norse *flana* 'to rush about'. EtymDB lists the verb without a source.
