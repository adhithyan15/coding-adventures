## You'll want to know: أغلبية
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C387-UMMA387-04]; assesses=[] -->

**أغلبية** — *aghlabiyya* — "a majority".

> صوتت الأغلبية ضد المشروع.
>
> *ṣawwatat al-aghlabiyya ḍidda al-mashrūʿ.*
>
> The majority voted against the project.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**أغلبية** (*aghlabiyya*) is built on the native Arabic root *gh-l-b*, "to overcome, to prevail" (Lane). *Aghlabiyya* is a modern abstract noun from *aghlab*, most: the greater part, a majority.
