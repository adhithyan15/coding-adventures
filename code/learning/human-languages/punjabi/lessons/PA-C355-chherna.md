## You'll want to know: ਛੇੜਨਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C355-RIDDLE355-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ਛੇੜਨਾ** — *chheṛnā* — "to tease".

> ਮੇਰਾ ਭਰਾ ਮੈਨੂੰ ਹਰ ਰੋਜ਼ ਛੇੜਦਾ ਹੈ।
>
> *merā bharā mainū̃ har roz chheṛdā hai.*
>
> My brother teases me every day.

Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਭੈਣ ਨੂੰ ਛੇੜਿਆ, *maiṁ bhaiṇ nū̃ chheṛiā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਛੇੜਨਾ** (*chheṛnā*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*ciḍ*, "be angry", with *\*cheḍ*, "make angry"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *cheṛnā*, "to provoke", under it (CDIAL 4794).
