## You'll want to know: ゆとり
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C357-KEIYAKU357-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ゆとり** — *yutori* — "leeway; room to spare".

> **おかねに ゆとりが あったら、ちょきんします。** — *okane ni yutori ga attara, chokin shimasu* — "If I have money to spare, I save it."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ゆとり** (*yutori*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, and its older origin is not settled; JMdict gives it no kanji spelling (JMdict).
