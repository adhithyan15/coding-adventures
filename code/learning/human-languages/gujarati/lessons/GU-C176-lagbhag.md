## You'll want to know: લગભગ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C176-CLOCK176-05]; assesses=[] -->

**લગભગ** — *lagbhag* — "almost, about".

> **તે લગભગ બે કલાકમાં આવશે.** — *te lagbhag be kalākmā̃ āvśe* — "He will come in about two hours."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

The origin of **લગભગ** (*lagbhag*) is not settled. CDIAL lists no Gujarati form of it, and EtymDB has no entry for it.
