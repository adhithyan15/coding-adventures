## You'll want to know: فارس
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C341-FARIS341-01]; assesses=[] -->

**فارس** — *fāris* — "a knight, a horseman".

> ركب الفارس حصانه الأبيض.
>
> *rakiba al-fāris ḥiṣānahu al-abyaḍ.*
>
> The knight rode his white horse.

Plural: فرسان, *fursān*.

فارس الأحلام: Prince Charming.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**فارس** (*fāris*) is built on the native Arabic root *f-r-s* (Lane), the root of *faras*, a horse. *Fāris* is the man on the horse; *furūsiyya* is horsemanship and knighthood.
