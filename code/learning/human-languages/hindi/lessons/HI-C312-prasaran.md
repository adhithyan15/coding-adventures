## You'll want to know: प्रसारण
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C312-SAMACHAR312-03]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रसारण** — *prasāraṇ* — "a broadcast".

> कल रात तूफ़ान की वजह से प्रसारण बंद रहा।
>
> *kal rāt tūfān kī vajah se prasāraṇ band rahā.*
>
> Last night the broadcast was off because of the storm.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रसारण** (*prasāraṇ*) is Sanskrit *prasāraṇa*, "a spreading out, an extending" (Monier-Williams): news spread out over the air. The sense of broadcasting is modern.
