## You'll want to know: 晚上
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C49-DAY49-03]; assesses=[] -->

**晚上** — *wǎnshang* — "evening".

> **晚上我看书** *wǎnshang wǒ kàn shū* — I read in the evenings.

The new character, in pieces: 晚 — 日 on the left and a seven-stroke piece on the right. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**晚上** (*wǎnshang*) is built from 晚 'dusk' and the light ending shang, as in 早上. 晚 is inherited from Middle Chinese *mjonX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'dusk'.
