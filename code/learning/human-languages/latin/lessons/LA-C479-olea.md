## You'll want to know: olea, oleae
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C479-POMARIUM-02]; assesses=[] -->

**olea, oleae** — "an olive tree; an olive".

> In colle multae oleae sunt.
>
> *On the hill there are many olive trees.*

First declension, feminine: *olea, oleae f.* A variant of *olīva*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*olea* was borrowed from Greek *elaia* 'olive, olive tree' (EtymDB), like *olīva* and *oleum* 'oil'.
