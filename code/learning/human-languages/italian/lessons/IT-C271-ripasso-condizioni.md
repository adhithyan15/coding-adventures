# Review — plans with a branch in them

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[IT-LEX-DIPENDE-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Someone asks *Vai alla festa domani?* Give the answer that decides
nothing. (*Dipende.*)

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[IT-GRAMMAR-SE-01, IT-GRAMMAR-SE-PRESENTE-01, IT-GRAMMAR-DIPENDE-DA-02, IT-GRAMMAR-DUE-FRECCE-01, IT-LEX-ANDATA-E-RITORNO-01, IT-GRAMMAR-BIGLIETTO-PER-02] -->

Three ways now to talk about a cause, and they differ in how sure you are.

> *Piove, **quindi** resto a casa.* — It is raining: decided.
> ***Se** piove, resto a casa.* — It might: the branch is ready.
> ***Dipende.*** — Not even the branch is named yet.

Put them together at a ticket window and you have a whole conversation:

> — *Vorrei un biglietto per Roma.* — *Andata e ritorno?*
> — *Dipende. Se torno domani, andata e ritorno. Se resto, solo andata.*

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[IT-GRAMMAR-SE-01, IT-ETYMON-SE-SI-02, IT-GRAMMAR-SE-PRESENTE-01, IT-LEX-DIPENDE-01] -->

[PAUSE 3s] Order the three from most sure to least. (***Quindi***, then
***se***, then ***dipende***.) Which tense goes in both halves of *se piove,
resto a casa*? (The **present**.) Which Latin word is *se*? (***Sī***, "if".)
