## You'll want to know: ચૂમવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C451-VALAG451-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ચૂમવું** — *cūmvũ* — "to kiss".

> માતા રોજ રાતે બાળકના કપાળને ચૂમે છે.
>
> *mātā roj rāte bāḷaknā kapāḷne cūme che.*
>
> Every night the mother kisses the child's forehead.

Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે બાળકનું કપાળ ચૂમ્યું, *teṇe bāḷaknũ kapāḷ cūmyũ*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ચૂમવું** (*cūmvũ*) is inherited from Sanskrit *cumbati*, "kisses"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb (CDIAL 4870).
