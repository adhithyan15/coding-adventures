# बिलकुल नहीं (bilkul nahīṁ) — not at all

## You'll want to know: बिलकुल नहीं
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C127-NOTATALL-02]; assesses=[] -->

**बिलकुल नहीं** (*bilkul nahīṁ*) — "not at all", "absolutely not".

**बिलकुल** is the "absolutely" you know; with **नहीं** it turns a no all the way down.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

The strongest no.
