## You'll want to know: dēngjì
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C316-PLACE316-01]; assesses=[] -->

*dēngjì* — "to register officially".

> *wǒmen qù pàichūsuǒ dēngjì* — We went to the police station to register.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*dēngjì* is built from *dēng* 'to go up' and 记 'to note down', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *tong* and *kiH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to rise' and 'to go forward': to enter in the record.

## What you've built
