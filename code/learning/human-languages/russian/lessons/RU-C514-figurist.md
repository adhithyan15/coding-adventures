## You'll want to know: фигурист
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C514-ICE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**фигурист** (*figuríst*) — "a figure skater".

> Фигурист выступил очень красиво.
>
> *Figuríst výstupil óchen krasívo.*
>
> The figure skater performed beautifully.

Masculine; feminine фигуристка.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*фигурист* is built on *фигура* 'figure', usually traced to Latin *figūra* 'a shape'; EtymDB has no entry.
