## You'll want to know: लोकतंत्र
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C354-NAGRIK354-04]; assesses=[] -->

**लोकतंत्र** — *loktantra* — "democracy".

> लोकतंत्र में लोग अपने नेता चुनते हैं।
>
> *loktantra meṁ log apne netā cunte haiṁ.*
>
> In a democracy the people choose their leaders.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**लोकतंत्र** (*loktantra*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *lokatantra*, "the course of the world" (Monier-Williams). Modern Hindi gives the compound of *loka*, "people", and *tantra*, "system", a new sense: rule by the people.
