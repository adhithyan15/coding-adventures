Portuguese, chapter 113: Word, Board, Rubber, Homework, Problem.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
a palavra — a word.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for a lesson, then the Portuguese for an exam.
[pause 3 seconds]
Read the two lines aloud, as one run: O cinema está atrás. A piscina está ao lado.

You'll want to know: a palavra.
a palavra — "a word".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: a palavra]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: a palavra, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say a palavra]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Portuguese for a lesson, then the Portuguese for an exam, then say a palavra again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does a palavra mean? ("A word".) Say it once more.
