## You'll want to know: پازل
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C509-GAMES-01]; assesses=[] -->

**پازل** (*pâzel*) — "a jigsaw puzzle".

> ما شبها با هم پازل درست میکنیم.
>
> *mâ shabhâ bâ ham pâzel dorost mikonim.*
>
> We do jigsaws together in the evenings.

*pâzel dorost kardan* — to do a jigsaw.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*pâzel* is a modern loan, usually traced to English *puzzle*. EtymDB has no entry for it.
