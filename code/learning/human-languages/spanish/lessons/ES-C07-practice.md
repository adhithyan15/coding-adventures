# Practice — asking real questions

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-GRAMMAR-AR-PRESENT-SINGULAR, ES-GRAMMAR-ER-PRESENT-SINGULAR, ES-GRAMMAR-IR-PRESENT-SINGULAR] -->

[PAUSE 2s] You now have all three verb families **and** two question words. This
is where it becomes a conversation: you can **ask** and **answer**, not just
recite. Drill until the questions come without thinking.

## Grammar Lens: three singular patterns
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-COMER, ES-GRAMMAR-ER-PRESENT-SINGULAR, ES-LEX-VIVIR, ES-GRAMMAR-IR-PRESENT-SINGULAR, ES-LEX-BEBER, ES-GRAMMAR-AR-PRESENT-SINGULAR, ES-GRAMMAR-PRO-DROP] -->

[PAUSE 1s] Conjugate *yo → tú → él/ella* — feel how little differs:

- **-ar** *hablar*: hablo · hablas · habla
- **-er** *comer*: como · comes · come
- **-ir** *vivir*: vivo · vives · vive

**Endings:** *-ar* → -o/-as/-a; *-er* and *-ir* → **-o/-es/-e** in these
three singular forms. The plural forms can wait.

## The exchange — use only what you know
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-DONDE, ES-LEX-EN, ES-LEX-VIVIR, ES-GRAMMAR-IR-PRESENT-SINGULAR, ES-LEX-QUE, ES-LEX-BEBER, ES-GRAMMAR-ER-PRESENT-SINGULAR, ES-LEX-CAFE, ES-LEX-ESTUDIAR, ES-LEX-ESPANOL] -->

> — ¿**Dónde vives**? — **Vivo** en Sevilla. ¿Y tú? — **Vivo** en México.
> — ¿**Qué estudias**? — **Estudio** español. ¿**Qué bebes**? — **Bebo** café.

## What you've built this chapter
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-COMER, ES-GRAMMAR-ER-PRESENT-SINGULAR, ES-ETYMON-COMEDERE, ES-LEX-VIVIR, ES-GRAMMAR-IR-PRESENT-SINGULAR, ES-ETYMON-VIVERE, ES-LEX-BEBER, ES-LEX-QUE, ES-ETYMON-QUID, ES-LEX-DONDE, ES-LEX-EN, ES-ETYMON-DE-UNDE] -->

- **comer / beber** (-er), **vivir** (-ir) — the two remaining verb families;
  singular endings **-o / -es / -e**.
- **qué** (← *quid*) and **dónde** (← *de unde*) — your first question words,
  each wearing the **accent** and opening with **¿**.
- **Real questions**: *¿Qué comes? ¿Dónde vives? ¿Dónde trabajas?* — and the first
  preposition, **en** ("in"), in the answers.

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-COMER, ES-GRAMMAR-ER-PRESENT-SINGULAR, ES-ETYMON-COMEDERE, ES-LEX-VIVIR, ES-GRAMMAR-IR-PRESENT-SINGULAR, ES-ETYMON-VIVERE, ES-LEX-BEBER, ES-LEX-QUE, ES-ETYMON-QUID, ES-LEX-DONDE, ES-LEX-EN, ES-ETYMON-DE-UNDE] -->

[PAUSE 3s] What are the singular endings for *-er/-ir* verbs? (*-o / -es / -e*.)
Give the "I" forms of *comer, vivir, beber*. (*Como, vivo, bebo*.) Ask "what do
you study?" and "where do you live?" (*¿Qué estudias? ¿Dónde vives?*) You can now
hold a simple back-and-forth — this book is fully in sentence territory. Next:
**numbers** (uno, dos, tres) and telling someone your age and phone.
