## You'll want to know: قبر
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C337-MARG-05]; assesses=[] -->

**قبر** (*qabr*) — "a grave".

> هر پنجشنبه سر قبر پدرش میرفت.
>
> *Har panjshanbe sar-e qabr-e pedarash miraft.*
>
> Every Thursday he used to go to his father's grave.

Broken plural *qobur*. Thursday is the usual day for visiting graves.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*qabr* is borrowed from Arabic *qabr*, "grave" (EtymDB).
