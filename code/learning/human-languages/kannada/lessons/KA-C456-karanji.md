# ಕಾರಂಜಿ (kārañji) — a fountain

## You'll want to know: ಕಾರಂಜಿ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C456-SMARAKA456-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ಕಾರಂಜಿ** — *kārañji* — "a fountain".

> ಉದ್ಯಾನದ ನಡುವೆ ಒಂದು ಸುಂದರ ಕಾರಂಜಿ ಇದೆ.
>
> *udyānada naḍuve ondu sundara kārañji ide.*
>
> There is a beautiful fountain in the middle of the park.

Many town circles have a ಕಾರಂಜಿ.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಕಾರಂಜಿ** (*kārañji*) is a Kannada word whose origin is not settled.
