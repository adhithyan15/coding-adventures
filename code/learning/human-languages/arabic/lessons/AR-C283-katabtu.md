# كتبت (katabtu) — the past, person by person

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[AR-GRAMMAR-C283-PRESENT-PERSONS-01, AR-LEX-C156-MADI156-05] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "we write", then "he writes". (*Naktubu*, *yaktubu*.)

[PAUSE 1s] You have said *qaḍaytu al-ʿuṭla fī al-qarya*, "I spent the
holiday in the village". That *-tu* at the back is "I" in the past. Here is
the rest of the set.

## Grammar Lens: the endings at the back
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-GRAMMAR-C283-PAST-PERSONS-01]; assesses=[AR-CONCEPT-C31-KATABA-01] -->

*Kataba*, he wrote, is the bare past. Every other person is an ending on the
same stem, *katab-*:

> **كتبتُ** *katabtu*, I wrote · **كتبتَ** *katabta*, you wrote (to a man) ·
> **كتبتِ** *katabti*, you wrote (to a woman) · **كتبَ** *kataba*, he wrote ·
> **كتبتْ** *katabat*, she wrote · **كتبنا** *katabnā*, we wrote ·
> **كتبوا** *katabū*, they wrote · **كتبتم** *katabtum*, you wrote (to several)

The present put the person at the front; the past puts it at the back.

Without its vowel marks, **كتبت** spells four of these at once: *katabtu*,
*katabta*, *katabti* and *katabat*. That is why they carry marks here, and
why, on an unmarked page, the rest of the sentence tells you who wrote.

## Grammar Lens: other verbs, and "did not"
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[AR-GRAMMAR-C283-PAST-PERSONS-01, AR-GRAMMAR-C39-MA-01, AR-LEX-C87-TIME87-01, AR-LEX-C93-TOWN93-02, AR-LEX-C117-THINGS117-04, AR-LEX-C55-FAMILY55-01] -->

> **أمس كتبت رسالة إلى جدتي.** — *ams katabtu risālatan ilā jaddatī* —
> Yesterday I wrote a letter to my grandmother.

> **ذهبنا إلى السوق.** — *dhahabnā ilā as-sūq* — We went to the market.

*Qaraʾa* gives *qaraʾtu*, I read; *dhahaba* gives *dhahabat*, she went.
The **ا** at the end of **كتبوا** is written and not said.

**ما** in front makes it "did not": *mā katabtu*, I didn't write;
*mā dhahabnā*, we didn't go.

[PAUSE 3s] Where does the past mark the person? (**At the back.**) "She
wrote"? (***Katabat.***) "We wrote"? (***Katabnā.***) How many persons can
