## You'll want to know: घोळणो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C182-JHATKO182-02]; assesses=[] -->

**घोळणो** — *ghoḷṇo* — "to dissolve; to stir in".

> हूं पाणी मांय गुड़ घोळूं हूं।
>
> *hū̃ pāṇī mā̃y guṛ ghoḷū̃ hū̃.*
>
> I dissolve jaggery in water.

Past (I …): म्हैं गुड़ घोळ्यो, *mhaĩ guṛ ghoḷyo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**घोळणो** (*ghoḷṇo*) is inherited from Sanskrit *gholayati*, "mixes, stirs together"; CDIAL lists Hindi *gholnā*, "to mix, dissolve", and Gujarati *ghoḷvũ* (CDIAL 4526). CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry.
