Lesson 1 of 5.
સિદ્ધાંત (siddhā̃t) — a principle (a theory).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a mystery, then the Gujarati for to uncover.

You'll want to know: સિદ્ધાંત.
સિદ્ધાંત — siddhā̃t — "a principle (a theory)".
આ સિદ્ધાંત કાગળ પર સારો છે. ā siddhā̃t kāgaḷ par sāro che. This theory looks good on paper.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
સિદ્ધાંત (siddhā̃t) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit siddhānta, "an established end, a settled doctrine" (Monier-Williams).

You'll want to know: સાંકળવું.
સાંકળવું — sā̃kaḷvũ — "to link".
ગુરુ નવો પાઠ જૂના પાઠ સાથે સાંકળે છે. guru navo pāṭh jūnā pāṭh sāthe sā̃kḷe che. The teacher links the new lesson to the old one.
Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે બંને વિચાર સાંકળ્યા, teṇe banne vicār sā̃kaḷyā.

The word, taken apart.
સાંકળવું (sā̃kaḷvũ) is built on sā̃kaḷ, "a chain", which CDIAL traces to Sanskrit śṛṅkhala, "a chain" (CDIAL 12580): to chain two things together.
