Telugu, chapter 165: To spill, To shave, To tighten, To prick, To knit.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
చిందు (cindu) — to spill.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to tear, then the Telugu for to wrap.

You'll want to know: చిందు.
చిందు — cindu — "to spill".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.

[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does చిందు (cindu) mean? ("To spill".) Say it once more.
