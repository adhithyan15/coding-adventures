# తాళి (tāḷi) — a wedding pendant

## You'll want to know: తాళి
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C346-VIVAHAM346-04]; assesses=[] -->

**తాళి** — *tāḷi* — "a wedding pendant".

> పెళ్ళికొడుకు పెళ్ళికూతురు మెడలో తాళి కట్టాడు.
>
> *peḷḷikoḍuku peḷḷikūturu meḍalō tāḷi kaṭṭāḍu.*
>
> The bridegroom tied the wedding pendant round the bride's neck.

తాళి కట్టు, to tie the tali: the moment a Telugu wedding is sealed.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**తాళి** (*tāḷi*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 3175 lists Telugu *tāli*, the small piece of gold the bridegroom ties at the neck of the bride, with Tamil *tāli*.
