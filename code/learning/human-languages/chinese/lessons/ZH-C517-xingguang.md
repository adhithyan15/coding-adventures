## You'll want to know: 星光
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C517-LIGHT517-04]; assesses=[] -->

**星光** — *xīngguāng* — "starlight".

> **山里的星光最好看** *shān lǐ de xīngguāng zuì hǎokàn* — The starlight is loveliest up in the hills.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**星光** (*xīngguāng*) is built from 星 'a star' and 光 'light', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *seng* and *kwang* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'bright'.
