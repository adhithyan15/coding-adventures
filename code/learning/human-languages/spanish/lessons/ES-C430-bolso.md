# el bolso — a hide, a purse, and one vowel that changes the object

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-C430-VIDA-03, ES-LEX-C360-REF-16] -->

[PAUSE 2s] You have clothes and now a jumper. Name the thing carried **beside**
the clothes rather than worn, and see whether you have it.

## You'll want to know: el bolso
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C430-VIDA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**el bolso** — the handbag.

Not a shopping bag and not a pocket. A *bolso* is the bag carried on the arm or
shoulder, and a shop will file it under accessories beside the shoes.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-C430-VIDA-04] -->

Greek **byrsa** meant **a hide, a piece of worked leather**. Latin took it as
**bursa**, and because a leather pouch is what money was carried in, the word
came to mean **purse**.

From that one pouch:

| word | what it kept |
|---|---|
| el bolso | the bag on your arm |
| el bolsillo | the little one sewn into your clothes |
| la bolsa | the bag, and the stock exchange |

English got **bursar**, **reimburse** and **purse** from the same leather.

The stock-exchange sense is the strangest and the best attested: traders in
fifteenth-century Bruges met at the house of a family called **Van der Buerse**,
and the name of the meeting place became the name of the market everywhere.

## Grammar Lens: the vowel that picks the object
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-C430-VIDA-04, ES-LEX-C430-VIDA-02] -->

**Bolso** and **bolsa** are one letter apart and name different things:

| word | what it is |
|---|---|
| el bolso | a handbag |
| la bolsa | a bag, a carrier bag, the stock exchange |

Ask for *una bolsa* in a shop and you get something to carry the shopping home
in. Ask for *un bolso* and you are shown the leather goods.

This is a pattern worth knowing rather than a single oddity: Spanish sometimes
uses the **-o / -a** pair to separate two related objects rather than two
genders of one. You have already met *el pantalón* keeping its own shape for the
same kind of reason.
