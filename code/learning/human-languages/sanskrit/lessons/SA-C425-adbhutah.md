# अद्भुतः (adbhutaḥ) — marvellous; wonderful

## You'll want to know: अद्भुतः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C425-SMARAKA425-01]; assesses=[] -->

**अद्भुतः** — *adbhutaḥ* — "marvellous; wonderful".

> मम यात्रायाः अनुभवः अद्भुतः अस्ति।
>
> *mama yātrāyāḥ anubhavaḥ adbhutaḥ asti.*
>
> The experience of my journey is marvellous.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अद्भुतः** (*adbhutaḥ*) is the word MW gives for wonderful, marvellous; MW gives no sure derivation, so its origin is uncertain (MW).
