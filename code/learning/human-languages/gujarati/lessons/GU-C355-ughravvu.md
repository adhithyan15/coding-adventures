## You'll want to know: ઉઘરાવવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C355-VERO355-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ઉઘરાવવું** — *ughrāvvũ* — "to collect money (from people)".

> ગામના જુવાનો ઉજાણી માટે ફાળો ઉઘરાવે છે.
>
> *gāmnā juvāno ujāṇī māṭe phāḷo ughrāve che.*
>
> The young people of the village are collecting money for a picnic.

Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે બધો ફાળો ઉઘરાવ્યો, *teṇe badho phāḷo ughrāvyo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ઉઘરાવવું** (*ughrāvvũ*) is inherited from *udgharati*, a form CDIAL reconstructs; CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb, "to collect money promised" (CDIAL 1971).
