Spanish, chapter 637: A favor o en contra.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
favorecer — to favour; to suit.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for honest, then the Spanish for to deserve.

You'll want to know: favorecer.
favorecer — "to favour; to suit".
La nueva ley favorece a las familias. The new law favours families.
Like conocer: favorezco. Ese color te favorece — that colour suits you.

The word, taken apart.
favorecer is built on favor with the ending -ecer; favor is a learned borrowing from Latin favor, from favere 'to be well disposed' (EtymDB).

You'll want to know: perjudicar.

The word, taken apart.
perjudicar is a learned borrowing from Latin praeiudicare 'to judge in advance, to harm a case', built on iudicare and iudex 'judge' (EtymDB).

You'll want to know: conveniente.
conveniente — "advisable, convenient".
Es conveniente llegar pronto. It is advisable to arrive early.
Es conveniente que + subjunctive.

The word, taken apart.
conveniente is a learned borrowing from Latin conveniens 'agreeing, fitting', the participle of convenire 'to come together', from venire 'to come' (EtymDB).
