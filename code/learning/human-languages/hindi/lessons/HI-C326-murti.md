## You'll want to know: मूर्ति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C326-KHANDAHAR326-03]; assesses=[] -->

**मूर्ति** — *mūrti* — "a statue; an image".

> मज़दूरों को मिट्टी में एक पत्थर की मूर्ति मिली।
>
> *mazdūroṁ ko miṭṭī meṁ ek patthar kī mūrti milī.*
>
> The labourers found a stone statue in the earth.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**मूर्ति** (*mūrti*) is Sanskrit *mūrti*, "a solid body, a form" (Monier-Williams): a figure of stone, metal or clay, and in a temple the image of a god.
