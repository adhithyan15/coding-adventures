## You'll want to know: गणना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C366-NISHKARSHA366-03]; assesses=[] -->

**गणना** — *gaṇanā* — "a calculation; a count".

> गणनायां दोषः अस्ति।
>
> *gaṇanāyāṃ doṣaḥ asti.*
>
> There is a mistake in the calculation.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**गणना** (*gaṇanā*) comes from the root √gaṇ, "to count"; MW gives *gaṇanā* as the same as *gaṇana*, "counting, calculation" (MW).
