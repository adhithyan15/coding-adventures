Telugu, chapter 182: Silence, Durable, Fake, Plain, Extra.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
నిశ్శబ్దం (niśśabdaṁ) — silence; quiet.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for wonderful, then the Telugu for ordinary.

You'll want to know: నిశ్శబ్దం.
నిశ్శబ్దం — niśśabdaṁ — "silence; quiet".

The word, taken apart.
నిశ్శబ్దం (niśśabdaṁ) is Sanskrit niḥśabda, "soundless", from śabda, "sound, noise" (CDIAL 12298), with the Telugu ending -ṁ.

Lesson 2 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: మన్నికైన.
మన్నికైన — mannikaina — "durable, long-lasting".

The word, taken apart.
మన్నికైన (mannikaina) is native Dravidian, built on mannu, "to last, be durable", one word with Tamil maṉṉu, "to endure" (DEDR 4778), with aina, "which is" (DEDR 333).
