Sanskrit, chapter 438: Summer Heat.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
आतपः (ātapaḥ) — sunshine; the sun's heat.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a drop, then the Sanskrit for drips.

You'll want to know: आतपः.
आतपः — ātapaḥ — "sunshine; the sun's heat".
मध्याह्ने आतपः उष्णः भवति। madhyāhne ātapaḥ uṣṇaḥ bhavati. At noon the sunshine is hot.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
आतपः (ātapaḥ) is built from ā and the root √tap, "to heat, to burn", which also gives tapati, "heats"; MW gives ātapa as heat, especially of the sun, sunshine (MW).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for scorching, then the Sanskrit for sweat.

You'll want to know: दीप्यते.
दीप्यते — dīpyate — "blazes; glows".
मध्याह्ने सूर्यः दीप्यते। madhyāhne sūryaḥ dīpyate. At noon the sun blazes.
Past (it …): सूर्यः दीप्तवान्, sūryaḥ dīptavān.

The word, taken apart.
दीप्यते (dīpyate) comes from the root √dīp, "to blaze, to shine", which also gives dīpa, "a lamp"; MW gives dīpyate as to blaze, flare, shine, with the participle dīpta (MW).
