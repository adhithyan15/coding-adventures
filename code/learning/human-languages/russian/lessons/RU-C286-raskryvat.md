## You'll want to know: раскрывать
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C286-OSVESHCHAT-03]; assesses=[] -->

**раскрывать** (*raskryvát*) — "to reveal, to uncover; to solve (a case)".

> Журналист раскрыл тайну компании.
>
> *Zhurnalíst raskrýl táynu kompánii.*
>
> The journalist uncovered the company's secret.

Imperfective; perfective *раскрыть* (*raskrýt*), *я раскрою*. *Раскрыть тайну* — to reveal a secret.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*раскрывать* is built on *рас-* "apart" and *крыть* "to cover", an inherited Slavic verb: to take the cover off. EtymDB lists no parent for the compound.
