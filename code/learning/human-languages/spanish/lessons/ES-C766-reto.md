## You'll want to know: el reto
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C766-RETO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**el reto** — "the challenge".

> Aprender chino es un gran reto.
>
> *Learning Chinese is a big challenge.*

*Desafío* is a synonym.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*reto* is built on *retar* (EtymDB), which comes from Old French *reter* "to accuse", from Latin *reputāre* "to reckon".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.
