## The exchange
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-PERFORMANCE-WELLBEING-01]; assesses=[ZH-DIALOGUE-MORNING, ZH-LEX-ZAOSHANGHAO, ZH-LEX-ZAOSHANG, ZH-LEX-ZENMEYANG-01, ZH-LEX-HENHAO-01, ZH-LEX-HEN-01, ZH-LEX-NE-01, ZH-LEX-ZAIJIAN] -->

> **A:** 早上好
>
> *zǎoshang hǎo* — good morning.
>
> **B:** 早上好 — *nǐ zěnmeyàng*
>
> *zǎoshang hǎo, nǐ zěnmeyàng* — good morning. How are you doing?
>
> **A:** 我很好 你呢
>
> *wǒ hěn hǎo, nǐ ne* — I'm fine. And you?
>
> **B:** *hái xíng, wǒ hěn máng* — not bad. I'm busy.
>
> **A:** *nǐ lèi ma* — are you tired?
>
> **B:** *hěn lèi, zàijiàn* — I am. Goodbye.
>
> **A:** 再见
>
> *zàijiàn* — goodbye.

The words whose characters this book has not written yet stay in pinyin; you
know them by ear, and here the ear carries them. The morning greeting is one of
the first things this book gave you, and this is the first exchange to carry on
past it.

## What you've built — four answers to one question
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ZH-PERFORMANCE-WELLBEING-01, ZH-SCRIPT-HEN-01, ZH-LEX-HEN-01] -->

One question, *nǐ zěnmeyàng*, and four answers, every one of them with a plain
很 or none at all:

- *wǒ hěn hǎo* — I'm fine.
- *hái xíng* — not bad.
- *wǒ hěn máng* — I'm busy.
- *wǒ hěn lèi* — I'm tired.

**Listen.** *Nǐ zěnmeyàng?*

**Speak.** Answer it four ways, then hand it back with 你呢.

[YOU WRITE: 很 — 彳 first, then 艮]
