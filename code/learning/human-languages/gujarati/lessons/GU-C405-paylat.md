## You'll want to know: પાયલટ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C405-FLIGHT405-02]; assesses=[] -->

**પાયલટ** — *pāylaṭ* — "a pilot".

> પાયલટે વિમાન ધીમે નીચે ઉતાર્યું.
>
> *pāyalṭe vimān dhīme nīce utāryũ.*
>
> The pilot brought the plane down slowly.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**પાયલટ** (*pāylaṭ*) is borrowed from English *pilot*.
