# ظ (zoe) — the letter that ends حافظ

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[UR-SCRIPT-C37-QAF-01, UR-SCRIPT-C37-ZUAD-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: write ق and ض once each, and name them.

[PAUSE 2s] Now say **حافظ** (*hāfiz*), "guardian".
You have read it for a long time. This lesson takes one letter out of it and puts
it in your hand.

## Script you'll notice: ظ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-SCRIPT-C37-ZOE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ظ** — *zoe*, the sound *z* again.

It ends **حافظ** (*hāfiz*). A closed **oval** on the line, a **tall upright**, and
**one dot**. It sounds exactly like **ض** from the last lesson: two spellings, one
sound, both kept from the Arabic words they came with.

## Writing: ظ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[UR-SCRIPT-C37-ZOE-01] -->

- **1.** start here: loop counterclockwise around the closed oval
- **2.** finish left along the baseline without lifting
- **3.** after one lift, draw the tall upright
- **4.** after another lift, place the single dot above — and only now lift

**Pen lifts: 2.**

> Stroke order is one attested teaching order; hands and teachers vary.
> Source: Zer o Zabar, To’e, zo’e, se, zhe, and ġhain, independent ظ handwriting animation and Zo’e instructions (Northwestern University Libraries Open Textbook, 2026).

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[UR-SCRIPT-C37-QAF-01, UR-SCRIPT-C37-ZUAD-01, UR-SCRIPT-C37-ZOE-01] -->

[PAUSE 1s]
- [YOU LOOK: at **حافظ** and point at ظ inside it]
- [YOU TRACE: ظ three times, saying *z* as you finish each one]
- [YOU WRITE: ض, then ظ, from memory]
- [YOU READ: **حافظ** aloud once more]

come from? (**حافظ**, *hāfiz*, "guardian".)
