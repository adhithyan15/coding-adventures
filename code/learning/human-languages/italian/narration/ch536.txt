Lesson 1 of 7.
l'inquilino — tenant.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for to fix, then the Italian for to paint.

You'll want to know: l'inquilino.
l'inquilino — "tenant".
I nuovi inquilini del secondo piano sono molto gentili. The new tenants on the second floor are very nice.
A woman is l'inquilina.

The word, taken apart.
inquilino is a learned borrowing from Latin inquilīnus "a tenant, a lodger" (EtymDB).

Lesson 2 of 7.
il padrone di casa — landlord.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for to paint, then the Italian for tenant.

You'll want to know: il padrone di casa.
il padrone di casa — "landlord".
Il padrone di casa ha aumentato l'affitto. The landlord has put up the rent.
La padrona di casa — landlady; also a host or hostess.

The word, taken apart.
padrone is inherited from Latin patrōnus "a protector, a patron" (EtymDB), from pater "father"; casa is inherited from Latin casa "a hut".
