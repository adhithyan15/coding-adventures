## You'll want to know: がまん
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C222-CARE222-05]; assesses=[] -->

**がまん** — *gaman* — "endurance; putting up with something".

> **こどもは がまんが にがてです。** — *kodomo wa gaman ga nigate desu* — "Children are bad at putting up with things."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**がまん** (*gaman*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *ga*, "self" (Middle Chinese *ngaX*) and *man*, "ridicule, laziness" (*maenH*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
