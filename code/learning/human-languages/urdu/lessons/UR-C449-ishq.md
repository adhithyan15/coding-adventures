## You'll want to know: عشق
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C449-LOVE449-05]; assesses=[] -->

**عشق** — *ishq* — "passionate love".

> اسے موسیقی سے عشق ہے۔
>
> *use mausīqī se ishq hai.*
>
> He is passionate about music.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**عشق** (*ishq*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *ʿishq*, "passionate love".
