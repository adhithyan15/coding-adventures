## You'll want to know: りょうきん
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C186-SAVE186-04]; assesses=[] -->

**りょうきん** — *ryōkin* — "fee; charge, fare".

> **りょうきんは いくらですか。** — *ryōkin wa ikura desu ka* — "How much is the fee?"

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**りょうきん** (*ryōkin*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *ryō*, "fee, materials" (Middle Chinese *lew*) and *kin*, "gold, money" (*kim*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
