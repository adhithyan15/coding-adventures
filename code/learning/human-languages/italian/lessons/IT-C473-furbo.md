## You'll want to know: furbo
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C473-SINCERITA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**furbo** — "sly, crafty; clever".

> Il gatto è furbo: apre la porta da solo.
>
> *The cat is crafty: it opens the door by itself.*

Often a compliment in speech: *che furbo!* — how clever!

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*furbo* was borrowed from French *fourbe* "a cheat" (EtymDB), which is built on *fourbir* "to polish", a verb of Germanic origin.
