Urdu, chapter 145: The Future, Next, Soon, a Plan, To Decide.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
مستقبل (mustaqbil) — the future.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for last, then the Urdu for to happen.

You'll want to know: مستقبل.
مستقبل — mustaqbil — "the future". Urdu's future adds گا, گی or گے to the verb's -ūṅ form: میں پڑھوں گا۔ — maiṅ paṛhūṅgā — "I will read." A woman says پڑھوں گی, paṛhūṅgī.

The word, taken apart.
مستقبل (mustaqbil) is a loan from Arabic mustaqbil, "coming to meet; the future", which reached Urdu through Persian. Its Arabic root is q-b-l (Lane). It is built on the same root as qabūl, "acceptance".

Lesson 2 of 5.
آئندہ (āindā) — next, in future.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for to happen, then the Urdu for the future.

You'll want to know: آئندہ.
آئندہ — āindā — "next, in future". آئندہ ہفتے — āindā hafte — "next week". On its own it means "from now on": آئندہ ایسا نہ کرنا۔ — āindā aisā na karnā — "Don't do that again."

The word, taken apart.
آئندہ (āindā) is a loan, usually traced to Persian āyanda, "coming". It is the present participle of āmadan, "to come".

Lesson 3 of 5.
جلد ہی (jald hī) — soon.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for the future, then the Urdu for next.

You'll want to know: جلد ہی.
جلد ہی — jald hī — "soon". جلد has two readings: jild, "skin" or "a volume", and jald, "quickly". Here it is jald, with the emphatic ہی: وہ جلد ہی آئے گا۔ — voh jald hī āegā — "He will come soon."

The phrase, taken apart.
جلد ہی (jald hī) is jald, "quick", usually traced to Persian jald, a word of Arabic origin, with the emphatic hī, "very".

Lesson 4 of 5.
پروگرام (progrām) — a plan, a programme.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for next, then the Urdu for soon.

You'll want to know: پروگرام.
پروگرام — progrām — "a plan, a programme". From English programme, and the everyday word for a plan: کل کا پروگرام بناتے ہیں۔ — kal kā progrām banāte haiṅ — "Let's make a plan for tomorrow."

The word, taken apart.
پروگرام (progrām) is a loan from English programme.

Lesson 5 of 5.
طے کرنا (tai karnā) — to decide, to settle.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for soon, then the Urdu for a plan.

You'll want to know: طے کرنا.
طے کرنا — tai karnā — "to decide, to settle". ہم نے طے کیا کہ... — ham ne tai kiyā ki — "We decided that..." The past of کرنا is کیا.

The phrase, taken apart.
طے کرنا (tai karnā) joins tai, "settled", and karnā, "to do". tai is Arabic ṭayy, "a folding up; a covering of distance", which reached Urdu through Persian (Lane, root ṭ-w-y); a matter that is folded up is settled. karnā comes from Sanskrit karoti, "does" (CDIAL 2814).
