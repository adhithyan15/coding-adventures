## You'll want to know: गंध
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C480-SPARSH480-03]; assesses=[] -->

**गंध** — *gandh* — "a smell; an odour".

> रसोई से मसालों की गंध आ रही है।
>
> *rasoī se masāloṁ kī gandh ā rahī hai.*
>
> The smell of spices is coming from the kitchen.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**गंध** (*gandh*) is inherited: CDIAL lists Hindi *gandh*, "smell", under Sanskrit *gandhá*, "smell" (CDIAL 4014). It can be pleasant or not; *khuśbū* is only a good smell.
