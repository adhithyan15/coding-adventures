# (म र न अ प ह य त ् ा ी) — eleven, and three kinds of thing

## Warm-up

[PAUSE 3s] Two letters from the book's first script pages. Read **म**, then
**न**, and name each.

## Script: eight letters, one killer, two hangers
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[HI-SCRIPT-RECOG-01, HI-SCRIPT-RECOG-110, HI-SCRIPT-RECOG-02, HI-SCRIPT-RECOG-113, HI-SCRIPT-RECOG-114, HI-SCRIPT-RECOG-115, HI-SCRIPT-RECOG-07, HI-SCRIPT-RECOG-03] -->

They arrived one or two per chapter and no page has gathered them. Sorted by
what kind of thing each one is:

| kind | pieces |
|---|---|
| **consonants** — each carries a built-in *a* | **म** *ma* · **र** *ra* · **न** *na* · **प** *pa* · **ह** *ha* · **य** *ya* · **त** *ta* |
| **a standing vowel** | **अ** *a* — the full-size letter, for when a word opens on the vowel |
| **the killer** | **्** — removes the built-in *a* |
| **hangers** | **ा** *ā* · **ी** *ī* — replace the built-in *a* |

**अ** is the one worth pausing on, because it is the same sound the consonants
already carry. A consonant has *a* inside it; **अ** is what you write when there
is no consonant to put it in.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[HI-SCRIPT-RECOG-05, HI-SCRIPT-RECOG-06, HI-SCRIPT-RECOG-116] -->

[PAUSE 1s] The three marks, cold, and what each does to the letter it touches.

- [YOU READ: **्** — say what it takes away]
- [YOU READ: **ा** — say what it replaces, and with what]
- [YOU READ: **ी** — the same, and say how it differs from the one above]

All three depend on one fact: a bare consonant is not silent. It already has a
vowel, and these either remove it or swap it.

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[HI-SCRIPT-RECOG-05, HI-SCRIPT-RECOG-03, HI-SCRIPT-RECOG-116] -->

[PAUSE 3s] What vowel is inside a bare Devanagari consonant? (**A short *a*.**)
What does **्** do? (**Removes it.**) And when do you need **अ** rather than
relying on that built-in vowel? (**When the word opens on the vowel.**)
