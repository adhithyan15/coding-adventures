## You'll want to know: il sovrano
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C328-IMPERO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**il sovrano** — "the sovereign, the ruler".

> Il sovrano ha lasciato il palazzo.
>
> *The ruler left the palace.*

Also an adjective: *uno stato sovrano*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*sovrano* is inherited from Vulgar Latin *superānus* (EtymDB), built on *super* "above".
