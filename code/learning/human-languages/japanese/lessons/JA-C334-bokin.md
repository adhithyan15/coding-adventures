## You'll want to know: ぼきん
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C334-FUKUSHI334-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ぼきん** — *bokin* — "fund-raising; a collection".

> **たいふうが あったので、えきで ぼきんを しています。** — *taifū ga atta node, eki de bokin o shite imasu* — "There was a typhoon, so people are collecting money at the station."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ぼきん** (*bokin*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *bo*, "recruit" (Middle Chinese *muH*) and *kin*, "gold" (*kim*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
