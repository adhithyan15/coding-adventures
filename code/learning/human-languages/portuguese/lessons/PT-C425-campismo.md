## You'll want to know: o campismo
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C425-DORMIR-05]; assesses=[] -->

**o campismo** — "camping".

> O parque de campismo tem duche e piscina.
>
> *The campsite has showers and a pool.*

*O parque de campismo* — the campsite. In Brazil: *o camping*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*campismo* is built on *campo* 'field' with *-ismo*; *campo* is inherited from Latin *campus* 'field, open plain' (EtymDB).
