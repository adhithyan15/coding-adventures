## You'll want to know: صاعقه
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C475-SAEGHE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**صاعقه** (*sâ'eqe*) — "a lightning strike, a thunderbolt".

> صاعقه به درخت بلند حیاط خورد.
>
> *Sâ'eqe be derakht-e boland-e hayât khord.*
>
> Lightning struck the tall tree in the courtyard.

*Sâ'eqe zad* — lightning struck.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*sâ'eqe* was borrowed from Arabic *ṣāʿiqa*, which EtymDB lists; it is usually glossed 'a thunderbolt'.
