## You'll want to know: しゅっさん
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C286-AKACHAN286-03]; assesses=[] -->

**しゅっさん** — *shussan* — "childbirth; giving birth".

> **あねは らいげつ しゅっさんの よていです。** — *ane wa raigetsu shussan no yotei desu* — "My elder sister is due to give birth next month."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**しゅっさん** (*shussan*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *shutsu*, "exit" (Middle Chinese *tsyhwit*) and *san*, "give birth" (*sreanX*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription). The first is shortened from *shutsu* to *shus-* before *san*.
