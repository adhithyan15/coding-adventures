## You'll want to know: píjiǔ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C102-DRINK102-04]; assesses=[] -->

*píjiǔ* — "beer".

> *wǒ bù hē píjiǔ* — I don't drink beer.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*píjiǔ* is built from pí and jiǔ 'alcoholic drink'. The second is inherited from Middle Chinese *tsjuwX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription); the first is borrowed for its sound from a European word for beer, usually traced to English *beer*.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for asking for what I want.
