Lesson 1 of 6.
सराय (sarāy) — an inn.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for to unload, then the Hindi for to put on board.

You'll want to know: सराय.
सराय — sarāy — "an inn".
पहले मुसाफ़िर सराय में रुकते थे। pahle musāfir sarāy meṁ rukte the. In the old days, travellers used to stop at an inn.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
सराय (sarāy) is usually traced to Persian sarāy, "a large house, a palace", which also named the walled inns built along the old trade roads; many towns still have a lane or a quarter called सराय.

Lesson 2 of 6.
धर्मशाला (dharmśālā) — a rest house (for pilgrims).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for to put on board, then the Hindi for an inn.

You'll want to know: धर्मशाला.
धर्मशाला — dharmśālā — "a rest house (for pilgrims)".
मंदिर के पास एक सस्ती धर्मशाला है। mandir ke pās ek sastī dharmśālā hai. There is a cheap rest house near the temple.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
धर्मशाला (dharmśālā) is a tatsama, Sanskrit dharmaśālā (Monier-Williams), from dharma, "duty, charity", and śālā, "a hall": a house kept for pilgrims and travellers at little or no cost.

Lesson 4 of 6.
ठहरना (ṭhaharnā) — to stop over; to stay (somewhere).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a rest house, then the Hindi for a lift.

You'll want to know: ठहरना.
ठहरना — ṭhaharnā — "to stop over; to stay (somewhere)".
हम दो रात एक होटल में ठहरे। ham do rāt ek hoṭal meṁ ṭhahre. We stayed two nights at a hotel.
Past (he …): वह धर्मशाला में ठहरा, vah dharmśālā meṁ ṭhahrā.

The word, taken apart.
ठहरना (ṭhaharnā) goes back to stabhira, "fixed, firm", a form CDIAL reconstructs (CDIAL 13680), which lists Hindi ṭhaharnā, "to stop". To ṭhaharnā is to stand still in one place for a while.

Lesson 5 of 6.
बिछाना (bichānā) — to spread out (a sheet or a mat); to make up (a bed).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a lift, then the Hindi for to stop over.

You'll want to know: बिछाना.
बिछाना — bichānā — "to spread out (a sheet or a mat); to make up (a bed)".
नौकर ने बिस्तर पर नई चादर बिछाई। naukar ne bistar par naī cādar bichāī. The servant spread a fresh sheet on the bed.
Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने फ़र्श पर दरी बिछाई, usne farś par darī bichāī.

The word, taken apart.
बिछाना (bichānā) goes back to vicchādayati, "spreads out", a form CDIAL reconstructs (CDIAL 11692), which lists Hindi bichānā, "to spread"; बिस्तर बिछाना is to make up a bed on the floor or a cot.
