## You'll want to know: पूर्वजः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C322-PURVAJA322-01]; assesses=[] -->

**पूर्वजः** — *pūrvajaḥ* — "an ancestor".

> मम पूर्वजाः तस्मिन् ग्रामे वसन्ति स्म।
>
> *mama pūrvajāḥ tasmin grāme vasanti sma.*
>
> My ancestors used to live in that village.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पूर्वजः** (*pūrvajaḥ*) is built from *pūrva*, "earlier, former", and *ja*, "born", from the root √jan; MW gives *pūrvaja* as born before, and an ancestor or forefather (MW).

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
