## You'll want to know: رسا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C429-QARIB429-05]; assesses=[] -->

**رسا** — *rasā* — "to dock; to anchor".

> رست السفينة في الميناء بعد الظهر.
>
> *rasat as-safīna fī al-mīnāʾ baʿda aẓ-ẓuhr.*
>
> The ship docked in the port in the afternoon.

Present: يرسو, *yarsū*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**رسا** (*rasā*) is built on the native Arabic root *r-s-w*, "to be firm, fixed" (Lane). Lane gives it of a mountain standing firm and of a ship coming to anchor.
