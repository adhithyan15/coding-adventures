# നാമകരണം (nāmakaraṇaṁ) — a naming ceremony

## You'll want to know: നാമകരണം
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C389-GRIHAM389-02]; assesses=[] -->

**നാമകരണം** — *nāmakaraṇaṁ* — "a naming ceremony".

> കുഞ്ഞിന്റെ നാമകരണം ഇരുപത്തിയെട്ടാം ദിവസം ആയിരുന്നു.
>
> *kuññinṟe nāmakaraṇaṁ irupattiyeṭṭāṁ divasaṁ āyirunnu.*
>
> The baby's naming was on the twenty-eighth day.

Also the naming of anything new, a road or a building.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**നാമകരണം** (*nāmakaraṇaṁ*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *nāmakaraṇa*, "the ceremony of naming after birth" (CDIAL 7066), from *nāman*, "a name", and *karaṇa*, "a making".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
