Punjabi, chapter 262: Fees and Fines.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ਫ਼ੀਸ (fīs) — a fee.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for to set off, then the Punjabi for departing.

You'll want to know: ਫ਼ੀਸ.
ਫ਼ੀਸ — fīs — "a fee".
ਲਾਇਬ੍ਰੇਰੀ ਦੀ ਫ਼ੀਸ ਬਹੁਤ ਘੱਟ ਹੈ। lāibrerī dī fīs bahut ghaṭṭ hai. The library fee is very low.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ਫ਼ੀਸ (fīs) is borrowed from English fees, the plural of fee.

What you've built.
One more word for reading notices and messages.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: fīs]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: fīs, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: lāibrerī dī fīs bahut ghaṭṭ hai. — The library fee is very low.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Punjabi for to set off, then the Punjabi for departing, then say fīs again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does ਫ਼ੀਸ (fīs) mean? ("A fee".) Say it once more.

You'll want to know: ਕਿਰਾਇਆ.
ਕਿਰਾਇਆ — kirāiā — "a fare; rent".
ਸ਼ਹਿਰ ਤੱਕ ਬੱਸ ਦਾ ਕਿਰਾਇਆ ਸਸਤਾ ਹੈ। shahir tak bass dā kirāiā sastā hai. The bus fare to town is cheap.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ਕਿਰਾਇਆ (kirāiā) is a loan, usually traced through Persian to Arabic kirā, "hire".

Lesson 5 of 5.
ਚੁਕਾਉਣਾ (chukāuṇā) — to pay off; to settle a bill.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for a fine, then the Punjabi for a receipt.

You'll want to know: ਚੁਕਾਉਣਾ.
ਚੁਕਾਉਣਾ — chukāuṇā — "to pay off; to settle a bill".
ਮੈਂ ਹਰ ਮਹੀਨੇ ਬੈਂਕ ਦਾ ਕਰਜ਼ਾ ਚੁਕਾਉਂਦਾ ਹਾਂ। maiṁ har mahīne baiṅk dā karzā chukāundā hāṁ. I pay off the bank debt every month.
Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਜੁਰਮਾਨਾ ਚੁਕਾਇਆ, maiṁ jurmānā chukāiā.

The word, taken apart.
ਚੁਕਾਉਣਾ (chukāuṇā) is inherited from a reconstructed cukk, "fall short of, stop"; CDIAL lists Punjabi cukāuṇā, "to finish with, settle", under it (CDIAL 4848).
