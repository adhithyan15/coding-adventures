# ಮೇಲೆ ಅವಲಂಬಿಸಿದೆ (mēle avalambiside) — it depends

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[KA-LEX-C318-AVALAMBISU-01, KA-LEX-C91-ABOUTYOU-04] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "to depend" (*avalambisu*) and "Our town depends on the rain." (*namma ūru maḷeya mēle avalambiside*.)

## You'll want to know: ಅದು … ಮೇಲೆ ಅವಲಂಬಿಸಿದೆ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-GRAMMAR-C318-DEPENDS-01]; assesses=[KA-LEX-C04-NAALE-SIGONA-01, KA-LEX-C20-HAVAMANA-02, KA-GRAMMAR-C317-ARE-CONDITIONAL-01, KA-GRAMMAR-C317-REAL-CONDITION-01, KA-LEX-C02-NIINU-NIIVU-01, KA-LEX-C215-KARANA215-02, KA-LEX-C157-ODU157-01, KA-LEX-C100-FIND-03, KA-LEX-C07-NUMBERS-6-10-01, KA-LEX-C50-LEAVE-04] -->

> **ನೀವು ನಾಳೆ ಬರುತ್ತೀರಾ?**
>
> *nīvu nāḷe baruttīrā?* — "Will you come tomorrow?"

> **ಅದು ಹವಾಮಾನದ ಮೇಲೆ ಅವಲಂಬಿಸಿದೆ. ಮಳೆ ಬಂದರೆ ನಾನು ಬರುವುದಿಲ್ಲ.**
>
> *adu havāmānada mēle avalambiside. maḷe bandare nānu baruvudilla.* — "It depends on the weather. If it rains, I won't come."

**ಬರುತ್ತೀರಾ** (*baruttīrā*, "will you come?"), said with respect; **ಬರುವುದಿಲ್ಲ** (*baruvudilla*, "(I) won't come"). **ಅದು … ಮೇಲೆ ಅವಲಂಬಿಸಿದೆ** is the reply when the honest answer is "maybe": name what it hangs on, then give the condition with **-ಅರೆ**.

Shorter, and very common:

> **ಅದು ನಿಮ್ಮ ಮೇಲೆ ಅವಲಂಬಿಸಿದೆ.**
>
> *adu nimma mēle avalambiside.* — "It's up to you."

Word for word, "it depends on you". Beside it sits **ಪ್ರಕಾರ**, "according to", for what a plan or a rule says: **ವೇಳಾಪಟ್ಟಿಯ ಪ್ರಕಾರ ಬಸ್ ಆರು ಗಂಟೆಗೆ ಹೊರಡುತ್ತದೆ** (*vēḷāpaṭṭiya prakāra bas āru gaṇṭege horaḍuttade*, "according to the timetable, the bus leaves at six"). Where **ಅವಲಂಬಿಸಿದೆ** says "it hangs on", **ಪ್ರಕಾರ** says "it follows".
