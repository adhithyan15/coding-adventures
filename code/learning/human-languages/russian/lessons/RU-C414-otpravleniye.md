## You'll want to know: отправление
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C414-PERRON-04]; assesses=[] -->

**отправление** (*otpravléniye*) — "departure".

> До отправления поезда осталось пять минут.
>
> *Do otpravléniya póyezda ostálos pyat minút.*
>
> There are five minutes left before the train departs.

Neuter; formal, used on boards and in announcements. *Почтовое отправление* — an item of mail.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*отправление* is built on *отправлять* 'to send off' with the ending *-ение*; EtymDB lists it with the sense 'departure'.
