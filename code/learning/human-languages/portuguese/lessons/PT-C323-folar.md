## You'll want to know: o folar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C323-CONSOADA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**o folar** — "the Easter bread".

> A madrinha oferece o folar ao afilhado.
>
> *The godmother gives the Easter bread to her godson.*

A sweet or savoury loaf, sometimes with whole eggs baked in.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

The origin of *folar* is not settled; EtymDB has the word with no source.
