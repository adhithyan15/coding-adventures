## You'll want to know: સામાન
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C203-TRIP203-02]; assesses=[] -->

**સામાન** — *sāmān* — "luggage, belongings".

> **મારો સામાન ભારે હતો.** — *māro sāmān bhāre hato* — "My luggage was heavy."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**સામાન** (*sāmān*) is a loan, usually traced to Persian *sāmān*, "goods, equipment". CDIAL has no entry for it.
