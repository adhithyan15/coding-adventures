Telugu, chapter 128: Chin, Wrist, Elbow, Skin, Blood.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
గడ్డం (gaḍḍaṁ) — the chin; a beard.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for an eyebrow, then the Telugu for a cheek.

You'll want to know: గడ్డం.
గడ్డం — gaḍḍaṁ — "the chin; a beard".

The word, taken apart.
గడ్డం (gaḍḍaṁ) is native Dravidian, one word with Kannada gaḍḍa, "the beard about the chin, the chin" (DEDR 1156). DEDR notes that Tamil keṭṭam, "beard", is a loan from Telugu, and compares Prakrit khaḍḍa, "beard".
