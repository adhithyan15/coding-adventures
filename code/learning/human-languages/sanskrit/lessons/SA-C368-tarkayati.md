## You'll want to know: तर्कयति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C368-SHANKA368-02]; assesses=[] -->

**तर्कयति** — *tarkayati* — "conjectures; guesses".

> यदि दीपः न ज्वलति, तर्हि तर्कयामि यत् सः गतः।
>
> *yadi dīpaḥ na jvalati, tarhi tarkayāmi yat saḥ gataḥ.*
>
> If no lamp is burning, I guess that he has gone.

Past (he …): सः कारणं तर्कितवान्, *saḥ kāraṇaṃ tarkitavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**तर्कयति** (*tarkayati*) is built on *tarka*, "conjecture, reasoning"; MW gives *√tark* "to conjecture, to guess, to suspect, to infer" (MW).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
