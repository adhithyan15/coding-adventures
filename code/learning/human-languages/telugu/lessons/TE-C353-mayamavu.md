# మాయమవు (māyamavu) — to vanish; to disappear

## Warm-up

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Telugu for to step aside, then the Telugu for a disappearance.

## You'll want to know: మాయమవు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C353-ADRSYAM353-02]; assesses=[] -->

**మాయమవు** — *māyamavu* — "to vanish; to disappear".

> నా సైకిలు రాత్రికి రాత్రే మాయమైంది.
>
> *nā saikilu rātriki rātrē māyamaindi.*
>
> My bicycle vanished overnight.

Past (I …): మాయమయ్యాను, *māyamayyānu*.

మాయం, vanished, with అవు, to become.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**మాయమవు** (*māyamavu*) is built on Sanskrit *māyā*, "supernatural power; illusion" (CDIAL 10060), with the Telugu verb *avu*, "to become": to go as if by magic.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
