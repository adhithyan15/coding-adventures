## You'll want to know: प्रथा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C367-PRATHA367-01]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रथा** — *prathā* — "a practice; a custom".

> सणाला नवे कपडे घालण्याची प्रथा आहे.
>
> *saṇālā nave kapḍe ghālaṇyācī prathā āhe.*
>
> There is a custom of wearing new clothes at festivals.

Feminine.

X ची प्रथा आहे, it is the custom to X.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रथा** (*prathā*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *prathā*, "spreading; fame" (Monier-Williams). Marathi uses it for a practice that has spread.
