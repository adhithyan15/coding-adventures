## Guided Practice: the distant band — every noun in the book, picked out one at a time
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[BN-GRAMMAR-C20-CLASSIFIER-01, BN-GRAMMAR-C20-EKTA-01, BN-LEX-C15-JAMA-01, BN-LEX-C15-KAPOR-01, BN-LEX-C14-LAL-01, BN-LEX-C14-KALO-01, BN-LEX-C14-NIL-01, BN-LEX-C14-SHADA-01, BN-LEX-C10-CHA-01, BN-LEX-C10-JOL-01, BN-LEX-C10-DUDH-01, BN-LEX-C10-BHAT-01, BN-LEX-C11-BHAI-01, BN-LEX-C11-BON-01, BN-LEX-C11-BONDHU-01, BN-LEX-C11-PORIBAR-01, BN-LEX-C12-CHOKH-01, BN-LEX-C12-NAK-01, BN-LEX-C12-MUKH-01] -->

[PAUSE 4s] This is what the chapter bought. Chapters ten to fifteen taught a
pantry, a family, a face and a wardrobe, and not one of those nouns could be
pointed at as **the** one or counted as **a** one. Every one of them can now.
Take them cold, from memory.

- [YOU SAY: the four things you drink and eat, each as **the** one — *chāṭā*, *jôlṭā*, *dudhṭā*, *bhātṭā*]
- [YOU SAY: the same four as **a** one — *ekṭā chā*, *ekṭā jôl*, *ekṭā dudh*, *ekṭā bhāt*]
- [YOU SAY: the family, respectfully — *bhāiṭi*, *bônṭi*, *bôndhuṭi*]
- [YOU SAY: the wardrobe and its colours — *lāl jāmāṭā*, *kālo kāpôṛṭā*, *nīl jāmāṭā*, *shādā jāmāṭā*]
- [YOU SAY: the face, one part at a time — *ekṭā chokh, ārekṭā chokh*, then *nākṭā*, *mukhṭā*]
- [YOU SAY: the whole family in one word — *pôribārṭā*]

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[BN-GRAMMAR-C20-CLASSIFIER-01, BN-GRAMMAR-C20-EKTA-01, BN-LEX-C20-AREKTA-01, BN-SCRIPT-TTA-01, BN-LEX-C17-AR-01, BN-LEX-C19-JOKHON-01] -->

[PAUSE 4s] Does Bengali have a word for **the**? (**No** — it has this ending.)
When a number stands in front, which word carries the ending? (**The number**.)
Which form is the careful one, for people? (**টি**.) And which letter had to be
taught before any of this chapter could be written down? (**ট**.)
