Marathi, chapter 251: To try, To keep going, To bring to a stop, To move, To heat.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
प्रयत्न करणे (prayatna karṇe) — to try.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for art, then the Marathi for a team.

You'll want to know: प्रयत्न करणे.
प्रयत्न करणे — prayatna karṇe — "to try".

The word, taken apart.
प्रयत्न is Sanskrit prayatna, "effort", a tatsama. करणे comes from Sanskrit karoti, "does" (CDIAL 2814).

Lesson 2 of 5.
चालू ठेवणे (cālū ṭhevṇe) — to keep going, to continue.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a team, then the Marathi for to try.

You'll want to know: चालू ठेवणे.
चालू ठेवणे — cālū ṭhevṇe — "to keep going, to continue".

The word, taken apart.
चालू, "going on", is built on cālṇẽ, "to move, walk", which comes from a reconstructed calyati, "goes" (CDIAL 4721). The origin of ठेवणे, "to keep", is not settled.
