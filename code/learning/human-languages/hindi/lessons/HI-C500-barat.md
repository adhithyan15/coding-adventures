## You'll want to know: बारात
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C500-BARAT500-04]; assesses=[] -->

**बारात** — *bārāt* — "the groom's wedding procession".

> बारात में सब लोग नाच रहे थे।
>
> *bārāt meṁ sab log nāc rahe the.*
>
> Everyone in the wedding procession was dancing.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**बारात** (*bārāt*) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Hindi *barāt*, "marriage procession", under Sanskrit *varayātrā*, built on *vara*, "bridegroom", and *yātrā*, "journey" (CDIAL 11322).
