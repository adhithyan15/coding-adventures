## You'll want to know: استقبال
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C401-ISTIQBAL401-03]; assesses=[] -->

**استقبال** — *istiqbāl* — "a welcome; a reception".

> دوستوں نے پھولوں سے ہمارا استقبال کیا۔
>
> *dostoṅ ne phūloṅ se hamārā istiqbāl kiyā.*
>
> Our friends welcomed us with flowers.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**استقبال** (*istiqbāl*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *istiqbāl*, "going out to meet, receiving"; **استقبال کرنا** is to welcome someone.
