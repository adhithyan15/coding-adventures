## You'll want to know: ભરોસો
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C541-SHAPATH541-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ભરોસો** — *bharoso* — "confidence (trust in someone)".

> જો તમે વાયદો પાળશો તો લોકો તમારા પર ભરોસો રાખશે.
>
> *jo tame vāydo pāḷśo to loko tamārā par bharoso rākhśe.*
>
> If you keep your word, people will trust you.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ભરોસો** (*bharoso*) goes back to a reconstructed *\*bharavaśya*, "dependence on support", and CDIAL lists the Gujarati word, "confidence" (CDIAL 9398). One places it on someone: **તમારા પર ભરોસો**.
