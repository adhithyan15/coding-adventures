## You'll want to know: भिणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C475-SAHAS475-05]; assesses=[] -->

**भिणे** — *bhiṇe* — "to be scared of; to fear".

> माझी बहीण कुत्र्यांना भिते.
>
> *mājhī bahīṇ kutryānnā bhite.*
>
> My sister is afraid of dogs.

Past (he …): तो भ्याला, *to bhyālā*.

What is feared takes -ला: कुत्र्याला भिणे. The past is irregular: तो भ्याला.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**भिणे** (*bhiṇe*) is a tadbhava, inherited from Sanskrit *bibheti*, "fears"; CDIAL lists Marathi *bhiṇẽ*, "to fear" (CDIAL 9241).
