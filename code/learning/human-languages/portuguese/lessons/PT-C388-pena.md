## You'll want to know: a pena
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C388-PENA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**a pena** — "the penalty, the sentence served; the pity; the feather".

> Ele cumpriu uma pena de dois anos.
>
> *He served a two-year sentence.*

*Que pena!* — what a pity; *vale a pena* — it is worth it. *Uma pena* is also a feather.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*pena* "penalty, pity" is inherited from Latin *poena* "penalty, punishment" (EtymDB, Lewis & Short), from Greek *poinḗ* "blood money, fine"; *pena* "feather" is a separate word, from Latin *penna*.
