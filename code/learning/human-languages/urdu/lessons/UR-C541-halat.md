## You'll want to know: حالات
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C541-SURAT541-02]; assesses=[] -->

**حالات** — *hālāt* — "circumstances".

> اگر حالات بدلے تو ہم اپنا پروگرام بدلیں گے۔
>
> *agar hālāt badle to ham apnā progrām badleṅ ge.*
>
> If circumstances change, we will change our plans.

Masculine, used in the plural.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**حالات** (*hālāt*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *ḥālāt*, the plural of *ḥāl*, "a state, a condition".
