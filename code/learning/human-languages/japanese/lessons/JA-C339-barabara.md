## You'll want to know: ばらばら
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C339-BUNBETSU339-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ばらばら** — *barabara* — "scattered; in pieces".

> **ふるいので、ほんが ばらばらです。** — *furui node, hon ga barabara desu* — "It is old, so the book is falling apart."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ばらばら** (*barabara*) is a native mimetic word, one of the Japanese words that picture a manner or a feeling in sound; JMdict marks it as mimetic. It is inherited, not borrowed, and its older origin is not settled (JMdict).
