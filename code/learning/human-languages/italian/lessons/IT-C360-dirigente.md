## You'll want to know: il dirigente
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C360-REPARTO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**il dirigente** — "the manager, the executive".

> Il dirigente vuole parlare con tutto il gruppo.
>
> *The manager wants to talk to the whole team.*

Higher than *il capo* of a small team. *Un dirigente scolastico* is a head teacher.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*dirigente* is the present participle of *dirigere* "to direct", from Latin *dīrigere* "to set straight, to guide" (Lewis & Short), built on *regere* "to rule, to steer".
