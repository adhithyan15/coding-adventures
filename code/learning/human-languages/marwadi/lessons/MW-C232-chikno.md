## You'll want to know: चिकणो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C232-EARTH232-03]; assesses=[] -->

**चिकणो** — *chikṇo* — "smooth; slippery".

> भाटो चिकणो है।
>
> *bhāṭo chikṇo hai.*
>
> The stone is smooth.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**चिकणो** (*chikṇo*) is inherited from Sanskrit *cikkaṇa*, "slippery, unctuous"; CDIAL lists Hindi *ciknā*, "oily, smooth", and a Gujarati cousin. CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry (CDIAL 4782).
