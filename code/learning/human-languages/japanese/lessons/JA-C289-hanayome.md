## You'll want to know: はなよめ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C289-KEKKON289-01]; assesses=[] -->

**はなよめ** — *hanayome* — "a bride".

> **はなよめは しろい きものを きていました。** — *hanayome wa shiroi kimono o kite imashita* — "The bride wore a white kimono."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**はなよめ** (*hanayome*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, built from *hana*, "flower", and *yome*, "bride" (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
