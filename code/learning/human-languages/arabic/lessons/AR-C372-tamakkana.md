## You'll want to know: تمكن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C372-AQABA372-05]; assesses=[] -->

**تمكن** — *tamakkana* — "to manage to".

> تمكن الطالب من حل المسألة.
>
> *tamakkana aṭ-ṭālib min ḥall al-masʾala.*
>
> The student managed to solve the problem.

Present: يتمكن, *yatamakkan*.

It takes من: تمكن من ….

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**تمكن** (*tamakkana*) is built on the native Arabic root *m-k-n*, "to be firm, to have power" (Lane). It is Form V, to become firmly able: to manage to do a thing.
