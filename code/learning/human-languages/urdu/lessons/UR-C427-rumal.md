## You'll want to know: رومال
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C427-POSHAK427-04]; assesses=[] -->

**رومال** — *rūmāl* — "a handkerchief".

> میری جیب میں ایک رومال ہے۔
>
> *merī jeb meṅ ek rūmāl hai.*
>
> There's a handkerchief in my pocket.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**رومال** (*rūmāl*) is built from Persian *rū*, "the face", and *māl*, "wiping": it is usually traced to Persian *rūmāl*.
