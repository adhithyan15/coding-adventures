## You'll want to know: supercilium, superciliī
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C515-VULT-04]; assesses=[] -->

**supercilium, superciliī** — "eyebrow".

> Senex supercilia alba habet.
>
> *The old man has white eyebrows.*

Neuter, second declension. Also a frown, and so pride: *supercilium* — haughtiness. English *supercilious*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**supercilium** is built on *super* "above" and *cilium* "eyelid" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short). English *supercilious* comes from it, from a raised eyebrow.
