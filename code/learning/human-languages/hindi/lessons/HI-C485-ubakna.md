## You'll want to know: उबकना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C485-CHAKKAR485-04]; assesses=[] -->

**उबकना** — *ubaknā* — "to retch; to throw up".

> बासी दूध पीकर बच्चा उबक गया।
>
> *bāsī dūdh pīkar baccā ubak gayā.*
>
> The child threw up after drinking stale milk.

Past (he …): वह उबक गया, *vah ubak gayā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**उबकना** (*ubaknā*) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Hindi *ubaknā*, "to vomit", under *\*ubbakka*, a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 2337). *ulṭī*, "vomiting", is the noun most people use.
