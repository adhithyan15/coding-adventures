# અવગણના (avgaṇnā) — disregard; being ignored

## You'll want to know: અવગણના
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C456-DUBHA456-04]; assesses=[] -->

**અવગણના** — *avgaṇnā* — "disregard; being ignored".

> માતા અને પિતાની અવગણના ન કરવી જોઈએ.
>
> *mātā ane pitānī avgaṇnā na karvī joīe.*
>
> One should not neglect one's mother and father.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**અવગણના** (*avgaṇnā*) is a tatsama, close to Sanskrit *avagaṇana*, "disregard, contempt", as Monier-Williams records it, with the feminine *-ā*.
