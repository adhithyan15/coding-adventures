# HL70 — Marwadi A1 joining and choice

## Status

Proposed for issue #17694 under the Marwadi bridge #12208 and the
Indian-language C2 programme #13295.

## Problem

After the exact direct-owner reconciliation in #17689, Marwadi's partial A1
inventory covers 98 of 197 named points. Its joining category remains 0/11:
the course has hundreds of nouns and short statements but has never directly
taught a learner to join two familiar things, offer alternatives, contrast two
statements, or connect a reason to a statement.

This tranche uses the Government of India's *Linguistic Survey of India —
Rajasthan, Part I* as its grammatical anchor. The survey identifies /Ar/ as a
Marwari conjunctive particle with "and / or" value, gives a noun-coordination
example, and identifies /pAN/ as "yet/but" with clause examples. The spelling
**अर** follows the track's central-Marwari Devanagari convention; **पण** is
also independently listed as a conjunction meaning "but" in the
Marwari–Urdu–English dictionary already cited by the track. The existing
chapter-117 atom supplies **क्यूंकै** (*kyū̃kai*, because).

## Required learner experience

Add one chapter-addressable four-lesson sequence after chapter 150. It must:

- teach **अर** first as the link between two familiar nouns;
- make the source-attested alternative value explicit in a short practical
  choice, and let the learner answer that choice with one familiar item;
- teach **पण** between two short, already-buildable copular statements;
- reuse, rather than reintroduce, **क्यूंकै** in a directly taught two-clause
  reason frame;
- reuse only already introduced nouns, adjectives, and the copula inside the
  modelled phrases;
- keep each lesson at or below five minutes and move from recognition through
  substitution to one small choice-and-reason exchange; and
- introduce no idiom, additional sense, or culture claim.

## Inventory evidence

Wire exactly five points to exact introduced atoms:

- `MW-A1-CJ-01` requires the new **अर** lexical atom and its noun-coordination
  frame;
- `MW-A1-CJ-03` requires that lexical atom and the separately taught
  alternative-choice frame;
- `MW-A1-CJ-04` requires the new **पण** lexical atom and contrast frame;
- `MW-A1-CJ-06` requires the existing **क्यूंकै** atom plus a new causal-clause
  frame; and
- `MW-A1-F3-02` requires the alternative-choice frame.

Coverage must move from 98/197 to 103/197, with unmapped points moving from 99
to 94 and the joining category moving from 0/11 to 4/11. No unrelated
subordination, preference-statement, or tense competence may be claimed by
implication.
