## You'll want to know: ごうけい
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C362-HIYOU362-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ごうけい** — *gōkei* — "the total; the sum".

> **ごうけいが ちがったら、おしえて ください。** — *gōkei ga chigattara, oshiete kudasai* — "If the total is wrong, please tell me."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ごうけい** (*gōkei*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *gō*, "join" (Middle Chinese *hop*) and *kei*, "plan" (*kejH*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
