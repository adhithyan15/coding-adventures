## You'll want to know: জরিপ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C355-NOMUNA355-03]; assesses=[] -->

**জরিপ** — *jôrip* — "a survey".

> আমরা পাড়ায় একটা জরিপ করলাম।
>
> *āmrā pāṛāy ekṭā jôrip kôrlām.*
>
> We carried out a survey in the neighbourhood.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**জরিপ** (*jôrip*) is usually traced to Arabic *jarīb*, a measure of land, through Persian; it first meant a land survey and then any survey.
