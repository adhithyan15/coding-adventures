Malayalam, chapter 499: In the Forest.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
വനം (vanaṁ) — a forest.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for a shark, then the Malayalam for to accumulate.

You'll want to know: വനം.
വനം — vanaṁ — "a forest".
ആ വനത്തിൽ ധാരാളം ആനകൾ ഉണ്ട്. ā vanattil dhārāḷaṁ ānakaḷ uṇṭŭ. There are many elephants in that forest.
A formal word; in speech people say കാട്. The forest department is the വനംവകുപ്പ്.

The word, taken apart.
വനം (vanaṁ) is borrowed from Sanskrit vana, "a forest, a wood" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 2 of 5.
വന്യജീവി (vanyajīvi) — a wild animal; wildlife.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for to accumulate, then the Malayalam for a forest.

You'll want to know: വന്യജീവി.
വന്യജീവി — vanyajīvi — "a wild animal; wildlife".
വനത്തിൽ വന്യജീവികളെ കാണാൻ ഞങ്ങൾ രാവിലെ പോയി. vanattil vanyajīvikaḷe kāṇān ñaṅṅaḷ rāvile pōyi. We went in the morning to see wild animals in the forest.
Plural: വന്യജീവികൾ, vanyajīvikaḷ.
വന്യ, wild, of the forest, plus ജീവി, a living thing.

The word, taken apart.
വന്യജീവി (vanyajīvi) is a Malayalam compound built from two Sanskrit words, vanya, "growing or living in a forest, wild", and jīvin, "living" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 3 of 5.
സങ്കേതം (saṅkētaṁ) — a sanctuary; a refuge.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for a forest, then the Malayalam for a wild animal.

You'll want to know: സങ്കേതം.
സങ്കേതം — saṅkētaṁ — "a sanctuary; a refuge".
ആ പക്ഷിസങ്കേതത്തിൽ പല തരം പക്ഷികൾ ഉണ്ട്. ā pakṣisaṅkētattil pala taraṁ pakṣikaḷ uṇṭŭ. That bird sanctuary has many kinds of birds.
A protected place: a bird sanctuary is a പക്ഷിസങ്കേതം; a wildlife sanctuary is a വന്യജീവിസങ്കേതം.

The word, taken apart.
സങ്കേതം (saṅkētaṁ) is borrowed from Sanskrit saṅketa, "an agreed sign; an appointed place" (Monier-Williams); in Malayalam it came to mean a place set aside.

Lesson 4 of 5.
കാട്ടാന (kāṭṭāna) — a wild elephant.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for a wild animal, then the Malayalam for a sanctuary.

You'll want to know: കാട്ടാന.
കാട്ടാന — kāṭṭāna — "a wild elephant".
രാത്രി ഒരു കാട്ടാന വയലിൽ ഇറങ്ങി. rātri oru kāṭṭāna vayalil iṟaṅṅi. At night a wild elephant came down into the fields.
Plural: കാട്ടാനകൾ, kāṭṭānakaḷ.
കാട്, the forest, plus ആന: a forest elephant, not a temple one.

The word, taken apart.
കാട്ടാന (kāṭṭāna) is a native Dravidian compound of കാട്, a forest (DEDR 1438), and ആന, an elephant (DEDR 5161).
