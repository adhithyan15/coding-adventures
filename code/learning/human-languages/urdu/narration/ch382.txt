Urdu, chapter 382: Prizes and Rewards.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
انعام (inām) — a prize; a reward.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a marvel, then the Urdu for well done.

You'll want to know: انعام.
انعام — inām — "a prize; a reward".
اگر تم جیتے تو تمہیں انعام ملے گا۔ agar tum jīte to tumheṅ inām mile gā. If you win, you will get a prize.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
انعام (inām) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic inʿām, "a bestowing of favour".

What you've built.
One more word for saying what happens if.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

اگر اس نے دوڑ جیتی تو اسے تمغہ ملے گا۔ agar us ne dauṛ jītī to use tamghā mile gā. If she wins the race, she will get a medal.

Lesson 5 of 5.
سنوارنا (sanvārnā) — to tidy; to make (something) look its best.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a medal, then the Urdu for deserving.

You'll want to know: سنوارنا.
سنوارنا — sanvārnā — "to tidy; to make (something) look its best".
اگر تم نے کمرہ سنوارا تو امی خوش ہوں گی۔ agar tum ne kamrā sanvārā to ammī khush hoṅ gī. If you tidy the room, your mother will be pleased.
Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے بال سنوارے, us ne bāl sanvāre.

The word, taken apart.
سنوارنا (sanvārnā) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin sãwārnā, "to arrange, to deck", under Sanskrit saṃvārayati (CDIAL 13021).
