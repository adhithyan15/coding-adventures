## You'll want to know: шарик
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C314-SALYUT-05]; assesses=[] -->

**шарик** (*shárik*) — "a balloon; a little ball".

> Мальчик держал красный шарик.
>
> *Málchik derzhál krásnyy shárik.*
>
> The boy was holding a red balloon.

A diminutive of *шар* "a ball, a sphere"; *воздушный шарик* — a balloon.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*шарик* is a diminutive of *шар* "a ball", whose origin is not settled; EtymDB lists no parent.
