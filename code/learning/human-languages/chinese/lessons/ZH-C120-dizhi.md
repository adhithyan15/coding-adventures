## You'll want to know: dìzhǐ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C120-TAKEAWAY120-03]; assesses=[] -->

*dìzhǐ* — "address".

> *nǐ jiā de dìzhǐ shì shénme* — What's your home address?

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*dìzhǐ* is built from dì 'ground' and zhǐ 'site', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *dijH* and *tsyiX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription); the dictionary glosses the second as the foundation of a building.
