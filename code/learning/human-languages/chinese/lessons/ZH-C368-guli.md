## You'll want to know: gǔlì
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C368-KEEPGOING368-04]; assesses=[] -->

*gǔlì* — "to encourage".

> *lǎoshī chángcháng gǔlì wǒmen* — The teacher often encourages us.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*gǔlì* is built from *gǔ* 'a drum' and *lì*, both inherited from Middle Chinese, *kuX* and *ljejH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first, quoting an older dictionary, 'to beat a drum', and the second 'to urge on'.
