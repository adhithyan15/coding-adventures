## You'll want to know: ചരമം
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C384-JANANAM384-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ചരമം** — *caramaṁ* — "an obituary; a death".

> അച്ഛൻ എന്നും രാവിലെ പത്രത്തിലെ ചരമം വായിക്കും.
>
> *acchan ennuṁ rāvile patrattile caramaṁ vāyikkuṁ.*
>
> Every morning father reads the obituaries in the paper.

Literally the last: a formal word for a death, and for the death notices in a newspaper.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ചരമം** (*caramaṁ*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *carama*, "last, ultimate, final" (Monier-Williams): the last event of a life.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
