# ಹಾಗಾಗಿ (hāgāgi) — so

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[KA-GRAMMAR-C311-THINK-THAT-01, KA-LEX-C115-WHY115-03, KA-LEX-C115-WHY115-02] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "I think this book is good". (*ī pustaka cennāgide anta nanage anisuttade*.) Then "that is why" and "therefore". (*adakkē*, *ādudarinda*.)

## You'll want to know: ಹಾಗಾಗಿ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-GRAMMAR-C311-HAGAGI-01]; assesses=[KA-LEX-C49-REPLY-05, KA-LEX-C284-KALA284-03, KA-LEX-C63-HEALTH-03, KA-LEX-C92-WORK-01, KA-GRAMMAR-C310-CONJUNCTIVE-01] -->

> **ನಿನ್ನೆ ಮಳೆ ಬಂತು. ಹಾಗಾಗಿ ನಾನು ಮನೆಯಲ್ಲೇ ಇದ್ದೆ.**
>
> *ninne maḷe bantu. hāgāgi nānu maneyallē idde.* — "Yesterday it rained. So I stayed at home."

The reason comes first, then **ಹಾಗಾಗಿ**, then the result. Taken apart, it is **ಹಾಗೆ**, "that way", and **ಆಗಿ**, the **-ಇ** form of **ಆಗು**: "that having happened". **ಮನೆಯಲ್ಲೇ** (*maneyallē*, "at home, nowhere else") is **ಮನೆಯಲ್ಲಿ**, "at home", with **-ಏ**, which leans on the word: that very place.

> **ನನಗೆ ಜ್ವರ ಇತ್ತು. ಹಾಗಾಗಿ ನಾನು ಶಾಲೆಗೆ ಹೋಗಲಿಲ್ಲ.**
>
> *nanage jvara ittu. hāgāgi nānu śālege hōgalilla.* — "I had a fever. So I did not go to school."

## You'll want to know: three words for 'so'
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-GRAMMAR-C311-SO-WORDS-01]; assesses=[KA-LEX-C115-WHY115-03, KA-LEX-C115-WHY115-02, KA-LEX-C100-FIND-03] -->

- **ಹಾಗಾಗಿ** — the everyday "so", in talk and in writing
- **ಅದಕ್ಕೇ** — "that is why", with weight on the reason: "that is exactly why"
- **ಆದುದರಿಂದ** — "therefore", in careful writing and in speeches

> **ಬಸ್ ಬರಲಿಲ್ಲ. ಅದಕ್ಕೇ ನಾನು ತಡವಾಗಿ ಬಂದೆ.**
>
> *bas baralilla. adakkē nānu taḍavāgi bande.* — "The bus didn't come. That's why I came late."

**ತಡವಾಗಿ** (*taḍavāgi*, "late"). **ಆದುದರಿಂದ** is built from **ಆಗು** as well: "from what happened". All three stand at the start of the result, never in front of the reason.

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[KA-GRAMMAR-C311-HAGAGI-01, KA-GRAMMAR-C311-SO-WORDS-01] -->
