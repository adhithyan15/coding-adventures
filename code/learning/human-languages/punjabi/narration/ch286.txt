Punjabi, chapter 286: Rain on the Way.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ਝੜੀ (jhaṛī) — a spell of steady rain.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for to get tired, then the Punjabi for dawn.

You'll want to know: ਝੜੀ.
ਝੜੀ — jhaṛī — "a spell of steady rain".
ਬਰਸਾਤ ਵਿੱਚ ਲੰਮੀ ਝੜੀ ਹੁੰਦੀ ਹੈ। barsāt vich lammī jhaṛī hundī hai. In the rainy season there is a long spell of steady rain.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ਝੜੀ (jhaṛī) is inherited from a reconstructed jhaḍī, "rainstorm"; CDIAL lists Punjabi jhaṛī, "continued rain", under it (CDIAL 5329).

Lesson 4 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for to thunder, then the Punjabi for to pour down.

You'll want to know: ਝੱਖੜ.
ਝੱਖੜ — jhakkhaṛ — "a squall; a gale".
ਝੱਖੜ ਨਾਲ ਪੁਰਾਣਾ ਰੁੱਖ ਡਿੱਗੇਗਾ। jhakkhaṛ nāl purāṇā rukkh ḍiggegā. The old tree will fall in the squall.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ਝੱਖੜ (jhakkhaṛ) is inherited from a reconstructed jhakk, "sudden movement or blast"; CDIAL lists Punjabi jhakkhaṛ, "squall", under it (CDIAL 5316).
