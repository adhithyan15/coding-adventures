## You'll want to know: قطرة
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C511-JURA511-03]; assesses=[] -->

**قطرة** — *qaṭra* — "a droplet; a drop".

> وضعت الممرضة قطرة في عيني.
>
> *waḍaʿat al-mumarriḍa qaṭra fī ʿaynī.*
>
> The nurse put a drop in my eye.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**قطرة** (*qaṭra*) is built on the native Arabic root *q-ṭ-r*, "to drip, to fall in drops" (Lane). Qiṭār, a train, which you know, is from the same root: a line of camels following one another, like drops.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
