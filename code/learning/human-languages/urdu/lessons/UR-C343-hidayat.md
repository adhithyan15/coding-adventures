## You'll want to know: ہدایت
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C343-MAHKAMA343-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ہدایت** — *hidāyat* — "an instruction; guidance".

> افسر نے ہمیں ہدایت دی۔
>
> *afsar ne hameṅ hidāyat dī.*
>
> The officer gave us instructions.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ہدایت** (*hidāyat*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *hidāya*, "guidance".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
