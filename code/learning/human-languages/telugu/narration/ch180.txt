ప్రధాన (pradhāna) is Sanskrit pradhāna, "chief thing", taken over whole as a learned loan, a tatsama (CDIAL 8671).

You'll want to know: మొత్తం.
మొత్తం — mottaṁ — "total, whole; the total".

The word, taken apart.
మొత్తం (mottaṁ) is native Dravidian, one word with Tamil mottam and Malayalam mottam (DEDR 5119). DEDR lists Telugu mottamu, "total, sum, whole".

Lesson 4 of 5.
గులాబీ రంగు (gulābī raṅgu) — pink.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for total, then the Telugu for every.

You'll want to know: గులాబీ రంగు.
గులాబీ రంగు — gulābī raṅgu — "pink".

The word, taken apart.
గులాబీ రంగు (gulābī raṅgu) pairs Persian gulābī, "rose-coloured", from gulāb, "rose", with raṅgu, "colour", from Sanskrit raṅga, "dye, colour" (CDIAL 10560).
