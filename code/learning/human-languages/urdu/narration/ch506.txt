Urdu, chapter 506: Scrubbing and Rinsing.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
صفائی (safāī) — cleaning; cleanliness.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for to tear (something), then the Urdu for to have (something) put away.

You'll want to know: صفائی.
صفائی — safāī — "cleaning; cleanliness".
ہم ہر اتوار کو گھر کی صفائی کرتے ہیں۔ ham har itvār ko ghar kī safāī karte haiṅ. We clean the house every Sunday.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
صفائی (safāī) is built on sāf, "clean", which is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic ṣāfī, "pure, clear", with the noun ending -āī.

Lesson 2 of 5.
مانجھنا (māṅjhnā) — to scour (pots).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for to have (something) put away, then the Urdu for cleaning.

You'll want to know: مانجھنا.
مانجھنا — māṅjhnā — "to scour (pots)".
اس نے راکھ سے برتن مانجھے۔ us ne rākh se bartan māṅjhe. She scoured the pots with ash.
Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے کڑاہی مانجھی, us ne kaṛāhī māṅjhī.

The word, taken apart.
مانجھنا (māṅjhnā) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin mā̃jnā, "to rub, to clean, to polish", under Sanskrit mārjati, "wipes, cleans" (CDIAL 10080).

چپکانا (chipkānā) — to stick (something) on; to glue.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for cleaning, then the Urdu for to scour (pots).

You'll want to know: چپکانا.
چپکانا — chipkānā — "to stick (something) on; to glue".
میں نے لفافے پر ٹکٹ چپکایا۔ maiṅ ne lifāfe par ṭikaṭ chipkāyā. I stuck a stamp on the envelope.
Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے دیوار پر اشتہار چپکایا, us ne dīvār par ishtihār chipkāyā.

The word, taken apart.
چپکانا (chipkānā) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousins cep, "gummy matter", and cepnā, "to stick on", under cippa, "gummy matter" (CDIAL 4819).

Lesson 4 of 5.
دھلنا (dhulnā) — to be washed; to come clean.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for to scour (pots), then the Urdu for to stick (something) on.

You'll want to know: دھلنا.
دھلنا — dhulnā — "to be washed; to come clean".
یہ چادر جلدی دھل گئی۔ yih chādar jaldī dhul gaī. This sheet washed clean quickly.
Past (it …): وہ چادر دھل گئی, voh chādar dhul gaī.

The word, taken apart.
دھلنا (dhulnā) is the intransitive partner of dhonā, "to wash", which CDIAL files under dhauvati, "washes", a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 6886).
