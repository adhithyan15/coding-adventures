Latin, chapter 376: Work and Workers.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
opus, operis — a work, a task; need (opus est).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Latin for unexpected, then the Latin for a movement.

You'll want to know: opus, operis.
opus, operis — "a work, a task; need (opus est)".
Mihi pecūniā opus est. I need money.
opus, operis n. Opus est + dative of the person and ablative of the thing: mihi pecūniā opus est, I need money. English operate comes from its family, and opera comes through Italian from the related opera, effort.

The word, taken apart.
opus is inherited from a Proto-Indo-European noun built on a root meaning "to work, to produce" (EtymDB), with ops "power, means" as a cousin (EtymDB; Lewis & Short). English operate comes from its family, and opera from the related opera "effort".

Lesson 2 of 5.
opifex, opificis — a craftsman, a worker.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Latin for a movement, then the Latin for a work.

You'll want to know: opifex, opificis.
opifex, opificis — "a craftsman, a worker".
Opifex mēnsam novam fēcit. The craftsman made a new table.
opifex, opificis m. and f. Built from opus, work, and faciō, to make: a work-maker.

The word, taken apart.
opifex is built from opus "work" and faciō "to make" (Lewis & Short; EtymDB puts the related ops "power, means" in place of opus), with the ending -fex of a maker.

Lesson 4 of 5.
operārius, operāriī — a labourer, a workman.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Latin for a craftsman, then the Latin for an artist.

You'll want to know: operārius, operāriī.
operārius, operāriī — "a labourer, a workman".
Operāriī tōtum diem in agrō labōrant. The labourers work all day in the field.
operārius, operāriī m., a man who works with his hands for a wage, built on opera, work, effort.

The word, taken apart.
operārius is built on opera "work, effort" (Lewis & Short), a word of the same family as opus, operis "work", with the ending -ārius of a person busy with something.
