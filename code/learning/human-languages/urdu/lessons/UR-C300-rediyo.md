## You'll want to know: ریڈیو
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C300-KHABAR300-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ریڈیو** — *reḍiyo* — "a radio".

> ریڈیو پر گاؤں کی خبر آئی۔
>
> *reḍiyo par gāoṅ kī khabar āī.*
>
> The news about the village came on the radio.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ریڈیو** (*reḍiyo*) is borrowed from English *radio*. News comes **ریڈیو پر**, on the radio.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
