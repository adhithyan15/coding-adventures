## You'll want to know: cabecear
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C509-BALIZA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**cabecear** — "to head (the ball)".

> O defesa cabeceou a bola para fora.
>
> *The defender headed the ball out.*

Built on *a cabeça*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*cabecear* is built on *cabeça* "head", which goes back to a Vulgar Latin form of Latin *caput* "head" (Lewis & Short).
