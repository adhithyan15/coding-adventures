## You'll want to know: बाष्पः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C450-VISADA450-04]; assesses=[] -->

**बाष्पः** — *bāṣpaḥ* — "tears".

> बालिकायाः मुखे बाष्पः अस्ति।
>
> *bālikāyāḥ mukhe bāṣpaḥ asti.*
>
> There are tears on the girl's face.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**बाष्पः** (*bāṣpaḥ*) MW records *bāṣpa*, also written *vāṣpa*, "a tear, tears" and "steam, vapour"; it lives on as Hindi *bhāp*, "steam" (CDIAL 9223); MW gives no derivation, so its origin is uncertain (MW).
