## You'll want to know: bâiller
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C486-NUITS-04]; assesses=[] -->

**bâiller** — "to yawn".

> Les élèves bâillent pendant le cours de l'après-midi.
>
> *The pupils yawn during the afternoon lesson.*

Do not mix it up with *bailler*, an old word for giving.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*bâiller* is inherited from Old French *baaillier*, from Vulgar Latin *bataclāre* (EtymDB), built on *batāre* "to gape".
