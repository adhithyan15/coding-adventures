# गँवाना (gãvānā) — to waste, to throw away (time, a chance)

## You'll want to know: गँवाना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C381-KASH381-04]; assesses=[] -->

**गँवाना** — *gãvānā* — "to waste, to throw away (time, a chance)".

> अगर तुम खेल में समय गँवाओगे तो काम पूरा नहीं होगा।
>
> *agar tum khel meṁ samay gãvāoge to kām pūrā nahīṃ hogā.*
>
> If you waste time playing, the work won't get done.

Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने पूरा दिन गँवाया, *usne pūrā din gãvāyā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**गँवाना** (*gãvānā*) is a tadbhava, inherited from Sanskrit *gamayati*, "makes go" (CDIAL 4028).
