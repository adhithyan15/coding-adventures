## You'll want to know: ਉੱਨ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C334-WOOL334-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ਉੱਨ** — *unn* — "wool".

> ਸਰਦੀ ਲਈ ਦਾਦੀ ਉੱਨ ਖ਼ਰੀਦਦੀ ਹੈ।
>
> *sardī laī dādī unn kharīddī hai.*
>
> Grandmother buys wool for the winter.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਉੱਨ** (*unn*) is inherited from Sanskrit *ūrṇā*, "wool"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *unn*, "wool", under it (CDIAL 2424).
