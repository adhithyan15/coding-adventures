## You'll want to know: adopter
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C492-FAMILLES-02]; assesses=[] -->

**adopter** — "to adopt".

> Mes voisins ont adopté une petite fille.
>
> *My neighbours have adopted a little girl.*

Also of an idea or a pet: *adopter un chien*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*adopter* is a learned borrowing from Latin *adoptāre* "to choose, to adopt" (EtymDB), built on *optāre* "to choose, to wish" (Lewis & Short).
