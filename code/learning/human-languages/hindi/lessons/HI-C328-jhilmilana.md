# झिलमिलाना (jhilmilānā) — to twinkle; to flicker

## You'll want to know: झिलमिलाना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C328-ROSHNI328-03]; assesses=[] -->

**झिलमिलाना** — *jhilmilānā* — "to twinkle; to flicker".

> खिड़कियों पर दीये झिलमिला रहे थे।
>
> *khiṛkiyoṁ par dīye jhilmilā rahe the.*
>
> Lamps were flickering on the windowsills.

Past (it …): वह तारा झिलमिलाया, *vah tārā jhilmilāyā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**झिलमिलाना** (*jhilmilānā*) is the verb of *jhilmil*, flickering, which CDIAL 5391 sets under *\*jhil*: small lights and stars **झिलमिलाते हैं**.
