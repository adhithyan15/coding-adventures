Bengali, chapter 432: Peaks, Valleys and Deserts.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
চূড়া (chūṛā) — a peak; a summit; a top.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for silt, then the Bengali for to fill in (a pond or low land).

You'll want to know: চূড়া.
চূড়া — chūṛā — "a peak; a summit; a top".
আমরা ভোরে পাহাড়ের চূড়ায় উঠলাম। āmrā bhore pāhāṛer chūṛāy uṭhlām. We climbed to the summit at dawn.
Also the top of anything tall: মন্দিরের চূড়া (chūṛā), the spire of a temple.

The word, taken apart.
চূড়া (chūṛā) is Sanskrit cūḍā, "the hair on the top of the head, a crest" (Monier-Williams); the sense of a mountain top is a natural step, though Monier-Williams gives the head first.

Lesson 4 of 5.
মরুভূমি (môrubhūmi) — a desert.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a valley, then the Bengali for a mound.

You'll want to know: মরুভূমি.
মরুভূমি — môrubhūmi — "a desert".
মরুভূমিতে দিনে গরম, রাতে ঠান্ডা। môrubhūmite dine gôrôm, rāte ṭhānḍā. In the desert it is hot by day and cold by night.

The word, taken apart.
মরুভূমি (môrubhūmi) joins Sanskrit maru, "a wilderness, a sandy waste", to bhūmi, "land, ground" (Monier-Williams for both).

Lesson 5 of 5.
জঙ্গল (jônggôl) — a forest; a jungle; overgrown land.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a mound, then the Bengali for a desert.

You'll want to know: জঙ্গল.
জঙ্গল — jônggôl — "a forest; a jungle; overgrown land".
গ্রামের পিছনে একটা বড় জঙ্গল আছে। grāmer pichhône ekṭā bôṛo jônggôl āchhe. Behind the village there is a big forest.
বন is a forest in general; জঙ্গল (jônggôl) is wild and thick, and also an overgrown patch of weeds.

The word, taken apart.
জঙ্গল (jônggôl) is inherited: CDIAL lists Bengali jaṅgal, "a forest", under Sanskrit jāṅgala, "arid, sparsely wooded; a desert region" (CDIAL 5177); the turn from dry land to thick forest happened in the modern languages, and English jungle is the same word.
