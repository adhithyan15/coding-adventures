## You'll want to know: киоск
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C445-FONAR-04]; assesses=[] -->

**киоск** (*kiósk*) — "a kiosk, a news stand".

> В киоске продают газеты и билеты.
>
> *V kióske prodayút gazéty i biléty.*
>
> The kiosk sells newspapers and tickets.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*киоск* came from French *kiosque* (EtymDB), which goes back through Turkish *köşk* 'a pavilion' to Persian.
