## You'll want to know: charlar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C502-HABLAR-03]; assesses=[] -->

**charlar** — "to chat".

> Charlamos un rato en el bar.
>
> *We chatted for a while in the bar.*

*La charla* is the chat, or a talk.
