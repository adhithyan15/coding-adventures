## You'll want to know: déneiger
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C443-HIVER-05]; assesses=[] -->

**déneiger** — "to clear of snow, to plough".

> Le matin, les camions déneigent les grandes rues.
>
> *In the morning the lorries clear the snow from the main streets.*

Very common in Québec and in the mountains: *le déneigement*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*déneiger* is built from *dé-* and *neige* 'snow', which goes back to Latin *nix, nivis* (Lewis & Short). EtymDB does not list it.
