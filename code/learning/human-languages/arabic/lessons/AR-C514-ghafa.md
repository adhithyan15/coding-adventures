# غفا (ghafā) — to doze off

## You'll want to know: غفا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C514-TAAB514-05]; assesses=[] -->

**غفا** — *ghafā* — "to doze off".

> غفا جدي أمام التلفاز.
>
> *ghafā jaddī amāma at-tilfāz.*
>
> My grandfather dozed off in front of the television.

Present: يغفو, *yaghfū*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**غفا** (*ghafā*) is built on the native Arabic root *gh-f-w*, "to doze, to sleep lightly" (Lane). Its last root letter w shows as alif in the past and as ū in the present.
