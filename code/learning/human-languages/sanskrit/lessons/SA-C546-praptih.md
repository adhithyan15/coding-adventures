## You'll want to know: प्राप्तिः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C546-VIPAKA546-04]; assesses=[] -->

**प्राप्तिः** — *prāptiḥ* — "an attainment".

> यदि लक्ष्यस्य प्राप्तिः भवति, तर्हि वयम् उत्सवं करिष्यामः।
>
> *yadi lakṣyasya prāptiḥ bhavati, tarhi vayam utsavaṃ kariṣyāmaḥ.*
>
> If we reach our goal, we will celebrate.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**प्राप्तिः** (*prāptiḥ*) is built on *pra*, "forth", and the root √āp, "to reach", which also gives *prāpnoti*; MW gives *prāpti* as reaching, obtaining, attainment (MW).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
