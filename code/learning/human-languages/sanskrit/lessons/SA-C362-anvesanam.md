## You'll want to know: अन्वेषणम्
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C362-ANUSANDHANA362-03]; assesses=[] -->

**अन्वेषणम्** — *anveṣaṇam* — "an inquiry; a search".

> आरक्षकः चौरस्य अन्वेषणं करोति।
>
> *ārakṣakaḥ caurasya anveṣaṇaṃ karoti.*
>
> The policeman searches for the thief.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अन्वेषणम्** (*anveṣaṇam*) is built on *anu*, "after", and the root √iṣ, "to seek"; MW gives *anv-√iṣ* "to seek, to search, to aim at", and *anv-eṣaṇa* the seeking itself (MW).
