## You'll want to know: புலவர்
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C325-OLAI325-03]; assesses=[] -->

**புலவர்** — *pulavar* — "a classical poet; a scholar".

> அந்தப் புலவர் பல பாடல்களை எழுதினார்.
>
> *antap pulavar pala pāṭalkaḷai eḻutiṉār.*
>
> That poet wrote many poems.

Said of the classical poets and of Tamil scholars.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**புலவர்** (*pulavar*), "a learned poet, a scholar", is a Tamil word whose origin is not settled in the sources at hand.
