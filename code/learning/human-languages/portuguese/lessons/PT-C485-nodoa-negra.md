## You'll want to know: a nódoa negra
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C485-ARRANHAO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**a nódoa negra** — "bruise".

> Bati com a perna na cama e fiquei com uma nódoa negra.
>
> *I hit my leg on the bed and got a bruise.*

Literally "a black stain". In Brazil: *a mancha roxa*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*nódoa* is inherited from Latin *notula* "a little mark" (EtymDB), a diminutive of *nota* "a mark"; *negra* comes from Latin *niger* "black" (Lewis & Short).
