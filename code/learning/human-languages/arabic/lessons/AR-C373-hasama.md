## You'll want to know: حسم
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C373-MAWQIF373-05]; assesses=[] -->

**حسم** — *ḥasama* — "to resolve; to settle".

> حسم المدير المسألة في يوم واحد.
>
> *ḥasama al-mudīr al-masʾala fī yawm wāḥid.*
>
> The manager settled the matter in a single day.

Present: يحسم, *yaḥsim*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**حسم** (*ḥasama*) is built on the native Arabic root *ḥ-s-m*, "to cut off" (Lane). *Ḥasama* is a Form I verb: to cut a question off, to settle it for good.

## What you've built

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
