## You'll want to know: der Nieselregen
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C461-NIESELREGEN-01]; assesses=[] -->

**der Nieselregen** — "the drizzle".

> Bei Nieselregen gehen wir trotzdem spazieren.
>
> *We go for a walk in the drizzle anyway.*

No plural.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Nieselregen* is built from *nieseln* 'to drizzle' and *Regen* 'rain', an inherited word and a cousin of English *rain* (EtymDB).
