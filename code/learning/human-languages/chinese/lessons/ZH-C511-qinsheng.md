## You'll want to know: 亲生
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C511-KIN511-04]; assesses=[] -->

**亲生** — *qīnshēng* — "one's own (by birth)".

> *tāmen bú shì qīnshēng xiōngdì* — They aren't brothers by birth.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**亲生** (*qīnshēng*) is built from 亲 'close' and 生 'to be born', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *tshin* and *srjaeng* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'to grow'.
