## You'll want to know: चौथो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C206-LANE206-02]; assesses=[] -->

**चौथो** — *chautho* — "fourth".

> मंदिर चौथी गळी मांय है।
>
> *mandir chauthī gaḷī mā̃y hai.*
>
> The temple is in the fourth lane.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**चौथो** (*chautho*) is inherited from Sanskrit *caturtha*, "fourth"; CDIAL lists Marwari *cotho*, "4th" (CDIAL 4600).
