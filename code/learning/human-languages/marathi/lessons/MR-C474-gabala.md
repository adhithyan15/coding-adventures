## You'll want to know: गबाळा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C474-SHISTA474-02]; assesses=[] -->

**गबाळा** — *gabāḷā* — "sloppy; untidy".

> गबाळे कपडे घालून तो मुलाखतीला गेला.
>
> *gabāḷe kapḍe ghālūn to mulākhtīlā gelā.*
>
> He went to the interview in sloppy clothes.

Agrees; the opposite of नीटनेटका.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**गबाळा** (*gabāḷā*) is a Marathi adjective whose origin is not settled.
