## You'll want to know: しょくひ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C497-MEAL497-02]; assesses=[] -->

**しょくひ** — *shokuhi* — "food costs".

> **せんげつは しょくひが たかかったです。** — *sengetsu wa shokuhi ga takakatta desu* — "Food costs were high last month."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**しょくひ** (*shokuhi*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *shoku*, "eat" (Middle Chinese *zyik*) and *hi*, "expense" (*phj+jH*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
