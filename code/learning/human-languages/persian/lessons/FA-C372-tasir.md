## You'll want to know: تاثیر
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C372-TASIR-01]; assesses=[] -->

**تاثیر** (*ta'sir*) — "an influence, an effect".

> این کتاب تاثیر زیادی روی من داشت.
>
> *in ketâb ta'sir-e ziyâdi ruye man dâsht.*
>
> This book had a big influence on me.

*Ta'sir dâshtan bar* or *ruye* — to have an effect on. Plural *ta'sirât*. Many writers put a hamza on its alef; here it is written plain.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**تاثیر** is usually traced to Arabic *ta'thīr* "effect", a verbal noun usually explained as leaving a trace; Persian says the Arabic *th* as *s*.
