## You'll want to know: acusar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C523-PRESO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**acusar** — "to accuse".

> Lo acusaron de robar el dinero.
>
> *They accused him of stealing the money.*

*Acusar a alguien de algo* — to accuse someone of something.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*acusar* comes from Latin *accūsāre* 'to call to account' (EtymDB), built on *ad* and *causa* 'a case at law'.
