## You'll want to know: pescar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C695-FAUNA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**pescar** — "to fish; to catch".

> Mi padre va a pescar al río.
>
> *My father goes fishing in the river.*

*El pescado* is fish as food; *el pez* is a live fish.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*pescar* is inherited from Vulgar Latin *piscāre*, Latin *piscārī* "to fish" (EtymDB), from *piscis* "fish".
