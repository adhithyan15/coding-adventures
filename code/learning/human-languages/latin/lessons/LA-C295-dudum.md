## You'll want to know: dūdum
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C295-NOCTU-05]; assesses=[] -->

**dūdum** — "a little while ago; for a long time".

> Epistulam, quam dūdum scrīpsī, nunc mittō.
>
> *I am now sending the letter that I wrote a little while ago.*

With *iam*, 'for a long time now': the track teaches *iamdūdum*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**dūdum** is built, says Lewis & Short, on *diū*, "for a long time", and *dum*, "while" (EtymDB lists the same parts): it is a while since.
