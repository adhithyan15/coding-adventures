## You'll want to know: metus, metūs
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C320-PAVOR-02]; assesses=[] -->

**metus, metūs** — "fear, dread".

> Propter metum hostium portās clausērunt.
>
> *Out of fear of the enemy they shut the gates.*

Masculine, 4th declension. A steady, lasting fear, beside *timor* (taught).

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**metus** is a Latin word whose earlier origin is not settled (Lewis & Short, EtymDB). Its verb is *metuō*, "to fear" (taught).
