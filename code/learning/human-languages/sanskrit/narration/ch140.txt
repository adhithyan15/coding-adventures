Sanskrit, chapter 140: A Notice, a Timetable, an Advert, a Bill, a Receipt.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
सूचना (sūcanā) — a notice.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for will be, then the Sanskrit for will go.

You'll want to know: सूचना.
सूचना — sūcanā — "a notice".
[once you have stopped driving — read: the notice on the shop door]
The notice on the shop door says अद्य विश्रामः। — "today, rest". The shop is closed today, so come back श्वः.
