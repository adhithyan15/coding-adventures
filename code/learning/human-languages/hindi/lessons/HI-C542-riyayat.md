## You'll want to know: रियायत
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C542-SAMJHAUTA542-02]; assesses=[] -->

**रियायत** — *riyāyat* — "a concession; a reduction (in a price)".

> मालिक ने किराए में थोड़ी रियायत दी।
>
> *Mālik ne kirāe meṁ thoṛī riyāyat dī.*
>
> The landlord took a little off the rent.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**रियायत** (*riyāyat*) is usually traced to Arabic *riʿāyat*, "consideration, concession", through Persian.
