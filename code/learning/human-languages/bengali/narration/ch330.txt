Bengali, chapter 330: Where It Came From.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
উৎস (utsô) — a source; an origin.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a female singer, then the Bengali for a female dancer.

You'll want to know: উৎস.
উৎস — utsô — "a source; an origin".
এই খবরের উৎস কী? ei khôbôrer utsô kī? Where did this news come from?

The word, taken apart.
উৎস (utsô) is Sanskrit utsa, a spring where water rises (Monier-Williams). Bengali uses it for the place anything first flows from, news or money or a river; that wider use is a later extension of the picture.

You'll want to know: সৃষ্টি করা.
সৃষ্টি করা — srishṭi kôrā — "to create; to give rise to".
নতুন নিয়মটা অনেক ঝামেলা সৃষ্টি করল। nôtun niyômṭā ônek jhāmelā srishṭi kôrlô. The new rule caused a lot of trouble.
Past (he/she …): তিনি একটা নতুন দল সৃষ্টি করলেন, tini ekṭā nôtun dôl srishṭi kôrlen.

The word, taken apart.
সৃষ্টি করা (srishṭi kôrā) is Sanskrit sṛṣṭi, creation or bringing forth (Monier-Williams), with করা 'to do'. It can make something good (a new team) or something unwelcome (trouble).

Lesson 3 of 5.
প্রভাব (prôbhāb) — an influence; an effect.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a source, then the Bengali for to create.

You'll want to know: প্রভাব.
প্রভাব — prôbhāb — "an influence; an effect".
আমার ওপর এই বইটার অনেক প্রভাব আছে। āmār opôr ei bôiṭār ônek prôbhāb āchhe. This book has had a big influence on me.

The word, taken apart.
প্রভাব (prôbhāb) is Sanskrit prabhāva, might or power (Monier-Williams). The Bengali sense, the pull one thing has on another, is a softening of that power; the influence falls on something, so it goes with -এর ওপর.

Lesson 4 of 5.
প্রতিক্রিয়া (prôtikriyā) — a reaction; a response.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for to create, then the Bengali for an influence.

You'll want to know: প্রতিক্রিয়া.
প্রতিক্রিয়া — prôtikriyā — "a reaction; a response".
খবরটা শুনে তার প্রতিক্রিয়া কী ছিল? khôbôrṭā shune tār prôtikriyā kī chhilô? How did he react when he heard the news?

The word, taken apart.
প্রতিক্রিয়া (prôtikriyā) is Sanskrit pratikriyā, from prati 'against, back' and kriyā 'an action': an action given back (Monier-Williams gives requital). The everyday sense 'a reaction' is a modern use of the same picture.

আমার বাবাই আমার প্রেরণা। āmār bābāi āmār preronā. My father is my inspiration.

The word, taken apart.
প্রেরণা (preronā) is Sanskrit preraṇa, setting in motion or urging on (Monier-Williams), in the feminine form. In Bengali it is the person or idea that pushes you forward.
