## You'll want to know: o indício
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C294-INVESTIGAR-05]; assesses=[] -->

**o indício** — "the clue, the sign".

> Não encontraram nenhum indício de assalto.
>
> *They found no sign of a robbery.*

Something that points to what happened.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*indício* is a learned borrowing from Latin *indicium* "a sign, evidence", from *index* "one who points out" (Lewis & Short).
