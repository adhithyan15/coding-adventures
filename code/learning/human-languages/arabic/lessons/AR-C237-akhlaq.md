## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**أخلاق** (*akhlāq*) is built on the native Arabic root *kh-l-q*, "to create; natural disposition" (Lane). *Akhlāq* is the plural of *khuluq*, "natural disposition".
