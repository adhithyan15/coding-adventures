## You'll want to know: लुण्ठति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C290-DASYU290-02]; assesses=[] -->

**लुण्ठति** — *luṇṭhati* — "plunders".

> यदि दस्युः आगच्छति, तर्हि ग्रामं लुण्ठति।
>
> *yadi dasyuḥ āgacchati, tarhi grāmaṃ luṇṭhati.*
>
> If a robber comes, then he plunders the village.

Past (he …): सः ग्रामं लुण्ठितवान्, *saḥ grāmaṃ luṇṭhitavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**लुण्ठति** (*luṇṭhati*) comes from the root √luṇṭh, which MW gives with several senses, among them to rob or plunder (MW).
