# ટુકડી (ṭukḍī) — a squad; a small party (of police or soldiers)

## You'll want to know: ટુકડી
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C310-TUKDI310-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ટુકડી** — *ṭukḍī* — "a squad; a small party (of police or soldiers)".

> પોલીસની એક ટુકડી જંગલમાં ગઈ.
>
> *polīsnī ek ṭukḍī jangalmā̃ gaī.*
>
> A police squad went into the forest.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ટુકડી** (*ṭukḍī*) is built on *ṭukḍo*, a piece, which CDIAL lists under *\*ṭukk*, "small", a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 5466): a small piece of a larger body of people.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
