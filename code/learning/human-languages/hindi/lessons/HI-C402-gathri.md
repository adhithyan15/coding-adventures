## You'll want to know: गठरी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C402-GATHRI402-01]; assesses=[] -->

**गठरी** — *gaṭhrī* — "a bundle (tied in cloth)".

> दादी के पास कपड़ों की एक गठरी थी।
>
> *dādī ke pās kapṛoṁ kī ek gaṭhrī thī.*
>
> Grandmother had a bundle of clothes.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**गठरी** (*gaṭhrī*) is a tadbhava of Sanskrit *grathna*, "a bunch" (CDIAL 4348), from the root of tying knots; CDIAL lists Hindi *gaṭhṛī*, "bundle".
