## You'll want to know: ほうもんする
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C347-HOUMON347-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ほうもんする** — *hōmon suru* — "to pay a visit; to call on".

> **もし じかんが あったら、あした ほうもんします。** — *moshi jikan ga attara, ashita hōmon shimasu* — "If I have time, I will call on you tomorrow."

Polite **ほうもんします**, *hōmonshimasu*; a する verb. Past (polite): **せんせいが うちを ほうもんしました。**, *sensei ga uchi o hōmon shimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ほうもんする** (*hōmon suru*) is built from a Sino-Japanese noun and the native verb *suru*, "to do". The noun, *hōmon*, is two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese: *hō*, "visit" (Middle Chinese *phjangH*) and *mon*, "question" (*mjunH*) (KANJIDIC2; JMdict; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
