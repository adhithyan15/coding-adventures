## You'll want to know: मियाद
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C548-AVADHI548-02]; assesses=[] -->

**मियाद** — *miyād* — "a term; an expiry date".

> दवा की मियाद दो साल है।
>
> *Davā kī miyād do sāl hai.*
>
> The medicine keeps for two years.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**मियाद** (*miyād*) is usually traced to Arabic *mīʿād*, "an appointed time", through Persian.
