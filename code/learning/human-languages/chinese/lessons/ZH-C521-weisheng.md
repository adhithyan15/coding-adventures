## You'll want to know: wèishēng
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C521-CHORES521-05]; assesses=[] -->

*wèishēng* — "hygiene; cleanliness".

> *chúfáng yídìng yào zhùyì wèishēng* — A kitchen has to be kept really clean.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*wèishēng* is built from *wèi* 'to guard' and 生 'life', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *hjwejH* and *srjaeng* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to protect' and the second 'to grow'. Guarding life as a word for hygiene is modern, and its path is not settled.
