das Restaurant — a restaurant.

An evening at the restaurant: say Guten Abend, then der Abend.

You'll want to know: das Restaurant.
das Restaurant — "a restaurant".

The word, taken apart.
Restaurant from French restaurant, "restoring", from restaurer, "to restore" from Latin restaurāre (EtymDB 2.1) — borrowed: a place that restores you.

Lesson 2 of 5.
die Bäckerei — a bakery.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for the post office; the post, then the German for a restaurant.

You'll want to know: die Bäckerei.
die Bäckerei — "a bakery".

The word, taken apart.
Bäckerei is the word for "baker" + -ei, the ending that names a trade's workplace (EtymDB 2.1). The baker is the one who bakes.

Lesson 3 of 5.
das Kino — a cinema.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for a restaurant, then the German for a bakery.

You'll want to know: das Kino.
das Kino — "a cinema".

The word, taken apart.
Kino is short for the word for the cinematograph, a modern coinage from Greek kínēma, "movement", and gráphein, "to write": the machine that records movement. EtymDB 2.1 has no source for Kino.

Lesson 5 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for a cinema, then the German for a theatre.

You'll want to know: das Rathaus.
das Rathaus — "a town hall".

The word, taken apart.
Rathaus is built from the word for "council", Old High German rāt from Proto-Germanic rēdaz, "advice" (EtymDB 2.1), and the word for "house". English rede, "counsel", is a cousin.
