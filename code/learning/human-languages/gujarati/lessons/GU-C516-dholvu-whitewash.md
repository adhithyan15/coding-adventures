## You'll want to know: ધોળવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C516-CHUNO516-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ધોળવું** — *dhoḷvũ* — "to whitewash".

> ગઈકાલે અમે આખું ઘર ધોળ્યું.
>
> *gaīkāle ame ākhũ ghar dhoḷyũ.*
>
> Yesterday we whitewashed the whole house.

Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે ઘર ધોળ્યું, *teṇe ghar dhoḷyũ*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ધોળવું** (*dhoḷvũ*) is inherited from Sanskrit *dhavalá*, "white", and CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb, "to whitewash" (CDIAL 6767).
