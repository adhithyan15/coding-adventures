## You'll want to know: previamente
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C277-FREQUENCIA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**previamente** — "beforehand, in advance".

> É preciso marcar a visita previamente.
>
> *You need to book the visit in advance.*

Formal; in speech, *antes* or *com antecedência*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*previamente* is built on *prévio* "prior" (EtymDB), from Latin *praevius* "going before", from *prae* "before" and *via* "way" (Lewis & Short).
