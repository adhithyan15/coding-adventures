## You'll want to know: ঘাঁটা
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C502-KOMPIUTAR502-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ঘাঁটা** — *ghā̃ṭā* — "to rummage; to fiddle with".

> ছেলেটা সারাদিন ফোন ঘাঁটে।
>
> *Chheleṭā sārādin phon ghā̃ṭe.*
>
> The boy fiddles with his phone all day.

Past (he/she …): তিনি আলমারি ঘাঁটলেন, *tini ālmāri ghā̃ṭlen*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ঘাঁটা** (*ghā̃ṭā*) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Bengali *ghā̃ṭā*, "to stir with the fingers", under Sanskrit *ghaṭṭayati* (CDIAL 4417).
