## You'll want to know: उपहसति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C457-HASA457-02]; assesses=[] -->

**उपहसति** — *upahasati* — "laughs at".

> बालाः मूर्खं वानरम् उपहसन्ति।
>
> *bālāḥ mūrkhaṃ vānaram upahasanti.*
>
> The children laugh at the silly monkey.

Past (he …): सः मित्रम् उपहसितवान्, *saḥ mitram upahasitavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**उपहसति** (*upahasati*) is built from *upa*, "towards", and the root √has, "to laugh", the root of **हसति**; MW gives "to laugh at, deride, ridicule" (MW).
