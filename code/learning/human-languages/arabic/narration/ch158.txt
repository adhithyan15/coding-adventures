Arabic, chapter 158: A Timetable, a Sign, the Rent, an Advert, a Receipt.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
جدول (jadwal) — a timetable, a table.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to decide, then the Arabic for to intend.

You'll want to know: جدول.
جدول — jadwal — "a timetable, a table".
[once you have stopped driving — read: the board — موعد الحافلة: الساعة الثامنة صباحا — mawʿid al-ḥāfila: as-sāʿa ath-thāmina ṣabāḥan — "Bus time: 8 a.m."]
Be there before eight.

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: jadwal]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: jadwal, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say jadwal]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Arabic for to decide, then the Arabic for to intend, then say jadwal again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does جدول (jadwal) mean? ("A timetable, a table".) Say it once more.

Lesson 6 of 7.
Looking back — a first pass.

Warm-up.
[pause 3 seconds]
Say the words for an advert and a receipt.

Guided Practice.
[pause 2 seconds after each]
[your turn — say: recently, already; indeed, last, once, to spend]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
Recently? (حديثا, ḥadīthan.) Already; indeed? (بالفعل, bi-l-fiʿl.) Last? (الماضي, al-māḍī.) Once? (ذات مرة, dhāta marra.) To spend? (قضى, qaḍā.)
