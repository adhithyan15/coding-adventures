## You'll want to know: روضة
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C398-TALIM398-03]; assesses=[] -->

**روضة** — *rawḍa* — "a kindergarten".

> يذهب ابني إلى الروضة كل صباح.
>
> *yadhhabu ibnī ilā ar-rawḍa kulla ṣabāḥ.*
>
> My son goes to kindergarten every morning.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**روضة** (*rawḍa*) is built on the native Arabic root *r-w-ḍ* (Lane), where it names a green meadow or garden. Modern Arabic made it the kindergarten, a garden of children.
