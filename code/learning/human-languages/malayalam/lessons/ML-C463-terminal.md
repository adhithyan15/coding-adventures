## You'll want to know: ടെർമിനൽ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C463-VIMANAM463-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ടെർമിനൽ** — *ṭerminal* — "a terminal".

> വിമാനത്താവളത്തിൽ രണ്ട് ടെർമിനലുകൾ ഉണ്ട്.
>
> *vimānattāvaḷattil raṇṭŭ ṭerminalukaḷ uṇṭŭ.*
>
> The airport has two terminals.

Plural: ടെർമിനലുകൾ, *ṭerminalukaḷ*.

Plural: add -ഉകൾ, as for most loans that end in a chillu.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ടെർമിനൽ** (*ṭerminal*) was borrowed from English *terminal*, a building where journeys begin and end.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for getting around.
