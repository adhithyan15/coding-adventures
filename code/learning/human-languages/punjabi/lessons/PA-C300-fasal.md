## You'll want to know: ਫ਼ਸਲ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C300-CROP300-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ਫ਼ਸਲ** — *fasal* — "a crop; a harvest".

> ਅਗਲੇ ਸਾਲ ਫ਼ਸਲ ਚੰਗੀ ਹੋਵੇਗੀ।
>
> *agle sāl fasal chaṅgī hovegī.*
>
> Next year the crop will be good.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਫ਼ਸਲ** (*fasal*) is a loan, usually traced through Persian to Arabic *fasl*, "season"; its sense here, "crop", is a shift from that.
