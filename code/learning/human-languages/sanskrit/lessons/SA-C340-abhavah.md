## You'll want to know: अभावः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C340-KARANA340-03]; assesses=[] -->

**अभावः** — *abhāvaḥ* — "a lack; an absence".

> जलस्य अभावः अस्ति, अतः वृक्षाः शुष्यन्ति।
>
> *jalasya abhāvaḥ asti, ataḥ vṛkṣāḥ śuṣyanti.*
>
> There is a lack of water, so the trees are drying up.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अभावः** (*abhāvaḥ*) is built from *a-*, "not", and *bhāva*, "being", from the root √bhū; MW gives *a-bhāva* the senses "non-existence, absence" (MW).
