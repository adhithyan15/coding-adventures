Hindi, chapter 346: A Heated Argument.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
बहस (bahas) — an argument, a debate.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a means, then the Hindi for an intention.

You'll want to know: बहस.
बहस — bahas — "an argument, a debate".
कल रात अरुण और मीरा में लंबी बहस हुई। kal rāt Aruṇ aur Mīrā meṁ lambī bahas huī. Last night Arun and Mira had a long argument.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
बहस (bahas) is usually traced to Arabic baḥth, "an inquiry, a discussion", which reached Hindi through Persian.

Lesson 3 of 5.

[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: झगड़ना.
झगड़ना — jhagaṛnā — "to quarrel".
दोनों भाई खिलौने के लिए झगड़ते हैं। donõ bhāī khilaune ke liye jhagaṛte haiṁ. The two brothers fight over the toy.
Past (he …): वह अपने भाई से झगड़ा, vah apne bhāī se jhagṛā.

The word, taken apart.
झगड़ना (jhagaṛnā) is inherited from jhagaḍ, "quarrel", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 5321).

Lesson 4 of 5.
टोकना (ṭoknā) — to interrupt, to cut in.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a line of reasoning, then the Hindi for to quarrel.

You'll want to know: टोकना.
टोकना — ṭoknā — "to interrupt, to cut in".
जब मीरा बोलती है तब अरुण उसे टोकता है। jab Mīrā boltī hai tab Aruṇ use ṭoktā hai. When Mira speaks, Arun interrupts her.
Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने मुझे टोका, usne mujhe ṭokā.

The word, taken apart.
टोकना (ṭoknā) is inherited from ṭōkk, "strike, bite", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 5476). CDIAL glosses the Hindi verb "to check, to question".
