## You'll want to know: せんじつ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C313-KIROKU313-04]; assesses=[] -->

**せんじつ** — *senjitsu* — "the other day".

> **せんじつ、ふるい ともだちに あいました。** — *senjitsu, furui tomodachi ni aimashita* — "The other day I met an old friend."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**せんじつ** (*senjitsu*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *sen*, "previous" (Middle Chinese *sen*) and *jitsu*, "day" (*nyit*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
