## You'll want to know: l'ottico
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C456-CURA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**l'ottico** — "the optician".

> Dall'ottico ho scelto occhiali nuovi.
>
> *At the optician's I chose new glasses.*

*Dall'ottico* — at the optician's.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ottico* is a learned borrowing from Greek *optikós* 'of sight', through Medieval Latin *opticus*.
