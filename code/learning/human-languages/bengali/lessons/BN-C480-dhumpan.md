## You'll want to know: ধূমপান
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C480-PUSHTI480-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ধূমপান** — *dhūmpān* — "smoking (tobacco)".

> ধূমপান স্বাস্থ্যের জন্য খারাপ।
>
> *dhūmpān svāsthyer jônyô khārāp.*
>
> Smoking is bad for your health.

ধূমপান করা, to smoke.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ধূমপান** (*dhūmpān*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *dhūmapāna*, "drinking smoke", inhaling smoke and, later, smoking tobacco (Monier-Williams).
