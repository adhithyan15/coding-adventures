## You'll want to know: できごと
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C280-KIJI280-03]; assesses=[] -->

**できごと** — *dekigoto* — "an event; a happening".

> **きのうの できごとを はなして ください。** — *kinō no dekigoto o hanashite kudasai* — "Please tell me what happened yesterday."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**できごと** (*dekigoto*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, built from *deki*, "coming about" (from *dekiru*), and *goto*, "matter" (from *koto*) (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
