## You'll want to know: टोमणा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C461-APMAN461-02]; assesses=[] -->

**टोमणा** — *ṭomṇā* — "a taunt; a dig".

> शेजारच्या बाईंनी आईला टोमणा मारला.
>
> *śejārcyā bāīnnī āīlā ṭomṇā mārlā.*
>
> The lady next door made a dig at Mother.

Masculine.

टोमणा मारणे, to make a dig: the target takes -ला.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**टोमणा** (*ṭomṇā*) is a Marathi noun whose origin is not settled; CDIAL lists a Marathi *ṭumṇī*, "goading", under *trumpati* (CDIAL 6070), but does not list this word.

## What you've built
