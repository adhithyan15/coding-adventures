Italian, chapter 511: Il risultato.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
segnare — to score; to mark, to note down.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for first-team player, then the Italian for to save.

You'll want to know: segnare.
segnare — "to score; to mark, to note down".
Ha segnato due gol nel primo tempo. He scored two goals in the first half.
Also to note down: segnare un numero.

The word, taken apart.
segnare is inherited from Latin signāre "to mark, to seal" (EtymDB), from signum "a mark".

Lesson 4 of 5.
la classifica — league table, ranking; chart.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for draw, then the Italian for to draw.

You'll want to know: la classifica.
la classifica — "league table, ranking; chart".
Siamo terzi in classifica. We are third in the league.
For music too: la classifica dei dischi.

The word, taken apart.
classifica comes from classificare "to classify", built on Latin classis "a class, a division" (Lewis & Short) and facere "to make".

Lesson 5 of 5.
eliminare — to knock out, to eliminate.

eliminare — "to knock out, to eliminate".
La nostra squadra è stata eliminata ai quarti. Our team was knocked out in the quarter-finals.

The word, taken apart.
eliminare is a learned borrowing from Latin ēlīmināre "to put out of doors" (Lewis & Short), from ē- and līmen "threshold".
