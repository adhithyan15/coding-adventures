## You'll want to know: yùdìng
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C200-HOTEL200-02]; assesses=[] -->

*yùdìng* — "to book ahead".

> *wǒ xiǎng yùdìng yí ge fángjiān* — I'd like to book a room.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*yùdìng* is built from *yù* 'beforehand' and *dìng* 'to order', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *yoH* and *dengX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'before'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for talking about plans.
