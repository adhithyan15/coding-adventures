## You'll want to know: विरोधः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C344-PAKSHA344-02]; assesses=[] -->

**विरोधः** — *virodhaḥ* — "opposition".

> नूतनस्य नियमस्य विरोधः भवति।
>
> *nūtanasya niyamasya virodhaḥ bhavati.*
>
> There is opposition to the new rule.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**विरोधः** (*virodhaḥ*) is built on *vi*, "apart", and the root √rudh, "to obstruct"; MW gives *vi-rodha* "opposition, hostility, quarrel" (MW).
