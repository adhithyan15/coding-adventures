## You'll want to know: промокать
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C465-LIVEN-03]; assesses=[] -->

**промокать** (*promokát*) — "to get soaked, to get wet through".

> Без зонта мы всегда промокаем.
>
> *Bez zónta my vsegdá promokáyem.*
>
> Without an umbrella we always get soaked.

Imperfective; perfective *промокнуть* (past *промок*). *Промокнуть до нитки* — to get soaked to the skin.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*промокать* is built from *про-* 'through' and *-мокать*, on the root of *мокрый* 'wet', which is inherited from Proto-Slavic *mokrŭ* (EtymDB).
