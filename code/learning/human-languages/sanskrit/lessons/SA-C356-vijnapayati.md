## You'll want to know: विज्ञापयति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C356-VIJNAPANA356-01]; assesses=[] -->

**विज्ञापयति** — *vijñāpayati* — "notifies; makes a formal request".

> कार्यालयः नूतनं नियमं विज्ञापयति।
>
> *kāryālayaḥ nūtanaṃ niyamaṃ vijñāpayati.*
>
> The office gives notice of a new rule.

Past (he …): सः अध्यक्षं विज्ञापितवान्, *saḥ adhyakṣaṃ vijñāpitavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**विज्ञापयति** (*vijñāpayati*) is built on *vi* and the root √jñā, "to know", in its causative form, "to make known"; MW gives the senses "to inform", "to apprise" and "to request" (MW).
