## You'll want to know: el racismo
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C605-PROBSOC-04]; assesses=[] -->

**el racismo** — "the racism".

> El racismo no tiene excusa.
>
> *Racism has no excuse.*

*Racista* — racist.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*racismo* is built on *raza* 'race' with *-ismo*; the origin of *raza* is not settled, and EtymDB traces it through Old Spanish *raça* to Latin *radius*.
