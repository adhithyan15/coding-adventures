## HL-C11Z — Japanese hiragana ne closes the hooked-loop gap

After Urdu **ب** landed, Japanese hiragana **ね** led the measured queue at
**3 affected realizations**.

Sirgazil's CC0 35-frame Wikimedia Commons animation supplies the two-run order:
descend through the short left vertical, lift once, then restart at the upper
right, cross left through the vertical, hook down along the diagonal, return to
the crossing, and continue clockwise around the lower-right loop. The new learner filmstrip fits that order to the
bundled Noto Sans JP outline without presenting print geometry as an independent
handwriting source.

This closes all 3 affected realizations for **ね** and reranks Japanese hiragana
**み** first at **3**.
