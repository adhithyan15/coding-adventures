## You'll want to know: おいる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C292-JINSEI292-01]; assesses=[] -->

**おいる** — *oiru* — "to grow old; to age".

> **ちちも すこし おいました。** — *chichi mo sukoshi oimashita* — "My father has aged a little too."

Polite **おいます**, *oimasu*; an ichidan verb. Past (polite): **そふが おいました。**, *sofu ga oimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**おいる** (*oiru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict).
