## You'll want to know: ひゃっかてん
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C190-TOWN190-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ひゃっかてん** — *hyakkaten* — "department store".

> **ひゃっかてんで かばんを かいます。** — *hyakkaten de kaban o kaimasu* — "I buy a bag at the department store."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ひゃっかてん** (*hyakkaten*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): three Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *hyak*, "hundred" (Middle Chinese *paek*), *ka*, "goods" (*xwaH*) and *ten*, "shop" (*temH*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
