## You'll want to know: तळणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C527-SHIJAVNE527-03]; assesses=[] -->

**तळणे** — *taḷṇe* — "to fry".

> पाहुण्यांसाठी आजीने गरम पापड तळले.
>
> *pāhuṇyāṁsāṭhī ājīne garam pāpaḍ taḷle.*
>
> Grandmother fried hot papads for the guests.

Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने पापड तळला, *tyāne pāpaḍ taḷlā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**तळणे** (*taḷṇe*) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi *taḷṇẽ*, "to fry, cook in ghee", under *\*talati*, "fries" (CDIAL 5736), beside Hindi *talnā*.
