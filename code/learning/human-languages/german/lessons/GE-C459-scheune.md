## You'll want to know: die Scheune
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C459-BAUERNHOF-03]; assesses=[] -->

**die Scheune** — "the barn".

> In der Scheune liegt das Heu für den Winter.
>
> *The hay for the winter is in the barn.*

Plural: *die Scheunen*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Scheune* is inherited through Middle High German *schiune* from Old High German *scugina* 'shed' (EtymDB).
