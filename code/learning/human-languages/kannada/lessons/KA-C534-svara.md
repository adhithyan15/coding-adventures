## You'll want to know: ಸ್ವರ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C534-SANGITA534-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸ್ವರ** — *svara* — "a musical note; a voice".

> ಸಂಗೀತದಲ್ಲಿ ಏಳು ಸ್ವರಗಳು ಇವೆ.
>
> *saṅgītadalli ēḷu svaragaḷu ive.*
>
> There are seven notes in music.

Plural: ಸ್ವರಗಳು, *svaragaḷu*.

Also a vowel, and the tone of a voice.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸ್ವರ** (*svara*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *svara*, "sound; a musical note" (CDIAL 13908).
