Punjabi, chapter 314: A Baby in the House.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ਜੰਮਣਾ (jammṇā) — to be born.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for to hand out, then the Punjabi for congratulations.

You'll want to know: ਜੰਮਣਾ.
ਜੰਮਣਾ — jammṇā — "to be born".
ਮੇਰੀ ਭੈਣ ਦੀ ਧੀ ਪਿਛਲੇ ਹਫ਼ਤੇ ਜੰਮੀ। merī bhaiṇ dī dhī pichhle hafte jammī. My sister's daughter was born last week.
Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਜਨਵਰੀ ਵਿੱਚ ਜੰਮਿਆ, maiṁ janvarī vich jammiā.

The word, taken apart.
ਜੰਮਣਾ (jammṇā) is inherited from Sanskrit janman, "birth, a creature"; CDIAL lists Punjabi jammṇā, "to be born", under it (CDIAL 5113).
