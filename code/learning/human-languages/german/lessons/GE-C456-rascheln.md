## You'll want to know: rascheln
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C456-LICHTUNG-05]; assesses=[] -->

**rascheln** — "to rustle".

> Im Herbst raschelt das Laub unter unseren Füßen.
>
> *In autumn the leaves rustle under our feet.*

*Es raschelt im Gebüsch* — something is rustling in the bushes.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*rascheln* is a sound word that imitates the noise of dry leaves or paper (EtymDB lists the sense 'to rustle').
