# ಸಂಭವಿಸು (sambhavisu) — to occur; to happen

## You'll want to know: ಸಂಭವಿಸು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C320-VARADI320-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸಂಭವಿಸು** — *sambhavisu* — "to occur; to happen".

> ನಿನ್ನೆ ರಾತ್ರಿ ನಮ್ಮ ಬೀದಿಯಲ್ಲಿ ಅಪಘಾತ ಸಂಭವಿಸಿತು.
>
> *ninne rātri namma bīdiyalli apaghāta sambhavisitu.*
>
> Last night an accident happened in our street.

Past (it …): ಸಂಭವಿಸಿತು, *sambhavisitu*.

An event is the subject, so the past is ಸಂಭವಿಸಿತು, it happened. It suits news reports.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸಂಭವಿಸು** (*sambhavisu*) is built on Sanskrit *sambhava*, "being or coming together, arising" (Monier-Williams), with the Kannada ending -*isu* that makes verbs of Sanskrit nouns.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
