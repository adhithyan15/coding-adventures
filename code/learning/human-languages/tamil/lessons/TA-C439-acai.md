## You'll want to know: அசை
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C439-MULAI439-05]; assesses=[] -->

**அசை** — *acai* — "to sway; to stir".

> காற்றில் இலைகள் மெதுவாக அசைந்தன.
>
> *kāṟṟil ilaikaḷ metuvāka acaintaṉa.*
>
> The leaves swayed slowly in the wind.

Past (it …): அசைந்தது, *acaintatu*.

அசைந்தது is it moved; அசைத்தேன், I moved something.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**அசை** (*acai*) is native Dravidian (DEDR 37), "to move, to stir; to shake".
