## You'll want to know: くもり
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C208-FORECAST208-02]; assesses=[] -->

**くもり** — *kumori* — "cloudy weather".

> **らいしゅうは くもりでしょう。** — *raishū wa kumori deshō* — "Next week will probably be cloudy."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**くもり** (*kumori*) is native Japanese (wago), derived from the verb *kumoru*, "to cloud over", inherited rather than borrowed (JMdict).
