## You'll want to know: le téléspectateur
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C280-TELE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**le téléspectateur** — "the viewer (of television)".

> Des millions de téléspectateurs ont vu le match.
>
> *Millions of viewers watched the match.*

Feminine: *la téléspectatrice*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*téléspectateur* joins Greek *tēle* "far" and *spectateur*, a learned borrowing from Latin *spectātor* "onlooker", from *spectāre* "to watch" (Lewis & Short).
