## You'll want to know: تحلیل کردن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C368-TAHLIL-02]; assesses=[] -->

**تحلیل کردن** (*tahlil kardan*) — "to analyse".

> استاد نتیجه آزمایش را تحلیل کرد.
>
> *ostâd natije-ye âzmâyesh râ tahlil kard.*
>
> The professor analysed the result of the experiment.

*Tahlil* + *kardan* (stem *kon-*). The object takes *râ*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**تحلیل کردن** joins *tahlil*, usually traced to Arabic *taḥlīl* "analysis", to the inherited Persian verb *kardan* "to do".
