Lesson 2 of 5.
One postposition for about — about or concerning.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Retrieve the familiar noun for a book, then leave a silent space after it.

Grammar Lens: direct owner.
ਬਾਰੇ (baare) — bāre — about; concerning.
Hear and say the whole word before reading it. Keep it as one whole postposition. Punjabi places a postposition after its noun; the next lesson will retrieve this new word before the familiar noun returns.

The word, taken apart — where the trail runs out.
Punjabi University Patiala explains the after-the-noun position. A dictionary entry records the “about / concerning” meaning and cites Bhai Maya Singh's 1895 dictionary. Punjab School Education Board Class 5 material independently uses the word in ordinary school prompts. Sources: Punjabi University Patiala, dictionary entry, and PSEB Class 5.
The exact historical derivation of modern written ਬਾਰੇ (baare) remains unknown in the cited sources. Its origin remains unknown here. This lesson therefore makes no etymological claim. Keep the present-day word whole.

Wrap-up Recall.

Lesson 4 of 5.
Put the postposition after one familiar noun — about the book.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Retrieve the familiar book noun, then the new postposition.

Grammar Lens.
ਕਿਤਾਬ ਬਾਰੇ (kitaab baare) — kitāb bāre — about the book.
Punjabi puts this postposition after the familiar noun: book first, then “about.” This chapter owns only that relationship word in this one gentle frame; the book noun remains old.
