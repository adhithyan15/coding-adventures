## You'll want to know: фантастика
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C524-GENRES-02]; assesses=[] -->

**фантастика** (*fantástika*) — "science fiction".

> Мой брат читает только фантастику.
>
> *Moy brat chitáyet tólko fantástiku.*
>
> My brother only reads science fiction.

Feminine. Also fantasy, and colloquially something amazing: *Это фантастика!*

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

EtymDB lists *фантастика* without a source; it is usually traced through *фантастический* to Greek *phantastikós* 'able to imagine'.
