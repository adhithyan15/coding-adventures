## You'll want to know: vomitar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C740-MAREO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**vomitar** — "to vomit, to be sick".

> El niño vomitó en el coche.
>
> *The boy was sick in the car.*

*Tener ganas de vomitar* — to feel sick.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*vomitar* is a learned borrowing from Latin *vomitāre* (EtymDB), from *vomere* "to throw up".
