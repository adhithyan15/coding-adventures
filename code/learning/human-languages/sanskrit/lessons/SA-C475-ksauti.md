## You'll want to know: क्षौति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C475-SVEDA475-03]; assesses=[] -->

**क्षौति** — *kṣauti* — "sneezes".

> धूल्या सः क्षौति।
>
> *dhūlyā saḥ kṣauti.*
>
> The dust makes him sneeze.

Past (he …): सः क्षुतवान्, *saḥ kṣutavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**क्षौति** (*kṣauti*) comes from the root √kṣu, "to sneeze", a second-class verb whose vowel strengthens to *au* (MW).
