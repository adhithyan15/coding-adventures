## You'll want to know: अन्तिमः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C405-JAVA405-04]; assesses=[] -->

**अन्तिमः** — *antimaḥ* — "last; final".

> अन्तिमं लोकयानं रात्रौ गच्छति।
>
> *antimaṃ lokayānaṃ rātrau gacchati.*
>
> The last bus leaves at night.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अन्तिमः** (*antimaḥ*) is built on *anta*, "an end", with the ending *-ima*; MW gives *antima* as final, ultimate, last (MW).
