# هجر (hajara) — to abandon, to leave

## You'll want to know: هجر
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C535-RIFAQ535-05]; assesses=[] -->

**هجر** — *hajara* — "to abandon, to leave".

> هجر الشاب قريته وسكن في المدينة.
>
> *hajara ash-shābb qaryatahu wa-sakana fī al-madīna.*
>
> The young man left his village and lived in the city.

Present: يهجر, *yahjur*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**هجر** (*hajara*) is built on the native Arabic root *h-j-r*, "to cut off, to forsake" (Lane). Lane gives hajara, aor. yahjuru, as to cut oneself off from someone.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
