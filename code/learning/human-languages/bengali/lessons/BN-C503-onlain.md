## You'll want to know: অনলাইন
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C503-ONLINE503-03]; assesses=[] -->

**অনলাইন** — *ônlāin* — "online".

> আজকাল আমরা অনলাইনে টিকিট কিনি।
>
> *Ājkāl āmrā ônlāine ṭikiṭ kini.*
>
> Nowadays we buy tickets online.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**অনলাইন** (*ônlāin*) is borrowed from English *online*; it takes the locative: অনলাইনে, online.
