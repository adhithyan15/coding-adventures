## You'll want to know: o coro
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C518-CORO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**o coro** — "choir; chorus".

> Canto num coro há dez anos.
>
> *I have sung in a choir for ten years.*

*Em coro* — all together.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*coro* is inherited through Old Portuguese (EtymDB) from Latin *chorus*, from Greek *khorós* "a dance, a band of dancers and singers".
