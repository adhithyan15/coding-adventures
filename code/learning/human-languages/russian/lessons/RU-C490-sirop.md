## You'll want to know: сироп
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C490-RECOVERY-05]; assesses=[] -->

**сироп** (*siróp*) — "syrup".

> Врач выписал сироп от кашля.
>
> *Vrach výpisal siróp ot káshlya.*
>
> The doctor prescribed cough syrup.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*сироп* was borrowed from French *sirop*, which comes from Arabic *šarāb* 'drink' (EtymDB).
