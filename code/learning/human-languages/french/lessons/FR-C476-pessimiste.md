## You'll want to know: pessimiste
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C476-INQUIETUDE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**pessimiste** — "pessimistic; pessimist".

> Mon oncle est pessimiste : il pense que tout va mal finir.
>
> *My uncle is pessimistic: he thinks everything will end badly.*

The same form for a man and a woman.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*pessimiste* is built on *pessimisme*, a modern learned word made from Latin *pessimus* "worst" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short).
