# આસ્થા (āsthā) — belief; faith (in God or an idea)

## You'll want to know: આસ્થા
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C386-AASTHA386-01]; assesses=[] -->

**આસ્થા** — *āsthā* — "belief; faith (in God or an idea)".

> જો તમારી આસ્થા મજબૂત હશે તો તમે હિંમત નહીં હારો.
>
> *jo tamārī āsthā majbūt haśe to tame himmat nahī̃ hāro.*
>
> If your faith is strong, you will not lose heart.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**આસ્થા** (*āsthā*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *āsthā*, "regard, care, confidence" (Monier-Williams).
