## You'll want to know: bordar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C500-COSTURA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**bordar** — "to embroider".

> A avó bordou flores num lenço.
>
> *Grandma embroidered flowers on a handkerchief.*

Embroidery is *o bordado*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*bordar* is linked in EtymDB with French *broder* "to embroider", which goes back to a Germanic word for an edge or border.
