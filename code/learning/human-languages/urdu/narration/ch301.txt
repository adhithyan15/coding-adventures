Urdu, chapter 301: Reporters.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
صحافی (sahāfī) — a journalist.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for an official notice, then the Urdu for a sensation.

You'll want to know: صحافی.
صحافی — sahāfī — "a journalist".
صحافی نے داروغے سے سوال پوچھے۔ sahāfī ne dāroghe se savāl pūchhe. The journalist asked the inspector questions.
Masculine or feminine, by the person meant.

The word, taken apart.
صحافی (sahāfī) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic ṣaḥāfa, "the press, journalism", with the ending -ī: one who writes for the papers.

Lesson 2 of 5.
موقع (mauqa) — the scene of an event; an occasion.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a sensation, then the Urdu for a journalist.

You'll want to know: موقع.
موقع — mauqa — "the scene of an event; an occasion".
صحافی موقع (mauqa) پر پہنچا۔ sahāfī mauqe par pahuṅchā. The journalist reached the scene.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
موقع (mauqa) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic mawqiʿ, "a place where something falls, a site". موقع پر is on the spot; the word also means a chance or an occasion.

Lesson 5 of 5.
لکھوانا (likhvānā) — to have written down; to dictate.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for an editor, then the Urdu for a detail.

You'll want to know: لکھوانا.
لکھوانا — likhvānā — "to have written down; to dictate".
گواہ نے اپنا بیان لکھوایا۔ gavāh ne apnā bayān likhvāyā. The witness had his statement written down.
Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے رپورٹ لکھوائی, us ne riporṭ likhvāī.

The word, taken apart.
لکھوانا (likhvānā) is the second causing form of لکھنا, to write, which CDIAL lists under Sanskrit likháti, "scratches, writes" (CDIAL 11048). The ending -vā- hands the writing to someone else.
