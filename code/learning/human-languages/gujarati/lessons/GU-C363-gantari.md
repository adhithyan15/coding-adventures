## You'll want to know: ગણતરી
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C363-ANKDO363-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ગણતરી** — *gaṇtarī* — "a calculation".

> મારી ગણતરી સાચી નીકળી.
>
> *mārī gaṇtarī sācī nīkḷī.*
>
> My calculation turned out right.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ગણતરી** (*gaṇtarī*) is built on **ગણવું** (*gaṇvũ*), "to count", from Sanskrit *gaṇayati*, "counts" (CDIAL 3993).
