## You'll want to know: proteger
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C504-PLANETA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**proteger** — "to protect".

> Hay que proteger los bosques.
>
> *We must protect the forests.*

The g becomes j before *o* and *a*: *protejo*.
