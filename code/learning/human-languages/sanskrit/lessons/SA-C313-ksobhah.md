## You'll want to know: क्षोभः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C313-UPADRAVA313-03]; assesses=[] -->

**क्षोभः** — *kṣobhaḥ* — "unrest; agitation".

> नगरे क्षोभः वर्धते।
>
> *nagare kṣobhaḥ vardhate.*
>
> Unrest is growing in the city.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**क्षोभः** (*kṣobhaḥ*) comes from the root √kṣubh, "to shake, be agitated"; MW gives *kṣobha* as shaking, agitation, disturbance (MW).
