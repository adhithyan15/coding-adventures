## You'll want to know: a censura
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C284-ESCANDALO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**a censura** — "censorship; criticism".

> No passado, a censura cortava as notícias.
>
> *In the past, censorship cut the news.*

Also blame: *uma censura* is a reproach.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*censura* is a learned borrowing from Latin *cēnsūra* "the office of the censor, a judgement" (EtymDB), from *cēnsēre* "to assess, to judge".
