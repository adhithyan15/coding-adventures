# ためる (tameru) — to save up; to store, to pile up

## You'll want to know: ためる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C186-SAVE186-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ためる** — *tameru* — "to save up; to store, to pile up".

> **いもうとは おかねを ためます。** — *imōto wa okane o tamemasu* — "My younger sister saves up money."

Polite **ためます**, *tamemasu*; an ichidan verb.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ためる** (*tameru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
