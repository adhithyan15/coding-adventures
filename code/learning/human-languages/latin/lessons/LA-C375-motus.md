## You'll want to know: mōtus, mōtūs
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C375-EXSISTO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**mōtus, mōtūs** — "a movement; a stirring (of the mind); a disturbance".

> Magnus mōtus in urbe fuit.
>
> *There was a great commotion in the city.*

*mōtus, mōtūs* m., from *moveō*. *Mōtus animī*, a stirring of the mind; *mōtus terrae*, an earthquake. English *motion* and *motor* belong to the family.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*mōtus* is the noun built on *moveō* "to move" (Lewis & Short), from its participle *mōtus*. English *motion* and *motor* belong to the same family.
