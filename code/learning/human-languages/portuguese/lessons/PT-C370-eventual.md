## You'll want to know: eventual
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C370-SENAO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**eventual** — "possible, potential (that may happen)".

> Levo um guarda-chuva para uma eventual chuva.
>
> *I am taking an umbrella in case of rain.*

A false friend: *eventual* means possible, not final.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*eventual* is a learned formation on Latin *ēventus* "an occurrence, an event" (EtymDB, Lewis & Short), from *ēvenīre* "to come out, to happen", on *venīre* "to come".
