## You'll want to know: esplodere
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C303-ESPLOSIONE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**esplodere** — "to explode, to blow up".

> Una bombola di gas è esplosa.
>
> *A gas cylinder exploded.*

With *essere*: *è esploso*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*esplodere* is a learned borrowing from Latin *explōdere* "to drive off with clapping" (Lewis & Short), from *ex* and *plaudere* "to clap".
