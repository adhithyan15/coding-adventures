## You'll want to know: sensible
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C727-SENSIBLE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**sensible** — "sensitive".

> Es un chico muy sensible.
>
> *He is a very sensitive boy.*

A false friend: sensible is *sensato*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*sensible* comes from Latin *sēnsibilis* "having feeling" (EtymDB), from *sentīre* "to feel"; Spanish kept the feeling sense.
