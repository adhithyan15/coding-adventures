## You'll want to know: सेनापती
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C330-SENAPATI330-01]; assesses=[] -->

**सेनापती** — *senāpatī* — "a commander".

> सेनापतीने सैन्याला थांबायला सांगितलं.
>
> *senāpatīne sainyālā thāmbāylā sāṅgitlaṁ.*
>
> The commander told the army to halt.

Masculine.

सेना, an army, + पती, a lord.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सेनापती** (*senāpatī*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *senāpati*, 'the lord of an army, a general' (Monier-Williams).
