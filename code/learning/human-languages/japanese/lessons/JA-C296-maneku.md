## You'll want to know: まねく
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C296-SAKAN296-03]; assesses=[] -->

**まねく** — *maneku* — "to beckon; to invite".

> **まつりに ともだちを まねきました。** — *matsuri ni tomodachi o manekimashita* — "I asked friends to the festival."

Polite **まねきます**, *manekimasu*; a godan verb. Past (polite): **ちちが ともだちを まねきました。**, *chichi ga tomodachi o manekimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**まねく** (*maneku*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict).
