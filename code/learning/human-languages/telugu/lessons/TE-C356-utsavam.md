# ఉత్సవం (utsavaṁ) — a public celebration; a festival

## You'll want to know: ఉత్సవం
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C356-UTSAVAM356-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ఉత్సవం** — *utsavaṁ* — "a public celebration; a festival".

> పట్టణంలో పెద్ద ఉత్సవం జరిగింది.
>
> *paṭṭaṇaṁlō pedda utsavaṁ jarigindi.*
>
> A big celebration took place in the town.

Plural: ఉత్సవాలు, *utsavālu*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ఉత్సవం** (*utsavaṁ*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *utsava*, "an enterprise", and later "a festival" (CDIAL 1876).
