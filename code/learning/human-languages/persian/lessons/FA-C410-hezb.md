## You'll want to know: حزب
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C410-LAYEHE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**حزب** (*hezb*) — "a political party".

> او عضو یک حزب کوچک است.
>
> *u 'ozv-e yek hezb-e kuchek ast.*
>
> She is a member of a small party.

Plural *ahzâb*, an Arabic broken plural.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**حزب** was borrowed from Arabic *ḥizb* "a party, a group" (EtymDB).
