## You'll want to know: ひよう
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C362-HIYOU362-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ひよう** — *hiyō* — "a cost; an expense".

> **ひようは ばしょに よります。** — *hiyō wa basho ni yorimasu* — "The cost depends on the place."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ひよう** (*hiyō*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *hi*, "expense" (Middle Chinese *phj+jH*) and *yō*, "utilize" (*yowngH*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
