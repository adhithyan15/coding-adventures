Hindi, chapter 353: Who Governs.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
संसद (saṁsad) — parliament.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for guilty, then the Hindi for bail.

You'll want to know: संसद.
संसद — saṁsad — "parliament".
संसद में आज नए क़ानून पर बहस हुई। saṁsad meṁ āj nae qānūn par bahas huī. Parliament debated the new law today.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
संसद (saṁsad) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit saṃsad, "an assembly" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 4 of 5.
प्रस्ताव (prastāv) — a proposal, a motion.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for governance, then the Hindi for a policy.

You'll want to know: प्रस्ताव.
प्रस्ताव — prastāv — "a proposal, a motion".
मंत्री ने संसद में नया प्रस्ताव रखा। mantrī ne saṁsad meṁ nayā prastāv rakhā. The minister put a new motion before parliament.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
प्रस्ताव (prastāv) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit prastāva, "an introduction; the subject of a talk" (Monier-Williams). Monier-Williams does not give the sense of a formal proposal, which is usually taken as a modern Hindi one.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ठहराना (ṭhahrānā) — to hold to be, to declare (guilty, responsible).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a policy, then the Hindi for a proposal.

You'll want to know: ठहराना.
ठहराना — ṭhahrānā — "to hold to be, to declare (guilty, responsible)".
लोगों ने सरकार को ज़िम्मेदार ठहराया। logoṁ ne sarkār ko zimmedār ṭhahrāyā. People held the government responsible.
Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने मुझे दोषी ठहराया, usne mujhe doṣī ṭhahrāyā.

The word, taken apart.
ठहराना (ṭhahrānā) is the causative of ठहरना, "to stop, to settle", which CDIAL traces to a reconstructed stabhira (CDIAL 13680): to make a judgement settle on someone.
