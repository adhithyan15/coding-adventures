## You'll want to know: o presépio
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C323-CONSOADA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**o presépio** — "the nativity scene, the crib".

> Todos os anos, a minha avó monta o presépio na sala.
>
> *Every year my grandmother sets up the nativity scene in the living room.*

*Montar o presépio* — to set up the crib.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*presépio* is a learned borrowing from Latin *praesaepium* "a stall, a manger" (EtymDB).
