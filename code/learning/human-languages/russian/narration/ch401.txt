Russian, chapter 401: Apps.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
приложение (prilozhéniye) — an app; an attachment; a supplement.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to break into, then the Russian for a virus.

You'll want to know: приложение.
приложение (prilozhéniye) — "an app; an attachment; a supplement".
Я скачал новое приложение. Ya skachál nóvoye prilozhéniye. I downloaded a new app.
Neuter. An app on a phone, an attachment to a letter, or a supplement to a magazine.

The word, taken apart.
приложение (prilozhéniye) is built on приложить "to put to, to attach", from при- "to" and ложить "to lay": something laid alongside. An app is the newest sense.

You'll want to know: устанавливать.
устанавливать (ustanávlivat) — "to install; to establish".
Он устанавливает программу на компьютер. On ustanávlivayet prográmmu na kompyúter. He is installing a program on the computer.
Imperfective; perfective установить (установлю, установишь). It also means to set up or establish: установить рекорд.

The word, taken apart.
устанавливать (ustanávlivat) is built from у- and ставить "to put, to stand up", on the root of стоять, Proto-Slavic stojati (EtymDB): to set a thing firmly in place.
