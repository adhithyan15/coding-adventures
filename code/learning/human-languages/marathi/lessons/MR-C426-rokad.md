## You'll want to know: रोकड
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C426-ROKAD426-01]; assesses=[] -->

**रोकड** — *rokaḍ* — "cash".

> दुकानदाराला रोकड हवी होती.
>
> *dukāndārālā rokaḍ havī hotī.*
>
> The shopkeeper wanted cash.

Feminine.

रोख is the adjective: रोख पैसे, cash money.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**रोकड** (*rokaḍ*) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi *rokaḍ*, "cash", under *\*rōkka*, "ready money" (CDIAL 10828).
