# दखल घेणे (dakhal gheṇe) — to take notice (of)

## You'll want to know: दखल घेणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C386-PRATIBANDH386-04]; assesses=[] -->

**दखल घेणे** — *dakhal gheṇe* — "to take notice (of)".

> शेवटी प्रशासनाने आमच्या तक्रारीची दखल घेतली.
>
> *śevṭī praśāsnāne āmacyā takrārīcī dakhal ghetlī.*
>
> In the end the administration took notice of our complaint.

Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने दखल घेतली, *tyāne dakhal ghetlī*.

What is noticed takes -ची: तक्रारीची दखल घेणे.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**दखल घेणे** (*dakhal gheṇe*) joins दखल, "notice, interference", a loan from Persian usually traced to Arabic *dakhl*, "entering", to घेणे, "to take".
