## You'll want to know: हिसाब
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C286-IMTIHAN286-02]; assesses=[] -->

**हिसाब** — *hisāb* — "sums".

> हिसाब दोरो कोनी।
>
> *hisāb doro konī.*
>
> Sums aren't hard.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**हिसाब** (*hisāb*) is usually traced, through Persian *hisāb*, to Arabic *ḥisāb*, "reckoning": sums at school, and the reckoning of money.
