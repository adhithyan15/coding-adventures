Gujarati, chapter 285: A Thief in the Night.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ચોર (cor) — a thief.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to pester, then the Gujarati for to hope.

You'll want to know: ચોર.
ચોર — cor — "a thief".
ગઈકાલે રાત્રે એક ચોર અમારા ઘરમાં આવ્યો. gaīkāle rātre ek cor amārā gharmā̃ āvyo. Last night a thief got into our house.
A masculine or feminine noun, by the person meant.

The word, taken apart.
ચોર (cor) is inherited from Sanskrit cōrá-, "a thief", and CDIAL lists the Gujarati word (CDIAL 4931). The verb ચોરવું, to steal, belongs to the same family.

Lesson 3 of 5.
નાસવું (nāsvũ) — to flee.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a thief, then the Gujarati for a theft.

You'll want to know: નાસવું.
નાસવું — nāsvũ — "to flee".
ચોરે પાકીટ ચોર્યું અને તે નાસી ગયો. core pākīṭ coryũ ane te nāsī gayo. The thief stole the wallet and ran off.
Past (he …): તે ચોર નાસી ગયો, te cor nāsī gayo.

The word, taken apart.
નાસવું (nāsvũ) is inherited from Sanskrit náśyati, "is lost, runs away", and CDIAL glosses the Gujarati verb "to flee" (CDIAL 7027). It is usually said with જવું: નાસી ગયો, he ran off.

Lesson 4 of 5.
પકડવું (pakaḍvũ) — to catch.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a theft, then the Gujarati for to flee.

You'll want to know: પકડવું.
પકડવું — pakaḍvũ — "to catch".
પોલીસે ચોરને બજારમાં પકડ્યો. polīse corne bajārmā̃ pakaḍyo. The police caught the thief in the market.
Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે ચોરને પકડ્યો, teṇe corne pakaḍyo.

The word, taken apart.
પકડવું (pakaḍvũ) comes from pakkaḍ, "to seize", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants, and CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb (CDIAL 7619). A person caught takes ને, and the verb agrees with him: ચોરને પકડ્યો.

Lesson 5 of 5.
લોકો (loko) — people.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to flee, then the Gujarati for to catch.

You'll want to know: લોકો.
લોકો — loko — "people".
પોલીસનું કામ લોકો પર પણ આધાર રાખે છે. polīsnũ kām loko par paṇ ādhār rākhe che. The work of the police depends on people too.
A masculine noun, used in the plural.

The word, taken apart.
લોકો (loko) is the plural of lok, from Sanskrit lōká-, "open space, the world", which later also meant "people" (CDIAL 11119). Gujarati uses it in the plural, and a verb with it is plural too: લોકો આવ્યા.
