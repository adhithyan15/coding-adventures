## You'll want to know: ತರ್ಕ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C377-TARKA377-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ತರ್ಕ** — *tarka* — "logic; reasoning".

> ಅವನ ತರ್ಕ ನನಗೆ ಸರಿ ಅನಿಸಲಿಲ್ಲ.
>
> *avana tarka nanage sari anisalilla.*
>
> His reasoning did not seem right to me.

ತರ್ಕಬದ್ಧ means reasoned, logical.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ತರ್ಕ** (*tarka*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *tarka*, "conjecture, reasoning" (CDIAL 5714).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
