# دلیل این است که (dalil in ast ke) — the reason is that

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[FA-LEX-C92-WHY92-04, FA-LEX-C293-TOWZIH-DADAN-01, FA-SCRIPT-C293-ZAD-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "a reason", then "to explain". (*Dalil*, *towzih dâdan*.)

[PAUSE 1s] An explanation is more than *chon*. Sometimes the reason needs a
sentence to itself.

## Grammar Lens: the reason, in a sentence of its own
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-GRAMMAR-DALIL-IN-AST-KE-01]; assesses=[FA-LEX-C133-ACT133-04, FA-LEX-C70-TOWN70-05, FA-LEX-AMADAN, FA-GRAMMAR-FEKR-MIKONAM-KE-01] -->

> **دیر رسیدم. دلیل این است که قطار دیر آمد.** — *dir residam. dalil in ast
> ke qatâr dir âmad* — I arrived late. The reason is that the train came
> late.

*Dalil*, "the reason", *in ast*, "is this", and *ke*, "that", which opens the
reason the way it opened the thought after *fekr mikonam*. The event comes
first and stands alone; then the reason gets the floor.

Before a longer explanation, offer it: **توضیح بدهم؟**, *towzih bedaham?*,
"shall I explain?". The subjunctive makes it a question about a thing not
yet done.

## Grammar Lens: a noun, or a sentence
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-GRAMMAR-CAUSE-NOUN-OR-CLAUSE-01]; assesses=[FA-LEX-C158-CHERA158-03, FA-LEX-C92-WHY92-03, FA-LEX-BARAN, FA-GRAMMAR-SIMPLE-PAST-01] -->

A cause that is one thing takes *be khâter-e*, with the ezafe joining the
noun:

> **به خاطر باران دیر رسیدم.** — *be khâter-e bârân dir residam* — I arrived
> late because of the rain.

A cause that is a whole happening takes *chon*, and a full sentence:

> **چون هوا سرد بود، دیر رسیدم.** — *chon havâ sard bud, dir residam* —
> Because it was cold, I arrived late.

So: a noun after *be khâter-e*, a verb after *chon*. "Because of the rain",
but "because it was cold".
