## You'll want to know: ಆದಾಯ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C400-BAJET400-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ಆದಾಯ** — *ādāya* — "income".

> ಅವರ ತಿಂಗಳ ಆದಾಯ ಕಡಿಮೆ.
>
> *avara tiṅgaḷa ādāya kaḍime.*
>
> Their monthly income is low.

ಆದಾಯ ತೆರಿಗೆ, income tax.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಆದಾಯ** (*ādāya*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *ādāya*, "taking, seizing" (Monier-Williams), from *ā-dā*, "to take": what one receives.
