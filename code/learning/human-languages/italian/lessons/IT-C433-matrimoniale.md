## You'll want to know: matrimoniale
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C433-CAMERA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**matrimoniale** — "double (of a bed or room); of marriage".

> Vorrei una camera matrimoniale per due notti.
>
> *I would like a double room for two nights.*

*Un letto matrimoniale*; a room with two beds is *una doppia*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*matrimoniale* is built on *matrimonio* 'marriage', a learned borrowing from Latin *mātrimōnium*, built on *māter* 'mother' (Lewis & Short).
