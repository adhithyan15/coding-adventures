## You'll want to know: অদ্ভুত
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C286-DEKHA286-03]; assesses=[] -->

**অদ্ভুত** — *ôdbhut* — "strange; odd".

> সেই রাতে আমরা পুকুরের পাশে একটা অদ্ভুত আলো দেখলাম।
>
> *sei rāte āmrā pukurer pāshe ekṭā ôdbhut ālo dekhlām.*
>
> That night we saw a strange light beside the pond.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**অদ্ভুত** (*ôdbhut*) is Sanskrit *adbhuta*, 'extraordinary, wonderful' (Monier-Williams); in Bengali it leans towards odd and uncanny.
