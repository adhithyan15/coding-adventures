## You'll want to know: ਖੂਹ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C303-WATER303-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ਖੂਹ** — *khūh* — "a well".

> ਖੂਹ ਦਾ ਪਾਣੀ ਠੰਢਾ ਹੈ।
>
> *khūh dā pāṇī ṭhaṇḍhā hai.*
>
> The water of the well is cold.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਖੂਹ** (*khūh*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*khūha*, "pit, well"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *khūh*, "well", under it (CDIAL 3912).
