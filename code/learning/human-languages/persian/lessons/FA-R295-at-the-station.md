# Review — a bad morning at the station

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[FA-LEX-RAFT-O-BARGASHT-01, FA-LEX-AZ-DAST-DADAN-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Ask for a return ticket to Tehran. (*Yek bilit-e raft-o bargasht
be Tehrân mikhâham.*) Then say you missed the train. (*Qatâr râ az dast
dâdam.*)

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[FA-GRAMMAR-PROBLEM-PAST-01, FA-LEX-C295-MASUL-01, FA-LEX-BEBAKHSHID-01, FA-GRAMMAR-CHETOR-MITAVANAM-01, FA-LEX-DAST-E-RAST-01, FA-GRAMMAR-POLITE-IMPERATIVE-01] -->

Two chapters, and most of a journey: ask the way, follow it, buy the ticket,
and when it goes wrong, say what happened and find someone who can help.

[PAUSE 2s] One morning, start to finish:

> — *bebakhshid, mas'ul-e istgâh kojâst?*
> — *dast-e chap ast.*
> — *bebakhshid, qatâr râ az dast dâdam. dir residam va qatâr raft. che
> kâr konam?*
> — *bâyad fardâ beravid. bilit-e raft-o bargasht dârid?*
> — *bale.*

The problem is all plain past. The help comes in the subjunctive: *konam*,
*beravid*.
