Urdu, chapter 315: Ancestors.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
اجداد (ajdād) — ancestors.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for an inscription, then the Urdu for ancient remains.

You'll want to know: اجداد.
اجداد — ajdād — "ancestors".
ہمارے اجداد کسان تھے۔ hamāre ajdād kisān the. Our ancestors were farmers.
Masculine, used in the plural.

The word, taken apart.
اجداد (ajdād) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic ajdād, the plural of jadd, "a grandfather": the grandfathers of the grandfathers.

You'll want to know: نسل.
نسل — nasl — "a generation; a line of descent".
ہر نسل نے یہ گھر سنبھالا۔ har nasl ne yih ghar sambhālā. Every generation looked after this house.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
نسل (nasl) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic nasl, "offspring, descendants". It is feminine: نئی نسل, the young generation.

Lesson 5 of 6.
اجڑنا (ujaṛnā) — to be ruined; to become deserted.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a generation, then the Urdu for a century.

You'll want to know: اجڑنا.
اجڑنا — ujaṛnā — "to be ruined; to become deserted".
سیلاب کے بعد گاؤں اجڑ گیا۔ sailāb ke bād gāoṅ ujaṛ gayā. After the flood the village was left deserted.
Past (it …): وہ شہر اجڑ گیا, voh shahr ujaṛ gayā.

The word, taken apart.
اجڑنا (ujaṛnā) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin ujaṛnā, "to be uprooted", under a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 1661). A place اجڑ گیا when its people left or were driven out.
