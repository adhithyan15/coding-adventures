## You'll want to know: भंडार
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C432-MAAL432-02]; assesses=[] -->

**भंडार** — *bhaṇḍār* — "a store; a stock".

> गाँव के भंडार में बहुत अनाज है।
>
> *gā̃v ke bhaṇḍār meṁ bahut anāj hai.*
>
> There is plenty of grain in the village store.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**भंडार** (*bhaṇḍār*) is a tadbhava of Sanskrit *bhāṇḍāgāra*, "a treasury", literally a house for goods (CDIAL 9442), which lists Hindi *bhā̃ḍār*, "a storehouse".
