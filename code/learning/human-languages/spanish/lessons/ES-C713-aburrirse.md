## You'll want to know: aburrirse
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C713-DIVERTIDO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**aburrirse** — "to get bored".

> Me aburro en las reuniones largas.
>
> *I get bored in long meetings.*

Reflexive: *me aburro*. *Aburrir* without *se* is to bore someone.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*aburrirse* is inherited from Latin *abhorrēre* "to shrink from in horror", through a spoken form *abhorrīre* (EtymDB); English *abhor* is the same word.
