# ಹಾಳಾಗು (hāḷāgu) — to get damaged; to be ruined

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[KA-LEX-C471-GADDALA471-04, KA-LEX-C471-GADDALA471-05] -->

## You'll want to know: ಹಾಳಾಗು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C472-HALU472-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ಹಾಳಾಗು** — *hāḷāgu* — "to get damaged; to be ruined".

> ಮಳೆಯಲ್ಲಿ ನನ್ನ ಫೋನ್ ಹಾಳಾಯಿತು.
>
> *maḷeyalli nanna phōn hāḷāyitu.*
>
> My phone got ruined in the rain.

Past (it …): ಹಾಳಾಯಿತು, *hāḷāyitu*.

ಹಾಳು ಮಾಡು, to spoil something.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಹಾಳಾಗು** (*hāḷāgu*) is native: ಹಾಳು, "ruin, a waste" (DEDR 4110, Kannada *pāḻ*), joined with ಆಗು, to become.
