## You'll want to know: exitus, exitūs
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C296-INITIUM-03]; assesses=[] -->

**exitus, exitūs** — "a way out; an outcome, an end".

> Nēmō exitum reī prōvidēre poterat.
>
> *Nobody could foresee the outcome of the affair.*

Masculine, 4th declension, from *exeō*, 'to go out'. English *exit*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**exitus** is built on *exeō*, "to go out", from *ex* and *eō*, "to go" (EtymDB): a going out, and so the way a thing comes out. English *exit* is from the same verb.
