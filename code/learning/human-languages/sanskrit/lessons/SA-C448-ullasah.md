## You'll want to know: उल्लासः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C448-BHAVA448-04]; assesses=[] -->

**उल्लासः** — *ullāsaḥ* — "merriment; high spirits".

> उत्सवे सर्वत्र उल्लासः अस्ति।
>
> *utsave sarvatra ullāsaḥ asti.*
>
> At the festival there is merriment everywhere.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**उल्लासः** (*ullāsaḥ*) is built from *ud*, "up", and the root √las, "to shine, to play"; MW gives "light" first and then "joy, merriness" (MW).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
