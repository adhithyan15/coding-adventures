## You'll want to know: a ladeira
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C438-SUBIDA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**a ladeira** — "the steep street, the slope".

> Moramos no cimo de uma ladeira.
>
> *We live at the top of a steep street.*

Old hill towns are full of *ladeiras*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ladeira* is built on *lado* 'side' (EtymDB), which is inherited from Latin *latus* 'side, flank': the side of a hill.
