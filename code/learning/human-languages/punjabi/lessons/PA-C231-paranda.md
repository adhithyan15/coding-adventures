## You'll want to know: ਪਰਾਂਦਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C231-DIN231-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ਪਰਾਂਦਾ** — *parāndā* — "a hair tassel; coloured yarn tied into a plait".

> ਭੈਣ ਦੀ ਗੁੱਤ ਵਿੱਚ ਲਾਲ ਪਰਾਂਦਾ ਹੈ।
>
> *bhaiṇ dī gutt vich lāl parāndā hai.*
>
> There is a red tassel in my sister's plait.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਪਰਾਂਦਾ** (*parāndā*) is inherited: CDIAL sets it under Sanskrit *prānta*, "end, edge", and lists Punjabi *parā̃dā*, "particoloured yarn used by women for tying up their hair" (CDIAL 8940).
