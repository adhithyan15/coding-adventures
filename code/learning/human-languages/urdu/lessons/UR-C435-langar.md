## You'll want to know: لنگر
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C435-KHALIJ435-02]; assesses=[] -->

**لنگر** — *langar* — "an anchor".

> ملاحوں نے لنگر اٹھایا۔
>
> *mallāhoṅ ne langar uṭhāyā.*
>
> The sailors weighed anchor.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**لنگر** (*langar*) is usually traced to Persian *langar*, "an anchor"; the same word names a free kitchen.
