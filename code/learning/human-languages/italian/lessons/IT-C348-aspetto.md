## You'll want to know: l'aspetto
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C348-VANTAGGIO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**l'aspetto** — "the aspect, the side (of a question); the look, the appearance".

> C'è un aspetto del problema che non abbiamo considerato.
>
> *There is one side of the problem that we have not considered.*

Also how something looks: *ha un bell'aspetto*. Do not mix it up with *aspetto* "I wait".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*aspetto* is a learned borrowing from Latin *aspectus* "a look, an appearance" (Lewis & Short), from *aspicere* "to behold", built on *specere* "to look". The verb *aspettare* "to wait" comes from the same Latin family.
