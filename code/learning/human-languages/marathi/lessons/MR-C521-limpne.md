## You'll want to know: लिंपणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C521-DURUSTI521-05]; assesses=[] -->

**लिंपणे** — *limpṇe* — "to plaster; to smear over".

> गवंड्याने भिंतीतली भेग मातीने लिंपली.
>
> *gavaṇḍyāne bhintītlī bheg mātīne limplī.*
>
> The mason plastered the crack in the wall with mud.

Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने भिंत लिंपली, *tyāne bhint limplī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**लिंपणे** (*limpṇe*) is a tadbhava, inherited from Sanskrit *limpati*, "smears" (CDIAL 11066).
