# પીડાવું (pīḍāvũ) — to suffer, to be in pain

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-LEX-C230-TOOTH230-02, GU-LEX-C230-TOOTH230-03] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Gujarati for jaw, then the Gujarati for tear.

## You'll want to know: પીડાવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C230-TOOTH230-04]; assesses=[] -->

**પીડાવું** — *pīḍāvũ* — "to suffer, to be in pain".

> **શું દર્દી પીડાય છે?** — *śũ dardī pīḍāy che?* — "Is the patient in pain?"

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**પીડાવું** (*pīḍāvũ*) is built on *pīḍā*, "pain, damage", a Sanskrit word taken over whole; CDIAL lists the inherited Gujarati *pīḍ*, "pain", under it (CDIAL 8227).
