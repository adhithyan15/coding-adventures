## You'll want to know: कफ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C481-SARDI481-02]; assesses=[] -->

**कफ** — *kaph* — "phlegm".

> सर्दीमुळे छातीत कफ झाला.
>
> *sardīmuḷe chātīt kaph jhālā.*
>
> The cold brought phlegm to the chest.

Masculine.

Masculine; in Ayurveda one of the three humours.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**कफ** (*kaph*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *kapha*, "phlegm" (CDIAL 2756).
