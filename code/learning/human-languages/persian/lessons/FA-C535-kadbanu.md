## You'll want to know: کدبانو
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C535-COSY-05]; assesses=[] -->

**کدبانو** (*kadbânu*) — "a capable housewife; the lady of the house".

> مادربزرگ کدبانو است و همه چیز را خودش درست میکند.
>
> *mâdarbozorg kadbânu ast va hame chiz râ khodash dorost mikonad.*
>
> Grandmother is a wonderful homemaker and makes everything herself.

Warm praise. Built on *kad* 'house' and *bânu* 'lady'.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*kadbânu* is a native Persian compound, usually explained as *kad*, 'house', and *bânu*, 'lady': the lady of the house. EtymDB has no entry for the compound.
