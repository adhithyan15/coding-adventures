## You'll want to know: نقي
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C477-QIRSH477-04]; assesses=[] -->

**نقي** — *naqī* — "pure; clean".

> الماء هنا نقي جدا.
>
> *al-māʾ hunā naqī jiddan.*
>
> The water here is very pure.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**نقي** (*naqī*) is built on the native Arabic root *n-q-y*, "to be clean" (Lane).
