## You'll want to know: couronner
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C323-REGNE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**couronner** — "to crown".

> Le nouveau roi a été couronné dans la grande église.
>
> *The new king was crowned in the great church.*

Figurative: *couronner une carrière* — to crown a career.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*couronner* is inherited from Latin *corōnāre* "to crown" (EtymDB), built on *corōna*.
