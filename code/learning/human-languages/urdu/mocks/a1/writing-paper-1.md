# Urdu A1 writing paper 1 — learner sheet

This is one **writing-only** paper for the project-defined Urdu A1
assessment, not a complete four-skill mock or a CEFR certificate. Use it
outside the lesson sequence: lessons remain at most five minutes, while this
paper has one continuous **20-minute** clock. The examiner keeps the rubric
separate. There is no answer model on this sheet.

Support-language directions are allowed at A1. Write your answers
independently in Urdu script, right to left. Approved Nastaliq or accessibility
Naskh presentation is fine; ordinary legible handwriting is enough. Do not
use Roman Urdu, Devanagari, a dictionary, translator, spell-checker, or
generative assistant. You may invent safe personal details.

Start the clock now. Spend **7 minutes** on Task 1, then move straight to
Task 2 for the remaining **13 minutes**. Do not pause or restart the clock.
Stop writing at 20 minutes; review only after the sheet is collected.

## Task 1 — practical form (7 minutes; 40 points)

A local library is starting a small reading circle. Complete **seven** short
fields so the organiser knows when and where to meet you. Use an invented name
or place if you prefer. Give a day and time you can come, a library or other
meeting place, a kind of book you like, and the name of a friend who may join.
Short Urdu words or phrases are enough; the labels are not sentences to copy.
