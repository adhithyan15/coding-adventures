## You'll want to know: قاد
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C339-GHAZW339-04]; assesses=[] -->

**قاد** — *qāda* — "to lead; to drive".

> قاد أبي السيارة طوال الليل.
>
> *qāda abī as-sayyāra ṭuwāla al-layl.*
>
> My father drove the car all night.

Present: يقود, *yaqūd*.

Of an army: قاد الجيش, he led the army.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**قاد** (*qāda*) is built on the native Arabic root *q-w-d* (Lane), "to lead a horse or camel by its rope"; a car is led the same way, so *qāda* is also to drive.
