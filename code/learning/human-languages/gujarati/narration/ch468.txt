Gujarati, chapter 468: Lies and Boasts.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
જૂઠું (jūṭhũ) — untruthful; lying.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for moral character, then the Gujarati for to be ashamed.

You'll want to know: જૂઠું.
જૂઠું — jūṭhũ — "untruthful; lying".
જૂઠું બોલવું સારું નથી. jūṭhũ bolvũ sārũ nathī. Telling lies is not good.

The word, taken apart.
જૂઠું (jūṭhũ) goes back to a reconstructed jhūṭṭha-, "false"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati word, "false" (CDIAL 5407).

Lesson 2 of 5.
ગપ્પું (gappũ) — a tall tale.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to be ashamed, then the Gujarati for untruthful.

You'll want to know: ગપ્પું.
ગપ્પું — gappũ — "a tall tale".
દાદાનાં ગપ્પાં સાંભળીને બધા હસ્યા. dādānā̃ gappā̃ sā̃bhḷīne badhā hasyā. Everyone laughed at my grandfather's tall tales.
A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
ગપ્પું (gappũ) goes back to a reconstructed gappa-, "talk", probably an imitative word; CDIAL lists the Gujarati gap, "tittle-tattle" (CDIAL 4022).

Lesson 3 of 5.
દંભ (dambh) — hypocrisy; pretence.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for untruthful, then the Gujarati for a tall tale.

You'll want to know: દંભ.
દંભ — dambh — "hypocrisy; pretence".
તેનું સ્મિત ફક્ત દંભ હતું. tenũ smit phakt dambh hatũ. His smile was nothing but pretence.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
દંભ (dambh) is a tatsama, Sanskrit dambha, "deceit, feigning, hypocrisy", as Monier-Williams records it.

Lesson 4 of 5.
શેખી (śekhī) — boasting.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a tall tale, then the Gujarati for hypocrisy.

You'll want to know: શેખી.
શેખી — śekhī — "boasting".
અરુણ પોતાની નવી ગાડીની શેખી મારે છે. Aruṇ potānī navī gāḍīnī śekhī māre che. Arun boasts about his new car.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
શેખી (śekhī) is usually traced to Persian šēxī, "pride, boasting", built on šēx, "an elder, a chief".

Lesson 5 of 5.
ભોળવવું (bhoḷavvũ) — to beguile; to mislead.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for hypocrisy, then the Gujarati for boasting.

You'll want to know: ભોળવવું.
ભોળવવું — bhoḷavvũ — "to beguile; to mislead".
લુચ્ચો વેપારી ગ્રાહકોને ખોટા વચનથી ભોળવે છે. lucco vepārī grāhkone khoṭā vacanthī bhoḷve che. The sly trader beguiles customers with false promises.
Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે બાળકને ભોળવ્યું, teṇe bāḷakne bhoḷavyũ.

The word, taken apart.
ભોળવવું (bhoḷavvũ) is built on ભોળું (bhoḷũ), "trusting", which goes back to a reconstructed bhulla- (CDIAL 9539): to make someone trusting, and so to fool them.
