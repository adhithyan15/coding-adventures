# کڑھنا (kuṛhnā) — to fret inwardly; to grieve in silence

## You'll want to know: کڑھنا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C443-SAD443-04]; assesses=[] -->

**کڑھنا** — *kuṛhnā* — "to fret inwardly; to grieve in silence".

> وہ اپنی ناکامی پر کڑھتا ہے۔
>
> *voh apnī nākāmī par kuṛhtā hai.*
>
> He frets over his failure.

Past (he …): وہ اس بات پر کڑھا, *voh is bāt par kuṛhā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**کڑھنا** (*kuṛhnā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *kuṛhnā*, "to be annoyed, to grieve", under Sanskrit *kruddhá*, "irritated" (CDIAL 3598).
