## You'll want to know: ಉದ್ಯಮಿ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C519-HANAKASU519-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ಉದ್ಯಮಿ** — *udyami* — "an entrepreneur; an industrialist".

> ಆ ಯುವ ಉದ್ಯಮಿ ಹೊಸ ಕಾರ್ಖಾನೆ ತೆರೆದನು.
>
> *ā yuva udyami hosa kārkhāne teredanu.*
>
> That young entrepreneur opened a new factory.

Plural: ಉದ್ಯಮಿಗಳು, *udyamigaḷu*.

ಉದ್ಯಮ, an industry or enterprise.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಉದ್ಯಮಿ** (*udyami*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *udyamin*, "undertaking, persevering" (Monier-Williams), built on *udyama*, "undertaking, striving after" (CDIAL 2048).
