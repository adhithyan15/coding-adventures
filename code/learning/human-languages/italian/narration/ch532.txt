Italian, chapter 532: Le pulizie.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
spazzare — to sweep.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for clothes peg, then the Italian for iron.

You'll want to know: spazzare.
spazzare — "to sweep".
Dopo cena spazzo la cucina. After dinner I sweep the kitchen.
With la scopa "the broom".

The word, taken apart.
spazzare is usually traced to Late Latin spatiāre (EtymDB), from spatium "space": to clear a space.

Lesson 3 of 5.

Pulisco il tavolo della cucina con uno straccio bagnato. I wipe the kitchen table with a wet cloth.
Old, torn clothes are stracci.

The word, taken apart.
straccio is built on stracciare "to tear", usually traced to Vulgar Latin extractiāre, from trahere "to drag" (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 5 of 5.
l'aspirapolvere — vacuum cleaner.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: l'aspirapolvere.
l'aspirapolvere — "vacuum cleaner".
Passo l'aspirapolvere in tutte le stanze. I vacuum all the rooms.
Passare l'aspirapolvere — to vacuum.

The word, taken apart.
aspirapolvere is built from aspirare "to suck in", from Latin aspīrāre "to breathe on", and polvere "dust", from Latin pulvis (Lewis & Short).
