# Review — a story in order

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[TA-LEX-C285-IRAM-01, TA-SCRIPT-RECOG-141, TA-GRAMMAR-C284-ONGOING-PAST-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "dampness". (*īram*.) Which letter opens it, and whose long partner is it? (**ஈ**: the long partner of **இ**.) Then "It was raining."
- [YOU WRITE: **ஈ** and **ஈரம்**, once each from memory]

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[TA-LEX-C285-IRAM-01, TA-SCRIPT-RECOG-141, TA-GRAMMAR-C285-FIRST-THEN-01, TA-GRAMMAR-C285-BEFORE-AFTER-01, TA-GRAMMAR-C285-VERBAL-PARTICIPLE-01, TA-GRAMMAR-C285-AFTER-DOING-01, TA-SKILL-C285-READ-STORY-01, TA-GRAMMAR-C284-WHEN-PAST-01, TA-GRAMMAR-C284-THERE-WAS-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s each]
- [YOU SAY: First I went to Grandmother's house. After that we drank tea.]
- [YOU SAY: While we were talking, suddenly it rained.]
- [YOU SAY: I walked to the bus station. My shirt was wet.]
- [YOU SAY: In the end I came home and ate.]
- [YOU SAY: a rainy day of your own, in four sentences, in order]
