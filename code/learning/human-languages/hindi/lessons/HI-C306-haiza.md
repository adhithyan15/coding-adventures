## You'll want to know: हैज़ा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C306-AKAL306-05]; assesses=[] -->

**हैज़ा** — *haizā* — "cholera".

> बाढ़ के बाद गाँव में हैज़ा फैल गया।
>
> *bāṛh ke bād gā̃v meṁ haizā phail gayā.*
>
> After the flood cholera broke out in the village.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**हैज़ा** (*haizā*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *hayḍa*, "cholera". Bad water after a flood was its usual cause.
