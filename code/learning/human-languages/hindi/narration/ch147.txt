Hindi, chapter 147: Rabbit, Deer, Fox, Wolf, Crow.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
खरगोश (khargoś) — a rabbit.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a donkey, then the Hindi for a sheep.

You'll want to know: खरगोश.
खरगोश — khargoś — "a rabbit".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: khargoś]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: khargoś, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say khargoś]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Hindi for a donkey, then the Hindi for a sheep, then say khargoś again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does खरगोश (khargoś) mean? ("A rabbit".) Say it once more.
