# ചുമതല (cumatala) — being in charge; a duty

## You'll want to know: ചുമതല
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C545-CUMATALA545-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ചുമതല** — *cumatala* — "being in charge; a duty".

> ഓഫീസിലെ പുതിയ പദ്ധതിയുടെ ചുമതല എനിക്കാണ്.
>
> *ōphīsile putiya paddhatiyuṭe cumatala enikkāṇŭ.*
>
> I am in charge of the office's new project.

ചുമതല ഏൽക്കുക, to take charge.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ചുമതല** (*cumatala*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 2677 lists it beside ചുമ, "a load", and ചുമക്കുക, "to carry": a duty is a load one carries (DEDR 2677).
