Tamil, chapter 96: Not at All, Is That So?.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
இல்லவே இல்லை (illavē illai) — not at all.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for not yet, then the Tamil for the same.

You'll want to know: இல்லவே இல்லை.
இல்லவே இல்லை (illavē illai) — "not at all".
It is இல்லை said twice, the first time with the -ஏ that says exactly: no, and no again.

What you've built.
The strongest no.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: illavē illai]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: illavē illai, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say illavē illai]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Tamil for not yet, then the Tamil for the same, then say illavē illai again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does இல்லவே இல்லை (illavē illai) mean? ("Not at all".) Say it once more.


Lesson 2 of 5.
அப்படியா? (appaṭiyā?) — is that so?

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for the same, then the Tamil for not at all.

You'll want to know: அப்படியா?
அப்படியா? (appaṭiyā?) — "is that so?", "oh, really?".
அப்படி is "like that", and -ஆ makes it a question.

What you've built.
A way to show you are listening.

Lesson 3 of 5.
அடடா (aṭaṭā) — oh dear!, wow!

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for not at all, then the Tamil for is that so.

You'll want to know: அடடா.
அடடா (aṭaṭā) — "oh dear!", or "wow!", by tone.
Said to bad news it is sympathy; said to good news it is delight. அடடா (aṭaṭā), அப்படியா? (appaṭiyā?) — "oh dear, is that so?".

What you've built.
A cry of surprise that can go either way.

Guided Practice.

[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: aṭaṭā, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say aṭaṭā!]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Tamil for not at all, then the Tamil for is that so, then say aṭaṭā again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does அடடா (aṭaṭā) mean? ("Oh dear!, wow!".) Say it once more.

Lesson 5 of 5.
தெரியாது (teriyātu) — (I) don't know.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for oh dear, then the Tamil for that's right.

You'll want to know: தெரியாது.
தெரியாது (teriyātu) — "don't know".
எனக்குத் தெரியாது (teriyātu) — "I don't know", literally "to me it is not known".

What you've built.
Not at all, is that so, oh dear, that's right, I don't know.
