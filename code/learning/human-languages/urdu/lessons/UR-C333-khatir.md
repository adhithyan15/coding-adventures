## You'll want to know: خاطر
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C333-MAQSAD333-02]; assesses=[] -->

**خاطر** — *khātir* — "for the sake of (کی خاطر)".

> اس نے اپنے بچوں کی خاطر نوکری چھوڑی۔
>
> *us ne apne bachchoṅ kī khātir naukrī chhoṛī.*
>
> She gave up her job for her children's sake.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**خاطر** (*khātir*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *khāṭir*, "the mind, regard"; after **کی** it means "out of regard for", "for the sake of".
