## Stroke order for tta, nna, sha, ssa, dha, bha, pha, kha, gha and ddha

Ten more consonants now have a cited stroke order: ಟ (KA-S151, chapter 73),
ಣ, ಶ and ಷ (KA-S164 to KA-S166, chapter 79), and ಧ, ಭ, ಫ, ಖ, ಘ and ಢ
(KA-S167 to KA-S172, chapter 80). Their writing blocks stop saying "copy the
shape you can see" and list the movements, the pen lifts and the source, in
the same form as the earlier consonants, and all ten letter lessons now print
a stroke-order filmstrip.

- **Source.** Gopala Krishna A's `Kannada-alphabet-<slug>.gif` animations on
  Wikimedia Commons (CC BY-SA 4.0, 25 May 2016), the series the vowels and the
  twenty consonants before them cite.
- **Lifts.** ಟ, ಣ and ಖ have no top bar and are one unbroken run. ಶ and ಭ
  lift once: ಶ for the hooked bar, ಭ for the tail. ಧ and ಢ are ದ and ಡ with
  a tail, so they lift twice. ಷ and ಫ are built on ಪ's body: ಷ adds a
  slanting stroke and ಫ a tail after the dot and the hooked bar, three lifts
  each. ಘ lifts three times, for the hooked bar, the dot and the tail.
- **Fewer lifts than the animation for ಭ and ಘ.** The animation lifts before
  ಭ's bar and between ಘ's two arms. Most people copying these letters in the
  Omniglot study drew them in fewer strokes, so the path runs back along ಭ's
  bar and back down ಘ's middle arm instead, and each lesson says so.
- **Three file names need a note.** That series spells ತ "tha", ದ "dha" and
  ಡ "da", so ಟ is filed as "ta", ಧ as "dhha" and ಢ as "dda". The KA-S151,
  KA-S167 and KA-S172 writing blocks say so next to the citation.
- ಠ (KA-S173) and ಞ (KA-S133) still ask only for copying: ಠ is left for a
  later batch, and no Commons file for ಞ has been confirmed.
