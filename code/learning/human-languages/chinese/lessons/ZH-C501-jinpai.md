## You'll want to know: jīnpái
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C501-WINNERS501-03]; assesses=[] -->

*jīnpái* — "gold medal".

> *tā zuì xiǎng yào yí kuài jīnpái* — What he wants most is a gold medal.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*jīnpái* is built from *jīn* 'gold' and *pái* 'a tablet', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *kim* and *bea* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'gold' and the second 'a signboard'.
