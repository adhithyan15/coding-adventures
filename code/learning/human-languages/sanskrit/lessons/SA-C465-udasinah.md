## You'll want to know: उदासीनः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C465-UDYAMIN465-02]; assesses=[] -->

**उदासीनः** — *udāsīnaḥ* — "indifferent".

> सः लाभे हानौ च उदासीनः अस्ति।
>
> *saḥ lābhe hānau ca udāsīnaḥ asti.*
>
> He is indifferent to gain and loss.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**उदासीनः** (*udāsīnaḥ*) is built from *ud*, "up, apart", and *āsīna*, "sitting", from the root √ās of **आस्ते**: sitting apart; MW gives "indifferent, free from affection" (MW).
