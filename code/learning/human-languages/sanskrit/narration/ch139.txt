Sanskrit, chapter 139: The Future, Tomorrow, Coming, Will Be, Will Go.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
भविष्यत् (bhaviṣyat) — the future.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for wanders, then the Sanskrit for grows.

You'll want to know: भविष्यत्.
भविष्यत् — bhaviṣyat — "the future". Sanskrit puts the future inside the verb, with -ष्य- before the ending: पठति — "reads" — becomes पठिष्यति — "will read".

Lesson 2 of 5.
श्वः (śvaḥ) — tomorrow.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for grows, then the Sanskrit for the future.

You'll want to know: श्वः.
श्वः — śvaḥ — "tomorrow". The time word stands first and the future follows: श्वः सः पठिष्यति। — "Tomorrow he will read." You already have परश्वः, the day after it.

Lesson 3 of 5.
आगामी (āgāmī) — coming, next.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for the future, then the Sanskrit for tomorrow.

You'll want to know: आगामी.
आगामी — āgāmī — "coming, next". It stands before the time word and takes its ending: आगामिनि सप्ताहे — "next week"; आगामिनि संवत्सरे — "next year".

Lesson 4 of 5.
भविष्यति (bhaviṣyati) — will be.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for tomorrow, then the Sanskrit for coming.

You'll want to know: भविष्यति.
भविष्यति — bhaviṣyati — "will be". The future of भवति, "is, becomes": श्वः (śvaḥ) रविवासरः भविष्यति। — "Tomorrow will be Sunday."

Lesson 5 of 5.
गमिष्यति (gamiṣyati) — will go.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for coming, then the Sanskrit for will be.

You'll want to know: गमिष्यति.
गमिष्यति — gamiṣyati — "will go". You met अगच्छत्, "he went"; this is the same verb looking forward: परश्वः सः आपणं गमिष्यति। — "The day after tomorrow he will go to the shop."
