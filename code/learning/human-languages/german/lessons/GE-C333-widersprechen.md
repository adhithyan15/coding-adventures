## You'll want to know: widersprechen
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C333-WIDERSPRUCH-01]; assesses=[] -->

**widersprechen** — "to contradict, to disagree with".

> Ich muss dir widersprechen: Der Film war gar nicht langweilig.
>
> *I have to contradict you: the film was not boring at all.*

Inseparable, with the dative: *ich widerspreche dir*, *ich habe ihm widersprochen*. Conjugated like *sprechen*: *er widerspricht*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*widersprechen* is inherited through Middle High German *widersprechen* from Old High German *widarsprehhan* (EtymDB), built from *wider* "against" and *sprechen* "to speak": to speak against.
