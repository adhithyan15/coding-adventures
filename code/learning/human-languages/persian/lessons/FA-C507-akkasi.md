## You'll want to know: عکاسی
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C507-HOBBIES-02]; assesses=[] -->

**عکاسی** (*'akkâsi*) — "photography".

> سرگرمی پدرم عکاسی است.
>
> *sargarmi-ye pedaram 'akkâsi ast.*
>
> My father's hobby is photography.

*'akkâs* 'photographer' + *-i*. *'akkâsi kardan* — to take photographs.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*'akkâsi* is built on *'akkâs*, 'photographer', with the Persian noun ending *-i* (EtymDB); *'akkâs* itself is built on *'aks*, a loan from Arabic.
