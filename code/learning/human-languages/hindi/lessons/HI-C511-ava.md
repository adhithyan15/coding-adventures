## You'll want to know: आँवा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C511-KUMHAR511-03]; assesses=[] -->

**आँवा** — *ā̃vā* — "a potter's kiln".

> कुम्हार ने कच्चे बर्तन आँवे में रखे।
>
> *Kumhār ne kacce bartan ā̃ve meṁ rakhe.*
>
> The potter put the unfired pots in the kiln.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**आँवा** (*ā̃vā*) is a tadbhava, inherited from Sanskrit *āpāka*, "a potter's kiln" (CDIAL 1202).
