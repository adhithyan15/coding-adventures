## You'll want to know: verhindern
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C347-ZUFALL-03]; assesses=[] -->

**verhindern** — "to prevent".

> Der Regen hat das Spiel nicht verhindert.
>
> *The rain did not prevent the game.*

Inseparable: *er verhindert, er hat verhindert*. *Verhindert sein* — to be unable to come.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*verhindern* is built with *ver-* on *hindern* "to hinder", a verb from the family of *hinter* "behind": you hold a thing back. EtymDB has no history for the verbs; Proto-Germanic *hinder* "behind" is the usual source.
