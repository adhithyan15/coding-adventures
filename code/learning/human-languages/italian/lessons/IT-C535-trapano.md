## You'll want to know: il trapano
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C535-ATTREZZI-01]; assesses=[] -->

**il trapano** — "drill".

> Mi presti il trapano? Devo fare un buco nel muro.
>
> *Can you lend me the drill? I have to make a hole in the wall.*

*Il trapano elettrico* — the power drill.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*trapano* comes from Latin *trepanum* "a drill", from Greek *trýpanon* "an auger, a borer" (EtymDB, under English *trepan*).
