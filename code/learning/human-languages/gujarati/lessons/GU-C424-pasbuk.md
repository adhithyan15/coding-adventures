## You'll want to know: પાસબુક
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C424-COUNTER424-02]; assesses=[] -->

**પાસબુક** — *pāsbuk* — "a passbook (of a bank account)".

> મારી પાસબુક ઘરે રહી ગઈ.
>
> *mārī pāsbuk ghare rahī gaī.*
>
> I left my passbook at home.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**પાસબુક** (*pāsbuk*) is borrowed from English *passbook*, the little book where a bank writes up your account.
