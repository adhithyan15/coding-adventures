## You'll want to know: ಪತನ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C373-UDAYA373-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ಪತನ** — *patana* — "a downfall; a fall".

> ಸೋಮಾರಿ ರಾಜರಿಂದ ಆ ಸಾಮ್ರಾಜ್ಯದ ಪತನ ಆಯಿತು.
>
> *sōmāri rājarinda ā sāmrājyada patana āyitu.*
>
> That empire fell because of lazy kings.

The opposite of ಉದಯ: the fall of a kingdom, a government or a price.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಪತನ** (*patana*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *patana*, "the act of flying or coming down, falling" (CDIAL 7724).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
