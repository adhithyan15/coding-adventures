Marwadi (Marwari), chapter 133: Chickpeas, Mung beans, Moth beans, Sesame, Jaggery.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
चणा (chaṇā) — chickpeas.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for maize, then the Marwadi for wheat.

You'll want to know: चणा.
चणा — chaṇā — "chickpeas".

The word, taken apart.
चणा (chaṇā) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from caṇa, "the chickpea". CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry (CDIAL 4579); the match is through Gujarati caṇā. Marwadi, like Gujarati, keeps the ṇ.

तिल (til) — sesame.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for mung beans, then the Marwadi for moth beans.

You'll want to know: तिल.
तिल — til — "sesame".

The word, taken apart.
तिल (til) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from tila, "sesame". CDIAL lists Old Marwari tila under it (CDIAL 5827). The Old Marwari row means "small particle", a sense the Sanskrit word also had. CDIAL thinks the Sanskrit word itself probably came from Munda.
