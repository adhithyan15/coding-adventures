## You'll want to know: 一生
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C322-BACKTHEN322-04]; assesses=[] -->

**一生** — *yìshēng* — "all one's life; a lifetime".

> **他一生都在这个学校工作** *tā yìshēng dōu zài zhège xuéxiào gōngzuò* — He worked at this school all his life.

A tone change: 一 is said with a falling tone, yì, before the level tone of shēng.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**一生** (*yìshēng*) is built from 一 'one' and 生 'to live', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *'jit* and *srjaeng* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which calls the first the start of the numbers and glosses the second 'to grow'.
