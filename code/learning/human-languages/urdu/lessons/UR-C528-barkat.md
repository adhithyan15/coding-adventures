## You'll want to know: برکت
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C528-BARKAT528-01]; assesses=[] -->

**برکت** — *barkat* — "a blessing; plenty".

> اگر بارش ہوئی تو کھیتوں میں برکت ہو گی۔
>
> *agar bārish huī to khetoṅ meṅ barkat ho gī.*
>
> If it rains, the fields will be blessed with plenty.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**برکت** (*barkat*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *baraka*, "a blessing, abundance".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
