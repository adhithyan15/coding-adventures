# અંગૂઠો (angūṭho) — thumb, big toe

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Gujarati for blister, then the Gujarati for wrist.

## You'll want to know: અંગૂઠો
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C229-LIMBS229-02]; assesses=[] -->

**અંગૂઠો** — *angūṭho* — "thumb, big toe".

> **મારો અંગૂઠો નથી દુખતો.** — *māro angūṭho nathī dukhto* — "My thumb does not hurt."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**અંગૂઠો** (*angūṭho*) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from *aṅguṣṭha*, "thumb; big toe" (CDIAL 137).
