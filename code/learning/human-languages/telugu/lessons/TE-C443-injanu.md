## You'll want to know: ఇంజను
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C443-VAHANAM443-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ఇంజను** — *iñjanu* — "an engine".

> బస్సు ఇంజను చాలా శబ్దం చేస్తోంది.
>
> *bassu iñjanu cālā śabdaṁ cēstōndi.*
>
> The bus engine is making a lot of noise.

The engine of a train is also its ఇంజను.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ఇంజను** (*iñjanu*) is a loan from English *engine*, with the Telugu final -ఉ.
