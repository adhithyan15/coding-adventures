## You'll want to know: o termómetro
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C486-LIGADURA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**o termómetro** — "thermometer".

> O termómetro marcava trinta e nove graus.
>
> *The thermometer read thirty-nine degrees.*

In Brazil: *termômetro*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*termómetro* is usually traced to French *thermomètre*, a learned coinage from Greek *thermós* "hot" and *métron* "measure".
