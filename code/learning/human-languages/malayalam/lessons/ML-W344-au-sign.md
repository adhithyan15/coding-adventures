# ൗ (au) — the sign inside സൗകര്യം

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ML-SCRIPT-RECOG-139, ML-SCRIPT-C114-AI-SIGN-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: write **െ** and **ൈ** once each, and name them. (The *e* sign and
the *ai* sign, both printed before their consonant.)

[PAUSE 2s] Now say **സൗകര്യം** (*saukaryaṁ*), "convenience". This lesson takes the sign in its
first syllable and puts it in your hand.

## Script you'll notice: ൗ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-SCRIPT-C344-AU-SIGN-01]; assesses=[ML-LEX-C344-SAUKARYAM-01, ML-SCRIPT-C342-AU-01, ML-SCRIPT-C340-II-01] -->

**ൗ** — the vowel sign *au*. It is printed after its consonant: **സ** + **ൗ** → **സൗ**, *sau*.

Its formal name is the *au* length mark. Older books also put **െ** in front of the consonant
for the same *au*, so you may meet both in print; they are read alike. The mark alone is the
modern spelling.

You have written this shape already, as the tail of **ഈ** and **ഔ**: a small lobe, a notch, and a
broader lobe sweeping back along its foot. Here it stands on its own.

## Writing: ൗ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ML-SCRIPT-C344-AU-SIGN-01] -->

One recorded hand writes **ൗ** in one stroke, with no lift: start low on the left and climb over
the small lobe, dip into the notch, climb over the broader lobe and down its right side, and sweep
back left along the foot.

Write **ൗ** yourself, slowly, and larger than it is printed. Copy the printed shape and keep the
order above.

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ML-SCRIPT-RECOG-139, ML-LEX-C344-SAUKARYAM-01, ML-SCRIPT-C344-AU-SIGN-01] -->

from? (**സൗകര്യം**, *saukaryaṁ*.) Which letters end in the same tail? (**ഈ**, **ഊ** and **ഔ**.)
