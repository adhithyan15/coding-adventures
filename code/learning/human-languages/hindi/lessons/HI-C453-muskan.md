## You'll want to know: मुस्कान
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C453-ANAND453-03]; assesses=[] -->

**मुस्कान** — *muskān* — "a smile".

> दादी की मुस्कान बहुत मीठी है।
>
> *dādī kī muskān bahut mīṭhī hai.*
>
> Grandmother has a very sweet smile.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**मुस्कान** (*muskān*) is the noun beside *muskānā*, "to smile", a by-form of *muskurānā*; CDIAL files *muskānā* under *\*muss*, a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 10227).
