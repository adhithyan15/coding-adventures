## Chapters 158-197: 200 A2 headwords, fifty of them verbs

The first of four Kannada A2 vocabulary tranches: forty chapters of five
words, in five runs, each closed by two review lessons. The word list was
drafted by theme, checked against every headword the course already teaches,
and reviewed by a native-level reader; words an earlier lesson already uses in
its text were left out, so the track gains no forward reference. Level gate:
361 headwords and 8 verbs short of A2.
