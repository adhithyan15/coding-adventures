## You'll want to know: किरकोळ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C288-GHATANA288-05]; assesses=[] -->

**किरकोळ** — *kirkoḷ* — "minor; petty".

> अपघात किरकोळ होता, कोणालाही लागलं नाही.
>
> *apghāt kirkoḷ hotā, koṇālāhī lāglaṁ nāhī.*
>
> The accident was minor; nobody was hurt.

किरकोळ keeps one form: किरकोळ अपघात, किरकोळ जखम. It also means retail: किरकोळ दुकान, a retail shop.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**किरकोळ** (*kirkoḷ*) is a Marathi word for small, scattered things, from retail goods to minor injuries; its origin is not settled.
