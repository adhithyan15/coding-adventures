## You'll want to know: તલવાર
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C321-TALVAR321-01]; assesses=[] -->

**તલવાર** — *talvār* — "a sword".

> સૈનિકના હાથમાં તલવાર હતી.
>
> *sainiknā hāthmā̃ talvār hatī.*
>
> The soldier had a sword in his hand.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**તલવાર** (*talvār*) goes back to Sanskrit *taravāri*, "a sword", and CDIAL lists Gujarati *tarvār*, "a one-edged sword" (CDIAL 5706); the *r* has become *l* in the spoken word.
