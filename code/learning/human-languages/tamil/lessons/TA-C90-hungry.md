# பசிக்கிறது (pasikkiṟatu) — (I) am hungry

## You'll want to know: பசிக்கிறது
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C90-MOOD-05]; assesses=[] -->

**பசிக்கிறது** (*pasikkiṟatu*) — "I am hungry", literally "it hungers".

It is **பசி**, "hunger", made into a verb. **எனக்குப் பசிக்கிறது** — "I'm hungry".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

Laughter, crying, tears, alone, and hunger as a verb.
