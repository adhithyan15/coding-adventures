## You'll want to know: さくひん
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C468-WORKS468-01]; assesses=[] -->

**さくひん** — *sakuhin* — "a work of art; a piece".

> **これは そふの さくひんです。** — *kore wa sofu no sakuhin desu* — "This is a work by my grandfather."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**さくひん** (*sakuhin*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *saku*, "make" (Middle Chinese *tsak*) and *hin*, "article" (*phimX*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
