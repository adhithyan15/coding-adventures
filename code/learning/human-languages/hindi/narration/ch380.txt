Hindi, chapter 380: Getting Out of Trouble.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
दिक़्क़त (diqqat) — a difficulty, a hassle.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for beware, then the Hindi for to slam.

You'll want to know: दिक़्क़त.
दिक़्क़त — diqqat — "a difficulty, a hassle".
अगर कोई दिक़्क़त होगी तो मुझे फ़ोन करो। agar koī diqqat hogī to mujhe fon karo. If there's any difficulty, phone me.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
दिक़्क़त (diqqat) is usually traced to Arabic diqqa, "fineness, exactness", which reached Hindi through Persian. The sense of a difficulty is a Persian and Hindi one.

अटकना — aṭaknā — "to get stuck, to be held up".
अगर दरवाज़ा अटकेगा तो उसे ज़ोर से धकेलो। agar darvāzā aṭkegā to use zor se dhakelo. If the door sticks, push it hard.
Past (it …): वह दरवाज़ा अटक गया, vah darvāzā aṭak gayā.

The word, taken apart.
अटकना (aṭaknā) is inherited from aṭṭakk, "obstruct, stop", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 182).

Lesson 5 of 5.
उबारना (ubārnā) — to pull out of trouble, to rescue.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a solution, then the Hindi for to get stuck.

You'll want to know: उबारना.
उबारना — ubārnā — "to pull out of trouble, to rescue".
अगर मीरा नहीं होती तो मुझे कौन उबारता? agar Mīrā nahīṃ hotī to mujhe kaun ubārtā? If Mira hadn't been there, who would have pulled me out?
Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने मुझे मुसीबत से उबारा, usne mujhe musībat se ubārā.

The word, taken apart.
उबारना (ubārnā) is inherited from udvārayati, "opens", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 2082). CDIAL glosses the Hindi verb "to release".
