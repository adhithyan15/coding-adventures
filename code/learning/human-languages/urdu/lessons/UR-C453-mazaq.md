## You'll want to know: مذاق
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C453-LAUGH453-05]; assesses=[] -->

**مذاق** — *mazāq* — "teasing; fun".

> میں نے یہ بات مذاق میں کہی۔
>
> *maiṅ ne yih bāt mazāq meṅ kahī.*
>
> I said it in fun.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**مذاق** (*mazāq*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *madhāq*, "taste"; the sense "fun, teasing" is the one Urdu gives it.
