## You'll want to know: संसद
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C357-SHASAN357-02]; assesses=[] -->

**संसद** — *saṁsad* — "parliament".

> संसदेत नव्या कायद्यावर चर्चा झाली.
>
> *saṁsdet navyā kāyadyāvar carcā jhālī.*
>
> The new law was discussed in parliament.

Feminine.

Feminine; its oblique is संसदे-: संसदेत, in parliament.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**संसद** (*saṁsad*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *saṁsad*, "an assembly, a session" (Monier-Williams).
