Spanish, chapter 604: Protestar.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
protestar — to protest; to complain.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for the justice, then the Spanish for unfair.

You'll want to know: protestar.
protestar — "to protest; to complain".
Los vecinos protestan por el ruido. The neighbours are protesting about the noise.
Protestar por or contra something.

The word, taken apart.
protestar is a learned borrowing from Latin protestari 'to declare publicly', built on testis 'witness' (Lewis & Short).

What you've built.
One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.

expresar is built on expreso (EtymDB), a learned borrowing from Latin expressus 'pressed out, clear', from exprimere, built on premere 'to press'.

You'll want to know: polémico.
polémico — "controversial".
Es un tema muy polémico. It is a very controversial subject.
La polémica — the controversy.

The word, taken apart.
polémico is a learned word usually traced to Greek polemikos 'warlike', built on polemos 'war', probably through French polémique; EtymDB does not list it.

Lesson 5 of 5.
reivindicar — to demand, to claim (a right).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for controversial, then the Spanish for to claim.

You'll want to know: reivindicar.
reivindicar — "to demand, to claim (a right)".
Los trabajadores reivindican mejores sueldos. The workers are demanding better pay.
Spelling: reivindiqué. La reivindicación — the demand.

The word, taken apart.
reivindicar is a learned word built from Latin rei, a form of res 'thing, property', and vindicare 'to claim' (Lewis & Short).
