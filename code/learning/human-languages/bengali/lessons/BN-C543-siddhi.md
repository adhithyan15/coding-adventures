## You'll want to know: সিদ্ধি
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C543-SIDDHI543-02]; assesses=[] -->

**সিদ্ধি** — *siddhi* — "success; attainment".

> অনেক বছরের চেষ্টার পরে বিজ্ঞানী সিদ্ধি পেলেন।
>
> *Ônek bôchhôrer cheshṭār pôre bigyānī siddhi pelen.*
>
> After many years of effort the scientist met with success.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**সিদ্ধি** (*siddhi*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *siddhi*, "accomplishment, success" (Monier-Williams).
