## You'll want to know: घोटा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C477-HAT477-05]; assesses=[] -->

**घोटा** — *ghoṭā* — "the ankle".

> जिन्यावरून उतरताना माझा घोटा वळला.
>
> *jinyāvarūn utartānā mājhā ghoṭā vaḷlā.*
>
> My ankle turned as I came down the stairs.

Masculine.

Masculine: घोट्याला, at the ankle.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**घोटा** (*ghoṭā*) is a Marathi noun whose origin is not settled.
