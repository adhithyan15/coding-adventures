Spanish, chapter 736: La cara.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
la frente — the forehead.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for the brain, then the Spanish for to beat.

You'll want to know: la frente.
la frente — "the forehead".
Tienes la frente caliente. Your forehead is hot.
La frente is the forehead; el frente is the front.

The word, taken apart.
frente is inherited from Latin frōns, frontem "forehead, front" (EtymDB), through Old Spanish fruente.

The word, taken apart.
mejilla is usually traced to Latin maxilla "jaw"; the Latin -x- became the Spanish j sound.

Levantar la barbilla — to lift your chin. El mentón means the same.

barbilla is built on barba "beard, chin" with the small ending -illa; barba is inherited from Latin barba (EtymDB).

Lesson 5 of 5.

la oreja — "the ear (outer)".
Lleva un pendiente en la oreja. He wears an earring in his ear.
La oreja is the outer ear; el oído is hearing and the inner ear.

The word, taken apart.
oreja is inherited from Latin auricula "little ear" through spoken oricla (EtymDB), a small form of auris "ear".
