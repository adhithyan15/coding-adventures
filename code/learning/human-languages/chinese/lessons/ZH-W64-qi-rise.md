## Script — the walking piece, carrying jǐ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-SCRIPT-QI-RISE-01]; assesses=[] -->

> 起

**Two pieces: the walking piece first, then the piece on its back.**

**The walking piece, seven strokes, read zǒu**, as in the word for to walk: a short horizontal, a vertical down through it, a longer horizontal; then below, a short vertical with a short bar to its right, a left-falling stroke, and last a long stroke that sweeps right along the whole bottom.

**The right piece, three strokes, read jǐ, 'self'**, not the jǐ of 机, sitting on the sweep: a horizontal that turns down, a short horizontal, then a vertical that bends right along the bottom and hooks up.

7 + 3 = ten.
