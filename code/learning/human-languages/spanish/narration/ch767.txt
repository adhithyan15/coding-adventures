Lesson 1 of 8.
meditar — to meditate; to ponder.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for the achievement, then the Spanish for talent.

You'll want to know: meditar.
meditar — "to meditate; to ponder".
Medito diez minutos cada mañana. I meditate for ten minutes every morning.
Also to think something over: meditar una decisión.

The word, taken apart.
meditar is a learned borrowing from Latin meditārī "to think over, to practise" (EtymDB).

Lesson 5 of 8.
el masaje — the massage.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for will, then the Spanish for to stretch.

You'll want to know: el masaje.
el masaje — "the massage".
Después del partido me dieron un masaje. After the match I had a massage.
Dar un masaje — to give a massage.

The word, taken apart.
masaje was borrowed from French massage (EtymDB), from masser "to knead". Beyond French the origin is not settled; Arabic and Greek sources have both been proposed.

Wrap-up Recall.

In your story, which tense set the scene? (The imperfect: llovía.) Which told the events? (The preterite: llegamos, comimos.) Why does feliz not change for ella? (It ends in a consonant.) What two things can esperar mean? (To wait and to hope.)
