## You'll want to know: гончар
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C502-CRAFTS-03]; assesses=[] -->

**гончар** (*gonchár*) — "a potter".

> Гончар делает красивые тарелки.
>
> *Gonchár délayet krasívyye tarélki.*
>
> The potter makes beautiful plates.

Masculine; genitive гончара.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*гончар* is inherited from Old East Slavic *gornьčarь*, derived from *gъrnьcь* 'pot' (EtymDB).
