## You'll want to know: ferrārius, ferrāriī
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C469-INCUS-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ferrārius, ferrāriī** — "a blacksmith, an ironworker".

> Ferrārius soleās ferreās equīs facit.
>
> *The blacksmith makes iron shoes for the horses.*

Second declension, masculine: *ferrārius, ferrāriī m.*, short for *faber ferrārius*. English *farrier* comes from it through French.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ferrārius* is built on *ferrum* 'iron' with the suffix *-ārius* (EtymDB; Lewis & Short). English *farrier*, a smith who shoes horses, comes from it through French.
