## You'll want to know: effringō, effringere
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C304-COMPREHENDO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**effringō, effringere** — "to break open".

> Fūr portam effrēgit et in domum intrāvit.
>
> *The thief broke the door open and went into the house.*

*effringō, effringere, effrēgī, effrāctum*: *ex* + *frangō* (taught). Plautus has doors smashed in this way.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**effringō** is built on *frangō*, "to break", with *ex-*, "out" (Lewis & Short); the vowel changes inside the compound.
