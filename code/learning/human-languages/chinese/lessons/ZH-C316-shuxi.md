## You'll want to know: shúxī
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C316-PLACE316-02]; assesses=[] -->

*shúxī* — "to know well; to be familiar with".

> *wǒ duì zhèr hái bù shúxī* — I don't know this place well yet.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*shúxī* is built from *shú* 'ripe' and *xī* 'in full', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *dzyuwk* and *sit* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'done' and the second 'in every detail'.
