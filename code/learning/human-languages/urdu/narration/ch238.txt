Urdu, chapter 238: Expert, Artist, Craftsman, Goldsmith, Potter.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
ماہر (māhir) — an expert.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a soldier; a constable, then the Urdu for an army.

You'll want to know: ماہر.
ماہر — māhir — "an expert".

What you've built.

Guided Practice.

Fog? (کہرا, kohrā.) Dew? (اوس, os.) Hail? (اولے, ole.) An earthquake? (زلزلہ, zalzalā.) A flood? (سیلاب, sailāb.)
