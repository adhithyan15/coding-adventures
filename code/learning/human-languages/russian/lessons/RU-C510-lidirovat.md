## You'll want to know: лидировать
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C510-RESULTS-05]; assesses=[] -->

**лидировать** (*lidírovat*) — "to be in the lead".

> После первого тайма лидирует наша команда.
>
> *Pósle pérvogo táyma lidíruyet násha kománda.*
>
> After the first half our team is in the lead.

Imperfective; present лидирую. Built on *лидер* — a leader.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*лидировать* is built on *лидер* 'a leader', which was borrowed from English *leader* (EtymDB), with *-ировать*; EtymDB has no entry for the verb.
