## You'll want to know: la vetta
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C462-MONTAGNA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**la vetta** — "the summit, the peak".

> Dalla vetta si vedono due laghi.
>
> *From the summit you can see two lakes.*

Like *la cima*, a little more literary.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*vetta* is traced in EtymDB to Latin *vitta* 'headband, ribbon'; it is inherited, and first meant the tip of a branch.
