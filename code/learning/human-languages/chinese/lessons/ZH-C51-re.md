## You'll want to know: rè
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C51-FEEL51-04]; assesses=[] -->

*rè* — "hot".

> *zhōngwǔ hěn rè* — It's hot at midday.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*rè* is inherited from Middle Chinese *nyet* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which the dictionary explains as burning, as if by fire.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying how you are.
