Latin, chapter 68: Sick, Tired, Hungry, Thirsty.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
aeger, aegra, aegrum — sick, ill.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Latin for medicine, then the Latin for an illness.

You'll want to know: aeger, aegra, aegrum.
aeger, aegra, aegrum — "sick, ill". The opposite of sānus.

What you've built.
One more word for sick, tired, hungry, thirsty.
