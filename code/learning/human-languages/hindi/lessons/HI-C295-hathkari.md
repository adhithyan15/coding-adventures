## You'll want to know: हथकड़ी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C295-ADALAT295-05]; assesses=[] -->

**हथकड़ी** — *hathkaṛī* — "handcuffs".

> पुलिस चोर को हथकड़ी में अदालत ले गई।
>
> *pulis cor ko hathkaṛī meṁ adālat le gaī.*
>
> The police took the thief to court in handcuffs.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**हथकड़ी** (*hathkaṛī*) joins **हाथ**, hand, with *kaṛī*, a metal ring, which CDIAL 2629 lists for Hindi under Sanskrit *kaṭa*: rings for the hands. The first part shortens, as **हाथ** does in compounds.
