Urdu, chapter 212: To iron, To grind, To fry, To boil, To taste.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
استری کرنا (istrī karnā) — to iron.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a servant, then the Urdu for a passer-by.

You'll want to know: استری کرنا.
استری کرنا — istrī karnā — "to iron".

The phrase, taken apart.
استری کرنا (istrī karnā) joins istrī, "an iron", usually traced to Portuguese estirar, "to stretch out", and karnā, "to do", from Sanskrit karoti, "does" (CDIAL 2814).

Lesson 4 of 5.
ابالنا (ubālnā) — to boil.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for to grind, then the Urdu for to fry.

You'll want to know: ابالنا.
ابالنا — ubālnā — "to boil".

The word, taken apart.
ابالنا (ubālnā) is a tadbhava, a word inherited and reshaped by sound change. It comes from ubbāl, "make boil", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 2339). Morgenstierne traced it to ud-, "up", and an Indo-European root "to boil"; CDIAL finds the forms hard to fit and notes other possible sources.
