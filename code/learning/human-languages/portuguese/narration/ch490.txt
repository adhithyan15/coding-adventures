Portuguese, chapter 490: Manter a forma.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
saudável — healthy.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for shiver, then the Portuguese for cramp.

You'll want to know: saudável.
saudável — "healthy".
Tento comer de forma saudável. I try to eat in a healthy way.
Plural saudáveis.

The word, taken apart.
saudável is built on saúde "health", which is inherited from Latin salūs "health, safety" (Lewis & Short).

What you've built.
One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.

Lesson 5 of 6.
a massagem — massage.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for to stretch, then the Portuguese for to sweat.

You'll want to know: a massagem.
a massagem — "massage".
Depois do jogo, o jogador fez uma massagem às costas. After the match, the player had a back massage.
Fazer uma massagem — to give or have a massage.

The word, taken apart.
massagem is usually traced to French massage, built on masser "to knead"; the source of the French verb is usually traced to Arabic, but it is not settled.
