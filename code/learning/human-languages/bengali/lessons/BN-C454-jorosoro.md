# জড়সড় (jôṛôsôṛô) — huddled up; shrinking (with shyness or cold)

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[BN-LEX-C454-SONGKOCH454-02, BN-LEX-C454-SONGKOCH454-03] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Bengali for being in two minds, then the Bengali for stiff and awkward.

## You'll want to know: জড়সড়
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C454-SONGKOCH454-04]; assesses=[] -->

**জড়সড়** — *jôṛôsôṛô* — "huddled up; shrinking (with shyness or cold)".

> শীতে কুকুরটা জড়সড় হয়ে বসে ছিল।
>
> *shīte kukurṭā jôṛôsôṛô hôye bôse chhilô.*
>
> The dog sat huddled up in the cold.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**জড়সড়** (*jôṛôsôṛô*) is a rhyming doubling whose first half probably belongs with Sanskrit *jaḍa*, "dull, numb with cold", under which CDIAL lists Bengali forms (CDIAL 5090).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
