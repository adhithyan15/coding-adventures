Portuguese, chapter 57: River, Lake, Stone, Sand, Island.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
o rio — a river.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for a mountain, then the Portuguese for a forest.

You'll want to know: o rio.
o rio — "a river". Rio de Janeiro is "the river of January".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: o rio]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: o rio, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say o rio]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Portuguese for a mountain, then the Portuguese for a forest, then say o rio again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does o rio mean? ("A river".) Say it once more.
