## You'll want to know: ગ્રંથ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C338-CHAPAV338-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ગ્રંથ** — *granth* — "a tome; a great book".

> પુસ્તકાલયમાં એક પ્રાચીન ગ્રંથ છે.
>
> *pustakālaymā̃ ek prācīn granth che.*
>
> There is an ancient tome in the library.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ગ્રંથ** (*granth*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *grantha*, "a knot", and then "a book", once leaves tied together (CDIAL 4350). It is a large or important book.
