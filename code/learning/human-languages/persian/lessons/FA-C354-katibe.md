## You'll want to know: کتیبه
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C354-KASHF-04]; assesses=[] -->

**کتیبه** (*katibe*) — "an inscription".

> روی سنگ یک کتیبه قدیمی بود.
>
> *Ru-ye sang yek katibe-ye qadimi bud.*
>
> There was an old inscription on the stone.

Carved on stone or set in tiles on a building.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*katibe* is usually traced to Arabic *katība* (EtymDB lists the Arabic word), built on the root *k-t-b* "to write", the root of *ketâb* "book".
