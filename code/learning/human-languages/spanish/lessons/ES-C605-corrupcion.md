## You'll want to know: la corrupción
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C605-PROBSOC-05]; assesses=[] -->

**la corrupción** — "the corruption".

> La gente está cansada de la corrupción.
>
> *People are tired of corruption.*

*Corrupto* — corrupt.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*corrupción* is a learned borrowing from Latin *corruptio* 'decay, spoiling', built on *corrumpere*, from *rumpere* 'to break' (EtymDB).
