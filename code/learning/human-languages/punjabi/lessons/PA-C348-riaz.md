## You'll want to know: ਰਿਆਜ਼
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C348-MUSIC348-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ਰਿਆਜ਼** — *riāz* — "music practice; regular practice".

> ਮੇਰੀ ਭੈਣ ਸਵੇਰੇ ਰਿਆਜ਼ ਕਰਦੀ ਹੈ।
>
> *merī bhaiṇ savere riāz kardī hai.*
>
> My sister practises her music in the morning.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਰਿਆਜ਼** (*riāz*) is a loan, usually traced through Persian to Arabic *riyāḍa*, "exercise".
