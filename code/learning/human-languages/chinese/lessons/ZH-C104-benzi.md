## You'll want to know: 本子
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C104-DESK104-01]; assesses=[] -->

**本子** — *běnzi* — "notebook".

> **我能用你的本子吗** *wǒ néng yòng nǐ de běnzi ma* — Could I use your notebook?

The new character, in pieces: 本 — the tree piece of 机, standing alone, with a short bar across its foot. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**本子** (*běnzi*) is built from 本 and the noun ending 子, both inherited from Middle Chinese, *pwonX* and *tsiX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription). The dictionary glosses the first 'root' and explains its shape as a tree with a mark at its foot; how it came to mean a book is not settled.
