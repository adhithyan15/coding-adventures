## You'll want to know: le soupçon
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C293-MYSTERE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**le soupçon** — "the suspicion; a hint".

> La police a des soupçons sur le voisin.
>
> *The police have suspicions about the neighbour.*

*Un soupçon de sel* — a hint of salt.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*soupçon* is inherited from Late Latin *suspectiō* "suspicion" (EtymDB), from *suspicere* "to look up at, to suspect".
