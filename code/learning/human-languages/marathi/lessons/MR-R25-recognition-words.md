# Recognition at distance: five things from further back

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MR-LEX-NUMBERS-ONE-TO-FIVE, MR-LEX-ASNE-AAHE] -->

[PAUSE 5s] Count to five out loud — *ek, don, tīn, chār, pāch* — and then put
**आहे** at the end of a sentence about yourself, where Marathi keeps it.

## Wrap-up Recall

then that you are learning it (**मी मराठी शिकतो / शिकते**). What did
**मित्र**'s root mean before it meant friend? (**To bind**.) Then stop.
