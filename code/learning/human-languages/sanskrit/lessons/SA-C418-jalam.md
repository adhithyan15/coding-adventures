## You'll want to know: जालम्
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C418-JALA418-01]; assesses=[] -->

**जालम्** — *jālam* — "a net".

> धीवरः नद्यां जालं क्षिपति।
>
> *dhīvaraḥ nadyāṃ jālaṃ kṣipati.*
>
> The fisherman casts a net into the river.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**जालम्** (*jālam*) is the word MW gives for a net for catching birds or fish; MW gives no derivation, so its origin is uncertain (MW).
