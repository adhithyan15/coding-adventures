## You'll want to know: વીણવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C537-PASANDGI537-04]; assesses=[] -->

**વીણવું** — *vīṇvũ* — "to pick out (one by one)".

> જો તમે સારી કેરીઓ વીણશો તો હું તે ખરીદીશ.
>
> *jo tame sārī kerīo vīṇśo to hũ te kharīdīś.*
>
> If you pick out the good mangoes, I'll buy them.

Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે કેરીઓ વીણી, *teṇe kerīo vīṇī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**વીણવું** (*vīṇvũ*) is inherited from Sanskrit *vícinōti*, "picks out, sorts", and CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb, "to pick, gather" (CDIAL 11686).

One more word for saying what happens if.
