## You'll want to know: مشاهد
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C356-JUMHUR356-02]; assesses=[] -->

**مشاهد** — *mushāhid* — "a viewer, a spectator".

> كتب مشاهد رسالة إلى القناة.
>
> *kataba mushāhid risāla ilā al-qanāh.*
>
> A viewer wrote a letter to the channel.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**مشاهد** (*mushāhid*) is built on the native Arabic root *sh-h-d* (Lane), "to be present, to witness". *Mushāhid* is the participle of the taught *shāhada*, to watch.
