Hindi, chapter 286: If, and Otherwise.
4 lessons. All 4 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 4.
अगर … तो … (agar … to …) — if, then.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say "if". (अगर, agar.) Then open a reply with "so, then": तो चलिए. The two words meet in this lesson.

You'll want to know: अगर … तो … with the future.
अगर बारिश होगी, तो हम घर पर रहेंगे। agar bāriś hogī, to ham ghar par raheṅge. — "If it rains, we will stay at home."
अगर opens the condition and तो opens what follows from it. When both halves are still to come, Hindi puts both in the future: होगी, रहेंगे. English says "if it rains"; Hindi says "if it will rain".
अगर तुम आओगे, तो मुझे अच्छा लगेगा। (agar tum āoge, to mujhe acchā lagegā.) — If you come, I'll be glad.
Keep both words: अगर at the start, तो at the turn.

You'll want to know: if it is so now.
अगर तुम्हें भूख लगी है, तो खाना खाओ। agar tumhẽ bhūkh lagī hai, to khānā khāo. — "If you're hungry, eat."
When the condition is true now, it stays in the present, and the second half can ask, advise or tell:
अगर समय है, तो आइए। (agar samay hai, to āie.) — If you have time, do come.
अगर आप बीमार हैं, तो आपको आराम करना चाहिए। (agar āp bīmār haiṁ, to āpko ārām karnā cāhiye.) — If you're ill, you should rest.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: "If you come, I'll be glad" — agar tum āoge, to mujhe acchā lagegā]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: "If you have time, do come" — agar samay hai, to āie]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: "If you're ill, you should rest" — agar āp bīmār haiṁ, to āpko ārām karnā cāhiye]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[question — say your answer, then pause 26 seconds]
Say that if it rains you will stay at home; then tell a friend: if you're hungry, eat.

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
Which word opens the condition? (अगर.) Which opens what follows? (तो.) "If it rains, we will stay at home"? (अगर बारिश होगी, तो हम घर पर रहेंगे: the future in both halves.) "If you're hungry, eat"? (अगर तुम्हें भूख लगी है, तो खाना खाओ.)


Lesson 2 of 4.
अगर ट्रेन छूट गई, तो … (agar ṭren chūṭ gaī, to …) — if it happens, and otherwise.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say "I missed my train". (मेरी ट्रेन छूट गई.) Then "if it rains, we will stay at home". (अगर बारिश होगी, तो हम घर पर रहेंगे.)

You'll want to know: the plain past after अगर.
अगर मेरी ट्रेन छूट गई, तो मैं बस से आऊँगा। agar merī ṭren chūṭ gaī, to maiṁ bas se āūṅgā. — "If I miss my train, I'll come by bus."
For one event that may or may not happen, Hindi can put it in the plain past after अगर, as if it had already happened: छूट गई. The result stays in the future. It is the past of a story, used to try a case out.
अगर आपको देर हो गई, तो मुझे फ़ोन कीजिए। (agar āpko der ho gaī, to mujhe fon kījiye.) — If you're running late, phone me.
अगर उसने हाँ कहा, तो हम कल चलेंगे। (agar usne hā̃ kahā, to ham kal caleṅge.) — If she says yes, we'll go tomorrow.

You'll want to know: नहीं तो, otherwise.
जल्दी चलो, नहीं तो देर हो जाएगी। jaldī calo, nahīṃ to der ho jāegī. — "Hurry up, otherwise we'll be late."
नहीं तो (nahīṃ to) is "if not, then": a warning about what follows if the first part does not happen. The first part is often a request. The warning is in the future: here हो जाएगी, the future of हो गई.
खाना खाओ, नहीं तो भूख लगेगी। (khānā khāo, nahīṃ to bhūkh lagegī.) — Eat, or you'll get hungry.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: "If you're running late, phone me" — agar āpko der ho gaī, to mujhe fon kījiye]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: "If she says yes, we'll go tomorrow" — agar usne hā̃ kahā, to ham kal caleṅge]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: "Eat, or you'll get hungry" — khānā khāo, nahīṃ to bhūkh lagegī]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[question — say your answer, then pause 26 seconds]
Say that if you miss your train you will come by bus, then warn a friend: hurry up, otherwise we'll be late.

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
"If I miss my train"? (अगर मेरी ट्रेन छूट गई.) Which tense goes in the result? (The future.) "Otherwise"? (नहीं तो.) "We'll be late"? (देर हो जाएगी.)


Lesson 3 of 4.
वरना (varnā) — otherwise, or else.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say "hurry up, otherwise we'll be late". (जल्दी चलो, नहीं तो देर हो जाएगी.)

You'll want to know: वरना.
वरना — varnā — "otherwise, or else".
जल्दी उठो, वरना ट्रेन छूट जाएगी। jaldī uṭho, varnā ṭren chūṭ jāegī. — "Get up early, or you'll miss the train."
It does the work of नहीं तो in one word, and stands in the same place, before the warning.
धीरे बोलो, वरना बच्चा जाग जाएगा। (dhīre bolo, varnā baccā jāg jāegā.) — Speak softly, or the child will wake up.

The word, taken apart.
वरना (varnā) is usually traced to Persian varna, "otherwise". It ends like a Hindi verb in -ना, but it is not one, and it never changes.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: varnā]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: "Get up early, or you'll miss the train" — jaldī uṭho, varnā ṭren chūṭ jāegī]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: "Speak softly, or the child will wake up" — dhīre bolo, varnā baccā jāg jāegā]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does वरना (varnā) mean? ("Otherwise, or else".) Which two words does it stand for? (नहीं तो.) Is it a verb? (No: it never changes.)


Lesson 4 of 4.
Review — if, and otherwise.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say "if it rains, we will stay at home". (agar bāriś hogī, to ham ghar par raheṅge.) Then "or else". (varnā.)

Guided Practice.
[pause 2 seconds after each]
[your turn — say: I hope it won't rain tomorrow. If it rains, we will stay at home.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: If you have time, come to the market with me.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: If I miss the bus, I'll have to come by train.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: It has been raining since the morning. If it doesn't stop, we will stay at home.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: Eat now, otherwise you'll be hungry later.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: Leave early, or you'll be late.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
"If it rains, we will stay at home"? (अगर बारिश होगी, तो हम घर पर रहेंगे.) "If you have time, come"? (अगर समय है, तो आइए.) "If I miss the bus"? (अगर मेरी बस छूट गई.) "Otherwise"? (नहीं तो, or वरना (varnā).)
