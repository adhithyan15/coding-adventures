6 lessons. You can do the first 2 of them in the car; after that you will want to have stopped.


Lesson 1 of 6.
shíxiàn — to come true; to make real.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for to be wasted (of effort), then the Mandarin for staying the same.

You'll want to know: shíxiàn.
shíxiàn — "to come true; to make real".
yàoshi yǒu qián wǒ de mèngxiǎng jiù néng shíxiàn — If I had the money, my dream could come true.
Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
shíxiàn is built from shí 'real' and 现 'to appear', both inherited from Middle Chinese, zyit and henH (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'full' and 'true' and the second 'to show'.

What you've built.

Lesson 3 of 6.
取 (qǔ) — the ear piece, then 又.

Before we start: this one needs your hands, so it is not a driving lesson. You will want a pen and something to write on and your eyes, for letter shapes on the page. You can listen to everything else now. I will say so again when we reach them, but leave these sections until you have stopped: Warm-up, Script — the ear piece, then 又 and Guided Practice.

变 又.
[once you have stopped driving — read: each one aloud, with nothing said first — biàn, yòu]

[once you have stopped driving — this part needs your eyes or your hands; if you are driving, skip ahead to the next part]
Script — the ear piece, then 又.
取 (qǔ).
Two pieces, left before right.
The left piece, the ear piece, six strokes. A bar across the top. A short vertical down on the left. A long vertical down on the right, past the foot. Two short bars between them. Then a stroke rising to the right at the foot.
The right piece, 又, two strokes, as at the foot of 变: a bar that turns and falls to the lower left, then a long stroke falling to the right across it.
6 + 2 = eight.

[once you have stopped driving — this part needs your eyes or your hands; if you are driving, skip ahead to the next part]
Guided Practice.
[once you have stopped driving — write: 取 (qǔ), all eight, with the model in front of you]
[once you have stopped driving — cover: write it again from memory, and count the pieces, not the strokes]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
How many strokes in 取 (qǔ)? (Eight.) Which vertical is longer? (The right one, past the foot.) What is the right piece? (又, as at the foot of 变.)
Source: Hanzi Writer Data 取 (qǔ), pinned stroke paths and medians.


Lesson 4 of 6.
成果 (chéngguǒ) — fruits of work; achievements.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for to come true, then the Mandarin for to obtain (through effort).

You'll want to know: 成果.
成果 — chéngguǒ — "fruits of work; achievements".
我们今年取得了很好的成果 wǒmen jīnnián qǔdé le hěn hǎo de chéngguǒ — We got good results this year.

The word, taken apart.
成果 (chéngguǒ) is built from 成 'to complete' and 果 'fruit', both inherited from Middle Chinese, dzyeng and kwaX (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'the fruit of a tree' and 'resolute'.
