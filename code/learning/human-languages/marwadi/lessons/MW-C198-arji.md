## You'll want to know: अरजी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C198-OFFICE198-03]; assesses=[] -->

**अरजी** — *arjī* — "an application".

> हूं अरजी लिखूं हूं।
>
> *hū̃ arjī likhū̃ hū̃.*
>
> I am writing an application.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अरजी** (*arjī*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *ʿarḍ*, "a presenting": an application lays a matter before an officer.
