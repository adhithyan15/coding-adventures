## You'll want to know: しつれい
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C327-REIGI327-03]; assesses=[] -->

**しつれい** — *shitsurei* — "rude; impolite".

> **ともだちに うそを 言ったら、しつれいです。** — *tomodachi ni uso o ittara, shitsurei desu* — "Lying to a friend is rude."

A na-adjective: な before a noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**しつれい** (*shitsurei*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *shitsu*, "lose" and *rei*, "bow" (Middle Chinese *lejX*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
