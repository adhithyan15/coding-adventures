## You'll want to know: offendō, offendere
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C314-OFFENDO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**offendō, offendere** — "to strike against, to stumble; to offend".

> Nocte pedem ad saxum offendī et cecidī.
>
> *At night I struck my foot against a stone and fell.*

*offendō, offendere, offendī, offēnsum*. First a knock against something, then hurt feelings. English *offend*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**offendō** joins *ob-*, "against", and an old verb *\*fendō*, "to strike" (EtymDB), also seen in *dēfendō*, "to ward off". English *offend* comes from it.
