## You'll want to know: руководить
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C357-SOTRUDNIK-04]; assesses=[] -->

**руководить** (*rukovodít*) — "to manage, to be in charge of".

> Она руководит большой командой.
>
> *Oná rukovodít bolshóy komándoy.*
>
> She manages a large team.

Imperfective; present *руковожу, руководишь*. It takes the instrumental: *руководить отделом*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**руководить** is a compound of *рука* "hand" and *водить* "to lead", from Proto-Slavic *\*voditi* "to lead" (EtymDB): to lead by the hand.
