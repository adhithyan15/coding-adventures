Latin, chapter 151: Proud, Kind, Cruel, Ugly, Beautiful.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
superbus, superba, superbum — proud.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Latin for timid, shy, then the Latin for bold.

You'll want to know: superbus, superba, superbum.
superbus, superba, superbum — "proud".

The word, taken apart.
superbus is built on super, "above" (Lewis & Short): one who sets himself above others. Greek hyperbios, "overbearing", is a close parallel (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 2 of 5.
benignus, benigna, benignum — kind.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Latin for bold, then the Latin for proud.

You'll want to know: benignus, benigna, benignum.
benignus, benigna, benignum — "kind".

The word, taken apart.
benignus is usually explained as bene, "well", plus the root of genus, "birth, kind" (Lewis & Short), on the model of malignus, "ill-natured": kindly by nature.

Lesson 3 of 5.
crūdēlis, crūdēle — cruel.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Latin for proud, then the Latin for kind.

You'll want to know: crūdēlis, crūdēle.
crūdēlis, crūdēle — "cruel".

The word, taken apart.
crūdēlis is built on crūdus, "raw, bloody" (Lewis & Short), usually traced to Proto-Indo-European krewh2-, "raw flesh, blood" (EtymDB 2.1). English raw is a cousin.

Lesson 5 of 5.
fōrmōsus, fōrmōsa, fōrmōsum — beautiful.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Latin for cruel, then the Latin for ugly.

You'll want to know: fōrmōsus, fōrmōsa, fōrmōsum.
fōrmōsus, fōrmōsa, fōrmōsum — "beautiful".

The word, taken apart.
fōrmōsus is built on fōrma, "shape, beauty" (Lewis & Short), with -ōsus, "full of": well-shaped. Where fōrma comes from is uncertain; a link with Greek morphē, "shape", is often suggested (EtymDB 2.1).
