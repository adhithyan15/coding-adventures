## You'll want to know: सम्मार्जनम्
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C510-AVAKARA510-05]; assesses=[] -->

**सम्मार्जनम्** — *sammārjanam* — "a thorough cleaning".

> उत्सवात् पूर्वं गृहस्य सम्मार्जनं भवति।
>
> *utsavāt pūrvaṃ gṛhasya sammārjanaṃ bhavati.*
>
> Before the festival the house gets a thorough cleaning.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सम्मार्जनम्** (*sammārjanam*) is built on *sam*, "thoroughly", and the root √mṛj, "to wipe, to clean", which also gives *mārjayati*; MW gives *sammārjana* as sweeping or cleansing thoroughly (MW).
