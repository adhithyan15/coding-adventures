## You'll want to know: sānō, sānāre
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C526-SANO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**sānō, sānāre** — "to heal, cure".

> Medicus vulnus mīlitis sānat.
>
> *The doctor heals the soldier's wound.*

*sānō, sānāre, sānāvī, sānātum*, from *sānus*. English *sanatorium*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**sānō** is built on *sānus* "sound, healthy" (Lewis & Short; EtymDB). English *sanatorium* comes from it.
