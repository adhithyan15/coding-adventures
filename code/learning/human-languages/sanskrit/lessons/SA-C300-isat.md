# ईषत् (īṣat) — slightly

## You'll want to know: ईषत्
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C300-BHUKAMPA300-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ईषत्** — *īṣat* — "slightly".

> प्रथमं भूमिः ईषत् कम्पते।
>
> *prathamaṃ bhūmiḥ īṣat kampate.*
>
> At first the ground shakes slightly.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ईषत्** (*īṣat*) MW gives *īṣat* as an indeclinable meaning a little, slightly, without a derivation, so its origin is uncertain (MW).
