## You'll want to know: ക്യൂ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C461-YATRA461-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ക്യൂ** — *kyū* — "a queue".

> ടിക്കറ്റിനായി വലിയ ക്യൂ ഉണ്ടായിരുന്നു.
>
> *ṭikkaṟṟināyi valiya kyū uṇṭāyirunnu.*
>
> There was a long queue for tickets.

You stand in a queue: ക്യൂവിൽ നിൽക്കുക.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ക്യൂ** (*kyū*) was borrowed from English *queue*, a line of people waiting their turn.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for getting around.
