## You'll want to know: l'avalanche
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C303-SEISME-02]; assesses=[] -->

**l'avalanche** — "the avalanche".

> Une avalanche a fermé la route.
>
> *An avalanche closed the road.*

Figurative: *une avalanche de lettres*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*avalanche* was borrowed from Franco-Provençal *avalançhe* (EtymDB), an Alpine word, shaped by *avaler* "to go down".
