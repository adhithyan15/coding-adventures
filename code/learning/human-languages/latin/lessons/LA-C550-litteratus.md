## You'll want to know: litterātus, litterāta, litterātum
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C550-LIBR-01]; assesses=[] -->

**litterātus, litterāta, litterātum** — "learned, well-read".

> Vir litterātus multōs librōs habet.
>
> *The learned man has many books.*

From *litterae* — letters, learning. English *literate*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**litterātus** is built on *littera* "a letter" (Lewis & Short; EtymDB): lettered, learned. English *literate* comes from it.
