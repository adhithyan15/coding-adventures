## You'll want to know: のばす
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C168-DELAY168-04]; assesses=[] -->

**のばす** — *nobasu* — "to put off; to lengthen".

> **らいしゅうの よていを のばします。** — *raishū no yotei o nobashimasu* — "I will put off next week's plans."

Polite **のばします**, *nobashimasu*; a godan verb.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**のばす** (*nobasu*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi, and its older origin is not settled (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
