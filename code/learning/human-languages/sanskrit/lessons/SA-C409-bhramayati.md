## You'll want to know: भ्रमयति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C409-CAKRA409-04]; assesses=[] -->

**भ्रमयति** — *bhramayati* — "sets spinning; turns round".

> बालः चक्रं भ्रमयति।
>
> *bālaḥ cakraṃ bhramayati.*
>
> The boy sets the wheel spinning.

Past (he …): सः चक्रं भ्रमितवान्, *saḥ cakraṃ bhramitavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**भ्रमयति** (*bhramayati*) is built on the root √bhram, "to wander, to turn", which gives *bhramati*, "wanders"; MW gives the causative participle *bhramita* as made to go round, whirled round (MW).
