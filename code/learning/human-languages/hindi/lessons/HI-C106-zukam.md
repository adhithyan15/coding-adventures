## You'll want to know: ज़ुकाम
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C106-UNWELL-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ज़ुकाम** (*zukām*) — "a cold", the kind with a runny nose.

**मुझे ज़ुकाम है** — "I have a cold", literally "to me there is a cold". The person sits in
**मुझे**, as in **मुझे चाय चाहिए**.

## What you've built
