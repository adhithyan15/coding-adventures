Urdu, chapter 508: Heat in the Pan.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
بگھارنا (baghārnā) — to temper (with hot spiced oil).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for to pat (into shape), then the Urdu for the heat of a flame.

You'll want to know: بگھارنا.
بگھارنا — baghārnā — "to temper (with hot spiced oil)".
امی نے زیرے سے دال بگھاری۔ ammī ne zīre se dāl baghārī. Mum tempered the lentils with cumin.
Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے دال بگھاری, us ne dāl baghārī.

The word, taken apart.
بگھارنا (baghārnā) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin baghārnā, "to brown onions and spices in hot oil", under Sanskrit vyaghārayati (CDIAL 12191).

Lesson 2 of 5.
بھوننا (bhūnnā) — to roast; to brown (in a pan).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for the heat of a flame, then the Urdu for to temper (with hot spiced oil).

You'll want to know: بھوننا.
بھوننا — bhūnnā — "to roast; to brown (in a pan)".
اس نے کڑاہی میں مونگ پھلی بھونی۔ us ne kaṛāhī meṅ mūṅg phalī bhūnī. She roasted peanuts in the wok.
Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے گوشت بھونا, us ne gosht bhūnā.

The word, taken apart.
بھوننا (bhūnnā) is probably inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousins bhujnā and bhūjnā, "fries, parches", under Sanskrit bhṛjjati, "roasts" (CDIAL 9583); the form bhūnnā, with its -n-, is not among its Hindi rows, so the link is likely rather than certain.

Lesson 3 of 5.
ڈھکن (ḍhakkan) — a lid.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for to temper (with hot spiced oil), then the Urdu for to roast in a pan.

You'll want to know: ڈھکن.
ڈھکن — ḍhakkan — "a lid".
دیگچی کا ڈھکن کہاں ہے؟ degchī kā ḍhakkan kahāṅ hai? Where is the lid of the cooking pot?
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
ڈھکن (ḍhakkan) is inherited from ḍhakk, "cover", a form CDIAL reconstructs, which also gives the Hindi cousin ḍhā̃knā, "to cover" (CDIAL 5574).

Lesson 5 of 5.
کفگیر (kafgīr) — a skimmer (a flat spoon).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a lid, then the Urdu for to cover.

You'll want to know: کفگیر.
کفگیر — kafgīr — "a skimmer (a flat spoon)".
کفگیر چولہے کے پاس ہے۔ kafgīr chūlhe ke pās hai. The skimmer is next to the stove.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
کفگیر (kafgīr) is usually traced to Persian kafgīr, "a skimmer", built from kaf, "foam", and gīr-, "to take".
