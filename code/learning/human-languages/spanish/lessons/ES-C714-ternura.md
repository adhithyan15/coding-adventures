## You'll want to know: la ternura
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C714-CARINO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**la ternura** — "tenderness".

> Mira a su hijo con ternura.
>
> *She looks at her son with tenderness.*

*Me da ternura* — it melts my heart.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ternura* is built on *tierno* "tender" with the ending *-ura*; *tierno* is inherited from Latin *tener* "soft, delicate" (EtymDB).
