Telugu, chapter 419: If Not, Then What.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
కాకపోతే (kākapōtē) — if not; otherwise.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for unavoidable, then the Telugu for if possible.

You'll want to know: కాకపోతే.
కాకపోతే — kākapōtē — "if not; otherwise".
బస్సు దొరికితే వస్తాను, కాకపోతే రేపు వస్తాను. bassu dorikitē vastānu, kākapōtē rēpu vastānu. If I get a bus I will come; if not, I will come tomorrow.
A cousin of లేకపోతే: if it does not turn out so.

The word, taken apart.
కాకపోతే (kākapōtē) is native Dravidian: kā-, a stem of avu, "to be" (DEDR 333), and pōtē, "if it goes", a form of pōvu, "to go" (DEDR 4572).

Lesson 2 of 5.
బట్టి (baṭṭi) — depending on; going by.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for if possible, then the Telugu for if not.

You'll want to know: బట్టి.
బట్టి — baṭṭi — "depending on; going by".
ధరను బట్టి నేను కొంటాను. dharanu baṭṭi nēnu koṇṭānu. I will buy it depending on the price.
The thing depended on takes -ని: ధరను బట్టి (baṭṭi).

The word, taken apart.
బట్టి (baṭṭi) is native Dravidian, usually taken as a form of paṭṭu, "to hold, to take hold of" (DEDR 4034): holding to a thing, going by it.

Lesson 3 of 5.
మినహాయింపు (minahāyimpu) — an exemption.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for if not, then the Telugu for depending on.

You'll want to know: మినహాయింపు.
మినహాయింపు — minahāyimpu — "an exemption".
విద్యార్థులకు ఫీజులో మినహాయింపు ఇచ్చారు. vidyārthulaku phījulō minahāyimpu iccāru. Students were given an exemption from the fee.
The noun of మినహాయించు, to exempt, which is built on మినహా, except: -ఇంపు makes a noun of an -ఇంచు verb.

The word, taken apart.
మినహాయింపు (minahāyimpu) is built on మినహా, "except", which is usually traced to Hindustani minhā, of Arabic origin, with -ఇంపు, which makes a noun of the verb మినహాయించు, to exempt.

Lesson 4 of 5.
ఏమైనప్పటికీ (ēmainappaṭikī) — in any case; whatever happens.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for depending on, then the Telugu for an exemption.

You'll want to know: ఏమైనప్పటికీ.
ఏమైనప్పటికీ — ēmainappaṭikī — "in any case; whatever happens".
ఏమైనప్పటికీ నేను పరీక్ష రాస్తాను. ēmainappaṭikī nēnu parīkṣa rāstānu. Whatever happens, I will sit the exam.
ఏమి, what, + అయినప్పటికీ, even if it is. Close to ఏదేమైనా, which you know.

The word, taken apart.
ఏమైనప్పటికీ (ēmainappaṭikī) is native Dravidian: ēmi, "what" (DEDR 5151), and ayinappaṭikī, "even though it is", built on avu, "to be" (DEDR 333).

Lesson 5 of 5.
సంభవించు (sambhaviñcu) — to occur; to take place.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for an exemption, then the Telugu for in any case.

You'll want to know: సంభవించు.
సంభవించు — sambhaviñcu — "to occur; to take place".
ఒకవేళ ప్రమాదం సంభవిస్తే, వెంటనే పోలీసులకు చెప్పాలి. okavēḷa pramādaṁ sambhavistē, veṇṭanē pōlīsulaku ceppāli. If an accident occurs, you must tell the police at once.
Past (it …): సంభవించింది, sambhaviñcindi.
Formal: for accidents and events, so its past is shown as సంభవించింది, it occurred. In talk, జరుగు.

The word, taken apart.
సంభవించు (sambhaviñcu) is built on Sanskrit saṁbhava, "origin", and also "possibility" (CDIAL 12963), with the Telugu verb ending -ఇంచు.
