Lesson 1 of 5.
چوراہا (chaurāhā) — a crossroads.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a lane, a narrow street, then the Urdu for a turn, a bend.

You'll want to know: چوراہا.
چوراہا — chaurāhā — "a crossroads".

The word, taken apart.
چوراہا (chaurāhā) is usually explained as chau-, "four", with Persian rāh, "road". CDIAL does not list it; it lists cauhaṭṭā, "a crossing of market streets", from catur-, "four" (CDIAL 4626).

Lesson 2 of 5.
آس پاس (ās pās) — around, nearby.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a turn, a bend, then the Urdu for a crossroads.

You'll want to know: آس پاس.
آس پاس — ās pās — "around, nearby".

The phrase, taken apart.
آس پاس (ās pās) is built on pās, "near", from Sanskrit pārśve, "near", a form of pārśva, "side" (CDIAL 8118). ās is a rhyming partner put in front of it; its own source is not settled.

ساتھ — sāth — "with, together".

The word, taken apart.
ساتھ (sāth) is inherited. It comes from Sanskrit sārthena, "in company with", a form of sārtha, "caravan, company" (CDIAL 13364).

Lesson 5 of 5.
تھانہ (thānā) — a police station.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for with, then the Urdu for a market.

You'll want to know: تھانہ.
تھانہ — thānā — "a police station".

The word, taken apart.
تھانہ (thānā) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from sthāna, "firm stance; place of abode" (CDIAL 13753). CDIAL glosses the Hindi word "police station".
