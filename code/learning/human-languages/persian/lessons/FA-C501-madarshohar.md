## You'll want to know: مادرشوهر
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C501-INLAWS-02]; assesses=[] -->

**مادرشوهر** (*mâdarshohar*) — "a mother-in-law (husband's mother)".

> مادرشوهرش زن مهربانی است.
>
> *mâdarshoharash zan-e mehrabâni ast.*
>
> Her mother-in-law is a kind woman.

*mâdar* 'mother' + *shohar* 'husband'. A wife's mother is *mâdarzan*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*mâdarshohar* is built on two native words: *mâdar*, 'mother', inherited from Middle Persian, with Sanskrit *mātṛ* and Russian *mat'* among its cousins (EtymDB), and *shohar*, 'husband'.
