Spanish, chapter 533: Por casualidad.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
la casualidad — chance, coincidence.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for to hide, then the Spanish for the secret.

You'll want to know: la casualidad.
la casualidad — "chance, coincidence".
Nos vimos por casualidad en el mercado. We met by chance at the market.
Por casualidad — by chance. ¡Qué casualidad! — what a coincidence!

The word, taken apart.
casualidad is built on casual 'happening by chance', from Latin cāsuālis, from cāsus 'a fall, a chance' (Lewis & Short), and cāsus is built on cadere 'to fall'.

Lesson 2 of 5.
la coincidencia — the coincidence.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for the secret, then the Spanish for chance.

You'll want to know: la coincidencia.
la coincidencia — "the coincidence".
No fue una coincidencia. It was not a coincidence.
Coincidir is to coincide or to agree.

The word, taken apart.
coincidencia is built on coincidir, from Medieval Latin coincidere 'to fall together', usually explained as co- and incidere 'to fall upon' (Lewis & Short), built on cadere 'to fall'.

Lesson 3 of 5.

You'll want to know: fortuito.
fortuito — "accidental, by chance".
Fue un hallazgo fortuito: buscaba otra cosa. It was a chance find: I was looking for something else.
Un caso fortuito is an accident nobody could foresee.

The word, taken apart.
fortuito is a learned borrowing from Latin fortuītus 'happening by chance' (EtymDB), built on fors 'chance, luck' (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 4 of 5.
inesperado — unexpected.

Warm-up.

Before the new one: say the Spanish for the coincidence, then the Spanish for accidental.

You'll want to know: inesperado.
inesperado — "unexpected".
Recibimos una visita inesperada. We had an unexpected visit.

The word, taken apart.
inesperado is built on esperado, the participle of esperar, with in- 'not'. Esperar is inherited from Latin spērāre 'to hope' (Lewis & Short).
