## You'll want to know: सराय
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C201-TOWN201-04]; assesses=[] -->

**सराय** — *sarāy* — "an inn".

> सराय टेसण सामी है।
>
> *sarāy ṭesaṇ sāmī hai.*
>
> The inn is opposite the station.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सराय** (*sarāy*) is usually traced to Persian *sarāy*, "a palace; an inn".
