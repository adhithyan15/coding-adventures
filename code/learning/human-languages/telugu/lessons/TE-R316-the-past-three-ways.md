# Review — the past three ways

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[TE-GRAMMAR-C316-PAST-SIMPLE-01, TE-GRAMMAR-C316-PAST-STEMS-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "Yesterday I went to the town." (*ninna nēnu paṭṭaṇāniki veḷḷānu*.) Then "she saw" and "I
ate". (*cūsindi*, *tinnānu*.)

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[TE-GRAMMAR-C316-PAST-SIMPLE-01, TE-GRAMMAR-C316-PAST-STEMS-01, TE-GRAMMAR-C316-SCENE-EVENT-01, TE-GRAMMAR-C316-PROGRESSIVE-PAST-01, TE-GRAMMAR-C316-PAST-HABITUAL-01, TE-GRAMMAR-C316-UNDEDI-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s each]
- [YOU SAY: We drank tea, and after that we saw a film.]
- [YOU SAY: While I was reading a book, Mother called.]
- [YOU SAY: At nine last night I was at home.]
- [YOU SAY: In my childhood I used to walk to school every day.]
- [YOU SAY: There used to be a small shop in our village. Now there is a big restaurant.]
- [YOU SAY: one thing you were doing yesterday evening, with -తున్నప్పుడు, and one thing that cut into it]

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[TE-GRAMMAR-C316-PAST-SIMPLE-01, TE-GRAMMAR-C316-SCENE-EVENT-01, TE-GRAMMAR-C316-PROGRESSIVE-PAST-01, TE-GRAMMAR-C316-PAST-HABITUAL-01, TE-GRAMMAR-C316-UNDEDI-01] -->

[PAUSE 3s] Which past is the event? (The plain past: **వెళ్ళాను**, **వచ్చింది**.) Which is the scene?
(**-తున్నప్పుడు**, or **-తున్నాను** with a past time.) Which is the habit? (**-ఏవాడిని**, **-ఏదాన్ని**.) "There
used to be"? (**ఉండేది**.)
