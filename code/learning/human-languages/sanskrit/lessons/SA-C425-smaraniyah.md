## You'll want to know: स्मरणीयः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C425-SMARAKA425-05]; assesses=[] -->

**स्मरणीयः** — *smaraṇīyaḥ* — "memorable".

> मम तीर्थयात्रा स्मरणीया अस्ति।
>
> *mama tīrthayātrā smaraṇīyā asti.*
>
> My pilgrimage is memorable.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**स्मरणीयः** (*smaraṇīyaḥ*) is built on the root √smṛ, "to remember", which also gives *smarati*, "remembers", with the ending *-anīya*; MW gives *smaraṇīya* as to be remembered, memorable (MW).
