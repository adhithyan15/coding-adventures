Kannada, chapter 476: Wildlife Sanctuaries.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ವನ್ಯಜೀವಿ (vanyajīvi) — wildlife; a wild animal.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to fell, then the Kannada for to conserve.

You'll want to know: ವನ್ಯಜೀವಿ.
ವನ್ಯಜೀವಿ — vanyajīvi — "wildlife; a wild animal".
ವನ್ಯಜೀವಿಗಳು ರಾತ್ರಿ ಕಾಡಿನ ರಸ್ತೆಯನ್ನು ದಾಟುತ್ತವೆ. vanyajīvigaḷu rātri kāḍina rasteyannu dāṭuttave. Wild animals cross the forest road at night.
Plural: ವನ್ಯಜೀವಿಗಳು, vanyajīvigaḷu.
ವನ್ಯ, of the forest, plus ಜೀವಿ, a living being.

The word, taken apart.
ವನ್ಯಜೀವಿ (vanyajīvi) is a modern compound built from Sanskrit words: vanya, "growing in a forest, wild" (CDIAL 11277), and jīvin, "living", on jīva, "a living being" (CDIAL 5239).

ಜೀವವೈವಿಧ್ಯ (jīvavaividhya) is a modern compound built from Sanskrit jīva, "a living being, life" (CDIAL 5239), and vaividhya, "variety", from vividha, "of many kinds".

Lesson 5 of 5.
ವಲಸೆ ಹೋಗು (valase hōgu) — to migrate.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a bird sanctuary, then the Kannada for biodiversity.

You'll want to know: ವಲಸೆ ಹೋಗು.
ವಲಸೆ ಹೋಗು — valase hōgu — "to migrate".
ಕೆಲವು ಹಕ್ಕಿಗಳು ಚಳಿಗಾಲದಲ್ಲಿ ದಕ್ಷಿಣಕ್ಕೆ ವಲಸೆ ಹೋಗುತ್ತವೆ. kelavu hakkigaḷu caḷigāladalli dakṣiṇakke valase hōguttave. Some birds migrate south in winter.
Past (I …): ವಲಸೆ ಹೋದೆ, valase hōde.
Of birds, and of people who leave home for work: ವಲಸೆ ಕಾರ್ಮಿಕ, a migrant worker.

The word, taken apart.
ವಲಸೆ ಹೋಗು (valase hōgu) is native Dravidian: DEDR 5278 lists Kannada valase, "flight, removal from home, emigration", here with ಹೋಗು, to go.
