## You'll want to know: frīgō, frīgere
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C354-ASSUS-04]; assesses=[] -->

**frīgō, frīgere** — "to fry, to parch".

> Coquus piscēs in oleō frīxit.
>
> *The cook fried the fish in oil.*

*frīgō, frīgere, frīxī, frīctum*, long *ī*, in a *sartāgō* (taught). English *fry* comes from it through French.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**frīgō** is compared by Lewis & Short with Greek *phrygō*, "to roast, to parch". English *fry* comes from it through French.
