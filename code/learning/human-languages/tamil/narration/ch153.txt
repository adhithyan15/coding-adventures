Tamil, chapter 153: To climb, To get down, To turn, To join, To bathe.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ஏறு (ēṟu) — to climb, to get on.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for to fly, then the Tamil for to fall.

You'll want to know: ஏறு.
ஏறு — ēṟu — "to climb, to get on".
