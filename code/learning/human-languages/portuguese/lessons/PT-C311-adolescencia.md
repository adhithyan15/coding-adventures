## You'll want to know: a adolescência
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C311-IDADES-02]; assesses=[] -->

**a adolescência** — "adolescence, the teenage years".

> Na adolescência, eu era muito tímido.
>
> *As a teenager I was very shy.*

*Um adolescente* — a teenager.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*adolescência* is a learned borrowing from Latin *adolēscentia* "youth", from *adolēscere* "to grow up" (Lewis & Short).
