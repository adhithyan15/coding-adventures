## You'll want to know: la vista
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C664-HABITACION-03]; assesses=[] -->

**la vista** — "the view; sight, eyesight".

> Desde aquí hay una vista preciosa.
>
> *There is a lovely view from here.*

*Con vistas al mar* — with a sea view. *Hasta la vista* — see you.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*vista* is built on *visto* "seen", the past form of *ver* "to see", which comes from Latin *vidēre* (EtymDB).
