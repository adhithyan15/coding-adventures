## You'll want to know: liáotiān
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C84-TOGETHER84-03]; assesses=[] -->

*liáotiān* — "to chat".

> *wǒmen hē chá liáotiān* — We drink tea and chat.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*liáotiān* is built from liáo and 天 'sky', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *lew* and *then* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription). The dictionary glosses the first as a helping word; how the pair came to mean chatting is not settled.
