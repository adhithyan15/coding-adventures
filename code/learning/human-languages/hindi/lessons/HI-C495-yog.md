## You'll want to know: योग
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C495-KASRAT495-02]; assesses=[] -->

**योग** — *yog* — "yoga".

> दादी रोज़ योग करती हैं, इसलिए वह चुस्त हैं।
>
> *dādī roz yog kartī haiṁ, isliye vah cust haiṁ.*
>
> Grandmother does yoga every day, which is why she is so spry.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**योग** (*yog*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *yoga*, first "the act of yoking, joining" (Monier-Williams); English took *yoga* from the same word.
