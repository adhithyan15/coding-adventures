# अन्त्येष्टिः (antyeṣṭiḥ) — funeral rites

## You'll want to know: अन्त्येष्टिः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C491-JARA491-04]; assesses=[] -->

**अन्त्येष्टिः** — *antyeṣṭiḥ* — "funeral rites".

> नद्याः तीरे अन्त्येष्टिः भवति।
>
> *nadyāḥ tīre antyeṣṭiḥ bhavati.*
>
> The funeral rites take place on the riverbank.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अन्त्येष्टिः** (*antyeṣṭiḥ*) is built from *antya*, "last", and *iṣṭi*, "a sacrifice", from the root √yaj; MW gives "funeral sacrifice" (MW).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
