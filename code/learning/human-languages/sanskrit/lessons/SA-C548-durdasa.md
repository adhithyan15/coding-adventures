## You'll want to know: दुर्दशा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C548-SANTVANA548-02]; assesses=[] -->

**दुर्दशा** — *durdaśā* — "a sorry plight".

> यदि दुर्दशा आगच्छति, तर्हि मित्रं साहाय्यं करोति।
>
> *yadi durdaśā āgacchati, tarhi mitraṃ sāhāyyaṃ karoti.*
>
> If hard times come, a friend helps.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**दुर्दशा** (*durdaśā*) is built on *dur*, "bad", and *daśā*, "a state, a condition"; MW gives *durdaśā* as a bad situation, misfortune (MW).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
