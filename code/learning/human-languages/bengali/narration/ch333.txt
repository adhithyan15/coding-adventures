Bengali, chapter 333: Making a Case.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
যুক্তি (jukti) — an argument; reasoning.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for mind you, then the Bengali for to hold good.

You'll want to know: যুক্তি.
যুক্তি — jukti — "an argument; reasoning".
তোমার যুক্তিটা ভালো, কিন্তু এটা সব জায়গায় খাটে না। tomār juktiṭā bhālo, kintu eṭā sôb jāygāy khāṭe nā. Your argument is good, but it doesn't hold everywhere.

The word, taken apart.
যুক্তি (jukti) is Sanskrit yukti, a joining and so a reasoning that holds together (Monier-Williams). It is a reasoned case, not a quarrel: তর্ক করা is to argue, যুক্তি is what you argue with.

What you've built.
One more word for giving reasons and opinions.

Lesson 2 of 5.
বক্তব্য (bôktôbyô) — what one has to say; a statement.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for to hold good, then the Bengali for an argument.

You'll want to know: বক্তব্য.
বক্তব্য — bôktôbyô — "what one has to say; a statement".
তোমার বক্তব্য আমরা সবাই শুনলাম। tomār bôktôbyô āmrā sôbāi shunlām. We all heard what you had to say.

The word, taken apart.
বক্তব্য (bôktôbyô) is Sanskrit vaktavya, 'what is to be said' (Monier-Williams). In Bengali it is the point a person puts forward in a meeting or a debate.

Lesson 3 of 5.
পক্ষ (pôkkhô) — a side in a dispute; a party.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for an argument, then the Bengali for a statement.

You'll want to know: পক্ষ.
পক্ষ — pôkkhô — "a side in a dispute; a party".
আমি তোমার পক্ষে আছি। āmi tomār pôkkhe āchhi. I'm on your side.

The word, taken apart.
পক্ষ (pôkkhô) is Sanskrit pakṣa, a wing, and so a side or party (Monier-Williams). To be on someone's side is -এর পক্ষে থাকা.

Lesson 4 of 5.
বিরুদ্ধে (biruddhe) — against.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a statement, then the Bengali for a side in a dispute.

You'll want to know: বিরুদ্ধে.
বিরুদ্ধে — biruddhe — "against".
গ্রামের সবাই নতুন আইনের বিরুদ্ধে। grāmer sôbāi nôtun āiner biruddhe. Everyone in the village is against the new law.

The word, taken apart.
বিরুদ্ধে (biruddhe) is Sanskrit viruddha, opposed or hindered (Monier-Williams), with the Bengali ending -এ. It follows a word with -এর: আইনের বিরুদ্ধে, against the law.

Lesson 5 of 5.
সমর্থন করা (sômôrthôn kôrā) — to support (a view or a person); to back.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a side in a dispute, then the Bengali for against.

You'll want to know: সমর্থন করা.
সমর্থন করা — sômôrthôn kôrā — "to support (a view or a person); to back".
আমরা সবাই মীরাকে সমর্থন করলাম। āmrā sôbāi mīrāke sômôrthôn kôrlām. We all backed Mira.
Past (he/she …): তিনি আমাদের যুক্তি সমর্থন করলেন, tini āmāder jukti sômôrthôn kôrlen.

The word, taken apart.
সমর্থন করা (sômôrthôn kôrā) is Sanskrit samarthana, which Monier-Williams gives both as deliberation and as establishing or vindicating a claim, with করা 'to do'. Bengali uses it for standing behind a view or a person.
