# たすける (tasukeru) — to rescue; to save

## You'll want to know: たすける
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C223-SAFETY223-04]; assesses=[] -->

**たすける** — *tasukeru* — "to rescue; to save".

> **いしゃは いのちを たすけます。** — *isha wa inochi o tasukemasu* — "Doctors save lives."

Polite **たすけます**, *tasukemasu*; an ichidan verb.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**たすける** (*tasukeru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi, and its older origin is not settled (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
