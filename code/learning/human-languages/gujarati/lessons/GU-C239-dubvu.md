# ડૂબવું (ḍūbvũ) — to sink, to drown

## You'll want to know: ડૂબવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C239-POND239-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ડૂબવું** — *ḍūbvũ* — "to sink, to drown".

> **મારો બૂટ કાદવમાં ડૂબ્યો.** — *māro būṭ kādavmā̃ ḍūbyo* — "My shoe sank into the mud."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ડૂબવું** (*ḍūbvũ*) comes from *\*ḍubb*, "sink", a reconstructed form that CDIAL reads as a reshuffled Middle Indo-Aryan *buḍḍaï*; it glosses the Gujarati verb "to sink" (CDIAL 5561).
