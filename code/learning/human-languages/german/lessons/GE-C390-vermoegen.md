## You'll want to know: das Vermögen
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C390-VERSCHWENDUNG-04]; assesses=[] -->

**das Vermögen** — "the fortune, the assets".

> Die Familie hat ein großes Vermögen.
>
> *The family has a large fortune.*

Plural: *die Vermögen*. In formal German also ability: *nach bestem Vermögen* — to the best of your ability.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Vermögen* is the old verb *vermögen* "to be able" made into a noun, built on *mögen*, from Proto-Germanic *maganą* "to be able" (EtymDB): what your means let you do.
