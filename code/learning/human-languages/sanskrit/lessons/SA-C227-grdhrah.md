## You'll want to know: गृध्रः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C227-THG227-05]; assesses=[] -->

**गृध्रः** — *gṛdhraḥ* — "a vulture".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*gṛdhra* is built on the root √gṛdh, "to be greedy" (MW): the vulture is the greedy bird. Both senses are in the Rigveda (CDIAL 4233), and it lives on as Hindi *gīdh*, "vulture".
