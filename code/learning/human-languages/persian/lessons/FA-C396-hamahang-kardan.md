## You'll want to know: هماهنگ کردن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C396-MONSAREF-04]; assesses=[] -->

**هماهنگ کردن** (*hamâhang kardan*) — "to coordinate, to arrange".

> من با همکارانم زمان جلسه را هماهنگ کردم.
>
> *man bâ hamkârânam zamân-e jalase râ hamâhang kardam.*
>
> I arranged the meeting time with my colleagues.

*Hamâhang* "in harmony": *ham* "same" + *âhang* "tune". *Bâ kasi hamâhang kardan* — to arrange something with someone.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**هماهنگ کردن** is native Persian, built from *ham* "same, together" + *âhang* "tune", which EtymDB traces to Middle Persian *āhang*, + *kardan*. To coordinate is to put people in one tune.
