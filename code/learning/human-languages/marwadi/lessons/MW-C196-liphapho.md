## You'll want to know: लिफाफो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C196-POST196-02]; assesses=[] -->

**लिफाफो** — *liphāpho* — "an envelope".

> लिफाफो खाली है।
>
> *liphāpho khālī hai.*
>
> The envelope is empty.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**लिफाफो** (*liphāpho*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *lifāfa*, "a wrapper".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for reading notices and messages.
