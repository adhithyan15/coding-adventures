Punjabi, chapter 84: Yellow, Red, Blue, Black, White.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ਪੀਲਾ (pīlā) — yellow.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for important, then the Punjabi for green.

You'll want to know: ਪੀਲਾ.
ਪੀਲਾ — pīlā — "yellow".

The word, taken apart.
ਪੀਲਾ (pīlā) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from pītala, "yellow" (CDIAL 8233). It is built on Sanskrit pīta, "yellow".
