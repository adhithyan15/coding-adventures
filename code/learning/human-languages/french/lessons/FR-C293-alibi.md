## You'll want to know: l'alibi
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C293-MYSTERE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**l'alibi** — "the alibi".

> Son alibi est solide: il était au cinéma.
>
> *His alibi is solid: he was at the cinema.*

Also an excuse: *un bon alibi pour ne pas venir*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*alibi* was borrowed from Latin *alibī* "elsewhere" (EtymDB), built on *alius* "other".
