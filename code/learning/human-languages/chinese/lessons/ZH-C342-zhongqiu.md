## You'll want to know: 中秋
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C342-AUTUMN342-02]; assesses=[] -->

**中秋** — *Zhōngqiū* — "the Mid-Autumn Festival".

> **中秋的时候我们都吃月饼** *Zhōngqiū de shíhou wǒmen dōu chī yuèbǐng* — At the moon festival we all eat mooncakes.

The new character, in pieces: 秋 — nine strokes: on the left the grain piece of 和, five strokes; on the right 火. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**中秋** (*Zhōngqiū*) is built from 中 'the middle' and 秋 'autumn', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *trjuwng* and *tshjuw* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first, among other senses, 'half', and says of the second, quoting an older dictionary, that it is when the grain ripens.
