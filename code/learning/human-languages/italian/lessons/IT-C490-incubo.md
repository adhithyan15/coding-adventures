## You'll want to know: l'incubo
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C490-SONNO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**l'incubo** — "nightmare".

> Stanotte ho fatto un incubo terribile.
>
> *Last night I had a terrible nightmare.*

*Fare un incubo* — to have a nightmare. Also a bad situation: *è stato un incubo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*incubo* comes from Late Latin *incubus* "a spirit that lies on the sleeper" (EtymDB), from *incubāre* "to lie upon" (Lewis & Short).
