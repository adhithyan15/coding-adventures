## You'll want to know: نت
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C519-SINGING-05]; assesses=[] -->

**نت** (*not*) — "a musical note; sheet music".

> او بدون نت پیانو میزند.
>
> *u bedun-e not piyâno mizanad.*
>
> He plays the piano without sheet music.

*not khândan* — to read music.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*not* is a loan, usually traced to French *note*, 'note'. EtymDB links the spelling only to English *net*, a different word.
