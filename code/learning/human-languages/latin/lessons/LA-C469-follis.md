## You'll want to know: follis, follis
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C469-INCUS-02]; assesses=[] -->

**follis, follis** — "a pair of bellows; a leather bag".

> Servus folle ignem auget.
>
> *The slave makes the fire bigger with the bellows.*

Third declension, masculine: *follis, follis m.* Also a blown-up leather ball and a money bag. English *fool* comes from it through French, from 'windbag'.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*follis* is inherited from PIE *bʰolǵʰnis* (EtymDB); Lewis & Short compare *flō* 'to blow'. English *fool* comes from it through Old French *fol*, from the sense 'windbag'.
