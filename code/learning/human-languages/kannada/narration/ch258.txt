Lesson 1 of 5.
ಮಜ್ಜಿಗೆ (majjige) — buttermilk.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for lentils, then the Kannada for tamarind.

You'll want to know: ಮಜ್ಜಿಗೆ.
ಮಜ್ಜಿಗೆ — majjige — "buttermilk".

The word, taken apart.
ಮಜ್ಜಿಗೆ (majjige) is shared with Tamil maccikai and Telugu majjiga, "buttermilk" (DEDR 4630). DEDR 4630 suggests, with a query, that it may be a loan from Indo-Aryan, comparing Prakrit majjiā, a curd dish.

The word, taken apart.
ಬೆಣ್ಣೆ (beṇṇe) is native Dravidian, one word with Tamil veṇṇai, "butter", which DEDR 5496 files under the root for "white, bright"; the entry also has Tamil veṇṇai.
