## You'll want to know: fēngjǐng
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C226-LAKE226-05]; assesses=[] -->

*fēngjǐng* — "scenery; landscape".

> *zhèr de fēngjǐng hěn piàoliang* — The scenery here is beautiful.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*fēngjǐng* is built from 风 'wind' and *jǐng* 'a view', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *pjuwng* and *kjaengX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription). The dictionary glosses the second 'bright' and 'light'; the sense 'a view' is not given there.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing things.
