Lesson 1 of 5.
هوا (havâ) — weather; air.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a party, then the Persian for a musical instrument.

You'll want to know: هوا.
هوا — havâ — "weather; air".

The word, taken apart.
havâ is borrowed from Arabic hawāʾ, "air". Its Arabic root is h-w-y (Lane).

Lesson 2 of 5.
یخ (yakh) — ice.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a musical instrument, then the Persian for weather.

You'll want to know: یخ.
یخ — yakh — "ice".

The word, taken apart.
yakh is a native Persian word, inherited rather than borrowed; it is usually traced to Middle Persian yax, "ice". EtymDB takes it back to Proto-Iranian aixa-, "ice".

سایه — sâye — "shade, a shadow".

The word, taken apart.
sâye is a native Persian word, inherited rather than borrowed; it is usually traced to Middle Persian sāyag, "shade". EtymDB takes it back to the Indo-European word for "shade" behind Greek skia.

دما — damâ — "temperature".

The word, taken apart.
damâ is built on dam, "breath, warmth", a native word, with the ending -â: the degree of warmth, a modern Persian coinage.
