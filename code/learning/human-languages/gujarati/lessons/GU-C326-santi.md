## You'll want to know: શાંતિ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C326-ZANDO326-03]; assesses=[] -->

**શાંતિ** — *śānti* — "peace".

> યુદ્ધ પછી ગામમાં ફરી શાંતિ આવી.
>
> *yuddh pachī gāmmā̃ pharī śānti āvī.*
>
> After the war, peace came back to the village.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**શાંતિ** (*śānti*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *śānti*, "tranquillity, peace" (CDIAL 12391). It is the noun beside **શાંત**, calm: **શાંતિ આવી**, peace came.
