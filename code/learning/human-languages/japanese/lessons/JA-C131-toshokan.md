## You'll want to know: としょかん
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C131-TOSHOKAN]; assesses=[JA-SCRIPT-TO-01, JA-SCRIPT-SHI-01, JA-SCRIPT-KA-01, JA-SCRIPT-N-01] -->

**としょかん** — *toshokan* — "a library".

Five signs, four beats: *to–sho–ka–n*. **し** with **ょ** after it is one beat,
*sho*, and **ん** is a beat on its own.
