## You'll want to know: शब्दकोडे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C532-BAITHE532-04]; assesses=[] -->

**शब्दकोडे** — *śabdakoḍe* — "a crossword".

> आजोबा रोज सकाळी एक शब्दकोडं सोडवतात.
>
> *ājobā roj sakāḷī ek śabdakoḍaṁ soḍavtāt.*
>
> Grandfather solves a crossword every morning.

Neuter.

शब्दकोडं सोडवणे, to solve a crossword; the dictionary writes शब्दकोडे.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**शब्दकोडे** (*śabdakoḍe*) is a Marathi compound built from शब्द, "word", from Sanskrit *śabda* (Monier-Williams), and कोडे, "a puzzle".
