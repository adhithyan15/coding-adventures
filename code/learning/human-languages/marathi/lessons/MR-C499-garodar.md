## You'll want to know: गरोदर
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C499-BAL499-01]; assesses=[] -->

**गरोदर** — *garodar* — "pregnant".

> माझी मावशी गरोदर आहे.
>
> *mājhī māvśī garodar āhe.*
>
> My aunt is pregnant.

Unchanging; the formal word is गर्भवती.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**गरोदर** (*garodar*) is usually traced to two Sanskrit words, *garbha*, "womb", and *udara*, "belly".
