## You'll want to know: el bache
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C642-CARRETERA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**el bache** — "the pothole; the bad patch".

> Cuidado con los baches.
>
> *Watch out for the potholes.*

*Pasar un bache* — to go through a rough patch.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

The origin of *bache* is not settled; it may imitate the thud of a wheel dropping into a hole.
