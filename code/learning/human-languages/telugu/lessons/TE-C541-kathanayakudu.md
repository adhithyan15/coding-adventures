## You'll want to know: కథానాయకుడు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C541-NATAKAM541-03]; assesses=[] -->

**కథానాయకుడు** — *kathānāyakuḍu* — "a hero; a male lead".

> ఆ సినిమాలో కథానాయకుడు ఒక రైతు.
>
> *ā sinimālō kathānāyakuḍu oka raitu.*
>
> In that film the hero is a farmer.

Feminine: కథానాయిక, the heroine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**కథానాయకుడు** (*kathānāyakuḍu*) is a learned compound built from Sanskrit *kathā*, "story" (CDIAL 2704), and *nāyaka*, "a leader; in drama, the hero" (Monier-Williams).
