# অনুশোচনা (ônushochônā) — remorse; repentance

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[BN-LEX-C446-SANTVONA446-05, BN-LEX-C447-APHSOS447-01] -->

## You'll want to know: অনুশোচনা
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C447-APHSOS447-02]; assesses=[] -->

**অনুশোচনা** — *ônushochônā* — "remorse; repentance".

> মিথ্যে বলার পরে অরুণের অনুশোচনা হল।
>
> *mitthe bôlār pôre ôruner ônushochônā hôlô.*
>
> After telling the lie, Arun felt remorse.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**অনুশোচনা** (*ônushochônā*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *anuśocana*, "sorrow, repentance" (Monier-Williams), grief that comes after the deed.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
