## You'll want to know: शिल्प
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C528-SHILP528-03]; assesses=[] -->

**शिल्प** — *śilp* — "a craft; craftsmanship".

> यह गाँव लकड़ी के शिल्प के लिए मशहूर है।
>
> *Yah gā̃v lakṛī ke śilp ke liye maśhūr hai.*
>
> This village is famous for its woodcraft.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**शिल्प** (*śilp*) is a tatsama, taken whole from Sanskrit *śilpa*, "artistic work, decoration" (Monier-Williams).
