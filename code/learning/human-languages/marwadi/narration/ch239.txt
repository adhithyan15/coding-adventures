Marwadi (Marwari), chapter 239: Crops.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
जुवार (juvār) — sorghum; the great millet.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for a ploughman, then the Marwadi for a seed.

You'll want to know: जुवार.
जुवार — juvār — "sorghum; the great millet".
खेत मांय जुवार हरी है। khet mā̃y juvār harī hai. The sorghum in the field is green.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
जुवार (juvār) is inherited from Sanskrit yavākāra, "shaped like barley"; CDIAL lists Old Marwari juvāri, "millet" (CDIAL 10437). In Marwadi it names sorghum, the tall millet, beside बाजरो.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for a seed, then the Marwadi for sorghum.

You'll want to know: सरसूं.
सरसूं — sarsū̃ — "mustard (the plant and its seed)".
सरसूं पीळी है। sarsū̃ pīḷī hai. The mustard is yellow.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
सरसूं (sarsū̃) is inherited from Sanskrit sarṣapa, "mustard seed"; CDIAL lists Hindi sarsõ, the mustard plant. CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry (CDIAL 13281).

Lesson 3 of 5.
कपास (kapās) — cotton (the plant).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for sorghum, then the Marwadi for mustard.

You'll want to know: कपास.
कपास — kapās — "cotton (the plant)".
हूं कपास बेचूं हूं। hū̃ kapās bechū̃ hū̃. I sell cotton.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
कपास (kapās) is inherited from Sanskrit karpāsa, "the cotton plant", which CDIAL takes, perhaps, from Austroasiatic; CDIAL lists Hindi and Gujarati kapās. CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry (CDIAL 2877).

Lesson 4 of 5.
बधणो (badhṇo) — to grow; to increase.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for mustard, then the Marwadi for cotton.

You'll want to know: बधणो.
बधणो — badhṇo — "to grow; to increase".
बरसात मांय बाजरो बधै है। barsāt mā̃y bājro badhai hai. The millet grows in the rains.
Past (it …): वो रूंख बध्यो, vo rū̃kh badhyo.

The word, taken apart.
बधणो (badhṇo) is inherited from Sanskrit vardhate, "grows"; CDIAL lists the Old Marwari verb badhaï, "to increase, to thrive" (CDIAL 11376).
