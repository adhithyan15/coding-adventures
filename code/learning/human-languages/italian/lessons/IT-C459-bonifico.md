## You'll want to know: il bonifico
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C459-BANCA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**il bonifico** — "the bank transfer".

> Pago la caparra con un bonifico.
>
> *I pay the deposit by bank transfer.*

*Fare un bonifico* — to make a transfer.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*bonifico* is built on *bonificare* 'to credit, to improve', a learned formation from Latin *bonus* 'good' and *facere* 'to make'.
