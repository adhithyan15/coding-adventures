## You'll want to know: هواپیمایی
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C431-PARVAZ-04]; assesses=[] -->

**هواپیمایی** (*havâpeymâyi*) — "an airline; aviation".

> برادرم در یک شرکت هواپیمایی کار میکند.
>
> *Barâdaram dar yek sherkat-e havâpeymâyi kâr mikonad.*
>
> My brother works for an airline.

Built on *havâpeymâ* 'aeroplane' + *-i*. An airline company is *sherkat-e havâpeymâyi*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*havâpeymâyi* is built on *havâpeymâ* 'aeroplane', from *havâ* 'air', borrowed from Arabic *hawāʾ* (EtymDB), and native *peymâ* 'measuring, crossing', with the ending *-i*.
