## You'll want to know: たぬき
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C300-KITSUNE300-02]; assesses=[] -->

**たぬき** — *tanuki* — "a raccoon dog".

> **たぬきが おじいさんの うちに きました。** — *tanuki ga ojiisan no uchi ni kimashita* — "A raccoon dog came to the old man's house."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**たぬき** (*tanuki*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict).
