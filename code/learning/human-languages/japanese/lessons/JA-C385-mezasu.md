## You'll want to know: めざす
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C385-KAIGAI385-05]; assesses=[] -->

**めざす** — *mezasu* — "to aim for; to make for".

> **ふねは みなみの しまを めざしています。** — *fune wa minami no shima o mezashite imasu* — "The ship is making for the southern islands."

Polite **めざします**, *mezashimasu*; a godan verb. Past (polite): **ふねが しまを めざしました。**, *fune ga shima o mezashimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**めざす** (*mezasu*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, built from *me*, "eye" and *zasu*, "pointing" (from *sasu*); its kanji were attached later as kun'yomi (JMdict).
