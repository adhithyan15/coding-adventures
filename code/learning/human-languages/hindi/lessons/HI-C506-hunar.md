## You'll want to know: हुनर
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C506-KARIGAR506-03]; assesses=[] -->

**हुनर** — *hunar* — "a skill; a craft".

> मेरे दादा के हाथों में बहुत हुनर था।
>
> *Mere dādā ke hāthoṁ meṁ bahut hunar thā.*
>
> My grandfather had great skill in his hands.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**हुनर** (*hunar*) is usually traced to Persian *hunar*, "skill, art".
