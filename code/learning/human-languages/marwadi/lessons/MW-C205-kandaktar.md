## You'll want to know: कंडक्टर
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C205-BUS205-05]; assesses=[] -->

**कंडक्टर** — *kaṇḍakṭar* — "a bus conductor".

> कंडक्टर टिकट देवै है।
>
> *kaṇḍakṭar ṭikaṭ devai hai.*
>
> The conductor hands out tickets.

A masculine or feminine noun, by the person meant.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**कंडक्टर** (*kaṇḍakṭar*) is borrowed from English *conductor*.
