# я опоздал на поезд (ya opozdál na póyezd) — I've missed the train

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[RU-LEX-C176-ACT176-02, RU-LEX-C135-ACT135-01, RU-LEX-TUDA-I-OBRATNO-01, RU-GRAMMAR-BILET-DO-NA-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "to be late" and "to lose". (*Opázdyvat. Teryát.*) Then
"a return ticket for the train". (*Bilét na póyezd tudá i obrátno.*)

## You'll want to know: я опоздал на поезд
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-OPOZDAL-NA-POEZD-01]; assesses=[RU-LEX-C79-TRAVEL-02, RU-LEX-C83-TIME83-04] -->

> **Я опоздал на поезд.** — *ya opozdál na póyezd* — I've missed the
> train. A woman says **Я опоздала на поезд.**

Russian says "I was late **for** the train", with **на**, as on a
ticket. **Опоздать** is the perfective partner of *опаздывать*: one
lateness, and the train is gone.

## Grammar Lens: a problem is a result
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-GRAMMAR-PROBLEM-PERFECTIVE-01]; assesses=[RU-GRAMMAR-PAST-PERFECTIVE-RESULT-01, RU-LEX-C143-ACT143-05, RU-LEX-C54-WORDS-04, RU-LEX-C215-PRICH215-04] -->

English often reports a problem with "I have": I've lost, it has gone.
Russian uses the perfective past, because the result is still true now:

> **К сожалению, я потерял билет.** — *k sozhaléniyu, ya poteryál bilét*
> — Unfortunately I've lost my ticket.
> **Поезд уже ушёл.** — *póyezd uzhé ushól* — The train has already left.

**Потерял** is the partner of *терять*; **ушёл**, of *уходить*.

## The exchange
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[RU-LEX-C112-THINGS112-01, RU-LEX-IZVINITE-01, RU-LEX-C136-NEG136-02, RU-LEX-C138-FUT138-02, RU-LEX-C69-TIME2-03, RU-LEX-POMOGITE-01] -->

> **— Извините, у меня проблема. Я опоздал на поезд.**
> **— Ничего. Есть поезд через час.**

*U menyá probléma* — "I have a problem", literally "at me, a problem".
*Nichevó. Yest póyezd chérez chas* — "Never mind. There's a train in an
hour." **Есть**, "there is", is the present of *быть* that Russian does say aloud.
When it is serious: **Помогите, пожалуйста!**

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[RU-LEX-C176-ACT176-02] -->

**опоздать** is *о-* with the root of **поздно**, "late". EtymDB 2.1 lists
*опоздать* without a parent; the usual account takes *поздно* back to a
Proto-Slavic *\*pozdŭ* and relates it to Latin *post*, "after".
