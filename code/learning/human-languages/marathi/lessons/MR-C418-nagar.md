## You'll want to know: नगर
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C418-NAGAR418-01]; assesses=[] -->

**नगर** — *nagar* — "a town".

> हे नगर नदीच्या काठावर वसलं आहे.
>
> *he nagar nadīcyā kāṭhāvar vaslaṁ āhe.*
>
> This town stands on the bank of the river.

Neuter.

नगर also ends many town names.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**नगर** (*nagar*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *nagara*, "a town, a city" (Monier-Williams).
