Kannada, chapter 101: Open, Close, Stop, Call.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ತೆರೆಯಿರಿ (tereyiri) — please open.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a station, then the Kannada for near.

You'll want to know: ತೆರೆಯಿರಿ.
ತೆರೆಯಿರಿ (tereyiri) — "please open", the polite ‑ಇರಿ form, like ಕಾಯಿರಿ, "please wait".
ಬಾಗಿಲು ತೆರೆಯಿರಿ (bāgilu tereyiri) — "please open the door".

What you've built.
A polite command on a known ending.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: tereyiri]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: tereyiri, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say bāgilu tereyiri]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Kannada for a station, then the Kannada for near, then say tereyiri again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does ತೆರೆಯಿರಿ (tereyiri) mean? ("Please open".) Say it once more.

Lesson 3 of 5.
ನಿಲ್ಲಿಸಿ (nillisi) — please stop.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for please open, then the Kannada for please close.

You'll want to know: ನಿಲ್ಲಿಸಿ.
ನಿಲ್ಲಿಸಿ (nillisi) — "please stop (it)", said to a driver or about a machine. It comes from ನಿಲ್ಲು, "to stop, to stand".

What you've built.
The request on a bus or in a taxi.
