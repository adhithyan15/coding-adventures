## You'll want to know: субтитры
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C527-SERIES-03]; assesses=[] -->

**субтитры** (*subtítry*) — "subtitles".

> Я смотрю фильмы с субтитрами.
>
> *Ya smotryú fílmy s subtítrami.*
>
> I watch films with subtitles.

Plural.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*субтитры* is built on *суб-* 'under' and *титр* 'a caption', usually traced through French *titre* to Latin *titulus* 'a label'; EtymDB has no entry.
