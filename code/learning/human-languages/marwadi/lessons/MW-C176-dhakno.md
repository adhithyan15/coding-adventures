## You'll want to know: ढांकणो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C176-GANTH176-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ढांकणो** — *ḍhā̃kṇo* — "to cover".

> मां दूध ढांकै है।
>
> *mā̃ dūdh ḍhā̃kai hai.*
>
> Mother covers the milk.

Past (I …): म्हैं दूध ढांक्यो, *mhaĩ dūdh ḍhā̃kyo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ढांकणो** (*ḍhā̃kṇo*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*ḍhakk*, "cover"; CDIAL lists Marwari *ḍhā̃kṇo* under it (CDIAL 5574).
