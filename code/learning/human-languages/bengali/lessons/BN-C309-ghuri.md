## You'll want to know: ঘুড়ি
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C309-GHURI309-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ঘুড়ি** — *ghuṛi* — "a paper kite; a kite (toy)".

> ছুটির দিনে ছেলেরা ছাদে ঘুড়ি নিয়ে খেলছিল।
>
> *chhuṭir dine chhelerā chhāde ghuṛi niye khelchhilô.*
>
> On the holiday the boys were playing with kites on the roof.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ঘুড়ি** (*ghuṛi*) is the paper kite; its origin is not settled. A kite the bird is **চিল**.
