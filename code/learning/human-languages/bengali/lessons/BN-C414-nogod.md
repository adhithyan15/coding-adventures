## You'll want to know: নগদ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C414-BANK414-05]; assesses=[] -->

**নগদ** — *nôgôd* — "cash; in cash".

> দোকানদার নগদ টাকা চাইলেন।
>
> *dokāndār nôgôd ṭākā chāilen.*
>
> The shopkeeper asked for cash.

Before a noun it means paid in hand: নগদ টাকা, cash; নগদে, in cash.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**নগদ** (*nôgôd*) is usually traced to Arabic *naqd*, "ready money", which reached Bengali through Persian.
