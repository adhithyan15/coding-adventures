## You'll want to know: infiel
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C745-RUPTURA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**infiel** — "unfaithful".

> Descubrió que su novio le era infiel.
>
> *She found out her boyfriend was unfaithful.*

*Ser infiel a alguien*. *La infidelidad* — infidelity.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*infiel* comes from Latin *īnfidēlis* "unfaithful", built on *in-* "not" and *fidēlis* "faithful", from *fidēs* "faith".
