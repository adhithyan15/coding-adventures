## You'll want to know: ಬಲವಂತ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C420-ALUKU420-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ಬಲವಂತ** — *balavanta* — "compulsion; force".

> ಯಾರ ಬಲವಂತವೂ ಇಲ್ಲದೆ ಅವನು ಕೆಲಸ ಬಿಟ್ಟನು.
>
> *yāra balavantavū illade avanu kelasa biṭṭanu.*
>
> He left the job without anyone forcing him.

ಬಲವಂತವಾಗಿ, by force; ಬಲವಂತ ಮಾಡು, to press someone.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಬಲವಂತ** (*balavanta*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *balavant*, "strong" (CDIAL 9166), from *bala*, "strength"; Kannada uses it for force put on someone.
