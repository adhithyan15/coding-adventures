## You'll want to know: ഹാജർ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C434-HOSTAL434-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ഹാജർ** — *hājar* — "attendance".

> ക്ലാസിൽ ഇന്ന് ഹാജർ കുറവായിരുന്നു.
>
> *klāsil innŭ hājar kuṟavāyirunnu.*
>
> Attendance in class was low today.

ഹാജരാകുക, to be present, is built on it.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ഹാജർ** (*hājar*) came from Arabic *ḥāḍir*, "present", and was borrowed from Hindustani *hāzir*; it is the noun inside ഹാജരാകുക.
