## You'll want to know: рифма
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C525-POETRY-03]; assesses=[] -->

**рифма** (*rífma*) — "a rhyme".

> В современных стихах часто нет рифмы.
>
> *V sovreménnykh stikhákh chásto net rífmy.*
>
> Modern poems often have no rhyme.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*рифма* is usually traced through German or Polish to French *rime*; EtymDB has no entry.
