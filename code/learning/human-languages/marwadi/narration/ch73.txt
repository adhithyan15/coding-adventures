Marwadi (Marwari), chapter 73: New, Old, Good, Bad, Long.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
नुवो (nuvo) — new.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for big, then the Marwadi for small.
