## You'll want to know: দোটানা
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C529-DOTANA529-02]; assesses=[] -->

**দোটানা** — *doṭānā* — "a dilemma; being torn two ways".

> আমি দোটানায় আছি: শহরে থাকব, না গ্রামে ফিরব?
>
> *Āmi doṭānāy āchhi: shôhôre thākbô, nā grāme phirbô?*
>
> I'm torn: shall I stay in the city or go back to the village?

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**দোটানা** (*doṭānā*) joins দো, "two", usually traced to Persian *do*, to টানা, "a pull": being pulled two ways at once.
