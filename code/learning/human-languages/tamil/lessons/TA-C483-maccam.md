## You'll want to know: மச்சம்
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C483-TOL483-05]; assesses=[] -->

**மச்சம்** — *maccam* — "a mole".

> அவளுடைய கன்னத்தில் ஒரு சிறிய மச்சம் இருக்கிறது.
>
> *avaḷuṭaiya kaṉṉattil oru ciṟiya maccam irukkiṟatu.*
>
> She has a small mole on her cheek.

A mark on the skin, often thought lucky.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**மச்சம்** (*maccam*) is native Dravidian (DEDR 4632), in an entry of words for marks and blemishes.
