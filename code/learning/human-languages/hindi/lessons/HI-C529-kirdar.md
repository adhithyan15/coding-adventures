## You'll want to know: किरदार
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C529-NATAK529-04]; assesses=[] -->

**किरदार** — *kirdār* — "a character; a role".

> नाटक में अरुण का किरदार एक बूढ़े किसान का है।
>
> *Nāṭak meṁ Aruṇ kā kirdār ek būṛhe kisān kā hai.*
>
> In the play Arun's role is an old farmer.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**किरदार** (*kirdār*) is usually traced to Persian *kirdār*, "a deed, conduct". The sense "a role in a play" is the one Hindi uses.
