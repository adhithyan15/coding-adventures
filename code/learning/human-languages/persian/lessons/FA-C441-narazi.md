## You'll want to know: ناراضی
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C441-DARKHAST-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ناراضی** (*nârâzi*) — "dissatisfied, unhappy (with something)".

> مشتریها از غذای رستوران ناراضی بودند.
>
> *Moshtari-hâ az ghazâ-ye restorân nârâzi budand.*
>
> The customers were unhappy with the restaurant's food.

*nâ-* 'not' + *râzi* 'satisfied'. The cause takes *az*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*nârâzi* is built from the native prefix *nâ-* 'not' and *râzi* 'satisfied', borrowed from Arabic *rāḍī* 'content, pleased' (EtymDB).
