## You'll want to know: a igualdade
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C363-CIDADAO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**a igualdade** — "the equality".

> Ela luta pela igualdade entre homens e mulheres.
>
> *She fights for equality between men and women.*

*Igualdade de oportunidades* — equal opportunities.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*igualdade* comes from Latin *aequālitās* "equality" through Old Portuguese *ygualdade* (EtymDB), built on *aequālis* "equal", on *aequus* "level, even" (Lewis & Short).
