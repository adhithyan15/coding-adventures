## You'll want to know: தாய்
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C492-KUTUMPAM492-02]; assesses=[] -->

**தாய்** — *tāy* — "a mother".

> தாய் தன் குழந்தையைத் தூக்கினாள்.
>
> *tāy taṉ kuḻantaiyait tūkkiṉāḷ.*
>
> The mother picked up her child.

Written and formal; at home அம்மா. தாய்மொழி, a mother tongue.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**தாய்** (*tāy*) is native Dravidian (DEDR 364), in an entry of words for mother such as ஆய் *āy*.
