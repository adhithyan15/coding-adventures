# மாறுவேடம் (māṟuvēṭam) — a disguise

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[TA-LEX-C337-SATHI337-03, TA-LEX-C337-SATHI337-04] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Tamil for to betray, then the Tamil for to deceive.

## You'll want to know: மாறுவேடம்
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C337-SATHI337-05]; assesses=[] -->

**மாறுவேடம்** — *māṟuvēṭam* — "a disguise".

> மன்னர் மாறுவேடத்தில் நகரத்தைச் சுற்றினார்.
>
> *maṉṉar māṟuvēṭattil nakarattaic cuṟṟiṉār.*
>
> The king went round the city in disguise.

மாறுவேடம் போடு, to put on a disguise.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**மாறுவேடம்** (*māṟuvēṭam*) joins native மாறு, "to be changed" (DEDR 4834), with வேடம், "costume", borrowed from Sanskrit *vēṣa*, "dress, assumed appearance" (CDIAL 12129).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
