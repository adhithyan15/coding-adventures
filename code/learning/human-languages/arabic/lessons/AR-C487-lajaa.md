# لجأ (lajaʾa) — to seek refuge

## You'll want to know: لجأ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C487-MALJA487-04]; assesses=[] -->

**لجأ** — *lajaʾa* — "to seek refuge".

> لجأ الناس إلى المدرسة أثناء الفيضان.
>
> *lajaʾa an-nās ilā al-madrasa athnāʾa al-fayaḍān.*
>
> People took refuge in the school during the flood.

Present: يلجأ, *yaljaʾ*.

It takes *ilā* before the place of refuge.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**لجأ** (*lajaʾa*) is built on the native Arabic root *l-j-ʾ*, "to take refuge" (Lane). It is the plain verb.
