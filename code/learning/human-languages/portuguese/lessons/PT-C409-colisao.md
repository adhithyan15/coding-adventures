## You'll want to know: a colisão
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C409-FILAS-03]; assesses=[] -->

**a colisão** — "the collision".

> Houve uma colisão entre dois carros na ponte.
>
> *There was a collision between two cars on the bridge.*

A formal word, common in the news.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*colisão* is a learned borrowing from Latin *collīsiō* (EtymDB), from *collīdere* 'to strike together' (Lewis & Short).
