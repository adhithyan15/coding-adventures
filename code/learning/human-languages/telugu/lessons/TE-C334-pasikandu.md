## You'll want to know: పసికందు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C334-JANANAM334-02]; assesses=[] -->

**పసికందు** — *pasikandu* — "a newborn baby".

> పసికందు రాత్రంతా ఏడ్చింది.
>
> *pasikandu rātrantā ēḍcindi.*
>
> The newborn cried all night.

Plural: పసికందులు, *pasikandulu*.

A tender word for a baby only days or weeks old.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**పసికందు** (*pasikandu*) is a Telugu compound whose second part, *kandu*, "an infant", is native Dravidian (DEDR 1411, with Tamil *kaṉṟu*, "a calf, a young one").
