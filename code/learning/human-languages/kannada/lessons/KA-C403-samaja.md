## You'll want to know: ಸಮಾಜ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C403-SAMAJA403-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸಮಾಜ** — *samāja* — "society".

> ಸಮಾಜದಲ್ಲಿ ಎಲ್ಲರಿಗೂ ಗೌರವ ಸಿಗಬೇಕು.
>
> *samājadalli ellarigū gaurava sigabēku.*
>
> Everyone in society should be respected.

ಸಾಮಾಜಿಕ, social.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸಮಾಜ** (*samāja*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *samāja*, "a meeting, an assembly" (CDIAL 13207).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
