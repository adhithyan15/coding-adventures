## You'll want to know: bǎocún
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C344-MEMORY344-01]; assesses=[] -->

*bǎocún* — "to keep; to save".

> *tā de xìn wǒ dōu bǎocún zài shūfáng lǐ* — I've kept all his letters in the study.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*bǎocún* is built from *bǎo* and *cún*, both inherited from Middle Chinese, *pawX* and *dzwon* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to keep safe' and the second 'to be there'.
