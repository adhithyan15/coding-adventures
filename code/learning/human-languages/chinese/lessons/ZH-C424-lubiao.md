## You'll want to know: lùbiāo
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C424-ROADS424-03]; assesses=[] -->

*lùbiāo* — "road sign".

> *lùbiāo shuō wǎng qián zǒu* — The sign says go straight on.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*lùbiāo* is built from 路 'a road' and *biāo* 'a mark', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *luH* and *pjiew* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second as the tip of a tree and 'to raise'.
