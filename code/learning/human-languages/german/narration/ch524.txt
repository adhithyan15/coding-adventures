German, chapter 524: Komödie und Tragödie.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
die Komödie — comedy.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for season, then the German for ratings.

You'll want to know: die Komödie.
die Komödie — "comedy".
Gestern haben wir eine lustige Komödie gesehen. Yesterday we saw a funny comedy.
Plural: die Komödien.

The word, taken apart.
Komödie comes from Latin cōmoedia, from Greek kōmōidía "comedy, a play" (EtymDB).

What you've built.

Lesson 5 of 5.
synchronisieren — to dub; to synchronise.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for documentary, then the German for subtitle.

You'll want to know: synchronisieren.
synchronisieren — "to dub; to synchronise".
Bei uns werden fast alle Filme synchronisiert. Here almost all films are dubbed.
Regular, Perfekt without ge-. Also to sync two devices.

The word, taken apart.
synchronisieren is built on synchron "at the same time", a learned word from Greek sýnchronos, built on khrónos "time"; EtymDB has no entry for it.
