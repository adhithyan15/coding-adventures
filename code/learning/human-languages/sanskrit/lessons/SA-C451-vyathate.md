## You'll want to know: व्यथते
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C451-ANUNAYA451-01]; assesses=[] -->

**व्यथते** — *vyathate* — "is pained; is distressed".

> मित्रस्य दुःखेन मीरा व्यथते।
>
> *mitrasya duḥkhena mīrā vyathate.*
>
> Mira is pained by her friend's sorrow.

Past (she …): सा व्यथितवती, *sā vyathitavatī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**व्यथते** (*vyathate*) comes from the root √vyath, "to tremble"; MW's ppp *vyathita* is "disquieted, distressed, afflicted" (MW).

## What you've built

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
