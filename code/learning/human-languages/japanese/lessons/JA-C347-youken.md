## You'll want to know: ようけん
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C347-HOUMON347-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ようけん** — *yōken* — "the matter in hand; one's business".

> **ようけんが おわったら、でんわして ください。** — *yōken ga owattara, denwa shite kudasai* — "Once your business is done, please phone me."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ようけん** (*yōken*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *yō*, "utilize" (Middle Chinese *yowngH*) and *ken*, "matter" (*gjenX*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
