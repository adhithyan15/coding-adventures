## You'll want to know: આંબો
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C443-TREE443-04]; assesses=[] -->

**આંબો** — *ā̃bo* — "a mango tree".

> ઉનાળામાં આંબા પર કેરીઓ પાકે છે.
>
> *unāḷāmā̃ ā̃bā par kerīo pāke che.*
>
> In summer mangoes ripen on the mango tree.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**આંબો** (*ā̃bo*) is a tadbhava of Sanskrit *āmra*, "the mango tree"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati word (CDIAL 1268). The fruit is કેરી.
