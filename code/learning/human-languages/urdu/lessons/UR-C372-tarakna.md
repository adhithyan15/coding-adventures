## You'll want to know: تڑکنا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C372-JAMNA372-04]; assesses=[] -->

**تڑکنا** — *taṛaknā* — "to crack (with a sharp sound)".

> اگر گلاس زیادہ گرم ہوا تو وہ تڑک جائے گا۔
>
> *agar glās zyādā garm huā to voh taṛak jāe gā.*
>
> If the glass gets too hot, it will crack.

Past (it …): وہ شیشہ تڑک گیا, *voh shīshā taṛak gayā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**تڑکنا** (*taṛaknā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *taṛaknā*, "to be cracked", under Sanskrit *traṭ*, a crackling sound (CDIAL 5988).
