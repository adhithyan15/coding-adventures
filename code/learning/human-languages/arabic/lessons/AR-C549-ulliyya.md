## You'll want to know: علية
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C549-BAB549-01]; assesses=[] -->

**علية** — *ʿulliyya* — "an attic, a loft".

> وضعنا الصناديق القديمة في العلية.
>
> *waḍaʿnā aṣ-ṣanādīq al-qadīma fī al-ʿulliyya.*
>
> We put the old boxes in the attic.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**علية** (*ʿulliyya*) is built on the native Arabic root *ʿ-l-w*, "to be high, to rise" (Lane). The ʿulliyya is the room at the top.
