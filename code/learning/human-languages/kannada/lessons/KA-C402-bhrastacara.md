# ಭ್ರಷ್ಟಾಚಾರ (bhraṣṭācāra) — corruption

## Warm-up

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Kannada for to promote, then the Kannada for to invest.

## You'll want to know: ಭ್ರಷ್ಟಾಚಾರ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C402-LANCHA402-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ಭ್ರಷ್ಟಾಚಾರ** — *bhraṣṭācāra* — "corruption".

> ಭ್ರಷ್ಟಾಚಾರದ ವಿರುದ್ಧ ಜನರು ಬೀದಿಗೆ ಇಳಿದರು.
>
> *bhraṣṭācārada viruddha janaru bīdige iḷidaru.*
>
> The people took to the streets against corruption.

ಭ್ರಷ್ಟ (fallen) + ಆಚಾರ (conduct).

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಭ್ರಷ್ಟಾಚಾರ** (*bhraṣṭācāra*) is built from Sanskrit *bhraṣṭa*, "fallen" (CDIAL 9655), and *ācāra*, "conduct" (CDIAL 1071): fallen conduct.
