## You'll want to know: ਹਿਸਾਬ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C278-STOCK278-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ਹਿਸਾਬ** — *hisāb* — "a reckoning; an account of money".

> ਦੁਕਾਨ ਦਾ ਹਿਸਾਬ ਸਹੀ ਹੈ।
>
> *dukān dā hisāb sahī hai.*
>
> The shop's accounts are correct.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਹਿਸਾਬ** (*hisāb*) is a loan, usually traced through Persian to Arabic *hisāb*, "counting, reckoning".
