## You'll want to know: انقطع
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C444-KAHRABA444-03]; assesses=[] -->

**انقطع** — *inqaṭaʿa* — "to be cut off".

> انقطعت الكهرباء في الفندق ليلة أمس.
>
> *inqaṭaʿat al-kahrabāʾ fī al-funduq laylat ams.*
>
> The electricity was cut off in the hotel last night.

Present: ينقطع, *yanqaṭiʿ*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**انقطع** (*inqaṭaʿa*) is built on the native Arabic root *q-ṭ-ʿ*, "to cut" (Lane). It is Form VII, "to be cut".
