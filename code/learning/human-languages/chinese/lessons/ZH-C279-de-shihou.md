## Grammar Lens: …的时候 and 正在, for what was going on
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-GRAMMAR-DE-SHIHOU-01]; assesses=[ZH-LEX-C46-WEEK46-03, ZH-LEX-C149-ZHENGZAI-01, ZH-GRAMMAR-ZAI-PROGRESSIVE-01, ZH-LEX-C69-MEALS69-01, ZH-LEX-C32-FAMILY32-01] -->

> **…的时候 after a clause says when; 正在 in the other clause says what was already going on at that moment.**

> **他来的时候 我正在吃饭** *tā lái de shíhou, wǒ zhèngzài chī fàn* — When he came, I was eating.
> **我回家的时候 妈妈正在做饭** *wǒ huí jiā de shíhou, māma zhèngzài zuòfàn* — When I got home, Mum was cooking.

The clause before 的时候 names the moment, and a bare verb is enough: 他来的时候. The other clause is the scene that was already running, with the 正在 of 我正在吃饭.

正在 has no tense of its own: the story around it puts the scene in the past.

All three characters of 的时候 are ones you write, so it prints whole for the first time.
