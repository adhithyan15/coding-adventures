## You'll want to know: भ्रमः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C477-BHRAMA477-01]; assesses=[] -->

**भ्रमः** — *bhramaḥ* — "dizziness".

> ज्वरेण मम भ्रमः भवति।
>
> *jvareṇa mama bhramaḥ bhavati.*
>
> The fever makes me dizzy.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**भ्रमः** (*bhramaḥ*) comes from the root √bhram, "to wander, to whirl", the root of **भ्रमति**; MW gives "wandering" and "giddiness, dizziness" (MW).
