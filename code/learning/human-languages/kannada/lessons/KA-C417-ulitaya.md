# ಉಳಿತಾಯ (uḷitāya) — savings

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[KA-LEX-C417-KANTU417-01, KA-LEX-C417-KANTU417-02] -->

## You'll want to know: ಉಳಿತಾಯ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C417-KANTU417-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ಉಳಿತಾಯ** — *uḷitāya* — "savings".

> ಉಳಿತಾಯದ ಹಣದಿಂದ ಅವಳು ಸೈಕಲ್ ಕೊಂಡಳು.
>
> *uḷitāyada haṇadinda avaḷu saikal koṇḍaḷu.*
>
> She bought a bicycle with her savings.

ಉಳಿತಾಯ ಖಾತೆ, a savings account.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಉಳಿತಾಯ** (*uḷitāya*) is built on the Kannada verb *uḷi*, "to remain, to be left over"; DEDR does not list *uḷitāya*, so the formation is not settled.
