## You'll want to know: 气话
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C357-SORRY357-02]; assesses=[] -->

**气话** — *qìhuà* — "something said in anger".

> **他说的是气话** *tā shuō de shì qìhuà* — He only said it because he was angry.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**气话** (*qìhuà*) is built from 气 'breath' and 话 'talk', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *khj+jH* and *hwaejH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'breath' and the second 'speech'.
