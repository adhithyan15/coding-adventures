Mandarin Chinese, chapter 61: Long and Short.
6 lessons. You can do the first one in the car; after that you will want to have stopped.


Lesson 1 of 6.
长 (cháng) — long.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for black, then the Mandarin for blue.

You'll want to know: 长.
长 — cháng — "long".
jīntiān de diànyǐng hěn cháng — Today's film is a long one.
The new character, in pieces: 长 (cháng) — four strokes: a short fall, a long bar, a vertical that flicks up at the foot, and a sweep down to the right. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

The word, taken apart.
长 (cháng) is inherited from Middle Chinese drjang (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'lasting', 'far' and 'for ever'.

What you've built.
One more word for everyday answers.

Lesson 2 of 6.
长 (cháng) — four strokes in one piece.

Before we start: this one needs your hands, so it is not a driving lesson. You will want a pen and something to write on and your eyes, for letter shapes on the page. You can listen to everything else now. I will say so again when we reach them, but leave these sections until you have stopped: Warm-up, Script — four strokes in one piece and Guided Practice.

[once you have stopped driving — read: each one aloud, with nothing said first — dà, bā]

[once you have stopped driving — this part needs your eyes or your hands; if you are driving, skip ahead to the next part]
Script — four strokes in one piece.
长 (cháng).
One piece, four strokes.
A short left-falling stroke at the top right, falling toward the left.
A long horizontal across the middle.
A vertical from the top left, down through the bar, that turns and flicks up to the right at the foot.
A long stroke falling to the right, from the bar down to the bottom right.

[once you have stopped driving — this part needs your eyes or your hands; if you are driving, skip ahead to the next part]
Guided Practice.
[once you have stopped driving — write: 长 (cháng), all four, with the model in front of you]
[once you have stopped driving — cover: write it again from memory, and count the pieces, not the strokes]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
How many strokes in 长 (cháng)? (Four.) Which stroke flicks up at the foot? (The vertical, the third.) Which way does the last one fall? (Down to the right.)
Source: Hanzi Writer Data 长 (cháng), pinned stroke paths and medians.
