## You'll want to know: unschuldig
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C286-URTEIL-05]; assesses=[] -->

**unschuldig** — "innocent".

> Am Ende war die Frau unschuldig.
>
> *In the end the woman was innocent.*

The opposite of *schuldig*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*unschuldig* joins *un-* "not" and *schuldig* "guilty", from Old High German *sculdig* (EtymDB).
