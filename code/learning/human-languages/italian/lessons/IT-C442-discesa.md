## You'll want to know: la discesa
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C442-SALITA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**la discesa** — "the descent, the downhill slope".

> In discesa la bici va da sola.
>
> *Downhill the bike goes by itself.*

*In discesa* — downhill; on the slopes, *la discesa* is the downhill run.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*discesa* is built on *discendere* 'to go down', which is inherited from Latin *dēscendere* (EtymDB), from *dē* 'down' and *scandere* 'to climb' (Lewis & Short).
