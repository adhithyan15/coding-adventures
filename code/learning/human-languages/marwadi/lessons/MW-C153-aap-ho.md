# आप … हो — you are

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MW-SCRIPT-WELLBEING-QUESTION-01, MW-GRAMMAR-C153-BE-HU-01, MW-GRAMMAR-C153-BE-HAI-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Ask the polite question: **आप कैसो हो?** Then say *I am here* and *Ram
is there*.

## Grammar Lens: हो after आप
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-GRAMMAR-C153-BE-HO-01]; assesses=[MW-SCRIPT-WELLBEING-QUESTION-01] -->

**आप कैसो हो?** ends on **हो**. After **आप**, *are* is **हो**:

> **आप अठै हो।**
>
> *āp aṭhai ho.* — You are here.

**हो** answers to **आप** the way **हूं** answers to **हूं**, and **है** to **वो**.

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MW-GRAMMAR-C153-BE-HO-01, MW-GRAMMAR-C153-BE-HU-01, MW-GRAMMAR-C153-BE-HAI-01, MW-CONJ-PAN-01, MW-GRAMMAR-ADVERSATIVE-PAN-01, MW-GRAMMAR-ALTERNATIVE-CHOICE-01, MW-FUNCTION-ASK-PREFERENCE-ALTERNATIVES-01, MW-LEX-C48-HEALTH48-01] -->

[PAUSE 1s]
- [YOU SAY: **आप बठै हो पण हूं अठै हूं।** — *āp baṭhai ho paṇ hū̃ aṭhai hū̃.* — You are there, but I am here.]
- [YOU SAY: **आप राजी हो।** — *āp rājī ho.* — You are happy.]
- [YOU SAY: **चाय अर पाणी?** — *chāy ar pāṇī?* — Tea or water?]
- [YOU SAY: **पाणी।** — *pāṇī.* — Water.]
