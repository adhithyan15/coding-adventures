## You'll want to know: ઉબકો
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C477-ODKAR477-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ઉબકો** — *ubko* — "a retch; a wave of nausea".

> બસમાં મીરાને ઉબકા આવ્યા.
>
> *basmā̃ Mīrāne ubkā āvyā.*
>
> Mira felt sick on the bus.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ઉબકો** (*ubko*) goes back to a reconstructed *\*ubbakka-*, "vomited"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati word, "retching" (CDIAL 2337).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
