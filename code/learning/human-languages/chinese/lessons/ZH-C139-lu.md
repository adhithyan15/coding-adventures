## You'll want to know: lǜ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C139-COLOUR139-01]; assesses=[] -->

*lǜ* — "green".

> *chūntiān de cǎo hěn lǜ* — In spring the grass is very green.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*lǜ* is inherited from Middle Chinese *ljowk* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'the colour of blue and yellow'. The regular sound change would give *lù*; why the everyday word is *lǜ* is not settled.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing things.
