# சித்தாந்தம் (cittāntam) — an ideology; a doctrine

## You'll want to know: சித்தாந்தம்
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C358-KOTPATU358-03]; assesses=[] -->

**சித்தாந்தம்** — *cittāntam* — "an ideology; a doctrine".

> அவருடைய சித்தாந்தம் எனக்குப் பிடிக்கவில்லை.
>
> *avaruṭaiya cittāntam eṉakkup piṭikkavillai.*
>
> I do not like his ideology.

A formal word for a whole system of ideas: அவருடைய சித்தாந்தம், his ideology.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**சித்தாந்தம்** (*cittāntam*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *siddhānta*, "established end, doctrine" (CDIAL 13404), from *siddha*, "settled", and *anta*, "end": a settled conclusion.
