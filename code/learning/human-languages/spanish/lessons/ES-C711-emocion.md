## You'll want to know: la emoción
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C711-EMOCION-01]; assesses=[] -->

**la emoción** — "emotion; excitement".

> ¡Qué emoción! Mañana nos vamos.
>
> *How exciting! We leave tomorrow.*

*¡Qué emoción!* — How exciting!

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*emoción* was borrowed from French *émotion* (EtymDB), which goes back to Latin *ēmovēre* "to stir up", from *movēre* "to move".
