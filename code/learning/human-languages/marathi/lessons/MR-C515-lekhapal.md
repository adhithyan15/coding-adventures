## You'll want to know: लेखापाल
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C515-VISHESH515-04]; assesses=[] -->

**लेखापाल** — *lekhāpāl* — "an accountant".

> लेखापाल दर महिन्याला कंपनीचा हिशेब तपासतो.
>
> *lekhāpāl dar mahinyālā kampanīcā hiśeb tapāsto.*
>
> The accountant checks the company's accounts every month.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**लेखापाल** (*lekhāpāl*) is a modern tatsama coinage from Sanskrit *lekhā*, "a line, an account", and *pāla*, "keeper" (Monier-Williams).
