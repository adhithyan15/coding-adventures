## You'll want to know: паника
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C301-KATASTROFA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**паника** (*pánika*) — "panic".

> Люди бежали в панике.
>
> *Lyúdi bezháli v pánike.*
>
> People were running in panic.

Feminine. *Не паникуй!* — don't panic!

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*паника* is usually traced through French *panique* to Greek *panikós* "of Pan", the god said to bring sudden fear; EtymDB lists no parent.
