## You'll want to know: a juventude
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C311-IDADES-03]; assesses=[] -->

**a juventude** — "youth".

> Na juventude, o meu avô foi pescador.
>
> *In his youth my grandfather was a fisherman.*

Also young people as a group: *a juventude de hoje*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*juventude* is a learned borrowing from Latin *iuventūs, iuventūtis* "youth", from *iuvenis* "young" (Lewis & Short).
