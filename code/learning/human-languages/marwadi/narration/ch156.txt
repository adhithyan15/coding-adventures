Marwadi (Marwari), chapter 156: Mine, In and From.
4 lessons. All 4 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 4.
म्हारो, म्हारी — my, agreeing with what is mine.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say my name is Ram, then this is my house. Then say a boy and a girl: छोरो, छोरी.

Grammar Lens: the thing decides.
म्हारो ends like छोरो, and it changes the way छोरो turns into छोरी: it follows the thing that is yours.
यो म्हारो घर है। yo mhāro ghar hai. — This is my house.
म्हारी गाय अठै है। mhārī gāy aṭhai hai. — My cow is here.
घर is masculine, so म्हारो; गाय is feminine, so म्हारी. A woman says म्हारो घर too: the ending follows the house, not the owner.

Guided Practice.
थारो, your, does the same: थारो दूध, थारी रोटी.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: म्हारी रोटी गरम है। — mhārī roṭī garam hai. — My roti is hot.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: थारो दूध अठै है। — thāro dūdh aṭhai hai. — Your milk is here.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: म्हारो भाई बठै है। — mhāro bhāī baṭhai hai. — My brother is there.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
My house? (म्हारो घर.) My cow? (म्हारी गाय.) Who decides the ending? (The thing that is yours.)
[question — say your answer, then pause 12 seconds]
Say: my cow is here.


Lesson 2 of 4.
रसोई मांय, मेज माथै — in and on.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say inside and on top: मांय, माथै. Then my cow is here.

Grammar Lens: a noun in front.
Put a noun in front of मांय and माथै, and they say in and on:
दूध रसोई मांय है। dūdh rasoī mā̃y hai. — The milk is in the kitchen.
किताब मेज माथै है। kitāb mej māthai hai. — The book is on the table.
कनै, near, works the same: घर कनै, near the house. Old Marwari already had kanai, "near", and māhī, "in, into" (CDIAL 2830, 9804).

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: दुकान बाजार मांय है। — dukān bājār mā̃y hai. — The shop is in the market.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: चाय मेज माथै है। — chāy mej māthai hai. — The tea is on the table.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: वो घर कनै है। — vo ghar kanai hai. — He is near the house.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
In the kitchen? (रसोई मांय.) On the table? (मेज माथै.)
[question — say your answer, then pause 12 seconds]
Say that the milk is in the kitchen.


Lesson 3 of 4.
बाजार सूं (sū̃) — from.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say the shop is in the market. Then he comes every day, and the question word where, कठै.

Grammar Lens: सूं.
सूं (sū̃) after a place says where someone comes from:
वो बाजार सूं आवै है। vo bājār sū̃ āvai hai. — He comes from the market.
हूं दुकान सूं आऊं हूं। hū̃ dukān sū̃ āū̃ hū̃. — I come from the shop.
Old Marwari wrote it -su: talā-su, "from below" (CDIAL 5731).

Guided Practice.
Ask with कठै in front of it:
आप कठै सूं आवो हो? āp kaṭhai sū̃ āvo ho? — Where do you come from?
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: आप कठै सूं आवो हो? — āp kaṭhai sū̃ āvo ho? — Where do you come from?]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: हूं बाजार सूं आऊं हूं। — hū̃ bājār sū̃ āū̃ hū̃. — I come from the market.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
From the market? (बाजार सूं (sū̃).) Where from? (कठै सूं (sū̃).)
[question — say your answer, then pause 15 seconds]
Ask politely: where do you come from?


Lesson 4 of 4.
Mine, in and from — a review.

Warm-up.
[pause 3 seconds]
Say my cow is here.

Guided Practice.
[pause 2 seconds after each]
[your turn — say: my house; my cow; your roti; your milk]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: the milk is in the kitchen; the book is on the table]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: the shop is near the house]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: where do you come from?; I come from the market]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: he comes from the shop every day]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
My cow? (म्हारी गाय.) In the kitchen? (रसोई मांय.) From the shop? (दुकान सूं (sū̃).)
[question — say your answer, then pause 15 seconds]
Say: my roti is on the table.
