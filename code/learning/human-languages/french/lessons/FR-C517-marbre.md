## You'll want to know: le marbre
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C517-SCULPTURE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**le marbre** — "marble".

> Dans le hall, il y a un escalier en marbre.
>
> *In the hall there is a marble staircase.*

*Rester de marbre* — to remain unmoved.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*le marbre* is inherited from Latin *marmor* "marble" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short), from Greek *mármaros*.
