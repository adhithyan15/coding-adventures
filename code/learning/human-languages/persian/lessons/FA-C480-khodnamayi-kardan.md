## You'll want to know: خودنمایی کردن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C480-PRIDE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**خودنمایی کردن** (*khodnamâyi kardan*) — "to show off".

> او جلوی دوستانش خودنمایی میکند.
>
> *u jelo-ye dustânash khodnamâyi mikonad.*
>
> He shows off in front of his friends.

*khod* 'self' + *namâyi* 'showing' (stem *namâ-*, from *namudan* 'to show') + *kardan*. Present: *khodnamâyi mikonad*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*khodnamâyi* is built on native Persian parts: *khod*, 'self', and *namâ-*, the present stem of *namudan*, 'to show', with the noun ending *-i*: showing oneself. *kardan*, 'to do', turns it into a verb; none of it is borrowed.
