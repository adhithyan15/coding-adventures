## You'll want to know: oppidānus, oppidānī
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C456-INCOLA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**oppidānus, oppidānī** — "a townsman, an inhabitant of a town".

> Oppidānī in forō mercātōrēs exspectant.
>
> *The townspeople wait for the merchants in the forum.*

Second declension, masculine: *oppidānus, oppidānī m.*, mostly plural. Also an adjective: of a country town.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*oppidānus* is built on *oppidum* 'town' with the suffix *-ānus* 'belonging to' (EtymDB; Lewis & Short).
