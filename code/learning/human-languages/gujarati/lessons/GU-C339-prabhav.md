## You'll want to know: પ્રભાવ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C339-ASAR339-02]; assesses=[] -->

**પ્રભાવ** — *prabhāv* — "an influence".

> ગુરુનો પ્રભાવ વિદ્યાર્થીઓ પર ઘણો છે.
>
> *guruno prabhāv vidyārthīo par ghaṇo che.*
>
> The teacher has a great deal of influence on the students.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**પ્રભાવ** (*prabhāv*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *prabhāva*, "might, power" (Monier-Williams). Gujarati uses it for the pull one person or thing has on another.
