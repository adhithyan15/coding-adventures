## You'll want to know: しょくば

**しょくば** — *shokuba* — "a workplace".

> **しょくばの みんなは しんせつです。** — *shokuba no minna wa shinsetsu desu* — "Everyone at my workplace is kind."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**しょくば** (*shokuba*) is a mixed word: Sino-Japanese *shoku*, "employment" (Middle Chinese *tsyik*), borrowed from Chinese, joined to native *ba*, "place", the kun'yomi of its second kanji (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
