## You'll want to know: le trône
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C323-REGNE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**le trône** — "the throne".

> Le fils du roi est monté sur le trône.
>
> *The king's son came to the throne.*

*Monter sur le trône* — to come to the throne.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*trône* comes from Old French *trone*, from Latin *thronus* "throne" (EtymDB), a borrowing from Greek *thrónos* "a seat".
