## You'll want to know: kēxuéjiā
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C488-TRADES488-03]; assesses=[] -->

*kēxuéjiā* — "scientist".

> *xiǎoshíhou wǒ zuì xiǎng dāng kēxuéjiā* — When I was little, what I wanted most was to be a scientist.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*kēxuéjiā* is built from *kē* 'a grade', 学 'to learn' and 家 'a household', all inherited from Middle Chinese, *khwa*, *haewk* and *kae* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'a rule' and 'a class'. The senses 'science' and 'someone skilled in it' are modern and not given there.
