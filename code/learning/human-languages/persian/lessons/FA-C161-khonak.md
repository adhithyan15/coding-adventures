## You'll want to know: خنک

**خنک** — *khonak* — "cool".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*khonak* is a native Persian word, inherited rather than borrowed; it is usually traced to Middle Persian *xunuk*, "cool".
