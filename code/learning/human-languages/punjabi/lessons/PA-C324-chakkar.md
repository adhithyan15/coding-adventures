## You'll want to know: ਚੱਕਰ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C324-BREATH324-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ਚੱਕਰ** — *chakkar* — "a dizzy spell".

> ਕੱਲ੍ਹ ਗਰਮੀ ਵਿੱਚ ਮੈਨੂੰ ਚੱਕਰ ਆਇਆ।
>
> *kall garmī vich mainū̃ chakkar āiā.*
>
> Yesterday I felt dizzy in the heat.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਚੱਕਰ** (*chakkar*) is inherited from Sanskrit *cakra*, "a wheel, a circle"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *cakkar*, "circuit, ring", under it (CDIAL 4538). The sense of a dizzy spell is not given there.
