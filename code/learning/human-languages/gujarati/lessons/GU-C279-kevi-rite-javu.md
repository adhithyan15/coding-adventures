# સ્ટેશન કેવી રીતે જવું? (sṭeśan kevī rīte javũ?) — asking the way

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-LEX-C279-RIT-01, GU-Q-KYAAN-01, GU-LEX-C74-WHERE1-03, GU-LEX-MAAF-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "how?" (*kevī rīte?*) Then "where" and "far". (**ક્યાં**, **દૂર**.) And "excuse me". (**માફ કરો**.)

## Grammar Lens: three questions
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-GRAMMAR-C279-WAY-ASK-01]; assesses=[GU-LEX-C85-TRAVEL-03, GU-LEX-C83-NUM3-04, GU-LEX-RASTO-01, GU-LEX-C195-LOST195-03] -->

> **માફ કરો, સ્ટેશન કેવી રીતે જવું?**
>
> *māph karo, sṭeśan kevī rīte javũ?* — "Excuse me, how do I get to the station?"

The verb stays in its **-વું** form: "how to go to the station?" Three questions do most of the work: where it is, **સ્ટેશન ક્યાં છે?**; how far, **કેટલું દૂર છે?**; and how, **સ્ટેશન કેવી રીતે જવું?** One more asks to be shown: **મને રસ્તો બતાવશો?** It is a polite future, "will you show me the way?": a request, not an order.

## You'll want to know: the answer
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-GRAMMAR-C279-WAY-GIVE-01]; assesses=[GU-LEX-C193-WAY193-04, GU-LEX-C193-WAY193-02, GU-LEX-C193-WAY193-03, GU-LEX-C194-CORNER194-04, GU-LEX-C193-WAY193-05, GU-LEX-C84-TOWN-05, GU-LEX-C194-CORNER194-03] -->

> **સીધા જાઓ, પછી પુલ પાસે ડાબી બાજુ વળો.**
>
> *sīdhā jāo, pachī pul pāse ḍābī bāju vaḷo.* — "Go straight, then turn left at the bridge."

Directions are said with **-ઓ**, the form of **માફ કરો**: **જાઓ**, **વળો**. **ડાબી** and **જમણી** follow **બાજુ**, which is feminine. Other pieces: **બેંકની સામે** (*beṅknī sāme*), "opposite the bank"; **સિગ્નલ પછી** (*signal pachī*), "after the signal"; **દૂર નથી, દસ મિનિટ છે** (*dūr nathī, das miniṭ che*), "it's not far, ten minutes".
