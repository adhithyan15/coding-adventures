## You'll want to know: ತಾರಸಿ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C546-NEMMADI546-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ತಾರಸಿ** — *tārasi* — "a terrace; a flat concrete roof".

> ಬೇಸಿಗೆಯಲ್ಲಿ ನಾವು ತಾರಸಿಯ ಮೇಲೆ ಮಲಗುತ್ತೇವೆ.
>
> *bēsigeyalli nāvu tārasiya mēle malaguttēve.*
>
> In summer we sleep on the terrace.

ತಾರಸಿ ಮನೆ, a house with a flat concrete roof.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ತಾರಸಿ** (*tārasi*) is the Kannada word for a flat concrete roof; its origin is not settled.
