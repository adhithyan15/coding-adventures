## You'll want to know: die Verwandtschaft
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C489-VERWANDTE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**die Verwandtschaft** — "relatives; kinship".

> Zu Weihnachten kommt die ganze Verwandtschaft.
>
> *All the relatives come for Christmas.*

A collective noun: all your relatives together. Plural, rare: *die Verwandtschaften*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Verwandtschaft* is built on *verwandt* "related" with *-schaft*; *verwandt* is the old participle of *verwenden* "to turn towards" (EtymDB).
