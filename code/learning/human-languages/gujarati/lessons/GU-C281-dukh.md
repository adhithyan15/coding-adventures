# દુઃખ (duḥkh) — sorrow; distress

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-LEX-C266-FEELINGS266-02, GU-LEX-C50-FEEL-01, GU-GRAMMAR-C147-THAYO-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say "contentment" and "happiness". (**સુખ**, **ખુશી**.) Then "it happened". (**થયું**.)

## You'll want to know: દુઃખ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C281-DUKH-01]; assesses=[GU-GRAMMAR-C146-DATIVE-SG-01, GU-LEX-C99-DEF99-05] -->

**દુઃખ** — *duḥkh* — "sorrow; distress", the opposite of **સુખ**. It is neuter. In speech its **ઃ** is light, close to *dukh*.

> **મને દુઃખ થયું.**
>
> *mane duḥkh thayũ.* — "I felt sad."

Word for word, "to me sorrow happened": the one who feels it takes **-ને**.

> **તેને કોઈ દુઃખ નથી.**
>
> *tene koī duḥkh nathī.* — "He has no troubles."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**દુઃખ** (*duḥkh*) is a tatsama: Sanskrit *duḥkha*, "pain, sorrow" (CDIAL 6375), written with the Sanskrit **ઃ** kept. CDIAL also lists an inherited Gujarati *dukh* from the same word.
