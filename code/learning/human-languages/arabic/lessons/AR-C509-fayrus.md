## You'll want to know: فيروس
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C509-ADWA509-02]; assesses=[] -->

**فيروس** — *fayrūs* — "a virus".

> الزكام سببه فيروس.
>
> *az-zukām sababuhu fayrūs.*
>
> A cold is caused by a virus.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**فيروس** (*fayrūs*) is a modern loan, usually traced to Latin *virus*, "poison, slime", which reached Arabic through European languages.
