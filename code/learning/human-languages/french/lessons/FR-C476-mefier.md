## You'll want to know: méfier
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C476-INQUIETUDE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**méfier** — "to be wary, to distrust (se méfier)".

> Je me méfie des gens trop gentils.
>
> *I am wary of people who are too nice.*

Always reflexive in use: *se méfier de*. The adjective is *méfiant*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*se méfier* is built from *mé-* "badly" and *se fier* "to trust", which goes back to Vulgar Latin *fīdāre*, from Latin *fīdere* "to trust" (Lewis & Short).
