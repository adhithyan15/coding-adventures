## You'll want to know: provisoire
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C371-ANNULER-03]; assesses=[] -->

**provisoire** — "temporary, provisional".

> C'est une solution provisoire, en attendant mieux.
>
> *It is a temporary solution, until something better comes.*

One form for both genders.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*provisoire* comes from Medieval Latin *prōvīsōrius*, built on Latin *prōvīsus*, the participle of *prōvidēre* "to foresee, to provide for" (Lewis & Short).
