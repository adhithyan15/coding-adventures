## You'll want to know: کنترل
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C532-WATCHING-02]; assesses=[] -->

**کنترل** (*kontorol*) — "a remote control; control".

> کنترل تلویزیون زیر مبل بود.
>
> *kontorol-e televiziyon zir-e mobl bud.*
>
> The TV remote was under the sofa.

*kontorol kardan* — to check, to control.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*kontorol* is a modern loan, usually traced to French *contrôle*. EtymDB has no entry for the Persian word.
