## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C376-REF-68]; assesses=[] -->

**el maíz** — "maize."

**Maíz** comes from **mahís**, a word in Taíno — the Arawakan language spoken
in the Caribbean islands Columbus reached in 1492. Spanish took it and handed
it on: English **maize** is the Spanish word with an English spelling.

It was among the very first American words to reach Europe. *Canoa* is in the
same log, recorded within a fortnight of landfall, and so are *hamaca* and
*cacique*.

A note on how to say that safely. It is tempting to promote *maíz* to **the**
first — a good line, and unprovable. Which word arrived first depends on
which manuscript you trust and which transcription of it, and *canoa* is the
usual claimant anyway. "Among the first" is a claim the evidence supports.
"The first" is a claim nobody can check, and this word does not need it.
