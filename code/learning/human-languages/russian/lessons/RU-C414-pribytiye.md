## You'll want to know: прибытие
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C414-PERRON-05]; assesses=[] -->

**прибытие** (*pribýtiye*) — "arrival".

> Время прибытия — десять часов.
>
> *Vrémya pribýtiya — désyat chasóv.*
>
> The arrival time is ten o'clock.

Neuter; formal. In speech people say *приезд*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*прибытие* is built on *прибыть* 'to arrive', from *при-* 'to, near' and *быть* 'to be'. EtymDB lists *прибыть* without a deeper source.
