## You'll want to know: असह्यः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C478-VEDANA478-05]; assesses=[] -->

**असह्यः** — *asahyaḥ* — "unbearable".

> तस्य वेदना असह्या अस्ति।
>
> *tasya vedanā asahyā asti.*
>
> His pain is unbearable.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**असह्यः** (*asahyaḥ*) is built from *a*, "not", and *sahya*, "to be borne", from the root √sah of **सहते**; MW gives "unbearable, insufferable" (MW).

## What you've built
