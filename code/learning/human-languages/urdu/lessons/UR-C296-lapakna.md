## You'll want to know: لپکنا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C296-BACHAO296-04]; assesses=[] -->

**لپکنا** — *lapaknā* — "to dart; to spring forward".

> وہ پانی کی طرف لپکا۔
>
> *voh pānī kī taraf lapkā.*
>
> He darted towards the water.

Past (she …): وہ لپکی, *voh lapkī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**لپکنا** (*lapaknā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *lapaknā*, "to leap up", under a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 10939).
