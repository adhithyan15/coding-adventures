# ここ — here, with one known sign twice

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[JA-SCRIPT-KO-01, JA-LEX-WAKARIMASHITA, JA-LEX-SUMIMASEN] -->

[PAUSE 12s] Write **こ**, say *wakarimashita*, then retrieve *sumimasen*.

## You'll want to know
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-KOKO]; assesses=[] -->

> **ここ** — *ko-ko* — here; this place

One owned sign appears twice. The word belongs to a larger pointing family that
will later contrast near me, near you, and over there. Today, keep only the
speaker-near place: **here**.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[JA-LEX-KOKO, JA-SCRIPT-KO-01] -->

1. Point beside yourself and say *koko*.
2. Read **こ | こ**.
3. Hear *ko-ko*, wait ten seconds, and write both signs.
