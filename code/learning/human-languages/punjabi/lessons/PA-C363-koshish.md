## You'll want to know: ਕੋਸ਼ਿਸ਼
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C363-WORK363-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ਕੋਸ਼ਿਸ਼** — *koshish* — "an effort; a try".

> ਮੈਂ ਹਰ ਰੋਜ਼ ਕੋਸ਼ਿਸ਼ ਕਰਦਾ ਹਾਂ।
>
> *maiṁ har roz koshish kardā hāṁ.*
>
> I make an effort every day.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਕੋਸ਼ਿਸ਼** (*koshish*) is a loan, usually traced to Persian *kōshish*, "effort".
