Hindi, chapter 476: The Face.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
चेहरा (cehrā) — a face.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a virtue, then the Hindi for a tendency.

You'll want to know: चेहरा.
चेहरा — cehrā — "a face".
धूप में मीरा का चेहरा लाल हो गया। dhūp meṁ Mīrā kā cehrā lāl ho gayā. Mira's face went red in the sun.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
चेहरा (cehrā) is usually traced to Persian chihra, "face, countenance". mũh is the face you wash; cehrā is the face you see.

नाना की मूँछें बहुत लंबी हैं। nānā kī mū̃cheṁ bahut lambī haiṁ. Grandfather has a very long moustache.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
मूँछ (mū̃ch) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Hindi mūch, "moustache", under mucchā, a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 10180). Hindi often uses the plural, mū̃cheṁ, for one moustache.

Lesson 5 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a moustache, then the Hindi for a beard.

You'll want to know: जबड़ा.
जबड़ा — jabṛā — "a jaw".
गिरने से अरुण के जबड़े में चोट लगी। girne se Aruṇ ke jabṛe meṁ coṭ lagī. Arun hurt his jaw in a fall.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
जबड़ा (jabṛā) is inherited: CDIAL lists Hindi jābṛā, "lower jaw-bone, jaw", under Sanskrit jámbha, "tooth, jaw" (CDIAL 5137); jabṛā is the common spoken form.
