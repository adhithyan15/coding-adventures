## You'll want to know: ಶಾಸಕ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C396-MASUDE396-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ಶಾಸಕ** — *śāsaka* — "a legislator".

> ನಮ್ಮ ಶಾಸಕರು ಊರಿನ ರಸ್ತೆ ಸರಿಪಡಿಸಿದರು.
>
> *namma śāsakaru ūrina raste saripaḍisidaru.*
>
> Our legislator got the town road repaired.

Plural: ಶಾಸಕರು, *śāsakaru*.

A member of a state assembly. ಶಾಸನ, a law or an inscription.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಶಾಸಕ** (*śāsaka*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *śāsaka*, "a ruler, a governor, an instructor" (Monier-Williams).
