# అలజడి (alajaḍi) — a commotion; unrest

## You'll want to know: అలజడి
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C377-VIPATTU377-02]; assesses=[] -->

**అలజడి** — *alajaḍi* — "a commotion; unrest".

> రాత్రి వీధిలో అలజడి మొదలైంది.
>
> *rātri vīdhilō alajaḍi modalaindi.*
>
> A commotion started in the street at night.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**అలజడి** (*alajaḍi*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 236 lists Telugu *alajaḍi*, "sorrow, affliction, calamity", under the word for distress; today it names a stir of unrest.
