Persian, chapter 537: Changes at Home.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
رنگ کردن (rang kardan) — to decorate with paint (a wall or room).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for untidy, then the Persian for rubbish.

You'll want to know: رنگ کردن.
رنگ کردن (rang kardan) — "to decorate with paint (a wall or room)".
ما دیوار اتاق را آبی رنگ کردیم. mâ divâr-e otâq râ âbi rang kardim. We painted the wall of the room blue.
rang 'colour, paint' + kardan. For pictures, naqqâshi kardan.

The word, taken apart.
rang kardan is built on two inherited words: rang, 'colour', which EtymDB traces to a Middle Persian word for 'colour, dye' (EtymDB), and kardan, 'to do'.

کاغذ دیواری (kâghaz divâri) — "wallpaper".
آنها کاغذ دیواری اتاق خواب را عوض کردند. ânhâ kâghaz divâri-ye otâq-e khâb râ 'avaz kardand. They changed the wallpaper in the bedroom.
kâghaz 'paper' + divâri 'of the wall'.

The word, taken apart.
kâghaz divâri joins kâghaz, 'paper', which EtymDB traces to Sogdian, another Iranian language (EtymDB), and divâri, from divâr, 'wall', which it traces to Middle Persian (EtymDB).

Lesson 3 of 5.
جابجا کردن (jâbejâ kardan) — to move (things), to rearrange.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to decorate with paint, then the Persian for wallpaper.

You'll want to know: جابجا کردن.
جابجا کردن (jâbejâ kardan) — "to move (things), to rearrange".
ما مبلها را جابجا کردیم. mâ moblhâ râ jâbejâ kardim. We moved the sofas around.
jâ 'place' + be 'to' + jâ 'place': place to place. + kardan.

The word, taken apart.
jâbejâ is built on native Persian jâ, 'place', which EtymDB traces to Middle Persian (EtymDB), said twice with be, 'to': from place to place. kardan is inherited.

خراب شدن (kharâb shodan) — "to break down; to go wrong".
یخچال دیروز خراب شد. yakhchâl diruz kharâb shod. The fridge broke down yesterday.
kharâb 'broken, ruined' + shodan 'to become'.

The word, taken apart.
kharâb comes from Arabic ḫarāb, 'ruin' (EtymDB). shodan, 'to become', is inherited Persian.

Lesson 5 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to move, then the Persian for to break down.

You'll want to know: صاحبخانه.
صاحبخانه (sâhebkhâne) — "a landlord, a landlady; the host".
صاحبخانه اجاره را بالا برد. sâhebkhâne ejâre râ bâlâ bord. The landlord put the rent up.
sâheb 'owner' + khâne 'house'. Also the host at a party.

The word, taken apart.
sâhebkhâne is built in Persian on sâheb, 'owner', from Arabic ṣāḥib, 'companion' (EtymDB), and native khâne, 'house'.
