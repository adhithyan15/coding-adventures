## You'll want to know: es sei denn
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C365-ESSEIDENN-01]; assesses=[] -->

**es sei denn** — "unless".

> Wir fahren morgen ans Meer, es sei denn, es regnet.
>
> *We are going to the seaside tomorrow, unless it rains.*

After *es sei denn* the clause keeps normal order, with the verb second: *es sei denn, es regnet*. *Sei* is the subjunctive (Konjunktiv I) of *sein*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*es sei denn* is a set phrase built on *sei*, the subjunctive of *sein* "to be", and *denn* "then", of the family of *dann* (EtymDB): "unless it be that …".
