## You'll want to know: साहजिकच
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C382-SHAKYA382-03]; assesses=[] -->

**साहजिकच** — *sāhjikac* — "naturally; of course".

> तो खूप थकला होता, साहजिकच लगेच झोपला.
>
> *to khūp thaklā hotā, sāhjikac lagec jhoplā.*
>
> He was very tired, so naturally he fell asleep at once.

साहजिक, natural, + -च.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**साहजिकच** (*sāhjikac*) is built from साहजिक, "natural", made on Sanskrit *sahaja*, "born together, innate" (Monier-Williams), with the stressing ending -च.

## What you've built
