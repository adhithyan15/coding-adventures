## You'll want to know: tèchǎn
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C247-TRIP247-03]; assesses=[] -->

*tèchǎn* — "a local speciality; a regional product".

> *zhèr de tèchǎn hěn hǎochī* — The local specialities here are delicious.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*tèchǎn* is built from *tè* 'special' and *chǎn* 'to produce', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *dok* and *sreanX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'a bull' and 'alone' and the second 'to give birth'; the ch of chǎn is not the regular outcome of that form.
