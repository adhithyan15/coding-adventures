Spanish, chapter 478: Antes del viaje.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
el equipaje — the luggage.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for to earn, then the Spanish for three hundred.

You'll want to know: el equipaje.
el equipaje — "the luggage".
El equipaje de mano no puede pesar más de diez kilos. Hand luggage cannot weigh more than ten kilos.
It is singular, like English luggage: mi equipaje.
