# はなしあう (hanashiau) — to discuss; to talk together

## You'll want to know: はなしあう
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C232-TALK232-01]; assesses=[] -->

**はなしあう** — *hanashiau* — "to discuss; to talk together".

> **どうりょうと かいぎで はなしあいます。** — *dōryō to kaigi de hanashiaimasu* — "I discuss things with my colleagues at the meeting."

Polite **はなしあいます**, *hanashiaimasu*; a godan verb.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**はなしあう** (*hanashiau*) is a native Japanese compound (wago), inherited rather than borrowed: it is built from *hanashi*, from *hanasu*, "to speak", and *au*, "to do together" (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
