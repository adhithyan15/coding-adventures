5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
spülen — to rinse, to wash up.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to send, then the German for to stroll.

You'll want to know: spülen.
spülen — "to rinse, to wash up".

The word, taken apart.
spülen from Middle High German spüelen from Old High German spuolen, "to rinse" (EtymDB 2.1) — inherited.
