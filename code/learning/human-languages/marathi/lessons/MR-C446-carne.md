## You'll want to know: चरणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C446-ABHAYARANYA446-05]; assesses=[] -->

**चरणे** — *carṇe* — "to graze".

> डोंगरावर गायी चरत होत्या.
>
> *ḍoṅgarāvar gāyī carat hotyā.*
>
> Cows were grazing on the hill.

Past (he …): तो चरला, *to carlā*.

Animals चरतात; to take them grazing is चरायला नेणे.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**चरणे** (*carṇe*) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi *ċarṇẽ*, "to move about, graze", under Sanskrit *carati*, "moves, grazes" (CDIAL 4686).
