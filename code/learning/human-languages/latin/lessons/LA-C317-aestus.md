## You'll want to know: aestus, aestūs
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C317-INCENDIUM-02]; assesses=[] -->

**aestus, aestūs** — "heat, glow; the tide".

> Propter aestum incendiī nēmō appropinquāre poterat.
>
> *Because of the heat of the fire nobody could come near.*

Masculine, 4th declension. The heat of fire or summer, the surge of the sea, and the tide. Related to *aestās* (taught).

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**aestus** is inherited from a Proto-Indo-European root meaning "to burn" (EtymDB), and is a cousin of *aestās*, "summer".
