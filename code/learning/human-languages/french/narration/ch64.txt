5 lessons. You can do the first one in the car; after that you will want to have stopped.


Lesson 1 of 5.
regarder — "to look at, to watch".

Warm-up.

Before the new one: say the French for to look for, then the French for to lose.

You'll want to know: regarder.
regarder — "to look at, to watch".

The word, taken apart.
regarder — built from re- and the French verb for "to keep, to watch over", which from Frankish wardon, "to watch". EtymDB 2.1 traces Italian guardare to the same Frankish verb.
