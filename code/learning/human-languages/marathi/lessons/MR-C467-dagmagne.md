## You'll want to know: डगमगणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C467-YATANA467-05]; assesses=[] -->

**डगमगणे** — *ḍagmagṇe* — "to wobble; to waver".

> संकटातही तिचा धीर डगमगला नाही.
>
> *saṅkaṭātahī ticā dhīr ḍagmaglā nāhī.*
>
> Even in the crisis her courage did not waver.

Past (he …): तो डगमगला, *to ḍagmaglā*.

Of a boat, a step or resolve: पाय डगमगणे, to stagger.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**डगमगणे** (*ḍagmagṇe*) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi *ḍagmagṇẽ*, "to tremble", under the expressive *\*ḍag* (CDIAL 5522).
