## You'll want to know: दीपशिखा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C397-DIPA397-02]; assesses=[] -->

**दीपशिखा** — *dīpaśikhā* — "a lamp's flame".

> वायुना दीपशिखा चलति।
>
> *vāyunā dīpaśikhā calati.*
>
> The lamp's flame flickers in the wind.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**दीपशिखा** (*dīpaśikhā*) is built from *dīpa*, "a lamp", and *śikhā*, "a tuft, a point of flame"; MW gives the compound as the flame of a lamp (MW).
