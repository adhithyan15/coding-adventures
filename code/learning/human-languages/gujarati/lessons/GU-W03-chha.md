# છ — the consonant chha

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-SCRIPT-U-MATRA-01] -->

Write the below-letter u hook once from memory. Keep it compact.

## Script — meet one form
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-SCRIPT-CHHA-01]; assesses=[] -->

Keep **છ** visible. It is the consonant chha, pronounced *chha*. Begin near the upper left and circle the upper-left lobe. Continue through the middle and around the broad lower body, then climb the outer right curve and circle the upper-right lobe without lifting.

## Writing — notice and trace
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-SCRIPT-CHHA-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: observe-trace -->

Finger-trace **છ** once, then air-trace it once. Notice two high lobes around a broad lower body; no pen lift.

## Writing — copy with the model
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-SCRIPT-CHHA-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: guided-copy -->

Copy visible **છ** once. Compare only the landmark named above and repair it once if needed.

## Writing — recall after hiding
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-SCRIPT-CHHA-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: delayed-copy -->
<!-- hl-activity: {"id":"GU-W03-chha-recall","kind":"text","assesses":["GU-SCRIPT-CHHA-01"],"prompt":"Hide the model and write the consonant chha from memory.","answer":"છ","accepted":[],"feedback":{"correct":"છ — the taught form is intact.","incorrect":"Look once at છ, hide it, and repair only the differing landmark."},"response_seconds":20} -->

Hide the model and write *chha* once. Uncover **છ**, compare, and stop after one repair.
