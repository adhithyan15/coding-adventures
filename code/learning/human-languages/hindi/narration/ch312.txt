Hindi, chapter 312: The Morning News.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
रेडियो (reḍiyo) — a radio.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for to collect, then the Hindi for spirit.

You'll want to know: रेडियो.
रेडियो — reḍiyo — "a radio".
दादा जी हर सुबह रेडियो सुनते हैं। dādā jī har subah reḍiyo sunte haiṁ. Grandfather listens to the radio every morning.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
रेडियो (reḍiyo) is borrowed from English radio, taken into Hindi as a masculine noun: रेडियो पर, on the radio.

Lesson 2 of 5.
समाचार (samācār) — the news (formal); a news report.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for spirit, then the Hindi for a radio.

You'll want to know: समाचार.
समाचार — samācār — "the news (formal); a news report".
रेडियो पर सुबह आठ बजे समाचार आते हैं। reḍiyo par subah āṭh baje samācār āte haiṁ. The news is on the radio at eight in the morning.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
समाचार (samācār) is Sanskrit samācāra (Monier-Williams), first conduct and practice and then news of what someone has done. It is the word of the broadcast; ख़बर is the word of conversation.

Lesson 3 of 5.
प्रसारण (prasāraṇ) — a broadcast.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a radio, then the Hindi for the news (formal).

You'll want to know: प्रसारण.
प्रसारण — prasāraṇ — "a broadcast".
कल रात तूफ़ान की वजह से प्रसारण बंद रहा। kal rāt tūfān kī vajah se prasāraṇ band rahā. Last night the broadcast was off because of the storm.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
प्रसारण (prasāraṇ) is Sanskrit prasāraṇa, "a spreading out, an extending" (Monier-Williams): news spread out over the air. The sense of broadcasting is modern.

Lesson 4 of 5.
सुर्ख़ी (surkhī) — a headline.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for the news (formal), then the Hindi for a broadcast.

You'll want to know: सुर्ख़ी.
सुर्ख़ी — surkhī — "a headline".
आज अख़बार की सबसे बड़ी सुर्ख़ी बाढ़ पर है। āj akhbār kī sabse baṛī surkhī bāṛh par hai. Today the newspaper's biggest headline is about the flood.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
सुर्ख़ी (surkhī) is usually traced to Persian surkhī, "redness", from surkh, red; it is said that headings were once set in red. सुर्ख़ियों में होना is to be in the headlines.

Lesson 5 of 5.
छपना (chapnā) — to be printed; to appear in print.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a broadcast, then the Hindi for a headline.

You'll want to know: छपना.
छपना — chapnā — "to be printed; to appear in print".
हादसे की ख़बर सब अख़बारों में छपी। hādse kī khabar sab akhbāroṁ meṁ chapī. The news of the accident was printed in every newspaper.
Past (it …): वह ख़बर छपी, vah khabar chapī.

The word, taken apart.
छपना (chapnā) is the intransitive partner of छापना, to print, which CDIAL 4994 sets under chapp, a pressing: what is printed छपता है.
