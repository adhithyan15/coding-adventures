Urdu, chapter 531: Trust and a Good Name.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
امانت (amānat) — something left in one's keeping.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for turned down, then the Urdu for to turn back.

You'll want to know: امانت.
امانت — amānat — "something left in one's keeping".
اگر اس نے امانت لوٹائی تو میں خوش ہوں گا۔ agar us ne amānat lauṭāī to maiṅ khush hūṅ gā. If he gives back what I left with him, I will be glad.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
امانت (amānat) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic amāna, "trustworthiness; a thing entrusted".

What you've built.
One more word for saying what happens if.

Lesson 3 of 5.
پاسداری (pāsdārī) — keeping to (a rule or a promise); honouring.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for something left in one's keeping, then the Urdu for one's given word.

You'll want to know: پاسداری.
پاسداری — pāsdārī — "keeping to (a rule or a promise); honouring".
اگر سب قانون کی پاسداری کرتے تو حادثے کم ہوتے۔ agar sab qānūn kī pāsdārī karte to hādse kam hote. If everyone kept to the law, there would be fewer accidents.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
پاسداری (pāsdārī) is usually traced to Persian pāsdārī, "guarding, keeping", built from pās, "watch, regard", -dār, "holding", and the noun ending -ī.

اگر دکاندار جھوٹ بولتا تو اس کی ساکھ جاتی۔ agar dukāndār jhūṭ boltā to us kī sākh jātī. If the shopkeeper told lies, his good name would be gone.

The word, taken apart.

Lesson 5 of 5.
نبھنا (nibhnā) — to be kept up; to last (of a bond).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for honouring a rule, then the Urdu for a good name.

You'll want to know: نبھنا.
نبھنا — nibhnā — "to be kept up; to last (of a bond)".
اگر دونوں سچے ہیں تو دوستی نبھے گی۔ agar donoṅ sachche haiṅ to dostī nibhe gī. If both of them are true friends, the friendship will last.
Past (it …): وہ دوستی نبھ گئی, voh dostī nibh gaī.

The word, taken apart.
نبھنا (nibhnā) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin nibhnā, "to last, to succeed", under Sanskrit nirvahati, "leads out, accomplishes" (CDIAL 7397).
