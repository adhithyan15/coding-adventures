## Script — 者, then the ear on the right
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-SCRIPT-DOU-01]; assesses=[] -->

> 都

**Two pieces, left before right, and every stroke in them is one you have made.**

**者, eight strokes**: first the foot of 在, a short bar, a vertical through it, then the long bar; then a long left-falling stroke from the upper right; then 日 under them.

**The ear piece, two strokes**, on the right, as 那 ends: across, back, round and hooking; then the long vertical.

8 + 2 = ten.
