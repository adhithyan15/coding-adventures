## You'll want to know: تأمل
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C395-MARJI395-05]; assesses=[] -->

**تأمل** — *taʾammala* — "to reflect".

> تأمل الرجل البحر طويلا.
>
> *taʾammala ar-rajul al-baḥr ṭawīlan.*
>
> The man contemplated the sea for a long time.

Present: يتأمل, *yataʾammal*.

It takes a direct object or في: تأمل في ….

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**تأمل** (*taʾammala*) is built on the native Arabic root *ʾ-m-l*, "to hope for" (Lane). It is Form V: to gaze on a thing long and carefully, to reflect on it.
