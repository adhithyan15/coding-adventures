## You'll want to know: il prurito
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C486-PRURITO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**il prurito** — "itch, itching".

> Questa lana mi dà prurito.
>
> *This wool makes me itch.*

*Avere prurito* — to itch.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*prurito* is a learned borrowing from Latin *prūrītus* "an itching" (Lewis & Short), from *prūrīre* "to itch".
