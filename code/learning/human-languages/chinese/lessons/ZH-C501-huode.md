## You'll want to know: huòdé
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C501-WINNERS501-04]; assesses=[] -->

*huòdé* — "to win (a prize); to be awarded".

> *tā huòdé le liǎng kuài jīnpái* — She won two gold medals.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*huòdé* is built from *huò* 'to get' and 得 'to gain', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *hweak* and *tok* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to get' and the second 'gain and loss'.
