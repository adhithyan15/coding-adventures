# Review — would, if only, and it depends

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[AR-GRAMMAR-C291-IDHA-PAST-01, AR-GRAMMAR-C291-IN-01, AR-GRAMMAR-C291-FA-RESULT-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "if it rains, we'll watch a film at home". (*Idhā amṭarat,
fa-sa-nushāhidu fīlman fī al-manzil*.) Then "if you arrive early", with the
other "if". (*In waṣalta mubakkiran*.)

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[AR-GRAMMAR-C292-DEPENDS-01, AR-GRAMMAR-C292-HASABA-01, AR-GRAMMAR-C292-LAW-01, AR-GRAMMAR-C292-LAYTA-01, AR-LEX-C292-SHART292-01] -->

Three lessons, and three distances from the facts. *Idhā*: it may well
happen, *idhā kāna ʿindī waqt, sa-usāfiru*. *Yaʿtamidu ʿalā*: it is still
open, *yaʿtamidu ʿalā al-waqt*. *Law*: it is not so, *law kāna ʿindī waqt
la-sāfartu*; and *layta*, the wish with no result.

[PAUSE 2s] All three, on one question:

> — *hal sa-tusāfiru?* — *yaʿtamidu ʿalā al-ʿamal. law kāna ʿindī waqt
> la-sāfartu al-usbūʿa al-qādim.*

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[AR-GRAMMAR-C292-LAW-01, AR-GRAMMAR-C292-DEPENDS-01, AR-GRAMMAR-C290-ELATIVE-01, AR-LEX-C289-TAJRIBA289-01, AR-LEX-C288-SAFAR288-03, AR-LEX-C282-AMAL282-04, AR-LEX-C280-AMAL280-01, AR-LEX-C287-TARIQ287-01, AR-GRAMMAR-C286-YANBAGHI-01, AR-GRAMMAR-C287-IMPERATIVE-01, AR-GRAMMAR-C287-DIRECTIONS-01] -->

[PAUSE 1s]
- [YOU SAY: "It depends on the person in charge" — *yaʿtamidu ʿalā al-masʾūl*]
- [YOU SAY: "If I were you, I would ask the way" — *law kuntu makānaka la-saʾaltu: kayfa aṣilu ilā al-maḥaṭṭa?*]
- [YOU SAY: "You ought to ask the client" — *yanbaghī an tasʾala al-ʿamīl*]
- [YOU SAY: "Take the first street on the right" — *khudh ash-shāriʿa al-awwala ʿalā al-yamīn*]
- [YOU SAY: "If only the sentence were shorter" — *layta al-jumlata kānat aqṣar*]
- [YOU SAY: "If it were a new experience, I would try it" — *law kānat tajribatan jadīdatan la-jarrabtuhā*]
- [YOU SAY: one thing you would do *law kāna ʿindī waqt*, and one you will do *idhā* something happens]

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[AR-GRAMMAR-C292-LAW-01, AR-GRAMMAR-C292-LAYTA-01, AR-GRAMMAR-C292-DEPENDS-01, AR-LEX-C292-SHART292-01] -->

[PAUSE 3s] Which "if" says it is not so? (***Law.***) What goes on its
result? (***La-.***) "It depends on the time"? (***Yaʿtamidu ʿalā
al-waqt.***) "If only"? (***Layta.***)
