## You'll want to know: کوهپایه
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C468-GHAR-04]; assesses=[] -->

**کوهپایه** (*kuhpâye*) — "the foothills".

> شهر ما در کوهپایه است و هوایش خنک است.
>
> *Shahr-e mâ dar kuhpâye ast va havâyash khonak ast.*
>
> Our town is in the foothills and its air is cool.

*kuh* 'mountain' + *pâye* 'base, foot'.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*kuhpâye* is a native compound of *kuh* 'mountain', from Middle Persian *kōf* (EtymDB), and *pâye* 'foot, base', from *pâ* 'foot'.
