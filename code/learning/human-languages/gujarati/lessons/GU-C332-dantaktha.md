## You'll want to know: દંતકથા
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C332-DANTKATHA332-01]; assesses=[] -->

**દંતકથા** — *dantakthā* — "a legend".

> દાદી અમને એક જૂની દંતકથા કહેતાં હતાં.
>
> *dādī amne ek jūnī dantakthā kahetā̃ hatā̃.*
>
> Grandmother used to tell us an old legend.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

is built with **કથા**, a tale, from Sanskrit *kathā*, "a story" (Monier-Williams); the history of its first part is not settled. A **દંતકથા** is told from one generation to the next.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
