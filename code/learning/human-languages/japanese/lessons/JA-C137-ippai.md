## You'll want to know: いっぱい
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-ANCHOR-IPPAI]; assesses=[JA-SCRIPT-I-01, JA-SCRIPT-SMALL-TSU-01] -->

**いっぱい** — *ippai* — "full", as a cup is full, or as you are after a big
meal. It also means "a lot": a lot of people, a lot of work.

Four beats: *i–p–pa–i*. **い**, the small **っ** that holds a beat, and the
last **い** you already write. The third sign is **は** with the small
circle at its upper right, and that circle makes a *p*: *ha* becomes *pa*.
The next lesson writes it.
