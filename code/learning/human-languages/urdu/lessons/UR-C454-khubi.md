## You'll want to know: خوبی
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C454-CHARACTER454-04]; assesses=[] -->

**خوبی** — *khūbī* — "a good quality; a strength".

> سچ بولنا اس کی سب سے بڑی خوبی ہے۔
>
> *sach bolnā us kī sab se baṛī khūbī hai.*
>
> Telling the truth is his greatest strength.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**خوبی** (*khūbī*) is usually traced to Persian *khūbī*, "goodness", built on *khūb*, "good, fine".
