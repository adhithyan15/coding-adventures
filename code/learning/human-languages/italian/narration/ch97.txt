Italian, chapter 97: Comb, Rubbish, Broom, Washing machine, Armchair.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
il pettine — a comb.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for a toothbrush, then the Italian for toothpaste.

You'll want to know: il pettine.
il pettine — "a comb".

The word, taken apart.
pettine from Latin pecten, "comb" — inherited. (EtymDB 2.1).

lavatrice — built from lavare, "to wash", with -trice, the ending for a female doer. EtymDB 2.1 has no entry; the parts are plain to see.
