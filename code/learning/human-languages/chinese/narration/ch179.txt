Mandarin Chinese, chapter 179: Laundry Day.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
洗衣机 (xǐyījī) — washing machine.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for lately, then the Mandarin for in the small hours.

You'll want to know: 洗衣机.
洗衣机 — xǐyījī — "washing machine".
我家有洗衣机 wǒ jiā yǒu xǐyījī — We've got a washing machine at home.

The word, taken apart.
洗衣机 (xǐyījī) is built from 洗 'to wash', 衣 'clothes' and 机 'machine', all three inherited from Middle Chinese, sejX, 'j+j and kj+j (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription); the dictionary glosses the last as the trigger of a crossbow, and 'machine' is a later sense.

What you've built.

Lesson 4 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for washing powder, then the Mandarin for to hang out to dry.

You'll want to know: yùn.
yùn — "to iron".
wǒ yào yùn yīfu — I'm going to do the ironing.
Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
yùn is inherited from Middle Chinese 'jut (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'to smooth silk with fire'. That reading would regularly give yù, so how today's yùn came about is not settled.
