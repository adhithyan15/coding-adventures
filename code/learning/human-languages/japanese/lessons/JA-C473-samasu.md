## You'll want to know: さます
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C473-SIMMER473-05]; assesses=[] -->

**さます** — *samasu* — "to let cool".

> **おちゃを すこし さまして ください。** — *ocha o sukoshi samashite kudasai* — "Please let the tea cool a little."

Polite **さまします**, *samashimasu*; a godan verb. Past (polite): **ははが みそしるを さましました。**, *haha ga misoshiru o samashimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**さます** (*samasu*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji spelling was attached to it later (JMdict).
