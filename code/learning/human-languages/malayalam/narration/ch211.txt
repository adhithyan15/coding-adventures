Malayalam, chapter 211: Married couple, Bridegroom, Bride, Stranger, Party.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ദമ്പതികൾ (dampatikaḷ) — a married couple.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for a motorbike, then the Malayalam for a taxi.

You'll want to know: ദമ്പതികൾ.
ദമ്പതികൾ — dampatikaḷ — "a married couple".

The word, taken apart.
ദമ്പതികൾ (dampatikaḷ) is Sanskrit dampatī, "the lord and lady of the house, a married pair", with the Malayalam plural -kaḷ.

Warm-up.

You'll want to know: അപരിചിതൻ.

The word, taken apart.
അപരിചിതൻ (aparicitan) is Sanskrit aparicita, "not acquainted": a-, "not", with the root of paricaya, "acquaintance" (CDIAL 7807), and the masculine ending -n.
