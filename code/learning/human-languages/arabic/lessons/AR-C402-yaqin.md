## You'll want to know: يقين
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C402-HATMAN402-05]; assesses=[] -->

**يقين** — *yaqīn* — "certainty".

> عندي يقين بأنه صادق.
>
> *ʿindī yaqīn bi-annahu ṣādiq.*
>
> I am certain that he is honest.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**يقين** (*yaqīn*) is built on the native Arabic root *y-q-n*, usually traced to the sense "to be sure". *Yaqīn* is certainty, knowledge without doubt.
