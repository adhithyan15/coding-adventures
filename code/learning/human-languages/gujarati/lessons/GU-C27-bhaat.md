# Bhāt reaches the page

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-SCRIPT-BHAAT-01]; assesses=[GU-LEX-BHAAT-01] -->

[PAUSE 5s] Before anything is uncovered, say the Gujarati word for
**rice**.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-SCRIPT-BHAAT-01, GU-LEX-BHAAT-01, GU-SCRIPT-BHA-01, GU-SCRIPT-AA-MATRA-01, GU-SCRIPT-TA-01, GU-PERFORMANCE-PLATE-FIVE-ORAL-01] -->
<!-- hl-activity: {"id":"GU-C27-bhaat-reading","kind":"text","assesses":["GU-SCRIPT-BHAAT-01","GU-LEX-BHAAT-01"],"prompt":"Read this word aloud and give its meaning: ભાત.","answer":"bhāt — rice","accepted":["bhāt rice"],"feedback":{"correct":"You read bhāt from signs you already knew.","incorrect":"Name the pieces one at a time, then read the word again."},"response_seconds":15} -->

Uncover **ભાત** once. Three pieces, none new: **ભ** from the courtesy chapter, the **ા** stroke from the greeting, and **ત** beside it. Name the pieces aloud in
order, then read the whole word once. This is the only word of the chapter the
hand is asked for; the other four stay in the ear for now, which is the
gloss-first order rather than an omission.

## Writing — guided copy, model in view
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-SCRIPT-BHAAT-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: guided-copy -->
<!-- hl-activity: {"id":"GU-C27-bhaat-guided-copy","kind":"text","assesses":["GU-SCRIPT-BHAAT-01"],"prompt":"With the model in view, copy rice once.","answer":"ભાત","accepted":[],"feedback":{"correct":"ભાત is rice.","incorrect":"Copy the pieces in order, checking each against the model."},"response_seconds":20} -->

Keep the model in view and copy **ભાત** once. Repair one shape if it
needs it; do not start a row.
