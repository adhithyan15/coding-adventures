# la pieza — the part that is missing

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-C434-MAQ-02, ES-LEX-C434-MAQ-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say *la máquina* and *el taller*. The machine is in the workshop and
one small thing is wrong with it. Name that thing.

## You'll want to know: la pieza
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C434-MAQ-03]; assesses=[] -->

**la pieza** — the part, the piece, the component.

*Falta una pieza* — a part is missing. *Era una pieza pequeña* — it was a small
part. Both are what a repair shop says to you.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-C434-MAQ-03] -->

**la pieza** is wider than English *part*. It also names a **room** in some
regions, and a **piece of music** almost everywhere.

| the phrase | what it means |
|---|---|
| una pieza de la lavadora | a component |
| una pieza de Mozart | a piece of music |

A word that covers several English words is not being vague. It is naming
something English happens to split: *a discrete thing that belongs to a larger
whole*. Hold that idea and all three uses follow from it.
