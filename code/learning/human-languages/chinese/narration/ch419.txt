Mandarin Chinese, chapter 419: Arrivals.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
jiējī — to meet someone off a plane.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for to deal with the paperwork, then the Mandarin for over the weight limit.

You'll want to know: jiējī.
jiējī — "to meet someone off a plane".
míngtiān wǒ zuò chūzūchē qù jīchǎng jiējī — Tomorrow I'm taking a taxi to the airport to meet someone.
Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
jiējī is built from jiē 'to meet' and 机 'a plane', both inherited from Middle Chinese, tsjep and kj+j (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to join' and 'to meet'.

What you've built.
One more word for getting around.

Lesson 2 of 6.
duìhuàn — to exchange money.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for over the weight limit, then the Mandarin for to meet someone off a plane.

You'll want to know: duìhuàn.
duìhuàn — "to exchange money".
wǒ zài jīchǎng duìhuàn le yìxiē qián — I changed some money at the airport.
Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
duìhuàn is built from duì and huàn 'to exchange', both inherited from Middle Chinese, dwajH and hwanH (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first as one of the eight trigrams and 'to please', and the second 'to exchange'; the sense 'to exchange' for the first is not given there.
