## Chapters 278-314: 185 more A2 headwords

The last of four Telugu A2 vocabulary tranches: thirty-seven chapters of five
words, in five runs, each closed by two review lessons; forty-five are verbs.
Telugu reached A2 with the third tranche, 45 headwords over the floor; this
one takes it to 1,430 headwords and 256 verbs at or below A2, and its verbs
close the B1 verb gap (250) as well.
