Russian, chapter 288: Theft.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
кража (krázha) — a theft.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a clue, then the Russian for a witness.

You'll want to know: кража.
кража (krázha) — "a theft".
В магазине была кража. V magazíne bylá krázha. There was a theft at the shop.
Feminine. Кража со взломом — burglary.

The word, taken apart.
кража (krázha) is inherited from Proto-Slavic kradja "a theft" (EtymDB), built on krasti "to steal".

You'll want to know: вор.
вор (vor) — "a thief".
Вор взял сумку и убежал. Vor vzyal súmku i ubezhál. The thief took the bag and ran off.
Genitive вора, plural воры, воров.

The word, taken apart.
вор (vor) is an old Russian word whose earlier sense was "a rogue, a rebel"; its origin is not settled, and EtymDB lists no parent.

Lesson 3 of 5.
красть (krast) — to steal.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a theft, then the Russian for a thief.

You'll want to know: красть.
красть (krast) — "to steal".
Кто-то украл мой велосипед. Kto-to ukrál moy velosipéd. Someone stole my bicycle.
Imperfective; perfective украсть (ukrást). Present краду, крадёшь (kradyósh); past крал, крала.

The word, taken apart.
красть (krast) is inherited from Proto-Slavic krasti "to steal", which EtymDB traces to PIE kreh₂d(ʰ)-.

Lesson 4 of 5.

Warm-up.

You'll want to know: карманник.
карманник (karmánnik) — "a pickpocket".
В автобусе работал карманник. V avtóbuse rabótal karmánnik. A pickpocket was at work on the bus.
Genitive карманника.

The word, taken apart.
карманник (karmánnik) is built on карман "a pocket", which is usually traced to a Turkic word for a purse; EtymDB lists карман "pocket" with no parent.

Warm-up.

You'll want to know: взлом.
взлом (vzlom) — "a break-in, forced entry".
Соседи не заметили взлома. Sosédi ne zamétili vzlóma. The neighbours did not notice the break-in.
Со взломом — by force.

The word, taken apart.
взлом (vzlom) is built on вз- and the root of ломать "to break", an inherited Slavic verb: a breaking open. EtymDB lists no parent.
