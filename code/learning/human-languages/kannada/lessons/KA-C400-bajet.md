## You'll want to know: ಬಜೆಟ್
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C400-BAJET400-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ಬಜೆಟ್** — *bajeṭ* — "a budget".

> ಈ ಸಲದ ಬಜೆಟ್ ರೈತರಿಗೆ ಒಳ್ಳೆಯದು.
>
> *ī salada bajeṭ raitarige oḷḷeyadu.*
>
> This year's budget is good for farmers.

ಮುಂಗಡ ಪತ್ರ is the formal Kannada name.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಬಜೆಟ್** (*bajeṭ*) is a loan from English *budget*.
