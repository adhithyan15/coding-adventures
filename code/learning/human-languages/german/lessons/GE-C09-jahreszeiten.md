# Practice — the four, and the seam

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GE-LEX-JAHR-01, GE-LEX-ZEIT-01, GE-LEX-JAHRESZEIT-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] No new words. A year, a time, and the four things they make between
them.

## Grammar Lens: the four seasons
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GE-LEX-FRUEHLING-01, GE-LEX-SOMMER-01, GE-LEX-HERBST-01, GE-LEX-WINTER-01, GE-LEX-FRUEH-01] -->

| German | built from | English |
|---|---|---|
| *der Frühling* | *früh* + *-ling* | spring |
| *der Sommer* | inherited whole | summer |
| *der Herbst* | inherited whole | autumn |
| *der Winter* | inherited whole | winter |

Three of the four are the plain twins of their English words. The fourth was
built inside German out of a word you learned one lesson earlier.

Not one of them was borrowed.

## Why it's said this way
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GE-GRAMMAR-NATIVE-VS-LOAN-01, GE-LEX-MONAT-01, GE-ETYMON-HERBST-HARVEST-01, GE-LEX-LENZ-01] -->

Hold the last three chapters together and the seam is exact.

* The **months** came from Rome, all twelve, names and calendar together.
* The **year**, the **time**, and the **seasons** did not. *Jahr*, *Zeit*,
  *Jahreszeit*, and the four seasons are German's own, and *Frühling* was still
  being built out of German parts long after the month names had arrived.

*Herbst* is the sharpest place to see it, because there English went the other
way: it kept the inherited word for the **work** and bought *autumn* for the
**season**. Two languages, one starting word, opposite outcomes — and the German
one is the one that needed no purchase.

And German kept a spare. *Lenz* is still there for spring, older than
*Frühling*, unneeded and unretired.

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GE-LEX-JAHRESZEIT-01, GE-ETYMON-HERBST-HARVEST-01, GE-LEX-LENZ-01, GE-GRAMMAR-NATIVE-VS-LOAN-01, GE-LEX-WINTER-01] -->

[PAUSE 3s] Name the four seasons. (**Der Frühling, der Sommer, der Herbst, der
Winter**.) Which one was built rather than inherited? (***Frühling***, from

did German borrow? (**None**.) And how many of the twelve month names?
(**All of them**.)
