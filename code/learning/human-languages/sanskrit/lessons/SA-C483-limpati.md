## You'll want to know: लिम्पति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C483-GUTIKA483-05]; assesses=[] -->

**लिम्पति** — *limpati* — "smears".

> वैद्यः व्रणे लेपं लिम्पति।
>
> *vaidyaḥ vraṇe lepaṃ limpati.*
>
> The physician smears ointment on the wound.

Past (he …): सः व्रणे लेपं लिप्तवान्, *saḥ vraṇe lepaṃ liptavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**लिम्पति** (*limpati*) comes from the root √lip, "to smear, to anoint", whose present stem takes a nasal (*li-m-pa*) (MW).
