## You'll want to know: बासण
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C171-BASAN171-01]; assesses=[] -->

**बासण** — *bāsaṇ* — "a utensil; a pot".

> रसोई मांय घणा बासण है।
>
> *rasoī mā̃y ghaṇā bāsaṇ hai.*
>
> There are a lot of pots in the kitchen.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**बासण** (*bāsaṇ*) is inherited from Sanskrit *vāsana*, "a box, a casket"; CDIAL lists Hindi *bāsan* and Gujarati *vāsaṇ*, "a pot, a dish" (CDIAL 11599). CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry.
