Sanskrit, chapter 77: Rooms of the House.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
प्रकोष्ठः (prakoṣṭhaḥ) — a room.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for spring, then the Sanskrit for summer.

You'll want to know: प्रकोष्ठः.
प्रकोष्ठः (prakoṣṭhaḥ) — "a room" of a गृहम्.

What you've built.
One part of the house.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: prakoṣṭhaḥ]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: prakoṣṭhaḥ, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say prakoṣṭhaḥ]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Sanskrit for spring, then the Sanskrit for summer, then say prakoṣṭhaḥ again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does प्रकोष्ठः (prakoṣṭhaḥ) mean? ("A room".) Say it once more.

Lesson 6 of 7.
How are you, really? — a first pass.

Warm-up.
[pause 3 seconds]
Name the roof, then ask where the key is.

Guided Practice.
[pause 2 seconds after each]
[your turn — say: you have a fever; you have a cough; you have a headache; name illness; wish someone health]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: sleep; you are hungry; you are thirsty; rest; all is well]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: sorrow, fear, anger, shyness, joy]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: the eye, the throat, the belly, the forehead, the tongue]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: a nail, the knee, the back, the shoulder, the lip]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
Fever? (ज्वरः.) Cough? (कासः.) Pain? (पीडा.) Illness? (रोगः.) Health? (आरोग्यम्.) Sleep, hunger, thirst, rest, ease? (निद्रा, क्षुधा, पिपासा, विश्रामः, सुखम्.) Sorrow, fear, anger, shyness, joy? (दुःखम्, भयम्, क्रोधः, लज्जा, हर्षः.) Eye, throat, belly, forehead, tongue? (नेत्रम्, कण्ठः, उदरम्, ललाटम्, जिह्वा.) Nail, knee, back, shoulder, lip? (नखः, जानु, पृष्ठम्, स्कन्धः, ओष्ठः.)

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
Runs, walks, sits down, gets up, falls? (धावति, चलति, उपविशति, उत्तिष्ठति, पतति.) Cooks, sleeps, washes, brings, gives? (पचति, स्वपिति, प्रक्षालयति, आनयति, ददाति.) Sings, tells, cries, calls, bows? (गायति, कथयति, रोदिति, आह्वयति, नमति.) Buys? (क्रीणाति.) Money, price, shop, market? (धनम्, मूल्यम्, आपणः, विपणिः.) Vehicle, chariot, boat, aeroplane? (वाहनम्, रथः, नौका, विमानम्.) Goes in? (प्रविशति.) Rain, cold, heat, spring, summer? (वर्षा, शीतम्, उष्णम्, वसन्तः, ग्रीष्मः.) Room, kitchen, bathroom, roof, key? (प्रकोष्ठः (prakoṣṭhaḥ), पाकशाला (pākaśālā), स्नानगृहम् (snānagṛham), छदिः (chadiḥ), कुञ्चिका (kuñcikā).)
