German, chapter 426: An der Rezeption.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
die Rezeption — the reception (desk).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to put up, then the German for to take down.

You'll want to know: die Rezeption.
die Rezeption — "the reception (desk)".
Den Schlüssel bekommt man an der Rezeption. You get the key at reception.
Plural: die Rezeptionen. Also der Empfang.

The word, taken apart.
Rezeption is a learned borrowing from Latin receptiō 'a receiving' (EtymDB), from recipere 'to take back, to receive' (Lewis & Short), probably through French réception.

What you've built.
One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.

Lesson 2 of 5.
anreisen — to arrive (at a place you are staying).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to take down, then the German for the reception.

You'll want to know: anreisen.
anreisen — "to arrive (at a place you are staying)".
Die Gäste reisen am Freitag an und bleiben eine Woche. The guests arrive on Friday and stay for a week.
Separable; Perfekt with sein. Der Anreisetag — the day of arrival.

The word, taken apart.
anreisen is built from an 'at, to' and reisen 'to travel', inherited from Old High German reisōn 'to set out' (EtymDB).

Lesson 4 of 5.
der Aufenthalt — the stay.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to arrive, then the German for to leave.

You'll want to know: der Aufenthalt.
der Aufenthalt — "the stay".
Wir wünschen Ihnen einen schönen Aufenthalt. We wish you a pleasant stay.
Plural: die Aufenthalte. At a station, der Aufenthalt is also how long a train stops.

The word, taken apart.
Aufenthalt is inherited from Middle High German ūfenthalt 'stay' (EtymDB), built on halten 'to hold, to stop', from Proto-Germanic haldaną.

Lesson 5 of 5.
auschecken — to check out.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to leave, then the German for the stay.

You'll want to know: auschecken.
auschecken — "to check out".
Bis elf Uhr müssen die Gäste auschecken. The guests have to check out by eleven o'clock.
Separable: ich checke aus, ich habe ausgecheckt.

The word, taken apart.
auschecken is built from aus 'out' and checken, borrowed from English check (EtymDB), on the model of English check out.
