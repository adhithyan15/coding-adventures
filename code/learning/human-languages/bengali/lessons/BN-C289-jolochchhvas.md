## You'll want to know: জলোচ্ছ্বাস
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C289-JOLOCHCHHAS289-01]; assesses=[] -->

**জলোচ্ছ্বাস** — *jôlochchhvās* — "a tidal surge".

> ঘূর্ণিঝড়ের সঙ্গে একটা বড় জলোচ্ছ্বাস এল।
>
> *ghūrnijhôṛer sôngge ekṭā bôṛo jôlochchhvās elô.*
>
> A great tidal surge came with the cyclone.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**জলোচ্ছ্বাস** (*jôlochchhvās*) joins **জল**, water, to Sanskrit *ucchvāsa*, 'a breathing out, a deep breath' (Monier-Williams): the sea heaving up over the land.
