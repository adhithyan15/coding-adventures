## You'll want to know: ਟੂਟੀ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C227-DIN227-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ਟੂਟੀ** — *ṭūṭī* — "a tap; a spout".

> ਮੈਂ ਟੂਟੀ ਖੋਲ੍ਹਦੀ ਹਾਂ ਅਤੇ ਮੂੰਹ ਧੋਂਦੀ ਹਾਂ।
>
> *maiṁ ṭūṭī kholhdī hāṁ ate mūnh dhondī hāṁ.*
>
> I turn on the tap and wash my face.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਟੂਟੀ** (*ṭūṭī*) is inherited from Sanskrit *tuṇḍa*, "beak, snout"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *ṭūṭī*, "spout" (CDIAL 5853).
