## You'll want to know: bescheiden
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C475-ANGEBER-04]; assesses=[] -->

**bescheiden** — "modest".

> Trotz ihres Erfolgs ist die Sängerin bescheiden geblieben.
>
> *Despite her success, the singer has stayed modest.*

Of things it means modest, small: *ein bescheidenes Gehalt*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*bescheiden* is usually traced to the past participle of Middle High German *bescheiden* "to decide, to allot", built on *scheiden* "to separate"; EtymDB lists the German word without a source.
