## You'll want to know: bēizi
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C58-THINGS58-04]; assesses=[] -->

*bēizi* — "cup; glass".

> *zhè shì nǐ de bēizi ma* — Is this your cup?

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*bēizi* is built from bēi and the noun ending zi, both inherited from Middle Chinese, *pwoj* and *tsiX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription). The dictionary explains the first, through an older book, as a kind of vessel.
