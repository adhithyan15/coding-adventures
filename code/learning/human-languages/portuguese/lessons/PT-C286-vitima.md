## You'll want to know: a vítima
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C286-DELITO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**a vítima** — "the victim".

> A vítima foi levada para o hospital.
>
> *The victim was taken to hospital.*

Feminine in form for a man or a woman: *ele foi a vítima*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*vítima* is a learned borrowing from Latin *victima* "an animal offered in sacrifice" (Lewis & Short).
