# మెచ్చుకో (meccukō) — to appreciate; to praise

## You'll want to know: మెచ్చుకో
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C504-ABHIMANAM504-04]; assesses=[] -->

**మెచ్చుకో** — *meccukō* — "to appreciate; to praise".

> నాన్న నా బొమ్మను మెచ్చుకున్నారు.
>
> *nānna nā bommanu meccukunnāru.*
>
> Dad praised my picture.

Past (I …): మెచ్చుకున్నాను, *meccukunnānu*.

Warmer than పొగడు, which can be flattery: మెచ్చుకో is honest praise.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**మెచ్చుకో** (*meccukō*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 4722 lists Telugu *meccu*, "to admire, to praise, to approve", in the article of Tamil *maruvu*; -కో is the reflexive ending.
