## You'll want to know: भूकंप
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C293-BHUKAMP293-01]; assesses=[] -->

**भूकंप** — *bhūkamp* — "an earthquake".

> जर भूकंप झाला तर आम्ही मोकळ्या मैदानात जाऊ.
>
> *jar bhūkamp jhālā tar āmhī mokaḷyā maidānāt jāū.*
>
> If there is an earthquake, we will go out to open ground.

Masculine.

भूकंप होणे, an earthquake happens; जर … झाला तर, if one happens, with the past after जर and the future after तर.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**भूकंप** (*bhūkamp*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *bhūkampa*, 'an earthquake' (Monier-Williams): *bhū*, 'the earth', and *kampa*, 'a trembling'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
