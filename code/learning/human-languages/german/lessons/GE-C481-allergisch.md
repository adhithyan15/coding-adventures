## You'll want to know: allergisch
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C481-ALLERGIE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**allergisch** — "allergic".

> Ich bin allergisch gegen Katzen.
>
> *I'm allergic to cats.*

*Allergisch gegen* + accusative, also as a joke: *allergisch gegen Montage*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*allergisch* is built on *Allergie* with the ending *-isch* (EtymDB); *Allergie* is a modern learned word from Greek *állos* "other" and *érgon* "work".
