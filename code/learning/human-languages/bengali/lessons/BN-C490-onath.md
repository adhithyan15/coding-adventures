## You'll want to know: অনাথ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C490-BICHCHHED490-03]; assesses=[] -->

**অনাথ** — *ônāth* — "an orphan; orphaned".

> বন্যায় বাবাকে হারিয়ে ছেলেটা অনাথ হল।
>
> *bônyāy bābāke hāriye chheleṭā ônāth hôlô.*
>
> Losing his father in the flood, the boy was orphaned.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**অনাথ** (*ônāth*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *anātha*, "having no master or protector" (Monier-Williams).
