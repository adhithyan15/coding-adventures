## You'll want to know: геймер
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C528-GAMING-05]; assesses=[] -->

**геймер** (*géymer*) — "a gamer".

> Мой брат — настоящий геймер.
>
> *Moy brat — nastoyáshchiy géymer.*
>
> My brother is a real gamer.

Masculine, colloquial.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*геймер* is usually traced to English *gamer*; EtymDB has no entry.
