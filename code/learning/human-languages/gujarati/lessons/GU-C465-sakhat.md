## You'll want to know: સખત
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C465-SAKHAT465-01]; assesses=[] -->

**સખત** — *sakhat* — "strict; severe".

> અમારા આચાર્ય ઘણા સખત છે.
>
> *amārā ācārya ghaṇā sakhat che.*
>
> Our principal is very strict.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**સખત** (*sakhat*) is usually traced to Persian *saxt*, "hard, firm".
