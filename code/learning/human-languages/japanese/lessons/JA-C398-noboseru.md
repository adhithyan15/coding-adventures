## You'll want to know: のぼせる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C398-ONSEN398-03]; assesses=[] -->

**のぼせる** — *noboseru* — "to feel dizzy (from the heat of a bath)".

> **ながく おふろに はいって、のぼせました。** — *nagaku ofuro ni haitte, nobosemashita* — "I stayed in the bath too long and felt dizzy."

Polite **のぼせます**, *nobosemasu*; an ichidan verb. Past (polite): **あにが おふろで のぼせました。**, *ani ga ofuro de nobosemashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**のぼせる** (*noboseru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese (JMdict).
