Sanskrit, chapter 268: Four services on one small map.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
Three service words are already yours — retrieve three familiar service words and the familiar location question.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say “here,” “there,” “where?”, and “is.” (Atra; tatra; kutra; asti.)

Guided Practice.
[pause 2 seconds after each]
[your turn — recall: पत्रालयः — patrālayaḥ — a post office]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: चिकित्सालयः — cikitsālayaḥ — a hospital]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: आरक्षकः — ārakṣakaḥ — a policeman]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

What you've built.
The post, health, and police referents were already in the book. You also already know how to ask where something is. One missing service word comes next.

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
Say the three service words again without looking.


Lesson 2 of 6.
वित्तकोषः (vittakoṣaḥ) — a financial bank.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Recall the Sanskrit for a riverbank, then the post office, hospital, and policeman. (Tīram; patrālayaḥ; cikitsālayaḥ; ārakṣakaḥ.)

You'll want to know: वित्तकोषः.
वित्तकोषः — vittakoṣaḥ — “a financial bank.” This is the institution that handles money, not the riverbank तीरम् that you already know.
The modern financial sense is explicitly attested in the Kosha Sanskrit Today “Places — Bank” glossary and in the Sanskrit Documents combined dictionary. This lesson teaches only that sourced financial-institution sense.

The word, taken apart.
वित्तकोषः (vittakoṣaḥ) is transparently formed from वित्त, “wealth, money,” and कोष, “treasury, store.” The compound names the financial institution; it does not change the older water-edge meaning of तीरम्.

Guided Practice.
[pause 2 seconds after each]
[your turn — say: वित्तकोषः (vittakoṣaḥ) — a financial bank]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — contrast: वित्तकोषः (vittakoṣaḥ) — financial bank; तीरम् — riverbank]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What is the sourced word in this lesson for a financial bank?


Lesson 3 of 6.
One small four-service location exchange — ask where four familiar services are and answer only here or there.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say “financial bank,” then “here,” “there,” “where?”, and “is.”

You'll want to know: four complete turns.
Keep the familiar question frame whole and change only its first word:
पत्रालयः कुत्र अस्ति? पत्रालयः तत्र अस्ति। — Where is the post office? The post office is there.
चिकित्सालयः कुत्र अस्ति? चिकित्सालयः अत्र अस्ति। — Where is the hospital? The hospital is here.
वित्तकोषः (vittakoṣaḥ) कुत्र अस्ति? वित्तकोषः (vittakoṣaḥ) तत्र अस्ति। — Where is the financial bank? The financial bank is there.
आरक्षकः कुत्र अस्ति? आरक्षकः अत्र अस्ति। — Where is the policeman? The policeman is here.
These four complete turns are the whole pattern. Do not use them to infer a new declension or a broader directions system.

Guided Practice.
[pause 3 seconds after each]
Ask where the post office is and answer “there.” Then ask where the hospital is and answer “here.”

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What stays fixed in all four questions? (Kutra asti?)


Lesson 4 of 6.
The second pair, from memory — retrieve the second pair of the bounded service-location exchange.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say “financial bank” and “policeman.”

Guided Practice.
[pause 4 seconds after each]
[your turn — ask and answer: Where is the financial bank? The financial bank is there.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — ask and answer: Where is the policeman? The policeman is here.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
Check only after speaking: वित्तकोषः (vittakoṣaḥ) कुत्र अस्ति? वित्तकोषः (vittakoṣaḥ) तत्र अस्ति। आरक्षकः कुत्र अस्ति? आरक्षकः अत्र अस्ति।.

What you've built.
You have now retrieved both halves of the four-service exchange. Nothing here adds directions, a police station, or a service transaction.

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
Ask the two questions once more, without looking.


Lesson 5 of 6.
Independent service locations: round one — independently complete one four-service location round without a visible model.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Recall the four small location pieces: here, there, where, is.

Guided Practice: one small map.
No Sanskrit model is shown before you answer.
[pause 12 seconds]
Ask where each of these is: the post office, hospital, financial bank, and policeman. Answer “there” for the post office and bank, and “here” for the hospital and policeman.

What you've built: boundary.
You can complete one small four-service location round. You have not yet learned directions, transactions, emergency requests, or institutional systems.

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
Name the four referents again without looking.


Lesson 6 of 6.
Independent service locations: round two — independently retrieve a second four-service location round without a visible model.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Contrast the financial bank with the riverbank one last time.

Guided Practice: a fresh arrangement.
No Sanskrit model is shown before you answer.
[pause 12 seconds]
Ask where each of the same four referents is. This time answer “here” for the post office and financial bank, and “there” for the hospital and policeman.

What you've built: boundary.
You can retrieve four service referents and one bounded location exchange. This is not evidence of directions, postal or banking transactions, medical or emergency communication, police-station vocabulary, human validation, learner success, or exam readiness.

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
Name the four services, then name the one exchange you can now complete with each.
