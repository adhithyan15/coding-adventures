## You'll want to know: xiànzhì
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C359-RULES359-04]; assesses=[] -->

*xiànzhì* — "to put a limit on; to restrict".

> *wǒmen yīnggāi xiànzhì háizi shàngwǎng de shíjiān* — We should limit how long children spend online.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*xiànzhì* is built from *xiàn* and *zhì*, both inherited from Middle Chinese, *heanX* and *tsyejH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'a measure' and 'a boundary', and the second 'to restrain'.
