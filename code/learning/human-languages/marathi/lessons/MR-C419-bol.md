## You'll want to know: बोळ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C419-VASTI419-04]; assesses=[] -->

**बोळ** — *boḷ* — "a narrow lane".

> या बोळातून बाजारात लवकर पोहोचता येतं.
>
> *yā boḷātūn bājārāt lavkar pohoctā yetaṁ.*
>
> You can reach the market quickly through this lane.

Masculine.

Narrower than a गल्ली, often a short cut.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**बोळ** (*boḷ*) is a Marathi word for a narrow passage between houses; its origin is not settled.
