# સાંધવું (sā̃dhvũ) — to mend (a tear)

## You'll want to know: સાંધવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C507-BHARAT507-03]; assesses=[] -->

**સાંધવું** — *sā̃dhvũ* — "to mend (a tear)".

> દરજીએ મારું જૂનું ખમીસ સાંધ્યું.
>
> *darjīe mārũ jūnũ khamīs sā̃dhyũ.*
>
> The tailor mended my old shirt.

Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે ખમીસ સાંધ્યું, *teṇe khamīs sā̃dhyũ*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**સાંધવું** (*sā̃dhvũ*) is inherited from Sanskrit *sáṁdadhāti*, "places on, puts together", and CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb, "to unite, attach" (CDIAL 12898).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
