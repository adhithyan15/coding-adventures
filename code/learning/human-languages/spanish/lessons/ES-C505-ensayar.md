## You'll want to know: ensayar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C505-ESTUDIO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ensayar** — "to rehearse, to practise".

> El grupo ensaya los viernes.
>
> *The band rehearses on Fridays.*

*El ensayo* is the rehearsal, or an essay.
