# ಉರಿಬಿಸಿಲು (uribisilu) — scorching sun

## You'll want to know: ಉರಿಬಿಸಿಲು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C488-BISIGALI488-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ಉರಿಬಿಸಿಲು** — *uribisilu* — "scorching sun".

> ಉರಿಬಿಸಿಲಿನಲ್ಲಿ ಕೂಲಿಗಳು ರಸ್ತೆ ಕೆಲಸ ಮಾಡುತ್ತಿದ್ದರು.
>
> *uribisilinalli kūligaḷu raste kelasa māḍuttiddaru.*
>
> Labourers were working on the road in the scorching sun.

Burning sunshine: the hottest hours of a summer day.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಉರಿಬಿಸಿಲು** (*uribisilu*) is a native Kannada compound of *uri*, "to burn, to blaze" (DEDR 656), and ಬಿಸಿಲು, "the heat and glare of the sun" (DEDR 5517).
