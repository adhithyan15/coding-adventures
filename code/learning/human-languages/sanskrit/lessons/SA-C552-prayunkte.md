## You'll want to know: प्रयुंक्ते
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C552-PRAYASA552-04]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रयुंक्ते** — *prayuṅkte* — "makes use of".

> यदि भवान् संगणकं प्रयुंक्ते, तर्हि कार्यं शीघ्रं भविष्यति।
>
> *yadi bhavān saṅgaṇakaṃ prayuṅkte, tarhi kāryaṃ śīghraṃ bhaviṣyati.*
>
> If you use a computer, the work will be quick.

Past (he …): सः संगणकं प्रयुक्तवान्, *saḥ saṅgaṇakaṃ prayuktavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रयुंक्ते** (*prayuṅkte*) is built on *pra*, "forth", and the root √yuj, "to yoke"; MW gives *prayuj* as to use, to employ, to practise (MW).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
