## You'll want to know: शेख़ी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C462-ABHIMAN462-03]; assesses=[] -->

**शेख़ी** — *śekhī* — "a boast; bragging".

> अरुण का दोस्त हमेशा अपनी गाड़ी की शेख़ी मारता है।
>
> *Aruṇ kā dost hameśā apnī gāṛī kī śekhī mārtā hai.*
>
> Arun's friend is always boasting about his car.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**शेख़ी** (*śekhī*) is usually traced to Persian *shēkhī*, "the airs of a sheikh", hence boasting. *śekhī mārnā* is "to brag".
