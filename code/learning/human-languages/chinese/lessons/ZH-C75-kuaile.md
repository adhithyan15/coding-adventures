## You'll want to know: 快乐
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C75-PARTY75-01]; assesses=[] -->

**快乐** — *kuàilè* — "joyful; happy".

> **我们很快乐** *wǒmen hěn kuàilè* — We're very happy.

The new character, in pieces: 乐 — a nearly flat fall, a turn, and then 小 with its middle hooked. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**快乐** (*kuàilè*) is built from 快 and 乐, both inherited from Middle Chinese, *khwaejH* and *lak* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription). The dictionary glosses the first as 'glad' and the second as 'joy': gladness twice over.
