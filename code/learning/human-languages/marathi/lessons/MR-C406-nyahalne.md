## You'll want to know: न्याहाळणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C406-PADPATH406-05]; assesses=[] -->

**न्याहाळणे** — *nyāhāḷṇe* — "to gaze at".

> मुलं दुकानातली खेळणी न्याहाळत होती.
>
> *mulaṁ dukānātlī kheḷṇī nyāhāḷat hotī.*
>
> The children were gazing at the toys in the shop.

Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने न्याहाळलं, *tyāne nyāhāḷlaṁ*.

A slow, careful look; the thing looked at is the object.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**न्याहाळणे** (*nyāhāḷṇe*) is a Marathi verb for looking at something slowly and closely; its origin is not settled.
