Marwadi (Marwari), chapter 227: The Village Well.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
कूवो (kūvo) — a well.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for dry land, then the Marwadi for laid waste.

You'll want to know: कूवो.
कूवो — kūvo — "a well".
गांव मांय एक कूवो है। gā̃v mā̃y ek kūvo hai. There's a well in the village.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
कूवो (kūvo) is inherited from Sanskrit kūpa, "a hole, a pit, a well"; CDIAL lists Marwari kūvo, a well without a masonry lining (CDIAL 3400).

कूवो घणो ऊंडो है। kūvo ghaṇo ū̃ḍo hai. The well is very deep.

The word, taken apart.
is inherited from a reconstructed ūṇḍa, "deep"; CDIAL lists Marwari ū̃ṛo (CDIAL 2402). गैरो is deep too; ऊंडो (ū̃ḍo) is said especially of a well or a pit that goes far down.

Lesson 4 of 5.
रहट (rahaṭ) — a Persian wheel (for raising water from a well).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for deep down, then the Marwadi for thirsty.

You'll want to know: रहट.
रहट — rahaṭ — "a Persian wheel (for raising water from a well)".
रहट दिनूगै चालै है। rahaṭ dinūgai chālai hai. The Persian wheel turns early in the morning.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
रहट (rahaṭ) is inherited from Sanskrit araghaṭṭa, "a wheel for raising water"; CDIAL lists Hindi rahaṭ, "a Persian wheel", and a Gujarati word for a waterwheel. CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry (CDIAL 596).

Lesson 5 of 5.
दुकाळ (dukāḷ) — a drought; a famine.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for thirsty, then the Marwadi for a Persian wheel.

You'll want to know: दुकाळ.
दुकाळ — dukāḷ — "a drought; a famine".
दुकाळ मांय पाणी कोनी। dukāḷ mā̃y pāṇī konī. In a drought there's no water.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
दुकाळ (dukāḷ) is inherited from Sanskrit duṣkāla, "an evil time", built from duṣ-, "bad", and kāla, "time"; CDIAL lists Old Marwari dūkāla, "famine" (CDIAL 6470).
