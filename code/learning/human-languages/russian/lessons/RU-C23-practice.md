# Payoff — one to five, and an answer to сколько

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[RU-LEX-SKOLKO-01, RU-LEX-PYAT-01, RU-ETYMON-ODIN-OYNOS] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Ask **сколько**, say **пять**, then say what **один** goes back to.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-PERFORMANCE-COUNT-FIVE]; assesses=[RU-LEX-ODIN-01, RU-LEX-DVA-01, RU-LEX-TRI-01, RU-LEX-CHETYRE-01, RU-LEX-PYAT-01, RU-ETYMON-DVA-DWOH, RU-ETYMON-TRI-TREYES, RU-ETYMON-CHETYRE-KWETWORES, RU-ETYMON-PYAT-PENKWE] -->

1. Hear each of the five named singly and out of order, and say what it is.
2. Count backwards from **пять** to **один**.
3. Read the five printed shuffled — **пять, один, четыре, три, два** — and say
   each.
4. Write all five from dictation. No new Cyrillic letter appears in any of them.
5. Hear **сколько?** and answer it with each of the five in turn.

Then say the English cousin of each: **one, two, three, four, five**. All five
have one. That is not luck — Russian's numerals are among the least worn in the
family, and this book's reader already owned most of them.
