## You'll want to know: फूल
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C188-PHUL188-01]; assesses=[] -->

**फूल** — *phūl* — "a flower".

> ये फूल लाल है।
>
> *ye phūl lāl hai.*
>
> This flower is red.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**फूल** (*phūl*) is inherited from Sanskrit *phulla*, "blown (of a flower)"; CDIAL lists Old Marwari *phūla* (CDIAL 9092).
