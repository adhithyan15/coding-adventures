# रेल (rel) — the railway

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[HI-LEX-C69-GOING-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say *gāṛī*, then write **गाड़ी**: ग, then the ा stroke, then ड with
its nuqta, then ी. Four pieces, all of them yours already.

## You'll want to know: रेल
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C69-GOING-02]; assesses=[] -->

**रेल** (*rel*) — "the railway".

English *rail*, borrowed whole in the nineteenth century and spelt with the े
matra, which is where an English *ay* sound usually lands in Devanagari.

रेल on its own is the system: the network, the line, the railway as an
institution. The train itself is **रेलगाड़ी** — रेल plus the गाड़ी you learnt in
the last lesson — and in speech people shorten that back to plain गाड़ी. Two
words you now hold combine into the third without anything new.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

गाड़ी and रेल, and the compound they make between them.
