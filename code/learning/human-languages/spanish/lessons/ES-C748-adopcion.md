## You'll want to know: la adopción
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C748-NIETOS-03]; assesses=[] -->

**la adopción** — "adoption".

> La adopción tardó dos años.
>
> *The adoption took two years.*

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*adopción* is a learned borrowing from Latin *adoptiō* (EtymDB), from *adoptāre* "to choose for oneself".
