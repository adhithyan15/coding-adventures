## You'll want to know: いじめる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C478-GROW478-03]; assesses=[] -->

**いじめる** — *ijimeru* — "to bully".

> **ともだちを いじめないで ください。** — *tomodachi o ijimenaide kudasai* — "Please do not bully your friends."

Polite **いじめます**, *ijimemasu*; an ichidan verb. Past (polite): **あにが おとうとを いじめました。**, *ani ga otōto o ijimemashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**いじめる** (*ijimeru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; JMdict writes it in kana (JMdict).
