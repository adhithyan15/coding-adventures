# కంటే (kaṇṭē) — bigger than, best of all

## Warm-up

"I would like to go to my home town". (*nāku mā ūriki veḷḷālani undi*.)

## You'll want to know: X కంటే
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-GRAMMAR-C323-KANTE-01]; assesses=[TE-LEX-C42-ADJ-01, TE-LEX-C119-THINGS119-05, TE-LEX-C39-TII-01, TE-LEX-C58-REPLY-02, TE-LEX-C34-ISHTAM-01, TE-LEX-C138-QUAL138-01, TE-LEX-C105-LOC105-02, TE-LEX-C133-THINGS133-05] -->

> **హైదరాబాదు విజయవాడ కంటే పెద్దది.**
>
> *haidarābādu vijayavāḍa kaṇṭē peddadi.* — "Hyderabad is bigger than Vijayawada."

> **నాకు టీ కంటే కాఫీ ఎక్కువ ఇష్టం.**
>
> *nāku ṭī kaṇṭē kāphī ekkuva iṣṭaṁ.* — "I like coffee more than tea."

The thing you measure against comes with **కంటే**, "than", after it; then the quality. The quality does not
change: no "bigger", only **పెద్దది** (*peddadi*, "a big one"). **ఎక్కువ**, "more", can stand before it to make the
"more" plain.

> **రైలు బస్సు కంటే వేగంగా వెళ్తుంది.**
>
> *railu bassu kaṇṭē vēgaṅgā veḷtundi.* — "The train goes faster than the bus."

## You'll want to know: అన్నిటికంటే, అందరికంటే
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-GRAMMAR-C323-MOST-01]; assesses=[TE-LEX-C173-STHALAM173-03, TE-LEX-C42-ADJ-03, TE-LEX-GRANDMOTHER, TE-LEX-C42-ADJ-01, TE-LEX-C320-URU-01] -->

> **మా ఊరిలో ఈ హోటలు అన్నిటికంటే మంచిది.**
>
> *mā ūrilō ī hōṭalu anniṭikaṇṭē mañcidi.* — "In our town this restaurant is the best."

> **మా ఇంట్లో అమ్మమ్మ అందరికంటే పెద్దది.**
>
> *mā iṇṭlō ammamma andarikaṇṭē peddadi.* — "At home, Grandmother is the eldest."

**అన్నిటికంటే** is "than all (things)"; **అందరికంటే** is "than everyone", for people. Better than all is the
best; bigger than everyone is the eldest.
