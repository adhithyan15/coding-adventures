## You'll want to know: आमः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C515-SVADA515-03]; assesses=[] -->

**आमः** — *āmaḥ* — "raw; uncooked".

> एतत् शाकम् आमम् अस्ति।
>
> *etat śākam āmam asti.*
>
> This vegetable is raw.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**आमः** (*āmaḥ*) is the adjective MW records as raw, uncooked, the opposite of *pakva*, "cooked"; MW gives no derivation, so its origin is uncertain (MW).
