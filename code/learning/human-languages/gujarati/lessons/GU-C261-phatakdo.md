## You'll want to know: ફટાકડો
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C261-KITES261-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ફટાકડો** — *phaṭākḍo* — "firecracker".

> **દિવાળીમાં મેં ફટાકડા ખરીદ્યા.** — *divāḷīmā̃ mẽ phaṭākḍā kharīdyā* — "I bought firecrackers at Diwali."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

The origin of **ફટાકડો** (*phaṭākḍo*) is not settled. It looks built on a sound-word for a bang, but CDIAL has no entry for it and our sources do not trace it.
