# ರಾಕ್ಷಸ (rākṣasa) — a demon; an ogre

## You'll want to know: ರಾಕ್ಷಸ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C340-DANTAKATHE340-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ರಾಕ್ಷಸ** — *rākṣasa* — "a demon; an ogre".

> ಧೈರ್ಯವಂತ ಹುಡುಗ ರಾಕ್ಷಸನನ್ನು ಸೋಲಿಸಿದನು.
>
> *dhairyavanta huḍuga rākṣasanannu sōlisidanu.*
>
> The brave boy defeated the ogre.

Plural: ರಾಕ್ಷಸರು, *rākṣasaru*.

A woman demon is a ರಾಕ್ಷಸಿ.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ರಾಕ್ಷಸ** (*rākṣasa*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *rākṣasa*, "demoniacal", and as a noun "a demon" (CDIAL 10672).
