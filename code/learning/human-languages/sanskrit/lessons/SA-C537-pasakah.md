## You'll want to know: पाशकः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C537-PASAKA537-01]; assesses=[] -->

**पाशकः** — *pāśakaḥ* — "a die".

> यदि पाशकः षट् दर्शयति, तर्हि त्वं जयसि।
>
> *yadi pāśakaḥ ṣaṭ darśayati, tarhi tvaṃ jayasi.*
>
> If the die shows six, you win.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पाशकः** (*pāśakaḥ*) is built on *pāśa*, "a cord, a noose", with the ending *-ka*; MW gives *pāśaka* as a snare, and also as a die (MW).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
