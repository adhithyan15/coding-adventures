## You'll want to know: بھلائی
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C346-KHIDMAT346-04]; assesses=[] -->

**بھلائی** — *bhalāī* — "welfare; the good (of others)".

> وہ ہمیشہ دوسروں کی بھلائی سوچتا ہے۔
>
> *voh hameshā dūsroṅ kī bhalāī sochtā hai.*
>
> He always thinks of the good of others.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**بھلائی** (*bhalāī*) is built on *bhalā*, "good", which is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *bhalā* under Sanskrit *bhalla*, "auspicious" (CDIAL 9408); *-āī* makes the noun.
