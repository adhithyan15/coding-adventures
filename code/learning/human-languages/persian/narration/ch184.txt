Persian, chapter 184: To accept, To refuse, To blow, To shine, To grow.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
قبول کردن (qabul kardan) — to accept.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to meet, then the Persian for to get to know.

You'll want to know: قبول کردن.
قبول کردن — qabul kardan — "to accept".

The word, taken apart.
qabul kardan pairs qabul, borrowed from Arabic qabūl, "acceptance, reception" (root q-b-l, Lane), with the Persian verb kardan, "to do, to make". kardan, "to do", is an inherited verb, from Middle Persian kardan (EtymDB).

Lesson 3 of 5.
وزیدن (vazidan) — to blow (of wind).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to accept, then the Persian for to refuse.

You'll want to know: وزیدن.
وزیدن — vazidan — "to blow (of wind)".

The word, taken apart.
vazidan is a native Persian word, inherited rather than borrowed; it is usually traced to Middle Persian wazīdan, "to blow". EtymDB takes it back to the Indo-European root h₂weh₁-, "to blow", the root usually also given for English wind.
