# ਫਿਰ ਮਿਲਾਂਗੇ (phir milāṁge) — "we'll meet again"

## The phrase, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-PHRASE-SEE-YOU-AGAIN-04]; assesses=[PA-LEX-PHIR-04, PA-LEX-MILAANGE-04] -->

**ਫਿਰ ਮਿਲਾਂਗੇ** = **ਫਿਰ** (*phir*, "again") + **ਮਿਲਾਂਗੇ** (*milāṁge*, "we will
meet") — "**we'll meet again**." Chapter 1 gave you *sat srī akāl*, the Sikh
greeting that doubles as a formal goodbye; *phir milāṁge* is the everyday,
hopeful one — the same "promise of return" the whole subcontinent prefers to a
blunt farewell (Hindi *phir milenge*, Bengali *āshi*, Tamil *pōy varugiṟēṉ*).
Even more casual: *phir mildē hāṁ* ("(we) meet again," present for a near
future).
