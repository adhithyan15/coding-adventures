## You'll want to know: आरामदेह
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C414-GUSALKHANA414-04]; assesses=[] -->

**आरामदेह** — *ārāmdeh* — "comfortable".

> होटल का बिस्तर बहुत आरामदेह था।
>
> *hoṭal kā bistar bahut ārāmdeh thā.*
>
> The hotel bed was very comfortable.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**आरामदेह** (*ārāmdeh*) is usually traced to Persian *ārāmdih*, "giving rest", from *ārām*, "rest", the word in आराम से. It does not change for gender.
