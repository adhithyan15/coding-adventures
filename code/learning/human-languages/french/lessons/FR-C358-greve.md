## You'll want to know: la grève
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C358-GREVE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**la grève** — "the strike".

> Les trains ne roulent pas : il y a une grève.
>
> *The trains are not running: there is a strike.*

*Faire grève*, *être en grève* — to be on strike. *La grève* is also a sandy shore.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*grève* "strike" is built on *grève* "sandy shore": the usual explanation is a riverside square of that name where people looking for work gathered. The origin of *grève* "shore" is not settled.
