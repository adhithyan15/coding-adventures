## You'll want to know: પ્યાર
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C451-VALAG451-04]; assesses=[] -->

**પ્યાર** — *pyār* — "love; loving fondness".

> માતાના પ્યારથી બાળક શાંત થઈ ગયું.
>
> *mātānā pyārthī bāḷak śānt thaī gayũ.*
>
> In its mother's love the child grew calm.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**પ્યાર** (*pyār*) goes back to Sanskrit *priyakāra*, "doing a kindness"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati word, "love" (CDIAL 8975).
