## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ছাতা** (*chhātā*) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from *chattra*, "parasol" (CDIAL 4972).
