## You'll want to know: doubler
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C410-BOUCHONS-05]; assesses=[] -->

**doubler** — "to overtake; to double".

> Il est dangereux de doubler dans un tunnel.
>
> *It is dangerous to overtake in a tunnel.*

*Dépasser* also means to overtake.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*doubler* is inherited through Old French *dobler* from Late Latin *dūplāre* 'to double', from *duplus* 'double' (EtymDB).
