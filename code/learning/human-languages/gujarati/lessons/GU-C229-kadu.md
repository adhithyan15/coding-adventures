# કાંડું (kā̃ḍũ) — wrist

## You'll want to know: કાંડું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C229-LIMBS229-01]; assesses=[] -->

**કાંડું** — *kā̃ḍũ* — "wrist".

> **શું તમારું કાંડું દુખે છે?** — *śũ tamārũ kā̃ḍũ dukhe che?* — "Does your wrist hurt?"

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**કાંડું** (*kā̃ḍũ*) is a tadbhava. CDIAL lists it, glossed "wrist", under Sanskrit *kāṇḍa*, "single joint of a plant", a word it thinks probably came from Dravidian, so the wrist is a joint (CDIAL 3023).
