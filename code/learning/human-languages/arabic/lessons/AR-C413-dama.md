## You'll want to know: دام
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C413-TATAWWUR413-05]; assesses=[] -->

**دام** — *dāma* — "to endure; to last".

> دامت الحفلة حتى منتصف الليل.
>
> *dāmat al-ḥafla ḥattā muntaṣaf al-layl.*
>
> The party lasted until midnight.

Present: يدوم, *yadūm*.

ما دام, *mā dāma*, as long as.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**دام** (*dāma*) is built on the native Arabic root *d-w-m*, "to last, to continue" (Lane). *Dāma* is a Form I verb: to endure, to last. Its present is *yadūm*.
