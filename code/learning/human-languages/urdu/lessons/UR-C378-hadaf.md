## You'll want to know: ہدف
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C378-HADAF378-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ہدف** — *hadaf* — "a target; a goal".

> اگر ہدف واضح ہے تو کام آسان ہو گا۔
>
> *agar hadaf vāzeh hai to kām āsān ho gā.*
>
> If the target is clear, the work will be easier.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ہدف** (*hadaf*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *hadaf*, "a target, a mark aimed at".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
