Malayalam, chapter 518: Dread and Longing.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ഭയം (bhayaṁ) — dread; fear.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for wrath, then the Malayalam for to be upset.

You'll want to know: ഭയം.
ഭയം — bhayaṁ — "dread; fear".
കുട്ടിക്ക് ഇരുട്ടിനെ ഭയമായിരുന്നു. kuṭṭikkŭ iruṭṭine bhayamāyirunnu. The child was afraid of the dark.
Written and formal; in speech പേടി is more usual. The one afraid takes -ക്ക്, the thing feared -എ.

The word, taken apart.
ഭയം (bhayaṁ) is borrowed from Sanskrit bhaya, "fear, peril", from the root bhī, "to fear" (CDIAL 9390).

Lesson 2 of 5.
ലജ്ജിക്കുക (lajjikkuka) — to feel ashamed.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for to be upset, then the Malayalam for dread.

You'll want to know: ലജ്ജിക്കുക.
ലജ്ജിക്കുക — lajjikkuka — "to feel ashamed".
തെറ്റ് ചെയ്തതിൽ അവൻ ലജ്ജിച്ചു. teṟṟŭ ceytatil avan lajjiccu. He felt ashamed of doing wrong.
Past: ലജ്ജിച്ചു, lajjiccu.
The noun is ലജ്ജ, shame; what one is ashamed of takes -ഇൽ.

The word, taken apart.
ലജ്ജിക്കുക (lajjikkuka) is built from Sanskrit lajjā, "shame, modesty" (CDIAL 10910), with the Malayalam verb ending -ിക്കുക.

Lesson 3 of 5.
ഏകാന്തത (ēkāntata) — loneliness; solitude.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for dread, then the Malayalam for to feel ashamed.

You'll want to know: ഏകാന്തത.
ഏകാന്തത — ēkāntata — "loneliness; solitude".
നഗരത്തിൽ മുത്തശ്ശിക്ക് ഏകാന്തത തോന്നി. nagarattil muttaśśikkŭ ēkāntata tōnni. In the city, grandmother felt lonely.
Built on ഏകാന്തം, a lonely place, with -ത, which makes a noun of a quality.

The word, taken apart.
ഏകാന്തത (ēkāntata) is borrowed from Sanskrit ekānta, "a lonely or retired place" (Monier-Williams), with the noun ending -tā. The Sanskrit word joins eka, "one", and anta, "end, side".

Lesson 4 of 5.
കൊതിക്കുക (kotikkuka) — to long for; to crave.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for to feel ashamed, then the Malayalam for loneliness.

You'll want to know: കൊതിക്കുക.
കൊതിക്കുക — kotikkuka — "to long for; to crave".
നാട്ടിലെ ഭക്ഷണം കഴിക്കാൻ ഞാൻ കൊതിച്ചു. nāṭṭile bhakṣaṇaṁ kaḻikkān ñān koticcu. I longed to eat the food of home.
Past: കൊതിച്ചു, koticcu.
What you long for takes -ആൻ (a verb) or -ഇന് (a noun). The noun is കൊതി, a craving.

The word, taken apart.
കൊതിക്കുക (kotikkuka) is native Dravidian: DEDR 2084 lists Malayalam കൊതി, "eagerness, greed", and the verb, "to covet, to crave" (DEDR 2084).
