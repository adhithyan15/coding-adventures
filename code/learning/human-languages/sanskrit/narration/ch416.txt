Sanskrit, chapter 416: Boatmen.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
नाविकः (nāvikaḥ) — a boatman.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for the far bank, then the Sanskrit for a causeway.

You'll want to know: नाविकः.
नाविकः — nāvikaḥ — "a boatman".
नाविकः नौकां नदीतीरम् आनयति। nāvikaḥ naukāṃ nadītīram ānayati. The boatman brings the boat to the riverbank.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
नाविकः (nāvikaḥ) is built on nau, "a boat", with the ending -ika; MW gives nāvika as a helmsman, a pilot, a sailor (MW).

Lesson 2 of 5.

अरित्रम् — aritram — "an oar".
नाविकस्य हस्ते अरित्रम् अस्ति। nāvikasya haste aritram asti. The boatman has an oar in his hand.
A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
अरित्रम् (aritram) is built on the root √ṛ, "to move", with the ending -itra: MW gives aritra as propelling and as an oar (MW).

Lesson 4 of 5.
वाहयति (vāhayati) — rows (a boat); drives.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for an oar, then the Sanskrit for a helmsman.

You'll want to know: वाहयति.
वाहयति — vāhayati — "rows (a boat); drives".
नाविकः अरित्रेण नौकां वाहयति। nāvikaḥ aritreṇa naukāṃ vāhayati. The boatman rows the boat with an oar.
Past (he …): सः नौकां वाहितवान्, saḥ naukāṃ vāhitavān.

The word, taken apart.
वाहयति (vāhayati) is built on the root √vah, "to carry", which gives vahati, "carries"; MW gives its causative as to make carry, to drive a chariot and to propel a boat, and KRM gives the participle vāhita (MW).

Lesson 5 of 5.
उत्तरति (uttarati) — comes ashore; lands.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a helmsman, then the Sanskrit for rows.

You'll want to know: उत्तरति.
उत्तरति — uttarati — "comes ashore; lands".
यात्रिकाः नौकायाः तीरे उत्तरन्ति। yātrikāḥ naukāyāḥ tīre uttaranti. The travellers come ashore from the boat.
Past (he …): सः नौकायाः उत्तीर्णवान्, saḥ naukāyāḥ uttīrṇavān.

The word, taken apart.
उत्तरति (uttarati) is built from ud, "up, out", and the root √tṝ, "to cross"; MW gives ut-tṝ as to pass out of the water, to disembark, with the participle uttīrṇa, "landed" (MW).
