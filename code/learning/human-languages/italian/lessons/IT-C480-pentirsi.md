## You'll want to know: pentirsi
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C480-RANCORE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**pentirsi** — "to regret, to repent".

> Mi sono pentito di quello che ho detto.
>
> *I regretted what I said.*

*Pentirsi di* + noun or infinitive.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*pentirsi* is inherited from Latin *paenitēre* "to cause regret" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short).
