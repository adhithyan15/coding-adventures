## You'll want to know: সাঁতলানো
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C514-UNUNE514-05]; assesses=[] -->

**সাঁতলানো** — *sā̃tlāno* — "to sauté (in a little oil)".

> আমি পেঁয়াজ আর রসুন সাঁতলালাম।
>
> *Āmi pẽyāj ār rôsun sā̃tlālām.*
>
> I sautéed the onion and garlic.

Past (he/she …): তিনি সবজি সাঁতলালেন, *tini sôbji sā̃tlālen*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**সাঁতলানো** (*sā̃tlāno*) comes from a reconstructed *saṁtalati*, "fries"; CDIAL lists Bengali *sā̃talā*, "to singe in oil or ghee" (CDIAL 12883).
