## You'll want to know: pèng
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C171-SWITCH171-05]; assesses=[] -->

*pèng* — "to touch; to bump into".

> *bié pèng zhège hěn rè* — Don't touch that: it's hot.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*pèng* No dictionary we use records an older form, so its history is not settled.

## What you've built
