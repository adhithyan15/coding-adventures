# (un aviso del curso) — read it as the person it is for

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-C436-GENTE-02, ES-LEX-C436-GENTE-05] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say *el grupo* and *recoger*. Both are in the notice you are about
to read.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-C436-GENTE-02, ES-LEX-C436-GENTE-03, ES-LEX-C436-GENTE-05] -->

Read it once straight through, then answer.

> **CURSO DE COCINA — AVISO**
> El **grupo** de los martes está completo. Podemos **ofrecer** plaza en el de
> los jueves. Los materiales se **recogen** en recepción el primer día.

[PAUSE 3s]
- [YOU SAY: which group is full]
- [YOU SAY: what they can offer instead]
- [YOU SAY: where and when you collect the materials]

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-C436-GENTE-01, ES-LEX-C436-GENTE-04, ES-LEX-C436-GENTE-02] -->

Now produce it. Say aloud:

[PAUSE 3s]
- [YOU SAY: I have changed to the Thursday group]
- [YOU SAY: a colleague from work lent me the book]
- [YOU SAY: I will collect the materials on the first day]

**Notice what kind of text this is.** It is not a conversation, not a story and
not a dialogue between two friends in a café. It is an administrative notice
addressed to somebody who has to act on it — and that is what the A2 reading
paper is mostly made of.

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-C436-GENTE-02, ES-LEX-C436-GENTE-03, ES-LEX-C436-GENTE-05] -->

[PAUSE 3s] Which group is full? (**el de los martes**.) What do they offer?
(**plaza el jueves**.) What do you do with the materials? (**recogerlos en
recepción**.)
