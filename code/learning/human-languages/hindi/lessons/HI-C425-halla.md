## You'll want to know: हल्ला
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C425-HALLA425-01]; assesses=[] -->

**हल्ला** — *hallā* — "an uproar, a commotion".

> गली में अचानक हल्ला हुआ।
>
> *galī meṁ acānak hallā huā.*
>
> Suddenly there was an uproar in the lane.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**हल्ला** (*hallā*) goes back to *\*halla*, "movement", a form CDIAL reconstructs (CDIAL 14017), which lists Hindi *hallā*, "tumult, noise".
