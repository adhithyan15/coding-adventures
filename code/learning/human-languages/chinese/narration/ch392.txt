Mandarin Chinese, chapter 392: A Change of Plan.
6 lessons. You can do the first one in the car; after that you will want to have stopped.


Lesson 1 of 6.
改 (gǎi) — to alter; to amend.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for to make it onto a train, then the Mandarin for when the time comes.

You'll want to know: 改.
改 — gǎi — "to alter; to amend".
时间改了我们下午三点见面 shíjiān gǎi le wǒmen xiàwǔ sān diǎn jiànmiàn — The time's changed: we're meeting at three in the afternoon.
The new character, in pieces: 改 (gǎi) — seven strokes in two pieces: on the left a bar that turns down, a short bar, and a vertical that bends right and flicks up; on the right the right piece of 做. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

The word, taken apart.
改 (gǎi) is inherited from Middle Chinese kojX (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'to change'.

Script — a bent piece, then the right piece of 做.
改 (gǎi).
Two pieces, left before right.
The left piece, three strokes. A bar that turns down. A short bar under it. Then a vertical that comes down, bends right along the foot and flicks up.
The right piece, four strokes, the right piece of 做. A short stroke falling to the left. A bar from it to the right. A long stroke falling to the left. Then a long stroke falling to the right across it.
3 + 4 = seven.

[once you have stopped driving — this part needs your eyes or your hands; if you are driving, skip ahead to the next part]
Guided Practice.
[once you have stopped driving — write: 改 (gǎi), all seven, with the model in front of you]
[once you have stopped driving — cover: write it again from memory, and count the pieces, not the strokes]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
How many strokes in 改 (gǎi)? (Seven.) Where have you made the right piece before? (On the right of 做.) How does the left piece end? (A bend to the right and a flick up.)

Lesson 3 of 6.
改期 (gǎiqī) — to change the date (of an event).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for when the time comes, then the Mandarin for to alter.

You'll want to know: 改期.
改期 — gǎiqī — "to change the date (of an event)".
要是下雨晚会就改期 yàoshi xiàyǔ wǎnhuì jiù gǎiqī — If it rains, the party will be moved to another date.

The word, taken apart.
改期 (gǎiqī) is built from 改 (gǎi) 'to alter' and 期 'a set time', both inherited from Middle Chinese, kojX and gi (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'an appointed meeting'.

Lesson 6 of 6.
改口 (gǎikǒu) — to change one's tune; to take back what one said.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for to make different, then the Mandarin for to change the name (of).

You'll want to know: 改口.
改口 — gǎikǒu — "to change one's tune; to take back what one said".
tā zuótiān shuō bú qù jīntiān yòu gǎikǒu le — Yesterday he said he wasn't going; today he's changed his tune.
Two third tones meet in this word: gǎi is said with a rising tone before kǒu, and the spelling keeps both marks.

The word, taken apart.
改口 (gǎikǒu) is built from 改 (gǎi) 'to alter' and 口 'the mouth', both inherited from Middle Chinese, kojX and khuwX (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'what a person speaks and eats with': altering what comes out of the mouth.
