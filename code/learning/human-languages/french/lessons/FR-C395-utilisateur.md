## You'll want to know: l'utilisateur
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C395-COMPTE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**l'utilisateur** — "the user".

> Chaque utilisateur a son propre profil.
>
> *Each user has their own profile.*

Feminine *utilisatrice*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*utilisateur* is built on *utiliser* "to use", from *utile* "useful", which comes from Latin *ūtilis* "useful", from *ūtī* "to use" (Lewis & Short).
