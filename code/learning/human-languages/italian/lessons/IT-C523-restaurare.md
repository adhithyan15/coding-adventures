## You'll want to know: restaurare
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C523-MOSTRA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**restaurare** — "to restore".

> Stanno restaurando la facciata del museo.
>
> *They are restoring the front of the museum.*

The noun is *il restauro*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*restaurare* is a learned borrowing from Latin *restaurāre* "to renew, to rebuild" (EtymDB).
