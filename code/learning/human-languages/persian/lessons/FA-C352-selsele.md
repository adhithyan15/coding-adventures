## You'll want to know: سلسله
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C352-BASTAN-05]; assesses=[] -->

**سلسله** (*selsele*) — "a dynasty; a chain".

> این کاخ را پادشاهان یک سلسله قدیمی ساختند.
>
> *In kâkh râ pâdeshâhân-e yek selsele-ye qadimi sâkhtand.*
>
> The kings of an old dynasty built this palace.

Its first meaning is "a chain": *selsele-ye kuh-hâ* "a mountain range".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*selsele* is borrowed from Arabic *silsila*, "chain" (EtymDB): a chain of kings.
