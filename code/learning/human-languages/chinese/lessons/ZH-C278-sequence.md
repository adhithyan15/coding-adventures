## Grammar Lens: 先, 然后, 最后 — first, then, last
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-GRAMMAR-SEQUENCE-01]; assesses=[ZH-LEX-C269-YOUNG269-02, ZH-LEX-C150-RANHOU-01, ZH-LEX-C278-ZUIHOU-01, ZH-LEX-C43-GO43-05, ZH-LEX-C255-PARTTIME255-01] -->

> **To tell things in order, put 先 before the first verb, 然后 at the front of the next step, and 最后 at the front of the last.**

> **我先吃饭 然后看书 最后回家** *wǒ xiān chī fàn, ránhòu kàn shū, zuìhòu huí jiā* — First I eat, then I read, and last I go home.

然后 is the *ránhòu* you have said since the chapter on the past. Now it prints: the next lesson puts 然 in your hand.

先 sits after the person, right before the verb: 我先吃饭. 然后 and 最后 open their clause, and the person need not be said again.

> **他先去学校 然后去打工** *tā xiān qù xuéxiào, ránhòu qù dǎgōng* — He goes to school first, then to his part-time job.

The order of the clauses is the order of the events.
