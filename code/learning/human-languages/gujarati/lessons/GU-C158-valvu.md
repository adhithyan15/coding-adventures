# વાળવું (vāḷvũ) — to sweep, to fold

## You'll want to know: વાળવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C158-SWEEP158-02]; assesses=[] -->

**વાળવું** — *vāḷvũ* — "to sweep, to fold".

> **તે સાવરણીથી કચરો વાળે છે.** — *te sāvarṇīthī kacro vāḷe che* — "She sweeps the rubbish with a broom."

It also means to fold or to turn.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**વાળવું** (*vāḷvũ*) is inherited, but the sweeping sense is not settled. CDIAL lists Gujarati *vāḷvũ*, "to fold, twist, turn", under Sanskrit *valati*, "turns", and does not list the sense of sweeping (CDIAL 11405).
