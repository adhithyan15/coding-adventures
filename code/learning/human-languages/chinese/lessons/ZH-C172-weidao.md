## You'll want to know: wèidao
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C172-TASTE172-01]; assesses=[] -->

*wèidao* — "taste; flavour".

> *zhège cài de wèidao hěn hǎo* — This dish tastes good.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*wèidao* is built from *wèi* 'taste' and *dao*, both inherited from Middle Chinese, *mj+jH* and *dawX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription). The dictionary glosses the first as the five tastes, sour, salty, sweet, bitter and hot, and the second 'a road'. In the word the second loses its tone.
