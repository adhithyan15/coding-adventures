## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[TE-GRAMMAR-C324-TE-CONDITIONAL-01, TE-GRAMMAR-C324-OKAVELA-TE-01, TE-GRAMMAR-C324-REAL-CONDITION-01, TE-GRAMMAR-C324-NEGATIVE-CONDITION-01, TE-GRAMMAR-C323-KANTE-01, TE-GRAMMAR-C323-MOST-01, TE-GRAMMAR-C323-SPAN-GA-01, TE-GRAMMAR-C323-FROM-UNTIL-01, TE-LEX-C323-KALAM-01, TE-SCRIPT-RECOG-178, TE-SCRIPT-RECOG-177, TE-SCRIPT-RECOG-176, TE-SCRIPT-RECOG-175, TE-SCRIPT-RECOG-174] -->

[PAUSE 2s each]
- [YOU SAY: If you come to my home town, I will be very happy.]
- [YOU SAY: If it rains tomorrow, we will not go. Otherwise we will go at six.]
- [YOU SAY: If by chance the bus does not come, let's take an auto.]
- [YOU SAY: It has been raining for two days. If it rains tomorrow, I will stay at home.]
- [YOU SAY: If you read this book, you will like it more than that one.]

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[TE-GRAMMAR-C324-TE-CONDITIONAL-01, TE-GRAMMAR-C324-OKAVELA-TE-01, TE-GRAMMAR-C324-REAL-CONDITION-01, TE-GRAMMAR-C324-NEGATIVE-CONDITION-01] -->

[PAUSE 3s] "If"? (**-తే**: **వస్తే**, **ఉంటే**, **పడితే**.) "If by chance"? (**ఒకవేళ … -తే**.) "If not"?
(**-అకపోతే**: **రాకపోతే**.) "Otherwise"? (**లేకపోతే**.)
