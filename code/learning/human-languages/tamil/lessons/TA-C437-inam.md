## You'll want to know: இனம்
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C437-VILANKU437-05]; assesses=[] -->

**இனம்** — *iṉam* — "a species; a kind".

> இந்தப் பறவை இனம் இப்போது மிக அபூர்வம்.
>
> *intap paṟavai iṉam ippōtu mika apūrvam.*
>
> This species of bird is now very rare.

Also a group of people or things of one kind.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**இனம்** (*iṉam*) is native Dravidian (DEDR 531), "a class, a group, a kind, a species".
