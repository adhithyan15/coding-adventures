## You'll want to know: رجسٹر
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C405-RAJISTAR405-01]; assesses=[] -->

**رجسٹر** — *rajisṭar* — "a register (book)".

> ہوٹل والے نے رجسٹر میں میرا نام لکھا۔
>
> *hoṭal vāle ne rajisṭar meṅ merā nām likhā.*
>
> The man at the hotel wrote my name in the register.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**رجسٹر** (*rajisṭar*) is borrowed from English *register*.
