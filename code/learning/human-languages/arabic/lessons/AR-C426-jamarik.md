## You'll want to know: جمارك
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C426-JAMARIK426-01]; assesses=[] -->

**جمارك** — *jamārik* — "customs (at a border)".

> وقفنا في الجمارك مدة طويلة.
>
> *waqafnā fī al-jamārik mudda ṭawīla.*
>
> We stood in customs for a long time.

The singular جمرك (*jumruk*) names one customs post; the plural names the service.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**جمارك** (*jamārik*) is usually traced to Turkish *gümrük*, which goes back to Greek *kommerkion*, a duty on trade.
