## You'll want to know: مراهق
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C531-KIBAR531-01]; assesses=[] -->

**مراهق** — *murāhiq* — "a teenager".

> ابن جارنا مراهق ويحب الموسيقى العالية.
>
> *ibn jārinā murāhiq wa-yuḥibbu al-mūsīqā al-ʿāliya.*
>
> Our neighbour's son is a teenager and loves loud music.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**مراهق** (*murāhiq*) is built on the native Arabic root *r-h-q*, "to come near" (Lane). Murāhiq is the Form III participle: one coming near to manhood.
