## You'll want to know: ખારું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C171-TASTE171-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ખારું** — *khārũ* — "salty".

> **આજે દાળ ખારી છે.** — *āje dāḷ khārī che* — "Today the dal is salty."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ખારું** (*khārũ*) is a tadbhava. It comes from Sanskrit *kṣāra*, "corrosive; alkali", and CDIAL glosses the Gujarati word "saline" (CDIAL 3674).
