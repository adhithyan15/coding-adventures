## You'll want to know: دستور پخت
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C355-DASTURPOKHT-01]; assesses=[] -->

**دستور پخت** (*dastur-e pokht*) — "a recipe".

> این دستور پخت را از مادربزرگم یاد گرفتم.
>
> *In dastur-e pokht râ az mâdarbozorgam yâd gereftam.*
>
> I learned this recipe from my grandmother.

*dastur* "instruction" + *pokht* "cooking", from *pokhtan* "to cook".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*dastur* "command, instruction" is a Persian word that EtymDB lists without a parent, and *pokht* "cooking" is the past stem of the native verb *pokhtan* "to cook".
