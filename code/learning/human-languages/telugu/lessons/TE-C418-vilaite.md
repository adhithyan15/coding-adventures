# వీలైతే (vīlaitē) — if possible

## You'll want to know: వీలైతే
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C418-SADHYAM418-05]; assesses=[] -->

**వీలైతే** — *vīlaitē* — "if possible".

> వీలైతే నేను రేపు మీ ఇంటికి వస్తాను.
>
> *vīlaitē nēnu rēpu mī iṇṭiki vastānu.*
>
> If possible, I will come to your house tomorrow.

వీలు, a chance, + అయితే, if it is. వీలు కాదు, it is not convenient.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**వీలైతే** (*vīlaitē*) is built from *vīlu*, "convenience, a chance", whose origin is uncertain, and *aitē*, "if it is", a form of *avu*, "to be" (DEDR 333).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
