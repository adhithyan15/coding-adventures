## You'll want to know: अनुग्रहः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C392-SAHAYAKA392-05]; assesses=[] -->

**अनुग्रहः** — *anugrahaḥ* — "a kindness; a favour".

> यदि अध्यापकस्य अनुग्रहः अस्ति, तर्हि ज्ञानं सुलभं भवति।
>
> *yadi adhyāpakasya anugrahaḥ asti, tarhi jñānaṃ sulabhaṃ bhavati.*
>
> If the teacher is kind, knowledge comes easily.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अनुग्रहः** (*anugrahaḥ*) is built on *anu*, "after", and the root √grah, "to seize, to take"; MW gives *anu-graha* "favour, kindness, conferring benefits" (MW).
