## You'll want to know: dǎnzi
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C332-GHOST332-01]; assesses=[] -->

*dǎnzi* — "courage; nerve".

> *xiǎoshíhou wǒ de dǎnzi hěn xiǎo* — When I was little I was very timid.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*dǎnzi* is built from *dǎn* 'the gall' and 子, here a light noun ending, both inherited from Middle Chinese, *tamX* and *tsiX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which pairs the first with the liver.

## What you've built
