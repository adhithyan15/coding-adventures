## You'll want to know: l'assenza
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C379-LICEO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**l'assenza** — "the absence".

> Se fai troppe assenze, non ti ammettono all'esame.
>
> *If you miss too many days, they will not let you sit the exam.*

*Fare un'assenza* — to miss a day of school.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*assenza* is a learned borrowing from Latin *absentia* "absence" (EtymDB), from *absēns* "absent", built on *abesse* "to be away", from *ab* and *esse* "to be".
