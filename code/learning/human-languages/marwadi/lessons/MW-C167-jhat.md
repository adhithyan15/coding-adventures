## You'll want to know: झट
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C167-KAM167-05]; assesses=[] -->

**झट** — *jhaṭ* — "at once".

> मां झट रोटी बणावै है।
>
> *mā̃ jhaṭ roṭī baṇāvai hai.*
>
> Mother makes the rotis in no time.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**झट** (*jhaṭ*) comes from a reconstructed *\*jhaṭ*, "a sudden movement"; CDIAL lists Hindi and Gujarati *jhaṭ*, "quickly" (CDIAL 5327). CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry.
