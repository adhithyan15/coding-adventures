## You'll want to know: صابن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C404-GHUSL404-02]; assesses=[] -->

**صابن** — *sābun* — "soap".

> غسل خانے میں صابن نہیں ہے۔
>
> *ghusl khāne meṅ sābun nahīṅ hai.*
>
> There's no soap in the washroom.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**صابن** (*sābun*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *ṣābūn*, "soap".
