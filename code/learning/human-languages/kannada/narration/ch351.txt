Kannada, chapter 351: The Rescue Operation.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಕಾರ್ಯಾಚರಣೆ (kāryācaraṇe) — a mission; an operation.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for fame, then the Kannada for to inaugurate.

You'll want to know: ಕಾರ್ಯಾಚರಣೆ.
ಕಾರ್ಯಾಚರಣೆ — kāryācaraṇe — "a mission; an operation".
ಪ್ರವಾಹದಲ್ಲಿ ರಕ್ಷಣಾ ಕಾರ್ಯಾಚರಣೆ ಎರಡು ದಿನ ನಡೆಯಿತು. pravāhadalli rakṣaṇā kāryācaraṇe eraḍu dina naḍeyitu. The rescue operation in the flood went on for two days.
Used of rescue, police and army work: ರಕ್ಷಣಾ ಕಾರ್ಯಾಚರಣೆ (kāryācaraṇe), a rescue operation. In a flood it brings food and ಔಷಧ, medicine, to people cut off by the water.

The word, taken apart.
ಕಾರ್ಯಾಚರಣೆ (kāryācaraṇe) is borrowed from Sanskrit, a modern compound of kārya, "a thing to be done, business" (CDIAL 3078), and ācaraṇa, "undertaking, performing" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 2 of 5.
ಸ್ವಯಂಸೇವಕ (svayaṃsēvaka) — a volunteer.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to inaugurate, then the Kannada for a mission.

You'll want to know: ಸ್ವಯಂಸೇವಕ.
ಸ್ವಯಂಸೇವಕ — svayaṃsēvaka — "a volunteer".
ನೂರಾರು ಸ್ವಯಂಸೇವಕರು ಸಂತ್ರಸ್ತರಿಗೆ ಊಟ ತಂದರು. nūrāru svayaṃsēvakaru santrastarige ūṭa tandaru. Hundreds of volunteers brought food to the victims.
Plural: ಸ್ವಯಂಸೇವಕರು, svayaṃsēvakaru.
ಸ್ವಯಂ, oneself, and ಸೇವಕ, one who serves: one who serves of his own will.

The word, taken apart.
ಸ್ವಯಂಸೇವಕ (svayaṃsēvaka) is borrowed from Sanskrit, a modern compound of svayam, "self, spontaneously" (CDIAL 13906), and sevaka, "one who serves" (Monier-Williams).

You'll want to know: ಅಪ್ಪಳಿಸು.

ದೊಡ್ಡ ಅಲೆಗಳು ದಡಕ್ಕೆ ಅಪ್ಪಳಿಸಿದವು. doḍḍa alegaḷu daḍakke appaḷisidavu. Huge waves crashed against the shore.
Past (it …): ಅಪ್ಪಳಿಸಿತು, appaḷisitu.
What is hit takes -ಗೆ. Storms, waves and news ಅಪ್ಪಳಿಸು (appaḷisu).

The word, taken apart.
ಅಪ್ಪಳಿಸು (appaḷisu) is native Dravidian: DEDR 157 lists Kannada appaḷisu, "to strike against, to slap".

Lesson 4 of 5.
ಆವರಿಸು (āvarisu) — to engulf; to cover.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a volunteer, then the Kannada for to crash against.

You'll want to know: ಆವರಿಸು.
ಆವರಿಸು — āvarisu — "to engulf; to cover".
ಬೆಂಕಿಯ ಹೊಗೆ ಇಡೀ ಬೀದಿಯನ್ನು ಆವರಿಸಿತು. beṅkiya hoge iḍī bīdiyannu āvarisitu. Smoke from the fire covered the whole street.
Past (it …): ಆವರಿಸಿತು, āvarisitu.
Smoke, darkness, fear and silence ಆವರಿಸು (āvarisu): ಕತ್ತಲೆ ಆವರಿಸಿತು, darkness fell.

The word, taken apart.
ಆವರಿಸು (āvarisu) is built on Sanskrit āvaraṇa, "covering, obstruction" (CDIAL 1413), with the Kannada verb ending -isu.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ಕೊಚ್ಚಿಹೋಗು (koccihōgu) — to be washed away.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to crash against, then the Kannada for to engulf.

You'll want to know: ಕೊಚ್ಚಿಹೋಗು.
ಕೊಚ್ಚಿಹೋಗು — koccihōgu — "to be washed away".
ಪ್ರವಾಹದಲ್ಲಿ ಹಳೆಯ ಸೇತುವೆ ಕೊಚ್ಚಿಹೋಯಿತು. pravāhadalli haḷeya sētuve koccihōyitu. The old bridge was washed away in the flood.
Past (it …): ಕೊಚ್ಚಿಹೋಯಿತು, koccihōyitu.
Things carried off by water: ಮನೆಗಳು ಕೊಚ್ಚಿಹೋದವು, houses were swept away.

The word, taken apart.
ಕೊಚ್ಚಿಹೋಗು (koccihōgu) is a Kannada compound of ಕೊಚ್ಚಿ and hōgu, "to go" (DEDR 4572); DEDR 1859 lists a Kannada koccu, "to cut away", but whether ಕೊಚ್ಚಿ is that verb is not settled.
