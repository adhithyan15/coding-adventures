## You'll want to know: दूर
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C282-DUR-01]; assesses=[HI-LEX-C69-GOING-03, HI-LEX-C40-DEIXIS-03, HI-LEX-C90-ROUT-01, HI-LEX-C92-DEG-04, HI-LEX-C71-TODAY-03, HI-LEX-C142-THINGS142-05] -->

**दूर** — *dūr* — "far, far away".

> **स्टेशन यहाँ से कितनी दूर है?**
>
> *sṭeśan yahā̃ se kitnī dūr hai?* — "How far is the station from here?"

Hindi asks **कितनी दूर**, with the feminine **कितनी**, whatever the place. **दूर** itself never changes: **गाँव दूर है**, **नदी दूर है**. The place you measure from takes **से**, as in **यहाँ से**.

- **बहुत दूर नहीं है।** (*bahut dūr nahīṁ hai.*) — It's not very far.
- **मेरा घर स्टेशन से दूर है।** (*merā ghar sṭeśan se dūr hai.*) — My house is far from the station.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**दूर** (*dūr*) comes from Sanskrit *dūra*, "distant" (CDIAL 6495), and has kept its shape almost unchanged.
