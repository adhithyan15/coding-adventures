## You'll want to know: पेट्रोल
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C401-INDHAN401-02]; assesses=[] -->

**पेट्रोल** — *peṭrol* — "petrol".

> पुढच्या गावात पेट्रोल मिळेल.
>
> *puḍhacyā gāvāt peṭrol miḷel.*
>
> We will get petrol in the next village.

Neuter.

पेट्रोल भरणे, to fill up with petrol; मिळणे takes the dative: मला पेट्रोल मिळालं, I got petrol.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पेट्रोल** (*peṭrol*) is a loan from English *petrol*.
