## You'll want to know: vēlificor, vēlificārī
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C438-CONSCENDO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**vēlificor, vēlificārī** — "to set sail, to sail".

> Ventus secundus est, et nautae laetī vēlificantur.
>
> *The wind is favourable, and the sailors set sail happily.*

Deponent: *vēlificor, vēlificārī, vēlificātus sum*. Also an active form *vēlificō*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*vēlificor* is built on *vēlum* 'sail' and *faciō* 'to make' (Lewis & Short): to make sail. EtymDB lists it without a parent.
