## You'll want to know: erholen
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C488-GENESEN-04]; assesses=[] -->

**erholen** — "to get some rest; to recover (sich erholen)".

> Im Urlaub habe ich mich gut erholt.
>
> *I had a good rest on holiday.*

Reflexive: *sich erholen von* — to recover from.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*erholen* is built on *er-* and *holen* "to fetch", which is inherited from Middle High German *holen* (EtymDB): to fetch your strength back.
