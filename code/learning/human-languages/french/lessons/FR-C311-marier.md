## You'll want to know: marier
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C311-OUI-03]; assesses=[] -->

**marier** — "to get married (as se marier); to marry (a couple), to marry off".

> Ils se sont mariés à la mairie.
>
> *They got married at the town hall.*

Reflexive for the couple: *ils se marient en juin*. Without *se*, the mayor marries them: *le maire les a mariés*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*marier* is inherited from Latin *marītāre* "to wed" (EtymDB), built on *marītus* "husband".
