## You'll want to know: たちあがる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C378-MANIN378-05]; assesses=[] -->

**たちあがる** — *tachiagaru* — "to stand up".

> **おじいさんが きたので、たちあがりました。** — *ojiisan ga kita node, tachiagarimashita* — "An old man came, so I stood up."

Polite **たちあがります**, *tachiagarimasu*; a godan verb. Past (polite): **こどもが たちあがりました。**, *kodomo ga tachiagarimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**たちあがる** (*tachiagaru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, built from *tachi*, "standing" (from *tatsu*) and *agaru*, "to rise"; its kanji were attached later as kun'yomi (JMdict).
