## You'll want to know: नहलाना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C484-SANVARNA484-03]; assesses=[] -->

**नहलाना** — *nahlānā* — "to bathe someone".

> माँ रोज़ सुबह बच्चे को नहलाती है।
>
> *māṁ roz subah bacce ko nahlātī hai.*
>
> Mother bathes the baby every morning.

Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने बच्चे को नहलाया, *usne bacce ko nahlāyā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**नहलाना** (*nahlānā*) is the causative partner of *nahānā*, "to bathe", which CDIAL lists under Sanskrit *snāti*, "bathes" (CDIAL 13786): to give someone else a bath.
