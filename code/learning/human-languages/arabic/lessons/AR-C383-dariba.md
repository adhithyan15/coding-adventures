## You'll want to know: ضريبة
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C383-IQTISAD383-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ضريبة** — *ḍarība* — "a tax".

> دفع أبي الضريبة أمس.
>
> *dafaʿa abī aḍ-ḍarība ams.*
>
> My father paid the tax yesterday.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ضريبة** (*ḍarība*) is built on the native Arabic root *ḍ-r-b*, "to strike, to beat" (Lane). A *ḍarība* is what is imposed, struck on someone: a tax.
