## You'll want to know: nier
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C338-DOUTE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**nier** — "to deny".

> Il nie avoir pris l'argent.
>
> *He denies taking the money.*

*Nier* + infinitive, or *nier que*. *Je nie, nous nions*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*nier* is inherited from Latin *negāre* "to say no, to deny" (Lewis & Short), through Old French *noier* (EtymDB).
