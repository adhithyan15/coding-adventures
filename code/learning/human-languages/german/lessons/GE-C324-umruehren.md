## You'll want to know: umrühren
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C324-RUEHREN-02]; assesses=[] -->

**umrühren** — "to give a stir, to stir round".

> Sie rührte die Soße alle fünf Minuten um.
>
> *She stirred the sauce every five minutes.*

Separable: *er rührt um*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*umrühren* joins *um* "around" and *rühren* "to stir", from Proto-Germanic *hrōzijaną* (EtymDB).
