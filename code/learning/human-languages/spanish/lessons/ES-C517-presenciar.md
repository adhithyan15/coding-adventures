## You'll want to know: presenciar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C517-TESTIGO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**presenciar** — "to witness, to be present at".

> Presencié el accidente desde la ventana.
>
> *I witnessed the accident from the window.*

It takes a direct object: *presenciar un partido* — to watch a match in person.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*presenciar* is built on *presencia* 'presence', which comes from Latin *praesentia* 'a being at hand' (Lewis & Short), from *praesēns* 'present'.
