## You'll want to know: રાસ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C554-RAAS554-01]; assesses=[] -->

**રાસ** — *rās* — "raas (a stick dance)".

> જો આજે રાત્રે વરસાદ નહીં પડે તો આપણે રાસ રમીશું.
>
> *jo āje rātre varsād nahī̃ paḍe to āpaṇe rās ramīśũ.*
>
> If it doesn't rain tonight, we'll dance raas.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**રાસ** (*rās*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *rāsa*, a country dance of cowherds (Monier-Williams). Gujarati "plays" it: **રાસ રમવો**.
