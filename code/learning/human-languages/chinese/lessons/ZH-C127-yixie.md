## You'll want to know: 一些
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C127-THESE127-04]; assesses=[] -->

**一些** — *yìxiē* — "some; a few".

> **我想喝一些水** *wǒ xiǎng hē yìxiē shuǐ* — I'd like to drink some water.

The tone of 一 changes here: yī is said yì before the first tone of xiē.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**一些** (*yìxiē*) is built from 一 'one' and 些 'a few', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *'jit* and *sjae* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription).
