# அரியணை (ariyaṇai) — a throne

## You'll want to know: அரியணை
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C328-MANNAR328-05]; assesses=[] -->

**அரியணை** — *ariyaṇai* — "a throne".

> புதிய மன்னர் அரியணையில் அமர்ந்தார்.
>
> *putiya maṉṉar ariyaṇaiyil amarntār.*
>
> The new king sat on the throne.

அரியணை ஏறு, to come to the throne.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**அரியணை** (*ariyaṇai*) is a Tamil compound built from அரி, "lion", and அணை, "support, prop" (DEDR 123): a lion seat.
