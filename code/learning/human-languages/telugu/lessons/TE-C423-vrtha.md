## You'll want to know: వృథా
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C423-PRATIPHALAM423-02]; assesses=[] -->

**వృథా** — *vṛthā* — "in vain; a waste".

> అతని కష్టం అంతా వృథా అయింది.
>
> *atani kaṣṭaṁ antā vṛthā ayindi.*
>
> All his effort went to waste.

వృథా చేయు, to waste: సమయం వృథా చేయు, to waste time.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**వృథా** (*vṛthā*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *vṛthā*, "at random; in vain, uselessly" (Monier-Williams).
