## You'll want to know: مقبض
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C549-BAB549-03]; assesses=[] -->

**مقبض** — *miqbaḍ* — "a handle".

> انكسر مقبض الباب.
>
> *inkasara miqbaḍ al-bāb.*
>
> The door handle broke.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**مقبض** (*miqbaḍ*) is built on the native Arabic root *q-b-ḍ*, "to grasp with the hand" (Lane). A miqbaḍ is the place where you grasp.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
