## You'll want to know: थुंकणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C492-UCAKI492-03]; assesses=[] -->

**थुंकणे** — *thuṅkṇe* — "to spit".

> रस्त्यावर थुंकणं चुकीचं आहे.
>
> *rastyāvar thuṅkṇaṁ cukīcaṁ āhe.*
>
> Spitting in the street is wrong.

Past (he …): तो थुंकला, *to thuṅklā*.

थुंकी, spittle.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**थुंकणे** (*thuṅkṇe*) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi *thũkṇẽ*, "spits", under the expressive *\*thukk* (CDIAL 6097).
