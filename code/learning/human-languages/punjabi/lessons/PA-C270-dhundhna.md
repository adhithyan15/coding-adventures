## You'll want to know: ਢੂੰਢਣਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C270-SEARCH270-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ਢੂੰਢਣਾ** — *ḍhūṇḍhṇā* — "to search for; to hunt for".

> ਮੈਂ ਸ਼ਹਿਰ ਵਿੱਚ ਡਾਕਖ਼ਾਨਾ ਢੂੰਢਦਾ ਹਾਂ।
>
> *maiṁ shahir vich ḍākkhānā ḍhūṇḍhdā hāṁ.*
>
> I search the town for the post office.

Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਥਾਣਾ ਢੂੰਢਿਆ, *maiṁ thāṇā ḍhūṇḍhiā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਢੂੰਢਣਾ** (*ḍhūṇḍhṇā*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*ḍhūṇḍh*, "seek"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *ḍhū̃ḍhṇā*, "to seek", under it (CDIAL 6839).
