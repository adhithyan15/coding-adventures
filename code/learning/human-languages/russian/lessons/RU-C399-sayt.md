## You'll want to know: сайт
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C399-SET-02]; assesses=[] -->

**сайт** (*sayt*) — "a website".

> У нашей школы есть свой сайт.
>
> *U náshey shkóly yest svoy sayt.*
>
> Our school has its own website.

Masculine. *На сайте* — on the website.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**сайт** was borrowed from English *site* (EtymDB), which is usually traced, through French, to Latin *situs* "a place, a position" (Lewis & Short).
