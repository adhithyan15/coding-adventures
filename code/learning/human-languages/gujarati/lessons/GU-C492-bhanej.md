## You'll want to know: ભાણેજ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C492-PIYAR492-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ભાણેજ** — *bhāṇej* — "a sister's child".

> મામા ભાણેજ માટે રમકડું લાવ્યા.
>
> *māmā bhāṇej māṭe ramakḍũ lāvyā.*
>
> Uncle brought a toy for his sister's child.

A masculine or feminine noun, by the person meant.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ભાણેજ** (*bhāṇej*) is inherited from Sanskrit *bhāginēya*, "sister's son"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati word (CDIAL 9433).
