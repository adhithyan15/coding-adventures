## You'll want to know: जिघ्रति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C469-INDRIYA469-05]; assesses=[] -->

**जिघ्रति** — *jighrati* — "smells".

> बालिका पुष्पं जिघ्रति।
>
> *bālikā puṣpaṃ jighrati.*
>
> The girl smells the flower.

Past (she …): सा पुष्पं घ्रातवती, *sā puṣpaṃ ghrātavatī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**जिघ्रति** (*jighrati*) comes from the root √ghrā, "to smell", whose present stem doubles the root (*ji-ghr-a*) (MW).
