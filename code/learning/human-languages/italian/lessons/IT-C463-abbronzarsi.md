## You'll want to know: abbronzarsi
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C463-SCOGLI-02]; assesses=[] -->

**abbronzarsi** — "to get a tan".

> Mi abbronzo subito al mare.
>
> *I tan quickly at the seaside.*

*L'abbronzatura* — the tan.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*abbronzarsi* is built from *a-* and *bronzo* 'bronze': turning the colour of bronze. The origin of *bronzo* is not settled.
