## You'll want to know: 只要
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C399-ONLYIF399-03]; assesses=[] -->

**只要** — *zhǐyào* — "as long as; provided (that)".

> **只要不下雨我们就去** *zhǐyào bú xiàyǔ wǒmen jiù qù* — As long as it doesn't rain, we'll go.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**只要** (*zhǐyào*) is built from 只 'only' and 要 'to need'. The second is inherited from Middle Chinese *'jiewH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'to bind by agreement'; the dictionary lists the first, *tsyeX* there, as a particle.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what would happen.
