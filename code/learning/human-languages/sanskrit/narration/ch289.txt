Sanskrit, chapter 289: What the Witness Saw.
5 lessons. You can do the first 2 of them in the car; after that you will want to have stopped.


Lesson 1 of 5.
अभिजानाति (abhijānāti) — recognizes.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for threatens, then the Sanskrit for a weapon.

You'll want to know: अभिजानाति.
अभिजानाति — abhijānāti — "recognizes".
यदि दीपः अभविष्यत्, साक्षी चौरम् अभ्यज्ञास्यत्। yadi dīpaḥ abhaviṣyat, sākṣī cauram abhyajñāsyat. If there had been a lamp, the witness would have recognized the thief.
Past (he …): सः चौरम् अभिज्ञातवान्, saḥ cauram abhijñātavān.
Future: अभिज्ञास्यति, abhijñāsyati.

The word, taken apart.
अभिजानाति (abhijānāti) is built from abhi, "towards", and the root √jñā, "to know"; MW gives abhi-jñā as to recognize or become aware of (MW).

Warm-up.

Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a weapon, then the Sanskrit for recognizes.

You'll want to know: वर्णयति.
वर्णयति — varṇayati — "describes".
साक्षी चौरस्य मुखं वर्णयति। sākṣī caurasya mukhaṃ varṇayati. The witness describes the thief's face.
Past (he …): सः मुखं वर्णितवान्, saḥ mukhaṃ varṇitavān.

The word, taken apart.
वर्णयति (varṇayati) is built on varṇa, "colour, outward appearance"; MW treats varṇayati as a verb made from that noun, to paint and to describe (MW).

Lesson 3 of 5.
निरीक्षते (nirīkṣate) — observes closely.

Before we start: this one needs your eyes, so it is not fully a driving lesson. You will want your eyes, because the lesson points at something written down. You can listen to all of it now and come back to the parts that need looking at once you have stopped.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for recognizes, then the Sanskrit for describes.

You'll want to know: निरीक्षते.
निरीक्षते — nirīkṣate — "observes closely".
साक्षी सर्वं निरीक्षते। sākṣī sarvaṃ nirīkṣate. The witness observes everything closely.
Past (he …): सः सर्वं निरीक्षितवान्, saḥ sarvaṃ nirīkṣitavān.

The word, taken apart.
निरीक्षते (nirīkṣate) is built from nir and the root √īkṣ, "to see"; MW gives nir-īkṣ as to look at, regard and observe (MW).

Lesson 4 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for describes, then the Sanskrit for observes closely.

You'll want to know: चिह्नम्.
चिह्नम् — cihnam — "a trace; a sign".
भित्तौ चौरस्य चिह्नम् अस्ति। bhittau caurasya cihnam asti. There is a mark of the thief on the wall.
A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
चिह्नम् (cihnam) MW gives cihna as a mark, a sign or a characteristic, without a derivation, so its origin is uncertain (MW).
