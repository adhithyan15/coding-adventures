## You'll want to know: टीम
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C280-MAIDAN280-01]; assesses=[] -->

**टीम** — *ṭīm* — "a team".

> म्हारी टीम जीतै है।
>
> *mhārī ṭīm jītai hai.*
>
> My team wins.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**टीम** (*ṭīm*) is borrowed from English *team*. It is feminine: **म्हारी टीम**.
