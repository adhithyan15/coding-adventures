## You'll want to know: la grossesse
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C493-BEBE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**la grossesse** — "pregnancy".

> Pendant sa grossesse, elle a continué à travailler.
>
> *During her pregnancy she kept on working.*

*Être enceinte* — to be pregnant.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*la grossesse* is built on *gros* "big" with *-esse*; *gros* is inherited from Late Latin *grossus* "thick".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
