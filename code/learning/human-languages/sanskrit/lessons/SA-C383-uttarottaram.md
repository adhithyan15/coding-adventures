## You'll want to know: उत्तरोत्तरम्
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C383-SANKALPA383-05]; assesses=[] -->

**उत्तरोत्तरम्** — *uttarottaram* — "more and more; step after step".

> यदि भवान् यतते, तर्हि उत्तरोत्तरं कुशलः भविष्यति।
>
> *yadi bhavān yatate, tarhi uttarottaraṃ kuśalaḥ bhaviṣyati.*
>
> If you keep trying, you will grow more and more skilled.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**उत्तरोत्तरम्** (*uttarottaram*) is built from *uttara*, "higher, later", said twice; MW gives *uttarottaram* "higher and higher, more and more, in constant continuation" (MW).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
