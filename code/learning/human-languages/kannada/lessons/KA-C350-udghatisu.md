# ಉದ್ಘಾಟಿಸು (udghāṭisu) — to inaugurate; to open

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[KA-LEX-C350-SANMANA350-03, KA-LEX-C350-SANMANA350-04] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Kannada for an honorific title, then the Kannada for fame.

## You'll want to know: ಉದ್ಘಾಟಿಸು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C350-SANMANA350-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ಉದ್ಘಾಟಿಸು** — *udghāṭisu* — "to inaugurate; to open".

> ಹಿರಿಯ ಲೇಖಕರು ಹೊಸ ಗ್ರಂಥಾಲಯವನ್ನು ಉದ್ಘಾಟಿಸಿದರು.
>
> *hiriya lēkhakaru hosa granthālayavannu udghāṭisidaru.*
>
> A senior writer opened the new library.

Past (I …): ಉದ್ಘಾಟಿಸಿದೆ, *udghāṭiside*.

Buildings, events and meetings are opened: ಸಮಾರಂಭವನ್ನು ಉದ್ಘಾಟಿಸು. The noun is ಉದ್ಘಾಟನೆ.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಉದ್ಘಾಟಿಸು** (*udghāṭisu*) is built on Sanskrit *udghāṭana*, "the act of opening, unlocking" (Monier-Williams), with the Kannada verb ending -*isu*.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
