## You'll want to know: تيار
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C471-KHALIJ471-04]; assesses=[] -->

**تيار** — *tayyār* — "a current (of water or air)".

> كان التيار في النهر قويا.
>
> *kāna at-tayyār fī an-nahr qawiyyan.*
>
> The current in the river was strong.

An electric current is *tayyār kahrabāʾī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**تيار** (*tayyār*) is listed by Lane under *t-y-r* as the waves of a sea or a great river; the origin is not settled.
