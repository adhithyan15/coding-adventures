Bengali, chapter 458: Honest and Deceitful.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
সৎ (sôt) — honest; upright.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for helpful to others, then the Bengali for noble.

You'll want to know: সৎ.
সৎ — sôt — "honest; upright".
সৎ লোকটা মানিব্যাগটা পুলিশকে দিল। sôt lokṭā mānibyāgṭā pulishke dilô. The honest man handed the wallet to the police.
সৎ (sôt) also marks a step-relation: সৎ (sôt) ভাই, a half-brother.

The word, taken apart.
সৎ (sôt) is a tatsama, Sanskrit sat, "being, existing" and "true, good, honest" (Monier-Williams); it ends in the new letter ৎ.

Lesson 2 of 5.
কপট (kôpôṭ) — insincere; two-faced; feigned.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for noble, then the Bengali for honest.

You'll want to know: কপট.
কপট — kôpôṭ — "insincere; two-faced; feigned".
লোকটার কপট হাসি দেখে মীরা তাকে বিশ্বাস করল না। lokṭār kôpôṭ hāsi dekhe mīrā tāke bishvās kôrlô nā. Seeing the man's false smile, Mira did not trust him.
কপট (kôpôṭ) রাগ, pretended anger: the kind a grandmother puts on.

The word, taken apart.
কপট (kôpôṭ) is a tatsama, Sanskrit kapaṭa, a noun for fraud and deceit (Monier-Williams); Bengali uses it as an adjective, for a smile or a feeling put on to mislead. It is a different word from Sanskrit kapāṭa, a door leaf.

You'll want to know: বিশ্বস্ত.
বিশ্বস্ত — bishvôstô — "trustworthy; loyal".
আমাদের পুরনো দারোয়ান বিশ্বস্ত লোক। āmāder purôno dāroyān bishvôstô lok. Our old gatekeeper is a trustworthy man.

The word, taken apart.
বিশ্বস্ত (bishvôstô) is a tatsama, Sanskrit viśvasta, "trusted, faithful" (Monier-Williams), from the same root as বিশ্বাস করা (taught), to trust.

The word, taken apart.
ধূর্ত (dhūrtô) is a tatsama, Sanskrit dhūrta, "cunning, crafty", and as a noun "a rogue, a cheat" (Monier-Williams).
