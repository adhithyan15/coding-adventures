## You'll want to know: der Gangplatz
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C418-BORD-03]; assesses=[] -->

**der Gangplatz** — "the aisle seat".

> Auf langen Flügen nimmt er lieber einen Gangplatz.
>
> *On long flights he prefers an aisle seat.*

Plural: *die Gangplätze*. *Der Gang* — the aisle, the corridor.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Gangplatz* is built from *Gang* 'passage, aisle', from *gehen* 'to go', inherited from Old High German *gang* (EtymDB), and *Platz* 'place', from Latin *platea*.
