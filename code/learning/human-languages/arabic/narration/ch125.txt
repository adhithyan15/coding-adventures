Arabic, chapter 125: Bucket, Broom, Rubbish, Basket, Box.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
دلو (dalw) — a bucket.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a button, then the Arabic for a nail.

You'll want to know: دلو.
دلو — dalw — "a bucket".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: dalw]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: dalw, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say dalw]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Arabic for a button, then the Arabic for a nail, then say dalw again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does دلو (dalw) mean? ("A bucket".) Say it once more.
