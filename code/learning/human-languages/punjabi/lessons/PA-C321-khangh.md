## You'll want to know: ਖੰਘ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C321-COLD321-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ਖੰਘ** — *khaṅgh* — "a cough".

> ਬੱਚੇ ਨੂੰ ਰਾਤ ਨੂੰ ਬਹੁਤ ਖੰਘ ਸੀ।
>
> *bachche nū̃ rāt nū̃ bahut khaṅgh sī.*
>
> The child had a bad cough at night.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਖੰਘ** (*khaṅgh*) goes back to a reconstructed *\*khaṅkh*, "cough, hawk", an imitative form; CDIAL lists Punjabi *khaṅgh*, "cough", under it (CDIAL 3763).
