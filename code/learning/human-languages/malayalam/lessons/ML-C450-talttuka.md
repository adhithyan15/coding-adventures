# താഴ്ത്തുക (tāḻttuka) — to lower

## Warm-up

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Malayalam for to go higher, then the Malayalam for to lift up.

## You'll want to know: താഴ്ത്തുക
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C450-SAMRAKSANAM450-01]; assesses=[] -->

**താഴ്ത്തുക** — *tāḻttuka* — "to lower".

> കടക്കാരൻ സാധനങ്ങളുടെ വില താഴ്ത്തി.
>
> *kaṭakkāran sādhanaṅṅaḷuṭe vila tāḻtti.*
>
> The shopkeeper lowered the prices of the goods.

Past: താഴ്ത്തി, *tāḻtti*.

The causative of താഴുക, to sink; താഴെ, below, is the same root.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**താഴ്ത്തുക** (*tāḻttuka*) is native Dravidian, the causative of താഴുക, "to sink, to come low" (DEDR 3178), the root of താഴെ, "below".
