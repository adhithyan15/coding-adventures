Gujarati, chapter 186: Paying at the till.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ચૂકવવું (cūkavvũ) — to pay, to settle.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for pair, then the Gujarati for to test.

You'll want to know: ચૂકવવું.
ચૂકવવું — cūkavvũ — "to pay, to settle".
હું દુકાનદારને પૈસા ચૂકવું છું. — hũ dukāndārne paisā cūkavũ chũ — "I pay the shopkeeper the money."

The word, taken apart.
ચૂકવવું (cūkavvũ) is a tadbhava, the causative of a verb built on cukk, "fall short of, stop", a reconstructed form. CDIAL glosses the Gujarati word "to discharge, settle" (CDIAL 4848).

Lesson 2 of 5.
રોકડ (rokaḍ) — cash.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to test, then the Gujarati for to pay.

You'll want to know: રોકડ.
રોકડ — rokaḍ — "cash".
મારી પાસે રોકડ નથી. — mārī pāse rokaḍ nathī — "I have no cash on me."
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
રોકડ (rokaḍ) is a tadbhava. CDIAL lists it, glossed "cash", under rōkka, "ready money", a reconstructed form, extended with the suffix -ḍ- (CDIAL 10828).

Lesson 5 of 5.
રસીદ (rasīd) — receipt.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for small change, then the Gujarati for banknote.

You'll want to know: રસીદ.
રસીદ — rasīd — "receipt".
દુકાનદાર રસીદ આપે છે. — dukāndār rasīd āpe che — "The shopkeeper gives a receipt."

The word, taken apart.
રસીદ (rasīd) is a loan, usually traced to Persian rasīd, "receipt"; EtymDB gives the same Persian source. CDIAL has no entry for it.
