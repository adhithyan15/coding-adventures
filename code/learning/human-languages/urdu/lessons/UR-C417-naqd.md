## You'll want to know: نقد
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C417-RAQAM417-03]; assesses=[] -->

**نقد** — *naqd* — "cash".

> دکاندار نقد پیسے مانگتا ہے۔
>
> *dukāndār naqd paise māṅgtā hai.*
>
> The shopkeeper wants cash.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**نقد** (*naqd*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *naqd*, "ready money".
