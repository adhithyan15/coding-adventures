Arabic, chapter 499: Honest and loyal.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
أمين (amīn) — honest, trustworthy.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to make angry, then the Arabic for to blame.

You'll want to know: أمين.
أمين — amīn — "honest, trustworthy".
المحاسب رجل أمين. al-muḥāsib rajul amīn. The accountant is an honest man.
It also names a keeper of something: أمين (amīn) المكتبة, the librarian.

The word, taken apart.
أمين (amīn) is built on the native Arabic root ʾ-m-n, "to be safe, to trust" (Lane). Āmin, safe, which you know, comes from the same root.

What you've built.
One more word for describing what you lived and felt.

[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Arabic for to make angry, then the Arabic for to blame, then say amīn again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does أمين (amīn) mean? ("Honest, trustworthy".) Say it once more.
