## You'll want to know: общественный
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C364-OBSHCHESTVO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**общественный** (*obshchéstvennyy*) — "public, social".

> Я езжу на работу на общественном транспорте.
>
> *Ya yézzhu na rabótu na obshchéstvennom tránsporte.*
>
> I go to work by public transport.

Public opinion is *общественное мнение*, and public transport is *общественный транспорт*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**общественный** is the adjective made from *общество* "society", which Russian took from Church Slavonic *obĭščĭstvo* (EtymDB).
