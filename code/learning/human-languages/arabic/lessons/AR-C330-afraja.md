## You'll want to know: أفرج
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C330-SIJN330-05]; assesses=[] -->

**أفرج** — *afraja* — "to release, to set free".

> أفرجت الشرطة عن الرجل في المساء.
>
> *afrajat ash-shurṭa ʿan ar-rajul fī al-masāʾ.*
>
> The police released the man in the evening.

Present: يفرج, *yufrij*.

It takes عن: أفرج عن السجين.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**أفرج** (*afraja*) is built on the native Arabic root *f-r-j* (Lane), "to open a gap; to relieve"; *faraj* is relief from trouble. It is Form IV, "to open the way out".
