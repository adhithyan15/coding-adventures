## You'll want to know: خبر فوری
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C315-KHABARFOWRI-01]; assesses=[] -->

**خبر فوری** (*khabar-e fowri*) — "breaking news".

> تلویزیون یک خبر فوری پخش کرد.
>
> *Televiziyon yek khabar-e fowri pakhsh kard.*
>
> The television broadcast a piece of breaking news.

*fowri* "urgent", from *fowr* "at once".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*khabar* "news" is borrowed from Arabic *khabar* (EtymDB); *fowri* "urgent" adds the Persian *-i* to *fowr* "at once", usually traced to Arabic *fawr*.
