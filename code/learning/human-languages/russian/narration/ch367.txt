Russian, chapter 367: Freedom and equality.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
свобода (svobóda) — freedom.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a protest, then the Russian for to protest.

You'll want to know: свобода.
свобода (svobóda) — "freedom".
Для меня свобода важнее денег. Dlya menyá svobóda vazhnéye déneg. For me, freedom matters more than money.
Feminine. Свобода слова — freedom of speech. На свободе — at large.

The word, taken apart.
свобода (svobóda) is inherited through Old East Slavic svoboda from Proto-Slavic svoboda "freedom" (EtymDB), usually traced to the root of свой "one’s own".

Lesson 2 of 5.
равенство (rávenstvo) — equality.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to protest, then the Russian for freedom.

You'll want to know: равенство.
равенство (rávenstvo) — "equality".
Мы верим в равенство всех людей. My vérim v rávenstvo vsekh lyudéy. We believe in the equality of all people.
Neuter. Равенство мужчин и женщин — equality of men and women.

The word, taken apart.
равенство (rávenstvo) is built on равный "equal", the Church Slavonic twin of native ровный "even, level"; both go back to Proto-Slavic orvĭnŭ. The ра- is the Church Slavonic shape, ро- the native one.

Lesson 3 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for freedom, then the Russian for equality.

You'll want to know: бедность.
бедность (bédnost) — "poverty".
Многие семьи живут в бедности. Mnógiye sémi zhivút v bédnosti. Many families live in poverty.
Feminine. From бедный, poor.

The word, taken apart.
бедность (bédnost) is built from бедный "poor" and the suffix -ость (EtymDB); бедный comes from беда "trouble", Proto-Slavic běda (EtymDB).

Lesson 5 of 5.
безработица (bezrabótitsa) — unemployment.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for poverty, then the Russian for wealth.

You'll want to know: безработица.
безработица (bezrabótitsa) — "unemployment".
В этом районе высокая безработица. V étom rayóne vysókaya bezrabótitsa. Unemployment is high in this area.
Feminine. A person out of work is безработный.

The word, taken apart.
безработица (bezrabótitsa) is built from без "without" and работа "work", which goes back to Proto-Slavic orbota "toil", on раб "a slave".
