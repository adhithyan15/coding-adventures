## You'll want to know: だまる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C435-CONTENT435-05]; assesses=[] -->

**だまる** — *damaru* — "to fall silent".

> **せんせいが きて、みんな だまりました。** — *sensei ga kite, minna damarimashita* — "The teacher came, and everyone fell silent."

Polite **だまります**, *damarimasu*; a godan verb. Past (polite): **こどもが だまりました。**, *kodomo ga damarimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**だまる** (*damaru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji spelling was attached to it later (JMdict).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
