## You'll want to know: दाग़
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C490-CHHALA490-05]; assesses=[] -->

**दाग़** — *dāġ* — "a scar; a stain".

> बचपन की चोट का दाग़ अरुण के माथे पर आज भी है।
>
> *bacpan kī coṭ kā dāġ Aruṇ ke māthe par āj bhī hai.*
>
> Arun still has the scar of a childhood injury on his forehead.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**दाग़** (*dāġ*) is usually traced to Persian *dāġ*, "a brand, a mark": a stain on cloth or a scar on skin.
