## You'll want to know: نفق
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C431-MURUR431-01]; assesses=[] -->

**نفق** — *nafaq* — "a tunnel".

> دخل القطار نفقا طويلا تحت الجبل.
>
> *dakhala al-qiṭār nafaqan ṭawīlan taḥta al-jabal.*
>
> The train went into a long tunnel under the mountain.

Plural: أنفاق, *anfāq*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**نفق** (*nafaq*) is built on the native Arabic root *n-f-q*, "to pass away, to run out" (Lane). Lane gives *nafaq* as a passage under the ground.
