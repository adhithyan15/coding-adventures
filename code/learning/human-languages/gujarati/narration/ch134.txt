Gujarati, chapter 134: Brave, Calm, Happy, Sad, Hungry.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
બહાદુર (bahādur) — brave.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for clever, then the Gujarati for lazy.

You'll want to know: બહાદુર.
બહાદુર (bahādur) — "brave".

The word, taken apart.
બહાદુર (bahādur) is a loan, usually traced through Persian bahādur to a Turkic or Mongol word for "hero".

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: ભૂખ્યું.
ભૂખ્યું (bhūkhyũ) — "hungry".

The word, taken apart.
ભૂખ્યું (bhūkhyũ) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from bubhukṣita, "hungry" (CDIAL 9287).
