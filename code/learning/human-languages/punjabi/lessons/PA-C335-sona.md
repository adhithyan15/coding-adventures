## You'll want to know: ਸੋਨਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C335-METAL335-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ਸੋਨਾ** — *sonā* — "gold".

> ਵਿਆਹ ਤੋਂ ਪਹਿਲਾਂ ਅਸੀਂ ਸੋਨਾ ਖ਼ਰੀਦਿਆ।
>
> *viāh ton pahilāṁ asīṁ sonā kharīdiā.*
>
> Before the wedding we bought gold.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਸੋਨਾ** (*sonā*) is inherited from Sanskrit *suvarṇa*, "golden; gold"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *sonā*, "gold", under it (CDIAL 13519).
