## You'll want to know: ਨਤੀਜਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C360-EXAM360-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ਨਤੀਜਾ** — *natījā* — "a result".

> ਇਮਤਿਹਾਨ ਦਾ ਨਤੀਜਾ ਕੱਲ੍ਹ ਆਵੇਗਾ।
>
> *imtihān dā natījā kall āvegā.*
>
> The exam result will come out tomorrow.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਨਤੀਜਾ** (*natījā*) is a loan, usually traced through Persian to Arabic *natīja*, "result".
