## You'll want to know: actuar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C759-ESCENARIO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**actuar** — "to act, to perform".

> Actúa en una obra de teatro.
>
> *She is acting in a play.*

Also to act, to take action. Forms: *actúo*, *actúas*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*actuar* comes from Medieval Latin *āctuāre* "to put into action" (EtymDB), built on *āctus*, from *agere* "to do".
