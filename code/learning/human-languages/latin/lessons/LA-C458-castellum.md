## You'll want to know: castellum, castellī
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C458-ANGIPORTUM-05]; assesses=[] -->

**castellum, castellī** — "a water tower, a reservoir; a fort, a stronghold".

> Aqua ex aquaeductū in castellum venit et inde in urbem.
>
> *Water comes from the aqueduct into the water tower and from there into the city.*

Second declension, neuter: *castellum, castellī n.*, a diminutive of *castrum* 'fort'. The *castellum aquae* shared the water out to the pipes. English *castle* comes from it.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*castellum* is a diminutive of *castrum* 'fort, fortified place' (EtymDB; Lewis & Short), and Lewis & Short also give the water reservoir of an aqueduct. English *castle* comes from it.
