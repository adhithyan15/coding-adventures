Gujarati, chapter 302: The Fire Engine.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
બંબો (bambo) — a fire engine.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to burst, then the Gujarati for a bang.

You'll want to know: બંબો.
બંબો — bambo — "a fire engine".
પાંચ મિનિટમાં બંબો આવી ગયો. pā̃c miniṭmā̃ bambo āvī gayo. The fire engine arrived within five minutes.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
બંબો (bambo) is probably from Portuguese bomba, "a pump": the fire engine began as a pump on wheels. The firemen are બંબાવાળા, the men of the pump.

Lesson 2 of 5.
બુઝાવવું (bujhāvvũ) — to extinguish.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a bang, then the Gujarati for a fire engine.

You'll want to know: બુઝાવવું.
બુઝાવવું — bujhāvvũ — "to extinguish".
લોકોએ પાણી નાખીને આગ બુઝાવી. lokoe pāṇī nākhīne āg bujhāvī. People poured water on the fire and put it out.
Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે આગ બુઝાવી, teṇe āg bujhāvī.

The word, taken apart.
બુઝાવવું (bujhāvvũ) belongs with bujhāvũ, to go out, which CDIAL lists under vijjhāyati, "is burnt out, is extinguished", a form it reconstructs; the doing verb continues that entry's causative line, vijjhāpayati (CDIAL 11703): to make a fire go out.

Lesson 3 of 5.
દોરડું (dorḍũ) — a rope.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a fire engine, then the Gujarati for to extinguish.

You'll want to know: દોરડું.
દોરડું — dorḍũ — "a rope".
તેમણે દોરડાથી એક બાળકને નીચે ઉતાર્યું. temṇe dorḍāthī ek bāḷakne nīce utāryũ. They let a child down on a rope.
A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
દોરડું (dorḍũ) belongs with દોરો, a thread: both go back to Sanskrit davara, "string", where CDIAL lists Gujarati doro, "string, rope" (CDIAL 6225). The ending -ḍũ makes the thicker thing, a rope.

Lesson 4 of 5.
ઓગળવું (ogaḷvũ) — to melt.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to extinguish, then the Gujarati for a rope.

You'll want to know: ઓગળવું.
ઓગળવું — ogaḷvũ — "to melt".
તડકામાં માખણ ઓગળી ગયું. taḍkāmā̃ mākhaṇ ogḷī gayũ. The butter melted in the sun.
Past (it …): તે બરફ ઓગળી ગયો, te baraph ogḷī gayo.

The word, taken apart.
ઓગળવું (ogaḷvũ) is inherited from Sanskrit avagalati, "trickles down", and CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb, "to trickle down, melt" (CDIAL 742). Ice and butter ઓગળે છે.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: ભડકો.
ભડકો — bhaḍko — "a blaze; a sudden flare of flame".
તપેલીમાં તેલ ખૂબ ગરમ થયું અને મોટો ભડકો થયો. tapelīmā̃ tel khūb garam thayũ ane moṭo bhaḍko thayo. The oil in the pan got too hot and flared up in a big blaze.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
comes from bhaṭ, "a sudden movement or noise", a form CDIAL reconstructs, under which it lists Gujarati bhaṛkũ, "a blaze" (CDIAL 9365). A ભડકો (bhaḍko) is flame that leaps up all at once.
