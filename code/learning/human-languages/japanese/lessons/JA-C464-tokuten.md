## You'll want to know: とくてん
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C464-CROWD464-03]; assesses=[] -->

**とくてん** — *tokuten* — "points scored; a score".

> **せんしゅが さいごに とくてんしました。** — *senshu ga saigo ni tokuten shimashita* — "The player scored at the end."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**とくてん** (*tokuten*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *toku*, "gain" (Middle Chinese *tok*) and *ten*, "point" (*temX*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
