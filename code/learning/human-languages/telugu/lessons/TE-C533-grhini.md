# గృహిణి (gṛhiṇi) — a homemaker

## You'll want to know: గృహిణి
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C533-SAMSARAM533-03]; assesses=[] -->

**గృహిణి** — *gṛhiṇi* — "a homemaker".

> మా అమ్మ గృహిణి.
>
> *mā amma gṛhiṇi.*
>
> My mother is a homemaker.

From గృహం, house.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**గృహిణి** (*gṛhiṇi*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *gṛhiṇī*, "the mistress of a house" (Monier-Williams).
