## You'll want to know: sellar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C656-FRONTERA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**sellar** — "to stamp; to seal".

> En la frontera me sellaron el pasaporte.
>
> *They stamped my passport at the border.*

*Sellar un sobre* — to seal an envelope.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*sellar* is inherited from Vulgar Latin *sigellāre*, Latin *sigillāre* "to seal" (EtymDB), from *sigillum* "a seal", a little *signum* "sign".
