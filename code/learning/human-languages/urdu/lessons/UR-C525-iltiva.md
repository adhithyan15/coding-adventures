## You'll want to know: التوا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C525-MOHLAT525-02]; assesses=[] -->

**التوا** — *iltivā* — "a postponement".

> اگر التوا نہیں ہوا تو امتحان کل ہو گا۔
>
> *agar iltivā nahīṅ huā to imtihān kal ho gā.*
>
> If it is not postponed, the exam will be tomorrow.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**التوا** (*iltivā*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *iltiwāʾ*, "a bending, a turning aside"; the sense "putting off" came later, and the sources we use do not show how it arose.
