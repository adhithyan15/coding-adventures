## You'll want to know: સાડી
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C154-CLOTH154-02]; assesses=[] -->

**સાડી** — *sāḍī* — "sari".

> **મારી બહેન સાડી પહેરે છે.** — *mārī bahen sāḍī pahere che* — "My sister wears a sari."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**સાડી** (*sāḍī*) is a tadbhava. It comes from Sanskrit *śāṭa*, *śāṭī*, "strip of cloth", and CDIAL glosses the Gujarati word "sari" (CDIAL 12381).
