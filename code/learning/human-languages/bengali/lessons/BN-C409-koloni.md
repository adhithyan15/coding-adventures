## You'll want to know: কলোনি
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C409-ELAKA409-03]; assesses=[] -->

**কলোনি** — *kôloni* — "a housing colony; an estate".

> মীরার মামা রেল কলোনিতে থাকেন।
>
> *mīrār māmā rel kôlonite thāken.*
>
> Mira's uncle lives in the railway colony.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**কলোনি** (*kôloni*) is borrowed from English *colony*, used in South Asia for a planned group of houses, often for one employer's staff.
