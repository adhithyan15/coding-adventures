Marathi, chapter 218: Spider, Buffalo, She-goat, Fog, Dew.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
कोळी (koḷī) — a spider.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a harbour, then the Marathi for a well.

You'll want to know: कोळी.
कोळी — koḷī — "a spider".

The word, taken apart.
कोळी (koḷī) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from kolika, "weaver" (CDIAL 3535). CDIAL glosses the Marathi word "a caste of watercarriers, a sort of spider". It cites a view linking the Sanskrit word with Tamil kōṭikar, "weaver", and a Munda word for "spider".

Lesson 4 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a buffalo, then the Marathi for a she-goat.

You'll want to know: धुके.
धुके — dhuke — "fog".

The word, taken apart.
The origin of धुके (dhuke) is not settled. CDIAL lists a Marathi dhumẽ, "fog", under Sanskrit dhūmra, "smoke-coloured" (CDIAL 6861), but has no entry for धुके.

Lesson 5 of 5.
दव (dav) — dew.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a she-goat, then the Marathi for fog.

You'll want to know: दव.
दव — dav — "dew".

The word, taken apart.
दव (dav) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from drava, "running, flowing; fluidity" (CDIAL 6623). CDIAL glosses the Marathi word "dew, dampness". Bloch derived it instead from udaka, "water".
