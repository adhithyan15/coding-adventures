## You'll want to know: عينة
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C456-HAJM456-03]; assesses=[] -->

**عينة** — *ʿayyina* — "a sample".

> أعطاني التاجر عينة صغيرة من العسل.
>
> *aʿṭānī at-tājir ʿayyina ṣaghīra min al-ʿasal.*
>
> The merchant gave me a small sample of the honey.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**عينة** (*ʿayyina*) is built on the native Arabic root *ʿ-y-n*, "the eye; a thing itself" (Lane). Modern Arabic uses it for a sample or a specimen.
