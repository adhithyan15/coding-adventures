Bengali, chapter 276: Something Happened.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ঘটনা (ghôṭônā) — an event; an incident.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a female singer, then the Bengali for a female dancer.

You'll want to know: ঘটনা.
ঘটনা — ghôṭônā — "an event; an incident".
আমরা কী করব, সেটা ঘটনার ওপর নির্ভর করে। āmrā kī kôrbô, seṭā ghôṭônār opôr nirbhôr kôre. What we do will depend on the incident.
ঘটনা (ghôṭônā) ঘটে, an event takes place: একটা ঘটনা (ghôṭônā) ঘটল, something happened.

The word, taken apart.
ঘটনা (ghôṭônā) is Sanskrit ghaṭanā, taken over whole as a tatsama; Monier-Williams gives it senses from effort and motion to a taking effect. It sits beside the verb ঘটা, to happen.

Lesson 2 of 5.
কিছু (kichhu) — something; some.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a female dancer, then the Bengali for an event.

You'll want to know: কিছু.
কিছু — kichhu — "something; some".
গতকাল রাতে পাড়ায় কিছু একটা ঘটল। gôtôkāl rāte pāṛāy kichhu ekṭā ghôṭlô. Something happened in the neighbourhood last night.
Before a noun it means some: কিছু (kichhu) টাকা, some money.

The word, taken apart.
কিছু (kichhu) comes down from Sanskrit kiṁcid, 'anything' (CDIAL 3144, which lists Bengali kichu). কিছু একটা is 'some one thing', something you cannot yet name.

Lesson 3 of 5.
অনেক (ônek) — many; a lot.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for an event, then the Bengali for something.

You'll want to know: অনেক.
অনেক — ônek — "many; a lot".
ঘটনাটা দেখতে অনেক লোক রাস্তায় এল। ghôṭônāṭā dekhte ônek lok rāstāy elô. A lot of people came out onto the road to see what had happened.

The word, taken apart.
অনেক (ônek) is Sanskrit aneka, an- 'not' and eka 'one' (Monier-Williams): 'not one' came to mean 'many'. এক, one, is the same eka.

Lesson 4 of 5.
মানুষ (mānush) — a human being; people.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for something, then the Bengali for many.

You'll want to know: মানুষ.
মানুষ — mānush — "a human being; people".
ঘটনার সময় ওখানে কিছু মানুষ ছিল। ghôṭônār sômôy okhāne kichhu mānush chhilô. There were a few people there when it happened.

The word, taken apart.
মানুষ (mānush) comes down from Sanskrit mānuṣa, 'human; a man' (CDIAL 10049, which lists Bengali mānus). It names one person or people in general; লোক is the plainer everyday word.

Lesson 5 of 5.
দুর্ঘটনা (durghôṭônā) — an accident.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for many, then the Bengali for a human being.

You'll want to know: দুর্ঘটনা.
দুর্ঘটনা — durghôṭônā — "an accident".
গতকাল সকালে স্টেশনের কাছে একটা বড় দুর্ঘটনা ঘটল। gôtôkāl sôkāle sṭeshôner kāchhe ekṭā bôṛo durghôṭônā ghôṭlô. There was a bad accident near the station yesterday morning.

The word, taken apart.
দুর্ঘটনা (durghôṭônā) joins Sanskrit dur-, 'bad, ill', to ঘটনা (ghôṭônā), an event; Monier-Williams has both parts. A bad happening is an accident.
