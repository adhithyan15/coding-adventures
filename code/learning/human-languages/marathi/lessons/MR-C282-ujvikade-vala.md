# उजवीकडे वळा (ujvīkaḍe vaḷā) — giving the way

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MR-POSTP-KADE, MR-LEX-C127-TIME127-03, MR-GRAMMAR-C282-ASK-WAY-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "go right" with the **-कडे** you know. (*ujvīkaḍe jā*.) Then "straight". (*saraḷ*.)

## You'll want to know: go, turn
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-GRAMMAR-C282-GIVE-WAY-01]; assesses=[MR-LEX-C253-ACT253-01, MR-LEX-C128-LOC128-01, MR-LEX-DAAVA, MR-LEX-C112-PLACE112-05] -->

> **सरळ जा, मग चौकात उजवीकडे वळा.**
>
> *saraḷ jā, mag caukāt ujvīkaḍe vaḷā.* — "Go straight, then turn right at the square."

**वळा** is **वळणे** with **-आ**, the polite "turn", built like **घ्या** and **बसा**. To a friend: **वळ** (*vaḷ*). **जा** serves both. With **पहिल्या**, "the first", the way gets precise: **पहिल्या चौकात डावीकडे वळा** (*pahilyā caukāt ḍāvīkaḍe vaḷā*), "turn left at the first square"; **पुढे जा** (*puḍhe jā*), "go on ahead".

## You'll want to know: what stands beside it
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-GRAMMAR-C282-LANDMARK-01]; assesses=[MR-POSTP-SAMOR, MR-LEX-C114-TOWN114-03, MR-LEX-C113-TOWN113-01, MR-POSTP-JAVAL] -->

> **स्टेशन मंदिराच्या समोर आहे.**
>
> *sṭeśan mandirācyā samor āhe.* — "The station is opposite the temple."

**-च्या** on the landmark, then the place word: **दुकानाच्या जवळ** (*dukānācyā javaḷ*), "near the shop"; **मंदिराच्या मागे** (*mandirācyā māge*), "behind the temple".
