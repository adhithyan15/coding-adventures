# دوران (daurān) — during

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[UR-GRAMMAR-C266-ONGOING-PAST-01, UR-LEX-C87-WORLD87-02] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say "it was raining". (*bārish ho rahī thī*.) Then "a journey". (*safar*.)

## You'll want to know: دوران
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C266-DAURAN-01]; assesses=[UR-LEX-C87-WORLD87-02, UR-LEX-BARISH, UR-GRAMMAR-C266-ONGOING-PAST-01] -->

**دوران** — *daurān* — "during".

> **سفر کے دوران بارش ہوئی۔**
>
> *safar ke daurān bārish huī.* — "It rained during the journey."

It stands after its noun, with **کے** (*ke*) in front of it, as in **کے لیے** (*ke liye*).

> **کھانے کے دوران وہ آیا۔**
>
> *khāne ke daurān voh āyā.* — "He came during the meal."

**کھانا** (*khānā*) becomes **کھانے** (*khāne*) before **کے**, as most masculine nouns in -ا do.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**دوران** (*daurān*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *dawarān*, "a turning round".
