## You'll want to know: বেয়াই
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C482-JETHA482-05]; assesses=[] -->

**বেয়াই** — *beyāi* — "a child's father-in-law; a co-father-in-law".

> বিয়ের পরে দুই বেয়াই বন্ধু হলেন।
>
> *biyer pôre dui beyāi bôndhu hôlen.*
>
> After the wedding the two fathers-in-law became friends.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**বেয়াই** (*beyāi*) is listed by CDIAL for Bengali, as a son's or daughter's father-in-law, under Sanskrit *vivāhin* (CDIAL 11925), from the same root as বিয়ে (taught), a wedding.
