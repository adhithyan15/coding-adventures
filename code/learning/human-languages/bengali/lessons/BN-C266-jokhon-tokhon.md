# যখন … তখন … (jôkhon … tôkhon …) — the scene and the event

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[BN-GRAMMAR-C266-ONGOING-PAST-01, BN-LEX-C19-JOKHON-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "I was reading". (*āmi pôṛchhilām*.) Chapter thirty paired **যখন**, "when", with **তখন**, "then": **যখন আমি আসি, তখন চা খাই**. Read it aloud.

## You'll want to know: the scene and the event
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-GRAMMAR-C266-SCENE-EVENT-01]; assesses=[BN-LEX-C19-JOKHON-01, BN-GRAMMAR-C266-ONGOING-PAST-01, BN-GRAMMAR-C264-PAST-PERSON-01, BN-LEX-C63-HOME63-04] -->

> **যখন আমি বই পড়ছিলাম, তখন মীরা এল।**
>
> *jôkhon āmi bôi pôṛchhilām, tôkhon mīrā elô.* — "I was reading when Mira came."

The **যখন** half is the scene, in the going-on past. The **তখন** half is the event that broke into it, in the plain past. English puts "when" on the event; Bengali puts **যখন** on the scene, and **তখন** opens the event.

## You'll want to know: তখন on its own
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-GRAMMAR-C266-TOKHON-01]; assesses=[BN-LEX-C78-TIME78-01, BN-LEX-C81-PLACE81-03, BN-LEX-C255-JINIS255-02, BN-GRAMMAR-C264-WAS-01] -->

**তখন** also stands alone in a story: "then, at that time".

> **তখন রাত ছিল। বাইরে ঝড় হচ্ছিল।**
>
> *tôkhon rāt chhilô. bāire jhôṛ hôchchhilô.* — "It was night then. A storm was going on outside."
