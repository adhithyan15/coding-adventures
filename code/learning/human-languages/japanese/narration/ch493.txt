Japanese, chapter 493: Freezer and Cupboard.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
れいとうこ (reitōko) — a freezer.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for to steam (food), then the Japanese for to peel.

You'll want to know: れいとうこ.
れいとうこ — reitōko — "a freezer".
れいとうこに こおりが あります。 — reitōko ni kōri ga arimasu — "There is ice in the freezer."

The word, taken apart.
れいとうこ (reitōko) is Sino-Japanese (kango): three Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are rei, "cool", tō, "frozen" (Middle Chinese tuwngH) and ko, "warehouse" (khuH) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).

Lesson 2 of 5.
とだな (todana) — a cupboard.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for to peel, then the Japanese for a freezer.

You'll want to know: とだな.
とだな — todana — "a cupboard".
ちゃわんを とだなに しまいます。 — chawan o todana ni shimaimasu — "I put the teacups away in the cupboard."

The word, taken apart.
とだな (todana) is a native Japanese compound (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese: built from to, "door" and dana, "shelf" (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).

Lesson 5 of 5.
れいとうする (reitō suru) — to freeze (food).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for a rice cooker, then the Japanese for the washing-up.

You'll want to know: れいとうする.
れいとうする — reitō suru — "to freeze (food)".
にくが のこったら、れいとうします。 — niku ga nokottara, reitōshimasu — "If there is meat left over, I freeze it."
Polite れいとうします, reitōshimasu; a する verb. Past (polite): ははが さかなを れいとうしました。, haha ga sakana o reitōshimashita.

The word, taken apart.
れいとうする (reitō suru) is the Sino-Japanese (kango) noun reitō with the native verb suru, "to do": two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are rei, "cool" and tō, "frozen" (Middle Chinese tuwngH) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
