## You'll want to know: نوبت گرفتن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C452-NOBAT-02]; assesses=[] -->

**نوبت گرفتن** (*nowbat gereftan*) — "to get an appointment; to take a queue number".

> برای دکتر از هفته قبل نوبت گرفتم.
>
> *Barâ-ye doktor az hafte-ye qabl nowbat gereftam.*
>
> I made the doctor's appointment a week ago.

*nowbat* + *gereftan* 'to take'. Present stem *gir-*: *nowbat migiram*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*nowbat gereftan* is built from *nowbat* 'a turn', borrowed from Arabic *nawba* (EtymDB), and the native verb *gereftan* 'to take', usually traced to Old Persian *grab-*.
