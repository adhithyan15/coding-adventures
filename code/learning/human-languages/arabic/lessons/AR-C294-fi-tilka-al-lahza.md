# في تلك اللحظة (fī tilka al-laḥẓa) — at that moment

## You'll want to know: في تلك اللحظة
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C294-FAJATAN294-05]; assesses=[] -->

**في تلك اللحظة** — *fī tilka al-laḥẓa* — "at that moment".

> في تلك اللحظة دخل الأستاذ الصف.
>
> *fī tilka al-laḥẓa dakhala al-ustādh aṣ-ṣaff.*
>
> At that moment the teacher came into the classroom.

A story moves on with it: something happens, and in that moment something else begins.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**في تلك اللحظة** (*fī tilka al-laḥẓa*) is "in that moment": *tilka* is "that" for a feminine noun, and *laḥẓa* is built on the native Arabic root *l-ḥ-ẓ*, "to glance" (Lane). A moment is as long as a glance.
