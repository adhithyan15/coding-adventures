Lesson 2 of 9.
小 (xiǎo) — centre, left, right.

Before we start: this one needs your hands, so it is not a driving lesson. You will want a pen and something to write on and your eyes, for letter shapes on the page. You can listen to everything else now — leave the section called Script — the shape until you have stopped, and I will say so again when we reach it.

Warm-up.
[pause 8 seconds]
Say small in Mandarin.

Script — the shape.
Draw the centre vertical first and finish its hook without lifting. Add the short left-falling mark. Finish with the right dot, leaving air around the centre. Three strokes; two lifts.

Guided Practice.
Trace 小 (xiǎo) once. Copy it once while saying centre, left, right.

Wrap-up Recall.

Lesson 9 of 9.
Payoff — three primary-school words in four skills.

Warm-up.
[pause 16 seconds]
Write 小 (xiǎo), 学, and 生 without a model.

Guided Practice.
Listening: hear the three forms in mixed order and choose each meaning.
Speaking: produce all three from meaning cards, with tones audible.
Reading: read all three character cards without pinyin.
Writing: hear all three and write them without a model.
Pass each skill separately. Repair and retry only the failed skill.
