## You'll want to know: けっていする
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C357-KEIYAKU357-05]; assesses=[] -->

**けっていする** — *kettei suru* — "to decide officially".

> **みんなが さんせいしたら、けっていします。** — *minna ga sansei shitara, kettei shimasu* — "If everyone agrees, it will be decided."

Polite **けっていします**, *ketteishimasu*; a する verb. Past (polite): **かいしゃが けっていしました。**, *kaisha ga kettei shimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**けっていする** (*kettei suru*) is built from a Sino-Japanese noun and the native verb *suru*, "to do". The noun, *kettei*, is two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese: *ketsu*, "decide" (Middle Chinese *kwet*) and *tei*, "determine" (*dengH*) (KANJIDIC2; JMdict; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription). The two join as *ket-tei*.
