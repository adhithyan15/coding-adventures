## You'll want to know: あぶら
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C175-COOK175-05]; assesses=[] -->

**あぶら** — *abura* — "oil; fat".

> **あぶらで にくを やきます。** — *abura de niku o yakimasu* — "I cook the meat in oil."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**あぶら** (*abura*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
