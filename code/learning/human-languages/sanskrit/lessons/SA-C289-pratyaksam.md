## You'll want to know: प्रत्यक्षम्
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C289-SAKSHYA289-05]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रत्यक्षम्** — *pratyakṣam* — "before one's eyes; in person".

> अहं प्रत्यक्षं दृष्टवान्।
>
> *ahaṃ pratyakṣaṃ dṛṣṭavān.*
>
> I saw it with my own eyes.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रत्यक्षम्** (*pratyakṣam*) is built from *prati*, "before, facing", and *akṣa*, "the eye"; MW gives *pratyakṣa* as present before the eyes, visible (MW).
