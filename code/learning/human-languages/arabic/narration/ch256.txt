Arabic, chapter 256: To fail, To pass, To explain, To translate, To give back.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
رسب (rasaba) — to fail (an exam).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to get better, then the Arabic for to examine.

You'll want to know: رسب.
رسب — rasaba — "to fail (an exam)".

The word, taken apart.
رسب (rasaba) is built on the native Arabic root r-s-b, "to sink" (Lane). Rasaba is "to sink to the bottom", hence "to fail".

Lesson 4 of 5.
ترجم (tarjama) — to translate.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to pass, then the Arabic for to explain.

You'll want to know: ترجم.
ترجم — tarjama — "to translate".

The word, taken apart.
ترجم (tarjama) is the four-letter verb that Lane gives with tarjumān, "interpreter". Tarjumān is usually traced to Aramaic targmānā; English dragoman comes from it.
