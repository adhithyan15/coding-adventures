## You'll want to know: मींडको
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C237-FIELD237-05]; assesses=[] -->

**मींडको** — *mī̃ḍko* — "a frog".

> बरसात मांय मींडको बोलै है।
>
> *barsāt mā̃y mī̃ḍko bolai hai.*
>
> In the rain the frog croaks.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**मींडको** (*mī̃ḍko*) is inherited from Sanskrit *maṇḍūka*, "a frog"; CDIAL lists Old Marwari *mīḍako*, "a frog" (CDIAL 9746).
