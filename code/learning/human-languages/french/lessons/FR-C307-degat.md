## You'll want to know: le dégât
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C307-DEGATS-01]; assesses=[] -->

**le dégât** — "the damage".

> L'orage a fait beaucoup de dégâts.
>
> *The storm did a lot of damage.*

Usually plural: *les dégâts*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*dégât* is built on Old French *degaster* "to lay waste", usually traced to Latin *dēvastāre* (EtymDB lists no parent), built on *vastus* "empty, waste" (Lewis & Short).
