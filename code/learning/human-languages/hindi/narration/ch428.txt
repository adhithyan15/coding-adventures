Hindi, chapter 428: Earning and Spending.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
कमाना (kamānā) — to earn.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a broker, then the Hindi for to pay off.

You'll want to know: कमाना.
कमाना — kamānā — "to earn".
मेरा भाई दुकान से अच्छा पैसा कमाता है। merā bhāī dukān se acchā paisā kamātā hai. My brother earns good money from his shop.
Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने बहुत पैसा कमाया, usne bahut paisā kamāyā.

The word, taken apart.
कमाना (kamānā) goes back to karmāpayati, "works, earns", a form CDIAL reconstructs (CDIAL 2897), built on karman, "work". कमाई, earnings, is its noun.

Lesson 2 of 5.
फूँकना (phū̃knā) — to blow (on); to squander.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for to pay off, then the Hindi for to earn.

You'll want to know: फूँकना.
फूँकना — phū̃knā — "to blow (on); to squander".
दादी ने फूँककर दीया बुझाया। dādī ne phū̃kkar dīyā bujhāyā. Grandmother blew out the lamp.
Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने अपना पैसा फूँक दिया, usne apnā paisā phū̃k diyā.
पैसा फूँकना (phū̃knā), to blow your money.

The word, taken apart.
फूँकना (phū̃knā) goes back to phūtka, "blowing", a form CDIAL reconstructs (CDIAL 9102), which lists Hindi phū̃knā, "to blow". Money that is blown on is gone like smoke.

Lesson 3 of 5.
मुनाफ़ा (munāfā) — a profit (in trade).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for to earn, then the Hindi for to blow.

You'll want to know: मुनाफ़ा.
मुनाफ़ा — munāfā — "a profit (in trade)".
इस साल दुकान में अच्छा मुनाफ़ा हुआ। is sāl dukān meṁ acchā munāfā huā. The shop made a good profit this year.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
मुनाफ़ा (munāfā) is usually traced to Arabic manāfiʿ, "profits, uses", through Persian: what a trader gains over the cost.

Lesson 5 of 5.
वसूलना (vasūlnā) — to collect (money owed); to recover (a debt).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a profit (in trade), then the Hindi for a budget.

You'll want to know: वसूलना.
वसूलना — vasūlnā — "to collect (money owed); to recover (a debt)".
दुकानदार हर हफ़्ते अपना उधार वसूलता है। dukāndār har hafte apnā udhār vasūltā hai. The shopkeeper collects what he is owed every week.
Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने अपना क़र्ज़ वसूला, usne apnā qarz vasūlā.

The word, taken apart.
वसूलना (vasūlnā) is a Hindi verb built on vasūl, "received, recovered", which is usually traced to Arabic wuṣūl, "arrival, receipt", through Persian.
