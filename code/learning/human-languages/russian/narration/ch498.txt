Russian, chapter 498: Friends and Classmates.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
дружить (druzhít) — to be friends.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to be naughty, then the Russian for disobedient.

You'll want to know: дружить.
дружить (druzhít) — "to be friends".
Мы дружим с детства. My drúzhim s détstva. We've been friends since childhood.
Imperfective; present дружу, дружишь. Дружить с кем.

The word, taken apart.
дружить (druzhít) is built on друг 'friend', which is inherited from Proto-Slavic drugъ 'friend' (EtymDB).

Lesson 3 of 5.
приятель (priyátel) — a pal, a friend.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to be friends, then the Russian for friendship.

You'll want to know: приятель.
приятель (priyátel) — "a pal, a friend".
Вчера я встретил старого приятеля. Vcherá ya vstrétil stárogo priyátelya. Yesterday I ran into an old friend.
Masculine; feminine приятельница. Less close than друг.

The word, taken apart.
приятель (priyátel) is inherited from Old East Slavic prijatelь, from Proto-Slavic prijateľь, derived from prijati 'to please, to favour' (EtymDB).

Lesson 5 of 5.
одноклассник (odnoklássnik) — a classmate.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a pal, then the Russian for someone of the same age.

You'll want to know: одноклассник.
одноклассник (odnoklássnik) — "a classmate".
В субботу я ходил на встречу с одноклассниками. V subbótu ya khodíl na vstréchu s odnoklássnikami. On Saturday I went to a reunion with my classmates.
Masculine; feminine одноклассница. At university: однокурсник.

The word, taken apart.
одноклассник (odnoklássnik) is a compound of один 'one' and класс 'class', which EtymDB traces to Latin classis, with -ник; EtymDB has no entry for одноклассник (odnoklássnik).
