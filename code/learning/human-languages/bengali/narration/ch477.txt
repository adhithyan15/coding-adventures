Bengali, chapter 477: Burns and Aches.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
যন্ত্রণা (jôntrônā) — agony; torment; severe pain.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for to pant, then the Bengali for to groan.

You'll want to know: যন্ত্রণা.
যন্ত্রণা — jôntrônā — "agony; torment; severe pain".
দাঁতের যন্ত্রণায় দাদু সারারাত জেগে ছিলেন। dā̃ter jôntrônāy dādu sārārāt jege chhilen. Grandfather lay awake all night in agony from his tooth.
ব্যথা (taught) is any pain; যন্ত্রণা (jôntrônā) is pain that torments, of the body or of the mind.

The word, taken apart.
যন্ত্রণা (jôntrônā) is a tatsama, Sanskrit yantraṇā, which Monier-Williams gives first for restraint and constraint and then for pain and anguish; Bengali keeps the pain.

গরম তেলে মীরার হাত পুড়ল। gôrôm tele mīrār hāt puṛlô. Mira's hand got burnt by hot oil.
Past (he/she …): সেই রুটি পুড়ল, sei ruṭi puṛlô.

The word, taken apart.
পোড়া (poṛā) is listed by CDIAL for Bengali, as burning and being burnt, under a reconstructed form (CDIAL 8397); পোড়ানো (taught), to burn something, is its causative.

Lesson 3 of 6.
টনটন করা (ṭônṭôn kôrā) — to throb; to ache dully.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for agony, then the Bengali for to get burnt.

You'll want to know: টনটন করা.
টনটন করা — ṭônṭôn kôrā — "to throb; to ache dully".
দাঁতটা টনটন করছে। dā̃tṭā ṭônṭôn kôrchhe. My tooth is throbbing.
Past (he/she …): সেই রোগীর মাথা টনটন করল, sei rogīr māthā ṭônṭôn kôrlô.

The word, taken apart.
টনটন করা (ṭônṭôn kôrā) is ṭônṭôn, an imitative word for a dull, throbbing ache, with kôrā, "to do" (CDIAL 2814).

Lesson 5 of 6.
গোঙানো (gongāno) — to moan.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for to throb, then the Bengali for to limp.

You'll want to know: গোঙানো.
গোঙানো — gongāno — "to moan".
ব্যথায় বুড়ো লোকটা গোঙাচ্ছিল। bæthāy buṛo lokṭā gongāchchhilô. The old man was moaning with pain.
Past (he/she …): তিনি ব্যথায় গোঙালেন, tini bæthāy gongālen.

The word, taken apart.
গোঙানো (gongāno) is an imitative verb for a low, wordless moan; it shows the new letter ঙ. No dictionary we use records an older form, so its history is not settled.
