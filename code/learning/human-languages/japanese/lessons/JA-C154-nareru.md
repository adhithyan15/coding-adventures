## You'll want to know: なれる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C154-HABIT154-05]; assesses=[] -->

**なれる** — *nareru* — "to get used to".

> **こどもは がっこうに なれます。** — *kodomo wa gakkō ni naremasu* — "Children get used to school."

Polite **なれます**, *naremasu*; an ichidan verb.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**なれる** (*nareru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi, and its older origin is not settled (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
