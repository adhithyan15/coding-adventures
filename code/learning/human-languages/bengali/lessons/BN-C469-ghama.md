## You'll want to know: ঘামা
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C469-GHAM469-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ঘামা** — *ghāmā* — "to sweat".

> গরমে বাসে বসে আমি ঘামছিলাম।
>
> *gôrôme bāse bôse āmi ghāmchhilām.*
>
> Sitting on the bus in the heat, I was sweating.

Past (he/she …): তিনি রোদে ঘামলেন, *tini rode ghāmlen*.

The noun is ঘাম, sweat: ঘামে ভেজা জামা, a shirt wet with sweat.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ঘামা** (*ghāmā*) is the verb of *ghām*, "sweat", which CDIAL lists for Bengali under Sanskrit *gharma*, "heat" (CDIAL 4445).
