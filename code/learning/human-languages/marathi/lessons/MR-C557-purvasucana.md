## You'll want to know: पूर्वसूचना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C557-KUVAT557-04]; assesses=[] -->

**पूर्वसूचना** — *pūrvasūcanā* — "advance notice".

> कोणतीही पूर्वसूचना न देता वीज गेली.
>
> *koṇtīhī pūrvasūcanā na detā vīj gelī.*
>
> The power went off without any advance notice.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पूर्वसूचना** (*pūrvasūcanā*) is a tatsama compound of Sanskrit *pūrva*, "before", and *sūcanā*, "indication" (Monier-Williams).
