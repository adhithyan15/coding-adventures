## You'll want to know: qualora
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C373-PURCHE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**qualora** — "if, in case (formal)".

> Qualora non foste soddisfatti, vi restituiamo i soldi.
>
> *Should you not be satisfied, we will give you your money back.*

Formal and written, followed by the subjunctive. In speech say *se*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*qualora* is built from *quale* "which", from Latin *quālis*, and *ora* "hour", from Latin *hōra* (Lewis & Short): "at whatever hour", and so "if ever".
