## You'll want to know: زبردستی
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C539-MUKHTAR539-01]; assesses=[] -->

**زبردستی** — *zabardastī* — "force (making someone do something)".

> اگر وہ نہیں چاہتا تو زبردستی ٹھیک نہیں۔
>
> *agar voh nahīṅ chāhtā to zabardastī ṭhīk nahīṅ.*
>
> If he doesn't want to, forcing him isn't right.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**زبردستی** (*zabardastī*) is built on *zabardast*, "having the upper hand", usually traced to Persian *zabardast*, from *zabar*, "above", and *dast*, "a hand", with the noun ending *-ī*.
