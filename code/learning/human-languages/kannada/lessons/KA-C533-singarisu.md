## You'll want to know: ಸಿಂಗರಿಸು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C533-KASUTI533-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸಿಂಗರಿಸು** — *siṅgarisu* — "to adorn; to dress up".

> ಹಬ್ಬಕ್ಕೆ ಮನೆಯನ್ನು ಹೂವುಗಳಿಂದ ಸಿಂಗರಿಸಿದರು.
>
> *habbakke maneyannu hūvugaḷinda siṅgarisidaru.*
>
> They decorated the house with flowers for the festival.

Past (I …): ಸಿಂಗರಿಸಿದೆ, *siṅgariside*.

Close to ಅಲಂಕರಿಸು; ಸಿಂಗಾರ is finery.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸಿಂಗರಿಸು** (*siṅgarisu*) is built on *siṅgāra*, "finery", a Kannada form of Sanskrit *śṛṅgāra*, which Monier-Williams glosses as love and also "elegant dress, finery" (Monier-Williams).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
