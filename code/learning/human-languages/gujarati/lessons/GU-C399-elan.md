## You'll want to know: એલાન
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C399-DELAY399-03]; assesses=[] -->

**એલાન** — *elān* — "a public announcement".

> સ્ટેશન પર એલાન સંભળાયું કે ટ્રેન મોડી છે.
>
> *sṭeśan par elān sambhḷāyũ ke ṭren moḍī che.*
>
> An announcement at the station said the train was late.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**એલાન** (*elān*) is usually traced to Arabic *iʿlān*, "a declaration, a making known", which reached Gujarati through Persian.
