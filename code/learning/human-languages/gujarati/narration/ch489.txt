Gujarati, chapter 489: Asking After Someone.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
કુશળ (kuśaḷ) — faring well; safe.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to play with a child, then the Gujarati for to give someone a drink.

You'll want to know: કુશળ.
કુશળ — kuśaḷ — "faring well; safe".
ઘરમાં બધા કુશળ છે કે નહીં? gharmā̃ badhā kuśaḷ che ke nahī̃? Is everyone at home well?

The word, taken apart.
કુશળ (kuśaḷ) is a tatsama, Sanskrit kuśala, "right, proper" and "well, healthy", as Monier-Williams records it.

Lesson 2 of 5.
શાતા (śātā) — ease; relief from pain.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to give someone a drink, then the Gujarati for faring well.

You'll want to know: શાતા.
શાતા — śātā — "ease; relief from pain".
મીરાએ ફોન કરીને દાદીની શાતા પૂછી. Mīrāe phon karīne dādīnī śātā pūchī. Mira phoned and asked how her grandmother was feeling.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
શાતા (śātā) probably goes back to Sanskrit śāta, "joy, pleasure, happiness", which Monier-Williams records from the old word lists; Gujarati uses it for ease after pain, and શાતા પૂછવી is to ask after someone who is ill.

Lesson 3 of 5.
દુઆ (duā) — a prayer for someone; a blessing.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for faring well, then the Gujarati for relief from pain.

You'll want to know: દુઆ.
દુઆ — duā — "a prayer for someone; a blessing".
દાદીની દુઆથી મારો તાવ મટી ગયો. dādīnī duāthī māro tāv maṭī gayo. Thanks to my grandmother's prayers my fever went away.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
દુઆ (duā) is usually traced to Arabic duʿā, "a prayer, a call", which reached Gujarati through Persian.

Lesson 4 of 5.
સંભારવું (sambhārvũ) — to recall; to remember (someone) fondly.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for relief from pain, then the Gujarati for a prayer for someone.

You'll want to know: સંભારવું.
સંભારવું — sambhārvũ — "to recall; to remember (someone) fondly".
દાદા રોજ જૂના મિત્રોને સંભારે છે. dādā roj jūnā mitrone sambhāre che. Every day my grandfather remembers his old friends.
Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે જૂના દિવસો સંભાર્યા, teṇe jūnā divso sambhāryā.

The word, taken apart.
સંભારવું (sambhārvũ) is inherited from Sanskrit saṃsmārayati, "causes to remember"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb, "to recollect" (CDIAL 13059).

Lesson 5 of 5.
હમદર્દી (hamdardī) — sympathy.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a prayer for someone, then the Gujarati for to recall.

You'll want to know: હમદર્દી.
હમદર્દી — hamdardī — "sympathy".
પડોશીઓએ અમારા દુઃખમાં હમદર્દી બતાવી. paḍośīoe amārā duḥkhmā̃ hamdardī batāvī. The neighbours showed sympathy in our sorrow.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
હમદર્દી (hamdardī) is usually traced to Persian hamdardī, "sharing pain", built from ham, "together", and dard, "pain".
