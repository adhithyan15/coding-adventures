German, chapter 88: Bee, Ant, Elephant, To fetch, To wish.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
die Biene — a bee.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for a lion, then the German for a monkey.

You'll want to know: die Biene.
die Biene — "a bee".

The word, taken apart.
Biene from Old High German bini from Proto-Germanic biniz, "bee" — inherited, a cousin of English bee. (EtymDB 2.1).

Lesson 2 of 5.
die Ameise — an ant.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for a monkey, then the German for a bee.

You'll want to know: die Ameise.
die Ameise — "an ant".

The word, taken apart.
Ameise from Old High German āmeiza from Proto-Germanic ēmaitijōn, built from ē-, "off", and maitaną, "to cut": the one that cuts things off. (EtymDB 2.1) English emmet, an old word for "ant", is the same word.
