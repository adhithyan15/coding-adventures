Arabic, chapter 496: Excited and relieved.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
متحمس (mutaḥammis) — excited, keen.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to put up with, then the Arabic for to be fed up.

You'll want to know: متحمس.
متحمس — mutaḥammis — "excited, keen".
ابني متحمس للرحلة إلى البحر. ibnī mutaḥammis li-r-riḥla ilā al-baḥr. My son is excited about the trip to the sea.
It takes لـ for what you look forward to.

The word, taken apart.
متحمس (mutaḥammis) is built on the native Arabic root ḥ-m-s, "to be hard, firm, vehement" (Lane). Ḥamās, enthusiasm, which you know, is from the same root.

What you've built.
One more word for describing what you lived and felt.

The word, taken apart.
تحمس (taḥammasa) is built on the native Arabic root ḥ-m-s, "to be hard, firm, vehement" (Lane). Form V taḥammasa is to grow keen.

Lesson 3 of 5.
ارتياح (irtiyāḥ) — relief.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for excited, then the Arabic for to get excited.

You'll want to know: ارتياح.
ارتياح — irtiyāḥ — "relief".
كان الارتياح واضحا على وجهها. kāna al-irtiyāḥ wāḍiḥan ʿalā wajhihā. Relief was clear on her face.

The word, taken apart.
ارتياح (irtiyāḥ) is built on the native Arabic root r-w-ḥ, "wind, breath, rest" (Lane). Irtiyāḥ is the verbal noun of Form VIII: breathing easy again.

The word, taken apart.
ارتاح (irtāḥa) is built on the native Arabic root r-w-ḥ, "wind, breath, rest" (Lane). It is Form VIII of the root.
