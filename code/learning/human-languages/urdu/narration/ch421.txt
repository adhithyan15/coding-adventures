Urdu, chapter 421: Papers, Cards and Fees.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
کاغذ (kāghaz) — paper; a document.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for to share out, then the Urdu for to cash (a cheque).

You'll want to know: کاغذ.
کاغذ — kāghaz — "paper; a document".
بینک والے نے کچھ کاغذ مانگے۔ baink vāle ne kuchh kāghaz māṅge. The man at the bank asked for some papers.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
کاغذ (kāghaz) is usually traced to Persian kāghaz, "paper"; the word probably reached Persian from further east, along with paper itself.

What you've built.
One more word for getting around.
