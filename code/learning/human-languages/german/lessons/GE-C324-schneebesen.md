## You'll want to know: der Schneebesen
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C324-RUEHREN-05]; assesses=[] -->

**der Schneebesen** — "the whisk".

> Mit dem Schneebesen schlug er die Sahne steif.
>
> *He whipped the cream stiff with the whisk.*

Plural: *die Schneebesen*. *Schnee* 'snow' plus *Besen* 'broom': beaten egg white looks like snow.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Schneebesen* joins *Schnee* "snow" and *Besen* "broom", Old High German *besamo*, Proto-Germanic *besamô* (EtymDB).
