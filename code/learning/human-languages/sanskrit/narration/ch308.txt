Sanskrit, chapter 308: Lending a Hand.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
समूहः (samūhaḥ) — a group; a crowd.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for orphaned, then the Sanskrit for safety.

You'll want to know: समूहः.
समूहः — samūhaḥ — "a group; a crowd".
एकः समूहः शिबिरे साहाय्यं करोति। ekaḥ samūhaḥ śibire sāhāyyaṃ karoti. A group helps out in the camp.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
समूहः (samūhaḥ) is built from sam, "together", and the root √ūh, "to move, push"; MW gives samūha as a collection, assemblage, multitude (MW).

सः दरिद्रस्य उपकरोति। saḥ daridrasya upakaroti. He does a poor man a kindness.
Past (he …): सः दरिद्रस्य उपकृतवान्, saḥ daridrasya upakṛtavān.

The word, taken apart.
उपकरोति (upakaroti) is built from upa, "near, towards", and the root √kṛ, "to do"; MW gives upa-kṛ as to assist, help, favour (MW).

Lesson 3 of 5.
विभजति (vibhajati) — divides; shares out.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a group, then the Sanskrit for does a kindness.

You'll want to know: विभजति.
विभजति — vibhajati — "divides; shares out".
ते अन्नं सर्वेषु विभजन्ति। te annaṃ sarveṣu vibhajanti. They share the food out among everyone.
Past (he …): सः अन्नं विभक्तवान्, saḥ annaṃ vibhaktavān.

The word, taken apart.
विभजति (vibhajati) is built from vi, "apart", and the root √bhaj, "to share", as in bhajati; MW gives vi-bhaj as to divide, distribute, apportion (MW).

Lesson 4 of 5.

[pause 2 seconds]

दाता — dātā — "a giver; a donor".
दाता शिबिराय धनं ददाति। dātā śibirāya dhanaṃ dadāti. The donor gives money to the camp.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
दाता (dātā) comes from the root √dā, "to give", with the ending -tṛ of the one who does it; MW gives dātṛ as a giver, donor (MW).

Lesson 5 of 5.
सहायः (sahāyaḥ) — a helper; a companion.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for shares out, then the Sanskrit for a donor.

You'll want to know: सहायः.
सहायः — sahāyaḥ — "a helper; a companion".
अरुणः वैद्यस्य सहायः अस्ति। aruṇaḥ vaidyasya sahāyaḥ asti. Arun is the doctor's helper.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
सहायः (sahāyaḥ) MW thinks it is probably built from saha, "with", and aya, "going", one who goes along with another, though some take it as a Prakrit form, so its origin is not settled (MW).
