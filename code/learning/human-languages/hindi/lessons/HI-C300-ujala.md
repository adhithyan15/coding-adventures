## You'll want to know: उजाला
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C300-DAHAK300-04]; assesses=[] -->

**उजाला** — *ujālā* — "light; brightness".

> आग का उजाला पूरे गाँव में फैल गया।
>
> *āg kā ujālā pūre gā̃v meṁ phail gayā.*
>
> The glow of the fire spread over the whole village.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**उजाला** (*ujālā*) is inherited from *\*ujjvālaka*, "bright", a form CDIAL reconstructs (CDIAL 1673). It is light that lets you see, the opposite of darkness: **सुबह का उजाला**, the light of morning.
