## You'll want to know: شکار
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C471-SHEKAR-01]; assesses=[] -->

**شکار** (*shekâr*) — "hunting; prey, game".

> شکار در این منطقه ممنوع است.
>
> *Shekâr dar in mantaqe mamnu' ast.*
>
> Hunting is forbidden in this area.

A hunter is a *shekârchi*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*shekâr* is a native word, which EtymDB lists with the senses 'hunting, game'; it is usually traced to Middle Persian *škār*.
