## You'll want to know: ఎగబడు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C379-BADHITUDU379-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ఎగబడు** — *egabaḍu* — "to throng; to crowd in".

> సినిమా టిక్కెట్ల కోసం జనం ఎగబడ్డారు.
>
> *sinimā ṭikkeṭla kōsaṁ janaṁ egabaḍḍāru.*
>
> People thronged for film tickets.

Past (I …): ఎగబడ్డాను, *egabaḍḍānu*.

It shows eagerness: everyone pushing to get there first.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ఎగబడు** (*egabaḍu*) is a Telugu verb whose origin is not settled.
