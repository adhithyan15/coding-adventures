## You'll want to know: こんかい
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C346-KONDO346-02]; assesses=[] -->

**こんかい** — *konkai* — "this time; on this occasion".

> **こんかい まけたら、つぎは がんばります。** — *konkai maketara, tsugi wa ganbarimasu* — "If we lose this time, we will try harder next time."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**こんかい** (*konkai*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *kon*, "now" (Middle Chinese *kim*) and *kai*, "-times" (*hwoj*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
