# ટ — retroflex tta

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-SCRIPT-UU-MATRA-01, GU-SCRIPT-TA-01] -->

Read **મૂ** once and point to its long-*ū* sign. Then write dental **ત** once
and say *ta* with the tongue at the teeth. Curl the tongue back for retroflex
*tta*, then put the old shapes away.

## Script — meet one form
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-SCRIPT-TTA-01]; assesses=[] -->

Keep **ટ** visible. Sweep across the rounded upper turn, bend diagonally down
through the middle, then circle the broad lower bowl and finish on its right
side without lifting.

## Writing — notice and trace
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-SCRIPT-TTA-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: observe-trace -->

Finger-trace **ટ** once, then air-trace it once. Notice upper turn, diagonal
middle, lower bowl; no pen lift.

## Writing — copy with the model
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-SCRIPT-TTA-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: guided-copy -->

Copy visible **ટ** once. Compare only the diagonal middle and lower bowl;
repair one landmark if needed.

## Writing — recall after hiding
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-SCRIPT-TTA-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: delayed-copy -->
<!-- hl-activity: {"id":"GU-W07-tta-recall","kind":"text","assesses":["GU-SCRIPT-TTA-01"],"prompt":"Hide the model and write retroflex tta from memory.","answer":"ટ","accepted":[],"feedback":{"correct":"ટ — the upper turn, diagonal, and lower bowl are intact.","incorrect":"Look once at ટ, hide it, and repair only the differing landmark."},"response_seconds":20} -->

Hide the model and write retroflex *tta* once. Uncover **ટ**, compare, and stop
after one repair.

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-SCRIPT-TTA-01, GU-SCRIPT-TA-01] -->

[PAUSE 3s] Read **ત ટ** aloud and keep dental *ta* apart from retroflex *tta*.
