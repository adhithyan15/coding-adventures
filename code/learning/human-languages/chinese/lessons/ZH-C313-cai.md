## You'll want to know: 才
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C313-MOVE313-02]; assesses=[] -->

**才** — *cái* — "only then; not until".

> **他十点才来** *tā shí diǎn cái lái* — He didn't come until ten.

The new character, in pieces: 才 — three strokes: a bar, a vertical that hooks to the left at the foot, and a long sweep down to the left across them: the hand piece of 打 with a sweep for its last stroke. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**才** (*cái*) is inherited from Middle Chinese *dzoj* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses it 'barely' under another, more complex spelling; the simple form, read the same, is a different word there, glossed 'use', 'strength' and 'talent'.

## What you've built

One more word for telling what happened.
