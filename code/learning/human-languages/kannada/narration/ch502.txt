Kannada, chapter 502: Good Manners.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಸಭ್ಯ (sabhya) — polite; decent.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for harshly frank, then the Kannada for to mimic mockingly.

You'll want to know: ಸಭ್ಯ.
ಸಭ್ಯ — sabhya — "polite; decent".
ಅವನು ಸಭ್ಯ ಹುಡುಗ, ಎಲ್ಲರ ಜೊತೆ ಗೌರವದಿಂದ ಮಾತಾಡುತ್ತಾನೆ. avanu sabhya huḍuga, ellara jote gauravadinda mātāḍuttāne. He is a polite boy; he speaks respectfully with everyone.
ಸಭೆ is an assembly: ಸಭ್ಯ (sabhya), fit to be in company.

The word, taken apart.
ಸಭ್ಯ (sabhya) is borrowed from Sanskrit sabhya, "being in an assembly", and so "polite" (CDIAL 13171).

Lesson 2 of 5.
ವಿಧೇಯ (vidhēya) — obedient; dutiful.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to mimic mockingly, then the Kannada for polite.

You'll want to know: ವಿಧೇಯ.
ವಿಧೇಯ — vidhēya — "obedient; dutiful".
ಆ ವಿಧೇಯ ಮಗ ಅಪ್ಪನ ಮಾತು ಕೇಳುತ್ತಾನೆ. ā vidhēya maga appana mātu kēḷuttāne. That dutiful son listens to his father.
Formal letters end with ತಮ್ಮ ವಿಧೇಯ (vidhēya), yours faithfully.

The word, taken apart.
ವಿಧೇಯ (vidhēya) is borrowed from Sanskrit vidheya, "to be done, to be governed", and so "docile, obedient" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 3 of 5.
ಶಿಸ್ತು (śistu) — discipline.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for polite, then the Kannada for obedient.

You'll want to know: ಶಿಸ್ತು.
ಶಿಸ್ತು — śistu — "discipline".
ಶಾಲೆಯಲ್ಲಿ ಶಿಸ್ತು ತುಂಬಾ ಮುಖ್ಯ. śāleyalli śistu tumbā mukhya. Discipline is very important at school.
ಶಿಸ್ತಾಗಿ, neatly, in good order.

The word, taken apart.
ಶಿಸ್ತು (śistu) is an everyday Kannada word whose origin is not settled; it is often compared with Sanskrit śiṣṭa, "taught, disciplined" (CDIAL 12479).

Lesson 4 of 5.
ಗಮನಿಸು (gamanisu) — to take note; to notice.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for obedient, then the Kannada for discipline.

You'll want to know: ಗಮನಿಸು.
ಗಮನಿಸು — gamanisu — "to take note; to notice".
ನಾನು ಅವಳ ಹೊಸ ಕನ್ನಡಕವನ್ನು ಗಮನಿಸಿದೆ. nānu avaḷa hosa kannaḍakavannu gamaniside. I noticed her new glasses.
Past (I …): ಗಮನಿಸಿದೆ, gamaniside.
ಗಮನ is attention: ಗಮನ ಕೊಡು, to pay attention.

The word, taken apart.
ಗಮನಿಸು (gamanisu) is built on Sanskrit gamana, "going" (CDIAL 4027), which in Kannada came to mean "attention", with the verb ending -isu.

ನನ್ನ ಪತ್ರಕ್ಕೆ ಕಚೇರಿ ಬೇಗ ಸ್ಪಂದಿಸಿತು. nanna patrakke kacēri bēga spandisitu. The office responded quickly to my letter.

The word, taken apart.
ಸ್ಪಂದಿಸು (spandisu) is built on Sanskrit spanda, "a tremor, throbbing" (CDIAL 13805), with the verb ending -isu.
