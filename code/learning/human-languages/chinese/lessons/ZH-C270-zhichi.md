## You'll want to know: zhīchí
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C270-AGREE270-04]; assesses=[] -->

*zhīchí* — "to support; to back".

> *wǒ zhīchí nǐ* — I'm behind you.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*zhīchí* is built from *zhī* and *chí* 'to hold', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *tsye* and *dri* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first with this very word and the second 'to hold'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying no and asking.
