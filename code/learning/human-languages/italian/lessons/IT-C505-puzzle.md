## You'll want to know: il puzzle
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C505-COLLEZIONI-03]; assesses=[] -->

**il puzzle** — "jigsaw puzzle".

> Abbiamo fatto un puzzle di mille pezzi.
>
> *We did a thousand-piece jigsaw.*

Said as in English; the plural is *i puzzle*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*puzzle* was borrowed from English *puzzle* (EtymDB); the origin of the English word is not settled.
