## You'll want to know: あつまる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C237-PHOTO237-04]; assesses=[] -->

**あつまる** — *atsumaru* — "to gather; to come together".

> **しゅうまつに ともだちが うちに あつまりました。** — *shūmatsu ni tomodachi ga uchi ni atsumarimashita* — "At the weekend my friends gathered at my house."

Polite **あつまります**, *atsumarimasu*; a godan verb. Its partner **あつめる**, *atsumeru*, takes an object.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**あつまる** (*atsumaru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
