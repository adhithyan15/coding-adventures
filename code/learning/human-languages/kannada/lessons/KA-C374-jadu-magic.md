# ಜಾದೂ (jādū) — magic; a magic trick

## You'll want to know: ಜಾದೂ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C374-JADU374-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ಜಾದೂ** — *jādū* — "magic; a magic trick".

> ಜಾತ್ರೆಯಲ್ಲಿ ಒಬ್ಬ ಮನುಷ್ಯ ಮಕ್ಕಳಿಗೆ ಜಾದೂ ತೋರಿಸಿದನು.
>
> *jātreyalli obba manuṣya makkaḷige jādū tōrisidanu.*
>
> At the fair a man showed the children magic tricks.

A magician on stage is a ಜಾದೂಗಾರ.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಜಾದೂ** (*jādū*) is a loan from Persian *jādū*, "magic, sorcery", which came through Hindustani.
