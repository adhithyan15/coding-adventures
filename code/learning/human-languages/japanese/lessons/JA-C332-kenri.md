## You'll want to know: けんり
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C332-KENRI332-01]; assesses=[] -->

**けんり** — *kenri* — "a right".

> **こどもにも けんりが あると おもいます。** — *kodomo ni mo kenri ga aru to omoimasu* — "I think children have rights too."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**けんり** (*kenri*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *ken*, "rights" (Middle Chinese *gjwen*) and *ri*, "advantage" (*lijH*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
