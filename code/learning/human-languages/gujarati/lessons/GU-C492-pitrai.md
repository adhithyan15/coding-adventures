## You'll want to know: પિતરાઈ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C492-PIYAR492-05]; assesses=[] -->

**પિતરાઈ** — *pitrāī* — "a cousin (on the father's side)".

> મારો પિતરાઈ ભાઈ મારી સાથે ભણે છે.
>
> *māro pitrāī bhāī mārī sāthe bhaṇe che.*
>
> My cousin studies with me.

A masculine or feminine noun, by the person meant.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**પિતરાઈ** (*pitrāī*) goes back to a reconstructed *\*pitryajāta-*, "father's brother's son"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati word, "relating to father's brother" (CDIAL 8193).
