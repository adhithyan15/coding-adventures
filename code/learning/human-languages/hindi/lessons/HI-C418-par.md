## You'll want to know: पार
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C418-NAZDIK418-03]; assesses=[] -->

**पार** — *pār* — "across; the far side".

> हमने नाव से नदी पार की।
>
> *hamne nāv se nadī pār kī.*
>
> We crossed the river by boat.

पार करना, to cross; नदी के पार, across the river; उस पार, on the far side.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पार** (*pār*) is a tadbhava of Sanskrit *pāra*, "the further bank, the far end" (CDIAL 8100), which lists Hindi *pār*, "on the other side of, across".
