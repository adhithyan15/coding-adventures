Hindi, chapter 321: The Attack.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
हमला (hamlā) — an attack.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a crown, then the Hindi for a royal court.

You'll want to know: हमला.
हमला — hamlā — "an attack".
दुश्मन ने रात को क़िले पर हमला किया। duśman ne rāt ko qile par hamlā kiyā. The enemy attacked the fort at night.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
हमला (hamlā) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic ḥamla, "a charge, an onset". What is attacked takes पर: क़िले पर हमला.

Lesson 2 of 5.
घेरना (ghernā) — to surround; to lay siege to.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a royal court, then the Hindi for an attack.

You'll want to know: घेरना.
घेरना — ghernā — "to surround; to lay siege to".
दुश्मन की सेना ने क़िले को घेर लिया। duśman kī senā ne qile ko gher liyā. The enemy army laid siege to the fort.
Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने गाँव को घेरा, usne gā̃v ko gherā.

The word, taken apart.
घेरना (ghernā) is inherited from ghir, "go round", a form CDIAL reconstructs (CDIAL 4474): to go all the way round something and close it in.

Lesson 3 of 5.
तोप (top) — a cannon.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for an attack, then the Hindi for to surround.

You'll want to know: तोप.
तोप — top — "a cannon".
क़िले की दीवार पर चार तोपें थीं। qile kī dīvār par cār topeṁ thīṁ. There were four cannons on the fort wall.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
तोप (top) is usually traced, through Persian, to Turkish top, "a ball", and so a gun that fires balls. It is feminine in Hindi: तोपें थीं.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for to surround, then the Hindi for a cannon.

You'll want to know: भिड़ना.
भिड़ना — bhiṛnā — "to clash; to come to blows".
दोनों सेनाएँ नदी के किनारे भिड़ीं। donõ senāẽ nadī ke kināre bhiṛīṁ. The two armies clashed on the riverbank.
Past (he …): वह सिपाही भिड़ गया, vah sipāhī bhiṛ gayā.

The word, taken apart.
भिड़ना (bhiṛnā) is inherited from bhiṭ, a form CDIAL reconstructs for meeting and crowding together (CDIAL 9490): two sides that भिड़ते हैं come right up against each other.

Lesson 5 of 5.
जूझना (jūjhnā) — to fight hard; to struggle.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a cannon, then the Hindi for to clash.

You'll want to know: जूझना.
जूझना — jūjhnā — "to fight hard; to struggle".
सिपाही तीन दिन तक दुश्मन से जूझे। sipāhī tīn din tak duśman se jūjhe. The soldiers fought the enemy for three days.
Past (he …): वह सिपाही जूझा, vah sipāhī jūjhā.

The word, taken apart.
जूझना (jūjhnā) goes back to Sanskrit yudhyate, "fights" (CDIAL 10502), the family of युद्ध, war. It is a long, hard fight, against an enemy or against trouble.
