## You'll want to know: jì
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C118-SEND118-02]; assesses=[] -->

*jì* — "to post; to send by post".

> *wǒ qù yóujú jì bāoguǒ* — I'm going to the post office to send a parcel.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*jì* is inherited from Middle Chinese *kjeH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'to entrust to someone'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for everyday actions.
