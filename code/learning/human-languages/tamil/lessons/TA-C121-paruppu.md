**பருப்பு** — *paruppu* — "lentils, dal".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**பருப்பு** (*paruppu*) is native Dravidian. DEDR 3978 lists Malayalam *parippu*, Kannada *pappu* and Telugu *pappu*.
