## You'll want to know: yuánliàng
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C275-SORRY275-02]; assesses=[] -->

*yuánliàng* — "to forgive".

> *qǐng yuánliàng wǒ* — Please forgive me.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*yuánliàng* is built from *yuán* and *liàng* 'to trust', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *ngjwon* and *ljangH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'a broad plain' and the second 'to trust'. How the pair came to mean forgiving is not settled.
