German, chapter 280: Das Verbrechen.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
das Verbrechen — the crime.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to happen, then the German for the incident.

You'll want to know: das Verbrechen.
das Verbrechen — "the crime".
Das Verbrechen geschah in der Nacht. The crime happened at night.
Plural: die Verbrechen. For a serious crime; a lesser offence is ein Vergehen.

The word, taken apart.
Verbrechen is the old verb verbrechen "to do wrong" used as a noun, built on brechen "to break", Proto-Germanic brekaną (EtymDB): to break the law.

Täter is built on Tat "deed", from Old High German tāt, Proto-Germanic dēdiz (EtymDB), a cousin of English deed.

Lesson 4 of 5.
das Opfer — the victim; the sacrifice.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for the criminal, then the German for the offender.

You'll want to know: das Opfer.
das Opfer — "the victim; the sacrifice".
Das Opfer konnte den Mann gut beschreiben. The victim could describe the man well.
Plural: die Opfer. Neuter for anyone: Er war das Opfer.

The word, taken apart.
Opfer is inherited from Old High German opfar "offering" (EtymDB), which is usually traced to Church Latin operārī "to work, to make an offering".

Lesson 5 of 5.

You'll want to know: der Tatort.
der Tatort — "the crime scene".
Die Polizei kam schnell zum Tatort. The police came quickly to the crime scene.
Plural: die Tatorte. Tat 'deed' plus Ort 'place'.

The word, taken apart.
Tatort joins Tat "deed", Proto-Germanic dēdiz, and Ort "place", Old High German ort "point, place" (EtymDB): the place of the deed.
