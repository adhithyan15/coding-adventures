Italian, chapter 482: Il viso da vicino.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
il sopracciglio — eyebrow.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for heel, then the Italian for big toe.

You'll want to know: il sopracciglio.
il sopracciglio — "eyebrow".
Quando è sorpreso alza un sopracciglio. When he is surprised he raises an eyebrow.
An irregular plural: le sopracciglia.

The word, taken apart.
sopracciglio is inherited from Latin supercilium "eyebrow" (Lewis & Short), built on super "above" and cilium "eyelid".

You'll want to know: le ciglia.

La bambina ha le ciglia lunghe. The little girl has long eyelashes.
The singular il ciglio also means an edge: il ciglio della strada — the roadside.

The word, taken apart.
ciglia is inherited from Latin cilia, the plural of cilium "eyelid" (Lewis & Short).
