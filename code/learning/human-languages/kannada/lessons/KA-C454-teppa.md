## You'll want to know: ತೆಪ್ಪ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C454-TEPPA454-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ತೆಪ್ಪ** — *teppa* — "a coracle; a raft".

> ನಾವು ತೆಪ್ಪದಲ್ಲಿ ನದಿಯನ್ನು ದಾಟಿದೆವು.
>
> *nāvu teppadalli nadiyannu dāṭidevu.*
>
> We crossed the river in a coracle.

A round basket boat, still used for river crossings.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ತೆಪ್ಪ** (*teppa*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 3414 lists Kannada *teppa*, "a raft, a float".
