## You'll want to know: مثال زدن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C366-BAHS-05]; assesses=[] -->

**مثال زدن** (*mesâl zadan*) — "to give an example".

> معلم برای هر کلمه یک مثال زد.
>
> *mo'allem barâye har kalame yek mesâl zad.*
>
> The teacher gave an example for each word.

*Mesâl* "example" + *zadan* "to strike" (present stem *zan-*). *Masalan* "for example" is the adverb.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**مثال** is usually traced to Arabic *mithāl* "example, likeness"; *zadan* "to strike" is the inherited Persian verb that also builds *harf zadan* "to talk".
