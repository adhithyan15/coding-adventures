## You'll want to know: प्रतिपालयति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C535-SATYASANDHA535-01]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रतिपालयति** — *pratipālayati* — "keeps to; abides by".

> यदि सः नियमं प्रतिपालयति, तर्हि सर्वे तं विश्वसन्ति।
>
> *yadi saḥ niyamaṃ pratipālayati, tarhi sarve taṃ viśvasanti.*
>
> If he keeps the rule, everyone trusts him.

Past (he …): सः नियमं प्रतिपालितवान्, *saḥ niyamaṃ pratipālitavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रतिपालयति** (*pratipālayati*) is built on *prati* and the root √pāl, "to guard, to keep", which also gives *pālayati*; MW gives *pratipālayati* as to protect, to keep, to observe, and to wait for (MW).
