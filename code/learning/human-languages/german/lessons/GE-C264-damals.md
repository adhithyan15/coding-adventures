## You'll want to know: damals
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C264-DAMALS-01]; assesses=[] -->

**damals** — "back then, at that time".

> Damals hatten wir kein Handy.
>
> *Back then we had no mobile phone.*

For a time that is over. In a story it goes with the *Präteritum*: *Damals wohnte ich auf dem Land*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*damals* is usually traced to Middle High German *dō māles* "at that time", from *dō* "then" and *Mal* "time, occasion"; EtymDB lists no parent.
