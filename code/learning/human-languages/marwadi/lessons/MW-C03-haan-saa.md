# हां सा — a respectful yes

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MW-SCRIPT-RA-01, MW-SCRIPT-MA-01, MW-LEX-RAAM-RAAM-SAA, MW-SCRIPT-HA-01, MW-SCRIPT-AA-MATRA-01, MW-SCRIPT-ANUSVARA-01, MW-SCRIPT-SAA-01, MW-REGISTER-SAA] -->

[PAUSE 6s] Say the Chapter 1 greeting from memory. Write **हां**, then **सा**,
as two separate known pieces.

## The exchange — join the two pieces
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-SCRIPT-HAAN-01, MW-LEX-HAAN-01]; assesses=[] -->

> **हां सा।** — *Hā(n) sā.* — Yes, respectfully.

The first word answers yes. Familiar **सा** keeps the answer respectful. A
scholarly Marwari text edition identifies *hā(n)-sā* as the polite affirmative,
and a Rajasthan Sahitya Akademi dialogue writes **हां सा** in context.

## Writing — guided, then delayed
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MW-SCRIPT-HAAN-01, MW-LEX-HAAN-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: delayed-copy -->

1. copy **हां सा** once while looking
2. point to the space between the answer and the respect word
3. hide the phrase for ten seconds
4. write **हां सा** from memory
5. compare **हां**, the space, and **सा** separately

## Wrap-up recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MW-SCRIPT-HAAN-01, MW-LEX-HAAN-01] -->
