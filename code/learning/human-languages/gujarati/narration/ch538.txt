Gujarati, chapter 538: In Two Minds.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
વિમાસણ (vimāsaṇ) — a quandary.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to pick out, then the Gujarati for to appeal.

You'll want to know: વિમાસણ.
વિમાસણ — vimāsaṇ — "a quandary".
જો તમે વિમાસણમાં પડશો તો મને ફોન કરો. jo tame vimāsaṇmā̃ paḍśo to mane phon karo. If you find yourself in a quandary, phone me.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
વિમાસણ (vimāsaṇ) is being caught between choices and uneasy about what will come; one "falls" into it: વિમાસણમાં પડવું. CDIAL lists the Gujarati word, with the sense "fearful anticipation", under Sanskrit vimarśana, "investigation" (CDIAL 11820).

Lesson 2 of 5.
ઢચુપચુ (ḍhacupcu) — undecided (in two minds).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to appeal, then the Gujarati for a quandary.

You'll want to know: ઢચુપચુ.
ઢચુપચુ — ḍhacupcu — "undecided (in two minds)".
જો તમે ઢચુપચુ રહેશો તો કોઈ તમારો વિશ્વાસ નહીં કરે. jo tame ḍhacupcu raheśo to koī tamāro viśvās nahī̃ kare. If you stay undecided, nobody will trust you.

The word, taken apart.
ઢચુપચુ (ḍhacupcu) is an expressive, jingling word, its sound swinging like the mind it describes. No dictionary we use records an older form, so its history is not settled.

Lesson 4 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for undecided, then the Gujarati for inner turmoil.

You'll want to know: ઘોળવું.
ઘોળવું — ghoḷvũ — "to mull over".
જો તમે આ વાત બે દિવસ ઘોળશો તો તમને જવાબ મળશે. jo tame ā vāt be divas ghoḷśo to tamne javāb maḷśe. If you mull this over for two days, you'll find the answer.
Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે વાત ઘોળી, teṇe vāt ghoḷī.

The word, taken apart.
ઘોળવું (ghoḷvũ) is filed by CDIAL under Sanskrit ghōlayati, "stirs, mixes", and CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb, "to shake about; to ponder on" (CDIAL 4526): a thought stirred round and round.

Lesson 5 of 5.
નિવેડો (niveḍo) — a settlement (of a matter).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for inner turmoil, then the Gujarati for to mull over.

You'll want to know: નિવેડો.
નિવેડો — niveḍo — "a settlement (of a matter)".
જો આપણે બેસીને વાત કરીશું તો નિવેડો આવશે. jo āpaṇe besīne vāt karīśũ to niveḍo āvśe. If we sit down and talk, the matter will be settled.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
નિવેડો (niveḍo) is how a matter ends once it is decided; a settlement "comes": નિવેડો આવશે. CDIAL records the Gujarati noun, "a settlement, a decision", under a reconstructed nirvarati, "restrains" (CDIAL 7392).
