## You'll want to know: rēmigō, rēmigāre
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C437-RUDENS-05]; assesses=[] -->

**rēmigō, rēmigāre** — "to row".

> Ventus nūllus est; itaque nautae rēmigant.
>
> *There is no wind; so the sailors row.*

A first-conjugation verb: *rēmigō, rēmigāre*; the perfect is rare. The rower is *rēmex, rēmigis*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*rēmigō* is built from *rēmus* 'oar' and *agō* 'to drive' (EtymDB; Lewis & Short): to drive the oars.
