# 什 — both halves are old news

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ZH-SCRIPT-REN-RADICAL-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Write 亻, the standing person — two strokes, at the left edge of a block.

## Script — the shape
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-SCRIPT-SHEN]; assesses=[ZH-SCRIPT-REN-RADICAL-01, ZH-SCRIPT-ZAO] -->

> 什

**Four strokes**, and after 我 that will feel like a rest.

**什 is 亻 beside 十**, and you have met both:

- **亻** you wrote in the very first word of this book, standing at the left of 你
- **十** you drew inside 早, underneath the sun

1. **亻's falling stroke**, from the upper centre down to the left
2. **亻's vertical**, straight down to the baseline
3. **十's horizontal**, left to right
4. **十's vertical**, down *through* that horizontal

Three pen lifts.

**Finish 亻 before starting 十** — left before right, the oldest habit you have.
And inside 十, horizontal before vertical, because the two cross.

A note on 十, since this is the second time it has turned up without being taught.
It is the character for **ten**, and you still do not need to write it on its own.
When it eventually arrives as a character in its own right, you will already have
drawn it twice.
