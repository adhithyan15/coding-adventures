## You'll want to know: خمیر
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C357-KHAMIR-01]; assesses=[] -->

**خمیر** (*khamir*) — "dough".

> نانوا خمیر را روی میز پهن کرد.
>
> *Nânvâ khamir râ ru-ye miz pahn kard.*
>
> The baker spread the dough out on a table.

Also any paste. *Khamir-e nân* "bread dough".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*khamir* is borrowed from Arabic *khamīr*, "leavened dough" (EtymDB).
