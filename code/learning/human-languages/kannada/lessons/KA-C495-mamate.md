## You'll want to know: ಮಮತೆ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C495-MAMATE495-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ಮಮತೆ** — *mamate* — "motherly love; deep attachment".

> ಅಮ್ಮನ ಮಮತೆಗೆ ಬೆಲೆ ಕಟ್ಟಲು ಆಗುವುದಿಲ್ಲ.
>
> *ammana mamatege bele kaṭṭalu āguvudilla.*
>
> A mother's love cannot be priced.

From Sanskrit ಮಮ, mine: the feeling that someone is your very own.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಮಮತೆ** (*mamate*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *mamatā*, "the state of mine, a sense of ownership, attachment" (Monier-Williams), built on *mama*, "mine".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
