## Grammar Lens: …不见了, it has gone missing
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-GRAMMAR-PROBLEM-01]; assesses=[ZH-LEX-C58-THINGS58-01, ZH-LEX-C196-TICKET196-01, ZH-LEX-C151-KANDAO-01, ZH-LEX-C116-CARRY116-05, ZH-LEX-C116-CARRY116-01, ZH-LEX-C103-USE103-05, ZH-LEX-C64-HELP64-02, ZH-TONE-SANDHI-BU, ZH-GRAMMAR-LE-CHANGED-STATE-01] -->

> **Name the thing, then say 不见了: it has gone missing.**

> **我的手机不见了** *wǒ de shǒujī bú jiàn le* — My phone's gone missing.
> **我的车票不见了 你看到了吗** *wǒ de chēpiào bú jiàn le, nǐ kàndào le ma* — My ticket's disappeared. Have you seen it?

不见 is 'not seen', and 了 says that is new: it was there, and now it is not. 不 before the fall of jiàn is *bú*.

*diū le* says you lost it yourself: *wǒ de qiánbāo diū le*. *huài le* says it broke. *qǐng bāng wǒ*, please help me, asks for help.
