## You'll want to know: はっしゃ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C374-SENRO374-04]; assesses=[] -->

**はっしゃ** — *hassha* — "departure (of a train or bus)".

> **でんしゃの はっしゃは くじです。** — *densha no hassha wa kuji desu* — "The train leaves at nine."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**はっしゃ** (*hassha*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *hatsu*, "departure" (Middle Chinese *pjot*) and *sha*, "car" (*tsyhae*), joined as *hassha* (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
