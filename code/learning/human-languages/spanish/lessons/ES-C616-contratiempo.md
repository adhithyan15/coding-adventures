## You'll want to know: el contratiempo
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C616-IMPREV-04]; assesses=[] -->

**el contratiempo** — "the setback, the mishap".

> Tuvimos un contratiempo con el coche.
>
> *We had a setback with the car.*

A small problem that upsets a plan.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*contratiempo* is built from *contra* 'against' and *tiempo*, inherited from Latin *tempus* 'time' (Lewis & Short).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.
