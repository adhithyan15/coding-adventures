## You'll want to know: かれ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C242-FRIENDS242-01]; assesses=[] -->

**かれ** — *kare* — "he; a boyfriend".

> **かれは がくせいですか。** — *kare wa gakusei desu ka* — "Is he a student?"

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**かれ** (*kare*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
