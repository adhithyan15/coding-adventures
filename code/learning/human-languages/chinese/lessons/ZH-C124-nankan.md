## You'll want to know: nánkàn
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C124-BUILD124-04]; assesses=[] -->

*nánkàn* — "ugly".

> *wǒ de xié hěn nánkàn* — My shoes are ugly.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*nánkàn* is built from *nán* 'hard' and 看 'to look at', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *nan* and *khanH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription): hard to look at.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing things.
