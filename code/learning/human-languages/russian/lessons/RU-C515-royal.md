## You'll want to know: рояль
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C515-INSTRUMENTS-01]; assesses=[] -->

**рояль** (*royál*) — "a grand piano".

> В зале стоит большой чёрный рояль.
>
> *V zále stoít bolshóy chyórnyy royál.*
>
> There is a big black grand piano in the hall.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

EtymDB lists *рояль*, glossed 'grand piano', without a source; it is usually traced to French *royal* 'royal'.
