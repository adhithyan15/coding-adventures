## You'll want to know: 年
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C47-DATE47-01]; assesses=[] -->

**年** — *nián* — "year".

> **一年有多少个星期** *yì nián yǒu duōshao ge xīngqī* — How many weeks are there in a year?

The new character, in pieces: 年 — six strokes: a short fall, two bars, a short vertical on the left, a long bar, and one long vertical through them. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**年** (*nián*) is inherited from Middle Chinese *nen* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'the grain is ripe'. One harvest a year is the usual explanation of the sense, though the step is not settled.
