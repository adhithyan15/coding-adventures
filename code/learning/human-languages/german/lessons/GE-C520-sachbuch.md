## You'll want to know: das Sachbuch
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C520-BUECHER-04]; assesses=[] -->

**das Sachbuch** — "non-fiction book".

> Zum Geburtstag bekam ich ein Sachbuch über Vögel.
>
> *For my birthday I got a non-fiction book about birds.*

Plural: *die Sachbücher*. Fiction is *die Belletristik*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Sachbuch* is built on *Sache* "thing, matter", inherited from Old High German *sahha*, and *Buch* "book", from Old High German *buoh* (EtymDB).
