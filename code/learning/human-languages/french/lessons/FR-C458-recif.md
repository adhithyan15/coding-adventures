## You'll want to know: le récif
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C458-LITTORAL-04]; assesses=[] -->

**le récif** — "the reef".

> Les bateaux évitent le récif près du port.
>
> *The boats steer clear of the reef near the harbour.*

*Un récif de corail* — a coral reef.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*récif* was borrowed from Spanish *arrecife*, which is usually traced to Arabic *ar-raṣīf* 'causeway, raised way'. EtymDB lists it without a source.
