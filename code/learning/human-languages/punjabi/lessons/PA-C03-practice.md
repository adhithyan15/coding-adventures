- [YOU SAY: the homophone trap — *hāṁ* means both "am" and "yes"]

## The word, taken apart — roots you now carry
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-ETYMON-QUESTION-K-03, PA-ETYMON-FIRST-PERSON-M-03, PA-ETYMON-NEGATIVE-NE-03] -->

[PAUSE 2s]
- *kivēṁ* ← the interrogative *k-* (PIE *\*kʷo-*), cousin of English *how/what*
- *maiṁ* ← *ma-* (PIE *\*me-*), cousin of English *me/my*
- *ṭhīk* — native Indian "fine," no European cognate
- *koī gall nahīṁ* — "no matter"; *nahīṁ* ← PIE *\*ne*, English *no*

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-LEX-KIVEIN-03, PA-ETYMON-QUESTION-K-03, PA-PHRASE-HOW-ARE-YOU-03, PA-GRAMMAR-TUSI-HO-03, PA-LEX-MAIN-03, PA-GRAMMAR-MAIN-HAAN-03, PA-ETYMON-FIRST-PERSON-M-03, PA-LEX-THIK-03, PA-PHRASE-I-AM-FINE-03, PA-PHRASE-NO-PROBLEM-03, PA-ETYMON-NEGATIVE-NE-03, PA-LEX-DHANVAAD-01] -->

[PAUSE 3s] A stranger asks *tusī̃ kivēṁ ho?* — give a full, polite reply, and
answer their thanks. (*Maiṁ ṭhīk hāṁ, dhannavād* — and to thanks, *koī gall
nahīṁ*.)
