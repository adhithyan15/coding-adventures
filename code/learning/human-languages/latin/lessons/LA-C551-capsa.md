## You'll want to know: capsa, capsae
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C551-CAPS-01]; assesses=[] -->

**capsa, capsae** — "a book box".

> Puer librōs in capsā portat.
>
> *The boy carries his books in a book box.*

Feminine, first declension, from *capiō* — to hold. English *case*, a box, comes from it through French.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**capsa** is built on *capiō* "to take, hold" (Lewis & Short; EtymDB). English *case* "box" comes from it through French, and *capsule* from its diminutive.
