## You'll want to know: अपचन
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C483-PACAN483-01]; assesses=[] -->

**अपचन** — *apcan* — "indigestion".

> तेलकट खाल्ल्याने मला अपचन झालं.
>
> *telkaṭ khāllyāne malā apcan jhālaṁ.*
>
> I got indigestion from eating oily food.

Neuter.

अ-, not, + पचन, digestion. अपचन होणे, with the dative.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अपचन** (*apcan*) is built from Sanskrit *a-*, "not", and *pacana*, "cooking, maturing" (Monier-Williams): a learned word for food not digested.
