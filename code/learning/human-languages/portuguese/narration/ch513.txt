Portuguese, chapter 513: Correr e pedalar.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
a maratona — marathon.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for volleyball, then the Portuguese for gymnastics.

You'll want to know: a maratona.
a maratona — "marathon".
Correu a maratona em menos de quatro horas. She ran the marathon in under four hours.
Also figurative: uma maratona de estudo.

The word, taken apart.
maratona comes from the Greek place name Marathṓn (EtymDB), itself from márathon "fennel"; the long race recalls a messenger's run from there.

What you've built.

Lesson 2 of 5.

You'll want to know: o atletismo.
o atletismo — "athletics".
Na escola, o atletismo é o meu desporto preferido. At school, athletics is my favourite sport.
An athlete is um atleta or uma atleta.

The word, taken apart.
atletismo is built on atleta, a learned borrowing through Latin āthlēta (Lewis & Short) from Greek athlētḗs "a competitor", from âthlos "a contest".
