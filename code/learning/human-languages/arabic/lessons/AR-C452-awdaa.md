## You'll want to know: أودع
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C452-FAR452-05]; assesses=[] -->

**أودع** — *awdaʿa* — "to deposit".

> أودع أبي المال في البنك.
>
> *awdaʿa abī al-māl fī al-bank.*
>
> My father deposited the money in the bank.

Present: يودع, *yūdiʿ*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**أودع** (*awdaʿa*) is built on the native Arabic root *w-d-ʿ*, "to leave, to let rest" (Lane). It is Form IV, "to leave a thing in someone's keeping".
