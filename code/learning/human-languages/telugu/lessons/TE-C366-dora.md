## You'll want to know: దొర
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C366-NIDHI366-04]; assesses=[] -->

**దొర** — *dora* — "a lord; a master".

> పూర్వం దొర గ్రామం అంతా ఏలేవాడు.
>
> *pūrvaṁ dora grāmaṁ antā ēlēvāḍu.*
>
> In the old days the lord ruled the whole village.

In the villages దొర was the landlord, and దొరసాని his wife.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**దొర** (*dora*) is a Telugu word for a lord or master; its origin is not settled.
