Arabic, chapter 490: Surprise and admiration.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
دهشة (dahsha) — astonishment.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to shiver, then the Arabic for to frighten.

You'll want to know: دهشة.
دهشة — dahsha — "astonishment".
فتح الولد الهدية بدهشة. fataḥa al-walad al-hadiyya bi-dahsha. The boy opened the present in astonishment.

The word, taken apart.
دهشة (dahsha) is built on the native Arabic root d-h-sh, "to be amazed, to be bewildered" (Lane). Dahsha is that amazed state.

What you've built.
One more word for describing what you lived and felt.

مندهش (mundahish) is built on the native Arabic root d-h-sh, "to be amazed, to be bewildered" (Lane). Mundahish is the participle of Form VII: one who has been struck with amazement.

Lesson 4 of 5.
تفاجأ (tafājaʾa) — to be surprised.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for astonished, then the Arabic for to be amazed.

You'll want to know: تفاجأ.
تفاجأ — tafājaʾa — "to be surprised".
تفاجأت عندما رأيت صديقي في المطار. tafājaʾtu ʿindamā raʾaytu ṣadīqī fī al-maṭār. I was surprised when I saw my friend at the airport.
Present: يتفاجأ, yatafājaʾ.
It takes بـ for the cause: تفاجأ (tafājaʾa) بالخبر.

The word, taken apart.
تفاجأ (tafājaʾa) is built on the native Arabic root f-j-ʾ, "to come upon suddenly" (Lane). Tafājaʾa is to be come upon suddenly: to be taken by surprise.

Lesson 5 of 5.
إعجاب (iʿjāb) — admiration.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to be amazed, then the Arabic for to be surprised.

You'll want to know: إعجاب.
إعجاب — iʿjāb — "admiration".
تكلم المدير عن عمل الفريق بإعجاب. takallama al-mudīr ʿan ʿamal al-farīq bi-iʿjāb. The manager spoke about the team's work with admiration.
It takes بـ for what is admired: إعجاب (iʿjāb) بالفريق.

The word, taken apart.
إعجاب (iʿjāb) is built on the native Arabic root ʿ-j-b, "to wonder, to marvel" (Lane). Iʿjāb is the verbal noun of Form IV, to make someone marvel.
