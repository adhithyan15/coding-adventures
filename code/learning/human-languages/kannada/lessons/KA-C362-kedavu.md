## You'll want to know: ಕೆಡವು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C362-NIRMANA362-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ಕೆಡವು** — *keḍavu* — "to demolish; to knock down".

> ಅವರು ಹಳೆಯ ಕಟ್ಟಡವನ್ನು ಕೆಡವಿದರು.
>
> *avaru haḷeya kaṭṭaḍavannu keḍavidaru.*
>
> They demolished the old building.

Past (I …): ಕೆಡವಿದೆ, *keḍavide*.

What is knocked down takes -ಅನ್ನು. A wrestler can ಕೆಡವು his rival too.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಕೆಡವು** (*keḍavu*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 1524 lists Kannada *keḍavu*, "to throw down, to fell", under words for lying down.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
