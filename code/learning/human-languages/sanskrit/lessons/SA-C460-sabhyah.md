## You'll want to know: सभ्यः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C460-SVABHAVA460-02]; assesses=[] -->

**सभ्यः** — *sabhyaḥ* — "refined; courteous".

> सभ्यः जनः कटु न वदति।
>
> *sabhyaḥ janaḥ kaṭu na vadati.*
>
> A refined person does not speak harshly.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सभ्यः** (*sabhyaḥ*) is built on **सभा**, "an assembly, a hall": fit for an assembly; MW gives "suitable to good society, courteous, polite, refined, civilized" (MW).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
