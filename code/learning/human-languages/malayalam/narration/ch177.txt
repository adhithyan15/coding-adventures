Malayalam, chapter 177: Nobody, Never, Nowhere, Anyone, Anything.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ആരും (āruṁ) — nobody (with a negative verb).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for to fall, then the Malayalam for to live.

You'll want to know: ആരും.
ആരും — āruṁ — "nobody (with a negative verb)". Malayalam negates a verb by joining ഇല്ല (illa) onto it: ആരും വന്നില്ല — āruṁ vannilla — "Nobody came." To say something is not a thing, it uses അല്ല (alla): ഇത് എന്റെ വീട് അല്ല — itŭ enṟe vīṭŭ alla — "This is not my house."

Lesson 4 of 5.
ആരെങ്കിലും (āreṅkiluṁ) — anyone, someone.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for never, then the Malayalam for nowhere.

You'll want to know: ആരെങ്കിലും.
ആരെങ്കിലും — āreṅkiluṁ — "anyone, someone". A yes-or-no question ends in -ഓ (-ō): ഉണ്ട് becomes ഉണ്ടോ. അവിടെ ആരെങ്കിലും ഉണ്ടോ? — aviṭe āreṅkiluṁ uṇṭō? — "Is anyone there?"
