## You'll want to know: आगन्तुकः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C414-DIGBHRAMA414-05]; assesses=[] -->

**आगन्तुकः** — *āgantukaḥ* — "a newcomer; a stranger".

> आगन्तुकः जनान् मार्गं पृच्छति।
>
> *āgantukaḥ janān mārgaṃ pṛcchati.*
>
> The newcomer asks people the way.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**आगन्तुकः** (*āgantukaḥ*) is built from *ā*, "towards", and the root √gam, "to go": one who has come; MW gives *āgantuka* as a new comer, a stranger, a guest (MW).
