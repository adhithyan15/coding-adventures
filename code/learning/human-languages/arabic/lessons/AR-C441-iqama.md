## You'll want to know: إقامة
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C441-ISTIQBAL441-03]; assesses=[] -->

**إقامة** — *iqāma* — "a residence; a stay".

> كانت إقامتنا في الفندق مريحة.
>
> *kānat iqāmatunā fī al-funduq murīḥa.*
>
> Our stay in the hotel was comfortable.

*biṭāqat iqāma* is a residence permit.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**إقامة** (*iqāma*) is built on the native Arabic root *q-w-m*, "to stand" (Lane). It is the verbal noun of Form IV, "staying on in a place".
