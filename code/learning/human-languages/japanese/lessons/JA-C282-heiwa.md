## You'll want to know: へいわ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C282-SEKAI282-03]; assesses=[] -->

**へいわ** — *heiwa* — "peace".

> **みんな へいわを ねがっています。** — *minna heiwa o negatte imasu* — "Everyone wishes for peace."

A na-adjective: な before a noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**へいわ** (*heiwa*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *hei*, "peace" (Middle Chinese *bjaeng*) and *wa*, "harmony" (*hwa*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
