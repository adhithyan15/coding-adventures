## You'll want to know: پھاڑنا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C505-ATARI505-04]; assesses=[] -->

**پھاڑنا** — *phāṛnā* — "to tear (something)".

> بچے نے پرانا اخبار پھاڑا۔
>
> *bachche ne purānā akhbār phāṛā.*
>
> The child tore up the old newspaper.

Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے خط پھاڑا, *us ne khat phāṛā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**پھاڑنا** (*phāṛnā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *phāṛnā*, "to split", under Sanskrit *sphāṭayati*, "causes to split" (CDIAL 13825).
