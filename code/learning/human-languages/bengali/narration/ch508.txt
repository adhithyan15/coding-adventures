Bengali, chapter 508: Rooms of a House.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
বৈঠকখানা (boiṭhôkkhānā) — a sitting room; a drawing room.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for to plaster, then the Bengali for to hammer in.

You'll want to know: বৈঠকখানা.
বৈঠকখানা — boiṭhôkkhānā — "a sitting room; a drawing room".
দাদু বৈঠকখানায় বসে পত্রিকা পড়েন। Dādu boiṭhôkkhānāy bôse pôtrikā pôṛen. My grandfather sits in the drawing room and reads the magazine.

The word, taken apart.
বৈঠকখানা (boiṭhôkkhānā) joins বৈঠক, "a sitting, a meeting", usually traced to Hindi baiṭhak, to khāna, "a house, a room", which is usually traced to Persian.

Lesson 2 of 5.
চিলেকোঠা (chilekoṭhā) — a small room on the roof; an attic.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for to hammer in, then the Bengali for a drawing room.

You'll want to know: চিলেকোঠা.
চিলেকোঠা — chilekoṭhā — "a small room on the roof; an attic".
পুরনো খেলনাগুলো চিলেকোঠায় আছে। Purôno khelnāgulo chilekoṭhāy āchhe. The old toys are in the room on the roof.

The word, taken apart.
চিলেকোঠা (chilekoṭhā) is the small room at the top of the stairs, on the roof. The second part is কোঠা, "a room", which CDIAL lists as Bengali koṭhā, "a brick-built house", under Sanskrit kōṣṭha, "a storeroom, an inner apartment" (CDIAL 3546); where the first part comes from is not settled.

রান্নাঘর — rānnāghôr — "a kitchen".
দিদিমা রান্নাঘরে মাছ ভাজছিলেন। Didimā rānnāghôre māchh bhājchhilen. My grandmother was frying fish in the kitchen.

The word, taken apart.
রান্নাঘর (rānnāghôr) joins রান্না, "cooking" (CDIAL 10615), to ঘর, "a room" (CDIAL 4428): the cooking room, the everyday word for a kitchen.

Lesson 4 of 5.
চৌকাঠ (choukāṭh) — a door frame; a threshold.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for an attic, then the Bengali for a kitchen.

You'll want to know: চৌকাঠ.
চৌকাঠ — choukāṭh — "a door frame; a threshold".
মিস্ত্রি দরজার নতুন চৌকাঠ বানাল। Mistri dôrjār nôtun choukāṭh bānālô. The workman made a new frame for the door.
Also the wooden sill at its foot: চৌকাঠে বসা, to sit on the threshold.

The word, taken apart.
চৌকাঠ (choukāṭh) comes from a reconstructed catuṣkāṣṭha, "a rectangular frame", made of catur, "four", and kāṣṭha, "wood"; CDIAL lists Bengali caukāṭh, "a wooden door frame" (CDIAL 4631).
