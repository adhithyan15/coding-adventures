## You'll want to know: कुम्हार
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C264-LUHAR264-04]; assesses=[] -->

**कुम्हार** — *kumhār* — "a potter".

> कुम्हार घड़ो बणावै है।
>
> *kumhār ghaṛo baṇāvai hai.*
>
> The potter makes a water pot.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**कुम्हार** (*kumhār*) is inherited from Sanskrit *kumbhakāra*, "potter". CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry; it lists Hindi *kumhār* (CDIAL 3310).
