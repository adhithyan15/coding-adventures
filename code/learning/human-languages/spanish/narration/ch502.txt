Spanish, chapter 502: Hablar con otros.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
presentar — to introduce; to present.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for the sign, then the Spanish for the fountain.

You'll want to know: presentar.
presentar — "to introduce; to present".
Te presento a mi amiga Ana. Let me introduce my friend Ana.
Presentar un documento — to hand in a document.

What you've built.
One more word for reading notices, forms and messages.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: presentar]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: presentar, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: a sentence of your own with presentar]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Spanish for the sign, then the Spanish for the fountain, then say presentar again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does presentar mean? ("To introduce; to present".) Say it once more.
