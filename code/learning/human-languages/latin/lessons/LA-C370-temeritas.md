## You'll want to know: temeritās, temeritātis
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C370-AUCTORITAS-05]; assesses=[] -->

**temeritās, temeritātis** — "rashness, recklessness".

> Temeritās iuvenis eum in perīculum dūxit.
>
> *The young man's rashness led him into danger.*

*temeritās, temeritātis* f.: acting blindly, without a plan. English *temerity*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*temeritās* is built on the adverb *temerē* "blindly, at random" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short). English *temerity* comes from it.
