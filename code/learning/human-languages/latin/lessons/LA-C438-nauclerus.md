## You'll want to know: nauclērus, nauclērī
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C438-CONSCENDO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**nauclērus, nauclērī** — "a ship's master, a shipowner".

> Nauclērus nautās vocat et nāvem parat.
>
> *The ship's master calls the sailors and gets the ship ready.*

Second declension, masculine: *nauclērus, nauclērī m.* The man who owns or commands a merchant ship.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*nauclērus* was borrowed from Greek *nauklēros* 'shipowner' (EtymDB; Lewis & Short), built from *naus* 'ship' and *klēros* 'lot, portion'.
