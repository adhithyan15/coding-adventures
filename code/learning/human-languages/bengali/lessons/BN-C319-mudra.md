# মুদ্রা (mudrā) — currency; a coin (of old)

## You'll want to know: মুদ্রা
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C319-PURATON319-05]; assesses=[] -->

**মুদ্রা** — *mudrā* — "currency; a coin (of old)".

> এই প্রাচীন মুদ্রাগুলো দুই হাজার বছরের পুরনো।
>
> *ei prāchīn mudrāgulo dui hājār bôchhôrer purôno.*
>
> These ancient coins are two thousand years old.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**মুদ্রা** (*mudrā*) is Sanskrit *mudrā*, a seal or a stamp, and so a stamped coin (Monier-Williams); today it also means a country's currency.
