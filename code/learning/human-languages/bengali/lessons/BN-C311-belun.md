## You'll want to know: বেলুন
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C311-MELA311-03]; assesses=[] -->

**বেলুন** — *belun* — "a balloon".

> বাবা আমাকে মেলা থেকে একটা লাল বেলুন কিনে দিলেন।
>
> *bābā āmāke melā theke ekṭā lāl belun kine dilen.*
>
> Father bought me a red balloon at the fair.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**বেলুন** (*belun*) is borrowed from English *balloon*.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
