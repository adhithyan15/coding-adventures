Latin, chapter 150: Careful, Lazy, Hard-working, Timid, Bold.

(antīquus, antīqua, antīquum.) Narrow? (angustus, angusta, angustum.) Low? (humilis, humile.) Thick? (crassus, crassa, crassum.) Thin? (tenuis, tenue.) Blind? (caecus, caeca, caecum.) Sharp? (acūtus, acūta, acūtum.) Sour? (acerbus, acerba, acerbum.) Alive? (vīvus, vīva, vīvum.) Gentle? (mītis, mīte.) Fierce? (ferōx, ferōcis.) Pleasant? (iūcundus, iūcunda, iūcundum.)
