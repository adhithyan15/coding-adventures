## You'll want to know: порыв
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C466-BURYA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**порыв** (*porýv*) — "a gust (of wind); an impulse".

> Сильный порыв ветра сорвал с него шляпу.
>
> *Sílnyy porýv vétra sorvál s negó shlyápu.*
>
> A strong gust of wind blew his hat off.

Masculine. *Порыв ветра* — a gust of wind.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*порыв* is built from *по-* and *рыв* 'a jerk, a tearing', on the root of *рвать* 'to tear'. EtymDB lists *порыв* without a deeper source.
