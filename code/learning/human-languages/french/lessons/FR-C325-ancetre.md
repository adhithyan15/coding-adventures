## You'll want to know: l'ancêtre
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C325-PASSE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**l'ancêtre** — "the ancestor".

> Mes ancêtres vivaient dans ce village.
>
> *My ancestors lived in this village.*

Also a forerunner: *l'ancêtre du vélo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ancêtre* comes from Old French *ancestre* (EtymDB), inherited from Latin *antecessor* "one who goes before", from *ante* and *cēdere*.
