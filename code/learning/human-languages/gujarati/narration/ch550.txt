Gujarati, chapter 550: Taking Aim.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
તીર (tīr) — an arrow.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to catch a ball, then the Gujarati for a medal.

You'll want to know: તીર.
તીર — tīr — "an arrow".
જો પવન નહીં હોય તો તીર સીધું જશે. jo pavan nahī̃ hoy to tīr sīdhũ jaśe. If there's no wind, the arrow will fly straight.
A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
તીર (tīr) is usually traced to Persian tīr, "an arrow".

જો તમારું તીર નિશાન પર લાગશે તો તમે જીતશો. jo tamārũ tīr niśān par lāgśe to tame jītśo. If your arrow hits the target, you'll win.
A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
નિશાન (niśān) is usually traced to Persian nishān, "a mark, a sign"; the mark one aims at is a target.

Lesson 3 of 5.
તાકવું (tākvũ) — to aim at.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for an arrow, then the Gujarati for a target.

You'll want to know: તાકવું.
તાકવું — tākvũ — "to aim at".
જો તમે ધ્યાનથી તાકશો તો તીર નિશાન પર લાગશે. jo tame dhyānthī tākśo to tīr niśān par lāgśe. If you aim carefully, the arrow will hit the target.
Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે નિશાન તાક્યું, teṇe niśān tākyũ.

The word, taken apart.
તાકવું (tākvũ) is filed by CDIAL under Sanskrit tarkáyati, "considers", and CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb, "to look at" (CDIAL 5716); one looks hard at what one aims at.

Lesson 4 of 5.
ભેદવું (bhedvũ) — to pierce (a target).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a target, then the Gujarati for to aim at.

You'll want to know: ભેદવું.
ભેદવું — bhedvũ — "to pierce (a target)".
જો અરુણ પહેલા તીરથી નિશાન ભેદત તો તે જીતી જાત. jo Aruṇ pahelā tīrthī niśān bhedat to te jītī jāt. If Arun had hit the target with the first arrow, he would have won.
Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે નિશાન ભેદ્યું, teṇe niśān bhedyũ.

The word, taken apart.
ભેદવું (bhedvũ) goes back to Sanskrit bhid, "to split, to pierce" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 5 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: દોડવીર.
દોડવીર — doḍvīr — "a runner (an athlete)".
જો આ દોડવીર રોજ દોડશે તો તે ચંદ્રક જીતશે. jo ā doḍvīr roj doḍśe to te candrak jītśe. If this runner runs every day, she will win a medal.
A masculine or feminine noun, by the person meant.

The word, taken apart.
દોડવીર (doḍvīr) is built from the stem of દોડવું, "to run", which CDIAL files under Sanskrit drávati (CDIAL 6624), and vīr, "a hero", from Sanskrit vīra (Monier-Williams).
