## You'll want to know: きょく
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C470-SONG470-01]; assesses=[] -->

**きょく** — *kyoku* — "a piece of music; a tune".

> **ははは この きょくが すきです。** — *haha wa kono kyoku ga suki desu* — "My mother likes this tune."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**きょく** (*kyoku*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): one Chinese character read with its on'yomi, a reading borrowed from Chinese. It is *kyoku*, "melody" (Middle Chinese *khjowk*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
