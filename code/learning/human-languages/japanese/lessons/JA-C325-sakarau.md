## You'll want to know: さからう
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C325-SHITAGAU325-02]; assesses=[] -->

**さからう** — *sakarau* — "to go against; to disobey".

> **ははに さからったので、あとで あやまりました。** — *haha ni sakaratta node, ato de ayamarimashita* — "I went against my mother, so I apologized later."

Polite **さからいます**, *sakaraimasu*; a godan verb. Past (polite): **むすこが さからいました。**, *musuko ga sakaraimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**さからう** (*sakarau*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, and its older origin is not settled. Its kanji was attached to it later as a kun'yomi, a native reading given to a Chinese character (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
