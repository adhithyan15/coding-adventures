## You'll want to know: شور
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C407-MARAMMAT407-03]; assesses=[] -->

**شور** — *shor* — "noise".

> سڑک کے شور سے میں سو نہیں سکی۔
>
> *saṛak ke shor se maiṅ so nahīṅ sakī.*
>
> I couldn't sleep for the noise from the road.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**شور** (*shor*) is usually traced to Persian *shor*, "an uproar, a clamour".
