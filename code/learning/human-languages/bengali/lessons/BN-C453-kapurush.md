## You'll want to know: কাপুরুষ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C453-MONOBOL453-03]; assesses=[] -->

**কাপুরুষ** — *kāpurush* — "a coward".

> বিপদ দেখে পালানো কাপুরুষের কাজ।
>
> *bipôd dekhe pālāno kāpurusher kāj.*
>
> Running away at the sight of danger is a coward's act.

ভীতু (taught) is timid; কাপুরুষ names the man himself, and it stings.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**কাপুরুষ** (*kāpurush*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *kāpuruṣa*, "a contemptible man, a coward" (Monier-Williams).
