## You'll want to know: نہلانا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C482-GROOM482-01]; assesses=[] -->

**نہلانا** — *nahlānā* — "to bathe (someone)".

> ماں نے بچے کو نہلایا۔
>
> *māṅ ne bachche ko nahlāyā.*
>
> The mother bathed the baby.

Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے کتے کو نہلایا, *us ne kutte ko nahlāyā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**نہلانا** (*nahlānā*) is the causative of **نہانا**, "to bathe", which CDIAL lists under Sanskrit *snāti*, "bathes" (CDIAL 13786).
