## You'll want to know: अजीब
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C454-ACHAMBHA454-04]; assesses=[] -->

**अजीब** — *ajīb* — "strange; odd".

> रात को खिड़की से एक अजीब आवाज़ आई।
>
> *rāt ko khiṛkī se ek ajīb āvāz āī.*
>
> A strange sound came through the window at night.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अजीब** (*ajīb*) is usually traced to Arabic *ʿajīb*, "wonderful, strange", which reached Hindi through Persian.
