## The exchange
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-PERFORMANCE-RESULT-01]; assesses=[ZH-LEX-C151-KANDAO-01, ZH-GRAMMAR-RESULT-COMPLEMENT-01, ZH-LEX-C151-XUEHUI-01, ZH-LEX-C151-TINGDONG-01, ZH-LEX-C151-KANDONG-01, ZH-LEX-C151-ZUOWAN-01, ZH-LEX-C62-FAST62-01] -->

> **A:** 你看到他了吗
>
> *nǐ kàndào tā le ma* — Did you see him?
>
> **B:** 看到了 他在那儿
>
> *kàndào le, tā zài nàr* — I did. He's over there.
>
> **A:** 他说什么
>
> *tā shuō shénme* — What's he saying?
>
> **A:** *nǐ tīngdǒng le ma* — Did you catch it?
>
> **B:** *méi tīngdǒng, tā shuō de tài kuài le* — No. He talks too fast.
>
> **A:** *nǐ zuòwán le ma* — Have you finished?
>
> **B:** 没有 我在看书
>
> *méiyǒu, wǒ zài kàn shū* — No, I'm reading.
>
> **A:** *zhè běn shū nǐ kàndǒng le ma* — Do you follow this book?
>
> **B:** *kàndǒng le* — Yes.
>
> **A:** 你学会开车了吗
>
> *nǐ xuéhuì kāichē le ma* — Have you learned to drive?
>
> **B:** 学会了
>
> *xuéhuì le* — I have.

Each yes repeats the verb and its result, with 了: 看到了, *kàndǒng le*, 学会了. Each no puts 没 in front: *méi tīngdǒng*.

## What you've built — five verbs that finish
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ZH-PERFORMANCE-RESULT-01, ZH-SCRIPT-DAO-ARRIVE-01, ZH-LEX-C151-KANDAO-01, ZH-LEX-C151-XUEHUI-01, ZH-LEX-C151-TINGDONG-01, ZH-LEX-C151-ZUOWAN-01, ZH-LEX-C58-THINGS58-01] -->

A verb, then its result; 没 when the result did not come:

- **我看到他了** *wǒ kàndào tā le* — I saw him.
- **我学会开车了** *wǒ xuéhuì kāichē le* — I've learned to drive.
- *wǒ méi tīngdǒng* — I didn't catch it.
- *wǒ zuòwán le* — I've finished.

**Listen.** *Nǐ kàndào wǒ de shǒujī le ma?*

**Speak.** Say one thing you have learned how to do, and one thing you have not finished.
