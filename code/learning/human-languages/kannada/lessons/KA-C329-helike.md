# ಹೇಳಿಕೆ (hēḷike) — a statement

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[KA-LEX-C329-MADHYAMA329-03, KA-LEX-C329-MADHYAMA329-04] -->

## You'll want to know: ಹೇಳಿಕೆ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C329-MADHYAMA329-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ಹೇಳಿಕೆ** — *hēḷike* — "a statement".

> ಪೊಲೀಸರ ಹೇಳಿಕೆ ಎಲ್ಲ ಪತ್ರಿಕೆಗಳಲ್ಲಿ ಬಂತು.
>
> *polīsara hēḷike ella patrikegaḷalli bantu.*
>
> The police statement appeared in every newspaper.

ಹೇಳು, to say, with -ಇಕೆ, which makes a noun of a verb: what is said.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಹೇಳಿಕೆ** (*hēḷike*) is a native Kannada noun made from the verb *hēḷu*, "to say" (DEDR 4430), with the ending -*ike* that turns verbs into nouns.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
