## You'll want to know: कीड़ा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C447-JHADI447-04]; assesses=[] -->

**कीड़ा** — *kīṛā* — "an insect; a worm".

> पत्ते पर एक हरा कीड़ा था।
>
> *patte par ek harā kīṛā thā.*
>
> There was a green insect on the leaf.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**कीड़ा** (*kīṛā*) is a tadbhava of Sanskrit *kīṭa*, "an insect, a worm" (CDIAL 3193), which lists Hindi *kīṛā*.
