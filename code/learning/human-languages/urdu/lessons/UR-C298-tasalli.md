## You'll want to know: تسلی
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C298-BICHHAR298-04]; assesses=[] -->

**تسلی** — *tasallī* — "comfort; reassurance".

> ماں کو پولیس سے تسلی ملی۔
>
> *māṅ ko polīs se tasallī milī.*
>
> The police reassured the mother.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**تسلی** (*tasallī*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *tasallī*, "consolation". **تسلی دینا** is to comfort someone.
