# Make the order yourself

## Warm-up

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[SA-LEX-C248-THG248-03, SA-PRON-C261-YUVAM-02] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say “rupee,” then say the independent object form for “you two.”
(*Rūpyakam; yuvām.*)

## Guided Practice — final retrieval

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[SA-LEX-C97-LOC97-02, SA-LEX-C160-THG160-01, SA-LEX-KHADATI-EAT, SA-LEX-C29-COURTESY-01, SA-PRON-DATIVE-ME-01, SA-LEX-C72-HOUSEWORK-05, SA-LEX-ANNAM-FOOD, SA-LEX-PANIYAM-WATER, SA-LEX-KSHIRAM-MILK, SA-GRAMMAR-C265-DADATU-01, SA-PATTERN-C265-POLITE-ORDER-02] -->

No Sanskrit model is shown before you answer. Imagine you are in a
**भोजनालयः**. Choose **अन्नम्**, **पानीयम्**, or **क्षीरम्**, and make the
complete polite request from memory.

[PAUSE 5s]

Now choose a second item and make a different request.

[PAUSE 5s]

Finally, name the meal and say that someone eats food.

## What you've built

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[SA-LEX-C29-COURTESY-01, SA-PRON-DATIVE-ME-01, SA-LEX-ANNAM-FOOD, SA-LEX-PANIYAM-WATER, SA-LEX-KSHIRAM-MILK, SA-GRAMMAR-C265-DADATU-01, SA-PATTERN-C265-POLITE-ORDER-02] -->

Your request should contain **कृपया**, **मह्यम्**, the chosen item, and
**ददातु**. For example, after answering: **कृपया मह्यं पानीयं ददातु** is the
track's water request. It preserves the chapter-24 distinction between
**जलम्**, water as a substance, and **पानीयम्**, a drink offered to someone.
**भोजनम्; अन्नं खादति** completes the last prompt.

## Grammar Lens: the boundary

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[SA-GRAMMAR-C265-DADATU-01, SA-PATTERN-C265-POLITE-ORDER-02] -->

This chapter gives you one useful request with familiar items. It is **not a
complete imperative paradigm**, a complete restaurant vocabulary, or evidence
that every possible meal exchange is covered.

## Wrap-up Recall

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[SA-GRAMMAR-C265-DADATU-01, SA-PATTERN-C265-POLITE-ORDER-02] -->

[PAUSE 3s] Without looking back, make one final polite order for a familiar
food or drink.
