## You'll want to know: ふかす
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C492-BOIL492-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ふかす** — *fukasu* — "to steam (food)".

> **いもを ふかして たべます。** — *imo o fukashite tabemasu* — "I steam potatoes and eat them."

Polite **ふかします**, *fukashimasu*; a godan verb. Past (polite): **そぼが いもを ふかしました。**, *sobo ga imo o fukashimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ふかす** (*fukasu*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi, and its older origin is not settled (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
