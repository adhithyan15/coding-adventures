## You'll want to know: దండోరా
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C342-SABHA342-01]; assesses=[] -->

**దండోరా** — *daṇḍōrā* — "a drum proclamation".

> గ్రామంలో ఒక దండోరా వినిపించింది.
>
> *grāmaṁlō oka daṇḍōrā vinipiñcindi.*
>
> A drum proclamation was heard in the village.

దండోరా వేయు, to proclaim with a drum: the old way to spread news through the whole ఊరు.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**దండోరా** (*daṇḍōrā*) is an old word for news cried with a drum through the streets; its origin is not settled.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
