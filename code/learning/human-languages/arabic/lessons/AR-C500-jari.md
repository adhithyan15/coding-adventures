# جريء (jarīʾ) — bold, daring

## You'll want to know: جريء
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C500-GHURUR500-05]; assesses=[] -->

**جريء** — *jarīʾ* — "bold, daring".

> قفز الولد الجريء في النهر.
>
> *qafaza al-walad al-jarīʾ fī an-nahr.*
>
> The bold boy jumped into the river.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**جريء** (*jarīʾ*) is built on the native Arabic root *j-r-ʾ*, "to be bold, to dare" (Lane). Jarīʾ is the adjective pattern of a lasting trait.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
