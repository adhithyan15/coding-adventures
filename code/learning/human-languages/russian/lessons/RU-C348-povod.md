## You'll want to know: повод
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C348-POVOD-01]; assesses=[] -->

**повод** (*póvod*) — "an occasion, a reason (for); a pretext".

> У нас есть повод для радости: сестра вышла замуж.
>
> *U nas yest póvod dlya rádosti: sestrá výshla zámuzh.*
>
> We have a reason to be happy: my sister has got married.

Masculine. *По поводу* + genitive — about, concerning. Its first sense is a horse’s rein.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**повод** is built from *по-* and *вод-*, the root of *водить* "to lead", from Proto-Slavic *\*voditi* "to lead, to conduct" (EtymDB). It was first the rein that leads a horse, then whatever leads to an event.
