Marathi, chapter 372: Research.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
संशोधन (saṁśodhan) — research.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to cram, then the Marathi for a certificate.

You'll want to know: संशोधन.
संशोधन — saṁśodhan — "research".
ती पाण्याच्या प्रदूषणावर संशोधन करते. tī pāṇyācyā pradūṣṇāvar saṁśodhan karte. She does research on water pollution.
Neuter.
संशोधन (saṁśodhan) करणे, to do research; the researcher is संशोधक.

The word, taken apart.
संशोधन (saṁśodhan) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit saṁśodhana, "purifying; correcting" (Monier-Williams).

विद्वान — vidvān — "a scholar; a learned person".
आजोबा संस्कृतचे मोठे विद्वान होते. ājobā saṁskṛtce moṭhe vidvān hote. Grandfather was a great scholar of Sanskrit.
Masculine.
Also an adjective, learned: विद्वान (vidvān) माणूस.

The word, taken apart.
विद्वान (vidvān) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit vidvas, "knowing, wise" (Monier-Williams). Its nominative is vidvān.
