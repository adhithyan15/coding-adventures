Russian, chapter 499: Visiting.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
навещать (naveshchát) — to visit (a person).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for someone of the same age, then the Russian for a classmate.

You'll want to know: навещать.
навещать (naveshchát) — "to visit (a person)".
По воскресеньям мы навещаем бабушку. Po voskresényam my naveshcháyem bábushku. On Sundays we visit Grandma.
Imperfective; perfective навестить (навещу). Takes the accusative of the person.

The word, taken apart.
навещать (naveshchát) is built on на- and the root of весть 'news'; EtymDB has no entry, and the link is usually made through навестить.

Lesson 2 of 5.
гостеприимный (gostepriímnyy) — hospitable.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a classmate, then the Russian for to visit.

You'll want to know: гостеприимный.
гостеприимный (gostepriímnyy) — "hospitable".
Хозяева были очень гостеприимные. Khozyáyeva býli óchen gostepriímnyye. Our hosts were very hospitable.
Short forms: гостеприимен, гостеприимна.

The word, taken apart.
гостеприимный (gostepriímnyy) is a compound of гость 'guest' and the old verb приимать 'to receive'; EtymDB has no entry.

Lesson 5 of 5.
гостить (gostít) — to stay as a guest.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for hospitality, then the Russian for a tea party.

You'll want to know: гостить.
гостить (gostít) — "to stay as a guest".
Летом я гостила у тёти в деревне. Létom ya gostíla u tyóti v derévne. In the summer I stayed with my aunt in the village.
Imperfective; present гощу, гостишь (ст becomes щ). Гостить у кого.

The word, taken apart.
гостить (gostít) is built on гость 'guest'; EtymDB lists it, glossed 'to be on a visit, to stay', without a source.
