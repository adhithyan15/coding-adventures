> **ਤੁਸੀਂ ਕੀ ਕਰਨਾ ਚਾਹੁੰਦੇ ਹੋ** — *tusiin kii karnaa chaahunde ho* — what do you want to do?

The respectful or plural subject keeps the same target form, while the familiar
second-person auxiliary closes the question.
