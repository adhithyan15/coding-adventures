## You'll want to know: ચીંધવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C413-WAY413-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ચીંધવું** — *cī̃dhvũ* — "to point out (with a finger)".

> એક માણસે અમને સ્ટેશનનો રસ્તો ચીંધ્યો.
>
> *ek māṇse amne sṭeśanno rasto cī̃dhyo.*
>
> A man pointed out the way to the station.

Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે રસ્તો ચીંધ્યો, *teṇe rasto cī̃dhyo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ચીંધવું** (*cī̃dhvũ*) No dictionary we use records an older form, so its history is not settled.
