## You'll want to know: ярость
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C472-ANGER-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ярость** (*yárost*) — "fury".

> В ярости он бросил телефон на пол.
>
> *V yárosti on brósil telefón ná pol.*
>
> In a rage he threw his phone on the floor.

Feminine. The adjective is *яростный* — furious.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ярость* is inherited from Proto-Slavic *jarostь* 'fury', derived from *jarъ* 'furious' (EtymDB), the root also behind *яркий* 'bright'.
