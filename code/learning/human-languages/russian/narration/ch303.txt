Russian, chapter 303: Birth.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
рождение (rozhdéniye) — birth.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to burn oneself, then the Russian for to cut oneself.

You'll want to know: рождение.
рождение (rozhdéniye) — "birth".
Рождение сына изменило их жизнь. Rozhdéniye sýna izmenílo ikh zhizn. The birth of their son changed their life.
Neuter. День рождения — a birthday.

The word, taken apart.
рождение (rozhdéniye) (EtymDB: "birth") is built on родить "to give birth", inherited from Proto-Slavic roditi (EtymDB), with the Church Slavonic -жд- where native Russian has -ж-.

Lesson 2 of 5.
рождаться (rozhdátsya) — to be born.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: рождаться.
рождаться (rozhdátsya) — "to be born".
Каждой весной у нашей кошки рождаются котята. Kázhdoy vesnóy u náshey kóshki rozhdáyutsya kotyáta. Every spring our cat has kittens.
Imperfective; perfective родиться (rodítsya): я родился в мае — I was born in May.

The word, taken apart.
рождаться (rozhdátsya) is built on родить "to give birth", inherited from Proto-Slavic roditi (EtymDB), in the Church Slavonic shape -жд-, with the reflexive -ся.

Lesson 3 of 5.
младенец (mladénets) — a baby, an infant.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for birth, then the Russian for to be born.

You'll want to know: младенец.
младенец (mladénets) — "a baby, an infant".
Младенец спал в кроватке. Mladénets spal v krovátke. The baby was asleep in the cot.
Genitive младенца. Bookish; in speech малыш or ребёнок (rebyónok).

The word, taken apart.
младенец (mladénets) is a Church Slavonic word, mladĭnĭcĭ (EtymDB), from Proto-Slavic moldŭ "young"; the native Russian shape of the same root is молодой.

Lesson 4 of 5.
беременная (berémennaya) — pregnant.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to be born, then the Russian for a baby.

You'll want to know: беременная.
беременная (berémennaya) — "pregnant".
Сестра сейчас беременна. Sestrá seychás berémenna. My sister is pregnant now.
The feminine form, since it describes a woman; short form беременна.

The word, taken apart.
беременная (berémennaya) (EtymDB: "pregnant") is usually traced to беремя "a burden, an armful" (EtymDB: Old East Slavic beremę), whose Church Slavonic shape is бремя: carrying a load. EtymDB lists no parent for беременная (berémennaya).

Lesson 5 of 5.
рожать (rozhát) — to give birth.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a baby, then the Russian for pregnant.

You'll want to know: рожать.
рожать (rozhát) — "to give birth".
Она родила дочку в марте. Oná rodilá dóchku v márte. She gave birth to a daughter in March.
Imperfective; perfective родить (rodít).

The word, taken apart.
рожать (rozhát) is inherited through Old East Slavic ražati from Proto-Slavic radjati (EtymDB), the imperfective partner of roditi "to give birth".
