## You'll want to know: まちあいしつ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C372-EKIMAE372-04]; assesses=[] -->

**まちあいしつ** — *machiaishitsu* — "a waiting room".

> **まちあいしつで でんしゃを まちました。** — *machiaishitsu de densha o machimashita* — "I waited for the train in the waiting room."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**まちあいしつ** (*machiaishitsu*) is built from both layers of the vocabulary: the native Japanese (wago) *machi*, "waiting" (from *matsu*), inherited rather than borrowed; the native Japanese (wago) *ai*, "meeting" (from *au*), inherited rather than borrowed; and the Sino-Japanese *shitsu*, "room" (Middle Chinese *syit*), an on'yomi borrowed from Chinese (JMdict; KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
