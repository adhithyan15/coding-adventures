## You'll want to know: उद्घाटन
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C559-KALPANA559-03]; assesses=[] -->

**उद्घाटन** — *udghāṭan* — "an opening; an inauguration".

> मंत्री ने नए पुल का उद्घाटन किया।
>
> *Mantrī ne nae pul kā udghāṭan kiyā.*
>
> The minister opened the new bridge.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**उद्घाटन** (*udghāṭan*) is a tatsama, taken whole from Sanskrit *udghāṭana*, "opening, unlocking" (Monier-Williams).
