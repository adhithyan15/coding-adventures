# ಸೌರಶಕ್ತಿ (sauraśakti) — solar power

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[KA-LEX-C474-TYAJYA474-03, KA-LEX-C474-TYAJYA474-04] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Kannada for to sort, then the Kannada for to forsake.

## You'll want to know: ಸೌರಶಕ್ತಿ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C474-TYAJYA474-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸೌರಶಕ್ತಿ** — *sauraśakti* — "solar power".

> ನಮ್ಮ ಶಾಲೆ ಸೌರಶಕ್ತಿಯಿಂದ ನಡೆಯುತ್ತದೆ.
>
> *namma śāle sauraśaktiyinda naḍeyuttade.*
>
> Our school runs on solar power.

ಸೌರ, of the sun, plus ಶಕ್ತಿ, power.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸೌರಶಕ್ತಿ** (*sauraśakti*) is a modern compound built from Sanskrit words: *saura*, "relating to the sun", and *śakti*, "power, energy" (Monier-Williams).
