## You'll want to know: ਛਾਬਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C251-GHR251-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ਛਾਬਾ** — *chhābā* — "a flat basket".

> ਛਾਬੇ ਵਿੱਚ ਸੇਬ ਹਨ।
>
> *chhābe vich seb han.*
>
> There are apples in the flat basket.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਛਾਬਾ** (*chhābā*) is inherited from Sanskrit *chadman*, "roof"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *chābbā*, "flat basket" (CDIAL 4981).
