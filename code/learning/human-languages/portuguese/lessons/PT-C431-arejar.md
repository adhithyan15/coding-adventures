## You'll want to know: arejar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C431-AQUECIMENTO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**arejar** — "to air (a room); to get some fresh air".

> Abro a janela para arejar o quarto.
>
> *I open the window to air the room.*

*Arejar a cabeça* — to clear your head.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*arejar* is built on *ar* 'air', which is inherited from Latin *āēr*, from Greek *aḗr* (Lewis & Short).

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
