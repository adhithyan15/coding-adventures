## You'll want to know: جوش
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C379-MEHNAT379-02]; assesses=[] -->

**جوش** — *josh* — "enthusiasm; fervour".

> اگر جوش ہے تو تھکن نہیں ہوتی۔
>
> *agar josh hai to thakan nahīṅ hotī.*
>
> If you are keen, you do not get tired.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**جوش** (*josh*) is usually traced to Persian *josh*, "boiling, ferment": feeling that bubbles up.
