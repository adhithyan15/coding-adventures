Lesson 1 of 6.
ਆਟਾ (āṭā) — flour.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for a churning pot, then the Punjabi for a churning stick.

You'll want to know: ਆਟਾ.
ਆਟਾ — āṭā — "flour".
ਆਟਾ ਡੱਬੇ ਵਿੱਚ ਹੈ। āṭā ḍabbe vich hai. The flour is in the box.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ਆਟਾ (āṭā) is inherited from a reconstructed ārta, "flour"; CDIAL lists Punjabi āṭṭā, "flour" (CDIAL 1338).

Lesson 3 of 6.

ਗੁੰਨ੍ਹਣਾ — gunnhṇā — "to knead".
ਮੈਂ ਪਰਾਤ ਵਿੱਚ ਆਟਾ ਗੁੰਨ੍ਹਦੀ ਹਾਂ। maiṁ parāt vich āṭā gunnhdī hāṁ. I knead the flour in the tray.
Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਆਟਾ ਗੁੰਨ੍ਹਿਆ, maiṁ āṭā gunnhiā.

ਮੈਂ ਪੇੜਾ ਪਤਲਾ ਵੇਲਦੀ ਹਾਂ। maiṁ peṛā patlā veldī hāṁ. I roll the ball of dough out thin.

ਵੇਲਣਾ (velṇā) is inherited from Sanskrit vēllayati, "causes to shake, kneads"; CDIAL lists Punjabi velṇā, "to roll out" (CDIAL 12121).
