## You'll want to know: せんろ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C374-SENRO374-01]; assesses=[] -->

**せんろ** — *senro* — "a railway track".

> **せんろの むこうに こうえんが あります。** — *senro no mukō ni kōen ga arimasu* — "There is a park on the other side of the railway line."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**せんろ** (*senro*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *sen*, "line" (Middle Chinese *sjenH*) and *ro*, "road" (*luH*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
