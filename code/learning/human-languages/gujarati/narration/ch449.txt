Gujarati, chapter 449: Waiting Eagerly.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
આતુર (ātur) — eager; impatient for.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a joke, then the Gujarati for to tease.

You'll want to know: આતુર.
આતુર — ātur — "eager; impatient for".
બાળકો દિવાળીની રજા માટે આતુર છે. bāḷko divāḷīnī rajā māṭe ātur che. The children are eager for the Diwali holidays.

The word, taken apart.
આતુર (ātur) is a tatsama: Monier-Williams gives Sanskrit ātura as "suffering, sick in body or mind" and also as "desirous of"; Gujarati keeps that second sense, eager and restless for something.
