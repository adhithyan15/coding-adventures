# ಸಂಶೋಧನೆ (saṃśōdhane) — research

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[KA-LEX-C406-VISHVAVIDYALAYA406-04, KA-LEX-C406-VISHVAVIDYALAYA406-05] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Kannada for a professor, then the Kannada for to instruct.

## You'll want to know: ಸಂಶೋಧನೆ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C407-SAMSHODHANE407-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸಂಶೋಧನೆ** — *saṃśōdhane* — "research".

> ಅವಳು ಕಾಡಿನ ಹಕ್ಕಿಗಳ ಬಗ್ಗೆ ಸಂಶೋಧನೆ ಮಾಡುತ್ತಾಳೆ.
>
> *avaḷu kāḍina hakkigaḷa bagge saṃśōdhane māḍuttāḷe.*
>
> She does research on forest birds.

ಸಂಶೋಧಕ, a researcher.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸಂಶೋಧನೆ** (*saṃśōdhane*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *saṃśodhana*, "completely purifying, correcting" (Monier-Williams).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
