Tamil, chapter 146: Bitter, Sour, Spicy, Rich, Poor.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
கசப்பு (kacappu) — bitter.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for dirty, then the Tamil for fast.

You'll want to know: கசப்பு.
கசப்பு — kacappu — "bitter".

The word, taken apart.
கசப்பு (kacappu) is native Dravidian. DEDR 1249 lists Malayalam kaśakka, "to be bitter". The entry compares Sanskrit kaṣāya, "astringent" (CDIAL 2974).

Lesson 4 of 5.
பணக்கார (paṇakkāra) — rich.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for sour, then the Tamil for spicy.

You'll want to know: பணக்கார.
பணக்கார — paṇakkāra — "rich".

The word, taken apart.
பணக்கார (paṇakkāra) is paṇam, "money", from Sanskrit paṇa, "a coin" (CDIAL 7715), with -kāra, "the one who has": "moneyed".
