Marwadi (Marwari), chapter 16: Wind, Cloud, and Rain: No New Signs.
9 lessons. All 9 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 9.
Hear havā — wind.

Warm-up.
[pause 22 seconds]
Recall the five-word travel payoff and say home.
[once you have stopped driving — write: नाम, पत्नी, and बाजार]

You'll want to know.
havā — wind.
Listen once, then say the two light beats. Keep the meaning in your ear; the spelling waits for the next lesson even though every sign is already familiar.

Wrap-up Recall.
Source: Marwari Pathshala, Lesson 7.
[question — say your answer, then pause 10 seconds]
What weather word does havā name?


Lesson 2 of 9.
हवा (havā) — wind on the page.

Warm-up.
[pause 18 seconds]
Say wind and money.
[once you have stopped driving — write: घर, then form ह, व, and ा once]

You'll want to know.
ह + वा becomes हवा — havā — wind.

Guided Practice.
Look, cover, and wait five seconds.
[once you have stopped driving — write: हवा (havā)]
No new sign has been added.

Wrap-up Recall.
Source: Marwari Pathshala, Lesson 7.
[question — say your answer, then pause 12 seconds]
From havā, write the weather word.

Lesson 7 of 9.
Retrieve wind, cloud, and rain — wind, cloud, and rain.

[pause 22 seconds]
[once you have stopped driving — write: घ, ज, बच्चा, and थारो]

Lesson 8 of 9.
Payoff — three weather words in four skills.

Warm-up.
[pause 25 seconds]
Say market and home.
[once you have stopped driving — write: both words]
Recall the twelve-label family payoff, then give the meanings of kāĩ and hai from the known name exchange.

Guided Practice.
Identify three heard words.
Produce three words from meaning cues.
Match three printed cards to meanings.
[once you have stopped driving — write: all three heard words without a model]
Pass each skill separately.

Wrap-up Recall.
Source: Marwari Pathshala, Lesson 7.
[question — say your answer, then pause 36 seconds]
Complete all four scored checks, then write wind, cloud, and rain.


Lesson 9 of 9.
Close the weather loop — close the three-word weather loop.

Warm-up.
[pause 22 seconds]
[once you have stopped driving — write: घ, ज, थ, and क]
Then name the four weather-payoff skills you just passed.

Guided Practice.
Without a model, hear one word, say its meaning, and read the other two in mixed order.
[once you have stopped driving — write: all three from meaning cues]

Wrap-up Recall.
Source: Marwari Pathshala, Lesson 7.
[question — say your answer, then pause 36 seconds]
After the four-skill pass, write wind, cloud, and rain without a model.
