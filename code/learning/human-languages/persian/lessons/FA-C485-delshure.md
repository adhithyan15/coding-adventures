## You'll want to know: دلشوره
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C485-WORRY-03]; assesses=[] -->

**دلشوره** (*delshure*) — "a nagging worry; butterflies".

> شب قبل از سفر دلشوره داشتم.
>
> *shab-e qabl az safar delshure dâshtam.*
>
> The night before the trip I had butterflies.

*del* 'heart' + *shure* 'stirring'. *delshure dâshtan* — to feel uneasy.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*delshure* is built on *del*, 'heart', which EtymDB traces to Middle Persian (EtymDB), and *shure*, usually linked with *shuridan*, 'to be stirred up': a churning heart.
