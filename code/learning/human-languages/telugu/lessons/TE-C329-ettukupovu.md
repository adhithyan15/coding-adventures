# ఎత్తుకుపోవు (ettukupōvu) — to make off with

## You'll want to know: ఎత్తుకుపోవు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C329-DONGATANAM329-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ఎత్తుకుపోవు** — *ettukupōvu* — "to make off with".

> దొంగలు బంగారం అంతా ఎత్తుకుపోయారు.
>
> *doṅgalu baṅgāraṁ antā ettukupōyāru.*
>
> The thieves made off with all the gold.

Past (I …): ఎత్తుకుపోయాను, *ettukupōyānu*.

Three pieces in one: ఎత్తు, to lift, -కు, for oneself, and పోవు, to go.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ఎత్తుకుపోవు** (*ettukupōvu*) is a native compound: *ettu*, "to lift up, take away" (DEDR 796), with the self-ending *-ku* and *pōvu*, "to go" (DEDR 4572).
