## You'll want to know: しょうひぜい
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C363-KAIKEI363-03]; assesses=[] -->

**しょうひぜい** — *shōhizei* — "consumption tax".

> **しょうひぜいが あがったら、かいものを ひかえるでしょう。** — *shōhizei ga agattara, kaimono o hikaeru deshō* — "If consumption tax goes up, people will probably cut down on shopping."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**しょうひぜい** (*shōhizei*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): three Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *shō*, "extinguish" (Middle Chinese *sjew*), *hi*, "expense" (*phj+jH*) and *zei*, "tax" (*sywejH*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
