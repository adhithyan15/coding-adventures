Lesson 1 of 6.
मल्लः (mallaḥ) — a wrestler.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for beaten, then the Sanskrit for hits.

You'll want to know: मल्लः.
मल्लः — mallaḥ — "a wrestler".
ग्रामस्य मल्लः बलवान् अस्ति। grāmasya mallaḥ balavān asti. The village wrestler is strong.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
मल्लः (mallaḥ) is the word MW records for a wrestler, a boxer; MW gives no derivation, so its origin is uncertain (MW).

सायं ग्रामे बाहुयुद्धं भवति। sāyaṃ grāme bāhuyuddhaṃ bhavati. There is wrestling in the village in the evening.

Lesson 3 of 6.
पातयति (pātayati) — throws down.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a wrestler, then the Sanskrit for wrestling.

You'll want to know: पातयति.
पातयति — pātayati — "throws down".
एकः मल्लः अन्यं भूमौ पातयति। ekaḥ mallaḥ anyaṃ bhūmau pātayati. One wrestler throws the other to the ground.
Past (he …): सः मल्लं भूमौ पातितवान्, saḥ mallaṃ bhūmau pātitavān.

The word, taken apart.
पातयति (pātayati) comes from the causative of the root √pat, "to fall", which also gives patati: to make fall, to throw down (MW).

Lesson 4 of 6.
उत्तोलयति (uttolayati) — hoists; lifts up.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for wrestling, then the Sanskrit for throws down.

You'll want to know: उत्तोलयति.
उत्तोलयति — uttolayati — "hoists; lifts up".
मल्लः गुरुं भारम् उत्तोलयति। mallaḥ guruṃ bhāram uttolayati. The wrestler lifts a heavy load.
Past (he …): सः भारम् उत्तोलितवान्, saḥ bhāram uttolitavān.

The word, taken apart.
उत्तोलयति (uttolayati) is built on ud, "up", and the causative of the root √tul, "to lift, to weigh", which also gives tolayati; MW gives uttolayati as to take up, to raise up (MW).

Lesson 5 of 6.
आह्वानम् (āhvānam) — a challenge; a summons.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for throws down, then the Sanskrit for hoists.

You'll want to know: आह्वानम्.
आह्वानम् — āhvānam — "a challenge; a summons".
मल्लः आह्वानं स्वीकरोति। mallaḥ āhvānaṃ svīkaroti. The wrestler accepts the challenge.
A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
आह्वानम् (āhvānam) is built on ā and the root √hve, "to call", which also gives āhvayati; MW gives āhvāna as calling, a summons, and a challenge (MW).
