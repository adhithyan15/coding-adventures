Punjabi, chapter 315: Bandits on the Road.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
ਡਾਕੂ (ḍākū) — a bandit.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for a son's son, then the Punjabi for a daughter's son.

You'll want to know: ਡਾਕੂ.
ਡਾਕੂ — ḍākū — "a bandit".
ਪਿਛਲੇ ਸਾਲ ਸਾਡੇ ਕਸਬੇ ਵਿੱਚ ਡਾਕੂ ਆਏ। pichhle sāl sāḍe kasbe vich ḍākū āe. Last year bandits came to our small town.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ਡਾਕੂ (ḍākū) is inherited from a reconstructed ḍākka, "a robber"; CDIAL lists Punjabi ḍākū, "robber", under it (CDIAL 5543).

ਰਾਤ ਨੂੰ ਬੱਸ ਉੱਤੇ ਹਮਲਾ ਹੋਇਆ। rāt nū̃ bass utte hamlā hoiā. At night there was an attack on the bus.

ਭੰਨਣਾ — bhannṇā — "to smash".
ਅਮਨ ਨੇ ਖਿੜਕੀ ਦਾ ਸ਼ੀਸ਼ਾ ਭੰਨਿਆ। Aman ne khiṛkī dā shīshā bhanniā. Aman smashed the windowpane.
Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਪੁਰਾਣਾ ਘੜਾ ਭੰਨਿਆ, maiṁ purāṇā ghaṛā bhanniā.

The word, taken apart.
ਭੰਨਣਾ (bhannṇā) is inherited from Sanskrit bhajyate, "is broken"; CDIAL lists Punjabi bhanṇā, "to break", under it (CDIAL 9361).

Lesson 4 of 6.
ਗਹਿਣਾ (gahiṇā) — a piece of jewellery.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for an attack, then the Punjabi for to smash.

You'll want to know: ਗਹਿਣਾ.
ਗਹਿਣਾ — gahiṇā — "a piece of jewellery".
ਮਾਤਾ ਜੀ ਦੇ ਗਹਿਣੇ ਅਲਮਾਰੀ ਵਿੱਚ ਸਨ। mātā jī de gahiṇe almārī vich san. Mother's jewellery was in the cupboard.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ਗਹਿਣਾ (gahiṇā) is inherited from Sanskrit grahaṇa, "seizing, holding"; CDIAL lists Punjabi gahiṇā, "a pledge, a pawn", under it; the sense of a jewel is given there only for Hindi gahnā (CDIAL 4364).
