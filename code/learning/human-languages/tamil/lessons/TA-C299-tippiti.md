## You'll want to know: தீப்பிடி
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C299-THEE299-01]; assesses=[] -->

**தீப்பிடி** — *tīppiṭi* — "to catch fire".

> ஒருவேளை வீடு தீப்பிடித்தால், நாங்கள் உடனே வெளியே ஓடுவோம்.
>
> *oruvēḷai vīṭu tīppiṭittāl, nāṅkaḷ uṭaṉē veḷiyē ōṭuvōm.*
>
> If the house were to catch fire, we would run out at once.

Past (it …): தீப்பிடித்தது, *tīppiṭittatu*.

The thing on fire is the subject; also written as two words, தீப் பிடி.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**தீப்பிடி** (*tīppiṭi*) is a Tamil compound built from தீ, "fire" (DEDR 3266), and பிடி, "to catch, seize" (DEDR 4148); the stop doubles after தீ.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
