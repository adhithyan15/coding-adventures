# Payoff — a twelve-label family map

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MW-PERFORMANCE-FAMILY-SEVEN-FOUR-SKILL-01, MW-PERFORMANCE-FAMILY-TEN-FOUR-SKILL-01, MW-COURTESY-AABHAAR-FORMAL-01] -->

[PAUSE 22s] Recall the seven-label and three-label checkpoints, then give the
formal gratitude response. These are vocabulary options, not a prescribed
family structure.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-PERFORMANCE-FAMILY-TWELVE-FOUR-SKILL-01]; assesses=[MW-LEX-MAA-01, MW-LEX-BAAP-01, MW-LEX-BHAI-01, MW-LEX-BAHAN-01, MW-LEX-DADA-01, MW-LEX-DADI-01, MW-LEX-PARIVAAR-01, MW-LEX-NANA-01, MW-SCRIPT-NANA-01, MW-LEX-NANI-01, MW-SCRIPT-NANI-01, MW-LEX-PATI-01, MW-SCRIPT-PATI-01, MW-LEX-PATNI-01, MW-SCRIPT-PATNI-01, MW-LEX-BACHCHA-01, MW-SCRIPT-BACHCHA-01, MW-SCRIPT-TA-01, MW-SCRIPT-CHA-01] -->

1. **Listen:** identify six randomly heard labels.
2. **Speak:** produce six different labels from relationship cues.
3. **Read:** match all twelve printed labels to meanings.
4. **Write:** write four heard labels without a model.

Score each skill separately. A strong spoken score cannot compensate for a
missed reading or writing score.
