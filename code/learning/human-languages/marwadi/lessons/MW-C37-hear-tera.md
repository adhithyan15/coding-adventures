## You'll want to know

> *terā* — **thirteen**

Three is *tīn*. Thirteen is *terā*: the **t** stays, the long **ī** flattens to
**e**, and the teens ending arrives. Two beats, both short of effort — *te-rā*.

You have now heard the pattern three times, and it is the pattern rather than the
words that is worth holding: **a consonant off the small number, then -ārā or
-erā**.
