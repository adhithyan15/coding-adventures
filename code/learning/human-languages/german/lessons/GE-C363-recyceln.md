## You'll want to know: recyceln
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C363-UMWELT-04]; assesses=[] -->

**recyceln** — "to recycle".

> Wir recyceln Papier, Glas und Plastik.
>
> *We recycle paper, glass and plastic.*

Said the English way: *ri-SAI-keln*. Perfekt: *hat recycelt*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*recyceln* was borrowed from English *recycle* (EtymDB), built from *re-* "again" and *cycle* "a round", which goes back through Latin *cyclus* to Greek *kýklos* "a ring".
