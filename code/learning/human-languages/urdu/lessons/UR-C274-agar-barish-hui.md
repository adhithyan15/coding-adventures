# اگر بارش ہوئی (agar bārish huī) — if it rains

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[UR-GRAMMAR-C274-IF-01, UR-GRAMMAR-C265-FUTURE-02, UR-LEX-BARISH] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "it will rain". (*bārish ho gī*.) Then "if you have time, come". (*agar vaqt hai, to āiye*.) Now a condition about tomorrow.

## You'll want to know: the plain past after اگر
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-GRAMMAR-C274-REAL-01]; assesses=[UR-LEX-BARISH, UR-LEX-C50-VERBS50-04, UR-LEX-C80-TOWN80-03, UR-LEX-C271-CHHUTNA-01, UR-GRAMMAR-C265-FUTURE-01] -->

> **اگر بارش ہوئی،**
>
> *agar bārish huī,*
>
> **تو ہم گھر پر رہیں گے۔**
>
> *to ham ghar par raheṅ ge.* — "If it rains, we'll stay at home."

**ہوئی** (*huī*) is the plain past, but the rain has not come: Urdu pictures it as already there, and the result is in the future.

> **اگر گاڑی چھوٹ گئی،**
>
> *agar gāṛī chhūṭ gaī,*
>
> **تو ہم بس سے جائیں گے۔**
>
> *to ham bas se jāeṅ ge.* — "If we miss the train, we'll go by bus."

## You'll want to know: the future after اگر
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-GRAMMAR-C274-REAL-02]; assesses=[UR-LEX-C76-TIME76-03, UR-LEX-C100-LOC100-04, UR-GRAMMAR-C265-FUTURE-01] -->

> **اگر وہ کل آئے گی،**
>
> *agar voh kal āe gī,*
>
> **تو ہم بازار جائیں گے۔**
>
> *to ham bāzār jāeṅ ge.* — "If she comes tomorrow, we'll go to the market."

The future can stand in both halves. Either way, the result is what will happen.
