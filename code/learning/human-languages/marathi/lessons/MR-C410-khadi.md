## You'll want to know: खाडी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C410-KHADI410-02]; assesses=[] -->

**खाडी** — *khāḍī* — "a creek; an inlet".

> खाडीत मच्छीमारांच्या होड्या उभ्या आहेत.
>
> *khāḍīt macchīmārāñcyā hoḍyā ubhyā āhet.*
>
> Fishermen’s boats are standing in the creek.

Feminine.

Where the sea runs inland between the hills of the coast.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**खाडी** (*khāḍī*) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi *khāḍī*, "creek, inlet", under *\*khaḍḍa*, "hole, pit" (CDIAL 3790).
