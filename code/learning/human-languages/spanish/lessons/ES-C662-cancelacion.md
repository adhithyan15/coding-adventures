## You'll want to know: la cancelación
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C662-RESERVA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**la cancelación** — "the cancellation".

> La cancelación es gratis hasta el viernes.
>
> *Cancellation is free until Friday.*

*Un seguro de cancelación* — cancellation insurance.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*cancelación* is built on *cancelar*, a learned borrowing from Latin *cancellāre* "to cross out with lines like a lattice", from *cancellī* "bars".
