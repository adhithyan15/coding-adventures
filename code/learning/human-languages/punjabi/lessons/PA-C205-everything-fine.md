## Grammar Lens
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-LEX-SABH-205, PA-LEX-THIK-03, PA-LEX-HAI-02] -->

> **ਸਭ ਠੀਕ ਹੈ** — *sabh thik hai* — everything is fine

For this one frame, put the whole word **ਸਭ** before familiar **ਠੀਕ ਹੈ**. Here
“all” is naturally understood as “everything.” Nothing else is added.
