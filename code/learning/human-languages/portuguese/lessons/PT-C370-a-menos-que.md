## You'll want to know: a menos que
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C370-SENAO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**a menos que** — "unless".

> Vamos a pé, a menos que chova.
>
> *We will walk, unless it rains.*

Takes the subjunctive.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*a menos que* is built from *a*, *menos* "less", inherited from Latin *minus* "less" (Lewis & Short), and *que*.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.
