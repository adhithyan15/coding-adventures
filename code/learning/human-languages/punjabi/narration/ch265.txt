Punjabi, chapter 265: This Side and That.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ਉਰੇ (ure) — on this side; nearer.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for a pole, then the Punjabi for a corner.

You'll want to know: ਉਰੇ.
ਉਰੇ — ure — "on this side; nearer".
ਬੱਸ ਪੁਲ ਤੋਂ ਉਰੇ ਰੁਕਦੀ ਹੈ। bass pul ton ure rukdī hai. The bus stops on this side of the bridge.

The word, taken apart.
ਉਰੇ (ure) is inherited from Sanskrit avara, "lower, hinder, nearer"; CDIAL lists Punjabi ure, "on this side", under it (CDIAL 805).

Lesson 3 of 5.
ਵਿਚਕਾਰ (vichkār) — in the middle; between.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for on this side, then the Punjabi for farther off.

You'll want to know: ਵਿਚਕਾਰ.
ਵਿਚਕਾਰ — vichkār — "in the middle; between".
ਬੈਂਕ ਸਕੂਲ ਅਤੇ ਦਫ਼ਤਰ ਦੇ ਵਿਚਕਾਰ ਹੈ। baiṅk sakūl ate daftar de vichkār hai. The bank is between the school and the office.

The word, taken apart.
ਵਿਚਕਾਰ (vichkār) is built on vicc, "middle", from a reconstructed vīcya, "middle"; CDIAL lists Punjabi vicc under it (CDIAL 12042). How the ending -kār joined it is not settled.

ਸ਼ਹਿਰ ਦੀ ਵਾਟ ਲੰਮੀ ਹੈ। shahir dī vāṭ lammī hai. The way to the town is long.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ਕੋਹ (koh) — a kos, about two miles.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for in the middle, then the Punjabi for the distance to travel.

You'll want to know: ਕੋਹ.
ਕੋਹ — koh — "a kos, about two miles".
ਮੇਰਾ ਪਿੰਡ ਸ਼ਹਿਰ ਤੋਂ ਦੋ ਕੋਹ ਦੂਰ ਹੈ। merā piṇḍ shahir ton do koh dūr hai. My village is two kos from the town.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ਕੋਹ (koh) is inherited from Sanskrit krośa, "a shout; a measure of distance, as far as a voice carries"; CDIAL lists Punjabi koh, "a distance of about two miles", under it (CDIAL 3611).
