Portuguese, chapter 411: Um carro de aluguer.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
o aluguer — the hire, the rental.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for tarmac, then the Portuguese for to wind.

You'll want to know: o aluguer.
o aluguer — "the hire, the rental".
O aluguer do carro custa quarenta euros por dia. The car hire costs forty euros a day.
In Brazil: o aluguel. The verb is alugar.

The word, taken apart.
aluguer is inherited through Old Portuguese aloguer from Latin allocāre 'to place, to hire out' (EtymDB), built on locus 'place'.

Lesson 2 of 5.

You'll want to know: o condutor.
o condutor — "the driver".
O segundo condutor também tem de mostrar a carta. The second driver also has to show his licence.
A carta de condução — the driving licence. O motorista drives for a living.

The word, taken apart.
condutor is a learned borrowing from Latin conductor (EtymDB), from condūcere 'to lead together', built on dūcere 'to lead'.

Lesson 3 of 5.
a matrícula — the number plate; the registration; the enrolment.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for the hire, then the Portuguese for the driver.

You'll want to know: a matrícula.
a matrícula — "the number plate; the registration; the enrolment".
Um carro com matrícula estrangeira parou à porta. A car with foreign number plates stopped at the door.
At school or university, a matrícula is enrolment.

The word, taken apart.
matrícula is a learned borrowing from Late Latin mātrīcula 'list, register' (EtymDB), a small form of mātrīx, built on māter 'mother'.

Lesson 4 of 5.
a quilometragem — the mileage (in kilometres).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for the driver, then the Portuguese for the number plate.

You'll want to know: a quilometragem.
a quilometragem — "the mileage (in kilometres)".
Neste aluguer, a quilometragem é ilimitada. With this hire, the mileage is unlimited.
Built on o quilómetro.

The word, taken apart.
quilometragem is built on quilómetro with -agem. Quilómetro is a learned coinage from Greek khílioi 'a thousand' and métron 'measure'; EtymDB has no entry for quilómetro.

Lesson 5 of 5.
a franquia — the excess (on insurance); the franchise.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for the number plate, then the Portuguese for the mileage.

You'll want to know: a franquia.
a franquia — "the excess (on insurance); the franchise".
Com este seguro, a franquia é de trezentos euros. With this insurance, the excess is three hundred euros.
The excess is the part you pay yourself after an accident.

The word, taken apart.
franquia is built on franco 'free, exempt', which was borrowed from French franc (EtymDB), first the name of the Franks: a franquia was a freedom from a charge.
