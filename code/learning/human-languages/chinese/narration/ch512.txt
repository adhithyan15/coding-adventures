Mandarin Chinese, chapter 512: Dreams.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
zhìxiàng — ambition in life; the goal one sets oneself.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for one's own (by birth), then the Mandarin for personally (doing it oneself).

You'll want to know: zhìxiàng.
zhìxiàng — "ambition in life; the goal one sets oneself".
wǒ zuì dà de zhìxiàng shì dāng yīshēng — My greatest ambition is to be a doctor.
Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
zhìxiàng is built from zhì 'a longing' and xiàng 'to face', both inherited from Middle Chinese, tsyiH and xjangH (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'what the mind longs for' and the second 'to face'.

Lesson 3 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for one's ambition in life, then the Mandarin for to pursue (a goal).

You'll want to know: 成才.
成才 — chéngcái — "to make something of oneself".
tā cóngxiǎo nǔlì xuéxí hòulái chéngcái le — He studied hard from childhood and later made something of himself.

The word, taken apart.
成才 (chéngcái) is built from 成 'to become' and 才 'talent', both inherited from Middle Chinese, dzyeng and dzoj (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to complete' and the second 'ability' and 'a gift for letters'.

Lesson 5 of 5.
名校 (míngxiào) — top school; famous university.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for to make something of oneself, then the Mandarin for to become famous.

You'll want to know: 名校.
名校 — míngxiào — "top school; famous university".
我想考上名校 wǒ xiǎng kǎoshàng míngxiào — I want to get into a top school.

The word, taken apart.
名校 (míngxiào) is built from 名 'a name' and 校 'a school', both inherited from Middle Chinese, mjieng and haewH (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second as the title of an officer; the sense 'a school' is not given there.
