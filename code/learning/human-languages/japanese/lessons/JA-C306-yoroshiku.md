## You'll want to know: よろしく
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C306-AISATSU306-02]; assesses=[] -->

**よろしく** — *yoroshiku* — "kind regards; please remember me".

> **せんせいに よろしく いって ください。** — *sensei ni yoroshiku itte kudasai* — "Please give my regards to your teacher."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**よろしく** (*yoroshiku*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, and it is usually written in kana (JMdict).

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
