## You'll want to know: auswendig
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C376-LERNEN-02]; assesses=[] -->

**auswendig** — "by heart".

> Die Kinder lernen das Lied auswendig.
>
> *The children are learning the song by heart.*

*Auswendig lernen* — to learn by heart; *auswendig können* — to know by heart.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*auswendig* is built from *aus* "out" and *wenden* "to turn", Old High German *wenten* (EtymDB), and first meant "on the outside": you can say the text without looking into the book.
