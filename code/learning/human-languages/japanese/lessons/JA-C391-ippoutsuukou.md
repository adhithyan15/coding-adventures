## You'll want to know: いっぽうつうこう
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C391-OUDAN391-03]; assesses=[] -->

**いっぽうつうこう** — *ippōtsūkō* — "one-way traffic".

> **この みちは いっぽうつうこうです。** — *kono michi wa ippōtsūkō desu* — "This street is one-way."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**いっぽうつうこう** (*ippōtsūkō*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): four Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *ichi*, "one" (Middle Chinese *'jit*), *hō*, "direction" (*pjang*), *tsū*, "traffic" (*thuwng*) and *kō*, "going" (*haeng*), joined as *ippōtsūkō* (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
