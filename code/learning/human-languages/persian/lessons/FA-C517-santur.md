## You'll want to know: سنتور
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C517-INSTRUMENTS-04]; assesses=[] -->

**سنتور** (*santur*) — "a santur (a hammered dulcimer)".

> صدای سنتور خیلی آرام است.
>
> *sedâ-ye santur kheyli ârâm ast.*
>
> The sound of the santur is very soft.

Played with two light hammers: *santur zadan*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*santur* is the name of a Persian instrument, but EtymDB lists it without a source, and its origin is not settled.
