Tamil, chapter 104: Language, Wedding, Birthday.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
மொழி (moḻi) — a language.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for a family name, then the Tamil for a pet name.

You'll want to know: மொழி.
மொழி (moḻi) — "a language".
தமிழ் என் மொழி (moḻi) — "Tamil is my language". தாய்மொழி is "mother tongue".

What you've built.
A language.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: moḻi]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: moḻi, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say tamiḻ eṉ moḻi]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Tamil for a family name, then the Tamil for a pet name, then say moḻi again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does மொழி (moḻi) mean? ("A language".) Say it once more.
