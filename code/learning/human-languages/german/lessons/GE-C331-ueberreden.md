## You'll want to know: überreden
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C331-UEBERZEUGEN-04]; assesses=[] -->

**überreden** — "to talk (someone) into, to persuade".

> Meine Freundin hat mich überredet, mit ins Kino zu kommen.
>
> *My friend talked me into coming along to the cinema.*

Inseparable: *sie überredet, sie hat überredet*. *Überreden* works with pressure and many words; *überzeugen* works with reasons.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*überreden* is built from *über* "over" and *reden* "to talk", inherited from Middle High German *reden* and Old High German *redōn* "to speak" (EtymDB): you talk someone over to your side.
