## You'll want to know: नींद
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C166-JAG166-03]; assesses=[] -->

**नींद** — *nī̃d* — "sleep".

> आज म्हारी नींद पूरी कोनी।
>
> *āj mhārī nī̃d pūrī konī.*
>
> I haven't had my full sleep today.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**नींद** (*nī̃d*) is inherited from Sanskrit *nidrā*, "sleep"; CDIAL lists Old Marwari *nīda* (CDIAL 7200). It is feminine: **म्हारी नींद**.
