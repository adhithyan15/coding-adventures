## You'll want to know: लग्न
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C316-LAGNA316-01]; assesses=[] -->

**लग्न** — *lagna* — "a wedding; a marriage".

> माझ्या मावशीचं लग्न पुढच्या महिन्यात आहे.
>
> *mājhyā māvśīcaṁ lagna puḍhacyā mahinyāt āhe.*
>
> My aunt's wedding is next month.

Neuter.

लग्न होणे, to get married; X-शी लग्न करणे, to marry X.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**लग्न** (*lagna*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *lagna*, 'attached, joined, fixed' (Monier-Williams): a joining of two people and two families.
