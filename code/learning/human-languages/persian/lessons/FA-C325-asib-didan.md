## You'll want to know: آسیب دیدن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C325-ZAMINKHORDAN-03]; assesses=[] -->

**آسیب دیدن** (*âsib didan*) — "to get hurt, to be damaged".

> دو نفر در این حادثه آسیب دیدند.
>
> *Do nafar dar in hâdese âsib didand.*
>
> Two people were hurt in this accident.

Literally "to see harm". Present stem *bin-*: *âsib mibinad*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*âsib* "harm, injury" and *didan* "to see" are both native Persian words; EtymDB lists no parent for either.
