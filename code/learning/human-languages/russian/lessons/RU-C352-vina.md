## You'll want to know: вина
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C352-VINA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**вина** (*viná*) — "guilt, fault, blame".

> Это не твоя вина.
>
> *Éto ne tvoyá viná.*
>
> It is not your fault.

Feminine, stressed on the end. *По моей вине* — through my fault. Not to be confused with *вино*, wine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**вина** is usually traced to Proto-Slavic *\*vina* "guilt, cause"; it is the root of *извиняться* "to apologise", which asks to be taken out of guilt.
