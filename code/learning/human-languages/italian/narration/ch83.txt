il ponte — a bridge.

You'll want to know: il ponte.
il ponte — "a bridge".

The word, taken apart.
ponte from Latin pons, pontis, "bridge" — inherited. (EtymDB 2.1).

Lesson 3 of 5.
la chiesa — a church.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for a bridge, then the Italian for a park.

You'll want to know: la chiesa.
la chiesa — "a church".

The word, taken apart.
chiesa from Latin ecclesia, "an assembly", later "the church" — inherited; Latin took it from Greek ekklesia. EtymDB 2.1 has no row for chiesa; it derives Venetian ciesa from ecclesia.
