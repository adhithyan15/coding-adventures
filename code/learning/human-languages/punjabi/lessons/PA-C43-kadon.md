## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-Q-KADON-01]; assesses=[PA-JOIN-JADON-01] -->

Put them side by side. This is the neatest pattern in the chapter:

> **ਕਦੋਂ** — *when?* · **ਜਦੋਂ** — *when …*

**One letter apart.** The **ਕ** asks; the **ਜ** answers. You learned the answer
two chapters ago without knowing it was half of a pair, and the question costs
you almost nothing now.

That pairing runs through the whole language, and you have now seen it three
times: **ਜੇ … ਤਾਂ**, **ਜੋ … ਉਹ**, and now **ਕਦੋਂ** against **ਜਦੋਂ**.
