## You'll want to know: souple
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C370-IMPREVU-05]; assesses=[] -->

**souple** — "supple; easygoing, adaptable".

> Mes horaires sont souples : je peux venir plus tard.
>
> *My hours are adaptable: I can come later.*

Of the body: supple. Of a person or a rule: easygoing.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*souple* is inherited from Latin *supplex* "bending the knee, humble" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short), built on *plicāre* "to fold".
