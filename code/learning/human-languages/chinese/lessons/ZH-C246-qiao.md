## You'll want to know: qiáo
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C246-HILLS246-02]; assesses=[] -->

*qiáo* — "bridge".

> *cūnzi lǐ de qiáo hěn lǎo* — The bridge in the village is very old.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*qiáo* is inherited from Middle Chinese *gjew* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'a beam over water'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what I do.
