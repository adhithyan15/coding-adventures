## You'll want to know: la pattuglia
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C294-PATTUGLIA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**la pattuglia** — "the patrol".

> Una pattuglia è arrivata in cinque minuti.
>
> *A patrol arrived in five minutes.*

*Essere di pattuglia* — to be on patrol.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*pattuglia* was borrowed from French *patrouille* "patrol" (EtymDB).
