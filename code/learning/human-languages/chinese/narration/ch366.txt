Mandarin Chinese, chapter 366: Take My Advice.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
bìmiǎn — to avoid.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for might as well, then the Mandarin for a word of advice.

You'll want to know: bìmiǎn.
bìmiǎn — "to avoid".
wǒmen yīnggāi bìmiǎn chūcuò — We should try not to slip up.
Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
bìmiǎn is built from bì and miǎn, both inherited from Middle Chinese, bjieH and mjenX (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to turn away' and the second 'to stop' and 'to escape'.

Lesson 2 of 5.
当心 (dāngxīn) — to watch out; to look out.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for a word of advice, then the Mandarin for to avoid.

You'll want to know: 当心.
当心 — dāngxīn — "to watch out; to look out".
下雨的时候开车必须当心 xiàyǔ de shíhou kāichē bìxū dāngxīn — You must take care driving in the rain.

The word, taken apart.
当心 (dāngxīn) is built from 当 and 心 'the heart', both inherited from Middle Chinese, tang and sim (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to match' and 'to preside'. How the pair came to mean watching out is not settled.

The word, taken apart.
zhíde is built from zhí and 得 'to get', both inherited from Middle Chinese, driH and tok (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to hold' and 'to meet with', and the second 'gain'. The second is said light here.
