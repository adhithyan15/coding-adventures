## You'll want to know: засоряться
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C432-PROTEKAT-03]; assesses=[] -->

**засоряться** (*zasoryátsya*) — "to get blocked, to clog".

> В старых домах часто засоряются трубы.
>
> *V stárykh domákh chásto zasoryáyutsya trúby.*
>
> In old houses the pipes often get blocked.

Imperfective; perfective *засориться*. *Раковина засорилась* — the sink is blocked.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*засоряться* is built from *за-*, *сор* 'litter, rubbish' and *-ся*: to fill up with rubbish. EtymDB lists *сор* without a source.
