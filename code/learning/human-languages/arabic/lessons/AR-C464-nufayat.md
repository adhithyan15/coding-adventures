## You'll want to know: نفايات
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C464-TALAWWUTH464-03]; assesses=[] -->

**نفايات** — *nufāyāt* — "waste; refuse".

> جمع العمال النفايات من الشارع.
>
> *jamaʿa al-ʿummāl an-nufāyāt min ash-shāriʿ.*
>
> The workers collected the waste from the street.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**نفايات** (*nufāyāt*) is built on the native Arabic root *n-f-y*, "to drive away, to cast out" (Lane). Waste is what is cast out.
