## You'll want to know: спасение
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C296-SPASATEL-03]; assesses=[] -->

**спасение** (*spaséniye*) — "rescue, salvation".

> Спасение пришло в последнюю минуту.
>
> *Spaséniye prishló v poslédnyuyu minútu.*
>
> Rescue came at the last minute.

Neuter.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*спасение* is built on *спасти* "to save", the perfective of *спасать*, Old East Slavic *sŭpasti*, from *pasti* "to herd, to guard" (EtymDB).
