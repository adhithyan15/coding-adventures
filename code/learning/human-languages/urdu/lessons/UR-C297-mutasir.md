## You'll want to know: متاثر
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C297-RAHAT297-05]; assesses=[] -->

**متاثر** — *mutāsir* — "affected (by a disaster)".

> سیلاب سے دو گاؤں متاثر ہوئے۔
>
> *sailāb se do gāoṅ mutāsir hue.*
>
> Two villages were affected by the flood.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**متاثر** (*mutāsir*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *mutaʾaththir*, "one that has taken an effect", a word from the same family as **اثر**.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
