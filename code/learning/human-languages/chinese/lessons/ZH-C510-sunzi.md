## You'll want to know: sūnzi
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C510-GRAND510-02]; assesses=[] -->

*sūnzi* — "grandson".

> *yéye zuì xǐhuan tā de sūnzi* — Grandpa loves his grandson most of all.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*sūnzi* is built from *sūn* 'a grandchild' and 子 'a son', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *swon* and *tsiX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first, after an older dictionary, 'the son of a son'.
