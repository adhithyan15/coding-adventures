## You'll want to know: गमणो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C243-CHOR243-04]; assesses=[] -->

**गमणो** — *gamṇo* — "to go missing; to get lost".

> म्हारी अंगूठी गमी।
>
> *mhārī aṅgūṭhī gamī.*
>
> My ring went missing.

Past (it …): वो रुपियो गम्यो, *vo rupiyo gamyo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**गमणो** (*gamṇo*) is inherited from Sanskrit *gamyate*, "one goes". CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry; it lists Hindi *gamnā*, "to be lost" (CDIAL 4033). The taught **गमावणो** (*gamāvṇo*), "to lose", is its causative partner.
