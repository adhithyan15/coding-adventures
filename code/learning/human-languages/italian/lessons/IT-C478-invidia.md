## You'll want to know: l'invidia
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C478-VERGOGNA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**l'invidia** — "envy".

> Nella sua voce c'era un po' di invidia.
>
> *There was a little envy in his voice.*

The adjective is *invidioso*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*invidia* is a learned borrowing from Latin *invidia* "envy, ill will" (EtymDB), from *invidēre* "to eye with ill will", built on *vidēre* "to see".
