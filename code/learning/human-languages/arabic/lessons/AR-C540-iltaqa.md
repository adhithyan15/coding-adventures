## You'll want to know: التقى
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C540-NADI540-03]; assesses=[] -->

**التقى** — *iltaqā* — "to meet up (with)".

> التقيت بزميلي في المقهى.
>
> *iltaqaytu bi-zamīlī fī al-maqhā.*
>
> I met up with my colleague at the café.

Present: يلتقي, *yaltaqī*.

It takes بـ, or a direct object.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**التقى** (*iltaqā*) is built on the native Arabic root *l-q-y*, "to meet; to throw" (Lane). Ilā l-liqāʾ, see you, which you know, is from the same root.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
