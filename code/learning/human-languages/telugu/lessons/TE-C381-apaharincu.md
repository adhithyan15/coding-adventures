# అపహరించు (apahariñcu) — to abduct; to carry off

## You'll want to know: అపహరించు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C381-SAHASAM381-02]; assesses=[] -->

**అపహరించు** — *apahariñcu* — "to abduct; to carry off".

> దొంగలు ఒక వ్యాపారిని అపహరించారు.
>
> *doṅgalu oka vyāpārini apahariñcāru.*
>
> The robbers abducted a merchant.

Past (I …): అపహరించాను, *apahariñcānu*.

A formal word: to kidnap a person, or to steal goods.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**అపహరించు** (*apahariñcu*) is built on Sanskrit *apaharaṇa*, "taking away, carrying off; stealing" (Monier-Williams), with the Telugu verb ending *-iñcu*.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
