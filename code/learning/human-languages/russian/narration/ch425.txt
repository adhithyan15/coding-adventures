Russian, chapter 425: Seasickness.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
отплывать (otplyvát) — to sail off, to set sail.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a mooring, then the Russian for a landing stage.

You'll want to know: отплывать.
отплывать (otplyvát) — "to sail off, to set sail".
Корабль отплывает ровно в полдень. Korábl otplyváyet róvno v pólden. The ship sets sail at exactly noon.
Imperfective; perfective отплыть (отплыву, отплывёшь).

The word, taken apart.
отплывать (otplyvát) is built from от- 'away' and -плывать, from плыть 'to swim, to sail', which is inherited from Proto-Slavic plyti (EtymDB).

Lesson 2 of 5.
причаливать (prichálivat) — to moor, to dock.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a landing stage, then the Russian for to sail off.

You'll want to know: причаливать.
причаливать (prichálivat) — "to moor, to dock".
Паром причаливает к пристани. Paróm prichálivayet k prístani. The ferry docks at the landing stage.
Imperfective; perfective причалить. Takes к + dative.

The word, taken apart.
причаливать (prichálivat) is built from при- 'to' and -чаливать, from чалить 'to tie up (a boat)'. EtymDB lists чалить without a source, so its origin is left open.

Lesson 3 of 5.
качка (káchka) — the rolling (of a ship), the swell.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to sail off, then the Russian for to moor.

You'll want to know: качка.
качка (káchka) — "the rolling (of a ship), the swell".
Из-за качки мама не могла спать. Iz-za káchki máma ne moglá spat. Because of the rolling Mum could not sleep.
Feminine. Built on качать — to rock.

The word, taken apart.
качка (káchka) is built on качать 'to rock, to swing'; EtymDB lists both words without a deeper source.

Lesson 4 of 5.
укачивать (ukáchivat) — to make seasick or carsick; to rock to sleep.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to moor, then the Russian for the rolling.

You'll want to know: укачивать.
укачивать (ukáchivat) — "to make seasick or carsick; to rock to sleep".
Меня всегда укачивает на море. Menyá vsegdá ukáchivayet na móre. I always get seasick at sea.
Mostly impersonal with the accusative: меня укачивает — I get motion sick. Perfective укачать.

The word, taken apart.
укачивать (ukáchivat) is built from у- and -качивать, from качать 'to rock': to rock someone until they are drowsy or sick. EtymDB does not list the verb.

Lesson 5 of 5.
якорь (yákor) — an anchor.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for the rolling, then the Russian for to make seasick or carsick.

You'll want to know: якорь.
якорь (yákor) — "an anchor".
Корабль бросил якорь недалеко от берега. Korábl brósil yákor nedalekó ot bérega. The ship dropped anchor not far from the shore.
Masculine; plural якоря. Бросить якорь (yákor) — to drop anchor.

The word, taken apart.
якорь (yákor) comes from Old East Slavic ękorĭ (EtymDB), an old loan that is usually traced, perhaps through Norse, to Greek ánkyra 'an anchor'.
