## You'll want to know: la catastrophe
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C305-NAUFRAGE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**la catastrophe** — "the catastrophe, the disaster".

> Le tremblement de terre a été une catastrophe.
>
> *The earthquake was a catastrophe.*

Informal: *c'est la catastrophe !* — it is a disaster!

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*catastrophe* was borrowed from Latin *catastropha*, from Greek *katastrophḗ* "an overturning, a sudden end" (EtymDB).
