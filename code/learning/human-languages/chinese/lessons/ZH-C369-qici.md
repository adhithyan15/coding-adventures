## You'll want to know: qícì
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C369-CLEAR369-04]; assesses=[] -->

*qícì* — "secondly; second in order".

> *shǒuxiān yīnggāi zhǔnshí qícì yīnggāi yǒu lǐmào* — First, be on time; second, be polite.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*qícì* is built from *qí* and *cì*, both inherited from Middle Chinese, *gi* and *tshijH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first as a particle and the second 'order' and 'the next'.
