Russian, chapter 245: Strange, Funny, Wonderful, Terrible, Excellent.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
странный (stránnyy) — strange.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for dark, then the Russian for light.

You'll want to know: странный.
странный (stránnyy) — "strange".

What you've built.

Guided Practice.

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does странный (stránnyy) mean? ("Strange".) Say it once more.
