## You'll want to know: zīxún
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C454-INFO454-02]; assesses=[] -->

*zīxún* — "to make an inquiry; to ask for advice".

> *nǐ kěyǐ qù fúwùtái zīxún* — You can ask at the information desk.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*zīxún* is built from *zī* 'to consult' and *xún* 'to ask', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *tsij* and *swin* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to take counsel' and the second 'to consult'.
