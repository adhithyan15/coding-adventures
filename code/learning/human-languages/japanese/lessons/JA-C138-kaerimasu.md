## You'll want to know: かえります
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C138-KAERIMASU]; assesses=[] -->

**かえります** — *kaerimasu* — "I go home", "I go back", to the place you
belong to: your home, your town, your country.

Its dictionary form is **かえる**, *kaeru*. It ends in *-eru*, but it
follows the *-u* pattern of **あるく**, not the pattern of **おきる**: the
last **る** becomes **り**, and **ます** follows. So it is *kaer-i-masu*,
never *kaemasu*. A few common verbs in *-eru* and *-iru* work this way, and
a word list marks them.

> **うちに かえります。** — *uchi ni kaerimasu* — "I go home." The particle
> **に** marks where you are going.
