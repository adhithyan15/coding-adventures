## You'll want to know: beneiden
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C466-NEID-04]; assesses=[] -->

**beneiden** — "to envy".

> Ich beneide dich um deinen ruhigen Job.
>
> *I envy you your quiet job.*

*Jemanden um etwas beneiden* — to envy someone something. Inseparable: *hat beneidet*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*beneiden* is built on the prefix *be-* and *Neid* "envy", a Germanic word usually traced to Old High German *nīd* (EtymDB lists the verb without a source).
