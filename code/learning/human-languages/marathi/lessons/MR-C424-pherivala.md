## You'll want to know: फेरीवाला
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C424-VIKRI424-02]; assesses=[] -->

**फेरीवाला** — *pherīvālā* — "a hawker".

> फेरीवाला गल्लीत भाजी विकतो.
>
> *pherīvālā gallīt bhājī vikto.*
>
> The hawker sells vegetables in the lane.

Masculine.

फेरी, a round, and -वाला, the one who does it.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**फेरीवाला** (*pherīvālā*) is built from **फेरी**, "a round", from the family of *\*phirati*, "turns" (CDIAL 9078), and -वाला, "the one who".
