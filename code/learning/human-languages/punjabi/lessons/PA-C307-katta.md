## You'll want to know: ਕੱਟਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C307-BUFF307-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ਕੱਟਾ** — *kaṭṭā* — "a buffalo calf".

> ਮੱਝ ਦਾ ਕੱਟਾ ਛੋਟਾ ਹੈ।
>
> *majjh dā kaṭṭā chhoṭā hai.*
>
> The buffalo's calf is small.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਕੱਟਾ** (*kaṭṭā*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*kaṭṭa*, "young male animal"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *kaṭṭā*, "yearling buffalo", under it (CDIAL 2645).
