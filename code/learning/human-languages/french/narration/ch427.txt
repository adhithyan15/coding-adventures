French, chapter 427: Le confort de la chambre.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
la climatisation — the air conditioning.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for the stay, then the French for to unpack.

You'll want to know: la climatisation.
la climatisation — "the air conditioning".
La climatisation fait trop de bruit la nuit. The air conditioning makes too much noise at night.
In speech, la clim. Climatisé — air-conditioned.

The word, taken apart.
climatisation is built on climat 'climate', from Latin clima, a learned word from Greek klíma 'slope, region' (Lewis & Short). EtymDB lists it without a source.

chauffage is built on chauffer 'to heat' with -age. Chauffer is inherited from Latin calefacere 'to make warm', from calēre 'to be warm' (Lewis & Short).

Warm-up.

You'll want to know: la taie.
la taie — "the pillowcase".
La taie d'oreiller sent la lessive. The pillowcase smells of washing powder.
In full: la taie d'oreiller.

The word, taken apart.
taie comes from Old French teie (EtymDB), from Latin thēca 'case, cover', which came from Greek thḗkē 'box' (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 5 of 5.
le peignoir — the bathrobe, the dressing gown.

[pause 2 seconds]

le peignoir — "the bathrobe, the dressing gown".
Après la douche, il met le peignoir de l'hôtel. After the shower he puts on the hotel bathrobe.
Un peignoir de bain — a bathrobe.

The word, taken apart.
peignoir is built on peigner 'to comb', from Latin pectināre, from pecten 'comb' (Lewis & Short): first the gown worn while your hair was combed.
