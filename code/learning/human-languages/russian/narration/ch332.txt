Russian, chapter 332: In the oven.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
запекать (zapekát) — to bake, to roast.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to shape, then the Russian for a filling.

You'll want to know: запекать.
запекать (zapekát) — "to bake, to roast".
Мама запекает рыбу в духовке. Máma zapekáyet rýbu v dukhóvke. Mum is baking fish in the oven.
Imperfective; perfective запечь (zapéch), запеку, запечёшь (zapechyósh).

The word, taken apart.
запекать (zapekát) is built on за- and печь "to bake", an inherited Slavic verb (Proto-Slavic pekti); EtymDB lists no parent for the compound.

Masculine; genitive противня.

противень (prótiven) is usually traced to против "opposite", a tray set facing the fire; EtymDB lists no parent.

Lesson 3 of 5.
разогревать (razogrevát) — to heat up, to warm up.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to bake, then the Russian for a baking tray.

You'll want to know: разогревать.
разогревать (razogrevát) — "to heat up, to warm up".
Я разогрел суп на плите. Ya razogrél sup na plité. I heated up the soup on the stove.
Imperfective; perfective разогреть (razogrét), разогрею.

The word, taken apart.
разогревать (razogrevát) is built on раз-, о- and греть "to warm", inherited from Proto-Slavic grěti "to heat, to warm", from PIE gʷʰer- (EtymDB).

Warm-up.

You'll want to know: обжаривать.
обжаривать (obzhárivat) — "to brown, to fry lightly".
Он обжарил лук на масле. On obzháril luk na másle. He browned the onion in oil.
Imperfective; perfective обжарить (obzhárit). Жарить — to fry.

The word, taken apart.
обжаривать (obzhárivat) is built on об- "all round" and жарить "to fry", from жар "heat", the same Slavic root as гореть "to burn".
