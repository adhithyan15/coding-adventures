Lesson 1 of 5.
મૃત્યુ (mṛtyu) — death.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a gathered crowd, then the Gujarati for a push.

You'll want to know: મૃત્યુ.
મૃત્યુ — mṛtyu — "death".
અકસ્માતમાં એક માણસનું મૃત્યુ થયું. akasmātmā̃ ek māṇasnũ mṛtyu thayũ. One man died in the accident.
A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
મૃત્યુ (mṛtyu) is a tatsama, Sanskrit mṛtyu, "death, dying" (Monier-Williams). It is the word of news reports: તેનું મૃત્યુ થયું, he died.

Lesson 2 of 5.
શોક (śok) — mourning; grief.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a push, then the Gujarati for death.

You'll want to know: શોક.
શોક — śok — "mourning; grief".
આખા ગામમાં શોક છે. ākhā gāmmā̃ śok che. The whole village is in mourning.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
શોક (śok) is a tatsama, Sanskrit śoka, "burning, sorrow" (Monier-Williams). It is grief that is shared and shown: શોક પાળવો, to keep mourning.

આઘાત — āghāt — "a shock (of bad news)".
દીકરાના મૃત્યુથી માતાને મોટો આઘાત લાગ્યો. dīkrānā mṛtyuthī mātāne moṭo āghāt lāgyo. Her son's death came as a terrible shock to the mother.

The word, taken apart.
આઘાત (āghāt) is a tatsama, Sanskrit āghāta, "striking, a blow" (Monier-Williams): bad news that hits like a blow. It લાગે છે, strikes you: મોટો આઘાત લાગ્યો.

Lesson 5 of 5.
સહેવું (sahevũ) — to bear; to endure.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a shock, then the Gujarati for consolation.

You'll want to know: સહેવું.
સહેવું — sahevũ — "to bear; to endure".
માતાએ મોટું દુઃખ સહ્યું. mātāe moṭũ duḥkh sahyũ. The mother bore a great sorrow.
Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે દુઃખ સહ્યું, teṇe duḥkh sahyũ.

The word, taken apart.
સહેવું (sahevũ) is inherited from Sanskrit sáhatē, "conquers; bears", and CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb, "bears" (CDIAL 13304). Its past is સહ્યું, as કહેવું gives કહ્યું.
