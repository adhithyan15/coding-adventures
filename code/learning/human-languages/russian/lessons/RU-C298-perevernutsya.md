## You'll want to know: перевернуться
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C298-NAEZD-05]; assesses=[] -->

**перевернуться** (*perevernútsya*) — "to overturn, to roll over".

> Машина перевернулась на дороге.
>
> *Mashína perevernúlas na doróge.*
>
> The car overturned on the road.

Perfective; imperfective *переворачиваться* (*perevoráchivatsya*). Also of a person in bed: to turn over.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*перевернуться* is built on *пере-* "over" and *-вернуть*, the one-off form of *вертеть* "to turn" (EtymDB), inherited from Proto-Slavic *vĭrtěti*, PIE *wert-* "to turn"; modern *вернуть* on its own means "to give back".
