# ગાંઠવું (gā̃ṭhvũ) — to heed; to pay attention to (someone)

## You'll want to know: ગાંઠવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C464-SANU464-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ગાંઠવું** — *gā̃ṭhvũ* — "to heed; to pay attention to (someone)".

> તોફાની દીકરો કોઈનું ગાંઠતો નથી.
>
> *tophānī dīkro koīnũ gā̃ṭhto nathī.*
>
> The naughty son heeds no one.

Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે કોઈનું ન ગાંઠ્યું, *teṇe koīnũ na gā̃ṭhyũ*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ગાંઠવું** (*gā̃ṭhvũ*) is inherited from Sanskrit *granthayati*, "ties"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb, "to tie" (CDIAL 4353), and Gujarati also uses it, mostly with a negative, for heeding someone.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
