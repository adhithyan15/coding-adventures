Marathi, chapter 400: Two Wheels and Three.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
वाहन (vāhan) — a vehicle.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a fellow traveller, then the Marathi for a wayfarer.

You'll want to know: वाहन.
वाहन — vāhan — "a vehicle".
या रस्त्यावर मोठ्या वाहनांना बंदी आहे. yā rastyāvar moṭhyā vāhanānnā bandī āhe. Large vehicles are banned on this road.
Neuter.
A formal, general word; the plural before an ending is वाहनां-: वाहनांना.

The word, taken apart.
वाहन (vāhan) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit vāhana, "conveying; a vehicle, a mount" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 2 of 5.
दुचाकी (ducākī) — a two-wheeler.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a wayfarer, then the Marathi for a vehicle.

You'll want to know: दुचाकी.
दुचाकी — ducākī — "a two-wheeler".
मीरा दुचाकीवरून कार्यालयात जाते. mīrā ducākīvarūn kāryālayāt jāte. Meera rides to the office on her two-wheeler.
Feminine.
Any scooter or motorbike; a three-wheeler is a तीनचाकी.

The word, taken apart.
दुचाकी (ducākī) is a Marathi compound of du-, "two", and चाक, "a wheel", inherited from Sanskrit cakra (CDIAL 4538), with the adjective ending -ई.

Lesson 3 of 5.
रिक्षा (rikṣā) — an autorickshaw.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a vehicle, then the Marathi for a two-wheeler.

You'll want to know: रिक्षा.
रिक्षा — rikṣā — "an autorickshaw".
स्टेशनवरून आम्ही रिक्षाने घरी आलो. sṭeśanvarūn āmhī rikṣāne gharī ālo. We came home from the station by autorickshaw.
Feminine.
The vehicle takes -ने for "by": रिक्षाने, बसने, by rickshaw, by bus.

The word, taken apart.
रिक्षा (rikṣā) is a loan from English rickshaw, which English took from Japanese; in Marathi it names the three-wheeled autorickshaw.

Lesson 5 of 5.
कोंबणे (kombṇe) — to stuff in; to cram.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for an autorickshaw, then the Marathi for a truck.

You'll want to know: कोंबणे.
कोंबणे — kombṇe — "to stuff in; to cram".
त्याने पिशवीत सगळे कपडे कोंबले. tyāne piśvīt sagḷe kapḍe komble. He stuffed all the clothes into the bag.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने कोंबलं, tyāne komblaṁ.
The container takes -त: पिशवीत कोंबणे (kombṇe); people crammed into a rickshaw are कोंबलेले.

The word, taken apart.
कोंबणे (kombṇe) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi kõbṇẽ, "to thump, thrust, force in, cram in", under kōbb, "strike" (CDIAL 3522).
