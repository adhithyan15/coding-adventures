Arabic, chapter 64: To go out, To work, To live, To wait, To come back.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
خرج (kharaja) — to go out (he went out).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to make, then the Arabic for to enter.
