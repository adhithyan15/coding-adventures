## You'll want to know: atténuer
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C353-REMEDE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**atténuer** — "to lessen, to soften".

> Ce médicament atténue la douleur.
>
> *This medicine eases the pain.*

*S'atténuer* — to fade, to ease.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*atténuer* is a learned borrowing from Latin *attenuāre* "to make thin, to weaken" (Lewis & Short), built on *tenuis* "thin".
