# এমনকি (emônki) — even

## You'll want to know: এমনকি
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C369-KINA369-02]; assesses=[] -->

**এমনকি** — *emônki* — "even".

> ঝড় হলে এমনকি বাসও বন্ধ থাকে।
>
> *jhôṛ hôle emônki bāso bôndhô thāke.*
>
> When there is a storm, even the buses stop running.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**এমনকি** (*emônki*) is এমন, 'such, so', with কি, 'what' (CDIAL 3164 lists Bengali ki): 'is it even so?' The source of এমন is not settled. The word marks the most surprising member of a list.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
