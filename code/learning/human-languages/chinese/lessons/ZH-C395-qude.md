## You'll want to know: 取得
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C395-RESULT395-02]; assesses=[] -->

**取得** — *qǔdé* — "to achieve; to obtain (through effort)".

> *tā qǔdé le hěn hǎo de chéngjì* — She got very good marks.

The new character, in pieces: 取 — eight strokes in two pieces: on the left the ear piece, six strokes; on the right 又, as at the foot of 变. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**取得** (*qǔdé*) is built from 取 'to take' and 得 'to get', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *tshjuX* and *tok* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to gather in' and 'to receive'.
