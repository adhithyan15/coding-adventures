# ಹವಾಗುಣ (havāguṇa) — climate

## You'll want to know: ಹವಾಗುಣ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C484-TAPAMANA484-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ಹವಾಗುಣ** — *havāguṇa* — "climate".

> ಗಿರಿಧಾಮದ ಹವಾಗುಣ ತಣ್ಣಗಿದೆ.
>
> *giridhāmada havāguṇa taṇṇagide.*
>
> The climate of the hill station is cool.

ಹವಾಮಾನ is the weather of a day; ಹವಾಗುಣ is the usual weather of a place.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಹವಾಗುಣ** (*havāguṇa*) is a Kannada compound of ಹವಾ, "air, weather", usually traced to Arabic *hawā*, and Sanskrit *guṇa*, "a quality" (CDIAL 4190).
