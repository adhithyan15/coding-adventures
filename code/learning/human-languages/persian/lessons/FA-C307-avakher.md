## You'll want to know: اواخر
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C307-AVAYEL-03]; assesses=[] -->

**اواخر** (*avâkher*) — "late in, the last part".

> اواخر پاییز اولین برف آمد.
>
> *Avâkher-e pâyiz avvalin barf âmad.*
>
> Late in the autumn the first snow fell.

Broken plural of *âkhar* "last": *avâkher-e shab* "late at night".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*avâkher* is the Arabic broken plural *awākhir* of *ākhir* "last" (EtymDB lists Arabic *ākhir*); Persian also has the singular as *âkhar*.
