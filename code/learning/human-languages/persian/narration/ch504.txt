Persian, chapter 504: Friends.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
دوستی (dusti) — friendship.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for spoiled, then the Persian for hide-and-seek.

You'll want to know: دوستی.
دوستی (dusti) — "friendship".
دوستی ما از دبیرستان شروع شد. dusti-ye mâ az dabirestân shoru' shod. Our friendship began in high school.
dust 'friend' + -i. As a modifier, 'friendly': mosâbeqe-ye dusti 'a friendly match'.

The word, taken apart.
dusti is built on native dust, 'friend', which is usually traced to Old Persian dauštā, 'beloved', with the noun ending -i. EtymDB lists dust without a source.

دوست شدن (dust shodan) — "to make friends".
او در مدرسه جدید زود با همه دوست شد. u dar madrese-ye jadid zud bâ hame dust shod. At the new school he quickly made friends with everyone.
bâ kasi dust shodan; dust 'friend' + shodan 'to become'.

The word, taken apart.
dust shodan is built on two inherited words: dust, 'friend', usually traced to Old Persian dauštā, 'beloved', and shodan, 'to become'.

رابطه (râbete) — "a relationship; a connection".
رابطه او با پدرش خیلی خوب است. râbete-ye u bâ pedarash kheyli khub ast. Her relationship with her father is very good.
Plural ravâbet (Arabic) in formal Persian; râbetehâ in speech.

بچه ها با غریبه (gharibe) ها حرف نمیزنند. bachchehâ bâ gharibehâ harf nemizanand. The children don't talk to strangers.

gharibe is built in Persian on gharib, 'strange', which was borrowed from Arabic ġarīb, 'outsider, foreigner' (EtymDB), with the native ending -e.

The word, taken apart.
hamdigar is built on two native Persian words: ham, 'together, same', which EtymDB traces to Middle Persian (EtymDB), and digar, 'other'.
