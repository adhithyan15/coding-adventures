## You'll want to know: plausible
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C367-HYPOTHESE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**plausible** — "plausible".

> Son explication est plausible.
>
> *His explanation is plausible.*

One form for both genders.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*plausible* is a learned borrowing from Latin *plausibilis* "deserving applause, acceptable" (Lewis & Short), from *plaudere* "to clap".
