## You'll want to know: اٹکنا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C368-HAL368-04]; assesses=[] -->

**اٹکنا** — *aṭaknā* — "to get stuck; to be held up".

> اگر میں راستے میں اٹک گیا تو فون کروں گا۔
>
> *agar maiṅ rāste meṅ aṭak gayā to fon karūṅ gā.*
>
> If I get stuck on the way, I will phone.

Past (it …): وہ ٹرک پل پر اٹک گیا, *voh ṭrak pul par aṭak gayā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**اٹکنا** (*aṭaknā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *aṭaknā*, "to stick", under *\*aṭṭakk*, a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 182).
