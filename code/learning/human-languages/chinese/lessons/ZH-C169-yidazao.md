## You'll want to know: 一大早
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C169-EARLY169-01]; assesses=[] -->

**一大早** — *yídàzǎo* — "first thing in the morning; at the crack of dawn".

> **一大早妈妈去买大米了** *yídàzǎo māma qù mǎi dàmǐ le* — First thing this morning my mum went out to buy rice.

The tone of 一 changes: yī is said yí before the fourth tone of dà.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**一大早** (*yídàzǎo*) is built from 一 'one', 大 'big' and 早 'early', all three inherited from Middle Chinese, *'jit*, *daH* and *tsawX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the last 'morning'.
