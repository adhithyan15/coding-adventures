## You'll want to know: પતાવવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C178-START178-02]; assesses=[] -->

**પતાવવું** — *patāvvũ* — "to finish, to settle".

> **તે આજે કામ પતાવશે.** — *te āje kām patāvśe* — "He will finish the work today."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**પતાવવું** (*patāvvũ*) is a tadbhava. CDIAL lists it, glossed "to have done with, settle", under Sanskrit *prāpta*, "reached, obtained" (CDIAL 8945).
