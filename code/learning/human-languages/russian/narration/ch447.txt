Russian, chapter 447: Repair shops.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
мастерская (masterskáya) — a workshop, a repair shop.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a drainpipe, then the Russian for a stall.

You'll want to know: мастерская.
мастерская (masterskáya) — "a workshop, a repair shop".
В нашем доме есть маленькая мастерская. V náshem dóme yest málenkaya masterskáya. There is a small workshop in our building.
An adjective used as a noun: в мастерской.

The word, taken apart.
мастерская (masterskáya) is built on мастер 'a master, a craftsman' with an adjective ending. EtymDB traces мастер through Old East Slavic and Old French maistre, which goes back to Latin magister 'a master'.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ремонт (remónt) — repairs, a repair; renovation.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a watchmaker, then the Russian for a locksmith.

You'll want to know: ремонт.
ремонт (remónt) — "repairs, a repair; renovation".
Ремонт туфель занял два дня. Remónt túfel zányal dva dnya. Repairing the shoes took two days.
Masculine. Ремонт квартиры — doing up a flat. The verb is ремонтировать.

The word, taken apart.
ремонт (remónt) is usually traced to French remonte; EtymDB links it with remonter 'to put back up, to restock'. The word probably first named fresh horses for the army.
