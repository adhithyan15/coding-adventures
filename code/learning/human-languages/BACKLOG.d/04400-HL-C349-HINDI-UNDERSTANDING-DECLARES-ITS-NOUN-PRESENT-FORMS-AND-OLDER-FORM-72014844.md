## HL-C349 — Hindi understanding declares its noun, present forms, and older form

After the future-pieces repair, Chapter 34's understanding lesson leaves a
second modern Hindi family outside its declaration. It explicitly names the
noun and stem **समझ**, builds and practises **समझता / समझती / समझते**, and
uses older **समुझना** to explain the verb's history, while the headword exposes
only **समझना**.

Declare the noun, three present forms, and older form beside the infinitive.
This makes the lesson's complete taught family visible to reports and downstream
study artifacts without adding new material.
