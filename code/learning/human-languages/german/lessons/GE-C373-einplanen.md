## You'll want to know: einplanen
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C373-UNVORHERGESEHEN-05]; assesses=[] -->

**einplanen** — "to allow for, to plan in".

> Für die Fahrt planen wir eine Stunde mehr ein.
>
> *We are allowing an extra hour for the drive.*

Separable: *wir planen ein*, *wir haben eingeplant*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*einplanen* is built from *ein* "in" and *planen* "to plan", from *Plan*, borrowed from French *plan* and going back to Latin *plānus* "flat" (EtymDB).
