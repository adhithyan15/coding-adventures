# লক্ষণ (lôkkhôn) — a symptom; a sign

## You'll want to know: লক্ষণ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C471-OSUKH471-02]; assesses=[] -->

**লক্ষণ** — *lôkkhôn* — "a symptom; a sign".

> জ্বর আর কাশি এই রোগের লক্ষণ।
>
> *jbôr ār kāshi ei roger lôkkhôn.*
>
> Fever and a cough are symptoms of this disease.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**লক্ষণ** (*lôkkhôn*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *lakṣaṇa*, "a mark, a sign", used also of the signs of a disease (Monier-Williams).
