## You'll want to know: ಒಂದು ನಿಮಿಷ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C86-WAIT-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ಒಂದು ನಿಮಿಷ** (*ondu nimiṣa*) — "one minute". **ಒಂದು** is "one", from the numbers
chapter; **ನಿಮಿಷ** (*nimiṣa*) is "minute".

Say it with a raised hand when you need a moment.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

A number you know and a new word for time.
