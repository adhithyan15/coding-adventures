## You'll want to know: いつまでも
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C356-JOUKEN356-05]; assesses=[] -->

**いつまでも** — *itsumademo* — "forever; for as long as you like".

> **じかんが あったら、いつまでも いて ください。** — *jikan ga attara, itsumademo ite kudasai* — "If you have time, stay as long as you like."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**いつまでも** (*itsumademo*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, and its older origin is not settled. JMdict notes that it is usually written in kana (JMdict).
