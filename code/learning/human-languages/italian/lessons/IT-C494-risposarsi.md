## You'll want to know: risposarsi
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C494-SEPARARSI-05]; assesses=[] -->

**risposarsi** — "to remarry".

> Dopo il divorzio mia madre si è risposata.
>
> *After the divorce my mother remarried.*

*Ri-* "again" plus *sposarsi*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*risposarsi* is built from *ri-* "again" and *sposarsi* "to marry", inherited from Latin *spōnsāre* "to betroth" (Lewis & Short), from *spondēre* "to promise".
