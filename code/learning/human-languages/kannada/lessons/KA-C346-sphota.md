## You'll want to know: ಸ್ಫೋಟ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C346-SPHOTA346-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸ್ಫೋಟ** — *sphōṭa* — "an explosion; a blast".

> ಕಾರ್ಖಾನೆಯಲ್ಲಿ ದೊಡ್ಡ ಸ್ಫೋಟ ಆಯಿತು.
>
> *kārkhāneyalli doḍḍa sphōṭa āyitu.*
>
> There was a big explosion at the factory.

Plural: ಸ್ಫೋಟಗಳು, *sphōṭagaḷu*.

ಸ್ಫೋಟಿಸು is to explode or to blow up.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸ್ಫೋಟ** (*sphōṭa*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *sphoṭa*, "bursting, opening, expansion" (Monier-Williams).
