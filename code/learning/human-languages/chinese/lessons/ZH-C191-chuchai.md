## You'll want to know: chūchāi
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C191-BACK191-05]; assesses=[] -->

*chūchāi* — "to travel for work; to go on a business trip".

> *bàba hòutiān yào chūchāi* — Dad is going away on business the day after tomorrow.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*chūchāi* is built from 出 'to go out' and *chāi* 'an errand'. The first is inherited from Middle Chinese *tsyhwit* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription); the dictionary lists the second under several readings, glossed 'uneven' and 'to choose', and gives no errand, so how the word came to mean a work trip is not settled.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for talking about plans.
