## You'll want to know: میچ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C521-MAICH521-01]; assesses=[] -->

**میچ** — *maich* — "a match (a game)".

> کل ہماری ٹیم کا میچ ہے۔
>
> *kal hamārī ṭīm kā maich hai.*
>
> Our team has a match tomorrow.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**میچ** (*maich*) is borrowed from English *match*.
