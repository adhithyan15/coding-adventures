## You'll want to know: тележка
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C416-PROVODNIK-04]; assesses=[] -->

**тележка** (*telézhka*) — "a trolley, a cart".

> На вокзале мы взяли тележку для багажа.
>
> *Na vokzále my vzyáli telézhku dlya bagázha.*
>
> At the station we took a trolley for the luggage.

Feminine; a diminutive of *телега* — a cart. In shops too: *тележка для покупок*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*тележка* is a diminutive of *телега* 'a cart' (EtymDB lists *тележка*). The origin of *телега* itself is not settled.
