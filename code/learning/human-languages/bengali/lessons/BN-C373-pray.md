## You'll want to know: প্রায়
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C373-EKEBARE373-04]; assesses=[] -->

**প্রায়** — *prāy* — "almost; nearly".

> কাজ প্রায় শেষ; কাল এলে দেখবে।
>
> *kāj prāy shesh; kāl ele dekhbe.*
>
> The work is almost finished; if you come tomorrow, you'll see.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**প্রায়** (*prāy*) is Sanskrit *prāya*, 'for the most part, almost' (Monier-Williams). With the ending -ই it gives the taught প্রায়ই, often.
