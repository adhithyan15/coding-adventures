# ತಲುಪು (talupu) — to reach; to arrive at

## You'll want to know: ತಲುಪು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C433-RAIL433-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ತಲುಪು** — *talupu* — "to reach; to arrive at".

> ರೈಲು ರಾತ್ರಿ ಹತ್ತು ಗಂಟೆಗೆ ಊರು ತಲುಪಿತು.
>
> *railu rātri hattu gaṇṭege ūru talupitu.*
>
> The train reached the town at ten at night.

Past (I …): ತಲುಪಿದೆ, *talupide*.

The place may take -ಅನ್ನು or no ending: ಊರನ್ನು ತಲುಪು, ಊರು ತಲುಪು. ತಲುಪಿಸು, to deliver, makes something else reach.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ತಲುಪು** (*talupu*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 3102 lists Kannada *talapu*, *talupu*, "to reach, to arrive", beside *talapisu*, "to cause to reach", the source of ತಲುಪಿಸು.
