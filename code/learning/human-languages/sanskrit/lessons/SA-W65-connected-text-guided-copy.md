# नमस्ते। अहं संस्कृतं वदामि। — two known sentences, one tiny text

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[SA-LEX-NAMASTE, SA-PATTERN-I-SPEAK-SANSKRIT, SA-SCRIPT-DANDA-01] -->

Read the whole text once: **नमस्ते। अहं संस्कृतं वदामि।** It says only two
things you already know: “Hello. I speak Sanskrit.” Every word, sign, conjunct,
and danda has already been taught. There is no new vocabulary here.

## Writing — guided copy of connected text
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[SA-LEX-NAMASTE, SA-PATTERN-I-SPEAK-SANSKRIT, SA-SCRIPT-DANDA-01, SA-SKILL-CONNECTED-READING] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: guided-copy -->

Keep the model visible. Copy **नमस्ते।** once. Leave one clear word space, then
copy **अहं संस्कृतं वदामि।** Read each word as your hand reaches it.

Copy the complete two-sentence text once more on a fresh line:

> **नमस्ते। अहं संस्कृतं वदामि।**

Check the two danda marks, the space after the first one, both anusvāra dots,
the vocalic-ṛ sign in **संस्कृतं**, and the joined **स्त** inside **नमस्ते**.
Credit requires the connected Devanagari text; romanization is not an answer.

## Wrap-up recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[SA-SCRIPT-DANDA-01] -->

How many sentences and how many danda marks did you write? (**Two and two.**)
