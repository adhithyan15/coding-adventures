## You'll want to know: میراث
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C353-DOWRAN-05]; assesses=[] -->

**میراث** (*mirâs*) — "heritage, a legacy".

> این بنای قدیمی میراث همه مردم است.
>
> *In banâ-ye qadimi mirâs-e hame-ye mardom ast.*
>
> This old building is the heritage of all the people.

Written with ث. *Mirâs-e farhangi* "cultural heritage".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*mirâs* is borrowed from Arabic *mīrāth*, "inheritance" (EtymDB).
