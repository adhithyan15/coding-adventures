## You'll want to know: చిలుకు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C549-GRHAM549-04]; assesses=[] -->

**చిలుకు** — *ciluku* — "to churn".

> అమ్మమ్మ పెరుగు చిలికి వెన్న తీసింది.
>
> *ammamma perugu ciliki venna tīsindi.*
>
> Grandma churned the curd and took out the butter.

Past (I …): చిలికాను, *cilikānu*.

Churning curd for butter or buttermilk.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**చిలుకు** (*ciluku*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 2570 lists Telugu *ciluku*, "to churn", with Tamil *ciluppu*, "to churn, to stir".
