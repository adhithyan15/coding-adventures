## You'll want to know: el rendimiento
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C593-TAREA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**el rendimiento** — "the performance, the output; the yield".

> Su rendimiento en el trabajo es excelente.
>
> *Her performance at work is excellent.*

*El rendimiento escolar* — performance at school.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*rendimiento* is built on *rendir* with *-miento* (EtymDB); *rendir* is inherited from spoken Latin *rendere*, Latin *reddere* 'to give back'.
