Portuguese, chapter 186: Different, Same, Similar, Fair, False.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
diferente — different.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for fearful, then the Portuguese for curious.

You'll want to know: diferente.
diferente — "different".

The word, taken apart.
diferente from Latin differens, differentis, "differing" (EtymDB 2.1) — a learned borrowing from Latin.

Lesson 3 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for different, then the Portuguese for the same.

You'll want to know: parecido / parecida.
parecido / parecida — "similar".

The word, taken apart.
parecido is the participle of the verb "to seem", which goes back to Latin parere, "to appear" (Lewis & Short).
