# सहणो (sahṇo) — to bear; to put up with

## You'll want to know: सहणो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C258-CHINTA258-04]; assesses=[] -->

**सहणो** — *sahṇo* — "to bear; to put up with".

> हूं दरद सहूं हूं।
>
> *hū̃ darad sahū̃ hū̃.*
>
> I bear the pain.

Past (I …): म्हैं दरद सह्यो, *mhaĩ darad sahyo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सहणो** (*sahṇo*) is inherited from Sanskrit *sahate*, "bears, suffers"; CDIAL lists Old Marwari *sahaï*, "bears", under it (CDIAL 13304).
