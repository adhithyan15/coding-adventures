## You'll want to know: geōmetria, geōmetriae
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C404-RHETORICA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**geōmetria, geōmetriae** — "geometry".

> Geōmetria terrae mēnsūram docet.
>
> *Geometry teaches the measuring of land.*

*geōmetria, geōmetriae* f., from Greek *geōmetria*, land-measuring: *gē*, earth + *metron*, a measure. English *geometry*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*geōmetria* was borrowed from Greek *geōmetria* "land-measuring" (EtymDB), built from *gē* "earth" and *metron* "a measure". English *geometry* comes from it.
