## You'll want to know: выдавать
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C451-POSOLSTVO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**выдавать** (*vydavát*) — "to issue, to hand out".

> Посольство выдаёт визы за неделю.
>
> *Posólstvo vydayót vízy za nedélyu.*
>
> The embassy issues visas within a week.

Imperfective; perfective *выдать*. Present *выдаю, выдаёшь*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*выдавать* is built from *вы-* 'out' and *давать* 'to give', which is inherited from Proto-Slavic *dāvati* (EtymDB).
