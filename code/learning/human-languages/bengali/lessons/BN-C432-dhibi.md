## You'll want to know: ঢিবি
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C432-CHURA432-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ঢিবি** — *ḍhibi* — "a mound; a heap of earth".

> মাঠের মাঝখানে একটা মাটির ঢিবি আছে।
>
> *māṭher mājhkhāne ekṭā māṭir ḍhibi āchhe.*
>
> There is a mound of earth in the middle of the field.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ঢিবি** (*ḍhibi*) is inherited: CDIAL lists Bengali *ḍhibi*, "a heap", under the reconstructed *ḍhappa*, "a lump" (CDIAL 5580).
