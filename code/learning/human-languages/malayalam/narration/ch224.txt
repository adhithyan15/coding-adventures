Malayalam, chapter 224: To mix, To serve, To taste, To fill, To cover.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
കലർത്തുക (kalarttuka) — to mix.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for to take off, then the Malayalam for to change.

You'll want to know: കലർത്തുക.
കലർത്തുക — kalarttuka — "to mix".

The word, taken apart.
കലർത്തുക (kalarttuka) is native Dravidian, the causative of the verb "to be mixed"; DEDR 1299 lists the root with Tamil kala, Kannada kali and Telugu kalayu. DEDR 1299 compares Sanskrit kalka.
