# தவிர (tavira) — except; apart from

## You'll want to know: தவிர
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C357-ENINUM357-03]; assesses=[] -->

**தவிர** — *tavira* — "except; apart from".

> ஞாயிறு தவிர எல்லா நாட்களிலும் கடை திறந்திருக்கும்.
>
> *ñāyiṟu tavira ellā nāṭkaḷilum kaṭai tiṟantirukkum.*
>
> The shop is open every day except Sunday.

It follows the noun: X தவிர, except X; அதைத் தவிர, apart from that.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**தவிர** (*tavira*) is native Dravidian: it is the infinitive of *tavir*, "to abstain, leave, avoid" (DEDR 3113), the verb தவிர் taught already: "leaving aside".
