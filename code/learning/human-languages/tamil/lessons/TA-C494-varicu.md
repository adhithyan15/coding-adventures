## You'll want to know: வாரிசு
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C494-MARAPU494-05]; assesses=[] -->

**வாரிசு** — *vāricu* — "an heir".

> அவருடைய மகன்தான் அந்தக் கடைக்கு வாரிசு.
>
> *avaruṭaiya makaṉtāṉ antak kaṭaikku vāricu.*
>
> His son is the heir to that shop.

The thing inherited takes -க்கு.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**வாரிசு** (*vāricu*) is usually traced to Arabic *wāriṯ*, "an heir", reaching Tamil through Hindustani; our sources do not document the path.
