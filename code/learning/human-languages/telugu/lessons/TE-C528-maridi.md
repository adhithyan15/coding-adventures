## You'll want to know: మరిది
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C528-TOBUTTUVU528-03]; assesses=[] -->

**మరిది** — *maridi* — "a husband's younger brother".

> పెళ్ళి తరువాత మా మరిది మాతోనే ఉన్నాడు.
>
> *peḷḷi taruvāta mā maridi mātōnē unnāḍu.*
>
> After the wedding my husband's younger brother lived with us.

Also a younger sister's husband.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**మరిది** (*maridi*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 4762 lists Telugu *maṟãdi*, "a spouse's younger brother, a younger sister's husband", beside *maṟãdalu*, the same for women.
