## You'll want to know: क्रोशः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C412-VIRAMA412-05]; assesses=[] -->

**क्रोशः** — *krośaḥ* — "a kos (a measure of distance)".

> ग्रामः इतः क्रोशं दूरम् अस्ति।
>
> *grāmaḥ itaḥ krośaṃ dūram asti.*
>
> The village is a kos away from here.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**क्रोशः** (*krośaḥ*) comes from the root √kruś, "to cry out": MW gives *krośa* as a cry and as a measure of distance, the range of the voice in calling, the kos; it lives on as Hindi *kos*, a distance of some two miles (MW; CDIAL 3611).
