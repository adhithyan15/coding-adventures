## You'll want to know: さがしまわる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C424-OTOSHIMONO424-04]; assesses=[] -->

**さがしまわる** — *sagashimawaru* — "to search all over".

> **さいふを さがしまわりました。** — *saifu o sagashimawarimashita* — "I searched everywhere for my wallet."

Polite **さがしまわります**, *sagashimawarimasu*; a godan verb. Past (polite): **あねが かぎを さがしまわりました。**, *ane ga kagi o sagashimawarimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**さがしまわる** (*sagashimawaru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, built from *sagashi*, "searching" (from *sagasu*) and *mawaru*, "to go round"; its kanji were attached later as kun'yomi (JMdict).
