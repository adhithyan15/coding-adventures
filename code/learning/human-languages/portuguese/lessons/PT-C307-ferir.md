## You'll want to know: ferir
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C307-FERIDO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ferir** — "to injure, to wound; to hurt".

> A queda feriu-lhe a perna.
>
> *The fall injured his leg.*

Present: *firo, feres, fere*. Also feelings: *ferir alguém*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ferir* is inherited from Latin *ferīre* "to strike" (EtymDB).
