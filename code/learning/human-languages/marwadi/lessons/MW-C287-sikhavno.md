## You'll want to know: सिखावणो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C287-GURU287-01]; assesses=[] -->

**सिखावणो** — *sikhāvṇo* — "to teach a skill".

> दादी सीवणो सिखावै है।
>
> *dādī sīvṇo sikhāvai hai.*
>
> Grandmother teaches sewing.

Past (I …): म्हैं गीत सिखायो, *mhaĩ gīt sikhāyo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सिखावणो** (*sikhāvṇo*) is built on **सीखणो**, to learn, as its causative: "to make learn". CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry, but lists Hindi *sikhānā*, "teaches", under Sanskrit *śikṣate* (CDIAL 12430).
