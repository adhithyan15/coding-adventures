## You'll want to know: なぜか
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C529-ANYWAY529-01]; assesses=[] -->

**なぜか** — *nazeka* — "for some reason".

> **なぜか あじは みずに よります。** — *nazeka aji wa mizu ni yorimasu* — "For some reason, the taste depends on the water."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**なぜか** (*nazeka*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, and usually written in kana: built from *naze*, "why", and the particle *ka* (JMdict).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
