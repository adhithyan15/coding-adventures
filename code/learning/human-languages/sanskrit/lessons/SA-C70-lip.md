## You'll want to know: ओष्ठः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C70-BODY2-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ओष्ठः** (*oṣṭhaḥ*) — "the lip". The sounds **प** and **म** are made with both lips.

## What you've built
