Marwadi (Marwari), chapter 52: To sit, To sleep, To get up, To walk, To run.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
बैठणो (baiṭhṇo) — to sit.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for to see, then the Marwadi for to hear.

You'll want to know: बैठणो.
बैठणो — baiṭhṇo — "to sit".

The word, taken apart.
बैठणो (baiṭhṇo) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from upaviśati, "sits down". CDIAL lists Old Marwari baiṭhaï under it (CDIAL 2245). The ṭh comes from its past participle, upaviṣṭa, "seated".

You'll want to know: उठणो.
उठणो — uṭhṇo — "to get up".

The word, taken apart.
उठणो (uṭhṇo) is a tadbhava, a word inherited and reshaped by sound change. It comes from ut-sthāti, "stands up", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants. CDIAL lists Old Marwari ūṭhaï under it (CDIAL 1900). It is built on ud, "up", and sthā, "stand".
