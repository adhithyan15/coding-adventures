## You'll want to know: नफो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C194-EARN194-04]; assesses=[] -->

**नफो** — *napho* — "a profit".

> आज दुकान मांय नफो कोनी।
>
> *āj dukān mā̃y napho konī.*
>
> The shop made no profit today.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**नफो** (*napho*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *nafʿ*, "profit, benefit".
