## You'll want to know: der Schaden
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C288-ZUSAMMENSTOSS-05]; assesses=[] -->

**der Schaden** — "the damage".

> Der Schaden am Auto war groß.
>
> *The damage to the car was great.*

Plural: *die Schäden*. *Schade!* — what a pity — comes from the same word.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Schaden* is usually traced to Old High German *scado* "damage", which EtymDB derives from Proto-Germanic *skaþô*; EtymDB lists English *scathe* as a cousin.
