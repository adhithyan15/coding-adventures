## You'll want to know: युक्तिवाद
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C343-MUDDA343-03]; assesses=[] -->

**युक्तिवाद** — *yuktivād* — "a reasoned argument".

> त्याचा युक्तिवाद ऐकून सगळे गप्प बसले.
>
> *tyācā yuktivād aikūn sagḷe gappa basle.*
>
> Hearing his argument, everyone fell silent.

Masculine.

युक्तिवाद करणे, to argue a case; a quarrel is भांडण.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**युक्तिवाद** (*yuktivād*) is built from two tatsamas, Sanskrit *yukti*, "contrivance, reasoning", and *vāda*, "speech, thesis" (Monier-Williams).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
