Portuguese, chapter 452: Cabelo e óculos.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
o barbeiro — the barber.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for the locksmith, then the Portuguese for to patch.

You'll want to know: o barbeiro.
o barbeiro — "the barber".
O barbeiro corta o cabelo ao meu avô há trinta anos. The barber has cut my grandfather's hair for thirty years.
Built on a barba.

The word, taken apart.
barbeiro is built from barba 'beard' and -eiro (EtymDB); barba is inherited from Latin barba.

What you've built.
One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.

Lesson 5 of 5.
a nódoa — the stain.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for the beautician, then the Portuguese for the optician.

You'll want to know: a nódoa.
a nódoa — "the stain".
Tenho uma nódoa de vinho na camisa. I have a wine stain on my shirt.
In Brazil: a mancha. Tirar uma nódoa — to get a stain out.

The word, taken apart.
nódoa is inherited from Latin notula 'little mark' (EtymDB), a small form of nota 'mark'.
