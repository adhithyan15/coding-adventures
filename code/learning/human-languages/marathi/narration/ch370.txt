Marathi, chapter 370: Talks and Essays.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
व्याख्यान (vyākhyān) — a lecture.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a fee, then the Marathi for a hostel.

You'll want to know: व्याख्यान.
व्याख्यान — vyākhyān — "a lecture".
प्राध्यापकांचं व्याख्यान दोन तास चाललं. prādhyāpkāñcaṁ vyākhyān don tās cāllaṁ. The professor's lecture went on for two hours.
Neuter.
व्याख्यान (vyākhyān) देणे, to give a lecture.

The word, taken apart.
व्याख्यान (vyākhyān) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit vyākhyāna, "comment, narration" (CDIAL 12188); the same entry lists an inherited Marathi vākhāṇ, "praise".

महाविद्यालयात पर्यावरणावर परिसंवाद झाला. mahāvidyālyāt paryāvarṇāvar parisaṁvād jhālā. A symposium on the environment was held at the college.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.

Lesson 5 of 5.
वक्ता (vaktā) — a speaker.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a speech, then the Marathi for a symposium.

You'll want to know: वक्ता.
वक्ता — vaktā — "a speaker".
आजचे वक्ते पुण्याहून आले आहेत. ājce vakte puṇyāhūn āle āhet. Today's speaker has come from Pune.
Masculine. Plural: वक्ते, vakte.
Plural वक्ते; respect takes the plural; a listener is श्रोता.

The word, taken apart.
वक्ता (vaktā) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit vaktṛ, "a speaker" (Monier-Williams). The Sanskrit nominative is vaktā.
