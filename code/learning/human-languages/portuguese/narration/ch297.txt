Portuguese, chapter 297: Em tribunal.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
a denúncia — the tip-off, the report to the police.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for the prisoner, then the Portuguese for the cell.

You'll want to know: a denúncia.
a denúncia — "the tip-off, the report to the police".
A polícia recebeu uma denúncia anónima. The police received an anonymous tip-off.
Fazer uma denúncia — to report someone.

The word, taken apart.
denúncia is a learned word built on Latin dēnūntiāre "to announce, to give notice", from nūntius "a messenger" (Lewis & Short).

What you've built.
One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.

Lesson 4 of 5.
condenar — to sentence, to condemn.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for the statement, then the Portuguese for the testimony.

You'll want to know: condenar.
condenar — "to sentence, to condemn".
O juiz condenou o homem a três anos de cadeia. The judge sentenced the man to three years in jail.
Condenar a + the punishment.

The word, taken apart.
condenar comes from Latin condemnāre "to sentence, to condemn", built on damnāre, from damnum "loss, harm" (Lewis & Short).
