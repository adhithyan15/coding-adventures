## You'll want to know: पाययति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C420-PRAPA420-02]; assesses=[] -->

**पाययति** — *pāyayati* — "gives a drink to".

> नारी प्रपायां पथिकान् जलं पाययति।
>
> *nārī prapāyāṃ pathikān jalaṃ pāyayati.*
>
> At the water shed a woman gives the wayfarers water to drink.

Past (she …): सा पथिकान् जलं पायितवती, *sā pathikān jalaṃ pāyitavatī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पाययति** (*pāyayati*) is built on the root √pā, "to drink", which gives *pibati*, "drinks"; its causative, "makes drink", is to give a drink to, and KRM gives the causative participle *pāyita* (MW).
