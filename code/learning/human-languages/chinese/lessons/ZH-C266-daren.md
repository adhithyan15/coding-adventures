## You'll want to know: 大人
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C266-AGE266-01]; assesses=[] -->

**大人** — *dàren* — "adult; grown-up".

> **大人和小孩都来了** *dàren hé xiǎohái dōu lái le* — The grown-ups and the children have all come.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**大人** (*dàren*) is built from 大 'big' and 人 'a person', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *daH* and *nyin* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription). In the word the second loses its tone.
