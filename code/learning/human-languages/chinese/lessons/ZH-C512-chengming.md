## You'll want to know: 成名
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C512-DREAMS512-04]; assesses=[] -->

**成名** — *chéngmíng* — "to become famous".

> **成名以后他感觉很不开心** *chéngmíng yǐhòu tā gǎnjué hěn bù kāixīn* — After he became famous, he felt very unhappy.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**成名** (*chéngmíng*) is built from 成 'to complete' and 名 'a name', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *dzyeng* and *mjieng* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'a name' and 'a title'.
