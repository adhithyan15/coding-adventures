## You'll want to know: માળો
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C445-BIRDS445-01]; assesses=[] -->

**માળો** — *māḷo* — "a nest".

> ચકલીએ બારી પાસે માળો બનાવ્યો.
>
> *caklīe bārī pāse māḷo banāvyo.*
>
> The sparrow built a nest by the window.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**માળો** (*māḷo*) goes back to *\*māḍa*, "an upper storey", a form CDIAL reconstructs; CDIAL lists a Gujarati word for a loft (CDIAL 9996). How it came to mean a nest is not settled.
