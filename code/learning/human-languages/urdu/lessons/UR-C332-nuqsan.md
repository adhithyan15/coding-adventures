## You'll want to know: نقصان
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C332-NUQSAN332-01]; assesses=[] -->

**نقصان** — *nuqsān* — "harm; a loss".

> بارش سے کسانوں کو نقصان ہوا۔
>
> *bārish se kisānoṅ ko nuqsān huā.*
>
> The rain did the farmers harm.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**نقصان** (*nuqsān*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *nuqṣān*, "a decrease, a loss".
