## You'll want to know: लाठी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C316-UPADRAV316-04]; assesses=[] -->

**लाठी** — *lāṭhī* — "a staff; a police baton".

> पुलिस ने भीड़ पर लाठियाँ चलाईं।
>
> *pulis ne bhīṛ par lāṭhiyā̃ calāīṁ.*
>
> The police used their batons on the crowd.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**लाठी** (*lāṭhī*) is inherited from *\*laṣṭi*, a stick, a form CDIAL reconstructs (CDIAL 10991). It is the long bamboo staff of a villager or a policeman.
