## You'll want to know: el sindicato
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C591-DESPIDO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**el sindicato** — "the trade union".

> El sindicato pide salarios más altos.
>
> *The union is asking for higher wages.*

A trade unionist is *un sindicalista*; a member is *un afiliado*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*sindicato* is built on *síndico* 'representative', a learned borrowing from Latin *syndicus* and Greek *syndikos* 'advocate' (EtymDB).
