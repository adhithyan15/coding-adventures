## You'll want to know: die Allergie
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C481-ALLERGIE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**die Allergie** — "allergy".

> Meine Tochter hat eine Allergie gegen Nüsse.
>
> *My daughter has an allergy to nuts.*

Plural: *die Allergien*. *Allergie gegen* + accusative.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Allergie* is a modern learned word built from Greek *állos* "other" and *érgon* "work": the body reacts in another way. EtymDB lists the German word without a source.
