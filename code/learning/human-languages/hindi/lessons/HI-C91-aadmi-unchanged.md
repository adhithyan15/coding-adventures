# दो आदमी — the plural you cannot see

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[HI-LEX-C91-NOUN-01, HI-CONCEPT-C39-AADMI-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] **आदमी** is masculine and ends in **-ई**, not **-आ**. You have a rule
for **-आ** and a rule for feminine nouns. Notice this word fits neither.

## You'll want to know: दो आदमी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C91-NOUN-04]; assesses=[] -->

**एक आदमी** — one man. **दो आदमी** — two men.

The word **does not change**.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[HI-LEX-C91-NOUN-04, HI-LEX-C91-NOUN-01] -->

A masculine noun that does **not** end in **-आ** has no plural form at all. It
stands still, and the sentence around it does the work:

| what tells you | how |
|---|---|
| the numeral | दो, तीन |
| the verb | हैं rather than है |
| an adjective | लंबे rather than लंबा |

**दो आदमी लंबे हैं।** — Two men are tall. The noun is the only word in that
sentence with nothing to show for it.

This is worth expecting rather than being surprised by, because a reading paper
will give you an unchanged noun and ask you to count. **The number lives in the
sentence, not always in the word.**

## Grammar Lens: the whole plural system, now complete
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[HI-LEX-C91-NOUN-04, HI-LEX-C91-NOUN-03, HI-LEX-C91-NOUN-01] -->

| noun | plural |
|---|---|
| बच्चा (m, -आ) | बच्चे |
| आदमी (m, other) | आदमी |
| किताब (f) | किताबें |
| भाषा (f) | भाषाएँ |

Four rows and every A1 noun you will meet is in one of them. Two of the four
add a nasal, one swaps a vowel, and one does nothing.

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[HI-LEX-C91-NOUN-04, HI-LEX-C91-NOUN-01] -->

[PAUSE 3s] What does a masculine noun ending in **-ई** do in the plural?
(Nothing.) So what tells you there is more than one? (The numeral, the verb and
any adjective.) How many shapes does the whole system have? (Four.)
