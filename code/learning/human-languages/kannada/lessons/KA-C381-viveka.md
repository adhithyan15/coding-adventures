## You'll want to know: ವಿವೇಕ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C381-PARIGANISU381-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ವಿವೇಕ** — *vivēka* — "good sense; discernment".

> ಕಷ್ಟದ ಸಮಯದಲ್ಲಿ ಅವಳು ವಿವೇಕದಿಂದ ನಡೆದುಕೊಂಡಳು.
>
> *kaṣṭada samayadalli avaḷu vivēkadinda naḍedukoṇḍaḷu.*
>
> In hard times she acted with good sense.

ವಿವೇಕಿ, a sensible person.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ವಿವೇಕ** (*vivēka*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *viveka*, "discrimination, the power to tell things apart" (Monier-Williams).
