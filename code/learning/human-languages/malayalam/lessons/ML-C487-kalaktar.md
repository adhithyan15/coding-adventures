## You'll want to know: കളക്ടർ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C487-NAGARAM487-04]; assesses=[] -->

**കളക്ടർ** — *kaḷakṭar* — "a district collector".

> വെള്ളപ്പൊക്കം കാരണം കളക്ടർ പള്ളിക്കൂടങ്ങൾക്ക് അവധി നൽകി.
>
> *veḷḷappokkaṁ kāraṇaṁ kaḷakṭar paḷḷikkūṭaṅṅaḷkkŭ avadhi nalki.*
>
> Because of the flood, the collector gave the schools a holiday.

The officer who runs a district; the title goes back to collecting the land revenue.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**കളക്ടർ** (*kaḷakṭar*) was borrowed from English *collector*, the officer in charge of a district.
