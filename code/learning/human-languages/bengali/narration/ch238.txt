Bengali, chapter 238: To die, To increase, To reduce, To chase, To believe.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
মরা (môrā) — to die.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for to cross, then the Bengali for to go on an outing.

You'll want to know: মরা.
মরা — môrā — "to die".

The word, taken apart.
মরা (môrā) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from marate, "will die" (CDIAL 9871). CDIAL lists Bengali marā, "dies", and thinks it possible or even probable that the verb continues this form directly.

তাড়া করা — tāṛā kôrā — "to chase".

The phrase, taken apart.
তাড়া করা (tāṛā kôrā) is tāṛā, "a chase", which belongs with Bengali tāṛāna, "to drive away", from Sanskrit tāḍayati, "beats, strikes" (CDIAL 5752), + kôrā, "to do", from Sanskrit karoti, "does" (CDIAL 2814).
