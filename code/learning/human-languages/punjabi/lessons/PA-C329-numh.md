## You'll want to know: ਨੂੰਹ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C329-INLAW329-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ਨੂੰਹ** — *nūṁh* — "a daughter-in-law".

> ਸਾਡੀ ਨੂੰਹ ਰੋਜ਼ ਸਵੇਰੇ ਸੈਰ ਕਰਦੀ ਹੈ।
>
> *sāḍī nūṁh roz savere sair kardī hai.*
>
> Our daughter-in-law goes for a walk every morning.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਨੂੰਹ** (*nūṁh*) is inherited from Sanskrit *snuṣā*, "a son's wife"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *nū̃h*, "son's wife", under it (CDIAL 13801).
