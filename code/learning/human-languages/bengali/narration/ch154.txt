রোগ (rog) — a disease.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for to thank, then the Bengali for to put up with.

You'll want to know: রোগ.
রোগ — rog — "a disease".

The word, taken apart.
রোগ (rog) is Sanskrit roga, "disease", taken over whole as a learned loan, a tatsama (CDIAL 10829).

Lesson 3 of 5.
কাশি (kāshi) — a cough.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a disease, then the Bengali for a cold.

You'll want to know: কাশি.
কাশি — kāshi — "a cough".

The word, taken apart.
কাশি (kāshi) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from kāsikā, "cough" (CDIAL 3138). CDIAL lists Bengali kāsi, "cough".

You'll want to know: মাথাব্যথা.

The word, taken apart.
মাথাব্যথা (māthābæthā) is māthā, "head", from Sanskrit mastaka, "head, skull" (CDIAL 9926), + bæthā, "pain", Sanskrit vyathā, "pain, distress", taken over whole.
