French, chapter 284: Ce que disent les journaux.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
révéler — to reveal, to disclose.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for to interview, then the French for the aerial.

You'll want to know: révéler.
révéler — "to reveal, to disclose".
Le journal a révélé le secret du chanteur. The paper revealed the singer's secret.
Se révéler — to turn out: il s'est révélé très utile.

The word, taken apart.
révéler is a learned borrowing from Latin revēlāre "to unveil" (EtymDB), from re- "back" and vēlum "a veil".

What you've built.
One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.

You'll want to know: rapporter.
rapporter — "to report, to relate; to bring back".
Le journaliste a rapporté ce qu'il avait vu. The journalist reported what he had seen.
Also to bring back: rapporter des souvenirs de vacances.

The word, taken apart.
rapporter is built on apporter "to bring", which comes from Latin apportāre (EtymDB), from ad and portāre "to carry" (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 3 of 5.
démentir — to deny, to refute (officially).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for to reveal, then the French for to report.

You'll want to know: démentir.
démentir — "to deny, to refute (officially)".
Le chanteur a démenti la nouvelle. The singer denied the news.
Conjugated like sortir: il dément, ils démentent.

The word, taken apart.
démentir is built on dé- and the verb for "to lie" (EtymDB), which is inherited from Latin mentīrī "to lie" (Lewis & Short): to show that something is false.

Lesson 4 of 5.
relater — to recount, to relate.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for to report, then the French for to deny.

You'll want to know: relater.
relater — "to recount, to relate".
Le livre relate la vie d'une famille pendant la guerre. The book recounts the life of a family during the war.
Formal and written; in speech, raconter.

The word, taken apart.
relater is built on Latin relātus (EtymDB), the past participle of referre "to carry back, to report" (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 5 of 5.
commenter — to comment on.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for to deny, then the French for to recount.

You'll want to know: commenter.
commenter — "to comment on".
Il commente les matchs à la radio. He commentates on matches on the radio.
No sur after it: commenter un match; with the noun, faire un commentaire sur.

The word, taken apart.
commenter is a learned borrowing from Latin commentārī "to think over, to write notes on" (Lewis & Short), which is linked to the root of mēns "mind".
