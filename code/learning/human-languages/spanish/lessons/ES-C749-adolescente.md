## You'll want to know: el adolescente
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C749-EDADES-03]; assesses=[] -->

**el adolescente** — "the teenager".

> Mi hijo es un adolescente.
>
> *My son is a teenager.*

*La adolescente* for a girl.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*adolescente* is a learned borrowing from Latin *adolēscēns* "growing up" (EtymDB), the participle of *adolēscere*.
