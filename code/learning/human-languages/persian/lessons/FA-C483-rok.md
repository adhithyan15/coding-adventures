## You'll want to know: رک
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C483-HONESTY-03]; assesses=[] -->

**رک** (*rok*) — "frank, blunt".

> او خیلی رک است و نظرش را میگوید.
>
> *u kheyli rok ast va nazarash râ miguyad.*
>
> She's very frank and says what she thinks.

Common in speech; also *rok-o-râst* 'straight out'.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*rok* is an everyday Persian word for frank, but no source used here gives its history, and its origin is not settled.
