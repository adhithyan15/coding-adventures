## You'll want to know: راه حل
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C377-BOHRAN-02]; assesses=[] -->

**راه حل** (*râh-e hall*) — "a solution".

> برای این مشکل یک راه حل ساده پیدا کردیم.
>
> *barâye in moshkel yek râh-e hall-e sâde peydâ kardim.*
>
> We found a simple solution to this problem.

*Râh* "way" + ezafe + *hall* "solving". Plural *râh-e hallhâ*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**راه حل** is literally "the way of solving": *râh* "road, way" is inherited Persian (EtymDB traces it back through Proto-Iranian), and *hall* "solving" comes from Arabic *ḥall*.
