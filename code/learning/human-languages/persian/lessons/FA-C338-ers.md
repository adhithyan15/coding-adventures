## You'll want to know: ارث
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C338-KHATM-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ارث** (*ers*) — "an inheritance".

> این فرش از مادربزرگم به ارث رسیده است.
>
> *In farsh az mâdarbozorgam be ers reside ast.*
>
> This carpet came down to me from my grandmother.

Written with ث. *Be ers residan* "to be inherited".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ers* is borrowed from Arabic *irth*, "inheritance" (EtymDB lists the Arabic word).
