## You'll want to know: broncearse
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C690-PLAYA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**broncearse** — "to get a tan".

> Me bronceo muy rápido.
>
> *I tan very quickly.*

*Estar bronceado* — to be tanned.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*broncearse* is built on *bronce* "bronze", which was borrowed from Italian *bronzo* (EtymDB): tanned skin turns the colour of bronze.
