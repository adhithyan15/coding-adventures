## You'll want to know: फबना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C434-SHUDDH434-04]; assesses=[] -->

**फबना** — *phabnā* — "to suit; to look good on".

> यह लाल कुर्ता तुम पर बहुत फबता है।
>
> *yah lāl kurtā tum par bahut phabtā hai.*
>
> This red kurta really suits you.

Past (it …): वह साड़ी उस पर फबी, *vah sāṛī us par phabī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**फबना** (*phabnā*) is filed by CDIAL under *\*sparvati*, "releases, succeeds, is fitting", a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 13808), with Hindi *phabnā*, "to be fitting".
