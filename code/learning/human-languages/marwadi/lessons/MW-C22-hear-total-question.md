> *ye kitṇo huā?* — **how much is that altogether?**

The same three-word shape, with *huā* where *kū hai* stood. The asking word
keeps its own ending here: *kitṇo*, not *kitṇe*.

The two questions do different jobs. *ye kitṇe kū hai?* asks the price of one
thing while you are still deciding. *ye kitṇo huā?* asks for the total once you
have stopped choosing. Hear both, one after the other, and say which one comes
last.
