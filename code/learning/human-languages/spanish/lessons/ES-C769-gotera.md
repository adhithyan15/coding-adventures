## You'll want to know: la gotera
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C769-GOTERA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**la gotera** — "the leak (in a roof)".

> Con la lluvia salió una gotera en el techo.
>
> *With the rain a leak appeared in the ceiling.*

Used for water coming through a roof or ceiling; a leaking pipe is *una fuga*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*gotera* is built on *gota* 'a drop' (EtymDB), which comes from Latin *gutta* 'a drop', with the ending *-era*.
