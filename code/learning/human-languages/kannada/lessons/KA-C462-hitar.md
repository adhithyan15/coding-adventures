## You'll want to know: ಹೀಟರ್
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C462-KOTHADI462-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ಹೀಟರ್** — *hīṭar* — "a heater".

> ಕೊಠಡಿಯಲ್ಲಿ ಹೀಟರ್ ಇದ್ದರೂ ಚಳಿ ಆಗುತ್ತಿತ್ತು.
>
> *koṭhaḍiyalli hīṭar iddarū caḷi āguttittu.*
>
> Even with a heater in the room it felt cold.

ಹೀಟರ್ ಹಾಕು, to put the heater on.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಹೀಟರ್** (*hīṭar*) is a loan from English *heater*.
