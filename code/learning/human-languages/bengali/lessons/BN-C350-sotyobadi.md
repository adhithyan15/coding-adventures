## You'll want to know: সত্যবাদী
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C350-ADORSHO350-04]; assesses=[] -->

**সত্যবাদী** — *sôtyôbādī* — "truthful; honest in speech".

> মীরা একজন সত্যবাদী মানুষ।
>
> *mīrā ekjôn sôtyôbādī mānush.*
>
> Mira is a truthful person.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**সত্যবাদী** (*sôtyôbādī*) is Sanskrit *satyavādin*, *satya* 'truth' with *vādin* 'speaking' (Monier-Williams): one whose word can be trusted.
