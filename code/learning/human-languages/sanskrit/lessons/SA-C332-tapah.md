## You'll want to know: तपः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C332-AKHYANA332-04]; assesses=[] -->

**तपः** — *tapaḥ* — "penance; austerity".

> ऋषिः वने कठिनं तपः करोति।
>
> *ṛṣiḥ vane kaṭhinaṃ tapaḥ karoti.*
>
> The seer does hard penance in the forest.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**तपः** (*tapaḥ*) comes from the root √tap, "to be hot, burn", as in *tapati*; MW gives *tapas* as warmth, heat, and religious austerity, penance (MW).
