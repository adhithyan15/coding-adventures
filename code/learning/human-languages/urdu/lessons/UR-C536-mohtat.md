## You'll want to know: محتاط
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C536-MOHTAT536-01]; assesses=[] -->

**محتاط** — *mohtāt* — "careful; cautious".

> اگر آپ محتاط ہوتے تو یہ حادثہ نہ ہوتا۔
>
> *agar āp mohtāt hote to yih hādsā na hotā.*
>
> If you had been careful, this accident would not have happened.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**محتاط** (*mohtāt*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *muḥtāṭ*, "on one's guard".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
