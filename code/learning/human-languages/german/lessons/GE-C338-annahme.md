## You'll want to know: die Annahme
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C338-VERMUTUNG-02]; assesses=[] -->

**die Annahme** — "the assumption; the acceptance".

> Wir sind von der falschen Annahme ausgegangen, dass das Museum montags offen ist.
>
> *We started from the wrong assumption that the museum is open on Mondays.*

Plural: *die Annahmen*. The noun of *annehmen*: *die Annahme eines Pakets* — accepting a parcel.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Annahme* is the noun of *annehmen*, built on *nehmen* "to take", inherited from Old High German *neman* and Proto-Germanic *nemaną* (EtymDB): something you take as given.
