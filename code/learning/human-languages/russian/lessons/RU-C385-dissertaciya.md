## You'll want to know: диссертация
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C385-OBRAZOVANIYE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**диссертация** (*dissertátsiya*) — "a dissertation, a thesis".

> Он пишет диссертацию о русской истории.
>
> *On píshet dissertátsiyu o rússkoy istórii.*
>
> He is writing a dissertation on Russian history.

Feminine. *Защитить диссертацию* — to defend a thesis.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**диссертация** is usually traced to Latin *dissertātiō* "a discourse" (Lewis & Short), from *dissertāre* "to discuss", the frequentative of *disserere* "to set out in order", built on *serere* "to join, to string together".
