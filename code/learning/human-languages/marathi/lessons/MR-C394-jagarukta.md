## You'll want to know: जागरूकता
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C394-BHAN394-03]; assesses=[] -->

**जागरूकता** — *jāgarūktā* — "alertness; public awareness".

> आरोग्याबद्दल लोकांमध्ये जागरूकता वाढली आहे.
>
> *ārogyābaddal lokāmmadhye jāgarūktā vāḍhlī āhe.*
>
> Awareness about health has grown among people.

Feminine.

जागरूक, alert, + -ता.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**जागरूकता** (*jāgarūktā*) is a learned noun built from Sanskrit *jāgarūka*, "wakeful, watchful" (Monier-Williams), with *-tā*, "-ness".
