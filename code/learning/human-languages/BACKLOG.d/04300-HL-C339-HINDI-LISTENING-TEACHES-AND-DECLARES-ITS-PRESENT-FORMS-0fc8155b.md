## HL-C339 — Hindi listening teaches and declares its present forms

After the dawn-noun repair, **सुनते** is the highest-ranked independent modern
Hindi form in the glossed-but-never-taught queue. Chapter 72 models and assesses
**आप सुनते हैं** in its when-then frame, and two later reviews require the same
sentence. Chapter 43 introduces only infinitive **सुनना**, however, then asks
learners to repeat that dictionary form without deriving the present family.

Derive **सुन**, **सुनता**, **सुनती**, and **सुनते** in Chapter 43, practise the
respectful sentence required later, recall the set, and declare the complete
family as the lesson headword. This closes the gap before the assessed use and
makes every taught form visible to reports and downstream cards.
