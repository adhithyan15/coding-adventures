## You'll want to know: rompre
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C491-RUPTURE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**rompre** — "to break up; to break".

> Il a rompu avec sa copine au mois de mai.
>
> *He broke up with his girlfriend in May.*

*Rompre avec quelqu'un* — to break up with someone. Present *il rompt*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*rompre* is inherited from Latin *rumpere* "to break, to burst" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short).
