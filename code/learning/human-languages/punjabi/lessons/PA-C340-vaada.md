## You'll want to know: ਵਾਅਦਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C340-AGREE340-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ਵਾਅਦਾ** — *vāadā* — "a promise".

> ਕੀ ਤੁਸੀਂ ਵਾਅਦਾ ਕਰਦੇ ਹੋ?
>
> *kī tusī̃ vāadā karde ho?*
>
> Do you promise?

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਵਾਅਦਾ** (*vāadā*) is a loan, usually traced through Persian to Arabic *waʿda*, "promise".
