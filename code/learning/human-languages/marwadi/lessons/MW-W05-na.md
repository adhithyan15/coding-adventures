# न — a front-of-the-mouth *n*

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MW-LEX-NAAM-01, MW-SCRIPT-NNA-01, MW-SCRIPT-AA-INDEPENDENT-01] -->

[PAUSE 8s] Write independent **आ** once. Say *nām* and its meaning. Read known **ण** with the tongue curled
back. Today the tongue moves forward.

## Script
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-SCRIPT-NA-01]; assesses=[MW-SCRIPT-NNA-01] -->

> न

Read **न** as *na*, with the tongue near the teeth. Do not replace it with
retroflex **ण** from **पाणी**.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MW-SCRIPT-NA-01, MW-SCRIPT-NNA-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: observe-trace -->

Keep **न** visible. Finger-trace it once, then trace one pencil model. Point to
**न** when you hear *na* and **ण** when you hear retroflex *ṇa*.

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MW-SCRIPT-NA-01] -->
<!-- hl-activity: {"id":"MW-W05-na-choice","kind":"text","assesses":["MW-SCRIPT-NA-01"],"prompt":"Write the sign that begins nām, name.","answer":"न","accepted":[],"feedback":{"correct":"Right: न begins नाम.","incorrect":"Use the dental sign न here; ण belongs in पाणी."},"response_seconds":8} -->

Cover the model and write **न** once.
