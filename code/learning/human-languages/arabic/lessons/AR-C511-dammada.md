## You'll want to know: ضمد
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C511-JURA511-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ضمد** — *ḍammada* — "to bind up (a wound)".

> ضمدت الممرضة جرح اللاعب.
>
> *ḍammadat al-mumarriḍa jurḥ al-lāʿib.*
>
> The nurse bound up the player's wound.

Present: يضمد, *yuḍammid*.

The noun ضمادة, a bandage, shares the root.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ضمد** (*ḍammada*) is built on the native Arabic root *ḍ-m-d*, "to bind, to bandage" (Lane). Form II ḍammada is to bandage.
