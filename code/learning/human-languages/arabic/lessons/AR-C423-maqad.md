## You'll want to know: مقعد
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C423-BAWWABA423-02]; assesses=[] -->

**مقعد** — *maqʿad* — "a seat".

> جلست في مقعد بجانب النافذة.
>
> *jalastu fī maqʿad bi-jānib an-nāfidha.*
>
> I sat in a seat beside the window.

Plural: مقاعد, *maqāʿid*.

The pattern *mafʿal* names a place: *maqʿad* is the place where one sits.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**مقعد** (*maqʿad*) is built on the native Arabic root *q-ʿ-d*, "to sit down" (Lane).
