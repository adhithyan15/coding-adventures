Russian, chapter 397: Paying for things.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
оплачивать (opláchivat) — to pay for.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for an exchange rate, then the Russian for an exchange.

You'll want to know: оплачивать.
оплачивать (opláchivat) — "to pay for".
Фирма оплачивает мне обед. Fírma opláchivayet mne obéd. The firm pays for my lunch.
Imperfective; perfective оплатить (оплачу, оплатишь). It takes a direct object with no за: оплачивать (opláchivat) счёт.

The word, taken apart.
оплачивать (opláchivat) is built from о- and платить "to pay", on плата "a payment", the root that also gives зарплата.

Lesson 3 of 5.
бесплатный (besplátnyy) — free (of charge).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to pay for, then the Russian for a payment.

You'll want to know: бесплатный.
бесплатный (besplátnyy) — "free (of charge)".
Вход в музей бесплатный. Vkhod v muzéy besplátnyy. Entry to the museum is free.
Бесплатно — for free. Free in the sense of not busy is свободный.

The word, taken apart.
бесплатный (besplátnyy) is built from без "without" and плата "a payment"; the з turns to с before п.

Lesson 5 of 5.
выгодный (výgodnyy) — profitable, a good deal.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for free, then the Russian for paid.

You'll want to know: выгодный.
выгодный (výgodnyy) — "profitable, a good deal".
Это очень выгодное предложение. Éto óchen výgodnoye predlozhéniye. This is a very good deal.
Выгодно — it pays, it is worth it.

The word, taken apart.
выгодный (výgodnyy) is built on выгода "benefit, gain", from вы- and the root of годиться "to be fit" and год, Proto-Slavic godŭ "a fitting time": what suits you well.
