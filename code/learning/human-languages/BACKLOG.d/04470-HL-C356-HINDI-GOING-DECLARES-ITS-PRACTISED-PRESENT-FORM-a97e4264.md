## HL-C356 — Hindi going declares its practised present form

Chapter 76 introduces **जाना** and immediately places it in **मैं स्टेशन जाता
हूँ**, explicitly connecting that form to the familiar speaking frame before
asking the learner to produce the whole sentence. Its declaration exposes only
the infinitive, leaving **जाता** looking untaught to the corpus report.

Declare **जाता** beside **जाना**. This makes the lesson's worked and practised
masculine present form visible to reports and downstream study artifacts without
inventing forms the lesson does not present.
