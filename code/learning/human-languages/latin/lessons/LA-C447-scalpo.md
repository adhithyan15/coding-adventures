## You'll want to know: scalpō, scalpere
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C447-CIMEX-03]; assesses=[] -->

**scalpō, scalpere** — "to scratch; to carve".

> Hospes caput et bracchia scalpit.
>
> *The guest scratches his head and arms.*

*scalpō, scalpere, scalpsī, scalptum*. Also to carve or engrave. English *scalpel* comes from *scalpellum*, a little knife, from the same root.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*scalpō* is inherited from PIE *(s)kel(H)p-*, from a root meaning 'to split, to cut' (EtymDB). English *scalpel* comes from *scalpellum*, a diminutive of *scalprum* 'knife', built on it (Lewis & Short).
