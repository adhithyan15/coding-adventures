## You'll want to know: लेबल
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C432-MAAL432-04]; assesses=[] -->

**लेबल** — *lebal* — "a label".

> पैकेट पर लेबल पढ़िए।
>
> *paikeṭ par lebal paṛhiye.*
>
> Read the label on the packet.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**लेबल** (*lebal*) is borrowed from English *label*.
