Lesson 1 of 6.
fēnlèi — to sort into kinds.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for our planet, then the Mandarin for eco-friendly (good for the environment).

You'll want to know: fēnlèi.
fēnlèi — "to sort into kinds".
lājī yīnggāi fēnlèi — Rubbish should be sorted.
Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
fēnlèi is built from fēn 'to divide' and lèi 'a kind', both inherited from Middle Chinese, pjun and lwijH (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to separate' and the second 'a sort'.

[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for eco-friendly (good for the environment), then the Mandarin for to sort into kinds.

You'll want to know: 全家.
全家 — quánjiā — "the whole family".
今天我们全家一起打扫房子 jīntiān wǒmen quánjiā yìqǐ dǎsǎo fángzi — Today the whole family is cleaning the house together.
The new character, in pieces: 全 (quán) — six strokes: a wide roof like 人, then a bar, a second bar, a vertical down from the first through the second, and a long bar at the foot. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

The word, taken apart.
全家 (quánjiā) is built from 全 (quán) 'whole' and 家 'a home', both inherited from Middle Chinese, dzjwen and kae (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'complete'.

Lesson 3 of 6.
全 (quán) — a wide roof, then three bars and a vertical.

Before we start: this one needs your hands, so it is not a driving lesson. You will want a pen and something to write on and your eyes, for letter shapes on the page. 人 安.
[once you have stopped driving — read: each one aloud, with nothing said first — rén, ān]

[once you have stopped driving — this part needs your eyes or your hands; if you are driving, skip ahead to the next part]
Script — a wide roof, then three bars and a vertical.
全 (quán).
Two pieces, top before bottom.
The roof, two strokes, like 人 opened wide: a long stroke falling to the left, then a long stroke falling to the right from near its top.
Under it, four strokes. A bar. A second bar under it. A vertical down from the first bar through the second. Then a long bar across the foot.
2 + 4 = six.

[once you have stopped driving — this part needs your eyes or your hands; if you are driving, skip ahead to the next part]
Guided Practice.
[once you have stopped driving — write: 全 (quán), all six, with the model in front of you]
[once you have stopped driving — cover: write it again from memory, and count the pieces, not the strokes]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
How many strokes in 全 (quán)? (Six.) What is the roof like? (人, opened wide.) Which stroke comes after the two bars? (The vertical.)

Lesson 4 of 6.
huíshōu — to recycle.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for to sort into kinds, then the Mandarin for the whole family.

You'll want to know: huíshōu.
huíshōu — "to recycle".
zhèxiē píngzi yīnggāi huíshōu yīnwèi hái néng yòng — These bottles should be recycled, because they can still be used.
Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
huíshōu is built from 回 'to turn back' and shōu 'to gather', both inherited from Middle Chinese, hwoj and syuw (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'to collect'. Recycling is a modern sense.
