## You'll want to know: বাহু
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C466-SHORIR466-02]; assesses=[] -->

**বাহু** — *bāhu* — "an arm (from shoulder to hand)".

> মীরা শিশুটিকে দুই বাহুতে তুলে নিল।
>
> *mīrā shishuṭike dui bāhute tule nilô.*
>
> Mira lifted the child up in both arms.

হাত (taught) is the hand and, loosely, the whole arm; বাহু is the arm itself.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**বাহু** (*bāhu*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *bāhu*, "arm" (CDIAL 9229), beside which CDIAL lists a worn-down Bengali *bāh*.
