Russian, chapter 217: Blunt, Smooth, Cool, Fresh, Modern.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
тупой (tupóy) — blunt.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for sad, then the Russian for strict.

You'll want to know: тупой.
тупой (tupóy) — "blunt".

The word, taken apart.
тупой (tupóy) from Proto-Slavic tǫpŭ — inherited. It has been compared with Old Norse þambr, "swollen", and Lithuanian tampýti, "to stretch", among others (Vasmer); the origin is not settled.

You'll want to know: современный.
современный (sovreménnyy) — "modern".

The word, taken apart.
современный (sovreménnyy) is со-, "together", with времен-, from время, "time", a Church Slavonic word (Vasmer): "of the same time", contemporary.
