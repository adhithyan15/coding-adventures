# টনটন করা (ṭônṭôn kôrā) — to throb; to ache dully

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[BN-LEX-C477-JBOLUNI477-01, BN-LEX-C477-JBOLUNI477-02] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Bengali for agony, then the Bengali for to get burnt.

## You'll want to know: টনটন করা
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C477-JBOLUNI477-03]; assesses=[] -->

**টনটন করা** — *ṭônṭôn kôrā* — "to throb; to ache dully".

> দাঁতটা টনটন করছে।
>
> *dā̃tṭā ṭônṭôn kôrchhe.*
>
> My tooth is throbbing.

Past (he/she …): সেই রোগীর মাথা টনটন করল, *sei rogīr māthā ṭônṭôn kôrlô*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**টনটন করা** (*ṭônṭôn kôrā*) is *ṭônṭôn*, an imitative word for a dull, throbbing ache, with *kôrā*, "to do" (CDIAL 2814).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
