# 体重 (tǐzhòng) — body weight

## You'll want to know: 体重
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C230-BODY230-02]; assesses=[] -->

**体重** — *tǐzhòng* — "body weight".

> **你的体重是多少** *nǐ de tǐzhòng shì duōshao* — How much do you weigh?

The new character, in pieces: 体 — seven strokes: the person piece 亻, then 本. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**体重** (*tǐzhòng*) is built from 体 'body' and 重 'heavy', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *thejX* and *drjowngX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'the body' and the second 'much' and 'thick'.
