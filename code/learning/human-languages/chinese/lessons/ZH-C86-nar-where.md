## You'll want to know: 哪儿
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C86-NAR-01]; assesses=[] -->

**哪儿** — *nǎr* — "where?".

> **你在哪儿** *nǐ zài nǎr* — Where are you?

> **我在那儿** *wǒ zài nàr* — I'm over there.

The next lesson puts the new character in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**哪儿** (*nǎr*) is built from 哪 'which', the *nǎ* of *nǐ shì nǎ guó rén*, and the r ending 儿. 哪 has no reading in the Guangyun: no dictionary we use records an older form, so its history is not settled.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ZH-LEX-C85-NAR-01] -->

The answer goes where the question word was, and nothing else moves: 你在哪儿 — 我在那儿. Only the tone tells the two words apart: *nǎr*, with the dip, asks; *nàr*, with the fall, answers.
