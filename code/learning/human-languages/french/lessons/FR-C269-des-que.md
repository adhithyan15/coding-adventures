## You'll want to know: dès que
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C269-QUAND-02]; assesses=[] -->

**dès que** — "as soon as".

> Dès que le film a fini, nous sommes partis.
>
> *As soon as the film ended, we left.*

For an event still to come, the future follows: *dès qu'il arrivera, on mangera*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*dès* is usually traced to Late Latin *dē ex* "from out of", the prepositions *dē* "from" and *ex* "out of" (Lewis & Short) run together; EtymDB lists no parent.
