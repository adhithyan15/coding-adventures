## You'll want to know: прокол
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C405-SHINA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**прокол** (*prokól*) — "a puncture".

> Из-за прокола мы час стояли у дороги.
>
> *Iz-za prokóla my chas stoyáli u dorógi.*
>
> Because of the puncture we stood by the road for an hour.

Masculine. *У меня прокол* — I have a puncture.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*прокол* is built on *проколоть* 'to pierce through', from *про-* 'through' and *колоть* 'to prick, to split'. EtymDB does not list *прокол*; its parts are ordinary Russian.
