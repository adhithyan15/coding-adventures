# عاطل (ʿāṭil) — unemployed, out of work

## You'll want to know: عاطل
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C522-AMAL522-05]; assesses=[] -->

**عاطل** — *ʿāṭil* — "unemployed, out of work".

> ابن عمي عاطل عن العمل.
>
> *ibn ʿammī ʿāṭil ʿan al-ʿamal.*
>
> My cousin is out of work.

The full phrase is عاطل عن العمل.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**عاطل** (*ʿāṭil*) is built on the native Arabic root *ʿ-ṭ-l*, "to be empty, without use" (Lane). Muʿaṭṭal, out of order, which you know, is from the same root.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
