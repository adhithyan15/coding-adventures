## You'll want to know: పాఠ్యాంశం
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C416-VYASAM416-04]; assesses=[] -->

**పాఠ్యాంశం** — *pāṭhyāṁśaṁ* — "a syllabus topic".

> పరీక్షలో అన్ని పాఠ్యాంశాల మీద ప్రశ్నలు వస్తాయి.
>
> *parīkṣalō anni pāṭhyāṁśāla mīda praśnalu vastāyi.*
>
> In the exam there are questions on every topic of the syllabus.

Plural: పాఠ్యాంశాలు, *pāṭhyāṁśālu*.

పాఠ్య, to be taught, + అంశం, a point.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**పాఠ్యాంశం** (*pāṭhyāṁśaṁ*) is built from Sanskrit *pāṭhya*, "to be taught" (Monier-Williams), and *aṁśa*, "a part" (CDIAL 2), the word in అంశం.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
