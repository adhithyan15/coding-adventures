## You'll want to know: bǔyú
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C443-CLEAN443-05]; assesses=[] -->

*bǔyú* — "to catch fish (for a living)".

> *cūnmín měitiān zuò chuán qù bǔyú* — The villagers go out fishing by boat every day.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*bǔyú* is built from *bǔ* 'to seize' and *yú* 'a fish', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *buH* and *ngjo* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to seize'; the falling-rising bǔ is irregular beside it.
