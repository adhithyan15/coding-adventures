# ق (qāf) — the letter that opens قمیض

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[UR-SCRIPT-C36-GAF-01, UR-SCRIPT-C37-FE-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: write گ and ف once each, and name them.

[PAUSE 2s] Now say **قمیض** (*qamīz*), "shirt".
You have read it for a long time. This lesson takes one letter out of it and puts
it in your hand.

## Script you'll notice: ق
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-SCRIPT-C37-QAF-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ق** — *qāf*, a *k* made far back in the throat.

It opens **قمیض** (*qamīz*), "shirt". Its head is the head of **ف**, from the last
lesson, but it carries **two dots**, and its bowl dips below the line.

## Writing: ق
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[UR-SCRIPT-C37-QAF-01] -->

- **1.** start here: loop clockwise around the rounded head above the main line
- **2.** continue down and left through the deep bowl without lifting
- **3.** after one lift, place the upper-right dot
- **4.** after another lift, place the upper-left dot — and only now lift

**Pen lifts: 2.**

> Stroke order is one attested teaching order; hands and teachers vary.
> Source: Zer o Zabar, Fe, qāf, ṭe, ḍāl, and ṛe, independent ق calligraphic and handwriting animations and Qāf instructions (Northwestern University Libraries Open Textbook, 2026).

## Guided Practice

[PAUSE 1s]
- [YOU LOOK: at **قمیض** and point at ق inside it]
- [YOU TRACE: ق three times, saying *q* as you finish each one]
- [YOU WRITE: ف, then ق, from memory]
- [YOU READ: **قمیض** aloud once more]
