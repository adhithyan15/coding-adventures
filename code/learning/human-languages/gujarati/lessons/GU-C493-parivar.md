# પરિવાર (parivār) — a household; family members

## You'll want to know: પરિવાર
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C493-PEDHI493-04]; assesses=[] -->

**પરિવાર** — *parivār* — "a household; family members".

> અમારા પરિવારમાં દસ માણસ છે.
>
> *amārā parivārmā̃ das māṇas che.*
>
> There are ten people in our household.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**પરિવાર** (*parivār*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *parivāra*, "a cover" and "the people around someone, a retinue", as Monier-Williams records it.
