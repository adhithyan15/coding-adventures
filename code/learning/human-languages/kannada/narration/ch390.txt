Kannada, chapter 390: The Law.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
ಕಾನೂನು (kānūnu) — a law; the law.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to exaggerate, then the Kannada for status.

You'll want to know: ಕಾನೂನು.
ಕಾನೂನು — kānūnu — "a law; the law".
ಈ ಕಾನೂನು ಮುಂದಿನ ತಿಂಗಳಿನಿಂದ ಬರುತ್ತದೆ. ī kānūnu mundina tiṅgaḷininda baruttade. This law comes in from next month.
ಕಾನೂನುಬದ್ಧ, legal; ಕಾನೂನುಬಾಹಿರ, illegal.

The word, taken apart.
ಕಾನೂನು (kānūnu) is a loan from Arabic qānūn, "a rule, a canon", through Persian and Hindustani; Arabic took it from Greek kanōn, "a rule".

ಕಾಯ್ದೆ — kāyde — "an act of law".
ಸರ್ಕಾರ ಹೊಸ ಕಾಯ್ದೆ ತಂದಿತು. sarkāra hosa kāyde tanditu. The government brought in a new act.
A law passed under a name: ಕಾಯ್ದೆ (kāyde). The law in general: ಕಾನೂನು (kānūnu).

The word, taken apart.
ಕಾಯ್ದೆ (kāyde) is a loan from Arabic qāʿida, "a rule, a base", which reached Kannada through Hindustani.

You'll want to know: ಪರಿಷ್ಕರಿಸು.

ಸರ್ಕಾರ ಹಳೆಯ ನಿಯಮಗಳನ್ನು ಪರಿಷ್ಕರಿಸಿತು. sarkāra haḷeya niyamagaḷannu pariṣkarisitu. The government revised the old rules.
Past (I …): ಪರಿಷ್ಕರಿಸಿದೆ, pariṣkariside.
ಪರಿಷ್ಕೃತ, revised.

The word, taken apart.
ಪರಿಷ್ಕರಿಸು (pariṣkarisu) is built on Sanskrit pariṣkāra, "adorning, preparing, refining" (CDIAL 7894), with the Kannada verb ending -isu.

Lesson 5 of 6.
ಹಸ್ತಕ್ಷೇಪ (hastakṣēpa) — interference.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to revise, then the Kannada for official.

You'll want to know: ಹಸ್ತಕ್ಷೇಪ.
ಹಸ್ತಕ್ಷೇಪ — hastakṣēpa — "interference".
ನಮ್ಮ ಮನೆಯ ವಿಷಯದಲ್ಲಿ ಬೇರೆಯವರ ಹಸ್ತಕ್ಷೇಪ ಬೇಡ. namma maneya viṣayadalli bēreyavara hastakṣēpa bēḍa. We want no outside interference in our family's matters.
Word by word it is throwing in a hand: ಹಸ್ತ + ಕ್ಷೇಪ.

The word, taken apart.
ಹಸ್ತಕ್ಷೇಪ (hastakṣēpa) is built from Sanskrit hasta, "a hand" (CDIAL 14024), and kṣepa, "a throw, a cast" (Monier-Williams).
