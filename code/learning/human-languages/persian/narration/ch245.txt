Persian, chapter 245: Swan, Nest, Wing, Horn, Football.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
قو (qu) — a swan.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a branch, then the Persian for a pine tree.

You'll want to know: قو.
قو — qu — "a swan".
