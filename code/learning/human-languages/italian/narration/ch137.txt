Italian, chapter 137: Last, Already, the Day before Yesterday, Recently, To Happen.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
scorso / scorsa — last (week, month, year).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for to find, then the Italian for to look for.

You'll want to know: scorso / scorsa.
scorso / scorsa — "last (week, month, year)". It follows the time word and agrees with it: la settimana scorsa, il mese scorso, l'anno scorso. With the passato prossimo: La settimana scorsa ho lavorato. — "Last week I worked."

Lesson 2 of 5.
già — already.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for to look for, then the Italian for last.

You'll want to know: già.
già — "already". It sits between ho and the participle: Ho già capito. — "I have already understood." Sono già tornato. — "I have already come back."

Lesson 4 of 5.
recentemente — recently.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for already, then the Italian for the day before yesterday.

You'll want to know: recentemente.
recentemente — "recently". Recentemente ho letto molto. — "Recently I have read a lot." The ending -mente makes an adverb, the way English uses -ly.
