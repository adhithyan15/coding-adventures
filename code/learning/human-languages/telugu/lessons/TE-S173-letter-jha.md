# ఝ — the breathed first letter in ఝంకారం

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[TE-LEX-C152-BUZZ-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say **ఝంకారం** once. What sound can it name? (A buzz or hum.)

[PAUSE 1s] Now slow the first piece: *jhaṅ*. The base letter carrying that
breathed **jh** is today's whole task.

## Script you'll notice: ఝ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-SCRIPT-RECOG-173]; assesses=[] -->

**ఝ** — *jha*.

In **ఝంకారం**, the dot-like **ం** closes the first syllable: **ఝం**. Remove
that sign and the base letter is **ఝ**.

This aspirated letter is uncommon in everyday Telugu vocabulary. That is why
the book gave you **ఝంకారం** before asking you to practise the shape.

## Writing: ఝ — five movements, five runs
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[TE-SCRIPT-RECOG-173] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: observe-trace -->

Follow the filmstrip slowly. Movements 1–3 travel clockwise through the three
round sections. Movement 4 curls upward through the flourish above them.
Movement 5 draws the separate stem downward beneath the middle-right side.

The numbered route is one attested school-style order; Telugu handwriting can
vary. Keep the five pen-down runs distinct and let all three bowls stay round.
