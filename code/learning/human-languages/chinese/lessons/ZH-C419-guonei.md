## You'll want to know: guónèi
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C419-ARRIVE419-05]; assesses=[] -->

*guónèi* — "domestic (inside the country)".

> *guónèi hángbān hěn piányi* — Domestic flights are cheap.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*guónèi* is built from 国 'a country' and *nèi* 'inside', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *kwok* and *nwojH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'to go in'.
