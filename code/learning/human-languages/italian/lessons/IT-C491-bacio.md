## You'll want to know: il bacio
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C491-INNAMORATI-05]; assesses=[] -->

**il bacio** — "kiss".

> Mi ha dato un bacio sulla guancia.
>
> *She gave me a kiss on the cheek.*

The plural is *i baci*. At the end of a message: *baci* — love.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*bacio* is inherited from Latin *bāsium* "a kiss" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short).
