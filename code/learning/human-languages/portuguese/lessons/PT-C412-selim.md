## You'll want to know: o selim
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C412-RODAS-03]; assesses=[] -->

**o selim** — "the saddle (of a bicycle)".

> O selim da bicicleta está muito alto.
>
> *The bicycle saddle is too high.*

A horse's saddle is *a sela*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*selim* is a small form of *sela* 'saddle', which is inherited from Latin *sella* 'seat' (EtymDB).
