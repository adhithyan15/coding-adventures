## You'll want to know: der Kompass
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C433-KOMPASS-02]; assesses=[] -->

**der Kompass** — "the compass".

> Im Wald hilft ihr der Kompass.
>
> *In the forest the compass helps her.*

Plural: *die Kompasse*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Kompass* was borrowed from Italian *compasso* 'compass, pair of compasses', usually traced to a Vulgar Latin verb built on Latin *passus* 'step'. EtymDB lists the German word without a source.
