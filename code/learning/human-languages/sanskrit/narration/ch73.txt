Sanskrit, chapter 73: Sing, Tell, Cry, Call.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
गायति (gāyati) — sings.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for he brings, then the Sanskrit for she gives.

You'll want to know: गायति.
गायति (gāyati) — "(he, she) sings". What is sung is a गीतम्.

What you've built.
The verb of a song.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: gāyati]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: gāyati, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say gītaṁ gāyati]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Sanskrit for he brings, then the Sanskrit for she gives, then say gāyati again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does गायति (gāyati) mean? ("Sings".) Say it once more.

Lesson 5 of 5.

The verb inside the greeting.
