Telugu, chapter 405: Money and the Economy.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
ఆర్థిక (ārthika) — economic; financial.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for independent, then the Telugu for unemployment.

You'll want to know: ఆర్థిక.
ఆర్థిక — ārthika — "economic; financial".
దేశం ఆర్థిక సమస్యల్లో ఉంది. dēśaṁ ārthika samasyallō undi. The country is in economic trouble.
Before a noun: ఆర్థిక (ārthika) సహాయం, financial help. ఆర్థికంగా, financially.

The word, taken apart.
ఆర్థిక (ārthika) is borrowed from Sanskrit ārthika, "significant; wise; rich" (Monier-Williams), from artha, "aim, wealth"; Telugu uses it for money matters.

Lesson 2 of 6.
ద్రవ్యోల్బణం (dravyōlbaṇaṁ) — inflation (of prices).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for unemployment, then the Telugu for economic.

You'll want to know: ద్రవ్యోల్బణం.
ద్రవ్యోల్బణం — dravyōlbaṇaṁ — "inflation (of prices)".
ద్రవ్యోల్బణం వల్ల ధరలు పెరుగుతున్నాయి. dravyōlbaṇaṁ valla dharalu perugutunnāyi. Prices are rising because of inflation.
A newspaper word; in talk, ధరలు పెరగడం, prices going up.

The word, taken apart.
ద్రవ్యోల్బణం (dravyōlbaṇaṁ) is built from Sanskrit dravya, "substance, property" (CDIAL 6625), and ulbaṇa, "laid over in addition" (Monier-Williams); the compound is a modern one.

Lesson 3 of 6.
పెట్టుబడి (peṭṭubaḍi) — an investment; capital.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for economic, then the Telugu for inflation.

You'll want to know: పెట్టుబడి.
పెట్టుబడి — peṭṭubaḍi — "an investment; capital".
అతను కొత్త దుకాణంలో పెట్టుబడి పెట్టాడు. atanu kotta dukāṇaṁlō peṭṭubaḍi peṭṭāḍu. He invested in a new shop.
పెట్టుబడి (peṭṭubaḍi) పెట్టు, to invest; the first part is పెట్టు, to put.

The word, taken apart.
పెట్టుబడి (peṭṭubaḍi) is native Telugu, built on peṭṭu, "to put, to place"; the origin of the second part, -బడి, is uncertain.

Lesson 5 of 6.
రుణం (ruṇaṁ) — a loan; a debt.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for an investment, then the Telugu for a budget.

You'll want to know: రుణం.
రుణం — ruṇaṁ — "a loan; a debt".
రైతులు బ్యాంకు నుంచి రుణం తీసుకున్నారు. raitulu byāṅku nuñci ruṇaṁ tīsukunnāru. The farmers took a loan from the bank.
Plural: రుణాలు, ruṇālu.
Formal beside అప్పు: banks and governments give రుణాలు.

The word, taken apart.
రుణం (ruṇaṁ) is borrowed from Sanskrit ṛṇa, "anything due, a debt, money owed" (Monier-Williams).
