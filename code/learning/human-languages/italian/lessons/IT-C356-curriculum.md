## You'll want to know: il curriculum
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C356-CANDIDATURA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**il curriculum** — "the CV, the résumé".

> Ho mandato il mio curriculum a dieci aziende.
>
> *I sent my CV to ten companies.*

Short for *curriculum vitae*; the plural is the same: *i curriculum*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*curriculum* is taken whole from Latin *curriculum* "a running, a race, a racetrack" (Lewis & Short), built on *currere* "to run"; *curriculum vitae* is "the path of a life".
