## You'll want to know: les décombres
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C303-SEISME-05]; assesses=[] -->

**les décombres** — "the rubble, the debris".

> Les secours cherchent dans les décombres.
>
> *The rescue teams are searching in the rubble.*

Plural only.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*décombres* is built on Old French *descombrer* "to clear away", the opposite of *encombrer* "to block"; the root is usually traced to a Gaulish word for a blockage, and the origin is not settled (EtymDB lists no parent).
