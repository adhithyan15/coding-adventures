# Review — at the station, and when things go wrong

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[SA-GRAMMAR-C280-DIRECTION-01, SA-GRAMMAR-C280-DIRECTION-02] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "where is the station?" and "go straight on". (*sthānakaṃ kutra asti?*, *ṛju gacchatu*.)

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[SA-GRAMMAR-C281-TICKET-01, SA-GRAMMAR-C281-TICKET-02, SA-LEX-C281-AGRIMAH-01, SA-GRAMMAR-C281-PROBLEM-01, SA-GRAMMAR-C281-PROBLEM-02, SA-LEX-C281-AUSADHAM-01, SA-SCRIPT-RECOG-228, SA-LEX-C172-TRV172-02, SA-LEX-C101-THINGS101-05] -->

[PAUSE 2s each]
- [YOU SAY: One ticket, please. How much is it?]
- [YOU SAY: When is the next train? It will leave in the evening.]
- [YOU SAY: My bag is lost, and the train has gone.]
- [YOU SAY: I am ill. Where is the medicine?]
- [YOU SAY: Please help.]
- [YOU READ: **औषधम्** aloud, and say the sign that is its sitting form]

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[SA-GRAMMAR-C281-TICKET-01, SA-GRAMMAR-C281-TICKET-02, SA-GRAMMAR-C281-PROBLEM-01, SA-GRAMMAR-C281-PROBLEM-02] -->

[PAUSE 3s] **द्वे यानपत्रे**: how many tickets? (Two.) **मम माला नष्टा**: why **नष्टा**? (**माला** is feminine.) Now the other way round: "the next bus"? (**अग्रिमं लोकयानम्**.)
