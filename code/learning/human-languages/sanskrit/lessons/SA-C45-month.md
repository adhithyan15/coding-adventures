## You'll want to know: मासः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C45-DAYTIME-04]; assesses=[] -->

**मासः** (*māsaḥ*) — "a month".

**मासः** is a month, and it is the moon's own word.

You have चन्द्रः for the moon overhead. **मासः** is that same moon counted: the stretch from one to the next.

Latin *mēnsis*, Greek *mēn*, English *month* and *moon* are all one word travelled west, and this is among the securest cousins in the whole family. Ends in **-अः**.
