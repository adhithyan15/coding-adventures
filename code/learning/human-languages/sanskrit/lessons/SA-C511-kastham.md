## You'll want to know: काष्ठम्
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C511-KASTHA511-01]; assesses=[] -->

**काष्ठम्** — *kāṣṭham* — "firewood; a piece of wood".

> माता चुल्ल्यां काष्ठं स्थापयति।
>
> *mātā cullyāṃ kāṣṭhaṃ sthāpayati.*
>
> Mother puts firewood in the stove.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**काष्ठम्** (*kāṣṭham*) is the word MW records for a piece of wood, timber, a stick; MW gives no derivation, so its origin is uncertain (MW).
