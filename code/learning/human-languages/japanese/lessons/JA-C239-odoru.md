## You'll want to know: おどる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C239-PASTIME239-01]; assesses=[] -->

**おどる** — *odoru* — "to dance".

> **まつりで みんなと おどりました。** — *matsuri de minna to odorimashita* — "I danced with everyone at the festival."

Polite **おどります**, *odorimasu*; a godan verb.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**おどる** (*odoru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
