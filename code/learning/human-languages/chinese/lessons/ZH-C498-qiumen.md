## You'll want to know: 球门
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C498-PITCH498-03]; assesses=[] -->

**球门** — *qiúmén* — "goalmouth; goal (in football)".

> **他站在球门前边** *tā zhàn zài qiúmén qiánbian* — He's standing in front of the goal.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**球门** (*qiúmén*) is built from 球 'a ball' and 门 'a gate', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *gjuw* and *mwon* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which says the character is made of two door leaves.
