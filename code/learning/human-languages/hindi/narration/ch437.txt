Hindi, chapter 437: The Monsoon.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
मानसून (mānsūn) — the monsoon.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a raincoat, then the Hindi for to pour (of rain).

You'll want to know: मानसून.
मानसून — mānsūn — "the monsoon".
इस साल मानसून जल्दी आया। is sāl mānsūn jaldī āyā. The monsoon came early this year.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
मानसून (mānsūn) is borrowed from English monsoon, a word English itself took from the Indian Ocean trade; Hindi also has its own word for the rainy season.

Lesson 2 of 6.
बरसात (barsāt) — the rainy season.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: बरसात.
बरसात — barsāt — "the rainy season".
बरसात में गाँव बहुत हरा हो जाता है। barsāt meṁ gā̃v bahut harā ho jātā hai. In the rainy season the village turns very green.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
बरसात (barsāt) is a tadbhava of Sanskrit varṣārātri, "the rainy season" (CDIAL 11398), literally the rain nights; CDIAL lists Hindi barsāt.

Lesson 4 of 6.
छाना (chānā) — to spread over (of clouds); to cover.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for the rainy season, then the Hindi for damp.

You'll want to know: छाना.
छाना — chānā — "to spread over (of clouds); to cover".
आसमान में काले बादल छा गए। āsmān meṁ kāle bādal chā gae. Dark clouds spread across the sky.
Past (it …): वह कोहरा पूरे शहर पर छा गया, vah kohrā pūre śahar par chā gayā.

The word, taken apart.
छाना (chānā) is a tadbhava of Sanskrit chādayati, "covers, veils" (CDIAL 5018), which lists Hindi chānā, "to thatch, spread around": clouds that छा जाते cover the sky like a roof.

Lesson 5 of 6.
पसीजना (pasījnā) — to sweat; to soften (of the heart).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for damp, then the Hindi for to spread over.

You'll want to know: पसीजना.
पसीजना — pasījnā — "to sweat; to soften (of the heart)".
बरसात की गर्मी में हाथ पसीजते हैं। barsāt kī garmī meṁ hāth pasījte haiṁ. In the damp heat of the rains your hands get sweaty.
Past (it …): वह गिलास ठंड से पसीज गया, vah gilās ṭhaṇḍ se pasīj gayā.

The word, taken apart.
पसीजना (pasījnā) goes back to prasvidyati, "sweats", a form CDIAL reconstructs (CDIAL 8896), which lists Hindi pasījnā; पसीना, sweat, is its cousin. A heart that पसीजता softens with pity.
