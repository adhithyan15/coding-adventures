# (una carta del colegio) — read it, then say what it wants

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-C466-INFORME-01, ES-LEX-C466-INFORME-03] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say **el director** and **el comportamiento**. A letter with both
words in it is rarely good news, and rarely as bad as it looks.

## Reading: the letter
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-C466-INFORME-02, ES-LEX-C466-INFORME-04] -->

> Estimada familia:
>
> Les escribo para informarles de que el **comportamiento** de su hijo en
> clase ha mejorado de forma **visible** durante este trimestre.
>
> Me gustaría **discutir** con ustedes cómo mantener esa mejora. Pueden pedir
> una cita en secretaría.
>
> Atentamente,
>
> La **directora**

## Grammar Lens: what the letter is not doing
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-C466-INFORME-03, ES-LEX-C466-INFORME-04] -->

A reader who knows *discutir* only as *argue* will read this as a summons. It
is not one, and two words tell you so.

| the word | what it settles |
|---|---|
| *ha mejorado* | the news is good |
| *discutir* **con ustedes** | a conversation, not a row |

*Discutir con* leans towards arguing when nothing else is on the table. Here a
topic follows it — *cómo mantener esa mejora* — which is exactly the mixed case
you were warned about, and the good news settles it. The preposition leans; the
content decides.

Note also **de forma visible**. The letter does not say the child is visible;
it says the improvement can be seen. That reading comes from *videre* — seeing
is done to a thing — rather than from the ending, which points either way.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-C466-INFORME-01, ES-LEX-C466-INFORME-02, ES-LEX-C466-INFORME-03, ES-LEX-C466-INFORME-04] -->

[PAUSE 3s] Say in one sentence what the letter reports and what it asks for.

[PAUSE 3s] Now say the same news to a friend, out of the formal register:
start with *se porta* instead of *el comportamiento*.

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-C466-INFORME-01, ES-LEX-C466-INFORME-02, ES-LEX-C466-INFORME-03, ES-LEX-C466-INFORME-04] -->

[PAUSE 3s] Who wrote it? (**La directora**.) What improved? (**El
comportamiento**.) How much? (**De forma visible**.) What does she want?
(**Discutir** — to talk it over.)
