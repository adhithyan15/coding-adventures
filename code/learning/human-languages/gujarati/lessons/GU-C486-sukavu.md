## You'll want to know: સુકાવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C486-AAROGYA486-05]; assesses=[] -->

**સુકાવું** — *sukāvũ* — "to waste away; to dry up".

> લાંબા તાવમાં બાળક સુકાઈ ગયું.
>
> *lā̃bā tāvmā̃ bāḷak sukāī gayũ.*
>
> The child wasted away during the long fever.

Past (it …): તે છોડ સુકાઈ ગયો, *te choḍ sukāī gayo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**સુકાવું** (*sukāvũ*) goes back to a reconstructed *\*śuṣkati*, "dries"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb, "to become emaciated" (CDIAL 12552).

## What you've built

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
