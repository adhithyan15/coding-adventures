## You'll want to know: rápido
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C482-COMO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**rápido** — "fast, quick".

> El tren rápido llega antes.
>
> *The fast train gets there sooner.*

As an adverb it also means quickly: *ven rápido*.
