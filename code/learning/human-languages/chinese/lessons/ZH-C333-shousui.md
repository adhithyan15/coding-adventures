## You'll want to know: shǒusuì
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C333-EVE333-04]; assesses=[] -->

*shǒusuì* — "to keep the New Year vigil".

> *chī le niányèfàn yǐhòu wǒmen yìqǐ shǒusuì* — After the reunion dinner we stayed up together.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*shǒusuì* is built from *shǒu* 'to guard' and 岁 'a year', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *syuwX* and *sjwejH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to keep watch over'.
