## You'll want to know: итог
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C285-SOBYTIYE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**итог** (*itóg*) — "the result, the total".

> В итоге мы решили остаться дома.
>
> *V itóge my reshíli ostátsya dóma.*
>
> In the end we decided to stay at home.

*В итоге* — in the end; *подвести итоги* — to sum up.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*итог* is usually traced to *и-*, a form of *из* "out", and the root of *течь* "to flow": what flows out at the end. EtymDB lists no parent.
