## You'll want to know: ぎょぎょう
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C490-SEA490-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ぎょぎょう** — *gyogyō* — "the fishing industry".

> **この みなとは ぎょぎょうの まちです。** — *kono minato wa gyogyō no machi desu* — "This port is a fishing town."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ぎょぎょう** (*gyogyō*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *gyo*, "fishing" and *gyō*, "business" (Middle Chinese *ngjaep*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
