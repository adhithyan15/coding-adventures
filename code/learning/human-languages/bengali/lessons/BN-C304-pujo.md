## You'll want to know: পুজো
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C304-UTSOB304-02]; assesses=[] -->

**পুজো** — *pujo* — "a puja; a worship festival".

> পুজোর সময় সবাই নতুন জামা পরে।
>
> *pujor sômôy sôbāi nôtun jāmā pôre.*
>
> At puja time everyone wears new clothes.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**পুজো** (*pujo*) is the spoken form of Sanskrit *pūjā*, 'honour, worship' (Monier-Williams), which the written word পূজা keeps. In Bengal পুজো alone means the great autumn festival.
