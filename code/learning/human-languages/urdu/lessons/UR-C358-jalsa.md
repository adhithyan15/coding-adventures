## You'll want to know: جلسہ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C358-JALSA358-01]; assesses=[] -->

**جلسہ** — *jalsā* — "a public meeting; a rally".

> کل شام میدان میں جلسہ ہو گا۔
>
> *kal shām maidān meṅ jalsā ho gā.*
>
> There will be a rally on the ground tomorrow evening.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**جلسہ** (*jalsā*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *jalsa*, "a sitting, a session".
