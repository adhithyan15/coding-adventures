## You'll want to know: fǎnbó
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C353-POINT353-04]; assesses=[] -->

*fǎnbó* — "to argue back; to refute".

> *yīnwèi tā shuō de hěn yǒu dàoli suǒyǐ wǒ méiyǒu fǎnbó* — He had a point, so I didn't argue back.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*fǎnbó* is built from *fǎn* and *bó*, both inherited from Middle Chinese, *pjonX* and *paewk* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to turn over' and the second 'a horse of mixed colour'. How the pair came to mean arguing back is not settled.
