## You'll want to know: brindar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C318-FESTEJAR-05]; assesses=[] -->

**brindar** — "to toast, to drink to".

> Brindámos à saúde da avó.
>
> *We drank to grandmother’s health.*

*Brindar a* + person or thing.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*brindar* is built on *brinde* "a toast" (EtymDB), which goes back to a German drinking phrase.
