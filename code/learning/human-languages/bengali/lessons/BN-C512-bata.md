## You'll want to know: বাটা
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C512-UNUN512-05]; assesses=[] -->

**বাটা** — *bāṭā* — "to grind (spices) on a stone".

> দিদিমা শিলনোড়ায় মশলা বাটলেন।
>
> *Didimā shilnoṛāy môshlā bāṭlen.*
>
> My grandmother ground the spices on the grinding stone.

Past (he/she …): তিনি আদা বাটলেন, *tini ādā bāṭlen*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**বাটা** (*bāṭā*) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Bengali *bāṭā*, "to pound, to crush", under Sanskrit *vartayati*, "makes turn" (CDIAL 11356).
