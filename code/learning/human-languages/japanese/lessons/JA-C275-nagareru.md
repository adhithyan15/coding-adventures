## You'll want to know: ながれる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C275-TSUNAMI275-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ながれる** — *nagareru* — "to flow; to be washed away".

> **かわの みずが はやく ながれています。** — *kawa no mizu ga hayaku nagarete imasu* — "The river is running fast."

Polite **ながれます**, *nagaremasu*; an ichidan verb. Past (polite): **くるまが ながれました。**, *kuruma ga nagaremashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ながれる** (*nagareru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict).
