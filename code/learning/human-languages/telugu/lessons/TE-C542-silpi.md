## You'll want to know: శిల్పి
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C542-SILPAM542-04]; assesses=[] -->

**శిల్పి** — *śilpi* — "a sculptor".

> ఆ శిల్పి రాతితో బొమ్మలు చెక్కుతాడు.
>
> *ā śilpi rātitō bommalu cekkutāḍu.*
>
> That sculptor carves figures from stone.

Plural శిల్పులు. చెక్కు, to carve.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**శిల్పి** (*śilpi*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *śilpin*, "skilled in art; an artificer" (CDIAL 12471).
