## You'll want to know: کفگیر
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C358-QABLAME-05]; assesses=[] -->

**کفگیر** (*kafgir*) — "a slotted spoon, a skimmer".

> برنج را با کفگیر در بشقاب کشید.
>
> *Berenj râ bâ kafgir dar boshqâb keshid.*
>
> She served the rice onto the plate with a skimmer.

*kaf* "foam" + *gir* "taker". *Kafgir be tah-e dig khord*, "the skimmer hit the bottom of the pot": nothing is left.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*kafgir* joins *kaf* "foam" and *gir-*, the present stem of *gereftan* "to take": the foam-taker. EtymDB lists the word without a parent.
