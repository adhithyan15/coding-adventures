## You'll want to know: おもいで
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C311-OMOIDE311-01]; assesses=[] -->

**おもいで** — *omoide* — "a memory; a keepsake".

> **なつやすみは いい おもいでに なりました。** — *natsuyasumi wa ii omoide ni narimashita* — "The summer holidays became a happy memory."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**おもいで** (*omoide*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, built from *omoi*, "thinking" (from *omou*), and *de*, "coming out" (from *deru*) (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
