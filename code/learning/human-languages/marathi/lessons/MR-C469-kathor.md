## You'll want to know: कठोर
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C469-KATHOR469-01]; assesses=[] -->

**कठोर** — *kaṭhor* — "harsh; stern".

> ते शिक्षक खूप कठोर आहेत.
>
> *te śikṣak khūp kaṭhor āhet.*
>
> That teacher is very stern.

Unchanging: कठोर शब्द, harsh words; कठोर नियम, strict rules.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**कठोर** (*kaṭhor*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *kaṭhora*, "hard, stiff; cruel" (CDIAL 2651).
