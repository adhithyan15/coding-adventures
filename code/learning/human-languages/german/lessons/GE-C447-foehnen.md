## You'll want to know: föhnen
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C447-FRISEUR-02]; assesses=[] -->

**föhnen** — "to blow-dry".

> Nach dem Waschen föhnt sie sich die Haare.
>
> *After washing her hair she blow-dries it.*

*Der Föhn* — the hair dryer, and also a warm wind from the mountains.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*föhnen* is built on *Föhn*, a warm dry wind in the mountains, which goes back through Old High German *phōnno* to Latin *favōnius* 'mild west wind' (EtymDB; Lewis & Short). The hair dryer was named after the wind.
