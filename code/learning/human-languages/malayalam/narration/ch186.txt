Malayalam, chapter 186: To share, To hug, To quarrel, To sulk, To tease.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
പങ്കുവെക്കുക (paṅkuvekkuka) — to share.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for to fly, then the Malayalam for to follow.

You'll want to know: പങ്കുവെക്കുക.
പങ്കുവെക്കുക — paṅkuvekkuka — "to share".

The word, taken apart.
പങ്കുവെക്കുക (paṅkuvekkuka) is native Dravidian, "lay out in shares": paṅku, "part, share" (DEDR 3808, with Tamil paku, "to divide"), and vekkuka, "to put, place" (DEDR 5549, with Tamil vai).

കെട്ടിപ്പിടിക്കുക (keṭṭippiṭikkuka) is native Dravidian, "bind and hold": the verb "to tie, clasp" (DEDR 1147, with Tamil kaṭṭu) and the verb "to seize, hold" (DEDR 4148, with Tamil piṭi). DEDR 1147 compares Sanskrit ghaṭṭa.

കളിയാക്കുക (kaḷiyākkuka) is native Dravidian, "make into a game": kaḷi, "play, jest" (DEDR 1374), and ākkuka, "to make" (DEDR 333). DEDR 1374 compares Sanskrit kalyā and kaḍ. DEDR 333 compares Sanskrit ām.
