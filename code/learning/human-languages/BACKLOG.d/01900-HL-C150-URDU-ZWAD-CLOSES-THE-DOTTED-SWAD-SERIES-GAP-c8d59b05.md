## HL-C150 — Urdu zwad closes the dotted swad-series gap

After Urdu retroflex re **ڑ** landed, Urdu zwad **ض** leads the reranked
source-gated queue at **1 affected realization** (tied with eight other glyphs
and selected first by the stable glyph ordering).

Northwestern University Libraries' open *Zer o Zabar* chapter on khe, ze,
zal, swad, and zwad identifies **ض** as the dotted member of the elongated
swad series and provides separate independent-form calligraphic and handwriting
animations. Michigan State University's open *Basic Urdu* independently
confirms the zwad name, its Urdu **z** sound, and the one-dot distinction from
swad.

This item closes the **ض** realization with its canonical Urdu letter record,
exact source ownership, a font-fitted independent learner path, and
cross-consumer evidence. The animations establish the body-first, dot-last
three-run order; the printed outline is used only to fit those movements.
