## You'll want to know: स्पष्टीकरण
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C342-HETU342-04]; assesses=[] -->

**स्पष्टीकरण** — *spaṣṭīkaraṇ* — "an explanation".

> उशिराबद्दल त्याने स्पष्टीकरण दिलं.
>
> *uśirābaddal tyāne spaṣṭīkaraṇ dilaṁ.*
>
> He gave an explanation for being late.

Neuter.

स्पष्टीकरण देणे, to give an explanation; it is built on स्पष्ट, clear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**स्पष्टीकरण** (*spaṣṭīkaraṇ*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *spaṣṭīkaraṇa*, "making clear" (Monier-Williams).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
