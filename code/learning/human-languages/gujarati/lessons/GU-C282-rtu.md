# ઋતુ (ṛtu) — a season

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-GRAMMAR-C282-THI-SINCE-01, GU-LEX-C93-TIME93-02, GU-LEX-C93-TIME93-03, GU-LEX-C93-TIME93-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say "summer", "winter" and "the monsoon". (**ઉનાળો**, **શિયાળો**, **ચોમાસું**.) Then **મોસમ**: "a season; the weather".

## You'll want to know: ઋતુ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C282-RTU-01]; assesses=[GU-LEX-C93-TIME93-04, GU-LEX-TRIJU, GU-LEX-C282-SAUTHI-01, GU-LEX-C83-NUM3-05] -->

**ઋતુ** — *ṛtu* — "a season". It is feminine. **મોસમ** is also the weather; **ઋતુ** is one part of the year.

> **અહીં ત્રણ ઋતુ છે: ઉનાળો, ચોમાસું અને શિયાળો.**
>
> *ahī̃ traṇ ṛtu che: unāḷo, comāsũ ane śiyāḷo.* — "There are three seasons here: summer, the monsoon and winter."

> **તમને કઈ ઋતુ સૌથી વધારે ગમે છે?**
>
> *tamne kaī ṛtu sauthī vadhāre game che?* — "Which season do you like most?"

**કઈ** is **કયું**, "which", following **ઋતુ**.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ઋતુ** (*ṛtu*) is a tatsama: Sanskrit *ṛtu*, "a fixed time; a season" (Monier-Williams), taken into Gujarati as it was, with the vowel letter **ઋ** that the next lesson writes.
