## You'll want to know: el ave
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C694-ANIMALES-02]; assesses=[] -->

**el ave** — "the large bird, the bird (formal); poultry".

> En el lago hay muchas aves.
>
> *There are a lot of birds on the lake.*

Feminine, but *el ave* because of the stressed a; plural *las aves*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ave* is inherited from Latin *avis* "a bird" (EtymDB).
