# उद्घाटन करणे (udghāṭan karṇe) — to inaugurate

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MR-LEX-C322-PURASKAR322-03, MR-LEX-C322-PURASKAR322-04] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Marathi for an honour, then the Marathi for to congratulate.

## You'll want to know: उद्घाटन करणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C322-PURASKAR322-05]; assesses=[] -->

**उद्घाटन करणे** — *udghāṭan karṇe* — "to inaugurate".

> महापौरांनी नव्या पुलाचं उद्घाटन केलं.
>
> *mahāpaurānnī navyā pulācaṁ udghāṭan kelaṁ.*
>
> The mayor inaugurated the new bridge.

Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने उद्घाटन केलं, *tyāne udghāṭan kelaṁ*.

What is opened takes -चं: शाळेचं उद्घाटन करणे.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**उद्घाटन करणे** (*udghāṭan karṇe*) joins *udghāṭan*, a tatsama taken from Sanskrit *udghāṭana*, 'opening, unlocking' (Monier-Williams), to *karṇe*, 'to do'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
