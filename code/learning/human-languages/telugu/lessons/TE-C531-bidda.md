# బిడ్డ (biḍḍa) — an infant; one's child

## You'll want to know: బిడ్డ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C531-SANTANAM531-01]; assesses=[] -->

**బిడ్డ** — *biḍḍa* — "an infant; one's child".

> ఆ తల్లి బిడ్డను ఎత్తుకుంది.
>
> *ā talli biḍḍanu ettukundi.*
>
> That mother picked up her baby.

Also one's own child at any age: నా బిడ్డ.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**బిడ్డ** (*biḍḍa*) is a Telugu word for a baby or one's own child; its origin is not settled.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
