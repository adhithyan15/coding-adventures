## You'll want to know: नस
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C479-NADI479-02]; assesses=[] -->

**नस** — *nas* — "a vein; a nerve".

> रक्त नसांमधून वाहतं.
>
> *rakta nasāmmadhūn vāhtaṁ.*
>
> Blood flows through the veins.

Feminine. Plural: नसा, *nasā*.

Plural नसा. नस दबणे, a nerve gets pinched.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**नस** (*nas*) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Marathi *nas*, "vein, nerve, tendon", under Sanskrit *snasā* (CDIAL 13784).
