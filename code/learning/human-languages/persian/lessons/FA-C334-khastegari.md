## You'll want to know: خواستگاری
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C334-ASHEQ-02]; assesses=[] -->

**خواستگاری** (*khâstegâri*) — "a marriage proposal".

> خواستگاری در خانه پدر دختر انجام شد.
>
> *Khâstegâri dar khâne-ye pedar-e dokhtar anjâm shod.*
>
> The proposal took place at the young woman's family home.

Traditionally the man's family visits the woman's family to ask for her hand. From *khâstan* "to want, to ask for".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*khâstegâri* is built on *khâste*, from the native verb *khâstan* "to want, to ask for", with the suffixes *-gâr* and *-i*: the asking. EtymDB lists no entry.
