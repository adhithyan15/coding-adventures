## You'll want to know: しょくざい
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C496-SOFT496-05]; assesses=[] -->

**しょくざい** — *shokuzai* — "an ingredient; foodstuff".

> **いちばで しょくざいを かいます。** — *ichiba de shokuzai o kaimasu* — "I buy ingredients at the market."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**しょくざい** (*shokuzai*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *shoku*, "eat" (Middle Chinese *zyik*) and *zai*, "ingredients" (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
