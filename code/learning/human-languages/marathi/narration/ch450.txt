Marathi, chapter 450: Mind and Heart.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
मन (man) — the mind; the heart, as the seat of feeling.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for dance, then the Marathi for a stage.

You'll want to know: मन.
मन — man — "the mind; the heart, as the seat of feeling".
आज माझं मन शांत आहे. āj mājhaṁ man śānt āhe. My mind is calm today.
Neuter.
Neuter: माझं मन (man). मनात, in the mind; मनापासून, from the heart.

The word, taken apart.
मन (man) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit manas, "mind" (CDIAL 9822); Marathi keeps the stem and drops the final -s.

Masculine. जीव (jīv) लावणे, to grow fond of someone; जिवंत, alive.

The word, taken apart.
जीव (jīv) is a tatsama, Sanskrit jīva, "alive, a living being"; CDIAL lists Marathi jīv, "living being, energy", under it (CDIAL 5239).

आश्चर्य — āścarya — "surprise; astonishment".
मीराला पाहून मला खूप आश्चर्य वाटलं. mīrālā pāhūn malā khūp āścarya vāṭlaṁ. I was very surprised to see Meera.
Neuter.
आश्चर्य (āścarya) वाटणे, to be surprised, with the dative: मला आश्चर्य (āścarya) वाटलं. आश्चर्यकारक, amazing.

The word, taken apart.
आश्चर्य (āścarya) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit āścarya, "wonderful" (CDIAL 1464); in Marathi it is a neuter noun, "surprise".

Lesson 5 of 5.
हेलावणे (helāvṇe) — to be deeply moved.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for happiness, then the Marathi for surprise.

You'll want to know: हेलावणे.
हेलावणे — helāvṇe — "to be deeply moved".
आईचं पत्र वाचून तो हेलावला. āīcaṁ patra vācūn to helāvlā. Reading his mother's letter, he was deeply moved.
Past (he …): तो हेलावला, to helāvlā.
Often with मन (man): तिचं मन (man) हेलावलं, her heart was stirred.

The word, taken apart.
हेलावणे (helāvṇe) is a Marathi verb whose origin is not settled.
