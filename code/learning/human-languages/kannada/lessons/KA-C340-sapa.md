## You'll want to know: ಶಾಪ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C340-DANTAKATHE340-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ಶಾಪ** — *śāpa* — "a curse".

> ರಾಕ್ಷಸನ ಶಾಪದಿಂದ ರಾಣಿ ಕಲ್ಲಾದಳು.
>
> *rākṣasana śāpadinda rāṇi kallādaḷu.*
>
> Through the demon's curse the queen turned to stone.

A curse is "given": ಶಾಪ ಕೊಡು, to curse.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಶಾಪ** (*śāpa*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *śāpa*, "a curse, abuse" (CDIAL 12393), from the root *śap*, "to curse".
