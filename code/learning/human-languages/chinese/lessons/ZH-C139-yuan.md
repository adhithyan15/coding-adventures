## You'll want to know: yuán
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C139-COLOUR139-04]; assesses=[] -->

*yuán* — "round".

> *zhè zhāng zhuōzi shì yuán de* — This table is a round one.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*yuán* is inherited from Middle Chinese *hjwen* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'the form of heaven'. The plain sense round is not among the glosses, so the step is not settled.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing things.
