Lesson 1 of 5.
উত্তেজনা (uttejônā) — excitement; tension.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a public statement, then the Bengali for to mention.

You'll want to know: উত্তেজনা.
উত্তেজনা — uttejônā — "excitement; tension".
খবরটা শুনে পাড়ায় উত্তেজনা ছড়াল। khôbôrṭā shune pāṛāy uttejônā chhôṛālô. When the news came, tension spread through the neighbourhood.

The word, taken apart.
উত্তেজনা (uttejônā) is Sanskrit uttejana (feminine uttejanā), 'incitement, stimulation' (Monier-Williams), from ut- 'up' and tejas 'sharpness, heat': a stirring up.

You'll want to know: রটানো.
রটানো — rôṭāno — "to spread a rumour; to put about".
কেউ রটাল যে চোর আবার আসবে। keu rôṭālô je chor ābār āsbe. Someone put it about that the thief would come back.
Past (he/she …): সেই লোক খবরটা রটাল, sei lok khôbôrṭā rôṭālô.

The word, taken apart.
রটানো (rôṭāno) is usually linked with Sanskrit raṭati, 'cries, yells' (CDIAL 10590): what is shouted about gets about.

Lesson 3 of 5.
হইচই (hôichôi) — an uproar; a commotion.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for tension, then the Bengali for to spread a rumour.

You'll want to know: হইচই.
হইচই — hôichôi — "an uproar; a commotion".
খবরটা শুনে পাড়ায় হইচই পড়ে গেল। khôbôrṭā shune pāṛāy hôichôi pôṛe gelô. When the news came, the neighbourhood was in an uproar.

The word, taken apart.
হইচই (hôichôi) is an expressive word, like English hubbub: its sound is the noise. হইচই পড়ে যাওয়া, an uproar breaks out. No dictionary we use records an older form, so its history is not settled.

Lesson 4 of 5.
ফিসফিস করা (phisphis kôrā) — to whisper.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for to spread a rumour, then the Bengali for an uproar.

You'll want to know: ফিসফিস করা.
ফিসফিস করা — phisphis kôrā — "to whisper".
ক্লাসে ছাত্ররা ফিসফিস করে গল্প করছিল। klāse chhātrôrā phisphis kôre gôlpo kôrchhilô. In class the students were whispering to each other.
Past (he/she …): সেই মেয়ে ফিসফিস করল, sei meye phisphis kôrlô.

The word, taken apart.
ফিসফিস করা (phisphis kôrā) puts ফিসফিস, an imitative word for the hiss of a whisper, before করা. ফিসফিস করে বলা, to say something in a whisper. No dictionary we use records an older form, so its history is not settled.

দুর্ঘটনার খবরে শহরে চাঞ্চল্য ছড়াল। durghôṭônār khôbôre shôhôre chānchôlyô chhôṛālô. News of the accident caused a stir in the town.

The word, taken apart.
চাঞ্চল্য (chānchôlyô) is Sanskrit cāñcalya, 'unsteadiness, restlessness' (Monier-Williams); a town in a stir is a restless town.
