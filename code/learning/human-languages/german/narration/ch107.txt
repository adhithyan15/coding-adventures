das Büro — an office.

[pause 2 seconds]
Say die Jahre, the plural of das Jahr. And remember that German IS its years: ich bin, not ich habe.

You'll want to know: das Büro.
das Büro — "an office".

The word, taken apart.
Büro from French bureau, "desk, office", from bure, "woollen cloth" (EtymDB 2.1) — borrowed: the cloth covered the desk, then named the desk and the room.

Lesson 3 of 5.
die Kreuzung — a crossroads.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for an office, then the German for a corner.

You'll want to know: die Kreuzung.
die Kreuzung — "a crossroads".

The word, taken apart.
Kreuzung is the verb "to cross" + -ung: a crossing. The verb is built on the word for "a cross", Old High German chrūzi from Latin crux (EtymDB 2.1), a borrowing.
