## You'll want to know: gǔzhé
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C301-ESCAPE301-04]; assesses=[] -->

*gǔzhé* — "to break a bone".

> *tā de tuǐ gǔzhé le* — He broke his leg.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*gǔzhé* is built from *gǔ* 'a bone' and *zhé* 'to snap', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *kwot* and *tsyet* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first, quoting an older dictionary, 'the core of the flesh' and the second 'to bend and break'.

## What you've built
