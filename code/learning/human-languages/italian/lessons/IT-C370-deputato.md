## You'll want to know: il deputato
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C370-MAGGIORANZA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**il deputato** — "the member of parliament, the deputy".

> Il deputato ha votato contro la riforma.
>
> *The member of parliament voted against the reform.*

A woman is *la deputata*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*deputato* is a learned borrowing from Late Latin *dēputātus* "assigned, sent", from *dēputāre* "to allot, to assign" (Lewis & Short), built on *putāre* "to reckon".
