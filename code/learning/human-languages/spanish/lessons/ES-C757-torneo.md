## You'll want to know: el torneo
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C757-CAMPEON-02]; assesses=[] -->

**el torneo** — "the tournament".

> Ganó el torneo de tenis.
>
> *She won the tennis tournament.*

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*torneo* is built on *tornear*, which is usually traced to Old French *tornoier* "to joust", from Latin *tornāre* "to turn": knights wheeled their horses.
