## You'll want to know: さっきょくする
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C471-STAGE471-05]; assesses=[] -->

**さっきょくする** — *sakkyoku suru* — "to compose music".

> **かのじょは この うたを さっきょくしました。** — *kanojo wa kono uta o sakkyoku shimashita* — "She composed this song."

Polite **さっきょくします**, *sakkyokushimasu*; a する verb. Past (polite): **せんせいが きょくを さっきょくしました。**, *sensei ga kyoku o sakkyoku shimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**さっきょくする** (*sakkyoku suru*) is Sino-Japanese (kango) *sakkyoku* with the native verb *suru*, "to do": two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *sa*, "make" (Middle Chinese *tsak*) and *kyoku*, "melody" (*khjowk*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
