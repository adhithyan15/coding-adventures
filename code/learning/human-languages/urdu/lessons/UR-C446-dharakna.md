# دھڑکنا (dhaṛaknā) — to throb; to pound (of the heart)

## You'll want to know: دھڑکنا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C446-WORRY446-05]; assesses=[] -->

**دھڑکنا** — *dhaṛaknā* — "to throb; to pound (of the heart)".

> امتحان سے پہلے میرا دل دھڑک رہا تھا۔
>
> *imtihān se pehle merā dil dhaṛak rahā thā.*
>
> Before the exam my heart was pounding.

Past (it …): وہ دل دھڑکا, *voh dil dhaṛkā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**دھڑکنا** (*dhaṛaknā*) is inherited from *dhaḍ*, "throb, beat", a form CDIAL reconstructs (CDIAL 6711); **دھڑکن**, "a heartbeat", is its noun.
