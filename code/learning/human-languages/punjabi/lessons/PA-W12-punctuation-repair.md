# Put the stops back

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-SKILL-CONNECTED-READING] -->

You have already checked endings, word spaces, and one spelling. Today those
are all fixed. Look only for where each familiar sentence stops.

## Reading — three missing marks
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-LEX-NAMASTE-01, PA-SKILL-CONNECTED-READING] -->

> ਨਮਸਤੇ ਮਨਨ ਮੇਰਾ ਨਾਂ ਅਮਨ ਹੈ ਮੈਂ ਪੰਜਾਬੀ ਬੋਲਦਾ ਹਾਂ

The greeting ends after the reader's name. The name fact ends after **ਹੈ**.
The language fact ends after **ਹਾਂ**. No word needs to move.

## Writing — repair only punctuation
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-SKILL-CONNECTED-READING] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: guided-copy -->

Insert **।** at those three stops. Keep every word and existing word space.
Leave one space after each new mark that is followed by another sentence.
