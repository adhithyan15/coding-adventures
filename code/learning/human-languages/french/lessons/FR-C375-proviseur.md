## You'll want to know: le proviseur
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C375-LYCEE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**le proviseur** — "the head teacher (of a lycée)".

> Le proviseur a parlé aux parents hier soir.
>
> *The head teacher spoke to the parents last night.*

In a *collège*, the head is *le principal*. Belgium: *le préfet*; Québec: *le directeur*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*proviseur* is a learned borrowing from Latin *prōvīsor* "one who foresees, a provider" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short), from *prōvidēre* "to foresee, to provide for".
