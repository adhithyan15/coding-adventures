## You'll want to know: कारखाना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C512-KARKHANA512-01]; assesses=[] -->

**कारखाना** — *kārkhānā* — "a factory".

> आमच्या शहराजवळ साखरेचा कारखाना आहे.
>
> *āmcyā śahrājvaḷ sākhrecā kārkhānā āhe.*
>
> There is a sugar factory near our town.

Masculine. Plural: कारखाने, *kārkhāne*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**कारखाना** (*kārkhānā*) is a loan from Persian *kārkhāna*, "workshop", from *kār*, "work", and *khāna*, "house".
