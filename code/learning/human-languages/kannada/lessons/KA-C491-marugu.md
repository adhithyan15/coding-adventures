# ಮರುಗು (marugu) — to feel pity; to grieve for

## You'll want to know: ಮರುಗು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C491-DUHKHA491-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ಮರುಗು** — *marugu* — "to feel pity; to grieve for".

> ಗಾಯಗೊಂಡ ಹಕ್ಕಿಯನ್ನು ನೋಡಿ ಮಗು ಮರುಗಿತು.
>
> *gāyagoṇḍa hakkiyannu nōḍi magu marugitu.*
>
> Seeing the injured bird, the child felt sorry for it.

Past (I …): ಮರುಗಿದೆ, *marugide*.

The one you feel for takes -ಗಾಗಿ: ಅವನಿಗಾಗಿ ಮರುಗು, to feel for him.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಮರುಗು** (*marugu*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 4769 lists Kannada *maṟugu*, "to burn, be very hot", and from there "to suffer, be distressed, grieve". Pity is pictured as a burning inside.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
