## You'll want to know: ಗೆದ್ದಲು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C477-PRANI477-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ಗೆದ್ದಲು** — *geddalu* — "a termite".

> ಗೆದ್ದಲು ಹಳೆಯ ಬಾಗಿಲನ್ನು ತಿಂದುಹಾಕಿತು.
>
> *geddalu haḷeya bāgilannu tinduhākitu.*
>
> Termites ate away the old door.

Also called white ants; their mound is a ಹುತ್ತ.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಗೆದ್ದಲು** (*geddalu*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 1548 lists Kannada *gedal*, *geddalu*, "a termite, a white ant".
