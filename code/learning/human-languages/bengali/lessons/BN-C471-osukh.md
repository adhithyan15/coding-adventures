## You'll want to know: অসুখ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C471-OSUKH471-01]; assesses=[] -->

**অসুখ** — *ôsukh* — "an illness".

> অসুখের জন্য অরুণ এক সপ্তাহ স্কুলে গেল না।
>
> *ôsukher jônyô ôrun ek sôptāhô skule gelô nā.*
>
> Arun missed a week of school because of illness.

অসুখ করা, to fall ill: আমার অসুখ করল.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**অসুখ** (*ôsukh*) is Sanskrit *asukha*, "unhappy, painful" (Monier-Williams): *a-*, "not", on *sukha*, the সুখ (ease) of an earlier lesson. Bengali made it the everyday word for an illness, a dis-ease.
