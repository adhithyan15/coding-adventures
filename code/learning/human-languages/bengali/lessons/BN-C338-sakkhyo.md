## You'll want to know: সাক্ষ্য
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C338-DOLIL338-03]; assesses=[] -->

**সাক্ষ্য** — *sākkhyô* — "testimony; evidence (given by a witness)".

> সাক্ষী আদালতে সাক্ষ্য দিল।
>
> *sākkhī ādālôte sākkhyô dilô.*
>
> The witness gave evidence in court.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**সাক্ষ্য** (*sākkhyô*) is Sanskrit *sākṣya*, testimony or evidence (Monier-Williams), the word for what a সাক্ষী, a witness, says. It is given with দেওয়া.
