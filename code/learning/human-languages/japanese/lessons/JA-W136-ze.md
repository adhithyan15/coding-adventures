# ぜ — せ, voiced to ze

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[JA-LEX-ANCHOR-KAZE, JA-LEX-C136-KAZOERU, JA-LEX-C127-QUAL127-04] -->

[PAUSE 15s] Three recalls, then the sign.

- [YOU RECALL: say *a wind*, or *a cold* — **R1**, one lesson back]
- [YOU RECALL: say *to count* — **R2**, five lessons back]
- [YOU RECALL: say *calm* — **R4**, eighty lessons back]

## Script — the change
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-SCRIPT-ZE-01]; assesses=[JA-SCRIPT-SE-01, JA-SCRIPT-DAKUTEN-01] -->

> **せ** *se* + **゛** → **ぜ** *ze*

Write **せ** in full, in the order you learned it. Then add the two short
strokes of the voicing mark at the upper right, the left one first, beside
the top of the right-hand vertical.

The *s* sign voices to *z*: *se* becomes *ze*. With it you write every sign
of the *z* row: **ざ じ ず ぜ ぞ**.

With **か** in front of it, you can now write the word from the last lesson:
**かぜ**, *kaze*, a wind, or a cold.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[JA-SCRIPT-ZE-01, JA-SCRIPT-SE-01, JA-SCRIPT-DAKUTEN-01, JA-LEX-ANCHOR-KAZE] -->

1. Write **せ**, then **ぜ**, and say *se*, *ze*.
2. Write **ざ じ ず ぜ ぞ** in order, and say each one.
3. Write **かぜ** from memory and say *kaze*.
