## You'll want to know: తరుగు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C548-VANTA548-03]; assesses=[] -->

**తరుగు** — *tarugu* — "to chop; to shred".

> వంట కోసం నేను ఉల్లిపాయలు తరిగాను.
>
> *vaṇṭa kōsaṁ nēnu ullipāyalu tarigānu.*
>
> I chopped the onions for the cooking.

Past (I …): తరిగాను, *tarigānu*.

Fine cutting of vegetables; కోయు is the general word for cutting.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**తరుగు** (*tarugu*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 3140 lists Telugu *taṟugu*, "to slice, to chop", with Tamil *taṟi*, "to cut off".
