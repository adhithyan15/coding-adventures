# Review — comparing, and how long

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[HI-LEX-C285-SABSE-01, HI-GRAMMAR-C285-MORE-LESS-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "Mira is taller than Arun". (*Mīrā Aruṇ se lambī hai*.) Then "the best". (*sabse acchā*.)

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[HI-LEX-C285-SABSE-01, HI-GRAMMAR-C285-MORE-LESS-01, HI-GRAMMAR-C285-SUPERLATIVE-GROUP-01, HI-GRAMMAR-C285-SINCE-FOR-01, HI-GRAMMAR-C285-ALL-DAY-01, HI-LEX-C56-ROAD-04, HI-LEX-C152-THINGS152-03, HI-GRAMMAR-C276-PAST-PERSON-01, HI-GRAMMAR-C280-RAY-MEIN-01, HI-GRAMMAR-C284-WANT-TO-BECOME-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s each]
- [YOU SAY: This shop is more expensive than that one.]
- [YOU SAY: In my opinion this is the best book in the library.]
- [YOU SAY: I have wanted to become a teacher for two years.]
- [YOU SAY: Yesterday it rained all day.]
- [YOU SAY: Since when have you lived here?]

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[HI-LEX-C285-SABSE-01, HI-GRAMMAR-C285-MORE-LESS-01, HI-GRAMMAR-C285-SUPERLATIVE-GROUP-01, HI-GRAMMAR-C285-SINCE-FOR-01, HI-GRAMMAR-C285-ALL-DAY-01] -->

[PAUSE 3s] "More expensive than"? (**से ज़्यादा महँगा**.) "The best in the library"? (**पुस्तकालय की सबसे अच्छी किताब**: **किताब** is feminine.) "For two years"? (**दो साल से**, with the present.) "All day"? (**दिन भर**.)
