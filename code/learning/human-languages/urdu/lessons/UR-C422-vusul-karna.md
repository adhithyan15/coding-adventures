## You'll want to know: وصول کرنا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C422-DALAL422-04]; assesses=[] -->

**وصول کرنا** — *vusūl karnā* — "to collect (money owed); to receive".

> دلال نے اپنی فیس وصول کی۔
>
> *dallāl ne apnī fīs vusūl kī.*
>
> The broker collected his fee.

Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے کرایہ وصول کیا, *us ne kirāyā vusūl kiyā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**وصول کرنا** (*vusūl karnā*) is built from *vusūl*, "receipt", usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *wuṣūl*, "arriving", and **کرنا**, "to do".
