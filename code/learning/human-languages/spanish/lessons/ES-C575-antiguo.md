## You'll want to know: antiguo
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C575-PASADO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**antiguo** — "old, ancient; former".

> Vivimos en un edificio muy antiguo.
>
> *We live in a very old building.*

Before a noun it can mean former: *mi antiguo jefe* — my old boss.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*antiguo* is inherited from Latin *antīquus* 'old, of former times' (EtymDB), through old Spanish *antigo*; it is built on *ante* 'before'.
