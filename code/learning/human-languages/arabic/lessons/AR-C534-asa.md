# عصى (ʿaṣā) — to disobey

## You'll want to know: عصى
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C534-NASIHA534-02]; assesses=[] -->

**عصى** — *ʿaṣā* — "to disobey".

> عصت البنت أمها وخرجت في المطر.
>
> *ʿaṣat al-bint ummahā wa-kharajat fī al-maṭar.*
>
> The girl disobeyed her mother and went out in the rain.

Present: يعصي, *yaʿṣī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**عصى** (*ʿaṣā*) is built on the native Arabic root *ʿ-ṣ-y*, "to resist, to rebel" (Lane). Its last root letter y shows as alif in the past and as ī in the present.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
