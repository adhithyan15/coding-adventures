## You'll want to know: ਬਾਕੀ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C280-CHANGE280-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ਬਾਕੀ** — *bākī* — "the rest; remaining".

> ਬਾਕੀ ਪੈਸੇ ਮੇਰੀ ਜੇਬ ਵਿੱਚ ਹਨ।
>
> *bākī paise merī jeb vich han.*
>
> The rest of the money is in my pocket.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਬਾਕੀ** (*bākī*) is a loan, usually traced through Persian to Arabic *bāqī*, "remaining".
