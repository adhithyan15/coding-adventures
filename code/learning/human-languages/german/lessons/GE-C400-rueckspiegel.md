## You'll want to know: der Rückspiegel
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C400-SICHT-04]; assesses=[] -->

**der Rückspiegel** — "the rear-view mirror".

> Vor dem Abbiegen schaut die Fahrerin in den Rückspiegel.
>
> *Before turning off, the driver looks in the rear-view mirror.*

Same form in the plural. The mirrors on the sides are *die Außenspiegel*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Rückspiegel* is built from *rück-* 'back' and *Spiegel* 'mirror'. *Spiegel* is an early borrowing from Latin *speculum* 'mirror', built on *specere* 'to look' (Lewis & Short); EtymDB lists Middle High German *spiegel*.
