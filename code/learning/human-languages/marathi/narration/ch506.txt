Marathi, chapter 506: Office Life.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
लिपिक (lipik) — a clerk.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a weaver, then the Marathi for to set up.

You'll want to know: लिपिक.
लिपिक — lipik — "a clerk".
लिपिकाने माझा अर्ज वाचला. lipikāne mājhā arja vāclā. The clerk read my application.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
लिपिक (lipik) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit lipika, "a scribe, a clerk" (Monier-Williams), built on lipi, "writing".

Lesson 2 of 5.
शिपाई (śipāī) — an office attendant; a peon.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to set up, then the Marathi for a clerk.

You'll want to know: शिपाई.
शिपाई — śipāī — "an office attendant; a peon".
शिपायाने सगळ्यांना चहा आणून दिला. śipāyāne sagḷyānnā cahā āṇūn dilā. The office attendant brought everyone tea.
Masculine.
The oblique is शिपाया-: शिपायाने, शिपायाला.

The word, taken apart.
शिपाई (śipāī) is a loan from Persian sipāhī, "soldier"; in a Marathi office it names the attendant who carries files and tea.

Masculine.

Lesson 4 of 5.
कागदपत्र (kāgadpatra) — a document; papers.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for an office attendant, then the Marathi for a rubber stamp.

You'll want to know: कागदपत्र.
कागदपत्र — kāgadpatra — "a document; papers".
मीराने सगळी कागदपत्रं एका पिशवीत ठेवली. mīrāne sagḷī kāgadpatraṁ ekā piśvīt ṭhevlī. Meera kept all the papers in one bag.
Neuter. Plural: कागदपत्रं, kāgadpatraṁ.
Mostly in the plural, कागदपत्रं, the papers for a task.

The word, taken apart.
कागदपत्र (kāgadpatra) is a Marathi compound of कागद, "paper", a loan from Persian kāghaz, and पत्र, "a leaf, a letter", from Sanskrit patra (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 5 of 5.
छाननी करणे (chānnī karṇe) — to scrutinize.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a rubber stamp, then the Marathi for a document.

You'll want to know: छाननी करणे.
छाननी करणे — chānnī karṇe — "to scrutinize".
लिपिकाने सगळ्या कागदपत्रांची छाननी केली. lipikāne sagḷyā kāgadpatrāñcī chānnī kelī. The clerk scrutinized all the papers.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने अर्जाची छाननी केली, tyāne arjācī chānnī kelī.
What is checked takes -ची: अर्जाची छाननी करणे (chānnī karṇe).

The word, taken apart.
छाननी करणे (chānnī karṇe) is छाननी, "close sifting, scrutiny", with करणे; the origin of छाननी is not settled.
