Bengali, chapter 129: Mosque, Church, Pencil, Eraser, Bag.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
মসজিদ (mosjid) — a mosque.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a train, then the Bengali for a ticket.

You'll want to know: মসজিদ.
মসজিদ — mosjid — "a mosque".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: mosjid]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: mosjid, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say mosjid]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Bengali for a train, then the Bengali for a ticket, then say mosjid again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does মসজিদ (mosjid) mean? ("A mosque".) Say it once more.
