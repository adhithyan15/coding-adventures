## You'll want to know: integrieren
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C361-EINWANDERUNG-04]; assesses=[] -->

**integrieren** — "to integrate".

> Der Verein hilft, neue Nachbarn zu integrieren.
>
> *The club helps to integrate new neighbours.*

*Sich integrieren* — to fit in. Perfekt: *hat integriert*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*integrieren* is a learned borrowing from Latin *integrāre* "to make whole, to renew" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short), from *integer* "whole, untouched".
