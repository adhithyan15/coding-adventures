## You'll want to know: la punition
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C494-EDUCATION-03]; assesses=[] -->

**la punition** — "punishment".

> Comme punition, il n'a pas eu de dessert.
>
> *As a punishment, he did not get any pudding.*

The verb is *punir* "to punish".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*la punition* is a learned borrowing from Latin *pūnītiō* "punishment" (EtymDB), from *pūnīre* "to punish", built on *poena* "penalty" (Lewis & Short).
