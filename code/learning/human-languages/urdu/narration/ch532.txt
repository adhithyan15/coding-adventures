Urdu, chapter 532: A Stroke of Luck.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
قسمت (qismat) — luck; fortune.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a good name, then the Urdu for to be kept up.

You'll want to know: قسمت.
قسمت — qismat — "luck; fortune".
اگر قسمت اچھی ہوئی تو ہم میچ جیتیں گے۔ agar qismat achchhī huī to ham maich jīteṅ ge. If our luck is good, we will win the match.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
قسمت (qismat) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic qisma, "a share, a portion": the share life deals one.
