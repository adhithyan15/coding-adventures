## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-PRON-OH-01]; assesses=[PA-LEX-MAIN-03, PA-LEX-YOU-REGISTER-02] -->

> **ਉਹ** — *oh* — **he**, **she**, or **it**

One word, three English ones. Punjabi's third person does not make you choose a
gender before you speak.

**ਉਹ** also means *that*, pointing at something away from both of you. The
pronoun and the pointing word are one word — which is the same doubling you have
already seen, where **ਤਾਂ** at the head of a result was a demonstrative doing a
joining job.

With this the pronoun set is complete: **ਮੈਂ**, **ਤੂੰ** / **ਤੁਸੀਂ**, **ਉਹ**.
