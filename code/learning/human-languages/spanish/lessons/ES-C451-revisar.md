# revisar — look at it again

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-VER, ES-LEX-C332-WORD-22] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say **ver** and **el aviso**. Both have the same Latin verb inside
them, and so does the word coming next.

## You'll want to know: revisar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C451-TURNO-06]; assesses=[ES-LEX-VER] -->

**revisar** — to check, to go over, to inspect.

*Voy a revisar el ticket* — I'll check the receipt. *Han revisado el coche* —
they've serviced the car. *Revisa las respuestas antes de entregar* — check
your answers before handing it in.

Two nouns come with it: **la revisión**, the check-up or the service, and **el
revisor**, the inspector who comes down the train.

## Grammar Lens: revisar is not mirar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-C451-TURNO-06, ES-LEX-VER] -->

Three verbs of looking, now sorted:

| what happens | the verb |
|---|---|
| it arrives at your eyes | **ver** |
| you aim your eyes at it | **mirar** |
| you go over it for faults | **revisar** |

*Veo el ticket* — the receipt is in my field of vision. *Miro el ticket* — I
direct my attention at it. *Reviso el ticket* — I am checking whether they
charged me twice.

Only the third carries the idea of a verdict at the end. That is why a
mechanic *revisa* a car and a passenger merely *mira* the timetable.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-C451-TURNO-06, ES-LEX-C332-WORD-22] -->

**re-vis-ar**: the prefix *re-*, again, on the stem *vis-* from Latin
**videre**, to see, through its participle *visus*.

So *revisar* is to see again — the second look, which is the one where you
find what the first look missed.

You have met this root twice already without the seeing being visible. **Ver**
is *videre* worn down to two letters. **El aviso** is *a-vis-o*, a bringing to
someone's sight. Add *revisar* and you have three words from one Latin verb,
each hiding it to a different degree. The English cousins run alongside:
**vision**, **advise**, **revise**.
