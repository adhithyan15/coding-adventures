Malayalam, chapter 440: On One Condition.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
വ്യവസ്ഥ (vyavastha) — a condition; a system.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for a reference, then the Malayalam for an author.

You'll want to know: വ്യവസ്ഥ.
വ്യവസ്ഥ — vyavastha — "a condition; a system".
നീയും വന്നാൽ മാത്രം ഞാൻ വരാം, അതാണ് എന്റെ വ്യവസ്ഥ. nīyuṁ vannāl mātraṁ ñān varāṁ, atāṇŭ enṟe vyavastha. I will come only if you come too; that is my condition.
It is also an order of things, a system: സാമ്പത്തിക വ്യവസ്ഥ (vyavastha), the economic system.

The word, taken apart.
വ്യവസ്ഥ (vyavastha) is borrowed from Sanskrit vyavasthā, from the verb vy-ava-sthā, "to stand apart; to be settled, fixed" (Monier-Williams): a condition is a thing settled in advance.

നിബന്ധന — nibandhana — "a stipulation; a term".
കരാറിൽ പല നിബന്ധനകളും ഉണ്ട്. karāṟil pala nibandhanakaḷuṁ uṇṭŭ. The contract has many stipulations.
Plural: നിബന്ധനകൾ, nibandhanakaḷ.
Plural: നിബന്ധനകൾ, the terms of an agreement.

The word, taken apart.
നിബന്ധന (nibandhana) is borrowed from Sanskrit nibandhana, "binding, fastening" (Monier-Williams): a stipulation binds the two sides.

Lesson 3 of 5.
ഉപാധി (upādhi) — a proviso; a condition.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for a condition, then the Malayalam for a stipulation.

You'll want to know: ഉപാധി.
ഉപാധി — upādhi — "a proviso; a condition".
ഉപാധികൾ ഒന്നുമില്ലാതെ അവർ സഹായം നൽകി. upādhikaḷ onnumillāte avar sahāyaṁ nalki. They gave help without any conditions.
Unconditional is ഉപാധികളില്ലാത്ത.

The word, taken apart.
ഉപാധി (upādhi) is borrowed from Sanskrit upādhi, "a point of view, an aim" (CDIAL 2299), and in Monier-Williams "that which is put in place of another thing".

Lesson 4 of 5.
ഇല്ലാത്തപക്ഷം (illāttapakṣaṁ) — if not; failing that.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for a stipulation, then the Malayalam for a proviso.

You'll want to know: ഇല്ലാത്തപക്ഷം.
ഇല്ലാത്തപക്ഷം — illāttapakṣaṁ — "if not; failing that".
ഫീസ് ഇന്ന് അടയ്ക്കണം, ഇല്ലാത്തപക്ഷം പേര് വെട്ടും. phīsŭ innŭ aṭaykkaṇaṁ, illāttapakṣaṁ pērŭ veṭṭuṁ. The fee must be paid today; if not, the name will be struck off.
A formal partner of ഇല്ലെങ്കിൽ.

The word, taken apart.
ഇല്ലാത്തപക്ഷം (illāttapakṣaṁ) is a Malayalam compound: ഇല്ലാത്ത, "that is not", native Dravidian (DEDR 2559), and പക്ഷം, "a side; a case", from Sanskrit pakṣa (CDIAL 7627): in the case that it is not.

Lesson 5 of 5.
അല്ലാത്തപക്ഷം (allāttapakṣaṁ) — or else; otherwise.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for a proviso, then the Malayalam for if not.

You'll want to know: അല്ലാത്തപക്ഷം.
അല്ലാത്തപക്ഷം — allāttapakṣaṁ — "or else; otherwise".
അവൻ വേഗം ഇറങ്ങി, അല്ലാത്തപക്ഷം ബസ് പോകുമായിരുന്നു. avan vēgaṁ iṟaṅṅi, allāttapakṣaṁ basŭ pōkumāyirunnu. He left quickly; otherwise the bus would have gone.
It joins a clause and the bad result that would follow without it.

The word, taken apart.
അല്ലാത്തപക്ഷം (allāttapakṣaṁ) is a Malayalam compound: അല്ലാത്ത, "that is not so", native Dravidian (DEDR 234), and പക്ഷം, "a case", from Sanskrit pakṣa (CDIAL 7627).
