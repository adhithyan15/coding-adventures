## You'll want to know: frankieren
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C448-POST-01]; assesses=[] -->

**frankieren** — "to put postage on, to stamp (a letter)".

> Sie frankiert den Brief und bringt ihn zur Post.
>
> *She puts a stamp on the letter and takes it to the post office.*

Past participle without *ge-*: *ein frankierter Umschlag* — a stamped envelope.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*frankieren* was borrowed from Italian *francare* 'to free from charges', built on *franco* 'free': a stamped letter travels free. EtymDB does not list the German verb, so the line is hedged.
