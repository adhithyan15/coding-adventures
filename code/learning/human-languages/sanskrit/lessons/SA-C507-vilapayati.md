## You'll want to know: विलापयति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C507-BHASTRA507-03]; assesses=[] -->

**विलापयति** — *vilāpayati* — "melts down".

> स्वर्णकारः अग्नौ सुवर्णं विलापयति।
>
> *svarṇakāraḥ agnau suvarṇaṃ vilāpayati.*
>
> The goldsmith melts the gold in the fire.

Past (he …): सः सुवर्णं विलापितवान्, *saḥ suvarṇaṃ vilāpitavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**विलापयति** (*vilāpayati*) is built on *vi*, "apart", and the causative of the root √lī, "to cling, to dissolve"; MW gives *vilī* the sense "to melt", and its causative *vilāpayati* makes a thing melt (MW).
