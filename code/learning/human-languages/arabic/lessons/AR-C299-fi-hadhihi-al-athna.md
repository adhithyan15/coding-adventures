# في هذه الأثناء (fī hādhihi al-athnāʾ) — meanwhile, in the meantime

## You'll want to know: في هذه الأثناء
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C299-ATHNA299-01]; assesses=[] -->

**في هذه الأثناء** — *fī hādhihi al-athnāʾ* — "meanwhile, in the meantime".

> في هذه الأثناء كان أخي ينتظر في السيارة.
>
> *fī hādhihi al-athnāʾ kāna akhī yantaẓiru fī as-sayyāra.*
>
> Meanwhile my brother was waiting in the car.

With كان and the present it sets a second scene beside the first.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**في هذه الأثناء** (*fī hādhihi al-athnāʾ*) is "in these folds": *athnāʾ* is a plural built on the native Arabic root *th-n-y*, "to fold, to double" (Lane), the root of *ithnān*, two.
