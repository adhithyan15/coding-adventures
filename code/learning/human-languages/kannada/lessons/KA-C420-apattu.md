# ಆಪತ್ತು (āpattu) — a calamity; a mishap

## You'll want to know: ಆಪತ್ತು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C420-ALUKU420-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ಆಪತ್ತು** — *āpattu* — "a calamity; a mishap".

> ಆಪತ್ತಿನ ಸಮಯದಲ್ಲಿ ನೆರೆಯವರು ಸಹಾಯ ಮಾಡಿದರು.
>
> *āpattina samayadalli nereyavaru sahāya māḍidaru.*
>
> In the time of trouble the neighbours helped.

ಆಪತ್ತು ಬರು, for trouble to strike.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಆಪತ್ತು** (*āpattu*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *āpad*, "misfortune, calamity" (CDIAL 1196), with the Kannada -*u* after the final consonant.
