## You'll want to know: اعتراف کردن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C376-TAQSIR-05]; assesses=[] -->

**اعتراف کردن** (*e'terâf kardan*) — "to admit, to confess".

> او به اشتباهش اعتراف کرد.
>
> *u be eshtebâhash e'terâf kard.*
>
> He admitted his mistake.

Takes *be*: *be chizi e'terâf kardan*. *E'terâf* "confession" + *kardan*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**اعتراف** was borrowed from Arabic *i'tirāf* "confession, admission" (EtymDB); with *kardan* "to do" it becomes a Persian compound verb.
