## You'll want to know: a causa de
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C581-CAUSA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**a causa de** — "because of".

> No salimos a causa del frío.
>
> *We did not go out because of the cold.*

Followed by a noun; *porque* is followed by a whole sentence.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*a causa de* is built on *causa*, a learned borrowing from Latin *causa* 'reason, cause' (EtymDB).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.
