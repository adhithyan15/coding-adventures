## You'll want to know: લાજવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C467-VARTAN467-05]; assesses=[] -->

**લાજવું** — *lājvũ* — "to be ashamed".

> ખોટું બોલ્યા પછી તે લાજી ગયો.
>
> *khoṭũ bolyā pachī te lājī gayo.*
>
> After telling a lie he felt ashamed.

Past (she …): તે લાજી ગઈ, *te lājī gaī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**લાજવું** (*lājvũ*) is inherited from Sanskrit *lajjatē*, "is ashamed"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb (CDIAL 10909).
