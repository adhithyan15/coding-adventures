## You'll want to know: verbrauchen
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C363-UMWELT-05]; assesses=[] -->

**verbrauchen** — "to consume, to use up".

> Unser alter Kühlschrank verbraucht zu viel Strom.
>
> *Our old fridge uses too much electricity.*

Inseparable: *er verbraucht, er hat verbraucht*. *Der Verbrauch* — consumption.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*verbrauchen* is built with *ver-* on *brauchen* "to need, to use", inherited from Old High German *brūhhan* and Proto-Germanic *brūkaną* "to use, to consume" (EtymDB).
