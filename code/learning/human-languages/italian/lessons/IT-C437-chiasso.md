## You'll want to know: il chiasso
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C437-RUMORE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**il chiasso** — "the din, the noise (of voices)".

> Al mercato c'è sempre chiasso.
>
> *At the market there is always a din.*

*Fare chiasso* — to make a noise; *chiassoso* — noisy.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*chiasso* is usually traced to Latin *classicum* 'trumpet call' (Lewis & Short), but the origin is not settled.
