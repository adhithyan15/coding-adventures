## You'll want to know: વીંધવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C321-TALVAR321-05]; assesses=[] -->

**વીંધવું** — *vī̃dhvũ* — "to pierce".

> સૈનિકે તલવારથી ઢાલ વીંધી.
>
> *sainike talvārthī ḍhāl vī̃dhī.*
>
> The soldier pierced the shield with his sword.

Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે ઢાલ વીંધી, *teṇe ḍhāl vī̃dhī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**વીંધવું** (*vī̃dhvũ*) comes from *\*vindhati*, a form CDIAL reconstructs, and CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb, "to pierce" (CDIAL 11784). It agrees with what is pierced: **ઢાલ વીંધી**.
