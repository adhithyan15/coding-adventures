## You'll want to know: शरणम्
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C548-SANTVANA548-05]; assesses=[] -->

**शरणम्** — *śaraṇam* — "a refuge; shelter".

> यदि वात्या आगच्छति, तर्हि वयं शरणं गच्छामः।
>
> *yadi vātyā āgacchati, tarhi vayaṃ śaraṇaṃ gacchāmaḥ.*
>
> If a storm comes, we go to shelter.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**शरणम्** (*śaraṇam*) comes from the root √śri, "to lean on, to resort to", as MW derives it (through a form √śṛ); MW gives *śaraṇa* as protecting, and a shelter, a place of refuge or rest, a house (MW).
