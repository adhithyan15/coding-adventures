## You'll want to know: れいぞうこ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C475-TABLE475-01]; assesses=[] -->

**れいぞうこ** — *reizōko* — "a fridge; a refrigerator".

> **ぎゅうにゅうは れいぞうこに あります。** — *gyūnyū wa reizōko ni arimasu* — "The milk is in the fridge."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**れいぞうこ** (*reizōko*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): three Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *rei*, "cool" (Middle Chinese *lengX*), *zō*, "storehouse" (*dzangH*), and *ko*, "warehouse" (*khuH*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
