## You'll want to know: सामान्यतः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C381-VISHESHATAH381-01]; assesses=[] -->

**सामान्यतः** — *sāmānyataḥ* — "generally; as a rule".

> सामान्यतः, यदि अहं श्रान्तः, तर्हि शीघ्रं स्वपिमि।
>
> *sāmānyataḥ, yadi ahaṃ śrāntaḥ, tarhi śīghraṃ svapimi.*
>
> Generally, if I am tired, I fall asleep quickly.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सामान्यतः** (*sāmānyataḥ*) is built on *sāmānya*, "common, general", with the ending *-tas*; MW gives *sāmānya-tas* "in general, generally" (MW).
