## You'll want to know: bēi
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C88-BEI-01]; assesses=[] -->

*bēi* — "a cup of; a glass of", a measure word.

> *liǎng bēi chá* — two cups of tea

> *yì bēi shuǐ* — a glass of water

Its character comes later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*bēi* is inherited from Middle Chinese *pwoj* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed through an older book as a cup. It is the bēi of *bēizi*; as a measure word it counts what a cup holds.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ZH-GRAMMAR-MEASURE-WORD-01, ZH-RULE-TONE-SANDHI-YI-01] -->

A measure word counts by the container: *liǎng bēi chá*, two cups' worth. 一 before its level tone falls: *yì bēi*.
