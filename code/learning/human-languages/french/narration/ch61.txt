Lesson 1 of 5.
enseigner — to teach.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for to play, then the French for to learn.

You'll want to know: enseigner.
enseigner — "to teach".

The word, taken apart.
enseigner from Old French enseignier from Vulgar Latin insignare, "to mark, to point out", from Latin signum, "a mark, a sign" — inherited. (EtymDB 2.1).
