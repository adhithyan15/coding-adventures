## You'll want to know: o nervosismo
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C475-NERVOS-01]; assesses=[] -->

**o nervosismo** — "nervousness".

> Antes do exame, o nervosismo não me deixou dormir.
>
> *Before the exam, nerves kept me awake.*

Built on *nervoso*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*nervosismo* is built on *nervoso* "nervous", from Latin *nervōsus* "sinewy, vigorous" (Lewis & Short), from *nervus* "a sinew".
