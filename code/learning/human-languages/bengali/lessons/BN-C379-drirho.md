## You'll want to know: দৃঢ়
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C379-SONGKOLPO379-03]; assesses=[] -->

**দৃঢ়** — *driṛhô* — "firm; resolute".

> তুমি দৃঢ় থাকলে কেউ তোমাকে থামাতে পারবে না।
>
> *tumi driṛhô thākle keu tomāke thāmāte pārbe nā.*
>
> If you stand firm, no one can stop you.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**দৃঢ়** (*driṛhô*) is Sanskrit *dṛḍha*, fixed and firm (Monier-Williams); the ḍh between vowels is written ঢ় and said ṛh.
