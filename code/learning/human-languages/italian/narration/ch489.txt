Italian, chapter 489: Ammalarsi e guarire.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ammalarsi — to fall ill.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for to operate, then the Italian for anaesthesia.

You'll want to know: ammalarsi.
ammalarsi — "to fall ill".
Ogni inverno mi ammalo almeno una volta. Every winter I fall ill at least once.
It takes essere: mi sono ammalato. Built on malato "ill".

The word, taken apart.
ammalarsi is built on malato "ill", usually traced to Latin male habitus "in poor condition" (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 2 of 5.
il sintomo — symptom.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for anaesthesia, then the Italian for to fall ill.

You'll want to know: il sintomo.
il sintomo — "symptom".
Quali sono i sintomi dell'influenza? What are the symptoms of flu?
The plural is i sintomi.

The word, taken apart.
sintomo is a learned borrowing, through Late Latin symptōma, from Greek sýmptōma "a happening, a mishap" (EtymDB, under English symptom), from sympíptein "to befall".

Lesson 3 of 5.
la diagnosi — diagnosis.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for to fall ill, then the Italian for symptom.

You'll want to know: la diagnosi.
la diagnosi — "diagnosis".
La diagnosi è chiara: è solo un raffreddore. The diagnosis is clear: it is only a cold.
The same form in the plural: le diagnosi.

The word, taken apart.
diagnosi is a learned borrowing, through Latin diagnōsis, from Greek diágnōsis (EtymDB, under English diagnosis), from diagignṓskein "to discern, to tell apart".

Lesson 4 of 5.
l'infezione — infection.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for symptom, then the Italian for diagnosis.

You'll want to know: l'infezione.
l'infezione — "infection".
Il medico mi ha dato un antibiotico per l'infezione. The doctor gave me an antibiotic for the infection.
Fare infezione — to get infected.

The word, taken apart.
infezione is a learned borrowing from Late Latin infectiō "a dyeing, a staining" (EtymDB), from inficere "to dip, to taint", built on facere "to make".

Lesson 5 of 5.
guarire — to recover, to get better; to heal.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for diagnosis, then the Italian for infection.

You'll want to know: guarire.
guarire — "to recover, to get better; to heal".
Sono guarito in pochi giorni. I got better in a few days.
With essere when you get better (sono guarito), with avere when you heal someone (il medico mi ha guarito).

The word, taken apart.
guarire comes from a Germanic verb, Frankish warjan "to protect, to defend" (EtymDB): first to guard, then to cure.
