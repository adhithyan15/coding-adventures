Gujarati, chapter 209: A breakdown on the road.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
બગડવું (bagaḍvũ) — to break down, to go bad.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for wheel, then the Gujarati for level crossing.

You'll want to know: બગડવું.
બગડવું — bagaḍvũ — "to break down, to go bad".
રસ્તામાં ગાડી બગડી. — rastāmā̃ gāḍī bagaḍī — "The car broke down on the way."

The word, taken apart.
બગડવું (bagaḍvũ) goes back to Sanskrit vighaṭatē, "flies apart, is marred, is broken", but CDIAL marks the Gujarati verb, glossed "to be spoilt", as borrowed from Hindi (CDIAL 11673).

અમારી ગાડી ટ્રાફિકમાં ફસાઈ. — amārī gāḍī ṭrāphikmā̃ phasāī — "Our car got stuck in traffic."

The word, taken apart.
ફસાવું (phasāvũ) is a tadbhava from the family that CDIAL lists under Sanskrit spāśáyati, "causes to be bound or stopped", with Gujarati phā̃svũ and phasāvvũ, "to ensnare" (CDIAL 13814).
