Russian, chapter 461: In the forest.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
поляна (polyána) — a clearing, a glade.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a pond, then the Russian for to babble.

You'll want to know: поляна.
поляна (polyána) — "a clearing, a glade".
На поляне мы увидели оленя. Na polyáne my uvídeli olénya. We saw a deer in the clearing.
Feminine. Built on поле — a field.

The word, taken apart.
поляна (polyána) is built on поле 'a field, open land' with -яна. EtymDB lists поляна (polyána) without a deeper source.

Lesson 2 of 5.
опушка (opúshka) — the edge of a forest.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to babble, then the Russian for a clearing.

You'll want to know: опушка.
опушка (opúshka) — "the edge of a forest".
На опушке леса стоит маленький дом. Na opúshke lésa stoít málenkiy dom. There is a small house at the edge of the forest.
Feminine. На опушке — at the forest edge.

The word, taken apart.
опушка (opúshka) is usually linked with опушить 'to edge with fur', on пух 'down': the forest's fringe. EtymDB lists the word without a source, so the line is hedged.

Lesson 3 of 5.
чаща (cháshcha) — a thicket, the depths of a forest.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a clearing, then the Russian for the edge of a forest.

You'll want to know: чаща.
чаща (cháshcha) — "a thicket, the depths of a forest".
Медведь ушёл в чащу. Medvéd ushyól v cháshchu. The bear went off into the thicket.
Feminine. В лесной чаще — deep in the forest.

The word, taken apart.
чаща (cháshcha) is inherited from Proto-Slavic čęsťa (EtymDB), built on the root of частый 'dense, frequent': where the trees stand thick.

Lesson 4 of 5.
мох (mokh) — moss.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for the edge of a forest, then the Russian for a thicket.

You'll want to know: мох.
мох (mokh) — "moss".
Камни у ручья покрыты мхом. Kámni u ruchyá pokrýty mkhom. The stones by the stream are covered in moss.
Masculine; the о drops out: мха, во мху.

The word, taken apart.
мох (mokh) is inherited from Proto-Slavic mŭxŭ 'moss' (EtymDB), which EtymDB takes back to a PIE root mews-.

Lesson 5 of 5.
шишка (shíshka) — a cone (of a pine or a fir); a bump.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a thicket, then the Russian for moss.

You'll want to know: шишка.
шишка (shíshka) — "a cone (of a pine or a fir); a bump".
Под елью лежат шишки. Pod yélyu lezhát shíshki. There are cones lying under the fir tree.
Feminine. Also a bump on the head; colloquial большая шишка (shíshka) — a big shot.

The word, taken apart.
шишка (shíshka) is inherited through Old East Slavic šišĭka from Proto-Slavic šišĭka (EtymDB).
