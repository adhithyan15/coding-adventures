German, chapter 265: Inzwischen.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
inzwischen — meanwhile; by now.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for recently, then the German for the day before yesterday.

You'll want to know: inzwischen.
inzwischen — "meanwhile; by now".
Ich habe gekocht, und inzwischen hat er die Teller geholt. I cooked, and meanwhile he fetched the plates.
Also 'by now': Inzwischen ist sie zehn Jahre alt.

The word, taken apart.
inzwischen joins in and zwischen "between". Zwischen comes from Old High German in zuiskēn "between the two", built on zuiski "twofold" and so on the word for "two" (EtymDB).

Lesson 2 of 5.
mittlerweile — by now, in the meanwhile.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for the day before yesterday, then the German for meanwhile.

You'll want to know: mittlerweile.
mittlerweile — "by now, in the meanwhile".
Mittlerweile wohnt sie in einer größeren Wohnung. By now she lives in a bigger flat.
Close to inzwischen, and a little more conversational.

The word, taken apart.
mittlerweile is built on mittler "middle" and Weile "a while", both inherited Germanic words; Weile is a cousin of English while. EtymDB lists no parent for the compound.

Lesson 3 of 5.
währenddessen — in the meantime, while that was going on.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for meanwhile, then the German for by now.

You'll want to know: währenddessen.
währenddessen — "in the meantime, while that was going on".
Sie telefonierte. Währenddessen kochte das Wasser. She was on the phone. In the meantime the water boiled.
Built on während. As an adverb, the verb comes second after it.

The word, taken apart.
währenddessen joins während "during", the present participle of währen "to last" (EtymDB), and dessen "of that": while that lasted.

Lesson 4 of 5.
bisher — so far, until now.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for by now, then the German for in the meantime.

You'll want to know: bisher.
bisher — "so far, until now".
Bisher ist alles gut gegangen. So far everything has gone well.
With the present or the Perfekt: Bisher habe ich nichts gehört.

The word, taken apart.
bisher joins bis "until" and her "hither, to here", so "up to here". Both are inherited Germanic words; EtymDB lists no parent.

Lesson 5 of 5.
längst — long ago, long since.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for in the meantime, then the German for so far.

You'll want to know: längst.
längst — "long ago, long since".
Der Zug ist längst abgefahren. The train left long ago.
Längst nicht means 'not nearly': Das ist längst nicht alles.

The word, taken apart.
längst is the superlative of lang "long", an inherited Germanic word (EtymDB): longest ago.
