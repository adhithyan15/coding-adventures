## You'll want to know: أتقن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C394-ITQAN394-01]; assesses=[] -->

**أتقن** — *atqana* — "to master".

> أتقن جدي لغتين.
>
> *atqana jaddī lughatayn.*
>
> My grandfather mastered two languages.

Present: يتقن, *yutqin*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**أتقن** (*atqana*) is built on the native Arabic root *t-q-n*, "to do a thing skilfully" (Lane). It is Form IV, to make a thing firm and exact: to master it. إتقان, mastery, is its verbal noun.
