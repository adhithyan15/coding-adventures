## You'll want to know: ఎలుక
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C70-CREATURE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ఎలుక** (*eluka*) — "a mouse, a rat".

ఎలుక is a mouse, and the same word does for a rat. Telugu does not split them the way English does; when the size matters, a word for size is added.

It is inherited. Any house with బియ్యం in it has an ఎలుక story, and the పిల్లి you already know earns its keep here.

Asked whether there is one about, most people say అవును before they have thought about it, and లేదు only after going to look.
