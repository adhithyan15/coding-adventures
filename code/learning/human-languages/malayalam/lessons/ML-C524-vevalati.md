## You'll want to know: വേവലാതി
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C524-APAMANAM524-05]; assesses=[] -->

**വേവലാതി** — *vēvalāti* — "fretting; agitation".

> മകൾ വൈകിയപ്പോൾ അമ്മ വേവലാതിയോടെ കാത്തിരുന്നു.
>
> *makaḷ vaikiyappōḷ amma vēvalātiyōṭe kāttirunnu.*
>
> When her daughter was late, Mother waited in a fret.

Spoken; വേവലാതിപ്പെടുക is to fret, to worry oneself.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**വേവലാതി** (*vēvalāti*) is a spoken word; no source we can open explains it, so its origin is not settled.
