# حنين (ḥanīn) — nostalgia, longing

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[AR-LEX-C493-NADAM493-05, AR-LEX-C494-KAABA494-01] -->

## You'll want to know: حنين
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C494-KAABA494-02]; assesses=[] -->

**حنين** — *ḥanīn* — "nostalgia, longing".

> شعر جدي بالحنين إلى قريته.
>
> *shaʿara jaddī bi-l-ḥanīn ilā qaryatihi.*
>
> My grandfather felt homesick for his village.

It takes إلى for what you long for.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**حنين** (*ḥanīn*) is built on the native Arabic root *ḥ-n-n*, "to yearn, to feel tenderness" (Lane). Ḥanīn is a yearning, often for home or for the past.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
