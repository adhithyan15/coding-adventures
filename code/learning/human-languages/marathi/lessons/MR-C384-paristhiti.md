## You'll want to know: परिस्थिती
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C384-PARISTHITI384-01]; assesses=[] -->

**परिस्थिती** — *paristhitī* — "circumstances; a situation".

> घरची परिस्थिती चांगली नव्हती, तरीही तो शिकला.
>
> *gharcī paristhitī cāṅglī navhatī, tarīhī to śiklā.*
>
> Things were hard at home; even so, he studied.

Feminine.

Feminine and singular: घरची परिस्थिती, the situation at home.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**परिस्थिती** (*paristhitī*) is a learned word built from Sanskrit *pari-*, "around", and *sthiti*, "standing, state" (Monier-Williams): how things stand all round.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
