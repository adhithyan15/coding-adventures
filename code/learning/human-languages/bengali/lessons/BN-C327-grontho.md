## You'll want to know: গ্রন্থ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C327-UPONNAS327-03]; assesses=[] -->

**গ্রন্থ** — *grônthô* — "a volume; a book (formal)".

> লাইব্রেরিতে কবির পুরনো গ্রন্থগুলো আছে।
>
> *lāibrerite kôbir purôno grônthôgulo āchhe.*
>
> The library has the poet's old volumes.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**গ্রন্থ** (*grônthô*) is Sanskrit *grantha*, 'tying, binding, stringing together' (Monier-Williams): leaves strung together into a book. It is the formal word; **বই** is the everyday one.
