## You'll want to know: ராணி
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C328-MANNAR328-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ராணி** — *rāṇi* — "a queen".

> ராணி அழகான தோட்டத்தில் நடந்தாள்.
>
> *rāṇi aḻakāṉa tōṭṭattil naṭantāḷ.*
>
> The queen walked in a beautiful garden.

Also the queen in chess and in cards.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ராணி** (*rāṇi*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *rājñī*, "queen" (CDIAL 10692), through a Prakrit form.
