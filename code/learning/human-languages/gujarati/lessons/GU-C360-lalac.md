## You'll want to know: લાલચ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C360-IMAN360-02]; assesses=[] -->

**લાલચ** — *lālac* — "temptation".

> તે પૈસાની લાલચમાં ફસાઈ ગયો.
>
> *te paisānī lālacmā̃ phasāī gayo.*
>
> He was lured by the money.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**લાલચ** (*lālac*) is usually traced to Sanskrit *lālasa*, "eagerly longing for" (CDIAL 11026), though CDIAL records no Gujarati form.
