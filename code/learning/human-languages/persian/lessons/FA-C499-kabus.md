## You'll want to know: کابوس
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C499-SLEEP-03]; assesses=[] -->

**کابوس** (*kâbus*) — "a nightmare".

> دیشب کابوس دیدم.
>
> *dishab kâbus didam.*
>
> I had a nightmare last night.

*kâbus didan* — to have a nightmare, like *khâb didan* 'to dream'.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*kâbus* comes from Arabic *kābūs*, 'nightmare' (EtymDB).
