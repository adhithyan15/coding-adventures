# اترانا (itrānā) — to put on airs; to strut

## You'll want to know: اترانا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C452-PRIDE452-03]; assesses=[] -->

**اترانا** — *itrānā* — "to put on airs; to strut".

> نئے کپڑے پہن کر وہ اترا رہی تھی۔
>
> *naye kapṛe pahan kar voh itrā rahī thī.*
>
> She was strutting about in her new clothes.

Past (he …): وہ اترایا, *voh itrāyā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**اترانا** (*itrānā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *itrānā*, "to strut", under Sanskrit *itvará*, "walking" (CDIAL 1566).
