## You'll want to know: まご
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C286-AKACHAN286-05]; assesses=[] -->

**まご** — *mago* — "a grandchild".

> **そふと そぼに まごが うまれました。** — *sofu to sobo ni mago ga umaremashita* — "My grandparents have a new grandchild."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**まご** (*mago*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict).
