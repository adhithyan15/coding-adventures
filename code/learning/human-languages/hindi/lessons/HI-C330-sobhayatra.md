## You'll want to know: शोभायात्रा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C330-MELA330-04]; assesses=[] -->

**शोभायात्रा** — *śobhāyātrā* — "a festive procession".

> शोभायात्रा कितने बजे चलेगी?
>
> *śobhāyātrā kitne baje calegī?*
>
> What time will the procession set off?

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**शोभायात्रा** (*śobhāyātrā*) joins Sanskrit *śobhā*, "splendour, beauty" (Monier-Williams), and **यात्रा**, a journey or procession: a procession for show, with music and decorated floats. Like **जुलूस**, it **निकलती है**.
