## You'll want to know: आस्तृणाति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C427-VASATI427-04]; assesses=[] -->

**आस्तृणाति** — *āstṛṇāti* — "spreads out".

> पथिकः भूमौ कम्बलम् आस्तृणाति।
>
> *pathikaḥ bhūmau kambalam āstṛṇāti.*
>
> The wayfarer spreads a blanket on the ground.

Past (he …): सः कम्बलम् आस्तीर्णवान्, *saḥ kambalam āstīrṇavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**आस्तृणाति** (*āstṛṇāti*) is built from *ā* and the root √stṛ, "to spread, to strew", which also gives *āstaraṇa*, "a carpet, a spread"; MW gives *ā-stṛ* as to spread or strew over, with the participle *āstīrṇa*, "spread" (MW).
