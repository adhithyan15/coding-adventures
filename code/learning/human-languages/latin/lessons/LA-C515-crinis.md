## You'll want to know: crīnis, crīnis
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C515-VULT-03]; assesses=[] -->

**crīnis, crīnis** — "hair, a lock of hair".

> Crīnēs puellae longī et flāvī sunt.
>
> *The girl's hair is long and golden.*

Masculine, third declension; usually plural. A word of poetry and fine prose; *capillus* is the everyday word.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**crīnis** is a native Latin noun; Lewis & Short and EtymDB compare *crista* "crest", but the origin is not settled.
