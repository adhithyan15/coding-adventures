## You'll want to know: ریڑھ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C465-BONE465-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ریڑھ** — *rīṛh* — "the backbone".

> بیٹھتے وقت ریڑھ کی ہڈی سیدھی رکھیے۔
>
> *baiṭhte vaqt rīṛh kī haḍḍī sīdhī rakhiye.*
>
> Keep your back straight when you sit.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ریڑھ** (*rīṛh*) is inherited from Sanskrit *rīḍhaka*, "backbone" (CDIAL 10749a).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
