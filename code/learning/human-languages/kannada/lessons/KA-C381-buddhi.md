## You'll want to know: ಬುದ್ಧಿ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C381-PARIGANISU381-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ಬುದ್ಧಿ** — *buddhi* — "intellect; wit".

> ಆ ಹುಡುಗನಿಗೆ ಒಳ್ಳೆಯ ಬುದ್ಧಿ ಇದೆ.
>
> *ā huḍuganige oḷḷeya buddhi ide.*
>
> That boy has a good mind.

Also advice: ಬುದ್ಧಿ ಹೇಳು, to give someone a piece of advice.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಬುದ್ಧಿ** (*buddhi*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *buddhi*, "intelligence, understanding" (CDIAL 9277), from *budh*, "to wake, to know".
