## You'll want to know: zhōnggào
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C365-YESBUT365-05]; assesses=[] -->

*zhōnggào* — "word of advice; warning".

> *nǐ yīnggāi tīng tā de zhōnggào* — You should take his advice.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*zhōnggào* is built from *zhōng* and *gào*, both inherited from Middle Chinese, *trjuwng* and *kawH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'selfless' and the second 'to report'.
