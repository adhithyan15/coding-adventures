## You'll want to know: le lien
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C398-RESEAU-04]; assesses=[] -->

**le lien** — "the link; the bond".

> Je t'envoie le lien vers la vidéo.
>
> *I am sending you the link to the video.*

Between people too: *un lien d'amitié*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*lien* is inherited from Latin *ligāmen* "a bond, a band" (EtymDB), from *ligāre* "to tie" (Lewis & Short).
