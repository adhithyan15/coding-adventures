## You'll want to know: حداد
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C319-AZA319-05]; assesses=[] -->

**حداد** — *ḥidād* — "mourning, a period of mourning".

> لبست الأسرة الأسود في أيام الحداد.
>
> *labisat al-usra al-aswad fī ayyām al-ḥidād.*
>
> The family wore black in the days of mourning.

أعلنت الحكومة الحداد ثلاثة أيام: three days of national mourning.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**حداد** (*ḥidād*) is built on the native Arabic root *ḥ-d-d* (Lane), the root of *ḥadd*, a limit, and of *ḥadīd*, iron. Lane gives *ḥidād* as a widow's holding back from finery and perfume: mourning.
