# થથરવું (thatharvũ) — to shudder; to tremble with fear

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-LEX-C453-BIK453-01, GU-LEX-C453-BIK453-02] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to be scared, then the Gujarati for awe.

## You'll want to know: થથરવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C453-BIK453-03]; assesses=[] -->

**થથરવું** — *thatharvũ* — "to shudder; to tremble with fear".

> સાપ જોઈને છોકરી થથરી ગઈ.
>
> *sāp joīne chokrī thathrī gaī.*
>
> The girl shuddered when she saw the snake.

Past (she …): તે થથરી ગઈ, *te thathrī gaī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**થથરવું** (*thatharvũ*) goes back to a reconstructed *\*thar-*, "tremble"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb (CDIAL 6092).
