# ਆਵੇਗੀ — she or it will come

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-FORM-AAVEGA-186, PA-TRAVEL-RAIL-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say **ਆਵੇਗਾ**, then say **ਰੇਲ**.

## Grammar Lens: transfer the feminine future
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-FORM-AAVEGI-186]; assesses=[PA-FORM-AAVEGA-186] -->

> **ਆਵੇਗੀ** — *aavegii* — she or it will come

Change the final **ਾ** to **ੀ**, just as in the first future pair. The verb
still means “come”; agreement is now feminine singular.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-FORM-AAVEGI-186, PA-FORM-AAVEGA-186, PA-TRAVEL-RAIL-01] -->

- [YOU HEAR: *aavegaa, aavegii*; YOU SAY: **ਆਵੇਗਾ, ਆਵੇਗੀ**]
- [YOU READ: **ਰੇਲ ਆਵੇਗੀ**]
- [YOU WRITE: copy **ਆਵੇਗੀ** once and check the final **ੀ**]

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-FORM-AAVEGI-186, PA-FORM-AAVEGA-186] -->

feminine **ੀ** here too.
