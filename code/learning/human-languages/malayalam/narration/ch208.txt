Lesson 1 of 5.
അച്ചാർ (accār) — a pickle.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for a ladle, then the Malayalam for a sieve.

You'll want to know: അച്ചാർ.
അച്ചാർ — accār — "a pickle".

The word, taken apart.
അച്ചാർ (accār) from Persian āchār, "pickle", which came in through Hindustani.

Lesson 3 of 5.
നെയ്യ് (neyyŭ) — ghee.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for a pickle, then the Malayalam for buttermilk.

You'll want to know: നെയ്യ്.
നെയ്യ് — neyyŭ — "ghee".

The word, taken apart.
നെയ്യ് (neyyŭ) is native Dravidian, one word with Tamil ney, Kannada ney and Telugu neyyi (DEDR 3746). Its older sense is any fat or oil.

Lesson 4 of 5.
വെണ്ണ (veṇṇa) — butter.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for buttermilk, then the Malayalam for ghee.

You'll want to know: വെണ്ണ.
വെണ്ണ — veṇṇa — "butter".

The word, taken apart.
വെണ്ണ (veṇṇa) is native Dravidian, "white fat": DEDR 5496 lists Tamil veṇ-ṇey, veṇṇai, "butter", the word for "white" joined to the word for fat.
