# (una oferta de trabajo) — read it, then do it yourself

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-C441-TRATO-01, ES-LEX-C441-TRATO-03] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say *el contrato* and *fijo*. The advert you are about to read uses
both, and the second one is the part that matters.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-C441-TRATO-01, ES-LEX-C441-TRATO-02, ES-LEX-C441-TRATO-03] -->

Read both, then answer.

**1 — oferta de empleo**

> Se busca ayudante de cocina. **Contrato fijo** a partir de enero, jornada
> completa. Se valora experiencia previa. Enviar currículum a
> empleo@cocinacentral.invalid.

**2 — un aviso del banco**

> Le recordamos que la cuota de su **préstamo** se cargará el día 5. Puede
> descargar la **factura** del mes en su área privada. Cualquier cambio de
> cuenta debe comunicarse **firmado**.

[PAUSE 3s]
- [YOU SAY: what kind of contract the job offers, and from when]
- [YOU SAY: which of the two documents you would have to sign]
- [YOU SAY: what the bank will do on the fifth]

## Why it's said this way
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-C441-TRATO-04, ES-LEX-C441-TRATO-05, ES-LEX-C441-TRATO-02] -->

Notice the last line of the second notice: **debe comunicarse firmado**.

There is no *you* in it. **Comunicarse** drains out the person exactly as
*celebrarse* did on the notice about the meeting, and **firmado** is the
participle doing an adjective's work, the way *cerrado* does on a shop door. The
sentence says *any change must be communicated in-a-signed-state*, and names
nobody at all.

That impersonal shape is the register of official Spanish, and it is worth
recognising rather than translating. When a notice stops naming who must act, it
is not being vague — it is saying the obligation attaches to the *document*, not
to a particular clerk.

Contrast the job advert: *se busca*, *se valora*, *enviar*. Same move. An advert
and a bank notice are written by the same hand.

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-C441-TRATO-01, ES-LEX-C441-TRATO-02, ES-LEX-C441-TRATO-03, ES-LEX-C441-TRATO-04, ES-LEX-C441-TRATO-05] -->

[PAUSE 3s] Now your turn, out loud and then in writing. Five sentences: say you
applied, say what contract you were offered, say you signed it, say you have a
loan to pay, and say when the bill arrives.

Then check yourself: did **fijo** agree with *contrato*, and did you use
**pedir** with *préstamo* rather than *preguntar*?
