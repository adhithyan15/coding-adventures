## You'll want to know: ठोकर
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C297-PHISAL297-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ठोकर** — *ṭhokar* — "a stumble; a knock".

> अरुण को पत्थर से ठोकर लगी।
>
> *Aruṇ ko patthar se ṭhokar lagī.*
>
> Arun tripped over a stone.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ठोकर** (*ṭhokar*) is inherited from *\*ṭhōkk*, a knock, a form CDIAL reconstructs (CDIAL 5513). The person who trips takes **को**: **अरुण को ठोकर लगी**, a knock struck Arun.
