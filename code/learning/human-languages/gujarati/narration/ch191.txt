Gujarati, chapter 191: Lending at interest.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ધીરવું (dhīrvũ) — to lend.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to cash a cheque, then the Gujarati for to borrow.

You'll want to know: ધીરવું.
ધીરવું — dhīrvũ — "to lend".
તે મિત્રને પૈસા ધીરે છે. — te mitrane paisā dhīre che — "He lends money to a friend."

The word, taken apart.
ધીરવું (dhīrvũ) is a tadbhava. CDIAL lists it, glossed "to trust, lend", under Sanskrit dhī́ra, "wise; steady, resolute" (CDIAL 6817).

Lesson 2 of 5.
વ્યાજ (vyāj) — interest on money.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to borrow, then the Gujarati for to lend.

You'll want to know: વ્યાજ.
વ્યાજ — vyāj — "interest on money".
આ ખાતામાં વ્યાજ ઓછું છે. — ā khātāmā̃ vyāj ochũ che — "This account pays little interest."

The word, taken apart.
The origin of વ્યાજ (vyāj) is not settled. It matches Sanskrit vyāja, "pretext", in form, but CDIAL has no entry for vyāja and no Gujarati word for interest, so the link is unconfirmed.

Lesson 3 of 5.
બચત (bacat) — savings.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to lend, then the Gujarati for interest on money.

You'll want to know: બચત.
બચત — bacat — "savings".
મારી બચત બેંકમાં છે. — mārī bacat bẽkmā̃ che — "My savings are in the bank."

The word, taken apart.
બચત (bacat) is built on the verb bacvũ, "to be left over", with the noun ending -at: what is left over. bacvũ came into Gujarati from Hindi and goes back to Sanskrit váñcati, "escapes from" (CDIAL 11208).

Lesson 4 of 5.
નફો (napho) — profit.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for interest on money, then the Gujarati for savings.

You'll want to know: નફો.
નફો — napho — "profit".
આ સોદામાં નફો થયો. — ā sodāmā̃ napho thayo — "This deal made a profit."

The word, taken apart.
નફો (napho) is a loan from Arabic nafʿ, "profit, benefit", which reached Gujarati through Persian. Its Arabic root is n-f-ʿ, "to be of use" (Lane).

Lesson 5 of 5.
ખોટ (khoṭ) — loss, shortfall.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for savings, then the Gujarati for profit.

You'll want to know: ખોટ.
ખોટ — khoṭ — "loss, shortfall".
વેપારીને ખોટ ગઈ. — vepārīne khoṭ gaī — "The merchant made a loss."
A relative of ખોટું, khoṭũ, 'false'.

The word, taken apart.
ખોટ (khoṭ) is a tadbhava. CDIAL lists it, glossed "mistake, loss, want", under khōṭi and khōṭṭa, "blemish", reconstructed forms, beside khoṭũ, "alloyed, bad" (CDIAL 3931).
