## You'll want to know: il rastrello
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C507-ORTO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**il rastrello** — "rake".

> Raccolgo le foglie con il rastrello.
>
> *I gather the leaves with the rake.*

*Rastrellare* — to rake.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*rastrello* is inherited from Latin *rāstellus* "a small rake" (EtymDB), a little form of *rāstrum*.
