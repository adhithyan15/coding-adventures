## You'll want to know: déballer
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C426-RECEPTION-05]; assesses=[] -->

**déballer** — "to unpack".

> Il a déballé ses affaires et rangé ses chemises.
>
> *He unpacked his things and put his shirts away.*

Figurative: *déballer tout* — to come out with everything.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*déballer* is built from *dé-* and *balle* 'bale, bundle', which is usually traced to a Frankish word meaning 'ball'. EtymDB does not list the verb.
