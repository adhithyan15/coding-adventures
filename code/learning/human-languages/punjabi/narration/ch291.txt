Punjabi, chapter 291: Warm and Cool.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ਠੰਢਾ (ṭhaṇḍhā) — cold; cool.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for to go down, then the Punjabi for to blow.

You'll want to know: ਠੰਢਾ.
ਠੰਢਾ — ṭhaṇḍhā — "cold; cool".
ਘੜੇ ਦਾ ਪਾਣੀ ਠੰਢਾ ਹੁੰਦਾ ਹੈ। ghaṛe dā pāṇī ṭhaṇḍhā hundā hai. Water from a clay pot is cool.

The word, taken apart.
ਠੰਢਾ (ṭhaṇḍhā) goes back to Sanskrit stabdha, "stiff, solidified (as water)"; CDIAL lists Punjabi ṭhāḍhā under it only as "upright", a word it marks as taken from Hindi; the sense "cold" is the one it gives for Lahnda ṭhaḍḍhā and Hindi ṭhaṇḍhā (CDIAL 13676).

Lesson 2 of 5.
ਨਿੱਘਾ (nigghā) — pleasantly warm; cosy.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for to blow, then the Punjabi for cool.

You'll want to know: ਨਿੱਘਾ.
ਨਿੱਘਾ — nigghā — "pleasantly warm; cosy".
ਇਹ ਕੰਬਲ ਬਹੁਤ ਨਿੱਘਾ ਹੈ। eh kambal bahut nigghā hai. This blanket is very warm.

The word, taken apart.
ਨਿੱਘਾ (nigghā) is inherited from a reconstructed nidāghya, "heat"; CDIAL lists Punjabi nigghā, "moderately warm", under it (CDIAL 7195).

Lesson 3 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for cool, then the Punjabi for pleasantly warm.

You'll want to know: ਤੱਤਾ.
ਤੱਤਾ — tattā — "hot to the touch".
ਚਾਹ ਬਹੁਤ ਤੱਤੀ ਹੈ। chāh bahut tattī hai. The tea is very hot.

The word, taken apart.
ਤੱਤਾ (tattā) is inherited from Sanskrit tapta, "heated, hot"; CDIAL lists Punjabi tattā, "hot, burning", under it (CDIAL 5679).

Lesson 5 of 5.
ਠਾਰਨਾ (ṭhārnā) — to cool something down.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for hot to the touch, then the Punjabi for lukewarm.

You'll want to know: ਠਾਰਨਾ.
ਠਾਰਨਾ — ṭhārnā — "to cool something down".
ਮੈਂ ਬੱਚੇ ਲਈ ਦੁੱਧ ਠਾਰਦਾ ਹਾਂ। maiṁ bachche laī dudh ṭhārdā hāṁ. I cool the milk down for the child.
Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਦੁੱਧ ਠਾਰਿਆ, maiṁ dudh ṭhāriā.

The word, taken apart.
ਠਾਰਨਾ (ṭhārnā) is the causative partner of ṭharnā, inherited from a reconstructed sthārayati, "makes firm"; CDIAL lists Punjabi ṭhārnā, "to cool", under it (CDIAL 13742).
