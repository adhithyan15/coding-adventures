## You'll want to know: el disturbio
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C570-PROTESTA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**el disturbio** — "the disturbance, the riot".

> Hubo disturbios en el centro después del partido.
>
> *There was rioting in the centre after the match.*

Usually in the plural: *los disturbios*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*disturbio* is built on *disturbar* (EtymDB), a learned word from Latin *disturbāre* 'to throw into disorder' (Lewis & Short), built on *turba* 'an uproar, a crowd'.

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
