## You'll want to know: ormeggiare
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C424-APPRODO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ormeggiare** — "to moor".

> Ormeggiamo la barca vicino al molo.
>
> *We moor the boat near the pier.*

*L'ormeggio* — the mooring.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ormeggiare* is usually traced to Greek *hórmos* 'anchorage, roadstead', but the path into Italian is not settled.
