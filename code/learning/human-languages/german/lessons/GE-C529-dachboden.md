## You'll want to know: der Dachboden
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C529-RENOVIEREN-05]; assesses=[] -->

**der Dachboden** — "attic, loft".

> Die alten Spielsachen liegen auf dem Dachboden.
>
> *The old toys are in the attic.*

Plural: *die Dachböden*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Dachboden* is built on *Dach* "roof", inherited from Old High German *dah*, from Proto-Germanic *þaką* (EtymDB), and *Boden* "floor".
