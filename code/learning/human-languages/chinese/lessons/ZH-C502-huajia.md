## You'll want to know: 画家
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C502-PAINT502-03]; assesses=[] -->

**画家** — *huàjiā* — "painter (an artist)".

> **小时候我想当画家** *xiǎoshíhou wǒ xiǎng dāng huàjiā* — When I was little I wanted to be a painter.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**画家** (*huàjiā*) is built from 画 'a painting' and 家 'a household', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *hweaH* and *kae* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'a dwelling'; the sense 'a master of an art' is not given there.
