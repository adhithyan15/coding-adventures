## You'll want to know: الٹنا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C284-TAKKAR284-04]; assesses=[] -->

**الٹنا** — *ulaṭnā* — "to overturn".

> ٹرک سڑک پر الٹ گیا۔
>
> *ṭrak saṛak par ulaṭ gayā.*
>
> The lorry overturned on the road.

Past (it …): وہ ٹرک الٹ گیا, *voh ṭrak ulaṭ gayā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**الٹنا** (*ulaṭnā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *ulaṭnā*, "to be overturned", under *\*ullaṭati*, a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 2368). A vehicle **الٹ گئی** when it lands on its side or its roof.
