# છકડો (chakḍo) — a chhakda (a country motor cart)

## You'll want to know: છકડો
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C402-CART402-01]; assesses=[] -->

**છકડો** — *chakḍo* — "a chhakda (a country motor cart)".

> ગામના માણસો છકડામાં બેસીને શહેર ગયા.
>
> *gāmnā māṇso chakḍāmā̃ besīne śaher gayā.*
>
> The village people rode into town in a chhakda.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**છકડો** (*chakḍo*) is a tadbhava of Sanskrit *śakaṭa*, "a cart"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati word for a cart (CDIAL 12236). Today the name belongs to the open three-wheeled motor cart of the Saurashtra roads.
