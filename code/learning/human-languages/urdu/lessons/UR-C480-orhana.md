# اوڑھانا (oṛhānā) — to cover someone up (with a blanket)

## You'll want to know: اوڑھانا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C480-CARE480-05]; assesses=[] -->

**اوڑھانا** — *oṛhānā* — "to cover someone up (with a blanket)".

> ماں نے بچے کو کمبل اوڑھایا۔
>
> *māṅ ne bachche ko kambal oṛhāyā.*
>
> The mother tucked a blanket over the child.

Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے مریض کو کمبل اوڑھایا, *us ne marīz ko kambal oṛhāyā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**اوڑھانا** (*oṛhānā*) is the causative of *oṛhnā*, "to wrap oneself in", inherited from *ōḍḍh*, a form CDIAL reconstructs (CDIAL 2547).
