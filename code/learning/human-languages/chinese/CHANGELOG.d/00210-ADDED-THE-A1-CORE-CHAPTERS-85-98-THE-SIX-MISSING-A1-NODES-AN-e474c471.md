## Added — the A1 core: chapters 85-98, the six missing A1 nodes and both A1 writing stages

- Fourteen hand-authored chapters, 85 lessons, on the six A1 spine nodes Chinese
  had not reached: where things are and where is it (在, 那儿, 哪儿, *lǐbian*,
  *wàibian*, *shàngbian*, *xiàbian*, *xǐshǒujiān*); is there any (没有, a grammar
  lesson on 不 against 没, *chuáng*, *dēng*, *yàoshi*, *bīngxiāng*); two cups of tea
  (两, the measure words *bēi*, *běn*, *zhāng*, *zhī*, and a pronunciation lesson on
  the tone of 一); give me that (给, 做, *fàng*, *dài*, *zhàn*); what I can do (会, 能,
  *kěyǐ*, *yóuyǒng*); what I'd like (想要, 不要, *xūyào*, 一点儿); me too (也, *càidān*,
  *fúwùyuán*, *biéde*); too much (太好了, 有点儿, *gānjìng*, *zāng*); all the same (都,
  *yíyàng*, *niánqīng*, *pàng*, *shòu*); why (为什么, *yǒushì*, *dǔchē*, *wàng*);
  because (因为, *suǒyǐ*, *shūfu*); my day in characters (小时, *měitiān*, *yǐhòu*).
  54 new headwords (19 in characters, 35 by ear with pinyin headwords), 14 verbs.
- Thirteen characters written, one a chapter, each directly after the word whose
  headword first shows it: 在 哪 没 两 给 能 要 也 太 都 为 因 时, with a stroke-by-stroke
  strip each. Every one is taught as the pieces the reader already writes.
- Chapter 97 reads a 57-character day written entirely in characters the reader
  writes, then proves controlled composition (ZH-W97-my-own-line): one line of the
  reader's own Chinese with nothing to copy, unwritten words in pinyin.
- Chapter 98 proves timed production (ZH-W98-with-a-clock): five form fields whose
  labels are all written characters and a 20-30 character message, under this
  book's own three-minute clock. It says what assessment-spec.md makes official
  (an HSK 3.0 Level 1-aligned, project-defined target whose candidate writes a
  small form and short message) and claims no HSK timing. A final review closes
  the core.
- Spine: 哪儿 realizes QUESTION-WHERE, 给 / 做 / *fàng* / *dài* / *zhàn* VERB-GIVE,
  VERB-DO-MAKE, VERB-PUT, VERB-BRING and VERB-STAND, 能 VERB-CAN, 想要 VERB-WANT and
  因为 CONNECTIVE-BECAUSE, each on its own node. `curriculum.d/_meta.json` adds 36
  concept aliases to tags that already teach the concept (喝 VERB-DRINK, 买
  VERB-BUY, *fángjiān* LODGING-ROOM, 天 TIME-DAY, 去 VERB-GO, 吗 QUESTION-POLAR, the
  new 不 — 没 lesson VERB-NEGATE, *suǒyǐ* CONNECTIVE-SO and the rest), and the
  omits of ASK-LOCATION, NAME-EVERYDAY-ACTIONS, NAME-EVERYDAY-THINGS,
  SAY-WHAT-I-HAVE-AND-CAN-DO, SAY-WHY, SAY-WHAT-I-WANT and NEGATE-AND-ASK empty;
  TIME-OF-DAY, SAY-WHAT-I-DO and GIVE-REASONS keep only the concepts no lesson
  teaches.
- Sound tag `tone-sandhi-yi`, with a `toneSandhi` row in `data/scripts/chinese.json`.
- Origins from the Guangyun in Baxter's transcription, every sense the gloss does
  not give hedged, irregular modern readings named (没 méi, 给 gěi, 都 dōu, 泳 yǒng,
  所 suǒ), and "not settled" where the Guangyun has no reading (哪, 做, 样, 脏, 胖).
- A1 headwords 347 -> 401 and verbs 73 -> 87; all twelve A1 spine nodes realized and
  no A1 writing stage missing. A1 is not attained: 600 headwords are needed.
