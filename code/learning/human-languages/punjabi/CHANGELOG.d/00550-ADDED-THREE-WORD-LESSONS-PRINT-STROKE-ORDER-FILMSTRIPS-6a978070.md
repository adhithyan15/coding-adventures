## Added — three word lessons print stroke-order filmstrips

ਪਰ (PA-C39-par-write), ਅਮਨ (PA-W02-aman) and ਮਨਨ (PA-W02-manan) now print a
numbered stroke-order strip (29 -> 32 Punjabi lessons). Each letter's body is
drawn as GNPS's tracing lesson draws it, without its own headline, and the
word's one headline (ਸਿਰੋਰੇਖਾ) is drawn last, left to right. That word-level
order is the book's convention, the order fluent writers are described as
using; GNPS draws each letter's headline first, and the single-letter strips
keep that order. ਅ ਪ ਮ, whose printed bar has a gap, are drawn whole and the
headline does not cross the gap. ਉਹ and ਉਮਰ will follow once ਉ has a source;
words with vowel signs, bindi, tippi or addak wait for those signs.
