## You'll want to know: لحاظ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C461-MANNERS461-01]; assesses=[] -->

**لحاظ** — *lihāz* — "consideration; regard (for others)".

> ہمیں بڑوں کا لحاظ رکھنا چاہیے۔
>
> *hameṅ baṛoṅ kā lihāz rakhnā chāhiye.*
>
> We should show consideration to our elders.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**لحاظ** (*lihāz*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *laḥāẓ*, "a glance", and so "regard" for someone.
