# चौकशी करणे (caukśī karṇe) — to inquire; to question

## You'll want to know: चौकशी करणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C290-POLICE290-03]; assesses=[] -->

**चौकशी करणे** — *caukśī karṇe* — "to inquire; to question".

> हवालदाराने दुकानदाराकडे चौकशी केली.
>
> *havāldārāne dukāndārākaḍe caukśī kelī.*
>
> The constable made inquiries with the shopkeeper.

Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने चौकशी केली, *tyāne caukśī kelī*.

The person asked takes -कडे; the matter takes -ची: अपघाताची चौकशी करणे, to look into an accident.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**चौकशी करणे** (*caukśī karṇe*) joins *caukśī*, 'an inquiry', to *karṇe*, 'to do'; the origin of *caukśī* is not settled.
