## You'll want to know: affermare
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C345-AFFERMARE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**affermare** — "to state, to assert".

> Il sindaco afferma che le tasse non aumenteranno.
>
> *The mayor states that taxes will not rise.*

Stronger than *dire*. *Affermarsi* is to become established, to succeed.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*affermare* is a learned borrowing from Latin *affirmāre* "to make firm, to assert" (Lewis & Short), built from *ad* "to" and *firmāre* "to strengthen", on *firmus* "firm".
