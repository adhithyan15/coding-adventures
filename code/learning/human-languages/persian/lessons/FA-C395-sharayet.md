## You'll want to know: شرایط
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C395-BASTE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**شرایط** (*sharâyet*) — "conditions, circumstances".

> در این شرایط نمیتوانیم سفر کنیم.
>
> *dar in sharâyet nemitavânim safar konim.*
>
> In these circumstances we cannot travel.

An Arabic broken plural of *shart* "condition". It also means terms: *sharâyet-e qarârdâd*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**شرایط** is an Arabic broken plural of *shart*, which was borrowed from Arabic *sharṭ* "condition" (EtymDB).
