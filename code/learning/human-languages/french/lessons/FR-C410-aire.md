## You'll want to know: l'aire
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C410-BOUCHONS-04]; assesses=[] -->

**l'aire** — "the rest area (on a motorway); the area".

> Nous mangeons sur une aire de repos.
>
> *We eat at a motorway rest area.*

A feminine noun: *une aire de repos*, *une aire de jeux*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*aire* is inherited from Latin *ārea* 'open space, threshing floor' (EtymDB).
