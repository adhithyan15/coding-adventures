Telugu, chapter 144: To cook, To cut, To break, To send, To look for.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
వండు (vaṇḍu) — to cook.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to sleep, then the Telugu for to get up.

You'll want to know: వండు.
వండు — vaṇḍu — "to cook".

The word, taken apart.
వండు (vaṇḍu) is native Dravidian. DEDR 5329 lists vaṇḍu, "to cook", and vaṇṭa, "cooking", with Kui vaja and Kuwi vaj-, "to cook".

You'll want to know: పగలగొట్టు.

The word, taken apart.
పగలగొట్టు (pagalagoṭṭu) is native Dravidian: pagulu, "to break, crack" (DEDR 3808), and koṭṭu, "to strike", one word with Tamil koṭṭu (DEDR 2063). DEDR compares Pali koṭṭeti, "to beat" (CDIAL 3241).
