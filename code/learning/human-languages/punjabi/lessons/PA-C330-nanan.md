## You'll want to know: ਨਣਾਨ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C330-SPOUSE330-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ਨਣਾਨ** — *naṇān* — "a husband's sister".

> ਮੇਰੀ ਨਣਾਨ ਹਰ ਐਤਵਾਰ ਸਾਡੇ ਘਰ ਆਉਂਦੀ ਹੈ।
>
> *merī naṇān har aitvār sāḍe ghar āundī hai.*
>
> My husband's sister comes to our house every Sunday.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਨਣਾਨ** (*naṇān*) is inherited from Sanskrit *nanāndṛ*, "a husband's sister"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *nanāṇ*, "husband's sister", under it (CDIAL 6946).
