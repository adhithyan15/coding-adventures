Persian, chapter 187: To slip, To accelerate, To harvest, To save, To deposit.
7 lessons. You can do the first 5 of them in the car; after that you will want to have stopped.


Lesson 1 of 7.
لیز خوردن (liz khordan) — to slip.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to shiver, then the Persian for to crawl.

You'll want to know: لیز خوردن.
لیز خوردن — liz khordan — "to slip".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: liz khordan]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: liz khordan, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say liz khordan]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Persian for to shiver, then the Persian for to crawl, then say liz khordan again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does لیز خوردن (liz khordan) mean? ("To slip".) Say it once more.

To take a shower? (دوش گرفتن, dush gereftan.) To pick up? (برداشتن, bardâshtan.) To watch? (تماشا کردن, tamâshâ kardan.) To look at? (نگاه کردن, negâh kardan.) To listen? (گوش دادن, gush dâdan.) To mix? (مخلوط کردن, makhlut kardan.) To stir? (هم زدن, ham zadan.) To boil? (جوشاندن, jushândan.) To fry? (سرخ کردن, sorkh kardan.) To chop? (خرد کردن, khord kardan.) To breathe? (نفس کشیدن, nafas keshidan.) To tidy? (مرتب کردن, morattab kardan.) To shake? (تکان دادن, tekân dâdan.) To cross? (رد شدن, rad shodan.) To stroll? (قدم زدن, qadam zadan.) To book? (رزرو کردن, rezerv kardan.) To hire? (استخدام کردن, estekhdâm kardan.) To repeat? (تکرار کردن, tekrâr kardan.) To translate? (ترجمه کردن, tarjome kardan.) To talk? (صحبت کردن, sohbat kardan.)
