# اگر باران بیاید (agar bârân biyâyad) — if it rains

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[FA-GRAMMAR-AGAR-SUBJ-01, FA-LEX-C298-ZHAKAT-01, FA-SCRIPT-C298-ZHE-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "if it's cold, put on a cardigan". (*Agar havâ sard bâshad,
zhâkat bepushid.*) Then "to come", and its present stem. (*Âmadan*, *â-*.)

[PAUSE 1s] In everyday Persian, rain comes: *bârân miâyad*, "it's raining",
is word for word "rain is coming". That puts *âmadan* into a rainy
condition.

## Grammar Lens: biyâyad, the subjunctive of âmadan
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-GRAMMAR-BIYAYAD-01]; assesses=[FA-LEX-AMADAN, FA-STEM-A, FA-GRAMMAR-SUBJUNCTIVE-BE-01, FA-LEX-BARAN] -->

*Be-* meets the stem *â-*, and a *y* slips in to keep the two vowels apart:
**بیاید**, *biyâyad*, "that it come". So *biyâyam*, *biyâyi*, *biyâyad*.

> **اگر باران بیاید، در خانه میمانیم.** — *agar bârân biyâyad, dar khâne
> mimânim* — If it rains, we'll stay at home.

Word for word, "if rain come, we stay in the house". *Mimânim* is *mândan*,
"to stay", on its stem *mân-*.

## Grammar Lens: three shapes for the result
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-GRAMMAR-REAL-CONDITION-01]; assesses=[FA-LEX-C135-ACT135-02, FA-LEX-C45-HOME45-01, FA-GRAMMAR-POLITE-IMPERATIVE-01, FA-GRAMMAR-BAYAD-SUBJ-01, FA-LEX-AZ-DAST-DADAN-01, FA-LEX-C133-ACT133-04] -->

A real condition can come true, and its result takes one of three shapes.

What will happen, in the plain present:

> **اگر دیر برسی، قطار را از دست میدهی.** — *agar dir beresi, qatâr râ az dast
> midahi* — If you arrive late, you'll miss the train.

What to do, as a polite instruction: *agar bârân biyâyad, zhâkat bepushid*,
if it rains, put on a cardigan.

What must happen, with *bâyad*: *agar bârân biyâyad, bâyad zud beravim*, if
it rains, we have to leave early.

The *agar* half keeps its subjunctive in all three. Only the result changes.

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[FA-GRAMMAR-REAL-CONDITION-01, FA-GRAMMAR-BIYAYAD-01, FA-LEX-AMADAN] -->

[PAUSE 3s] "That it come"? (***Biyâyad.***) Why the *y*? (To keep **two
vowels apart**.) What are the three shapes of the result? (**A present, a
polite instruction, *bâyad***.) What stays the same in the *agar* half? (**The
subjunctive.**)
