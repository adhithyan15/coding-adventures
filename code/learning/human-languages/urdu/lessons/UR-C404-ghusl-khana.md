## You'll want to know: غسل خانہ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C404-GHUSL404-01]; assesses=[] -->

**غسل خانہ** — *ghusl khānā* — "a washroom; a bathroom".

> کمرے کے ساتھ ایک غسل خانہ ہے۔
>
> *kamre ke sāth ek ghusl khānā hai.*
>
> There is a washroom next to the room.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**غسل خانہ** (*ghusl khānā*) is built from *ghusl*, "washing", usually traced to Arabic *ghusl*, and Persian *khāna*, "a house, a room".
