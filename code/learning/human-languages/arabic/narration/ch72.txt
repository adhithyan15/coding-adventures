Arabic, chapter 72: Fork, Bottle, Door, Kitchen, Garden.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
شوكة (shawka) — a fork.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a plate, then the Arabic for a knife.

You'll want to know: شوكة.
شوكة — shawka — "a fork".
