Lesson 1 of 5.
le supermarché — a supermarket.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for a car park, then the French for a lift.

You'll want to know: le supermarché.
le supermarché — "a supermarket".

The word, taken apart.
supermarché — built from super-, Latin for "above", and the French word for "market", which from Latin mercatus, "trade" (Lewis & Short); modelled on English supermarket.
