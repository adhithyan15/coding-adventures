## You'll want to know: zǒngzhī
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C370-KEY370-05]; assesses=[] -->

*zǒngzhī* — "in short; in a word".

> *zǒngzhī wǒ juéde nǐ yīnggāi qù* — In short, I think you should go.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*zǒngzhī* is built from *zǒng* and *zhī*, both inherited from Middle Chinese, *tsuwngX* and *tsyi* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to bind together' and 'all', and the second 'to go'. How the pair came to mean in short is not settled.
