## You'll want to know: بازنشستگی
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C379-ESTEFA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**بازنشستگی** (*bâzneshastegi*) — "retirement".

> پدربزرگم بعد از بازنشستگی بیشتر کتاب میخواند.
>
> *pedarbozorgam ba'd az bâzneshastegi bishtar ketâb mikhânad.*
>
> Since he retired, my grandfather reads more.

From *bâzneshaste* "retired": *bâz* "back" + *neshaste* "seated".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**بازنشستگی** is native Persian, built from *bâz* "back" + *neshaste*, the participle of *neshastan* "to sit", + *-gi*. A retired person has sat back down.
