## You'll want to know: признавать
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C341-KOMPROMISS-05]; assesses=[] -->

**признавать** (*priznavát*) — "to admit, to acknowledge".

> Он не любит признавать свои ошибки.
>
> *On ne lyúbit priznavát svoí oshíbki.*
>
> He does not like admitting his mistakes.

Imperfective; present *признаю, признаёшь*; perfective *признать*. Built on *знать*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**признавать** is built from *при-* and *знать* "to know", which is inherited from Proto-Slavic *\*znati* (EtymDB). To admit a thing is to own that you know it.
