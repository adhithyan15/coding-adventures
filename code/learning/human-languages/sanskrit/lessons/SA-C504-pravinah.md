# प्रवीणः (pravīṇaḥ) — skilled; versed

## You'll want to know: प्रवीणः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C504-AVEDANA504-05]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रवीणः** — *pravīṇaḥ* — "skilled; versed".

> सा संगणके प्रवीणा अस्ति।
>
> *sā saṅgaṇake pravīṇā asti.*
>
> She is skilled with computers.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रवीणः** (*pravīṇaḥ*) is built on *pra* and *vīṇā*, "the lute", as MW gives it; MW gives *pravīṇa* as skilful, clever, versed in a thing (MW).
