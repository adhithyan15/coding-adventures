## You'll want to know: कथा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C334-KATHA334-01]; assesses=[] -->

**कथा** — *kathā* — "a tale; a sacred story".

> दादी हर रात एक नई कथा सुनाती थीं।
>
> *dādī har rāt ek naī kathā sunātī thīṁ.*
>
> Grandmother used to tell a new tale every night.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**कथा** (*kathā*) is Sanskrit *kathā*, "a story, a conversation" (Monier-Williams). Beside **कहानी**, it is the old or sacred tale.
