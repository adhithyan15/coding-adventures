## You'll want to know: भौंह
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C476-CHEHRA476-02]; assesses=[] -->

**भौंह** — *bhauṁh* — "an eyebrow".

> दादा की भौंहें सफ़ेद हैं।
>
> *dādā kī bhauṁheṁ safed haiṁ.*
>
> Grandfather's eyebrows are white.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**भौंह** (*bhauṁh*) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Hindi *bhaũh*, "eyebrow", under Sanskrit *bhrū*, "eyebrow" (CDIAL 9688).
