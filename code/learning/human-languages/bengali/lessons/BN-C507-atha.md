## You'll want to know: আঠা
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C507-PHATOL507-03]; assesses=[] -->

**আঠা** — *āṭhā* — "glue; paste".

> ছেঁড়া বইটা আঠা লাগিয়ে জুড়লাম।
>
> *Chhẽṛā bôiṭā āṭhā lāgiye juṛlām.*
>
> I stuck the torn book back together with glue.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**আঠা** (*āṭhā*) comes from a reconstructed *aṭṭha*; CDIAL lists Bengali *āṭhā*, "gum, paste" (CDIAL 186).
