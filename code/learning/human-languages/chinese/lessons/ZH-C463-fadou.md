## You'll want to know: fādǒu
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C463-FEAR463-04]; assesses=[] -->

*fādǒu* — "to tremble; to shiver".

> *wàibian hěn lěng wǒ de shǒu zài fādǒu* — It's freezing outside, and my hands are shaking.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*fādǒu* is built from 发 'to set going' and *dǒu* 'to shake', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *pjot* and *tuwX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to start' and the second as shaking something out.
