## You'll want to know: तराजू
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C425-TARAJU425-01]; assesses=[] -->

**तराजू** — *tarājū* — "a pair of scales".

> भाजीवाला तराजूने कांद्यांचं वजन करतो.
>
> *bhājīvālā tarājūne kāndyāñcaṁ vajan karto.*
>
> The vegetable seller weighs the onions on the scales.

Masculine.

A masculine noun; the plural is the same.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**तराजू** (*tarājū*) is a loan usually traced to Persian *tarāzū*, "a balance, a pair of scales".
