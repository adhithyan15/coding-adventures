# (reading) — a message with your own sentences in it

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MR-SKILL-READ-LABELS] -->

[PAUSE 2s] The last lesson gave you single words. This gives each one a
sentence to stand in.

[PAUSE 1s] You have written every line below. This time somebody wrote it to
you.

## Reading
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-SKILL-READ-MESSAGE]; assesses=[MR-LEX-VACHNE] -->

> नमस्कार मीरा.
> माझं नाव अरुण आहे.
> भेटून आनंद झाला.
> मला मराठी येते.
> मी मराठी वाचतो.
> धन्यवाद.

[PAUSE 3s] Read it through once, without stopping.

[PAUSE 3s] Again — and notice you did not assemble it line by line the second
time. You took it in.

## You'll want to know: the same sentences, the other way round
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MR-SKILL-READ-MESSAGE] -->

[PAUSE 2s] These are the lines from your own six-field profile and your own
message, in the order you wrote them.

[PAUSE 2s] Writing them, you chose each one and had all the time you wanted.
Reading them, they arrive in somebody else's order and at their pace. That is
a different skill, and it is why this is a separate lesson rather than a
review.

[PAUSE 2s] Six lines is not much. Meeting them from the other side is the
whole exercise.
