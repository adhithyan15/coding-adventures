## You'll want to know: ਧੁੰਦ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C289-COLD289-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ਧੁੰਦ** — *dhund* — "fog; mist".

> ਪੋਹ ਵਿੱਚ ਸਵੇਰੇ ਧੁੰਦ ਹੋਵੇਗੀ।
>
> *poh vich savere dhund hovegī.*
>
> In Poh there will be fog in the morning.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਧੁੰਦ** (*dhund*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*dhūmāndha*, "dim-sighted"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *dhund*, "mist, haziness", under it (CDIAL 6858).
