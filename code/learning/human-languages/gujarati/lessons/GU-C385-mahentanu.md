## You'll want to know: મહેનતાણું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C385-PURASKAR385-03]; assesses=[] -->

**મહેનતાણું** — *mahentāṇũ* — "a fee (payment for work)".

> જો કામ સારું થશે તો હું વધારે મહેનતાણું ચૂકવીશ.
>
> *jo kām sārũ thaśe to hũ vadhāre mahentāṇũ cūkvīś.*
>
> If the work is good, I will pay a higher fee.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**મહેનતાણું** (*mahentāṇũ*) is built on *mahenat*, "hard work", usually traced to Arabic *miḥnat*, "trial, toil", through Persian, with the ending *-āṇũ*: what labour earns.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
