## You'll want to know: zhàn
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C89-ZHAN-01]; assesses=[] -->

*zhàn* — "to stand".

> *qǐng zhàn zài zhèr* — Please stand here.

> *tā zhàn zài ménkǒu* — He's standing in the doorway.

Its character comes later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*zhàn* is inherited from Middle Chinese *treamH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'in common speech, to stand alone'. The same word and character end *huǒchēzhàn*, a railway station.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ZH-LEX-C89-FANG-01, ZH-LEX-C85-ZAI-01] -->

*zhàn* takes 在 and a place, as *fàng* does: *zhàn zài ménkǒu*, standing in the doorway.
