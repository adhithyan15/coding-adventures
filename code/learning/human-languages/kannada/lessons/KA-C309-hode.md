# ಹೋದೆ (hōde) — the past, person by person

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[KA-LEX-C307-KELASA307-04, KA-LEX-C307-KELASA307-05, KA-GRAMMAR-C32-TINNU-02, KA-LEX-C32-TINNU-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the last two things you named, chalk and a ruler. (*sīmesuṇṇa*, *skēlu*.) Then "I ate", with the past bead you met in **ತಿಂದೆ**. (*tinde*.)

[PAUSE 1s] A story needs that past for everyone in it, not only for "I".

## You'll want to know: the past, person by person
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-GRAMMAR-C309-PAST-PERSON-01]; assesses=[KA-LEX-C284-KALA284-03, KA-LEX-C91-ABOUTYOU-04, KA-LEX-C32-HOOGU-01, KA-LEX-C05-MAADU-01] -->

> **ನಿನ್ನೆ ನಾನು ಊರಿಗೆ ಹೋದೆ.**
>
> *ninne nānu ūrige hōde.* — "Yesterday I went to my home town."

**ಹೋದೆ** is "I went". Like **ತಿಂದೆ**, it is three pieces: the stem, the past bead **-ದ-**, and the person ending **-ಎ**. Most verbs put an **-ಇ-** in front of the bead: **ಮಾಡು** → **ಮಾಡಿದ-**. Hold that still and change only the ending:

- **ನಾನು ಮಾಡಿದೆ** (*nānu māḍide*) — I did
- **ನೀನು ಮಾಡಿದೆ** (*nīnu māḍide*) — you did, to a friend or a child: the same ending as "I"
- **ಅವನು ಮಾಡಿದನು** (*avanu māḍidanu*) — he did
- **ಅವಳು ಮಾಡಿದಳು** (*avaḷu māḍidaḷu*) — she did
- **ನಾವು ಮಾಡಿದೆವು** (*nāvu māḍidevu*) — we did
- **ನೀವು ಮಾಡಿದಿರಿ** (*nīvu māḍidiri*) — you did, to several people or with respect
- **ಅವರು ಮಾಡಿದರು** (*avaru māḍidaru*) — they did; also "he" or "she", said with respect

"It" steps out of line: **ಅದು ಮಾಡಿತು** (*adu māḍitu*), with **-ಇತು** and no bead. Two "it" verbs you will hear every day: **ಆಯಿತು** (*āyitu*, "it happened, it became") and **ಬಂತು** (*bantu*, "it came").

## You'll want to know: the stems that change
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-GRAMMAR-C309-PAST-STEMS-01]; assesses=[KA-LEX-C32-BAA-01, KA-LEX-GIVE, KA-LEX-C32-IRU-01, KA-LEX-C32-NOODU-01, KA-LEX-C33-OODU-01, KA-LEX-C147-ACT147-01] -->

A few of the commonest verbs change their stem for the past. They are worth learning as pairs:

- **ಬರು** → **ಬಂದೆ** (*bande*), "I came" (**ಬರು** is the shape of **ಬಾ** that carries endings)
- **ಹೋಗು** → **ಹೋದೆ** (*hōde*), "I went"; it went: **ಹೋಯಿತು** (*hōyitu*)
- **ಕೊಡು** → **ಕೊಟ್ಟೆ** (*koṭṭe*), "I gave"
- **ಇರು** → **ಇದ್ದೆ** (*idde*), "I was"; it was: **ಇತ್ತು** (*ittu*)
- **ತಿನ್ನು** → **ತಿಂದೆ** (*tinde*), "I ate"

The rest keep the **-ಇ-** of **ಮಾಡಿದೆ**: **ನೋಡಿದೆ** (*nōḍide*), "I saw"; **ಓದಿದೆ** (*ōdide*), "I read". A stem that already ends in **-ಇ** takes the bead straight on: **ಕುಡಿ** → **ಕುಡಿದೆ** (*kuḍide*), "I drank".
