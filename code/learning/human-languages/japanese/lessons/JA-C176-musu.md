## You'll want to know: むす
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C176-FRY176-04]; assesses=[] -->

**むす** — *musu* — "to steam".

> **ばんごはんに やさいを むします。** — *bangohan ni yasai o mushimasu* — "I steam vegetables for dinner."

Polite **むします**, *mushimasu*; a godan verb.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**むす** (*musu*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
