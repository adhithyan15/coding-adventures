Russian, chapter 154: So, Since, If, Although, Otherwise.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
поэтому (poétomu) — so, therefore.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a monument, then the Russian for a fountain.

You'll want to know: поэтому.
поэтому (poétomu) — "so, therefore".

The word, taken apart.
поэтому (poétomu) is по, "by, after", with этому, a form of этот, "this": "for this reason". Vasmer builds этот from a pointing э and the old tŭ of тот.

Lesson 2 of 5.
так как (tak kak) — since, as.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a fountain, then the Russian for so.

You'll want to know: так как.
так как (tak kak) — "since, as".

The word, taken apart.
так как (tak kak) is так, "so", with как, "how, as": "so as, since". так comes from the pronoun тот, "that", and как from the question stem of кто, "who" (Vasmer).

Lesson 3 of 5.
если (yésli) — if.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for so, then the Russian for since.

You'll want to know: если.
если (yésli) — "if".

The word, taken apart.
если (yésli) is old естьли, still in Karamzin: есть, "is", with ли, the asking word (Vasmer). It spread in the 1500s and 1600s, perhaps under Polish jeśli.

Lesson 4 of 5.
хотя (khotyá) — although.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for since, then the Russian for if.

You'll want to know: хотя.
хотя (khotyá) — "although".

The word, taken apart.
хотя (khotyá) is the old present participle of хотеть, "to want", Old Russian хотя (khotyá), "wanting" (Vasmer); хоть, "at least, even", is its short form.
