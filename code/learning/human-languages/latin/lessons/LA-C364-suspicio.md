## You'll want to know: suspīciō, suspīciōnis
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C364-CONCLUDO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**suspīciō, suspīciōnis** — "a suspicion, a mistrust".

> In eum suspīciō cecidit.
>
> *Suspicion fell on him.*

*suspīciō, suspīciōnis* f., with a long *ī*; *suspicor*, to suspect, is its verb. English *suspicion*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*suspīciō* comes from *suspiciō* "to look up at; to suspect" (Lewis & Short), built from *sub* "up from under" and *speciō* "to look": a look from below. English *suspicion* comes from it.
