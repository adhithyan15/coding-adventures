## You'll want to know: सहकर्मी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C507-DAFTAR507-01]; assesses=[] -->

**सहकर्मी** — *sahkarmī* — "a colleague; a co-worker".

> मीरा अपनी सहकर्मी के साथ दोपहर का खाना खाती है।
>
> *Mīrā apnī sahkarmī ke sāth dopahar kā khānā khātī hai.*
>
> Mira has lunch with her colleague.

Masculine or feminine, by the person meant.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सहकर्मी** (*sahkarmī*) is a modern compound of two Sanskrit words, *saha*, "with", and *karmin*, "active, busy" (Monier-Williams): one who works alongside you.
