## You'll want to know: testor, testārī
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C399-PERFIDIA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**testor, testārī** — "to call to witness; to testify".

> Deōs testor mē vērum dīcere.
>
> *I call the gods to witness that I speak the truth.*

Deponent: *testor, testārī, testātus sum*, built on *testis*, a witness. *Testāmentum*, a will, is its noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*testor* is built on *testis* "a witness" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short). English *testament* and *testify* come from its family.
