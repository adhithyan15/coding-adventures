## You'll want to know: spōnsālia, spōnsālium
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C327-SPONSA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**spōnsālia, spōnsālium** — "a betrothal; an engagement party".

> Ad spōnsālia multī amīcī vēnērunt.
>
> *Many friends came to the engagement party.*

Neuter, plural only. A ring was often given at the *spōnsālia*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**spōnsālia** is built on *spōnsālis*, "of a betrothal", from *spōnsa* (EtymDB).
