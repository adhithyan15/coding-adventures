Lesson 1 of 5.
nusquam — nowhere.

Warm-up.

You'll want to know: nusquam.
nusquam — "nowhere".

The word, taken apart.
nusquam is ne, "not", fused with usquam, "anywhere" (Lewis & Short): not anywhere.

Lesson 3 of 5.
passim — here and there.

Warm-up.

Before the new one: say the Latin for nowhere, then the Latin for from all sides.

You'll want to know: passim.
passim — "here and there".

The word, taken apart.
passim is built on passus, "spread out", the participle of pandō, "to spread" (Lewis & Short), with -im, an adverb ending: scattered about.
