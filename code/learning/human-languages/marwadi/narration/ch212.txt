Marwadi (Marwari), chapter 212: A Quarter and a Half.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
सवा (savā) — plus a quarter.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for tight, then the Marwadi for to fit.

You'll want to know: सवा.
सवा — savā — "plus a quarter".
सवा सेर आटो दो। savā ser āṭo do. Give me a seer and a quarter of flour.

The word, taken apart.
सवा (savā) is inherited from Sanskrit sapāda, "increased by a fourth"; CDIAL lists Marwari savā, "plus a quarter" (CDIAL 13134).

Lesson 2 of 5.

[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for to fit, then the Marwadi for plus a quarter.

You'll want to know: डेढ.
डेढ — ḍeḍh — "one and a half".
डेढ किलो चावळ दो। ḍeḍh kilo chāvaḷ do. Give me one and a half kilos of rice.

The word, taken apart.
डेढ (ḍeḍh) is inherited from Sanskrit dvyardha, "one and a half". CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry, but lists Hindi ḍeṛh and Sindhi ḍeḍhu, "one and a half" (CDIAL 6698).

दूणो — dūṇo — "double; twice as much".
आज भाव दूणो है। āj bhāv dūṇo hai. Today the price is double.

The word, taken apart.
दूणो (dūṇo) is inherited from a reconstructed duguṇa, "double". CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry, but lists Hindi dūnā, "double" (CDIAL 6390).

Lesson 5 of 5.
छटांक (chhaṭā̃k) — a chhatak (a sixteenth of a seer).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for two and a half, then the Marwadi for double.

You'll want to know: छटांक.
छटांक — chhaṭā̃k — "a chhatak (a sixteenth of a seer)".
एक छटांक जीरो दो। ek chhaṭā̃k jīro do. Give me a chhatak of cumin.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
छटांक (chhaṭā̃k) is inherited from a reconstructed ṣaṭṭaṅka, "weight of six ṭaṅkas". CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry, but lists Hindi chaṭā̃k, "a sixteenth of a ser" (CDIAL 12785).
