Spanish, chapter 688: El mar.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
el océano — the ocean.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for to clamber, then the Spanish for to explore.

You'll want to know: el océano.
el océano — "the ocean".
El océano está muy frío. The ocean is very cold.
El océano is bigger than el mar.

The word, taken apart.
océano is a learned borrowing from Latin ōceanus, from Greek Ōkeanós (EtymDB), the great river that the Greeks thought ran round the world.
