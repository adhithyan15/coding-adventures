## You'll want to know: mortālis, mortāle
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C346-DEUS-04]; assesses=[] -->

**mortālis, mortāle** — "mortal".

> Omnēs hominēs mortālēs sunt.
>
> *All human beings are mortal.*

Two endings, from *mors*, 'death'. As a noun, *mortālēs*: human beings.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**mortālis** is built on *mors*, "death" (EtymDB): subject to death. English *mortal* comes from it.
