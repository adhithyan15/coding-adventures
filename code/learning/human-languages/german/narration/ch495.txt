German, chapter 495: Kinder erziehen.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
erziehen — to bring up, to raise.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to reconcile, then the German for reconciliation.

You'll want to know: erziehen.
erziehen — "to bring up, to raise".
Sie hat ihre drei Kinder allein erzogen. She brought up her three children on her own.
Irregular: erzog, hat erzogen.

The word, taken apart.
erziehen is built on er- and ziehen "to pull, to draw", inherited from Old High German ziohan, from Proto-Germanic teuhaną (EtymDB).

Lesson 2 of 5.
die Erziehung — upbringing.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for reconciliation, then the German for to bring up.

You'll want to know: die Erziehung.
die Erziehung — "upbringing".
Die Eltern streiten oft über die Erziehung. The parents often argue about how to bring up the children.
No plural. School learning is die Bildung.

The word, taken apart.
Erziehung is built on erziehen with -ung (EtymDB lists both); ziehen goes back to Proto-Germanic teuhaną "to pull, to lead".

Lesson 3 of 5.
verwöhnen — to spoil, to pamper.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to bring up, then the German for upbringing.

You'll want to know: verwöhnen.
verwöhnen — "to spoil, to pamper".
Die Großeltern verwöhnen ihre Enkel mit Süßigkeiten. The grandparents spoil their grandchildren with sweets.
Verwöhnt — spoilt. Sich verwöhnen lassen — to let yourself be pampered.

The word, taken apart.
verwöhnen is usually traced to Middle High German verwenen "to accustom wrongly", built on ver- and the root of gewöhnen "to get used to"; EtymDB lists the word without a source.

Lesson 4 of 5.
gehorchen — to obey.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for upbringing, then the German for to spoil.

You'll want to know: gehorchen.
gehorchen — "to obey".
Der Hund gehorcht nur meiner Mutter. The dog only obeys my mother.
With a dative: dem Lehrer gehorchen. Inseparable: hat gehorcht.

The word, taken apart.
gehorchen is built on horchen "to listen, to hearken" (EtymDB) with ge-: to listen and do as you are told.

In der Pubertät hatte mein Sohn oft schlechte Laune. During puberty my son was often in a bad mood.

Pubertät is a learned borrowing from Latin pūbertās "the age of maturity", built on pūbēs "grown up" (Lewis & Short).
