## You'll want to know: હાનિ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C370-KHATRO370-02]; assesses=[] -->

**હાનિ** — *hāni* — "harm".

> જો તમે વધારે દવા લેશો તો શરીરને હાનિ થશે.
>
> *jo tame vadhāre davā leśo to śarīrne hāni thaśe.*
>
> If you take too much medicine, it will harm your body.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**હાનિ** (*hāni*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *hāni*, "abandonment, loss, damage" (Monier-Williams).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
