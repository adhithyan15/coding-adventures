Lesson 1 of 5.
ડાકુ (ḍāku) — a bandit.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: ડાકુ.
ડાકુ — ḍāku — "a bandit".
જૂના વખતમાં આ જંગલમાં ડાકુ રહેતા હતા. jūnā vakhatmā̃ ā jangalmā̃ ḍāku rahetā hatā. In the old days bandits lived in this forest.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
comes from ḍākka, "robber, robbery", a form CDIAL reconstructs; CDIAL lists Gujarati ḍāko, an attack by robbers, in the same entry (CDIAL 5543). A ડાકુ (ḍāku) belongs to a gang that robs on the roads.

Lesson 2 of 5.
બંદૂક (bandūk) — a gun.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to undergo, then the Gujarati for a bandit.

You'll want to know: બંદૂક.
બંદૂક — bandūk — "a gun".
ડાકુએ કહ્યું કે જો કોઈ ભાગશે તો તે બંદૂક ચલાવશે. ḍākue kahyũ ke jo koī bhāgśe to te bandūk calāvśe. The bandit said that if anyone ran, he would fire his gun.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
બંદૂક (bandūk) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic bunduq, a hazelnut and then a small round pellet; the step to a gun is said to have come through the pellet bow. It is feminine, and a gun is fired with ચલાવવું: બંદૂક ચલાવી.

ધમકી — dhamkī — "a threat".

The word, taken apart.
ધમકી (dhamkī) comes from dhammakka, "a threat", a form CDIAL reconstructs, and CDIAL lists the Gujarati noun (CDIAL 6736). You give a threat: ધમકી આપી.

Lesson 4 of 5.
ધમકાવવું (dhamkāvvũ) — to threaten.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a gun, then the Gujarati for a threat.

You'll want to know: ધમકાવવું.
ધમકાવવું — dhamkāvvũ — "to threaten".
એક ડાકુએ બંદૂક બતાવીને મુસાફરોને ધમકાવ્યા. ek ḍākue bandūk batāvīne musāphrone dhamkāvyā. One bandit showed his gun and threatened the passengers.
Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે મુસાફરોને ધમકાવ્યા, teṇe musāphrone dhamkāvyā.

The word, taken apart.
ધમકાવવું (dhamkāvvũ) belongs with ધમકી (dhamkī), a threat: CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb in the same entry, dhammakka (CDIAL 6736). To threaten is to frighten someone with words or a weapon.

Lesson 5 of 5.
છીનવવું (chīnavvũ) — to snatch.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a threat, then the Gujarati for to threaten.

You'll want to know: છીનવવું.
છીનવવું — chīnavvũ — "to snatch".
ડાકુઓએ મારી થેલી છીનવી લીધી. ḍākuoe mārī thelī chīnvī līdhī. The bandits snatched my bag.
Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે થેલી છીનવી, teṇe thelī chīnvī.

The word, taken apart.
છીનવવું (chīnavvũ) is set by CDIAL under Sanskrit chinná, "cut off", where it lists the Gujarati verb (CDIAL 5047): what is snatched is torn away from its owner. It often takes લેવું: છીનવી લીધી, snatched it away.
