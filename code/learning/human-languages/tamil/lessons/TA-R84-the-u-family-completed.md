# (உ ஊ ு ூ — and the two that open o)

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[TA-SCRIPT-READ-UNAVU-02, TA-SCRIPT-READ-UUR-02] -->

[PAUSE 3s] Read **உணவு**, then **ஊர்**. Say what the first letter of each is
doing differently.

## Script: the square the u-family always formed
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[TA-SCRIPT-READ-UNAVU-02, TA-SCRIPT-UU-VOWEL-01, TA-SCRIPT-UU-SIGN-01] -->

Four pieces, arriving in four separate chapters, and they were a grid the whole
time:

| | standing alone | hanging on a consonant |
|---|---|---|
| **short u** | **உ** | **ு** |
| **long ū** | **ஊ** | **ூ** |

**உணவு** holds both columns of the top row — it opens with the standing **உ** and
closes with the hanging **ு**, the same vowel wearing two shapes in one word.
That word is the argument for the grid, and it is the reason this page exists:
no chapter could show the square, because no chapter had all four corners.

## Reading: the words that carried them in
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[TA-SCRIPT-READ-UNAVU-02, TA-SCRIPT-READ-UUR-02, TA-SCRIPT-READ-MUUNRU-02, TA-SCRIPT-READ-PESU-02] -->

| word | means | the piece it brought |
|---|---|---|
| **உணவு** | food | standing **உ** against hanging **ு** |
| **ஊர்** | town, home place | standing **ஊ**, the long partner |
| **மூன்று** | three | the hanging **ூ**, closing the family |
| **பேசு** | speak | the long-e sign **ே** |

## Script: o, standing and hanging
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[TA-SCRIPT-O-VOWEL-01, TA-SCRIPT-READ-ONRU-02, TA-SCRIPT-OO-SIGN-01, TA-SCRIPT-READ-PO-02] -->

**ஒன்று** ("one") opens with **ஒ**, the standing short o. **போ** ("go") carries
**ோ**, the hanging long one — and **ோ** is the odd shape of the set, because it
is built from **two** marks, one placed before its consonant and one after.

So a single vowel can be written on both sides of the letter it belongs to at
once. Reading it means taking the whole group in before saying anything.
