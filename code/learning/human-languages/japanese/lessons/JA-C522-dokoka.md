## You'll want to know: どこか
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C522-SOME522-03]; assesses=[] -->

**どこか** — *dokoka* — "somewhere".

> **もし どこか いい みせが あったら、おしえて ください。** — *moshi dokoka ii mise ga attara, oshiete kudasai* — "If there is a good shop somewhere, please tell me."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**どこか** (*dokoka*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, and usually written in kana: built from *doko*, "where", and the particle *ka* (JMdict).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
