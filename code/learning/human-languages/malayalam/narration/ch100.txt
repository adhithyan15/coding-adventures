Malayalam, chapter 100: What You Wear.
5 lessons. You can do the first 3 of them in the car; after that you will want to have stopped.


Lesson 1 of 5.
മുണ്ട് (muṇṭŭ) — a mundu.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say the word for cloth. Then say new.

You'll want to know.
മുണ്ട് — muṇṭŭ — a mundu, the length of cloth worn wrapped at the waist.
ഇത് പുതിയ മുണ്ടാണ്. — itŭ putiya muṇṭāṇŭ — "This is a new mundu."

The word, taken apart.
The book gave you the cloth before it gave you the garment. തുണി is the stuff; മുണ്ട് (muṇṭŭ) is the thing made of it and worn.
Two things in that sentence you already know how to do, and neither is new:
പുതിയ sits in front and does not change — that is the whole adjective pattern from the chapter that taught it.
മുണ്ട് (muṇṭŭ) ends in the breathed half-u, so ആണ് lands straight on it: മുണ്ടാണ്, nothing slid in — the same join പാട്ട് takes.
So the only new thing here is the word itself.

Lesson 2 of 5.
സാരി (sāri) — a sari.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say a mundu. Then say old.

You'll want to know.
സാരി — sāri — a sari.
ഇത് പഴയ സാരിയാണ്. — itŭ paḻaya sāriyāṇŭ — "This is an old sari."

The word, taken apart.
Two garments, two different landings. മുണ്ട് (muṇṭŭ) ends in the half-u and takes ആണ് straight on. സാരി (sāri) ends in ഇ, and you know what ഇ does:
[a table of 2 rows, read aloud]
word: മുണ്ട് (muṇṭŭ). joined to ആണ്: മുണ്ടാണ് (muṇṭāṇŭ).
word: സാരി. joined to ആണ്: സാരിയാണ് (sāriyāṇŭ).
The യ in സാരിയാണ് is the one കളി and ഭംഗി take, for the reason you were given then: ഇ calls for it.
പഴയ behaves as പുതിയ did — in front, unchanged.

Lesson 3 of 5.
ചെരുപ്പ് (ceruppŭ) — a sandal.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say a sari. Then say a mundu.

You'll want to know.
ചെരുപ്പ് — ceruppŭ — a sandal, the everyday open footwear.
ഇത് പഴയ ചെരുപ്പാണ്. — itŭ paḻaya ceruppāṇŭ — "This is an old sandal."

The word, taken apart.
This word is not a garment, and the book wants both. മുണ്ട് (muṇṭŭ) and സാരി (sāri) go on the body; ചെരുപ്പ് (ceruppŭ) goes on the feet.
Its ending you have already handled twice. ചെരുപ്പ് (ceruppŭ) finishes in the breathed half-u, like മുണ്ട് (muṇṭŭ), so ആണ് lands straight on it: ചെരുപ്പാണ്.
[a table of 2 rows, read aloud]
word: മുണ്ട് (muṇṭŭ). joined to ആണ്: മുണ്ടാണ് (muṇṭāṇŭ).
word: ചെരുപ്പ് (ceruppŭ). joined to ആണ്: ചെരുപ്പാണ് (ceruppāṇŭ).

Lesson 4 of 5.
തൊപ്പി (toppi) — a cap.

Before we start: this one needs your eyes, so it is not fully a driving lesson. You will want your eyes, because the lesson points at something written down. You can listen to all of it now and come back to the parts that need looking at once you have stopped.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say a sandal. Then say new.

You'll want to know.
തൊപ്പി — toppi — a cap.
ഇത് പുതിയ തൊപ്പിയാണ്. — itŭ putiya toppiyāṇŭ — "This is a new cap."

The word, taken apart.
Four things worn, and only two endings between them. തൊപ്പി (toppi) ends in ഇ, so it joins the way സാരി (sāri) does:
[a table of 4 rows, read aloud]
ends in: half-u. word: മുണ്ട് (muṇṭŭ). joined to ആണ്: മുണ്ടാണ് (muṇṭāṇŭ).
ends in: half-u. word: ചെരുപ്പ് (ceruppŭ). joined to ആണ്: ചെരുപ്പാണ് (ceruppāṇŭ).
ends in: ഇ. word: സാരി. joined to ആണ്: സാരിയാണ് (sāriyāṇŭ).
ends in: ഇ. word: തൊപ്പി. joined to ആണ്: തൊപ്പിയാണ് (toppiyāṇŭ).
Nothing in this chapter asked you to learn a join. Both were already yours — the half-u from the song word, the യ from the game and the beauty words. What the chapter gave you is four things to wear.

Lesson 5 of 5.
(a mundu, a sari, a sandal, a cap) (muṇṭuṁ sāriyuṁ enna ōrmma) — from cold.

Warm-up.
[pause 3 seconds]
Close the lessons before this one. Say a mundu, a sari, a sandal, a cap.

Grammar Lens: two endings, and an adjective that never moves.
Four things to wear, and between them only two endings:
[a table of 4 rows, read aloud]
ends in: half-u. word: മുണ്ട് (muṇṭŭ). joined to ആണ്: മുണ്ടാണ് (muṇṭāṇŭ).
ends in: half-u. word: ചെരുപ്പ് (ceruppŭ). joined to ആണ്: ചെരുപ്പാണ് (ceruppāṇŭ).
ends in: ഇ. word: സാരി. joined to ആണ്: സാരിയാണ് (sāriyāṇŭ).
ends in: ഇ. word: തൊപ്പി. joined to ആണ്: തൊപ്പിയാണ് (toppiyāṇŭ).
Neither join was new. The half-u one arrived with the song word; the യ arrived with the game and the beauty words. This chapter spent all four of its words on things, and none on grammar.

What you've built.
ഇത് പുതിയ മുണ്ടാണ്. — itŭ putiya muṇṭāṇŭ — this is a new mundu. ഇത് പഴയ സാരിയാണ്. — itŭ paḻaya sāriyāṇŭ — this is an old sari.
പുതിയ and പഴയ sit in front and do not change — the same shape they had in the chapter that taught them, with no ending added for the noun that follows.
