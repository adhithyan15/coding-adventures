## You'll want to know: تطعيم
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C509-ADWA509-04]; assesses=[] -->

**تطعيم** — *taṭʿīm* — "a vaccination".

> أخذ الأطفال التطعيم في المدرسة.
>
> *akhadha al-aṭfāl at-taṭʿīm fī al-madrasa.*
>
> The children had their vaccination at school.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**تطعيم** (*taṭʿīm*) is built on the native Arabic root *ṭ-ʿ-m*, "to eat, to taste" (Lane), the root of *ṭaʿām*, food. Lane gives Form II *ṭaʿʿama* as "to graft" a branch; modern Arabic uses the same word for putting a vaccine into the body.
