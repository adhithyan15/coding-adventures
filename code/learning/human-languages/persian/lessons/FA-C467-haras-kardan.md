## You'll want to know: هرس کردن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C467-BAGH-05]; assesses=[] -->

**هرس کردن** (*haras kardan*) — "to prune".

> باغبان در زمستان درختها را هرس میکند.
>
> *Bâghbân dar zemestân derakht-hâ râ haras mikonad.*
>
> The gardener prunes the trees in winter.

*haras* 'pruning' + *kardan*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*haras kardan* is built from *haras* 'pruning', whose origin is not settled, and *kardan* 'to do', inherited from Middle Persian *kardan* (EtymDB).
