## You'll want to know: 西部
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C438-NORTH438-03]; assesses=[] -->

**西部** — *xībù* — "western part; the western regions".

> **中国西部的山很高** *Zhōngguó xībù de shān hěn gāo* — The mountains in western China are very high.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**西部** (*xībù*) is built from 西 'west' and 部 'a part', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *sej* and *buX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription).
