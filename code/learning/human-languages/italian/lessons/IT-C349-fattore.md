## You'll want to know: il fattore
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C349-CAUSA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**il fattore** — "the factor".

> Il prezzo è un fattore importante nella scelta.
>
> *The price is an important factor in the choice.*

*Il fattore tempo* — the time factor. In the country, *il fattore* was also the farm manager.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*fattore* comes from Latin *factor* "maker, doer, performer" (EtymDB), built on *facere* "to make": one of the things that make a result.
