## You'll want to know: ধ্বংস
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C293-BHUMIKOMPO293-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ধ্বংস** — *dhvôngsô* — "destruction; ruin".

> ভূমিকম্পে পুরো শহরটা ধ্বংস হল।
>
> *bhūmikômpe puro shôhôrṭā dhvôngsô hôlô.*
>
> The whole city was destroyed in the earthquake.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ধ্বংস** (*dhvôngsô*) is Sanskrit *dhvaṃsa*, 'a falling down, ruin' (Monier-Williams), the learned noun beside **ধসে পড়া**, which wore the same root down to ধস. ধ্বংস হওয়া, to be destroyed.
