## You'll want to know: બેટ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C258-CRICKET258-03]; assesses=[] -->

**બેટ** — *beṭ* — "bat".

> **તેણે નવું બેટ ખરીદ્યું.** — *teṇe navũ beṭ kharīdyũ* — "He bought a new bat."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**બેટ** (*beṭ*) is a loan from English *bat*, the cricket bat.
