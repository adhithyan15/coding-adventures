# انتصر (intaṣara) — to win a victory; to triumph

## You'll want to know: انتصر
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C315-NASR315-03]; assesses=[] -->

**انتصر** — *intaṣara* — "to win a victory; to triumph".

> انتصر فريقنا في المباراة الأخيرة.
>
> *intaṣara farīqunā fī al-mubārāh al-akhīra.*
>
> Our team won the last match.

Present: ينتصر, *yantaṣir*.

انتصر على: to triumph over.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**انتصر** (*intaṣara*) is built on the native Arabic root *n-ṣ-r* (Lane), "to help against an enemy". It is Form VIII, "to get the victory".
