## You'll want to know: residir
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C684-CIUDADANO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**residir** — "to reside, to live".

> Residimos en la ciudad desde hace años.
>
> *We have lived in the city for years.*

A formal word for *vivir*, common on forms.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*residir* is a learned borrowing from Latin *residēre* "to remain, to settle" (EtymDB), built on *sedēre* "to sit".

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
