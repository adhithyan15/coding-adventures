## You'll want to know: ઘૂઘરો
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C554-RAAS554-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ઘૂઘરો** — *ghūghro* — "an ankle bell".

> જો તું પગમાં ઘૂઘરા પહેરીશ તો નાચતી વખતે મીઠો અવાજ આવશે.
>
> *jo tũ pagmā̃ ghūghrā paherīś to nāctī vakhte mīṭho avāj āvśe.*
>
> If you wear ankle bells, they'll ring sweetly as you dance.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ઘૂઘરો** (*ghūghro*) comes from *\*ghuṅghura*, a form CDIAL reconstructs, and CDIAL lists the Gujarati word, "a jingling bell" (CDIAL 4477).
