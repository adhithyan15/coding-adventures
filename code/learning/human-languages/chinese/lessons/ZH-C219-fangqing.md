## You'll want to know: fàngqíng
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C219-CLOUD219-05]; assesses=[] -->

*fàngqíng* — "to clear up (of the sky)".

> *xiàwǔ huì fàngqíng* — It'll clear up in the afternoon.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*fàngqíng* is built from *fàng* 'to let go' and *qíng* 'clear'. The first is inherited from Middle Chinese *pjangH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'to drive out'; the dictionary glosses the second, *dzjeng* there, 'a clear sky'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
