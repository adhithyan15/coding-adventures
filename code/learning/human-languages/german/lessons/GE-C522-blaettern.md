## You'll want to know: blättern
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C522-VERLAG-02]; assesses=[] -->

**blättern** — "to leaf (through)".

> Im Wartezimmer blättert sie in einer Zeitschrift.
>
> *In the waiting room she leafs through a magazine.*

*In einem Buch blättern* — to leaf through a book.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*blättern* is built on *Blatt* "leaf, page", inherited from Old High German *blat*, from Proto-Germanic *bladą* (EtymDB).
