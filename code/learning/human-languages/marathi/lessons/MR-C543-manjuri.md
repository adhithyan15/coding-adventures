## You'll want to know: मंजुरी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C543-HOKAR543-01]; assesses=[] -->

**मंजुरी** — *mañjurī* — "approval; sanction".

> नवीन रस्त्याला सरकारची मंजुरी मिळाली.
>
> *navīn rastyālā sarkārcī mañjurī miḷālī.*
>
> The new road got the government's approval.

Feminine.

What is approved takes -ला: रस्त्याला मंजुरी.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**मंजुरी** (*mañjurī*) is a loan from Persian *manẓūrī*, "approval", usually traced to Arabic *manẓūr*, "seen, accepted".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
