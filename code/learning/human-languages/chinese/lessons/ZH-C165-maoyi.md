## You'll want to know: máoyī
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C165-COAT165-02]; assesses=[] -->

*máoyī* — "sweater; jumper".

> *wǒ de máoyī hěn hǎokàn* — My sweater looks nice.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*máoyī* is built from *máo* 'hair, wool' and 衣 'garment', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *maw* and *'j+j* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first as the hair of brows and of beasts.
