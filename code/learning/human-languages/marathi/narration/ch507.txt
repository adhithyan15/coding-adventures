Lesson 1 of 5.
भरती (bhartī) — recruitment; enrolment.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a document, then the Marathi for to scrutinize.

You'll want to know: भरती.
भरती — bhartī — "recruitment; enrolment".
पोलिसांची भरती पुढच्या महिन्यात आहे. polisāñcī bhartī puḍhcyā mahinyāt āhe. The police recruitment is next month.
Feminine.
भरती (bhartī) होणे, to be recruited; भरती (bhartī) करणे, to recruit.

The word, taken apart.
भरती (bhartī) is built on भरणे, "to fill": a recruitment fills the empty places. The verb is inherited from Sanskrit bharati, "bears, fills" (CDIAL 9397).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: प्रशिक्षण.
प्रशिक्षण — praśikṣaṇ — "training".
नोकरीच्या आधी मला एक महिना प्रशिक्षण मिळालं. nokrīcyā ādhī malā ek mahinā praśikṣaṇ miḷālaṁ. Before the job I got a month of training.
Neuter.
The trainer is प्रशिक्षक.

The word, taken apart.
प्रशिक्षण (praśikṣaṇ) is a tatsama coinage from Sanskrit pra-, "forth", and śikṣaṇa, "teaching, instruction" (Monier-Williams).

[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: हातोटी.
हातोटी — hātoṭī — "a knack; a deft touch".
माझ्या आजीला भाकरी करण्याची चांगली हातोटी आहे. mājhyā ājīlā bhākrī karṇyācī cāṅglī hātoṭī āhe. My grandmother has a real knack for making bhakri.
Feminine.
The one who has it takes -ला: तिला हातोटी (hātoṭī) आहे.

The word, taken apart.
हातोटी (hātoṭī) is built on हात, "hand", inherited from Sanskrit hasta (CDIAL 14024): a knack sits in the hands.

Lesson 5 of 5.
रुजू होणे (rujū hoṇe) — to join a post; to report for duty.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for training, then the Marathi for a knack.

You'll want to know: रुजू होणे.
रुजू होणे — rujū hoṇe — "to join a post; to report for duty".
अरुण सोमवारी नवीन कार्यालयात रुजू झाला. aruṇ somvārī navīn kāryālayāt rujū jhālā. Arun joined the new office on Monday.
Past (he …): तो कामावर रुजू झाला, to kāmāvar rujū jhālā.
The place takes -त or -वर: कार्यालयात रुजू होणे (rujū hoṇe), कामावर रुजू होणे (rujū hoṇe).

The word, taken apart.
रुजू होणे (rujū hoṇe) is रुजू, a loan usually traced to Arabic rujūʿ, "turning to", through Persian, with होणे: to present oneself for duty.
