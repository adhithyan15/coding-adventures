## You'll want to know: il principe
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C325-REGNO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**il principe** — "the prince".

> Il principe è arrivato a cavallo.
>
> *The prince arrived on horseback.*

Plural *i principi*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*principe* is inherited from Latin *prīnceps* "the first, the chief" (EtymDB).
