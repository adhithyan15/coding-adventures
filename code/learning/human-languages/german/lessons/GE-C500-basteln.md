## You'll want to know: basteln
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C500-SAMMELN-03]; assesses=[] -->

**basteln** — "to do crafts, to make things by hand".

> Mit den Kindern bastle ich Sterne aus Papier.
>
> *I make paper stars with the children.*

*Ich bastle* — the *e* of *-eln* usually drops.
