# जाणार आहे (jāṇār āhe) — a plan, and won't

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MR-GRAMMAR-C277-FUTURE-PERSON-01, MR-LEX-C160-BHAV160-03] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "I will go". (*mī jāīn*.) Then "next week". (*puḍhcyā āṭhavḍyāt*.)

## You'll want to know: -णार आहे, 'going to'
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-GRAMMAR-C277-NAR-PLAN-01]; assesses=[MR-LEX-AAHE-COPULA, MR-LEX-JANE, MR-LEX-KARNE, MR-LEX-C109-ADJ109-02, MR-LEX-C115-TOWN115-04] -->

> **पुढच्या आठवड्यात मी गावाला जाणार आहे.**
>
> *puḍhcyā āṭhavḍyāt mī gāvālā jāṇār āhe.* — "Next week I am going to go to the village."

Take **-णे** off the dictionary form and put **-णार** on: **जाणे** → **जाणार** (*jāṇār*), **करणे** → **करणार** (*karṇār*). Then the present of "to be" carries the person: **मी जाणार आहे** (*mī jāṇār āhe*), **तुम्ही जाणार आहात** (*tumhī jāṇār āhāt*), and, with **आहोत** (*āhot*, "we are"), **आम्ही जाणार आहोत** (*āmhī jāṇār āhot*). It says a plan already made, where **जाईन** can be a promise made on the spot.

## You'll want to know: -णार नाही, 'won't'
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-GRAMMAR-C277-NAR-NAHI-01]; assesses=[MR-GRAMMAR-NEGATE-NAHI, MR-LEX-YENE] -->

> **मी उद्या येणार नाही.**
>
> *mī udyā yeṇār nāhī.* — "I won't come tomorrow."

Put **नाही** in place of **आहे**. This is the everyday "won't" for every person: **तो जाणार नाही** (*to jāṇār nāhī*), **आम्ही करणार नाही** (*āmhī karṇār nāhī*).
