## You'll want to know: xiǎngniàn
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C325-PASSED325-05]; assesses=[] -->

*xiǎngniàn* — "to miss someone".

> *wàigōng qùshì yǐhòu wǒ hěn xiǎngniàn tā* — After my grandpa died I missed him very much.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*xiǎngniàn* is built from 想 'to think' and *niàn* 'to think of', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *sjangX* and *nemH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses both 'to think'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
