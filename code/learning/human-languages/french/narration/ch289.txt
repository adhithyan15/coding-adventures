French, chapter 289: Une agression.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
l'agression — the attack, the assault.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for to swindle, then the French for the fraud.

You'll want to know: l'agression.
l'agression — "the attack, the assault".
Une agression a eu lieu près du marché. An assault took place near the market.
An attack on a person. A hostile mood is l'agressivité.

The word, taken apart.
agression is a learned borrowing from Latin aggressiō "a going towards", from aggredī "to approach, to attack", built on gradī "to step" (Lewis & Short).

What you've built.

Lesson 4 of 5.
menacer — to threaten.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for to attack, then the French for the attacker.

You'll want to know: menacer.
menacer — "to threaten".
Il a menacé le vendeur avec un couteau. He threatened the shop assistant with a knife.
Menacer de + infinitive: il a menacé de partir.

The word, taken apart.
menacer is inherited from Vulgar Latin mināciāre "to threaten" (EtymDB), built on Latin mināx "threatening".
