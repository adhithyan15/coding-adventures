# ਕੀ ਤੁਸੀਂ ਚਾਹ ਪੀਂਦੇ ਹੋ? — do you drink tea?

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-GRAMMAR-VOWEL-STEM-PLURAL-211, PA-GRAMMAR-POLAR-KI-214] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say *you drink tea*. Then *are you a traveller?*

## Grammar Lens: ਕੀ and the every-day present
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-GRAMMAR-POLAR-HABITUAL-215]; assesses=[PA-GRAMMAR-POLAR-KI-214, PA-GRAMMAR-VOWEL-STEM-PLURAL-211, PA-GRAMMAR-KHANDA-PINDA-211, PA-GRAMMAR-VOWEL-STEM-211] -->

Nothing new to build: put **ਕੀ** in front of a present sentence, and let the
voice rise.

> **ਕੀ ਤੁਸੀਂ ਚਾਹ ਪੀਂਦੇ ਹੋ?** — *kī tusīṁ chāh pīnde ho?* — Do you drink tea?

> **ਕੀ ਉਹ ਰੋਜ਼ ਸਕੂਲ ਜਾਂਦੀ ਹੈ?** — *kī uh roz sakūl jāndī hai?* — Does she go to school every day?

> **ਕੀ ਤੁਸੀਂ ਰੋਜ਼ ਕੰਮ ਕਰਦੇ ਹੋ?** — *kī tusīṁ roz kamm karde ho?* — Do you work every day?
