## You'll want to know: ਸਿਪਾਹੀ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C272-CROWD272-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ਸਿਪਾਹੀ** — *sipāhī* — "a police constable; a soldier".

> ਚੌਂਕ ਵਿੱਚ ਇੱਕ ਸਿਪਾਹੀ ਹੈ।
>
> *chauṅk vich ikk sipāhī hai.*
>
> There is a constable in the square.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਸਿਪਾਹੀ** (*sipāhī*) is a loan, usually traced to Persian *sipāhī*, "a soldier".
