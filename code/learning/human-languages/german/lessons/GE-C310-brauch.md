## You'll want to know: der Brauch
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C310-KARNEVAL-05]; assesses=[] -->

**der Brauch** — "the custom".

> Das ist ein alter Brauch in unserem Dorf.
>
> *That is an old custom in our village.*

Plural: *die Bräuche*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Brauch* comes from Middle High German *brūch*, Old High German *brūh* "use" (EtymDB), related to *brauchen*: what people do by use.
