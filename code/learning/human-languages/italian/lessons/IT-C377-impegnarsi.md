## You'll want to know: impegnarsi
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C377-IMPEGNO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**impegnarsi** — "to commit yourself, to make an effort".

> Se ti impegni, passi l'esame.
>
> *If you make an effort, you will pass the exam.*

*Impegnarsi a fare qualcosa* — to undertake to do something.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*impegnarsi* is built on *impegnare* "to pledge", from *pegno* "a pledge", inherited from Latin *pignus* (Lewis & Short): to give yourself as a pledge.
