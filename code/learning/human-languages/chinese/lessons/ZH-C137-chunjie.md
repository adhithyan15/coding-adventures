## You'll want to know: 春节
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C137-HOLIDAY137-02]; assesses=[] -->

**春节** — *Chūnjié* — "the Spring Festival; Chinese New Year".

> **春节前大家都打扫** *Chūnjié qián dàjiā dōu dǎsǎo* — Before the festival, everybody cleans the house.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**春节** (*Chūnjié*) is built from 春 'spring' and 节 'festival', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *tsyhwin* and *tset* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription): the festival at the head of spring.
