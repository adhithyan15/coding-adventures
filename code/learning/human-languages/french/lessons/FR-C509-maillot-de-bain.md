## You'll want to know: le maillot de bain
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C509-EAU-03]; assesses=[] -->

**le maillot de bain** — "swimsuit, swimming trunks".

> J'ai oublié mon maillot de bain à l'hôtel.
>
> *I left my swimsuit at the hotel.*

Often *le maillot* alone.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*le maillot de bain* joins *maillot*, built on *maille* "a stitch, a mesh", from Latin *macula* "a spot, a mesh", and *bain* "bath", from Latin *balneum* (Lewis & Short).
