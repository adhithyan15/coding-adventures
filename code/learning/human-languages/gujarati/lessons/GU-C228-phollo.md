## You'll want to know: ફોલ્લો
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C228-SKIN228-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ફોલ્લો** — *phollo* — "blister, boil".

> **આ ફોલ્લો મોટો નથી.** — *ā phollo moṭo nathī* — "This blister is not big."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ફોલ્લો** (*phollo*) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from *sphoṭa*, "swelling, boil" (CDIAL 13854).
