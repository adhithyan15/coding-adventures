## You'll want to know: ちょうじょ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C476-KIN476-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ちょうじょ** — *chōjo* — "the eldest daughter".

> **ちょうじょは いしゃに なりました。** — *chōjo wa isha ni narimashita* — "Our eldest daughter became a doctor."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ちょうじょ** (*chōjo*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *chō*, "senior" (Middle Chinese *trjangX*) and *jo*, "woman" (*nrjoX*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
