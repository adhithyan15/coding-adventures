## You'll want to know: la pédale
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C401-CONDUITE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**la pédale** — "the pedal".

> Le pied droit reste près de la pédale.
>
> *The right foot stays near the pedal.*

*Pédaler* — to pedal, on a bike.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*pédale* was borrowed from Italian *pedale*, which is inherited from Latin *pedālis* 'of the foot', from *pēs* 'foot' (EtymDB).
