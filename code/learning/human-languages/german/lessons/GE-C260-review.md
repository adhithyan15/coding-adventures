# Review — if, and it depends

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GE-GRAMMAR-WENN-FALLS-01, GE-ETYMON-FALLS-FALL-02] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "in case the train is late". (*Falls der Zug Verspätung
hat.*)

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GE-GRAMMAR-WENN-PRAESENS-01, GE-GRAMMAR-VERB-KOMMA-VERB-02, GE-LEX-ES-KOMMT-DARAUF-AN-01, GE-LEX-HAENGT-DAVON-AB-02] -->

Three lessons, and a plan can now branch. A real condition with *wenn*, or
with *falls* when you mean only "in case". Both halves in the present, even
for tomorrow. Verb, comma, verb when the condition comes first. And when
you cannot promise, *es kommt darauf an* or *das hängt davon ab*, with *ob*
for the open question.

[PAUSE 2s] One exchange that uses most of it:

> *Kommst du morgen?*
> *Es kommt darauf an, ob ich Zeit habe. Wenn ich Zeit habe, komme ich.
> Falls nicht, rufe ich dich an.*

*Falls nicht*, "if not", is a whole condition in two words.
