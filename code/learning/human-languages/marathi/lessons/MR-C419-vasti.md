## You'll want to know: वस्ती
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C419-VASTI419-01]; assesses=[] -->

**वस्ती** — *vastī* — "a settlement; a neighbourhood".

> डोंगराखाली एक लहान वस्ती आहे.
>
> *ḍoṅgarākhālī ek lahān vastī āhe.*
>
> There is a small settlement at the bottom of the hill.

Feminine.

Also the people who live there: सगळी वस्ती जमली, the whole neighbourhood gathered.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**वस्ती** (*vastī*) belongs with Marathi *vasṇẽ*, "to settle", inherited from Sanskrit *vasati*, "stays, dwells" (CDIAL 11435); how the noun was formed is not settled.
