## You'll want to know: বিমা
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C535-SURAKSHA535-02]; assesses=[] -->

**বিমা** — *bimā* — "insurance".

> গাড়ির বিমা থাকলে দুর্ঘটনার পরে কোম্পানি খরচ দেবে।
>
> *Gāṛir bimā thākle durghôṭônār pôre kompāni khôrôch debe.*
>
> If the car is insured, the company will pay the costs after an accident.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**বিমা** (*bimā*) is usually traced to Persian *bīma*, built on *bīm*, "fear": cover against what you fear.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
