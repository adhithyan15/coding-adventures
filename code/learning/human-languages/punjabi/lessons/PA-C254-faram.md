# ਫ਼ਾਰਮ (fāram) — a form to fill in

## You'll want to know: ਫ਼ਾਰਮ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C254-FORM254-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ਫ਼ਾਰਮ** — *fāram* — "a form to fill in".

> ਬੈਂਕ ਵਿੱਚ ਇਹ ਫ਼ਾਰਮ ਭਰੋ।
>
> *baiṅk vich eh fāram bharo.*
>
> Fill in this form at the bank.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਫ਼ਾਰਮ** (*fāram*) is borrowed from English *form*.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for reading notices and messages.
