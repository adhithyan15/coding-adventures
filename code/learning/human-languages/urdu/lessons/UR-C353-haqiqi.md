## You'll want to know: حقیقی
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C353-NAZARIYA353-03]; assesses=[] -->

**حقیقی** — *haqīqī* — "real; actual".

> اس کی خوشی حقیقی تھی۔
>
> *us kī khushī haqīqī thī.*
>
> Her happiness was real.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**حقیقی** (*haqīqī*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *ḥaqīqī*, "true, real".
