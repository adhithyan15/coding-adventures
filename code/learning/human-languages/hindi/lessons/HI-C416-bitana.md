## You'll want to know: बिताना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C416-KHEMA416-05]; assesses=[] -->

**बिताना** — *bitānā* — "to pass (time)".

> हमने छुट्टियाँ गाँव में बिताईं।
>
> *hamne chuṭṭiyā̃ gā̃v meṁ bitāīṁ.*
>
> We spent the holidays in the village.

Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने बचपन गाँव में बिताया, *usne bacpan gā̃v meṁ bitāyā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**बिताना** (*bitānā*) is the causative partner of बीतना, "to pass (of time)", from Sanskrit *vṛtta*, "turned; passed" (CDIAL 12069). गुज़ारना and बिताना say the same thing; बिताना is the plainer, warmer word.
