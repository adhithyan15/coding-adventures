## You'll want to know: ಉಪಹಾರ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C464-UPAHARA464-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ಉಪಹಾರ** — *upahāra* — "breakfast; a light meal".

> ಹೋಟೆಲ್ನಲ್ಲಿ ಉಪಹಾರಕ್ಕೆ ಇಡ್ಲಿ ಮತ್ತು ದೋಸೆ ಇತ್ತು.
>
> *hōṭelnalli upahārakke iḍli mattu dōse ittu.*
>
> At the hotel there were idlis and dosas for breakfast.

ಬೆಳಗಿನ ಉಪಹಾರ, morning breakfast; ತಿಂಡಿ is the everyday word.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಉಪಹಾರ** (*upahāra*) is borrowed from Sanskrit: Monier-Williams gives *upahāra* as "an offering, a present". The sense of a light meal is a later Indian one.
