## You'll want to know: дистанция
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C512-RACE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**дистанция** (*distántsiya*) — "a distance (in a race)".

> Она бегает на длинные дистанции.
>
> *Oná bégayet na dlínnyye distántsii.*
>
> She runs long-distance races.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*дистанция* was borrowed from Latin *distantia* 'distance' (EtymDB), from *distāre* 'to stand apart' (Lewis & Short).
