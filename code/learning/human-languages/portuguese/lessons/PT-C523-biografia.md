## You'll want to know: a biografia
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C523-LIVROS-03]; assesses=[] -->

**a biografia** — "biography".

> Estou a ler uma biografia de um pintor famoso.
>
> *I am reading a biography of a famous painter.*

*Autobiografia* — a life written by its subject.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*biografia* is a learned word built from Greek *bíos* "life" and *gráphein* "to write".
