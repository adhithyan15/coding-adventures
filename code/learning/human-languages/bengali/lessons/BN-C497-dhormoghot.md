## You'll want to know: ধর্মঘট
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C497-ISTOPHA497-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ধর্মঘট** — *dhôrmôghôṭ* — "a strike by workers".

> আজ বাসের ধর্মঘট, তাই আমি হেঁটে অফিসে গেলাম।
>
> *Āj bāser dhôrmôghôṭ, tāi āmi hẽṭe ôphise gelām.*
>
> There's a bus strike today, so I walked to the office.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ধর্মঘট** (*dhôrmôghôṭ*) joins Sanskrit *dharma*, "what is established, duty" (Monier-Williams), and *ghaṭa*, "a water pot" (CDIAL 4406); how the pair came to mean a strike is not settled.
