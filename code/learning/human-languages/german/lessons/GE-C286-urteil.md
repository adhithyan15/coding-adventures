## You'll want to know: das Urteil
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C286-URTEIL-01]; assesses=[] -->

**das Urteil** — "the verdict, the judgement".

> Das Urteil kam am Freitag.
>
> *The verdict came on Friday.*

Plural: *die Urteile*. *Ein Urteil fällen* — to pass judgement.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Urteil* goes back to Proto-Germanic *uzdailiją* "judgement" (EtymDB), from *uz-* "out" and the root of *Teil* "part": what is dealt out. English *ordeal* is a cousin.
