## You'll want to know: o enterro
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C315-FUNERAL-03]; assesses=[] -->

**o enterro** — "the burial".

> Depois da missa, houve o enterro.
>
> *After the mass came the burial.*

Built on *enterrar*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*enterro* is built on *enterrar* "to bury" (EtymDB), from Vulgar Latin *interrāre* "to put in the earth", from *terra*.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
