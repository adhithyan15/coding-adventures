## You'll want to know: растапливать
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C335-OSTYVAT-04]; assesses=[] -->

**растапливать** (*rastáplivat*) — "to melt (butter); to light (a stove)".

> Она растопила масло в кастрюле.
>
> *Oná rastopíla máslo v kastryúle.*
>
> She melted the butter in a saucepan.

Imperfective; perfective *растопить* (*rastopít*). Also *растопить печь* — to light a stove.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*растапливать* is built on *рас-* and *топить* "to heat, to melt", inherited from Proto-Slavic *topiti* "to heat" (EtymDB), the root of *тёплый* "warm".
