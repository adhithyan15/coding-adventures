Hindi, chapter 515: Something Has Come Loose.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ढीला (ḍhīlā) — loose; slack.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for to polish, to make shine, then the Hindi for to melt (something).

You'll want to know: ढीला.
ढीला — ḍhīlā — "loose; slack".
यह कमीज़ मुझे बहुत ढीली है। Yah kamīz mujhe bahut ḍhīlī hai. This shirt is very loose on me.

The word, taken apart.
ढीला (ḍhīlā) is a tadbhava, inherited from ḍhila, "slack", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 5590).

Lesson 4 of 5.
कसना (kasnā) — to tighten.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a screw, then the Hindi for a screwdriver.

You'll want to know: कसना.
कसना — kasnā — "to tighten".
अरुण ने पेचकस से सब पेंच कस दिए। Aruṇ ne peckas se sab peñc kas diye. Arun tightened all the screws with the screwdriver.
Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने पेंच कसा, usne peñc kasā.

The word, taken apart.
कसना (kasnā) is a tadbhava, inherited from Sanskrit karṣati, "draws, pulls" (CDIAL 2908). CDIAL gives the Hindi verb as "to tighten".

Lesson 5 of 5.

You'll want to know: खड़खड़ाना.
खड़खड़ाना — khaṛkhaṛānā — "to rattle".
हवा में पुरानी खिड़की खड़खड़ा रही है। Havā meṁ purānī khiṛkī khaṛkhaṛā rahī hai. The old window is rattling in the wind.
Past (it …): वह दरवाज़ा खड़खड़ाया, vah darvāzā khaṛkhaṛāyā.

The word, taken apart.
खड़खड़ाना (khaṛkhaṛānā) is a tadbhava, inherited from Sanskrit khaṭakhaṭāyatē, "crackles", a sound word (CDIAL 3771). CDIAL gives the Hindi verb as "to make a noise with".
