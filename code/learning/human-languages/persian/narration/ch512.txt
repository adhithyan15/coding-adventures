Persian, chapter 512: Teams and Fans.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
هوادار (havâdâr) — a fan, a supporter.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for walking, then the Persian for rowing.

You'll want to know: هوادار.
هوادار (havâdâr) — "a fan, a supporter".
هواداران تیم تا آخر بازی ماندند. havâdârân-e tim tâ âkhar-e bâzi mândand. The team's fans stayed until the end of the match.
havâ 'desire, liking' + dâr 'having'. Plural havâdârân.

The word, taken apart.
havâdâr is built in Persian on havâ, 'desire, liking', a loan usually traced to Arabic, and dâr, the stem of native dâshtan, 'to have'. EtymDB has no entry for it.

ورزشکار (varzeshkâr) — "an athlete, a sportsperson".
او ورزشکار است و هر روز تمرین میکند. u varzeshkâr ast va har ruz tamrin mikonad. She's an athlete and trains every day.
varzesh 'sport' + -kâr 'one who does'.

The word, taken apart.
varzeshkâr is built on native Persian parts: varzesh, 'sport', from the verb varzidan, 'to practise', and kâr, 'work', which EtymDB traces to Middle Persian (EtymDB).

Lesson 3 of 5.
حریف (harif) — an opponent, a rival.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a fan, then the Persian for an athlete.

You'll want to know: حریف.
حریف (harif) — "an opponent, a rival".
حریف ما تیم خیلی قوی بود. harif-e mâ tim-e kheyli qavi bud. Our opponent was a very strong team.
In speech: harifash nemisham — I'm no match for him.

The word, taken apart.
harif is a loan, usually traced to Arabic ḥarīf, 'a fellow in the same trade'; Persian shifted it to 'rival'. EtymDB lists it without a source.

You'll want to know: لیگ.
لیگ (lig) — "a league".
تیم ما در لیگ اول شد. tim-e mâ dar lig avval shod. Our team came first in the league.
lig-e bartar — the top league.

The word, taken apart.
lig is a modern loan, usually traced to French ligue or English league. EtymDB has no entry for the Persian word.

Lesson 5 of 5.
امتیاز (emtiyâz) — a point (in a score).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for an opponent, then the Persian for a league.

You'll want to know: امتیاز.
امتیاز (emtiyâz) — "a point (in a score)".
تیم ما سه امتیاز گرفت. tim-e mâ se emtiyâz gereft. Our team got three points.
Plural emtiyâzât. It also means 'privilege, advantage'.

The word, taken apart.
emtiyâz was borrowed from Arabic imtiyāz (EtymDB), a word for distinction and privilege; Persian also uses it for a point in a game.
