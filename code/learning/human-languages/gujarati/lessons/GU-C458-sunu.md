## You'll want to know: સૂનું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C458-VIRAH458-03]; assesses=[] -->

**સૂનું** — *sūnũ* — "deserted; empty and silent".

> દીકરીના લગ્ન પછી ઘર સૂનું લાગે છે.
>
> *dīkrīnā lagna pachī ghar sūnũ lāge che.*
>
> After the daughter's wedding the house feels empty.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**સૂનું** (*sūnũ*) is inherited from Sanskrit *śūnya*, "empty"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati word (CDIAL 12567).
