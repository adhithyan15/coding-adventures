## You'll want to know: der Reichtum
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C359-GERECHTIGKEIT-04]; assesses=[] -->

**der Reichtum** — "wealth, riches".

> Sein Reichtum hat ihn nicht glücklich gemacht.
>
> *His wealth did not make him happy.*

Plural: *die Reichtümer*. From *reich* — rich.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Reichtum* is inherited from Middle High German *rīchtuom* and Old High German *rīhhituom* (EtymDB), built on *reich* "rich" with *-tum*; EtymDB lists Dutch *rijkdom* and English *richdom* as cousins.
