## You'll want to know: てんき
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C207-WEATHER207-01]; assesses=[] -->

**てんき** — *tenki* — "the weather; fine weather".

> **あしたの てんきは いいでしょう。** — *ashita no tenki wa ii deshō* — "The weather will probably be good tomorrow."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**てんき** (*tenki*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *ten*, "heaven, sky" (Middle Chinese *then*) and *ki*, "air, breath" (*khj+jH*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
