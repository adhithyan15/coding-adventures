## You'll want to know: la advertencia
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C585-PROPONE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**la advertencia** — "the warning".

> Fue una advertencia seria.
>
> *It was a serious warning.*

*Hacer una advertencia* — to give a warning.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*advertencia* is built on *advertir*, inherited from Latin *advertere* 'to turn towards' (EtymDB).

## What you've built
