## You'll want to know: از این رو
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C371-DALIL-03]; assesses=[] -->

**از این رو** (*az in ru*) — "hence, for this reason".

> قیمتها بالا رفت، از این رو مردم کمتر خرید کردند.
>
> *qeymathâ bâlâ raft, az in ru mardom kamtar kharid kardand.*
>
> Prices went up; hence people bought less.

Formal, mostly written. *Az* "from" + *in* "this" + *ru* "face, side".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**از این رو** is native Persian, literally "from this face": *az* "from" (EtymDB traces it to Old Persian), *in* "this" and *ru* "face, side".
