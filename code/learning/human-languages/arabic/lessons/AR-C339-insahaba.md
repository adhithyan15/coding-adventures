## You'll want to know: انسحب
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C339-GHAZW339-05]; assesses=[] -->

**انسحب** — *insaḥaba* — "to withdraw, to pull out".

> انسحب اللاعب من المباراة.
>
> *insaḥaba al-lāʿib min al-mubārāh.*
>
> The player pulled out of the match.

Present: ينسحب, *yansaḥib*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**انسحب** (*insaḥaba*) is built on the native Arabic root *s-ḥ-b* (Lane), "to drag along", the root of the taught *saḥaba*. It is Form VII, "to be drawn back".
