## You'll want to know: ಅಂಶ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C384-GRAHISU384-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ಅಂಶ** — *aṃśa* — "an aspect; a point".

> ಈ ಪಾಠದಲ್ಲಿ ಮೂರು ಮುಖ್ಯ ಅಂಶಗಳು ಇವೆ.
>
> *ī pāṭhadalli mūru mukhya aṃśagaḷu ive.*
>
> This lesson has three main points.

Plural: ಅಂಶಗಳು, *aṃśagaḷu*.

Also a share or a part.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಅಂಶ** (*aṃśa*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *aṃśa*, "a share, a portion, a part" (Monier-Williams).
