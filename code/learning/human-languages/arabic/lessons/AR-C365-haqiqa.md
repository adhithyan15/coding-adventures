## You'll want to know: حقيقة
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C365-HAQIQA365-01]; assesses=[] -->

**حقيقة** — *ḥaqīqa* — "a fact".

> هذه حقيقة لا يعرفها كثير من الناس.
>
> *hādhihi ḥaqīqa lā yaʿrifuhā kathīr min an-nās.*
>
> This is a fact that many people do not know.

في الحقيقة, *fī al-ḥaqīqa*, in fact.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**حقيقة** (*ḥaqīqa*) is built on the native Arabic root *ḥ-q-q*, "to be true, to be due" (Lane). A *ḥaqīqa* is what is truly so: a fact, the truth.
