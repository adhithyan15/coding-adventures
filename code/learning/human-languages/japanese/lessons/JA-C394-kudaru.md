## You'll want to know: くだる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C394-SAKAMICHI394-04]; assesses=[] -->

**くだる** — *kudaru* — "to descend; to go down (a slope or river)".

> **さかみちを くだって、かわまで いきました。** — *sakamichi o kudatte, kawa made ikimashita* — "I went down the hill road as far as the river."

Polite **くだります**, *kudarimasu*; a godan verb. Past (polite): **ふねが かわを くだりました。**, *fune ga kawa o kudarimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**くだる** (*kudaru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict).
