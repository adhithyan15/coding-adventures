## You'll want to know: उजाड़
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C226-DUNE226-05]; assesses=[] -->

**उजाड़** — *ujāṛ* — "laid waste; desolate".

> वो गांव उजाड़ है।
>
> *vo gā̃v ujāṛ hai.*
>
> That village is deserted.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**उजाड़** (*ujāṛ*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*ujjaṭa*, "uprooted, laid waste"; CDIAL lists Old Marwari *ujāṛi*, "a deserted place" (CDIAL 1660b).
