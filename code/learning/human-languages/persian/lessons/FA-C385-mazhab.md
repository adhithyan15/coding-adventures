## You'll want to know: مذهب
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C385-JAMEE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**مذهب** (*mazhab*) — "a religion, a faith".

> در این شهر مردم مذهبهای مختلفی دارند.
>
> *dar in shahr mardom mazhabhâ-ye mokhtalefi dârand.*
>
> In this city people follow different religions.

Plural *mazâheb*, an Arabic broken plural. Also a branch within a religion.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**مذهب** is usually traced to Arabic *madhhab* "a way to go, a school of thought"; Persian says the Arabic *dh* as *z*.
