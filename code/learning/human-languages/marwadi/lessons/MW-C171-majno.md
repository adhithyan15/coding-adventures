## You'll want to know: मांजणो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C171-BASAN171-03]; assesses=[] -->

**मांजणो** — *mā̃jṇo* — "to scrub (pots)".

> हूं आथण बासण मांजूं हूं।
>
> *hū̃ āthaṇ bāsaṇ mā̃jū̃ hū̃.*
>
> I scrub the pots in the evening.

Past (I …): म्हैं बासण मांज्या, *mhaĩ bāsaṇ mā̃jyā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**मांजणो** (*mā̃jṇo*) is inherited from Sanskrit *mārjati*, "rubs clean, wipes off"; CDIAL lists Hindi *mā̃jnā* and Gujarati *mā̃jvũ* (CDIAL 10080). CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry.
