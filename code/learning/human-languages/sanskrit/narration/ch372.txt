Sanskrit, chapter 372: What Stands in the Way.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
विघ्नः (vighnaḥ) — an obstacle.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a time limit, then the Sanskrit for sets about.

You'll want to know: विघ्नः.
विघ्नः — vighnaḥ — "an obstacle".
यदि विघ्नः आगच्छति, तर्हि धैर्यं न त्यजतु। yadi vighnaḥ āgacchati, tarhi dhairyaṃ na tyajatu. If an obstacle comes, do not lose heart.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
विघ्नः (vighnaḥ) is built on vi and the root √han, "to strike"; MW gives vi-ghna "an obstacle, an impediment, a hindrance" (MW).

You'll want to know: बाधा.
बाधा — bādhā — "a hindrance; harm".
यदि मार्गे बाधा अस्ति, तर्हि अन्यं मार्गं गच्छतु। yadi mārge bādhā asti, tarhi anyaṃ mārgaṃ gacchatu. If there is a hindrance on the road, take another road.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
बाधा (bādhā) comes from the root √bādh, "to press, to harass"; MW gives bādha "annoyance, obstacle, trouble" and the feminine bādhā "injury, hurt" (MW).

Lesson 4 of 5.
विलम्बयति (vilambayati) — delays (something); holds up.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a hindrance, then the Sanskrit for hinders.

You'll want to know: विलम्बयति.
विलम्बयति — vilambayati — "delays (something); holds up".
यदि वर्षा कार्यं विलम्बयति, तर्हि अहं श्वः करिष्यामि। yadi varṣā kāryaṃ vilambayati, tarhi ahaṃ śvaḥ kariṣyāmi. If the rain holds up the work, I will finish tomorrow.
Past (it …): वर्षा यात्रां विलम्बितवती, varṣā yātrāṃ vilambitavatī.

The word, taken apart.
विलम्बयति (vilambayati) is built on vi and the root √lamb, "to hang", in its causative form; MW gives it the senses "to cause to linger, to detain, to delay" (MW).

Lesson 5 of 5.
अपनयति (apanayati) — removes; takes away.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for hinders, then the Sanskrit for holds up.

You'll want to know: अपनयति.
अपनयति — apanayati — "removes; takes away".
यदि मार्गे पाषाणः अस्ति, तर्हि तम् अपनयतु। yadi mārge pāṣāṇaḥ asti, tarhi tam apanayatu. If there is a stone on the road, remove it.
Past (he …): सः पाषाणम् अपनीतवान्, saḥ pāṣāṇam apanītavān.

The word, taken apart.
अपनयति (apanayati) is built on apa, "away", and the root √nī, "to lead"; MW gives apa-nīta "led away from" and the verb the sense "to take away, to remove" (MW).
