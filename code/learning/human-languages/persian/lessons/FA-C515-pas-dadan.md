## You'll want to know: پاس دادن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C515-MOVES-02]; assesses=[] -->

**پاس دادن** (*pâs dâdan*) — "to pass (the ball)".

> او توپ را به دوستش پاس داد.
>
> *u tup râ be dustash pâs dâd.*
>
> He passed the ball to his friend.

*pâs* 'a pass' + *dâdan* 'to give'; present stem *deh-*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*pâs* was borrowed from English *pass* (EtymDB). *dâdan*, 'to give', is inherited Persian.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
