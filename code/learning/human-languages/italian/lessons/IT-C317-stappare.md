## You'll want to know: stappare
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C317-SPUMANTE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**stappare** — "to uncork, to open (a bottle)".

> Chi stappa la bottiglia?
>
> *Who is opening the bottle?*

The opposite of *tappare*, to stop up.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*stappare* is *s-* "un-" with *tappo* "cork", from Frankish *tappo* (EtymDB).
