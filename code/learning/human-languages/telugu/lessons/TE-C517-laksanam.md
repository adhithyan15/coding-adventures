# లక్షణం (lakṣaṇaṁ) — a symptom; a trait

## You'll want to know: లక్షణం
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C517-LAKSANAM517-01]; assesses=[] -->

**లక్షణం** — *lakṣaṇaṁ* — "a symptom; a trait".

> జ్వరం, దగ్గు ఆ జబ్బు లక్షణాలు.
>
> *jvaraṁ, daggu ā jabbu lakṣaṇālu.*
>
> Fever and cough are the symptoms of that illness.

Plural: లక్షణాలు, *lakṣaṇālu*.

Also a person's traits: మంచి లక్షణాలు, good qualities.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**లక్షణం** (*lakṣaṇaṁ*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *lakṣaṇa*, "line, mark" (CDIAL 10882): the marks by which an illness or a person is known.
