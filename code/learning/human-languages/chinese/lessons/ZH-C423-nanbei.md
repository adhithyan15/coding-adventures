## You'll want to know: 南北
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C423-SOUTH423-05]; assesses=[] -->

**南北** — *nánběi* — "north and south".

> *Zhōngguó de nánběi hěn bù yíyàng* — The north and the south of China are very different.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**南北** (*nánběi*) is built from 南 'south' and 北 'north', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *nom* and *pok* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription).
