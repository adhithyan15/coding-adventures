Gujarati, chapter 83: Fifty to a Thousand, and How Much.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
પચાસ (pacās) — fifty.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for thirty, then the Gujarati for forty.

You'll want to know: પચાસ.
પચાસ (pacās) — "fifty".

What you've built.
One more word for fifty to a thousand, and how much.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: pacās]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: pacās, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say pacās]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Gujarati for thirty, then the Gujarati for forty, then say pacās again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does પચાસ (pacās) mean? ("Fifty".) Say it once more.
