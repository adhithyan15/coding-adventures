## You'll want to know: क्षीणः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C303-DURBHIKSHA303-03]; assesses=[] -->

**क्षीणः** — *kṣīṇaḥ* — "wasted away; weakened".

> क्षुधया बालाः क्षीणाः सन्ति।
>
> *kṣudhayā bālāḥ kṣīṇāḥ santi.*
>
> The children are wasted with hunger.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**क्षीणः** (*kṣīṇaḥ*) comes from the root √kṣi, "to waste, destroy"; MW gives *kṣīṇa* as diminished, wasted, worn away, emaciated (MW).
