## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**সপ্তাহ** (*sôptāhô*) is Sanskrit *saptāha*, "period of seven days", taken over whole as a learned loan, a tatsama. It is *sapta*, "seven", + *aha*, "day".
