# Review — advice, and why

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[FA-GRAMMAR-SUBJUNCTIVE-BE-01, FA-GRAMMAR-MIKHAHAM-SUBJ-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "I want to go", then "you should go". (*Mikhâham beravam*,
*bâyad beravi*.)

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[FA-GRAMMAR-SUBJUNCTIVE-BE-01, FA-GRAMMAR-BAYAD-SUBJ-01, FA-GRAMMAR-BEHTAR-AST-SUBJ-01, FA-GRAMMAR-DALIL-IN-AST-KE-01, FA-GRAMMAR-CAUSE-NOUN-OR-CLAUSE-01, FA-LEX-C293-TOWZIH-DADAN-01] -->

Four lessons, and one new verb shape doing a great deal of work. *Be-* in
place of *mi-* gives the subjunctive, and it follows *mikhâham*, *bâyad* and
*behtar ast*: a thing wanted, needed or advised. The reason comes after, with
*chon* and a sentence, *be khâter-e* and a noun, or *dalil in ast ke* when
it needs a sentence of its own. *Towzih dâdan* names what you are doing.

[PAUSE 2s] One piece of advice, built the whole way:

> **به نظر من بهتر است زود بخوابی. دلیل این است که فردا باید زود بروی.**
>
> *be nazar-e man behtar ast zud bekhâbi. dalil in ast ke fardâ bâyad zud
> beravi.*
>
> If you ask me, you'd better sleep early. The reason is that tomorrow you
> have to leave early.
