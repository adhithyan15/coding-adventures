રજા (rajā) — "a holiday, leave".

The word, taken apart.
રજા (rajā) is a loan, usually traced through Persian razā, "consent, leave", to Arabic riḍā, "contentment, approval". Its Arabic root is r-ḍ-w (Lane).

Lesson 4 of 5.
તહેવાર (tahevār) — a festival.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a minute, then the Gujarati for a holiday.

You'll want to know: તહેવાર.
તહેવાર (tahevār) — "a festival".

The word, taken apart.
તહેવાર (tahevār) is a tadbhava, a word inherited and reshaped by sound change. It comes from tithivāra, "a festival", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 5811). It is tithi, "lunar day", and vāra, "day, turn".

Lesson 5 of 5.

You'll want to know: હોળી.
હોળી (hoḷī) — "Holi, the festival of colours". A તહેવાર (tahevār) of spring.

The word, taken apart.
હોળી (hoḷī) is a tadbhava of Sanskrit holā, "spring festival", also written holī (CDIAL 14182).
