## You'll want to know: تعمق
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C394-ITQAN394-03]; assesses=[] -->

**تعمق** — *taʿammaqa* — "to go deeper".

> تعمق الطالب في دراسة التاريخ.
>
> *taʿammaqa aṭ-ṭālib fī dirāsat at-tārīkh.*
>
> The student went deeper into the study of history.

Present: يتعمق, *yataʿammaq*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**تعمق** (*taʿammaqa*) is built on the native Arabic root *ʿ-m-q*, "to be deep" (Lane). It is Form V, to go deep into a thing. عميق, deep, is from the same root.
