Persian, chapter 257: To miss, To grow up, To feel, To promise, To complain.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
دلتنگ شدن (deltang shodan) — to miss (someone).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to travel, then the Persian for to retire.

You'll want to know: دلتنگ شدن.
دلتنگ شدن — deltang shodan — "to miss (someone)".

The word, taken apart.
deltang shodan is "to become narrow-hearted": del, "heart" (inherited, EtymDB), with tang, "narrow", and shodan. All are native Persian. shodan, "to become", is an inherited verb; it is usually traced to Middle Persian šudan, "to go".

Lesson 3 of 5.
احساس کردن (ehsâs kardan) — to feel.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to miss, then the Persian for to grow up.

You'll want to know: احساس کردن.
احساس کردن — ehsâs kardan — "to feel".

The word, taken apart.
ehsâs kardan pairs ehsâs, borrowed from Arabic iḥsās, "perceiving, feeling" (root ḥ-s-s, Lane), with the Persian verb kardan, "to do, to make". kardan, "to do", is an inherited verb, from Middle Persian kardan (EtymDB).

You'll want to know: قول دادن.
قول دادن — qol dâdan — "to promise".

The word, taken apart.
qol dâdan pairs qol, borrowed from Arabic qawl, "saying, word" (root q-w-l, Lane), with the Persian verb dâdan, "to give". dâdan, "to give", is an inherited verb; EtymDB takes it back to the Indo-European root deh₃-, "to give".
