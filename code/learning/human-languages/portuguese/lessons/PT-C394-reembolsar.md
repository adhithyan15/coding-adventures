## You'll want to know: reembolsar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C394-GARANTIA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**reembolsar** — "to refund, to pay back".

> A loja reembolsou-me o dinheiro.
>
> *The shop refunded my money.*

*Ser reembolsado* — to get your money back.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*reembolsar* is built from *re-* and *embolsar* "to pocket", on *bolsa* "purse", from Late Latin *bursa* "oxhide, purse" (EtymDB).
