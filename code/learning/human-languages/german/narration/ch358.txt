German, chapter 358: Die Gesellschaft.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
die Gesellschaft — society; company; the firm.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to support, then the German for the support.

You'll want to know: die Gesellschaft.
die Gesellschaft — "society; company; the firm".
In unserer Gesellschaft leben immer mehr alte Menschen. More and more old people live in our society.
Plural: die Gesellschaften. Also company, being with others: in guter Gesellschaft; and a firm, as in GmbH.

The word, taken apart.
Gesellschaft is inherited from Middle High German gesellescaft and Old High German giselliscaft (EtymDB), built on Geselle "companion", usually explained as first someone who shared your hall, with -schaft.

Lesson 2 of 5.
die Demokratie — democracy.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for the support, then the German for society.

You'll want to know: die Demokratie.
die Demokratie — "democracy".
In einer Demokratie wählen die Bürger das Parlament. In a democracy the citizens elect parliament.
Plural: die Demokratien. The stress is on the last syllable: de-mo-kra-TIE.

The word, taken apart.
Demokratie was borrowed from French démocratie (EtymDB), from Latin dēmocratia and Greek dēmokratía, built from dêmos "the common people" and krátos "strength, power".

Lesson 3 of 5.
abstimmen — to vote (on); to coordinate.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for society, then the German for democracy.

You'll want to know: abstimmen.
abstimmen — "to vote (on); to coordinate".
Am Sonntag stimmen die Bürger über den neuen Bahnhof ab. On Sunday the citizens vote on the new station.
Separable: sie stimmen ab, sie haben abgestimmt. Abstimmen über + accusative. Termine abstimmen — to coordinate dates.

The word, taken apart.
abstimmen is built from ab and stimmen, a verb made from Stimme "voice, vote" (EtymDB): you give your vote.

Lesson 4 of 5.
die Abstimmung — the vote, the ballot.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for democracy, then the German for to vote.

You'll want to know: die Abstimmung.
die Abstimmung — "the vote, the ballot".
Das Ergebnis der Abstimmung war sehr knapp. The result of the vote was very close.
Plural: die Abstimmungen. In Switzerland die Volksabstimmung, a vote of the whole people, takes place several times a year.

The word, taken apart.
Abstimmung is the noun of abstimmen, formed with -ung; the verb is made from Stimme "voice, vote" (EtymDB).

Lesson 5 of 5.
beitragen — to contribute.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to vote, then the German for the vote.

You'll want to know: beitragen.
beitragen — "to contribute".
Jeder kann etwas zu einem schönen Fest beitragen. Everyone can contribute something to a nice party.
Separable and strong: er trägt bei, er hat beigetragen. Der Beitrag — the contribution; also a fee or an article.

The word, taken apart.
beitragen is built from bei "by, to" and tragen "to carry", from Proto-Germanic draganą (EtymDB): you carry your share to the pile.
