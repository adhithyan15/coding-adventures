## You'll want to know: えいがかん
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C193-SIGHT193-03]; assesses=[] -->

**えいがかん** — *eigakan* — "cinema; movie theatre".

> **ともだちと えいがかんに いきます。** — *tomodachi to eigakan ni ikimasu* — "I go to the cinema with a friend."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**えいがかん** (*eigakan*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): three Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *ei*, "reflect" (Middle Chinese *'jaengH*), *ga*, "picture" (*hweaH*) and *kan*, "building" (*kwanH*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
