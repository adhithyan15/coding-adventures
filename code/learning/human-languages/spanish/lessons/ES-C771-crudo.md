## You'll want to know: crudo
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C771-SABOR-04]; assesses=[] -->

**crudo** — "raw; uncooked".

> No como sushi porque no me gusta el pescado crudo.
>
> *I don't eat sushi because I don't like raw fish.*

*El petróleo crudo* — crude oil. *Lo tiene crudo* (Spain, informal) — he's got it tough.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*crudo* is inherited from Latin *crūdus* 'raw, uncooked, bloody' (EtymDB), through old Spanish *crudo*.
