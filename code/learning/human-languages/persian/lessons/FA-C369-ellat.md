## You'll want to know: علت
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C369-ELLAT-01]; assesses=[] -->

**علت** (*'ellat*) — "a cause, a reason".

> علت بیماری او هنوز معلوم نیست.
>
> *'ellat-e bimâri-ye u hanuz ma'lum nist.*
>
> The cause of his illness is still not known.

Plural *'elal*, an Arabic broken plural. *Be 'ellat-e* — because of.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**علت** comes from Arabic *'illa* (EtymDB), a word for both a cause and an illness; in Persian it means the cause or reason for something.
