## You'll want to know: سسرال
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C488-INLAWS488-02]; assesses=[] -->

**سسرال** — *susrāl* — "the in-laws' home".

> شادی کے بعد وہ سسرال گئی۔
>
> *shādī ke bād voh susrāl gaī.*
>
> After the wedding she went to her in-laws' home.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**سسرال** (*susrāl*) is inherited from *śvaśuraśālā*, "the father-in-law's house", a form CDIAL reconstructs (CDIAL 12755); **سسر**, "a father-in-law", is its first part.
