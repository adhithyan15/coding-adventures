## You'll want to know: तूणीरः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C325-GADA325-03]; assesses=[] -->

**तूणीरः** — *tūṇīraḥ* — "a quiver".

> वीरस्य तूणीरे बाणाः सन्ति।
>
> *vīrasya tūṇīre bāṇāḥ santi.*
>
> There are arrows in the hero's quiver.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**तूणीरः** (*tūṇīraḥ*) MW gives *tūṇīra* as the same as *tūṇa*, a quiver, without a further derivation, so its origin is uncertain (MW).
