Gujarati, chapter 398: Dozing on the Train.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ઝોકું (jhokũ) — a nap; a nod of the head in sleep.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to shove, then the Gujarati for to fill up.

You'll want to know: ઝોકું.
ઝોકું — jhokũ — "a nap; a nod of the head in sleep".
બપોરે દાદાને ટ્રેનમાં ઝોકું આવ્યું. bapore dādāne ṭrenmā̃ jhokũ āvyũ. In the afternoon grandfather nodded off on the train.
A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
ઝોકું (jhokũ) seems to belong with the Gujarati verb jhukvũ, "to bend", which CDIAL lists under jhukkati, "stoops", a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 5399): the head bends forward as you doze. CDIAL gives no form for ઝોકું itself, so the link is hedged.

Lesson 2 of 5.
ઉજાગરો (ujāgro) — a sleepless night.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to fill up, then the Gujarati for a nap.

You'll want to know: ઉજાગરો.
ઉજાગરો — ujāgro — "a sleepless night".
આખી રાત ટ્રેનમાં મને ઉજાગરો થયો. ākhī rāt ṭrenmā̃ mane ujāgro thayo. I had a sleepless night on the train.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ઉજાગરો (ujāgro) goes back to ujjāgrat, "awake", a form CDIAL reconstructs; CDIAL lists the Gujarati word, waking through the night (CDIAL 1665).

ટ્રેન ચાલતી હતી ત્યારે બાળકો ઊંઘતાં હતાં. ṭren cāltī hatī tyāre bāḷko ū̃ghtā̃ hatā̃. While the train was moving the children were asleep.
Past (he …): તે આખી રાત ઊંઘ્યો, te ākhī rāt ū̃ghyo.

The word, taken apart.
ઊંઘવું (ū̃ghvũ) goes back to uṅghati, "sleeps", a form CDIAL reconstructs; CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb (CDIAL 1632). ઊંઘ, sleep, is the noun beside it.

ઝટકો (jhaṭko) seems to be built on ઝટ, "quickly, at once", which CDIAL lists for Gujarati under jhaṭ, a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 5327): a sudden quick shake.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ખખડવું (khakhaḍvũ) — to rattle.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to be asleep, then the Gujarati for a jolt.

You'll want to know: ખખડવું.
ખખડવું — khakhaḍvũ — "to rattle".
જૂની બારી આખો દિવસ ખખડતી હતી. jūnī bārī ākho divas khakhaḍtī hatī. The old window rattled all day.
Past (it …): તે બારી ખખડી, te bārī khakhḍī.

The word, taken apart.
ખખડવું (khakhaḍvũ) sounds like the noise it names. CDIAL lists a Gujarati verb of the same kind, khaṛkhaṛvũ, "to rattle", under Sanskrit khaṭakhaṭāyatē, "crackles" (CDIAL 3771), beside Hindi khaṛkhaṛānā and Marathi khaḍkhaḍṇẽ; it gives no form for ખખડવું itself, so the link is hedged.
