# ਪੂੰਝਣਾ (pūnjhṇā) — to wipe

## You'll want to know: ਪੂੰਝਣਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C228-DIN228-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ਪੂੰਝਣਾ** — *pūnjhṇā* — "to wipe".

> ਮੈਂ ਤੌਲੀਏ ਨਾਲ ਮੂੰਹ ਪੂੰਝਦਾ ਹਾਂ।
>
> *maiṁ taulīe nāl mūnh pūnjhdā hāṁ.*
>
> I wipe my face with a towel.

Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਮੇਜ਼ ਪੂੰਝਿਆ, *maiṁ mez pūnjhiā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਪੂੰਝਣਾ** (*pūnjhṇā*) is inherited from Sanskrit *prōñchati*, "wipes off"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *pū̃jhṇā*, "wipes off" (CDIAL 9011).
