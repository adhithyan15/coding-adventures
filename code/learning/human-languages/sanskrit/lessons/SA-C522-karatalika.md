## You'll want to know: करतालिका
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C522-PADANYASA522-05]; assesses=[] -->

**करतालिका** — *karatālikā* — "clapping; applause".

> नृत्यं दृष्ट्वा पिता करतालिकां करोति।
>
> *nṛtyaṃ dṛṣṭvā pitā karatālikāṃ karoti.*
>
> After the dance, father claps.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**करतालिका** (*karatālikā*) is built from *kara*, "the hand", and *tālikā*, "clapping", a word of the same family as *tāla*, "beating time"; MW gives *karatālikā* as clapping the hands and beating time by clapping (MW).
