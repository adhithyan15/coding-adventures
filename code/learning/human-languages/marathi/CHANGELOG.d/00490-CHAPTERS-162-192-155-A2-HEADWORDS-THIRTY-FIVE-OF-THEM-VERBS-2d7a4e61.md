## Chapters 162-192: 155 A2 headwords, thirty-five of them verbs

The first of four Marathi A2 vocabulary tranches. Thirty-one chapters of five
words each, in four runs of at most nine chapters. Each run closes with two
reviews.

- **Chapters 162-168: thirty-five verbs**, many of them compound verbs with
  **करणे**, **घेणे** or **देणे**: *पेटवणे*, *विझवणे*, *आभार मानणे*,
  *तपासणे*, *सही करणे*, *जाहीर करणे*, *ओलांडणे*, *सुधारणे*, *बिघडणे*,
  *भाग घेणे*, *घाबरणे*, *श्वास घेणे*, *सोडून देणे* and *संशय घेणे*.
- **Chapters 169-170: health and feelings** (*आरोग्य*, *मेंदू*, *फुफ्फुस*,
  *लाज*, *कंटाळा*, *संयम*, *नशीब*).
- **Chapter 171: time** (*क्वचित*, *मग*, *शेवटी*, *अजून*, *अचानक*).
- **Chapter 172: place** (*भोवती*, *सगळीकडे*, *इतरत्र*, *तिकडे*,
  *मध्यभागी*).
- **Chapter 173: joining ideas** (*तरीही*, *जरी*, *असूनही*, *दुर्दैवाने*,
  *सुदैवाने*).
- **Chapters 174-177: twenty qualities**, such as *आरामदायक*, *सभ्य*,
  *विचित्र*, *वेगळा / वेगळी*, *शक्य*, *प्रेमळ* and *नम्र*.
- **Chapters 178-192: seventy-five nouns**:
  - the flat (*सदनिका*, *जेवणघर*, *सज्जा*);
  - the courts and elections (*न्यायालय*, *महापौर*, *निवडणूक*);
  - the arts (*लेखक / लेखिका*, *चित्रकार*, *पत्रकार*);
  - weddings (*नवरदेव*, *नवरी*);
  - money (*सवलत*, *नाणे*, *कर्ज*);
  - monuments (*किल्ला*, *स्मारक*, *पुतळा*);
  - the environment (*हवामान*, *पर्यावरण*, *प्रदूषण*);
  - sport (*व्यायामशाळा*, *विजय*, *स्पर्धा*);
  - the Marathi table (*पापड*, *चटणी*, *कोशिंबीर*, *मोदक*);
  - dress (*विजार*, *रुमाल*, *बांगडी*);
  - creatures (*खेकडा*, *गरुड*, *कोकिळा*);
  - the street (*वाहतूक*, *प्रवासी*, *गर्दी*);
  - the office (*व्यवस्थापक*, *माहिती*, *बैठक*, *करार*, *मुलाखत*).
- **Candidate screen.** Every headword is checked against the letters the
  track teaches to write and against earlier lesson bodies. Spelling variants
  of taught words were left out (*हरिण* beside *हरीण*, *खरा* beside *खरे*).
  Glosses are disambiguated against taught near-synonyms: *हरणे* is "to be
  defeated", against *हरवणे*, and *लपणे* is "to take cover", against
  *लपवणे*.
- **Pins:** the Marathi lesson-content budget goes from 867 to 1030, and the
  per-chapter size pin gains chapters 162-192.
- **Level gate at A2:** verb vocabulary 20 short -> 0; vocabulary 554 short
  -> 399. The strict book compile is clean at 1153 pages.
