## You'll want to know: ಪರಾಮರ್ಶಿಸು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C422-MANADANDA422-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ಪರಾಮರ್ಶಿಸು** — *parāmarśisu* — "to refer to; to consult".

> ಉತ್ತರ ಬರೆಯುವ ಮೊದಲು ನಾನು ನಿಘಂಟನ್ನು ಪರಾಮರ್ಶಿಸಿದೆ.
>
> *uttara bareyuva modalu nānu nighaṇṭannu parāmarśiside.*
>
> Before writing the answer I consulted the dictionary.

Past (I …): ಪರಾಮರ್ಶಿಸಿದೆ, *parāmarśiside*.

Of books and records. ಪರಾಮರ್ಶನ ಗ್ರಂಥ, a reference book.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಪರಾಮರ್ಶಿಸು** (*parāmarśisu*) is built on Sanskrit *parāmarśa*, "seizing; consideration" (Monier-Williams), with the Kannada verb ending -*isu*.
