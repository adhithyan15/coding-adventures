## You'll want to know: भीषण
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C295-SANKAT295-05]; assesses=[] -->

**भीषण** — *bhīṣaṇ* — "dreadful; terrible".

> महामार्गावर काल भीषण अपघात झाला.
>
> *mahāmārgāvar kāl bhīṣaṇ apghāt jhālā.*
>
> There was a terrible accident on the highway yesterday.

भीषण keeps one form: भीषण आग, भीषण अपघात.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**भीषण** (*bhīṣaṇ*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *bhīṣaṇa*, 'terrifying' (Monier-Williams).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
