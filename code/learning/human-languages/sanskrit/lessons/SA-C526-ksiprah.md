## You'll want to know: क्षिप्रः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C526-ABHYASYATI526-03]; assesses=[] -->

**क्षिप्रः** — *kṣipraḥ* — "quick".

> सः क्षिप्रः धावकः अस्ति।
>
> *saḥ kṣipraḥ dhāvakaḥ asti.*
>
> He is a quick runner.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**क्षिप्रः** (*kṣipraḥ*) comes from the root √kṣip, "to throw", as MW lists it: springing back like a bow, and so quick, speedy, swift (MW).
