# ઉ — independent u

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-SCRIPT-U-MATRA-01] -->

Write attached short-*u* sign **ુ** once and say “needs a consonant.” Put it
away. The same vowel standing alone needs a whole letter.

## Script — meet one form
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-SCRIPT-INDEPENDENT-U-01]; assesses=[] -->

Keep **ઉ** visible. Circle the small upper bowl to the middle cusp. Continue
around the broad lower bowl, then climb the tall outer curve and finish at the
upper right without lifting.

## Writing — notice and trace
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-SCRIPT-INDEPENDENT-U-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: observe-trace -->

Finger-trace **ઉ** once, then air-trace it once. Notice upper bowl, middle cusp,
lower bowl, outer return; no pen lift.

## Writing — copy with the model
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-SCRIPT-INDEPENDENT-U-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: guided-copy -->

Copy visible **ઉ** once. Compare only the two bowls and outer return; repair
one landmark if needed.

## Writing — recall after hiding
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-SCRIPT-INDEPENDENT-U-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: delayed-copy -->
<!-- hl-activity: {"id":"GU-W06-independent-u-recall","kind":"text","assesses":["GU-SCRIPT-INDEPENDENT-U-01"],"prompt":"Hide the model and write independent short u from memory.","answer":"ઉ","accepted":[],"feedback":{"correct":"ઉ — the two bowls and tall outer return are intact.","incorrect":"Look once at ઉ, hide it, and repair only the differing landmark."},"response_seconds":20} -->

Hide the model and write independent *u* once. Uncover **ઉ**, compare, and stop
after one repair.

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-SCRIPT-INDEPENDENT-U-01, GU-SCRIPT-U-MATRA-01] -->

[PAUSE 3s] Point to the whole vowel in **ઉ ુ**, then point to the attached
sign.
