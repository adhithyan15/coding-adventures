German, chapter 58: Back, Tongue, Lip, Toe, Stomach.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
der Rücken — the back (of the body).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for a hair; hair, then the German for the belly.

You'll want to know: der Rücken.
der Rücken — "the back (of the body)".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: der Rücken]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: der Rücken, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say der Rücken]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the German for a hair; hair, then the German for the belly, then say der Rücken again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does der Rücken mean? ("The back (of the body)".) Say it once more.
