## You'll want to know: bostezar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C761-SERIE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**bostezar** — "to yawn".

> Bostezo mucho cuando tengo sueño.
>
> *I yawn a lot when I am sleepy.*

*El bostezo* — the yawn. Spelling: *bostecé*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*bostezar* is usually traced to Latin *ōscitāre* "to gape, to yawn" (EtymDB); how it gained its *b-* is not clear.
