## You'll want to know: استنتج
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C362-MANTIQ362-04]; assesses=[] -->

**استنتج** — *istantaja* — "to deduce; to conclude".

> استنتجت من كلامه أنه متعب.
>
> *istantajtu min kalāmihi annahu mutʿab.*
>
> I concluded from what he said that he was tired.

Present: يستنتج, *yastantij*.

Followed by أن: استنتج أن …. A related noun is نتيجة, a result.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**استنتج** (*istantaja*) is built on the native Arabic root *n-t-j*, "to bring forth young" (Lane). It is Form X, to seek the outcome: to draw a conclusion.
