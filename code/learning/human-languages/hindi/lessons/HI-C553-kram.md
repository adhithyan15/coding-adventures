## You'll want to know: क्रम
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C553-KRAM553-03]; assesses=[] -->

**क्रम** — *kram* — "order; sequence".

> शब्दों का क्रम बदलिए।
>
> *Śabdoṁ kā kram badliye.*
>
> Change the order of the words.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**क्रम** (*kram*) is a tatsama, taken whole from Sanskrit *krama*, "a step; order, succession" (Monier-Williams).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
