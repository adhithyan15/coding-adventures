## You'll want to know: ہجوم
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C321-MELA321-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ہجوم** — *hujūm* — "a crowd".

> میلے میں بڑا ہجوم تھا۔
>
> *mele meṅ baṛā hujūm thā.*
>
> There was a big crowd at the fair.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ہجوم** (*hujūm*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *hujūm*, "a rushing in, an onset"; in Urdu it is the crowd itself.
