## You'll want to know: o árbitro
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C508-ARBITRO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**o árbitro** — "referee".

> O árbitro mostrou um cartão amarelo.
>
> *The referee showed a yellow card.*

A woman is *a árbitra*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*árbitro* is a learned borrowing from Latin *arbiter* "a judge, an umpire" (EtymDB).
