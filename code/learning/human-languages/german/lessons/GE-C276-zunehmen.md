## You'll want to know: zunehmen
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C276-UMFRAGE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**zunehmen** — "to increase; to put on weight".

> Der Verkehr nahm am Abend zu.
>
> *The traffic increased in the evening.*

Separable: *es nimmt zu*. For a person, to put on weight: *Er hat zugenommen*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*zunehmen* is built on *nehmen* "to take", from Old High German *neman*, Proto-Germanic *nemaną* (EtymDB), with *zu*: to take on more.
