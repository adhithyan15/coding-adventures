# కృశించు (kṛśiñcu) — to waste away; to pine

## Warm-up

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Telugu for discouragement, then the Telugu for to yearn.

## You'll want to know: కృశించు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C502-NIRASA502-05]; assesses=[] -->

**కృశించు** — *kṛśiñcu* — "to waste away; to pine".

> దిగులుతో ఆమె రోజురోజుకీ కృశించింది.
>
> *digulutō āme rōjurōjukī kṛśiñcindi.*
>
> From grief she wasted away day by day.

Past (I …): కృశించాను, *kṛśiñcānu*.

A formal word for growing thin from illness or sorrow.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**కృశించు** (*kṛśiñcu*) is built on Sanskrit *kṛśa*, "lean, emaciated, weak" (Monier-Williams), with the Telugu verb ending -ఇంచు.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
