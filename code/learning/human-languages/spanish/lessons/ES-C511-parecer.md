## You'll want to know: parecer
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C511-HACER-01]; assesses=[] -->

**parecer** — "to seem".

> Parece fácil, pero no lo es.
>
> *It seems easy, but it is not.*

*¿Qué te parece?* — what do you think? It takes a *z* in *parezco*.
