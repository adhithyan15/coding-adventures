## You'll want to know: પાસો
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C533-PASO533-01]; assesses=[] -->

**પાસો** — *pāso* — "a die".

> જો તારો પાસો છ પર પડશે તો તું ફરી રમીશ.
>
> *jo tāro pāso cha par paḍśe to tũ pharī ramīś.*
>
> If your die lands on six, you'll play again.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**પાસો** (*pāso*) goes back to Sanskrit *pāśa*, "a die", and CDIAL lists the Gujarati word (CDIAL 8132).
