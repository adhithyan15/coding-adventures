## You'll want to know: el insecto
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C694-ANIMALES-03]; assesses=[] -->

**el insecto** — "the insect".

> Por la noche hay muchos insectos.
>
> *There are a lot of insects at night.*

*Una picadura de insecto* — an insect bite.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*insecto* is a learned borrowing from Latin *insectum* "cut into" (EtymDB), from *in* and *secāre* "to cut": an insect body looks cut into parts.
