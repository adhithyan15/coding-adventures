## You'll want to know: l'interrogazione
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C382-RIPASSO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**l'interrogazione** — "the oral test (at school); the questioning".

> Se domani c'è l'interrogazione di storia, stasera studio.
>
> *If there is an oral history test tomorrow, I will study tonight.*

At school the teacher questions one pupil aloud in front of the class: *essere interrogato*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*interrogazione* is a learned borrowing from Latin *interrogātiō* "a questioning, an examination" (EtymDB), from *interrogāre* "to question", built from *inter* "between" and *rogāre* "to ask".
