## You'll want to know: la aerolínea
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C652-AEROPUERTO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**la aerolínea** — "the airline".

> La aerolínea nos paga el hotel por el retraso.
>
> *The airline is paying for our hotel because of the delay.*

Also *la compañía aérea*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*aerolínea* is built from *aero-*, from Greek *aḗr* "air", and *línea* "line", a learned borrowing from Latin *līnea* (EtymDB).
