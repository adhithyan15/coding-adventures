## You'll want to know: めいし
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C452-BOSS452-05]; assesses=[] -->

**めいし** — *meishi* — "a business card".

> **かいぎで めいしを わたしました。** — *kaigi de meishi o watashimashita* — "I handed over my business card at the meeting."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**めいし** (*meishi*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *mei*, "name" (Middle Chinese *mjieng*) and *shi*, "calling card" (*tshjeH*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
