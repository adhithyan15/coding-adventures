Persian, chapter 384: Effort, success and strikes.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
اعتصاب (e'tesâb) — a strike.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a rival, then the Persian for advertising.

You'll want to know: اعتصاب.
اعتصاب (e'tesâb) — "a strike".
کارگرها برای حقوق بیشتر اعتصاب کردند. kârgarhâ barâye hoquq-e bishtar e'tesâb kardand. The workers went on strike for higher pay.
E'tesâb kardan — to go on strike. Plural e'tesâbât.

The word, taken apart.
اعتصاب (e'tesâb) was borrowed from Arabic i'tiṣāb (EtymDB), a verbal noun usually explained as banding together; in modern Persian it is a strike.

Lesson 2 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for advertising, then the Persian for a strike.

You'll want to know: اتحادیه.
اتحادیه (ettehâdiye) — "a union, an association".
اتحادیه کارگران با مدیرها مذاکره کرد. ettehâdiye-ye kârgarân bâ modirhâ mozâkere kard. The workers' union negotiated with the managers.
Ettehâd "unity" + -iye. Ettehâdiye-ye kârgari — a trade union.

The word, taken apart.
اتحادیه (ettehâdiye) is built on ettehâd "unity", usually traced to Arabic ittiḥād "becoming one", with the ending -iye.

Lesson 3 of 5.
زحمت کشیدن (zahmat keshidan) — to work hard, to take pains.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a strike, then the Persian for a union.

You'll want to know: زحمت کشیدن.
زحمت کشیدن (zahmat keshidan) — "to work hard, to take pains".
مادرم برای ما خیلی زحمت کشید. mâdaram barâye mâ kheyli zahmat keshid. My mother worked very hard for us.
Zahmat "trouble, effort" + keshidan "to pull" (stem kesh-). A polite thanks: zahmat keshidid, you went to a lot of trouble.

The word, taken apart.
زحمت was borrowed from Arabic zaḥma "pressing, thronging" (EtymDB); with the inherited Persian verb keshidan "to pull, to bear", it means to bear the trouble.

Lesson 4 of 5.
موفق شدن (movaffaq shodan) — to succeed.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a union, then the Persian for to work hard.

You'll want to know: موفق شدن.
موفق شدن (movaffaq shodan) — "to succeed".
بالاخره در امتحان موفق شد. bâlâkhare dar emtehân movaffaq shod. In the end she passed the exam.
Movaffaq "successful" + shodan. Dar chizi movaffaq shodan — to succeed in something.

The word, taken apart.
موفق شدن (movaffaq shodan) joins movaffaq "successful", usually traced to Arabic muwaffaq "granted success", to the inherited Persian verb shodan "to become".

Lesson 5 of 5.
شکست خوردن (shekast khordan) — to be defeated, to lose.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to work hard, then the Persian for to succeed.

You'll want to know: شکست خوردن.
شکست خوردن (shekast khordan) — "to be defeated, to lose".
تیم ما در مسابقه شکست خورد. tim-e mâ dar mosâbeqe shekast khord. Our team was defeated in the match.
Shekast "defeat" + khordan "to eat" (stem khor-): literally to eat a defeat.

The word, taken apart.
شکست خوردن (shekast khordan) is native Persian, built from shekast, the past stem of shekastan "to break", which EtymDB traces to Middle Persian škastan and PIE skeyd-, and khordan "to eat", inherited too.
