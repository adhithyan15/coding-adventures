## You'll want to know: सुधा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C508-NIRMANA508-05]; assesses=[] -->

**सुधा** — *sudhā* — "lime plaster".

> भित्तिः सुधया श्वेता भवति।
>
> *bhittiḥ sudhayā śvetā bhavati.*
>
> The wall turns white with lime plaster.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सुधा** (*sudhā*) is built on *su*, "good", and the root √dhe, "to drink": MW gives *sudhā* first as nectar, the drink of the gods, and also as whitewash, plaster and mortar (MW).
