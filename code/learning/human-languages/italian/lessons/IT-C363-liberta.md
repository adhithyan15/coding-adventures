## You'll want to know: la libertà
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C363-CITTADINO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**la libertà** — "the freedom, the liberty".

> La libertà di parola è importante.
>
> *Freedom of speech is important.*

*In libertà* — free, at large. Stress on the last syllable.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*libertà* is a learned borrowing from Latin *lībertās, lībertātis* "freedom" (Lewis & Short), from *līber* "free", a different word from *liber* "book".
