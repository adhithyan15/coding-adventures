French, chapter 415: En ville sans voiture.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
la station — the metro station, the stop; the taxi rank; the resort.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for the trolley, then the French for the shuttle.

You'll want to know: la station.
la station — "the metro station, the stop; the taxi rank; the resort".
Je descends à la prochaine station de métro. I get off at the next metro stop.
For trains, la gare; for the metro, la station. Une station de ski — a ski resort.

The word, taken apart.
station comes from Old French estation, a learned borrowing from Latin statiō 'a standing, a post', from stāre 'to stand' (EtymDB).

What you've built.

Lesson 2 of 6.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for the shuttle, then the French for the metro station.

You'll want to know: la rame.
la rame — "the metro train, the set of carriages".
La rame de métro est pleine à huit heures. The metro train is full at eight o'clock.
La rame is also an oar; that is a different word.

The word, taken apart.
rame in this sense comes from rame 'ream, stack', borrowed through Catalan raima (EtymDB) from Arabic rizma 'bundle': a row of carriages, like a stack of sheets.

tramway was borrowed from English tramway (EtymDB). English tram 'rail' comes from Middle Dutch trame 'beam'.

You'll want to know: la trottinette.
la trottinette — "the scooter (kick or electric)".
Mon voisin va au bureau en trottinette électrique. My neighbour goes to the office on an electric scooter.
Une trottinette électrique — an e-scooter.

The word, taken apart.
trottinette is built on trottiner 'to trot along' with -ette (EtymDB). Trotter 'to trot' is usually traced to a Germanic word.
