## You'll want to know: generoso
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C472-DIFETTI-03]; assesses=[] -->

**generoso** — "generous".

> È una persona generosa e aiuta sempre gli amici.
>
> *She is a generous person and always helps her friends.*

Of food: *una porzione generosa* — a big helping.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*generoso* is a learned borrowing from Latin *generōsus* "of noble birth, noble-minded" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short), built on *genus* "birth, family".
