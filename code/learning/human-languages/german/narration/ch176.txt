German, chapter 176: To mean, To hurry, To keep, To describe, To enter.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
bedeuten — to mean.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to bathe, then the German for to build.

You'll want to know: bedeuten.
bedeuten — "to mean".

The word, taken apart.
bedeuten is be- + the verb "to interpret", Old High German diuten, "to make plain to the people" (EtymDB 2.1). It shares its root with Deutsch, "of the people".
