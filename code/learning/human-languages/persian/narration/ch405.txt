Persian, chapter 405: Legal or not.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
قانونی (qânuni) — legal, lawful.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to observe, then the Persian for to break.

You'll want to know: قانونی.
قانونی (qânuni) — "legal, lawful".
این کار کاملا قانونی است. in kâr kâmelan qânuni ast. This is completely legal.
Qânun "law" + -i.

The word, taken apart.
قانونی (qânuni) is qânun "law" with the Persian adjective suffix -i. Qânun comes from Arabic qānūn, which was taken from Greek kanōn "rule, measuring rod".

Lesson 2 of 5.
غیرقانونی (gheyr-e qânuni) — illegal.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to break, then the Persian for legal.

You'll want to know: غیرقانونی.
غیرقانونی (gheyr-e qânuni) — "illegal".
فروش این دارو غیرقانونی است. forush-e in dâru gheyr-e qânuni ast. Selling this medicine is illegal.
Gheyr "non-, other than" + qânuni.

The word, taken apart.
غیرقانونی (gheyr-e qânuni) joins gheyr, from Arabic ghayr "other than" (EtymDB), to qânuni "legal", from qânun "law" (Arabic qānūn, from Greek kanōn).

Lesson 3 of 5.
تخلف (takhallof) — an offence, a violation.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for legal, then the Persian for illegal.

You'll want to know: تخلف.
تخلف (takhallof) — "an offence, a violation".
این کار یک تخلف جدی است. in kâr yek takhallof-e jeddi ast. This is a serious offence.
Plural takhallofât. Lighter than a crime, such as traffic offences.

The word, taken apart.
تخلف (takhallof) is usually traced to Arabic takhalluf, a verbal noun usually explained as staying behind or failing to comply, from the same Arabic family as mokhâlef.

Lesson 4 of 5.
مجازات (mojâzât) — a punishment, a penalty.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for illegal, then the Persian for an offence.

You'll want to know: مجازات.
مجازات (mojâzât) — "a punishment, a penalty".
مجازات دزدی زندان است. mojâzât-e dozdi zendân ast. The punishment for theft is prison.
Mojâzât kardan — to punish.

The word, taken apart.
مجازات (mojâzât) was borrowed from Arabic mujāzāt (EtymDB), a word usually glossed as requital, paying back a deed.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: مجوز.

برای ساختن خانه به مجوز نیاز داریم. barâye sâkhtan-e khâne be mojavvez niyâz dârim. We need a permit to build a house.
Plural mojavvezhâ. Mojavvez gereftan — to get a permit.

The word, taken apart.
مجوز (mojavvez) is usually traced to Arabic mujawwiz "what permits, what allows".
