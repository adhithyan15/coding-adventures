[PAUSE 4s] Two chapters ago nothing in this book could be anywhere and no
demonstrative could stand in front of a postposition. Run the distance, cold.

- [YOU SAY: the question family — *kyā*, *kaun*, *kahāṅ*]
- [YOU SAY: the answer family — *yih*, *yahāṅ*; *voh*, *vahāṅ*]
- [YOU SAY: the drinks, placed — *chāy yahāṅ hai*, *pānī vahāṅ hai*]
- [YOU SAY: the whole phrase bending together — *is kāle kamre meṅ*]
- [YOU SAY: people, respectfully and not — *is ko*, then of a mother, *un ko*]
- [YOU SAY: the family, placed — *māṅ yahāṅ haiṅ*, *bahan vahāṅ hai*]
- [YOU SAY: the weather — *sardī hai*, and *yahāṅ sardī hai*]
- [YOU SAY: a denial — *voh yahāṅ nahīṅ hai*]
