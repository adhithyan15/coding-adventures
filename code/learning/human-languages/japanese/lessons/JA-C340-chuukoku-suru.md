## You'll want to know: ちゅうこくする
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C340-JOGEN340-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ちゅうこくする** — *chūkoku suru* — "to warn; to advise against".

> **あぶないから、いしゃが ちゅうこくしました。** — *abunai kara, isha ga chūkoku shimashita* — "It is dangerous, so the doctor warned me."

Polite **ちゅうこくします**, *chūkokushimasu*; a する verb. Past (polite): **いしゃが ちゅうこくしました。**, *isha ga chūkoku shimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ちゅうこくする** (*chūkoku suru*) is built from a Sino-Japanese noun and the native verb *suru*, "to do". The noun, *chūkoku*, is two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese: *chū*, "loyalty" and *koku*, "tell" (Middle Chinese *kowk*) (KANJIDIC2; JMdict; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
