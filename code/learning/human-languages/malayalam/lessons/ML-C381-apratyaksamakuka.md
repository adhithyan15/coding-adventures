# അപ്രത്യക്ഷമാകുക (apratyakṣamākuka) — to disappear

## Warm-up

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Malayalam for silence, then the Malayalam for to whisper.

## You'll want to know: അപ്രത്യക്ഷമാകുക
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C381-IRUTTU381-05]; assesses=[] -->

**അപ്രത്യക്ഷമാകുക** — *apratyakṣamākuka* — "to disappear".

> കള്ളൻ ഇരുട്ടിൽ അപ്രത്യക്ഷമായി.
>
> *kaḷḷan iruṭṭil apratyakṣamāyi.*
>
> The thief disappeared into the darkness.

Past: അപ്രത്യക്ഷമായി, *apratyakṣamāyi*.

അ-, not, + പ്രത്യക്ഷം, before the eyes, + ആകുക, to become: to go out of sight.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**അപ്രത്യക്ഷമാകുക** (*apratyakṣamākuka*) is built from Sanskrit *pratyakṣa*, "visible, before the eyes" (CDIAL 8635), with *a-*, "not", and the Malayalam ആകുക, "to become".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
