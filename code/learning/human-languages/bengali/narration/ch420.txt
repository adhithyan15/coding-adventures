Bengali, chapter 420: Sales, Hawkers and Markets.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
বিক্রি (bikri) — a sale; selling.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a pledge (for a loan), then the Bengali for one's means.

You'll want to know: বিক্রি.
বিক্রি — bikri — "a sale; selling".
শীতে কম্বলের বিক্রি ভালো হয়। shīte kômbôler bikri bhālo hôy. Blankets sell well in winter.
বিক্রি (bikri) করা, to sell; বিক্রি (bikri) হওয়া, to be sold.

The word, taken apart.
বিক্রি (bikri) goes back to Sanskrit vikrayá, "a sale" (CDIAL 11638), under which CDIAL lists the older Bengali bikā and biki; বিক্রি is the half-learned form in use today.

Lesson 2 of 5.
ফেরিওয়ালা (pherioyālā) — a hawker; a street seller.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for one's means, then the Bengali for a sale (selling).

You'll want to know: ফেরিওয়ালা.
ফেরিওয়ালা — pherioyālā — "a hawker; a street seller".
দুপুরে গলিতে ফেরিওয়ালা হাঁকছিল। dupure gôlite pherioyālā hā̃kchhilô. At midday a hawker was crying his wares in the lane.

The word, taken apart.
ফেরিওয়ালা (pherioyālā) joins ফেরি, "going round selling", from the root of ফেরা, to turn (CDIAL 9078), to -ওয়ালা, the ending for one who deals in a thing: the man who goes round.

Lesson 3 of 5.
দালাল (dālāl) — a broker; a middleman.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a sale (selling), then the Bengali for a hawker.

You'll want to know: দালাল.
দালাল — dālāl — "a broker; a middleman".
দালাল বাসার জন্য অগ্রিম চাইল। dālāl bāsār jônyô ôgrim chāilô. The broker asked for an advance for the flat.

The word, taken apart.
দালাল (dālāl) is usually traced to Arabic dallāl, "a broker, a guide", through Persian; the word often carries a hint of distrust.

Lesson 4 of 5.
হাট (hāṭ) — a village market (on set days).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a hawker, then the Bengali for a broker.

You'll want to know: হাট.
হাট — hāṭ — "a village market (on set days)".
আমাদের গ্রামে বুধবার হাট বসে। āmāder grāme budhbār hāṭ bôse. In our village the market is held every Wednesday.
A বাজার opens every day; a হাট (hāṭ) বসে, sits, on its own days of the week.

The word, taken apart.
হাট (hāṭ) is inherited: CDIAL lists Bengali hāṭ, "a shop, a market", under Sanskrit haṭṭa, "a market, a fair" (CDIAL 13944).

Lesson 5 of 5.
বিকোনো (bikono) — to be sold; to sell (for a price).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a broker, then the Bengali for a village market.

You'll want to know: বিকোনো.
বিকোনো — bikono — "to be sold; to sell (for a price)".
হাটে মাছটা ভালো দামে বিকোল। hāṭe māchhṭā bhālo dāme bikolô. At the market the fish sold for a good price.
Past (he/she …): সেই মাছ ভালো দামে বিকোল, sei māchh bhālo dāme bikolô.
The goods are the subject: আম বিকোয়, mangoes sell.

The word, taken apart.
বিকোনো (bikono) is inherited: CDIAL lists Bengali bikā, "to sell", under Sanskrit vikrīyatē, "is sold" (CDIAL 11642).
