## You'll want to know: झल्लाना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C468-DHAIRYA468-05]; assesses=[] -->

**झल्लाना** — *jhallānā* — "to snap irritably".

> बार बार वही सवाल सुनकर पिता झल्ला गए।
>
> *bār bār vahī savāl sunkar pitā jhallā gae.*
>
> Hearing the same question again and again, my father snapped.

Past (he …): वह झल्ला गया, *vah jhallā gayā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**झल्लाना** (*jhallānā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists Hindi *jhallānā*, "to burn", beside *jhallā*, "hot", under *\*jhal*, "flash", a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 5352). The sense "to flare up in irritation" is a turn CDIAL does not give, though the heat behind it is plain.
