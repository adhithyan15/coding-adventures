## You'll want to know: o reboque
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C406-AVARIA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**o reboque** — "the tow truck; the tow, the trailer".

> Chamámos o reboque e esperámos uma hora.
>
> *We called the tow truck and waited an hour.*

In Brazil: *o guincho*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*reboque* is built on *rebocar* 'to tow', which goes back to Latin *remulcum* 'towrope' (EtymDB).
