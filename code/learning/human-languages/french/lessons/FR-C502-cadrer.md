## You'll want to know: cadrer
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C502-PHOTO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**cadrer** — "to frame (a picture)".

> Elle a mal cadré la photo : la tour est coupée.
>
> *She framed the photo badly: the tower is cut off.*

Also to fit: *ça ne cadre pas avec ses idées*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*cadrer* is built on *cadre* "frame", borrowed from Italian *quadro*, from Latin *quadrum* "a square" (Lewis & Short).
