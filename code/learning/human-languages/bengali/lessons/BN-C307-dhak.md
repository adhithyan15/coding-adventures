## You'll want to know: ঢাক
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C307-DHAK307-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ঢাক** — *ḍhāk* — "a dhak; a big festival drum".

> পুজোর সকালে একটা লোক ঢাক বাজাচ্ছিল।
>
> *pujor sôkāle ekṭā lok ḍhāk bājāchchhilô.*
>
> On the morning of the puja a man was playing the dhak.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ঢাক** (*ḍhāk*) is usually linked with Sanskrit *ḍhakkā*, 'a large drum' (Monier-Williams). Its ঢ is the breathy partner of ড.
