Bengali, chapter 365: Coming to Terms.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
মতভেদ (môtbhed) — a difference of opinion.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for to be devastated, then the Bengali for extreme.

You'll want to know: মতভেদ.
মতভেদ — môtbhed — "a difference of opinion".
এই বিষয়ে আমাদের মতভেদ আছে। ei bishôye āmāder môtbhed āchhe. We disagree on this subject.

The word, taken apart.
মতভেদ (môtbhed) is Sanskrit mata, an opinion, with bheda, a split or difference (Monier-Williams): where opinions part.

What you've built.

Lesson 2 of 5.
সমঝোতা (sômjhotā) — an agreement reached; an understanding.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for extreme, then the Bengali for a difference of opinion.

You'll want to know: সমঝোতা.
সমঝোতা — sômjhotā — "an agreement reached; an understanding".
শেষে দুই দল সমঝোতায় এল। sheshe dui dôl sômjhotāy elô. In the end the two sides reached an agreement.

The word, taken apart.
সমঝোতা (sômjhotā) is usually traced to Hindustani samjhautā, an understanding, built on samajh, understanding: what two sides reach when each understands the other. সমঝোতায় আসা is to come to terms.

Lesson 3 of 5.
নিষ্পত্তি (nishpôtti) — a settlement; a resolution (of a case or a dispute).

Warm-up.

Before the new one: say the Bengali for a difference of opinion, then the Bengali for an agreement reached.

You'll want to know: নিষ্পত্তি.
নিষ্পত্তি — nishpôtti — "a settlement; a resolution (of a case or a dispute)".
মামলাটার নিষ্পত্তি হয়ে গেল। māmlāṭār nishpôtti hôye gelô. The case was settled.

The word, taken apart.
নিষ্পত্তি (nishpôtti) is Sanskrit niṣpatti, completion, a thing brought to its end (Monier-Williams). Bengali uses it for the final settling of a case or a quarrel.

Lesson 4 of 5.
মানানো (mānāno) — to talk round; to win over; to suit.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for an agreement reached, then the Bengali for a settlement.

You'll want to know: মানানো.
মানানো — mānāno — "to talk round; to win over; to suit".
শেষে আমি বাবাকে মানালাম। sheshe āmi bābāke mānālām. In the end I talked my father round.
Past (he/she …): তিনি সবাইকে মানালেন, tini sôbāike mānālen.

The word, taken apart.
মানানো (mānāno) is the causative of মানা 'to accept' (CDIAL 9857 lists Bengali mānā): to make someone accept, to bring them round. A colour or a dress মানায় when it suits the one wearing it.

The word, taken apart.
সর্বসম্মত (sôrbôsômmôtô) is Sanskrit sarva, all, with sammata, agreed or approved (Monier-Williams): agreed by everyone.
