Kannada, chapter 382: Facts and Illusions.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಸಂಗತಿ (saṅgati) — a fact; a matter.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to reflect on, then the Kannada for intellect.

You'll want to know: ಸಂಗತಿ.
ಸಂಗತಿ — saṅgati — "a fact; a matter".
ಈ ಸಂಗತಿ ಯಾರಿಗೂ ಗೊತ್ತಿರಲಿಲ್ಲ. ī saṅgati yārigū gottiralilla. Nobody knew this fact.
ವಿಷಯ is a topic; ಸಂಗತಿ (saṅgati) is something that is so.

The word, taken apart.
ಸಂಗತಿ (saṅgati) is borrowed from Sanskrit saṃgati, "a coming together, a meeting" (Monier-Williams); Kannada uses it for a matter or a fact.

ನೀರು ಇದೆ ಅನ್ನುವುದು ಬರೀ ಭ್ರಮೆ ಆಗಿತ್ತು. nīru ide annuvudu barī bhrame āgittu. That there was water was only an illusion.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ಮನಗಾಣು (managāṇu) — to realise.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for an illusion, then the Kannada for existence.

You'll want to know: ಮನಗಾಣು.
ಮನಗಾಣು — managāṇu — "to realise".
ನನ್ನ ತಪ್ಪನ್ನು ನಾನು ತಡವಾಗಿ ಮನಗಂಡೆ. nanna tappannu nānu taḍavāgi managaṇḍe. I realised my mistake late.
Past (I …): ಮನಗಂಡೆ, managaṇḍe.
Its past is built on ಕಂಡ: ಮನಗಂಡನು, he realised.

The word, taken apart.
ಮನಗಾಣು (managāṇu) is built from two parts: mana, "mind" (Sanskrit manas, CDIAL 9822), and native kāṇ, "to see" (DEDR 1443): to see with the mind.
