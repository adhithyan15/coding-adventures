## You'll want to know: gelare
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C468-GELO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**gelare** — "to freeze".

> Stanotte è gelato il lago.
>
> *Last night the lake froze.*

*Si gela!* — it is freezing!

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*gelare* is inherited from Latin *gelāre* 'to freeze' (EtymDB), from *gelū* 'frost'.
