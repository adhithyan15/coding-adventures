## You'll want to know: परम्परा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C330-PARAMPARA330-01]; assesses=[] -->

**परम्परा** — *paramparā* — "a tradition".

> अस्माकं ग्रामे प्राचीना परम्परा अस्ति।
>
> *asmākaṃ grāme prācīnā paramparā asti.*
>
> Our village has an old tradition.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**परम्परा** (*paramparā*) is built from *para*, "next, other", doubled with an *m* between: one after another; MW gives *paramparā* as an uninterrupted series, succession, and tradition (MW).
