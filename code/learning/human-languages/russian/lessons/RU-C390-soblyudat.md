## You'll want to know: соблюдать
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C390-PORYADOK-01]; assesses=[] -->

**соблюдать** (*soblyudát*) — "to observe, to keep (a rule)".

> Все должны соблюдать правила.
>
> *Vse dolzhný soblyudát právila.*
>
> Everyone must observe the rules.

Imperfective; perfective *соблюсти*, rare. *Соблюдать закон, диету, тишину*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**соблюдать** is built from *со-* and *блюсти* "to guard, to keep", an old Slavic verb also behind *наблюдать* "to observe".
