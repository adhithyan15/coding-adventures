# ಜಲಾಶಯ (jalāśaya) — a reservoir

## You'll want to know: ಜಲಾಶಯ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C483-JALASHAYA483-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ಜಲಾಶಯ** — *jalāśaya* — "a reservoir".

> ಈ ವರ್ಷ ಜಲಾಶಯ ಪೂರ್ತಿ ತುಂಬಿದೆ.
>
> *ī varṣa jalāśaya pūrti tumbide.*
>
> This year the reservoir is completely full.

ಜಲ, water, plus ಆಶಯ, a receptacle.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಜಲಾಶಯ** (*jalāśaya*) is built from two Sanskrit words: *jala*, "water" (CDIAL 5155), and *āśaya*, "a resting place, a receptacle" (CDIAL 1453).
