Persian, chapter 435: Taxis and porters.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
دربست (darbast) — a private hire (a taxi taken for yourself alone).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to get a seat, then the Persian for to miss.

You'll want to know: دربست.
دربست (darbast) — "a private hire (a taxi taken for yourself alone)".
تا فرودگاه دربست گرفتیم. Tâ forudgâh darbast gereftim. We took a private taxi to the airport.
A shared taxi picks up several people on a fixed route; darbast means you hire the whole car.

The word, taken apart.
darbast is usually analysed as a native compound of dar 'in' and bast 'closed', the past stem of bastan 'to tie, to close', which EtymDB traces to the PIE root bʰendʰ-: the whole car, closed to others.

Lesson 3 of 6.
مسافربری (mosâferbari) — a coach company; passenger transport.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a private hire, then the Persian for a car service.

You'll want to know: مسافربری.
مسافربری (mosâferbari) — "a coach company; passenger transport".
بلیت اتوبوس را از مسافربری خریدیم. Bilit-e otobus râ az mosâferbari kharidim. We bought the bus ticket from the coach company.
mosâfer 'passenger' + bar 'carrying' + -i.

The word, taken apart.
mosâferbari is built from mosâfer 'traveller', borrowed from Arabic musāfir, and native bar, the present stem of bordan 'to carry', with the ending -i.

باربر (bârbar) چمدانهای ما را تا تاکسی برد. Bârbar chamedân-hâ-ye mâ râ tâ tâksi bord. The porter took our suitcases to the taxi.
bâr 'load' + bar 'carrier'.

bârbar is a native compound of bâr 'load', which EtymDB lists, and bar, the present stem of bordan 'to carry': a load-carrier.

Lesson 5 of 6.
رساندن (resândan) — to give someone a lift, to drop off; to deliver.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a coach company, then the Persian for a porter.

You'll want to know: رساندن.
رساندن (resândan) — "to give someone a lift, to drop off; to deliver".
همکارم مرا با ماشینش تا خانه رساند. Hamkâram marâ bâ mâshinash tâ khâne resând. My colleague gave me a lift home in his car.
The causative of residan 'to arrive': to make someone arrive. Present stem resân-: miresânam.

The word, taken apart.
resândan is built on residan 'to arrive', usually traced to Middle Persian rasīdan, with the causative ending -ândan: to make arrive.
