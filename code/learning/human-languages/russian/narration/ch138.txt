Russian, chapter 138: Future, in a Week, a Plan, To Plan, To Hope.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
будущий (búdushchiy) — future, next.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for last, then the Russian for to happen.

You'll want to know: будущий.
будущий (búdushchiy) — "future, next". One Russian future is буду and an infinitive: Я буду читать. — ya búdu chitát — "I will read." в будущем году is "next year".
