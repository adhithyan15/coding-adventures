## You'll want to know: धास्ती
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C455-DHASTI455-01]; assesses=[] -->

**धास्ती** — *dhāstī* — "dread".

> परीक्षेची धास्ती तिच्या मनात होती.
>
> *parīkṣecī dhāstī ticyā manāt hotī.*
>
> Dread of the exam was on her mind.

Feminine.

Feminine: परीक्षेची धास्ती, dread of the exam. धास्ती वाटणे, to feel dread, with the dative.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**धास्ती** (*dhāstī*) is a Marathi noun whose origin is not settled.
