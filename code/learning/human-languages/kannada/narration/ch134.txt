Kannada, chapter 134: Rat, Buffalo, Sheep, Face, Tongue.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಇಲಿ (ili) — a rat, a mouse.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a donkey, then the Kannada for a camel.

You'll want to know: ಇಲಿ.
ಇಲಿ — ili — "a rat, a mouse".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: ili]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: ili, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say ili]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Kannada for a donkey, then the Kannada for a camel, then say ili again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does ಇಲಿ (ili) mean? ("A rat, a mouse".) Say it once more.
