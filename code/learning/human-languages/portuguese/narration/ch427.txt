Portuguese, chapter 427: Reservar e desmarcar.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
a caução — the security deposit; bail.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for the tent peg, then the Portuguese for the mosquito net.

You'll want to know: a caução.
a caução — "the security deposit; bail".
Pagámos uma caução de cem euros pela casa. We paid a hundred-euro deposit for the house.
You get a caução back at the end if nothing is broken.

The word, taken apart.
caução is a learned borrowing from Latin cautiō 'caution, guarantee' (EtymDB), built on cavēre 'to take care'.

What you've built.
One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.

Lesson 2 of 5.
desmarcar — to call off, to cancel (an appointment or booking).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for the mosquito net, then the Portuguese for the security deposit.

You'll want to know: desmarcar.
desmarcar — "to call off, to cancel (an appointment or booking)".
Desmarquei o quarto porque fiquei doente. I cancelled the room because I fell ill.
Marcar — to book; desmarcar — to call it off.

The word, taken apart.
desmarcar is built from des- and marcar 'to mark, to book'. Marcar is usually linked with marco 'boundary stone', from Germanic markō 'boundary' (EtymDB).

Lesson 3 of 5.

You'll want to know: o cancelamento.
o cancelamento — "the cancellation".
O cancelamento é gratuito até dois dias antes. Cancellation is free up to two days before.
The verb is cancelar.

The word, taken apart.
cancelamento is built on cancelar 'to cancel', a learned borrowing from Latin cancellāre 'to cross out with lattice lines', on cancellī 'lattice, railings' (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 5 of 5.
remarcar — to rebook, to reschedule.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for the cancellation, then the Portuguese for to bring forward.

You'll want to know: remarcar.
remarcar — "to rebook, to reschedule".
A agência remarcou o voo para sexta. The agency rebooked the flight for Friday.
Also to mark a price again.

The word, taken apart.
remarcar is built from re- 'again' and marcar 'to mark, to book', which is usually linked with marco 'boundary stone', from Germanic markō 'boundary' (EtymDB).
