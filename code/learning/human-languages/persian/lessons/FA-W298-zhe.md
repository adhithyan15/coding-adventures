# ژ (zhe) — the letter that opens ژاکت

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[FA-SCRIPT-C22-ZE-01, FA-SCRIPT-C24-PE-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: write ز and پ once each, and name them.

[PAUSE 2s] Now say **ژاکت** (*zhâkat*), "a cardigan". You met it a moment ago.
This lesson takes its first letter out and puts it in your hand.

## Script you'll notice: ژ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-SCRIPT-C298-ZHE-01]; assesses=[FA-LEX-C298-ZHAKAT-01] -->

**ژ** — *zhe*, the sound in the middle of English *measure*.

It opens **ژاکت**. It is **ز** with **three dots** in place of one, and like
**ز** it takes nothing on its left: after **ژ** the pen lifts, and **ا** stands
on its own before **کت**.

Arabic has no *zh*, so the Arabic alphabet had no letter for it. Persian made
four letters of its own from Arabic ones, with extra dots or an extra stroke:
**پ** from **ب**, **چ** from **ج**, **گ** from **ک**, and **ژ** from **ز**. You
have now written all four.

## Writing: ژ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[FA-SCRIPT-C298-ZHE-01] -->

Put your pen on ژ and follow its line. Copy the shape you can see — slowly,
and larger than it is printed.

> This book does not yet tell you **where to start this letter or which way to
> travel**. It is not written down here until it can be written down with a
> source. Copying what is in front of you needs no such source, and it is how the
> shape gets into your hand in the meantime.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[FA-SCRIPT-C22-ZE-01, FA-SCRIPT-C24-PE-01, FA-LEX-C298-ZHAKAT-01, FA-SCRIPT-C298-ZHE-01] -->

[PAUSE 1s]
- [YOU FIND: ژ at the start of **ژاکت**, and point at its three dots]
- [YOU TRACE: ژ three times, saying *zh* as you finish each one]
- [YOU WRITE: ز, then ژ, from memory: one dot, then three]
- [YOU WRITE: **ژاکت**, lifting the pen after ژ and after ا]
- [YOU READ: **ژاکت** aloud once more]

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[FA-SCRIPT-C24-PE-01, FA-LEX-C298-ZHAKAT-01, FA-SCRIPT-C298-ZHE-01] -->

[PAUSE 3s] Which letter is this — ژ? (***zhe***, the *zh* of *measure*.) Which
word did it come from? (**ژاکت**, *zhâkat*, "a cardigan".) Name the four
letters Persian added. (**پ چ ژ گ**.)
