## You'll want to know: a edição
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C280-PUBLICAR-04]; assesses=[] -->

**a edição** — "the edition, the issue".

> Comprei a edição de domingo.
>
> *I bought the Sunday edition.*

Also film or video editing.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*edição* is a learned borrowing from Latin *ēditiō* "a bringing forth, a publishing", from *ēdere* "to put out", built on *dare* "to give" (Lewis & Short).
