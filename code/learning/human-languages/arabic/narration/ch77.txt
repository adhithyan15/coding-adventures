Arabic, chapter 77: Star, Cloud, Sun, Moon, Sea.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
نجم (najm) — a star.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for sand, then the Arabic for a field.

You'll want to know: نجم.
نجم — najm — "a star".

What you've built.

Guided Practice.

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
