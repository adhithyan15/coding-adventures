## You'll want to know: kěwàng
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C533-WISH533-03]; assesses=[] -->

*kěwàng* — "to thirst for; to crave".

> *tā hěn kěwàng dāng yīshēng* — He longs to be a doctor.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*kěwàng* is built from *kě* 'thirsty' and 望 'to look out for', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *khat* and *mjangH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'hunger and thirst'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what would happen.
