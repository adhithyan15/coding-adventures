# ગેરંટી (geranṭī) — a guarantee

## You'll want to know: ગેરંટી
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C372-VIMO372-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ગેરંટી** — *geranṭī* — "a guarantee".

> જો પંખો બગડશે તો દુકાનદાર ગેરંટીને લીધે તેને મફત સુધારશે.
>
> *jo paṅkho bagaḍśe to dukāndār geranṭīne līdhe tene maphat sudhārśe.*
>
> If the fan breaks down, the shopkeeper will repair it free under the guarantee.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ગેરંટી** (*geranṭī*) is borrowed from English *guarantee*.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
