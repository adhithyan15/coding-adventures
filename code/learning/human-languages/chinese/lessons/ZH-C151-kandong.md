## You'll want to know: kàndǒng
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C151-KANDONG-01]; assesses=[] -->

*kàndǒng* — "to understand (on reading or watching)".

> *zhè běn shū wǒ kàndǒng le* — I understood this book.

Its second character comes later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*kàndǒng* is built from 看 'to look; to read' and *dǒng* 'to understand'. 看 is inherited from Middle Chinese *khanH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription); the character for *dǒng* has no reading in the Guangyun, so its history is not settled.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ZH-LEX-C151-TINGDONG-01, ZH-GRAMMAR-TOPIC-COMMENT-01] -->

The thing can go first, as the topic: *zhè běn shū*, then who, then the verb and its result.

*tīngdǒng* for the ear, *kàndǒng* for the eye: one result, two ways in.
