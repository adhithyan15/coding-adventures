## You'll want to know: ساک
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C433-SAK-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ساک** (*sâk*) — "a holdall, a travel bag".

> یک ساک کوچک برای سفر کافی است.
>
> *Yek sâk-e kuchek barâ-ye safar kâfi ast.*
>
> A small bag is enough for the trip.

*Sâk-e varzeshi* — a sports bag.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*sâk* was borrowed from French and is usually traced to French *sac* 'bag'. EtymDB does not list the Persian word.
