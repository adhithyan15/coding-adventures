## You'll want to know: справедливый
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C345-SPRAVEDLIVO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**справедливый** (*spravedlívyy*) — "fair, even-handed".

> Наш учитель строгий, но справедливый.
>
> *Nash uchítel strógiy, no spravedlívyy.*
>
> Our teacher is strict but fair.

*Справедливо* — fairly, or it is fair.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**справедливый** is usually traced to the root of *правда* "truth" and *право* "right", Proto-Slavic *\*pravŭ* "right, correct" (EtymDB), through a Church Slavonic formation.
