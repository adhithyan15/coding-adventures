## You'll want to know: la difesa
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C392-DIFESA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**la difesa** — "the defence".

> L'avvocato della difesa parla per ultimo.
>
> *The defence lawyer speaks last.*

In sport, the defence too. *Per legittima difesa* — in self-defence.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*difesa* is inherited from Late Latin *dēfēnsa* "a defending" (EtymDB), from *dēfendere* "to ward off, to defend".
