## You'll want to know: el mérito
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C595-CARRERA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**el mérito** — "the merit, the credit".

> Todo el mérito es suyo.
>
> *All the credit is hers.*

*Tener mérito* — to deserve credit.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*mérito* is a learned borrowing from Latin *meritum* 'what one deserves', the participle of *merere* 'to earn' (EtymDB).

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
