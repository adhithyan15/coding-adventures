## You'll want to know: しょくよく
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C220-WEIGHT220-05]; assesses=[] -->

**しょくよく** — *shokuyoku* — "an appetite".

> **しょくよくは ありますか。** — *shokuyoku wa arimasu ka* — "Do you have an appetite?"

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**しょくよく** (*shokuyoku*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *shoku*, "to eat" (Middle Chinese *zyik*) and *yoku*, "desire" (*yowk*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
