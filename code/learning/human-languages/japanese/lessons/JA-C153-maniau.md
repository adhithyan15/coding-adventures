## You'll want to know: まにあう
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C153-OUTING153-05]; assesses=[] -->

**まにあう** — *maniau* — "to be in time; to make it".

> **まいあさ でんしゃに まにあいます。** — *maiasa densha ni maniaimasu* — "Every morning I make it to the train in time."

Polite **まにあいます**, *maniaimasu*; a godan verb.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**まにあう** (*maniau*) is native Japanese, built from *ma*, "interval, space", the particle *ni*, and *au*, "to fit": to fit inside the time. All three parts are inherited rather than borrowed (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
