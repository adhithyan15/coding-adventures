# (reading) — six words off a signboard

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MW-SCRIPT-CHAAY-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] You have written all six of these. Not one is new.

[PAUSE 1s] What is new is that nobody is going to say them first.

## Reading
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-SKILL-READ-SIGNBOARD]; assesses=[MW-SCRIPT-ROTI-01, MW-SCRIPT-KAPDA-01, MW-SCRIPT-PAISA-01] -->

> चाय
> रोटी
> कपड़ा
> रिक्शा
> पैसा
> भाव

[PAUSE 3s] Read down the list once. Do not sound out each sign — look at the
whole word and let it arrive.

[PAUSE 3s] Again. Notice which came at once and which you had to assemble.
Both are fine; the difference is what practice removes.

## You'll want to know
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MW-SCRIPT-RIKSHA-01, MW-SCRIPT-BHAAV-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Every one of these is a word you would actually meet painted on
something — a stall, a cart, a board above a counter.

[PAUSE 2s] Writing a word and reading it are not the same skill. Writing gives
you all the time you want and the letters in order. A signboard gives you one
look, and the word has to arrive whole.

[PAUSE 2s] That is why six is enough for one sitting.
