## You'll want to know: ஒப்பி
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C312-VIRIVAKA312-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ஒப்பி** — *oppi* — "to recite".

> மாணவன் பாடத்தை மனப்பாடமாக ஒப்பித்தான்.
>
> *māṇavaṉ pāṭattai maṉappāṭamāka oppittāṉ.*
>
> The student recited the lesson by heart.

Past (I …): ஒப்பித்தேன், *oppittēṉ*.

Said of reciting a lesson or a poem from memory.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ஒப்பி** (*oppi*), "to recite, to hand over", is a Tamil verb whose origin is not settled in the sources at hand.
