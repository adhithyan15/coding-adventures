# હું વાંચતો હતો (hũ vā̃cto hato) — was reading, used to read

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-GRAMMAR-C145-NEGATE-01, GU-GRAMMAR-C147-HATO-01, GU-GRAMMAR-C147-HATU-HATA-01, GU-JOIN-JYAARE-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "I don't read", the A2 way. (*hũ nathī vā̃cto*.) Keep its **-તો**. Then say "I was at home". (*hũ ghare hato*.)

## Grammar Lens: -તો with હતો
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-GRAMMAR-C275-ONGOING-PAST-01]; assesses=[GU-LEX-VANCHVU, GU-LEX-PUSTAK-01, GU-LEX-C54-MOVE-02, GU-LEX-C94-TIME94-03, GU-LEX-C77-WHEN-05] -->

> **હું પુસ્તક વાંચતો હતો.**
>
> *hũ pustak vā̃cto hato.* — "I was reading a book."

The stem takes **-તો**, then comes **હતો**, and both follow the person:

| who | was reading |
|---|---|
| a man | **વાંચતો હતો** (*vā̃cto hato*) |
| a woman | **વાંચતી હતી** (*vā̃ctī hatī*) |
| a neuter thing | **વાંચતું હતું** (*vā̃ctũ hatũ*) |
| several, or respect | **વાંચતા હતા** (*vā̃ctā hatā*) |

There is no **-એ** here, even with an object: **મીરા ચા પીતી હતી** (*Mīrā cā pītī hatī*), "Mira was drinking tea". The doer form goes with the plain past, **પીધી**.

The same form tells what used to happen: **પહેલાં અરુણ રોજ ચાલતો હતો** (*pahelā̃ Aruṇ roj cālto hato*), "Arun used to walk every day".

## You'll want to know: the scene and the event
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-GRAMMAR-C275-SCENE-EVENT-01]; assesses=[GU-LEX-C86-JOURNEY-04, GU-LEX-AAVVU, GU-LEX-C206-ARRIVE206-01, GU-LEX-C55-HOUSEWORK-01] -->

> **હું વાંચતો હતો ત્યારે ફોન આવ્યો.**
>
> *hũ vā̃cto hato tyāre phon āvyo.* — "I was reading when the phone rang."

**વાંચતો હતો** is the scene: it was going on. **આવ્યો** is the event that broke in. **ત્યારે** (*tyāre*), "at that time", joins them, and **જ્યારે** can open the scene: **જ્યારે અરુણ ઘરે પહોંચ્યો, ત્યારે મીરા રાંધતી હતી** (*jyāre Aruṇ ghare pahõcyo, tyāre Mīrā rā̃dhtī hatī*), "when Arun got home, Mira was cooking".
