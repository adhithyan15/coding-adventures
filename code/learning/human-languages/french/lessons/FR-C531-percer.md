## You'll want to know: percer
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C531-BRICOLAGE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**percer** — "to drill, to pierce".

> Mon voisin perce un trou dans le mur.
>
> *My neighbour is drilling a hole in the wall.*

Spelling: *nous perçons*. *Une perceuse* — a drill.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*percer* is inherited from Old French *percier* (EtymDB), from Vulgar Latin *pertūsiāre*, built on Latin *pertundere* "to bore through" (Lewis & Short).
