## You'll want to know: দ্বিধা
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C454-SONGKOCH454-02]; assesses=[] -->

**দ্বিধা** — *dvidhā* — "doubt; being in two minds".

> নতুন চাকরি নিয়ে অরুণের মনে দ্বিধা ছিল।
>
> *nôtun chākri niye ôruner mône dvidhā chhilô.*
>
> Arun was in two minds about the new job.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**দ্বিধা** (*dvidhā*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *dvidhā*, "in two ways, divided" (Monier-Williams); Bengali turned the adverb into a noun, a mind split in two.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
