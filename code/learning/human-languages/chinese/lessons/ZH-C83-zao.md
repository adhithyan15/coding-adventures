## You'll want to know: 早
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C83-WAIT83-02]; assesses=[] -->

**早** — *zǎo* — "early".

> **明天的火车很早** *míngtiān de huǒchē hěn zǎo* — Tomorrow's train leaves early.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**早** (*zǎo*) is inherited from Middle Chinese *tsawX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'morning'. Early is not given there; from morning to early is the likely step, but it is not settled.
