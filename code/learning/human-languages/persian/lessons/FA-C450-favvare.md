## You'll want to know: فواره
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C450-MARKAZ-05]; assesses=[] -->

**فواره** (*favvâre*) — "a fountain".

> بچه ها کنار فواره پارک بازی میکردند.
>
> *Bachche-hâ kenâr-e favvâre-ye pârk bâzi mikardand.*
>
> The children were playing by the fountain in the park.

A spring in the ground is *cheshme*; *favvâre* sends water up.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*favvâre* was borrowed from Arabic *fawwāra* (EtymDB), a word for water that gushes up.
