## You'll want to know: cosechar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C692-SEMBRAR-03]; assesses=[] -->

**cosechar** — "to harvest, to reap".

> En septiembre cosechan la uva.
>
> *In September they harvest the grapes.*

*La cosecha* is the harvest.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*cosechar* is built on *cosecha* "harvest", which comes from Latin *collēcta* "a gathering" (EtymDB), from *colligere* "to gather".
