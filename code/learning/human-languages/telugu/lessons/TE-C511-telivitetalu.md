# తెలివితేటలు (telivitēṭalu) — wits; intelligence

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Telugu for self-confidence, then the Telugu for curiosity.

## You'll want to know: తెలివితేటలు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C511-GNAPAKAM511-01]; assesses=[] -->

**తెలివితేటలు** — *telivitēṭalu* — "wits; intelligence".

> ఆమె తెలివితేటలతో సమస్యను పరిష్కరించింది.
>
> *āme telivitēṭalatō samasyanu pariṣkariñcindi.*
>
> She solved the problem with her wits.

Plural in form. తెలివి is understanding.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**తెలివితేటలు** (*telivitēṭalu*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 3433 lists Telugu *telivi*, "understanding", from *teliyu*, "to be known, to be clear"; *tēṭalu* goes with it as a pair word.
