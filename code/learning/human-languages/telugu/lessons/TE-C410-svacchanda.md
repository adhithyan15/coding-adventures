## You'll want to know: స్వచ్ఛంద
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C410-SEVA410-03]; assesses=[] -->

**స్వచ్ఛంద** — *svacchanda* — "voluntary".

> ఆమె ఒక స్వచ్ఛంద సంస్థలో పని చేస్తుంది.
>
> *āme oka svacchanda saṁsthalō pani cēstundi.*
>
> She works for a voluntary organisation.

స్వచ్ఛంద సంస్థ, a voluntary organisation; స్వచ్ఛందంగా, of one’s own will.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**స్వచ్ఛంద** (*svacchanda*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *svacchanda*, "one’s own free will; acting at pleasure, independent" (Monier-Williams).
