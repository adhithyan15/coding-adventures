## You'll want to know: die Beerdigung
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C303-BEERDIGUNG-03]; assesses=[] -->

**die Beerdigung** — "the funeral".

> Zur Beerdigung kamen viele Leute.
>
> *Many people came to the funeral.*

Plural: *die Beerdigungen*. In Austria also *das Begräbnis*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Beerdigung* is built on *beerdigen* "to bury", from *Erde* "earth", an inherited Germanic word (EtymDB).

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
