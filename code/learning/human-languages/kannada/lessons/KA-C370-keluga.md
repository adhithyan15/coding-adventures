## You'll want to know: ಕೇಳುಗ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C370-PATRIKODYAMA370-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ಕೇಳುಗ** — *kēḷuga* — "a listener".

> ರೇಡಿಯೋ ಕಾರ್ಯಕ್ರಮಕ್ಕೆ ತುಂಬಾ ಕೇಳುಗರು ಫೋನ್ ಮಾಡಿದರು.
>
> *rēḍiyō kāryakramakke tumbā kēḷugaru phōn māḍidaru.*
>
> Many listeners phoned the radio programme.

Plural: ಕೇಳುಗರು, *kēḷugaru*.

Like ಓದುಗ: ಕೇಳು, to listen, with -ಗ.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಕೇಳುಗ** (*kēḷuga*) is a native Kannada noun: *kēḷu*, "to hear, to listen" (DEDR 2017), with the ending -*ga*, "one who does".
