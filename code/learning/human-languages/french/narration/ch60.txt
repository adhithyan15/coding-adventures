French, chapter 60: Cry, Wash, Cook, Play, Learn.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
pleurer — to cry.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for a teacher, then the French for to laugh.

You'll want to know: pleurer.
pleurer — "to cry".

The word, taken apart.
pleurer from Old French plorer from Latin plorare, "to wail, to weep aloud" — inherited. (EtymDB 2.1).
