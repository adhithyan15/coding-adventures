# પાર (pār) — the far side (of a river or a road)

## You'll want to know: પાર
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C413-WAY413-03]; assesses=[] -->

**પાર** — *pār* — "the far side (of a river or a road)".

> નદી પાર એક નાનું ગામ છે.
>
> *nadī pār ek nānũ gām che.*
>
> There is a small village on the far side of the river.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**પાર** (*pār*) is a tadbhava of Sanskrit *pāra*, "the further bank, the furthest end"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati word (CDIAL 8100).
