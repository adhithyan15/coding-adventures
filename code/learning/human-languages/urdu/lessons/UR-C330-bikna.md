## You'll want to know: بکنا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C330-SAFHA330-04]; assesses=[] -->

**بکنا** — *biknā* — "to be sold; to sell".

> اس کی کتاب جلدی بک گئی۔
>
> *us kī kitāb jaldī bik gaī.*
>
> His book sold quickly.

Past (it …): وہ کتاب بکی, *voh kitāb bikī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *biknā*, "to be sold", under Sanskrit *vikrīyatē* (CDIAL 11642). It is the partner of **بیچنا**: you **بیچنا** a thing, and it **بکنا**.
