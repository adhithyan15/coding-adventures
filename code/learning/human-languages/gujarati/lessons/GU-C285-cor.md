## You'll want to know: ચોર
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C285-CHOR285-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ચોર** — *cor* — "a thief".

> ગઈકાલે રાત્રે એક ચોર અમારા ઘરમાં આવ્યો.
>
> *gaīkāle rātre ek cor amārā gharmā̃ āvyo.*
>
> Last night a thief got into our house.

A masculine or feminine noun, by the person meant.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ચોર** (*cor*) is inherited from Sanskrit *cōrá-*, "a thief", and CDIAL lists the Gujarati word (CDIAL 4931). The verb **ચોરવું**, to steal, belongs to the same family.
