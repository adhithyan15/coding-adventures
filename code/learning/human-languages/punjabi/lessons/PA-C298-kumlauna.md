## You'll want to know: ਕੁਮਲਾਉਣਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C298-THORN298-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ਕੁਮਲਾਉਣਾ** — *kumlāuṇā* — "to wilt; to wither".

> ਤੇਜ਼ ਧੁੱਪ ਵਿੱਚ ਫੁੱਲ ਕੁਮਲਾਉਂਦੇ ਹਨ।
>
> *tez dhupp vich phull kumlāunde han.*
>
> Flowers wilt in strong sunshine.

Past (it …): ਉਹ ਫੁੱਲ ਕੁਮਲਾਇਆ, *oh phull kumlāiā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਕੁਮਲਾਉਣਾ** (*kumlāuṇā*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*kōmh*, "wither"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *kumlāuṇā*, "to wither", under it (CDIAL 3524).
