# अडखळणे (aḍkhaḷṇe) — to stumble

## You'll want to know: अडखळणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C493-TOL493-03]; assesses=[] -->

**अडखळणे** — *aḍkhaḷṇe* — "to stumble".

> दगडाला पाय लागून तो अडखळला.
>
> *dagḍālā pāy lāgūn to aḍkhaḷlā.*
>
> His foot hit a stone and he stumbled.

Past (he …): तो अडखळला, *to aḍkhaḷlā*.

Of feet and of words: बोलताना अडखळणे, to stumble over words.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अडखळणे** (*aḍkhaḷṇe*) is a Marathi verb whose origin is not settled.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
