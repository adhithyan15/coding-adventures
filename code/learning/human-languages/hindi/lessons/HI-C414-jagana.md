## You'll want to know: जगाना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C414-GUSALKHANA414-05]; assesses=[] -->

**जगाना** — *jagānā* — "to wake (someone)".

> मुझे सुबह पाँच बजे जगाइए।
>
> *mujhe subah pā̃c baje jagāiye.*
>
> Please wake me at five in the morning.

Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने मुझे जल्दी जगाया, *usne mujhe jaldī jagāyā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**जगाना** (*jagānā*) is the causative partner of जागना, from Sanskrit *jāgrati*, "are awake" (CDIAL 5175), which lists Hindi *jagānā*: you जागना yourself, and you जगाना someone else.
