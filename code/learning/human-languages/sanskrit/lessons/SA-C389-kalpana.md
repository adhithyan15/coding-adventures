## You'll want to know: कल्पना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C389-SHAKYA389-04]; assesses=[] -->

**कल्पना** — *kalpanā* — "imagination; a fancy".

> यदि कल्पना न अभविष्यत्, तर्हि कविः काव्यं न अकरिष्यत्।
>
> *yadi kalpanā na abhaviṣyat, tarhi kaviḥ kāvyaṃ na akariṣyat.*
>
> Had there been no imagination, the poet would have made no poem.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**कल्पना** (*kalpanā*) comes from the root √kḷp, "to be fit, to form"; MW gives *kalpanā* "creating in the mind, assuming anything to be real, fiction" (MW).
