## You'll want to know: антракт
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C519-CURTAIN-02]; assesses=[] -->

**антракт** (*antrákt*) — "an interval".

> В антракте мы пили кофе в буфете.
>
> *V antrákte my píli kófe v buféte.*
>
> In the interval we had coffee at the bar.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*антракт* was borrowed from French *entr'acte* 'the time between the acts' (EtymDB).
