Russian, chapter 526: Covers and Bookmarks.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
обложка (oblózhka) — a cover (of a book).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to rhyme, then the Russian for by heart.

You'll want to know: обложка.
обложка (oblózhka) — "a cover (of a book)".
Мне понравилась обложка этой книги. Mne ponrávilas oblózhka étoy knígi. I liked the cover of this book.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
обложка (oblózhka) is built on обложить 'to cover, to lay round', from the root of ложить 'to lay'; EtymDB has no entry.

Lesson 3 of 5.
листать (listát) — to leaf through.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a cover, then the Russian for a bookmark.

You'll want to know: листать.
листать (listát) — "to leaf through".
В очереди я листал журнал. V ócheredi ya listál zhurnál. While I was in the queue I leafed through a magazine.
Imperfective; perfective пролистать.

The word, taken apart.
листать (listát) is built on лист 'a leaf, a sheet of paper'; EtymDB lists the verb without a source.

Lesson 4 of 5.
издательство (izdátelstvo) — a publishing house.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a bookmark, then the Russian for to leaf through.

You'll want to know: издательство.
издательство (izdátelstvo) — "a publishing house".
Его роман вышел в большом издательстве. Yegó román výshel v bolshóm izdátelstve. His novel came out with a big publishing house.
Neuter.

The word, taken apart.
издательство (izdátelstvo) is built on издатель 'a publisher', from издать 'to publish', which is built on из- and дать 'to give'; EtymDB lists it, glossed 'publishing house', without a source.
