# ഉൾക്കൊള്ളുക (uḷkkoḷḷuka) — to hold; to take in

## You'll want to know: ഉൾക്കൊള്ളുക
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C456-VIBHAJANAM456-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ഉൾക്കൊള്ളുക** — *uḷkkoḷḷuka* — "to hold; to take in".

> ആ ഹാളിൽ അഞ്ഞൂറ് പേരെ ഉൾക്കൊള്ളാം.
>
> *ā hāḷil aññūṟŭ pēre uḷkkoḷḷāṁ.*
>
> That hall can hold five hundred people.

Past: ഉൾക്കൊണ്ടു, *uḷkkoṇṭu*.

Also to accept an idea: ആ ആശയം അവൻ ഉൾക്കൊണ്ടു, he took that idea to heart.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ഉൾക്കൊള്ളുക** (*uḷkkoḷḷuka*) is a native Malayalam compound: ഉൾ, "inside", and കൊള്ളുക, "to hold, to contain, to receive" (DEDR 2151): to take inside.
