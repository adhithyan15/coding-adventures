## You'll want to know: نجات
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C296-BACHAO296-01]; assesses=[] -->

**نجات** — *najāt* — "deliverance; release (from danger or trouble)".

> صبح تک سب کو پانی سے نجات ملی۔
>
> *subah tak sab ko pānī se najāt milī.*
>
> By morning everyone had been delivered from the water.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**نجات** (*najāt*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *najāt*, "deliverance, escape". The one set free takes **کو** and the danger takes **سے**: **پانی سے نجات ملی**. It is feminine.
