# ಟಿಪ್ಪಣಿ (ṭippaṇi) — a note; a comment

## You'll want to know: ಟಿಪ್ಪಣಿ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C409-PRABANDHA409-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ಟಿಪ್ಪಣಿ** — *ṭippaṇi* — "a note; a comment".

> ಉಪನ್ಯಾಸದ ಸಮಯದಲ್ಲಿ ನಾನು ಟಿಪ್ಪಣಿ ಮಾಡಿಕೊಂಡೆ.
>
> *upanyāsada samayadalli nānu ṭippaṇi māḍikoṇḍe.*
>
> I took notes during the lecture.

ಟಿಪ್ಪಣಿ ಮಾಡಿಕೊ, to take notes for oneself.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಟಿಪ್ಪಣಿ** (*ṭippaṇi*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *ṭippaṇī*, "a gloss, a commentary" (Monier-Williams).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
