## You'll want to know: congelar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C464-GEADA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**congelar** — "to freeze".

> No inverno, a água do lago congela.
>
> *In winter, the water in the lake freezes.*

Also food: *congelar a sopa*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*congelar* is a learned borrowing from Latin *congelāre* 'to freeze together' (EtymDB), on *gelū* 'frost'.
