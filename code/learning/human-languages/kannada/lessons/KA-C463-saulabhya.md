# ಸೌಲಭ್ಯ (saulabhya) — a facility; an amenity

## You'll want to know: ಸೌಲಭ್ಯ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C463-SEVE463-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸೌಲಭ್ಯ** — *saulabhya* — "a facility; an amenity".

> ಲಾಡ್ಜ್ನಲ್ಲಿ ಇಂಟರ್ನೆಟ್ ಸೌಲಭ್ಯ ಇಲ್ಲ.
>
> *lāḍjnalli iṇṭarneṭ saulabhya illa.*
>
> The lodge has no internet facility.

Plural: ಸೌಲಭ್ಯಗಳು, *saulabhyagaḷu*.

ಸೌಲಭ್ಯಗಳು, facilities: water, power, parking.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸೌಲಭ್ಯ** (*saulabhya*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *saulabhya*, "easiness of attainment" (Monier-Williams); in Kannada it names what makes a stay easy.
