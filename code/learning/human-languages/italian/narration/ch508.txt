Italian, chapter 508: In palestra.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
allenarsi — to train, to work out.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for rake, then the Italian for to hoe.

You'll want to know: allenarsi.
allenarsi — "to train, to work out".
Mi alleno tre volte alla settimana. I train three times a week.
The coach is l'allenatore.

The word, taken apart.
allenarsi is built on lena "vigour, breath", with a- and the verb ending; the origin of lena is not settled.

Lesson 3 of 5.
la flessione — push-up; bending.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for to train, then the Italian for training.

You'll want to know: la flessione.
la flessione — "push-up; bending".
Ogni mattina faccio venti flessioni. Every morning I do twenty push-ups.
Also il piegamento. In grammar, la flessione is inflection.

The word, taken apart.
flessione is a learned borrowing from Latin flexiō "a bending" (Lewis & Short), from flectere "to bend".

gli addominali — "abs; sit-ups".
L'allenatore ci fa fare cento addominali. The coach makes us do a hundred sit-ups.
The muscles, and also the exercise: fare gli addominali.
