## You'll want to know: चकला
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C520-AATA520-03]; assesses=[] -->

**चकला** — *caklā* — "a round board for rolling dough".

> चकला और बेलन मेज़ पर रखे हैं।
>
> *Caklā aur belan mez par rakhe haiṁ.*
>
> The rolling board and the rolling pin are on the table.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**चकला** (*caklā*) is a tadbhava, inherited from *\*cakrala*, "round and flat", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 4548).
