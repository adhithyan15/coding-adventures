German, chapter 531: Aufwärmen und abwaschen.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
aufwärmen — to warm up, to reheat.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for bathrobe, then the German for pyjamas.

You'll want to know: aufwärmen.
aufwärmen — "to warm up, to reheat".
Die Suppe von gestern wärmen wir heute auf. We're reheating yesterday's soup today.
Separable. Also in sport: sich aufwärmen — to warm up.

The word, taken apart.
aufwärmen is built on auf "up" and wärmen "to warm", from warm, inherited from Old High German warm, from Proto-Germanic warmaz (EtymDB).

Lesson 2 of 5.
die Mikrowelle — microwave.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for pyjamas, then the German for to warm up.

You'll want to know: die Mikrowelle.
die Mikrowelle — "microwave".
Er macht die Milch in der Mikrowelle warm. He heats the milk in the microwave.
Plural: die Mikrowellen.

The word, taken apart.
Mikrowelle is built on mikro-, from Greek mikrós "small", and Welle "wave" (EtymDB lists Welle); it is usually explained as a loan translation of English microwave.

Lesson 3 of 5.
der Wasserkocher — kettle.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to warm up, then the German for microwave.

You'll want to know: der Wasserkocher.
der Wasserkocher — "kettle".
Der Wasserkocher ist kaputt, deshalb kochen wir das Wasser im Topf. The kettle is broken, so we're boiling the water in a pan.
Plural: die Wasserkocher.

The word, taken apart.
Wasserkocher is built on Wasser "water", inherited from Old High German wazzar, and Kocher "cooker", from kochen, an early borrowing from Latin coquere "to cook" (EtymDB).

Lesson 4 of 5.
abwaschen — to wash up, to do the dishes.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for microwave, then the German for kettle.

You'll want to know: abwaschen.
abwaschen — "to wash up, to do the dishes".
Nach dem Abendessen wäscht mein Bruder ab. After dinner my brother does the washing-up.
Separable, irregular: wusch ab, hat abgewaschen. Also spülen.

The word, taken apart.
abwaschen is built on ab "off" and waschen "to wash", inherited from Old High German wascan, from Proto-Germanic waskaną (EtymDB).

Lesson 5 of 5.
abtrocknen — to dry (dishes, yourself).

Warm-up.

You'll want to know: abtrocknen.
abtrocknen — "to dry (dishes, yourself)".
Ich wasche ab, und du trocknest ab. I wash and you dry.
Separable. Sich abtrocknen — to dry yourself.

The word, taken apart.
abtrocknen is built on ab "off" and trocknen "to dry", from trocken "dry", inherited from Middle High German trucken (EtymDB).
