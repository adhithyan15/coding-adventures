Hindi, chapter 233: North, South, East, West, Right.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
उत्तर (uttar) — north.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for to whisper, then the Hindi for to scold.

You'll want to know: उत्तर.
उत्तर — uttar — "north".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: uttar]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: uttar, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say uttar]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Hindi for to whisper, then the Hindi for to scold, then say uttar again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does उत्तर (uttar) mean? ("North".) Say it once more.
