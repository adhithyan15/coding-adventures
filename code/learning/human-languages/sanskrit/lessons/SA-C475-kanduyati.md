## You'll want to know: कण्डूयति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C475-SVEDA475-04]; assesses=[] -->

**कण्डूयति** — *kaṇḍūyati* — "scratches".

> शुनकः कर्णं कण्डूयति।
>
> *śunakaḥ karṇaṃ kaṇḍūyati.*
>
> The dog scratches its ear.

Past (it …): शुनकः कर्णं कण्डूयितवान्, *śunakaḥ karṇaṃ kaṇḍūyitavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**कण्डूयति** (*kaṇḍūyati*) is built on *kaṇḍū*, "itching, the itch"; MW gives "to scratch, rub" and "to itch" (MW).
