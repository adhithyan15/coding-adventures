## You'll want to know: جرم
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C407-JORM-01]; assesses=[] -->

**جرم** (*jorm*) — "a crime".

> دزدی یک جرم است.
>
> *dozdi yek jorm ast.*
>
> Theft is a crime.

Plural *jarâyem*, an Arabic broken plural. More serious than *takhallof*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**جرم** is usually traced to Arabic *jurm* "offence, crime", from the same Arabic family as *jarime* "a fine".
