## You'll want to know: coller
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C397-FICHIER-04]; assesses=[] -->

**coller** — "to paste; to stick".

> Elle a collé le texte dans son message.
>
> *She pasted the text into her message.*

*Copier-coller* — copy and paste. Informal: *être collé à un examen* — to fail it.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*coller* is built on *colle* "glue", which comes through Vulgar Latin from Greek *kólla* "glue" (EtymDB).
