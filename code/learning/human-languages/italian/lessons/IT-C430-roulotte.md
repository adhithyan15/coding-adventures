## You'll want to know: la roulotte
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C430-TENDA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**la roulotte** — "the caravan".

> I nonni passano l'estate in roulotte.
>
> *My grandparents spend the summer in a caravan.*

Said the French way; towed behind a car. Plural unchanged.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*roulotte* was borrowed from French *roulotte* (EtymDB), built on *rouler* 'to roll', which goes back to Latin *rota* 'wheel'.
