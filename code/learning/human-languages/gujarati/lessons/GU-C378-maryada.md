## You'll want to know: મર્યાદા
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C378-HAD378-02]; assesses=[] -->

**મર્યાદા** — *maryādā* — "propriety; a bound one keeps to".

> જો તમે મર્યાદામાં રહીને બોલશો તો બધા સાંભળશે.
>
> *jo tame maryādāmā̃ rahīne bolśo to badhā sā̃bhaḷśe.*
>
> If you speak within bounds, everyone will listen.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**મર્યાદા** (*maryādā*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *maryādā*, "a frontier, a bound" (Monier-Williams); Gujarati also uses it for the bounds of decent behaviour.
