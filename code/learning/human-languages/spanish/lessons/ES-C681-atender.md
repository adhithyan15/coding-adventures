## You'll want to know: atender
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C681-COMERCIO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**atender** — "to serve (a customer); to attend to; to pay attention".

> ¿Ya le atienden?
>
> *Are you being served?*

The e becomes ie under stress: *atiendo*, *atiende*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*atender* comes from Latin *attendere* "to stretch towards, to give heed" (EtymDB), built on *tendere* "to stretch".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.
