Gujarati, chapter 541: Promises and Trust.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
શપથ (śapath) — a solemn oath.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to hold fast, then the Gujarati for an oath.

You'll want to know: શપથ.
શપથ — śapath — "a solemn oath".
જો તમે શપથ લેશો તો તમારે સાચું બોલવું પડશે. jo tame śapath leśo to tamāre sācũ bolvũ paḍśe. If you take the oath, you will have to tell the truth.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
શપથ (śapath) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit śapatha, "a curse; an oath" (Monier-Williams). One takes it formally: શપથ લેવા.

Lesson 3 of 5.
ભરોસો (bharoso) — confidence (trust in someone).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a solemn oath, then the Gujarati for an undertaking.

You'll want to know: ભરોસો.
ભરોસો — bharoso — "confidence (trust in someone)".
જો તમે વાયદો પાળશો તો લોકો તમારા પર ભરોસો રાખશે. jo tame vāydo pāḷśo to loko tamārā par bharoso rākhśe. If you keep your word, people will trust you.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ભરોસો (bharoso) goes back to a reconstructed bharavaśya, "dependence on support", and CDIAL lists the Gujarati word, "confidence" (CDIAL 9398). One places it on someone: તમારા પર ભરોસો.

[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: છટકબારી.
છટકબારી — chaṭakbārī — "a loophole".
જો નિયમમાં કોઈ છટકબારી હશે તો કોઈ તે નિયમ નહીં પાળે. jo niyammā̃ koī chaṭakbārī haśe to koī te niyam nahī̃ pāḷe. If there is a loophole in the rule, nobody will keep it.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
છટકબારી (chaṭakbārī) is built from છટકવું, "to slip away", which CDIAL files under chaṭṭ (CDIAL 4968), and બારી, "a window": a window to slip out through.

Warm-up.

જો તમને મારો પ્રસ્તાવ ગમશે તો આપણે સાથે કામ કરીશું. jo tamne māro prastāv gamśe to āpaṇe sāthe kām karīśũ. If you like my proposal, we'll work together.

પ્રસ્તાવ (prastāv) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit prastāva, "an introduction, a prelude" (Monier-Williams); in Gujarati it is a proposal put forward.
