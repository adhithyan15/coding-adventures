## You'll want to know: उत्सव
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C332-PARAMPARA332-03]; assesses=[] -->

**उत्सव** — *utsav* — "a celebration; a festival".

> गाँव का उत्सव तीन दिन चला।
>
> *gā̃v kā utsav tīn din calā.*
>
> The village celebration went on for three days.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**उत्सव** (*utsav*) is Sanskrit *utsava*, "a festival, a joyful occasion" (Monier-Williams), the formal word beside **त्योहार**.
