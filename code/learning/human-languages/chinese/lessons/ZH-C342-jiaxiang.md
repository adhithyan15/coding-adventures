## You'll want to know: jiāxiāng
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C342-AUTUMN342-05]; assesses=[] -->

*jiāxiāng* — "hometown; native place".

> *Zhōngqiū de wǎnshang wǒ hěn xiǎng jiāxiāng* — On the night of the moon festival I miss home very much.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*jiāxiāng* is built from 家 'a home' and *xiāng* 'a village', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *kae* and *xjang* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'a village community'.
