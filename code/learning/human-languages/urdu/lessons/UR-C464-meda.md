## You'll want to know: معدہ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C464-ORGAN464-03]; assesses=[] -->

**معدہ** — *medā* — "the stomach as an organ".

> میرا معدہ کمزور ہے۔
>
> *merā medā kamzor hai.*
>
> I have a weak stomach.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**معدہ** (*medā*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *maʿida*, "stomach"; **پیٹ** is the belly you can point to.
