Latin, chapter 231: To turn, To enjoy, To shape, To complain, To be missing.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
vertō, vertere — to turn.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Latin for to announce, then the Latin for to appear.

You'll want to know: vertō, vertere.
vertō, vertere — "to turn".

The word, taken apart.
vertō is inherited, usually traced to Proto-Indo-European wert-, "to turn" (EtymDB 2.1); Sanskrit vart-, "to turn", is a cousin (Lewis & Short), and so is English -ward in words like toward.
