## You'll want to know: दूरध्वनी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C516-PHON516-03]; assesses=[] -->

**दूरध्वनी** — *dūrdhvanī* — "a telephone; a landline".

> कार्यालयातला दूरध्वनी सकाळपासून वाजत होता.
>
> *kāryālayātlā dūrdhvanī sakāḷpāsūn vājat hotā.*
>
> The office telephone had been ringing since morning.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**दूरध्वनी** (*dūrdhvanī*) is a modern tatsama coinage from Sanskrit *dūra*, "far", and *dhvani*, "sound" (Monier-Williams).
