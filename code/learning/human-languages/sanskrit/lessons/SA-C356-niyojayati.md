## You'll want to know: नियोजयति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C356-VIJNAPANA356-02]; assesses=[] -->

**नियोजयति** — *niyojayati* — "appoints".

> राजा नूतनं लिपिकं नियोजयति।
>
> *rājā nūtanaṃ lipikaṃ niyojayati.*
>
> The king appoints a new clerk.

Past (he …): सः सदस्यं नियोजितवान्, *saḥ sadasyaṃ niyojitavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**नियोजयति** (*niyojayati*) is built on *ni*, "down, in", and the root √yuj, "to join", in its causative form; MW gives *ni-√yuj* the senses "to enjoin, to order" and "to appoint, to instal" (MW).
