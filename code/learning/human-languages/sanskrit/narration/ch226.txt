Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
Cotton? (कार्पासः, kārpāsaḥ.) Silk? (कौशेयम्, kauśeyam.) Leather? (चर्म, carma.) A knot? (ग्रन्थिः, granthiḥ.) A small stool? (पीठिका, pīṭhikā.) An instrument? (साधनम्, sādhanam.) A load? (भारः, bhāraḥ.) Goods for sale? (पण्यम्, paṇyam.) A shopkeeper? (आपणिकः, āpaṇikaḥ.) A newspaper? (पत्रिका, patrikā.) A traveller? (यात्रिकः, yātrikaḥ.) A guide? (मार्गदर्शकः, mārgadarśakaḥ.) A holy ford? (तीर्थम्, tīrtham.) A hermitage? (आश्रमः, āśramaḥ.) A monastery? (मठः, maṭhaḥ.)
