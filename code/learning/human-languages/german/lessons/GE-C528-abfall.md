## You'll want to know: der Abfall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C528-MUELL-03]; assesses=[] -->

**der Abfall** — "rubbish, waste".

> Im Park liegt viel Abfall auf dem Boden.
>
> *There's a lot of rubbish on the ground in the park.*

Plural: *die Abfälle*. In speech also *der Müll*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Abfall* is built on *ab* "off" and *Fall* "fall", inherited from Old High German *val* (EtymDB): what falls off.
