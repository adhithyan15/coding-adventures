Marathi, chapter 88: Orange, Lemon, Breakfast, Egg, Yoghurt.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
संत्रे (santre) — an orange.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for oil, then the Marathi for grapes.

You'll want to know: संत्रे.
संत्रे — santre — "an orange".

The word, taken apart.
The origin of संत्रे (santre) is uncertain. It is often linked with the town of Cintra (Sintra) in Portugal, but CDIAL has no entry for it.

Lesson 2 of 5.
लिंबू (limbū) — a lemon.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for grapes, then the Marathi for an orange.

You'll want to know: लिंबू.
लिंबू — limbū — "a lemon".

The word, taken apart.
लिंबू (limbū) goes back to Sanskrit nimbū, "lime", which CDIAL takes from Austroasiatic (CDIAL 7247). CDIAL lists Marathi nīṁbūṁ; the form with l- matches Gujarati lĩbu, "lime, lemon".

Lesson 3 of 5.
नाश्ता (nāshtā) — breakfast.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for an orange, then the Marathi for a lemon.

You'll want to know: नाश्ता.
नाश्ता — nāshtā — "breakfast".

The word, taken apart.
नाश्ता (nāshtā) is a loan, usually traced to Persian nāshtā, "not having eaten". The meal is named for the fast it ends.

Lesson 4 of 5.
अंडे (aṇḍe) — an egg.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a lemon, then the Marathi for breakfast.

You'll want to know: अंडे.
अंडे — aṇḍe — "an egg".

The word, taken apart.
अंडे (aṇḍe) is a tadbhava of Sanskrit āṇḍa, "egg" (CDIAL 1111). CDIAL reports Mayrhofer's view that the word is of Munda origin.

Lesson 5 of 5.
दही (dahī) — yoghurt.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for breakfast, then the Marathi for an egg.

You'll want to know: दही.
दही — dahī — "yoghurt".

The word, taken apart.
दही (dahī) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from dadhi, "thick sour milk" (CDIAL 6146).
