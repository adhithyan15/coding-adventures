## You'll want to know: शुल्क
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C410-PASPORT410-05]; assesses=[] -->

**शुल्क** — *śulk* — "a fee; a duty (formal)".

> नई घड़ी के लिए हमें कस्टम पर शुल्क देना पड़ा।
>
> *naī ghaṛī ke liye hamẽ kasṭam par śulk denā paṛā.*
>
> We had to pay duty at customs on the new watch.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**शुल्क** (*śulk*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *śulka*, "a price" and also a toll or duty taken at roads and ferries (Monier-Williams); offices, customs and the ticket window at a monument all use it for a fee.
