## You'll want to know: la muffa
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C437-RUMORE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**la muffa** — "the mould".

> Sul muro del bagno c'è la muffa.
>
> *There is mould on the bathroom wall.*

*Sapere di muffa* — to smell musty.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*muffa* is usually traced to a Germanic word for mould or a musty smell, but the origin is not settled.
