## You'll want to know: こえる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C394-SAKAMICHI394-05]; assesses=[] -->

**こえる** — *koeru* — "to cross over (a mountain or border)".

> **やまを こえて、となりの まちに いきました。** — *yama o koete, tonari no machi ni ikimashita* — "I crossed the mountain and went to the next town."

Polite **こえます**, *koemasu*; an ichidan verb. Past (polite): **ちちが やまを こえました。**, *chichi ga yama o koemashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**こえる** (*koeru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict).
