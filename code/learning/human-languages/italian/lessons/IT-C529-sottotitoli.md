## You'll want to know: i sottotitoli
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C529-CINEMA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**i sottotitoli** — "subtitles".

> Guardo i film in inglese con i sottotitoli.
>
> *I watch films in English with subtitles.*

The singular *il sottotitolo* is also a subheading.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*sottotitoli* is built from *sotto* "under", from Latin *subtus*, and *titolo* "title", from Latin *titulus* (Lewis & Short).
