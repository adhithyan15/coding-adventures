# nämlich — you see (adding the reason); namely

## You'll want to know: nämlich
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C332-BEGRUENDEN-03]; assesses=[] -->

**nämlich** — "you see (adding the reason); namely".

> Ich komme heute nicht. Ich bin nämlich krank.
>
> *I am not coming today. I am ill, you see.*

*Nämlich* adds the reason after the fact and stands after the verb, not at the start: *Ich bin nämlich krank.* It also means namely: *zwei Farben, nämlich Rot und Blau*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*nämlich* goes back to Middle High German *nemelīch* "by name, expressly", built on *Name* "name". EtymDB has no reliable entry for the adverb, so this follows the usual account: first you named the thing, later the reason.
