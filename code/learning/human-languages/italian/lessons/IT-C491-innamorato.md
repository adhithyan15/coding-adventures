## You'll want to know: l'innamorato
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C491-INNAMORATI-03]; assesses=[] -->

**l'innamorato** — "sweetheart; in love".

> Gli innamorati passeggiano sul lungomare.
>
> *The sweethearts stroll along the seafront.*

As an adjective: *è innamorato di lei* — he is in love with her.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*innamorato* is the past participle of *innamorare* "to make fall in love" (EtymDB), built on *amore* "love", inherited from Latin *amor*.
