## You'll want to know: مخالفت کردن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C361-MOVAFEQAT-02]; assesses=[] -->

**مخالفت کردن** (*mokhâlefat kardan*) — "to oppose, to object to".

> پدرم با سفر من مخالفت کرد.
>
> *pedaram bâ safar-e man mokhâlefat kard.*
>
> My father opposed my trip.

Takes *bâ*: *bâ chizi mokhâlefat kardan*. *Mokhâlefat* "opposition" + *kardan* (stem *kon-*).

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**مخالفت** is derived from *mokhâlef* "opposed, contrary" (EtymDB), a word of Arabic origin, usually traced to Arabic *mukhālafa* "opposition". *Kardan* "to do" is inherited Persian.
