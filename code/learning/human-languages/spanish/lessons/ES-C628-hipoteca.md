## You'll want to know: la hipoteca
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C628-BANCO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**la hipoteca** — "the mortgage".

> Pagamos la hipoteca cada mes.
>
> *We pay the mortgage every month.*

*Hipotecar* — to mortgage.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*hipoteca* is a learned borrowing from Latin *hypotheca*, taken from Greek *hypothēkē* 'a pledge, a deposit', built from *hypo* 'under' and *thēkē* 'a place to put things' (EtymDB).
