# HL107 — Punjabi A2 `ਹੁੰਦਾ` prerequisite

Issue: #18500

After PR #18340 merged, an aggregate-only audit of the preserved confidential
Punjabi A2 mock drafts against exact merged main
`ada380f82e20bdd1aaba0ec6c29188febfb3d2be` finds **ਹੁੰਦਾ** without an
instructional owner. It occurs once in the confidential learner material and
twice in the confidential examiner material. Neither confidential body nor the
remaining candidate set belongs in the public corpus.

Chapter 207 closes that one-form gap through one deliberately narrow frame:
**ਇਹ ਰੋਜ਼ ਹੁੰਦਾ ਹੈ** means “This happens every day.” The voice-first opener
retrieves only already-owned **ਹੋਣਾ** and **ਰੋਜ਼**. One lesson directly owns
exact **ਹੁੰਦਾ**, an immediate lesson retrieves its sound, meaning, and script,
a bounded frame lesson places it between familiar **ਇਹ ਰੋਜ਼** and **ਹੈ**, and a
four-skill payoff retrieves the complete frame again. The target has one direct
owner and three later retrievals. It must not leak into authored,
generated-book, or narration copy before its owner.

The new atom is `PA-FORM-HUNDA-207`. The direct owner presents **ਹੁੰਦਾ** only as
the masculine singular non-perfect/habitual form of already-owned **ਹੋਣਾ**.
The chapter does not teach generic standalone “is,” the progressive,
counterfactual “would,” past **ਹੁੰਦਾ ਸੀ**, feminine or plural forms, or the
wider present-habitual agreement system. Every Gurmukhi codepoint and the
rendered tippi cluster are already model-visible in the earlier script ramp;
this chapter therefore adds no script owner.

Punjabi University Patiala's lexical statistics identifies **ਹੁੰਦਾ** (*hundā*)
as the masculine singular non-perfect form of **ਹੋ**, with continuous or
habitual use: <https://www.learnpunjabi.org/statistics.html>. Its reference
grammar lists **ਹੋਣਾ** with stem **ਹੋ** and gives **ਹੁੰਦਾ** as Present II on
pages 86 and 88:
<https://pt.learnpunjabi.org/assets/A%20Reference%20Grammar_Final.pdf>.
Punjabi University lesson 13 describes the agreeing *-dā* construction with a
present auxiliary: <https://pt.learnpunjabi.org/lesson13.aspx?l=13>. Its verb
reference independently identifies **ਹੈ** as the third-person singular present
auxiliary: <https://www.learnpunjabi.org/verb.html>.

Those sources support the modern form and the one bounded habitual frame. They
do not justify a new etymology for an inflected form: the course reuses the
history already attached to **ਹੋਣਾ**, keeps **ਹੁੰਦਾ** whole, and makes no
broader historical claim.

This is the thirtieth prerequisite tranche exposed by Punjabi A2 mock drafting.
It does not by itself unblock mock 1 and is not mock-readiness, attainment,
external-equivalence, or human-validation evidence. Authored and generated
ownership remains sharded by language, chapter, lesson, or test case; no shared
aggregate is introduced.
