Sanskrit, chapter 309: The Morning News.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
वृत्तान्तः (vṛttāntaḥ) — an account; a report.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a donor, then the Sanskrit for a helper.

You'll want to know: वृत्तान्तः.
वृत्तान्तः — vṛttāntaḥ — "an account; a report".
पत्रिकायां जलौघस्य वृत्तान्तः अस्ति। patrikāyāṃ jalaughasya vṛttāntaḥ asti. There is an account of the flood in the newspaper.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
वृत्तान्तः (vṛttāntaḥ) is built from vṛtta, "what has happened", and anta, "an end"; MW gives vṛttānta as an occurrence or event and also as tidings, a report, a tale (MW).

Lesson 2 of 6.
प्रसारयति (prasārayati) — broadcasts; spreads out.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a helper, then the Sanskrit for an account.

You'll want to know: प्रसारयति.
प्रसारयति — prasārayati — "broadcasts; spreads out".
आकाशवाणी वार्तां प्रसारयति। ākāśavāṇī vārtāṃ prasārayati. The radio broadcasts the news.
Past (it …): आकाशवाणी वार्तां प्रसारितवती, ākāśavāṇī vārtāṃ prasāritavatī.

The word, taken apart.
प्रसारयति (prasārayati) is built from pra and the causative of the root √sṛ, "to run, flow"; MW gives pra-sārayati as to stretch out, extend, spread out, and to broadcast is its modern use (MW).

आकर्णयति — ākarṇayati — "listens to".
पिता प्रातः वार्ताम् आकर्णयति। pitā prātaḥ vārtām ākarṇayati. Father listens to the news in the morning.
Past (he …): सः वार्ताम् आकर्णितवान्, saḥ vārtām ākarṇitavān.

The word, taken apart.
आकर्णयति (ākarṇayati) is built on ā and karṇa, "the ear": MW gives ākarṇayati as a verb made from them, to give ear to, listen (MW).

Lesson 5 of 6.
वार्ताहरः (vārtāharaḥ) — a news reporter.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for listens to, then the Sanskrit for main.

You'll want to know: वार्ताहरः.
वार्ताहरः — vārtāharaḥ — "a news reporter".
वार्ताहरः यामिकं पृच्छति। vārtāharaḥ yāmikaṃ pṛcchati. The reporter questions the watchman.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
वार्ताहरः (vārtāharaḥ) is built from vārtā, "news" (MW writes vārttā), and hara, "carrying", from the root √hṛ; MW gives it as a carrier of tidings, a messenger, and a news reporter is its modern use (MW).
