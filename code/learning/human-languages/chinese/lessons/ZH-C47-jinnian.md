## You'll want to know: 今年
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C47-DATE47-04]; assesses=[] -->

**今年** — *jīnnián* — "this year".

> **我今年十八岁** *wǒ jīnnián shíbā suì* — I'm eighteen this year.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**今年** (*jīnnián*) is built from 今 'now' and 年 'year', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *kim* and *nen* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for times and partings.
