## You'll want to know: मीचणो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C278-KAHANI278-03]; assesses=[] -->

**मीचणो** — *mīchṇo* — "to shut one's eyes".

> छोरो आंख मीचै है अर गिणै है।
>
> *chhoro ā̃kh mīchai hai ar giṇai hai.*
>
> The boy shuts his eyes and counts.

Past (I …): म्हैं आंख मीची, *mhaĩ ā̃kh mīchī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**मीचणो** (*mīchṇo*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*micc*, "close the eyes, blink"; CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry, but lists Hindi *mīcnā* and Gujarati *micvũ*, both "to close the eyes" (CDIAL 10118). In a hiding game the seeker shuts both eyes and counts.
