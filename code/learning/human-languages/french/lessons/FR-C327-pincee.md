## You'll want to know: la pincée
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C327-RECETTE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**la pincée** — "the pinch (of salt, of pepper)".

> Il ajoute une pincée de sel.
>
> *He adds a pinch of salt.*

As much as you hold between two fingers.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*pincée* is built on *pincer* "to pinch", whose origin is not settled (EtymDB lists no parent).
