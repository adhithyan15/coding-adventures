## You'll want to know: ایمان
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C345-QADR345-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ایمان** — *īmān* — "faith; good faith".

> اس کا ایمان مضبوط ہے۔
>
> *us kā īmān mazbūt hai.*
>
> Her faith is strong.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ایمان** (*īmān*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *īmān*, "faith"; **ایماندار**, "honest", is built on it.
