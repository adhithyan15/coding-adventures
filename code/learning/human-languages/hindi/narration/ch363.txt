Hindi, chapter 363: A Study.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
अध्ययन (adhyayan) — a study (a piece of research).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for an analysis, then the Hindi for a trial.

You'll want to know: अध्ययन.
अध्ययन — adhyayan — "a study (a piece of research)".
मीरा बच्चों की नींद पर अध्ययन कर रही है। Mīrā baccoṁ kī nīnd par adhyayan kar rahī hai. Mira is doing a study on children's sleep.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
अध्ययन (adhyayan) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit adhyayana, "reading, studying" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 4 of 5.
तुलना करना (tulnā karnā) — to compare.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for according to, then the Hindi for a sample.

You'll want to know: तुलना करना.
तुलना करना — tulnā karnā — "to compare".
दोनों नमूनों की तुलना करो। donõ namūnoṁ kī tulnā karo. Compare the two samples.
Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने दोनों की तुलना की, usne donõ kī tulnā kī.

The word, taken apart.
तुलना करना (tulnā karnā) joins तुलना to करना, "to do". तुलना is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit tulanā, "equality, likeness" (Monier-Williams).

You'll want to know: आँकना.
आँकना — ā̃knā — "to assess, to estimate (a value)".
बैंक ने घर की क़ीमत आँकी। baiṅk ne ghar kī qīmat ā̃kī. The bank assessed the value of the house.
Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने नुक़सान आँका, usne nuqsān ā̃kā.

The word, taken apart.
आँकना (ā̃knā) is a tadbhava, inherited from Sanskrit aṅkayati, "marks" (CDIAL 104).
