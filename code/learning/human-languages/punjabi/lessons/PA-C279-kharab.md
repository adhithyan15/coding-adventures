## You'll want to know: ਖ਼ਰਾਬ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C279-SWAP279-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ਖ਼ਰਾਬ** — *kharāb* — "faulty; spoilt".

> ਮੇਰਾ ਨਵਾਂ ਫ਼ੋਨ ਖ਼ਰਾਬ ਹੈ।
>
> *merā navāṁ fon kharāb hai.*
>
> My new phone is faulty.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਖ਼ਰਾਬ** (*kharāb*) is a loan, usually traced through Persian to Arabic *kharāb*, "ruin".
