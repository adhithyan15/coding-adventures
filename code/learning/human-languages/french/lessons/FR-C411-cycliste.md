## You'll want to know: le cycliste
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C411-ACCIDENT-04]; assesses=[] -->

**le cycliste** — "the cyclist".

> Le cycliste roulait sur la droite.
>
> *The cyclist was riding on the right.*

Same form for a woman: *une cycliste*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*cycliste* is built on *cycle* 'bicycle', a learned word from Latin *cyclus*, from Greek *kýklos* 'circle, wheel' (Lewis & Short).
