## You'll want to know: recomposé
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C492-FAMILLES-01]; assesses=[] -->

**recomposé** — "blended (of a family)".

> Nous sommes une famille recomposée avec cinq enfants.
>
> *We are a blended family with five children.*

*Une famille recomposée* — parents and children from earlier unions living together.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*recomposé* is built from *re-* and *composé*, from *composer* "to put together", which goes back to Latin *compōnere*, reshaped after *poser*; both rest on *pōnere* "to put" (Lewis & Short).
