You'll want to know: 当.
当 — dāng — "to work as; to be", in a job or a role.
我想当医生 wǒ xiǎng dāng yīshēng — I want to be a doctor.
tā dāng le liǎng nián lǎoshī — He worked as a teacher for two years.
The next lesson puts it in your hand. It is the dāng of dāngshí.

The word, taken apart.
当 (dāng) is inherited from Middle Chinese tang (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription). The dictionary lists the character under two readings, tang and tangH, and glosses tang 'to match', 'to face' and 'to be in charge'; 'to work as' is not given there, so how the word came by it is not settled.

What you've built.
当 takes a job or a role after it: 当医生, dāng lǎoshī.
With a length of time, it follows the pattern you have: dāng le liǎng nián lǎoshī.

Script — the top of 常, then the foot of 雪.
当 (dāng).
Two pieces, and you write both already.
Three short strokes on top, as on top of 常: the short vertical in the middle first, then the dot to its left, then the short left-falling stroke to its right.
Three horizontals joined on the right, as at the foot of 雪: a horizontal that turns down at its right end, a horizontal across the middle, and a horizontal along the foot.
3 + 3 = six.

[once you have stopped driving — cover: write it again from memory, and count the pieces, not the strokes]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
How many strokes in 当 (dāng)? (Six.) Which stroke comes first? (The short vertical in the middle.) Where have you written its lower piece? (At the foot of 雪.)

Lesson 3 of 7.
以后我想… (yǐhòu wǒ xiǎng …) — later, I want to.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Words you have, put together to look ahead.
[once you have stopped driving — read: 我想当医生, aloud — wǒ xiǎng dāng yīshēng]
[your turn — recall: say jiānglái, in the future — R3, durable]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say 以后 — yǐhòu, later — R3]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Grammar Lens: 以后 and 想, looking ahead.
A time word for later, then 想 or 要, says what you want to do or be.
以后我想去中国工作 yǐhòu wǒ xiǎng qù Zhōngguó gōngzuò — Later on I want to go and work in China. 以后你想当什么 yǐhòu nǐ xiǎng dāng shénme — What do you want to be later on?
以后 here is later, some time to come, at the front of the sentence. jiānglái reaches further: some day, in the future.
想 is a wish; 要 is a plan you mean to keep, as in 下个月我要去中国.

Lesson 4 of 7.
mèngxiǎng — a dream for your life.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: last lesson's plan, and a word for what happens in sleep.
[once you have stopped driving — read: 以后我想去中国工作, aloud — yǐhòu wǒ xiǎng qù Zhōngguó gōngzuò]
[your turn — recall: say zuòmèng, to dream — R3, durable]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

You'll want to know: mèngxiǎng.
mèngxiǎng — "a dream", the one you have for your life.
wǒ de mèngxiǎng shì dāng yīshēng — My dream is to be a doctor.
Its first character comes later. The second is the 想 you write.

The word, taken apart.
mèngxiǎng is built from mèng 'a dream' and 想 'to think', both inherited from Middle Chinese. The Guangyun lists the first under two readings, mjuwng and mjuwngH, and under the second describes the spirit wandering in sleep; 想 is sjangX, glossed 'to think' (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription).

What you've built.
zuòmèng is dreaming in your sleep; mèngxiǎng is the dream you have for your life.
It is a noun, and 是 links it to the dream itself: wǒ de mèngxiǎng shì ….

mùbiāo — a goal.

You'll want to know: mùbiāo.
mùbiāo — "a goal; a target".
wǒ de mùbiāo shì shuō hǎo Hànyǔ — My goal is to speak Chinese well.
Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
mùbiāo is built from mù 'the eye' and biāo 'a mark', both inherited from Middle Chinese, mjuwk and pjiew (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'the eye' and the second 'to raise' and 'the tip of a tree'. 'A goal' for the pair is its own sense.

What you've built.
mèngxiǎng is what you hope for; mùbiāo is what you aim at, and can reach step by step.

Grammar Lens: 最, the most of all.
最 before 喜欢 or an adjective picks out the top of the group.
我最喜欢中国菜 wǒ zuì xǐhuan Zhōngguó cài — I like Chinese food best. 这家书店最大 zhè jiā shūdiàn zuì dà — This bookshop is the biggest.
zuì you have said since the chapter on order; now it prints. 比 sets two things side by side, 我比他大; 最 needs no second thing: it is the most of all.
The story on the mountain ended on it: 那天是我最开心的一天.

The exchange.
A: 以后你想当什么 yǐhòu nǐ xiǎng dāng shénme — What do you want to be later on? B: 我想当医生 wǒ xiǎng dāng yīshēng — I want to be a doctor. A: 为什么 wèishénme — Why? B: 因为我妈妈是医生 yīnwèi wǒ māma shì yīshēng — Because my mother is a doctor. A: nǐ de mèngxiǎng shì shénme — What's your dream? B: wǒ de mèngxiǎng shì qù Zhōngguó gōngzuò — My dream is to work in China. A: 你去过中国吗 nǐ qù guo Zhōngguó ma — Have you been to China? B: 去过 我最喜欢中国菜 qù guo, wǒ zuì xǐhuan Zhōngguó cài — I have. I like Chinese food best.
当 (dāng) answers 当什么; 因为 answers 为什么; and B's last line looks back with 过 before it looks forward again.

Reading — the exchange at once.
[once you have stopped driving — read: the exchange through once without stopping, then answer the question below]
[question — say your answer, then pause 12 seconds]
Why does B want to be a doctor?

我最喜欢中国菜 wǒ zuì xǐhuan Zhōngguó cài — the best of all.
Listen. Yǐhòu nǐ xiǎng zuò shénme gōngzuò?
Speak. Say what you want to be or do later, and one goal on the way.
