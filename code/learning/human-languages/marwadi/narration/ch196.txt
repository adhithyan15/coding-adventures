Marwadi (Marwari), chapter 196: At the Post.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
डाक (ḍāk) — the post; mail.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for remaining, then the Marwadi for to be left over.

You'll want to know: डाक.
डाक — ḍāk — "the post; mail".
आज डाक कोनी आई। āj ḍāk konī āī. The post didn't come today.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
डाक (ḍāk) is inherited from a reconstructed ḍakka, "relay, post". CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry, but lists Hindi and Gujarati ḍāk, "relay, post, posting stage" (CDIAL 5519).

Lesson 4 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for an envelope, then the Marwadi for a parcel.

You'll want to know: ठिकाणो.
ठिकाणो — ṭhikāṇo — "an address; whereabouts".
म्हारो ठिकाणो चिट्ठी माथै है। mhāro ṭhikāṇo chiṭṭhī māthai hai. My address is on the letter.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ठिकाणो (ṭhikāṇo) is inherited from a reconstructed ṭhīkka, "firm, right"; CDIAL lists Old Marwari ṭhīka, "trace, whereabouts", and Hindi ṭhikānā under it (CDIAL 5503).

Lesson 5 of 5.
मोकलणो (mokalṇo) — to send off.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for a parcel, then the Marwadi for an address.

You'll want to know: मोकलणो.
मोकलणो — mokalṇo — "to send off".
हूं काल पारसल मोकलसूं। hū̃ kāl pārsal mokalsū̃. I'll send the parcel off tomorrow.
Past (I …): म्हैं चिट्ठी मोकली, mhaĩ chiṭṭhī moklī.

The word, taken apart.
मोकलणो (mokalṇo) is inherited from a reconstructed mukna, "loosed"; CDIAL lists the Old Marwari verb mokaï, "sends", and Sindhi mokilaṇu, "to send", with the l this spelling keeps (CDIAL 10157): to send a thing is to let it go.
