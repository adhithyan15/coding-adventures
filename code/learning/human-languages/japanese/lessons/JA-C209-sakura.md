## You'll want to know: さくら

**さくら** — *sakura* — "a cherry tree; cherry blossom".

> **らいしゅう さくらが さきます。** — *raishū sakura ga sakimasu* — "The cherry trees will bloom next week."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**さくら** (*sakura*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi, and its older origin is not settled (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
