Sanskrit, chapter 70: Nail, Knee and Shoulder.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
नखः (nakhaḥ) — a nail.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for the forehead, then the Sanskrit for the tongue.

You'll want to know: नखः.
नखः (nakhaḥ) — "a nail", at the tip of every finger.

What you've built.
The end of a finger.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: nakhaḥ]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: nakhaḥ, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say nakhaḥ]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Sanskrit for the forehead, then the Sanskrit for the tongue, then say nakhaḥ again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does नखः (nakhaḥ) mean? ("A nail".) Say it once more.
