Kannada, chapter 106: See You Soon.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
ವಿದಾಯ (vidāya) — farewell.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for love, then the Kannada for appreciation.

You'll want to know: ವಿದಾಯ.
ವಿದಾಯ (vidāya) — "farewell", a formal parting word for speeches and letters.

What you've built.
The formal goodbye.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: vidāya]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: vidāya, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say vidāya]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Kannada for love, then the Kannada for appreciation, then say vidāya again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does ವಿದಾಯ (vidāya) mean? ("Farewell".) Say it once more.

Lesson 4 of 7.
ಫೋನ್ ಮಾಡಿ (phōn māḍi) — please phone.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for today, then the Kannada for later.

You'll want to know: ಫೋನ್ ಮಾಡಿ.
ಫೋನ್ ಮಾಡಿ (phōn māḍi) — "please phone (me)", literally "please do a phone". ಮಾಡಿ is the polite form of ಮಾಡು, "do".

What you've built.
A way to stay in touch after goodbye.

Lesson 5 of 7.
ಸಿಗುತ್ತೇನೆ (siguttēne) — I'll see you.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for later, then the Kannada for please phone.

You'll want to know: ಸಿಗುತ್ತೇನೆ.
ಸಿಗುತ್ತೇನೆ (siguttēne) — "I'll see you", literally "I will be met". It is the same verb as ಸಿಗೋಣ (sigōṇa), "let's meet".
ನಾಳೆ ಸಿಗುತ್ತೇನೆ (nāḷe siguttēne) — "I'll see you tomorrow".

What you've built.
The last word of the tranche: a promise to meet.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: siguttēne]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: siguttēne, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say nāḷe siguttēne]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Kannada for later, then the Kannada for please phone, then say siguttēne again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does ಸಿಗುತ್ತೇನೆ (siguttēne) mean? ("I'll see you".) Say it once more.


Lesson 6 of 7.
Out and about — a first pass.

Warm-up.
[pause 3 seconds]
Ask a friend to phone you, and promise to see them tomorrow.

Guided Practice.
[pause 2 seconds after each]
[your turn — say: name boredom, enthusiasm, shyness, alarm and peace of mind]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: take a breath; name skin and blood; say you have a stomach ache; ask someone's weight]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: ask for the map and one ticket; ask where the bus stop is; ask if it is near]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: please open the door; please close it; please stop here; please call the doctor; it's urgent!]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
Boredom? (ಬೇಸರ.) Enthusiasm? (ಉತ್ಸಾಹ.) Shyness? (ನಾಚಿಕೆ.) Alarm? (ಗಾಬರಿ.) Peace of mind? (ನೆಮ್ಮದಿ.) Breath, skin, blood? (ಉಸಿರು, ಚರ್ಮ, ರಕ್ತ.) Stomach ache? (ಹೊಟ್ಟೆನೋವು.) Weight? (ತೂಕ.) Map, ticket, bus, station? (ನಕ್ಷೆ, ಟಿಕೆಟ್, ಬಸ್, ನಿಲ್ದಾಣ.) Near? (ಹತ್ತಿರ.) Open, close, stop, call? (ತೆರೆಯಿರಿ, ಮುಚ್ಚಿ, ನಿಲ್ಲಿಸಿ, ಕರೆಯಿರಿ.) Urgent? (ತುರ್ತು.)


Lesson 7 of 7.

Warm-up.

Guided Practice.
[pause 2 seconds after each]
[your turn — say: I want a different one; I made a mistake; everything is fine; only one; for no reason]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: name your city, your country and a state]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: name business, a worker, the police, a driver and a shop]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
Other? (ಬೇರೆ.) Wrong? (ತಪ್ಪು.) All? (ಎಲ್ಲಾ.) Only one? (ಒಂದೇ.) For no reason? (ಸುಮ್ಮನೆ.) House, street, city, country, state? (ಮನೆ, ಬೀದಿ, ನಗರ, ದೇಶ, ರಾಜ್ಯ.) Business, worker, police, driver, shop? (ವ್ಯಾಪಾರ, ಕೆಲಸಗಾರ, ಪೊಲೀಸ್, ಚಾಲಕ, ಅಂಗಡಿ.) Best wishes? (ಶುಭಾಶಯಗಳು.) Festival? (ಹಬ್ಬ.) Hospitality? (ಆತಿಥ್ಯ.) Love? (ಪ್ರೀತಿ.) Appreciation? (ಮೆಚ್ಚುಗೆ.) Farewell? (ವಿದಾಯ (vidāya).) Today? (ಇವತ್ತು (ivattu).) Later? (ನಂತರ (nantara).) Please phone? (ಫೋನ್ ಮಾಡಿ (phōn māḍi).) I'll see you? (ಸಿಗುತ್ತೇನೆ (siguttēne).)
