## You'll want to know: curar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C488-CURA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**curar** — "to cure, to heal".

> O xarope não curou a tosse, mas ajudou.
>
> *The syrup did not cure the cough, but it helped.*

*Curar-se* — to get well.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*curar* is inherited from Latin *cūrāre* "to take care of" (EtymDB), built on *cūra* "care".
