## You'll want to know: प्रवाहः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C302-JALAUGHA302-03]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रवाहः** — *pravāhaḥ* — "a current".

> नद्याः प्रवाहः वेगेन वहति।
>
> *nadyāḥ pravāhaḥ vegena vahati.*
>
> The river's current flows fast.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रवाहः** (*pravāhaḥ*) is built from *pra*, "forth", and the root √vah, "to carry, flow", as in *pravahati*, "flows"; MW gives *pravāha* as a stream or current (MW).
