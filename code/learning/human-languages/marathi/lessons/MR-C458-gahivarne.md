## You'll want to know: गहिवरणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C458-DILASA458-05]; assesses=[] -->

**गहिवरणे** — *gahivarṇe* — "to be choked up; to be overcome with emotion".

> मुलीचं भाषण ऐकून वडील गहिवरले.
>
> *mulīcaṁ bhāṣaṇ aikūn vaḍīl gahivarle.*
>
> Father was choked with emotion listening to his daughter's speech.

Past (he …): तो गहिवरला, *to gahivarlā*.

Tears near, voice thick: गहिवरून येणे, to be overcome.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**गहिवरणे** (*gahivarṇe*) is a Marathi verb whose origin is not settled.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
