Gujarati, chapter 224: At the doctor's.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
દાક્તર (dāktar) — doctor.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for common cold, then the Gujarati for to sneeze.

You'll want to know: દાક્તર.
દાક્તર — dāktar — "doctor".
શું દાક્તર આજે અહીં છે? — śũ dāktar āje ahī̃ che? — "Is the doctor here today?"
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
દાક્તર (dāktar) is a loan, usually traced to English doctor; EtymDB lists Persian dāktar as the go-between.

Lesson 2 of 6.

You'll want to know: દર્દી.
દર્દી — dardī — "patient".
શું દર્દી ઘરે છે? — śũ dardī ghare che? — "Is the patient at home?"
A man or a woman.

The word, taken apart.
દર્દી (dardī) is built on dard, "pain", a loan usually traced to Persian dard, with the suffix -ī, "one who has". CDIAL has no entry for it.

Lesson 3 of 6.
નાડી (nāḍī) — pulse.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for doctor, then the Gujarati for patient.

You'll want to know: નાડી.
નાડી — nāḍī — "pulse".
શું દાક્તર નાડી જુએ છે? — śũ dāktar nāḍī jue che? — "Is the doctor taking the pulse?"
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
નાડી (nāḍī) comes from Sanskrit nāḍī, "tubular stalk, tubular organ, tube". CDIAL lists Gujarati nāṛī, "pulse", and nāṛ, "vein, pulse" (CDIAL 7047).

Lesson 4 of 6.
ધબકવું (dhabakvũ) — to throb, to beat.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for patient, then the Gujarati for pulse.

You'll want to know: ધબકવું.
ધબકવું — dhabakvũ — "to throb, to beat".
શું તેનું હૃદય ધબકે છે? — śũ tenũ hṛday dhabke che? — "Is his heart beating?"

The word, taken apart.
The origin of ધબકવું (dhabakvũ) is not settled. It is probably imitative of the beat, like dhaṛakvũ, "to throb", which CDIAL lists under dhaḍ, "throb, beat" (CDIAL 6711).

Lesson 5 of 6.
શ્વાસ (śvās) — breath.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for pulse, then the Gujarati for to throb.

You'll want to know: શ્વાસ.
શ્વાસ — śvās — "breath".
શું તે શ્વાસ લે છે? — śũ te śvās le che? — "Is he breathing?"
A masculine noun. With લેવું it means to breathe.

The word, taken apart.
શ્વાસ (śvās) is Sanskrit śvāsa, "breath, breathing", taken over whole as a learned loan, a tatsama. The inherited form of the same word is Gujarati sās, "breath" (CDIAL 12769).
