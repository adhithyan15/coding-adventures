## You'll want to know: l'éponge
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C528-MENAGE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**l'éponge** — "sponge".

> Il nettoie la table de la cuisine avec une éponge.
>
> *He wipes down the kitchen table with a sponge.*

Feminine. *Passer l'éponge* — to let bygones be bygones.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*l'éponge* is inherited, through Old French *esponge*, from Latin *spongia* (EtymDB), a borrowing from Greek *spongiá* "a sponge".
