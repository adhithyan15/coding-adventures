# मी गेलो (mī gelo) — the past, one small ending at a time

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MR-LEX-C159-PAS159-05, MR-LEX-MI, MR-LEX-JANE] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say **घडणे**, "to happen", then **मी**, "I", and **जाणे**, "to go".

## You'll want to know: who did it?
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-GRAMMAR-C159-PAST-PERSON-01]; assesses=[MR-LEX-MI, MR-LEX-TU-TUMHI, MR-LEX-TO-TI-TE, MR-LEX-JANE] -->

> **काल मी पुण्याला गेलो.**
>
> *kāl mī puṇyālā gelo.* — "Yesterday I went to Pune."

The past shows who did it. A man says **मी गेलो**; a woman says **मी गेले**.
With **तो / ती**, use **तो गेला / ती गेली**. With respect or several people,
use **तुम्ही गेलात**. Hear the same gender endings you already know from
**माझा / माझी / माझं**, now after **-ल-**.

## You'll want to know: a few useful stems
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-GRAMMAR-C159-PAST-STEMS-01]; assesses=[MR-LEX-YENE, MR-LEX-BASNE, MR-LEX-JHOPNE, MR-LEX-RAHNE, MR-LEX-C84-VERBS84-04, MR-GRAMMAR-NEGATE-NAHI] -->

Most verbs put **-ल-** onto the stem: **बसणे → बसलो**, **झोपणे → झोपलो**,
**पडणे → पडला**. Two everyday verbs change more: **येणे → आलो / आली** and
**होणे → झाला / झाली**. Put **नाही** after the completed form for "did not":
**मी गेलो नाही**.

[PAUSE 1s]
- [YOU SAY: "Yesterday I went to Pune" — *kāl mī puṇyālā gelo* or *gele*]
- [YOU SAY: "She came home in the evening" — *tī sandhyākāḷī gharī ālī*]
- [YOU SAY: "We sat. We slept." — *āmhī baslo. āmhī jhoplo.*]

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MR-GRAMMAR-C159-PAST-PERSON-01, MR-GRAMMAR-C159-PAST-STEMS-01] -->

[PAUSE 3s] "I went"? (**मी गेलो**, or **मी गेले**.) "He went"? (**तो गेला**.)
"She came"? (**ती आली**.) "I did not go"? (**मी गेलो नाही**.)
