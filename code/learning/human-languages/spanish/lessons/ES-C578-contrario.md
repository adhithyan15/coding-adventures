## You'll want to know: contrario
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C578-DESACUER-05]; assesses=[] -->

**contrario** — "opposed, against; opposite".

> Mi hermano es contrario a la idea.
>
> *My brother is against the idea.*

*Al contrario* — on the contrary. *Lo contrario* — the opposite.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*contrario* is a learned borrowing from Latin *contrarius* 'opposite', built on *contra* 'against' (Lewis & Short).
