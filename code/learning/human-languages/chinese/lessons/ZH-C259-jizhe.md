## You'll want to know: jìzhě
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C259-JOB259-04]; assesses=[] -->

*jìzhě* — "journalist; reporter".

> *tā shì yí ge jìzhě* — He's a journalist.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*jìzhě* is built from *jì* 'to record' and *zhě* 'one who', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *kiH* and *tsyaeX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to note down' and calls the second a particle.
