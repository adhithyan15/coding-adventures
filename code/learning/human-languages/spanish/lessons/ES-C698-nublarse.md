## You'll want to know: nublarse
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C698-CIELO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**nublarse** — "to cloud over".

> Por la tarde se nubló.
>
> *It clouded over in the afternoon.*

Also of the eyes or the mind: *se le nubló la vista*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*nublarse* comes from Latin *nūbilāre* "to be cloudy" (EtymDB), built on *nūbilus* "cloudy", from *nūbēs* "cloud".
