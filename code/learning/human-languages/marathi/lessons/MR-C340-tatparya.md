## You'll want to know: तात्पर्य
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C340-RAHASYA340-02]; assesses=[] -->

**तात्पर्य** — *tātparya* — "the moral; the gist".

> या गोष्टीचं तात्पर्य असं आहे की खोटं बोलू नये.
>
> *yā goṣṭīcaṁ tātparya asaṁ āhe kī khoṭaṁ bolū naye.*
>
> The moral of this story is that one should not lie.

Neuter.

Children's stories end with तात्पर्य, the moral.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**तात्पर्य** (*tātparya*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *tātparya*, 'aim, purport' (Monier-Williams).
