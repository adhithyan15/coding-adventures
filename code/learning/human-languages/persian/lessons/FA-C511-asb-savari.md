## You'll want to know: اسب سواری
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C511-OUTDOORS-03]; assesses=[] -->

**اسب سواری** (*asb savâri*) — "horse riding".

> او در روستا اسب سواری یاد گرفت.
>
> *u dar rustâ asb savâri yâd gereft.*
>
> She learned to ride in the village.

*asb* 'horse' + *savâri* 'riding' (*savâr* 'mounted').

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*asb savâri* is built on two native words. *asb*, 'horse', is inherited, and EtymDB lists Latin *equus* and Greek *hippos* as cousins (EtymDB); *savâri* is built on *savâr*, 'mounted'.
