## You'll want to know: qǔqián
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C195-BANK195-02]; assesses=[] -->

*qǔqián* — "to take out money".

> *wǒ yào qù yínháng qǔqián* — I'm going to the bank to get some money out.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*qǔqián* is built from *qǔ* 'to take' and 钱 'money', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *tshjuX* and *dzjen* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to gather in' and 'to receive'.
