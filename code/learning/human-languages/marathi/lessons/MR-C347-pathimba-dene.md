# पाठिंबा देणे (pāṭhimbā deṇe) — to support; to back

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Marathi for to reject, then the Marathi for to oppose.

## You'll want to know: पाठिंबा देणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C347-VIRODH347-02]; assesses=[] -->

**पाठिंबा देणे** — *pāṭhimbā deṇe* — "to support; to back".

> माझ्या मित्रांनी मला पूर्ण पाठिंबा दिला.
>
> *mājhyā mitrānnī malā pūrṇa pāṭhimbā dilā.*
>
> My friends gave me full support.

Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने पाठिंबा दिला, *tyāne pāṭhimbā dilā*.

The person backed takes -ला: मला पाठिंबा दिला.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पाठिंबा देणे** (*pāṭhimbā deṇe*) joins पाठिंबा, "backing", a Marathi noun whose origin is not settled, to देणे, "to give".
