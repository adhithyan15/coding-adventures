# (una oferta y una despedida) — read it, then do it yourself

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-C445-PUESTO-02, ES-LEX-C445-PUESTO-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say *la responsabilidad* and *jubilarse*. The two texts below are
about the same desk: one person is leaving it and somebody else is wanted for it.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-C445-PUESTO-02, ES-LEX-C445-PUESTO-03, ES-LEX-C445-PUESTO-04] -->

Read both, then answer.

**1 — oferta de empleo**

> Se busca persona **responsable** para el turno de tarde. Se **exige**
> experiencia previa y se valorará saber **enseñar** a personal nuevo. Contrato
> fijo. Interesados, enviar currículum antes del día 30.

**2 — una nota en el tablón**

> Después de treinta y un años, me **jubilo** el viernes. Ha sido un placer.
> Dejo el turno en buenas manos. Os espero a todos a las seis en el bar de
> enfrente.

[PAUSE 3s]
- [YOU SAY: what the job requires, and what it merely prefers]
- [YOU SAY: how long the second person has worked there]
- [YOU SAY: which of the two texts has a person in it]

## Why it's said this way
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-C445-PUESTO-03, ES-LEX-C445-PUESTO-04, ES-LEX-C445-PUESTO-05] -->

The advert separates two things English often blurs.

**Se exige** experiencia — it is *required*, and without it you are not eligible.
**Se valorará** saber enseñar — it will be *valued*, a point in your favour and
nothing more. A reader who treats those as the same sentence either rules
themselves out for no reason or applies for something they cannot do.

That distinction is the single most useful thing on the page, and it is carried
entirely by the verb.

Notice too that the advert has **no people**: *se busca*, *se exige*, *se
valorará*, and even *interesados* is a plural adjective standing in for a noun.
The leaving note is the opposite — *me jubilo*, *dejo*, *os espero*, first
person throughout. One desk, two registers, and you can now read both.

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-C445-PUESTO-01, ES-LEX-C445-PUESTO-02, ES-LEX-C445-PUESTO-03, ES-LEX-C445-PUESTO-04, ES-LEX-C445-PUESTO-05] -->

[PAUSE 3s] Now your turn, out loud and then in writing. Five sentences applying
for that job: say you are responsible, say you have the experience they require,
say you have taught new staff before, ask them to repeat the closing date, and
say you are sorry the other person is retiring.

Then check yourself: did you keep **se exige** and **se valorará** apart?
