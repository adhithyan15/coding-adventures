# సంఘీభావం (saṅghībhāvaṁ) — solidarity

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[TE-LEX-C409-AIKYATA409-02, TE-LEX-C409-AIKYATA409-03] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Telugu for harmony, then the Telugu for unity.

## You'll want to know: సంఘీభావం
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C409-AIKYATA409-04]; assesses=[] -->

**సంఘీభావం** — *saṅghībhāvaṁ* — "solidarity".

> రైతులకు విద్యార్థులు సంఘీభావం తెలిపారు.
>
> *raitulaku vidyārthulu saṅghībhāvaṁ telipāru.*
>
> The students expressed solidarity with the farmers.

సంఘీభావం తెలుపు, to show solidarity; the group takes -కి.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**సంఘీభావం** (*saṅghībhāvaṁ*) is built from Sanskrit *saṁgha*, "an association" (CDIAL 12854), and *bhāva*, "being, state" (CDIAL 9475): the state of standing together.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
