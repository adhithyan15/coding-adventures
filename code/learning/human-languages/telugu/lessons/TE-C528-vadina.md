## You'll want to know: వదిన
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C528-TOBUTTUVU528-05]; assesses=[] -->

**వదిన** — *vadina* — "an elder brother's wife".

> మా వదిన చాలా బాగా వంట చేస్తుంది.
>
> *mā vadina cālā bāgā vaṇṭa cēstundi.*
>
> My elder brother's wife cooks very well.

Also a husband's or a wife's elder sister.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**వదిన** (*vadina*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 4690 lists Telugu *vadine*, "an elder sister-in-law, an elder brother's wife".
