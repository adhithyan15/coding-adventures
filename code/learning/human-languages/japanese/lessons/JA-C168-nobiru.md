# のびる (nobiru) — to be put off; to grow longer

## You'll want to know: のびる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C168-DELAY168-05]; assesses=[] -->

**のびる** — *nobiru* — "to be put off; to grow longer".

> **まつりは らいしゅうに のびます。** — *matsuri wa raishū ni nobimasu* — "The festival will be put off until next week."

Polite **のびます**, *nobimasu*; an ichidan verb. Its transitive partner is **のばす**, *nobasu*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**のびる** (*nobiru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi, and its older origin is not settled (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
