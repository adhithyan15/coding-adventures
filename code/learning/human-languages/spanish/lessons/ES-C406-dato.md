# el dato — one thing given as information

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-C305-NAME-01, ES-LEX-DIRECCION] -->

[PAUSE 2s] A form may ask for your name and address: *el nombre, la dirección*.
Each answer is **un dato**.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-DATO]; assesses=[] -->

**Dato** comes straight from Latin **datum**, “something given.” English kept
**datum** for one item and **data** for several; Spanish made the everyday noun
**el dato**, plural **los datos**.

A name, address or telephone number can each be **un dato**. Together they are
**los datos personales**, personal details.

## Grammar Lens: one or several
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-DATO, ES-LEX-C305-NAME-01, ES-LEX-DIRECCION] -->

- **un dato** — one piece of information
- **los datos** — the information / details
- **nombre y dirección: dos datos** — name and address: two details
