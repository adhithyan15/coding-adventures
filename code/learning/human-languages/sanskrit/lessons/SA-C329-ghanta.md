## You'll want to know: घण्टा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C329-PUJA329-02]; assesses=[] -->

**घण्टा** — *ghaṇṭā* — "a bell".

> मन्दिरस्य घण्टायाः शब्दः मधुरः अस्ति।
>
> *mandirasya ghaṇṭāyāḥ śabdaḥ madhuraḥ asti.*
>
> The sound of the temple bell is sweet.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**घण्टा** (*ghaṇṭā*) MW gives *ghaṇṭā* as a bell, and a metal plate struck as a gong, without a derivation, so its origin is uncertain (MW).
