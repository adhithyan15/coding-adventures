## You'll want to know: اصرار کرنا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C374-SAHARA374-05]; assesses=[] -->

**اصرار کرنا** — *isrār karnā* — "to insist".

> اگر آپ اصرار کرتے ہیں تو میں آؤں گا۔
>
> *agar āp isrār karte haiṅ to maiṅ āūṅ gā.*
>
> If you insist, I will come.

Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے آنے پر اصرار کیا, *us ne āne par isrār kiyā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**اصرار کرنا** (*isrār karnā*) is built from *isrār*, usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *iṣrār*, "persisting", and **کرنا**, "to do".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
