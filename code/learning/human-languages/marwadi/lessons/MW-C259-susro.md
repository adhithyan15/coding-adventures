## You'll want to know: सुसरो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C259-SASU259-02]; assesses=[] -->

**सुसरो** — *susro* — "a father-in-law".

> म्हारो सुसरो खेत मांय है।
>
> *mhāro susro khet mā̃y hai.*
>
> My father-in-law is in the field.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सुसरो** (*susro*) is inherited from Sanskrit *śvaśura*, "father-in-law". CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry; it lists Hindi *susrā* (CDIAL 12753).
