Tamil, chapter 172: Future, Tonight, Suddenly, In advance, Calendar.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
எதிர்காலம் (etirkālam) — the future.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for never, then the Tamil for a delay.

You'll want to know: எதிர்காலம்.
எதிர்காலம் — etirkālam — "the future".

The word, taken apart.
எதிர்காலம் (etirkālam) is native etir, "in front" (DEDR 795), and kālam, "time", from Sanskrit kāla (CDIAL 3084): "the time in front".
