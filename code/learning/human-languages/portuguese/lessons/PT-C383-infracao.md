## You'll want to know: a infração
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C383-EXCECAO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**a infração** — "the offence, the infringement (of a rule)".

> Estacionar ali é uma infração.
>
> *Parking there is an offence.*

AO90 *infração*; older *infracção*. *Infração de trânsito* — traffic offence.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*infração* is a learned borrowing from Latin *īnfrāctiō* "a breaking" (Lewis & Short), from *īnfringere* "to break, to infringe", built on *frangere* "to break".
