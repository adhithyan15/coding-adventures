## You'll want to know: irritar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C475-NERVOS-02]; assesses=[] -->

**irritar** — "to irritate, to annoy".

> O barulho da rua irrita-me.
>
> *The noise from the street annoys me.*

*Irritar-se* — to get annoyed: *irrito-me com facilidade*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*irritar* is a learned borrowing from Latin *irrītāre* "to provoke, to stir up" (EtymDB).
