## You'll want to know: мемуары
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C524-GENRES-05]; assesses=[] -->

**мемуары** (*memuáry*) — "memoirs".

> Старый актёр написал мемуары.
>
> *Stáryy aktyór napisál memuáry.*
>
> The old actor wrote his memoirs.

Plural only.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*мемуары* was borrowed from French *mémoires* 'memoirs' (EtymDB), which is usually traced to Latin *memoria* 'memory'.
