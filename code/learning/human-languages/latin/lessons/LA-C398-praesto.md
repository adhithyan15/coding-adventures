## You'll want to know: praestō, praestāre
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C398-PRAESTO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**praestō, praestāre** — "to make good, to fulfil; to excel".

> Fidem praestitit.
>
> *He kept his word.*

*praestō, praestāre, praestitī, praestitum*. *Fidem praestāre*, to make one's word good; with the dative, to stand out above: *omnibus praestat*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*praestō* is built from *prae* "before, in front" and *stō* "to stand" (Lewis & Short): to stand out in front, then to stand good for a promise.
