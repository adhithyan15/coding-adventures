## You'll want to know: માર્ગ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C413-WAY413-02]; assesses=[] -->

**માર્ગ** — *mārg* — "a route; a way (formal)".

> શહેરમાં જવાનો સૌથી ટૂંકો માર્ગ કયો છે?
>
> *śahermā̃ javāno sauthī ṭū̃ko mārg kayo che?*
>
> What is the shortest route into the city?

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**માર્ગ** (*mārg*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *mārga*, first the track of a wild animal and then any road or way (Monier-Williams); signs and books use it where speech says રસ્તો.
