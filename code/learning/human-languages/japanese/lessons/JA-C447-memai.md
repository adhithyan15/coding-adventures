## You'll want to know: めまい
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C447-TIRED447-01]; assesses=[] -->

**めまい** — *memai* — "dizziness".

> **あさ おきた とき、めまいが しました。** — *asa okita toki, memai ga shimashita* — "When I got up this morning, I felt dizzy."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**めまい** (*memai*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese. JMdict marks it as usually written in kana and lists five kanji spellings for it, so no single spelling shows where it comes from (JMdict).
