# കഥാപാത്രം (kathāpātraṁ) — a story character

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ML-LEX-C562-RAGAM562-04, ML-LEX-C562-RAGAM562-05] -->

## You'll want to know: കഥാപാത്രം
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C563-RANGAM563-01]; assesses=[] -->

**കഥാപാത്രം** — *kathāpātraṁ* — "a story character".

> നോവലിലെ പ്രധാന കഥാപാത്രം ഒരു കർഷകനാണ്.
>
> *nōvalile pradhāna kathāpātraṁ oru karṣakanāṇŭ.*
>
> The main character of the novel is a farmer.

കഥ a story + പാത്രം a vessel: the vessel that carries the story.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**കഥാപാത്രം** (*kathāpātraṁ*) is a learned compound built from Sanskrit *kathā*, "a story" (CDIAL 2704), and *pātra*, "a vessel, a dish" (CDIAL 8055).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
