## You'll want to know: запрещать
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C389-ZAPRET-01]; assesses=[] -->

**запрещать** (*zapreshchát*) — "to forbid, to ban".

> Врач запрещает мне есть сладкое.
>
> *Vrach zapreshcháyet mne yest sládkoye.*
>
> The doctor forbids me to eat sweet things.

Imperfective; the perfective is *запретить* (*запрещу, запретишь*). *Запрещать кому* + infinitive. *Запрещено* — it is forbidden.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**запрещать** is built from *за-* and the root of *претить* "to be repugnant", once "to threaten"; the *щ* is the Church Slavonic softening of *т*, where Russian has *ч*.
