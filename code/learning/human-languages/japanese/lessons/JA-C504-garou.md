## You'll want to know: がろう
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C504-ART504-03]; assesses=[] -->

**がろう** — *garō* — "an art gallery".

> **まちの がろうに いきました。** — *machi no garō ni ikimashita* — "I went to a gallery in town."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**がろう** (*garō*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *ga*, "picture" (Middle Chinese *hweaH*) and *rō*, "corridor" (*lang*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
