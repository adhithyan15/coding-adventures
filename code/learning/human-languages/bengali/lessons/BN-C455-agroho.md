## You'll want to know: আগ্রহ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C455-DHOIRJO455-03]; assesses=[] -->

**আগ্রহ** — *āgrôhô* — "keen interest; eagerness".

> ইতিহাসে অরুণের আগ্রহ আছে।
>
> *itihāse ôruner āgrôhô āchhe.*
>
> Arun has a keen interest in history.

আগ্রহী, keen, interested.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**আগ্রহ** (*āgrôhô*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *āgraha*, "insisting on, strong inclination" (Monier-Williams); Bengali softened the insistence to keen interest.
