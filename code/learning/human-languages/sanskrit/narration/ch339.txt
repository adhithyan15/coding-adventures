Sanskrit, chapter 339: The Prince's Choice.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
युवराजः (yuvarājaḥ) — a crown prince.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for hell, then the Sanskrit for reaps.

You'll want to know: युवराजः.
युवराजः — yuvarājaḥ — "a crown prince".
युवराजः अश्वेन नगरम् आगच्छति। yuvarājaḥ aśvena nagaram āgacchati. The crown prince comes to the city on horseback.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
युवराजः (yuvarājaḥ) is built from yuvan, "young", and rājan, "a king"; MW gives yuvarāja as a young king, an heir-apparent, a crown prince (MW).

वृणीते — vṛṇīte — "chooses".
कुमारी युवराजं वृणीते। kumārī yuvarājaṃ vṛṇīte. The maiden chooses the crown prince.
Past (she …): सा युवराजं वृतवती, sā yuvarājaṃ vṛtavatī.

The word, taken apart.
वृणीते (vṛṇīte) comes from the root √vṛ, "to choose" (MW's second √vṛ), the root of vara too; MW gives vṛta as chosen, selected (MW).

Lesson 5 of 7.
न्यायः (nyāyaḥ) — justice; fairness.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for chooses, then the Sanskrit for disguised.

You'll want to know: न्यायः.
न्यायः — nyāyaḥ — "justice; fairness".
युवराजः न्यायेन राज्यं शास्ति। yuvarājaḥ nyāyena rājyaṃ śāsti. The crown prince rules the kingdom with justice.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
न्यायः (nyāyaḥ) is built from ni and the root √i, "to go": MW gives nyāya as a rule or method, a right or fit manner, propriety, and a lawsuit or judicial sentence, and justice is its everyday modern sense (MW).
