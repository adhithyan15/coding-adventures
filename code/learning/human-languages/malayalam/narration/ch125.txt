Malayalam, chapter 125: More of the Family.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
പേരക്കുട്ടി (pērakkuṭṭi) — a grandchild.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for welcome, then the Malayalam for good wishes.

You'll want to know: പേരക്കുട്ടി.
പേരക്കുട്ടി (pērakkuṭṭi) — "a grandchild". It ends in കുട്ടി (kuṭṭi), "child", which you already know.

What you've built.
A known word inside a new one.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: pērakkuṭṭi]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: pērakkuṭṭi, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say pērakkuṭṭi]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Malayalam for welcome, then the Malayalam for good wishes, then say pērakkuṭṭi again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does പേരക്കുട്ടി (pērakkuṭṭi) mean? ("A grandchild".) Say it once more.

Lesson 4 of 5.

അമ്മാവൻ (ammāvan) — "uncle", the mother's brother. It begins with അമ്മ, "mother".
