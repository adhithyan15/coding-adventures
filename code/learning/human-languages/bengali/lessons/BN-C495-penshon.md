## You'll want to know: পেনশন
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C495-BETON495-04]; assesses=[] -->

**পেনশন** — *penshôn* — "a pension".

> দাদু প্রতি মাসে পেনশন পান।
>
> *Dādu prôti māse penshôn pān.*
>
> My grandfather gets a pension every month.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**পেনশন** (*penshôn*) is borrowed from English *pension*.
