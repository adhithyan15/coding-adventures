## You'll want to know: ప్రజాభిప్రాయం
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C411-SATAM411-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ప్రజాభిప్రాయం** — *prajābhiprāyaṁ* — "public opinion".

> ప్రజాభిప్రాయం ఆ ప్రతిపాదనకు వ్యతిరేకంగా ఉంది.
>
> *prajābhiprāyaṁ ā pratipādanaku vyatirēkaṅgā undi.*
>
> Public opinion is against that proposal.

ప్రజా, of the people, + అభిప్రాయం, an opinion, which you know.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ప్రజాభిప్రాయం** (*prajābhiprāyaṁ*) is built from Sanskrit *prajā*, "offspring, people", and *abhiprāya*, "aim, intention" (Monier-Williams), the word Telugu has as అభిప్రాయం.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
