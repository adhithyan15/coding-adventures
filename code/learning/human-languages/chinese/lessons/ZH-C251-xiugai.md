## You'll want to know: xiūgǎi
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C251-ESSAY251-05]; assesses=[] -->

*xiūgǎi* — "to revise; to correct (a piece of work)".

> *zhè piān zuòwén nǐ yào xiūgǎi* — You'll need to revise this essay.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*xiūgǎi* is built from *xiū* and *gǎi*, both inherited from Middle Chinese, *sjuw* and *kojX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to put in order' and 'to adorn', and the second 'to change'.
