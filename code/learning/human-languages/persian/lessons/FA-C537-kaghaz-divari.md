## You'll want to know: کاغذ دیواری
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C537-CHANGES-02]; assesses=[] -->

**کاغذ دیواری** (*kâghaz divâri*) — "wallpaper".

> آنها کاغذ دیواری اتاق خواب را عوض کردند.
>
> *ânhâ kâghaz divâri-ye otâq-e khâb râ 'avaz kardand.*
>
> They changed the wallpaper in the bedroom.

*kâghaz* 'paper' + *divâri* 'of the wall'.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*kâghaz divâri* joins *kâghaz*, 'paper', which EtymDB traces to Sogdian, another Iranian language (EtymDB), and *divâri*, from *divâr*, 'wall', which it traces to Middle Persian (EtymDB).
