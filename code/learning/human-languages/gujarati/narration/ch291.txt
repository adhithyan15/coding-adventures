Gujarati, chapter 291: Surrounded.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
અપહરણ (apaharaṇ) — a kidnapping.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to threaten, then the Gujarati for to snatch.

You'll want to know: અપહરણ.
અપહરણ — apaharaṇ — "a kidnapping".
ગઈકાલે એક વેપારીના દીકરાનું અપહરણ થયું. gaīkāle ek vepārīnā dīkrānũ apaharaṇ thayũ. Yesterday a merchant's son was kidnapped.
A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
અપહરણ (apaharaṇ) is a tatsama, Sanskrit apaharaṇa, "a carrying away, stealing" (Monier-Williams). In Gujarati it is the carrying off of a person: દીકરાનું અપહરણ થયું.

Lesson 2 of 5.
ગુમ (gum) — missing.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to snatch, then the Gujarati for a kidnapping.

You'll want to know: ગુમ.
ગુમ — gum — "missing".
છોકરો ત્રણ દિવસથી ગુમ હતો. chokro traṇ divasthī gum hato. The boy had been missing for three days.

The word, taken apart.
ગુમ (gum) is usually traced to Persian gum, "lost". It describes a person or a thing nobody can find: છોકરો ગુમ છે; ગુમ થયો, went missing.

Lesson 3 of 5.
ઘેરવું (ghervũ) — to surround.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a kidnapping, then the Gujarati for missing.

You'll want to know: ઘેરવું.
ઘેરવું — ghervũ — "to surround".
પોલીસે આખું મકાન ઘેર્યું. polīse ākhũ makān gheryũ. The police surrounded the whole building.
Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે મકાન ઘેર્યું, teṇe makān gheryũ.

The word, taken apart.
ઘેરવું (ghervũ) comes from ghēr-, "to make go round", a form CDIAL reconstructs, and CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb, "to surround" (CDIAL 4474).

Lesson 4 of 5.
કેદી (kedī) — a prisoner; a captive.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for missing, then the Gujarati for to surround.

You'll want to know: કેદી.
કેદી — kedī — "a prisoner; a captive".
જો પોલીસ સમયસર પહોંચી ગઈ તો કેદી બચી જશે. jo polīs samaysar pahõcī gaī to kedī bacī jaśe. If the police get there in time, the captive will be saved.
A masculine or feminine noun, by the person meant.

The word, taken apart.
કેદી (kedī) is usually traced to Persian qaidī, "a prisoner", from qaid, captivity, a word Persian took from Arabic. It is used of a man or a woman.

Lesson 5 of 5.
છોડાવવું (choḍāvvũ) — to set free; to get released.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to surround, then the Gujarati for a prisoner.

You'll want to know: છોડાવવું.
છોડાવવું — choḍāvvũ — "to set free; to get released".
રાત્રે પોલીસે છોકરાને છોડાવ્યો. rātre polīse chokrāne choḍāvyo. During the night the police freed the boy.
Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે છોકરાને છોડાવ્યો, teṇe chokrāne choḍāvyo.

The word, taken apart.
છોડાવવું (choḍāvvũ) is the causative of છોડવું, to let go, which CDIAL sets under Sanskrit kṣōṭayati (CDIAL 3747): to make someone be let go, and so to get a captive freed.
