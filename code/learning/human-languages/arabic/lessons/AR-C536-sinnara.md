## You'll want to know: صنارة
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C536-SAYD536-02]; assesses=[] -->

**صنارة** — *ṣinnāra* — "a fishing rod".

> نسي أبي صنارته في القارب.
>
> *nasiya abī ṣinnāratahu fī al-qārib.*
>
> My father forgot his fishing rod in the boat.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**صنارة** (*ṣinnāra*), a fish hook and a fishing rod, was borrowed from Syriac *ṣenārtā*, "a fishing gaff, a hooked tool" (EtymDB).
