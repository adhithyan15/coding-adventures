## You'll want to know: el recién nacido
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C544-PARTO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**el recién nacido** — "the newborn baby".

> El recién nacido pesó tres kilos.
>
> *The newborn baby weighed three kilos.*

It agrees: *una niña recién nacida*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*el recién nacido* is built on *recién*, the short form of *reciente*, from Latin *recēns* 'fresh, new', and *nacido*, the participle of *nacer*, from Latin *nāscī* 'to be born' (Lewis & Short).
