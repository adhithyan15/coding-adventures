## You'll want to know: आशा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C375-ASHA375-01]; assesses=[] -->

**आशा** — *āśā* — "a hope, an expectation".

> मुझे आशा है कि अगर मौसम अच्छा रहेगा तो फ़सल अच्छी होगी।
>
> *mujhe āśā hai ki agar mausam acchā rahegā to fasal acchī hogī.*
>
> I hope that if the weather stays good, the harvest will be good.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**आशा** (*āśā*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *āśā*, "a wish, a hope" (Monier-Williams).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
