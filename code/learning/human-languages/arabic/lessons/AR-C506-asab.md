## You'll want to know: عصب
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C506-WAJH506-05]; assesses=[] -->

**عصب** — *ʿaṣab* — "a nerve".

> أصاب الحادث عصبا في يده.
>
> *aṣāba al-ḥādith ʿaṣaban fī yadihi.*
>
> The accident damaged a nerve in his hand.

Plural: أعصاب, *aʿṣāb*.

أعصاب also means nerves as in calm: أعصابه قوية.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**عصب** (*ʿaṣab*) is built on the native Arabic root *ʿ-ṣ-b*, "to bind, to tie tight" (Lane). In the old language ʿaṣab was a sinew, the cord that binds the body.
