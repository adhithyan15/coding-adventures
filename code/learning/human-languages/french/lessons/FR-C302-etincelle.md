## You'll want to know: l'étincelle
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C302-INCENDIE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**l'étincelle** — "the spark".

> Une petite étincelle a suffi.
>
> *One small spark was enough.*

Figurative too: *l'étincelle d'une idée*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*étincelle* is inherited from Vulgar Latin *stincilla* "spark" (EtymDB), an altered form of Latin *scintilla*.
