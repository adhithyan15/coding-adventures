## You'll want to know: سحری
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C322-ROZA322-02]; assesses=[] -->

**سحری** — *sahrī* — "the meal before dawn (in a fast)".

> ہم نے سویرے سحری کھائی۔
>
> *ham ne savere sahrī khāī.*
>
> We ate the pre-dawn meal early in the morning.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**سحری** (*sahrī*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *saḥar*, "the time before dawn", with the ending *-ī*: the meal eaten then, before the fast begins.
