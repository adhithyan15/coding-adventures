## You'll want to know: lìshǐ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C321-ANCIENT321-01]; assesses=[] -->

*lìshǐ* — "history".

> *wǒ zài dàxué xué guo Zhōngguó de lìshǐ* — I studied Chinese history at university.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*lìshǐ* is built from *lì* 'to pass through' and *shǐ* 'a record', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *lek* and *sriX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to go through' and calls the second 'one who records events'.
