## You'll want to know: દ્વિધા
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C389-NIRNAY389-03]; assesses=[] -->

**દ્વિધા** — *dvidhā* — "a dilemma".

> જો બંને નોકરી સારી હશે તો મને દ્વિધા થશે.
>
> *jo banne nokrī sārī haśe to mane dvidhā thaśe.*
>
> If both jobs are good, I will be in two minds.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**દ્વિધા** (*dvidhā*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *dvidhā*, "in two ways, divided" (Monier-Williams): a mind split two ways.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
