## You'll want to know: ربى
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C530-ATFAL530-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ربى** — *rabbā* — "to bring up".

> ربت أمي خمسة أولاد.
>
> *rabbat ummī khamsat awlād.*
>
> My mother brought up five children.

Present: يربي, *yurabbī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ربى** (*rabbā*) is built on the native Arabic root *r-b-w*, "to grow, to increase" (Lane). Form II rabbā is to make a child grow.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
