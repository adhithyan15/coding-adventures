## You'll want to know: பகடை
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C529-VILAIYATTU529-04]; assesses=[] -->

**பகடை** — *pakaṭai* — "a die; dice".

> அவன் பகடையை உருட்டினான்.
>
> *avaṉ pakaṭaiyai uruṭṭiṉāṉ.*
>
> He rolled the dice.

One rolls dice with உருட்டு (*uruṭṭu*).

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

The origin of **பகடை** (*pakaṭai*) is not settled: no dictionary we can check gives its source.
