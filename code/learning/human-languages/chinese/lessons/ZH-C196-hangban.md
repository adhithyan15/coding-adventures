## You'll want to know: hángbān
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C196-TICKET196-03]; assesses=[] -->

*hángbān* — "scheduled flight".

> *zhège hángbān wǎndiǎn le* — This flight is delayed.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*hángbān* is built from *háng* 'to sail' and *bān* 'a shift, a set run', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *hang* and *paen* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription). The dictionary glosses the first 'a boat' and the second as dividing jade tokens; how the pair came to mean a flight is not settled.
