Hindi, chapter 541: Something in the Way.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
रुकावट (rukāvaṭ) — an obstacle, a hold-up.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a trick, then the Hindi for an effort, an endeavour (formal).

You'll want to know: रुकावट.
रुकावट — rukāvaṭ — "an obstacle, a hold-up".
सड़क पर एक पेड़ गिरा था, इसलिए रास्ते में रुकावट थी। Saṛak par ek peṛ girā thā, isliye rāste meṁ rukāvaṭ thī. A tree had fallen on the road, so the way was blocked.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
रुकावट (rukāvaṭ) is the noun of ruknā, "to stop", which CDIAL lists beside roknā under a reconstructed rokk (CDIAL 10827), with the ending -āvaṭ that makes nouns of verbs.

Lesson 2 of 5.
झमेला (jhamelā) — a hassle, a tiresome business.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for an effort, an endeavour (formal), then the Hindi for an obstacle, a hold-up.

You'll want to know: झमेला.
झमेला — jhamelā — "a hassle, a tiresome business".
दफ़्तर में काग़ज़ों का बड़ा झमेला है। Daftar meṁ kāġzoṁ kā baṛā jhamelā hai. There is a big hassle with papers at the office.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
झमेला (jhamelā) is a tadbhava, inherited from jhamm, "entanglement", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 5340). CDIAL gives the Hindi word as "wrangling".

Lesson 3 of 5.
अटकाना (aṭkānā) — to get (something) stuck; to hold up.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for an obstacle, a hold-up, then the Hindi for a hassle, a tiresome business.

You'll want to know: अटकाना.
अटकाना — aṭkānā — "to get (something) stuck; to hold up".
उसने पतंग पेड़ पर अटका दी। Usne patang peṛ par aṭkā dī. He got the kite stuck in the tree.
Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने काम अटकाया, usne kām aṭkāyā.

The word, taken apart.
अटकाना (aṭkānā) is the causative of aṭaknā, "to stick, to stop", which CDIAL files under aṭṭakk, a reconstructed form (CDIAL 182).

Lesson 4 of 5.
फंदा (phandā) — a noose; a snare.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a hassle, a tiresome business, then the Hindi for to get (something) stuck.

You'll want to know: फंदा.
फंदा — phandā — "a noose; a snare".
किसान ने चूहों के लिए एक फंदा रखा। Kisān ne cūhoṁ ke liye ek phandā rakhā. The farmer set a snare for the rats.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
फंदा (phandā) is probably related to Hindi phā̃d and phā̃s, "a noose, a net", which CDIAL files under Sanskrit spāśa, "a fetter, a noose" (CDIAL 13813); CDIAL does not list phandā itself.

Lesson 5 of 5.
उलझाना (uljhānā) — to tangle; to confuse.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for to get (something) stuck, then the Hindi for a noose.

You'll want to know: उलझाना.
उलझाना — uljhānā — "to tangle; to confuse".
बच्चे ने धागा उलझा दिया। Bacce ne dhāgā uljhā diyā. The child tangled up the thread.
Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने बात उलझाई, usne bāt uljhāī.

The word, taken apart.
उलझाना (uljhānā) is the causative of ulajhnā, "to get tangled", which CDIAL files under Sanskrit uparundhati, "obstructs" (CDIAL 2221).
