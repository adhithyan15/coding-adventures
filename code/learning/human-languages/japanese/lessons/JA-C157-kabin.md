## You'll want to know: かびん

**かびん** — *kabin* — "a vase".

> **しろい かびんは たかいです。** — *shiroi kabin wa takai desu* — "The white vase is expensive."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**かびん** (*kabin*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *ka*, "flower" (Middle Chinese *xwae*) and *bin*, "bottle, jar" (*beng*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
