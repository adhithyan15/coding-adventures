## You'll want to know: با توجه به
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C371-DALIL-05]; assesses=[] -->

**با توجه به** (*bâ tavajjoh be*) — "considering, given".

> با توجه به قیمت، این ماشین خوب است.
>
> *bâ tavajjoh be qeymat, in mâshin khub ast.*
>
> Considering the price, this car is good.

*Bâ* "with" + *tavajjoh* "attention" + *be* "to". It takes a noun; with a clause, *bâ tavajjoh be inke* … .

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**با توجه به** is literally "with attention to": *bâ* and *be* are Persian, and *tavajjoh* "attention" is usually traced to Arabic *tawajjuh* "turning towards".
