## You'll want to know: പുല്ല്
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C66-PADDY-02]; assesses=[] -->

**പുല്ല്** (*pullŭ*) — "grass".

Native Dravidian, matching Tamil *pul*, and it ends the way നെല്ല് ends — a doubled *l* with the vowel shaved off, so the two words rhyme on the tail.

They are the field's two crops seen from opposite sides. One is planted and counted; the other turns up on its own and is cut for the animals.

Which makes this the *paśu*'s word rather than the farmer's: grass is what the cow is walked out to.
