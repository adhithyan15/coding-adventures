যুদ্ধ (juddhô) — a war.

Warm-up.

You'll want to know: যুদ্ধ.
যুদ্ধ — juddhô — "a war".

The word, taken apart.
যুদ্ধ (juddhô) is Sanskrit yuddha, "fought; battle", taken over whole as a learned loan, a tatsama (CDIAL 10499).
