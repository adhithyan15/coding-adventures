# Review — feelings and hopes

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[KA-LEX-C314-AUSADHA-01, KA-SCRIPT-RECOG-175, KA-LEX-C310-SVACCHA-01, KA-SCRIPT-RECOG-174] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Read **ಔಷಧ** and **ಸ್ವಚ್ಛ** aloud. (*auṣadha*, *svaccha*.) Which letter opens the first, and which sits small inside the second? (**ಔ**; **ಛ**.)

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[KA-LEX-C315-KHUSI-01, KA-GRAMMAR-C315-FEELING-DATIVE-01, KA-GRAMMAR-C315-FEELING-STATE-01, KA-LEX-C315-GURI-01, KA-GRAMMAR-C315-WISH-BEKU-ANTA-01, KA-GRAMMAR-C315-BECOME-01, KA-GRAMMAR-C314-MISSED-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s each]
- [YOU SAY: I was very glad to see you.]
- [YOU SAY: When the bus got away, I got angry. Then I felt low.]
- [YOU SAY: I am happy now.]
- [YOU SAY: This year I have a goal: I would love to go to Mysuru.]
- [YOU SAY: My younger sister dreams of becoming a teacher.]
- [YOU WRITE: **ಖುಷಿ** and **ಗುರಿ**]

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[KA-LEX-C315-KHUSI-01, KA-GRAMMAR-C315-FEELING-DATIVE-01, KA-GRAMMAR-C315-FEELING-STATE-01, KA-LEX-C315-GURI-01, KA-GRAMMAR-C315-WISH-BEKU-ANTA-01, KA-GRAMMAR-C315-BECOME-01] -->
