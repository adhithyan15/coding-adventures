## You'll want to know: सन्देहः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C288-APARADHA288-02]; assesses=[] -->

**सन्देहः** — *sandehaḥ* — "a suspicion; a doubt".

> मम सन्देहः अस्ति।
>
> *mama sandehaḥ asti.*
>
> I have a suspicion.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सन्देहः** (*sandehaḥ*) is built from *sam*, "together", and the root √dih, "to smear, heap up"; MW's first sense is a mass stuck together, and its usual sense doubt or uncertainty, and a suspicion is its everyday modern use (MW).
