## You'll want to know: declarar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C515-RUMOR-04]; assesses=[] -->

**declarar** — "to declare, to state".

> El presidente declaró que no sabía nada.
>
> *The president stated that he knew nothing.*

In court it means to testify, and at customs to declare goods: *¿Algo que declarar?*

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*declarar* comes from Latin *dēclārāre* 'to make clear, to show' (EtymDB), a learned word built on *clārus* 'clear'. English *declare* is the same Latin verb.
