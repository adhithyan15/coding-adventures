## You'll want to know: governare
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C328-IMPERO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**governare** — "to govern, to rule".

> Ha governato il paese per dieci anni.
>
> *He governed the country for ten years.*

*Il governo* — the government.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*governare* is inherited from Latin *gubernāre* "to steer a ship" (EtymDB), from Greek *kybernân*.
