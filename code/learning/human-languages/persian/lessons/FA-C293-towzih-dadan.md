# توضیح دادن (towzih dâdan) — to explain

## Warm-up

## You'll want to know: توضیح دادن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C293-TOWZIH-DADAN-01]; assesses=[FA-LEX-C158-CHERA158-05, FA-GRAMMAR-RA-OBJECT-01, FA-GRAMMAR-SIMPLE-PAST-01] -->

**توضیح دادن** — *towzih dâdan* — "to explain".

> **او مشکل را توضیح داد.** — *u moshkel râ towzih dâd* — She explained the
> problem.

It runs on *dâdan*, "to give", whose present stem is **deh-**: *towzih
midaham*, I explain; *towzih bedahid*, in the subjunctive, "that you explain".
So **باید توضیح بدهم**, *bâyad towzih bedaham*, is "I have to explain".

You have *sharh dâdan* from chapter two hundred and twenty-three. It lays a
thing out at length. *Towzih dâdan* is the one you reach for every day: make
it clear.

Say *tow-zih*, two beats, with a breathy *h* at the end. Its third letter is
a *z* you have not written yet, and the next lesson puts it in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[FA-LEX-C223-KONESH223-03] -->

EtymDB 2.1 has no entry for *towzih* itself. It is the Arabic noun *tawḍīḥ*,
usually explained as "making clear", on the root *w-ḍ-ḥ* of Arabic *wāḍiḥ*,
"clear, obvious", which EtymDB does list.

*Dâdan* is Persian, and EtymDB takes it back to the Indo-European root for
"to give" that also gave Latin *dō*, "I give". So the compound is "to give
clarity".
