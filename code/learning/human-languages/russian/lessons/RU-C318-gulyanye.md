## You'll want to know: гулянье
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C318-TRADITSIYA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**гулянье** (*gulyánye*) — "festivities, a fête".

> На площади было большое гулянье.
>
> *Na plóshchadi býlo bolshóye gulyánye.*
>
> There were big festivities on the square.

Neuter; also spelt *гуляние*. *Народное гулянье* — public festivities.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*гулянье* is built on *гулять* "to stroll, to make merry", an East Slavic verb whose origin is not settled; EtymDB lists no parent.
