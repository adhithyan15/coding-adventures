## You'll want to know: توتلا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C485-CHILDHOOD485-03]; assesses=[] -->

**توتلا** — *totlā* — "lisping; speaking like a small child".

> چھوٹا بچہ توتلی زبان میں بولتا ہے۔
>
> *chhoṭā bachchā totlī zabān meṅ boltā hai.*
>
> The little child talks in baby talk.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**توتلا** (*totlā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *totlā* under *tōtta*, "stammering", a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 5965).
