# ർ (chillu r) — the r that ends ഞായർ

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ML-SCRIPT-C114-DHA-01, ML-SCRIPT-C114-BA-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: write ധ and ബ once each, and name them.

[PAUSE 2s] Now say **ഞായർ** (*ñāyar*), "Sunday".
You have read it for a long time. This lesson takes one letter out of it and puts
it in your hand.

## Script you'll notice: ർ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-SCRIPT-C114-CHILLU-RR-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ർ** — *chillu r*, an *r* with no vowel after it.

It ends **ഞായർ** (*ñāyar*), "Sunday". A *chillu* is the form a consonant takes when
it closes a word with no vowel of its own. You already write other chillus; this is
the *r* one.

## Writing: ർ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ML-SCRIPT-C114-CHILLU-RR-01] -->

- **1.** start here: climb around the left arch and carry the upper shoulder right
- **2.** without lifting, sweep clockwise around the right loop and return to the upper crossing
- **3.** without lifting, rise into the chillu hook and curl left above the line — and only now lift

**Pen lifts: 0.** The pen never leaves the paper.

> Stroke order is one attested teaching order; hands and teachers vary.
