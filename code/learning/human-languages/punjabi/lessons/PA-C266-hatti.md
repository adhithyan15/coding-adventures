## You'll want to know: ਹੱਟੀ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C266-TOWN266-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ਹੱਟੀ** — *haṭṭī* — "a small shop".

> ਗਲੀ ਵਿੱਚ ਇੱਕ ਹੱਟੀ ਹੈ ਅਤੇ ਉੱਥੇ ਦੁੱਧ ਮਿਲਦਾ ਹੈ।
>
> *galī vich ikk haṭṭī hai ate utthe dudh mildā hai.*
>
> There is a little shop in the lane, and you can get milk there.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਹੱਟੀ** (*haṭṭī*) is inherited from Sanskrit *haṭṭa*, "market, fair", beside *haṭṭī*, "petty market"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *haṭṭ*, "shop", under it (CDIAL 13944).
