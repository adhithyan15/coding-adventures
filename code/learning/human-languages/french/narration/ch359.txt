French, chapter 359: Au bureau.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
le cadre — the executive, the manager; the frame.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for the employee, then the French for the overtime.

You'll want to know: le cadre.
le cadre — "the executive, the manager; the frame".
Mon oncle est cadre dans une grande entreprise. My uncle is an executive in a big company.
Un cadre is a salaried manager; le cadre is also a frame, or a setting.

The word, taken apart.
cadre was borrowed from Italian quadro "a square, a frame" (EtymDB), from Latin quadrum "a square" (Lewis & Short).

Chaque matin, je fais la liste de mes tâches. Every morning, I make a list of my tasks.

The word, taken apart.
tâche is usually traced to Medieval Latin taxa "an assessed amount, set work", from Latin taxāre "to appraise" (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 3 of 5.
le délai — the deadline, the time allowed.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for the executive, then the French for the task.

You'll want to know: le délai.
le délai — "the deadline, the time allowed".
Nous avons un délai de deux semaines pour finir. We have two weeks to finish.
A false friend: un délai is the time you have, not a delay.

The word, taken apart.
délai comes from Old French delai (EtymDB), a noun formed on delaier "to put off". The further origin is not settled.

Lesson 4 of 5.

You'll want to know: la prolongation.
la prolongation — "the extension".
J'ai demandé une prolongation de mon contrat. I asked for an extension of my contract.
In football, les prolongations — extra time.

The word, taken apart.
prolongation comes from Late Latin prōlongātiō (EtymDB), from prōlongāre "to lengthen", built on longus "long" (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 5 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for the deadline, then the French for the extension.

You'll want to know: la livraison.
la livraison — "the delivery".
La livraison est gratuite le samedi. Delivery is free on Saturdays.
From livrer. Livraison à domicile — home delivery.

The word, taken apart.
livraison is built from livrer and -aison (EtymDB). livrer is inherited from Latin līberāre "to set free" (Lewis & Short).
