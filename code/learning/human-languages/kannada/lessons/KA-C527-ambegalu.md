# ಅಂಬೆಗಾಲು (ambegālu) — crawling on all fours

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[KA-LEX-C527-POSHANE527-02, KA-LEX-C527-POSHANE527-03] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Kannada for to bring up, then the Kannada for to nourish.

## You'll want to know: ಅಂಬೆಗಾಲು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C527-POSHANE527-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ಅಂಬೆಗಾಲು** — *ambegālu* — "crawling on all fours".

> ಮಗು ಅಂಬೆಗಾಲು ಇಡುತ್ತಾ ಅಡುಗೆಮನೆಗೆ ಬಂದಿತು.
>
> *magu ambegālu iḍuttā aḍugemanege banditu.*
>
> The baby crawled into the kitchen.

ಅಂಬೆಗಾಲು ಇಡು, to crawl, of a baby.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಅಂಬೆಗಾಲು** (*ambegālu*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 180 lists Kannada *ambegālu*, "the hands and knees placed on the ground for crawling".
