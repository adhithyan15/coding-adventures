## You'll want to know: la faculté
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C376-FAC-01]; assesses=[] -->

**la faculté** — "the faculty, the university; the ability".

> Elle étudie à la faculté de médecine.
>
> *She studies at the faculty of medicine.*

In speech, *la fac*: *aller à la fac*. It can also mean an ability.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*faculté* is a learned borrowing from Latin *facultās* "ability, power" (Lewis & Short), from *facilis* "easy, doable".
