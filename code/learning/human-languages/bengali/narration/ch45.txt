5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
খুশি (khushi) — happy.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for pain, then the Bengali for hunger.

You'll want to know: খুশি.
খুশি — khushi — "happy".

The word, taken apart.
খুশি (khushi) is a loan, usually traced to Persian khushī, "happiness, joy". It is built on Persian khush, "happy".

Lesson 3 of 5.
বাবা (bābā) — a father.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for happy, then the Bengali for a doctor.

You'll want to know: বাবা.
বাবা — bābā — "a father".

The word, taken apart.
বাবা (bābā) is a tadbhava, a word inherited and reshaped by sound change. It comes from bābba, "father", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 9209). CDIAL calls these nursery words.

Lesson 4 of 5.
ছেলে (chhele) — a son; a boy.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a doctor, then the Bengali for a father.

You'll want to know: ছেলে.
ছেলে — chhele — "a son; a boy".

The word, taken apart.
CDIAL, in its addenda, lists ছেলে (chhele), "boy", under Sanskrit chagala, "goat", beside the form chēlaka, "goat" (CDIAL 4963): the word for a boy seems to have begun as a word for a young goat.
