## You'll want to know: gōngyuán
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C100-TOWN100-03]; assesses=[] -->

*gōngyuán* — "park".

> *wǒmen qù gōngyuán wán* — We're going to the park to play.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*gōngyuán* is built from gōng 'public' and yuán 'garden', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *kuwng* and *hjwon* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription): a garden for everyone.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for asking where things are.
