## You'll want to know: खेदः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C448-BHAVA448-05]; assesses=[] -->

**खेदः** — *khedaḥ* — "distress; weariness of heart".

> मम मित्रं रुग्णम्, अतः मम खेदः अस्ति।
>
> *mama mitraṃ rugṇam, ataḥ mama khedaḥ asti.*
>
> My friend is ill, so I am distressed.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**खेदः** (*khedaḥ*) comes from the root √khid, "to be depressed"; MW gives "lassitude, depression" and "pain, distress" (MW).
