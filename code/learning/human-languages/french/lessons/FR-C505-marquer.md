## You'll want to know: marquer
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C505-TERRAIN-05]; assesses=[] -->

**marquer** — "to score; to mark".

> Elle a marqué deux buts dans le match.
>
> *She scored two goals in the match.*

*Marquer un but* — to score a goal. Also to note down: *marquer une adresse*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*marquer* is built on *marque* "a mark, a sign", a word of Germanic origin related to English *mark*.
