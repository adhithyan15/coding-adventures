## You'll want to know: ಕಣ್ಣಾಮುಚ್ಚಾಲೆ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C531-ATIKE531-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ಕಣ್ಣಾಮುಚ್ಚಾಲೆ** — *kaṇṇāmuccāle* — "hide-and-seek".

> ಮಕ್ಕಳು ತೋಟದಲ್ಲಿ ಕಣ್ಣಾಮುಚ್ಚಾಲೆ ಆಡುತ್ತಿದ್ದರು.
>
> *makkaḷu tōṭadalli kaṇṇāmuccāle āḍuttiddaru.*
>
> The children were playing hide-and-seek in the garden.

From ಕಣ್ಣು, eye, and ಮುಚ್ಚು, to close: the seeker closes his eyes.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಕಣ್ಣಾಮುಚ್ಚಾಲೆ** (*kaṇṇāmuccāle*) is a native Kannada game name built on ಕಣ್ಣು, "eye", and ಮುಚ್ಚು, "to close"; the origin of the ending is not settled.
