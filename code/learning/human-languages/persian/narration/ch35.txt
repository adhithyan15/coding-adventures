Persian, chapter 35: To eat, To drink, To see, To hear, To sit.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
خوردن (khordan) — to eat.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a gentleman; Mr, then the Persian for a lady; Mrs.

You'll want to know: خوردن.
خوردن — khordan — "to eat".

The word, taken apart.
khordan is a native Persian verb, inherited rather than borrowed: EtymDB traces it to the Middle Persian stem xwar-, "to eat", and further to Proto-Iranian hwar-, "to eat, to consume".

Lesson 2 of 5.
نوشیدن (nushidan) — to drink.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a lady; Mrs, then the Persian for to eat.

You'll want to know: نوشیدن.
نوشیدن — nushidan — "to drink".

The word, taken apart.
nushidan is a Persian verb built on nuš, "sweet drink", with the verb ending -idan. nuš is usually traced to Middle Persian anōš, "immortality, ambrosia".

You'll want to know: نشستن.
نشستن — neshastan — "to sit".

The word, taken apart.
neshastan is a native Persian word, inherited rather than borrowed; it is usually traced to Middle Persian nišastan, "to sit". It is usually analysed as ni-, "down", on the Indo-European root that also gives English sit.
