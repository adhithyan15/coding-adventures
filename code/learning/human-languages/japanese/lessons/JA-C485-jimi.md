## You'll want to know: じみ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C485-CLOTH485-04]; assesses=[] -->

**じみ** — *jimi* — "plain; quiet (in colour)".

> **ちちの ふくは いつも じみです。** — *chichi no fuku wa itsumo jimi desu* — "My father's clothes are always plain."

A na-adjective: な before a noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**じみ** (*jimi*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *ji*, "ground" (Middle Chinese *dijH*) and *mi*, "flavor" (*mj+jH*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
