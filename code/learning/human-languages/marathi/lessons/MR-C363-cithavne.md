## You'll want to know: चिथावणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C363-SATTA363-05]; assesses=[] -->

**चिथावणे** — *cithāvṇe* — "to incite; to provoke".

> कोणीतरी गर्दीला चिथावलं.
>
> *koṇītrī gardīlā cithāvlaṁ.*
>
> Someone incited the crowd.

Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने चिथावलं, *tyāne cithāvlaṁ*.

The one incited takes -ला; चिथावणी, incitement.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**चिथावणे** (*cithāvṇe*) is built on an older verb, Marathi *cithṇẽ*, "to take offence", which CDIAL lists under *\*citth-*, "pound, tear" (CDIAL 4802): to make someone take offence.
