German, chapter 284: Der Verdacht.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
der Verdacht — the suspicion.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to observe, then the German for to recount.

You'll want to know: der Verdacht.
der Verdacht — "the suspicion".
Der Verdacht fiel auf den Nachbarn. Suspicion fell on the neighbour.
No plural. Unter Verdacht stehen — to be under suspicion.

The word, taken apart.
Verdacht is usually traced to the old verb verdenken "to suspect", built on denken "to think"; EtymDB lists no parent.

Lesson 3 of 5.
ermitteln — to investigate; to find out.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for the suspicion, then the German for suspicious.

You'll want to know: ermitteln.
ermitteln — "to investigate; to find out".
Die Polizei ermittelte wochenlang. The police investigated for weeks.
Gegen jemanden ermitteln — to investigate someone.

The word, taken apart.
ermitteln is built on Mittel "middle, means", an inherited Germanic word (EtymDB), with the prefix er-; EtymDB lists no parent for the verb itself.

Lesson 5 of 5.
festnehmen — to arrest, to detain.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to investigate, then the German for the investigation.

You'll want to know: festnehmen.
festnehmen — "to arrest, to detain".
Die Polizei nahm den Mann am Bahnhof fest. The police arrested the man at the station.
Separable: sie nehmen ihn fest, sie haben ihn festgenommen. Verhaften means the same.

The word, taken apart.
festnehmen joins fest "firm", Old High German festi (EtymDB), and nehmen "to take", Proto-Germanic nemaną (EtymDB): to take and hold.
