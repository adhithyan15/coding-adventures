## You'll want to know: वार्ड
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C491-DAKHIL491-02]; assesses=[] -->

**वार्ड** — *vārḍ* — "a hospital ward".

> दादा का बिस्तर वार्ड के कोने में है।
>
> *dādā kā bistar vārḍ ke kone meṁ hai.*
>
> Grandfather's bed is in the corner of the ward.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**वार्ड** (*vārḍ*) is borrowed from English *ward*.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
