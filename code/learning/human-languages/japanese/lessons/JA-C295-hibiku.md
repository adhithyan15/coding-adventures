## You'll want to know: ひびく
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C295-HATSUMOUDE295-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ひびく** — *hibiku* — "to ring out; to echo".

> **ゆうべ、てらの かねが ひびいていました。** — *yūbe, tera no kane ga hibiite imashita* — "Last night the temple bell was ringing out."

Polite **ひびきます**, *hibikimasu*; a godan verb. Past (polite): **かねの おとが ひびきました。**, *kane no oto ga hibikimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ひびく** (*hibiku*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict).
