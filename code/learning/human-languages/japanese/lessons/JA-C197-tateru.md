## You'll want to know: たてる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C197-CITY197-04]; assesses=[] -->

**たてる** — *tateru* — "to build; to put up".

> **えきの まえに ひゃっかてんを たてます。** — *eki no mae ni hyakkaten o tatemasu* — "They will build a department store in front of the station."

Polite **たてます**, *tatemasu*; an ichidan verb.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**たてる** (*tateru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
