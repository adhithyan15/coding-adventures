## You'll want to know: der Zweifel
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C337-IRRTUM-03]; assesses=[] -->

**der Zweifel** — "the doubt".

> Ich habe keinen Zweifel: Sie ist die beste Spielerin.
>
> *I have no doubt: she is the best player.*

Plural: *die Zweifel*. *Ohne Zweifel* — without doubt.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Zweifel* is inherited from Middle High German *zwīvel*, Old High German *zwīfal* and Proto-Germanic *twīflaz* (EtymDB), built on the word for "two": a mind split in two.
