# ਤਾਰੀਖ਼ — find the date line

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-LEX-C100-TIME100-05] -->

You met **ਤਾਰੀਖ਼** as “a date.” Read it aloud once. Its Persian source
*tārīkh* also means a date or history; here the form asks for a date only.

## Reading — one familiar word in a new place
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-FORM-LABEL-DATE-01]; assesses=[PA-LEX-C100-TIME100-05] -->

On this fictional beginner form, **ਤਾਰੀਖ਼: ______** labels the date line.
Point to the label. The blank is where a date goes; it is not a phone or age
line. No real personal information is requested.

## Guided Practice — copy the label
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-FORM-LABEL-DATE-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: guided-copy -->

Copy **ਤਾਰੀਖ਼:** once, including the colon. Leave its value blank.
