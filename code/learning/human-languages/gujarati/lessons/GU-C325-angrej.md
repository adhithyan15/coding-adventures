## You'll want to know: અંગ્રેજ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C325-AZADI325-03]; assesses=[] -->

**અંગ્રેજ** — *angrej* — "an Englishman; the English (rulers)".

> અંગ્રેજો ઘણાં વરસ અહીં રહ્યા.
>
> *angrejo ghaṇā̃ varas ahī̃ rahyā.*
>
> The English stayed here for many years.

A masculine or feminine noun, by the person meant.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**અંગ્રેજ** (*angrej*) is usually traced, through Persian *angrez*, to Portuguese *inglês*, "English". In the plural it is the English rulers of colonial times: **અંગ્રેજો**.
