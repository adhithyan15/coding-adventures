## You'll want to know: die Stornierung
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C420-STORNO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**die Stornierung** — "the cancellation".

> Die Stornierung kostet zwanzig Euro.
>
> *The cancellation costs twenty euros.*

Plural: *die Stornierungen*. Also *das Storno*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Stornierung* is built on *stornieren* with the ending *-ung*; the verb was borrowed from Italian *stornare* (EtymDB).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.
