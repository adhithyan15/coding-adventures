Arabic, chapter 367: Grasping ideas.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
أدرك (adraka) — to realize.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to believe someone, then the Arabic for to infer.

You'll want to know: أدرك.
أدرك — adraka — "to realize".
أدركت أنني نسيت مفتاحي. adraktu annanī nasītu miftāḥī. I realized that I had forgotten my key.
Present: يدرك, yudrik.

The word, taken apart.
أدرك (adraka) is built on the native Arabic root d-r-k, "to overtake, to reach" (Lane). It is Form IV, to catch up with: a thought catches up with you, and you realize.

تصور (taṣawwara) — to conceive.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: تصور.
تصور — taṣawwara — "to conceive".
لم أتصور أن الرحلة طويلة إلى هذا الحد. lam ataṣawwar anna ar-riḥla ṭawīla ilā hādhā al-ḥadd. I did not imagine the journey was this long.
Present: يتصور, yataṣawwar.
The noun تصور (taṣawwara), taṣawwur, an idea, a conception.

The word, taken apart.
تصور (taṣawwara) is built on the native Arabic root ṣ-w-r, "to form, to shape" (Lane). It is Form V of the root of ṣūra, "a picture": to form a picture in the mind.

Lesson 4 of 5.
وعي (waʿy) — awareness.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to conceive, then the Arabic for to grasp.

You'll want to know: وعي.
وعي — waʿy — "awareness".
عند الشباب وعي كبير بالصحة. ʿinda ash-shabāb waʿy kabīr bi-ṣ-ṣiḥḥa. Young people have great awareness of health.
فقد وعيه, faqada waʿyahu, he lost consciousness.

The word, taken apart.
وعي (waʿy) is built on the native Arabic root w-ʿ-y, "to retain, to keep in mind" (Lane). Waʿy is the verbal noun of waʿā, "to hold in mind": awareness.
