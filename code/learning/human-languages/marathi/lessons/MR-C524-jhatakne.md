## You'll want to know: झटकणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C524-SAPHAI524-04]; assesses=[] -->

**झटकणे** — *jhaṭakṇe* — "to shake off; to dust".

> आजीने गच्चीवर चादर झटकली.
>
> *ājīne gaccīvar cādar jhaṭaklī.*
>
> Grandmother shook out the sheet on the terrace.

Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने चादर झटकली, *tyāne cādar jhaṭaklī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**झटकणे** (*jhaṭakṇe*) is inherited: CDIAL sets Marathi *jhaṭakṇẽ* among the words of sudden movement under *\*jhaṭ* (CDIAL 5327).
