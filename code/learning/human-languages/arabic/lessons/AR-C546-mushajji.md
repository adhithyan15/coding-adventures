## You'll want to know: مشجع
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C546-MUBARA546-02]; assesses=[] -->

**مشجع** — *mushajjiʿ* — "a supporter, a fan".

> غنى المشجعون في الملعب.
>
> *ghannā al-mushajjiʿūn fī al-malʿab.*
>
> The supporters sang in the stadium.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**مشجع** (*mushajjiʿ*) is built on the native Arabic root *sh-j-ʿ*, "to be brave" (Lane). Mushajjiʿ is the participle of shajjaʿa, to encourage.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
