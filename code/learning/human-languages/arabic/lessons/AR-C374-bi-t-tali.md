# بالتالي (bi-t-tālī) — consequently

## You'll want to know: بالتالي
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C374-RABT374-01]; assesses=[] -->

**بالتالي** — *bi-t-tālī* — "consequently".

> كان الطريق مغلقا، بالتالي تأخر الجميع.
>
> *kāna aṭ-ṭarīq mughlaqan, bi-t-tālī taʾakhkhara al-jamīʿ.*
>
> The road was closed; consequently everyone was late.

Usually with و: وبالتالي, and as a result.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**بالتالي** (*bi-t-tālī*) joins *bi-*, "by", with *at-tālī*, "the one that follows", built on the native Arabic root *t-l-w*, "to follow" (Lane). Literally, by what follows.
