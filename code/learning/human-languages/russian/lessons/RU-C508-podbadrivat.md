## You'll want to know: подбадривать
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C508-FANS-04]; assesses=[] -->

**подбадривать** (*podbádrivat*) — "to cheer on, to encourage".

> Родители подбадривают детей во время соревнований.
>
> *Rodíteli podbádrivayut detéy vo vrémya sorevnovániy.*
>
> Parents cheer their children on during competitions.

Imperfective; perfective подбодрить. Built on *бодрый* — brisk, cheerful.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*подбадривать* is built on *под-* and *бодрый* 'brisk, cheerful'; EtymDB has no entry for the verb, and *бодрый* is inherited from Proto-Slavic *bъdrъ* 'alert, cheerful' (EtymDB).
