German, chapter 79: Cup, Plate, Glass, Fork, Knife.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
die Tasse — a cup.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for a mirror, then the German for a flat.

You'll want to know: die Tasse.
die Tasse — "a cup".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: die Tasse]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: die Tasse, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say die Tasse]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the German for a mirror, then the German for a flat, then say die Tasse again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does die Tasse mean? ("A cup".) Say it once more.
