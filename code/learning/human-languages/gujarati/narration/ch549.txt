Gujarati, chapter 549: Goals and Medals.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ફૂટબોલ (phūṭbol) — football.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a winner, then the Gujarati for to win it for (a team).

You'll want to know: ફૂટબોલ.
ફૂટબોલ — phūṭbol — "football".
જો શનિવારે મેદાન ખાલી હશે તો અમે ફૂટબોલ રમીશું. jo śanivāre medān khālī haśe to ame phūṭbol ramīśũ. If the ground is free on Saturday, we'll play football.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ફૂટબોલ (phūṭbol) is borrowed from English football.

Lesson 4 of 5.
ઝીલવું (jhīlvũ) — to catch a ball.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a goal (in a game), then the Gujarati for a score.

You'll want to know: ઝીલવું.
ઝીલવું — jhīlvũ — "to catch a ball".
જો તું દડો ઝીલત તો આપણે જીતી જાત. jo tũ daḍo jhīlat to āpaṇe jītī jāt. If you had caught the ball, we would have won.
Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે દડો ઝીલ્યો, teṇe daḍo jhīlyo.

The word, taken apart.
ઝીલવું (jhīlvũ) is to catch something thrown or falling. CDIAL lists Marathi jhelṇẽ, "to catch", and Hindi jhelnā, "to undergo; to take", under a reconstructed jhēlati, "undergoes" (CDIAL 5413); it gives no Gujarati form, so its history is not settled.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ચંદ્રક (candrak) — a medal.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a score, then the Gujarati for to catch a ball.

You'll want to know: ચંદ્રક.
ચંદ્રક — candrak — "a medal".
જો મીરા પહેલી આવશે તો તેને ચંદ્રક મળશે. jo Mīrā pahelī āvśe to tene candrak maḷśe. If Mira comes first, she'll get a medal.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ચંદ્રક (candrak) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit candraka, "the moon; a ring shaped like the moon" (Monier-Williams): a round disc. It is built on ચંદ્ર, the moon.
