# Hide, then mix the familiar descriptions

One older action word joins the five descriptions just recalled. This is an
untimed word review, not a new A1 form, message, or scored mock.

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-LEX-C148-ACT148-01] -->

Hear the cue **to hide**. [PAUSE 4s] Say the earlier verb before the
spoken model: **ਲੁਕਣਾ** (*lukṇā*).

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-LEX-C147-QUAL147-01, PA-LEX-C147-QUAL147-02, PA-LEX-C147-QUAL147-03, PA-LEX-C147-QUAL147-04, PA-LEX-C147-QUAL147-05, PA-LEX-C148-ACT148-01] -->

Mix the old meanings. Pause after each cue before its spoken model:

- **young** — [PAUSE 4s] **ਜਵਾਨ** (*javān*).
- **to hide** — [PAUSE 4s] **ਲੁਕਣਾ** (*lukṇā*).
- **weak** — [PAUSE 4s] **ਕਮਜ਼ੋਰ** (*kamzor*).
- **rich** — [PAUSE 4s] **ਅਮੀਰ** (*amīr*).
- **strong** — [PAUSE 4s] **ਤਕੜਾ** (*takṛā*).
- **poor** — [PAUSE 4s] **ਗ਼ਰੀਬ** (*garīb*).

Every Punjabi word on this list was already taught. The order, not the
language, is new.

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-LEX-C147-QUAL147-01, PA-LEX-C147-QUAL147-02, PA-LEX-C147-QUAL147-03, PA-LEX-C147-QUAL147-04, PA-LEX-C147-QUAL147-05, PA-LEX-C148-ACT148-01] -->

Hear **poor**, **strong**, **to hide**, **rich**, **weak**, then **young**.
Pause after each cue and recall its Punjabi word before the model:
**ਗ਼ਰੀਬ**, **ਤਕੜਾ**, **ਲੁਕਣਾ**, **ਅਮੀਰ**, **ਕਮਜ਼ੋਰ**, **ਜਵਾਨ**.
Revisit only a missed old word.
