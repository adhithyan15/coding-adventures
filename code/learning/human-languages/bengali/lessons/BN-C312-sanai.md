## You'll want to know: সানাই
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C312-ONUSHTHAN312-03]; assesses=[] -->

**সানাই** — *sānāi* — "a shehnai (a wedding pipe)".

> বিয়েতে একটা লোক সারাদিন সানাই বাজাল।
>
> *biyete ekṭā lok sārādin sānāi bājālô.*
>
> At the wedding a man played the shehnai all day.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**সানাই** (*sānāi*) is usually traced to Persian *shahnāī*, 'the king's pipe'; its reedy sound means a wedding in Bengal.
