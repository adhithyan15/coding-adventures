Spanish, chapter 759: Sobre el escenario.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
el escenario — the stage; the setting.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for to skate, then the Spanish for the fan.

You'll want to know: el escenario.
el escenario — "the stage; the setting".
El cantante salió al escenario. The singer came out on stage.
Also a setting or scenario: el escenario del crimen.

The word, taken apart.
escenario is built on escena with the ending -ario; escena comes from Latin scaena, from Greek skēnḗ "tent, stage" (EtymDB).

What you've built.
One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.

Lesson 4 of 6.
actuar — to act, to perform.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for the show, then the Spanish for to applaud.

You'll want to know: actuar.
actuar — "to act, to perform".
Actúa en una obra de teatro. She is acting in a play.
Also to act, to take action. Forms: actúo, actúas.

The word, taken apart.
actuar comes from Medieval Latin āctuāre "to put into action" (EtymDB), built on āctus, from agere "to do".
