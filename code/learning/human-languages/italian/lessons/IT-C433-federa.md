## You'll want to know: la federa
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C433-CAMERA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**la federa** — "the pillowcase".

> La federa del cuscino è macchiata.
>
> *The pillowcase is stained.*

*Il lenzuolo* is the sheet.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*federa* was borrowed from Lombardic *fedara* 'feather' (EtymDB): first the cover of a feather pillow.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
