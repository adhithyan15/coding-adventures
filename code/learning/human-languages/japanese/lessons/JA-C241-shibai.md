## You'll want to know: しばい
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C241-OUTING241-02]; assesses=[] -->

**しばい** — *shibai* — "a stage play; theatre".

> **せんげつ ははと しばいを みました。** — *sengetsu haha to shibai o mimashita* — "Last month I saw a play with my mother."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**しばい** (*shibai*) is a native Japanese compound (wago), inherited rather than borrowed: it is built from *shiba*, "turf, lawn", and *i*, from *iru*, "to be, to sit" (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
