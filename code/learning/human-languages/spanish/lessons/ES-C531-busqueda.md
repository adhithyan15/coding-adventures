## You'll want to know: la búsqueda
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C531-BUSQUEDA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**la búsqueda** — "the search".

> La búsqueda duró toda la noche.
>
> *The search lasted all night.*

*En busca de* — in search of.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*búsqueda* is built on *buscar* 'to look for' (EtymDB). The origin of *buscar* itself is not settled.

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
