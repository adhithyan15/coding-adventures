## You'll want to know: कूंडो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C186-DHAKKAN186-02]; assesses=[] -->

**कूंडो** — *kū̃ḍo* — "an earthen basin".

> कूंडो भारी है।
>
> *kū̃ḍo bhārī hai.*
>
> The earthen basin is heavy.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**कूंडो** (*kū̃ḍo*) is inherited from Sanskrit *kuṇḍa*, "a bowl, a waterpot"; CDIAL lists Hindi *kū̃ḍā*, a clay pot that dough is kneaded in (CDIAL 3264). CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry.
