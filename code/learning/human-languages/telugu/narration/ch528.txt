Telugu, chapter 528: Brothers and Sisters.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
తోబుట్టువు (tōbuṭṭuvu) — a sibling.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a resignation, then the Telugu for to quit.

You'll want to know: తోబుట్టువు.
తోబుట్టువు — tōbuṭṭuvu — "a sibling".
నాకు ఇద్దరు తోబుట్టువులు ఉన్నారు. nāku iddaru tōbuṭṭuvulu unnāru. I have two siblings.
Plural: తోబుట్టువులు, tōbuṭṭuvulu.
Literally born with: brothers and sisters alike.

The word, taken apart.
తోబుట్టువు (tōbuṭṭuvu) is a native compound: tōḍa, "with" (DEDR 3563), and puṭṭu, "to be born" (DEDR 4264): one born with you.

Lesson 2 of 5.
అన్నదమ్ములు (annadammulu) — brothers; a set of brothers.

[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to quit, then the Telugu for a sibling.

You'll want to know: అన్నదమ్ములు.
అన్నదమ్ములు — annadammulu — "brothers; a set of brothers".
ఆ అన్నదమ్ములు కలిసి వ్యాపారం చేస్తున్నారు. ā annadammulu kalisi vyāpāraṁ cēstunnāru. Those brothers run a business together.
A pair word: అన్న, elder brother, + తమ్ముడు, younger brother.

The word, taken apart.
అన్నదమ్ములు (annadammulu) is a native pair word: anna, "elder brother" (DEDR 131), and tammuḍu, "younger brother" (DEDR 3085).

Lesson 3 of 5.
మరిది (maridi) — a husband's younger brother.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a sibling, then the Telugu for brothers.

You'll want to know: మరిది.
మరిది — maridi — "a husband's younger brother".
పెళ్ళి తరువాత మా మరిది మాతోనే ఉన్నాడు. peḷḷi taruvāta mā maridi mātōnē unnāḍu. After the wedding my husband's younger brother lived with us.
Also a younger sister's husband.

The word, taken apart.
మరిది (maridi) is native Dravidian: DEDR 4762 lists Telugu maṟãdi, "a spouse's younger brother, a younger sister's husband", beside maṟãdalu, the same for women.

Lesson 4 of 5.
ఆడపడుచు (āḍapaḍucu) — a husband's sister.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for brothers, then the Telugu for a husband's younger brother.

You'll want to know: ఆడపడుచు.
ఆడపడుచు — āḍapaḍucu — "a husband's sister".
పండుగకు ఆడపడుచు ఇంటికి వచ్చింది. paṇḍugaku āḍapaḍucu iṇṭiki vaccindi. My husband's sister came home for the festival.
Also a married daughter of the house visiting home.

The word, taken apart.
ఆడపడుచు (āḍapaḍucu) is built from ఆడ, "female", and native paḍucu, "a girl", which DEDR 3840 lists with paḍãti, "a woman".

Lesson 5 of 5.
వదిన (vadina) — an elder brother's wife.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a husband's younger brother, then the Telugu for a husband's sister.

You'll want to know: వదిన.
వదిన — vadina — "an elder brother's wife".
మా వదిన చాలా బాగా వంట చేస్తుంది. mā vadina cālā bāgā vaṇṭa cēstundi. My elder brother's wife cooks very well.
Also a husband's or a wife's elder sister.

The word, taken apart.
వదిన (vadina) is native Dravidian: DEDR 4690 lists Telugu vadine, "an elder sister-in-law, an elder brother's wife".
