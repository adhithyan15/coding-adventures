German, chapter 143: The Rent, an Advert, an Appointment, Opening Hours, a Receipt.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
die Miete — the rent.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for next year, then the German for to plan.

You'll want to know: die Miete.
die Miete — "the rent". The noun behind mieten: Die Miete ist hoch. — "The rent is high."

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.
