## You'll want to know: جائز
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C354-MAQUL354-03]; assesses=[] -->

**جائز** — *jāiz* — "legitimate; allowed".

> آپ کا سوال جائز ہے۔
>
> *āp kā savāl jāiz hai.*
>
> Your question is a fair one.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**جائز** (*jāiz*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *jāʾiz*, "permitted".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
