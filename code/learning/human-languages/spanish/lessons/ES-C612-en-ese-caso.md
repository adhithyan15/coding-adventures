## You'll want to know: en ese caso
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C612-SINO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**en ese caso** — "in that case".

> —No tengo coche. —En ese caso, vamos en tren.
>
> *I do not have a car. In that case, we will go by train.*

It answers a condition that someone has given.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*en ese caso* is built on *caso*, a learned borrowing from Latin *casus* 'an event', from *cadere* 'to fall' (EtymDB).
