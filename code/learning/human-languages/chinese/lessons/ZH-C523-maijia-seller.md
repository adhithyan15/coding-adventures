## You'll want to know: 卖家
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C523-TRADE523-03]; assesses=[] -->

**卖家** — *màijiā* — "seller".

> **我觉得这个卖家最好** *wǒ juéde zhège màijiā zuì hǎo* — I think this seller is the best.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**卖家** (*màijiā*) is built from 卖 'to sell' and 家, both inherited from Middle Chinese, *meaH* and *kae* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription). The dictionary glosses the second 'a dwelling'; its use for the one who does a trade is not given there.
