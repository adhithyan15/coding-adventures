## You'll want to know: あぶない
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C223-SAFETY223-01]; assesses=[] -->

**あぶない** — *abunai* — "dangerous".

> **この みちは あぶないですか。** — *kono michi wa abunai desu ka* — "Is this road dangerous?"

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**あぶない** (*abunai*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi, and its older origin is not settled (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
