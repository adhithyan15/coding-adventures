## You'll want to know: ঘাড়
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C466-SHORIR466-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ঘাড়** — *ghāṛ* — "the back of the neck; the nape".

> সারাদিন ল্যাপটপে কাজ করে অরুণের ঘাড়ে ব্যথা।
>
> *sārādin læpṭôpe kāj kôre ôruner ghāṛe bæthā.*
>
> After a day's work on the laptop, Arun's neck aches.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ঘাড়** (*ghāṛ*) is listed by CDIAL for Bengali under Sanskrit *ghāṭā*, "the back of the neck" (CDIAL 4459).
