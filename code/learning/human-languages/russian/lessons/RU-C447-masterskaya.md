## You'll want to know: мастерская
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C447-MASTERSKAYA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**мастерская** (*masterskáya*) — "a workshop, a repair shop".

> В нашем доме есть маленькая мастерская.
>
> *V náshem dóme yest málenkaya masterskáya.*
>
> There is a small workshop in our building.

An adjective used as a noun: *в мастерской*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*мастерская* is built on *мастер* 'a master, a craftsman' with an adjective ending. EtymDB traces *мастер* through Old East Slavic and Old French *maistre*, which goes back to Latin *magister* 'a master'.
