# (el mensaje del vecino) — read it, then answer

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-C470-PARAGUAS-01, ES-LEX-C470-PARAGUAS-04] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say **el paraguas** and **avisar**. One is lost and the other is
what the message is asking for.

## Reading: the message
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-C470-PARAGUAS-01, ES-LEX-C470-PARAGUAS-02] -->

To the building group, this morning:

> Hola, ¿sois vosotros los del piso doce? Me he dejado un **paraguas** azul,
> con el **mango** de madera, en el ascensor esta mañana y ya no lo
> **encuentro** por ninguna parte. Si lo habéis visto, **avisadme**, por
> favor.

Two things to notice before you answer.

**Azul, de madera.** The colour is the umbrella's and the wood is the
handle's. Spanish hangs each description on the noun it belongs to, and *de
madera* is how you say what a thing is made of.

**Por ninguna parte.** The stacking is
[no … nada](./ES-C267-no-nada.md)'s rule, unchanged: *no* in front of the verb
and the negative word after it, both required. What is new is only the phrase.
*Ninguna parte* joins *nada*, *nadie* and *nunca* as a negative word, and it is
two words rather than one.

The trap is the English. *No lo encuentro por ninguna parte* comes out as *I
can't find it **anywhere***, which looks positive — so the pull is to reach
for *en cualquier parte*. Spanish keeps the negative on both halves.

## Grammar Lens: what the message is actually asking
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-C470-PARAGUAS-03, ES-LEX-C470-PARAGUAS-04] -->

Read the last sentence again. It does not ask anyone to look.

| what it says | what it does not say |
|---|---|
| *si lo habéis visto* | *buscadlo* |

The writer is asking to be told about something that may already have
happened, not asking the building to start a search. That is *avisar* doing
exactly the work it was built for, and it is the polite shape of this kind of
message: you ask for information, not labour.

Answer in the same register.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-C470-PARAGUAS-01, ES-LEX-C470-PARAGUAS-02, ES-LEX-C470-PARAGUAS-03, ES-LEX-C470-PARAGUAS-04] -->

[PAUSE 3s] Say what was lost, where, and what it looks like — colour and
handle both.

[PAUSE 3s] Now answer the message twice. First: you have seen it, and you say
where. Then: you have not, and you offer to let them know if it turns up.
