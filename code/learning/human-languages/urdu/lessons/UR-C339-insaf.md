## You'll want to know: انصاف
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C339-IKHTIYAR339-02]; assesses=[] -->

**انصاف** — *insāf* — "justice; fairness".

> سب کو انصاف ملنا چاہیے۔
>
> *sab ko insāf milnā chāhiye.*
>
> Everyone should get justice.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**انصاف** (*insāf*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *inṣāf*, "equity".
