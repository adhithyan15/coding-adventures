## You'll want to know: पंक्चर
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C401-INDHAN401-04]; assesses=[] -->

**पंक्चर** — *paṅkcar* — "a puncture; a flat tyre".

> वाटेत सायकलचं चाक पंक्चर झालं.
>
> *vāṭet sāykalcaṁ cāk paṅkcar jhālaṁ.*
>
> On the way the bicycle wheel got a puncture.

Neuter.

पंक्चर होणे, to get a puncture; पंक्चर काढणे, to mend one.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पंक्चर** (*paṅkcar*) is a loan from English *puncture*.
