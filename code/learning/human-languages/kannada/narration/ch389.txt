Kannada, chapter 389: Praise and Prestige.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಶ್ಲಾಘಿಸು (ślāghisu) — to commend.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for well-being, then the Kannada for to condemn.

You'll want to know: ಶ್ಲಾಘಿಸು.
ಶ್ಲಾಘಿಸು — ślāghisu — "to commend".
ಊರಿನ ಜನರು ಆ ಹುಡುಗನ ಧೈರ್ಯವನ್ನು ಶ್ಲಾಘಿಸಿದರು. ūrina janaru ā huḍugana dhairyavannu ślāghisidaru. The villagers commended the boy's courage.
Past (I …): ಶ್ಲಾಘಿಸಿದೆ, ślāghiside.
What is commended takes -ಅನ್ನು. ಶ್ಲಾಘನೀಯ, praiseworthy.

The word, taken apart.
ಶ್ಲಾಘಿಸು (ślāghisu) is built on Sanskrit ślāghā, "boasting; praise" (CDIAL 12736), with the Kannada verb ending -isu.

You'll want to know: ಪುರಸ್ಕರಿಸು.

ಶಾಲೆ ಉತ್ತಮ ವಿದ್ಯಾರ್ಥಿಗಳನ್ನು ಪುರಸ್ಕರಿಸಿತು. śāle uttama vidyārthigaḷannu puraskarisitu. The school honoured its best students.
Past (I …): ಪುರಸ್ಕರಿಸಿದೆ, puraskariside.
ಪುರಸ್ಕಾರ, an honour or an award.

The word, taken apart.
ಪುರಸ್ಕರಿಸು (puraskarisu) is built on Sanskrit puraskāra, "placing in front, honouring" (Monier-Williams), with the Kannada verb ending -isu.

Lesson 3 of 5.

You'll want to know: ಪ್ರತಿಷ್ಠೆ.

The word, taken apart.
ಉತ್ಪ್ರೇಕ್ಷಿಸು (utprēkṣisu) is built on Sanskrit utprekṣā, which in poetics names a fanciful comparison (Monier-Williams), with the Kannada verb ending -isu.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ಸ್ಥಾನಮಾನ (sthānamāna) — status; standing.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for prestige, then the Kannada for to exaggerate.

You'll want to know: ಸ್ಥಾನಮಾನ.
ಸ್ಥಾನಮಾನ — sthānamāna — "status; standing".
ಊರಿನಲ್ಲಿ ಅವರಿಗೆ ಒಳ್ಳೆಯ ಸ್ಥಾನಮಾನ ಇದೆ. ūrinalli avarige oḷḷeya sthānamāna ide. They have a good standing in the town.
ಸ್ಥಾನ (place) + ಮಾನ (honour).

The word, taken apart.
ಸ್ಥಾನಮಾನ (sthānamāna) is built from Sanskrit sthāna, "a place, a position" (CDIAL 13753), paired with māna, "honour, respect" (Monier-Williams).
