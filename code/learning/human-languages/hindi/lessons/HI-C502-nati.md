## You'll want to know: नाती
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C502-POTA502-02]; assesses=[] -->

**नाती** — *nātī* — "a grandson (a daughter's son)".

> नानी अपने नाती को कहानी सुना रही हैं।
>
> *nānī apne nātī ko kahānī sunā rahī haiṁ.*
>
> Grandmother is telling her grandson a story.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**नाती** (*nātī*) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Hindi *nātī*, "daughter's son", under Sanskrit *náptṛ*, "grandson" (CDIAL 6955b). Hindi keeps the father's side (*potā*) and the mother's side (*nātī*) apart.
