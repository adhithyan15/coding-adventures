## You'll want to know: मजबूर
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C366-VAJIB366-05]; assesses=[] -->

**मजबूर** — *majbūr* — "forced, compelled".

> बारिश की वजह से हम घर लौटने पर मजबूर हुए।
>
> *bāriś kī vajah se ham ghar lauṭne par majbūr hue.*
>
> The rain forced us to go back home.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**मजबूर** (*majbūr*) is usually traced to Arabic *majbūr*, "compelled", which reached Hindi through Persian.
