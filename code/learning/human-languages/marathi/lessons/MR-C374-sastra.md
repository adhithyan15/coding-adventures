## You'll want to know: शास्त्र
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C374-PRAKRIYA374-05]; assesses=[] -->

**शास्त्र** — *śāstra* — "a science; a discipline".

> मला लहानपणापासून शास्त्र आवडतं.
>
> *malā lahānapṇāpāsūn śāstra āvaḍtaṁ.*
>
> I have liked science since I was a child.

Neuter.

Also at the end of names of fields: भाषाशास्त्र, linguistics.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**शास्त्र** (*śāstra*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *śāstra*, "command, advice, treatise" (CDIAL 12422).
