## You'll want to know: навсегда
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C278-VPERVYYE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**навсегда** (*navsegdá*) — "for ever, for good".

> Он уехал из города навсегда.
>
> *On uyékhal iz góroda navsegdá.*
>
> He left the town for good.

*Раз и навсегда* — once and for all.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*навсегда* is built on *на* and *всегда* "always", itself built on *весь* "all"; EtymDB lists no parent for *всегда*.
