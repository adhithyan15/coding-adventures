## You'll want to know: اشارہ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C363-ANDESHA363-02]; assesses=[] -->

**اشارہ** — *ishārā* — "a hint; a signal".

> اگر مدد چاہیے تو مجھے اشارہ دیجیے۔
>
> *agar madad chāhiye to mujhe ishārā dījiye.*
>
> If you need help, give me a sign.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**اشارہ** (*ishārā*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *ishāra*, "a sign, a gesture".
