## You'll want to know: द्विधा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C541-DVIDHA541-03]; assesses=[] -->

**द्विधा** — *dvidhā* — "in two ways".

> यदि मार्गः द्विधा भवति, तर्हि कुत्र गच्छामि?
>
> *yadi mārgaḥ dvidhā bhavati, tarhi kutra gacchāmi?*
>
> If the road splits in two, which way do I go?

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**द्विधा** (*dvidhā*) is built on *dvi*, "two", with the ending *-dhā*, "in ... ways"; MW gives *dvidhā* as in two ways or parts, divided (MW).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
