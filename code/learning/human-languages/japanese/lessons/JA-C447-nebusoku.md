## You'll want to know: ねぶそく
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C447-TIRED447-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ねぶそく** — *nebusoku* — "lack of sleep".

> **ねぶそくで あたまが いたいです。** — *nebusoku de atama ga itai desu* — "My head aches from lack of sleep."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ねぶそく** (*nebusoku*) is a mixed word: native *ne*, "sleeping", from *neru*, joined to Sino-Japanese *busoku*, "shortage", from *bu*, "negative" (Middle Chinese *pjuw*), and *soku*, "be sufficient" (*tsjowk*), readings borrowed from Chinese (JMdict, KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
