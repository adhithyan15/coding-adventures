## You'll want to know: la conquête
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C324-CONQUETE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**la conquête** — "the conquest".

> La conquête de l'espace a commencé en 1957.
>
> *The conquest of space began in 1957.*

Informal: *sa dernière conquête* — his latest girlfriend or boyfriend.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*conquête* comes from Old French *conqueste*, usually traced to Vulgar Latin *conquaesita* "things sought and won", built on *quaerere* "to seek" (Lewis & Short).
