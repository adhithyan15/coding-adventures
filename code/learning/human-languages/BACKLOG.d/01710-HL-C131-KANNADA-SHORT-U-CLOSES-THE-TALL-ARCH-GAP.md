## HL-C131 — Kannada short u closes the tall-arch gap

After Japanese hiragana **ゆ** landed, Kannada independent vowel **ಉ** led the
measured queue at **2 affected realizations**.

Gopala Krishna A's CC BY-SA 4.0, 35-frame Wikimedia Commons animation supplies
the zero-lift order: turn around the compact upper-left loop, descend through
the broad lower-left bowl, climb over the tall middle arch into the lower-right
bowl, then sweep around the outer-right curve to the open upper terminal. The
learner filmstrip fits that order to the bundled Noto Sans Kannada outline
without presenting print geometry as an independent handwriting source.

This closes both affected realizations for **ಉ** and reranks Arabic **ق** first
at **2**.
