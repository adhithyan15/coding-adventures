Gujarati, chapter 78: Monday to Friday.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
સોમવાર (somvār) — Monday.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for later, then the Gujarati for before.

You'll want to know: સોમવાર.
સોમવાર (somvār) — "Monday". Every day name ends in વાર, day.

What you've built.
One more word for monday to friday.
