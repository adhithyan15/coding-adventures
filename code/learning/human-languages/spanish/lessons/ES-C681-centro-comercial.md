## You'll want to know: el centro comercial
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C681-COMERCIO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**el centro comercial** — "the shopping centre, the mall".

> El centro comercial abre hasta las diez.
>
> *The shopping centre is open until ten.*

In parts of Latin America also *el mall* or *el shopping*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*centro* comes from Latin *centrum*, Greek *kéntron* "a sharp point"; *comercial* is built on *comercio*, a learned borrowing from Latin *commercium* "trade".
