## You'll want to know: જાસૂસ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C292-KAUBHAND292-04]; assesses=[] -->

**જાસૂસ** — *jāsūs* — "a detective; a spy".

> જાસૂસે કહ્યું કે જો ઠગ હજી શહેરમાં છે તો પોલીસ તેને પકડશે.
>
> *jāsūse kahyũ ke jo ṭhag hajī śahermā̃ che to polīs tene pakaḍśe.*
>
> The detective said that if the swindler was still in the city, the police would catch him.

A masculine or feminine noun, by the person meant.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**જાસૂસ** (*jāsūs*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *jāsūs*, "a spy". In stories it is the detective who works out who did it.
