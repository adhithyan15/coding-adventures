# Give the message its first gentle timer

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MR-A1-MESSAGE-LINE-GREETING, MR-A1-MESSAGE-LINE-NAME, MR-A1-MESSAGE-LINE-MEETING, MR-A1-MESSAGE-LINE-THANKS, MR-A1-MESSAGE-LINE-SEE-AGAIN, MR-A1-MESSAGE-LINE-SEE-TOMORROW] -->

Close the earlier message. Mira is your reader; introduce Arun, thank her, and
say you will meet tomorrow. This is familiar material, not new vocabulary.

## Writing — generous partial timer
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MR-A1-MESSAGE-LINE-GREETING, MR-A1-MESSAGE-LINE-NAME, MR-A1-MESSAGE-LINE-MEETING, MR-A1-MESSAGE-LINE-THANKS, MR-A1-MESSAGE-LINE-SEE-AGAIN, MR-A1-MESSAGE-LINE-SEE-TOMORROW] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: timed-assessment-production -->

Set a **90-second partial timer**. On blank paper write only the opening and
closing lines, without a model or romanization. Stop when it rings; keep the
first attempt visible. The full 30–40-word message comes next lesson.

<!-- hl-activity: {"id":"MR-A1M19-separate-repair","kind":"text","assesses":["MR-A1-MESSAGE-LINE-SEE-TOMORROW"],"prompt":"After scoring, on a separate line repair only the spelling in उदय भेटू.","answer":"उद्या भेटू.","accepted":[],"feedback":{"correct":"This orthographic repair is separate from the stopped message attempt.","incorrect":"Correct only the tomorrow word; keep the first attempt unchanged."},"response_seconds":15} -->

Check **task fulfilment** (reader, introduction, thanks, tomorrow),
**organisation** (opening before closing), **Marathi control** (familiar
forms), and **orthographic control** (Devanagari letters and spacing). After
scoring, make one 20-second **repair** to your weakest dimension on a separate
line. Do not replace the timed attempt. This is not a full A1 mock.
