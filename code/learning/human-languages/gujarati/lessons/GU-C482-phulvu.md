## You'll want to know: ફૂલવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C482-THOKAR482-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ફૂલવું** — *phūlvũ* — "to swell up; to puff out".

> પડ્યા પછી પગની ઘૂંટી ફૂલી ગઈ.
>
> *paḍyā pachī pagnī ghū̃ṭī phūlī gaī.*
>
> After the fall my ankle swelled up.

Past (it …): તે ઘૂંટી ફૂલી ગઈ, *te ghū̃ṭī phūlī gaī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ફૂલવું** (*phūlvũ*) is inherited from Sanskrit *phullati*, "expands, opens like a flower"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb, "to swell, blossom" (CDIAL 9093).
