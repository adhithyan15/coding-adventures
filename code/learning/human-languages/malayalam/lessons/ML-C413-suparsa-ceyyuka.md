## You'll want to know: ശുപാർശ ചെയ്യുക
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C413-UPADESAM413-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ശുപാർശ ചെയ്യുക** — *śupārśa ceyyuka* — "to recommend".

> ഞാൻ ആ പുസ്തകം എല്ലാവർക്കും ശുപാർശ ചെയ്യുന്നു.
>
> *ñān ā pustakaṁ ellāvarkkuṁ śupārśa ceyyunnu.*
>
> I recommend that book to everyone.

Past: ശുപാർശ ചെയ്തു, *śupārśa ceytu*.

The noun ശുപാർശ is a recommendation, also a word put in for someone: ഒരു ശുപാർശക്കത്ത്, a letter of recommendation.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ശുപാർശ ചെയ്യുക** (*śupārśa ceyyuka*) is a phrase verb built on the noun ശുപാർശ, "a recommendation", which is usually traced to Persian *sifāriš*, "recommendation"; ചെയ്യുക, "to do", makes it a verb.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
