## You'll want to know: તળિયું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C473-JANGH473-02]; assesses=[] -->

**તળિયું** — *taḷiyũ* — "the sole of the foot".

> ગરમ રેતીથી પગનાં તળિયાં બળ્યાં.
>
> *garam retīthī pagnā̃ taḷiyā̃ baḷyā̃.*
>
> The hot sand burned the soles of my feet.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**તળિયું** (*taḷiyũ*) is inherited from Sanskrit *tala*, "base, bottom"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati word, "bottom, flat surface, sole" (CDIAL 5731).
