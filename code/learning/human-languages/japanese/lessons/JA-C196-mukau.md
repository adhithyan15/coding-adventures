## You'll want to know: むかう
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C196-ADDR196-01]; assesses=[] -->

**むかう** — *mukau* — "to head for; to face".

> **いま えきに むかいます。** — *ima eki ni mukaimasu* — "I am heading for the station now."

Polite **むかいます**, *mukaimasu*; a godan verb.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**むかう** (*mukau*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
