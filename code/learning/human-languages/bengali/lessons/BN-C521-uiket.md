## You'll want to know: উইকেট
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C521-BAT521-02]; assesses=[] -->

**উইকেট** — *uikeṭ* — "a wicket".

> বলটা উইকেটে লাগল।
>
> *Bôlṭā uikeṭe lāglô.*
>
> The ball hit the wicket.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**উইকেট** (*uikeṭ*) is borrowed from English *wicket*.
