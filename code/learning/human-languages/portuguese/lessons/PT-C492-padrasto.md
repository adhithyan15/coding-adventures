## You'll want to know: o padrasto
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C492-FAMILIA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**o padrasto** — "stepfather".

> Ela dá-se muito bem com o padrasto.
>
> *She gets on very well with her stepfather.*

Built on Latin *pater* "father", not on *pai*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*padrasto* is inherited from Latin *patraster* (EtymDB), built on *pater* "father"; in Latin inscriptions the word named a father-in-law (Lewis & Short), and in Portuguese it settled on a stepfather.
