## You'll want to know: la mejilla
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C736-CARA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**la mejilla** — "the cheek".

> Me dio un beso en la mejilla.
>
> *She kissed me on the cheek.*

Colloquially also *el cachete* in Latin America.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*mejilla* is usually traced to Latin *maxilla* "jaw"; the Latin *-x-* became the Spanish *j* sound.
