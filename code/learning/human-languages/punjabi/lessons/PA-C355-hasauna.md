## You'll want to know: ਹਸਾਉਣਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C355-RIDDLE355-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ਹਸਾਉਣਾ** — *hasāuṇā* — "to make someone laugh".

> ਅਮਨ ਸਭ ਨੂੰ ਹਸਾਉਂਦਾ ਹੈ।
>
> *Aman sabh nū̃ hasāundā hai.*
>
> Aman makes everyone laugh.

Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਬੱਚਿਆਂ ਨੂੰ ਹਸਾਇਆ, *maiṁ bachchiāṁ nū̃ hasāiā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਹਸਾਉਣਾ** (*hasāuṇā*) is the causative partner of *hassṇā*, "to laugh", which goes back to a reconstructed *\*hasyate*, "one laughs"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *hassṇā* under it (CDIAL 14048).
