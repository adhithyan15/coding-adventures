# ઢ — retroflex ddha

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-SCRIPT-DHA-01] -->

Write dental **ધ** once and say *dha* with the tongue at the teeth. Curl the
tongue back and keep the breathy release for retroflex *ddha*.

## Script — meet one form
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-SCRIPT-DDHA-01]; assesses=[] -->

Keep **ઢ** visible. Sweep right across the high shoulder, descend through the
middle and circle the broad outer lower bowl, then continue around the small
inner loop without lifting.

## Writing — notice and trace
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-SCRIPT-DDHA-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: observe-trace -->

Finger-trace **ઢ** once, then air-trace it once. Notice high shoulder, outer
bowl, inner loop; no pen lift.

## Writing — copy with the model
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-SCRIPT-DDHA-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: guided-copy -->

Copy visible **ઢ** once. Compare only the outer bowl and inner loop; repair one
landmark if needed.

## Writing — recall after hiding
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-SCRIPT-DDHA-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: delayed-copy -->
<!-- hl-activity: {"id":"GU-W07-ddha-recall","kind":"text","assesses":["GU-SCRIPT-DDHA-01"],"prompt":"Hide the model and write retroflex aspirated ddha from memory.","answer":"ઢ","accepted":[],"feedback":{"correct":"ઢ — the shoulder, outer bowl, and inner loop are intact.","incorrect":"Look once at ઢ, hide it, and repair only the differing landmark."},"response_seconds":20} -->

Hide the model and write retroflex *ddha* once. Uncover **ઢ**, compare, and stop
after one repair.

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-SCRIPT-DDHA-01, GU-SCRIPT-DHA-01] -->

[PAUSE 3s] Read **ધ ઢ** aloud and keep dental *dha* apart from retroflex
*ddha*.
