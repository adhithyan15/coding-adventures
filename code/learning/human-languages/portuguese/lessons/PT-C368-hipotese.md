## You'll want to know: a hipótese
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C368-HIPOTESE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**a hipótese** — "the hypothesis; the chance, the possibility".

> Há uma hipótese de o voo chegar mais cedo.
>
> *There is a chance that the flight will arrive earlier.*

In Portugal, *hipótese* also means chance: *não tenho hipótese* — I have no chance. *Na hipótese de* — in the event of.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*hipótese* is a learned borrowing from Late Latin *hypothesis* (EtymDB), from Greek *hupóthesis* "base, basis of an argument, supposition", built on *hupó* "under" and *thésis* "a placing".
