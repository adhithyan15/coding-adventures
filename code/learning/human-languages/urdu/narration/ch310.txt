Urdu, chapter 310: Swords and Shields.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
تلوار (talvār) — a sword.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a cannon, then the Urdu for to clash.

You'll want to know: تلوار.
تلوار — talvār — "a sword".
سپاہی کے پاس تلوار تھی۔ sipāhī ke pās talvār thī. The soldier had a sword.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
تلوار (talvār) is inherited from Sanskrit taravāri, "a sword" (CDIAL 5706), where CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin talwār. It is feminine.

Lesson 5 of 5.
لشکر (lashkar) — a host; an army on the march.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a spear, then the Urdu for a bow for arrows.

You'll want to know: لشکر.
لشکر — lashkar — "a host; an army on the march".
لشکر شام کو شہر پہنچا۔ lashkar shām ko shahr pahuṅchā. The army reached the city in the evening.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
لشکر (lashkar) is usually traced to Persian lashkar, "an army, a camp". It is the old word of the war stories; فوج is the army of today.
