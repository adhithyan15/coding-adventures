## You'll want to know: यानी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C350-MISAAL350-03]; assesses=[] -->

**यानी** — *yānī* — "that is to say, in other words".

> वह परसों आएगा, यानी बुधवार को।
>
> *vah parsoṁ āegā, yānī budhvār ko.*
>
> He'll come the day after tomorrow, that is, on Wednesday.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**यानी** (*yānī*) is usually traced to Arabic *yaʿnī*, "it means", which reached Hindi through Persian.
