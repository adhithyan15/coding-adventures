Russian, chapter 468: Heat.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
жара (zhará) — hot weather, heat.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to feel the cold, then the Russian for to slip.

You'll want to know: жара.
жара (zhará) — "hot weather, heat".
В июле в городе стояла жара. V iyúle v górode stoyála zhará. In July the town had a spell of hot weather.
Feminine. В жару — in hot weather.

The word, taken apart.
жара (zhará) goes with жар 'heat'. EtymDB takes жара (zhará) from Proto-Slavic žārĭ and the PIE root gʷʰer- 'to heat'; the same root is in гореть 'to burn'.

Lesson 2 of 7.
душный (dúshnyy) — stuffy, close.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to slip, then the Russian for hot weather.

You'll want to know: душный.
душный (dúshnyy) — "stuffy, close".
Был душный летний вечер. Byl dúshnyy létniy vécher. It was a stuffy summer evening.
Душно — it is stuffy.

The word, taken apart.
душный (dúshnyy) is built on дух 'breath, air' with -ный: air that chokes the breath. EtymDB lists душный (dúshnyy) without a deeper source.

Lesson 4 of 7.
потеть (potét) — to sweat.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for stuffy, then the Russian for stuffiness.

You'll want to know: потеть.
потеть (potét) — "to sweat".
В жару я сильно потею. V zharú ya sílno potéyu. In hot weather I sweat a lot.
Imperfective; perfective вспотеть. Present потею, потеешь. Окна потеют — the windows mist up.

The word, taken apart.
потеть (potét) is built on пот 'sweat', which is inherited from Proto-Slavic potŭ 'sweat' (EtymDB); EtymDB lists потеть (potét) itself without a source.

Lesson 5 of 7.
таять (táyat) — to melt, to thaw.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for stuffiness, then the Russian for to sweat.

You'll want to know: таять.
таять (táyat) — "to melt, to thaw".
Мороженое быстро тает на солнце. Morózhenoye býstro táyet na sólntse. The ice cream melts quickly in the sun.
Imperfective; perfective растаять. Present таю, таешь.

The word, taken apart.
таять (táyat) is inherited from Proto-Slavic tajati (EtymDB), with the sense 'to melt, to thaw'.
