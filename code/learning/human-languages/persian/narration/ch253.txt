Persian, chapter 253: To get off, To enter, To go out, To push, To throw.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
پیاده شدن (piyâde shodan) — to get off (a vehicle).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a guard, then the Persian for a street sweeper.

You'll want to know: پیاده شدن.
پیاده شدن — piyâde shodan — "to get off (a vehicle)".

The word, taken apart.
piyâde shodan is "to become on foot": piyâde is inherited, from Middle Persian payādag, "foot soldier" (EtymDB, which also lists the sense "chess pawn"). shodan, "to become", is an inherited verb; it is usually traced to Middle Persian šudan, "to go".

Lesson 4 of 5.
هل دادن (hel dâdan) — to push.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to enter, then the Persian for to go out.

You'll want to know: هل دادن.
هل دادن — hel dâdan — "to push".

The word, taken apart.
hel dâdan pairs hel, "a push", with dâdan, "to give". Where hel comes from is not settled. dâdan, "to give", is an inherited verb; EtymDB takes it back to the Indo-European root deh₃-, "to give".
