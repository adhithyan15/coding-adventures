Sanskrit, chapter 89: Beautiful, Far and Near.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
सुन्दरः (sundaraḥ) — beautiful.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a son, then the Sanskrit for a daughter.

You'll want to know: सुन्दरः.
सुन्दरः (sundaraḥ) — "beautiful". सुन्दरः मयूरः — "a beautiful peacock".

What you've built.
Before the noun, and it agrees.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: sundaraḥ]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: sundaraḥ, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say sundaraḥ mayūraḥ]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Sanskrit for a son, then the Sanskrit for a daughter, then say sundaraḥ again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does सुन्दरः (sundaraḥ) mean? ("Beautiful".) Say it once more.

Lesson 5 of 7.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: मधुरः.
मधुरः (madhuraḥ) — "sweet", from मधु, "honey". मधुरम् आम्रम् — "a sweet mango".

What you've built.
Honey, as a describing word.

Lesson 6 of 7.

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
Mango, banana, oil, bread, pudding? (आम्रम्, कदलीफलम्, तैलम्, रोटिका, पायसम्.) Elephant, horse, camel, mouse, cat? (गजः, अश्वः, उष्ट्रः, मूषकः, मार्जारः.) Crow, parrot, peacock, tortoise, frog? (काकः, शुकः, मयूरः, कूर्मः, मण्डूकः.) Tree, seed, root, garden, forest? (वृक्षः, बीजम्, मूलम्, उद्यानम्, अरण्यम्.) Lake, well, pond, island, shore? (सरः, कूपः, तडागः, द्वीपः, तीरम्.) School, student, teacher, lesson, exam? (विद्यालयः, छात्रः, अध्यापकः, पाठः, परीक्षा.)

Girl, young man, friend, son, daughter? (बालिका, युवकः, सखी, पुत्रः, पुत्री.) Beautiful, far, near, hard, sweet? (सुन्दरः (sundaraḥ), दूरम् (dūram), समीपम् (samīpam), कठिनः (kaṭhinaḥ), मधुरः (madhuraḥ).)
