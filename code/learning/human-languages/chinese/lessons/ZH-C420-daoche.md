## You'll want to know: dàochē
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C420-PARK420-04]; assesses=[] -->

*dàochē* — "to reverse the car; to back up".

> *tā zài tíngchēchǎng dàochē* — He's reversing in the car park.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*dàochē* is built from *dào* 'backwards' and 车 'vehicle', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *tawH* and *tsyhae* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first as hanging upside down.

One more word for getting around.
