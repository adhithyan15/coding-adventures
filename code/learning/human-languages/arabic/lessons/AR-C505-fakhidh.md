## You'll want to know: فخذ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C505-ADHM505-03]; assesses=[] -->

**فخذ** — *fakhidh* — "a thigh".

> أكلنا فخذ الدجاج المشوي.
>
> *akalnā fakhidh ad-dajāj al-mashwī.*
>
> We ate the grilled chicken thigh.

Plural: أفخاذ, *afkhādh*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**فخذ** (*fakhidh*) is built on the native Arabic root *f-kh-dh*, "the thigh" (Lane). Lane also gives fakhidh as a branch of a tribe, below the clan.
