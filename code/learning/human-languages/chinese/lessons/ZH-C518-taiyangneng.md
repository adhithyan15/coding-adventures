## You'll want to know: 太阳能
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C518-SUN518-03]; assesses=[] -->

**太阳能** — *tàiyángnéng* — "solar energy; solar power".

> **我们家用了五年太阳能** *wǒmen jiā yòng le wǔ nián tàiyángnéng* — We've used solar power at home for five years.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**太阳能** (*tàiyángnéng*) is built from 太阳 'the sun' and 能 'energy', all inherited from Middle Chinese, *thajH*, *yang* and *nong* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription). The dictionary glosses the last 'skilled' and 'able'; the sense 'energy' is modern, and is not given there.
