## You'll want to know: வாந்தி
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C88-DOCTOR-02]; assesses=[] -->

**வாந்தி** (*vānti*) — "vomiting".

**வாந்தி வருகிறது** — "I feel sick", literally "vomiting is coming".
