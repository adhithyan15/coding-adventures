## You'll want to know: तोडगा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C548-ADTHALA548-02]; assesses=[] -->

**तोडगा** — *toḍgā* — "a way out; a solution".

> दोन्ही भावांनी भांडणावर एक चांगला तोडगा काढला.
>
> *donhī bhāvānnī bhāṇḍṇāvar ek cāṅglā toḍgā kāḍhlā.*
>
> The two brothers found a good way out of the quarrel.

Masculine.

तोडगा काढणे, to find a way out.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**तोडगा** (*toḍgā*) is usually traced to तोडणे, "to break", which CDIAL lists under Sanskrit *trōṭayati*, "breaks" (CDIAL 6079): a way of breaking a deadlock. The ending -गा is not explained there.
