## You'll want to know: partidario
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C578-DESACUER-04]; assesses=[] -->

**partidario** — "in favour; supporter".

> Soy partidario de trabajar en casa.
>
> *I am in favour of working from home.*

*Ser partidario de* + noun or infinitive. As a noun: *los partidarios del plan*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*partidario* is built on *partido* 'a side, a party', from *partir* 'to divide', which is inherited from Latin *partire*, built on *pars* 'part' (Lewis & Short).
