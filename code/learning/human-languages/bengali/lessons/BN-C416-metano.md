## You'll want to know: মেটানো
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C416-KISTI416-05]; assesses=[] -->

**মেটানো** — *meṭāno* — "to settle (a bill); to make good".

> হোটেল ছাড়ার আগে আমরা বিল মেটালাম।
>
> *hoṭel chhāṛār āge āmrā bil meṭālām.*
>
> Before leaving the hotel we settled the bill.

Past (he/she …): তিনি বিল মেটালেন, *tini bil meṭālen*.

Also of a quarrel or a need: ঝগড়া মেটানো, to settle a quarrel; খিদে মেটানো, to satisfy hunger.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**মেটানো** (*meṭāno*) is the causing form of মেটা, which CDIAL lists as Bengali *meṭā*, "to be wiped out", under Sanskrit *mṛṣṭá*, "rubbed, washed" (CDIAL 10299): to settle a bill is to wipe it out.
