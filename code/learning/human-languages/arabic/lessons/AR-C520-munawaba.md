## You'll want to know: مناوبة
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C520-DAWAM520-02]; assesses=[] -->

**مناوبة** — *munāwaba* — "a shift, a turn on duty".

> عند الممرضة مناوبة في الليل.
>
> *ʿinda al-mumarriḍa munāwaba fī al-layl.*
>
> The nurse has a night shift.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**مناوبة** (*munāwaba*) is built on the native Arabic root *n-w-b*, "to come in turn" (Lane). Munāwaba is the verbal noun of Form III: taking turns.
