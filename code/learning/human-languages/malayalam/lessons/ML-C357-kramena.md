## You'll want to know: ക്രമേണ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C357-PINNITU357-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ക്രമേണ** — *kramēṇa* — "gradually".

> മഴ ക്രമേണ കുറഞ്ഞു.
>
> *maḻa kramēṇa kuṟaññu.*
>
> The rain gradually eased.

A Sanskrit form kept whole: it never changes its ending.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ക്രമേണ** (*kramēṇa*) is borrowed whole from Sanskrit *krameṇa*, "in regular course, by degrees" (CDIAL 3577), a form of *krama*, "a step, a series": things change step by step.
