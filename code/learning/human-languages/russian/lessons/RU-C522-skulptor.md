## You'll want to know: скульптор
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C522-SCULPTURE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**скульптор** (*skúlptor*) — "a sculptor".

> Скульптор работал над памятником целый год.
>
> *Skúlptor rabótal nad pámyatnikom tsélyy god.*
>
> The sculptor worked on the monument for a whole year.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*скульптор* is a learned borrowing from Latin *sculptor*, built on *sculpere* 'to carve' (Lewis & Short); EtymDB has no entry.
