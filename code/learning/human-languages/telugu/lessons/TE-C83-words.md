# (reading) — six words, read down as well as along

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[TE-LEX-C46-ASK-05] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Every word below you have written. Not one is new.

[PAUSE 1s] What is new is that nobody says them first.

## Reading
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-SKILL-READ-WORDS]; assesses=[TE-LEX-C52-WELCOME-01, TE-LEX-C52-WELCOME-02] -->

> పుస్తకం
> తలుపు
> కుర్చీ
> నది
> గాలి
> దీపం

[PAUSE 3s] Read down the list once, without stopping.

[PAUSE 3s] Again — and look at **పుస్తకం** and **కుర్చీ**. Something in each of
them sits below the line rather than on it.

## You'll want to know
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[TE-LEX-C55-ROAD-01, TE-LEX-C59-WEATHER-01, TE-LEX-C46-ASK-03] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Telugu stacks a consonant underneath its neighbour instead of
putting it alongside. So a word can be two syllables wide and three levels
deep.

[PAUSE 2s] A reader has to look down a word as well as along it, and that is
the habit this list is for. **నది** and **గాలి** have nothing below;
**పుస్తకం** has two things.

[PAUSE 2s] Six words, and the tall ones are not longer — they are deeper.
