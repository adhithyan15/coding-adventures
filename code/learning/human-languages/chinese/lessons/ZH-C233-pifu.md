## You'll want to know: pífū
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C233-LOOKS233-05]; assesses=[] -->

*pífū* — "skin".

> *tā de pífū hěn bái* — She has very fair skin.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*pífū* is built from *pí* 'hide' and *fū* 'skin'. The first is inherited from Middle Chinese *bje* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed with this very word, 'skin'; the dictionary glosses the second, *pju* there, the same way.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing things.
