## You'll want to know: ràngzuò
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C376-PUBLIC376-04]; assesses=[] -->

*ràngzuò* — "to give up one's seat (to someone)".

> *zài dìtiě lǐ niánqīngrén yīnggāi ràngzuò* — On the underground, young people should give up their seats.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ràngzuò* is built from *ràng* 'to yield' and *zuò* 'a seat', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *nyangH* and *dzwaH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to give way' and the second 'a bed or a seat'.
