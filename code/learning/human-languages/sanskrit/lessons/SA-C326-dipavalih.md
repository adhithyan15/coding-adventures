## You'll want to know: दीपावलिः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C326-DIPAVALI326-01]; assesses=[] -->

**दीपावलिः** — *dīpāvaliḥ* — "the festival of lamps; a row of lamps".

> दीपावल्यां सर्वत्र दीपाः ज्वलन्ति।
>
> *dīpāvalyāṃ sarvatra dīpāḥ jvalanti.*
>
> At Diwali lamps burn everywhere.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**दीपावलिः** (*dīpāvaliḥ*) is built from *dīpa*, "a lamp", and *āvali*, "a row"; MW gives *dīpāvali* as a row of lights, a night illumination, and the festival of Diwali is its modern use (MW).
