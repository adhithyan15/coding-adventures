## You'll want to know: ぎむ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C332-KENRI332-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ぎむ** — *gimu* — "a duty; an obligation".

> **しみんには ぎむも あると おもいます。** — *shimin ni wa gimu mo aru to omoimasu* — "I think citizens have duties as well."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ぎむ** (*gimu*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *gi*, "righteousness" and *mu*, "duties" (Middle Chinese *mjuH*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
