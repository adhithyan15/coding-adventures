# منع کرنا (manā karnā) — to forbid; to tell (someone) not to

## You'll want to know: منع کرنا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C366-IJAZAT366-05]; assesses=[] -->

**منع کرنا** — *manā karnā* — "to forbid; to tell (someone) not to".

> اگر استاد نے منع کیا تو ہم نہیں کھیلیں گے۔
>
> *agar ustād ne manā kiyā to ham nahīṅ kheleṅ ge.*
>
> If the teacher says no, we will not play.

Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے مجھے منع کیا, *us ne mujhe manā kiyā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**منع کرنا** (*manā karnā*) is built from *manā*, usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *manʿ*, "holding back, prevention", and **کرنا**, "to do".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
