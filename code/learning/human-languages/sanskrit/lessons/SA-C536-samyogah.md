## You'll want to know: संयोगः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C536-ADRSTA536-03]; assesses=[] -->

**संयोगः** — *saṃyogaḥ* — "a coincidence; a conjunction".

> यदि संयोगः भवति, तर्हि त्वां श्वः द्रक्ष्यामि।
>
> *yadi saṃyogaḥ bhavati, tarhi tvāṃ śvaḥ drakṣyāmi.*
>
> If chance brings us together, I'll see you tomorrow.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**संयोगः** (*saṃyogaḥ*) is built on *sam*, "together", and *yoga*, "joining", from the root √yuj; MW gives *saṃyoga* as conjunction, connection, union; "a coincidence" is its modern use (MW).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
