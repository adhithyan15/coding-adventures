## You'll want to know: pendurar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C531-BRICOLAGE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**pendurar** — "to hang (up)".

> Pendurei o quadro na parede da sala.
>
> *I hung the picture on the living-room wall.*

*Pendurar o casaco* — to hang up your coat.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*pendurar* is already found in Old Portuguese (EtymDB) and is usually traced to Latin *pendēre* "to hang" (Lewis & Short).
