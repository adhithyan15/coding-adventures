## You'll want to know: استراحة
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C440-TIDHKAR440-03]; assesses=[] -->

**استراحة** — *istirāḥa* — "a rest stop; a break".

> وقفت الحافلة عند استراحة على الطريق.
>
> *waqafat al-ḥāfila ʿinda istirāḥa ʿalā aṭ-ṭarīq.*
>
> The bus stopped at a rest stop on the road.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**استراحة** (*istirāḥa*) is built on the native Arabic root *r-w-ḥ*, "to be at ease; breath, wind" (Lane). It is the verbal noun of Form X *istarāḥa*, "to rest".
