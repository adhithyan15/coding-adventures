## You'll want to know: ちゅうしゃじょう
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C202-DRIVE202-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ちゅうしゃじょう** — *chūshajō* — "a car park".

> **ちゅうしゃじょうは えきの となりです。** — *chūshajō wa eki no tonari desu* — "The car park is next to the station."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ちゅうしゃじょう** (*chūshajō*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): three Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *chū*, "to stop over" (Middle Chinese *trjuH*), *sha*, "cart" (*tsyhae*) and *jō*, "place" (*drjang*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
