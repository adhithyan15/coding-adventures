# ಫಿರಂಗಿ (phiraṅgi) — a cannon

## You'll want to know: ಫಿರಂಗಿ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C342-MUTTIGE342-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ಫಿರಂಗಿ** — *phiraṅgi* — "a cannon".

> ಕೋಟೆಯ ಗೋಡೆಯ ಮೇಲೆ ಹಳೆಯ ಫಿರಂಗಿ ಇದೆ.
>
> *kōṭeya gōḍeya mēle haḷeya phiraṅgi ide.*
>
> There is an old cannon on the fort wall.

Plural: ಫಿರಂಗಿಗಳು, *phiraṅgigaḷu*.

Many old forts still show their ಫಿರಂಗಿಗಳು.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಫಿರಂಗಿ** (*phiraṅgi*) is a loan from Persian *firangī*, "a Frank, a European": the guns came with European traders and soldiers.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
