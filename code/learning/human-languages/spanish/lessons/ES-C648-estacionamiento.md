## You'll want to know: el estacionamiento
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C648-APARCAR-02]; assesses=[] -->

**el estacionamiento** — "the car park, the parking lot; parking".

> El estacionamiento del hotel es gratis.
>
> *The hotel car park is free.*

Spain says *el aparcamiento* or *el parking*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*estacionamiento* is built on *estacionar* "to park", which comes from *estación*, Latin *statiō* "a standing still" (Lewis & Short).
