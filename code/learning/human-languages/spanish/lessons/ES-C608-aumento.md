## You'll want to know: el aumento
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C608-AUMENTA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**el aumento** — "the increase, the rise".

> Pedimos un aumento de sueldo.
>
> *We are asking for a pay rise.*

*Un aumento de sueldo* — a pay rise.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*aumento* is a learned borrowing from Latin *augmentum* 'an increase', built on *augere* 'to increase' (EtymDB).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.
