Tamil, chapter 428: A Spray of Rain.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
சாரல் (cāral) — a spray of rain; drizzle.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for to scorch, then the Tamil for to be sultry.

You'll want to know: சாரல்.
சாரல் — cāral — "a spray of rain; drizzle".
மலையில் காலை முழுவதும் சாரல் அடித்தது. malaiyil kālai muḻuvatum cāral aṭittatu. On the hill a fine rain blew all morning.
சாரல் (cāral) அடி is said of fine rain blown by the wind.

The word, taken apart.
சாரல் (cāral) is native Dravidian (DEDR 2482), "drizzling rain".

Lesson 2 of 5.

Warm-up.

Before the new one: say the Tamil for to be sultry, then the Tamil for a spray of rain.

You'll want to know: தூறு.
தூறு — tūṟu — "to drizzle".
வெளியே மழை தூறிக்கொண்டிருந்தது. veḷiyē maḻai tūṟikkoṇṭiruntatu. It was drizzling outside.
Past (it …): தூறியது, tūṟiyatu.
The noun is தூறல், a drizzle.

The word, taken apart.
தூறு (tūṟu) is native Dravidian (DEDR 3398), "to drizzle"; the same article gives தூறல், "drizzling".

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for to drizzle, then the Tamil for to pour down.

You'll want to know: தேங்கு.
தேங்கு — tēṅku — "to stagnate; to collect (of water)".
தெருவில் மழைநீர் தேங்கியது. teruvil maḻainīr tēṅkiyatu. Rainwater collected in the street.
Past (it …): தேங்கியது, tēṅkiyatu.
Of work too: வேலை தேங்கியது, the work piled up.

The word, taken apart.
தேங்கு (tēṅku) is native Dravidian (DEDR 3453), "to fill, to rise to the brim, to collect".

Lesson 5 of 5.
வடி (vaṭi) — to drain away; to ebb.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for to pour down, then the Tamil for to stagnate.

You'll want to know: வடி.
வடி — vaṭi — "to drain away; to ebb".
மழை நின்றதும் தண்ணீர் வடிந்தது. maḻai niṉṟatum taṇṇīr vaṭintatu. As soon as the rain stopped, the water drained away.
Past (it …): வடிந்தது, vaṭintatu.
With an object, to strain: சாதம் வடி (vaṭi), to drain rice.

The word, taken apart.
வடி (vaṭi) is native Dravidian (DEDR 5221), "to drip, to trickle, to ebb".
