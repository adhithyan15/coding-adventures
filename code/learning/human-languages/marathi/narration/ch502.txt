Lesson 3 of 5.
दुरावणे (durāvṇe) — to drift apart.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a separation, then the Marathi for bickering.

You'll want to know: दुरावणे.
दुरावणे — durāvṇe — "to drift apart".
शहर बदलल्यावर जुने मित्र दुरावले. śahar badallyāvar june mitra durāvle. After moving towns, old friends drifted apart.
Past (he …): तो दुरावला, to durāvlā.
From दूर, far: to grow distant. दुरावा, estrangement.

The word, taken apart.
दुरावणे (durāvṇe) is a Marathi verb built on दूर, "far", from Sanskrit dūra, "distant" (CDIAL 6495), with -āvṇe.

Lesson 5 of 5.
अबोला धरणे (abolā dharṇe) — to stop speaking to.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to drift apart, then the Marathi for to seethe.

You'll want to know: अबोला धरणे.
अबोला धरणे — abolā dharṇe — "to stop speaking to".
भांडणानंतर तिने भावाशी अबोला धरला. bhāṇḍṇānantar tine bhāvāśī abolā dharlā. After the quarrel she stopped speaking to her brother.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने अबोला धरला, tyāne abolā dharlā.
अबोला, silence kept out of hurt; the person takes -शी. अबोला सोडणे, to break the silence.

The word, taken apart.
अबोला धरणे (abolā dharṇe) is a Marathi phrase built on अबोला, "not speaking", the negative a- with bol-, the root of बोलणे, "to speak" (CDIAL 9321), and धरणे, "to hold".
