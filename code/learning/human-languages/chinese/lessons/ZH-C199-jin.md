## You'll want to know: jǐn
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C199-SIZE199-04]; assesses=[] -->

*jǐn* — "tight".

> *zhè shuāng xié tài jǐn le* — These shoes are too tight.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*jǐn* is inherited from Middle Chinese *kjinX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'tightly bound'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for asking for what I want.
