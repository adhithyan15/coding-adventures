Persian, chapter 130: Healthy, Coarse, Tiny, Little, Much.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
سالم (sâlem) — healthy.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for wise, then the Persian for ignorant.

You'll want to know: سالم.
سالم — sâlem — "healthy".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: sâlem]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: sâlem, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say sâlem]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Persian for wise, then the Persian for ignorant, then say sâlem again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does سالم (sâlem) mean? ("Healthy".) Say it once more.
