## You'll want to know: 路人
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C456-LOST456-03]; assesses=[] -->

**路人** — *lùrén* — "passer-by; stranger in the street".

> **我的地图不见了我问了一个路人** *wǒ de dìtú bú jiàn le wǒ wèn le yí ge lùrén* — My map had gone, so I asked a passer-by.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**路人** (*lùrén*) is built from 路 'a road' and 人 'a person', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *luH* and *nyin* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription).
