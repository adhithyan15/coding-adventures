## You'll want to know: 买卖
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C523-TRADE523-02]; assesses=[] -->

**买卖** — *mǎimai* — "buying and selling for a living; a business deal".

> **他做了二十年买卖** *tā zuò le èrshí nián mǎimai* — He has made his living buying and selling for twenty years.

The new character, in pieces: 卖 — eight strokes in two pieces: 十 on top, two strokes; and 买 under it, six strokes. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**买卖** (*mǎimai*) is built from 买 'to buy' and 卖 'to sell', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *meaX* and *meaH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first, after an older dictionary, 'to trade' and the second 'to put goods out'. The two differ only in their tones, and in the word the second is said light.
