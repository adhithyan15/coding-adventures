## You'll want to know: छेदना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C510-BADHAI510-05]; assesses=[] -->

**छेदना** — *chednā* — "to bore; to pierce".

> बढ़ई ने कील से लकड़ी छेदी।
>
> *Baṛhaī ne kīl se lakṛī chedī.*
>
> The carpenter bored through the wood with a nail.

Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने काग़ज़ छेदा, *usne kāġaz chedā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**छेदना** (*chednā*) is a tadbhava, inherited from Sanskrit *chidra*, "a hole" (CDIAL 5043). CDIAL gives the Hindi verb as "to bore".
