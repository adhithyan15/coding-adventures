## You'll want to know: rühren
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C324-RUEHREN-01]; assesses=[] -->

**rühren** — "to stir; to move (the heart)".

> Er rührte die Suppe langsam.
>
> *He stirred the soup slowly.*

Also to touch the heart: *Das Lied rührte sie*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*rühren* is inherited from Old High German *ruoren*, from Proto-Germanic *hrōzijaną* "to stir" (EtymDB).
