## You'll want to know: onerō, onerāre
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C433-IUMENTUM-05]; assesses=[] -->

**onerō, onerāre** — "to load, to burden".

> Servī mūlōs sarcinīs onerant.
>
> *The slaves load the mules with baggage.*

*onerō, onerāre, onerāvī, onerātum*, with the thing in the ablative. English *onerous* and *exonerate* come from the same root.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*onerō* is built on *onus, oneris* 'a load, a burden', from PIE *h₃énh₂os* (EtymDB; Lewis & Short). English *onerous* and *exonerate* come from the same family.
