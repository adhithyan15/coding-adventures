Russian, chapter 365: The state and power.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
государство (gosudárstvo) — the state, a country.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for social, then the Russian for an inhabitant.

You'll want to know: государство.
государство (gosudárstvo) — "the state, a country".
Государство помогает пенсионерам. Gosudárstvo pomogáyet pensionéram. The state helps pensioners.
Neuter. Государственный — state, public: государственный язык.

The word, taken apart.
государство (gosudárstvo) is built on государь "a sovereign", which is usually traced to older господарь "a lord, a master", from господь "lord".

Lesson 2 of 5.
правительство (pravítelstvo) — the government.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for an inhabitant, then the Russian for the state.

You'll want to know: правительство.
правительство (pravítelstvo) — "the government".
Правительство обещало повысить пенсии. Pravítelstvo obeshchálo povýsit pénsii. The government promised to raise pensions.
Neuter. The cabinet that runs a country; власти is broader.

The word, taken apart.
правительство (pravítelstvo) is built on правитель "a ruler", from править "to rule, to steer", from правый "right", Proto-Slavic pravŭ "right, correct" (EtymDB).

Lesson 3 of 5.
власть (vlast) — power, authority.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for the state, then the Russian for the government.

You'll want to know: власть.
власть (vlast) — "power, authority".
В этой стране власть часто меняется. V étoy strané vlast chásto menyáyetsya. In this country power often changes hands.
Feminine. The plural власти means the authorities.

The word, taken apart.
власть (vlast) "power, authority" has the Church Slavonic -ла-: Church Slavonic vlastĭ and native волость both come from Proto-Slavic volstĭ "rule, power" (EtymDB). Волость was once a district under someone’s rule.

Lesson 4 of 5.
политика (polítika) — politics; a policy.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for the government, then the Russian for power.

You'll want to know: политика.
политика (polítika) — "politics; a policy".
Я не интересуюсь политикой. Ya ne interesúyus polítikoy. I am not interested in politics.
Feminine. Both politics and a policy: политика (polítika) компании.

The word, taken apart.
политика (polítika) is usually traced, through western languages, to Greek politikḗ "the art of running a state", built on pólis "a city, a state".

Lesson 5 of 5.
политик (polítik) — a politician.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for power, then the Russian for politics.

You'll want to know: политик.
политик (polítik) — "a politician".
Этот политик часто выступает по телевизору. Étot polítik chásto vystupáyet po televízoru. This politician often speaks on television.
Masculine, also of a woman. Not to be confused with политика (polítika), politics.

The word, taken apart.
политик (polítik) is usually traced, through German, to Greek politikós "of the citizens, of the state", built on pólis "a city, a state".
