Gujarati, chapter 296: The Ambulance Arrives.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
એમ્બ્યુલન્સ (embyulans) — an ambulance.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a fine, then the Gujarati for to crush.

You'll want to know: એમ્બ્યુલન્સ.
એમ્બ્યુલન્સ — embyulans — "an ambulance".
દસ મિનિટમાં એમ્બ્યુલન્સ આવી. das miniṭmā̃ embyulans āvī. The ambulance came within ten minutes.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
એમ્બ્યુલન્સ (embyulans) is borrowed from English ambulance, and it is feminine: એમ્બ્યુલન્સ આવી, the ambulance came.

Lesson 2 of 5.
ઉપાડવું (upāḍvũ) — to pick up; to carry off.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to crush, then the Gujarati for an ambulance.

You'll want to know: ઉપાડવું.
ઉપાડવું — upāḍvũ — "to pick up; to carry off".
અરુણે અને એક સિપાઈએ ઘાયલ છોકરાને ઉપાડ્યો. Aruṇe ane ek sipāīe ghāyal chokrāne upāḍyo. Arun and a constable picked up the injured boy.
Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે થેલી ઉપાડી, teṇe thelī upāḍī.

The word, taken apart.
ઉપાડવું (upāḍvũ) goes back to Sanskrit útpātayati, "causes to fly up or rise", and CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb, "to lift up" (CDIAL 1810). It is lifting something to take it away: ઘાયલ છોકરાને ઉપાડ્યો.

Lesson 5 of 5.
હાલત (hālat) — a condition (of health or repair).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a bandage, then the Gujarati for a stitch.

You'll want to know: હાલત.
હાલત — hālat — "a condition (of health or repair)".
હવે દર્દીની હાલત સારી છે. have dardīnī hālat sārī che. The patient is in a good condition now.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
હાલત (hālat) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic ḥāla, "a state, a condition". It is feminine: તેની હાલત ગંભીર છે, his condition is serious.
