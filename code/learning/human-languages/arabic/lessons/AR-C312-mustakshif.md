## You'll want to know: مستكشف
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C312-KASHF312-04]; assesses=[] -->

**مستكشف** — *mustakshif* — "an explorer".

> عبر المستكشف الصحراء على جمل.
>
> *ʿabara al-mustakshif aṣ-ṣaḥrāʾ ʿalā jamal.*
>
> The explorer crossed the desert on a camel.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**مستكشف** (*mustakshif*) is built on the native Arabic root *k-sh-f* (Lane), "to uncover". *Mustakshif* is the Form X participle, "one who seeks to uncover".
