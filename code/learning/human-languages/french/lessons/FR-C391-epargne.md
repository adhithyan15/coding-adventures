## You'll want to know: l'épargne
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C391-EPARGNE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**l'épargne** — "the savings, saving".

> Ils ont mis leur épargne sur un compte à la banque.
>
> *They put their savings in a bank account.*

Feminine. Formal; in speech, *les économies*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*épargne* is formed from *épargner*, inherited through Old French *espargnier* from a Frankish *sparanjan* "to spare" (EtymDB).
