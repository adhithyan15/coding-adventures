Portuguese, chapter 434: A reclamação.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
reclamar — to complain; to claim.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for mould, then the Portuguese for dirt.

You'll want to know: reclamar.
reclamar — "to complain; to claim".
Reclamámos do barulho, mas ninguém fez nada. We complained about the noise, but nobody did anything.
Reclamar de — to complain about.

The word, taken apart.
reclamar is a learned borrowing from Latin reclāmāre 'to cry out against' (EtymDB), built on clāmāre 'to shout'.

Lesson 5 of 5.
a indemnização — the compensation (money), the damages.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for to grumble, then the Portuguese for to regret.

You'll want to know: a indemnização.
a indemnização — "the compensation (money), the damages".
A companhia pagou uma indemnização pelo atraso. The company paid compensation for the delay.
In Brazil: a indenização.

The word, taken apart.
indemnização is built on indemnizar 'to compensate', a learned formation on Latin indemnis 'unharmed', from in- 'not' and damnum 'loss' (Lewis & Short).
