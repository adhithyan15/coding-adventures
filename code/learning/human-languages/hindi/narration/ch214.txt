Hindi, chapter 214: Smooth, Busy, Lazy, Honest, True.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
चिकना (cikanā) — smooth, greasy.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for sweet, then the Hindi for soft.

You'll want to know: चिकना.
चिकना — cikanā — "smooth, greasy".

The word, taken apart.
चिकना (cikanā) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from cikkaṇa, "slippery, unctuous" (CDIAL 4782).

The word, taken apart.
व्यस्त (vyast) is Sanskrit vyasta, "scattered, distracted", taken over whole as a learned loan, a tatsama. Hindi uses it for "busy".
