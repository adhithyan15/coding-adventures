## You'll want to know: いけばな
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C466-ARTS466-02]; assesses=[] -->

**いけばな** — *ikebana* — "flower arranging".

> **ははは いけばなが じょうずです。** — *haha wa ikebana ga jōzu desu* — "My mother is good at flower arranging."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**いけばな** (*ikebana*) is a native Japanese compound (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese: *ike*, "keeping alive (ikeru)" and *bana*, "flower (hana)" (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
