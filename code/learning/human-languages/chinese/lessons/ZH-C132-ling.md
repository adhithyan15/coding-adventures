## You'll want to know: líng
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C132-BIG132-03]; assesses=[] -->

*líng* — "zero".

> *tā yìbǎi líng yī suì le* — He is a hundred and one.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*líng* is inherited from Middle Chinese *leng* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'to fall' and, after an older book, 'a gentle rain'. The sense zero is not given there; how it came is not settled.
