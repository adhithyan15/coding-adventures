## You'll want to know: o saxofone
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C517-SOPROS-03]; assesses=[] -->

**o saxofone** — "saxophone".

> Ouvimos um solo de saxofone no bar.
>
> *We heard a saxophone solo in the bar.*

Often shortened to *o sax*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*saxofone* comes from French *saxophone* (EtymDB), built on the surname of its Belgian inventor and Greek *phōnḗ* "sound".
