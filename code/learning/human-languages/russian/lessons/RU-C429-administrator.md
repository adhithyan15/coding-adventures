## You'll want to know: администратор
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C429-ZASELENIYE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**администратор** (*administrátor*) — "a receptionist (at a hotel); an administrator".

> Администратор дал нам ключ от номера.
>
> *Administrátor dal nam klyuch ot nómera.*
>
> The receptionist gave us the key to the room.

Masculine, for a woman too. The desk itself is often called *ресепшен*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*администратор* is usually traced to Latin *administrātor* 'a manager' (EtymDB lists the Latin word), from *administrāre* 'to attend to, to manage'. EtymDB does not list the Russian word.
