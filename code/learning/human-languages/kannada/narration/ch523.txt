Kannada, chapter 523: Partners and Households.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಸಂಸಾರ (saṃsāra) — family life; a household.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a sculptor, then the Kannada for to carve.

You'll want to know: ಸಂಸಾರ.
ಸಂಸಾರ — saṃsāra — "family life; a household".
ಅಕ್ಕ ತನ್ನ ಸಂಸಾರವನ್ನು ಚೆನ್ನಾಗಿ ನಡೆಸುತ್ತಾಳೆ. akka tanna saṃsāravannu cennāgi naḍesuttāḷe. My elder sister runs her household well.
ಸಂಸಾರಿ, a family man or woman.

The word, taken apart.
ಸಂಸಾರ (saṃsāra) is borrowed from Sanskrit saṃsāra, from saṃ-sṛ, "to go round", the round of worldly life (Monier-Williams); in Kannada it is the life of a family.

Lesson 4 of 5.
ವಿರಹ (viraha) — the pain of parting; separation from a loved one.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a homemaker, then the Kannada for a partner.

You'll want to know: ವಿರಹ.
ವಿರಹ — viraha — "the pain of parting; separation from a loved one".
ಗಂಡ ವಿದೇಶಕ್ಕೆ ಹೋದಾಗ ಅವಳಿಗೆ ವಿರಹದ ನೋವು ಆಯಿತು. gaṇḍa vidēśakke hōdāga avaḷige virahada nōvu āyitu. When her husband went abroad, she felt the pain of separation.
A favourite theme of songs and poems.

The word, taken apart.
ವಿರಹ (viraha) is borrowed from Sanskrit viraha, "desertion, separation, especially of lovers" (CDIAL 11851).
