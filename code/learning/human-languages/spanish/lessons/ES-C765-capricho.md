## You'll want to know: el capricho
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C765-ILUSION-03]; assesses=[] -->

**el capricho** — "the whim, the treat".

> Me di un capricho y compré chocolate.
>
> *I treated myself and bought chocolate.*

*Darse un capricho* — to treat yourself. *Caprichoso* — fussy, impulsive.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*capricho* was borrowed from Italian *capriccio* "whim, fancy" (EtymDB). The Italian word is often explained as *capo* "head" plus *riccio* "hedgehog", hair on end, but that is not certain.
