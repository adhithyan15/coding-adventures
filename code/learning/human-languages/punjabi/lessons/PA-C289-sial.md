## You'll want to know: ਸਿਆਲ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C289-COLD289-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ਸਿਆਲ** — *siāl* — "winter; the cold season".

> ਸਿਆਲ ਵਿੱਚ ਮੈਂ ਕੰਬਲ ਵਰਤਾਂਗਾ।
>
> *siāl vich maiṁ kambal vartāṅgā.*
>
> In winter I will use a blanket.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਸਿਆਲ** (*siāl*) is inherited from Sanskrit *śītakāla*, "cold season"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *siāl*, "winter", under it (CDIAL 12486).
