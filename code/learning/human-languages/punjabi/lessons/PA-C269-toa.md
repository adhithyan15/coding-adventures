## You'll want to know: ਟੋਆ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C269-WAY269-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ਟੋਆ** — *ṭoā* — "a pothole; a hole in the ground".

> ਸੜਕ ਵਿੱਚ ਇੱਕ ਡੂੰਘਾ ਟੋਆ ਹੈ।
>
> *saṛak vich ikk ḍū̃ghā ṭoā hai.*
>
> There is a deep pothole in the road.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਟੋਆ** (*ṭoā*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*trōpa*, "piercing, hole"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *ṭoā*, "hole, pit", under it (CDIAL 6082).
