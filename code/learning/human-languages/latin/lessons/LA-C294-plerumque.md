## You'll want to know: plērumque
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C294-IDENTIDEM-04]; assesses=[] -->

**plērumque** — "mostly, usually".

> Plērumque post merīdiem dormiēbat.
>
> *He usually slept in the afternoon.*

The neuter of *plērusque*, 'the greater part': for the most part.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**plērumque** is the neuter of *plērusque*, "the greater part, most" (Lewis & Short), used as an adverb: for the most part.
