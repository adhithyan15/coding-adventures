## You'll want to know: verfahren
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C435-UMWEG-05]; assesses=[] -->

**verfahren** — "to get lost driving (mostly reflexive: sich verfahren); to proceed".

> Ohne Navi hat sich mein Vater verfahren.
>
> *Without the satnav my father got lost.*

Reflexive for getting lost: *ich habe mich verfahren*. *Das Verfahren* — the procedure.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*verfahren* is inherited from Middle High German *vervarn* and Old High German *firfaran* (EtymDB), built from *ver-* and *fahren* 'to travel'.
