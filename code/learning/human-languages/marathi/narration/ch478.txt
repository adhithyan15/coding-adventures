Marathi, chapter 478: Bones and Joints.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
कवटी (kavṭī) — the skull.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for the calf, then the Marathi for the ankle.

You'll want to know: कवटी.
कवटी — kavṭī — "the skull".
संग्रहालयात एका प्राचीन माणसाची कवटी होती. saṅgrahālayāt ekā prācīn māṇsācī kavṭī hotī. There was the skull of an ancient man in the museum.
Feminine.
Feminine; also the shell of a coconut.

The word, taken apart.
कवटी (kavṭī) is a Marathi noun whose origin is not settled.

Lesson 4 of 5.
आतडे (ātḍe) — the gut; an intestine.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for the spine, then the Marathi for a rib.

You'll want to know: आतडे.
आतडे — ātḍe — "the gut; an intestine".
पोटात आतडी असतात. poṭāt ātḍī astāt. The intestines are in the belly.
Neuter. Plural: आतडी, ātḍī.
Neuter; the plural आतडी is the intestines.

The word, taken apart.
आतडे (ātḍe) is a tadbhava, inherited from Sanskrit āntra, "entrail"; CDIAL lists Marathi ā̃tḍẽ, "entrail" (CDIAL 1182).

Lesson 5 of 5.
सांधा (sāndhā) — a joint.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a rib, then the Marathi for the gut.

You'll want to know: सांधा.
सांधा — sāndhā — "a joint".
गुडघा हा मोठा सांधा आहे. guḍghā hā moṭhā sāndhā āhe. The knee is a big joint.
Masculine. Plural: सांधे, sāndhe.
Masculine; also a seam or a join.

The word, taken apart.
सांधा (sāndhā) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Marathi sā̃dhā, "joint, knuckle, knot", under Sanskrit saṁdhi, "joining" (CDIAL 12913).
