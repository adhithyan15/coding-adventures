## HL-C271 — Gujarati's core-verb chapters are now R4-measurable, and unserviced

Found while adding Gujarati chapter 28. The tranche moved four numbers the right
way — pre-A1 headwords 44 -> 52, headwords with no romanization 25 -> 0, script
closure findings 2 -> 0, ear-drivable share 52% -> 56% — and one number the wrong
way: whole-track reinforcement misses 299 -> 339.

The rise is not a skipped return. Every atom chapter 28 introduces is serviced
inside chapter 28, and the chapter additionally closes R3 for the five map words
and gives `GU-PERFORMANCE-MAP-TEN-FOUR-SKILL-01` its first revisit in the track's
history. What changed is **eligibility**. R4 opens 80 lessons after an atom
appears, so a 165-lesson track could only ever measure atoms introduced at or
before position 84. At 179 lessons it reaches position 98 — and positions 85-98
are chapters 13-16, the six core verbs plus the mind-and-page and
taking/asking/helping/liking verbs plus water.

Thirty-six atoms across those fourteen lessons are now R4-eligible and every one
of them misses:

- `GU-LEX-JAVU`, `GU-LEX-AAVVU`, `GU-LEX-KHAAVU`, `GU-LEX-JOVU`,
  `GU-LEX-JAANVU` (chapter 13, positions 85-89)
- `GU-LEX-VICHARVU`, `GU-LEX-SAMAJVU`, `GU-LEX-VANCHVU`, `GU-LEX-LAKHVU`
  (chapter 14, positions 90-93)
- `GU-LEX-LEVU`, `GU-LEX-PUCHHVU`, `GU-LEX-MADAD-KARVI`, `GU-LEX-GAMVU`
  (chapter 15, positions 94-97)
- `GU-LEX-PAANI` and `GU-PHRASE-MEHERBANI-KARINE` (chapter 16, position 98)
- plus their etymon, sound, grammar, and form atoms

The fix is a fifth-return slab in the shape chapters 19-23 already established:
zero-new-atom lessons placed 80 or more positions after each introduction, each
scoring recognition and production separately. Roughly fourteen lessons. It was
deliberately not folded into a vocabulary chapter, because stuffing thirty-six
unrelated verb atoms into a chapter about times of day would have moved the
number without teaching anything.

Two smaller items found in the same pass and also not done:

- **The pre-A1 verb-vocabulary blocker is structural, not a content gap.**
  Gujarati teaches nineteen verb headwords and exactly **one** of them counts,
  because the rest hang off `SPINE-SAY-WHAT-I-DO`, `SPINE-NAME-EVERYDAY-ACTIONS`
  and `SPINE-SAY-WHAT-I-LIKE`, which the shared spine stages at A1/A2. The gate
  wants five verb headwords at or below pre-A1. No amount of Gujarati authoring
  moves this; it needs a decision about where those spine nodes sit, and that
  decision is cross-track.
- **Six of chapter 28's eight time words are still oral only.** *savār*, *bapor*,
  *sānj*, *divas*, *mahino*, and *atyāre* are glossed and drivable but never
  written. Every sign they need is already taught, so the writing return is a
  later chapter's work and needs no new glyph lesson — which is exactly the
  gloss-first-then-glyph interleaving the ramp asks for, left deliberately open.
