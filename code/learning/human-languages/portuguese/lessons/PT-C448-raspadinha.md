## You'll want to know: a raspadinha
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C448-TABACARIA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**a raspadinha** — "the scratch card".

> A minha tia compra uma raspadinha todas as semanas.
>
> *My aunt buys a scratch card every week.*

Built on *raspar*, to scratch off.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*raspadinha* is a small form built on *raspar* 'to scrape', which is inherited from Vulgar Latin *raspāre* (EtymDB).
