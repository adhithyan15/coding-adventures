# ಗುಣಮಟ್ಟ (guṇamaṭṭa) — quality; standard

## You'll want to know: ಗುಣಮಟ್ಟ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C468-GATRA468-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ಗುಣಮಟ್ಟ** — *guṇamaṭṭa* — "quality; standard".

> ಈ ಅಂಗಡಿಯ ಬಟ್ಟೆಗಳ ಗುಣಮಟ್ಟ ಚೆನ್ನಾಗಿದೆ.
>
> *ī aṅgaḍiya baṭṭegaḷa guṇamaṭṭa cennāgide.*
>
> The quality of the clothes in this shop is good.

ಗುಣ, a quality, plus ಮಟ್ಟ, a level.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಗುಣಮಟ್ಟ** (*guṇamaṭṭa*) joins Sanskrit *guṇa*, "a quality" (CDIAL 4190), and native *maṭṭa*, "measure, level, limit" (DEDR 4660).
