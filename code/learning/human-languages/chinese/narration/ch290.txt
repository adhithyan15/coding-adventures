Mandarin Chinese, chapter 290: In the News.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
xīnwén — the news; news on TV and radio.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for if, then the Mandarin for otherwise.

You'll want to know: xīnwén.
xīnwén — "the news; news on TV and radio".
wǒ měitiān tīng xīnwén — I listen to the news every day.
Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
xīnwén is built from xīn 'new' and wén 'to hear', both inherited from Middle Chinese, sin and mjun (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'new, as against old' and the second 'to know by its sound': what is newly heard.

Lesson 2 of 5.
bàodào — to report on; a news report.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for otherwise, then the Mandarin for the news on TV.

You'll want to know: bàodào.
bàodào — "to report on; a news report".
diànshì bàodào le zhège xiāoxi — The news was reported on TV.
Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
bàodào is built from bào 'to report' and dào, both inherited from Middle Chinese, pawH and dawX (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to report' and the second 'a road' and 'a principle'; its sense 'to say' is not given there.

Lesson 4 of 5.
zhǔchírén — presenter; host of a show.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for to report on the news, then the Mandarin for a radio station.

You'll want to know: zhǔchírén.
zhǔchírén — "presenter; host of a show".
zhège jiémù de zhǔchírén hěn yǒuyìsi — The host of this show is a lot of fun.
Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
zhǔchírén is built from zhǔ 'to be in charge', chí 'to hold' and 人 'a person', all inherited from Middle Chinese, tsyuX, dri and nyin (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to be in charge of' and the second 'to hold': the person who holds the show together.

Lesson 5 of 5.
热点 (rèdiǎn) — hot topic; what everyone is talking about.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for a radio station, then the Mandarin for the presenter of a show.

You'll want to know: 热点.
热点 — rèdiǎn — "hot topic; what everyone is talking about".
这是今天的热点 zhè shì jīntiān de rèdiǎn — This is today's hot topic.

The word, taken apart.
热点 (rèdiǎn) is built from 热 'hot' and 点 'a dot', both inherited from Middle Chinese, nyet and temX (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'burning, as by fire' and the second 'a dot or stroke': a hot spot, the story everyone is talking about.
