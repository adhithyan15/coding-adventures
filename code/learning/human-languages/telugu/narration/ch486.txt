Telugu, chapter 486: Weights and Measures.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
కొలత (kolata) — a measurement.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for an exchange rate, then the Telugu for to dodge.

You'll want to know: కొలత.
కొలత — kolata — "a measurement".
దర్జీ నా చొక్కా కొలతలు తీసుకున్నాడు. darjī nā cokkā kolatalu tīsukunnāḍu. The tailor took my measurements for a shirt.
Plural: కొలతలు, kolatalu.
The noun of కొలుచు, to measure. కొలతలు తీసుకో, to take measurements.

The word, taken apart.
కొలత (kolata) is native Dravidian: DEDR 1827 lists Telugu kolãta, "measurement, measuring", beside kolucu, "to measure".

Lesson 2 of 5.
పరిమాణం (parimāṇaṁ) — size; quantity.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to dodge, then the Telugu for a measurement.

You'll want to know: పరిమాణం.
పరిమాణం — parimāṇaṁ — "size; quantity".
ఆ పెట్టె పరిమాణం చాలా పెద్దది. ā peṭṭe parimāṇaṁ cālā peddadi. That box is very big in size.
Size of a thing, or an amount: నీటి పరిమాణం (parimāṇaṁ), the quantity of water.

The word, taken apart.
పరిమాణం (parimāṇaṁ) is borrowed from Sanskrit parimāṇa, "measure, size, quantity" (Monier-Williams), from pari-mā, "to measure out".

Lesson 3 of 5.
నమూనా (namūnā) — a sample; a pattern.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a measurement, then the Telugu for size.

You'll want to know: నమూనా.
నమూనా — namūnā — "a sample; a pattern".
దుకాణదారు బట్ట నమూనా చూపించాడు. dukāṇadāru baṭṭa namūnā cūpiñcāḍu. The shopkeeper showed a sample of the cloth.
A sample of goods, and a pattern or model to copy.

The word, taken apart.
నమూనా (namūnā) is a loan from Persian namūna, "a sample, a specimen, a model", which reached Telugu through Hindustani.

Lesson 4 of 5.
విలువ (viluva) — value; worth.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for size, then the Telugu for a sample.

You'll want to know: విలువ.
విలువ — viluva — "value; worth".
ఆ పాత నాణేలకు ఇప్పుడు చాలా విలువ ఉంది. ā pāta nāṇēlaku ippuḍu cālā viluva undi. Those old coins are worth a lot now.
Value in money and in esteem: మాటకు విలువ (viluva), the worth of a promise.

The word, taken apart.
విలువ (viluva) is native Dravidian: DEDR 5421 lists Telugu viluva, "price, value, cost", beside vilucu, "to sell, to buy".

Lesson 5 of 5.
తూచు (tūcu) — to weigh.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a sample, then the Telugu for value.

You'll want to know: తూచు.
తూచు — tūcu — "to weigh".
కొట్టువాడు బియ్యం తూచి ఇచ్చాడు. koṭṭuvāḍu biyyaṁ tūci iccāḍu. The shopkeeper weighed out the rice for us.
Past (I …): తూచాను, tūcānu.
తూకం, weight, is its noun. Also to weigh words: మాటలు తూచి మాట్లాడు, to speak carefully.

The word, taken apart.
తూచు (tūcu) is native Dravidian: DEDR 3376 lists Telugu tūcu, "to weigh, to find the weight of", beside tūgu, "to hang, to swing".
