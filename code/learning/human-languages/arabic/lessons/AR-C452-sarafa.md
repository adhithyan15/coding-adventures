# صرف (ṣarafa) — to change money; to spend

## You'll want to know: صرف
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C452-FAR452-04]; assesses=[] -->

**صرف** — *ṣarafa* — "to change money; to spend".

> صرفت مالا في المطار.
>
> *ṣaraftu mālan fī al-maṭār.*
>
> I changed money at the airport.

Present: يصرف, *yaṣrif*.

*siʿr aṣ-ṣarf* is the exchange rate.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**صرف** (*ṣarafa*) is built on the native Arabic root *ṣ-r-f*, "to turn away, to change" (Lane). It is the root of *ṣarrāf*, "a money changer".
