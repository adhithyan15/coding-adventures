Arabic, chapter 101: Neighbourhood, Alley, Building, Bathroom, Balcony.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
حي (ḥayy) — a neighbourhood.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for an airport, then the Arabic for a port.

You'll want to know: حي.
حي — ḥayy — "a neighbourhood".

Wrap-up Recall.
