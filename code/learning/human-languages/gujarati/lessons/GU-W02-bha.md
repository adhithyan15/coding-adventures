# ભ — broad loop, inner turn, separate spine, bha

## Warm-up

## Script — meet the shape
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-SCRIPT-BHA-01]; assesses=[] -->

Keep **ભ** visible. It is **bha**, one consonant sound with the inherent *a*.
Begin at the lower left, circle clockwise around the broad loop, wind through
the compact inner turn, and exit across the long right shoulder. Lift once;
descend the tall right spine and turn through its lower foot.

## Writing — notice and trace
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-SCRIPT-BHA-01] -->
