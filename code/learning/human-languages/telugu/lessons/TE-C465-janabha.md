## You'll want to know: జనాభా
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C465-NIVASI465-02]; assesses=[] -->

**జనాభా** — *janābhā* — "population".

> మా పట్టణం జనాభా లక్ష కంటే ఎక్కువ.
>
> *mā paṭṭaṇaṁ janābhā lakṣa kaṇṭē ekkuva.*
>
> The population of our town is more than a hundred thousand.

జనాభా లెక్కలు, a census.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**జనాభా** (*janābhā*) is a Telugu word for population whose origin is not settled.
