## You'll want to know: കൈപ്പറ്റുക
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C490-TAPAL490-03]; assesses=[] -->

**കൈപ്പറ്റുക** — *kaippaṟṟuka* — "to receive officially; to take receipt of".

> ഓഫീസിലെ ഗുമസ്തൻ എന്റെ അപേക്ഷ കൈപ്പറ്റി.
>
> *ōphīsile gumastan enṟe apēkṣa kaippaṟṟi.*
>
> The office clerk received my application.

Past: കൈപ്പറ്റി, *kaippaṟṟi*.

Literally to take into the hand: കൈ plus പറ്റുക, to get hold of. Common on forms and receipts.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**കൈപ്പറ്റുക** (*kaippaṟṟuka*) is a native Dravidian compound of കൈ, the hand (DEDR 2023), and പറ്റുക, to take hold of (DEDR 4034).
