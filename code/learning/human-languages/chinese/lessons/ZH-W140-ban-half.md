## Script — two dots, two bars, one vertical
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-SCRIPT-BAN-HALF-01]; assesses=[] -->

> 半

**One piece, five strokes.**

**Two short dots** at the top, leaning toward each other: the left one falls to the lower right, the right one to the lower left.

**Two horizontals**, short over long, the two bars of 二.

**A long vertical** from above the shorter bar, straight down through both to the foot.

2 + 2 + 1 = five.
