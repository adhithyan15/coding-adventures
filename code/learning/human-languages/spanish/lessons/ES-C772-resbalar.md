## You'll want to know: resbalar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C772-MONTE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**resbalar** — "to slip".

> Cuidado, que las piedras resbalan.
>
> *Careful, the stones are slippery.*

*Resbalarse* is to slip and fall: *Me resbalé en el barro.*

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*resbalar* is built on old Spanish *esbarar* 'to slide' with *re-* (EtymDB). Where *esbarar* came from is not settled.
