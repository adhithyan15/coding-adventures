## You'll want to know: दसखत
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C199-SEAL199-02]; assesses=[] -->

**दसखत** — *daskhat* — "a signature".

> अठै दसखत करो।
>
> *aṭhai daskhat karo.*
>
> Sign here, please.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**दसखत** (*daskhat*) is usually traced to Persian *dastkhaṭṭ*, "handwriting".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for reading notices and messages.
