## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**കാവ്** (*kāvŭ*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 1416 lists it, "enclosure, garden, holy enclosure", under the verb "to guard, keep", with Tamil *kā*.
