## You'll want to know: उपायनम्
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C532-UPAYANA532-01]; assesses=[] -->

**उपायनम्** — *upāyanam* — "a present".

> यदि मित्रम् आगच्छति, तर्हि अहं तस्मै उपायनं ददामि।
>
> *yadi mitram āgacchati, tarhi ahaṃ tasmai upāyanaṃ dadāmi.*
>
> If my friend comes, I will give him a present.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**उपायनम्** (*upāyanam*) is built on *upa*, *ā* and the root √i, "to go": a coming near; MW gives *upāyana* also as an offer, a present, a gift (MW).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
