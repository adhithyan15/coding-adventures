## You'll want to know: せわ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C334-FUKUSHI334-04]; assesses=[] -->

**せわ** — *sewa* — "looking after; care".

> **いぬの せわを するから、はやく かえります。** — *inu no sewa o suru kara, hayaku kaerimasu* — "I look after the dog, so I go home early."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**せわ** (*sewa*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *se*, "world" (Middle Chinese *syejH*) and *wa*, "talk" (*hwaejH*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
