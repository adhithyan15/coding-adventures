Kannada, chapter 396: Making Laws.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಮಸೂದೆ (masūde) — a draft law; a bill.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to implement, then the Kannada for a committee.

You'll want to know: ಮಸೂದೆ.
ಮಸೂದೆ — masūde — "a draft law; a bill".
ಮಂತ್ರಿ ಹೊಸ ಮಸೂದೆಯ ಬಗ್ಗೆ ಮಾತಾಡಿದರು. mantri hosa masūdeya bagge mātāḍidaru. The minister spoke about the new bill.
A ಮಸೂದೆ (masūde) becomes a ಕಾಯ್ದೆ once it is passed.

The word, taken apart.
ಮಸೂದೆ (masūde) is usually traced to Arabic musawwada, "a draft", through Hindustani masauda.

Lesson 2 of 5.
ಅಂಗೀಕರಿಸು (aṅgīkarisu) — to approve; to pass.

[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a committee, then the Kannada for a draft law.

You'll want to know: ಅಂಗೀಕರಿಸು.
ಅಂಗೀಕರಿಸು — aṅgīkarisu — "to approve; to pass".
ಸಂಸತ್ತು ಮಸೂದೆಯನ್ನು ಅಂಗೀಕರಿಸಿತು. saṃsattu masūdeyannu aṅgīkarisitu. Parliament passed the bill.
Past (I …): ಅಂಗೀಕರಿಸಿದೆ, aṅgīkariside.
ಅಂಗೀಕಾರ, approval.

The word, taken apart.
ಅಂಗೀಕರಿಸು (aṅgīkarisu) is built on Sanskrit aṅgīkāra, "agreement, promise" (Monier-Williams), with the Kannada verb ending -isu.

Lesson 3 of 5.

ತಿದ್ದುಪಡಿ — tiddupaḍi — "an amendment".
ಕಾನೂನಿಗೆ ಒಂದು ತಿದ್ದುಪಡಿ ಬೇಕು. kānūnige ondu tiddupaḍi bēku. The law needs an amendment.
From ತಿದ್ದು, to correct. ತಿದ್ದುಪಡಿ (tiddupaḍi) ತರು, to bring an amendment.

The word, taken apart.
ತಿದ್ದುಪಡಿ (tiddupaḍi) is native Dravidian, built on tiddu, "to make straight, to correct" (DEDR 3251).

Lesson 4 of 5.
ಶಾಸಕ (śāsaka) — a legislator.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to approve, then the Kannada for an amendment.

You'll want to know: ಶಾಸಕ.
ಶಾಸಕ — śāsaka — "a legislator".
ನಮ್ಮ ಶಾಸಕರು ಊರಿನ ರಸ್ತೆ ಸರಿಪಡಿಸಿದರು. namma śāsakaru ūrina raste saripaḍisidaru. Our legislator got the town road repaired.
Plural: ಶಾಸಕರು, śāsakaru.
A member of a state assembly. ಶಾಸನ, a law or an inscription.

The word, taken apart.
ಶಾಸಕ (śāsaka) is borrowed from Sanskrit śāsaka, "a ruler, a governor, an instructor" (Monier-Williams).
