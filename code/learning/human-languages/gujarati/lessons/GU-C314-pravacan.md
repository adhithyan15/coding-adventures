## You'll want to know: પ્રવચન
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C314-HADTAL314-04]; assesses=[] -->

**પ્રવચન** — *pravacan* — "a talk; a speech".

> સાંજે ગુરુએ ચોકમાં પ્રવચન આપ્યું.
>
> *sā̃je gurue cokmā̃ pravacan āpyũ.*
>
> In the evening the teacher gave a speech in the square.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**પ્રવચન** (*pravacan*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *pravacana*, "speaking, teaching, exposition" (Monier-Williams). A speech is given: **પ્રવચન આપ્યું**.
