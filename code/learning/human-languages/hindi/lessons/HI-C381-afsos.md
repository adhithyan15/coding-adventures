## You'll want to know: अफ़सोस
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C381-KASH381-02]; assesses=[] -->

**अफ़सोस** — *afsos* — "regret, a pity".

> अफ़सोस है कि अगर मैं जल्दी आता तो मीरा से मिलता।
>
> *afsos hai ki agar maiṁ jaldī ātā to Mīrā se miltā.*
>
> It's a pity: if I had come earlier, I would have met Mira.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अफ़सोस** (*afsos*) is usually traced to Persian *afsōs*, "regret, alas".
