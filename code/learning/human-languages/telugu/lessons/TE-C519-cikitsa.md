## You'll want to know: చికిత్స
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C519-CIKITSA519-01]; assesses=[] -->

**చికిత్స** — *cikitsa* — "treatment; therapy".

> మంచి చికిత్స వల్ల ఆమె త్వరగా కోలుకుంది.
>
> *mañci cikitsa valla āme tvaragā kōlukundi.*
>
> Thanks to good treatment she recovered quickly.

చికిత్స చేయు, to treat; చికిత్స పొందు, to receive treatment.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**చికిత్స** (*cikitsa*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *cikitsā*, "medical attendance, the practice of medicine" (Monier-Williams).
