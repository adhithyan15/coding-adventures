## Script — the frame of 两, and two crosses
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-SCRIPT-WANG-NET-01]; assesses=[] -->

> 网

**The frame, two strokes**, as in 两: a vertical down the left, then a stroke across the top that turns down the right side and hooks.

**Two small crosses inside, two strokes each**, left before right: a left-falling stroke, then a dot across it.

2 + 2 + 2 = six.
