Lesson 1 of 5.
die Bewerbung — an application.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for a description, then the German for a visit.

You'll want to know: die Bewerbung.
die Bewerbung — "an application".

The word, taken apart.
Bewerbung is bewerben, "to apply", + -ung. The verb is built on Old High German werban (EtymDB 2.1).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for an application, then the German for a relationship.

You'll want to know: der Eindruck.
der Eindruck — "an impression".

The word, taken apart.
Eindruck from Middle High German īndruc (EtymDB 2.1): ein-, "in", + the word for "a press, a pressing": what is pressed into the mind, after Latin impressiō.

[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: die Eintrittskarte.
die Eintrittskarte — "an admission ticket".

The word, taken apart.
Eintrittskarte is Eintritt, "entry", + -s- + Karte, "card": a card that lets you step in. Karte is borrowed from Latin charta, "paper", itself from Greek (EtymDB 2.1).
