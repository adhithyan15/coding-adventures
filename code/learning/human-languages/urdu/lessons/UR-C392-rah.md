## You'll want to know: راہ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C392-STAND392-03]; assesses=[] -->

**راہ** — *rāh* — "a path; a way".

> یہ راہ سیدھی دریا تک جاتی ہے۔
>
> *yih rāh sīdhī daryā tak jātī hai.*
>
> This path runs straight to the river.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**راہ** (*rāh*) is usually traced to Persian *rāh*, "a road, a way"; it is the first half of **راہگیر**, "a passer-by".
