## You'll want to know: rancunier
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C475-RANCUNE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**rancunier** — "resentful, unforgiving".

> Ma voisine est rancunière : elle n'oublie rien.
>
> *My neighbour is resentful: she forgets nothing.*

Feminine *rancunière*. Someone who keeps grudges.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*rancunier* is built on *rancune* "grudge", which goes back to Latin *rancor* "a rancid smell, a grudge" (Lewis & Short).
