## You'll want to know: ભક્ત
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C330-GARBO330-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ભક્ત** — *bhakt* — "a devotee".

> મંદિરમાં ઘણા ભક્ત આવ્યા.
>
> *mandirmā̃ ghaṇā bhakt āvyā.*
>
> Many devotees came to the temple.

A masculine or feminine noun, by the person meant.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ભક્ત** (*bhakt*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *bhakta*, "devoted to, attached to" (Monier-Williams). The same Sanskrit word, in the sense of a share of food, gave **ભાત**, rice.
