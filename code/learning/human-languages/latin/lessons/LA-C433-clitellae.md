## You'll want to know: clītellae, clītellārum
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C433-IUMENTUM-04]; assesses=[] -->

**clītellae, clītellārum** — "a pack saddle (for an ass or mule)".

> Asinus clītellās portat et in clītellīs saccī sunt.
>
> *The donkey carries a pack saddle, and on the pack saddle there are sacks.*

First declension, feminine plural: *clītellae, clītellārum f.* One thing, plural form.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*clītellae* is inherited: EtymDB traces it to Proto-Italic *kleitrelā*. Lewis & Short give the sense but no origin.
