## You'll want to know: приложение
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C401-PRILOZHENIYA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**приложение** (*prilozhéniye*) — "an app; an attachment; a supplement".

> Я скачал новое приложение.
>
> *Ya skachál nóvoye prilozhéniye.*
>
> I downloaded a new app.

Neuter. An app on a phone, an attachment to a letter, or a supplement to a magazine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**приложение** is built on *приложить* "to put to, to attach", from *при-* "to" and *ложить* "to lay": something laid alongside. An app is the newest sense.
