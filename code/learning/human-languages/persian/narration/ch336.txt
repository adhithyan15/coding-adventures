Persian, chapter 336: Divorce and loss.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
طلاق (talâq) — a divorce.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a mahr, then the Persian for a trousseau.

You'll want to know: طلاق.
طلاق (talâq) — "a divorce".
طلاق (talâq) آنها برای بچه ها سخت بود. Talâq-e ânhâ barâ-ye bachche-hâ sakht bud. Their divorce was hard on the children.
Traditionally talâq dâdan is said of the husband.

The word, taken apart.
talâq is borrowed from Arabic ṭalāq, "divorce" (EtymDB).

Lesson 2 of 5.
طلاق گرفتن (talâq gereftan) — to get divorced.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a trousseau, then the Persian for a divorce.

You'll want to know: طلاق گرفتن.
طلاق گرفتن (talâq gereftan) — "to get divorced".
آنها بعد از ده سال طلاق گرفتند. Ânhâ ba'd az dah sâl talâq gereftand. They got divorced after ten years.
With az: az shoharash talâq gereft "she divorced her husband". Present talâq migirad.

The word, taken apart.
talâq is borrowed from Arabic ṭalāq (EtymDB); gereftan "to take" is inherited Persian, from the Old Persian root grab- "to seize".

Lesson 3 of 5.
بیوه (bive) — a widow; widowed.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a divorce, then the Persian for to get divorced.

You'll want to know: بیوه.
بیوه (bive) — "a widow; widowed".
مادربزرگم سی سال بیوه بود. Mâdarbozorgam si sâl bive bud. My grandmother was a widow for thirty years.
Mostly of women; mard-e bive is "a widower".

The word, taken apart.
bive is a native Persian word. EtymDB lists it without a parent, so its deeper origin is not settled here.

Lesson 5 of 5.
سرپرستی (sarparasti) — custody, guardianship.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a widow, then the Persian for an orphan.

You'll want to know: سرپرستی.
سرپرستی (sarparasti) — "custody, guardianship".
سرپرستی (sarparasti) بچه با مادرش است. Sarparasti-ye bachche bâ mâdarash ast. The child's mother has custody.
sar "head" + parast "one who tends" + -i. Also "supervision" at work.

The word, taken apart.
sarparasti is built from Persian sar "head" and parast-, the stem of parastidan "to tend, to worship", with the noun suffix -i. All of it is native.
