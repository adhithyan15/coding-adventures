## You'll want to know: प्रेरणा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C377-PRABHAV377-04]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रेरणा** — *prerṇā* — "inspiration; motivation".

> आईच्या कष्टांतून मला प्रेरणा मिळाली.
>
> *āīcyā kaṣṭāntūn malā prerṇā miḷālī.*
>
> I drew inspiration from my mother's hard work.

Feminine.

X ची प्रेरणा घेणे, to take inspiration from X.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रेरणा** (*prerṇā*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *preraṇa*, "driving on, urging" (Monier-Williams).
