Persian, chapter 525: Painting.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
نقاش (naqqâsh) — a painter.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to play a role, then the Persian for an actor.

You'll want to know: نقاش.
نقاش (naqqâsh) — "a painter".
این نقاش از گلها نقاشی میکند. in naqqâsh az golhâ naqqâshi mikonad. This painter paints flowers.
Also a house painter. Plural naqqâshân or naqqâshhâ.

The word, taken apart.
naqqâsh comes from Arabic naqqāš (EtymDB), the Arabic word for one who paints or engraves, from the same root as naqsh.

Lesson 2 of 5.
بوم (bum) — a canvas (for painting).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for an actor, then the Persian for a painter.

You'll want to know: بوم.
بوم (bum) — "a canvas (for painting)".
نقاش یک بوم بزرگ خرید. naqqâsh yek bum-e bozorg kharid. The painter bought a large canvas.
Other words are spelled the same: bum 'land' in marz-o-bum 'homeland', and bum 'owl'.

The word, taken apart.
EtymDB traces Persian bum, 'land, ground', to Middle Persian and on to Old Persian (EtymDB). The painter's bum, the ground a picture is laid on, is usually linked with it, but the link is not settled.

Lesson 3 of 5.
قلم مو (qalam mu) — a paintbrush.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a painter, then the Persian for a canvas.

You'll want to know: قلم مو.
قلم مو (qalam mu) — "a paintbrush".
او با یک قلم موی نازک کار میکند. u bâ yek qalam mu-ye nâzok kâr mikonad. He works with a thin paintbrush.
qalam 'pen' + mu 'hair': a hair pen.

The word, taken apart.
qalam mu is built in Persian on qalam, 'pen', from Arabic qalam (EtymDB), and mu, 'hair', which EtymDB traces to Middle Persian (EtymDB).

Lesson 4 of 5.
آبرنگ (âbrang) — watercolour.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a canvas, then the Persian for a paintbrush.

You'll want to know: آبرنگ.
آبرنگ (âbrang) — "watercolour".
دخترم با آبرنگ یک دریا کشید. dokhtaram bâ âbrang yek daryâ keshid. My daughter painted a sea in watercolour.
âb 'water' + rang 'colour'.

The word, taken apart.
âbrang is built on two inherited words: âb, 'water', and rang, 'colour, dye', both of which EtymDB traces to Middle Persian (EtymDB).
