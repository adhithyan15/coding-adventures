# ちる (chiru) — to scatter; to fall (of petals or leaves)

## You'll want to know: ちる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C209-SEASON209-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ちる** — *chiru* — "to scatter; to fall (of petals or leaves)".

> **あした さくらが ちります。** — *ashita sakura ga chirimasu* — "The cherry blossoms will fall tomorrow."

Polite **ちります**, *chirimasu*; a godan verb.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ちる** (*chiru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi, and its older origin is not settled (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
