## You'll want to know: публиковать
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C280-SOOBSHCHAT-03]; assesses=[] -->

**публиковать** (*publikovát*) — "to publish".

> Газета публикует эти письма.
>
> *Gazéta publikúyet éti písma.*
>
> The newspaper publishes these letters.

Imperfective; the perfective is *опубликовать* (*opublikovát*). Present *публикую*, *публикуешь*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*публиковать* goes back to Latin *pūblicāre* "to make public", from *pūblicus* "of the people" (Lewis & Short), with the Russian verb ending *-овать*; EtymDB lists the Russian word with no parent.
