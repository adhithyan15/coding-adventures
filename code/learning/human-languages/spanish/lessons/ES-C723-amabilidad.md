## You'll want to know: la amabilidad
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C723-GENEROSO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**la amabilidad** — "kindness".

> Gracias por su amabilidad.
>
> *Thank you for your kindness.*

A polite formula in letters and shops. The adjective is *amable*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*amabilidad* is a learned borrowing from Latin *amābilitās* (EtymDB), built on *amābilis* "lovable", from *amāre* "to love".
