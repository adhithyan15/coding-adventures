## You'll want to know: ಮೌಲ್ಯ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C387-SAVALU387-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ಮೌಲ್ಯ** — *maulya* — "a value; worth".

> ಹಳೆಯ ನಾಣ್ಯಗಳಿಗೆ ಈಗ ಹೆಚ್ಚು ಮೌಲ್ಯ ಇದೆ.
>
> *haḷeya nāṇyagaḷige īga heccu maulya ide.*
>
> Old coins are worth more now.

ಮೌಲ್ಯಗಳು, values (moral or social).

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಮೌಲ್ಯ** (*maulya*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *maulya*, "price" (CDIAL 10373), a form of *mūlya*, "worth".
