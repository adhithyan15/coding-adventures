## You'll want to know: juger
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C342-JUGEMENT-02]; assesses=[] -->

**juger** — "to judge".

> Il ne faut pas juger les gens sur leurs vêtements.
>
> *You should not judge people by their clothes.*

*Nous jugeons*, with an *e* to keep the *g* soft.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*juger* is inherited from Latin *iūdicāre* "to pass judgement" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short), from *iūdex* "judge", itself built from *iūs* "law" and *dīcere* "to say".
