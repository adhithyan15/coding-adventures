## You'll want to know: টেকা
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C316-GHERA316-04]; assesses=[] -->

**টেকা** — *ṭekā* — "to hold out; to last".

> দুর্গটা তিন মাস টিকল।
>
> *durgôṭā tin mās ṭiklô.*
>
> The fort held out for three months.

Past (he/she …): সেই দুর্গ টিকল, *sei durgô ṭiklô*.

The stem vowel rises in the past: টিকল, held out.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**টেকা** (*ṭekā*) is filed in CDIAL 5420 under a Middle Indo-Aryan *ṭakk-*, 'remain, stop', which lists Bengali *ṭẽkā* and *ṭikā*, 'to remain'.
