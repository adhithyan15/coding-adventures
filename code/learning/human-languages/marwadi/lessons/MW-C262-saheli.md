## You'll want to know: सहेली
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C262-JANO262-03]; assesses=[] -->

**सहेली** — *sahelī* — "a woman's friend".

> म्हारी सहेली मेंहदी लगावै है।
>
> *mhārī sahelī mẽhdī lagāvai hai.*
>
> My friend is putting on henna.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सहेली** (*sahelī*) is inherited from Sanskrit *sakhī*, "a woman's confidante"; CDIAL lists Old Marwari *sahelī* under it (CDIAL 13074).
