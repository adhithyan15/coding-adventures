## You'll want to know: iūrō, iūrāre
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C310-IURO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**iūrō, iūrāre** — "to swear (an oath)".

> Testis iūrāvit sē vēra dīcere.
>
> *The witness swore that he was telling the truth.*

*iūrō, iūrāre, iūrāvī, iūrātum*, with accusative and infinitive. English *jury* comes from it through French.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**iūrō** is built on *iūs*, "right, law" (EtymDB): to bind oneself by law. English *jury* comes from it through French.
