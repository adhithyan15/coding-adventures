## You'll want to know: ਢੋਲ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C347-DRUM347-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ਢੋਲ** — *ḍhol* — "a drum; a big two-sided drum".

> ਮੈਨੂੰ ਢੋਲ ਦੀ ਆਵਾਜ਼ ਬਹੁਤ ਪਸੰਦ ਹੈ।
>
> *mainū̃ ḍhol dī āvāz bahut pasand hai.*
>
> I really like the sound of the drum.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਢੋਲ** (*ḍhol*) is inherited from Sanskrit *ḍhola*, "drum"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *ḍhol*, "drum", under it (CDIAL 5608).
