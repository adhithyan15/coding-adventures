## You'll want to know: die Kritik
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C340-KRITIK-05]; assesses=[] -->

**die Kritik** — "the criticism; the review".

> Der neue Film hat gute Kritiken bekommen.
>
> *The new film got good reviews.*

Plural: *die Kritiken*. *Kritik üben an* + dative — to criticize.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Kritik* was borrowed from French *critique* (EtymDB), which goes back through Latin *criticus* to Greek *kritikós* "able to judge", from *krínein* "to judge".
