## You'll want to know: کارآگاه
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C320-KARAGAH-01]; assesses=[] -->

**کارآگاه** (*kârâgâh*) — "a detective".

> کارآگاه همه چیز را با دقت نگاه کرد.
>
> *Kârâgâh hame chiz râ bâ deqqat negâh kard.*
>
> The detective looked at everything carefully.

*kâr* "work" + *âgâh* "aware": one who is aware of the case.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*kârâgâh* is a modern compound built from Persian *kâr* "work" and *âgâh* "aware, informed", both native words.
