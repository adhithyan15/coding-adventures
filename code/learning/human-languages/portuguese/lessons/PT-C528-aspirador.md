## You'll want to know: o aspirador
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C528-LIMPEZA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**o aspirador** — "vacuum cleaner".

> O aspirador faz muito barulho.
>
> *The vacuum cleaner makes a lot of noise.*

Built on *aspirar* with *-dor*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*aspirador* is built on *aspirar* "to breathe in, to suck in", from Latin *aspīrāre* (EtymDB).

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
