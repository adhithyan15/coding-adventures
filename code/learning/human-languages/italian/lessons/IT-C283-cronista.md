## You'll want to know: il cronista
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C283-REDAZIONE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**il cronista** — "the news reporter".

> Il cronista ha parlato con i vicini.
>
> *The reporter spoke to the neighbours.*

A woman is *la cronista*: same form.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*cronista* is built on *cronaca* "chronicle, news" with the ending *-ista*; the root is Greek *khrónos* "time" (EtymDB).
