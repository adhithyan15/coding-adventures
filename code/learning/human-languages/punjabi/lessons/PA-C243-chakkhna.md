## You'll want to know: ਚੱਖਣਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C243-KAM243-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ਚੱਖਣਾ** — *chakkhṇā* — "to taste".

> ਕੀ ਤੁਸੀਂ ਖੀਰ ਚੱਖੋਗੇ?
>
> *kī tusī̃ khīr chakkhoge?*
>
> Will you taste the rice pudding?

Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਖੀਰ ਚੱਖੀ, *maiṁ khīr chakkhī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਚੱਖਣਾ** (*chakkhṇā*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*cakṣati*, "tastes"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *cakkhṇā*, "to taste" (CDIAL 4557).
