You'll want to know: la seconde.
la seconde — "a second".

The word, taken apart.
seconde from Medieval Latin secunda, short for secunda minuta, "the second small part" of an hour — a learned borrowing. (EtymDB 2.1).

Lesson 2 of 5.

Warm-up.

Before the new one: say the French for an address, then the French for a second.

You'll want to know: la monnaie.
la monnaie — "change (coins); currency".

The word, taken apart.
monnaie from Old French moneie from Latin moneta, "a mint, coin", from Juno Moneta, in whose temple Rome struck its coins — inherited. (EtymDB 2.1).
