Tamil, chapter 349: Thinking It Through.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
சிந்தி (cinti) — to think deeply; to reflect.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for a consensus, then the Tamil for some people.

You'll want to know: சிந்தி.
சிந்தி — cinti — "to think deeply; to reflect".
முடிவு செய்வதற்கு முன் அவள் நன்றாகச் சிந்தித்தாள். muṭivu ceyvataṟku muṉ avaḷ naṉṟākac cintittāḷ. She thought it over carefully before deciding.
Past (I …): சிந்தித்தேன், cintittēṉ.
What you think about takes -ஐப் பற்றி: அதைப் பற்றிச் சிந்தி (cinti). The noun is சிந்தனை, a thought.

The word, taken apart.
சிந்தி (cinti) is a verb built on Sanskrit cintā, "thought, care" (CDIAL 4816), with the loan-verb ending -இ.

What you've built.

அவன் பதிலைச் சரியாக ஊகித்தான். avaṉ patilaic cariyāka ūkittāṉ. He guessed the answer correctly.

What is guessed takes -ஐ: பதிலை ஊகி (ūki), to guess the answer.

The word, taken apart.
ஊகி (ūki) is a verb built on Sanskrit ūha, "consideration", Prakrit "reasoning, guess" (CDIAL 2443), with the loan-verb ending -இ.

Lesson 3 of 5.
அனுமானி (aṉumāṉi) — to assume; to presume.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for to think deeply, then the Tamil for to guess.

You'll want to know: அனுமானி.
அனுமானி — aṉumāṉi — "to assume; to presume".
அவர் வரமாட்டார் என்று நான் அனுமானித்தேன். avar varamāṭṭār eṉṟu nāṉ aṉumāṉittēṉ. I assumed that he would not come.
Past (I …): அனுமானித்தேன், aṉumāṉittēṉ.
The assumption is a clause with என்று: … என்று அனுமானி (aṉumāṉi).

The word, taken apart.
அனுமானி (aṉumāṉi) is a verb built on Sanskrit anumāna, "inference, thought" (CDIAL 328), the term Indian logic used for reasoning from a sign to what it shows.

Lesson 4 of 5.
கருதுகோள் (karutukōḷ) — a hypothesis.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for to guess, then the Tamil for to assume.

You'll want to know: கருதுகோள்.
கருதுகோள் — karutukōḷ — "a hypothesis".
விஞ்ஞானி ஒரு புதிய கருதுகோளை முன்வைத்தார். viññāṉi oru putiya karutukōḷai muṉvaittār. The scientist put forward a new hypothesis.
கருதுகோள் (karutukōḷ) முன்வை, to put forward a hypothesis.

The word, taken apart.
கருதுகோள் (karutukōḷ) is a modern Tamil coinage built from native karutu, "to intend, suppose, consider" (DEDR 1283), and kōḷ, "taking, opinion, tenet", the noun of கொள்: "what is supposed".

Lesson 5 of 5.
பலர் (palar) — many people.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for to assume, then the Tamil for a hypothesis.

You'll want to know: பலர்.
பலர் — palar — "many people".
பலர் இந்தக் கருத்தை ஏற்றுக்கொண்டார்கள். palar intak karuttai ēṟṟukkoṇṭārkaḷ. Many people accepted this view.
பலருக்கு, to many people: the dative of a word in -அர் adds -உக்கு.

The word, taken apart.
பலர் (palar) is native Dravidian: DEDR 3987 lists Tamil pala, "many, several", and palar, "many persons", with the personal plural -அர்.
