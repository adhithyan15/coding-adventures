## You'll want to know: けつろん
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C317-SANSEI317-05]; assesses=[] -->

**けつろん** — *ketsuron* — "a conclusion".

> **けつろんは あしたの かいぎで きめた ほうが いいです。** — *ketsuron wa ashita no kaigi de kimeta hō ga ii desu* — "We had better reach a conclusion at tomorrow's meeting."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**けつろん** (*ketsuron*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *ketsu*, "tie" (Middle Chinese *ket*) and *ron*, "argument" (*lwon*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
