## You'll want to know: tōnstrīna, tōnstrīnae
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C467-SUTRINA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**tōnstrīna, tōnstrīnae** — "a barber's shop".

> In tōnstrīnā hominēs sedent et fābulās narrant.
>
> *In the barber's shop men sit and tell stories.*

First declension, feminine: *tōnstrīna, tōnstrīnae f.*, from *tōnsor* 'barber'. A favourite place for gossip.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*tōnstrīna* is built on *tōnsor* 'barber' (Lewis & Short), which comes from *tondeō* 'to shear, to clip'.
