## You'll want to know: आसार
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C546-TAQDIR546-02]; assesses=[] -->

**आसार** — *āsār* — "signs; prospects (of something coming)".

> आसमान में बादल हैं, बारिश के आसार हैं।
>
> *Āsmān meṁ bādal haiṁ, bāriś ke āsār haiṁ.*
>
> There are clouds in the sky: rain looks likely.

Masculine.

Used in the plural: बारिश के आसार हैं.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**आसार** (*āsār*) is usually traced to Arabic *āthār*, "traces, signs", through Persian.
