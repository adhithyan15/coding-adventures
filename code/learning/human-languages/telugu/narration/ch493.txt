Telugu, chapter 493: Heading Home.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
సామగ్రి (sāmagri) — equipment; materials.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a false step, then the Telugu for confusion.

You'll want to know: సామగ్రి.
సామగ్రి — sāmagri — "equipment; materials".
కొండ ఎక్కడానికి కావలసిన సామగ్రి అంతా సర్దాం. koṇḍa ekkaḍāniki kāvalasina sāmagri antā sardāṁ. We packed all the gear we needed for the climb.
Things needed for a task: వంట సామగ్రి (sāmagri), cooking things; కట్టడ సామగ్రి (sāmagri), building materials.

The word, taken apart.
సామగ్రి (sāmagri) is borrowed from Sanskrit sāmagrī, "totality, the whole of what is needed" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 2 of 7.
సాగు (sāgu) — to go on; to proceed.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for confusion, then the Telugu for equipment.

You'll want to know: సాగు.
సాగు — sāgu — "to go on; to proceed".
మా ప్రయాణం హాయిగా సాగింది. mā prayāṇaṁ hāyigā sāgindi. Our journey went on pleasantly.
Past (I …): సాగాను, sāgānu.
Said of a journey, a meeting or a life: సాఫీగా సాగింది, it went smoothly. As a noun సాగు (sāgu) is farming.

The word, taken apart.
సాగు (sāgu) is native Dravidian: DEDR 2430 lists Telugu sāgu, "to go on, to proceed, to continue".

Lesson 3 of 7.
తిప్పు (tippu) — to turn round; to twist.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for equipment, then the Telugu for to go on.

You'll want to know: తిప్పు.
తిప్పు — tippu — "to turn round; to twist".
డ్రైవరు బండిని వెనక్కి తిప్పాడు. ḍraivaru baṇḍini venakki tippāḍu. The driver turned the cart back.
Past (I …): తిప్పాను, tippānu.
The causative of తిరుగు, to turn: to make something turn. తాళంచెవి తిప్పు (tippu), to turn a key.

The word, taken apart.
తిప్పు (tippu) is native Dravidian, usually traced to the family of తిరుగు, "to turn, to go round" (DEDR 3246), as its causative.

Lesson 4 of 7.
చెదిరిపోవు (cediripōvu) — to scatter; to disperse.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to go on, then the Telugu for to turn round.

You'll want to know: చెదిరిపోవు.
చెదిరిపోవు — cediripōvu — "to scatter; to disperse".
వర్షం రాగానే జనం చెదిరిపోయారు. varṣaṁ rāgānē janaṁ cediripōyāru. As soon as the rain came the crowd scattered.
Past (it …): చెదిరిపోయింది, cediripōyindi.
Said of a crowd, of clouds and of a dream. Its subject is a thing, so the past shown is the third person: చెదిరిపోయింది, it scattered.

The word, taken apart.
చెదిరిపోవు (cediripōvu) is native Dravidian: DEDR 1546 lists Telugu cedaru, "to be scattered, dispersed", here with పోవు, "to go" (DEDR 4572).

Lesson 5 of 7.
వెనుదిరుగు (venudirugu) — to turn back; to head back.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to turn round, then the Telugu for to scatter.

You'll want to know: వెనుదిరుగు.
వెనుదిరుగు — venudirugu — "to turn back; to head back".
వర్షం వల్ల మేము మధ్యలోనే వెనుదిరిగాం. varṣaṁ valla mēmu madhyalōnē venudirigāṁ. Because of the rain we turned back halfway.
Past (I …): వెనుదిరిగాను, venudirigānu.
వెను, back, + తిరుగు, to turn: to turn round and go back the way you came. Said of travellers, and of a side that gives up.

The word, taken apart.
వెనుదిరుగు (venudirugu) is native Dravidian: వెను, "the back", which DEDR 5488 lists as Telugu ven(n)u beside venuka, "behind", and తిరుగు, "to turn, to go round" (DEDR 3246), whose త becomes ద inside the compound.
