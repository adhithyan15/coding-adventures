## Chapter 6 — Five verbs at the core *(authored)*

**ہونا** *honā* → **جانا** *jānā* → **آنا** *ānā* → **بولنا** *bolnā* →
**جاننا** *jānnā*. The track's first verbs, and its first A2 spine node. One
machine carries the whole chapter: every infinitive ends in **-نا** *-nā*, and
stripping that ending yields the stem, so each verb after the first costs
almost nothing. The present tense is then shown making two agreements at once —
the participle for gender and number, the copula for person — a thing English
never does. Etymology runs deep here (*jānnā* ← *jñā-*, cousin of English
*know*), goes honestly cold where it must (*bolnā* stops at Prakrit
*bollaï*), and the Persian layer is placed beside the Indo-Aryan core rather
than above it. One new letter only: **ب**, taught against already-read **پ**.
