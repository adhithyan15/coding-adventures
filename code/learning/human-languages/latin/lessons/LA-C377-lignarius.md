## You'll want to know: lignārius, lignāriī
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C377-FULLO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**lignārius, lignāriī** — "a carpenter, a worker in wood".

> Lignārius mēnsās et sellās facit.
>
> *The carpenter makes tables and chairs.*

*lignārius, lignāriī* m., often *faber lignārius*: a smith (*faber*) who works in wood, *lignum*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*lignārius* is built on *lignum* "wood" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short), with the ending *-ārius* of a man busy with something.
