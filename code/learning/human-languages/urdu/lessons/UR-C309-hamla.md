## You'll want to know: حملہ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C309-HAMLA309-01]; assesses=[] -->

**حملہ** — *hamlā* — "an attack".

> فوج نے رات کو حملہ کیا۔
>
> *fauj ne rāt ko hamlā kiyā.*
>
> The army attacked at night.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**حملہ** (*hamlā*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *ḥamla*, "a charge, an onset". **حملہ کیا** is attacked.
