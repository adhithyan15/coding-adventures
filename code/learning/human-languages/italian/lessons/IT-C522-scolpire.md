## You'll want to know: scolpire
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C522-SCULTURA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**scolpire** — "to sculpt, to carve".

> Ha scolpito una piccola statua nel legno.
>
> *He carved a small statue out of wood.*

An *-isc-* verb: *scolpisco*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*scolpire* comes from Latin *sculpere* "to carve, to chisel" (EtymDB).
