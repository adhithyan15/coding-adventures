## You'll want to know: la manifestation
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C363-MANIFESTATION-01]; assesses=[] -->

**la manifestation** — "the demonstration, the protest march; the event".

> Il y a une manifestation dans le centre-ville samedi.
>
> *There is a demonstration in the town centre on Saturday.*

Often shortened to *la manif*. Also any public event: *une manifestation sportive*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*manifestation* comes from Late Latin *manifēstātiō* "a making plain" (EtymDB), from *manifestus* "clear, plain" (Lewis & Short).
