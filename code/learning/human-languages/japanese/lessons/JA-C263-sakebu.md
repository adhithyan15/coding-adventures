## You'll want to know: さけぶ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C263-KYUUKYUU263-03]; assesses=[] -->

**さけぶ** — *sakebu* — "to shout; to cry out".

> **こどもが かわで さけびました。** — *kodomo ga kawa de sakebimashita* — "A child cried out by the river."

Polite **さけびます**, *sakebimasu*; a godan verb. Past (polite): **おとこが さけびました。**, *otoko ga sakebimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**さけぶ** (*sakebu*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict).
