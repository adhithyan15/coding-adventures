## You'll want to know: переход
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C440-PEREKHOD-01]; assesses=[] -->

**переход** (*perekhód*) — "a crossing; an underpass".

> Подземный переход рядом с остановкой.
>
> *Podzémnyy perekhód ryádom s ostanóvkoy.*
>
> The underpass is next to the bus stop.

A masculine noun. *Пешеходный переход* — a pedestrian crossing; *подземный переход* — an underpass.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*переход* is built from *пере-* 'across' and *ход* 'a going', which is inherited from Proto-Slavic *xodŭ* 'motion' (EtymDB).
