## You'll want to know: संविद्
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C534-SAMVID534-02]; assesses=[] -->

**संविद्** — *saṃvid* — "an agreement".

> यदि भवान् तेन सह संविदं करोति, तर्हि कार्यं भविष्यति।
>
> *yadi bhavān tena saha saṃvidaṃ karoti, tarhi kāryaṃ bhaviṣyati.*
>
> If you come to an agreement with him, the work will get done.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**संविद्** (*saṃvid*) is built on *sam*, "together", and the root √vid, "to know": a knowing together; MW gives *saṃvid* as consciousness, and as a mutual understanding, an agreement (MW).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
