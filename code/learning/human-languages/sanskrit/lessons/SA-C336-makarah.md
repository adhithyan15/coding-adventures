## You'll want to know: मकरः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C336-NITI336-03]; assesses=[] -->

**मकरः** — *makaraḥ* — "a sea monster; a crocodile".

> नद्याम् एकः मकरः वसति।
>
> *nadyām ekaḥ makaraḥ vasati.*
>
> A crocodile lives in the river.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**मकरः** (*makaraḥ*) MW gives *makara* as a kind of sea monster, sometimes confused with the crocodile, shark or dolphin, without a derivation, so its origin is uncertain (MW).
