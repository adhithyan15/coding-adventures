## You'll want to know: a fratura
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C307-FERIDO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**a fratura** — "the fracture, the break".

> Ela tem uma fratura no braço.
>
> *She has a fractured arm.*

Before the 1990 spelling reform, *fractura*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*fratura* is a learned borrowing from Latin *frāctūra* "a breaking", from *frangere* "to break" (Lewis & Short).

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
