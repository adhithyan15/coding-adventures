## You'll want to know: कूटना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C523-CHAKU523-03]; assesses=[] -->

**कूटना** — *kūṭnā* — "to pound".

> उसने अदरक को पत्थर से कूटा।
>
> *Usne adrak ko patthar se kūṭā.*
>
> She pounded the ginger with a stone.

Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने लहसुन कूटा, *usne lahsun kūṭā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**कूटना** (*kūṭnā*) is a tadbhava, inherited from Sanskrit *kuṭṭayati*, "pounds" (CDIAL 3241).
