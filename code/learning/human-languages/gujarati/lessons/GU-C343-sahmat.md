# સહમત (sahmat) — in agreement

## You'll want to know: સહમત
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C343-TEKO343-04]; assesses=[] -->

**સહમત** — *sahmat* — "in agreement".

> હું તમારા મત સાથે સહમત છું.
>
> *hũ tamārā mat sāthe sahmat chũ.*
>
> I agree with your view.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**સહમત** (*sahmat*) is built from Sanskrit *saha*, "with", and *mata*, "an opinion" (Monier-Williams): of the same opinion.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
