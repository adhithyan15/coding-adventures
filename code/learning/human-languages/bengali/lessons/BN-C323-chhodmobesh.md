## You'll want to know: ছদ্মবেশ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C323-NAYOK323-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ছদ্মবেশ** — *chhôdmôbesh* — "a disguise".

> খলনায়ক সন্ন্যাসীর ছদ্মবেশে প্রাসাদে ঢুকল।
>
> *khôlnāyôk sônnyāsīr chhôdmôbeshe prāsāde ḍhuklô.*
>
> The villain got into the palace disguised as a holy man.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ছদ্মবেশ** (*chhôdmôbesh*) joins Sanskrit *chadman*, 'a covering, a deceptive dress' (Monier-Williams), to *veśa*, 'dress': false clothes.
