## You'll want to know: बिफरना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C456-KRODH456-04]; assesses=[] -->

**बिफरना** — *bipharnā* — "to fly into a rage".

> कम पैसे देखकर दुकानदार बिफर गया।
>
> *kam paise dekhkar dukāndār biphar gayā.*
>
> Seeing how little money he had been given, the shopkeeper flew into a rage.

Past (he …): वह बिफर गया, *vah biphar gayā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**बिफरना** (*bipharnā*) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Hindi *bipharnā*, "to become enraged", under *\*vispharati*, "quivers, trembles", a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 12014): someone shaking with anger. It is an outburst, much stronger than *ciṛhnā*.
