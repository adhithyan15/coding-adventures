Lesson 1 of 5.
le repos — rest.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for the liver, then the French for a kidney.

You'll want to know: le repos.
le repos — "rest".

The word, taken apart.
repos — formed from the verb for "to rest", from Late Latin repausare, "to rest", from pausare, "to pause". (EtymDB 2.1).

You'll want to know: l'amour.
l'amour — "love".

The word, taken apart.
amour from Old French amor from Latin amor, "love" — inherited (EtymDB 2.1). Its -our, where French usually has -eur as in douleur, is often put down to the love poetry of the south.
