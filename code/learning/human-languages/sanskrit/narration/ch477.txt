Sanskrit, chapter 477: Feeling Unwell.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
भ्रमः (bhramaḥ) — dizziness.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a figure, then the Sanskrit for nourishes.

You'll want to know: भ्रमः.
भ्रमः — bhramaḥ — "dizziness".
ज्वरेण मम भ्रमः भवति। jvareṇa mama bhramaḥ bhavati. The fever makes me dizzy.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
भ्रमः (bhramaḥ) comes from the root √bhram, "to wander, to whirl", the root of भ्रमति; MW gives "wandering" and "giddiness, dizziness" (MW).
