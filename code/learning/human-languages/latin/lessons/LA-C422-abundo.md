## You'll want to know: abundō, abundāre
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C422-DIVITIAE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**abundō, abundāre** — "to have plenty (of), to abound; to overflow".

> Urbs frūmentō abundat.
>
> *The city has plenty of grain.*

*abundō, abundāre, abundāvī, abundātum* + ablative, built on *unda*, a wave: to overflow. English *abound* and *abundant*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*abundō* is built from *ab* "away, over" and *undō* "to surge", from *unda* "a wave" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short): to flow over. English *abound* and *abundant* come from it.
