## You'll want to know: yóujiàn
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C201-POST201-03]; assesses=[] -->

*yóujiàn* — "email; mail".

> *lǎoshī de yóujiàn hěn zhòngyào* — The teacher's email is important.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*yóujiàn* is built from *yóu* 'post' and *jiàn* 'an item', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *hjuw* and *gjenX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'a portion'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for reading notices and messages.
