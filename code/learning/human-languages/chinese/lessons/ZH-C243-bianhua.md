## You'll want to know: biànhuà
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C243-SEASON243-03]; assesses=[] -->

*biànhuà* — "to change over time; a change".

> *zhèr de tiānqì biànhuà hěn dà* — The weather here changes a lot.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*biànhuà* is built from *biàn* 'to alter' and *huà* 'to transform', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *pjenH* and *xwaeH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses each with the other.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing things.
