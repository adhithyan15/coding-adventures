# ৌ (-ou) — the sign around the ন of নৌকা

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[BN-LEX-C271-NOUKA-01, BN-SCRIPT-O-MATRA-01, BN-SCRIPT-E-MATRA-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say **নৌকা**, "a boat". Then write **নো** (*no*): **ে** in front of the **ন**, **া** after it.

## Script you'll notice: ৌ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-SCRIPT-C271-AU-KAR-01]; assesses=[BN-SCRIPT-O-MATRA-01, BN-SCRIPT-E-MATRA-01, BN-LEX-C271-NOUKA-01] -->

**ৌ** — *-ou*, the vowel of **নৌকা**. Like **ো**, it is written on both sides of its consonant: one part in front, one after.

Set the print of **নৌ** beside **নো**. In front of the **ন**, both have **ে**. After it, **নো** has an upright stroke like **া**. **নৌ** has an upright stroke too, but its top stands above the headline in a hook, bent over to the left, its tip at the upper left.

## Writing: ৌ — copy the printed shape
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[BN-SCRIPT-C271-AU-KAR-01] -->

Put your pen beside **নৌ** and copy the shape you can see, slowly and large: the **ে** in front, the **ন**, then the stroke after it with its hook above the headline. Check the copy against **নো**: only the hook differs.

> This book does not yet tell you where to start ৌ or which way the pen goes. That is not written down here until it can be written down with a source. Copying the printed shape needs no such source, and it is how the shape becomes yours in the meantime.
