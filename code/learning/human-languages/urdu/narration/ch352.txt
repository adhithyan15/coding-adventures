Urdu, chapter 352: Ask an Expert.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
مضمون (mazmūn) — an article; an essay.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for to compare, then the Urdu for to assess.

You'll want to know: مضمون.
مضمون — mazmūn — "an article; an essay".
میں نے اخبار میں اس کا مضمون پڑھا۔ maiṅ ne akhbār meṅ us kā mazmūn paṛhā. I read her article in the newspaper.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
مضمون (mazmūn) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic maḍmūn, "contents, what is contained".

Lesson 5 of 5.
چھانٹنا (chhānṭnā) — to pick out; to sort (the good from the bad).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for an intellectual, then the Urdu for knowledge.

You'll want to know: چھانٹنا.
چھانٹنا — chhānṭnā — "to pick out; to sort (the good from the bad)".
ماہر نے اچھے نمونے چھانٹے۔ māhir ne achchhe namūne chhānṭe. The expert picked out the good samples.
Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے سیب چھانٹے, us ne seb chhānṭe.

The word, taken apart.
چھانٹنا (chhānṭnā) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin chā̃ṭnā, "to sift, to select", under chaṇṭ, a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 4970).
