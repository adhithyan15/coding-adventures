# عثر (ʿathara) — to come across, to find

## You'll want to know: عثر
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C312-KASHF312-03]; assesses=[] -->

**عثر** — *ʿathara* — "to come across, to find".

> عثر الولد على مفتاح في الحديقة.
>
> *ʿathara al-walad ʿalā miftāḥ fī al-ḥadīqa.*
>
> The boy came across a key in the garden.

Present: يعثر, *yaʿthur*.

It takes على: عثر على.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**عثر** (*ʿathara*) is built on the native Arabic root *ʿ-th-r* (Lane), "to stumble", the root of the taught *taʿaththara*. To stumble upon a thing is to find it.
