Lesson 1 of 6.
a pobreza — the poverty.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for the prejudice, then the Portuguese for the stereotype.

You'll want to know: a pobreza.
a pobreza — "the poverty".
A pobreza diminuiu nos últimos anos. Poverty has gone down in recent years.
Viver na pobreza — to live in poverty.

The word, taken apart.
pobreza is inherited from Old Portuguese pobreza (EtymDB), built on pobre "poor", from Latin pauper "poor" (Lewis & Short), with the suffix -eza.

Lesson 2 of 6.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for the stereotype, then the Portuguese for the poverty.

You'll want to know: a discriminação.
a discriminação — "the discrimination".
A lei proíbe a discriminação no trabalho. The law forbids discrimination at work.
Discriminação contra as mulheres — discrimination against women.

The word, taken apart.
discriminação is a learned borrowing from Late Latin discrīminātiō, a term of rhetoric for "the contrasting of opposite thoughts", on discrīmināre "to divide, to separate", built on discrīmen "that which separates or divides" (Lewis & Short).

You'll want to know: a imigração.
a imigração — "the immigration".
A imigração mudou muito as cidades. Immigration has changed the cities a lot.
Imigrante — immigrant; a emigração — emigration, very common in Portuguese history.

The word, taken apart.
imigração is built on imigrar "to immigrate", a learned borrowing from Latin immigrāre "to remove or go into" (Lewis & Short), from in and migrāre "to move".
