## You'll want to know: निषेध करणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C362-ANDOLAN362-04]; assesses=[] -->

**निषेध करणे** — *niṣedh karṇe* — "to protest; to condemn".

> सगळ्यांनी या निर्णयाचा निषेध केला.
>
> *sagaḷyānnī yā nirṇayācā niṣedh kelā.*
>
> Everyone condemned this decision.

Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने निषेध केला, *tyāne niṣedh kelā*.

What is protested takes -चा: निर्णयाचा निषेध.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**निषेध करणे** (*niṣedh karṇe*) joins the tatsama निषेध, Sanskrit *niṣedha*, "keeping off, prohibition, denial" (Monier-Williams), to करणे, "to do".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
