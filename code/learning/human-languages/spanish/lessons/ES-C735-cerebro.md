## You'll want to know: el cerebro
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C735-HUESO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**el cerebro** — "the brain".

> El cerebro necesita dormir.
>
> *The brain needs sleep.*

*El cerebro del grupo* — the brains of the group.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*cerebro* is usually described as a learned borrowing from Latin *cerebrum* "brain".
