# (reading) — a notice at the dairy

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MW-LEX-C164-NOTICE164-01, MW-LEX-C164-NOTICE164-02, MW-LEX-C164-NOTICE164-03] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say *open*, *closed* and *until*.

[PAUSE 1s] Nobody is talking to anybody here. A notice says what you may do, and
when.

## Reading
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-SKILL-C164-READ-NOTICE-01]; assesses=[MW-SKILL-READ-NOTICE, MW-SKILL-CONNECTED-READING, MW-LEX-C155-FOOD155-01, MW-SCRIPT-C155-DHA-01, MW-LEX-C60-VERBS60-04, MW-LEX-C134-THINGS134-02, MW-LEX-C164-NOTICE164-01, MW-LEX-C164-NOTICE164-02, MW-LEX-C164-NOTICE164-03, MW-GRAMMAR-C156-POSTP-SU-01] -->

> **दूध, दही अर छाछ**
>
> **दुकान रोज दिनूगै ६ सूं १० तांई खुली है।**
> **आथण दुकान ५ सूं ८ तांई खुली है।**
> **रविवार दुकान दिनूगै ६ सूं १२ तांई खुली है।**
> **दोपार अर रात दुकान बंद है।**
> **सोमवार दुकान बंद है।**
> **भाव: दूध ५४।**
> **आज घी कोनी।**
> **दही घणो आछो है!**
> **आभार।**

[PAUSE 3s]
[YOU READ: the notice once, straight through, without translating]

[PAUSE 3s]
[YOU READ: it a second time, and find every line that says *when*]

## You'll want to know
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MW-LEX-C95-TIME95-04, MW-LEX-C90-TIME90-01, MW-LEX-C78-TIME78-02, MW-LEX-C91-TIME91-02, MW-LEX-C92-TIME92-02, MW-SCRIPT-BHAAV-01, MW-SCRIPT-GHEE-01, MW-GRAMMAR-C157-KONI-ISNOT-01, MW-SCRIPT-GHANO-01, MW-SCRIPT-AABHAAR-01, MW-SCRIPT-DIGIT-FIVE-01, MW-SCRIPT-DIGIT-SIX-01, MW-SCRIPT-DIGIT-EIGHT-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Most lines open on a *when*: **रोज**, **आथण**, **रविवार**, **दोपार**,
**सोमवार**. Each stretch of hours runs **सूं** … **तांई**. **भाव** is the price,
written in digits. The line with **!** is an advert, set into the notice to bring
you in.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MW-SKILL-C164-READ-NOTICE-01, MW-LEX-C164-NOTICE164-01, MW-LEX-C164-NOTICE164-02, MW-LEX-C164-NOTICE164-03, MW-LEX-C91-TIME91-02] -->

[PAUSE 2s each]
- [YOU ANSWER: is the shop open on Monday? (No: **सोमवार दुकान बंद है।**)]
- [YOU ANSWER: until when is it open on Sunday morning? (Until twelve: **१२ तांई**.)]
- [YOU ANSWER: is there ghee today? (No: **आज घी कोनी।**)]
