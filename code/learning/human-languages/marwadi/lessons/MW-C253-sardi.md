## You'll want to know: सरदी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C253-SARDI253-01]; assesses=[] -->

**सरदी** — *sardī* — "a cold (the illness)".

> बेटो बीमार है, सरदी है।
>
> *beṭo bīmār hai, sardī hai.*
>
> My son is ill; he has a cold.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सरदी** (*sardī*) is usually traced to Persian *sardī*, "coldness"; in Marwari it names the illness.
