## You'll want to know: vorgestern
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C264-DAMALS-05]; assesses=[] -->

**vorgestern** — "the day before yesterday".

> Vorgestern war ich krank, aber heute geht es mir gut.
>
> *The day before yesterday I was ill, but today I am well.*

The partner of *übermorgen*, the day after tomorrow.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*vorgestern* joins *vor* "before" and *gestern* "yesterday", an inherited Germanic word and a cousin of the *yester-* in English *yesterday*; EtymDB lists no parent for the compound.
