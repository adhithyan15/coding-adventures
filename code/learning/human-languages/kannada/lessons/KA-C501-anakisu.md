# ಅಣಕಿಸು (aṇakisu) — to mimic mockingly; to mock

## You'll want to know: ಅಣಕಿಸು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C501-SAUMYA501-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ಅಣಕಿಸು** — *aṇakisu* — "to mimic mockingly; to mock".

> ಹುಡುಗರು ಮುದುಕನ ನಡಿಗೆಯನ್ನು ಅಣಕಿಸಿದರು.
>
> *huḍugaru mudukana naḍigeyannu aṇakisidaru.*
>
> The boys mocked the old man's walk.

Past (I …): ಅಣಕಿಸಿದೆ, *aṇakiside*.

Mocking by copying someone's voice or walk; ಅಣಕ, mockery.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಅಣಕಿಸು** (*aṇakisu*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 111 lists Kannada *aṇaka*, "mockery, derision", and *aṇakisu*, "to mock, deride".
