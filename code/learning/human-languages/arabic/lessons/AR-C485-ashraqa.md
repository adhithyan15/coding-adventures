## You'll want to know: أشرق
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C485-SAMA485-04]; assesses=[] -->

**أشرق** — *ashraqa* — "to shine; to rise (of the sun)".

> أشرقت الشمس بعد الضباب.
>
> *ashraqat ash-shams baʿda aḍ-ḍabāb.*
>
> The sun came out after the fog.

Present: يشرق, *yushriq*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**أشرق** (*ashraqa*) is built on the native Arabic root *sh-r-q*, "to rise (of the sun)" (Lane). It is Form IV; *sharq*, "east", and *shurūq*, "sunrise", share the root.
