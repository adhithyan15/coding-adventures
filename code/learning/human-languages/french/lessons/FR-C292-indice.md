## You'll want to know: l'indice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C292-INDICES-04]; assesses=[] -->

**l'indice** — "the clue; the index, the rating".

> Le détective a trouvé un indice important.
>
> *The detective found an important clue.*

Also a number on a scale: *l'indice des prix*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*indice* is a learned borrowing from Latin *indicium* "a sign, a proof" (EtymDB), built on *index* "pointer, sign".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.
