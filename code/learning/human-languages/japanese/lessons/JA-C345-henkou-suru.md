## You'll want to know: へんこうする
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C345-HENKOU345-01]; assesses=[] -->

**へんこうする** — *henkō suru* — "to change a plan; to alter (a date)".

> **もし あめが ふったら、よていを へんこうします。** — *moshi ame ga futtara, yotei o henkō shimasu* — "If it rains, we will change the plan."

Polite **へんこうします**, *henkōshimasu*; a する verb. Past (polite): **かいしゃが よていを へんこうしました。**, *kaisha ga yotei o henkō shimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**へんこうする** (*henkō suru*) is built from a Sino-Japanese noun and the native verb *suru*, "to do". The noun, *henkō*, is two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese: *hen*, "change" (Middle Chinese *pjenH*) and *kō*, "renew" (*kaeng*) (KANJIDIC2; JMdict; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
