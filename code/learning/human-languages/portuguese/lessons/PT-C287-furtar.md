## You'll want to know: furtar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C287-ROUBO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**furtar** — "to steal (quietly), to pinch".

> Alguém furtou a minha carteira no metro.
>
> *Someone pinched my wallet on the underground.*

Without force; with force, *roubar*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*furtar* is built on *furto* "theft" (EtymDB), from Latin *fūrtum*, from *fūr* "a thief".
