## You'll want to know: ذمہ دار
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C331-SABAB331-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ذمہ دار** — *zimme-dār* — "responsible".

> سارا اپنے کام کی ذمہ دار ہے۔
>
> *sārā apne kām kī zimme-dār hai.*
>
> Sara is responsible for her own work.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ذمہ دار** (*zimme-dār*) is usually traced to Persian *zimmadār*, "answerable, in charge": *zimma*, a charge one carries, with *-dār*, "holding".
