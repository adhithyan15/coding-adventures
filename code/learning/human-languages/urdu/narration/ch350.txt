Urdu, chapter 350: What the Figures Say.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
اوسط (ausat) — an average.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a discovery, then the Urdu for to search out.

You'll want to know: اوسط.
اوسط — ausat — "an average".
اس سال بارش اوسط سے کم ہوئی۔ is sāl bārish ausat se kam huī. This year it rained less than average.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
اوسط (ausat) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic awsaṭ, "middle, central".

Lesson 2 of 5.
اعداد (adād) — figures (statistics).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for to search out, then the Urdu for an average.

You'll want to know: اعداد.
اعداد — adād — "figures (statistics)".
یہ اعداد کیا بتاتے ہیں؟ yih adād kyā batāte haiṅ? What do these figures tell us?
Masculine, used in the plural.

The word, taken apart.
اعداد (adād) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic aʿdād, "numbers", the plural of ʿadad, "a number".

Lesson 4 of 5.
شرح (sharah) — a rate.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for figures, then the Urdu for an analysis.

You'll want to know: شرح.
شرح — sharah — "a rate".
بینک کی شرح بڑھ گئی۔ baink kī sharah baṛh gaī. The bank's rate has gone up.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
شرح (sharah) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic sharḥ, "an explanation, a laying out"; Urdu also uses it for a rate (of interest, of growth), a sense our sources do not trace.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ابھارنا (ubhārnā) — to bring out; to raise (a question).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for an analysis, then the Urdu for a rate.

You'll want to know: ابھارنا.
ابھارنا — ubhārnā — "to bring out; to raise (a question)".
اس تحقیق نے نئے سوال ابھارے۔ is tahqīq ne naye savāl ubhāre. This research raised new questions.
Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے سوال ابھارا, us ne savāl ubhārā.

The word, taken apart.
ابھارنا (ubhārnā) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin ubhārnā, "to lift", under Sanskrit údbharati, "raises" (CDIAL 2038).
