## You'll want to know: démouler
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C333-PETRIR-05]; assesses=[] -->

**démouler** — "to turn out (of a tin)".

> Elle démoule le gâteau quand il est froid.
>
> *She turns out the cake when it is cold.*

The opposite of putting into *le moule*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*démouler* is built on *dé-* and *moule* "mould", which comes from Latin *modulus* "a small measure" (EtymDB).
