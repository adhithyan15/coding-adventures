## You'll want to know: あずかる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C397-MINSHUKU397-05]; assesses=[] -->

**あずかる** — *azukaru* — "to look after (something for someone)".

> **ともだちの にもつを あずかりました。** — *tomodachi no nimotsu o azukarimashita* — "I looked after my friend's luggage."

Polite **あずかります**, *azukarimasu*; a godan verb. Past (polite): **りょかんが にもつを あずかりました。**, *ryokan ga nimotsu o azukarimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**あずかる** (*azukaru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict). It is the partner of *azukeru*, "to deposit" (JMdict).
