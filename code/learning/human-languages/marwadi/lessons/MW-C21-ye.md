# ये — this, on the page

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-SCRIPT-YE-01]; assesses=[MW-LEX-YE-01, MW-SCRIPT-CHAAY-01] -->

[PAUSE 16s] Say *this*, then write tea.

## You'll want to know
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MW-SCRIPT-YA-01, MW-SCRIPT-E-MATRA-01] -->

> **य** + **े** → **ये** — *ye* — this

In **चाय** the glide **य** closed a word and carried no vowel mark. Here the
same sign opens a word and carries **े** above the headstroke. One sign, two
jobs, and nothing new to learn.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MW-SCRIPT-YE-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: delayed-copy -->

Look at **ये**, cover it, wait five seconds, and write it. Check that **े** sits
on top of the headstroke and not beside the stem.
