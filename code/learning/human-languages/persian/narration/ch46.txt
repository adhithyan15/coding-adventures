Persian, chapter 46: Wall, Bed, Cupboard, Ground, Mirror.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
دیوار (divâr) — a wall.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a chair, then the Persian for a table.

You'll want to know: دیوار.
دیوار — divâr — "a wall".

The word, taken apart.
divâr is a native Persian word, inherited rather than borrowed: it continues Middle Persian dēwār, "wall" (EtymDB).
