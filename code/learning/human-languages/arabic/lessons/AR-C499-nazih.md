## You'll want to know: نزيه
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C499-AMANA499-02]; assesses=[] -->

**نزيه** — *nazīh* — "upright, honest".

> كان جدي رجلا نزيها.
>
> *kāna jaddī rajulan nazīhan.*
>
> My grandfather was an upright man.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**نزيه** (*nazīh*) is built on the native Arabic root *n-z-h*, "to keep far from what is base" (Lane). Lane gives nazīh as one who keeps away from what is unseemly.
