## You'll want to know: اتهام
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C408-KOLAHBARDARI-05]; assesses=[] -->

**اتهام** (*ettehâm*) — "a charge, an accusation".

> او این اتهام را قبول نکرد.
>
> *u in ettehâm râ qabul nakard.*
>
> He did not accept this accusation.

Plural *ettehâmât*. *Be ettehâm-e* … — on a charge of … .

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**اتهام** was borrowed from Arabic *ittihām* "accusation" (EtymDB).
