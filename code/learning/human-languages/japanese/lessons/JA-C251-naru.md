# なる (naru) — to become; to get

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[JA-GRAMMAR-C251-NAI-01, JA-LEX-C53-ADJ53-04, JA-LEX-C25-FAMILY25-05] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new word: say "I will not go", to a friend. (*ikanai*.) Then say "cold" and "a teacher". (*samui*, *sensei*.)

## You'll want to know: なる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C251-NARU-01]; assesses=[JA-LEX-ANI, JA-LEX-C208-FORECAST208-04, JA-LEX-C158-LIGHTS158-05] -->

**なる** — *naru* — "to become", "to get". Polite **なります**, *narimasu*; a godan verb. Past (polite): **あには せんせいに なりました**, *ani wa sensei ni narimashita*.

After a noun it takes **に**:

> **あには せんせいに なりました。**
>
> *ani wa sensei ni narimashita.* — "My elder brother became a teacher."

After an i-adjective it takes the **-く** form you know from **はやく**: **さむい** becomes **さむく**.

> **さむく なりました。**
>
> *samuku narimashita.* — "It got cold."

> **そらが くらく なりました。**
>
> *sora ga kuraku narimashita.* — "The sky got dark."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**なる** (*naru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese. JMdict marks it as usually written in kana, as here, and gives "to become" and "to get" as its first senses (JMdict).
