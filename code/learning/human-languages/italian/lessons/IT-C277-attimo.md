## You'll want to know: l'attimo
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C277-DURATA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**l'attimo** — "the moment, a split second".

> Aspetta un attimo, arrivo!
>
> *Wait a moment, I am coming!*

*Un attimo!* — ‘One moment!’ — is heard everywhere.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*attimo* is usually traced to Latin *atomus*, from Greek *átomos* "that cannot be cut" (Lewis & Short): the smallest slice of time.
