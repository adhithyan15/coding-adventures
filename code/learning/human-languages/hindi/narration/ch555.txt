Hindi, chapter 555: Ups and Downs.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
फलना (phalnā) — to bear fruit; to pay off.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for approximately, then the Hindi for actually.

You'll want to know: फलना.
फलना — phalnā — "to bear fruit; to pay off".
आँगन का पेड़ बहुत फला। Āṁgan kā peṛ bahut phalā. The tree in the courtyard bore a lot of fruit.
Past (it …): वह पेड़ फला, vah peṛ phalā.

The word, taken apart.
फलना (phalnā) is a tadbhava, inherited from Sanskrit phalati, "bears fruit" (CDIAL 9057).

Lesson 2 of 5.
डोलना (ḍolnā) — to sway; to rock; to waver.

Warm-up.

Before the new one: say the Hindi for actually, then the Hindi for to bear fruit.

You'll want to know: डोलना.
डोलना — ḍolnā — "to sway; to rock; to waver".
तेज़ हवा में नाव डोल रही थी। Tez havā meṁ nāv ḍol rahī thī. The boat was rocking in the strong wind.
Past (it …): वह झूला डोला, vah jhūlā ḍolā.

The word, taken apart.
डोलना (ḍolnā) is a tadbhava, inherited from Sanskrit dōlāyatē, "moves to and fro" (CDIAL 6585). CDIAL gives the Hindi verb as "to oscillate".

Lesson 3 of 5.
टिकाना (ṭikānā) — to rest (something) against; to steady.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for to bear fruit, then the Hindi for to sway.

You'll want to know: टिकाना.
टिकाना — ṭikānā — "to rest (something) against; to steady".
उसने सीढ़ी दीवार से टिका दी। Usne sīṛhī dīvār se ṭikā dī. He leaned the ladder against the wall.
Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने साइकिल दीवार से टिकाई, usne sāikil dīvār se ṭikāī.

The word, taken apart.
टिकाना (ṭikānā) is the causative of ṭiknā, "to stop, remain", which CDIAL files under ṭakk, "remain, stop", a reconstructed form (CDIAL 5420).

Lesson 4 of 5.
गिरावट (girāvaṭ) — a fall; a drop (in prices, in level).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for to sway, then the Hindi for to rest (something) against.

You'll want to know: गिरावट.
गिरावट — girāvaṭ — "a fall; a drop (in prices, in level)".
सब्ज़ियों के दाम में गिरावट आई है। Sabziyoṁ ke dām meṁ girāvaṭ āī hai. Vegetable prices have dropped.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
गिरावट (girāvaṭ) is the noun of girnā, "to fall", which comes from girati, "drips, falls", a form CDIAL reconstructs (CDIAL 4159), with the ending -āvaṭ that makes nouns of verbs.

Lesson 5 of 5.
पतन (patan) — a downfall; a decline.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for to rest (something) against, then the Hindi for a fall.

You'll want to know: पतन.
पतन — patan — "a downfall; a decline".
बुरी आदतें इंसान के पतन का कारण बनती हैं। Burī ādteṁ insān ke patan kā kāraṇ bantī haiṁ. Bad habits bring about a person's downfall.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
पतन (patan) is a tatsama, taken whole from Sanskrit patana, "fall, decline, ruin" (Monier-Williams).
