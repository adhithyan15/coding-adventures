## You'll want to know: Běijīng
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C36-WHERE36-04]; assesses=[] -->

*Běijīng* — "Beijing".

> *wǒmen lái Běijīng le* — We've come to Beijing.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Běijīng* is built from běi 'north' and jīng 'capital', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *pok* and *kjaeng* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription): the northern capital.
