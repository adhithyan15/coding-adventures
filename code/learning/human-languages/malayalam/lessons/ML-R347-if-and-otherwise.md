# Review — if, and otherwise

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ML-GRAMMAR-C347-REAL-CONDITION-01, ML-GRAMMAR-C347-ENKIL-VERB-01, ML-SCRIPT-C346-VOCALIC-R-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Write **ഋതു** once from memory. Then say "if it rains" and "if you are coming". (*maḻa peytāl*, *nī varunnuṇṭeṅkil*.)

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ML-GRAMMAR-C347-ENKIL-VERB-01, ML-GRAMMAR-C347-ILLENKIL-01, ML-GRAMMAR-C347-REAL-CONDITION-01, ML-GRAMMAR-C347-NEGATIVE-CONDITION-01, ML-GRAMMAR-C342-ANAM-01, ML-GRAMMAR-C346-SPAN-AAYI-01, ML-GRAMMAR-C346-SINCE-MUTAL-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s each]
- [YOU SAY: "If it rains, I will not come. If I get time tomorrow, I will call."]
- [YOU SAY: "If you are coming, I will come too."]
- [YOU SAY: "If he is at home, I will call. If there is time, let us go to a film."]
- [YOU SAY: "Come quickly, otherwise the bus will go."]
- [YOU SAY: "If you do not come, I will call."]
- [YOU SAY: "It has rained since yesterday. If it rains tomorrow, I will not come." — *innale mutal maḻayāṇŭ. nāḷe maḻa peytāl ñān varilla.*]
- [YOU SAY: "If you want to go to Kerala, you must learn Malayalam." — *ninakkŭ kēraḷattil pōkaṇameṅkil nī malayāḷaṁ paṭhikkaṇaṁ.*]

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ML-GRAMMAR-C347-ENKIL-VERB-01, ML-GRAMMAR-C347-ILLENKIL-01, ML-GRAMMAR-C347-REAL-CONDITION-01, ML-GRAMMAR-C347-NEGATIVE-CONDITION-01] -->

*uṇṭeṅkil*.) "Otherwise"? (**ഇല്ലെങ്കിൽ**.)
