## You'll want to know: સંગ્રહ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C524-SANGRAH524-01]; assesses=[] -->

**સંગ્રહ** — *sangrah* — "a collection".

> અરુણ પાસે જૂના સિક્કાઓનો મોટો સંગ્રહ છે.
>
> *Aruṇ pāse jūnā sikkāono moṭo sangrah che.*
>
> Arun has a big collection of old coins.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**સંગ્રહ** (*sangrah*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *saṃgraha*, "holding together, gathering" (Monier-Williams).
