## You'll want to know: zhāi
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C225-FARM225-04]; assesses=[] -->

*zhāi* — "to pick fruit; to pick flowers".

> *wǒmen qù nóngcūn zhāi píngguǒ* — We're going to the countryside to pick apples.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*zhāi* is inherited from Middle Chinese *treak* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'to take with the hand'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what I do.
