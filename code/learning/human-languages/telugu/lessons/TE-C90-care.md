# జాగ్రత్త (jāgratta) — care, caution

## You'll want to know: జాగ్రత్త
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C90-LEAVE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**జాగ్రత్త** (*jāgratta*) — "care, caution".

**జాగ్రత్తగా వెళ్ళండి** — "go carefully", what a host says at the door, where English says
"take care". The **-గా** is the ending that makes a manner.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

The kindest thing to say to someone leaving.
