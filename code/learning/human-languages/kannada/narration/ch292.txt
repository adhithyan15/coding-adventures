Kannada, chapter 292: Deserted, Crisp, Tidy, Messy, Convenient.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ನಿರ್ಜನ (nirjana) — deserted.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for wonderful, then the Kannada for interesting.

You'll want to know: ನಿರ್ಜನ.
ನಿರ್ಜನ — nirjana — "deserted".

The word, taken apart.
ನಿರ್ಜನ (nirjana) is Sanskrit nirjana, "unpeopled", taken over whole as a learned loan, a tatsama (CDIAL 7322).

Lesson 3 of 5.
ಅಚ್ಚುಕಟ್ಟಾದ (accukaṭṭāda) — tidy, well-organised.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for deserted, then the Kannada for crisp.

You'll want to know: ಅಚ್ಚುಕಟ್ಟಾದ.
ಅಚ್ಚುಕಟ್ಟಾದ — accukaṭṭāda — "tidy, well-organised".

The word, taken apart.
ಅಚ್ಚುಕಟ್ಟಾದ (accukaṭṭāda) is accu, "a mould, a stamp" (DEDR 47), kaṭṭu, "to bind, build" (DEDR 1147), and āda, "that has become": "made to a mould". DEDR 47 compares, with a query, Sanskrit sañcaka, "a mould".
