## You'll want to know: لو دادن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C321-QASAM-04]; assesses=[] -->

**لو دادن** (*low dâdan*) — "to give away, to betray (a secret or a person)".

> دوستش او را لو داد.
>
> *Dustash u râ low dâd.*
>
> His friend gave him away.

Colloquial. Present *low midahad*. Compare *low raftan* "to be found out".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*dâdan* "to give" is inherited Persian. The origin of *low* "revealed, exposed" is not settled; EtymDB lists no entry.

## What you've built
