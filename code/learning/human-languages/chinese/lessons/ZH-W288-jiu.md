## Script — a dot, a bar, 口 and 小, then a four-stroke right side
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-SCRIPT-JIU-01]; assesses=[] -->

> 就

**Four pieces: three you write, and one new one on the right.**

**A dot and a horizontal**, as 文 opens.

**口, three strokes**, under them.

**小, three strokes**, under 口: the hooked vertical, then the left dot, then the right dot.

**The right side, four strokes**: a horizontal; a long left-falling stroke; a vertical that bends right along the foot and hooks up; and last the dot at the upper right.

2 + 3 + 3 + 4 = twelve.
