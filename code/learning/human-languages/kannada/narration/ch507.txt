Kannada, chapter 507: Small Body Sounds.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ತೇಗು (tēgu) — to belch; to burp.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to stretch, then the Kannada for an eyelid.

You'll want to know: ತೇಗು.
ತೇಗು — tēgu — "to belch; to burp".
ಊಟದ ನಂತರ ಮಗು ತೇಗಿತು. ūṭada nantara magu tēgitu. After the meal the baby burped.
Past (I …): ತೇಗಿದೆ, tēgide.
ತೇಗು (tēgu) is also the burp itself.

The word, taken apart.
ತೇಗು (tēgu) is native Dravidian: DEDR 3451 lists Kannada tēgu, tēku, "to belch; a belch", beside Tamil tēkku.

ಮಿಟುಕಿಸು — miṭukisu — "to blink; to wink".
ಬಿಸಿಲಿನಲ್ಲಿ ಅವನು ಕಣ್ಣು ಮಿಟುಕಿಸಿದನು. bisilinalli avanu kaṇṇu miṭukisidanu. In the sunshine he blinked.
Past (I …): ಮಿಟುಕಿಸಿದೆ, miṭukiside.
ಕಣ್ಣು ಮಿಟುಕಿಸು (miṭukisu), to blink; to wink at someone, add -ಗೆ.

The word, taken apart.
ಮಿಟುಕಿಸು (miṭukisu) is native Dravidian: DEDR 4845 lists Kannada miṭakisu, "to open and shut the eyes rapidly, blink, wink".

Lesson 3 of 5.
ಏದುಸಿರು ಬಿಡು (ēdusiru biḍu) — to pant; to gasp.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to belch, then the Kannada for to blink.

You'll want to know: ಏದುಸಿರು ಬಿಡು.
ಏದುಸಿರು ಬಿಡು — ēdusiru biḍu — "to pant; to gasp".
ಮೆಟ್ಟಿಲು ಹತ್ತಿ ಅಜ್ಜಿ ಏದುಸಿರು ಬಿಟ್ಟರು. meṭṭilu hatti ajji ēdusiru biṭṭaru. Grandmother panted after climbing the stairs.
Past (I …): ಏದುಸಿರು ಬಿಟ್ಟೆ, ēdusiru biṭṭe.
ಏದುಸಿರು is panting breath, built on ಉಸಿರು, breath; ಬಿಡು, to let out.

The word, taken apart.
ಏದುಸಿರು ಬಿಡು (ēdusiru biḍu) is a native Kannada phrase: usiru, "breath" (DEDR 645), and biḍu, "to let go" (DEDR 5393). The origin of the first part, ēd-, is not settled.
