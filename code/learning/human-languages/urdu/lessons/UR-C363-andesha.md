## You'll want to know: اندیشہ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C363-ANDESHA363-01]; assesses=[] -->

**اندیشہ** — *andeshā* — "an apprehension; a misgiving".

> اگر بارش ہوئی تو سیلاب کا اندیشہ ہے۔
>
> *agar bārish huī to sailāb kā andeshā hai.*
>
> If it rains, there is a fear of floods.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**اندیشہ** (*andeshā*) is usually traced to Persian *andēsha*, "thought, anxiety": a thought that worries.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
