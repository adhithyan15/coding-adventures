Persian, chapter 497: Bones and Joints.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
کتف (ketf) — a shoulder blade; shoulder.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for an eyelid, then the Persian for the gum.

You'll want to know: کتف.
کتف (ketf) — "a shoulder blade; shoulder".
او کیف سنگینی روی کتفش داشت. u kif-e sangini ru-ye ketfash dâsht. She had a heavy bag on her shoulder.
shâne is the shoulder and ketf the shoulder blade, but in speech they overlap.

The word, taken apart.
ketf comes from Arabic kitf, 'shoulder' (EtymDB).

Lesson 2 of 5.
مفصل (mafsal) — a joint (of the body).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for the gum, then the Persian for a shoulder blade.

You'll want to know: مفصل.
مفصل (mafsal) — "a joint (of the body)".
مادربزرگ در زمستان درد مفصل دارد. mâdarbozorg dar zemestân dard-e mafsal dârad. Grandmother has joint pain in winter.
Plural mafâsel (Arabic). The same letters read mofassal mean 'detailed'.

The word, taken apart.
mafsal is a loan, usually traced to Arabic mafṣil, 'joint'. EtymDB's entry under this spelling is the other reading, mufaṣṣal, 'divided' (EtymDB), which Persian keeps as mofassal, 'detailed'.

Lesson 3 of 5.
ناف (nâf) — the navel.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a shoulder blade, then the Persian for a joint.

You'll want to know: ناف.
ناف (nâf) — "the navel".
پرستار بند ناف بچه را بست. parastâr band-e nâf-e bachche râ bast. The nurse tied the baby's umbilical cord.
band-e nâf — the umbilical cord; nâf-e shahr — the very heart of a town.

The word, taken apart.
nâf is inherited: EtymDB traces it to a Proto-Indo-European word for 'navel', and lists Sanskrit nābhi and Avestan as cousins (EtymDB).

Lesson 4 of 5.
ستون فقرات (sotun-e faqârât) — the spine, backbone.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a joint, then the Persian for the navel.

You'll want to know: ستون فقرات.
ستون فقرات (sotun-e faqârât) — "the spine, backbone".
تصادف به ستون فقرات او آسیب زد. tasâdof be sotun-e faqârât-e u âsib zad. The accident injured his spine.
sotun 'pillar' + faqârât 'vertebrae' (an Arabic plural): the pillar of vertebrae.

The word, taken apart.
sotun-e faqârât is built in Persian on inherited sotun, 'pillar', usually traced to Middle Persian stūn, and faqârât, 'vertebrae', a loan usually traced to the Arabic plural faqārāt.
