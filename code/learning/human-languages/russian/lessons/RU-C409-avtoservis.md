## You'll want to know: автосервис
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C409-POLOMKA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**автосервис** (*avtosérvis*) — "a car repair shop, a garage".

> Мы оставили машину в автосервисе на два дня.
>
> *My ostávili mashínu v avtosérvise na dva dnya.*
>
> We left the car at the garage for two days.

Masculine. In speech, often *сервис*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*автосервис* is built from *авто-*, short for *автомобиль* 'a car', and *сервис* 'service', which was borrowed from English *service* (EtymDB). EtymDB traces the English word through Old French to Latin *servitium*.
