## You'll want to know: कासते
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C478-VEDANA478-02]; assesses=[] -->

**कासते** — *kāsate* — "coughs".

> शीतेन बालः रात्रौ कासते।
>
> *śītena bālaḥ rātrau kāsate.*
>
> In the cold the child coughs at night.

Past (he …): सः रात्रौ कासितवान्, *saḥ rātrau kāsitavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**कासते** (*kāsate*) comes from the root √kās, "to cough", the root of **कासः** (MW).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
