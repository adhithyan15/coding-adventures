## You'll want to know: шествие
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C316-KARNAVAL-04]; assesses=[] -->

**шествие** (*shéstviye*) — "a procession".

> Шествие шло по центру города два часа.
>
> *Shéstviye shlo po tséntru góroda dva chasá.*
>
> The procession moved through the town centre for two hours.

Neuter.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*шествие* is a Church Slavonic word, *šĭstvie* "a going, a procession" (EtymDB), built on *шествовать* "to walk solemnly".
