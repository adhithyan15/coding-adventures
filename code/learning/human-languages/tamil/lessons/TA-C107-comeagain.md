# மீண்டும் வாருங்கள் (mīṇṭum vāruṅkaḷ) — please come again

## Warm-up

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Tamil for see you later, then the Tamil for a good journey.

## You'll want to know: மீண்டும் வாருங்கள்
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C107-LEAVE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**மீண்டும் வாருங்கள்** (*mīṇṭum vāruṅkaḷ*) — "please come again".

**மீண்டும்** is "again", with the welcome **வாருங்கள்**.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

Take leave, shall we go, see you, a good journey, come again.
