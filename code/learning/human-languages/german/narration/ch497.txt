German, chapter 497: Frech und trotzig.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
trotzig — defiant.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for pram, then the German for dummy.

You'll want to know: trotzig.
trotzig — "defiant".
Der Kleine ist müde und deshalb trotzig. The little one is tired and so he's being defiant.
Die Trotzphase — the terrible twos.

The word, taken apart.
trotzig is built on Trotz "defiance", from Middle High German traz (EtymDB), with -ig.

Lesson 2 of 6.
schimpfen — to scold; to grumble.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for dummy, then the German for defiant.

You'll want to know: schimpfen.
schimpfen — "to scold; to grumble".
Die Mutter schimpft, weil die Kinder so laut sind. The mother is scolding because the children are so noisy.
Mit jemandem schimpfen — to tell someone off. Über etwas schimpfen — to grumble about something.

The word, taken apart.
schimpfen goes back to Middle High German schimpfen "to joke, to mock"; the meaning later shifted to scolding. EtymDB lists the German word without a source.

Lesson 5 of 6.

You'll want to know: das Taschengeld.
das Taschengeld — "pocket money".
Von seinem Taschengeld kauft er Comics. He buys comics with his pocket money.
No plural.

The word, taken apart.
Taschengeld is built on Tasche "pocket", inherited from Old High German tasca, and Geld "money", from Old High German gelt "payment" (EtymDB).
