## You'll want to know: جمانا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C509-CHHACHH509-03]; assesses=[] -->

**جمانا** — *jamānā* — "to set (curd)".

> امی رات کو دہی جماتی ہیں۔
>
> *ammī rāt ko dahī jamātī haiṅ.*
>
> Mum sets the yoghurt at night.

Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے دہی جمایا, *us ne dahī jamāyā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**جمانا** (*jamānā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists Hindi *jamānā* and *jāman*, "a piece of curd for curdling milk", under Sanskrit *yamyatē*, "is held" (CDIAL 10428).
