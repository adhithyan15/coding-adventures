# ਚਿਕਨਾ (chiknā) — slick; greasy

## You'll want to know: ਚਿਕਨਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C296-SLIP296-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ਚਿਕਨਾ** — *chiknā* — "slick; greasy".

> ਮੀਂਹ ਤੋਂ ਬਾਅਦ ਫ਼ਰਸ਼ ਚਿਕਨਾ ਹੈ।
>
> *mīṁh ton bāad farsh chiknā hai.*
>
> After the rain the floor is slick.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਚਿਕਨਾ** (*chiknā*) is inherited from Sanskrit *cikkaṇa*, "slippery, unctuous"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *ciknā*, "sticky, oily, smooth", under it (CDIAL 4782).
