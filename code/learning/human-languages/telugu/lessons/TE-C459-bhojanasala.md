## You'll want to know: భోజనశాల
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C459-PHALAHARAM459-02]; assesses=[] -->

**భోజనశాల** — *bhōjanaśāla* — "a dining hall".

> హోటలు భోజనశాల కింది అంతస్తులో ఉంది.
>
> *hōṭalu bhōjanaśāla kindi antastulō undi.*
>
> The hotel dining hall is on the ground floor.

భోజనం, a meal, + శాల, a hall.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**భోజనశాల** (*bhōjanaśāla*) is a compound of two words borrowed from Sanskrit: *bhojana*, "eating, a meal", and *śālā*, "a shed, a hall" (CDIAL 12414).
