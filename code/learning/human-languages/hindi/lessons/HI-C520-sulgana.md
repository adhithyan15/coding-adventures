## You'll want to know: सुलगाना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C520-AATA520-01]; assesses=[] -->

**सुलगाना** — *sulgānā* — "to kindle; to light (a fire)".

> दादी ने सुबह चूल्हा सुलगाया।
>
> *Dādī ne subah cūlhā sulgāyā.*
>
> Grandmother lit the stove in the morning.

Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने आग सुलगाई, *usne āg sulgāī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सुलगाना** (*sulgānā*) is the causative of *sulagnā*, "to catch fire", which CDIAL files under Sanskrit *saṁlagna*, "attached, joined" (CDIAL 12999).
