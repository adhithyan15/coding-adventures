## You'll want to know: चुप

**चुप** — *chup* — "silent".

> चुप रैवो।
>
> *chup raivo.*
>

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**चुप** (*chup*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*cuppa*, "silent"; CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry, but lists Hindi *cup*, "silent" (CDIAL 4864).
