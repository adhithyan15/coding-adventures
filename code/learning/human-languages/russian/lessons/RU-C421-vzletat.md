## You'll want to know: взлетать
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C421-VZLYOT-01]; assesses=[] -->

**взлетать** (*vzletát*) — "to take off".

> Самолёт медленно взлетает над морем.
>
> *Samolyót médlenno vzletáyet nad mórem.*
>
> The plane slowly takes off over the sea.

Imperfective; perfective *взлететь* (*взлечу, взлетишь*). *Вз-* means up.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*взлетать* is built from *вз-* 'up' and *летать* 'to fly'. EtymDB lists the noun *взлёт* from Proto-Slavic *vŭzletŭ*.
