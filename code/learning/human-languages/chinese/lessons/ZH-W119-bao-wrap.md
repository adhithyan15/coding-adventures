## Script — a wrapping stroke, and three strokes inside
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-SCRIPT-BAO-WRAP-01]; assesses=[] -->

> 包

**The wrapping piece, two strokes**: a short left-falling stroke, then a stroke across that turns down the right side and hooks.

**The inside, three strokes**: a stroke across that turns down, a short horizontal under it, and a vertical that bends right along the bottom and hooks up, out past the wrapping, as the last stroke of 也 does.

2 + 3 = five.
