## You'll want to know: সমাধি
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C320-SMRITI320-03]; assesses=[] -->

**সমাধি** — *sômādhi* — "a tomb".

> পাহাড়ের ওপরে এক বীরের সমাধি আছে।
>
> *pāhāṛer opôre ek bīrer sômādhi āchhe.*
>
> On top of the hill is the tomb of a hero.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**সমাধি** (*sômādhi*) is Sanskrit *samādhi*, 'a putting together' and the deep stillness of meditation (Monier-Williams), which also records it as the shrine or tomb of a saint; Bengali uses it for any tomb.
