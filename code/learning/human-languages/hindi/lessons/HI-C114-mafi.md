## You'll want to know: माफ़ी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C114-SORRY-01]; assesses=[] -->

**माफ़ी** (*māfī*) — "an apology, pardon", the noun.

**माफ़ी चाहता हूँ** — "I beg your pardon" (a woman says **चाहती**). It shares its root with
**माफ़ कीजिए**.

## What you've built
