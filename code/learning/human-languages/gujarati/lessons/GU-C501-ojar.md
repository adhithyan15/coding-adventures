## You'll want to know: ઓજાર
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C501-KARIGAR501-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ઓજાર** — *ojār* — "a tool of a trade".

> સુથાર પોતાનાં ઓજારો એક પેટીમાં રાખે છે.
>
> *suthār potānā̃ ojāro ek peṭīmā̃ rākhe che.*
>
> The carpenter keeps his tools in a box.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ઓજાર** (*ojār*) is usually traced to Arabic *awzār*, a plural meaning "loads" or "gear", which reached Gujarati through Persian. The plural is **ઓજારો**.
