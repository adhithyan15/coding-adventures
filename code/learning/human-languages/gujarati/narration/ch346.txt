Gujarati, chapter 346: For Example.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
દાખલો (dākhlo) — an example.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to list, then the Gujarati for to change one's stand.

You'll want to know: દાખલો.
દાખલો — dākhlo — "an example".
હું તમને એક દાખલો આપીશ. hũ tamne ek dākhlo āpīś. Let me give you an example.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
દાખલો (dākhlo) is usually traced to Persian dākhila, "an entry, an admission", a word of Arabic origin; "an example" is a sense Gujarati gave it.

What you've built.
One more word for giving reasons and opinions.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to change one's stand, then the Gujarati for an example.

You'll want to know: ઉદાહરણ.
ઉદાહરણ — udāharaṇ — "an instance".
ગુરુએ પાઠ સમજાવવા માટે એક ઉદાહરણ આપ્યું. gurue pāṭh samjāvvā māṭe ek udāharaṇ āpyũ. The teacher gave an instance to explain the lesson.
A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
ઉદાહરણ (udāharaṇ) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit udāharaṇa, "relating, declaring" (Monier-Williams), and so a case cited to make a point.

Lesson 5 of 6.
સરખાવવું (sarkhāvvũ) — to compare.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for reasoning, then the Gujarati for different.

You'll want to know: સરખાવવું.
સરખાવવું — sarkhāvvũ — "to compare".
બે દુકાનોના ભાવ સરખાવીને પછી ખરીદો. be dukānonā bhāv sarkhāvīne pachī kharīdo. Compare the prices at two shops and then buy.
Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે બંને ભાવ સરખાવ્યા, teṇe banne bhāv sarkhāvyā.

The word, taken apart.
સરખાવવું (sarkhāvvũ) is built on sarkhũ, "alike", which CDIAL traces to Sanskrit sadṛkṣa, "like" (CDIAL 13119), with the causative ending -āvvũ: to set things side by side.
