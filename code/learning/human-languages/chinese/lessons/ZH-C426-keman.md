## You'll want to know: kèmǎn
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C426-ROOM426-02]; assesses=[] -->

*kèmǎn* — "fully booked; with no vacancies".

> *duìbuqǐ jīntiān kèmǎn le* — Sorry, we're full tonight.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*kèmǎn* is built from 客 'a guest' and *mǎn* 'full', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *khaek* and *manX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'full, filled'.
