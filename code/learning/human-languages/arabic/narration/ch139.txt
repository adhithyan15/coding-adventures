Arabic, chapter 139: Beard, Moustache, Chest, Waist, Elbow.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
لحية (liḥya) — a beard.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a forehead, then the Arabic for an eyebrow.

You'll want to know: لحية.
لحية — liḥya — "a beard".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: liḥya]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: liḥya, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say liḥya]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Arabic for a forehead, then the Arabic for an eyebrow, then say liḥya again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does لحية (liḥya) mean? ("A beard".) Say it once more.

Lesson 6 of 7.

[your turn — say: figs, a watermelon, apricots, soup, a cake]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: grass, a seed, a root, a branch, an island]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: a coast, a waterfall, a cave, a valley, a hill]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: light, shade, darkness, a cloud, a rabbit]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]

(غصن, ghuṣn.) An island? (جزيرة, jazīra.) A coast? (ساحل, sāḥil.) A waterfall? (شلال, shallāl.) A cave? (كهف, kahf.) A valley? (وادي, wādī.) A hill? (تل, tall.) A desert? (صحراء, ṣaḥrāʾ.) A lake? (بحيرة, buḥayra.) Lightning? (برق, barq.) Thunder? (رعد, raʿd.) A storm? (عاصفة, ʿāṣifa.) Light? (ضوء, ḍawʾ.) Shade? (ظل, ẓill.) Darkness? (ظلام, ẓalām.) A cloud? (غيمة, ghayma.) A rabbit? (أرنب, arnab.)
