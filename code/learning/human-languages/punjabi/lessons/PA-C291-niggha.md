## You'll want to know: ਨਿੱਘਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C291-WARM291-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ਨਿੱਘਾ** — *nigghā* — "pleasantly warm; cosy".

> ਇਹ ਕੰਬਲ ਬਹੁਤ ਨਿੱਘਾ ਹੈ।
>
> *eh kambal bahut nigghā hai.*
>
> This blanket is very warm.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਨਿੱਘਾ** (*nigghā*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*nidāghya*, "heat"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *nigghā*, "moderately warm", under it (CDIAL 7195).
