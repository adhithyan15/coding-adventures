## You'll want to know: पहेली
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C278-KAHANI278-02]; assesses=[] -->

**पहेली** — *pahelī* — "a riddle".

> मास्टर एक पहेली पूछै है।
>
> *māsṭar ek pahelī pūchhai hai.*
>
> The teacher asks a riddle.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पहेली** (*pahelī*) is inherited from Sanskrit *prahelikā*, "a riddle"; CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry, but lists Hindi *pahelī*, "riddle" (CDIAL 8911).
