## You'll want to know: cǎihóng
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C238-SKY238-05]; assesses=[] -->

*cǎihóng* — "rainbow".

> *yǔ tíng le yǒu cǎihóng* — The rain's stopped, and there's a rainbow.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*cǎihóng* is built from *cǎi* 'colour' and *hóng* 'rainbow'. The first is inherited from Middle Chinese *tshojX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'brilliance'; the dictionary glosses the second, *huwng* there, with an old two-character word for the rainbow.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing things.
