## You'll want to know: dēserō, dēserere
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C537-IURG-05]; assesses=[] -->

**dēserō, dēserere** — "to abandon, desert".

> Amīcus vērus amīcum in perīculō nōn dēserit.
>
> *A true friend does not abandon his friend in danger.*

*dēserō, dēserere, dēseruī, dēsertum*: *dē-* 'away' + *serō* 'to join' — to break a tie. English *desert*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**dēserō** is built on *dē-* "away" and *serō* "to join, link" (Lewis & Short): to break off a tie. English *desert* comes from its participle *dēsertus*.
