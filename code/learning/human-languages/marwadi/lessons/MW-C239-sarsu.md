# सरसूं (sarsū̃) — mustard (the plant and its seed)

## You'll want to know: सरसूं
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C239-CROP239-02]; assesses=[] -->

**सरसूं** — *sarsū̃* — "mustard (the plant and its seed)".

> सरसूं पीळी है।
>
> *sarsū̃ pīḷī hai.*
>
> The mustard is yellow.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सरसूं** (*sarsū̃*) is inherited from Sanskrit *sarṣapa*, "mustard seed"; CDIAL lists Hindi *sarsõ*, the mustard plant. CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry (CDIAL 13281).
