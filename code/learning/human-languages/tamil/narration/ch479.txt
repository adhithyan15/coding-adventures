Tamil, chapter 479: Muscles and Nerves.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
தசை (tacai) — a muscle; flesh.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for a kidney, then the Tamil for the intestines.

You'll want to know: தசை.
தசை — tacai — "a muscle; flesh".
உடற்பயிற்சி செய்தால் தசைகள் உறுதியாகும். uṭaṟpayiṟci ceytāl tacaikaḷ uṟutiyākum. If you exercise, your muscles get firm.
Plural: தசைகள், tacaikaḷ.
Plural தசைகள்.

The word, taken apart.
தசை (tacai) is native Dravidian (DEDR 3016), for flesh, muscle and the pulp of fruit.

Lesson 3 of 5.

You'll want to know: நெஞ்சு.
நெஞ்சு — neñcu — "the bosom; the chest; the heart".
படிக்கட்டு ஏறியதும் எனக்கு நெஞ்சு வலித்தது. paṭikkaṭṭu ēṟiyatum eṉakku neñcu valittatu. After climbing the stairs I had a pain in my chest.
Also the heart as the seat of feeling: நெஞ்சு (neñcu) நிறைந்த நன்றி, heartfelt thanks.

The word, taken apart.
நெஞ்சு (neñcu) is native Dravidian (DEDR 3736), for the chest and the heart as the seat of courage and feeling.

Lesson 5 of 5.
துடி (tuṭi) — to throb; to writhe.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for the bosom, then the Tamil for a rib.

You'll want to know: துடி.
துடி — tuṭi — "to throb; to writhe".
வலியில் அவன் துடித்தான். valiyil avaṉ tuṭittāṉ. He writhed in pain.
Past (I …): துடித்தேன், tuṭittēṉ.
Also of the heartbeat (இதயம் துடிக்கிறது) and of eagerness: போகத் துடித்தான், he was itching to go.

The word, taken apart.
துடி (tuṭi) is native Dravidian (DEDR 3294). DEDR keeps துடி "lip" and துடி "a small drum" apart as other entries.
