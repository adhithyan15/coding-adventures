## You'll want to know: granizar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C699-TORMENTA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**granizar** — "to hail".

> Ayer granizó en la sierra.
>
> *It hailed in the mountains yesterday.*

Used in the third person, like *llover*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*granizar* is built on *granizo* "hail", from *grano* "grain", inherited from Latin *grānum*.
