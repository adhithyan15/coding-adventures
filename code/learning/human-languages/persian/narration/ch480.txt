Persian, chapter 480: Pride and Modesty.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
غرور (ghorur) — pride; vanity.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for an invoice, then the Persian for a cash machine.

You'll want to know: غرور.
غرور (ghorur) — "pride; vanity".
غرور او دوستانش را ناراحت میکند. ghorur-e u dustânash râ nârâhat mikonad. His pride upsets his friends.
A noun, usually with a bad sense: too much pride. ghorur dâshtan — to be vain.

The word, taken apart.
ghorur was borrowed from Arabic ġurūr, which EtymDB glosses 'pride' (EtymDB).

Lesson 2 of 5.
مغرور (maghrur) — arrogant; conceited.

برادرش خیلی مغرور است و به کسی سلام نمیکند. barâdarash kheyli maghrur ast va be kasi salâm nemikonad. Her brother is very arrogant and doesn't greet anyone.
An adjective. maghrur shodan — to become full of oneself.

maghrur comes from Arabic maġrūr (EtymDB). It is usually linked with Arabic ġurūr, 'pride', the source of ghorur.

Lesson 3 of 5.
فروتن (forutan) — humble, modest.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for pride, then the Persian for arrogant.

You'll want to know: فروتن.
فروتن (forutan) — "humble, modest".
او با اینکه مشهور است، خیلی فروتن است. u bâ inke mashhur ast, kheyli forutan ast. Although he is famous, he is very humble.
Praise, not blame. The noun is forutani 'humility'.

The word, taken apart.
forutan is a native Persian compound, usually explained as foru, 'down', and tan, 'body': someone who bows low. EtymDB has no entry for it, so the analysis is hedged; it is not a loan.

Lesson 4 of 5.
افتخار کردن (eftekhâr kardan) — to be proud (of).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for arrogant, then the Persian for humble.

You'll want to know: افتخار کردن.
افتخار کردن (eftekhâr kardan) — "to be proud (of)".
مادرم به موفقیت من افتخار میکند. mâdaram be movaffaqiyat-e man eftekhâr mikonad. My mother is proud of my success.
eftekhâr 'pride, honour' + kardan 'to do'; present stem kon-. What you are proud of takes be: be chizi eftekhâr kardan. Unlike ghorur, a good kind of pride.

The word, taken apart.
eftekhâr was borrowed from Arabic iftiḫār; EtymDB links it with Arabic faḫr, 'honour, pride' (EtymDB). kardan, 'to do', is inherited Persian.

Lesson 5 of 5.
خودنمایی کردن (khodnamâyi kardan) — to show off.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for humble, then the Persian for to be proud.

You'll want to know: خودنمایی کردن.
خودنمایی کردن (khodnamâyi kardan) — "to show off".
او جلوی دوستانش خودنمایی میکند. u jelo-ye dustânash khodnamâyi mikonad. He shows off in front of his friends.
khod 'self' + namâyi 'showing' (stem namâ-, from namudan 'to show') + kardan. Present: khodnamâyi mikonad.

The word, taken apart.
khodnamâyi is built on native Persian parts: khod, 'self', and namâ-, the present stem of namudan, 'to show', with the noun ending -i: showing oneself. kardan, 'to do', turns it into a verb; none of it is borrowed.
