## You'll want to know: पुरावा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C343-MUDDA343-05]; assesses=[] -->

**पुरावा** — *purāvā* — "proof; evidence".

> तुमच्याकडे याचा काही पुरावा आहे का?
>
> *tumacyākaḍe yācā kāhī purāvā āhe kā?*
>
> Do you have any proof of this?

Masculine. Plural: पुरावे, *purāve*.

X चा पुरावा, proof of X; पुरावा देणे, to give evidence.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पुरावा** (*purāvā*) is a Marathi noun whose origin is not settled.
