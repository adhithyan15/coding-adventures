Portuguese, chapter 132: To wash, To get up, To sit down, To fall, To go up.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
lavar-se — to wash (oneself).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for to drive, then the Portuguese for to get dressed.

You'll want to know: lavar-se.
lavar-se — "to wash (oneself)".

The word, taken apart.
lavar from Latin lavare, "to wash" (EtymDB 2.1) — inherited. -se means "oneself".
