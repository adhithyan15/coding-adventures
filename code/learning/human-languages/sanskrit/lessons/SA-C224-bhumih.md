## You'll want to know: भूमिः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C224-THG224-05]; assesses=[] -->

**भूमिः** — *bhūmiḥ* — "the ground, land".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*bhūmi* is built on the root √bhū, "to be, become" (MW), the root of *bhavati*: the earth is where things come to be. It is a Rigvedic word (CDIAL 9557). MW compares √bhū with English *be*.
