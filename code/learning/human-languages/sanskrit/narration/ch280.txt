Sanskrit, chapter 280: Which Way to the Station?.
2 lessons. Both can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 2.
स्थानकं कुत्र अस्ति? (sthānakaṃ kutra asti?) — asking the way.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say "where?" and "please". (kutra, kṛpayā.) You are in a town you do not know, and you need the station.

You'll want to know: where, how, how far.
स्थानकं कुत्र अस्ति? sthānakaṃ kutra asti? — "Where is the station?"
कियत् दूरम् अस्ति? kiyat dūram asti? — "How far is it?"
कियत् (kiyat), "how much", with दूरम् (dūram), "far", asks the distance, and कथं गच्छामि? (kathaṃ gacchāmi?) asks "how do I get there?" The answer:
बहु दूरं न अस्ति। bahu dūraṃ na asti. — "It is not very far."

You'll want to know: left, right, straight on.
वामतः (vāmataḥ), "on the left", is वामः, "left", with -taḥ; दक्षिणतः (dakṣiṇataḥ), "on the right", is दक्षिणः with it. ऋजु (ṛju), "straight", is ऋजुः said as an adverb. What to do takes -तु, the polite ending of ददातु (dadātu): गच्छतु (gacchatu), "please go".
ऋजु गच्छतु। ṛju gacchatu. — "Go straight on."
ततः वामतः गच्छतु। tataḥ vāmataḥ gacchatu. — "Then turn left."
स्थानकं दक्षिणतः अस्ति। sthānakaṃ dakṣiṇataḥ asti. — "The station is on the right."
-तु speaks to भवान्, or to भवती (bhavatī), a woman:
भवती अत्र तिष्ठतु। bhavatī atra tiṣṭhatu. — "Please wait here."

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: "Please go left" — kṛpayā vāmataḥ gacchatu]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: "Please go right" — kṛpayā dakṣiṇataḥ gacchatu]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: "Please go straight on" — kṛpayā ṛju gacchatu]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[question — say your answer, then pause 28 seconds]
Ask politely where the station is, then answer that it is on the right: go straight on, then right.

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
"Where is the station?" (स्थानकं कुत्र अस्ति? (sthānakaṃ kutra asti?)) "How far is it?" (कियत् दूरम् अस्ति?) "On the left"? (वामतः.) "Please go"? (गच्छतु.)


Lesson 2 of 2.
Review — asking the way.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say "you should read every day", to a friend. (tvaṃ pratidinaṃ paṭheḥ.) Then "therefore she is staying at home". (tasmāt sā gṛhe tiṣṭhati.)

Guided Practice.
[pause 2 seconds after each]
[your turn — say: Please, where is the shop?]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: How do I get to the school?]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: How far is the station? It is quite near.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: Go straight on, then turn left.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: The station is on the right. Please wait here.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
वामतः or दक्षिणतः, for "on the right"? (दक्षिणतः.) Which ending makes a request polite? (-तु: गच्छतु, तिष्ठतु.) Now the other way round: what does कथं गच्छामि? ask? ("How do I get there?")
