## You'll want to know: راجع
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C451-MAKHFAR451-04]; assesses=[] -->

**راجع** — *rājaʿa* — "to check over; to go back to (an office)".

> راجعت الموظفة الأوراق.
>
> *rājaʿat al-muwaẓẓafa al-awrāq.*
>
> The clerk checked over the papers.

Present: يراجع, *yurājiʿ*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**راجع** (*rājaʿa*) is built on the native Arabic root *r-j-ʿ*, "to return" (Lane). It is Form III, "to go back over".
