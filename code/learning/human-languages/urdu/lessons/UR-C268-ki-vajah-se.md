# کی وجہ سے (kī vajah se) — the reason

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[UR-LEX-KYUNKI, UR-LEX-C220-VAJAH220-03, UR-GRAMMAR-C268-OPINION-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "because" and "a reason". (*kyūṅki*, *vajah*.) Then "I think so". (*mujhe lagtā hai*.) An opinion wants a reason.

## You'll want to know: کیونکہ and چونکہ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-GRAMMAR-C268-REASON-01]; assesses=[UR-LEX-KYUNKI, UR-LEX-C159-VAJAH159-02, UR-LEX-C159-VAJAH159-01, UR-LEX-C41-HEALTH41-02, UR-GRAMMAR-C264-WAS-01] -->

> **میں نہیں آیا۔**
>
> *maiṅ nahīṅ āyā.* — "I didn't come."

> **میں نہیں آیا کیونکہ میں بیمار تھا۔**
>
> *maiṅ nahīṅ āyā kyūṅki maiṅ bīmār thā.* — "I didn't come because I was ill."

**کیونکہ** (*kyūṅki*) puts the reason second. **چونکہ** (*chūṅki*) puts it first, and the other half opens with **اس لیے** (*is liye*):

> **چونکہ میں بیمار تھا،**
>
> *chūṅki maiṅ bīmār thā,*
>
> **اس لیے میں نہیں آیا۔**
>
> *is liye maiṅ nahīṅ āyā.* — "Since I was ill, I didn't come."

## You'll want to know: a noun as the reason
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-GRAMMAR-C268-REASON-02]; assesses=[UR-LEX-C220-VAJAH220-03, UR-LEX-BARISH, UR-LEX-C77-TIME77-04, UR-LEX-C221-SIFAT221-01, UR-LEX-C50-VERBS50-04] -->

> **بارش کی وجہ سے ہم گھر پر رہے۔**
>
> *bārish kī vajah se ham ghar par rahe.* — "Because of the rain we stayed at home."

**وجہ** (*vajah*) is feminine, so **کی** (*kī*). The noun goes in front:

> **دیر کی وجہ سے وہ ناراض تھی۔**
>
> *der kī vajah se voh nārāz thī.* — "She was cross because of the delay."
