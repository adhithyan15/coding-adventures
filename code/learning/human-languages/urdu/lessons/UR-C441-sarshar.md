## You'll want to know: سرشار
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C441-JOY441-04]; assesses=[] -->

**سرشار** — *sarshār* — "brimming over (with a feeling)".

> دلہن خوشی سے سرشار تھی۔
>
> *dulhan khushī se sarshār thī.*
>
> The bride was brimming over with joy.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**سرشار** (*sarshār*) is usually traced to Persian *sarshār*, "full to the brim".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
