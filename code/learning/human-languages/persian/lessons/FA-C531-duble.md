## You'll want to know: دوبله
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C531-SCREEN-04]; assesses=[] -->

**دوبله** (*duble*) — "dubbed; dubbing".

> پدربزرگ فیلمهای دوبله را بیشتر دوست دارد.
>
> *pedarbozorg filmhâ-ye duble râ bishtar dust dârad.*
>
> Grandfather prefers dubbed films.

*duble kardan* — to dub.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*duble* is a modern loan, usually traced to French *doublé*, 'doubled'. EtymDB has no entry for it.
