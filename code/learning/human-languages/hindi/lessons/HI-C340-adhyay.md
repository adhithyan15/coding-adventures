## You'll want to know: अध्याय
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C340-UPANYAS340-04]; assesses=[] -->

**अध्याय** — *adhyāy* — "a chapter of a book".

> पहले अध्याय में नायक गाँव छोड़ता है।
>
> *pahle adhyāy meṁ nāyak gā̃v choṛtā hai.*
>
> In the first chapter the hero leaves his village.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अध्याय** (*adhyāy*) is Sanskrit *adhyāya*, "a reading, a lesson", and so a chapter (Monier-Williams).
