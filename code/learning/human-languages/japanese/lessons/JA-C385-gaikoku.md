## You'll want to know: がいこく
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C385-KAIGAI385-02]; assesses=[] -->

**がいこく** — *gaikoku* — "a foreign country".

> **がいこくの おかねに りょうがえしました。** — *gaikoku no okane ni ryōgae shimashita* — "I changed my money into foreign currency."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**がいこく** (*gaikoku*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *gai*, "outside" (Middle Chinese *ngwajH*) and *koku*, "country" (*kwok*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
