## You'll want to know: 气候
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C243-SEASON243-01]; assesses=[] -->

**气候** — *qìhòu* — "climate".

> **西边的气候不好风很大** *xībiān de qìhòu bù hǎo fēng hěn dà* — The climate in the west isn't good: it's very windy.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**气候** (*qìhòu*) is built from 气 'air' and 候 'a season', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *khj+jH* and *huwH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'to wait on'; the sense of a season is not given there.
