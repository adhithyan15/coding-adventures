## You'll want to know: ಮಸಿ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C473-PARISARA473-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ಮಸಿ** — *masi* — "soot".

> ಹಳೆಯ ಬಸ್ಸಿನ ಹೊಗೆಯಿಂದ ಗೋಡೆಗೆ ಮಸಿ ಹಿಡಿಯಿತು.
>
> *haḷeya bassina hogeyinda gōḍege masi hiḍiyitu.*
>
> The smoke of an old bus left soot on the wall.

In older Kannada it was also ink.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಮಸಿ** (*masi*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 5101 lists Kannada *masi*, "soot, lampblack, ink", beside Tamil *mai*. Sanskrit *maṣi*, *masi*, "lampblack, ink", was itself taken from Dravidian, as Turner notes (CDIAL 9920).
