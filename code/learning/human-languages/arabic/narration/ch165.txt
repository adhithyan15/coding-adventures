Arabic, chapter 165: To delete, To begin, To end, To continue, To finish.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
حذف (ḥadhafa) — to delete.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to believe, then the Arabic for to remember.

You'll want to know: حذف.
حذف — ḥadhafa — "to delete".
