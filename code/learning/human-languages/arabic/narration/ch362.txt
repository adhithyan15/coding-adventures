Arabic, chapter 362: Logic and the mind.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
منطق (manṭiq) — logic.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to stir up, then the Arabic for to wonder.

You'll want to know: منطق.
منطق — manṭiq — "logic".
لا أفهم منطق هذا القرار. lā afhamu manṭiq hādhā al-qarār. I do not understand the logic of this decision.
The adjective is منطقي, manṭiqī, logical.

The word, taken apart.
منطق (manṭiq) is built on the native Arabic root n-ṭ-q, "to speak" (Lane). Lane gives manṭiq as speech, the putting of ideas into words; later it became the word for logic.

Lesson 2 of 5.
مغالطة (mughālaṭa) — a fallacy.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to wonder, then the Arabic for logic.

You'll want to know: مغالطة.
مغالطة — mughālaṭa — "a fallacy".
في هذا الكلام مغالطة واضحة. fī hādhā al-kalām mughālaṭa wāḍiḥa. There is a clear fallacy in what was said.

The word, taken apart.
مغالطة (mughālaṭa) is built on the native Arabic root gh-l-ṭ, "to make a mistake" (Lane). It is the Form III verbal noun, leading someone into error: a fallacy, an argument that misleads.

Lesson 3 of 5.
عقل (ʿaql) — the mind.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for logic, then the Arabic for a fallacy.

You'll want to know: عقل.
عقل — ʿaql — "the mind".
فكر الرجل بعقله لا بقلبه. fakkara ar-rajul bi-ʿaqlihi lā bi-qalbih. The man thought with his head, not with his heart.
Plural: عقول, ʿuqūl.

The word, taken apart.
عقل (ʿaql) is built on the native Arabic root ʿ-q-l, "to tie, to restrain" (Lane). Lane gives the root as tying a camel's leg; ʿaql is the mind, which holds a person back from folly.

Lesson 4 of 5.
استنتج (istantaja) — to deduce; to conclude.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a fallacy, then the Arabic for the mind.

You'll want to know: استنتج.
استنتج — istantaja — "to deduce; to conclude".
استنتجت من كلامه أنه متعب. istantajtu min kalāmihi annahu mutʿab. I concluded from what he said that he was tired.
Present: يستنتج, yastantij.
Followed by أن: استنتج (istantaja) أن …. A related noun is نتيجة, a result.

The word, taken apart.
استنتج (istantaja) is built on the native Arabic root n-t-j, "to bring forth young" (Lane). It is Form X, to seek the outcome: to draw a conclusion.

هناك تناقض بين كلامه وعمله. hunāka tanāquḍ bayna kalāmihi wa-ʿamalih. There is a contradiction between his words and his deeds.

تناقض (tanāquḍ) is built on the native Arabic root n-q-ḍ, "to undo, to unravel" (Lane). It is the Form VI verbal noun: two things that undo each other, a contradiction.
