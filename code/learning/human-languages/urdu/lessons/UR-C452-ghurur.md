## You'll want to know: غرور
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C452-PRIDE452-01]; assesses=[] -->

**غرور** — *ghurūr* — "arrogance; conceit".

> اسے اپنی طاقت پر غرور ہے۔
>
> *use apnī tāqat par ghurūr hai.*
>
> He is conceited about his strength.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**غرور** (*ghurūr*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *ghurūr*, "self-delusion, vanity".
