Japanese, chapter 49: Tall, Low, Cheap, Fast, Easy.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
たかい (takai) — tall; expensive.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for long, then the Japanese for short.

You'll want to know: たかい.
たかい — takai — "tall; expensive".

The word, taken apart.
たかい (takai) is a native Japanese word, inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, and its older origin is not settled. Its kanji was attached to it later as a kun'yomi, a native reading given to a Chinese character (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
