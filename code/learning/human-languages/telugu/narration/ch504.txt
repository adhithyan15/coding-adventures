Telugu, chapter 504: Admiration and Praise.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ఆరాధించు (ārādhiñcu) — to adore; to worship.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to fondle, then the Telugu for fondness.

You'll want to know: ఆరాధించు.
ఆరాధించు — ārādhiñcu — "to adore; to worship".
ఆ అమ్మాయి తన అక్కను ఆరాధిస్తుంది. ā ammāyi tana akkanu ārādhistundi. That girl adores her elder sister.
Past (I …): ఆరాధించాను, ārādhiñcānu.
The noun is ఆరాధన, adoration.

The word, taken apart.
ఆరాధించు (ārādhiñcu) is built on Sanskrit ārādhana, "worship" (CDIAL 1316), the noun of ārādhayati, "to win over", with the Telugu verb ending -ఇంచు.

Lesson 3 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to adore, then the Telugu for to admire.

అభిమాని — abhimāni — "an admirer; a fan".
నా తమ్ముడు ఆ ఆటగాడికి పెద్ద అభిమాని. nā tammuḍu ā āṭagāḍiki pedda abhimāni. My younger brother is a big fan of that player.

The word, taken apart.
అభిమాని (abhimāni) is borrowed from Sanskrit abhimānin, built on abhimāna (CDIAL 524): one who holds someone in high regard.

Lesson 4 of 5.
మెచ్చుకో (meccukō) — to appreciate; to praise.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to admire, then the Telugu for an admirer.

You'll want to know: మెచ్చుకో.
మెచ్చుకో — meccukō — "to appreciate; to praise".
నాన్న నా బొమ్మను మెచ్చుకున్నారు. nānna nā bommanu meccukunnāru. Dad praised my picture.
Past (I …): మెచ్చుకున్నాను, meccukunnānu.
Warmer than పొగడు, which can be flattery: మెచ్చుకో (meccukō) is honest praise.

The word, taken apart.
మెచ్చుకో (meccukō) is native Dravidian: DEDR 4722 lists Telugu meccu, "to admire, to praise, to approve", in the article of Tamil maruvu; -కో is the reflexive ending.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ప్రశంస (praśaṁsa) — acclaim; praise.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for an admirer, then the Telugu for to appreciate.

You'll want to know: ప్రశంస.
ప్రశంస — praśaṁsa — "acclaim; praise".
ఆ సినిమాకు చాలా ప్రశంసలు వచ్చాయి. ā sinimāku cālā praśaṁsalu vaccāyi. That film won a lot of acclaim.
Plural: ప్రశంసలు, praśaṁsalu.
Often plural: ప్రశంసలు అందుకో, to receive praise.

The word, taken apart.
ప్రశంస (praśaṁsa) is borrowed from Sanskrit praśaṁsā, "praise, commendation, fame" (Monier-Williams).
