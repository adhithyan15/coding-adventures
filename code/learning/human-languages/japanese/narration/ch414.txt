Japanese, chapter 414: Hot, Damp Days.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
しつど (shitsudo) — humidity.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for a continent, then the Japanese for a desert.

You'll want to know: しつど.
しつど — shitsudo — "humidity".
なつの 日本は しつどが たかいです。 — natsu no nihon wa shitsudo ga takai desu — "Japan is humid in summer."

The word, taken apart.
しつど (shitsudo) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are shitsu, "damp" (Middle Chinese syip) and do, "degrees" (duH) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).

Lesson 2 of 5.
じめじめ (jimejime) — damp and humid.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for a desert, then the Japanese for humidity.

You'll want to know: じめじめ.
じめじめ — jimejime — "damp and humid".
つゆは まいにち じめじめしています。 — tsuyu wa mainichi jimejime shite imasu — "In the rainy season it is damp and sticky every day."

The word, taken apart.
じめじめ (jimejime) is a native mimetic word that pictures damp, sticky air: an imitative Japanese form, inherited rather than borrowed, whose older origin is not settled (JMdict).

Lesson 3 of 5.
ひざし (hizashi) — sunlight; the sun's rays.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for humidity, then the Japanese for damp and humid.

You'll want to know: ひざし.
ひざし — hizashi — "sunlight; the sun's rays".
なつの ひざしは つよいです。 — natsu no hizashi wa tsuyoi desu — "The summer sun is strong."

The word, taken apart.
ひざし (hizashi) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, built from hi, "sun" and zashi, "shining" (from sasu); its kanji were attached later as kun'yomi (JMdict).
