# ಮಿನುಗು (minugu) — to twinkle; to glitter

## You'll want to know: ಮಿನುಗು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C489-IBBANI489-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ಮಿನುಗು** — *minugu* — "to twinkle; to glitter".

> ಕತ್ತಲೆಯಲ್ಲಿ ಆಕಾಶದಲ್ಲಿ ಚುಕ್ಕಿಗಳು ಮಿನುಗುತ್ತಿದ್ದವು.
>
> *kattaleyalli ākāśadalli cukkigaḷu minuguttiddavu.*
>
> In the dark the stars were twinkling in the sky.

Past (it …): ಮಿನುಗಿತು, *minugitu*.

Of stars, lights and jewels.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಮಿನುಗು** (*minugu*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 4876 lists Kannada *miṇuku*, *minuku*, *minugu*, "to glitter, to sparkle", the family of ಮಿಂಚು, lightning.
