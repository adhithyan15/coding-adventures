# حتى لو (ḥattā law) — even if

## You'll want to know: حتى لو
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C404-SHART404-03]; assesses=[] -->

**حتى لو** — *ḥattā law* — "even if".

> سأذهب حتى لو أمطرت.
>
> *sa-adhhabu ḥattā law amṭarat.*
>
> I will go even if it rains.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**حتى لو** (*ḥattā law*) joins the native Arabic particles *ḥattā*, "even, until", and *law*, "if": even if. The deeper origin of such particles is not settled.

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
