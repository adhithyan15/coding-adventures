## You'll want to know: la garantía
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C614-RIESGO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**la garantía** — "the guarantee, the warranty".

> El móvil tiene dos años de garantía.
>
> *The phone has a two-year warranty.*

*Estar en garantía* — to be under warranty.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*garantía* comes from *garante* 'guarantor'; EtymDB links it with Catalan *garantia*, and the family is usually traced to Old French *garant*, from a Frankish word for a protector.
