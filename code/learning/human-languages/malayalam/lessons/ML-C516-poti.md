## You'll want to know: പൊടി
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C516-TATASSAM516-02]; assesses=[] -->

**പൊടി** — *poṭi* — "dust; powder".

> വേനലിൽ വഴിയിൽ നിറയെ പൊടിയാണ്.
>
> *vēnalil vaḻiyil niṟaye poṭiyāṇŭ.*
>
> In the dry season the road is full of dust.

Also any powder: മുളകുപൊടി, chilli powder.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**പൊടി** (*poṭi*) is native Dravidian, "dust, powder" (DEDR 4481).
