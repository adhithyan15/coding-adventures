## You'll want to know: しゅうにゅう
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C360-SHUUNYUU360-01]; assesses=[] -->

**しゅうにゅう** — *shūnyū* — "income".

> **しゅうにゅうが ふえたら、ひっこしたいです。** — *shūnyū ga fuetara, hikkoshitai desu* — "If my income goes up, I want to move house."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**しゅうにゅう** (*shūnyū*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *shū*, "income" (Middle Chinese *syuw*) and *nyū*, "enter" (*nyip*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
