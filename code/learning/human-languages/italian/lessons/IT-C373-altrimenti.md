## You'll want to know: altrimenti
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C373-PURCHE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**altrimenti** — "otherwise, or else".

> Sbrigati, altrimenti perdiamo l'autobus.
>
> *Hurry up, otherwise we will miss the bus.*

Neutral in speech and writing.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*altrimenti* is built from *altro* "other" and the adverb ending *-mente* (EtymDB); *altro* is inherited from Latin *alter* "the other", and *-mente* from *mēns, mentis* "mind": "in another way".
