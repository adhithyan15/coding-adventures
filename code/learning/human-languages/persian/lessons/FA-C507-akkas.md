## You'll want to know: عکاس
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C507-HOBBIES-01]; assesses=[] -->

**عکاس** (*'akkâs*) — "a photographer".

> یک عکاس از مسابقه عکس گرفت.
>
> *yek 'akkâs az mosâbeqe 'aks gereft.*
>
> A photographer took photos of the match.

Built on *'aks* 'photo'. *'aks gereftan* — to take a photo.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*'akkâs* is built on *'aks*, 'picture', a loan usually traced to Arabic *ʿaks*, 'reflection', in the Arabic pattern for a person who does something.
