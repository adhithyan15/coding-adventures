## You'll want to know: necken
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C497-TROTZIG-04]; assesses=[] -->

**necken** — "to tease".

> Mein großer Bruder neckt mich gern wegen meiner Haare.
>
> *My big brother likes to tease me about my hair.*

Gentle, friendly teasing.
