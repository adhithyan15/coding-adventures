## Grammar Lens: change only the ending
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-FORM-KARDE-183]; assesses=[] -->

> **ਕਰਦੇ** — *karde* — do; work, with plural or respectful agreement

The stem stays *kar-*. The visible ending changes from **ਦਾ** to **ਦੇ**. In a complete sentence: **ਉਹ ਕੰਮ ਕਰਦੇ ਹਨ** — “They work,” or respectful “He works.”
