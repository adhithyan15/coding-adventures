## You'll want to know: چمکنا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C292-TUFAN292-03]; assesses=[] -->

**چمکنا** — *chamaknā* — "to flash; to gleam".

> آسمان میں بجلی چمکی۔
>
> *āsmān meṅ bijlī chamkī.*
>
> Lightning flashed in the sky.

Past (it …): وہ بجلی چمکی, *voh bijlī chamkī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**چمکنا** (*chamaknā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *camaknā*, "to glitter", under *\*camakka*, a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 4676).
