## You'll want to know: सज्जनः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C358-ACHARA358-03]; assesses=[] -->

**सज्जनः** — *sajjanaḥ* — "a good person".

> सज्जनः सदा सत्यं वदति।
>
> *sajjanaḥ sadā satyaṃ vadati.*
>
> A good person tells the truth at all times.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सज्जनः** (*sajjanaḥ*) is built from *sat*, "good", and *jana*, "a person"; MW gives *saj-jana* "a good or virtuous or wise man" (MW).
