Kannada, chapter 513: Getting Better.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಚೇತರಿಸಿಕೊ (cētarisiko) — to recover; to pick up.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a stroke, then the Kannada for to faint.

You'll want to know: ಚೇತರಿಸಿಕೊ.
ಚೇತರಿಸಿಕೊ — cētarisiko — "to recover; to pick up".
ಆಪರೇಷನ್ ನಂತರ ಅಮ್ಮ ಬೇಗ ಚೇತರಿಸಿಕೊಂಡರು. āparēṣan nantara amma bēga cētarisikoṇḍaru. After the operation mother recovered quickly.
Past (I …): ಚೇತರಿಸಿಕೊಂಡೆ, cētarisikoṇḍe.
From what: -ಇಂದ. ಚೇತರಿಕೆ is recovery.

The word, taken apart.
ಚೇತರಿಸಿಕೊ (cētarisiko) is usually traced to Sanskrit cetana, "conscious, sentient; the mind" (Monier-Williams), with -isi-ko: to bring life back into oneself. The exact path is not settled.

Lesson 2 of 5.
ಉಣಿಸು (uṇisu) — to feed (a child or the sick).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to faint, then the Kannada for to recover.

You'll want to know: ಉಣಿಸು.
ಉಣಿಸು — uṇisu — "to feed (a child or the sick)".
ಅಮ್ಮ ಜ್ವರ ಬಂದ ಮಗನಿಗೆ ಮೆತ್ತಗಿನ ಅನ್ನ ಉಣಿಸಿದರು. amma jvara banda maganige mettagina anna uṇisidaru. Mother fed her feverish son soft rice.
Past (I …): ಉಣಿಸಿದೆ, uṇiside.
The one fed takes -ಗೆ. It is the causative of the old verb ಉಣ್, to eat a meal, as in ಊಟ.

The word, taken apart.
ಉಣಿಸು (uṇisu) is native Dravidian: DEDR 600 lists Kannada uṇ, uṇṇu, "to eat a meal; of children, to take mother's milk"; -isu makes "to make someone eat".

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: ಕಷಾಯ.
ಕಷಾಯ — kaṣāya — "a herbal decoction".
ಶೀತಕ್ಕೆ ಅಜ್ಜಿ ಶುಂಠಿಯ ಕಷಾಯ ಮಾಡಿದರು. śītakke ajji śuṇṭhiya kaṣāya māḍidaru. Grandmother made a ginger decoction for the cold.
Boiled from ginger, pepper or tulasi leaves and drunk hot.

The word, taken apart.
ಕಷಾಯ (kaṣāya) is borrowed from Sanskrit kaṣāya, "astringent; an astringent juice" (CDIAL 2974).
