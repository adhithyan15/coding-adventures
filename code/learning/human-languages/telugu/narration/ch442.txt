Telugu, chapter 442: On the Rails.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
పట్టా (paṭṭā) — a rail; a track.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for security, then the Telugu for a certificate.

You'll want to know: పట్టా.
పట్టా — paṭṭā — "a rail; a track".
పిల్లలు రైలు పట్టాల దగ్గర ఆడకూడదు. pillalu railu paṭṭāla daggara āḍakūḍadu. Children must not play near the railway tracks.
Plural: పట్టాలు, paṭṭālu.
The rails of a line are పట్టాలు. పట్టా (paṭṭā) is also a strip of land and a title deed.

The word, taken apart.
పట్టా (paṭṭā) is usually traced to Sanskrit paṭṭa, "a slab, a tablet, a strip" (CDIAL 7699); a rail is a long strip of iron.

Lesson 3 of 5.
రాకపోకలు (rākapōkalu) — comings and goings; traffic.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a rail, then the Telugu for the metro.

You'll want to know: రాకపోకలు.
రాకపోకలు — rākapōkalu — "comings and goings; traffic".
వరద వల్ల ఆ రోడ్డు మీద రాకపోకలు నిలిచాయి. varada valla ā rōḍḍu mīda rākapōkalu nilicāyi. Because of the flood, traffic on that road stopped.
రాక, coming, + పోక, going, + the plural -లు. The news uses it for the traffic on a road.

The word, taken apart.
రాకపోకలు (rākapōkalu) is native Dravidian: రాక, "coming", the noun of రా, "to come" (DEDR 5270), and పోక, "going", the noun of పోవు, "to go" (DEDR 4572), with the plural -లు.

Lesson 4 of 5.
కిక్కిరిసిపోవు (kikkirisipōvu) — to be packed; to be crammed.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for the metro, then the Telugu for comings and goings.

You'll want to know: కిక్కిరిసిపోవు.
కిక్కిరిసిపోవు — kikkirisipōvu — "to be packed; to be crammed".
సాయంత్రం బస్సు జనంతో కిక్కిరిసిపోయింది. sāyantraṁ bassu janantō kikkirisipōyindi. The evening bus was packed with people.
Past (it …): కిక్కిరిసిపోయింది, kikkirisipōyindi.
What fills the place takes -తో: జనంతో, with people. పోవు at the end marks a change that has happened completely. Its subject is a thing, so the past shown is the third person: కిక్కిరిసిపోయింది, it was packed.

The word, taken apart.
కిక్కిరిసిపోవు (kikkirisipōvu) is a Telugu compound whose first part, కిక్కిరియు, "to be crammed", has an origin that is not settled; the second part is the native పోవు, "to go" (DEDR 4572), which marks a change that is complete.

Lesson 5 of 5.
పట్టాలు తప్పు (paṭṭālu tappu) — to derail.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for comings and goings, then the Telugu for to be packed.

You'll want to know: పట్టాలు తప్పు.
పట్టాలు తప్పు — paṭṭālu tappu — "to derail".
నిన్న రాత్రి ఒక సరుకు రైలు పట్టాలు తప్పింది. ninna rātri oka saruku railu paṭṭālu tappindi. Last night a goods train went off the rails.
Past (it …): పట్టాలు తప్పింది, paṭṭālu tappindi.
Word for word: to miss the rails. It is also said of a plan that goes wrong. Its subject is a thing, so the past shown is the third person: పట్టాలు తప్పింది, it went off the rails.

The word, taken apart.
పట్టాలు తప్పు (paṭṭālu tappu) is a Telugu phrase built from పట్టాలు, "rails", usually traced to Sanskrit paṭṭa (CDIAL 7699), and the native verb తప్పు, "to slip, to miss, to go wrong" (DEDR 3071).
