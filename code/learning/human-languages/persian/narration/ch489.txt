Persian, chapter 489: Manners and Temperament.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
مودب (mo'addab) — polite, well-mannered.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for generous, then the Persian for caring.

You'll want to know: مودب.
مودب (mo'addab) — "polite, well-mannered".
بچه های آنها خیلی مودب هستند. bachchehâ-ye ânhâ kheyli mo'addab hastand. Their children are very polite.
Built on adab 'good manners'. The opposite is biadab.

The word, taken apart.
mo'addab is a loan, usually traced to Arabic muʾaddab, 'well-bred', a word built on adab, 'manners'. EtymDB has no entry for it.

Lesson 2 of 5.
بیادب (biadab) — rude, impolite.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for caring, then the Persian for polite.

You'll want to know: بیادب.
بیادب (biadab) — "rude, impolite".
آن مرد با فروشنده بیادب بود. ân mard bâ forushande biadab bud. That man was rude to the shop assistant.
bi- 'without' + adab 'manners'. bâ kasi biadab budan — to be rude to someone.

The word, taken apart.
biadab is built in Persian from the prefix bi-, 'without', and adab, 'manners', a loan usually traced to Arabic adab. EtymDB has no entry for it.

زودرنج (zudranj) — "touchy, easily hurt".
او زودرنج است و با کوچکترین حرفی ناراحت میشود. u zudranj ast va bâ kuchektarin harfi nârâhat mishavad. She's touchy and gets upset at the smallest remark.

The word, taken apart.
zudranj is built on two native Persian words: zud, 'quickly', and ranj, 'suffering', the noun behind ranjidan: quick to be hurt. EtymDB has no entry for it.

Lesson 4 of 5.
کمرو (kamru) — shy, timid (in company).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for rude, then the Persian for touchy.

You'll want to know: کمرو.
کمرو (kamru) — "shy, timid (in company)".
پسرشان کمرو است و در مهمانی حرف نمیزند. pesarashân kamru ast va dar mehmâni harf nemizanad. Their son is shy and doesn't talk at parties.
kam 'little' + ru 'face': little face, little boldness. The opposite is porru 'cheeky'.

The word, taken apart.
kamru is built on two native Persian words, kam, 'little', and ru, 'face'. It is the mirror of porru, 'cheeky', built on por, 'full'.

Lesson 5 of 5.
مسخره کردن (maskhare kardan) — to make fun of, to mock.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for touchy, then the Persian for shy.

You'll want to know: مسخره کردن.
مسخره کردن (maskhare kardan) — "to make fun of, to mock".
او لباس من را مسخره کرد. u lebâs-e man râ maskhare kard. He made fun of my clothes.
maskhare 'ridiculous; a laughing stock' + kardan; present stem kon-. The person mocked takes râ.

The word, taken apart.
maskhare comes from Arabic masḫara, 'buffoon, anything ridiculous' (EtymDB). kardan, 'to do', is inherited Persian.
