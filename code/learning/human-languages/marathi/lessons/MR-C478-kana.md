## You'll want to know: कणा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C478-HAD478-02]; assesses=[] -->

**कणा** — *kaṇā* — "the spine; backbone".

> पाठीचा कणा सरळ ठेवून तो बसला.
>
> *pāṭhīcā kaṇā saraḷ ṭhevūn to baslā.*
>
> He sat with his spine straight.

Masculine.

पाठीचा कणा, the spine; also, figuratively, the backbone of something.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**कणा** (*kaṇā*) is a Marathi noun whose origin is not settled.
