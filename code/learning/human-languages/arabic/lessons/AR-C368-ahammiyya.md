## You'll want to know: أهمية
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C368-AHAMMIYYA368-01]; assesses=[] -->

**أهمية** — *ahammiyya* — "importance".

> يعرف الجميع أهمية النوم.
>
> *yaʿrifu al-jamīʿ ahammiyyat an-nawm.*
>
> Everyone knows the importance of sleep.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**أهمية** (*ahammiyya*) is built on the native Arabic root *h-m-m*, "to concern, to trouble" (Lane). *Ahammiyya* is a modern abstract noun from *ahamm*, more important: importance.
