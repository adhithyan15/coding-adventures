French, chapter 290: Le crime.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
le crime — the crime; the murder.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for to threaten, then the French for the threat.

You'll want to know: le crime.
le crime — "the crime; the murder".
Ce crime a choqué toute la ville. This crime shocked the whole town.
In French, un crime is a serious offence, often a murder.

The word, taken apart.
crime is inherited from Latin crīmen "a charge, a crime" (EtymDB).

The killer is le meurtrier, la meurtrière.

The word, taken apart.
meurtre is built on Old French murtrir "to murder", which is usually traced to a Frankish verb related to English murder (EtymDB lists no parent).
