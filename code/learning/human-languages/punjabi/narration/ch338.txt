Punjabi, chapter 338: Is It Allowed?.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ਇਜਾਜ਼ਤ (ijāzat) — permission.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for a bit (to soften a request), then the Punjabi for to send for.

You'll want to know: ਇਜਾਜ਼ਤ.
ਇਜਾਜ਼ਤ — ijāzat — "permission".
ਅਧਿਆਪਕ ਇਜਾਜ਼ਤ ਨਹੀਂ ਦਿੰਦਾ ਹੈ। adhiāpak ijāzat nahīṁ dindā hai. The teacher does not give permission.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ਇਜਾਜ਼ਤ (ijāzat) is a loan, usually traced through Persian to Arabic ijāza, "permission".

What you've built.
One more word for asking and answering.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: ijāzat]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: ijāzat, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: adhiāpak ijāzat nahīṁ dindā hai. — The teacher does not give permission.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Punjabi for a bit (to soften a request), then the Punjabi for to send for, then say ijāzat again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does ਇਜਾਜ਼ਤ (ijāzat) mean? ("Permission".) Say it once more.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ਟੋਕਣਾ (ṭokṇā) — to interrupt; to stop someone with a remark.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for approved, then the Punjabi for an order.

You'll want to know: ਟੋਕਣਾ.
ਟੋਕਣਾ — ṭokṇā — "to interrupt; to stop someone with a remark".
ਮਿਹਰਬਾਨੀ ਕਰਕੇ ਮੈਨੂੰ ਨਾ ਟੋਕੋ। miharbānī karke mainū̃ nā ṭoko. Please don't interrupt me.
Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਅਮਨ ਨੂੰ ਟੋਕਿਆ, maiṁ Aman nū̃ ṭokiā.

The word, taken apart.
ਟੋਕਣਾ (ṭokṇā) is inherited from a reconstructed ṭōkk, "strike, bite"; CDIAL lists Punjabi ṭokṇā, "to hinder", under it (CDIAL 5476).
