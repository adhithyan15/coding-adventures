Persian, chapter 167: Ice cream, Chocolate, Biscuit, Soft drink, Jam.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
بستنی (bastani) — ice cream.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for cash, then the Persian for a blouse.

You'll want to know: بستنی.
بستنی — bastani — "ice cream".

The word, taken apart.
bastani is usually explained as built on bastan, "to bind, to set, to freeze", with -i: something set solid. bastan is native Persian. (EtymDB links it to an unrelated Romanian word.)

Lesson 4 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for chocolate, then the Persian for a biscuit.

You'll want to know: نوشابه.
نوشابه — nushâbe — "a soft drink".

The word, taken apart.
nushâbe is nush, "sweet drink", with âbe, from âb, "water": a modern Persian coinage. âb is inherited (Middle Persian āb, EtymDB).
