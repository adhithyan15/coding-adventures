## You'll want to know: финиш
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C512-RACE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**финиш** (*fínish*) — "the finish".

> Перед финишем он обогнал всех.
>
> *Péred fínishem on obognál vsekh.*
>
> Before the finish he overtook everyone.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*финиш* was borrowed from English *finish* (EtymDB).
