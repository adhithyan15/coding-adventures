## You'll want to know: ಹುಸಿ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C374-JADU374-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ಹುಸಿ** — *husi* — "false; empty".

> ಆ ಜಾದೂಗಾರನ ಮಾತು ಹುಸಿ ಆಯಿತು.
>
> *ā jādūgārana mātu husi āyitu.*
>
> The magician's words turned out to be false.

ಹುಸಿ ಬೆದರಿಕೆ, an empty threat. ಹುಸಿ is also a lie.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಹುಸಿ** (*husi*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 4531 lists Kannada *pusi*, "to become or prove untrue, to lie", and as a noun "a falsehood"; old *p-* became *h-*.
