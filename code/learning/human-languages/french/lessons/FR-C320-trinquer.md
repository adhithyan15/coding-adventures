## You'll want to know: trinquer
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C320-TOAST-01]; assesses=[] -->

**trinquer** — "to clink glasses, to drink to".

> Nous avons trinqué aux mariés.
>
> *We clinked glasses to the bride and groom.*

Informal: *c'est moi qui ai trinqué* — I paid the price.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*trinquer* was borrowed from German *trinken* "to drink" (EtymDB).
