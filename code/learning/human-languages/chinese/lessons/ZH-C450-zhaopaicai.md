## You'll want to know: zhāopáicài
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C450-LOCAL450-05]; assesses=[] -->

*zhāopáicài* — "a restaurant's signature dish".

> *zhè shì zhè jiā fànguǎn de zhāopáicài* — This is the restaurant's signature dish.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*zhāopáicài* is built from *zhāopái* 'a shop sign' and 菜 'a dish', all inherited from Middle Chinese, *tsyew*, *bea* and *tshojH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to beckon' and the second as a board with writing on it.
