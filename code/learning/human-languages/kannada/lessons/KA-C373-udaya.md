## You'll want to know: ಉದಯ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C373-UDAYA373-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ಉದಯ** — *udaya* — "a rise; a dawn".

> ಆ ರಾಜ್ಯದ ಉದಯ ಒಂದು ಸಣ್ಣ ಹಳ್ಳಿಯಲ್ಲಿ ಆಯಿತು.
>
> *ā rājyada udaya ondu saṇṇa haḷḷiyalli āyitu.*
>
> The rise of that kingdom began in a small village.

ಸೂರ್ಯೋದಯ is sunrise. Of a kingdom or a leader, its rise.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಉದಯ** (*udaya*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *udaya*, "rise, rising of the sun" (CDIAL 1931).
