## You'll want to know: سربالایی
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C448-MIYANBOR-02]; assesses=[] -->

**سربالایی** (*sarbâlâyi*) — "an uphill slope, a climb".

> دوچرخه سواری در سربالایی سخت است.
>
> *Docharkhe-savâri dar sarbâlâyi sakht ast.*
>
> Cycling uphill is hard.

*sar* 'head' + *bâlâ* 'up' + *-i*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*sarbâlâyi* is a native compound of *sar* 'head', from Middle Persian *sar* (EtymDB), and *bâlâ* 'up', usually traced to Middle Persian *bālāy*, with the ending *-i*.
