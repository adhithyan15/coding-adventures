## You'll want to know: बहस
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C346-BAHAS346-01]; assesses=[] -->

**बहस** — *bahas* — "an argument, a debate".

> कल रात अरुण और मीरा में लंबी बहस हुई।
>
> *kal rāt Aruṇ aur Mīrā meṁ lambī bahas huī.*
>
> Last night Arun and Mira had a long argument.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**बहस** (*bahas*) is usually traced to Arabic *baḥth*, "an inquiry, a discussion", which reached Hindi through Persian.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
