## You'll want to know: wèn
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C54-LEARN54-04]; assesses=[] -->

*wèn* — "to ask".

> *wǒ wèn lǎoshī* — I'll ask the teacher.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*wèn* is inherited from Middle Chinese *mjunH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'to inquire'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for asking politely.
