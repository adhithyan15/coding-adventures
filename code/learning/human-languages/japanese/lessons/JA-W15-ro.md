# ろ — one turn, and the r you already make

## Script — the shape
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-SCRIPT-RO-01]; assesses=[JA-SCRIPT-RA-01] -->

> **ろ** — *ro*

Write **ら** once. **ろ** starts the same way and ends differently: a short
stroke across the top, then down and round to the left and back up, closing the
loop instead of leaving a hook.

The sound is the one **ら** already taught you — a single tap of the tongue,
neither the English *r* nor the English *l*.

This is the first new sign in two chapters, and it is here for one word.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[JA-SCRIPT-RO-01, JA-SCRIPT-RA-01] -->

1. Trace **ろ** once and copy it once.
2. Hide it; write it after ten seconds.
3. Write **ら** and **ろ** side by side and say both, one tap each.
