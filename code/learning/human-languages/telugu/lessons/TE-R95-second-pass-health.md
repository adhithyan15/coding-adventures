# From ill to well — a second pass

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[TE-LEX-C92-DOCTOR-01, TE-LEX-C92-DOCTOR-05, TE-LEX-C93-FINE-05] -->

[PAUSE 3s] You are ill. Say you have a cold, you feel weak, and your body aches.

## Grammar Lens: ill and well in one frame
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[TE-LEX-C92-DOCTOR-05, TE-LEX-C93-FINE-01, TE-LEX-C93-FINE-02, TE-LEX-C93-FINE-03] -->

| not well | well again |
|---|---|
| **నాకు నీరసంగా ఉంది** | **హాయిగా ఉంది** |
| **జలుబు చేసింది** | **క్షేమంగా ఉన్నారా?** |

The **-గా ఉంది** frame carries both halves: the weakness and the ease.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[TE-LEX-C92-DOCTOR-01, TE-LEX-C92-DOCTOR-02, TE-LEX-C92-DOCTOR-03, TE-LEX-C92-DOCTOR-04, TE-LEX-C92-DOCTOR-05, TE-LEX-C93-FINE-01, TE-LEX-C93-FINE-02, TE-LEX-C93-FINE-03, TE-LEX-C93-FINE-04, TE-LEX-C93-FINE-05, TE-LEX-C95-ASK-04, TE-LEX-C95-ASK-05] -->

[PAUSE 2s each]
- [YOU SAY: ask where the hospital is; say you have been hurt]
- [YOU SAY: tell a friend to take the medicine]
- [YOU SAY: a week later: ask an elder if they are keeping well, and a guest if they are comfortable]
- [YOU SAY: agree gladly; then, when a word fails, ask to be shown, or say you could not hear]
