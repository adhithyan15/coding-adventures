## You'll want to know: ひねる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C448-STIFF448-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ひねる** — *hineru* — "to twist".

> **ころんで、あしくびを ひねりました。** — *koronde, ashikubi o hinerimashita* — "I fell over and twisted my ankle."

Polite **ひねります**, *hinerimasu*; a godan verb. Past (polite): **せんしゅが こしを ひねりました。**, *senshu ga koshi o hinerimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ひねる** (*hineru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji spelling was attached to it later (JMdict).
