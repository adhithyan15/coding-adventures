## You'll want to know: うながす
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C341-SETTOKU341-02]; assesses=[] -->

**うながす** — *unagasu* — "to urge; to prompt".

> **じかんが ないので、せんせいが がくせいを うながしました。** — *jikan ga nai node, sensei ga gakusei o unagashimashita* — "There was no time, so the teacher hurried the students along."

Polite **うながします**, *unagashimasu*; a godan verb. Past (polite): **せんせいが うながしました。**, *sensei ga unagashimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**うながす** (*unagasu*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, and its older origin is not settled. Its kanji was attached to it later as a kun'yomi, a native reading given to a Chinese character (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
