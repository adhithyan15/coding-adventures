## You'll want to know: залог
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C429-ZASELENIYE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**залог** (*zalóg*) — "a deposit (security)".

> При заселении мы оставили залог.
>
> *Pri zaselénii my ostávili zalóg.*
>
> When we checked in we left a deposit.

Masculine. *Под залог* — against a deposit; also on bail.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*залог* is built from *за-* and *-лог*, the root of *положить* 'to lay': something laid down as a pledge. EtymDB lists the word with the senses 'pledge, deposit, security'.
