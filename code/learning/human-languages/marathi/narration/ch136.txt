Lesson 1 of 5.
कोट (koṭ) — a coat.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for study, then the Marathi for a shirt.

You'll want to know: कोट.
कोट — koṭ — "a coat".

The word, taken apart.
कोट (koṭ) is a loan from English coat. It is not the inherited Marathi koṭ, "fort".

Lesson 3 of 5.
पोळी (poḷī) — a chapati.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a coat, then the Marathi for a jumper.

You'll want to know: पोळी.
पोळी — poḷī — "a chapati".

The word, taken apart.
पोळी (poḷī) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from pauli, "a cake of scorched grain and ghee" (CDIAL 8423). CDIAL glosses the Marathi word "wheaten cake", and suggests, with a "perhaps", a link with Prakrit paülaï, "cooks", or with pūpalā, "a cake".

Lesson 5 of 5.
वरण (varaṇ) — plain dal.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a chapati, then the Marathi for a vegetable dish.

You'll want to know: वरण.
वरण — varaṇ — "plain dal".

The word, taken apart.
CDIAL lists Marathi varaṇ, "tasty dish of pulse", under a reconstructed aparānna, built from Sanskrit apara, "other, later", and anna, "food" (CDIAL 442). CDIAL calls the link very doubtful: the loss of the first a- and the single -ṇ- are not explained.
