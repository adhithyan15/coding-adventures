# सापडणे (sāpaḍṇe) — to be found; to turn up

## You'll want to know: सापडणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C301-DISNE301-02]; assesses=[] -->

**सापडणे** — *sāpaḍṇe* — "to be found; to turn up".

> बागेत मला एक सोन्याची अंगठी सापडली.
>
> *bāget malā ek sonyācī aṅgṭhī sāpaḍlī.*
>
> I found a gold ring in the garden.

Past (to him …): त्याला सापडलं, *tyālā sāpaḍlaṁ*.

A dative-subject verb like दिसणे: मला किल्ली सापडली, I found the key; चोर सापडला, the thief was caught.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सापडणे** (*sāpaḍṇe*) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Marathi *sāpaḍṇe*, 'to fall into, be got hold of', under Sanskrit *saṁpatati*, 'flies together, reaches, happens' (CDIAL 12930).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
