## You'll want to know: 原来
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C537-TURNS537-02]; assesses=[] -->

**原来** — *yuánlái* — "so (it turns out); as it turns out".

> **原来你也在这儿** *yuánlái nǐ yě zài zhèr* — So you're here too!

The new character, in pieces: 原 — ten strokes: the cliff, a bar and a long stroke falling to the left, as at the top of 愿; under it 白, five strokes, and 小, three strokes: 愿 without its heart. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**原来** (*yuánlái*) is built from 原 and 来 'to come', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *ngjwon* and *loj* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription). The dictionary glosses the first as wide flat land, a plain; the sense 'origin' is not given there, and how the pair came to mean 'as it turns out' is not settled.
