Russian, chapter 314: Fireworks.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
салют (salyút) — a firework display; a salute.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a decoration, then the Russian for to dress up.

You'll want to know: салют.
салют (salyút) — "a firework display; a salute".
В полночь начался салют. V pólnoch nachalsyá salyút. At midnight the firework display began.
Праздничный салют (salyút) — a festive firework display.

The word, taken apart.
салют (salyút) is usually traced to French salut "greeting", from Latin salūs "health, safety" (Lewis & Short); EtymDB glosses the Russian word "salute; fireworks" but lists no parent.

Мы смотрели фейерверк с балкона. My smotréli feyervérk s balkóna. We watched the fireworks from the balcony.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
фейерверк (feyervérk) was borrowed from German Feuerwerk (EtymDB), from Feuer "fire" and Werk "work".

Lesson 3 of 5.
конфетти (konfettí) — confetti.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a firework display, then the Russian for fireworks.

You'll want to know: конфетти.
конфетти (konfettí) — "confetti".
Конфетти было везде. Konfettí býlo vezdé. There was confetti everywhere.
Neuter and indeclinable.

The word, taken apart.
конфетти (konfettí) was borrowed from Italian confetti (EtymDB), the plural of confetto "a sugared sweet", once thrown at carnivals; it shares its source, Latin conficere "to prepare", with конфета.

Warm-up.

Before the new one: say the Russian for fireworks, then the Russian for confetti.

You'll want to know: хлопушка.
хлопушка (khlopúshka) — "a party popper, a cracker".
Хлопушка громко хлопнула. Khlopúshka grómko khlópnula. The party popper went off with a loud bang.
Feminine; genitive plural хлопушек.

The word, taken apart.
хлопушка (khlopúshka) is built on хлопать "to bang, to clap", a word that imitates the sound; EtymDB lists no parent.

Мальчик держал красный шарик. Málchik derzhál krásnyy shárik. The boy was holding a red balloon.
