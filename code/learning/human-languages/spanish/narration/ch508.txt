Spanish, chapter 508: Cómo es.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
increíble — incredible, unbelievable.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for the inhabitant, then the Spanish for the debate.

You'll want to know: increíble.
increíble — "incredible, unbelievable".
¡Es increíble! It is incredible!

What you've built.
One more word for reading notices, forms and messages.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: increíble]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: increíble, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: a sentence of your own with increíble]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Spanish for the inhabitant, then the Spanish for the debate, then say increíble again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does increíble mean? ("Incredible, unbelievable".) Say it once more.
