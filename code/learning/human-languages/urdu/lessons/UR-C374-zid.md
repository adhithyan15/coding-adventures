## You'll want to know: ضد
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C374-SAHARA374-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ضد** — *zid* — "stubbornness; insistence".

> اگر تم نے ضد کی تو کچھ نہیں ملے گا۔
>
> *agar tum ne zid kī to kuchh nahīṅ mile gā.*
>
> If you are stubborn, you will get nothing.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ضد** (*zid*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *ḍidd*, "the opposite"; Urdu uses it for stubborn insistence, a sense our sources do not trace.
