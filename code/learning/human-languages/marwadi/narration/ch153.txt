Marwadi (Marwari), chapter 153: Am, Is, Are.
5 lessons. You can do the first 3 of them in the car; after that you will want to have stopped.


Lesson 1 of 5.
हूं and है — am and is.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say I am fine, then he is ill. You have said am and is since the greeting; now they get a name.

Grammar Lens: हूं … हूं.
In हूं ठीक हूं। the first हूं is I and the last one is am: the same word, at both ends.
हूं अठै हूं। hū̃ aṭhai hū̃. — I am here.

Grammar Lens: है.
Anyone or anything you talk about takes है, is: वो, राम, the tea.
राम बठै है। rām baṭhai hai. — Ram is there.
चाय गरम है। chāy garam hai. — The tea is hot.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: हूं अठै हूं पण राम बठै है। — hū̃ aṭhai hū̃ paṇ rām baṭhai hai. — I am here, but Ram is there.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: हूं राजी हूं क्यूंकै चाय गरम है। — hū̃ rājī hū̃ kyū̃kai chāy garam hai. — I am happy because the tea is hot.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: वो उदास है। — vo udās hai. — He is sad.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
I am ends on? (हूं.) He is? (है.)
[question — say your answer, then pause 20 seconds]
Say that you are here, but Ram is there.


Lesson 2 of 5.
आप … हो (āp … ho) — you are.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Ask the polite question: आप कैसो हो? Then say I am here and Ram is there.

Grammar Lens: हो after आप.
आप कैसो हो? ends on हो. After आप, are is हो:
आप अठै हो। āp aṭhai ho. — You are here.
हो answers to आप the way हूं answers to हूं, and है to वो.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: आप बठै हो पण हूं अठै हूं। — āp baṭhai ho paṇ hū̃ aṭhai hū̃. — You are there, but I am here.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: आप राजी हो। — āp rājī ho. — You are happy.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: चाय अर पाणी? — chāy ar pāṇī? — Tea or water?]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: पाणी। — pāṇī. — Water.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
Which are goes with आप? (हो.) And with वो? (है.)
[question — say your answer, then pause 12 seconds]
Say politely: you are here.


Lesson 3 of 5.
ऊंट (ū̃ṭ) — a camel.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say a cow, a horse and an elephant. (गाय, घोड़ो, हाथी.)

You'll want to know: ऊंट.
ऊंट — ū̃ṭ — "a camel".
वो ऊंट है। vo ū̃ṭ hai. — That is a camel.
It opens on a letter you have not written yet: the long ū, standing alone. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

The word, taken apart.
ऊंट (ū̃ṭ) is inherited from Sanskrit uṣṭra, which named a buffalo as well as a camel. CDIAL lists Old Marwari uṭa in the same entry (CDIAL 2387).

What you've built.
ऊंट is masculine, and takes है like anything you talk about: ऊंट अठै है। (ū̃ṭ aṭhai hai.), the camel is here.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: ū̃ṭ]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: वो ऊंट है। — vo ū̃ṭ hai. — That is a camel.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say a cow, then ū̃ṭ again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does ऊंट (ū̃ṭ) mean? ("A camel".) Say it once more.
[question — say your answer, then pause 10 seconds]
Say the Marwadi for 'a camel'.


Lesson 4 of 5.
ऊ (ū) — the letter that opens ऊंट (ū̃ṭ).

Before we start: this one needs your hands, so it is not a driving lesson. You will want a pen and something to write on, your eyes, for letter shapes on the page and your eyes or your hands for the steps that ask you to read, point, write or gesture. You can listen to everything else now. I will say so again when we reach them, but leave these sections until you have stopped: Warm-up, Script you'll notice: ऊ, Guided Practice and Wrap-up Recall.

[once you have stopped driving — this part needs your eyes or your hands; if you are driving, skip ahead to the next part]
Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: write उ once, and name it. Then say हूं (hū̃): its long ū is the sign ू.
[pause 2 seconds]
Now say ऊंट (ū̃ṭ), "a camel". It opens on the long vowel standing alone, and this lesson puts that letter in your hand.

[once you have stopped driving — this part needs your eyes or your hands; if you are driving, skip ahead to the next part]
Script you'll notice: ऊ.
ऊ — ū, the long u.
It opens ऊंट (ū̃ṭ). It is the letter उ with one more part: a separate loop on its right. उ and ऊ (ū) pair the way the signs ु and ू do, short and long.

Writing: ऊ.
1. curve down and left around the upper bowl, then sweep back through the waist and around the lower loop without lifting.
2. lift at the waist and sweep the right-hand loop up, around, and down-left.
3. lift and draw the top shirorekhā left-to-right.
Pen lifts: 2.
verified modern printed teaching form; everyday handwriting may simplify the curves.
Stroke order is one attested teaching order; hands and teachers vary. Source: Saurmandal, ‘Devanagari ऊ (ū) stroke order.svg’, panels 1–3, Wikimedia Commons, 5 August 2023.

[once you have stopped driving — this part needs your eyes or your hands; if you are driving, skip ahead to the next part]
Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[once you have stopped driving — look: at ऊंट (ū̃ṭ) and point at ऊ (ū) inside it]
[once you have stopped driving — trace: ऊ three times, saying ū as you finish each one]
[once you have stopped driving — write: उ, then ऊ (ū), from memory]
[once you have stopped driving — read: ऊंट (ū̃ṭ) aloud once more]

[once you have stopped driving — this part needs your eyes or your hands; if you are driving, skip ahead to the next part]
Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
Which letter is this — ऊ? (ū, the long u.) Which word did it come from? (ऊंट, ū̃ṭ, "a camel".)
[once you have stopped driving — question: Write the letter that opens ū̃ṭ (a camel).]


Lesson 5 of 5.
Am, is, are — a review.

Warm-up.
[pause 3 seconds]
Say I am here.

Guided Practice.
The three forms side by side, now that each one is taught:
[a table of 3 rows, read aloud]
who: हूं. to be: हूं.
who: आप. to be: हो.
who: वो, राम, ऊंट (ū̃ṭ). to be: है.
[pause 2 seconds after each]
[your turn — say: I am here; you are there; Ram is there]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: that is a camel; the camel is here]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: I am happy because the tea is hot]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: you are there, but I am here]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: tea or water?, and answer tea]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[once you have stopped driving — read: ऊंट (ū̃ṭ), and name its first letter]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
You are? (आप … हो (āp … ho).) The camel is? (ऊंट (ū̃ṭ) … है.) I am? (हूं … हूं.)
[question — say your answer, then pause 20 seconds]
Say: you are here, but the camel is there.
