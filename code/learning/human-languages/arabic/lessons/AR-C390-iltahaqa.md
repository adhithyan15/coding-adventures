## You'll want to know: التحق
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C390-KULLIYYA390-05]; assesses=[] -->

**التحق** — *iltaḥaqa* — "to enrol".

> التحق ابني بالجامعة هذه السنة.
>
> *iltaḥaqa ibnī bi-l-jāmiʿa hādhihi as-sana.*
>
> My son enrolled at the university this year.

Present: يلتحق, *yaltaḥiq*.

It takes بـ: التحق بـ ….

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**التحق** (*iltaḥaqa*) is built on the native Arabic root *l-ḥ-q*, "to overtake, to catch up" (Lane). It is Form VIII, to attach oneself to: to join, to enrol.
