# હતો — *was*, and an ending that listens to the subject

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-GRAMMAR-C146-POLAR-SHUN-01, GU-GRAMMAR-C146-POSS-AGREE-01, GU-LEX-C101-DEF101-05] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Ask *do you live here?* Then say *my brother* and *my sister*: their
endings **-ો** and **-ી** are about to come back. And the word for *there*:
**ત્યાં**.

## Grammar Lens: છે becomes હતો
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-GRAMMAR-C147-HATO-01]; assesses=[GU-LEX-C101-DEF101-05, GU-CONCEPT-C03-HUN-01] -->

> **હું ત્યાં હતો.** — *hũ tyā̃ hato.* — I was there.

In the present the copula changes for the person: **છું**, **છે**, **છો**. In
the past it stops caring about the person and starts caring about gender, with
the endings *my* uses:

> **હું ત્યાં હતી.** — *hũ tyā̃ hatī.* — I was there, said by a woman.

## You'll want to know: હતું and હતા
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-GRAMMAR-C147-HATU-HATA-01]; assesses=[GU-LEX-GHAR-01, GU-LEX-C72-SIZE-02, GU-LEX-C145-NEG145-01, GU-LEX-C101-DEF101-04] -->

A neuter subject takes **હતું**. More than one person, or respect, takes **હતા**:

> **ઘર નાનું હતું.** — *ghar nānũ hatũ.* — The house was small.
> **તેઓ અહીં હતા.** — *teo ahī̃ hatā.* — They were here.

- **હતો** — he, or a man saying *I*;
- **હતી** — she, or a woman saying *I*;
- **હતું** — a neuter thing;
- **હતા** — more than one, or respect.

**મારો મારી મારું મારા** and **હતો હતી હતું હતા** end alike, sign for sign.
