Telugu, chapter 215: Desert, Rock, Open ground, Theatre, Stadium.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ఎడారి (eḍāri) — a desert.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a capital city, then the Telugu for a district.

You'll want to know: ఎడారి.
ఎడారి — eḍāri — "a desert".

The word, taken apart.
ఎడారి (eḍāri) is Dravidian as far as DEDR records it: DEDR 781 lists eḍāri, "a desert, wilderness", and notes that Tamil eṭār, "open plain", was borrowed from Telugu.

Lesson 3 of 5.
మైదానం (maidānaṁ) — an open ground, a plain.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a desert, then the Telugu for a rock.

You'll want to know: మైదానం.
మైదానం — maidānaṁ — "an open ground, a plain".

The word, taken apart.
మైదానం (maidānaṁ) is a loan from Persian maidān, "open plain, field", which came through Hindustani and took the Telugu ending -ṁ.
