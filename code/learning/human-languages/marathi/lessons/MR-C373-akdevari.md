## You'll want to know: आकडेवारी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C373-PADDHAT373-03]; assesses=[] -->

**आकडेवारी** — *ākḍevārī* — "statistics; figures".

> सर्वेक्षणाची आकडेवारी उद्या जाहीर होईल.
>
> *sarvekṣaṇācī ākḍevārī udyā jāhīr hoīl.*
>
> The survey's figures will be announced tomorrow.

Feminine.

आकडा, a figure, + -वारी: figures taken together.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**आकडेवारी** (*ākḍevārī*) is built on आकडा, "a figure, a number", with the ending -वारी; the origin of आकडा is not settled.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
