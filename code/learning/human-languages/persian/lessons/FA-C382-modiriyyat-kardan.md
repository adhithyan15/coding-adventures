## You'll want to know: مدیریت کردن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C382-PROZHE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**مدیریت کردن** (*modiriyat kardan*) — "to manage, to run".

> مادرم یک رستوران کوچک را مدیریت میکند.
>
> *mâdaram yek resturân-e kuchek râ modiriyat mikonad.*
>
> My mother runs a small restaurant.

*Modiriyyat* "management", built on *modir* "manager", + *kardan*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**مدیریت** is built on *modir* "manager", borrowed from Arabic *mudīr*, with the abstract ending *-iyat*; *kardan* "to do" is the inherited Persian verb.
