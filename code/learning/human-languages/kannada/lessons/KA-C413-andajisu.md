# ಅಂದಾಜಿಸು (andājisu) — to estimate

## You'll want to know: ಅಂದಾಜಿಸು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C413-PANA413-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ಅಂದಾಜಿಸು** — *andājisu* — "to estimate".

> ಈ ಕೆಲಸಕ್ಕೆ ಹತ್ತು ದಿನ ಬೇಕು ಅಂತ ನಾನು ಅಂದಾಜಿಸಿದೆ.
>
> *ī kelasakke hattu dina bēku anta nānu andājiside.*
>
> I estimated that this job needs ten days.

Past (I …): ಅಂದಾಜಿಸಿದೆ, *andājiside*.

ಅಂದಾಜು, an estimate; ಅಂದಾಜು ಮಾಡು, to guess.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಅಂದಾಜಿಸು** (*andājisu*) is built on *andāju*, "an estimate", a loan from Persian *andāza*, "a measure", through Hindustani, with the Kannada verb ending -*isu*.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
