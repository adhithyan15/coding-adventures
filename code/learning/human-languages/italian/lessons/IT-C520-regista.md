## You'll want to know: il regista
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C520-RECITARE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**il regista** — "director".

> Il regista ha cambiato la fine dello spettacolo.
>
> *The director changed the ending of the play.*

The same form for a woman: *la regista*. The work is *la regia*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*regista* is built on *regia* "direction", usually traced to French *régie* "management", from *régir* "to govern", from Latin *regere* "to rule" (EtymDB, under French *régie*).
