Urdu, chapter 264: Where I Went, and Where I Was.
3 lessons. All 3 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 3.
وہ گیا (voh gayā) — the past, by the one who went.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say "pleased to meet you". (āp se mil kar khushī huī.) Its last word, ہوئی (huī), "happened", is a past. This chapter writes the past in Urdu script.

You'll want to know: the past follows the one who went.
وہ گیا۔ voh gayā. — "He went."
وہ گئی۔ voh gaī. — "She went."
گیا (gayā) is جانا (jānā) in the past, with no ہے (hai) after it. Its ending follows the one who went, as اچھا (achchhā) follows its noun:
وہ گھر گیا۔ voh ghar gayā. — "He went home."
سارا گھر گئی۔ Sārā ghar gaī. — "Sara went home."
ہم بازار گئے۔ ham bāzār gae. — "We went to the market."
[a table of 4 rows, read aloud]
who went: a man. the past: گیا (gayā).
who went: a woman. the past: گئی (gaī).
who went: men; ہم; آپ. the past: گئے (gae).
who went: women. the past: گئیں (gaīṅ).

You'll want to know: how a verb takes the ending.
Most verbs put the ending on the stem: بیٹھنا (baiṭhnā) gives بیٹھا (baiṭhā) and بیٹھی (baiṭhī).
وہ بیٹھی۔ voh baiṭhī. — "She sat down."
A stem that ends in a vowel adds -یا for a man and -ئی for a woman:
وہ کل آیا۔ voh kal āyā. — "He came yesterday."
سارا کل آئی۔ Sārā kal āī. — "Sara came yesterday."
سونا (sonā) gives سویا (soyā), and اٹھنا (uṭhnā) gives اٹھا (uṭhā). ہونا (honā) gives ہوا (huā) and ہوئی (huī): کیا ہوا؟ (kyā huā?), "what happened?" A bare stem with گیا says a change is complete: وہ سو گیا (voh so gayā), "he fell asleep". "Did not" puts نہیں (nahīṅ) before the verb:
میں نہیں گیا۔ maiṅ nahīṅ gayā. — "I didn't go."

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: "He came home" — voh ghar āyā]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: "She came home" — voh ghar āī]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: "We came home" — ham ghar āe]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[question — say your answer, then pause 22 seconds]
Say that he went home, then that Sara came yesterday.

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
"She went"? (وہ گئی.) "He sat down"? (وہ بیٹھا.) "What happened?" (کیا ہوا؟) Who decides the ending? (The one who went.)


Lesson 2 of 3.
وہ یہاں تھا (voh yahāṅ thā) — was, were, used to.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say "he went" and "she came". (voh gayā, voh āī.) Then "he is here". (voh yahāṅ hai.) Now put ہے (hai) into the past.

You'll want to know: تھا, تھی, تھے.
وہ یہاں تھا۔ voh yahāṅ thā. — "He was here."
وہ یہاں تھی۔ voh yahāṅ thī. — "She was here."
تھا (thā) is ہے in the past. Like گیا, it follows the person:
میں گھر پر تھا۔ maiṅ ghar par thā. — "I was at home."
سارا بیمار تھی۔ Sārā bīmār thī. — "Sara was ill."
ہم اسکول میں تھے۔ ham iskūl meṅ the. — "We were at school."
[a table of 4 rows, read aloud]
who: a man. was: تھا (thā).
who: a woman. was: تھی (thī).
who: men; ہم; آپ. was: تھے (the).
who: women. was: تھیں (thīṅ).
کل آپ کہاں تھے؟ kal āp kahāṅ the? — "Where were you yesterday?"

You'll want to know: what I used to do.
Keep the present جاتا (jātā) and put تھا where ہے stood. That is a habit in the past, "used to":
میں اسکول جاتا تھا۔ maiṅ iskūl jātā thā. — "I used to go to school."
وہ ہر روز آتی تھی۔ voh har roz ātī thī. — "She used to come every day."
Both halves follow the person: جاتی تھی (jātī thī) for a woman, جاتے تھے (jāte the) for men.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: "I was at the office" — maiṅ daftar meṅ thā]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: "She was at the office" — voh daftar meṅ thī]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: "We were at the office" — ham daftar meṅ the]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[question — say your answer, then pause 24 seconds]
Say that Sara was ill, then that you used to go to school.

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
"She was ill"? (وہ بیمار تھی.) "We were at home"? (ہم گھر پر تھے.) "I used to go"? (میں جاتا تھا, or جاتی تھی.) Which word decides تھا or تھی? (The person.)


Lesson 3 of 3.
Review — where I went, and where I was.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say "he went" and "she was here". (voh gayā, voh yahāṅ thī.)

Guided Practice.
[pause 2 seconds after each]
[your turn — say: Yesterday I went to the market. Sara went home.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: He came, and then he sat down.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: I didn't go. I was ill.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: Where were you yesterday? We were at home.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: I used to go to school every day.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
وہ گئی or وہ گیا (voh gayā), for Sara? (گئی.) Now the other way round: what does تھے tell you about the people? (Men, or ہم, or آپ.) "I used to come"? (میں آتا تھا.) In every one, who decides the ending? (The one who went, or who was.)
