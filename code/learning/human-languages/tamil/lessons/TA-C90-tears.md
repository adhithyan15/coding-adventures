# கண்ணீர் (kaṇṇīr) — tears

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[TA-LEX-C90-MOOD-01, TA-LEX-C90-MOOD-02] -->

## You'll want to know: கண்ணீர்
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C90-MOOD-03]; assesses=[] -->

**கண்ணீர்** (*kaṇṇīr*) — "tears", literally "eye-water".

It is **கண்**, "eye", and **நீர்**, "water".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

Tears, made of eye and water.
