## You'll want to know: सुविधा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C415-BICHANA415-01]; assesses=[] -->

**सुविधा** — *suvidhā* — "an amenity".

> या विश्रामगृहात सगळ्या सुविधा आहेत.
>
> *yā viśrāmgṛhāt sagaḷyā suvidhā āhet.*
>
> This guest house has all the amenities.

Feminine.

A feminine noun; the plural is the same in the direct case.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सुविधा** (*suvidhā*) is a modern word built from Sanskrit *su-*, "good", and *vidhā*, "arrangement, manner" (Monier-Williams).
