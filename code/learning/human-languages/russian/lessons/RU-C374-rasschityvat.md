## You'll want to know: рассчитывать
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C374-OZHIDANIYA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**рассчитывать** (*rasschítyvat*) — "to count on, to rely on; to calculate".

> Мы рассчитываем на твою помощь.
>
> *My rasschítyvayem na tvoyú pómoshch.*
>
> We are counting on your help.

Imperfective; perfective *рассчитать* (to calculate). *Рассчитывать на* + accusative — to count on.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**рассчитывать** is built from *рас-* and *считать* "to count", on the root of *число* "number", Proto-Slavic *\*čislo* (EtymDB). To count on someone was first to reckon them in.
