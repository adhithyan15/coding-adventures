## You'll want to know: सान्त्वना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C548-SANTVANA548-03]; assesses=[] -->

**सान्त्वना** — *sāntvanā* — "consolation".

> यदि भ्राता दुःखी अस्ति, तर्हि तस्मै सान्त्वनां ददातु।
>
> *yadi bhrātā duḥkhī asti, tarhi tasmai sāntvanāṃ dadātu.*
>
> If your brother is unhappy, offer him comfort.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सान्त्वना** (*sāntvanā*) comes from the root √sāntv, "to console, to conciliate"; MW gives *sāntvana*, beside *sāntvanā*, as soothing with kind words, consolation (MW).
