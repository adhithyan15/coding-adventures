Gujarati, chapter 246: Marks and results.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
પરિણામ (pariṇām) — result, outcome.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for eraser, then the Gujarati for to pronounce.

You'll want to know: પરિણામ.
પરિણામ — pariṇām — "result, outcome".
આજે પરિણામ આવે છે. — āje pariṇām āve che — "The results come out today."
A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
પરિણામ (pariṇām) is Sanskrit pariṇāma, "change, outcome", taken over whole as a tatsama; EtymDB lists it as borrowed from Sanskrit, and CDIAL has no entry for it (EtymDB).

Lesson 2 of 5.
ગુણ (guṇ) — marks; quality.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to pronounce, then the Gujarati for result.

You'll want to know: ગુણ.
ગુણ — guṇ — "marks; quality".
તે ગણિતમાં સારા ગુણ લાવે છે. — te gaṇitmā̃ sārā guṇ lāve che — "He gets good marks in maths."
A masculine noun; it also means a good quality.

The word, taken apart.
ગુણ (guṇ) is Sanskrit guṇa, "a strand of a cord" and then "quality, good quality", taken over as a tatsama (CDIAL 4190).

Lesson 3 of 5.
નાપાસ થવું (nāpās thavũ) — to fail an exam.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for result, then the Gujarati for marks.

You'll want to know: નાપાસ થવું.
નાપાસ થવું — nāpās thavũ — "to fail an exam".
તે ગણિતમાં નાપાસ થાય છે. — te gaṇitmā̃ nāpās thāy che — "He fails in maths."

The word, taken apart.
નાપાસ થવું (nāpās thavũ) is built from nāpās, "not passed", with the verb thavũ, "to become". Nāpās joins nā, "not", usually traced to Persian, to a loan from English pass.

Lesson 4 of 5.
અભ્યાસ (abhyās) — studies, study.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for marks, then the Gujarati for to fail an exam.

You'll want to know: અભ્યાસ.
અભ્યાસ — abhyās — "studies, study".
તે રોજ બે કલાક અભ્યાસ કરે છે. — te roj be kalāk abhyās kare che — "She studies for two hours every day."
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
અભ્યાસ (abhyās) is Sanskrit abhyāsa, "repetition, practice", taken over whole as a tatsama. CDIAL lists it under the verb abhyasyati, "practises", with Old Gujarati ahīāsivaũ, "to practise" (CDIAL 546).

Lesson 5 of 5.
મહેનત કરવી (mahenat karvī) — to work hard.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to fail an exam, then the Gujarati for studies.

You'll want to know: મહેનત કરવી.
મહેનત કરવી — mahenat karvī — "to work hard".
અમે પરિણામ માટે મહેનત કરીએ છીએ. — ame pariṇām māṭe mahenat karīe chīe — "We work hard for the results."

The word, taken apart.
મહેનત કરવી (mahenat karvī) is built from mahenat, "hard work", a loan from Arabic miḥnat, "trial, affliction", which reached Gujarati through Persian (Lane), and the verb karvī, "to do".
