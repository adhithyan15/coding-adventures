## You'll want to know: le klaxon
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C402-VITRES-03]; assesses=[] -->

**le klaxon** — "the horn (of a car)".

> Dans les rues étroites, on entend souvent le klaxon des taxis.
>
> *In the narrow streets you often hear the taxis' horns.*

First a trade name; now an ordinary noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*klaxon* was borrowed from English *klaxon* (EtymDB), first the trade name of an electric car horn.
