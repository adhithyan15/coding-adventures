## You'll want to know: पार्टी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C370-SABHA370-04]; assesses=[] -->

**पार्टी** — *pārṭī* — "a political party; a party".

> उसकी पार्टी चुनाव जीत गई।
>
> *uskī pārṭī cunāv jīt gaī.*
>
> His party won the election.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पार्टी** (*pārṭī*) is a loan from English *party*.
