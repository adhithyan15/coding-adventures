## You'll want to know: акварель
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C521-PAINTING-03]; assesses=[] -->

**акварель** (*akvarél*) — "watercolour".

> Она рисует акварелью.
>
> *Oná risúyet akvarélyu.*
>
> She paints in watercolour.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*акварель* was borrowed from French *aquarelle* (EtymDB), which is usually traced to Italian *acquerello*, built on *acqua* 'water', from Latin *aqua*.
