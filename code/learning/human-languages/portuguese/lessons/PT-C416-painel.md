## You'll want to know: o painel
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C416-ATRASOS-01]; assesses=[] -->

**o painel** — "the board, the panel".

> O painel da estação mostra as partidas.
>
> *The station board shows the departures.*

*O painel solar* — the solar panel. *O painel* of a car is the dashboard.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*painel* came from Old French *panel*, from Vulgar Latin *pannellus*, a small form of Latin *pannus* 'piece of cloth' (EtymDB).
