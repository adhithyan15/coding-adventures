## You'll want to know: il burrone
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C462-MONTAGNA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**il burrone** — "the ravine, the gorge".

> Non avvicinarti al burrone!
>
> *Do not go near the ravine!*

Deep and steep.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*burrone* is built from *borro* 'ditch, gully' and the big-size ending *-one* (EtymDB); the deeper origin of *borro* is not settled.
