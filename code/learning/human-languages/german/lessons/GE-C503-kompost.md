## You'll want to know: der Kompost
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C503-GARTEN-05]; assesses=[] -->

**der Kompost** — "compost".

> Die Schalen von Obst und Gemüse kommen auf den Kompost.
>
> *Fruit and vegetable peel goes on the compost.*

Plural, rare: *die Komposte*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Kompost* was borrowed from French *compost* (EtymDB), which goes back to Latin *compositum* "something put together", from *compōnere*.
