## You'll want to know: زائچہ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C535-SHAGUN535-05]; assesses=[] -->

**زائچہ** — *zāichā* — "a horoscope".

> اگر نجومی نے زائچہ بنایا تو وہ پیسے مانگے گا۔
>
> *agar najūmī ne zāichā banāyā to voh paise māṅge gā.*
>
> If the astrologer draws up a horoscope, he will ask for money.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**زائچہ** (*zāichā*) is usually traced to Persian *zāyicha*, "a horoscope", a chart of the stars at a birth.
