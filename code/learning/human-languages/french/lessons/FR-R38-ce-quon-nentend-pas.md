## Guided Practice: the distant band — everything, in the plural and the feminine

## Guided Practice: and none of it
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[FR-GRAM-PAS-DE-01, FR-GRAMMAR-NEGATION-04, FR-GRAM-DU-PARTITIF-07, FR-GRAM-DE-LA-01, FR-GRAM-DES-01, FR-GRAM-NE-CHUTE-01, FR-LEX-PAIN-01, FR-LEX-LAIT-05, FR-LEX-EAU-03, FR-LEX-CAFE-01, FR-LEX-ILYA-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s]
- [YOU SAY: the four shapes, then their one negative — "du, de la, de l', des" →

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[FR-PHON-CONSONNE-FINALE-01, FR-GRAM-PLURIEL-S-01, FR-GRAM-FEMININ-E-01, FR-GRAM-PLURIEL-X-01, FR-GRAM-PAS-DE-01] -->

[PAUSE 4s] Why is the plural *-s* silent? (**Because every final consonant
is.**) Why is the feminine *-e* audible even though it is silent? (**It stops the
consonant before it being final.**) Why is *chevaux* not irregular? (**Its *l*
became a *u***.) And what do all four partitives become after a negative? (**A
bare *de*.**)
