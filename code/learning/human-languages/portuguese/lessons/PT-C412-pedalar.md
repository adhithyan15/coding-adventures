## You'll want to know: pedalar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C412-RODAS-04]; assesses=[] -->

**pedalar** — "to pedal, to cycle".

> Pedalamos ao longo do rio todos os domingos.
>
> *We cycle along the river every Sunday.*

Built on *o pedal*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*pedalar* is built on *pedal*, a learned borrowing from Latin *pedālis* 'of the foot' (EtymDB).
