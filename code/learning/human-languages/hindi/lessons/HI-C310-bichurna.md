## You'll want to know: बिछुड़ना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C310-BICHHUD310-01]; assesses=[] -->

**बिछुड़ना** — *bichuṛnā* — "to be separated".

> मेले में एक बच्चा अपनी माँ से बिछुड़ गया।
>
> *mele meṁ ek baccā apnī māṁ se bichuṛ gayā.*
>
> At the fair a child got separated from his mother.

Past (he …): वह बच्चा बिछुड़ गया, *vah baccā bichuṛ gayā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**बिछुड़ना** (*bichuṛnā*) is inherited from *\*vikṣuṭati*, a form CDIAL reconstructs (CDIAL 11651). The one you are parted from takes **से**: **माँ से बिछुड़ गया**.
