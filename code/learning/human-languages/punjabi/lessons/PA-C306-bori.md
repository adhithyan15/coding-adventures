## You'll want to know: ਬੋਰੀ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C306-WINNOW306-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ਬੋਰੀ** — *borī* — "a sack".

> ਇੱਕ ਬੋਰੀ ਵਿੱਚ ਬਹੁਤ ਕਣਕ ਹੁੰਦੀ ਹੈ।
>
> *ikk borī vich bahut kaṇak hundī hai.*
>
> A sack holds a lot of wheat.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਬੋਰੀ** (*borī*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*bōra*, "sacking, sack"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *borī*, "sack, coarse bag", under it (CDIAL 9320).
