## You'll want to know: fēnzhōng
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C143-CLOCK143-02]; assesses=[] -->

*fēnzhōng* — "minute (a length of time)".

> *shí fēnzhōng yǐhòu wǒmen zǒu* — We're leaving in ten minutes.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*fēnzhōng* is built from *fēn*, the minute of the clock, and *zhōng* 'bell', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *pjun* and *tsyowng* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription); the dictionary glosses the second as a musical instrument.
