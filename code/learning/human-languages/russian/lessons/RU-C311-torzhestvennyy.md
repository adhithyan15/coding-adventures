## You'll want to know: торжественный
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C311-PRAZDNOVAT-04]; assesses=[] -->

**торжественный** (*torzhéstvennyy*) — "solemn, ceremonial".

> Это был торжественный момент.
>
> *Éto byl torzhéstvennyy momént.*
>
> It was a solemn moment.

*Торжественно* — solemnly.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*торжественный* is built on *торжество* "a celebration", inherited from Old East Slavic *tŭržĭstvo* (EtymDB).
