Malayalam, chapter 227: To note down, To be present, To take part, To lose one's way, To park.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
കുറിച്ചെടുക്കുക (kuṟicceṭukkuka) — to note down.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for to finish, then the Malayalam for to check.

You'll want to know: കുറിച്ചെടുക്കുക.
കുറിച്ചെടുക്കുക — kuṟicceṭukkuka — "to note down".

The word, taken apart.
കുറിച്ചെടുക്കുക (kuṟicceṭukkuka) is native Dravidian, "take up having noted": the verb "to mark, note, write" (DEDR 1847, with Tamil kuṟi) and the verb "to take up" (DEDR 851).

Lesson 2 of 5.
ഹാജരാകുക (hājarākuka) — to be present, to attend.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for to check, then the Malayalam for to note down.

You'll want to know: ഹാജരാകുക.
ഹാജരാകുക — hājarākuka — "to be present, to attend".

The word, taken apart.
ഹാജരാകുക (hājarākuka) joins hājar, "present, in attendance", from Arabic ḥāḍir by way of Hindustani, to native ākuka, "to become" (DEDR 333). DEDR 333 compares Sanskrit ām.
