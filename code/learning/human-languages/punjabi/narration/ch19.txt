Punjabi, chapter 19: The Third Form Line: Residence.
6 lessons. The first lesson already needs your eyes or your hands, so save this one for when you have stopped.


Lesson 1 of 6.
Select one known residence value — select between two separately practised residence values.

Before we start: this one needs your hands, so it is not a driving lesson. You will want a pen and something to write on, your eyes, for letter shapes on the page and your eyes or your hands for the steps that ask you to read, point, write or gesture. You can listen to everything else now. I will say so again when we reach them, but leave these sections until you have stopped: Warm-up, Script — attach one cue to each value, Guided Practice and Wrap-up recall.

[once you have stopped driving — this part needs your eyes or your hands; if you are driving, skip ahead to the next part]
Warm-up.
Read the closed bank once:
A — ਪਿੰਡ B — ਸ਼ਹਿਰ.

[once you have stopped driving — this part needs your eyes or your hands; if you are driving, skip ahead to the next part]
Script — attach one cue to each value.
Point to A, then ਪਿੰਡ. Point to B, then ਸ਼ਹਿਰ. Cover the bank and say which value B requests. Selection is separate from spelling.

[once you have stopped driving — this part needs your eyes or your hands; if you are driving, skip ahead to the next part]
Guided Practice.
Reveal, point, cover, and select once more. Do not write the form line yet.

[once you have stopped driving — this part needs your eyes or your hands; if you are driving, skip ahead to the next part]
Wrap-up recall.
These cues belong only to this two-item bank.
[once you have stopped driving — question: In this closed practice bank, which Gurmukhi value is paired with B?]


Lesson 2 of 6.
Fill one supported residence field — fill one residence line with a visible two-value bank.

Before we start: this one needs your hands, so it is not a driving lesson. You will want a pen and something to write on. You can listen to everything else now. I will say so again when we reach it, but leave this section until you have stopped: Wrap-up recall.

Warm-up.
Keep this bank visible:
A — ਪਿੰਡ B — ਸ਼ਹਿਰ.

Writing — model visible.
Complete ਰਿਹਾਇਸ਼: __________ (residence field) for A. Read the label, select the A value, and copy it. This is supported entry, not independent writing evidence.

Guided Practice.
Compare selection, spelling, spacing, and placement in separate passes.

[once you have stopped driving — this part needs your eyes or your hands; if you are driving, skip ahead to the next part]
Wrap-up recall.
Stop after one supported line.
[once you have stopped driving — question: With the two-value bank visible, complete the residence field for A.]


Lesson 3 of 6.
Give the residence value its own space — separate the residence label and value clearly.

Before we start: this one needs your hands, so it is not a driving lesson. You will want a pen and something to write on and your eyes or your hands for the steps that ask you to read, point, write or gesture. You can listen to everything else now. I will say so again when we reach them, but leave these sections until you have stopped: Warm-up, Guided Practice and Wrap-up recall.

[once you have stopped driving — this part needs your eyes or your hands; if you are driving, skip ahead to the next part]
Warm-up.
Read ਰਿਹਾਇਸ਼: ਪਿੰਡ (residence field spacing). Point once to the label and once to the value.

Writing — one spacing pass.
Copy ਰਿਹਾਇਸ਼:, leave a clear space, then copy ਪਿੰਡ. Judge only the boundary after the colon.

[once you have stopped driving — this part needs your eyes or your hands; if you are driving, skip ahead to the next part]
Guided Practice.
Cover the model and repeat once. If the boundary is unclear, repair only the space.

[once you have stopped driving — this part needs your eyes or your hands; if you are driving, skip ahead to the next part]
Wrap-up recall.
Selection and memory are not scored in this spacing pass.
[once you have stopped driving — question: Copy the residence field with a clear space after the colon.]


Lesson 4 of 6.
Hide, wait, then fill the residence field — fill one residence line after hiding the known bank.

Before we start: this one needs your hands, so it is not a driving lesson. You will want a pen and something to write on. You can listen to everything else now. I will say so again when we reach it, but leave this section until you have stopped: Wrap-up recall.

Warm-up.
Read A — ਪਿੰਡ, B — ਸ਼ਹਿਰ once. Hide the bank and count slowly to ten.

Writing — short delayed entry.
With the bank hidden, complete ਰਿਹਾਇਸ਼: __________ (residence field after a short delay) for B. Finish the attempt before revealing the bank.

Guided Practice.
Reveal and compare selection, spelling, spacing, then placement. Do not repair yet.

[once you have stopped driving — this part needs your eyes or your hands; if you are driving, skip ahead to the next part]
Wrap-up recall.
One delayed line is enough.
[once you have stopped driving — question: After hiding the bank for ten seconds, complete the residence field for B.]


Lesson 5 of 6.
Repair one residence-field difference — repair one dimension of a completed residence field.

Before we start: this one needs your hands, so it is not a driving lesson. You will want a pen and something to write on. You can listen to everything else now. I will say so again when we reach it, but leave this section until you have stopped: Wrap-up recall.

Warm-up.
Compare ਰਿਹਾਇਸ਼:ਸ਼ਹਿਰ with ਰਿਹਾਇਸ਼: ਸ਼ਹਿਰ (residence field repair). The letters match; one boundary differs.

Writing — one repair pass.
Rewrite the first line with a clear space after the colon. Leave every letter unchanged.

Guided Practice.
Name the repaired dimension: selection, spelling, spacing, or placement. Stop after one repair.

[once you have stopped driving — this part needs your eyes or your hands; if you are driving, skip ahead to the next part]
Wrap-up recall.
The next lesson asks for a fresh line rather than a repair.
[once you have stopped driving — question: Repair only the spacing in ਰਿਹਾਇਸ਼:ਸ਼ਹਿਰ.]


Lesson 6 of 6.
One no-model residence field — choose and write one known residence value from a nonverbal cue with no model.

Before we start: this one needs your hands, so it is not a driving lesson. You will want a pen and something to write on. You can listen to everything else now. I will say so again when we reach it, but leave this section until you have stopped: Wrap-up recall.

Warm-up.
Close the previous lesson. There is no value bank, support-language label, or romanized answer below.

Writing — independent controlled choice.
Complete the requested field:
A — ਰਿਹਾਇਸ਼: __________ (residence field).
Choose the known value attached to A and write it from memory. Do not reopen an earlier lesson until the attempt is complete.

Guided Practice — separate repair passes.
After writing, compare selection, spelling, spacing, and placement. Repair only the first differing dimension.

[once you have stopped driving — this part needs your eyes or your hands; if you are driving, skip ahead to the next part]
Wrap-up recall.
This closes only the residence-field ladder. The remaining form fields are separate backlog work.
[once you have stopped driving — question: With no bank, romanization, or copyable answer, complete the residence field for A.]
