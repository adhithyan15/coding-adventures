Spanish, chapter 628: En el banco.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
el efectivo — the cash.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for to get into debt, then the Spanish for to invest.

You'll want to know: el efectivo.
el efectivo — "the cash".
¿Paga en efectivo o con tarjeta? Are you paying in cash or by card?
En efectivo — in cash. As an adjective it means effective.

The word, taken apart.
efectivo is a learned borrowing from Latin effectivus 'productive', built on effectus, from efficere 'to bring about' (Lewis & Short).

hipoteca is a learned borrowing from Latin hypotheca, taken from Greek hypothēkē 'a pledge, a deposit', built from hypo 'under' and thēkē 'a place to put things' (EtymDB).

Lesson 5 of 5.
retirar — to withdraw, to take out; to remove.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for the mortgage, then the Spanish for the interest.

You'll want to know: retirar.
retirar — "to withdraw, to take out; to remove".
Retiro dinero del cajero. I take money out of the cash machine.
In speech people often say sacar dinero. Retirarse — to retire, to withdraw.

The word, taken apart.
retirar is built on tirar 'to pull, to throw' with re-; tirar is usually traced to a Germanic verb meaning 'to tear' (EtymDB), but its origin is not settled.
