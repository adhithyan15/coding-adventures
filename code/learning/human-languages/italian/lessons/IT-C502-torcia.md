## You'll want to know: la torcia
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C502-CAMPEGGIO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**la torcia** — "torch, flashlight".

> Di notte usiamo la torcia per trovare la tenda.
>
> *At night we use the torch to find the tent.*

In speech also *la pila*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*torcia* is usually traced to Vulgar Latin *torca* "something twisted", from Latin *torquēre* "to twist" (Lewis & Short): a torch was twisted straw or rope.
