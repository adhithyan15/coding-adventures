## You'll want to know: die Konferenz
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C278-VERHANDELN-05]; assesses=[] -->

**die Konferenz** — "the conference".

> Die Konferenz war im Rathaus.
>
> *The conference was in the town hall.*

Plural: *die Konferenzen*. Stress at the end: *Kon-fe-RENZ*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Konferenz* comes from Medieval Latin *conferentia*, built on *conferre* "to bring together", from *con-* "together" and *ferre* "to carry" (EtymDB).
