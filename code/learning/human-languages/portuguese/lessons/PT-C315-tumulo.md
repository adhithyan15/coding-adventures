## You'll want to know: o túmulo
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C315-FUNERAL-05]; assesses=[] -->

**o túmulo** — "the tomb, the grave".

> No túmulo havia flores frescas.
>
> *There were fresh flowers on the grave.*

*A campa* is a simpler grave in the ground.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*túmulo* is a learned borrowing from Latin *tumulus* "a mound" (EtymDB), from *tumēre* "to swell".
