Urdu, chapter 224: Iron, Broom, Jug, Dinner plate, Small bowl.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
استری (istrī) — an iron (for clothes).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for polite, then the Urdu for beautiful.

You'll want to know: استری.
استری — istrī — "an iron (for clothes)".

The word, taken apart.
استری (istrī) is usually traced to Portuguese estirar, "to stretch out", for the pressing out of creases.

جھاڑو (jhāṛū) — a broom.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for beautiful, then the Urdu for an iron.

You'll want to know: جھاڑو.
جھاڑو — jhāṛū — "a broom".

The word, taken apart.
جھاڑو (jhāṛū) is built on jhāṛnā, "to sweep, shake off", which CDIAL lists under a reconstructed jhāṭayati, "shakes down" (CDIAL 5328).

Lesson 5 of 5.
کٹوری (kaṭorī) — a small bowl.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a jug, then the Urdu for a dinner plate.

You'll want to know: کٹوری.
کٹوری — kaṭorī — "a small bowl".

The word, taken apart.
کٹوری (kaṭorī) is a tadbhava, a word inherited and reshaped by sound change. It comes from kaṭṭora, "cup", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 2648).
