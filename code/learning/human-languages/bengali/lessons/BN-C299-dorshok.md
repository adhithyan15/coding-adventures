## You'll want to know: দর্শক
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C299-SOMPROCHAR299-05]; assesses=[] -->

**দর্শক** — *dôrshôk* — "a viewer; a spectator".

> খেলাটা দেখতে স্টেডিয়ামে হাজার হাজার দর্শক এল।
>
> *khelāṭā dekhte sṭeḍiyāme hājār hājār dôrshôk elô.*
>
> Thousands of spectators came to the stadium to watch the match.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**দর্শক** (*dôrshôk*) is Sanskrit *darśaka*, 'seeing, one who looks' (Monier-Williams), from *√dṛś*, 'to see'.
