Marathi, chapter 472: Stubborn and Hasty.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
हट्टी (haṭṭī) — stubborn.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for understanding, then the Marathi for common sense.

You'll want to know: हट्टी.
हट्टी — haṭṭī — "stubborn".
हट्टी बाळ खेळण्यासाठी रडत राहिलं. haṭṭī bāḷ kheḷṇyāsāṭhī raḍat rāhilaṁ. The stubborn baby kept crying for the toy.
From हट्ट, insistence: हट्ट करणे, to insist like a child. Unchanging.

The word, taken apart.
हट्टी (haṭṭī) is a Marathi adjective built on हट्ट, "insistence", which goes back to Sanskrit haṭha, "force; obstinacy" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 3 of 5.
लहरी (lahrī) — moody; whimsical.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for stubborn, then the Marathi for obstinate.

You'll want to know: लहरी.
लहरी — lahrī — "moody; whimsical".
आमचे मामा लहरी आहेत, कधी हसतात तर कधी रागावतात. āmce māmā lahrī āhet, kadhī hastāt tar kadhī rāgāvtāt. Our uncle is moody: sometimes he laughs, sometimes he gets angry.
From लहर, a wave or a whim, + -ई. Unchanging.

The word, taken apart.
लहरी (lahrī) is a Marathi adjective built on लहर, "wave, caprice", which CDIAL lists under Sanskrit laharī, "large wave" (CDIAL 10999).

Warm-up.

You'll want to know: उतावळा.
उतावळा — utāvḷā — "hasty; rash".
उतावळा माणूस घाईत चुका करतो. utāvḷā māṇūs ghāīt cukā karto. A hasty person makes mistakes in a rush.
Agrees: उतावळा (utāvḷā), उतावळी, उतावळं.

The word, taken apart.
उतावळा (utāvḷā) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi utāvḷā, "impetuous", under the reconstructed uttāpala (CDIAL 1788).

Lesson 5 of 5.
अधीर (adhīr) — impatient.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for moody, then the Marathi for hasty.

You'll want to know: अधीर.
अधीर — adhīr — "impatient".
निकाल ऐकायला सगळे अधीर झाले होते. nikāl aikāylā sagḷe adhīr jhāle hote. Everyone was impatient to hear the result.
अ-, not, + धीर, patience: अधीर (adhīr) होणे, to grow impatient.

The word, taken apart.
अधीर (adhīr) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit adhīra (Monier-Williams), the negative a- with dhīra, "steady".
