## You'll want to know: l'attestation
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C385-DEMARCHE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**l'attestation** — "the official statement, the attestation".

> La banque demande une attestation de domicile.
>
> *The bank is asking for proof of address.*

A signed paper that confirms a fact. Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*attestation* is a learned borrowing from Late Latin *attestātiō* (EtymDB), from *attestārī* "to bear witness to" (Lewis & Short), built on *testis* "witness".
