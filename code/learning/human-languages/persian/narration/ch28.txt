Persian, chapter 28: Hair, Finger, Shoulder, Neck, Face.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
مو (mu) — hair.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a mouth, then the Persian for a tooth.

You'll want to know: مو.
مو — mu — "hair".

The word, taken apart.
mu is a native Persian word, inherited rather than borrowed: it continues Middle Persian mōy, "hair" (EtymDB).

Lesson 5 of 5.
صورت (surat) — the face.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a shoulder; a comb, then the Persian for the neck.

You'll want to know: صورت.
صورت — surat — "the face".

The word, taken apart.
surat is borrowed from Arabic ṣūra, "form, image". Its Arabic root is ṣ-w-r (Lane). Persian uses it for the face.
