## You'll want to know: l'imprevisto
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C374-IMPREVISTO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**l'imprevisto** — "the unexpected problem, the hitch".

> Scusa il ritardo, ho avuto un imprevisto.
>
> *Sorry I am late, something came up.*

As an adjective: unexpected. *Salvo imprevisti* — barring the unexpected.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*imprevisto* is built from *in-* "not" and *previsto* "foreseen", past participle of *prevedere*, from Latin *praevidēre* "to foresee" (Lewis & Short), on *vidēre* "to see".
