## You'll want to know: درجه
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C473-HAVASHENASI-03]; assesses=[] -->

**درجه** (*daraje*) — "a degree (of temperature); a grade".

> امروز دمای هوا سی درجه است.
>
> *Emruz damâ-ye havâ si daraje ast.*
>
> Today the temperature is thirty degrees.

*Daraje-ye yek* — first class.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*daraje* was borrowed from Arabic *daraja* 'a step, a degree' (EtymDB).

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
