## You'll want to know: अनिवार्य
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C380-ANIVARYA380-01]; assesses=[] -->

**अनिवार्य** — *anivārya* — "compulsory".

> शाळेत मराठी हा विषय अनिवार्य आहे.
>
> *śāḷet marāṭhī hā viṣay anivārya āhe.*
>
> Marathi is a compulsory subject at school.

Invariable; in speech सक्तीचं.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अनिवार्य** (*anivārya*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *anivārya*, "not to be kept off, unavoidable" (Monier-Williams).
