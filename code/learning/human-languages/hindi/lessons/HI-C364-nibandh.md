## You'll want to know: निबंध
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C364-VISHESHAGYA364-04]; assesses=[] -->

**निबंध** — *nibandh* — "an essay".

> मीरा ने विज्ञान पर एक निबंध लिखा।
>
> *Mīrā ne vijñān par ek nibandh likhā.*
>
> Mira wrote an essay on science.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**निबंध** (*nibandh*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *nibandha*, "binding; a literary composition" (Monier-Williams).
