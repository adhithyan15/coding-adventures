## You'll want to know: la plaie
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C299-CHUTE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**la plaie** — "the wound, the cut".

> L'infirmière a nettoyé la plaie.
>
> *The nurse cleaned the wound.*

Informal: *quelle plaie !* — what a pain!

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*plaie* is inherited from Latin *plāga* "a blow, a wound" (EtymDB).
