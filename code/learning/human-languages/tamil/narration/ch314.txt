Tamil, chapter 314: A Moment to Remember.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
தருணம் (taruṇam) — a moment; an occasion.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for at long last, then the Tamil for earlier.

You'll want to know: தருணம்.
தருணம் — taruṇam — "a moment; an occasion".
அது என் வாழ்க்கையின் மிக மகிழ்ச்சியான தருணம். atu eṉ vāḻkkaiyiṉ mika makiḻcciyāṉa taruṇam. That was the happiest moment of my life.
Also the right moment: சரியான தருணம் (taruṇam).

The word, taken apart.
தருணம் (taruṇam), "a moment, an occasion", is a learned Tamil word whose origin is not settled in the sources at hand.

Lesson 2 of 5.
நினைவுகூர் (niṉaivukūr) — to recall; to remember.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for earlier, then the Tamil for a moment.

You'll want to know: நினைவுகூர்.
நினைவுகூர் — niṉaivukūr — "to recall; to remember".
தாத்தா தன் பள்ளி நாட்களை நினைவுகூர்ந்தார். tāttā taṉ paḷḷi nāṭkaḷai niṉaivukūrntār. Grandfather recalled his school days.
Past (I …): நினைவுகூர்ந்தேன், niṉaivukūrntēṉ.
A written word; in speech நினைத்துப் பார், to think back.

The word, taken apart.
நினைவுகூர் (niṉaivukūr) is a Tamil compound built from நினைவு, "remembrance" (DEDR 3683), and கூர்: DEDR has கூர், "to be sharp, keen" (DEDR 1898), and கூர், "to abound" (DEDR 1899), and which of them is in this compound is not settled.

Lesson 3 of 5.
நாளடைவில் (nāḷaṭaivil) — over time.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for a moment, then the Tamil for to recall.

You'll want to know: நாளடைவில்.
நாளடைவில் — nāḷaṭaivil — "over time".
நாளடைவில் அவன் புதிய ஊருக்குப் பழகினான். nāḷaṭaivil avaṉ putiya ūrukkup paḻakiṉāṉ. Over time he got used to the new town.
நாள், day + அடைவு, reaching + -இல்: as the days go by.

The word, taken apart.
நாளடைவில் (nāḷaṭaivil) is built from native நாள், "day" (DEDR 3656), and அடைவு, "reaching", from அடை, "to reach" (DEDR 79), with -இல்.

Lesson 4 of 5.
அதற்குள் (ataṟkuḷ) — by then; by that time.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for to recall, then the Tamil for over time.

You'll want to know: அதற்குள்.
அதற்குள் — ataṟkuḷ — "by then; by that time".
நான் நிலையத்துக்கு வந்தேன், ஆனால் அதற்குள் ரயில் போய்விட்டது. nāṉ nilaiyattukku vantēṉ, āṉāl ataṟkuḷ rayil pōyviṭṭatu. I got to the station, but by then the train had gone.
அது + -க்கு + உள், within that; after a verb, வருவதற்குள், before … could come.

The word, taken apart.
அதற்குள் (ataṟkuḷ) is built from அது, "that" (DEDR 1), and உள், "inside" (DEDR 698): within that time.

இதையடுத்து — itaiyaṭuttu — "following this".
இதையடுத்து, காவல்துறை புலனாய்வைத் தொடங்கியது. itaiyaṭuttu, kāvaltuṟai pulaṉāyvait toṭaṅkiyatu. Following this, the police began an investigation.

The word, taken apart.
இதையடுத்து (itaiyaṭuttu) is built from இதை, "this", and அடுத்து, "next to", from native அடு, "to be next, near" (DEDR 79).
