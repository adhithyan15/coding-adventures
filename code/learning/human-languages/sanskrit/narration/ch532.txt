Sanskrit, chapter 532: Choosing a Gift.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
उपायनम् (upāyanam) — a present.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a prayer, then the Sanskrit for a desire.

You'll want to know: उपायनम्.
उपायनम् — upāyanam — "a present".
यदि मित्रम् आगच्छति, तर्हि अहं तस्मै उपायनं ददामि। yadi mitram āgacchati, tarhi ahaṃ tasmai upāyanaṃ dadāmi. If my friend comes, I will give him a present.
A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
उपायनम् (upāyanam) is built on upa, ā and the root √i, "to go": a coming near; MW gives upāyana also as an offer, a present, a gift (MW).

गुच्छः — gucchaḥ — "a bunch of flowers".
यदि पुष्पाणि सुन्दराणि सन्ति, तर्हि एकं गुच्छं क्रीणामि। yadi puṣpāṇi sundarāṇi santi, tarhi ekaṃ gucchaṃ krīṇāmi. If the flowers are pretty, I'll buy a bunch.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
गुच्छः (gucchaḥ) is the word MW gives for a bush and for a bunch of flowers, a cluster of blossoms, the same as gutsa; MW gives no further derivation, so its origin is uncertain (MW).

Lesson 5 of 5.
उपहरति (upaharati) — presents; offers.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for fine cloth, then the Sanskrit for a girdle.

You'll want to know: उपहरति.
उपहरति — upaharati — "presents; offers".
यदि गुरुः आगच्छति, तर्हि शिष्यः तस्मै फलानि उपहरति। yadi guruḥ āgacchati, tarhi śiṣyaḥ tasmai phalāni upaharati. If the teacher comes, the pupil offers him fruit.
Past (he …): सः गुरवे पुष्पाणि उपहृतवान्, saḥ gurave puṣpāṇi upahṛtavān.

The word, taken apart.
उपहरति (upaharati) is built on upa, "near", and the root √hṛ, "to carry", which also gives harati; MW gives upahṛ as to bring near, to offer, to present (MW).
