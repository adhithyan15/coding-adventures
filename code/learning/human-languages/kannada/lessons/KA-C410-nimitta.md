# ನಿಮಿತ್ತ (nimitta) — on account of; an occasion

## You'll want to know: ನಿಮಿತ್ತ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C410-HIGAGI410-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ನಿಮಿತ್ತ** — *nimitta* — "on account of; an occasion".

> ಹಬ್ಬದ ನಿಮಿತ್ತ ಶಾಲೆಗೆ ಎರಡು ದಿನ ರಜೆ ಇದೆ.
>
> *habbada nimitta śālege eraḍu dina raje ide.*
>
> On account of the festival, the school has two days off.

It follows a noun in -ದ / -ಯ: ಮದುವೆಯ ನಿಮಿತ್ತ, for the wedding. A formal word for a reason or an occasion.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ನಿಮಿತ್ತ** (*nimitta*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *nimitta*, "a cause, a reason" (CDIAL 7241); in Sanskrit, too, its case forms mean "on account of" (Monier-Williams).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
