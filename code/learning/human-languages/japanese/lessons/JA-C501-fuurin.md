## You'll want to know: ふうりん
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C501-MOON501-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ふうりん** — *fūrin* — "a wind chime".

> **なつに ふうりんの おとが します。** — *natsu ni fūrin no oto ga shimasu* — "In summer you hear the wind chimes."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ふうりん** (*fūrin*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *fū*, "wind" (Middle Chinese *pjuwng*) and *rin*, "bell" (*leng*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
