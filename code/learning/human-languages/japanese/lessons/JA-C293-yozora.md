## You'll want to know: よぞら
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C293-HANABI293-04]; assesses=[] -->

**よぞら** — *yozora* — "the night sky".

> **よぞらに はなびが ひろがりました。** — *yozora ni hanabi ga hirogarimashita* — "Fireworks spread across the night sky."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**よぞら** (*yozora*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, built from *yo*, "night", and *zora*, "sky" (from *sora*) (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
