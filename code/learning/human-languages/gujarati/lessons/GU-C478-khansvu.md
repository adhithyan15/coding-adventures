## You'll want to know: ખાંસવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C478-KAPH478-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ખાંસવું** — *khā̃svũ* — "to cough".

> દર્દી આખી રાત ખાંસતો હતો.
>
> *dardī ākhī rāt khā̃sto hato.*
>
> The patient was coughing all night.

Past (he …): તે આખી રાત ખાંસ્યો, *te ākhī rāt khā̃syo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ખાંસવું** (*khā̃svũ*) is inherited from Sanskrit *kāsatē*, "coughs"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb (CDIAL 3135), beside the noun **ખાંસી** (*khā̃sī*), "a cough".
