## You'll want to know: a nostalgia
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C478-SOLIDAO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**a nostalgia** — "nostalgia".

> A avó olha para as fotografias antigas com nostalgia.
>
> *Grandma looks at the old photographs with nostalgia.*

Close to *a saudade*, but more about a time that is gone.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*nostalgia* comes from New Latin *nostalgia* (EtymDB), a modern coinage from Greek *nóstos* "return home" and *álgos* "pain".
