## You'll want to know: দেনা
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C416-KISTI416-02]; assesses=[] -->

**দেনা** — *denā* — "a debt (owed); what one owes".

> দোকানদারের কাছে আমাদের কিছু দেনা আছে।
>
> *dokāndārer kāchhe āmāder kichhu denā āchhe.*
>
> We owe the shopkeeper some money.

ধার is a loan; দেনা is what is still to be paid back.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**দেনা** (*denā*) is built on দে-, the stem of দেওয়া, to give, the Bengali verb that goes back to Sanskrit *dadāti* (Monier-Williams): what one has still to give.
