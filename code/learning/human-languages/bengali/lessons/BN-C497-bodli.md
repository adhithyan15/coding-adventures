## You'll want to know: বদলি
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C497-ISTOPHA497-02]; assesses=[] -->

**বদলি** — *bôdli* — "a job transfer; a transfer to another place".

> বাবার নতুন শহরে বদলি হল।
>
> *Bābār nôtun shôhôre bôdli hôlô.*
>
> My father was transferred to a new city.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**বদলি** (*bôdli*) is usually traced to Arabic *badal*, "exchange, a substitute", through Persian: one post given in exchange for another.
