# 儿 — fall, then bend and hook

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ZH-LEX-ERZI-01, ZH-SCRIPT-REN-01] -->

[PAUSE 12s] Say **son**, then write 人.

## Script — the shape
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-SCRIPT-ER-CHAR-01]; assesses=[] -->

> **儿**

Two strokes. First a short fall down-left. Then start high on the right, descend,
bend right along the floor, and hook upward **without lifting**. Unlike 人, the
two strokes do not meet at the top.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ZH-SCRIPT-ER-CHAR-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: observe-trace -->

Trace **儿** once. Copy it once. Point to the bend that keeps it apart from 人.
