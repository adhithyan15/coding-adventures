## You'll want to know: پانسیون
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C437-MOSAFERKHANE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**پانسیون** (*pânsiyon*) — "a boarding house; full board".

> دانشجوها در یک پانسیون زندگی میکنند.
>
> *Dâneshju-hâ dar yek pânsiyon zendegi mikonand.*
>
> The students live in a boarding house.

Also meals included with a room: *bâ pânsiyon* — with full board.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*pânsiyon* was borrowed from French and is usually traced to French *pension* 'board and lodging'. EtymDB does not list the Persian word.
