## You'll want to know: اطمینان
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C393-ETMINAN-03]; assesses=[] -->

**اطمینان** (*etminân*) — "certainty, confidence".

> با اطمینان میگویم که حق با توست.
>
> *bâ etminân miguyam ke haq bâ tost.*
>
> I can say for certain that you are right.

*Etminân dâshtan* — to be sure; *etminân dâdan* — to reassure.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**اطمینان** was borrowed from Arabic *iṭmi'nān* "calm, assurance" (EtymDB), from the same Arabic family as *muṭma'inn* "sure", the source of Persian *motma'en*.
