## You'll want to know: il detenuto
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C393-CARCERE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**il detenuto** — "the prisoner, the inmate".

> I detenuti possono studiare in carcere.
>
> *The inmates can study in prison.*

Past participle of *detenere* "to hold". A woman is *la detenuta*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*detenuto* is the past participle of *detenere* "to hold, to detain", from Latin *dētinēre* "to hold back" (Lewis & Short), built on *tenēre* "to hold".
