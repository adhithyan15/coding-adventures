## You'll want to know: चबाना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C479-MUNH479-04]; assesses=[] -->

**चबाना** — *cabānā* — "to chew".

> दादा के दाँत कमज़ोर हैं, इसलिए वह रोटी नहीं चबा सकते।
>
> *dādā ke dā̃t kamzor haiṁ, isliye vah roṭī nahīṃ cabā sakte.*
>
> Grandfather's teeth are weak, so he can't chew bread.

Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने रोटी चबाई, *usne roṭī cabāī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**चबाना** (*cabānā*) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Hindi *cabānā*, "to bite the lip, gnaw, crunch", under Sanskrit *cárvati*, "chews" (CDIAL 4711).
