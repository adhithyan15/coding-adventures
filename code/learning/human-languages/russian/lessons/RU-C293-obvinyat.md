## You'll want to know: обвинять
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C293-SUD-05]; assesses=[] -->

**обвинять** (*obvinyát*) — "to accuse".

> Его обвиняют в краже.
>
> *Yevó obvinyáyut v krázhe.*
>
> He is accused of theft.

Imperfective; perfective *обвинить* (*obvinít*). The person is in the accusative, the charge with *в* + prepositional.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*обвинять* is built on *об-* and *вина* "guilt", an inherited Slavic word: to lay guilt on someone. EtymDB lists no parent for the verb.
