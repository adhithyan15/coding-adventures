## You'll want to know: قدر
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C345-QADR345-01]; assesses=[] -->

**قدر** — *qadr* — "worth; esteem".

> وہ پیسے کی قدر جانتا ہے۔
>
> *voh paise kī qadr jāntā hai.*
>
> He knows the value of money.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**قدر** (*qadr*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *qadr*, "measure, worth".
