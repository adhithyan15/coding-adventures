## You'll want to know: عذاب
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C326-LANAT326-05]; assesses=[] -->

**عذاب** — *azāb* — "torment; a punishing suffering".

> یہ قحط ایک عذاب تھا۔
>
> *yih qahat ek azāb thā.*
>
> This famine was a torment.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**عذاب** (*azāb*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *ʿadhāb*, "torment, punishment". Urdu says its middle letter as *z*.
