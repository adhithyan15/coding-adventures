Gujarati, chapter 265: Love and marriage.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
પ્રેમ કરવો (prem karvo) — to love.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to recognize, then the Gujarati for to embrace.

You'll want to know: પ્રેમ કરવો.
પ્રેમ કરવો — prem karvo — "to love".
શું તે તેની પત્નીને પ્રેમ કરે છે? — śũ te tenī patnīne prem kare che? — "Does he love his wife?"

The word, taken apart.
પ્રેમ કરવો (prem karvo) is built from prem, Sanskrit prēmán, "love", taken over as a tatsama (CDIAL 8999; EtymDB), and the verb karvo, "to do".

Lesson 2 of 5.
પરણવું (paraṇvũ) — to marry, to get married.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to embrace, then the Gujarati for to love.

You'll want to know: પરણવું.
પરણવું — paraṇvũ — "to marry, to get married".
શું તમારો ભાઈ આ વરસે પરણે છે? — śũ tamāro bhāī ā varse paraṇe che? — "Is your brother getting married this year?"

The word, taken apart.
પરણવું (paraṇvũ) is a tadbhava. It comes from Sanskrit páriṇayati, "leads round" the fire, and so "marries" (CDIAL 7819).

The word, taken apart.
The origin of સગાઈ (sagāī) is not settled. It looks built on sagũ, "related", with -āī, but CDIAL has no entry for either word.

Lesson 4 of 5.
વહુ (vahu) — daughter-in-law, bride.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to marry, then the Gujarati for engagement.

You'll want to know: વહુ.
વહુ — vahu — "daughter-in-law, bride".
નવી વહુ ઘરમાં નથી. — navī vahu gharmā̃ nathī — "The new bride is not in the house."

The word, taken apart.
વહુ (vahu) is a tadbhava. It comes from Sanskrit vadhū́, "bride, young wife"; CDIAL glosses the Gujarati word "son's wife" (CDIAL 11250).

Lesson 5 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for engagement, then the Gujarati for daughter-in-law.

You'll want to know: વિશ્વાસ.
વિશ્વાસ — viśvās — "trust, faith".
શું તમને મારામાં વિશ્વાસ નથી? — śũ tamne mārāmā̃ viśvās nathī? — "Don't you trust me?"
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
વિશ્વાસ (viśvās) is Sanskrit viśvāsa, "trust, confidence", taken over whole as a tatsama; CDIAL also lists an inherited Old Gujarati vīsāsa, "trust" (CDIAL 11966).
