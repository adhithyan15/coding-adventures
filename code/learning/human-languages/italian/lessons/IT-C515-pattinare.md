## You'll want to know: pattinare
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C515-NEVE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**pattinare** — "to skate".

> D'inverno pattiniamo sul ghiaccio in piazza.
>
> *In winter we skate on the ice in the square.*

Skates are *i pattini*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*pattinare* is built on *pattino* "skate"; the origin of *pattino* is not settled, and it may come from an old word *patta* "paw".
