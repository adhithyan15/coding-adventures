## Writing

1. Award form credit for six or seven independently completed Bengali-script
   fields with internally consistent safe details. The learner may invent a
   name, age, city, language, day, and date. Do not demand a real phone number.
   Bengali digits are welcome; a declared accessibility accommodation may
   permit other digits without replacing Bengali written responses.
2. The message must address তনয় as a new neighbor, welcome the reader,
   identify the writer and city, and invite shared reading at a place and time
   in 30–40 Bengali words. Accept a natural equivalent and safe invented
   details. Do not score agreement with one model sentence.

## Speaking

1. Accept relevant short answers to all five personal questions, including a
   chosen identity and invented age or city.
2. The one-minute description must identify a familiar person, say where the
   learner meets them, and describe an activity they share.
3. The bookshop exchange must include a greeting, book request, price
   question, and polite closing. Short repair or repetition is acceptable.

The learner need not reproduce a particular name, city, or sentence.
