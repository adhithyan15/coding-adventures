## You'll want to know: گنج
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C348-PARI-04]; assesses=[] -->

**گنج** (*ganj*) — "a treasure".

> آنها زیر درخت یک گنج پیدا کردند.
>
> *Ânhâ zir-e derakht yek ganj peydâ kardand.*
>
> They found a treasure under the tree.

*Ganjine* is "a treasury, a store".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
