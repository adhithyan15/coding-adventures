## You'll want to know: le couvercle
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C529-TABLE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**le couvercle** — "lid".

> Avec le couvercle sur la casserole, ça cuit plus vite.
>
> *With the lid on the pan, it cooks faster.*

Built on *couvrir* "to cover".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*le couvercle* is inherited from Latin *cooperculum* "a lid", from *cooperīre* "to cover completely" (Lewis & Short).
