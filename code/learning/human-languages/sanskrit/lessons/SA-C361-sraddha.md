## You'll want to know: श्रद्धा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C361-SHRADDHA361-01]; assesses=[] -->

**श्रद्धा** — *śraddhā* — "faith; trust".

> मम वैद्ये श्रद्धा अस्ति।
>
> *mama vaidye śraddhā asti.*
>
> I have faith in my doctor.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**श्रद्धा** (*śraddhā*) is built from *śrat*, "trust, truth", and the root √dhā, "to put"; MW gives *śrad-dhā* "faith, trust, confidence, belief" (MW).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
