Italian, chapter 292: Rapina in banca.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
la rapina — the robbery, the hold-up.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for to rob someone, then the Italian for the stolen goods.

You'll want to know: la rapina.
la rapina — "the robbery, the hold-up".
C'è stata una rapina in banca stamattina. There was a bank robbery this morning.
With force or threats; a mano armata means armed.

The word, taken apart.
rapina is inherited from Latin rapīna "robbery, plunder" (EtymDB), from rapere "to seize".

Lesson 3 of 5.
il rapinatore — the robber.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for the robbery, then the Italian for to rob.

You'll want to know: il rapinatore.
il rapinatore — "the robber".
Il rapinatore è scappato in moto. The robber got away on a motorbike.
-tore makes the person who does it: rapinare, rapinatore.

The word, taken apart.
rapinatore is rapinare with the agent ending -tore, from Latin -tor; the root is Latin rapere "to seize" (EtymDB).

Lesson 5 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: il bottino.
il bottino — "the loot, the haul".
Il bottino è di diecimila euro. The haul is ten thousand euros.
Also the spoils of war.

The word, taken apart.
bottino was borrowed from French butin "booty" (EtymDB), which goes back to a Germanic word for booty, related to German Beute (EtymDB).
