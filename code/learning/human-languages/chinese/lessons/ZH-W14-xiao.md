# 小 — centre, left, right

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ZH-LEX-XIAO-01] -->

[PAUSE 8s] Say **small** in Mandarin.

## Script — the shape
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-SCRIPT-XIAO-01]; assesses=[] -->

Draw the centre vertical first and finish its hook without lifting. Add the
short left-falling mark. Finish with the right dot, leaving air around the
centre. Three strokes; two lifts.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ZH-SCRIPT-XIAO-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: observe-trace -->

Trace **小** once. Copy it once while saying **centre, left, right**.
