# ভালো, and the reply it lives in

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[BN-CONCEPT-C03-BHALO-01] -->

[PAUSE 5s] Say *good*.

## Guided Practice — the distant band
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[BN-CONCEPT-C03-BHALO-01, BN-CONCEPT-C01-ASHI-01] -->

- [YOU SAY: *good, well* — *bhālo*]
- [YOU SAY: "I am well" — *āmi bhālo āchhi*]
- [YOU SAY: the goodbye that literally means "I'll come again" — *āshi*]
- [YOU SAY: why a Bengali speaker prefers that goodbye (**it promises a
  return**)]

**ভালো** has appeared in more lessons than any other word in this book, which
makes it the fair test in the other direction: not "did a rare thing survive?"
but "did constant exposure actually put a thing where you can get at it?"

If it came back instantly, that is a result worth having. Frequency is supposed
to do that, and it is worth knowing when it worked.

## Writing — from sound
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[BN-SCRIPT-BHALO-READ-01, BN-SCRIPT-ACHHI-READ-01] -->

Cover every model. Write *good*, then *am*, then the two together with *I* in
front.
