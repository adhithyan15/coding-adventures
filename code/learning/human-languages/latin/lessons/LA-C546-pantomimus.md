## You'll want to know: pantomīmus, pantomīmī
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C546-CAVE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**pantomīmus, pantomīmī** — "a mime dancer".

> Pantomīmus sine verbīs fābulam saltat.
>
> *The mime dancer dances a story without words.*

Masculine, second declension, from Greek. A solo dancer who told myths with gestures; the best were stars.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**pantomīmus** was borrowed from Greek *pantomimos*, built on *pan* "all" and *mimeisthai* "to imitate" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short). English *pantomime* comes from it.
