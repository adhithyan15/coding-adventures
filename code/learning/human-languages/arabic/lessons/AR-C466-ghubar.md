## You'll want to know: غبار
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C466-GHUBAR466-01]; assesses=[] -->

**غبار** — *ghubār* — "fine dust; dust in the air".

> كان الغبار على كل الكراسي.
>
> *kāna al-ghubār ʿalā kull al-karāsī.*
>
> There was dust on all the chairs.

*turāb* is earth and soil; *ghubār* is the dust that rises and settles.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**غبار** (*ghubār*) is built on the native Arabic root *gh-b-r*, "dust" (Lane).
