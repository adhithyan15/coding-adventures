## You'll want to know: trepar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C687-EXCURSION-04]; assesses=[] -->

**trepar** — "to clamber, to climb (using your hands)".

> Los niños trepan a los árboles.
>
> *The children climb trees.*

*Escalar* is the sport; *trepar* is any scrambling climb.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

The origin of *trepar* is not settled; it may imitate the sound of feet stamping.
