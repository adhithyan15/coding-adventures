# મને બીક લાગે છે (mane bīk lāge che) — how I feel

## Grammar Lens: a feeling that comes to me
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-GRAMMAR-C281-FEEL-NOUN-01]; assesses=[GU-LEX-C50-FEEL-02, GU-LEX-C184-RATE184-02, GU-LEX-AAVVU, GU-GRAMMAR-C146-DATIVE-SG-01] -->

> **મને બીક લાગે છે.**
>
> *mane bīk lāge che.* — "I'm afraid."

Many feelings are nouns that happen to the one who feels them, and that one takes **-ને**. Each noun has its own verb:

| the feeling | now | in the past |
|---|---|---|
| fear, **બીક** | **મને બીક લાગે છે** | **મને બીક લાગી** |
| worry, **ચિંતા** | **મને ચિંતા થાય છે** | **મને ચિંતા થઈ** |
| sorrow, **દુઃખ** | **મને દુઃખ થાય છે** | **મને દુઃખ થયું** |
| anger, **ગુસ્સો** | **મને ગુસ્સો આવે છે** | **મને ગુસ્સો આવ્યો** |

The past follows the feeling: **બીક** is feminine, **લાગી**; **ગુસ્સો** is masculine, **આવ્યો**.

## You'll want to know: I am happy
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-GRAMMAR-C281-FEEL-ADJ-01]; assesses=[GU-LEX-C134-QUAL134-03, GU-LEX-C134-QUAL134-04, GU-LEX-C206-ARRIVE206-02] -->

> **આજે હું ખુશ છું.**
>
> *āje hũ khuś chũ.* — "Today I'm happy."

Other feelings are describing words, and the one who feels them is a plain subject: **હું ખુશ છું**, **તે ઉદાસ હતી** (*te udās hatī*), "she was sad". Tiredness is a past: **હું થાકી ગયો છું** (*hũ thākī gayo chũ*), "I'm tired", word for word "I have got tired".
