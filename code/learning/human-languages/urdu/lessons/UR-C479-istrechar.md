## You'll want to know: اسٹریچر
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C479-WARD479-05]; assesses=[] -->

**اسٹریچر** — *isṭrechar* — "a stretcher".

> دو آدمی اسٹریچر اٹھا کر لائے۔
>
> *do ādmī isṭrechar uṭhā kar lāe.*
>
> Two men carried in the stretcher.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**اسٹریچر** (*isṭrechar*) is borrowed from English *stretcher*.
