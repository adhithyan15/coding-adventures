## You'll want to know: सूचना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C191-NOTICE191-01]; assesses=[] -->

**सूचना** — *sūchnā* — "a notice".

> दुकान माथै नुवी सूचना है।
>
> *dukān māthai nuvī sūchnā hai.*
>
> There is a new notice on the shop.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सूचना** (*sūchnā*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *sūcanā*, "indication, communication" (Monier-Williams).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for reading notices and messages.
