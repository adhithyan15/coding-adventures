Hindi, chapter 345: What It Is For.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
उद्देश्य (uddeśya) — a purpose, an aim.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for to make worse, then the Hindi for a source.

You'll want to know: उद्देश्य.
उद्देश्य — uddeśya — "a purpose, an aim".
हमारी बैठक का उद्देश्य क्या है? hamārī baiṭhak kā uddeśya kyā hai? What is the purpose of our meeting?
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
उद्देश्य (uddeśya) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit uddeśya, "that which one has in view" (Monier-Williams).
