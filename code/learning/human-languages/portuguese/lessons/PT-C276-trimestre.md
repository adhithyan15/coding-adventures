## You'll want to know: o trimestre
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C276-QUINZENA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**o trimestre** — "the quarter, three months".

> A empresa vendeu muito no primeiro trimestre.
>
> *The company sold a lot in the first quarter.*

Firms count their year in *trimestres*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*trimestre* is a learned borrowing from Latin *trimestris* "of three months" (EtymDB), from *trēs* "three" and *mēnsis* "month".
