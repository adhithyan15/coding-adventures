## You'll want to know: صحنه
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C523-THEATRE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**صحنه** (*sahne*) — "a stage; a scene".

> بازیگرها روی صحنه آمدند.
>
> *bâzigarhâ ru-ye sahne âmadand.*
>
> The actors came on stage.

Also a scene in a film or a play. Plural *sahnehâ*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*sahne* is a loan, usually traced to Arabic *ṣaḥn*, 'courtyard, open space'. EtymDB lists it without a source.
