Marathi, chapter 284: Feelings and Hopes.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
राग (rāg) — anger.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say "my bag is lost" and "I missed my train". (mājhī piśvī haravlī, mājhī gāḍī cuklī.) Then "to get angry". (rāgāvṇe.)

You'll want to know: राग.
राग — rāg — "anger".
मला खूप राग आला. malā khūp rāg ālā. — "I got very angry."
रागावणे, "to get angry", is built on this noun. Anger is something that comes to you: "to me anger came".

The word, taken apart.
राग (rāg) comes from Sanskrit rāga, which CDIAL lists as "colour, redness" (CDIAL 10673) and "passion, love" (CDIAL 10674). Marathi uses it for anger; how the sense moved there is not settled. The same Sanskrit word gives the rāga of Indian music.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: rāg]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: "I got very angry" — malā khūp rāg ālā]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: rāg, then rāgāvṇe, the verb built on it]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does राग (rāg) mean? ("Anger".) Which verb is built on it? (रागावणे.)


Lesson 2 of 6.
भीती (bhītī) — fear.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say "anger" and "to be afraid". (rāg, ghābarṇe.)

You'll want to know: भीती.
भीती — bhītī — "fear".
मला सापाची भीती वाटते. malā sāpācī bhītī vāṭte. — "I am afraid of snakes."
What you fear takes -ची: सापाची भीती (bhītī), "fear of snakes". The feeling वाटते, "is felt", and it is feminine, so the verb ends in -ते.

The word, taken apart.
भीती (bhītī) is a tatsama: Sanskrit bhīti, "fear" (CDIAL 9512), from the root bhī, "to be afraid". Marathi writes it with a long final ī.

[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: bhītī, once more, and ghābarṇe, "to be afraid"]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does भीती (bhītī) mean? ("Fear".) "I am afraid of snakes"? (मला सापाची भीती (bhītī) वाटते.)


Lesson 3 of 6.
मला राग आला (malā rāg ālā) — feelings that come to you.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say "I got very angry" and "I am afraid of snakes". (malā khūp rāg ālā, malā sāpācī bhītī vāṭte.) Then the first line you learned: bheṭūn ānand jhālā.

You'll want to know: the feeling is the subject.
मला राग आला. malā rāg ālā. — "I got angry."
The one who feels stands in the dative, as with आवडणे and वाटणे. The feeling is the subject, and the past agrees with it: राग आला (masculine), भीती (bhītī) वाटली (feminine), कंटाळा आला (kaṇṭāḷā ālā), "I got bored". Ask it the same way: तुम्हाला राग आला का? (tumhālā rāg ālā kā?)
Two feeling words you know are adjectives instead, and they agree with the person: ती आनंदी आहे (tī ānandī āhe), "she is happy"; तो दुःखी होता (to duḥkhī hotā), "he was sad".

You'll want to know: which verb each feeling takes.
Three verbs carry feelings, and each feeling has its own:
आला, "came": राग (rāg) आला, कंटाळा आला.
वाटलं, "was felt": भीती (bhītī) वाटली, लाज वाटली (lāj vāṭlī), "I felt ashamed".
झालं, "came about": आनंद झाला, दुःख झालं (duḥkh jhālaṁ), "I was sad".
Learn the feeling with its verb, as you learned भेटून आनंद झाला whole.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: "I got angry" — malā rāg ālā]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: "She felt afraid" — tilā bhītī vāṭlī]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: "We were glad" — āmhālā ānand jhālā]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: "He got bored" — tyālā kaṇṭāḷā ālā]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
आम्हाला (āmhālā, "to us").
[question — say your answer, then pause 24 seconds]
Say that you got angry, then that she felt afraid.

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
"I got angry"? (मला राग आला (malā rāg ālā).) "I felt ashamed"? (मला लाज वाटली.) "We were glad"? (आम्हाला आनंद झाला.) What does the verb agree with? (The feeling.)


Lesson 4 of 6.
ध्येय (dhyey) — a goal.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say "a wish" and "a dream". (icchā, svapna.) Then "we were glad". (āmhālā ānand jhālā.)

You'll want to know: ध्येय.
ध्येय — dhyey — "a goal, an aim".
माझं ध्येय मोठं आहे. mājhaṁ dhyey moṭhaṁ āhe. — "My goal is a big one."
ध्येय (dhyey) is neuter: माझं ध्येय (dhyey). A wish can stay a wish; a goal is what you work toward.

The word, taken apart.
ध्येय (dhyey) is a tatsama: Sanskrit dhyeya, "to be meditated on, to be pondered" (Monier-Williams), from the root of dhyāna, "meditation". Marathi uses it for the aim one keeps the mind fixed on.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: dhyey]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: "My goal is a big one" — mājhaṁ dhyey moṭhaṁ āhe]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: a wish, a dream and a goal: icchā, svapna, dhyey]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does ध्येय (dhyey) mean? ("A goal, an aim".) Is it masculine, feminine or neuter? (Neuter: माझं ध्येय (dhyey).)


Lesson 5 of 6.
मला शिक्षक व्हायचं आहे (malā śikṣak vhāycaṁ āhe) — wanting to do, wanting to be.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Say "my goal is a big one". (mājhaṁ dhyey moṭhaṁ āhe.) Then "how do I get to the station?", with its जायचं. (sṭeśanlā kasaṁ jāycaṁ?)

You'll want to know: मला … -ायचं आहे, 'I want to'.
मला मराठीत गोष्ट लिहायची आहे. malā marāṭhīt goṣṭa lihāycī āhe. — "I want to write a story in Marathi."
The -ायच- form, with आहे and the dative: मला जायचं आहे (malā jāycaṁ āhe), "I want to go". With an object it agrees with the thing, as the past does: गोष्ट लिहायची आहे, पुस्तक वाचायचं आहे (pustak vācāycaṁ āhe). And the "not": मला जायचं नाही (malā jāycaṁ nāhī), "I don't want to go".

You'll want to know: व्हायचं, 'to become'.
मला शिक्षक व्हायचं आहे. malā śikṣak vhāycaṁ āhe. — "I want to become a teacher."
व्हायचं is होणे, "to become", in the same form. The wish words you know can follow it: ते माझं स्वप्न आहे (te mājhaṁ svapna āhe), "that is my dream"; ते माझं ध्येय आहे (te mājhaṁ dhyey āhe), "that is my goal".

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: "I want to become a teacher" — malā śikṣak vhāycaṁ āhe]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: "I want to write a story in Marathi" — malā marāṭhīt goṣṭa lihāycī āhe]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: "I don't want to go" — malā jāycaṁ nāhī]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[question — say your answer, then pause 24 seconds]
Say that you want to become a teacher, then that you want to read a book.

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
"I want to go"? (मला जायचं आहे.) "I want to become a teacher"? (मला शिक्षक व्हायचं आहे (malā śikṣak vhāycaṁ āhe).) "I don't want to go"? (मला जायचं नाही.)
