## You'll want to know: ぶらぶら
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C396-YUKISAKI396-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ぶらぶら** — *burabura* — "strolling; aimlessly".

> **しゅうまつは まちを ぶらぶら あるきました。** — *shūmatsu wa machi o burabura arukimashita* — "At the weekend I strolled around the town."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ぶらぶら** (*burabura*) is a native mimetic word that pictures an easy, aimless stroll: an imitative Japanese form, inherited rather than borrowed, whose older origin is not settled (JMdict).
