## You'll want to know: કદર
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C385-PURASKAR385-02]; assesses=[] -->

**કદર** — *kadar* — "appreciation".

> જો સાહેબ તમારા કામની કદર કરશે તો તમારો પગાર વધશે.
>
> *jo sāheb tamārā kāmnī kadar karśe to tamāro pagār vadhśe.*
>
> If the boss appreciates your work, your pay will go up.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**કદર** (*kadar*) is usually traced to Arabic *qadr*, "worth, value", through Persian.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
