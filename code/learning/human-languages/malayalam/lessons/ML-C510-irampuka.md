# ഇരമ്പുക (irampuka) — to rumble; to roar

## You'll want to know: ഇരമ്പുക
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C510-MANJU510-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ഇരമ്പുക** — *irampuka* — "to rumble; to roar".

> രാത്രി കടൽ ഉറക്കെ ഇരമ്പി.
>
> *rātri kaṭal uṟakke irampi.*
>
> At night the sea roared loudly.

Past: ഇരമ്പി, *irampi*.

For the sea, the wind, engines and a crowd.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ഇരമ്പുക** (*irampuka*) is native Dravidian, "to roar as the sea, to bluster" (DEDR 489).
