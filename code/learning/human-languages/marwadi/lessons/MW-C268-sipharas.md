## You'll want to know: सिफारस
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C268-KAIVNO268-05]; assesses=[] -->

**सिफारस** — *siphāras* — "a good word for someone; a recommendation".

> भाई म्हारी सिफारस करै है।
>
> *bhāī mhārī siphāras karai hai.*
>
> My brother puts in a good word for me.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सिफारस** (*siphāras*) is usually traced to Persian *sifārish*, "a recommendation". It is feminine: **म्हारी सिफारस**, a good word for me.
