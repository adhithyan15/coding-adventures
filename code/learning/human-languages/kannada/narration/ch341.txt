Kannada, chapter 341: Our Family Line.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ವಂಶ (vaṃśa) — a lineage; a dynasty.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a demon, then the Kannada for a curse.

You'll want to know: ವಂಶ.
ವಂಶ — vaṃśa — "a lineage; a dynasty".
ಆ ವಂಶದ ರಾಜರು ಮುನ್ನೂರು ವರ್ಷ ಆಳಿದರು. ā vaṃśada rājaru munnūru varṣa āḷidaru. The kings of that dynasty ruled for three hundred years.
ವಂಶವೃಕ್ಷ is a family tree.

The word, taken apart.
ವಂಶ (vaṃśa) is borrowed from Sanskrit vaṃśa, "a bamboo cane", and from its joints, "a line of descent, a race" (Monier-Williams).

ಈ ಮನೆಯಲ್ಲಿ ನಾಲ್ಕು ತಲೆಮಾರಿನವರು ಒಟ್ಟಿಗೆ ಇದ್ದರು. ī maneyalli nālku talemārinavaru oṭṭige iddaru. Four generations lived together in this house.
Plural: ತಲೆಮಾರುಗಳು, talemārugaḷu.

The word, taken apart.
ತಲೆಮಾರು (talemāru) is built on native tale, "head, top" (DEDR 3103); the origin of the second part, -ಮಾರು, is not settled.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for an ancestor, then the Kannada for a family generation.

You'll want to know: ಮನೆತನ.
ಮನೆತನ — manetana — "a family line; a household".
ಅವರದು ಹಳೆಯ ಶ್ರೀಮಂತ ಮನೆತನ. avaradu haḷeya śrīmanta manetana. Theirs is an old wealthy family.
ಮನೆ, house, with -ತನ, "-ness": the house as a family through time.

The word, taken apart.
ಮನೆತನ (manetana) is native Dravidian: DEDR 4776 lists Kannada manetana, "household, household life", built on mane, "a house".

Lesson 5 of 5.
ಅರಸ (arasa) — a monarch; a king.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a family generation, then the Kannada for a family line.

You'll want to know: ಅರಸ.
ಅರಸ — arasa — "a monarch; a king".
ಆ ಅರಸ ಪ್ರಜೆಗಳಿಗಾಗಿ ಕೆರೆಗಳನ್ನು ಕಟ್ಟಿಸಿದನು. ā arasa prajegaḷigāgi keregaḷannu kaṭṭisidanu. That king had lakes built for his people.
Plural: ಅರಸರು, arasaru.
An older Kannada form beside ರಾಜ; ಅರಮನೆ, a palace, is the king's house.

The word, taken apart.
ಅರಸ (arasa) goes back to Sanskrit rājan, "a chieftain, a king" (CDIAL 10679): DEDR 201 lists Kannada arasa, arasu, "a king, a lord", and ara-mane, "a palace", and derives them from the Sanskrit word. It is an old reshaped loan, beside the learned ರಾಜ.
