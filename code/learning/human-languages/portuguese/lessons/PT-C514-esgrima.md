## You'll want to know: a esgrima
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C514-RINGUE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**a esgrima** — "fencing".

> Na esgrima, os atletas usam uma máscara.
>
> *In fencing, the athletes wear a mask.*

The sport with swords.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*esgrima* comes from Old Italian *scrima* (EtymDB), which is usually traced to a Germanic verb meaning "to protect".
