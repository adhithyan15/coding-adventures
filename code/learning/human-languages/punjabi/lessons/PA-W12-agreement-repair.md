# Repair only the endings

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-SKILL-CONNECTED-READING] -->

Recall two old endings: the name sentence says **ਹੈ**; the speaker sentence
with **ਮੈਂ** says **ਹਾਂ**. Today no word order or spelling changes.

## Reading — spot one dimension
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-SKILL-CONNECTED-READING] -->

> ਮੇਰਾ ਨਾਂ ਅਮਨ ਹਾਂ। ਮੈਂ ਪੰਜਾਬੀ ਬੋਲਦਾ ਹੈ।

Both endings have traded places. The names, spaces, sentence marks, and
other words are already right. Point only to the two endings.

## Writing — repair the endings
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-SKILL-CONNECTED-READING] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: guided-copy -->

Rewrite the two sentences, changing **ਹਾਂ** after **ਅਮਨ** to **ਹੈ** and
**ਹੈ** after **ਬੋਲਦਾ** to **ਹਾਂ**. Leave every other part untouched.
