## You'll want to know: లారీ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C445-RAHADARI445-02]; assesses=[] -->

**లారీ** — *lārī* — "a lorry; a truck".

> లారీ నిండా ఇసుక ఉంది.
>
> *lārī niṇḍā isuka undi.*
>
> The lorry is full of sand.

Plural: లారీలు, *lārīlu*.

నిండా, full of, follows the load: ఇసుక, sand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**లారీ** (*lārī*) is a loan from English *lorry*, a goods truck.
