Malayalam, chapter 539: Dozing, Panting and Eating Well.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
മയങ്ങുക (mayaṅṅuka) — to doze off; to drowse.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for to hurt, then the Malayalam for to pass out.

You'll want to know: മയങ്ങുക.
മയങ്ങുക — mayaṅṅuka — "to doze off; to drowse".
ഊണ് കഴിഞ്ഞ് മുത്തശ്ശി കസേരയിൽ ഇരുന്ന് മയങ്ങി. ūṇŭ kaḻiññŭ muttaśśi kasērayil irunnŭ mayaṅṅi. After lunch grandmother dozed off in her chair.
Past: മയങ്ങി, mayaṅṅi.
A light sleep; the noun is മയക്കം, drowsiness, as after a meal or a medicine.

The word, taken apart.
മയങ്ങുക (mayaṅṅuka) is native Dravidian: DEDR 4706 gives it as to be drowsy or giddy, with മയക്കം, drowsiness (DEDR 4706).

Lesson 2 of 5.

Before the new one: say the Malayalam for to pass out, then the Malayalam for to doze off.

You'll want to know: കിതയ്ക്കുക.
കിതയ്ക്കുക — kitaykkuka — "to pant; to gasp".
പടി കയറി വന്ന മുത്തച്ഛൻ കിതച്ചു. paṭi kayaṟi vanna muttacchan kitaccu. Grandfather was panting after climbing the steps.
Past: കിതച്ചു, kitaccu.
The past is കിതച്ചു; the noun is കിതപ്പ്.

The word, taken apart.
കിതയ്ക്കുക (kitaykkuka) looks native, but DEDR has no entry that clearly holds it, so its origin is not settled.

You'll want to know: നീറുക.
നീറുക — nīṟuka — "to smart; to sting".
മുറിവിൽ മരുന്ന് വച്ചപ്പോൾ നീറി. muṟivil marunnŭ vaccappōḷ nīṟi. It stung when the medicine went on the wound.
Past: നീറി, nīṟi.
The sting of a cut or of smoke in the eyes; a grief can also നീറുക (nīṟuka) inside.

The word, taken apart.
നീറുക (nīṟuka) is native Dravidian: DEDR 3693 lists it with words for burning to ashes and for a grief that burns (DEDR 3693).

[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: ഭക്ഷണക്രമം.
ഭക്ഷണക്രമം — bhakṣaṇakramaṁ — "a diet; eating habits".
വൈദ്യൻ പറഞ്ഞതുപോലെ ഞാൻ ഭക്ഷണക്രമം മാറ്റി. vaidyan paṟaññatupōle ñān bhakṣaṇakramaṁ māṟṟi. I changed my diet as the doctor said.
ഭക്ഷണം food + ക്രമം order, an arrangement.

The word, taken apart.
ഭക്ഷണക്രമം (bhakṣaṇakramaṁ) is a learned compound built from ഭക്ഷണം, from Sanskrit bhakṣaṇa, "eating" (Monier-Williams), and krama, "a step, a series, an order" (CDIAL 3577).

കുട്ടികൾക്ക് പോഷകാഹാരം കിട്ടണം. kuṭṭikaḷkkŭ pōṣakāhāraṁ kiṭṭaṇaṁ. Children must get nutritious food.
