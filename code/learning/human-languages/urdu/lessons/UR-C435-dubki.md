## You'll want to know: ڈبکی
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C435-KHALIJ435-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ڈبکی** — *ḍubkī* — "a dip (under water)".

> سارا نے جھیل میں ایک ڈبکی لی۔
>
> *sārā ne jhīl meṅ ek ḍubkī lī.*
>
> Sara ducked under the water in the lake.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ڈبکی** (*ḍubkī*) is built on *ḍub-*, "sink", from *ḍubb*, a form CDIAL reconstructs (CDIAL 5561); **ڈبکی لگانا** is to take a dip.
