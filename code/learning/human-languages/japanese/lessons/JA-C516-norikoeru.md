# のりこえる (norikoeru) — to get over; to overcome

## You'll want to know: のりこえる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C516-AHEAD516-02]; assesses=[] -->

**のりこえる** — *norikoeru* — "to get over; to overcome".

> **もし この しけんを のりこえたら、そつぎょうです。** — *moshi kono shiken o norikoetara, sotsugyō desu* — "If I get through this exam, I graduate."

Polite **のりこえます**, *norikoemasu*; an ichidan verb. Past (polite): **かれが びょうきを のりこえました。**, *kare ga byōki o norikoemashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**のりこえる** (*norikoeru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, built from *noru*, "to ride", and *koeru*, "to cross"; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
