## You'll want to know: ущерб
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C353-USHCHERB-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ущерб** (*ushchérb*) — "damage, loss".

> Пожар причинил большой ущерб.
>
> *Pozhár prichiníl bolshóy ushchérb.*
>
> The fire caused great damage.

Masculine. *Нанести ущерб* — to cause damage. *Луна на ущербе* — the moon is waning.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ущерб** is usually traced to *у-* and the root of *щерба*, *щербина* "a notch, a gap": damage is a piece broken out of something.
