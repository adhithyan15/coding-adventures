## You'll want to know: задержка
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C423-ZADERZHKA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**задержка** (*zadérzhka*) — "a delay".

> Из-за тумана была большая задержка.
>
> *Iz-za tumána bylá bolsháya zadérzhka.*
>
> Because of the fog there was a long delay.

Feminine. *Рейс с задержкой* — a delayed flight.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*задержка* is built on *задержать* 'to hold back', from *за-* and *держать* 'to hold'. *Держать* is inherited from Proto-Slavic *dĭržati* 'to hold' (EtymDB).
