Hindi, chapter 503: The Generations.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
गृहस्थी (gṛhasthī) — a household; family life.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for to rock, then the Hindi for only (child).

You'll want to know: गृहस्थी.
गृहस्थी — gṛhasthī — "a household; family life".
शादी के बाद मीरा ने अपनी गृहस्थी सँभाली। śādī ke bād Mīrā ne apnī gṛhasthī sãbhālī. After her marriage Mira took charge of her household.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
गृहस्थी (gṛhasthī) is Sanskrit gṛhastha, "a householder", built on gṛha, "house", and stha, "standing in" (Monier-Williams), with the ending -ī: the life and business of a household.

Lesson 2 of 5.
मुखिया (mukhiyā) — the head (of a family or village).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for only (child), then the Hindi for a household.

You'll want to know: मुखिया.
मुखिया — mukhiyā — "the head (of a family or village)".
दादा हमारे परिवार के मुखिया हैं। dādā hamāre parivār ke mukhiyā haiṁ. Grandfather is the head of our family.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
मुखिया (mukhiyā) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Hindi mukhyā, "chief", under Sanskrit múkhya, "pertaining to the face" and "chief" (CDIAL 10174); mukhiyā is the usual spelling. múkhya is built on múkha, "face, front", the word behind mũh.

Lesson 3 of 5.
बुज़ुर्ग (buzurg) — an elder; elderly.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a household, then the Hindi for the head (of a family or village).

You'll want to know: बुज़ुर्ग.
बुज़ुर्ग — buzurg — "an elder; elderly".
गाँव के बुज़ुर्ग पेड़ के नीचे बैठकर बात करते हैं। gā̃v ke buzurg peṛ ke nīce baiṭhkar bāt karte haiṁ. The village elders sit under the tree and talk.
Masculine or feminine, by the person meant.

The word, taken apart.
बुज़ुर्ग (buzurg) is usually traced to Persian buzurg, "great", and so "an elder". It is a respectful word for anyone old.

Lesson 4 of 5.
पीढ़ी (pīṛhī) — a generation.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for the head (of a family or village), then the Hindi for an elder.

You'll want to know: पीढ़ी.
पीढ़ी — pīṛhī — "a generation".
हमारे घर में तीन पीढ़ियाँ एक साथ रहती हैं। hamāre ghar meṁ tīn pīṛhiyā̃ ek sāth rahtī haiṁ. Three generations live together in our house.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
पीढ़ी (pīṛhī) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Hindi pīṛhī both as "small square stool" and as "generation" under Sanskrit pīṭha, "seat" (CDIAL 8222).

Lesson 5 of 5.
पोसना (posnā) — to nurture; to rear.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for an elder, then the Hindi for a generation.

You'll want to know: पोसना.
पोसना — posnā — "to nurture; to rear".
दादी ने हमारे परिवार की तीन पीढ़ियों को पोसा। dādī ne hamāre parivār kī tīn pīṛhiyoṁ ko posā. Grandmother has nurtured three generations of our family.
Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने बच्चों को पोसा, usne baccoṁ ko posā.

The word, taken apart.
पोसना (posnā) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Hindi posnā, "to nourish, rear", under Sanskrit pōṣáyati, "nourishes" (CDIAL 8410).
