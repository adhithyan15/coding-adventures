## You'll want to know: شکستگی
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C325-ZAMINKHORDAN-05]; assesses=[] -->

**شکستگی** (*shekastegi*) — "a fracture, a break".

> دکتر گفت شکستگی پایش جدی نیست.
>
> *Doktor goft shekastegi-ye pâyash jeddi nist.*
>
> The doctor said the break in his leg was not serious.

From *shekaste* "broken", the participle of *shekastan* "to break".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*shekastegi* is built on *shekaste* "broken", the participle of the native verb *shekastan* "to break", with the noun suffix *-gi*.
