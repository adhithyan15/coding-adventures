## You'll want to know: वंशः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C322-PURVAJA322-02]; assesses=[] -->

**वंशः** — *vaṃśaḥ* — "a family line; a dynasty".

> सः राजा प्राचीनस्य वंशस्य अस्ति।
>
> *saḥ rājā prācīnasya vaṃśasya asti.*
>
> That king comes from an ancient line.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**वंशः** (*vaṃśaḥ*) is built on the image of a bamboo cane: MW explains the sense of a lineage, race or dynasty from the line of joints in a bamboo (MW).
