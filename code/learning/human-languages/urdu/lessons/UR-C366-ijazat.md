## You'll want to know: اجازت
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C366-IJAZAT366-03]; assesses=[] -->

**اجازت** — *ijāzat* — "permission".

> اگر ابو نے اجازت دی تو میں جاؤں گا۔
>
> *agar abbū ne ijāzat dī to maiṅ jāūṅ gā.*
>
> If my father gives permission, I will go.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**اجازت** (*ijāzat*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *ijāza*, "permission".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
