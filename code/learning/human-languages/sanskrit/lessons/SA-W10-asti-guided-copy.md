# अस्ति — copy one known word with its joined middle

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[SA-LEX-ASTI] -->

Read **अस्ति** aloud and say “is.” This is not new vocabulary. Find its three
parts: **अ + स्त + ि**. The short-*i* sign is written before the joined shape
you read it after: **स्ति**, *sti*.

## Writing — guided conjunct copy
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[SA-LEX-ASTI, SA-SCRIPT-RECOG-03, SA-SCRIPT-RECOG-05, SA-SCRIPT-RECOG-107, SA-SCRIPT-RECOG-108, SA-SCRIPT-RECOG-109] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: guided-copy -->

Keep **अस्ति** visible and copy it once. Pause after **अ**, then copy the joined
**स्त**, then place the short-*i* sign to make **स्ति**. Keep one continuous
headline across the joined cluster.

Compare your word with **अस्ति**. Check the independent **अ**, the smaller
first consonant in **स्त**, and the short-*i* sign. Read the finished word and
say its meaning.

## Wrap-up recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[SA-LEX-ASTI] -->

Which part of **अस्ति** is a conjunct? (**स्त**, *s* joined to *ta*.)
