## You'll want to know: かなしむ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C291-SOUSHIKI291-03]; assesses=[] -->

**かなしむ** — *kanashimu* — "to grieve; to mourn".

> **かぞくは みんな かなしんでいます。** — *kazoku wa minna kanashinde imasu* — "The whole family is in mourning."

Polite **かなしみます**, *kanashimimasu*; a godan verb. Past (polite): **かぞくが かなしみました。**, *kazoku ga kanashimimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**かなしむ** (*kanashimu*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict).
