## You'll want to know: உதை
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C532-SANDAI532-04]; assesses=[] -->

**உதை** — *utai* — "to kick".

> அவன் பந்தை வேகமாக உதைத்தான்.
>
> *avaṉ pantai vēkamāka utaittāṉ.*
>
> He kicked the ball hard.

Past (I …): உதைத்தேன், *utaittēṉ*.

What is kicked takes -ஐ: பந்தை உதை. The noun is the same: ஒரு உதை, a kick.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**உதை** (*utai*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 616 gives உதை (*utai*), "to kick, to plant the foot against".
