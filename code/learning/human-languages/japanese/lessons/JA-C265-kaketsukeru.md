## You'll want to know: かけつける
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C265-KEMURI265-05]; assesses=[] -->

**かけつける** — *kaketsukeru* — "to rush to the scene".

> **おまわりさんが すぐ かけつけました。** — *omawarisan ga sugu kaketsukemashita* — "A police officer was there in no time."

Polite **かけつけます**, *kaketsukemasu*; an ichidan verb. Past (polite): **しょうぼうしゃが かけつけました。**, *shōbōsha ga kaketsukemashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**かけつける** (*kaketsukeru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict).
