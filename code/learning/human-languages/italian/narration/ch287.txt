Italian, chapter 287: In onda.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
la diretta — the live broadcast.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for censorship, then the Italian for to censor.

You'll want to know: la diretta.
la diretta — "the live broadcast".
Guardiamo la partita in diretta. Let us watch the match live.
In diretta — live; the opposite is in differita, recorded.

The word, taken apart.
diretta is the feminine of diretto "direct", from Latin dīrēctus "straight", the past participle of dīrigere "to set straight" (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 3 of 5.
trasmettere — to broadcast; to pass on.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for the live broadcast, then the Italian for the programme.

You'll want to know: trasmettere.
trasmettere — "to broadcast; to pass on".
La radio trasmette musica tutta la notte. The radio broadcasts music all night.
Conjugated like mettere: ha trasmesso.

The word, taken apart.
trasmettere is a learned borrowing from Latin trānsmittere "to send across" (EtymDB), from trāns and mittere "to send".

Cambia canale, per favore! Change the channel, please!
In a city built on water, il canale is a canal.

Lesson 5 of 5.
l'emittente — the broadcaster, the station.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: l'emittente.
l'emittente — "the broadcaster, the station".
È un'emittente locale: trasmette solo in questa regione. It is a local station: it only broadcasts in this region.
Feminine: un'emittente.

The word, taken apart.
emittente is a learned word from Latin ēmittēns, the present participle of ēmittere "to send out" (Lewis & Short).
