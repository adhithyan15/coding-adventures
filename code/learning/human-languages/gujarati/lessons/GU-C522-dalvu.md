## You'll want to know: દળવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C522-KHAYNI522-05]; assesses=[] -->

**દળવું** — *daḷvũ* — "to mill grain".

> દાદી ઘઉં દળીને લોટ બનાવતી હતી.
>
> *dādī ghaũ daḷīne loṭ banāvtī hatī.*
>
> Grandmother used to grind wheat into flour.

Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે ઘઉં દળ્યા, *teṇe ghaũ daḷyā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**દળવું** (*daḷvũ*) is inherited from Sanskrit *dálati*, "bursts, splits", and CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb, "to crush, grind" (CDIAL 6216).
