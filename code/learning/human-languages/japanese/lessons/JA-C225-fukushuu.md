## You'll want to know: ふくしゅう
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C225-STUDY225-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ふくしゅう** — *fukushū* — "revision; going over lessons".

> **まいにち うちで ふくしゅうを します。** — *mainichi uchi de fukushū o shimasu* — "I do my revision at home every day."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ふくしゅう** (*fukushū*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *fuku*, "go back, again" (Middle Chinese *bjuwk*) and *shū*, "learn" (*zip*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
