## You'll want to know: o entroncamento
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C436-INDICACOES-04]; assesses=[] -->

**o entroncamento** — "the road junction; the railway junction".

> No entroncamento, viramos à esquerda.
>
> *At the junction, we turn left.*

Where two roads or railway lines meet like branches on a trunk.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*entroncamento* is built on *entroncar* 'to join together', on *tronco* 'trunk', which is inherited from Latin *truncus* (EtymDB).
