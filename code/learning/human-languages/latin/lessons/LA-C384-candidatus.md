## You'll want to know: candidātus, candidātī
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C384-SUFFRAGIUM-03]; assesses=[] -->

**candidātus, candidātī** — "a candidate".

> Candidātus cīvēs in forō salūtābat.
>
> *The candidate was greeting the citizens in the Forum.*

*candidātus, candidātī* m., from *candidus*, shining white: a man seeking office wore a toga whitened with chalk. English *candidate*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*candidātus* is built on *candidus* "shining white" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short): dressed in white. English *candidate* comes from it.
