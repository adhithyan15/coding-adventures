## You'll want to know: заселяться
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C429-ZASELENIYE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**заселяться** (*zaselyátsya*) — "to check into a hotel; to move in".

> В гостиницу можно заселяться после двух часов.
>
> *V gostínitsu mózhno zaselyátsya pósle dvukh chasóv.*
>
> You can check in to the hotel after two o'clock.

Imperfective; perfective *заселиться*. Takes *в* + accusative.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*заселяться* is built from *за-*, *селить* 'to settle' and *-ся*. *Селить* goes with *село* 'a village', which is inherited from Proto-Slavic *selo* (EtymDB).
