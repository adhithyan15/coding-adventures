# फूलना (phūlnā) — to puff up; to swell; to bloom

## You'll want to know: फूलना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C520-AATA520-05]; assesses=[] -->

**फूलना** — *phūlnā* — "to puff up; to swell; to bloom".

> गरम तवे पर रोटी फूल गई।
>
> *Garam tave par roṭī phūl gaī.*
>
> The roti puffed up on the hot griddle.

Past (it …): वह रोटी फूल गई, *vah roṭī phūl gaī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**फूलना** (*phūlnā*) is a tadbhava, inherited from Sanskrit *phullati*, "expands, opens (as a flower)" (CDIAL 9093). CDIAL gives the Hindi verb as "to swell, blossom".
