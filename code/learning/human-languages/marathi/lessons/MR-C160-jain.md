# मी जाईन (mī jāīn) — the future, person by person

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MR-LEX-C160-BHAV160-05, MR-LEX-UDYA, MR-LEX-BHETU] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "to plan", then "tomorrow", then **उद्या भेटू**.

## You'll want to know: who will do it?
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-GRAMMAR-C160-FUTURE-PERSON-01]; assesses=[MR-LEX-JANE, MR-LEX-MI, MR-LEX-TU-TUMHI, MR-LEX-TO-TI-TE] -->

> **उद्या मी पुण्याला जाईन.**
>
> *udyā mī puṇyālā jāīn.* — "Tomorrow I will go to Pune."

Use **मी जाईन**, **तू जाशील**, **तो / ती जाईल**, **आम्ही जाऊ**, **तुम्ही
जाल**, **ते जातील**. The future changes with the person, but not gender: a man
and a woman both say **मी जाईन**.

## You'll want to know: a few future stems
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-GRAMMAR-C160-FUTURE-STEMS-01]; assesses=[MR-LEX-YENE, MR-LEX-KARNE, MR-LEX-VACHNE, MR-LEX-C84-VERBS84-04, MR-LEX-C159-PAS159-05] -->

The endings stay while the stem changes: **येणे → येईन / येईल**, **करणे →
करीन / करेल**, **वाचणे → वाचीन / वाचेल**, **पडणे → पडेल**. Ask **काय
होईल?**, "What will happen?"

[PAUSE 1s]
- [YOU SAY: "Tomorrow I will go to Pune" — *udyā mī puṇyālā jāīn*]
- [YOU SAY: "It will rain in the evening" — *sandhyākāḷī pāūs paḍel*]
- [YOU SAY: "We will meet tomorrow" — *āmhī udyā bheṭū*]

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MR-GRAMMAR-C160-FUTURE-PERSON-01, MR-GRAMMAR-C160-FUTURE-STEMS-01] -->

[PAUSE 3s] "I will go"? (**जाईन**.) "We will go"? (**जाऊ**.) "You will go"
with respect? (**जाल**.) "They will go"? (**जातील**.) "It will rain"? (**पडेल**.)
