Gujarati, chapter 286: Calling the Police.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ગુનો (guno) — a crime.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to catch, then the Gujarati for people.

You'll want to know: ગુનો.
ગુનો — guno — "a crime".
ચોરી એક ગુનો છે. corī ek guno che. Theft is a crime.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ગુનો (guno) is usually traced to Persian gunāh, "a sin, a fault"; in Gujarati it is an offence against the law. It is masculine: મોટો ગુનો, a serious crime.

સિપાઈ (sipāī) is usually traced to Persian sipāhī, "a soldier", the word English took as sepoy. In Gujarati it is the constable on the beat.

Lesson 3 of 5.
શંકા (śankā) — a suspicion.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a crime, then the Gujarati for a constable.

You'll want to know: શંકા.
શંકા — śankā — "a suspicion".
મીરાને ચોકીદાર પર શંકા હતી. Mīrāne cokīdār par śankā hatī. Mira suspected the watchman.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
શંકા (śankā) is a tatsama, Sanskrit śaṅkā, "fear, distrust" (CDIAL 12258). The person suspected takes પર: ચોકીદાર પર શંકા, a suspicion about the watchman.

Lesson 4 of 5.
ખૂબ (khūb) — very; a great deal.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a constable, then the Gujarati for a suspicion.

You'll want to know: ખૂબ.
ખૂબ — khūb — "very; a great deal".
ચોરી પછી મીરા ખૂબ ડરી ગઈ. corī pachī Mīrā khūb ḍarī gaī. After the theft Mira was badly frightened.

The word, taken apart.
ખૂબ (khūb) is usually traced to Persian khūb, "good, fine". In Gujarati it makes a word stronger: ખૂબ સારું, very good; ખૂબ ડરી ગઈ, got very frightened.

Lesson 5 of 5.
છુપાવવું (chupāvvũ) — to hide (something).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a suspicion, then the Gujarati for very.

You'll want to know: છુપાવવું.
છુપાવવું — chupāvvũ — "to hide (something)".
ચોરે પૈસા પથ્થર નીચે છુપાવ્યા. core paisā paththar nīce chupāvyā. The thief hid the money under a stone.
Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે પૈસા છુપાવ્યા, teṇe paisā chupāvyā.

The word, taken apart.
છુપાવવું (chupāvvũ) is the causative of chupāvũ, to be hidden, which CDIAL lists under chupp, "to hide", a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 5058). You hide a thing, and the verb agrees with it: પૈસા છુપાવ્યા.
