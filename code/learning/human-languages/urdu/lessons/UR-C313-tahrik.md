## You'll want to know: تحریک
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C313-TEHRIK313-02]; assesses=[] -->

**تحریک** — *tahrīk* — "a movement (of people)".

> ہر شہر میں آزادی کی تحریک تھی۔
>
> *har shahr meṅ āzādī kī tahrīk thī.*
>
> There was a freedom movement in every city.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**تحریک** (*tahrīk*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *taḥrīk*, "a setting in motion": people moved to act together.
