## You'll want to know: ಪೊದೆ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C479-SASYA479-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ಪೊದೆ** — *pode* — "a bush; a thicket".

> ಮೊಲ ಪೊದೆಯೊಳಗೆ ಓಡಿತು.
>
> *mola podeyoḷage ōḍitu.*
>
> The rabbit ran into the bush.

Low, thick growth where small animals hide.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಪೊದೆ** (*pode*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 4509 lists Kannada *pode*, "a thatch, a bush, a bundle", beside *podaṟ*, "a thicket".
