## You'll want to know: hageln
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C462-UNWETTER-04]; assesses=[] -->

**hageln** — "to hail".

> Im Mai hat es plötzlich gehagelt.
>
> *In May it suddenly hailed.*

With *es*: *es hagelt*, *es hat gehagelt*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*hageln* is inherited from Middle High German *hagelen* (EtymDB), built on *Hagel* 'hail'.
