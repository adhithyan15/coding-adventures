## You'll want to know: അലറുക
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C517-VIKARAM517-03]; assesses=[] -->

**അലറുക** — *alaṟuka* — "to bellow; to roar".

> ദേഷ്യം വന്നപ്പോൾ അയാൾ അലറി.
>
> *dēṣyaṁ vannappōḷ ayāḷ alaṟi.*
>
> When he got angry, he bellowed.

Past: അലറി, *alaṟi*.

Said of an angry person, a tiger or a storm; the past is അലറി.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**അലറുക** (*alaṟuka*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 245 gives the Malayalam verb as to roar or bellow, as an elephant or a tiger does (DEDR 245).
