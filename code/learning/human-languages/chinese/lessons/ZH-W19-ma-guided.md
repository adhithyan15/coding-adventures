# 吗 — copy it, and keep the mouth small

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ZH-SCRIPT-MA-01, ZH-ORTHO-KANSHU-01, ZH-SCRIPT-KOU, ZH-LEX-KANJIAN-01, ZH-ORTHO-KANJIAN-01, ZH-LEX-XIAOXUE-01] -->

[PAUSE 12s] Read **看书** and give its meaning. Then air-write **mouth, mouth,
mouth — turn, turn-and-hook, long bar**. Say **see** and **primary school**,
and write **看见**.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ZH-SCRIPT-MA-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: guided-copy -->

Copy **吗** twice beside the model. On the second copy, stop after the third
stroke and check two things before going on: the 口 fills about a third of the
width, and its bottom sits above the middle of the square.

## Guided Practice — Spaced Return
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ZH-ORTHO-KANSHU-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: dictation-transcription -->

Hear **read a book** and write 看书 once without a model.
