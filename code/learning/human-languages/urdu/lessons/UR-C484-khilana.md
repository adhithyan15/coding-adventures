## You'll want to know: کھلانا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C484-RAISE484-04]; assesses=[] -->

**کھلانا** — *khilānā* — "to feed".

> ماں بچے کو کھانا کھلاتی ہے۔
>
> *māṅ bachche ko khānā khilātī hai.*
>
> The mother feeds the child.

Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے بچے کو کھانا کھلایا, *us ne bachche ko khānā khilāyā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**کھلانا** (*khilānā*) is the causative of **کھانا**, "to eat", inherited from Sanskrit *khādati*, "chews, eats" (CDIAL 3865).
