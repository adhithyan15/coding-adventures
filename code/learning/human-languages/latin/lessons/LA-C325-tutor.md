## You'll want to know: tūtor, tūtōris
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C325-BULLA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**tūtor, tūtōris** — "a guardian, a protector".

> Avunculus tūtor puerōrum factus est.
>
> *Their uncle became the boys' guardian.*

Masculine, 3rd declension. A child with no father had a *tūtor* to keep his property safe. English *tutor*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**tūtor** is built on *tueor*, "to watch over, to protect" (Lewis & Short): a protector. English *tutor* comes from it.
