## You'll want to know: zhuīqiú
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C512-DREAMS512-02]; assesses=[] -->

*zhuīqiú* — "to pursue (a goal); to chase a dream".

> *wǒ xiǎng zhuīqiú zìjǐ de mèngxiǎng* — I want to follow my own dream.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*zhuīqiú* is built from *zhuī* 'to chase' and *qiú* 'to seek', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *trwij* and *gjuw* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to chase' and the second 'to search for'.
