Persian, chapter 228: Sizdah Bedar, Chaharshanbe Suri, New Year, Turn of the year, Birth.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
سیزده بدر (sizdah bedar) — Sizdah Bedar (the outdoor 13th day of Nowruz).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for an accident, then the Persian for a dentist.

You'll want to know: سیزده بدر.
سیزده بدر — sizdah bedar — "Sizdah Bedar (the outdoor 13th day of Nowruz)".

The word, taken apart.
sizdah bedar is built from sizdah, "thirteen" (se, "three", with dah, "ten"), and be-dar, "to the door, outside". All the pieces are inherited Persian words.

Lesson 2 of 5.
چهارشنبه سوری (chahârshanbe suri) — Chaharshanbe Suri (the fire festival before Nowruz).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a dentist, then the Persian for Sizdah Bedar.

You'll want to know: چهارشنبه سوری.
چهارشنبه سوری — chahârshanbe suri — "Chaharshanbe Suri (the fire festival before Nowruz)".

The word, taken apart.
chahârshanbe suri is "festive Wednesday": chahârshanbe, "Wednesday" (chahâr, "four", with shanbe, "Sabbath", EtymDB), with suri, usually taken as "festive", from sur, "feast".

sâl-e now is "the new year": sâl, "year", continues Middle Persian sāl (EtymDB), and now, "new", is inherited too.

Lesson 4 of 5.
تحویل سال (tahvil-e sâl) — the turn of the year (the moment of the New Year).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for Chaharshanbe Suri, then the Persian for the New Year.

You'll want to know: تحویل سال.
تحویل سال — tahvil-e sâl — "the turn of the year (the moment of the New Year)".

The word, taken apart.
tahvil-e sâl is "the turning over of the year": tahvil is borrowed from Arabic taḥwīl, "transfer, turning" (root ḥ-w-l, Lane), and sâl, "year", is inherited (EtymDB).
