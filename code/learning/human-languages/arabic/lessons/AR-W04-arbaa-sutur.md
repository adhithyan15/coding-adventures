# Four lines, and the joiner that is not a separate word

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[AR-LEX-C38-WA-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] The last lesson asked for writing **finished on time**. This one asks
for writing that **holds together**.

[PAUSE 1s] Pick one person who will read it.

## You'll want to know
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[AR-LEX-C38-LAKIN-01, AR-LEX-C40-LIANNA-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Connected writing is not **longer** writing. Four short lines can be
connected; one long line can fail to be. What makes it connected is that a
sentence stops standing on its own.

[PAUSE 2s] You hold three joiners. **و** adds. **لكن** sets against. **لأن**
gives the reason.

[PAUSE 2s] And the first of those does something the others do not. **لكن** and
**لأن** stand as words, with space on both sides. **و** does not. It is written
**onto the front of the next word**, with no space at all.

[PAUSE 2s] So in Arabic the join is not a word the eye can skip over on its way
to the content. It is a letter the following word now begins with — the two
things being joined are physically fastened, and a reader meets the joint before
they meet the second thing.

[PAUSE 2s] A hand that leaves a space after **و** has not made a small
typographic slip. It has written a letter that now belongs to nothing.

## Writing — connected composition
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[AR-LEX-C40-LIANNA-01, AR-LEX-C38-WA-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: connected-composition -->

[PAUSE 2s] Write **four lines** to the person you picked, right to left.

At least one must use **و** — attached, no space. At least one must use **لأن**.

[PAUSE 3s] Use only words you already write. This lesson adds no vocabulary; the
whole exercise is the joining.

## Wrap-up recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[AR-LEX-C38-WA-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Cover your first line and read the second alone. Does it still make
sense without the first? If it does, you wrote a **list** — which is where you
were one lesson ago.

[PAUSE 2s] Then look at every **و** you wrote. Is there a space after it? There
should not be.
