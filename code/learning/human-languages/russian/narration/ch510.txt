Russian, chapter 510: Winning and Losing.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
поражение (porazhéniye) — a defeat.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a goal, then the Russian for to score.

You'll want to know: поражение.
поражение (porazhéniye) — "a defeat".
После поражения игроки долго молчали. Pósle porazhéniya igrokí dólgo molcháli. After the defeat the players were silent for a long time.
Neuter. Потерпеть поражение (porazhéniye) — to suffer a defeat.

The word, taken apart.
поражение (porazhéniye) is built on поразить 'to strike, to defeat', from разить 'to strike'; EtymDB lists it, glossed 'defeat', without a source.

Lesson 2 of 5.
ничья (nichyá) — a tie (in a game).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to score, then the Russian for a defeat.

You'll want to know: ничья.
ничья (nichyá) — "a tie (in a game)".
Ничья устроила обе команды. Nichyá ustróila óbe komándy. The tie suited both teams.
Feminine, declined like an adjective. Сыграть вничью — to end level.

The word, taken apart.
ничья (nichyá) comes from ничья (nichyá) игра 'nobody's game' (EtymDB), built on ничей 'nobody's'.

Lesson 3 of 5.

Warm-up.

You'll want to know: соперник.
соперник (sopérnik) — "a rival, an opponent".
Сегодня у нас очень сильный соперник. Segódnya u nas óchen sílnyy sopérnik. Today we have a very strong opponent.
Masculine; feminine соперница.

The word, taken apart.
соперник (sopérnik) is inherited from Old East Slavic sъpьrьnikъ, built with the prefix that EtymDB traces to Proto-Slavic sǫ- 'together'.

Lesson 5 of 5.
лидировать (lidírovat) — to be in the lead.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a rival, then the Russian for a loss.

You'll want to know: лидировать.
лидировать (lidírovat) — "to be in the lead".
После первого тайма лидирует наша команда. Pósle pérvogo táyma lidíruyet násha kománda. After the first half our team is in the lead.
Imperfective; present лидирую. Built on лидер — a leader.

The word, taken apart.
лидировать (lidírovat) is built on лидер 'a leader', which was borrowed from English leader (EtymDB), with -ировать; EtymDB has no entry for the verb.
