## You'll want to know: 飞
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C413-FLY413-02]; assesses=[] -->

**飞** — *fēi* — "to fly".

> **飞机飞得很高** *fēijī fēi de hěn gāo* — The plane is flying very high.

The new character, in pieces: 飞 — three strokes: a bar that turns and slants down to the right, ending in a hook up; then a short stroke falling to the left and a dot, near the top on the right. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**飞** (*fēi*) is inherited from Middle Chinese *pj+j* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'to fly, to soar'.
