## You'll want to know: ていえん
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C405-TEIEN405-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ていえん** — *teien* — "a landscaped garden".

> **てらの ていえんは しずかでした。** — *tera no teien wa shizuka deshita* — "The temple garden was quiet."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ていえん** (*teien*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *tei*, "garden" (Middle Chinese *deng*) and *en*, "park" (*hjwon*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
