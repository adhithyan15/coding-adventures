## You'll want to know: le store
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C430-BESTIOLES-05]; assesses=[] -->

**le store** — "the blind (on a window)".

> Le store est cassé, il ne descend plus.
>
> *The blind is broken; it will not come down any more.*

*Baisser le store* — to pull down the blind.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*store* is usually traced to Italian *stora*; behind it is Latin *storea* 'mat of rushes' (EtymDB).
