Lesson 1 of 6.
じゅけんする (juken suru) — to sit an entrance exam.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for a forecast, then the Japanese for an outlook.

You'll want to know: じゅけんする.
じゅけんする — juken suru — "to sit an entrance exam".
もし らいねん じゅけんしたら、まいにち べんきょうします。 — moshi rainen jukenshitara, mainichi benkyō shimasu — "If I sit the entrance exam next year, I will study every day."
Polite じゅけんします, jukenshimasu; a する verb. Past (polite): むすこが ふたつの がっこうを じゅけんしました。, musuko ga futatsu no gakkō o jukenshimashita.

The word, taken apart.
じゅけんする (juken suru) is the Sino-Japanese (kango) noun juken with the native verb suru, "to do": two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are ju, "accept" (Middle Chinese dzyuwX) and ken, "verification" (ngjemH) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).

Lesson 2 of 6.
うかる (ukaru) — to pass (an exam).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for an outlook, then the Japanese for to sit an entrance exam.

You'll want to know: うかる.
うかる — ukaru — "to pass (an exam)".
もし しけんに うかったら、みんなで いわいます。 — moshi shiken ni ukattara, minna de iwaimasu — "If I pass the exam, we will all celebrate."
Polite うかります, ukarimasu; a godan verb. Past (polite): あねが しけんに うかりました。, ane ga shiken ni ukarimashita.

The word, taken apart.
うかる (ukaru) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi, and its older origin is not settled (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
