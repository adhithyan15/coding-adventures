# অনুভব করা (ônubhôb kôrā) — to feel; to sense

## You'll want to know: অনুভব করা
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C438-MON438-04]; assesses=[] -->

**অনুভব করা** — *ônubhôb kôrā* — "to feel; to sense".

> মীরা বন্ধুর ব্যথা অনুভব করল।
>
> *mīrā bôndhur bæthā ônubhôb kôrlô.*
>
> Mira felt her friend's pain.

Past (he/she …): তিনি ভয় অনুভব করলেন, *tini bhôy ônubhôb kôrlen*.

অনুভব alone is the noun: what you feel from the inside.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**অনুভব করা** (*ônubhôb kôrā*) is Sanskrit *anubhava*, "perception, understanding" (Monier-Williams), with *kôrā*, "to do" (CDIAL 2814).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
