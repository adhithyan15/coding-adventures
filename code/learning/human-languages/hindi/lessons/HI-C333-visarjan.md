## You'll want to know: विसर्जन
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C333-GHAT333-02]; assesses=[] -->

**विसर्जन** — *visarjan* — "immersion (of an image)".

> शाम को नदी में मूर्ति का विसर्जन हुआ।
>
> *śām ko nadī meṁ mūrti kā visarjan huā.*
>
> In the evening the image was immersed in the river.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**विसर्जन** (*visarjan*) is Sanskrit *visarjana*, "a letting go, a sending away" (Monier-Williams): at the end of a festival the image is let go into the water.
