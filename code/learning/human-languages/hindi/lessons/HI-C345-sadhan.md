## You'll want to know: साधन
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C345-UDDESHYA345-04]; assesses=[] -->

**साधन** — *sādhan* — "a means, a resource".

> गाँव तक जाने का साधन सिर्फ़ बस है।
>
> *gā̃v tak jāne kā sādhan sirf bas hai.*
>
> The only way to get to the village is the bus.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**साधन** (*sādhan*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *sādhana*, "a means, an instrument" (Monier-Williams).
