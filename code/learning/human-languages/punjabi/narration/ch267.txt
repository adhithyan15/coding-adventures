Punjabi, chapter 267: Which Floor, Which Office?.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ਥਾਣਾ (thāṇā) — a police station.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for a roadside eatery, then the Punjabi for a building.

You'll want to know: ਥਾਣਾ.
ਥਾਣਾ — thāṇā — "a police station".
ਥਾਣਾ ਬਾਜ਼ਾਰ ਦੇ ਨੇੜੇ ਹੈ। thāṇā bāzār de neṛe hai. The police station is near the market.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ਥਾਣਾ (thāṇā) is inherited from Sanskrit sthāna, "firm stance, place of abode"; CDIAL lists Punjabi thāṇā, "place, police station", under it (CDIAL 13753).

Lesson 2 of 5.
ਮੰਜ਼ਿਲ (manzil) — a storey; a floor of a building.

Warm-up.

Before the new one: say the Punjabi for a building, then the Punjabi for a police station.

You'll want to know: ਮੰਜ਼ਿਲ.
ਮੰਜ਼ਿਲ — manzil — "a storey; a floor of a building".
ਮੇਰਾ ਦਫ਼ਤਰ ਦੂਜੀ ਮੰਜ਼ਿਲ ਉੱਤੇ ਹੈ। merā daftar dūjī manzil utte hai. My office is on the second floor.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ਮੰਜ਼ਿਲ (manzil) is a loan, usually traced through Persian to Arabic manzil, "a halting place".

Lesson 3 of 5.
ਡਾਕਖ਼ਾਨਾ (ḍākkhānā) — a post office.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for a police station, then the Punjabi for a storey.

You'll want to know: ਡਾਕਖ਼ਾਨਾ.
ਡਾਕਖ਼ਾਨਾ — ḍākkhānā — "a post office".
ਡਾਕਖ਼ਾਨਾ ਥਾਣੇ ਦੇ ਸਾਹਮਣੇ ਹੈ। ḍākkhānā thāṇe de sāhmaṇe hai. The post office is opposite the police station.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ਡਾਕਖ਼ਾਨਾ (ḍākkhānā) is built from ḍāk, "the post", for which CDIAL gives no Punjabi form (CDIAL 5519), and khānā, "house", usually traced to Persian khāna.
