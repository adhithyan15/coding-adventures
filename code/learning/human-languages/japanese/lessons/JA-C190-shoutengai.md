## You'll want to know: しょうてんがい
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C190-TOWN190-02]; assesses=[] -->

**しょうてんがい** — *shōtengai* — "shopping street; shopping district".

> **えきの まえに しょうてんがいが あります。** — *eki no mae ni shōtengai ga arimasu* — "There is a shopping street in front of the station."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**しょうてんがい** (*shōtengai*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): three Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *shō*, "trade" (Middle Chinese *syang*), *ten*, "shop" (*temH*) and *gai*, "street" (*kea*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
