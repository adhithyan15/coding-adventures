Russian, chapter 103: Hat, Jacket, Shirt, Trousers, Dress.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
шапка (shápka) — a hat.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for shoes, then the Russian for socks.
