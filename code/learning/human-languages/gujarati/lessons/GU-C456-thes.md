## You'll want to know: ઠેસ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C456-DUBHA456-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ઠેસ** — *ṭhes* — "a knock; a hurt to one's pride".

> તેના જવાબથી મને ઘણી ઠેસ લાગી.
>
> *tenā javābthī mane ghaṇī ṭhes lāgī.*
>
> His answer was a hard knock for me.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ઠેસ** (*ṭhes*) goes back to a reconstructed *\*ṭhēcc-*, "push"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati word, "knock" (CDIAL 5511), and Gujarati also uses it for a knock to the feelings.
