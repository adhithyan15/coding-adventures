Marwadi (Marwari), chapter 110: To share out, To mislay, To reach, To fly, To weigh.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
बांटणो (bā̃ṭṇo) — to share out.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for to lose (a game), then the Marwadi for to hit.

You'll want to know: बांटणो.
बांटणो — bā̃ṭṇo — "to share out".

The word, taken apart.
बांटणो (bā̃ṭṇo) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from vaṇṭati, "shares". CDIAL lists Old Marwari bāṃṭaï under it (CDIAL 11238).

You'll want to know: गमावणो.
गमावणो — gamāvṇo — "to mislay, to lose (a thing)".

The word, taken apart.
गमावणो (gamāvṇo) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from gamayati, "causes to go". CDIAL lists Old Marwari gavī under it (CDIAL 4028). The Old Marwari row means "lost"; the sense moved from "send away" to "lose".

Lesson 3 of 5.
पूगणो (pūgṇo) — to reach, to arrive.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for to share out, then the Marwadi for to mislay.

You'll want to know: पूगणो.
पूगणो — pūgṇo — "to reach, to arrive".

The word, taken apart.
पूगणो (pūgṇo) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from pūryate, "is filled". CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry (CDIAL 8342); the match is through Gujarati pugvũ. The Gujarati row means "to reach", as पूगणो (pūgṇo) does; Old Gujarati pūjaï means "reaches".
