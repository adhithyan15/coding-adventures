## You'll want to know: काजळ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C183-HATH183-04]; assesses=[] -->

**काजळ** — *kājaḷ* — "kohl".

> छोरी आंख मांय काजळ लगावै है।
>
> *chhorī ā̃kh mā̃y kājaḷ lagāvai hai.*
>
> The girl puts kohl in her eyes.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**काजळ** (*kājaḷ*) is inherited from Sanskrit *kajjala*, "lamp-black", a word CDIAL thinks may come from Dravidian or Munda; it lists Gujarati *kājaḷ* and Hindi *kājal* (CDIAL 2622). CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry.
