## You'll want to know: dàhǎi
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C238-SKY238-04]; assesses=[] -->

*dàhǎi* — "the open sea".

> *wǒ hěn xǐhuan dàhǎi* — I really love the sea.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*dàhǎi* is built from 大 'big' and *hǎi* 'sea', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *daH* and *xojX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing things.
