## You'll want to know: ひみつ

**ひみつ** — *himitsu* — "a secret".

> **これは かぞくの ひみつですか。** — *kore wa kazoku no himitsu desu ka* — "Is this a family secret?"

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ひみつ** (*himitsu*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *hi*, "hidden" (Middle Chinese *pijH*) and *mitsu*, "close, dense" (*mit*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
