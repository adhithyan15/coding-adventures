## You'll want to know: てま
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C480-MAKE480-04]; assesses=[] -->

**てま** — *tema* — "time and effort; trouble".

> **この しごとは てまが かかります。** — *kono shigoto wa tema ga kakarimasu* — "This work takes a lot of time and effort."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**てま** (*tema*) is a native Japanese compound (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese: built from *te*, "hand" and *ma*, "interval" (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
