## You'll want to know: शिबिका
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C295-CETANA295-03]; assesses=[] -->

**शिबिका** — *śibikā* — "a litter; a stretcher".

> ते आहतं शिबिकया नयन्ति।
>
> *te āhataṃ śibikayā nayanti.*
>
> They carry the injured man on a litter.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**शिबिका** (*śibikā*) MW gives *śibikā* as a palanquin, a litter or a bier, without a derivation, so its origin is uncertain; a stretcher is its modern use (MW).
