# పీఠభూమి (pīṭhabhūmi) — a plateau

## You'll want to know: పీఠభూమి
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C474-SIKHARAM474-02]; assesses=[] -->

**పీఠభూమి** — *pīṭhabhūmi* — "a plateau".

> ఆ పీఠభూమి మీద వర్షం తక్కువ.
>
> *ā pīṭhabhūmi mīda varṣaṁ takkuva.*
>
> There is little rain on that plateau.

పీఠ, a seat, a stool, + భూమి, land: flat land raised up like a stool.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**పీఠభూమి** (*pīṭhabhūmi*) is a compound of two words borrowed from Sanskrit: *pīṭha*, "a stool, a bench" (CDIAL 8222), and *bhūmi*, "earth, ground" (CDIAL 9557).
