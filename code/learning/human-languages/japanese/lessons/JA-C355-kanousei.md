## You'll want to know: かのうせい
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C355-TABUN355-04]; assesses=[] -->

**かのうせい** — *kanōsei* — "a possibility; a chance".

> **くもったら、あめの かのうせいが あるでしょう。** — *kumottara, ame no kanōsei ga aru deshō* — "If it clouds over, there will be a chance of rain."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**かのうせい** (*kanōsei*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): three Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *ka*, "can" (Middle Chinese *khaX*), *nō*, "ability" (*nong*) and *sei*, "nature" (*sjengH*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
