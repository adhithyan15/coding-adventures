Sanskrit, chapter 300: When the Ground Shook.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
भूकम्पः (bhūkampaḥ) — an earthquake.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for keeps away, then the Sanskrit for a misfortune.

You'll want to know: भूकम्पः.
भूकम्पः — bhūkampaḥ — "an earthquake".
ह्यः रात्रौ भूकम्पः अभवत्। hyaḥ rātrau bhūkampaḥ abhavat. There was an earthquake last night.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
भूकम्पः (bhūkampaḥ) is built from bhū, "the earth", and kampa, "trembling"; MW gives bhūkampa as an earthquake (MW).

Lesson 2 of 5.
कम्पते (kampate) — shakes; trembles.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a misfortune, then the Sanskrit for an earthquake.

You'll want to know: कम्पते.
कम्पते — kampate — "shakes; trembles".
भूमिः कम्पते, जनाः पलायन्ते। bhūmiḥ kampate, janāḥ palāyante. The ground shakes and people run away.
Past (it …): भूमिः कम्पितवती, bhūmiḥ kampitavatī.

The word, taken apart.
कम्पते (kampate) comes from the root √kamp, "to tremble, shake"; MW compares Greek kamptō (MW).

[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: ईषत्.
ईषत् — īṣat — "slightly".
प्रथमं भूमिः ईषत् कम्पते। prathamaṃ bhūmiḥ īṣat kampate. At first the ground shakes slightly.

The word, taken apart.
ईषत् (īṣat) MW gives īṣat as an indeclinable meaning a little, slightly, without a derivation, so its origin is uncertain (MW).

गर्तः (gartaḥ) MW gives this garta as the same word as karta, a hollow, hole or cave, and gives it no root, so its origin is uncertain (MW).
