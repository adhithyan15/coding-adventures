German, chapter 496: Ein Baby im Haus.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
stillen — to breastfeed; to quench.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to obey, then the German for puberty.

You'll want to know: stillen.
stillen — "to breastfeed; to quench".
Sie stillt ihr Baby alle drei Stunden. She breastfeeds her baby every three hours.
Also to quench: den Durst stillen.

The word, taken apart.
stillen is built on still "quiet, calm" (EtymDB): first to make quiet, then to calm a baby at the breast.

What you've built.
One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.

Lesson 5 of 5.

Ohne Schnuller schläft das Baby nicht ein. The baby won't fall asleep without a dummy.
Plural: die Schnuller. In Switzerland: der Nuggi.

The word, taken apart.
Schnuller is usually traced to the dialect verb schnullen "to suck"; EtymDB has no entry for it.
