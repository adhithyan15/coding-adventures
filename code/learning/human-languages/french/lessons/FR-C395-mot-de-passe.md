## You'll want to know: le mot de passe
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C395-COMPTE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**le mot de passe** — "the password".

> J'ai encore oublié mon mot de passe.
>
> *I forgot my password again.*

The plural is *les mots de passe*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*mot de passe* is a word that lets you pass. *mot* comes from Late Latin *muttum* "a mutter, a grunt" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short), and *passer* from Vulgar Latin *passāre*, built on *passus* "a step" (Lewis & Short).
