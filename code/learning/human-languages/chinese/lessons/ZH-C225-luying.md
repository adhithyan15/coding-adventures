## You'll want to know: lùyíng
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C225-FARM225-05]; assesses=[] -->

*lùyíng* — "to camp out".

> *zhōumò wǒmen qù lùyíng* — We're going camping at the weekend.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*lùyíng* is built from *lù* 'dew, the open air' and *yíng* 'a camp'. The second is inherited from Middle Chinese *yweng* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'to build'; the dictionary explains the first, *luH* there, as dew. How the pair came to mean camping is not settled.
