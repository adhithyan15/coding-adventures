Arabic, chapter 258: To respect, To forgive, To lie, To take part, To disagree.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
احترم (iḥtarama) — to respect.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to be afraid, then the Arabic for to smile.

You'll want to know: احترم.
احترم — iḥtarama — "to respect".

[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does احترم (iḥtarama) mean? ("To respect".) Say it once more.
