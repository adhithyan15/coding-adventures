## You'll want to know: la frontera
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C552-EXILIO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**la frontera** — "the border, the frontier".

> Cruzaron la frontera de noche.
>
> *They crossed the border at night.*

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*frontera* is usually traced to an old word for 'facing, opposite', from Latin *frōns*, *frontem* 'forehead, front' (EtymDB). The border is the line where two countries face each other.
