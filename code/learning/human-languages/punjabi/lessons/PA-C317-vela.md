## You'll want to know: ਵੇਲਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C317-PAST317-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ਵੇਲਾ** — *velā* — "a particular time".

> ਉਸ ਵੇਲੇ ਮੈਂ ਸਕੂਲ ਵਿੱਚ ਸੀ।
>
> *us vele maiṁ sakūl vich sī.*
>
> At that time I was at school.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਵੇਲਾ** (*velā*) is inherited from Sanskrit *velā*, "a limit, a time"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *velā*, "time", under it (CDIAL 12115).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
