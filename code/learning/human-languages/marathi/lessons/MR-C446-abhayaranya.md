## You'll want to know: अभयारण्य
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C446-ABHAYARANYA446-02]; assesses=[] -->

**अभयारण्य** — *abhayāraṇya* — "a sanctuary".

> अभयारण्यात शिकार करायला बंदी आहे.
>
> *abhayāraṇyāt śikār karāylā bandī āhe.*
>
> Hunting is banned in the sanctuary.

Neuter.

अभय, without fear, and अरण्य, a forest.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अभयारण्य** (*abhayāraṇya*) is a modern word built from Sanskrit *abhaya*, "without fear, safe", and *araṇya*, "a forest" (Monier-Williams): a forest where animals are safe.
