## You'll want to know: گردشگر
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C472-TABIAT-03]; assesses=[] -->

**گردشگر** (*gardeshgar*) — "a tourist".

> در تابستان این جزیره پر از گردشگر است.
>
> *Dar tâbestân in jazire por az gardeshgar ast.*
>
> In summer this island is full of tourists.

*gardesh* 'outing' + *-gar* 'doer'. Tourism is *gardeshgari*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*gardeshgar* is a native compound of *gardesh* 'an outing', built on *gashtan* 'to turn, to wander', and the ending *-gar* 'doer'.
