# ಮುಖವಾಡ (mukhavāḍa) — a mask

## You'll want to know: ಮುಖವಾಡ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C536-NATAKA536-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ಮುಖವಾಡ** — *mukhavāḍa* — "a mask".

> ನಾಟಕದಲ್ಲಿ ನಟರು ಮುಖವಾಡ ಹಾಕಿದ್ದರು.
>
> *nāṭakadalli naṭaru mukhavāḍa hākiddaru.*
>
> The actors wore masks in the play.

Also a false face: ಅವನ ಮುಖವಾಡ ಕಳಚಿತು, his mask slipped.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಮುಖವಾಡ** (*mukhavāḍa*) is built on Sanskrit *mukha*, "mouth, face" (CDIAL 10158); the origin of the ending -*vāḍa* is not settled.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
