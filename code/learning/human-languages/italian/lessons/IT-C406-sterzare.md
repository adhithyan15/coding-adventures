## You'll want to know: sterzare
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C406-GUIDA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**sterzare** — "to steer, to swerve".

> Ho sterzato per evitare un cane.
>
> *I swerved to avoid a dog.*

*Lo sterzo* is the steering; *sterzare a sinistra* — to turn the wheel left.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*sterzare* is built on *sterzo* 'steering', which was borrowed from Lombardic *sterz* 'the handle of a plough' (EtymDB): the farmer guided the plough by it.
