## You'll want to know: улика
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C287-PRESTUPLENIYE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**улика** (*ulíka*) — "a clue, a piece of evidence".

> Полицейский нашёл важную улику.
>
> *Politséyskiy nashyól vázhnuyu ulíku.*
>
> The policeman found an important clue.

Evidence as a whole is plural: *улики*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*улика* is built on *уличить* "to catch out, to expose", which is usually traced to *лик* "face": to bring face to face with the facts. EtymDB lists no parent.
