## You'll want to know: flotar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C688-MAR-05]; assesses=[] -->

**flotar** — "to float".

> En este mar flotas sin esfuerzo.
>
> *In this sea you float easily.*

*El flotador* is a rubber ring.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*flotar* was borrowed from French *flotter* "to float" (EtymDB), a word of Germanic origin, a cousin of English *float*.
