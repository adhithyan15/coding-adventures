# 年 — the year, six strokes

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[JA-LEX-C253-NENJUU-01, JA-SCRIPT-KANJI-TSUKI-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say *nenjū*, all year round. Then call up the four strokes of **月**: left side, top and right side, two bars.

## Script — the shape
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-SCRIPT-KANJI-NEN-01]; assesses=[JA-SCRIPT-KANJI-TSUKI-01] -->

> **年** — *nen*

Its parts:

- a short falling stroke at the top
- three bars
- a short stem on the left
- a long centre stem

Six strokes, five lifts:

1. draw the short falling stroke at the top, down to the left
2. lift, draw the top bar from left to right
3. lift, draw the second bar from left to right
4. lift, draw the short stem on the left, down from the second bar
5. lift, draw the long third bar from left to right
6. lift, draw the long centre stem from the top bar straight down through the others

The long stem comes last and runs through the bars below the top one. Here it is read **ねん** (*nen*), as in **年じゅう**; this lesson asks only for that reading.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[JA-SCRIPT-KANJI-NEN-01, JA-LEX-C253-NENJUU-01] -->

1. Find **年** in **年じゅう** and say *nenjū*.
2. Copy **年** three times, saying *nen* at each one.
3. Find it once more, in **ここは 年じゅう あついです** (*koko wa nenjū atsui desu*), and say the line.
