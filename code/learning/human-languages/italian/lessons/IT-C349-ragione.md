## You'll want to know: la ragione
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C349-CAUSA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**la ragione** — "the reason; reason (the faculty)".

> Per quale ragione sei arrivato tardi?
>
> *For what reason did you arrive late?*

*Avere ragione* — to be right; *dare ragione a qualcuno* — to say someone is right.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ragione* is inherited from Latin *ratiō, ratiōnis* "a reckoning, an account, reason" (Lewis & Short), built on *rērī* "to think, to reckon".
