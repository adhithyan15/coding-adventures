French, chapter 293: Un mystère.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
le mystère — the mystery.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for the clue, then the French for the print.

You'll want to know: le mystère.
le mystère — "the mystery".
Personne ne sait où est le tableau: c'est un mystère. Nobody knows where the painting is: it is a mystery.
The adjective is mystérieux.

The word, taken apart.
mystère is a learned borrowing from Latin mystērium, from Greek mustḗrion "a secret rite" (Lewis & Short).
