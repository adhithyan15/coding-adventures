## You'll want to know: مرعى
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C478-QATI478-02]; assesses=[] -->

**مرعى** — *marʿā* — "a pasture".

> ذهب القطيع إلى المرعى.
>
> *dhahaba al-qaṭīʿ ilā al-marʿā.*
>
> The herd went to the pasture.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**مرعى** (*marʿā*) is built on the native Arabic root *r-ʿ-y*, "to pasture, to tend" (Lane). The pattern *mafʿal* makes it the place of grazing.
