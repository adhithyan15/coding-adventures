## You'll want to know: chūjìng
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C417-CUSTOMS417-03]; assesses=[] -->

*chūjìng* — "to leave a country".

> *chūjìng de shíhou tāmen kàn le wǒ de hùzhào* — When I left the country, they looked at my passport.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*chūjìng* is built from 出 'to go out' and *jìng* 'a border', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *tsyhwit* and *kjaengX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription).
