## You'll want to know: досматривать
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C420-DOSMOTR-02]; assesses=[] -->

**досматривать** (*dosmátrivat*) — "to search, to inspect (luggage)".

> На границе досматривают все сумки.
>
> *Na granítse dosmátrivayut vse súmki.*
>
> At the border they search all the bags.

Imperfective; perfective *досмотреть*. Also to watch to the end: *досмотреть фильм*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*досматривать* is built from *до-* 'thoroughly, to the end' and *-сматривать*, from *смотреть* 'to look', which EtymDB relates to Proto-Slavic *motriti* 'to watch'.
