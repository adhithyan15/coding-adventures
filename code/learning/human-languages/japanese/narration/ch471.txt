Japanese, chapter 471: On Stage.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ぶたい (butai) — a stage.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for an enka ballad, then the Japanese for whistling.

You'll want to know: ぶたい.
ぶたい — butai — "a stage".
ぶたいで うたいました。 — butai de utaimashita — "I sang on stage."

The word, taken apart.
ぶたい (butai) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are bu, "dance" (Middle Chinese mjuX) and tai, "pedestal" (doj) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).

はくしゅする — hakushu suru — "to applaud; to clap".
えんそうの あと、みんな はくしゅしました。 — ensō no ato, minna hakushu shimashita — "After the performance, everyone applauded."
Polite はくしゅします, hakushushimasu; a する verb. Past (polite): かんきゃくが はくしゅしました。, kankyaku ga hakushu shimashita.

The word, taken apart.
はくしゅする (hakushu suru) is Sino-Japanese (kango) hakushu with the native verb suru, "to do": two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are haku, "clap" (Middle Chinese phaek) and shu, "hand" (syuwX) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
