## You'll want to know: tolérer
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C381-AUTORISER-03]; assesses=[] -->

**tolérer** — "to tolerate, to put up with".

> Le professeur ne tolère pas les retards.
>
> *The teacher does not tolerate lateness.*

*Je tolère*, with *è* before a silent ending.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*tolérer* is a learned borrowing from Latin *tolerāre* "to bear, to endure" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short).
