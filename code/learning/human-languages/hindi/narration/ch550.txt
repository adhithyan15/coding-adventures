Hindi, chapter 550: Making Up One's Mind.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
मर्ज़ी (marzī) — one's own wish; one's choice.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for preference, then the Hindi for to hesitate, to hold back.

You'll want to know: मर्ज़ी.
मर्ज़ी — marzī — "one's own wish; one's choice".
छुट्टी के दिन मैं अपनी मर्ज़ी से उठता हूँ। Chuṭṭī ke din maiṁ apnī marzī se uṭhtā hūṁ. On a day off I get up when I like.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
मर्ज़ी (marzī) is usually traced to Arabic marḍī, "pleasing, approved", through Persian.

What you've built.
One more word for saying what happens if.

Lesson 2 of 5.
मनपसंद (manpasand) — of one's own choosing, favourite.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for to hesitate, to hold back, then the Hindi for one's own wish.

You'll want to know: मनपसंद.
मनपसंद — manpasand — "of one's own choosing, favourite".
मैंने अपनी मनपसंद किताब ख़रीदी। Maiṁne apnī manpasand kitāb kharīdī. I bought the book I wanted.

The word, taken apart.
मनपसंद (manpasand) joins man, "mind", from Sanskrit manas (CDIAL 9822), and pasand, "liked", which is usually traced to Persian pasand: what the mind likes.

Lesson 5 of 5.
चयन (cayan) — a selection; a choice (formal).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a ban, a prohibition, then the Hindi for favourite.

You'll want to know: चयन.
चयन — cayan — "a selection; a choice (formal)".
टीम के लिए ग्यारह खिलाड़ियों का चयन हुआ। Ṭīm ke liye gyārah khilāṛiyoṁ kā cayan huā. Eleven players were picked for the team.
Masculine.
A formal word.

The word, taken apart.
चयन (cayan) is a tatsama, taken whole from Sanskrit cayana, "piling up, collecting" (Monier-Williams). Hindi uses it for picking out, a selection.
