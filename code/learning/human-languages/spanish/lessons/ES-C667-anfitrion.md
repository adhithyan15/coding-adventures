## You'll want to know: el anfitrión
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C667-APARTAMENTO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**el anfitrión** — "the host".

> El anfitrión nos espera en la puerta.
>
> *The host is waiting for us at the door.*

A woman is *la anfitriona*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*anfitrión* comes from the name *Anfitrión*, Greek *Amphitryōn* (EtymDB), a Theban hero of Greek legend; in an old comedy a great dinner is given at his house.
