## You'll want to know: لقب
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C342-TURATH342-04]; assesses=[] -->

**لقب** — *laqab* — "a title; a nickname".

> كان لقب جدي في القرية الحكيم.
>
> *kāna laqab jaddī fī al-qarya al-ḥakīm.*
>
> My grandfather’s nickname in the village was the Wise One.

Plural: ألقاب, *alqāb*.

In sport: لقب البطولة, the title.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**لقب** (*laqab*) is built on the native Arabic root *l-q-b* (Lane), where *laqab* is a name given to a person besides his own, for praise or in jest.
