## You'll want to know: きゃく
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C183-STAFF183-01]; assesses=[] -->

**きゃく** — *kyaku* — "customer; guest, visitor".

> **この みせは きゃくが おおいです。** — *kono mise wa kyaku ga ōi desu* — "This shop has many customers."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**きゃく** (*kyaku*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): one Chinese character, "guest, customer", read with its on'yomi, a reading borrowed from Chinese (Middle Chinese *khaek*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
