## You'll want to know: ていしゃ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C374-SENRO374-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ていしゃ** — *teisha* — "stopping (of a train or bus)".

> **この でんしゃは つぎの えきで ていしゃします。** — *kono densha wa tsugi no eki de teisha shimasu* — "This train stops at the next station."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ていしゃ** (*teisha*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *tei*, "halt" (Middle Chinese *deng*) and *sha*, "car" (*tsyhae*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
