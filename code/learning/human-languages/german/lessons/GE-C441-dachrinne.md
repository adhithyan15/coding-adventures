## You'll want to know: die Dachrinne
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C441-FASSADE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**die Dachrinne** — "the gutter (on a roof)".

> Die Dachrinne ist voll mit Laub.
>
> *The gutter is full of leaves.*

Plural: *die Dachrinnen*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Dachrinne* is built from *Dach* 'roof' and *Rinne* 'channel', both inherited: *Rinne* goes back to Old High German *rinna*, from the verb for running water (EtymDB).
