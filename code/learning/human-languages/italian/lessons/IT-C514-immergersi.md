## You'll want to know: immergersi
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C514-ACQUA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**immergersi** — "to dive under, to immerse oneself".

> Ci siamo immersi per vedere i pesci.
>
> *We dived under to watch the fish.*

The past participle is *immerso*. *Immergersi nella lettura* — to lose oneself in reading.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*immergersi* comes from Latin *immergere* "to dip into" (Lewis & Short), from *mergere* "to dip, to sink".
