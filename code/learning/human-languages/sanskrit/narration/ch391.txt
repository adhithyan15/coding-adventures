Sanskrit, chapter 391: Better and Worse.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
परमः (paramaḥ) — supreme; highest.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for worthwhile, then the Sanskrit for experiences.

You'll want to know: परमः.
परमः — paramaḥ — "supreme; highest".
यदि मनः शान्तम्, तर्हि परमं सुखं भवति। yadi manaḥ śāntam, tarhi paramaṃ sukhaṃ bhavati. If the mind is calm, there is the highest happiness.

The word, taken apart.
परमः (paramaḥ) is built on para, "far, beyond", with the superlative ending -ma; MW gives parama "chief, highest, primary, best, most excellent" (MW).

उपयुक्तः (upayuktaḥ) is built on upa, "towards", and the root √yuj, "to join"; MW gives upa-yukta "suitable, fit, appropriate, useful" (MW).

Lesson 5 of 5.
दूषयति (dūṣayati) — spoils; pollutes.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for in keeping with, then the Sanskrit for suitable.

You'll want to know: दूषयति.
दूषयति — dūṣayati — "spoils; pollutes".
यदि यन्त्राणि जलं दूषयन्ति, तर्हि मत्स्याः म्रियन्ते। yadi yantrāṇi jalaṃ dūṣayanti, tarhi matsyāḥ mriyante. If machines pollute the water, the fish die.
Past (it …): धूमः वायुं दूषितवान्, dhūmaḥ vāyuṃ dūṣitavān.

The word, taken apart.
दूषयति (dūṣayati) comes from the root √duṣ, "to become bad", as a verb; MW gives dūṣaya "to corrupt, to spoil, to contaminate" and dūṣita "spoiled, corrupted" (MW).
