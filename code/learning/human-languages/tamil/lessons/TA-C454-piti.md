## You'll want to know: பீதி
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C454-PAYAM454-02]; assesses=[] -->

**பீதி** — *pīti* — "panic; dread".

> நிலநடுக்கத்தின்போது மக்களிடம் பீதி பரவியது.
>
> *nilanaṭukkattiṉpōtu makkaḷiṭam pīti paraviyatu.*
>
> During the earthquake panic spread among the people.

பீதி அடை, to panic (literally to reach panic).

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**பீதி** (*pīti*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *bhīti*, "fear" (CDIAL 9512); Tamil writes the *bh* as ப.
