Marathi, chapter 500: Raising Children.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
संगोपन (saṅgopan) — upbringing.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to crawl, then the Marathi for to give to drink.

You'll want to know: संगोपन.
संगोपन — saṅgopan — "upbringing".
आजीने माझं संगोपन केलं. ājīne mājhaṁ saṅgopan kelaṁ. My grandmother brought me up.
Neuter.
संगोपन (saṅgopan) करणे, to bring up.

The word, taken apart.
संगोपन (saṅgopan) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit saṃgopana, "hiding or guarding well" (Monier-Williams); in Marathi it is the guarding of a child as it grows.

You'll want to know: सांभाळणे.
सांभाळणे — sāmbhāḷṇe — "to take care of; to look after".
आई कामावर गेली की आजी बाळाला सांभाळते. āī kāmāvar gelī kī ājī bāḷālā sāmbhāḷte. When Mother goes to work, Grandmother looks after the baby.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने बाळ सांभाळलं, tyāne bāḷ sāmbhāḷlaṁ.
Also to handle with care: सांभाळून, carefully.

The word, taken apart.
सांभाळणे (sāmbhāḷṇe) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi sā̃bhāḷṇẽ, "to take care of", under Sanskrit sambharati (CDIAL 12961).

Lesson 4 of 5.
लाड करणे (lāḍ karṇe) — to pamper.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to take care of, then the Marathi for to nurture.

You'll want to know: लाड करणे.
लाड करणे — lāḍ karṇe — "to pamper".
आजोबा नातवाचे खूप लाड करतात. ājobā nātvāce khūp lāḍ kartāt. Grandfather pampers his grandson a lot.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने लाड केले, tyāne lāḍ kele.
लाड is plural here; the one pampered takes the genitive: मुलाचे लाड करणे (lāḍ karṇe).

The word, taken apart.
लाड करणे (lāḍ karṇe) is a Marathi phrase built on लाड, "fondling", which CDIAL lists under the reconstructed lāḍya (CDIAL 11013), with करणे.

Lesson 5 of 5.
दत्तक घेणे (dattak gheṇe) — to adopt.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to nurture, then the Marathi for to pamper.

You'll want to know: दत्तक घेणे.
दत्तक घेणे — dattak gheṇe — "to adopt".
त्यांनी एक मुलगी दत्तक घेतली. tyānnī ek mulgī dattak ghetlī. They adopted a girl.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने बाळ दत्तक घेतलं, tyāne bāḷ dattak ghetlaṁ.
दत्तक, adopted: दत्तक मुलगा, an adopted son.

The word, taken apart.
दत्तक घेणे (dattak gheṇe) is a Marathi phrase built on दत्तक, a tatsama of Sanskrit dattaka, a son given in adoption (Monier-Williams), with घेणे, "to take".
