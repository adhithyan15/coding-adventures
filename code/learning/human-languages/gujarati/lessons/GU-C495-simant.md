## You'll want to know: સીમંત
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C495-SAGARBHA495-03]; assesses=[] -->

**સીમંત** — *sīmant* — "a baby-shower ceremony".

> સાતમા મહિને ભાભીનું સીમંત થયું.
>
> *sātmā mahine bhābhīnũ sīmant thayũ.*
>
> In her seventh month my sister-in-law had her baby-shower ceremony.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**સીમંત** (*sīmant*) is a tatsama: Monier-Williams gives Sanskrit *sīmanta* as "the parting of the hair" and also as the hair-parting rite held for a woman expecting a child; Gujarati uses the word for that ceremony.
