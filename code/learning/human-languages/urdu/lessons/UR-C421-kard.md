## You'll want to know: کارڈ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C421-KAGHAZ421-02]; assesses=[] -->

**کارڈ** — *kārḍ* — "a card".

> میں دکان پر کارڈ سے پیسے دیتی ہوں۔
>
> *maiṅ dukān par kārḍ se paise detī hūṅ.*
>
> At the shop I pay by card.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**کارڈ** (*kārḍ*) is borrowed from English *card*.
