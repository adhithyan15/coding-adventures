Russian, chapter 491: In-laws.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
свекровь (svekróv) — a husband's mother, a mother-in-law.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to swallow, then the Russian for syrup.

You'll want to know: свекровь.
свекровь (svekróv) — "a husband's mother, a mother-in-law".
Моя свекровь прекрасно готовит. Moyá svekróv prekrásno gotóvit. My mother-in-law is a wonderful cook.
Feminine. The husband's mother.

The word, taken apart.
свекровь (svekróv) is inherited from Proto-Slavic svekry, which EtymDB traces to PIE sweḱrúh₂ 'mother-in-law'.

Lesson 3 of 5.
свёкор (svyókor) — a husband's father, a father-in-law.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a husband's mother, then the Russian for a wife's mother.

You'll want to know: свёкор.
свёкор (svyókor) — "a husband's father, a father-in-law".
Свёкор помог нам с ремонтом. Svyókor pomóg nam s remóntom. My father-in-law helped us with the renovation.
Masculine; genitive свёкра (the vowel drops). The husband's father.

The word, taken apart.
свёкор (svyókor) is inherited from Proto-Slavic svekrъ through Old East Slavic svekrъ, which EtymDB traces to PIE swéḱuros 'father-in-law'; свекровь (svekróv) goes back to the related PIE sweḱrúh₂ (EtymDB).
