## You'll want to know: разогревать
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C332-ZAPEKAT-03]; assesses=[] -->

**разогревать** (*razogrevát*) — "to heat up, to warm up".

> Я разогрел суп на плите.
>
> *Ya razogrél sup na plité.*
>
> I heated up the soup on the stove.

Imperfective; perfective *разогреть* (*razogrét*), *разогрею*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*разогревать* is built on *раз-*, *о-* and *греть* "to warm", inherited from Proto-Slavic *grěti* "to heat, to warm", from PIE *gʷʰer-* (EtymDB).
