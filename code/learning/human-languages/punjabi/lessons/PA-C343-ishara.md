## You'll want to know: ਇਸ਼ਾਰਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C343-MIXUP343-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ਇਸ਼ਾਰਾ** — *ishārā* — "a hint; a sign made to someone".

> ਕੀ ਤੁਸੀਂ ਮੇਰਾ ਇਸ਼ਾਰਾ ਸਮਝਦੇ ਹੋ?
>
> *kī tusī̃ merā ishārā samajhde ho?*
>
> Do you get my hint?

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਇਸ਼ਾਰਾ** (*ishārā*) is a loan, usually traced through Persian to Arabic *ishāra*, "sign".
