## You'll want to know: ವ್ಯಕ್ತಿತ್ವ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C500-SVABHAVA500-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ವ್ಯಕ್ತಿತ್ವ** — *vyaktitva* — "personality".

> ಅವರ ವ್ಯಕ್ತಿತ್ವ ಎಲ್ಲರಿಗೂ ಇಷ್ಟ.
>
> *avara vyaktitva ellarigū iṣṭa.*
>
> Everyone likes his personality.

ವ್ಯಕ್ತಿ, a person + -ತ್ವ, the Sanskrit ending for "-ness".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ವ್ಯಕ್ತಿತ್ವ** (*vyaktitva*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *vyaktitva*, "distinctness, individuality, personality" (Monier-Williams), built on *vyakti*, "an individual".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
