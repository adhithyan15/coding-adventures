## You'll want to know: credível
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C342-AFIRMAR-04]; assesses=[] -->

**credível** — "credible, believable".

> A história dele não é muito credível.
>
> *His story is not very credible.*

Of a person or a story. *Pouco credível* — hard to believe.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*credível* is a learned formation on Latin *crēdibilis* "worthy of belief, credible" (Lewis & Short), from *crēdere* "to believe, to trust".
