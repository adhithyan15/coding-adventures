## You'll want to know: ゆうやけ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C416-YOAKE416-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ゆうやけ** — *yūyake* — "a sunset glow; a red evening sky".

> **うみの ゆうやけは きれいでした。** — *umi no yūyake wa kirei deshita* — "The sunset over the sea was beautiful."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ゆうやけ** (*yūyake*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, built from *yū*, "evening" and *yake*, "burning" (from *yaku*); its kanji were attached later as kun'yomi (JMdict).
