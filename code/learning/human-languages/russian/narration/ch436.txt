Russian, chapter 436: Getting a refund.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
возврат (vozvrát) — a refund; a return.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a tip, then the Russian for to be rude.

You'll want to know: возврат.
возврат (vozvrát) — "a refund; a return".
Без чека возврат невозможен. Bez chéka vozvrát nevozmózhen. Without a receipt a refund is not possible.
Masculine. Возврат денег — a refund.

The word, taken apart.
возврат (vozvrát) is built from воз- 'back' and -врат 'a turning', the Church Slavonic shape of native -ворот, on the root of вертеть 'to turn' (EtymDB lists возврат (vozvrát)).

Lesson 2 of 5.
требовать (trébovat) — to demand.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to be rude, then the Russian for a refund.

You'll want to know: требовать.
требовать (trébovat) — "to demand".
Клиент требует вернуть деньги. Kliyént trébuyet vernút déngi. The customer demands his money back.
Imperfective; perfective потребовать. Present требую. Takes the genitive or an infinitive.

The word, taken apart.
требовать (trébovat) is usually linked with the old word треба 'a need; an offering'. EtymDB lists the verb without a source, so the link is hedged; it takes треба from Church Slavonic trěba.

Lesson 3 of 5.
возмущаться (vozmushchátsya) — to be indignant, to protest.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a refund, then the Russian for to demand.

You'll want to know: возмущаться.
возмущаться (vozmushchátsya) — "to be indignant, to protest".
Пассажиры возмущались, когда рейс отменили. Passazhíry vozmushchális, kogdá reys otmeníli. The passengers were outraged when the flight was cancelled.
Imperfective; the perfective is возмутиться. Takes the instrumental: возмущаться (vozmushchátsya) ценами.

The word, taken apart.
возмущаться (vozmushchátsya) is built from воз- 'up', мутить 'to stir up, to trouble' and -ся. Мутить is inherited from Proto-Slavic mǫtiti 'to stir, to trouble' (EtymDB).

Lesson 4 of 5.
устранять (ustranyát) — to fix, to eliminate (a fault).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to demand, then the Russian for to be indignant.

You'll want to know: устранять.
устранять (ustranyát) — "to fix, to eliminate (a fault)".
Сантехник быстро устраняет неполадки. Santékhnik býstro ustranyáyet nepoládki. The plumber fixes faults quickly.
Imperfective; perfective устранить. Formal: устранить проблему.

The word, taken apart.
устранять (ustranyát) is built from у- and -странять, on страна in its old sense 'a side': to move something to one side. EtymDB takes страна from Church Slavonic, from Proto-Slavic storna 'side, land'.

Lesson 5 of 5.
квитанция (kvitántsiya) — a receipt (for a payment or a service).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to be indignant, then the Russian for to fix.

You'll want to know: квитанция.
квитанция (kvitántsiya) — "a receipt (for a payment or a service)".
Кассир дал мне квитанцию. Kassír dal mne kvitántsiyu. The cashier gave me a receipt.
Feminine. A квитанция (kvitántsiya) is a paper slip for a payment or a service; a till receipt is a чек.

The word, taken apart.
квитанция (kvitántsiya) was borrowed from Dutch kwitantie (EtymDB), from Middle Dutch quitantie.
