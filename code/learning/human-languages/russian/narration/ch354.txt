Russian, chapter 354: Better or worse.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
улучшать (uluchshát) — to improve, to make better.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to suffer, then the Russian for to make worse.

You'll want to know: улучшать.
улучшать (uluchshát) — "to improve, to make better".
Чтение улучшает память. Chténiye uluchsháyet pámyat. Reading improves your memory.
Imperfective; perfective улучшить. Улучшаться — to get better.

The word, taken apart.
улучшать (uluchshát) is built on лучший "better", which is usually traced to Old Russian лучии "better, more fitting".

Lesson 2 of 5.
увеличиваться (uvelíchivatsya) — to increase, to grow.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to make worse, then the Russian for to improve.

You'll want to know: увеличиваться.
увеличиваться (uvelíchivatsya) — "to increase, to grow".
Цены увеличиваются каждый год. Tsény uvelíchivayutsya kázhdyy god. Prices increase every year.
Imperfective; the perfective is увеличиться. The transitive verb is увеличивать, to increase something.

The word, taken apart.
увеличиваться (uvelíchivatsya) is built on великий "great", inherited from Proto-Slavic velikŭ, from velĭjĭ "great" (EtymDB): to become greater.

Lesson 3 of 5.
уменьшаться (umenshátsya) — to decrease, to get smaller.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to improve, then the Russian for to increase.

You'll want to know: уменьшаться.
уменьшаться (umenshátsya) — "to decrease, to get smaller".
Боль постепенно уменьшается. Bol postepénno umensháyetsya. The pain is gradually decreasing.
Imperfective; perfective уменьшиться. Built on меньше, less.

The word, taken apart.
уменьшаться (umenshátsya) is built from у- and меньше "less", an old comparative Russian inherited from Proto-Slavic; it is not built on малый, which has a different root.

повышать (povyshát) — "to raise, to increase".
Кофе повышает давление. Kófe povysháyet davléniye. Coffee raises blood pressure.
Imperfective; the perfective is повысить (повышу, повысишь). Повышать голос — to raise one’s voice.

повышать (povyshát) is built from по- and выше "higher", the comparative of высокий "high", inherited from Proto-Slavic vysokŭ "high, tall" (EtymDB).
