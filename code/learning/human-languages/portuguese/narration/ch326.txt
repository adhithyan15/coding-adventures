Portuguese, chapter 326: Lendas e mitos.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
a lenda — the legend.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for the villain, then the Portuguese for the ending.

You'll want to know: a lenda.
a lenda — "the legend".
Segundo a lenda, ninguém pode entrar na gruta à noite. According to the legend, nobody may go into the cave at night.
Conta a lenda que… — legend has it that…

The word, taken apart.
lenda comes down from Old Portuguese leenda (EtymDB), from Medieval Latin legenda "things to be read", from legere "to read".

o mito — "the myth".
É um mito: a água fria não faz mal. It is a myth: cold water does no harm.
Also a false belief, as here.

The word, taken apart.

Lesson 4 of 5.

Warm-up.

You'll want to know: a fábula.
a fábula — "the fable".
Na fábula, o leão e o rato ficam amigos. In the fable, the lion and the mouse become friends.
A short story with animals and a lesson at the end.

The word, taken apart.
fábula was borrowed from Latin fābula "a story, a tale" (EtymDB), from fārī "to speak".
