## You'll want to know: لشکر
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C310-DHAL310-05]; assesses=[] -->

**لشکر** — *lashkar* — "a host; an army on the march".

> لشکر شام کو شہر پہنچا۔
>
> *lashkar shām ko shahr pahuṅchā.*
>
> The army reached the city in the evening.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**لشکر** (*lashkar*) is usually traced to Persian *lashkar*, "an army, a camp". It is the old word of the war stories; **فوج** is the army of today.
