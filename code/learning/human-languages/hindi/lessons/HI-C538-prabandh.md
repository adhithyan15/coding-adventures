## You'll want to know: प्रबंध
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C538-PRABANDH538-03]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रबंध** — *prabandh* — "management; arrangements".

> दावत का प्रबंध मेरी चाची ने किया।
>
> *Dāvat kā prabandh merī cācī ne kiyā.*
>
> My aunt saw to the arrangements for the feast.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रबंध** (*prabandh*) is a tatsama, taken whole from Sanskrit *prabandha*, "a connection, a tie" (Monier-Williams). Hindi uses it for managing and arranging.
