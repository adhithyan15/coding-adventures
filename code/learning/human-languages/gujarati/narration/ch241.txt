Gujarati, chapter 241: Flowers that bloomed and died.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ખીલવું (khīlvũ) — to bloom, to blossom.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to irrigate, then the Gujarati for rose.

You'll want to know: ખીલવું.
ખીલવું — khīlvũ — "to bloom, to blossom".
ગઈકાલે ગુલાબ ખીલ્યું. — gaīkāle gulāb khīlyũ — "Yesterday the rose bloomed."

The word, taken apart.
ખીલવું (khīlvũ) comes from khill-, "be open, expand", one of the reconstructed forms that CDIAL gathers under khiḍ; it glosses the Gujarati verb "to bloom, expand" (CDIAL 3882).

Lesson 3 of 5.
મૂળ (mūḷ) — root.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to bloom, then the Gujarati for to wilt.

You'll want to know: મૂળ.
મૂળ — mūḷ — "root".
છોડનું મૂળ સૂકું હતું. — choḍnũ mūḷ sūkũ hatũ — "The plant's root was dry."

The word, taken apart.
મૂળ (mūḷ) is a tadbhava. It comes from Sanskrit mūla, "root", and CDIAL glosses the Gujarati word "root of tree, origin" (CDIAL 10250).
