## Fixed — the adjective and question-word warm-ups say what the word does

The eleven chapter lessons that meet *this, that, here, there, who, where* and
the five common adjectives (*big, small, new, old, good*) all opened with one
copied warm-up: "You can already name things. This is how you POINT at them."
That is true of the four pointing words and nothing else. The question words
now say "This is how you ASK about them" and the adjectives "This is how you
DESCRIBE them" — the words a listener hears match the word they are about to
learn.
