German, chapter 498: Freunde und Nachbarn.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
kennenlernen — to get to know, to meet.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to tease, then the German for pocket money.

You'll want to know: kennenlernen.
kennenlernen — "to get to know, to meet".
Wir haben uns im Sprachkurs kennengelernt. We met on a language course.
Separable: ich lerne ihn kennen, hat kennengelernt. Also written as two words: kennen lernen.

The word, taken apart.
kennenlernen is built on kennen "to know", inherited from Old High German kennan, from Proto-Germanic kannijaną, and lernen "to learn" (EtymDB).

What you've built.

Lesson 3 of 5.
die Bekanntschaft — acquaintance.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to get to know, then the German for on friendly terms.

You'll want to know: die Bekanntschaft.
die Bekanntschaft — "acquaintance".
Im Urlaub habe ich eine nette Bekanntschaft gemacht. I made a nice acquaintance on holiday.
Plural: die Bekanntschaften. Jemandes Bekanntschaft machen — to get to know someone.

The word, taken apart.
Bekanntschaft is built on bekannt "known" with -schaft; bekannt belongs with kennen "to know", from Proto-Germanic kannijaną (EtymDB).

Lesson 4 of 5.
die Nachbarschaft — neighbourhood.

Warm-up.

You'll want to know: die Nachbarschaft.
die Nachbarschaft — "neighbourhood".
In unserer Nachbarschaft gibt es einen schönen Park. There's a nice park in our neighbourhood.
Also the neighbours themselves: Die ganze Nachbarschaft hat geholfen.

The word, taken apart.
Nachbarschaft is built on Nachbar "neighbour", inherited from Middle High German nāchbūre "one who lives near" (EtymDB), with -schaft.
