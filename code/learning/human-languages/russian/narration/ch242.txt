Lesson 1 of 5.
библиотека (bibliotéka) — a library.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for opposite, then the Russian for between.

You'll want to know: библиотека.
библиотека (bibliotéka) — "a library".

The word, taken apart.
библиотека (bibliotéka) — from Peter the Great's time; it came through Polish biblioteka from Latin bibliothēca, Greek bibliothḗkē, "a store of books" (Vasmer). Older Russian borrowed the Greek word as вивлиофика.

Before the new one: say the Russian for between, then the Russian for a library.

You'll want to know: университет.
университет (universitét) — "a university".

The word, taken apart.
университет (universitét) — the first was in Moscow in 1755; it came through German Universität from Latin ūniversitās (litterārum), "the whole body (of learning)" (Vasmer).

Lesson 3 of 5.
кафе (kafé) — a café.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a library, then the Russian for a university.

You'll want to know: кафе.
кафе (kafé) — "a café".

The word, taken apart.
кафе (kafé) comes from French café or German Kaffee, "coffee" (Vasmer), a word that goes back through Turkish kahve to Arabic qahwa. A café is a coffee house.

The word, taken apart.
зоопарк (zoopárk) is зоо-, from Greek zôion, "an animal", with парк, "a park", from French parc, "an enclosure".
