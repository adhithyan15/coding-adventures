French, chapter 269: Au moment où.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
lorsque — when (careful style).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for lastly, then the French for to finish.

You'll want to know: lorsque.
lorsque — "when (careful style)".
Lorsque je suis arrivé, la fête avait déjà commencé. When I arrived, the party had already started.
It means quand in a time clause, a little more written. Before a vowel it becomes lorsqu': lorsqu'il pleut.

The word, taken apart.
lorsque joins lors "then" and que "that". Lors is traced to Latin illā hōrā "at that hour" (EtymDB), from hōra "hour".

Lesson 2 of 5.
dès que — as soon as.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for to finish, then the French for when.

You'll want to know: dès que.
dès que — "as soon as".
Dès que le film a fini, nous sommes partis. As soon as the film ended, we left.
For an event still to come, the future follows: dès qu'il arrivera, on mangera.

The word, taken apart.
dès is usually traced to Late Latin dē ex "from out of", the prepositions dē "from" and ex "out of" (Lewis & Short) run together; EtymDB lists no parent.

Lesson 3 of 5.
au moment où — at the moment when, right as.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for when, then the French for as soon as.

You'll want to know: au moment où.
au moment où — "at the moment when, right as".
Au moment où je sortais, le téléphone a sonné. Right as I was going out, the phone rang.
After moment, French says où, not quand: au moment où il est entré.

The word, taken apart.
moment comes from Latin mōmentum (EtymDB), taken over as a learned word; Lewis & Short gloss it "movement, motion", built on movēre "to move".

Lesson 4 of 5.
aussitôt — at once, immediately.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for as soon as, then the French for at the moment when.

You'll want to know: aussitôt.
aussitôt — "at once, immediately".
Il a vu la lettre et il l'a aussitôt ouverte. He saw the letter and opened it at once.
Aussitôt que means the same as dès que: as soon as.

The word, taken apart.
aussitôt joins aussi "so" and tôt "early"; tôt is usually traced to Latin tostum "scorched, burnt", from torrēre "to roast" (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 5 of 5.
tandis que — while, whereas.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for at the moment when, then the French for at once.

You'll want to know: tandis que.
tandis que — "while, whereas".
Il lisait tandis que sa sœur jouait du piano. He was reading while his sister was playing the piano.
Two uses: time, 'while', and contrast, 'whereas': il aime la mer, tandis que je préfère la montagne.

The word, taken apart.
tandis is inherited from Latin tam diū "for so long" (EtymDB), from tam "so" and diū "for a long time" (Lewis & Short).
