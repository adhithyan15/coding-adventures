## You'll want to know: تساءل
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C361-QADIYYA361-05]; assesses=[] -->

**تساءل** — *tasāʾala* — "to wonder".

> تساءلت أمي عن سبب التأخير.
>
> *tasāʾalat ummī ʿan sabab at-taʾkhīr.*
>
> My mother wondered about the reason for the delay.

Present: يتساءل, *yatasāʾal*.

Form VI of the root of سأل, to ask: to ask oneself.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**تساءل** (*tasāʾala*) is built on the native Arabic root *s-ʾ-l*, "to ask" (Lane). It is Form VI of *saʾala*, "to ask": to ask oneself, to wonder.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
