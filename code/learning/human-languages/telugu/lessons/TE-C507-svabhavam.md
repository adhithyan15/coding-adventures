# స్వభావం (svabhāvaṁ) — temperament; nature

## You'll want to know: స్వభావం
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C507-SVABHAVAM507-01]; assesses=[] -->

**స్వభావం** — *svabhāvaṁ* — "temperament; nature".

> మా తాతయ్యది చాలా మంచి స్వభావం.
>
> *mā tātayyadi cālā mañci svabhāvaṁ.*
>
> My grandfather has a very good nature.

Whose nature it is: -ది స్వభావం, as in మా తాతయ్యది.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**స్వభావం** (*svabhāvaṁ*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *svabhāva*, "one's own state, inborn disposition" (Monier-Williams), from *sva*, "own", and *bhāva*, "being".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
