Bengali, chapter 493: At the Office.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ডেস্ক (ḍesk) — a desk.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a bell-metal smith, then the Bengali for to string together.

You'll want to know: ডেস্ক.
ডেস্ক — ḍesk — "a desk".
আমার ডেস্কে অনেক ফাইল আছে। Āmār ḍeske ônek phāil āchhe. There are lots of files on my desk.

The word, taken apart.
ডেস্ক (ḍesk) is borrowed from English desk.

Lesson 2 of 5.
কেরানি (kerāni) — a clerk.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for to string together, then the Bengali for a desk.

You'll want to know: কেরানি.
কেরানি — kerāni — "a clerk".
কেরানি (kerāni) ফাইলটা টেবিলে রাখল। Kerāni phāilṭā ṭebile rākhlô. The clerk put the file on the table.

The word, taken apart.
কেরানি (kerāni) is probably from Sanskrit karaṇa, which Monier-Williams glosses as "a writer, a scribe"; how the ending arose is not settled.

Lesson 3 of 5.
সিলমোহর (silmohôr) — an official seal; a rubber stamp.

Before the new one: say the Bengali for a desk, then the Bengali for a clerk.

You'll want to know: সিলমোহর.
সিলমোহর — silmohôr — "an official seal; a rubber stamp".
কেরানি (kerāni) ফর্মে সিলমোহর লাগাল। Kerāni phôrme silmohôr lāgālô. The clerk stamped the form with the official seal.

The word, taken apart.
সিলমোহর (silmohôr) probably joins English seal to মোহর, "a seal", usually traced to Persian muhr: the same thing said twice, in two languages.

Lesson 5 of 5.
টাইপ করা (ṭāip kôrā) — to type.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for an official seal, then the Bengali for an office messenger.

You'll want to know: টাইপ করা.
টাইপ করা — ṭāip kôrā — "to type".
কেরানি (kerāni) চিঠিটা টাইপ করল। Kerāni chiṭhiṭā ṭāip kôrlô. The clerk typed the letter.
Past (he/she …): তিনি ফর্মটা টাইপ করলেন, tini phôrmṭā ṭāip kôrlen.

The word, taken apart.
টাইপ করা (ṭāip kôrā) joins type, borrowed from English, to করা, "to do".
