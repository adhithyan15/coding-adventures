# إ (alif with hamza below) — the alif that opens إلى

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[AR-SCRIPT-C46-SAD-01, AR-SCRIPT-C46-DAL-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: write ص and د once each, and name them.

[PAUSE 2s] Now say **إلى اللقاء** (*ilā l-liqāʾ*), "see you".
You have read it for a long time. This lesson takes one letter out of it and puts
it in your hand.

## Script you'll notice: إ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-SCRIPT-C47-ALIF-HAMZA-BELOW-01]; assesses=[] -->

**إ** — *alif* with a **hamza below**, read *i* at the start of a word.

It opens **إلى** (*ilā*), "until", in **إلى اللقاء** (*ilā l-liqāʾ*), "see you". You
already write **ا** and the hamza **ء**. Put the small hamza **under** the alif and
the word opens on *i*; put it over the alif (**أ**) and it opens on *a* or *u*.

## Writing: إ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[AR-SCRIPT-C47-ALIF-HAMZA-BELOW-01] -->

Put your pen on إ and follow its line. Copy the shape you can see — slowly,
and larger than it is printed.

> This book does not yet tell you **where to start this character or which way
> to travel**. It is not written down here until it can be written down with a
> source. Copying what is in front of you needs no such source, and it is how the
> shape gets into your hand in the meantime.
