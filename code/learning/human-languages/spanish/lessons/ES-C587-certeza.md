## You'll want to know: la certeza
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C587-EVIDENTE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**la certeza** — "the certainty".

> No tengo la certeza.
>
> *I am not certain.*

*Con certeza* — for sure.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*certeza* is built on *cierto* with the ending *-eza*; *cierto* is inherited from Latin *certus* 'settled, sure' (Lewis & Short).
