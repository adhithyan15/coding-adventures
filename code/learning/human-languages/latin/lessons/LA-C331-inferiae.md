## You'll want to know: īnferiae, īnferiārum
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C331-LUCTUS-05]; assesses=[] -->

**īnferiae, īnferiārum** — "offerings to the dead".

> Fīliī īnferiās ad sepulcrum patris tulērunt.
>
> *The sons brought offerings to their father's tomb.*

Feminine, plural only. Wine, milk and flowers were poured or laid at the grave.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**īnferiae** is built on *īnferī*, "those below, the dead", from *īnferus*, "lower" (Lewis & Short): gifts for those below.
