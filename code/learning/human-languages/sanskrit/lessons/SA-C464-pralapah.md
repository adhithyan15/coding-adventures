## You'll want to know: प्रलापः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C464-VACALA464-05]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रलापः** — *pralāpaḥ* — "prattle".

> तस्य प्रलापं कः शृणोति?
>
> *tasya pralāpaṃ kaḥ śṛṇoti?*
>
> Who listens to his prattle?

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रलापः** (*pralāpaḥ*) is built from *pra* and the root √lap, "to talk"; MW gives "talk, prattling, chattering" and also "lamentation" (MW).
