## You'll want to know: guàshī
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C285-GUASHI-01]; assesses=[] -->

*guàshī* — "to report the loss of" a card or a document.

> *wǒ yào guàshī wǒ de xìnyòngkǎ* — I need to report my credit card lost.

> *wǒ děi qù guàshī* — I have to go and report it lost.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*guàshī* is built from guà 'to hang' and shī 'to lose', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *kweaH* and *syit* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to hang' and the second 'to let slip'. 'To report a loss' for the pair is its own sense, which neither gloss gives.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ZH-LEX-C195-BANK195-03, ZH-LEX-C282-DEI-01] -->

*guàshī* is what you do at a bank or a police desk once a card or a passport is gone, so nobody else can use it.

The thing follows it: *guàshī wǒ de xìnyòngkǎ*.
