Telugu, chapter 149: To walk, To close, To open, To drink, To cry.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
నడుచు (naḍucu) — to walk.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to sing, then the Telugu for to sell.

You'll want to know: నడుచు.
నడుచు — naḍucu — "to walk".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: naḍucu]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: naḍucu, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say naḍucu]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Telugu for to sing, then the Telugu for to sell, then say naḍucu again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does నడుచు (naḍucu) mean? ("To walk".) Say it once more.

Lesson 6 of 7.
Describing words and verbs — a first pass.

Warm-up.
[pause 3 seconds]
Say the words for to drink and to cry.

Guided Practice.
[pause 2 seconds after each]
[your turn — say: safe, a lie; untrue, grey; ash, golden, spoiled]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: sweet, bitter-tasting, spicy, courage; brave, lazy]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: hard-working, clever, terrible, black, white]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: red, to fly, to run, to sleep, to get up]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: to cook, to cut, to break, to send, to look for]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
Safe? (సురక్షితం, surakṣitaṁ.) A lie; untrue? (అబద్ధం, abaddhaṁ.) Grey; ash? (బూడిద, būḍida.) Golden? (బంగారు, baṅgāru.) Spoiled? (పాడైన, pāḍaina.) Sweet? (తీయని, tīyani.) Bitter-tasting? (చేదైన, cēdaina.) Spicy? (కారమైన, kāramaina.) Courage; brave? (ధైర్యం, dhairyaṁ.) Lazy? (సోమరి, sōmari.) Hard-working? (కష్టపడే, kaṣṭapaḍē.) Clever? (తెలివైన, telivaina.) Terrible? (భయంకరమైన, bhayaṅkaramaina.) Black? (నల్లని, nallani.) White? (తెల్లని, tellani.) Red? (ఎర్రటి, erraṭi.) To fly? (ఎగురు, egiru.) To run? (పరిగెత్తు, parigettu.) To sleep? (నిద్రపో, nidrapō.) To get up? (లేచు, lēcu.) To cook? (వండు, vaṇḍu.) To cut? (కోయు, kōyu.) To break? (పగలగొట్టు, pagalagoṭṭu.) To send? (పంపు, pampu.) To look for? (వెతుకు, vetuku.)
