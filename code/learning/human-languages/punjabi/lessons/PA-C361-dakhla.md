## You'll want to know: ਦਾਖ਼ਲਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C361-COLLEGE361-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ਦਾਖ਼ਲਾ** — *dākhlā* — "admission (to a school or college)".

> ਮੈਨੂੰ ਕਾਲਜ ਵਿੱਚ ਦਾਖ਼ਲਾ ਮਿਲਿਆ।
>
> *mainū̃ kālaj vich dākhlā miliā.*
>
> I got admission to the college.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਦਾਖ਼ਲਾ** (*dākhlā*) is a loan, usually traced through Persian to Arabic *dākhila*, "entry".
