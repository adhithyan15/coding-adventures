## You'll want to know: el herido
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C525-HERIDA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**el herido** — "the injured person; injured".

> Hay tres heridos graves.
>
> *There are three people seriously injured.*

As an adjective: *Está herido* — he is injured.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*herido* is built on the participle of *herir* 'to wound', which is inherited from Latin *ferīre* 'to strike' (EtymDB).
