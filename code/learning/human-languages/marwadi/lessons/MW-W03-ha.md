# ह — *ha*, one new sign

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MW-DIALOGUE-GREETING-01, MW-SCRIPT-AA-INDEPENDENT-01, MW-SCRIPT-BHA-01, MW-SCRIPT-AABHAAR-01, MW-LEX-AABHAAR-01, MW-COURTESY-AABHAAR-FORMAL-01] -->

[PAUSE 8s] Give the Chapter 1 greeting reply from memory. Then, with **आभार**
covered, say it as formal thanks and write it once. Uncover only long enough to
check each sign.

## Script — one sign and one sound
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-SCRIPT-HA-01]; assesses=[] -->

> **ह** — *ha*

Let the air pass freely as you say *ha*. Do not add the rest of the coming word
yet; today the eye, ear, voice, and hand meet only **ह**.

## Writing — observe, trace, copy
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MW-SCRIPT-HA-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: observe-trace -->

1. look at **ह** for three seconds
2. finger-trace its lower curve and upright line
3. copy **ह** twice
4. say *ha* after each copy

## Wrap-up recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MW-SCRIPT-HA-01] -->
<!-- hl-activity: {"id":"MW-W03-ha-read","kind":"text","assesses":["MW-SCRIPT-HA-01"],"prompt":"Read the one new sign ह and name its sound.","answer":"ha","accepted":["ह"],"feedback":{"correct":"Right: ह is ha.","incorrect":"Let air pass freely: ह is ha."},"response_seconds":8} -->

Cover the model for five seconds. Write **ह**, uncover, and compare.
