## You'll want to know: साज
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C233-GEAR233-03]; assesses=[] -->

**साज** — *sāj* — "trappings (of a camel or a horse); harness".

> ऊंट माथै लाल साज है।
>
> *ū̃ṭ māthai lāl sāj hai.*
>
> The camel wears red trappings.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**साज** (*sāj*) is inherited from Sanskrit *sajja*, "equipped, put in place"; CDIAL lists Old Marwari *sāja*, "accoutrements" (CDIAL 13095).
