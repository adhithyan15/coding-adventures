Lesson 3 of 5.
મિલકત (milkat) — property.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for authority, then the Gujarati for a chance.
[pause 1 second]
From which number up does -mũ make every ordinal? (From sāt, seven: sātmũ.)

You'll want to know: મિલકત.
મિલકત (milkat) — "property".

The word, taken apart.
મિલકત (milkat) is a loan, usually traced through Persian milkiyat, "ownership", to Arabic milk, "possession". Its Arabic root is m-l-k (Lane).
