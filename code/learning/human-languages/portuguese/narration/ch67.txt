Portuguese, chapter 67: Cheap, Tall, Low, Full, Empty.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
barato — cheap.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for difficult, then the Portuguese for expensive; dear.

You'll want to know: barato.
barato — "cheap". The opposite of caro.

The word, taken apart.
barato comes from an old verb for trading and selling low, whose own origin is uncertain. Old French barater, behind English barter, belongs to the same family.
