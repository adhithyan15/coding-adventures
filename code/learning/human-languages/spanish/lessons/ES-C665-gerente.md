## You'll want to know: el gerente
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C665-QUEJA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**el gerente** — "the manager".

> Quiero hablar con el gerente.
>
> *I want to speak to the manager.*

A woman is *la gerente*, sometimes *la gerenta*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*gerente* is a learned borrowing from Latin *gerēns, gerentis* "managing" (EtymDB), from *gerere* "to carry on, to manage".
