Gujarati, chapter 530: Deadlines.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
અવધિ (avdhi) — a time limit.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for half-finished, then the Gujarati for final.

You'll want to know: અવધિ.
અવધિ — avdhi — "a time limit".
જો તમે આ અવધિમાં અરજી નહીં કરો તો તમે આ તક ગુમાવશો. jo tame ā avdhimā̃ arjī nahī̃ karo to tame ā tak gumāvśo. If you don't apply within this time limit, you will lose this chance.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
અવધિ (avdhi) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit avadhi, "a term, a limit" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 2 of 5.
તાકીદ (tākīd) — urgency.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for final, then the Gujarati for a time limit.

You'll want to know: તાકીદ.
તાકીદ — tākīd — "urgency".
જો કામમાં તાકીદ હશે તો હું રવિવારે પણ આવીશ. jo kāmmā̃ tākīd haśe to hũ ravivāre paṇ āvīś. If the work is urgent, I'll come in on Sunday too.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
તાકીદ (tākīd) is usually traced to Arabic taʾkīd, "emphasis, insistence", through Persian; in Gujarati it is the pressing need to get something done.

Lesson 4 of 5.
ઠેલવું (ṭhelvũ) — to push back (a date).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for urgency, then the Gujarati for extra time.

You'll want to know: ઠેલવું.
ઠેલવું — ṭhelvũ — "to push back (a date)".
જો વરસાદ પડશે તો અમે પ્રવાસ આવતા અઠવાડિયા પર ઠેલીશું. jo varsād paḍśe to ame pravās āvtā aṭhvāḍiyā par ṭhelīśũ. If it rains, we will push the trip back to next week.
Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે પ્રવાસ ઠેલ્યો, teṇe pravās ṭhelyo.

The word, taken apart.
ઠેલવું (ṭhelvũ) comes from ṭhēḍḍ, a form CDIAL reconstructs, and CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb as "to push" (CDIAL 5512); a plan or a date is pushed back.
