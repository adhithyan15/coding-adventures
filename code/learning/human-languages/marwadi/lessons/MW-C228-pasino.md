## You'll want to know: पसीनो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C228-HEAT228-03]; assesses=[] -->

**पसीनो** — *pasīno* — "sweat".

> जेठ मांय पसीनो आवै है।
>
> *jeṭh mā̃y pasīno āvai hai.*
>
> You sweat in early summer.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पसीनो** (*pasīno*) is inherited from Sanskrit *prasvinna*, "sweated"; CDIAL lists Hindi *pasīnā* and a Gujarati word for sweat. CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry (CDIAL 8897).
