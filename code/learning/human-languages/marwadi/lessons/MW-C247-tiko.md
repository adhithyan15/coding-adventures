# टीको (ṭīko) — a mark on the forehead

## You'll want to know: टीको
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C247-BYAV247-02]; assesses=[] -->

**टीको** — *ṭīko* — "a mark on the forehead".

> म्हारो टीको लाल है।
>
> *mhāro ṭīko lāl hai.*
>
> The mark on my forehead is red.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**टीको** (*ṭīko*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*ṭikka*, "mark, spot"; CDIAL lists Marwari *ṭīko*, a mark worn on the forehead, under it (CDIAL 5458).
