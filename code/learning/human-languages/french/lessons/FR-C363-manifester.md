## You'll want to know: manifester
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C363-MANIFESTATION-02]; assesses=[] -->

**manifester** — "to demonstrate (in the street); to show".

> Les étudiants manifestent contre la nouvelle loi.
>
> *The students are demonstrating against the new law.*

*Manifester sa joie* — to show your joy.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*manifester* is a learned borrowing from Latin *manifēstāre* "to make plain, to show" (Lewis & Short), from *manifestus* "clear, plain".
