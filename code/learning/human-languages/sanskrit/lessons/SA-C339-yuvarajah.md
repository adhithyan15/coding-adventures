## You'll want to know: युवराजः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C339-YUVARAJA339-01]; assesses=[] -->

**युवराजः** — *yuvarājaḥ* — "a crown prince".

> युवराजः अश्वेन नगरम् आगच्छति।
>
> *yuvarājaḥ aśvena nagaram āgacchati.*
>
> The crown prince comes to the city on horseback.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**युवराजः** (*yuvarājaḥ*) is built from *yuvan*, "young", and *rājan*, "a king"; MW gives *yuvarāja* as a young king, an heir-apparent, a crown prince (MW).
