## You'll want to know: पळ काढणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C300-LACH300-04]; assesses=[] -->

**पळ काढणे** — *paḷ kāḍhṇe* — "to flee; to take to one's heels".

> पोलिसांना पाहिल्यावर चोराने पळ काढला.
>
> *polisānnā pāhilyāvar corāne paḷ kāḍhlā.*
>
> When he saw the police, the thief fled.

Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने पळ काढला, *tyāne paḷ kāḍhlā*.

The agent takes -ने and पळ is masculine: चोराने पळ काढला. Plainer: पळून जाणे.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पळ काढणे** (*paḷ kāḍhṇe*) is built from *paḷ*, 'flight', of the family of Sanskrit *palāyate*, 'flees' (CDIAL 7955), and *kāḍhṇe*, 'to take out'.
