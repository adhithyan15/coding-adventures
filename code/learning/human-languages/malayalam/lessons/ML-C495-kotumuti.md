# കൊടുമുടി (koṭumuṭi) — a peak; a summit

## You'll want to know: കൊടുമുടി
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C495-MALA495-01]; assesses=[] -->

**കൊടുമുടി** — *koṭumuṭi* — "a peak; a summit".

> മഞ്ഞുകാലത്ത് ആ കൊടുമുടിയിൽ മഞ്ഞ് വീഴും.
>
> *maññukālattŭ ā koṭumuṭiyil maññŭ vīḻuṁ.*
>
> In winter snow falls on that peak.

Also the peak of anything: the top of fame or of a career.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**കൊടുമുടി** (*koṭumuṭi*) is a Malayalam compound whose second part is മുടി, the hair or crown of the head (DEDR 4921); the origin of the first part is not settled.
