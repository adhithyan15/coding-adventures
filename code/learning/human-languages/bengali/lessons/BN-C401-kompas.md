## You'll want to know: কম্পাস
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C401-DIK401-04]; assesses=[] -->

**কম্পাস** — *kômpās* — "a compass".

> বনে আমরা কম্পাস দেখে পথ খুঁজলাম।
>
> *bône āmrā kômpās dekhe pôth khũjlām.*
>
> In the forest we looked for the path by the compass.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**কম্পাস** (*kômpās*) is borrowed from English *compass*.
