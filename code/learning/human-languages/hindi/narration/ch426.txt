Hindi, chapter 426: Sums and Cash.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
रक़म (raqam) — a sum, an amount (of money).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a stink, then the Hindi for to seep.

You'll want to know: रक़म.
रक़म — raqam — "a sum, an amount (of money)".
यह रक़म बहुत बड़ी है। yah raqam bahut baṛī hai. This is a very large sum.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
रक़म (raqam) is usually traced to Arabic raqam, "a written figure, a mark", through Persian: the figure written on a cheque, and so the sum itself.

What you've built.
One more word for getting around.

मैंने दुकानदार को नक़द पैसे दिए। maiṁne dukāndār ko naqad paise diye. I paid the shopkeeper in cash.

The word, taken apart.
नक़द (naqad) is usually traced to Arabic naqd, "ready money", through Persian; Hindi breaks the final qd with a vowel.
