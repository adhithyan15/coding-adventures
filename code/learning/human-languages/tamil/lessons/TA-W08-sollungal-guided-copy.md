# சொல்லுங்கள் — one visible copy

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[TA-SCRIPT-SHORT-O-01, TA-LEX-SOLLUNGAL-01] -->

Point to **சொ** at the start of **சொல்லுங்கள்**. Read *so*, then say the whole
request: *solluṅgaḷ*, **please tell me**.

## Writing — guided copy
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-SCRIPT-WRITE-SOLLUNGAL-02]; assesses=[TA-SCRIPT-SHORT-O-01, TA-LEX-SOLLUNGAL-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: guided-copy -->

Copy **சொல்லுங்கள்** once beneath its visible model. Pause after **சொ** and
check that the short-*o* sign reaches around **ச**. Look back after every piece
if needed. Say the request once when you finish.

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[TA-SCRIPT-SHORT-O-01, TA-SCRIPT-WRITE-SOLLUNGAL-02, TA-LEX-SOLLUNGAL-01] -->

[PAUSE 3s] Point to the short-*o* syllable in **சொல்லுங்கள்**. (**சொ**.) What
does the word ask? (**Please tell me.**)
