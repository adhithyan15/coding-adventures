Gujarati, chapter 89: Questions and Answers.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
વિદ્યાર્થી (vidyārthī) — a student.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a letter, then the Gujarati for a gift.

You'll want to know: વિદ્યાર્થી.
વિદ્યાર્થી (vidyārthī) — "a student". Built on વિદ્યા, learning.

What you've built.
One more word for questions and answers.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: vidyārthī]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: vidyārthī, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say vidyārthī]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Gujarati for a letter, then the Gujarati for a gift, then say vidyārthī again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does વિદ્યાર્થી (vidyārthī) mean? ("A student".) Say it once more.
