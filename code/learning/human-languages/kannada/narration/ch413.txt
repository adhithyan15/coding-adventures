Kannada, chapter 413: Taking a Chance.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಲಾಟರಿ (lāṭari) — a lottery.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for hardship, then the Kannada for to ward off.

You'll want to know: ಲಾಟರಿ.
ಲಾಟರಿ — lāṭari — "a lottery".
ಅವನಿಗೆ ಲಾಟರಿಯಲ್ಲಿ ಹಣ ಬಂತು. avanige lāṭariyalli haṇa bantu. He won money in the lottery.
ಲಾಟರಿ (lāṭari) ಹೊಡೆ, to win the lottery.

The word, taken apart.
ಲಾಟರಿ (lāṭari) is a loan from English lottery.

Lesson 2 of 5.
ಜೂಜು (jūju) — gambling.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to ward off, then the Kannada for a lottery.

You'll want to know: ಜೂಜು.
ಜೂಜು — jūju — "gambling".
ಜೂಜಿನಿಂದ ಅವನು ಮನೆ ಕಳೆದುಕೊಂಡನು. jūjininda avanu mane kaḷedukoṇḍanu. He lost his house through gambling.

The word, taken apart.
ಜೂಜು (jūju) is usually traced to Prakrit jūa, "gambling", from Sanskrit dyūta, "gambling" (CDIAL 6608); Kannada is not among the forms CDIAL lists.

Lesson 4 of 5.
ಅಂದಾಜಿಸು (andājisu) — to estimate.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for gambling, then the Kannada for a bet.

You'll want to know: ಅಂದಾಜಿಸು.
ಅಂದಾಜಿಸು — andājisu — "to estimate".
ಈ ಕೆಲಸಕ್ಕೆ ಹತ್ತು ದಿನ ಬೇಕು ಅಂತ ನಾನು ಅಂದಾಜಿಸಿದೆ. ī kelasakke hattu dina bēku anta nānu andājiside. I estimated that this job needs ten days.
Past (I …): ಅಂದಾಜಿಸಿದೆ, andājiside.
ಅಂದಾಜು, an estimate; ಅಂದಾಜು ಮಾಡು, to guess.

The word, taken apart.
ಅಂದಾಜಿಸು (andājisu) is built on andāju, "an estimate", a loan from Persian andāza, "a measure", through Hindustani, with the Kannada verb ending -isu.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ಸರಾಸರಿ (sarāsari) — an average.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a bet, then the Kannada for to estimate.

You'll want to know: ಸರಾಸರಿ.
ಸರಾಸರಿ — sarāsari — "an average".
ತರಗತಿಯ ಸರಾಸರಿ ಅಂಕ ಅರವತ್ತು. taragatiya sarāsari aṅka aravattu. The class's average mark is sixty.
ಸರಾಸರಿ (sarāsari) ತೆಗೆ, to work out the average.

The word, taken apart.
ಸರಾಸರಿ (sarāsari) is usually traced to Persian sarāsarī, "from end to end, general", which reached Kannada through Hindustani or Marathi.
