## You'll want to know: হাট
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C420-HAT420-04]; assesses=[] -->

**হাট** — *hāṭ* — "a village market (on set days)".

> আমাদের গ্রামে বুধবার হাট বসে।
>
> *āmāder grāme budhbār hāṭ bôse.*
>
> In our village the market is held every Wednesday.

A বাজার opens every day; a হাট বসে, sits, on its own days of the week.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**হাট** (*hāṭ*) is inherited: CDIAL lists Bengali *hāṭ*, "a shop, a market", under Sanskrit *haṭṭa*, "a market, a fair" (CDIAL 13944).
