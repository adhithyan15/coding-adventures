# సందేహించు (sandēhiñcu) — to have misgivings

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[TE-LEX-C387-VIMARSA387-05, TE-LEX-C388-SANDEHAM388-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Telugu for to find fault with, then the Telugu for a misgiving.

## You'll want to know: సందేహించు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C388-SANDEHAM388-02]; assesses=[] -->

**సందేహించు** — *sandēhiñcu* — "to have misgivings".

> అతను నిజం చెప్పాడా అని నేను సందేహించాను.
>
> *atanu nijaṁ ceppāḍā ani nēnu sandēhiñcānu.*
>
> I doubted whether he had told the truth.

Past (I …): సందేహించాను, *sandēhiñcānu*.

What you doubt is often a question with అని: చెప్పాడా అని సందేహించు.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**సందేహించు** (*sandēhiñcu*) is built on Sanskrit *saṁdeha*, "doubt" (Monier-Williams), with the Telugu verb ending -ఇంచు; the noun is సందేహం.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
