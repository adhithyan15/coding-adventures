## You'll want to know: なま
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C496-SOFT496-03]; assesses=[] -->

**なま** — *nama* — "raw; uncooked".

> **にほんで なまの たまごを たべます。** — *nihon de nama no tamago o tabemasu* — "In Japan people eat raw eggs."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**なま** (*nama*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi, and its older origin is not settled (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
