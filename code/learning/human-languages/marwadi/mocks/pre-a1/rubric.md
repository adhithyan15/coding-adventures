# Marwadi pre-A1 mock rubric

This rubric scores mocks 1 and 2 against [`task-shapes/pre-a1.json`](../../task-shapes/pre-a1.json).
Each of reading, listening, writing, and speaking is a separate 100-point
paper. The project-defined threshold is at least 60/100 on every paper; a high
score on another paper cannot compensate for one below the threshold.

## Reading — 100 points

<!-- mock-part:prea1-reading-signs-and-words -->
### Signs and words — 40 points

Divide 40 points equally across the six keyed items; retain one decimal place.
Score meaning selection and Devanagari recognition, not reading aloud.
<!-- /mock-part:prea1-reading-signs-and-words -->

<!-- mock-part:prea1-reading-phrases-and-notices -->
### Phrases and notices — 30 points

Divide 30 points equally across the four keyed items. Score recognition of the
communicative purpose or situation.
<!-- /mock-part:prea1-reading-phrases-and-notices -->

<!-- mock-part:prea1-reading-personal-details -->
### Personal details — 30 points

Divide 30 points equally across the four keyed items. Score identification of
the asked-for name or family detail only.
<!-- /mock-part:prea1-reading-personal-details -->

## Listening — 100 points

Play each item twice at 70–90 words per minute with an eight-second answer
pause. Score only the selected answer. Do not show transcripts, romanization,
or an answer-bearing picture label.

<!-- mock-part:prea1-listening-sounds-and-words -->
### Sounds and words — 40 points

Divide 40 points equally across the six keyed items; retain one decimal place.
Score known-word recognition.
<!-- /mock-part:prea1-listening-sounds-and-words -->

<!-- mock-part:prea1-listening-greeting-responses -->
### Greeting responses — 30 points

Divide 30 points equally across the four keyed items. Score communicative
function and appropriate-response selection.
<!-- /mock-part:prea1-listening-greeting-responses -->

<!-- mock-part:prea1-listening-personal-details -->
### Personal details — 30 points

Divide 30 points equally across the four keyed items. Score the stated detail;
do not infer facts absent from the recording.
<!-- /mock-part:prea1-listening-personal-details -->

## Writing — 100 points

<!-- mock-part:prea1-writing-delayed-recall -->
### Delayed recall — 20 points

Award 10 points per word: 5 for recognizable ordered Devanagari letters, 3 for
required vowel signs and other marks, and 2 for legibility.
<!-- /mock-part:prea1-writing-delayed-recall -->

<!-- mock-part:prea1-writing-dictation -->
### Dictation — 40 points

Award 10 points per item: 5 for recognizable sound-to-letter mapping, 3 for
required vowel signs and marks, and 2 for legibility and word boundaries.
<!-- /mock-part:prea1-writing-dictation -->

<!-- mock-part:prea1-writing-bounded-production -->
### Bounded independent production — 40 points

Award 20 points per response: 8 for completing the purpose, 7 for recognizable
Devanagari word formation, 3 for required signs and marks, and 2 for legibility.
Do not demand a single memorized name or greeting. A romanized, traced, or
visibly copied response earns zero: it is not independent writing evidence.
<!-- /mock-part:prea1-writing-bounded-production -->

## Speaking — 100 points

The interlocutor may repeat each prompt once slowly without penalty. Within
each part award 40% for completing the communicative purpose, 25% for
intelligibility to a trained Marwadi speaker, 20% for an appropriate taught
word or chunk, and 15% for responding without an answer script. A memorized
chunk is valid at pre-A1; support-language-only speech is not Marwadi evidence.

<!-- mock-part:prea1-speaking-greeting-and-identity -->
### Greeting and identity — 30 points

Score both the greeting response and the supplied identity.
<!-- /mock-part:prea1-speaking-greeting-and-identity -->

<!-- mock-part:prea1-speaking-familiar-questions -->
### Familiar questions — 40 points

Score relevance and intelligibility across the three short responses. Accept
any taught form that accomplishes the purpose.
<!-- /mock-part:prea1-speaking-familiar-questions -->

<!-- mock-part:prea1-speaking-short-request -->
### Short request — 30 points

Score whether the pictured-item request is intelligible and appropriate. Do
not require the example response word for word.
<!-- /mock-part:prea1-speaking-short-request -->

## Readiness boundary

This form is structurally executable, not a claim of attainment, exam
readiness, or external equivalence. Before such a claim, record a clean timed
administration, qualified Marwadi review, standard setting, and book-only
human validation under the assessment specification.
