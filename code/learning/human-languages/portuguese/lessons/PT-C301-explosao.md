## You'll want to know: a explosão
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C301-INCENDIO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**a explosão** — "the explosion".

> Ouvimos uma explosão na fábrica.
>
> *We heard an explosion at the factory.*

Also of feelings: *uma explosão de alegria*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*explosão* is a learned borrowing from Latin *explōsiō* "a driving off by clapping" (EtymDB), from *explōdere*, built on *plaudere* "to clap".
