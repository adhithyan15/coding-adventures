Tamil, chapter 147: Young, Old, Clever, Lazy, Brave.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
இளைய (iḷaiya) — young, younger.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for rich, then the Tamil for poor.
[pause 2 seconds]
An adjective goes before the noun and does not change: பெரிய வீடு, சிறிய வீடு.

You'll want to know: இளைய.
இளைய — iḷaiya — "young, younger".
