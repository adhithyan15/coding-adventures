## You'll want to know: утверждать
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C283-UTVERZHDAT-01]; assesses=[] -->

**утверждать** (*utverzhdát*) — "to claim, to assert".

> Он утверждает, что видел медведя.
>
> *On utverzhdáyet, shto vídel medvédya.*
>
> He claims that he saw a bear.

In this sense imperfective. The perfective *утвердить* (*utverdít*) means "to approve": *утвердить план*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*утверждать* is built on *у-* and the root of *твёрдый* "firm", Proto-Slavic *tvĭrdŭ*, in its Church Slavonic shape *-твержд-*; EtymDB lists no parent for the verb.
