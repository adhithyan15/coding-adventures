# An old word for believing

Hear one familiar meaning, pause, and retrieve its Punjabi word before the
model. This is a gentle return, not a new word or a scored mock.

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-LEX-C152-ACT152-04] -->

Hear **to believe, to agree**. [PAUSE 4s] Recall **ਮੰਨਣਾ** (*mannṇā*).

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-LEX-C152-ACT152-04] -->

Hear **ਮੰਨਣਾ**. [PAUSE 4s] Recall **to believe, to agree**.
Then hear the English cue again. [PAUSE 4s] Say **ਮੰਨਣਾ** before the model.
Only this old word is needed.

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-LEX-C152-ACT152-04] -->

One last cue: **to believe, to agree**. [PAUSE 4s] Recall **ਮੰਨਣਾ**.
Listen and retry once if it does not come back.
