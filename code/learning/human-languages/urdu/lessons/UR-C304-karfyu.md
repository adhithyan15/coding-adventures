## You'll want to know: کرفیو
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C304-FASAD304-05]; assesses=[] -->

**کرفیو** — *karfyū* — "a curfew".

> شام سے صبح تک کرفیو تھا۔
>
> *shām se subah tak karfyū thā.*
>
> There was a curfew from evening until morning.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

is borrowed from English *curfew*. During a **کرفیو** nobody may go out.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
