## You'll want to know: सनई
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C317-AHER317-04]; assesses=[] -->

**सनई** — *sanaī* — "a shehnai".

> लग्नघरात सकाळपासून सनई वाजत होती.
>
> *lagnagharāt sakāḷpāsūn sanaī vājat hotī.*
>
> The shehnai had been playing in the wedding house since morning.

Feminine.

A reed pipe played at weddings and festivals.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सनई** (*sanaī*) names the reed pipe of weddings. It is usually traced to Persian, to *surnāy*, 'a festive pipe', or to *šahnāy*, 'a royal pipe'; which one, and by what path, is not settled.
