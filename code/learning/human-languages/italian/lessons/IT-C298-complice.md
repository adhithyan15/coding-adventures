## You'll want to know: il complice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C298-BANDA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**il complice** — "the accomplice".

> Il rapinatore aveva un complice in macchina.
>
> *The robber had an accomplice in the car.*

Also an adjective: *uno sguardo complice*, a knowing look.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*complice* is a learned borrowing from Late Latin *complex, complicis* "closely joined, an accomplice", from *complicāre* "to fold together" (Lewis & Short).
