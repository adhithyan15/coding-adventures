# Review — how it felt, and what comes next

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[FR-GRAM-SENTIMENT-ETAIS-01, FR-GRAM-ESPERER-INFINITIF-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "my mother was worried". (*Ma mère était inquiète.*) Then "I
hope to come back". (*J'espère revenir.*)

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[FR-GRAM-CONTENT-DE-02, FR-GRAM-MON-REVE-02, FR-GRAM-DECOR-EVENEMENT-01, FR-GRAM-RECIT-ORDRE-02, FR-LEX-C138-VERBES90-01] -->

Two lessons, and an experience has a shape: what happened, how it felt, and
what you want next. The feeling is scene, in the imparfait: *j'étais
content*, *j'étais nerveux*. What it was about takes *de*: *content du
voyage*. And the future comes in three strengths: *je voudrais*, *j'espère*,
*mon rêve, c'est de*.

[PAUSE 2s] Here is a journey told with all three:

> *D'abord, j'étais nerveux, parce que je ne connaissais pas la ville. Mais
> ensuite, j'étais content du voyage. J'espère revenir la semaine prochaine.*

*Je ne connaissais pas* is *connaître* in the imparfait, on the *nous
connaissons* stem: a not-knowing that went on, so it is scene.

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[FR-GRAM-SENTIMENT-ETAIS-01, FR-GRAM-ESPERER-INFINITIF-01, FR-GRAM-MON-REVE-02] -->

[PAUSE 3s] Is *j'étais nerveux* scene or event? (**Scene.**) What goes
between *j'espère* and *revenir*? (**Nothing.**) Say "my dream is to live by
the sea". (***Mon rêve, c'est de vivre près de la mer.***)
