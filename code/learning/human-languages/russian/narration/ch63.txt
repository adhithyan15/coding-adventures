Russian, chapter 63: White, Black, Red, Blue, Green.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
белый (bélyy) — white.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a duck, then the Russian for a goat.

You'll want to know: белый.
белый (bélyy) — "white".

What you've built.
One more word for white, black, red, blue, green.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: bélyy]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: bélyy, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say bélyy]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Russian for a duck, then the Russian for a goat, then say bélyy again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does белый (bélyy) mean? ("White".) Say it once more.
