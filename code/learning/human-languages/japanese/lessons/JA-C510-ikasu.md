## You'll want to know: いかす
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C510-CHANCE510-04]; assesses=[] -->

**いかす** — *ikasu* — "to make good use of".

> **もし ちえを いかしたら、いい しごとが みつかるでしょう。** — *moshi chie o ikashitara, ii shigoto ga mitsukaru deshō* — "If you put that know-how to good use, you will probably find a good job."

Polite **いかします**, *ikashimasu*; a godan verb. Past (polite): **かれが ちえを いかしました。**, *kare ga chie o ikashimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**いかす** (*ikasu*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi, and its older origin is not settled (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
