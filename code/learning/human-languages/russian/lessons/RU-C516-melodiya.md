## You'll want to know: мелодия
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C516-MELODY-01]; assesses=[] -->

**мелодия** (*melódiya*) — "a melody, a tune".

> Эта мелодия мне очень знакома.
>
> *Éta melódiya mne óchen znakóma.*
>
> This tune is very familiar to me.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*мелодия* comes from Greek *melōidía* 'singing, chanting', built on *mélos* 'song' (EtymDB).
