## You'll want to know: えがお
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C245-JOY245-05]; assesses=[] -->

**えがお** — *egao* — "a smile; a smiling face".

> **そぼは いつも えがおですか。** — *sobo wa itsumo egao desu ka* — "Is my grandmother always smiling?"

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**えがお** (*egao*) is a native Japanese compound (wago), inherited rather than borrowed: it is built from *e*, from *emu*, "to smile", and *kao*, "face", with *k* voiced to *g* (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
