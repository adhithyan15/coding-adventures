## You'll want to know: へいき
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C426-FEEL426-03]; assesses=[] -->

**へいき** — *heiki* — "unbothered; all right".

> **ちちは さむい あさも へいきです。** — *chichi wa samui asa mo heiki desu* — "My father does not mind even cold mornings."

A na-adjective: な before a noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**へいき** (*heiki*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *hei*, "peace" (Middle Chinese *bjaeng*) and *ki*, "spirit" (*khj+jH*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
