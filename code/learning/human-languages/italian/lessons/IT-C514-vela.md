## You'll want to know: la vela
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C514-ACQUA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**la vela** — "sail; sailing".

> D'estate faccio un corso di vela.
>
> *In summer I take a sailing course.*

*Andare a vela* — to sail; *la barca a vela* — sailing boat.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*vela* is inherited from Latin *vēla*, the plural of *vēlum* "a sail, a covering" (EtymDB).
