## You'll want to know: bouleverser
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C472-NOSTALGIE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**bouleverser** — "to deeply upset, to overwhelm; to turn upside down".

> Cette nouvelle a bouleversé toute la famille.
>
> *This news deeply upset the whole family.*

Also of plans or a room: *il a bouleversé nos projets*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*bouleverser* is built from *boule* "ball", from Latin *bulla* "a bubble", and *verser* "to tip over", from Latin *versāre* "to turn", built on *vertere* (Lewis & Short): to send rolling like a ball.
