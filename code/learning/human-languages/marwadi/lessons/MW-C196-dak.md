## You'll want to know: डाक
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C196-POST196-01]; assesses=[] -->

**डाक** — *ḍāk* — "the post; mail".

> आज डाक कोनी आई।
>
> *āj ḍāk konī āī.*
>
> The post didn't come today.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**डाक** (*ḍāk*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*ḍakka*, "relay, post". CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry, but lists Hindi and Gujarati *ḍāk*, "relay, post, posting stage" (CDIAL 5519).
