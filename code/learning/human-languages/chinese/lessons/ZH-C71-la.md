## You'll want to know: là
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C71-TASTE71-03]; assesses=[] -->

*là* — "spicy; hot".

> *zhèr de cài hěn là* — The food here is really spicy.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*là* is inherited from Middle Chinese *lat* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'pungent'; the dictionary writes it with a variant form of the character.
