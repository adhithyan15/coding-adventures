## You'll want to know: めんせつ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C457-JOBHUNT457-02]; assesses=[] -->

**めんせつ** — *mensetsu* — "a job interview; an interview".

> **あした かいしゃで めんせつが あります。** — *ashita kaisha de mensetsu ga arimasu* — "I have an interview at a company tomorrow."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**めんせつ** (*mensetsu*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *men*, "face" (Middle Chinese *mjienH*) and *setsu*, "contact" (*tsjep*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
