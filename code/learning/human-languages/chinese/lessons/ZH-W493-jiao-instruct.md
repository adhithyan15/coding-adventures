## Script — the top of lǎo over zǐ, then four strokes
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-SCRIPT-JIAO-INSTRUCT-01]; assesses=[] -->

> 教

**Two pieces, left before right.**

**The left piece, seven strokes.** First the top of 老, four strokes, as you wrote them: a short bar, a vertical through it, a long bar, and a long stroke falling to the left. Then 子 *zǐ*, three strokes, tucked under it: a bar that turns and falls to the left, a vertical with a hook, and a stroke rising to the right.

**The right piece, four strokes**: a short stroke falling to the left, a bar, a long stroke falling to the left, and a long stroke falling to the right.

4 + 3 + 4 = eleven.
