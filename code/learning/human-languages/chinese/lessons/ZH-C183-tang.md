## You'll want to know: tǎng
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C183-INOUT183-05]; assesses=[] -->

*tǎng* — "to lie down".

> *wǒ hěn lèi xiǎng tǎng yíhuìr* — I'm tired; I want to lie down for a bit.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*tǎng* No dictionary we use records an older form, so its history is not settled.
