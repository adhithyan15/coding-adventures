## You'll want to know: ઊડવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C237-WINGS237-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ઊડવું** — *ūḍvũ* — "to fly up, to fly".

> **કાગડો આકાશમાં ઊડ્યો.** — *kāgḍo ākāśmā̃ ūḍyo* — "The crow flew up into the sky."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ઊડવું** (*ūḍvũ*) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from *uḍḍīyate*, "flies up" (CDIAL 1697).
