## You'll want to know: 西南
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C423-SOUTH423-03]; assesses=[] -->

**西南** — *xīnán* — "southwest; the Southwest (of China)".

> *wǒmen wǎng xīnán kāi* — We're driving southwest.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**西南** (*xīnán*) is built from 西 'west' and 南 'south', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *sej* and *nom* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription).
