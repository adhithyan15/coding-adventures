## You'll want to know: впервые
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C278-VPERVYYE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**впервые** (*vpervýye*) — "for the first time".

> Я впервые увидел море в девять лет.
>
> *Ya vpervýye uvídel móre v dévyat let.*
>
> I saw the sea for the first time at nine.

Also *в первый раз*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*впервые* is built on *в* and *первый* "first", which is inherited from Proto-Slavic *pĭrvŭ* "first" (EtymDB).
