## You'll want to know: ਬਰੀਕ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C297-TOUCH297-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ਬਰੀਕ** — *barīk* — "fine; fine-grained".

> ਦਰਿਆ ਦੇ ਕਿਨਾਰੇ ਬਰੀਕ ਰੇਤ ਹੈ।
>
> *dariā de kināre barīk ret hai.*
>
> There is fine sand on the bank of the river.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਬਰੀਕ** (*barīk*) is a loan, usually traced to Persian *bārīk*, "thin, fine".
