## You'll want to know: la declaración
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C515-RUMOR-03]; assesses=[] -->

**la declaración** — "the statement; the declaration".

> La vecina hizo una declaración a la policía.
>
> *The neighbour made a statement to the police.*

*Prestar declaración* is to give evidence.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*declaración* is a learned borrowing from Latin *dēclārātiō* 'a making clear' (EtymDB), built on *dēclārāre*, from *clārus* 'clear, bright'.
