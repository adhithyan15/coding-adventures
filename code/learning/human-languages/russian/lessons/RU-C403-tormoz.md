## You'll want to know: тормоз
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C403-RUL-02]; assesses=[] -->

**тормоз** (*tórmoz*) — "a brake".

> У этой старой машины плохие тормоза.
>
> *U étoy stároy mashíny plokhíye tormozá.*
>
> This old car has bad brakes.

Plural *тормоза*. *Нажать на тормоз* — to put on the brake.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*тормоз* goes back to Greek *tórmos* 'a socket into which a peg fits' (EtymDB). How the word reached Russian is not settled.
