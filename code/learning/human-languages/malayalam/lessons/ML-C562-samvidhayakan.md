## You'll want to know: സംവിധായകൻ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C562-RAGAM562-03]; assesses=[] -->

**സംവിധായകൻ** — *saṁvidhāyakan* — "a director".

> ആ സിനിമയുടെ സംവിധായകന് സമ്മാനം കിട്ടി.
>
> *ā sinimayuṭe saṁvidhāyakanŭ sammānaṁ kiṭṭi.*
>
> The director of that film won a prize.

സംവിധാനം ചെയ്യുക is to direct a film.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**സംവിധായകൻ** (*saṁvidhāyakan*) is built on Sanskrit *saṁvidhāna*, "arrangement, management" (Monier-Williams), with the agent ending -അകൻ: the one who arranges.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
