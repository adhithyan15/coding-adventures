## You'll want to know: तापमान
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C439-TAP439-03]; assesses=[] -->

**तापमान** — *tāpmān* — "temperature".

> रात को तापमान बहुत नीचे गिर गया।
>
> *rāt ko tāpmān bahut nīce gir gayā.*
>
> At night the temperature dropped a long way.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**तापमान** (*tāpmān*) is a modern compound of Sanskrit *tāpa*, "heat", and *māna*, "measure" (Monier-Williams for both): the measure of heat.
