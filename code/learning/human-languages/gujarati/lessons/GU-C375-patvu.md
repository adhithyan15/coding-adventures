## You'll want to know: પતવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C375-JARURI375-04]; assesses=[] -->

**પતવું** — *patvũ* — "to get done".

> જો કામ વહેલું પતશે તો આપણે ફિલ્મ જોવા જઈશું.
>
> *jo kām vahelũ patśe to āpaṇe philm jovā jaīśũ.*
>
> If the work gets done early, we will go and see a film.

Past (it …): તે કામ પત્યું, *te kām patyũ*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**પતવું** (*patvũ*) is the intransitive partner of **પતાવવું** (*patāvvũ*), "to finish"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb, "to be settled", under Sanskrit *prāpta*, "obtained" (CDIAL 8945).
