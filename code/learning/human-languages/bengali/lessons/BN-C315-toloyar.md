## You'll want to know: তলোয়ার
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C315-OSTRO315-02]; assesses=[] -->

**তলোয়ার** — *tôloyār* — "a sword".

> রাজকুমার তলোয়ার নিয়ে শত্রুর সঙ্গে লড়লেন।
>
> *rājkumār tôloyār niye shôtrur sôngge lôṛlen.*
>
> The prince took up his sword and fought the enemy.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**তলোয়ার** (*tôloyār*) is usually traced to Sanskrit *taravāri*, 'a one-edged sword' (CDIAL 5706; Monier-Williams), which Monier-Williams wonders may stand for *tala-v-*.
