## You'll want to know: استغل
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C388-FASAD388-05]; assesses=[] -->

**استغل** — *istaghalla* — "to exploit".

> استغل صاحب المصنع العمال.
>
> *istaghalla ṣāḥib al-maṣnaʿ al-ʿummāl.*
>
> The factory owner exploited the workers.

Present: يستغل, *yastaghill*.

Also, neutrally, to make use of: استغل الوقت, made good use of the time.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**استغل** (*istaghalla*) is built on the native Arabic root *gh-l-l*, "to yield produce" (Lane). It is Form X, to seek the yield of a thing: to make use of it, or to exploit it.
