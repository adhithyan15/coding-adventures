## You'll want to know: मना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C191-NOTICE191-03]; assesses=[] -->

**मना** — *manā* — "forbidden".

> अठै खाणो मना है।
>
> *aṭhai khāṇo manā hai.*
>
> No eating here.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**मना** (*manā*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *manʿ*, "prevention, holding back".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for reading notices and messages.
