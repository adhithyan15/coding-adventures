## You'll want to know: l'acompte
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C433-DEDOMMAGEMENT-01]; assesses=[] -->

**l'acompte** — "the down payment, the part payment".

> Pour le gîte, nous avons versé un acompte de cent euros.
>
> *For the cottage we paid a hundred euros up front.*

A masculine noun. *Verser un acompte* — to pay a part in advance.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*acompte* comes from *à compte* 'on account', from *compte*, built on *compter* 'to count', which goes back to Latin *computāre* (Lewis & Short). EtymDB does not list it.
