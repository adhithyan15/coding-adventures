Marwadi (Marwari), chapter 14: Three Useful Places: Two New Signs.
9 lessons. You can do the first 1 of them in the car; after that you will want to have stopped.

Lesson 2 of 9.
ज (ja) — the middle of market.

Before we start: this one needs your hands, so it is not a driving lesson. You will want a pen and something to write on and your eyes, for letter shapes on the page. You can listen to everything else now — leave the section called Script — one sign until you have stopped, and I will say so again when we reach it.

Warm-up.
[pause 18 seconds]
Say market and polite yes, write हां, बच्चा, and familiar च once.

Script — one sign.
ज — ja.

Guided Practice.
Trace once, copy once, cover the model, and write ज (ja) from sound.

Wrap-up Recall.
Source: Unicode Devanagari chart.

Write the new Devanagari sign for ja.


Lesson 3 of 9.
बाजार (bājār) — market on the page.

Warm-up.
[pause 20 seconds]
Say market and child. Give polite yes, recall the seven-label payoff, then write ब, ज (ja), ा, and र.

You'll want to know.
बा + जार becomes बाजार — bājār — market.

Guided Practice.
Look, cover, wait five seconds, and write the word. Check both long-vowel marks.

Wrap-up Recall.
Source: Marwari Pathshala, Lesson 5.
[question — say your answer, then pause 12 seconds]
From bājār, write the place word.

ghar — home; house.

Lesson 8 of 9.
मंदिर (mandir) — a new place from known signs.

Warm-up.
[pause 20 seconds]
Say temple, write म, ं, द, ि, र, ी, then write घर (ghar) and नानी.

You'll want to know.
मं + दि + र becomes मंदिर — mandir — temple.

Guided Practice.
Look, cover, wait five seconds, and write the word. Check the nasal mark and the short-i position separately.

Wrap-up Recall.
Source: Marwari Pathshala, Lesson 5.
[question — say your answer, then pause 12 seconds]
From mandir, write the place word.
