## You'll want to know: いずれか
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C528-OR528-05]; assesses=[] -->

**いずれか** — *izureka* — "either one; one of them".

> **もし じかんが あったら、いずれかの しあいに きて ください。** — *moshi jikan ga attara, izureka no shiai ni kite kudasai* — "If you have time, please come to one of the matches."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**いずれか** (*izureka*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, and usually written in kana: built from *izure*, "which", and the particle *ka* (JMdict).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
