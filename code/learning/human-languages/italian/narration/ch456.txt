Italian, chapter 456: Capelli e occhiali.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
il parrucchiere — the hairdresser.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for to shorten, then the Italian for the locksmith.

You'll want to know: il parrucchiere.
il parrucchiere — "the hairdresser".
Ho appuntamento dal parrucchiere alle tre. I have an appointment at the hairdresser's at three.
La parrucchiera; andare dal parrucchiere.

The word, taken apart.
parrucchiere is built on parrucca 'wig': first the wig-maker. The origin of parrucca is not settled.

What you've built.
One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.

Lesson 3 of 5.

You'll want to know: l'estetista.
l'estetista — "the beautician".
L'estetista lavora vicino al parrucchiere. The beautician works near the hairdresser's.
One form for a man or a woman.

The word, taken apart.
estetista is built on estetica 'beauty care', a learned borrowing from Greek aisthētikós 'of the senses'.

Lesson 5 of 5.
smacchiare — to remove stains from.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for the beautician, then the Italian for the optician.

You'll want to know: smacchiare.
smacchiare — "to remove stains from".
In lavanderia possono smacchiare la giacca. At the dry cleaner's they can get the stains out of the jacket.
La macchia — a stain.

The word, taken apart.
smacchiare is built from s- and macchia 'stain', which is inherited from Latin macula 'spot' (Lewis & Short).
