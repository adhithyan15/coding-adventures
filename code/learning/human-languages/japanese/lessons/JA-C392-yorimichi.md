# よりみち (yorimichi) — a stop on the way; a side trip

## You'll want to know: よりみち
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C392-YORIMICHI392-01]; assesses=[] -->

**よりみち** — *yorimichi* — "a stop on the way; a side trip".

> **しごとの あとで、ほんやに よりみちしました。** — *shigoto no ato de, hon'ya ni yorimichi shimashita* — "After work I stopped off at a bookshop."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**よりみち** (*yorimichi*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, built from *yori*, "dropping in" (from *yoru*) and *michi*, "road"; its kanji were attached later as kun'yomi (JMdict).
