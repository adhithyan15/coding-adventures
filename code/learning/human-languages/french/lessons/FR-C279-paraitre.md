## You'll want to know: paraître
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C279-REDIGER-04]; assesses=[] -->

**paraître** — "to come out, to be published; to seem".

> Le magazine paraît tous les jeudis.
>
> *The magazine comes out every Thursday.*

Two senses: to be published, and *il paraît que* — it seems, people say. The current spelling also allows *paraitre*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*paraître* is inherited from Late Latin *pārēscere*, built on Latin *pārēre* "to appear, to come in sight" (Lewis & Short).
