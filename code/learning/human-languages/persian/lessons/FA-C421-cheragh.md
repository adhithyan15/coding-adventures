## You'll want to know: چراغ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C421-BUGH-03]; assesses=[] -->

**چراغ** (*cherâgh*) — "a light, a lamp".

> شب چراغهای ماشین را روشن میکنیم.
>
> *Shab cherâgh-hâ-ye mâshin râ roshan mikonim.*
>
> At night we switch on the car's lights.

*Cherâgh-e qermez* — a red light; *cherâgh-e râhnamâ* — an indicator.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*cherâgh* is inherited: EtymDB traces it to Middle Persian *čirāgh* 'lamp' and also links it with a Parthian form of the same word.
