## You'll want to know: ಕುಂಟು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C509-ELUBU509-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ಕುಂಟು** — *kuṇṭu* — "to limp".

> ಕಾಲಿಗೆ ಗಾಯ ಆಗಿದ್ದರಿಂದ ಅವನು ಕುಂಟುತ್ತಿದ್ದನು.
>
> *kālige gāya āgiddarinda avanu kuṇṭuttiddanu.*
>
> Because his leg was hurt, he was limping.

Past (I …): ಕುಂಟಿದೆ, *kuṇṭide*.

ಕುಂಟ is a lame man; ಕುಂಟಾಟ is hopscotch.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಕುಂಟು** (*kuṇṭu*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 1688 lists Kannada *kuṇṭu*, "to limp, halt, hop", and *kuṇṭa*, "a lame man".
