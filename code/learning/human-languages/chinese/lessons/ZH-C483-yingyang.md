## You'll want to know: yíngyǎng
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C483-BETTER483-03]; assesses=[] -->

*yíngyǎng* — "nutrition; nourishment".

> *shuǐguǒ hěn yǒu yíngyǎng nǐ yīnggāi měi tiān chī* — Fruit is very good for you, so you should eat some every day.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*yíngyǎng* is built from *yíng* 'to build' and *yǎng* 'to nourish', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *yweng* and *yangX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to make' and the second 'to raise' and 'to feed'.
