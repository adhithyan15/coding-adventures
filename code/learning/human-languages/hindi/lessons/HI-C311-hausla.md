## You'll want to know: हौसला
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C311-MADAD311-05]; assesses=[] -->

**हौसला** — *hauslā* — "spirit; morale".

> स्वयंसेवकों ने पीड़ितों का हौसला बढ़ाया।
>
> *svayaṁsevakoṁ ne pīṛitoṁ kā hauslā baṛhāyā.*
>
> The volunteers raised the victims' spirits.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**हौसला** (*hauslā*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *ḥawṣala*, first the crop of a bird and then capacity; in Hindi it is the courage to keep going. **हौसला बढ़ाना** is to encourage.
