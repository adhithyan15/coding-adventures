## You'll want to know: статья
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C279-IZVESTIYE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**статья** (*statyá*) — "an article".

> Он написал статью о нашей школе.
>
> *On napisál statyú o náshey shkóle.*
>
> He wrote an article about our school.

Genitive *статьи*. Also an article of a law: *статья закона*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*статья* is usually traced to the root of *стать* "to stand, to become", inherited from Proto-Slavic *stati* (EtymDB), with an old sense "a part in its place"; EtymDB lists no parent for *статья* itself.
