## You'll want to know: le héros
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C521-AUTEURS-03]; assesses=[] -->

**le héros** — "hero; main character".

> Le héros du livre est un vieux marin.
>
> *The hero of the book is an old sailor.*

The *h* is aspirated: *le héros*, *les héros* with no liaison.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*le héros* comes from Latin *hērōs*, a borrowing from Greek *hḗrōs* "a demigod, a hero" (EtymDB).
