## You'll want to know: la conclusione
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C278-PRINCIPIO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**la conclusione** — "the conclusion".

> Alla fine siamo arrivati a una conclusione.
>
> *In the end we reached a conclusion.*

*In conclusione* — in conclusion.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*conclusione* is a learned borrowing from Latin *conclūsiō* "a closing, a conclusion" (EtymDB), from *conclūdere*.
