## You'll want to know: fēngsú
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C338-VISIT338-05]; assesses=[] -->

*fēngsú* — "custom; social custom".

> *měi ge dìfang de fēngsú dōu bù yíyàng* — Every place has its own customs.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*fēngsú* is built from 风 'wind' and *sú* 'usage', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *pjuwng* and *zjowk* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second with this very word.
