## You'll want to know: ぬぐ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C149-DRESS149-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ぬぐ** — *nugu* — "to take off (clothes, shoes)".

> **うちで くつを ぬぎます。** — *uchi de kutsu o nugimasu* — "I take off my shoes at home."

Polite **ぬぎます**, *nugimasu*; a godan verb.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ぬぐ** (*nugu*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi, and its older origin is not settled (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
