Russian, chapter 324: Victory.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
победа (pobéda) — a victory, a win.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to fight, then the Russian for an enemy.

You'll want to know: победа.
победа (pobéda) — "a victory, a win".
Победа была очень важной для всех. Pobéda bylá óchen vázhnoy dlya vsekh. The victory was very important for everyone.
Feminine. День Победы — Victory Day, 9 May.

The word, taken apart.
победа (pobéda) is inherited from Old East Slavic poběda "victory, defeat" (EtymDB), built on по- and беда "misfortune": first the trouble one side brings on the other.

Lesson 2 of 5.

You'll want to know: побеждать.
побеждать (pobezhdát) — "to win, to defeat".
Наша команда победила в финале. Násha kománda pobedíla v finále. Our team won the final.
Imperfective; perfective победить (pobedít), which has no I form in the future: Russians say я одержу победу.

The word, taken apart.
побеждать (pobezhdát) is built on победа (pobéda) "victory", Old East Slavic poběda (EtymDB), in the Church Slavonic shape -жд-.

Lesson 3 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a victory, then the Russian for to win.

You'll want to know: воевать.
воевать (voyevát) — "to wage war, to fight".
Его дедушка воевал четыре года. Yevó dédushka voyevál chetýre góda. His grandfather fought in the war for four years.
Imperfective; present воюю, воюешь. Воевать с + instrumental.

The word, taken apart.
воевать (voyevát) is built on воин "a warrior", from Proto-Slavic vojinŭ, with the verb ending -евать (EtymDB).

Lesson 4 of 5.
погибать (pogibát) — to die (in war or an accident), to perish.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to win, then the Russian for to wage war.

You'll want to know: погибать.
погибать (pogibát) — "to die (in war or an accident), to perish".
В той войне погибло много людей. V toy voyné pogíblo mnógo lyudéy. Many people died in that war.
Imperfective; perfective погибнуть (pogíbnut), past погиб. For a violent death, not an illness.

The word, taken apart.
погибать (pogibát) is built on по- and гибнуть "to perish", inherited from Proto-Slavic gybnǫti (EtymDB), the root of гибель "doom".

You'll want to know: освобождать.
освобождать (osvobozhdát) — "to free, to liberate; to vacate".
Солдаты освободили город. Soldáty osvobodíli górod. The soldiers liberated the town.
Imperfective; perfective освободить (osvobodít), освобожу. Also to free up: освободить место.

The word, taken apart.
освобождать (osvobozhdát) is built on о- and свобода "freedom", in the Church Slavonic shape -жд-; EtymDB lists освободить with no parent.
