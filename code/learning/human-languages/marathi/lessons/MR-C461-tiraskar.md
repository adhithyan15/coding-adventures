## You'll want to know: तिरस्कार
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C461-APMAN461-03]; assesses=[] -->

**तिरस्कार** — *tiraskār* — "contempt".

> त्याला खोटेपणाचा तिरस्कार वाटतो.
>
> *tyālā khoṭepaṇācā tiraskār vāṭto.*
>
> He feels contempt for dishonesty.

Masculine.

तिरस्कार वाटणे, to feel contempt, with the dative; तिरस्कार करणे, to scorn.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**तिरस्कार** (*tiraskār*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *tiraskāra*, "placing aside, concealment" (Monier-Williams); in Marathi it is contempt.
