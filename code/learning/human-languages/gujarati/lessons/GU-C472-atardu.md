## You'll want to know: આંતરડું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C472-NAS472-04]; assesses=[] -->

**આંતરડું** — *ā̃tarḍũ* — "an intestine".

> વાસી ભોજનથી આંતરડામાં ચેપ લાગે છે.
>
> *vāsī bhojanthī ā̃tarḍāmā̃ cep lāge che.*
>
> Stale food causes infection in the intestines.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**આંતરડું** (*ā̃tarḍũ*) is inherited from Sanskrit *āntra*, "entrail"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati word, "entrail" (CDIAL 1182), with the noun ending *-ḍũ*.
