## You'll want to know: кредит
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C394-DOLG-02]; assesses=[] -->

**кредит** (*kredít*) — "a loan, credit".

> Они взяли кредит на машину.
>
> *Oní vzyáli kredít na mashínu.*
>
> They took out a loan for a car.

Masculine. *Брать кредит* — to take out a loan. *В кредит* — on credit.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**кредит** is usually traced, through German and French, to Latin *crēditum* "a loan", from *crēdere* "to trust, to lend" (Lewis & Short): a loan rests on trust.
