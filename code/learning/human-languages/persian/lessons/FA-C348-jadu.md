## You'll want to know: جادو
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C348-PARI-02]; assesses=[] -->

**جادو** (*jâdu*) — "magic, a spell".

> با جادو در باز شد.
>
> *Bâ jâdu dar bâz shod.*
>
> The door opened by magic.

*Jâdui* "magic, magical": *farsh-e jâdui* "a magic carpet".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*jâdu* is a native Persian word; EtymDB lists it without a parent.
