## You'll want to know: جا افتادن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C358-QABLAME-04]; assesses=[] -->

**جا افتادن** (*jâ oftâdan*) — "to be well cooked (of a stew); to settle in".

> خورش بعد از دو ساعت جا افتاد.
>
> *Khoresh ba'd az do sâ'at jâ oftâd.*
>
> After two hours the stew was nicely cooked.

Of a stew, the flavours have come together. Of a bone, "to go back into place"; of an idea, "to be accepted".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*jâ* "place" is inherited from Middle Persian (EtymDB), and *oftâdan* "to fall" is native Persian: to fall into place.
