## HL-C353 — Hindi helping declares its conjunct-verb present forms

Chapter 35 uses **मदद करना** to teach how a Hindi conjunct verb works: the noun
stays fixed while **करना** produces **मदद करता / मदद करती / मदद करते**. All
three present forms are explained and practised, but the lesson declaration
exposes only the infinitive, leaving respectful-plural **करते** looking untaught
to the corpus report.

Declare the three complete present phrases beside the infinitive. This makes the
lesson's central noun-plus-verb pattern visible to reports and downstream study
artifacts without adding new material.
