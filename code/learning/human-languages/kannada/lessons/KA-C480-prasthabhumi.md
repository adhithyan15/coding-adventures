## You'll want to know: ಪ್ರಸ್ಥಭೂಮಿ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C480-BAYALU480-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ಪ್ರಸ್ಥಭೂಮಿ** — *prasthabhūmi* — "a plateau; a tableland".

> ಈ ಪ್ರಸ್ಥಭೂಮಿಯಲ್ಲಿ ಮಳೆ ಕಡಿಮೆ.
>
> *ī prasthabhūmiyalli maḷe kaḍime.*
>
> There is little rain on this plateau.

ಪ್ರಸ್ಥ, flat land, plus ಭೂಮಿ, land.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಪ್ರಸ್ಥಭೂಮಿ** (*prasthabhūmi*) is a modern compound built from Sanskrit words: *prastha*, "flat land" (CDIAL 8868), and *bhūmi*, "earth, land" (CDIAL 9557).
