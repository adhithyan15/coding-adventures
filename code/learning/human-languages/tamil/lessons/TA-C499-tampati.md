## You'll want to know: தம்பதி
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C499-MANAM499-04]; assesses=[] -->

**தம்பதி** — *tampati* — "a married couple".

> அந்தத் தம்பதி ஐம்பது வருடமாக ஒன்றாக வாழ்கிறார்கள்.
>
> *antat tampati aimpatu varuṭamāka oṉṟāka vāḻkiṟārkaḷ.*
>
> That couple has lived together for fifty years.

One word for the pair; the verb is plural.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**தம்பதி** (*tampati*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *dampati*, "lord of the house", whose dual names the married pair (Monier-Williams).
