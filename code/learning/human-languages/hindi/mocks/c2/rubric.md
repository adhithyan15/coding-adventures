# Hindi C2 mock rubric

Score either C2 mock against [`task-shapes/c2.json`](../../task-shapes/c2.json).
Reading, listening, writing, and speaking are independent 100-point papers.
Every paper must reach 60/100; compensation across skills is prohibited.

## Reading — 100 points

Specialist argument, public discourse, specialist synthesis, and literary
intertext parts are worth 25 points each. Divide each part equally among its ten
keyed items. Items may require resolving ambiguity, layered and quoted voices,
presupposition, allusion, framing and stance shifts, intertextual relations,
implicit premises, specialist evidence evaluation, and rhetorical or stylistic
effect. Credit the answer best supported by the complete discourse, not one
merely compatible with an isolated sentence.

## Listening — 100 points

Rapid multiparty interaction, extended argument, stylistic narrative, and
culturally dense media are worth 25 points each. Each recording plays once at
165–200 words per minute after an unscored orienting preview. Eight items per
part are equally weighted. Natural overlap, ellipsis, irony, self-repair,
regional features, and changing register may appear, but acoustic obscurity
must not replace comprehension. No transcript, Romanization, dictionary,
translator, or learner-controlled replay is permitted.

## Writing — 100 points

The 220–280 word synthesis/reframing is worth 40 points: multi-source accuracy
10, selection/reframing 8, audience/register 7, organisation/cohesion 6,
vocabulary/grammar 6, orthographic control 3. Reframing must preserve material
qualifications while changing emphasis for the named reader. The 420–520 word
contrasting-genre text is worth 60: conceptual precision and fulfilment 16,
organisation/cohesion 11, genre/register mastery 10, rhetorical flexibility 7,
vocabulary/grammar 11, orthographic control 5. A response outside the range
loses only credit for evidence or control it fails to demonstrate. Romanized,
translated, copied, or memorised model text earns no independent-writing credit.

## Speaking — 100 points

Six-minute synthesis mediation is worth 35: source fidelity/mediation 9,
conceptual precision 7, organisation 6, audience/register 5,
vocabulary/grammar 5, pronunciation/fluency 3. Multiparty negotiation is worth
30: stakeholder mediation 7, interaction/repair 6, register adaptation 5,
negotiated resolution 5, vocabulary/grammar 4, pronunciation/fluency 3. Defence
and second-audience reformulation is worth 35: sustained defence 8,
qualification/precision 7, reformulation 6, interaction/repair 5, register 4,
vocabulary/grammar 3, pronunciation/fluency 2. Score only the learner's own
contribution. Paper keywords made during 15 minutes of preparation are allowed;
a written answer script is not.

## Readiness boundary

These are project-defined C2 targets, not an official CEFR qualification.
Readiness claims require clean timed administrations, calibrated double-scoring
of productive work, qualified human review of Hindi language and culturally
dense material, and recording review. Until then, report an executable target
and observed paper scores, never a proven C2 pass.
