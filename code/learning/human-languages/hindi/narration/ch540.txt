Hindi, chapter 540: Ways and Means.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
तजुर्बा (tajurbā) — experience (gained over time).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a wish (for someone's good), then the Hindi for to resolve.

You'll want to know: तजुर्बा.
तजुर्बा — tajurbā — "experience (gained over time)".
अरुण को इस काम का दस साल का तजुर्बा है। Aruṇ ko is kām kā das sāl kā tajurbā hai. Arun has ten years' experience of this work.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
तजुर्बा (tajurbā) is usually traced to Arabic tajriba, "a trial, a test", through Persian; Hindi uses it for what years of doing something teach you.

What you've built.
One more word for saying what happens if.
