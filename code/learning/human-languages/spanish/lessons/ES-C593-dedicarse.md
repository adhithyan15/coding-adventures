## You'll want to know: dedicarse
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C593-TAREA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**dedicarse** — "to do for a living; to devote oneself".

> ¿A qué te dedicas?
>
> *What do you do for a living?*

*¿A qué te dedicas?* is the usual way to ask about someone's job. Spelling: *me dediqué*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*dedicarse* is a learned borrowing from Latin *dedicare* 'to devote, to dedicate', built on *dicare* 'to proclaim', a relative of *dicere* 'to say' (Lewis & Short).
