## You'll want to know: परिहरति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C374-PRAMADA374-03]; assesses=[] -->

**परिहरति** — *pariharati* — "avoids".

> यदि भवान् क्रोधं परिहरति, तर्हि सुखी भविष्यति।
>
> *yadi bhavān krodhaṃ pariharati, tarhi sukhī bhaviṣyati.*
>
> If you avoid anger, you will be happy.

Past (he …): सः विवादं परिहृतवान्, *saḥ vivādaṃ parihṛtavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**परिहरति** (*pariharati*) is built on *pari*, "around", and the root √hṛ, "to take"; MW gives *pari-hṛta* "shunned, avoided" (MW).

## What you've built
