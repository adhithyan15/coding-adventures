# తిరస్కరించు (tiraskariñcu) — to reject; to turn down

## You'll want to know: తిరస్కరించు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C386-ABHYANTARAM386-05]; assesses=[] -->

**తిరస్కరించు** — *tiraskariñcu* — "to reject; to turn down".

> కంపెనీ నా దరఖాస్తును తిరస్కరించింది.
>
> *kampenī nā darakhāstunu tiraskariñcindi.*
>
> The company rejected my application.

Past (I …): తిరస్కరించాను, *tiraskariñcānu*.

Firm and formal: it goes with applications, offers and ideas.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**తిరస్కరించు** (*tiraskariñcu*) is built on Sanskrit *tiraskāra*, "a placing aside", and also "disdain" (Monier-Williams), with the Telugu verb ending -ఇంచు.

## What you've built

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
