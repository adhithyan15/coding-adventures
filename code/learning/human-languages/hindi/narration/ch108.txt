Hindi, chapter 108: Fear, Anger, Sadness.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
डर (ḍar) — fear.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for weakness, then the Hindi for health.

You'll want to know: डर.
डर (ḍar) — "fear".
मुझे डर (ḍar) लग रहा है — "I am scared", literally "fear is striking me". Feelings take the मुझे frame.

What you've built.
The first feeling.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: ḍar]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: ḍar, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say mujhe ḍar lag rahā hai]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Hindi for weakness, then the Hindi for health, then say ḍar again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does डर (ḍar) mean? ("Fear".) Say it once more.
