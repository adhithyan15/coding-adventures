Persian, chapter 256: To download, To use, To try, To travel, To retire.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
دانلود کردن (dânlod kardan) — to download.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to bite, then the Persian for to chew.

You'll want to know: دانلود کردن.
دانلود کردن — dânlod kardan — "to download".

The word, taken apart.
dânlod kardan pairs dânlod, borrowed from English download, with the Persian verb kardan. kardan, "to do", is an inherited verb, from Middle Persian kardan (EtymDB).

Lesson 5 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: بازنشسته شدن.
بازنشسته شدن — bâzneshaste shodan — "to retire".

The word, taken apart.
bâzneshaste shodan is "to become sat back": bâz, "back", with neshaste, "seated" (from neshastan), and shodan. All are native Persian. shodan, "to become", is an inherited verb; it is usually traced to Middle Persian šudan, "to go".
