## You'll want to know: la fanfare
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C316-CELEBRER-04]; assesses=[] -->

**la fanfare** — "the brass band; a fanfare".

> La fanfare jouait devant la mairie.
>
> *The brass band was playing in front of the town hall.*

*En fanfare* — with a lot of noise.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*fanfare* is usually explained as imitating the sound of trumpets; the origin is not settled (EtymDB lists no parent).
