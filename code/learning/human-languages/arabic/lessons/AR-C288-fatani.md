# فاتني القطار (fātanī al-qiṭār) — I missed the train

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[AR-LEX-C288-SAFAR288-03, AR-LEX-C288-SAFAR288-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "I want to speak to the person in charge". (*Urīdu an
atakallama maʿa al-masʾūl*.) Then ask for a return ticket. (*Tadhkirat
dhahāb wa-iyāb*.)

[PAUSE 1s] Now something has gone wrong. Arabic reports it in a shape English
does not use.

## Grammar Lens: the thing did it
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-GRAMMAR-C288-PROBLEM-PAST-01]; assesses=[AR-LEX-C92-TOWN92-01, AR-LEX-C74-SCHOOL74-01, AR-LEX-C215-SHAY215-03, AR-LEX-C255-ACT255-01, AR-SCRIPT-C288-HAMZA-YAA-01] -->

> **فاتني القطار.** — *fātanī al-qiṭār* — I missed the train.

Word for word it is "the train passed me by". *Fāta* is "it passed, it
slipped by", *-nī* is "me", and the train is the subject, after its verb.
Your own mistake is told as the train's doing.

> **ضاعت حقيبتي.** — *ḍāʿat ḥaqībatī* — My bag is lost.

*Ḍāʿa* is "to get lost", and the bag, feminine, gives the verb its *-at*.
So *ḍāʿat amtiʿatī*, my luggage is lost; *taʾakhkharat aṭ-ṭāʾira*, the plane
is late. Each opens with the past verb and then names the thing.

## Grammar Lens: asking for help
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-GRAMMAR-C288-ASK-HELP-01]; assesses=[AR-GRAMMAR-C286-YAJIBU-01, AR-CONCEPT-C23-AFWAN-01, AR-LEX-C64-VERBS64-02, AR-LEX-C288-SAFAR288-03] -->

> **ماذا يجب أن أعمل؟** — *mādhā yajibu an aʿmala?* — What should I do?

*Mādhā* is "what", before a verb. Open with *ʿafwan*, "excuse me", and
close, if you need to, with *urīdu an atakallama maʿa al-masʾūl*.

- [YOU SAY: "I missed the train" — *fātanī al-qiṭār*]
- [YOU SAY: "My bag is lost" — *ḍāʿat ḥaqībatī*]

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[AR-GRAMMAR-C288-PROBLEM-PAST-01, AR-GRAMMAR-C288-ASK-HELP-01] -->

[PAUSE 3s] "I missed the train"? (***Fātanī al-qiṭār.***) Who is the subject
in it? (**The train**: it passed me by.) Why *ḍāʿat* and not *ḍāʿa*? (**The
bag is feminine.**) "What should I do?" (***Mādhā yajibu an aʿmala?***)
