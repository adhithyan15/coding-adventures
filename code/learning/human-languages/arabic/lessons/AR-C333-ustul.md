## You'll want to know: أسطول
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C333-BAHR333-03]; assesses=[] -->

**أسطول** — *usṭūl* — "a fleet".

> أرسل الملك أسطولا إلى الجزيرة.
>
> *arsala al-malik usṭūlan ilā al-jazīra.*
>
> The king sent a fleet to the island.

Plural: أساطيل, *asāṭīl*.

Also a fleet of buses or lorries.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**أسطول** (*usṭūl*) is usually traced to Greek *stolos*, "an expedition, a fleet".
