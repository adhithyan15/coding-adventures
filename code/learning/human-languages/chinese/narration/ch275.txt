Mandarin Chinese, chapter 275: Saying Sorry.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
dàoqiàn — to apologise.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for handicrafts, then the Mandarin for a sports fan.

You'll want to know: dàoqiàn.
dàoqiàn — "to apologise".
tā dàoqiàn le — He apologised.
Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
dàoqiàn is built from dào and qiàn, both inherited from Middle Chinese, dawX and kheamH (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'a road' and 'a principle'. It gives the second other senses; how the pair came to mean an apology is not settled.

What you've built.
One more word for saying no and asking.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: dàoqiàn]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: dàoqiàn, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: tā dàoqiàn le — He apologised.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Mandarin for handicrafts, then the Mandarin for a sports fan, then say dàoqiàn again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does dàoqiàn mean? ("To apologise".) Say it once more.
