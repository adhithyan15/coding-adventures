# خ (khe) — the letter that opens خدا

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[FA-SCRIPT-C23-HE-JIMI-01, FA-SCRIPT-C23-JIM-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: write ح and ج once each, and name them.

[PAUSE 2s] Now say **خدا** (*khodâ*), "God".
You have read it for a long time. This lesson takes one letter out of it and puts
it in your hand.

## Script you'll notice: خ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-SCRIPT-C23-KHE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**خ** — *khe*, a *kh* scraped at the back of the throat.

It opens **خدا** (*khodâ*). The same head and bowl as **ح**, with **one dot above**.
One skeleton, and the dot decides the sound.

## Writing: خ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[FA-SCRIPT-C23-KHE-01] -->

- **1.** start here: draw the short upper head from left to right
- **2.** without lifting, continue down and around the deep bowl
- **3.** lift once, then place the dot above — and only now lift

**Pen lifts: 1.**

> Stroke order is one attested teaching order; hands and teachers vary.
> Source: Persian Online, How to Write Persian Characters, خ demonstration at 00:49–00:54 (Liberal Arts Instructional Technology Services, Univ. of Texas at Austin).

[PAUSE 1s]
- [YOU LOOK: at **خدا** and point at خ inside it]
- [YOU TRACE: خ three times, saying *kh* as you finish each one]
- [YOU WRITE: ج, then خ, from memory]
- [YOU READ: **خدا** aloud once more]
