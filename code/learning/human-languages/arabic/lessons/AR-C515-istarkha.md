# استرخى (istarkhā) — to relax

## You'll want to know: استرخى
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C515-NAWM515-02]; assesses=[] -->

**استرخى** — *istarkhā* — "to relax".

> استرخت أمي على الأريكة.
>
> *istarkhat ummī ʿalā al-arīka.*
>
> My mother relaxed on the sofa.

Present: يسترخي, *yastarkhī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**استرخى** (*istarkhā*) is built on the native Arabic root *r-kh-w*, "to be slack, loose" (Lane). Form X istarkhā is to let the body go loose.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
