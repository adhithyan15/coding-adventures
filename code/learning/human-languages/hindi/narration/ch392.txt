Hindi, chapter 392: Getting Ready.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
तैयारी (taiyārī) — preparation.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for without a break, then the Hindi for to repeat.

You'll want to know: तैयारी.
तैयारी — taiyārī — "preparation".
अगर तैयारी अच्छी होगी तो परीक्षा से डर नहीं लगेगा। agar taiyārī acchī hogī to parīkṣā se ḍar nahīṃ lagegā. If your preparation is good, you won't be afraid of the exam.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
तैयारी (taiyārī) is usually traced to Arabic tayyār, "ready", which reached Hindi through Persian. The Hindi noun adds the ending -ī.

You'll want to know: आज़माना.
आज़माना — āzmānā — "to try out, to test".
अगर यह दवा काम नहीं करेगी तो डाक्टर दूसरी दवा आज़माएगा। agar yah davā kām nahīṃ karegī to ḍākṭar dūsrī davā āzmāegā. If this medicine doesn't work, the doctor will try another one.
Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने नई दवा आज़माई, usne naī davā āzmāī.

The word, taken apart.
आज़माना (āzmānā) is usually traced to Persian āzmā-, "test". This is the stem of āzmūdan, "to test".

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for to try out, then the Hindi for a method.

You'll want to know: अनुभव.
अनुभव — anubhav — "experience".
अगर तुम्हें अनुभव होगा तो यह नौकरी जल्दी मिलेगी। agar tumhẽ anubhav hogā to yah naukrī jaldī milegī. If you have experience, you'll get this job quickly.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
अनुभव (anubhav) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit anubhava, "perception, experience" (Monier-Williams).
