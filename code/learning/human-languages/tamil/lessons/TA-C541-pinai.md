## You'll want to know: பிணை
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C541-KAPPEEDU541-05]; assesses=[] -->

**பிணை** — *piṇai* — "bail; a surety".

> நீதிபதி அவருக்குப் பிணை வழங்கினார்.
>
> *nītipati avarukkup piṇai vaḻaṅkiṉār.*
>
> The judge granted him bail.

Also a person who stands surety for a loan. The verb பிணை means "to tie, to link".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**பிணை** (*piṇai*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 4160 gives பிணை (*piṇai*), "to link, to tie, to fasten", and the noun for being bound together; bail and surety are later senses.
