## You'll want to know: بہانہ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C527-MASRUF527-04]; assesses=[] -->

**بہانہ** — *bahānā* — "an excuse".

> اگر وہ نہیں آیا تو کوئی بہانہ بنائے گا۔
>
> *agar voh nahīṅ āyā to koī bahānā banāe gā.*
>
> If he doesn't come, he will make up some excuse.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**بہانہ** (*bahānā*) is usually traced to Persian *bahāna*, "a pretext, an excuse".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
