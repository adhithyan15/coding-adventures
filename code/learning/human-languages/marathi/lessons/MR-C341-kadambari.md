## You'll want to know: कादंबरी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C341-KADAMBARI341-01]; assesses=[] -->

**कादंबरी** — *kādambarī* — "a novel".

> मी सुट्टीत एक मोठी कादंबरी वाचली.
>
> *mī suṭṭīt ek moṭhī kādambarī vāclī.*
>
> I read a long novel during the holidays.

Feminine.

कादंबरीकार, a novelist.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**कादंबरी** (*kādambarī*) comes from Sanskrit *Kādambarī*, the title of a celebrated prose romance (Monier-Williams); Marathi made the title the word for every novel.
