## You'll want to know: اکیلا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C450-MISS450-02]; assesses=[] -->

**اکیلا** — *akelā* — "on one's own".

> وہ اکیلا اسکول جاتا ہے۔
>
> *voh akelā iskūl jātā hai.*
>
> He goes to school on his own.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**اکیلا** (*akelā*) is inherited from *ēkkala*, "alone", a form CDIAL reconstructs from the word for "one" (CDIAL 2506).
