## You'll want to know: премия
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C362-POVYSHENIYE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**премия** (*prémiya*) — "a bonus; a prize".

> В конце года все получили премию.
>
> *V kontsé góda vse poluchíli prémiyu.*
>
> At the end of the year everyone got a bonus.

Feminine. A bonus at work, or a prize: *литературная премия*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**премия** is usually traced, through German *Prämie*, to Latin *praemium* "a reward, a prize" (Lewis & Short), built from *prae* "before" and *emere* "to take, to buy".
