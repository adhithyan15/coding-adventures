## You'll want to know: विश्वसनीयः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C462-SATHA462-04]; assesses=[] -->

**विश्वसनीयः** — *viśvasanīyaḥ* — "trustworthy; reliable".

> विश्वसनीयः वणिक् उचितं मूल्यं वदति।
>
> *viśvasanīyaḥ vaṇik ucitaṃ mūlyaṃ vadati.*
>
> A trustworthy merchant names a fair price.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**विश्वसनीयः** (*viśvasanīyaḥ*) is built on *vi-√śvas*, "to trust", the root of **विश्वसिति**, with the ending *-anīya*, "to be ...-ed"; MW gives "to be trusted or relied on, reliable, trustworthy" (MW).
