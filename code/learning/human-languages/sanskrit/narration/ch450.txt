Sanskrit, chapter 450: Sadness and Tears.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
विषादः (viṣādaḥ) — dejection.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for pleases, then the Sanskrit for a thrill.

You'll want to know: विषादः.
विषादः — viṣādaḥ — "dejection".
परीक्षायाः परिणामेन छात्रस्य विषादः अभवत्। parīkṣāyāḥ pariṇāmena chātrasya viṣādaḥ abhavat. The student was dejected by the exam result.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
विषादः (viṣādaḥ) is built from vi, "apart", and the root √sad, "to sit, to sink": a sinking of the heart; MW gives "dejection, depression, despondency" (MW).

बालिकायाः मुखे बाष्पः अस्ति। bālikāyāḥ mukhe bāṣpaḥ asti. There are tears on the girl's face.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
बाष्पः (bāṣpaḥ) MW records bāṣpa, also written vāṣpa, "a tear, tears" and "steam, vapour"; it lives on as Hindi bhāp, "steam" (CDIAL 9223); MW gives no derivation, so its origin is uncertain (MW).

निःश्वासः (niḥśvāsaḥ) is built from niḥ, "out", and the root √śvas, "to breathe", the root of श्वासः: a breath let out (MW).
