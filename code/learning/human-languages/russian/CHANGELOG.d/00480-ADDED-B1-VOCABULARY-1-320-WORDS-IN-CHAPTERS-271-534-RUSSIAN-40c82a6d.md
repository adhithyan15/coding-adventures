## Added — B1 vocabulary: 1,320 words in chapters 271–534; Russian attains B1

- **264 new chapters (271–534) teach 1,320 new headwords, one word per lesson.** 456 of them are verbs, which clears the 250-verb target the track was 58 short of. Each lesson gives:
  - the word in Cyrillic with its stressed romanization;
  - a short example in Cyrillic, its romanization word for word, and its translation;
  - a note with the gender or irregular forms, and for a verb its aspect partner and the case it governs;
  - a "The word, taken apart" origin.

  Each word is practised again in the next two lessons. A review lesson closes every eight chapters, and each tranche's last chapter gets one more short review. Every chapter sits on one of the five B1 spine nodes:
  - 271–336, SPINE-NARRATE-EVENTS (330 words): time and sequence, news and reports, crime and the courts, accidents and emergencies, life events, celebrations, stories and history, and cooking step by step.
  - 337–402, SPINE-GIVE-REASONS and SPINE-EXPRESS-CONDITION (330 words): opinion and argument, cause and consequence, work, society, conditions and plans, study and exams, rules and the law, money, and technology. The connectors (*однако*, *зато*, *поскольку*, *благодаря*, *несмотря на*, *при условии*, *в случае*, *даже если* and others) each say what they take.
  - 403–468, SPINE-HANDLE-TRAVEL (330 words): the road and the car, stations, airports and ports, lodging and complaints, finding the way, the town and its services, shops, nature, and weather.
  - 469–534, SPINE-DESCRIBE-EXPERIENCE (330 words): feelings and character, health and the body, family and relationships, hobbies, sport, music and the arts, reading and the screen, and life at home.
- **Russian now teaches 2,500+ headwords at or below B1**, past the level gate's target. With the B1 core (chapters 261–270), Russian attains B1.
- **Sources.** Every origin was checked against EtymDB 2.1. Its Russian coverage is thin, so about 150 words that it lists without a source rest on transparent Russian word-building or say the origin is usually traced or not settled. Church Slavonic forms are told apart from native ones, and borrowings say where they came from. An independent review of each tranche fixed errors in 182 of the 1,320 words (13.8%), mostly etymology claims and some stress marks.
- **No forward references and no hidden duplicates.** Every candidate headword was screened against every earlier lesson's text, and duplicates with ё written as е were ruled out. Russian's forward-reference count stays at its pin. Root slugs follow the track's `slavic-<root>` and `pie-<root>` shape.
