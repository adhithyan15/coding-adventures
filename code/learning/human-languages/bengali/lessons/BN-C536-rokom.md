## You'll want to know: রকম
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C536-ROKOM536-03]; assesses=[] -->

**রকম** — *rôkôm* — "a kind; a sort".

> দোকানে তিন রকম চা আছে।
>
> *Dokāne tin rôkôm chā āchhe.*
>
> The shop has three kinds of tea.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**রকম** (*rôkôm*) is usually traced to Arabic *raqm*, "a mark, a figure", through Persian.
