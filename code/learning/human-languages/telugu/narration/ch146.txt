Telugu, chapter 146: To reach, To put on, To call, To count, To wash.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
చేరు (cēru) — to reach, to join.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to jump, then the Telugu for to get down.

You'll want to know: చేరు.
చేరు — cēru — "to reach, to join".
