# билет туда и обратно (bilét tudá i obrátno) — a return ticket

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[RU-LEX-C54-WORDS-04, RU-LEX-C79-TRAVEL-02, RU-LEX-C110-THINGS110-02, RU-GRAMMAR-K-DATIVE-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "a ticket", "a train" and "the till". (*Bilét. Póyezd.
Kássa.*) Then ask the way to the till. (*Kak proytí k kásse?*)

## You'll want to know: туда и обратно
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-TUDA-I-OBRATNO-01]; assesses=[RU-LEX-C184-MESTO184-03, RU-LEX-ODIN-01, RU-LEX-POZHALUYSTA] -->

**туда и обратно** — *tudá i obrátno* — "there and back". At the till it
is a return ticket:

> **— Один билет до Москвы, пожалуйста. — Туда и обратно?** — *odín bilét
> do Moskvý, pozhálusta. tudá i obrátno?* — One ticket to Moscow, please.
> Return?
> **— Да, туда и обратно.** — *da, tudá i obrátno* — Yes, a return.

A single is **в одну сторону** — *v odnú stóronu* — "in one direction".

## Grammar Lens: до a place, на a train
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-GRAMMAR-BILET-DO-NA-01]; assesses=[RU-GRAMMAR-DO-GENITIVE, RU-GRAMMAR-DO-NA-ROUTE-01, RU-LEX-C79-TRAVEL-02, RU-LEX-C79-TRAVEL-01] -->

A ticket goes **до** the place, as far as it, and the place changes as it
did after *до* in the street: *светофор*, *до светофора*; *Москва*, **до
Москвы**.

A ticket is **на** the train or the bus, and *поезд* and *автобус* keep
their plain form: **билет на поезд**, **билет на автобус** — *bilét na
póyezd, bilét na avtóbus*. After *два*, *билет* adds **-а**: **два
билета**.

## The exchange
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[RU-LEX-SKOLKO-01, RU-LEX-C204-ACT204-04, RU-LEX-C76-NUM2-04, RU-LEX-C231-RABOTA231-02] -->

> **— Сколько стоит билет? — Тысяча рублей.**

*Skólko stóit bilét?* — "How much is the ticket?" *Týsyacha rubléy* — "A
thousand roubles." After *тысяча*, *рубль* takes the counting form
**рублей**.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[RU-LEX-C184-MESTO184-03] -->

**обратно** is the adverb of *обратный*, "reverse, return". EtymDB 2.1
has no entry for *обратный*, but takes the verb *обратить* back to
Proto-Slavic *\*ob(v)ortiti*. By the usual account that is *об-*,
"around", with a root of turning, kin to **вертеть**, "to turn", from
Proto-Indo-European *\*wert-*: a ticket that turns round. **туда** is "to that place", on the
same pointing stem as *тот*.
