Persian, chapter 114: Ruler, Board, Class, Exam, Teacher.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
خط کش (khatkesh) — a ruler.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for an envelope, then the Persian for a pencil sharpener.

You'll want to know: خط کش.
خط کش — khatkesh — "a ruler".

The word, taken apart.
khatkesh is khat, "line", with -kesh, "drawing" (from keshidan, "to draw"): the line-drawer. khat is borrowed from Arabic ḫaṭṭ, "line, writing" (root ḫ-ṭ-ṭ, Lane); kesh is native Persian.
