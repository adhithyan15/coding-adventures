## You'll want to know: ٹھونکنا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C497-TAKHTA497-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ٹھونکنا** — *ṭhoṅknā* — "to knock in; to hammer (a nail)".

> اس نے ہتھوڑے سے دیوار میں کیل ٹھونکی۔
>
> *us ne hathauṛe se dīvār meṅ kīl ṭhoṅkī.*
>
> He hammered a nail into the wall.

Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے تختے میں کیل ٹھونکی, *us ne takhte meṅ kīl ṭhoṅkī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ٹھونکنا** (*ṭhoṅknā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *ṭhoknā*, "to knock, to make firm", under *\*ṭhōkk*, "knock", a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 5513).
