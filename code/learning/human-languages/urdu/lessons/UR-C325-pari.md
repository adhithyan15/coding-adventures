## You'll want to know: پری
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C325-DEV325-02]; assesses=[] -->

**پری** — *parī* — "a fairy".

> پری نے بچے کو ایک تحفہ دیا۔
>
> *parī ne bachche ko ek tohfā diyā.*
>
> The fairy gave the child a gift.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**پری** (*parī*) is usually traced to Persian *parī*, "a fairy". Urdu calls a beautiful face **پری جیسا**.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
