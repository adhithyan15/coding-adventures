## You'll want to know: پژوهش
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C402-TAHQIQ-02]; assesses=[] -->

**پژوهش** (*pazhuhesh*) — "scholarly research, a study".

> این پژوهش پنج سال طول کشید.
>
> *in pazhuhesh panj sâl tul keshid.*
>
> This study took five years.

A native, formal near-synonym of *tahqiq*. *Pazhuheshgar* — a researcher.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**پژوهش** is native Persian, built on *pazhuh-*, the stem of *pazhuhidan* "to search, to inquire", with the noun suffix *-esh*.
