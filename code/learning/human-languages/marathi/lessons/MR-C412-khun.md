## You'll want to know: खूण
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C412-DISHA412-03]; assesses=[] -->

**खूण** — *khūṇ* — "a mark; a landmark".

> मंदिर ही आमच्या गल्लीची खूण आहे.
>
> *mandir hī āmcyā gallīcī khūṇ āhe.*
>
> The temple is the landmark of our lane.

Feminine.

खूण करणे, to make a mark or a sign with the hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**खूण** (*khūṇ*) is a Marathi word for a mark, a sign or a landmark; its origin is not settled.
