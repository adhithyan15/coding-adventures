## You'll want to know: das Schach
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C501-BRETTSPIEL-02]; assesses=[] -->

**das Schach** — "chess".

> Mein Sohn spielt jeden Dienstag Schach im Verein.
>
> *My son plays chess at the club every Tuesday.*

No plural. *Schach!* — check! *Schachmatt* — checkmate.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Schach* came through Medieval Latin *scaccus* from Arabic, which took it from Persian *shāh* "king" (EtymDB): the game is named after its key piece.
