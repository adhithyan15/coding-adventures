## You'll want to know: टोकन
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C433-QATAR433-02]; assesses=[] -->

**टोकन** — *ṭokan* — "a token".

> डाकघर में पहले टोकन लीजिए।
>
> *ḍākghar meṁ pahle ṭokan lījie.*
>
> At the post office, take a token first.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**टोकन** (*ṭokan*) is borrowed from English *token*, the numbered slip that keeps your place in a queue.
