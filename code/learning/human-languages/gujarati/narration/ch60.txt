Gujarati, chapter 60: Salt, Chilli and Something Sweet.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
મરચું (marcũ) — a chilli.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for potatoes, then the Gujarati for a tomato.

You'll want to know: મરચું.
મરચું (marcũ) — "a chilli".

The word, taken apart.
મરચું (marcũ) is a tadbhava. CDIAL lists it under maricca, a variant of Sanskrit marīca, "peppercorn", and reports that the Sanskrit word came from Austroasiatic (CDIAL 9875). The name moved from black pepper to the chilli.

The word, taken apart.
પૂરી (pūrī) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from pūrikā, "cake" (CDIAL 8331).
