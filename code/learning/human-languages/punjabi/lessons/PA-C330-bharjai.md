## You'll want to know: ਭਰਜਾਈ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C330-SPOUSE330-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ਭਰਜਾਈ** — *bharjāī* — "a brother's wife".

> ਮੇਰੀ ਭਰਜਾਈ ਸਕੂਲ ਵਿੱਚ ਬੱਚਿਆਂ ਨੂੰ ਪੰਜਾਬੀ ਸਿਖਾਉਂਦੀ ਹੈ।
>
> *merī bharjāī sakūl vich bachchiāṁ nū̃ panjābī sikhāundī hai.*
>
> My brother's wife teaches Punjabi to children at school.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਭਰਜਾਈ** (*bharjāī*) is inherited from Sanskrit *bhrāturjāyā*, "a brother's wife"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *bharjāī*, "brother's wife", under it (CDIAL 9660).
