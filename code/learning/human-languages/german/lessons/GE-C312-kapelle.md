## You'll want to know: die Kapelle
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C312-VOLKSFEST-05]; assesses=[] -->

**die Kapelle** — "the band (of musicians); the chapel".

> Eine Kapelle spielte alte Lieder.
>
> *A band played old songs.*

Plural: *die Kapellen*. Also a small church, a chapel.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Kapelle* comes from Middle High German *kappelle* (EtymDB), from Medieval Latin *capella* "a little cloak", then, as the story is usually told, the chapel where a famous cloak was kept, then its musicians.
