## You'll want to know: xiànr
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C341-MOONCAKE341-03]; assesses=[] -->

*xiànr* — "filling".

> *zhège yuèbǐng de xiànr shì dòushā* — This mooncake has a bean-paste filling.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*xiànr* No dictionary we use records an older form, so its history is not settled. The ending r is the 儿 of 一会儿.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
