## You'll want to know: ਪਾਸਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C263-TURN263-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ਪਾਸਾ** — *pāsā* — "a side".

> ਸਕੂਲ ਸੜਕ ਦੇ ਦੂਜੇ ਪਾਸੇ ਹੈ।
>
> *sakūl saṛak de dūje pāse hai.*
>
> The school is on the other side of the road.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਪਾਸਾ** (*pāsā*) is inherited from Sanskrit *pārśva*, "region of the ribs, side"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *pāssā*, "side, direction", under it (CDIAL 8118).
