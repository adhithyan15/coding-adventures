# ఒడ్డు (oḍḍu) — a riverbank; a shore

## You'll want to know: ఒడ్డు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C453-TEPPA453-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ఒడ్డు** — *oḍḍu* — "a riverbank; a shore".

> పిల్లలు నది ఒడ్డున ఆడుకుంటున్నారు.
>
> *pillalu nadi oḍḍuna āḍukuṇṭunnāru.*
>
> The children are playing on the riverbank.

ఒడ్డున, on the bank, is the usual form. The bank of a pond, a river or a sea.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ఒడ్డు** (*oḍḍu*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 965 lists Telugu *oḍḍu*, "a bank", with Kannada *oḍḍu*, "a bank, a causeway".
