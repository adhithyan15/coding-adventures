## You'll want to know: مسخره کردن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C489-MANNERS-05]; assesses=[] -->

**مسخره کردن** (*maskhare kardan*) — "to make fun of, to mock".

> او لباس من را مسخره کرد.
>
> *u lebâs-e man râ maskhare kard.*
>
> He made fun of my clothes.

*maskhare* 'ridiculous; a laughing stock' + *kardan*; present stem *kon-*. The person mocked takes *râ*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*maskhare* comes from Arabic *masḫara*, 'buffoon, anything ridiculous' (EtymDB). *kardan*, 'to do', is inherited Persian.
