## You'll want to know: 球赛
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C500-MATCH500-02]; assesses=[] -->

**球赛** — *qiúsài* — "a ball game (a football or basketball match)".

> **这是我看过的最好的一场球赛** *zhè shì wǒ kàn guo de zuì hǎo de yì chǎng qiúsài* — This is the best match I've ever seen.

The new character, in pieces: 赛 — fourteen strokes in three pieces: the roof of 家 *jiā* on top, three strokes; in the middle seven strokes, two short bars, two verticals, a long bar and two strokes spreading left and right; at the foot four strokes. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**球赛** (*qiúsài*) is built from 球 'a ball' and 赛 'a contest', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *gjuw* and *sojH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'to repay'; the sense 'a contest' is not given there.
