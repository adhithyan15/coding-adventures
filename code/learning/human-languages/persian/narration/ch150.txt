Lesson 2 of 5.
پاشنه (pâshne) — a heel.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to expect, then the Persian for the body.

You'll want to know: پاشنه.
پاشنه — pâshne — "a heel".

The word, taken apart.
pâshne is a native Persian word, inherited rather than borrowed; it is usually traced to Middle Persian pāšnag, "heel". EtymDB takes it back to an Indo-Iranian word for "heel", the word behind Sanskrit pārṣṇi.

Lesson 5 of 5.
قرص (qors) — a pill, a tablet.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for the throat, then the Persian for a wound.

You'll want to know: قرص.
قرص — qors — "a pill, a tablet".

The word, taken apart.
qors is borrowed from Arabic qurṣ, "disc, round flat cake". Its Arabic root is q-r-ṣ (Lane). Persian uses it for a round tablet.
