## Writing

1. Accept any six relevant, comprehensible entries in Devanagari. The values are
   personal and need not match the reading or listening names.
2. Award content for identity, city, one language, meeting place, and meeting
   time. A possible response is:

   **नमस्ते रीमा। मेरा नाम अमन है और मैं दिल्ली से हूँ। मैं हिंदी और थोड़ी
   अंग्रेज़ी बोलता हूँ। क्या हम कल पाँच बजे स्कूल के बाहर मिलें? फिर मिलेंगे।**

This example is for scorers only. Do not show it to the learner or require its
wording.

## Speaking

No single script is required. The interview must answer all five prompts; the
description must sustain about one minute on one coherent familiar topic; the
role-play must include greeting, drink request, price question, and polite
close. Apply the rubric for relevance, interaction, Hindi control, and
intelligibility.
