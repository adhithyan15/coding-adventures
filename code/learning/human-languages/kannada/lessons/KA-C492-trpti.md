## You'll want to know: ತೃಪ್ತಿ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C492-HARSHA492-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ತೃಪ್ತಿ** — *tṛpti* — "satisfaction; contentment".

> ಒಳ್ಳೆಯ ಊಟದ ನಂತರ ಅಜ್ಜನಿಗೆ ತೃಪ್ತಿ ಆಯಿತು.
>
> *oḷḷeya ūṭada nantara ajjanige tṛpti āyitu.*
>
> After a good meal, grandfather felt satisfied.

ತೃಪ್ತಿ ಆಗು, to feel satisfied; its opposite is ಅತೃಪ್ತಿ.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ತೃಪ್ತಿ** (*tṛpti*) is borrowed whole from Sanskrit *tṛpti*, "satisfaction, contentment" (Monier-Williams).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
