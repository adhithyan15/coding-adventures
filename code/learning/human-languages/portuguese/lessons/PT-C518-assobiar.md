## You'll want to know: assobiar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C518-CORO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**assobiar** — "to whistle".

> O carteiro assobia a mesma canção todas as manhãs.
>
> *The postman whistles the same song every morning.*

A whistle with the mouth is *um assobio*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*assobiar* is usually traced to Latin *sībilāre* "to hiss, to whistle" (Lewis & Short).
