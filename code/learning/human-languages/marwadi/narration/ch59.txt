Lesson 2 of 5.
केळो (keḷo) — a banana.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for rice, then the Marwadi for an apple.

You'll want to know: केळो.
केळो — keḷo — "a banana".

The word, taken apart.
केळो (keḷo) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from kadala, "the banana plant". CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry (CDIAL 2712); the match is through Hindi kelā and Gujarati keḷũ. CDIAL takes the Sanskrit word itself from an Austroasiatic language.

आलू (ālū) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from ālu, "esculent root". CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry (CDIAL 1388); the match is through Hindi ālū. The potato took the name of an older edible root.
