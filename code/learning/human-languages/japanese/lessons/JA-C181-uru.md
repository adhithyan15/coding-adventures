## You'll want to know: うる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C181-SHOP181-04]; assesses=[] -->

**うる** — *uru* — "to sell".

> **あの みせは やさいを うります。** — *ano mise wa yasai o urimasu* — "That shop sells vegetables."

Polite **うります**, *urimasu*; a godan verb.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**うる** (*uru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
