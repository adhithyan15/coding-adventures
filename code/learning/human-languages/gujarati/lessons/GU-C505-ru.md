## You'll want to know: રૂ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C505-RENTIYO505-02]; assesses=[] -->

**રૂ** — *rū* — "raw cotton (for spinning)".

> મારી દાદી રૂમાંથી દોરો બનાવતી હતી.
>
> *mārī dādī rūmā̃thī doro banāvtī hatī.*
>
> My grandmother used to make thread from cotton.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**રૂ** (*rū*) comes from *\*rū-a-*, "cotton", a form CDIAL reconstructs, and CDIAL lists the Gujarati word as carded cotton (CDIAL 10798).
