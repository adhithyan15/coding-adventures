## You'll want to know: फेरफटका
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C411-SAFAR411-03]; assesses=[] -->

**फेरफटका** — *pherphaṭkā* — "a stroll".

> जेवणानंतर आम्ही बागेत फेरफटका मारला.
>
> *jevṇānantar āmhī bāget pherphaṭkā mārlā.*
>
> After dinner we took a stroll in the garden.

Masculine.

फेरफटका मारणे, to take a stroll.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**फेरफटका** (*pherphaṭkā*) is an echo compound built on **फेर**, "a turn, a round", from the family of *\*phirati*, "turns" (CDIAL 9078), with *phaṭkā* added for rhythm.
