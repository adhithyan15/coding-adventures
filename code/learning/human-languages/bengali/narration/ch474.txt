Bengali, chapter 474: Long Illnesses.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
হাঁপানি (hā̃pāni) — asthma.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for an epidemic, then the Bengali for to be struck down.

You'll want to know: হাঁপানি.
হাঁপানি — hā̃pāni — "asthma".
শীতে দাদুর হাঁপানি বাড়ে। shīte dādur hā̃pāni bāṛe. Grandfather's asthma gets worse in winter.

The word, taken apart.
হাঁপানি (hā̃pāni) is the noun of hā̃pāno, "to pant", which CDIAL lists for Bengali under a reconstructed root for panting (CDIAL 13973): the panting illness.

রক্তচাপ — rôktôchāp — "blood pressure".
ডাক্তার বাবার রক্তচাপ মাপলেন। ḍāktār bābār rôktôchāp māplen. The doctor measured my father's blood pressure.

The word, taken apart.
রক্তচাপ (rôktôchāp) puts রক্ত (taught), blood, with chāp, "pressure", from the family CDIAL lists for Bengali under a reconstructed capp, "press" (CDIAL 4674).
