## You'll want to know: фара
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C406-BAGAZHNIK-03]; assesses=[] -->

**фара** (*fára*) — "a headlight".

> Вечером водитель включил фары.
>
> *Vécherom vodítel vklyuchíl fáry.*
>
> In the evening the driver switched on the headlights.

Usually plural: *фары*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*фара* was borrowed from French *phare* 'a lighthouse; a headlight' (EtymDB). *Phare* goes back to Latin *pharus* 'a lighthouse', from Greek *pháros* (EtymDB).
