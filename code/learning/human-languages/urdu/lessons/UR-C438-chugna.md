## You'll want to know: چگنا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C438-CHONCH438-04]; assesses=[] -->

**چگنا** — *chugnā* — "to peck (at); to pick up (grain)".

> کبوتر زمین سے چاول چگتے ہیں۔
>
> *kabūtar zamīn se chāval chugte haiṅ.*
>
> The pigeons peck rice off the ground.

Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے چاول چگے, *us ne chāval chuge*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**چگنا** (*chugnā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *cugnā*, "to peck, to pick up", under *cugyati*, a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 4852).
