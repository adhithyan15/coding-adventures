## You'll want to know: धुसफुसणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C502-DURAVA502-04]; assesses=[] -->

**धुसफुसणे** — *dhusphusṇe* — "to seethe; to fume quietly".

> भावाने ऐकलं नाही म्हणून ती धुसफुसत होती.
>
> *bhāvāne aiklaṁ nāhī mhaṇūn tī dhusphusat hotī.*
>
> She was fuming because her brother did not listen.

Past (he …): तो धुसफुसला, *to dhusphuslā*.

Anger kept under the breath; धुसफूस, simmering discontent.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**धुसफुसणे** (*dhusphusṇe*) is a Marathi verb whose origin is not settled.
