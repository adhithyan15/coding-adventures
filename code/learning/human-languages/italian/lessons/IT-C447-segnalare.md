## You'll want to know: segnalare
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C447-UFFICI-04]; assesses=[] -->

**segnalare** — "to report, to point out".

> Ho segnalato il furto alla polizia.
>
> *I reported the theft to the police.*

*La segnalazione* — a report, a notice.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*segnalare* is built on *segnale* 'signal', a learned borrowing from Late Latin *signāle*, from *signum* 'sign' (Lewis & Short).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
