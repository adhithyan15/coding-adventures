Lesson 1 of 5.
सण (saṇ) — a festival.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for education, then the Marathi for a feeling.

You'll want to know: सण.
सण — saṇ — "a festival".

The word, taken apart.
सण (saṇ) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from kṣaṇa, "moment; festival" (CDIAL 3642). CDIAL glosses the Marathi word "festival, holiday".

मेजवानी — mejvānī — "a feast".

मेजवानी (mejvānī) is a loan, usually traced to Persian mēzbānī, "hospitality", built on mēzbān, "host".
