# -पेक्षा (-pekṣā) — than, and the most

## Warm-up

## You'll want to know: -पेक्षा, 'than'
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-GRAMMAR-C285-PEKSHA-01]; assesses=[MR-LEX-C113-TOWN113-05, MR-LEX-C114-TOWN114-01, MR-LEX-DUDH, MR-LEX-CHAHA, MR-LEX-AVADNE, MR-LEX-GHAR, MR-LEX-LAHAAN] -->

> **गाडी बसपेक्षा जलद आहे.**
>
> *gāḍī baspekṣā jalad āhe.* — "The train is faster than the bus."

**-पेक्षा** goes on the thing you compare against, in its bent form: **घर** → **घरापेक्षा** (*gharāpekṣā*), **चहा** → **चहापेक्षा** (*cahāpekṣā*). The adjective stays as it is; Marathi needs no "-er". For a quantity, add **जास्त** (*jāsta*, "more") or **कमी**, "less": **मला चहापेक्षा दूध जास्त आवडतं** (*malā cahāpekṣā dūdh jāsta āvaḍtaṁ*), "I like milk more than tea".

## You'll want to know: सर्वात, 'the most'
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-GRAMMAR-C285-SARVAT-01]; assesses=[MR-LEX-C135-THINGS135-03, MR-LEX-UNCH, MR-LEX-MOTHA, MR-LEX-MITRA] -->

> **मीरा वर्गात सर्वात उंच आहे.**
>
> *mīrā vargāt sarvāt uñc āhe.* — "Meera is the tallest in the class."

**सर्वात** (*sarvāt*) is "of all": **सर्वात मोठा** (*sarvāt moṭhā*), "the biggest"; **सर्वात जवळ** (*sarvāt javaḷ*), "the nearest".
