Russian, chapter 408: Speeding and fines.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
скорость (skórost) — speed.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to turn round, then the Russian for a hard shoulder.

You'll want to know: скорость.
скорость (skórost) — "speed".
В городе можно ехать со скоростью до шестидесяти километров в час. V górode mózhno yékhat so skórostyu do shestidesyatí kilométrov v chas. In town you can drive at a speed of up to sixty kilometres an hour.
Feminine. На большой скорости — at high speed.

The word, taken apart.
скорость (skórost) is built on скорый 'fast' with the ending -ость. Скорый is inherited from Proto-Slavic skorŭ 'fast' (EtymDB).

Lesson 2 of 5.
превышать (prevyshát) — to exceed.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a hard shoulder, then the Russian for speed.

You'll want to know: превышать.
превышать (prevyshát) — "to exceed".
Мой брат никогда не превышает скорость. Moy brat nikogdá ne prevysháyet skórost. My brother never exceeds the speed limit.
Imperfective; perfective превысить (превышу, превысишь). Formal.

The word, taken apart.
превышать (prevyshát) is built from пре- 'beyond' and -вышать, on выше 'higher', the comparative of высокий 'high'. EtymDB does not list the verb.

Lesson 3 of 5.
инспектор (inspéktor) — an inspector; a traffic police officer.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for speed, then the Russian for to exceed.

You'll want to know: инспектор.
инспектор (inspéktor) — "an inspector; a traffic police officer".
Инспектор попросил водителя выйти из машины. Inspéktor poprosíl vodítelya výyti iz mashíny. The inspector asked the driver to get out of the car.
Masculine; plural инспекторы, in speech also инспектора.

The word, taken apart.
инспектор (inspéktor) is usually traced to Latin inspector 'an examiner', from inspicere 'to look into', built on specere 'to look' (Lewis & Short). EtymDB lists the Russian word without a source, so the route is hedged.

Lesson 4 of 5.
права (pravá) — a driving licence; rights.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to exceed, then the Russian for an inspector.

You'll want to know: права.
права (pravá) — "a driving licence; rights".
Инспектор проверил права и паспорт. Inspéktor provéril pravá i pásport. The inspector checked the driving licence and the passport.
The plural of право (právo) — a right; the stress moves: права (pravá). In full, водительские права.

The word, taken apart.
права (pravá) is the plural of право 'a right', built on правый 'right, straight'. EtymDB lists право beside its Ukrainian cousin právo: a licence is the right to drive.

Lesson 5 of 5.
штрафовать (shtrafovát) — to fine.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for an inspector, then the Russian for a driving licence.

You'll want to know: штрафовать.
штрафовать (shtrafovát) — "to fine".
Здесь часто штрафуют за скорость. Zdes chásto shtrafúyut za skórost. People often get fined for speeding here.
Imperfective; perfective оштрафовать. Present штрафую. Takes the accusative and за: штрафовать (shtrafovát) водителя за скорость (skórost).

The word, taken apart.
штрафовать (shtrafovát) was borrowed from German strafen 'to punish' (EtymDB), alongside the noun штраф 'a fine' from German Strafe.
