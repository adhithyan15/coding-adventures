## You'll want to know: पसारणो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C170-DHOVO170-03]; assesses=[] -->

**पसारणो** — *pasārṇo* — "to spread out to dry; to stretch out".

> हूं छात माथै कपड़ा पसारूं हूं।
>
> *hū̃ chhāt māthai kapṛā pasārū̃ hū̃.*
>
> I spread the clothes out on the roof.

Past (I …): म्हैं कपड़ा पसार्या, *mhaĩ kapṛā pasāryā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पसारणो** (*pasārṇo*) is inherited from Sanskrit *prasārayati*, "stretches out, extends"; CDIAL lists Marwari *pasārṇo*, "to spread out" (CDIAL 8838).
