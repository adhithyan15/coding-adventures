## You'll want to know: la cárcel
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C522-JUEZ-05]; assesses=[] -->

**la cárcel** — "the prison, the jail".

> Pasó tres años en la cárcel.
>
> *He spent three years in prison.*

*La prisión* is a more formal word.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*cárcel* is inherited from Latin *carcer* 'a prison, an enclosure', through old Spanish *carcel* (EtymDB).
