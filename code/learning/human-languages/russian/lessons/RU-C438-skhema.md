## You'll want to know: схема
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C438-UKAZATEL-05]; assesses=[] -->

**схема** (*skhéma*) — "a route map, a diagram".

> У входа в метро висит схема линий.
>
> *U vkhóda v metró visít skhéma líniy.*
>
> There is a map of the lines at the metro entrance.

Feminine. *Схема метро* — the metro map.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*схема* is usually traced to Greek *skhêma* 'a form, a shape' (EtymDB lists the Greek word), probably through Latin *schēma* or German *Schema*; EtymDB does not list the Russian word.
