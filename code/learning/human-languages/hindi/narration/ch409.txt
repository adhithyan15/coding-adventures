Hindi, chapter 409: Airports and Flights.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
एयरपोर्ट (eyarporṭ) — an airport.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a path, then the Hindi for to chase off.

You'll want to know: एयरपोर्ट.
एयरपोर्ट — eyarporṭ — "an airport".
एयरपोर्ट शहर से काफ़ी दूर है। eyarporṭ śahar se kāfī dūr hai. The airport is quite far from the city.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
एयरपोर्ट (eyarporṭ) is borrowed from English airport, the word most people use in speech.

Lesson 2 of 5.
विमान (vimān) — an aircraft (formal).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for to chase off, then the Hindi for an airport.

You'll want to know: विमान.
विमान — vimān — "an aircraft (formal)".
विमान बादलों के ऊपर उड़ रहा था। vimān bādloṁ ke ūpar uṛ rahā thā. The plane was flying above the clouds.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
विमान (vimān) is a tatsama, Sanskrit vimāna, the self-moving chariot of the gods in the old stories and later any vehicle (Monier-Williams); modern Hindi gave it the aircraft.

Lesson 4 of 5.
उड़ान (uṛān) — a flight.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for an aircraft, then the Hindi for a pilot.

You'll want to know: उड़ान.
उड़ान — uṛān — "a flight".
मेरी उड़ान रात दस बजे है। merī uṛān rāt das baje hai. My flight is at ten at night.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
उड़ान (uṛān) is the noun of उड़ना, "to fly", which goes back to Sanskrit uḍḍīyate, "flies up" (CDIAL 1697); CDIAL does not list the noun itself.

Lesson 5 of 5.
आगमन (āgaman) — arrival (formal).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a pilot, then the Hindi for a flight.

You'll want to know: आगमन.
आगमन — āgaman — "arrival (formal)".
विमान का आगमन सुबह सात बजे है। vimān kā āgaman subah sāt baje hai. The plane arrives at seven in the morning.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
आगमन (āgaman) is a tatsama, Sanskrit āgamana, "coming, arriving" (Monier-Williams), from the root gam, "go": the word on station and airport boards.
