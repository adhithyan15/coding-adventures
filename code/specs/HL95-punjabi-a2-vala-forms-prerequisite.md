# HL95 — Punjabi A2 `ਵਾਲਾ`–`ਵਾਲੇ` prerequisite

Issue: #18070

After chapter 194 merged, the confidential Punjabi A2 mock audit against exact
main `90ce0a60dabbe772c4171dd31ca661d7df41c8f8` finds **ਵਾਲੇ** without an
instructional owner. It occurs twice in learner material and twice in
confidential examiner material. It is the highest-priority remaining gap
shared by both surfaces. This spec records counts only; neither confidential
body belongs in the public corpus.

Chapter 195 teaches one deliberately narrow associative-adjective contrast:

- **ਖਿੜਕੀ ਵਾਲਾ ਕਮਰਾ** — a room with a window;
- **ਖਿੜਕੀ ਵਾਲੇ ਕਮਰੇ ਵਿੱਚ** — in the room with a window.

The voice-first opener retrieves only familiar **ਖਿੜਕੀ**, **ਕਮਰਾ**, and
**ਕਮਰੇ ਵਿੱਚ**. One lesson directly owns the bounded associative construction
and masculine singular direct **ਵਾਲਾ**. After an immediate retrieval lesson,
a separate direct owner introduces exact **ਵਾਲੇ** as masculine singular
oblique agreeing with **ਕਮਰੇ** before **ਵਿੱਚ**. Two more contrast/payoff
lessons retrieve both forms. Neither form may leak into authored, generated
book, or narration copy before its owner.

The two new atoms are `PA-GRAMMAR-VALA-ASSOCIATIVE-195` and
`PA-FORM-VALE-MASC-OBLIQUE-195`. The chapter does not teach masculine plural,
honorific, feminine, plural-oblique **ਵਾਲਿਆਂ**, nominal “the one(s),”
occupational or agentive, infinitival-relative, prospective, or idiomatic
uses. It does not claim that this form is selected with every noun or
postposition. Chapter 121's **ਵਾਲੀ**, “earring,” is a separate lexical
homograph and supplies no grammatical feminine form here.

The grammatical boundary follows Gill and Gleason's masculine adjective
direct/oblique paradigm and their discussion of noun or conditional-infinitive
plus **ਵਾਲਾ** as an adjective phrase in *A Reference Grammar of Punjabi*
(1969), pp. 71–72 and 174–175:
<https://pt.learnpunjabi.org/assets/A%20Reference%20Grammar_Final.pdf>.
This source supports the bounded analysis, not a complete **ਵਾਲਾ** paradigm.

This is the eighteenth prerequisite tranche exposed by Punjabi A2 mock
drafting. It does not by itself unblock mock 1 and is not mock-readiness,
attainment, external-equivalence, or human-validation evidence. Authored and
generated ownership remains sharded by language, chapter, lesson, or test
case; no shared aggregate is introduced.
