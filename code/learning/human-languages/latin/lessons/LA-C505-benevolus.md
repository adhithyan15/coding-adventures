## You'll want to know: benevolus, benevola, benevolum
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C505-CLEM-04]; assesses=[] -->

**benevolus, benevola, benevolum** — "kindly, well-wishing".

> Vīcīnus benevolus nōbīs semper adest.
>
> *Our kindly neighbour is always there for us.*

*bene* 'well' + *volō* 'to wish'. Often of a friend's goodwill.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**benevolus** is built on *bene* "well" and *volō* "to wish" (Lewis & Short).
