Italian, chapter 423: Sul traghetto.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
la crociera — the cruise.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for to row, then the Italian for the canoe.

You'll want to know: la crociera.
la crociera — "the cruise".
I miei genitori vanno in crociera. My parents are going on a cruise.
La velocità di crociera — cruising speed.

The word, taken apart.
crociera is built on croce 'cross', which is inherited from Latin crux (Lewis & Short): first a ship crossing back and forth over a stretch of sea.

What you've built.

cabina was borrowed from French cabine (EtymDB); where the French word comes from is less certain.

Lesson 4 of 5.
la prua — the bow (of a ship), the prow.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for the cabin, then the Italian for the sailor.

You'll want to know: la prua.
la prua — "the bow (of a ship), the prow".
Dalla prua si vede il porto. The harbour is in sight from the bow.
A prua — at the front of the ship.

The word, taken apart.
prua is inherited from Latin prōra 'prow' (EtymDB), from Greek prōira, probably through a dialect of the coast.
