## You'll want to know: سکو
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C429-RAHAHAN-02]; assesses=[] -->

**سکو** (*sakku*) — "a platform (at a station); a raised step".

> قطار از سکوی دو حرکت میکند.
>
> *Qatâr az sakku-ye do harekat mikonad.*
>
> The train leaves from platform two.

Also a stone step or ledge in front of a house.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

The origin of *sakku* is not settled, and EtymDB does not list it. It first meant a raised stone seat or ledge, and later a station platform.
