Gujarati, chapter 491: A Wedding in the Family.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
જમાઈ (jamāī) — a son-in-law.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a husband's elder brother's wife, then the Gujarati for married life.

You'll want to know: જમાઈ.
જમાઈ — jamāī — "a son-in-law".
અમારા જમાઈ ઘણા નમ્ર છે. amārā jamāī ghaṇā namra che. Our son-in-law is very polite.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
જમાઈ (jamāī) is inherited from Sanskrit jāmātṛ, "daughter's husband"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati word (CDIAL 5198).
