# అనువాదం (anuvādaṁ) — a translation

## You'll want to know: అనువాదం
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C540-SAHITYAM540-05]; assesses=[] -->

**అనువాదం** — *anuvādaṁ* — "a translation".

> ఆ నవలకు తెలుగు అనువాదం వచ్చింది.
>
> *ā navalaku telugu anuvādaṁ vaccindi.*
>
> A Telugu translation of that novel has come out.

అనువాదం చేయు, to translate.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**అనువాదం** (*anuvādaṁ*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *anuvāda*, "saying again, repetition; translation" (Monier-Williams).
