## You'll want to know: సమాజం
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C408-SAMAJAM408-01]; assesses=[] -->

**సమాజం** — *samājaṁ* — "society; a community".

> సమాజంలో ప్రతి ఒక్కరికీ ఒక బాధ్యత ఉంది.
>
> *samājaṁlō prati okkarikī oka bādhyata undi.*
>
> Everyone has a responsibility in society.

Also a smaller community: మన సమాజం, our community.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**సమాజం** (*samājaṁ*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *samāja*, "a festive gathering" (CDIAL 13207), which Monier-Williams also glosses "an assembly, a society".
