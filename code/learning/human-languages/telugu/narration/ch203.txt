Telugu, chapter 203: To scold, To fight, To grow up, To bloom, To rain down.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
తిట్టు (tiṭṭu) — to scold, to curse.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to register, then the Telugu for to practise.

You'll want to know: తిట్టు.
తిట్టు — tiṭṭu — "to scold, to curse".

The word, taken apart.
తిట్టు (tiṭṭu) is native Dravidian, one word with Tamil tiṭṭu and Kannada tiṭṭu (DEDR 3220). DEDR lists Telugu tiṭṭu, "to curse, scold".

Lesson 4 of 5.

You'll want to know: పూయు.
పూయు — pūyu — "to bloom, to flower".

The word, taken apart.
పూయు (pūyu) is native Dravidian. DEDR 4345 lists pūcu, "to blossom", beside pū, "flower". DEDR compares, with a query, Sanskrit puṣpa, "flower" (CDIAL 8303). DEDR 4352 pūyu, "to smear", is a different word.
