## You'll want to know: ವ್ಯಾಖ್ಯಾನ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C384-GRAHISU384-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ವ್ಯಾಖ್ಯಾನ** — *vyākhyāna* — "an interpretation".

> ಆ ಹಳೆಯ ಹಾಡಿಗೆ ಹಲವು ವ್ಯಾಖ್ಯಾನಗಳು ಇವೆ.
>
> *ā haḷeya hāḍige halavu vyākhyānagaḷu ive.*
>
> That old song has many interpretations.

Plural: ವ್ಯಾಖ್ಯಾನಗಳು, *vyākhyānagaḷu*.

Also a commentary on a text.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ವ್ಯಾಖ್ಯಾನ** (*vyākhyāna*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *vyākhyāna*, "explaining, commenting" (CDIAL 12188).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
