## You'll want to know: ডিগ্রি
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C361-SHIKKHA361-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ডিগ্রি** — *ḍigri* — "a university degree".

> অরুণ বিজ্ঞানে ডিগ্রি পেল।
>
> *ôrun bigyāne ḍigri pelô.*
>
> Arun got a degree in science.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ডিগ্রি** (*ḍigri*) is borrowed from English *degree*, with the English d heard as the Bengali ḍ.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
