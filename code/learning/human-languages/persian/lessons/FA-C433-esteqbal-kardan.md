# استقبال کردن (esteqbâl kardan) — to meet (someone arriving), to welcome

## You'll want to know: استقبال کردن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C433-SAK-04]; assesses=[] -->

**استقبال کردن** (*esteqbâl kardan*) — "to meet (someone arriving), to welcome".

> دوستانم در فرودگاه از من استقبال کردند.
>
> *Dustânam dar forudgâh az man esteqbâl kardand.*
>
> My friends met me at the airport.

The person met takes *az*: *az u esteqbâl kardim*. Also to welcome an idea.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*esteqbâl kardan* is built from *esteqbâl* 'a welcome', borrowed from Arabic *istiqbāl*, which EtymDB derives from *istaqbala* 'to receive, to meet, to face', and *kardan* 'to do'.

## What you've built
