## You'll want to know: रजिस्टर
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C415-RAJISTAR415-02]; assesses=[] -->

**रजिस्टर** — *rajisṭar* — "a register (book)".

> मैनेजर ने रजिस्टर में मेरा नाम लिखा।
>
> *mainejar ne rajisṭar meṁ merā nām likhā.*
>
> The manager wrote my name in the register.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**रजिस्टर** (*rajisṭar*) is borrowed from English *register*, the book at a hotel desk where every guest's name goes.
