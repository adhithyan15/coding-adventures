# ಹೇಗೆ ಹೋಗಬೇಕು? (hēge hōgabēku?) — asking the way

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[KA-LEX-C41-DEIXIS-06, KA-LEX-C03-HEGE-01, KA-GRAMMAR-C312-OBLIGATIVE-01, KA-LEX-C64-JOIN-04] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "where?" (*elli*) and "how?" (*hēge*). Then "I must go early tomorrow." (*nānu nāḷe bēga hōgabēku*.)

## You'll want to know: ಹೇಗೆ ಹೋಗಬೇಕು? and ಎಲ್ಲಿದೆ?
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-GRAMMAR-C313-ASK-WAY-01]; assesses=[KA-ETYMON-C09-KSHAMISI-01, KA-LEX-C100-FIND-03, KA-LEX-C100-FIND-04, KA-LEX-C84-BODY-04] -->

> **ಕ್ಷಮಿಸಿ, ಬಸ್ ನಿಲ್ದಾಣಕ್ಕೆ ಹೇಗೆ ಹೋಗಬೇಕು?**
>
> *kṣamisi, bas nildāṇakke hēge hōgabēku?* — "Excuse me, how do I get to the bus station?"

Word for word it is "how must (one) go to the bus station", with the **-ಬೇಕು** of "must". The place takes the "to" ending: **ನಿಲ್ದಾಣಕ್ಕೆ**, **ಆಸ್ಪತ್ರೆಗೆ**. **ಕ್ಷಮಿಸಿ**, "sorry", opens the question politely, the way English says "excuse me".

> **ಆಸ್ಪತ್ರೆ ಎಲ್ಲಿದೆ?**
>
> *āspatre ellide?* — "Where is the hospital?"

**ಎಲ್ಲಿದೆ** is **ಎಲ್ಲಿ**, "where", run together with **ಇದೆ**, "is".

## You'll want to know: how far
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-GRAMMAR-C313-HOW-FAR-01]; assesses=[KA-LEX-C41-DEIXIS-03, KA-LEX-C107-LOC107-04, KA-LEX-C100-FIND-05, KA-LEX-C58-REPLY-01] -->

> **ಇಲ್ಲಿಂದ ಎಷ್ಟು ದೂರ?**
>
> *illinda eṣṭu dūra?* — "How far is it from here?"

**ಇಲ್ಲಿಂದ** is **ಇಲ್ಲಿ**, "here", with **-ಇಂದ**, "from". **ಎಷ್ಟು ದೂರ** is "how much distance". The answers:

> **ಹತ್ತಿರ ಇದೆ.**
>
> *hattira ide.* — "It's near."

> **ಸ್ವಲ್ಪ ದೂರ ಇದೆ.**
>
> *svalpa dūra ide.* — "It's a little far."
