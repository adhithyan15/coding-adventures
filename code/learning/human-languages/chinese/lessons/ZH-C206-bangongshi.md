## You'll want to know: bàngōngshì
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C206-FLOOR206-04]; assesses=[] -->

*bàngōngshì* — "office".

> *lǎoshī zài bàngōngshì* — The teacher is in the office.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*bàngōngshì* is built from *bàn* 'to handle', 公 'public' and *shì* 'a room', all inherited from Middle Chinese, *beanH*, *kuwng* and *syit* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to provide': a room for public business.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for asking where things are.
