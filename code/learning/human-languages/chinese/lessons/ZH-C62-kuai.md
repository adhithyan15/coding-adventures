## You'll want to know: 快
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C62-FAST62-01]; assesses=[] -->

**快** — *kuài* — "fast; quick".

> **我的手机很快** *wǒ de shǒujī hěn kuài* — My phone is fast.

The new character, in pieces: 快 — on the left the heart piece from the foot of 想, standing upright, and on the right a four-stroke piece read guài. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**快** (*kuài*) is inherited from Middle Chinese *khwaejH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'to one's liking' and 'glad'. The sense 'fast' is not given there, so how it got it is not settled.
