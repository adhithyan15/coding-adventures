## You'll want to know: उभारणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C334-KRANTI334-03]; assesses=[] -->

**उभारणे** — *ubhārṇe* — "to erect; to raise".

> लोकांनी चौकात वीराचा पुतळा उभारला.
>
> *lokānnī caukāt vīrācā putḷā ubhārlā.*
>
> People put up the hero's statue in the square.

Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने उभारलं, *tyāne ubhārlaṁ*.

What is raised agrees: पुतळा उभारला, इमारत उभारली.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**उभारणे** (*ubhārṇe*) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Marathi *ubhārṇe*, 'to raise', under Sanskrit *udbharati*, 'raises' (CDIAL 2038).
