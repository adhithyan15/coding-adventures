## You'll want to know: ইজেল
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C524-TULI524-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ইজেল** — *ijel* — "an easel".

> শিল্পী ইজেলে একটা নতুন ছবি রাখলেন।
>
> *Shilpī ijele ekṭā nôtun chhôbi rākhlen.*
>
> The artist put a new picture on the easel.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ইজেল** (*ijel*) is borrowed from English *easel*.
