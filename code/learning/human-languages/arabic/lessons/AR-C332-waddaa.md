# ودع (waddaʿa) — to bid farewell

## You'll want to know: ودع
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C332-HIJRA332-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ودع** — *waddaʿa* — "to bid farewell".

> ودعت الأم ابنها في المطار.
>
> *waddaʿat al-umm ibnahā fī al-maṭār.*
>
> The mother said goodbye to her son at the airport.

Present: يودع, *yuwaddiʿ*.

A farewell is وداع; مع السلامة is what is said.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ودع** (*waddaʿa*) is built on the native Arabic root *w-d-ʿ* (Lane), "to leave, to let be". It is Form II, "to take leave of"; *wadāʿ* is a farewell.
