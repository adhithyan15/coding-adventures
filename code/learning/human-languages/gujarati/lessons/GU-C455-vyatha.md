## You'll want to know: વ્યથા
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C455-GAMGIN455-02]; assesses=[] -->

**વ્યથા** — *vyathā* — "anguish".

> તેણે પોતાની વ્યથા કોઈને કહી નહીં.
>
> *teṇe potānī vyathā koīne kahī nahī̃.*
>
> She told no one about her anguish.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**વ્યથા** (*vyathā*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *vyathā*, "agitation, uneasiness, pain, anguish", as Monier-Williams records it.
