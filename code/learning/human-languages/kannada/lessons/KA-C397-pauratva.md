## You'll want to know: ಪೌರತ್ವ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C397-NAGARIKA397-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ಪೌರತ್ವ** — *pauratva* — "citizenship".

> ಅವಳಿಗೆ ಈ ದೇಶದ ಪೌರತ್ವ ಸಿಕ್ಕಿತು.
>
> *avaḷige ī dēśada pauratva sikkitu.*
>
> She got the citizenship of this country.

ಪೌರ (citizen) + -ತ್ವ (-ship).

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಪೌರತ್ವ** (*pauratva*) is built on Sanskrit *paura*, "a citizen" (CDIAL 8419), with -*tva*, "-ship".
