## You'll want to know: la maggioranza
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C370-MAGGIORANZA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**la maggioranza** — "the majority".

> La maggioranza degli studenti è d'accordo.
>
> *The majority of the students agree.*

*La maggioranza* of parliament is the governing side.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*maggioranza* is built on *maggiore* "greater", inherited from Latin *māior* "greater" (Lewis & Short), the comparative of *magnus* "great".
