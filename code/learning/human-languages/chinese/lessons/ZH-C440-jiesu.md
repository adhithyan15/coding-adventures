## You'll want to know: jièsù
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C440-HILLS440-05]; assesses=[] -->

*jièsù* — "to lodge with a local family; to stay the night at someone's home".

> *zhǎo bu dào jiǔdiàn de shíhou wǒmen zài cūnmín jiā jièsù* — When we can't find a hotel, we stay the night with a village family.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*jièsù* is built from *jiè* 'to borrow' and *sù* 'to stay the night', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *tsjaeH* and *sjuwk* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to borrow' and the second 'a lodging'.
