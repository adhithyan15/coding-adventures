Portuguese, chapter 63: Lion, Monkey, Bee, Ant, Elephant.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
o leão — a lion.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for a sheep, then the Portuguese for a goat.

You'll want to know: o leão.
o leão — "a lion".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: o leão]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: o leão, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say o leão]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Portuguese for a sheep, then the Portuguese for a goat, then say o leão again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does o leão mean? ("A lion".) Say it once more.
