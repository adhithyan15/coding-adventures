# ತೊಯ್ಯು (toyyu) — to get soaked; to get drenched

## You'll want to know: ತೊಯ್ಯು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C485-MUNGARU485-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ತೊಯ್ಯು** — *toyyu* — "to get soaked; to get drenched".

> ಕೊಡೆ ಇಲ್ಲದೆ ನಾನು ಪೂರ್ತಿ ತೊಯ್ದೆ.
>
> *koḍe illade nānu pūrti toyde.*
>
> Without an umbrella I got soaked through.

Past (I …): ತೊಯ್ದೆ, *toyde*.

ಒದ್ದೆ is wet; ತೊಯ್ಯು is to become wet through.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ತೊಯ್ಯು** (*toyyu*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 3555 lists Kannada *tuy*, *toy*, *toyyu*, "to be or become wet or moist".
