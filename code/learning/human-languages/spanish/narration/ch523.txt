Spanish, chapter 523: El preso.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
el preso — the prisoner.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for to sentence, then the Spanish for the prison.

You'll want to know: el preso.
el preso — "the prisoner".
El preso salió de la cárcel ayer. The prisoner came out of prison yesterday.
A woman is la presa. As an adjective: Está preso — he is in prison.

The word, taken apart.
preso is inherited from Latin prēnsus, a shorter form of prehēnsus 'seized' (EtymDB), the participle of prehendere 'to take hold of'. A prisoner is a person who has been seized.

Lesson 3 of 5.
confesar — to confess.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for the prisoner, then the Spanish for to accuse.

You'll want to know: confesar.
confesar — "to confess".
Al final confesó la verdad. In the end he confessed the truth.
Stem change: confieso, confiesas.

The word, taken apart.
confesar is usually traced to a spoken Latin verb made from cōnfessus, the participle of cōnfitērī 'to acknowledge, to confess' (Lewis & Short), from fatērī 'to admit'.

You'll want to know: liberar.
liberar — "to free, to release".
Liberaron a los presos el lunes. The prisoners were released on Monday.
Liberar a alguien — to set someone free.

The word, taken apart.
liberar is a learned borrowing from Latin līberāre 'to set free' (EtymDB), built on līber 'free'. It is not the liber 'bark, book' behind libro.
