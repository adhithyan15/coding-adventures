## You'll want to know: पाजणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C499-BAL499-05]; assesses=[] -->

**पाजणे** — *pājṇe* — "to give to drink".

> आईने बाळाला दूध पाजलं.
>
> *āīne bāḷālā dūdh pājlaṁ.*
>
> The mother fed the baby milk.

Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने बाळाला दूध पाजलं, *tyāne bāḷālā dūdh pājlaṁ*.

The one given the drink takes -ला: बाळाला पाजणे.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पाजणे** (*pājṇe*) is a tadbhava, inherited from Sanskrit *pāyayati*, "gives to drink"; CDIAL lists Marathi *pājṇẽ* (CDIAL 8098).
