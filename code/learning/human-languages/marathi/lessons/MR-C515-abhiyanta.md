## You'll want to know: अभियंता
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C515-VISHESH515-01]; assesses=[] -->

**अभियंता** — *abhiyantā* — "an engineer".

> माझी मावशी अभियंता आहे आणि पूल बांधते.
>
> *mājhī māvśī abhiyantā āhe āṇi pūl bāndhte.*
>
> My aunt is an engineer and builds bridges.

Masculine.

One word for a man or a woman.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अभियंता** (*abhiyantā*) is a modern Marathi coinage built from Sanskrit parts: *abhi-*, "towards", and *yantṛ*, "one who guides or restrains", related to *yantra*, "a machine" (Monier-Williams).
