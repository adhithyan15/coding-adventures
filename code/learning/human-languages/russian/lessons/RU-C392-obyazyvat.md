## You'll want to know: обязывать
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C392-OGRANICHENIYA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**обязывать** (*obyázyvat*) — "to oblige, to require".

> Договор обязывает нас закончить работу в мае.
>
> *Dogovór obyázyvayet nas zakónchit rabótu v máye.*
>
> The contract obliges us to finish the work in May.

Imperfective; perfective *обязать* (*обяжу, обяжешь*). *Обязывать кого* + infinitive. *Я обязан* — I am obliged.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**обязывать** is built from *о-* and *вязать* "to tie, to bind", from Proto-Slavic *\*vęzati*: to oblige someone is to bind them.
