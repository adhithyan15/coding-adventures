Marathi, chapter 448: Sewage and Stench.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
सांडपाणी (sāṇḍpāṇī) — waste water; sewage.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to chirp, then the Marathi for migration.

You'll want to know: सांडपाणी.
सांडपाणी — sāṇḍpāṇī — "waste water; sewage".
सांडपाणी नदीत सोडू नये. sāṇḍpāṇī nadīt soḍū naye. Waste water should not be let into the river.
Neuter.
Used water from homes and factories, which should be cleaned before it reaches a river.

The word, taken apart.
सांडपाणी (sāṇḍpāṇī) is built from sāṇḍ-, "spilt, let go", and पाणी, "water", inherited from Sanskrit pānīya (CDIAL 8082); the first part is not settled.

Lesson 2 of 5.
दुर्गंधी (durgandhī) — a stench.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for migration, then the Marathi for waste water.

You'll want to know: दुर्गंधी.
दुर्गंधी — durgandhī — "a stench".
सांडपाण्यामुळे गल्लीत दुर्गंधी पसरली. sāṇḍpāṇyāmuḷe gallīt durgandhī pasarlī. A stench spread through the lane because of the sewage.
Feminine.
A feminine noun; the opposite is सुगंध, a fragrance.

The word, taken apart.
दुर्गंधी (durgandhī) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit durgandha, "a bad smell, a stink" (Monier-Williams).

You'll want to know: प्रदूषित.
प्रदूषित — pradūṣit — "polluted".
शहरातली हवा प्रदूषित झाली आहे. śahrātlī havā pradūṣit jhālī āhe. The air in the city has become polluted.
It does not change: प्रदूषित (pradūṣit) हवा, प्रदूषित (pradūṣit) पाणी.

The word, taken apart.
प्रदूषित (pradūṣit) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit pradūṣita, "corrupted, spoilt" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 4 of 5.
कुजणे (kujṇe) — to rot.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a stench, then the Marathi for polluted.

You'll want to know: कुजणे.
कुजणे — kujṇe — "to rot".
पावसात लाकूड कुजलं. pāvsāt lākūḍ kujlaṁ. The wood rotted in the rain.
Past (he …): तो कुजला, to kujlā.
Wood, leaves and fruit कुजतात slowly.

The word, taken apart.
कुजणे (kujṇe) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi kuj̈ṇẽ, "to rot", under kujjati, "rots" (CDIAL 3222).

Lesson 5 of 5.
सडणे (saḍṇe) — to decay.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for polluted, then the Marathi for to rot.

You'll want to know: सडणे.
सडणे — saḍṇe — "to decay".
टोपलीतले आंबे सडले. ṭoplītle āmbe saḍle. The mangoes in the basket went rotten.
Past (he …): तो सडला, to saḍlā.
Sharper than कुजणे (kujṇe): it often smells.

The word, taken apart.
सडणे (saḍṇe) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi saḍṇẽ, "to rot", under Sanskrit śaṭati (CDIAL 12268).
