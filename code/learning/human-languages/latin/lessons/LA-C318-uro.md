## You'll want to know: ūrō, ūrere
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C318-CREMO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ūrō, ūrere** — "to burn; to scorch".

> Sōl agrōs ūrēbat.
>
> *The sun was scorching the fields.*

*ūrō, ūrere, ussī, ustum*. It has many compounds; English *combustion* comes from *combūrō*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ūrō** is an inherited verb; Lewis & Short compare Greek *heuō*, "to singe". English *combustion* comes from its compound *combūrō*.
