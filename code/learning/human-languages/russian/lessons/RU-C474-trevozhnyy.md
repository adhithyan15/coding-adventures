## You'll want to know: тревожный
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C474-WORRY-02]; assesses=[] -->

**тревожный** (*trevózhnyy*) — "anxious; alarming".

> У неё был тревожный голос.
>
> *U neyó byl trevózhnyy gólos.*
>
> Her voice sounded anxious.

Of a person, anxious; of news, alarming.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*тревожный* is built on *тревога* 'alarm, anxiety', which is inherited from Proto-Slavic *trьvoga* (EtymDB).
