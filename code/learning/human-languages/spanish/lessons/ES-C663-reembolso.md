## You'll want to know: el reembolso
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C663-TEMPORADA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**el reembolso** — "the refund".

> Pedí el reembolso del billete.
>
> *I asked for a refund on the ticket.*

*Contra reembolso* — cash on delivery.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*reembolso* is built on *reembolsar*, from *re-* and *embolsar* "to put in the purse"; *bolsa* comes from Late Latin *bursa*, from Greek *býrsa* "hide".
