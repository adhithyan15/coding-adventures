Arabic, chapter 137: Spider, Rooster, Cat, Owl, Crow.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
عنكبوت (ʿankabūt) — a spider.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a bee, then the Arabic for a butterfly.

You'll want to know: عنكبوت.
عنكبوت — ʿankabūt — "a spider".

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
