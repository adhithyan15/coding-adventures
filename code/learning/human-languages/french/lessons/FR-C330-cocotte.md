## You'll want to know: la cocotte
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C330-MIJOTER-05]; assesses=[] -->

**la cocotte** — "the casserole dish, the cast-iron pot".

> Elle fait cuire le poulet dans une cocotte.
>
> *She cooks the chicken in a casserole dish.*

*La cocotte-minute* — the pressure cooker. In baby talk, *une cocotte* is a hen.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

The origin of *cocotte* is not settled: one sense is a child's word for a hen, the other an older word for a pot (EtymDB lists no parent).
