## You'll want to know: とこのま
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C400-TOKONOMA400-01]; assesses=[] -->

**とこのま** — *tokonoma* — "an alcove (for flowers or a hanging scroll)".

> **とこのまに はなを かざりました。** — *tokonoma ni hana o kazarimashita* — "I put flowers in the alcove."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**とこのま** (*tokonoma*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, built from *toko*, "raised floor", the particle *no* and *ma*, "space, room"; its kanji were attached later as kun'yomi (JMdict).
