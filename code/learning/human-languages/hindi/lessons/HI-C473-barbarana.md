## You'll want to know: बड़बड़ाना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C473-BATUNI473-04]; assesses=[] -->

**बड़बड़ाना** — *baṛbaṛānā* — "to grumble; to mutter".

> काम ज़्यादा था, इसलिए नौकर बड़बड़ा रहा था।
>
> *kām zyādā thā, isliye naukar baṛbaṛā rahā thā.*
>
> There was too much work, so the servant was grumbling.

Past (she …): वह कुछ बड़बड़ाई, *vah kuch baṛbaṛāī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**बड़बड़ाना** (*baṛbaṛānā*) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Hindi *baṛbaṛānā*, "to grumble", under *\*baḍabaḍa*, a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 9122); the doubled syllable is the sound of muttering.
