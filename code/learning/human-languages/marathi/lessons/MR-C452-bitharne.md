## You'll want to know: बिथरणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C452-DHAKKA452-04]; assesses=[] -->

**बिथरणे** — *bitharṇe* — "to panic; to run wild".

> फटाक्याचा आवाज ऐकून घोडा बिथरला.
>
> *phaṭākyācā āvāj aikūn ghoḍā bitharlā.*
>
> The horse panicked at the sound of the firecracker.

Past (he …): तो बिथरला, *to bitharlā*.

Of animals and people who lose their heads in fright: घोडा बिथरला, the horse bolted.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**बिथरणे** (*bitharṇe*) is inherited by CDIAL's account: it lists Marathi *bitharṇẽ*, "to misconduct oneself", beside *itharṇẽ*, "to run wild", under Sanskrit *itvara*, "walking; mean, cruel" (CDIAL 1566). How the *b-* arose is not explained there, so the link is not certain.
