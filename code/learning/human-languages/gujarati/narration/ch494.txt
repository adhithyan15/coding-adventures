Gujarati, chapter 494: Growing Up, Growing Old.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
બાળપણ (bāḷpaṇ) — childhood.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a household, then the Gujarati for offspring.

You'll want to know: બાળપણ.
બાળપણ — bāḷpaṇ — "childhood".
દાદા પોતાના બાળપણની વાર્તા કહે છે. dādā potānā bāḷpaṇnī vārtā kahe che. Grandfather tells stories of his childhood.
A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
બાળપણ (bāḷpaṇ) goes back to a reconstructed bālatvana-, "childhood", built on Sanskrit bāla, "a child"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati word (CDIAL 9218).

Lesson 2 of 5.
ઉછરવું (ucharvũ) — to grow up; to be brought up.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for offspring, then the Gujarati for childhood.

You'll want to know: ઉછરવું.
ઉછરવું — ucharvũ — "to grow up; to be brought up".
મીરા ગામમાં ઉછરી છે. Mīrā gāmmā̃ uchrī che. Mira grew up in a village.
Past (he …): તે મામાના ઘરે ઉછર્યો, te māmānā ghare ucharyo.

The word, taken apart.
ઉછરવું (ucharvũ) is inherited from Sanskrit ucchrīyatē, "is raised"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb, "to be brought up" (CDIAL 1864). The causative ઉછેરવું (uchervũ), "to bring up", is its partner.

Lesson 3 of 5.
કિશોર (kiśor) — a teenage boy.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for childhood, then the Gujarati for to grow up.

You'll want to know: કિશોર.
કિશોર — kiśor — "a teenage boy".
તે કિશોર ઘણો હોશિયાર છે. te kiśor ghaṇo hośiyār che. That teenage boy is very clever.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
કિશોર (kiśor) is a tatsama, Sanskrit kiśora, which Monier-Williams gives first as "a colt" and then as a youth.

બસમાં વૃદ્ધ માણસને બેસવાની જગ્યા આપો. basmā̃ vṛddh māṇasne besvānī jagyā āpo. On the bus, give your seat to an elderly person.
A masculine or feminine noun, by the person meant.

The word, taken apart.
વૃદ્ધ (vṛddh) is a tatsama, Sanskrit vṛddha, "grown, old", as Monier-Williams records it.
