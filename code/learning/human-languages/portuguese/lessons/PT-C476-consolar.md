## You'll want to know: consolar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C476-DESILUSAO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**consolar** — "to console, to comfort".

> Consolei a minha amiga depois da má notícia.
>
> *I comforted my friend after the bad news.*

The noun is *o consolo* — comfort.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*consolar* comes from Latin *cōnsōlārī* "to comfort, to console" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short).
