## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-FORM-HOVEGA-186, PA-FORM-HOVEGI-186, PA-LEX-KAMM-05, PA-TRAVEL-JOURNEY-01] -->

- [YOU HEAR: *kamm hovegaa*; YOU SAY: **ਕੰਮ ਹੋਵੇਗਾ**]
- [YOU HEAR: *yaatraa hovegii*; YOU SAY: **ਯਾਤਰਾ ਹੋਵੇਗੀ**]
- [YOU READ: **ਹੋਵੇਗਾ · ਹੋਵੇਗੀ**]
- [YOU WRITE: cover the pair and write **ਹੋਵੇਗਾ, ਹੋਵੇਗੀ**]

The stem and future meaning stay together. Only the final agreement vowel
changes.
