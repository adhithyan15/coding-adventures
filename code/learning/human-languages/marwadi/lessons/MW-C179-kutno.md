# कूटणो (kūṭṇo) — to pound

## You'll want to know: कूटणो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C179-PISO179-02]; assesses=[] -->

**कूटणो** — *kūṭṇo* — "to pound".

> दादी सिल माथै मिरच कूटै है।
>
> *dādī sil māthai mirach kūṭai hai.*
>
> Grandmother pounds chillies on the grinding stone.

Past (I …): म्हैं मिरच कूटी, *mhaĩ mirach kūṭī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**कूटणो** (*kūṭṇo*) is inherited from Sanskrit *kuṭṭayati*, "crushes, pounds"; CDIAL lists Hindi *kūṭnā* and Gujarati *kuṭvũ* (CDIAL 3241). CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry.
