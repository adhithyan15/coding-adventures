Gujarati, chapter 432: Gold and Silver.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
સોનું (sonũ) — gold.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for wholesale, then the Gujarati for to hoard.

You'll want to know: સોનું.
સોનું — sonũ — "gold".
દાદી પાસે સોનાની વીંટી છે. dādī pāse sonānī vī̃ṭī che. Grandmother has a gold ring.
A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
સોનું (sonũ) is a tadbhava of Sanskrit suvarṇa, "of a good colour", and so "gold"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati word (CDIAL 13519).

તેણે ચાંદીની વાટકી કપડાથી ઘસી. teṇe cā̃dīnī vāṭkī kapḍāthī ghasī. She rubbed the silver bowl with a cloth.

ઘસવું (ghasvũ) is a tadbhava of Sanskrit gharṣati, "rubs"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb, "to rub, to polish" (CDIAL 4450).

Lesson 5 of 6.
ચમકાવવું (camkāvvũ) — to make shine; to polish until it shines.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for jewellery, then the Gujarati for to rub.

You'll want to know: ચમકાવવું.
ચમકાવવું — camkāvvũ — "to make shine; to polish until it shines".
તેણે જૂનું ઘરેણું ઘસીને ચમકાવ્યું. teṇe jūnũ ghareṇũ ghasīne camkāvyũ. He rubbed the old ornament until it shone.
Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે વીંટી ચમકાવી, teṇe vī̃ṭī camkāvī.

The word, taken apart.
ચમકાવવું (camkāvvũ) is the causative partner of ચમકવું, "to shine", which goes back to camakka, a form CDIAL reconstructs (CDIAL 4676): to make a thing shine.
