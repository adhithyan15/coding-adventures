## You'll want to know: el presente
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C575-PASADO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**el presente** — "the present".

> Hay que vivir el presente.
>
> *You have to live in the present.*

As an adjective it means present: *Estaba presente.* In class, *¡Presente!* — here!

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*presente* is a learned borrowing from Latin *praesēns* 'at hand, present' (Lewis & Short), built on *prae* 'before' and the verb *esse* 'to be'.
