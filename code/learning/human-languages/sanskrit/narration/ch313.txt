Sanskrit, chapter 313: Trouble in the Town.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
उपद्रवः (upadravaḥ) — a disturbance; a riot.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a lie, then the Sanskrit for doubtful.

You'll want to know: उपद्रवः.
उपद्रवः — upadravaḥ — "a disturbance; a riot".
नगरे सहसा उपद्रवः घटितः। nagare sahasā upadravaḥ ghaṭitaḥ. A disturbance suddenly broke out in the city.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
उपद्रवः (upadravaḥ) is built from upa and the root √dru, "to run"; MW gives upadrava as that which attacks suddenly, a misfortune, and also national commotion or rebellion (MW).

वेष्टयति — veṣṭayati — "surrounds".
आरक्षकाः विपणिं वेष्टयन्ति। ārakṣakāḥ vipaṇiṃ veṣṭayanti. The police surround the market.
Past (he …): सः विपणिं वेष्टितवान्, saḥ vipaṇiṃ veṣṭitavān.

The word, taken apart.
वेष्टयति (veṣṭayati) comes from the root √veṣṭ, "to wind or twist round"; MW gives its causative veṣṭayati as to wrap up, enclose, surround (MW).
