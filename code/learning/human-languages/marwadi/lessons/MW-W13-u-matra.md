# ु — short *u* after a consonant

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MW-LEX-DUKAN-01, MW-SCRIPT-DA-01, MW-SCRIPT-UU-MATRA-01, MW-SCRIPT-AU-MATRA-01, MW-SCRIPT-DDA-01, MW-SCRIPT-PATNI-01, MW-SCRIPT-LA-01] -->

[PAUSE 24s] Say shop, write **द**, **ू**, **ौ**, **ड**, **ल**, and **पत्नी**.

## Script — one mark
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-SCRIPT-U-MATRA-01]; assesses=[] -->

> **ु** — short *u* after a consonant

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MW-SCRIPT-U-MATRA-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: observe-trace -->

Trace **ु** below **द** once, copy **दु** once, cover it, and write **दु** from
sound. Keep it shorter than familiar **दू**.
