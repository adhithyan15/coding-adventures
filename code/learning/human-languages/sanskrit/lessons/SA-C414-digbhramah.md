## You'll want to know: दिग्भ्रमः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C414-DIGBHRAMA414-01]; assesses=[] -->

**दिग्भ्रमः** — *digbhramaḥ* — "losing one's bearings".

> वने पथिकस्य दिग्भ्रमः भवति।
>
> *vane pathikasya digbhramaḥ bhavati.*
>
> In the forest the wayfarer loses his bearings.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**दिग्भ्रमः** (*digbhramaḥ*) is built from *diś*, "a direction", and *bhrama*, "wandering, confusion", from the root √bhram, "to wander"; MW gives the compound as confusion about the quarters of the compass, mistaking the way (MW).
