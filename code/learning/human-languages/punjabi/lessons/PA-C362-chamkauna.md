# ਚਮਕਾਉਣਾ (chamkāuṇā) — to polish; to make something shine

## You'll want to know: ਚਮਕਾਉਣਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C362-SKILL362-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ਚਮਕਾਉਣਾ** — *chamkāuṇā* — "to polish; to make something shine".

> ਮੈਂ ਹਰ ਹਫ਼ਤੇ ਪਿੱਤਲ ਦੇ ਭਾਂਡੇ ਚਮਕਾਉਂਦਾ ਹਾਂ।
>
> *maiṁ har hafte pittal de bhāṇḍe chamkāundā hāṁ.*
>
> I polish the brass pots every week.

Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਭਾਂਡੇ ਚਮਕਾਏ, *maiṁ bhāṇḍe chamkāe*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਚਮਕਾਉਣਾ** (*chamkāuṇā*) is the causative partner of *camakṇā*, "to shine", which goes back to a reconstructed *\*camakka*, "sudden movement"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *camakṇā*, "to shine", under it (CDIAL 4676).
