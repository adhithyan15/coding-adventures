## You'll want to know: ಸಾಧ್ಯತೆ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C376-PURAVE376-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸಾಧ್ಯತೆ** — *sādhyate* — "a possibility; a chance".

> ನಾಳೆ ಮಳೆ ಬರುವ ಸಾಧ್ಯತೆ ಇದೆ.
>
> *nāḷe maḷe baruva sādhyate ide.*
>
> There is a chance of rain tomorrow.

A verb in -ುವ comes before it: ಬರುವ ಸಾಧ್ಯತೆ, the chance of coming.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಸಾಧ್ಯತೆ** (*sādhyate*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *sādhyatā*, "feasibility" (Monier-Williams), built on *sādhya*, "that can be done", with the ending -*tā*, "-ness".
