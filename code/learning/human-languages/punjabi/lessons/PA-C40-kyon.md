## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-Q-KYON-01]; assesses=[PA-ETYMON-QUESTION-K-03] -->

> **ਕਿਉਂ** — *kyoṁ* — **why**

The same **ਕ** as every question word in the family. This one has been missing:
the book named the family when it taught *kivēṁ*, listed its members, and **ਕਿਉਂ** was
the one it left out — so a reader could ask several things and never **why**.

That is not a small omission. *Why* is the question that asks for a reason, and
the next lesson is the word that answers it.
