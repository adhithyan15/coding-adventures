## You'll want to know: die Wäscheleine
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C527-WAESCHE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**die Wäscheleine** — "clothes line".

> Im Sommer hängen wir die Wäsche auf die Wäscheleine.
>
> *In summer we hang the washing on the line.*

Plural: *die Wäscheleinen*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Wäscheleine* is built on *Wäsche* "washing" and *Leine* "line, rope", inherited from Old High German *līna*, from Proto-Germanic *līnōn* (EtymDB).
