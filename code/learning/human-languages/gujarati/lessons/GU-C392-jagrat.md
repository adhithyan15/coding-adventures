## You'll want to know: જાગ્રત
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C392-SAJJ392-02]; assesses=[] -->

**જાગ્રત** — *jāgrat* — "watchful".

> જો ચોકીદાર જાગ્રત હશે તો કોઈ અંદર નહીં ઘૂસે.
>
> *jo cokīdār jāgrat haśe to koī andar nahī̃ ghūse.*
>
> If the watchman is alert, nobody will get in.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**જાગ્રત** (*jāgrat*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *jāgrat*, "waking, watchful" (Monier-Williams).
