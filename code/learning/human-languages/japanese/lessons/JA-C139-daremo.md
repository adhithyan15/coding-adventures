## You'll want to know: だれも
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C139-DAREMO]; assesses=[] -->

**だれも** — *dare mo* — "nobody", "not anyone". Like **なにも**, it needs
a negative verb.

> **ここには だれも いません。** — *koko ni wa dare mo imasen* — "There is
> nobody here."

**いません**, *imasen*, is the negative of **います**, *imasu*, "someone is
there", the verb Japanese uses for people and animals.
