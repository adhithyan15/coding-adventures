## You'll want to know: بهبود
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C374-BEHBUD-01]; assesses=[] -->

**بهبود** (*behbud*) — "an improvement; recovery".

> حال پدربزرگ رو به بهبود است.
>
> *hâl-e pedarbozorg ru be behbud ast.*
>
> Grandfather's health is improving.

*Beh* "good, better" + *bud* "being". For health and for situations alike.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**بهبود** is a native Persian compound built from *beh* "good, better" and *bud*, the past stem of *budan* "to be": a better state of being.
