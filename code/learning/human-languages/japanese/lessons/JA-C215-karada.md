## You'll want to know: からだ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C215-BODY215-01]; assesses=[] -->

**からだ** — *karada* — "the body".

> **これは からだに いいですか。** — *kore wa karada ni ii desu ka* — "Is this good for your body?"

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**からだ** (*karada*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi, and its older origin is not settled (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
