## You'll want to know: قطار
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C391-QATAR391-02]; assesses=[] -->

**قطار** — *qatār* — "a queue; a row".

> ٹکٹ کے لیے لمبی قطار تھی۔
>
> *ṭikaṭ ke liye lambī qatār thī.*
>
> There was a long queue for tickets.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *qiṭār*, "a line of camels"; in Urdu any line of people waiting is a **قطار**.
