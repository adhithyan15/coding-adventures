Portuguese, chapter 364: Na rua.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
protestar — to protest.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for the freedom, then the Portuguese for the equality.

You'll want to know: protestar.
protestar — "to protest".
Os moradores protestaram contra o barulho. The residents protested against the noise.
Protestar contra — to protest against.

The word, taken apart.
protestar is a learned borrowing from Latin prōtestārī "to declare in public, to bear witness" (EtymDB, Lewis & Short), built from prō "in public" and testārī "to bear witness", on testis "witness".

What you've built.
One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.

You'll want to know: a manifestação.
a manifestação — "the demonstration, the march; the expression (of a feeling)".
Milhares de pessoas foram à manifestação. Thousands of people went to the demonstration.

The word, taken apart.
manifestação is a learned borrowing from Latin manifēstātiō "a manifesting, a manifestation" (EtymDB, Lewis & Short), from manifēstāre "to show", on manifēstus "clear, evident".

exigir — "to demand, to require".

The word, taken apart.
exigir is a learned borrowing from Latin exigere "to drive out, to demand" (EtymDB), built from ex and agere "to drive" (Lewis & Short).
