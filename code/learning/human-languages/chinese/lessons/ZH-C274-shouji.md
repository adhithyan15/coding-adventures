## You'll want to know: shōují
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C274-FREE274-02]; assesses=[] -->

*shōují* — "to collect things".

> *wǒ xǐhuan shōují yóupiào* — I like collecting stamps.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*shōují* is built from *shōu* 'to gather in' and *jí* 'to assemble', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *syuw* and *dzip* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to gather in' and the second 'to gather'.
