## You'll want to know: মন্দা
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C424-DAM424-03]; assesses=[] -->

**মন্দা** — *môndā* — "a slump; slack trade".

> গত বছর ব্যবসায় মন্দা ছিল।
>
> *gôtô bôchhôr bæbsāy môndā chhilô.*
>
> Last year business was slack.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**মন্দা** (*môndā*) goes back to Sanskrit *manda*, "slow, weak, slack" (Monier-Williams): trade that has gone slow.
