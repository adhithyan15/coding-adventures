Mandarin Chinese, chapter 266: Grown-Ups and Children.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
大人 (dàren) — adult; grown-up.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for stingy, then the Mandarin for nervous.

You'll want to know: 大人.
大人 — dàren — "adult; grown-up".
大人和小孩都来了 dàren hé xiǎohái dōu lái le — The grown-ups and the children have all come.

The word, taken apart.
大人 (dàren) is built from 大 'big' and 人 'a person', both inherited from Middle Chinese, daH and nyin (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription). In the word the second loses its tone.

What you've built.
One more word for what I have and can do.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: dàren]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: dàren, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: dàren hé xiǎohái dōu lái le — The grown-ups and the children have all come.]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Mandarin for stingy, then the Mandarin for nervous, then say dàren again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does 大人 (dàren) mean? ("Adult; grown-up".) Say it once more.


Lesson 2 of 5.
bǎobao — baby; darling (said to a small child).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for nervous, then the Mandarin for a grown-up.

You'll want to know: bǎobao.
bǎobao — "baby; darling (said to a small child)".
wǒ jiějie de bǎobao hěn kě'ài — My sister's baby is really cute.
Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
bǎobao is built from bǎo 'a treasure', said twice, the second time with no tone. It is inherited from Middle Chinese pawX (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'a treasure'; its use for a baby is not given there.

What you've built.
One more word for what I have and can do.
