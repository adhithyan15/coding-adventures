## You'll want to know: la bourse
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C379-DIPLOME-05]; assesses=[] -->

**la bourse** — "the grant, the scholarship; the stock exchange".

> Elle a reçu une bourse pour étudier à l'étranger.
>
> *She got a grant to study abroad.*

*La Bourse* is the stock exchange. Once a purse.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*bourse* is inherited through Old French *borse* "purse" (EtymDB) from Late Latin *bursa* "a leather bag", from Greek *búrsa* "a hide".
