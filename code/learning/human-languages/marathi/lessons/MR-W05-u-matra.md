# ु — short *u*, a small hook below

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One mark went to the left. This one goes straight down, and it is the mark you will meet most often of all.

## Script
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-SCRIPT-U-MATRA-01]; assesses=[MR-SCRIPT-I-MATRA-01] -->

> ु

The short-*u* mark is a small hook tucked **under** the letter, opening to the
right. It hangs from the foot of the stem, not from the headline.

**तु** is *tu* — the opening syllable of the polite *tumhī* you already say.
Start at the base of **त**, dip below the line, and curl right. Keep it small:
its long partner is the same hook drawn deeper.

## Writing — guided copy
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MR-SCRIPT-U-MATRA-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: guided-copy -->

Copy **तु** twice with the model visible, saying *tu* as the hook closes. Then
write the short-*i* mark once beside it from memory and check it still leans
left.
