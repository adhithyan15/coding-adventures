## You'll want to know: जमना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C384-BARF384-02]; assesses=[] -->

**जमना** — *jamnā* — "to freeze, to set".

> अगर रात को बहुत ठंड होगी तो पानी जम जाएगा।
>
> *agar rāt ko bahut ṭhaṇḍ hogī to pānī jam jāegā.*
>
> If it is very cold at night, the water will freeze.

Past (it …): वह पानी जम गया, *vah pānī jam gayā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**जमना** (*jamnā*) is a tadbhava, inherited from Sanskrit *yamyate*, "is fixed" (CDIAL 10428).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
