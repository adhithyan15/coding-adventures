## You'll want to know: شباك
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C436-KUSHK436-03]; assesses=[] -->

**شباك** — *shubbāk* — "a ticket window; a window".

> اشترى أبي التذاكر من الشباك.
>
> *ishtarā abī at-tadhākir min ash-shubbāk.*
>
> My father bought the tickets at the ticket window.

Plural: شبابيك, *shabābīk*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**شباك** (*shubbāk*) is built on the native Arabic root *sh-b-k*, "to interlace, to entwine" (Lane). A *shubbāk* was first a latticed window.
