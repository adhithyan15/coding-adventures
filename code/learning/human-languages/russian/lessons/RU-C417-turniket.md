## You'll want to know: турникет
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C417-MARSHRUTKA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**турникет** (*turnikét*) — "a turnstile".

> У входа в метро стоят турникеты.
>
> *U vkhóda v metró stoyát turnikéty.*
>
> There are turnstiles at the metro entrance.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*турникет* was borrowed from French *tourniquet* (EtymDB), built on *tourner* 'to turn', which goes back to Latin *tornāre* 'to turn on a lathe' (Lewis & Short).
