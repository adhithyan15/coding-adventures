## You'll want to know: ردی
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C505-ATARI505-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ردی** — *raddī* — "waste paper; scrap".

> ہم پرانے اخبار ردی میں بیچتے ہیں۔
>
> *ham purāne akhbār raddī meṅ bechte haiṅ.*
>
> We sell our old newspapers as waste paper.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ردی** (*raddī*) is usually traced to Persian *raddī*, "rejected, worthless".
