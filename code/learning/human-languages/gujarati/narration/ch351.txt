Gujarati, chapter 351: Facts and Mix-ups.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
હકીકત (hakīkat) — a fact.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for seized, then the Gujarati for to recover a debt.

You'll want to know: હકીકત.
હકીકત — hakīkat — "a fact".
આ વાર્તા નથી, હકીકત છે. ā vārtā nathī, hakīkat che. This isn't a story; it's a fact.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
હકીકત (hakīkat) is usually traced to Arabic ḥaqīqa, "truth, reality", through Persian.

ગેરસમજ — gersamaj — "a misunderstanding".
આ નાની ગેરસમજને કારણે બે મિત્રો લડ્યા. ā nānī gersamajne kāraṇe be mitro laḍyā. The two friends fought because of this small misunderstanding.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ગેરસમજ (gersamaj) is built from ger-, "mis-, not", usually traced to Persian ghair, "other", and samaj, "understanding", the noun of સમજવું (samajvũ), from Sanskrit saṃbudhyatē (CDIAL 12959).
