## You'll want to know: ਪਾਰਸਲ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C256-DAK256-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ਪਾਰਸਲ** — *pārsal* — "a parcel".

> ਅਮਨ ਨੇ ਮੈਨੂੰ ਇੱਕ ਪਾਰਸਲ ਭੇਜਿਆ।
>
> *Aman ne mainū̃ ikk pārsal bhejiā.*
>
> Aman sent me a parcel.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਪਾਰਸਲ** (*pārsal*) is borrowed from English *parcel*.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for reading notices and messages.
