## You'll want to know: しゅちょうする
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C320-TACHIBA320-03]; assesses=[] -->

**しゅちょうする** — *shuchō suru* — "to insist; to claim".

> **ただしいと おもったから、つよく しゅちょうしました。** — *tadashii to omotta kara, tsuyoku shuchō shimashita* — "He thought he was right, so he insisted strongly."

Polite **しゅちょうします**, *shuchōshimasu*; a する verb. Past (polite): **おとうとが しゅちょうしました。**, *otōto ga shuchō shimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**しゅちょうする** (*shuchō suru*) is built from a Sino-Japanese noun and the native verb *suru*, "to do". The noun, *shuchō*, is two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese: *shu*, "main thing" (Middle Chinese *tsyuX*) and *chō*, "stretch" (*trjang*) (KANJIDIC2; JMdict; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
