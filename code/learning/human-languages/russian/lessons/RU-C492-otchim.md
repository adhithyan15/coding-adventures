## You'll want to know: отчим
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C492-STEPFAMILY-01]; assesses=[] -->

**отчим** (*ótchim*) — "a stepfather".

> Отчим относился к нему как к родному сыну.
>
> *Ótchim otnosílsya k nemú kak k rodnómu sýnu.*
>
> His stepfather treated him like his own son.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*отчим* is built on *отец* 'father', through the old adjective *отчий* 'father's'; EtymDB has no entry for *отчим*.
