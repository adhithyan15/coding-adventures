## You'll want to know: لڑھکانا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C522-PHURTI522-03]; assesses=[] -->

**لڑھکانا** — *luṛhkānā* — "to roll (something) along".

> بچے نے گیند میری طرف لڑھکائی۔
>
> *bachche ne gend merī taraf luṛhkāī.*
>
> The child rolled the ball towards me.

Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے پتھر لڑھکایا, *us ne patthar luṛhkāyā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**لڑھکانا** (*luṛhkānā*) is the causative of *luṛhaknā*, "to roll", which CDIAL lists under Sanskrit *luṭhati*, "rolls, wallows" (CDIAL 11079).
