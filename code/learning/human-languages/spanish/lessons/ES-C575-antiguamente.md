## You'll want to know: antiguamente
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C575-PASADO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**antiguamente** — "in the old days, formerly".

> Antiguamente la gente viajaba a caballo.
>
> *In the old days people travelled on horseback.*

Usually with the imperfect: *Antiguamente aquí había un río.*

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*antiguamente* is built on *antiguo* and the ending *-mente*, from Latin *mente* 'with a mind'. *Antiguo* comes from Latin *antīquus* 'of former times' (EtymDB).
