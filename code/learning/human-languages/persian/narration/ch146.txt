Lesson 1 of 5.
به یاد آوردن (be yâd âvardan) — to remember.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to believe, then the Persian for to call.

You'll want to know: به یاد آوردن.
به یاد آوردن — be yâd âvardan — "to remember".

The word, taken apart.
be yâd âvardan is "to bring to memory": be, "to", yâd, "memory" (usually traced to Middle Persian ayād), and âvardan, "to bring". All are native Persian. âvardan, "to bring", is an inherited verb; it is usually traced to Middle Persian āwurdan.

You'll want to know: به نظر رسیدن.
به نظر رسیدن — be nazar residan — "to seem".

The word, taken apart.
be nazar residan is "to reach the view": be, "to", nazar, "look, view", and residan, "to reach". nazar is borrowed from Arabic naẓar, "look, sight" (root n-ẓ-r, Lane); be and residan are native. residan, "to reach", is an inherited verb; it is usually traced to Middle Persian rasīdan.
