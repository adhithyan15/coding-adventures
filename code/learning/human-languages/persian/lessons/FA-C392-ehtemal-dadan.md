## You'll want to know: احتمال دادن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C392-EHTEMAL-02]; assesses=[] -->

**احتمال دادن** (*ehtemâl dâdan*) — "to think likely, to suspect".

> من احتمال میدهم که او دیر بیاید.
>
> *man ehtemâl midaham ke u dir biyâyad.*
>
> I think he will probably come late.

*Ehtemâl* + *dâdan* (stem *deh-*); *ke* + subjunctive follows.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**احتمال دادن** is literally "to give a likelihood": *ehtemâl* is usually traced to Arabic *iḥtimāl*, and *dâdan* "to give" is traced by EtymDB to PIE *deh₃-*.
