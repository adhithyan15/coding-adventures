Marathi, chapter 103: Ant, Duck, New, Old, Bad.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
मुंगी (muṅgī) — an ant.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a camel, then the Marathi for a butterfly.

You'll want to know: मुंगी.
मुंगी — muṅgī — "an ant".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: muṅgī]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: muṅgī, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say muṅgī]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Marathi for a camel, then the Marathi for a butterfly, then say muṅgī again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does मुंगी (muṅgī) mean? ("An ant".) Say it once more.
