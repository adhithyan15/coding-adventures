## You'll want to know: der Halbbruder
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C491-PATEN-01]; assesses=[] -->

**der Halbbruder** — "half-brother".

> Mein Halbbruder ist zehn Jahre jünger als ich.
>
> *My half-brother is ten years younger than me.*

Plural: *die Halbbrüder*. Likewise *die Halbschwester*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Halbbruder* is built on *halb* "half", inherited from Old High German *halb*, from Proto-Germanic *halbaz* (EtymDB), and *Bruder* "brother".
