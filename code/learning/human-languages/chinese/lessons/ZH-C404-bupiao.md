## You'll want to know: bǔpiào
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C404-TICKET404-03]; assesses=[] -->

*bǔpiào* — "to buy a ticket on board".

> *méiyǒu piào dehuà kěyǐ shàngchē bǔpiào* — If you've no ticket, you can buy one on the train.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*bǔpiào* is built from *bǔ* 'to mend' and 票 'a ticket'. The first is inherited from Middle Chinese *puX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'to patch'; no dictionary we use records an older form of 票, so its history is not settled.
