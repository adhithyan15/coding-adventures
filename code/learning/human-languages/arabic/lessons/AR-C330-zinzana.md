# زنزانة (zinzāna) — a prison cell

## You'll want to know: زنزانة
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C330-SIJN330-03]; assesses=[] -->

**زنزانة** — *zinzāna* — "a prison cell".

> كانت الزنزانة صغيرة ومظلمة.
>
> *kānat az-zinzāna ṣaghīra wa-muẓlima.*
>
> The cell was small and dark.

Plural: زنازين, *zanāzīn*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**زنزانة** (*zinzāna*) is not in the old dictionaries Lane drew on; it belongs to later Arabic, and the origin is not settled.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
