## You'll want to know: त्वरते
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C315-VISPHOTA315-05]; assesses=[] -->

**त्वरते** — *tvarate* — "hurries".

> सर्वे चिकित्सालयं प्रति त्वरन्ते।
>
> *sarve cikitsālayaṃ prati tvarante.*
>
> Everyone hurries towards the hospital.

Past (he …): सः चिकित्सालयं प्रति त्वरितवान्, *saḥ cikitsālayaṃ prati tvaritavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**त्वरते** (*tvarate*) comes from the root √tvar, "to hurry, make haste" (MW).
