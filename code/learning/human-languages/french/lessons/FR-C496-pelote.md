## You'll want to know: la pelote
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C496-COUTURE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**la pelote** — "ball (of wool or string)".

> Le chat joue avec une pelote de laine.
>
> *The cat is playing with a ball of wool.*

*Une pelote de laine* — a ball of wool. *La pelote basque* is a ball game.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*la pelote* is inherited from Vulgar Latin *pilotta* "a small ball" (EtymDB), built on Latin *pila* "a ball" (Lewis & Short).
