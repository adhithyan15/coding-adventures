## You'll want to know: 死心
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C462-SURPRISE462-04]; assesses=[] -->

**死心** — *sǐxīn* — "to give up hope".

> *wǒ děng le tā sān nián xiànzài sǐxīn le* — I waited three years for him, and now I've given up.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**死心** (*sǐxīn*) is built from 死 'to die' and 心 'heart', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *sijX* and *sim* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which explains the first, after an older book, as coming to an end: a heart that has stopped hoping.
