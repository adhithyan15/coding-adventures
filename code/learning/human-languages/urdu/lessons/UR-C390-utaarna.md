## You'll want to know: اتارنا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C390-RAVANA390-05]; assesses=[] -->

**اتارنا** — *utārnā* — "to take down; to drop off".

> قلی نے ڈبے سے ہمارا سامان اتارا۔
>
> *qulī ne ḍabbe se hamārā sāmān utārā.*
>
> The porter took our luggage down from the carriage.

Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے صندوق اتارا, *us ne sandūq utārā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**اتارنا** (*utārnā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists Hindi *utārnā*, "to take across, to make descend", under Sanskrit *úttarati* and its causative *uttārayati* (CDIAL 1770). It is the causing partner of **اترنا**, "to get off".
