## You'll want to know: consentire
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C354-OSTACOLO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**consentire** — "to allow, to make possible; to consent".

> Il nuovo treno consente di arrivare al mare in un'ora.
>
> *The new train makes it possible to reach the sea in an hour.*

*Consentire a qualcuno di fare qualcosa*. Formal next to *permettere*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*consentire* is inherited from Latin *cōnsentīre* "to agree, to feel together" (EtymDB), built from *con-* "together" and *sentīre* "to feel".
