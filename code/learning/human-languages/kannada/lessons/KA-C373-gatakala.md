# ಗತಕಾಲ (gatakāla) — the past; bygone days

## You'll want to know: ಗತಕಾಲ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C373-UDAYA373-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ಗತಕಾಲ** — *gatakāla* — "the past; bygone days".

> ಅಜ್ಜ ಯಾವಾಗಲೂ ಗತಕಾಲದ ವೈಭವದ ಬಗ್ಗೆ ಮಾತಾಡುತ್ತಾರೆ.
>
> *ajja yāvāgalū gatakālada vaibhavada bagge mātāḍuttāre.*
>
> Grandfather always talks about the glory of the past.

ಗತ, gone, and ಕಾಲ, time.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಗತಕಾಲ** (*gatakāla*) is borrowed from Sanskrit, a compound of *gata*, "gone, past" (CDIAL 4008), and *kāla*, "time" (Monier-Williams).

One more word for telling what happened.
