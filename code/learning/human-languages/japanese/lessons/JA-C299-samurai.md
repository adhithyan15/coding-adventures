## You'll want to know: さむらい
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C299-OUSAMA299-03]; assesses=[] -->

**さむらい** — *samurai* — "a samurai; a warrior".

> **つよい さむらいが むらに きました。** — *tsuyoi samurai ga mura ni kimashita* — "A strong samurai came to the village."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**さむらい** (*samurai*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict).
