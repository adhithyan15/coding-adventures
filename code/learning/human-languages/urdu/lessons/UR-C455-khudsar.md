## You'll want to know: خودسر
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C455-FLAW455-03]; assesses=[] -->

**خودسر** — *khudsar* — "headstrong".

> وہ خودسر لڑکا کسی کی نہیں سنتا۔
>
> *voh khudsar laṛkā kisī kī nahīṅ suntā.*
>
> That headstrong boy listens to nobody.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**خودسر** (*khudsar*) is usually traced to Persian *khudsar*, "self-willed", built from **خود**, "oneself", and **سر**, "head".
