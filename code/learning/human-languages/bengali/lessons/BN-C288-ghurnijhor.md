## You'll want to know: ঘূর্ণিঝড়
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C288-GHURNIJHOR288-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ঘূর্ণিঝড়** — *ghūrnijhôṛ* — "a cyclone".

> সমুদ্র থেকে একটা বড় ঘূর্ণিঝড় আসছিল।
>
> *sômudrô theke ekṭā bôṛo ghūrnijhôṛ āschhilô.*
>
> A big cyclone was coming in from the sea.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ঘূর্ণিঝড়** (*ghūrnijhôṛ*) joins Sanskrit *ghūrṇi*, 'a whirling' (Monier-Williams), to **ঝড়**, a storm: a whirling storm.
