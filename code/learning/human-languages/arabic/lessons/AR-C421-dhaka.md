## You'll want to know: ذكاء
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C421-FURSA421-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ذكاء** — *dhakāʾ* — "intelligence".

> الذكاء وحده لا يكفي.
>
> *adh-dhakāʾ waḥdahu lā yakfī.*
>
> Intelligence alone is not enough.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ذكاء** (*dhakāʾ*) is built on the native Arabic root *dh-k-w*, usually traced to the sense "to be bright, sharp of mind". *Dhakāʾ* is sharpness of mind: intelligence. ذكي, clever, is from the same root.
