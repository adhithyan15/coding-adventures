Russian, chapter 357: Colleagues and managers.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
сотрудник (sotrúdnik) — an employee, a member of staff.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for on the one hand, then the Russian for on the other hand.

You'll want to know: сотрудник.
сотрудник (sotrúdnik) — "an employee, a member of staff".
В нашей фирме двадцать сотрудников. V náshey fírme dvádtsat sotrúdnikov. There are twenty employees in our firm.
Masculine; a woman is сотрудница. Formal; at work people also say коллега.

The word, taken apart.
сотрудник (sotrúdnik) is built from со- "together" and труд "work, labour", the root of трудный "hard": one who works alongside you.

Lesson 2 of 5.
руководитель (rukovodítel) — a manager, a head.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for on the other hand, then the Russian for an employee.

You'll want to know: руководитель.
руководитель (rukovodítel) — "a manager, a head".
Мой руководитель часто ездит в командировки. Moy rukovodítel chásto yézdit v komandiróvki. My manager often goes on business trips.
Masculine, also of a woman. Научный руководитель (rukovodítel) — a thesis supervisor.

The word, taken apart.
руководитель (rukovodítel) is built on руководить (rukovodít), a compound of рука "hand" and водить "to lead", from Proto-Slavic voditi (EtymDB): one who leads others by the hand.

Lesson 3 of 5.
подчинённый (podchinyónnyy) — a subordinate.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for an employee, then the Russian for a manager.

You'll want to know: подчинённый.
подчинённый (podchinyónnyy) — "a subordinate".
Начальник добрый, подчинённые его любят. Nachálnik dóbryy, podchinyónnyye yegó lyúbyat. The boss is kind; his staff love him.
An adjective used as a noun: мой подчинённый (podchinyónnyy), моя подчинённая.

The word, taken apart.
подчинённый (podchinyónnyy) is the participle of подчинить "to put under someone’s authority", built from под "under" and чин "rank, order", the root of причина and чинить.

Lesson 4 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a manager, then the Russian for a subordinate.

You'll want to know: руководить.
руководить (rukovodít) — "to manage, to be in charge of".
Она руководит большой командой. Oná rukovodít bolshóy komándoy. She manages a large team.
Imperfective; present руковожу, руководишь. It takes the instrumental: руководить (rukovodít) отделом.

The word, taken apart.
руководить (rukovodít) is a compound of рука "hand" and водить "to lead", from Proto-Slavic voditi "to lead" (EtymDB): to lead by the hand.

Lesson 5 of 5.
коллектив (kollektív) — a team, the staff.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a subordinate, then the Russian for to manage.

You'll want to know: коллектив.
коллектив (kollektív) — "a team, the staff".
У нас дружный коллектив. U nas drúzhnyy kollektív. We have a close-knit team.
Masculine. The people one works with, taken together.

The word, taken apart.
коллектив (kollektív) is usually traced, through German, to Latin collēctīvus "gathered together", from colligere "to gather together" (Lewis & Short).
