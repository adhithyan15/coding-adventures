# వాఙ్మయం (vāṅmayaṁ) — literature, the whole body of expression in words

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[TE-SKILL-CONNECTED-READING, TE-LEX-C46-ASK-05] -->

[PAUSE 2s] You already know **పుస్తకం**, a book, and you have held a short
passage without translating every word. This lesson names the larger body that
books and spoken works belong to.

## You'll want to know: వాఙ్మయం
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C150-LITERATURE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**వాఙ్మయం** — *vāṅmayaṁ* — "literature", or more broadly, expression made of
words.

Say it in three gentle pieces: *vāṅ · ma · yaṁ*. The first piece ends with the
velar nasal heard in English *sing*. The spelling keeps that sound as **ఙ్**
before **మ**: **వా-ఙ్-మ-యం**.

The word came through Sanskrit: *vāc* is speech, and *maya* means made of or
filled with. A single **పుస్తకం** is one book; **వాఙ్మయం** is the world of words
that books, speeches, and other verbal works make together.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[TE-LEX-C150-LITERATURE-01] -->

One book, then the wider field it can belong to. The unusual spelling has had
a meaning before it becomes a handwriting exercise.
