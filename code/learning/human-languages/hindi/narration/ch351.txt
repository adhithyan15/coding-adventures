Hindi, chapter 351: Rights and Duties.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
अधिकार (adhikār) — a right.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for explicit, then the Hindi for to list.

You'll want to know: अधिकार.
अधिकार — adhikār — "a right".
हर बच्चे को पढ़ने का अधिकार है। har bacce ko paṛhne kā adhikār hai. Every child has the right to study.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
अधिकार (adhikār) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit adhikāra, "authority, jurisdiction" (Monier-Williams). Hindi has narrowed it to a right that someone holds.

Lesson 3 of 5.
पालन करना (pālan karnā) — to abide by, to follow (a rule).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a right, then the Hindi for a duty.

You'll want to know: पालन करना.
पालन करना — pālan karnā — "to abide by, to follow (a rule)".
हमें नियमों का पालन करना चाहिए। hamẽ niyamoṁ kā pālan karnā cāhiye. We should follow the rules.
Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने नियम का पालन किया, usne niyam kā pālan kiyā.

The word, taken apart.
पालन करना (pālan karnā) joins पालन to करना, "to do". पालन is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit pālana, "guarding, protecting" (Monier-Williams). Hindi uses it for keeping to a rule.
