## You'll want to know: پیک
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C456-PEYK-01]; assesses=[] -->

**پیک** (*peyk*) — "a courier, a delivery rider".

> پیک غذا را جلوی در آورد.
>
> *Peyk ghazâ râ jolow-ye dar âvard.*
>
> The delivery rider brought the food to the door.

*Peyk-e motori* — a motorbike courier.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*peyk* is inherited: EtymDB traces it to Middle Persian *payg*, which is usually glossed 'a messenger, a runner'.
