## You'll want to know: بلغ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C348-TUHMA348-05]; assesses=[] -->

**بلغ** — *ballagha* — "to inform the police; to report".

> بلغ الجار الشرطة عن الصوت.
>
> *ballagha al-jār ash-shurṭa ʿan aṣ-ṣawt.*
>
> The neighbour told the police about the noise.

Present: يبلغ, *yuballigh*.

بلغ عن: to report something. The plain verb, *balagha*, is to reach.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**بلغ** (*ballagha*) is built on the native Arabic root *b-l-gh* (Lane), "to reach". It is Form II, "to make news reach someone".
