# शुक्लपक्षः (śuklapakṣaḥ) — the bright fortnight

## You'll want to know: शुक्लपक्षः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C444-CANDRA444-03]; assesses=[] -->

**शुक्लपक्षः** — *śuklapakṣaḥ* — "the bright fortnight".

> शुक्लपक्षे चन्द्रः प्रतिदिनं वर्धते।
>
> *śuklapakṣe candraḥ pratidinaṃ vardhate.*
>
> In the bright fortnight the moon grows every day.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**शुक्लपक्षः** (*śuklapakṣaḥ*) is built from *śukla*, "bright, white", and *pakṣa*, "a wing, a side", which MW also gives as the half of a lunar month; MW gives the compound as the light half of the month (MW).
