# દેવી (devī) — a goddess

## You'll want to know: દેવી
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C332-DANTKATHA332-04]; assesses=[] -->

**દેવી** — *devī* — "a goddess".

> દેવીએ દૈત્યને હરાવ્યો.
>
> *devīe daityane harāvyo.*
>
> The goddess defeated the demon.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**દેવી** (*devī*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *devī*, "a goddess" (Monier-Williams). As the doer she takes **એ**, and the verb agrees with the demon defeated: **દૈત્યને હરાવ્યો**.
