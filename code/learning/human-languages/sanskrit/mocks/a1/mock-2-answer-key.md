## Writing

Score the form for seven relevant fields. Score the message for a greeting,
recognisable identity, book or subject, brief reason, proposed place and time,
question about the arrangement, and polite close to Ravi. Fictional details are
valid and no exact sentence is required. The message must be 30–40 independently
produced Sanskrit words. Apply the analytic bands in the rubric, including
word-final visarga or anusvara, continuous śirorekhā, and unjoined word-boundary
control.

## Speaking

Score communicative achievement rather than verbatim reproduction. The
interview needs relevant personal responses; the description needs a familiar
person, place, activity, and relationship; the role-play needs a fruit choice,
price handling, a request for two, and a polite close. Accept a documented
pronunciation tradition when it is internally consistent. Apply the rubric to
the recording independently; examiner assistance beyond the permitted repeat
is recorded and reflected in interaction scoring.
