## You'll want to know: જાળવવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C356-BANDHARAN356-05]; assesses=[] -->

**જાળવવું** — *jāḷavvũ* — "to preserve".

> આપણે આપણા હક જાળવવા જોઈએ.
>
> *āpaṇe āpṇā hak jāḷavvā joīe.*
>
> We must protect our rights.

Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે જૂનું ઘર જાળવ્યું, *teṇe jūnũ ghar jāḷavyũ*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**જાળવવું** (*jāḷavvũ*) CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb, "to keep unharmed", under a reconstructed *yal*, "endure, stay" (CDIAL 10430).
