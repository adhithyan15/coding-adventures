## You'll want to know: lěng
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C51-FEEL51-03]; assesses=[] -->

*lěng* — "cold".

> *jīntiān hěn lěng* — It's cold today.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*lěng* is inherited from Middle Chinese *lengX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'cold'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying how you are.
