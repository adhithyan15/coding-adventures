## You'll want to know: ઉમળકો
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C447-HARAKH447-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ઉમળકો** — *umaḷko* — "warmth of feeling; eager affection".

> માસીએ ઉમળકાથી બારણું ખોલ્યું.
>
> *māsīe umaḷkāthī bārṇũ kholyũ.*
>
> My aunt opened the door with warm delight.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ઉમળકો** (*umaḷko*) goes back to a reconstructed *\*ummal-*, "rise, bubble up": a feeling that wells over. CDIAL lists the Gujarati word as "emotion, ardent love" (CDIAL 2346).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
