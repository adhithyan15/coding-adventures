# કથળવું (kathaḷvũ) — to deteriorate

## Warm-up

## You'll want to know: કથળવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C387-BAHETAR387-05]; assesses=[] -->

**કથળવું** — *kathaḷvũ* — "to deteriorate".

> જો ખોટ વધશે તો ધંધો કથળશે.
>
> *jo khoṭ vadhśe to dhandho kathaḷśe.*
>
> If the losses grow, the business will go downhill.

Past (it …): તે ધંધો કથળ્યો, *te dhandho kathaḷyo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**કથળવું** (*kathaḷvũ*) CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb, "to be put out of order, to be spoilt", under a reconstructed *kusthala*, "a bad place" (CDIAL 3383).
