## You'll want to know: حضارة
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C318-HADARA318-01]; assesses=[] -->

**حضارة** — *ḥaḍāra* — "a civilization".

> درسنا حضارة قديمة في المدرسة.
>
> *darasnā ḥaḍāra qadīma fī al-madrasa.*
>
> We studied an ancient civilization at school.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**حضارة** (*ḥaḍāra*) is built on the native Arabic root *ḥ-ḍ-r* (Lane), "to be present", the root of *ḥaḍara*, to attend, and of the taught *ḥaḍḍara*, to prepare. Lane gives *ḥaḍāra* as dwelling in towns and villages, the opposite of desert life.
