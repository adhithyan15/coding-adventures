## You'll want to know: سند
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C410-LAYEHE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**سند** (*sanad*) — "a document; a deed".

> سند خانه به نام مادرم است.
>
> *sanad-e khâne be nâm-e mâdaram ast.*
>
> The deed of the house is in my mother's name.

Plural *asnâd*, an Arabic broken plural.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**سند** was borrowed from Arabic *sanad* (EtymDB), a word usually glossed as a support, what a claim leans on; in Persian it is a document or a deed.
