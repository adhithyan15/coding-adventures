<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C139-KHANDAN139-03]; assesses=[] -->

**اجاره** — *ejâre* — "rent". The money for a flat: **اجاره خانه** — *ejâre-ye khâne* — "the rent for the house".
