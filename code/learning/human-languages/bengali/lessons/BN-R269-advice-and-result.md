# Review — ought to, have to, and so

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[BN-GRAMMAR-C269-UCHIT-SHOULD-01, BN-GRAMMAR-C269-TAI-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "You ought to rest" and "so we stayed at home". (*tomār bishrām neoyā uchit*; *tāi āmrā bāṛite thāklām*.)

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[BN-LEX-C269-UCHIT-01, BN-SCRIPT-C269-U-01, BN-GRAMMAR-C269-UCHIT-SHOULD-01, BN-GRAMMAR-C269-TE-HOBE-01, BN-GRAMMAR-C269-TAI-01, BN-GRAMMAR-C269-SEJONNO-01, BN-GRAMMAR-C268-I-THINK-01, BN-GRAMMAR-C268-KARON-BECAUSE-01, BN-LEX-C45-HEALTH45-02, BN-LEX-C64-SCHOOL64-03] -->

[PAUSE 2s each]
- [YOU SAY: I think you ought to go to the doctor.]
- [YOU SAY: You shouldn't go alone at night.]
- [YOU SAY: Tomorrow I have to go to school early.]
- [YOU SAY: There was no bus, so we walked.]
- [YOU SAY: Mira was tired. That's why she stayed at home.]
- [YOU WRITE: **উচিত**, then **কারণ**]

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[BN-LEX-C269-UCHIT-01, BN-SCRIPT-C269-U-01, BN-GRAMMAR-C269-UCHIT-SHOULD-01, BN-GRAMMAR-C269-TE-HOBE-01, BN-GRAMMAR-C269-TAI-01, BN-GRAMMAR-C269-SEJONNO-01] -->

[PAUSE 3s] Advice or a must: **উচিত** or **-তে হবে**? (Advice is **উচিত**.) Which opens the result: **কারণ** or **তাই**? (**তাই**.) Which letter opens **উচিত**? (**উ**.)
