Russian, chapter 364: Society and its people.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
общество (óbshchestvo) — society.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a competitor, then the Russian for to cooperate.

You'll want to know: общество.
общество (óbshchestvo) — "society".
В современном обществе много проблем. V sovreménnom óbshchestve mnógo problém. There are many problems in modern society.
Neuter. It also means a society or club: общество (óbshchestvo) любителей книги.

The word, taken apart.
общество (óbshchestvo) was taken from Church Slavonic obĭščĭstvo (EtymDB), built on общий "common, shared": society is what people have in common.

Lesson 2 of 5.
население (naseléniye) — the population.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to cooperate, then the Russian for society.

You'll want to know: население.
население (naseléniye) — "the population".
Население города — больше миллиона человек. Naseléniye góroda — bólshe millióna chelovék. The population of the town is over a million people.
Neuter. Население страны — a country’s population.

The word, taken apart.
население (naseléniye) is built on населить "to people, to settle", from на- and the root of селить "to settle" and село "a village": the people settled in a place.

Lesson 3 of 5.
общественный (obshchéstvennyy) — public, social.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for society, then the Russian for the population.

You'll want to know: общественный.
общественный (obshchéstvennyy) — "public, social".
Я езжу на работу на общественном транспорте. Ya yézzhu na rabótu na obshchéstvennom tránsporte. I go to work by public transport.
Public opinion is общественное мнение, and public transport is общественный (obshchéstvennyy) транспорт.

The word, taken apart.
общественный (obshchéstvennyy) is the adjective made from общество (óbshchestvo) "society", which Russian took from Church Slavonic obĭščĭstvo (EtymDB).

Lesson 4 of 5.
социальный (sotsiálnyy) — social.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for the population, then the Russian for public.

You'll want to know: социальный.
социальный (sotsiálnyy) — "social".
Он занимается социальными проблемами. On zanimáyetsya sotsiálnymi problémami. He deals with social problems.
Социальные сети — social networks. Социальная помощь — welfare.

The word, taken apart.
социальный (sotsiálnyy) is usually traced, through western languages, to Latin sociālis "of companionship, of allies", built on socius "a companion" (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 5 of 5.
житель (zhítel) — an inhabitant, a resident.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for public, then the Russian for social.

You'll want to know: житель.
житель (zhítel) — "an inhabitant, a resident".
Жители деревни против новой дороги. Zhíteli derévni prótiv nóvoy dorógi. The villagers are against the new road.
Masculine; a woman is жительница. Жители города — the people of a town.

The word, taken apart.
житель (zhítel) is built on жить "to live", inherited from Proto-Slavic žiti, with the agent suffix -тель: one who lives there.
