## You'll want to know: tuìhuò
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C202-TILL202-03]; assesses=[] -->

*tuìhuò* — "to take goods back; to return goods to a shop".

> *zhège kěyǐ tuìhuò ma* — Can I bring this back?

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*tuìhuò* is built from *tuì* 'to give back' and *huò* 'goods', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *thwojH* and *xwaH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'to draw back' and 'wealth'.
