## You'll want to know: रूळ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C396-AAGGADI396-02]; assesses=[] -->

**रूळ** — *rūḷ* — "a rail; a railway track".

> आगगाडी रुळावरून घसरली.
>
> *āggāḍī ruḷāvarūn ghasarlī.*
>
> The train came off the rails.

Masculine.

Before an ending the long ऊ shortens: रूळ, but रुळावर, on the rails.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**रूळ** (*rūḷ*) names one steel rail of a track, and in the plural the line itself; where the word comes from is not settled.
