## You'll want to know: せき
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C215-BODY215-05]; assesses=[] -->

**せき** — *seki* — "a cough".

> **せきは でません。** — *seki wa demasen* — "I do not have a cough."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**せき** (*seki*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji spelling came later, and its older origin is not settled (JMdict).
