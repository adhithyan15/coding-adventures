## You'll want to know: ضاحية
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C454-IMARA454-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ضاحية** — *ḍāḥiya* — "a suburb".

> يسكن عمي في ضاحية بعيدة عن المركز.
>
> *yaskunu ʿammī fī ḍāḥiya baʿīda ʿan al-markaz.*
>
> My uncle lives in a suburb far from the centre.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ضاحية** (*ḍāḥiya*) is built on the native Arabic root *ḍ-ḥ-w*, "to be in the sun; to come into view" (Lane). Lane gives *ḍāḥiya* as the outer, exposed side or tract of a place; *ḍuḥā*, "late morning", shares the root.
