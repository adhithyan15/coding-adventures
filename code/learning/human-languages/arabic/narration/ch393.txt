Arabic, chapter 393: Tests and admission.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
اختبار (ikhtibār) — a test.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a supervisor, then the Arabic for to study for.

You'll want to know: اختبار.
اختبار — ikhtibār — "a test".
نجحت في اختبار القيادة. najaḥtu fī ikhtibār al-qiyāda. I passed the driving test.

The word, taken apart.
اختبار (ikhtibār) is built on the native Arabic root kh-b-r, "to know thoroughly, to test" (Lane). Ikhtibār is the Form VIII verbal noun of ikhtabara, to test: a test.

Lesson 4 of 5.
تفوق (tafawwaqa) — to excel.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for admission, then the Arabic for an average.

You'll want to know: تفوق.
تفوق — tafawwaqa — "to excel".
تفوق ابن جارنا في الرياضيات. tafawwaqa ibn jārinā fī ar-riyāḍiyyāt. Our neighbour's son excelled in mathematics.
Present: يتفوق, yatafawwaq.
It takes في for the field, and على for the people one beats.

The word, taken apart.
تفوق (tafawwaqa) is built on the native Arabic root f-w-q, "to be above" (Lane). It is Form V, to rise above others: to excel. فوق, above, is from the same root.

[pause 2 seconds]

قيم المعلم عمل كل طالب. qayyama al-muʿallim ʿamal kulli ṭālib. The teacher evaluated each student's work.

The word, taken apart.
قيم (qayyama) is built on the native Arabic root q-w-m, "to stand, to rise" (Lane). It is Form II, to set a value on a thing: to evaluate. قيمة, a value, is from the same root.
