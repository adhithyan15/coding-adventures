French, chapter 432: Parler au gérant.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
le gérant — the manager.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for to disturb, then the French for unhappy.

You'll want to know: le gérant.
le gérant — "the manager".
Le gérant de l'hôtel est venu voir la chambre. The hotel manager came to check the room.
Feminine la gérante.

The word, taken apart.
gérant is built on gérer 'to manage', a learned borrowing from Latin gerere 'to carry on, to run' (Lewis & Short). EtymDB lists it without a source.

What you've built.
One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.

Lesson 2 of 5.
le désagrément — the inconvenience, the nuisance.

Warm-up.

Before the new one: say the French for unhappy, then the French for the manager.

You'll want to know: le désagrément.
le désagrément — "the inconvenience, the nuisance".
Nous sommes désolés pour ce désagrément. We are sorry for the inconvenience.
A polite word in apologies.

The word, taken apart.
désagrément is built from dés- and agrément 'pleasure', from agréer 'to please', from gré 'liking', which goes back to Latin grātum 'something pleasing' (Lewis & Short). EtymDB does not list it.

Lesson 4 of 5.
le ménage — the cleaning, the housework; the household.

You'll want to know: le ménage.
le ménage — "the cleaning, the housework; the household".
Le ménage n'a pas été fait dans notre chambre. Our room has not been cleaned.
Faire le ménage — to do the cleaning.

The word, taken apart.
ménage comes from Old French manage 'dwelling, household' (EtymDB), usually traced to a word built on Latin manēre 'to stay'.

Lesson 5 of 5.
défectueux — faulty, defective.

Warm-up.

You'll want to know: défectueux.
défectueux — "faulty, defective".
La télévision est défectueuse depuis notre arrivée. The television has been faulty since we arrived.

The word, taken apart.
défectueux is a learned borrowing from Medieval Latin dēfectuōsus, from dēfectus 'lack', from dēficere 'to fail', built on facere 'to do' (Lewis & Short).
