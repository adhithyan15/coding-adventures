Lesson 1 of 6.
અંગારો (angāro) — an ember.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to hold back, then the Gujarati for safe.

You'll want to know: અંગારો.
અંગારો — angāro — "an ember".
ચૂલામાં હજી અંગારા સળગતા હતા. cūlāmā̃ hajī angārā saḷagtā hatā. Embers were still glowing in the stove.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
અંગારો (angāro) is inherited from Sanskrit aṅgāra, "charcoal", and CDIAL lists Gujarati ãgāro, "glowing charcoal" (CDIAL 125). Embers stay in a stove after the flames have gone: અંગારા.

Lesson 3 of 6.
જ્વાળા (jvāḷā) — a flame.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for an ember, then the Gujarati for smoke.

You'll want to know: જ્વાળા.
જ્વાળા — jvāḷā — "a flame".
આગની જ્વાળાઓ છત સુધી પહોંચી. āgnī jvāḷāo chat sudhī pahõcī. The flames reached the ceiling.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
જ્વાળા (jvāḷā) is a tatsama, Sanskrit jvālā, "a flame, a blaze" (Monier-Williams). It is feminine, and fire comes in the plural: જ્વાળાઓ, flames.

Lesson 4 of 6.
સળગાવવું (saḷgāvvũ) — to set alight; to light.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for smoke, then the Gujarati for a flame.

You'll want to know: સળગાવવું.
સળગાવવું — saḷgāvvũ — "to set alight; to light".
કોઈએ કચરો સળગાવ્યો અને આગ ફેલાઈ ગઈ. koīe kacro saḷgāvyo ane āg phelāī gaī. Someone set fire to the rubbish and the fire spread.
Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે ચૂલો સળગાવ્યો, teṇe cūlo saḷgāvyo.

The word, taken apart.
સળગાવવું (saḷgāvvũ) is the causative of સળગવું, to catch fire, which goes back to Sanskrit saṁlagna, "closely attached"; CDIAL lists both verbs (CDIAL 12999). You light a stove with it too: ચૂલો સળગાવ્યો.

You'll want to know: દાઝવું.
દાઝવું — dājhvũ — "to be scalded; to get a burn".
ગરમ તેલથી મીરાનો હાથ દાઝી ગયો. garam telthī Mīrāno hāth dājhī gayo. Mira burned her hand on hot oil.
Past (he …): તે છોકરો દાઝી ગયો, te chokro dājhī gayo.

The word, taken apart.
દાઝવું (dājhvũ) is inherited from Sanskrit dahyátē, "is burnt", and CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb, "to be burnt, be scalded" (CDIAL 6248). It is skin that દાઝે છે; wood and paper બળે છે.
