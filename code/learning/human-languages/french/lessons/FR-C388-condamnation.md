## You'll want to know: la condamnation
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C388-PEINE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**la condamnation** — "the conviction, the sentence; the condemnation".

> Après sa condamnation, il a perdu son emploi.
>
> *After his conviction, he lost his job.*

The *m* is silent here too.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*condamnation* is a learned borrowing from Latin *condemnātiō* "a condemnation" (Lewis & Short), from *condemnāre* "to sentence", built on *damnum* "loss, harm".
