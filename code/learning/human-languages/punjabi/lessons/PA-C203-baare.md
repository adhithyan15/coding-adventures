# One postposition for about

## Warm-up

[PAUSE 2s] Retrieve the familiar noun for a book, then leave a silent space
after it.

## Grammar Lens: direct owner
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-GRAMMAR-BAARE-203]; assesses=[] -->

> **ਬਾਰੇ** — *bāre* — about; concerning

Hear and say the whole word before reading it. Keep it as one whole
postposition. Punjabi places a postposition after its noun; the next lesson
will retrieve this new word before the familiar noun returns.

## The word, taken apart — where the trail runs out
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-GRAMMAR-BAARE-203] -->

Punjabi University Patiala explains the after-the-noun position. A dictionary
entry records the “about / concerning” meaning and cites Bhai Maya Singh's 1895
dictionary. Punjab School Education Board Class 5 material independently uses
the word in ordinary school prompts.
Sources: [Punjabi University Patiala](https://www.learnpunjabi.org/postposition.html),
[dictionary entry](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/%E0%A8%AC%E0%A8%BE%E0%A8%B0%E0%A9%87),
and [PSEB Class 5](https://static.pseb.ac.in/media/1654685607_5th%20Punjabi-second-language.pdf).

The exact historical derivation of modern written **ਬਾਰੇ** remains unknown in
the cited sources. Its origin remains unknown here. This lesson therefore makes
no etymological claim. Keep the present-day word whole.
