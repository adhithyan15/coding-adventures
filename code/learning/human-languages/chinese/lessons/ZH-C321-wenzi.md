## You'll want to know: 文字
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C321-ANCIENT321-05]; assesses=[] -->

**文字** — *wénzì* — "script; written language".

> **汉字是很古老的文字** *Hànzì shì hěn gǔlǎo de wénzì* — Chinese characters are a very old script.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**文字** (*wénzì*) is built from 文 'writing' and 字 'a character', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *mjun* and *dziH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'writing' and 'beautiful'; for the second it gives 'an ornament' and 'to nurse', and the sense 'a written character' is not given there.
