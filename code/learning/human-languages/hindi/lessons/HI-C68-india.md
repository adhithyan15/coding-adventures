## You'll want to know: भारत
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C68-ORIGIN-02]; assesses=[] -->

**भारत** (*Bhārat*) — "India", the country's name in its own language.

The name is older than the country. भरत (*Bharata*) is a figure the Sanskrit
epics name, and भारत is the form meaning "belonging to Bharata" — the land of
his people. English *India* took a different route entirely, through Greek and
Persian from सिंधु (*Sindhu*), the river.

So one country carries two names that started as two different things: a people
and a river. In Hindi you will hear भारत on the news and हिंदुस्तान in the
street, and both mean the same place.
