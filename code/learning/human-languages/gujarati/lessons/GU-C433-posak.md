## You'll want to know: પોશાક
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C433-OUTFIT433-01]; assesses=[] -->

**પોશાક** — *pośāk* — "an outfit; a costume".

> લગ્નમાં તેણે નવો પોશાક પહેર્યો.
>
> *lagnamā̃ teṇe navo pośāk paheryo.*
>
> He wore a new outfit at the wedding.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**પોશાક** (*pośāk*) is usually traced to Persian *pōšāk*, "clothing, dress".
