## You'll want to know: まんが

**まんが** — *manga* — "comics; a comic book".

> **きのう おとうとは まんがを よみました。** — *kinō otōto wa manga o yomimashita* — "Yesterday my younger brother read comics."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**まんが** (*manga*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *man*, "unrestrained" (Middle Chinese *manH*) and *ga*, "picture" (*hweaH*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
