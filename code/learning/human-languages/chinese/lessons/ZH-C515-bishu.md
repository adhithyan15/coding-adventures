## You'll want to know: bìshǔ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C515-SEASONS515-04]; assesses=[] -->

*bìshǔ* — "to escape the summer heat".

> *xiàtiān wǒ hé fùmǔ qù shān lǐ bìshǔ* — In summer I go up into the hills with my parents to get away from the heat.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*bìshǔ* is built from *bì* 'to avoid' and *shǔ* 'heat', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *bjieH* and *syoX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to turn away from' and the second 'heat'.
