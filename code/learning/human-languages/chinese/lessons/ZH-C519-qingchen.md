## You'll want to know: qīngchén
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C519-OUTDOORS519-02]; assesses=[] -->

*qīngchén* — "early in the morning; at daybreak".

> *fùqīn měitiān qīngchén qù gōngyuán pǎobù* — My father goes running in the park early every morning.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*qīngchén* is built from *qīng* 'clear' and *chén* 'dawn', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *tshjeng* and *dzyin* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'clear, still' and the second 'early' and 'bright'.
