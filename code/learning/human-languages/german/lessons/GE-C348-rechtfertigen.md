## You'll want to know: rechtfertigen
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C348-VERANTWORTUNG-03]; assesses=[] -->

**rechtfertigen** — "to justify (oneself)".

> Er musste sich vor seinem Chef rechtfertigen.
>
> *He had to justify himself to his boss.*

Inseparable, although it looks like a compound: *er rechtfertigt sich, er hat sich gerechtfertigt*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*rechtfertigen* is built from *recht* "right", from Proto-Germanic *rehtaz* (EtymDB), and *fertigen* "to make ready", from *fertig*: to set something right.
