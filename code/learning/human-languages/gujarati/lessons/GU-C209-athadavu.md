# અથડાવું (athaḍāvũ) — to collide, to bump into

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-LEX-C209-BREAKDOWN209-01, GU-LEX-C209-BREAKDOWN209-02] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Gujarati for to break down, then the Gujarati for to push.

## You'll want to know: અથડાવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C209-BREAKDOWN209-03]; assesses=[] -->

**અથડાવું** — *athaḍāvũ* — "to collide, to bump into".

> **એક સાઈકલ ગાડી સાથે અથડાઈ.** — *ek saikal gāḍī sāthe athaḍāī* — "A bicycle ran into the car."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**અથડાવું** (*athaḍāvũ*) is a tadbhava. CDIAL lists *athaṛvũ*, "to come into collision with", under *\*āstudati*, "strikes against", a reconstructed form (CDIAL 1510).
