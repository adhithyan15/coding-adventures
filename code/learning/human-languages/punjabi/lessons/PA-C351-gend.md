## You'll want to know: ਗੇਂਦ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C351-BALL351-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ਗੇਂਦ** — *gend* — "a ball".

> ਬੱਚੇ ਬਾਹਰ ਗੇਂਦ ਨਾਲ ਖੇਡਦੇ ਹਨ।
>
> *bachche bāhar gend nāl kheḍde han.*
>
> The children play outside with a ball.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਗੇਂਦ** (*gend*) is inherited from Sanskrit *genduka*, "a ball for play"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *gẽd*, "ball", under it (CDIAL 4248).
