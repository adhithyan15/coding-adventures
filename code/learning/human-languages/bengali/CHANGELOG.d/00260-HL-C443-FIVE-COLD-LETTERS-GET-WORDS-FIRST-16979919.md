## HL-C443: five cold letters get words first

Gentle writing asks for every letter to be taken from a word the reader
already says. Five Bengali letter lessons came before any word holding their
letter. Each now follows a short word lesson that holds it:

| letter | word placed just before it |
|---|---|
| ই | **ইট** (*iṭ*, a brick) |
| এ | **একা** (*ekā*, alone) |
| জ | **জাল** (*jāl*, a net) |
| ও | **ওজন** (*ojôn*, weight) |
| ় | **পাড়া** (*pāṛā*, a neighbourhood) |

Each word is revisited by its letter lesson and by the lesson after it.
