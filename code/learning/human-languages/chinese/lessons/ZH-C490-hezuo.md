## You'll want to know: hézuò
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C490-PRESSURE490-02]; assesses=[] -->

*hézuò* — "to work together; to cooperate".

> *gēn tā hézuò wǒ gǎnjué hěn kāixīn* — I really enjoy working with him.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*hézuò* is built from *hé* 'to join' and 作 'to do', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *hop* and *tsak* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to join together' and the second 'to do' and 'to make'.
