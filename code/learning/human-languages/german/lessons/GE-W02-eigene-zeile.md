# Your own line — the first writing with no model

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GE-LEX-HALLO-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] You have written **Hallo** four ways: traced, copied, copied from
memory, and written from the sound alone.

[PAUSE 1s] Every one of those had something to reach for. This one does not.

## Writing — controlled composition
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GE-LEX-MEIN-01, GE-LEX-UND-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: controlled-composition -->

[PAUSE 2s] Write **one line** about yourself: a greeting, then your name.

Nothing is supplied. You choose the words, and the supply you are choosing from
is closed — only what this book has taught you.

[PAUSE 2s] Before you write, one thing to decide deliberately rather than by
accident: **which nouns get a capital?** In German the answer is *all of them*,
not only the ones that name a person or a place. Your line will have at least
one, and an English-trained hand will lower-case it without noticing.

[PAUSE 3s] Write the line. Then read it back and check the capitals **before**
you check anything else.

## Wrap-up recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GE-LEX-MEIN-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Which German nouns take a capital letter? (**All of them.**)

[PAUSE 2s] And what did you have to choose, that the last four lessons chose for
you? (**The words.**)
