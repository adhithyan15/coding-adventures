Kannada, chapter 491: Grief and Regret.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಕೊರಗು (koragu) — to pine; to brood.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for embarrassment, then the Kannada for unease.

You'll want to know: ಕೊರಗು.
ಕೊರಗು — koragu — "to pine; to brood".
ಕೆಲಸ ಹೋದ ಮೇಲೆ ಅವನು ದಿನವೂ ಕೊರಗುತ್ತಿದ್ದನು. kelasa hōda mēle avanu dinavū koraguttiddanu. After he lost his job, he used to brood every day.
Past (I …): ಕೊರಗಿದೆ, koragide.
A quiet, long sorrow kept inside: ಮನಸ್ಸಿನಲ್ಲೇ ಕೊರಗು (koragu), to grieve in silence.

The word, taken apart.
ಕೊರಗು (koragu) is native Dravidian: DEDR 1778 gives Kannada koragu, "to lose its sap, shrivel, waste away", and also "to sorrow". A grief that dries a person up is the picture behind it.

Lesson 2 of 5.
ಮರುಗು (marugu) — to feel pity; to grieve for.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for unease, then the Kannada for to pine.

You'll want to know: ಮರುಗು.
ಮರುಗು — marugu — "to feel pity; to grieve for".
ಗಾಯಗೊಂಡ ಹಕ್ಕಿಯನ್ನು ನೋಡಿ ಮಗು ಮರುಗಿತು. gāyagoṇḍa hakkiyannu nōḍi magu marugitu. Seeing the injured bird, the child felt sorry for it.
Past (I …): ಮರುಗಿದೆ, marugide.
The one you feel for takes -ಗಾಗಿ: ಅವನಿಗಾಗಿ ಮರುಗು (marugu), to feel for him.

The word, taken apart.
ಮರುಗು (marugu) is native Dravidian: DEDR 4769 lists Kannada maṟugu, "to burn, be very hot", and from there "to suffer, be distressed, grieve". Pity is pictured as a burning inside.

Lesson 3 of 5.
ಸಂಕಟ (saṅkaṭa) — anguish; distress.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to pine, then the Kannada for to feel pity.

You'll want to know: ಸಂಕಟ.
ಸಂಕಟ — saṅkaṭa — "anguish; distress".
ಅಜ್ಜಿಯ ಕಾಯಿಲೆ ನೋಡಿ ನನಗೆ ಸಂಕಟ ಆಯಿತು. ajjiya kāyile nōḍi nanage saṅkaṭa āyitu. Seeing grandmother's illness, I felt anguish.
A tight, pressing pain of the mind, and also a tight spot: ಸಂಕಟದಲ್ಲಿ ಸಿಕ್ಕಿಕೊ, to be caught in a crisis.

The word, taken apart.
ಸಂಕಟ (saṅkaṭa) is borrowed from Sanskrit saṃkaṭa, "brought together, narrow, crowded; dangerous, critical" (Monier-Williams). Kannada uses it for a narrow place of the mind: distress.

Lesson 4 of 5.
ವಿಷಾದ (viṣāda) — regret; sorrow.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to feel pity, then the Kannada for anguish.

You'll want to know: ವಿಷಾದ.
ವಿಷಾದ — viṣāda — "regret; sorrow".
ಆ ತಪ್ಪಿಗೆ ನನಗೆ ತುಂಬಾ ವಿಷಾದ ಇದೆ. ā tappige nanage tumbā viṣāda ide. I deeply regret that mistake.
Formal and polite: notices say ವಿಷಾದಿಸುತ್ತೇವೆ, we regret.

The word, taken apart.
ವಿಷಾದ (viṣāda) is borrowed from Sanskrit viṣāda, "a drooping state, languor" and so dejection (Monier-Williams); in Kannada it is the sorrow of regret.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ಪಶ್ಚಾತ್ತಾಪ (paścāttāpa) — remorse; repentance.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for anguish, then the Kannada for regret.

You'll want to know: ಪಶ್ಚಾತ್ತಾಪ.
ಪಶ್ಚಾತ್ತಾಪ — paścāttāpa — "remorse; repentance".
ಸುಳ್ಳು ಹೇಳಿದ್ದಕ್ಕೆ ಅವನಿಗೆ ಪಶ್ಚಾತ್ತಾಪ ಆಯಿತು. suḷḷu hēḷiddakke avanige paścāttāpa āyitu. He felt remorse for having lied.
For what: -ಕ್ಕೆ. ಪಶ್ಚಾತ್ತಾಪ (paścāttāpa) ಪಡು, to repent.

The word, taken apart.
ಪಶ್ಚಾತ್ತಾಪ (paścāttāpa) is borrowed from Sanskrit paścāttāpa, literally "after-pain": sorrow, regret, repentance (Monier-Williams). It is built from paścāt, "afterwards", and tāpa, "heat, pain".
