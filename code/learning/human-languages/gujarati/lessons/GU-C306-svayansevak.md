## You'll want to know: સ્વયંસેવક
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C306-RAHAT306-01]; assesses=[] -->

**સ્વયંસેવક** — *svayansevak* — "a volunteer".

> એક સ્વયંસેવકે બાળકોને દૂધ આપ્યું.
>
> *ek svayansevke bāḷkone dūdh āpyũ.*
>
> A volunteer gave the children milk.

A masculine or feminine noun, by the person meant.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**સ્વયંસેવક** (*svayansevak*) is built from Sanskrit *svayam*, "oneself, of one's own accord", and *sevaka*, "one who serves" (Monier-Williams): someone who serves of their own will.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
