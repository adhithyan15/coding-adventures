## You'll want to know: la dragée
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C311-OUI-05]; assesses=[] -->

**la dragée** — "the sugared almond".

> Les invités ont reçu des dragées.
>
> *The guests were given sugared almonds.*

Given at weddings and christenings in France and Belgium.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*dragée* comes from Old French *dragie* (EtymDB), usually traced to Latin *tragēmata* "sweets, dessert", a borrowing from Greek.
