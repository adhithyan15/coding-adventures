## You'll want to know: あてる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C509-LOTTERY509-05]; assesses=[] -->

**あてる** — *ateru* — "to hit (the mark); to guess right".

> **もし こたえを あてたら、おかしを かいます。** — *moshi kotae o atetara, okashi o kaimasu* — "If you guess the answer, I will buy you some sweets."

Polite **あてます**, *atemasu*; an ichidan verb. Past (polite): **おとうとが こたえを あてました。**, *otōto ga kotae o atemashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**あてる** (*ateru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi, and its older origin is not settled (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
