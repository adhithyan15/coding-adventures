## You'll want to know: cupīdō, cupīdinis
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C500-CUPI-01]; assesses=[] -->

**cupīdō, cupīdinis** — "desire, longing".

> Cupīdō pecūniae multōs hominēs malōs facit.
>
> *Desire for money makes many people bad.*

Feminine in prose, third declension; as the god of love it is masculine. Built on *cupiō*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**cupīdō** is built on *cupiō* "to desire" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short); *cupiditās*, already known, comes from the same verb, and English *cupidity* from that.
