## You'll want to know: జననం
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C334-JANANAM334-01]; assesses=[] -->

**జననం** — *jananaṁ* — "a birth".

> ఆ పాప జననం ఆసుపత్రిలో జరిగింది.
>
> *ā pāpa jananaṁ āsupatrilō jarigindi.*
>
> The baby's birth took place in the hospital.

A formal word for birth: జనన తేదీ is the date of birth on a form.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**జననం** (*jananaṁ*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *janana*, "birth" (CDIAL 5099), from the root *jan*, "to be born".
