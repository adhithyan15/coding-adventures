Marathi, chapter 430: Payments and Donations.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
मोबदला (mobadlā) — a payment in return.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to recover money, then the Marathi for a lease.

You'll want to know: मोबदला.
मोबदला — mobadlā — "a payment in return".
कामाचा योग्य मोबदला मिळाला पाहिजे. kāmācā yogya mobadlā miḷālā pāhije. Work should get a fair payment.
Masculine.
A fee or compensation for work or for land taken.

The word, taken apart.
मोबदला (mobadlā) is a loan through Persian, usually traced to Arabic mubādala, "an exchange".

Lesson 2 of 5.
देणगी (deṇgī) — a donation.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a lease, then the Marathi for a payment in return.

You'll want to know: देणगी.
देणगी — deṇgī — "a donation".
त्यांनी शाळेला मोठी देणगी दिली. tyānnī śāḷelā moṭhī deṇgī dilī. They gave the school a large donation.
Feminine.
Built on देणे, to give; the receiver takes -ला.

The word, taken apart.
देणगी (deṇgī) is built on देणे, "to give", with a noun ending; the shape of the ending is not settled.

Lesson 3 of 5.
वर्गणी (vargaṇī) — a subscription; a contribution.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a payment in return, then the Marathi for a donation.

You'll want to know: वर्गणी.
वर्गणी — vargaṇī — "a subscription; a contribution".
उत्सवासाठी सगळ्यांनी वर्गणी दिली. utsavāsāṭhī sagaḷyānnī vargaṇī dilī. Everyone gave a contribution for the festival.
Feminine.
A share each member pays: मासिक वर्गणी (vargaṇī), a monthly subscription.

The word, taken apart.
वर्गणी (vargaṇī) is a Marathi word for a share paid into a common fund; its origin is not settled.

तिघा भावांनी शेताचे तीन हिस्से केले. tighā bhāvānnī śetāce tīn hisse kele. The three brothers split the field into three shares.
Masculine. Plural: हिस्से, hisse.

Lesson 5 of 5.
उरणे (urṇe) — to be left over.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a subscription, then the Marathi for a share.

You'll want to know: उरणे.
उरणे — urṇe — "to be left over".
जेवणानंतर थोडा भात उरला. jevṇānantar thoḍā bhāt urlā. After the meal a little rice was left over.
Past (he …): तो उरला, to urlā.
What is left is the subject: पैसे उरले, money was left.

The word, taken apart.
उरणे (urṇe) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi urṇẽ, "to be left as a residue", under urvara, "surplus, left over" (CDIAL 2356).
