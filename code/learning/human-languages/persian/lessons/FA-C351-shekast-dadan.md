## You'll want to know: شکست دادن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C351-SEPAH-04]; assesses=[] -->

**شکست دادن** (*shekast dâdan*) — "to defeat, to beat".

> سپاه کوچک دشمن را شکست داد.
>
> *Sepâh-e kuchak doshman râ shekast dâd.*
>
> The small army defeated the enemy.

*shekast* "a break, a defeat" + *dâdan* "to give". Present *shekast midahad*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*shekast* "a break, a defeat" is the past stem of the native verb *shekastan* "to break", and *dâdan* "to give" is inherited Persian: to give someone a defeat.
