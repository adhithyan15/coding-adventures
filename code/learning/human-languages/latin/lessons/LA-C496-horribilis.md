## You'll want to know: horribilis, horribile
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C496-PAV-04]; assesses=[] -->

**horribilis, horribile** — "dreadful, horrible".

> Tempestās horribilis nāvem frēgit.
>
> *A dreadful storm broke the ship.*

Two endings. From *horreō* — to bristle, to shudder. English *horrible*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**horribilis** is built on *horreō* "to bristle, to shudder" with *-ibilis* (Lewis & Short; EtymDB). English *horrible* comes from it.
