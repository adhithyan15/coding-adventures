## You'll want to know: সুপারিশ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C536-ROKOM536-01]; assesses=[] -->

**সুপারিশ** — *supārish* — "a recommendation".

> ডাক্তার সুপারিশ করলে আমি এই ওষুধটা কিনব।
>
> *Ḍāktār supārish kôrle āmi ei oshudhṭā kinbô.*
>
> If the doctor recommends it, I'll buy this medicine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**সুপারিশ** (*supārish*) is usually traced to Persian *sifārish*, "a commission, a recommendation".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
