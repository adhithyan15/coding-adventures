## You'll want to know: xǐyīfěn
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C179-WASH179-02]; assesses=[] -->

*xǐyīfěn* — "washing powder".

> *xǐyīfěn méiyǒu le* — We're out of washing powder.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*xǐyīfěn* is built from 洗 'to wash', 衣 'clothes' and *fěn* 'powder', all three inherited from Middle Chinese, *sejX*, *'j+j* and *pjunX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), whose example for the last is a white powder made from lead.
