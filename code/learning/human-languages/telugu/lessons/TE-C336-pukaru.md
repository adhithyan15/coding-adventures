# పుకారు (pukāru) — a rumour

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[TE-LEX-C335-KIRITAM335-04, TE-LEX-C335-KIRITAM335-05] -->

## You'll want to know: పుకారు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C336-PUKARU336-01]; assesses=[] -->

**పుకారు** — *pukāru* — "a rumour".

> బజారులో ఒక పుకారు వినిపించింది.
>
> *bajārulō oka pukāru vinipiñcindi.*
>
> A rumour was going round the market.

పుకారు పుట్టించు, to start a rumour.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**పుకారు** (*pukāru*) is the everyday Telugu word for a rumour; its origin is not settled.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
