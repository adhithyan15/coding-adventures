## You'll want to know: der Hustensaft
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C479-SCHNUPFEN-03]; assesses=[] -->

**der Hustensaft** — "cough syrup".

> Der Hustensaft schmeckt nach Kirschen.
>
> *The cough syrup tastes of cherries.*

Plural: *die Hustensäfte*. Built on *der Husten* — cough, and *der Saft* — juice.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Hustensaft* is built on *Husten* "cough", from Old High German *huosto*, and *Saft* "juice", from Middle High German *saf* (EtymDB).
