## You'll want to know: bǐ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C104-DESK104-02]; assesses=[] -->

*bǐ* — "pen".

> *nǐ yǒu bǐ ma* — Have you got a pen?

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*bǐ* is inherited from Middle Chinese *pit* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), the writing brush; the dictionary repeats a story that a general of the Qin dynasty made the first one.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for everyday things.
