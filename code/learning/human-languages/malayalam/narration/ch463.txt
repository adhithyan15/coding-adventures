Malayalam, chapter 463: Taking Off.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ടെർമിനൽ (ṭerminal) — a terminal.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for to make a booking, then the Malayalam for to buy a ticket.

You'll want to know: ടെർമിനൽ.
ടെർമിനൽ — ṭerminal — "a terminal".
വിമാനത്താവളത്തിൽ രണ്ട് ടെർമിനലുകൾ ഉണ്ട്. vimānattāvaḷattil raṇṭŭ ṭerminalukaḷ uṇṭŭ. The airport has two terminals.
Plural: ടെർമിനലുകൾ, ṭerminalukaḷ.
Plural: add -ഉകൾ, as for most loans that end in a chillu.

The word, taken apart.
ടെർമിനൽ (ṭerminal) was borrowed from English terminal, a building where journeys begin and end.

You'll want to know: റൺവേ.
റൺവേ — ṟaṇvē — "a runway".
വിമാനം റൺവേയിലൂടെ വേഗത്തിൽ ഓടി. vimānaṁ ṟaṇvēyilūṭe vēgattil ōṭi. The plane ran fast along the runway.

The word, taken apart.
റൺവേ (ṟaṇvē) was borrowed from English runway, the strip where planes land and take off.

Lesson 3 of 5.
വിമാനക്കമ്പനി (vimānakkampani) — an airline.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for a terminal, then the Malayalam for a runway.

You'll want to know: വിമാനക്കമ്പനി.
വിമാനക്കമ്പനി — vimānakkampani — "an airline".
ആ വിമാനക്കമ്പനിയുടെ ടിക്കറ്റിന് വില കുറവാണ്. ā vimānakkampaniyuṭe ṭikkaṟṟinŭ vila kuṟavāṇŭ. That airline's tickets are cheap.
Literally a plane company: വിമാനം, a plane, plus കമ്പനി. In the joined word the -ം of വിമാനം drops and the ക of കമ്പനി doubles.

The word, taken apart.
വിമാനക്കമ്പനി (vimānakkampani) is a Malayalam compound built from വിമാനം, a plane, borrowed from Sanskrit vimāna, a car of the gods that moves through the air (CDIAL 11823), and കമ്പനി, which was borrowed from English company.

Lesson 4 of 5.
പറന്നുയരുക (paṟannuyaruka) — to lift off; to take off.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for a runway, then the Malayalam for an airline.

You'll want to know: പറന്നുയരുക.
പറന്നുയരുക — paṟannuyaruka — "to lift off; to take off".
വിമാനം കൃത്യസമയത്ത് പറന്നുയർന്നു. vimānaṁ kṛtyasamayattŭ paṟannuyarnnu. The plane took off on time.
Past: പറന്നുയർന്നു, paṟannuyarnnu.
Two verbs joined: പറന്ന്, having flown, and ഉയരുക, to rise. Landing is ഇറങ്ങുക, to come down.

The word, taken apart.
പറന്നുയരുക (paṟannuyaruka) is native Dravidian, a compound of പറക്കുക, to fly (DEDR 4020), and ഉയരുക, to rise: the plane flies and rises.

വിമാനത്താവളത്തിൽ ഞാൻ ബാഗുകൾ ഒരു ട്രോളിയിൽ വച്ചു. vimānattāvaḷattil ñān bāgukaḷ oru ṭrōḷiyil vaccu. At the airport I put the bags on a trolley.
A shopping trolley in a supermarket is a ട്രോളി (ṭrōḷi) too.

The word, taken apart.
ട്രോളി (ṭrōḷi) was borrowed from English trolley, a cart on wheels for bags.
