Malayalam, chapter 361: Ages and Occasions.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
യുഗം (yugaṁ) — an era; an age.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for luck, then the Malayalam for as fate willed.

You'll want to know: യുഗം.
യുഗം — yugaṁ — "an era; an age".
ഇത് ഇന്റർനെറ്റിന്റെ യുഗമാണ്. itŭ inṟarneṟṟinṟe yugamāṇŭ. This is the age of the internet.
In old tales a യുഗം (yugaṁ) is one of the great ages of the world; today it is any era.

The word, taken apart.
യുഗം (yugaṁ) is borrowed from Sanskrit yuga, first "a yoke, a pair" (CDIAL 10482), and in later Sanskrit also an age of the world (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 2 of 5.
ഭൂതകാലം (bhūtakālaṁ) — the past.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for as fate willed, then the Malayalam for an era.

You'll want to know: ഭൂതകാലം.
ഭൂതകാലം — bhūtakālaṁ — "the past".
അവൻ ഭൂതകാലത്തെക്കുറിച്ച് ഒന്നും പറഞ്ഞില്ല. avan bhūtakālattekkuṟiccŭ onnuṁ paṟaññilla. He said nothing about the past.
In grammar, the past tense. ഭാവി, the future, is its opposite.

The word, taken apart.
ഭൂതകാലം (bhūtakālaṁ) is borrowed from Sanskrit bhūtakāla, "past time; the past tense" (Monier-Williams): bhūta, "what has been", and kāla, "time" (CDIAL 3084), the word that gave കാലം.

Lesson 3 of 5.
സന്ദർഭം (sandarbhaṁ) — an occasion; a context.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for an era, then the Malayalam for the past.

You'll want to know: സന്ദർഭം.
സന്ദർഭം — sandarbhaṁ — "an occasion; a context".
ആ സന്ദർഭത്തിൽ ഞാൻ ഒന്നും പറഞ്ഞില്ല. ā sandarbhattil ñān onnuṁ paṟaññilla. On that occasion I said nothing.
Also the context of a word or a sentence.

The word, taken apart.
സന്ദർഭം (sandarbhaṁ) is borrowed from Sanskrit saṁdarbha, "a stringing or weaving together; a composition" (Monier-Williams). An occasion is how things are strung together at one time.

Lesson 4 of 5.
തുടക്കം (tuṭakkaṁ) — a beginning; a start.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for the past, then the Malayalam for an occasion.

You'll want to know: തുടക്കം.
തുടക്കം — tuṭakkaṁ — "a beginning; a start".
കഥയുടെ തുടക്കം വളരെ രസകരമായിരുന്നു. kathayuṭe tuṭakkaṁ vaḷare rasakaramāyirunnu. The beginning of the story was very interesting.
The noun of തുടങ്ങുക, to begin; its opposite is ഒടുക്കം, the end.

The word, taken apart.
തുടക്കം (tuṭakkaṁ) is native Dravidian, the noun of തുടങ്ങുക (tuṭaṅṅuka), "to begin" (DEDR 3481), as ഒടുക്കം is the noun of ഒടുങ്ങുക.

Lesson 5 of 5.
അപ്രതീക്ഷിതമായി (apratīkṣitamāyi) — unexpectedly.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for an occasion, then the Malayalam for a beginning.

You'll want to know: അപ്രതീക്ഷിതമായി.
അപ്രതീക്ഷിതമായി — apratīkṣitamāyi — "unexpectedly".
അമ്മാവൻ ഇന്നലെ അപ്രതീക്ഷിതമായി വീട്ടിൽ വന്നു. ammāvan innale apratīkṣitamāyi vīṭṭil vannu. Uncle came home unexpectedly yesterday.
From പ്രതീക്ഷിക്കുക, to expect: അ-, not, + expected + -ആയി.

The word, taken apart.
അപ്രതീക്ഷിതമായി (apratīkṣitamāyi) is built from Sanskrit pratīkṣita, "expected" (CDIAL 8621), with a-, "not", in front and the Malayalam adverb ending -ആയി. It is a cousin of പ്രതീക്ഷ (pratīkṣa), "hope".
