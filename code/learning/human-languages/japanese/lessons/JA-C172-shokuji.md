## You'll want to know: しょくじ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C172-MEALS172-01]; assesses=[] -->

**しょくじ** — *shokuji* — "meal; dining".

> **まいにち かぞくと しょくじを します。** — *mainichi kazoku to shokuji o shimasu* — "I have meals with my family every day."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**しょくじ** (*shokuji*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *shoku*, "eat, food" (Middle Chinese *zyik*) and *ji*, "matter, thing" (*dzriH*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
