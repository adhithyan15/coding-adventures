# بننا چاہتی ہوں (bannā chāhtī hūṅ) — what I want to become

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[UR-LEX-C272-BANNA-01, UR-LEX-C272-UMMID-01, UR-LEX-C51-VERBS51-02] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "she became a teacher". (*voh ustād banī*.) Then "I want to go". (*maiṅ jānā chāhtā hūṅ*.) Put the two together.

## You'll want to know: I want to become
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-GRAMMAR-C272-AMBITION-01]; assesses=[UR-LEX-C46-FAMILY46-01, UR-LEX-C51-VERBS51-02, UR-LEX-C153-SEHAT153-01, UR-LEX-C228-CHIZ228-03] -->

> **میں استاد ہوں۔**
>
> *maiṅ ustād hūṅ.* — "I'm a teacher."

> **میں استاد بننا چاہتی ہوں۔**
>
> *maiṅ ustād bannā chāhtī hūṅ.* — "I want to become a teacher."

**چاہنا** (*chāhnā*) takes **بننا** whole, as it takes **جانا**: **بننا چاہتی ہوں**, a woman speaking.

> **وہ نرس بننا چاہتا ہے۔**
>
> *voh nars bannā chāhtā hai.* — "He wants to become a nurse."

## You'll want to know: my dream, my hope
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-GRAMMAR-C272-AMBITION-02]; assesses=[UR-LEX-C216-SEHAT216-04, UR-LEX-C272-UMMID-01, UR-GRAMMAR-C265-FUTURE-01] -->

> **میرا خواب استاد بننا ہے۔**
>
> *merā khvāb ustād bannā hai.* — "My dream is to become a teacher."

**خواب** (*khvāb*) is masculine: **میرا**. A hope looks ahead, so it takes the future:

> **مجھے امید ہے کہ میں استاد بنوں گی۔**
>
> *mujhe ummīd hai ki maiṅ ustād banūṅ gī.* — "I hope I'll become a teacher."
