# నాకు కావాలి (nāku kāvāli) — I want it

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[TE-GRAMMAR-C79-WHO-04, TE-LEX-C79-WHO-05] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: what did *gāru* mean?

## You'll want to know: నాకు కావాలి
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C80-WISH-01]; assesses=[] -->

**నాకు కావాలి** (*nāku kāvāli*) — "I want it". The new word in it is **కావాలి**, "is wanted".

You already have **వద్దు**, "no, I don't want it", which left you able to refuse a thing and unable to ask for one. కావాలి is the missing half.

**నాకు టీ కావాలి** — "I want tea." The wanter sits in the dative నాకు, exactly like the knower of తెలుసు and the liker of ఇష్టం. It is the same Telugu design a fourth time.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

A request, and the opposite of వద్దు.
