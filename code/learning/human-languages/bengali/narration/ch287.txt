Bengali, chapter 287: In a Split Second.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
মুহূর্ত (muhūrtô) — a moment; an instant.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a shadow, then the Bengali for to observe.

You'll want to know: মুহূর্ত.
মুহূর্ত — muhūrtô — "a moment; an instant".
এক মুহূর্তের জন্য আমি কিছু বুঝলাম না। ek muhūrter jônyô āmi kichhu bujhlām nā. For a moment I didn't understand anything.

The word, taken apart.
মুহূর্ত (muhūrtô) is Sanskrit muhūrta, 'a moment, an instant' (Monier-Williams), and also a fixed thirtieth of the day; that is why the word also names the auspicious hour for a wedding.

Lesson 2 of 5.
চকিতে (chôkite) — in a flash; in an instant.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for to observe, then the Bengali for a moment.

You'll want to know: চকিতে.
চকিতে — chôkite — "in a flash; in an instant".
চকিতে চোরটা জানালা দিয়ে লাফিয়ে পালাল। chôkite chorṭā jānālā diye lāphiye pālālô. In a flash the thief jumped out of the window and ran.

The word, taken apart.
চকিতে (chôkite) is Sanskrit cakita, 'trembling, startled' (Monier-Williams), in the -e form: 'in a start'. The sense 'in a flash' is the Bengali one.

Lesson 3 of 5.
আঁকড়ে ধরা (ā̃kṛe dhôrā) — to clutch; to cling to.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: আঁকড়ে ধরা.
আঁকড়ে ধরা — ā̃kṛe dhôrā — "to clutch; to cling to".
ভয়ে শিশুটি বাবার হাত আঁকড়ে ধরল। bhôye shishuṭi bābār hāt ā̃kṛe dhôrlô. The frightened child clung to his father's hand.
Past (he/she …): সেই শিশু দড়িটা আঁকড়ে ধরল, sei shishu dôṛiṭā ā̃kṛe dhôrlô.

The word, taken apart.
আঁকড়ে ধরা (ā̃kṛe dhôrā) is the -e form of আঁকড়া — CDIAL 100 lists Bengali ã̄kṛā, 'a hook, a tendril', under Sanskrit aṅka, 'a hook' — before ধরা, to hold: to hold on the way a hook holds.

Lesson 4 of 5.
তৎক্ষণাৎ (tôtkkhônāt) — at once; immediately.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for in an instant, then the Bengali for to clutch.

You'll want to know: তৎক্ষণাৎ.
তৎক্ষণাৎ — tôtkkhônāt — "at once; immediately".
আওয়াজ শুনে দারোগা তৎক্ষণাৎ বাইরে এলেন। āoyāj shune dārogā tôtkkhônāt bāire elen. Hearing the noise, the inspector came out at once.

The word, taken apart.
তৎক্ষণাৎ (tôtkkhônāt) is Sanskrit tatkṣaṇāt, 'at that very moment' (Monier-Williams): tat, 'that', and kṣaṇa, 'an instant'. It belongs to the news and to careful speech.

Lesson 5 of 5.
আছড়ে পড়া (āchhṛe pôṛā) — to crash down; to be dashed down.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for to clutch, then the Bengali for at once.

You'll want to know: আছড়ে পড়া.
আছড়ে পড়া — āchhṛe pôṛā — "to crash down; to be dashed down".
ঝড়ে একটা বড় গাছ বাড়ির ছাদে আছড়ে পড়ল। jhôṛe ekṭā bôṛo gāchh bāṛir chhāde āchhṛe pôṛlô. In the storm a big tree came crashing down on the roof of the house.
Past (he/she …): সেই গাছ রাস্তায় আছড়ে পড়ল, sei gāchh rāstāy āchhṛe pôṛlô.

The word, taken apart.
আছড়ে পড়া (āchhṛe pôṛā) is the -e form of আছড়ানো, to dash down — CDIAL 1081 lists Bengali āchṛāna, 'to throw down hard', and āchāṛ, 'a dashing down', under ācchardati, 'pours on'; how the sense moved is not set out there — before পড়া in its sense 'to fall'.
