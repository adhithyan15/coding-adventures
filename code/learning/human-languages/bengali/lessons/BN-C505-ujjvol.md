## You'll want to know: উজ্জ্বল
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C505-ALO505-02]; assesses=[] -->

**উজ্জ্বল** — *ujjvôl* — "bright".

> আজ চাঁদটা উজ্জ্বল।
>
> *Āj chā̃dṭā ujjvôl.*
>
> The moon is bright tonight.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**উজ্জ্বল** (*ujjvôl*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *ujjvala*, "blazing up, luminous" (Monier-Williams).
