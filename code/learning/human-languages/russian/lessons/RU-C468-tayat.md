## You'll want to know: таять
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C468-ZHARA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**таять** (*táyat*) — "to melt, to thaw".

> Мороженое быстро тает на солнце.
>
> *Morózhenoye býstro táyet na sólntse.*
>
> The ice cream melts quickly in the sun.

Imperfective; perfective *растаять*. Present *таю, таешь*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*таять* is inherited from Proto-Slavic *tajati* (EtymDB), with the sense 'to melt, to thaw'.
