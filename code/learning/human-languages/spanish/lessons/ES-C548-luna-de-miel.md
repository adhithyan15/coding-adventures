## You'll want to know: la luna de miel
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C548-BANQUETE-05]; assesses=[] -->

**la luna de miel** — "the honeymoon".

> Pasaron la luna de miel en Cuba.
>
> *They spent their honeymoon in Cuba.*

*Irse de luna de miel* — to go on honeymoon.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*luna de miel* was built on Latin *lūna* 'moon' and *mel* 'honey' (Lewis & Short), and is usually said to copy English *honeymoon*, through French *lune de miel*.
