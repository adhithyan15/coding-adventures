## You'll want to know: गुल्मः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C446-SHIKHARA446-03]; assesses=[] -->

**गुल्मः** — *gulmaḥ* — "a thicket; a bush".

> अधित्यकायां गुल्माः तृणानि च सन्ति।
>
> *adhityakāyāṃ gulmāḥ tṛṇāni ca santi.*
>
> On the plateau there are bushes and grasses.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**गुल्मः** (*gulmaḥ*) is the word MW gives for a cluster or clump of trees, a thicket, a bush, a shrub; MW gives no derivation, so its origin is uncertain (MW).
