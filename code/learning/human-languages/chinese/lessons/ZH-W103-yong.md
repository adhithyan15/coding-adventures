## Script — the moon frame, two bars, a vertical
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-SCRIPT-YONG-01]; assesses=[] -->

> 用

**The frame, two strokes**, as on the right of 明: a left-falling stroke down the left side, then a stroke across the top that turns down the right side and hooks.

**Two horizontals inside**, one above the other.

**A vertical down the middle**, from the top through both bars to the foot.

2 + 2 + 1 = five.
