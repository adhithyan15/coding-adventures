## You'll want to know: सातवाँ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C76-ORDINAL-02]; assesses=[] -->

**सातवाँ** (*sātvā̃*) — "**seventh**".

**सातवाँ दिन** (*sātvā̃ din*), **सातवीं किताब** (*sātvī̃ kitāb*).

**सात** stands whole and **-वाँ** goes on. After the hole at six the ending runs
without interruption, and it will keep running for the rest of the number line.

Notice what you are now doing: you are not learning a word, you are applying
something. Say *sāt* and the seventh follows from it without a second act of
memory. That is what a rule buys, and it is why the four irregular ones were
worth meeting first — they were the price, and it has been paid.
