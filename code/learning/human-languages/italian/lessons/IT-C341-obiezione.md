## You'll want to know: l'obiezione
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C341-OBIEZIONE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**l'obiezione** — "the objection".

> Nessuna obiezione? Allora cominciamo.
>
> *No objections? Then let us begin.*

*Fare un'obiezione* — to raise an objection. *Nessuna obiezione* — no objection.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*obiezione* is a learned borrowing from Late Latin *obiectiō* "a throwing against, an objection", from *obicere* "to throw against" (Lewis & Short), built on *iacere* "to throw".
