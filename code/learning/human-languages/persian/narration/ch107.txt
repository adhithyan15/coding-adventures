Persian, chapter 107: Mosquito, Fly, Chin, Cheek, Elbow.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
پشه (pashe) — a mosquito.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a duck, then the Persian for a bee.
