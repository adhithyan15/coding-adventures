### Spanish A1 food tranche — three exam-derived words (chapter 403)

Chapter 403 adds **el pollo**, **la verdura** and **la paella** as the food
cluster required by mock 1 items 7, 32 and 48. The lessons preserve the
composition-aware safety decision from HL24: *pollo* contrasts only with
*polvo*, the omitted inflections are documented in source, and *echar* never
appears near the contrast. *Verdura* also records its marked secondary sense
instead of reducing *verde* to “just the colour.”

Two retrieval lessons then move the three nouns through the actual exam
contexts: a menu, a supermarket announcement and a family kitchen.
