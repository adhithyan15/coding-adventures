Lesson 4 of 5.
چھری (chhurī) — a knife.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a cup, then the Urdu for a spoon.

You'll want to know: چھری.
چھری — chhurī — "a knife".

The word, taken apart.
چھری (chhurī) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from kṣurī, "knife, dagger" (CDIAL 3727). It is the feminine of kṣura, "razor".
