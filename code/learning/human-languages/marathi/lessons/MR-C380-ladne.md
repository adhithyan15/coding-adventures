## You'll want to know: लादणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C380-ANIVARYA380-04]; assesses=[] -->

**लादणे** — *lādṇe* — "to impose".

> आईवडिलांनी मुलांवर आपली मतं लादू नयेत.
>
> *āīvḍilānnī mulāṁvar āplī mataṁ lādū nayet.*
>
> Parents should not impose their views on their children.

Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने लादलं, *tyāne lādlaṁ*.

The one burdened takes -वर; also to load a cart.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**लादणे** (*lādṇe*) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Marathi *lādṇẽ*, "to heap up, load", under Sanskrit *lardayati*, "loads" (CDIAL 10966).
