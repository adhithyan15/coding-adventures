## You'll want to know: تاسیس کردن
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C354-KASHF-03]; assesses=[] -->

**تاسیس کردن** (*ta'sis kardan*) — "to found, to establish".

> پدربزرگم این مدرسه را تاسیس کرد.
>
> *Pedarbozorgam in madrese râ ta'sis kard.*
>
> My grandfather founded this school.

Careful print adds a hamza sign. Present *ta'sis mikonad*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ta'sis* is borrowed from Arabic *ta'sīs*, "a founding, laying a foundation" (EtymDB lists the Arabic word). *kardan* "to do" is inherited Persian.
