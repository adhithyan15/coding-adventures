## You'll want to know: shuōfú
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C353-POINT353-01]; assesses=[] -->

*shuōfú* — "to talk someone round; to persuade".

> *wǒ juéde nǐ néng shuōfú tā* — I think you can talk him round.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*shuōfú* is built from 说 'to say' and *fú*, both inherited from Middle Chinese, *sywet* and *bjuwk* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to tell' and the second 'to serve' and 'clothes'. How the pair came to mean persuading is not settled.
