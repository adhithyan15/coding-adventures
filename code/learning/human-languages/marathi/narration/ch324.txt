Marathi, chapter 324: Remembering a Life.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
निधन (nidhan) — a passing; a death.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for an anniversary, then the Marathi for a closing.

You'll want to know: निधन.
निधन — nidhan — "a passing; a death".
प्रसिद्ध गायकाच्या निधनाची बातमी काल आली. prasiddha gāykācyā nidhanācī bātmī kāl ālī. News of the famous singer's passing came yesterday.
Neuter.
The respectful word: X-चं निधन (nidhan) झालं.

The word, taken apart.
निधन (nidhan) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit nidhana, 'a settling, a close; death' (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 3 of 5.
आयोजन करणे (āyojan karṇe) — to organise.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a passing, then the Marathi for a tribute.

You'll want to know: आयोजन करणे.
आयोजन करणे — āyojan karṇe — "to organise".
मित्रांनी त्यांच्या आठवणीत एका सभेचं आयोजन केलं. mitrānnī tyāñcyā āṭhavṇīt ekā sabhecaṁ āyojan kelaṁ. Friends organised a meeting in his memory.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने आयोजन केलं, tyāne āyojan kelaṁ.
What is organised takes -चं: स्पर्धेचं आयोजन करणे (āyojan karṇe).

The word, taken apart.
आयोजन करणे (āyojan karṇe) joins āyojan, a tatsama taken from Sanskrit āyojana, 'a joining, a bringing together' (Monier-Williams), to karṇe, 'to do'.

Lesson 4 of 5.
पार पडणे (pār paḍṇe) — to go off; to be carried through.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a tribute, then the Marathi for to organise.

You'll want to know: पार पडणे.
पार पडणे — pār paḍṇe — "to go off; to be carried through".
सगळा कार्यक्रम शांतपणे पार पडला. sagḷā kāryakram śāntapṇe pār paḍlā. The whole event went off peacefully.
Past (he …): तो पार पडला, to pār paḍlā.
Intransitive, said of events: लग्न छान पार पडलं.

The word, taken apart.
पार पडणे (pār paḍṇe) is built from pār, 'the far bank, the end', inherited from Sanskrit pāra (CDIAL 8100), and paḍṇe, 'to fall': to come through to the other side.

Lesson 5 of 5.
रचणे (racṇe) — to compose; to arrange.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to organise, then the Marathi for to go off.

You'll want to know: रचणे.
रचणे — racṇe — "to compose; to arrange".
त्यांच्या मित्राने त्यांच्यावर एक कविता रचली. tyāñcyā mitrāne tyāñcyāvar ek kavitā raclī. His friend composed a poem about him.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने रचलं, tyāne raclaṁ.
Transitive: कविता रचणे (racṇe), to compose a poem; दगड रचणे (racṇe), to stack stones.

The word, taken apart.
रचणे (racṇe) is built on Sanskrit racayati, 'produces, arranges, composes' (Monier-Williams).
