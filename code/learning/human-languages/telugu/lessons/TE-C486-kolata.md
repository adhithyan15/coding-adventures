## You'll want to know: కొలత
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C486-KOLATA486-01]; assesses=[] -->

**కొలత** — *kolata* — "a measurement".

> దర్జీ నా చొక్కా కొలతలు తీసుకున్నాడు.
>
> *darjī nā cokkā kolatalu tīsukunnāḍu.*
>
> The tailor took my measurements for a shirt.

Plural: కొలతలు, *kolatalu*.

The noun of కొలుచు, to measure. కొలతలు తీసుకో, to take measurements.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**కొలత** (*kolata*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 1827 lists Telugu *kolãta*, "measurement, measuring", beside *kolucu*, "to measure".
