Persian, chapter 93: Therefore, Pillow, Blanket, Curtain, Nail.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
بنابراین (banâbarin) — therefore.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a reason, then the Persian for a cause.

You'll want to know: بنابراین.
بنابراین — banâbarin — "therefore".

The word, taken apart.
banâbarin is banâ, "foundation", with bar, "on", and in, "this": "on this basis". banâ is borrowed from Arabic bināʾ, "building" (root b-n-y, Lane); bar and in are native Persian.

Lesson 2 of 5.
بالش (bâlesh) — a pillow.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a cause, then the Persian for therefore.

You'll want to know: بالش.
بالش — bâlesh — "a pillow".

The word, taken apart.
bâlesh is a native Persian word, inherited rather than borrowed: it continues Middle Persian bāliš, "pillow" (EtymDB). EtymDB takes it back to an Indo-European root usually also given for English bolster.
