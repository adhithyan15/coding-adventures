## You'll want to know: tollerare
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C385-VIETATO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**tollerare** — "to tolerate, to put up with".

> Non tollero i ritardi.
>
> *I do not put up with lateness.*

Stress: *tòllero*. *Non tollerare il caldo* — not to cope with the heat.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*tollerare* is a learned borrowing from Latin *tolerāre* "to bear, to endure" (Lewis & Short).
