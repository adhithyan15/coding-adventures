## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सच्चा** (*saccā*) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from *satya*, "true" (CDIAL 13112).
