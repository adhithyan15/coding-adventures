French, chapter 379: Le diplôme.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
le diplôme — the diploma, the degree.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for the average, then the French for the resit.

You'll want to know: le diplôme.
le diplôme — "the diploma, the degree".
Il a enfin son diplôme d'infirmier. He finally has his nursing diploma.
Passer un diplôme — to take a qualification.

The word, taken apart.
diplôme comes from Latin diplōma "a letter folded double, a state letter of recommendation" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short), from Greek díplōma "a folded paper", built on diplóos "double".

What you've built.
One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.

Lesson 2 of 5.
l'épreuve — the test, the exam paper; the ordeal.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for the resit, then the French for the diploma.

You'll want to know: l'épreuve.
l'épreuve — "the test, the exam paper; the ordeal".
La première épreuve de l'examen dure trois heures. The first paper of the exam lasts three hours.
Une épreuve écrite, une épreuve orale. In sport, an event. Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
épreuve is formed on éprouver "to test, to feel" (EtymDB), built from é- and prouver, which is inherited from Latin probāre "to test, to examine" (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 3 of 5.
la mention — the distinction, the honours (on an exam); the mention.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for the diploma, then the French for the test.

You'll want to know: la mention.
la mention — "the distinction, the honours (on an exam); the mention".
Elle a eu son diplôme avec mention. She got her diploma with honours.
Mention bien, mention très bien — with honours, with high honours.

The word, taken apart.
mention is a learned borrowing from Latin mentiō "a calling to mind, a mention" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short), related to mēns "mind".
