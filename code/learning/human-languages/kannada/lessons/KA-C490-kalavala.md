# ಕಳವಳ (kaḷavaḷa) — unease; agitation

## You'll want to know: ಕಳವಳ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C490-BHAYA490-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ಕಳವಳ** — *kaḷavaḷa* — "unease; agitation".

> ರಾತ್ರಿ ಮಗ ಬರಲಿಲ್ಲ, ಅಮ್ಮನಿಗೆ ಕಳವಳ ಆಯಿತು.
>
> *rātri maga baralilla, ammanige kaḷavaḷa āyitu.*
>
> The son had not come by night; his mother grew uneasy.

A restless worry that will not settle; ಕಳವಳಗೊಳ್ಳು, to grow uneasy.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಕಳವಳ** (*kaḷavaḷa*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 1306 lists Kannada *kaḷakaḷa* and *kaḷavaḷike*, "agitation of the mind, distress", and the verb *kaḷavaḷisu*, "to be agitated".
