## You'll want to know: دکترا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C403-TAHSILAT-05]; assesses=[] -->

**دکترا** (*doktorâ*) — "a doctorate, a PhD".

> عمویم دکترای شیمی دارد.
>
> *'amuyam doktorâ-ye shimi dârad.*
>
> My uncle has a doctorate in chemistry.

*Doktorâ gereftan* — to get a PhD.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**دکترا** is usually traced to French *doctorat* "doctorate", built on Latin *doctor* "teacher" (Lewis & Short), from *docere* "to teach".
