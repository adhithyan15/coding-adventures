## You'll want to know: ممنوع
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C404-MOQARRARAT-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ممنوع** (*mamnu'*) — "forbidden, prohibited".

> سیگار کشیدن در اتوبوس ممنوع است.
>
> *sigâr keshidan dar otobus mamnu' ast.*
>
> Smoking is forbidden on the bus.

On signs: *vorud mamnu'* — no entry.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ممنوع** is usually traced to Arabic *mamnū'* "prevented, forbidden".
