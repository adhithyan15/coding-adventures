Spanish, chapter 505: Estudiar.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
la educación — education; manners.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for the factory, then the Spanish for to damage.

You'll want to know: la educación.
la educación — "education; manners".
La educación es gratuita. Education is free.
Tener buena educación — to have good manners.

The word, taken apart.
educación from Latin educatio, "a bringing up" (EtymDB 2.1), from educare, "to rear" — a learned word. Manners are part of a good upbringing.

What you've built.
One more word for reading notices, forms and messages.

You'll want to know: asistir.
asistir — "to attend".
Asisto a clase todos los días. I attend class every day.
A false friend: asistir a means to attend; to help is usually ayudar.

The word, taken apart.
asistir from Latin assistere, "to stand by" (EtymDB 2.1): ad- and sistere, "to stand" — a learned word. To attend is to stand by.

Lesson 4 of 5.

You'll want to know: ensayar.
ensayar — "to rehearse, to practise".
El grupo ensaya los viernes. The band rehearses on Fridays.
El ensayo is the rehearsal, or an essay.

The word, taken apart.
ensayar is built on ensayo, "a trial, a rehearsal", from Late Latin exagium, "a weighing" (EtymDB 2.1).

éxito from Latin exitus, "a going out, an outcome" (EtymDB 2.1) — a learned word. It means success, not exit: Spanish kept the good outcome.
