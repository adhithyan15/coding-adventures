# દુપટ્ટો (dupaṭṭo) — scarf, stole

## You'll want to know: દુપટ્ટો
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C154-CLOTH154-03]; assesses=[] -->

**દુપટ્ટો** — *dupaṭṭo* — "scarf, stole".

> **તેનો દુપટ્ટો લાલ છે.** — *teno dupaṭṭo lāl che* — "Her scarf is red."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**દુપટ્ટો** (*dupaṭṭo*) is built from *du-*, "two", and *paṭṭo*, "strip of cloth". Under Sanskrit *paṭṭa*, "cloth", which it thinks probably non-Aryan, CDIAL lists the kindred Gujarati *pāṭ*, "strip of cloth", and *pāṭo*, "bandage", but no form of the compound itself (CDIAL 7700).
