## You'll want to know: l'arbitro
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C509-ARBITRO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**l'arbitro** — "referee".

> L'arbitro ha sbagliato tutto oggi.
>
> *The referee got everything wrong today.*

A woman is *l'arbitra*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*arbitro* is a learned borrowing from Latin *arbiter* "a witness, a judge" (Lewis & Short).
