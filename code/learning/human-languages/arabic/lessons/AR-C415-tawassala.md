## You'll want to know: توصل
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C415-INJAZ415-04]; assesses=[] -->

**توصل** — *tawaṣṣala* — "to arrive at".

> توصل الباحثون إلى نتيجة جديدة.
>
> *tawaṣṣala al-bāḥithūn ilā natīja jadīda.*
>
> The researchers arrived at a new result.

Present: يتوصل, *yatawaṣṣal*.

It takes إلى: توصل إلى ….

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**توصل** (*tawaṣṣala*) is built on the native Arabic root *w-ṣ-l*, "to arrive, to join" (Lane). It is Form V, to work one's way to a thing: to arrive at, to reach.
