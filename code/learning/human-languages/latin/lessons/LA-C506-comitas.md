## You'll want to know: cōmitās, cōmitātis
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C506-COMI-02]; assesses=[] -->

**cōmitās, cōmitātis** — "friendliness, affability".

> Cōmitāte suā multōs amīcōs habet.
>
> *Through his friendliness he has many friends.*

Feminine, third declension, from *cōmis*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**cōmitās** is built on *cōmis* "courteous" with *-itās* (Lewis & Short; EtymDB).
