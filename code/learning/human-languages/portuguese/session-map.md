# Session Map — Portuguese Chapter 1 (Greetings)

How the lessons compose into commute sessions. Mechanics in
[`HL00`](../../../specs/HL00-human-language-curriculum-framework.md); same
spaced-repetition schedule as the other tracks (a word from session *N*
resurfaces at *N+1, N+3, N+7, N+15*). Lessons named by slug; this map is the
authoritative order.

## Chapter 1 — Greetings

| Session | Lesson | Word | Root / hook |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | ola | olá | "hi" (rootless interjection; twin of Spanish *hola*) |
| 2 | bom | bom / boa | "good" ← *bonus*; nasal *bõ*; agreement |
| 3 | o-a | o / a | grammatical **gender** ← *ille/illa* (eroded to one vowel) |
| 4 | dia | dia | first noun (masc., despite *-a*); ← *dies* |
| 5 | bom-dia | bom dia | assembled; "good morning" |
| 6 | tarde | tarde / boa tarde | afternoon (fem.); ← *tardus* ("late"; *tardy*) |
| 7 | noite | noite / boa noite | night (fem.); Latin *-ct-* → *-it-* (like *nuit*) |
| 8 | obrigado | obrigado / obrigada | "thanks" ← *obligātus* ("obliged"); agrees with speaker |
| 9 | practice | (recap) | the greetings together |
