## You'll want to know: ફેરફાર
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C531-JHANJHAT531-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ફેરફાર** — *pherphār* — "a change".

> જો યોજનામાં કોઈ ફેરફાર થશે તો હું તમને કહીશ.
>
> *jo yojnāmā̃ koī pherphār thaśe to hũ tamne kahīś.*
>
> If there is any change in the plan, I will tell you.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ફેરફાર** (*pherphār*) is built on *pher*, "a turn", which CDIAL files under *\*phirati*, "turns" (CDIAL 9078), doubled with a rhyming *phār*.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
