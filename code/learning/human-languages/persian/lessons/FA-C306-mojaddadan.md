## You'll want to know: مجددا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C306-TADRIJ-05]; assesses=[] -->

**مجددا** (*mojaddadan*) — "again, anew (formal)".

> او مجددا به آن شرکت زنگ زد.
>
> *U mojaddadan be ân sherkat zang zad.*
>
> She phoned that company again.

Formal and official; in speech *dobâre*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*mojaddadan* is the Arabic adverb of *mujaddad*, "renewed" (EtymDB lists Arabic *mujaddad*), made with the ending *-an*.
