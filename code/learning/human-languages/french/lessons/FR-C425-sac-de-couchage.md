## You'll want to know: le sac de couchage
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C425-CAMPING-03]; assesses=[] -->

**le sac de couchage** — "the sleeping bag".

> Il fait froid la nuit, alors je dors dans mon sac de couchage.
>
> *It is cold at night, so I sleep in my sleeping bag.*

In speech, often *le duvet*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*sac* is inherited from Latin *saccus*, from Greek *sákkos* 'bag' (EtymDB); *couchage* is built on *coucher* 'to lie down'.
