## You'll want to know: der Bauernhof
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C459-BAUERNHOF-01]; assesses=[] -->

**der Bauernhof** — "the farm".

> Im Urlaub wohnen wir auf einem Bauernhof.
>
> *On holiday we stay on a farm.*

Plural: *die Bauernhöfe*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Bauernhof* is built from *Bauer* 'farmer', inherited from Old High German *būr* (EtymDB), and *Hof* 'yard, farm'.
