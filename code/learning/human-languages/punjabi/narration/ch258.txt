Punjabi, chapter 258: Borrowing and Earning.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ਉਧਾਰ (udhār) — credit; money lent.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for to withdraw, then the Punjabi for to go up.

You'll want to know: ਉਧਾਰ.
ਉਧਾਰ — udhār — "credit; money lent".
ਮੈਂ ਉਧਾਰ ਨਹੀਂ ਲੈਂਦਾ ਹਾਂ। maiṁ udhār nahīṁ laindā hāṁ. I do not take things on credit.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ਉਧਾਰ (udhār) is inherited from Sanskrit uddhāra, "raising; a loan"; CDIAL lists Punjabi udhār, "loan", under it (CDIAL 2018).

Lesson 5 of 5.
ਕਮਾਉਣਾ (kamāuṇā) — to earn.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for a coin, then the Punjabi for a banknote.

You'll want to know: ਕਮਾਉਣਾ.
ਕਮਾਉਣਾ — kamāuṇā — "to earn".
ਮੇਰੀ ਭੈਣ ਦਫ਼ਤਰ ਵਿੱਚ ਚੰਗੇ ਪੈਸੇ ਕਮਾਉਂਦੀ ਹੈ। merī bhaiṇ daftar vich chaṅge paise kamāundī hai. My sister earns good money at the office.
Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਬਹੁਤ ਪੈਸੇ ਕਮਾਏ, maiṁ bahut paise kamāe.

The word, taken apart.
ਕਮਾਉਣਾ (kamāuṇā) is inherited from a reconstructed karmāpayati, "works, earns"; CDIAL lists Punjabi kamāuṇā, "to work, earn", under it (CDIAL 2897).
