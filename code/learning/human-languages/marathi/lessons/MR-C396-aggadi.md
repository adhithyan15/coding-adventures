## You'll want to know: आगगाडी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C396-AAGGADI396-01]; assesses=[] -->

**आगगाडी** — *āggāḍī* — "a train; a steam train".

> आगगाडी पुलावरून हळू जाते.
>
> *āggāḍī pulāvarūn haḷū jāte.*
>
> The train goes slowly over the bridge.

Feminine. Plural: आगगाड्या, *āggāḍyā*.

The plural is आगगाड्या; in everyday talk गाडी alone often means the train.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**आगगाडी** (*āggāḍī*) is a Marathi compound from the age of steam: **आग**, "fire", inherited from Sanskrit *agni* (CDIAL 55), and **गाडी**, "a cart", inherited from *\*gāḍḍa* (CDIAL 4116).
