## You'll want to know: چوسنا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C467-MOUTH467-04]; assesses=[] -->

**چوسنا** — *chūsnā* — "to suck".

> بچہ اپنا انگوٹھا چوستا ہے۔
>
> *bachchā apnā angūṭhā chūstā hai.*
>
> The baby sucks his thumb.

Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے آم چوسا, *us ne ām chūsā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**چوسنا** (*chūsnā*) is inherited from Sanskrit *cūṣati*, "sucks" (CDIAL 4898).
