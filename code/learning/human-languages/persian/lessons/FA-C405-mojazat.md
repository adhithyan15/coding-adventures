## You'll want to know: مجازات
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C405-QANUNI-04]; assesses=[] -->

**مجازات** (*mojâzât*) — "a punishment, a penalty".

> مجازات دزدی زندان است.
>
> *mojâzât-e dozdi zendân ast.*
>
> The punishment for theft is prison.

*Mojâzât kardan* — to punish.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**مجازات** was borrowed from Arabic *mujāzāt* (EtymDB), a word usually glossed as requital, paying back a deed.
