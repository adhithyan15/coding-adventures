## You'll want to know: entsorgen
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C528-MUELL-05]; assesses=[] -->

**entsorgen** — "to dispose of".

> Alte Batterien muss man richtig entsorgen.
>
> *Old batteries have to be disposed of properly.*

Inseparable: *hat entsorgt*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*entsorgen* is built on *ent-* "away" and *Sorge* "care, worry", inherited from Old High German *sorga*, from Proto-Germanic *surgō* (EtymDB): to take away a worry.
