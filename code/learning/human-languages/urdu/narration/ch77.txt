You'll want to know: کبھی.
کبھی — kabhī — "sometimes, ever".

The word, taken apart.
کبھی (kabhī) is inherited. CDIAL lists it under Sanskrit evam eva, "exactly so", crossed with the question stem ka- of kyā and kaun (CDIAL 2528).

Lesson 5 of 5.
پہلے (pehle) — before, first.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for quickly; early, then the Urdu for a delay; late.

You'll want to know: پہلے.
پہلے — pehle — "before, first".

The word, taken apart.
پہلے (pehle) is the adverb form of pahlā, "first". CDIAL lists pahlā under prathilla, a variant of a reconstructed prathila, "first", built on Sanskrit prathama, "first" (CDIAL 8652).
