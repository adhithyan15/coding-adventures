## You'll want to know: لاڈ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C384-TARBIYAT384-04]; assesses=[] -->

**لاڈ** — *lāḍ* — "pampering; fond indulgence".

> اگر آپ نے بہت لاڈ کیا تو بچہ بگڑے گا۔
>
> *agar āp ne bahut lāḍ kiyā to bachchā bigṛe gā.*
>
> If you pamper the child too much, he will be spoilt.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**لاڈ** (*lāḍ*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *lāṛ*, "affection", under *\*lāḍya*, a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 11013).
