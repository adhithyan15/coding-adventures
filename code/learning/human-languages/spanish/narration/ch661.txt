Spanish, chapter 661: En recepción.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
el recepcionista — the receptionist.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for the guesthouse, then the Spanish for the hostel.

You'll want to know: el recepcionista.
el recepcionista — "the receptionist".
El recepcionista me da la llave. The receptionist gives me the key.
One form for both: el recepcionista, la recepcionista.

The word, taken apart.
recepcionista is built on recepción, a learned borrowing from Latin receptiō "a receiving" (EtymDB), from recipere "to receive".

What you've built.

Lesson 2 of 5.
el botones — the bellboy, the porter (in a hotel).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for the hostel, then the Spanish for the receptionist.

You'll want to know: el botones.
el botones — "the bellboy, the porter (in a hotel)".
El botones sube las maletas. The bellboy takes the suitcases up.
One person, but the word ends in -s: el botones, los botones.

The word, taken apart.
botones is built on botón "button", which comes from French bouton; the porter is named for the rows of buttons on his uniform.

Los huéspedes desayunan a las ocho. The guests have breakfast at eight.

huésped is inherited from Latin hospitem, a form of hospes "guest; host" (EtymDB).

Lesson 4 of 5.
la estancia — the stay; the room; the ranch (Southern Cone).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for the bellboy, then the Spanish for the guest.

You'll want to know: la estancia.
la estancia — "the stay; the room; the ranch (Southern Cone)".
Disfrute de su estancia. Enjoy your stay.
In Argentina and Uruguay una estancia is a large farm.

The word, taken apart.
estancia is built on estar "to be, to stay", which is inherited from Latin stāre "to stand".

Lesson 5 of 5.
el conserje — the concierge, the porter; the caretaker.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Spanish for the guest, then the Spanish for the stay.

You'll want to know: el conserje.
el conserje — "the concierge, the porter; the caretaker".
El conserje nos recomienda un restaurante. The concierge recommends a restaurant to us.
In a school or a block of flats, el conserje is the caretaker.

The word, taken apart.
conserje was borrowed from French concierge. Where the French word comes from is not settled.
