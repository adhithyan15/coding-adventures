## You'll want to know: しめきり
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C453-PAPERS453-03]; assesses=[] -->

**しめきり** — *shimekiri* — "a cut-off date".

> **しめきりは らいしゅうの きんようびです。** — *shimekiri wa raishū no kin'yōbi desu* — "The cut-off date is next Friday."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**しめきり** (*shimekiri*) is a native Japanese compound (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese: *shime*, "fastening (shimeru)" and *kiri*, "cutting (kiru)" (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
