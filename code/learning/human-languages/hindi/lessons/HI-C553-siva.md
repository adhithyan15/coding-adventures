## You'll want to know: सिवा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C553-KRAM553-01]; assesses=[] -->

**सिवा** — *sivā* — "except (के सिवा)".

> रविवार के सिवा दुकान हर दिन खुली रहती है।
>
> *Ravivār ke sivā dukān har din khulī rahtī hai.*
>
> The shop is open every day except Sunday.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सिवा** (*sivā*) is usually traced to Arabic *siwā*, "except", through Persian.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
