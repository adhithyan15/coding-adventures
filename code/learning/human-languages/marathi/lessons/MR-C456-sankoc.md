## You'll want to know: संकोच
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C456-SANKOC456-02]; assesses=[] -->

**संकोच** — *saṅkoc* — "hesitation; reserve".

> मोठ्या माणसांसमोर बोलायला त्याला संकोच वाटतो.
>
> *moṭhyā māṇsāṁsamor bolāylā tyālā saṅkoc vāṭto.*
>
> He feels shy about speaking in front of grown-ups.

Masculine.

संकोच वाटणे, to feel shy about something: the dative plus the -ायला form. संकोची, reserved.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**संकोच** (*saṅkoc*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *saṃkoca*, "contraction, shrinking together" (Monier-Williams); the shrinking is now of the self.
