## You'll want to know: ऊन
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C236-BEAST236-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ऊन** — *ūn* — "wool".

> भेड़ ऊन देवै है।
>
> *bheṛ ūn devai hai.*
>
> Sheep give wool.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ऊन** (*ūn*) is inherited from Sanskrit *ūrṇā*, "wool"; CDIAL lists Hindi and Gujarati *ūn*. CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry (CDIAL 2424).
