## You'll want to know: عاقل
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C501-HIKMA501-04]; assesses=[] -->

**عاقل** — *ʿāqil* — "sensible".

> تكلم مثل رجل عاقل.
>
> *takallama mithla rajul ʿāqil.*
>
> He spoke like a sensible man.

The noun عقل is the mind.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**عاقل** (*ʿāqil*) is built on the native Arabic root *ʿ-q-l*, "to bind; to understand" (Lane). Maʿqūl, reasonable, which you know, is from the same root.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
