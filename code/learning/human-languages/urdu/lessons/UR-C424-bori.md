## You'll want to know: بوری
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C424-MAAL424-03]; assesses=[] -->

**بوری** — *borī* — "a sack".

> مزدور نے چاول کی بوری اٹھائی۔
>
> *mazdūr ne chāval kī borī uṭhāī.*
>
> The labourer lifted the sack of rice.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**بوری** (*borī*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousins *borā*, "a gunny bag", and *borī* under *bōra*, "sacking, a sack", a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 9320).
