## You'll want to know: संतोष
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C463-SUKUN463-02]; assesses=[] -->

**संतोष** — *santoṣ* — "contentment; satisfaction".

> दादा को अपने जीवन से पूरा संतोष है।
>
> *dādā ko apne jīvan se pūrā santoṣ hai.*
>
> Grandfather is completely content with his life.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**संतोष** (*santoṣ*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *saṁtoṣa*, "satisfaction, contentedness" (Monier-Williams).
