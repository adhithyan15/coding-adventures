## You'll want to know: recensire
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C526-RECENSIONE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**recensire** — "to review".

> Il giornale ha recensito il suo ultimo romanzo.
>
> *The newspaper reviewed her latest novel.*

An *-isc-* verb: *recensisco*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*recensire* is a learned borrowing from Latin *recēnsēre* "to count over, to review" (Lewis & Short), from *cēnsēre* "to assess".
