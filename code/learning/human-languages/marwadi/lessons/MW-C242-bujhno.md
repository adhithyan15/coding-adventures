# बुझणो (bujhṇo) — to go out (of a flame)

## You'll want to know: बुझणो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C242-FIRE242-02]; assesses=[] -->

**बुझणो** — *bujhṇo* — "to go out (of a flame)".

> रात मांय दीयो बुझ्यो।
>
> *rāt mā̃y dīyo bujhyo.*
>
> The lamp went out in the night.

Past (it …): वो चूल्हो बुझ्यो, *vo chūlho bujhyo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**बुझणो** (*bujhṇo*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*vijjhāyati*, "is burnt out, is extinguished"; CDIAL lists the Old Marwari partner *bujhāvaï*, "to extinguish", under it (CDIAL 11703).
