## You'll want to know: die Statistik
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C276-UMFRAGE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**die Statistik** — "the statistics; a statistic".

> Die Statistik zeigt mehr Unfälle im Winter.
>
> *The statistics show more accidents in winter.*

Plural: *die Statistiken*. Stress on the middle: *Sta-TIS-tik*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Statistik* is usually traced to Neo-Latin *statisticus* "of the state", built on Latin *status* "state, condition" (Lewis & Short): first, facts about a state. EtymDB lists no parent.
