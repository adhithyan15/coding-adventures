Russian, chapter 418: Changing trains.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
пересаживаться (peresázhivatsya) — to change trains or buses.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a route, then the Russian for a fixed-route minibus.

You'll want to know: пересаживаться.
пересаживаться (peresázhivatsya) — "to change trains or buses".
Мы пересаживаемся на другой поезд в центре. My peresázhivayemsya na drugóy póyezd v tséntre. We change to another train in the centre.
Imperfective; perfective пересесть (пересяду, пересядешь). Takes на + accusative.

The word, taken apart.
пересаживаться (peresázhivatsya) is built from пере- 'across', -саживать, from садить 'to seat', and -ся: to seat oneself somewhere else. EtymDB lists садить without a deeper source.

Lesson 2 of 6.
конечная (konéchnaya) — the last stop, the terminus.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a fixed-route minibus, then the Russian for to change trains or buses.

You'll want to know: конечная.
конечная (konéchnaya) — "the last stop, the terminus".
Мы едем до конечной. My yédem do konéchnoy. We are going to the last stop.
Short for конечная (konéchnaya) остановка; an adjective used as a noun.

The word, taken apart.
конечная (konéchnaya) is short for конечная (konéchnaya) остановка 'the final stop'. Конечный is built on конец 'an end', which is inherited from Proto-Slavic konĭcĭ 'an end' (EtymDB).

Lesson 3 of 6.
пригородный (prígorodnyy) — suburban, local (of trains and buses).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to change trains or buses, then the Russian for the last stop.

You'll want to know: пригородный.
пригородный (prígorodnyy) — "suburban, local (of trains and buses)".
Бабушка едет на дачу на пригородном поезде. Bábushka yédet na dáchu na prígorodnom póyezde. Grandma goes to her country cottage on the local train.
The stress is on the first syllable: prígorodnyy.

The word, taken apart.
пригородный (prígorodnyy) is built from при- 'near', город 'a town' and -ный. Город is inherited from Proto-Slavic gordŭ 'an enclosure' (EtymDB).

Lesson 4 of 6.
проезжать (proyezzhát) — to go past, to drive through; to miss (one's stop).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for the last stop, then the Russian for suburban.

You'll want to know: проезжать.
проезжать (proyezzhát) — "to go past, to drive through; to miss (one's stop)".
Автобус проезжает мимо нашего дома. Avtóbus proyezzháyet mímo náshego dóma. The bus goes past our house.
Imperfective; perfective проехать (проеду, проедешь). Я проехал свою остановку — I missed my stop.

The word, taken apart.
проезжать (proyezzhát) is built from про- 'past, through' and -езжать, from the root of ездить 'to ride, to travel'. EtymDB lists проезжать (proyezzhát) without a deeper source.

Lesson 5 of 6.
провожать (provozhát) — to see off.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for suburban, then the Russian for to go past.

You'll want to know: провожать.
провожать (provozhát) — "to see off".
Родители провожают сына на вокзале. Rodíteli provozháyut sýna na vokzále. The parents are seeing their son off at the station.
Imperfective; perfective проводить (провожу, проводишь). The imperfective проводить is a different verb — to spend (time).

The word, taken apart.
провожать (provozhát) is built on проводить 'to lead along, to accompany', from про- and водить 'to lead'. Водить is inherited from Proto-Slavic voditi (EtymDB).
