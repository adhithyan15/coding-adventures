## You'll want to know: erstatten
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C420-STORNO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**erstatten** — "to refund, to pay back".

> Die Firma erstattet uns die Kosten für das Hotel.
>
> *The company refunds us the cost of the hotel.*

Formal. *Anzeige erstatten* — to report a crime to the police.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*erstatten* is built from *er-* and the old verb *statten* 'to put in place', from *Statt* 'place', an inherited Germanic word (EtymDB lists *erstatten*): to put money back where it was.
