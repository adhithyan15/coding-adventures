## You'll want to know: dá'àn
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C268-EXAM268-04]; assesses=[] -->

*dá'àn* — "answer to a question".

> *nǐ de dá'àn duì le* — Your answer is right.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*dá'àn* is built from *dá* 'to answer' and *àn*, both inherited from Middle Chinese, *top* and *'anH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to match' and the second 'a low table'. How the pair came to mean an answer is not settled.
