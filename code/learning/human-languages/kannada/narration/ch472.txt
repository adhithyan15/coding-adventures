Kannada, chapter 472: When Things Go Wrong.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಹಾಳಾಗು (hāḷāgu) — to get damaged; to be ruined.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to tug, then the Kannada for to spill over.

You'll want to know: ಹಾಳಾಗು.
ಹಾಳಾಗು — hāḷāgu — "to get damaged; to be ruined".
ಮಳೆಯಲ್ಲಿ ನನ್ನ ಫೋನ್ ಹಾಳಾಯಿತು. maḷeyalli nanna phōn hāḷāyitu. My phone got ruined in the rain.
Past (it …): ಹಾಳಾಯಿತು, hāḷāyitu.
ಹಾಳು ಮಾಡು, to spoil something.

The word, taken apart.
ಹಾಳಾಗು (hāḷāgu) is native: ಹಾಳು, "ruin, a waste" (DEDR 4110, Kannada pāḻ), joined with ಆಗು, to become.

Lesson 2 of 5.
ಏಮಾರು (ēmāru) — to be off guard; to be fooled.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to spill over, then the Kannada for to get damaged.

You'll want to know: ಏಮಾರು.
ಏಮಾರು — ēmāru — "to be off guard; to be fooled".
ನಾನು ಒಂದು ಕ್ಷಣ ಏಮಾರಿದೆ, ಆಗ ಯಾರೋ ನನ್ನ ಚೀಲ ಕದ್ದರು. nānu ondu kṣaṇa ēmāride, āga yārō nanna cīla kaddaru. I let my guard down for a moment, and then someone stole my bag.
Past (I …): ಏಮಾರಿದೆ, ēmāride.
A moment of carelessness that someone takes advantage of.

The word, taken apart.
ಏಮಾರು (ēmāru) is usually linked to the native Dravidian family that DEDR 898 collects, Tamil and Telugu ēmāṟu, "to be beguiled, to be deceived"; DEDR gives the Kannada only with a query, as vēmāṟu, "to be careless", so the link is not settled.

Lesson 3 of 5.
ಪಾರಾಗು (pārāgu) — to escape unharmed; to get through safely.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to get damaged, then the Kannada for to be off guard.

You'll want to know: ಪಾರಾಗು.
ಪಾರಾಗು — pārāgu — "to escape unharmed; to get through safely".
ಅಪಘಾತದಲ್ಲಿ ಎಲ್ಲಾ ಪ್ರಯಾಣಿಕರು ಪಾರಾದರು. apaghātadalli ellā prayāṇikaru pārādaru. All the passengers came through the accident safely.
Past (I …): ಪಾರಾದೆ, pārāde.
What one escapes takes -ಇಂದ: ಅಪಾಯದಿಂದ ಪಾರಾಗು (pārāgu).

The word, taken apart.
ಪಾರಾಗು (pārāgu) joins Sanskrit pāra, "the further bank, the far end" (CDIAL 8100), and the native verb ಆಗು: to reach the other side.

Lesson 4 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to be off guard, then the Kannada for to escape unharmed.

You'll want to know: ಆಶ್ರಯ.
ಆಶ್ರಯ — āśraya — "shelter; refuge".
ಮಳೆ ಬಂದಾಗ ನಾವು ಒಂದು ಅಂಗಡಿಯಲ್ಲಿ ಆಶ್ರಯ ಪಡೆದೆವು. maḷe bandāga nāvu ondu aṅgaḍiyalli āśraya paḍedevu. When it rained we took shelter in a shop.
ಆಶ್ರಯ (āśraya) ಪಡೆ, to take shelter; ಆಶ್ರಯ (āśraya) ಕೊಡು, to give shelter.

The word, taken apart.
ಆಶ್ರಯ (āśraya) is borrowed from Sanskrit āśraya, "a resting place, a refuge, a dwelling" (CDIAL 1466).

Lesson 5 of 5.
ಸಹಾಯವಾಣಿ (sahāyavāṇi) — a helpline.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to escape unharmed, then the Kannada for shelter.

You'll want to know: ಸಹಾಯವಾಣಿ.
ಸಹಾಯವಾಣಿ — sahāyavāṇi — "a helpline".
ಚೀಲ ಸಿಗದಿದ್ದಾಗ ನಾವು ಸಹಾಯವಾಣಿಗೆ ಫೋನ್ ಮಾಡಿದೆವು. cīla sigadiddāga nāvu sahāyavāṇige phōn māḍidevu. When we could not find the bag we rang the helpline.
ಸಹಾಯ, help, plus ವಾಣಿ, voice.

The word, taken apart.
ಸಹಾಯವಾಣಿ (sahāyavāṇi) is a modern compound built from Sanskrit words: sahāya, "a companion, a helper" (CDIAL 13309), and vāṇī, "voice" (CDIAL 11490).
