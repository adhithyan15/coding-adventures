## You'll want to know: きょうみ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C433-LONG433-04]; assesses=[] -->

**きょうみ** — *kyōmi* — "an interest; curiosity".

> **おんがくに きょうみが あります。** — *ongaku ni kyōmi ga arimasu* — "I am interested in music."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**きょうみ** (*kyōmi*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *kyō*, "interest" (Middle Chinese *xingH*) and *mi*, "taste" (*mj+jH*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
