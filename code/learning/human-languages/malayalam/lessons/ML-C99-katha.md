# കഥ (katha) — a story

## You'll want to know
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C99-STORY-01]; assesses=[] -->

**കഥ** — *katha* — **a story.**

> **ഇത് നല്ല കഥയാണ്.** — *itŭ nalla kathayāṇŭ* — "This is a good story."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ML-LEX-C99-STORY-01, ML-LEX-C98-CINEMA-01, ML-LEX-AANU-01] -->

**You have a word that ends exactly as this one does.** **കഥ**'s last letter is
**ഥ**, carrying its own **അ** — and **സിനിമ** ends in **അ** too. So the same
**യ** slides in:

| ends in | word | joined to **ആണ്** |
|---|---|---|
| **അ** | **സിനിമ** | **സിനിമയാണ്** (*sinimayāṇŭ*) |
| **അ** | **കഥ** | **കഥയാണ്** (*kathayāṇŭ*) |

**That is the whole chapter in three words.** **പാട്ട്** gives a sound up,
**നൃത്തം** re-spells one, and **കഥ** takes a letter in — three endings, three
outcomes, and you were told each one before you met the word it lands on.
