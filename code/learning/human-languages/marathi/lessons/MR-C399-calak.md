## You'll want to know: चालक
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C399-VAHAK399-01]; assesses=[] -->

**चालक** — *cālak* — "a driver".

> चालकाने बस हळूहळू थांबवली.
>
> *cālakāne bas haḷūhaḷū thāmbavlī.*
>
> The driver brought the bus slowly to a stop.

Masculine.

The agent of a past transitive verb takes -ने: चालकाने बस थांबवली.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**चालक** (*cālak*) is a tatsama formation on *cāl-*, the causative stem of Sanskrit *cal*, "to move", with the agent ending *-aka*: the one who makes the vehicle move. The same stem gives *cālana*, "causing to move" (Monier-Williams).
