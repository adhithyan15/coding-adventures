## You'll want to know: दिवाला
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C430-DIVALA430-01]; assesses=[] -->

**दिवाला** — *divālā* — "bankruptcy".

> उस दुकानदार का दिवाला निकल गया।
>
> *us dukāndār kā divālā nikal gayā.*
>
> That shopkeeper went bankrupt.

Masculine.

दिवाला निकलना, to go bankrupt.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**दिवाला** (*divālā*) is filed by CDIAL under Sanskrit *dīpāvali*, "a row of lamps" (CDIAL 6358), beside दिवाली; how a row of lamps came to mean bankruptcy is not explained there.
