Punjabi, chapter 236: Scrubbing the Pots.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ਮਾਂਜਣਾ (mānjṇā) — to scrub (pots).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for mud plaster, then the Punjabi for to plaster.

You'll want to know: ਮਾਂਜਣਾ.
ਮਾਂਜਣਾ — mānjṇā — "to scrub (pots)".
ਮੈਂ ਰਾਤ ਨੂੰ ਭਾਂਡੇ ਮਾਂਜਦੀ ਹਾਂ। maiṁ rāt nū̃ bhāṇḍe mānjdī hāṁ. At night I scrub the pots.
Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਭਾਂਡੇ ਮਾਂਜੇ, maiṁ bhāṇḍe mānje.

The word, taken apart.
ਮਾਂਜਣਾ (mānjṇā) is inherited from Sanskrit mārjati, "wipes off, rubs clean"; CDIAL lists Punjabi mā̃jṇā, "to rub" (CDIAL 10080). The pot-scouring sense is CDIAL's Lahnda gloss: mā̃jhaṇ, "to clean out a vessel with sand or ashes".

Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਗਲਾਸ ਹੰਘਾਲਿਆ, maiṁ galās haṅghāliā.

The word, taken apart.
ਹੰਘਾਲਣਾ (haṅghālṇā) is inherited from a reconstructed āghālayati, "rinses"; CDIAL lists Punjabi hãghālṇā, "to rinse" (CDIAL 1058).

You'll want to know: ਉਲੱਦਣਾ.
ਉਲੱਦਣਾ — uladdṇā — "to tip out; to empty".
ਮੈਂ ਬਾਲਟੀ ਦਾ ਗੰਦਾ ਪਾਣੀ ਬਾਹਰ ਉਲੱਦਦੀ ਹਾਂ। maiṁ bālṭī dā gandā pāṇī bāhar uladddī hāṁ. I tip the dirty water from the bucket outside.
Past (I …): ਮੈਂ ਪਾਣੀ ਉਲੱਦਿਆ, maiṁ pāṇī uladdiā.

The word, taken apart.
ਉਲੱਦਣਾ (uladdṇā) is inherited from a reconstructed ullardayati, "overloads"; CDIAL lists Punjabi uladdṇā, "to overturn, upset" (CDIAL 2372).

Lesson 4 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for to rinse, then the Punjabi for to tip out.

You'll want to know: ਲੋਟਾ.
ਲੋਟਾ — loṭā — "a small water pot".
ਲੋਟੇ ਵਿੱਚ ਪਾਣੀ ਹੈ। loṭe vich pāṇī hai. There is water in the pot.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ਲੋਟਾ (loṭā) is inherited from a reconstructed lōṭṭa, "waterpot"; CDIAL lists Punjabi loṭṭā, "waterpot" (CDIAL 11133).
