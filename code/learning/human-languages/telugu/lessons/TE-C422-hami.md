# హామీ (hāmī) — a guarantee; an assurance

## You'll want to know: హామీ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C422-HAMI422-01]; assesses=[] -->

**హామీ** — *hāmī* — "a guarantee; an assurance".

> ప్రభుత్వం రైతులకు సహాయం చేస్తామని హామీ ఇచ్చింది.
>
> *prabhutvaṁ raitulaku sahāyaṁ cēstāmani hāmī iccindi.*
>
> The government assured the farmers that it would help.

హామీ ఇచ్చు, to promise, to guarantee.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**హామీ** (*hāmī*) has an origin that is not settled; it may have come into Telugu through Hindustani.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
