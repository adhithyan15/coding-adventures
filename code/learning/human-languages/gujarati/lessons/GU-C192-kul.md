## You'll want to know: કુલ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C192-BILL192-02]; assesses=[] -->

**કુલ** — *kul* — "total, in all".

> **કુલ સો રૂપિયા થયા.** — *kul so rūpiyā thayā* — "That came to a hundred rupees in all."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**કુલ** (*kul*) is a loan from Arabic *kull*, "all, the whole", which reached Gujarati through Persian. Its Arabic root is *k-l-l* (Lane).
