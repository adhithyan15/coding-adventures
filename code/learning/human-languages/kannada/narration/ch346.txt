Kannada, chapter 346: An Explosion and a Shock.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಸ್ಫೋಟ (sphōṭa) — an explosion; a blast.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a spy, then the Kannada for to sneak in.

You'll want to know: ಸ್ಫೋಟ.
ಸ್ಫೋಟ — sphōṭa — "an explosion; a blast".
ಕಾರ್ಖಾನೆಯಲ್ಲಿ ದೊಡ್ಡ ಸ್ಫೋಟ ಆಯಿತು. kārkhāneyalli doḍḍa sphōṭa āyitu. There was a big explosion at the factory.
Plural: ಸ್ಫೋಟಗಳು, sphōṭagaḷu.
ಸ್ಫೋಟಿಸು is to explode or to blow up.

The word, taken apart.
ಸ್ಫೋಟ (sphōṭa) is borrowed from Sanskrit sphoṭa, "bursting, opening, expansion" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 2 of 5.
ತತ್ತರಿಸು (tattarisu) — to reel; to be shaken.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: ತತ್ತರಿಸು.
ತತ್ತರಿಸು — tattarisu — "to reel; to be shaken".
ಆ ಸುದ್ದಿಯಿಂದ ಇಡೀ ಊರು ತತ್ತರಿಸಿತು. ā suddiyinda iḍī ūru tattarisitu. The whole town reeled at that news.
Past (it …): ತತ್ತರಿಸಿತು, tattarisitu.
A place or a person shaken by a blow or a loss.

The word, taken apart.
ತತ್ತರಿಸು (tattarisu) is native Dravidian: DEDR 3061 lists Kannada tattaṟa, tattaṟi, "perplexity, confusion", beside Tamil tattaram, "tremulousness, quivering, flurry", here with the verb ending -isu.

ಕಂಪಿಸು — kampisu — "to tremble; to shake".
ಸ್ಫೋಟದಿಂದ ಹತ್ತಿರದ ಮನೆಗಳು ಕಂಪಿಸಿದವು. sphōṭadinda hattirada manegaḷu kampisidavu. The houses nearby shook from the blast.
Past (it …): ಕಂಪಿಸಿತು, kampisitu.
The ground, a building or a voice trembles: ಭೂಮಿ ಕಂಪಿಸಿತು.

The word, taken apart.
ಕಂಪಿಸು (kampisu) is built on Sanskrit kampa, "trembling, a tremor" (CDIAL 2765), with the Kannada verb ending -isu.

ಆ ರಾತ್ರಿ ಪ್ರಳಯ ಬಂದ ಹಾಗೆ ಅನಿಸಿತು. ā rātri praḷaya banda hāge anisitu. That night it felt as if the end of the world had come.
In the old stories, the flood that ends the world; in talk, any huge disaster.

The word, taken apart.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ಗಾಯಾಳು (gāyāḷu) — an injured person; a casualty.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to tremble, then the Kannada for a deluge.

You'll want to know: ಗಾಯಾಳು.
ಗಾಯಾಳು — gāyāḷu — "an injured person; a casualty".
ಸ್ಫೋಟದ ನಂತರ ಗಾಯಾಳುಗಳನ್ನು ಆಸ್ಪತ್ರೆಗೆ ಕರೆದುಕೊಂಡು ಹೋದರು. sphōṭada nantara gāyāḷugaḷannu āspatrege karedukoṇḍu hōdaru. After the explosion the injured were taken to hospital.
Plural: ಗಾಯಾಳುಗಳು, gāyāḷugaḷu.
ಗಾಯ, a wound, and ಆಳು, a person: one who is hurt. A news word after accidents and blasts.

The word, taken apart.
ಗಾಯಾಳು (gāyāḷu) is a Kannada compound of ಗಾಯ, "a wound", which goes back to Sanskrit ghāta, "a blow, a bruise" (CDIAL 4460), and the native Dravidian āḷ, "a person" (DEDR 399): a wounded person.
