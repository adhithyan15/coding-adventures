Keep this key separate from the learner paper. It is an original project-defined
mock, not an official HSK paper. Read each listening recording script twice at
a natural, careful A1 pace. Do not show scripts, pinyin, or model answers to the
learner. Record 21–30 at 2–10 seconds each and 31–40 at 4–18 seconds each;
check actual recordings before use. Add brief lead/trail silence to a very short
cue rather than stretching the Mandarin unnaturally. The paper's English
options are fixed.

## Writing — 100 points

There is no single answer bank. Score the learner's independently produced
Chinese with the form-specific rubric. The examples below are assessor
calibration only and must never be supplied as a copyable model.

<!-- mock-part:a1-writing-practical-form -->
41. Five fields, 8 points each. Possible valid contents: an actual name;
    我是学生 or 我不是学生; 我在家看书 or 我在大学看书; 我有书 or 我有水;
    早上好 or 你好. Accept other truthful, comprehensible A1 equivalents.
<!-- /mock-part:a1-writing-practical-form -->

<!-- mock-part:a1-writing-short-message -->
42. Require 2–4 independently produced clauses: greeting, university-today
    answer, one thing to have, and one simple question back. A
    calibration response could be “你好！我今天去大学。我有书。你呢？”
    Do not require these exact words; the rubric scores communication.
<!-- /mock-part:a1-writing-short-message -->

## Speaking — 100 points

The examiner says these prompts aloud in Mandarin without showing this page.
One slow repeat is allowed. Time and record the responses for independent
scoring. Avoid coaching, paraphrasing, or supplying content words.

<!-- mock-part:a1-speaking-personal-exchange -->
43. “你好！” — a reciprocal greeting, e.g. 你好, is appropriate.
44. “你叫什么名字？” — the learner gives a name and asks yours.
45. “你是学生吗？” — the learner answers truthfully and asks back.
46. “你今天在家看书吗？” — the learner names a place or answers truthfully.
47. “你想买什么？” — the learner names something to buy.
<!-- /mock-part:a1-speaking-personal-exchange -->

<!-- mock-part:a1-speaking-rehearsed-role-play -->
48. Tell the learner in English that you are the station helper, then ask
    “你好，你要去哪里？” Respond “门口在前边。” if asked for the doorway;
    answer the follow-up “出口在门口。” The learner must initiate both
    place questions and sustain a 20–45-second exchange. Do not score a
    memorized monologue as an exchange.
<!-- /mock-part:a1-speaking-rehearsed-role-play -->

Score each skill out of 100. A pass requires 60/100 in **each** skill, not a
compensating average. Keep the scripts and calibration answers secure.
