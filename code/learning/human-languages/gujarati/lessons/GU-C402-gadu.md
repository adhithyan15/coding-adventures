## You'll want to know: ગાડું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C402-CART402-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ગાડું** — *gāḍũ* — "a cart (a bullock cart)".

> બળદનું ગાડું ધીમે ચાલે છે.
>
> *baḷadnũ gāḍũ dhīme cāle che.*
>
> The bullock cart moves slowly.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ગાડું** (*gāḍũ*) goes back to *\*gāḍḍa*, "a cart", a form CDIAL reconstructs; CDIAL lists the Gujarati word (CDIAL 4116). ગાડી, a car, is its relative: the cart came first.
