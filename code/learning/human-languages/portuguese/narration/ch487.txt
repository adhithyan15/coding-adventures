Portuguese, chapter 487: Na consulta.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
o sintoma — symptom.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for sprain, then the Portuguese for bandage.

You'll want to know: o sintoma.
o sintoma — "symptom".
A febre e a tosse são sintomas da gripe. Fever and a cough are symptoms of flu.
Masculine, though it ends in -a: o sintoma.

The word, taken apart.
sintoma goes back through Latin to Greek sýmptōma "a happening, a chance event, a symptom" (EtymDB).

Fazer análises — to have tests done. Singular a análise — an analysis.

análise is a learned word, usually traced through Medieval Latin to Greek análysis "a loosening, a breaking up".

Lesson 4 of 5.
receitar — to prescribe.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for (medical) tests, then the Portuguese for diagnosis.

You'll want to know: receitar.
receitar — "to prescribe".
O médico receitou-me um xarope. The doctor prescribed me a syrup.
Built on a receita, which is a prescription and also a recipe.

The word, taken apart.
receitar is built on receita "a prescription, a recipe", from Latin recepta "things received" (EtymDB), from recipere "to take back" (Lewis & Short).
