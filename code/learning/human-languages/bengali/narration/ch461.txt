Bengali, chapter 461: Stubborn and Restless.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
জেদি (jedi) — headstrong; stubborn.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for selfish, then the Bengali for to oppress.

You'll want to know: জেদি.
জেদি — jedi — "headstrong; stubborn".
আমার জেদি বোন খেলনাটা ছাড়ল না। āmār jedi bon khelnāṭā chhāṛlô nā. My headstrong sister would not let go of the toy.

The word, taken apart.
জেদি (jedi) is jed, "stubbornness", usually traced to Arabic ḍidd, "opposition", through Persian, with the ending -i.

You'll want to know: চঞ্চল.
চঞ্চল — chônchôl — "restless; never still".
চঞ্চল শিশুটা এক জায়গায় বসে থাকে না। chônchôl shishuṭā ek jāygāy bôse thāke nā. The fidgety child never stays sitting in one place.

The word, taken apart.
চঞ্চল (chônchôl) is a tatsama, Sanskrit cañcala, "moving to and fro, unsteady, flickering" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 4 of 6.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: খামখেয়ালি.
খামখেয়ালি — khāmkheyāli — "whimsical; capricious".
আমার খামখেয়ালি মামা হঠাৎ চাকরি ছাড়লেন। āmār khāmkheyāli māmā hôṭhāt chākri chhāṛlen. My whimsical uncle suddenly quit his job.

The word, taken apart.
খামখেয়ালি (khāmkheyāli) is usually traced to Persian khām, "raw, unripe", and khayāl, "a fancy" (Arabic, through Persian), the খেয়াল of খেয়াল করা (taught): a half-baked fancy.

Lesson 5 of 6.
গোঁ ধরা (gõ dhôrā) — to dig one's heels in.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for never still, then the Bengali for whimsical.

You'll want to know: গোঁ ধরা.
গোঁ ধরা — gõ dhôrā — "to dig one's heels in".
নতুন সাইকেলের জন্য ছেলেটা গোঁ ধরল। nôtun sāikeler jônyô chheleṭā gõ dhôrlô. The boy dug his heels in about getting a new bicycle.
Past (he/she …): তিনি গোঁ ধরলেন, tini gõ dhôrlen.

The word, taken apart.
গোঁ ধরা (gõ dhôrā) is gõ, a stubborn will, which CDIAL lists for Bengali as a whim (CDIAL 4025), with ধরা (taught), to hold (CDIAL 6747).
