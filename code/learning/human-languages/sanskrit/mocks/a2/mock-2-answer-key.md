## Writing

Task 1 must name the morning registration, give a reason, request the evening
class, and ask for confirmation. Task 2 must state the old problem, explain a
feedback-led change, give two effects, and name one remaining limitation.
Apply the analytic rubric; no fixed script is required or available to the
learner.

## Speaking

The interview needs relevant reasons or details. The account must sustain
90–120 seconds with a problem–action–result sequence. The role-play must obtain
the new time and place, explain a difficulty, compare two alternatives, choose
one, and confirm the plan. Score interactional adaptation rather than memorised
wording, using one consistently documented pronunciation tradition.
