## Chapters 100-149: 250 headwords, can, want and why, and Telugu attains A1

Telugu had four A1 gaps:

- It was 245 headwords short.
- It was 30 verbs short.
- Six A1 spine nodes had no segment: ASK-LOCATION, SAY-WHAT-I-HAVE-AND-CAN-DO,
  SAY-WHAT-I-WANT, SAY-WHY, NAME-EVERYDAY-THINGS and DESCRIBE-QUALITIES.
- Nineteen atoms were revisited fewer than twice.

**The words.** Fifty chapters of five:

- **places and directions:** **కుడి**, **ఎడమ**, **తిన్నగా**, **దగ్గర**, **దూరం**,
  **లోపల**, **బయట**, the street, the bridge, the temple, the library and more
  (ASK-LOCATION)
- **can:** **సామర్థ్యం**, **నైపుణ్యం**, **హక్కు**, **అవకాశం**, **పరికరం**, **ఆస్తి**,
  **యజమాని**, **బలం** (SAY-WHAT-I-HAVE-AND-CAN-DO)
- **want:** **కోరిక**, **ఆశ**, **కల**, **ఉద్దేశం**, **ప్రణాళిక**, **కోరు**,
  **నిర్ణయించు** (SAY-WHAT-I-WANT)
- **why:** **కారణం**, **అందువల్ల**, **వల్ల**, **అందుకే** (SAY-WHY)
- **things:** 136 of them, covering the house, things you carry and wear,
  food, nature, animals, the body and getting around (NAME-EVERYDAY-THINGS)
- **qualities:** thirty-four describing words (DESCRIBE-QUALITIES)
- **verbs:** thirty-six stems, in the bare-command form the track already uses

**Checks.** Every candidate was checked against every earlier headword,
romanization and lesson text, with Telugu letter boundaries. Every word uses
only letters the book has already taught the reader to write. Five candidates
were dropped because they need **ఊ** or **ఓ**, which the book has not taught
yet. Forward references stay at 11, and letter anchoring stays at zero cold
letters.

**The revisits.** Twenty-seven thin atoms each get a warm-up retrieval:

- adjectives before the noun
- the ordinals **పదవ** and **పదకొండవ**
- counting from one to ten
- the chapter-83 reading skills
- **ఇక్కడ**, **అక్కడ** and **ఎక్కడ**
- the seasons
- the months and **ఆదివారం**
- the afternoon and night greetings
- eight commands
- **తాత** and **అమ్మమ్మ**
