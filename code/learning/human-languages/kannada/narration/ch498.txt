Kannada, chapter 498: Trust and Sympathy.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ನಂಬು (nambu) — to believe; to trust.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for modesty, then the Kannada for self-respect.

You'll want to know: ನಂಬು.
ನಂಬು — nambu — "to believe; to trust".
ಅವನ ಮಾತನ್ನು ಯಾರೂ ನಂಬಲಿಲ್ಲ. avana mātannu yārū nambalilla. Nobody believed what he said.
Past (I …): ನಂಬಿದೆ, nambide.
What or whom you believe takes -ಅನ್ನು. The noun is ನಂಬಿಕೆ, trust.

The word, taken apart.
ನಂಬು (nambu) is native Dravidian: DEDR 3600 lists Kannada nambu, "to trust, believe", and nambike, "trust", beside Tamil nampu.

Lesson 2 of 5.
ಮನ್ನಿಸು (mannisu) — to forgive; to excuse.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for self-respect, then the Kannada for to believe.

You'll want to know: ಮನ್ನಿಸು.
ಮನ್ನಿಸು — mannisu — "to forgive; to excuse".
ತಡವಾಗಿ ಬಂದಿದ್ದಕ್ಕೆ ಮನ್ನಿಸಿ ಅಂತ ಅವನು ಕೇಳಿಕೊಂಡನು. taḍavāgi bandiddakke mannisi anta avanu kēḷikoṇḍanu. He asked to be excused for coming late.
Past (I …): ಮನ್ನಿಸಿದೆ, manniside.
Polite: ಮನ್ನಿಸಿ, excuse me, close to ಕ್ಷಮಿಸಿ.

The word, taken apart.
ಮನ್ನಿಸು (mannisu) is usually traced to Sanskrit mānana, "honouring, showing respect" (Monier-Williams), with the verb ending -isu; to pardon someone is to treat them with regard. The path is not settled.

Lesson 4 of 5.
ಕರುಣೆ (karuṇe) — compassion; mercy.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to forgive, then the Kannada for suspicion.

You'll want to know: ಕರುಣೆ.
ಕರುಣೆ — karuṇe — "compassion; mercy".
ಬಡವರ ಮೇಲೆ ಅವಳಿಗೆ ತುಂಬಾ ಕರುಣೆ ಇದೆ. baḍavara mēle avaḷige tumbā karuṇe ide. She has great compassion for the poor.
Close to ದಯೆ, kindness; ಕರುಣೆ (karuṇe) is pity that moves you.

The word, taken apart.
ಕರುಣೆ (karuṇe) is borrowed from Sanskrit karuṇā, "pity" (CDIAL 2811); the final -ā became -ೆ, as in many Kannada loans.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ಸಹಾನುಭೂತಿ (sahānubhūti) — sympathy.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for suspicion, then the Kannada for compassion.

You'll want to know: ಸಹಾನುಭೂತಿ.
ಸಹಾನುಭೂತಿ — sahānubhūti — "sympathy".
ನೆರೆಯವರು ನಮ್ಮ ನಷ್ಟಕ್ಕೆ ಸಹಾನುಭೂತಿ ತೋರಿಸಿದರು. nereyavaru namma naṣṭakke sahānubhūti tōrisidaru. The neighbours showed sympathy for our loss.
ಸಹ, with + ಅನುಭೂತಿ, feeling: feeling along with someone.

The word, taken apart.
ಸಹಾನುಭೂತಿ (sahānubhūti) is a learned compound built from Sanskrit saha, "with", and anubhūti, "perception, experience" (Monier-Williams).
