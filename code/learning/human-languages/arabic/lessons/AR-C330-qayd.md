## You'll want to know: قيد
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C330-SIJN330-04]; assesses=[] -->

**قيد** — *qayd* — "a shackle; a restriction".

> وضعت الشرطة القيد في يدي اللص.
>
> *waḍaʿat ash-shurṭa al-qayd fī yaday al-liṣṣ.*
>
> The police put the handcuffs on the thief’s hands.

Plural: قيود, *quyūd*.

على قيد الحياة: alive, "in the bond of life".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**قيد** (*qayd*) is built on the native Arabic root *q-y-d* (Lane), where *qayd* is the hobble put on a horse or camel, and *qayyada*, "to tie; to note down".
