## You'll want to know: पसारा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C474-SHISTA474-04]; assesses=[] -->

**पसारा** — *pasārā* — "a mess; clutter".

> मुलांच्या खोलीत खूप पसारा आहे.
>
> *mulāñcyā kholīt khūp pasārā āhe.*
>
> There is a big mess in the children's room.

Masculine.

Masculine: पसारा करणे, to make a mess; पसारा आवरणे, to clear it up.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पसारा** (*pasārā*) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Marathi *pasārā*, "stretching out, expansion", under Sanskrit *prasāra*, "extension" (CDIAL 8835). Things spread out all over a room make a mess.
