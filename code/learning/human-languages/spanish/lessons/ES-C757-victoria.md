## You'll want to know: la victoria
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C757-CAMPEON-03]; assesses=[] -->

**la victoria** — "victory, win".

> Fue una victoria importante.
>
> *It was an important win.*

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*victoria* is a learned borrowing from Latin *victōria* "victory" (EtymDB), from *vincere* "to conquer".
