## You'll want to know: کارشناس
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C381-SARPARAST-03]; assesses=[] -->

**کارشناس** (*kârshenâs*) — "an expert, an analyst".

> یک کارشناس خانه را دید و قیمت گذاشت.
>
> *yek kârshenâs khâne râ did va qeymat gozâsht.*
>
> An expert looked at the house and set a price.

*Kâr* "work" + *shenâs* "knowing". *Kârshenâsi* is also a bachelor's degree.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**کارشناس** is native Persian, built from *kâr* "work" + *shenâs-*, the present stem of *shenâkhtan* "to know", which EtymDB traces through Old Persian to PIE *ǵneh₃-* "to know".
