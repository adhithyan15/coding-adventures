## You'll want to know: sījī
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C80-JOB80-03]; assesses=[] -->

*sījī* — "driver".

> *tā shì chūzūchē sījī* — He's a taxi driver.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*sījī* is built from sī 'to be in charge' and 机 'machine', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *si* and *kj+j* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription): the one in charge of the machine.
