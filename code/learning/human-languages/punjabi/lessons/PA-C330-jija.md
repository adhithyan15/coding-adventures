## You'll want to know: ਜੀਜਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C330-SPOUSE330-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ਜੀਜਾ** — *jījā* — "a sister's husband".

> ਮੇਰਾ ਜੀਜਾ ਬਹੁਤ ਹੱਸਦਾ ਹੈ।
>
> *merā jījā bahut hassdā hai.*
>
> My sister's husband laughs a lot.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਜੀਜਾ** (*jījā*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*jījja*, "a nursery word for breast, mother, sister, relative"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *jījā*, "sister's husband", under it (CDIAL 5232).
