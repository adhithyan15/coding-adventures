# બેસણું (besṇũ) — a prayer meeting after a death

## You'll want to know: બેસણું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C499-MARAN499-04]; assesses=[] -->

**બેસણું** — *besṇũ* — "a prayer meeting after a death".

> સોમવારે સાંજે દાદાનું બેસણું છે.
>
> *somvāre sā̃je dādānũ besṇũ che.*
>
> Grandfather's prayer meeting is on Monday evening.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**બેસણું** (*besṇũ*) is built on **બેસવું** (*besvũ*), "to sit", which goes back to Sanskrit *upaviśati*, "sits down" (CDIAL 2245): the family sits together to receive visitors.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
