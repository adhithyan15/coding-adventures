## You'll want to know: پاساژ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C460-VITRIN-02]; assesses=[] -->

**پاساژ** (*pâsâzh*) — "a shopping arcade, a mall".

> در پاساژ یک کافه کوچک هست.
>
> *Dar pâsâzh yek kâfe-ye kuchek hast.*
>
> There is a small café in the arcade.

Iranian towns have many *pâsâzh*, covered buildings full of small shops.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*pâsâzh* was borrowed from French *passage* (EtymDB), a passage, and in French also a covered arcade of shops.
