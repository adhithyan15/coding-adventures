## You'll want to know: os escombros
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C302-SISMO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**os escombros** — "the rubble, the ruins".

> Os bombeiros procuram pessoas nos escombros.
>
> *The firefighters are searching the rubble for people.*

Almost always plural.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

The origin of *escombros* is not settled; it sits beside Spanish *escombro*, and EtymDB has no entry.
