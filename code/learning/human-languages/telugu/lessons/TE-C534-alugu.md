## You'll want to know: అలుగు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C534-GODAVA534-03]; assesses=[] -->

**అలుగు** — *alugu* — "to sulk; to take offence".

> బొమ్మ కొనలేదని పాప అలిగింది.
>
> *bomma konalēdani pāpa aligindi.*
>
> The little girl sulked because nobody bought her the doll.

Past (I …): అలిగాను, *aligānu*.

The noun is అలక, a sulk.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**అలుగు** (*alugu*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 304 lists Telugu *alugu*, "to be angry or displeased, to take offence", and *aluka*, "displeasure".
