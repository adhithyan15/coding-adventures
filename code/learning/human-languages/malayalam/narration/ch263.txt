Malayalam, chapter 263: Carpenter, Mason, Watchman, Bakery, Supermarket.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
ആശാരി (āśāri) — a carpenter.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for a temple priest, then the Malayalam for a singer.

You'll want to know: ആശാരി.
ആശാരി — āśāri — "a carpenter".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: āśāri]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: āśāri, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say āśāri]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Malayalam for a temple priest, then the Malayalam for a singer, then say āśāri again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does ആശാരി (āśāri) mean? ("A carpenter".) Say it once more.

A shopkeeper? (കച്ചവടക്കാരൻ, kaccavaṭakkāran.) A weighing scale? (തുലാസ്, tulāsŭ.) A kilo? (കിലോ, kilō.) A thing? (സാധനം, sādhanaṁ.)
