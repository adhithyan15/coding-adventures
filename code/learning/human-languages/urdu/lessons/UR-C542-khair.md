## You'll want to know: خیر
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C542-KHAIR542-02]; assesses=[] -->

**خیر** — *khair* — "well (moving on)".

> خیر، اگر وہ نہیں آیا تو ہم چلیں گے۔
>
> *khair, agar voh nahīṅ āyā to ham chaleṅ ge.*
>
> Well, if he doesn't come, we'll leave.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**خیر** (*khair*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *khayr*, "good": all is well, let us move on.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
