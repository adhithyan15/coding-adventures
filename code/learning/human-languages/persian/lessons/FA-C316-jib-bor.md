## You'll want to know: جیب بر
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C316-DOZD-04]; assesses=[] -->

**جیب بر** (*jib bor*) — "a pickpocket".

> در اتوبوس یک جیب بر گوشی مرا برد.
>
> *Dar otobus yek jib bor gushi-ye marâ bord.*
>
> On the bus a pickpocket took my phone.

*jib* "pocket" + *bor*, the present stem of *boridan* "to cut": a pocket-cutter.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*jib* "pocket" is borrowed from Arabic *jayb* (EtymDB). *bor-* is the present stem of the native verb *boridan* "to cut".
