## You'll want to know: বেয়ারা
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C407-BOKSHISH407-02]; assesses=[] -->

**বেয়ারা** — *beyārā* — "a bearer; a waiter (in a hotel)".

> বেয়ারা আমাদের ঘরে চা নিয়ে এল।
>
> *beyārā āmāder ghôre chā niye elô.*
>
> The bearer brought tea to our room.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**বেয়ারা** (*beyārā*) is usually traced to English *bearer*, the old word in Indian hotels and clubs for a servant who carries and serves.
