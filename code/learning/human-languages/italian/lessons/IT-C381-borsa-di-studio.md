## You'll want to know: la borsa di studio
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C381-DOCENTE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**la borsa di studio** — "the scholarship, the grant".

> Se ho buoni voti, posso avere una borsa di studio.
>
> *If I get good marks, I can get a scholarship.*

Literally a purse for study.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*borsa* is inherited from Late Latin *bursa* "a leather bag", from Greek *býrsa* "a hide"; *studio* is a learned borrowing from Latin *studium* "zeal, eagerness, study" (Lewis & Short).
