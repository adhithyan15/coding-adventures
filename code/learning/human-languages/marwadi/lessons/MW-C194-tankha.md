## You'll want to know: तनखा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C194-EARN194-03]; assesses=[] -->

**तनखा** — *tankhā* — "a salary".

> म्हारी तनखा घणी कोनी।
>
> *mhārī tankhā ghaṇī konī.*
>
> My salary isn't large.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**तनखा** (*tankhā*) is usually traced to Persian *tankhwāh*, "salary".
