## You'll want to know: विचारसरणी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C367-PRATHA367-03]; assesses=[] -->

**विचारसरणी** — *vicārsarṇī* — "an ideology; a way of thinking".

> दोघांची विचारसरणी अगदी वेगळी आहे.
>
> *doghāñcī vicārsarṇī agdī vegḷī āhe.*
>
> The two have very different ways of thinking.

Feminine.

विचार + सरणी, thought + path.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**विचारसरणी** (*vicārsarṇī*) is a learned compound built from विचार, "thought", and Sanskrit *saraṇi*, "path, way" (Monier-Williams).

## What you've built
