## You'll want to know: il chiacchierone
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C471-TIMIDEZZA-04]; assesses=[] -->

**il chiacchierone** — "chatterbox; talkative".

> Il mio vicino è un gran chiacchierone.
>
> *My neighbour is a real chatterbox.*

A woman is *la chiacchierona*. It is built on *chiacchierare* "to chat".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*chiacchierone* is built on *chiacchierare* "to chat" with *-one*, the ending for someone who does a lot of something (EtymDB). The verb itself is usually taken to imitate the sound of chatter.
