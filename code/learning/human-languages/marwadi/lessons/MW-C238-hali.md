## You'll want to know: हाळी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C238-SOW238-04]; assesses=[] -->

**हाळी** — *hāḷī* — "a ploughman".

> हाळी तड़कै खेत जावै है।
>
> *hāḷī taṛkai khet jāvai hai.*
>
> The ploughman goes to the field at daybreak.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**हाळी** (*hāḷī*) is inherited from Sanskrit *hālika*, "a ploughman", from *hala*, "a plough"; CDIAL lists Hindi *hālī* and Gujarati *hāḷi*, "a ploughman". CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry (CDIAL 14067).
