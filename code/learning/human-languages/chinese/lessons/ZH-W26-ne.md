## Script — the same mouth, a different right half
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-SCRIPT-NE-01]; assesses=[] -->

> 呢

**Eight strokes**, and the first three are ones you have written since the fourth
chapter.

1. 口's **shù** down the left side
2. 口's **héngzhé** across the top and down the right
3. 口's **héng** closing the floor

That is the same 口, in the same order, set small and high in the upper-left third
of the square — **exactly where 吗 puts it.** Finish it before the right half
starts.

Then 尼 *ní*, which supplies only the sound:

4. the **héngzhé** across the top of the right half and down at its right
5. the **héng** beneath it
6. the long **piě**, falling from the top all the way down to the lower left,
   past the bottom of 口
7. a short **piě** inside, falling left
8. the **shùwāngōu**, turning along the base and finishing to the right

**Stroke 6 is the one that carries the character.** It starts high and reaches
the lower-left corner, and everything after it tucks inside.
