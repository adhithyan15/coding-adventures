## You'll want to know: きず
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C218-HOSPITAL218-04]; assesses=[] -->

**きず** — *kizu* — "a wound; a cut".

> **きずは ふかいですか。** — *kizu wa fukai desu ka* — "Is the wound deep?"

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**きず** (*kizu*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi, and its older origin is not settled (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
