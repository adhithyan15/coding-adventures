## You'll want to know: ગુલામ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C325-AZADI325-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ગુલામ** — *gulām* — "a slave".

> જૂના વખતમાં ઘણા લોકો ગુલામ હતા.
>
> *jūnā vakhatmā̃ ghaṇā loko gulām hatā.*
>
> In the old days many people were slaves.

A masculine or feminine noun, by the person meant.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *ġulām*, "a boy, a servant". In Gujarati it is a slave, and a people not free are spoken of as **ગુલામ**.
