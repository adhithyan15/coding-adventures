## You'll want to know: घोंगावणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C439-VAVTAL439-04]; assesses=[] -->

**घोंगावणे** — *ghoṅgāvṇe* — "to howl; to roar".

> रात्रभर वारा घोंगावत होता.
>
> *rātrabhar vārā ghoṅgāvat hotā.*
>
> The wind howled all night.

Past (he …): तो घोंगावला, *to ghoṅgāvlā*.

For strong wind and for swarming bees.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**घोंगावणे** (*ghoṅgāvṇe*) is an echo verb for the roar of wind or the drone of bees; its origin is not settled.
