## You'll want to know: 请客
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C173-INVITE173-05]; assesses=[] -->

**请客** — *qǐngkè* — "to pay for everyone; to treat (the others)".

> **今天我请客每个人一杯茶** *jīntiān wǒ qǐngkè měi ge rén yì bēi chá* — It's on me today: a cup of tea each.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**请客** (*qǐngkè*) is built from 请 'to ask, to invite' and 客 'guest', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *tshjengX* and *khaek* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription): to have the others as your guests, and so to pay.
