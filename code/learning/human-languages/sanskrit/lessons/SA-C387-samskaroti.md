## You'll want to know: संस्करोति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C387-UNNATI387-04]; assesses=[] -->

**संस्करोति** — *saṃskaroti* — "refines; improves".

> यदि लेखकः लेखं संस्करोति, तर्हि सः उत्तमः भविष्यति।
>
> *yadi lekhakaḥ lekhaṃ saṃskaroti, tarhi saḥ uttamaḥ bhaviṣyati.*
>
> If the writer polishes his article, it will be excellent.

Past (he …): सः लेखं संस्कृतवान्, *saḥ lekhaṃ saṃskṛtavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**संस्करोति** (*saṃskaroti*) is built on *sam*, "together", and the root √kṛ, "to make", with an inserted *s*; MW gives *saṃ-s-√kṛ* "to put together, to prepare, to refine" and *saṃ-skṛta* "perfected" (MW).
