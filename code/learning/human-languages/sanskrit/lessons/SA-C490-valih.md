## You'll want to know: वलिः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C490-RUPA490-04]; assesses=[] -->

**वलिः** — *valiḥ* — "a wrinkle".

> वृद्धस्य ललाटे वलिः अस्ति।
>
> *vṛddhasya lalāṭe valiḥ asti.*
>
> There is a wrinkle on the old man's forehead.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**वलिः** (*valiḥ*) MW gives "a fold of the skin, a wrinkle" and lists the spellings *bali* and *valī* beside it; MW gives no derivation, so its origin is uncertain (MW).
