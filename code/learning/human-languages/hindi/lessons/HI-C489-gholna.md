## You'll want to know: घोलना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C489-MARHAM489-01]; assesses=[] -->

**घोलना** — *gholnā* — "to dissolve; to stir in".

> मीरा ने पानी में दवा घोली।
>
> *Mīrā ne pānī meṁ davā gholī.*
>
> Mira dissolved the medicine in water.

Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने दवा घोली, *usne davā gholī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**घोलना** (*gholnā*) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Hindi *gholnā*, "to mix, dissolve, melt", under Sanskrit *ghōlayati* (CDIAL 4526).
