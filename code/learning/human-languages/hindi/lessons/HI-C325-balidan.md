## You'll want to know: बलिदान
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C325-ANDOLAN325-04]; assesses=[] -->

**बलिदान** — *balidān* — "a sacrifice".

> उस आंदोलन में हज़ारों लोगों ने बलिदान दिया।
>
> *us āndolan meṁ hazāroṁ logoṁ ne balidān diyā.*
>
> Thousands of people gave their lives in that movement.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**बलिदान** (*balidān*) is Sanskrit *balidāna*, *bali*, an offering, and *dāna*, giving (Monier-Williams). In history it is giving one's life for others.
