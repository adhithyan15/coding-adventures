## You'll want to know: terō, terere
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C351-CONCIDO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**terō, terere** — "to grind, to rub".

> Ancilla piper pistillō terēbat.
>
> *The maid was grinding pepper with the pestle.*

*terō, terere, trīvī, trītum*. English *trite*, worn out by rubbing.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**terō** is an inherited verb; Lewis & Short compare Greek *teirō*, "to wear away". English *trite* comes from its participle *trītus*.
