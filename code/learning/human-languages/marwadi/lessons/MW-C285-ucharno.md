## You'll want to know: उचारणो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C285-AKHAR285-05]; assesses=[] -->

**उचारणो** — *uchārṇo* — "to pronounce".

> छोरो आखर उचारै है।
>
> *chhoro ākhar uchārai hai.*
>
> The boy pronounces the letters.

Past (I …): म्हैं सबद उचार्यो, *mhaĩ sabad uchāryo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**उचारणो** (*uchārṇo*) is inherited from Sanskrit *uccārayati*, "utters"; CDIAL lists Old Marwari *ūcaraï*, "recites", and Hindi *ucārnā*, "to pronounce" (CDIAL 1641).
