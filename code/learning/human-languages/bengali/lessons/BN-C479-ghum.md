## You'll want to know: ঘুম
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C479-GHUM479-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ঘুম** — *ghum* — "sleep".

> কাল রাতে আমার ভালো ঘুম হল।
>
> *kāl rāte āmār bhālo ghum hôlô.*
>
> I slept well last night.

ঘুম পাওয়া, to feel sleepy: আমার ঘুম পাচ্ছে.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ঘুম** (*ghum*) is listed by CDIAL for Bengali, as dozing, under a reconstructed verb of turning round (CDIAL 4485); it shows the new letter ঘ.
