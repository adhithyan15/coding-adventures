## You'll want to know: خراش
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C285-PHISAL285-05]; assesses=[] -->

**خراش** — *kharāsh* — "a scratch; a graze".

> ہاتھ پر ایک خراش تھی۔
>
> *hāth par ek kharāsh thī.*
>
> There was a scratch on the hand.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**خراش** (*kharāsh*) is usually traced to Persian *kharāsh*, "a scratching, a scraping". It is feminine: **ایک خراش تھی**.
