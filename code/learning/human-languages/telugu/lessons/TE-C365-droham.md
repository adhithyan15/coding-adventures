## You'll want to know: ద్రోహం
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C365-DROHAM365-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ద్రోహం** — *drōhaṁ* — "a betrayal; treachery".

> సేనాధిపతి రాజుకు ద్రోహం చేశాడు.
>
> *sēnādhipati rājuku drōhaṁ cēśāḍu.*
>
> The commander betrayed the king.

ద్రోహం చేయు, to betray; the one betrayed takes -కు.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ద్రోహం** (*drōhaṁ*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *droha*, "injury, perfidy, treachery" (Monier-Williams).
