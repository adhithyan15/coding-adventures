## You'll want to know: فطم
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C530-ATFAL530-04]; assesses=[] -->

**فطم** — *faṭama* — "to wean".

> فطمت أختي ابنها بعد سنتين.
>
> *faṭamat ukhtī ibnahā baʿda sanatayn.*
>
> My sister weaned her son after two years.

Present: يفطم, *yafṭim*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**فطم** (*faṭama*) is built on the native Arabic root *f-ṭ-m*, "to cut off" (Lane). Lane gives faṭama, aor. yafṭimu, as to cut off: weaning cuts the child off the breast.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
