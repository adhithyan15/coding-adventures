Hindi, chapter 227: To tear, To tear, To bring up, To split, To sow.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
फटना (phaṭnā) — to tear, to burst.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for to be made, then the Hindi for to break.

You'll want to know: फटना.
फटना — phaṭnā — "to tear, to burst".

The word, taken apart.
फटना (phaṭnā) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from sphāṭayati, "causes to split" (CDIAL 13825). phaṭnā is the self-acting form, with the short vowel such verbs take.

The word, taken apart.
चीरना (cīrnā) is a tadbhava, a word inherited and reshaped by sound change. It comes from cīrayati, "splits", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 4844). It is built on cīra, "strip of cloth".
