## You'll want to know: क्षतम्
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C480-DAMSA480-03]; assesses=[] -->

**क्षतम्** — *kṣatam* — "an injury".

> क्रीडायां तस्य पादे क्षतम् अभवत्।
>
> *krīḍāyāṃ tasya pāde kṣatam abhavat.*
>
> He hurt his foot while playing.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**क्षतम्** (*kṣatam*) comes from the root √kṣaṇ, "to wound"; MW gives "wounded, hurt, injured" and the noun "a wound, an injury" (MW).
