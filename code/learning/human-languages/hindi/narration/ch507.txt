Hindi, chapter 507: People at Work.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
सहकर्मी (sahkarmī) — a colleague; a co-worker.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for to shape, then the Hindi for a mechanic.

You'll want to know: सहकर्मी.
सहकर्मी — sahkarmī — "a colleague; a co-worker".
मीरा अपनी सहकर्मी के साथ दोपहर का खाना खाती है। Mīrā apnī sahkarmī ke sāth dopahar kā khānā khātī hai. Mira has lunch with her colleague.
Masculine or feminine, by the person meant.

The word, taken apart.
सहकर्मी (sahkarmī) is a modern compound of two Sanskrit words, saha, "with", and karmin, "active, busy" (Monier-Williams): one who works alongside you.

Lesson 3 of 5.
उस्ताद (ustād) — a master (of a craft or an art); a teacher.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a colleague, then the Hindi for an officer.

You'll want to know: उस्ताद.
उस्ताद — ustād — "a master (of a craft or an art); a teacher".
मेरे उस्ताद ने मुझे लकड़ी का काम सिखाया। Mere ustād ne mujhe lakṛī kā kām sikhāyā. My master taught me woodwork.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
उस्ताद (ustād) is usually traced to Persian ustād, "a master". In Hindi it is the master of a craft or an art, and the teacher who passes it on.

Warm-up.

Before the new one: say the Hindi for an officer, then the Hindi for a master (of a craft or an art).

कौशल — kauśal — "skill; know-how (formal)".
हर कारीगर के पास अपना कौशल होता है। Har kārīgar ke pās apnā kauśal hotā hai. Every craftsman has his own skill.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
कौशल (kauśal) is a tatsama, taken whole from Sanskrit kauśala, "well-being; skilfulness, cleverness" (Monier-Williams).
