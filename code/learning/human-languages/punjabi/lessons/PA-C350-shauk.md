## You'll want to know: ਸ਼ੌਕ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C350-SHOW350-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ਸ਼ੌਕ** — *shauk* — "a hobby; a liking".

> ਮੇਰਾ ਸ਼ੌਕ ਸੰਗੀਤ ਹੈ।
>
> *merā shauk saṅgīt hai.*
>
> My hobby is music.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਸ਼ੌਕ** (*shauk*) is a loan, usually traced through Persian to Arabic *shawq*, "longing".
