## You'll want to know: उत्सः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C419-VAPI419-02]; assesses=[] -->

**उत्सः** — *utsaḥ* — "a spring of water".

> पर्वते शीतलः उत्सः अस्ति।
>
> *parvate śītalaḥ utsaḥ asti.*
>
> There is a cool spring on the mountain.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**उत्सः** (*utsaḥ*) comes from the root √ud, "to wet", as MW gives it; MW gives *utsa* as a spring or fountain (MW).
