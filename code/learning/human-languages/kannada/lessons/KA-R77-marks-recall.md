## Script: the whole set, sorted by what it asks of you
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[KA-SCRIPT-RECOG-152, KA-SCRIPT-RECOG-153, KA-SCRIPT-RECOG-154] -->

| you write these | you read these |
|---|---|
| . , ? | : ( ) " " — |

**Every one of them came from the Latin alphabet**, so there was never a second
system to learn — which is why this chapter is short and why nothing in it
looked unfamiliar.

The split in that table is the part worth keeping. A reader at this level puts
full stops, commas and question marks on the page themselves, and recognises the
rest when a notice or a form puts them there.
