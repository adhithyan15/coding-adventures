Italian, chapter 452: Giornali e tabacchi.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
la tabaccheria — the tobacconist's.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for to peek, then the Italian for the shop window.

You'll want to know: la tabaccheria.
la tabaccheria — "the tobacconist's".
In tabaccheria compro i biglietti dell'autobus. At the tobacconist's I buy bus tickets.
Marked by a big T sign; it sells tickets, stamps and phone credit too.

The word, taken apart.
tabaccheria is built on tabacco 'tobacco', which was borrowed from Spanish tabaco, a word of Caribbean origin.

What you've built.
One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.

Lesson 3 of 6.
l'edicola — the newsstand, the kiosk.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for the tobacconist's, then the Italian for the tobacconist.

You'll want to know: l'edicola.
l'edicola — "the newsstand, the kiosk".
All'edicola compro il giornale ogni mattina. At the newsstand I buy the paper every morning.
It also means a small shrine with a statue in a wall.

The word, taken apart.
edicola is a learned borrowing from Latin aedicula 'small shrine' (EtymDB), from aedēs 'temple, dwelling': the kiosk looks like a little shrine.
