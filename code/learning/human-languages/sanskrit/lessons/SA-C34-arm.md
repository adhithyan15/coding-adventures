## You'll want to know: बाहुः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C34-LIMB-01]; assesses=[] -->

**बाहुः** (*bāhuḥ*) — "an arm".

You already have **हस्तः**, the hand. **बाहुः** is the arm above it.

Its English cousin is *bough* — the arm of a tree, which is exactly the picture Sanskrit had. Greek *pēkhus*, "forearm", is the same word again, and it named the cubit that measured out temples and ships. Ends in *-uḥ*, like **तरुः** and **सेतुः**.
