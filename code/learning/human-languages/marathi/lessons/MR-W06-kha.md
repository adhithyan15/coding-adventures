# ख — *ka* with the breath let out

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

Say *ka* with your hand in front of your mouth. Now say it again and push the air out after it. That second sound has its own letter.

## Script
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-SCRIPT-KHA-01]; assesses=[MR-SCRIPT-A-INDEPENDENT-01] -->

> ख

**ख** is *kha*: **क** said with a puff of breath released after it. Marathi keeps
the two apart the way English keeps *pin* and *bin* apart — swap them and you
have a different word.

The shape is not क with a mark added; it is its own letter. Draw the left loop,
then the upright with its foot, then the headline across the top. You will need
it for *khāṇe*, "to eat".

## Writing — observe and trace
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MR-SCRIPT-KHA-01] -->
<!-- hl-writing-stage: observe-trace -->

Trace **ख** twice with the model visible. Say *ka*, then *kha*, as your pen
finishes each one, and listen for the puff that only the second has.
