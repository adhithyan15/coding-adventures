## You'll want to know: آنکنا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C543-ATKAL543-04]; assesses=[] -->

**آنکنا** — *āṅknā* — "to put a value on; to appraise".

> اگر سنار نے انگوٹھی آنکی تو قیمت بتائے گا۔
>
> *agar sunār ne angūṭhī āṅkī to qīmat batāe gā.*
>
> If the goldsmith values the ring, he'll tell us the price.

Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے زمین کی قیمت آنکی, *us ne zamīn kī qīmat āṅkī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**آنکنا** (*āṅknā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *ã̄knā*, "to mark, to appraise", under Sanskrit *aṅkayati*, "marks" (CDIAL 104).
