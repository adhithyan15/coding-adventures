## You'll want to know: લોભ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C334-KHAJANO334-03]; assesses=[] -->

**લોભ** — *lobh* — "greed".

> લોભને લીધે ચોર ફસાઈ ગયો.
>
> *lobhne līdhe cor phasāī gayo.*
>
> Greed got the thief into trouble.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**લોભ** (*lobh*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *lobha*, "eager desire, greed" (Monier-Williams): wanting more than one needs, the fault of many story villains.
