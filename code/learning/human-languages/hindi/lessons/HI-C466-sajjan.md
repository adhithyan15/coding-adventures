## You'll want to know: सज्जन
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C466-DAYA466-04]; assesses=[] -->

**सज्जन** — *sajjan* — "a gentleman; a decent person".

> दरवाज़े पर एक सज्जन आपसे मिलना चाहते हैं।
>
> *darvāze par ek sajjan āpse milnā cāhte haiṁ.*
>
> There's a gentleman at the door who would like to see you.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सज्जन** (*sajjan*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *saj-jana*, "a good or virtuous man", built on *sat*, "good", and *jana*, "person" (Monier-Williams). It is the polite word for a man one does not know.
