Lesson 1 of 5.
das Fieber — a fever.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for a toe, then the German for the stomach.

You'll want to know: das Fieber.
das Fieber — "a fever".

The word, taken apart.
Fieber from Old High German fiebar from Latin febris, "fever" (EtymDB 2.1) — borrowed from Latin, as English fever was.

Lesson 2 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for the stomach, then the German for a fever.

You'll want to know: der Arzt.
der Arzt — "a doctor (a man)".

The word, taken apart.
Arzt from Old High German arzat from Late Latin archiāter from Greek arkhiātros, "chief physician" (EtymDB 2.1) — borrowed, the title of a court doctor worn down to the everyday word.

Lesson 4 of 5.
das Krankenhaus — a hospital.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for a doctor, then the German for a doctor.

You'll want to know: das Krankenhaus.
das Krankenhaus — "a hospital".

The word, taken apart.
Krankenhaus is built from the word for "ill" and the word for "house": a house of the sick. The "ill" word from Old High German krank from Proto-Germanic krangaz. (EtymDB 2.1).
