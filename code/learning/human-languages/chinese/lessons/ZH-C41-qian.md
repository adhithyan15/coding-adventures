## You'll want to know: qián
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C41-MONEY41-02]; assesses=[] -->

*qián* — "money".

> *zhège duōshao qián* — How much is this one?

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*qián* is inherited from Middle Chinese *dzjen* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'coin'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for asking politely.
