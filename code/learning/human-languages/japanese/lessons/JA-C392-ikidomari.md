## You'll want to know: いきどまり
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C392-YORIMICHI392-03]; assesses=[] -->

**いきどまり** — *ikidomari* — "a dead end".

> **この みちは いきどまりです。** — *kono michi wa ikidomari desu* — "This road is a dead end."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**いきどまり** (*ikidomari*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, built from *iki*, "going" (from *iku*) and *domari*, "stopping" (from *tomaru*); its kanji were attached later as kun'yomi (JMdict).
