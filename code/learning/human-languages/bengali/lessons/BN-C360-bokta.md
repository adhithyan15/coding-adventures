## You'll want to know: বক্তা
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C360-BOKTRITA360-02]; assesses=[] -->

**বক্তা** — *bôktā* — "a speaker".

> সভার প্রধান বক্তা একজন বিজ্ঞানী।
>
> *sôbhār prôdhān bôktā ekjôn bigyānī.*
>
> The main speaker at the meeting is a scientist.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**বক্তা** (*bôktā*) is Sanskrit *vaktṛ*, a speaker (Monier-Williams), in its nominative form vaktā.
