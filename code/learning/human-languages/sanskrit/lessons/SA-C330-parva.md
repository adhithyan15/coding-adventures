## You'll want to know: पर्व
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C330-PARAMPARA330-04]; assesses=[] -->

**पर्व** — *parva* — "a festival day".

> अद्य पर्व अस्ति, जनाः नूतनानि वस्त्राणि धारयन्ति।
>
> *adya parva asti, janāḥ nūtanāni vastrāṇi dhārayanti.*
>
> Today is a festival day, and people wear new clothes.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पर्व** (*parva*) is the nominative of *parvan*, which MW gives first as a knot or joint, then a break, a section, and also a festival or holiday; MW gives no root, so its origin is left uncertain here (MW).
