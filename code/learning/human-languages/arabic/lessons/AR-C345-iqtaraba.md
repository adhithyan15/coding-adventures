## You'll want to know: اقترب
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C345-MUWAJAHA345-04]; assesses=[] -->

**اقترب** — *iqtaraba* — "to approach, to come near".

> اقترب الكلب من الطفل ببطء.
>
> *iqtaraba al-kalb min aṭ-ṭifl bi-buṭʾ.*
>
> The dog came slowly closer to the child.

Present: يقترب, *yaqtarib*.

It takes من: اقترب من.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**اقترب** (*iqtaraba*) is built on the native Arabic root *q-r-b* (Lane), "to be near", the root of the taught *qarīb*. It is Form VIII, "to draw near".
