# சூழ்நிலை (cūḻnilai) — circumstances; the setting

## Warm-up

## You'll want to know: சூழ்நிலை
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C385-CULNILAI385-01]; assesses=[] -->

**சூழ்நிலை** — *cūḻnilai* — "circumstances; the setting".

> இந்தச் சூழ்நிலையில் நான் என்ன செய்வது?
>
> *intac cūḻnilaiyil nāṉ eṉṉa ceyvatu?*
>
> What am I to do in these circumstances?

எந்தச் சூழ்நிலையிலும், in any circumstances.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**சூழ்நிலை** (*cūḻnilai*) is native: *cūḻ*, "to surround, encompass" (DEDR 2698), and *nilai*, "a standing, a state" (DEDR 3675): the state of what surrounds us.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
