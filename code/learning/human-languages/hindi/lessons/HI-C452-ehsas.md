## You'll want to know: एहसास
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C452-BHAVNA452-02]; assesses=[] -->

**एहसास** — *ehsās* — "an awareness; a sense of something".

> अरुण को अपनी ग़लती का एहसास हुआ।
>
> *Aruṇ ko apnī ġaltī kā ehsās huā.*
>
> Arun realized he had made a mistake.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**एहसास** (*ehsās*) is usually traced to Arabic *iḥsās*, "perceiving, feeling", which reached Hindi through Persian.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
