## You'll want to know: निष्ठा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C361-SHRADDHA361-02]; assesses=[] -->

**निष्ठा** — *niṣṭhā* — "steadfastness; devotion".

> अरुणस्य कार्ये निष्ठा अस्ति।
>
> *aruṇasya kārye niṣṭhā asti.*
>
> Arun is devoted to his work.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**निष्ठा** (*niṣṭhā*) is built on *ni*, "down", and the root √sthā, "to stand"; MW gives *ni-ṣṭhā* "firmness, steadiness, attachment, devotion" (MW).
