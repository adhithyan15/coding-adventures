## You'll want to know: os portes
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C454-CORREIOS-03]; assesses=[] -->

**os portes** — "the postage, the delivery charge".

> A encomenda custa dez euros mais os portes.
>
> *The parcel costs ten euros plus postage.*

*Portes grátis* — free delivery.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*porte* is built on *portar* 'to carry', from Latin *portāre* 'to carry' (Lewis & Short): the price of carrying something.
