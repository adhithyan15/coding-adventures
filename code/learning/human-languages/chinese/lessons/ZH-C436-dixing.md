## You'll want to know: dìxíng
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C436-SOUTH436-01]; assesses=[] -->

*dìxíng* — "terrain; lie of the land".

> *zhège dìqū de dìxíng hěn tèbié* — The land in this region has an unusual shape.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*dìxíng* is built from 地 'the earth' and *xíng* 'a shape', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *dijH* and *heng* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second as the look of a thing.
