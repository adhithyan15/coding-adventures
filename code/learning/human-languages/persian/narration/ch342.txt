Persian, chapter 342: Lights and fireworks.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
تزیین کردن (tazyin kardan) — to decorate.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a balloon, then the Persian for a candle.

You'll want to know: تزیین کردن.
تزیین کردن (tazyin kardan) — "to decorate".
ما خانه را با گل تزیین کردیم. Mâ khâne râ bâ gol tazyin kardim. We decorated the house with flowers.
Also spelled تزئین. Present tazyin mikonad.

The word, taken apart.
tazyin is usually traced to Arabic tazyīn, "adornment"; EtymDB lists no entry. kardan "to do" is inherited Persian.

Lesson 2 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a candle, then the Persian for to decorate.

You'll want to know: چراغانی.
چراغانی (cherâghâni) — "festive lights, illuminations".
خیابان ها برای جشن چراغانی بود. Khiyâbân-hâ barâ-ye jashn cherâghâni bud. The streets were lit up for the celebration.
From cherâgh "lamp": strings of lights for a festival.

The word, taken apart.
cherâghâni is built on cherâgh "lamp", a Persian word, with the suffix -âni; EtymDB lists no entry.

Lesson 3 of 5.
آتش بازی (âtash bâzi) — fireworks.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to decorate, then the Persian for festive lights.

You'll want to know: آتش بازی.
آتش بازی (âtash bâzi) — "fireworks".
نیمه شب آتش بازی شروع شد. Nime shab âtash bâzi shoru' shod. At midnight the fireworks began.
Literally "fire play".

The word, taken apart.
âtash "fire" is inherited from Middle Persian (EtymDB), and bâzi "play, game" is native Persian too: fire play.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: رژه.
رژه (rezhe) — "a parade, a march-past".
روز جشن رژه از جلوی خانه ما رد شد. Ruz-e jashn rezhe az jelo-ye khâne-ye mâ radd shod. On the day of the celebration the parade passed our house.
Written with ژ. Rezhe raftan "to march in a parade".

The word, taken apart.
The origin of rezhe "a parade" is not settled, and EtymDB lists no entry.

Lesson 5 of 5.
شادی (shâdi) — joy, happiness.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for fireworks, then the Persian for a parade.

You'll want to know: شادی.
شادی (shâdi) — "joy, happiness".
مردم از شادی فریاد میزدند. Mardom az shâdi faryâd mizadand. People were shouting for joy.
From shâd "glad". Shâdi kardan is "to rejoice".

The word, taken apart.
shâdi adds the noun suffix -i to shâd "glad", a native Persian word. EtymDB lists it without a parent.
