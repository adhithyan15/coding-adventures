## You'll want to know: lo straccio
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C532-PULIZIE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**lo straccio** — "rag, cloth".

> Pulisco il tavolo della cucina con uno straccio bagnato.
>
> *I wipe the kitchen table with a wet cloth.*

Old, torn clothes are *stracci*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*straccio* is built on *stracciare* "to tear", usually traced to Vulgar Latin *extractiāre*, from *trahere* "to drag" (Lewis & Short).
