## You'll want to know: 上场
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C500-MATCH500-05]; assesses=[] -->

**上场** — *shàngchǎng* — "to take the field; to come on (to play)".

> **这场比赛我没有上场** *zhè chǎng bǐsài wǒ méiyǒu shàngchǎng* — I didn't get on the pitch in this match.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**上场** (*shàngchǎng*) is built from 上 'to go up' and 场 'a ground', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *dzyangX* and *drjang* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to climb' and the second 'a threshing floor'.
