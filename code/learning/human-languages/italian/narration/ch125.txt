Lesson 1 of 5.
rumoroso — noisy.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for closed, then the Italian for quiet.

You'll want to know: rumoroso.
rumoroso — "noisy".

The word, taken apart.
rumoroso — built from rumore, "noise", with -oso, "full of". rumore from Latin rumor, "talk, rumour" — inherited. (EtymDB 2.1).

You'll want to know: interessante.
interessante — "interesting".

The word, taken apart.
interessante — built from the verb interessare, "to concern", with -ante, "-ing". The verb goes back to Latin interesse, "to be between", whose form interest meant "it concerns, it matters" (Lewis & Short). EtymDB 2.1 has no row for its origin.
