Lesson 1 of 5.
le sol — the floor, the ground.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: le sol.
le sol — "the floor, the ground".

The word, taken apart.
sol from Latin solum, "ground, floor" — a learned borrowing. (EtymDB 2.1).

Lesson 2 of 5.
l'escalier — the stairs.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for a bedroom, then the French for the floor.

You'll want to know: l'escalier.
l'escalier — "the stairs".

The word, taken apart.
escalier from Occitan escalier from Latin scalaria, "steps", from scala, "a ladder" — borrowed from the south in the 1500s. (EtymDB 2.1 has the Middle French form.) The word French inherited from scala is the one for "ladder".
