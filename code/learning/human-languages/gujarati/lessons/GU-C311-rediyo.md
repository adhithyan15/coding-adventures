## You'll want to know: રેડિયો
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C311-SAMACHAR311-04]; assesses=[] -->

**રેડિયો** — *reḍiyo* — "a radio".

> રેડિયો પર સમાચાર આવતા હતા.
>
> *reḍiyo par samācār āvtā hatā.*
>
> The news was on the radio.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**રેડિયો** (*reḍiyo*) is borrowed from English *radio*, and it is masculine: **રેડિયો પર**, on the radio.
