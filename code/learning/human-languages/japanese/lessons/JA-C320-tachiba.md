## You'll want to know: たちば
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C320-TACHIBA320-01]; assesses=[] -->

**たちば** — *tachiba* — "a position; a point of view".

> **せんせいの たちばも かんがえた ほうが いいです。** — *sensei no tachiba mo kangaeta hō ga ii desu* — "You should think about the teacher's position too."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**たちば** (*tachiba*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese: it is built from *tachi*, "standing (tatsu)" and *ba*, "place", and its kanji were attached later as kun'yomi (JMdict).
