## You'll want to know: ちゃわん
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C175-COOK175-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ちゃわん** — *chawan* — "rice bowl; teacup".

> **ちゃわんに ごはんを もります。** — *chawan ni gohan o morimasu* — "I dish up rice into a bowl."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ちゃわん** (*chawan*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *cha*, "tea" (Middle Chinese *drae*) and *wan*, "bowl" (*'wanX*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
