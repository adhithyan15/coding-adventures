## You'll want to know: अंत
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C548-AVADHI548-03]; assesses=[] -->

**अंत** — *ant* — "the end; an ending".

> कहानी का अंत बहुत अच्छा था।
>
> *Kahānī kā ant bahut acchā thā.*
>
> The story had a very good ending.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अंत** (*ant*) is a tatsama, taken whole from Sanskrit *anta*, "end, limit" (Monier-Williams).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
