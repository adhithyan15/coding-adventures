## Script — the ear piece, then 又
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-SCRIPT-QU-TAKE-01]; assesses=[] -->

> 取

**Two pieces, left before right.**

**The left piece, the ear piece, six strokes.** A bar across the top. A short vertical down on the left. A long vertical down on the right, past the foot. Two short bars between them. Then a stroke rising to the right at the foot.

**The right piece, 又, two strokes,** as at the foot of 变: a bar that turns and falls to the lower left, then a long stroke falling to the right across it.

6 + 2 = eight.
