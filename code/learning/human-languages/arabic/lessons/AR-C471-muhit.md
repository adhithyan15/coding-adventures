## You'll want to know: محيط
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C471-KHALIJ471-02]; assesses=[] -->

**محيط** — *muḥīṭ* — "an ocean".

> عبرت السفينة المحيط في شهر.
>
> *ʿabarat as-safīna al-muḥīṭ fī shahr.*
>
> The ship crossed the ocean in a month.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**محيط** (*muḥīṭ*) is built on the native Arabic root *ḥ-w-ṭ*, "to surround" (Lane). It is the Form IV participle: the sea that surrounds the land.
