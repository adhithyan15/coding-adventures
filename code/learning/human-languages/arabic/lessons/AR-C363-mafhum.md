## You'll want to know: مفهوم
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C363-NAZARIYYA363-03]; assesses=[] -->

**مفهوم** — *mafhūm* — "a concept".

> مفهوم الصداقة مختلف عند كل شخص.
>
> *mafhūm aṣ-ṣadāqa mukhtalif ʿinda kulli shakhṣ.*
>
> The concept of friendship differs from person to person.

Also an adjective, understood, clear: هذا مفهوم.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**مفهوم** (*mafhūm*) is built on the native Arabic root *f-h-m*, "to understand" (Lane). *Mafhūm* is the passive participle of *fahima*, "to understand": what is understood, a concept.
