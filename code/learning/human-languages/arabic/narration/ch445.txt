Arabic, chapter 445: Trams and lorries.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ترام (trām) — a tram.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for an adapter, then the Arabic for to light up.

You'll want to know: ترام.
ترام — trām — "a tram".
ركبنا الترام إلى وسط المدينة. rakibnā at-trām ilā wasaṭ al-madīna. We took the tram to the city centre.

The word, taken apart.
ترام (trām) is usually traced to English tram.

Lesson 2 of 5.
شاحنة (shāḥina) — a lorry; a truck.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to light up, then the Arabic for a tram.

You'll want to know: شاحنة.
شاحنة — shāḥina — "a lorry; a truck".
وقفت شاحنة كبيرة في وسط الطريق. waqafat shāḥina kabīra fī wasaṭ aṭ-ṭarīq. A big lorry stopped in the middle of the road.

The word, taken apart.
شاحنة (shāḥina) is built on the native Arabic root sh-ḥ-n, "to load (a ship)" (Lane). It is an active participle: the vehicle that carries loads; shāḥin, "a charger", shares the root.

Lesson 3 of 5.
مقود (miqwad) — a steering wheel.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a tram, then the Arabic for a lorry.

You'll want to know: مقود.
مقود — miqwad — "a steering wheel".
أمسك أبي المقود بقوة. amsaka abī al-miqwad bi-quwwa. My father held the steering wheel tightly.

The word, taken apart.
مقود (miqwad) is built on the native Arabic root q-w-d, "to lead" (Lane). The tool pattern mifʿal makes it the thing one steers with.

Lesson 4 of 5.
تمهل (tamahhala) — to slow down; to take one's time.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a lorry, then the Arabic for a steering wheel.

You'll want to know: تمهل.
تمهل — tamahhala — "to slow down; to take one's time".
تمهل أبي عند المنعطف. tamahhala abī ʿinda al-munʿaṭaf. My father slowed down at the bend.
Present: يتمهل, yatamahhal.

The word, taken apart.
تمهل (tamahhala) is built on the native Arabic root m-h-l, "to act gently, without haste" (Lane). It is Form V.
