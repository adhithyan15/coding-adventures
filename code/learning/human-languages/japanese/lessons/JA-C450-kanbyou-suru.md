## You'll want to know: かんびょうする
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C450-CLINIC450-05]; assesses=[] -->

**かんびょうする** — *kanbyō suru* — "to nurse the sick".

> **ははが ちちを かんびょうしています。** — *haha ga chichi o kanbyō shite imasu* — "My mother is nursing my father."

Polite **かんびょうします**, *kanbyōshimasu*; a する verb. Past (polite): **むすめが そぼを かんびょうしました。**, *musume ga sobo o kanbyō shimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**かんびょうする** (*kanbyō suru*) is Sino-Japanese (kango) *kanbyō* with the native verb *suru*, "to do": two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *kan*, "watch over" (Middle Chinese *khan*) and *byō*, "sick" (*bjaengH*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
