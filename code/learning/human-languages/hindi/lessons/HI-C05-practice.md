# Chapter 5 — The first verbs

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[HI-CONCEPT-C05-BOLNA-01, HI-CONCEPT-C05-MAINHINDIBOLTAHUN-01, HI-CONCEPT-C05-RAHNA-01, HI-CONCEPT-C05-KARNA-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] No new words. Three verbs, one engine — build real sentences about
yourself.

## How to answer — build the sentences
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-CONCEPT-C05-PRACTICE-01]; assesses=[HI-CONCEPT-C05-BOLNA-01, HI-CONCEPT-C05-MAINHINDIBOLTAHUN-01, HI-CONCEPT-C05-RAHNA-01, HI-CONCEPT-C05-KARNA-01] -->

[PAUSE 1s each]
- [YOU SAY: "I speak Hindi" (m./f.) — *maiṁ hindī boltā/boltī hūṁ*]
- [YOU SAY: "I live in Delhi" — *maiṁ Dillī meṁ rahtā/rahtī hūṁ*]
- [YOU SAY: "I work" — *maiṁ kām kartā/kartī hūṁ*]
- [YOU SAY: "you (resp.) speak Hindi" — *āp hindī bolte haiṁ*]

## Grammar lens — the engine
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[HI-CONCEPT-C05-BOLNA-01, HI-CONCEPT-C05-KARNA-01, HI-CONCEPT-C05-MAINHINDIBOLTAHUN-01, HI-CONCEPT-C05-PRACTICE-01, HI-CONCEPT-C05-RAHNA-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s]
- [YOU SAY: the ending every infinitive carries (*-nā*)]
- [YOU SAY: the two agreements in a present sentence (*-tā/-tī/-te* for gender/number; *hūṁ/hai/haiṁ* for person)]
- [YOU SAY: where the verb sits, and where "in" sits (verb last; *meṁ* after the noun)]

## What you've built — roots you now carry
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[HI-CONCEPT-C05-BOLNA-01, HI-CONCEPT-C05-KARNA-01, HI-CONCEPT-C05-MAINHINDIBOLTAHUN-01, HI-CONCEPT-C05-PRACTICE-01, HI-CONCEPT-C05-RAHNA-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s]
- *bolnā* "to speak" (Prakrit *bolla-*)
- *rahnā* "to live" ← *rah-* "to remain"
- *karnā* "to do" ← *√kṛ* — the root of *karma*, *namaskār*, *Sanskrit*
- *hindī* ← *sindhu*, the river Indus

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[HI-CONCEPT-C05-BOLNA-01, HI-CONCEPT-C05-KARNA-01, HI-CONCEPT-C05-MAINHINDIBOLTAHUN-01, HI-CONCEPT-C05-PRACTICE-01, HI-CONCEPT-C05-RAHNA-01] -->

[PAUSE 3s] In one breath, tell me your language, your city, and your work in Hindi
(as a woman). (*Maiṁ hindī boltī hūṁ. Maiṁ … meṁ rahtī hūṁ. Maiṁ kām kartī hūṁ.*)
