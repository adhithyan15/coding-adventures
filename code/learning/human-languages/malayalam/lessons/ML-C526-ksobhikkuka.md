# ക്ഷോഭിക്കുക (kṣōbhikkuka) — to get agitated; to flare up

## Warm-up

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Malayalam for confusion, then the Malayalam for to be stumped.

## You'll want to know: ക്ഷോഭിക്കുക
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C526-RASAM526-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ക്ഷോഭിക്കുക** — *kṣōbhikkuka* — "to get agitated; to flare up".

> ശമ്പളം കിട്ടാതെ വന്നപ്പോൾ തൊഴിലാളികൾ ക്ഷോഭിച്ചു.
>
> *śampaḷaṁ kiṭṭāte vannappōḷ toḻilāḷikaḷ kṣōbhiccu.*
>
> When their wages did not come, the workers grew agitated.

Past: ക്ഷോഭിച്ചു, *kṣōbhiccu*.

Formal; the noun is ക്ഷോഭം, agitation, also of the sea.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ക്ഷോഭിക്കുക** (*kṣōbhikkuka*) is built from Sanskrit *kṣobha*, "shaking, agitation, emotion" (Monier-Williams), with the Malayalam verb ending -ിക്കുക.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
