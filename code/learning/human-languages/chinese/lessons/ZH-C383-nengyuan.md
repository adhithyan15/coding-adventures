## You'll want to know: néngyuán
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C383-ENERGY383-03]; assesses=[] -->

*néngyuán* — "energy; sources of energy".

> *yīnwèi néngyuán hěn guì suǒyǐ diànfèi yě hěn guì* — Energy is expensive, so the electricity bill is high too.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*néngyuán* is built from 能 'able' and *yuán* 'a source', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *nong* and *ngjwon* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'skilled' and the second 'where water rises'. Energy as power for machines is a modern sense.
