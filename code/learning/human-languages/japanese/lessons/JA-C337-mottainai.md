## You'll want to know: もったいない
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C337-SETSUYAKU337-05]; assesses=[] -->

**もったいない** — *mottainai* — "wasteful; too good to waste".

> **もったいないから、すてないで ください。** — *mottainai kara, sutenaide kudasai* — "It's a waste, so please do not throw it away."

An i-adjective.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**もったいない** (*mottainai*) is built from the Sino-Japanese *mottai*, two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese, *mochi*, "not", shortened to *mot-*, and *tai*, "body" (Middle Chinese *thejX*), and the native *nai*, "not". JMdict notes that it is usually written in kana (KANJIDIC2; JMdict; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
