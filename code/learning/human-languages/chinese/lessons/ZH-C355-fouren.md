## You'll want to know: fǒurèn
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C355-REFUSE355-04]; assesses=[] -->

*fǒurèn* — "to deny".

> *wǒ juéde tā bù yīnggāi fǒurèn* — I don't think he should deny it.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*fǒurèn* is built from *fǒu* and *rèn*, both inherited from Middle Chinese, *pjuwX* and *nyinH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first, quoting an older dictionary, 'not', and the second 'to recognize'.
