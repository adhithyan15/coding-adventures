## You'll want to know: أوجب
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C410-IBAHA410-02]; assesses=[] -->

**أوجب** — *awjaba* — "to make obligatory".

> أوجب القانون التعليم للأطفال.
>
> *awjaba al-qānūn at-taʿlīm li-l-aṭfāl.*
>
> The law made schooling obligatory for children.

Present: يوجب, *yūjib*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**أوجب** (*awjaba*) is built on the native Arabic root *w-j-b*, "to be due, binding" (Lane). It is Form IV, to make binding: to make obligatory. واجب, a duty, is from the same root.
