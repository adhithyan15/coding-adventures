# উঁচু (ũchu) — high; raised

## You'll want to know: উঁচু
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C289-JOLOCHCHHAS289-03]; assesses=[] -->

**উঁচু** — *ũchu* — "high; raised".

> সবাই উঁচু জায়গায় আশ্রয় নিল।
>
> *sôbāi ũchu jāygāy āshrôy nilô.*
>
> Everyone took shelter on higher ground.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**উঁচু** (*ũchu*) comes down from Sanskrit *ucca*, 'tall, lofty' (CDIAL 1634, which lists Bengali *uc* and *ũc*); its opposite is **নিচু**, low.
