## You'll want to know: dòngjiāng
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C514-WEATHER514-04]; assesses=[] -->

*dòngjiāng* — "to be frozen stiff".

> *wǒ děng le yí ge xiǎoshí shǒu dōu dòngjiāng le* — I waited an hour, and my hands were frozen stiff.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*dòngjiāng* is built from *dòng* 'to freeze' and *jiāng* 'stiff', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *tuwngH* and *kjang* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'ice, freezing' and the second as falling over flat.
