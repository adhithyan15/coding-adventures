## You'll want to know: अनामत
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C417-ANAMAT417-01]; assesses=[] -->

**अनामत** — *anāmat* — "a deposit; money held in trust".

> खोली सोडताना अनामत परत मिळते.
>
> *kholī soḍtānā anāmat parat miḷte.*
>
> You get the deposit back when you leave the room.

Feminine.

It comes back when you leave, if nothing is broken.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अनामत** (*anāmat*) is a loan through Persian, usually traced to Arabic *amānat*, "something held in trust, a deposit".
