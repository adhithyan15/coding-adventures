# അനുകമ്പ (anukampa) — compassion

## You'll want to know: അനുകമ്പ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C520-ANUKAMPA520-01]; assesses=[] -->

**അനുകമ്പ** — *anukampa* — "compassion".

> രോഗികളോട് ആ നഴ്സിന് വലിയ അനുകമ്പ ഉണ്ടായിരുന്നു.
>
> *rōgikaḷōṭŭ ā naḻsinŭ valiya anukampa uṇṭāyirunnu.*
>
> That nurse had great compassion for the patients.

A word of writing and speeches; the one pitied takes -ഓട്.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**അനുകമ്പ** (*anukampa*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *anukampā*, "sympathy, compassion" (Monier-Williams), from *anu-kamp*, "to tremble along with someone".

## What you've built

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
