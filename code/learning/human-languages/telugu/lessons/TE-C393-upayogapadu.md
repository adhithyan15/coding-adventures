## You'll want to know: ఉపయోగపడు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C393-PRAYOJANAM393-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ఉపయోగపడు** — *upayōgapaḍu* — "to be useful; to come in handy".

> మీ సలహా నాకు చాలా ఉపయోగపడింది.
>
> *mī salahā nāku cālā upayōgapaḍindi.*
>
> Your advice was very useful to me.

Past (it …): ఉపయోగపడింది, *upayōgapaḍindi*.

The person helped takes -కి, and the thing that helps is the subject: its past is shown as ఉపయోగపడింది, it came in handy.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ఉపయోగపడు** (*upayōgapaḍu*) is built from Sanskrit *upayoga*, "use, application" (Monier-Williams), the word in ఉపయోగించు, and the native verb *paḍu* (DEDR 3853).
