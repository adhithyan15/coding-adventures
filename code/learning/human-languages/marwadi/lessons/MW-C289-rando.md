## You'll want to know: रंदो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C289-MASIN289-04]; assesses=[] -->

**रंदो** — *rando* — "a carpenter's plane".

> रंदो भारी है।
>
> *rando bhārī hai.*
>
> The plane is heavy.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**रंदो** (*rando*) is usually traced to Persian *randa*, "a carpenter's plane".
