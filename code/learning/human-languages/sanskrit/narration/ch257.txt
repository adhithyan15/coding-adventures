Holy basil? (तुलसी, tulasī.) An insect? (कीटः, kīṭaḥ.) A spider? (लूता, lūtā.) A scorpion? (वृश्चिकः, vṛścikaḥ.) A lizard? (गोधा, godhā.) A crocodile? (नक्रः, nakraḥ.)
