Malayalam, chapter 122: Quick Replies.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ശരിക്കും (śarikkuṁ) — really.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for a station, then the Malayalam for a map.

You'll want to know: ശരിക്കും.
ശരിക്കും (śarikkuṁ) — "really, truly", built on ശരി (śari), "right, okay". ശരിക്കും? — "really?".

What you've built.
Okay, turned into really.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: śarikkuṁ]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: śarikkuṁ, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say śarikkuṁ?]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Malayalam for a station, then the Malayalam for a map, then say śarikkuṁ again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does ശരിക്കും (śarikkuṁ) mean? ("Really".) Say it once more.
