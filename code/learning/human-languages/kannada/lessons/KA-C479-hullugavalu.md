# ಹುಲ್ಲುಗಾವಲು (hullugāvalu) — a grassland; a pasture

## You'll want to know: ಹುಲ್ಲುಗಾವಲು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C479-SASYA479-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ಹುಲ್ಲುಗಾವಲು** — *hullugāvalu* — "a grassland; a pasture".

> ಹುಲ್ಲುಗಾವಲಿನಲ್ಲಿ ಜಿಂಕೆಗಳು ಮೇಯುತ್ತಿದ್ದವು.
>
> *hullugāvalinalli jiṅkegaḷu mēyuttiddavu.*
>
> Deer were grazing on the grassland.

ಹುಲ್ಲು, grass, plus ಕಾವಲು, guarded land.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಹುಲ್ಲುಗಾವಲು** (*hullugāvalu*) is a native Kannada compound of ಹುಲ್ಲು, "grass" (DEDR 4300), and *kāvalu*, "guarded land, a reserve", built on *kā*, "to guard" (DEDR 1416).
