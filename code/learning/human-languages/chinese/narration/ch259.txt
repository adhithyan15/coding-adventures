Mandarin Chinese, chapter 259: What Do You Do?.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
zhíyè — occupation; profession.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for witty, then the Mandarin for personality.

You'll want to know: zhíyè.
zhíyè — "occupation; profession".
nǐ de zhíyè shì shénme — What do you do for a living?
Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
zhíyè is built from zhí 'a duty' and yè 'a task', both inherited from Middle Chinese, tsyik and ngjaep (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which quotes two older dictionaries for the first: 'to be in charge' and 'work'.

What you've built.
One more word for saying what I do.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: zhíyè]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: zhíyè, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: nǐ de zhíyè shì shénme — What do you do for a living?]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Mandarin for witty, then the Mandarin for personality, then say zhíyè again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does zhíyè mean? ("Occupation; profession".) Say it once more.


Lesson 2 of 6.
作家 (zuòjiā) — writer; author.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for personality, then the Mandarin for an occupation.

You'll want to know: 作家.
作家 — zuòjiā — "writer; author".
他是作家写了十本书 tā shì zuòjiā xiěle shí běn shū — He's an author and has written ten books.

The word, taken apart.
作家 (zuòjiā) is built from 作 'to make' and 家, both inherited from Middle Chinese, tsak and kae (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'a dwelling'. 家 also names a master of a craft, a sense the dictionary does not give, so how it came is not settled.

What you've built.
One more word for saying what I do.
