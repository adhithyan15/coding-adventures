(かね, kane.) A hakama? (はかま, hakama.) A cotton kimono? (ゆかた, yukata.) A flame? (ほのお, honoo.) A lamp? (あかり, akari.) Light? (ひかり, hikari.) A gate? (もん, mon.) A wood? (はやし, hayashi.) A bud? (つぼみ, tsubomi.) A rock? (いわ, iwa.) A pebble? (こいし, koishi.)
