Gujarati, chapter 516: Lime and Paint.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
ચૂનો (cūno) — lime wash.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a pipe, then the Gujarati for a plumber.

You'll want to know: ચૂનો.
ચૂનો — cūno — "lime wash".
દિવાળી પહેલાં અમે દીવાલો પર ચૂનો લગાડીએ છીએ. divāḷī pahelā̃ ame dīvālo par cūno lagāḍīe chīe. Before Diwali we put lime wash on the walls.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ચૂનો (cūno) is a tadbhava, inherited from Sanskrit cūrṇa, "powder", and CDIAL lists the Gujarati word as "lime" (CDIAL 4889).

Lesson 3 of 6.
રંગ (rang) — a colour.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for lime wash, then the Gujarati for to whitewash.

You'll want to know: રંગ.
રંગ — rang — "a colour".
તમને કયો રંગ ગમે છે? tamne kayo rang game che? Which colour do you like?
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
રંગ (rang) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit raṅga, "colour, paint, dye" (Monier-Williams). It also means paint: લીલો રંગ, green paint.

The word, taken apart.
રંગવું (rangvũ) is built on રંગ (rang), "colour", from Sanskrit raṅga, "colour, dye" (Monier-Williams); it is also used for dyeing cloth.
