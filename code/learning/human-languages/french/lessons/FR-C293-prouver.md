## You'll want to know: prouver
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C293-MYSTERE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**prouver** — "to prove".

> Il ne peut pas prouver qu'il était chez lui.
>
> *He cannot prove that he was at home.*

*Prouver que* + indicative.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*prouver* is inherited from Latin *probāre* "to test, to prove, to approve" (EtymDB).
