## You'll want to know: le buraliste
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C449-BURALISTE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**le buraliste** — "the tobacconist".

> Le buraliste vend aussi des timbres et des journaux.
>
> *The tobacconist also sells stamps and newspapers.*

He runs *le bureau de tabac*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*buraliste* is built on *bureau* 'office, desk' with *-iste* (EtymDB). *Bureau* first meant a coarse woollen cloth that covered a desk.
