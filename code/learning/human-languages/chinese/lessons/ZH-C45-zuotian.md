## You'll want to know: zuótiān
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C45-TODAY45-03]; assesses=[] -->

*zuótiān* — "yesterday".

> *zuótiān wǒ hěn lèi* — I was really tired yesterday.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*zuótiān* is built from zuó 'the day before' and 天 'day', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *dzak* and *then* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription); the dictionary glosses *dzak* as the day one night back.

## What you've built

One more word for times and partings.
