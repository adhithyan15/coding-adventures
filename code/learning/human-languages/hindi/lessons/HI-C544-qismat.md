## You'll want to know: क़िस्मत
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C544-KISMAT544-01]; assesses=[] -->

**क़िस्मत** — *qismat* — "luck; fortune".

> उसकी क़िस्मत अच्छी है, उसे हमेशा सीट मिल जाती है।
>
> *Uskī qismat acchī hai, use hameśā sīṭ mil jātī hai.*
>
> He is lucky: he always gets a seat.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**क़िस्मत** (*qismat*) is usually traced to Arabic *qisma*, "a share, a portion", through Persian. One's share, handed out in advance, is one's luck.
