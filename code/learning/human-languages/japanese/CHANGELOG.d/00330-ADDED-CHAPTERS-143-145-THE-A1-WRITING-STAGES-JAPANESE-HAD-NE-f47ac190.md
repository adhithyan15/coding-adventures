## Added — chapters 143–145, the A1 writing stages Japanese had never proven

Three writing lessons, no new vocabulary, kana or atoms in any of them. They
sit on `SPINE-SAY-WHAT-I-WANT`, the A1 node where the track's last kana
writing chapters (131–137) already live, and they close the two cumulative
writing stages the level gate named at A1 (`controlled-composition`,
`timed-assessment-production`) plus `connected-composition`, so Japanese
matches the French and Arabic ladders.

- **143 — my own line.** Controlled composition: one line of the learner's
  own day from a closed supply of taught words. The Japanese-specific check
  arrives with it: **a small sign is a different sign, not a smaller copy.**
  A full-size つ in きっぷ, or ゆ in しゅみ, writes a different word or none.
  Every sign gets the same square, and stroke order is kept because it is what
  holds the shape when the clock arrives.
- **144 — with a clock.** Timed production, and stated plainly: **the JLPT has
  no writing paper at any level**; it tests vocabulary, grammar, reading and
  listening only. So there is no official A1 writing clock, and the lesson
  says its clock is the book's own. The task follows the JF Standard A1
  Can-dos the spec names (CEFR-aligned), a small form and a short personal
  message, with the message at the 30–40 sign length set in
  `assessment-spec.md#a1`. Length is counted in signs, small ones included,
  because Japanese has no spaces to count words by. Only the five-field form
  is clocked: a three-minute timer, then at most one minute checking, so the
  directed time (240 s) stays inside the five-minute lesson cap; the message
  is described, not run. A field the book has given no word for takes a
  number or the nearest owned word, never a looked-up one. The lesson is typed
  `practice` rather than `writing`: a timed stage is never a zero-new-atom
  writing contract, so as `writing` it would have been measurement-blind,
  and as an explicit retrieval step (introduces nothing, practises its
  atoms) the gentle ramp can measure it. Each of the three lessons carries
  one checked activity (きっぷ, しゅみ, ねます), as every Japanese lesson does. It ends on JLPT's real N5
  rule: 80/180 overall plus sectional minimums of 38/120 and 19/60, none of
  which measures writing, which is why this contract scores writing as its own
  60/100 paper.
- **145 — four lines, and the joint you do not write.** Connected composition
  without a conjunction, because the track has not taught one and the lesson
  does not smuggle one in. Four lines to a named friend hold together by a
  time word at the front of each line and by the subject Japanese leaves out
  once it is clear (taught in chapter 138). The check is falsifiable: count
  the lines that name who does it; if every line does, it is a list.

No mock, readiness, calibration or attainment claim is made beyond the
machine-measured writing stages.
