Hindi, chapter 109: Laughter and Tears.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
हँसी (hãsī) — laughter.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for troubled, then the Hindi for worry.

You'll want to know: हँसी.
हँसी (hãsī) — "laughter". It is feminine.
मुझे हँसी (hãsī) आ गई — "I burst out laughing", literally "laughter came to me", like anger.

What you've built.
Laughter, which comes to you like anger does.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: hãsī]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: hãsī, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say mujhe hãsī ā gaī]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Hindi for troubled, then the Hindi for worry, then say hãsī again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does हँसी (hãsī) mean? ("Laughter".) Say it once more.


Lesson 2 of 5.
आँसू (ā̃sū) — tears.

[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for worry, then the Hindi for laughter.

You'll want to know: आँसू.
आँसू (ā̃sū) — "tears", a teardrop.
आँख is the eye you know; आँसू (ā̃sū) is what it sheds. Both open on the same nasal आँ.

What you've built.
Tears, beside the eye.

Lesson 3 of 5.

You'll want to know: दुख.
दुख (dukh) — "sorrow, grief".
मुझे दुख (dukh) है — "I am sorry", said in sympathy, when someone tells you bad news. It is not the माफ़ कीजिए of an apology.

What you've built.
The sympathy sorry, apart from the apology sorry.

Lesson 4 of 5.
अकेला (akelā) — alone.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for tears, then the Hindi for sorrow.

You'll want to know: अकेला.
अकेला (akelā) — "alone". A woman says अकेली.
मैं अकेला (akelā) हूँ — "I am alone". It opens on एक, "one", worn down.

What you've built.
Alone, from the word for one.
