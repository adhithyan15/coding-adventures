## You'll want to know: ruīna, ruīnae
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C313-RUINA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ruīna, ruīnae** — "a collapse, a fall; ruins".

> Post ruīnam aedificiī nihil nisi saxa manēbat.
>
> *After the collapse of the building nothing remained but stones.*

Feminine, from *ruō*. The plural *ruīnae*: ruins. English *ruin*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ruīna** is built on *ruō*, "to rush down, to collapse" (EtymDB): a falling down. English *ruin* comes from it through French.
