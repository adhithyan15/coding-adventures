## Script — the top of yì, then four strokes
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-SCRIPT-QIN-KIN-01]; assesses=[] -->

> 亲

**Two pieces, top before bottom.**

**The top, five strokes**, as on top of 意: a dot, a bar, a dot on the left and a short stroke falling to the left on the right, then a long bar.

**The bottom, four strokes**: a bar, a vertical down the middle that hooks to the left at the foot, a short stroke falling to the left, and a dot falling to the right.

5 + 4 = nine.
