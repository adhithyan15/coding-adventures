## You'll want to know: ಇನ್ನೊಂದು ಸಲ ಹೇಳಿ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C71-MORE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ಇನ್ನೊಂದು ಸಲ ಹೇಳಿ** (*innondu sala hēḷi*) — "please say it once more."

ಸಲ (*sala*) is a time in the counting sense — one time, two times — so ಇನ್ನೊಂದು
ಸಲ is "one more time". The verb is the polite ಹೇಳಿ you met with the sentence
about the way.

This is the single most useful sentence a beginner owns, and until now this book
had no way to build it. Everything else you can survive without; you cannot
survive without asking somebody to repeat themselves.

Put ದಯವಿಟ್ಟು in front and it is as polite as it gets: **ದಯವಿಟ್ಟು ಇನ್ನೊಂದು ಸಲ
ಹೇಳಿ**. Said with a slight rise it is warm rather than demanding, and nobody in
Karnataka will mind hearing it — a stranger trying the language is worth the
second go.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

The repair sentence. With it, every other sentence in this book gets a
second chance.
