## You'll want to know: выпускной
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C305-VOSPITYVAT-04]; assesses=[] -->

**выпускной** (*vypusknóy*) — "a graduation party, a prom".

> На выпускном все танцевали до утра.
>
> *Na vypusknóm vse tantseváli do utrá.*
>
> At the graduation party everyone danced until morning.

Short for *выпускной вечер*; an adjective that serves as a noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*выпускной* is built on *выпуск* "a graduating class", from *выпускать* "to let out", whose root is Proto-Slavic *pustiti* "to let go" (EtymDB).
