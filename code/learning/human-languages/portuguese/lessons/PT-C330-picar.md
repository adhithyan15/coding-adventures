## You'll want to know: picar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C330-DESCASCAR-02]; assesses=[] -->

**picar** — "to chop finely, to mince; to sting".

> O cozinheiro picou a cebola e o alho.
>
> *The cook chopped the onion and garlic finely.*

Also of insects: *a abelha picou-me*. *Carne picada* — mince.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*picar* is inherited from Vulgar Latin *piccāre* "to prick, to puncture" (EtymDB).
