## You'll want to know: നേടുക
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C447-NETTAM447-01]; assesses=[] -->

**നേടുക** — *nēṭuka* — "to achieve; to gain".

> അവൾ പരീക്ഷയിൽ ഒന്നാം സ്ഥാനം നേടി.
>
> *avaḷ parīkṣayil onnāṁ sthānaṁ nēṭi.*
>
> She achieved first place in the exam.

Past: നേടി, *nēṭi*.

Its past is നേടി: ഒന്നാം സ്ഥാനം നേടി, won first place.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**നേടുക** (*nēṭuka*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 3766 lists Malayalam *nēṭuka*, "to obtain, to get", with Tamil *nēṭu*, "to seek, to earn".
