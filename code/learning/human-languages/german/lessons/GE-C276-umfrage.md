## You'll want to know: die Umfrage
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C276-UMFRAGE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**die Umfrage** — "the survey, the poll".

> Bei einer Umfrage sagten viele Leute Nein.
>
> *In a survey many people said no.*

Plural: *die Umfragen*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Umfrage* joins *um* "around" and *Frage* "question", both inherited Germanic words: a question asked all around. EtymDB lists no parent for the compound.
