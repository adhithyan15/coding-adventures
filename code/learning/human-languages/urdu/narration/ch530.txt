Urdu, chapter 530: Yes or No.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
مرضی (marzī) — one's own choice; consent.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for complicated, then the Urdu for to tangle.

You'll want to know: مرضی.
مرضی — marzī — "one's own choice; consent".
اگر آپ کی مرضی ہے تو ہم چلیں گے۔ agar āp kī marzī hai to ham chaleṅ ge. If you wish, we will go.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
مرضی (marzī) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic marḍī, "pleasing, approved".

What you've built.

Lesson 5 of 5.
پلٹنا (palaṭnā) — to turn back; to go back (on one's word).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a disagreement, then the Urdu for turned down.

You'll want to know: پلٹنا.
پلٹنا — palaṭnā — "to turn back; to go back (on one's word)".
اگر وہ اپنی بات سے پلٹا تو کیا ہو گا؟ agar voh apnī bāt se palṭā to kyā ho gā? If he goes back on his word, what will happen?
Past (he …): وہ اپنی بات سے پلٹ گیا, voh apnī bāt se palaṭ gayā.

The word, taken apart.
پلٹنا (palaṭnā) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin palaṭnā, "to overturn", under pallaṭṭ, "turn", a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 7968).
