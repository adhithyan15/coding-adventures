Bengali, chapter 530: Put Off or Settled.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
মুলতুবি (multubi) — adjourned; put off.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for to lean forward, then the Bengali for a whim.

You'll want to know: মুলতুবি.
মুলতুবি — multubi — "adjourned; put off".
ঝড় হলে মিটিং মুলতুবি হবে। Jhôṛ hôle miṭing multubi hôbe. If there is a storm, the meeting will be put off.

The word, taken apart.
মুলতুবি (multubi) is usually traced to Arabic multawī, "deferred", through Persian.

Lesson 3 of 5.
আগাম (āgām) — in advance; an advance.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for adjourned, then the Bengali for fixed in advance.

You'll want to know: আগাম.
আগাম — āgām — "in advance; an advance".
আগাম (āgām) টাকা দিলে হোটেল আমাদের জন্য ঘর রাখবে। Āgām ṭākā dile hoṭel āmāder jônyô ghôr rākhbe. If we pay in advance, the hotel will keep a room for us.

The word, taken apart.
আগাম (āgām) is built on আগ, "the front", from Sanskrit agra (CDIAL 68), the root of আগে, "before"; how the ending arose is not settled.

Lesson 4 of 5.
টালবাহানা করা (ṭālbāhānā kôrā) — to stall; to keep making excuses.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for fixed in advance, then the Bengali for in advance.

You'll want to know: টালবাহানা করা.
টালবাহানা করা — ṭālbāhānā kôrā — "to stall; to keep making excuses".
দোকানদার টাকা ফিরিয়ে দিতে টালবাহানা করল। Dokāndār ṭākā phiriye dite ṭālbāhānā kôrlô. The shopkeeper kept stalling about giving the money back.
Past (he/she …): তিনি সই করতে টালবাহানা করলেন, tini sôi kôrte ṭālbāhānā kôrlen.

The word, taken apart.
টালবাহানা করা (ṭālbāhānā kôrā) joins টাল, "evasion", from the family of ṭalati, "moves aside" (CDIAL 5450, which lists Bengali ṭālā, "to evade"), and বাহানা, "a pretext", usually traced to Persian bahāna, to করা, "to do".

Lesson 5 of 5.
সাব্যস্ত করা (sābyôstô kôrā) — to settle on; to decide finally.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for in advance, then the Bengali for to stall.

You'll want to know: সাব্যস্ত করা.
সাব্যস্ত করা — sābyôstô kôrā — "to settle on; to decide finally".
অনেক ভেবে আমরা শুক্রবার যাওয়া সাব্যস্ত করলাম। Ônek bhebe āmrā shukrôbār jāoyā sābyôstô kôrlām. After much thought we settled on going on Friday.
Past (he/she …): তিনি তারিখটা সাব্যস্ত করলেন, tini tārikhṭā sābyôstô kôrlen.

The word, taken apart.
সাব্যস্ত করা (sābyôstô kôrā) joins সাব্যস্ত, "established, settled", to করা, "to do". সাব্যস্ত is usually traced to Arabic thābit, "firm", through Persian sābit; how its ending arose is not settled.
