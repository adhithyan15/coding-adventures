## You'll want to know: ignāvia, ignāviae
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C508-IGNA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ignāvia, ignāviae** — "cowardice; laziness".

> Ignāvia mīlitum ducem īrātum facit.
>
> *The soldiers' cowardice makes the general angry.*

Feminine, first declension, from *ignāvus* — lazy, cowardly.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ignāvia** is built on *ignāvus* "idle, cowardly", from *in-* "not" and old *gnāvus* "busy, active" (Lewis & Short).
