## You'll want to know: der Rauch
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C293-BRENNEN-02]; assesses=[] -->

**der Rauch** — "the smoke".

> Aus dem Fenster kam dicker Rauch.
>
> *Thick smoke came out of the window.*

No plural. *Rauchen* — to smoke — comes from it.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Rauch* is an inherited Germanic word; EtymDB lists English *reek* and Dutch *rook* "smoke" among its cousins.
