Telugu, chapter 166: To continue, To observe, To correct, To mutter, To separate.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
కొనసాగు (konasāgu) — to continue.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to prick, then the Telugu for to knit.

You'll want to know: కొనసాగు.
కొనసాగు — konasāgu — "to continue".

The word, taken apart.
కొనసాగు (konasāgu) is native Dravidian. Its second part is sāgu, "to go on, continue", one word with Kannada sāgu (DEDR 2430); its first part matches kona, "end, tip" (DEDR 2174), though DEDR does not list the compound.

Lesson 4 of 5.
గొణుగు (goṇugu) — to mutter, to grumble.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to observe, then the Telugu for to correct.

You'll want to know: గొణుగు.
గొణుగు — goṇugu — "to mutter, to grumble".

The word, taken apart.
గొణుగు (goṇugu) is Dravidian. DEDR 1685 lists nasalised forms of goṇugu, "to murmur, mutter, grumble", with Tamil kuṇukuṇu, "to murmur", and points to related sound-words across the region, such as Hindi gungun, "murmur" (CDIAL 4013).
