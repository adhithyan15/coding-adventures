## You'll want to know: ぶつける
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C264-KOROBU264-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ぶつける** — *butsukeru* — "to bump (something against); to hit".

> **こどもが つくえに あたまを ぶつけました。** — *kodomo ga tsukue ni atama o butsukemashita* — "A child banged his head on a desk."

Polite **ぶつけます**, *butsukemasu*; an ichidan verb. Past (polite): **あにが あたまを ぶつけました。**, *ani ga atama o butsukemashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ぶつける** (*butsukeru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, and it is usually written in kana (JMdict). It is the transitive partner of *butsukaru*, "to bump into".
