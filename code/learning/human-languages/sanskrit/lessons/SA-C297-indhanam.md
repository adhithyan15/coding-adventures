## You'll want to know: इन्धनम्
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C297-NIRVAPANA297-01]; assesses=[] -->

**इन्धनम्** — *indhanam* — "fuel; firewood".

> शुष्कम् इन्धनं शीघ्रं ज्वलति।
>
> *śuṣkam indhanaṃ śīghraṃ jvalati.*
>
> Dry fuel burns fast.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**इन्धनम्** (*indhanam*) comes from the root √indh, "to kindle, light"; MW gives *indhana* as kindling and as fuel (MW).
