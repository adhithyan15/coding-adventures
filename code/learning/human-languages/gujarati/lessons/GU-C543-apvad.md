## You'll want to know: અપવાદ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C543-APVAD543-01]; assesses=[] -->

**અપવાદ** — *apvād* — "an exception".

> જો તમે બીમાર હશો તો અમે અપવાદ કરીશું.
>
> *jo tame bīmār haśo to ame apvād karīśũ.*
>
> If you are ill, we will make an exception.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**અપવાદ** (*apvād*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *apavāda*, "speaking against; a refutation" (Monier-Williams); in grammar and in rules it is the case that does not follow.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
