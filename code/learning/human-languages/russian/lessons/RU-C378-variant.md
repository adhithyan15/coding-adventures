## You'll want to know: вариант
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C378-DOGOVORILIS-03]; assesses=[] -->

**вариант** (*variánt*) — "an option, a version".

> Есть два варианта: поезд или самолёт.
>
> *Yest dva variánta: póyezd íli samolyót.*
>
> There are two options: the train or the plane.

Masculine. *Лучший вариант* — the best option. It is also a version of a text.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**вариант** was borrowed from French *variante* (EtymDB), which goes back to Latin *variāns*, the participle of *variāre* "to change, to vary", built on *varius* "various" (Lewis & Short).
