Japanese, chapter 217: At the Doctor's.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
かんじゃ (kanja) — a patient.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for to get better, then the Japanese for to shiver.

You'll want to know: かんじゃ.
かんじゃ — kanja — "a patient".
びょういんに かんじゃは おおいですか。 — byōin ni kanja wa ōi desu ka — "Are there many patients at the hospital?"

The word, taken apart.
かんじゃ (kanja) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are kan, "to suffer, illness" (Middle Chinese hwaenH) and ja, "person" (tsyaeX) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription). The second is voiced from sha to ja.
