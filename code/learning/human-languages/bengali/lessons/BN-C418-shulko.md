# শুল্ক (shulkô) — a duty; a tax on goods

## You'll want to know: শুল্ক
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C418-MUNAFA418-02]; assesses=[] -->

**শুল্ক** — *shulkô* — "a duty; a tax on goods".

> বিদেশি জিনিসের ওপর শুল্ক দিতে হয়।
>
> *bideshi jiniser opôr shulkô dite hôy.*
>
> You have to pay duty on foreign goods.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**শুল্ক** (*shulkô*) is Sanskrit *śulka*, "a toll, a tax, a duty, especially at ferries and roads" (Monier-Williams), taken over whole.
