Hindi, chapter 359: The Issue at Hand.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
मुद्दा (muddā) — an issue, a point at issue.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for greed, then the Hindi for to make known.

You'll want to know: मुद्दा.
मुद्दा — muddā — "an issue, a point at issue".
आज की बैठक में पानी सबसे बड़ा मुद्दा था। āj kī baiṭhak meṁ pānī sabse baṛā muddā thā. Water was the biggest issue at today's meeting.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
मुद्दा (muddā) is usually traced to Arabic muddaʿā, "that which is claimed", which reached Hindi through Persian.

Lesson 2 of 5.
समझौता (samjhautā) — a compromise, a settlement.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for to make known, then the Hindi for an issue.

You'll want to know: समझौता.
समझौता — samjhautā — "a compromise, a settlement".
आख़िर दोनों परिवारों में समझौता हुआ। ākhir donõ parivāroṁ meṁ samjhautā huā. In the end the two families reached a settlement.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
समझौता (samjhautā) is a noun made on समझना, "to understand", which CDIAL traces to Sanskrit saṃbudhyate, "understands" (CDIAL 12959): an understanding reached between two sides.

Lesson 5 of 5.
छेड़ना (cheṛnā) — to raise (a topic); to tease.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Hindi for a dispute, then the Hindi for to get tangled.

You'll want to know: छेड़ना.
छेड़ना — cheṛnā — "to raise (a topic); to tease".
बैठक में अरुण ने पानी का मुद्दा छेड़ा। baiṭhak meṁ Aruṇ ne pānī kā muddā cheṛā. At the meeting Arun brought up the water issue.
Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने वही बात फिर छेड़ी, usne vahī bāt phir cheṛī.

The word, taken apart.
छेड़ना (cheṛnā) is inherited from chēḍ-, a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants and files under ciḍ, "be angry" (CDIAL 4794). CDIAL glosses the Hindi verb "to touch, to irritate"; raising a topic is a sense it does not give.
