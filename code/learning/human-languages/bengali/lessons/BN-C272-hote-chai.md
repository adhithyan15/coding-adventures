# … হতে চাই (hôte chāi) — what I want to become

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[BN-GRAMMAR-C272-FEELING-GENITIVE-01, BN-GRAMMAR-C269-TE-HOBE-01, BN-LEX-C51-VERBS51-03] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "I'm enjoying it here". (*āmār ekhāne bhālo lāgchhe*.) Then "I have to go". (*āmāke jete hôbe*.) Its **যেতে** is the **-তে** form you need now.

## You'll want to know: want to
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-GRAMMAR-C272-WANT-TO-BE-01]; assesses=[BN-LEX-C51-VERBS51-03, BN-LEX-C45-HEALTH45-02, BN-LEX-C07-HOWA-01, BN-LEX-C67-NATURE67-03, BN-LEX-C07-DEKHA-01] -->

> **আমি ডাক্তার হতে চাই।**
>
> *āmi ḍāktār hôte chāi.* — "I want to be a doctor."

**চাই** is **চাওয়া**, "to want", in the present: "I want". With the **-তে** form of another verb it is "want to": **হতে চাই**, "want to be"; **দেখতে চাই** (*dekhte chāi*), "want to see"; **যেতে চাই** (*jete chāi*), "want to go". Here the one who wants is **আমি**, not **আমার**: wanting is something you do.

## You'll want to know: my wish
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-GRAMMAR-C272-WISH-01]; assesses=[BN-LEX-C96-WANT96-01, BN-LEX-C96-WANT96-03, BN-LEX-C66-NATURE66-01] -->

For a wish, use **ইচ্ছা**, "a wish", or **স্বপ্ন**, "a dream", which you know, with the **-র** form of the verb:

> **আমার পাহাড়ে যাওয়ার ইচ্ছা আছে।**
>
> *āmār pāhāṛe jāoyār ichchhā āchhe.* — "I'd like to go to the mountains."

**যাওয়ার ইচ্ছা**, "a wish of going". Said aloud, **ইচ্ছা** is often *ichchhe*, and you will see it written **ইচ্ছে** too.
