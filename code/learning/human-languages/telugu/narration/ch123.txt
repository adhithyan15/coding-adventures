Telugu, chapter 123: Tiger, Lion, Bear, Monkey, Rabbit.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
పులి (puli) — a tiger.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a horse, then the Telugu for an elephant.

You'll want to know: పులి.
పులి — puli — "a tiger".

What you've built.
