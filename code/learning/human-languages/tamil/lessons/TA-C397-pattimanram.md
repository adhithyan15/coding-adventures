# பட்டிமன்றம் (paṭṭimaṉṟam) — a public debate

## You'll want to know: பட்டிமன்றம்
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C397-MAKKALATCI397-02]; assesses=[] -->

**பட்டிமன்றம்** — *paṭṭimaṉṟam* — "a public debate".

> விழாவில் ஒரு சுவாரசியமான பட்டிமன்றம் நடந்தது.
>
> *viḻāvil oru cuvāraciyamāṉa paṭṭimaṉṟam naṭantatu.*
>
> An interesting debate was held at the festival.

A Tamil stage debate: two teams argue a question before an audience, and a judge gives the verdict.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**பட்டிமன்றம்** (*paṭṭimaṉṟam*) is built on native *maṉṟam*, "hall, assembly, court" (DEDR 4777); the origin of the first part, *paṭṭi*, is not settled.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
