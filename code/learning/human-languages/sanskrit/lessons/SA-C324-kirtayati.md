## You'll want to know: कीर्तयति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C324-PURA324-05]; assesses=[] -->

**कीर्तयति** — *kīrtayati* — "tells of; celebrates".

> ग्रन्थः तस्य युद्धस्य शौर्यं कीर्तयति।
>
> *granthaḥ tasya yuddhasya śauryaṃ kīrtayati.*
>
> The book tells of the valour shown in that war.

Past (he …): सः वीरस्य शौर्यं कीर्तितवान्, *saḥ vīrasya śauryaṃ kīrtitavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**कीर्तयति** (*kīrtayati*) comes from the root √kīrt, "to mention, tell, name, celebrate", which belongs with *kīrti*, "fame" (MW).
