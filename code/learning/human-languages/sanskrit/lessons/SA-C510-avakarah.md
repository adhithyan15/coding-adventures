## You'll want to know: अवकरः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C510-AVAKARA510-01]; assesses=[] -->

**अवकरः** — *avakaraḥ* — "rubbish; sweepings".

> प्रकोष्ठे बहु अवकरः अस्ति।
>
> *prakoṣṭhe bahu avakaraḥ asti.*
>
> There is a lot of rubbish in the room.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अवकरः** (*avakaraḥ*) is built on *ava*, "down", and the root √kṝ, "to scatter"; MW gives *avakara* as dust or sweepings (MW).
