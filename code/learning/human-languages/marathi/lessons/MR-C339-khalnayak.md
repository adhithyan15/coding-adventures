## You'll want to know: खलनायक
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C339-NAYAK339-02]; assesses=[] -->

**खलनायक** — *khalnāyak* — "a villain".

> शेवटी खलनायकाला शिक्षा झाली.
>
> *śevṭī khalnāyakālā śikṣā jhālī.*
>
> In the end the villain was punished.

Masculine.

खल, wicked, + नायक, a lead.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**खलनायक** (*khalnāyak*) is a modern compound built from Sanskrit *khala*, 'a wicked man', and *nāyaka*, 'a leader; the hero of a drama' (Monier-Williams).
