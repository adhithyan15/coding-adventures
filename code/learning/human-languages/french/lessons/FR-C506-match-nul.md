## You'll want to know: le match nul
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C506-RESULTATS-01]; assesses=[] -->

**le match nul** — "draw, tie (in sport)".

> Les deux équipes ont fait match nul.
>
> *The two teams drew.*

*Faire match nul* — to draw.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*le match nul* joins *match*, borrowed from English, and *nul* "null, none", from Latin *nūllus* "not any" (Lewis & Short).
