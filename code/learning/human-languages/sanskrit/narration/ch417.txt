Sanskrit, chapter 417: Sandbanks and Shores.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
पुलिनम् (pulinam) — a sandbank.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for rows, then the Sanskrit for comes ashore.

You'll want to know: पुलिनम्.
पुलिनम् — pulinam — "a sandbank".
नद्याः मध्ये पुलिनम् अस्ति। nadyāḥ madhye pulinam asti. There is a sandbank in the middle of the river.
A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
पुलिनम् (pulinam) is the word MW gives for a sandbank, a small island left by a river; MW gives no derivation, so its origin is uncertain (MW).

फेनः (phenaḥ) — foam.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: फेनः.
फेनः — phenaḥ — "foam".
तरंगेषु श्वेतः फेनः अस्ति। taraṅgeṣu śvetaḥ phenaḥ asti. There is white foam on the waves.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
फेनः (phenaḥ) is the word MW gives for foam, froth; MW connects it, probably, with a root √phaṇ, so its origin is uncertain; MW compares English foam and Slavonic pěna (MW).

Lesson 5 of 6.
वेला (velā) — the coast; the shoreline.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a bubble, then the Sanskrit for dry land.

You'll want to know: वेला.
वेला — velā — "the coast; the shoreline".
वेलायां सिकता उष्णा अस्ति। velāyāṃ sikatā uṣṇā asti. The sand on the coast is hot.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
वेला (velā) is the word MW gives for a limit or boundary, a time, and the boundary of sea and land, the coast; MW gives no derivation, so its origin is uncertain (MW).
