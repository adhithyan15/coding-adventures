## You'll want to know: در نتیجه
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C370-PAYAMAD-03]; assesses=[] -->

**در نتیجه** (*dar natije*) — "as a result, consequently".

> او زیاد کار کرد و در نتیجه بیمار شد.
>
> *u ziyâd kâr kard va dar natije bimâr shod.*
>
> He worked too much, and as a result he fell ill.

*Dar* "in" + *natije* "result". A whole clause follows; *dar natije-ye* + noun means "as a result of".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**در نتیجه** joins the inherited Persian preposition *dar* "in" to *natije*, from Arabic *natīja* "result" (EtymDB): in the result.
