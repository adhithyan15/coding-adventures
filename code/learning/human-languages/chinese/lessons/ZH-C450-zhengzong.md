## You'll want to know: zhèngzōng
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C450-LOCAL450-04]; assesses=[] -->

*zhèngzōng* — "authentic; the real thing (of food)".

> *zhè jiā fànguǎn de cài hěn zhèngzōng* — The food at this restaurant is authentic.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*zhèngzōng* is built from 正 'right' and *zōng* 'the source', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *tsyengH* and *tsowng* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'the origin' and 'to honour'.
