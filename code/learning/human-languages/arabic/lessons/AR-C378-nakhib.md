# ناخب (nākhib) — a voter

## You'll want to know: ناخب
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C378-INTIKHAB378-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ناخب** — *nākhib* — "a voter".

> ذهب كل ناخب إلى مدرسة القرية.
>
> *dhahaba kullu nākhib ilā madrasat al-qarya.*
>
> Every voter went to the village school.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ناخب** (*nākhib*) is built on the native Arabic root *n-kh-b*, "to choose, to select" (Lane). *Nākhib* is the active participle, one who chooses: a voter.
