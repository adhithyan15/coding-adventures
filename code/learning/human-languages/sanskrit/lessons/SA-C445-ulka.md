## You'll want to know: उल्का
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C445-ULKA445-01]; assesses=[] -->

**उल्का** — *ulkā* — "a shooting star; a meteor".

> रात्रौ आकाशे उल्का पतति।
>
> *rātrau ākāśe ulkā patati.*
>
> At night a shooting star falls across the sky.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**उल्का** (*ulkā*) comes from the root √uṣ, "to burn", to which MW refers it; MW gives *ulkā* as a fiery phenomenon in the sky, a meteor, a firebrand (MW).
