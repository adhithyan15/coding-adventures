## You'll want to know: はなみ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C501-MOON501-02]; assesses=[] -->

**はなみ** — *hanami* — "flower viewing; cherry-blossom viewing".

> **はるに こうえんで はなみを します。** — *haru ni kōen de hanami o shimasu* — "In spring we have a blossom-viewing party in the park."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**はなみ** (*hanami*) is a native Japanese compound (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese: built from *hana*, "flower" and *mi*, "looking" (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
