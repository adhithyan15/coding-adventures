## You'll want to know: आत्मकथा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C342-PRAKASHAK342-02]; assesses=[] -->

**आत्मकथा** — *ātmakathā* — "an autobiography".

> उस नेता ने अपनी आत्मकथा लिखी।
>
> *us netā ne apnī ātmakathā likhī.*
>
> That leader wrote his autobiography.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**आत्मकथा** (*ātmakathā*) joins Sanskrit *ātman*, "self", and **कथा**, a story (Monier-Williams): the story of oneself, told by oneself.
