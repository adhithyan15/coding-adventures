Sanskrit, chapter 173: Throws, Lets go, Gets, Serves, Enjoys.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
क्षिपति (kṣipati) — throws.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for tourism, then the Sanskrit for a tourist.

You'll want to know: क्षिपति.
क्षिपति — kṣipati — "throws".

The word, taken apart.
kṣipati comes from the root √kṣip "throw, cast, strike" (CDIAL 3683), already in the Rigveda, and inherited from Indo-Iranian (EtymDB). nikṣipati "puts in" is built on the same root.

Lesson 5 of 5.
रमते (ramate) — enjoys, delights in.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for gets, then the Sanskrit for serves.

You'll want to know: रमते.
रमते — ramate — "enjoys, delights in".

The word, taken apart.
ramate comes from the root √ram "stop, rest, take pleasure in" (CDIAL 10628), inherited from Indo-European (EtymDB); MW compares Greek eramai "love". It lives on as Gujarati ramvũ "to enjoy oneself, saunter about" (CDIAL 10628).
