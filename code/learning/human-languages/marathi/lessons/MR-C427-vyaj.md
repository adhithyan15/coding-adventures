## You'll want to know: व्याज
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C427-VYAJ427-01]; assesses=[] -->

**व्याज** — *vyāj* — "interest on money".

> मी दर महिन्याला कर्जावर व्याज भरतो.
>
> *mī dar mahinyālā karjāvar vyāj bharto.*
>
> Every month I pay interest on the loan.

Neuter.

व्याज मिळणे, to earn interest; व्याज भरणे, to pay it.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**व्याज** (*vyāj*) is the Marathi word for interest on money; its origin is not settled.
