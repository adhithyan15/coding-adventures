## You'll want to know: بسانا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C413-KOTHI413-05]; assesses=[] -->

**بسانا** — *basānā* — "to found (a town); to settle (people)".

> کسانوں نے دریا کے پاس ایک بستی بسائی۔
>
> *kisānoṅ ne daryā ke pās ek bastī basāī.*
>
> Farmers founded a settlement near the river.

Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے نیا شہر بسایا, *us ne nayā shahr basāyā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**بسانا** (*basānā*) is built on *basnā*, "to dwell", which CDIAL lists under Sanskrit *vásati*, "dwells" (CDIAL 11435): it is the form for making people settle; **بستی**, "a settlement", belongs to the same family.
