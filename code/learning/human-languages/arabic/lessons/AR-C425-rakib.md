## You'll want to know: راكب
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C425-RAKIB425-02]; assesses=[] -->

**راكب** — *rākib* — "a passenger; a rider".

> نام راكب بجانبي أثناء الرحلة.
>
> *nāma rākib bi-jānibī athnāʾa ar-riḥla.*
>
> A passenger beside me slept during the flight.

Plural: ركاب, *rukkāb*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**راكب** (*rākib*) is built on the native Arabic root *r-k-b*, "to ride" (Lane). It is the active participle of *rakiba*, "to ride": one who rides.
