Lesson 1 of 6.
અધિકારી (adhikārī) — an official.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for a candidate, then the Gujarati for to be elected.

You'll want to know: અધિકારી.
અધિકારી — adhikārī — "an official".
કચેરીમાં એક અધિકારીએ મારી અરજી તપાસી. kacerīmā̃ ek adhikārīe mārī arjī tapāsī. An official at the office checked my application.
A masculine or feminine noun, by the person meant.

The word, taken apart.
અધિકારી (adhikārī) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit adhikārin, "having authority" (Monier-Williams). It shares its source with અધિકાર (adhikār), "authority", but names the person who holds it.

Lesson 5 of 6.
નીમવું (nīmvũ) — to appoint.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for approval, then the Gujarati for permission.

You'll want to know: નીમવું.
નીમવું — nīmvũ — "to appoint".
સરકાર નવા અધિકારીને નીમશે. sarkār navā adhikārīne nīmśe. The government will appoint a new official.
Past, with તેણે (he or she …): તેણે નવો ચોકીદાર નીમ્યો, teṇe navo cokīdār nīmyo.

The word, taken apart.
નીમવું (nīmvũ) is inherited from Sanskrit niyamayati, "restrains"; CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb, "to fix, to appoint" (CDIAL 7251). નિયમ (niyam), "a rule", is its learned cousin.
