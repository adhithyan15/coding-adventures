## You'll want to know: ದೋಚು
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C328-DAROODE328-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ದೋಚು** — *dōcu* — "to loot; to rob".

> ಕಳ್ಳರು ಅಂಗಡಿಯನ್ನು ದೋಚಿದರು.
>
> *kaḷḷaru aṅgaḍiyannu dōcidaru.*
>
> The robbers looted the shop.

Past (I …): ದೋಚಿದೆ, *dōcide*.

The place or person robbed takes -ಅನ್ನು: ಮನೆಯನ್ನು ದೋಚು.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ದೋಚು** (*dōcu*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 3541 lists Kannada *dōcu*, "to plunder, rob", beside Telugu *dō̃cu*, "to plunder, pillage".
