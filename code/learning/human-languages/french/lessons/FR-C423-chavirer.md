## You'll want to know: chavirer
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C423-CROISIERE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**chavirer** — "to capsize; to tip over".

> La petite barque a chaviré près des rochers.
>
> *The little boat capsized near the rocks.*

Figurative: *son cœur chavire* — her heart turns over.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*chavirer* was borrowed from Provençal *capvirar* 'to turn upside down' (EtymDB), from *cap* 'head', from Latin *caput*, and *virar* 'to turn'.
