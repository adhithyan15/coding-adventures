## Script — the speech piece, then the right piece of 起
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-SCRIPT-JI-NOTE-01]; assesses=[] -->

> 记

**Two pieces, left before right.** Count the pieces, not the strokes.

**The left piece, two strokes.** The speech piece of 说: a dot, then a short stroke that runs right, turns down, and rises to the right at the foot.

**The right piece, three strokes**, as on the right of 起: a bar that turns down, a bar across the middle, then a vertical that bends right along the foot and hooks up.

2 + 3 = five.
