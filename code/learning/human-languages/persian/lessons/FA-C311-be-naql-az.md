## You'll want to know: به نقل از
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C311-MANBA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**به نقل از** (*be naql az*) — "quoting, citing".

> روزنامه به نقل از وزیر نوشت که قیمت ها بالا میرود.
>
> *Ruznâme be naql az vazir nevesht ke qeymat-hâ bâlâ miravad.*
>
> Quoting the minister, the newspaper wrote that prices were going up.

*naql* "transfer, relaying" is the same noun as in *naql kardan* "to relate".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*naql* is borrowed from Arabic *naql*, "transport, transfer" (EtymDB); *be* "to" and *az* "from" are inherited Persian.
