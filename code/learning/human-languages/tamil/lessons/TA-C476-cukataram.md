## You'll want to know: சுகாதாரம்
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C476-KIRUMI476-04]; assesses=[] -->

**சுகாதாரம்** — *cukātāram* — "hygiene; public health".

> பள்ளியில் சுகாதாரம் பற்றி ஒரு வகுப்பு நடந்தது.
>
> *paḷḷiyil cukātāram paṟṟi oru vakuppu naṭantatu.*
>
> There was a class on hygiene at school.

சுகாதாரமான, hygienic.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**சுகாதாரம்** (*cukātāram*) is usually traced to a learned compound of Sanskrit *sukha*, "ease, well-being", and *ādhāra*, "support" (Monier-Williams).
