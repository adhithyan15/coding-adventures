## You'll want to know: மாதம்
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C73-WHEN-04]; assesses=[] -->

**மாதம்** (*mādam*) — "a month".

A month.

You already know twelve of them by name — the Tamil months you met in their own chapter. This
is the word for the unit itself, the one you need to say "one month" rather than to name a
particular one.

And another **ம்** ending — like கோபம், பயம் and பணம் before it — on a word whose middle
vowel is the long **ா**.
