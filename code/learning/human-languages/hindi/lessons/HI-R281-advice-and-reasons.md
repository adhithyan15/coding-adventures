# Review — advice, and so

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[HI-GRAMMAR-C281-CHAHIYE-SHOULD-01, HI-LEX-C281-ATAH-01, HI-SCRIPT-C281-VISARGA-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "you should rest". (*tumhẽ ārām karnā cāhiye*.) Then read **अतः** aloud, and write its last sign: the upper dot, then the lower.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[HI-GRAMMAR-C281-CHAHIYE-SHOULD-01, HI-GRAMMAR-C281-PADNA-MUST-01, HI-GRAMMAR-C281-ISLIYE-RESULT-01, HI-GRAMMAR-C281-TO-SO-01, HI-LEX-C281-ATAH-01, HI-SCRIPT-C281-VISARGA-01, HI-GRAMMAR-C280-VAJAH-SE-01, HI-GRAMMAR-C280-LAGTA-KI-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s each]
- [YOU SAY: You should drink water. I had to go to the office.]
- [YOU SAY: It was raining, so we stayed at home.]
- [YOU SAY: You're ill? Then you should rest.]
- [YOU SAY: I think we will have to wait.]
- [YOU READ: **कल छुट्टी है, अतः दफ़्तर बंद रहेगा।** Then say it the everyday way.]

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[HI-GRAMMAR-C281-CHAHIYE-SHOULD-01, HI-GRAMMAR-C281-PADNA-MUST-01, HI-GRAMMAR-C281-ISLIYE-RESULT-01, HI-GRAMMAR-C281-TO-SO-01, HI-LEX-C281-ATAH-01, HI-SCRIPT-C281-VISARGA-01] -->

[PAUSE 3s] "Should"? (**चाहिए**, after a verb in **-ना**.) "Had to"? (**पड़ा**.) "So", between two sentences? (**इसलिए**, or in writing **अतः**.) "So then", opening a reply? (**तो**.) Where does **◌ः** sit? (To the right of its syllable.)
