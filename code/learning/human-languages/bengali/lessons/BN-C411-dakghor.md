# ডাকঘর (ḍākghôr) — a post office

## You'll want to know: ডাকঘর
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C411-DAKGHOR411-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ডাকঘর** — *ḍākghôr* — "a post office".

> আমি ডাকঘর থেকে একটা খাম কিনলাম।
>
> *āmi ḍākghôr theke ekṭā khām kinlām.*
>
> I bought an envelope at the post office.

ডাক is the post itself: ডাকে আসা, to come by post.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ডাকঘর** (*ḍākghôr*) joins ডাক, which CDIAL lists as Bengali *ḍāk*, "a relay, the post, a posting stage" (CDIAL 5519), to **ঘর**, "a house": the house of the post.
