## You'll want to know: はいしゃ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C217-DOCTOR217-04]; assesses=[] -->

**はいしゃ** — *haisha* — "a dentist".

> **あした はいしゃに いきますか。** — *ashita haisha ni ikimasu ka* — "Are you going to the dentist tomorrow?"

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**はいしゃ** (*haisha*) is built from native *ha*, "tooth", inherited, and Sino-Japanese *isha*, "doctor", borrowed from Chinese (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
