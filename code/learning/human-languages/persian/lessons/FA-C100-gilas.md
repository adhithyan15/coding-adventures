**گیلاس** — *gilâs* — "cherries".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*gilâs* is usually traced to Greek *kerasion*, "cherry" (EtymDB), the word that also reached English as *cherry* through Latin and French.
