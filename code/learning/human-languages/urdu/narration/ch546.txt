Urdu, chapter 546: The Hard Way.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
مشقت (mashaqqat) — toil; hard labour.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a stage, then the Urdu for durable.

You'll want to know: مشقت.
مشقت — mashaqqat — "toil; hard labour".
اگر مشقت نہ ہوتی تو یہ پل کبھی نہ بنتا۔ agar mashaqqat na hotī to yih pul kabhī na bantā. Without hard labour this bridge would not have been built at all.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
مشقت (mashaqqat) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic mashaqqa, "hardship, toil".

Lesson 2 of 5.
ہاتھ بٹانا (hāth baṭānā) — to lend a hand.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for durable, then the Urdu for toil.

You'll want to know: ہاتھ بٹانا.
ہاتھ بٹانا — hāth baṭānā — "to lend a hand".
اگر سب نے ہاتھ بٹایا تو کام ختم ہو گا۔ agar sab ne hāth baṭāyā to kām khatm ho gā. If everyone lends a hand, the work will get finished.
Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے امی کا ہاتھ بٹایا, us ne ammī kā hāth baṭāyā.

The word, taken apart.
ہاتھ بٹانا (hāth baṭānā) is built from ہاتھ (hāth), "a hand", and baṭānā, "to share in", which sits beside the Hindi cousin bā̃ṭnā, "shares", that CDIAL lists under Sanskrit váṇṭati (CDIAL 11238): to take a share of someone's work.

Lesson 4 of 5.
جوجھنا (jūjhnā) — to grapple with (a hard task).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for to lend a hand, then the Urdu for ease.

You'll want to know: جوجھنا.
جوجھنا — jūjhnā — "to grapple with (a hard task)".
اگر ہم ساتھ جوجھے تو یہ مشکل ختم ہو گی۔ agar ham sāth jūjhe to yih mushkil khatm ho gī. If we grapple with it together, this trouble will end.
Past (he …): وہ بیماری سے جوجھا, voh bīmārī se jūjhā.

The word, taken apart.
جوجھنا (jūjhnā) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin jūjhnā, "to fight", under Sanskrit yudhyatē, "fights" (CDIAL 10502).
