## You'll want to know: খরা
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C292-KHORA292-01]; assesses=[] -->

**খরা** — *khôrā* — "a drought".

> এই বছর খরার জন্য ধান হল না।
>
> *ei bôchhôr khôrār jônyô dhān hôlô nā.*
>
> This year the rice failed because of the drought.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**খরা** (*khôrā*) is filed in CDIAL 3819 under Sanskrit *khara*, 'hard, sharp', also 'hot' of a wind, which lists Bengali *kharā*, 'hot and dry', and as a verb 'to parch'. The noun 'a drought', the parching season, is the Bengali use.
