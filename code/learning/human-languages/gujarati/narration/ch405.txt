Gujarati, chapter 405: Flights and Gates.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ઉડાન (uḍān) — a flight.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for an uphill climb, then the Gujarati for to jump over.

You'll want to know: ઉડાન.
ઉડાન — uḍān — "a flight".
અમારી ઉડાન આવતીકાલે સવારે છે. amārī uḍān āvtīkāle savāre che. Our flight is tomorrow morning.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ઉડાન (uḍān) is built on ઊડવું, "to fly", a tadbhava of Sanskrit uḍḍīyatē, "flies up" (CDIAL 1697): the flying itself, and then the journey by air.

What you've built.
One more word for getting around.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ચકરાવો (cakrāvo) — a circle in the air; a detour.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for an airport gate, then the Gujarati for a suitcase.

You'll want to know: ચકરાવો.
ચકરાવો — cakrāvo — "a circle in the air; a detour".
વિમાને શહેર ઉપર બે ચકરાવા લીધા અને પછી નીચે ઊતર્યું. vimāne śaher upar be cakrāvā līdhā ane pachī nīce ūtaryũ. The plane circled twice over the city and then came down.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ચકરાવો (cakrāvo) seems to be built on Sanskrit cakra, "a wheel" (Monier-Williams): a round that a plane, a bird or a traveller makes before coming down or going on.
