## You'll want to know: arriscado
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C373-RISCO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**arriscado** — "risky".

> Atravessar a rua com o semáforo vermelho é arriscado.
>
> *Crossing the street on a red light is risky.*

Built on *arriscar* — to risk.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*arriscado* is the participle of *arriscar* "to risk", which EtymDB analyses as *a-* + *risco* + *-ar* (EtymDB); the origin of *risco* "risk" is not settled.
