## You'll want to know: اصول
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C345-QADR345-04]; assesses=[] -->

**اصول** — *usūl* — "a principle".

> میرے کچھ اصول ہیں۔
>
> *mere kuchh usūl haiṅ.*
>
> I have some principles.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**اصول** (*usūl*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *uṣūl*, "roots, foundations"; Urdu uses the word as a singular, "a principle".
