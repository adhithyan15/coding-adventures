# ઉચાટ (ucāṭ) — anxious unease

## You'll want to know: ઉચાટ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C454-UCAT454-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ઉચાટ** — *ucāṭ* — "anxious unease".

> દીકરો મોડે સુધી ઘરે ન આવ્યો, તેથી માતાને ઉચાટ થયો.
>
> *dīkro moḍe sudhī ghare na āvyo, tethī mātāne ucāṭ thayo.*
>
> The son had not come home till late, so his mother grew anxious.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ઉચાટ** (*ucāṭ*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Gujarati word, "impatience, anxiety", under Sanskrit *uccaṭati*, "goes away" (CDIAL 1635): a mind that will not stay put.
