## You'll want to know: にゅうがく
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C288-NYUUGAKU288-01]; assesses=[] -->

**にゅうがく** — *nyūgaku* — "entering school".

> **おとうとは らいねん にゅうがくします。** — *otōto wa rainen nyūgaku shimasu* — "My little brother starts school next year."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**にゅうがく** (*nyūgaku*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *nyū*, "enter" (Middle Chinese *nyip*) and *gaku*, "learning" (*haewk*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
