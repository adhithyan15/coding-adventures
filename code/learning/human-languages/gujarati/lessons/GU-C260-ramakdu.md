## You'll want to know: રમકડું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C260-GAMES260-03]; assesses=[] -->

**રમકડું** — *ramakḍũ* — "toy".

> **મેં બાળક માટે રમકડું ખરીદ્યું.** — *mẽ bāḷak māṭe ramakḍũ kharīdyũ* — "I bought a toy for the child."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**રમકડું** (*ramakḍũ*), a thing to play with, is built on the taught *ramvũ*, "to play", with *-kḍũ*. CDIAL derives *ramvũ* from Sanskrit *rámatē*, "takes pleasure in" (CDIAL 10628), but lists no *ramakḍũ*.
