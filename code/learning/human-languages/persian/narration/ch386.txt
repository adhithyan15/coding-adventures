Persian, chapter 386: Fairness in society.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
برابری (barâbari) — equality.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for elderly, then the Persian for a religion.

You'll want to know: برابری.
برابری (barâbari) — "equality".
آنها برای برابری زن و مرد تلاش میکنند. ânhâ barâye barâbari-ye zan o mard talâsh mikonand. They work for equality between women and men.
From barâbar "equal" + -i. The opposite is nâbarâbari.

The word, taken apart.
برابری (barâbari) is native Persian, built on barâbar "equal, level" with the noun suffix -i.

عدالت ('edâlat) — "justice".
مردم از دولت عدالت میخواهند. mardom az dowlat 'edâlat mikhâhand. People want justice from the government.
'Âdel — fair, righteous. In speech mikhâhand is mikhân.

The word, taken apart.
عدالت ('edâlat) was borrowed from Arabic 'adāla "justice, fairness" (EtymDB), from the same Arabic family as 'adl "justice".

Lesson 5 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for justice, then the Persian for poverty.

You'll want to know: ثروت.
ثروت (servat) — "wealth".
ثروت او از پدرش به او رسیده است. servat-e u az pedarash be u reside ast. His wealth came to him from his father.
Servatmand — wealthy. ث sounds like s.

The word, taken apart.
ثروت (servat) was borrowed from Arabic tharwa "wealth" (EtymDB); Persian says the Arabic th as s.
