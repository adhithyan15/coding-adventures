## You'll want to know: தளபதி
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C334-THER334-05]; assesses=[] -->

**தளபதி** — *taḷapati* — "a commander; a general".

> தளபதி படையை முன்னால் நின்று நடத்தினார்.
>
> *taḷapati paṭaiyai muṉṉāl niṉṟu naṭattiṉār.*
>
> The commander led the army from the front.

Today also a general in an army.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**தளபதி** (*taḷapati*) is usually traced to Sanskrit, *dala*, "a troop", and *pati*, "lord"; Monier-Williams lists *dalapati*.
