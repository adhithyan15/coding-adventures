# काल — yesterday or tomorrow

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MW-LEX-C79-TIME79-04, MW-LEX-C90-TIME90-05, MW-GRAMMAR-C162-FUT-NEG-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say *I won't go tomorrow*. Then say **काल** and **परसूं** on their own.

## Grammar Lens: the verb decides
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-GRAMMAR-C162-KAL-TENSE-01]; assesses=[MW-LEX-C79-TIME79-04, MW-GRAMMAR-C159-GAYO-01, MW-GRAMMAR-C162-FUT-1SG-01] -->

**काल** is one word for two days, and the verb tells you which:

> **हूं काल गयो।**
>
> *hū̃ kāl gayo.* — I went yesterday.

> **हूं काल जासूं।**
>
> *hū̃ kāl jāsū̃.* — I will go tomorrow.

**परसूं** works the same way, for the day before yesterday or the day after
tomorrow.

- [YOU SAY: **हूं काल आसूं।** — *hū̃ kāl āsū̃.* — I will come tomorrow.]
- [YOU SAY: **मां परसूं आई।** — *mā̃ parsū̃ āī.* — Mother came the day before yesterday.]
