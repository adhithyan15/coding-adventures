## You'll want to know: तय करना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C370-SABHA370-05]; assesses=[] -->

**तय करना** — *tay karnā* — "to fix (a date), to decide".

> सभा ने अगली बैठक की तारीख़ तय की।
>
> *sabhā ne aglī baiṭhak kī tārīkh tay kī.*
>
> The meeting settled the date of the next one.

Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने दिन तय किया, *usne din tay kiyā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**तय करना** (*tay karnā*) joins तय to करना, "to do". तय is usually traced to Arabic *ṭayy*, "folding up", which reached Hindi through Persian. The sense of settling a matter is a Persian and Hindi one.
