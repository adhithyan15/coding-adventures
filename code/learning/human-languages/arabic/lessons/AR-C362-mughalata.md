## You'll want to know: مغالطة
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C362-MANTIQ362-02]; assesses=[] -->

**مغالطة** — *mughālaṭa* — "a fallacy".

> في هذا الكلام مغالطة واضحة.
>
> *fī hādhā al-kalām mughālaṭa wāḍiḥa.*
>
> There is a clear fallacy in what was said.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**مغالطة** (*mughālaṭa*) is built on the native Arabic root *gh-l-ṭ*, "to make a mistake" (Lane). It is the Form III verbal noun, leading someone into error: a fallacy, an argument that misleads.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for giving reasons and opinions.
