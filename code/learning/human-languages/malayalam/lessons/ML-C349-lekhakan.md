## You'll want to know: ലേഖകൻ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C349-VARTTA349-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ലേഖകൻ** — *lēkhakan* — "a correspondent; a writer".

> പത്രത്തിന്റെ ലേഖകൻ ഗ്രാമത്തിൽ എത്തി.
>
> *patrattinṟe lēkhakan grāmattil etti.*
>
> The newspaper's correspondent reached the village.

A woman writer or correspondent is ലേഖിക. A correspondent writes about events: ലേഖകൻ ആഘോഷം കണ്ടു, the correspondent saw the celebration.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ലേഖകൻ** (*lēkhakan*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *lekhaka*, "a writer, a scribe, a clerk" (Monier-Williams), with the Malayalam masculine ending -ൻ. It belongs to the same family as ലേഖനം (*lēkhanaṁ*), "an article".
