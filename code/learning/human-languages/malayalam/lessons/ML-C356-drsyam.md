## You'll want to know: ദൃശ്യം
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C356-VIVARANAM356-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ദൃശ്യം** — *dṛśyaṁ* — "footage; a scene".

> അപകടത്തിന്റെ ദൃശ്യം ടിവിയിൽ വന്നു.
>
> *apakaṭattinṟe dṛśyaṁ ṭiviyil vannu.*
>
> Footage of the accident came on TV.

Plural: ദൃശ്യങ്ങൾ, *dṛśyaṅṅaḷ*.

Plural ദൃശ്യങ്ങൾ: the pictures or footage of an event.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ദൃശ്യം** (*dṛśyaṁ*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *dṛśya*, "visible, worth seeing" (Monier-Williams), from *dṛś*, "to see".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
