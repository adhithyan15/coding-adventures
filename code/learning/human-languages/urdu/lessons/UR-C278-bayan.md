## You'll want to know: بیان
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C278-THANA278-05]; assesses=[] -->

**بیان** — *bayān* — "a statement".

> یہ گواہ کے بیان پر منحصر ہے۔
>
> *yih gavāh ke bayān par munhasir hai.*
>
> It depends on the witness's statement.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *bayān*, "a making clear, an account". A **گواہ** gives a **بیان** to the police.
