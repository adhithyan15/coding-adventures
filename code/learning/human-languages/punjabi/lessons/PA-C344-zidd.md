## You'll want to know: ਜ਼ਿੱਦ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C344-ARGUE344-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ਜ਼ਿੱਦ** — *zidd* — "stubbornness; insistence".

> ਹੁਣ ਜ਼ਿੱਦ ਨਾ ਕਰੋ।
>
> *huṇ zidd nā karo.*
>
> Don't be stubborn now.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਜ਼ਿੱਦ** (*zidd*) is a loan, usually traced through Persian to Arabic *ḍidd*, "opposition".
