## You'll want to know: پھاٹک
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C388-SIGNAL388-03]; assesses=[] -->

**پھاٹک** — *phāṭak* — "a gate; a level crossing".

> گاڑی آ رہی تھی، اس لیے پھاٹک بند تھا۔
>
> *gāṛī ā rahī thī, is liye phāṭak band thā.*
>
> A train was coming, so the level crossing was shut.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**پھاٹک** (*phāṭak*) is inherited from *phāṭṭakka*, "a gate", a form CDIAL reconstructs (CDIAL 9069).
