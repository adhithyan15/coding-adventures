## You'll want to know: சுரண்டு
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C370-VARUMAI370-05]; assesses=[] -->

**சுரண்டு** — *curaṇṭu* — "to exploit; to scrape".

> அந்த முதலாளி தொழிலாளர்களைச் சுரண்டினார்.
>
> *anta mutalāḷi toḻilāḷarkaḷaic curaṇṭiṉār.*
>
> That employer exploited the workers.

Past (I …): சுரண்டினேன், *curaṇṭiṉēṉ*.

Who is exploited takes -ஐ. Its first sense is to scrape, as a burnt pot is scraped clean.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**சுரண்டு** (*curaṇṭu*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 1564 lists Tamil *curaṇṭu*, "to scrape", in the family of *cirai*, "to shave, cut"; to exploit is to scrape someone bare.
