# ચંચળ (cancaḷ) — never still; fickle

## You'll want to know: ચંચળ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C462-CANCAL462-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ચંચળ** — *cancaḷ* — "never still; fickle".

> નાનો ભત્રીજો ઘણો ચંચળ છે.
>
> *nāno bhatrījo ghaṇo cancaḷ che.*
>
> My little nephew is never still.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ચંચળ** (*cancaḷ*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *cañcala*, "moving to and fro, unsteady", as Monier-Williams records it.
