## You'll want to know: みる
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C236-FILM236-02]; assesses=[] -->

**みる** — *miru* — "to watch; to see".

> **きのう うちで えいがを みました。** — *kinō uchi de eiga o mimashita* — "Yesterday I watched a film at home."

Polite **みます**, *mimasu*; an ichidan verb.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**みる** (*miru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict, KANJIDIC2).
