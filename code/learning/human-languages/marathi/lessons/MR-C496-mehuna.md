## You'll want to know: मेहुणा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C496-SASAR496-03]; assesses=[] -->

**मेहुणा** — *mehuṇā* — "a wife's brother".

> माझा मेहुणा मला भेटायला आला.
>
> *mājhā mehuṇā malā bheṭāylā ālā.*
>
> My wife's brother came to see me.

Masculine.

Also a sister's husband; मेहुणी, a wife's sister.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**मेहुणा** (*mehuṇā*) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Marathi *mehuṇā*, "wife's brother, sister's husband", under Sanskrit *maithuna* (CDIAL 10341).
