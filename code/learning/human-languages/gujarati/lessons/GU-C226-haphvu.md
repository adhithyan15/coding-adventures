# હાંફવું (hā̃phvũ) — to pant, to be out of breath

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[GU-LEX-C225-UNWELL225-05, GU-LEX-C226-BREATH226-01] -->

## You'll want to know: હાંફવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C226-BREATH226-02]; assesses=[] -->

**હાંફવું** — *hā̃phvũ* — "to pant, to be out of breath".

> **દાદા હવે નથી હાંફતા.** — *dādā have nathī hā̃phtā* — "Grandfather is not out of breath now."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**હાંફવું** (*hā̃phvũ*) comes from *\*hamph*, "pant", an imitative reconstructed form; CDIAL lists the Gujarati verb, glossed "to pant" (CDIAL 13973).
