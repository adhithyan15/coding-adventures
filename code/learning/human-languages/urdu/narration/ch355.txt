Urdu, chapter 355: Praise and Blame.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
تعریف (tārīf) — praise.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a groundless fear, then the Urdu for unwarranted.

You'll want to know: تعریف.
تعریف — tārīf — "praise".
استاد نے سارا کی تعریف کی۔ ustād ne sārā kī tārīf kī. The teacher praised Sara.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
تعریف (tārīf) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic taʿrīf, "making known, description"; Urdu uses it for describing someone well, "praise".

Lesson 4 of 5.
سراہنا (sarāhnā) — to commend; to praise.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for fault, then the Urdu for reproach.

You'll want to know: سراہنا.
سراہنا — sarāhnā — "to commend; to praise".
سب نے اس کے کام کو سراہا۔ sab ne us ke kām ko sarāhā. Everyone praised her work.
Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے کوشش کو سراہا, us ne koshish ko sarāhā.

The word, taken apart.
سراہنا (sarāhnā) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin sarāhnā under Sanskrit ślāghate, "praises" (CDIAL 12734).

جھیلنا — jhelnā — "to endure; to put up with".

Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے سردی جھیلی, us ne sardī jhelī.

The word, taken apart.
