## The exchange
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-PERFORMANCE-PROBLEM-01]; assesses=[ZH-GRAMMAR-PROBLEM-01, ZH-GRAMMAR-POTENTIAL-01, ZH-GRAMMAR-DE-SHIHOU-01, ZH-GRAMMAR-V-ZAI-PLACE-01, ZH-GRAMMAR-YINGGAI-01, ZH-LEX-C285-LIANXI-01, ZH-LEX-C285-ZHENGJIAN-01, ZH-LEX-C285-GUASHI-01, ZH-LEX-C282-DEI-01, ZH-LEX-C196-TICKET196-01, ZH-LEX-C83-WAIT83-03] -->

> **A:** 我的车票不见了
>
> *wǒ de chēpiào bú jiàn le* — My ticket's gone.
>
> **B:** 你找了吗
>
> *nǐ zhǎo le ma* — Have you looked for it?
>
> **A:** 找了 我找不到
>
> *zhǎo le, wǒ zhǎo bu dào* — I have. I can't find it.
>
> **B:** 你上车的时候有吗
>
> *nǐ shàngchē de shíhou yǒu ma* — Did you have it when you got on?
>
> **A:** 有 我坐在这儿的时候还有
>
> *yǒu, wǒ zuò zài zhèr de shíhou hái yǒu* — Yes. I still had it when I sat down here.
>
> **B:** *nǐ yīnggāi liánxì chēzhàn* — You should get in touch with the station.
>
> **A:** *wǒ de zhèngjiàn yě bú jiàn le* — My papers are gone too.
>
> **B:** *nà nǐ děi mǎshàng guàshī* — Then you'll have to report them lost straight away.

A answers with the verb alone, 找了, and B leads with the moment, 你上车的时候. 还有 says A still had it then.

## What you've built — when something goes missing
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ZH-PERFORMANCE-PROBLEM-01, ZH-GRAMMAR-PROBLEM-01, ZH-GRAMMAR-POTENTIAL-01, ZH-LEX-C285-ZHAOHUI-01, ZH-LEX-C285-GUASHI-01, ZH-LEX-C285-LIANXI-01, ZH-LEX-C285-ZHENGJIAN-01] -->

What to say, from the bad news to the end of it:

- **我的车票不见了** *wǒ de chēpiào bú jiàn le* — it's gone.
- **我找不到** *wǒ zhǎo bu dào* — I can't find it.
- *guàshī*, *liánxì* — report it, and leave a way to reach you.
- **我找回了我的手机** *wǒ zhǎohuí le wǒ de shǒujī* — and, with luck, it comes back.

**Listen.** *Nǐ de shénme bú jiàn le?*

**Speak.** Report something missing at a desk, in four sentences: what, when, what you need, how to reach you.
