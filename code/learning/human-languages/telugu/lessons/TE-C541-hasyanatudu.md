## You'll want to know: హాస్యనటుడు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C541-NATAKAM541-04]; assesses=[] -->

**హాస్యనటుడు** — *hāsyanaṭuḍu* — "a comedian; a comic actor".

> ఆ హాస్యనటుడిని చూస్తేనే అందరూ నవ్వుతారు.
>
> *ā hāsyanaṭuḍini cūstēnē andarū navvutāru.*
>
> The mere sight of that comedian makes everyone laugh.

హాస్యం, humour, + నటుడు, actor.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**హాస్యనటుడు** (*hāsyanaṭuḍu*) is a compound built from Sanskrit *hāsya*, "laughable; laughter" (CDIAL 14071), and నటుడు, "an actor".
