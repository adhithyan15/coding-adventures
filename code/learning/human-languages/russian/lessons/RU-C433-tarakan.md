## You'll want to know: таракан
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C433-SHUM-03]; assesses=[] -->

**таракан** (*tarakán*) — "a cockroach".

> На кухне мы увидели таракана.
>
> *Na kúkhne my uvídeli tarakána.*
>
> We saw a cockroach in the kitchen.

Masculine. Colloquial: *тараканы в голове* — odd ideas.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

The origin of *таракан* is not settled; it is often traced to a Turkic word. EtymDB lists the Russian word without a source.
