## You'll want to know: līberālitās, līberālitātis
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C506-COMI-05]; assesses=[] -->

**līberālitās, līberālitātis** — "generosity".

> Līberālitāte dominī servus lībertātem accipit.
>
> *Through his master's generosity the slave receives his freedom.*

Feminine, third declension, from *līberālis* — befitting a free person. English *liberality*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**līberālitās** is built on *līberālis* "befitting a free person", from *līber* "free" (Lewis & Short). English *liberality* comes from it.
