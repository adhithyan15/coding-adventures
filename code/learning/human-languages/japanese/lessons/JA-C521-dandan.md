## You'll want to know: だんだん
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C521-GRADUAL521-02]; assesses=[] -->

**だんだん** — *dandan* — "little by little; gradually".

> **くすりを のんだら、ちちは だんだん よく なるでしょう。** — *kusuri o nondara, chichi wa dandan yoku naru deshō* — "If he takes the medicine, my father will gradually get better."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**だんだん** (*dandan*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): one Chinese character read with its on'yomi, reading borrowed from Chinese. It is *dan*, "grade" (Middle Chinese *dwanH*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
