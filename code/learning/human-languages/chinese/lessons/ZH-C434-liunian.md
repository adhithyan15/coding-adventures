## You'll want to know: liúniàn
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C434-GROUP434-04]; assesses=[] -->

*liúniàn* — "to keep as a souvenir".

> *wǒmen zài ménkǒu zhàoxiàng liúniàn* — We're having our photo taken at the entrance to remember the trip.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*liúniàn* is built from *liú* 'to keep' and *niàn* 'to remember', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *ljuw* and *nemH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to stay' and the second 'to think of'.
