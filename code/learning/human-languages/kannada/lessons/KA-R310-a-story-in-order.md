# Review — a story in order

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[KA-LEX-C310-SVACCHA-01, KA-SCRIPT-RECOG-174, KA-GRAMMAR-C309-ONGOING-PAST-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "clean". (*svaccha*.) Which letter sits small inside it, and what does it add to **ಚ**? (**ಛ**: the breath.) Then "At nine last night I was reading a book."
- [YOU WRITE: **ಛ** and **ಸ್ವಚ್ಛ**, once each from memory]

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[KA-LEX-C310-SVACCHA-01, KA-SCRIPT-RECOG-174, KA-GRAMMAR-C310-FIRST-THEN-01, KA-GRAMMAR-C310-BEFORE-AFTER-01, KA-GRAMMAR-C310-CONJUNCTIVE-01, KA-GRAMMAR-C310-AFTER-DOING-01, KA-SKILL-C310-READ-STORY-01, KA-GRAMMAR-C309-WHILE-01, KA-GRAMMAR-C309-THERE-WAS-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s each]
- [YOU SAY: First we cleaned the house. After that Mother cooked.]
- [YOU SAY: Father went to the station and brought Grandmother.]
- [YOU SAY: After the meal, Grandmother told a story.]
- [YOU SAY: In the end, at ten at night, the guests left.]
- [YOU SAY: the day the guests came, in your own words, in four sentences]
