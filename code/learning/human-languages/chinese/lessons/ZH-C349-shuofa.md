## You'll want to know: 说法
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C349-VIEW349-03]; assesses=[] -->

**说法** — *shuōfa* — "version of events; way of putting it".

> **我觉得这个说法不对** *wǒ juéde zhège shuōfa bú duì* — I don't think that's the right way to put it.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**说法** (*shuōfa*) is built from 说 'to say' and 法 'a rule', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *sywet* and *pjop* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to tell' and the second 'a rule'. The second is said light here.
