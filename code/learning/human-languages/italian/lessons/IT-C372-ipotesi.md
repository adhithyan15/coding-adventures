## You'll want to know: l'ipotesi
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C372-IPOTESI-02]; assesses=[] -->

**l'ipotesi** — "the hypothesis, the possibility".

> Nell'ipotesi che il treno sia in ritardo, prendiamo un taxi.
>
> *If the train happens to be late, we will take a taxi.*

The same in the plural: *le ipotesi*. *Nell'ipotesi che* takes the subjunctive.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ipotesi* is a learned borrowing from Late Latin *hypothesis*, from Greek *hypóthesis* "a placing under, a supposition", built from *hypó* "under" and *thésis* "a placing", the word behind Latin *thesis* "a proposition" (Lewis & Short).
