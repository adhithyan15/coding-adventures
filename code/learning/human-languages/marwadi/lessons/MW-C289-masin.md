## You'll want to know: मसीन
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C289-MASIN289-01]; assesses=[] -->

**मसीन** — *masīn* — "a machine".

> मसीन नुवी है।
>
> *masīn nuvī hai.*
>
> The machine is new.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**मसीन** (*masīn*) is borrowed from English *machine*. It is feminine: **मसीन नुवी है**.
