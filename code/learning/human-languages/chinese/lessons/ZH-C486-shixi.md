## You'll want to know: shíxí
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C486-JOBHUNT486-04]; assesses=[] -->

*shíxí* — "to do an internship; to work as a trainee".

> *wǒ zài yínháng shíxí le sān ge yuè* — I did a three-month internship at a bank.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*shíxí* is built from *shí* 'real' and *xí* 'to practise', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *zyit* and *zip* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'full' and 'true' and the second 'to learn'.
