## You'll want to know: ਥੈਲਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C275-FIT275-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ਥੈਲਾ** — *thailā* — "a bag; a shopping bag".

> ਮੇਰਾ ਥੈਲਾ ਸਬਜ਼ੀ ਨਾਲ ਭਰਿਆ ਹੈ।
>
> *merā thailā sabzī nāl bhariā hai.*
>
> My bag is full of vegetables.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਥੈਲਾ** (*thailā*) is inherited from Sanskrit *sthavi*, "sack, bag"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *thailā*, "bag", under it (CDIAL 13746).
