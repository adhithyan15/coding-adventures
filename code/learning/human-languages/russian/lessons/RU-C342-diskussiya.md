## You'll want to know: дискуссия
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C342-OBSUZHDAT-03]; assesses=[] -->

**дискуссия** (*diskússiya*) — "a debate, an organised discussion".

> После фильма началась интересная дискуссия.
>
> *Pósle fílma nachalás interésnaya diskússiya.*
>
> After the film an interesting debate began.

Feminine. More formal than *обсуждение*: a public or organised debate.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**дискуссия** was borrowed from Latin *discussiō* (EtymDB) "a shaking apart, an examination", from *discutere* "to strike apart, to examine" (Lewis & Short).
