## You'll want to know: पद्धत
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C373-PADDHAT373-05]; assesses=[] -->

**पद्धत** — *paddhat* — "a method; a way".

> शिकवण्याची त्यांची पद्धत वेगळी आहे.
>
> *śikvaṇyācī tyāñcī paddhat vegḷī āhe.*
>
> Their way of teaching is different.

Feminine.

Feminine: X ची पद्धत, the way of X.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पद्धत** (*paddhat*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *paddhati*, "a path, a track, a way" (Monier-Williams). Marathi drops the final -i.
