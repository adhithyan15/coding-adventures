## You'll want to know: ملاپ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C298-BICHHAR298-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ملاپ** — *milāp* — "a reunion; a coming together".

> شام کو ماں بیٹے کا ملاپ ہوا۔
>
> *shām ko māṅ beṭe kā milāp huā.*
>
> In the evening mother and son were reunited.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ملاپ** (*milāp*) is built on **ملنا**, to meet, which CDIAL lists for Hindi under Sanskrit *miláti*, "meets" (CDIAL 10133): a meeting again after a parting.
