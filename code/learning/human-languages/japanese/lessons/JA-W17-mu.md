## Script — the shape
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-SCRIPT-MU-01]; assesses=[JA-SCRIPT-NE-01] -->

> **む** — *mu*

Three strokes, in this order, as KanjiVG records them: a horizontal across the
upper left; then a long stroke from above it running down and looping at the
foot; then a small separate mark up on the right.

That third mark is the one to watch. It is not attached to anything, and a
**む** without it is not a **む**.

**ね** also ends in a loop at the foot. The difference is what comes before it
and that separate mark on the right.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[JA-SCRIPT-MU-01, JA-SCRIPT-NE-01] -->

1. Trace **む** once, counting three lifts.
2. Copy it; hide it; write it again.
3. Check one thing: is the right-hand mark there, and separate?
