## You'll want to know: il malinteso
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C346-MALINTESO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**il malinteso** — "the misunderstanding".

> Mi dispiace, è stato un malinteso.
>
> *I am sorry, it was a misunderstanding.*

*C'è stato un malinteso* — there has been a misunderstanding.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*malinteso* is built from *male* "badly", from Latin *male*, and *inteso*, past participle of *intendere* "to understand", from Latin *intendere* "to stretch towards, to direct the mind" (Lewis & Short), built on *tendere*.
