## You'll want to know: pàochá
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C166-TEA166-05]; assesses=[] -->

*pàochá* — "to make tea (to steep the leaves)".

> *wǒ yòng kāishuǐ pàochá* — I make the tea with boiling water.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*pàochá* is built from *pào* 'to soak' and 茶 'tea', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *phaew* and *drae* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription). The dictionary glosses the first 'bubbles on water'; soaking is not given there, and *phaew* would regularly give a first tone, so how *pào* came about is not settled.
