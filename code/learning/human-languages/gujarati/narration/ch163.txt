Gujarati, chapter 163: Breakfast and the family meal.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
નાસ્તો (nāsto) — breakfast, snack.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for clay pot, then the Gujarati for corner.

You'll want to know: નાસ્તો.
નાસ્તો — nāsto — "breakfast, snack".
અમે સવારે નાસ્તો કરીએ છીએ. — ame savāre nāsto karīe chīe — "We have breakfast in the morning."

The word, taken apart.
નાસ્તો (nāsto) is a loan from Persian nāshta, "breakfast"; EtymDB lists the Persian word. CDIAL, which follows inherited words, has no entry for it.

Lesson 2 of 5.
ભોજન (bhojan) — meal, food.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for corner, then the Gujarati for breakfast.

You'll want to know: ભોજન.
ભોજન — bhojan — "meal, food".
આજનું ભોજન સારું છે. — ājnũ bhojan sārũ che — "Today's meal is good."

The word, taken apart.
ભોજન (bhojan) is Sanskrit bhojana, "eating; food", taken over whole as a learned loan, a tatsama. CDIAL lists only the inherited Old Gujarati bhoyaṇa, "food, meal", under that word (CDIAL 9630).

Lesson 3 of 5.
જમવું (jamvũ) — to have a meal, to dine.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for breakfast, then the Gujarati for meal.

You'll want to know: જમવું.
જમવું — jamvũ — "to have a meal, to dine".
અમે સાંજે ઘરે જમીએ છીએ. — ame sā̃je ghare jamīe chīe — "We eat at home in the evening."

The word, taken apart.
જમવું (jamvũ) is a tadbhava, inherited through Prakrit. CDIAL lists it under Sanskrit jámati, "eats", and again under jimati, and says it may come from either (CDIAL 5126, 5267).

Lesson 4 of 5.
પીરસવું (pīrasvũ) — to serve (food).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for meal, then the Gujarati for to have a meal.

You'll want to know: પીરસવું.
પીરસવું — pīrasvũ — "to serve (food)".
માતા થાળીમાં ભાત પીરસે છે. — mātā thāḷīmā̃ bhāt pīrase che — "Mother serves rice on the plate."

The word, taken apart.
પીરસવું (pīrasvũ) is a tadbhava. It comes from Sanskrit pariveṣati, "attends, serves food", and CDIAL glosses the Gujarati verb "to serve a meal" (CDIAL 7888).

વહેંચવું (vahẽcvũ) is a tadbhava. It comes from Sanskrit vibhakta, "divided, distributed", and CDIAL glosses the Gujarati verb "divides, distributes" (CDIAL 11808).
