## You'll want to know: zìxíngchē
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C106-STOP106-04]; assesses=[] -->

*zìxíngchē* — "bicycle".

> *zhè shì wǒ de zìxíngchē* — This is my bike.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*zìxíngchē* is built from zì 'self', xíng 'to go' and 车 'vehicle', all inherited from Middle Chinese, *dzijH*, *haeng* and *tsyhae* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription). The dictionary glosses *haeng* 'to walk' and *dzijH* 'from'; the bicycle is a modern sense of the whole.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
