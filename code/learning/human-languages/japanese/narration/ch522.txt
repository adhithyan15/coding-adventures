Japanese, chapter 522: Something, Someone, Somewhere.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
なにか (nanika) — something; anything.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for finally (after a long wait), then the Japanese for a good deal.

You'll want to know: なにか.
なにか — nanika — "something; anything".
もし なにか あったら、でんわして ください。 — moshi nanika attara, denwa shite kudasai — "If anything happens, please call me."

The word, taken apart.
なにか (nanika) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese: built from nani, "what", and the particle ka (JMdict).

What you've built.
One more word for saying what happens if.
