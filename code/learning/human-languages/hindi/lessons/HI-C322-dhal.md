## You'll want to know: ढाल
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C322-HATHIYAR322-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ढाल** — *ḍhāl* — "a shield".

> सिपाही के एक हाथ में तलवार और दूसरे में ढाल थी।
>
> *sipāhī ke ek hāth meṁ talvār aur dūsre meṁ ḍhāl thī.*
>
> The soldier had a sword in one hand and a shield in the other.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ढाल** (*ḍhāl*) goes back to Sanskrit *ḍhāla*, "a shield" (CDIAL 5583). CDIAL marks the Hindi word masculine; standard Hindi today treats it as feminine.
