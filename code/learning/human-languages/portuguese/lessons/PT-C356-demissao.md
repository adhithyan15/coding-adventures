## You'll want to know: a demissão
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C356-DESPEDIMENTO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**a demissão** — "the resignation".

> Ela apresentou a demissão ao chefe.
>
> *She handed in her resignation to the boss.*

*Apresentar a demissão* — to hand in your notice. In Brazil, *demissão* is also being fired.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*demissão* is a learned borrowing from Latin *dīmissiō* "a sending out, a sending away" (Lewis & Short), from *dīmittere* "to send away", built on *mittere* "to send".
