## You'll want to know: ಪೀಠಿಕೆ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C364-DINACHARI364-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ಪೀಠಿಕೆ** — *pīṭhike* — "a preface; an introduction".

> ಪುಸ್ತಕದ ಪೀಠಿಕೆಯನ್ನು ಲೇಖಕರ ಸ್ನೇಹಿತ ಬರೆದನು.
>
> *pustakada pīṭhikeyannu lēkhakara snēhita baredanu.*
>
> The book's preface was written by the author's friend.

The opening part that comes before the story; also the background to a talk.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಪೀಠಿಕೆ** (*pīṭhike*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *pīṭhikā*, "a stool, a base, a pedestal" (Monier-Williams): the base a book stands on.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
