## You'll want to know: संस्कारः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C493-SAMSKARA493-01]; assesses=[] -->

**संस्कारः** — *saṃskāraḥ* — "a rite of passage".

> विवाहः अपि एकः संस्कारः अस्ति।
>
> *vivāhaḥ api ekaḥ saṃskāraḥ asti.*
>
> A wedding too is a rite of passage.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**संस्कारः** (*saṃskāraḥ*) is built from *sam* and the root √kṛ, "to make": a making perfect; MW gives "a sacred or sanctifying ceremony", twelve of them in a life (MW).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
