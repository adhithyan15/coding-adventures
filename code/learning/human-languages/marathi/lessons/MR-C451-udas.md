## You'll want to know: उदास
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C451-MOOD451-02]; assesses=[] -->

**उदास** — *udās* — "gloomy; low, downcast".

> पावसाळ्यात अरुण कधीकधी उदास असतो.
>
> *pāvsāḷyāt aruṇ kadhīkadhī udās asto.*
>
> In the monsoon Arun is sometimes low.

Unchanging: उदास मुलगा, उदास मुलगी. उदास वाटणे, to feel low.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**उदास** (*udās*) is usually traced to Sanskrit *udāsa*, a noun, "indifference, apathy" (Monier-Williams); in Marathi it is an adjective of low spirits.
