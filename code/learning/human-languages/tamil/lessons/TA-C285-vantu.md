# வந்து (vantu) — having come, and then

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[TA-GRAMMAR-C285-FIRST-THEN-01, TA-GRAMMAR-C284-PAST-PERSON-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "First I drank tea. After that I went to school." (*mutalil nāṉ tēnīr kuṭittēṉ. ataṉ piṟaku paḷḷikkup pōṉēṉ.*)

[PAUSE 1s] Two sentences, two full verbs. Tamil can fold them into one.

## You'll want to know: the -உ and -இ form
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-GRAMMAR-C285-VERBAL-PARTICIPLE-01]; assesses=[TA-LEX-VEEDU-01, TA-LEX-SAAPPIDU-01, TA-LEX-C68-SHOP-01, TA-LEX-C44-ASK-05, TA-LEX-C68-SHOP-04, TA-LEX-C04-POY-VARUGIREN-01, TA-LEX-TAYAVUSEYTU-01, TA-GRAMMAR-C284-PAST-STEMS-01] -->

> **நான் வீட்டுக்கு வந்து சாப்பிட்டேன்.**
>
> *nāṉ vīṭṭukku vantu cāppiṭṭēṉ.* — "I came home and ate."

The first verb carries no person and no time: **வந்து**, "having come". Only the last verb says who and when. So a chain of these runs on to one full verb at the end:

> **அவன் கடைக்குப் போய் ஒரு புத்தகம் வாங்கி வீட்டுக்கு வந்தான்.**
>
> *avaṉ kaṭaikkup pōy oru puttakam vāṅki vīṭṭukku vantāṉ.* — "He went to the shop, bought a book and came home."

Build it from the "I" past. Where the past has **-இன்-** (**பேசினேன்**, **வாங்கினேன்**), the form ends in **-இ**: **பேசி**, **வாங்கி**. Elsewhere, put **-உ** in place of **-ஏன்**: **வந்தேன்** → **வந்து**, **பார்த்தேன்** → **பார்த்து**, **செய்தேன்** → **செய்து**. A few go their own way, among them **போ** → **போய்** and **சொல்** → **சொல்லி**.

You have said it for a long time: **போய் வருகிறேன்**, "having gone, I come (back)"; **தயவுசெய்து**, "having done (a kindness)".

## You'll want to know: -த பிறகு, after doing
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-GRAMMAR-C285-AFTER-DOING-01]; assesses=[TA-LEX-C48-LEAVE-04, TA-LEX-C71-DAY-02, TA-GRAMMAR-C284-WHEN-PAST-01] -->

> **சாப்பிட்ட பிறகு நான் தூங்கினேன்.**
>
> *cāppiṭṭa piṟaku nāṉ tūṅkiṉēṉ.* — "After eating, I slept."

**சாப்பிட்ட** is the piece you met in **வந்த போது**. Before **பிறகு** it gives "after doing": **வந்த பிறகு**, "after coming". The **-உ** / **-இ** form ties two actions tightly; **-த பிறகு** marks a clear step in time.
