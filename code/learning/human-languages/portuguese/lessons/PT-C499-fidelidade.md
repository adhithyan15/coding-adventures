## You'll want to know: a fidelidade
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C499-GRATIDAO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**a fidelidade** — "faithfulness, fidelity".

> A fidelidade é importante num casamento.
>
> *Faithfulness is important in a marriage.*

*Cartão de fidelidade* — a loyalty card.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*fidelidade* is a learned borrowing from Latin *fidēlitās* "faithfulness" (Lewis & Short), from *fidēlis*.
