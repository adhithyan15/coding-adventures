## You'll want to know: たいりく
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C413-HEIYA413-04]; assesses=[] -->

**たいりく** — *tairiku* — "a continent".

> **ふねで たいりくに わたりました。** — *fune de tairiku ni watarimashita* — "We crossed over to the continent by ship."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**たいりく** (*tairiku*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *tai*, "large" (Middle Chinese *dajH*) and *riku*, "land" (*ljuwk*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
