## Chapters 46-91: 230 headwords, nineteen verbs, and Gujarati attains pre-A1

Gujarati was 225 headwords and 4 verbs short of pre-A1 (HL09 §3.1). This entry
adds forty-six chapters of five words each, and four review lessons: two after
chapter 68 and two after chapter 91. Every word is chained into the next two
lessons, so each atom is revisited twice.

- **46-68**:

    bring, drink; sing, dance, laugh, listen, learn)

  - the pantry, produce, snacks and the table
  - furniture and the house
  - the land, water and weather

- **69-91**:

  - eleven to a thousand
  - the town, travel, a journey, clothes and things you carry

Each headword was checked four ways:

- it is new to the track;
- it uses only signs the script lessons teach (so no ડ yet: ઠંડી, થોડું
  and ખાંડ wait);
- it is covered by the Gujarati script data;
- it does not appear as a whole word in earlier lesson text.
