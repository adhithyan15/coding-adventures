## You'll want to know: अस्पष्ट
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C395-UPAYUKTA395-05]; assesses=[] -->

**अस्पष्ट** — *aspaṣṭa* — "unclear".

> फोनवर तुझा आवाज अस्पष्ट येत आहे.
>
> *phonvar tujhā āvāj aspaṣṭa yet āhe.*
>
> Your voice is coming through unclear on the phone.

अ + स्पष्ट, not clear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अस्पष्ट** (*aspaṣṭa*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *aspaṣṭa*, "not clear, indistinct" (Monier-Williams).
