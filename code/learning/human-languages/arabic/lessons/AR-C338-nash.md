## You'll want to know: نعش
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C338-MAWTA338-05]; assesses=[] -->

**نعش** — *naʿsh* — "a coffin, a bier".

> حمل أربعة رجال النعش.
>
> *ḥamala arbaʿat rijāl an-naʿsh.*
>
> Four men carried the coffin.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**نعش** (*naʿsh*) is built on the native Arabic root *n-ʿ-sh* (Lane), "to raise up". The stars of the Great Bear are *banāt naʿsh*, "the daughters of the bier".
