# మారువేషం (māruvēṣaṁ) — a disguise

## You'll want to know: మారువేషం
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C348-DOPIDI348-05]; assesses=[] -->

**మారువేషం** — *māruvēṣaṁ* — "a disguise".

> దొంగ పాలవాడి మారువేషంలో వచ్చాడు.
>
> *doṅga pālavāḍi māruvēṣaṁlō vaccāḍu.*
>
> The thief came disguised as a milkman.

మారు, other or changed, with వేషం, dress: మారువేషంలో, in disguise.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**మారువేషం** (*māruvēṣaṁ*) is built from *māru*, "other, changed", and Sanskrit *veṣa*, "dress, an assumed appearance" (Monier-Williams).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
