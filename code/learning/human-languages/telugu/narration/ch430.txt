Telugu, chapter 430: Balance and Control.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
బలపరచు (balaparacu) — to strengthen; to back up.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to improve, then the Telugu for to turn into.

You'll want to know: బలపరచు.
బలపరచు — balaparacu — "to strengthen; to back up".
ఆ సాక్ష్యం అతని వాదనను బలపరిచింది. ā sākṣyaṁ atani vādananu balaparicindi. That evidence strengthened his argument.
Past (I …): బలపరిచాను, balaparicānu.
బలం, strength, which you know, + -పరచు.

The word, taken apart.
బలపరచు (balaparacu) is built from Sanskrit bala, "power, strength" (CDIAL 9161), the word in బలం, and -పరచు, a native ending that makes a verb (DEDR 3853 lists paṟacu, "to cause to feel", beside paḍu).

Lesson 4 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to weaken, then the Telugu for balance.

You'll want to know: నియంత్రించు.
నియంత్రించు — niyantriñcu — "to control; to regulate".
ప్రభుత్వం ధరలను నియంత్రించాలి. prabhutvaṁ dharalanu niyantriñcāli. The government should control prices.
Past (I …): నియంత్రించాను, niyantriñcānu.
The noun is నియంత్రణ, control.

The word, taken apart.
నియంత్రించు (niyantriñcu) is built on Sanskrit niyantraṇa, "restraining, checking, governing" (Monier-Williams), with the Telugu verb ending -ఇంచు.
