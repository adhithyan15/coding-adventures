## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**कमाई** (*kamāī*) is the noun of *kamānā*, "to earn", which CDIAL lists under a reconstructed *\*karmāpayati*, "works, earns", built on Sanskrit *karman*, "work" (CDIAL 2897).
