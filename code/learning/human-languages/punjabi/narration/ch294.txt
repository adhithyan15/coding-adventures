Punjabi, chapter 294: Poor Ground.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
ਕੱਲਰ (kallar) — salty barren soil.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for a slope, then the Punjabi for a hillock.

You'll want to know: ਕੱਲਰ.
ਕੱਲਰ — kallar — "salty barren soil".
ਕੱਲਰ ਵਿੱਚ ਕਣਕ ਚੰਗੀ ਨਹੀਂ ਹੁੰਦੀ ਹੈ। kallar vich kaṇak chaṅgī nahīṁ hundī hai. Wheat does not grow well in salty soil.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ਕੱਲਰ (kallar) is inherited from a reconstructed kallara, "salt barren soil"; CDIAL lists Punjabi kallar, "salt barren soil", under it (CDIAL 2954).

ਉਜਾੜ — ujāṛ — "desolate; deserted".
ਰਾਤ ਨੂੰ ਇਹ ਸੜਕ ਉਜਾੜ ਹੁੰਦੀ ਹੈ। rāt nū̃ eh saṛak ujāṛ hundī hai. At night this road is deserted.

Lesson 4 of 6.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for barren, then the Punjabi for desolate.

You'll want to know: ਢੇਲਾ.
ਢੇਲਾ — ḍhelā — "a clod of earth".
ਸਾਡੇ ਖੇਤ ਵਿੱਚ ਵੱਡੇ ਢੇਲੇ ਹਨ। sāḍe khet vich vaḍḍe ḍhele han. There are big clods in our field.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ਢੇਲਾ (ḍhelā) is inherited from a reconstructed ḍala, "lump", in its form ḍhēlla; CDIAL lists Punjabi ḍhelā, "clod", under it (CDIAL 5536).
