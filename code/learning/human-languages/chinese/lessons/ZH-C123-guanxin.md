## You'll want to know: guānxīn
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C123-PEOPLE123-05]; assesses=[] -->

*guānxīn* — "to care about".

> *māma hěn guānxīn wǒ* — Mum really cares about me.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*guānxīn* is built from *guān*, the word for shutting, and *xīn* 'heart', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *kwaen* and *sim* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription). The dictionary glosses the first as the bar that shuts a door; how the pair came to mean caring is not settled.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing things.
