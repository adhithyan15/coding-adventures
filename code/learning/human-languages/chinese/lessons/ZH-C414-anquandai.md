## You'll want to know: ānquándài
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C414-CABIN414-05]; assesses=[] -->

*ānquándài* — "seat belt".

> *měi ge zuòwèi dōu yǒu ānquándài* — Every seat on the plane has a seat belt.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ānquándài* is built from *ānquán* 'safe' and *dài* 'a belt', all inherited from Middle Chinese, *'an*, *dzjwen* and *tajH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the last as a sash worn with clothes.
