## You'll want to know: сеть
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C399-SET-01]; assesses=[] -->

**сеть** (*set*) — "a network; the internet; a net".

> Я нашёл эту информацию в сети.
>
> *Ya nashyól étu informátsiyu v setí.*
>
> I found this information online.

Feminine. *В сети* — online. *Социальная сеть* — a social network. First of all a fishing net.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**сеть** is inherited from Proto-Slavic *\*sětĭ* "a net, a snare" (EtymDB). The internet is a net in the old fisherman’s sense.
