## You'll want to know: 红火
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C334-REDENV334-05]; assesses=[] -->

**红火** — *hónghuǒ* — "prosperous; flourishing".

> **这家饭店很红火** *zhè jiā fàndiàn hěn hónghuǒ* — This restaurant is doing very well.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**红火** (*hónghuǒ*) is built from 红 'red' and 火 'fire', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *huwng* and *xwaX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'a colour' and the second, quoting an older dictionary, 'to blaze'.
