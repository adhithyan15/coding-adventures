## You'll want to know: ಆಣೆ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C366-GHANATE366-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ಆಣೆ** — *āṇe* — "an oath; a vow".

> ನಾನು ಸತ್ಯ ಹೇಳುತ್ತೇನೆ ಅಂತ ಅವನು ಆಣೆ ಮಾಡಿದನು.
>
> *nānu satya hēḷuttēne anta avanu āṇe māḍidanu.*
>
> He swore an oath that he would tell the truth.

ಆಣೆ ಮಾಡು or ಆಣೆ ಇಡು, to swear. ಆಣೆ is also an old coin, a sixteenth of a rupee.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಆಣೆ** (*āṇe*) is usually traced to Sanskrit *ājñā*, "a command" (CDIAL 1095), through a Prakrit form *āṇā*.
