Russian, chapter 456: Currency and warranties.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
обменник (obménnik) — a bureau de change.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to break, then the Russian for to shortchange.

You'll want to know: обменник.
обменник (obménnik) — "a bureau de change".
В аэропорту есть обменник. V aeroportú yest obménnik. There is a bureau de change at the airport.
Colloquial; officially пункт обмена.

The word, taken apart.
обменник (obménnik) is built on обмен 'an exchange' with -ник, from менять 'to change', on мена 'an exchange', which is inherited from Proto-Slavic měna (EtymDB).

Lesson 2 of 5.
валюта (valyúta) — a foreign currency, a currency.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to shortchange, then the Russian for a bureau de change.

You'll want to know: валюта.
валюта (valyúta) — "a foreign currency, a currency".
Мы поменяли валюту в банке. My pomenyáli valyútu v bánke. We changed currency at the bank.
Feminine. In Russia валюта (valyúta) usually means foreign money.

The word, taken apart.
валюта (valyúta) was borrowed from Italian valuta 'currency' (EtymDB), which goes back to Latin valēre 'to be worth'.

Lesson 3 of 5.
гарантия (garántiya) — a warranty, a guarantee.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a bureau de change, then the Russian for a foreign currency.

You'll want to know: гарантия.
гарантия (garántiya) — "a warranty, a guarantee".
На этот телефон гарантия два года. Na étot telefón garántiya dva góda. This phone has a two-year warranty.
Feminine. По гарантии — under warranty.

The word, taken apart.
гарантия (garántiya) is usually traced to French garantie 'a guarantee' (EtymDB lists the French word), from Old French garantir 'to vouch for', a word of Germanic origin.

Lesson 4 of 5.
бракованный (brakóvannyy) — faulty, defective (of goods).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a foreign currency, then the Russian for a warranty.

You'll want to know: бракованный.
бракованный (brakóvannyy) — "faulty, defective (of goods)".
Мы вернули бракованный чайник в магазин. My vernúli brakóvannyy cháynik v magazín. We returned the faulty kettle to the shop.
Built on браковать — to reject as faulty.

The word, taken apart.
бракованный (brakóvannyy) is built on браковать 'to reject as faulty', from брак 'a defect'. That брак is usually traced to Low German brak 'damaged'; it is a different word from брак 'a marriage'. EtymDB lists браковать without a source.

Lesson 5 of 5.
экономить (ekonómit) — to save (money or time), to economise.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a warranty, then the Russian for faulty.

You'll want to know: экономить.
экономить (ekonómit) — "to save (money or time), to economise".
Студенты экономят на всём. Studénty ekonómyat na vsyom. Students save money on everything.
Imperfective; the perfective is сэкономить. Present экономлю, экономишь. Takes на + prepositional.

The word, taken apart.
экономить (ekonómit) is built on экономия 'thrift', which is usually traced to Greek oikonomía 'running a household'. EtymDB lists экономить (ekonómit) and экономия without a source.
