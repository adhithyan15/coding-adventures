## You'll want to know: न्हावी
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C504-KAMGAR504-04]; assesses=[] -->

**न्हावी** — *nhāvī* — "a barber".

> रविवारी न्हाव्याच्या दुकानात मोठी रांग होती.
>
> *ravivārī nhāvyācyā dukānāt moṭhī rāṅg hotī.*
>
> On Sunday there was a long queue at the barber's shop.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**न्हावी** (*nhāvī*) is a tadbhava: CDIAL sets Marathi *nhāvī* beside Sanskrit *nāpita*, "barber", under *\*snāpitṛ*, "one who bathes others" (CDIAL 13792).
