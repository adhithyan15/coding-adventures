## You'll want to know: injuste
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C364-JUSTICE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**injuste** — "unfair, unjust".

> C'est injuste : il a eu la même note que moi sans travailler.
>
> *It is unfair: he got the same mark as me without working.*

One form for both genders.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*injuste* is a learned borrowing from Latin *iniūstus* "unjust" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short), built from *in-* "not" and *iūstus* "right, fair".
