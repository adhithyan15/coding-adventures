Telugu, chapter 339: The Army Marches.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
సైన్యం (sainyaṁ) — an army.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for the past, then the Telugu for in those days.

You'll want to know: సైన్యం.
సైన్యం — sainyaṁ — "an army".
శత్రువు సైన్యం కోట దగ్గరకు వచ్చింది. śatruvu sainyaṁ kōṭa daggaraku vaccindi. The enemy army came near the fort.
Plural: సైన్యాలు, sainyālu.

The word, taken apart.
సైన్యం (sainyaṁ) is borrowed from Sanskrit sainya, "belonging to an army; an army" (CDIAL 13602), built on senā, "an army".

Lesson 2 of 5.
సైనికుడు (sainikuḍu) — a soldier.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for in those days, then the Telugu for an army.

You'll want to know: సైనికుడు.
సైనికుడు — sainikuḍu — "a soldier".
సైనికుడు రాత్రంతా కోటకు కాపలా కాశాడు. sainikuḍu rātrantā kōṭaku kāpalā kāśāḍu. The soldier guarded the fort all night.
Plural: సైనికులు, sainikulu.
The -ఇక ending makes one belonging to something: సైనికుడు (sainikuḍu), a man of the army.

The word, taken apart.
సైనికుడు (sainikuḍu) is borrowed from Sanskrit sainika, "a soldier, a man of the army" (Monier-Williams), with the Telugu ending -uḍu.

Lesson 3 of 5.
సేనాధిపతి (sēnādhipati) — a commander; a general.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for an army, then the Telugu for a soldier.

You'll want to know: సేనాధిపతి.
సేనాధిపతి — sēnādhipati — "a commander; a general".
సేనాధిపతి సైనికులతో మాట్లాడాడు. sēnādhipati sainikulatō māṭlāḍāḍu. The commander spoke with the soldiers.
సేన, an army, and అధిపతి, a lord or chief.

The word, taken apart.
సేనాధిపతి (sēnādhipati) is borrowed from Sanskrit, a compound of senā, "an army", and adhipati, "a lord, master" (CDIAL 255).

Lesson 4 of 5.
కవాతు (kavātu) — a march; a parade.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a soldier, then the Telugu for a commander.

You'll want to know: కవాతు.
కవాతు — kavātu — "a march; a parade".
సైనికులు మైదానంలో కవాతు చేశారు. sainikulu maidānaṁlō kavātu cēśāru. The soldiers marched on the parade ground.
కవాతు (kavātu) చేయు, to march in step.

The word, taken apart.
కవాతు (kavātu) is the Telugu word for a drill march; its origin is not settled.

Lesson 5 of 5.
దండెత్తు (daṇḍettu) — to march against; to invade.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a commander, then the Telugu for a march.

You'll want to know: దండెత్తు.
దండెత్తు — daṇḍettu — "to march against; to invade".
ఆ రాజు పొరుగు రాజ్యం మీద దండెత్తాడు. ā rāju porugu rājyaṁ mīda daṇḍettāḍu. That king marched against the neighbouring kingdom.
Past (I …): దండెత్తాను, daṇḍettānu.
The target takes మీద: X మీద దండెత్తు (daṇḍettu).

The word, taken apart.
దండెత్తు (daṇḍettu) is a native compound: daṇḍu, "an army, a host" (DEDR 3055), and ettu, "to lift, raise; to set out" (DEDR 796): to raise an army against someone.
