## You'll want to know: कद
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C158-ASK158-01]; assesses=[MW-GRAMMAR-C154-PRES-POLITE-01, MW-GRAMMAR-C155-VSTEM-MORE-01] -->

**कद** — *kad* — "when".

> **आप कद आवो हो?**
>
> *āp kad āvo ho?* — When do you come?

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**कद** (*kad*) asks *when*. Monier-Williams gives Sanskrit *kadā*, "when?", but
CDIAL has no entry for **कद**, so how the Marwari word came down is not settled.
**कदी**, "sometimes, ever", opens the same way.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MW-LEX-C158-ASK158-01, MW-LEX-C96-LOC96-01] -->

**कद** sits where the answer will go, as **कठै** does: **वो कद जावै है?** (*vo kad
jāvai hai?*), when does he go?
