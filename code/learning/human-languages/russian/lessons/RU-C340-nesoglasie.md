## You'll want to know: несогласие
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C340-SOGLASIYE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**несогласие** (*nesoglásiye*) — "disagreement".

> Он выразил несогласие с нашим планом.
>
> *On výrazil nesoglásiye s náshim plánom.*
>
> He expressed disagreement with our plan.

Neuter. *Несогласие с* + instrumental.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**несогласие** is *не-* "not" in front of *согласие* "agreement", which is built on Church Slavonic *глас* "voice", Proto-Slavic *\*golsŭ* (EtymDB).
