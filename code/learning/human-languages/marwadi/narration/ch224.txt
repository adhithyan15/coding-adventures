Marwadi (Marwari), chapter 224: Fun at the Fair.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
फिरणो (phirṇo) — to wander about; to roam.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for a flag, then the Marwadi for a show.

You'll want to know: फिरणो.
फिरणो — phirṇo — "to wander about; to roam".
हूं काल बाजार मांय फिरसूं। hū̃ kāl bājār mā̃y phirsū̃. Tomorrow I'll wander round the market.
Past (he …): वो बाजार मांय फिर्यो, vo bājār mā̃y phiryo.

The word, taken apart.
फिरणो (phirṇo) is inherited from a reconstructed phirati, "moves, wanders, turns"; CDIAL lists the Old Marwari verb phiraï, "turns, walks about" (CDIAL 9078). फेर, again, is from the same family.

Lesson 2 of 5.
कठपूतळी (kaṭhpūtḷī) — a puppet.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for a show, then the Marwadi for to wander about.

You'll want to know: कठपूतळी.
कठपूतळी — kaṭhpūtḷī — "a puppet".
कठपूतळी नाचै है। kaṭhpūtḷī nāchai hai. The puppet dances.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
कठपूतळी (kaṭhpūtḷī) is built from kāṭh, "wood" (CDIAL 3120), and pūtḷī, "a doll", from a reconstructed putrala, "a puppet"; CDIAL lists Old Marwari pūtaḷī, "an image" (CDIAL 8269). It is a wooden doll worked on strings.

Lesson 3 of 5.
लाडू (lāḍū) — a laddu (a round sweet).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for to wander about, then the Marwadi for a puppet.

You'll want to know: लाडू.
लाडू — lāḍū — "a laddu (a round sweet)".
म्हैं लाडू खायो। mhaĩ lāḍū khāyo. I ate a laddu.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
लाडू (lāḍū) is inherited from Sanskrit laḍḍu, "a kind of sweetmeat"; CDIAL lists Hindi and Gujarati lāḍū, a ball of sweetened meal. CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry (CDIAL 10926).

Lesson 4 of 5.
उछळणो (uchhaḷṇo) — to leap up; to bounce.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for a puppet, then the Marwadi for a laddu.

You'll want to know: उछळणो.
उछळणो — uchhaḷṇo — "to leap up; to bounce".
छोरो ढोल सुणै है अर उछळै है। chhoro ḍhol suṇai hai ar uchhaḷai hai. The boy hears the drum and leaps up.
Past (he …): वो छोरो उछळ्यो, vo chhoro uchhaḷyo.

The word, taken apart.
उछळणो (uchhaḷṇo) is inherited from a reconstructed ut-śalati, "springs up"; CDIAL lists the Old Marwari verb ūchaḷaï, "flies up, flutters" (CDIAL 1843).

Lesson 5 of 5.
नगाड़ो (nagāṛo) — a kettledrum.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marwadi for a laddu, then the Marwadi for to leap up.

You'll want to know: नगाड़ो.
नगाड़ो — nagāṛo — "a kettledrum".
म्हैं नगाड़ो सुण्यो। mhaĩ nagāṛo suṇyo. I heard the kettledrum.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
नगाड़ो (nagāṛo) is usually traced to Persian naqqāra, "a kettledrum", which Persian took from Arabic; Marwadi says g for its q.
