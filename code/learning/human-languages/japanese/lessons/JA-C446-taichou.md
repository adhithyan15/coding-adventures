## You'll want to know: たいちょう
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C446-CHILL446-05]; assesses=[] -->

**たいちょう** — *taichō* — "physical condition; shape".

> **せんしゅうから たいちょうが よく ありません。** — *senshū kara taichō ga yoku arimasen* — "I have not been in good shape since last week."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**たいちょう** (*taichō*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *tai*, "body" (Middle Chinese *thejX*) and *chō*, "harmonize" (*dew*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
