## You'll want to know: گیزر
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C404-GHUSL404-03]; assesses=[] -->

**گیزر** — *gīzar* — "a water heater".

> سردیوں میں گیزر کا پانی گرم ہوتا ہے۔
>
> *sardiyoṅ meṅ gīzar kā pānī garm hotā hai.*
>
> In winter the water from the heater is hot.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**گیزر** (*gīzar*) is borrowed from English *geyser*, used for a gas or electric water heater.
