## You'll want to know: पावनः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C398-GHATTA398-04]; assesses=[] -->

**पावनः** — *pāvanaḥ* — "purifying; holy".

> नद्याः जलं पावनम् अस्ति।
>
> *nadyāḥ jalaṃ pāvanam asti.*
>
> The river's water is purifying.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पावनः** (*pāvanaḥ*) comes from the root √pū, "to make clean, to purify"; MW gives *pāvana* as purifying, pure, holy (MW).
