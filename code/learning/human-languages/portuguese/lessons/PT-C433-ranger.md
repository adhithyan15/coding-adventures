## You'll want to know: ranger
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C433-BARULHO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ranger** — "to creak; to grind (teeth)".

> A porta do armário range muito.
>
> *The wardrobe door creaks a lot.*

*Ranger os dentes* — to grind your teeth.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ranger* is usually traced to Latin *ringī* 'to snarl, to show the teeth' (Lewis & Short), but the origin is not settled.
