## You'll want to know: palma, palmae
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C337-PALMA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**palma, palmae** — "the palm of victory; the palm of the hand; a palm tree".

> Aurīga palmam et corōnam accēpit.
>
> *The charioteer received the palm and a crown.*

Feminine. The flat of the hand, then the palm tree, whose branch was given to winners.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**palma** is inherited from a Proto-Indo-European form for "the flat of the hand" (EtymDB), matching Greek *palamē*. The palm tree was named from its hand-like leaves. English *palm* comes from it.
