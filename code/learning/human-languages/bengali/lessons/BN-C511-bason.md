## You'll want to know: বাসন
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C511-BASON511-01]; assesses=[] -->

**বাসন** — *bāsôn* — "dishes; pots and pans".

> খাওয়ার পরে আমি বাসন ধুই।
>
> *Khāoyār pôre āmi bāsôn dhui.*
>
> After eating I wash the dishes.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**বাসন** (*bāsôn*) probably goes back to Sanskrit *vāsana*, "a box, a casket"; CDIAL gives no Bengali form there, but lists Prakrit *vāsaṇa*, "a vessel, a plate", and Hindi *bāsan*, "a pot, a dish" (CDIAL 11599).
