## You'll want to know: o crepúsculo
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C275-ENTARDECER-04]; assesses=[] -->

**o crepúsculo** — "the twilight".

> No crepúsculo, o céu fica cor de laranja.
>
> *At twilight the sky turns orange.*

A literary word for the half-light after sunset.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*crepúsculo* is a learned borrowing from Latin *crepusculum* "twilight" (Lewis & Short).
