Lesson 1 of 5.
अहवाल (ahvāl) — a report.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for sweat, then the Marathi for a tear.

You'll want to know: अहवाल.
अहवाल — ahvāl — "a report".

The word, taken apart.
अहवाल (ahvāl) is a loan, usually traced through Persian to Arabic aḥwāl, "states, conditions", the plural of ḥāl, "state"; its root is ḥ-w-l (Lane).

Lesson 3 of 5.
खडू (khaḍū) — chalk.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a report, then the Marathi for a subject.

You'll want to know: खडू.
खडू — khaḍū — "chalk".

The word, taken apart.
खडू (khaḍū) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from khaṭikā, "chalk" (CDIAL 3773). CDIAL glosses the Marathi word "a kind of pipeclay".
