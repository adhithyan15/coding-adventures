## You'll want to know: みかける
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C387-DEMUKAERU387-02]; assesses=[] -->

**みかける** — *mikakeru* — "to happen to see; to catch sight of".

> **くうこうで せんせいを みかけました。** — *kūkō de sensei o mikakemashita* — "I happened to see my teacher at the airport."

Polite **みかけます**, *mikakemasu*; an ichidan verb. Past (polite): **あねが えきで せんせいを みかけました。**, *ane ga eki de sensei o mikakemashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**みかける** (*mikakeru*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji was attached later as a kun'yomi (JMdict). Its first part is *mi*, from *miru*, "to see" (JMdict).
