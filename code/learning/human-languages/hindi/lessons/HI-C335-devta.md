## You'll want to know: देवता
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C335-DEVTA335-02]; assesses=[] -->

**देवता** — *devtā* — "a god; a deity".

> कथा में देवता और राक्षस लड़े।
>
> *kathā meṁ devtā aur rākṣas laṛe.*
>
> In the tale the gods and the demons fought.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**देवता** (*devtā*) is Sanskrit *devatā*, "divinity, a deity" (Monier-Williams). In Hindi it is masculine and means a god.
