French, chapter 327: La recette.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
la recette — the recipe; the takings.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for the resistance, then the French for to commemorate.

You'll want to know: la recette.
la recette — "the recipe; the takings".
Ma grand-mère m'a donné sa recette de tarte. My grandmother gave me her tart recipe.
In a shop, la recette is the day's takings.

The word, taken apart.
recette comes from Old French recete, from Latin recepta "things received" (EtymDB), from recipere "to take back, to receive".

Elle a acheté tous les ingrédients au marché. She bought all the ingredients at the market.

The word, taken apart.
ingrédient is a learned borrowing from Latin ingrediēns "going in" (EtymDB), from ingredī "to enter", built on gradī "to step".

Lesson 4 of 5.
la cuillerée — the spoonful.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the French for the ingredient, then the French for the pinch.

You'll want to know: la cuillerée.
la cuillerée — "the spoonful".
Elle met deux cuillerées de sucre dans son café. She puts two spoonfuls of sugar in her coffee.
Also spelt cuillérée. Une cuillerée à soupe — a tablespoonful.

The word, taken apart.
cuillerée is built on cuiller "spoon" (EtymDB), which is inherited from Latin cochlear "a spoon" (Lewis & Short).

Lesson 5 of 5.

Warm-up.

You'll want to know: préchauffer.
préchauffer — "to preheat".
Il préchauffe le four à 180 degrés. He preheats the oven to 180 degrees.
Préchauffer le four is the first line of many recipes.

The word, taken apart.
préchauffer is built on pré- "before" and chauffer "to heat", which is inherited from Latin calefacere "to make warm" (Lewis & Short).
