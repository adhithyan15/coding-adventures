## You'll want to know: o trompete
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C517-SOPROS-02]; assesses=[] -->

**o trompete** — "trumpet".

> O vizinho toca trompete à noite.
>
> *The neighbour plays the trumpet at night.*

*A trombeta* is an older word for a trumpet.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*trompete* is usually traced to French *trompette*, a diminutive of *trompe* "a horn", which goes back to a Germanic word.
