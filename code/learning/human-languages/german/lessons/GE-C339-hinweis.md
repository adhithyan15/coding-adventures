## You'll want to know: der Hinweis
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C339-HINWEIS-03]; assesses=[] -->

**der Hinweis** — "the hint, the tip".

> Danke für den Hinweis! Ich hatte den Fehler nicht bemerkt.
>
> *Thanks for the tip! I had not noticed the mistake.*

Plural: *die Hinweise*. On a sign, *Hinweis* is a notice.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*Hinweis* is the noun of *hinweisen*, built from *hin* "towards there" and *weisen* "to show, to direct" (EtymDB).
