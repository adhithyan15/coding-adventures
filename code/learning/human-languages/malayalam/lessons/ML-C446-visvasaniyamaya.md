## You'll want to know: വിശ്വസനീയമായ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C446-VYAKTAM446-05]; assesses=[] -->

**വിശ്വസനീയമായ** — *viśvasanīyamāya* — "reliable; credible".

> ആ വാർത്ത വിശ്വസനീയമായ ഒരു ഉറവിടത്തിൽ നിന്നാണ്.
>
> *ā vārtta viśvasanīyamāya oru uṟaviṭattil ninnāṇŭ.*
>
> That news is from a reliable source.

The -നീയ ending means fit to be: വിശ്വസനീയ, fit to be trusted.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**വിശ്വസനീയമായ** (*viśvasanīyamāya*) is built from Sanskrit *viśvasanīya*, "to be trusted, reliable, credible" (Monier-Williams), with the adjective ending -ആയ.
