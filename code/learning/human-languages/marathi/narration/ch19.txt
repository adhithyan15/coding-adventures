Marathi, chapter 19: Six Small Form Labels.
10 lessons. The first lesson already needs your eyes or your hands, so save this one for when you have stopped.


Lesson 1 of 10.
नाव — the first form label.

Before we start: this one needs your hands, so it is not a driving lesson. You will want a pen and something to write on. You can listen to all of it now and come back to the parts that need looking at once you have stopped.

Warm-up.
You already know नाव as “name.” On a form, the same word becomes a request: write the name after it. Today the answer is the familiar practice name मीरा.

Writing — observe and trace one pair.
Trace only नाव: मीरा (nāv: Mīrā). Keep the colon between the printed label and the answer. Then cover the line and point to the part that asks the question.

Wrap-up recall.
Stop after one label and one known answer.
[question — say your answer, then pause 12 seconds]
Which printed Marathi label asks for the person's name?


Lesson 2 of 10.
शहर — the city box.

Before we start: this one needs your hands, so it is not a driving lesson. You will want a pen and something to write on. You can listen to all of it now and come back to the parts that need looking at once you have stopped.

Warm-up.
The known sentence मी पुण्यात राहतो / राहते put the practice person in Pune. A short form asks for that place with शहर — “city.”

Writing — observe and trace one pair.
Trace only शहर: पुणे (śahar: Puṇe). Say “city, Pune” once. Do not add an address; this box asks for one small thing.

Wrap-up recall.
Two labels are enough for one sitting.
[question — say your answer, then pause 12 seconds]
Which label asks for the city?


Lesson 3 of 10.
भाषा — the language box.

Before we start: this one needs your hands, so it is not a driving lesson. You will want a pen and something to write on. You can listen to all of it now and come back to the parts that need looking at once you have stopped.

Warm-up.
You can already say मी मराठी बोलतो / बोलते. A form compresses that whole sentence to a label and a value: भाषा: मराठी (bhāṣā: marāṭhī) — “language: Marathi.”

Writing — observe and trace one pair.
Trace भाषा: मराठी (bhāṣā: marāṭhī) once. Keep भाषा on the question side and मराठी on the answer side.

Wrap-up recall.
Stop before the form grows longer.
[question — say your answer, then pause 12 seconds]
Which label asks which language the person uses?


Lesson 4 of 10.
आवडते पेय — favorite drink.

Before we start: this one needs your hands, so it is not a driving lesson. You will want a pen and something to write on. You can listen to all of it now and come back to the parts that need looking at once you have stopped.

Warm-up.
The known drink is चहा. The label आवडते पेय means “favorite drink.” पेय is neuter, so the describing word takes neuter -ते: आवडते.

Writing — observe and trace one pair.
Trace आवडते पेय: चहा (āvaḍte pey: chahā) once. Box only the ending -ते. The next lesson will change that ending for a feminine label; do not learn both today.

Wrap-up recall.
One agreement ending is enough.
[question — say your answer, then pause 15 seconds]
Complete the printed label for the neuter word pey: आवड__ पेय


Lesson 5 of 10.
आवडती कृती — favorite activity.

Before we start: this one needs your hands, so it is not a driving lesson. You will want a pen and something to write on. You can listen to all of it now and come back to the parts that need looking at once you have stopped.

Warm-up.
Change one thing. कृती, “activity,” is feminine, so “favorite” becomes feminine आवडती. The familiar activity value is वाचणे, “reading.”

Writing — observe and trace one pair.
Trace आवडती कृती: वाचणे (āvaḍtī kṛtī: vācaṇe) once. Underline only -ती. Compare without rewriting: आवडते पेय but आवडती कृती.

Wrap-up recall.
Stop after the one-ending contrast.
[question — say your answer, then pause 15 seconds]
Complete the printed label for feminine kruti: आवड__ कृती


Lesson 6 of 10.
मित्राचे नाव — friend's name.

Before we start: this one needs your hands, so it is not a driving lesson. You will want a pen and something to write on. You can listen to all of it now and come back to the parts that need looking at once you have stopped.

Warm-up.
You know मित्र, friend, and नाव, name. Marathi links them as मित्राचे नाव, “the friend's name.” The -चे form agrees with neuter नाव. The familiar practice answer is अरुण.

Writing — observe and trace one pair.
Trace मित्राचे नाव: अरुण (mitrāce nāv: Aruṇ) once. Circle -चे and stop; no possessive table is needed for this form.

Wrap-up recall.
All six labels now have one first encounter.
[question — say your answer, then pause 16 seconds]
Which label asks for the friend's name?


Lesson 7 of 10.
Copy the first three rows — guided copy of the first three form labels and their already-known values.

Before we start: this one needs your hands, so it is not a driving lesson. You will want a pen and something to write on. You can listen to all of it now and come back to the parts that need looking at once you have stopped.

Warm-up.
Say the three meanings once: name, city, language. Keep the model closed until you can say all three in that order.

Writing — guided copy.
Copy exactly three short rows:
नाव: मीरा (nāv: Mīrā) शहर: पुणे (śahar: Puṇe) भाषा: मराठी (bhāṣā: marāṭhī).
After each row, point left of the colon and say what that label asks. Repair only a missing or misplaced character; do not add a fourth row.

Wrap-up recall.
Close the model before the next lesson.
[question — say your answer, then pause 18 seconds]
Give the three labels in order: name, city, language.

Lesson 9 of 10.
Hide, wait, write the first three labels — delayed writing of the first three form labels from meaning cues, with no visible model.

Before we start: this one needs your hands, so it is not a driving lesson. You will want a pen and something to write on. You can listen to all of it now and come back to the parts that need looking at once you have stopped.

Warm-up.
Close the copy lesson. Say name, city, language once, then start the ten-second delay.

Writing — delayed copy.
Close the copy lesson and wait ten seconds. From the meaning cues name, city, language, write the three Marathi labels. There is no Devanagari model and no romanization in this attempt.
Only after all three are written, reopen the model. Check one row at a time and repair only the first differing character.

Wrap-up recall.
Stop after one delayed set.
[question — say your answer, then pause 35 seconds]
After a delay, write name, city, language as Marathi form labels.


Lesson 10 of 10.
Hide, wait, write the last three labels — delayed writing of the last three form labels, with agreement and possessive repaired separately.

Before we start: this one needs your hands, so it is not a driving lesson. You will want a pen and something to write on. You can listen to all of it now and come back to the parts that need looking at once you have stopped.

Warm-up.
Close the copy lesson. Say favorite drink, favorite activity, friend's name once, then start the ten-second delay.

Writing — delayed copy.

Then compare in two bounded passes: first -ते / -ती, then -चे. If the first pass differs, repair it and stop; leave the second pass for tomorrow.

Wrap-up recall.
The labels are ready; choosing answers belongs to the next runway.
