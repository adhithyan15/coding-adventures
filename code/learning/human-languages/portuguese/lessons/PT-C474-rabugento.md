## You'll want to know: rabugento
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C474-RABUGENTO-01]; assesses=[] -->

**rabugento** — "grumpy".

> De manhã, antes do café, sou rabugento.
>
> *In the morning, before coffee, I am grumpy.*

Informal. *Ser rabugento* is a trait; *estar rabugento* is a mood.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*rabugento* is built on *rabugem* "mange in dogs" (EtymDB), which goes back to Latin *rōbīgō* "rust, blight" (Lewis & Short): a grumpy person is as touchy as a dog with mange.
