## You'll want to know: il lampione
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C448-ARREDO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**il lampione** — "the street lamp".

> Il lampione davanti a casa è spento.
>
> *The street lamp in front of the house is off.*

A big *lampa*, a lamp.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*lampione* is a big *lampa* 'lamp' (EtymDB), which was borrowed from Old French *lampe*, from Latin *lampas* and Greek *lampás* 'torch'.
