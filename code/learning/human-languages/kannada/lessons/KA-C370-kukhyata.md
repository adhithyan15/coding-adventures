# ಕುಖ್ಯಾತ (kukhyāta) — notorious

## You'll want to know: ಕುಖ್ಯಾತ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C370-PATRIKODYAMA370-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ಕುಖ್ಯಾತ** — *kukhyāta* — "notorious".

> ಆ ಕುಖ್ಯಾತ ಕಳ್ಳನನ್ನು ಕೊನೆಗೆ ಪೊಲೀಸರು ಹಿಡಿದರು.
>
> *ā kukhyāta kaḷḷanannu konege polīsaru hiḍidaru.*
>
> The police finally caught that notorious thief.

ಕು-, bad, and ಖ್ಯಾತ, known: well known for bad deeds. ಪ್ರಖ್ಯಾತ is famous.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಕುಖ್ಯಾತ** (*kukhyāta*) is borrowed from Sanskrit: *ku-*, "bad", with *khyāta*, "named, well known, notorious" (Monier-Williams).
