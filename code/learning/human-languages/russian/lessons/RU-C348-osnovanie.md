## You'll want to know: основание
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C348-POVOD-02]; assesses=[] -->

**основание** (*osnovániye*) — "grounds, a basis; a founding".

> У тебя нет оснований так думать.
>
> *U tebyá net osnovániy tak dúmat.*
>
> You have no grounds for thinking so.

Neuter. *На основании* + genitive — on the basis of. It also means the founding of a city: *основание Москвы*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**основание** is built on *основать* "to found", from *основа* "a base, a warp", inherited from Proto-Slavic *\*osnova* (EtymDB).
