# ಉಬ್ಬರ (ubbara) — high tide; a surge

## You'll want to know: ಉಬ್ಬರ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[KA-LEX-C454-TEPPA454-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ಉಬ್ಬರ** — *ubbara* — "high tide; a surge".

> ಸಂಜೆ ಉಬ್ಬರದಲ್ಲಿ ಅಲೆಗಳು ದಡದವರೆಗೆ ಬಂದವು.
>
> *sañje ubbaradalli alegaḷu daḍadavarege bandavu.*
>
> At high tide in the evening the waves came up to the bank.

Its pair is ಇಳಿತ, low tide.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ಉಬ್ಬರ** (*ubbara*) is native Dravidian, built on *ubbu*, "to swell, to rise" (DEDR 666), the family that also names the rising flood tide.
