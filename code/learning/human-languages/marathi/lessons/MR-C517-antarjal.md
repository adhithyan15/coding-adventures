## You'll want to know: आंतरजाल
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C517-JAL517-02]; assesses=[] -->

**आंतरजाल** — *āntarjāl* — "the internet".

> गावात आता आंतरजाल चांगलं चालतं.
>
> *gāvāt ātā āntarjāl cāṅglaṁ cāltaṁ.*
>
> The internet works well in the village now.

Neuter.

In speech, इंटरनेट; आंतरजाल is the Marathi word, a net between.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**आंतरजाल** (*āntarjāl*) is a modern tatsama coinage from Sanskrit *antara*, "between", and *jāla*, "net" (Monier-Williams).
