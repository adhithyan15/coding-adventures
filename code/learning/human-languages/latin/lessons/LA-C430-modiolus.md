## You'll want to know: modiolus, modiolī
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C430-TEMO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**modiolus, modiolī** — "a wheel hub, the nave of a wheel; a small measure".

> In mediā rotā modiolus est.
>
> *In the middle of the wheel is the hub.*

Second declension, masculine: *modiolus, modiolī m.* A little measure: the hub looks like a small barrel.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*modiolus* is a diminutive of *modius* 'a peck measure' (Lewis & Short), which is built on *modus* 'measure' (EtymDB). The round hub of a wheel looked like a small measuring tub.
