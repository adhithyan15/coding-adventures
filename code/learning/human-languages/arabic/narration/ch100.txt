Arabic, chapter 100: Lift, Corridor, Public square, Airport, Port.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
مصعد (miṣʿad) — a lift.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a language, then the Arabic for a story.
[pause 1 second]
[once you have stopped driving — read: ال, then say what it means]
(Al-, "the" — the al- hiding in algebra and alcohol.)

You'll want to know: مصعد.
مصعد — miṣʿad — "a lift".

The word, taken apart.
مصعد (miṣʿad) is built on the native Arabic root ṣ-ʿ-d, "to go up, to ascend" (Lane). Miṣʿad is an instrument noun, "a thing for going up"; the lift is a modern sense.

Lesson 3 of 5.
ميدان (maydān) — a public square, a field.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for a lift, then the Arabic for a corridor.

You'll want to know: ميدان.
ميدان — maydān — "a public square, a field".

The word, taken apart.
ميدان (maydān) is listed by Lane under m-y-d as a race-ground for horses. He quotes two native derivations, from māda, "to move", or from madana, "to dwell"; some modern scholars trace it instead to an Iranian source, so the origin is not settled.
