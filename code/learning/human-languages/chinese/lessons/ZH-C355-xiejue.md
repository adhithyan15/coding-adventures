## You'll want to know: xièjué
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C355-REFUSE355-01]; assesses=[] -->

*xièjué* — "to decline politely".

> *wǒ qǐng tā chī fàn tā xièjué le* — I invited him to dinner, but he politely declined.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*xièjué* is built from 谢 and *jué*, both inherited from Middle Chinese, *zjaeH* and *dzjwet* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to take leave' and the second 'to cut off'.
