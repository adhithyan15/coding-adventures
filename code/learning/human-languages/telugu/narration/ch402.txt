Telugu, chapter 402: Governing.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
శాసనసభ (śāsanasabha) — a legislative assembly.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for national, then the Telugu for politics.

You'll want to know: శాసనసభ.
శాసనసభ — śāsanasabha — "a legislative assembly".
కొత్త చట్టం గురించి శాసనసభలో చర్చ జరిగింది. kotta caṭṭaṁ guriñci śāsanasabhalō carca jarigindi. There was a debate in the assembly about the new law.
శాసన, law-making, + సభ, an assembly. Its members are శాసనసభ్యులు.

The word, taken apart.
శాసనసభ (śāsanasabha) is built from Sanskrit śāsana, "instructing; a command, a royal edict" (CDIAL 12420), and sabhā, "an assembly, a hall" (CDIAL 13167).

Lesson 2 of 5.
మంత్రి (mantri) — a minister.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for politics, then the Telugu for a legislative assembly.

You'll want to know: మంత్రి.
మంత్రి — mantri — "a minister".
విద్యా మంత్రి పాఠశాలను చూడటానికి వచ్చారు. vidyā mantri pāṭhaśālanu cūḍaṭāniki vaccāru. The education minister came to visit the school.
Plural: మంత్రులు, mantrulu.
ముఖ్యమంత్రి, the chief minister of a state.

The word, taken apart.
మంత్రి (mantri) is borrowed from Sanskrit mantrin, "wise, eloquent", and as a noun "a counsellor" (CDIAL 9838), from mantra, "counsel".

Lesson 3 of 5.
అధికారం (adhikāraṁ) — power; authority.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a legislative assembly, then the Telugu for a minister.

You'll want to know: అధికారం.
అధికారం — adhikāraṁ — "power; authority".
ఆ పని చేసే అధికారం అతనికి లేదు. ā pani cēsē adhikāraṁ ataniki lēdu. He has no authority to do that.
అధికారి, an officer, which you know, is the one who holds it.

The word, taken apart.
అధికారం (adhikāraṁ) is borrowed from Sanskrit adhikāra, "authority; government, rule, administration" (Monier-Williams); అధికారి, an officer, is the same word.

Lesson 4 of 5.
పాలించు (pāliñcu) — to govern; to rule.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for a minister, then the Telugu for power.

You'll want to know: పాలించు.
పాలించు — pāliñcu — "to govern; to rule".
ఆ కుటుంబం ఆ ప్రాంతాన్ని చాలా కాలం పాలించింది. ā kuṭumbaṁ ā prāntānni cālā kālaṁ pāliñcindi. That family ruled the region for a long time.
Past (I …): పాలించాను, pāliñcānu.
The noun is పాలన, rule.

The word, taken apart.
పాలించు (pāliñcu) is built on Sanskrit pālana, "protecting, nourishing" (CDIAL 8128), with the Telugu verb ending -ఇంచు; Telugu uses it for ruling and governing.

ఆమోదించు — āmōdiñcu — "to approve; to pass (a bill)".
శాసనసభ కొత్త చట్టాన్ని ఆమోదించింది. śāsanasabha kotta caṭṭānni āmōdiñcindi. The assembly passed the new law.

The word, taken apart.
ఆమోదించు (āmōdiñcu) is built on Sanskrit āmoda, "joy, serenity, pleasure" (Monier-Williams), with the Telugu verb ending -ఇంచు; Telugu uses it for giving approval.
