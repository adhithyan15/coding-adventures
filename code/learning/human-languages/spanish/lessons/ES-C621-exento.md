## You'll want to know: exento
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C621-IMPIDE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**exento** — "exempt, free from".

> Los niños están exentos de pagar.
>
> *Children are exempt from paying.*

*Exento de* + noun or infinitive.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*exento* is a learned borrowing from Latin *exemptus* 'taken out, freed', from *eximere*, built on *emere* 'to take' (Lewis & Short).
