# మెప్పించు (meppiñcu) — to impress; to win approval

## You'll want to know: మెప్పించు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C501-NAVVU501-04]; assesses=[] -->

**మెప్పించు** — *meppiñcu* — "to impress; to win approval".

> ఆ పిల్లవాడు తన పాటతో అందరినీ మెప్పించాడు.
>
> *ā pillavāḍu tana pāṭatō andarinī meppiñcāḍu.*
>
> That boy impressed everyone with his song.

Past (I …): మెప్పించాను, *meppiñcānu*.

To do something so well that others praise you; మెప్పు is the praise you earn.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**మెప్పించు** (*meppiñcu*) is native Dravidian: DEDR 4722 lists Telugu *meppincu*, "to please, to satisfy, to win the approval or admiration of", beside *meccu*, "to praise", and *meppu*, "praise".
