## You'll want to know: el desastre
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C528-DESASTRE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**el desastre** — "the disaster".

> La fiesta fue un desastre.
>
> *The party was a disaster.*

In everyday speech it is anything that goes badly: *¡Qué desastre!*

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*desastre* is usually traced to Occitan *desastre* or Italian *disastro*, where it meant 'bad star', built on *des-* and the word for 'star', from Latin *astrum* (Lewis & Short). A disaster was blamed on the stars.
