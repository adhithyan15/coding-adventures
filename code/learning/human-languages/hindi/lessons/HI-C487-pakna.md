## You'll want to know: पकना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C487-SANKRAMAN487-05]; assesses=[] -->

**पकना** — *paknā* — "to fester; to ripen".

> पुराना घाव पक गया और बहुत दर्द हुआ।
>
> *purānā ghāv pak gayā aur bahut dard huā.*
>
> The old wound festered and hurt a lot.

Past (it …): वह घाव पक गया, *vah ghāv pak gayā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पकना** (*paknā*) is a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Hindi *paknā*, "to be cooked, become ripe, suppurate", under Sanskrit *pakvá*, "cooked, ripe" (CDIAL 7621). Fruit ripens and a wound festers with the same verb; *pakānā*, "to cook", is its causative.
