## You'll want to know: el anillo
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C547-NOVIOS-05]; assesses=[] -->

**el anillo** — "the ring".

> Le regaló un anillo de oro.
>
> *He gave her a gold ring.*

*El anillo de compromiso* — the engagement ring.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*anillo* is inherited from Latin *ānellus* 'a little ring' (EtymDB), a small form of *ānulus* 'ring'.
