# Hear *tīn* — three

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[MW-PERFORMANCE-REQUEST-FOUR-SKILL-01, MW-LEX-PAANI-01, MW-LEX-EK-01, MW-LEX-DO-TWO-01] -->

[PAUSE 18s] Recall the five-word asking payoff, say water, then count as far as
this book has taken you.

## You'll want to know
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-TEEN-01]; assesses=[MW-LEX-PAANI-01, MW-LEX-EK-01, MW-LEX-DO-TWO-01] -->

> *tīn* — **three**

The vowel is long, and it is the same long vowel the ear learned in *pāṇī* in
chapter 4. Hold it. A short one turns the number into something else.

Count aloud: *ek, do, tīn*. Three words in, and two of them arrived with no new
sign for the hand at all.
