## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**புகைப்படம்** (*pukaippaṭam*) is native *pukai*, "smoke" (DEDR 4240), and *paṭam*, "picture", from Sanskrit *paṭa*, "cloth" (CDIAL 7692): "smoke picture". Why "smoke" is not certain.
