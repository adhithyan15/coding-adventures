## You'll want to know: podar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C457-ARVORES-05]; assesses=[] -->

**podar** — "to prune".

> O jardineiro poda as árvores no inverno.
>
> *The gardener prunes the trees in winter.*

*A poda* — the pruning.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*podar* is inherited from Latin *putāre* 'to cut back branches; to reckon' (EtymDB).
