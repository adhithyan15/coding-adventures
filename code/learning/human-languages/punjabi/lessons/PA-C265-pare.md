## You'll want to know: ਪਰੇ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C265-SIDE265-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ਪਰੇ** — *pare* — "farther off; beyond".

> ਸਟੇਸ਼ਨ ਪਿੰਡ ਤੋਂ ਬਹੁਤ ਪਰੇ ਹੈ।
>
> *saṭeshan piṇḍ ton bahut pare hai.*
>
> The station is a long way beyond the village.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਪਰੇ** (*pare*) is inherited from Sanskrit *para*, "distant, further, other"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *pare*, "beyond, further", under it (CDIAL 7793).
