Persian, chapter 310: Confirming and passing on.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
تکذیب کردن (takzib kardan) — to refute, to deny (a report).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to announce, then the Persian for to state.

You'll want to know: تکذیب کردن.
تکذیب کردن (takzib kardan) — "to refute, to deny (a report)".
وزیر این خبر را تکذیب کرد. Vazir in khabar râ takzib kard. The minister denied this report.
For denying a report or a rumour in public. Present takzib mikonad. The opposite is ta'yid kardan.

The word, taken apart.
takzib is usually traced to Arabic takdhīb, "calling something a lie"; EtymDB lists no entry. kardan "to do" is inherited Persian.

Lesson 3 of 5.
پیگیری کردن (peygiri kardan) — to follow up, to pursue.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to refute, then the Persian for to confirm.

You'll want to know: پیگیری کردن.
پیگیری کردن (peygiri kardan) — "to follow up, to pursue".
خبرنگار این خبر را تا آخر پیگیری کرد. Khabarnegâr in khabar râ tâ âkhar peygiri kard. The journalist followed this story through to the end.
pey "footstep, track" + giri "taking": to keep following the trail of something. Present peygiri mikonad.

The word, taken apart.
peygiri joins pey "footstep, track" and giri "taking", from gereftan "to take", both native Persian; kardan "to do" is inherited too. EtymDB lists no entry for the compound.

Lesson 4 of 5.
خبر دادن (khabar dâdan) — to let someone know, to tell the news.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to confirm, then the Persian for to follow up.

You'll want to know: خبر دادن.
خبر دادن (khabar dâdan) — "to let someone know, to tell the news".
دوستم به من خبر داد که برادرش ازدواج کرده است. Dustam be man khabar dâd ke barâdarash ezdevâj karde ast. My friend let me know that his brother had got married.
The person told takes be: be mâdaram khabar dâdam. Present stem dah-.

The word, taken apart.
khabar "news" is borrowed from Arabic khabar (EtymDB). dâdan "to give" is inherited Persian: to give the news.

Lesson 5 of 5.
اطلاع دادن (ettelâ' dâdan) — to inform, to notify.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to follow up, then the Persian for to let someone know.

You'll want to know: اطلاع دادن.
اطلاع دادن (ettelâ' dâdan) — "to inform, to notify".
هتل به ما اطلاع داد که اتاق آماده است. Hotel be mâ ettelâ' dâd ke otâq âmâde ast. The hotel informed us that the room was ready.
More formal than khabar dâdan. Present ettelâ' midahad.

The word, taken apart.
ettelâ' "information" is usually traced to Arabic iṭṭilā'; EtymDB lists the Persian word with no parent. dâdan "to give" is inherited Persian.
