## You'll want to know: praedō, praedōnis
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C303-FURTUM-04]; assesses=[] -->

**praedō, praedōnis** — "a robber, a pirate".

> Praedōnēs mercātōrem cēpērunt et aurum eius retinuērunt.
>
> *Robbers captured a merchant and kept his gold.*

Masculine, 3rd declension. At sea a *praedō* is a pirate.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**praedō** is built on *praeda*, "loot, booty" (EtymDB): one who takes booty.
