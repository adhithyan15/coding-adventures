# HL65 — Kannada A2 connected composition

## Status

Proposed for issue #17658 under the Indian-language C2 programme #13295.

## Problem

Kannada's writing runway reaches a genuine short timed A1 checkpoint, but it
does not yet ask the learner to plan and independently connect a longer text.
The completion planner therefore reports `connected-composition` as Kannada
A2's sole cumulative writing-stage gap.

## Required learner experience

Add one chapter-addressable writing lesson after chapter 307. The lesson must:

- name one reader and one practical purpose;
- ask for a 70–90-word Kannada-script message about a familiar day and a visit;
- provide an eight-box content plan without supplying Kannada sentences;
- require familiar actions, time anchors, sequence, contrast or reason, and a
  direct question;
- show no Kannada model, sentence bank, word bank, romanization, or copyable
  answer;
- make the first connected attempt untimed and prohibit dictionaries, grammar
  references, translators, generative assistants, and spell-checkers;
- introduce no language, script, idiom, sense, or culture knowledge; and
- review the same attempt for task fulfilment, organisation, Kannada language,
  and Kannada-script control.

## Assessment boundary

This is one gentle curriculum attempt, not the complete 30-minute A2 writing
paper. It must not claim A2 attainment, readiness, mock passage, calibration,
standard setting, independent review, or human validation.

## Ownership and verification

The lesson, chapter, curriculum path, extension, membership, book target,
generated book chapter, narration, hashes, modality evidence, test, changelog,
and session-map entry each use their direct shard owner. A focused Kannada
corpus test must pin the model-free task, taught-atom boundary, chapter payoff,
and complete A2 writing-stage ladder. Ordinary authoring must not edit a shared
track aggregate.
