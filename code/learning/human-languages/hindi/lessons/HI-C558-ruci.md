## You'll want to know: रुचि
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C558-KHVAHISH558-03]; assesses=[] -->

**रुचि** — *ruci* — "an interest; a liking (for something)".

> मीरा की संगीत में गहरी रुचि है।
>
> *Mīrā kī saṅgīt meṁ gahrī ruci hai.*
>
> Mira has a deep interest in music.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**रुचि** (*ruci*) is a tatsama, taken whole from Sanskrit *ruci*, "light; liking, taste, relish" (Monier-Williams).
