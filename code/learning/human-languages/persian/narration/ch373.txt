Persian, chapter 373: Rising and falling.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
افزایش (afzâyesh) — an increase, a rise.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to give rise to, then the Persian for a reaction.

You'll want to know: افزایش.
افزایش (afzâyesh) — "an increase, a rise".
افزایش قیمت نان همه را نگران کرد. afzâyesh-e qeymat-e nân hame râ negarân kard. The rise in the price of bread worried everyone.
From afzâ-, the present stem of afzudan "to add, to increase". The opposite is kâhesh.

The word, taken apart.
افزایش (afzâyesh) is native Persian, built on afzâ-, the present stem of afzudan "to add, to increase", with the noun suffix -yesh.

Lesson 2 of 5.
افزایش یافتن (afzâyesh yâftan) — to increase, to go up.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a reaction, then the Persian for an increase.

You'll want to know: افزایش یافتن.
افزایش یافتن (afzâyesh yâftan) — "to increase, to go up".
تعداد دانشجوها هر سال افزایش مییابد. te'dâd-e dâneshjuhâ har sâl afzâyesh miyâbad. The number of students goes up every year.
Formal; yâftan "to find" has the present stem yâb-. In speech, ziyâd shodan.

The word, taken apart.
افزایش یافتن (afzâyesh yâftan) is native Persian, built from afzâyesh "increase" with yâftan "to find", which in formal Persian turns a noun of change into a verb.

You'll want to know: کاهش.
کاهش (kâhesh) — "a decrease, a reduction".
کاهش باران برای کشاورزها مشکل بزرگی است. kâhesh-e bârân barâye keshâvarzhâ moshkel-e bozorgi ast. Less rain is a big problem for farmers.

کاهش (kâhesh) is native Persian, built on kâh-, the present stem of kâstan "to lessen, to reduce", with the noun suffix -esh.

Lesson 4 of 5.
کاهش دادن (kâhesh dâdan) — to reduce, to cut.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to increase, then the Persian for a decrease.

You'll want to know: کاهش دادن.
کاهش دادن (kâhesh dâdan) — "to reduce, to cut".
شرکت ساعت کار را کاهش داد. sherkat sâ'at-e kâr râ kâhesh dâd. The company cut working hours.
Kâhesh + dâdan "to give" (present stem deh-). The object takes râ.

The word, taken apart.
کاهش دادن (kâhesh dâdan) is native Persian, built from kâhesh "decrease" with dâdan "to give", which EtymDB traces to PIE deh₃- "to give".

Lesson 5 of 5.
رشد کردن (roshd kardan) — to grow.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a decrease, then the Persian for to reduce.

You'll want to know: رشد کردن.
رشد کردن (roshd kardan) — "to grow".
این شهر در ده سال گذشته خیلی رشد کرده است. in shahr dar dah sâl-e gozashte kheyli roshd karde ast. This city has grown a lot in the last ten years.
Roshd "growth" + kardan. For people, plants, cities and economies.

The word, taken apart.
رشد was borrowed from Arabic rushd (EtymDB), a word usually glossed as maturity or right guidance; in Persian it means growth.
