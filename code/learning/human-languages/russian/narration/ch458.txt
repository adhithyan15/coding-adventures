Russian, chapter 458: Mountains and valleys.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
равнина (ravnína) — a plain (flat land).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to deliver, then the Russian for a courier.

You'll want to know: равнина.
равнина (ravnína) — "a plain (flat land)".
За рекой начинается широкая равнина. Za rekóy nachináyetsya shirókaya ravnína. Beyond the river a wide plain begins.
Feminine. Равнина has the Church Slavonic ра- where native ровный has ро-.

The word, taken apart.
равнина (ravnína) is built on равный, the Church Slavonic shape of native ровный 'level, even', with -ина. EtymDB takes ровный from Proto-Slavic orvĭnŭ.

Lesson 2 of 6.
долина (dolína) — a valley.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a courier, then the Russian for a plain.

You'll want to know: долина.
долина (dolína) — "a valley".
В долине течёт маленькая река. V dolíne techyót málenkaya reká. A small river flows through the valley.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
долина (dolína) is built on the old word дол 'a valley', still heard in по горам, по долам 'over hill and dale'. Дол is inherited from Proto-Slavic dolŭ 'a dale, a valley' (EtymDB); EtymDB lists долина (dolína) itself without a source.

Зимой на этом склоне катаются на лыжах. Zimóy na étom sklóne katáyutsya na lýzhakh. In winter people ski on this slope.
Masculine. На склоне лет — in old age.

The word, taken apart.
склон (sklon) is built on склонить 'to bend, to incline', from клонить 'to lean'. EtymDB lists склон (sklon) without a deeper source.

Lesson 5 of 6.
ущелье (ushchélye) — a gorge, a ravine.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a slope, then the Russian for a summit.

You'll want to know: ущелье.
ущелье (ushchélye) — "a gorge, a ravine".
Дорога идёт по узкому ущелью. Doróga idyót po úzkomu ushchélyu. The road runs through a narrow gorge.
Neuter.

The word, taken apart.
ущелье (ushchélye) is usually traced to щель 'a crack, a gap', with у- and -ье: a deep crack between mountains. EtymDB does not list it, so the line is hedged.
