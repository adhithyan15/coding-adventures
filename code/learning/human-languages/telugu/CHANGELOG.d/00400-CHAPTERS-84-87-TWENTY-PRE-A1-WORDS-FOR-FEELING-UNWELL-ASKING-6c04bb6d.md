Reinforcement is built into the tranche rather than paid for later: each word
lesson practises the two atoms before it, so every new atom has two later
revisits, and the two review lessons in chapter 87 give the last words theirs.
Every word was checked against the track first: none was taught or printed in
an earlier lesson, and every glyph is one the track already teaches.
