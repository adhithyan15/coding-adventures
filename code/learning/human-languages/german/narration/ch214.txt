German, chapter 214: Cheerful, Terrible, Patient, Stingy, Cosy.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
fröhlich — cheerful.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for fantastic, then the German for damp.

You'll want to know: fröhlich.
fröhlich — "cheerful".

The word, taken apart.
fröhlich is the word for "glad", Middle High German vrō from Old High German frō (EtymDB 2.1), + -lich: merry. English frolic came from the Dutch cousin.

The word, taken apart.
geizig is the word for "avarice" + -ig. It goes back to Middle High German gītesen, "to be greedy" (EtymDB 2.1), and EtymDB lists Old English ġītsian, "to covet", beside it.
