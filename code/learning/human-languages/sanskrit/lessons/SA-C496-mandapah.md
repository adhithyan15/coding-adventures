## You'll want to know: मण्डपः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C496-VIVAHA496-04]; assesses=[] -->

**मण्डपः** — *maṇḍapaḥ* — "a pavilion".

> वधूः मण्डपं प्रविशति।
>
> *vadhūḥ maṇḍapaṃ praviśati.*
>
> The bride enters the pavilion.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**मण्डपः** (*maṇḍapaḥ*) MW divides it *maṇḍa-pa* but gives no further derivation, so its origin is uncertain; it gives "an open hall or temporary shed erected on festive occasions, a pavilion" (MW).
