## You'll want to know: 生病
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C240-SICK240-01]; assesses=[] -->

**生病** — *shēngbìng* — "to get sick; to fall ill".

> **我生病了头很疼不想吃东西** *wǒ shēngbìng le tóu hěn téng bù xiǎng chī dōngxi* — I'm ill: my head really hurts and I don't want to eat.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**生病** (*shēngbìng*) is built from 生 'to grow' and 病 'illness', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *srjaeng* and *bjaengH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'worry' and 'suffering' and explains it, after an older book, as an illness grown worse.
