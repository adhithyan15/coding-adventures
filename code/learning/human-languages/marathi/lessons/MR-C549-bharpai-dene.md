# भरपाई देणे (bharpāī deṇe) — to compensate

## You'll want to know: भरपाई देणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C549-SANRAKSHAN549-01]; assesses=[] -->

**भरपाई देणे** — *bharpāī deṇe* — "to compensate".

> विमा कंपनीने पुरात बुडालेल्या गाडीची भरपाई दिली.
>
> *vimā kampanīne purāt buḍālelyā gāḍīcī bharpāī dilī.*
>
> The insurance company paid for the car that sank in the flood.

Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने भरपाई दिली, *tyāne bharpāī dilī*.

What is lost takes -ची: गाडीची भरपाई देणे.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**भरपाई देणे** (*bharpāī deṇe*) is भरपाई, "making good", built on भरणे, "to fill", inherited from Sanskrit *bharati* (CDIAL 9397), with देणे.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
