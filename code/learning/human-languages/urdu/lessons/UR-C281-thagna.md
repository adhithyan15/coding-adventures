## You'll want to know: ٹھگنا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C281-THAG281-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ٹھگنا** — *ṭhagnā* — "to cheat; to swindle".

> اس نے مجھے ٹھگا۔
>
> *us ne mujhe ṭhagā.*
>
> He cheated me.

Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے دکاندار کو ٹھگا, *us ne dukāndār ko ṭhagā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ٹھگنا** (*ṭhagnā*) is inherited from *\*ṭhagg*, a form CDIAL reconstructs (CDIAL 5489), which also lists the noun *ṭhag*, a swindler. English *thug* was taken from that noun.
