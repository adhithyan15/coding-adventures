Marathi, chapter 296: A Crash on the Road.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
धडकणे (dhaḍakṇe) — to collide; to crash.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a crisis, then the Marathi for dreadful.

You'll want to know: धडकणे.
धडकणे — dhaḍakṇe — "to collide; to crash".
भरधाव गाडी झाडावर धडकली. bhardhāv gāḍī jhāḍāvar dhaḍaklī. The speeding car crashed into a tree.
Past (he …): तो धडकला, to dhaḍaklā.
What is hit takes -वर or -ला: भिंतीवर धडकणे (dhaḍakṇe), to crash into a wall.

The word, taken apart.
धडकणे (dhaḍakṇe) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi dhaḍakṇe, 'to throb', under dhaḍ, 'throb, beat' (CDIAL 6711); the sense of a crash grows from the thud.

सायकल घसरली आणि तो खांबावर आदळला. sāykal ghasarlī āṇi to khāmbāvar ādaḷlā. The bicycle skidded and he slammed into a pole.

Lesson 3 of 5.
बेशुद्ध (beśuddha) — unconscious.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to collide, then the Marathi for to slam into.

You'll want to know: बेशुद्ध.
बेशुद्ध — beśuddha — "unconscious".
अपघातानंतर तो काही मिनिटं बेशुद्ध होता. apghātānantar to kāhī miniṭaṁ beśuddha hotā. After the accident he was unconscious for a few minutes.
बेशुद्ध (beśuddha) होणे, to faint; शुद्धीवर येणे, to come round.

The word, taken apart.
बेशुद्ध (beśuddha) is built from the Persian prefix be-, 'without', and Sanskrit śuddha, 'clear, pure' (Monier-Williams), here in the sense of a clear mind.

Lesson 4 of 5.
सावरणे (sāvarṇe) — to recover; to steady oneself.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to slam into, then the Marathi for unconscious.

You'll want to know: सावरणे.
सावरणे — sāvarṇe — "to recover; to steady oneself".
थोड्या वेळाने तो त्या भीतीतून सावरला. thoḍyā veḷāne to tyā bhītītūn sāvarlā. After a while he recovered from that fright.
Past (he …): तो सावरला, to sāvarlā.
The trouble takes -तून: भीतीतून सावरणे (sāvarṇe). Transitive too: स्वतःला सावरणे (sāvarṇe), to steady oneself.

The word, taken apart.
सावरणे (sāvarṇe) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi sāvarṇe, 'to gather up, recover, rectify', under saṁvarati, 'restrains' (CDIAL 13015).

Lesson 5 of 5.
हुकणे (hukṇe) — to be missed; to miss a chance.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for unconscious, then the Marathi for to recover.

You'll want to know: हुकणे.
हुकणे — hukṇe — "to be missed; to miss a chance".
मला उशीर झाला आणि माझी बस हुकली. malā uśīr jhālā āṇi mājhī bas huklī. I was late and missed my bus.
Past (he …): तो हुकला, to huklā.
As with चुकणे, the thing missed is the subject and agrees: बस हुकली, संधी हुकली, the chance was missed.

The word, taken apart.
हुकणे (hukṇe) is a Marathi verb for a chance, a bus or an aim that slips by; its origin is not settled.
