## You'll want to know: le partisan
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C345-PARTISAN-03]; assesses=[] -->

**le partisan** — "the supporter (of an idea)".

> Les partisans du projet sont contents.
>
> *The supporters of the project are happy.*

*Être partisan de* — to be in favour of. Feminine *partisane*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*partisan* was borrowed from Italian *partigiano* "a supporter of a side" (EtymDB), built on *parte* "part, side", from Latin *pars, partis* (Lewis & Short).
