# గర్వపడు (garvapaḍu) — to be proud

## You'll want to know: గర్వపడు
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C498-SIGGU498-03]; assesses=[] -->

**గర్వపడు** — *garvapaḍu* — "to be proud".

> కూతురి విజయం చూసి తల్లిదండ్రులు గర్వపడ్డారు.
>
> *kūturi vijayaṁ cūsi tallidaṇḍrulu garvapaḍḍāru.*
>
> Seeing their daughter succeed, the parents were proud.

Past (I …): గర్వపడ్డాను, *garvapaḍḍānu*.

The noun గర్వం can also mean arrogance; the verb is the good kind of pride.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**గర్వపడు** (*garvapaḍu*) is built from గర్వం, "pride", from Sanskrit *garva*, "pride, arrogance" (CDIAL 4064) and the native verb *paḍu*, "to feel, to suffer" (DEDR 3853).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
