## You'll want to know: ஒடி
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TA-LEX-C443-VIRISAL443-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ஒடி** — *oṭi* — "to break off".

> புயலில் மரக்கிளை ஒடிந்தது.
>
> *puyalil marakkiḷai oṭintatu.*
>
> A branch broke off in the storm.

Past (it …): ஒடிந்தது, *oṭintatu*.

ஒடிந்தது is it broke off; ஒடித்தேன், I broke it off.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ஒடி** (*oṭi*) is native Dravidian (DEDR 946), "to break, to break off, to snap".
