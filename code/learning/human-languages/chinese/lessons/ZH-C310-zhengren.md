## You'll want to know: zhèngrén
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C310-SUSPECT310-02]; assesses=[] -->

*zhèngrén* — "witness".

> *tā shì zhège shìgù de zhèngrén* — He's a witness to the accident.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*zhèngrén* is built from *zhèng* 'proof' and 人 'a person', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *tsyingH* and *nyin* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to verify'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
