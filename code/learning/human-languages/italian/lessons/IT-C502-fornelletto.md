## You'll want to know: il fornelletto
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C502-CAMPEGGIO-03]; assesses=[] -->

**il fornelletto** — "camping stove".

> Abbiamo cucinato la pasta sul fornelletto.
>
> *We cooked the pasta on the camping stove.*

A little *fornello* "burner".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*fornelletto* is a little form of *fornello* "burner", built on *forno* "oven", inherited from Latin *furnus* "an oven" (Lewis & Short).
