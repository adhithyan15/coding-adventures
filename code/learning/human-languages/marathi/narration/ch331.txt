Marathi, chapter 331: The Siege.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
वेढा (veḍhā) — a siege.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to stand guard, then the Marathi for to battle.

You'll want to know: वेढा.
वेढा — veḍhā — "a siege".
शत्रूचा वेढा सहा महिने चालला. śatrūcā veḍhā sahā mahine cāllā. The enemy's siege lasted six months.
Masculine.
वेढा (veḍhā) घालणे, to lay siege; वेढा (veḍhā) उठवणे, to lift it.

The word, taken apart.
वेढा (veḍhā) is inherited, of the family of Sanskrit veṣṭa, 'a band, an enclosure': CDIAL lists Marathi veḍh, 'a ring, a circumference', under it (CDIAL 12130), and veḍhṇẽ, 'to twist, surround', under veṣṭayati, 'surrounds' (CDIAL 12132).

Lesson 2 of 5.
घेरणे (gherṇe) — to surround.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to battle, then the Marathi for a siege.

You'll want to know: घेरणे.
घेरणे — gherṇe — "to surround".
सैन्याने किल्ल्याला चारी बाजूंनी घेरलं. sainyāne killyālā cārī bājūnnī gherlaṁ. The army surrounded the fort on all sides.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने घेरलं, tyāne gherlaṁ.
What is surrounded takes -ला: किल्ल्याला घेरणे (gherṇe).

The word, taken apart.
घेरणे (gherṇe) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi gherṇe, 'to surround', under ghir, 'go round' (CDIAL 4474).

Lesson 3 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for a siege, then the Marathi for to surround.

You'll want to know: खंदक.
खंदक — khandak — "a moat; a trench".
किल्ल्याभोवती पाण्याने भरलेला खंदक होता. killyābhovtī pāṇyāne bharlelā khandak hotā. There was a moat full of water round the fort.
Masculine.
खंदक (khandak) खणणे, to dig a trench.

The word, taken apart.
खंदक (khandak) is a loan from Persian xandaq, usually traced to Arabic khandaq, 'a trench, a moat'.

Lesson 4 of 5.
तटबंदी (taṭbandī) — a rampart; fortifications.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to surround, then the Marathi for a moat.

You'll want to know: तटबंदी.
तटबंदी — taṭbandī — "a rampart; fortifications".
किल्ल्याची तटबंदी अजून मजबूत आहे. killyācī taṭbandī ajūn majbūt āhe. The fort's ramparts are still strong.
Feminine.
तट, a wall, + बंदी, closing.

The word, taken apart.
तटबंदी (taṭbandī) is built from taṭ, 'a wall', from Sanskrit taṭa, 'a slope, a bank' (Monier-Williams), and Persian bandī, 'a closing, a fastening'.
