## You'll want to know: la pesca

**la pesca** — "fishing".

> La pesca es su gran afición.
>
> *Fishing is his great hobby.*

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*pesca* is built on *pescar* (EtymDB), which is inherited from Latin *piscārī* "to fish".
