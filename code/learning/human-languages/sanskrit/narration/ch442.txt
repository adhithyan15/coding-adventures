Sanskrit, chapter 442: Sunrise.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
सूर्योदयः (sūryodayaḥ) — sunrise.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for hoarfrost, then the Sanskrit for veils.

You'll want to know: सूर्योदयः.
सूर्योदयः — sūryodayaḥ — "sunrise".
वयं सूर्योदयात् पूर्वं प्रतिष्ठामहे। vayaṃ sūryodayāt pūrvaṃ pratiṣṭhāmahe. We set out before sunrise.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
सूर्योदयः (sūryodayaḥ) is built from sūrya, "the sun", and udaya, "rising", from ud and the root √i, "to go"; MW gives the compound as sunrise (MW).

Lesson 2 of 5.
उदेति (udeti) — rises in the sky.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for veils, then the Sanskrit for sunrise.

You'll want to know: उदेति.
उदेति — udeti — "rises in the sky".
प्रातः सूर्यः उदेति। prātaḥ sūryaḥ udeti. In the morning the sun rises.
Past (it …): सूर्यः उदितवान्, sūryaḥ uditavān.

The word, taken apart.
उदेति (udeti) is built from ud, "up", and the root √i, "to go", which also give udaya, "rising"; MW gives ud-i as to go up and to rise, as the sun does, with the participle udita, "risen" (MW).

Lesson 3 of 5.
प्रकाशते (prakāśate) — grows bright; shines out.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for sunrise, then the Sanskrit for rises in the sky.

You'll want to know: प्रकाशते.
प्रकाशते — prakāśate — "grows bright; shines out".
सूर्योदये आकाशः प्रकाशते। sūryodaye ākāśaḥ prakāśate. At sunrise the sky grows bright.
Past (it …): आकाशः प्रकाशितवान्, ākāśaḥ prakāśitavān.

The word, taken apart.
प्रकाशते (prakāśate) is built from pra, "forth", and the root √kāś, "to shine", which also gives prakāśa, "light"; MW gives pra-kāś as to become visible, to shine, with the participle prakāśita (MW).

Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for rises in the sky, then the Sanskrit for grows bright.

You'll want to know: क्षितिजम्.
क्षितिजम् — kṣitijam — "the horizon".
प्रातः क्षितिजम् अरुणं भवति। prātaḥ kṣitijam aruṇaṃ bhavati. In the morning the horizon turns red.
A neuter noun.

The word, taken apart.
क्षितिजम् (kṣitijam) is built from kṣiti, "the earth", and ja, "born": MW gives kṣitija as earth-born and, as a neuter, the horizon (MW).

Lesson 5 of 5.
विभाति (vibhāti) — dawns; shines forth.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for grows bright, then the Sanskrit for the horizon.

You'll want to know: विभाति.
विभाति — vibhāti — "dawns; shines forth".
शनैः रात्रिः विभाति। śanaiḥ rātriḥ vibhāti. Slowly the night turns to dawn.
Past (it …): रात्रिः विभातवती, rātriḥ vibhātavatī.

The word, taken apart.
विभाति (vibhāti) is built from vi and the root √bhā, "to shine"; MW gives vi-bhā as to shine forth, and its participle vibhāta for a night that has grown light, the morning that has dawned (MW).
