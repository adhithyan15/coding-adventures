## You'll want to know: chéngqiáng
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C446-OLDTOWN446-04]; assesses=[] -->

*chéngqiáng* — "city wall; the wall round a city".

> *qù chéngqiáng zěnme zǒu* — How do I get to the city wall?

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*chéngqiáng* is built from 城 'a walled town' and *qiáng* 'a wall', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *dzyeng* and *dzjang* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for getting around.
