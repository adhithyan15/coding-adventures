## You'll want to know: सौंफ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C185-MASALO185-05]; assesses=[] -->

**सौंफ** — *saumph* — "fennel seed".

> सौंफ मीठी है।
>
> *saumph mīṭhī hai.*
>
> Fennel seed is sweet.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**सौंफ** (*saumph*) is inherited from Sanskrit *śatapuṣpa*, "having a hundred flowers", a name for the plant; CDIAL lists Hindi *sauph* (CDIAL 12283). CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry.
