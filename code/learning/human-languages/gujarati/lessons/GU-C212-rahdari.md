## You'll want to know: રાહદારી
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C212-REST212-03]; assesses=[] -->

**રાહદારી** — *rāhdārī* — "pedestrian, passer-by".

> **એક રાહદારીએ મને સરનામું કહ્યું.** — *ek rāhdārīe mane sarnāmũ kahyũ* — "A passer-by told me the address."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**રાહદારી** (*rāhdārī*) is built from *rāh*, "road, way", usually traced to Persian *rāh*, "road", the Persian suffix *-dār*, "holder", and the ending *-ī*: one who keeps to the road.
