Lesson 1 of 6.
योगायोग (yogāyog) — a coincidence.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for protection, then the Marathi for reliance.

You'll want to know: योगायोग.
योगायोग — yogāyog — "a coincidence".
बाजारात जुना मित्र भेटला, हा मोठा योगायोग होता. bājārāt junā mitra bheṭlā, hā moṭhā yogāyog hotā. Meeting an old friend in the market was a great coincidence.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
योगायोग (yogāyog) is a tatsama, Sanskrit yogāyoga, yoga, "joining", with ayoga, its opposite, glossed as fitness and unfitness (Monier-Williams). Marathi uses it for things that happen to meet.

Before the new one: say the Marathi for reliance, then the Marathi for a coincidence.

You'll want to know: जुगार.
जुगार — jugār — "gambling".
जुगारात त्याने सगळे पैसे घालवले. jugārāt tyāne sagḷe paise ghālavle. He lost all his money gambling.
Masculine.
जुगार (jugār) खेळणे, to gamble.

The word, taken apart.
जुगार (jugār) is usually linked with Sanskrit dyūtakāra, "gambler", under which CDIAL lists Prakrit juāra and Hindi juwārī (CDIAL 6609). CDIAL gives no Marathi form, so the path is not settled.

Lesson 4 of 6.
नाणेफेक (nāṇephek) — a coin toss.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for gambling, then the Marathi for good fortune.

You'll want to know: नाणेफेक.
नाणेफेक — nāṇephek — "a coin toss".
सामन्याआधी कर्णधारांनी नाणेफेक केली. sāmnyāādhī karṇadhārānnī nāṇephek kelī. Before the match the captains tossed a coin.
Feminine.
नाणेफेक (nāṇephek) करणे, to toss a coin.

The word, taken apart.
नाणेफेक (nāṇephek) is a Marathi compound built from नाणे, "a coin", inherited from Sanskrit nāṇaka, "coin" (CDIAL 7050), and फेक, "a throw", from फेकणे.

Lesson 5 of 6.
पैज लावणे (paij lāvṇe) — to bet.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for good fortune, then the Marathi for a coin toss.

You'll want to know: पैज लावणे.
पैज लावणे — paij lāvṇe — "to bet".
कोण जिंकेल यावर दोन मित्रांनी पैज लावली. koṇ jiṅkel yāvar don mitrānnī paij lāvlī. Two friends bet on who would win.
Past, with -ने (he …): त्याने पैज लावली, tyāne paij lāvlī.
The question takes -वर: यावर पैज लावणे (paij lāvṇe).

The word, taken apart.
पैज लावणे (paij lāvṇe) is पैज, a tadbhava: CDIAL lists Marathi paij, "bet, promise", under Sanskrit pratijñā, "promise" (CDIAL 8563), with लावणे.
