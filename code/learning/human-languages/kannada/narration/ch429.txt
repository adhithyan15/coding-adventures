Kannada, chapter 429: Hopes and Proposals.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಆಶಿಸು (āśisu) — to hope for.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for applicability, then the Kannada for overall.

You'll want to know: ಆಶಿಸು.
ಆಶಿಸು — āśisu — "to hope for".
ಮಗ ಪರೀಕ್ಷೆಯಲ್ಲಿ ಪಾಸಾಗುತ್ತಾನೆ ಅಂತ ತಾಯಿ ಆಶಿಸಿದಳು. maga parīkṣeyalli pāsāguttāne anta tāyi āśisidaḷu. The mother hoped her son would pass the exam.
Past (I …): ಆಶಿಸಿದೆ, āśiside.
What is hoped comes before ಅಂತ or ಎಂದು.

The word, taken apart.
ಆಶಿಸು (āśisu) is built on Sanskrit āśā, "wish, desire, hope" (Monier-Williams), with the Kannada verb ending -isu.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ಪರಿಹಾರ (parihāra) — a solution; compensation.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to propose, then the Kannada for to suggest.

You'll want to know: ಪರಿಹಾರ.
ಪರಿಹಾರ — parihāra — "a solution; compensation".
ಈ ಸಮಸ್ಯೆಗೆ ಸುಲಭ ಪರಿಹಾರ ಇಲ್ಲ. ī samasyege sulabha parihāra illa. There is no easy solution to this problem.
Also compensation: ಬೆಳೆ ನಷ್ಟಕ್ಕೆ ಪರಿಹಾರ (parihāra).

The word, taken apart.
ಪರಿಹಾರ (parihāra) is borrowed from Sanskrit parihāra, "leading round, avoiding; a remedy" (CDIAL 7901).
