## You'll want to know: illecito
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C390-LEGALE-04]; assesses=[] -->

**illecito** — "unlawful, illicit".

> La polizia ha scoperto un traffico illecito di animali.
>
> *The police uncovered an illicit trade in animals.*

As a noun, *un illecito* is an offence: *un illecito amministrativo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*illecito* comes from Latin *illicitus* "not allowed, unlawful" (Lewis & Short), built from *in-* "not" and *licitus*, on *licēre* "to be allowed".
