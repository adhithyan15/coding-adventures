## You'll want to know: लोटणे
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C305-GATVARSH305-05]; assesses=[] -->

**लोटणे** — *loṭṇe* — "to elapse; to push".

> त्या घटनेला आता दहा वर्षं लोटली.
>
> *tyā ghaṭnelā ātā dahā varṣaṁ loṭlī.*
>
> Ten years have now passed since that event.

Past (he …): तो लोटला, *to loṭlā*.

Of time, with -ला: घटनेला वर्षं लोटली, years have passed since the event. Also to push: दार लोटणे.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**लोटणे** (*loṭṇe*) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi *loṭṇẽ*, 'to roll, wallow', under *\*lōrtati*, 'rolls' (CDIAL 11156). The senses 'to push' and, of time, 'to pass' are most likely the same verb: time rolls on.
