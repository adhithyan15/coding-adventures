## You'll want to know: છૂટ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C187-PRICE187-05]; assesses=[] -->

**છૂટ** — *chūṭ* — "discount; freedom".

> **આ દુકાનમાં દસ ટકા છૂટ છે.** — *ā dukānmā̃ das ṭakā chūṭ che* — "This shop gives ten per cent off."

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**છૂટ** (*chūṭ*) is a tadbhava from the family that CDIAL lists under *\*kṣuṭyatē*, "is released", a reconstructed form; its Gujarati relatives there are *chuṭvũ*, "to get loose", and *chuṭũ*, "loosened" (CDIAL 3707).
