Punjabi, chapter 298: Thorns and Bushes.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ਕੰਡਾ (kaṇḍā) — a thorn.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for blunt, then the Punjabi for to prick.

You'll want to know: ਕੰਡਾ.
ਕੰਡਾ — kaṇḍā — "a thorn".
ਮੇਰੇ ਪੈਰ ਵਿੱਚ ਕੰਡਾ ਹੈ। mere pair vich kaṇḍā hai. There is a thorn in my foot.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ਕੰਡਾ (kaṇḍā) is inherited from Sanskrit kaṇṭa, "thorn"; CDIAL lists Punjabi kaṇḍā under it (CDIAL 2668).

Lesson 3 of 5.
ਕਿੱਕਰ (kikkar) — an acacia tree.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for a thorn, then the Punjabi for a bush.

You'll want to know: ਕਿੱਕਰ.
ਕਿੱਕਰ — kikkar — "an acacia tree".
ਕਿੱਕਰ ਦੇ ਕੰਡੇ ਤਿੱਖੇ ਹੁੰਦੇ ਹਨ। kikkar de kaṇḍe tikkhe hunde han. The thorns of the acacia are sharp.
A feminine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ਕਿੱਕਰ (kikkar) is inherited from a reconstructed kikkara, "Acacia arabica"; CDIAL lists Punjabi kikkar under it (CDIAL 3151). Its gender varies: CDIAL marks kikkar masculine, beside a feminine kikkarī; this lesson treats ਕਿੱਕਰ as feminine.

You'll want to know: ਸੰਘਣਾ.
ਸੰਘਣਾ — saṅghṇā — "dense; thick with growth".
ਇਹ ਜੰਗਲ ਬਹੁਤ ਸੰਘਣਾ ਹੈ। eh jaṅgal bahut saṅghṇā hai. This forest is very dense.

The word, taken apart.
ਸੰਘਣਾ (saṅghṇā) is inherited from a reconstructed saṁghana, "very dense"; CDIAL lists Punjabi saṅghṇā, "thick, close together", under it (CDIAL 12856).

Lesson 5 of 5.
ਕੁਮਲਾਉਣਾ (kumlāuṇā) — to wilt; to wither.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for an acacia tree, then the Punjabi for dense.

You'll want to know: ਕੁਮਲਾਉਣਾ.
ਕੁਮਲਾਉਣਾ — kumlāuṇā — "to wilt; to wither".
ਤੇਜ਼ ਧੁੱਪ ਵਿੱਚ ਫੁੱਲ ਕੁਮਲਾਉਂਦੇ ਹਨ। tez dhupp vich phull kumlāunde han. Flowers wilt in strong sunshine.
Past (it …): ਉਹ ਫੁੱਲ ਕੁਮਲਾਇਆ, oh phull kumlāiā.

The word, taken apart.
ਕੁਮਲਾਉਣਾ (kumlāuṇā) is inherited from a reconstructed kōmh, "wither"; CDIAL lists Punjabi kumlāuṇā, "to wither", under it (CDIAL 3524).
