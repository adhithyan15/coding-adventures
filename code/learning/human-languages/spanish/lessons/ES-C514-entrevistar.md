## You'll want to know: entrevistar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C514-REPORTE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**entrevistar** — "to interview".

> Entrevistaron al alcalde en la radio.
>
> *They interviewed the mayor on the radio.*

*La entrevista* is the interview. It takes personal *a* with people: *entrevistar a alguien*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*entrevistar* is built on *entrevista*, made from *entre* 'between' and *vista* 'a sight', and usually said to follow French *entrevue*. *Vista* comes from Latin *vidēre* 'to see' (EtymDB).
