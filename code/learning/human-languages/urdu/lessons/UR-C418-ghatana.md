# گھٹانا (ghaṭānā) — to bring down (a price); to reduce

## You'll want to know: گھٹانا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C418-HISAB418-05]; assesses=[] -->

**گھٹانا** — *ghaṭānā* — "to bring down (a price); to reduce".

> بینک نے سود گھٹایا۔
>
> *baink ne sūd ghaṭāyā.*
>
> The bank lowered its interest rate.

Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے قیمت گھٹائی, *us ne qīmat ghaṭāī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**گھٹانا** (*ghaṭānā*) is built on *ghaṭnā*, "to decrease", which CDIAL lists under *ghaṭṭati*, a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 4415): this is the form for making something smaller.
