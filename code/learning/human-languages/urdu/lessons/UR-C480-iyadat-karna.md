## You'll want to know: عیادت کرنا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C480-CARE480-01]; assesses=[] -->

**عیادت کرنا** — *iyādat karnā* — "to visit the sick".

> ہم نے ہسپتال جا کر دادا کی عیادت کی۔
>
> *ham ne haspatāl jā kar dādā kī iyādat kī.*
>
> We went to the hospital to visit my grandfather.

Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے مریض کی عیادت کی, *us ne marīz kī iyādat kī*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**عیادت کرنا** (*iyādat karnā*) is built from **عیادت**, usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *ʿiyāda*, "visiting the sick", and **کرنا**, "to do".
