## You'll want to know: situado
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C669-PLANO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**situado** — "located, situated".

> El hotel está situado en el centro.
>
> *The hotel is located in the centre.*

Used with *estar*. Latin America often says *ubicado*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*situado* is the past form of *situar* "to place", which is built on Latin *situs* "position, site".
