## You'll want to know: पिरोना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C513-DARZI513-04]; assesses=[] -->

**पिरोना** — *pironā* — "to thread (a needle); to string (beads)".

> दर्ज़ी ने सूई में धागा पिरोया।
>
> *Darzī ne sūī meṁ dhāgā piroyā.*
>
> The tailor threaded the needle.

Past, with ने (he or she …): उसने माला में फूल पिरोए, *usne mālā meṁ phūl piroe*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**पिरोना** (*pironā*) is a tadbhava, inherited from Sanskrit *parivayati*, "weaves round" (CDIAL 7869). CDIAL gives the Hindi verb as "to plait, string (pearls), thread a needle".
