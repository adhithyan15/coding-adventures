German, chapter 63: Neighbour, Guest, Grandson, Pupil, Pupil.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
der Nachbar — a neighbour.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for an uncle, then the German for an aunt.

You'll want to know: der Nachbar.
der Nachbar — "a neighbour".

The word, taken apart.
Nachbar from Middle High German nāchbūre (EtymDB 2.1), "the one who dwells near": the word for "near" + an old word for "dweller". English neighbour is built the same way.

der Schüler — "a pupil (a boy)".

The word, taken apart.
Schüler from Middle High German schuolære, built on the word for "school", Old High German scuola from Latin schola (EtymDB 2.1) — a borrowed stem with the German ending -er.

die Schülerin — "a pupil (a girl)".

The word, taken apart.
Schülerin is Schüler + -in, the ending that names a woman or girl (EtymDB 2.1). The stem goes back to Latin schola, "school".
