## You'll want to know: त्याग
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C358-SEVA358-02]; assesses=[] -->

**त्याग** — *tyāg* — "renunciation, giving something up".

> माँ ने बच्चों के लिए बहुत त्याग किया।
>
> *māṁ ne baccoṁ ke liye bahut tyāg kiyā.*
>
> Mother gave up a great deal for her children.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**त्याग** (*tyāg*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *tyāga*, "leaving, giving up" (Monier-Williams).
