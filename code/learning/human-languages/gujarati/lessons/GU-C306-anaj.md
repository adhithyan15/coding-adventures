## You'll want to know: અનાજ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C306-RAHAT306-04]; assesses=[] -->

**અનાજ** — *anāj* — "grain (as food)".

> છાવણીમાં રોજ અનાજ અને દૂધ આવે છે.
>
> *chāvṇīmā̃ roj anāj ane dūdh āve che.*
>
> Grain and milk come to the camp every day.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

goes back to Sanskrit *annādya*, "food", and CDIAL lists the Gujarati noun, "grain" (CDIAL 398). Wheat, rice and millet are all **અનાજ**.
