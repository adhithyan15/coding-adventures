## You'll want to know: بعید
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FA-LEX-C392-EHTEMAL-04]; assesses=[] -->

**بعید** (*ba'id*) — "unlikely; far-fetched".

> بعید است که او امروز بیاید.
>
> *ba'id ast ke u emruz biyâyad.*
>
> It is unlikely that he will come today.

*Ba'id ast ke* + subjunctive. Its first meaning is "far".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**بعید** is usually traced to Arabic *ba'īd* "far, remote": an unlikely thing is far from happening.
