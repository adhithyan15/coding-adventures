Lesson 1 of 8.
બજાર (bajār) — hear market first.

Warm-up.
Listen twice: bajār. It means market. Do not study the Gujarati spelling yet; hold only the sound and meaning.

Guided Practice.

Lesson 8 of 8.
Three places in four skills — market — home — temple.

Before we start: this one needs your hands, so it is not a driving lesson. You will want a pen and something to write on. You can listen to all of it now and come back to the parts that need looking at once you have stopped.

Warm-up.
[pause 12 seconds]
Say market, home, and temple from their meaning cues.

Guided Practice.
Score four short checks separately: identify three heard words, say three from meaning, read three printed cards, and write one heard word. Reach full marks in each skill; repair only the failed part.

Wrap-up Recall.
Sources: Gujarati Wiktionary word index and Cambridge English–Gujarati Dictionary: temple.
[question — say your answer, then pause 35 seconds]
After all four skills pass separately, write market, home, and temple in order.
