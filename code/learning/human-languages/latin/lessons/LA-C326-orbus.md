## You'll want to know: orbus, orba, orbum
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C326-SENECTUS-04]; assesses=[] -->

**orbus, orba, orbum** — "orphaned; childless, bereft".

> Puella orba ab avunculō ēducāta est.
>
> *The orphaned girl was brought up by her uncle.*

Bereft of parents or of children; with the ablative, bereft of anything.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**orbus** is an inherited word that Lewis & Short compare with Greek *orphanos*, "orphan", the source of English *orphan*.
