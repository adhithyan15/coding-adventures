## You'll want to know: أنشد
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C545-SHIR545-03]; assesses=[] -->

**أنشد** — *anshada* — "to recite (verse); to chant".

> أنشد الطلاب نشيد المدرسة.
>
> *anshada aṭ-ṭullāb nashīd al-madrasa.*
>
> The students sang the school anthem.

Present: ينشد, *yunshid*.

The noun نشيد is an anthem or a chant.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**أنشد** (*anshada*) is built on the native Arabic root *n-sh-d*, "to seek; to recite verse" (Lane). Form IV anshada is to recite verse aloud.
