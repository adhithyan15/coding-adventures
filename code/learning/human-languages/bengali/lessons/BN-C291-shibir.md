## You'll want to know: শিবির
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C291-TRAN291-03]; assesses=[] -->

**শিবির** — *shibir* — "a camp".

> শিবিরে প্রতিদিন দুই হাজার মানুষ খাবার পেত।
>
> *shibire prôtidin dui hājār mānush khābār petô.*
>
> Two thousand people got food at the camp every day.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**শিবির** (*shibir*) is Sanskrit *śibira*, a royal camp or an army's tents (Monier-Williams); today it is any camp, from relief camps to medical camps.
