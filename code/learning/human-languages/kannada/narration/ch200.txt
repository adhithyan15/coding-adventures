Kannada, chapter 200: To get well, To itch, To die, To finish, To start.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಗುಣವಾಗು (guṇavāgu) — to get well, to heal.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to iron, then the Kannada for to change.

You'll want to know: ಗುಣವಾಗು.
ಗುಣವಾಗು — guṇavāgu — "to get well, to heal".

The word, taken apart.
ಗುಣವಾಗು (guṇavāgu) is guṇa, "good quality, health", from Sanskrit guṇa (CDIAL 4190), with native āgu, "to become" (DEDR 333): "to become well". DEDR 333 compares Sanskrit ām.

ಸಾಯು — sāyu — "to die".

The word, taken apart.
ಸಾಯು (sāyu) is native Dravidian, one word with Tamil cā (DEDR 2426). DEDR lists sāy, "to die", and sāvu, "death". DEDR 2426 compares Sanskrit śava.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ಶುರು ಮಾಡು (śuru māḍu) — to start.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: ಶುರು ಮಾಡು.

The word, taken apart.
ಶುರು ಮಾಡು (śuru māḍu) is śuru, "beginning", from Arabic šurūʿ, "a beginning", which came through Persian and Hindustani, and native māḍu, "to do" (DEDR 4797).
