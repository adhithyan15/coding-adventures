## You'll want to know: 特意
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C450-LOCAL450-03]; assesses=[] -->

**特意** — *tèyì* — "specially; for that very purpose".

> **我们特意去吃这儿的风味小吃** *wǒmen tèyì qù chī zhèr de fēngwèi xiǎochī* — We went specially to try the local snacks here.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**特意** (*tèyì*) is built from 特 'single' and 意 'an intention', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *dok* and *'iH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the second 'the will'.
