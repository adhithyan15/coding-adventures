Urdu, chapter 435: Bays and Nets.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
خلیج (khalīj) — a bay; a gulf.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for to toss (up), then the Urdu for to dip.

You'll want to know: خلیج.
خلیج — khalīj — "a bay; a gulf".
خلیج کا پانی گہرا اور نیلا ہے۔ khalīj kā pānī gahrā aur nīlā hai. The water in the bay is deep and blue.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
خلیج (khalīj) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic khalīj, "a bay, a gulf".

What you've built.
One more word for getting around.

Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
جال (jāl) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin jāl, "a net for catching birds or fish", under Sanskrit jāla (CDIAL 5213).

Lesson 5 of 5.
ڈبکی (ḍubkī) — a dip (under water).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a net, then the Urdu for a seashell.

You'll want to know: ڈبکی.
ڈبکی — ḍubkī — "a dip (under water)".
سارا نے جھیل میں ایک ڈبکی لی۔ sārā ne jhīl meṅ ek ḍubkī lī. Sara ducked under the water in the lake.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
ڈبکی (ḍubkī) is built on ḍub-, "sink", from ḍubb, a form CDIAL reconstructs (CDIAL 5561); ڈبکی لگانا is to take a dip.
