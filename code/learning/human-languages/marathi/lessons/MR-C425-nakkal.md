## You'll want to know: नक्कल
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C425-TARAJU425-05]; assesses=[] -->

**नक्कल** — *nakkal* — "a copy; an imitation".

> हा फोटो मूळ चित्राची नक्कल आहे.
>
> *hā phoṭo mūḷ citrācī nakkal āhe.*
>
> This photo is a copy of the original painting.

Feminine.

नक्कल करणे, to copy or to mimic someone.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**नक्कल** (*nakkal*) is a loan through Persian, usually traced to Arabic *naql*, "a transfer, a copy".
