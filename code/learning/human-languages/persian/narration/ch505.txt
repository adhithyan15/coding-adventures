Persian, chapter 505: Support and Comfort.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
حمایت کردن (hemâyat kardan) — to support, to back.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for a stranger, then the Persian for each other.

You'll want to know: حمایت کردن.
حمایت کردن (hemâyat kardan) — "to support, to back".
خانواده ام همیشه از من حمایت میکنند. khânevâde-am hamishe az man hemâyat mikonand. My family always supports me.
az kasi hemâyat kardan; hemâyat 'protection, support' + kardan.

The word, taken apart.
hemâyat comes from Arabic ḥimāya, 'protection' (EtymDB). kardan, 'to do', is inherited Persian.

Lesson 2 of 5.
سرزنش کردن (sarzanesh kardan) — to scold, to blame.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for each other, then the Persian for to support.

You'll want to know: سرزنش کردن.
سرزنش کردن (sarzanesh kardan) — "to scold, to blame".
مادر بچه را به خاطر دروغ سرزنش کرد. mâdar bachche râ be khâter-e dorugh sarzanesh kard. The mother scolded the child for lying.
sar 'head' + zan 'striking' + -esh, then kardan. The reason takes be khâter-e.

The word, taken apart.
sarzanesh is built on native Persian parts: EtymDB takes it as sar, 'head', zan, the stem of zadan, 'to hit', and the noun ending -esh (EtymDB): striking on the head, in words.

Lesson 3 of 5.
دلداری دادن (deldâri dâdan) — to comfort, to console.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to support, then the Persian for to scold.

You'll want to know: دلداری دادن.
دلداری دادن (deldâri dâdan) — "to comfort, to console".
دوستم بعد از امتحان به من دلداری داد. dustam ba'd az emtehân be man deldâri dâd. My friend comforted me after the exam.
del 'heart' + dâr 'keeping' + -i, then dâdan 'to give'. The person comforted takes be.

The word, taken apart.
deldâri is built on native Persian parts: del, 'heart', which EtymDB traces to Middle Persian (EtymDB), dâr, the stem of dâshtan, and -i: keeping someone's heart up. dâdan, 'to give', is inherited.

Lesson 4 of 5.
بغل کردن (baghal kardan) — to hug; to pick up (a child).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to scold, then the Persian for to comfort.

You'll want to know: بغل کردن.
بغل کردن (baghal kardan) — "to hug; to pick up (a child)".
پدر دخترش را بغل کرد. pedar dokhtarash râ baghal kard. The father hugged his daughter.
baghal 'armpit; side; embrace' + kardan. baghal alone also means 'beside': baghal-e man.

The word, taken apart.
baghal means 'armpit, side, embrace', but EtymDB has no entry for it, and its origin is not settled. kardan, 'to do', is inherited Persian.

Lesson 5 of 5.
نوازش کردن (navâzesh kardan) — to caress, to stroke.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Persian for to comfort, then the Persian for to hug.

You'll want to know: نوازش کردن.
نوازش کردن (navâzesh kardan) — "to caress, to stroke".
او گربه را آرام نوازش کرد. u gorbe râ ârâm navâzesh kard. She stroked the cat gently.
navâzesh 'a caress', from the stem navâz- of navâkhtan, + kardan.

The word, taken apart.
navâzesh is built on navâz-, the present stem of the native verb navâkhtan, 'to caress; to play music', with the noun ending -esh. EtymDB has no entry for it.
