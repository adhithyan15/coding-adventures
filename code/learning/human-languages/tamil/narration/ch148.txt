Tamil, chapter 148: Deep, Wide, Narrow, Dry, Wet.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
ஆழமான (āḻamāṉa) — deep.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Tamil for lazy; a lazy person, then the Tamil for brave.

You'll want to know: ஆழமான.
ஆழமான — āḻamāṉa — "deep".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: āḻamāṉa]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: āḻamāṉa, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say āḻamāṉa]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Tamil for lazy; a lazy person, then the Tamil for brave, then say āḻamāṉa again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does ஆழமான (āḻamāṉa) mean? ("Deep".) Say it once more.

Lesson 6 of 7.
Animals and the body — a first pass.

Warm-up.
[pause 3 seconds]
Say the words for dry and wet.

Guided Practice.
[pause 2 seconds after each]
[your turn — say: grapes, a pomegranate, a pickle, a waterfall, a hill]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: an island, an insect, a spider, a pig, a donkey]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: a camel, a duck, a cheek, a wrist, an elbow]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: a thumb, bad, beautiful, long, short]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
Grapes? (திராட்சை, tirāṭcai.) A pomegranate? (மாதுளை, mātuḷai.) A pickle? (ஊறுகாய், ūṟukāy.) A waterfall? (அருவி, aruvi.) A hill? (குன்று, kuṉṟu.) An island? (தீவு, tīvu.) An insect? (பூச்சி, pūcci.) A spider? (சிலந்தி, cilanti.) A pig? (பன்றி, paṉṟi.) A donkey? (கழுதை, kaḻutai.) A camel? (ஒட்டகம், ottakam.) A duck? (வாத்து, vāttu.) A cheek? (கன்னம், kaṉṉam.) A wrist? (மணிக்கட்டு, maṇikkaṭṭu.) An elbow? (முழங்கை, muḻaṅkai.) A thumb? (கட்டைவிரல், kaṭṭaiviral.) Bad? (கெட்ட, keṭṭa.) Beautiful? (அழகான, aḻakāṉa.) Long? (நீளமான, nīḷamāṉa.) Short? (குட்டையான, kuṭṭaiyāṉa.)

(சுத்தமான, cuttamāṉa.) Dirty? (அழுக்கான, aḻukkāṉa.) Fast? (வேகமான, vēkamāṉa.) Bitter? (கசப்பு, kacappu.) Sour? (புளிப்பு, puḷippu.) Spicy? (காரம், kāram.) Rich? (பணக்கார, paṇakkāra.) Poor? (ஏழை, ēḻai.) Young? (இளைய, iḷaiya.) Old? (வயதான, vayatāṉa.) Clever; a clever person? (புத்திசாலி, putticāli.) Lazy; a lazy person? (சோம்பேறி, cōmpēṟi.) Brave? (தைரியமான, tairiyamāṉa.) Deep?
