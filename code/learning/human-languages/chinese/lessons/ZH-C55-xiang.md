## You'll want to know: 想
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C55-LIKE55-01]; assesses=[] -->

**想** — *xiǎng* — "to want to; to think; to miss".

> **我想说中国话** *wǒ xiǎng shuō Zhōngguó huà* — I'd like to speak Chinese.

The new character, in pieces: 想 — a tree piece and an eye piece side by side, over a heart piece. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

*xiǎng* before another verb says what you would like to do: *wǒ xiǎng shuō*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**想** (*xiǎng*) is inherited from Middle Chinese *sjangX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'to think'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for everyday answers.
