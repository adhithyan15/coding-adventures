Marathi, chapter 165: To miss, To improve, To get worse, To observe, To take part.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
चुकणे (cukṇe) — to miss; to go wrong.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to increase, then the Marathi for to reduce.

You'll want to know: चुकणे.
चुकणे — cukṇe — "to miss; to go wrong".

The word, taken apart.
चुकणे (cukṇe) is a tadbhava, a word inherited and reshaped by sound change. It comes from cukk, "fall short of, stop", a form CDIAL reconstructs from its descendants (CDIAL 4848). CDIAL says Prakrit cukka, "fallen, mistaken", took the place of Sanskrit cyuta, "fallen".

Lesson 3 of 5.
बिघडणे (bighaḍṇe) — to get worse, to break down.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for to miss; to go wrong, then the Marathi for to improve.

You'll want to know: बिघडणे.
बिघडणे — bighaḍṇe — "to get worse, to break down".

The word, taken apart.
बिघडणे (bighaḍṇe) goes back to Sanskrit vighaṭate, "flies apart, is marred, is broken" (CDIAL 11673). CDIAL marks the Marathi form as taken from Hindi bigaṛnā.

Lesson 4 of 5.
निरीक्षण करणे (nirīkṣaṇ karṇe) — to observe.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: निरीक्षण करणे.
निरीक्षण करणे — nirīkṣaṇ karṇe — "to observe".

The word, taken apart.
निरीक्षण is Sanskrit nirīkṣaṇa, "looking at, observing", a tatsama built on īkṣ, "to see". करणे comes from Sanskrit karoti, "does" (CDIAL 2814).

भाग घेणे — bhāg gheṇe — "to take part".

The word, taken apart.
भाग, "share, part", CDIAL lists under Sanskrit bhāgya, "entitled to a share", which it takes as also meaning "part" (CDIAL 9435). घेणे comes from Sanskrit gṛbhāyati, "takes, seizes" (CDIAL 4236).
