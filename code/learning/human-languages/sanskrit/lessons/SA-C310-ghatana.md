## You'll want to know: घटना
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C310-GHATANA310-01]; assesses=[] -->

**घटना** — *ghaṭanā* — "an event".

> अद्य नगरे एका घटना घटिता।
>
> *adya nagare ekā ghaṭanā ghaṭitā.*
>
> Something happened in the city today.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**घटना** (*ghaṭanā*) comes from the root √ghaṭ, which also gives *ghaṭate*, "happens"; MW gives *ghaṭanā* as exertion, motion and a manner of acting, and an event is its modern sense (MW).
