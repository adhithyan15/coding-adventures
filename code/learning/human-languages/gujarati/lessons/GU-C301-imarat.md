## You'll want to know: ઇમારત
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C301-IMARAT301-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ઇમારત** — *imārat* — "a large building".

> ગઈકાલે રાત્રે શહેરની એક જૂની ઇમારતમાં આગ લાગી.
>
> *gaīkāle rātre śahernī ek jūnī imāratmā̃ āg lāgī.*
>
> Last night a fire broke out in an old building in the city.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ઇમારત** (*imārat*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *ʿimāra*, "building, construction". It is a large building, where **મકાન** is any building or house.
