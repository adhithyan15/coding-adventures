## You'll want to know: માળા
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C329-RANGOLI329-04]; assesses=[] -->

**માળા** — *māḷā* — "a garland".

> મીરાએ ફૂલની માળા બનાવી.
>
> *Mīrāe phūlnī māḷā banāvī.*
>
> Mira made a garland of flowers.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**માળા** (*māḷā*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *mālā*, "a wreath, a garland" (CDIAL 10092). It is feminine: **ફૂલની માળા**.
