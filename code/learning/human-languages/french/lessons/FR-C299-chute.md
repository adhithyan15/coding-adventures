## You'll want to know: la chute
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C299-CHUTE-01]; assesses=[] -->

**la chute** — "the fall; the drop".

> Ma grand-mère a fait une chute dans l'escalier.
>
> *My grandmother had a fall on the stairs.*

*Les chutes de neige* — snowfalls; *une chute d'eau* — a waterfall.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*chute* is built on *chu*, the old past participle of *choir* "to fall" (EtymDB), which is inherited from Latin *cadere* "to fall" (Lewis & Short).
