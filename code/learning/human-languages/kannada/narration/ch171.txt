Kannada, chapter 171: Always, Now and then, Date, Weekend, Before.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ಯಾವಾಗಲೂ (yāvāgalū) — always.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a blister, then the Kannada for nutritious.

You'll want to know: ಯಾವಾಗಲೂ.
ಯಾವಾಗಲೂ — yāvāgalū — "always".

The word, taken apart.
ಯಾವಾಗಲೂ (yāvāgalū) is native Dravidian: yāvāga, "when", from yāva, "which" (DEDR 5151), and āga, "time" (DEDR 1), with -lū, "even": "at every which time".
