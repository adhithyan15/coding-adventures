## You'll want to know: sereno
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C474-UMORE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**sereno** — "calm, serene; clear (of the sky)".

> Dopo le vacanze mi sento più sereno.
>
> *After the holidays I feel calmer.*

Of the sky: *il cielo è sereno* — the sky is clear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*sereno* is inherited from Latin *serēnus* "clear, bright, calm" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short), said first of the sky.
