# ਨੋਟਿਸ (noṭis) — a notice

## You'll want to know: ਨੋਟਿਸ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C253-RULE253-02]; assesses=[] -->

**ਨੋਟਿਸ** — *noṭis* — "a notice".

> ਦਫ਼ਤਰ ਦੇ ਦਰਵਾਜ਼ੇ ਉੱਤੇ ਨਵਾਂ ਨੋਟਿਸ ਹੈ।
>
> *daftar de darvāze utte navāṁ noṭis hai.*
>
> There is a new notice on the office door.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਨੋਟਿਸ** (*noṭis*) is borrowed from English *notice*.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for reading notices and messages.
