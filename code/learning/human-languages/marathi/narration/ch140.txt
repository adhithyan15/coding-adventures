Marathi, chapter 140: Root, Seed, Soil, Rock, Wave.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
मूळ (mūḷ) — a root.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Marathi for grass, then the Marathi for a branch.

You'll want to know: मूळ.
मूळ — mūḷ — "a root".

What you've built.
One more word for this chapter.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: mūḷ]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: mūḷ, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: say mūḷ]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Marathi for grass, then the Marathi for a branch, then say mūḷ again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does मूळ (mūḷ) mean? ("A root".) Say it once more.
