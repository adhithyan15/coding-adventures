## You'll want to know: अंधार
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C519-UJED519-04]; assesses=[] -->

**अंधार** — *andhār* — "darkness".

> रात्री बागेत खूप अंधार असतो.
>
> *rātrī bāget khūp andhār asto.*
>
> At night it is very dark in the garden.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**अंधार** (*andhār*) is a tadbhava, inherited from Sanskrit *andhakāra*, "darkness" (CDIAL 386).
