## You'll want to know: взрыв
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C301-KATASTROFA-02]; assesses=[] -->

**взрыв** (*vzryv*) — "an explosion".

> Мы услышали громкий взрыв.
>
> *My uslýshali grómkiy vzryv.*
>
> We heard a loud explosion.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*взрыв* is built on *взрывать* "to blow up", which is usually traced to *вз-* "up" and *рыть* "to dig": to throw the earth up. EtymDB lists no parent.
