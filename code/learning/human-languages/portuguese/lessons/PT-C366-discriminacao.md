## You'll want to know: a discriminação
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C366-SOCIEDADE-02]; assesses=[] -->

**a discriminação** — "the discrimination".

> A lei proíbe a discriminação no trabalho.
>
> *The law forbids discrimination at work.*

*Discriminação contra as mulheres* — discrimination against women.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*discriminação* is a learned borrowing from Late Latin *discrīminātiō*, a term of rhetoric for "the contrasting of opposite thoughts", on *discrīmināre* "to divide, to separate", built on *discrīmen* "that which separates or divides" (Lewis & Short).
