Russian, chapter 82: This, This, That, That, Such.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
этот (étot) — this (masculine).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a park, then the Russian for a taxi.

You'll want to know: этот.
этот (étot) — "this (masculine)".

The word, taken apart.
этот (étot) is the pointing sound э, "look", with тот (tot), "that" (Vasmer); examples date from the 1500s.

Lesson 3 of 5.
тот (tot) — that (masculine).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for this, then the Russian for this.

You'll want to know: тот.
тот (tot) — "that (masculine)".

The word, taken apart.
тот (tot) from Proto-Slavic tŭ, ta, to, "that" — inherited. Vasmer lists Lithuanian tàs, Sanskrit ta-, Greek tó and Gothic þata: English that and the are cousins.

Lesson 5 of 5.
такой (takóy) — such, like this.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for that, then the Russian for that.

You'll want to know: такой.
такой (takóy) — "such, like this".

The word, taken apart.
такой (takóy) is так, "so, thus", with an adjective ending, Proto-Slavic takŭjĭ; Vasmer compares Lithuanian tóks, "such". It is built as какой, "which", is.
