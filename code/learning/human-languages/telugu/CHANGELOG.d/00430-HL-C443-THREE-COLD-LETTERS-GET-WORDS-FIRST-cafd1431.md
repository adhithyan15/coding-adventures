## HL-C443: three cold letters get words first

Gentle writing asks for every letter to be taken from a word the reader
already says. Three Telugu letter lessons came before any word holding their
letter. Each now follows a short word lesson that holds it:

| letter | word placed just before it |
|---|---|
| డ | **డప్పు** (*ḍappu*, a drum) |
| హ | **హంస** (*haṃsa*, a swan) |
| ఇ | **ఇటుక** (*iṭuka*, a brick) |

Each word is revisited by its letter lesson and by the lesson after it. The
ten digit lessons stay cold: no word headword holds a digit, so there is no
word to take them from.

    telugu cold letter lessons   13  ->  10 (all ten are digits; ceiling ratcheted)
