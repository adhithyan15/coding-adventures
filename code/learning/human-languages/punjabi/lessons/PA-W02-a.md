# ਅ — a vowel that can stand first

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-SCRIPT-NAMASTE-READ-01] -->

Point to the first known piece in **ਨਮਸਤੇ**: **ਨ**. A consonant can carry an
unwritten *a*. Today a vowel stands by itself at the beginning of a word.

## Script
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-SCRIPT-INDEPENDENT-A-01]; assesses=[] -->

> **ਅ**

Read it as short *a*. It is one complete letter, not a mark attached to a
consonant. That is why it can begin the practice name in the next lesson.

## Writing — observe and trace
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-SCRIPT-INDEPENDENT-A-01] -->

Keep **ਅ** visible. Follow one printed model with your finger, then trace it
once. Compare only three things: the top line, the upright, and the lower turn.

## Wrap-up recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-SCRIPT-INDEPENDENT-A-01] -->
