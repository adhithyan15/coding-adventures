Arabic, chapter 227: To be shy, To worry, To get to know, To quarrel, To make up.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
خجل (khajila) — to be shy, to be embarrassed.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Arabic for to inform, then the Arabic for to agree.

You'll want to know: خجل.
خجل — khajila — "to be shy, to be embarrassed".

The word, taken apart.
خجل (khajila) is built on the native Arabic root kh-j-l, "to be ashamed, shy" (Lane). Khajila is the plain verb, "he was abashed".

[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: تشاجر.
تشاجر — tashājara — "to quarrel".

The word, taken apart.
تشاجر (tashājara) is built on the native Arabic root sh-j-r, "to be intertwined; trees" (Lane). Lane gives the root's sense of things intertwined; tashājara, Form VI, is to quarrel, and shajar, "trees", is on the same root.
