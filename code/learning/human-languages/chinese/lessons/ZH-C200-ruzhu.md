## You'll want to know: rùzhù
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C200-HOTEL200-04]; assesses=[] -->

*rùzhù* — "to check in (at a hotel)".

> *wǒmen xiàwǔ liǎng diǎn kěyǐ rùzhù* — We can check in at two in the afternoon.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*rùzhù* is built from *rù* 'to enter' and *zhù* 'to stay', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *nyip* and *drjuH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'within' and the second 'to stop'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for talking about plans.
