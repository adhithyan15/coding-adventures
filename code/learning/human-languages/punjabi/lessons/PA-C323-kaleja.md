## You'll want to know: ਕਲੇਜਾ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C323-BODY323-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ਕਲੇਜਾ** — *kalejā* — "the liver".

> ਕਲੇਜਾ ਢਿੱਡ ਦੇ ਸੱਜੇ ਪਾਸੇ ਹੁੰਦਾ ਹੈ।
>
> *kalejā ḍhiḍḍ de sajje pāse hundā hai.*
>
> The liver is on the right side of the belly.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਕਲੇਜਾ** (*kalejā*) is inherited from Sanskrit *kāleyaka*, "a part of the intestines; the liver"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *kalejā*, "liver", under it (CDIAL 3103).
