## You'll want to know: l'ago
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[IT-LEX-C500-CUCITO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**l'ago** — "needle".

> Mi passi l'ago e il filo?
>
> *Can you pass me the needle and thread?*

The plural is *gli aghi*. *Cercare un ago in un pagliaio* — a needle in a haystack.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*ago* is inherited from Latin *acus* "a needle" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short).
