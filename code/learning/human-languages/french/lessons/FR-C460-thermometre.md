## You'll want to know: le thermomètre
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C460-METEO-05]; assesses=[] -->

**le thermomètre** — "the thermometer".

> Ce matin, le thermomètre indique moins cinq.
>
> *This morning the thermometer reads minus five.*

Also for a fever.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*thermomètre* is a learned formation from Greek *thermós* 'hot' and *métron* 'measure' (EtymDB lists the word).
