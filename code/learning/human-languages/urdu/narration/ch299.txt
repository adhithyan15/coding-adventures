Lesson 1 of 6.
امداد (imdād) — aid; relief supplies.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for reassurance, then the Urdu for a reunion.

You'll want to know: امداد.
امداد — imdād — "aid; relief supplies".
حکومت نے امداد بھیجی۔ hukūmat ne imdād bhejī. The government sent aid.
Feminine.

The word, taken apart.
امداد (imdād) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic imdād, "a reinforcing, a supply". It is feminine: امداد بھیجی.

رضاکار — razākār — "a volunteer".
سو رضاکار گاؤں پہنچے۔ sau razākār gāoṅ pahuṅche. A hundred volunteers reached the village.
Masculine or feminine, by the person meant.

The word, taken apart.
رضاکار (razākār) is usually traced to Persian razākār, built from Arabic riḍā, "consent", and Persian -kār, "one who does": one who works by choice.

Lesson 3 of 6.
جمع کرنا (jama karnā) — to collect; to gather (things).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for aid, then the Urdu for a volunteer.

You'll want to know: جمع کرنا.
جمع کرنا — jama karnā — "to collect; to gather (things)".
بچوں نے کپڑے جمع کیے۔ bachchoṅ ne kapṛe jama kiye. The children collected clothes.
Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے پیسے جمع کیے, us ne paise jama kiye.

The word, taken apart.
جمع کرنا (jama karnā) is built from jama, usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic jamʿ, "a gathering together", and کرنا. The things gathered agree: کپڑے جمع کیے.

Lesson 4 of 6.
حوصلہ (hauslā) — courage; morale.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a volunteer, then the Urdu for to collect.

You'll want to know: حوصلہ.
حوصلہ — hauslā — "courage; morale".
رضاکاروں نے لوگوں کا حوصلہ بڑھایا۔ razākāroṅ ne logoṅ kā hauslā baṛhāyā. The volunteers raised people's spirits.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
حوصلہ (hauslā) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic ḥawṣala, "a bird's crop", and so capacity, the room to bear things; in Urdu it is courage. حوصلہ بڑھانا is to raise someone's spirits.
