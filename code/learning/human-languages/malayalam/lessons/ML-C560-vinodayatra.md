# വിനോദയാത്ര (vinōdayātra) — an excursion; a pleasure trip

## Warm-up

[PAUSE 2s] Before the new one: say the Malayalam for to cherish, then the Malayalam for to ask forgiveness.

## You'll want to know: വിനോദയാത്ര
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C560-SILPAM560-01]; assesses=[] -->

**വിനോദയാത്ര** — *vinōdayātra* — "an excursion; a pleasure trip".

> സ്കൂളിൽ നിന്ന് കുട്ടികൾ വിനോദയാത്രയ്ക്ക് പോയി.
>
> *skūḷil ninnŭ kuṭṭikaḷ vinōdayātraykkŭ pōyi.*
>
> The children went on a school excursion.

വിനോദം a pastime + യാത്ര a journey.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**വിനോദയാത്ര** (*vinōdayātra*) is a learned compound built from Sanskrit *vinoda*, "diversion, pastime" (Monier-Williams), and *yātrā*, "a journey" (CDIAL 10456).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
