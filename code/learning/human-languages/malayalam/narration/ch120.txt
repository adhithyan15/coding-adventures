Malayalam, chapter 120: Show Me, Help Me.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
കാണിക്കൂ (kāṇikkū) — please show.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for meaning, then the Malayalam for please wait.

You'll want to know: കാണിക്കൂ.
കാണിക്കൂ (kāṇikkū) — "please show (me)", from കാണുക (kāṇuka), "to see".

The word, taken apart.
കാണിക്കൂ (kāṇikkū) is native Dravidian, the causative of kāṇuka, "to see": "make see"; DEDR 1443 lists the root with Tamil kāṇ and Kannada kāṇ.

What you've built.
To see, turned into to show.

Lesson 2 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]

You'll want to know: കേട്ടില്ല.
കേട്ടില്ല (kēṭṭilla) — "(I) didn't hear", കേട്ടു, "heard", with ഇല്ല, "not". Follow it with ഒന്നുകൂടി പറയൂ.

The word, taken apart.
കേട്ടില്ല (kēṭṭilla) is native Dravidian: kēṭṭu, "heard", the past of the hearing verb (DEDR 2017, with Tamil kēḷ), with illa, "not" (DEDR 2559).

What you've built.
Heard, and the no that follows.

Lesson 3 of 5.
മനസ്സിലായില്ല (manassilāyilla) — I didn't understand.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for please show, then the Malayalam for I didn't hear.

You'll want to know: മനസ്സിലായില്ല.
മനസ്സിലായില്ല (manassilāyilla) — "I didn't understand", literally "it didn't come into the mind": മനസ്സ് (manassŭ), "mind".

The word, taken apart.
മനസ്സിലായില്ല (manassilāyilla) is "it did not become in the mind": Sanskrit manas, "mind" (CDIAL 9822), with the place ending, then native āyi, "became" (DEDR 333), and illa, "not" (DEDR 2559). DEDR 333 compares Sanskrit ām.

What you've built.
Mind, and the no that closes it.

The word, taken apart.
സഹായിക്കൂ (sahāyikkū) is built on Sanskrit sahāya, "companion, helper" (CDIAL 13309), with the ending that makes a Malayalam verb, here in its request form.

What you've built.

Lesson 5 of 5.
നിർത്തൂ (nirttū) — please stop.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for I didn't understand, then the Malayalam for please help.

You'll want to know: നിർത്തൂ.
നിർത്തൂ (nirttū) — "please stop (it)", said to a driver or about a noise. ഇവിടെ നിർത്തൂ — "please stop here", with ഇവിടെ, "here".

The word, taken apart.
നിർത്തൂ (nirttū) is native Dravidian, the causative of the verb "to stand": "make stand"; DEDR 3675 lists the root with Tamil nil and Kannada nil.

What you've built.
The request in a taxi.
