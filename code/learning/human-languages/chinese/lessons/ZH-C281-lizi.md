## You'll want to know: lìzi
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C281-LIZI-01]; assesses=[] -->

*lìzi* — "an example".

> *qǐng shuō yí ge lìzi* — Please give an example.

Its first character comes later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*lìzi* is built from lì 'a precedent' and the ending 子, both inherited from Middle Chinese, *ljejH* and *tsiX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription). The dictionary glosses the first 'to compare' and 'all of a kind'.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ZH-LEX-C281-BIRU-01] -->

比如 brings an example in; *lìzi* is the example itself, a noun you can count: *yí ge lìzi*.

So a teacher asks *qǐng shuō yí ge lìzi*, and you answer with 比如 ….
