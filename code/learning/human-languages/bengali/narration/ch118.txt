Lesson 1 of 5.
রসগোল্লা (rôsôgollā) — rasgulla.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for a radish, then the Bengali for uncooked rice.

You'll want to know: রসগোল্লা.
রসগোল্লা — rôsôgollā — "rasgulla".

The word, taken apart.
রসগোল্লা (rôsôgollā) is rôs, "juice, syrup", Sanskrit rasa, + gollā, "ball", which belongs with Bengali golā, "ball", from Sanskrit gōla (CDIAL 4321).

পায়েস — pāyes — "rice pudding".

The word, taken apart.
পায়েস (pāyes) is Sanskrit pāyasa, "rice boiled in milk", lightly reshaped. CDIAL has that entry but lists no Bengali form under it (CDIAL 8099).
