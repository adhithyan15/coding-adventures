## You'll want to know: gùzhí
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C470-FAULTS470-02]; assesses=[] -->

*gùzhí* — "stubborn".

> *wǒ yéye bǐ wǒ nǎinai gùzhí* — My grandfather is more stubborn than my grandmother.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*gùzhí* is built from *gù* 'firm' and *zhí* 'to hold', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *kuH* and *tsyip* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'firm' and the second 'to hold' and 'to keep': holding on to one's view.
