# One condolence, held as a whole

## Warm-up

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[SA-LEX-MAMA, SA-LEX-C68-FEELINGS-01, SA-LEX-C182-WEL182-02, SA-LEX-C232-ACT232-03, SA-LEX-C239-WEL239-04] -->

[PAUSE 3s] Recall **मम**, “my”; **दुःखम्**, “sorrow”; **शोकः**, “grief”;
**म्रियते**, “dies”; and **दया**, “compassion.” These known words prepare the
meaning. They are not yet a condolence you can say to somebody.

## You'll want to know: one attested condolence

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-PATTERN-C272-CONDOLENCE-01]; assesses=[] -->

> **मम संवेदनाः शोकसन्तप्त-परिजनैः सह सन्ति।**
>
> *mama saṃvedanāḥ śokasantapta-parijanaiḥ saha santi.*
>
> “My condolences are with the bereaved family members.”

A contemporary Sanskrit report from
[*Hindusthān Samācār*](https://sanskrit.hindusthansamachar.in/Encyc/2026/5/28/Chief-Minister-Expresses-Grief-Demise-Poet-Dr-Bash.php)
quotes the same complete sentence, including the editorial hyphen inside
**शोकसन्तप्त-परिजनैः**. The report types an ASCII colon after **परिजनै**; the
lesson normalizes that one character to the Devanagari visarga **ः** shown
above. All India Radio Sanskrit bulletins independently use plural
**शोकसंवेदनाः** for condolences directed to bereaved families. The sentence
here is therefore attested contemporary Sanskrit, not a translation silently
built from an English formula.

## Grammar Lens: keep the formula whole

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[SA-PATTERN-C272-CONDOLENCE-01] -->

Hear the sentence in three meaning-chunks:

- **मम संवेदनाः** — “my condolences”
- **शोकसन्तप्त-परिजनैः सह** — “with the bereaved family members”
- **सन्ति** — “are”

For now, keep these chunks together as one whole social formula. This lesson
does not teach the instrumental plural or license new sentences from its
pieces.

The linked report also says **तस्मै विनम्र-श्रद्धाञ्जलिः**, a respectful
tribute directed to the person who died. That is a different act. The sentence
you are learning offers a condolence to the bereaved family; it is not a
tribute to the person who died.
