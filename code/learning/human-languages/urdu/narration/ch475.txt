Urdu, chapter 475: Long Illnesses.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
دائمی (dāimī) — chronic; lasting.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for measles, then the Urdu for smallpox.

You'll want to know: دائمی.
دائمی — dāimī — "chronic; lasting".
دادی کو دائمی کھانسی ہے۔ dādī ko dāimī khāṅsī hai. My grandmother has a chronic cough.

The word, taken apart.
دائمی (dāimī) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic dāʾimī, "lasting, perpetual".
