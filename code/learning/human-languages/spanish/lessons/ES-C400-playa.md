## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-PLAYA]; assesses=[] -->

**la playa** — "the beach."

It comes from Late Latin **plagia**, a flat stretch of shore. The dictionary's
first definition keeps both pieces: the edge of the sea or a large river,
formed by sand on a surface that is nearly level.

Across parts of South America the old flat-open-ground idea widened beyond the
water. A **playa de estacionamiento** can be an open parking area. That is not
a new unrelated word; it is the same flat expanse put to another use.

Now keep the required contrast visible:

- **la playa** — the beach;
- **la plaza** — the square.

The words differ at one place, and that place matters. Do not drop either word;
say the pair until the ending decides it before context has to rescue you.
