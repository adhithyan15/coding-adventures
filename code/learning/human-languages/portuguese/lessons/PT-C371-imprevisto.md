## You'll want to know: o imprevisto
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C371-IMPREVISTO-04]; assesses=[] -->

**o imprevisto** — "the unexpected problem, the hitch".

> Tive um imprevisto e cheguei tarde.
>
> *Something unexpected came up and I arrived late.*

*Salvo imprevistos* — barring the unexpected.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*imprevisto* is built from *in-* "not" and *previsto* "foreseen", the participle of *prever*, on Latin *vidēre* "to see" (Lewis & Short).
