# Review — if it rains

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[AR-GRAMMAR-C290-ELATIVE-01, AR-GRAMMAR-C290-SUPERLATIVE-01, AR-GRAMMAR-C290-MUNDHU-SPAN-01, AR-GRAMMAR-C290-QABLA-AGO-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "the train is better than the bus" and "the biggest city".
(*Al-qiṭār afḍal min al-ḥāfila*, *akbar madīna*.) Then "for two years" and
"a week ago". (*Mundhu sanatayn*, *qabla usbūʿ*.)

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[AR-GRAMMAR-C291-IDHA-PAST-01, AR-GRAMMAR-C291-IN-01, AR-GRAMMAR-C291-FA-RESULT-01, AR-GRAMMAR-C286-FUTURE-01] -->

Two lessons, and a condition with both its halves. The condition: *idhā* or
*in* with a past verb, meaning something that may well happen. The result: a
plain present as it is, or *fa-* in front when it opens with *sa-*, *lan*, an
order or a noun.

[PAUSE 2s] A plan with its condition in one breath:

> **إذا كان الطقس جميلا، فسنذهب إلى البحر.** — *idhā kāna aṭ-ṭaqs jamīlan,
> fa-sa-nadhhabu ilā al-baḥr* — If the weather is nice, we'll go to the sea.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[AR-GRAMMAR-C291-FA-RESULT-01, AR-GRAMMAR-C291-IDHA-PAST-01, AR-LEX-C195-ACT195-04, AR-LEX-C280-AMAL280-05, AR-LEX-C281-AMAL281-01, AR-GRAMMAR-C289-BECOME-01, AR-GRAMMAR-C286-LAN-01] -->

[PAUSE 1s]
- [YOU SAY: *idhā kāna aṭ-ṭaqs jamīlan, fa-sa-nadhhabu ilā al-baḥr*]
- [YOU SAY: "If you get lost, phone me" — *idhā ḍalalta, fa-ttaṣil bī*]
- [YOU SAY: "If the pharmacist phones, I'll give him the number" — *idhā ittaṣala aṣ-ṣaydalī, fa-sa-uʿṭīhi ar-raqm*]
- [YOU SAY: "If I learn a lot, I'll become an engineer" — *idhā taʿallamtu kathīran, fa-sa-uṣbiḥu muhandisan*]
- [YOU SAY: "If it rains, I won't travel" — *idhā amṭarat, fa-lan usāfira*]
- [YOU SAY: two plans of your own for the weekend: one with *idhā* and a plain present, one with *fa-sa-*]

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[AR-GRAMMAR-C291-IDHA-PAST-01, AR-GRAMMAR-C291-IN-01, AR-GRAMMAR-C291-FA-RESULT-01] -->

[PAUSE 3s] "If it rains, we won't go out"? (***Idhā amṭarat, fa-lan
nakhruja.***) Which tense follows *idhā*? (**The past.**) Which "if" sits in
*in shāʾa allāh*? (***In.***)
