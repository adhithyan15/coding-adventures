## You'll want to know: претензия
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C434-ZHALOBA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**претензия** (*preténziya*) — "a formal complaint, a claim".

> Мы отправили претензию в турфирму.
>
> *My otprávili preténziyu v turfírmu.*
>
> We sent a formal complaint to the travel agency.

Feminine. *У меня к вам претензия* — I have a complaint to make to you.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*претензия* is usually traced, probably through German, to Medieval Latin *praetēnsiō* 'a claim', from *praetendere* 'to stretch out, to put forward' (Lewis & Short). EtymDB does not list it, so the route is hedged.
