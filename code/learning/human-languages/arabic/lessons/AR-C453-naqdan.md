## You'll want to know: نقدا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C453-QIST453-02]; assesses=[] -->

**نقدا** — *naqdan* — "in cash".

> دفعت ثمن التذكرة نقدا.
>
> *dafaʿtu thaman at-tadhkira naqdan.*
>
> I paid for the ticket in cash.

The noun *naqd* is cash; the ending *-an* makes the adverb.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**نقدا** (*naqdan*) is built on the native Arabic root *n-q-d*, "to pick out good coins" (Lane). *Naqd* is ready money.
