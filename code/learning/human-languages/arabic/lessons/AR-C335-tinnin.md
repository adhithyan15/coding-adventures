## You'll want to know: تنين
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C335-SIHR335-03]; assesses=[] -->

**تنين** — *tinnīn* — "a dragon".

> طار التنين فوق الجبل.
>
> *ṭāra at-tinnīn fawqa al-jabal.*
>
> The dragon flew over the mountain.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**تنين** (*tinnīn*) is given by Lane, under *t-n-n*, as a huge serpent. It was borrowed from Aramaic *tannīnā* (EtymDB); Hebrew has *tannīn*, a sea monster.
