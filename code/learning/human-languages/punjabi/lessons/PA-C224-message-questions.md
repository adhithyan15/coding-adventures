# The message, by ear

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-READ-MESSAGE-224] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Hold Aman's message in mind: yesterday, and tomorrow.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-READ-MESSAGE-224, PA-GRAMMAR-PAST-GIA-216, PA-GRAMMAR-PAST-NEG-218, PA-FORM-AAVEGI-186, PA-GRAMMAR-FUT-1SG-221, PA-LEX-SHAID-223] -->

[PAUSE 2s each]
- [YOU HEAR: *Aman kall kitthe giā?*] Answer: **ਉਹ ਬਾਜ਼ਾਰ ਗਿਆ।**
- [YOU HEAR: *kī Aman ne chāh pītī?*] Answer: **ਨਹੀਂ ਜੀ, ਉਸ ਨੇ ਚਾਹ ਨਹੀਂ ਪੀਤੀ।**
- [YOU HEAR: *rel kadoṁ āvegī?*] Answer: **ਰੇਲ ਨੌਂ ਵਜੇ ਆਵੇਗੀ।**
- [YOU HEAR: *Aman kinne vaje saṭeshan jāvegā?*] Answer: **ਅੱਠ ਵਜੇ।**

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[PA-READ-MESSAGE-224, PA-GRAMMAR-US-NE-220] -->

[PAUSE 3s] Tell the message back in three lines: what Aman did, what he didn't,
and what will happen tomorrow.
