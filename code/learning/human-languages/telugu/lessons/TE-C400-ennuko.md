# ఎన్నుకో (ennukō) — to elect; to vote in

## You'll want to know: ఎన్నుకో
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[TE-LEX-C400-ENNIKA400-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ఎన్నుకో** — *ennukō* — "to elect; to vote in".

> విద్యార్థులు ఆమెను తరగతి నాయకురాలిగా ఎన్నుకున్నారు.
>
> *vidyārthulu āmenu taragati nāyakurāligā ennukunnāru.*
>
> The students elected her class leader.

Past (I …): ఎన్నుకున్నాను, *ennukunnānu*.

Close to ఎంచుకో, to choose, which you know; ఎన్నుకో is choosing by a vote.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ఎన్నుకో** (*ennukō*) is native Dravidian: *ennu*, "to count, to reckon, to judge" (DEDR 793), with -కో, from *konu*, "to take" (DEDR 2151): to pick out for oneself.
