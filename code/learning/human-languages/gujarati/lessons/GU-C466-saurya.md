## You'll want to know: શૌર્ય
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C466-SHAURYA466-03]; assesses=[] -->

**શૌર્ય** — *śaurya* — "valour".

> આગ વખતે દાદાએ ઘણું શૌર્ય બતાવ્યું.
>
> *āg vakhte dādāe ghaṇũ śaurya batāvyũ.*
>
> During the fire my grandfather showed great valour.

A neuter noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**શૌર્ય** (*śaurya*) is a tatsama, Sanskrit *śaurya*, "heroism, valour, prowess", as Monier-Williams records it.
