Japanese, chapter 430: Anger and Dislike.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
いかり (ikari) — anger; rage.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Japanese for hard to bear, then the Japanese for to comfort.

You'll want to know: いかり.
いかり — ikari — "anger; rage".
ちちの いかりは よく わかります。 — chichi no ikari wa yoku wakarimasu — "I understand my father's anger well."

The word, taken apart.
いかり (ikari) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese; its kanji spelling was attached to it later (JMdict).
