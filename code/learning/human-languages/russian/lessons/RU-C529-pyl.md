## You'll want to know: пыль
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C529-CLEANING-01]; assesses=[] -->

**пыль** (*pyl*) — "dust".

> На полках много пыли.
>
> *Na pólkakh mnógo pýli.*
>
> There's a lot of dust on the shelves.

Feminine; no plural.

<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->
