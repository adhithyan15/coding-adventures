# (un anuncio de trabajo) — four lines, five new words

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-C435-PAPEL-03, ES-LEX-C435-PAPEL-04] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say *experiencia previa*. The advert you are about to read turns on
that phrase.

## Guided Practice
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-C435-PAPEL-03, ES-LEX-C435-PAPEL-04, ES-LEX-C435-PAPEL-01, ES-LEX-C435-PAPEL-02] -->

Read it once straight through, then answer.

> **SE BUSCA DEPENDIENTE/A**
> Tienda del centro. Imprescindible **experiencia previa** de al menos un año.
> Enviar **currículum** y traer la **documentación** el día de la entrevista.

[PAUSE 3s]
- [YOU SAY: how much experience they want]
- [YOU SAY: what you have to send]
- [YOU SAY: what you have to bring, and when]

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-C435-PAPEL-01, ES-LEX-C435-PAPEL-05, ES-LEX-TRABAJO] -->

Now produce it. Say aloud:

[PAUSE 3s]
- [YOU SAY: I have two years of previous experience]
- [YOU SAY: I am sending my CV today]
- [YOU SAY: my neighbour is retired and does not work now]

**Five chapters, twenty-five words, and no new grammar at all.** Everything you
said a moment ago uses tenses you held before this run started. What was missing
was the vocabulary an ordinary week is made of — orders, buildings, machines,
paperwork — and none of it appears in a beginner's dialogue, because beginners'
dialogues are about holidays and hobbies.

## Wrap-up Recall
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ES-LEX-C435-PAPEL-01, ES-LEX-C435-PAPEL-02, ES-LEX-C435-PAPEL-03] -->

[PAUSE 3s] What do you send? (**el currículum**.) What do you bring? (**la
documentación**.) What is essential? (**experiencia previa**.)
