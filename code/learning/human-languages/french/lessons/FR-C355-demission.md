## You'll want to know: la démission
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C355-DEMISSION-03]; assesses=[] -->

**la démission** — "the resignation".

> Il a donné sa démission lundi.
>
> *He handed in his resignation on Monday.*

*Donner sa démission* — to resign.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*démission* is a learned borrowing from Latin *dēmissiō* "a letting down, a lowering" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short), from *dēmittere* "to send down", built on *mittere* "to send".
