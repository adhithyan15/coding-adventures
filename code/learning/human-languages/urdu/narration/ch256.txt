7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
شادی (shādī) — a wedding, a marriage.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a licence, then the Urdu for parking.

شادی — shādī — "a wedding, a marriage".

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
