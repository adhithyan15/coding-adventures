## You'll want to know: iūrgium, iūrgiī
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C537-IURG-01]; assesses=[] -->

**iūrgium, iūrgiī** — "a quarrel, dispute".

> Inter frātrēs saepe iūrgium est.
>
> *There is often a quarrel between the brothers.*

Neuter, second declension. A quarrel of words, at home or between neighbours.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**iūrgium** is built on *iūrgō* "to quarrel", which Lewis & Short takes from *iūs* "right, law" and *agō* "to drive"; EtymDB agrees on *iūs*.
