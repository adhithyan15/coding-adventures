## You'll want to know: orbita, orbitae
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C425-AGGER-04]; assesses=[] -->

**orbita, orbitae** — "a wheel rut, a track".

> In viā vetere altae orbitae sunt, et plaustrum lentē it.
>
> *On the old road there are deep ruts, and the wagon goes slowly.*

First declension, feminine: *orbita, orbitae f.* English *orbit* is the same word: the track a planet runs in.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*orbita* is built on *orbis* 'circle, wheel' (EtymDB; Lewis & Short): the track a wheel leaves. English *orbit* comes from it.
