## You'll want to know: গালিচা
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C509-GALICHA509-01]; assesses=[] -->

**গালিচা** — *gālichā* — "a rug; a carpet".

> বসার ঘরে একটা লাল গালিচা আছে।
>
> *Bôsār ghôre ekṭā lāl gālichā āchhe.*
>
> There's a red rug in the sitting room.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**গালিচা** (*gālichā*) is usually traced to Persian *ghālīcha*, "a small carpet", itself thought to come from Turkish.
