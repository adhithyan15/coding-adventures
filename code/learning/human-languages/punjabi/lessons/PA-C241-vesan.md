## You'll want to know: ਵੇਸਣ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C241-KAM241-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ਵੇਸਣ** — *vesaṇ* — "gram flour".

> ਮੈਂ ਵੇਸਣ ਨਾਲ ਪਕੌੜੇ ਬਣਾਉਂਦੀ ਹਾਂ।
>
> *maiṁ vesaṇ nāl pakauṛe baṇāundī hāṁ.*
>
> I make pakoras with gram flour.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਵੇਸਣ** (*vesaṇ*) is inherited from Sanskrit *vēsana*, "flour of a particular vegetable product"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *vesaṇ*, "gram flour" (CDIAL 12133).
