Urdu, chapter 73: Fat, Thin, Light, Heavy, Important.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
موٹا (moṭā) — fat, thick.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for empty, then the Urdu for sweet.

You'll want to know: موٹا.
موٹا — moṭā — "fat, thick".

The word, taken apart.
موٹا (moṭā) comes from a reconstructed mōṭṭa, one of a group of related forms that CDIAL heads muṭṭa, "defective" (CDIAL 10187): words for things stumpy, blunt or lumpish. CDIAL glosses the Hindi word "fat".

Lesson 2 of 5.
پتلا (patlā) — thin.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for sweet, then the Urdu for fat.

You'll want to know: پتلا.
پتلا — patlā — "thin".

The word, taken apart.
پتلا (patlā) goes back to Sanskrit pattrala, "leafy", which CDIAL takes to have meant "leaf-like, thin" (CDIAL 7736). It is built on pattra, "leaf": a thin thing is as flat as a leaf.

Lesson 3 of 5.
ہلکا (halkā) — light (in weight).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for fat, then the Urdu for thin.

You'll want to know: ہلکا.
ہلکا — halkā — "light (in weight)".

The word, taken apart.
ہلکا (halkā) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from laghu, "light" (CDIAL 10896). CDIAL notes that its l and h swapped places and that it took an added -k-.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ضروری (zarūrī) — important, necessary.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for light, then the Urdu for heavy.

You'll want to know: ضروری.
ضروری — zarūrī — "important, necessary".

The word, taken apart.
ضروری (zarūrī) is a loan from Arabic ḍarūrī, "necessary", which reached Urdu through Persian. Its Arabic root is ḍ-r-r (Lane).
