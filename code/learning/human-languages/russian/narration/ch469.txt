Russian, chapter 469: Jealousy and Envy.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ревность (révnost) — jealousy.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for a task, then the Russian for experience.

You'll want to know: ревность.
ревность (révnost) — "jealousy".
Из-за ревности они часто ссорятся. Iz-za révnosti oní chásto ssóryatsya. They often quarrel because of jealousy.
Feminine; no plural in everyday use.

The word, taken apart.
ревность (révnost) is a noun in -ость on the stem ревн-, which it shares with ревновать (revnovát) and ревнивый (revnívyy); EtymDB links it to Church Slavonic rĭvenie 'strife, quarrel'. An older sense, 'zeal', is still met in bookish Russian.

Lesson 2 of 5.
ревновать (revnovát) — to be jealous.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for experience, then the Russian for jealousy.

You'll want to know: ревновать.
ревновать (revnovát) — "to be jealous".
Она ревнует мужа к его коллегам. Oná revnúyet múzha k yegó kollégam. She gets jealous of her husband's colleagues.
Imperfective; the present is ревную, ревнуешь (-ова- becomes -у-). Ревновать кого к кому — to be jealous about someone because of someone. Perfective приревновать.

The word, taken apart.
ревновать (revnovát) is built with -овать on the stem it shares with ревность (révnost) 'jealousy'; EtymDB lists the verb without a source and links the noun to Church Slavonic rĭvenie 'strife'.

Lesson 3 of 5.
ревнивый (revnívyy) — jealous (by nature).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for jealousy, then the Russian for to be jealous.

You'll want to know: ревнивый.
ревнивый (revnívyy) — "jealous (by nature)".
Мой муж очень ревнивый. Moy muzh óchen revnívyy. My husband is very jealous.
Describes a jealous character. Short forms: ревнив, ревнива.

The word, taken apart.
ревнивый (revnívyy) is built on the stem of ревность (révnost) 'jealousy' with the adjective ending -ивый; EtymDB lists the adjective without a source.

Lesson 4 of 5.
зависть (závist) — envy.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to be jealous, then the Russian for jealous.

You'll want to know: зависть.
зависть (závist) — "envy".
Коллеги говорили о его успехе без зависти. Kollégi govoríli o yegó uspékhe bez závisti. His colleagues talked about his success without envy.
Feminine. С завистью — enviously.

The word, taken apart.
зависть (závist) is built on за- and the root of видеть 'to see', so envy is a way of looking at what another person has; видеть is inherited from Proto-Slavic viděti, from PIE weyd- 'to see, to know' (EtymDB).

Lesson 5 of 5.
завидовать (zavídovat) — to envy.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for jealous, then the Russian for envy.

You'll want to know: завидовать.
завидовать (zavídovat) — "to envy".
Я не завидую богатым людям. Ya ne zavíduyu bogátym lyúdyam. I don't envy rich people.
Imperfective; the present is завидую, завидуешь. Takes the dative: завидовать (zavídovat) брату. Perfective позавидовать.

The word, taken apart.
завидовать (zavídovat) is built on the same за- and вид- as зависть (závist) 'envy'; the root is that of видеть 'to see', inherited from Proto-Slavic viděti (EtymDB).
