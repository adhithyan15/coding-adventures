## You'll want to know: उपकरण
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C518-YANTRA518-02]; assesses=[] -->

**उपकरण** — *upakaraṇ* — "a device; an appliance".

> स्वयंपाकघरात विजेची खूप उपकरणं आहेत.
>
> *svayampākgharāt vijecī khūp upakaraṇaṁ āhet.*
>
> There are many electric appliances in the kitchen.

Neuter. Plural: उपकरणं, *upakaraṇaṁ*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**उपकरण** (*upakaraṇ*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *upakaraṇa*, "an instrument, a means" (Monier-Williams).
