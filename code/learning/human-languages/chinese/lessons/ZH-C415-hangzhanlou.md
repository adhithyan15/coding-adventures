## You'll want to know: hángzhànlóu
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C415-TERMINAL415-01]; assesses=[] -->

*hángzhànlóu* — "airport terminal".

> *wǒmen de hángbān zài èr hào hángzhànlóu* — Our flight goes from terminal two.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*hángzhànlóu* is built from *háng* 'to sail, to fly', 站 'a station' and *lóu* 'a building', all inherited from Middle Chinese, *hang*, *treamH* and *luw* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'a boat' and the third as a building of several storeys.
