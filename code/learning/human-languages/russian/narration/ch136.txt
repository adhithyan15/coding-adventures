Russian, chapter 136: Nobody, Nothing, Nowhere, To Nowhere, What For.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
никто (niktó) — nobody.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to draw, then the Russian for to travel.

You'll want to know: никто.
никто (niktó) — "nobody". Russian still puts не before the verb: Никто не знает. — niktó ne znáyet — "Nobody knows." Two negatives make one "no".

Lesson 2 of 5.
ничего (nichevó) — nothing.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for to travel, then the Russian for nobody.

You'll want to know: ничего.
ничего (nichevó) — "nothing". Spelled with г, said with a v, like сегодня. Я ничего не знаю. — ya nichevó ne znáyu — "I don't know anything." On its own, Ничего! means "It's all right!", for instance after Извините!

Lesson 4 of 5.
никуда (nikudá) — (to) nowhere.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Russian for nothing, then the Russian for nowhere.

You'll want to know: никуда.
никуда (nikudá) — "(to) nowhere". It is куда with ни-, so it answers "where to?": Я никуда не иду. — ya nikudá ne idú — "I'm not going anywhere."
