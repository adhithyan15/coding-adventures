## You'll want to know: تحقیق
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C349-TAHQIQ349-01]; assesses=[] -->

**تحقیق** — *tahqīq* — "research".

> وہ پانی پر تحقیق کر رہی تھی۔
>
> *voh pānī par tahqīq kar rahī thī.*
>
> She was doing research on water.

Feminine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**تحقیق** (*tahqīq*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *taḥqīq*, "verification, finding out what is true".
