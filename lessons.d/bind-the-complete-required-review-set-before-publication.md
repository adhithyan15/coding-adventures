# Bind the complete required review set before publication

Independent preflight withheld the unexecuted D1 publisher because iterating a
mutable external review map could pass vacuously if that map became empty. The
actual manifest was correct, and no push used that helper version. Preserve its
bytes/hash and the finding. Require the exact expected two review path/hash
entries and bind the complete manifest SHA in the final report, then rehash and
independently review the corrected publisher before executing it.

The corrected helper published D1 through one ordinary push and verified actual
REST/Git agreement. Candidate source and its test evidence stayed frozen during
the repair. This lesson was recorded immediately in an external filled draft
and installed with the next bounded D2 specification after every producer was
consumed. A loop over a supplied collection proves nothing about required
members unless the complete expected set is admitted first.

When installing a filled draft into a generated lesson, replace the complete
template body, including its trailing placeholder. The first D2 validation
caught that leftover line; removing it completed the lesson before commit.
