## You'll want to know: jiàngluò
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C413-FLY413-04]; assesses=[] -->

*jiàngluò* — "to land; to come down onto the runway".

> *fēijī jiàngluò yǐhòu wǒmen zuò dìtiě qù bīnguǎn* — Once the plane has landed, we'll take the metro to the hotel.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*jiàngluò* is built from *jiàng* 'to come down' and *luò* 'to fall', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *kaewngH* and *lak* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'down, to come down' and the second as leaves falling from a tree.
