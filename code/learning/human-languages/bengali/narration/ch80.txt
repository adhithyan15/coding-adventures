Lesson 1 of 5.
দেরি (deri) — lateness; late.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for now, then the Bengali for always.

You'll want to know: দেরি.
দেরি — deri — "lateness; late".

The word, taken apart.
দেরি (deri) is a loan, usually traced to Persian dēr, "late; a long time". Bengali adds the ending -i.

Lesson 2 of 5.
আগে (āge) — before.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for always, then the Bengali for lateness; late.

You'll want to know: আগে.
আগে — āge — "before".

The word, taken apart.
আগে (āge) is a tadbhava, a word inherited from Sanskrit and reshaped by sound change. It comes from agra, "top, front" (CDIAL 68). CDIAL lists āge under the locative agre, "in front".

Lesson 3 of 5.
পরে (pôre) — after, later.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for lateness; late, then the Bengali for before.

You'll want to know: পরে.
পরে — pôre — "after, later".

The word, taken apart.
পরে (pôre) is pôr, "later", with the ending -e. It goes back to Sanskrit para, "further, later", whose param means "after"; CDIAL has that entry but lists no Bengali form under it (CDIAL 7793).

Lesson 5 of 5.
দুপুর (dupur) — midday.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for after, then the Bengali for time.

You'll want to know: দুপুর.
দুপুর — dupur — "midday".

The word, taken apart.
দুপুর (dupur) is usually explained as Sanskrit dviprahara, "the second watch" of the day: dvi, "two", + prahara, "a watch of about three hours", which CDIAL lists as Bengali pahar (CDIAL 8900). CDIAL has no entry for the compound, and asks whether prahara is a Sanskrit form of Persian pahra, "watch".
