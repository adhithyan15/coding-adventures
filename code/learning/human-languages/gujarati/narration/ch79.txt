Gujarati, chapter 79: The Weekend, Tomorrow and Yesterday.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
શનિવાર (śanivār) — Saturday.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for Thursday, then the Gujarati for Friday.

You'll want to know: શનિવાર.
શનિવાર (śanivār) — "Saturday".

The word, taken apart.
શનિવાર (śanivār) is a Sanskrit compound, a tatsama: śani, "the planet Saturn", and vāra, "day of the week" (CDIAL 11547).

Lesson 3 of 5.
આવતીકાલ (āvtīkāl) — tomorrow.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for Saturday, then the Gujarati for Sunday.

You'll want to know: આવતીકાલ.
આવતીકાલ (āvtīkāl) — "tomorrow". Literally "the coming કાલ".

The word, taken apart.
આવતીકાલ (āvtīkāl) is "the coming kāl": āvtī, from āvvũ, "to come" (CDIAL 1200), and kāl, which CDIAL lists under Sanskrit kalya, "dawn; tomorrow" (CDIAL 3104).

સમય (samay) is Sanskrit samaya, "meeting; agreement; time", taken over whole as a learned loan, a tatsama (CDIAL 13185). CDIAL lists the inherited Gujarati form as samo, "time, season".
