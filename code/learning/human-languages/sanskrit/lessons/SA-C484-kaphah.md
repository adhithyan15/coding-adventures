## You'll want to know: कफः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C484-AYURVEDA484-04]; assesses=[] -->

**कफः** — *kaphaḥ* — "phlegm".

> प्रतिश्याये कफः वर्धते।
>
> *pratiśyāye kaphaḥ vardhate.*
>
> With a cold, phlegm increases.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**कफः** (*kaphaḥ*) MW gives "phlegm, as one of the three humours"; MW gives no derivation, so its origin is uncertain (MW).
