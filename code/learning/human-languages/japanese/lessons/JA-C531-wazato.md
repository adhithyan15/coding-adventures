## You'll want to know: わざと
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C531-PURPOSE531-01]; assesses=[] -->

**わざと** — *wazato* — "on purpose; deliberately".

> **もし わざと まけたら、みんな おこるでしょう。** — *moshi wazato maketara, minna okoru deshō* — "If you lose on purpose, everyone will be angry."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**わざと** (*wazato*) is a native Japanese word (wago), inherited rather than borrowed from Chinese, and usually written in kana; its older origin is not settled (JMdict).
