# причина в том, что … — giving the reason at length

## Warm-up
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[RU-GRAMMAR-DOLZHEN-01, RU-LEX-POCHEMU-01, RU-LEX-C185-PRICH185-01] -->

[PAUSE 2s] Say "you must ring Mum". (*Ty dólzhen pozvonít máme.*) Then
"a reason". (*Prichína.*) Advice asks for a reason back.

## Grammar Lens: причина в том, что …
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-GRAMMAR-PRICHINA-V-TOM-01]; assesses=[RU-GRAMMAR-CHTO-COMPLEMENTISER-01, RU-LEX-C53-THINGS-02, RU-LEX-C135-ACT135-01] -->

> **— Почему ты не позвонил? — Причина в том, что я потерял телефон.** —
> *pochemú ty ne pozvoníl? prichína v tom, shto ya poteryál telefón* — Why
> didn't you ring? The reason is that I lost my phone.

Word for word, "the reason is in that, that …": **в том** points ahead,
and **что** brings in the explanation, with its comma. It is weightier
than *потому что*.

## Grammar Lens: an explanation in three steps
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-GRAMMAR-EXPLAIN-STEPS-01]; assesses=[RU-LEX-MOCH-01, RU-LEX-POTOMU-CHTO-01, RU-GRAMMAR-POETOMU-RESULT-01] -->

What happened, then **поэтому**, then what followed:

> **Я потерял телефон, поэтому не мог позвонить.** — *ya poteryál
> telefón, poétomu ne mog pozvonít* — I lost my phone, so I couldn't ring.

**Мог** is the past of *мочь*, one of the few without an *л*: *мог*,
*могла*, *могли*.

## Why it's said this way: the aspect of an explanation
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[RU-LEX-C37-PEOPLE-03, RU-LEX-C228-VESHCH228-02, RU-LEX-PONIMAT, RU-GRAMMAR-ASPECT-PARTNER, RU-LEX-MEDLENNO-01, RU-LEX-C174-ACT174-03, RU-GRAMMAR-PAST-IMPERFECTIVE-PROCESS-01, RU-GRAMMAR-PAST-PERFECTIVE-RESULT-01, RU-LEX-C75-NUM1-02, RU-LEX-GOVORIT] -->

> **Учитель говорил быстро, и я не понимал правило. Потом он повторил
> медленно, и я понял.** — *uchítel govoríl býstro, i ya ne ponimál
> právilo. potóm on povtoríl médlenno, i ya pónyal* — The teacher was
> talking fast and I didn't understand the rule. Then he said it again
> slowly, and I understood.

A process with no result, then two deeds done: **повторил**, from the
partner of *повторять*, and **понял**, from *понять*, stressed on the
front: *pónyal*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[RU-LEX-C185-PRICH185-01] -->

**причина** is *при-* with **чин**, by the usual account "order, rank".
EtymDB 2.1 lists
*причина* itself without a parent, and takes *чин* through Old East Slavic
*činŭ* to Proto-Slavic *\*činŭ*. A reason, on this reading, is what set
things in motion.
