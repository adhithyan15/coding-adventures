## You'll want to know: requīrō, requīrere
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C451-PERCONTOR-03]; assesses=[] -->

**requīrō, requīrere** — "to ask after, to look for; to need".

> Hospes caupōnam bonam requīrit.
>
> *The visitor asks after a good inn.*

*requīrō, requīrere, requīsīvī, requīsītum*. English *require* and *request* come from it.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*requīrō* is built from *re-* 'again' and *quaerō* 'to seek, to ask' (Lewis & Short), with the vowel weakened in the compound. English *require* and *request* come from it.
