Portuguese, chapter 242: Cooked, Ripe, Dark, Clear, Distant.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
cozido / cozida — cooked, boiled.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for unknown, then the Portuguese for simple.

You'll want to know: cozido / cozida.
cozido / cozida — "cooked, boiled".

The word, taken apart.
cozido is "cooked": the participle of the verb "to cook", which goes back to Latin coquere, "to cook".

Lesson 4 of 5.
claro / clara — clear, pale (of a colour).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for ripe; mature, then the Portuguese for dark.

You'll want to know: claro / clara.
claro / clara — "clear, pale (of a colour)".

The word, taken apart.
claro from Latin clarus, "bright, clear" (EtymDB 2.1) — semi-learned. It kept its cl-, though Latin cl- usually became ch-; Old Portuguese also said craro.
