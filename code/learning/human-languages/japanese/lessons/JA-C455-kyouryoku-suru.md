## You'll want to know: きょうりょくする
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C455-TEAM455-01]; assesses=[] -->

**きょうりょくする** — *kyōryoku suru* — "to cooperate".

> **みんなで きょうりょくして、しごとを おえました。** — *minna de kyōryoku shite, shigoto o oemashita* — "We all cooperated and finished the work."

Polite **きょうりょくします**, *kyōryokushimasu*; a する verb. Past (polite): **どうりょうが きょうりょくしました。**, *dōryō ga kyōryoku shimashita*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**きょうりょくする** (*kyōryoku suru*) is Sino-Japanese (kango) *kyōryoku* with the native verb *suru*, "to do": two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *kyō*, "cooperation" (Middle Chinese *hep*) and *ryoku*, "power" (*lik*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
