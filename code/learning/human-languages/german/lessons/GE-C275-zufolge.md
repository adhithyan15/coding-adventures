## You'll want to know: zufolge
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C275-ANGEBLICH-03]; assesses=[] -->

**zufolge** — "according to".

> Dem Bericht zufolge gab es keine Verletzten.
>
> *According to the report there were no injured people.*

It follows a dative noun: *dem Bericht zufolge*. Formal and common in the news.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*zufolge* joins *zu* and *Folge* "what follows", from Middle High German *volge* (EtymDB): following the report.
