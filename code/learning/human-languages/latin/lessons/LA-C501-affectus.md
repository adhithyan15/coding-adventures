## You'll want to know: affectus, affectūs
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[LA-LEX-C501-AFFE-03]; assesses=[] -->

**affectus, affectūs** — "a mood, state of mind".

> Affectūs animī in vōce audīmus.
>
> *We hear the moods of the mind in the voice.*

Masculine, fourth declension, from *afficiō*. English *affection* comes from the related *affectiō*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**affectus** is the action noun of *afficiō* "to affect" (EtymDB; Lewis & Short): the state into which something puts the mind.
