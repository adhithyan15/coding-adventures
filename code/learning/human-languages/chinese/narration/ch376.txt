Mandarin Chinese, chapter 376: Out in Public.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
huódòng — an activity; an organised event.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for a volunteer, then the Mandarin for each other.

You'll want to know: huódòng.
huódòng — "an activity; an organised event".
zhège zhōumò gōngyuán lǐ yǒu shénme huódòng — What's on in the park this weekend?
Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

The word, taken apart.
huódòng is built from huó 'alive' and dòng 'to move', both inherited from Middle Chinese, hwat and duwngX (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'not dead' and the second 'to stir'. The sense 'an event' is later and not given there.

Lesson 2 of 5.
社工 (shègōng) — social worker.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for each other, then the Mandarin for an organised event.

You'll want to know: 社工.
社工 — shègōng — "social worker".
我觉得社工的工作很重要 wǒ juéde shègōng de gōngzuò hěn zhòngyào — I think social workers do important work.

The word, taken apart.
社工 (shègōng) is built from 社 'society' and 工 'work', both inherited from Middle Chinese, dzyaeX and kuwng (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which lists the first with the god of grain and glosses the second 'a craft'. The word is a modern short form of 'social work'.

Lesson 5 of 5.
guǎngchǎngwǔ — square dancing.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Mandarin for to treat with care, then the Mandarin for to give up one's seat.

You'll want to know: guǎngchǎngwǔ.
guǎngchǎngwǔ — "square dancing".
wǒ māma měitiān wǎnshang dōu qù tiào guǎngchǎngwǔ — My mum goes square dancing every evening.
Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.
Three third tones meet in this word: guǎng and chǎng are both said with a rising tone before wǔ, and the spelling keeps all three marks.

The word, taken apart.
guǎngchǎngwǔ is built from guǎngchǎng 'a public square' and wǔ 'to dance'. All three parts are inherited from Middle Chinese, kwangX, drjang and mjuX (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'wide' and the second 'a threshing floor'. The dancing itself is a custom of recent decades.

What you've built.
One more word for giving reasons.
