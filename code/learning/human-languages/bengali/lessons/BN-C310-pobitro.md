## You'll want to know: পবিত্র
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C310-SHONKHO310-05]; assesses=[] -->

**পবিত্র** — *pôbitrô* — "holy; sacred".

> এই পুকুরের জল পবিত্র।
>
> *ei pukurer jôl pôbitrô.*
>
> The water of this pond is holy.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**পবিত্র** (*pôbitrô*) is Sanskrit *pavitra*, a means of purifying, a strainer, and so 'pure, holy' (Monier-Williams).
