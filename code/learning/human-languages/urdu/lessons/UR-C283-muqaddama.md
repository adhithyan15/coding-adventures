## You'll want to know: مقدمہ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C283-ADALAT283-03]; assesses=[] -->

**مقدمہ** — *muqaddamā* — "a court case; a lawsuit".

> مقدمہ ایک سال چلا۔
>
> *muqaddamā ek sāl chalā.*
>
> The case went on for a year.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**مقدمہ** (*muqaddamā*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *muqaddama*, "what is put first, a preface"; how it came to mean a court case is not certain. A case **چلتا ہے**: it goes on.
