## You'll want to know: बराबर
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C356-BARABAR356-01]; assesses=[] -->

**बराबर** — *barābar* — "equal".

> क़ानून के सामने सब लोग बराबर हैं।
>
> *qānūn ke sāmne sab log barābar haiṁ.*
>
> Everyone is equal before the law.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**बराबर** (*barābar*) is usually traced to Persian *barābar*, "equal, level".
