## You'll want to know: ঘাট
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C397-GHAT397-01]; assesses=[] -->

**ঘাট** — *ghāṭ* — "a landing place; riverside steps; a ghat".

> সকালে ঘাটে অনেক লোক স্নান করে।
>
> *sôkāle ghāṭe ônek lok snān kôre.*
>
> In the morning many people bathe at the ghat.

The steps down to a river or pond, and the place where boats come in.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ঘাট** (*ghāṭ*) is inherited: CDIAL lists Bengali *ghāṭ*, "a mountain pass, a landing place", under Sanskrit *ghaṭṭa*, "a landing place, a quay" (CDIAL 4414); English *ghat* is the same word, borrowed.
