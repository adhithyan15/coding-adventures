Kannada, chapter 497: Pride and Modesty.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
ಹೊಗಳು (hogaḷu) — to praise.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for eagerness, then the Kannada for relieved.

You'll want to know: ಹೊಗಳು.
ಹೊಗಳು — hogaḷu — "to praise".
ಶಿಕ್ಷಕರು ನನ್ನ ಕಥೆಯನ್ನು ಹೊಗಳಿದರು. śikṣakaru nanna katheyannu hogaḷidaru. The teacher praised my story.
Past (I …): ಹೊಗಳಿದೆ, hogaḷide.
What is praised takes -ಅನ್ನು. ಹೊಗಳಿಕೆ is praise.

The word, taken apart.
ಹೊಗಳು (hogaḷu) is native Dravidian: DEDR 4235 lists an older Kannada form in p-, "to praise; praise, renown". Old p- became h- in the modern language.

What you've built.
One more word for describing what you lived and felt.

[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for relieved, then the Kannada for to praise.

You'll want to know: ಅಭಿಮಾನ.
ಅಭಿಮಾನ — abhimāna — "pride; fond regard".
ನಮ್ಮ ಭಾಷೆಯ ಬಗ್ಗೆ ನಮಗೆ ಅಭಿಮಾನ ಇದೆ. namma bhāṣeya bagge namage abhimāna ide. We are proud of our language.
Warm pride in what you love; ಅಭಿಮಾನಿ is a fan.

The word, taken apart.
ಅಭಿಮಾನ (abhimāna) is borrowed from Sanskrit abhimāna, "pride" (CDIAL 524), from the root man, "to think".

Lesson 3 of 6.
ಅಹಂಕಾರ (ahaṅkāra) — arrogance; conceit.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for to praise, then the Kannada for pride.

You'll want to know: ಅಹಂಕಾರ.
ಅಹಂಕಾರ — ahaṅkāra — "arrogance; conceit".
ಹಣ ಬಂದ ಮೇಲೆ ಅವನಿಗೆ ಅಹಂಕಾರ ಬಂದಿತು. haṇa banda mēle avanige ahaṅkāra banditu. Once the money came, he grew arrogant.
From ಅಹಂ, I, and ಕಾರ, making: making everything about "I".

The word, taken apart.
ಅಹಂಕಾರ (ahaṅkāra) is borrowed from Sanskrit ahaṃkāra, "the making of self, egotism, pride" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 5 of 6.
ಸ್ವಾಭಿಮಾನ (svābhimāna) — self-respect.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for arrogance, then the Kannada for modesty.

You'll want to know: ಸ್ವಾಭಿಮಾನ.
ಸ್ವಾಭಿಮಾನ — svābhimāna — "self-respect".
ಅವನು ಸ್ವಾಭಿಮಾನದಿಂದ ಆ ಹಣವನ್ನು ನಿರಾಕರಿಸಿದನು. avanu svābhimānadinda ā haṇavannu nirākarisidanu. Out of self-respect he refused that money.
ಸ್ವ, self + ಅಭಿಮಾನ (abhimāna), pride: the word from earlier in this chapter, turned on yourself.

The word, taken apart.
ಸ್ವಾಭಿಮಾನ (svābhimāna) is a compound built from Sanskrit: sva, "own, self", and abhimāna, "pride" (CDIAL 524).
