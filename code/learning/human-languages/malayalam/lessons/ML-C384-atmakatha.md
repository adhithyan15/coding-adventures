## You'll want to know: ആത്മകഥ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C384-JANANAM384-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ആത്മകഥ** — *ātmakatha* — "an autobiography".

> ആ നടന്റെ ആത്മകഥ ഒരുപാട് പേർ വായിച്ചു.
>
> *ā naṭanṟe ātmakatha orupāṭŭ pēr vāyiccu.*
>
> Many people read that actor's autobiography.

ആത്മ-, self, + കഥ, a story: the story of oneself.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ആത്മകഥ** (*ātmakatha*) is built from two Sanskrit words, *ātman*, "breath, soul, self" (CDIAL 1135), and *kathā*, "speech, a story" (CDIAL 2704), the source of കഥ (*katha*).
