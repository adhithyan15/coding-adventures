## You'll want to know: দূষণ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C434-PROKRITI434-03]; assesses=[] -->

**দূষণ** — *dūshôn* — "pollution".

> শহরে গাড়ির ধোঁয়ায় দূষণ হয়।
>
> *shôhôre gāṛir dhõyāy dūshôn hôy.*
>
> In the city car fumes cause pollution.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**দূষণ** (*dūshôn*) is Sanskrit *dūṣaṇa*, "spoiling, corrupting, vitiating" (Monier-Williams); the sense "pollution" is a modern use.
