## You'll want to know: zhāng
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C88-ZHANG-01]; assesses=[] -->

*zhāng* — a measure word for flat things: tables, beds, tickets.

> *yì zhāng zhuōzi* — a table

> *liǎng zhāng piào* — two tickets

Its character comes later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*zhāng* is inherited from Middle Chinese *trjang* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), glossed 'to stretch out; to spread'. Counting things that lie spread flat is a later use, not given there.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ZH-LEX-C87-CHUANG-01] -->

A table, a bed, a ticket: things with a flat top or a flat face take *zhāng*.
