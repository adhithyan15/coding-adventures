## You'll want to know: प्रियंवदः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C467-SISTA467-05]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रियंवदः** — *priyaṃvadaḥ* — "affable; kindly spoken".

> प्रियंवदा मीरा अतिथिना सह मधुरं वदति।
>
> *priyaṃvadā mīrā atithinā saha madhuraṃ vadati.*
>
> Affable Mira chats sweetly with the guest.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रियंवदः** (*priyaṃvadaḥ*) is built from **प्रियः**, "dear, pleasant", and *vada*, "speaking", from the root √vad of **वदति**: one who says pleasant things; MW gives "speaking kindly, agreeable, affable" (MW).

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for describing what you lived and felt.
