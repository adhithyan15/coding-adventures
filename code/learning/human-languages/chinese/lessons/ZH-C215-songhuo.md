## You'll want to know: sònghuò
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C215-DELIVER215-01]; assesses=[] -->

*sònghuò* — "to deliver goods".

> *tāmen míngtiān sònghuò* — They're delivering tomorrow.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*sònghuò* is built from *sòng* 'to send' and *huò* 'goods', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *suwngH* and *xwaH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which glosses the first 'to send off'.
