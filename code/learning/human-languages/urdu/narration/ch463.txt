Urdu, chapter 463: Talkers and Quiet Ones.
6 lessons. All 6 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 6.
بڑبڑانا (baṛbaṛānā) — to mutter; to grumble.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for innocent, then the Urdu for to be led astray.

You'll want to know: بڑبڑانا.
بڑبڑانا — baṛbaṛānā — "to mutter; to grumble".
دادا اکیلے بیٹھ کر بڑبڑاتے ہیں۔ dādā akele baiṭh kar baṛbaṛāte haiṅ. My grandfather sits on his own and mutters.
Past (he …): وہ کچھ بڑبڑایا, voh kuchh baṛbaṛāyā.

The word, taken apart.
بڑبڑانا (baṛbaṛānā) is inherited from baḍabaḍa, "grumble, mutter", a form CDIAL reconstructs and treats as an imitation of the sound (CDIAL 9122).

Lesson 4 of 6.
سرگوشی کرنا (sargoshī karnā) — to whisper.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for idle chat, then the Urdu for hushed.

You'll want to know: سرگوشی کرنا.
سرگوشی کرنا — sargoshī karnā — "to whisper".
دونوں سہیلیاں کلاس میں سرگوشی کر رہی تھیں۔ donoṅ saheliyāṅ klās meṅ sargoshī kar rahī thīṅ. The two friends were whispering in class.
Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے کان میں سرگوشی کی, us ne kān meṅ sargoshī kī.

The word, taken apart.
سرگوشی کرنا (sargoshī karnā) is built from سرگوشی, usually traced to Persian sargōshī, "a whisper", from sar, "head", and gōsh, "ear", and کرنا, "to do".
