## Added — chapters 261-270, the B1 core that realizes all five B1 spine nodes

- Ten chapters and 34 lessons (sequences 16000-16330), two chapters per B1
  node, each chapter ending on a short review. Every B1 spine concept has its
  own concept lesson, so the five spine ledgers (`curriculum.d/spine/0250` to
  `0290`) no longer omit anything. Paths and extensions RU-PATH/RU-EXT-261 to
  270 (ranks 2660-2750), all `stage: "B1"`.
- **261-262, SPINE-NARRATE-EVENTS.** The track had only shown the past in one
  word lesson, so RU-C261-chital teaches it first: -ть becomes -л, agreeing as
  -л, -ла, -ло, -ли, and был, была, было, были. RU-C261-prochital
  (ASPECT-ONGOING-PAST) sets читал against прочитал: the imperfective for the
  process, a habit or the bare fact (Ты читал эту книгу?), the perfective for
  one finished deed with a result. RU-C261-poka joins two halves: пока for two
  scenes at once, когда with a perfective for the moment that cut in or came
  next. RU-C262-snachala-potom (CONNECTIVE-THEN) adds затем to the taught
  сначала, потом, наконец and the chain of perfectives; RU-C262-rasskazat
  (NARRATIVE-SEQUENCE) teaches the partner of рассказывать and the scene,
  steps, end frame; RU-C262-chtenie is a 92-word story in the past.
- **263-264, SPINE-GIVE-REASONS.** по-моему and мне кажется, что beside the
  taught я думаю, что (OPINION-I-THINK); поэтому and так что against потому
  что (CONNECTIVE-SO); тебе стоит, лучше and ты должен with an infinitive
  (MODAL-SHOULD); причина в том, что … and an explanation in three steps
  (EXPLANATION-GIVE). It uses the taught причина rather than объяснить,
  because the track has not taught ъ and letter anchoring allows no letter
  that is read but never written.
- **265-266, SPINE-HANDLE-TRAVEL.** Извините, как пройти к …? with к and the
  dative (DIRECTION-ASK); идите прямо, поверните направо, до светофора;
  билет туда и обратно, до a place and на a train (TRANSPORT-TICKET); я
  опоздал на поезд and the perfective past for a problem (PROBLEM-REPORT).
- **267-268, SPINE-DESCRIBE-EXPERIENCE.** я был рад, я устал, мне было скучно
  (ADJECTIVE-FEELING); мечтать and я надеюсь, что … будет (AMBITION-EXPRESS);
  -ее with чем and the six learned whole, больше, меньше, лучше, хуже, дороже,
  дешевле (COMPARISON-BASIC); уже with the present, … назад with the past, and
  год, года, лет (TIME-DURATION).
- **269-270, SPINE-EXPRESS-CONDITION.** если with a comma at the seam, and ли
  for "whether" (CONNECTIVE-IF); если with the future on both sides and the
  perfective future (CONDITIONAL-REAL); это зависит от … with the genitive
  (CONDITION-DEPENDS); я бы хотел and вы не могли бы …? (MODAL-WOULD); если бы
  … бы for what is not so; RU-W270-pismo, six connected lines about a trip.
- Six new headwords: рассказать, как пройти к …?, билет туда и обратно, я
  опоздал на поезд, мечтать, это зависит от … . Short function
  words (бы, рад, должен, лучше, чем, затем) are taught inside grammar
  lessons, which register no forward-reference matcher.
- Etymologies cite EtymDB 2.1 where it carries the word (быть, идти, тот,
  мой, так, вид, казать, чин, долг, правый, вертеть, висеть, рад, мечта,
  лето, год, к)
  and Lewis & Short for Latin dependere; elsewhere they are
  marked as the usual account or the plain reading of the parts. EtymDB's
  entry for если glosses its есть as "to eat", and the lesson says so.
- Every new atom is practised by at least two later lessons.

An independent review then fixed errors in 11 of the 34 lessons. Every past
form, aspect pair and agreement held. The fixes were:
- *моя* in the reading, which no lesson had taught, and an unflagged *Москве*;
- two later headwords (*долг*, *право*) named in an etymology;
- the *мог / могла / могли* claim (only *мог* lacks the л);
- the *пока* / *когда* split, now a leaning rather than a law;
- an EtymDB attribution for *обратный*.

Chapter 266 then gained two lessons, so the B1 vocabulary can use the letter ъ,
which the track had never taught:
- RU-C266-obyavleniye, *объявление*, "an announcement", met at the station;
- RU-W266-tvyordyy-znak, the letter lesson for ъ, anchored in that word.
