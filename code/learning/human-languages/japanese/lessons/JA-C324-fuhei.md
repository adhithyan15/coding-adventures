## You'll want to know: ふへい
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[JA-LEX-C324-MONKU324-03]; assesses=[] -->

**ふへい** — *fuhei* — "discontent; grumbling".

> **しごとが おおいので、みんな ふへいを 言っています。** — *shigoto ga ōi node, minna fuhei o itte imasu* — "There is a lot of work, so everyone is grumbling."

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ふへい** (*fuhei*) is Sino-Japanese (kango): two Chinese characters read with their on'yomi, readings borrowed from Chinese. They are *fu*, "negative" (Middle Chinese *pjut*) and *hei*, "even" (*bjaeng*) (KANJIDIC2; Middle Chinese in Baxter's transcription).
