## You'll want to know: रुचिः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C516-AVAKASA516-05]; assesses=[] -->

**रुचिः** — *ruciḥ* — "a liking".

> मम भगिन्याः संगीते रुचिः अस्ति।
>
> *mama bhaginyāḥ saṅgīte ruciḥ asti.*
>
> My sister has a liking for music.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**रुचिः** (*ruciḥ*) comes from the root √ruc, "to shine, to be pleasing", which also gives *rocate*; MW gives *ruci* as light, and as liking, taste, relish (MW).
