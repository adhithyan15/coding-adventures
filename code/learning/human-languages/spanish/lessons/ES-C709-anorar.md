## You'll want to know: añorar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C709-SOLEDAD-05]; assesses=[] -->

**añorar** — "to long for, to yearn for".

> Añoro los veranos de mi infancia.
>
> *I long for the summers of my childhood.*

A little literary; it takes a direct object: *añoro mi casa*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*añorar* was borrowed from Catalan *enyorar*, which comes from Latin *ignōrāre* "not to know" (EtymDB); the sense moved from not knowing where someone is to missing them.
