## You'll want to know: déranger
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C431-RECLAMATION-04]; assesses=[] -->

**déranger** — "to disturb, to bother".

> La musique des voisins nous a dérangés toute la nuit.
>
> *The neighbours' music disturbed us all night.*

*Ne pas déranger* — do not disturb, on a hotel door.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*déranger* comes from Old French *desrengier* 'to throw out of line', from *des-* and *rengier* 'to put in a row', built on *reng* 'row' (EtymDB).
