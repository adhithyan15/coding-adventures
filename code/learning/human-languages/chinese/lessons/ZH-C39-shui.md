## You'll want to know: 水
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C39-DRINK39-02]; assesses=[] -->

**水** — *shuǐ* — "water".

> **我喜欢喝水** *wǒ xǐhuan hē shuǐ* — I like drinking water.

The new character, in pieces: 水 — a hooked vertical down the middle, one stroke on its left that runs across and falls away, and two strokes on its right. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**水** (*shuǐ*) is inherited from Middle Chinese *sywijX* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription), which the dictionary explains as one of the five elements, the one of the north, and as 'level'.
