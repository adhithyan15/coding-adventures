# وزارة (wizāra) — a ministry

## You'll want to know: وزارة
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[AR-LEX-C377-HUKUMA377-03]; assesses=[] -->

**وزارة** — *wizāra* — "a ministry".

> تعمل أمي في وزارة الصحة.
>
> *taʿmalu ummī fī wizārat aṣ-ṣiḥḥa.*
>
> My mother works at the ministry of health.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**وزارة** (*wizāra*) is built on the native Arabic root *w-z-r*, "to bear a heavy load" (Lane). *Wizāra* is the office of a *wazīr*: a ministry.
