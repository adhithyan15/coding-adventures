## You'll want to know: उत्पादन
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C512-KARKHANA512-02]; assesses=[] -->

**उत्पादन** — *utpādan* — "production; output".

> या कारखान्यात रोज हजार सायकलींचं उत्पादन होतं.
>
> *yā kārkhānyāt roj hajār sāyaklīñcaṁ utpādan hotaṁ.*
>
> A thousand bicycles are made in this factory every day.

Neuter.

उत्पादन होणे, to be produced.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**उत्पादन** (*utpādan*) is a tatsama, taken from Sanskrit *utpādana*, "bringing forth, producing" (Monier-Williams).
