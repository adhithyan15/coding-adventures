## You'll want to know: ответственность
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C390-PORYADOK-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ответственность** (*otvétstvennost*) — "responsibility".

> Родители несут ответственность за детей.
>
> *Rodíteli nesút otvétstvennost za detéy.*
>
> Parents bear responsibility for their children.

Feminine. *Нести ответственность за* + accusative — to bear responsibility for.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ответственность** is built on *ответственный* "responsible", from *ответ* "an answer", from *от-* "back" and Old Russian *větŭ* "counsel": having to answer for something.
