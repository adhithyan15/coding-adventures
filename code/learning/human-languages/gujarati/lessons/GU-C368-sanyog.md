## You'll want to know: સંયોગ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C368-NASIB368-03]; assesses=[] -->

**સંયોગ** — *sanyog* — "a coincidence".

> જો આ સંયોગ ન હોત તો અમે એક જ ટ્રેનમાં ન હોત.
>
> *jo ā sanyog na hot to ame ek ja ṭrenmā̃ na hot.*
>
> But for this coincidence, we would not have been on the same train.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**સંયોગ** (*sanyog*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *saṃyoga*, "a joining, a connection" (Monier-Williams). **સંજોગ** (*sanjog*), "a circumstance", is the same word in an older, inherited shape.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
