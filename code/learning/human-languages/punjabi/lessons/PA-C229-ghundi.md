## You'll want to know: ਘੁੰਡੀ
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PA-LEX-C229-DIN229-05]; assesses=[] -->

**ਘੁੰਡੀ** — *ghuṇḍī* — "a knotted cloth button".

> ਮੇਰੇ ਕੁੜਤੇ ਉੱਤੇ ਤਿੰਨ ਘੁੰਡੀਆਂ ਹਨ।
>
> *mere kuṛte utte tinn ghuṇḍīāṁ han.*
>
> My kurta has three cloth buttons.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ਘੁੰਡੀ** (*ghuṇḍī*) is inherited from a reconstructed *\*ghuṇṭa*, "knot, tag, button"; CDIAL lists Punjabi *ghuṇḍī*, "knot, button" (CDIAL 4483).
