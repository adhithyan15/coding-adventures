Lesson 1 of 5.
la cartella — a folder, a school bag.

Warm-up.

You'll want to know: la cartella.
la cartella — "a folder, a school bag".

The word, taken apart.
cartella — built from carta, "paper", with -ella, "little": a holder for papers. carta from Latin charta, "paper". (EtymDB 2.1).

Lesson 2 of 5.
la busta paga — a pay slip.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for a queue, then the Italian for a folder.

You'll want to know: la busta paga.
la busta paga — "a pay slip".

The phrase, taken apart.
busta paga is "pay envelope": busta from Old French boiste, "a box"; paga, "pay", is the noun of pagare, "to pay", which EtymDB 2.1 traces to Latin pax, "peace": paying a creditor made peace with him.
