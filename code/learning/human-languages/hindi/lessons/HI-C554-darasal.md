## You'll want to know: दरअसल
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C554-NATIJATAN554-05]; assesses=[] -->

**दरअसल** — *darasal* — "actually; in fact".

> दरअसल मैं कल बीमार था।
>
> *Darasal maiṁ kal bīmār thā.*
>
> Actually, I was ill yesterday.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**दरअसल** (*darasal*) is usually traced to Persian *dar-aṣl*, "in origin, in fact".

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for saying what happens if.
