Malayalam, chapter 139: How Long?.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
സമയം (samayaṁ) — time.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Malayalam for a tiger, then the Malayalam for a rabbit.

You'll want to know: സമയം.
സമയം (samayaṁ) — "time". സമയം എത്ര? — "what time is it?"

What you've built.
The question on every street.

Guided Practice.
[pause 1 second]
[your turn — say: samayaṁ]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: samayaṁ, once more]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — say: ask samayaṁ etra?]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]
[your turn — recall: say the Malayalam for a tiger, then the Malayalam for a rabbit, then say samayaṁ again]
[pause 8 seconds for the answer]

Wrap-up Recall.
[pause 3 seconds]
What does സമയം (samayaṁ) mean? ("Time".) Say it once more.

മിനിറ്റ് (miniṟṟŭ) — "a minute", from English. ഒരു മിനിറ്റ് — "one minute", said with a raised hand.

What you've built.
The polite way to ask for a moment.

Before the new one: say the Malayalam for time, then the Malayalam for a minute.

You'll want to know: മണിക്കൂർ.
മണിക്കൂർ (maṇikkūr) — "an hour". It begins with മണി, the word in ഒരു മണി, "one o'clock".

What you've built.
