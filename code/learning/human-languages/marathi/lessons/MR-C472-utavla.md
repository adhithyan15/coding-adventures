## You'll want to know: उतावळा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C472-HATTI472-04]; assesses=[] -->

**उतावळा** — *utāvḷā* — "hasty; rash".

> उतावळा माणूस घाईत चुका करतो.
>
> *utāvḷā māṇūs ghāīt cukā karto.*
>
> A hasty person makes mistakes in a rush.

Agrees: उतावळा, उतावळी, उतावळं.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**उतावळा** (*utāvḷā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi *utāvḷā*, "impetuous", under the reconstructed *\*uttāpala* (CDIAL 1788).
