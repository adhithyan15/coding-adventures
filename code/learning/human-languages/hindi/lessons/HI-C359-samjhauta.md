## You'll want to know: समझौता
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C359-MUDDA359-02]; assesses=[] -->

**समझौता** — *samjhautā* — "a compromise, a settlement".

> आख़िर दोनों परिवारों में समझौता हुआ।
>
> *ākhir donõ parivāroṁ meṁ samjhautā huā.*
>
> In the end the two families reached a settlement.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**समझौता** (*samjhautā*) is a noun made on समझना, "to understand", which CDIAL traces to Sanskrit *saṃbudhyate*, "understands" (CDIAL 12959): an understanding reached between two sides.
