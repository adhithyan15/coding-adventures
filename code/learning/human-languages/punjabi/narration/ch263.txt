Punjabi, chapter 263: Right, Left and the Bend.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
ਮੋੜ (moṛ) — a bend in the road; a turning.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for a receipt, then the Punjabi for to pay off.

You'll want to know: ਮੋੜ.
ਮੋੜ — moṛ — "a bend in the road; a turning".
ਅਗਲੇ ਮੋੜ ਉੱਤੇ ਇੱਕ ਦੁਕਾਨ ਹੈ। agle moṛ utte ikk dukān hai. There is a shop at the next bend.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
ਮੋੜ (moṛ) is inherited from Sanskrit muṭati, "twists"; CDIAL lists Punjabi moṛ, "turn in a road", under it (CDIAL 10186).

Lesson 3 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for a bend in the road, then the Punjabi for to turn.

You'll want to know: ਪਾਸਾ.
ਪਾਸਾ — pāsā — "a side".
ਸਕੂਲ ਸੜਕ ਦੇ ਦੂਜੇ ਪਾਸੇ ਹੈ। sakūl saṛak de dūje pāse hai. The school is on the other side of the road.

Lesson 5 of 5.
ਖੱਬਾ (khabbā) — left-hand; on the left.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Punjabi for a side, then the Punjabi for right-hand.

You'll want to know: ਖੱਬਾ.
ਖੱਬਾ — khabbā — "left-hand; on the left".
ਮੋੜ ਤੋਂ ਖੱਬੇ ਪਾਸੇ ਮੁੜੋ। moṛ ton khabbe pāse muṛo. At the bend, turn to the left.

The word, taken apart.
ਖੱਬਾ (khabbā) is perhaps inherited from Sanskrit kharva, "mutilated, imperfect"; CDIAL lists Punjabi khabbā, "mutilated, imperfect", and a Sindhi form meaning "left-handed" under it (CDIAL 3832). How the sense "left" arose is not settled.
