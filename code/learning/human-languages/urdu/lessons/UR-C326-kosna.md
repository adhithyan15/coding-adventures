## You'll want to know: کوسنا
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[UR-LEX-C326-LANAT326-02]; assesses=[] -->

**کوسنا** — *kosnā* — "to curse; to rail at".

> دادی نے بادشاہ کو کوسا۔
>
> *dādī ne bādshāh ko kosā.*
>
> Grandmother cursed the king.

Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے اسے کوسا, *us ne use kosā*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**کوسنا** (*kosnā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists the Hindi cousin *kosnā*, "to curse", under Sanskrit *krṓśati*, "cries out" (CDIAL 3612).
