## You'll want to know: a recuperação
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C488-CURA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**a recuperação** — "recovery".

> A recuperação da perna partida demorou três meses.
>
> *The recovery from the broken leg took three months.*

Built on *recuperar*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*recuperação* is built on *recuperar*, a learned borrowing from Latin *recuperāre* "to regain" (EtymDB); compare Latin *recuperātiō*.
