## You'll want to know: храпеть
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C488-NIGHT-02]; assesses=[] -->

**храпеть** (*khrapét*) — "to snore".

> Мой муж так храпит, что я не могу заснуть.
>
> *Moy muzh tak khrapít, chto ya ne mogú zasnút.*
>
> My husband snores so loudly that I can't get to sleep.

Imperfective; present храплю, храпишь (п becomes пл). The noun is *храп* — snoring.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

EtymDB lists *храпеть*, glossed 'to snore', without a source; it is usually taken as imitative.
