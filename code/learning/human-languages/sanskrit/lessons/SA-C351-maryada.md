## You'll want to know: मर्यादा
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C351-VIVEKA351-05]; assesses=[] -->

**मर्यादा** — *maryādā* — "the bounds of propriety".

> बालः मर्यादां न त्यजेत्।
>
> *bālaḥ maryādāṃ na tyajet.*
>
> A child should not overstep what is proper.

A feminine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**मर्यादा** (*maryādā*) MW gives *maryādā* "a limit, a boundary" and "the bounds of morality and propriety"; MW doubts whether it comes from *maryā* with *dā* or with *ādā*, so its origin is uncertain (MW).
