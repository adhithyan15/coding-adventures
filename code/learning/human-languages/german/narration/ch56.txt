German, chapter 56: Leg, Tooth, Neck, Shoulder, Knee.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
das Bein — a leg.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to open, then the German for to close.

You'll want to know: das Bein.
