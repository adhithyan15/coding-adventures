## You'll want to know: ઝટકો
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C398-DOZE398-04]; assesses=[] -->

**ઝટકો** — *jhaṭko* — "a jolt; a jerk".

> ટ્રેન અચાનક અટકી અને ડબ્બામાં ઝટકો લાગ્યો.
>
> *ṭren acānak aṭkī ane ḍabbāmā̃ jhaṭko lāgyo.*
>
> The train stopped suddenly and the carriage gave a jolt.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**ઝટકો** (*jhaṭko*) seems to be built on ઝટ, "quickly, at once", which CDIAL lists for Gujarati under *\*jhaṭ*, a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 5327): a sudden quick shake.
