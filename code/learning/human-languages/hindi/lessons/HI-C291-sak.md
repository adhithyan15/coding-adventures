## You'll want to know: शक
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C291-GAVAHI291-04]; assesses=[] -->

**शक** — *śak* — "suspicion; doubt".

> मुझे उस आदमी पर शक है।
>
> *mujhe us ādmī par śak hai.*
>
> I suspect that man.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**शक** (*śak*) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic *shakk*, "doubt". The person you suspect takes **पर**: **मुझे उस पर शक है**.
