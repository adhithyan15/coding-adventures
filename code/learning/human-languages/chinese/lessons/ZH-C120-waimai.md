## You'll want to know: wàimài
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C120-TAKEAWAY120-01]; assesses=[] -->

*wàimài* — "takeaway; food delivered".

> *wǒmen jīntiān wǎnshang chī wàimài* — We're having a takeaway tonight.

Its characters come later; for now it is a word for the ear.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*wàimài* is built from 外 'outside' and mài 'to sell', both inherited from Middle Chinese, *ngwajH* and *meaH* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription): food sold to be eaten somewhere else.
