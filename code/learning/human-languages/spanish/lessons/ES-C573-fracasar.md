## You'll want to know: fracasar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C573-LOGRO-02]; assesses=[] -->

**fracasar** — "to fail".

> El proyecto fracasó por falta de dinero.
>
> *The project failed for lack of money.*

Used for plans and projects; to fail an exam is *suspender*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*fracasar* was borrowed from Italian *fracassare* 'to smash, to shatter' (EtymDB). A plan that fails is smashed.
