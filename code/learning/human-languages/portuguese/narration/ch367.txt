Portuguese, chapter 367: Dar uma mão.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
a comunidade — the community.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for the immigration, then the Portuguese for the integration.

You'll want to know: a comunidade.
a comunidade — "the community".
A comunidade do bairro organizou uma festa. The neighbourhood community organised a party.
A comunidade portuguesa — the Portuguese community abroad.

The word, taken apart.
comunidade is a learned borrowing from Latin commūnitās "community, fellowship" (EtymDB, Lewis & Short), built on commūnis "common".

What you've built.
One more word for telling, explaining and describing at B1.

Lesson 3 of 5.
o voluntário — the volunteer; voluntary.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for the community, then the Portuguese for the association.

You'll want to know: o voluntário.
o voluntário — "the volunteer; voluntary".
Os voluntários limparam a praia no sábado. The volunteers cleaned the beach on Saturday.
As an adjective, voluntary: trabalho voluntário. Feminine a voluntária.

The word, taken apart.
voluntário is a learned borrowing from Latin voluntārius "willing, of free will, voluntary" (EtymDB, Lewis & Short), built on voluntās "will", from velle "to want".
