## You'll want to know: માણવું
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C253-HOBBY253-03]; assesses=[] -->

**માણવું** — *māṇvũ* — "to enjoy, to relish".

> **અમે ગઈકાલે રજા માણી.** — *ame gaīkāle rajā māṇī* — "We enjoyed the holiday yesterday."

Not the taught **માનવું**, *mānvũ*, "to believe".

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**માણવું** (*māṇvũ*) is a tadbhava. CDIAL lists it, glossed "to enjoy", under Sanskrit *mānáyati*, "esteems, honours" (CDIAL 10047).
