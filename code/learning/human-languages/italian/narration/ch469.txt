Italian, chapter 469: Vento e tempesta.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
la bufera — the blizzard, the storm.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for the dew, then the Italian for to deep-freeze.

You'll want to know: la bufera.
la bufera — "the blizzard, the storm".
In montagna c'è stata una bufera di neve. In the mountains there was a snowstorm.
Una bufera di vento — a gale.

The word, taken apart.
bufera is built on bufare 'to blow' (EtymDB), a word that imitates a gust of wind; the origin is not settled beyond that.

Lesson 5 of 5.
il nubifragio — the cloudburst, the downpour.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Italian for the gust, then the Italian for to blow.

You'll want to know: il nubifragio.
il nubifragio — "the cloudburst, the downpour".
Il nubifragio ha allagato le strade. The cloudburst flooded the streets.
A lot of rain in a short time.

The word, taken apart.
nubifragio is a learned borrowing from Medieval Latin nūbifragium (EtymDB), from nūbēs 'cloud' and frangere 'to break': the cloud breaks.
