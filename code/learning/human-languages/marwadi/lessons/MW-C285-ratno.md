## You'll want to know: रटणो
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MW-LEX-C285-AKHAR285-04]; assesses=[] -->

**रटणो** — *raṭṇo* — "to learn by heart".

> छोरो पाठ रटै है।
>
> *chhoro pāṭh raṭai hai.*
>
> The boy learns the lesson by heart.

Past (I …): म्हैं पाठ रट्यो, *mhaĩ pāṭh raṭyo*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**रटणो** (*raṭṇo*) is inherited from Sanskrit *raṭati*, "cries, yells"; CDIAL gives no Marwari form in this entry, but lists Hindi *raṭnā*, "to cry out, repeat" (CDIAL 10590). Saying a thing over and over is how it is learnt by heart.
