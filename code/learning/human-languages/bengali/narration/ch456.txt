Bengali, chapter 456: A Person's Character.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
চরিত্র (chôritrô) — character; moral nature.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Bengali for eager, then the Bengali for to be able to wait.

You'll want to know: চরিত্র.
চরিত্র — chôritrô — "character; moral nature".
লোকটার চরিত্র ভালো নয়। lokṭār chôritrô bhālo nôy. That man does not have a good character.
চরিত্র (chôritrô) is also a character in a story: গল্পের চরিত্র (chôritrô).

The word, taken apart.
চরিত্র (chôritrô) is a tatsama, Sanskrit caritra, "going", then "behaviour, deeds" and "nature, disposition" (Monier-Williams).

অরুণ তিন মাসে সাঁতার রপ্ত করল। ôrun tin māse sā̃tār rôptô kôrlô. Arun mastered swimming in three months.
Past (he/she …): তিনি কাজটা রপ্ত করলেন, tini kājṭā rôptô kôrlen.

The word, taken apart.
রপ্ত করা (rôptô kôrā) is rôptô, "practised, mastered", whose origin is not settled, with kôrā, "to do" (CDIAL 2814).
