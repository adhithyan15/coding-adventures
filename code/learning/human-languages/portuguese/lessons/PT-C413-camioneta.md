## You'll want to know: a camioneta
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[PT-LEX-C413-PARAGEM-03]; assesses=[] -->

**a camioneta** — "the coach, the long-distance bus".

> Fomos para o sul de camioneta.
>
> *We went south by coach.*

In Portugal *a camioneta* runs between towns and *o autocarro* inside a town. In Brazil: *o ônibus*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*camioneta* is a small form of *camião* 'lorry', which was borrowed from French *camion* 'truck' (EtymDB); the French word's origin is not settled.
