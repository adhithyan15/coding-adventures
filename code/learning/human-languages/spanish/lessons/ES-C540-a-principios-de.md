## You'll want to know: a principios de
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C540-TEMPORADA-01]; assesses=[] -->

**a principios de** — "at the beginning of".

> Llegaron a principios de junio.
>
> *They arrived at the beginning of June.*

Used with months, years and centuries: *a principios del siglo XX*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*a principios de* is built on *principio*, from Latin *prīncipium* 'a beginning' (Lewis & Short), which comes from *prīnceps* 'first'.
