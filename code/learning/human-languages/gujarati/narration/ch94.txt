Gujarati, chapter 94: Autumn, Early, Every day, Date, Right now.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
પાનખર (pānkhar) — autumn.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for the monsoon, then the Gujarati for spring.

You'll want to know: પાનખર.
પાનખર (pānkhar) — "autumn".

The word, taken apart.
પાનખર (pānkhar) is "leaf-fall": pān, "leaf", from Sanskrit parṇa (CDIAL 7918), and the stem of kharvũ, "to fall, fade", from kṣarati, "trickles; melts away" (CDIAL 3663).

Lesson 4 of 5.
તારીખ (tārīkh) — a date.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for early, then the Gujarati for every day.

You'll want to know: તારીખ.
તારીખ (tārīkh) — "a date".

The word, taken apart.
તારીખ (tārīkh) is a loan from Arabic tārīkh, "a dating, a date", which reached Gujarati through Persian. Its Arabic root is ʾ-r-kh (Lane).

Lesson 5 of 5.
હમણાં (hamṇā̃) — right now, a moment ago.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Gujarati for every day, then the Gujarati for a date.
[pause 1 second]
Say "tea too." (Chā paṇ: straight after a noun, paṇ means "also.")

You'll want to know: હમણાં.
હમણાં (hamṇā̃) — "right now, a moment ago".

The word, taken apart.
CDIAL lists Gujarati hamṇo, "now", under Sanskrit adhunā, "now", through a form haũṇ- (CDIAL 268). હમણાં (hamṇā̃) (hamṇāṁ) is another form of that word.
