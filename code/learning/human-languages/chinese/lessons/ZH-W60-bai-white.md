## Script — a short fall over 日
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-SCRIPT-BAI-WHITE-01]; assesses=[] -->

> 白

**Two pieces, top to bottom.**

**A short left-falling stroke** at the top, from the right down to the left.

**日, four strokes**, the same four your hand makes in 日: the left side, the top that turns down the right side, the middle bar, and the bar that closes the bottom.

1 + 4 = five.
