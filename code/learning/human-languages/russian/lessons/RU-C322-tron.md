## You'll want to know: трон
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C322-RYTSAR-05]; assesses=[] -->

**трон** (*tron*) — "a throne".

> Король сидел на золотом троне.
>
> *Koról sidél na zolotóm tróne.*
>
> The king sat on a golden throne.

*Взойти на трон* — to come to the throne.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*трон* came through Latin *thronus* "throne" from Greek *thrónos* "a chair, a throne" (EtymDB).
