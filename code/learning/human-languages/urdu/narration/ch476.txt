Urdu, chapter 476: At the Doctor's.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
معائنہ کرنا (muāinā karnā) — to examine (a patient).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for cancer, then the Urdu for afflicted.

You'll want to know: معائنہ کرنا.
معائنہ کرنا — muāinā karnā — "to examine (a patient)".
ڈاکٹر نے مریض کا معائنہ کیا۔ ḍākṭar ne marīz kā muāinā kiyā. The doctor examined the patient.
Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے بچے کا معائنہ کیا, us ne bachche kā muāinā kiyā.

The word, taken apart.
معائنہ کرنا (muāinā karnā) is built from معائنہ, usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic muʿāyana, "looking at closely", and کرنا, "to do".

Lesson 4 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a medical test, then the Urdu for a diagnosis.

You'll want to know: حکیم.
حکیم — hakīm — "a traditional physician".
دادا حکیم کی دوا کھاتے ہیں۔ dādā hakīm kī davā khāte haiṅ. My grandfather takes medicine from a traditional physician.
Masculine.

The word, taken apart.
حکیم (hakīm) is usually traced, through Persian, to Arabic ḥakīm, "a wise man"; in Urdu it is the healer of the old Greek and Arabic medicine.

Lesson 5 of 5.
بلوانا (bulvānā) — to have (someone) sent for.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Urdu for a diagnosis, then the Urdu for a traditional physician.

You'll want to know: بلوانا.
بلوانا — bulvānā — "to have (someone) sent for".
امی نے ڈاکٹر کو گھر بلوایا۔ ammī ne ḍākṭar ko ghar bulvāyā. Mum had the doctor called to the house.
Past, with نے (he or she …): اس نے حکیم کو بلوایا, us ne hakīm ko bulvāyā.

The word, taken apart.
بلوانا (bulvānā) is built on بلانا, "to call", which CDIAL lists under bōll, "speak", a form it reconstructs (CDIAL 9321): the form for having someone else do the calling.
