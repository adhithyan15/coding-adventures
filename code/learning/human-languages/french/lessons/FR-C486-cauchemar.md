## You'll want to know: le cauchemar
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C486-NUITS-03]; assesses=[] -->

**le cauchemar** — "nightmare".

> Cette nuit, j'ai fait un cauchemar terrible.
>
> *Last night I had a terrible nightmare.*

*Faire un cauchemar* — to have a nightmare.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*le cauchemar* comes from Old French *cauquemare* (EtymDB), built from *caucher* "to press", from Latin *calcāre* "to tread", and Middle Dutch *mare* "a night ghost", a Germanic word for a spirit that sits on the sleeper.
