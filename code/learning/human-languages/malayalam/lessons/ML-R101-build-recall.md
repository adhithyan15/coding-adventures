## Grammar Lens: three of them go in front, and one cannot
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ML-LEX-C101-SHORT-01, ML-LEX-C101-THIN-01, ML-LEX-C101-STOUT-01, ML-GRAMMAR-C42-ADJ-SYSTEM, ML-CONCEPT-C36-KUTTI-01] -->

Three of this chapter's four words are **adjectives**, and all three behave the
way every adjective in the book has:

| adjective | before the noun |
|---|---|
| **കുറിയ** | **കുറിയ കുട്ടി** (*kuṟiya kuṭṭi*) |
| **മെലിഞ്ഞ** | **മെലിഞ്ഞ കുട്ടി** (*meliñña kuṭṭi*) |
| **തടിച്ച** | **തടിച്ച കുട്ടി** (*taṭicca kuṭṭi*) |

They end **-ിയ**, **-ഞ്ഞ** and **-ച്ച** — three different shapes, all closing on
**-a**, none of them changing for the noun behind it.

**ഉയരം is the odd one, and not because it is irregular.** It is a **noun**, so
there is nothing for it to stand in front of. It uses the frame you were given
for *having*:

> **എനിക്ക് ഉയരമുണ്ട്.** — *enikku uyaramuṇṭŭ* — to-me height there-is — *I am
> tall.*

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[ML-LEX-C101-HEIGHT-01, ML-CONCEPT-C32-UNDU-01, ML-CONCEPT-C06-DATIVE-SUBJECT-01, ML-LEX-C42-ADJ-02] -->

**The book still has no single word for *tall*.** It has a noun for height and a
way to say that someone has it, and between them that is the whole meaning.

That is worth noticing rather than papering over: **ചെറിയ** and **കുറിയ** are
almost the same word and do not mean the same thing — one is *small*, the other
*short* — and *tall* is the mirror of neither. You reach it by another route.

## Wrap-up Recall

[PAUSE 3s] Short, stout, height? (**കുറിയ, തടിച്ച, ഉയരം**.) Which of the three
cannot go in front of a noun? (**ഉയരം** — it is a noun itself.) So how do you
