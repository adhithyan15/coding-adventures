## You'll want to know: निषीदति
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C402-DHARMASHALA402-03]; assesses=[] -->

**निषीदति** — *niṣīdati* — "sits down to rest".

> श्रान्तः पथिकः वृक्षस्य छायायां निषीदति।
>
> *śrāntaḥ pathikaḥ vṛkṣasya chāyāyāṃ niṣīdati.*
>
> The tired wayfarer sits down to rest in the shade of a tree.

Past (he …): सः छायायां निषण्णवान्, *saḥ chāyāyāṃ niṣaṇṇavān*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**निषीदति** (*niṣīdati*) is built from *ni*, "down", and the root √sad, "to sit"; MW gives *ni-ṣad* as to sit down, rest or settle, with the participle *niṣaṇṇa*, "seated, resting" (MW).
