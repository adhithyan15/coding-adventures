German, chapter 69: Fish, Meat, Chicken, Fruit, Apple.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
der Fisch — a fish.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the German for to paint, then the German for to clean.

You'll want to know: der Fisch.
der Fisch — "a fish".

The word, taken apart.
Fisch from Old High German fisk from Proto-Germanic fiskaz — inherited, the same word as English fish. (EtymDB 2.1).
