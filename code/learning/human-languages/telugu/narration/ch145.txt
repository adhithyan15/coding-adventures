Telugu, chapter 145: To learn, To teach, To forget, To jump, To get down.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
నేర్చుకో (nērcukō) — to learn.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to send, then the Telugu for to look for.

You'll want to know: నేర్చుకో.
నేర్చుకో — nērcukō — "to learn".

The word, taken apart.
నేర్చుకో (nērcukō) is native Dravidian: nērcu, "to learn", which DEDR 3770 lists, with -konu, "for oneself".

Lesson 5 of 5.
దిగు (digu) — to get down, to get off.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Telugu for to forget, then the Telugu for to jump.

You'll want to know: దిగు.
దిగు — digu — "to get down, to get off".

The word, taken apart.
దిగు (digu) is native Dravidian, one word with Tamil iḻi and Kannada iḻi (DEDR 502). DEDR lists Telugu digu, "to descend, alight".
