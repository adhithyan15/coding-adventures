## You'll want to know: dégeler
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[FR-LEX-C463-FROID-04]; assesses=[] -->

**dégeler** — "to thaw".

> Au printemps, la rivière dégèle.
>
> *In spring the river thaws.*

*Le dégel* — the thaw.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*dégeler* is built from *dé-* and *geler* 'to freeze' (EtymDB lists it), which is inherited from Latin *gelāre*.
