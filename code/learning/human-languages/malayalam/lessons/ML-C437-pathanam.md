# പഠനം (paṭhanaṁ) — studies; a study

## You'll want to know: പഠനം
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ML-LEX-C437-GANITAM437-05]; assesses=[] -->

**പഠനം** — *paṭhanaṁ* — "studies; a study".

> അവന്റെ പഠനം കഴിഞ്ഞ വർഷം തീർന്നു.
>
> *avanṟe paṭhanaṁ kaḻiñña varṣaṁ tīrnnu.*
>
> His studies finished last year.

The noun of പഠിക്കുക, to learn. A study, a piece of research, is also a പഠനം.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**പഠനം** (*paṭhanaṁ*) is borrowed from Sanskrit *paṭhana*, "reading" (CDIAL 7713), from the root *paṭh*, "to read aloud", the root of പഠിക്കുക.
