## You'll want to know: 山
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ZH-LEX-C68-NATURE68-01]; assesses=[] -->

**山** — *shān* — "mountain; hill".

> **我喜欢山** *wǒ xǐhuan shān* — I love the mountains.

The new character, in pieces: 山 — three peaks standing on one base. The next lesson puts it in your hand.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**山** (*shān*) is inherited from Middle Chinese *srean* (Guangyun, in Baxter's transcription); the dictionary quotes an old book saying the mountain brings forth all things.
