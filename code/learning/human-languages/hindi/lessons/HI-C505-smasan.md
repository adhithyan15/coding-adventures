## You'll want to know: श्मशान
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C505-SHOK505-02]; assesses=[] -->

**श्मशान** — *śmaśān* — "a cremation ground".

> परिवार के लोग श्मशान तक गए।
>
> *parivār ke log śmaśān tak gae.*
>
> The family went as far as the cremation ground.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**श्मशान** (*śmaśān*) is a tatsama, borrowed from Sanskrit *śmaśāna*, "a place for burning dead bodies" (Monier-Williams).
