# Unit 14: Survival Phrases

## Warm-up

[PAUSE 2s] Say all seven question words from Unit 13. Today's phrases are
what you reach for when someone answers too fast to follow.

## New Material

**no entiendo** — I don't understand
> *entender* ← Latin *intendere* ("to stretch toward, direct one's
> attention" → English *intend*, *intent*, *intense*).

**¿puede repetir?** — can you repeat that?
> *repetir* ← Latin *repetere* (*re-* "again" + *petere* "to seek" →
> English *repeat*, *petition*, *compete*).

**más despacio, por favor** — more slowly, please
> *despacio* ← Latin *de spatio* ("with space/room" — cognate with English
> *space*).

**perdón** — sorry / pardon
> ← Latin *perdonare* (*per-* "thoroughly" + *donare* "to give" → English
> *pardon*, *donate*, *donor*).

**disculpe** — excuse me
> ← Latin *dis-* + *culpa* ("blame" → English *culpable*, *culprit*,
> *exculpate* — literally "to remove blame").

## Guided Practice

- [YOU SAY: "no entiendo. ¿puede repetir?"]
- [YOU SAY: "más despacio, por favor"]
