Kannada, chapter 375: The Relief Camp.
7 lessons. All 7 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 7.
ಶಿಬಿರ (śibira) — a camp.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a noise, then the Kannada for to kill.

You'll want to know: ಶಿಬಿರ.
ಶಿಬಿರ — śibira — "a camp".
ಪ್ರವಾಹದ ನಂತರ ಶಾಲೆಯಲ್ಲಿ ಒಂದು ಶಿಬಿರ ತೆರೆದರು. pravāhada nantara śāleyalli ondu śibira teredaru. After the flood they opened a camp in the school.
Plural: ಶಿಬಿರಗಳು, śibiragaḷu.
Also a training or health camp: ಆರೋಗ್ಯ ಶಿಬಿರ (śibira).

The word, taken apart.
ಶಿಬಿರ (śibira) is borrowed from Sanskrit śibira, "a royal camp, a tent, an entrenchment" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 2 of 7.

ನಿರಾಶ್ರಿತ — nirāśrita — "a refugee; homeless".
ಶಿಬಿರದಲ್ಲಿ ಐನೂರು ನಿರಾಶ್ರಿತರು ಇದ್ದರು. śibiradalli ainūru nirāśritaru iddaru. There were five hundred refugees in the camp.
Plural: ನಿರಾಶ್ರಿತರು, nirāśritaru.
ನಿರ್-, without, and ಆಶ್ರಿತ, sheltered: one without shelter.

The word, taken apart.
ನಿರಾಶ್ರಿತ (nirāśrita) is borrowed from Sanskrit: nir-, "without", with āśrita, "sheltered, depending on" (Monier-Williams).

Lesson 4 of 7.
ಹಸ್ತಾಂತರಿಸು (hastāntarisu) — to hand over.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for a refugee, then the Kannada for condolence.

You'll want to know: ಹಸ್ತಾಂತರಿಸು.
ಹಸ್ತಾಂತರಿಸು — hastāntarisu — "to hand over".
ಸ್ವಯಂಸೇವಕರು ಊಟದ ಪೊಟ್ಟಣಗಳನ್ನು ಅಧಿಕಾರಿಗಳಿಗೆ ಹಸ್ತಾಂತರಿಸಿದರು. svayaṃsēvakaru ūṭada poṭṭaṇagaḷannu adhikārigaḷige hastāntarisidaru. The volunteers handed over the food parcels to the officials.
Past (I …): ಹಸ್ತಾಂತರಿಸಿದೆ, hastāntariside.
What is handed over takes -ಅನ್ನು; who receives it takes -ಗೆ.

The word, taken apart.
ಹಸ್ತಾಂತರಿಸು (hastāntarisu) is built from Sanskrit hasta, "the hand" (CDIAL 14024), and antara, "the inside, another" (CDIAL 357): into another's hand, with the Kannada verb ending -isu.

Lesson 5 of 7.
ಧಾವಿಸು (dhāvisu) — to rush; to hasten.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Kannada for condolence, then the Kannada for to hand over.

You'll want to know: ಧಾವಿಸು.
ಧಾವಿಸು — dhāvisu — "to rush; to hasten".
ಸುದ್ದಿ ಕೇಳಿ ವೈದ್ಯರು ಶಿಬಿರಕ್ಕೆ ಧಾವಿಸಿದರು. suddi kēḷi vaidyaru śibirakke dhāvisidaru. Hearing the news, the doctors rushed to the camp.
Past (I …): ಧಾವಿಸಿದೆ, dhāviside.
Where one rushes takes -ಗೆ: ನೆರವಿಗೆ ಧಾವಿಸು (dhāvisu), to rush to help.

The word, taken apart.
ಧಾವಿಸು (dhāvisu) is built on Sanskrit dhāv, "to run, to flow" (Monier-Williams), with the Kannada verb ending -isu.
