## You'll want to know: કિલો
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GU-LEX-C183-GROCER183-05]; assesses=[] -->

**કિલો** — *kilo* — "kilo, kilogram".

> **એક કિલો ચોખા સો રૂપિયા છે.** — *ek kilo cokhā so rūpiyā che* — "A kilo of rice costs a hundred rupees."

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**કિલો** (*kilo*) is a loan from English *kilo*, short for *kilogram*.
