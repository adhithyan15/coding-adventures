## You'll want to know: andernfalls
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[GE-LEX-C365-ESSEIDENN-03]; assesses=[] -->

**andernfalls** — "otherwise, if not".

> Wir müssen jetzt gehen, andernfalls verpassen wir den Bus.
>
> *We have to go now; otherwise we will miss the bus.*

The verb comes right after it: *andernfalls verpassen wir …*. A formal *sonst*.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*andernfalls* grew out of *andern Falls* "in the other case", from *ander* "other" and *Fall* "fall, case", Old High German *val* (EtymDB), with the adverb ending *-s*.
