Sanskrit, chapter 385: Competing.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
स्पर्धते (spardhate) — competes.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for carries out, then the Sanskrit for encourages.

You'll want to know: स्पर्धते.
स्पर्धते — spardhate — "competes".
यदि मीरा स्पर्धते, तर्हि सा प्रथमा भविष्यति। yadi mīrā spardhate, tarhi sā prathamā bhaviṣyati. If Mira competes, she will come first.
Past (she …): सा स्पर्धितवती, sā spardhitavatī.

The word, taken apart.
स्पर्धते (spardhate) comes from the root √spardh, "to contend, to vie"; MW gives spardhita "contending or competing together" (MW).

You'll want to know: प्रतिद्वन्द्वी.
प्रतिद्वन्द्वी — pratidvandvī — "a rival".
यदि प्रतिद्वन्द्वी बलवान्, तर्हि अधिकः अभ्यासः आवश्यकः। yadi pratidvandvī balavān, tarhi adhikaḥ abhyāsaḥ āvaśyakaḥ. If the rival is strong, more practice is needed.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
प्रतिद्वन्द्वी (pratidvandvī) is built on prati, "against", and dvandva, "a pair, a strife"; MW gives prati-dvaṃdvin "an adversary, a rival" (MW).

Lesson 3 of 5.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for competes, then the Sanskrit for a rival.

You'll want to know: जेता.
जेता — jetā — "a winner; a victor".
यदि भवान् जेता भवति, तर्हि पारितोषिकं लभते। yadi bhavān jetā bhavati, tarhi pāritoṣikaṃ labhate. If you are the winner, you get the prize.
A masculine noun.

The word, taken apart.
जेता (jetā) is built on the root √ji, "to win", with the agent ending -tṛ; MW gives jetṛ "victorious, triumphant, a conqueror" (MW).

Lesson 4 of 5.
पुरस्करोति (puraskaroti) — rewards (with a prize).

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Sanskrit for a rival, then the Sanskrit for a winner.

You'll want to know: पुरस्करोति.
पुरस्करोति — puraskaroti — "rewards (with a prize)".
यदि छात्रः प्रथमः, तर्हि विद्यालयः तं पुरस्करोति। yadi chātraḥ prathamaḥ, tarhi vidyālayaḥ taṃ puraskaroti. If the student comes first, the school rewards him.
Past (he …): सः छात्रं पुरस्कृतवान्, saḥ chātraṃ puraskṛtavān.

The word, taken apart.
पुरस्करोति (puraskaroti) is built from puras, "in front", and the root √kṛ, "to make"; MW gives puras-√kṛ "to place before" and "to respect, to honour", and "rewards with a prize" is its modern sense (MW).
