## You'll want to know: বিনয়ী
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[BN-LEX-C459-BINOYI459-01]; assesses=[] -->

**বিনয়ী** — *binôyī* — "modest; humble".

> গায়িকা খুব বিখ্যাত, কিন্তু তিনি বিনয়ী।
>
> *gāyikā khub bikhyātô, kintu tini binôyī.*
>
> The singer is very famous, but she is modest.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**বিনয়ী** (*binôyī*) is *binôy*, Sanskrit *vinaya*, "training, discipline, modesty" (Monier-Williams), with the ending *-ī*, "having".
