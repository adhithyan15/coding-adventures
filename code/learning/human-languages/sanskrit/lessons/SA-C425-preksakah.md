## You'll want to know: प्रेक्षकः
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C425-SMARAKA425-03]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रेक्षकः** — *prekṣakaḥ* — "an onlooker; a spectator".

> स्मारकस्य समीपे प्रेक्षकाः सन्ति।
>
> *smārakasya samīpe prekṣakāḥ santi.*
>
> There are onlookers near the memorial.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**प्रेक्षकः** (*prekṣakaḥ*) is built from *pra*, "forth", and the root √īkṣ, "to see", with the agent ending *-aka*; MW gives *prekṣaka* as a spectator, a member of an audience (MW).
