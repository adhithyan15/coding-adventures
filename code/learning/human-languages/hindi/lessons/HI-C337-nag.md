## You'll want to know: नाग
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[HI-LEX-C337-KHAZANA337-03]; assesses=[] -->

**नाग** — *nāg* — "a cobra; a serpent".

> ख़ज़ाने के पास एक नाग रहता था।
>
> *khazāne ke pās ek nāg rahtā thā.*
>
> A cobra lived beside the treasure.

Masculine.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

is Sanskrit *nāga*, "a serpent, a cobra" (Monier-Williams). In tales the **नाग** guards hidden treasure.

## What you've built
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

One more word for telling what happened.
