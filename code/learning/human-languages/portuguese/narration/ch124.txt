Portuguese, chapter 124: Nice, Kind, Serious, Right, Wrong.
5 lessons. All 5 can be done entirely by ear.


Lesson 1 of 5.
simpático — nice, friendly.

Warm-up.
[pause 2 seconds]
Before the new one: say the Portuguese for boring, then the Portuguese for fun, funny.

You'll want to know: simpático.
simpático — "nice, friendly".

The word, taken apart.
simpático from French sympathique, "likeable" (EtymDB 2.1) — borrowed. Behind it is Greek sympatheia, "fellow feeling".

Before the new one: say the Portuguese for fun, funny, then the Portuguese for nice.

You'll want to know: gentil.
gentil — "kind".

The word, taken apart.
gentil from Latin gentilis, "of the same clan", from gens, "clan" (Lewis & Short). EtymDB 2.1 traces it only to Old Portuguese gentil.
