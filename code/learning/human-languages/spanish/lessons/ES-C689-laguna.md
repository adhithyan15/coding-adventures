## You'll want to know: la laguna
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[ES-LEX-C689-AGUA-03]; assesses=[] -->

**la laguna** — "the lagoon, the small lake".

> Hay patos en la laguna.
>
> *There are ducks on the lagoon.*

*Tener lagunas* — to have gaps in your memory.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

*laguna* is inherited from Latin *lacūna* "a ditch, a pool, a gap" (EtymDB), from *lacus* "lake".
