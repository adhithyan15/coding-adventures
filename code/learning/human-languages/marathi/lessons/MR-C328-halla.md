## You'll want to know: हल्ला
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[MR-LEX-C328-LADHAI328-02]; assesses=[] -->

**हल्ला** — *hallā* — "an attack".

> पहाटे सैन्यावर अचानक हल्ला झाला.
>
> *pahāṭe sainyāvar acānak hallā jhālā.*
>
> At dawn the army was suddenly attacked.

Masculine.

X-वर हल्ला करणे, to attack X.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**हल्ला** (*hallā*) is inherited: CDIAL lists Marathi *hallā*, 'an attack', under *\*halla*, 'movement' (CDIAL 14017).
