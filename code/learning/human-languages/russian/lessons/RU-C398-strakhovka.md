## You'll want to know: страховка
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[RU-LEX-C398-ARENDA-05]; assesses=[] -->

**страховка** (*strakhóvka*) — "insurance; a safety rope".

> У меня есть медицинская страховка.
>
> *U menyá yest meditsínskaya strakhóvka.*
>
> I have medical insurance.

Feminine. Colloquial for *страхование*. It is also a climber’s safety rope.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**страховка** is built on *страховать* "to insure", from *страх* "fear".
