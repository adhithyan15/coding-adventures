## You'll want to know: वक्ता
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[SA-LEX-C343-VADA343-03]; assesses=[] -->

**वक्ता** — *vaktā* — "a speaker".

> वक्ता स्पष्टं वदेत्।
>
> *vaktā spaṣṭaṃ vadet.*
>
> A speaker should speak clearly.

A masculine noun.

## The word, taken apart
<!-- hl-knowledge: introduces=[]; assesses=[] -->

**वक्ता** (*vaktā*) is built on the root √vac, "to speak", with the agent ending *-tṛ*; MW gives *vaktṛ* "one who speaks, a speaker" (MW).
